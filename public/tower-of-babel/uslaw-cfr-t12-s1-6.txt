12 C.F.R. § 1.6 — Convertible securities.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1.6
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1 (Investment Securities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A national bank may not purchase securities convertible into stock at the option of the issuer.
