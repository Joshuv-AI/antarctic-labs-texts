45 C.F.R. § 630.210 — To whom must I distribute my drug-free workplace statement?
Citation: us:cfr:45:630.210
Creator: National Science Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-630.210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 630 (Governmentwide Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Financial Assistance)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must require that a copy of the statement described in § 630.205 be given to each employee who will be engaged in the performance of any Federal award.
