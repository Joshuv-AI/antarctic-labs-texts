25 U.S.C. § 5332 — Sovereign immunity and trusteeship rights unaffected
Citation: us:usc:25:5332
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section5332
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 46 (Indian Self-Determination And Education Assistance); Subchapter I (Indian Self-Determination). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed as—
(1)
affecting, modifying, diminishing, or otherwise impairing the sovereign immunity from suit enjoyed by an Indian tribe; or
(2)
authorizing or requiring the termination of any existing trust responsibility of the United States with respect to the Indian people.
