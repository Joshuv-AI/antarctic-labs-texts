16 U.S.C. § 1613 — Promulgation of regulations
Citation: us:usc:16:1613
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section1613
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 36 (Forest And Rangeland Renewable Resources Planning); Subchapter I (Planning). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall prescribe such regulations as he determines necessary and desirable to carry out the provisions of this subchapter.
