Thank you to our 2023 reviewers
Published: 2023

I want to thank all the reviewers who have volunteered their time and expertise to evaluate submissions considered for publication in MTNA. Your contributions have helped to maintain the highest scientific and ethical standards for our journal. Our primary goal remains to offer research articles at the cutting edge of oligonucleotide therapeutics and delivery, including novel clinical developments of oligonucleotide-based drugs. Your commitment has been critical for the success of the journal. We sincerely appreciate your efforts and hope for your continued support in the coming year.

Paloma Giangrande, Editor-in-Chief
