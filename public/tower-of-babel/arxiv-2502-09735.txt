A proof of generic Green’s conjecture in odd genus
Michael Kemeny

Abstract
. In this note, we give a new proof of Voisin’s theorem [ V ] resembling [ K1 , §1, §2] .



1.
The proof

Our goal is to give a proof of Voisin’s theorem
[
V
]

[
K1

\mathcal{Z}

{\mathbf{P}}

with
(L^{\prime})^{2}=4k+2
together with the class of a smooth rational curve

(L^{\prime}\cdot\Delta)=0

Lemma 1.1
.

We have
\tau^{*}I_{\hat{\mathcal{Z}}}\simeq I_{\mathcal{Z}}
,
\tau_{*}I_{\mathcal{Z}}\simeq I_{\hat{\mathcal{Z}}}
and
\tau_{*}(I_{\mathcal{Z}}\otimes p^{*}\mathcal{O}_{X}(-\Delta))\simeq I_{\hat{{\mathcal{Z}}}}\otimes\hat{p}^{*}I_{v}

Proof.

We have the two exact sequences

\displaystyle 0

\displaystyle\to\mathcal{\mathcal{}}{O}_{X}\boxtimes\mathcal{O}_{{\mathbf{P}}}(-2)\xrightarrow{\text{id}}E\boxtimes\mathcal{O}_{{\mathbf{P}}}(-1)\to p^{*}L^{\prime}\otimes I_{\mathcal{Z}}\to 0\text{ and}

\displaystyle 0

\displaystyle\to\mathcal{\mathcal{}}{O}_{\hat{X}}\boxtimes\mathcal{O}_{{\mathbf{P}}_{2}}(-2)\xrightarrow{\text{id}}\hat{E}\boxtimes\mathcal{O}_{{\mathbf{P}}_{2}}(-1)\to{\hat{p}}^{*}{\hat{L}}\otimes I_{\hat{\mathcal{Z}}}\to 0.

\mu_{*}\mathcal{O}_{X}\simeq\mathcal{O}_{\hat{X}}

\hat{X}\times{\mathbf{P}}_{2}

\tau_{*}

The following lemma is important for us.

Lemma 1.2
.

The sheaves
q^{*}q_{*}(p^{*}L\otimes I_{\mathcal{Z}})
and
q^{*}q_{*}(p^{*}L^{\prime}\otimes I_{\mathcal{Z}})
are locally free on
X\times{\mathbf{P}}
. Further, the natural morphisms
q^{*}q_{*}(p^{*}L\otimes I_{\mathcal{Z}})\to p^{*}L\otimes I_{\mathcal{Z}}
and
q^{*}q_{*}(p^{*}L^{\prime}\otimes I_{\mathcal{Z}})\to p^{*}L^{\prime}\otimes I_{\mathcal{Z}}

Proof.

Now
i_{*}q_{*}(p^{*}L^{\prime}\otimes I_{\mathcal{Z}})\simeq\hat{q}_{*}\tau_{*}(p^{*}L^{\prime}\otimes I_{\mathcal{Z}})\simeq\hat{q}_{*}(\hat{p}^{*}\hat{L}\otimes\tau_{*}I_{\mathcal{Z}})\simeq\hat{q}_{*}(\hat{p}^{*}\hat{L}\otimes I_{\hat{{\mathcal{Z}}}})
, Lemma
1.1
.
By miracle flatness
I_{\hat{{\mathcal{Z}}}}
is flat over
{\mathbf{P}}_{2}
. We have the exact sequence
0\to\mathcal{O}_{\hat{X}}\xrightarrow{s}\hat{E}\to\hat{L}\otimes I_{Z(s)}\to 0,

\mathrm{H}^{1}(\mathcal{O}_{\hat{X}})=0

for any

Next,
i_{*}q_{*}(p^{*}(L^{\prime}(-\Delta))\otimes I_{\mathcal{Z}})\simeq\hat{q}_{*}\tau_{*}(p^{*}(L^{\prime}(-\Delta))\otimes I_{\mathcal{Z}})\simeq\hat{q}_{*}(\hat{p}^{*}\hat{L}\otimes I_{\hat{{\mathcal{Z}}}}\otimes\hat{p}^{*}I_{v}).

To proceed, we first need a lemma.

Lemma 1.3
.

The sheaves
R^{1}\hat{q}_{*}(\hat{L}\otimes I_{\hat{{\mathcal{Z}}}})
and
R^{1}\hat{q}_{*}(\hat{p}^{*}\hat{L}\otimes I_{\hat{{\mathcal{Z}}}}\otimes\hat{p}^{*}I_{v})
on
{\mathbf{P}}_{2}
are line bundles.

Proof.

We have the exact sequence
0\to\mathcal{O}_{\hat{X}}\xrightarrow{t}\hat{E}\to\hat{L}\otimes I_{Z(t)}\to 0
for
t\neq 0\in\mathrm{H}^{0}(\hat{E})
. Since
\mathrm{H}^{1}(\hat{E})=\mathrm{H}^{2}(\hat{E})=0
, this gives
h^{1}(\hat{X},\hat{L}\otimes I_{Z(t)})=h^{2}(\mathcal{O}_{\hat{X}})=1
and so
R^{1}\hat{q}_{*}(\hat{p}^{*}\hat{L}\otimes I_{\hat{{\mathcal{Z}}}})
is a line bundle by Grauert’s theorem. To study
R^{1}\hat{q}_{*}(\hat{p}^{*}\hat{L}\otimes I_{\hat{{\mathcal{Z}}}}\otimes\hat{p}^{*}I_{v})
, we use Grauert’s theorem plus the exact sequence

0\to I_{v}\xrightarrow{s}\hat{E}\otimes I_{v}\to\hat{L}\otimes I_{Z(s)}\otimes I_{v}\to 0

of sheaves on
\hat{X}
, for
s\neq 0\in\mathrm{H}^{0}(\hat{E})
. Note that the finite map
\hat{{\mathcal{Z}}}\to{\mathbf{P}}_{2}
is flat by miracle flatness. Now we claim
\mathrm{H}^{i}(\hat{X},\hat{E}\otimes I_{v})=0
for
i=1,2
. Indeed,
\mu_{*}\mathcal{O}_{X}(-\Delta)\simeq I_{v}
and
R^{i}\mu_{*}\mathcal{O}_{X}(-\Delta)=0
for
i>0
.
Thus,
R^{i}\mu_{*}(E(-\Delta))\simeq\hat{E}\otimes R^{i}\mu_{*}\mathcal{O}_{X}(-\Delta)=0
for
i>0
. So
h^{i}(\hat{X},\hat{E}\otimes I_{v})=h^{i}(X,E(-\Delta))
for
i>0
. But this vanishes, as
E(-\Delta)
is a Lazarsfeld–Mukai bundle,
[
K1
, Lemma 3.5]
. So
h^{1}(\hat{L}\otimes I_{Z(s)}\otimes I_{v})=h^{2}(I_{v})=h^{2}(\mathcal{O}_{\hat{X}})=1
, from the exact sequence
0\to I_{v}\to\mathcal{O}_{\hat{X}}\to\mathcal{O}_{v}\to 0.

So
R^{1}\hat{q}_{*}(\hat{p}^{*}\hat{L}\otimes I_{\hat{{\mathcal{Z}}}}\otimes\hat{p}^{*}I_{v})
is a line bundle.

The next result is analogous to

Proposition 1.4
.

Define a coherent sheaf on
{\mathbf{P}}
by
W:=\mathrm{Coker}\left(q_{*}(p^{*}L^{\prime}\otimes I_{\mathcal{Z}})\to q_{*}p^{*}L^{\prime}\right).

Then
W
is locally free of rank

. Further, define a coherent sheaf by
\widetilde{W}:=\mathrm{Coker}\left(q_{*}(p^{*}L\otimes I_{\mathcal{Z}})\to q_{*}p^{*}L\right).

Then
\widetilde{W}
is also locally free of rank
k+1
.

Proof.

Define
W^{\prime}:=\mathrm{Coker}\left(\hat{q}_{*}(\hat{p}^{*}\hat{L}\otimes I_{\hat{{\mathcal{Z}}}})\to\hat{q}_{*}\hat{p}^{*}\hat{L}\right)
,

\hat{W}:=\mathrm{Coker}\left(\hat{q}_{*}(\hat{p}^{*}\hat{L}\otimes I_{\hat{{\mathcal{Z}}}}\otimes\hat{p}^{*}I_{v})\to\hat{q}_{*}(\hat{p}^{*}\hat{L}\otimes\hat{p}^{*}I_{v})\right)

on
{\mathbf{P}}_{2}
. We will show they are locally free. For the first sheaf,
we have the exact sequence

0\to W^{\prime}\to\hat{q}_{*}({\hat{p}^{*}\hat{L}}_{|_{\hat{{\mathcal{Z}}}}})\to R^{1}\hat{q}_{*}(\hat{p}^{*}\hat{L}\otimes I_{\hat{{\mathcal{Z}}}})\to 0.

By Lemma
1.3
,
R^{1}\hat{q}_{*}(\hat{p}^{*}\hat{L}\otimes I_{\hat{{\mathcal{Z}}}})
is a line bundle. The finite map
\hat{{\mathcal{Z}}}\to{\mathbf{P}}_{2}
is flat by miracle flatness, so, by Grauert’s theorem,
\hat{q}_{*}({\hat{p}^{*}\hat{L}}_{|_{\hat{{\mathcal{Z}}}}})
is a vector bundle of rank
k+2
. So
W^{\prime}
is locally free of rank
k+1
. Define

S:=\mathrm{Coker}\left(\hat{q}_{*}(\hat{p}^{*}\hat{L}\otimes I_{\hat{{\mathcal{Z}}}}\otimes\hat{p}^{*}I_{v})\to\hat{q}_{*}\hat{p}^{*}\hat{L}\right).

We have an exact sequence

0\to\hat{q}_{*}(\hat{p}^{*}\hat{L}\otimes p^{*}I_{v})\to\hat{q}_{*}\hat{p}^{*}\hat{L}\to\hat{q}_{*}(\hat{p}^{*}\hat{L}_{|_{p\times{\mathbf{P}}_{2}}})\to 0,

and
\hat{q}_{*}(\hat{p}^{*}\hat{L}_{|_{p\times{\mathbf{P}}_{2}}})
is a line bundle on
{\mathbf{P}}_{2}
. Indeed, it suffices to show
R^{1}\hat{q}_{*}(\hat{p}^{*}\hat{L}\otimes\hat{p}^{*}I_{v})=0
, and by Grauert’s theorem for this it suffices to note
h^{1}(X,L)=h^{1}(X,L^{\prime}(-\Delta))=h^{1}(\hat{X},\hat{L}\otimes I_{v})=0
,
[
K1
, Lemma 3.1]
. So we have an exact sequence

0\to\hat{W}\to S\to\hat{q}_{*}(\hat{p}^{*}\hat{L}_{|_{p\times{\mathbf{P}}_{2}}})\to 0,

where
\hat{q}_{*}(\hat{p}^{*}\hat{L}_{|_{p\times{\mathbf{P}}_{2}}})
is a line bundle. To show
\hat{W}
is locally free, it suffices to show
S
is locally free.

Let

denote the closed subscheme
\mathcal{Z}\bigcup\Delta\times{\mathbf{P}}
defined by the product ideal
I_{\mathcal{Z}}\,p^{*}I_{\Delta}.
Since
\Delta\times{\mathbf{P}}
is a divisor, this ideal is isomorphic to
I_{\mathcal{Z}}\otimes p^{*}I_{\Delta}.
Set
\hat{T}\subseteq\hat{X}\times{\mathbf{P}}_{2}
to be the closed subscheme defined by the product ideal

Applying the left-exact functor
\tau_{*}
, and using from Lemma

\tau_{*}(I_{\mathcal{Z}}\otimes p^{*}I_{\Delta})\simeq I_{\hat{{\mathcal{Z}}}}\otimes\hat{p}^{*}I_{v}

. So the zero locus of

\hat{T}:=\hat{{\mathcal{Z}}}\bigcup\{v\times{\mathbf{P}}_{2}\}

Lemma 1.5
.

The natural map
l\,:\,\widetilde{W}\to W
, induced by the map
L\hookrightarrow L^{\prime}
, given by multiplication by a nonzero section
s_{\Delta}\in\mathrm{H}^{0}(\mathcal{O}_{X}(\Delta))
, is injective.

Proof.

Recall
W:=\mathrm{Coker}\left(q_{*}(p^{*}L^{\prime}\otimes I_{\mathcal{Z}})\to q_{*}p^{*}L^{\prime}\right)
and
\widetilde{W}:=\mathrm{Coker}\left(q_{*}(p^{*}L\otimes I_{\mathcal{Z}})\to q_{*}p^{*}L\right).

We have a commutative diagram

{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces 0}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\widetilde{W}}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces q_{*}((p^{*}L)_{|_{\mathcal{Z}}})}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces R^{1}q_{*}(p^{*}L\otimes I_{\mathcal{Z}})}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces 0}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces 0}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces W}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces q_{*}((p^{*}L^{\prime})_{|_{\mathcal{Z}}})}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces R^{1}q_{*}(p^{*}L^{\prime}\otimes I_{\mathcal{Z}})}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces 0}
\scriptstyle{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces l}
\scriptstyle{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces q_{*}({\cdot s_{p^{*}\Delta}}_{|_{\mathcal{Z}}})}

To prove
l
is injective, it suffices to show
q_{*}({\cdot{s_{p^{*}\Delta}}_{|_{\mathcal{Z}}}})
is injective, and, since
q_{*}
is left-exact, for this it suffices to show multiplication by
{s_{p^{*}\Delta}}_{|_{\mathcal{Z}}}
yields an injective map

(p^{*}L)_{|_{\mathcal{Z}}}\to(p^{*}L^{\prime})_{|_{\mathcal{Z}}}
and for this it suffices to note
\mathcal{Z}
is irreducible and is not contained in
\Delta\times{\mathbf{P}}
.
∎

We now prove the first of three needed statements for the proof.

Lemma 1.6
.

The natural morphism
g\,:\,\mathrm{H}^{1}(B,\pi^{*}(\bigwedge^{k+1}\Gamma_{2}({p^{\prime}}^{*}L^{\prime}))\to\mathrm{H}^{1}(B,\bigwedge^{k+1}\Gamma_{1}({p^{\prime}}^{*}L^{\prime})
is an isomorphism. Further
\det\Gamma_{i}\simeq{q^{\prime}}^{*}\mathcal{O}_{{\mathbf{P}}}(k+1)(D)
, for
i=1,2
.

Proof.

The natural map
\widetilde{W}\to W
is injective by Lemma
1.5
. We now prove
\det\widetilde{W}\simeq\det W
. Since
q_{*}p^{*}L
and
q_{*}p^{*}L^{\prime}
are trivial vector bundles, it is equivalent to prove
\det q_{*}(p^{*}L\otimes I_{\mathcal{Z}})\simeq\det q_{*}(p^{*}L^{\prime}\otimes I_{\mathcal{Z}})
, and then
\det\widetilde{W}\simeq\det W\simeq\det q_{*}(p^{*}L\otimes I_{\mathcal{Z}})^{-1}
. We have the exact sequence

0\to\mathcal{\mathcal{}}{O}_{X}\boxtimes\mathcal{O}_{{\mathbf{P}}}(-2)\xrightarrow{\text{id}}E\boxtimes\mathcal{O}_{{\mathbf{P}}}(-1)\to p^{*}L^{\prime}\otimes I_{\mathcal{Z}}\to 0

and applying
q_{*}
get an exact sequence

0\to\mathcal{O}_{{\mathbf{P}}}(-2)\to\mathrm{H}^{0}(E)\otimes\mathcal{O}_{{\mathbf{P}}}(-1)\to q_{*}(p^{*}L^{\prime}\otimes I_{\mathcal{Z}})\to 0.

Since
h^{0}(E)=k+3
, this gives
\det q_{*}(p^{*}L^{\prime}\otimes I_{\mathcal{Z}})\simeq\mathcal{O}_{{\mathbf{P}}}(-k-1)
. Next, from the exact sequence

0\to\mathcal{\mathcal{}}{O}_{X}(-\Delta)\boxtimes\mathcal{O}_{{\mathbf{P}}}(-2)\xrightarrow{\text{id}}E(-\Delta)\boxtimes\mathcal{O}_{{\mathbf{P}}}(-1)\to p^{*}L^{\prime}(-\Delta)\otimes I_{\mathcal{Z}}\to 0,

and
h^{0}(E(-\Delta))=k+1

[
K1
, Lemma 3.5]
, we get
\det q_{*}(p^{*}L\otimes I_{\mathcal{Z}})\simeq\det\mathrm{H}^{0}(E(-\Delta))\otimes\mathcal{O}_{{\mathbf{P}}}(-1)\simeq\mathcal{O}_{{\mathbf{P}}}(-k-1)
, as required. From the defining sequences for
\Gamma_{i}
,
i=1,2
we see
\det\Gamma_{i}\simeq\det{q^{\prime}}^{*}W(-D)\simeq{q^{\prime}}^{*}\mathcal{O}_{{\mathbf{P}}}(k+1)(-D)
. So,

\bigwedge^{k+1}{\Gamma_{2}}\simeq q^{\prime*}\det\widetilde{W}(-D)\simeq q^{\prime*}\det W(-D)\simeq\bigwedge^{k+1}{\Gamma_{1}}
. The natural injective map
\bigwedge^{k+1}{\Gamma_{2}}\hookrightarrow\bigwedge^{k+1}{q^{\prime}}^{*}\widetilde{W}\hookrightarrow\bigwedge^{k+1}W
, which lands in
\bigwedge^{k+1}{\Gamma_{1}}
, is an injective map
\bigwedge^{k+1}{\Gamma_{2}}\hookrightarrow\bigwedge^{k+1}{\Gamma_{1}}
between isomorphic line bundles on a projective space and hence must be an isomorphism as required.
∎

Theorem 1.8
.

Let
X
denote a K3 surface of Picard rank
2
, generated by by a big and nef line bundle
L^{\prime}
with
(L^{\prime})^{2}=4k+2
together with the class of a smooth rational curve
\Delta
with
(L^{\prime}\cdot\Delta)=0
for
k\geq 4
, as above. Define the line bundle
L:=L^{\prime}(-\Delta).
Then
\mathrm{K}_{k-1,2}(C,\omega_{C})=0
.

Proof.

It remains to show
f\,:\,\mathrm{H}^{1}(B,\pi^{*}(\bigwedge^{k+1}\mathcal{M}_{L}(p^{*}L^{\prime}))\to\mathrm{H}^{1}(B,\bigwedge^{k+1}\Gamma_{2}({p^{\prime}}^{*}L^{\prime}))

is surjective.
From the exact sequence
0\to S_{2}\to\pi^{*}\mathcal{M}_{L}\to\Gamma_{2}\to 0,

we obtain the exact sequence

\ldots\to S_{2}\otimes\bigwedge^{k}\pi^{*}\mathcal{M}_{L}({p^{\prime}}^{*}L^{\prime})\to\bigwedge^{k+1}\pi^{*}\mathcal{M}_{L}({p^{\prime}}^{*}L^{\prime})\to\bigwedge^{k+1}\Gamma_{2}({p^{\prime}}^{*}L^{\prime})\to 0.

It suffices if

\mathrm{H}^{1+i}(\mathrm{Sym}^{i}(S_{2})\otimes\bigwedge^{k+1-i}\pi^{*}\mathcal{M}_{L}({p^{\prime}}^{*}L^{\prime}))=0,
for
1\leq i\leq k+1
. Now, we have the exact sequence

0\to S_{2}\to{q^{\prime}}^{*}{q^{\prime}}_{*}({p^{\prime}}^{*}L\otimes I_{D})\to{p^{\prime}}^{*}L\otimes I_{D}\to 0
and define

\mathcal{H}:={q^{\prime}}_{*}({p^{\prime}}^{*}L\otimes I_{D})\simeq q_{*}(p^{*}L\otimes I_{\mathcal{Z}}).
From the exact sequence

0\to\mathcal{\mathcal{}}{O}_{X}(-\Delta)\boxtimes\mathcal{O}_{{\mathbf{P}}}(-2)\xrightarrow{\text{id}}E(-\Delta)\boxtimes\mathcal{O}_{{\mathbf{P}}}(-1)\to p^{*}L\otimes I_{\mathcal{Z}}\to 0,

by applying
q_{*}
, we get
\mathcal{H}\simeq\mathrm{H}^{0}(X,E(-\Delta))\otimes\mathcal{O}_{{\mathbf{P}}}(-1).
We have the exact sequence

0\to\mathrm{Sym}^{i}S_{2}\to\mathrm{Sym}^{i}{q^{\prime}}^{*}\mathcal{H}\to\mathrm{Sym}^{i-1}{q^{\prime}}^{*}\mathcal{H}\otimes{p^{\prime}}^{*}{L^{\prime}}\otimes I_{D}\to 0
.
It suffices to have

\mathrm{H}^{1+i}(\mathrm{Sym}^{i}{q^{\prime}}^{*}\mathcal{H}\otimes\bigwedge^{k+1-i}\pi^{*}\mathcal{M}_{L}(p^{*}L^{\prime}))=\mathrm{H}^{i}(\mathrm{Sym}^{i-1}{q^{\prime}}^{*}\mathcal{H}\otimes\bigwedge^{k+1-i}\pi^{*}\mathcal{M}_{L}(p^{*}{L^{\prime}}^{2}\otimes I_{\mathcal{Z}}))=0,

for
1\leq i\leq k+1
.
So, it suffices to have

\mathrm{H}^{1+i}(X\times{\mathbf{P}},\bigwedge^{k+1-i}M_{L}(L^{\prime})\boxtimes\mathcal{O}_{{\mathbf{P}}}(-i))=\mathrm{H}^{i}(X\times{\mathbf{P}},(\bigwedge^{k+1-i}M_{L}({L^{\prime}}^{2})\boxtimes\mathcal{O}_{{\mathbf{P}}}(-i+1))\otimes I_{\mathcal{Z}})=0

for
1\leq i\leq k+1
, where
{\mathbf{P}}\simeq{\mathbf{P}}^{k+2}
. The first vanishing is immediate by the Künneth formula. For the second, by the above exact sequence, it is enough to have

\mathrm{H}^{i}(X\times{\mathbf{P}}^{k+2},\bigwedge^{k+1-i}M_{L}({L^{\prime}}\otimes E)\boxtimes\mathcal{O}_{{\mathbf{P}}}(-i))=\mathrm{H}^{i+1}(X\times{\mathbf{P}}^{k+2},(\bigwedge^{k+1-i}M_{L}({L^{\prime}})\boxtimes\mathcal{O}_{{\mathbf{P}}}(-i-1))=0,

for
1\leq i\leq k+1
, which is again immediate by the Künneth formula.

∎
