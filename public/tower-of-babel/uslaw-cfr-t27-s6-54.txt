27 C.F.R. § 6.54 — Advertising in retailer publications.
Citation: us:cfr:27:6.54
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-6.54
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 6 (“Tied-House”). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purchase, by an industry member, of advertising in a retailer publication for distribution to consumers or the general public constitutes paying the retailer for advertising within the meaning of the Act.
