24 C.F.R. § 291.430 — Elimination of lead-based paint hazards.
Citation: us:cfr:24:291.430
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-291.430
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 291 (Disposition Of Hud-Acquired And -Owned Single Family Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Lead-Based Paint Poisoning Prevention Act (42 U.S.C. 4821-4846), the Residential Lead-Based Paint Hazard Reduction Act of 1992 (42 U.S.C. 4851-4856), and implementing regulations at part 35, subparts A, B, F, and R of this title, apply to activities covered by this subpart.
