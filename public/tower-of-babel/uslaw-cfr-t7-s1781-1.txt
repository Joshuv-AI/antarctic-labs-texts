7 C.F.R. § 1781.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1781.1
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1781.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1781 (Resource Conservation And Development (Rcd) Loans And Watershed (Ws) Loans And Advances). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part prescribes the policies and procedures for making:
(a) Watershed (WS) loans and Watershed (WS) advances for works of improvement in a watershed project; and
(b) Resource Conservation and Development (RCD) loans for measures or projects needed to implement the RCD area plan to achieve objectives in an RCD area.
