32 C.F.R. § 1615.8 — Cancellation of registration.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1615.8
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1615.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1615 (Administration Of Registration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director of Selective Service may cancel the registration of any particular registrant or of a registrant who comes within a specified group of registrants.
