40 C.F.R. § 63.6130 — How do I demonstrate initial compliance with the emission and operating limitations?
Citation: us:cfr:40:63.6130
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-63.6130
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 63 (National Emission Standards For Hazardous Air Pollutants For Source Categories). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You must demonstrate initial compliance with each emission and operating limitation that applies to you according to Table 4 of this subpart.
(b) You must submit the Notification of Compliance Status containing results of the initial compliance demonstration according to the requirements in § 63.6145(f).
