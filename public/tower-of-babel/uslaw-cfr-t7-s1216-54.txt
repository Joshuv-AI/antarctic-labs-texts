7 C.F.R. § 1216.54 — Operating reserve.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1216.54
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Miscellaneous Commodities), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1216.54
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1216 (Peanut Promotion, Research, And Information Order). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board shall establish an operating monetary reserve and may carry over to subsequent fiscal years excess funds in a reserve so established; Provided, that funds in the reserve shall not exceed any fiscal year's anticipated expenses.
