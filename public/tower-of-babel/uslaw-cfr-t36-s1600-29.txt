36 C.F.R. § 1600.29 — Fees.
Citation: us:cfr:36:1600.29
Creator: Morris K. Udall Scholarship And Excellence In National Environmental Policy Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1600.29
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1600 (Public Availability Of Documents And Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Foundation will charge fees for duplication of records under the Privacy Act in the same way in which it charges duplication fees under § 1600.10. No search or review fee will be charged for any record.
