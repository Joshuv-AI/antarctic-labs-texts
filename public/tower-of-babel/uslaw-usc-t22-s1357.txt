22 U.S.C. § 1357 — Trade agreements with the Philippines
Citation: us:usc:22:1357
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section1357
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 15 (The Republic Of The Philippines); Subchapter IV (General Provisions Relating To Trade Relations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Until July 4, 1974, no trade agreement shall be made with the Philippines under section 1351 of title 19, unless, prior to such time, the President of the United States has made the proclamation provided for in section 1347 of this title, or the executive agreement provided for in subchapter III of this chapter has been terminated.
