42 U.S.C. § 6953 — Development of markets for recovered materials
Citation: us:usc:42:6953
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section6953
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 82 (Solid Waste Disposal); Subchapter V (Duties Of Secretary Of Commerce In Resource And Recovery). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Commerce shall within two years after September 1, 1979, take such actions as may be necessary to—
(1)
identify the geographical location of existing or potential markets for recovered materials;
(2)
identify the economic and technical barriers to the use of recovered materials; and
(3)
encourage the development of new uses for recovered materials.
