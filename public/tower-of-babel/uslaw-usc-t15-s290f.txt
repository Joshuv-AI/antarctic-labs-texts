15 U.S.C. § 290f — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:15:290f
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section290f
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 7A (Standard Reference Data Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated to carry out this chapter, $1.86 million for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1969. Notwithstanding the provisions of any other law, no appropriations for any fiscal year may be made for the purpose of this chapter after fiscal year 1969 unless previously authorized by legislation hereafter enacted by the Congress.
