46 U.S.C. § 30909 — Security
Citation: us:usc:46:30909
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section30909
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 309 (Suits In Admiralty Against The United States). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Neither the United States nor a federally-owned corporation may be required to give a bond or admiralty stipulation in a civil action under this chapter.
