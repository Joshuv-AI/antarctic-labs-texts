10 C.F.R. § 725.2 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:10:725.2
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-725.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 725 (Permits For Access To Restricted Data). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part apply to any person within or under the jurisdiction of the United States who desires access to Restricted Data for use in his business, profession or trade.
