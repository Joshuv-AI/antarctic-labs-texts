33 C.F.R. § 6.16-1 — -1 Reporting of sabotage, subversive activity, or an actual or threatened cyber incident.
Citation: us:cfr:33:6.16-1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-6.16-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 6 (Protection And Security Of Vessels, Harbors, And Waterfront Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Evidence of sabotage, subversive activity, or an actual or threatened cyber incident involving or endangering any vessel, harbor, port, or waterfront facility, including any data, information, network, program, system, or other digital infrastructure thereon or therein, shall be reported immediately to the Federal Bureau of Investigation, the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (for any cyber incident), and the Captain of the Port, or to their respective representatives.
