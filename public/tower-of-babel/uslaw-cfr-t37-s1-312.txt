37 C.F.R. § 1.312 — Amendments after allowance.
Citation: us:cfr:37:1.312
Creator: United States Patent And Trademark Office, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-1.312
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 1 (Rules Of Practice In Patent Cases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No amendment may be made as a matter of right in an application after the mailing of the notice of allowance. Any amendment filed pursuant to this section must be filed before or with the payment of the issue fee, and may be entered on the recommendation of the primary examiner, approved by the Director, without withdrawing the application from issue.
