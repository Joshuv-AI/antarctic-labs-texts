11 C.F.R. § 115.5 — Individuals and sole proprietors.
Citation: us:cfr:11:115.5
Creator: Federal Election Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-11/section-115.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 11 (Federal Elections), Part 115 (Federal Contractors). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Individuals or sole proprietors who are Federal contractors are prohibited from making contributions or expenditures from their business, personal, or other funds under their dominion or control. The spouse of an individual or sole proprietor who is a Federal contractor is not prohibited from making a personal contribution or expenditure in his or her name.
