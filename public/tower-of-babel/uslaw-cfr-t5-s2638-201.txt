5 C.F.R. § 2638.201 — In general.
Citation: us:cfr:5:2638.201
Creator: Office Of Government Ethics
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-2638.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 2638 (Executive Branch Ethics Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart establishes certain procedures of the executive branch ethics program. The procedures set forth in this subpart are in addition to procedures established elsewhere in this chapter and in the program advisories and other issuances of the Office of Government Ethics.
