TILBURY DOCKS, on the north shore of the Thames, in the
county of Essex, England. They lie opposite Gravesend 25 m.
below London Bridge and about the same distance from the Nore,
being thus within the port of London. They were constructed
in 1886 by the East & West India Docks Company, and were
later owned by the London & India Docks Company. The
docks are four in number, having, with tidal basin and entrance
locks, a total area of 74 acres. The depth of water in the tidal
basin is 25 ft. at low tide and 44 ft. at high tide. The length of
quayage is about 22 m., and there is extensive warehousing as
well as accommodation for passengers, as the largest passenger
steamers trading with the Port of London lie here. Railway
communication is provided by the London, Tilbury & Southend
line, and there is direct connexion for goods traffic with all the
northern lines.
