43 C.F.R. § 4.765 — How can an order upon reconsideration be challenged?
Citation: us:cfr:43:4.765
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4.765
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4 (Department Of The Interior Hearings And Appeals Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An order upon reconsideration may be appealed to the Board as provided in § 4.783 of this subpart.
(b) No person or entity may file successive petitions for reconsideration in the same case.
