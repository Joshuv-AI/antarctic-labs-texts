33 U.S.C. § 512 — Obstruction of navigation
Citation: us:usc:33:512
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section512
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 11 (Bridges Over Navigable Waters); Subchapter II (Alteration Of Bridges). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No bridge shall at any time unreasonably obstruct the free navigation of any navigable waters of the United States.
