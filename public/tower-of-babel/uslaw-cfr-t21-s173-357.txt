21 C.F.R. § 173.357 — Materials used as fixing agents in the immobilization of enzyme preparations.
Citation: us:cfr:21:173.357
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-173.357
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 173 (Secondary Direct Food Additives Permitted In Food For Human Consumption). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Fixing agents may be safely used in the immobilization of enzyme preparations in accordance with the following conditions:
(a) The materials consist of one or more of the following:
(1) Substances generally recognized as safe in food.
(2) Substances identified in this subparagraph and subject to such limitations as are provided:
(b) The fixed enzyme preparation is washed to remove residues of the fixing materials.
