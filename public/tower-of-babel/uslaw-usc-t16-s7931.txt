16 U.S.C. § 7931 — Respect for treaties and rights
Citation: us:usc:16:7931
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section7931
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 98 (Sportsmen’S Access To Federal Land); Subchapter III (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this title 1 or the amendments made by this title— 1
(1)
affects or modifies any treaty or other right of any federally recognized Indian Tribe; or
(2)
modifies any provision of Federal law relating to migratory birds or to endangered or threatened species.
