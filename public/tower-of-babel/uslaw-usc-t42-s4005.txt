42 U.S.C. § 4005 — Definitions applicable to Homeowner Flood Insurance Affordability Act of 2014
Citation: us:usc:42:4005
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section4005
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 50 (National Flood Insurance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For purposes of this title,1 the following definitions shall apply:
(1) Administrator
The term “Administrator” means the Administrator of the Federal Emergency Management Agency.
(2) National Flood Insurance Program
The term “National Flood Insurance Program” means the program established under the National Flood Insurance Act of 1968 (42 U.S.C. 4001 et seq.).
