48 C.F.R. § 832.7001-1 — 832.7001-1 Data transmission.
Citation: us:cfr:48:832.7001-1
Creator: Chapter 8—Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-832.7001-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 832 (Contract Financing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contractor shall submit electronic payment requests through—
(a) VA's Electronic Invoice Presentment and Payment System at the current website address provided in the contract; or
(b) A system that conforms to the X12 electronic data interchange (EDI) formats established by the Accredited Standards Center (ASC) chartered by the American National Standards Institute (ANSI).
