Long run control of nonhomogeneous Markov processes
Łukasz Stettner

Abstract
In the paper average reward per unit time and average risk sensitive reward functionals are considered for controlled nonhomogeneous Markov processes. Existence of solutions to suitable Bellman equations is shown. Continuity of the value functions with respect to risk parameter is also proved. Finally stability of functionals with respect to pointwise convergence of Markov controls is studied.

Introduction

Let
(X_{n})
be a discrete time inhomogeneous Markov process on
(\Omega,F,(F_{n}),\mathbb{P})
taking values in a complete separable metric space
E
endowed with Borel
\sigma
field
{\cal{E}}
and with controlled transition operator
\mathbb{P}_{n}^{a_{n}}(X_{n},\cdot)
in generic time
n\in\mathbb{N}
with compact set of control parameters
U
. Denote by
C(E)
the space of continuous bounded functions on
E
. We shall assume so called controlled Feller property i.e. for each
n\in\mathbb{N}
and
f\in C(E)
the mapping
E\times U\ni(x,a)\mapsto\int_{E}f(y)\mathbb{P}_{n}^{a}(x,dy)
is continuous. Markov process is controlled using sequence
V=(a_{0},a_{1},\ldots,a_{n}\ldots)
such that
a_{n}\in U
is an
F_{n}
adapted random variable. We denote by
\mathbb{E}_{x}^{V}
expected value corresponding to controlled Markov process with the use of the sequence
V
and starting at time
0
from
x
. We will be interested to maximize the following long run functionals: average reward per unit time

J_{x}(V)=\liminf_{n\to\infty}{1\over n}\mathbb{E}_{x}^{V}\left[\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}c_{i}(X_{i},a_{i})\right]

(1)

or long run risk sensitive functional

J_{x}^{r}(V,\gamma)=\liminf_{n\to\infty}{1\over n\gamma}\ln\left(\mathbb{E}_{x}^{V}\left[\exp\left\{\gamma\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}c_{i}(X_{i},a_{i})\right\}\right]\right).

(2)

where
c_{i}:E\times U\mapsto R
is a continuous bounded function and
\gamma\neq 0
is a risk factor. The functional (
2
) has important financial interpretation (see
[
12
]
) since it measures not only expected value but also higher moments with weights depending on the risk factor
\gamma
.
To solve control problems with functionals (
1
) and (
2
) we have to study Bellman equations which are of the form for
n\in\mathbb{N}_{0}=\mathbb{N}\cup\left\{0\right\}
.

w_{n}(x)=\sup_{a\in U}\left[c_{n}(x,a)-\lambda_{n}+\int_{E}w_{n+1}(y)\mathbb{P}_{n}^{a}(x,dy)\right]

(3)

\tilde{w}_{n}(x,\gamma)=\sup_{a\in U}\left\{c_{n}(x,a)-\lambda_{n}^{\gamma}+{1\over\gamma}\ln\left[\int_{E}e^{\gamma\tilde{w}_{n+1}(y,\gamma)}\mathbb{P}_{n}^{a}(x,dy)\right]\right\},

(4)

where we are looking for functions
w_{n}
,
\tilde{w}_{n}(\cdot,\gamma)\in C(E)
and constants
\lambda_{n}
,
\lambda_{n}^{\gamma}
respectively. As one can see solutions
w_{n}
,
w_{n}(\cdot,\gamma)
to (
3
) and (
12
) are defined up to an additive constant. Therefore we shall look for such solutions in the space
C_{sp}(E)
which is a quotient
C(E)
with respect to the following equivalence relation: for
v_{1},v_{2}\in C(E)
we have
v_{1}\equiv v_{2}
when
\|v_{1}-v_{2}\|_{sp}:=\sup_{x,x^{\prime}\in E}v_{1}(x)-v_{2}(x)-(v_{1}(x^{\prime})-v_{2}(x^{\prime}))=0
. To study solutions so called additive and multiplicative Poisson equations we shall need the space
B(E)
of bounded Borel measurable functions and its quotient space
B_{sp}(E)
(with the same equivalence relation) endowed with the above defined span norm
\|\cdot\|_{sp}
.

Main technical theorem

Denote by
\|c_{n}\|_{sp}=\sup_{x,x^{\prime}\in E}\sup_{a,a^{\prime}\in U}c(x,a)-c(x^{\prime},a^{\prime})
. We have

Theorem 2
.

Assume we are given a sequence of operators
T_{n}:C_{sp}(E)\mapsto C_{sp}(E)
such that

(i)

\|T_{n}v_{1}-T_{n}v_{2}\|_{sp}\leq\Delta_{n}\|v_{1}-v_{2}\|_{sp}
for
v_{1},v_{2}\in C_{sp}(E)
,

(ii)

\|T_{n}0\|_{sp}\leq\|c_{n}\|_{sp}
,

(iii)

\lim_{k\to\infty}\Delta_{n}\Delta_{n+1}\ldots\Delta_{n+k}\to 0
,

(iv)

R_{n}:=\|c_{n}\|_{sp}+\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}\Delta_{n}\ldots\Delta_{n+i}\|c_{n+i+1}\|_{sp}<\infty
,

Then there is a sequence
w_{n}\in C_{sp}(E)
such that

\|T_{n}w_{n+1}-w_{n}\|_{sp}=0\quad\mbox{for $n=0,1,\ldots$}.

(15)

Moreover
\|w_{n}\|_{sp}\leq R_{n}
and

\|T_{n}T_{n+1}\ldots T_{n+k-1}0-w_{n}\|_{sp}\leq\Delta_{n}\ldots\Delta_{n+k-1}\|w_{n+k}\|_{sp}.

(16)

Furthermore when
\sup_{n}\|w_{n}\|_{sp}<\infty
functions
w_{n}
are unique up to an additive constant.

Remark 1
.

Sufficient condition for
\sup_{n}\|w_{n}\|_{sp}<\infty
is that
\sup_{n}R_{n}<\infty
. When
c=\sup_{n}\|c_{n}\|_{sp}<\infty
and
\sup_{n}\Delta_{n}:=\Delta<1
then
R_{n}\leq c\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}\Delta^{i}={c\over 1-\Delta}
.

Proof.

For a fixed
n\in\mathbb{N}
consider a sequence of functions
(w_{n}^{i})
such that
w_{n}^{0}\equiv 0
,
w_{n}^{k}(x):=T_{n}w_{n+1}^{k-1}(x)
for
k=1,2,\ldots
. Then we have

w_{n}^{k}(x)=T_{n}w_{n+1}^{k-1}(x)=T_{n}T_{n+1}w_{n+2}^{k-2}(x)=\ldots=T_{n}T_{n+1}\ldots T_{n+k-1}w_{n+k}^{0}(x)

(17)

and therefore using (i)

\|w_{n}^{k}-w_{n}^{k+r}|_{sp}=\|T_{n}T_{n+1}\ldots T_{n+k-1}0-T_{n}T_{n+1}\ldots T_{n+k-1}w_{n+k}^{r}\|_{sp}\leq\Delta_{n}\Delta_{n+1}\ldots\Delta_{n+k-1}\|w_{n+k}^{r}\|_{sp}.

(18)

By (ii) and (i) we have (taking into account that
w_{n}^{0}\equiv 0
)

\|w_{n}^{k}\|_{sp}\leq\|T_{n}w_{n+1}^{k-1}-T_{n}w_{n+1}^{0}\|_{sp}+\|T_{n}w_{n+1}^{0}\|_{sp}\leq\Delta_{n}\|w_{n+1}^{k-1}\|_{sp}+\|c_{n}\|_{sp}

(19)

and iterating, using (iv) we obtain

\displaystyle\|w_{n}^{k}\|_{sp}\leq\Delta_{n}\Delta_{n+1}\|w_{n+2}^{k-2}\|_{sp}+\Delta_{n}\|c_{n+1}\|_{sp}+\|c_{n}\|_{sp}

\displaystyle\leq\ldots\leq\Delta_{n}\Delta_{n+1}\ldots\Delta_{n+k-1}\|w_{n+k}^{0}\|_{sp}+\sum_{i=0}^{k-2}\Delta_{n}\ldots\Delta_{n+i}\|c_{n+i+1}\|_{sp}+\|c_{n}\|_{sp}\leq R_{n}

(20)

Consequently using (iii) and (iv) we have that for each
n
the sequence
(w_{n}^{k})
is bounded in the span norm and satisfies Cauchy condition. Therefore for each
n
there is a function
w_{n}\in C_{sp}(E)
such that
\|w_{n}^{k}-w_{n}\|_{sp}\to 0
as
k\to\infty
.
Furthermore we have

\displaystyle\|T_{n}w_{n+1}-w_{n}\|_{sp}\leq\|T_{n}w_{n+1}-T_{n}w_{n+1}^{k-1}\|_{sp}+\|T_{n}w_{n+1}^{k-1}-w_{n}^{k}\|_{sp}+\|w_{n}^{k}-w_{n}\|_{sp}

(21)

\displaystyle\leq\Delta_{n}\|w_{n+1}-w_{n+1}^{k-1}\|_{sp}+\|w_{n}^{k}-w_{n}\|_{sp}\to 0

as
k\to\infty
, so that
\|T_{n}w_{n+1}-w_{n}\|_{sp}=0
. Since
\|w_{n}^{k}\|_{sp}\leq R_{n}
we also have that

\|w_{n}\|_{sp}\leq R_{n}
. Note that using (i) we have

\|T_{n}T_{n+1}\ldots T_{n+k-1}0-T_{n}T_{n+1}\ldots T_{n+k-1}w_{n+k}\|_{sp}\leq\Delta_{n}\ldots\Delta_{n+k-1}\|w_{n+k}\|_{sp}.

(22)

from which (
16
) follows.

Assume additionally that
\sup_{n}\|w_{n}\|_{sp}<\infty
and that there is another sequence
\tilde{w}_{n}\in C_{sp}(E)
such that
\|\tilde{w}_{n}-w_{n}\|_{sp}\neq 0
for some
n\in\mathbb{N}
and
\|T_{n}\tilde{w}_{n}-\tilde{w}_{n}\|_{sp}=0
and
\sup_{n}\|\tilde{w_{n}}\|_{sp}<\infty
. Then by (iii)

\displaystyle\|w_{n}-\tilde{w}_{n}\|_{sp}=\|T_{n}w_{n+1}-T_{n}\tilde{w}_{n+1}\|_{sp}\leq

\displaystyle\Delta_{n}\|w_{n+1}-\tilde{w}_{n+1}\|_{xsp}=\Delta_{n}\|T_{n+1}w_{n+2}-T_{n+1}\tilde{w}_{n+2}\|_{sp}

\displaystyle\leq\Delta_{n}\Delta_{n+1}\|w_{n+2}-\tilde{w}_{n+2}\|_{sp}\leq\ldots\leq\Delta_{n}\Delta_{n+1}\ldots\Delta_{n+k}\|w_{n+k+1}-\tilde{w}_{n+k+1}\|_{sp}\to 0

(23)

as
k\to\infty
and
\|\tilde{w}_{n}-w_{n}\|_{sp}=0
.
This completes the proof.
