25 U.S.C. § 4102 — Administration through Office of Native American Programs
Citation: us:usc:25:4102
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section4102
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 43 (Native American Housing Assistance And Self-Determination). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Housing and Urban Development shall carry out this chapter through the Office of Native American Programs of the Department of Housing and Urban Development.
