40 C.F.R. § 116.1 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:40:116.1
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-116.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 116 (Designation Of Hazardous Substances). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This regulation designates hazardous substances under section 311(b)(2)(A) of the Federal Water Pollution Control Act (the Act). The regulation applies to discharges of substances designated in Table 116.4.
