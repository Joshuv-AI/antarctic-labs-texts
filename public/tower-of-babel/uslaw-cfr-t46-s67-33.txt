46 C.F.R. § 67.33 — Individual.
Citation: us:cfr:46:67.33
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-67.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 67 (Documentation Of Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An individual is a citizen if native-born, naturalized, or a derivative citizen of the United States, or otherwise qualifies as a United States citizen.
