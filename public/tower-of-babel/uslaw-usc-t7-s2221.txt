7 U.S.C. § 2221 — Details of persons from or to office of Secretary
Citation: us:usc:7:2221
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2221
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 55 (Department Of Agriculture). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Details may be made from or to the office of the Secretary when necessary and the services of the person whom it is proposed to detail are not required in that office.
