27 C.F.R. § 1.29 — Individual plant or premises.
Citation: us:cfr:27:1.29
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-1.29
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 1 (Basic Permit Requirements Under The Federal Alcohol Administration Act, Nonindustrial Use Of Distilled Spirits And Wine, Bulk Sales And Bottling Of Distilled Spirits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An application for a basic permit must be filed, and permit issued, to cover each individual plant or premises where any of the businesses specified in section 103 of the Act is engaged in.
