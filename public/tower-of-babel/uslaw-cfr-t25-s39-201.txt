25 C.F.R. § 39.201 — Does ISEF reflect the actual cost of school operations?
Citation: us:cfr:25:39.201
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-39.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 39 (The Indian School Equalization Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

ISEF does not attempt to assess the actual cost of school operations either at the local school level or in the aggregate nationally. ISEF is a relative distribution of available funds at the local school level by comparison with all other Bureau-funded schools.
