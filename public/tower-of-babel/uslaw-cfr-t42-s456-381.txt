42 C.F.R. § 456.381 — Reports of evaluations and plans of care.
Citation: us:cfr:42:456.381
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-456.381
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 456 (Utilization Control). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A written report of each evaluation and plan of care must be entered in the applicant's or beneficiary's record—
(a) At the time of admission; or
(b) If the individual is already in the ICF, immediately upon completion of the evaluation or plan.
