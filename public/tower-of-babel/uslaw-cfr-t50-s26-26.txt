50 C.F.R. § 26.26 — Exception for entry for use of emergency shelter.
Citation: us:cfr:50:26.26
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-26.26
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 26 (Public Entry And Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A permit is not required for access to any national wildlife area for temporary shelter or temporary protection in the event of emergency conditions.
