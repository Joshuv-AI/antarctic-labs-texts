31 C.F.R. § 23.18 — Age distinctions contained in Treasury regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:31:23.18
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-23.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 23 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Age In Programs And Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance From The Department Of The Treasury). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any age distinctions contained in a rule or regulation issued by Treasury shall be presumed to be necessary to the achievement of a statutory objective of the program or activity to which the rule or regulation applies, notwithstanding the provisions of § 23.13.
