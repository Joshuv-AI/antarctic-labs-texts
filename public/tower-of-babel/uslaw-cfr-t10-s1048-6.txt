10 C.F.R. § 1048.6 — Posting.
Citation: us:cfr:10:1048.6
Creator: Department Of Energy (General Provisions)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1048.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1048 (Trespassing On Strategic Petroleum Reserve Facilities And Other Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Notices stating the pertinent prohibitions of §§ 1048.3 and 1048.4 and the penalties of § 1048.5 will be conspicuously posted at all entrances of each facility or parcel of real property subject to the regulations in this part, and at such intervals along the perimeters thereof as will provide reasonable assurance of notice to persons about to enter.
