10 C.F.R. § 95.59 — Inspections.
Citation: us:cfr:10:95.59
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-95.59
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 95 (Facility Security Clearance And Safeguarding Of National Security Information And Restricted Data). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission shall make inspections and reviews of the premises, activities, records and procedures of any licensee, certificate holder, or other person subject to the regulations in this part as the Commission and CSA deem necessary to effect the purposes of the Act, E.O. 13526, as amended, or any predecessor or successor order, and/or NRC rules.
