40 C.F.R. § 310.1 — What is the purpose of this part?
Citation: us:cfr:40:310.1
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-310.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 310 (Reimbursement To Local Governments For Emergency Response To Hazardous Substance Releases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part sets up procedures for EPA to reimburse local governments for certain emergency response costs. Local governments may receive up to $25,000 to help lighten financial burdens related to emergency response to hazardous substance releases. This reimbursement does NOT replace funding that local governments normally provide for emergency response.
