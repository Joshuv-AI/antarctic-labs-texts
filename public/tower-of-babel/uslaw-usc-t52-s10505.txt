52 U.S.C. § 10505 — Penalty
Citation: us:usc:52:10505
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title52-section10505
Context: United States Code, Title 52 (Voting And Elections); Chapter 105 (Supplemental Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever shall deprive or attempt to deprive any person of any right secured by section 10501, 10502, or 10503 of this title shall be fined not more than $5,000, or imprisoned not more than five years, or both.
