46 U.S.C. § 30522 — Loss by fire
Citation: us:usc:46:30522
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section30522
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 305 (Exoneration And Limitation Of Liability); Subchapter II (Exoneration And Limitation Of Liability). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The owner of a vessel is not liable for loss or damage to merchandise on the vessel caused by a fire on the vessel unless the fire resulted from the design or neglect of the owner.
