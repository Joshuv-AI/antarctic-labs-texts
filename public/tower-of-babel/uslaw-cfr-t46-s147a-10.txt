46 C.F.R. § 147A.10 — A.10 Notice to Captain of the Port.
Citation: us:cfr:46:147A.10
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-147A.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 147A (Part 147A—Interim Regulations For Shipboard Fumigation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless otherwise authorized by the Captain of the Port, at least 24 hours before fumigation the operator of the vessel shall notify the Coast Guard Captain of the Port, for the area where the vessel is to be fumigated, of the time and place of the fumigation, and the name of the vessel that is to be fumigated.
