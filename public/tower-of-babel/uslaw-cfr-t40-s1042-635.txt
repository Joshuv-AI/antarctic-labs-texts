40 C.F.R. § 1042.635 — National security exemption.
Citation: us:cfr:40:1042.635
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1042.635
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1042 (Control Of Emissions From New And In-Use Marine Compression-Ignition Engines And Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Engines qualify for a national security exemption as described in 40 CFR 1068.225. This applies to both freshly manufactured and remanufactured engines.
