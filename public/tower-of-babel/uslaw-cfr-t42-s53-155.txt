42 C.F.R. § 53.155 — Modification of loans.
Citation: us:cfr:42:53.155
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-53.155
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 53 (Grants, Loans And Loan Guarantees For Construction And Modernization Of Hospitals And Medical Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No official of the Department of Health and Human Services will approve any proposal to modify the terms of a loan guaranteed under title VI of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 291 et seq.) and this subpart which would permit the use of the guaranteed loan (or the guarantee) as collateral for an issue of tax-exempt securities.
