40 C.F.R. § 141.510 — Is my system subject to the new finished water reservoir requirements?
Citation: us:cfr:40:141.510
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-141.510
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 141 (National Primary Drinking Water Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All subpart H systems which serve fewer than 10,000 are subject to this requirement.
