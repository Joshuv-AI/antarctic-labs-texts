36 U.S.C. § 70706 — Property exempt from taxation
Citation: us:usc:36:70706
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section70706
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 707 (Frederick Douglass Memorial And Historical Association). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any property formerly occupied by the late Frederick Douglass as his homestead, commonly called Cedar Hill, in the village of Anacostia, District of Columbia, and owned by the corporation, is exempt from taxation as long as the property is used for the purposes of the corporation.
