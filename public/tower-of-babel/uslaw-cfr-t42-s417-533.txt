42 C.F.R. § 417.533 — Part B carrier responsibilities.
Citation: us:cfr:42:417.533
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-417.533
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 417 (Health Maintenance Organizations, Competitive Medical Plans, And Health Care Prepayment Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In paying for Part B services furnished to its enrollees by suppliers, the HMO or CMP must—
(a) Determine the eligibility of individuals to receive those services through the HMO or CMP;
(b) Make proper coverage decisions and appropriate payment as authorized under § 421.200 of this chapter for the services for which its Medicare enrollees are eligible; and
(c) Carry out any other procedures that CMS may require.
