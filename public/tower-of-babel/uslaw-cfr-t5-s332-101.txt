5 C.F.R. § 332.101 — General policy of competition.
Citation: us:cfr:5:332.101
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-332.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 332 (Recruitment And Selection Through Competitive Examination). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Examinations for entrance into the competitive service shall be open competitive, except that OPM may authorize noncompetitive examinations when sufficient competent persons do not compete.
(b) An examination for promotion, demotion, reassignment, transfer, or reinstatement may be a noncompetitive examination.
