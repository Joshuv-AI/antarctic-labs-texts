31 U.S.C. § 9102 — Establishing and acquiring corporations
Citation: us:usc:31:9102
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section9102
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 91 (Government Corporations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An agency may establish or acquire a corporation to act as an agency only by or under a law of the United States specifically authorizing the action.
