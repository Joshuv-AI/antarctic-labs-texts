46 C.F.R. § 401.640 — Record for decision.
Citation: us:cfr:46:401.640
Creator: Coast Guard (Great Lakes Pilotage), Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-401.640
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 401 (Great Lakes Pilotage Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The transcript of testimony and oral argument at the hearing, together with any exhibits received, will be made part of the record for decision, and the record will be available to the respondent or applicant on payment of costs thereof.
