30 C.F.R. § 755.15 — Termination.
Citation: us:cfr:30:755.15
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-755.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 755 (Tribal-Federal Intergovernmental Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An agreement may be terminated by either party upon written notice to the other specifying the date upon which the agreement will be terminated. The date of termination shall be no less than 30 days from the date of the notice.
