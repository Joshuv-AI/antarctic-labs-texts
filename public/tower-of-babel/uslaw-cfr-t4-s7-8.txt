4 C.F.R. § 7.8 — Services to employees.
Citation: us:cfr:4:7.8
Creator: Government Accountability Office
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-4/section-7.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 4 (Accounts), Part 7 (Personnel Relations And Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of chapter 79 of title 5, United States Code, and the Office of Personnel Management implementing regulations apply to Government Accountability Office employees.
