42 C.F.R. § 413.372 — AKI dialysis payment rate.
Citation: us:cfr:42:413.372
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-413.372
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 413 (Principles Of Reasonable Cost Reimbursement; Payment For End-Stage Renal Disease Services; Prospectively Determined Payment Rates For Skilled Nursing Facilities; Payment For Acute Kidney Injury Dialysis). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The amount of payment for AKI dialysis services shall be the base rate for renal dialysis services determined for such year under section 1881(b)(14), that is, the ESRD base rate as set forth in § 413.220, updated by the ESRD bundled market basket percentage increase factor minus a productivity adjustment as set forth in § 413.196(d)(1), adjusted for wages as set forth in § 413.231, and adjusted by any other amounts deemed appropriate by the Secretary under § 413.373.
