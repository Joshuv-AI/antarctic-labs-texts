7 U.S.C. § 2561 — Remedy for infringement of plant variety protection
Citation: us:usc:7:2561
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2561
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 57 (Plant Variety Protection); Subchapter III (Plant Variety Protection And Rights); Part L (Remedies For Infringement Of Plant Variety Protection, And Other Actions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An owner shall have remedy by civil action for infringement of plant variety protection under section 2541 of this title. If a variety is sold under the name of a variety shown in a certificate, there is a prima facie presumption that it is the same variety.
