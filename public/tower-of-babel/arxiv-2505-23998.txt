The Mostowski Bridge
Ali Enayat

Abstract
. In 1950, Novak and Mostowski showed that 𝖦𝖡 \mathsf{GB} (Gödel-Bernays theory of classes) is conservative over 𝖹𝖥 \mathsf{ZF} , and therefore by Gödel’s second incompleteness theorem the consistency of 𝖹𝖥 \mathsf{ZF} is unprovable in 𝖦𝖡 \mathsf{GB} . In the same year Mostowski unveiled a contrasting result: 𝖦𝖡 \mathsf{GB} provides a truth-definition for 𝖹𝖥 \mathsf{ZF} -formulae. Here we first give an expository account of Mostowski’s construction and surrounding results, and then we show that the construction bridges the domain of Tarski-style truth theories over 𝖯𝖠 \mathsf{PA} with natural extensions of 𝖠𝖢𝖠 0 \mathsf{ACA}_{0} (the arithmetical counterpart of 𝖦𝖡 \mathsf{GB} ).

3. THE MOSTOWSKI CONSTRUCTION

In this section we first present the details of the construction
of the Mostowski truth predicate for
\mathsf{ZF}^{\pm\infty}
within
\mathsf{GB}^{\pm\infty}
, and then we will explore its various
ramifications. Mostowski’s original presentation
[
Mo
]
focuses on
\mathsf{GB}
, but the construction
works equally well for
\mathsf{GB}^{-\infty}
. In what follows
\omega

is the collection of finite ordinals as viewed within
\mathsf{GB}^{\pm\infty}
; note that
\omega
is a proper class in
\mathsf{GB}^{-\infty}.

3.1. Definition.
Reasoning in
\mathsf{GB}^{\pm\infty}
, the
Mostowski cut
, denoted
\mathsf{C}_{\mathsf{Most}}
, consists
of
k\in\omega
such that there is a class
T
with the property that
T

is a
\mathsf{Depth}_{k}
-truth class for the structure
(V,\in)
.
12
12

12

In the terminology of models of arithmetic, a
cut
of a nonstandard
model
\mathcal{M}
of arithmetic is an initial segment of
\mathcal{M}

that is closed under immediate successors. This terminology can be readily
applied to
\omega
-nonstandard models of set theory.
Here
V
is the
class of all sets, and
\mathsf{Depth}_{k}
is the collection of
\mathcal{L}_{\mathsf{ZF}}
-formulae
\varphi
with
\mathsf{depth}(\varphi)\leq k
;
here
\mathsf{depth}(\varphi)
is the length of the longest path in the
parsing tree of
\varphi
(also known as the formation/syntactic tree).
Thus
\mathsf{Depth}_{0}=\varnothing
, and
\mathsf{Depth}_{1}
consists of
atomic formulae.
13
13

13

We can also define another cut
\mathsf{D}_{\mathsf{Most}}
consisting of
numbers
k
such that there is a class
T
such that
T
is a
\Sigma_{k}
-truth class for the structure
(V,\in)
. All the results of this section
can be established,
mutatis mutandis
, for
\mathsf{D}_{\mathsf{Most}}
. Clearly
\mathsf{D}_{\mathsf{Most}}
is an initial segment of
\mathsf{C}_{\mathsf{Most}}.

3.2. Lemma.

Provably in

\mathsf{GB}^{\pm\infty}
,
\mathsf{C}_{\mathsf{Most}}

is a cut of
\omega
.

Proof.
Suppose
(\mathcal{M},\mathfrak{X})\models\mathsf{GB}^{\pm\infty}.
By default
\mathsf{C}_{\mathsf{Most}}^{(\mathcal{M},\mathfrak{X})}
contains
0
since
\mathsf{Depth}_{0}=\varnothing.
We
will show that
\mathsf{C}_{\mathsf{Most}}^{(\mathcal{M},\mathfrak{X})}
contains
1
and is closed under the successor operation; the definition of

\mathsf{C}_{\mathsf{Most}}
makes it clear that it is closed
under initial segments. To see that
1\in\mathsf{C}_{\mathsf{Most}}
,
consider the formula
\sigma(x)
below:

\sigma(x):=\exists a\ \exists b\left[\left(\left(x=\ulcorner c_{a}=c_{b}\urcorner\right)\wedge(a=b)\right)\vee\left(\left(x=\ulcorner c_{a}\in c_{b}\urcorner\right)\wedge(a\in b)\right)\right].

Since
\mathfrak{X}
includes all
\mathcal{M}
-definable sets,
there is some
T_{1}\in\mathfrak{X}
such that:

(\mathcal{M},T_{1})\models\forall x(x\in T_{1}\leftrightarrow\sigma(x)).

Clearly
T_{1}
is a
\mathsf{Depth}_{1}
-truth class over
\mathcal{M}
. To see that
\mathsf{C}_{\mathsf{Most}}
is closed under
successors, let
k\in\mathsf{C}_{\mathsf{Most}}
with
k\geq 1.
Then by
definition there is some
T\in\mathfrak{X}
that is a
\mathsf{Depth}_{k}
-truth class over
\mathcal{M}
. Choose
T^{\prime}\in\mathfrak{X}
such
that:

(\mathcal{M},T,T^{\prime})\models\forall x\ \left[x\in T^{\prime}\leftrightarrow\lambda(x,T)\right],

where

\lambda(x,T):=\left[\left(\mathsf{depth}(x)\leq k\right)\wedge x\in T\right]\vee\left(\mathsf{depth}(x)=k+1\wedge[\mathsf{Neg}(x)\vee\mathsf{Exist}(x)\vee\mathsf{Disj}(x)]\right),

and

\mathsf{Neg}(x):=\exists y\left[\left(x=\lnot y\right)\wedge\lnot y\in T\right],

\mathsf{Exist}(x):=\exists y\ \left[\exists v\left(x=\exists v\ y(v)\right)\wedge\exists a\ y(c_{a})\in T\right]
, and

\mathsf{Disj}(x):=\exists y_{1}\exists y_{2}\left[\left(x=y_{1}\vee y_{2}\right)\wedge\left(y_{1}\in T\vee y_{2}\in T\right)\right].

Clearly
T^{\prime}
is a
\mathsf{Depth}_{k+1}
-truth class over

\mathcal{M}
; thus
k+1\in\mathsf{C}_{\mathsf{Most}}
, as desired.
\square
