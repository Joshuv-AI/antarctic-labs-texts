39 C.F.R. § 255.3 — Nondiscrimination under any program or activity conducted by the Postal Service.
Citation: us:cfr:39:255.3
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-255.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 255 (Access Of Persons With Disabilities To Postal Service Programs, Activities, Facilities, And Electronic And Information Technology). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In accordance with section 504 of the Rehabilitation Act, no qualified individual with a disability shall, solely by reason of his or her disability, be excluded from participation in, be denied the benefits of, or be subjected to discrimination under, any program or activity conducted by the Postal Service.
