12 C.F.R. § 650.80 — Final discharge and release of the conservator.
Citation: us:cfr:12:650.80
Creator: Farm Credit Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-650.80
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 650 (Federal Agricultural Mortgage Corporation General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

At such time as the conservator shall be relieved of its conservatorship duties, the conservator shall file a report on the conservator's activities with the Farm Credit Administration. The conservator shall thereupon be completely and finally released.
