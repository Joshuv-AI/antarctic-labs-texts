20 C.F.R. § 404.722 — Rebuttal of a presumption of death.
Citation: us:cfr:20:404.722
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-404.722
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 404 (Federal Old-Age, Survivors And Disability Insurance (1950- )). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A presumption of death made based on § 404.721(b) can be rebutted by evidence that establishes that the person is still alive or explains the individual's absence in a manner consistent with continued life rather than death.
