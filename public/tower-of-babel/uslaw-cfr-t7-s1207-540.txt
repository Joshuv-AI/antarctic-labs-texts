7 C.F.R. § 1207.540 — Confidential books, records, and reports.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1207.540
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Miscellaneous Commodities), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1207.540
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1207 (Potato Research And Promotion Plan). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All information obtained from the books, records, and reports of handler and importers and all information with respect to refunds of assessments made to individual producers and importers shall be kept confidential in the manner and to the extent provided for in § 1207.352 of the Plan.
