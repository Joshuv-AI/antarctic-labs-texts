40 C.F.R. § 442.10 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:40:442.10
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-442.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 442 (Transportation Equipment Cleaning Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to discharges resulting from the cleaning of tank trucks and intermodal tank containers which have been used to transport chemical or petroleum cargos.
