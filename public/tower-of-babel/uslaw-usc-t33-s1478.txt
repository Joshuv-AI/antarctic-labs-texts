33 U.S.C. § 1478 — Personal, flag state, and foreign state considerations
Citation: us:usc:33:1478
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section1478
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 28 (Pollution Casualties On The High Seas: United States Intervention). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In the direction and conduct of measures under this chapter the Secretary shall use his best endeavors to—
(1)
assure the avoidance of risk to human life;
(2)
render all possible aid to distressed persons, including facilitating repatriation of ships’ crews; and
(3)
not unnecessarily interfere with rights and interests of others, including the flag state of any ship involved, other foreign states threatened by damage, and persons otherwise concerned.
