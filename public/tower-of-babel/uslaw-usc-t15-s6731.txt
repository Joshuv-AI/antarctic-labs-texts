15 U.S.C. § 6731 — General application
Citation: us:usc:15:6731
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section6731
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 93 (Insurance); Subchapter II (Redomestication Of Mutual Insurers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This subchapter shall only apply to a mutual insurance company in a State which has not enacted a law which expressly establishes reasonable terms and conditions for a mutual insurance company domiciled in such State to reorganize into a mutual holding company.
