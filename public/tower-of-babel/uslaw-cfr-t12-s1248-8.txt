12 C.F.R. § 1248.8 — De minimis exception.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1248.8
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1248.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1248 (Uniform Mortgage-Backed Securities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

FHFA may exclude from the requirements of this part covered programs, policies, or practices of an Enterprise as long as those covered programs, policies, or practices do not affect more than $5 billion in unpaid principal balance of that Enterprises' TBA-eligible MBS.
