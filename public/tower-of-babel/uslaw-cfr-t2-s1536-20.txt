2 C.F.R. § 1536.20 — Does this part apply to me?
Citation: us:cfr:2:1536.20
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1536.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1536 (Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Financial Assistance)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part and, through this part, pertinent portions of the OMB guidance in Subparts A through F of 2 CFR part 182 (see table at 2 CFR 182.115(b)) apply to you if you are a—
(a) Recipient of a Environmental Protection Agency grant or cooperative agreement; or
(b) Environmental Protection Agency awarding official.
