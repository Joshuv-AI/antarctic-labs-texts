12 C.F.R. § 34.61 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:12:34.61
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-34.61
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 34 (Real Estate Lending And Appraisals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart, issued pursuant to section 304 of the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation Improvement Act of 1991, 12 U.S.C. 1828(o), prescribes standards for real estate lending to be used by national banks in adopting internal real estate lending policies.
