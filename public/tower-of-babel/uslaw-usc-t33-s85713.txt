33 U.S.C. § 857–13 — National Advisory Committee on Oceans and Atmosphere
Citation: us:usc:33:857–13
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section857–13
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 17 (National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is hereby established a committee of 18 members to be known as the National Advisory Committee on Oceans and Atmosphere (hereinafter in sections 857–13 to 857–18 of this title referred to as the “Committee”).
