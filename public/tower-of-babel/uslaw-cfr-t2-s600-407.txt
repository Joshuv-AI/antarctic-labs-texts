2 C.F.R. § 600.407 — Prior written approval (prior approval).
Citation: us:cfr:2:600.407
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-600.407
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 600 (The Uniform Administrative Requirements, Cost Principles, And Audit Requirements For Federal Awards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The non-Federal entity must seek the prior written approval for indirect or special or unusual costs prior to incurring such costs where DOS is the cognizant agency.
