7 C.F.R. § 4280.241 — Application withdrawal.
Citation: us:cfr:7:4280.241
Creator: Rural Business-Cooperative Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-4280.241
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 4280 (Loans And Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

During the period between the submission of an application and award approval, the Applicant must notify the Agency in writing if the Project is no longer viable or the Applicant no longer is requesting financial assistance for the Project. When the Applicant notifies the Agency, the application will be withdrawn from consideration for funding.
