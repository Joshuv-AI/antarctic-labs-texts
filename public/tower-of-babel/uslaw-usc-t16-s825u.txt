16 U.S.C. § 825u — Interest rate on power bonds held by Administrator of General Services
Citation: us:usc:16:825u
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section825u
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 12 (Federal Regulation And Development Of Power); Subchapter III (Licensees And Public Utilities; Procedural And Administrative Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator of General Services or his successor in interest is authorized to reduce the rate of interest to 2½ per centum on all power bonds held by such Agency issued by States, public authorities, counties, municipalities, and other subdivisions of State governments for power projects financed by the Public Works Administration.
