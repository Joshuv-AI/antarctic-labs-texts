46 U.S.C. § 51903 — Multiple awards
Citation: us:usc:46:51903
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section51903
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part B (Merchant Marine Service); Chapter 519 (Merchant Marine Awards). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An individual may not be awarded more than one of any type of decoration or medal under this chapter. For each succeeding act or service justifying the same decoration or medal, a suitable device may be awarded to be worn with the decoration or medal.
