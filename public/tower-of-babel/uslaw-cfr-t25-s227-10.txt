25 C.F.R. § 227.10 — Minerals other than oil and gas.
Citation: us:cfr:25:227.10
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-227.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 227 (Leasing Of Certain Lands In Wind River Indian Reservation, Wyoming, For Oil And Gas Mining). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unreserved, unwithdrawn, and unallotted lands which have not been leased for oil and gas under the act of August 21, 1916 (39 Stat. 519) and which are not chiefly valuable therefor, are subject to mineral application or mineral entry, for minerals other than oil and gas, under the supervision of the Bureau of Land Management.
