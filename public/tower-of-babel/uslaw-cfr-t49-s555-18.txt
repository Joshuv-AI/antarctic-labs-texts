49 C.F.R. § 555.18 — Temporary exemption labels.
Citation: us:cfr:49:555.18
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-555.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 555 (Temporary Exemption From Motor Vehicle Safety And Bumper Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An alterer or final-stage manufacturer of a vehicle that is covered by one or more exemptions issued under this sub-part shall affix a label that meets meet all the requirements of 49 CFR 555.9.
