16 U.S.C. § 837c — Contract limitations and conditions for use of electric energy and peaking capacity of plants in other marketing areas for use within Pacific Northwest
Citation: us:usc:16:837c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section837c
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 12F (Pacific Northwest Consumer Power Preference; Reciprocal Priority In Other Regions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any contract of the Secretary for the sale or exchange of electric energy generated at, or peaking capacity of, Federal hydroelectric plants in marketing areas outside the Pacific Northwest for use within the Pacific Northwest shall be subject to limitations and conditions corresponding to those provided in sections 837a and 837b of this title for any contract for the sale or exchange of hydroelectric energy or peaking capacity generated within the Pacific Northwest for use outside the Pacific Northwest.
