40 C.F.R. § 419.50 — Applicability; description of the integrated subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:419.50
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-419.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 419 (Petroleum Refining Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to all discharges resulting from any facility that produces petroleum products by the use of topping, cracking, lube oil manufacturing processes, and petrochemical operations, whether or not the facility includes any process in addition to topping, cracking, lube oil manufacturing processes, and petrochemical operations.
