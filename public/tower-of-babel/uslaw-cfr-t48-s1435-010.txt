48 C.F.R. § 1435.010 — 1435.010 Scientific and technical reports.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1435.010
Creator: Chapter 14—Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1435.010
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1435 (Research And Development Contracting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a Research and Development (R&D) contract results involve classified or national security information, the CO shall follow the agency procedures prescribed in DIAR 1404.403 prior to making the results available. Copies of publications and reports are also required to be sent to the DOI Departmental Library, 1849 C Street, NW., MS-2258, Main Interior Building, Washington, DC 20240.
