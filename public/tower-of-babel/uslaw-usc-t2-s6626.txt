2 U.S.C. § 6626 — Reimbursements to Sergeant at Arms and Doorkeeper of Senate for equipment provided to Senators, etc., which has been lost, stolen, damaged, or otherwise unaccounted for; deposit of receipts
Citation: us:usc:2:6626
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section6626
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 65 (Senate Officers And Administration); Subchapter III (Sergeant At Arms And Doorkeeper Of Senate); Part B (General Powers And Duties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Sergeant at Arms and Doorkeeper of the Senate shall deposit in the United States Treasury for credit to the appropriation account, within the contingent fund of the Senate, for the “Sergeant at Arms and Doorkeeper of the Senate”, all moneys received by him as reimbursement for equipment provided to Senators, committee chairmen, and other officers and employees of the Senate, which has been lost, stolen, damaged, or otherwise unaccounted for.
