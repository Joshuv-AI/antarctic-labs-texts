31 U.S.C. § 1309 — Social security tax
Citation: us:usc:31:1309
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section1309
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 13 (Appropriations); Subchapter I (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Amounts made available for the compensation of officers and employees of the United States Government may be used to pay taxes imposed on an agency as an employer under chapter 21 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (26 U.S.C. 3101 et seq.).
