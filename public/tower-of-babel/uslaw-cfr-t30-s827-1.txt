30 C.F.R. § 827.1 — Scope.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 827 (Permanent Program Performance Standards—Coal Preparation Plants Not Located Within The Permit Area Of A Mine). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part sets forth requirements for coal preparation plants operated in connection with a coal mine but outside the permit area for a specific mine.
