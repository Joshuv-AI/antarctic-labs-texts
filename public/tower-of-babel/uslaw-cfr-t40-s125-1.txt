40 C.F.R. § 125.1 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:40:125.1
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-125.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 125 (Criteria And Standards For The National Pollutant Discharge Elimination System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart establishes criteria and standards for the imposition of technology-based treatment requirements in permits under section 301(b) of the Act, including the application of EPA promulgated effluent limitations and case-by-case determinations of effluent limitations under section 402(a)(1) of the Act.
