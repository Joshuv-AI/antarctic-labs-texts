20 C.F.R. § 684.920 — What provisions of law or regulations may not be waived?
Citation: us:cfr:20:684.920
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-684.920
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 684 (Indian And Native American Programs Under Title I Of The Workforce Innovation And Opportunity Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Requirements relating to:
(a) Wage and labor standards;
(b) Worker rights;
(c) Participation and protection of workers and participants;
(d) Grievance procedures;
(e) Judicial review; and
(f) Non-discrimination may not be waived.
