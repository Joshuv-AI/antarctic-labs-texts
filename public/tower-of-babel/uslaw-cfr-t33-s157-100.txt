33 C.F.R. § 157.100 — Plans for U.S. tank vessels: Submission.
Citation: us:cfr:33:157.100
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-157.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 157 (Rules For The Protection Of The Marine Environment Relating To Tank Vessels Carrying Oil In Bulk). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Before each U.S. tank vessel having a COW system under § 157.10(e), § 157.10a(a)(2), or § 157.10c(b)(2) is inspected under § 157.140, the owner or operator of that vessel must submit to the Coast Guard plans that include—
(1) A drawing or diagram of the COW pumping and piping system that meets 46 CFR 56.01-10(d);
(2) The design of each COW machine;
(3) The arrangement, location, and installation of the COW machines; and
(4) Except as allowed in § 157.104, the projected direct impingement pattern of crude oil from the nozzles of the COW machines on the surfaces of each tank, showing the surface areas not reached by direct impingement.
(b) Plans under paragraph (a) of this section must be submitted to the Officer in Charge, Marine Inspection, of the zone in which the COW system is installed or to the Commanding Officer (MSC), Attn: Marine Safety Center, U.S. Coast Guard Stop 7430, 2703 Martin Luther King Jr. Avenue SE., Washington, DC 20593-7430.
