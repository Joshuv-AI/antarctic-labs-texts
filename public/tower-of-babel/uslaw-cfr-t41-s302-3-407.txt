41 C.F.R. § 302-3.407 — -3.407 Relocation allowances not covered when permanently assigned to temporary official station.
Citation: us:cfr:41:302-3.407
Creator: Chapter 302—Relocation Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-302-3.407
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 302-3 (3—Relocation Allowance By Specific Type). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the employee is permanently assigned to their temporary official station, the agency may not pay:
(a) Expenses of a househunting trip for the employee and their spouse to the temporary official station under part 302-5 of this chapter; or
(b) Residence transaction expenses for selling a residence or breaking a lease at the temporary official station under part 302-11 of this chapter.
