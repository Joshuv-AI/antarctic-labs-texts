32 C.F.R. § 245.26 — Aircraft being recovered.
Citation: us:cfr:32:245.26
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-245.26
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 245 (Plan For The Emergency Security Control Of Air Traffic (Escat)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Aircraft being recovered will be expedited to home or an alternate base. Search and Rescue aircraft may be expedited on their missions. Such aircraft may be diverted to avoid critical areas or takeoff may be delayed to prevent saturation of airspace.
