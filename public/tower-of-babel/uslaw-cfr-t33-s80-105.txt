33 C.F.R. § 80.105 — Calais, ME to Cape Small, ME.
Citation: us:cfr:33:80.105
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-80.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 80 (Colregs Demarcation Lines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The 72 COLREGS shall apply on the harbors, bays, and inlets on the east coast of Maine from International Bridge at Calais, ME to the southwesternmost extremity of Bald Head at Cape Small.
