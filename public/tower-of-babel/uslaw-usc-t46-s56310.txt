46 U.S.C. § 56310 — Voluntary purchase or charter agreements
Citation: us:usc:46:56310
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section56310
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part E (Control Of Merchant Marine Capabilities); Chapter 563 (Emergency Acquisition Of Vessels). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

During any period in which vessels may be requisitioned under this chapter as amended,1 the President is authorized through the Secretary of Transportation to acquire by voluntary agreement of purchase or charter the ownership or use of any merchant vessel not owned by citizens of the United States.
