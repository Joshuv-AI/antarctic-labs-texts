34 C.F.R. § 84.210 — To whom must I distribute my drug-free workplace statement?
Citation: us:cfr:34:84.210
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-84.210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 84 (Governmentwide Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Financial Assistance)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must require that a copy of the statement described in § 84.205 be given to each employee who will be engaged in the performance of any Federal award.
