21 U.S.C. § 613 — Plans and reassessments
Citation: us:usc:21:613
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title21-section613
Context: United States Code, Title 21 (Food And Drugs); Chapter 12 (Meat Inspection); Subchapter I (Inspection Requirements; Adulteration And Misbranding). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall require that each establishment subject to inspection under this chapter shall, at a minimum—
(1)
prepare and maintain current procedures for the recall of all meat or meat food products produced and shipped by the establishment;
(2)
document each reassessment of the process control plans of the establishment; and
(3)
upon request, make the procedures and reassessed process control plans available to inspectors appointed by the Secretary for review and copying.
