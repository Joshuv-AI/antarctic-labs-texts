15 U.S.C. § 8712 — Effect on other laws
Citation: us:usc:15:8712
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section8712
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 113 (Concrete Masonry Products Research, Education, And Promotion). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This chapter shall not affect or preempt any other Federal or State law authorizing research, education, and promotion relating to concrete masonry products.
