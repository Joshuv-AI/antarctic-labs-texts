10 U.S.C. § 10108 — Navy Reserve: administration
Citation: us:usc:10:10108
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section10108
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization And Administration); Chapter 1003 (Reserve Components Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Navy Reserve is the reserve component of the Navy. It shall be organized, administered, trained, and supplied under the direction of the Chief of Naval Operations.
(b)
The bureaus and offices of the executive part of the Department of the Navy have the same relation and responsibility to the Navy Reserve as they do to the Regular Navy.
