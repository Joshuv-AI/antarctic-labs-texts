DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY


Notice of Postponement of Meeting of the Environmental Management 
Site-Specific Advisory Board, Savannah River Site

AGENCY: U.S. Department of Energy.

ACTION: Notice of meeting postponement.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This Notice announces the postponement of a previously 
announced Environmental Management Site-Specific Advisory Board, 
Savannah River Site, meeting.

DATES: The meeting of the Environmental Management Site-Specific 
Advisory Board, Savannah River Site, scheduled for May 19, 2026, is 
postponed.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: James Tanner, Office of External 
Affairs, U.S. Department of Energy, Savannah River Operations Office, 
P.O. Box A, Aiken, SC 29802; Phone: (803) 646-2167; or Email: 
[email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The meeting Environmental Management Site-
Specific Advisory Board, Savannah River Site, at the Advanced 
Manufacturing Collaborative, 4345 Trolley Line Road, Aiken, South 
Carolina 29801 and to be streamed on YouTube is postponed. The meeting 
will be rescheduled, and a new notice will be published in the Federal 
Register. The original notice was published on April 25, 2026, FR Doc. 
2026-07296 at 91 FR 20158.
    Signing Authority: This document of the Department of Energy was

document in electronic format for publication, as an official document

    Signed in Washington, DC, on May 7, 2026.
