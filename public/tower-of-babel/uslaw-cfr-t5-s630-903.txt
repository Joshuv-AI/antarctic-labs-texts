5 C.F.R. § 630.903 — Administrative procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:5:630.903
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-630.903
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 630 (Absence And Leave). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each Federal agency shall establish and administer procedures to permit the voluntary transfer of annual leave consistent with this subpart.
