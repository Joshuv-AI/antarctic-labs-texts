25 C.F.R. § 514.12 — When does a notice of late submission and/or a proposed late fee become a final order of the Commission and final agency action?
Citation: us:cfr:25:514.12
Creator: National Indian Gaming Commission, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-514.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 514 (Fees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the recipient fails to appeal under subchapter H of this chapter, the notice and the proposed late fee shall become a final order of the Commission and final agency action.
