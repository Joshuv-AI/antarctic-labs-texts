16 C.F.R. § 1.96 — Compromise of penalty.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1.96
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1.96
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1 (General Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission may compromise any penalty or proposed penalty at any time, with leave of court when necessary, taking into account the nature and degree of violation and the impact of a penalty upon a particular respondent.
