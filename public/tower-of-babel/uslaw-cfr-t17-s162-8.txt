17 C.F.R. § 162.8 — Acceptable delivery methods of opt-out notices.
Citation: us:cfr:17:162.8
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-162.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 162 (Protection Of Consumer Information Under The Fair Credit Reporting Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) In general. The opt-out notice must be provided so that each consumer can reasonably be expected to receive actual notice.
(b) Electronic notices. For opt-out notices provided electronically, the notice may be provided in compliance with either the electronic disclosure provisions in § 1.4 of this title or the provisions in section 101 of the Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act, 15 U.S.C. 7001 et seq.
