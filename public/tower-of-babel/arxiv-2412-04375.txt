Restricted Phase Space Thermodynamics of 4D Dyonic AdS Black Holes: Insights from Kaniadakis Statistics and Emergence of Superfluid λ \lambda -Phase Transition
Abhishek Baruah, Prabwal Phukon a,

Abstract
We study the thermodynamics of 4 ​ D 4D dyonic AdS black hole in the Kaniadakis statistics framework using the Restricted Phase Space (RPST) formalism. This framework provides a non-extensive extension of classical statistical mechanics, drawing inspiration from relativistic symmetries and presenting a fresh perspective on black hole thermodynamics. Our study analyzes how including Kaniadakis entropy modifies the phase transition of the dyonic black holes. We consider the central charge C C and its conjugate chemical potential μ \mu as the thermodynamic variable along with others except the pressure and volume. Due to the addition of the magnetic charge Q ~ m \tilde{Q}_{m} , the study of the phase transition becomes much richer by obtaining a non-equilibrium phase transition from an unstable small black hole to a stable large black hole along with the Van der Waals phase transition in the T − S T-S processes. In the F − T F-T plot, we get an extra Hawking-Page phase transition. Including the deformation parameter κ \kappa introduces an unstable (ultra-large BH) branch seen in almost all the plots. Turning off the magnetic charge flips the direction of the phase transition seen during its presence. We observe a novel phenomenon that is the superfluid λ \lambda phase transition in the mixed ( Φ ~ e , Q ~ m ) (\tilde{\Phi}_{e},\tilde{Q}_{m}) which is due to the additional Q ~ m \tilde{Q}_{m} inclusion. Also, in the plots varying κ \kappa match with the plot varying C C which underlines some sort of correspondence in its meaning which is not possible to observe in Gibbs-Boltzmann statistics. As the entropy models change the homogeneity is not lost where mass is of the first order and the rest is zeroth order. Finally, the μ − C \mu-C processes in quite similar across black hole systems and entropy formulation marking some kind of universality of this process.

T

S
and how it was enhanced by the introduction of EPST and Visser’s formalism. 2
