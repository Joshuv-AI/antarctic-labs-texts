42 U.S.C. § 1588 — Sale of vacant land to local housing authorities; sale of personal property
Citation: us:usc:42:1588
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1588
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 9 (Housing Of Persons Engaged In National Defense); Subchapter VII (Disposal Of War And Veterans’ Housing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Notwithstanding any other provision of law, any land acquired under subchapters II to VII of this chapter or any other Act in connection with war or veterans’ housing, but upon which no dwellings are located at the time of sale, may be sold at fair value, as determined by the Secretary of Housing and Urban Development to any agency organized for slum clearance or to provide subsidized housing for persons of low income.
(b)
Notwithstanding any other provision of law, any personal property held under subchapters II to VII of this chapter, and not sold with a project or building, may be sold at fair value, as determined by the Secretary of Housing and Urban Development to any agency organized for slum clearance or to provide subsidized housing for persons of low income. Any sale of personal property under this subsection shall be made on a cash basis, payable at the time of settlement.
