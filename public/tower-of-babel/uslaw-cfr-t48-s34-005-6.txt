48 C.F.R. § 34.005-6 — 34.005-6 Full production.
Citation: us:cfr:48:34.005-6
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-34.005-6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 34 (Major System Acquisition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Contracts for full production of successfully tested major systems selected from the full-scale development phase may be awarded if the agency head (a) reaffirms the mission need and program objectives and (b) grants approval to proceed with production.
