41 C.F.R. § 302-8.101 — -8.101 Where HHG may be stored.
Citation: us:cfr:41:302-8.101
Creator: Chapter 302—Relocation Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-302-8.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 302-8 (8—Allowances For Extended Storage Of Household Goods (Hhg)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

HHG may be stored either in:
(a) Available Government-owned storage space; or
(b) Suitable commercial storage space obtained by the Government if:
(1) Government-owned space is not available; or
(2) Commercial storage space is more economical or suitable because of location, transportation costs, or for other reasons.
