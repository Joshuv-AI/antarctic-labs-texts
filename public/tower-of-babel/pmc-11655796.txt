Unveiling hsa_circ_0007439: a novel suppressor of nasopharyngeal carcinoma via miR-556-5p/PTEN Axis
Authors: Ming Li, Jing Yang, WanRong Feng, DongXue Fu, Yang Bai
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
AbstractObjectiveThere has been no explanation for the exact mechanism of circRNAs in nasopharyngeal carcinoma (NPC). Circ_0007439 was investigated in this study to determine its role and mechanism in NPC.MethodsTo identify circ_0007439, microRNA (miR)-556-5p, and PTEN mRNA in NPC tissues and cells, RT-qPCR was employed. PTEN protein was quantified using western blot analysis. The impact of circ_0007439 on cellular activities was assessed using CCK-8, colony formation, Transwell, and flow cytometry, in that order. The interaction of miR-556-5p with circ_0007439 or PTEN mRNA was confirmed through a dual luciferase reporter gene assay and RIP assay.Resultscirc_0007439 and PTEN levels were low in NPC tissues and cells, while miR-556-5p was highly expressed. NPC cells were inhibited in proliferation, migration, invasion, and anti-apoptosis when circ_0007439 was upregulated or miR-556-5p was reduced. The circ_0007439 molecule acted as a molecular sponge that inhibited miR-556-5p’s suppression of PTEN expression. Additionally, reduction of PTEN weakened the inhibitive effects of elevating circ_0007439 or reducing miR-556-5p on NPC cells.ConclusionCirc_0007439 mediates miR-556-5p/PTEN axis to inhibit NPC progression as an potential target for NPC therapy.Supplementary InformationThe online version contains supplementary material available at 10.1007/s12672-024-01656-z.Keywords: Circ_0007439, MiR-556-5p, PTEN, Nasopharyngeal carcinoma, Proliferation, Apoptosis

Introduction

In terms of incidence, nasopharyngeal carcinoma (NPC) is a common head and neck cancer that affects humans globally [1–3]. Radiotherapy and chemoradiotherapy are improving treatment approaches, but the outcomes are still unsatisfactory [4, 5]. Therefore, elucidating the mechanisms underpinning NPC progression will allow better therapeutic strategies to be found.

Cells and tissues contain abundant and stable circular noncoding RNAs, called circRNAs [6]. A wide range of diseases may be linked to circRNAs by serving as miRNA sponges, RNA binding proteins, and transcription and translation regulators [7]. It has previously been reported that NPC is associated with abnormal expression of circRNAs and that these circRNAs participate in its development [8–10]. According to Yang et al.., curcumin mediates abnormal circRNAs in NPC, thereby increasing its sensitivity to radiotherapy [11]. NPC cells express high levels of circCRIM1, which promotes FOXQ1 expression by competitively binding miR-422a, contributing to metastasis and chemotherapy resistance [12]. Circ-ZNF609 regulates miR-188/ELF2 to promote NPC cell growth [13]. A role of CircCTDP1 is to regulate TGF-2 via miR-320b/HOXA10, thereby promoting NPC growth [14].

High-throughput sequencing data from four normal and four NPC samples in the GSE143797 dataset were analyzed. There were 260 differentially expressed genes, with 92 genes up-regulated and 168 genes down-regulated. Among these genes, circ_0007439 has a significant downregulation in NPC tumor tissues, suggesting it may function as a tumor suppressor. Phosphatase and tensin homolog deleted on chromosome 10 (PTEN) has been confirmed to be associated with NPC [15, 16]. It has been also indicated that alterations in PTEN levels caused by the absorption of certain miRNAs might inhibit NPC progression [17]. This study revealed that circ_0000345 disrupted NPC cell progression by competing with miR-556-5p to mediate PTEN.

Materials and methods

Bioinformatics analysis

A high-throughput sequencing analysis of four normal and four NPC samples from GSE143797 dataset was performed. DESeq2 was used to identify circRNAs that are differentially expressed [18]. A differential expression of circRNA screen was performed based on |log2 (fold change)| > 1 and p < 0.05 [19]. CircRNAs showing increased and decreased expression levels were classified based on log2 (fold change) > 1 and log2 (fold change) < − 1, respectively.

Clinical samples

Cancer tissues and paracancerous tissues were collected from 30 NPC patients in Beidahuang Group General Hospital (Branch 1). Every tissue sample from NPC patients was collected through biopsy or surgical procedures and verified via histopathological analysis. Immediately upon collection, the tissue specimens were stored at −80 °C. The study was approved by the Ethics Committee of Beidahuang Group General Hospital (Branch 1) and written informed consent was obtained from all NPC patients.

Cell culture

Normal nasopharyngeal epithelial cells (NP69) and NPC cell lines (6-10B, SUNE1, HONE-1 and HNE1) purchased from Shanghai Huzhen biotechnology were cultured in RPMI-1640 medium (Gibco, USA) containing 10% fetal bovine serum (FBS, Gibco) and 1% penicillin/streptomycin (Sangon, Shanghai, China) at 37 °C and 5% CO2.

Cell transfection

circ_0007439 overexpression (oe-circ_0007439), oe-NC, short hairpin RNA (shRNA) against circ_0007439, sh-NC, miR-556-5p mimic, mimic NC, miR-556-5p inhibitor, miR-556-5p inhibitor, inhibitor NC, PTEN small interfering RNA (si-PTEN), and si-NC were obtained from RiboBio (Guangzhou, China). Every vector or oligonucleotide was introduced into SUNE1 cells using Lipofectamine 2000 (Invitrogen, USA). Following a 24-hour transfection period, the cells were gathered for additional analysis.

CCK-8

Cell viability was detected by CCK-8 assay. SUNE1 cells (5 × 103) were cultured in 96-well plates at 37 °C for 72 h. Subsequently, each well received 10 µL of CCK-8 solution (Beyotime, Shanghai, China) and was incubated at 37 °C for 4 h. Absorption at 450 nm was gauged using a Bio-Rad microplate reader (USA).

Colony formation assay

Cell proliferation was detected by colony formation assay. Cells were cultured in 6-well plates with approximately 1 × 103 cells/well. After 14 days, 4% paraformaldehyde was added to each well and incubated for 30 min, and then the cells were stained with 1% crystal violet (Corning, USA) for 10 min. The number of colonies was counted under a microscope (NIKON, Japan).

Transwell

Cell migration and invasion were detected by Transwell assay. SUNE1 cells (2 × 105), grown in a serum-free environment, were introduced into either the upper chamber (Corning) coated (for invasion tests) or the uncoated chamber (for migration tests) using Matrigel (BD Biosciences). The lower chamber was supplemented with RPMI-1640 medium, which includes 10% FBS from Thermo Fisher Scientific. Following a 24-hour incubation period, cells in the upper chamber were removed, while those in the lower chamber were stabilized with methanol for 10 min, followed by a 20-min staining with 0.1% crystal violet. The cells were tallied using a microscope (NIKON, Japan).

Flow cytometry

The cells underwent staining with Annexin V-FITC and PI using the Annexin V-FITC apoptosis detection kit (KEYGEN, Guangzhou, China). Flow cytometry (BD Biosciences) was used to measure the percentage of apoptosis.

RT-qPCR

The total RNA was extracted utilizing the Trizol reagent (Invitrogen). Subsequently, the extracted RNA underwent reverse transcription into cDNA with the PrimeScript RT kit (Takara, Dalian, China), followed by RT-qPCR using the SYBR Green PCR kit (Takara). GAPDH and U6 acted as the internal standard controls. circ_0007439, miR-556-5p, and PTEN were calculated using the 2−ΔΔCT method. Listed in Table 1 are the primer sequences.

Western blot

The lysis of NPC tissues and cells was achieved using RIPA lysis buffer (Pierce, USA), followed by the collection of the supernatant post-centrifugation. The total proteins were measured using the BCA assay kit (Beyotime). Identical quantities of proteins (30 µg) underwent separation through 10–12% SDS-PAGE (Beyotime) and were then electrotransferred onto polyvinylidene difluoride membranes (EMD, Millipore), sealed with 5% skim milk powder for one hour at 25 °C, and then detected with primary antibodies PTEN (1:1000, ab31392, Abcam) and GAPDH (1:1000, ab8245, Abcam) at 4 °C overnight, and co-incubated with horseradish peroxidase-coupled secondary antibody (1:5000, ab6721, Abcam) for 2 h. Visualization was performed using an ECL detection kit (Santa Cruz). Bands were quantified using Quantity One software (Bio-Rad).

Dual luciferase reporter gene assay

The bioinformatics websites CircInteractome (https://circinteractome.nia.nih.gov) or starBase (http://starbase.sysu.edu.cn/) revealed that miR-556-5p binds to the circ_0007439 or PTEN binding sites. After amplification, the wild-type sequence of circ_0007439 (circ_0007439-WT) or PTEN 3’UTR (PTEN-WT) was inserted into pmiR-GLO vector (Promega, USA). Mutant circ_0007439 (circ_0007439-MUT) or PTEN 3’UTR (PTEN-MUT) was inserted into the pmiR-GLO (Promega) vector. Subsequently, the reporter vector underwent co-transfection with miR-556-5p mimic or mimic NC into SUNE1 cells, utilizing Lipofectamine 2000 (Thermo Fisher Scientific). The activity of luciferase was quantified with the dual luciferase reporter gene assay kit from LMAI Bio following a 48-hour incubation period.

RIP assay

The RIP assay was performed completely according to the instructions of the RNA immunoprecipitation kit (Sigma-Aldrich), and the cell lysate was collected after adding the RIP lysis buffer and then added to magnetic beads coupled with Ago2 or IgG antibody (Sigma-Aldrich). RNA in the complex on the magnetic beads was then captured to detect circ_0007439, miR-556-5p, and PTEN.

Statistical analysis

For statistical evaluation, both SPSS 21.0 software (IBM, USA) and GraphPad Primer 6.0 (GraphPad, USA) were utilized. Data from a minimum of three separate experiments were presented as mean ± standard deviation. Comparisons between two groups of measures obeying normal distribution were made using unpaired t-tests, and comparisons between multiple groups were made using one-way ANOVA and Tukey’s post hoc tests. Statistical analysis of hsa_circ_0007439, miR-556-5p, and PTEN mRNA expression data in NPC tissues and paracancerous tissues was performed using paired t-test. The Pearson correlation coefficient was employed to examine the relationship between miR-556-5p in NPC tissues and circ_0007439 or PTEN mRNA in these tissues. p < 0.05 was considered statistically significant.

Results

Circ_0007439 expression is down-regulated in NPC

Analysis of high-throughput sequencing data from four normal and four NPC samples in the GSE143797 dataset revealed a total of 260 differentially expressed genes, of which 92 genes were up-regulated and 168 genes were down-regulated (Fig. 1A). Among them, 10 circRNAs with largest |log2 (fold change)| were, in order, hsa_circ_0081534, hsa_circ_0007439, hsa_circ_0002375, hsa_circ_0004788, hsa_circ_0066568, hsa_circ _0025967, hsa_circ_0109717, hsa_circ_0093674, hsa_circ_0104021 and hsa_circ_0135761 (Fig. 1B). Then, the expression of the above circRNAs was further examined in normal tissues and NPC tissues, and the results showed that the most obvious change in the expression of hsa_circ_0007439 was observed (Supplementary Fig. 1). In addition, the parental gene of hsa_circ_0007439 was protein phosphatase 1 catalytic subunit (PPP1CB). It has been shown that PPP1CB expression is down-regulated in gastric cancer patients and that low expression levels of PPP1CB are associated with poorer overall survival [20]. Therefore, we selected circ_0007439 for subsequent studies.

circ_0007439 expression is down-regulated in NPC tissues and cells. A, B Results of high-throughput sequencing data analysis; C RT-qPCR detection of circ_0007439 expression in NPC tissues; D RT-qPCR detection of circ_0007439 expression in NPC cells. * P < 0.05 compared with NP69 cells; # P < 0.05 compared with SUNE1 cells

In NPC tissues, RT-qPCR found that circ_0007439 was significantly down-regulated (Fig. 1C). Low circ_0007439 expression was similarly observed in NPC cell lines (6-10B, SUNE1, HONE-1 and HNE1), and circ_0007439 expression in SUNE1 cell line was significantly lower than circ_0007439 expression in other NPC cell lines (Fig. 1D), and therefore, we chose SUNE1 cells for the subsequent experiments. These findings indicate that circ_0007439 expression is downregulated in NPC tissues and cells.

Elevating circ_0007439 inhibits NPC cell progression

Then, The role of circ_0007439 in NPC cells was explored. Transfection was conducted to alter circ_0007439 in SUNE1 cells and detection of the transfection efficiency was done by RT-qPCR (Fig. 2A). The role of circ_0007439 in NPC cells was explored by CCK-8, colony formation, Transwell, and flow cytometry experiments. Elevating circ_0007439 significantly inhibited the viability of SUNE1 cells (Fig. 2B), proliferation (Fig. 2C, D), migration and invasion (Fig. 2E, F) whilst significantly enhancing apoptosis (Fig. 2G, H). And reducing circ_0007439 had the opposite effect on NPC cells as elevating circ_0007439 (Fig. 2B–H). These data suggest that upregulation of circ_0007439 exerts anticancer effects by inhibiting NPC cell proliferation, migration and invasion and promoting apoptosis.

Elevating circ_0007439 inhibits NPC cell proliferation, migration and invasion and promotes apoptosis. A RT-qPCR to detect transfection efficiency; B CCK-8 to detect cell viability after intervention circ_0007439 expression; C, D Colony formation assay to detect cell proliferation after intervention circ_0007439 expression; E, F Transwell to detect cell migration and invasion after intervention circ_0007439 expression; G, H Flow cytometry to detect cell apoptosis after intervention circ_0007439 expression. * P < 0.05 compared with oe-NC group; # P < 0.05 compared with sh-NC group

circ_0007439 sponge adsorbs and down-regulates mir-556-5p expression

In our exploration of circ_0007439’s possible miRNA, the bioinformatics site CircInteractome was utilized for analysis, revealing circ_0007439’s binding site with miR-556-5p (Fig. 3A). Findings from the dual luciferase reporter gene test showed that miR-556-5p mimic significantly reduced luciferase activity in the circ_0007439-WT group (Fig. 3B). RIP experiments also showed that circ_0007439 was significantly enriched with miR-556-5p in the Ago2 group (Fig. 3C). RT-qPCR results demonstrated that in NPC tissues, miR-556-5p expression was significantly upregulated (Fig. 3D) and this expression pattern was negatively correlated with circ_0007439 expression (Fig. 3E). miR-556-5p level was upregulated in NPC cells (Fig. 3F). In SUNE1 cells with oe-circ_0007439 transfection, miR-556-5p level significantly decreased, whereas it significantly increased in cells transfected with sh-circ_0007439 (Fig. 3G). In summary, the circ_0007439 adsorbs and down-regulates miR-556-5p expression.

A Bioinformatics website predicted the binding site of circ_0007439 to miR-556-5p; B, C Dual luciferase reporter gene assay assay and RIP assay verified the binding relationship between circ_0007439 and miR-556-5p; D RT-qPCR assay for miR-556-5p expression in NPC tissues; E Pearson correlation coefficient analysis of the correlation between circ_0007439 and miR-556-5p expression in NPC tissues; F RT-qPCR detection of miR-556-5p expression in NPC cells; G RT-qPCR detection of miR-556-5p expression after intervention of circ_0007439 expression. * P < 0.05 compared with NP69 cells

Reducing mir-556-5p inhibits NPC cell development

To further investigate the role of miR-556-5p in NPC cells, miR-556-5p oligonucleotides were transfected into SUNE1 cells, and the transfection efficiencies were detected by RT-qPCR (Fig. 4A). As a result of downregulation of miR-556-5p, the viability (Fig. 4B), proliferation (Fig. 4C, D), migration and invasion (Fig. 4E, F) were impaired, whereas apoptosis was stimulated (Fig. 4G, H). And the effect of up-regulated miR-556-5p on NPC cells was opposite to that of down-regulated miR-556-5p (Fig. 4B-H).

Reducing miR-556-5p inhibits NPC cell proliferation, migration and invasion and promotes apoptosis. A RT-qPCR to detect transfection efficiency; B CCK-8 to detect cell viability after intervening miR-556-5p expression; C, D Colony formation assay to detect cell proliferation after intervening miR-556-5p expression; E, F Transwell to detect cell migration and invasion after intervening miR-556-5p expression; G, H Flow cytometry to detect intervening apoptosis after miR-556-5p expression. * P < 0.05 compared with the inhibitor NC group; # P < 0.05 compared with the mimic NC group

Subsequently, we delved deeper into the downstream target mRNAs of miR-556-5p via the bioinformatics site starBase, discovering that miR-556-5p possesses a specific binding site with PTEN (Fig. 5A). Dual luciferase reporter gene assay experiments were performed, which showed that luciferase activity was significantly reduced in SUNE1 cells co-transfected with PTEN-WT and miR-556-5p mimic (Fig. 5B). RIP revealed a notable increase in miR-556-5p and PTEN mRNA levels after Ago2 treatment (Fig. 5C). PTEN mRNA showed a decrease in NPC tissues (Fig. 5D) and showed an inverse relationship with miR-556-5p expression (Fig. 5E). Similarly, PTEN mRNA expression was significantly downregulated in NPC cells (Fig. 5F). In SUNE1 cells treated with miR-556-5p inhibitor, there was an increase in PTEN expression, whereas it decreased in cells treated with miR-556-5p mimic (Fig. 5G).

Circ_0007439 inhibits NPC progression through modulation of the miR-556-5p/PTEN axis

Finally, we explored the role of circ_0007439/miR-556-5p/PTEN axis in NPC cells by transfecting oe-circ_0007439 + si-NC, oe-circ_0007439 + si-PTEN, miR-556-5p inhibitor + si-NC, miR-556-5p inhibitor + si-PTEN into SUNE1 cells, and successful transfection was verified by RT-qPCR and Western Blot (Fig. 6A). oe-circ_0007439 or miR-556-5p inhibitor-mediated reduction in SUNE1 cell viability was reversed by si-PTEN (Fig. 6B), as well as proliferation (Fig. 6C, D), migration and invasion (Fig. 6E, F). Meanwhile, si-PTEN weakened the oe-circ_0007439 or miR-556-5p inhibitor-mediated increase in apoptosis (Fig. 6G, H). In summary, circ_0007439 inhibits NPC progression by regulating the miR-556-5p/PTEN axis.

Discussion

Lately, a wealth of data has suggested that circRNAs are crucial in the development of NPC [13, 21, 22]. This study investigates the biological significance of circ_0007439 to NPC progression. NPC cells showed significant downregulation of circ_0007439. Furthermore, circ_0007439 inhibited cell growth, suggesting that it may be a tumor suppressor.

CircRNAs are implicated in tumorigenesis through their ability to act as sponges or by competing with miRNAs for their endogenous RNAs [23–25]. For the first time, miR-556-5p was found to be a target gene of circ_0007439. It has been proposed that miR-556-5p expression is up-regulated in lung adenocarcinoma, and elevating miR-556-5p promotes lung adenocarcinoma cell viability and migration, and inhibits apoptosis [26]. Furthermore, miR-556-5p expression appears to increase in gastric cancer cases, and miR-556-5p depletion suppresses cancer cell proliferation [27]. Furthermore, reducing miR-556-5p levels suppresses cell survival and the development of tumors, triggering cell death in cancer cells treated with cisplatin, while increasing miR-556-5p enhances the resistance of cancer cells to cisplatin [28]. The current research found increased miR-556-5p expression in NPC tissues and cells. When diminished, miR-556-5p hindered the growth of NPC cells.

This research discovered that PTEN is a target gene of miR-556-5p. Circ_0007439 acted as a competing endogenous RNA to inhibit miR-556-5p, thereby regulating its downstream target, PTEN. PTEN, a tumor suppressor gene, is involved in the progression of NPC [17, 29, 30]. We also confirmed that PTEN was anticancer in NPC, which is consistent with our previous studies. In this study, reducing PTEN could reduce the anticancer effect of elevating circ_0007439 or reducing miR-556-5p on NPC cells. Accordingly, these results suggest circ_0007439 regulates miR-556-5p/PTEN axis to promote NPC progression. Nevertheless, this research has certain constraints and drawbacks. For instance, the SUNE1 cell line represents just a single NPC cell type, making it insufficient to assess the whole spectrum of NPC. Our findings have only been validated in vitro with cellular experiments, and further validation in vivo with animals is needed.

Therefore, this study has demonstrated for the first time the biological significance of circ_0007439 in NPC. NPC cells are inhibited via miR-556-5p/PTEN axis by Circ_0007439, thereby suppressing their proliferation, migration and invasion. Thus, this study underscores a new ceRNA network involving crucial regulators of NPC, which could be a viable target for therapy.
