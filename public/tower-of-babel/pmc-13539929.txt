Retraction Note: Identification of TLN1 as a prognostic biomarker to effect cell proliferation and differentiation in acute myeloid leukemia
Authors: Di Cui, Xilong Cui, Xiaoliang Xu, Wenjing Zhang, Yu Yu, Yingxin Gao, Chuanzhong Mei, Weiwei Zheng
Published: 2026

Retraction Note: BMC Cancer 22, 1027 (2022)

https://doi.org/10.1186/s12885-022-10099-0

The Editors have retracted this article. After publication, concerns were raised about some of the images in this article. Specifically, similarities were detected between panels HL60/NC and HL60/Si2 in Fig. 7C. In addition, concerns were raised regarding the ethics approval documents provided by authors, which could not be validated by the publisher. The authors responses to these concerns were also insufficient. Therefore, the Editors have lost confidence in the content of this article, and in how the study was conducted.

Author Weiwei Zhang disagrees with this retraction. Authors Di Cui, Xilong Cui, Xiaoliang Xu, Wenjing Zhang, Yu Yu, Yingxin Gao, and Chuanzhong Mei did not respond to correspondence from the Publisher about this retraction.
