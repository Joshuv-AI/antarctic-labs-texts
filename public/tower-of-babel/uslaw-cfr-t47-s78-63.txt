47 C.F.R. § 78.63 — Antenna structure marking and lighting.
Citation: us:cfr:47:78.63
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-78.63
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 78 (Cable Television Relay Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The owner of each antenna structure is responsible for ensuring that the structure, if required, is painted and/or illuminated in accordance with part 17 of this chapter. In the event of default by the owner, each licensee shall be responsible for ensuring that the structure complies with applicable painting and lighting requirements.
