43 U.S.C. § 665 — Rights of way over reservoir sites for wagon road, railroad, or other highway
Citation: us:usc:43:665
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section665
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 15 (Appropriation Of Waters; Reservoir Sites). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In the form provided by existing law, the Secretary of the Interior may file and approve surveys and plats of any right of way for a wagon road, railroad, or other highway over and across any reservoir site when in his judgment the public interests will not be injuriously affected thereby.
