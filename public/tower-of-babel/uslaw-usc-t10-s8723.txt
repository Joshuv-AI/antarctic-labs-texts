10 U.S.C. § 8723 — Periodic re-examination of production requirements
Citation: us:usc:10:8723
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8723
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (General Administration); Chapter 869 (Naval Petroleum Reserves). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall from time to time reexamine the need for the production of petroleum from oil shale for national defense when that production is authorized under section 8722 of this title. If he finds that the authorized quantity is no longer needed, he shall reduce production to the amount currently needed for national defense.
