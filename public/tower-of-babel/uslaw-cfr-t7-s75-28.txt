7 C.F.R. § 75.28 — When a request for an appeal inspection may be withdrawn.
Citation: us:cfr:7:75.28
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-75.28
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 75 (Provisions For Inspection And Certification Of Quality Of Agricultural And Vegetable Seeds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A request for an appeal inspection may be withdrawn by the applicant at any time before the appeal inspection is performed: Provided, that, the appellant shall pay any expenses incurred in connection with the appeal as provided in § 75.46.
