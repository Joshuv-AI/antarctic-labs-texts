21 C.F.R. § 107.200 — Food and Drug Administration-required recall.
Citation: us:cfr:21:107.200
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-107.200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 107 (Infant Formula). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When the Food and Drug Administration determines that an adulterated or misbranded infant formula presents a risk to human health, a manufacturer shall immediately take all actions necessary to recall that formula, extending to and including the retail level, consistent with the requirements of this subpart.
