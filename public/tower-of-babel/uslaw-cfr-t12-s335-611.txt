12 C.F.R. § 335.611 — Initial statement of beneficial ownership of securities (Form 3).
Citation: us:cfr:12:335.611
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-335.611
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 335 (Securities Of State Nonmember Banks And State Savings Associations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This form shall be filed in lieu of SEC Form 3 pursuant to SEC rules for initial statements of beneficial ownership of securities. The FDIC is authorized to solicit the information required by this form pursuant to sections 16(a) and 23(a) of the Exchange Act (15 U.S.C. 78p and 78w) and the rules and regulations thereunder. SEC regulations referenced in this form are codified at 17 CFR part 240.
