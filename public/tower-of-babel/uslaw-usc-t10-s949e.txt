10 U.S.C. § 949e — Continuances
Citation: us:usc:10:949e
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section949e
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 47A (Military Commissions); Subchapter IV (Trial Procedure). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The military judge in a military commission under this chapter may, for reasonable cause, grant a continuance to any party for such time, and as often, as may appear to be just.
