48 C.F.R. § 49.402-8 — 49.402-8 Reporting information.
Citation: us:cfr:48:49.402-8
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-49.402-8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 49 (Termination Of Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer, in accordance with agency procedures, shall ensure that information relating to the termination for default notice and a subsequent withdrawal or a conversion to a termination for convenience is reported in accordance with 42.1503(h).
