16 U.S.C. § 1676 — Issuance of rules and regulations for implementation of provisions and coordination with agricultural, research, extension, and teaching provisions
Citation: us:usc:16:1676
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section1676
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 36 (Forest And Rangeland Renewable Resources Planning); Subchapter III (Extension Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized to issue such rules and regulations as the Secretary deems necessary to implement the provisions of this subchapter and to coordinate this subchapter with title XIV of the Food and Agriculture Act of 1977 [7 U.S.C. 3101 et seq.].
