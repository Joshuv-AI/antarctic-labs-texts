42 C.F.R. § 456.500 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:42:456.500
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-456.500
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 456 (Utilization Control). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For hospitals and mental hospitals, this subpart—
(a) Prescribes conditions for the availability of FFP relating to UR plans;
(b) Prescribes conditions for granting a waiver of UR plan requirements; and
(c) Prescribes conditions for granting a variance in UR plan requirements for remote facilities.
