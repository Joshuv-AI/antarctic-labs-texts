10 C.F.R. § 51.94 — Requirement to consider final environmental impact statement.
Citation: us:cfr:10:51.94
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-51.94
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 51 (Environmental Protection Regulations For Domestic Licensing And Related Regulatory Functions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The final environmental impact statement, together with any comments and any supplement, will accompany the application or petition for rulemaking through, and be considered in, the Commission's decisionmaking process. The final environmental impact statement, together with any comments and any supplement, will be made a part of the record of the appropriate adjudicatory or rulemaking proceeding.
