Multilevel Picard approximations overcome the curse of dimensionality when approximating semilinear heat equations with gradient-dependent nonlinearities in L p L^{p} -sense
Tuan Anh Nguyen

Abstract
. We prove that multilevel Picard approximations are capable of approximating solutions of semilinear heat equations in L p L^{p} -sense, p ∈ [ 2 , ∞ ) {p}\in[2,\infty) , in the case of gradient-dependent, Lipschitz-continuous nonlinearities, in the sense that the computational effort of the multilevel Picard approximations grow at most polynomially in both the dimension d d and the reciprocal 1 / ϵ 1/\epsilon of the prescribed accuracy ϵ \epsilon .

Introduction

Partial differential equations (PDEs) are important tools to analyze many real world phenomena, e.g., in financial engineering, economics, quantum mechanics, or statistical physics to name but a few.
In most of the cases such high-dimensional nonlinear PDEs cannot be solved explicitly. It is one of
the most challenging problems in applied mathematics to approximately solve high-dimensional nonlinear PDEs.
In particular, it is very difficult to find approximation schemata for nonlinear PDEs for which one can
rigorously prove that they do overcome the so-called
curse of dimensionality
