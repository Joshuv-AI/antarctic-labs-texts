19 C.F.R. § 208.4 — Who may file a petition, request, or resolution.
Citation: us:cfr:19:208.4
Creator: United States International Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-208.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 208 (Investigations Of United States-Mexico Cross-Border Long-Haul Trucking Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An investigation under this subpart may be commenced on the basis of a petition properly filed by an interested party described in § 208.2(i) of this part which is representative of a United States long-haul trucking services industry; at the request of the President or the Trade Representative; or upon the resolution of the Committee on Ways and Means of the House of Representatives or the Committee on Finance of the Senate.
