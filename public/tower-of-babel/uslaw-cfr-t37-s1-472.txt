37 C.F.R. § 1.472 — Changes in person, name, or address of applicants and inventors.
Citation: us:cfr:37:1.472
Creator: United States Patent And Trademark Office, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-1.472
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 1 (Rules Of Practice In Patent Cases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All requests for a change in person, name or address of applicants and inventor be sent to the United States Receiving Office until the time of issuance of the international search report. Thereafter requests for such changes should be submitted to the International Bureau.
