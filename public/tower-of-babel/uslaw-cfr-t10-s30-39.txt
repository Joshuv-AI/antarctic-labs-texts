10 C.F.R. § 30.39 — Commission action on applications to renew or amend.
Citation: us:cfr:10:30.39
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-30.39
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 30 (Rules Of General Applicability To Domestic Licensing Of Byproduct Material). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In considering an application to renew or amend a license or to amend a sealed source or device registration certificate, the Commission will apply the applicable criteria set forth in § 30.33 and parts 32 through 36 and 39 of this chapter.
