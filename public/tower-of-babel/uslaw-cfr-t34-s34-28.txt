34 C.F.R. § 34.28 — Refunds of amounts collected in error.
Citation: us:cfr:34:34.28
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-34.28
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 34 (Administrative Wage Garnishment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If a hearing official determines under §§ 34.16 and 34.17 that a person does not owe the debt described in our notice or that an administrative wage garnishment under this part was barred by law at the time of the collection action, we promptly refund any amount collected by means of this garnishment.
(b) Unless required by Federal law or contract, we do not pay interest on a refund.
