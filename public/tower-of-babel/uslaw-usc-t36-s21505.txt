36 U.S.C. § 21505 — Charges for medical services
Citation: us:usc:36:21505
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section21505
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 215 (American Hospital Of Paris). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation may charge a reasonable compensation for providing medical and surgical services or may provide those services without charge. Amounts received under this section shall be used to carry out the purposes of the corporation.
