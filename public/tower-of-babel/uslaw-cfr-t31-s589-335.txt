31 C.F.R. § 589.335 — Special Russian crude oil project.
Citation: us:cfr:31:589.335
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-589.335
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 589 (Ukraine-/Russia-Related Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term special Russian crude oil project means a project intended to extract crude oil from:
(a) The exclusive economic zone of the Russian Federation in waters more than 500 feet deep;
(b) Russian artic offshore locations; or
(c) Shale formation located in the Russian Federation.
