20 U.S.C. § 9708 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:20:9708
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section9708
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 77 (Financial Literacy And Education Improvement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated to the Commission such sums as may be necessary to carry out this chapter, including administrative expenses of the Commission.
