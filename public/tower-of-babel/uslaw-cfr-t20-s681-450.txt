20 C.F.R. § 681.450 — For how long must a local Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act youth program serve a participant?
Citation: us:cfr:20:681.450
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-681.450
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 681 (Youth Activities Under Title I Of The Workforce Innovation And Opportunity Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Local youth programs must provide service to a participant for the amount of time necessary to ensure successful preparation to enter postsecondary education and/or unsubsidized employment. While there is no minimum or maximum time a youth can participate in the WIOA youth program, programs must link participation to the individual service strategy and not the timing of youth service provider contracts or program years.
