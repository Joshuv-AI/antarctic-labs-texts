21 C.F.R. § 882.1620 — Intracranial pressure monitoring device.

Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-882.1620
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 882 (Neurological Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. An intracranial pressure monitoring device is a device used for short-term monitoring and recording of intracranial pressures and pressure trends. The device includes the transducer, monitor, and interconnecting hardware.
(b) Classification. Class II (performance standards).
