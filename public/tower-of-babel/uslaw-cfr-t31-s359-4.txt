31 C.F.R. § 359.4 — In what form are Series I savings bonds issued?
Citation: us:cfr:31:359.4
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-359.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 359 (Offering Of United States Savings Bonds, Series I). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Series I savings bonds are issued in book-entry form. Effective January 1, 2012, Treasury discontinued the issuance of definitive Series I savings bonds.
