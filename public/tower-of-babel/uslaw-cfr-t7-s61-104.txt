7 C.F.R. § 61.104 — Sampling and certification of samples and grades.
Citation: us:cfr:7:61.104
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-61.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 61 (Cottonseed Sold Or Offered For Sale For Crushing Purposes (Inspection, Sampling And Certification)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The drawing, preparation, and certification of samples of cottonseed, and certification of grades of cottonseed shall be performed in accordance with methods approved from time to time for the purposes by the Director, or his representatives.
