7 C.F.R. § 4279.15 — Exception authority.
Citation: us:cfr:7:4279.15
Creator: Rural Business-Cooperative Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-4279.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 4279 (Guaranteed Loanmaking). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator may, on a case-by-case basis, grant an exception to any requirement or provision of this subpart provided that such an exception is in the best financial interests of the Federal government. Exercise of this authority cannot be in conflict with applicable law.
