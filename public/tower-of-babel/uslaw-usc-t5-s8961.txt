5 U.S.C. § 8961 — Jurisdiction of courts
Citation: us:usc:5:8961
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section8961
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart G (Insurance And Annuities); Chapter 89A (Enhanced Dental Benefits). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The district courts of the United States have original jurisdiction, concurrent with the United States Court of Federal Claims, of a civil action or claim against the United States under this chapter after such administrative remedies as required under section 8953(d) have been exhausted, but only to the extent judicial review is not precluded by any dispute resolution or other remedy under this chapter.
