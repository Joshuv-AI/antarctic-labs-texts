25 C.F.R. § 700.67 — Habitation.
Citation: us:cfr:25:700.67
Creator: The Office Of Navajo And Hopi Indian Relocation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-700.67
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 700 (Commission Operations And Relocation Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term habitation means the dwelling(s) of each household required to relocate under the term of the Act.
