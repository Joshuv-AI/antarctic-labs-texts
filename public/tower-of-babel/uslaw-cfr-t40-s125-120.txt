40 C.F.R. § 125.120 — Scope and purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:40:125.120
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-125.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 125 (Criteria And Standards For The National Pollutant Discharge Elimination System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart establishes guidelines for issuance of National Pollutant Discharge Elimination System (NPDES) permits for the discharge of pollutants from a point source into the territorial seas, the contiguous zone, and the oceans.
