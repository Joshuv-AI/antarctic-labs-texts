33 U.S.C. § 2607 — Fees
Citation: us:usc:33:2607
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section2607
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 39 (Shore Protection From Municipal Or Commercial Waste); Subchapter I (Shore Protection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Transportation may collect a fee under section 9701 of title 31 of not more than $1,000, from each person to whom a permit is issued under this subchapter for a permitting system and to maintain information.
