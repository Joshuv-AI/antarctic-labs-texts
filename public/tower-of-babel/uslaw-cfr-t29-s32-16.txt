29 C.F.R. § 32.16 — Listing of employment openings.
Citation: us:cfr:29:32.16
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-32.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 32 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Handicap In Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Recipients should request State employment security agencies to refer qualified handicapped individuals for consideration for employment.
