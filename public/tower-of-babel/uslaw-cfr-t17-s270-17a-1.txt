17 C.F.R. § 270.17a-1 — a-1 Exemption of certain underwriting transactions exempted by § 270.10f-1.
Citation: us:cfr:17:270.17a-1
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-270.17a-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 270 (Rules And Regulations, Investment Company Act Of 1940). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any transaction exempted pursuant to § 270.10f-1 shall be exempt from the provisions of section 17(a)(1) of the Act (54 Stat. 815; 15 U.S.C. 80a-17).
