33 C.F.R. § 110.81 — Muskegon Lake, Mich.
Citation: us:cfr:33:110.81
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-110.81
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 110 (Anchorage Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Muskegon Lake West. The waters of the southwest side of Muskegon Lake enclosed by a line beginning at latitude 43°13′24″ N., longitude 86°19′18.5″ W.; thence 145°T to latitude 43°13′07.5″ N., longitude 86°19′02.5″ W.; thence 230°T to latitude 43°13′04″ N., longitude 86°19′08.5″ W.; thence along the shoreline to the point of origin.
(b) Muskegon Lake East. The waters of the southeast side of Muskegon Lake enclosed by a line beginning at latitude 43°14′04″ N., longitude 86°15′47″ W.; thence 277°T to latitude 43°14′06.5″ N., longitude 86°16′27″ W.; thence 205°T to the shore; thence along the shoreline to the point of origin.
