Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 74 (Foreign Affairs Agencies Consolidation); Subchapter II (United States Arms Control And Disarmament Agency); Part B (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as otherwise provided in section 1223 or 1225, any reference in any statute, reorganization plan, Executive order, regulation, agreement, determination, or other official document or proceeding to—
(1)
the Director of the United States Arms Control and Disarmament Agency, the Director of the Arms Control and Disarmament Agency, or any other officer or employee of the United States Arms Control and Disarmament Agency or the Arms Control and Disarmament Agency shall be deemed to refer to the Secretary of State; or
(2)
the United States Arms Control and Disarmament Agency or the Arms Control and Disarmament Agency shall be deemed to refer to the Department of State.
