25 C.F.R. § 75.15 — Current membership roll.
Citation: us:cfr:25:75.15
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-75.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 75 (Revision Of The Membership Roll Of The Eastern Band Of Cherokee Indians, North Carolina). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The membership roll of the Eastern Band of Cherokee Indians shall be kept current by striking therefrom the names of persons who have relinquished their membership in the Band as provided in § 75.17 and of deceased persons upon receipt of a death certificate or other evidence of death acceptable to the Tribal Enrollment Office, and by adding thereto the names of individuals who meet the qualifications and are accepted for membership in the Band as set forth in this part.
