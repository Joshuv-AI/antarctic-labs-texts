34 C.F.R. § 81.4 — Membership and assignment to cases.
Citation: us:cfr:34:81.4
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-81.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 81 (General Education Provisions Act—Enforcement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Secretary appoints Administrative Law Judges as members of the OALJ.
(b) The Secretary appoints one of the members of the OALJ to be the chief judge. The chief judge is responsible for the efficient and effective administration of the OALJ.
(c) The chief judge assigns an ALJ to each case or class of cases within the jurisdiction of the OALJ.
