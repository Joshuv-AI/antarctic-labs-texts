2 U.S.C. § 1109 — Authorization for appropriations
Citation: us:usc:2:1109
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section1109
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 22 (John C. Stennis Center For Public Service Training And Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary to carry out this chapter.
