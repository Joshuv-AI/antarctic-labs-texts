42 C.F.R. § 93.235 — Research misconduct proceeding.
Citation: us:cfr:42:93.235
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-93.235
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 93 (Public Health Service Policies On Research Misconduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Research misconduct proceeding means any actions related to alleged research misconduct taken under this part, including allegation assessments, inquiries, investigations, ORI oversight reviews, and appeals under subpart E of this part.
