22 U.S.C. § 268b — Advances from appropriation “Boundary line, Alaska and Canada, and the United States and Canada”
Citation: us:usc:22:268b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section268b
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 7 (International Bureaus, Congresses, Etc.). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Advances of money under the appropriation “Boundary line, Alaska and Canada, and the United States and Canada”, may be made to the commissioner on the part of the United States and by his authority to chiefs of parties prior to March 2, 1921.
