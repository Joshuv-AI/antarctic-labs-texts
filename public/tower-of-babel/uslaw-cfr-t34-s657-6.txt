34 C.F.R. § 657.6 — What regulations apply to this program?
Citation: us:cfr:34:657.6
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 657 (Foreign Language And Area Studies Fellowships Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following regulations apply to this program:
(a) The regulations in 34 CFR part 655.
