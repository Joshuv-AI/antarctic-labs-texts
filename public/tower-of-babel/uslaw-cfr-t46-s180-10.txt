46 C.F.R. § 180.10 — Applicability to vessels on an international voyage.
Citation: us:cfr:46:180.10
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-180.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 180 (Lifesaving Equipment And Arrangements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A vessel on an international voyage subject to the International Convention for the Safety of Life at Sea, 1974, (SOLAS) must meet the requirements in subchapter W of this chapter for passenger vessels in the same service, instead of the requirements of this part.
