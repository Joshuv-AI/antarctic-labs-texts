5 C.F.R. § 1639.12 — Deposit of funds collected.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1639.12
Creator: Federal Retirement Thrift Investment Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1639.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1639 (Claims Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All funds owed to the Board and collected under this part will be deposited in the Thrift Savings Fund. Funds owed to other agencies and collected under this part will be credited to the account designated by the creditor agency for the receipt of the funds.
