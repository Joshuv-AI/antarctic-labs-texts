40 C.F.R. § 146.63 — Area of review.
Citation: us:cfr:40:146.63
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-146.63
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 146 (Underground Injection Control Program: Criteria And Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For the purposes of Class I hazardous waste wells, this section shall apply to the exclusion of § 146.6. The area of review for Class I hazardous waste injection wells shall be a 2-mile radius around the well bore. The Director may specify a larger area of review based on the calculated cone of influence of the well.
