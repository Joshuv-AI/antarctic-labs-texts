Abstract
We attempt to apply the Regge trajectory approach to the doubly heavy triquarks ( ( Q ​ q ) ​ Q ¯ ′ ) ((Qq)\bar{Q}^{\prime}) ( Q , Q ′ = b , c , q = u , d , s ) (Q,\,Q^{\prime}=b,\,c;q=u,\,d,\,s) . We propose the Regge trajectory relations for the doubly heavy triquarks, and then employ them to crudely estimate the spectra of the triquarks ( ( c ​ u ) ​ c ¯ ) ((cu)\bar{c}) , ( ( c ​ u ) ​ b ¯ ) ((cu)\bar{b}) , ( ( c ​ s ) ​ c ¯ ) ((cs)\bar{c}) , ( ( c ​ s ) ​ b ¯ ) ((cs)\bar{b}) , ( ( b ​ u ) ​ c ¯ ) ((bu)\bar{c}) , ( ( b ​ u ) ​ b ¯ ) ((bu)\bar{b}) , ( ( b ​ s ) ​ c ¯ ) ((bs)\bar{c}) , and ( ( b ​ s ) ​ b ¯ ) ((bs)\bar{b}) . The λ \lambda -trajectories and the ρ \rho -trajectories are investigated. The triquark Regge trajectory becomes a new and very simple approach for estimating the spectra of triquarks. It also provides a simple method to investigate the ρ \rho -mode and σ \sigma -mode excitations of pentaquarks and hexaquarks in the triquark picutre. Moreover, the spin-averaged masses of the ground states of pentaquarks ( c ¯ ​ ( c ​ u ) ) ​ ( c ​ u ) (\bar{c}(cu))(cu) , ( b ¯ ​ ( b ​ u ) ) ​ ( b ​ u ) (\bar{b}(bu))(bu) and ( c ¯ ​ ( c ​ u ) ) ​ ( b ​ u ) (\bar{c}(cu))(bu) are estimated, which are consistent with other theoretical predictions.

take simple forms. However, for the
\rho
-trajectories, it is noteworthy that the main parts of the complete forms and the fitted formulas share the same functional form,
M=m_{R}+\beta\sqrt{x+c_{0}}
. However, the values of the parameters
m_{R}
and
\beta

For example, for the main part of the complete form of the radial
\rho
-trajectory for the states
|n^{3}s_{1},1S\rangle
of triquark
((cu)\bar{c})
,
m_{R}=3.13
and
\beta=0.751988
(see Eqs. (

) and (
34
)); these values are readily computed using Eqs. (
II.4
), (
II.4
), and (
29
), or Eqs. (
II.4
), (

), and (

). By contrast, for the fitted
\rho
-trajectory,
m_{R}=3.3257
and
\beta=0.741064
(see Eq. (

)). As discussed above and in Sec.
III.2
, the
\rho
-trajectories for doubly heavy triquarks cannot be obtained by merely mimicking the meson Regge trajectories; instead, they should be constructed based on the actual structure and substructure of the triquark.
Otherwise, the
\rho
-trajectories must rely solely on fitting existing theoretical or future experimental data.

IV
Conclusions

In this work, we attempt to apply the Regge trajectory approach to the doubly heavy triquarks
((Qq)\bar{Q}^{\prime})

(Q,\,Q^{\prime}=b,\,c;\,q=u,d,s)
. We propose the Regge trajectory relations for the doubly heavy triquarks, and then employ them to crudely estimate the spectra of the doubly heavy triquarks
((cu)\bar{c})
,
((cu)\bar{b})
,
((cs)\bar{c})
,
((cs)\bar{b})
,
((bu)\bar{c})
,
((bu)\bar{b})
,
((bs)\bar{c})
, and
((bs)\bar{b})
.

The
\lambda
- and
\rho
-trajectories for the doubly heavy triquarks are investigated. For the doubly heavy triquarks
((Qq)\bar{Q}^{\prime})
, the
\lambda
-trajectories follow the behavior
M{\sim}x_{\lambda}^{2/3}
(
x_{\lambda}=L,\,N_{r}
), while the
\rho
-trajectories follow the behavior
M{\sim}\sqrt{x_{\rho}}
(
x_{\rho}=l,\,n_{r}
).
The triquark Regge trajectory is a new and very simple approach for estimating the spectra of triquarks. It also provides a simple method to investigate easily the
\rho
-mode and
\sigma
-mode excitations of pentaquarks and hexaquarks in the triquark picture.

Triquarks are colored states and are not observable. In the diquark-triquark model, the colorless pentaquarks are composed of one triquark and one diquark, and they can be observed. We crudely estimate the spin-averaged masses of the ground states of the pentaquarks
(\bar{c}(cu))(cu)
,
(\bar{b}(bu))(bu)
, and
(\bar{c}(cu))(bu)
by employing the obtained triquark Regge trajectories. The estimated results are consistent with other theoretical predictions.
