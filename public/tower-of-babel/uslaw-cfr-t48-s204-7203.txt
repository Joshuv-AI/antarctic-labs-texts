48 C.F.R. § 204.7203 — 204.7203 Contract clause.
Citation: us:cfr:48:204.7203
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-204.7203
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 204 (Administrative And Information Matters). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Include the clause at 252.204-7004, DoD Antiterrorism Awareness Training for Contractors, in solicitations and contracts, including solicitations and contracts using FAR part 12 procedures for the acquisition of commercial products and commercial services, when contractor personnel require routine physical access to a Federally-controlled facility or military installation.
