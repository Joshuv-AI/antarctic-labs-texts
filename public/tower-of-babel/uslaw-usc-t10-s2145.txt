10 U.S.C. § 2145 — Adjustments of amount of educational assistance and of subsistence allowance
Citation: us:usc:10:2145
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section2145
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Training And Education); Chapter 106A (Educational Assistance For Persons Enlisting For Active Duty). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Once each year, the Secretary of Defense shall adjust the amount of educational assistance which may be provided to any person in any standard academic year under section 2143 of this title, and the amount of the subsistence allowance authorized under section 2144 of this title for pursuit of a course of instruction on a full-time basis, in a manner consistent with the change over the preceding twelve-month period in the average actual cost of attendance at public institutions of higher education.
(b)
In this section, the term “actual cost of attendance” has the meaning given the term “cost of attendance” by section 472 of the Higher Education Act of 1965 (20 U.S.C. 1087ll).
