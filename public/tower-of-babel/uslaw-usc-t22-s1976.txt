22 U.S.C. § 1976 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:22:1976
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section1976
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 25 (Protection Of Vessels On The High Seas And In Territorial Waters Of Foreign Countries). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated such amounts as may be necessary to carry out the provisions of this chapter.
