38 U.S.C. § 3483 — Approval of courses
Citation: us:usc:38:3483
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section3483
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part III (Readjustment And Related Benefits); Chapter 34 (Veterans’ Educational Assistance); Subchapter IV (Payments To Eligible Veterans; Veteran-Student Services). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An eligible veteran shall receive the benefits of this chapter while enrolled in a course of education offered by an educational institution only if such course is approved in accordance with the provisions of subchapter I 1 of chapter 36 of this title.
