16 U.S.C. § 8213 — Prohibition against implementation of regulatory authority by Federal agencies through partnerships
Citation: us:usc:16:8213
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section8213
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 101 (National Fish Habitat Conservation Through Partnerships). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any Partnership designated under this chapter—
(1)
shall be for the sole purpose of promoting fish conservation; and
(2)
shall not be used to implement any regulatory authority of any Federal agency.
