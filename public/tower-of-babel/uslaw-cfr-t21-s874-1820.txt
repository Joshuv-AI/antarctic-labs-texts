21 C.F.R. § 874.1820 — Surgical nerve stimulator/locator.
Citation: us:cfr:21:874.1820
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-874.1820
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 874 (Ear, Nose, And Throat Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. A surgical nerve stimulator/locator is a device that is intended to provide electrical stimulation to the body to locate and identify nerves and to test their excitability.
(b) Classification. Class II.
