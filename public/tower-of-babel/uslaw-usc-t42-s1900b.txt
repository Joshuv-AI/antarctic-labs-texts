42 U.S.C. § 1900b — Amendment, modification, or repeal of authorizations for execution of contracts for research
Citation: us:usc:42:1900b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1900b
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 16B (Contracts For Scientific And Technological Research). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing contained in this chapter is intended to amend, modify, or repeal any provisions of law administered by the Secretary of the Interior which authorize the making of contracts for research.
