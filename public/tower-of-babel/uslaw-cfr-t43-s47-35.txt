43 C.F.R. § 47.35 — Must lands exchanged be of equal value?
Citation: us:cfr:43:47.35
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-47.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 47 (Land Exchange Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Hawaiian home lands to be exchanged must be of equal or lesser value than the lands to be received in the exchange, as determined by the appraisal. Once the market value is established by an approved appraisal, an administrative determination as to the equity of the exchange can be made based on the market value reflected in the approved appraisal.
