Abstract
Results are presented on χ b ​ 1 , 2 ​ ( 2 ​ P ) → ω ​ Υ ​ ( 1 ​ S ) \chi_{b1,2}(2P)\mbox{$\rightarrow$}\omega\mathchar 28935\relax(1S) transitions from e + ​ e − → Υ ⁡ ( 3 ​ S ) → γ ​ χ b ​ 1 , 2 ​ ( 2 ​ P ) e^{+}e^{-}\mbox{$\rightarrow$}\mathchar 28935\relax(3S)\mbox{$\rightarrow$}\gamma\chi_{b1,2}(2P) decays. The data were collected with the B A B AR detector at the PEP-II asymmetric-energy e + ​ e − e^{+}e^{-} collider at SLAC. The integrated luminosity of the data sample is 28.0 fb -1 , corresponding to 121.3 × 10 6 121.3\times 10^{6} Υ ⁡ ( 3 ​ S ) \mathchar 28935\relax(3S) decays. Signals of χ b ​ 1 , 2 ​ ( 2 ​ P ) \chi_{b1,2}(2P) are observed over a negligible background. Improved precision measurements of branching fractions are obtained. First measurements of the χ b ​ 1 , 2 ​ ( 2 ​ P ) \chi_{b1,2}(2P) angular distributions are performed. No evidence is found for the presence of a χ b ​ 0 ​ ( 2 ​ P ) → ω ​ Υ ​ ( 1 ​ S ) \chi_{b0}(2P)\mbox{$\rightarrow$}\omega\mathchar 28935\relax{(1S)} decay mode.

I
Introduction

The strongly bound
b\bar{b}
meson system, bottomonium, exhibits a rich positronium-like structure that is a laboratory for verifying perturbative and nonperturbative
QCD calculations
Besson:1993mm
;
Eichten:2007qx
;
Brambilla:2010cs
. Potential models and lattice calculations provide good descriptions of the mass structure and radiative transitions below the open-flavor threshold.
Precision spectroscopy probes spin-dependent and relativistic effects. Quark-antiquark potential formulations
have been successful at describing the bottomonium system phenomenologically
Besson:1993mm
;
Eichten:2007qx
;
Brambilla:2010cs
.

In 2004, the CLEO Collaboration
CLEO:2003vio
reported the first observation of the transitions
\chi_{bJ}(2P)\mbox{$\rightarrow$}\omega\mathchar 28935\relax(1S)
, produced in radiative decays of
(5.81\pm 0.12)\times 10^{6}~\mathchar 28935\relax(3S)
mesons. The
\chi_{b}(2P)
branching fractions of the
J=1
and
J=2
states were measured to be
(1.63^{+0.35+0.16}_{-0.31-0.15})\%
and
(1.10^{+0.32+0.11}_{-0.28-0.10})\%,
respectively.
The implications of these results are discussed in Ref.
Voloshin:2003zz
.

The observation of the near-threshold transition of the
c\bar{c}
state
\chi_{c1}\mbox{$\rightarrow$}\omega{J\mskip-3.0mu/\mskip-2.0mu\psi\mskip 2.0mu}
by the Belle Collaboration
Belle:2004lle
is of particular interest. A similar decay mode could exist for the
b\bar{b}
state
\chi_{b0}(2P)\mbox{$\rightarrow$}\omega\mathchar 28935\relax{(1S)}

given the predicted width of about 2.6
{\mathrm{MeV}}
for this resonance
Godfrey:2015dia
.
Given the low significance of the reconstructed signal, no search was performed by CLEO for a
\chi_{b0}(2P)
contribution, expected to be strongly suppressed by the
\omega\mathchar 28935\relax{(1S)}
mass threshold.
More recently,
\chi_{bJ}(2P)\mbox{$\rightarrow$}\omega\mathchar 28935\relax{(1S)}
decay modes have been confirmed by Belle
Belle:2024azd
with additional evidence for a
\chi_{b0}(2P)
decay to this final state.
Precise measurements of branching fractions for radiative decays between bottomonium states have been reported, recently, by the
B
A
B
AR
Collaboration
BaBar:2014och
.

IV.1
Measurement of the
\chi_{b1,2}(2P)
fractions

To measure the
\chi_{b1}(2P)
and
\chi_{b2}(2P)
fractional contributions in the selected data, described by the
m_{rec}(\gamma_{s})
variable, the method of
Channel Likelihood
Condon:1974rh
is used.
An unbinned maximum likelihood fit to the data is performed, using an iterative procedure.
The convergence criterion
is defined by the difference in the fractions between the last two iterations and can be set to an arbitrary value (typically
10^{-5}
after about 25 iterations).
The total pdf (
\cal L
) is described by

{\cal L}=c_{1}\cdot f(\chi_{b1}(2P))+c_{2}\cdot f(\chi_{b2}(2P))+ps

(14)

where
c_{1}
and
c_{2}
are the fractional contributions for
\chi_{b1}(2P)
and
\chi_{b2}(2P)
. The symbol
ps
indicates the residual contribution (in this case the background), normalized as

ps=(1-c_{1}-c_{2})\cdot b
with the factor
b
fixed to a constant value (
b=1
). The symbols
f(\chi_{b1}(2P))
and
f(\chi_{b2}(2P))
indicate the
functional lineshapes describing the
\chi_{b1}(2P)
and
\chi_{b2}(2P)
contributions, integrated over the
m_{rec}(\gamma_{s})
fit range. The method assigns, after the fit, weights
w(\chi_{b1}(2P))
,
w(\chi_{b2}(2P))
, and
w(ps)
to each event for it to belong to the different components included in the definition of the pdf function.

The
\chi_{b1,2}(2P)
functional lineshapes are described by the Breit-Wigner functions (
BW
)

BW(m)=\frac{1}{(m_{0}-m)^{2}+\it\Gamma^{2}/4}

(15)

convolved with the detector resolution functions and the
m_{0}
values fixed to known values for
\chi_{b1,2}(2P)

PDG
. The value of
\it\Gamma
is set to an arbitrary value of
\it\Gamma
=0.1
{\mathrm{MeV}}
in the range of the small expected widths of these states
Godfrey:2015dia
.

The resolution functions
\delta(m_{rec}(\gamma_{s}))
are
evaluated separately for the
\chi_{b1}(2P)
and
\chi_{b2}(2P)
as the difference
between the MC-generated and reconstructed
m_{rec}(\gamma_{s})
.
To remove
multiple combinations, MC truth information is used.
The distributions are fitted by the sum of a Gaussian function (
G
, c=57%, with
\sigma=7.5

{\mathrm{MeV}}/c^{2}
)
and a Crystal-Ball function (
CB
, with
\sigma=3

{\mathrm{MeV}}/c^{2}
),

BW_{c}(x)=BW(x)\otimes[(1-c)\cdot CB(x)+c\cdot G(x)],

(16)

similarly for both
\chi_{b}(2P)
states.
The fitting model is validated on signal
\mu^{+}\mu^{-}
MC simulations by performing fits with known
\chi_{b1,2}(2P)
contributions. Good agreement is found, within the uncertainties, between the input and fitted fractional contribution values.
