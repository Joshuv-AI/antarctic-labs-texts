46 U.S.C. § 57308 — Use of vessels at least 25 years old
Citation: us:usc:46:57308
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section57308
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part F (Government-Owned Merchant Vessels); Chapter 573 (Vessel Trade-In Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An obsolete vessel acquired under this chapter that is or becomes at least 25 years old may not be used for commercial operation. However, the vessel may be used—
(1)
during a period in which vessels may be requisitioned under chapter 563 of this title; or
(2)
except as otherwise provided in this subtitle, on trade routes serving only the foreign trade of the United States.
