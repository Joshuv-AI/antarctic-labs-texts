An alternative ℚ \mathbb{Q} -form of the cyclotomic double shuffle Lie algebra
Hidekazu Furusho, Khalef Yaddaden

Abstract
. We present an alternative ℚ \mathbb{Q} -form for Racinet’s cyclotomic double shuffle Lie algebra, inspired by the double shuffle relations among congruent multiple zeta values studied by Yuan and Zhao. Our main result establishes an invariance characterization theorem, demonstrating how these two ℚ \mathbb{Q} -forms can be reconstructed from each other under Galois action.

Introduction

Multiple zeta values (MZVs in short) are real numbers defined by the following series

\zeta(k_{1},\dots,k_{r})=\sum_{n_{1}>\dots>n_{r}>0}\frac{1}{n_{1}^{k_{1}}\cdots n_{r}^{k_{r}}},

where
r,k_{1},\dots,k_{r}
are positive integers with
k_{1}\neq 1
(
[
Zag94
]
). These values appear in the studies of many subjects in mathematics and physics such as mixed Tate motives, knot invariant, the KZ-equation and Feynman integrals to name a few (for example, the reader may refer to
[
B15
,
BF
,
Zha16
]
for a comprehensive presentation).

For a positive integer
N
, one may consider two level
N
generalizations of multiple zeta values, such that MZV correspond to the
N=1
case. The first generalization (cf.
[
Gon98
]
), called
multiple polylogarithm values at
N
th
roots of unity
(
N
-MPVs in short), are complex numbers defined by the following series

(0.1)

\operatorname{Li}_{(k_{1},\dots,k_{r})}(\zeta_{1},\dots,\zeta_{r})=\sum_{n_{1}>\dots>n_{r}>0}\frac{\zeta_{1}^{n_{1}}\cdots\zeta_{r}^{n_{r}}}{n_{1}^{k_{1}}\cdots n_{r}^{k_{r}}},

where
r,k_{1},\dots,k_{r}
are positive integers and
\zeta_{1},\dots,\zeta_{r}
are
N
th
roots of unity with
(k_{1},\zeta_{1})\neq(1,1)
.
They are also called multiple
L
-values in
[
AK04
]
, cyclotomic multiple zeta values in
[
Ter04
]
or the colored multiple zeta values in
[
BJOP02
]
.
The second generalization, called
N
-congruent multiple zeta values
(
N
-CMZVs in short) are real numbers defined by the following series

(0.2)

\zeta_{(\alpha_{1},\dots,\alpha_{r})}^{\bmod N}{(k_{1},\dots,k_{r})}=\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}n_{1}>\dots>n_{r}>0\\
n_{j}\in\alpha_{j}\ 1\leq j\leq r\end{subarray}}\frac{1}{n_{1}^{k_{1}}\cdots n_{r}^{k_{r}}},

where
r,k_{1},\dots,k_{r}\in\mathbb{Z}_{>0}
with
k_{1}\neq 1
and
\alpha_{1},\dots,\alpha_{r}\in\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z}
. They are also called the
level
N
multiple zeta value
in
[
YZ16
]
. Denote by
\iota:\llbracket 1,N\rrbracket\to\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z}
the substitution that identifies each element of
\llbracket 1,N\rrbracket
with its equivalence class.
It has been established in
[
YZ16
, (8)]
that
N
-CMZVs (
0.2
) are related to
N
-MPVs (
0.1
) by the following formula

(0.3)

\zeta_{(\alpha_{1},\dots,\alpha_{r})}^{\bmod N}{(k_{1},\dots,k_{r})}=\frac{1}{N^{r}}\sum_{m_{1}=1}^{N}\cdots\sum_{m_{r}=1}^{N}\zeta_{N}^{-(m_{1}\iota^{-1}(\alpha_{1})+\cdots+m_{r}\iota^{-1}(\alpha_{r}))}\operatorname{Li}_{(k_{1},\dots,k_{r})}(\zeta_{N}^{m_{1}},\dots,\zeta_{N}^{m_{r}}),

where
\zeta_{N}:=\exp\left(\frac{2\pi\mathrm{i}}{N}\right)
.

The values (
0.1
) can be expressed as an iterated integral as follows (see for example
[
Gon98
]
)

(0.4)

\operatorname{Li}_{(k_{1},\dots,k_{r})}(\zeta_{1},\dots,\zeta_{r})=\int_{0}^{1}\Omega_{0}^{k_{1}-1}\Omega_{\zeta_{1}}\Omega_{0}^{k_{2}-1}\Omega_{\zeta_{1}\zeta_{2}}\cdots\Omega_{0}^{k_{r}-1}\Omega_{\zeta_{1}\cdots\zeta_{r}},

where
\Omega_{0}=\frac{dt}{t}
and
\Omega_{\zeta}=\frac{dt}{\zeta^{-1}-t}
, for any root of unity
\zeta
. Thanks to formula (
0.3
) and the iterated integral expression (
0.4
), one deduces the following identity (see
[
YZ16
, p.185]
)

\zeta_{(\alpha_{1},\dots,\alpha_{r})}^{\bmod N}{(k_{1},\dots,k_{r})}=\frac{1}{N^{r}}\int_{0}^{1}\omega^{k_{1}-1}\omega_{\alpha_{1}-\alpha_{2}}\cdots\omega^{k_{r-1}-1}\omega_{\alpha_{r-1}-\alpha_{r}}\omega^{k_{r}-1}\omega_{\alpha_{r}},

where
\omega=\frac{dt}{t}
and
\omega_{\alpha}=\frac{Nt^{\iota^{-1}(\alpha)-1}dt}{1-t^{N}}
for any
\alpha\in\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z}
. One may also check that
[
YZ16
, p.185]

(0.5)

\omega=\Omega_{0}\text{ and }\omega_{\alpha}=\sum_{m=1}^{N}\zeta_{N}^{-m\iota^{-1}(\alpha)}\Omega_{\zeta_{N}^{m}}.

Using iterated sums and iterated integral expression, one deduces linear and algebraic relations between
N
-MPVs called the (regularized)
double shuffle
relations. To describe these relations Hoffmann
[
Hof97
]
introduced a word algebra setting whose letters are free noncommutative variables assimilated to the differential
1
-forms
\Omega_{0}
and
\Omega_{\zeta}
(
\zeta\in\mu_{N}
). This setting has been utilized to provide two kinds of algebraic frameworks for these relations. The first one, introduced by Racinet
[
Rac02
]
uses noncommutative formal power series Hopf algebras. This framework introduced a
\mathbb{Q}
-Lie algebra
\mathfrak{dmr}_{0}^{\mu_{N}}
, which is a key ingredient in Racinet’s main result on the torsor structure of (regularized) double shuffle and distribution relations. The second framework, introduced by Arakawa-Kaneko
[
AK04
]
, was inspired by Ihara-Kaneko-Zagier
[
IKZ
]
(
N=1
) and utilizes noncommutative free polynomial algebras. The equivalence between the two frameworks is related to dual nature of the relation between algebras and Hopf algebras in general.
The relation (
0.3
) suggests that
N
-CMZVs also satisfy (regularized) double shuffle relations. In fact, this has been introduced by Yuan-Zhao
[
YZ16
]
and written down precisely by Kanno in
[
K24
]
thanks to the word algebra setting whose letters are the free noncommutative variables associated to the differential
1
-forms
\omega
and
\omega_{\alpha}
(
\alpha\in\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z}
).

In this paper, we utilize the identities (
0.5
) to construct an algebra isomorphism between the free noncommutative series algebras with coefficients in the cyclotomic field
\mathbb{Q}(\mu_{N})
associated to each set of differential
1
-forms (see Lemma
3.1
). This enables us to introduce a dual equivalent of the formalism of
[
K24
]
, which facilitates the formulation of double shuffle relations among
N
-CMZVs within the formalism of
[
Rac02
]
. We then show that this algebra isomorphism is in fact a Hopf algebra isomorphism with respect to the newly introduced coproducts and the coproducts of
[
Rac02
]
, thus establishing the relations between both formalisms. After that, we define a
\mathbb{Q}
-Lie algebra
\mathfrak{dmr}_{0}^{[N]}
related to the (regularized) double shuffle relations among
N
-CMZVs and establish the following result:

Theorem 0.1

(Theorem
3.11
)
.

There is a
\mathbb{Q}(\mu_{N})
-Lie algebra isomorphism

\mathbb{Q}(\mu_{N})\ \hat{\otimes}_{\mathbb{Q}}\ \mathfrak{dmr}_{0}^{[N]}\xrightarrow{\simeq}\mathbb{Q}(\mu_{N})\ \hat{\otimes}_{\mathbb{Q}}\ \mathfrak{dmr}_{0}^{\mu_{N}}.

Finally, we focus our interest in the
\mathbb{Q}
-forms of these Lie algebra. In order to do so, we introduce a suitable action of the Galois group
\mathrm{Gal}(\mathbb{Q}(\mu_{N})/\mathbb{Q})
, which enables us to establish the following result:

Theorem 0.2

(Theorem
3.16
)
.

There is a
\mathbb{Q}
-Lie algebra isomorphism between
\mathfrak{dmr}_{0}^{\mu_{N}}
(resp.
\mathfrak{dmr}_{0}^{[N]}
) and the invariant subspace of
\mathbb{Q}(\mu_{N})\ \hat{\otimes}_{\mathbb{Q}}\ \mathfrak{dmr}_{0}^{[N]}
(resp.
\mathbb{Q}(\mu_{N})\ \hat{\otimes}_{\mathbb{Q}}\ \mathfrak{dmr}_{0}^{\mu_{N}}
) under the action of
\mathrm{Gal}(\mathbb{Q}(\mu_{N})/\mathbb{Q})
.

Notation

Throughout this paper, let
N\geq 3
be a positive integer and
\mu_{N}
be the group of complex roots of unity. Denote by
\iota:\llbracket 1,N\rrbracket\to\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z}
the substitution that identifies each element of
\llbracket 1,N\rrbracket
with its equivalence class. Finally, let
\mathbb{Q}
be a commutative
\mathbb{Q}
-algebra with unit.

Shuffle and harmonic algebras

The

(

(a)

The

1\ \sh\ w=w\ \sh\ 1=w,

uw_{1}\ \sh\ vw_{2}=u(w_{1}\ \sh\ vw_{2})+v(uw_{1}\ \sh\ w_{2}),

\mathfrak{dmr}_{0}^{\mu_{N}}

For

\displaystyle x_{0}\mapsto 0,\text{ and }\,x_{\zeta}\mapsto[x_{\zeta},t_{\zeta}(\psi)]\text{ for }\zeta\in\mu_{N},

(iii)

1
1

1
