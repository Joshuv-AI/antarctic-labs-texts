21 C.F.R. § 117.301 — Records subject to the requirements of this subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:21:117.301
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-117.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 117 (Current Good Manufacturing Practice, Hazard Analysis, And Risk-Based Preventive Controls For Human Food). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Except as provided by paragraphs (b) and (c) of this section, all records required by this part are subject to all requirements of this subpart.
(b) The requirements of § 117.310 apply only to the written food safety plan.
(c) The requirements of § 117.305(b), (d), (e), and (f) do not apply to the records required by § 117.201.
