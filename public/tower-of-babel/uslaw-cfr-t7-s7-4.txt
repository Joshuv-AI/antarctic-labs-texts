7 C.F.R. § 7.4 — Selection of committee members.
Citation: us:cfr:7:7.4
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-7.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 7 (Selection And Functions Of Farm Service Agency State And County Committees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) State committee members will be selected by the Secretary and will serve at the pleasure of the Secretary.
(b) County committee members will be elected as specified in § 7.11 of this part or appointed as specified in § 7.17 of this part.
