16 U.S.C. § 460s–2 — Establishment; notice in Federal Register
Citation: us:usc:16:460s–2
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section460s–2
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter LXXVII (Pictured Rocks National Lakeshore). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As soon as practicable after October 15, 1966, and following the acquisition by the Secretary of an acreage within the boundaries of the area which in his opinion is efficiently administrable for the purposes of this subchapter, he shall establish the Pictured Rocks National Lakeshore by publication of notice thereof in the Federal Register.
