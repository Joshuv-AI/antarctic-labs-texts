48 C.F.R. § 31.205-3 — 31.205-3 Bad debts.
Citation: us:cfr:48:31.205-3
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-31.205-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 31 (Contract Cost Principles And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Bad debts, including actual or estimated losses arising from uncollectible accounts receivable due from customers and other claims, and any directly associated costs such as collection costs, and legal costs are unallowable.
