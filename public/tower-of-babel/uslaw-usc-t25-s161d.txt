25 U.S.C. § 161d — Disposition of accrued interest
Citation: us:usc:25:161d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section161d
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 4 (Performance By United States Of Obligations To Indians); Subchapter III (Deposit, Care, And Investment Of Indian Moneys). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The interest accruing on Indian tribal funds under sections 161a to 161c of this title shall be subject to the same disposition as prescribed by existing law for the respective principal funds.
