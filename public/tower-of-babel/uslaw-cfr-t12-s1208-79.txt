12 C.F.R. § 1208.79 — Prohibited actions by employer.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1208.79
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1208.79
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1208 (Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Debt Collection Improvement Act of 1996 prohibits an employer from discharging, refusing to employ, or taking disciplinary action against the debtor due to the issuance of a withholding order under this subpart E.
