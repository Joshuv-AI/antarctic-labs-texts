15 U.S.C. § 790h — Congressional access to energy information; disclosure by Congress
Citation: us:usc:15:790h
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section790h
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 16B (Federal Energy Administration); Subchapter II (Office Of Energy Information And Analysis). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director shall promptly provide upon request any energy information in the possession of the Office to any duly established committee of the Congress. Such information shall be deemed the property of such committee and may not be disclosed except in accordance with the rules of such committee and the Rules of the House of Representatives or the Senate and as permitted by law.
