25 C.F.R. § 135.22 — Penalty.
Citation: us:cfr:25:135.22
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-135.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 135 (Construction Assessments, Crow Indian Irrigation Project). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To all assessments not paid on the due date there shall be added a penalty of one-half of one percent per month or fraction thereof, from the due date so long as the delinquency continues.
