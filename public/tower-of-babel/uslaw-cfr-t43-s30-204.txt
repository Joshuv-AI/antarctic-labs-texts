43 C.F.R. § 30.204 — May I request that a formal probate proceeding be conducted instead of a summary probate proceeding?
Citation: us:cfr:43:30.204
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-30.204
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 30 (Indian Probate Hearings Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No. Formal probate proceedings are available only for estates that contain trust or restricted land or contain trust personalty in an amount greater than $300.
