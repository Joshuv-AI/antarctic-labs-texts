10 U.S.C. § 1567 — Duration of military protective orders
Citation: us:usc:10:1567
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1567
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 80 (Miscellaneous Investigation Requirements And Other Duties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A military protective order issued by a military commander shall remain in effect until such time as the military commander terminates the order or issues a replacement order.
