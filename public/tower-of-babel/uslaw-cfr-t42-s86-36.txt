42 C.F.R. § 86.36 — Duration and continuation.
Citation: us:cfr:42:86.36
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-86.36
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 86 (Grants For Education Programs In Occupational Safety And Health). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Direct traineeship awards may be made for varying periods not in excess of 2 years. The Secretary may make one or more continuation awards for an additional period if he finds that satisfactory progress is being made toward accomplishment of the purpose of the initial traineeship award. Additional support may be provided on appropriate justification after expiration of the period of support in the previous award.
