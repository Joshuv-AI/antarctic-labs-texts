38 U.S.C. § 1731 — Assistance to the Republic of the Philippines
Citation: us:usc:38:1731
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section1731
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part II (General Benefits); Chapter 17 (Hospital, Nursing Home, Domiciliary, And Medical Care); Subchapter IV (Hospital Care And Medical Treatment For Veterans In The Republic Of The Philippines). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President is authorized to assist the Republic of the Philippines in fulfilling its responsibility in providing medical care and treatment for Commonwealth Army veterans and new Philippine Scouts in need of such care and treatment for service-connected disabilities and non-service-connected disabilities under certain conditions.
