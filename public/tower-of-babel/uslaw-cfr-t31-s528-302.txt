31 C.F.R. § 528.302 — Ally of the United States.
Citation: us:cfr:31:528.302
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-528.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 528 (International Criminal Court-Related Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term ally of the United States means:
(a) A government of a member country of the North Atlantic Treaty Organization (NATO); or
(b) A government of a “major non-NATO ally,” as that term is defined by section 2013(7) of the American Servicemembers' Protection Act of 2002 (22 U.S.C. 7432(7)).
