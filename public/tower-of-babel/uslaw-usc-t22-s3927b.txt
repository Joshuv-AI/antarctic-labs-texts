22 U.S.C. § 3927b — Reporting requirement on chiefs of mission
Citation: us:usc:22:3927b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section3927b
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 52 (Foreign Service); Subchapter II (Management Of Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 30 days following the end of each calendar quarter, the Secretary shall submit to the appropriate congressional committees—
(1)
a list of every chief of mission or United States representative overseas with the rank of Ambassador who, during the prior quarter, was granted approval by the Under Secretary of State for Management to be outside a country of assignment for purposes other than official travel or temporary duty orders; and
(2)
the number of days each such chief of mission or United States representative overseas with the rank of Ambassador was outside a country of assignment during the previous quarter for purposes other than official travel or temporary duty orders.
