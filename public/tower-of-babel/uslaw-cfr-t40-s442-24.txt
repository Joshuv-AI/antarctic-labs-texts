40 C.F.R. § 442.24 — New source performance standards (NSPS).
Citation: us:cfr:40:442.24
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-442.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 442 (Transportation Equipment Cleaning Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any new point source subject to this subpart must achieve the following performance standards: Standards for BOD5, TSS, oil and grease (HEM), fluoranthene, phenanthrene and pH are the same as the corresponding limitation specified in § 442.21.
