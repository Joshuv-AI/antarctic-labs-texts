24 C.F.R. § 6.13 — Hearings and appeals.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 6 (Nondiscrimination In Programs And Activities Receiving Assistance Under Title I Of The Housing And Community Development Act Of 1974). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) When a Recipient requests an opportunity for a hearing, in accordance with § 6.12(b)(3), the General Counsel will follow the notification procedures set forth in 24 CFR 180.415. The hearing, and any petition for review, will be conducted in accordance with the procedures set forth in 24 CFR part 180.
(b) After a hearing is held and a final agency decision is rendered under 24 CFR part 180, the Recipient may seek judicial review in accordance with section 111(c) of the Act.
