5 C.F.R. § 890.1204 — Effective date of coverage.
Citation: us:cfr:5:890.1204
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-890.1204
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 890 (Federal Employees Health Benefits Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless the U.S. Department of State determines that a later date is appropriate, coverage under § 890.1203(b) is effective on August 2, 1990, for hostages in Iraq and Kuwait and on the later of the date hostage status began or June 1, 1982, for hostages in Lebanon.
