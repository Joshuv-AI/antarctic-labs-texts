42 U.S.C. § 292c — Sources of funds
Citation: us:usc:42:292c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section292c
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter V (Health Professions Education); Part A (Student Loans); Subpart i (Insured Health Education Assistance Loans To Graduate Students). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Loans made by eligible lenders in accordance with this subpart shall be insurable by the Secretary whether made from funds fully owned by the lender or from funds held by the lender in a trust or similar capacity and available for such loans.
