6 U.S.C. § 488b — Inspection and auditing of records
Citation: us:usc:6:488b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title6-section488b
Context: United States Code, Title 6 (Domestic Security); Chapter 1 (Homeland Security Organization); Subchapter VIII (Coordination With Non-Federal Entities; Inspector General; United States Secret Service; Coast Guard; General Provisions); Part J (Secure Handling Of Ammonium Nitrate). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall establish a process for the periodic inspection and auditing of the records maintained by owners of ammonium nitrate facilities for the purpose of monitoring compliance with this part or for the purpose of deterring or preventing the misappropriation or use of ammonium nitrate in an act of terrorism.
