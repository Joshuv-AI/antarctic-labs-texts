33 U.S.C. § 416 — Appropriations for removal of sunken water craft
Citation: us:usc:33:416
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section416
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 9 (Protection Of Navigable Waters And Of Harbor And River Improvements Generally); Subchapter I (In General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Such sum of money as may be necessary to execute sections 414(a) and 415 of this title is hereby appropriated out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to be paid out on the requisition of the Secretary of the Army.
