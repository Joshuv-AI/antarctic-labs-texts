31 C.F.R. § 208.8 — Recipient responsibilities.
Citation: us:cfr:31:208.8
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-208.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 208 (Management Of Federal Agency Disbursements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each recipient who is required to receive payment by electronic funds transfer shall provide the information necessary to effect payment by electronic funds transfer. For recipients who do not designate a bank account for the receipt of payments, Treasury may disburse payments to a Treasury-sponsored account or to an account to which the recipient is receiving other Federal payments.
