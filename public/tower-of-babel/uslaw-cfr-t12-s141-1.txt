12 C.F.R. § 141.1 — When do the definitions in this part apply?

Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 141 (Definitions For Regulations Affecting Federal Savings Associations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).
