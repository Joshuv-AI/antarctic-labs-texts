5 C.F.R. § 1400.205 — Savings provision.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1400.205
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management And Office Of The Director Of National Intelligence
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1400.205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1400 (Designation Of National Security Positions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No provision of the rule in this part may be applied to make an adverse inference in pending administrative proceedings. However, the redesignation of a position may require that the occupant of that position undergo a new adjudication. An administrative proceeding is deemed to be pending from the date of the agency or OPM notice described in § 1400.301(c)(1).
