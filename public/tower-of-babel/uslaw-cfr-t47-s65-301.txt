47 C.F.R. § 65.301 — Cost of equity.
Citation: us:cfr:47:65.301
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-65.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 65 (Interstate Rate Of Return Prescription, Procedures, And Methodologies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The cost of equity shall be determined in represcription proceedings after giving full consideration to the evidence in the record, including such evidence as the Commission may officially notice.
