37 C.F.R. § 11.15 — Refusal to recognize a practitioner.
Citation: us:cfr:37:11.15
Creator: United States Patent And Trademark Office, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-11.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 11 (Representation Of Others Before The United States Patent And Trademark Office). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any practitioner authorized to appear before the Office may be suspended, excluded, or reprimanded in accordance with the provisions of this Part. Any practitioner who is suspended or excluded under this Part shall not be entitled to practice before the Office in patent, trademark, or other non-patent matters while suspended or excluded.
