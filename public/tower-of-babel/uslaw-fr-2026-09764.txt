A. Overview of Information Collection:

    Title of Information Collection: Energy and Performance Information 
Center (EPIC).
    OMB Approval Number: 2577-0274.
    Type of Request: Revision of a currently approved collection.
    Form Numbers: N/A.
    Description of the need for the information and proposed use: EPIC 
automates the collection of the Five-Year Plan and Annual Statement/
Budget/Performance and Evaluation (P&E) forms from grantees. These 
required forms were previously collected in hard copy on Forms HUD-
50075.1 and HUD-50075.2 under collection OMB control number 2577-0157. 
These forms collect data on the eventual and actual use of funds. 
Electronic collection enables the Department to aggregate information 
about the way grantees use Federal funds. Tracking Federal funds paid 
through the Public Housing Capital Fund, the only Federal funding 
stream dedicated to the capital needs of the nation's last resort 
housing option, is crucial to understanding how the Department can 
properly and efficiently assist grantees to meet the Department's goals 
in maintaining and improving public housing as a safe, decent, and 
sustainable housing option for low-income residents. EPIC also 
automates the collection of signed documents required by 24 CFR 905 to 
gain access to funds awarded by HUD. These forms are covered under the 
Public Housing Capital Fund Program, OMB control number 2577-0157. 
Finally, EPIC allows PHAs to request additional funding sources, such 
as Operating Funds for Capital Fund-eligible activities. The EPIC data 
system is equipped to collect Physical Needs Assessment data, should 
this data be required in the future. EPIC is equipped to collect 
information about the Energy Performance Contract process, including 
energy efficiency improvements. EPIC is equipped to track development 
of public housing with Federal funds and through other means, including 
mixed-finance development.
    Respondents: Public Housing Agencies.

B. Solicitation of Public Comment

    This notice is soliciting comments from members of the public and 
affected parties concerning the collection of information described in 
Section A on the following:
    (1) Whether the proposed collection of information is necessary for 
the proper performance of the functions of the agency, including 
whether the information will have practical utility;
    (2) The accuracy of the agency's estimate of the burden of the 
proposed collection of information;
    (3) Ways to enhance the quality, utility, and clarity of the 
information to be collected; and
    (4) Ways to minimize the burden of the collection of information on 
those who are to respond, including the use of appropriate automated 
collection techniques or other forms of information technology, e.g., 
permitting electronic submission of responses.
    HUD encourages interested parties to submit comments in response to 
these questions.
