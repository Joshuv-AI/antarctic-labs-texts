34 U.S.C. § 12404 — Disbursement
Citation: us:usc:34:12404
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section12404
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 121 (Violent Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Subchapter III (Violence Against Women); Part F (National Stalker And Domestic Violence Reduction). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 90 days after the receipt of an application under this part, the Attorney General shall either provide grant funds or shall inform the applicant why grant funds are not being provided.
