45 C.F.R. § 1705.12 — Exemptions.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1705.12
Creator: National Commission On Libraries And Information Science
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1705.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1705 (Privacy Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No Commission records system is exempted from the provisions of 5 U.S.C. 552a as permitted under certain conditions by 5 U.S.C. 552a (j) and (k).
