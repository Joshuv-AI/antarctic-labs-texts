14 C.F.R. § 194.307 — Applicability of rules for eligible on-demand operations.
Citation: us:cfr:14:194.307
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-194.307
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 194 (Special Federal Aviation Regulation No. 120—Powered-Lift: Pilot Certification And Training; Operations Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person may operate a powered-lift in an eligible on-demand operation under part 135 of this chapter without complying with the requirements specified for the second in command of a fixed-wing aircraft contained in § 135.4(a)(3) of this chapter.
