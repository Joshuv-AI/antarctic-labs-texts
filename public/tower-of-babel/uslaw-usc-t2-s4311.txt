2 U.S.C. § 4311 — Approval of employment and compensation of committee employees by House standing committees
Citation: us:usc:2:4311
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section4311
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 43 (Congressional Committees); Subchapter II (House Of Representatives). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Standing committees of the House shall have authority to approve the employment and compensation of committee employees (other than special and select committee employees) from the effective date of the beginning of each Congress, or such subsequent date as their service commenced.
