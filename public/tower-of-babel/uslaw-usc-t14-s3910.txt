14 U.S.C. § 3910 — Radio station deemed government station
Citation: us:usc:14:3910
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section3910
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 39 (Coast Guard Auxiliary). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any radio station, while assigned to authorized Coast Guard duty shall be deemed to be a radio station of the Coast Guard and a “government station” within the meaning of section 305 of the Communications Act of 1934 (47 U.S.C. 305).
