42 U.S.C. § 12007 — No antitrust immunity or defenses
Citation: us:usc:42:12007
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section12007
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 125 (Renewable Energy And Energy Efficiency Technology Competitiveness). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be deemed to convey to any person, partnership, corporation, or other entity immunity from civil or criminal liability under any antitrust law or to create defenses to actions under any antitrust law. As used in this section, “antitrust laws” means those Acts set forth in section 12 of title 15.
