46 C.F.R. § 173.053 — Sailing school vessels.
Citation: us:cfr:46:173.053
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-173.053
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 173 (Special Rules Pertaining To Vessel Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) In addition to the requirements in §§ 173.054 through 173.063, each sailing school vessel must comply with the provisions of subpart A of part 171 of this subchapter.
(b) In addition to regular passengers, for the purpose of complying with §§ 171.070 through 171.073 and § 171.080, the following will also be counted as passengers:
(1) Sailing school students.
(2) Sailing school instructors.
(3) Guests.
