12 C.F.R. § 604.410 — Scope of application.
Citation: us:cfr:12:604.410
Creator: Farm Credit Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-604.410
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 604 (Farm Credit Administration Board Meetings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this part apply to meetings of the Board, and do not apply to conferences or other gatherings of employees of the Farm Credit Administration who meet or join with others, except at meetings of the Board, to deliberate official agency business.
