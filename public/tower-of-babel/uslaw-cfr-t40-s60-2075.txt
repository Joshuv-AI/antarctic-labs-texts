40 C.F.R. § 60.2075 — When must the operator training course be completed?
Citation: us:cfr:40:60.2075
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-60.2075
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 60 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The operator training course must be completed by the later of the three dates specified in paragraphs (a) through (c) of this section:
(a) Six months after your CISWI startup;
(b) December 3, 2001; and
(c) The date before an employee assumes responsibility for operating the CISWI or assumes responsibility for supervising the operation of the CISWI.
