40 C.F.R. § 436.180 — Applicability; description of the phosphate rock subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:436.180
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-436.180
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 436 (Mineral Mining And Processing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to the mining and the processing of phosphate bearing rock, ore or earth for the phosphate content.
