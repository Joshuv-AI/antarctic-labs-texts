45 C.F.R. § 1640.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1640.1
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1640.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1640 (Application Of Federal Law To Lsc Recipients). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to ensure that recipients use their LSC funds in accordance with Federal law related to the proper use of Federal funds. This part also provides notice to recipients of the consequences of a violation of such Federal laws by a recipient, its employees or board members.
