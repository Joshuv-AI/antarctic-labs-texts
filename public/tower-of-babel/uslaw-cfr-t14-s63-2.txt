14 C.F.R. § 63.2 — Certification of foreign flight crewmembers other than pilots.
Citation: us:cfr:14:63.2
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-63.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 63 (Certification: Flight Crewmembers Other Than Pilots). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A person who is neither a United States citizen nor a resident alien is issued a certificate under this part (other than under § 63.23 or § 63.42) outside the United States only when the Administrator finds that the certificate is needed for the operation of a U.S.-registered civil aircraft.
