16 U.S.C. § 690h — “Person” defined
Citation: us:usc:16:690h
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section690h
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 6 (Game And Bird Preserves; Protection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in sections 690 to 690i of this title the term “person” includes an individual, partnership, association, or corporation.
