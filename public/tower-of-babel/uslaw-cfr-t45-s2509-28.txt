45 C.F.R. § 2509.28 — How will significant guidance be issued when there are exigent circumstances?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2509.28
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2509.28
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2509 (Administrative Practice And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Under exigent circumstances, such as safety, health, or when statutory deadlines or court order or other compelling cause require CNCS to act more quickly than normal review procedures allow, CNCS will notify OIRA as soon as possible and, to the extent practicable, comply with the requirements of this subpart at the earliest opportunity.
