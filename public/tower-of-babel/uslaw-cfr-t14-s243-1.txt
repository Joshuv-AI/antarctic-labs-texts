14 C.F.R. § 243.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:14:243.1
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-243.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 243 (Passenger Manifest Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to ensure that the U.S. government has prompt and adequate information in case of an aviation disaster on covered flight segments.
