33 C.F.R. § 125.11 — Form of Coast Guard Port Security Card.
Citation: us:cfr:33:125.11
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-125.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 125 (Identification Credentials For Persons Requiring Access To Waterfront Facilities Or Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Coast Guard Port Security Card issued by the Coast Guard under the provisions of this subchapter shall be a laminated card bearing photograph, signature, fingerprint, and personal description of the holder, and other pertinent data.
