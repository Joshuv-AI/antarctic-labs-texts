17 C.F.R. § 200.61 — Impressions of influence.
Citation: us:cfr:17:200.61
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-200.61
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 200 (Organization; Conduct And Ethics; And Information And Requests). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A member should not, by his conduct, permit the impression to prevail that any person can improperly influence him, that any person unduly enjoys his favor or that he is affected in any way by the rank, position, prestige, or affluence of any person.
