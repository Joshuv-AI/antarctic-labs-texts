45 C.F.R. § 309.125 — On what basis is Federal funding of Tribal IV-D programs determined?
Citation: us:cfr:45:309.125
Creator: Office Of Child Support Services, Administration Of Families And Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-309.125
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 309 (Tribal Child Support Enforcement (Iv-D) Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Federal funding of Tribal IV-D programs is based on information contained in the Tribal IV-D application. The application must include a proposed budget and a description of the nature and scope of the Tribal IV-D program and must give assurance that the program will be administered in conformity with applicable requirements of title IV-D of the Act, regulations contained in this part, and other official issuances of the Department that specifically state applicability to Tribal IV-D programs.
