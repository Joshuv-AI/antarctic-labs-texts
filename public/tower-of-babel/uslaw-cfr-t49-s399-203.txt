49 C.F.R. § 399.203 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:49:399.203
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-399.203
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 399 (Employee Safety And Health Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to all trucks and truck-tractors, having a high profile cab-over-engine (COE) configuration, for entrance, egress and back of cab access, manufactured on and after September 1, 1982.
