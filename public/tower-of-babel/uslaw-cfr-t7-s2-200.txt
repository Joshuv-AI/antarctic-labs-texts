7 C.F.R. § 2.200 — Principal Deputy General Counsel.
Citation: us:cfr:7:2.200
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-2.200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 2 (Delegations Of Authority By The Secretary Of Agriculture And General Officers Of The Department). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Pursuant to § 2.31, the following delegation of authority is made by the General Counsel to the Principal Deputy General Counsel, to be exercised only during the absence or unavailability of the General Counsel: Perform all duties and exercise all powers that are now or which may hereafter be delegated to the General Counsel.
