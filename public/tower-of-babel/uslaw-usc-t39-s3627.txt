39 U.S.C. § 3627 — Adjusting free rates
Citation: us:usc:39:3627
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title39-section3627
Context: United States Code, Title 39 (Postal Service); Part IV (Mail Matter); Chapter 36 (Postal Rates, Classes, And Services); Subchapter I (Provisions Relating To Market-Dominant Products). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If Congress fails to appropriate an amount authorized under section 2401(c) of this title for any class of mail sent free of postage under section 3217 or 3403–3406 1 the rate for that class may be adjusted in accordance with the provisions of this subchapter so that the increased revenues received from the users of such class will equal the amount for that class that the Congress was to appropriate.
