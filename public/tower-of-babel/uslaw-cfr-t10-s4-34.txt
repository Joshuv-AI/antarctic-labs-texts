10 C.F.R. § 4.34 — Information to beneficiaries and participants.
Citation: us:cfr:10:4.34
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-4.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 4 (Nondiscrimination In Federally Assisted Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance From The Commission). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each recipient shall make available to participants, beneficiaries, and other interested persons such information regarding the provisions of this subpart and its applicability to the program for which the recipient receives Federal financial assistance, and make such information available to them in such manner, as the responsible NRC official finds necessary to apprise such persons of the protections against discrimination assured them by the Act and this subpart.
