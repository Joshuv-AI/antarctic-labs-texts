28 U.S.C. § 1692 — Process and orders affecting property in different districts
Citation: us:usc:28:1692
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section1692
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part V (Procedure); Chapter 113 (Process). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In proceedings in a district court where a receiver is appointed for property, real, personal, or mixed, situated in different districts, process may issue and be executed in any such district as if the property lay wholly within one district, but orders affecting the property shall be entered of record in each of such districts.
