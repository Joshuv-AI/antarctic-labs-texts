21 C.F.R. § 101.71 — Health claims: claims not authorized.
Citation: us:cfr:21:101.71
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-101.71
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 101 (Food Labeling). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Health claims not authorized for foods in conventional food form or for dietary supplements of vitamins, minerals, herbs, or other similar substances:
(a) Dietary fiber and cardiovascular disease.
(b) Zinc and immune function in the elderly.
