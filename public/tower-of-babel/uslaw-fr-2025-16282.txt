from specified requirements of Federal Aviation Regulations. The

Petition for Exemption

    Docket No.: FAA-2025-1268.
    Petitioner: Tactical Electronics and Military Supply LLC.
    Section(s) of 14 CFR Affected: Sec.  107.36.
    Description of Relief Sought: Tactical Electronics and Military 
Supply LLC seeks relief for limited dispensing of Hazardous Material, 
as defined in 49 CFR 171.8, with small unmanned aircraft systems (UAS) 
weighing less than 55 pounds (lbs.), including payload. The petitioner 
proposes to conduct research, development, testing and evaluation, 
along with realistic training for Department of Defense (DoD) customers 
regarding offensive and defensive use of Kinetic small UAS platforms.
