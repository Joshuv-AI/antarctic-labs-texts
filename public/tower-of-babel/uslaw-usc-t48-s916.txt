48 U.S.C. § 916 — Ratification of previous legislation
Citation: us:usc:48:916
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section916
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 4 (Puerto Rico); Subchapter VII (Low Rent Housing Projects And Elimination Of Sub-Standard Housing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All legislation heretofore enacted by the Legislature of Puerto Rico dealing with the subject matter of sections 911 to 916 of this title and not inconsistent herewith is ratified and confirmed.
