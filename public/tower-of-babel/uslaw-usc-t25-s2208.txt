25 U.S.C. § 2208 — Conveyancing authority upon sale or exchange of tribal lands; removal of trust status of individually owned lands
Citation: us:usc:25:2208
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section2208
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 24 (Indian Land Consolidation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall have the authority to issue deeds, patents, or such other instruments of conveyance needed to effectuate a sale or exchange of tribal lands made pursuant to the terms of this chapter and to remove, at the request of an Indian owner, the trust status of individually held lands or interests therein, where authorized by law.
