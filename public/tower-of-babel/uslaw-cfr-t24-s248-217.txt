24 C.F.R. § 248.217 — Revisions to plan of action.
Citation: us:cfr:24:248.217
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-248.217
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 248 (Prepayment Of Low Income Housing Mortgages). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The owner may from time to time revise the plan of action before its approval as may be necessary to obtain the commissioner's approval thereof. An owner shall submit any revision to the Commissioner, and provide a copy of the revision and all documentation supporting the revision except for that documentation deemed “proprietary information” under § 248.101, to the parties, and in the manner, specified in § 248.213(a).
