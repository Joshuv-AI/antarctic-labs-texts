49 U.S.C. § 26104 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:49:26104
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section26104
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part D (High-Speed Rail); Chapter 261 (High-Speed Rail Assistance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Fiscal Years 2006 Through 2013.—
There are authorized to be appropriated to the Secretary—
(1)
$30,000,000 for carrying out section 26101; and
(2)
$30,000,000 for carrying out section 26102,
for each of the fiscal years 2006 through 2013.
(b) Funds To Remain Available.—
Funds made available under this section shall remain available until expended.
