16 U.S.C. § 410kkk–5 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:16:410kkk–5
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section410kkk–5
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter LIX–II (Lewis And Clark National Historical Park). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary to carry out this subchapter.
