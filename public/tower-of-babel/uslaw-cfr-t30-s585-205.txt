30 C.F.R. § 585.205 — How will leases be mapped?
Citation: us:cfr:30:585.205
Creator: Bureau Of Ocean Energy Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-585.205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 585 (Renewable Energy On The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

BOEM will prepare leasing maps and official protraction diagrams of areas of the OCS. The areas included in each lease will be in accordance with the appropriate leasing map or official protraction diagram.
