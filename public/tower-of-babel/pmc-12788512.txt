To Evaluate the Efficacy of Ozonated Olive Oil with Microneedling for Gingival Inflammation—A Clinical Study
Authors: Aishwarya Duble, Hiroj Bagde, Meenakshi Boddun, Kavita Lohiya, Ritika Rathi, Nosheen Riza
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
Background:Scaling and root planing (SRP) remains the gold standard for managing gingival and periodontal diseases. However, adjunctive therapies like ozone and microneedling have emerged as potential methods for enhancing clinical outcomes. This study aimed to evaluate the efficacy of combining microneedling with ozonated olive oil in patients with plaque-induced gingivitis.Methods:Forty-five patients diagnosed with plaque-induced gingivitis were randomly assigned into three groups: Group 1 received SRP alone (control), Group 2 received SRP with microneedling, and Group 3 received SRP with microneedling plus ozonated olive oil. Clinical assessments, including Plaque Index (PI) and Gingival Index (GI), were recorded at baseline and after 15 days of treatment.Results:All three groups showed improvements in both the Plaque Index and Gingival Index. However, Group 3 (SRP with microneedling plus ozonated olive oil) exhibited the greatest reduction in both indices, with the results being statistically significant compared to the other two groups (P < 0.05).Conclusion:The combination of microneedling and ozonated olive oil appears to be a highly effective, minimally invasive adjunct to SRP for managing plaque-induced gingivitis. This therapy demonstrated superior clinical outcomes in terms of plaque reduction and gingival inflammation compared to SRP alone or SRP with microneedling.

INTRODUCTION

Dental plaque biofilm-induced gingivitis is an inflammatory condition limited to the gingiva and triggered by the host’s immune response to microbial plaque. It does not involve deeper periodontal structures and is reversible with proper oral hygiene.[1] The plaque contains a mix of gram-positive and gram-negative bacteria, motile rods, and spirochetes, supporting the nonspecific plaque hypothesis. Clinically, gingivitis presents as redness, swelling, and bleeding on probing and may progress to periodontitis if untreated.[2]

While mechanical plaque control remains the primary treatment, adjuncts like chlorhexidine are commonly used, despite side effects such as staining and altered taste. Ozone therapy, available in oil, water, or gas form, offers antimicrobial and healing benefits. Ozonated oil is a stable formulation created by infusing ozone into vegetable oil.[3]

Microneedling is a minimally invasive method that enhances the absorption of topical agents. This study evaluates the combined effect of microneedling and ozonated olive oil as an adjunct to scaling in the management of gingivitis.

OBJECTIVE OF STUDY

The study aims to evaluate the efficacy of ozone oil with microneedling for gingival inflammation and reduction of plaque index and gingival index after using the microneedling technique and ozone oil.

MATERIALS AND METHODS

This clinical study was conducted in the Department of Periodontology, Chhattisgarh Dental College and Research Institute, Rajnandgaon, India, with ethical approval (Ref: CDCRI/DEAN/ETHICS COMMITTEE/PERIO/PG-01/2024). Written informed consent was obtained from all participants.

Study design and participants

A total of 45 systemically healthy patients (18-50 years) with moderate to severe gingival inflammation in the anterior region were randomly divided into three groups (n = 15 each):

Group 1 (Control): Scaling and Root Planing (SRP)

Group 1 (Control): Scaling and Root Planing (SRP)

Group 2: SRP + Microneedling

Group 2: SRP + Microneedling

Group 3: SRP + Microneedling + Ozonated Olive Oil

Group 3: SRP + Microneedling + Ozonated Olive Oil

Inclusion criteria were no prior periodontal treatment, good systemic health, and willingness to follow oral hygiene instructions. Exclusion criteria included systemic illness, periodontitis, tobacco use, pregnancy, lactation, or recent antibiotic/steroid therapy.

Clinical procedure

Gingival and plaque status were assessed at baseline and 15 days post-treatment using the Gingival Index (Loe and Silness, 1963) and Plaque Index (Silness and Loe, 1964). All participants received full-mouth ultrasonic scaling and oral hygiene instructions.

In Groups 2 and 3, local anaesthesia (2% lignocaine with 1:80,000 adrenaline) was administered. Microneedling was performed using the Dr. Pen Ultima N2-C derma pen (12-needle disposable tip, 1.5 mm depth), applied in a stamping motion for 30-40 seconds per tooth.

In Group 3, ozonated olive oil (Ozonoid, Dentozone India) was applied with a cotton roll for 5 minutes post-microneedling, followed by periodontal dressing.

Patients were advised to avoid brushing, interdental cleaning, and consumption of hot, acidic, or hard foods for 24 hours. The control group continued routine oral hygiene.

Intergroup Analysis

Plaque Index (PI) and Gingival Index (GI) were measured at baseline and after 15 days. All groups showed statistically significant improvements (P < 0.05). PI reductions were Group 1 – 0.41, Group 2 – 0.61, and Group 3 – 0.75. GI reductions were Group 1 – 0.53, Group 2 – 0.71, and Group 3 – 0.82. Group 3 showed the greatest improvement in both indices.

Intergroup comparisons revealed that microneedling alone was significantly more effective than SRP (PI difference: 0.20 ± 0.06; GI: 0.18 ± 0.05). Microneedling with ozonated olive oil outperformed both SRP (PI: 0.34 ± 0.08; GI: 0.29 ± 0.07) and microneedling alone (PI: 0.14 ± 0.04; GI: 0.11 ± 0.03), with all differences being statistically significant.

These findings suggest that combining microneedling with ozonated olive oil provides a synergistic and more effective approach for reducing plaque and gingival inflammation.

DISCUSSION

This clinical study evaluated the efficacy of microneedling combined with ozonated olive oil as an adjunct to scaling in managing gingival inflammation. All three groups—SRP alone, microneedling, and microneedling with ozonated oil—showed significant improvements in Plaque Index (PI) and Gingival Index (GI), with the greatest reduction observed in the combination group. This suggests a synergistic effect of microneedling-enhanced absorption and ozone’s antimicrobial and healing properties.

Our results align with findings by Gandhi et al.,[4] who reported that ozonated olive oil was as effective as chlorhexidine in reducing clinical parameters and microbial load, with fewer side effects. Kaur et al.,[5] also found ozonated water effective as a subgingival irrigant, supporting ozone’s role as a beneficial adjunct in periodontal therapy. Despite the shorter duration, our study achieved similar results, likely due to the prolonged action of ozonated oil and enhanced delivery via microneedling.

Mostafa et al.[3] demonstrated improved gingival outcomes when microneedling was combined with natural oils, reinforcing our findings that microneedling enhances topical efficacy.

The antimicrobial potential of ozone is well-supported by Nagayoshi et al.[6] and Adhami et al.,[7] while Indurkar and Verma[8] confirmed ozonated oil’s effectiveness in managing gingival inflammation, further validating our results.

In conclusion, microneedling with ozonated olive oil is a promising, minimally invasive adjunctive therapy for gingival inflammation. The combination enhances clinical outcomes through improved delivery and antimicrobial action, making it a valuable addition to non-surgical periodontal treatment approaches.
