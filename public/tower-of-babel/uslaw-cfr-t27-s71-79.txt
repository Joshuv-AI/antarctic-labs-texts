27 C.F.R. § 71.79 — Suspension, revocation, or annulment.
Citation: us:cfr:27:71.79
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-71.79
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 71 (Rules Of Practice In Permit Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The administrative law judge who presides at the hearing in proceedings for the suspension, revocation and annulment of permits shall make the initial decision.
(b) If no hearing is requested, the appropriate TTB officer shall make the initial decision.
