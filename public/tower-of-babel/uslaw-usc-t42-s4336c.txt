42 U.S.C. § 4336c — Adoption of categorical exclusions
Citation: us:usc:42:4336c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section4336c
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 55 (National Environmental Policy); Subchapter I (Policies And Goals). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An agency may adopt a categorical exclusion listed in another agency’s NEPA procedures for a category of proposed agency actions for which the categorical exclusion was established consistent with this paragraph. The agency shall—
(1)
identify the categorical exclusion listed in another agency’s NEPA procedures that covers a category of proposed actions or related actions;
(2)
consult with the agency that established the categorical exclusion to ensure that the proposed adoption of the categorical exclusion to a category of actions is appropriate;
(3)
identify to the public the categorical exclusion that the agency plans to use for its proposed actions; and
(4)
document adoption of the categorical exclusion.
