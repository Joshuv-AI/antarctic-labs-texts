NLO QCD sum rules analysis of 1 − ⁣ + 1^{-+} tetraquark states
Wei-Yang Lai, Hong-Ying Jin

Abstract
We present a next-to-leading order QCD sum rule analysis of J P ​ C = 1 − ⁣ + J^{PC}=1^{-+} light tetraquark states. By investigating various compact tetraquark and molecular configurations, we determine the mass spectrum of these states. Our calculations exclude the possibility that π 1 ​ ( 1400 ) \pi_{1}(1400) is a tetraquark or hybrid-tetraquark mixture. This suggests that it may not exist, which is consistent with recent experimental results. In contrast, we obtained multiple 1 − ⁣ + 1^{-+} states around 2.0 ​ GeV 2.0\,\text{GeV} that match well with π 1 ​ ( 2015 ) \pi_{1}(2015) , which makes us confident that π 1 ​ ( 2015 ) \pi_{1}(2015) is a tetraquark candidate. As for π 1 ​ ( 1600 ) \pi_{1}(1600) , our results indicate that the tetraquark currents tend to couple to heavier states, reducing the possibility of it being a tetraquark, while earlier studies suggested the opposite.
