15 U.S.C. § 1674 — Restriction on discharge from employment by reason of garnishment
Citation: us:usc:15:1674
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1674
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 41 (Consumer Credit Protection); Subchapter II (Restrictions On Garnishment). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Termination of employment
No employer may discharge any employee by reason of the fact that his earnings have been subjected to garnishment for any one indebtedness.
(b) Penalties
Whoever willfully violates subsection (a) of this section shall be fined not more than $1,000, or imprisoned not more than one year, or both.
