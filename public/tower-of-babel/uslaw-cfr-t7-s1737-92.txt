7 C.F.R. § 1737.92 — Loan documents.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1737.92
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1737.92
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1737 (Pre-Loan Policies And Procedures Common To Insured And Guaranteed Telecommunications Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Following approval of the loan, RUS shall forward the necessary loan documents to the borrower for execution, delivery, recording, and filing, as directed by RUS. See 7 CFR part 1758 for details (or RUS Bulletins 320-4, 320-22, 321-2, 322-2, 323-1, or 326-1).
