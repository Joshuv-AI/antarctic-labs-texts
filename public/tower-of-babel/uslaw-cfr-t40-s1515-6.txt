40 C.F.R. § 1515.6 — What are CEQ's procedures for preserving records?
Citation: us:cfr:40:1515.6
Creator: Council On Environmental Quality
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1515.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1515 (Freedom Of Information Act Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) CEQ preserves records pursuant to title 44 of the United States Code and the Records Schedules issued by the Archivist of the United States.
(b) CEQ will not dispose of or destroy agency records that are the subject of a pending request, appeal, or lawsuit under the FOIA.
