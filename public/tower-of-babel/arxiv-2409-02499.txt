Sphaleron

The sphaleron energy functional consists of three components: the contribution of gauge fields, Higgs kinetic energy and effective potential
[

E_{sph}=\int dx^{3}\left[\frac{1}{4}W_{ij}^{e}W_{ij}^{e}+(D_{i}\phi)^{\dagger}(D_{i}\phi)+V_{eff}\right].

(8)

The contributions of exotic Higgs bosons (

[

\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\phi(r)=\frac{v_{0}}{\sqrt{2}}h(r)in_{e}\sigma^{e}\left(\begin{array}[]{cc}0\\
1\end{array}\right),\\
W_{i}^{e}(r)=\frac{2}{g}\epsilon^{eij}n_{j}\frac{f(r)}{r},\end{array}\right.

(9)

where
n_{i}\equiv\frac{x^{i}}{r}
and

is the radial coordinate in the spherical coordinates. The index

runs from 1 to 3.
\sigma^{e}
are Pauli matrices. The functions

are profile functions, which characterize bubbles.

From Eqs. (
8

9

[

\displaystyle E^{T}_{sph,b}=\frac{4\pi v_{b0}}{g}\int_{0}^{\infty}d\xi\
