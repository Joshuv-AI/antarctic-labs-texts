46 U.S.C. § 3712 — Notification of noncompliance
Citation: us:usc:46:3712
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section3712
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part B (Inspection And Regulation Of Vessels); Chapter 37 (Carriage Of Liquid Bulk Dangerous Cargoes). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall notify the owner, charterer, managing operator, agent, master, or individual in charge of a vessel found not to be in compliance with a regulation prescribed under this part and state how compliance may be achieved.
