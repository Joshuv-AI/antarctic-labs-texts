42 C.F.R. § 415.176 — Renal dialysis services.
Citation: us:cfr:42:415.176
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-415.176
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 415 (Services Furnished By Physicians In Providers, Supervising Physicians In Teaching Settings, And Residents In Certain Settings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In the case of renal dialysis services, physicians who are not paid under the physician monthly capitation payment method (as described in § 414.314 of this chapter) must meet the requirements of §§ 415.170 and 415.172 (concerning physician fee schedule payment for services of teaching physicians).
