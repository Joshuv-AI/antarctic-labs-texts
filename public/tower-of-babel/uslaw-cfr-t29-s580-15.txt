29 C.F.R. § 580.15 — Responsibility of the Office of Administrative Law Judges for the administrative record.
Citation: us:cfr:29:580.15
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-580.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 580 (Civil Money Penalties—Procedures For Assessing And Contesting Penalties). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon receipt of a petition seeking review of the Decision and Order of an Administrative Law Judge, the Chief Administrative Law Judge shall promptly forward a copy of the complete hearing record to the Secretary.
