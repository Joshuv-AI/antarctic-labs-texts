2 C.F.R. § 1532.1225 — When will the EPA debarring official make a decision on my reinstatement request?
Citation: us:cfr:2:1532.1225
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1532.1225
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1532 (Nonprocurement Debarment And Suspension). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The EPA debarring official will make a decision regarding your reinstatement request under § 1532.1205(a), when the administrative record is complete, and he or she can determine whether the condition giving rise to the CAA or CWA conviction has been corrected-usually within 45 days of closing the administrative record.
(b) A reinstatement request is not officially before the debarring official while you are having informal discussions under § 1532.1205(b).
