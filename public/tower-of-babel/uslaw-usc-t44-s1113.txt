44 U.S.C. § 1113 — Annual reports: exclusion of irrelevant matter
Citation: us:usc:44:1113
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section1113
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 11 (Executive And Judiciary Printing And Binding). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Executive officers, before transmitting their annual reports, shall carefully examine them and all accompanying documents, and exclude all matter, including engravings, maps, drawings, and illustrations, except such as they certify in their letters transmitting the reports are necessary and relate entirely to the transaction of the public business.
