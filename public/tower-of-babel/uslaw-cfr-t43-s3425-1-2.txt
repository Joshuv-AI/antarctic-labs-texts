43 C.F.R. § 3425.1-2 — -2 Contents of application.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3425.1-2
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3425.1-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3420 (Competitive Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No specific form of application is required. Three copies of the application, including preliminary and other data required by this subpart shall be filed. The lands applied for shall be described in accordance with subpart 3471 of this title. The application must be accompanied by the filing fee (43 CFR 3473.2).
