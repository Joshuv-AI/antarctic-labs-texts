49 U.S.C. § 41303 — Transfers of permits
Citation: us:usc:49:41303
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section41303
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part A (Air Commerce And Safety); Subpart ii (Economic Regulation); Chapter 413 (Foreign Air Transportation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A permit issued under section 41302 of this title may be transferred only when the Secretary of Transportation approves the transfer because the transfer is in the public interest.
