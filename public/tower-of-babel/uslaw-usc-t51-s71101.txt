51 U.S.C. § 71101 — Reaffirmation of policy
Citation: us:usc:51:71101
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section71101
Context: United States Code, Title 51 (National And Commercial Space Programs); Chapter 711 (Near-Earth Objects). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Congress reaffirms the policy set forth in section 20102(g) of this title (relating to surveying near-Earth asteroids and comets).
