21 C.F.R. § 571.102 — Effective date of regulation.
Citation: us:cfr:21:571.102
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 571 (Food Additive Petitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A regulation published in accordance with § 571.100(a) shall become effective upon publication in the Federal Register.
