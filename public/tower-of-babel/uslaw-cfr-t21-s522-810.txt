21 C.F.R. § 522.810 — Embutramide, chloroquine, and lidocaine solution.
Citation: us:cfr:21:522.810
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-522.810
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 522 (Implantation Or Injectable Dosage Form New Animal Drugs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Specifications. Each milliliter (mL) of solution contains 135 milligrams (mg) embutramide; 45 mg chloroquine phosphate, U.S.P.; and 1.9 mg lidocaine, U.S.P.
(b) Sponsor. See No. 069043 in § 510.600(c) of this chapter.
(c) Conditions of use in dogs—(1) Amount. One mL per 5 pounds of body weight.
(2) Indications for use. For euthanasia.
(3) Limitations. Not for use in animals intended for food. Federal law restricts this drug to use by or on the order of a licensed veterinarian.
