7 C.F.R. § 612.6 — Application for water supply forecast service.
Citation: us:cfr:7:612.6
Creator: Natural Resources Conservation Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-612.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 612 (Snow Surveys And Water Supply Forecasts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Requests for obtaining water supply forecasts or related assistance may be directed to any NRCS office in the western states areas. NRCS offices are described in part 600 of this chapter.
