7 U.S.C. § 1738h — Disbursement of environmental funds
Citation: us:usc:7:1738h
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1738h
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 41 (Food For Peace); Subchapter VI (Enterprise For The Americas Initiative). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Funds in an Environmental Fund shall be disbursed only pursuant to a framework agreement entered into pursuant to section 1738f of this title.
