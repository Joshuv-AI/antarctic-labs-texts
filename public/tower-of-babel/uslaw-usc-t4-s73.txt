4 U.S.C. § 73 — Same; removal from seat of Government
Citation: us:usc:4:73
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title4-section73
Context: United States Code, Title 4 (Flag And Seal, Seat Of Government, And The States); Chapter 3 (Seat Of The Government). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In case of the prevalence of a contagious or epidemic disease at the seat of government, the President may permit and direct the removal of any or all the public offices to such other place or places as he shall deem most safe and convenient for conducting the public business.
