47 C.F.R. § 15.401 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:47:15.401
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-15.401
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 15 (Radio Frequency Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart sets out the regulations for Unlicensed National Information Infrastructure (U-NII) devices operating in the 5.15-5.35 GHz, 5.47-5.895 GHz bands, and 5.925-7.125 GHz bands.
