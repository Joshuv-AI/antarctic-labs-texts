39 C.F.R. § 3010.103 — Procedural schedules in docketed proceedings.
Citation: us:cfr:39:3010.103
Creator: Postal Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-3010.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 3010 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Procedural schedules shall be established and may be periodically modified for each matter that is assigned a docket designation.
