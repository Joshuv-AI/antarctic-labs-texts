5 U.S.C. § 3103 — Employment at seat of Government only for services rendered
Citation: us:usc:5:3103
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section3103
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart B (Employment And Retention); Chapter 31 (Authority For Employment); Subchapter I (Employment Authorities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An individual may be employed in the civil service in an Executive department at the seat of Government only for services actually rendered in connection with and for the purposes of the appropriation from which he is paid. An individual who violates this section shall be removed from the service.
