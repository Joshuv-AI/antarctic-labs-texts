15 C.F.R. § 922.85 — Review of State permits and leases for certain aquaculture projects.
Citation: us:cfr:15:922.85
Creator: National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-922.85
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 922 (National Marine Sanctuary Program Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

NOAA has described in a Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) with the State of California how the State will consult and coordinate with NOAA to review any new, amended or expanded lease or permit application for aquaculture projects in Tomales Bay involving introduced species.
