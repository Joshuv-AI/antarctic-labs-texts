10 C.F.R. § 719.51 — What information must be forwarded to the General Counsel's Office concerning contractor submissions to Department Counsel under this part?
Citation: us:cfr:10:719.51
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-719.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 719 (Contractor Legal Management Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Department Counsel must submit through the General Counsel reporting system, the reviewed costs and status updates for all matters involving retained counsel, including but not limited to contractor litigation. The reports are to be received by the 15th day of the month following the end of each quarter of the fiscal year.
