51 U.S.C. § 60503 — Reauthorization of Glory Mission
Citation: us:usc:51:60503
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section60503
Context: United States Code, Title 51 (National And Commercial Space Programs); Chapter 605 (Earth Science). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Congress reauthorizes the Administration to continue with development of the Glory Mission, which will examine how aerosols and solar energy affect the Earth’s climate.
