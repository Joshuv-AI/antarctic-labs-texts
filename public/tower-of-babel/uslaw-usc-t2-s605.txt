2 U.S.C. § 605 — Sale or lease of property, supplies, or services
Citation: us:usc:2:605
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section605
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 17 (Congressional Budget Office). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Any sale or lease of property, supplies, or services to the Congressional Budget Office shall be deemed to be a sale or lease to the Congress subject to section 4103 of this title.
(b)
Subsection (a) shall apply with respect to fiscal years beginning after September 30, 1996.
