35 U.S.C. § 329 — Appeal
Citation: us:usc:35:329
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title35-section329
Context: United States Code, Title 35 (Patents); Part III (Patents And Protection Of Patent Rights); Chapter 32 (Post-Grant Review). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A party dissatisfied with the final written decision of the Patent Trial and Appeal Board under section 328(a) may appeal the decision pursuant to sections 141 through 144. Any party to the post-grant review shall have the right to be a party to the appeal.
