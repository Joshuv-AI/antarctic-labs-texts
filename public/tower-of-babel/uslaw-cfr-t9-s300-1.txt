9 C.F.R. § 300.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:9:300.1
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-300.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 300 (Agency Mission And Organization). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part describes the duties and organization of the Food Safety and Inspection Service (FSIS), an agency of the United States Department of Agriculture (USDA). It also includes rules on the access of government employees to regulated places of business.
