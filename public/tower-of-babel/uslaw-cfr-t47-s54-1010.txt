47 C.F.R. § 54.1010 — Record retention for Mobility Fund Phase I.
Citation: us:cfr:47:54.1010
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-54.1010
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 54 (Universal Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A winning bidder authorized to receive Mobility Fund Phase I support and its agents are required to retain any documentation prepared for, or in connection with, the award of Mobility Fund Phase I support for a period of not less than ten (10) years after the date on which the winning bidder receives its final disbursement of Mobility Fund Phase I support.
