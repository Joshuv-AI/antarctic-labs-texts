Critical curve for weakly coupled system of semilinear Euler-Poisson-Darboux-Tricomi equations
Yuequn Li, Fei Guo

Abstract
. This paper investigates a weakly coupled system of semilinear Euler-Poisson-Darboux-Tricomi equations (EPDTS) with power-type nonlinear terms. More precisely, in the case where the damping terms dominate over the mass terms, the critical curve in the p − q p-q plane that delineates the threshold between global existence and blow-up for the EPDTS is given by Γ m ​ ( n , p , q , β 1 , β 2 ) = 0 , \Gamma_{m}(n,p,q,\beta_{1},\beta_{2})=0, where Γ m \Gamma_{m} is defined by ( 1.16 ). Through the construction of new test functions, the blow-up problem is addressed when Γ m ​ ( n , p , q , β 1 , β 2 ) ≥ 0 \Gamma_{m}(n,p,q,\beta_{1},\beta_{2})\geq 0 . Based on the ( L 1 ∩ L 2 ) − L 2 (L^{1}\cap L^{2})-L^{2} estimates of the solution to the corresponding linear equation established in our previous work [ 17 ] , we derive the global existence of solutions with small initial data when Γ m ​ ( n , p , q , β 1 , β 2 ) < 0 \Gamma_{m}(n,p,q,\beta_{1},\beta_{2})<0 , provided that the damping terms prevail over the mass terms.



Keywords
: Semilinear Euler-Poisson-Darboux-Tricomi equations; Weakly coupled system; Critical curve; Test functions;
(L^{1}\cap L^{2})-L^{2}
estimates.

Introduction

This paper investigates the critical curve problem for the weakly coupled system of semilinear Euler-Poisson-Darboux-Tricomi equations

\left\{\begin{aligned} &\partial_{t}^{2}u-t^{2m}\Delta u+\frac{\mu_{1}}{t}\partial_{t}u+\frac{\nu_{1}^{2}}{t^{2}}u=|v|^{p},&\quad&t>1,\ x\in\mathbb{R}^{n},\\
&\partial_{t}^{2}v-t^{2m}\Delta v+\frac{\mu_{2}}{t}\partial_{t}v+\frac{\nu_{2}^{2}}{t^{2}}v=|u|^{q},&\quad&t>1,\ x\in\mathbb{R}^{n},\\
&(u,\partial_{t}u,v,\partial_{t}v)(1,x)=(u_{0},u_{1},v_{0},v_{1})(x),&\quad&x\in\mathbb{R}^{n},\end{aligned}\right.

(1.1)

where
m>-1
,
n\geq 1
denotes the space dimension,
\mu_{i},\nu_{i}^{2}
,

are nonnegative numbers and
p,q>1
. We find that there exists a curve related to
\mu_{i},\nu_{i}
,
i=1,2
in
p-q

1.1

The critical exponent for the classical semilinear wave equation

\partial_{t}^{2}u-\Delta u=|u|^{p}

(1.2)

p_{S}(n)

denotes the space dimension. Namely, local solutions with small initial data exist globally when
p>p_{S}(n)
and the blow-up phenomenon occurs if
1<p\leq p_{S}(n)
no matter how small the initial data may be. Here
p_{S}(1)=+\infty
, and for
n\geq 2
,
p_{S}(n)
is the positive root of the quadratic equation

(n-1)p^{2}-(n+1)p-2=0,

(1.3)

see
[

,
8
,
10
,

,
15
,
31
,
33
,
35
,
37
,
38

[

\left\{\begin{aligned} &\partial_{t}^{2}u-\Delta u=|v|^{p},&\quad&t>0,\ x\in\mathbb{R}^{n},\\
&\partial_{t}^{2}v-\Delta v=|u|^{q},&\quad&t>0,\ x\in\mathbb{R}^{n},\\
&(u,\partial_{t}u,v,\partial_{t}v)(0,x)=(u_{0},u_{1},v_{0},v_{1})(x),&\quad&x\in\mathbb{R}^{n}\end{aligned}\right.

(1.4)

with
p,q>1

\Gamma_{W}(n,p,q)=0
, where

\Gamma_{W}(n,p,q):=\max\Big\{\frac{p+2+q^{-1}}{pq-1},\frac{q+2+p^{-1}}{pq-1}\Big\}-\frac{n-1}{2}.

(1.5)

Roughly speaking, the small data global solutions to (
1.4

\Gamma_{W}(n,p,q)<0

\Gamma_{W}(n,p,q)\geq 0

Observe that
\Gamma_{W}(n,p,p)=0
if and only if (
1.3

1.4

However, the study of coupled system (
1.4

1.2

\Gamma_{W}(n,p,q)\geq 0
expands the blow-up range compared to the single case:
1<p\leq p_{S}(n),1<q\leq p_{S}(n)
. In fact, note that

1<p\leq p_{S}(n)\iff\frac{1+p^{-1}}{p-1}\geq\frac{n-1}{2}

(1.6)

\max\Big\{\frac{p+2+q^{-1}}{pq-1},\frac{q+2+p^{-1}}{pq-1}\Big\}\leq\max\Big\{\frac{1+p^{-1}}{p-1},\frac{1+q^{-1}}{q-1}\Big\}

(1.7)

1.6

1.7

p=q
. This observation indicates that there may exist some
p
or
q

p_{S}(n)
such that
\Gamma_{W}(n,p,q)\geq 0
(the blow-up region). For example, for
n=3
, choosing
p=2<p_{S}(3)=1+\sqrt{2}<q=2.5
, a simple calculation yields
\Gamma_{W}(3,2,2.5)=0.25>0

Consider the weakly coupled system of semilinear damped wave equations

\left\{\begin{aligned} &\partial_{t}^{2}u-\Delta u+b(t)\partial_{t}u=|v|^{p},&\quad&t>0,\ x\in\mathbb{R}^{n},\ p>1,\\
&\partial_{t}^{2}v-\Delta v+b(t)\partial_{t}v=|u|^{q},&\quad&t>0,\ x\in\mathbb{R}^{n},\ q>1,\\

(1.8)

If

\Gamma_{DW}(n,p,q):=\max\Big\{\frac{p+1}{pq-1},\frac{q+1}{pq-1}\Big\}-\frac{n}{2},

highlighting that the appearance of the damping terms

For the weakly coupled system of semilinear wave equations with scale-invariant damping and mass

\left\{\begin{aligned} &\partial_{t}^{2}u-\Delta u+\frac{\mu_{1}}{t}\partial_{t}u+\frac{\nu_{1}^{2}}{t^{2}}u=|v|^{p},&\quad&t>1,\ x\in\mathbb{R}^{n},\ p>1,\\
&\partial_{t}^{2}v-\Delta v+\frac{\mu_{2}}{t}\partial_{t}v+\frac{\nu_{2}^{2}}{t^{2}}v=|u|^{q},&\quad&t>1,\ x\in\mathbb{R}^{n},\ q>1,\\
&(u,v,\partial_{t}u,\partial_{t}v)(1,x)=(u_{0},u_{1},v_{0},v_{1})(x),&\quad&x\in\mathbb{R}^{n}.\end{aligned}\right.

(1.10)

Chen-Palmieri
[
2
]
showed that, in the case of

\delta_{i}:=(\mu_{i}-1)^{2}-4\nu_{i}^{2}\geq(n+1)^{2},\ i=1,2,

(1.11)

the critical curve for (
1.10
) is given by
\Gamma_{0}(n,p,q,\beta_{1},\beta_{2})=0
, where

\Gamma_{0}(n,p,q,\beta_{1},\beta_{2}):=\max\Big\{\frac{p+1}{pq-1}-\frac{\beta_{1}-1}{2},\frac{q+1}{pq-1}-\frac{\beta_{2}-1}{2}\Big\}-\frac{n}{2},

(1.12)

where
\beta_{i}=\frac{\mu_{i}+1-\sqrt{\delta_{i}}}{2},i=1,2
.
Clearly, the study of the critical curve for (
1.10
) is based on the corresponding single power-type semilinear wave equation

\partial_{t}^{2}u-\Delta u+\frac{\mu}{t}\partial_{t}u+\frac{\nu^{2}}{t^{2}}u=|u|^{p}.

(1.13)

Since the linear equation of (
1.13
) is invariant under the transformation
\tilde{u}(\lambda t,\lambda x)
, the damping term
\frac{\mu}{t}\partial_{t}u
and the mass term
\frac{\nu^{2}}{t^{2}}u
can be viewed as having the same scaling. Following Palmieri’s analysis, this balance induces an interaction where the relative sizes of
\mu
and
\nu^{2}
play a decisive role in determining whether the damping term or the mass term prevails in (
1.13
). In fact, the magnitude of the number
\delta:=(\mu-1)^{2}-4\nu^{2}
can characterize this interaction. Specifically, Nascimento-Palmieri-Reissig
[
22
,
26
,
29
]
proved that for any
\delta\geq 0,
the local (in time) solutions to (
1.13
) blow up in finite time when
1<p\leq p_{F}(n+\frac{\mu-1-\sqrt{\delta}}{2})
by means of the test function approach. They then established the decay estimates of solutions to the corresponding linear equation of (
1.13
) through the application of Fourier analysis techniques, thus gave that the solutions to (
1.13
) exist globally if
p>p_{F}(n+\frac{\mu-1-\sqrt{\delta}}{2})
for any
\delta\geq(n+2\sigma-1)^{2}
, where
\sigma
is a positive number describing the regularity of the initial data, and
p_{F}
is Fujita exponent mentioned above. Roughly speaking, when the damping term dominates over the mass term, in other words,
\delta
is relatively large, (
1.13
) is parabolic-like as
p_{F}(n+\frac{\mu-1-\sqrt{\delta}}{2})
is its critical exponent. In
[
30
]
, Palmieri-Tu also proved the blow-up phenomenon if
1<p\leq p_{S}(n+\mu)
for any
\delta\geq 0
. Palmieri-Reissig
[
28
]
proved that the solution to (
1.13
) also blows up in finite time when
1<p\leq\max\{p_{F}(n+\frac{\mu-1-\sqrt{\delta}}{2}),p_{S}(n+\mu)\}
. Moreover, for other ranges of
\delta
, partial results can be found in
[
3
,
22
,
26
,
28
,
29
,
30
]
. From the above results, it is clear that the value of
\delta
plays a significant role in the interplay between the Strauss exponent, the Fujita exponent, and their balance. It is also worth mentioning that the critical curve for (
1.10
) is not merely a straightforward extension of the corresponding results for the single case (
1.13
), and this perspective is explained comprehensively by Chen-Palmieri in
[
2
]
.

In our recent paper
[
17
]
, we studied the single Euler-Poisson-Darboux-Tricomi equation with a power nonlinearity

\left\{\begin{aligned} &\partial_{t}^{2}u-t^{2m}\Delta u+\frac{\mu}{t}\partial_{t}u+\frac{\nu^{2}}{t^{2}}u=|u|^{p},&\quad&t>1,\ m>-1,\\
&u(1,x)=u_{0}(x),\ \partial_{t}u(1,x)=u_{1}(x),&\quad&x\in\mathbb{R}^{n}.\end{aligned}\right.

(1.14)

By applying Fourier analysis methods, the corresponding linear problem

\partial_{t}^{2}u-t^{2m}\Delta u+\frac{\mu}{t}\partial_{t}u+\frac{\nu^{2}}{t^{2}}u=0

(1.15)

1.14

1.14

p>p_{F}((m+1)n+\frac{\mu-1-\sqrt{\delta}}{2})
in the case of
\delta\geq(m+1)^{2}(n+2\sigma-1)^{2}
, where
\sigma>0
.
Combining this with the blow-up result established by Palmieri
[
27
]

1<p\leq p_{F}((m+1)n+\frac{\mu-1-\sqrt{\delta}}{2})
for any
\delta\geq 0

is the critical exponent of (

\delta
is large to a certain extent. As shown in
[
17
,
27
]
,
\delta
continues to serve as the measure of the interaction between the damping term and mass term in (
1.14

Comparing the blow-up results of the wave equation (
1.13

(i=1,2)

\Gamma_{m}(n,p,q,\beta_{1},\beta_{2}):=\max\Big\{\frac{p+1}{pq-1}-\frac{\beta_{1}-1}{2},\frac{q+1}{pq-1}-\frac{\beta_{2}-1}{2}\Big\}-\frac{(m+1)n}{2}

(1.16)

1.12

\Gamma_{m}(n,p,q,\beta_{1},\beta_{2})<0,

(1.17)

Through our main results, we also conclude that the study of the weakly coupled system (
