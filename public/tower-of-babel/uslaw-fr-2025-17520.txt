Minnesota: Final Approval of State Underground Storage Tank 
Program Revisions, Codification, and Incorporation by Reference

AGENCY: Environmental Protection Agency (EPA).

ACTION: Proposed rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to the Solid Waste Disposal Act of 1965, as amended 
(commonly known as the Resource Conservation and Recovery Act (RCRA)), 
the Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) is proposing to approve 
revisions to the State of Minnesota's Underground Storage Tank (UST) 
program submitted by the Minnesota Pollution Control Agency. This 
action also proposes to codify EPA's approval of Minnesota's state 
program and incorporate by reference those provisions of Minnesota's 
regulations that EPA has determined meet the requirements for approval. 
The provisions will be subject to EPA's inspection and enforcement 
authorities under RCRA Subtitle I and other applicable statutory and 
regulatory provisions.
