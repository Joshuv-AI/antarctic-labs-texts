40 C.F.R. § 413.60 — Applicability: Description of the chemical etching and milling subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:413.60
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-413.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 413 (Electroplating Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart apply to discharges of process wastewaters resulting from the chemical milling or etching of ferrous or nonferrous materials.
