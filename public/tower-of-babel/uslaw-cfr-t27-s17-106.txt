27 C.F.R. § 17.106 — Consents of surety.
Citation: us:cfr:27:17.106
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-17.106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 17 (Drawback On Taxpaid Distilled Spirits Used In Manufacturing Nonbeverage Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The principal and surety shall execute on form TTB F 5000.18, Change in Bond (Consent of Surety), any consents of surety to changes in the terms of bonds. Form TTB F 5000.18 shall be executed with the same formality and proof of authority as is required for the execution of bonds.
