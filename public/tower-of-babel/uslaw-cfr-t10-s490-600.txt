10 C.F.R. § 490.600 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:10:490.600
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-490.600
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 490 (Alternative Fuel Transportation Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart sets forth the rules applicable to investigations under titles III, IV, V, and VI of the Act and to enforcement of sections 501, 503(b), 507, 508, or 514 of the Act, or any regulation issued under such sections.
