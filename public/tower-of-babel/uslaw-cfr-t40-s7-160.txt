40 C.F.R. § 7.160 — Burden of proof.
Citation: us:cfr:40:7.160
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-7.160
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 7 (Nondiscrimination In Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Assistance From The Environmental Protection Agency). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The burden of proving that an age distinction or other action falls within the exceptions outlined in §§ 7.150 and 7.155 is on the recipient of EPA financial assistance.
