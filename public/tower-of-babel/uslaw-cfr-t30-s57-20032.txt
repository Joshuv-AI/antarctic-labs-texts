30 C.F.R. § 57.20032 — Two-way communication equipment for underground operations.
Citation: us:cfr:30:57.20032
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-57.20032
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 57 (Safety And Health Standards—Underground Metal And Nonmetal Mines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Telephones or other two-way communication equipment with instructions for their use shall be provided for communication from underground operations to the surface.
