14 C.F.R. § 17.41 — Final orders.
Citation: us:cfr:14:17.41
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-17.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 17 (Procedures For Protests And Contract Disputes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All final FAA orders regarding protests or contract disputes under this part are to be issued by the FAA Administrator or by a delegee of the Administrator.
