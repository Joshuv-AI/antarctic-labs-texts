7 C.F.R. § 1051.11 — California quota program.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1051 (Milk In The California Milk Marketing Area). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

California Quota Program means the applicable provisions of the California Food and Agriculture Code, and related provisions of the pooling plan administered by the California Department of Food and Agriculture (CDFA).
