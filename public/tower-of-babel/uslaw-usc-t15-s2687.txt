15 U.S.C. § 2687 — Regulations
Citation: us:usc:15:2687
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section2687
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 53 (Toxic Substances Control); Subchapter IV (Lead Exposure Reduction). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The regulations of the Administrator under this subchapter shall include such recordkeeping and reporting requirements as may be necessary to insure the effective implementation of this subchapter. The regulations may be amended from time to time as necessary.
