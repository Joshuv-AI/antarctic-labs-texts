41 C.F.R. § 102-34.200 — -34.200 Government motor vehicle official use.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-34.200
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-34.200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-34 (34—Motor Vehicle Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Official use of a Government motor vehicle is using a Government motor vehicle to perform your agency's mission(s), as authorized by your agency.
