21 C.F.R. § 169.177 — Vanilla flavoring.
Citation: us:cfr:21:169.177
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-169.177
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 169 (Food Dressings And Flavorings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Vanilla flavoring conforms to the definition and standard of identity and is subject to any requirement for label statement of ingredients prescribed for vanilla extract by § 169.175, except that its content of ethyl alcohol is less than 35 percent by volume.
(b) The specified name of the food is “Vanilla flavoring”.
