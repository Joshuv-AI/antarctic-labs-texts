7 U.S.C. § 1981f — Underwriting forms and standards
Citation: us:usc:7:1981f
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1981f
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 50 (Agricultural Credit); Subchapter IV (Administrative Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In the administration of this chapter, the Secretary shall, to the extent practicable, use underwriting forms, standards, practices, and terminology similar to the forms, standards, practices, and terminology used by lenders in the private sector.
