5 C.F.R. § 919.700 — When may the suspending official issue a suspension?
Citation: us:cfr:5:919.700
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-919.700
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 919 (Governmentwide Debarment And Suspension (Nonprocurement)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Suspension is a serious action. Using the procedures of this subpart and subpart F of this part, the suspending official may impose suspension only when that official determines that—
(a) There exists an indictment for, or other adequate evidence to suspect, an offense listed under § 919.800(a), or
(b) There exists adequate evidence to suspect any other cause for debarment listed under § 919.800(b) through (d); and
(c) Immediate action is necessary to protect the public interest.
