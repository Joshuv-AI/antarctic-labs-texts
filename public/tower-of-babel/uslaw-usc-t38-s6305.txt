38 U.S.C. § 6305 — Outstationing of counseling and outreach personnel
Citation: us:usc:38:6305
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section6305
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part IV (General Administrative Provisions); Chapter 63 (Outreach Activities); Subchapter I (Outreach Services Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may station employees of the Department at locations other than Department offices, including educational institutions, to provide—
(1)
counseling and other assistance regarding benefits under this title to veterans and other persons eligible for benefits under this title; and
(2)
outreach services under this subchapter.
