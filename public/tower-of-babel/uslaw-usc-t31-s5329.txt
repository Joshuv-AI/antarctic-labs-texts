31 U.S.C. § 5329 — Staff commentaries
Citation: us:usc:31:5329
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section5329
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 53 (Monetary Transactions); Subchapter II (Records And Reports On Monetary Instruments Transactions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall—
(1)
publish all written rulings interpreting this subchapter; and
(2)
annually issue a staff commentary on the regulations issued under this subchapter.
