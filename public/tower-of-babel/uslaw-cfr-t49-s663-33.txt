49 C.F.R. § 663.33 — Description of post-delivery audit.
Citation: us:cfr:49:663.33
Creator: Federal Transit Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-663.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 663 (Pre-Award And Post-Delivery Audits Of Rolling Stock Purchases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A post-delivery audit under this part includes—
(a) A post-delivery Buy America certification as described in § 663.35 of this part;
(b) A post-delivery purchaser's requirements certification as described in § 663.37 of this part; and
(c) When appropriate, a manufacturer's Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard self-certification information as described in § 663.41 or § 663.43 of this part.
