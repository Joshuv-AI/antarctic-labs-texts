18 U.S.C. § 3072 — Determination of entitlement; maximum amount; Presidential approval; conclusiveness
Citation: us:usc:18:3072

Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part II (Criminal Procedure); Chapter 204 (Rewards For Information Concerning Terrorist Acts And Espionage). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Attorney General shall determine whether an individual furnishing information described in section 3071 is entitled to a reward and the amount to be paid.
