43 C.F.R. § 3809.200 — What kinds of agreements may BLM and a State make under this subpart?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3809.200
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3809.200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3800 (Mining Claims Under The General Mining Laws). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To prevent unnecessary administrative delay and to avoid duplication of administration and enforcement, BLM and a State may make the following kinds of agreements:
(a) An agreement to provide for a joint Federal/State program; and
(b) An agreement under § 3809.202 which provides that, in place of BLM administration, BLM defers to State administration of some or all of the requirements of this subpart subject to the limitations in § 3809.203.
