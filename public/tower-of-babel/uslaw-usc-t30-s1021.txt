30 U.S.C. § 1021 — Federal exemption from State water laws
Citation: us:usc:30:1021
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section1021
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 23 (Geothermal Resources). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall constitute an express or implied claim or denial on the part of the Federal Government as to its exemption from State water laws.
