17 C.F.R. § 144.3 — Testimony by present or former Commission employees.
Citation: us:cfr:17:144.3
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-144.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 144 (Procedures Regarding The Disclosure Of Information And The Testimony Of Present Or Former Officers And Employees In Response To Subpoenas Or Other Demands Of A Court). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) In any proceeding to which the Commission is not a party, an employee of the Commission shall not testify concerning matters related to the business of the Commission unless authorized to do so by the Commission upon the advice of the General Counsel.
(b) In any proceeding, an employee or former employee of the Commission shall not testify concerning non-public matters related to the business of the Commission unless authorized to do so by the Commission upon the advice of the General Counsel. See § 140.735-9 of these regulations.
