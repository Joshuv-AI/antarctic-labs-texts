41 C.F.R. § 102-39.5 — -39.5 Scope.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-39 (39—Replacement Of Personal Property Pursuant To The Exchange/Sale Authority). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part covers the exchange/sale authority and applies to all personal property owned by executive agencies worldwide. Use of “you” throughout this part refers to executive agencies.
