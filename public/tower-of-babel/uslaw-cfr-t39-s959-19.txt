39 C.F.R. § 959.19 — Witness fees.
Citation: us:cfr:39:959.19
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-959.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 959 (Private Express Statutes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Postal Service does not pay fees and expenses for a respondent's witnesses or for depositions requested by a respondent.
