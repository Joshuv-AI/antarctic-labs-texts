43 C.F.R. § 3834.11 — Which fees must I pay to maintain a mining claim or site and when do I pay them?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3834.11
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3834.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3834 (Required Fees For Mining Claims Or Sites). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For all mining claims or sites—paying the maintenance fee(s) in lieu of performing assessment work satisfies the requirements of the mining law and FLPMA. See § 3830.21 of this chapter for fee amounts.
(a) Location fee and initial maintenance fee. When you first record a mining claim or site with the BLM, you must pay a location fee and an initial maintenance fee for the assessment year in which you located the mining claim or site.
(b) Annual maintenance fee. You must pay an annual maintenance fee on or before September 1st of each year in order to maintain a mining claim or site for the upcoming assessment year.
