50 C.F.R. § 13.4 — Emergency variation from requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:50:13.4
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-13.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 13 (General Permit Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director may approve variations from the requirements of this part when he finds that an emergency exists and that the proposed variations will not hinder effective administration of this subchapter B, and will not be unlawful.
