40 C.F.R. § 610.35 — Driveability and performance tests.
Citation: us:cfr:40:610.35
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-610.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 610 (Fuel Economy Retrofit Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the Administrator determines that driveability and performance of a vehicle may be adversely affected by the use of a device, a number of automobiles to be determined by the Administrator will be subjected to the driveability and performance tests discussed in §§ 610.62 and 610.63, respectively.
