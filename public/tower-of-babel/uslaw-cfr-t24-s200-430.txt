24 C.F.R. § 200.430 — Sanctions.
Citation: us:cfr:24:200.430
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-200.430
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 200 (Introduction To Fha Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Failure or refusal to comply and give satisfactory assurances of future compliance with the requirements of this subpart shall be proper basis for applying sanctions. The sanctions shall be applied in accordance with the provisions of Executive Order 11246 and the relevant regulations of the Secretary of Labor.
