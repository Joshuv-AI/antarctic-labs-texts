12 C.F.R. § 1080.12 — Orders requiring witnesses to testify or provide other information and granting immunity.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1080.12
Creator: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1080.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1080 (Rules Relating To Investigations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director has the nondelegable authority to request approval from the Attorney General of the United States for the issuance of an order requiring a witness to testify or provide other information and granting immunity under 18 U.S.C. 6004.
