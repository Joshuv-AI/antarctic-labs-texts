28 C.F.R. § 814.8 — Representation at the hearing.
Citation: us:cfr:28:814.8
Creator: Court Services And Offender Supervision Agency For The District Of Columbia
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-814.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 814 (Salary Offset Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The creditor agency may be represented by legal counsel.
(b) The employee may be self-represented or may be represented by an individual of the employee's choosing, at the employee's expense.
