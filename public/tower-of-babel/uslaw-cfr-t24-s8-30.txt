24 C.F.R. § 8.30 — Rental rehabilitation program.
Citation: us:cfr:24:8.30
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-8.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 8 (Nondiscrimination Based On Handicap In Federally Assisted Programs And Activities Of The Department Of Housing And Urban Development). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each grantee or state recipient in the rental rehabilitation program shall, subject to the priority in 24 CFR 511.10(l) and in accordance with other requirements in 24 CFR part 511, give priority to the selection of projects that will result in dwelling units being made readily accessible to and usable by individuals with handicaps.
