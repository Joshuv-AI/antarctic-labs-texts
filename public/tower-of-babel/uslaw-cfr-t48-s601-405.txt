48 C.F.R. § 601.405 — 601.405 Deviations pertaining to treaties and executive agreements.
Citation: us:cfr:48:601.405
Creator: Chapter 6—Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-601.405
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 601 (Department Of State Acquisition Regulations System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Procurement Executive shall determine whether a deviation pertaining to treaties and executive agreements is authorized under FAR 1.405 or that a request for deviation is required under FAR 1.405(e).
