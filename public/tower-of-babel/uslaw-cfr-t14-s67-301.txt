14 C.F.R. § 67.301 — Eligibility.
Citation: us:cfr:14:67.301
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-67.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 67 (Medical Standards And Certification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To be eligible for a third-class airman medical certificate, or to remain eligible for a third-class airman medical certificate, a person must meet the requirements of this subpart.
