34 C.F.R. § 101.12 — Authority for representation.
Citation: us:cfr:34:101.12
Creator: Office For Civil Rights, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-101.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 101 (Practice And Procedure For Hearings Under Part 100 Of This Title). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any individual acting in a representative capacity in any proceeding may be required to show his authority to act in such capacity.
