2 C.F.R. § 1128.100 — Purpose of FMS Article I.
Citation: us:cfr:2:1128.100
Creator: Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1128.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1128 (Recipient Financial And Program Management: General Award Terms And Conditions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

FMS Article I specifies standards for recipients' financial management systems. It thereby implements OMB guidance in:
(a) 2 CFR 200.302, 200.303, and 200.328; and
(b) 2 CFR 200.301 and 200.328, as they relate to associations between financial data and performance accomplishments and reporting.
