13 C.F.R. § 315.3 — Confidential Business Information.
Citation: us:cfr:13:315.3
Creator: Economic Development Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-315.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 315 (Trade Adjustment Assistance For Firms). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

EDA will follow the procedures set forth in 15 CFR 4.9 for the submission of Confidential Business Information. Submitters should clearly mark and designate as confidential any Confidential Business Information.
