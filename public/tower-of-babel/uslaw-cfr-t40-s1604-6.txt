40 C.F.R. § 1604.6 — Public availability of accidental release records.
Citation: us:cfr:40:1604.6
Creator: Chemical Safety And Hazard Investigation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1604.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1604 (Reporting Of Accidental Releases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Accidental release records collected by the CSB under this part may be obtained by making a request in accordance with 40 CFR part 1601, the CSB's procedures for the disclosure of records under the Freedom of Information Act. The CSB will process requests, and if appropriate, disclose such records, in accordance with 40 CFR part 1601 and relevant Federal information disclosure laws.
