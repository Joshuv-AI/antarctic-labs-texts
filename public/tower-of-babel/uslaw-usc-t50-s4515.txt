50 U.S.C. § 4515 — Presidential power to ration gasoline among classes of end-users unaffected
Citation: us:usc:50:4515
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section4515
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 55 (Defense Production); Subchapter I (Priorities And Allocations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to authorize the President to institute, without the approval of the Congress, a program for the rationing of gasoline among classes of end-users.
