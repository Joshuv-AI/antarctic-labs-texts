14 C.F.R. § 252.8 — Extent of smoking restrictions.
Citation: us:cfr:14:252.8
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-252.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 252 (Smoking Aboard Aircraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The restrictions on smoking described in §§ 252.4 and 252.5 shall apply to all locations within the aircraft.
