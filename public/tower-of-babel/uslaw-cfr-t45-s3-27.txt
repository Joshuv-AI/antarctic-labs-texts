45 C.F.R. § 3.27 — Bicycles.
Citation: us:cfr:45:3.27
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-3.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 3 (Conduct Of Persons And Traffic On The National Institutes Of Health Federal Enclave). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A person may not operate a bicycle, motorbike, or similar vehicle without a horn or other warning device, and, if the vehicle is operated between dusk and dawn, it must be equipped with an operating headlight, and taillight or reflector.
