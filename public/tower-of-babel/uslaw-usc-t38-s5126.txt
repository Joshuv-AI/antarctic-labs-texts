38 U.S.C. § 5126 — Benefits not to be denied based on lack of mailing address
Citation: us:usc:38:5126
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section5126
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part IV (General Administrative Provisions); Chapter 51 (Claims, Effective Dates, And Payments); Subchapter III (Payment Of Benefits). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Benefits under laws administered by the Secretary may not be denied a claimant on the basis that the claimant does not have a mailing address.
