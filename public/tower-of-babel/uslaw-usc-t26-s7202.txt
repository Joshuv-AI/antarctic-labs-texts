26 U.S.C. § 7202 — Willful failure to collect or pay over tax
Citation: us:usc:26:7202
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section7202
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 75 (Crimes, Other Offenses, And Forfeitures); Subchapter A (Crimes); Part I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any person required under this title to collect, account for, and pay over any tax imposed by this title who willfully fails to collect or truthfully account for and pay over such tax shall, in addition to other penalties provided by law, be guilty of a felony and, upon conviction thereof, shall be fined not more than $10,000, or imprisoned not more than 5 years, or both, together with the costs of prosecution.
