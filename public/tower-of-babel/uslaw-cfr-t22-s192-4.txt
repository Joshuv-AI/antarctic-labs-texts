22 C.F.R. § 192.4 — Notification of eligible persons.
Citation: us:cfr:22:192.4
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-192.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 192 (Victims Of Terrorism Compensation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director General of the Foreign Service for the Department of State, or other Agency Head in domestic situations, shall be responsible for notifying each individual determined to be eligible for benefits under the Act, or if that person is not available, a representative or family member of the eligible individual.
