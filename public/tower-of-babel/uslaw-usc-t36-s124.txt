36 U.S.C. § 124 — National Freedom Day
Citation: us:usc:36:124
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section124
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part A (Observances And Ceremonies); Chapter 1 (Patriotic And National Observances). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President may issue each year a proclamation designating February 1 as National Freedom Day to commemorate the signing by Abraham Lincoln on February 1, 1865, of the joint resolution adopted by the Senate and the House of Representatives that proposed the 13th amendment to the Constitution.
