46 C.F.R. § 386.9 — Gambling.
Citation: us:cfr:46:386.9
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-386.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 386 (Regulations Governing Public Buildings And Grounds At The United States Merchant Marine Academy). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless permitted by Executive or Department of Transportation Order, participating in games of chance for money or other consideration, or in the operation of gambling devices, or the conduct of a lottery or a pool, or the selling or purchasing of numbers tickets, is prohibited on Academy property.
