45 C.F.R. § 92.210 — Nondiscrimination in the use of patient care decision support tools.
Citation: us:cfr:45:92.210
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-92.210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 92 (Nondiscrimination In Health Programs Or Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) General prohibition. A covered entity must not discriminate on the basis of race, color, national origin, sex, age, or disability in its health programs or activities through the use of patient care decision support tools.
(b) Identification of risk. A covered entity has an ongoing duty to make reasonable efforts to identify uses of patient care decision support tools in its health programs or activities that employ input variables or factors that measure race, color, national origin, sex, age, or disability.
(c) Mitigation of risk. For each patient care decision support tool identified in paragraph (b) of this section, a covered entity must make reasonable efforts to mitigate the risk of discrimination resulting from the tool's use in its health programs or activities.
