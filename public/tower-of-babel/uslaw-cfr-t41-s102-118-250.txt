41 C.F.R. § 102-118.250 — -118.250 Interest applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-118.250
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-118.250
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-118 (118—Transportation Payment And Audit). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Government can charge interest on an amount due from a TSP. This procedure is provided for within the Debt Collection Act (31 U.S.C. 3717), the Federal Claims Collection Standards (31 CFR chapter IX), and 41 CFR part 105-55.
