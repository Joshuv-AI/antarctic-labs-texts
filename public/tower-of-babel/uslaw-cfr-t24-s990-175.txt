24 C.F.R. § 990.175 — Utilities expense level: Computation of the current consumption level.
Citation: us:cfr:24:990.175
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-990.175
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 990 (The Public Housing Operating Fund Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The current consumption level shall be the actual amount of each utility consumed during the 12-month period ending June 30th that is 6 months prior to the first day of the applicable funding period.
