48 U.S.C. § 744 — Coasting trade laws
Citation: us:usc:48:744
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section744
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 4 (Puerto Rico); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The coasting trade between Puerto Rico and the United States shall be regulated in accordance with the provisions of law applicable to such trade between any two great coasting districts of the United States.
