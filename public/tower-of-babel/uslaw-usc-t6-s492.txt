6 U.S.C. § 492 — Function
Citation: us:usc:6:492
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title6-section492
Context: United States Code, Title 6 (Domestic Security); Chapter 1 (Homeland Security Organization); Subchapter IX (National Homeland Security Council). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The function of the Council shall be to advise the President on homeland security matters.
