12 C.F.R. § 160.36 — De minimis investments.
Citation: us:cfr:12:160.36
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-160.36
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 160 (Lending And Investment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Federal savings association may invest in the aggregate up to the greater of 1% of its total capital or $250,000 in community development investments of the type permitted for a national bank under 12 CFR part 24.
