48 C.F.R. § 42.800 — 42.800 Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:48:42.800
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-42.800
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 42 (Contract Administration And Audit Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes policies and procedures for (a) issuing notices of intent to disallow costs and (b) disallowing costs already incurred during the course of performance.
