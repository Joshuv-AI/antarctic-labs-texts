Abstract
. We introduce a notion of an operad of complexity m m , for m ≥ 1 m\geq 1 . Operads of complexity 1 1 are monoids in the category of ℕ \mathbb{N} -indexed collections, with monoidal product given by the Day convolution, and operads of complexity 2 2 are non-symmetric operads. In general, we prove that the operad for operads of complexity m m is a suboperad of the m m -th stage filtration of the lattice path operad introduced by Batanin and Berger [ 2 ] . Finally, we exhibit fibration sequences involving this new notion, extending the results of Turchin [ 12 ] and Dwyer-Hess [ 8 ] .

Definition 2.4
