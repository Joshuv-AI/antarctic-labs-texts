16 U.S.C. § 3344 — Construction with other laws
Citation: us:usc:16:3344
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section3344
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 52 (Salmon And Steelhead Conservation And Enhancement); Subchapter V (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed—
(1)
to diminish Federal, State, or tribal jurisdiction, responsibility, or rights in the field of resource enhancement and management, or control of water resources, submerged lands, or navigable waters; nor to limit the authority of Congress to authorize and fund projects; or
(2)
as superseding, modifying, or repealing any existing applicable law, except as provided for in this section.
