7 C.F.R. § 614.14 — Computation of time.
Citation: us:cfr:7:614.14
Creator: Natural Resources Conservation Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-614.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 614 (Nrcs Appeal Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The word “days” as used in this final rule means calendar days, unless specifically stated otherwise.
(b) Deadlines for any action under this part, including deadlines for filing and decisions which fall on a Saturday, Sunday, Federal holiday, or other day on which the relevant NRCS office is closed during normal business hours, will be extended to close of business the next working day.
