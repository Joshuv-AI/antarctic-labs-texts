48 C.F.R. § 46.800 — 46.800 Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:48:46.800
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-46.800
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 46 (Quality Assurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes policies and procedures for limiting contractor liability for loss of or damage to property of the Government that (a) occurs after acceptance and (b) results from defects or deficiencies in the supplies delivered or services performed.
