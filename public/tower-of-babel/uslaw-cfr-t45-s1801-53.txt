45 C.F.R. § 1801.53 — Postponement of payment.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1801.53
Creator: Harry S. Truman Scholarship Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1801.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1801 (Harry S. Truman Scholarship Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A Scholar may request the Foundation to postpone one or more payments because of sickness or other circumstances.
(b) If the Foundation grants a postponement, it may impose conditions as it deems appropriate.
