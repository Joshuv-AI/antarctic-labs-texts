19 C.F.R. § 361.106 — Fees.
Citation: us:cfr:19:361.106
Creator: International Trade Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-361.106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 361 (Aluminum Import Monitoring And Analysis System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No fees will be charged for obtaining a username, issuing an aluminum import license or accessing the aluminum import monitoring system.
