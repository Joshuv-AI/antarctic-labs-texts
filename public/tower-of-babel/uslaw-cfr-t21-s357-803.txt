21 C.F.R. § 357.803 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:21:357.803
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 357 (Miscellaneous Internal Drug Products For Over-The-Counter Human Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As used in this subpart:
(a) Colostomy. An external operative opening of the colon.
(b) Deodorant for internal use. An ingredient taken internally to reduce odors arising from conditions such as colostomies, ileostomies, or fecal incontinence.
(c) Ileostomy. An external operative opening from the ileum.
(d) Incontinence. An inability to retain urine or feces.
