2 C.F.R. § 3474.10 — Clarification regarding 2 CFR 200.207. 2
Citation: us:cfr:2:3474.10
Creator: Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-3474.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 3474 (Uniform Administrative Requirements, Cost Principles, And Audit Requirements For Federal Awards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary or a pass-through entity may, in appropriate circumstances, designate the specific conditions established under 2 CFR 200.207 as “high-risk conditions” and designate a non-Federal entity subject to specific conditions established under § 200.207 as “high-risk”.
