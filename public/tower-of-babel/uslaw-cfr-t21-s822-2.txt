21 C.F.R. § 822.2 — What is the purpose of this part?
Citation: us:cfr:21:822.2
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-822.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 822 (Postmarket Surveillance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to implement our postmarket surveillance authority to maximize the likelihood that postmarket surveillance plans will result in the collection of useful data. These data can reveal unforeseen adverse events, the actual rate of anticipated adverse events, or other information necessary to protect the public health.
