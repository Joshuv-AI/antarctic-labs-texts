46 C.F.R. § 308.600 — Records retention requirement.
Citation: us:cfr:46:308.600
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-308.600
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 308 (War Risk Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The records specified in §§ 308.8, 308.517, and 308.548 of this part shall be retained until a release is granted by MARAD, at which time MARAD will take custody of the records.
