46 C.F.R. § 151.40-2 — -2 Materials.
Citation: us:cfr:46:151.40-2
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-151.40-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 151 (Barges Carrying Bulk Liquid Hazardous Material Cargoes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Materials used in the construction of temperature or pressure control systems shall be suitable for the intended application and meet the requirements of Subchapter F and the Special Requirements section of this subchapter.
