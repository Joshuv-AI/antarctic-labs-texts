22 U.S.C. § 1373 — Suspension of Philippine Trade Act of 1946
Citation: us:usc:22:1373
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section1373
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 15 (The Republic Of The Philippines); Subchapter IV–A (Trade Relations Under Revised Agreement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Philippine Trade Act of 1946 [22 U.S.C. 1251 et seq.] (except section 506(a) of this title [22 U.S.C. 1356] relating to termination of payments into Philippine Treasury, and except amendments and repeals made by such Act) shall not apply during such time as the revised agreement is in effect.
