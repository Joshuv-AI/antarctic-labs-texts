36 U.S.C. § 153107 — Annual report
Citation: us:usc:36:153107
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section153107
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1531 (The National Society Of The Daughters Of The American Revolution). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation shall submit an annual report to the Secretary of the Smithsonian Institution on the activities of the corporation. The Secretary shall communicate to Congress any part of the report that the Secretary considers of national interest and importance.
