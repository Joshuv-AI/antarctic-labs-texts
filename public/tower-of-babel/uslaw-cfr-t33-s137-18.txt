33 C.F.R. § 137.18 — Duties of persons specified in § 137.1(a).
Citation: us:cfr:33:137.18
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-137.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 137 (Oil Spill Liability: Standards For Conducting All Appropriate Inquiries Under The Innocent Land-Owner Defense). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In order to make all appropriate inquiries, persons seeking to establish the liability protection under § 137.1(a) must conduct the inquiries and investigations as required in this part and ensure that the inquiries and investigations required to be made by environmental professionals are made.
