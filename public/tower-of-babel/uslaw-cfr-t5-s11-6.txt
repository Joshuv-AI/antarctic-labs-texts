5 C.F.R. § 11.6 — Appeals.
Citation: us:cfr:5:11.6
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-11.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 11 (Probationary And Trial Periods (Rule Xi)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Director of OPM may by regulation prescribe circumstances under and procedures by which employees terminated from a probationary or trial period may appeal such termination.
(b) Except as otherwise required by law, such appeals shall be the sole and exclusive means of appealing terminations during probationary or trial periods.
