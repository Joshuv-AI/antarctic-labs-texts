45 C.F.R. § 148.124 — Certification and disclosure of coverage.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 148 (Requirements For The Individual Health Insurance Market). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) General rule. The rules for providing certificates of creditable coverage and demonstrating creditable coverage have been superseded by the prohibition on preexisting condition exclusions. See § 147.108 of this subchapter for rules prohibiting the imposition of a preexisting condition exclusion.
(b) Applicability. The provisions of this section apply beginning December 31, 2014.
