42 U.S.C. § 292k — Participation by Federal credit unions in Federal, State, and private student loan insurance programs
Citation: us:usc:42:292k
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section292k
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter V (Health Professions Education); Part A (Student Loans); Subpart i (Insured Health Education Assistance Loans To Graduate Students). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, Federal credit unions shall, pursuant to regulations of the Administrator of the National Credit Union Administration, have power to make insured loans to eligible students in accordance with the provisions of this subpart relating to Federal insured loans.
