Kempe Changes in H H -Free Graphs
Manoj Belavadi, Kathie Cameron

Abstract
Given a k k -colouring of a graph G G and two of the colours, a Kempe chain is a connected component of the subgraph of G G induced by the vertices coloured with one of these two colours. A Kempe swap changes one colouring into another by interchanging the colours of the vertices in a Kempe chain. Two colourings are Kempe equivalent if each can be obtained from the other by a series of Kempe swaps; the set of Kempe equivalent colourings is called a Kempe class . For a graph G G , let χ ⁡ ( G ) \chi(G) denote its chromatic number and let 𝒞 k ​ ( G ) \mathcal{C}_{k}(G) denote the set of all k k -colourings of G G . We say G G is Kempe connected if for all k ≥ χ ⁡ ( G ) k\geq\chi(G) , 𝒞 k ​ ( G ) \mathcal{C}_{k}(G) forms a Kempe class. For a graph H H , graph G G is called H H -free if no induced subgraph of G G is isomorphic to H H . We prove that every H H -free graph is Kempe connected if and only if H H is an induced subgraph of the path on four vertices, P 4 P_{4} . The graph 2 K 2 K_{2} consists of four vertices and two edges which are not adjacent. We prove that for all p ≥ 0 p\geq 0 , there is a k k -colourable 2 K 2 K_{2} -free graph G G such that 𝒞 k + p ​ ( G ) \mathcal{C}_{k+p}(G) does not form a Kempe class. Keywords : Kempe chain, Kempe swap, forbidden induced subgraph, k k -colouring, frozen colouring, P 4 P_{4} -free graph, 2 ​ K 2 2K_{2} -free graph.

Observation 1

Lemma 1

Let
