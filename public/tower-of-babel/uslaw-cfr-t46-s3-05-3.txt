46 C.F.R. § 3.05-3 — -3 Oceanographic research vessel.
Citation: us:cfr:46:3.05-3
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-3.05-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 3 (Designation Of Oceanographic Research Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

“An oceanographic research vessel is a vessel which the U.S. Coast Guard finds is employed exclusively in one or more of the following:
(a) Oceanographic instruction;
(b) Limnologic instruction;
(c) Oceanographic research; or,
(d) Limnologic research.”
