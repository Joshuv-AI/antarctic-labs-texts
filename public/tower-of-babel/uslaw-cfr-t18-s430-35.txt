18 C.F.R. § 430.35 — Amendments.
Citation: us:cfr:18:430.35
Creator: Delaware River Basin Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-430.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 430 (Ground Water Protection Area: Pennsylvania). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon request by any interested party, or on its own motion, the Commission may consider amendment of this regulation, and modify the geographic boundaries of the protected area, in accordance with Article 10 of the Compact.
