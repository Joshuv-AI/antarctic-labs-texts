20 C.F.R. § 686.740 — What services are provided for program graduates?
Citation: us:cfr:20:686.740
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-686.740
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 686 (The Job Corps Under Title I Of The Workforce Innovation And Opportunity Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

According to procedures issued by the Secretary, career transition and support services must be provided to program graduates for up to 12 months after graduation.
