43 U.S.C. § 2433 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:43:2433
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section2433
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 42 (Rural Water Supply); Subchapter II (Twenty-First Century Water Works). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated such sums as are necessary to carry out this subchapter, to remain available until expended.
