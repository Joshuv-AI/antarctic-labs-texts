38 U.S.C. § 7733 — Personnel
Citation: us:usc:38:7733
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section7733
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part V (Boards, Administrations, And Services); Chapter 77 (Veterans Benefits Administration); Subchapter II (Quality Assurance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall ensure that the number of full-time employees of the Veterans Benefits Administration assigned to quality assurance functions under this subchapter is adequate to perform the quality assurance functions for which they have responsibility.
