Poincaré polynomials of moduli spaces of one-dimensional sheaves on the projective plane
Shuai Guo, Longting Wu

Abstract
. Let M β M_{\beta} denote the moduli space of stable one-dimensional sheaves on a del Pezzo surface S S , supported on curves of class β \beta with Euler characteristic one. We show that the divisibility property of the Poincaré polynomial of M β M_{\beta} , proposed by Choi-van Garrel-Katz-Takahashi follows from Bousseau’s conjectural refined sheaves/Gromov-Witten correspondence. Since this correspondence is known for S = ℙ 2 S=\mathbb{P}^{2} , our result proves Choi-van Garrel-Katz-Takahashi’s conjecture in this case. For S = ℙ 2 S=\mathbb{P}^{2} , our proof also introduces a novel approach to computing the Poincaré polynomials using Gromov-Witten invariants of local ℙ 2 \mathbb{P}^{2} and a local elliptic curve. Specifically, we compute the Poincaré polynomials of M d M_{d} with degrees d ≤ 16 d\leq 16 and derive a closed formula for the leading Betti numbers b i ​ ( M d ) b_{i}(M_{d}) with d ≥ 6 d\geq 6 and i ≤ 4 ​ d − 22 i\leq 4d-22 . We also propose a conjectural formula for the leading Betti numbers b i ​ ( M d ) b_{i}(M_{d}) with d ≥ 4 d\geq 4 and i ≤ 6 ​ d − 20 i\leq 6d-20 . In the Appendix (by M. Moreira), a more general conjecture concerning the higher range Betti numbers of M d M_{d} is presented, along with another conjecture that involves refinements from the perverse/Chern filtration.

A.2.
Perverse/Chern refinement

One of the reasons why the study of the cohomology of
