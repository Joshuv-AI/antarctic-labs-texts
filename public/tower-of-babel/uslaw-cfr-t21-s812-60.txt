21 C.F.R. § 812.60 — IRB composition, duties, and functions.
Citation: us:cfr:21:812.60
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-812.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 812 (Investigational Device Exemptions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An IRB reviewing and approving investigations under this part shall comply with the requirements of part 56 in all respects, including its composition, duties, and functions.
