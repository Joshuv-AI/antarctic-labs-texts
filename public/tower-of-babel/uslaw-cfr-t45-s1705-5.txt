45 C.F.R. § 1705.5 — Disclosure of requested information to individuals.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1705.5
Creator: National Commission On Libraries And Information Science
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1705.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1705 (Privacy Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon verification of identity, the System Manager shall disclose to the individual: (a) The information contained in the record which pertains to that individual; and (b) the accounting of disclosures of the record, if any, required by 5 U.S.C. 552a(c).
