45 C.F.R. § 164.106 — Relationship to other parts.
Citation: us:cfr:45:164.106
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-164.106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 164 (Security And Privacy). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In complying with the requirements of this part, covered entities and, where provided, business associates, are required to comply with the applicable provisions of parts 160 and 162 of this subchapter.
