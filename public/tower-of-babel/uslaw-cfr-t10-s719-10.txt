10 C.F.R. § 719.10 — Who must submit a Legal Management Plan?
Citation: us:cfr:10:719.10
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-719.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 719 (Contractor Legal Management Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Contractors who are parties to contracts identified under § 719.3(a) and (b) must submit a Legal Management Plan.
