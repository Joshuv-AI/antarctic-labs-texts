42 C.F.R. § 485.707 — Condition of participation: Compliance with Federal, State, and local laws.
Citation: us:cfr:42:485.707
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-485.707
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 485 (Conditions Of Participation: Specialized Providers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The organization and its staff are in compliance with all applicable Federal, State, and local laws and regulations.
(a) Standard: Licensure of organization. In any State in which State or applicable local law provides for the licensing of organizations, a clinic, rehabilitation agency, or public health agency is licensed in accordance with applicable laws.
(b) Standard: Licensure or registration of personnel. Staff of the organization are licensed or registered in accordance with applicable laws.
