Let

For a field

Selmer groups of elliptic curves over number fields are fundamental objects in arithmetic geometry, offering deep insights into the Mordell–Weil group, the Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer conjecture, and Iwasawa theory. In
[
7
]
, Mazur and Rubin introduced the concept of Selmer near-companion curves, formalizing the idea that certain pairs of elliptic curves exhibit similar behavior in their Selmer groups when twisted by quadratic characters. They conjectured that if two elliptic curves

and

are

-Selmer near-companions, then

and

should be isomorphic as

-modules.

Definition 1.1
.

Let

and

be elliptic curves over a number
K
. We say

and

such that for every
\chi\in\mathrm{Hom}(G_{K},\{\pm 1\}),

\ |\dim_{p}\mathrm{Sel}_{p}(E_{2}^{\chi}/K)-\dim_{p}\mathrm{Sel}_{p}(E_{1}^{\chi}/K)|<C.

[

and
{E_{2}}

-Selmer near companions over

-module isomorphism

(The original conjecture is about

.

[

Let

Yu proved the conjecture for

[

.

Theorem 1.4
.

Let
E_{1}
and
E_{2}
be elliptic curves over a number field
K
. If they are
2

, then there is a

-module isomorphism

In this paper, we extend the framework of Mazur and Rubin by introducing the notion of

-twisted Selmer near-companion curves (abbreviated as

Definition 1.5

We say

and

\ |\dim_{p}\mathrm{Sel}_{p}(E_{2}/K,\chi)-\dim_{p}\mathrm{Sel}_{p}(E_{1}/K,\chi)|<C.

We are interested in elliptic curves that are

-TSNC over

. Our main theorems are as follows:

Theorem 1.6
.

Let
E_{1}
and

be elliptic curves over a number field

. Let

for

Suppose that

Assume that one of the following conditions holds:

for some

, or

(ii)

, or

(iii)

and

, or

Then

and

are not

-TSNC over

-TSNC over

Theorem 1.7

Let
E_{1}
and

-module isomorphism

Suppose that

and

over

(iv)

Proposition 8.4
.

Assume
[M_{1}:K(\mu_{p})]\leq[M_{2}:K(\mu_{p})]

[M_{2}:M_{1}\cap M_{2}]\neq p
.
Then, for any positive integer
d

\chi\in\mathcal{C}(K)

r_{p}(E_{1},\chi)=r_{p}(E_{1})+2d\quad\text{and}\quad r_{p}(E_{2},\chi)=r_{p}(E_{2}).

Proof.

We claim that for
\chi^{\prime}\in\mathcal{C}(K)
, there exist infinitely many
\chi\in\mathcal{C}(K)

\displaystyle r_{p}(E_{1},\chi\chi^{\prime})=r_{p}(E_{1},\chi^{\prime})+2\quad\text{and}

\displaystyle r_{p}(E_{2},\chi\chi^{\prime})=r_{p}(E_{2},\chi^{\prime}).

Then the result follows by induction.

Let
N
be the Galois closure of
L_{E_{1},\chi^{\prime}}K(\sqrt[p]{\Delta_{2}})
over
K
, where
\Delta_{2}
is a generator of
\mathcal{A}_{2}

N
and
M_{2}
are linearly disjoint.
Choose a prime
\mathfrak{q}\notin\Sigma(\chi^{\prime})
such that
\mathrm{Fr}_{\mathfrak{q}}|_{N}=1
and
(\mathrm{Fr}_{\mathfrak{q}}|_{M_{2}})^{p}\neq 1
. Then
\mathfrak{q}\in\mathcal{P}_{2}(E_{1})\cap\mathcal{P}_{0}(E_{2})
and
\mathrm{loc}_{\mathfrak{q}}(\mathrm{Sel}_{p}(E_{1}/K,\chi^{\prime}))=0

[
4
, Proposition 7.2]
, there exist
(p-1)
-characters
\psi_{\mathfrak{q}}\in\mathcal{C}(K_{\mathfrak{q}})
such that
r_{p}(E_{1},\chi^{\prime},\psi_{\mathfrak{q}})=r_{p}(E_{1},\chi^{\prime})+2
, where
\mathrm{Sel}_{p}(E_{1},\chi^{\prime},\psi_{\mathfrak{q}})
is defined by the following exact sequence:

0\rightarrow\mathrm{Sel}_{p}(E_{1},\chi^{\prime},\psi_{\mathfrak{q}})\rightarrow H^{1}(K,E_{1}[p])\rightarrow{\frac{H^{1}(K_{\mathfrak{q}},E_{1}[p])}{\gamma_{\mathfrak{q}}(\chi^{\prime}_{\mathfrak{q}}\psi_{\mathfrak{q}})}}\underset{v\neq\mathfrak{q}}{\textstyle\bigoplus}{\displaystyle\frac{H^{1}(K_{v},E_{1}[p])}{\gamma_{v}(\chi^{\prime}{{}_{v}})}}.

Let
\Sigma(\chi^{\prime},\mathfrak{q}):=\Sigma(\chi^{\prime})\cup\{\mathfrak{q}\}
.
Define

•

Q:=(\Sigma(\chi^{\prime},\mathfrak{q})\cup\mathcal{P}_{0})^{c},

•

J:=\mathcal{O}_{K,\Sigma(\chi^{\prime},\mathfrak{q})}^{\times},

•

G:=\prod_{\mathfrak{p}\in\mathcal{P}_{0}}\mathcal{O}_{\mathfrak{p}}^{\times},

•

H:=\prod_{\mathfrak{p}\in Q}\mathcal{O}_{\mathfrak{p}}^{\times}\times\prod_{v\in\Sigma(\chi^{\prime},\mathfrak{q})}K_{v}^{\times}.

By Lemma
3.3
, the image of map

\mathcal{C}(K)\longrightarrow\prod_{\mathfrak{p}\in Q}\mathrm{Hom}(\mathcal{O}_{\mathfrak{p}}^{\times},\mu_{p})\times\prod_{v\in\Sigma(\chi^{\prime},\mathfrak{q})}\mathrm{Hom}(K_{v}^{\times},\mu_{p})

is equal to

\{f\in\prod_{\mathfrak{p}\in Q}\mathrm{Hom}(\mathcal{O}_{\mathfrak{p}}^{\times},\mu_{p})\times\prod_{v\in\Sigma(\chi^{\prime},\mathfrak{q})}\mathrm{Hom}(K_{v}^{\times},\mu_{p}):f(\mathcal{A}_{3})=1\},

where

\mathcal{A}_{3}:=\ker(\mathcal{O}_{K,\Sigma(\chi^{\prime},\mathfrak{q})}^{\times}/{(\mathcal{O}_{K,\Sigma(\chi^{\prime},\mathfrak{q})}^{\times})^{p}}\rightarrow\prod_{\mathfrak{q}\in\mathcal{P}_{0}}\mathcal{O}_{\mathfrak{q}}^{\times}/{(\mathcal{O}_{\mathfrak{q}}^{\times})^{p}}).

Let

\psi=(\psi_{v})\in\prod_{\mathfrak{p}\in Q}\mathrm{Hom}(\mathcal{O}_{\mathfrak{p}}^{\times},\mu_{p})\times\prod_{v\in\Sigma(\chi^{\prime},\mathfrak{q})}\mathrm{Hom}(K_{v}^{\times},\mu_{p})

be such that

\psi_{\mathfrak{p}}
is trivial for
\mathfrak{p}\in Q
,

•

\psi_{v}=1_{v}
for
v\in\Sigma(\chi^{\prime})
,

•

\psi_{\mathfrak{q}}
is the character chosen above.

Then

for
i=1,2

8.3
,
\psi(\mathcal{A}_{1}\mathcal{A}_{2})=1

Thus, there exists
{\chi}\in\mathcal{C}(K)

∎
