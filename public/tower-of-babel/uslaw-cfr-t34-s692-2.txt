34 C.F.R. § 692.2 — Who is eligible to participate in the LEAP Program?
Citation: us:cfr:34:692.2
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-692.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 692 (Leveraging Educational Assistance Partnership Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) State participation. A State that meets the requirements in §§ 692.20 and 692.21 is eligible to receive payments under the LEAP program.
(b) Student participation. A student must meet the requirements of § 692.40 to be eligible to receive assistance from a State under the LEAP program.
