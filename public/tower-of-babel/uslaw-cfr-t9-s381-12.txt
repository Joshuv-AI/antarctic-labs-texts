9 C.F.R. § 381.12 — Effect of religious dietary laws exemptions on other persons.
Citation: us:cfr:9:381.12
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-381.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 381 (Poultry Products Inspection Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Whenever a slaughterer or processor is granted an exemption under § 381.11 with respect to the slaughtering or processing of any poultry or poultry products under this part, under specified conditions, the sale, offer for sale, transportation and other handling in commerce by any person of such poultry and poultry products in accordance with such conditions is hereby authorized, except as restricted by the Act.
