16 U.S.C. § 7643 — Engagement of United States diplomatic missions
Citation: us:usc:16:7643
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section7643
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 95 (Eliminate, Neutralize, And Disrupt Wildlife Trafficking); Subchapter IV (Programs To Address The Escalating Wildlife Trafficking Crisis). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As soon as practicable but not later than 2 years after October 7, 2016, each chief of mission to a focus country should begin to implement the recommendations contained in the strategic plan required under section 7631(a)(2) of this title, among other goals, for the country.
