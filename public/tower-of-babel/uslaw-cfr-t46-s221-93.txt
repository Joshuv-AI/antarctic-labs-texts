46 C.F.R. § 221.93 — Collection of civil penalties.
Citation: us:cfr:46:221.93
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-221.93
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 221 (Regulated Transactions Involving Documented Vessels And Other Maritime Interests). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Within 30 days after receipt of the Hearing Officer's decision, or a decision on appeal, the Party must submit payment of any assessed penalty in the manner specified in the decision letter. Failure to make timely payment will result in the institution of appropriate action to collect the penalty.
