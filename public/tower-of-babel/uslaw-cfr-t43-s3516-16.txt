43 C.F.R. § 3516.16 — What must I include with my application?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3516.16
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3516.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3500 (Leasing Of Solid Minerals Other Than Coal And Oil Shale). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must agree to pay the annual charge identified in the permit, and provide the following information:
(a) Specific reasons why you need the additional lands;
(b) A description of the lands applied for;
(c) Any information demonstrating that the lands are suitable and appropriate for your needs; and
(d) Evidence that the lands are unoccupied and unappropriated.
