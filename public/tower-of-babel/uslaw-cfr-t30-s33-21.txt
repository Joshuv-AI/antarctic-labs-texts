30 C.F.R. § 33.21 — Modification of test equipment.
Citation: us:cfr:30:33.21
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-33.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 33 (Dust Collectors For Use In Connection With Rock Drilling In Coal Mines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For test purposes the unit or system may be modified, such as by attaching instruments or measuring devices, at MSHA's discretion; but such modification shall not alter its performance.
