43 C.F.R. § 2440.2 — General criterion.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2440.2
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2440.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2440 (Segregation By Classification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The public lands classified or proposed to be classified under the regulations of this part will be kept open to (i.e., not segregated from) as many forms of disposal as possible consistent with the purposes of the classification and the resource values of the lands.
