42 C.F.R. § 421.212 — Railroad Retirement Board contracts.
Citation: us:cfr:42:421.212
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-421.212
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 421 (Medicare Contracting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In accordance with this subpart C, the Railroad Retirement Board contracts with DMEPOS regional carriers designated by CMS, as set forth in § 421.210(e)(2), for processing claims for Medicare-eligible Railroad Retirement beneficiaries, for the same contract period as the contracts entered into between CMS and the DMEPOS regional carriers.
