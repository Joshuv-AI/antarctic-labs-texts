24 C.F.R. § 103.10 — What can I do if I believe someone is discriminating against me in the sale, rental, finance, or advertisement of housing?
Citation: us:cfr:24:103.10
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Equal Opportunity, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-103.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 103 (Fair Housing—Complaint Processing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You can notify HUD if you believe there has been discrimination against you in any activity related to housing because of race, color, religion, national origin, sex, disability, or the presence of children under the age of 18 in a household.
