16 U.S.C. § 1220c — Denial of applications; finality of decision

Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 25B (Reefs For Marine Life Conservation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A decision by the Secretary denying any application for a 1 obsolete ship under this chapter is final.
