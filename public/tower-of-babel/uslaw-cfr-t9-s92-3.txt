9 C.F.R. § 92.3 — Movement restrictions.
Citation: us:cfr:9:92.3
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-92.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 92 (Importation Of Animals And Animal Products: Procedures For Requesting Recognition Of Regions And Compartments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Whenever the European Commission (EC) establishes a quarantine for a disease in the European Union in a region the Animal and Plant Health Inspection Service recognizes as one in which the disease is not known to exist and the EC imposes prohibitions or other restrictions on the movement of animals or animal products from the quarantined area in the European Union, such animals and animal products are prohibited importation into the United States.
