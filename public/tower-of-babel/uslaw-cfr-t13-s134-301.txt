13 C.F.R. § 134.301 — Scope of the rules in this subpart C.
Citation: us:cfr:13:134.301
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-134.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 134 (Rules Of Procedure Governing Cases Before The Office Of Hearings And Appeals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The rules of practice in this subpart C apply to all appeals to OHA from:
(a) Formal size determinations made by an SBA Government Contracting Area Office, under part 121 of this chapter, or by a Disaster Area Office, in connection with applications for disaster loans; and
(b) NAICS code designations, pursuant to part 121 of this chapter.
