48 C.F.R. § 1522.804-2 — 1522.804-2 Construction.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1522.804-2
Creator: Chapter 15—Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1522.804-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1522 (Application Of Labor Laws To Government Acquisitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each contracting office having construction contract responsibility shall maintain a list of geographical areas subject to affirmative action requirements. The list can be obtained from the Office of Federal Contract Compliance Programs, U.S. Department of Labor.
