33 C.F.R. § 150.205 — What are the language requirements for deepwater port personnel?
Citation: us:cfr:33:150.205
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-150.205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 150 (Deepwater Ports: Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Only persons who read, write, and speak English may occupy the essential management positions outlined in the operations manual.
