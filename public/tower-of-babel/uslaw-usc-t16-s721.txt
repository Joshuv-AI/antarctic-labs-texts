16 U.S.C. § 721 — Short title; “person” defined
Citation: us:usc:16:721
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section721
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 8 (Upper Mississippi River National Wildlife And Fish Refuge). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This chapter may be cited as “The Upper Mississippi River National Wildlife and Fish Refuge Act.” The term “person” as used therein includes an individual, partnership, association, or corporation.
