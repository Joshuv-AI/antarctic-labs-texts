20 C.F.R. § 1002.247 — Does USERRA provide the employee with protection against discharge?
Citation: us:cfr:20:1002.247
Creator: Office Of The Assistant Secretary For Veterans' Employment And Training Service, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-1002.247
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 1002 (Regulations Under The Uniformed Services Employment And Reemployment Rights Act Of 1994). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. If the employee's most recent period of service in the uniformed services was more than 30 days, he or she must not be discharged except for cause—
(a) For 180 days after the employee's date of reemployment if his or her most recent period of uniformed service was more than 30 days but less than 181 days; or,
(b) For one year after the date of reemployment if the employee's most recent period of uniformed service was more than 180 days.
