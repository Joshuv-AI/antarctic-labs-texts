22 U.S.C. § 5472 — Report on confidence building measures by Poland and Hungary
Citation: us:usc:22:5472
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section5472
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 63 (Support For East European Democracy (Seed)); Subchapter VI (Reports To Congress). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 180 days after November 28, 1989, the President shall submit a report to the Congress identifying—
(1)
the confidence building measures Poland and Hungary could undertake to facilitate the negotiation of agreements, including bilateral customs and technology transfer agreements, that would encourage greater direct private sector investment in that country; and
(2)
the confidence building measures Poland and Hungary could undertake with respect to the treatment accorded those countries under the Export Administration Act of 1979.
