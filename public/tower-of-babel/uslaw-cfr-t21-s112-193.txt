21 C.F.R. § 112.193 — What are the provisions for coordination of education and enforcement?
Citation: us:cfr:21:112.193
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-112.193
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 112 (Standards For The Growing, Harvesting, Packing, And Holding Of Produce For Human Consumption). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Under section 419(b)(2)(A) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act, FDA coordinates education and enforcement activities by State, territorial, tribal, and local officials by helping develop education, training, and enforcement approaches.
