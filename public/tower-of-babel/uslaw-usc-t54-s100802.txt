54 U.S.C. § 100802 — Interpretation and education authority
Citation: us:usc:54:100802
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section100802
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 1008 (Education And Interpretation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall ensure that management of System units and related areas is enhanced by the availability and use of a broad program of the highest quality interpretation and education.
