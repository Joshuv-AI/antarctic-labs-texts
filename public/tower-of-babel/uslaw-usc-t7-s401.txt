7 U.S.C. § 401 — Establishment of bureau
Citation: us:usc:7:401
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section401
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 16 (Bureau Of Dairy Industry). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is established in the Department of Agriculture a bureau to be known as the “Bureau of Dairy Industry.”
