39 C.F.R. § 3045.10 — Duration.
Citation: us:cfr:39:3045.10
Creator: Postal Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-3045.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 3045 (Rules For Market Tests Of Experimental Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A market test may not exceed 24 months in duration unless the Commission authorizes an extension pursuant to a request filed by the Postal Service under § 3045.11.
