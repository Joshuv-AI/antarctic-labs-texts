12 C.F.R. § 207.8 — Release of information under FOIA.
Citation: us:cfr:12:207.8
Creator: Federal Reserve System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-207.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 207 (Disclosure And Reporting Of Cra-Related Agreements (Regulation G)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board will make covered agreements and annual reports available to the public in accordance with the Freedom of Information Act (5 U.S.C. 552 et seq.) and the Board's Rules Regarding the Availability of Information (12 CFR part 261). A party to a covered agreement may request confidential treatment of proprietary and confidential information in a covered agreement or an annual report under those procedures.
