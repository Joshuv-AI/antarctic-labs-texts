30 C.F.R. § 100.4 — Unwarrantable failure and immediate notification.
Citation: us:cfr:30:100.4
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-100.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 100 (Criteria And Procedures For Proposed Assessment Of Civil Penalties). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The minimum penalty for any citation or order issued under section 104(d)(1) of the Mine Act shall be $3,022.
(b) The minimum penalty for any order issued under section 104(d)(2) of the Mine Act shall be $6,041.
(c) The penalty for failure to provide timely notification to the Secretary under section 103(j) of the Mine Act will be not less than $7,555 and not more than $90,649 for the following accidents:
(1) The death of an individual at the mine, or
(2) An injury or entrapment of an individual at the mine, which has a reasonable potential to cause death.
