40 C.F.R. § 60.6030 — What records must I keep for the materials separation plan and siting analysis?
Citation: us:cfr:40:60.6030
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-60.6030
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 60 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must keep records of all the following five items:
(a) The date of each record.
(b) The final materials separation plan.
(c) The final siting analysis.
(d) A record of the location and date of the public meetings.
(e) Your responses to the public comments received during the public comment periods for the materials separation plan and siting analysis.
