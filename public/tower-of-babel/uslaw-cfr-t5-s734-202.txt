5 C.F.R. § 734.202 — Permitted activities.
Citation: us:cfr:5:734.202
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-734.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 734 (Political Activities Of Federal Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees may take an active part in political activities, including political management and political campaigns, to the extent not expressly prohibited by law and this part.
