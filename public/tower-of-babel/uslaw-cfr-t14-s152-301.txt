14 C.F.R. § 152.301 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:14:152.301
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-152.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 152 (Airport Aid Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart contains accounting and reporting requirements applicable to—
(a) Each sponsor of a project for airport development;
(b) Each sponsor of a project for airport master planning; and
(c) Each planning agency conducting a project for airport system planning.
