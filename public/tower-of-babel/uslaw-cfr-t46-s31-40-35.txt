46 C.F.R. § 31.40-35 — -35 Availability of certificates.

Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 31 (Inspection And Certification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Convention certificates shall be on board the vessel and readily available for examination at all times.
