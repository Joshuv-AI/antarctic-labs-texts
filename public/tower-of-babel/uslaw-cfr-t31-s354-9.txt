31 C.F.R. § 354.9 — Liability of Sallie Mae and Federal Reserve Banks.
Citation: us:cfr:31:354.9
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-354.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 354 (Regulations Governing Book-Entry Securities Of The Student Loan Marketing Association (Sallie Mae)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Sallie Mae and the Federal Reserve Banks may rely on the information provided in a Transfer Message, and are not required to verify the information. Sallie Mae and the Federal Reserve Banks shall not be liable for any action taken in accordance with the information set out in a Transfer Message or evidence submitted in support thereof.
