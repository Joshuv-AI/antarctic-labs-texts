7 C.F.R. § 3565.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3565.1
Creator: Rural Housing Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3565.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3565 (Guaranteed Rural Rental Housing Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of the Guaranteed Rural Rental Housing Program (GRRHP) is to increase the supply of affordable rural rental housing, through the use of loan guarantees that encourage partnerships between the Rural Housing Service, private lenders and public agencies.
