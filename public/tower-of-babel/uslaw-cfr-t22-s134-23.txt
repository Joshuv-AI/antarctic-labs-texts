22 C.F.R. § 134.23 — Reply.
Citation: us:cfr:22:134.23
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-134.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 134 (Equal Access To Justice Act; Implementation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Within 15 days after service of an answer, the applicant may file a reply. If the reply is based on any alleged facts not already in the record of the proceeding, the applicant shall include with the reply either supporting affidavits or a request for further proceedings under § 134.26.
