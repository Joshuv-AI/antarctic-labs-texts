48 C.F.R. § 213.302-3 — 213.302-3 Obtaining contractor acceptance and modifying purchase orders.
Citation: us:cfr:48:213.302-3
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-213.302-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 213 (Simplified Acquisition Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(1) Require written acceptance of purchase orders for classified acquisitions.
(2) See PGI 213.302-3 for guidance on the use of unilateral modifications.
(3) A supplemental agreement converts a unilateral purchase order to a bilateral agreement. If not previously included in the purchase order, incorporate the clause at 252.243-7001, Pricing of Contract Modifications, in the Standard Form 30, and obtain the contractor's acceptance by signature on the Standard Form 30.
