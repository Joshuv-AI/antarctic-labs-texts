The Manakov-Zakharov-Ward model as an integrable decoupling limit of the membrane


Abstract
A novel decoupling limit of the membrane is proposed, leading to the ( 1 + 2 ) (1+2) -dimensional classically integrable model originally introduced by Manakov, Zakharov, and Ward. This limit is the large-wrapping regime of a membrane propagating toy background of the form ℝ t × T 2 × G \mathbb{R}_{t}\times T^{2}\times G subject to scaling limit, where G G is a Lie group and the geometry is supported by a four-form flux. Such toy backgrounds can arise from consistent eleven-dimensional supergravity solutions, exemplified by the uplift of the pure NSNS AdS 3 × {}_{3}\times S 3 × {}^{3}\times T 4 background. The scaling limit can be interpreted as similtaneous small tension and non- or hyper-relativistic limit.

The fate of reparameterisation invariance.

At the level of the original membrane action, the model possesses reparameterisation invariance. In particular, one might be tempted to choose

There are several ways to choose interesting concrete realisations and interpret the combined scaling limits:

\omega\rightarrow\infty,\quad\frac{\bar{R}}{R\omega}\rightarrow 0,\quad T\sim\frac{T_{0}}{\bar{R}^{2}R\omega}.
