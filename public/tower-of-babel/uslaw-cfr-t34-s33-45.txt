34 C.F.R. § 33.45 — Deposit in Treasury of United States.
Citation: us:cfr:34:33.45
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-33.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 33 (Program Fraud Civil Remedies Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All amounts collected pursuant to this part are deposited as miscellaneous receipts in the Treasury of the United States, except as provided in 31 U.S.C. 3806(g).
