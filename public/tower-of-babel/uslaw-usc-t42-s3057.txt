42 U.S.C. § 3057 — Statement of purpose
Citation: us:usc:42:3057
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section3057
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 35 (Programs For Older Americans); Subchapter X (Grants For Native Americans). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the purpose of this subchapter to promote the delivery of supportive services, including nutrition services to American Indians, Alaskan Natives, and Native Hawaiians that are comparable to services provided under subchapter III.
