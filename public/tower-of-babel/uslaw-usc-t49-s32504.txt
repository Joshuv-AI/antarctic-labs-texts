49 U.S.C. § 32504 — Certificates of compliance
Citation: us:usc:49:32504
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section32504
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part C (Information, Standards, And Requirements); Chapter 325 (Bumper Standards). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Under regulations prescribed by the Secretary of Transportation, a manufacturer or distributor of a passenger motor vehicle or passenger motor vehicle equipment subject to a standard prescribed under section 32502 of this title shall give the distributor or dealer at the time of delivery a certificate that the vehicle or equipment complies with the standard.
