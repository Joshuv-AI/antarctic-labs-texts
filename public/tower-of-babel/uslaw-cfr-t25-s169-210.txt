25 C.F.R. § 169.210 — May a grantee mortgage a right-of-way?
Citation: us:cfr:25:169.210
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-169.210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 169 (Rights-Of-Way Over Indian Land). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A grantee may mortgage a right-of-way, if the grant expressly allows mortgaging. The grantee must meet the consent requirements in § 169.107, unless the grant expressly allows for mortgaging without consent, and must obtain our approval for the mortgage.
