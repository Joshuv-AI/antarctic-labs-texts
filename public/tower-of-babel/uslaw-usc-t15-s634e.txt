15 U.S.C. § 634e — Assistance of Government agencies
Citation: us:usc:15:634e
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section634e
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 14A (Aid To Small Business). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each department, agency, and instrumentality of the Federal Government is authorized and directed to furnish to the Chief Counsel for Advocacy such reports and other information as he deems necessary to carry out his functions under sections 634a to 634g of this title.
