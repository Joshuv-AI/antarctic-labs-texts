22 U.S.C. § 2101 — Statement of purpose
Citation: us:usc:22:2101
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section2101
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 30 (International Cooperation In Health And Medical Research). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the purpose of this chapter—
(1)
to advance the status of the health sciences in the United States and thereby the health of the American people through cooperative endeavors with other countries in health research, and research training; and
(2)
to advance the international status of the health sciences through cooperative enterprises in health research, research planning, and research training.
