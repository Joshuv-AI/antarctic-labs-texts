38 U.S.C. § 5123 — Rounding down of pension rates
Citation: us:usc:38:5123
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section5123
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part IV (General Administrative Provisions); Chapter 51 (Claims, Effective Dates, And Payments); Subchapter III (Payment Of Benefits). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The monthly or other periodic rate of pension payable to an individual under section 1521, 1541, or 1542 of this title or under section 306(a) of the Veterans’ and Survivors’ Pension Improvement Act of 1978 (Public Law 95–588), if not a multiple of $1, shall be rounded down to the nearest dollar.
