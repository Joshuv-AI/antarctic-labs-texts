30 C.F.R. § 250.238 — What well or completions information must my DWOP include?
Citation: us:cfr:30:250.238
Creator: Bureau Of Safety And Environmental Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-250.238
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 250 (Oil And Gas And Sulphur Operations In The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must include the following information in your DWOP, as applicable, to be consistent with the activities to be addressed in the associated well permit(s):
(a) A description and schematic of the typical wellbore, casing, and completion;
(b) Information concerning the drilling and completion systems; and
(c) Design and fabrication information for each wellbore riser system (e.g., drilling, completion, workover, intervention, injection, or production) deployed from a floating production facility or TLP.
