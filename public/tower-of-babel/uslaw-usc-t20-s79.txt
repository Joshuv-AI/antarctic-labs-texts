20 U.S.C. § 79 — Barro Colorado Island in Gatun Lake to be set aside
Citation: us:usc:20:79
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section79
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 3 (Smithsonian Institution, National Museums And Art Galleries); Subchapter IX (Canal Zone Biological Area). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President is authorized and directed to set aside within the Canal Zone an area in Gatun Lake known as Barro Colorado Island in which the natural features shall, except in event of declared national emergency, be left in their natural state for scientific observation and investigation.
