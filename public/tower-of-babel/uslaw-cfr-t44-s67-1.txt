44 C.F.R. § 67.1 — Purpose of part.
Citation: us:cfr:44:67.1
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-67.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 67 (Appeals From Proposed Flood Elevation Determinations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to establish procedures implementing the provisions of section 110 of Flood Disaster Protection Act of 1973.
