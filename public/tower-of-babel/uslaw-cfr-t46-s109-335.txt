46 C.F.R. § 109.335 — Stowage of work vests.
Citation: us:cfr:46:109.335
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-109.335
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 109 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The master or person in charge shall insure that no work vest is stowed where life preservers are stowed.
