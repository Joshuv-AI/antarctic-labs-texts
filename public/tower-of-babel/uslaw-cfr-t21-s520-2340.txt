21 C.F.R. § 520.2340 — Tepoxalin.

Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-520.2340
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 520 (Oral Dosage Form New Animal Drugs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Specifications. Each tablet contains 30, 50, 100, or 200 milligrams (mg) tepoxalin.
(b) Sponsor. See No. 000061 in § 510.600(c) of this chapter.
(c) Conditions of use in dogs—(1) Amount. 10 mg per kilogram (/kg) daily; or 20 mg/kg on the initial day of treatment, followed by 10 mg/kg daily.
(2) Indications for use. For the control of pain and inflammation associated with osteoarthritis.
(3) Limitations. Federal law restricts this drug to use by or on the order of a licensed veterinarian.
