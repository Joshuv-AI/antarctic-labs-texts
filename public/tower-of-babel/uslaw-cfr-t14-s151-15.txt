14 C.F.R. § 151.15 — Federal-aid Airport Program: Policy affecting runway or taxiway remarking.
Citation: us:cfr:14:151.15
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-151.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 151 (Federal Aid To Airports). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No project for developing or improving an airport may be approved for the Program unless it provides for runway or taxiway remarking if the present marking is obliterated by construction, alteration or repair work included in a FAAP project or by the required routing of construction equipment used therein.
