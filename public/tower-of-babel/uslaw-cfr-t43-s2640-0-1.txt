43 C.F.R. § 2640.0-1 — -1 Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2640.0-1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2640.0-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2640 (Faa Airport Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart sets forth procedures for the issuance of conveyance documents for lands under the jurisdiction of the Department of the Interior to public agencies for use as airports and airways.
