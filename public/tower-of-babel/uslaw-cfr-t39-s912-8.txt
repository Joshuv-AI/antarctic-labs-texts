39 C.F.R. § 912.8 — Sufficiency of evidence and information submitted.
Citation: us:cfr:39:912.8
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-912.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 912 (Procedures To Adjudicate Claims For Personal Injury Or Property Damage Arising Out Of The Operation Of The U.S. Postal Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In order to exhaust the administrative remedy provided, a claimant shall submit substantial evidence to prove the extent of any losses incurred and any injury sustained, so as to provide the Postal Service with sufficient evidence for it to properly evaluate the claim.
