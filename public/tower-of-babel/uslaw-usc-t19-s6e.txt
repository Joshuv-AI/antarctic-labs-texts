19 U.S.C. § 6e — Overtime compensation based on standard or daylight saving time
Citation: us:usc:19:6e
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section6e
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 1 (Collection Districts, Ports, And Officers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

On and after June 30, 1949, overtime compensation of customs officers and employees, as authorized by law, shall be based either on standard or daylight saving time, whichever is observed where overtime services are performed.
