7 C.F.R. § 661.2 — Public access and copying.
Citation: us:cfr:7:661.2
Creator: Natural Resources Conservation Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-661.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 661 (Public Information And Right To Privacy). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Natural Resources Conservation Service will make available for public inspection and copying those materials covered by 5 U.S.C. 552(a)(2) as set out in the Secretary's regulations.
