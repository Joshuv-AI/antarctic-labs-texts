16 U.S.C. § 460ss–6 — Definitions

Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter CIV (Klamath River Conservation Area). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this subchapter—
(1)
The term “program” means the Klamath River Basin Conservation Area Restoration Program established under section 460ss–1(b) of this title.
(2)
The term “Secretary” means the Secretary of the Interior.
