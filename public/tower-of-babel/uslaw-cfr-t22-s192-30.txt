22 C.F.R. § 192.30 — Eligibility for benefits.
Citation: us:cfr:22:192.30
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-192.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 192 (Victims Of Terrorism Compensation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A person designated as a captive or family member of a captive under subpart A of this subchapter, shall be eligible for benefits under this subpart.
