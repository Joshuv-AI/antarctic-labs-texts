48 C.F.R. § 7.502 — 7.502 Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:48:7.502
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-7.502
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 7 (Acquisition Planning). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The requirements of this subpart apply to all contracts for services. This subpart does not apply to services obtained through either personnel appointments, advisory committees, or personal services contracts issued under statutory authority.
