50 C.F.R. § 70.7 — Land-use management.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 70 (National Fish Hatcheries). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The land-use management provisions set forth in part 29 of this chapter are equally applicable to national fish hatchery areas.
