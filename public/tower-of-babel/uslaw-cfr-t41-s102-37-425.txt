41 C.F.R. § 102-37.425 — -37.425 Administrative information required to GSA.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-37.425
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-37.425
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-37 (37—Donation Of Surplus Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

FAA must:
(a) Provide copies of internal instructions that outline the scope of FAA's oversight program for enforcing compliance with the terms and conditions of transfer; and
(b) Report any compliance actions involving donations to public airports.
