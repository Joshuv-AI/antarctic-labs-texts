12 C.F.R. § 347.208 — Assessment base deductions by insured branch.
Citation: us:cfr:12:347.208
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-347.208
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 347 (International Banking). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Deposits in an insured branch to the credit of the foreign bank or any of its offices, branches, agencies, or wholly owned subsidiaries may be deducted from the assessment base of the insured branch.
