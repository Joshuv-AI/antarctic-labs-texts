50 C.F.R. § 82.11 — Forms of vouchers.
Citation: us:cfr:50:82.11
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-82.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 82 (Administrative Procedures For Grants-In-Aid (Marine Mammal Protection Act Of 1972)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Vouchers, on forms provided by the Secretary, showing amounts expended on each project, and the Federal portion claimed to be due on account thereof, shall be certified and submitted to the Director by the grantee.
