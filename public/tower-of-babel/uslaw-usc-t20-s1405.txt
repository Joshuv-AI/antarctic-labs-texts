20 U.S.C. § 1405 — Employment of individuals with disabilities
Citation: us:usc:20:1405
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1405
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 33 (Education Of Individuals With Disabilities); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall ensure that each recipient of assistance under this chapter makes positive efforts to employ and advance in employment qualified individuals with disabilities in programs assisted under this chapter.
