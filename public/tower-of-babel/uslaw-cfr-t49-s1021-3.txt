49 C.F.R. § 1021.3 — Enforcement collection designee.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1021.3
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1021.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1021 (Administrative Collection Of Enforcement Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director, Office of Public Assistance, Governmental Affairs, and Compliance, Surface Transportation Board, is the Board's designee to take all necessary action administratively to settle by collection, compromise, suspension or termination, enforcement claims within the contemplation of the Federal Claims Collection Act of 1966.
