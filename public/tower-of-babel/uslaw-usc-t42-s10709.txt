42 U.S.C. § 10709 — Presidential coordination
Citation: us:usc:42:10709
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section10709
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 113 (State Justice Institute). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President may, to the extent not inconsistent with any other applicable law, direct that appropriate support functions of the Federal Government may be made available to the Institute in carrying out its functions under this chapter.
