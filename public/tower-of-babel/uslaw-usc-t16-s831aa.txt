16 U.S.C. § 831aa — Laws repealed
Citation: us:usc:16:831aa
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section831aa
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 12A (Tennessee Valley Authority). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All acts or parts of acts in conflict with this chapter are repealed, so far as they affect the operations contemplated by this chapter.
