15 U.S.C. § 2104 — Imports
Citation: us:usc:15:2104
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section2104
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 48 (Hobby Protection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any item imported into the United States in violation of section 2101(a) or (b) of this title or regulations under section 2101(c) of this title shall be subject to seizure and forfeiture under the customs laws.
