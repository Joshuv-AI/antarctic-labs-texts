42 U.S.C. § 13108 — Savings provisions
Citation: us:usc:42:13108
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section13108
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 133 (Pollution Prevention). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to modify or interfere with the implementation of title III of the Superfund Amendments and Reauthorization Act of 1986 [42 U.S.C. 11001 et seq.].
(b)
Nothing contained in this chapter shall be construed, interpreted or applied to supplant, displace, preempt or otherwise diminish the responsibilities and liabilities under other State or Federal law, whether statutory or common.
