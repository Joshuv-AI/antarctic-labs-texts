34 U.S.C. § 12144 — Applications
Citation: us:usc:34:12144
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section12144
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 121 (Violent Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Subchapter II (Crime Prevention); Part B (Model Intensive Grant Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

To request a grant under this part the chief local elected official of an area shall—
(1)
prepare and submit to the Attorney General an application in such form, at such time, and in accordance with such procedures, as the Attorney General shall establish; and
(2)
provide an assurance that funds received under this part shall be used to supplement, not supplant, non-Federal funds that would otherwise be available for programs funded under this part.
