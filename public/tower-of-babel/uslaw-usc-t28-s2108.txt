28 U.S.C. § 2108 — Proof of amount in controversy
Citation: us:usc:28:2108
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section2108
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part V (Procedure); Chapter 133 (Review—Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Where the power of any court of appeals to review a case depends upon the amount or value in controversy, such amount or value, if not otherwise satisfactorily disclosed upon the record, may be shown and ascertained by the oath of a party to the case or by other competent evidence.
