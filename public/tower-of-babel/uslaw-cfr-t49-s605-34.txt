49 C.F.R. § 605.34 — Remedy where there has been a violation of the agreement.
Citation: us:cfr:49:605.34
Creator: Federal Transit Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-605.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 605 (School Bus Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the Administrator determines, pursuant to this subpart, that there has been a violation of the terms of the agreement or 49 U.S.C. 5323(f)(2), he or she may bar a recipient or operator from the receipt of further financial assistance for public transportation.
