43 C.F.R. § 418.30 — Provisions required in future contracts.
Citation: us:cfr:43:418.30
Creator: Bureau Of Reclamation, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-418.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 418 (Operating Criteria And Procedures For The Newlands Reclamation Project, Nevada). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Bureau must provide in new, amended, or replacement contracts for the operation and maintenance of Project works, for the reservation by the Secretary of rights and options to enforce this part.
