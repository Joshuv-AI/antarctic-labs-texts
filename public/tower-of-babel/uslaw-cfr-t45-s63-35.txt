45 C.F.R. § 63.35 — Dual compensation.
Citation: us:cfr:45:63.35
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-63.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 63 (Grant Programs Administered By The Office Of The Assistant Secretary For Planning And Evaluation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a project staff member or consultant of one grantee is involved simultaneously in two or more projects supported by any funds either under this part or otherwise, he/she may not be compensated for more than 100 percent of his/her time from any funds during any part of the period of dual involvement.
