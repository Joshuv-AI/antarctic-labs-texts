40 C.F.R. § 439.4 — General limitation or standard for pH.
Citation: us:cfr:40:439.4
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-439.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 439 (Pharmaceutical Manufacturing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The pH must remain within the range 6.0 to 9.0 in any discharge subject to BPT, BCT or NSPS limitations or standards in this part.
