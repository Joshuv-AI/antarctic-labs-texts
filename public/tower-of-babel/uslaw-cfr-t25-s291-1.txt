25 C.F.R. § 291.1 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:25:291.1
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-291.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 291 (Class Iii Gaming Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part establish procedures that the Secretary will use to promulgate rules for the conduct of Class III Indian gaming when:
(a) A State and an Indian tribe are unable to voluntarily agree to a compact and;
(b) The State has asserted its immunity from suit brought by an Indian tribe under 25 U.S.C. 2710(d)(7)(B).
