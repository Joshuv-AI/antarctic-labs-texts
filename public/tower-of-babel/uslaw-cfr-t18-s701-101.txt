18 C.F.R. § 701.101 — Field committees.
Citation: us:cfr:18:701.101
Creator: Water Resources Council
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-701.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 701 (Council Organization). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Council may establish or continue already established regional committees to carry out assigned functions at field level.
