46 C.F.R. § 179.115 — Applicability to existing vessels.
Citation: us:cfr:46:179.115
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-179.115
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 179 (Subdivision, Damage Stability, And Watertight Integrity). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An existing vessel must comply with the subdivision, damage stability, and watertight integrity regulations which were applicable to the vessel on March 10, 1996, or, as an alternative, the vessel may comply with the regulations in this part.
