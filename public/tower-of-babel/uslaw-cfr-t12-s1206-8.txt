12 C.F.R. § 1206.8 — Enforcement of payment.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1206.8
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1206.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1206 (Assessments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director may enforce the payment of any assessment under 12 U.S.C. 4631 (cease-and-desist proceedings), 12 U.S.C. 4632 (temporary cease-and-desist orders), and 12 U.S.C. 4626 (civil money penalties).
