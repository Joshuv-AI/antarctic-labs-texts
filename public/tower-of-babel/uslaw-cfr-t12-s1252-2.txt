12 C.F.R. § 1252.2 — Effective duration.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1252.2
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1252.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1252 (Portfolio Holdings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part shall be in effect for each Enterprise so long as—
(a) This part has not been superseded through amendment, and
(b) The Enterprise remains subject to the terms and obligations of the respective Senior Preferred Stock Purchase Agreement.
