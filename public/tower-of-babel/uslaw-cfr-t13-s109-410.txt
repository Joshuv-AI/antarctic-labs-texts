13 C.F.R. § 109.410 — Loan limits—loans to Eligible Small Business Concerns.
Citation: us:cfr:13:109.410
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-109.410
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 109 (Intermediary Lending Pilot Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No small business (including Affiliates) may have more than $200,000 outstanding under this program at one time. The provisions of § 120.151 do not apply to loans under this program.
