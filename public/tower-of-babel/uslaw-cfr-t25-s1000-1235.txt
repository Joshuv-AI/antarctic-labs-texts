25 C.F.R. § 1000.1235 — How does the Secretary respond to a waiver request?
Citation: us:cfr:25:1000.1235
Creator: Office Of The Assistant Secretary, Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-1000.1235
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 1000 (Annual Funding Agreements Under The Tribal Self-Government Act Amendments To The Indian Self-Determination And Education Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Within 10 business days of receipt, the officials designated by the Secretary in § 1000.1215 will email to the Tribe/Consortium a letter:
(a) Acknowledging receipt of the waiver request; and
(b) Identifying the date the waiver request was received and the last day of the applicable statutory review period.
