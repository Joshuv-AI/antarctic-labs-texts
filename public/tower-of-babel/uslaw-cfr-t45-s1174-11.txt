45 C.F.R. § 1174.11 — Referral of complaint and answer to the ALJ.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1174.11
Creator: National Foundation On The Arts And The Humanities
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1174.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1174 (Program Fraud Civil Remedies Act Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When the reviewing official receives an answer, he or she must simultaneously file the complaint, the answer, and a designation of the authority's representative with the ALJ.
