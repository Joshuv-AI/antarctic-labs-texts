40 C.F.R. § 422.10 — Applicability; description of the phosphorus production subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:422.10
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-422.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 422 (Phosphate Manufacturing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to discharges of pollutants resulting from the production of phosphorus and ferrophosphorus by smelting of phosphate ore.
