15 U.S.C. § 7101 — Establishment of the Interagency Committee
Citation: us:usc:15:7101
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section7101
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 97 (Women’S Business Enterprise Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is established an interagency committee to be known as the Interagency Committee on Women’s Business Enterprise.
