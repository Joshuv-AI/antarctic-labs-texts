45 C.F.R. § 162.1801 — Coordination of benefits transaction.
Citation: us:cfr:45:162.1801
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-162.1801
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 162 (Administrative Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The coordination of benefits transaction is the transmission from any entity to a health plan for the purpose of determining the relative payment responsibilities of the health plan, of either of the following for health care:
(a) Claims.
(b) Payment information.
