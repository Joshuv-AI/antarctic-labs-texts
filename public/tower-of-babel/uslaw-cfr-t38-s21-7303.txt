38 C.F.R. § 21.7303 — Revision of decisions.
Citation: us:cfr:38:21.7303
Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-21.7303
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 21 (Veteran Readiness And Employment And Education). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The revision of a decision on which an action was predicated is subject to the following sections:
(a) Clear and unmistakable error, § 3.105(a) of this chapter; and
(b) Difference of opinion, § 3.105(b) of this chapter.
