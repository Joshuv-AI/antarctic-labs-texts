31 C.F.R. § 315.43 — Effective date of request for payment.
Citation: us:cfr:31:315.43
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-315.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 315 (Regulations Governing U.S. Savings Bonds, Series A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, J, And K, And U.S. Savings Notes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Department of the Treasury will treat the receipt of a bond with an appropiate request for payment by:
(a) A Federal Reserve Bank or Branch,
(b) The Bureau of the Fiscal Service, or
(c) A paying agent authorized to pay that bond, as the date upon which the rights of the parties are fixed for the purpose of payment.
