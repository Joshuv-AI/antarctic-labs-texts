43 C.F.R. § 3838.91 — What if I fail to comply with this part?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3838.91
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3838.91
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3838 (Special Procedures For Locating And Recording Mining Claims And Tunnel Sites On Stockraising Homestead Act (Srha) Lands). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you fail to comply with the requirements in this part, the NOITL is void. Mining claims or tunnel sites located under a void NOITL are null and void from the beginning and we will cancel them.
