7 C.F.R. § 4290.1720 — Characteristics of Agency's guarantee.
Citation: us:cfr:7:4290.1720
Creator: Rural Business-Cooperative Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-4290.1720
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 4290 (Rural Business Investment Company (“Rbic”) Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the Agency agrees to guarantee a RBIC's Debentures, such guarantee will be unconditional, irrespective of the validity, regularity or enforceability of the Debentures or any other circumstances that might constitute a legal or equitable discharge or defense of a guarantor. Pursuant to its guarantee, the Agency will make timely payments of principal and interest on the Debentures.
