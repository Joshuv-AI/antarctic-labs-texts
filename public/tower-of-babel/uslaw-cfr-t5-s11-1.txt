5 C.F.R. § 11.1 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:5:11.1
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-11.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 11 (Probationary And Trial Periods (Rule Xi)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This rule applies to probationary periods in the competitive service and trial periods in the excepted service, except where provided otherwise by statute. It has no application to probationary periods in the Senior Executive Service.
