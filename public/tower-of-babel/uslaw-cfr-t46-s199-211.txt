46 C.F.R. § 199.211 — Lifebuoys.
Citation: us:cfr:46:199.211
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-199.211
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 199 (Lifesaving Systems For Certain Inspected Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each passenger vessel must carry the number of lifebuoys prescribed in table 199.211 of this section.
(b) Notwithstanding § 199.70(a)(3)(ii), each passenger vessel under 60 meters (196 feet) in length must carry at least six lifebuoys with self-igniting lights.
