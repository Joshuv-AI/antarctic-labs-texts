31 C.F.R. § 8.40 — Conflicting interests.
Citation: us:cfr:31:8.40
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-8.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 8 (Practice Before The Bureau Of Alcohol, Tobacco And Firearms). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No attorney, certified public accountant, or enrolled practitioner may represent conflicting interests in practice before the Bureau, except by express consent of all directly interested parties after full disclosure has been made.
