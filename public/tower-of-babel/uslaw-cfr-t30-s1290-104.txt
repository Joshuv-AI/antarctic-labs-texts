30 C.F.R. § 1290.104 — What may I not appeal under this part?
Citation: us:cfr:30:1290.104
Creator: Office Of Natural Resources Revenue, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-1290.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 1290 (Appeal Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may not appeal:
(a) An action that is not an order, as defined in this part; or
(b) A determination of the surety amount or financial solvency under 30 CFR part 243, parts B or C.
