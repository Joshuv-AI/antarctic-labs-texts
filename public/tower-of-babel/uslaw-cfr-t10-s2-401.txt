10 C.F.R. § 2.401 — Notice of hearing on construction permit or combined license applications pursuant to appendix N of 10 CFR part 50, 52, or 53.
Citation: us:cfr:10:2.401
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-2.401
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 2 (Agency Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) In the case of applications under appendix N of part 50 or § 53.1470 of this chapter for construction permits for nuclear power reactors of the type described in § 50.22 or part 53 of this chapter, or applications under appendix N of part 52 or § 53.1470 of this chapter for combined licenses, the Secretary will issue notices of hearing pursuant to § 2.104.
(b) The notice of hearing will also state the time and place of the hearings on any separate phase of the proceeding.
