16 U.S.C. § 408f — Former Siskiwit Islands Bird Reservation
Citation: us:usc:16:408f
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section408f
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter LII (Isle Royale National Park). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Siskiwit Islands Bird Reservation is abolished and shall after March 6, 1942, be a part of the Isle Royale National Park.
