19 C.F.R. § 122.3 — Availability of forms.
Citation: us:cfr:19:122.3
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-122.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 122 (Air Commerce Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The forms mentioned in this part may be purchased from the director of port of entry. A small quantity of each form is set aside by port directors for free distribution and official use.
