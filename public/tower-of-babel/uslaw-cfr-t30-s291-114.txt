30 C.F.R. § 291.114 — How do I appeal to the IBLA?
Citation: us:cfr:30:291.114
Creator: Bureau Of Safety And Environmental Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-291.114
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 291 (Open And Nondiscriminatory Access To Oil And Gas Pipelines Under The Outer Continental Shelf Lands Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any party, except as provided in § 291.115(b), adversely affected by a decision of the BSEE Director under this part may appeal to the Interior Board of Land Appeals (IBLA) under the procedures in 43 CFR part 4, subpart E.
