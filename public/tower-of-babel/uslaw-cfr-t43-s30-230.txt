43 C.F.R. § 30.230 — Who pays witnesses' costs?
Citation: us:cfr:43:30.230
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-30.230
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 30 (Indian Probate Hearings Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Interested parties who desire a witness to testify at a hearing must make their own financial and other arrangements for the witness.
