51 U.S.C. § 20164 — International cooperation
Citation: us:usc:51:20164
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section20164
Context: United States Code, Title 51 (National And Commercial Space Programs); Chapter 201 (National Aeronautics And Space Program); Subchapter IV (Upper Atmosphere Research). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In carrying out the provisions of this subchapter, the Administration, subject to the direction of the President and after consultation with the Secretary of State, shall make every effort to enlist the support and cooperation of appropriate scientists and engineers of other countries and international organizations.
