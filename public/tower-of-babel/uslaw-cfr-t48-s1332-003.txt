48 C.F.R. § 1332.003 — 1332.003 Simplified acquisition procedures financing.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1332.003
Creator: Chapter 13—Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1332.003
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1332 (Contract Financing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Contract financing may be provided for purchases made under the authority of FAR Part 13. Contract financing shall be made in accordance with FAR Part 32.
