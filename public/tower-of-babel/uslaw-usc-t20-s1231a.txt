20 U.S.C. § 1231a — Collection and dissemination of information
Citation: us:usc:20:1231a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1231a
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 31 (General Provisions Concerning Education); Subchapter III (General Requirements And Conditions Concerning Operation And Administration Of Education Programs: General Authority Of Secretary); Part 1 (General Authority). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall—
(1)
prepare and disseminate to State and local educational agencies and institutions information concerning applicable programs, and cooperate with other Federal officials who administer programs affecting education in disseminating information concerning such programs;
(2)
inform the public regarding federally supported education programs; and
(3)
collect data and information on applicable programs for the purpose of obtaining objective measurements of the effectiveness of such programs in achieving the intended purposes of such programs.
