51 U.S.C. § 50703 — Annual report
Citation: us:usc:51:50703
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section50703
Context: United States Code, Title 51 (National And Commercial Space Programs); Chapter 507 (Office Of Space Commerce). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Commerce shall submit an annual report on the activities of the Office, including planned programs and expenditures, to the Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation of the Senate and the Committee on Science and Technology of the House of Representatives.
