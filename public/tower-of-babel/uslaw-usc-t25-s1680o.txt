25 U.S.C. § 1680o — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:25:1680o
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section1680o
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 18 (Indian Health Care); Subchapter VI (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated such sums as are necessary to carry out this chapter for fiscal year 2010 and each fiscal year thereafter, to remain available until expended.
