49 C.F.R. § 376.42 — Lease of equipment by regulated carriers.
Citation: us:cfr:49:376.42
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-376.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 376 (Lease And Interchange Of Vehicles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Authorized carriers may lease equipment and drivers from private carriers, for periods of less than 30 days, in the manner set forth in § 376.22.
