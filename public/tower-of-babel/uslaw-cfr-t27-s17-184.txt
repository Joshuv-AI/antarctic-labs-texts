27 C.F.R. § 17.184 — Distilled spirits container marks.
Citation: us:cfr:27:17.184
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-17.184
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 17 (Drawback On Taxpaid Distilled Spirits Used In Manufacturing Nonbeverage Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All marks required by part 19 of this chapter shall remain on containers of taxpaid distilled spirits until the contents are emptied. Whenever such a container is emptied, such marks shall be completely obliterated.
