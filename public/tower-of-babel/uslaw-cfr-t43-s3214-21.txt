43 C.F.R. § 3214.21 — What must I do if I want to use a certificate of deposit to back my bond?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3214.21
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3214.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3200 (Geothermal Resource Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Your certificate of deposit must:
(a) Be issued by a Federally-insured financial institution authorized to do business in the United States;
(b) Include on its face the statement, “This certificate cannot be redeemed by any party without approval by the Secretary of the Interior or the Secretary's delegate;” and
(c) Be payable to the Department of the Interior, Bureau of Land Management.
