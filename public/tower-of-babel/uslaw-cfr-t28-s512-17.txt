28 C.F.R. § 512.17 — Monitoring approved research projects.
Citation: us:cfr:28:512.17
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-512.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 512 (Research). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The BRRB shall monitor all research projects for compliance with Bureau policies. At a minimum, yearly reviews will be conducted.
