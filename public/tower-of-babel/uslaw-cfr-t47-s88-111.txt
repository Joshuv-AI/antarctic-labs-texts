47 C.F.R. § 88.111 — Certification required.
Citation: us:cfr:47:88.111
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-88.111
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 88 (Uncrewed Aircraft System Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each transmitter utilized for operation under this part and each transmitter marketed as set forth in § 2.803 of this chapter must be certified by the Commission for use in part 88 services following the procedures set forth in part 2, subpart J of this chapter.
