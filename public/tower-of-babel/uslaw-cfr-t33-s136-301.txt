33 C.F.R. § 136.301 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:33:136.301
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-136.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 136 (Oil Spill Liability Trust Fund; Claims Procedures; Designation Of Source; And Advertisement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes the requirements concerning designation of the source or sources of the discharge or threat of discharge and advertisement of these designations, including the procedures by which claims may be presented to the responsible party or guarantor.
