10 C.F.R. § 862.7 — Designation of sites.
Citation: us:cfr:10:862.7
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-862.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 862 (Restrictions On Aircraft Landing And Air Delivery At Department Of Energy Nuclear Sites). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) DOE shall designate sites covered by this part as deemed necessary, consistent with the national security and public safety, through notice in the Federal Register.
(b) This part shall be effective as to any facility, installation, or real property on publication in the Federal Register of the notice designating the site.
(c) Upon designation of a site, the cognizant Manager of Operations may inform the public of such designation through press release or posting of notice at airfields in the vicinity of the designated site.
