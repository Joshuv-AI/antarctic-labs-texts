45 C.F.R. § 1322.37 — Title VI and Title III coordination for emergency and disaster preparedness.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1322.37
Creator: Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1322.37
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1322 (Grants To Indian Tribes And Native Hawaiian Grantees For Supportive, Nutrition, And Caregiver Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Tribal organization or Hawaiian Native grantee under Title VI of the Act and State and area agencies funded under Title III of the Act should coordinate in emergency and disaster preparedness planning, response, and recovery. A Tribal organization or Hawaiian Native grantee must have policies and procedures in place for how they will communicate and coordinate with State agencies and area agencies regarding emergency and disaster preparedness planning, response, and recovery.
