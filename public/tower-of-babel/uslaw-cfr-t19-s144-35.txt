19 C.F.R. § 144.35 — Withdrawal of vessel and aircraft supplies and equipment.
Citation: us:cfr:19:144.35
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-144.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 144 (Warehouse And Rewarehouse Entries And Withdrawals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Supplies and equipment for vessels and aircraft may be withdrawn from warehouse under the procedures set forth in this subpart and in §§ 10.59 through 10.65 of this chapter.
