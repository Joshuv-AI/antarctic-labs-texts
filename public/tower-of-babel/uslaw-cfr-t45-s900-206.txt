45 C.F.R. § 900.206 — Environmental impact statement.
Citation: us:cfr:45:900.206
Creator: Denali Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-900.206
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 900 (National Environmental Policy Act Implementing Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An EIS is required when the project is determined to have a potentially significant impact on the human environment. EISs shall be prepared in accordance with subpart D of this part.
