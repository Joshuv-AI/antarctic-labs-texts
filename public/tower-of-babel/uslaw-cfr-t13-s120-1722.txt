13 C.F.R. § 120.1722 — SBA's offset rights.
Citation: us:cfr:13:120.1722
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-120.1722
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 120 (Business Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

SBA shall have the right to offset any amount owed by Lender to SBA, including, without limitation, an offset against CSA's obligation to pay Lender pursuant to any Section 504 First Mortgage Loan Pool Guarantee Agreement.
