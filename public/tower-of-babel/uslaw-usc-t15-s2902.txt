15 U.S.C. § 2902 — Purpose
Citation: us:usc:15:2902
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section2902
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 56 (National Climate Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the purpose of the Congress in this chapter to establish a national climate program that will assist the Nation and the world to understand and respond to natural and man-induced climate processes and their implications.
