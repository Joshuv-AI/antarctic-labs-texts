42 C.F.R. § 422.402 — Federal preemption of State law.
Citation: us:cfr:42:422.402
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-422.402
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 422 (Medicare Advantage Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The standards established under this part supersede any State law or regulation (other than State licensing laws or State laws relating to plan solvency) with respect to the MA plans that are offered by MA organizations.
