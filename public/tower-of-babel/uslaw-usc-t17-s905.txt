17 U.S.C. § 905 — Exclusive rights in mask works
Citation: us:usc:17:905
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title17-section905
Context: United States Code, Title 17 (Copyrights); Chapter 9 (Protection Of Semiconductor Chip Products). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The owner of a mask work provided protection under this chapter has the exclusive rights to do and to authorize any of the following:
(1)
to reproduce the mask work by optical, electronic, or any other means;
(2)
to import or distribute a semiconductor chip product in which the mask work is embodied; and
(3)
to induce or knowingly to cause another person to do any of the acts described in paragraphs (1) and (2).
