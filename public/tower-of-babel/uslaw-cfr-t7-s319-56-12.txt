7 C.F.R. § 319.56-12 — -12 Importation of frozen fruits and vegetables.
Citation: us:cfr:7:319.56-12
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-319.56-12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 319 (Foreign Quarantine Notices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Frozen fruits and vegetables may be imported into the United States in accordance with § 319.56-3. Such fruits and vegetables must be held in accordance with the requirements for importing frozen fruits and vegetables in part 305 of this chapter..
