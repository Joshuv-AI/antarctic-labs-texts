28 C.F.R. § 10.2 — Language of registration statement.
Citation: us:cfr:28:10.2
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-10.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 10 (Registration Of Certain Organizations Carrying On Activities Within The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Registration statements must be in English if possible. If in a foreign language they must be accompanied by an English translation certified under oath by the translator, before a notary public or other person authorized by law to administer oaths for general purposes as a true and adequate translation. The statements, with the exception of signature, must be typewritten if practicable but will be accepted if written legibly in ink.
