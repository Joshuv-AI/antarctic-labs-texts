43 C.F.R. § 5511.3-4 — -4 Removal by agent.
Citation: us:cfr:43:5511.3-4
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-5511.3-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 5510 (Free Use Of Timber). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A free-use permittee may procure the timber by agent. Such agent shall not, however, be paid more than fair compensation for the time, labor and money expended in procuring timber and processing it, and no charge shall be made by such agent for the timber itself. No part of the timber may be used in payment for services in obtaining it or processing it.
