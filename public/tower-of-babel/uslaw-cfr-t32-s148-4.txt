32 C.F.R. § 148.4 — Policy documentation.
Citation: us:cfr:32:148.4
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-148.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 148 (National Policy And Implementation Of Reciprocity Of Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agency heads shall ensure that any policy documents their agency issues setting out facilities security policies and procedures incorporate the policy set out herein, and that such policies are reasonable, effective, efficient, and enable and promote interagency reciprocity.
