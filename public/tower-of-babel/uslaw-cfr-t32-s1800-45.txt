32 C.F.R. § 1800.45 — Notification of decision and right of judicial review.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1800.45
Creator: National Counterintelligence Center
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1800.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1800 (Public Access To Nacic Records Under The Freedom Of Information Act (Foia)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Coordinator shall promptly prepare and communicate the decision of the Director, NACIC to the requester. With respect to any decision to deny information, that correspondence shall state the reasons for the decision, identify the officer responsible, and include a notice of a right to judicial review.
