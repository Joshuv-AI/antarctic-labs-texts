29 C.F.R. § 4302.3 — Penalty amount.
Citation: us:cfr:29:4302.3
Creator: Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-4302.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 4302 (Penalties For Failure To Provide Certain Multiemployer Plan Notices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The maximum daily amount of the penalty under section 4302 of ERISA shall be $365.
