21 C.F.R. § 1143.9 — Irremovable or permanent required warning statements.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1143.9
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1143.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1143 (Minimum Required Warning Statements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The warning statements required by this section must be indelibly printed on or permanently affixed to the package or advertisement. These warnings, for example, must not be printed or placed on a product label affixed to a clear outer wrapper that is likely to be removed to access the product within the package.
