41 C.F.R. § 304-9.2 — -9.2 Accepting contributions and awards.
Citation: us:cfr:41:304-9.2
Creator: Chapter 304—Payment Of Travel Expenses From A Non-Federal Source
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-304-9.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 304-9 (9—Contributions And Awards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies may allow an employee to accept contributions and awards pertaining to training and payments incident to attendance at meetings when specifically authorized to do so in accordance with Office of Personnel Management guidelines (5 CFR part 410, subpart E).
