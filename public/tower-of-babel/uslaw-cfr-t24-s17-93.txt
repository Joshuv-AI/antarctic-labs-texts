24 C.F.R. § 17.93 — Result if employee fails to meet deadlines.
Citation: us:cfr:24:17.93
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-17.93
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 17 (Administrative Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee waives the right to a hearing, and will have his or her disposable pay offset in accordance with the Secretary's offset schedule, if the employee:
(a) Fails to file a petition for a hearing as prescribed in § 17.91; or
(b) Is scheduled to appear and fails to appear at the hearing.
