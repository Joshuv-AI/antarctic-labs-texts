21 C.F.R. § 203.11 — Applications for reimportation to provide emergency medical care.
Citation: us:cfr:21:203.11
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-203.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 203 (Prescription Drug Marketing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Applications for reimportation for emergency medical care shall be submitted to the director of the FDA District Office in the district where reimportation is sought (addresses found in part 5, subpart M of this chapter).
(b) Applications for reimportation to provide emergency medical care shall be reviewed and approved or disapproved by each district office.
