31 C.F.R. § 14.2 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:31:14.2
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-14.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 14 (Right To Financial Privacy Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of these regulations is to authorize Departmental units to request financial records from a financial institution pursuant to the formal written request procedure authorized by section 1108 of the Act, and to set forth the conditions under which such requests may be made.
