52 U.S.C. § 20928 — Requiring majority approval for actions
Citation: us:usc:52:20928
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title52-section20928
Context: United States Code, Title 52 (Voting And Elections); Chapter 209 (Election Administration Improvement); Subchapter II (Commission); Part A (Establishment And General Organization); Subpart 1 (Election Assistance Commission). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any action which the Commission is authorized to carry out under this chapter may be carried out only with the approval of at least three of its members.
