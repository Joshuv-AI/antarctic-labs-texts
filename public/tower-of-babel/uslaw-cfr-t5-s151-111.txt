5 C.F.R. § 151.111 — Permissible activities.
Citation: us:cfr:5:151.111
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-151.111
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 151 (Political Activity Of State Or Local Officers Or Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) All State or local officers or employees are free to engage in political activity to the widest extent consistent with the restrictions imposed by law and this part. A State or local officer or employee may participate in all political activity not specifically restricted by law and this part, including candidacy for office in a nonpartisan election and candidacy for political party office.
