9 C.F.R. § 321.2 — Cooperation of States in Federal programs.
Citation: us:cfr:9:321.2
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-321.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 321 (Cooperation With States And Territories). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Under the “Talmadge-Aiken Act” of September 28, 1962 (7 U.S.C. 450), the Administrator is authorized to utilize employees and facilities of any State in carrying out Federal functions under the Federal Meat Inspection Act. A cooperative program for this purpose is called a Federal-State program.
