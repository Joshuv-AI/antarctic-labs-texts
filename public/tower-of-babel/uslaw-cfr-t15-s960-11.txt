15 C.F.R. § 960.11 — No additional conditions.
Citation: us:cfr:15:960.11
Creator: National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-960.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 960 (Licensing Of Private Remote Sensing Space Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No other conditions shall be included in a license granted under this part, or imposed in such a license after the license has been issued, except in accordance with the provisions of § 960.13 or § 960.17.
