16 U.S.C. § 6234 — Relationship to other laws
Citation: us:usc:16:6234
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section6234
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 81A (National Forest Organizational Camp Fee Improvement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as specifically provided by this chapter, nothing in this chapter supersedes or otherwise affects any provision of law, regulation, or policy regarding the issuance or administration of authorizations for organizational camps regarding the occupancy and use of National Forest System lands.
