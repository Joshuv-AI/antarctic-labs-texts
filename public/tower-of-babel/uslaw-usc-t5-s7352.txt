5 U.S.C. § 7352 — Excessive and habitual use of intoxicants
Citation: us:usc:5:7352
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section7352
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart F (Labor-Management And Employee Relations); Chapter 73 (Suitability, Security, And Conduct); Subchapter V (Misconduct). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An individual who habitually uses intoxicating beverages to excess may not be employed in the competitive service.
