10 U.S.C. § 10107 — Army National Guard of the United States: status when not in Federal service
Citation: us:usc:10:10107
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section10107
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization And Administration); Chapter 1003 (Reserve Components Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When not on active duty, members of the Army National Guard of the United States shall be administered, armed, equipped, and trained in their status as members of the Army National Guard.
