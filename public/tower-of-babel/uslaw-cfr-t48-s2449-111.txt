48 C.F.R. § 2449.111 — 2449.111 Review of proposed settlements.
Citation: us:cfr:48:2449.111
Creator: Chapter 24—Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-2449.111
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2449 (Termination Of Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Head of the Contracting Activity shall establish internal procedures to ensure the independent review of proposed termination settlements in excess of $100,000.
