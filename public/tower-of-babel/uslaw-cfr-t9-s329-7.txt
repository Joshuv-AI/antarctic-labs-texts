9 C.F.R. § 329.7 — Procedure for seizure, condemnation, and disposition.
Citation: us:cfr:9:329.7
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-329.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 329 (Detention; Seizure And Condemnation; Criminal Offenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any article or livestock subject to seizure and condemnation under this part shall be liable to be proceeded against and seized and condemned, and disposed of, at any time, on an appropriate pleading in any United States district court, or other proper court specified in section 404 of the Act, within the jurisdiction of which the article or livestock is found.
