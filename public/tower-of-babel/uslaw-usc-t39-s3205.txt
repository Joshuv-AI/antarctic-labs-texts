39 U.S.C. § 3205 — Accounting for penalty covers
Citation: us:usc:39:3205
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title39-section3205
Context: United States Code, Title 39 (Postal Service); Part IV (Mail Matter); Chapter 32 (Penalty And Franked Mail). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Executive departments and agencies, independent establishments of the Government of the United States, and organizations and persons authorized by law to use penalty mail, shall account for all penalty covers through the Postal Service.
