34 C.F.R. § 76.660 — Use of private school personnel.
Citation: us:cfr:34:76.660
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-76.660
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 76 (State-Administered Formula Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A subgrantee may use program funds to pay for the services of an employee of a private school if:
(a) The employee performs the services outside of his or her regular hours of duty; and
(b) The employee performs the services under public supervision and control.
