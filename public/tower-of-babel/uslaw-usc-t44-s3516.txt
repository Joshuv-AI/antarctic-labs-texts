44 U.S.C. § 3516 — Rules and regulations

Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 35 (Coordination Of Federal Information Policy); Subchapter I (Federal Information Policy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director shall promulgate rules, regulations, or procedures necessary to exercise the authority provided by this subchapter.
