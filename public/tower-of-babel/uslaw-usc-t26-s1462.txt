26 U.S.C. § 1462 — Withheld tax as credit to recipient of income
Citation: us:usc:26:1462
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section1462
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 3 (Withholding Of Tax On Nonresident Aliens And Foreign Corporations); Subchapter B (Application Of Withholding Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Income on which any tax is required to be withheld at the source under this chapter shall be included in the return of the recipient of such income, but any amount of tax so withheld shall be credited against the amount of income tax as computed in such return.
