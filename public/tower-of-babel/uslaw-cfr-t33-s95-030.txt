33 C.F.R. § 95.030 — Evidence of under the influence of alcohol or a dangerous drug.
Citation: us:cfr:33:95.030
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-95.030
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 95 (Operating A Vessel While Under The Influence Of Alcohol Or A Dangerous Drug). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Acceptable evidence of when a vessel operator is under the influence of alcohol or a dangerous drug includes, but is not limited to:
(a) Personal observation of an individual's manner, disposition, speech, muscular movement, general appearance, or behavior; or,
(b) A chemical test.
