29 C.F.R. § 403.6 — Personal responsibility of signatories of reports.
Citation: us:cfr:29:403.6
Creator: Office Of Labor-Management Standards, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-403.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 403 (Labor Organization Annual Financial Reports). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each individual required to sign a report under section 201(b) of the Act and under this part shall be personally responsible for the filing of such report and for any statement contained therein which he knows to be false.
