7 C.F.R. § 4290.1120 — General eligibility requirements for Leverage.
Citation: us:cfr:7:4290.1120
Creator: Rural Business-Cooperative Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-4290.1120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 4290 (Rural Business Investment Company (“Rbic”) Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To be eligible for Leverage, you must be in compliance with the Act, the regulations in this part, and your Participation Agreement.
