50 U.S.C. § 857 — Compliance with other registration statutes
Citation: us:usc:50:857
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section857
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 23 (Internal Security); Subchapter V (Registration Of Certain Persons Trained In Foreign Espionage Systems). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Compliance with the registration provisions of this subchapter does not relieve any person from compliance with any other applicable registration statute.
