34 C.F.R. § 603.22 — Inclusion on list.
Citation: us:cfr:34:603.22
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-603.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 603 (Secretary'S Recognition Procedures For State Agencies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any State agency which desires to be listed by the Secretary as meeting the criteria set forth in § 603.24 should apply in writing to the Director, Division of Eligibility and Agency Evaluation, Office of Postsecondary Education, Department of Education, Washington, DC 20202.
