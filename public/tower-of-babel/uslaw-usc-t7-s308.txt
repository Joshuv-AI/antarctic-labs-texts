7 U.S.C. § 308 — Reports by State governors of sale of scrip
Citation: us:usc:7:308
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section308
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 13 (Agricultural And Mechanical Colleges); Subchapter I (College-Aid Land Appropriation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The governors of the several States to which scrip shall be issued under the provisions of this subchapter shall be required to report annually to Congress all sales made of such scrip until the whole shall be disposed of, the amount received for the same, and what appropriation has been made of the proceeds.
