28 C.F.R. § 1.4 — Offenses against the laws of possessions or territories of the United States.
Citation: us:cfr:28:1.4
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-1.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 1 (Executive Clemency). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Petitions for executive clemency shall relate only to violations of laws of the United States. Petitions relating to violations of laws of the possessions of the United States or territories subject to the jurisdiction of the United States should be submitted to the appropriate official or agency of the possession or territory concerned.
