48 C.F.R. § 244.403 — 244.403 Contract clause.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 244 (Subcontracting Policies And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Use the clause at 252.244-7000, Subcontracts for Commercial Products and Commercial Services, in solicitations and contracts, including solicitations and contracts using FAR part 12 procedures for the acquisition of commercial products and commercial services.
