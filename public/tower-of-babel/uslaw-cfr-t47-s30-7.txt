47 C.F.R. § 30.7 — Lower 37 GHz band—Shared coordinated service.

Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-30.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 30 (Upper Microwave Flexible Use Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For licensing and operational rules applicable to the Lower 37 GHz Band, see subpart F of this part. Unless otherwise noted, the technical standards in subpart C of this part shall apply to the Lower 37 GHz band.
