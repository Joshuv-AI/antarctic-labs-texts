48 C.F.R. § 622.406-9 — 622.406-9 Withholding from or suspension of contract payments.
Citation: us:cfr:48:622.406-9
Creator: Chapter 6—Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-622.406-9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 622 (Application Of Labor Laws To Government Acquisitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The authority to suspend contract payments pursuant to FAR 22.406-9(b) is delegated, without power of redelegation, to the head of the contracting activity.
