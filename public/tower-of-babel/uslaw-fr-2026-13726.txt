Revisions to Financial Forms Reporting and Filing Requirements;

AGENCY: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

ACTION: Notice of proposed rulemaking; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission published a document 
in the Federal Register of June 24, 2026, concerning a notice of 
proposed rulemaking seeking comment on the Commission's proposal to 
amend certain annual and quarterly financial forms, and its current 
regulations governing certain FERC financial forms. The document 
contained an error.

DATES: This correction is effective July 7, 2026, and applicable as of 
June 24, 2026.
