34 C.F.R. § 675.32 — Program description.
Citation: us:cfr:34:675.32
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-675.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 675 (Federal Work-Study Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An institution may expend up to the lesser of $50,000 or 10 percent of its FWS allocation and reallocation for an award year to establish or expand a program under which the institution, separately or in combination with other eligible institutions, locates and develops jobs, including community service jobs, for currently enrolled students.
