49 C.F.R. § 214.3 — Application.
Citation: us:cfr:49:214.3
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-214.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 214 (Railroad Workplace Safety). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to railroads that operate rolling equipment on track that is part of the general railroad system of transportation.
