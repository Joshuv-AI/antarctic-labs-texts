10 C.F.R. § 1016.14 — Protection of Restricted Data while in use.
Citation: us:cfr:10:1016.14
Creator: Department Of Energy (General Provisions)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1016.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1016 (Safeguarding Of Restricted Data By Access Permittees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

While in use, classified matter containing Restricted Data shall be under the direct control of a person with the appropriate access authorization and need to know. Unauthorized access to the Restricted Data shall be precluded.
