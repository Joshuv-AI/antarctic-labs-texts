30 C.F.R. § 918.16 — Required program amendments.
Citation: us:cfr:30:918.16
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-918.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 918 (Louisiana). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Pursuant to 30 CFR 732.17(f)(1), Louisiana is required to submit to OSM by the specified date the following written, proposed program amendment, or a description of an amendment to be proposed, that meets the requirements of SMCRA and 30 CFR chapter VII and a timetable for enactment that is consistent with Louisiana's established administrative or legislative procedures.
