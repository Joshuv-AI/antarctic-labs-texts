43 C.F.R. § 3203.13 — How often will BLM hold a competitive lease sale?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3203.13
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3203.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3200 (Geothermal Resource Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

BLM will hold a competitive lease sale at least once every 2 years for lands available for leasing in a state that has nominations pending. A sale may include lands in more than one state. BLM may hold a competitive lease sale in a state that has no nominations pending.
