29 C.F.R. § 1425.6 — Use of third-party mediation assistance.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1425.6
Creator: Federal Mediation And Conciliation Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1425.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1425 (Mediation Assistance In The Federal Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the parties should mutually agree to third-party mediation assistance other than that of the Service, both parties shall immediately inform the Service in writing of this agreement. Such written communication shall be filed with the regional director of the region in which the negotiation is scheduled, and shall state what alternate assistance the parties have agreed to use.
