10 C.F.R. § 70.20 — General license to own special nuclear material.
Citation: us:cfr:10:70.20
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-70.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 70 (Domestic Licensing Of Special Nuclear Material). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A general license is hereby issued to receive title to and own special nuclear material without regard to quantity. Notwithstanding any other provision of this chapter, a general licensee under this section is not authorized to acquire, deliver, receive, possess, use, transfer, import, or export special nuclear material, except as authorized in a specific license.
