19 U.S.C. § 3007 — Publication of Harmonized Tariff Schedule
Citation: us:usc:19:3007
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section3007
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 18 (Implementation Of Harmonized Tariff Schedule). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
The Commission shall compile and publish, at appropriate intervals, and keep up to date the Harmonized Tariff Schedule and related information in the form of printed copy; and, if, in its judgment, such format would serve the public interest and convenience—
(1)
in the form of microfilm images; or
(2)
in the form of electronic media.
(b) Content
Publications under subsection (a), in whatever format, shall contain—
(1)
the then current Harmonized Tariff Schedule;
(2)
statistical annotations and related statistical information formulated under section 1484(f) of this title; and
(3)
such other matters as the Commission considers to be necessary or appropriate to carry out the purposes enumerated in the Preamble to the Convention.
