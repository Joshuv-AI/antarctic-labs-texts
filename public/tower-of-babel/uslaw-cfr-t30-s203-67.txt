30 C.F.R. § 203.67 — What economic criteria must I meet to get royalty relief on an authorized field or project?
Citation: us:cfr:30:203.67
Creator: Bureau Of Safety And Environmental Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-203.67
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 203 (Relief Or Reduction In Royalty Rates). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We will not approve applications if we determine that royalty relief cannot make the field, development project, or expansion project economically viable. Your field or project must be uneconomic while you are paying royalties and must become economic with royalty relief.
