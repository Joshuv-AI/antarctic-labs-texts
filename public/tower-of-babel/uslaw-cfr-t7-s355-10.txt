7 C.F.R. § 355.10 — Permission to engage in business concerning nonlisted terrestrial plants.
Citation: us:cfr:7:355.10
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-355.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 355 (Endangered Species Regulations Concerning Terrestrial Plants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary hereby grants permission for any person engaged in business as an importer, exporter, or reexporter of terrestrial plants, other than terrestrial plants listed in 50 CFR 17.12 or 23.23, to engage in such business without a protected plant permit issued under § 355.11.
