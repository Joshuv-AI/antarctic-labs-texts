2 U.S.C. § 1536 — Assistance to Congressional Budget Office
Citation: us:usc:2:1536
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section1536
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 25 (Unfunded Mandates Reform); Subchapter II (Regulatory Accountability And Reform). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director of the Office of Management and Budget shall—
(1)
collect from agencies the statements prepared under section 1532 of this title; and
(2)
periodically forward copies of such statements to the Director of the Congressional Budget Office on a reasonably timely basis after promulgation of the general notice of proposed rulemaking or of the final rule for which the statement was prepared.
