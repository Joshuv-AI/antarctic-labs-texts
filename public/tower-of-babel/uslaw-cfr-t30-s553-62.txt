30 C.F.R. § 553.62 — What are the designated applicant's notification obligations regarding a claim?
Citation: us:cfr:30:553.62
Creator: Bureau Of Ocean Energy Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-553.62
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 553 (Oil Spill Financial Responsibility For Offshore Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you are a designated applicant, and you receive a claim for removal costs and damages, then within 15 calendar days of receipt of a claim you must notify:
(a) Your guarantors; and
(b) The responsible parties for whom you are acting as the designated applicant.
