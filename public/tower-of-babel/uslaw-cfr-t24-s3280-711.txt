24 C.F.R. § 3280.711 — Instructions.
Citation: us:cfr:24:3280.711
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-3280.711
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 3280 (Manufactured Home Construction And Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Operating instructions for each appliance must be provided with the homeowner's manual. An additional copy of the operating instructions must be provided with each appliance unless the appliance is affixed with a permanent Quick Response (QR) Code.
