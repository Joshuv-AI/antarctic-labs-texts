12 C.F.R. § 703.15 — Prohibited investment activities.
Citation: us:cfr:12:703.15
Creator: National Credit Union Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-703.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 703 (Investment And Deposit Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Adjusted trading or short sales. A Federal credit union may not engage in adjusted trading or short sales.
