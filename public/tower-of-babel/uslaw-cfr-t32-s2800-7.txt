32 C.F.R. § 2800.7 — Designation of chairperson for Ad Hoc Committees.
Citation: us:cfr:32:2800.7
Creator: Office Of The Vice President Of The United States
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-2800.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 2800 (Security Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Counsel to the Vice President is designated as the responsible official to chair Ad Hoc Committees as necessary to act on all suggestions and complaints with respect to the administration of the information security program.
