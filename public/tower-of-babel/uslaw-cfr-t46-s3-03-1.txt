46 C.F.R. § 3.03-1 — -1 Vessels subject to the requirements of this part.
Citation: us:cfr:46:3.03-1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-3.03-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 3 (Designation Of Oceanographic Research Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part are applicable to U.S. flag vessels desiring designation as oceanographic research vessels in accordance with 46 U.S.C. 2101.
