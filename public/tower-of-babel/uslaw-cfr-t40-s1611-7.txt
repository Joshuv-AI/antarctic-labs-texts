40 C.F.R. § 1611.7 — Testimony by current CSB employees regarding prior activity.
Citation: us:cfr:40:1611.7
Creator: Chemical Safety And Hazard Investigation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1611.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1611 (Testimony By Employees In Legal Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any testimony regarding any incident within the CSB's jurisdiction, or any expert testimony arising from employment prior to CSB service is prohibited absent approval by the General Counsel. Approval shall only be given if testimony will not violate § 1611.1 and § 1611.3, and is subject to whatever conditions the General Counsel finds necessary to promote the purposes of this part as set forth in § 1611.1 and § 1611.3.
