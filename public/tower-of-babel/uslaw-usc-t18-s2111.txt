18 U.S.C. § 2111 — Special maritime and territorial jurisdiction
Citation: us:usc:18:2111
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section2111
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 103 (Robbery And Burglary). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever, within the special maritime and territorial jurisdiction of the United States, by force and violence, or by intimidation, takes or attempts to take from the person or presence of another anything of value, shall be imprisoned not more than fifteen years.
