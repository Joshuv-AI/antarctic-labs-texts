10 C.F.R. § 70.37 — Disclaimer of warranties.
Citation: us:cfr:10:70.37
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-70.37
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 70 (Domestic Licensing Of Special Nuclear Material). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Neither the Government nor the Commission makes any warranty or other representation that special nuclear material (a) will not result in injury or damage when used for purposes approved by the Commission, (b) will accomplish the results for which it is requested and approved by the Commission, or (c) is safe for any other use.
