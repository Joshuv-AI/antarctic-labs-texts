42 U.S.C. § 12891 — Program authority
Citation: us:usc:42:12891
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section12891
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 130 (National Affordable Housing); Subchapter IV (Hope For Homeownership Of Multifamily And Single Family Homes); Part B (Hope For Homeownership Of Single Family Homes). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized to make—
(1)
planning grants to help applicants develop homeownership programs in accordance with this part; and
(2)
implementation grants to enable applicants to carry out homeownership programs in accordance with this part.
