7 C.F.R. § 550.104 — Competition.
Citation: us:cfr:7:550.104
Creator: Agricultural Research Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-550.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 550 (General Administrative Policy For Non-Assistance Cooperative Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

REE Agencies may enter into non-assistance cooperative agreements, as authorized by this part, without regard to any requirements for competition specified in 2 CFR 200.202 and 200.206. (7 U.S.C. 3318(e)).
