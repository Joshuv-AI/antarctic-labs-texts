40 C.F.R. § 35.132 — Requirements summary.
Citation: us:cfr:40:35.132
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-35.132
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 35 (State And Local Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Applicants and recipients of Performance Partnership Grants must meet:
(a) The requirements in §§ 35.100 to 35.118, which apply to all environmental program grants, including Performance Partnership Grants; and
(b) The requirements in §§ 35.130 to 35.138, which apply only to Performance Partnership Grants.
