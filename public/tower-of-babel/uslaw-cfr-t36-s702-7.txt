36 C.F.R. § 702.7 — Weapons and explosives.
Citation: us:cfr:36:702.7
Creator: Library Of Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-702.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 702 (Conduct On Library Premises). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except where duly authorized by law, and in the performance of law enforcement functions, no person shall carry firearms, other dangerous or deadly weapons, or explosives, either openly or concealed, while on the premises.
