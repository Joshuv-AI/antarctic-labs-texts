14 C.F.R. § 93.130 — Suspension of allocations.
Citation: us:cfr:14:93.130
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-93.130
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 93 (Special Air Traffic Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator may suspend the effectiveness of any allocation prescribed in § 93.123 and the reservation requirements prescribed in § 93.125 if he finds such action to be consistent with the efficient use of the airspace. Such suspension may be terminated whenever the Administrator determines that such action is necessary for the efficient use of the airspace.
