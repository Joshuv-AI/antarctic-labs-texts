20 C.F.R. § 408.610 — When will we send your SVB payments to a representative payee?
Citation: us:cfr:20:408.610
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-408.610
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 408 (Special Benefits For Certain World War Ii Veterans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In determining when we will pay your benefits to a representative payee, we follow the rules in § 404.2010(a) of this chapter.
