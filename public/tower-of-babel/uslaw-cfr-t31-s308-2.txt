31 C.F.R. § 308.2 — Exchange for definitive securities.
Citation: us:cfr:31:308.2
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-308.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 308 (General Regulations Governing Full-Paid Interim Certificates). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon surrender of a full-paid interim certificate to a Federal Reserve Bank, or to the Treasury Department, Washington, DC 20226, the definitive securities described therein, when prepared, will be delivered. Exchanges shall be made on like par amount basis.
