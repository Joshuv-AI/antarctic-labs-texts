31 C.F.R. § 10.75 — Proposed findings and conclusions.
Citation: us:cfr:31:10.75
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-10.75
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 10 (Practice Before The Internal Revenue Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except in cases where the respondent has failed to answer the complaint or where a party has failed to appear at the hearing, the parties must be afforded a reasonable opportunity to submit proposed findings and conclusions and their supporting reasons to the Administrative Law Judge.
