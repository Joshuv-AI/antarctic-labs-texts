31 C.F.R. § 352.0 — Offering of bonds.
Citation: us:cfr:31:352.0
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-352.0
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 352 (Offering Of United States Savings Bonds, Series Hh). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary of the Treasury offered to the people of the United States, United States Savings Bonds of Series HH in exchange for eligible United States Savings Bonds of Series E and EE and United States Savings Notes (Freedom Shares). This offering is being withdrawn and will terminate at the close of business on August 31, 2004.
