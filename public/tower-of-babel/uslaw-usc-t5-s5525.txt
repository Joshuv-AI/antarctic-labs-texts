5 U.S.C. § 5525 — Allotment and assignment of pay
Citation: us:usc:5:5525
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section5525
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart D (Pay And Allowances); Chapter 55 (Pay Administration); Subchapter III (Advancement, Allotment, And Assignment Of Pay). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The head of each agency may establish procedures under which each employee of the agency is permitted to make allotments and assignments of amounts out of his pay for such purpose as the head of the agency considers appropriate. For purposes of this section, the term “agency” includes the Office of the Architect of the Capitol.
