18 U.S.C. § 342 — Operation of a common carrier under the influence of alcohol or drugs
Citation: us:usc:18:342
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section342
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 17A (Common Carrier Operation Under The Influence Of Alcohol Or Drugs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever operates or directs the operation of a common carrier while under the influence of alcohol or any controlled substance (as defined in section 102 of the Controlled Substances Act (21 U.S.C. 802)), shall be imprisoned not more than fifteen years or fined under this title, or both.
