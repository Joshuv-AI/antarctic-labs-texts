14 C.F.R. § 1240.113 — Financial accounting.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1240.113
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1240.113
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1240 (Inventions And Contributions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

NASA shall provide for appropriate database and accounting system(s) to ensure that award payments are recorded and disbursed in an orderly fashion and in the proper amounts to proper awardees.
