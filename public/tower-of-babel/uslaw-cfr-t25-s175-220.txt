25 C.F.R. § 175.220 — How long do rate and fee adjustments stay in effect?
Citation: us:cfr:25:175.220
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-175.220
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 175 (Electric Power Utilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

These adjustments remain in effect until we conduct a review and determine adjustments are necessary.
