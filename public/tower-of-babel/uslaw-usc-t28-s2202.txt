28 U.S.C. § 2202 — Further relief
Citation: us:usc:28:2202
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section2202
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part VI (Particular Proceedings); Chapter 151 (Declaratory Judgments). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Further necessary or proper relief based on a declaratory judgment or decree may be granted, after reasonable notice and hearing, against any adverse party whose rights have been determined by such judgment.
