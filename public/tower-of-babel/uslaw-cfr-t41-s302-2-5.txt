41 C.F.R. § 302-2.5 — -2.5 Requirement to provide agency with actual place of residence.
Citation: us:cfr:41:302-2.5
Creator: Chapter 302—Relocation Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-302-2.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 302-2 (2—Employee Eligibility Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For a transfer/appointment to an OCONUS location, the employee must immediately provide their agency with the information needed to determine their actual place of residence and to document it into their service agreement.
