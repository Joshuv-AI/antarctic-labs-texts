36 C.F.R. § 703.22 — Where to serve demands.
Citation: us:cfr:36:703.22
Creator: Library Of Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-703.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 703 (Disclosure Or Production Of Records Or Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Requesting parties must serve subpoenas:
(a) For Congressional Research Service matters: Director, Congressional Research Service, LM 203, Library of Congress, Washington, DC 20540.
(b) For Law Library matters: Law Librarian, LM 240, Library of Congress, Washington, DC 20540.
(c) For all other matters: General Counsel, LM 601, Library of Congress, Washington, DC 20540.
