54 U.S.C. § 300319 — Tribal land
Citation: us:usc:54:300319
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section300319
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 3003 (Definitions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this division, the term “tribal land” means—
(1)
all land within the exterior boundaries of any Indian reservation; and
(2)
all dependent Indian communities.
