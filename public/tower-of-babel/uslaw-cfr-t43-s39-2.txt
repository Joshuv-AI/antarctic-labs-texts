43 C.F.R. § 39.2 — Requests for Hearings.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 39 (Collection Of Debts By Administrative Wage Garnishment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any request for a hearing under 31 CFR 285.11 must be filed with the Director, Office of Hearings and Appeals, U.S. Department of the Interior, 801 N. Quincy Street, Suite 300, Arlington, Virginia 22203.
