10 C.F.R. § 470.16 — Cost sharing and funds from other sources.
Citation: us:cfr:10:470.16
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-470.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 470 (Appropriate Technology Small Grants Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Proposers are encouraged to offer to share in the costs of their proposed projects or to arrange that other entities provide cost sharing on their behalf. Regional Program Managers, with the consent of the proposer, may work with States, local governments or other entities to obtain supplemental funding.
