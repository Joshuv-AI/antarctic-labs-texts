An internal description of constructible objects in an ∞ \infty -topos
Li He

Abstract
. We give an internal description of constructible objects in an ∞ \infty -topos. More precisely, P P -consctructible objects are locally constant objects internal to Fun ⁡ ( P , An ) \mathrm{Fun}(P,\mathrm{An}) , for any noetherian poset P P .



1.
Introduction

The study of constructible sheaves has a very long history.
By definition, constructible sheaves are built from
locally constant sheaves. In this paper, we will provide
a new point of view that constructible sheaves coincide with locally constant sheaves in the internal world.

The
\infty
-category of constructible sheaves can help us to understand the exodromy phenomenon.
In
[
Lur17a
, Appendix A]
,
Lurie studied constructible sheaves and exit paths
under the conical assumption.
Later, in
[
CJ23
, Section 3]
, Clausen-Jansen
used the atomic generation assumption
on the
\infty
-category of constructible sheaves to study the exodromy,
making the conical assumption go away.
And Haine-Porta-Teyssier further studied
the exodromy beyond conicality in more general setup
in
[
HPT24
]
.
Now, in this paper,
we will provide an internal description of the
\infty
-category
of constructible sheaves,
which should be helpful to understand the exodromy phenomenon.

We let
\mathscr{X}
be an
\infty
-topos.
Recall from
[
Lur17a
, Appendix A.1]
that
an object
X\in\mathscr{X}
is
constant
, if

X
lies in the essential image of the constant functor

\pi^{*}:\mathrm{An}\to\mathscr{X}
.
An object
X\in\mathscr{X}
is
locally constant
,
if there exists an effective epimorphism

\sqcup_{i}U_{i}\twoheadrightarrow 1_{\mathscr{X}}
,
such that

(X\times U_{i}\to U_{i})\in\mathscr{X}_{/U_{i}}
is constant, for each

.
Let
\mathrm{LC}(\mathscr{X})\subset\mathscr{X}
be the full
subcategory of
\mathscr{X}
spanned by the locally constant objects
in
\mathscr{X}
.

We let
\mathscr{X}
be an
\infty
-topos and

Haine-Porta-Teyssier defines the concept of

For each

of

via the cartesian square

{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\mathscr{X}}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\mathrm{An}\simeq\mathrm{Fun}(\{p\},\mathrm{An})}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\mathrm{Fun}(P,\mathrm{An})}
\scriptstyle{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces i_{p*}}
\scriptstyle{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces i_{p*}}

in

In this paper, we define the concept of local constancy

\infty
-topos

Definition 1.1
.

We fix a geometric morphism
\pi^{*}:\mathscr{B}\to\mathscr{X}
of
\infty
-topoi. An object
X\in\mathscr{X}
is
\mathscr{B}

{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\mathrm{LC}_{\mathscr{B}}(\mathscr{X})}

{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\mathscr{X}}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\prod_{p\in P}\mathscr{X}_{p}}

Our main result says that if

Theorem 1.2
.

Suppose we are given a
P
-stratification

If

is a noetherian poset,
then there is a canonical equivalence

\mathrm{LC}_{\mathrm{Fun}(P,\mathrm{An})}(\mathscr{X})\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\sim}}{{\to}}\mathrm{Cons}_{P}(\mathscr{X}).

From this theorem, we know that the
\infty
-category

\mathrm{Cons}_{P}(\mathscr{X})
of
constructible sheaves are essentially the
\infty
-category

\mathrm{LC}_{\mathrm{Fun}(P,\mathrm{An})}(\mathscr{X})
of locally constant sheaves internal to

\mathrm{Fun}(P,\mathrm{An})
.

We start from an useful observation.

Lemma 3.1

In other words, the canonical map

By the direct computation.

Corollary 3.8
.

If we are given a geometric morphism

\mathscr{B}\simeq\mathrm{Fun}(P_{\geq p},\mathrm{An})\to\mathscr{X}
,
where
P
is a noetherian poset and
p\in P
,
then the induced square
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\mathrm{LC}_{\mathscr{B}}(\mathscr{X})}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\prod_{q\geq p}\mathrm{LC}(\mathscr{X}_{q})}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\mathscr{X}}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\prod_{q\geq p}\mathscr{X}_{q}}

is cartesian in
\mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}
.

Proof.

We prove it by noetherian induction on
p\in P
.
Suppose it is true for

\mathrm{Fun}(P_{\geq q},\mathrm{An})
, where
q>p
.
We need to show that it is also true for

\mathrm{Fun}(P_{\geq p},\mathrm{An})
.
Note that we have

P_{\geq p}\setminus\{p\}=\bigcup_{q>p}P_{\geq q},

where
\{p\}\subset P_{\geq p}
is closed and

\bigcup_{q>p}P_{\geq q}\subset P_{\geq p}

is open.
Thus by Lemma
2.6
,
we have the cartesian square
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\mathrm{LC}_{\mathscr{B}}(\mathscr{X})}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\mathrm{LC}(\mathscr{X}_{p})\times\mathrm{LC}_{\mathscr{U}}(\mathscr{X}_{\mathscr{U}})}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\mathscr{X}}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\mathscr{X}_{p}\times\mathscr{X}_{\mathscr{U}},}

where
\mathscr{B}\simeq\mathrm{Fun}(P_{\geq p},\mathrm{An})

and
\mathscr{U}\simeq\mathrm{Fun}(\bigcup_{q>p}P_{\geq q},\mathrm{An})\simeq\lim_{q>p}\mathrm{Fun}(P_{\geq q},\mathrm{An})\simeq\lim_{q>p}\mathscr{B}_{q}.

By Proposition
3.7
,
the inclusion

\mathrm{LC}_{\mathscr{U}}(\mathscr{X}_{\mathscr{U}})\hookrightarrow\mathscr{X}_{\mathscr{U}}

is exactly the inclusion

\lim_{q>p}\mathrm{LC}_{\mathscr{B}_{q}}(\mathscr{X}_{\mathscr{B}_{q}})\hookrightarrow\lim_{q>p}\mathscr{X}_{\mathscr{B}_{q}}.

By the induction assumption, for each

, we have the cartesian square
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\mathrm{LC}_{\mathscr{B}_{q}}(\mathscr{X}_{\mathscr{B}_{q}})}

{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\mathscr{X}_{\mathscr{B}_{q}}}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\prod_{r\geq q}\mathscr{X}_{r}.}

, we get the cartesian square
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\mathrm{LC}_{\mathscr{U}}(\mathscr{X}_{\mathscr{U}})\simeq\lim_{q>p}\mathrm{LC}_{\mathscr{B}_{q}}(\mathscr{X}_{\mathscr{B}_{q}})}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\lim_{q>p}\prod_{r\geq q}\mathrm{LC}(\mathscr{X}_{r})\simeq\prod_{r>p}\mathrm{LC}(\mathscr{X}_{r})}

{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\mathscr{X}}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\mathscr{X}_{p}\times\mathscr{X}_{\mathscr{U}}}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\prod_{r\geq p}\mathscr{X}_{r}.}
