22 U.S.C. § 10809 — Prohibition on purchase of sovereign debt of the Russian Federation by United States persons
Citation: us:usc:22:10809
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section10809
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 115 (Sanctions With Respect To The Russian Federation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Upon the enactment of this Act, the purchase of sovereign debt of the Government of the Russian Federation by any United States person (including a United States financial institution) is prohibited.
