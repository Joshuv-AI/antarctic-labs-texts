15 U.S.C. § 4605 — Export of semiconductor manufacturing
Citation: us:usc:15:4605
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section4605
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 72 (Semiconductor Research); Subchapter I (Cooperative Research Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any export of materials, equipment, and technology developed by Sematech in whole or in part with financial assistance provided under section 4602(a) of this title shall be subject to the Export Administration Act of 1979 (50 U.S.C. App. 2401 et seq.) 1 and shall not be subject to the Arms Export Control Act [22 U.S.C. 2751 et seq.].
