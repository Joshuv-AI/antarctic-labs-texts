31 C.F.R. § 215.6 — Parties.
Citation: us:cfr:31:215.6
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-215.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 215 (Withholding Of District Of Columbia, State, City And County Income Or Employment Taxes By Federal Agencies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The parties to this agreement are the Secretary and the State, city or county which has entered into this agreement pursuant to 5 U.S.C. 5516, 5517, or 5520 and Executive Order 11997 (June 22, 1977).
