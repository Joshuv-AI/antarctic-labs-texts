40 C.F.R. § 166.22 — Consultation with the Secretary of Agriculture and Governors of the States.
Citation: us:cfr:40:166.22
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-166.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 166 (Exemption Of Federal And State Agencies For Use Of Pesticides Under Emergency Conditions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Agency, in determining whether or not such emergency conditions exist, shall consult with the Secretary of Agriculture and the Governor of any State concerned if they request such determination.
