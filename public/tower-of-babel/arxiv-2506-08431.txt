Conclusion

In this paper, we introduce and study a new weak class of spaces, namely
\pi g\widehat{D}
-normal spaces by using
\pi g\widehat{D}
-open sets. The relationships among normal, strongly quasi-normal, almost softly
\pi g\widehat{D}

\pi g\widehat{D}

\pi g\widehat{D}
- normal, quasi

- normal, mildly
\pi g\widehat{D}
- normal. The main result of this paper is that softly
\pi g\widehat{D}

\pi g\widehat{D}
- normal spaces. Of course, the entire content will be a successful tool for the researchers for finding the way to obtain the results in the context of such types of normal spaces have many possibilities of applications in digital topology and computer graphics.
