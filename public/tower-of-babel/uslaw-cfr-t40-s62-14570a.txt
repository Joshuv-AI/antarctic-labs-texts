40 C.F.R. § 62.14570a — a When must the operator training course be completed?
Citation: us:cfr:40:62.14570a
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-62.14570a
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 62 (Approval And Promulgation Of State Plans For Designated Facilities And Pollutants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The operator training course must be completed by the later of the three dates specified in paragraphs (a) through (c) of this section.
(a) January 10, 2025.
(b) Six months after CISWI startup; or
(c) Six months after an employee assumes responsibility for operating the CISWI or assumes responsibility for supervising the operation of the CISWI.
