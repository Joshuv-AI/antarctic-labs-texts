15 C.F.R. § 700.90 — Protection against claims.
Citation: us:cfr:15:700.90
Creator: Bureau Of Industry And Security, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-700.90
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 700 (Defense Priorities And Allocations System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A person shall not be held liable for damages or penalties for any act or failure to act resulting directly or indirectly from compliance with any provision of this part, or an official action, notwithstanding that such provision or action shall subsequently be declared invalid by judicial or other competent authority.
