48 C.F.R. § 2432.906 — 2432.906 Making payments.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2432 (Contract Financing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) General. The authority to make the determination prescribed in FAR 32.906(a) is delegated to the HCA. Before making this determination, the HCA shall consult with the appropriate payment office to ensure that procedures are in place to permit timely payment.
