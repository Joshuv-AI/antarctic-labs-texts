19 C.F.R. § 112.25 — Bonded carriers.
Citation: us:cfr:19:112.25
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-112.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 112 (Carriers, Cartmen, And Lightermen). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A carrier or freight forwarder who has filed a bond on Customs Form 301 containing the bond conditions set forth in § 113.63 of this chapter may transport merchandise within a port for which the bond provides coverage.
