19 U.S.C. § 4065 — Compensation authority
Citation: us:usc:19:4065
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section4065
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 26 (Dominican Republic-Central America Free Trade); Subchapter III (Relief From Imports); Part A (Relief From Imports Benefiting From The Agreement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For purposes of section 123 of the Trade Act of 1974 (19 U.S.C. 2133), any import relief provided by the President under section 4063 of this title shall be treated as action taken under chapter 1 of title II of such Act [19 U.S.C. 2251 et seq.].
