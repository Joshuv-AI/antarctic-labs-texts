24 C.F.R. § 266.130 — Reinsurance.
Citation: us:cfr:24:266.130
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-266.130
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 266 (Housing Finance Agency Risk-Sharing Program For Insured Affordable Multifamily Project Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Reinsurance will be permitted for the portion of the HFA risk, subject to the following requirements:
(a) Neither HUD's nor the HFA's position shall be subordinated;
(b) The reinsurance may not be used to reduce any reserve or fund balance requirements; and
(c) Such reinsurance does not incur an obligation to the Federal Government.
