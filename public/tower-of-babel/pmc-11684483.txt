Reviewers
Published: 2024

On behalf of all authors and editors, we thank the following reviewers for their generous assistance in evaluating manuscripts submitted to Investigative Ophthalmology & Visual Science during the past year. The list comprises all those, including editors, who completed one or more reviews between January 1, 2023, and December 31, 2023.

Thoughtful and unbiased reviews are essential to maintaining the high quality of our journal – as such, we appreciate the significant effort required to read, analyze, and evaluate a scientific manuscript.

The reviewers below conducted 2800 reviews between January 1, 2023, and December 31, 2023. As a testament to the quality of our reviewers, over 70% were rated “good” or “exceptional,” and the average time to return a review was 13.9 days. Investigative Ophthalmology & Visual Science would not be the outstanding journal that it is without the dedicated service of these individuals. If you have questions about the peer review process at Investigative Ophthalmology & Visual Science or are interested in serving as a reviewer, please contact the Editor-in-Chief, Joseph Carroll, at iovs.eic@arvo.org.
