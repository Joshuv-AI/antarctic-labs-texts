Empowering Tri‐Functional Palladium's Catalytic Activity and Durability in Electrocatalytic Formic Acid Oxidation Reaction via Innovative Self‐Caging and Alloying Strategies (Adv. Sci. 46/2024)
Authors: Chan‐Woo Lee, Sun Young Jung, Jeong Ho Ryu, Gyeom Seong Jeon, Ashish Gaur, Min Su Cho, Ghulam Ali, Mingony Kim, Kyung Yoon Chung, Arpan Kumar Nayak, Seoyoon Shin, Jiseok Kwon, Taeseup Song, Tae Ho Shin, HyukSu Han
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
Abstract Formic Acidic Oxidation Reaction In article number 2405725, Taeseup Song, Tae Ho Shin, HyukSu Han, and co‐workers develop a novel nanomaterial engineering approach to design a self‐caged and alloyed Pd‐based electrocatalyst that demonstrates exceptional durability and activity for formic acid oxidation reaction. A conductive carbon frame embedded with Pd‐Zr alloyed nanoparticles can be formed by utilizing a metal‐organic framework (MOF) as a precursor. The self‐caging and alloying process occurs in situ during electrochemical reactions. This enables the encapsulation of Pd‐Zr particles within neighboring frames effectively mitigating particle aggregation and consequential enhancing catalytic performance.
