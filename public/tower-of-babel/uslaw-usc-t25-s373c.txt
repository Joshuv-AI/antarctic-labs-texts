25 U.S.C. § 373c — Sections 373a and 373b as inapplicable to certain Indians
Citation: us:usc:25:373c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section373c
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 10 (Descent And Distribution; Heirs Of Allottee). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of sections 373a and 373b of this title shall not apply to the Indians of the Five Civilized Tribes or the Osage Reservation, in Oklahoma.
