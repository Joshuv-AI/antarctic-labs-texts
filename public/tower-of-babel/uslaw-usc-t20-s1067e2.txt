20 U.S.C. § 1067e–2 — Evaluation and accountability plan
Citation: us:usc:20:1067e–2
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1067e–2
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 28 (Higher Education Resources And Student Assistance); Subchapter III (Institutional Aid); Part E (Minority Science And Engineering Improvement Program); Subpart 2 (Programs In Stem Fields). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall develop an evaluation and accountability plan for projects funded under this subpart. Such plan shall include, if the Secretary determines that it is practical, an objective measure of the impact of such projects, such as a measure of whether underrepresented minority student enrollment in courses related to science, technology, engineering, and mathematics increases at the secondary and postsecondary levels.
