25 C.F.R. § 115.815 — How does a tribe request trust funds from a tribal trust account?
Citation: us:cfr:25:115.815
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-115.815
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 115 (Trust Funds For Tribes And Individual Indians). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To request trust funds from a tribal trust account, a tribe may:
(a) Make a written request to the BIA or the OTFM that is signed by the proper authorizing official(s), list the amount of trust funds to be withdrawn, provide any additional documentation or information required by law to withdraw certain trust funds, and must include a tribal resolution approving the withdrawal of the specified amount of trust funds; or
(b) Contact the OTFM to withdraw funds in accordance with the Trust Reform Act and 25 CFR part 1200.
