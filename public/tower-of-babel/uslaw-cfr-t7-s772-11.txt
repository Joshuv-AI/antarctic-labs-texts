7 C.F.R. § 772.11 — Transfer and assumption—IMP loans.
Citation: us:cfr:7:772.11
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-772.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 772 (Servicing Minor Program Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Transfers and assumptions for IMP loans are processed in accordance with 7 CFR part 765. Any remaining transferor liability will be serviced in accordance with § 772.9(c) of this subpart.
