32 C.F.R. § 1651.2 — Time within which registrants may appeal.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1651.2
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1651.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1651 (Classification By District Appeal Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The registrant who wishes to appeal must file the appeal with his local board within 15 days after the date he is mailed a notice of classification action. The registrant who wishes a personal appearance before the district appeal board must file the request at the same time he files the appeal.
