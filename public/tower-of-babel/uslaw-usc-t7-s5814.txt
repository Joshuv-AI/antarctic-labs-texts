7 U.S.C. § 5814 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:7:5814
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section5814
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 88 (Research); Subchapter I (Sustainable Agriculture Research And Education); Part A (Best Utilization Of Biological Applications). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated to carry out this part $40,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2013 through 2023. Of amounts appropriated to carry out this part for a fiscal year, not less than $15,000,000, or not less than two thirds of any such appropriation, whichever is greater, shall be used to carry out sections 5811 and 5812 of this title.
