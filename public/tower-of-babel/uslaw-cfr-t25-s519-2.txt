25 C.F.R. § 519.2 — Designation of an agent by a management contractor or a tribal operator.
Citation: us:cfr:25:519.2
Creator: National Indian Gaming Commission, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-519.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 519 (Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

By written notification to the Commission, a management contractor or a tribal operator shall designate an agent for service of any official determination, order, or notice of violation.
