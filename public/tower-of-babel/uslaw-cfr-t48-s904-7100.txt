48 C.F.R. § 904.7100 — 904.7100 Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:48:904.7100
Creator: Chapter 9—Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-904.7100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 904 (Administrative Matters). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart implements section 836 of the Fiscal Year 1993 Defense Authorization Act (Pub. L. 102-484) which prohibits the award of a Department of Energy contract under the national security program to an entity controlled by a foreign government if it is necessary for that entity to be given access to information in a proscribed category of information in order to perform the contract.
