21 C.F.R. § 211.134 — Drug product inspection.
Citation: us:cfr:21:211.134
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-211.134
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 211 (Current Good Manufacturing Practice For Finished Pharmaceuticals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Packaged and labeled products shall be examined during finishing operations to provide assurance that containers and packages in the lot have the correct label.
(b) A representative sample of units shall be collected at the completion of finishing operations and shall be visually examined for correct labeling.
(c) Results of these examinations shall be recorded in the batch production or control records.
