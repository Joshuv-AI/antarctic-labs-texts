Art management platform based on virtual scenes
Authors: Jialiang He, Xin Jiang, Hong Tao, Yu Zhang, Shuhuan Lin
Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
AbstractThis article is based on the form of “VR + Art Platform” and designs an art management platform that integrates display, trading, and socializing. The development of front-end work is carried out using Unity 3D engine, and modeling software such as 3ds Max is used to model the models used in the system. The backend is developed using IntelliJ IDEA platform, and the SSM framework is built and combined with MySQL database to implement the functions of each module in detail. The system also realizes the interactive connection between front-end scenes and back-end management, and achieves data transmission. When developing for users, platforms need to provide complex recommendation functions. Given that existing recommendation algorithms often perform inaccurately and efficiently when dealing with changes in user preferences and diverse interaction needs on art platforms. This article proposes a research on art management platform recommendation algorithm by combining graph neural network and exhibition design rules. The introduction of attention mechanism enables the graph neural network model to more accurately aggregate important neighbor node information, thereby improving the accuracy of node representation and personalized recommendation effect, and compensating for the shortcomings of traditional recommendation systems in aesthetics and display optimization, making the recommendation results more practical. Compared to existing virtual platforms, the proposed method explicitly highlights its advantages by significantly improving recommendation accuracy through graph neural networks, while simultaneously enhancing display aesthetics and user engagement via integrated exhibition design rules.Keywords: Virtual scene, Graph neural network, Exhibition design, Art management platformSubject terms: Cultural and media studies, Cultural and media studies, Mathematics and computing

Introduction

With the rapid development of the Internet, it has a very positive significance in promoting the art platform. The Internet serves the art platform, art creators, and art lovers1. The new media art platform is an important way to spread art culture2. It uses a variety of emerging technologies to empower art3. But when we see the positive side, we also need to see the negative side. Firstly, there are limitations in the platform management personnel, with varying levels of quality and lack of professionalism, which affects the dissemination of cultural information; Secondly, the existing platforms suffer from poor management and lack complete construction standards; Finally, some platforms still remain in a flat space for display, unable to provide people with a good viewing and experience. Although some platforms have developed three-dimensional effects, they only provide display functions, and there are still deficiencies in other functions.

With the continuous progress of the times, a good inspiration has been given to the public, which is to adopt the form of "VR + art platform" and use emerging scientific and technological means to make art exhibitions more immersive and diverse4. This project aims to create a new platform that encompasses display, trading, and socializing, changing the traditional mode of art display and circulation on trading websites, and integrating and sharing art information in a virtual environment for timely interaction. Firstly, VR technology is used to provide an immersive virtual exhibition environment for this platform. In terms of platform design, modeling software such as 3ds Max is used to construct and optimize the models. In the platform, users can interact with their favorite artworks and express their level of appreciation through comments and ratings; Secondly, users can complete art transactions in virtual scenes while appreciating artworks, without having to detach from the art display, enhancing the user’s integrated experience. The advantages of low operating costs and low time costs achieved by this experience lower the threshold for art transactions; Again, by combining personalized recommendations with exhibition design, analyzing users’ historical behavior, actively exploring their interests and preferences, recommending valuable information to users, and applying exhibition design aesthetics in the art platform, enhancing users’ dependence on the platform, and establishing a long-term stable relationship between the system and users; Finally, in order to enable users to exchange art, learn from each other’s strengths, and make progress together on the platform, an interactive module has been added. After adding like-minded friends, both parties can achieve online communication, which effectively solves the problem of user communication and increases user stickiness to a certain extent.

Despite the adoption of 3D visualization in current virtual art platforms, most systems merely simulate physical walls without considering professional curatorial logic, spatial aesthetics, and intelligent organization. While lightweight convolutional neural networks (CNNs) have shown great success in efficient visual feature extraction and object detection in various domains5–8, relying solely on visual features is insufficient for art curation. There remains a critical need to integrate spatial exhibition design rules with intelligent representation learning to bridge the gap between algorithmic accuracy and aesthetic presentation.

In summary, a virtual scene based art management platform can improve the experience of consumers and artists to a certain extent, ensure smooth sales channels for art, promote the healthy development of the art market, help enhance the overall artistic taste of society, strengthen the construction of social spiritual civilization, accelerate the speed of information flow and cultural exchange, break the boundaries between art creators and ordinary art enthusiasts, shorten the distance between art and the public, and improve the perceived value and transfer value of art consumers.

Related work

With the rapid development of virtual reality technology, its application in the field of art is becoming increasingly widespread. VR + Art Platform "refers to a digital art display, trading, and experience platform built using virtual reality technology9. This type of platform digitizes and presents artworks in a virtual environment through technologies such as 3D modeling and panoramic shooting10. Users can obtain immersive art appreciation experiences through VR devices11. As an emerging art display and trading model, it is changing the pattern of the traditional art industry12.

Foreign research mainly focuses on immersive experiences and virtual exhibitions. Its research in the field of "VR + art platforms" started earlier and has achieved rich results. In terms of immersive experience, researchers have explored how to enhance the emotional resonance and cognitive depth of art viewing through VR technology. For example, the VR art project developed by Tate Modern in the UK allows viewers to “step into” the interior of famous paintings and gain a new perspective on artistic interpretation; Internationally renowned art institutions such as the Louvre and the Metropolitan Museum of Art have launched VR art experience projects; Technology companies such as HTC Vive have also collaborated with art institutions to develop specialized VR art applications. In terms of virtual exhibitions, the American Museum Alliance has promoted a series of VR exhibition standardization research, developed technical specifications and evaluation systems for virtual exhibitions; The European Digital Cultural Heritage Project is committed to developing high fidelity digital solutions for artworks, ensuring that virtual displays of artworks can accurately reproduce the details of the original work; In addition, foreign scholars are also concerned about the application of VR technology in the protection and restoration of artworks, establishing virtual backups of artworks through digital twin technology.

Domestic research focuses more on technological applications and business model innovation. Although the research on "VR + art platforms" in China started relatively late, it has developed rapidly and presented distinct application-oriented characteristics. The research focuses mainly on three aspects: technological implementation paths, business model innovation, and user experience optimization. At the technical implementation level, domestic scholars are concerned about how to lower the technical threshold of VR art platforms and improve content production efficiency. For example, the automated 3D modeling system for artworks developed by the Zhejiang University team has greatly improved digital efficiency. In terms of business models, domestic research has explored the integration model of VR art platforms with e-commerce and social media. Alibaba’s "VR Art Museum" project attempts to combine art VR display with online transactions, while Tencent integrates VR art content into social platforms to enhance user interaction experience. These practices provide useful references for the commercial operation of VR art platforms. In terms of user experience, major museums in China have launched roaming systems in conjunction with virtual reality, such as the Natural History Museum in Zhejiang, which has brought revolutionary changes to art display, trading, and experience through VR technology. Technology companies such as Baidu and Huawei have also laid out their own unique solutions in the VR art field. It is worth noting that domestic VR art platforms place more emphasis on mobile applications, which is in sharp contrast to the model dominated by high-end VR devices in foreign countries.

As an emerging field that integrates art and technology, the "VR + Art Platform" is reshaping various aspects of the art ecosystem. Despite facing challenges, with the advancement of science and technology and the expansion of application scenarios, it is expected to become an important driving force for the digital transformation of the art industry.

In addition to virtual environment construction, recommendation algorithms play a crucial role in personalized user experiences. Recent advancements in recommendation systems have explored diverse architectures to capture complex user interests. For instance, Hmamba leverages hyperbolic spaces for sequential recommendation13, and large language models (LLMs) have been integrated into bi-level multi-interest learning frameworks14.

In summary, an overall evaluation reveals a significant gap in current research: existing VR-based art platforms lack intelligent curation, while these advanced recommendation algorithms primarily focus on user-item interaction accuracy, ignoring the spatial aesthetics required for 3D art exhibitions. Our proposed approach bridges this gap by jointly optimizing user preferences via GNNs and display aesthetics via exhibition design rules.

System implementation

Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) can model high-order dependencies between nodes in graph structures, making them suitable for tasks such as social relationship modeling, content recommendation, and interest propagation15. In the art recommendation system, the association between users and artworks not only includes basic behaviors such as rating or browsing, but also implies multi-level artistic styles, cultural preferences, and display scene requirements, which enables GNN to achieve better performance in such tasks. Therefore, this study combines the modeling capability of GNN, introduces a multidimensional feature fusion strategy, embeds exhibition design rules in the algorithm structure, and strives to achieve a balanced optimization of recommendation results between interest relevance and display aesthetics.

Art exhibition design

Exhibition design and rule construction

The core of exhibition design is to optimize the display of artworks, so that users can feel the harmonious beauty and cultural value of art when viewing them. This article introduces exhibition design rules as an innovative point into recommendation systems to compensate for the shortcomings of traditional recommendation systems in aesthetics and display optimization, thereby making the recommendation results more practical.

System evaluation

Experimental data

This article selected the WikiArt dataset from the WikiArt website for art recommendation research. The dataset contains 81,444 art works, and each image is accompanied by a corresponding class label. The partial images of the dataset are shown in Fig. 3.

Partial images of art dataset.

During data preprocessing, all artwork images were uniformly resized to 224× 224 pixels, and entries with missing metadata were removed to ensure data quality. For model training and evaluation, the dataset was randomly split into training, validation, and testing sets with a ratio of 8:1:1. To prevent data leakage, user interaction sequences were strictly partitioned chronologically.

Conclusion

This article discusses the virtual art recommendation algorithm that integrates exhibition design rules and graph neural networks. The system constructs a user art bipartite graph and combines multidimensional features and time decay mechanisms to accurately depict changes in user interests. Adopting a multi-layer GCN structure to achieve high-order relationship modeling, and improving the stability of the model through regularization and Dropout. After introducing the self attention mechanism, the model can dynamically adjust the weight of information aggregation, enhancing its ability to identify key nodes. The joint training framework integrates recommendation accuracy with presentation aesthetic objectives, optimizing overall performance with a multi task loss function. The experimental results show that the model has good performance in personalized recommendation, diversity, and exhibition matching, which promotes the application value of virtual art platforms in both immersive experience and cultural communication.
