34 C.F.R. § 299.18 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:34:299.18
Creator: Office Of Elementary And Secondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-299.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 299 (General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this subpart apply to part A of Title I and applicable programs under section 8501(b)(1) of the ESEA under which the Secretary is authorized to waive the requirements for providing services to private school children, teachers or other educational personnel, and families, as applicable, and to implement a bypass.
