7 U.S.C. § 2424 — Death or incapacity of breeder
Citation: us:usc:7:2424
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2424
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 57 (Plant Variety Protection); Subchapter II (Protectability Of Plant Varieties And Certificates Of Protection); Part E (Applications; Form; Who May File; Relating Back; Confidentiality). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Legal representatives of deceased breeders and of those under legal incapacity may make application for plant variety protection upon compliance with the requirements and on the same terms and conditions applicable to the breeder or the successor in interest of the breeder.
