16 U.S.C. § 3608 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:16:3608
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section3608
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 56 (North Atlantic Salmon Fishing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated from time to time such sums as may be necessary for carrying out the purposes and provisions of the Convention and this chapter including—
(1)
necessary travel expenses of the Commissioners and alternate Commissioners in accordance with the Federal Travel Regulation and sections 5701, 5702, 5704 through 5708, and 5731 of title 5; and
(2)
the United States contribution to the Organization as provided in Article 16 of the Convention, not to exceed $50,000 for fiscal year 1983, and not to exceed, for each succeeding fiscal year, the amount assessed by the Organization for the United States for such year.
