2 U.S.C. § 276a — Expenditures
Citation: us:usc:2:276a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section276a
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 9 (Office Of Legislative Counsel); Subchapter I (Senate). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

With the approval of the President Pro Tempore of the Senate, the Legislative Counsel of the Senate may make such expenditures as may be necessary or appropriate for the functioning of the Office of the Legislative Counsel of the Senate.
