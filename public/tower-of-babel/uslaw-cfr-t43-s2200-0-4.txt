43 C.F.R. § 2200.0-4 — -4 Responsibilities.

Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2200.0-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2200 (Exchanges: General Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director of the Bureau of Land Management has the responsibility of carrying out the functions of the Secretary of the Interior under these regulations.
