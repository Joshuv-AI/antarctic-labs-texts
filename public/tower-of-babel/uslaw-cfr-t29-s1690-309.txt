29 C.F.R. § 1690.309 — Interpretation of the Order.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1690.309
Creator: Equal Employment Opportunity Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1690.309
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1690 (Procedures On Interagency Coordination Of Equal Employment Opportunity Issuances). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Subject to the dispute resolution procedures set forth above and in accordance with the objectives set forth in 1-201 and the procedures in 1-303 of the Order, the EEOC shall interpret the meaning and intent of the Order. EEOC also will issue procedural changes under the Order, as appropriate, after advice and consultation with affected agencies as provided for in these procedures.
