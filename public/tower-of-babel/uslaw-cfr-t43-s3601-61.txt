43 C.F.R. § 3601.61 — When may BLM cancel my contract or permit?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3601.61
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3601.61
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3600 (Mineral Materials Disposal). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

BLM may cancel your contract or free use permit if you:
(a) Fail to comply with the provisions of the Materials Act of 1947, as amended (30 U.S.C. 601 et seq.);
(b) Fail to comply with any applicable regulations; or
(c) Default in the performance of any material term, covenant, or stipulation in the contract.
