29 C.F.R. § 1905.51 — Finality for purposes of judicial review.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1905.51
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1905.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1905 (Rules Of Practice For Variances, Limitations, Variations, Tolerances, And Exemptions Under The Williams-Steiger Occupational Safety And Health Act Of 1970). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Only a decision by the Assistant Secretary shall be deemed final agency action for purposes of judicial review. A decision by a hearing examiner which becomes final for lack of appeal is not deemed final agency action for purposes of 5 U.S.C. 704.
