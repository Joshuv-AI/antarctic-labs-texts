36 C.F.R. § 1012.7 — Can I get an authenticated copy of a Presidio Trust record?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1012.7
Creator: Presidio Trust
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1012.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1012 (Legal Process: Testimony By Employees And Production Of Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. We may provide an authenticated copy of a Presidio Trust record, for purposes of admissibility under Federal, State or Tribal law. We will do this only if the record has been officially released or would otherwise be released under parts 1007 or 1008 of this chapter, or this part.
