29 C.F.R. § 783.17 — “American vessel”.
Citation: us:cfr:29:783.17
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-783.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 783 (Application Of The Fair Labor Standards Act To Employees Employed As Seamen). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Section 3(p) of the Act, added by the 1961 Amendments, defines “American vessel” to include “any vessel which is documented or numbered under the laws of the United States.” This definition and its effect with respect to the application of the Act to employment of individuals as seamen are discussed in subsequent sections of this part.
