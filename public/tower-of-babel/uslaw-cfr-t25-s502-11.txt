25 C.F.R. § 502.11 — House banking game.
Citation: us:cfr:25:502.11
Creator: National Indian Gaming Commission, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-502.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 502 (Definitions Of This Chapter). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

House banking game means any game of chance that is played with the house as a participant in the game, where the house takes on all players, collects from all losers, and pays all winners, and the house can win.
