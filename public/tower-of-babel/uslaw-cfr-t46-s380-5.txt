46 C.F.R. § 380.5 — Exception to procedure.
Citation: us:cfr:46:380.5
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-380.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 380 (Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Maritime Subsidy Board or the Maritime Administrator may dispense with the publication of notice when not inconsistent with applicable laws.
