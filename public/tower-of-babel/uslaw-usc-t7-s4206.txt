7 U.S.C. § 4206 — Grants, contracts, etc., authority
Citation: us:usc:7:4206
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section4206
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 73 (Farmland Protection Policy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may carry out the purposes of this chapter, with existing facilities and funds otherwise available, through the use of grants, contracts, or such other means as the Secretary deems appropriate.
