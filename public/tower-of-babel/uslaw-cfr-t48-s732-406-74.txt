48 C.F.R. § 732.406-74 — 732.406-74 Revocation of the LOC.
Citation: us:cfr:48:732.406-74
Creator: Chapter 7—Agency For International Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-732.406-74
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 732 (Contract Financing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If during the term of the contract M/CFO/CMP believes that the LOC should be revoked, M/CFO/CMP may, after consultation with the cognizant contracting officer(s) and GC, revoke the LOC by written notification to the contractor. A copy of any such revocation notice will immediately be provided to the cognizant contracting officer(s).
