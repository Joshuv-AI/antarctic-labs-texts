20 U.S.C. § 3507 — Existing references to transferor officials or bodies deemed references to transferee officials or bodies
Citation: us:usc:20:3507
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section3507
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 48 (Department Of Education); Subchapter V (Transitional, Savings, And Conforming Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

With respect to any function transferred by this chapter and exercised on or after May 4, 1980, reference in any other Federal law to any department, commission, or agency or any officer or office the functions of which are so transferred shall be deemed to refer to the Secretary, other official, or component of the Department to which this chapter transfers such functions.
