14 C.F.R. § 29.411 — Ground clearance: tail rotor guard.
Citation: us:cfr:14:29.411
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-29.411
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 29 (Airworthiness Standards: Transport Category Rotorcraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) It must be impossible for the tail rotor to contact the landing surface during a normal landing.
(b) If a tail rotor guard is required to show compliance with paragraph (a) of this section—
(1) Suitable design loads must be established for the guard: and
(2) The guard and its supporting structure must be designed to withstand those loads.
