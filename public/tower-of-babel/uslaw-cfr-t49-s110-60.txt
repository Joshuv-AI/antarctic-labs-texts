49 C.F.R. § 110.60 — Cost sharing.
Citation: us:cfr:49:110.60
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-110.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 110 (Hazardous Materials Public Sector Training And Planning Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The recipient must provide 20 percent of the direct and indirect costs of all activities approved in the grant agreement with non-Federal funds.
