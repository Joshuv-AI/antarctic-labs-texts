49 U.S.C. § 28505 — Rules and regulations
Citation: us:usc:49:28505
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section28505
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part E (Miscellaneous); Chapter 285 (Commuter Rail Mediation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Within 1 year after the date of enactment of this section, the Board shall issue such rules and regulations as may be necessary to carry out this chapter.
