Local well-posedness for a class of semilinear Moore-Gibson-Thompson equations with subcritical nonlinearities Thanks: ∗ Research partially supported by CNPq # 303039/2021-3, Brazil.
Flank D. M. Bezerra ∗, Luís M. Salge

Abstract
. In this paper, we study a class of higher-order semilinear evolution equations inspired by the Moore-Gibson-Thompson model introduced by Dell’Oro, Liverani and Pata (2023), involving strongly elliptic operators of order ( 2 ​ m 2m ) with homogeneous boundary conditions. The associated unbounded linear operator is a sectorial operator with zero belonging to the resolvent set, allowing the construction of fractional powers spaces, and the analysis of their spectral properties. We prove the local well-posedness of the corresponding semilinear Cauchy problem under subcritical nonlinearities. Our framework clarifies the role of extrapolation spaces and fractional domains in handling the lack of accretivity and bounded imaginary powers of the operator. Mathematics Subject Classification 2020 : 35G10, 35K46, 47A75, 47D03, Key words and phrases: sectorial operator; fractional powers; analytic semigroup; higher order equation.

Finally, in this section, we prove a local solvability result for the problem (

f
and

. We observe that (

|f_{i}(s_{1})-f_{i}(s_{2})|\leq c(1+|s_{1}|^{\varrho-1}+|s_{2}|^{\varrho-1})|s_{1}-s_{2}|

for any
s_{1},s_{2}\in\mathbb{R}
,
i=1,2
, for some

Lemma 3.1
.

Let
f
be a twice continuously differentiable function such that the growth conditions (
1.6
) and (

) hold. Then

(3.2)

\|f_{i}(u_{1})-f_{i}(u_{2})\|_{L^{\frac{2N}{N+2m}}(\Omega)}\leq c(1+\|u_{1}\|_{H^{m}(\Omega)}^{\varrho-1}+\|u_{2}\|_{H^{m}(\Omega)}^{\varrho-1})\|u_{1}-u_{2}\|_{H^{m}(\Omega)},

for any
u_{1},u_{2}\in H^{m}(\Omega)
, and
i=1,2
.

Proof:

From (
3.1
), the Hölder inequality, and the Sobolev embeddings we obtain

\begin{split}\|f_{i}(u_{1})-f_{i}(u_{2})\|_{L^{\frac{2N}{N+2m}}(\Omega)}&\leq c\left[\int_{\Omega}(1+|u_{1}|^{\varrho_{i}-1}+|u_{2}|^{\varrho_{i}-1})^{\frac{2N}{N+2m}}|u_{1}-u_{2}|^{\frac{2N}{N+2m}}dx\right]^{\frac{N+2m}{2N}}\\
&\leq c|1+\|1+|u_{1}|^{\varrho_{i}-1}+|u_{2}|^{\varrho_{i}-1}\|_{L^{\frac{N}{2m}}(\Omega)}\|u_{1}+u_{2}\|_{L^{\frac{2N}{N-2m}}(\Omega)}\\
&\leq C\left(1+\|1+\|u_{1}\|_{L^{\frac{N(\varrho_{1}-1)}{2m}}(\Omega)}^{\varrho_{i}-1}+\|u_{2}\|_{L^{\frac{N(\varrho_{1}-1)}{2m}}(\Omega)}^{\varrho_{i}-1}\right)\|u_{1}+u_{2}\|_{L^{\frac{2N}{N-2m}}(\Omega)}.\end{split}

Since

\dfrac{2N(\varrho_{i}-1)}{4m}\leq\dfrac{2N}{N-2m}

we have

L^{\frac{N(\varrho_{i}-1)}{2m}}(\Omega)\supset H^{m}(\Omega).

for any
i=1,2
.

We observe that if
\varrho_{i}=\frac{N+2m}{N-2m}
, then
L^{\frac{N(\varrho_{i}-1)}{2m}}(\Omega)=L^{\frac{4mN}{N-2m}}(\Omega)
, and
(L^{\frac{4mN}{N-2m}}(\Omega))^{*}=L^{\frac{2N}{N+2m}}(\Omega)
.
This completes the proof.
\Box
