12 U.S.C. § 4640 — Notice of service
Citation: us:usc:12:4640
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section4640
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 46 (Government Sponsored Enterprises); Subchapter III (Enforcement Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any service required or authorized to be made by the Director under this subchapter may be made by registered mail, or in such other manner reasonably calculated to give actual notice as the Director may by regulation or otherwise provide.
