27 C.F.R. § 24.40 — Gauging and measuring.
Citation: us:cfr:27:24.40
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-24.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 24 (Wine). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Appropriate TTB officers may require the proprietor to furnish the necessary facilities and assistance to gauge or measure wine or spirits in any container or to examine any apparatus, equipment, container, or material on wine premises.
