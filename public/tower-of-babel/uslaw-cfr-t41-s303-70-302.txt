41 C.F.R. § 303-70.302 — -70.302 Interment expenses for immediate family member.
Citation: us:cfr:41:303-70.302
Creator: Chapter 303—Payment Of Expenses Connected With The Death Of Certain Employees
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 303-70 (70—Agency Requirements For Payment Of Expenses Connected With The Death Of Certain Employees And Immediate Family Members). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies may not pay interment expenses when an immediate family member, residing with the employee, dies while the employee is stationed OCONUS.
