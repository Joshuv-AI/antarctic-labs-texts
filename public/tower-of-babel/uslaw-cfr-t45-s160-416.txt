45 C.F.R. § 160.416 — Authority to settle.
Citation: us:cfr:45:160.416
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-160.416
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 160 (General Administrative Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this subpart limits the authority of the Secretary to settle any issue or case or to compromise any penalty.
