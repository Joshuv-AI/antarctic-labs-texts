46 C.F.R. § 57.05-4 — -4 Welder qualification by procedure tests.
Citation: us:cfr:46:57.05-4
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-57.05-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 57 (Welding And Brazing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Qualification tests of welders may be omitted for welders who weld satisfactory procedure qualification test assemblies as required by subpart 57.03.
