23 C.F.R. § 710.301 — General.
Citation: us:cfr:23:710.301
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-710.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 710 (Right-Of-Way And Real Estate). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The project development process typically follows a sequence of actions and approvals in order to qualify for funding. The key steps in this process typically are planning, environmental review, project agreement/authorization, acquisition, construction advertising, and construction.
