42 U.S.C. § 6242 — Coordination with import quota system
Citation: us:usc:42:6242
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section6242
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 77 (Energy Conservation); Subchapter I (Domestic Supply Availability); Part B (Strategic Petroleum Reserve). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No quantitative restriction on the importation of any petroleum product into the United States imposed by law shall apply to volumes of any such petroleum product imported into the United States for storage in the Reserve.
