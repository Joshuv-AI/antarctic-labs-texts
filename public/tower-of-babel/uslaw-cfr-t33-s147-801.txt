33 C.F.R. § 147.801 — Boxer Platform safety zone.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 147 (Safety Zones). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Description. The Boxer Platform is located at position 27°56′48″ N, 90°59′48″ W. The area within 500 meters (1640.4 feet) from each point on the structure's outer edge, not to extend into the adjacent East—West Gulf of America Fairway is a safety zone.
(b) Regulation. No vessel may enter or remain in this safety zone except:
(1) An attending vessel;
(2) A vessel under 100 feet in length overall not engaged in towing; or
(3) A vessel authorized by the Commander, USCG Heartland District.
