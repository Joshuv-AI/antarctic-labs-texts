16 U.S.C. § 5012 — Disposition of property
Citation: us:usc:16:5012
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section5012
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 70 (North Pacific Anadromous Stocks Convention). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall dispose of any United States property held by the International North Pacific Fisheries Commission on the date of its termination in a manner that would further the purposes of this chapter.
