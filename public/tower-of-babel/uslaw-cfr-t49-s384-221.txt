49 C.F.R. § 384.221 — Out-of-service regulations (intoxicating beverage).
Citation: us:cfr:49:384.221
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-384.221
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 384 (State Compliance With Commercial Driver'S License Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The State shall adopt, and enforce on operators of CMVs as defined in §§ 383.5 and 390.5 of this title, the provisions of § 392.5 (a) and (c) of this title in accordance with the Motor Carrier Safety Assistance Program as contained in 49 CFR part 350 and applicable policy and guidelines.
