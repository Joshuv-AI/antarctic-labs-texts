48 C.F.R. § 333.209 — 333.209 Suspected fraudulent claims.
Citation: us:cfr:48:333.209
Creator: Chapter 3—Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-333.209
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 333 (Protests, Disputes, And Appeals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer shall submit any instance of a contractor's suspected fraudulent claim to the Office of Inspector General for investigation.
