39 U.S.C. § 5601 — Sea post service
Citation: us:usc:39:5601
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title39-section5601
Context: United States Code, Title 39 (Postal Service); Part V (Transportation Of Mail); Chapter 56 (Transportation Of Mail By Vessel). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Postal Service may maintain sea post service on ocean vessels conveying mail to and from the United States.
