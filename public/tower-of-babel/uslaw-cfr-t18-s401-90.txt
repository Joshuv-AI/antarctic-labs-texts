18 C.F.R. § 401.90 — Appeals from final Commission action; Time for appeals.
Citation: us:cfr:18:401.90
Creator: Delaware River Basin Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-401.90
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 401 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any party participating in a hearing conducted pursuant to the provisions of this subpart may appeal any final Commission action. To be timely, such an appeal must be filed with an appropriate federal court, as provided in Article 15.1(p) of the Commission's Compact, within forty-five (45) days of final Commission action.
