28 C.F.R. § 811.14 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:28:811.14
Creator: Court Services And Offender Supervision Agency For The District Of Columbia
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-811.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 811 (Sex Offender Registration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The terms “attends school,” “Court,” “in custody or under supervision,” “sex offender,” and “works” shall have the same meaning as set forth in Section 2 of the Sex Offender Registration Act of 1999 (D.C. Official Code Section 22-4001).
(b) The term “the Act” means the Sex Offender Registration Act of 1999 (D.C. Official Code Section 22-4001 et seq.).
(c) The term “days” means business days unless otherwise specified.
(d) In relation to a motor vehicle, the term “owns” includes both exclusive ownership and co-ownership, and the term “owner” includes both exclusive owners and co-owners.
