25 U.S.C. § 5130 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:25:5130
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section5130
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 45 (Protection Of Indians And Conservation Of Resources). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purposes of this title: 1
(1)
The term “Secretary” means the Secretary of the Interior.
(2)
The term “Indian tribe” means any Indian or Alaska Native tribe, band, nation, pueblo, village or community that the Secretary of the Interior acknowledges to exist as an Indian tribe.
(3)
The term “list” means the list of recognized tribes published by the Secretary pursuant to section 5131 of this title.
