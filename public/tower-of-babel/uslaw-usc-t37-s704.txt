37 U.S.C. § 704 — Allotments: officers of Public Health Service
Citation: us:usc:37:704
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title37-section704
Context: United States Code, Title 37 (Pay And Allowances Of The Uniformed Services); Chapter 13 (Allotments And Assignments Of Pay). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Commissioned officers of the Public Health Service who are on active duty may, under regulations prescribed by the President, make allotments from their pay.
