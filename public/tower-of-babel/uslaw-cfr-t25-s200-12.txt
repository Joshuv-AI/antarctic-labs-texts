25 C.F.R. § 200.12 — Contract term incorporation.
Citation: us:cfr:25:200.12
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-200.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 200 (Terms And Conditions: Coal Leases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The requirements of 30 CFR part 750 shall be incorporated in all existing and new contracts entered into for coal mining on Indian lands.
