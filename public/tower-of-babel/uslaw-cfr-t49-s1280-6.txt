49 C.F.R. § 1280.6 — Storage of classified documents.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1280.6
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1280.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1280 (Handling Of National Security Information And Classified Material). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All classified documents shall be stored in the safe located in Room 5325 of the STB headquarters building. In those instances where the Emergency Coordinator is not available to receive classified documents, they may be stored, unopened, in the safe located in Room 5325.
