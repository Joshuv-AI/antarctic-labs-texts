42 U.S.C. § 5913 — Evaluation by National Institute of Standards and Technology of energy-related inventions prior to awarding of grants by Secretary; promulgation of regulations
Citation: us:usc:42:5913
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section5913
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 74 (Nonnuclear Energy Research And Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The National Institute of Standards and Technology shall give particular attention to the evaluation of all promising energy-related inventions, particularly those submitted by individual inventors and small companies for the purpose of obtaining direct grants from the Secretary. The National Institute of Standards and Technology is authorized to promulgate regulations in the furtherance of this section.
