45 C.F.R. § 92.211 — Nondiscrimination in the delivery of health programs and activities through telehealth services.
Citation: us:cfr:45:92.211
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-92.211
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 92 (Nondiscrimination In Health Programs Or Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A covered entity must not, in delivery of its health programs and activities through telehealth services, discriminate on the basis of race, color, national origin, sex, age, or disability.
