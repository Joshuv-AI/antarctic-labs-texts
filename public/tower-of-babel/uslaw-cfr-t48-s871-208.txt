48 C.F.R. § 871.208 — 871.208 Rehabilitation facilities.
Citation: us:cfr:48:871.208
Creator: Chapter 8—Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-871.208
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 871 (Vocational Rehabilitation And Employment Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Charges by rehabilitation facilities for the rehabilitation services provided under 38 U.S.C. chapter 31 are paid in the same manner as charges for educational and vocational services through contract, agreement, or other arrangement.
