42 C.F.R. § 59a.6 — a.6 How may funds or materials be used?
Citation: us:cfr:42:59a.6
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-59a.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 59a (PART 59a—NATIONAL LIBRARY OF MEDICINE GRANTS). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The grantee shall expend funds or use materials provided by a grant under this subpart solely for the purposes for which the funds or materials were granted, in accordance with the pertinent provisions of the approved application and budget, the regulations of this subpart, the terms and conditions of the award, and the applicable cost principles in 2 CFR parts 200 and 300, subpart E.
