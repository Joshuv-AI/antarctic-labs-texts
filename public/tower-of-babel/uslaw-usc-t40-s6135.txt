40 U.S.C. § 6135 — Parades, assemblages, and display of flags in the Supreme Court Building and grounds
Citation: us:usc:40:6135
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section6135
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Part C (Federal Building Complexes); Chapter 61 (United States Supreme Court Building And Grounds); Subchapter IV (Prohibitions And Penalties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is unlawful to parade, stand, or move in processions or assemblages in the Supreme Court Building or grounds, or to display in the Building and grounds a flag, banner, or device designed or adapted to bring into public notice a party, organization, or movement.
