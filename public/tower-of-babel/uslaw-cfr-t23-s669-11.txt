23 C.F.R. § 669.11 — Certification submittal.
Citation: us:cfr:23:669.11
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-669.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 669 (Enforcement Of Heavy Vehicle Use Tax). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Governor or an official designated by the Governor, shall each year submit the certification, including the supporting material specified in § 669.9 to the FHWA Division Administrator prior to January 1.
