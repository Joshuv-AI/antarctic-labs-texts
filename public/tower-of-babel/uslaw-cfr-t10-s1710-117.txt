10 C.F.R. § 1710.117 — Refunds.
Citation: us:cfr:10:1710.117
Creator: Defense Nuclear Facilities Safety Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1710.117
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1710 (Federal Employee Salary Offset Procedures For The Collection Of A Debt Owed To The Federal Government). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Board will refund promptly any amounts deducted to satisfy debts owed to the United States when the debt is waived, found not owed to the United States, or when directed by an administrative or judicial order.
(b) Unless required or permitted by law or contract, refunds under this section may not bear interest.
