Correction: Association between TyG-BMI and early-onset hypertension: evidence from NHANES
Authors: Wushuang Li, Chenliang Ge, Jianyong Zhou
Published: 2026

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-025-92159-6, published online 12 March 2025

The original version of this Article contained an error in the reporting of the NHANES cycles included in the analysis, in that the analyses included data from the 2007–2018 cycles, but the 2011–12 cycle was excluded due to the absence of triglyceride data. The Article was revised as follows.

In the Abstract,

“We analyzed data from 5193 adults with age under 40 years from the National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (NHANES) 2009–2018."

now reads:

"We analyzed data from 5193 adults with age under 40 years from the National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (NHANES) cycles 2007–2008, 2009–2010, 2013–2014, 2015–2016, and 2017–2018."

In the Methods, under the subheading ‘Study population’,

"Our analysis encompassed NHANES public data files spanning from 2009 to 2018. From an initial dataset of 50,086 participants, 14,946 individuals with available triglyceride, fasting plasma glucose (FPG), BMI data were identified."

now reads:

"Our analysis encompassed five specific NHANES cycles: 2007–2008, 2009–2010, 2013–2014, 2015–2016, and 2017–2018. From an initial dataset of 50,086 participants drawn from these five cycles, 14,946 individuals with available triglyceride, fasting plasma glucose (FPG), and BMI data were identified."

The original Article has been corrected.
