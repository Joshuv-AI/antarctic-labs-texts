31 C.F.R. § 590.305 — Foreign person.
Citation: us:cfr:31:590.305
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-590.305
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 590 (Transnational Criminal Organizations Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term foreign person means any citizen or national of a foreign state, or any entity organized under the laws of a foreign state or existing in a foreign state, including any such individual or entity who is also a United States person.
