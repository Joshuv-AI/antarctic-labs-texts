Cosmological Unstraightening
Nima Rasekh

Abstract
. The unstraightening construction due to Lurie [ Lur09 ] establishes an equivalence between presheaves and fibrations, using one prominent model of ( ∞ , 1 ) (\infty,1) -categories, namely quasi-categories. In this work we generalize this result by proving that for all ∞ \infty -cosmoi of ( ∞ , 1 ) (\infty,1) -categories in the sense of Riehl and Verity [ RV22 ] , which includes quasi-categories but also complete Segal spaces or 1 1 -complicial sets, their corresponding notions of fibrations and presheaves are biequivalent ∞ \infty -cosmoi via a natural zig-zag of cosmological biequivalences. The major idea that makes this possible is a lift of the quasi-categorical unstraightening construction to a cosmological biequivalence.

1.
Introduction

The theory of
(\infty,1)
-categories has played a key conceptual role in many aspects of mathematics, including topology, geometry and representation theory. This has necessitated a rigorous development, which has primarily followed two strands. The
model-dependent
approach uses specific models, such as quasi-categories or complete Segal spaces, to develop the desired theory, whereas the
model-independent approach
, such as
\infty
-cosmoi
[
RV22
]
or the axiomatization of
[
BSP21
]
, focus on universal properties. Often one starts with a model-dependent definition, benefiting from the point-set properties of the model, only to then later develop a more universal approach, which generalizes the results to all models. An elegant example is given by
adjunctions of
(\infty,1)
-categories
, which were first defined for quasi-categories strongly relying on properties this model provides
[
Lur09
, Definition 5.2.2.1]
, but then redeveloped in the context of
\infty
-cosmoi, using
2
-categorical ideas
[
RV22
, Definition 2.1.1]
in a way that generalizes the original approach
[
RV22
, F.5]
.

For some quasi-categorical notions their model-independent analogues, while studied, have remained less understood. One interesting example is the unstraightening construction. It has first been introduced by Lurie
[
Lur09
]
and subsequently studied by many others
[
HM15
,
Ste17
,
RV18
,
Cis19
,
CN22
]
, primarily using quasi-categories. Generally speaking it provides an equivalence between (right or Cartesian) fibrations and presheaves (valued in spaces or
\infty
-categories). It is a key ingredient in a variety of very important higher categorical constructions, such as monoidal structures. While both fibrations and presheaves provide us with examples of
\infty
-cosmoi, the unstraightening construction itself does not satisfy the required technical conditions (as we observe in
Section

2.1
). This means the transition of the unstraightening construction from a model dependent approach via quasi-categories to a model independent approach via
\infty
-cosmoi has remained incomplete.

In this work we lift the unstraightening functor to a
cosmological biequivalence
between
\infty
-cosmoi. The key technical insight that makes this possible is the fibrational version of the
universal simplicial model category
, which is a concept introduced by Rezk, Schwede and Shipley
[
RSS01
]
. It was already observed by Joyal and Tierney that the universal simplicial model structure on quasi-categories is given by complete Segal spaces
[
JT07
, Section 8]
, and this was generalized by the author to a relation between Cartesian fibrations on marked simplicial sets and Cartesian fibrations on marked simplicial spaces
[
Ras21
]
. We use this insight to lift the unstraightening construction from (marked) simplicial sets to (marked) simplicial spaces, making it simplicial along the way, fulfilling the precise technical requirement to obtain cosmological biequivalences

s\mathcal{U}\mathrm{n}_{\mathcal{Q}\mathcal{C}\mathrm{at},S}\colon\mathcal{F}\mathrm{un}(\mathfrak{C}[S]^{op},\mathcal{K}\mathrm{an})\to\mathcal{R}\mathcal{F}\mathrm{ib}_{\mathcal{Q}\mathcal{C}\mathrm{at}}(S),\hskip 21.68121pts\mathcal{U}\mathrm{n}^{+}_{\mathcal{Q}\mathcal{C}\mathrm{at},S}\colon\mathcal{F}\mathrm{un}(\mathfrak{C}[S]^{op},1{-}\mathcal{C}\mathrm{omp})^{core}\to\mathcal{C}\mathrm{art}_{\mathcal{Q}\mathcal{C}\mathrm{at}}(S)^{core}

from the
\infty
-cosmoi of (space or
\infty
-category valued) presheaves, to the
\infty
-cosmoi of (right or Cartesian) fibrations (
Corollary

3.3
/
Corollary

3.8
). Building on this result, we show that for every
\infty
-cosmos of
(\infty,1)
-categories
\mathcal{K}
(which includes complete Segal spaces, Segal categories and
1
-complicial sets) their notion of fibrations and functors are cosmologically biequivalent

\mathcal{F}\mathrm{un}(\mathfrak{C}_{\mathcal{K}}[S]^{op},\mathcal{K}\mathrm{an})\simeq\mathcal{R}\mathcal{F}\mathrm{ib}_{\mathcal{K}}(S),\hskip 21.68121pt\mathcal{F}\mathrm{un}(\mathfrak{C}_{\mathcal{K}}[\mathscr{C}]^{op},1{-}\mathcal{C}\mathrm{omp})^{core}\simeq\mathcal{C}\mathrm{art}_{\mathcal{K}}(\mathscr{C})^{core}

via natural zig-zags of cosmological biequivalences (
Corollary

3.5
/
Corollary

3.10
). Finally, for the particular relevant choices of
\infty
-cosmoi, such as complete Segal spaces, one gets a direct unstraightening cosmological biequivalence (
Corollary

3.6
/
Corollary

3.11
)

s\mathcal{U}\mathrm{n}_{\mathcal{K},S}\colon\mathcal{F}\mathrm{un}(\mathfrak{C}[S]^{op},\mathcal{K}\mathrm{an})\to\mathcal{R}\mathcal{F}\mathrm{ib}_{\mathcal{Q}\mathcal{C}\mathrm{at}}(S),\hskip 21.68121pts\mathcal{U}\mathrm{n}^{+}_{\mathcal{K},S}\colon\mathcal{F}\mathrm{un}(\mathfrak{C}[S]^{op},1{-}\mathcal{C}\mathrm{omp})^{core}\to\mathcal{C}\mathrm{art}_{\mathcal{Q}\mathcal{C}\mathrm{at}}(S)^{core}

Note that while
3.3
,
3.5
and
3.6
are the best possible result,
3.8
,
3.10
and
3.11
could theoretically permit a generalization via an enrichment over quasi-categories. Obtaining such a result currently faces significant theoretical challenges, further discussed in
Section

4
.

The cosmological biequivalences we obtain also have further computational benefits. Indeed, we often want to analyze the straightening-unstraightening adjunction at the level of underlying
\infty
-categories. We can always obtain an adjoint equivalence of quasi-categories out of every Quillen equivalence, however the general construction is computationally challenging
[
LMG15
]
. On the other hand, a
simplicially enriched
Quillen adjunction of simplicially enriched model categories permits a very direct description of the underlying adjoint equivalence of quasi-categories via the homotopy coherent nerve
[
Lur09
, Proposition 5.2.4.6]
, which we present in
Corollary

3.4
and
Corollary

3.9
.

\infty

\infty
-cosmoi, as explained in
Section

2.4
.

2.4.
\infty
-Cosmoi

\infty
-cosmoi are categories strictly enriched over quasi-categories with finite weighted limits and a notion of fibration, called
isofibration

[
RV22
, Definition 1.2.1]
. The guiding example is the
\infty
-cosmos of quasi-categories (
\mathcal{Q}\mathcal{C}\mathrm{at}
)
[
RV22
, Proposition 1.2.10]
, but it also includes other models such as complete Segal spaces (
css
), Segal categories (
\mathcal{S}\mathrm{eg}
) and
1
-complicial sets (
1{-}\mathcal{C}\mathrm{omp}
)
[
RV22
, Example 1.2.24]
. These
\infty
-cosmoi can all be obtained by starting with a model structure enriched over the Joyal model structure in which all fibrant objects are cofibrant, and then restricting to the full enriched subcategory of fibrant objects
[
RV22
, Proposition. E.1.1]
, and hence are called the
underlying
\infty
-cosmos
of the model structure.

The
\infty
-cosmoi from the previous paragraph are in fact all equivalent to the
\infty
-cosmos of quasi-categories
[
RV22
, Example 1.3.9]
, in the following precise sense. First recall a cosmological functor of
\infty
-cosmoi is an enriched functor that preserves fibrations and limits
[
RV22
, Definition 1.3.1]
and it is a cosmological biequivalence if it is fully faithful and essentially surjective
[
RV22
, Definition 1.3.8]
. Now, for each one of these examples
\mathcal{K}
, the functor
\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{K}}(1,-)\colon\mathcal{K}\to\mathcal{Q}\mathcal{C}\mathrm{at}
that evaluates at the terminal object and we denote by
\mathcal{U}\mathrm{nd}
(as it gives us the
underlying quasi-category
) is a cosmological biequivalence. Given their importance such
\infty
-cosmoi are called
\infty
-cosmoi of
(\infty,1)
-categories

[
RV22
, Definition 1.3.10]
. Notice, prominent examples of
\infty
-cosmoi of
(\infty,1)
-categories also have explicitly known inverse biequivalences
\mathcal{N}\mathrm{erve}\colon\mathcal{Q}\mathcal{C}\mathrm{at}\to\textsc{css}
,
\mathcal{N}\mathrm{erve}\colon\mathcal{Q}\mathcal{C}\mathrm{at}\to\mathcal{S}\mathrm{eg}
and
(-)^{\natural}\colon\mathcal{Q}\mathcal{C}\mathrm{at}\to 1{-}\mathcal{C}\mathrm{omp}
.

As every Kan complex is in particular a quasi-category, appropriately chosen Kan enriched categories also give us
\infty
-cosmoi, called
discrete

[
RV22
, Definition 1.2.26]
. The key example of a discrete
\infty
-cosmos is the category of Kan complexes (
\mathcal{K}\mathrm{an}
)
[
RV22
, Proposition 1.2.12]
. For a given
\infty
-cosmos
\mathcal{K}
, we can restrict each mapping quasi-category to its maximal underlying Kan complex (also called the core
[
RV22
, Definition 12.1.8]
), to obtain the discrete
\infty
-cosmos
\mathcal{K}^{core}
.

For every
\infty
-cosmos
\mathcal{K}
, there is a model independent way to study various fibrations over an
\infty
-category
\mathscr{C}
in
\mathcal{K}
. As a result we can define the
\infty
-cosmos of Cartesian fibrations over
\mathscr{C}
, which we denote by
\mathcal{C}\mathrm{art}_{\mathcal{K}}(\mathscr{C})

[
RV22
, Definition 5.2.1]
, and its sub-
\infty
-cosmos of right fibrations
\mathcal{R}\mathcal{F}\mathrm{ib}_{\mathcal{K}}(\mathscr{C})
(there called
discrete Cartesian fibrations

[
RV22
, Definition 5.5.3]
as explained in
[
RV22
, Digression 5.5.4]
).

Let
\mathcal{C}
be a simplicially enriched category. We want to construct the
\infty
-cosmos of functors from
\mathcal{C}
with values in spaces (or
\infty
-categories). More explicitly, we would like to construct the
\infty
-cosmoi
\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C},\mathcal{K}\mathrm{an})
and
\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C},1{-}\mathcal{C}\mathrm{omp})
. Unfortunately, due to the strict enrichment, the naive functor categories would not be
\infty
-cosmoi and would hence have to be suitably derived. Fortunately, the
\infty
-cosmoi
\mathcal{K}\mathrm{an}
and
1{-}\mathcal{C}\mathrm{omp}
both arise as the underlying
\infty
-cosmos of a model structure, and in those cases the functor
\infty
-cosmos admits a more direct construction, which we discuss next.

Let
M
be a combinatorial model category enriched over the Joyal model structure with all objects cofibrant, and denote its underlying
\infty
-cosmos (the full subcategory of fibrant objects) by
\mathcal{M}
. Then the category of enriched functors
\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C},M)^{inj}
with the injective model structure is again enriched over the Joyal model structure with all objects cofibrant
[
Lur09
, Proposition A.3.3.2]
, and hence has an underlying
\infty
-cosmos, which we denote by
\mathcal{F}\mathrm{un}(\mathcal{C},\mathcal{M})
.

We can now apply this construction of the functor
\infty
-cosmos to our situation of interest. For a given
\infty
-cosmos of
(\infty,1)
-categories
\mathcal{K}
and
\infty
-category
\mathscr{C}
in
\mathcal{K}

A cosmological unstraightening for Cartesian fibrations

We now generalize this result from right fibrations to Cartesian fibrations. Let
s\mathrm{St}^{+}_{S}
be the unique simplicially enriched lift of
\mathrm{St}^{+}_{S}
along
(p_{1}^{+})^{*}
.
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces(s\mathscr{S}\mathrm{et}^{+})_{/S}}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces(s\mathscr{S}^{+})_{/S}}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces{\mathrm{Fun}(\mathfrak{C}[S]^{op},s\mathscr{S}\mathrm{et}^{+})}}
\scriptstyle{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces(p^{+}_{1})^{*}}
\scriptstyle{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\mathrm{St}^{+}_{S}}
\scriptstyle{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces s\mathrm{St}^{+}_{S}}

Concretely, for a given simplicial set
T
over
S
we have
s\mathrm{St}_{S}^{+}(T\times\Delta[n]\to S)=s\mathrm{St}_{S}^{+}(T\to S)\times\{\Delta[n]^{\#}\}
, where
\{\Delta[n]^{\#}\}
denotes the constant functor with value
\Delta[n]^{\#}
. Again, by construction

preserves colimits and hence has a right adjoint
s\mathrm{Un}_{S}^{+}\colon\mathrm{Fun}(\mathfrak{C}[S]^{op},s\mathscr{S}\mathrm{et}^{+})\to(s\mathscr{S}^{+})_{/S}
. Notice, as we have
s\mathrm{St}^{+}_{S}(p_{1}^{+})^{*}=\mathrm{St}^{+}_{S}
, by uniqueness of right adjoints we have
(i^{+}_{1})^{*}s\mathrm{Un}_{S}^{+}=\mathrm{Un}_{S}^{+}
. We can now prove the main result.

Theorem 3.7
.

(s\mathrm{St}^{+}_{S},s\mathrm{Un}^{+}_{S})
is a simplicially enriched Quillen equivalence.

Proof.

We follow the same steps as in the proof of
Theorem

3.2
. Indeed, as
\mathrm{St}_{S}^{+}
is left Quillen (by
2.2
) it takes
\partial\Delta[n]\to\Delta[n]
over
S
to a cofibration in
\mathrm{Fun}(\mathfrak{C}[S]^{op},s\mathscr{S}\mathrm{et}^{+})^{proj}
and so
s\mathrm{Un}^{+}_{S}
takes fibrant objects to Reedy fibrations. Moreover, by
[
Ras23a
, Theorem 1.2]
,
[
Ras21
, Corollary 2.46]
and
[
Lur09
, Theorem 3.1.5.1]
a Reedy fibration in

is a Cartesian fibration if its image under

is a Cartesian fibration and so
s\mathrm{Un}_{S}^{+}
takes fibrant objects to Cartesian fibrations, as
\mathrm{Un}_{S}^{+}
does. Similarly,
s\mathrm{Un}_{S}^{+}
takes fibrations (weak equivalences) between fibrant objects to fibrations (weak equivalences) between Cartesian fibrations. Hence, by
[
JT07
, Proposition 7.15]
,
(s\mathrm{St}^{+}_{S},s\mathrm{Un}^{+}_{S})
is a Quillen adjunction. Finally, by
2.2
,
2.4
and
2
-out-of-
3
it is a simplicially enriched Quillen equivalence.
∎

4.
Challenges Towards A Quasi-categorically enriched cosmological Unstraightening

The model structures on the categories
\mathrm{Fun}(\mathfrak{C}[S]^{op},s\mathscr{S}\mathrm{et}^{+})
and
s\mathscr{S}\mathrm{et}^{+}_{/S}
can also be enriched over quasi-categories
[
Lur09
, Remark 3.1.4.5]
and the straightening construction is non-strictly enriched as well
[
Lur09
, Corollary 3.2.1.15]
. Hence, one could imagine a lift of the unstraightening construction

\mathrm{Un}^{+}_{S}\colon\mathrm{Fun}(\mathfrak{C}[S]^{op},s\mathscr{S}\mathrm{et}^{+})\to s\mathscr{S}\mathrm{et}^{+}_{/S},

that is in fact strictly enriched (meaning cotensored) over quasi-categories. However, existing results do not permit such generalization. Indeed, what we would need is a proof for the following conjecture.

Conjecture 4.1
.

There exists a model structure on
s\mathscr{S}^{+}
with the following specifications:

(1)

It is Quillen equivalent to the Joyal model structure.

(2)

The inclusion
i^{+}_{1}\colon s\mathscr{S}\mathrm{et}^{+}\to s\mathscr{S}^{+}
is left Quillen from the Cartesian model structure.

(3)

The inclusion
i_{1}\colon s\mathscr{S}\mathrm{et}\to s\mathscr{S}^{+}
is left Quillen from the Joyal model structure.

Assuming we had such a model structure, we could conceivably repeat the arguments in
Theorem

3.7
to obtain a quasi-categorically enriched lift for the unstraightening construction.

On a first view, settling such a conjecture might appear straightforward. Indeed, the model structure on marked simplicial spaces described in
Section

2.3
almost satisfies these conditions, with the sole difference that
i_{1}\colon s\mathscr{S}\mathrm{et}\to s\mathscr{S}^{+}
is left Quillen from the Kan model structure. However, as of yet such a model structure has remained out of reach. In fact, one way to obtain such a model structure would be to have a working model structure for double
\infty
-categories on bisimplicial sets which interacts well with the “square construction”, which takes an
(\infty,2)
-category to a double
\infty
-category. The existence was already conjectured by Gaitsgory and Rozenblyum
[
GR17
, Chapter 10, Theorems 4.1.3 and 5.2.3]
. While the conjecture has now been settled in the case of strict double categories
[
GMSV23
, Theorem 6.1]
, the
\infty
-categorical case has remained open.
