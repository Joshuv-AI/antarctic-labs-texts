10 U.S.C. § 12684 — Reserves: separation for absence without authority or sentence to imprisonment
Citation: us:usc:10:12684
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section12684
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel Generally); Chapter 1221 (Separation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President or the Secretary concerned may drop from the rolls of the armed force concerned any Reserve—
(1)
who has been absent without authority for at least three months;
(2)
who may be separated under section 12687 of this title by reason of a sentence to confinement adjudged by a court-martial; or
(3)
who is sentenced to confinement in a Federal or State penitentiary or correctional institution after having been found guilty of an offense by a court other than a court-martial or other military court, and whose sentence has become final.
