17 U.S.C. § 1008 — Prohibition on certain infringement actions
Citation: us:usc:17:1008
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title17-section1008
Context: United States Code, Title 17 (Copyrights); Chapter 10 (Digital Audio Recording Devices And Media); Subchapter D (Prohibition On Certain Infringement Actions, Remedies, And Arbitration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No action may be brought under this title alleging infringement of copyright based on the manufacture, importation, or distribution of a digital audio recording device, a digital audio recording medium, an analog recording device, or an analog recording medium, or based on the noncommercial use by a consumer of such a device or medium for making digital musical recordings or analog musical recordings.
