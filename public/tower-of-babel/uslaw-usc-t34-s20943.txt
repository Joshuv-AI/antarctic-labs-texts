34 U.S.C. § 20943 — Federal assistance in identification and location of sex offenders relocated as a result of a major disaster
Citation: us:usc:34:20943
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section20943
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 209 (Child Protection And Safety); Subchapter I (Sex Offender Registration And Notification); Part B (Improving Federal Criminal Law Enforcement To Ensure Sex Offender Compliance With Registration And Notification Requirements And Protection Of Children From Violent Predators). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Attorney General shall provide assistance to jurisdictions in the identification and location of a sex offender relocated as a result of a major disaster.
