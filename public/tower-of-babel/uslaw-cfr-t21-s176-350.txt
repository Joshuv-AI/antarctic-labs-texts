21 C.F.R. § 176.350 — Tamarind seed kernel powder.
Citation: us:cfr:21:176.350
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-176.350
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 176 (Indirect Food Additives: Paper And Paperboard Components). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Tamarind seed kernel powder may be safely used as a component of articles intended for use in producing, manufacturing, packing, processing, preparing, treating, packaging, transporting, or holding food, subject to the provisions of this section.
(a) Tamarind seed kernel powder is the ground kernel of tamarind seed (Tamarindus indica L.) after removal of the seed coat.
(b) It is used in the manufacture of paper and paperboard.
