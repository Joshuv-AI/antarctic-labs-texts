28 U.S.C. § 513 — Attorney General to advise Secretaries of military departments
Citation: us:usc:28:513
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section513
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part II (Department Of Justice); Chapter 31 (The Attorney General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When a question of law arises in the administration of the Department of the Army, the Department of the Navy, or the Department of the Air Force, the cognizance of which is not given by statute to some other officer from whom the Secretary of the military department concerned may require advice, the Secretary of the military department shall send it to the Attorney General for disposition.
