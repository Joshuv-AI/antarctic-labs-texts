25 C.F.R. § 15.103 — How do I begin the probate process?
Citation: us:cfr:25:15.103
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-15.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 15 (Probate Of Indian Estates, Except For Members Of The Osage Nation And The Five Civilized Tribes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As soon as possible, contact any of the following offices to inform us of the decedent's death:
(a) The agency or BIA regional office nearest to where the decedent was enrolled;
(b) Any agency or BIA regional office; or
(c) The Trust Beneficiary Call Center in OST.
