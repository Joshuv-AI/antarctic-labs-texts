43 C.F.R. § 3101.25 — Computation.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3101.25
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3101.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3100 (Oil And Gas Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The accountable acreage of a party owning an undivided interest in a lease will be the party's proportionate part of the total lease acreage.
