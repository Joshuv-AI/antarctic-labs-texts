5 C.F.R. § 1651.18 — Payment to one bars payment to another.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1651.18
Creator: Federal Retirement Thrift Investment Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1651.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1651 (Death Benefits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Payment made to a beneficiary(ies) in accordance with this part, based upon information received before payment, bars any claim by any other person.
