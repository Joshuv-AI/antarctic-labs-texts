18 U.S.C. § 1545 — Safe conduct violation
Citation: us:usc:18:1545
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section1545
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 75 (Passports And Visas). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever violates any safe conduct or passport duly obtained and issued under authority of the United States shall be fined under this title, imprisoned not more than 10 years, or both.
