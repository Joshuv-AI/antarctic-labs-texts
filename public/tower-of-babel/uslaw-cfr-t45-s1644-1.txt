45 C.F.R. § 1644.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1644.1
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1644.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1644 (Disclosure Of Case Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this rule is to ensure that recipients disclose to the public and to the Corporation certain information on cases filed in court by their attorneys.
