48 C.F.R. § 223.7402 — 223.7402 Prohibition.
Citation: us:cfr:48:223.7402
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-223.7402
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 223 (Environment, Sustainable Acquisition, And Material Safety). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Do not procure any fire-fighting agent that contains in excess of one part per billion perfluoroalkyl substances or polyfluoroalkyl substances. Procurements of fire-fighting agent for use solely onboard ocean-going vessels are exempt from this prohibition.
