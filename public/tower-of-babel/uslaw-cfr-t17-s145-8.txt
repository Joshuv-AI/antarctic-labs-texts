17 C.F.R. § 145.8 — Fees for records services.
Citation: us:cfr:17:145.8
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-145.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 145 (Commission Records And Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A schedule of fees for record services, including locating, and making records available, and copying, appears in appendix B to this part. Copies of the schedule of fees may also be obtained upon request made in person, by telephone or by mail from the FOIA compliance staff or at any regional office of the Commission.
