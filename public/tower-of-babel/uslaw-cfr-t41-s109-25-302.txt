41 C.F.R. § 109-25.302 — -25.302 Office furniture, furnishings, and equipment.
Citation: us:cfr:41:109-25.302
Creator: Chapter 109—Department Of Energy Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-109-25.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 109-25 (25—General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director, Office of Management, Program Secretarial Officer (PSO), and designated contractors shall establish criteria for the use of office furniture, furnishings, and equipment.
