12 C.F.R. § 28.17 — Notice of change in activity or operations.
Citation: us:cfr:12:28.17
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-28.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 28 (International Banking Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Notice. A Federal branch or agency shall notify the OCC if:
(a) It changes its corporate title;
(b) It changes its mailing address;
(c) It converts to a state branch, state agency, or representative office; or
(d) The parent foreign bank changes the designation of its home state.
