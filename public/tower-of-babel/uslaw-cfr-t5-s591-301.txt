5 C.F.R. § 591.301 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:5:591.301
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-591.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 591 (Allowances And Differentials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes the regulations required by section 5942 of title 5, United States Code, for the payment of an allowance based on duty at remote worksites.
