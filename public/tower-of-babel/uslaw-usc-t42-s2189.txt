42 U.S.C. § 2189 — Federally financed research
Citation: us:usc:42:2189
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2189
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 23 (Development And Control Of Atomic Energy); Subchapter XII (Patents And Inventions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall affect the right of the Commission to require that patents granted on inventions, made or conceived during the course of federally financed research or operations, be assigned to the United States.
