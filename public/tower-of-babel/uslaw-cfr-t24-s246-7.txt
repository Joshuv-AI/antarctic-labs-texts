24 C.F.R. § 246.7 — Notice to tenants.
Citation: us:cfr:24:246.7
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-246.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 246 (Local Rent Control). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

At least 30 days before filing a formal request to HUD for preemption of local rent control regulations, the mortgagor shall notify the tenants of its intention to so file. Copies of the Notice shall be:
(a) Delivered directly or by mail to each tenant; and
(b) Posted in at least 3 conspicuous places within each structure or building in which the affected dwelling units are located.
