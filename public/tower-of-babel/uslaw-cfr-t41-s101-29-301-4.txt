41 C.F.R. § 101-29.301-4 — -29.301-4 Periodic review of Federal product descriptions.
Citation: us:cfr:41:101-29.301-4
Creator: Chapter 101—Federal Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-101-29.301-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 101-29 (29—Federal Product Descriptions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Assistant Administrator for Federal Supply and Services is responsible for establishing a program for periodically reviewing Federal product descriptions to determine whether revision, cancellation or reauthorization (validation) is appropriate. The frequency of the review shall be based on the degree of change in the technology of the product covered by the description and shall be conducted at least once every 5 years.
