29 C.F.R. § 500.156 — Scope of agreements with Federal agencies.
Citation: us:cfr:29:500.156
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-500.156
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 500 (Migrant And Seasonal Agricultural Worker Protection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Every agreement between the Secretary and any other Federal agency under the authority referred to in § 500.155 of this part shall contain terms and conditions mutually agreeable to both parties, and shall contain such delegation of authority as the Secretary deems useful.
