40 U.S.C. § 15507 — Payment of non-Federal share for certain Federal grant programs
Citation: us:usc:40:15507
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section15507
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Chapter 155 (Financial Assistance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Amounts made available to carry out this subtitle shall be available for the payment of the non-Federal share for any project carried out under another Federal grant program—
(1)
for which a Commission is not the sole or primary funding source; and
(2)
that is consistent with the authorities of the applicable Commission.
