7 U.S.C. § 1338 — Transfer of quotas
Citation: us:usc:7:1338
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1338
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 35 (Agricultural Adjustment Act Of 1938); Subchapter II (Loans, Parity Payments, Consumer Safeguards, Marketing Quotas, And Marketing Certificates); Part B (Marketing Quotas); Subpart iii (Marketing Quotas—Wheat). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Farm marketing quotas for wheat shall not be transferable, but, in accordance with regulations prescribed by the Secretary for such purpose, any farm marketing quota in excess of the supply of wheat for such farm for any marketing year may be allocated to other farms on which the acreage allotment has not been exceeded.
