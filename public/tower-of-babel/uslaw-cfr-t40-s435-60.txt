40 C.F.R. § 435.60 — Applicability; description of the stripper subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:435.60
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-435.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 435 (Oil And Gas Extraction Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to those onshore facilities which produce 10 barrels per well per calendar day or less of crude oil and which are operating at the maximum feasible rate of production and in accordance with recognized conservation practices. These facilities are engaged in production, and well treatment in the oil and gas extraction industry.
