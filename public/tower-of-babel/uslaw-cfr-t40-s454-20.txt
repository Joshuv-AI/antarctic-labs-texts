40 C.F.R. § 454.20 — Applicability; description of the manufacture of gum rosin and turpentine subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:454.20
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-454.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 454 (Gum And Wood Chemicals Manufacturing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to discharges resulting from the manufacture of gum rosin and turpentine.
