41 C.F.R. § 109-40.5004 — -40.5004 Government bills of lading.
Citation: us:cfr:41:109-40.5004
Creator: Chapter 109—Department Of Energy Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-109-40.5004
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 109-40 (40—Transportation And Traffic Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In those instances where DOE cost-type contractors are authorized to use Government bills of lading, specific employees of cost-type contractors will be authorized by the contracting officer to issue such Government bills of lading (see Title V, U.S. Government Accounting Office Policy and Procedures Manual for Guidance of Federal Agencies).
