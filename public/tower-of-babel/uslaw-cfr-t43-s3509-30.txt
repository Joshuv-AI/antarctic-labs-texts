43 C.F.R. § 3509.30 — May I withdraw my application for a future interest lease?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3509.30
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3509.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3500 (Leasing Of Solid Minerals Other Than Coal And Oil Shale). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. You must file the withdrawal with BLM before the lease is signed. BLM will retain any fees already paid for processing the application.
