49 C.F.R. § 611.107 — Relation to the planning processes.
Citation: us:cfr:49:611.107
Creator: Federal Transit Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-611.107
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 611 (Major Capital Investment Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All New Starts and Small Starts projects proposed for funding assistance under this part must emerge from the metropolitan and Statewide planning process, consistent with 23 CFR part 450, and be included in the fiscally constrained long-range transportation plan required under 23 CFR part 450.
