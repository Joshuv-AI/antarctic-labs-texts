10 C.F.R. § 70.18 — Types of licenses.
Citation: us:cfr:10:70.18
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-70.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 70 (Domestic Licensing Of Special Nuclear Material). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Licenses for special nuclear material are of two types: general and specific. Any general license provided in this part is effective without the filing of applications with the Commission or the issuance of licensing documents to particular persons. Specific licenses are issued to named persons upon applications filed pursuant to the regulations in this part.
