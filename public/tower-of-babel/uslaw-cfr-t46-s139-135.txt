46 C.F.R. § 139.135 — Addition and removal of auditors and surveyors.
Citation: us:cfr:46:139.135
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-139.135
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 139 (Third-Party Organizations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A TPO must maintain a list of current and former auditors and surveyors.
(b) To add an auditor or surveyor, the TPO must submit that person's experience, background, and qualifications to the TVNCOE.
(c) The TVNCOE must be notified when an auditor or surveyor is removed from employment.
