Editorial expression of concern: Epigenetic reactivation of estrogen receptor-α (ERα) by genistein enhances hormonal therapy sensitivity in ERα-negative breast cancer
Authors: Yuanyuan Li, Syed M Meeran, Shweta N Patel, Huaping Chen, Tabitha M Hardy, Trygve O Tollefsbol
Published: 2026

Correction: Mol Cancer 12, 9 (2013)

https://doi.org/10.1186/1476-4598-12-9; Published: 4 February 2013

The Editor-in-Chief would like to alert readers that concerns were raised regarding similarities of Acetyl-H3K9, Acetyl-H4, Dimethyl-H3K3, and Input band sections between Figs. 2A and 3A of another article [1].

The authors have provided original data that could not be sufficiently validated and maintain that this error does not affect the final results, findings, and conclusions. Therefore, readers are advised to interpret the data listed above with caution.

Author Trygve Tollefsbol has stated on behalf of all authors that they disagree to this EeoC.
