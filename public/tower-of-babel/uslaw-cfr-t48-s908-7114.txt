48 C.F.R. § 908.7114 — 908.7114 Wiretapping and eavesdropping equipment.
Citation: us:cfr:48:908.7114
Creator: Chapter 9—Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-908.7114
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 908 (Required Sources Of Supplies And Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Acquisition by DOE offices and contractors of devices primarily designed to be used surreptitiously to overhear or record conversations is prohibited.
