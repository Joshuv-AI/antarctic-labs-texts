16 U.S.C. § 541e — Availability of funds for acquisition of lands, etc., within added area
Citation: us:usc:16:541e
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section541e
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 2 (National Forests); Subchapter II (Scenic Areas). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding the provisions of section 200306(a)(2) of title 54, moneys appropriated from the Land and Water Conservation Fund shall be available for the acquisition of any lands, waters, or interests therein within the area added to the Siuslaw National Forest by this subchapter.
