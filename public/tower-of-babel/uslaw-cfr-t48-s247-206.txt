48 C.F.R. § 247.206 — 247.206 Preparation of solicitations and contracts.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 247 (Transportation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Consistent with FAR 15.304 and 215.304, consider using the following as evaluation factors or subfactors:
(1) Record of claims involving loss or damage; and
(2) Commitment of transportation assets to readiness support (e.g., Civil Reserve Air Fleet and Voluntary Intermodal Sealift Agreement).
