7 C.F.R. § 1486.201 — Eligible commodities.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1486.201
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1486.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1486 (Emerging Markets Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any agricultural commodity or product thereof, excluding tobacco, that is comprised of at least 50 percent by weight, exclusive of added water, of agricultural commodities grown or raised in the United States is eligible for funding. Projects that seek support for multiple commodities are also eligible.
