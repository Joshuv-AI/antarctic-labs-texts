45 C.F.R. § 1149.39 — Who pays the costs for a subpoena?
Citation: us:cfr:45:1149.39
Creator: National Foundation On The Arts And The Humanities
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1149.39
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1149 (Program Fraud Civil Remedies Act Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The party requesting a subpoena must pay the cost of the fees and mileage of any witness subpoenaed in the amounts that would be payable to a witness in a proceeding in United States District Court. A check for witness fees and mileage must accompany the subpoena when served, except that when a subpoena is issued on behalf of the NEA, a check for witness fees and mileage need not accompany the subpoena.
