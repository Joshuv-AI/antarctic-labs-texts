10 U.S.C. § 10153 — Standby Reserve: status of members
Citation: us:usc:10:10153
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section10153
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization And Administration); Chapter 1005 (Elements Of Reserve Components). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

While in an inactive status, a Reserve is not eligible for pay or promotion and (as provided in section 12734(a) of this title) does not accrue credit for years of service under chapter 1223 of this title.
