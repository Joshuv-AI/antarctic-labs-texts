15 U.S.C. § 713a–12 — Deposit of net realized gain of Commodity Credit Corporation in Treasury
Citation: us:usc:15:713a–12
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section713a–12
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 15 (Economic Recovery); Subchapter I (Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In the event the accounts of the Commodity Credit Corporation reflect a net realized gain for any such fiscal year, the amount of such net realized gain shall be deposited in the Treasury by the Commodity Credit Corporation and shall be credited to miscellaneous receipts.
