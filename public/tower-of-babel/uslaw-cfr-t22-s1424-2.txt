22 C.F.R. § 1424.2 — Who may file a petition.
Citation: us:cfr:22:1424.2
Creator: Foreign Service Labor Relations Board; Federal Labor Relations Authority; General Counsel Of The Federal Labor Relations Authority; And The Foreign Service Impasse Disputes Panel
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-1424.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 1424 (Expedited Review Of Negotiability Issues). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A petition for review of a negotiability issue may be filed by the exclusive representative which is a party to the negotiations.
