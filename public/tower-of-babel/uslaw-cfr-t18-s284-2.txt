18 C.F.R. § 284.2 — Refunds and interest.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 284 (Certain Sales And Transportation Of Natural Gas Under The Natural Gas Policy Act Of 1978 And Related Authorities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Refunds. Any rate or charge collected for any sale, transportation, or assignment conducted pursuant to this part which exceeds the rates or charges authorized by this part shall be refunded.
(b) Interest. All refunds made pursuant to this section must include interest at an amount determined in accordance with § 154.501(d) of this chapter.
