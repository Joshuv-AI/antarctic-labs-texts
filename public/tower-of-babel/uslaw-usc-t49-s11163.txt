49 U.S.C. § 11163 — Cost availability
Citation: us:usc:49:11163
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section11163
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part A (Rail); Chapter 111 (Operations); Subchapter IV (Railroad Cost Accounting). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As required by the rules of the Board governing discovery in Board proceedings, rail carriers shall make relevant cost data available to shippers, States, ports, communities, and other interested parties that are a party to a Board proceeding in which such data are required.
