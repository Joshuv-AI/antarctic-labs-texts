42 C.F.R. § 37.80 — Availability of records for radiographs.
Citation: us:cfr:42:37.80
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-37.80
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 37 (Specifications For Medical Examinations Of Coal Miners). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Medical information and radiographs on miners will be released by NIOSH only with the written consent from the miner, or if the miner is deceased, written consent from the miner's widow or widower, next of kin, or legal representative.
(b) To the extent authorized, original film radiographs will be made available for examination only at the NIOSH facility in Morgantown, WV.
