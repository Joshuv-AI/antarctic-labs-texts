10 U.S.C. § 1131 — Purple Heart: limitation to members of the armed forces
Citation: us:usc:10:1131
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1131
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 57 (Decorations And Awards). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The decoration known as the Purple Heart (authorized to be awarded pursuant to Executive Order 11016) may only be awarded to a person who is a member of the armed forces at the time the person is killed or wounded under circumstances otherwise qualifying that person for award of the Purple Heart.
