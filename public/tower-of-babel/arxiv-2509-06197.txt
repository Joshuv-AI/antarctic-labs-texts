Lagrangian interpretation of Abel-Jacobi mappings associated to Fano threefolds
Rodolfo Aguilar, Mark Green and Phillip Griffiths

Abstract
. Using the general framework due to Donagi-Markman [ DM96 ] and Markushevich [ Mar08 ] we shall derive an expression for the differential of Abel-Jacobi mappings on Fano threefolds. This formula involves information normal to the Lagrangian submanifolds constructed in [ DM96 ] and [ Mar08 ] . It may be applied to give new proofs of a number of classical results about these varieties.
