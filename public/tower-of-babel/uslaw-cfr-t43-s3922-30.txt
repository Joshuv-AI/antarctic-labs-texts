43 C.F.R. § 3922.30 — Application—Additional information.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3922.30
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3922.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3920 (Oil Shale Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

At any time during processing of the application, or the environmental or similar assessments of the application, the BLM may request additional information from the applicant. Failure to provide the best available and most accurate information may result in suspension or termination of processing of the application, or in a decision to deny the application.
