2 U.S.C. § 4913 — Regulations of Page Board
Citation: us:usc:2:4913
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section4913
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 49 (Congressional Pages); Subchapter II (House Of Representatives). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Page Board shall have authority to prescribe such regulations as may be necessary to carry out sections 4911 to 4913 of this title.
