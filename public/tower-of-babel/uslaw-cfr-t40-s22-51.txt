40 C.F.R. § 22.51 — Presiding Officer.
Citation: us:cfr:40:22.51
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-22.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 22 (Consolidated Rules Of Practice Governing The Administrative Assessment Of Civil Penalties And The Revocation/Termination Or Suspension Of Permits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Presiding Officer shall be a Regional Judicial Officer. The Presiding Officer shall conduct the hearing, and rule on all motions until an initial decision has become final or has been appealed.
