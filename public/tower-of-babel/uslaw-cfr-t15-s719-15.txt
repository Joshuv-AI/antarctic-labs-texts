15 C.F.R. § 719.15 — Procedural stipulations.
Citation: us:cfr:15:719.15
Creator: Bureau Of Industry And Security, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-719.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 719 (Enforcement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless otherwise ordered and subject to § 719.16 of the CWCR, a written stipulation agreed to by all parties and filed with the ALJ will modify the procedures established by this part.
