44 C.F.R. § 209.4 — Allocation and availability of funds.
Citation: us:cfr:44:209.4
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-209.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 209 (Supplemental Property Acquisition And Elevation Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) We will allocate available funds based on the number and value of properties that meet the eligibility criteria and whose owners want to participate in an acquisition or elevation project.
(b) We may reallocate funds for which we do not receive and approve adequate applications. We will obligate most available funds within 12 months following the deadline for submitting applications, unless extenuating circumstances exist.
