41 C.F.R. § 102-36.50 — -36.50 Excess personal property screening period.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-36.50
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-36.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-36 (36—Disposition Of Excess Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The screening period starts when GSA receives the report of excess personal property. GSA determines the duration of the screening period. GSA may adjust the screening period in coordination with the holding agency.
