46 C.F.R. § 298.1 — Purpose.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 298 (Vessel And Shipyard Financing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Vessel and Shipyard Financing Program supports new vessel construction, vessel reconstruction, reconditioning, and repair, and shipyard modernization in the United States through financing guarantees.
