40 C.F.R. § 408.120 — Applicability; description of the Southern non-breaded shrimp processing in the contiguous States subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:408.120
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-408.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 408 (Canned And Preserved Seafood Processing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to discharges resulting from the processing of non-breaded shrimp in the Southern contiguous States, including North and South Carolina, Georgia, Florida, Alabama, Mississippi, Louisiana, and Texas. The effluent limitations contained in this subpart L are applicable to existing facilities processing more than 908 kg (2000 lbs) of raw material per day on any day during a calendar year and all new sources.
