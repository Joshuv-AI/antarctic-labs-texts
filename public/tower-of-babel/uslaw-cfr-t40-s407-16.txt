40 C.F.R. § 407.16 — Pretreatment standards for new sources.
Citation: us:cfr:40:407.16
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-407.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 407 (Canned And Preserved Fruits And Vegetables Processing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any new source subject to this subpart that introduces process wastewater pollutants into a publicly owned treatment works must comply with 40 CFR part 403.
