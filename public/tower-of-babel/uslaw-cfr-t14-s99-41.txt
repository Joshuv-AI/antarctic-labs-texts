14 C.F.R. § 99.41 — General.
Citation: us:cfr:14:99.41
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-99.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 99 (Security Control Of Air Traffic). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The airspace above the areas described in this subpart is established as an ADIZ. The lines between points described in this subpart are great circles except that the lines joining adjacent points on the same parallel of latitude are rhumb lines.
