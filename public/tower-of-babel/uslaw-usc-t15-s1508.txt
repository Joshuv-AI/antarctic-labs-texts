15 U.S.C. § 1508 — General Counsel
Citation: us:usc:15:1508
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1508
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 40 (Department Of Commerce). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There shall be in the Department of Commerce a General Counsel, who shall be appointed by the President, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate.
