33 C.F.R. § 181.13 — Removal of labels.
Citation: us:cfr:33:181.13
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-181.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 181 (Manufacturer Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person may remove a label required by this part or remove or alter any information on a label required by this part, unless authorized by the Commandant.
