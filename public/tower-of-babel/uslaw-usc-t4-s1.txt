4 U.S.C. § 1 — Flag; stripes and stars on
Citation: us:usc:4:1
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title4-section1
Context: United States Code, Title 4 (Flag And Seal, Seat Of Government, And The States); Chapter 1 (The Flag). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The flag of the United States shall be thirteen horizontal stripes, alternate red and white; and the union of the flag shall be forty-eight stars, white in a blue field.
