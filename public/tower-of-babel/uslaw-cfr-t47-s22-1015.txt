47 C.F.R. § 22.1015 — Repeater operation.
Citation: us:cfr:47:22.1015
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-22.1015
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 22 (Public Mobile Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Offshore central stations may be used as repeater stations provided that the licensee is able to maintain control of the station, and in particular, to turn the transmitter off, regardless of whether associated subscriber stations are transmitting at the time.
