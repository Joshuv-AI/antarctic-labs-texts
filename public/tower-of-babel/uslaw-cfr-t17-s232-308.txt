17 C.F.R. § 232.308 — Type size and font; legibility.
Citation: us:cfr:17:232.308
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-232.308
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 232 (Regulation S-T—General Rules And Regulations For Electronic Filings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Provisions relating to type size, font and other legibility requirements shall not apply to electronic format documents.
