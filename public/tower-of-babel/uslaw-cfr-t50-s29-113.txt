50 C.F.R. § 29.113 — What hydrologic standards must I meet?
Citation: us:cfr:50:29.113
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-29.113
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 29 (Land Use Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must:
(a) Construct facilities in a manner that maintains hydrologic movement and function.
(b) Not cause measurable degradation of surface water or groundwater beyond that of existing conditions.
(c) Conduct operations in a manner that maintains natural processes of erosion and sedimentation.
