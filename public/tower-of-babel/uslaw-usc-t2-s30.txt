2 U.S.C. § 30 — Term of service of Members of Congress as trustees or directors of corporations or institutions appropriated for
Citation: us:usc:2:30
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section30
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 2 (Organization Of Congress). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In all cases where Members of Congress or Senators are appointed to represent Congress on any board of trustees or board of directors of any corporation or institution to which Congress makes any appropriation, the term of said Members or Senators, as such trustee or director, shall continue until the expiration of two months after the first meeting of the Congress chosen next after their appointment.
