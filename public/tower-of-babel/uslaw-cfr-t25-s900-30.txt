25 C.F.R. § 900.30 — When the Secretary declines all or a portion of a proposal, is the Secretary required to provide an Indian tribe or tribal organization with technical assistance?
Citation: us:cfr:25:900.30
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior, And Indian Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-900.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 900 (Contracts Under The Indian Self-Determination And Education Assistance Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. The Secretary shall provide additional technical assistance to overcome the stated objections, in accordance with section 102(b) of the Act, and shall provide any necessary requested technical assistance to develop any modifications to overcome the Secretary's stated objections.
