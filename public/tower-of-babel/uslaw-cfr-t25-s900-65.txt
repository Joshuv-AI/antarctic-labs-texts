25 C.F.R. § 900.65 — What programmatic reports and data shall the Indian tribe or tribal organization provide?
Citation: us:cfr:25:900.65
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior, And Indian Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-900.65
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 900 (Contracts Under The Indian Self-Determination And Education Assistance Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless required by statute, there are no mandatory reporting requirements. Each Indian tribe or tribal organization shall negotiate with the Secretary the type and frequency of program narrative and program data report(s) which respond to the needs of the contracting parties and that are appropriate for the purposes of the contract. The extent of available resources will be a consideration in the negotiations.
