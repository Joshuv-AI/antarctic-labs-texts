22 U.S.C. § 4608 — Independence and limitations
Citation: us:usc:22:4608
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section4608
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 56 (United States Institute Of Peace). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Nothing in this chapter may be construed as limiting the authority of the Office of Management and Budget to review and submit comments on the Institute’s budget request at the time it is transmitted to the Congress.
(b)
No political test or political qualification may be used in selecting, appointing, promoting, or taking any other personnel action with respect to any officer, employee, agent, or recipient of Institute funds or services or in selecting or monitoring any grantee, contractor, person, or entity receiving financial assistance under this chapter.
