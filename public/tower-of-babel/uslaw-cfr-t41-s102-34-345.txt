41 C.F.R. § 102-34.345 — -34.345 Government motor vehicle records.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-34.345
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-34.345
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-34 (34—Motor Vehicle Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each agency is responsible for developing and keeping adequate accounting and reporting procedures for Government motor vehicles. These will ensure accurate recording of inventory, cost, and operational data needed to manage and control motor vehicles, and will satisfy reporting requirements.
