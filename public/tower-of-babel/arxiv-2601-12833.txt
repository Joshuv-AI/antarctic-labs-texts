Quantum State Preparation of Ferromagnetic Magnons by Parametric Driving
Monika E. Mycroft, Rostyslav O. Serha, Andrii V. Chumak, Carlos Gonzalez-Ballestero

Abstract
We propose a method to prepare and certify Gaussian quantum states of the ferromagnetic resonance spin-wave modes in ferromagnets using a longitudinal drive. Contrary to quantum optics-based strategies, our approach harnesses a purely magnonic feature—the spin-wave nonlinearity—to generate magnon squeezing. This resource is used to prepare vacuum-squeezed states, as well as entangled states between modes of different magnets coupled via a microwave cavity. We propose methods to detect such states with classical methods, such as ferromagnetic resonance or local pickup coils, and quantify the required detection efficiency. We analytically solve the case of ellipsoidal yttrium iron garnet ferrimagnets, but our method applies to a vast range of shapes and sizes. Our work enables quantum magnonics experiments without single-magnon sources or detectors (qubits), thus bringing the quantum regime within reach of the wider magnonics community.

In this Letter, we propose a method to prepare and measure Gaussian vacuum-squeezed and entangled states of the ferromagnetic resonance (FMR) mode of millimeter-sized magnets using only external magnetic fields, specifically a parametric drive, and linear classical measurements.
Being realizable on most current experimental platforms, our work enables exploration of core open questions in quantum magnonics, such as what the fundamental limits to quantum state certification or the nature of magnon quantum decoherence are.

In conclusion, we propose a method to prepare single- and two-mode Gaussian quantum states of FMR modes in magnets. The approach applies to a broad range of magnet sizes and to any geometry with unequal

x-
and
y-
FMR mode amplitudes, such as in-plane magnetized disks or slabs. Combined with low temperatures and conventional microwave driving, it enables the preparation of arbitrary multimode Gaussian states with up to 3 dB of squeezing. We also introduce certification methods based on standard classical techniques available in most magnonics laboratories. This work opens access to the quantum regime of magnons using conventional setups without qubits. Future work will explore protocols exceeding the 3 dB limit and exploit highly sensitive squeezed states to probe magnon decoherence, which remains poorly understood despite recent experimental interest
44
;
43
;
42
. Notably, the squeezed states considered here involve

\sim 10^{20}
spins, vastly exceeding atom-based spin-squeezed systems

Derivation of the two-magnon master equation

We apply the Bogoliubov transformation
\hat{s}_{\text{B},j}=c_{\alpha}\hat{s}_{j}+s_{\alpha}\hat{s}_{j}^{\dagger}
, with
c_{\alpha}\equiv\cosh\alpha
and
s_{\alpha}\equiv\sinh\alpha
,
to the total Hamiltonian
\hat{\mathcal{H}}_{\text{tot}}=\hat{\mathcal{H}}_{1}+\hat{\mathcal{H}}_{2}+\hat{\mathcal{H}}_{c}+\hat{\mathcal{H}}_{\text{int}}

The Bogoliubov transformation also modifies the magnon dissipators

\displaystyle\mathcal{L}_{\hat{s}_{j}\hat{s}^{\dagger}_{j}}[\hat{\rho}_{\rm mc}]=

\displaystyle-s_{\alpha}c_{\alpha}\left(\mathcal{L}_{\hat{s}_{\text{B},j}\hat{s}_{\text{B},j}}[\hat{\rho}_{\rm mc}]+\mathcal{L}_{\hat{s}^{\dagger}_{\text{B},j}\hat{s}^{\dagger}_{\text{B},j}}\right)+

(20)

\displaystyle+c_{\alpha}^{2}\mathcal{L}_{\hat{s}_{\text{B},j}\hat{s}^{\dagger}_{\text{B},j}}+s_{\alpha}^{2}\mathcal{L}_{\hat{s}^{\dagger}_{\text{B},j}\hat{s}_{\text{B},j}}.

We choose
\tanh 2\alpha=2r/\Delta_{\text{m}}

\hat{\mathcal{H}}_{\text{tot}}
into the sum of a diagonal part,
\hat{\mathcal{H}}_{\text{diag}}=\sum_{j=1,2}\hbar\Delta_{\text{B}}\hat{s}_{\text{B},j}^{\dagger}\hat{s}_{\text{B},j}+\hbar\Delta_{\text{c}}\hat{c}^{\dagger}\hat{c}

\displaystyle\hat{\mathcal{H}}_{\text{int}}/\hbar=\!\sum_{j=1,2}\!g_{\text{mc}}\hat{c}^{\dagger}\left(c_{\alpha}\hat{s}_{\text{B},j}-s_{\alpha}\hat{s}_{\text{B},j}^{\dagger}\right)+\text{H.c.},

\displaystyle\hat{S}=\!\sum_{j=1,2}\!\hat{c}\left(\frac{g_{\text{mc}}c_{\alpha}}{\Delta_{\text{c}}-\Delta_{\text{B}}}\hat{s}_{\text{B},j}-\frac{g_{\text{mc}}s_{\alpha}}{\Delta_{\text{c}}+\Delta_{\text{B}}}\hat{s}_{\text{B},j}^{\dagger}\right)\!-\!\text{H.c.}

5

7

The magnon operators transform as
