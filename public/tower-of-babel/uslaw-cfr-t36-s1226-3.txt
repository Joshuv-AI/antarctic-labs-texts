36 C.F.R. § 1226.3 — What standards are used as guidance for this part?

Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1226.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1226 (Implementing Disposition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

These regulations conform with guidance in ISO 15489-1:2001, Information and documentation—Records management, sections 8.3.7 (Retention and disposition), 8.5 (Discontinuing records systems), 9.2 (Determining how long to retain records), and 9.9 (Implementing disposition).
