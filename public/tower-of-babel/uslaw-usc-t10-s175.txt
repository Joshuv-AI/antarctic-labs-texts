10 U.S.C. § 175 — Reserve Forces Policy Board
Citation: us:usc:10:175
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section175
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization And General Military Powers); Chapter 7 (Boards, Councils, And Committees). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is in the Office of the Secretary of Defense a Reserve Forces Policy Board. The functions, membership, and organization of that board are set forth in section 10301 of this title.
