5 C.F.R. § 185.118 — Rights of parties.
Citation: us:cfr:5:185.118
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-185.118
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 185 (Program Fraud Civil Remedies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise limited by this part, all parties may:
(a) Be accompanied, represented, and advised by a representative;
(b) Participate in any conference held by the ALJ;
(c) Conduct discovery as provided under § 185.122;
(d) Agree to stipulations of fact or law, which shall be made a part of the record;
(e) Present evidence relevant to the issues at the hearing;
(f) Present and cross-examine witnesses;
(g) Present oral arguments at the hearing as permitted by the ALJ; and
(h) Submit written briefs and proposed findings of fact and conclusions of law after the hearing.
