46 U.S.C. § 30308 — Nonapplication
Citation: us:usc:46:30308
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section30308
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 303 (Death On The High Seas). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) State Law.—
This chapter does not affect the law of a State regulating the right to recover for death.
(b) Internal Waters.—
This chapter does not apply to the Great Lakes or waters within the territorial limits of a State.
