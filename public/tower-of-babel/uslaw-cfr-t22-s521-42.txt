22 C.F.R. § 521.42 — Judicial review.

Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-521.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 521 (Implementation Of The Program Fraud Civil Remedies Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Section 3805 of title 31, United States Code, authorizes judicial review by an appropriate United States District Court of a final decision of the Director imposing penalties or assessments under this part and specifies the procedures for such.
