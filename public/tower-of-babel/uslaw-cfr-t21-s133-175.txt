21 C.F.R. § 133.175 — Pasteurized cheese spread.
Citation: us:cfr:21:133.175
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-133.175
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 133 (Cheeses And Related Cheese Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Pasteurized cheese spread is the food which conforms to the definition and standard of identity, and is subject to the requirements for label statement of ingredients, prescribed for pasteurized process cheese spread by § 133.179, except that no emulsifying agent as prescribed by § 133.179(e) is used.
