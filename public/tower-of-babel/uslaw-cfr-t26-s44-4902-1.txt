26 C.F.R. § 44.4902-1 — -1 Partnership liability.
Citation: us:cfr:26:44.4902-1
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-44.4902-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 44 (Taxes On Wagering; Effective January 1, 1955). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any number of persons doing business in copartnership shall be required to pay but one special tax. The district director may issue a special tax stamp to a copartnership in a firm or trade name, provided the names and addresses of all members of the partnership are disclosed on Form 11-C.
