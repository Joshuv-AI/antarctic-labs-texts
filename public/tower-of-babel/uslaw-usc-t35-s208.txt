35 U.S.C. § 208 — Regulations governing Federal licensing
Citation: us:usc:35:208
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title35-section208
Context: United States Code, Title 35 (Patents); Part II (Patentability Of Inventions And Grant Of Patents); Chapter 18 (Patent Rights In Inventions Made With Federal Assistance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Commerce is authorized to promulgate regulations specifying the terms and conditions upon which any federally owned invention, other than inventions owned by the Tennessee Valley Authority, may be licensed on a nonexclusive, partially exclusive, or exclusive basis.
