41 U.S.C. § 1711 — Value engineering
Citation: us:usc:41:1711
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title41-section1711
Context: United States Code, Title 41 (Public Contracts); Chapter 17 (Agency Responsibilities And Procedures). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each executive agency shall establish and maintain cost-effective procedures and processes for analyzing the functions of a program, project, system, product, item of equipment, building, facility, service, or supply of the agency. The analysis shall be—
(1)
performed by qualified agency or contractor personnel; and
(2)
directed at improving performance, reliability, quality, safety, and life cycle costs.
