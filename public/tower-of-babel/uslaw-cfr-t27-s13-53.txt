27 C.F.R. § 13.53 — Appeal of notice of revocation.
Citation: us:cfr:27:13.53
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-13.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 13 (Labeling Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Within 45 days after the date of receipt of a notice of revocation by operation of law or regulations, the certificate holder may file a written appeal with the appropriate TTB officer. The appeal should set forth the reasons why the certificate holder believes that the regulation or law at issue does not require the revocation of the certificate.
