21 C.F.R. § 112.122 — What buildings are subject to the requirements of this subpart?
Citation: us:cfr:21:112.122
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-112.122
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 112 (Standards For The Growing, Harvesting, Packing, And Holding Of Produce For Human Consumption). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Buildings subject to the requirements of this subpart include:
(a) Any fully- or partially-enclosed building used for covered activities, including minimal structures that have a roof but do not have any walls; and
(b) Storage sheds, buildings, or other structures used to store food contact surfaces (such as harvest containers and food-packing materials).
