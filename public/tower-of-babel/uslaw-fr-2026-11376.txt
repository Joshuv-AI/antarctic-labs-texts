DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Foreign-Trade Zones Board

[B-46-2025]


Foreign-Trade Zone (FTZ) 81; Authorization of Production 
Activity; SubCom, LLC; (Undersea Fiber-Optic Cables and Repeaters); 
Newington, New Hampshire

    On November 20, 2025, SubCom, LLC submitted a notification of 
proposed production activity to the FTZ Board for its facility within 
FTZ 81, in Newington, New Hampshire.
    The notification was processed in accordance with the regulations 
of the FTZ Board (15 CFR part 400), including notice in the Federal 
Register inviting public comment (90 FR 53276, November 25, 2025). On 
June 3, 2026, the applicant was notified of the FTZ Board's decision 
that no further review of the activity is warranted at this time. The 
production activity described in the notification was authorized, 
subject to the FTZ Act and the FTZ Board's regulations, including 
section 400.14.

    Dated: June 3, 2026.
Elizabeth Whiteman,
Executive Secretary.
