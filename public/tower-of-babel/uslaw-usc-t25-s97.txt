25 U.S.C. § 97 — Proposals or bids for contracts to be preserved
Citation: us:usc:25:97
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section97
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 4 (Performance By United States Of Obligations To Indians); Subchapter I (Purchase Of Supplies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In all lettings of contracts in connection with the Indian Service, the proposals or bids received shall be filed and preserved..1
