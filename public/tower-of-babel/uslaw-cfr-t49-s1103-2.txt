49 C.F.R. § 1103.2 — Attorneys-at-law—qualifications and requirements to practice before the Board.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1103.2
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1103.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1103 (Practitioners). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person who is a member in good standing of the bar of the highest court of any State, Commonwealth, possession, territory, or the District of Columbia may represent persons before the Board.
