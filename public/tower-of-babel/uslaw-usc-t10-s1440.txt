10 U.S.C. § 1440 — Annuities not subject to legal process
Citation: us:usc:10:1440
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1440
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 73 (Annuities Based On Retired Or Retainer Pay); Subchapter I (Retired Serviceman’S Family Protection Plan). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as provided in section 1437(c)(3)(B) of this title, no annuity payable under this subchapter is assignable or subject to execution, levy, attachment, garnishment, or other legal process.
