46 C.F.R. § 69.57 — Gross tonnage ITC.
Citation: us:cfr:46:69.57
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-69.57
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 69 (Measurement Of Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Gross tonnage ITC (GT ITC) is determined by the following formula GT ITC = K1 V, in which V = total volume of all enclosed spaces in cubic meters and K1 = 0.2 + 0.02 log10 V.
