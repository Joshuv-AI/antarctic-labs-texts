33 C.F.R. § 62.35 — Mooring buoys.
Citation: us:cfr:33:62.35
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-62.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 62 (United States Aids To Navigation System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Mooring Buoys are white with a blue horizontal band. This distinctive color scheme is recommended to facilitate identification and to avoid confusion with aids to navigation.
