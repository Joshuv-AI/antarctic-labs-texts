43 C.F.R. § 2.2 — What kinds of records are not covered by the regulations in subparts A through I of this part?
Citation: us:cfr:43:2.2
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2 (Freedom Of Information Act; Records And Testimony). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Subparts A through I of this part do not apply to records that fall under the law enforcement exclusions in 5 U.S.C. 552(c)(1)-(3). These exclusions may be used only in the limited circumstances delineated by the statute and require both prior approval from the Deputy Chief FOIA Officer and the recording of their use and approval process.
