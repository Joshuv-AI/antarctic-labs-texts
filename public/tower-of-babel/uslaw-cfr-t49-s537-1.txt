49 C.F.R. § 537.1 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:49:537.1
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-537.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 537 (Automotive Fuel Economy Reports). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part establishes requirements for automobile manufacturers to submit reports to the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration regarding their efforts to improve automotive fuel economy.
