14 C.F.R. § 297.23 — Waiver of sovereign immunity.
Citation: us:cfr:14:297.23
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-297.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 297 (Foreign Air Freight Forwarders And Foreign Cooperative Shippers Associations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

By accepting an approval registration form under this part, a carrier waives any right it may possess to assert any defense of sovereign immunity from suit in any action or proceeding instituted against the carrier in any court or other tribunal in the United States based upon any claim arising out of operations by the carrier under this part.
