36 C.F.R. § 5.13 — Nuisances.
Citation: us:cfr:36:5.13
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-5.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 5 (Commercial And Private Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The creation or maintenance of a nuisance upon the federally owned lands of a park area or upon any private lands within a park area under the exclusive legislative jurisdiction of the United States is prohibited.
