43 C.F.R. § 30.218 — How may the transcript of a deposition be used?
Citation: us:cfr:43:30.218
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-30.218
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 30 (Indian Probate Hearings Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A transcript of a deposition taken under this part may be offered by any party or the judge in a hearing if the judge finds that the evidence is otherwise admissible and if either:
(a) The witness is unavailable; or
(b) The interest of fairness is served by allowing the transcript to be used.
