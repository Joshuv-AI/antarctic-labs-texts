42 U.S.C. § 5049 — Prohibition of Federal control of educational institution or school system
Citation: us:usc:42:5049
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section5049
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 66 (Domestic Volunteer Services); Subchapter IV (Administration And Coordination). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing contained in this chapter shall be construed to authorize any department, agency, officer, or employee of the United States to exercise any direction, supervision, or control over the curriculum, program of instruction, administration, or personnel of any education institution or school system.
