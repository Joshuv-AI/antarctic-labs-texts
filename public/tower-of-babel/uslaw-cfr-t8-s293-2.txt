8 C.F.R. § 293.2 — Interest rate.
Citation: us:cfr:8:293.2
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-293.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 293 (Deposit Of And Interest On Cash Received To Secure Immigration Bonds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Interest on cash deposited to secure immigration bonds will be at the rate as determined by the Secretary of the Treasury, but in no case will exceed 3 per centum per annum or be less than zero. The rate will be published by Treasury on the Treasury Web site or through another mechanism.
