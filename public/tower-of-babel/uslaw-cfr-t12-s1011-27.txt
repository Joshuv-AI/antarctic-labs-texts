12 C.F.R. § 1011.27 — Fair housing.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1011.27
Creator: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1011.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1011 (Purchasers' Revocation Rights, Sales Practices And Standards (Regulation K)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Title VIII of the Civil Rights Act of 1968, 42 U.S.C. 3601, et seq., and its implementing regulations and guidelines apply to land sales transactions to the extent warranted by the facts of the transaction.
