42 U.S.C. § 7625a — Statutory construction
Citation: us:usc:42:7625a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section7625a
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 85 (Air Pollution Prevention And Control); Subchapter III (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The parenthetical cross references in any provision of this chapter to other provisions of the chapter, or other provisions of law, where the words “relating to” or “pertaining to” are used, are made only for convenience, and shall be given no legal effect.
