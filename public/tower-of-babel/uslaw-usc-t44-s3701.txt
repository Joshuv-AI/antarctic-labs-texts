44 U.S.C. § 3701 — Advertisements for contracts in District of Columbia
Citation: us:usc:44:3701
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section3701
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 37 (Advertisements By Government Agencies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Advertisements for contracts for the public service may not be published in any newspaper published and printed in the District of Columbia unless the supplies or labor covered by the advertisement are to be furnished or performed in the District of Columbia or in the adjoining counties of Maryland or Virginia.
