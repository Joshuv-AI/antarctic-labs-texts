36 U.S.C. § 70506 — Exclusive right to name
Citation: us:usc:36:70506
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section70506
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 705 (The Foundation Of The Federal Bar Association). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation has the exclusive right to use the name “The Foundation of the Federal Bar Association”.
