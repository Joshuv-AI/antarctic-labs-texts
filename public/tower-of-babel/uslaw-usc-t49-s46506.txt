49 U.S.C. § 46506 — Application of certain criminal laws to acts on aircraft
Citation: us:usc:49:46506
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section46506
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part A (Air Commerce And Safety); Subpart iv (Enforcement And Penalties); Chapter 465 (Special Aircraft Jurisdiction Of The United States). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An individual on an aircraft in the special aircraft jurisdiction of the United States who commits an act that—
(1)
if committed in the special maritime and territorial jurisdiction of the United States (as defined in section 7 of title 18) would violate section 113, 114, 661, 662, 1111, 1112, 1113, or 2111 or chapter 109A of title 18, shall be fined under title 18, imprisoned under that section or chapter, or both; or
(2)
if committed in the District of Columbia would violate section 9 of the Act of July 29, 1892 (D.C. Code § 22-1112), shall be fined under title 18, imprisoned under section 9 of the Act, or both.
