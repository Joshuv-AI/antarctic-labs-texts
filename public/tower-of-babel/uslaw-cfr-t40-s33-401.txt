40 C.F.R. § 33.401 — What does this subpart require?
Citation: us:cfr:40:33.401
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-33.401
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 33 (Participation By Disadvantaged Business Enterprises In United States Environmental Protection Agency Programs). A recipient must negotiate with the appropriate EPA award official or his/her designee, fair share objectives for MBE and WBE participation in procurement under the financial assistance agreements.
