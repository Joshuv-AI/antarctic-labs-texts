22 U.S.C. § 7811 — Sense of Congress regarding negotiations with North Korea
Citation: us:usc:22:7811
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section7811
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 85 (North Korean Human Rights); Subchapter I (Promoting The Human Rights Of North Koreans). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the sense of Congress that the human rights of North Koreans should remain a key element in future negotiations between the United States, North Korea, and other concerned parties in Northeast Asia.
