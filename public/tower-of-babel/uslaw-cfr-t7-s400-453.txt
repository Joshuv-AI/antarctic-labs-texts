7 C.F.R. § 400.453 — Exhaustion of administrative remedies.
Citation: us:cfr:7:400.453
Creator: Federal Crop Insurance Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-400.453
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 400 (General Administrative Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All administrative remedies contained herein or incorporated herein by reference must be exhausted before Judicial Review in the United States Courts may be sought, unless review is specifically required by statute.
