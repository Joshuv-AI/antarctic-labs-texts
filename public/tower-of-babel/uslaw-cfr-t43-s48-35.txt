43 C.F.R. § 48.35 — When must the Secretary determine if the proposed amendment requires Congressional approval?
Citation: us:cfr:43:48.35
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-48.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 48 (Amendments To The Hawaiian Homes Commission Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary will review the documents submitted by the Chairman, and if they meet the requirements of § 48.15, the Secretary will determine within 60 days after receiving them if the proposed amendment requires Congressional approval.
