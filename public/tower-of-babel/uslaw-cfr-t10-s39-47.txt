10 C.F.R. § 39.47 — Radioactive markers.
Citation: us:cfr:10:39.47
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-39.47
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 39 (Licenses And Radiation Safety Requirements For Well Logging). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The licensee may use radioactive markers in wells only if the individual markers contain quantities of licensed material not exceeding the quantities specified in § 30.71 of this chapter. The use of markers is subject only to the requirements of § 39.37.
