20 C.F.R. § 802.306 — Action on request for oral argument.
Citation: us:cfr:20:802.306
Creator: Benefits Review Board, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-802.306
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 802 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As expeditiously as possible after the date upon which a request for oral argument is received, the Board shall determine whether the request shall be granted or denied.
