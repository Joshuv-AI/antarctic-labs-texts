23 C.F.R. § 460.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:23:460.1
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-460.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 460 (Public Road Mileage For Apportionment Of Highway Safety Funds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to prescribe the policies and procedures followed in identifying and reporting public road mileage for utilization in the statutory formula for the apportionment of highway safety funds under 23 U.S.C. 402(c).
