14 U.S.C. § 547 — Rewards for apprehension of persons interfering with aids to navigation
Citation: us:usc:14:547
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section547
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 5 (Functions And Powers); Subchapter III (Aids To Navigation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Coast Guard may offer and pay rewards for the apprehension and conviction, or for information helpful therein, of persons found interfering in violation of law with aids to navigation maintained by the Coast Guard; or for information leading to the discovery of missing Coast Guard property or to recovery thereof.
