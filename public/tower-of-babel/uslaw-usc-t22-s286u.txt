22 U.S.C. § 286u — Dollar-Special Drawing Rights substitution account
Citation: us:usc:22:286u
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section286u
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 7 (International Bureaus, Congresses, Etc.); Subchapter XV (International Monetary Fund And Bank For Reconstruction And Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the sense of the Congress that the Secretary of the Treasury and the United States Executive Director of the Fund shall encourage member countries of the Fund to negotiate a dollar-Special Drawing Rights substitution account in which equitable burden sharing would exist among participants in the account.
