43 C.F.R. § 49.800 — Is casual collecting allowed on lands administered by NPS or FWS?
Citation: us:cfr:43:49.800
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-49.800
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 49 (Paleontological Resources Preservation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No. Casual collecting of paleontological resources is not allowed on lands administered by NPS or FWS. On those lands, collecting any paleontological resource must be conducted in accordance with a permit as described in subpart B of this part.
