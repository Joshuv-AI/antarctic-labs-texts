7 C.F.R. § 412.4 — Requests for records.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 412 (Public Information—Freedom Of Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director of the Appeals, Litigation and Legal Liaison staff, RMA located at the above stated address, is the person authorized to receive Freedom of Information Act and to determine whether to grant or deny such requests in accordance with 7 CFR 1.8.
