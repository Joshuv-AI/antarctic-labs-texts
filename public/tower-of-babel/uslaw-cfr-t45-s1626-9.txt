45 C.F.R. § 1626.9 — Change in circumstances.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1626.9
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1626.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1626 (Restrictions On Legal Assistance To Aliens). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If, to the knowledge of the recipient, a client who was an eligible alien becomes ineligible through a change in circumstances, continued representation is prohibited by this part and a recipient must discontinue representation consistent with applicable rules of professional responsibility.
