41 U.S.C. § 133 — Executive agency
Citation: us:usc:41:133
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title41-section133
Context: United States Code, Title 41 (Public Contracts); Chapter 1 (Definitions); Subchapter II (Division B Definitions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In division B, the term “executive agency” means—
(1)
an executive department specified in section 101 of title 5;
(2)
a military department specified in section 102 of title 5;
(3)
an independent establishment as defined in section 104(1) of title 5; and
(4)
a wholly owned Government corporation fully subject to chapter 91 of title 31.
