36 C.F.R. § 1232.3 — What standards are used as guidance for this part?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1232.3
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1232.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1232 (Transfer Of Records To Records Storage Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

These regulations conform with guidance provided in ISO 15489-1:2001 Paragraphs 7.1 (Principles of records management programmes), 8.3.3 (Physical storage medium and protection), 8.3.6 (Access, retrieval and use), 8.3.7 (Retention and disposition), 9.6 (Storage and handling), and 9.8.3 (Location and tracking) apply to records creation and maintenance.
