20 C.F.R. § 411.650 — Is there a process for resolving disputes between ENs that are not State VR agencies and PMs, other than disputes on a payment request?
Citation: us:cfr:20:411.650
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-411.650
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 411 (The Ticket To Work And Self-Sufficiency Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. Under the agreement to assist us in administering the Ticket to Work program, a PM is required to have procedures to resolve disputes with ENs that do not involve an EN's payment request. (See § 411.590 for the process for resolving disputes on EN payment requests.) This process must ensure that:
(a) The EN can seek a solution through the PM's internal grievance procedures; and
(b) If the PM's internal grievance procedures do not result in a mutually agreeable solution, the PM shall refer the dispute to us for a decision.
