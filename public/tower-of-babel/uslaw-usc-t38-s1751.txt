38 U.S.C. § 1751 — Screening, counseling, and medical treatment
Citation: us:usc:38:1751
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section1751
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part II (General Benefits); Chapter 17 (Hospital, Nursing Home, Domiciliary, And Medical Care); Subchapter VI (Sickle Cell Anemia). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized to carry out a comprehensive program of providing sickle cell anemia screening, counseling, treatment, and information under the provisions of this chapter.
