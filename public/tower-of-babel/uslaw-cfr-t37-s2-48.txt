37 C.F.R. § 2.48 — Office does not issue duplicate registrations.
Citation: us:cfr:37:2.48
Creator: United States Patent And Trademark Office, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-2.48
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 2 (Rules Of Practice In Trademark Cases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If two applications on the same register would result in registrations that are exact duplicates, the Office will permit only one application to mature into registration, and will refuse registration in the other application.
