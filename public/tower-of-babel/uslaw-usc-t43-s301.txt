43 U.S.C. § 301 — Rules and regulations
Citation: us:usc:43:301
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section301
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 7 (Homesteads); Subchapter X (Stock-Raising Homestead). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Interior is authorized to make all necessary rules and regulations in harmony with the provisions and purposes of this subchapter for the purpose of carrying the same into effect.
