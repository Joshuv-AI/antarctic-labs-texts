10 U.S.C. § 7839 — Settlement of accounts: oaths
Citation: us:usc:10:7839
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7839
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Procurement); Chapter 783 (Accountability And Responsibility). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Army may detail any employee of the Department of the Army to administer oaths required by law in the settlement of an officer’s accounts for clothing and other military supplies. An oath administered under this section shall be without expense to the person to whom it is administered.
