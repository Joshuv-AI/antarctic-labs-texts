29 C.F.R. § 784.9 — “Person.”
Citation: us:cfr:29:784.9
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-784.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 784 (Provisions Of The Fair Labor Standards Act Applicable To Fishing And Operations On Aquatic Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As used in the Act (including the definition of “enterprise” set forth below in § 784.10), “person” is defined as meaning “an individual, partnership, association, corporation, business trust, legal representative, or any organized group of persons” (Act, section 3(a)).
