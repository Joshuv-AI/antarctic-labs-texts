22 U.S.C. § 3006 — Commission report to Congress; periodic reports; expenditure of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:22:3006
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section3006
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 45 (Commission On Security And Cooperation In Europe). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commission is authorized and directed to report to the House of Representatives and the Senate with respect to the matters covered by this chapter on a periodic basis and to provide information to Members of the House and Senate as requested. For each fiscal year for which an appropriation is made the Commission shall submit to Congress a report on its expenditures under such appropriation.
