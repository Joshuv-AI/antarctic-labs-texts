30 C.F.R. § 556.521 — When is my lease effective?
Citation: us:cfr:30:556.521
Creator: Bureau Of Ocean Energy Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-556.521
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 556 (Leasing Of Sulfur Or Oil And Gas And Financial Assurance Requirements In The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Your lease is effective on the first day of the month following the date that BOEM executes the lease. You may request in writing, before BOEM executes the lease, that your lease be effective as of the first day of the month in which BOEM executes the lease. If BOEM agrees to make the lease effective as of the earlier date, BOEM will so indicate when it executes the lease.
