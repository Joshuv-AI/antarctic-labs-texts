38 U.S.C. § 7114 — Internship program
Citation: us:usc:38:7114
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section7114
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part V (Boards, Administrations, And Services); Chapter 71 (Board Of Veterans’ Appeals). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall establish a competitive internship program of the Board for individuals enrolled in the first or second year of law schools accredited by the American Bar Association.
