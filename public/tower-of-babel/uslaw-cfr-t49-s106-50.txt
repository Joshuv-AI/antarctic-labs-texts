49 C.F.R. § 106.50 — Defined terms used in this subpart.

Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 106 (Rulemaking Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following defined terms (see part 105, subpart A, of this subchapter) appear in this subpart: File; Person; Political subdivision; State.
