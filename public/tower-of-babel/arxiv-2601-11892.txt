4.
Discussion and Conclusion

The transition from the Gregory-Leibniz series to the continued fraction (
3

The sequences

Furthermore, the equivalence mapping

In conclusion, this work provides a rigorous analytic validation for a notable Ramanujan Machine conjecture. By reconciling algorithmic outputs with the classical theory of
{}_{2}F_{1}
contiguous ratios, we demonstrate that these identities are grounded in the structural properties of hypergeometric functions. This methodology establishes a template for the formal verification of emerging conjectures in the transcendental landscape.
