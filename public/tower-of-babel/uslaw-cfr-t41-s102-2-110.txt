41 C.F.R. § 102-2.110 — -2.110 When must agencies provide their follow-up letters?
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-2.110
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-2.110
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-2 (2—Federal Management Regulation System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) For an individual deviation, once the action is complete.
(b) For a class deviation, at the end of each twelve-month period from the time you first took the deviation and at the end of the deviation period.
