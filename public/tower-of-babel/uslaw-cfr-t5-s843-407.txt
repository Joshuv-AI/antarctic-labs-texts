5 C.F.R. § 843.407 — Disabilities.
Citation: us:cfr:5:843.407
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-843.407
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 843 (Federal Employees Retirement System—Death Benefits And Employee Refunds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A child is eligible for continued annuity because the child is incapable of self-support if the Social Security Administration finds that the child is eligible for continued social security child's benefits because the child is incapable of self-support.
