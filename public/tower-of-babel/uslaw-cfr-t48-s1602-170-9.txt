48 C.F.R. § 1602.170-9 — 1602.170-9 Health benefits plan.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1602.170-9
Creator: Chapter 16—Office Of Personnel Management Federal Employees Health Benefits Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1602.170-9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1602 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Health benefits plan means a group insurance policy, contract, medical or hospital service agreement, membership or subscription contract, or similar group arrangements provided by a carrier for the purpose of providing, arranging for, delivering, paying for, or reimbursing any of the costs of health care services.
