30 C.F.R. § 1218.701 — What is ONRR's authority to issue these regulations?
Citation: us:cfr:30:1218.701
Creator: Office Of Natural Resources Revenue, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-1218.701
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 1218 (Collection Of Royalties, Rentals, Bonuses, And Other Monies Due The Federal Government). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The ONRR is issuing the regulations in this subpart under the authority of the FCCS, the Debt Collection Act of 1982, and the Debt Collection Improvement Act of 1996, 31 U.S.C. 3711, 3716-3718, and 3720A.
(b) The regulations in this subpart adopt and supplement the FCCS as necessary.
