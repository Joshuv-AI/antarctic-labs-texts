7 U.S.C. § 2481 — Plant variety protection
Citation: us:usc:7:2481
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2481
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 57 (Plant Variety Protection); Subchapter II (Protectability Of Plant Varieties And Certificates Of Protection); Part H (Certificates Of Plant Variety Protection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
If it appears that a certificate of plant variety protection should be issued on an application, a written notice of allowance shall be given or mailed to the owner. The notice shall specify the sum, constituting the issue fee, which shall be paid within one month thereafter.
(b)
Upon timely payment of this sum, and provided that deposit of seed has been made in accordance with section 2422(3) 1 of this title, the certificate of plant variety protection shall issue.
(c)
If any payment required by this section is not timely made, but is submitted with an additional fee prescribed by the Secretary within nine months after the due date or within such further time as the Secretary may allow, it shall be accepted.
