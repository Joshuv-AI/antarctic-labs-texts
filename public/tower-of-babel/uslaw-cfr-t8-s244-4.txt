8 C.F.R. § 244.4 — Ineligible aliens.
Citation: us:cfr:8:244.4
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-244.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 244 (Temporary Protected Status For Nationals Of Designated States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An alien is ineligible for Temporary Protected Status if the alien:
(a) Has been convicted of any felony or two or more misdemeanors, as defined in § 244.1, committed in the United States, or
(b) Is an alien described in section 208(b)(2)(A) of the Act.
