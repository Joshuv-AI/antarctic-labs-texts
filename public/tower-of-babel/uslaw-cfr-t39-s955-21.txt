39 C.F.R. § 955.21 — (Rule 21) Withdrawal of attorney.
Citation: us:cfr:39:955.21
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-955.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 955 (Rules Of Practice Before The Postal Service Board Of Contract Appeals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An attorney who wishes to withdraw from an appeal must notify the Board. The notice must include the name, email address, mailing address, and telephone number of the person who will assume responsibility for representing the party.
