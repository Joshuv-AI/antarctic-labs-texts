21 C.F.R. § 898.11 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:21:898.11
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-898.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 898 (Performance Standard For Electrode Lead Wires And Patient Cables). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Electrode lead wires and patient cables intended for use with a medical device shall be subject to the performance standard set forth in § 898.12.
