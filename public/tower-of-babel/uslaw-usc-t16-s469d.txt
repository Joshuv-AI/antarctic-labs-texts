16 U.S.C. § 469d — Ice Age National Scientific Reserve; statement of purpose
Citation: us:usc:16:469d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section469d
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1A (Historic Sites, Buildings, Objects, And Antiquities); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the purpose of sections 469d to 469i of this title to assure protection, preservation, and interpretation of the nationally significant values of Wisconsin continental glaciation, including moraines, eskers, kames, kettleholes, drumlins, swamps, lakes, and other reminders of the ice age.
