34 C.F.R. § 602.39 — Severability.
Citation: us:cfr:34:602.39
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-602.39
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 602 (The Secretary'S Recognition Of Accrediting Agencies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If any provision of this subpart or its application to any person, act, or practice is held invalid, the remainder of the subpart or the application of its provisions to any person, act, or practice shall not be affected thereby.
