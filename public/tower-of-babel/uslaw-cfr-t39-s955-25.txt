39 C.F.R. § 955.25 — (Rule 25) Indefinite suspension.
Citation: us:cfr:39:955.25
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-955.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 955 (Rules Of Practice Before The Postal Service Board Of Contract Appeals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In certain cases, appeals need to be suspended indefinitely for reasons not within the Board's control. If the suspension has continued, or it appears that it will continue, for an inordinate length of time, the Board may suspend the appeal. If neither party moves to lift the suspension within one year, the Board may dismiss the appeal with prejudice.
