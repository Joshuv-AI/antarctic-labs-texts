16 U.S.C. § 8413 — Recreation budget crosscut
Citation: us:usc:16:8413
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section8413
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 103 (Expanding Public Lands Outdoor Recreation Experiences); Subchapter I (Outdoor Recreation And Infrastructure); Part A (Outdoor Recreation Policy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 30 days after the end of each fiscal year, beginning with fiscal year 2025, the Director of the Office of Management and Budget shall submit to Congress and make public online a report that describes and itemizes the total amount of funding relating to outdoor recreation that was obligated in the preceding fiscal year in accounts in the Treasury for the Department of the Interior and the Department of Agriculture.
