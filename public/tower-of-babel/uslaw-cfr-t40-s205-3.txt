40 C.F.R. § 205.3 — Number and gender.
Citation: us:cfr:40:205.3
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-205.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 205 (Transportation Equipment Noise Emission Controls). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As used in this part, words in the singular shall be deemed to import the plural, and words in the masculine gender shall be deemed to import the feminine and vice versa, as the case may require.
