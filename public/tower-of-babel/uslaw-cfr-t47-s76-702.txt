47 C.F.R. § 76.702 — Public access.
Citation: us:cfr:47:76.702
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-76.702
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 76 (Multichannel Video And Cable Television Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A cable operator may refuse to transmit any public access program or portion of a public access program that the operator reasonably believes contains obscenity.
