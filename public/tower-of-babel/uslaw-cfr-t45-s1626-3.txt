45 C.F.R. § 1626.3 — Prohibition.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1626.3
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1626.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1626 (Restrictions On Legal Assistance To Aliens). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Recipients may not provide legal assistance for or on behalf of an ineligible alien. For purposes of this part, legal assistance does not include normal intake and referral services.
