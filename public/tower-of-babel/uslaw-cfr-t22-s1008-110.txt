22 C.F.R. § 1008.110 — Are any of my Federal assistance awards exempt from this part?
Citation: us:cfr:22:1008.110
Creator: Inter-American Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-1008.110
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 1008 (Governmentwide Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Financial Assistance)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part does not apply to any award that the Inter-American Foundation President or designee determines that the application of this part would be inconsistent with the international obligations of the United States or the laws or regulations of a foreign government.
