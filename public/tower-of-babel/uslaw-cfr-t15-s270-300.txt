15 C.F.R. § 270.300 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:15:270.300
Creator: National Institute Of Standards And Technology, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-270.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 270 (National Construction Safety Teams). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

During the course of an investigation conducted pursuant to the Act, evidence will be collected, and information will be created by the Team, NIST, and other investigation participants. This subpart sets forth the policy and procedures for the collection, preservation, and protection of evidence obtained and information created pursuant to an investigation.
