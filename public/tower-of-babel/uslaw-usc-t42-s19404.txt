42 U.S.C. § 19404 — Applicability

Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 164 (Speak Out Act). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This chapter shall apply with respect to a claim that is filed under Federal, State, or Tribal law on or after December 7, 2022.
