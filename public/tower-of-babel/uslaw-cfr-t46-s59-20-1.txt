46 C.F.R. § 59.20-1 — -1 Carbon-steel or alloy-steel castings.
Citation: us:cfr:46:59.20-1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-59.20-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 59 (Repairs To Boilers, Pressure Vessels, And Appurtenances). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Defects in carbon-steel or alloy-steel castings may be repaired by welding. The repairs must be performed in accordance with the material specification to which the casting was originally supplied.
