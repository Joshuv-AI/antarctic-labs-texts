19 C.F.R. § 220.15 — Application of other Commission rules.
Citation: us:cfr:19:220.15
Creator: United States International Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-220.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 220 (Process For Consideration Of Petitions For Duty Suspensions And Reductions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Commission rules applicable to the initiation and conduct of investigations, including rules set out in subpart B of part 201 of this chapter (except § 201.9 (methods employed in obtaining information), § 201.14(a) (computation of time), and § 201.15 (attorneys or agents)), shall not apply to Commission proceedings under this part.
