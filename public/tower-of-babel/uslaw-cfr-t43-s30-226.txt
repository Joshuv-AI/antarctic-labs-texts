43 C.F.R. § 30.226 — Is a record made of formal probate hearings?
Citation: us:cfr:43:30.226
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-30.226
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 30 (Indian Probate Hearings Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The judge must make a verbatim recording of all formal probate hearings. The judge will order the transcription of recordings of hearings as the judge determines necessary.
(b) If the judge orders the transcription of a hearing, the judge will make the transcript available to interested parties on request.
