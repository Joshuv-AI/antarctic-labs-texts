46 C.F.R. § 131.820 — Branch lines of fire-extinguishing system.
Citation: us:cfr:46:131.820
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-131.820
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 131 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The valves of each branch line in the fire extinguishing system must be plainly and permanently marked, indicating the spaces served.
