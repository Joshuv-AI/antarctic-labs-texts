39 U.S.C. § 505 — Officer of the Postal Regulatory Commission representing the general public
Citation: us:usc:39:505
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title39-section505
Context: United States Code, Title 39 (Postal Service); Part I (General); Chapter 5 (Postal Regulatory Commission). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Postal Regulatory Commission shall designate an officer of the Postal Regulatory Commission in all public proceedings (such as developing rules, regulations, and procedures) who shall represent the interests of the general public.
