10 C.F.R. § 1040.89-2 — -2 Notice to subrecipients.
Citation: us:cfr:10:1040.89-2
Creator: Department Of Energy (General Provisions)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1040.89-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1040 (Nondiscrimination In Federally Assisted Programs Or Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where a recipient awards Federal financial assistance from DOE to its subrecipients, the recipient shall provide the subrecipients written notice of their obligations under these regulations.
