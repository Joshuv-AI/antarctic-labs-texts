42 U.S.C. § 6101 — Statement of purpose
Citation: us:usc:42:6101
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section6101
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 76 (Age Discrimination In Federally Assisted Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the purpose of this chapter to prohibit discrimination on the basis of age in programs or activities receiving Federal financial assistance.
