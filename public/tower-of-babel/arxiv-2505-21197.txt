Some identities which involve Stirling numbers
Stefano Frixione

Abstract
During the course of an ongoing work on the small- x x behaviour of parton distribution functions, some identities have been found which involve Stirling numbers of the first and the second kind, as well as binomial coefficients. Without any claim of originality I report them in this note.
