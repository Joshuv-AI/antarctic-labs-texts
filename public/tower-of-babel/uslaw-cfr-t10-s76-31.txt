10 C.F.R. § 76.31 — Periodic application requirement.
Citation: us:cfr:10:76.31
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-76.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 76 (Certification Of Gaseous Diffusion Plants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Corporation shall periodically apply to the Commission for a certificate of compliance, in accordance with § 76.36, on or before April 15 of the year specified in an existing certificate of compliance as determined by the Commission, but not less frequently than every 5 years.
