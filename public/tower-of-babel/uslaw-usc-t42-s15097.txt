42 U.S.C. § 15097 — Reporting
Citation: us:usc:42:15097
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section15097
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 144 (Developmental Disabilities Assistance And Bill Of Rights); Subchapter II (Family Support). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A State that receives a grant under this subchapter shall prepare and submit to the Secretary, at the end of the grant period, a report containing the results of State efforts to develop and implement, or expand and enhance, a statewide system of family support services for families of children with disabilities.
