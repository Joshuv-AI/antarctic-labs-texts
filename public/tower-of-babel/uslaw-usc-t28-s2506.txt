28 U.S.C. § 2506 — Interest of witness
Citation: us:usc:28:2506
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section2506
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part VI (Particular Proceedings); Chapter 165 (United States Court Of Federal Claims Procedure). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A witness in a suit in the United States Court of Federal Claims shall not be exempt or disqualified because he is a party to or interested in such suit.
