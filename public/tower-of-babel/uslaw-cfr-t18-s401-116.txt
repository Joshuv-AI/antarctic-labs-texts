18 C.F.R. § 401.116 — Disclosure to consultants, advisory committees, State and local government officials, and other special government employees.
Citation: us:cfr:18:401.116
Creator: Delaware River Basin Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-401.116
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 401 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Data and information otherwise exempt from public disclosure may be disclosed to Commission consultants, advisory committees, state and local government officials, and other special government employees for use only in their work in cooperation with the Commission. Such persons are thereafter subject to the same restrictions with respect to the disclosure of such data and information as any other Commission employee.
