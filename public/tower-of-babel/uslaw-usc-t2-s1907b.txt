2 U.S.C. § 1907b — Funds available for workers compensation payments
Citation: us:usc:2:1907b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section1907b
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 29 (Capitol Police); Subchapter I (Organization And Administration); Part A (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Available balances of expired United States Capitol Police appropriations shall be available to the Capitol Police to make the deposit to the credit of the Employees’ Compensation Fund required by section 8147(b) of title 5.
