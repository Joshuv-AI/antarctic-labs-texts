7 U.S.C. § 2716 — Regulations
Citation: us:usc:7:2716
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2716
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 60 (Egg Research And Consumer Information). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized to make regulations with force and effect of law, as may be necessary to carry out the provisions of this chapter and the powers vested in him by this chapter.
