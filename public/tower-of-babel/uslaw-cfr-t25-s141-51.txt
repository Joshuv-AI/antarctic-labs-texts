25 C.F.R. § 141.51 — Authority to close unlicensed reservation businesses.
Citation: us:cfr:25:141.51
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-141.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 141 (Business Practices On The Navajo, Hopi And Zuni Reservations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commissioner shall close any reservation business subject to the provisions of this part that does not hold a valid license or temporary permit.
