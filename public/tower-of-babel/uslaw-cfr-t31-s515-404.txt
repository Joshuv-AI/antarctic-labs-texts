31 C.F.R. § 515.404 — Transactions between principal and agent.
Citation: us:cfr:31:515.404
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-515.404
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 515 (Cuban Assets Control Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A transaction between any person within the United States and any principal, agent, home office, branch, or correspondent, outside the United States of such person is a transaction prohibited by § 515.201 to the same extent as if the parties to the transaction were in no way affiliated or associated with each other.
