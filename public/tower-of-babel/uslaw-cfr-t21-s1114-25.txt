21 C.F.R. § 1114.25 — Communication between FDA and applicants.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1114.25
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1114.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1114 (Premarket Tobacco Product Applications). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

During the course of reviewing an application, FDA may communicate with an applicant about relevant matters, including scientific, medical, and procedural issues that arise during the review process and inspections. These communications may take the form of telephone conversations, letters, electronic communications, or meetings, and will be documented in the administrative file in accordance with § 10.65 of this chapter.
