46 C.F.R. § 9.15 — Application form.
Citation: us:cfr:46:9.15
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-9.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 9 (Extra Compensation For Overtime Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An application on a form prescribed by the Commandant of the Coast Guard, shall be filed with the office being requested to furnish overtime services before such assignment can be made.
