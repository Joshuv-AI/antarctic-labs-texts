42 C.F.R. § 61.34 — Qualifications.
Citation: us:cfr:42:61.34
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-61.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 61 (Fellowships). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Scholastic and other qualifications shall be prescribed by the Secretary for each service fellowship, or series of service fellowships. Each individual appointed to a service fellowship shall:
(a) Have presented satisfactory evidence of general suitability, including professional and personal fitness; and
(b) Possess any other qualifications as reasonably may be prescribed.
