42 C.F.R. § 405.353 — Certification of amount that will be adjusted against individual title II or railroad retirement benefits.
Citation: us:cfr:42:405.353
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-405.353
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 405 (Federal Health Insurance For The Aged And Disabled). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As soon as practicable after any adjustment is determined to be necessary, the Secretary, for purposes of this subpart, shall certify the amount of the overpayment or payment (see § 405.350) with respect to which the adjustment is to be made. If the adjustment is to be made by decreasing subsequent payments under the Railroad Retirement Act of 1937, such certification shall be made to the Railroad Retirement Board.
