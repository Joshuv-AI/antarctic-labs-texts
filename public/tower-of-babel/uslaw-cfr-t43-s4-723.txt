43 C.F.R. § 4.723 — By what means may the presiding officer issue a notice, order, or decision?
Citation: us:cfr:43:4.723
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4.723
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4 (Department Of The Interior Hearings And Appeals Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Electronic. A presiding officer's notice, order, or decision may be issued electronically under the terms specified in the OHA Standing Order on WELSA Proceedings.
(b) Non-electronic. A presiding officer's notice, order, or decision may be issued non-electronically by U.S. mail or commercial courier to the Project Director or a party in interest using their record address as provided under § 4.22(b) or, if not provided, their last known mailing address.
