25 U.S.C. § 5503 — Submarginal lands of United States held in trust for Stockbridge Munsee Indian Community
Citation: us:usc:25:5503
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section5503
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 47 (Conveyance Of Submarginal Land). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All of the right, title, and interest of the United States in all the minerals including gas and oil underlying the submarginal lands declared to be held in trust for the Stockbridge Munsee Indian Community by the Act of October 9, 1972 (86 Stat. 795), are hereby declared to be held by the United States in trust for the Stockbridge Munsee Indian Community.
