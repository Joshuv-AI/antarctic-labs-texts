20 U.S.C. § 1087hh — General authority of Secretary
Citation: us:usc:20:1087hh
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1087hh
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 28 (Higher Education Resources And Student Assistance); Subchapter IV (Student Assistance); Part E (Federal Perkins Loans). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In carrying out the provisions of this part, the Secretary is authorized—
(1)
to consent to modification, with respect to rate of interest, time of payment of any installment of principal and interest or any portion thereof, or any other provision of any note evidencing a loan which has been made under this part;
(2)
to enforce, pay, compromise, waive, or release any right, title, claim, lien, or demand, however acquired, including any equity or any right of redemption;
(3)
to conduct litigation in accordance with the provisions of section 1082(a)(2) of this title; and
(4)
to enter into a contract or other arrangement with State or nonprofit agencies and, on a competitive basis, with collection agencies for servicing and collection of loans under this part.
