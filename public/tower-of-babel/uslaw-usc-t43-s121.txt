43 U.S.C. § 121 — Discontinuance of land offices by President
Citation: us:usc:43:121
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section121
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 5 (Land Districts). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the Interior, the President may order the discontinuance of any land office and the transfer of any of its business and archives to any other land office within the same State or Territory.
