12 C.F.R. § 721.6 — May a credit union derive income from activities approved under this part?
Citation: us:cfr:12:721.6
Creator: National Credit Union Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-721.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 721 (Incidental Powers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may earn income for those activities determined to be incidental to your business.
