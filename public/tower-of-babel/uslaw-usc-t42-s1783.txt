42 U.S.C. § 1783 — Appropriations for administrative expense
Citation: us:usc:42:1783
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1783
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 13A (Child Nutrition). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are hereby authorized to be appropriated for any fiscal year such sums as may be necessary to the Secretary for the Secretary’s administrative expense under this chapter.
