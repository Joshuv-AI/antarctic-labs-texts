14 U.S.C. § 550 — Marking pierheads in certain lakes
Citation: us:usc:14:550
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section550
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 5 (Functions And Powers); Subchapter III (Aids To Navigation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commandant of the Coast Guard shall properly mark all pierheads belonging to the United States situated on the northern and northwestern lakes, whenever he is duly notified by the department charged with the construction or repair of pierheads that the construction or repair of any such pierhead has been completed.
