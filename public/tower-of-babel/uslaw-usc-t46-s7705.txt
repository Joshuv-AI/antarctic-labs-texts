46 U.S.C. § 7705 — Subpenas and oaths
Citation: us:usc:46:7705
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section7705
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part E (Merchant Seamen Licenses, Certificates, And Documents); Chapter 77 (Suspension And Revocation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
An official designated to investigate or preside at a hearing on matters that are grounds for suspension or revocation of licenses, certificates of registry, and merchant mariners’ documents may administer oaths and issue subpenas to compel the attendance and testimony of witnesses and the production of records or other evidence during investigations and at hearings.
(b)
The jurisdictional limits of a subpena issued under this section are the same as, and are enforceable in the same manner as, subpenas issued under chapter 63 of this title.
