21 C.F.R. § 1316.32 — Notice of proceeding; time and place.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1316.32
Creator: Drug Enforcement Administration, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1316.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1316 (Administrative Functions, Practices, And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Appropriate notice designating the time and place for the hearing shall be given to the person. Upon request, timely and properly made, by the person to whom notice has been given, the time or place of the hearing, or both, may be changed if the request states reasonable grounds for such change. Such request shall be addressed to the Special Agent in Charge who issued the notice.
