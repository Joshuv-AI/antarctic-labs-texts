48 C.F.R. § 32.409 — 32.409 Contracting officer action.
Citation: us:cfr:48:32.409
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-32.409
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 32 (Contract Financing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After analysis of the contractor's application and any appropriate investigation, the contracting officer shall recommend approval or disapproval and transmit the request and recommendation to the approving authority designated under 32.402(e).
