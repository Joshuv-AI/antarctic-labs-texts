19 U.S.C. § 3012 — Reference to Harmonized Tariff Schedule
Citation: us:usc:19:3012
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section3012
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 18 (Implementation Of Harmonized Tariff Schedule). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any reference in any law to the “Tariff Schedules of the United States”, “the Tariff Schedules”, “such Schedules”, and any other general reference that clearly refers to the old Schedules shall be treated as a reference to the Harmonized Tariff Schedule.
