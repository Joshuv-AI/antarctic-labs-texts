18 C.F.R. § 1319.60 — Disposition of real property.
Citation: us:cfr:18:1319.60
Creator: Tennessee Valley Authority
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-1319.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 1319 (Floodplains And Wetlands). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When TVA property in a floodplain or wetland is proposed for lease, easement, right-of-way, or disposal to non-Federal public or private parties and the action will not result in disturbance of the floodplain or wetland, a floodplain or wetland evaluation is not required. However, the conveyance document must:
(a) Require the other party to comply with all applicable Federal, State or local floodplain and wetland regulations, and
(b) Identify other appropriate restrictions to minimize destruction, loss, or degradation of floodplains and wetlands and to preserve and enhance their natural and beneficial values, except when prohibited by law or unenforceable by TVA, or otherwise, the property must be withheld from conveyance or use.
