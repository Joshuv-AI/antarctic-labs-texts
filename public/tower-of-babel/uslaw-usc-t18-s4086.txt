18 U.S.C. § 4086 — Temporary safe-keeping of federal offenders by marshals
Citation: us:usc:18:4086
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section4086
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part III (Prisons And Prisoners); Chapter 305 (Commitment And Transfer). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

United States marshals shall provide for the safe-keeping of any person arrested, or held under authority of any enactment of Congress pending commitment to an institution.
