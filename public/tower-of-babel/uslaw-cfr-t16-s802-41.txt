16 C.F.R. § 802.41 — Corporations or unincorporated entities at time of formation.
Citation: us:cfr:16:802.41
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-802.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 802 (Exemption Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Whenever any person(s) contributing to the formation of an entity are subject to the requirements of the Act by reason of § 801.40 or § 801.50 of this chapter, the new entity need not file the notification required by the Act and § 803.1 of this chapter.
