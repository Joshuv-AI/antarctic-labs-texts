20 C.F.R. § 361.15 — Non-waiver of rights.
Citation: us:cfr:20:361.15
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-361.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 361 (Recovery Of Debts Owed To The United States Government By Government Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

So long as there are no statutory or contractual provisions to the contrary, no employee involuntary payment (of all or a portion of a debt) collected under these regulations will be interpreted as a waiver of any rights that the employee may have under 5 U.S.C. 5514.
