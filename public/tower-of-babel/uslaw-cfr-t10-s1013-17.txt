10 C.F.R. § 1013.17 — Rights of parties.
Citation: us:cfr:10:1013.17
Creator: Department Of Energy (General Provisions)
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1013 (Administrative False Claims And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise limited by this part, all parties may—
(a) Be accompanied, represented, and advised by a representative;
(b) Participate in any conference held by the ALJ;
(c) Conduct discovery;
(d) Agree to stipulations of fact or law, which shall be made part of the record;
(e) Present evidence relevant to the issues at the hearing;
(f) Present and cross-examine witnesses;
(g) Present oral arguments at the hearing as permitted by the ALJ; and
(h) Submit written briefs and proposed findings of fact and conclusions of law.
