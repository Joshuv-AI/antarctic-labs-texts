12 C.F.R. § 1291.61 — Recovery of subsidy for member noncompliance.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1291.61
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1291.61
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1291 (Federal Home Loan Banks' Affordable Housing Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Bank shall recover from a member the amount of any AHP subsidy (plus interest, if appropriate) not used in compliance with the commitments in the member's AHP application or the requirements of this part as a result of the actions or omissions of the member.
