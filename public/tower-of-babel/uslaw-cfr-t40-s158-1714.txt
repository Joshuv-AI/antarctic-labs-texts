40 C.F.R. § 158.1714 — Chiggers.
Citation: us:cfr:40:158.1714
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-158.1714
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 158 (Data Requirements For Pesticides). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the pesticide product labeling makes a claim against chiggers, then testing is required using the following test species: Chigger (Trombicula alfreddugesi).
