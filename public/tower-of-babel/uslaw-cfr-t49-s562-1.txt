49 C.F.R. § 562.1 — Scope and purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:49:562.1
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-562.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 562 (Lighting And Marking Of Agricultural Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part establishes minimum lighting and marking standards for new agricultural equipment as required by the Moving Ahead for Progress in the 21st Century Act (Sec. 31601, Pub. L. 112-141).
