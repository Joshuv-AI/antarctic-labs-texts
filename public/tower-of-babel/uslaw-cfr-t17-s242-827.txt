17 C.F.R. § 242.827 — Core Principle 10—Antitrust considerations.
Citation: us:cfr:17:242.827
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 242 (Regulations M, Sho, Ats, Ac, Nms, Se, And Sbsr, And Customer Margin Requirements For Security Futures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless necessary or appropriate to achieve the purposes of the Act, the security-based swap execution facility shall not:
(a) Adopt any rules or take any actions that result in any unreasonable restraint of trade; or
(b) Impose any material anticompetitive burden on trading or clearing.
