41 C.F.R. § 101-27.209-2 — -27.209-2 Items to be reported as excess.
Citation: us:cfr:41:101-27.209-2
Creator: Chapter 101—Federal Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-101-27.209-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 101-27 (27—Inventory Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Shelf-life items which do not meet the criteria in subpart 101-27.5 of this part, which would, if returned to GSA, adversely affect the GSA nationwide stock position, or which are returned to GSA and are determined unsuitable for issue, will be reported as excess under the provisions of part 101-43 of this chapter.
