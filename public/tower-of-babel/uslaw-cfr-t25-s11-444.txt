25 C.F.R. § 11.444 — Carrying concealed weapons.
Citation: us:cfr:25:11.444
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-11.444
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 11 (Courts Of Indian Offenses And Law And Order Code). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A person who goes about in public places armed with a dangerous weapon concealed upon his or her person is guilty of a misdemeanor unless he or she has a permit to do so signed by a magistrate of the Court of Indian Offenses.
