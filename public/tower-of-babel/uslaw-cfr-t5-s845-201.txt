5 C.F.R. § 845.201 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:5:845.201
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-845.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 845 (Federal Employees Retirement System—Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes procedures to be followed by the Office of Personnel Management (OPM), which are consistent with the Federal Claims Collection Standards (FCCS) (Chapter II of title 4, Code of Federal Regulations), in the collection of debts owed to the Fund.
