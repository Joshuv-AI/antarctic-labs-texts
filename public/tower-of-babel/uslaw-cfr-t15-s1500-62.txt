15 C.F.R. § 1500.62 — Patents, copyrights, trademarks, information, publications, and product formulations.
Citation: us:cfr:15:1500.62
Creator: Office Of The Under-Secretary For Economic Affairs, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-1500.62
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 1500 (Concrete Masonry Research, Education, And Promotion). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Ownership and allocation of rights to patents, copyrights, inventions, or publications, developed through the use of non-Federal funds remitted to the Board under the Order shall be determined by written agreement between the Board and the party(ies) receiving funds for the development of such inventions, patents, copyrights or publications.
