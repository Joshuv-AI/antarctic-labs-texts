26 U.S.C. § 753 — Partner receiving income in respect of decedent
Citation: us:usc:26:753
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section753
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 1 (Normal Taxes And Surtaxes); Subchapter K (Partners And Partnerships); Part II (Contributions, Distributions, And Transfers); Subpart D (Provisions Common To Other Subparts). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The amount includible in the gross income of a successor in interest of a deceased partner under section 736(a) shall be considered income in respect of a decedent under section 691.
