29 C.F.R. § 38.78 — Determinations regarding acceptance of complaints.
Citation: us:cfr:29:38.78
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-38.78
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 38 (Implementation Of The Nondiscrimination And Equal Opportunity Provisions Of The Workforce Innovation And Opportunity Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director must decide whether CRC will accept a particular complaint for resolution. For example, a complaint need not be accepted if:
(a) It has not been timely filed;
(b) CRC has no jurisdiction over the complaint; or
(c) CRC has previously decided the matter.
