Introduction to the Global Congress Abstracts

The Society for Maternal‐Fetal Medicine (SMFM) is dedicated to the advancement of optimal and equitable perinatal outcomes for all people who desire or experience pregnancy. Founded in 1977, SMFM is the medical professional society for obstetricians who have additional training in high‐risk, complicated pregnancies. SMFM represents more than 6500 members who care for high‐risk pregnant people and provides education, promotes research, and engages in advocacy to reduce disparities and optimize the health of high‐risk pregnant people and their families. SMFM and its members are dedicated to optimizing maternal and fetal outcomes and assuring medically appropriate treatment options are available to all patients.

Every other year, SMFM hosts the SMFM Global Congress, the Society's premier international gathering of leaders in obstetrics and maternal‐fetal medicine dedicated to advancing research, education, and clinical excellence in maternal and fetal care. The SMFM Global Congress showcases innovative approaches to pregnancy care, highlights groundbreaking scientific research, and fosters meaningful collaboration among experts from around the world. Together, these efforts support the shared goal of improving outcomes for pregnant patients and their families globally.

The abstracts published in this supplement reflect the breadth and impact of the scientific discoveries, clinical innovations, and collaborative work presented at the meeting. All abstracts submitted to the Congress undergo rigorous peer review by the SMFM Abstract Review Committee, with selections made based on scientific merit, relevance, and contribution to maternal‐fetal medicine and obstetric care. The research presented herein represents important advances in the field and contributes to the ongoing improvement of pregnancy care worldwide.
