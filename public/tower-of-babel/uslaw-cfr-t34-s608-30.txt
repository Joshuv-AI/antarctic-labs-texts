34 C.F.R. § 608.30 — What is the procedure for approving and disapproving grant applications?
Citation: us:cfr:34:608.30
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-608.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 608 (Strengthening Historically Black Colleges And Universities Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary—
(a) Approves any application that satisfies the requirements of § 608.10 and § 608.20; and
(b) Does not disapprove any application, or any modification of an application, without affording the applicant reasonable notice and opportunity for a hearing.
