15 C.F.R. § 2007.6 — Information open to public inspection.
Citation: us:cfr:15:2007.6
Creator: Office Of The United States Trade Representative
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-2007.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 2007 (Regulations Of The U.S. Trade Representative Pertaining To Eligibility Of Articles And Countries For The Generalized System Of Preference Program (Gsp (15 Cfr Part 2007))). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

With exception of information subject to § 2007.7 any person may, upon request inspect at the Office of the United States Trade Representative:
(a) Any written request, brief, or similar submission of information made pursuant to this part; and
(b) Any stenographic record of any public hearings which may be held pursuant to this part.
