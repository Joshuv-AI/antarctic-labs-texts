43 C.F.R. § 1882.0-5 — -5 Definitions.

Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-1882.0-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 1880 (Financial Assistance, Local Governments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As used in this subpart, the term:
(a) Secretary means the Secretary of the Interior.
(b) Director means the Director, Bureau of Land Management.
(c) Act means the Act of February 25, 1920, as amended (30 U.S.C. 181).
