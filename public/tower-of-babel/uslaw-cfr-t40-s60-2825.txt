40 C.F.R. § 60.2825 — What must I include in the notifications of achievement of increments of progress?
Citation: us:cfr:40:60.2825
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-60.2825
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 60 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Your notification of achievement of increments of progress must include the three items described in paragraphs (a) through (c) of this section:
(a) Notification that the increment of progress has been achieved;
(b) Any items required to be submitted with each increment of progress (see § 60.2840); and
(c) Signature of the owner or operator of the incinerator.
