39 C.F.R. § 956.5 — (Rule 5) Scope of hearing; evidentiary standard.
Citation: us:cfr:39:956.5
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-956.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 956 (Administrative Wage Garnishment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A hearing under this part will be conducted by the Hearing Official to determine:
(1) The existence of the debt; and
(2) The amount of the debt.
(b) The Postal Service must prove its case against a debtor by a preponderance of the evidence.
