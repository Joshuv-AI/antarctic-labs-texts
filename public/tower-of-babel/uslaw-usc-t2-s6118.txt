2 U.S.C. § 6118 — Long-distance telephone calls for Vice President
Citation: us:usc:2:6118
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section6118
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 61 (Senate Leadership); Subchapter II (Vice President And President Pro Tempore). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Commencing January 20, 1949, the provisions of existing law relating to long-distance telephone calls for Senators shall be equally applicable to the Vice President of the United States.
