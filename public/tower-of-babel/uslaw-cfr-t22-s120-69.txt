22 C.F.R. § 120.69 — Port Directors.
Citation: us:cfr:22:120.69
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-120.69
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 120 (Purpose And Definitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Port Directors means the U.S. Customs and Border Protection Port Directors at the U.S. Customs and Border Protection Ports of Entry (other than the port of New York, New York where their title is the Area Directors).
