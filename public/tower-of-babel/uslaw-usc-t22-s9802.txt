22 U.S.C. § 9802 — Statement of policy
Citation: us:usc:22:9802
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section9802
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 105 (Global Fragility). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the policy of the United States to seek to stabilize conflict-affected areas and prevent violence and fragility globally, including by—
(1)
ensuring that all relevant Federal departments and agencies coordinate to achieve coherent, long-term goals for programs designed to carry out such policy;
(2)
seeking to improve global, regional, and local coordination of relevant international and multilateral development and donor organizations regarding efforts to carry out such policy; and
(3)
enhancing the effectiveness of United States foreign assistance programs and activities to carry out such policy, including by improving assessment, monitoring, and evaluation conducted by the relevant Federal departments and agencies.
