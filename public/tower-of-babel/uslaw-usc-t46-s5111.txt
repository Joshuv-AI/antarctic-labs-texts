46 U.S.C. § 5111 — Providing loading information
Citation: us:usc:46:5111
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section5111
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part C (Load Lines Of Vessels); Chapter 51 (Load Lines). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may prescribe regulations requiring the owner, charterer, managing operator, and agent of a vessel to provide loading information (including information on loading distribution, stability, and margin of strength) to the master or individual in charge of the vessel in a language the master or individual understands.
