42 C.F.R. § 411.2 — Conclusive effect of QIO determinations on payment of claims.
Citation: us:cfr:42:411.2
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-411.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 411 (Exclusions From Medicare And Limitations On Medicare Payment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a utilization and quality control quality improvement organization (QIO) has assumed review responsibility, in accordance with part 466 of this chapter, for services furnished to Medicare beneficiaries, Medicare payment is not made for those services unless the conditions of subpart C of part 466 of this chapter are met.
