Explicit evaluation of the q q -Stokes matrices for certain confluent hypergeometric q q -difference equations
Jinghong Lin, Yiming Ma, Xiaomeng Xu

Abstract
We prove a connection formula for the basic hypergeomtric function φ n − 1 n ​ ( a 1 , … , a n − 1 , 0 , b 1 , … , b n − 1 , q , z ) {}_{n}\varphi_{n-1}\left(a_{1},...,a_{n-1},0;b_{1},...,b_{n-1};q,z\right) by using the q q -Borel resummation. As an application, we compute q q -Stokes matrices of a special confluent hypergeometric q q -difference system with an irregular singularity. We show that by letting q → 1 q\rightarrow 1 , the q q -Stokes matrices recover the known expressions of the Stokes matrices of the corresponding confluent hypergeometric differential system.

(a;q)_{n}:=\left\{\begin{array}[]{lr}1,&\text{if}\ \ n=0,\\

\theta_{q}(z):=\sum_{n\in\mathbb{Z}}q^{\frac{n(n-1)}{2}}z^{n},\hskip 10.00002ptz\in\mathbb{C}^{*}.

It satisfies

\theta_{q}(q^{n}z)=z^{-n}q^{-\frac{n(n-1)}{2}}\theta_{q}(z)=\theta_{q}\left(\frac{1}{q^{n-1}z}\right),\hskip 10.00002ptn\in\mathbb{Z},

\theta_{q}(z)=(q,-z,-q/z;q)_{\infty}.

(7)

•

The
