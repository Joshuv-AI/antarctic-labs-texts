40 C.F.R. § 430.110 — Applicability; description of the fine and lightweight papers from purchased pulp subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:430.110
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-430.110
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 430 (The Pulp, Paper, And Paperboard Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to discharges resulting from the production of: fine paper at nonintegrated mills; and lightweight paper at nonintegrated mills.
