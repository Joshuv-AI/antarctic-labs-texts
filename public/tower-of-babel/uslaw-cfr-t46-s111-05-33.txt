46 C.F.R. § 111.05-33 — -33 Equipment safety grounding (bonding) conductors.
Citation: us:cfr:46:111.05-33
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-111.05-33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 111 (Electric Systems—General Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each equipment-grounding conductor must be sized in accordance with Section 250.122 of NFPA 70 (incorporated by reference; see § 110.10-1 of this subchapter).
(b) Each equipment-grounding conductor (other than a system-grounding conductor) of a cable must be permanently identified as a grounding conductor in accordance with the requirements of Section 250.119 of NFPA 70.
