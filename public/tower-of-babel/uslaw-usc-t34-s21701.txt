34 U.S.C. § 21701 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:34:21701
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section21701
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 217 (Elder Abuse Prevention And Prosecution). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter—
(1)
the terms “abuse”, “adult protective services”, “elder”, “elder justice”, “exploitation”, “law enforcement”, and “neglect” have the meanings given those terms in section 1397j of title 42;
(2)
the term “elder abuse” includes abuse, neglect, and exploitation of an elder; and
(3)
the term “State” means each of the several States of the United States, the District of Columbia, the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico, and any other territory or possession of the United States.
