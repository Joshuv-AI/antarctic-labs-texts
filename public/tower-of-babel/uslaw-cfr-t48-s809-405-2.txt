48 C.F.R. § 809.405-2 — 809.405-2 Restrictions on subcontracting.
Citation: us:cfr:48:809.405-2
Creator: Chapter 8—Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-809.405-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 809 (Contractor Qualifications). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Authority to make the written determination required under FAR 9.405-2 consenting to a contractor's use of a subcontractor who is suspended, proposed for debarment, or debarred is delegated to the SDO. This authority is further delegated to the HCA, who may delegate this authority, in writing, to a designee.
