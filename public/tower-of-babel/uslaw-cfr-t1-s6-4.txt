1 C.F.R. § 6.4 — Monthly list of sections affected.
Citation: us:cfr:1:6.4
Creator: Administrative Committee Of The Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-1/section-6.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 1 (General Provisions), Part 6 (Indexes And Ancillaries). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-23 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A monthly list of sections of the Code of Federal Regulations affected shall be separately published on a cumulative basis during each calendar year. The list shall identify the sections of the Code specifically affected by documents published in the Federal Register during the period it covers.
