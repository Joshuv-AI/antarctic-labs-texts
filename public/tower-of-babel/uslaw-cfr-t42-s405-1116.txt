42 C.F.R. § 405.1116 — Effect of dismissal of request for Council review or request for hearing.
Citation: us:cfr:42:405.1116
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-405.1116
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 405 (Federal Health Insurance For The Aged And Disabled). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The dismissal of a request for Council review or denial of a request for review of a dismissal issued by an ALJ or attorney adjudicator is binding and not subject to further review unless reopened and vacated by the Council. The Council's dismissal of a request for hearing is also binding and not subject to judicial review.
