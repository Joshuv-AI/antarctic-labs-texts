46 C.F.R. § 9.17 — Protests.
Citation: us:cfr:46:9.17

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 9 (Extra Compensation For Overtime Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Protests against the exaction of extra compensation shall be forwarded to the Commandant of the Coast Guard.
