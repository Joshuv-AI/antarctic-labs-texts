40 C.F.R. § 432.60 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:40:432.60
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-432.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 432 (Meat And Poultry Products Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to discharges of process wastewater resulting from the production of fresh meat cuts, such as steaks, roasts, chops, etc. by a meat cutter.
