7 C.F.R. § 1421.14 — Obtaining peanut MALs.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1421.14
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1421.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1421 (Grains And Similarly Handled Commodities—Marketing Assistance Loans And Loan Deficiency Payments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Peanuts MALs to individual producers may be obtained through:
(1) County offices; or
(2) A designated Marketing Association or a CMA approved by CCC.
(b) The MAL documents will not be presented for disbursement unless the peanuts pledged as collateral for the MAL are eligible as specified in § 1421.8. If the peanuts were ineligible at the time of the disbursement, the total amount disbursed under MAL, or as an LDP, plus charges and interest will be refunded promptly.
