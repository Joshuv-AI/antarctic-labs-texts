30 C.F.R. § 1243.12 — May I substitute a demonstration of financial solvency for a bond posted before the effective date of this rule?
Citation: us:cfr:30:1243.12
Creator: Office Of Natural Resources Revenue, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-1243.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 1243 (Suspensions Pending Appeal And Bonding—Office Of Natural Resources Revenue). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you appealed an order before June 14, 1999 and you submitted an ONRR-specified surety instrument to suspend compliance with that order, you may replace the surety with a demonstration of financial solvency under this part at an administratively convenient time, such as when the surety instrument is due for renewal.
