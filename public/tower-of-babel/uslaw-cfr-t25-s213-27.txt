25 C.F.R. § 213.27 — Rate of rental for nonutilized gas wells.
Citation: us:cfr:25:213.27
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-213.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 213 (Leasing Of Restricted Lands Of Members Of Five Civilized Tribes, Oklahoma, For Mining). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the gas from a gas producing well is not marketed or utilized, other than for operation of the lease, then for each such well the lessee shall pay such rental as may be determined by the supervisor and approved by the Secretary of the Interior, calculated from the date of the completion of the well. Payment of annual gas rentals shall be made within 30 days from the date such payment becomes due.
