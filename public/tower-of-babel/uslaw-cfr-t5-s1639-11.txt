5 C.F.R. § 1639.11 — Cross-servicing agreement with the Department of the Treasury.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1639.11
Creator: Federal Retirement Thrift Investment Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1639.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1639 (Claims Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board will enter into a cross-servicing agreement with the Department of the Treasury which will authorize Treasury to take all of the debt collection actions described in this part. These debt collection services will be provided to the Board in accordance with 31 U.S.C. 3701 et seq.
