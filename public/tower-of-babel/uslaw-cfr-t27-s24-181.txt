27 C.F.R. § 24.181 — Use of sugar.
Citation: us:cfr:27:24.181
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-24.181
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 24 (Wine). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Only sugar, as defined in § 24.10, may be used in the production of standard wine. The quantity of sugar used will be determined either by measuring the increase in volume or by considering that each 13.5 pounds of pure dry sugar results in a volumetric increase of one gallon.
