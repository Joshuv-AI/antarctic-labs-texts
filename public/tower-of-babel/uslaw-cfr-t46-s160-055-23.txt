46 C.F.R. § 160.055-23 — -23 Procedure for approval of design or material change.
Citation: us:cfr:46:160.055-23
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-160.055-23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 160 (Lifesaving Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The manufacturer must submit any proposed changes in design, material, or construction to typeapproval@uscg.mil for approval before changing life preserver production methods.
(b) Only the Commandant or a designated representative may make determinations of equivalence of design, construction, and materials.
