27 C.F.R. § 45.45a — a Notice for pipe tobacco.
Citation: us:cfr:27:45.45a
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-45.45a
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 45 (Removal Of Tobacco Products And Cigarette Papers And Tubes, Without Payment Of Tax, For Use Of The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Product designation. Every package of pipe tobacco shall, before removal subject to tax, have adequately imprinted thereon, or on a label securely affixed thereto, the designation “pipe tobacco.”
(b) Product weight. Every package of pipe tobacco shall, before removal subject to tax, have adequately imprinted thereon, or on a label securely affixed thereto, a clear statement of the actual pounds and ounces of the product contained therein.
