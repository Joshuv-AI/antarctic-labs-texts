2 U.S.C. § 2011 — Assignment of unoccupied space
Citation: us:usc:2:2011
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section2011
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 30 (Operation And Maintenance Of Capitol Complex); Subchapter I (House Of Representatives). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Unoccupied space in said building shall be assigned by the Architect of the Capitol under the direction of the commission and subject to the control of the House of Representatives.
