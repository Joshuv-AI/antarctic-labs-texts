49 C.F.R. § 512.9 — What are the requirements if the information comes from a third party?
Citation: us:cfr:49:512.9
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-512.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 512 (Confidential Business Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where confidentiality is claimed for information obtained by the submitter from a third party, such as a supplier, the submitter is responsible for obtaining from the third party the information that is necessary to comply with § 512.4 of this part, including a certificate in the form set out in appendix A to this Part.
