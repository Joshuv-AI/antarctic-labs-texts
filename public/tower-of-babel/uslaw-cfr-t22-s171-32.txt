22 C.F.R. § 171.32 — Denial of Public Access to Confidential Financial Disclosure Reports—OGE Form 450.
Citation: us:cfr:22:171.32
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-171.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 171 (Public Access To Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No member of the public shall have access to confidential financial disclosure reports filed pursuant to 5 CFR 2634, subpart I, except pursuant to the order of a Federal court or as otherwise provided under the Privacy Act. See 5 U.S.C. 552a.
