31 C.F.R. § 29.403 — Applications filed with the Benefits Administrator.
Citation: us:cfr:31:29.403
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-29.403
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 29 (Federal Benefit Payments Under Certain District Of Columbia Retirement Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All claimants for Federal Benefit Payments must file applications for benefits (including applications for retirement, refunds of contributions, and death benefits) with the Benefits Administrator.
