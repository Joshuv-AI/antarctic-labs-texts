45 C.F.R. § 162.1901 — Medicaid pharmacy subrogation transaction.
Citation: us:cfr:45:162.1901
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-162.1901
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 162 (Administrative Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Medicaid pharmacy subrogation transaction is the transmission of a claim from a Medicaid agency to a payer for the purpose of seeking reimbursement from the responsible health plan for a pharmacy claim the State has paid on behalf of a Medicaid recipient.
