47 C.F.R. § 80.711 — Use of U.S. Government frequencies.
Citation: us:cfr:47:80.711
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-80.711
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 80 (Stations In The Maritime Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Alaska-public fixed stations may be authorized to use frequencies assigned to U.S. Government radio stations for communications with Government stations or for coordination of Government activities.
