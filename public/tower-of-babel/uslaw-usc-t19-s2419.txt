19 U.S.C. § 2419 — Administration
Citation: us:usc:19:2419
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section2419
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 12 (Trade Act Of 1974); Subchapter III (Enforcement Of United States Rights Under Trade Agreements And Response To Certain Foreign Trade Practices). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Trade Representative shall—
(1)
issue regulations concerning the filing of petitions and the conduct of investigations and hearings under this subchapter,
(2)
keep the petitioner regularly informed of all determinations and developments regarding the investigation conducted with respect to the petition under this subchapter, including the reasons for any undue delays, and
(3)
submit a report to the House of Representatives and the Senate semiannually describing—
(A)
the petitions filed and the determinations made (and reasons therefor) under section 2412 of this title,
(B)
developments in, and the current status of, each investigation or proceeding under this subchapter,
(C)
the actions taken, or the reasons for no action, by the Trade Representative under section 2411 of this title with respect to investigations conducted under this subchapter, and
(D)
the commercial effects of actions taken under section 2411 of this title.
