16 C.F.R. § 604.1 — Severability.
Citation: us:cfr:16:604.1
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-604.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 604 (Fair Credit Reporting Act Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All parts and subparts of this subchapter are separate and severable from one another. If any part or subpart is stayed or determined to be invalid, the Commission intends that the remaining parts and subparts shall continue in effect.
