48 C.F.R. § 1.400 — 1.400 Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1.400
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1.400
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes the policies and procedures for authorizing deviations from the FAR. Exceptions pertaining to the use of forms prescribed by the FAR are covered in part 53 rather than in this subpart.
