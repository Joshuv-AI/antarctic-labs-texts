5 C.F.R. § 351.401 — Determining retention standing.
Citation: us:cfr:5:351.401
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-351.401
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 351 (Reduction In Force). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each agency shall determine the retention standing of each competing employee on the basis of the factors in this subpart and in subpart E of this part.
