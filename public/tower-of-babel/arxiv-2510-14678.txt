Transverse momentum dependent gluon density in a proton at low x x in the Laplace transform method Preprint: APS/123-QED
G.R. Boroun, Phuoc Ha, A.V. Kotikov, A.V. Lipatov

Abstract
We investigate the gluon distribution in a proton at very low x x , both integrated and transverse momentum dependent, using the Laplace transform technique. By accounting for leading and main next-to-leading contributions, we derive compact analytical expressions for the gluon densities valid in the asymptotic limit x → 0 x\to 0 . Our results closely match those from other analytical and numerical approaches, with the main advantage being the simplicity of the expressions, which capture the essential features of more complex calculations.

Here we present the analytic results for the Sudakov form factor given by (

).
After some algebra, we obtain the the following expression for

\ln T_{a}({\mathbf{k}}_{T}^{2},\mu^{2})
with angular ordering condition,
\Delta=k/(k+\mu)
:

\displaystyle\ln T_{a}({\mathbf{k}}_{T}^{2},\mu^{2})=-\frac{4C_{a}}{\beta_{0}}\left[s_{1}R_{a}(x_{0})+I_{a}(x_{0})+\sum_{i=1}^{3}\delta_{a}^{(i)}(x_{0})\right],

(23)

where
a=g
or
q
,
C_{g}=C_{A}
,
C_{q}=C_{F}
and

\displaystyle s_{1}=\ln\left(\frac{a_{s}({\mathbf{k}}_{T}^{2})}{a_{s}(\mu^{2})}\right),\quad x_{0}=1-\Delta,

\displaystyle R_{q}(x_{0})=\ln\frac{1}{\Delta}-\frac{3}{4}x_{0}^{2},\quad R_{g}(x_{0})=\ln\frac{1}{\Delta}-\left(1-\frac{T_{f}}{2C_{A}}\right)x_{0}^{2}+\frac{1}{12}\left(1-\frac{2T_{f}}{C_{A}}\right)x_{0}^{3}(4-3x_{0}),

\displaystyle I_{a}(x_{0})=-{1\over 2}\left[\ln\frac{\mu^{2}}{{\mathbf{k}}_{T}^{2}}+\ln\left(\frac{{\mathbf{k}}_{T}^{2}}{\Lambda_{\rm QCD}^{2}}\right)\ln\left({\ln{\mathbf{k}}_{T}^{2}/\Lambda_{\rm QCD}^{2}\over\ln\mu^{2}/\Lambda_{\rm QCD}^{2}}\right)\right],\quad\delta^{(1)}_{a}(x_{0})=-2\beta_{0}a_{s}(\mu^{2})j_{0}(x_{0}),

\displaystyle\delta^{(2)}_{a}(x_{0})=-4\beta^{2}_{0}a_{s}^{2}(\mu^{2})j^{(2)}_{0}(x_{0}),\quad\delta^{(3)}_{q}(x_{0})=-3\beta_{0}a_{s}(\mu^{2})j_{1}(x_{0}),

\displaystyle\delta^{(3)}_{g}(x_{0})=2\beta_{0}a_{s}(\mu^{2})\left[\left(1-\frac{2T_{f}}{C_{A}}\right)\left[j_{2}(x_{0})-j_{3}(x_{0})\right]-\left(2-\frac{T_{f}}{C_{A}}\right)j_{1}(x_{0})\right],

(24)

with

\displaystyle j_{0}(x_{0})=\ln x_{1}\ln x_{0}-\frac{\zeta_{2}}{2}-{\rm Li}_{2}(-x_{1}),\quad x_{1}=\frac{1-x_{0}}{x_{0}},

\displaystyle j_{0}^{(2)}(x_{0})=\ln^{2}x_{1}\ln x_{0}+\frac{3\zeta_{3}}{2}+2{\rm Li}_{3}(-x_{1})-2\ln x_{1}{\rm Li}_{2}(-x_{1}),

\displaystyle j_{1}(x_{0})=\frac{1}{2}\left[x^{2}_{0}\ln x_{1}-\ln(2\Delta)+\frac{1}{2}-x_{0}\right],

\displaystyle j_{2}(x_{0})=\frac{1}{3}\left[x^{3}_{0}\ln x_{1}-\ln(2\Delta)+\frac{5}{8}-x_{0}-\frac{x_{0}^{2}}{2}\right],

\displaystyle j_{3}(x_{0})=\frac{1}{4}\left[x^{4}_{0}\ln x_{1}-\ln(2\Delta)+\frac{2}{3}-x_{0}-\frac{x_{0}^{2}}{2}-\frac{x_{0}^{3}}{3}\right].

(25)

Here
\zeta_{k}
are the Euler
\zeta_{k}
-values and

Devoto

\displaystyle{\rm Li}_{2}(x)=-\int^{1}_{0}\,\frac{dz}{z}\,\ln(1-xz),\quad{\rm Li}_{3}(x)=\int^{1}_{0}\,\frac{dz}{z}\,\ln z\ln(1-xz).

(26)
