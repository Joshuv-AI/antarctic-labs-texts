31 C.F.R. § 597.404 — Setoffs prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:31:597.404
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-597.404
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 597 (Foreign Terrorist Organizations Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A setoff against blocked funds (including a blocked account) by a U.S. financial institution is a prohibited transaction under § 597.201 if effected after the effective date.
