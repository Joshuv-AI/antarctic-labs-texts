40 U.S.C. § 3104 — Furniture for new buildings
Citation: us:usc:40:3104
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section3104
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Part A (General); Chapter 31 (General); Subchapter I (Oversight And Regulation Of Public Buildings). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Furniture for all new public buildings shall be acquired in accordance with plans and specifications approved by the Administrator of General Services.
