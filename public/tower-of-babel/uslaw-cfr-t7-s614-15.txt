7 C.F.R. § 614.15 — Implementation of final NAD determinations.
Citation: us:cfr:7:614.15
Creator: Natural Resources Conservation Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-614.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 614 (Nrcs Appeal Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) No later than 30 days after a NAD determination becomes a final administrative decision of USDA, NRCS will implement the determination.
(b) Biannually, NRCS must file a report on the status of implementation of final administrative determinations in accordance with section 14009 of the 2008 Farm Bill.
