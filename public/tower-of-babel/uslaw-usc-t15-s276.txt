15 U.S.C. § 276 — Ownership of facilities
Citation: us:usc:15:276
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section276
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 7 (National Institute Of Standards And Technology). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In the absence of specific agreement to the contrary, additional facilities, including equipment, purchased pursuant to the performance of services authorized by section 273 of this title shall become the property of the Department of Commerce.
