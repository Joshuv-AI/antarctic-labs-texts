43 C.F.R. § 2920.9-2 — -2 Operation and maintenance.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2920.9-2
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2920.9-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2920 (Leases, Permits And Easements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The authorized officer shall inspect and monitor the operation and maintenance of the land use authorization area, its facilities and improvements to assure compliance with the plan of management and protection of the resources, the environment and the public health, safety and welfare, and the holder of the land use authorization shall take corrective action as required by the authorized officer.
