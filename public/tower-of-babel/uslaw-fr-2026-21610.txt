notice allows for 60 days for public comments.

    Abstract: This Information Collection Request (ICR) renewal 
supports activities to implement the National Oil and Hazardous 
Substances Pollution Contingency Plan (NCP), Subpart J (40 CFR 
300.900), Use of Dispersants and Other Chemicals and Biological Agents. 
The use of bioremediation agents, dispersants, surface washing agents, 
solidifiers, herding agents and other substances (e.g. sorbents) in 
response to oil spills in U.S. waters or adjoining shorelines is 
governed by Subpart J of the NCP regulation (40 CFR 300.900). Subpart J 
requirements include criteria for listing oil spill mitigating agents 
on the NCP Product Schedule and sorbent products on the Sorbent Product 
List. EPA's regulation, which is codified at 40 CFR 300.900, requires 
that EPA prepare a schedule of ``dispersants,

[[Page 66700]]

other chemicals, and other spill mitigating devices and substances, if 
any, that may be used in carrying out the NCP.'' Requirements under 
Subpart J of the NCP are required by section 311(d)(2)(G) of the Clean 
Water Act (CWA), as amended by the Oil Pollution Act of 1990. The NCP 
Product Schedule and Sorbent Product List are used by Federal On-Scene 
Coordinators (FOSCs), Regional Response Teams (RRTs), and Area Planners 
to identify spill mitigating agents in preparation for and response to 
oil spills. Under Subpart J, manufacturers who want to add a product to 
the NCP Product Schedule or Sorbent Product List must submit technical 
product data including applicable toxicity and efficacy testing data to 
the EPA as stipulated in 40 CFR 300.915. Subpart J requires the 
manufacturer to submit the corresponding technical product data along 
with other detailed information to the EPA Office of Resource 
Conservation and Recovery, Office of Land and Emergency Management. The 
NCP Product Schedule and Sorbent Product List are available to Federal 
On-Scene Coordinators (FOSCs), Regional Response Teams (RRTs), and Area 
Committees for helping them select the most appropriate products to use 
in various spill scenarios.
    In June 2023 EPA promulgated a final rule (88 FR 38280, June 12, 
2023). This rule revised Subpart J of the NCP by adding new testing and 
listing criteria for products to be listed on the NCP Product Schedule 
and establishing the Sorbent Product List. Effective June 10, 2026, 
only products that are submitted and listed based on the amended 
testing and listing criteria will be listed on the NCP Product 
Schedule. All products identified as sorbents prior to the June 2023 
rule remained available for use until December 12, 2025, at which time 
all sorbent products must have submitted information as applicable 
under the amended testing and listing requirements in June 2023 rule 
and be listed in the new Sorbent Product List. As of September 2026, 
the NCP Product Schedule included seven manufacturers and seven 
products (four dispersants and three surface washing agents). There are 
currently 12 sorbents listed on the Sorbent Product List. EPA estimates 
that for each year of this ICR renewal period five manufacturers will 
apply to list a product on the NCP Product Schedule and 10 manufactures 
will apply to list a sorbent on the Sorbent Product List. Therefore, 
over the three-year period covered by this ICR renewal, EPA estimates 
an additional 15 products may be listed on the NCP Product Schedule, 
and an additional 30 sorbents may be listed on the Sorbent Product 
List.
    At 40 CFR 300.950, respondents may claim certain information in the 
technical product data submissions as proprietary business information 
(PBI). EPA will handle such claims pursuant to the provisions in 40 CFR 
part 2, subpart B. Such information must be submitted separately from 
non-PBI information, clearly identified, and clearly marked 
``Proprietary Business Information.'' If the applicant fails to make 
such a claim at the time of submittal, EPA may make the information 
available to the public without further notice.

    Respondents/affected entities: Respondents include, but are not 
limited to, manufacturers of bioremediation agents, dispersants, 
herding agents, surface washing agents, solidifiers and sorbents.
    Respondent's obligation to respond: Required to obtain or retain a 
benefit (40 CFR 300.900).
    Estimated number of respondents: 15 (total).
    Frequency of response: Each manufacturer responds once per product 
submittal.
    Total estimated burden: 318 hours (per year). Burden is defined at 
5 CFR 1320.03(b).
    Total estimated cost: $391,400 (per year), which includes $103,867 
in operation & maintenance costs.
    Changes in the estimates: There is an increase of 8 hours in the 
total estimated respondent burden compared with the ICR currently 
approved by OMB. This is due to changes in testing and listing 
requirements as a result of the June 2023 rule.
