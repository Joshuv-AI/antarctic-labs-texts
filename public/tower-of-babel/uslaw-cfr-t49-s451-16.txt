49 C.F.R. § 451.16 — Action by approval authority-individual approval.
Citation: us:cfr:49:451.16
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-451.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 451 (Testing And Approval Of Containers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The approval authority arranges with the manufacturer or owner to witness testing in accordance with Annex II to the convention. Upon witnessing successful completion of the tests, the approval authority issues to the owner a notice of approval that authorizes the attachment of a safety approval plate.
