21 C.F.R. § 348.10 — Analgesic, anesthetic, and antipruritic active ingredients.
Citation: us:cfr:21:348.10
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-348.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 348 (External Analgesic Drug Products For Over-The-Counter Human Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The active ingredient of the product consists of any of the following within the specified concentration established for each ingredient:
(a) Male genital desensitizers. (1) Benzocaine, 3 to 7.5 percent in a water-soluble base.
(2) Lidocaine in a metered spray with approximately 10 milligrams per spray.
(b) [Reserved]
