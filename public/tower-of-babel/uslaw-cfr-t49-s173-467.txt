49 C.F.R. § 173.467 — Tests for demonstrating the ability of Type B and fissile materials packagings to withstand accident conditions in transportation.
Citation: us:cfr:49:173.467
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-173.467
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 173 (Shippers—General Requirements For Shipments And Packagings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each Type B packaging or packaging for fissile material must meet the test requirements prescribed in 10 CFR part 71 for ability to withstand accident conditions in transportation.
