42 U.S.C. § 14307 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:42:14307
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section14307
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 137 (Management Of Rechargeable Batteries And Batteries Containing Mercury); Subchapter I (Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated such sums as are necessary to carry out this chapter.
