47 C.F.R. § 25.301 — Satellite Emergency Notification Devices (SENDs).
Citation: us:cfr:47:25.301
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-25.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 25 (Satellite Communications). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No device described by the marketer or seller using the terms “SEND” or “Satellite Emergency Notification Device” may be marketed or sold in the United States unless it complies with the requirements of RTCM 12800.0 (incorporated by reference, see § 25.108).
