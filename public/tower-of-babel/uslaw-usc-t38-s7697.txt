38 U.S.C. § 7697 — Relationship to Educational Assistance Program
Citation: us:usc:38:7697
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section7697
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part V (Boards, Administrations, And Services); Chapter 76 (Health Professionals Educational Assistance Program); Subchapter VIII (Specialty Education Loan Repayment Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Assistance under the Specialty Education Loan Repayment Program may be in addition to other assistance available to individuals under the Educational Assistance Program.
