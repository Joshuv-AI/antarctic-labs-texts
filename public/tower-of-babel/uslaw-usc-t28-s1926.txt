28 U.S.C. § 1926 — Court of Federal Claims

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section1926
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part V (Procedure); Chapter 123 (Fees And Costs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Judicial Conference of the United States shall prescribe from time to time the fees and costs to be charged and collected in the United States Court of Federal Claims.
(b)
The court and its officers shall collect only such fees and costs as the Judicial Conference prescribes. The court may require advance payment of fees by rule.
