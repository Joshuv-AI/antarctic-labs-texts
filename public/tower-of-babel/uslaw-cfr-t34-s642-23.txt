34 C.F.R. § 642.23 — How does the Secretary ensure geographic distribution of awards?
Citation: us:cfr:34:642.23
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-642.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 642 (Training Program For Federal Trio Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary, to the greatest extent possible, awards grants for Training Program projects that will be carried out in all of the regions of the Nation in order to assure accessibility to prospective training participants.
