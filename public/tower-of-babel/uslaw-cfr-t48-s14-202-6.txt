48 C.F.R. § 14.202-6 — 14.202-6 Final review of invitations for bids.
Citation: us:cfr:48:14.202-6
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-14.202-6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 14 (Sealed Bidding). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each invitation for bids shall be thoroughly reviewed before issuance to detect and correct discrepancies or ambiguities that could limit competition or result in the receipt of nonresponsive bids. Contracting officers are responsible for the reviews.
