38 C.F.R. § 12.13 — Posting of notice of the provisions of Pub. L. No. 734, 75th Congress (38 U.S.C. 16-16j).
Citation: us:cfr:38:12.13
Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-12.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 12 (Disposition Of Veteran'S Personal Funds And Effects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In order that all persons who bring property on premises of the Department of Veterans Affairs may be advised of the existence of the act of June 25, 1938 (38 U.S.C. 16-16j), and that it affects such property, notice thereof (Form 4-1182), shall be permanently posted in at least one prominent place on the premises of each field facility where persons are likely to see such notice.
