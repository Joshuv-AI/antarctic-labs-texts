10 U.S.C. § 7536 — Equipment: post bakeries, schools, kitchens, and mess halls
Citation: us:usc:10:7536
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7536
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Procurement); Chapter 763 (Procurement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Money necessary for the following items for the use of enlisted members of the Army may be spent from appropriations for regular supplies:
(1)
Equipment for post bakeries.
(2)
Furniture, textbooks, paper, and equipment for post schools.
(3)
Tableware and mess furniture for kitchens and mess halls.
