24 C.F.R. § 180.655 — Witnesses.
Citation: us:cfr:24:180.655
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Equal Opportunity, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-180.655
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 180 (Consolidated Hud Hearing Procedures For Civil Rights Matters). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Witnesses shall testify under oath or affirmation.
(b) If a witness fails or refuses to testify, the failure or refusal to answer any question found by the ALJ to be proper may be grounds for striking all or part of the testimony that may have been given by the witness, or for any other action deemed appropriate by the ALJ.
