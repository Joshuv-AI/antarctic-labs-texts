7 C.F.R. § 917.15 — Representation area.
Citation: us:cfr:7:917.15
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-917.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 917 (Fresh Pears And Peaches Grown In California). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Representation area means any one of the districts or groups of districts which are designated for nominating members and alternate members to the commodity committees under § 917.21 or as changed pursuant to § 917.35(g).
