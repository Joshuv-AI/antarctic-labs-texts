5 C.F.R. § 532.101 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:5:532.101
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-532.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 532 (Prevailing Rate Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part provides common policies, systems, and practices for uniform application by all agencies subject to section 5342 of title 5, United States Code, in fixing pay for prevailing rate employees as nearly as is consistent with the public interest in accordance with prevailing rates.
