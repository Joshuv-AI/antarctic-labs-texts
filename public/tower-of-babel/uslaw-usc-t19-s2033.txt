19 U.S.C. § 2033 — Applicability of antidumping provisions and antitrust laws
Citation: us:usc:19:2033
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section2033
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 8 (Automotive Products); Subchapter IV (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing contained in this chapter shall be construed to affect or modify the provisions of subtitle B of title VII of the Tariff Act of 1930 [19 U.S.C. 1673 et seq.] or of any of the antitrust laws as designated in section 12 of title 15.
