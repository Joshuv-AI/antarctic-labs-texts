18 C.F.R. § 725.8 — Report, plan and recommendation development and review.
Citation: us:cfr:18:725.8
Creator: Water Resources Council
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-725.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 725 (Implementation Of Executive Orders 11988, Floodplain Management And 11990, Protection Of Wetlands). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All reports, plans and recommendations received under section 104 of Pub. L. 89-80 shall be reviewed by the Council for reflection of and opportunities to meet the objectives of E.O. 11988 and 11990. This review shall be based on the criteria in § 725.7(a)(1) through (5), on E.O. 11988 and 11990, and on the Council's Floodplain Management Guidelines (43 FR 6030).
