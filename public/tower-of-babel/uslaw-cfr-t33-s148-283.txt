33 C.F.R. § 148.283 — When is the application process stopped before the application is approved or denied?
Citation: us:cfr:33:148.283
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-148.283
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 148 (Deepwater Ports: General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commandant (CG-5P) recommends to MARAD that the application process be suspended before the application is approved or denied if:
(a) All applications are withdrawn before MARAD approves one of them; or
(b) There is only one application; it is incomplete, and the applicant does not respond to a request by the Commandant (CG-5P) for further information, as per § 148.107 of this part.
