30 C.F.R. § 44.20 — Designation of administrative law judge.
Citation: us:cfr:30:44.20
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-44.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 44 (Rules Of Practice For Petitions For Modification Of Mandatory Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Within 5 days after receipt of a referral of a request for hearing in a petition for modification proceeding, the Chief Administrative Law Judge shall designate an administrative law judge appointed under section 3105 of Title 5 of the United States Code to preside over the hearing.
