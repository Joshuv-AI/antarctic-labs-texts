16 C.F.R. § 1203.5 — Construction requirements—projections.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1203.5
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1203.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1203 (Safety Standard For Bicycle Helmets). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any unfaired projection extending more than 7 mm (0.28 in.) from the helmet's outer surface shall break away or collapse when impacted with forces equivalent to those produced by the applicable impact-attenuation tests in § 1203.17 of this standard. There shall be no fixture on the helmet's inner surface projecting more than 2 mm into the helmet interior.
