42 C.F.R. § 412.352 — Budget neutrality adjustment.
Citation: us:cfr:42:412.352
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-412.352
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 412 (Prospective Payment Systems For Inpatient Hospital Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For FY 1992 through FY 1995, CMS will determine an adjustment to the hospital-specific rate and the Federal rate proportionately so that the estimated aggregate payments under this subpart for inpatient hospital capital costs each fiscal year will equal 90 percent of what CMS estimates would have been paid for capital-related costs on a reasonable cost basis under § 413.130 of this chapter.
