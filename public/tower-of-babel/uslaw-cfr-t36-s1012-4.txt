36 C.F.R. § 1012.4 — If I serve a subpoena duces tecum, must I also submit a Touhy request?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1012.4
Creator: Presidio Trust
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1012.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1012 (Legal Process: Testimony By Employees And Production Of Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. If you serve a subpoena for employee testimony or if you serve a subpoena duces tecum for records in the possession of the Presidio Trust, you also must submit a Touhy Request.
