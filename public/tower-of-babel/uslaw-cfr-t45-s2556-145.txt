45 C.F.R. § 2556.145 — May AmeriCorps pursue other remedies against a VISTA project for a sponsor's material failure to comply with any other requirement not set forth in this subpart?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2556.145
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2556.145
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2556 (Volunteers In Service To America). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The procedures established by this subpart do not preclude AmeriCorps from pursuing any other remedies authorized by law.
