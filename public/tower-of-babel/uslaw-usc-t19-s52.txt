19 U.S.C. § 52 — Payment of compensation and expenses
Citation: us:usc:19:52
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section52
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 1 (Collection Districts, Ports, And Officers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The compensation of all customs officers and employees provided for by sections 6, 7, 8, 13, and 51 of this title, and the expenses authorized by section 48 of this title, shall be paid from the appropriation for the collection of the revenue from customs.
