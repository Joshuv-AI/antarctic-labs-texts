47 C.F.R. § 69.309 — Other investment.
Citation: us:cfr:47:69.309
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-69.309
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 69 (Access Charges). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Investment that is not apportioned pursuant to §§ 69.302 through 69.307 shall be apportioned among the interexchange category, the billing and collection category and access elements in the same proportions as the combined investment that is apportioned pursuant to §§ 69.303 through 69.307.
