30 C.F.R. § 1227.805 — How may a State obtain a new delegation agreement after termination?
Citation: us:cfr:30:1227.805
Creator: Office Of Natural Resources Revenue, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-1227.805
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 1227 (Delegation To States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After your delegation agreement is terminated, you may apply again for delegation by beginning with the proposal process under this part.
