43 C.F.R. § 49.135 — Can a permit-related decision be appealed?
Citation: us:cfr:43:49.135
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-49.135
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 49 (Paleontological Resources Preservation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. Permit applicants and permittees may appeal the denial of a permit application, and the modification, suspension, revocation, or cancellation of an issued permit.
