SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

[Disaster Declaration #21840 and #21841; KANSAS Disaster Number KS-
20043]


Presidential Declaration Amendment of a Major Disaster for Public 
Assistance Only for the State of Kansas

AGENCY: U.S. Small Business Administration.

ACTION: Amendment 1.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This is an amendment of the Presidential declaration of a 
major disaster for Public Assistance Only for the State of Kansas 
(FEMA-4943-DR), dated September 1, 2026.
    Incident: Severe Storms, Straight-line Winds, Tornadoes, and 
Flooding.

DATES: Issued on October 13, 2026.
    Incident Period: June 18, 2026 through June 30, 2026.
    Physical Loan Application Deadline Date: November 2, 2026.
    Economic Injury (EIDL) Loan Application Deadline Date: June 1, 
2027.

ADDRESSES: Visit the MySBA Loan Portal at https://lending.sba.gov to 
apply for a disaster assistance loan.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Jennifer Talarico, Office of Disaster 
Recovery, U.S. Small Business Administration, 409 3rd Street SW, Suite 
6050, Washington, DC 20416, (202) 205-6734.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The notice of the President's major disaster 
declaration for Private Non-Profit organizations in the State of 
Kansas, dated September 1, 2026, is hereby amended to include the 
following area as adversely affected by the disaster.

Primary County: Lane.

    All other information in the original declaration remains 
unchanged.

(Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance Number 59008)
