36 C.F.R. § 905.735-501 — -501 Form and content of statements.
Citation: us:cfr:36:905.735-501
Creator: Pennsylvania Avenue Development Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-905.735-501
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 905 (Standards Of Conduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Statements of employment and financial interests required to be submitted under this subpart by employees and special Government employees shall contain the information required in the formats prescribed by the Civil Service Commission in the Federal Personnel Manual.
