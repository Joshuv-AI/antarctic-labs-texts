7 C.F.R. § 3402.15 — Faculty vitae.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3402.15
Creator: National Institute Of Food And Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3402.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3402 (Food And Agricultural Sciences National Needs Graduate And Postgraduate Fellowship Grants Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This section should include a Summary Vita, no more than 2 pages excluding publications listing, for each faculty member contributing significantly to institutional competence at the level of graduate study for the national need area addressed in the application. Applicants should arrange the faculty vitae with the Project Director(s) first, followed by the remaining faculty, in alphabetical order.
