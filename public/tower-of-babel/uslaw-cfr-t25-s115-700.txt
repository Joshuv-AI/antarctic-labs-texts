25 C.F.R. § 115.700 — Why is money held in trust for tribes and individual Indians?
Citation: us:cfr:25:115.700
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-115.700
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 115 (Trust Funds For Tribes And Individual Indians). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Congress has passed a number of laws that require the Secretary to establish and administer trust fund accounts for Indian tribes and certain individual Indians who have an interest(s) in trust lands, trust resources, or trust assets.
