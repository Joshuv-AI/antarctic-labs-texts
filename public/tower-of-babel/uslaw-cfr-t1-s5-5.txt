1 C.F.R. § 5.5 — Supplement to the Code of Federal Regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:1:5.5
Creator: Administrative Committee Of The Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-1/section-5.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 1 (General Provisions), Part 5 (General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-23 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Federal Register serves as a daily supplement to the Code of Federal Regulations. Each document that is subject to codification and published in a daily issue shall be keyed to the Code of Federal Regulations.
