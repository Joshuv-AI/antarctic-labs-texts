45 C.F.R. § 1322.11 — Purpose of services allotments under Title VI.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1322.11
Creator: Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1322.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1322 (Grants To Indian Tribes And Native Hawaiian Grantees For Supportive, Nutrition, And Caregiver Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Title VI of the Act authorizes the distribution of Federal funds to Tribal organizations and a Hawaiian Native grantee for the following categories of services:
(1) Supportive services;
(2) Nutrition services; and
(3) Family caregiver support program services.
(b) Funds authorized under these categories are for the purpose of assisting a Tribal organization or Hawaiian Native grantee to develop or enhance comprehensive and coordinated community-based systems for older Native Americans and family caregivers.
