5 C.F.R. § 930.111 — State license in possession.
Citation: us:cfr:5:930.111
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-930.111
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 930 (Programs For Specific Positions And Examinations (Miscellaneous)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An operator or incidental operator will have a State license in his or her possession at all times while driving a Government-owned or -leased motor vehicle on a public highway.
