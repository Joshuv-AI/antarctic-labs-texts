30 C.F.R. § 72.701 — Respiratory equipment; gas, dusts, fumes, or mists.
Citation: us:cfr:30:72.701
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-72.701
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 72 (Health Standards For Coal Mines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Respiratory equipment approved by NIOSH under 42 CFR part 84 shall be provided to persons exposed for short periods to inhalation hazards from gas, dusts, fumes, or mists. When the exposure is for prolonged periods, other measures to protect such persons or to reduce the hazard shall be taken.
