5 C.F.R. § 534.302 — Coverage.
Citation: us:cfr:5:534.302
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-534.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 534 (Pay Under Other Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to employees in executive level and staff positions in temporary organizations. Such employees are not subject to the provisions applicable to General Schedule employees covered by chapter 51 and subchapter III of chapter 53 of title 5, United States Code.
