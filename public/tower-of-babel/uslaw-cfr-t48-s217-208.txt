48 C.F.R. § 217.208 — 217.208 Solicitation provisions and contract clauses.
Citation: us:cfr:48:217.208
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-217.208
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 217 (Special Contracting Methods). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Sealed bid solicitations shall not include provisions for evaluations of options unless the contracting officer determines that there is a reasonable likelihood that the options will be exercised (10 U.S.C. 3206(e)). This limitation also applies to sealed bid solicitations for the contracts excluded by FAR 17.200.
