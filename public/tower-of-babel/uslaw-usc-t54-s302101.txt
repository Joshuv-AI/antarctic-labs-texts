54 U.S.C. § 302101 — Maintenance by Secretary
Citation: us:usc:54:302101
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section302101
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 3021 (National Register Of Historic Places). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may expand and maintain a National Register of Historic Places composed of districts, sites, buildings, structures, and objects significant in American history, architecture, archeology, engineering, and culture.
