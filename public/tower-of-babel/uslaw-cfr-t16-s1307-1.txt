16 C.F.R. § 1307.1 — Scope and application.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1307.1
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1307.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1307 (Prohibition Of Children'S Toys And Child Care Articles Containing Specified Phthalates). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part prohibits the manufacture for sale, offer for sale, distribution in commerce or importation into the United States of any children's toy or child care article containing any of the phthalates specified in § 1307.3.
