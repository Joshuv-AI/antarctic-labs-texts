40 C.F.R. § 121.2 — When certification is required.
Citation: us:cfr:40:121.2
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-121.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 121 (State Certification Of Activities Requiring A Federal License Or Permit). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Certification or waiver is required for any Federal license or permit that authorizes any activity which may result in any discharge from a point source into waters of the United States.
