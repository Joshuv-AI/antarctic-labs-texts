Methods: a call for papers
Published: 2024

The journal GENETICS is calling for submission of papers for a Special Issue on Experimental and Computational Methods.

Current research in genetics comprises analyses of single genes up to those that are genomic in scale, with high-throughput sequencing, phenotyping, and other approaches routinely generating massive amounts of data. The ability to use these genetic and genomic data to advance knowledge requires advances in both experimental and computational methods. This Special Issue will highlight both types of methods as well as studies that benchmark and compare existing experimental or computational tools.

For experimental methods, we will consider manuscripts describing technologies on topics that include DNA sequencing, synthesis, recombination, and editing; mutagenesis; cloning, transformation, and genome engineering; genotyping and phenotyping; variant analysis; chromosome structure; gene expression; single cell analysis; optogenetics; imaging; cell lineage tracing; and nontraditional model organisms.

For computational genomics, we seek methods that include the integration of multimodal data or that enhance computational efficiency for genomic data; modeling (e.g. of perturbation effects, chromatin dynamics, interactions between genetic and environmental factors, and single cell and time series data); prediction (e.g. of genes, binding sites, functions, regulatory networks, and nucleic acid and protein modifications); genome assembly; metagenomics; cell type annotation; spatial transcriptomics; differential expression; visualization; polygenic risk scores; and design of primers and probes.

In the areas of population, quantitative and statistical genetics, we invite submissions that include methods in spatial population genetics, the inference of selection and demography, and the analysis of ancestral recombination graphs and tree sequences. In addition, this series welcomes contributions that describe new simulation and/or machine learning methods.

The Special Issue will launch in Summer 2025 and articles will be published online shortly after acceptance via Advance Access. Papers will be collected and navigable and promoted on social media and other venues.

Authors are invited to submit manuscripts by March 1, 2025. Manuscripts will be reviewed and edited through the standard peer review process and according to the standards of the journal. At submission, please choose the “Special Issue: Methods” article type in the submission system.

Please share with your colleagues, and do not hesitate to contact any of the Special Issue Editors or the GENETICS Editorial Office with questions, suggestions, or presubmission inquiries.
