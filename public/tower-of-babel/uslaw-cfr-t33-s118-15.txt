33 C.F.R. § 118.15 — Penalty for interference or obstruction.
Citation: us:cfr:33:118.15
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-118.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 118 (Bridge Lighting And Other Signals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person violating the provisions of § 118.10 of this chapter shall be deemed guilty of a misdemeanor and be subject to a fine not exceeding $500 for each offense. Each day during which such violation shall continue shall be considered a new offense.
