12 C.F.R. § 48.8 — Capital requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:12:48.8
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-48.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 48 (Retail Foreign Exchange Transactions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A national bank offering or entering into retail forex transactions must be well capitalized as defined by 12 CFR part 6.
