41 C.F.R. § 50-203.22 — -203.22 Effective date of determinations.
Citation: us:cfr:41:50-203.22
Creator: Chapter 50—Public Contracts, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-50-203.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 50-203 (203—Rules Of Practice). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any minimum wage determination issued as a result of hearings held under this subpart shall take effect not less than 30 days after due notice is given of the issuance thereof by publication in the Federal Register, or at such time prior thereto as may be provided therein upon good cause found and published therewith.
