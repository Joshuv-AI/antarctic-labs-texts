15 U.S.C. § 6410 — Relation to other programs
Citation: us:usc:15:6410
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section6410
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 90 (Propane Education And Research). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter may be construed to preempt or supersede any other program relating to propane education and research organized and operated under the laws of the United States or any State.
