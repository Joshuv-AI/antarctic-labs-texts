7 C.F.R. § 1777.15 — Public infrastructure—Rates and terms.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1777.15
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1777.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1777 (Water And Waste Facility Loans And Grants To Alleviate Health Risks). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Public infrastructure loans will bear interest at not more than the maximum rate of 5 percent per annum. The rates and terms will be in accordance with 7 CFR 1780 Water and Waste Loans and Grants.
