Abstract
. We build on the perspective of the works [ 10 ] , [ 16 ] , [ 9 ] , [ 8 ] , [ 6 ] (and others) and study the dynamical arithmetic complexity of rational points in projective varieties. Our main results make progress towards the attractive problem of asymptotic complexity of coordinate size dynamics in the sense formulated by Matsuzawa, in [ 16 , Question 1.1.2] , and building on earlier work of Silverman [ 22 ] . A key tool to our approach here is a novel formulation of conjectural Vojta type inequalities for log canonical pairs and with respect to finite extensions of number fields. Among other features, these conjectured Diophantine arithmetic height inequalities raise the question of existence of log resolutions with respect to finite extensions of number fields which is another novel concept which we formulate in precise terms here and also which is of an independent interest.

Absolute values, local Weil and height functions

Our conventions about absolute values, local Weil and height functions are as in
[
9
, Section 2]
. For example, if
X
is a projective variety over
\mathbf{K}
and if

is a Cartier divisor on

\mathbf{F}
then fixing a

\mathbf{F}

\lambda_{D}(x,v)=\lambda_{\mathcal{D}}(x,v):=\max_{j=0,\dots,k}\min_{i=0,\dots,\ell}\left|\frac{s_{j}}{t_{i}s_{D}}(x)\right|_{v,\mathbf{K}}\text{ for $x\in(X\setminus\operatorname{Supp}(D))(\mathbf{K})$. }

Here, we have fixed

with

and

{\mathcal{O}}_{X_{\mathbf{F}}}(D)\simeq N\otimes M^{-1}

for line bundles
N
and
M
on

had having respective global generating sections

\mathbf{s}:=(s_{0},\dots,s_{k})\text{ and }\mathbf{t}:=(t_{0},\dots,t_{\ell})\text{;}

s_{D}
is a meromorphic section of
{\mathcal{O}}_{X_{\mathbf{F}}}(D)
with associated Cartier divisor equal to
D
. Finally,
(X\setminus\operatorname{Supp}(D))(\mathbf{K})
is the set of those
\mathbf{K}
-rational points

whose image in

does not lie in the support of

.

Local Weil functions for
\mathbb{Q}
-Cartier divisors on
X
and defined over
\mathbf{F}
are defined in the evident way via
\mathbb{Q}
-linear extension of the theory of local Weil functions for Cartier divisors.

the logarithmic height functions on

that are determined by lines bundles

on

and defined over
\mathbf{K}
. Especially, if

Pairs, log resolutions and discrepancies

In our study of Problem

,

,
discrepancies

(among others).

For our purposes here a

\mathbf{K}
and a
\mathbb{Q}
-Weil divisor

on
X
which is defined over
\mathbf{F}
and which is such that

-Cartier. Here

is a canonical divisor on

.

The concept of

for a pair
(X,D)
, see Definition

, is important for our perspective here. For the case that

is defined over
\mathbf{K}

Definition 3.1

Let
(X,D)
be a pair with

\mathbf{K}

\mathbf{F}/\mathbf{K}

\mathbf{K}

\mu^{-1}_{\mathbf{F}}(\operatorname{Supp}D)\bigcup\operatorname{Ex}(\mu_{\mathbf{F}})\text{,}

To place Definition
3.1

\mathbf{K}
, existence of such log resolutions is a consequence of work of Hironaka
[
12
]
. (See also
[
14
]
and
[
1
]
.) On the other hand, in complete generality, existence of

log resolutions, for example when

is defined

\mathbf{F}

, with

\mathbf{F}

3.1
.

We refrain from a further discussion here about existence of
\mathbf{F}/\mathbf{K}
log resolutions in the sense that we have formulated in Definition

(X,D)
, with

\mathbf{K}

of

for each exceptional divisor

We say that a geometrically normal projective variety

-Cartier. Further, as in

)

\text{
divisors $E_{i}$
}\end{subarray}}a_{i}E_{i}

where

.

4.
Multiplier ideal sheaves and log canonical thresholds

Let
X
be a projective variety over
\mathbf{K}
and having log canonical singularities. Let
\mathbf{F}/\mathbf{K}
be a finite extension and let
\mathcal{I}\subset{\mathcal{O}}_{X_{\mathbf{F}}}
be a nonzero ideal sheaf. Let

be a positive rational number. The
multiplier ideal sheaf

\mathcal{J}(X,\mathcal{I}^{c})
associated with
\mathcal{I}
and

is defined by fixing a log resolution

\mu\colon X^{\prime}\rightarrow X_{\mathbf{F}}

of
(X,\mathcal{I})
and setting

\mathcal{J}(X,\mathcal{I}^{c}):=\mu_{*}{\mathcal{O}}_{X^{\prime}}(\mathrm{K}_{X^{\prime}/X}-\lfloor cF\rfloor)\text{.}

Here,
\mu
is a projective birational morphism which is such that

•

X^{\prime}
is a nonsingular variety over
\mathbf{F}
;

•

\mathcal{I}{\mathcal{O}}_{X^{\prime}}={\mathcal{O}}_{X^{\prime}}(-F)
for some effective Cartier divisor

on
X_{\mathbf{F}}
;

•

\operatorname{Exc}(\mu)\bigcup\operatorname{Supp}F
is a simple normal crossings divisor on
X
and
\operatorname{Exc}(\mu)
is the exceptional locus; and

•

\mathrm{K}_{X^{\prime}/X}:=\mathrm{K}_{X^{\prime}}-\mu^{*}\mathrm{K}_{X_{\mathbf{F}}}
is the relative canonical divisor.

Let

. The log canonical threshold of
\mathcal{I}
at

is defined to be

\operatorname{lct}(\mathcal{I};x):=\inf\{c\in\mathbb{Q}_{>0}:\mathcal{J}(X,\mathcal{I}^{c})_{x}\subset\mathfrak{m}_{x}\}\text{.}

The log canonical threshold of
(X,\mathcal{I})
is defined to be

\operatorname{(}X,\mathcal{I}):=\min\{\operatorname{lct}(\mathcal{I};x):x\in X(\overline{\mathbf{K}})\}\text{.}

Example 4.1
.

Consider the case that
\mathcal{I}={\mathcal{O}}_{X_{\mathbf{F}}}(-D)
is the ideal sheaf of a nonzero effective Cartier divisor

on

and defined over
\mathbf{F}
. In this case, we denote

\operatorname{lct}(D)=\operatorname{lct}(X,D):=\operatorname{lct}(X,{\mathcal{O}}_{X_{\mathbf{F}}}(-D))\text{;}

then

can be described, as in

, p. 74]
and
[

, Equation 13]

\displaystyle\operatorname{lct}(D)=\operatorname{lct}(X,D)

\displaystyle=\sup\{c\in\mathbb{Q}:(X,cD)\text{ is log canonical}\}

\displaystyle=\inf_{E}\frac{1+a(E,X)}{\operatorname{ord}_{E}(D)}

where the infimum is taken over all exceptional divisors
E
over
X_{\overline{\mathbf{K}}}
and supported on some projective model
X^{\prime}\rightarrow X_{\overline{\mathbf{K}}}
and where

a(E,X):=\mathrm{ord}_{E}(\mathrm{K}_{X^{\prime}/X_{\overline{\mathbf{K}}}})

is the
discrepancy
of
E
over
X
. For later use, we note in particular, that if

has canonical singularities then

(4.1)

\operatorname{lct}(D)\geqslant\inf_{E}\frac{1}{\operatorname{ord}_{E}(D)}\text{.}

Definition 4.2

When

over

\sum_{v\in S}\lambda_{\Gamma^{\prime\prime}}(\cdot,v)+h_{\mathrm{K}_{X^{\prime\prime}}}(\cdot)\\

In what follows at times we implicitly enlarge

\Gamma^{\prime\prime}=(\mu_{\mathbf{F}}^{\prime\prime})^{*}(\mathrm{K}_{X^{\prime}_{\mathbf{F}}}+D^{\prime})-\mathrm{K}_{X^{\prime\prime}_{\mathbf{F}}}

Preliminaries about arithmetic dynamical degrees

Our main result towards Problem
1.1
is Theorem

. Its formulation utilizes the concept of arithmetic dynamical degree from
[
13
]
. Here, we fix the most basic preliminary details in this regard which are important for our purposes here.

Let
X
be a geometrically normal projective variety over a number field
\mathbf{K}
. Let
h_{H}(\cdot)
be a logarithmic height function on
X
that is associated to an ample line bundle
H
on
X
and defined over
\mathbf{K}
. Set

be a surjective
\mathbf{K}
-morphism. Then the

of

with respect to

is the well-defined quantity (i.e., independent of
H
)

\alpha_{f}(x)=\lim_{n\to\infty}h_{H}^{+}(f^{(n)}(x))^{1/n}\text{.}

Here
f^{(n)}\colon X\rightarrow X
is the
n
th interate of
f
.

In our study of Problem
1.1
a key role is played by the following more flexible form of
[
16
, Proposition 1.5]
.

Proposition 6.1
.

Let
L
be a big line bundle on a geometrically normal projective variety
X
and defined over
\mathbf{K}
. Use
\mathbb{Q}
-linear equivalence to write
L\sim_{\mathbb{Q}}A+N
for
A
ample and
N
effective. Let
f\colon X\rightarrow X
be a surjective morphism. Let
x\in X(\mathbf{K})
. There exists a proper Zariski closed subset
Z\subsetneq X
together with a non-negative integer
\ell
and positive real numbers
C_{1}
,
C_{2}
which are such that

C_{1}n^{\ell}\alpha_{f}(x)^{n}\leqslant\max\{1,h_{L}(f^{(n)}(x))\}\leqslant C_{2}n^{\ell}\alpha_{f}(x)^{n}

for all
n\geqslant 1
with the property that
f^{(n)}(x)\not\in\operatorname{Supp}(N)
. Here
\alpha_{f}(x)
is the arithmetic degree of

with respect to

.

Proof.

The first step is to deduce the case that

is ample. This goes through exactly as in
[
21
, Proof of Theorem 1.1]
.
To boost this to the case that

is big, we using
\mathbb{Q}

Theorem 7.1
.

Let
X
be a geometrically normal projective variety and defined over
\mathbf{K}
. Assume that

has canonical singularities. Let

be a big line bundle on

and defined over
\mathbf{K}

be a nonzero and effective
\mathbb{Q}
-Cartier divisor on

. We allow the possibility that

is defined over some finite extension
\mathbf{F}/\mathbf{K}
. Let
f\colon X\rightarrow X
be a surjective morphism. Let

. Further

•

assume that the forward orbit
\mathrm{O}_{f}(x)
is

in the sense that the forward orbit
\mathrm{O}_{f}(x)
has finite intersection with all proper Zariski closed subsets of
X
;

•

assume that
\alpha_{f}(x)>1

•

choose

•

let

•

\frac{(e+\epsilon)^{m}}{\alpha_{f}(x)^{m}}<\epsilon_{0}\text{; and}

1.3

\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{\sum_{v\in S}\lambda_{D}(f^{(n)}(x),v)}{h_{L}(f^{(n)}(x))}=0\text{.}

h_{L}(\cdot)\geqslant 1
.

1.3
applied to
\left(X,\frac{1}{(e+\epsilon)^{m}}(f^{(m)})^{*}D\right)

\sum_{v\in S}\lambda_{D}(f^{(n)}(x),v)\leqslant\sum_{v\in S}\lambda_{(f^{(m)})^{*}D}(f^{(n-m)}(x),v)+\mathrm{O}(1)\\
\leqslant\epsilon_{0}\alpha_{f}(x)^{m}(h_{L}(f^{(n-m)}(x))-h_{\mathrm{K}_{X}}(f^{(n-m)}(x)))+\mathrm{O}_{m}(1)

\leqslant\epsilon_{0}\alpha_{f}(x)^{m}\frac{C_{3}C_{2}(n-m)^{\ell}\alpha_{f}(x)^{n-m}}{C_{1}n^{\ell}\alpha_{f}(x)^{n}}+\frac{\mathrm{O}_{m}(1)}{h_{L}(f^{(n)}(x))}\\

Let

When

]

the scheme theoretic inverse image of

Proof of Theorem

As in Example

Indeed, without loss of generality, we may and do assume that

Now let

By the conclusion of Conjecture
1.3
applied to

\epsilon^{\prime}=1
, it follows that there is a proper Zariski closed subset

(8.1)

\sum_{v\in S}\lambda_{D}(f^{(n)}(x),v)\\
\leqslant\epsilon_{0}\alpha_{f}(x)^{m_{0}}(h_{L}(f^{(n-m_{0})}(x))-h_{\mathrm{K}_{X}}(f^{(n-m_{0})}(x)))+\mathrm{O}_{m_{0}}(1)

(8.2)

\sum_{v\not\in S}\lambda_{D}(f^{(n)}(x),v)\\

,

and
h_{L}(f^{(n)}(x))\to\infty

∎
