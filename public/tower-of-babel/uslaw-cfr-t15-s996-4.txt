15 C.F.R. § 996.4 — Liability.
Citation: us:cfr:15:996.4
Creator: National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-996.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 996 (Quality Assurance And Certification Requirements For Noaa Hydrographic Products And Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Government of the United States shall not be liable for any negligence by producers of hydrographic products certified under this part.
