27 C.F.R. § 70.331 — Fraudulent returns, statements, or other documents.
Citation: us:cfr:27:70.331
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-70.331
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 70 (Procedure And Administration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person who willfully delivers or discloses to any officer or employee of the Bureau any list, return, account, statement, or other document, known by him to be fraudulent or to be false as to any material matter, shall be fined not more than $10,000 ($50,000 in the case of a corporation) or imprisoned not more than 1 year, or both.
