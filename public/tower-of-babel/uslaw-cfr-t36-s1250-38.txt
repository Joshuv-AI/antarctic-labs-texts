36 C.F.R. § 1250.38 — In what format does NARA provide copies?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1250.38
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1250.38
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1250 (Nara Records Subject To Foia). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After all applicable fees are paid, we provide you copies of records in the format you request if the records already exist in that format, or if they are readily reproducible in the format you request.
