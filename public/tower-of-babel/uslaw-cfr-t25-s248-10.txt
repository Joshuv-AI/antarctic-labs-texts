25 C.F.R. § 248.10 — Appeals from administrative actions.
Citation: us:cfr:25:248.10
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-248.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 248 (Use Of Columbia River Indian In-Lieu Fishing Sites). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any decision made by the Regional Director under this part shall be subject to appeal to the Assistant Secretary—Indian Affairs, and any decision of the Assistant Secretary—Indian Affairs on such an appeal may be appealed to the Secretary of the Interior in accordance with part 2 of this chapter.
