40 C.F.R. § 60.539b — b What parts of the General Provisions do not apply to me?
Citation: us:cfr:40:60.539b
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-60.539b
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 60 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following provisions of subpart A of part 60 do not apply to this subpart:
(a) Section 60.7;
(b) Section 60.8(a), (c), (d), (e), (f)(1), and (g);
(c) Section 60.14; and
(c) Section 60.15(d).
