48 C.F.R. § 316.603-3 — 316.603-3 Limitations.
Citation: us:cfr:48:316.603-3
Creator: Chapter 3—Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-316.603-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 316 (Types Of Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An official one level above the contracting officer shall make the written determination, to be included in the contract file, that no other contract type is suitable and to approve all letter contract modifications. No letter contract or modification can exceed the limits prescribed in FAR 16.603-2(c).
