46 C.F.R. § 28.150 — Emergency Position Indicating Radio Beacons (EPIRBs).
Citation: us:cfr:46:28.150
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-28.150
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 28 (Requirements For Commercial Fishing Industry Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each vessel must be equipped with an emergency position indicating radio beacon (EPIRB) as required by 46 CFR part 25, subpart 25.26.
