21 C.F.R. § 207.55 — What additional drug listing information may FDA require?
Citation: us:cfr:21:207.55
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-207.55
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 207 (Requirements For Foreign And Domestic Establishment Registration And Listing For Human Drugs, Including Drugs That Are Regulated Under A Biologics License Application, And Animal Drugs, And The National Drug Code). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For a particular listed drug, upon our request, the registrant must briefly state the basis for its belief that the drug is not subject to section 505 or 512 of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act or section 351 of the Public Health Service Act.
