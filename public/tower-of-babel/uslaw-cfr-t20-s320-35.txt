20 C.F.R. § 320.35 — Review of decision of hearings officer on motion of Board.
Citation: us:cfr:20:320.35
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-320.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 320 (Initial Determinations Under The Railroad Unemployment Insurance Act And Reviews Of And Appeals From Such Determinations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board may, on its own motion, review a decision of the hearings officer on the basis of the evidence previously submitted in the case, and may designate any employee of the Board to take additional evidence and to report his findings to the Board.
