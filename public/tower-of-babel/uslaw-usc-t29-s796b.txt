29 U.S.C. § 796b — Eligibility for receipt of services
Citation: us:usc:29:796b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section796b
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 16 (Vocational Rehabilitation And Other Rehabilitation Services); Subchapter VII (Independent Living Services And Centers For Independent Living); Part A (Individuals With Significant Disabilities); Subpart 1 (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Services may be provided under this part to any individual with a significant disability, as defined in section 705(21)(B) of this title.
