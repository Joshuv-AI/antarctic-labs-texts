45 C.F.R. § 81.43 — Reduction of time to file documents.
Citation: us:cfr:45:81.43
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-81.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 81 (Practice And Procedure For Hearings Under Part 80 Of This Title). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For good cause, the reviewing authority or the presiding officer, with respect to matters pending before them, may reduce any time limit prescribed by the rules in this part, except as provided by law or in part 80 of this title.
