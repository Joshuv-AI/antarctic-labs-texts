39 U.S.C. § 201 — United States Postal Service
Citation: us:usc:39:201
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title39-section201
Context: United States Code, Title 39 (Postal Service); Part I (General); Chapter 2 (Organization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is established, as an independent establishment of the executive branch of the Government of the United States, the United States Postal Service.
