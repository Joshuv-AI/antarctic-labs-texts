19 C.F.R. § 148.112 — Evidence of purchase.
Citation: us:cfr:19:148.112
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-148.112
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 148 (Personal Declarations And Exemptions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A sales slip, invoice, or other evidence of purchase, shall be presented with the declaration for all unaccompanied articles.
