46 C.F.R. § 203.6 — Oral evidentiary hearing before one or more members.
Citation: us:cfr:46:203.6
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-203.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 203 (Procedures Relating To Conduct Of Certain Hearings Under The Merchant Marine Act, 1936, As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an oral evidentiary hearing is to be conducted, the Maritime Administration, or the Maritime Subsidy Board or one or more of its members, may conduct such hearing. A member who is not present at the hearing may participate in the consideration and the decision of the case where the oral evidentiary hearing, if held, has been stenographically recorded in full and transcribed for the member's review.
