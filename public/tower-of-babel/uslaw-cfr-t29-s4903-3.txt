29 C.F.R. § 4903.3 — Do these regulations adopt the Federal Claims Collection Standards (FCCS)?
Citation: us:cfr:29:4903.3
Creator: Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-4903.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 4903 (Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part adopts and incorporates all provisions of FCCS. This part also supplements the FCCS by prescribing procedures consistent with FCCS, as necessary and appropriate for PBGC operations.
