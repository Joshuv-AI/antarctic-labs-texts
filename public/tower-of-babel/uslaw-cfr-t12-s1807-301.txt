12 C.F.R. § 1807.301 — Eligible Uses.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1807.301
Creator: Community Development Financial Institutions Fund, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1807.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1807 (Capital Magnet Fund). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Recipient must use its CMF Award to finance and support Affordable Housing Activities and/or Economic Development Activities through the following Eligible Uses:
(a) To capitalize Loan Loss Reserves;
(b) To capitalize a Revolving Loan Fund;
(c) To capitalize an Affordable Housing Fund;
(d) To capitalize an Economic Development Activity Fund;
(e) To make Risk-Sharing Loans; and
(f) To provide Loan Guarantees.
