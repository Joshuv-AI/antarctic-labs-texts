24 C.F.R. § 75.7 — Requirements applicable to HUD NOFAs for Section 3 covered programs.
Citation: us:cfr:24:75.7
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-75.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 75 (Economic Opportunities For Low- And Very Low-Income Persons). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All notices of funding availability (NOFAs) issued by HUD that announce the availability of funding covered by § 75.3 will include notice that this part is applicable to the funding and may include, as appropriate for the specific NOFA, points or bonus points for the quality of Section 3 plans.
