21 C.F.R. § 357.180 — Professional labeling.
Citation: us:cfr:21:357.180
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-357.180
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 357 (Miscellaneous Internal Drug Products For Over-The-Counter Human Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The labeling provided to health professionals (but not to the general public) may contain an additional indication: “For the treatment of common roundworm infestation.”
