25 C.F.R. § 502.26 — Tribal Gaming Regulatory Authority (TGRA).
Citation: us:cfr:25:502.26
Creator: National Indian Gaming Commission, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-502.26
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 502 (Definitions Of This Chapter). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Tribal Gaming Regulatory Authority (TGRA) means the entity authorized by Tribal law to regulate gaming conducted pursuant to the Indian Gaming Regulatory Act.
