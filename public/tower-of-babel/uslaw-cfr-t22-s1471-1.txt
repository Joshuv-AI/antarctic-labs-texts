22 C.F.R. § 1471.1 — Request for Panel consideration.
Citation: us:cfr:22:1471.1
Creator: Foreign Service Labor Relations Board; Federal Labor Relations Authority; General Counsel Of The Federal Labor Relations Authority; And The Foreign Service Impasse Disputes Panel
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-1471.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 1471 (Procedures Of The Panel). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If direct negotiations and other voluntary arrangements for settlement, if any, fail to resolve a negotiation impasse:
(a) Either party, or the parties jointly, may request the Panel to consider the matter by filing a request as hereinafter provided; or
(b) The Panel may, pursuant to 22 U.S.C. 4110(a), undertake consideration of the matter upon request of the Executive Director.
