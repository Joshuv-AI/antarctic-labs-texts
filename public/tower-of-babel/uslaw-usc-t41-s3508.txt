41 U.S.C. § 3508 — Right to examine contractor records
Citation: us:usc:41:3508
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title41-section3508
Context: United States Code, Title 41 (Public Contracts); Chapter 35 (Truthful Cost Or Pricing Data). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purpose of evaluating the accuracy, completeness, and currency of cost or pricing data required to be submitted by this chapter, an executive agency shall have the authority provided by section 4706(b)(2) of this title.
