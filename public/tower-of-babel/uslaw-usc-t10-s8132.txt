10 U.S.C. § 8132 — Regular Navy: transfers, line and staff corps
Citation: us:usc:10:8132
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8132
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 815 (Original Appointments). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
A regular officer of the Navy in a staff corps in a grade not above lieutenant commander may be appointed in the line of the Navy to the same grade.
(b)
A regular officer in the line of the Navy in a grade not above lieutenant commander may be appointed to the same grade in a staff corps under regulations prescribed by the Secretary of Defense.
