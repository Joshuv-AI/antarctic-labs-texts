14 C.F.R. § 194.245 — Pilot qualification and program management requirements to operate powered-lift under subpart K to part 91 of this chapter.
Citation: us:cfr:14:194.245
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-194.245
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 194 (Special Federal Aviation Regulation No. 120—Powered-Lift: Pilot Certification And Training; Operations Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Section 91.1055(a) of this chapter applies to powered-lift operating under subpart K to part 91.
(b) Reference to class of aircraft in § 91.1055(b)(2) of this chapter is inapplicable when a powered-lift is used for the operation under subpart K to part 91.
