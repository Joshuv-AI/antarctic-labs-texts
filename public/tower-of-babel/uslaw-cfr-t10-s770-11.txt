10 C.F.R. § 770.11 — When does a claim “accrue” for purposes of notifying the Field Office Manager under § 770.9(a) of this part?
Citation: us:cfr:10:770.11
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-770.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 770 (Transfer Of Real Property At Defense Nuclear Facilities For Economic Development). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For purposes of § 770.9(a) of this part, a claim “accrues” on the date on which the person asserting the claim knew, or reasonably should have known, that the injury to person or property was caused or contributed to by the release or threatened release of a hazardous substance, pollutant, or contaminant as a result of DOE activities at the defense nuclear facility on which the real property is located.
