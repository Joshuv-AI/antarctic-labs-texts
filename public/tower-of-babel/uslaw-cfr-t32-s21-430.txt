32 C.F.R. § 21.430 — What are the responsibilities of the head of the awarding or administering activity?
Citation: us:cfr:32:21.430
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-21.430
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 21 (DoD GRANTS AND AGREEMENTS—GENERAL MATTERS). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When designated by the Head of the DoD Component or his or her designee (see 32 CFR 21.425), the head of the awarding or administering activity (i.e., the HCA) is responsible for the awards made by or assigned to that activity. He or she must supervise and establish internal policies and procedures for that activity's awards.
