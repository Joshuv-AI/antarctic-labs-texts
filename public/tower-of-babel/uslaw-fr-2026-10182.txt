North Pacific Fishery Management Council; Public Meeting

AGENCY: National Marine Fisheries Service (NMFS), National Oceanic and 
Atmospheric Administration (NOAA), Commerce.

ACTION: Notice of public meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Center of Independent Experts (CIE) review of the Alaska 
sablefish stock assessment will be held June 16, 2026, through June 18, 
2026.

DATES: The meeting will be held on June 16, 2026, through June 18, 
2026, inclusive, from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. Alaska Time.

ADDRESSES: The meeting will be in-person only and will be held at the 
Ted Stevens Marine Institute, 17109 Pt Lena Loop Rd., Juneau, AK 99801. 
The

[[Page 29942]]

meeting will be in the Royce Room (Room 131).

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Daniel Goethel, Alaska Fishery Science 
Center staff (Auke Bay Lab); email: [email protected]; phone: 
(305) 361-4231.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

Agenda

Tuesday, June 16, 2026, Through Thursday, June 18, 2026

    The CIE will review the Alaska sablefish stock assessment. The 
agenda is subject to change, and the latest version and meeting 
materials will be posted at https://github.com/dgoethel-noaa/2026_Sablefish_CIE/tree/main.
