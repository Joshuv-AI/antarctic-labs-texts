33 C.F.R. § 151.2080 — Penalties.
Citation: us:cfr:33:151.2080
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 151 (Vessels Carrying Oil, Noxious Liquid Substances, Garbage, Municipal Or Commercial Waste, And Ballast Water). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A person who violates this subpart is liable for a civil penalty not to exceed $35,000. Each day of a continuing violation constitutes a separate violation. A vessel operated in violation of the regulations is liable in rem for any civil penalty assessed under this subpart for that violation.
(b) A person who knowingly violates the regulations of this subpart is guilty of a class C felony.
