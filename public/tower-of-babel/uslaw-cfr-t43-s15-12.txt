43 C.F.R. § 15.12 — Closing of Preserve.
Citation: us:cfr:43:15.12
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-15.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 15 (Key Largo Coral Reef Preserve). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Preserve may be closed to public use in the event of emergency conditions encouraged within the Preserve.
