29 C.F.R. § 18.1007 — Testimony or written admission of party.
Citation: us:cfr:29:18.1007
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-18.1007
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 18 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure For Administrative Hearings Before The Office Of Administrative Law Judges). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Contents of writings, recordings, or photographs may be proved by the testimony or deposition of the party against whom offered or by that party's written admission, without accounting for the nonproduction of the original.
