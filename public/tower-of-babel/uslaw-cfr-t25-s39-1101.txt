25 C.F.R. § 39.1101 — Addition of pre-kindergarten as a weight factor to the Indian School Equalization Formula in fiscal year 1982.
Citation: us:cfr:25:39.1101
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-39.1101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 39 (The Indian School Equalization Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director, in consultation with the tribes and school boards, shall determine appropriate weight factors needed to include pre-kindergarten programs in the Indian School Equalization Formula in fiscal year 1982. Based on a needs assessment, to be completed by January 1, 1980, pre-kindergarten programs shall be included in the Bureau's education request for fiscal year 1982.
