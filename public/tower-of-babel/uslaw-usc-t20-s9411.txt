20 U.S.C. § 9411 — Local allocations
Citation: us:usc:20:9411
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section9411
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 75 (Early Learning Opportunities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
The Lead State Agency shall allocate to Local Councils in the State not less than 93 percent of the funds provided to the State under this chapter for a fiscal year.
(b) Limitation
The Lead State Agency shall allocate funds provided under this chapter on the basis of the population of the locality served by the Local Council.
