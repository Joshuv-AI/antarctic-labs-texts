22 U.S.C. § 6961 — Establishment of Task Force
Citation: us:usc:22:6961
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section6961
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 77 (United States-China Relations); Subchapter IV (Trade And Rule Of Law Issues In The People’S Republic Of China); Part A (Task Force On Prohibition Of Importation Of Products Of Forced Or Prison Labor From The People’S Republic Of China). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is hereby established a task force on prohibition of importation of products of forced or prison labor from the People’s Republic of China (hereafter in this part referred to as the “Task Force”).
