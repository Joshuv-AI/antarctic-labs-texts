36 C.F.R. § 1238.24 — What are NARA inspection requirements for temporary microform records?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1238.24
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1238 (Microforms Records Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

NARA recommends, but does not require, that agencies use the inspection procedures described in § 1238.22(a).
