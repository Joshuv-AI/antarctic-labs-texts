48 C.F.R. § 1401.7001-1 — 1401.7001-1 Review and approval by Assistant Secretaries.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1401.7001-1
Creator: Chapter 14—Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1401.7001-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1401 (Department Of The Interior Acquisition Regulation System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Contract actions shall be reviewed and approved by Assistant Secretaries as prescribed in 211-255 DM. Their approvals shall be obtained before requesting any other approvals prescribed in the DIAR.
