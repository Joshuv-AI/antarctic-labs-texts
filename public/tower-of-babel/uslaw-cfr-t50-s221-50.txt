50 C.F.R. § 221.50 — When and where will the hearing be held?
Citation: us:cfr:50:221.50
Creator: National Marine Fisheries Service, National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-221.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 221 (Conditions And Prescriptions In Ferc Hydropower Licenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Except as provided in paragraph (b) of this section, the hearing will be held at the time and place set at the initial prehearing conference under § 221.40, generally within 25 days after the date set for completion of discovery.
(b) On motion by a party or on the ALJ's initiative, the ALJ may change the date, time, or place of the hearing if he or she finds:
(1) That there is good cause for the change; and
(2) That the change will not unduly prejudice the parties and witnesses.
