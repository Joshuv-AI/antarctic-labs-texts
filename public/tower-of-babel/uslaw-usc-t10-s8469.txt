10 U.S.C. § 8469 — Band: composition
Citation: us:usc:10:8469
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8469
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Education And Training); Chapter 853 (United States Naval Academy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Naval Academy Band shall be composed of one leader, one second leader, and such enlisted members of the Navy as may be assigned.
(b)
In determining years of service for the purpose of retirement, and in determining eligibility for reenlistment bonus, the members who are assigned as leader and second leader shall be treated as if they had not been so assigned.
(c)
The enlisted members assigned to the Naval Academy Band shall be distributed in grade substantially the same as in the United States Navy Band.
