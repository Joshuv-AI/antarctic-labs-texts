25 U.S.C. § 2904 — No restrictions
Citation: us:usc:25:2904
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section2904
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 31 (Native American Languages). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The right of Native Americans to express themselves through the use of Native American languages shall not be restricted in any public proceeding, including publicly supported education programs.
