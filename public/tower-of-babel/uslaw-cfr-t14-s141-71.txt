14 C.F.R. § 141.71 — Applicability.

Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 141 (Pilot Schools). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes the operating rules applicable to a pilot school or provisional pilot school certificated under the provisions of this part.
