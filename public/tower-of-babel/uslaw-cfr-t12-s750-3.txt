12 C.F.R. § 750.3 — Prohibited indemnification payments.
Citation: us:cfr:12:750.3
Creator: National Credit Union Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-750.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 750 (Golden Parachute And Indemnification Payments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A federally insured credit union must not make or agree to make any prohibited indemnification payment, except as permitted by this chapter. 1
