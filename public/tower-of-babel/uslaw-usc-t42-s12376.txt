42 U.S.C. § 12376 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:42:12376
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section12376
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 127 (Coordinated Services For Children, Youth, And Families); Subchapter II (White House Conference On Children, Youth, And Families). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For purposes of this subchapter—
(1)
the term “Conference” means the 1993 White House Conference on Children, Youth, and Families; and
(2)
the terms “child”, “youth”, and “young individual” means 1 an individual who is less than 21 years of age.
