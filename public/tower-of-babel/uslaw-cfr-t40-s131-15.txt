40 C.F.R. § 131.15 — Authorizing the use of schedules of compliance for water quality-based effluent limits in NPDES permits.
Citation: us:cfr:40:131.15
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-131.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 131 (Water Quality Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a State intends to authorize the use of schedules of compliance for water quality-based effluent limits in NPDES permits, the State must adopt a permit compliance schedule authorizing provision. Such authorizing provision is a water quality standard subject to EPA review and approval under section 303 of the Act and must be consistent with sections 502(17) and 301(b)(1)(C) of the Act.
