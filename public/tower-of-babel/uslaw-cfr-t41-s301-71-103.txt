41 C.F.R. § 301-71.103 — -71.103 Required information for travel authorizations.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-71.103
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-71.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-71 (71—Agency Travel Accountability Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Travel authorizations must include:
(a) Name(s) of employee(s);
(b) Signature of the proper authorizing official;
(c) Purpose of travel;
(d) Conditions or limitations of the authorization;
(f) Estimated travel costs (including estimated costs for the entire period for open authorizations); and
(g) A statement confirming the employee(s) is/are authorized to travel.
