34 C.F.R. § 367.60 — When may a DSA make subawards or contracts?
Citation: us:cfr:34:367.60
Creator: Office Of Special Education And Rehabilitative Services, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-367.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 367 (Independent Living Services For Older Individuals Who Are Blind). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A DSA may operate or administer the program or projects under this part to carry out the purposes specified in § 367.1, either directly or through—
(a) Subawards to public or private nonprofit agencies or organizations; or
(b) Contracts with individuals, entities, or organizations that are not public or private nonprofit agencies or organizations.
