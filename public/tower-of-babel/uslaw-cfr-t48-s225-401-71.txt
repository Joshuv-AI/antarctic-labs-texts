48 C.F.R. § 225.401-71 — 225.401-71 Products or services in support of operations in Afghanistan.
Citation: us:cfr:48:225.401-71
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-225.401-71
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 225 (Foreign Acquisition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When acquiring products or services, other than small arms, in support of operations in Afghanistan, if using a procedure specified in 225.7703-1(a)(2) or (3), the procedures of subpart 25.4 are not applicable.
