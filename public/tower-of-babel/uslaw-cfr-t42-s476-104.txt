42 C.F.R. § 476.104 — Coordination of activities.
Citation: us:cfr:42:476.104
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-476.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 476 (Quality Improvement Organization Review). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In order to achieve efficient and economical review, a QIO must coordinate its activities (including information exchanges) with the activities of—
(a) Medicare administrative contractors, fiscal intermediaries, and carriers.
(b) Other QIOs; and
(c) Other public or private review organizations as may be appropriate.
