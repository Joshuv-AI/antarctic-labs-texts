27 C.F.R. § 46.14 — Termination of liability.
Citation: us:cfr:27:46.14
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-46.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 46 (Miscellaneous Regulations Relating To Tobacco Products And Cigarette Papers And Tubes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Bonds on TTB Form 5620.10 will be terminated by the appropriate TTB officer on receipt of satisfactory evidence that the person giving the bond has disposed of the articles covered by the bond and that he bore the ultimate burden of the amount claimed and that no understanding or agreement exists whereby he will be relieved of such burden or shift such burden to another person.
