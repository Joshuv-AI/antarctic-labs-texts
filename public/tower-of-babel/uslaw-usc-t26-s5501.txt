26 U.S.C. § 5501 — Establishment
Citation: us:usc:26:5501
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section5501
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 51 (Distilled Spirits, Wines, And Beer); Subchapter H (Miscellaneous Plants And Warehouses); Part I (Vinegar Plants). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Plants for the production of vinegar by the vaporizing process, where distilled spirits of not more than 15 percent of alcohol by volume are to be produced exclusively for use in the manufacture of vinegar on the premises, may be established under this part.
