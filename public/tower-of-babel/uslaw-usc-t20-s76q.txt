20 U.S.C. § 76q — Sole national memorial to the late John F. Kennedy within the city of Washington and environs
Citation: us:usc:20:76q
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section76q
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 3 (Smithsonian Institution, National Museums And Art Galleries); Subchapter V (John F. Kennedy Center For The Performing Arts). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The John F. Kennedy Center for the Performing Arts, designated by this subchapter, shall be the sole national memorial to the late John Fitzgerald Kennedy within the city of Washington and its environs.
