34 C.F.R. § 642.31 — What are unallowable costs?
Citation: us:cfr:34:642.31
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-642.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 642 (Training Program For Federal Trio Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Costs that may not be charged against a grant under this program include the following:
(a) Research not directly related to the evaluation or improvement of the project.
(b) Construction, renovation, or remodeling of any facilities.
(c) Stipends, tuition fees, and other direct financial assistance to trainees other than those participating in internships.
