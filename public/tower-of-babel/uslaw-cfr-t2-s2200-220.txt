2 C.F.R. § 2200.220 — What contracts and subcontracts, in addition to those listed in 2 CFR 180.220, are covered transactions?
Citation: us:cfr:2:2200.220
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-2200.220
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 2200 (Nonprocurement Debarment And Suspension). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Although the OMB guidance at 2 CFR 180.220(c) allows a Federal agency to do so (also see optional lower tier coverage in the figure in the appendix to 2 CFR part 180), Corporation for National and Community Service does not extend coverage of nonprocurement suspension and debarment requirements beyond first-tier procurement contracts under a covered nonprocurement transaction.
