45 C.F.R. § 2584.124 — Conditions.
Citation: us:cfr:45:2584.124
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2584.124
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2584 (Protection Of Human Subjects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

With respect to any research project or any class of research projects the department or agency head of either the conducting or supporting Federal department or agency may impose additional conditions prior to or at the time of approval when in the judgment of the department or agency head additional conditions are necessary for the protection of human subjects.
