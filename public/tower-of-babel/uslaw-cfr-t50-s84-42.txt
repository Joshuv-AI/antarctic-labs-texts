50 C.F.R. § 84.42 — What if a grant agreement is not signed?
Citation: us:cfr:50:84.42
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-84.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 84 (National Coastal Wetlands Conservation Grant Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Monies that have been allocated for a grant will be held until December 31 of the following year. If a grant agreement has not been signed by the State and the Service and, therefore, the money has not been obligated for the approved grant by that date, the funds automatically are returned to the Program account in Washington.
