49 C.F.R. § 583.3 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:49:583.3
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-583.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 583 (Automobile Parts Content Labeling). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to manufacturers of new passenger motor vehicles manufactured or imported for sale in the United States, suppliers of passenger motor vehicle equipment, and dealers of new passenger motor vehicles.
