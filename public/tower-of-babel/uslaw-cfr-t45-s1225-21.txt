45 C.F.R. § 1225.21 — Judicial review.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1225.21
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1225.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1225 (Member And Volunteer Discrimination Complaint Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An applicant, candidate, member or volunteer is authorized to file a civil action in an appropriate U.S. District Court:
(1) Within thirty (30) calendar days of his or her receipt of the notice of final action taken by the agency; or
(2) After one hundred eighty (180) calendar days from the date of filing a formal discrimination complaint with the agency if there has been no final agency action.
