28 U.S.C. § 2404 — Death of defendant in damage action
Citation: us:usc:28:2404
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section2404
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part VI (Particular Proceedings); Chapter 161 (United States As Party Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A civil action for damages commenced by or on behalf of the United States or in which it is interested shall not abate on the death of a defendant but shall survive and be enforceable against his estate as well as against surviving defendants.
