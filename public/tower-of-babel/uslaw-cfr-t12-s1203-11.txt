12 C.F.R. § 1203.11 — Confidentiality of net worth exhibit.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1203.11
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1203.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1203 (Equal Access To Justice Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless otherwise ordered by the Director, or required by law, the statement of net worth will be for the confidential use of the adjudicative officer, the Director, and agency counsel.
