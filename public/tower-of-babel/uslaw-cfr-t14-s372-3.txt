14 C.F.R. § 372.3 — Waiver.
Citation: us:cfr:14:372.3
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-372.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 372 (Overseas Military Personnel Charters). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A waiver of any of the provisions of this part may be granted by the Department upon its own initiative, or upon the submission by a charter operator of a written request therefor: Provided, That such a waiver is in the public interest and it appears to the Department that special or unusual circumstances warrant a departure from the provisions set forth herein.
