19 U.S.C. § 4684 — Duties related to Mexico
Citation: us:usc:19:4684
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section4684
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 29 (United States–Mexico–Canada Agreement Implementation); Subchapter VI (Labor Monitoring And Enforcement); Part D (Forced Labor). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Task Force shall—
(1)
develop, in consultation with the appropriate congressional committees, an enforcement plan regarding goods produced by or with forced labor in Mexico; and
(2)
report to the Interagency Labor Committee with respect to any concerns relating to the enforcement of the prohibition under section 1307 of this title with respect to Mexico, including any allegations that may be filed with respect to forced labor in Mexico.
