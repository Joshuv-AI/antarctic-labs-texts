48 C.F.R. § 1803.901 — 1803.901 Definition.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1803.901
Creator: Chapter 18—National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1803.901
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1803 (Improper Business Practices And Personal Conflicts Of Interest). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Abuse of authority, as used in this subpart, means an arbitrary and capricious exercise of authority that is inconsistent with the mission of NASA or the successful performance of a NASA contract.
