14 C.F.R. § 254.2 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:14:254.2
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-254.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 254 (Domestic Baggage Liability). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to any air carrier that provides charter or scheduled passenger service in interstate or intrastate air transportation.
