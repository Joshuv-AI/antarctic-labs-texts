46 U.S.C. § 40706 — Exceptions
Citation: us:usc:46:40706
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section40706
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part A (Ocean Shipping); Chapter 407 (Controlled Carriers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This chapter does not apply to—
(1)
a controlled carrier of a foreign country whose vessels are entitled by a treaty of the United States to receive national or most-favored-nation treatment; or
(2)
a trade served only by controlled carriers.
