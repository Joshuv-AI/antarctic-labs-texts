36 C.F.R. § 1002.61 — Residing on Federal lands.
Citation: us:cfr:36:1002.61
Creator: Presidio Trust
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1002.61
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1002 (Resource Protection, Public Use And Recreation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Residing within the area administered by the Presidio Trust, other than on privately owned lands, except pursuant to the terms and conditions of a permit, lease or contract, is prohibited.
(b) Violation of the terms and conditions of a permit issued in accordance with this section is prohibited and may result in the suspension or revocation of the permit.
