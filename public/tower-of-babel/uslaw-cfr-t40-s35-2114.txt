40 C.F.R. § 35.2114 — Value engineering.

Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-35.2114
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 35 (State And Local Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If the project has not received Step 2 grant assistance the applicant shall conduct value engineering if the total estimated cost of building the treatment works is more than $10 million.
(b) The value engineering recommendations shall be implemented to the maximum extent feasible.
