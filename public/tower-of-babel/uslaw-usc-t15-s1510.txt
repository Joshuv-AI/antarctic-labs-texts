15 U.S.C. § 1510 — Clerical assistants
Citation: us:usc:15:1510
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 40 (Department Of Commerce). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There shall also be such clerical assistants as may from time to time be authorized by the Congress.
