30 C.F.R. § 14.20 — Flame resistance.
Citation: us:cfr:30:14.20
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-14.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 14 (Requirements For The Approval Of Flame-Resistant Conveyor Belts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Conveyor belts for use in underground coal mines must be flame-resistant and:
(a) Tested in accordance with § 14.22 of this part; or
(b) Tested in accordance with an alternate test determined by MSHA to be equivalent under 30 CFR §§ 6.20 and 14.4(e).
