20 C.F.R. § 411.410 — Does each referral from an EN to a State VR agency require its own agreement?
Citation: us:cfr:20:411.410
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-411.410
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 411 (The Ticket To Work And Self-Sufficiency Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No. The agreements between ENs and State VR agencies should be broad-based and apply to all beneficiaries who may be referred by the EN to the State VR agency for services, although an EN and a State VR agency may want to enter into an individualized agreement to meet the needs of a single beneficiary.
