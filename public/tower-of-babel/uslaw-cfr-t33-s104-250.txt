33 C.F.R. § 104.250 — Procedures for interfacing with facilities and other vessels.
Citation: us:cfr:33:104.250
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-104.250
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 104 (Maritime Security: Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The vessel owner or operator must ensure that there are measures for interfacing with facilities and other vessels at all MARSEC Levels.
(b) For each U.S. flag vessel that calls on foreign ports or facilities, the vessel owner or operator must ensure procedures for interfacing with those ports and facilities are established.
