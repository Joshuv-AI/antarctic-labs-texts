33 C.F.R. § 5.24 — Procedure for assignment to duty.
Citation: us:cfr:33:5.24
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-5.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 5 (Coast Guard Auxiliary). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Members and facilities may be assigned to duty by any of the following procedures:
(a) Verbal or written orders issued by competent Coast Guard authority;
(b) The actual performance of an authorized activity or mission by a qualified member of the Auxiliary; or
(c) Other procedures as designated by the Commandant.
