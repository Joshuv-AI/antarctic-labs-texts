42 U.S.C. § 8853 — Lending for energy production and conservation projects by production credit associations, Federal land banks, and banks for cooperatives
Citation: us:usc:42:8853
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section8853
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 96 (Biomass Energy And Alcohol Fuels); Subchapter III (Rural, Agricultural, And Forestry Biomass Energy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Farm Credit Administration shall encourage production credit associations, Federal land banks, and banks for cooperatives to use existing authorities to make loans to eligible persons for commercially feasible biomass energy projects.
