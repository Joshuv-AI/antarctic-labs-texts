10 C.F.R. § 820.81 — Amount of penalty.
Citation: us:cfr:10:820.81
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-820.81
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 820 (Procedural Rules For Doe Nuclear Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person subject to a penalty under 42 U.S.C. 2282a shall be subject to a civil penalty in an amount not to exceed $262,614 for each such violation. If any violation under 42 U.S.C. 2282a is a continuing one, each day of such violation shall constitute a separate violation for the purpose of computing the applicable civil penalty.
