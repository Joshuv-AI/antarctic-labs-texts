20 C.F.R. § 225.42 — Notice of the percentage amount of a cost-of-living increase.
Citation: us:cfr:20:225.42
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-225.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 225 (Primary Insurance Amount Determinations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The percentage amount of the cost-of-living increase is published in the Federal Register by the Secretary of Health and Human Services within 45 days of the end of the measuring period used in finding the increase.
