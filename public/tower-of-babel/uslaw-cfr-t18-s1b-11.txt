18 C.F.R. § 1b.11 — b.11 Limitation on participation.
Citation: us:cfr:18:1b.11
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-1b.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 1b (PART 1b—RULES RELATING TO INVESTIGATIONS). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

There are no parties, as that term is used in adjudicative proceedings, in an investigation under this part and no person may intervene or participate as a matter of right in any investigation under this part.
