45 C.F.R. § 286.90 — How many hours per week must an adult or minor head-of-household participate in work-related activities to count in the numerator of the work participation rate?
Citation: us:cfr:45:286.90
Creator: Office Of Family Assistance (Assistance Programs), Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-286.90
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 286 (Tribal Tanf Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

During the month, an adult or minor head-of-household must participate in work activities for at least the minimum average number of hours per week specified in the Tribe's approved Tribal Family Assistance Plan.
