25 C.F.R. § 137.7 — Private ownership defined.
Citation: us:cfr:25:137.7
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-137.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 137 (Reimbursement Of Construction Costs, San Carlos Indian Irrigation Project, Arizona). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term “private ownership” used in this public notice includes all lands of the San Carlos irrigation project that have or may be designated by the Secretary of the Interior that are situated outside of the boundaries of the Gila River Indian Reservation.
