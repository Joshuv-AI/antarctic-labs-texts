42 U.S.C. § 2000d–4 — Federal authority and financial assistance to programs or activities by way of contract of insurance or guaranty
Citation: us:usc:42:2000d–4
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2000d–4
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 21 (Civil Rights); Subchapter V (Federally Assisted Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this subchapter shall add to or detract from any existing authority with respect to any program or activity under which Federal financial assistance is extended by way of a contract of insurance or guaranty.
