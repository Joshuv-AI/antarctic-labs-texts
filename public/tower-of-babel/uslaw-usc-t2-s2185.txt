2 U.S.C. § 2185 — Estimates for improvements in grounds
Citation: us:usc:2:2185
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section2185
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 30 (Operation And Maintenance Of Capitol Complex); Subchapter VIII (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All changes and improvements in the Capitol grounds, including approaches to the Capitol, shall be estimated for in detail, showing what modifications are proposed and the estimate cost of the same.
