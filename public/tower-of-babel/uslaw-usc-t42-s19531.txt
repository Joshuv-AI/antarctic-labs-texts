42 U.S.C. § 19531 — Severability
Citation: us:usc:42:19531
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section19531
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 165 (Increasing Housing Supply); Subchapter III (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If any provision of this Act, or the application thereof to any person or circumstance, is held invalid, the remainder of the Act, and the application of such provisions to other persons or circumstances, shall not be affected thereby.
