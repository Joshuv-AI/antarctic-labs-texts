16 C.F.R. § 239.4 — “Lifetime” and similar representations.
Citation: us:cfr:16:239.4
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-239.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 239 (Guides For The Advertising Of Warranties And Guarantees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an advertisement uses “lifetime,” “life,” or similar representations to describe the duration of a warranty or guarantee, then the advertisement should disclose, with such clarity and prominence as will be noticed and understood by prospective purchasers, the life to which the representation refers.
