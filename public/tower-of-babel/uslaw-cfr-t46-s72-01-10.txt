46 C.F.R. § 72.01-10 — -10 Vessels using fuel having a flashpoint of 110 degrees F. or lower.
Citation: us:cfr:46:72.01-10
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-72.01-10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 72 (Construction And Arrangement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Where liquid fuel having a flashpoint of 110 degrees F. or lower is carried for main or auxiliary machinery or for starting purposes, such machinery and fuel tanks shall be in separate vapor tight compartments separating each from the other and from the remainder of the vessel.
