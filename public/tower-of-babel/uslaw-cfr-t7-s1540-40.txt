7 C.F.R. § 1540.40 — Applicability of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1540.40
Creator: Foreign Agricultural Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1540.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1540 (International Agricultural Trade). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to requests for emergency relief from duty-free imports of perishable products filed with the Department of Agriculture under section 204(e) of the Andean Trade Preference Act, title II of Public Law 102-182, 105 Stat. 1236 (19 U.S.C. 3201 et seq.) (the “Act”).
