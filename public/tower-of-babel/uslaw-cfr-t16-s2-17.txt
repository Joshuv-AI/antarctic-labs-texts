16 C.F.R. § 2.17 — Statutory delays of notifications and prohibitions of disclosure.
Citation: us:cfr:16:2.17
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-2.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 2 (Nonadjudicative Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon authorization by the Commissioner who issues compulsory process pursuant to § 2.7(a) or, alternatively, upon authorization by the General Counsel, Commission attorneys may seek to delay notifications or prohibit disclosures pursuant to the Right to Financial Privacy Act (12 U.S.C. 3409), the Electronic Communications Privacy Act (18 U.S.C. 2705), or section 7 of the U.S. SAFE WEB Act (15 U.S.C. 57b-2a).
