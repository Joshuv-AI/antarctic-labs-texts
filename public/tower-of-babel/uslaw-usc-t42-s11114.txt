42 U.S.C. § 11114 — Guidelines of Secretary
Citation: us:usc:42:11114
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section11114
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 117 (Encouraging Good Faith Professional Review Activities); Subchapter I (Promotion Of Professional Review Activities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may establish, after notice and opportunity for comment, such voluntary guidelines as may assist the professional review bodies in meeting the standards described in section 11112(a) of this title.
