20 C.F.R. § 229.33 — When a divorced spouse can be included in the computation of the overall minimum rate.
Citation: us:cfr:20:229.33
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-229.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 229 (Social Security Overall Minimum Guarantee). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A divorced spouse annuitant can be included in the computation of the overall minimum rate in the later of the month in which:
(1) The employee first is eligible for an increase in his or her annuity rate under the overall minimum, as shown in § 229.22; or
(2) The divorced spouse annuity begins.
