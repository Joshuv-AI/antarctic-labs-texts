48 C.F.R. § 3027.305-4 — 3027.305-4 Conveyance of invention rights acquired by the Government.
Citation: us:cfr:48:3027.305-4
Creator: Chapter 30—Department Of Homeland Security, Homeland Security Acquisition Regulation (Hsar)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-3027.305-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 3027 (Patents, Data, And Copyrights). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer shall ensure that solicitations and contracts which include a patent rights clause include a means for the contractor to report inventions made in the course of contract performance and at contract completion. This requirement may be fulfilled by requiring the contractor to submit a DD Form 882, Report of Inventions and Subcontract.
