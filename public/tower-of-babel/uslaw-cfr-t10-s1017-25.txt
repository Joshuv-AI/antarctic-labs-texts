10 C.F.R. § 1017.25 — Reproduction.
Citation: us:cfr:10:1017.25
Creator: Department Of Energy (General Provisions)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1017.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1017 (Identification And Protection Of Unclassified Controlled Nuclear Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A document marked as containing UCNI may be reproduced without the permission of the originator to the minimum extent necessary consistent with the need to carry out official duties, provided the reproduced document is marked and protected in the same manner as the original document.
