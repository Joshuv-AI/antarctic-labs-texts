21 C.F.R. § 111.515 — When must a returned dietary supplement be destroyed, or otherwise suitably disposed of?
Citation: us:cfr:21:111.515
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-111.515
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 111 (Current Good Manufacturing Practice In Manufacturing, Packaging, Labeling, Or Holding Operations For Dietary Supplements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must destroy, or otherwise suitably dispose of, any returned dietary supplement unless the outcome of a material review and disposition decision is that quality control personnel do the following:
(a) Approve the salvage of the returned dietary supplement for redistribution or
(b) Approve the returned dietary supplement for reprocessing.
