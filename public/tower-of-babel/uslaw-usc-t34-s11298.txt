34 U.S.C. § 11298 — Authority of Inspectors General
Citation: us:usc:34:11298
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section11298
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 111 (Juvenile Justice And Delinquency Prevention); Subchapter IV (Missing Children). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
An Inspector General appointed under section 403 or 415 of title 5 may authorize staff to assist the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children—
(1)
by conducting reviews of inactive case files to develop recommendations for further investigations; and
(2)
by engaging in similar activities.
(b) Limitations
(1) Priority
An Inspector General may not permit staff to engage in activities described in subsection (a) if such activities will interfere with the duties of the Inspector General under chapter 4 of title 5.
(2) Funding
No additional funds are authorized to be appropriated to carry out this section.
