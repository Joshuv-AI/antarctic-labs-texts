34 C.F.R. § 387.41 — What are allowable costs?
Citation: us:cfr:34:387.41
Creator: Office Of Special Education And Rehabilitative Services, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-387.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 387 (Innovative Rehabilitation Training). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to those allowable costs established under 34 CFR 75.530-75.562, the following items are allowable under Innovative Rehabilitation training projects—
(a) Student stipends;
(b) Tuition and fees; and
(c) Student travel in conjunction with training assignments.
