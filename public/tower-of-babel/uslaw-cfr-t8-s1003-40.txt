8 C.F.R. § 1003.40 — Local operating procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:8:1003.40
Creator: Executive Office For Immigration Review, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-1003.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 1003 (Executive Office For Immigration Review). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An Immigration Court having administrative control over Records of Proceedings may establish local operating procedures, provided that:
(a) Such operating procedure(s) shall not be inconsistent with any provision of this chapter;
(b) A majority of the judges of the local Immigration Court shall concur in writing therein; and
(c) The Chief Immigration Judge has approved the proposed operating procedure(s) in writing.
