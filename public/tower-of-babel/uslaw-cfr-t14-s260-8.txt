14 C.F.R. § 260.8 — Disclosing material restrictions, conditions, or limitations.
Citation: us:cfr:14:260.8
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-260.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 260 (Refunds For Airline Fare And Ancillary Service Fees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In carrying out the requirements of § 260.6(c), a covered carrier must clearly and conspicuously disclose, no later than at the time of voucher or credit offer, any material restrictions, limitations, or conditions on travel credits, vouchers, or other compensation, including but not limited to validity period, advance purchase requirement, capacity restrictions, and blackout dates, regardless of whether consumers are entitled to a refund.
