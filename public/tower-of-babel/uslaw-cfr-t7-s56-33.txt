7 C.F.R. § 56.33 — Report of violations.
Citation: us:cfr:7:56.33
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-56.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 56 (Voluntary Grading Of Shell Eggs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each grader shall report, in the manner prescribed by the Administrator, all violations of and noncompliance with the Act and the regulations in this part of which such grader has knowledge.
