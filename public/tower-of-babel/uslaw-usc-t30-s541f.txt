30 U.S.C. § 541f — Disbursement of moneys
Citation: us:usc:30:541f
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section541f
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 12A (Entry And Location On Coal Lands On Discovery Of Source Material). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All moneys received under the provisions of this chapter shall be paid into the Treasury of the United States and distributed in the same manner as provided in section 191 of this title and sections 437, 438, and 439 1 of title 48.
