31 C.F.R. § 561.313 — Financial services.
Citation: us:cfr:31:561.313
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-561.313
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 561 (Iranian Financial Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term financial services includes loans, transfers, accounts, insurance, investments, securities, guarantees, foreign exchange, letters of credit, and commodity futures or options.
