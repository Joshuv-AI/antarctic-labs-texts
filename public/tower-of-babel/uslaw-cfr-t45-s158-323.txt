45 C.F.R. § 158.323 — State contact information.
Citation: us:cfr:45:158.323
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-158.323
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 158 (Issuer Use Of Premium Revenue: Reporting And Rebate Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A State must provide the name, telephone number, e-mail address, and mailing address of the person the Secretary may contact regarding the request for an adjustment to the MLR standard.
