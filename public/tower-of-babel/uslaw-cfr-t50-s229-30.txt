50 C.F.R. § 229.30 — Basis.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 229 (Authorization For Commercial Fisheries Under The Marine Mammal Protection Act Of 1972). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Section 118(f)(9) of the Act authorizes the Director, NMFS, to impose regulations governing commercial fishing operations, when necessary, to implement a take reduction plan in order to protect or restore a marine mammal stock or species covered by such a plan.
