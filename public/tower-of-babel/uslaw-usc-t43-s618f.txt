43 U.S.C. § 618f — Repayment of advances for flood control
Citation: us:usc:43:618f
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section618f
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 12A (Boulder Canyon Project); Subchapter II (Boulder Canyon Project Adjustment Act). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The first $25,000,000 of advances made to the Colorado River Dam Fund for the project shall be deemed to be the sum allocated to flood control by section 617a(b) of this title and repayment thereof shall be deferred without interest until June 1, 1987, after which time such advances so allocated to flood control shall be repayable to the Treasury as the Congress shall determine.
