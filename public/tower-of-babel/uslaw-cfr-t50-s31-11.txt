50 C.F.R. § 31.11 — Donation and loan of wildlife specimens.
Citation: us:cfr:50:31.11
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-31.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 31 (Wildlife Species Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Wildlife specimens may be donated or loaned to public institutions for specific purposes. Donation or loans of resident species of wildlife will not be made unless the recipient has secured the approval of the State.
