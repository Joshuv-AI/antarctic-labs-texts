49 C.F.R. § 1122.8 — Limitation on participation.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1122.8
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1122.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1122 (Board-Initiated Investigations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No party who is not the subject of a Board-Initiated Investigation may intervene or participate as a matter of right in any such Board-Initiated Investigation under this part.
