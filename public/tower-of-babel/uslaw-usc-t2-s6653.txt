2 U.S.C. § 6653 — Postage allowance for Chaplain of Senate
Citation: us:usc:2:6653
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section6653
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 65 (Senate Officers And Administration); Subchapter IV (Chaplain). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Senate is authorized and directed to procure and furnish each fiscal year (commencing with the fiscal year ending September 30, 1982) to the Chaplain of the Senate, upon the request of the Chaplain of the Senate, United States postage stamps in such amounts as may be necessary for the mailing of postal matters arising in connection with his official business.
