45 C.F.R. § 162.1401 — Health care claim status transaction.
Citation: us:cfr:45:162.1401
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-162.1401
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 162 (Administrative Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The health care claim status transaction is the transmission of either of the following:
(a) An inquiry from a health care provider to a health plan to determine the status of a health care claim.
(b) A response from a health plan to a health care provider about the status of a health care claim.
