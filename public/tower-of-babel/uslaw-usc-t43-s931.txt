43 U.S.C. § 931 — Navigable rivers as public highways
Citation: us:usc:43:931
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section931
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 22 (Rights-Of-Way And Other Easements In Public Lands). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All navigable rivers, within the territory occupied by the public lands, shall remain and be deemed public highways; and, in all cases where the opposite banks of any streams not navigable belong to different persons, the stream and the bed thereof shall become common to both.
