12 U.S.C. § 2125 — Dividends
Citation: us:usc:12:2125
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section2125
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 23 (Farm Credit System); Subchapter III (Banks For Cooperatives); Part A (Banks For Cooperatives). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Dividends may be payable only on nonvoting investment stock, if declared by the board of directors of the bank, subject to the general direction of the Farm Credit Administration.
