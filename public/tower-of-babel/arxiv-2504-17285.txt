Some remarks on Liouville type theorems for the 3D steady tropical climate model
Yanyan Dong, Zhibing Zhang

Abstract
. Observing the special structure of the system and using the Poincaré-Sobolev inequality, we establish Liouville type theorems for the 3D steady tropical climate model under certain conditions on u u , v v , ∇ θ \nabla\theta . Our results extend and improve a Liouville type result of Cho-In-Yang (arXiv:2312.17441).

1.
Introduction

We consider the following stationary tropical climate model in three dimension

\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}-\Delta u+(u\cdot\nabla)u+\nabla\pi+\divg(v\otimes v)=0,\\
-\Delta v+(u\cdot\nabla)v+\nabla\theta+(v\cdot\nabla)u=0,\\
-\Delta\theta+u\cdot\nabla\theta+\divg v=0,\\
\divg u=0,\end{array}\right.

(1.1)

where
u=(u_{1},u_{2},u_{3})
is the barotropic mode,
v=(v_{1},v_{2},v_{3})
is the first baroclinic mode of vector velocity,
\theta
is the temperature, and
\pi
is the pressure.

To our knowledge, there are relatively few Liouville type results for the tropical climate models so far. Ding and Wu
[
4
]
investigated the tropical climate model and proved Liouville type theorems under one of the following assumptions

\displaystyle(\mathrm{i})~~~~u\in L^{p}(\mathbb{R}^{3})~~~~~~~~~~~~\text{and}~~~~~~~~~~~~v,\theta\in L^{p}(\mathbb{R}^{3})\cap L^{3}(\mathbb{R}^{3})~~~~~~~~~~~~\text{with}~~~~~~~~~~~~3\leq p\leq\frac{9}{2},

\displaystyle(\mathrm{ii})~~~~u\in L^{p,q}(\mathbb{R}^{3})~~~~~~~~~~~~\text{and}~~~~~~~~~~~~v,\theta\in L^{p,q}(\mathbb{R}^{3})\cap L^{3,q}~~~~~~~~~~~~\text{with}~~~~~~~~~~~~3\leq p\leq\frac{9}{2},\;3<q<+\infty,

\displaystyle(\mathrm{iii})~~~~u\in L^{3}(\mathbb{R}^{3}),v\in L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{3})~~~~~~~~~~~~\text{and}~~~~~~~~~~~~\nabla u,\nabla v,\nabla\theta\in L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{3}),

where
L^{p,q}(\mathbb{R}^{3})
is the Lorentz space. Later, Yuan and Wang
[
9
]
extended their results to local Morrey spaces. Cho et al.
[
2
]
improved the result of Ding and Wu by removing the condition
\nabla u,\nabla v\in L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{3})
from the above assumption
(\mathrm{iii})
.
Recently, Cho et al.
[
3
]
established an improved Liouville type theorem for (
1.1
) under one of the following conditions

\displaystyle(\mathrm{i})\;p\in\left(\frac{3}{2},3\right),\;q,r\in[1,2),\;\liminf_{R\rightarrow+\infty}(G(u;p,R)+H(v;q,R)+H(\theta;r,R))<+\infty,

(1.2a)

\displaystyle(\mathrm{ii})\liminf_{R\rightarrow+\infty}(G(u;3,R)+H(v;2,R))=0,\;\limsup_{R\rightarrow+\infty}H(\theta;2,R)<+\infty,

(1.2b)

\displaystyle(\mathrm{iii})\liminf_{R\rightarrow+\infty}(G(u;3,R)+H(\theta;2,R))=0,\;\limsup_{R\rightarrow+\infty}H(v;2,R)<+\infty,

(1.2c)

where the functions
G
and
H
are defined by

G(f;p,R):=R^{-\left(\frac{2}{p}-\frac{1}{3}\right)}\|f\|_{L^{p}(B_{2R}\backslash B_{R/2})}~~~~~~~~~~~~and~~~~~~~~~~~~H(f;p,R):=R^{-\left(\frac{3}{2p}-\frac{1}{4}\right)}\|f\|_{L^{p}(B_{2R}\backslash B_{R/2})}.

Very recently, Fang and Zhang
[
5
]
extended and improved the work of Cho et al.
[
3
]
by combining an iteration argument and some delicate estimates of several integrals.

\displaystyle C\left(\|u\|_{L^{6}(B_{2R}\backslash B_{R})}^{2}+\|v\|_{L^{6}(B_{2R}\backslash B_{R})}^{2}+\|\nabla\theta\|_{L^{2}(B_{2R}\backslash B_{R})}^{2}\right)+\frac{1}{2}\|\nabla u\|_{L^{2}(B_{2R}\backslash B_{R})}^{2}

Consequently, we derive that

\displaystyle f(R)\leq

\displaystyle C\left(\|u\|_{L^{6}(B_{2R}\backslash B_{R})}^{2}+\|v\|_{L^{6}(B_{2R}\backslash B_{R})}^{2}+\|\nabla\theta\|_{L^{2}(B_{2R}\backslash B_{R})}^{2}\right)+\frac{1}{2}\|\nabla u\|_{L^{2}(B_{2R}\backslash B_{R})}^{2}

\displaystyle+CR^{\frac{3p}{6-p}\alpha-1}X_{\alpha,p,R}^{\frac{3p}{6-p}}\|u\|_{L^{6}(B_{2R}\backslash B_{R})}^{\frac{18-6p}{6-p}}+CR^{-1+\alpha+\frac{2(3-p^{\prime})q}{(6-q)p^{\prime}}\beta}X_{\alpha,p,R}Y_{\beta,q,R}^{\frac{2(3-p^{\prime})q}{(6-q)p^{\prime}}}\|v\|_{L^{6}(B_{2R}\backslash B_{R})}^{\frac{12p^{\prime}-6q}{(6-q)p^{\prime}}}
