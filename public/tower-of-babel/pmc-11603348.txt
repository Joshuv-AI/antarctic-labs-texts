Author Correction: From wearables to performance: how acceptance of IoT devices influences physical education results in college students
Authors: Yuming Xu, Jun Peng, Fengshi Jing, Hao Ren
Published: 2024

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-024-75071-3, published online 10 October 2024

The original version of this Article contained an error, where the university of the study participants was incorrectly stated as ‘Guangzhou University of Finance’.

As a result, in the Abstract,

“The study enrolled 150 first-year students from Guangzhou University of Finance, divided equally into an experimental group and a control group.”

now reads:

“The study enrolled 150 first-year students from Guangdong University of Finance & Economics, divided equally into an experimental group and a control group.”

In addition, in the Materials and methods section, under the subheading ‘Participants’,

“This study enrolled 150 first-year students from Guangzhou University of Finance as participants.”

now reads:

“This study enrolled 150 first-year students from Guangdong University of Finance & Economics as participants.”

The original Article has been corrected.
