26 U.S.C. § 2515 — Treatment of generation-skipping transfer tax
Citation: us:usc:26:2515
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section2515
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 12 (Gift Tax); Subchapter B (Transfers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In the case of any taxable gift which is a direct skip (within the meaning of chapter 13), the amount of such gift shall be increased by the amount of any tax imposed on the transferor under chapter 13 with respect to such gift.
