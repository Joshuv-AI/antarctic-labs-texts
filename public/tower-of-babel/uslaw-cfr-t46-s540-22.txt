46 C.F.R. § 540.22 — Proof of financial responsibility, when required.
Citation: us:cfr:46:540.22
Creator: Federal Maritime Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-540.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 540 (Passenger Vessel Financial Responsibility). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No vessel shall embark passengers at U.S. ports unless a Certificate (Casualty) has been issued to or covers the owner or charterer of such vessel.
