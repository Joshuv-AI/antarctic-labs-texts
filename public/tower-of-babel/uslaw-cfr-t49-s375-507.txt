49 C.F.R. § 375.507 — Must I determine the weight of a shipment?
Citation: us:cfr:49:375.507
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-375.507
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 375 (Transportation Of Household Goods In Interstate Commerce; Consumer Protection Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) When you transport household goods on a non-binding estimate dependent upon the shipment weight, you must determine the weight of each shipment transported before the assessment of any charges.
(b) You must weigh the shipment upon a certified scale.
(c) You must provide a written explanation of volume to weight conversions, when you provide an estimate by volume and convert the volume to weight.
