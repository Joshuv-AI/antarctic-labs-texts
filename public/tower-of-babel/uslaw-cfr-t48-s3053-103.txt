48 C.F.R. § 3053.103 — 3053.103 Exceptions.

Creator: Chapter 30—Department Of Homeland Security, Homeland Security Acquisition Regulation (Hsar)
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 3053 (Forms). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Requests for exceptions to forms contained in (FAR) 48 CFR part 53 and to DHS forms in (HSAR) 48 CFR part 3053 shall be submitted, as prescribed in (FAR) 48 CFR 53.103, to the CPO.
