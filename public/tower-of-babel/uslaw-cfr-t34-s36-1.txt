34 C.F.R. § 36.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:34:36.1
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-36.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 36 (Adjustment Of Civil Monetary Penalties For Inflation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to make inflation adjustments to the civil monetary penalties within the jurisdiction of the Department of Education. These penalties are subject to review and adjustment as necessary at least once every 4 years in accordance with the Federal Civil Penalties Inflation Adjustment Act of 1990, as amended.
