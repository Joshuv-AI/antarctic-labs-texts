\displaystyle\ \ \ \ \ \frac{\partial}{\partial t}\arctan\left(\frac{\Im\xi(s)}{\Re\xi(s)}\right)

\displaystyle\frac{\frac{\partial\Im\xi(s)}{\partial t}\Re\xi(s)-\frac{\partial\Re\xi(s)}{\partial t}\Im\xi(s)}{\left(\Re\xi(s)\right)^{2}+\left(\Im\xi(s)\right)^{2}}=\frac{\partial\angle[\xi(s)]}{\partial t}
