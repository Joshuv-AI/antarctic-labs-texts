40 C.F.R. § 57.401 — General requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:40:57.401
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-57.401
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 57 (Primary Nonferrous Smelter Orders). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided in subpart E, each NSO shall require the smelter owner to prevent all violations of the NAAQS in the smelter's designated liability area (DLA) through the operation of an approved supplementary control system (SCS).
