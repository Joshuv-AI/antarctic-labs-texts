47 C.F.R. § 8.207 — Responsible party.
Citation: us:cfr:47:8.207
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-8.207
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 8 (Part 8— INTERNET TRANSPARENCY FOR CONSUMERS). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In the case of a complying consumer IoT product that has been granted authorization to use the FCC IoT Label, the applicant to whom that grant of cybersecurity labeling authorization is issued is responsible for continued compliance with the program requirements for continued use of the FCC IoT Label.
