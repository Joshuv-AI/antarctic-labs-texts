50 C.F.R. § 260.31 — Application for SIP Approved Establishment.
Citation: us:cfr:50:260.31
Creator: National Marine Fisheries Service, National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-260.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 260 (Inspection And Certification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person desiring to process and pack fish and fishery products and other marine ingredients as an SIP Approved Establishment must receive approval of their written and implemented food management system per the application procedures which are detailed in the SIP Manual.
