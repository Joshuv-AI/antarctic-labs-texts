30 C.F.R. § 750.16 — Performance standards.
Citation: us:cfr:30:750.16
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-750.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 750 (Requirements For Surface Coal Mining And Reclamation Operations On Indian Lands). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After OSM issues a permit under this part, a person conducting surface coal mining operations on Indian lands shall do so in accordance with parts 816, 817, 819, 822, 823, 824, 827, and 828 of this chapter. Prior to that time, the person conducting surface coal mining and reclamation operations shall adhere to the performance standards of 30 CFR chapter VII, subchapter B.
