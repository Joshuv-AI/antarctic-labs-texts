A high-performance general computer cursor control scheme based on a hybrid BCI combining motor imagery and eye-tracking
Authors: Jiakai Zhang, Yuqi Zhang, Xinlong Zhang, Boyang Xu, Huanqing Zhao, Tinghui Sun, Ju Wang, Shaojie Lu, Xiaoyan Shen
Published: 2026

Main text

(iScience 27, 110164; June 21, 2024)

In the originally published version of this article, the EEG dataset deposited and made available during post-publication review did not correspond to the original acquisition layer data underlying the study. Due to a post-acceptance data-archiving error, duplicated EEG files were present across different subject identifiers in the deposited dataset.

The authors have since provided the correct acquisition layer EEG data, consisting of the original OpenViBE files generated during data collection, along with supporting materials to verify data integrity. These data were reviewed by one of the original reviewers and found to be technically consistent. The originally deposited dataset has been replaced, and the correct dataset is now available: https://github.com/eytydgshib/MI-BCI-real. The authors apologize for any confusion this error may have caused.
