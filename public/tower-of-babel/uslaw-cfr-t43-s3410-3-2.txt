43 C.F.R. § 3410.3-2 — -2 Limitations on exploration licenses.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3410.3-2
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3410.3-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3410 (Exploration Licenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The issuance of exploration licenses for an area shall not preclude the issuance of a Federal coal lease under applicable regulations for that area. If such a lease is issued for lands included in an exploration license, the authorized officer shall cancel the exploration license on the effective date of the lease for those lands which are common to both.
