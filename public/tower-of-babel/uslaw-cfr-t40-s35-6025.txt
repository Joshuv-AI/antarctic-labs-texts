40 C.F.R. § 35.6025 — Exceptions from this subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:40:35.6025
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-35.6025
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 35 (State And Local Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

On a case-by-case basis, EPA will consider requests for official exceptions from the non-statutory provisions of this subpart. Refer to the requirements regarding exceptions described in 2 CFR 1500.4.
