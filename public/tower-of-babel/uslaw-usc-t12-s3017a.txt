12 U.S.C. § 3017a — Class A notes as paid-in capital of the Bank
Citation: us:usc:12:3017a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section3017a
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 31 (National Consumer Cooperative Bank); Subchapter I (Establishment And Operation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Only for purposes of section 3017(a) of this title, class A notes shall be deemed to be paid-in capital of the Bank.
