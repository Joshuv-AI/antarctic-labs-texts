30 C.F.R. § 1202.352 — Minimum royalty.
Citation: us:cfr:30:1202.352
Creator: Office Of Natural Resources Revenue, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-1202.352
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 1202 (Royalties). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In no event shall the lessee's annual royalty payments for any producing lease be less than the minimum royalty established by the lease.
