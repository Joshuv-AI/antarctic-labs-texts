36 C.F.R. § 1206.24 — What type of proposal is ineligible for a publications grant?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1206.24
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1206.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1206 (National Historical Publications And Records Commission). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Commission does not support:
(1) Historical research apart from what is necessary for editing documentary publications; or
(2) Documentary editing projects to publish the papers of someone who has been deceased for fewer than ten years.
(b) Other programmatic limitations established by the Commission are found in the grant opportunity announcements and the NHPRC Web site.
