7 C.F.R. § 65.218 — Pre-labeled.
Citation: us:cfr:7:65.218
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-65.218
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 65 (Country Of Origin Labeling Of Lamb, Chicken, And Goat Meat, Perishable Agricultural Commodities, Macadamia Nuts, Pecans, Peanuts, And Ginseng). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Pre-labeled means a covered commodity that has the commodity's country of origin and the name and place of business of the manufacturer, packer, or distributor on the covered commodity itself, on the package in which it is sold to the consumer, or on the master shipping container. The place of business information must include at a minimum the city and state or other acceptable locale designation.
