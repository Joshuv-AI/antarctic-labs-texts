9 C.F.R. § 319.280 — Scrapple.
Citation: us:cfr:9:319.280
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-319.280
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 319 (Definitions And Standards Of Identity Or Composition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

“Scrapple” shall contain not less than 40 percent meat and/or meat byproducts computed on the basis of the fresh weight, exclusive of bone. Mechanically Separated (Species) may be used in accordance with § 319.6. The meal or flour used may be derived from grain and/or soybeans.
