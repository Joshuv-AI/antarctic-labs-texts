25 U.S.C. § 1487 — Loans eligible for insurance
Citation: us:usc:25:1487
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section1487
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 17 (Financing Economic Development Of Indians And Indian Organizations); Subchapter II (Loan Guaranty And Insurance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any loans insured hereunder shall be restricted to those made by a financial institution subject to examination and supervision by an agency of the United States, a State, or the District of Columbia, and to loans made by Indian organizations from their own funds to other tribes or organizations of Indians.
