32 C.F.R. § 865.123 — Approval of exceptions to directive.
Citation: us:cfr:32:865.123
Creator: Department Of The Air Force
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-865.123
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 865 (Personnel Review Boards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Only the Secretary of the Air Force may authorize or approve a waiver of, or exception to, any part of this subpart.
