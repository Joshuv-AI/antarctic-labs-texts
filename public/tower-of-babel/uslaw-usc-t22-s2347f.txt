22 U.S.C. § 2347f — Consultation requirement
Citation: us:usc:22:2347f
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section2347f
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 32 (Foreign Assistance); Subchapter II (Military Assistance And Sales); Part V (International Military Education And Training). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The selection of foreign personnel for training under this part shall be made in consultation with the United States defense attache to the relevant country.
