48 C.F.R. § 1001.104 — 1001.104 Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1001.104
Creator: Chapter 10—Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1001.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1001 (Department Of The Treasury Acquisition Regulation (Dtar) System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The DTAR applies to all acquisitions of supplies and services, which obligate appropriated funds. For acquisitions made from non-appropriated funds, the Senior Procurement Executive will determine the rules and procedures that will apply. The DTAR does not apply to the acquisitions of the U.S. Mint.
