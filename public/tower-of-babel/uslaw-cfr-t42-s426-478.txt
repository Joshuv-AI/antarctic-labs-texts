42 C.F.R. § 426.478 — Retiring or revising an LCD during the Board's review of an ALJ's decision.
Citation: us:cfr:42:426.478
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-426.478
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 426 (Review Of National Coverage Determinations And Local Coverage Determinations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A contractor may retire or revise an LCD during the Board's review of an ALJ's decision using the same process described in § 426.420. If an LCD is retired or revised to remove completely the challenged provision(s), the aggrieved party who sought the review is entitled to individual claim review provided at § 426.488(b).
