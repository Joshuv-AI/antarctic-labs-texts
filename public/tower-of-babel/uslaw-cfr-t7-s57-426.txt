7 C.F.R. § 57.426 — Retention.
Citation: us:cfr:7:57.426
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-57.426
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 57 (Inspection Of Eggs (Egg Products Inspection Act)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Retention tags or other devices and methods as may be approved by the Administrator shall be used for the identification and control of products which are not in compliance with the regulations or are held for further examination. No product, shall be released for use until it has been made acceptable. Such identification shall not be removed by anyone other than an inspector.
