42 U.S.C. § 13261 — Prohibited acts
Citation: us:usc:42:13261
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section13261
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 134 (Energy Policy); Subchapter III (Availability And Use Of Replacement Fuels, Alternative Fuels, And Alternative Fueled Private Vehicles). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It shall be unlawful for any person to violate any provision of section 13251, 13253(b), 13257, or 13263a of this title, or any regulation issued under such sections.
