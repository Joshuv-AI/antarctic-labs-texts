5 C.F.R. § 2418.3 — Do these regulations adopt the Federal Claims Collection Standards (FCCS)?
Citation: us:cfr:5:2418.3
Creator: Federal Labor Relations Authority, General Counsel Of The Federal Labor Relations Authority And Federal Service Impasses Panel
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-2418.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 2418 (Flra Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part adopts and incorporates all provisions of the FCCS. This part also supplements the FCCS by prescribing procedures consistent with the FCCS, as necessary and appropriate for FLRA operations.
