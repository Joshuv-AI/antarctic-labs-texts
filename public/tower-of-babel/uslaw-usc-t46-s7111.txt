46 U.S.C. § 7111 — Oral examinations for licenses
Citation: us:usc:46:7111
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section7111
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part E (Merchant Seamen Licenses, Certificates, And Documents); Chapter 71 (Licenses And Certificates Of Registry). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An individual may take an oral examination for a license to serve on a fishing, fish processing, or fish tender vessel not required to be inspected under part B of this subtitle.
