46 C.F.R. § 54.25-3 — -3 Steel plates (modifies UCS-6).

Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-54.25-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 54 (Pressure Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The steels listed in UCS-6(b) of Section VIII of the ASME BPVC (incorporated by reference; see § 54.01-1) will be allowed only in Class III pressure vessels (see table 2 to § 54.01-5(b)).
