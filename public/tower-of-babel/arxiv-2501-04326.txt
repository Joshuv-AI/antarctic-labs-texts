On the solutions to variable-order fractional p p -Laplacian evolution equation with L 1 L^{1} -data
Sixuan Liu, Gang Dong, Hui Bi, Boying Wu

Abstract




Abstract:

This study investigates Dirichlet boundary condition related to a class of nonlinear parabolic problem with nonnegative
L^{1}
-data, which has a variable-order fractional
p
-Laplacian operator.
The existence and uniqueness of renormalized solutions and entropy solutions to the equation is proved.
To address the significant challenges encountered during this process, we use approximation and energy methods.
In the process of proving, the well-posedness of weak solutions to the problem has been established initially, while also establishing a comparative result of solutions.

keywords:
Fractional
p
-Laplacian, variable-order, renormalized solutions, entropy solutions, existence and uniqueness.

Introduction

Assume that
\Omega\subset\mathbb{R}^{N}
is a bounded domain with a Lipschitz boundary
\partial\Omega
, where
N\geq 2,\;T>0
, and
f
is a measurable function. This article studies the following variable-order nonlinear parabolic problem:

\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}u_{t}+(-\Delta)_{p}^{s(\cdot,\cdot)}u=f&\text{in}\hskip 11.74988pt\Omega_{T}=\Omega\times(0,T),\\
u=0&\text{in}\hskip 11.74988pt(\mathbb{R}^{N}\backslash\Omega)\times(0,T),\\
u(x,0)=u_{0}(x)&\text{in}\hskip 11.74988pt\Omega,\end{array}\right.

(1)

where
1<p<+\infty,s:\mathbb{R}^{N}\times\mathbb{R}^{N}\to(0,1)
is a symmetric continuous function,

ps\left(x,y\right)<N
holds for any
(x,y)\in\mathbb{R}^{N}\times\mathbb{R}^{N}

And
(-\Delta)_{p}^{s(\cdot,\cdot)}
is the variable-order fractional

\begin{split}(-\Delta)_{p}^{s(\cdot,\cdot)}u(x,t)&:=\mathrm{P.V.}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}\frac{|u(x,t)-u(y,t)|^{p-2}\big(u(x,t)-u(y,t)\big)}{|x-y|^{N+p{s(x,y)}}}dy\\
&=\lim_{\varepsilon\to 0^{+}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}\setminus B_{\varepsilon}(x)}\frac{|u(x,t)-u(y,t)|^{p-2}\big(u(x,t)-u(y,t)\big)}{|x-y|^{N+p{s\left(x,y\right)}}}dy,\end{split}

where
(x,t)\in\mathbb{R}^{N}\times\mathbb{R}^{+}
,
P.V. is a commonly used abbreviation for “in the principal value sense”. It is also a normalization factor. Moreover, we assume that
f
and

are nonnegative functions.

A variational model utilizing a variable-order function

It can be characterized as

In the mathematical research, for the case where

Mathematical preliminaries and main result

In this section, we recall some definitions and basic properties of variable-order fractional Sobolev spaces at first.

Suppose that
\Omega\subset\mathbb{R}^{N}
is a bounded domain,
1<p<+\infty,\;s\left(x,y\right):\mathbb{R}^{N}\times\mathbb{R}^{N}\to(0,1)
is a symmetric continuous function satisfies

0<s^{-}=\inf_{(x,y)\in\mathbb{R}^{N}\times\mathbb{R}^{N}}s(x,y)\leq s(x,y)\leq s^{+}=\sup_{(x,y)\in\mathbb{R}^{N}\times\mathbb{R}^{N}}s(x,y)<1.

The Gagliardo seminorm of a measurable function
u
in
{\mathbb{R}^{N}}
with variable order follows

[u]_{W^{s(\cdot,\cdot),p}(\mathbb{R}^{N})}=\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}\dfrac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{p}}{|x-y|^{N+p{s\left(x,y\right)}}}dxdy\right)^{\frac{1}{p}},

the variable-order fractional Sobolev-Slobodeckij spaces is defined as a Banach space

W^{s(\cdot,\cdot),p}(\mathbb{R}^{N})=\left\{u\in L^{p}(\mathbb{R}^{N}):\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}\dfrac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{p}}{|x-y|^{N+p{s\left(x,y\right)}}}dxdy\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}<+\infty\right\},

characterized by a norm denoted as

\|u\|_{W^{s(\cdot,\cdot),p}(\mathbb{R}^{N})}=\|u\|_{L^{p}(\mathbb{R}^{N})}+[u]_{W^{s(\cdot,\cdot),p}(\mathbb{R}^{N})}.

Consequently,
W^{s(\cdot,\cdot),p}(\Omega)
is separable and reflexive.

Denote
D_{\Omega}=(\mathbb{R}^{N}\times\mathbb{R}^{N})\backslash(\Omega^{c}\times\Omega^{c})
, where
\Omega^{c}=\mathbb{R}^{N}\backslash\Omega
.
The linear space
X^{s(\cdot,\cdot),p}(\Omega)
of Lebesgue measurable functions
u:\mathbb{R}^{N}\to\mathbb{R}
is denoted as

\left\{u\in L^{p}(\mathbb{R}^{N}):\left(\int_{\Omega}|u(x)|^{p}dx+\iint_{D_{\Omega}}\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{p}}{|x-y|^{N+p{s(x,y)}}}dxdy\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}<+\infty\right\}.

It is evident that both bounded and Lipschitz functions are included in the space
X^{s(\cdot,\cdot),p}(\Omega)
, therefore
X^{s(\cdot,\cdot),p}(\Omega)
is not trivial, and

X_{0}^{s(\cdot,\cdot),p}(\Omega)=\left\{u\in X^{s(\cdot,\cdot),p}(\Omega):u=0\;a.e.\;\text{in}\;\Omega^{c}\right\}.

Lemma 2.1
.

Let
\Omega\subset\mathbb{R}^{N}
be a bounded domain, and
m(\Omega)\subset\mathbb{R}^{N}
be a measurable set with finite measure. Fix
x\in\mathbb{R}^{N}
, let
p>1,\;s(x,y)\in(0,1)
, there holds

\int_{\Omega^{c}}\frac{1}{|x-y|^{N+p{s\left(x,y\right)}}}dy\geq c\cdot m(\Omega)^{-\frac{p{s^{+}}}{N}}

for a suitable constant
c=c(N,p,s^{+})>0
.

Proof.

Observe that
\displaystyle\rho=\left(\frac{m(\Omega)}{\omega_{n}}\right)^{\frac{1}{n}}
and

m\big(\Omega^{c}\cap B_{\rho}(x)\big)=m\big(\Omega\cap B_{\rho}^{c}(x)\big)
, then

\begin{split}\int_{\Omega^{c}}\frac{1}{|x-y|^{N+p{s\left(x,y\right)}}}dy&=\int_{\Omega^{c}\cap B_{\rho}(x)}\frac{1}{|x-y|^{N+p{s\left(x,y\right)}}}dy+\int_{\Omega^{c}\cap B_{\rho}^{c}(x)}\frac{1}{|x-y|^{N+p{s\left(x,y\right)}}}dy\\
&\geq\int_{\Omega^{c}\cap B_{\rho}(x)}\frac{1}{\rho^{N+p{s\left(x,y\right)}}}dy+\int_{\Omega^{c}\cap B_{\rho}^{c}(x)}\frac{1}{|x-y|^{N+p{s\left(x,y\right)}}}dy\\
&\geq\frac{m(\Omega^{c}\cap B_{\rho}(x))}{\rho^{N+p{s^{+}}}}+\int_{\Omega^{c}\cap B_{\rho}^{c}(x)}\frac{1}{|x-y|^{N+p{s^{+}}}}dy\\
&=\frac{m(\Omega\cap B_{\rho}^{c}(x))}{\rho^{N+p{s^{+}}}}+\int_{\Omega^{c}\cap B_{\rho}^{c}(x)}\frac{1}{|x-y|^{N+p{s^{+}}}}dy\\
&\geq\int_{\Omega\cap B_{\rho}^{c}(x)}\frac{1}{|x-y|^{N+p{s^{+}}}}dy+\int_{\Omega^{c}\cap B_{\rho}^{c}(x)}\frac{1}{|x-y|^{N+p{s^{+}}}}dy\\
&=\int_{B_{\rho}^{c}(x)}\frac{1}{|x-y|^{N+p{s^{+}}}}dy.\end{split}

Therefore

\int_{\Omega^{c}}\frac{1}{|x-y|^{N+p{s\left(x,y\right)}}}dy\geq c\cdot m(\Omega)^{-\frac{p{s^{+}}}{N}}.

∎

Lemma 2.2
.

Let
\Omega\subset\mathbb{R}^{N}
be a bounded domain, for every function
u\in X_{0}^{s(\cdot,\cdot),p}(\Omega)
, then

\int_{\Omega}|u(x)|^{p}dx\leq C\iint_{D_{\Omega}}\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{p}}{|x-y|^{N+p{s\left(x,y\right)}}}dxdy

holds for a positive constant

Proof.

\begin{split}&\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{p}}{|x-y|^{N+p{s\left(x,y\right)}}}dxdy\\
&=\int_{\Omega\cup{\Omega^{c}}}\left(\int_{\Omega}\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{p}}{|x-y|^{N+p{s\left(x,y\right)}}}dy+\int_{\Omega^{c}}\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{p}}{|x-y|^{N+p{s\left(x,y\right)}}}dy\right)dx\\
&=\int_{\Omega}\int_{\Omega}\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{p}}{|x-y|^{N+p{s\left(x,y\right)}}}dxdy+2\int_{\Omega}|u(x)|^{p}dx\int_{\Omega^{c}}\frac{1}{|x-y|^{N+p{s\left(x,y\right)}}}dy.\end{split}

According to the Lemma
2.1
, we have

\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{p}}{|x-y|^{N+p{s\left(x,y\right)}}}dxdy-\int_{\Omega}\int_{\Omega}\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{p}}{|x-y|^{N+p{s\left(x,y\right)}}}dxdy\geq 2c\int_{\Omega}|u(x)|^{p}dx

i.e. for any
p>1
, there exists a positive constant
C=C(N,p,s^{+},\Omega)

\int_{\Omega}|u(x)|^{p}dx\leq C\iint_{D_{\Omega}}\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{p}}{|x-y|^{N+p{s\left(x,y\right)}}}dxdy.

(2)

∎

Therefore, for any

\iint_{D_{\Omega}}\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{p}}{|x-y|^{N+p{s\left(x,y\right)}}}dxdy\leq\|u\|_{W^{s(\cdot,\cdot),p}(\mathbb{R}^{N})}^{p}\leq C\iint_{D_{\Omega}}\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{p}}{|x-y|^{N+p{s\left(x,y\right)}}}dxdy,

we can endow

with the equivalent norm

\left\|u\right\|_{X_{0}^{s(\cdot,\cdot),p}(\Omega)}=\left(\iint_{D_{\Omega}}\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{p}}{|x-y|^{N+p{s\left(x,y\right)}}}dxdy\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}.

Note that

For

(-\Delta)_{p}^{s(\cdot,\cdot)}u(x):=\text{P.V.}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{p-2}\big(u(x)-u(y)\big)}{|x-y|^{N+p{s(x,y)}}}dy,

for all

\langle(-\Delta)_{p}^{s(\cdot,\cdot)}u,v\rangle=\frac{1}{2}\iint_{\mathbb{R}^{N}\times\mathbb{R}^{N}}\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{p-2}\big(u(x)-u(y)\big)}{|x-y|^{N+p{s(x,y)}}}\cdot\big(v(x)-v(y)\big)dxdy.

Consequently, if

Herein

where

The corresponding parabolic space

L^{p}(0,T;X_{0}^{s(\cdot,\cdot),p}(\Omega))=\left\{u\in L^{p}(\Omega_{T}):\|u\|_{L^{p}(0,T;X_{0}^{s(\cdot,\cdot),p}(\Omega))}<+\infty\right\},

with the norm

\left\|u\right\|_{L^{p}(0,T;X_{0}^{s(\cdot,\cdot),p}(\Omega))}=\left(\int_{0}^{T}\iint_{D_{\Omega}}\frac{|u(x,t)-u(y,t)|^{p}}{|x-y|^{N+p{s\left(x,y\right)}}}dxdydt\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}.

L^{p}(0,T;X_{0}^{s(\cdot,\cdot),p}(\Omega))
is a Banach space whose dual space is
L^{p^{\prime}}(0,T;X_{0}^{s(\cdot,\cdot),p}(\Omega)^{*})

Let us denote

\displaystyle\widetilde{u}(x,y,t)=u(x,t)-u(y,t),

\displaystyle d\sigma=\frac{1}{|x-y|^{N+p{s\left(x,y\right)}}}dxdy.

\lim_{h\to\infty}\iiint_{\{(x,y,t):(u(x,t),u(y,t))\in D_{h}\}}|\widetilde{u}(x,y,t)|^{p-1}d\sigma dt=0.

(2) For every function

Remark 2.4

(H^{\prime}(u)\phi)(x,t)-(H^{\prime}(u)\phi)(y,t)

\mathcal{A}_{p}^{s(\cdot,\cdot)}:L^{2}(\Omega)\to[0,+\infty]
that is closely associated with our problem, there holds

\mathcal{A}_{p}^{s(\cdot,\cdot)}\left(u_{n}\right)=\begin{cases}\displaystyle\frac{1}{2p}\int_{D_{\Omega}}\left|\widetilde{u}_{n}(x,y)\right|^{p}d\sigma\hskip 11.74988pt&\text{if}\;u_{n}\in L^{2}(\Omega)\cap X_{0}^{s(\cdot,\cdot),p}(\Omega)\\
+\infty\hskip 11.74988pt&\text{if}\;u_{n}\in L^{2}(\Omega)\backslash X_{0}^{s(\cdot,\cdot),p}(\Omega)\end{cases}

By Fatou’s Lemma, it is easy to see that
\mathcal{A}_{p}^{s(\cdot,\cdot)}
is weak lower semicontinuous and convex in
L^{2}(\Omega)
,
then we have that the subdifferential
\partial\mathcal{A}_{p}^{s(\cdot,\cdot)}
is a maximal monotone operator in
L^{2}(\Omega)
.
Consequently, the following definition is presented regarding
\partial\mathcal{A}_{p}^{s(\cdot,\cdot)}
.

Definition 3.3
.

The operator
A_{p}^{s(\cdot,\cdot)}
in
L^{2}(\Omega)\times L^{2}(\Omega)
is denoted by
v_{n}\in A_{p}^{s(\cdot,\cdot)}(u_{n})
, if
u_{n},\varphi\in L^{2}(\Omega)\cap X_{0}^{s(\cdot,\cdot),p}(\Omega)
,

and

\int_{\Omega}v_{n}\varphi dx+\frac{1}{2}\int_{D_{\Omega}}|\widetilde{u}_{n}(x,y)|^{p-2}\widetilde{u}_{n}(x,y)\big(\varphi(x)-\varphi(y)\big)d\sigma=0.

Next, we prove the conditions that the operator
A_{p}^{s(\cdot,\cdot)}
satisfied for the application of Nonlinear Semigroup Theory.

Theorem 3.4
.

The operator
A_{p}^{s(\cdot,\cdot)}
is
m
-completely accretive in
L^{2}(\Omega)
with dense domain and satisfied
A_{p}^{s(\cdot,\cdot)}=\partial\mathcal{A}_{p}^{s(\cdot,\cdot)}

Proof.

We prove by several steps.

Step 1
Firstly, we let
u_{n1},\;u_{n2}\in X_{0}^{s(\cdot,\cdot),p}(\Omega),\;v_{n1},\;v_{n2}\in A_{p}^{s(\cdot,\cdot)}(u_{n}),\;\gamma\in P_{0}
, that is,
\gamma\in C^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}),\;0\leq\gamma\leq 1,\;\mathrm{supp}(\gamma^{\prime})
is compact,
0\not\in\mathrm{supp}(\gamma),\;\gamma(u_{n1}-u_{n2})=\varphi\in X_{0}^{s(\cdot,\cdot),p}(\Omega)\cap L^{\infty}(\Omega)

\begin{split}&\int_{\Omega}\big(v_{n1}(x)-v_{n2}(x)\big)\gamma\big(u_{n1}(x)-u_{n2}(x)\big)dx\\
&+\frac{1}{2}\int_{D_{\Omega}}\Big(|\widetilde{u}_{n1}(x,y)|^{p-2}\widetilde{u}_{n1}(x,y)-|\widetilde{u}_{n2}(x,y)|^{p-2}\widetilde{u}_{n2}(x,y)\Big)\\
&\cdot\Big[\gamma\big(u_{n1}(x)-u_{n2}(x)\big)-\gamma\big(u_{n1}(y)-u_{n2}(y)\big)\Big]d\sigma\\
&=0,\end{split}

thus

\begin{split}&\int_{\Omega}\big(v_{n1}(x)-v_{n2}(x)\big)\gamma\big(u_{n1}(x)-u_{n2}(x)\big)dx\\
&\geq\frac{1}{2}\int_{D_{\Omega}}\Big(|\widetilde{u}_{n2}(x,y)|^{p-2}\widetilde{u}_{n2}(x,y)-|\widetilde{u}_{n1}(x,y)|^{p-2}\widetilde{u}_{n1}(x,y)\Big)\\
&\cdot\Big[\gamma\big(u_{n1}(x)-u_{n2}(x)\big)-\gamma\big(u_{n1}(y)-u_{n2}(y)\big)\Big]d\sigma.\end{split}

The left side of the above formula is
v_{n}
, the right side
|\widetilde{u}_{n}(x,y)|^{p-2}\widetilde{u}_{n}(x,y)
, this process is monotonically increasing, it indicates that
A_{p}^{s(\cdot,\cdot)}
is completely accretive.

Step 2
Given function
h(x)
a.e. in

for any

By Young’s inequality, we know that

Thus

has a lower boundary and
\displaystyle\inf\limits_{u\in L^{2}(\Omega)}E(u)
is the infimum. According to the definition of the infimum, there exists a minimalist sequence
u_{k}\in X_{0}^{s(\cdot,\cdot),p}(\Omega)\cap L^{2}(\Omega)
such that
\displaystyle\lim\limits_{k\to\infty}E(u_{k})=\inf\limits_{u\in L^{2}(\Omega)}E(u)
with
k\in(0,+\infty)
i.e.
E(u_{k})\leq C
for a constant
C>0
. Therefore

\mathcal{A}_{p}^{s(\cdot,\cdot)}(u_{k})+\frac{1}{2}\|u_{k}\|_{L^{2}(\Omega)}^{2}-\int_{\Omega}hu_{k}dx\leq C,\;

i.e.

\mathcal{A}_{p}^{s\>(\cdot,\cdot)}(u_{k})+\frac{1}{4}\|u_{k}\|_{L^{2}(\Omega)}^{2}\leq C+\|h\|_{L^{2}(\Omega)}^{2}.

By Lemma 1 in
[
22

, we derive that

is bounded in
X_{0}^{s(\cdot,\cdot),p}(\Omega)
.
There exists a weak convergence subsequence

is a lower semicontinuous function by Theorem

We conclude that
E(u_{n})=\inf\limits_{u\in L^{2}(\Omega)}E(u)
,
u_{n}
is the unique minimizer and satisfies the Euler-Lagrange equation because of the convexity. Then we have

\begin{split}\left.\frac{dE(u_{n}+\varepsilon\varphi)}{d\varepsilon}\right|_{\varepsilon=0}&=\frac{1}{2}\int_{D_{\Omega}}|\widetilde{u}_{n}(x,y)|^{p-2}\widetilde{u}_{n}(x,y)\big(\varphi(x)-\varphi(y)\big)d\sigma\\
&-\int_{\Omega}\big(h(x)-u_{n}(x)\big)\varphi(x)dx=0.\end{split}

According to the Definition
3.3
, let
h-u_{n}=v_{n}\in A_{p}^{s(\cdot,\cdot)}(u_{n})
,
we derive
L^{2}(\Omega)\subset\mathrm{R}(\mathrm{Id}+A_{p}^{s(\cdot,\cdot)})
,
thereby the operator

is
m
-completely accretive.

Step 3
Since the operator
A_{p}^{s(\cdot,\cdot)}
is

-completely accretive in

&=0.\end{split}

due to inequality
\displaystyle|m|^{p-2}m(n-m)\leq\frac{1}{p}(|n|^{p}-|m|^{p})

\begin{split}&\int_{\Omega}\big({v^{\prime}_{n}}(x)-{u_{n}}(x)\big)^{2}dx\\

Lemma 3.6
.

Let

Proof.

Let the sequence
u_{n}
be a weak solution, taking
\varphi=T_{k}(u_{n})-T_{k}(u)

\begin{split}&\int_{\Omega}(u_{n})_{t}\varphi dx+\frac{1}{2}\int_{D_{\Omega}}|\widetilde{u}_{n}(x,y)|^{p-2}\widetilde{u}_{n}(x,y)\\
&\cdot\Big[T_{k}(u_{n})(x)-T_{k}(u)(x)-T_{k}(u_{n})(y)+T_{k}(u)(y)\Big]d\sigma\\
&=\int_{\Omega}f\big(T_{k}(u_{n})-T_{k}(u)\big)dx\end{split}

Donate

\displaystyle U(x,y)

\displaystyle=|\widetilde{u}_{n}(x,y)|^{p-2}\widetilde{u}_{n}(x,y),

\displaystyle T(x,y)

\displaystyle=\big|T_{k}(u_{n})(x)-T_{k}(u_{n})(y)\big|^{p-2}\cdot\Big[T_{k}(u_{n})(x)-T_{k}(u_{n})(y)\Big].

The above equation can be rewritten as:

A\leq B+\int_{\Omega}f_{n}\big(T_{k}(u_{n})-T_{k}(u)\big)dx,

where

\begin{split}A&=\int_{D_{\Omega}}|\widetilde{u}_{n}(x,y)|^{p-2}\widetilde{u}_{n}(x,y)\cdot\Big[T_{k}(u_{n})(x)-T_{k}(u_{n})(y)\Big]d\sigma\\
&=\int_{D_{\Omega}}\big|T_{k}(u_{n})(x)-T_{k}(u_{n})(y)\big|^{p}d\sigma\\
&+\int_{D_{\Omega}}\big(U(x,y)-T(x,y)\big)\Big[T_{k}(u_{n})(x)-T_{k}(u_{n})(y)\Big]d\sigma.\end{split}

Owing to Young’s inequality

T(x,y)\Big[T_{k}(u)(x)-T_{k}(u)(y)\Big]\leq\frac{1}{m}\big(T(x,y)\big)^{m}+\frac{1}{n}\big[T_{k}(u)(x)-T_{k}(u)(y)\big]^{n}

\begin{split}B&=\int_{D_{\Omega}}|\widetilde{u}_{n}(x,y)|^{p-2}\widetilde{u}_{n}(x,y)\cdot\Big[T_{k}(u)(x)-T_{k}(u)(y)\Big]d\sigma\\
&=\int_{D_{\Omega}}T(x,y)\Big[T_{k}(u)(x)-T_{k}(u)(y)\Big]d\sigma\\
&+\int_{D_{\Omega}}\big(U(x,y)-T(x,y)\big)\Big[T_{k}(u)(x)-T_{k}(u)(y)\Big]d\sigma\\
&\leq\int_{D_{\Omega}}\frac{p-1}{p}\big|T_{k}(u_{n})(x)-T_{k}(u_{n})(y)\big|^{p}d\sigma\\
&+\int_{D_{\Omega}}\frac{1}{p}\big|T_{k}(u)(x)-T_{k}(u)(y)\big|^{p}d\sigma\\
&+\int_{D_{\Omega}}\big(U(x,y)-T(x,y)\big)\Big[T_{k}(u)(x)-T_{k}(u)(y)\Big]d\sigma.\end{split}

Thus

\begin{split}&\int_{D_{\Omega}}\frac{1}{p}\big|T_{k}(u_{n})(x)-T_{k}(u_{n})(y)\big|^{p}d\sigma\\
&+\int_{D_{\Omega}}\big(U(x,y)-T(x,y)\big)\cdot\Big[T_{k}(u_{n})(x)-T_{k}(u)(x)-T_{k}(u_{n})(y)+T_{k}(u)(y)\Big]d\sigma\\
&\leq\int_{D_{\Omega}}\frac{1}{p}\big|T_{k}(u)(x)-T_{k}(u)(y)\big|^{p}d\sigma+\int_{\Omega}f_{n}\big(T_{k}(u_{n})-T_{k}(u)\big)dx.\end{split}

We assume
S(x,y)
a.e. in
{D_{\Omega}}

In the second term of the left side, for

, we divide

into the following four parts:
{D_{\Omega}}=Q_{1}\cup Q_{2}\cup Q_{3}\cup Q_{4}

\displaystyle Q_{1}

\displaystyle=\{(x,y)\in{D_{\Omega}}:u_{n}(x)\leq k,\;u_{n}(y)\leq k\},

\displaystyle Q_{2}

\displaystyle=\{(x,y)\in{D_{\Omega}}:u_{n}(x)\geq k,\;u_{n}(y)\geq k\},

\displaystyle Q_{3}

\displaystyle=\{(x,y)\in{D_{\Omega}}:u_{n}(x)\leq k,\;u_{n}(y)\geq k\},

\displaystyle Q_{4}

\displaystyle=\{(x,y)\in{D_{\Omega}}:u_{n}(x)\geq k,\;u_{n}(y)\leq k\}.

(1) If

.

(2) If

(3) If

\begin{split}\frac{1}{p}\big|T_{k}(u_{n})(x)-T_{k}(u_{n})(y)\big|^{p}d\sigma&=\int_{D_{\Omega}}\liminf_{n\to\infty}\frac{1}{p}\big|T_{k}(u_{n})(x)-T_{k}(u_{n})(y)\big|^{p}d\sigma\\

&\leq\liminf_{n\to\infty}\int_{D_{\Omega}}\frac{1}{p}\Big(2^{p}\big|T_{k}(u_{n})(x)-T_{k}(u_{n})(y)\big|^{p}+2^{p}\big|T_{k}(u)(x)-T_{k}(u)(y)\big|^{p}\\
&-\big|\big(T_{k}(u_{n})(x)-T_{k}(u_{n})(y)\big)-\big(T_{k}(u)(x)-T_{k}(u)(y)\big)\big|^{p}\Big)d\sigma\\

&-\limsup_{n\to\infty}\int_{D_{\Omega}}\frac{1}{p}\big|\big(T_{k}(u_{n})(x)-T_{k}(u_{n})(y)\big)-\big(T_{k}(u)(x)-T_{k}(u)(y)\big)\big|^{p}d\sigma,\end{split}

Consequently

\int_{D_{\Omega}}\frac{1}{p}\big|\big(T_{k}(u_{n})(x)-T_{k}(u_{n})(y)\big)-\big(T_{k}(u)(x)-T_{k}(u)(y)\big)\big|^{p}d\sigma\to 0.

We conclude

T_{k}(u_{n})\to T_{k}(u)\hskip 11.74988pt\text{strongly in}\hskip 11.74988ptX_{0}^{s(\cdot,\cdot),p}(\Omega).
