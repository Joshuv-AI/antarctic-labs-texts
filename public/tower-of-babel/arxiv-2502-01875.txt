Abstract
. In this paper we apply a formula of the very-well poised ϕ 2 ​ k + 3 2 ​ k + 4 {}_{2k+4}\phi_{2k+3} to write a k k -tuple sum of q q -series as a linear combination of terms wherein each term is a product of expressions of the form 1 ( q ​ y , q ​ y − 1 , q ) ∞ \frac{1}{(qy,qy^{-1};q)_{\infty}} . As an application, we shall express a variety of sums and double sums of q q -series as linear combinations of infinite products. Our formulas are motivated by their connection to overpartition pairs.

Theorem 1
.

Let

and
N\in\mathbb{N}_{0}
. Then
there holds

\sum_{m_{1},\ldots,m_{k-1}=0}^{\infty}\frac{(b_{2},b_{2}^{-1};q)_{m_{1}}(b_{3},b_{3}^{-1};q)_{m_{1}+m_{2}}\cdots(b_{k},b_{k}^{-1};q)_{m_{1}+\cdots m_{k-1}}q^{\sum_{i=1}^{k-1}(k-i)m_{i}}}{(qb_{1},qb_{1}^{-1};q)_{m_{1}}(qb_{2},qb_{2}^{-1};q)_{m_{1}+m_{2}}\cdots(qb_{k-1},qb_{k-1}^{-1};q)_{m_{1}+\cdots m_{k-1}}}

=(qb_{k},q/b_{k};q)_{\infty}\prod_{i=1}^{k}(1-b_{i})(1-b_{i}^{-1})\sum_{i=1}^{k}\frac{A_{k,i}(b_{1},\ldots,b_{k})}{(b_{i},b_{i}^{-1};q)_{\infty}},

where
A_{1,1}(b_{1})=1
and

A_{k,i}(b_{1},\ldots,b_{k})=\begin{cases}C(b_{k},b_{k-1})A_{k-1,k-1}(b_{1},\ldots,b_{k-2},b_{k})&\text{if $i=k$,}\\
-C(b_{k},b_{k-1})A_{k-1,k-1}(b_{1},\ldots,b_{k-1})&\text{if $i=k-1$,}\\
C(b_{k},b_{k-1})\Big(A_{k-1,i}(b_{1},\ldots,b_{k-2},b_{k})&\\
\qquad\qquad-A_{k-1,i}(b_{1},\ldots,b_{k-1})\Big)&\text{if $1\leq i\leq k-2$.}\end{cases}

We state the special cases

Corollary 1
.

We have

\begin{split}{\rm(a)\ }&\sum_{n\geq 0}\frac{q^{n}(z,z^{-1};q)_{n}}{(qy,q/y;q)_{n}}=C(z,y)\Big((1-y)(1-y^{-1})-\frac{(z,z^{-1};q)_{\infty}}{(qy,q/y;q)_{\infty}}\Big).\\
{\rm(b)\ }&\sum_{m,n\geq 0}\frac{q^{2m+n}(y,y^{-1};q)_{m}(z,z^{-1};q)_{m+n}}{(qx,q/x;q)_{m}(qy,q/y;q)_{m+n}}\\
&=D(x,y)C(z,y)C(z,x)-D(x,z)C(z,y)C(y,x)\frac{(qz,q/z;q)_{\infty}}{(qy,q/y;q)_{\infty}}\\
&+D(y,z)C(z,y)\big(C(y,x)-C(z,x)\big)\frac{(qz,q/z;q)_{\infty}}{(qx,q/x;q)_{\infty}}.\end{split}

Definition 1
.

An overpartition
[
11
]
of
n
is a partition of
n
where the first
occurrence of each part may be overlined. The number of overpartitions of
n
, written

\overline{p}(n)
, has the following generating function

\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\overline{p}(n)q^{n}=\frac{(-q;q)_{\infty}}{(q;q)_{\infty}}.

Note that overlined parts in overpartitions are distinct by definition.
We will say that an overpartition has distinct parts if its non-overlined parts are also distinct and we let

\overline{p}_{d}(n)
count the number of such overpartitions. Clearly we have

\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\overline{p}_{d}(n)q^{n}=(-q;q)_{\infty}(-q;q)_{\infty}=(-q;q)_{\infty}^{2}.

Definition 2
.

An overpartition pair
[
15
]
of
n
is a pair of overpartitions
\pi=(\lambda_{1},\lambda_{2})
where the sum
of all the parts is
n
. The number of all overpartition pairs of
n
, written
\overline{pp}(n)
, has the following
generating function

\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\overline{pp}(n)q^{n}=\frac{(-q;q)_{\infty}^{2}}{(q;q)_{\infty}^{2}}.

We say that an overpartition pair
(\lambda_{1},\lambda_{2})
has distinct parts if both
\lambda_{1}

and
\lambda_{2}
have distinct parts and we let
\overline{pp}_{d}(n)
denote the number of such overpartition pairs. We clearly have

\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\overline{pp}_{d}(n)q^{n}=\frac{(-q;q)_{\infty}^{2}}{(-q;q)_{\infty}^{2}}=(-q;q)_{\infty}^{4}.

. Furthermore, combining the recurrence relation (
8.5
) with the basic case (
8.8
) yields

F_{k}(b_{1},\ldots,b_{k})=C(b_{k},b_{k-1})\Big(\sum_{i=1}^{k-1}\frac{A_{k-1,i}(b_{1},\ldots,b_{k-2},b_{k})}{(b_{i},b_{i}^{-1};q)_{\infty}}

-\sum_{i=1}^{k-1}\frac{A_{k-1,i}(b_{1},\ldots,b_{k-1})}{(b_{i},b_{i}^{-1};q)_{\infty}}\Big)

=C(b_{k},b_{k-1})\Big(\sum_{i=1}^{k-2}\frac{A_{k-1,i}(b_{1},\ldots,b_{k-2},b_{k})}{(b_{i},b_{i}^{-1};q)_{\infty}}

+\frac{A_{k-1,k-1}(b_{1},\ldots,b_{k-2},b_{k})}{(b_{k},b_{k}^{-1};q)_{\infty}}-\sum_{i=1}^{k-1}\frac{A_{k-1,i}(b_{1},\ldots,b_{k-1})}{(b_{i},b_{i}^{-1};q)_{\infty}}\Big).

This implies that

\begin{split}A_{k,k}(b_{1},\ldots,b_{k})&=C(b_{k},b_{k-1})A_{k-1,k-1}(b_{1},\ldots,b_{k-2},b_{k}),\\
A_{k,k-1}(b_{1},\ldots,b_{k})&=-C(b_{k},b_{k-1})A_{k-1,k-1}(b_{1},\ldots,b_{k-1}),\end{split}

and for
1\leq i\leq k-2
,

A_{k,i}(b_{1},\ldots,b_{k})=C(b_{k},b_{k-1})\Big(A_{k-1,i}(b_{1},\ldots,b_{k-2},b_{k})-A_{k-1,i}(b_{1},\ldots,b_{k-1})\Big).

That is,

A_{k,i}(b_{1},\ldots,b_{k})=\begin{cases}C(b_{k},b_{k-1})A_{k-1,k-1}(b_{1},\ldots,b_{k-2},b_{k})&\text{if $i=k$,}\\
-C(b_{k},b_{k-1})A_{k-1,k-1}(b_{1},\ldots,b_{k-1})&\text{if $i=k-1$,}\\
C(b_{k},b_{k-1})\Big(A_{k-1,i}(b_{1},\ldots,b_{k-2},b_{k})&\\
\qquad\qquad-A_{k-1,i}(b_{1},\ldots,b_{k-1})\Big)&\text{if $1\leq i\leq k-2$.}\end{cases}

(8.9)

Finally combine (
8.2
) with (
8.8
) and (
8.9
) to deduce the desired identity.

9.
Concluding remarks

For simplicity of notation,
let
F(y,z)
denote the left hand side of Corollary
1
(a), let

K(x,y,z)
denote the left hand-side of Corollary
1
(b),
and let
L(x,y,z)
be the dual of
K(x,y,z)
given by

L(x,y,z)=\sum_{n,m\geq 0}\frac{q^{2n+m}(z,z^{-1};q)_{n}(y,y^{-1};q)_{n+m}}{(qy,q/y;q)_{n}(qx,q/x;q)_{n+m}}.

Then it is natural to ask for a companion of
Corollary
1
(b) dealing with the
L(x,y,z)
.
We have the following connection between
K(x,y,z)
and
L(x,y,z)

K(x,y,z)=\sum_{m,n\geq 0}\frac{q^{2m+n}(y,y^{-1};q)_{m}(z,z^{-1};q)_{m+n}}{(qx,q/x;q)_{m}(qy,q/y;q)_{m+n}}

=\sum_{m\geq 0}\frac{q^{m}(y,y^{-1};q)_{m}}{(qx,q/x;q)_{m}}\sum_{n\geq m}\frac{q^{n}(z,z^{-1};q)_{n}}{(qy,q/y;q)_{n}}
