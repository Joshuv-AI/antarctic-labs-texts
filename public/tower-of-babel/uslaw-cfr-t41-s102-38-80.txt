41 C.F.R. § 102-38.80 — -38.80 Buyer eligibility.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-38.80
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-38.80
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-38 (38—Sale Of Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Generally, you may sell Federal personal property to anyone of legal age. You must not enter into a contract with persons or entities debarred or suspended from purchasing Federal property unless your agency head or designee responsible for the disposal action determines there is a compelling reason to do so.
