2 U.S.C. § 285f — Expenditures
Citation: us:usc:2:285f
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section285f
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 9A (Office Of Law Revision Counsel). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In accordance with policies and procedures approved by the Speaker, the Law Revision Counsel is authorized to make such expenditures as may be necessary or appropriate for the functioning of the Office.
