29 C.F.R. § 779.106 — Employees employed by an independent employer.
Citation: us:cfr:29:779.106
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-779.106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 779 (The Fair Labor Standards Act As Applied To Retailers Of Goods Or Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where the work of an employee would be closely related and directly essential to the production of goods for commerce if he were employed by a producer of the goods, the mere fact that the employee is employed by an independent employer will not justify a different answer. (See §§ 776.17(c) and 776.19 of this chapter.)
