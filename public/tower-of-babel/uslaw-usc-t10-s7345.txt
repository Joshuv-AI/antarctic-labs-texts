10 U.S.C. § 7345 — Restoration to former grade: retired warrant officers and enlisted members
Citation: us:usc:10:7345
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7345
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 743 (Retired Grade). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each retired warrant officer or enlisted member of the Army who has been advanced on the retired list to a higher commissioned grade under section 7344 of this title, and who applies to the Secretary of the Army within three months after his advancement, shall, if the Secretary approves, be restored on the retired list to his former warrant officer or enlisted status, as the case may be.
