47 C.F.R. § 80.25 — License term.
Citation: us:cfr:47:80.25
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-80.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 80 (Stations In The Maritime Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Licenses for ship stations in the maritime services will normally be issued for a term of ten years from the date of original issuance, or renewal.
(b) Licenses other than ship stations in the maritime services will normally be issued for a term of ten years from the date of original issuance, major modification, or renewal.
