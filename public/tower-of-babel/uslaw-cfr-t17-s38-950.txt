17 C.F.R. § 38.950 — Core Principle 18.
Citation: us:cfr:17:38.950
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-38.950
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 38 (Designated Contract Markets). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The board of trade shall maintain records of all activities relating to the business of the contract market:
(a) In a form and manner that is acceptable to the Commission; and
(b) For a period of at least 5 years.
