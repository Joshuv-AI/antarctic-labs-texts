40 C.F.R. § 227.8 — Limitations on the disposal rates of toxic wastes.
Citation: us:cfr:40:227.8
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-227.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 227 (Criteria For The Evaluation Of Permit Applications For Ocean Dumping Of Materials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No wastes will be deemed acceptable for ocean dumping unless such wastes can be dumped so as not to exceed the limiting permissible concentration as defined in § 227.27; Provided, That this § 227.8 does not apply to those wastes for which specific criteria are established in § 227.11 or § 227.12. Total quantities of wastes dumped at a site may be limited as described in § 228.8.
