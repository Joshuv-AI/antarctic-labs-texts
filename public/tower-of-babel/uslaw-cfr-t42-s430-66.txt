42 C.F.R. § 430.66 — Designation of presiding officer for hearing.
Citation: us:cfr:42:430.66
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-430.66
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 430 (Grants To States For Medical Assistance Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The presiding officer at a hearing is the Administrator or his designee.
(b) The designation of the presiding officer is in writing. A copy of the designation is served on all parties.
