24 C.F.R. § 1000.242 — When does the requirement for exemption from taxation apply to affordable housing activities?
Citation: us:cfr:24:1000.242
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-1000.242
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 1000 (Native American Housing Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The requirement for exemption from taxation applies only to rental and lease-purchase homeownership units assisted with IHBG funds which are owned by the Indian tribe or TDHE.
