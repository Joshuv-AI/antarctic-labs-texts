39 C.F.R. § 951.4 — Authorization of appearance may be required.
Citation: us:cfr:39:951.4
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-951.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 951 (Eligibility To Practice Before The Postal Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Judicial Officer, the head of any department of the Postal Service or any Administrative Law Judge may require any person to present satisfactory evidence of his authority to represent the person for whom he appears.
