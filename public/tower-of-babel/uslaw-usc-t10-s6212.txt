10 U.S.C. § 6212 — Multi-year plan for clean-up
Citation: us:usc:10:6212
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section6212
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part VI (Elements Of Department Of Defense And Other Matters); Subpart B (Atomic Energy Defense); Chapter 604 (Defense Environmental Cleanup Matters); Subchapter IV (Savannah River Site, South Carolina). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Energy shall develop and implement a multi-year plan for the clean-up of nuclear waste at the Savannah River Site that results, or has resulted, from the following:
(1)
Nuclear weapons activities carried out at the site.
(2)
The processing, treating, packaging, and disposal of Department of Energy domestic and foreign spent nuclear fuel rods at the site.
