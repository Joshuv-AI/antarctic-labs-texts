40 C.F.R. § 763.167 — Processing prohibitions.
Citation: us:cfr:40:763.167
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-763.167
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 763 (Asbestos). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) After August 27, 1990, no person shall process for any use, either in the United States or for export, any of the asbestos-containing products listed at § 763.165(a).
(b) After August 26, 1996, no person shall process for any use, either in the United States or for export, any of the asbestos-containing products listed at § 763.165(b).
