10 U.S.C. § 2385 — Arms and ammunition: immunity from taxation
Citation: us:usc:10:2385
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section2385
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Property); Chapter 141 (Miscellaneous Provisions Relating To Property). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No tax on the sale or transfer of firearms, pistols, revolvers, shells, or cartridges may be imposed on such articles when bought with funds appropriated for a military department.
