AGENCY: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA), Department

ACTION: Notice of application for exemption; request for comments.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: FMCSA requests public comment on PAM Transport Inc.'s (PAM) 
application for an exemption from the requirement that a holder of a 
commercial learner's permit (CLP) who has successfully passed the 
commercial driver's license (CDL) skills test but not yet been issued 
CDL credentials be accompanied by a CDL holder in the front seat, while 
the commercial motor vehicle (CMV) is driven by the CLP holder. PAM 
states that the CDL holder would remain in the CMV while the CLP holder 
is driving, but not necessarily in the passenger seat of the vehicle. 
FMCSA is required by statute to publish a notice explaining each 
exemption request. This notice does not indicate what decision FMCSA 
will ultimately reach on the request. After reviewing the application, 
safety analyses, and public comments submitted, FMCSA will grant or 
deny the exemption.

II. Legal Basis

    FMCSA has authority under 49 U.S.C. 31136(e) and 31315(b) to grant 
exemptions from the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Regulations (FMCSRs). 
FMCSA must publish a notice of each exemption request in the Federal 
Register (49 CFR 381.315(a)). The Agency must provide the public an 
opportunity to inspect the information relevant to the application, 
including the applicant's safety analysis. The Agency must provide an 
opportunity for public comment on the request.
    The Agency reviews the application, safety analyses, and public 
comments submitted and determines whether granting the exemption would 
likely achieve a level of safety equivalent to, or greater than, the 
level that would be achieved absent such exemption, pursuant to the 
standard set forth in 49 U.S.C. 31315(b)(1). The Agency must publish 
its decision in the Federal Register (49 CFR 381.315(b)). If granted, 
the notice will identify the regulatory provision(s) from which the 
exempted party will be exempt, the effective period, and all terms and 
conditions of the exemption (49 CFR 381.315(c)(1)). If the exemption is 
denied, the notice will explain the reason for the denial (49 CFR 
381.315(c)(2)). The exemption may be renewed (49 CFR 381.300(b)).

III. Applicant's Request

Current Regulatory Requirements

    The Agency's CDL regulations in 49 CFR 383.25(a)(1) require that a 
CLP holder always be accompanied by the holder of a valid CDL for the 
group (e.g., Group A or B) and with any endorsement(s) necessary to 
operate the CMV. The CDL holder must be physically present in the front 
seat of the vehicle next to the CLP holder or, in the case of a 
passenger vehicle, directly behind or in the first row behind the 
driver and must have the CLP holder under observation and direct 
supervision.

Applicant's Request

    PAM recruits and develops entry-level commercial drivers through 
partnerships with CDL training programs, including programs where 
training is conducted in a State other than the driver's State of 
domicile. The applicant seeks a 5-year exemption from the provisions in 
49 CFR 383.25(a)(1) to allow CLP holders who have successfully passed a 
CDL skills test and are thus eligible to receive a CDL to drive a CMV 
without a CDL holder present in the front passenger seat of the 
vehicle. PAM states that the exemption, if granted, would allow the CLP 
holder to begin employment in a timelier manner, strengthen their newly 
acquired driving skills, and participate in a revenue-generating trip 
back to their State of domicile to obtain their CDL documents.
    PAM estimates that approximately 500-700 CLP holders would operate 
each year under the terms of the exemption. Absent the exemption, PAM 
states that the driver's transition into productive employment would be 
delayed until the driver's State of domicile issues the CDL or the 
driver is sent home in a non-driving capacity. The applicant asserts 
that this outcome is inefficient for PAM and presents a lost employment 
opportunity for the driver. PAM states that the administrative waiting 
period results in operational inefficiencies and financial hardships 
for already-trained drivers, and the potential degradation of recently 
acquired driving skills. PAM further adds that as the trucking industry 
is already experiencing significant driver turnover, requiring drivers 
to remain idle during this period may increase attrition and discourage 
workforce participation.

Applicant's Equivalent Level of Safety

    PAM asserts that exemption will result in a level of safety that is 
equivalent to, or greater than, the level of safety without the 
exemption, as it would limit CMV operation under the exemption to 
drivers who have passed the CDL skills test and therefore have 
demonstrated that they meet stringent qualification requirements. 
Before a driver may operate under the exemption, PAM would verify that 
the driver: (1) holds a valid CLP issued by the driver's State of 
domicile; (2) holds a valid State-issued driver's license from the 
jurisdiction that issued the CLP; (3) has successfully completed the 
applicable CDL skills examination and possesses evidence of this 
completion; (4) holds all endorsements required for the CMV being 
operated; (5) is medically qualified to operate the CMV; (6) is not 
subject to an out-of-service order or suspension of driving privileges; 
(7) satisfies PAM's driver qualification requirements; and (8) has a 
qualified CDL holder with the appropriate CDL class and endorsements 
physically present in the CMV while the CLP holder is operating the CMV 
under the exemption. PAM would also require the driver to

[[Page 67647]]

maintain possession of the required documentation, including a valid 
State driver's license, valid CLP, documentation demonstrating 
successful completion of the CDL skills test, and a copy of the FMCSA 
exemption, and ensure that these documents are accessible in the event 
of an inspection by authorized Federal, State, or local enforcement 
officials. PAM would document operations under the exemption and 
continuously monitor the safety performance of the CLP holder and adds 
that any driver who becomes ineligible under the conditions of the 
exemption would be immediately removed from operation under the 
exemption.
    A copy of PAM's application for exemption is available for review 
in the docket for this notice.
