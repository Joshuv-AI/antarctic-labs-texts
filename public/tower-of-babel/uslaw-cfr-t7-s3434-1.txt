7 C.F.R. § 3434.1 — Applicability of regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3434.1
Creator: National Institute Of Food And Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3434.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3434 (Hispanic-Serving Agricultural Colleges And Universities Certification Process). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part establishes the process to certify and designate a group of eligible educational institutions as Hispanic-Serving Agricultural Colleges and Universities, as authorized by Section 7101 of the Food, Conservation, and Energy Act of 2008 (FCEA), 7 U.S.C. 3103; Public Law 110-246.
