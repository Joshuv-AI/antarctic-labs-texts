27 C.F.R. § 20.66 — Registry of stills.
Citation: us:cfr:27:20.66
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-20.66
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 20 (Distribution And Use Of Denatured Alcohol And Rum). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of subpart C of part 29 of this chapter are applicable to stills or distilling apparatus located on the premises of a permittee used for distilling. As provided under § 29.55, the listing of a still in the permit application (Form 5150.22), and approval of the application, constitutes registration of the still.
