48 U.S.C. § 1958 — Referendum costs
Citation: us:usc:48:1958
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section1958
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 18 (Micronesia, Marshall Islands, And Palau); Subchapter II (Palau); Part B (Implementation Of Compact). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Interior shall provide such sums as may be necessary for a further referendum on approval of the Compact, if one is required, or other appropriate costs associated with the approval process in Palau.
