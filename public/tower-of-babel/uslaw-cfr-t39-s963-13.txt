39 C.F.R. § 963.13 — Subpoenas and witness fees not authorized.
Citation: us:cfr:39:963.13
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-963.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 963 (Pandering). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Postal Service is not authorized to issue subpoenas requiring the attendance or testimony of witnesses, nor to pay fees and expenses for a petitioner's witnesses or for depositions requested by a petitioner.
