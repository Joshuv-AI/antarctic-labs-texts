48 C.F.R. § 1602.170-15 — 1602.170-15 Subcontractor.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1602.170-15
Creator: Chapter 16—Office Of Personnel Management Federal Employees Health Benefits Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1602.170-15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1602 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Subcontractor means any supplier, distributor, vendor, or firm that furnishes supplies or services to or for a prime contractor or another subcontractor, except for providers of direct medical services or supplies pursuant to the Carrier's health benefits plan.
