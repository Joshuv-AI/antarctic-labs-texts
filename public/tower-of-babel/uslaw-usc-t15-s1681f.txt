15 U.S.C. § 1681f — Disclosures to governmental agencies
Citation: us:usc:15:1681f
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1681f
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 41 (Consumer Credit Protection); Subchapter III (Credit Reporting Agencies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding the provisions of section 1681b of this title, a consumer reporting agency may furnish identifying information respecting any consumer, limited to his name, address, former addresses, places of employment, or former places of employment, to a governmental agency.
