48 C.F.R. § 952.203-1 — 952.203-1 Identification of contractor employees.
Citation: us:cfr:48:952.203-1
Creator: Chapter 9—Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-952.203-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 952 (Solicitation Provisions And Contract Clauses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As prescribed at 903.1004, insert the following clause:
Contractors and their employees shall be properly identified in communications (e.g., email communications, texts, video and teleconference calls, etc.) and in meetings so that all participants can differentiate between Federal employees and contractor employees.
