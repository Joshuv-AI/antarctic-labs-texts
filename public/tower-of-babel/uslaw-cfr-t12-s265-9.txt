12 C.F.R. § 265.9 — Functions delegated to the Director of the Division of International Finance.
Citation: us:cfr:12:265.9
Creator: Federal Reserve System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-265.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 265 (Rules Regarding Delegation Of Authority). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director of the Division of International Finance (or the Director's delegatee) is authorized:
(a) Establishment of foreign accounts. To approve the establishment of foreign accounts and the terms of any account-related agreements with the Federal Reserve Bank of New York under section 14(e) of the Federal Reserve Act (12 U.S.C. 358).
(b) [Reserved]
