14 C.F.R. § 1216.501 — Purpose and need.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1216.501
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1216.501
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1216 (Environmental Quality). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The environmental impact statement will include the purpose and need for the proposed agency action based on NASA's statutory authority. When the proposed agency action concerns a non-Federal project sponsor, the purpose and need for the proposed agency action will also be informed by the goals of the non-Federal project sponsor.
