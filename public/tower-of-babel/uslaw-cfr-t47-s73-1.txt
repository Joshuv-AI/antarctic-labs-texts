47 C.F.R. § 73.1 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:47:73.1
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-73.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 73 (Radio Broadcast Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart contains those rules which apply exclusively to the AM broadcast service and are in addition to those rules in subpart H which are common to all AM, FM and TV broadcast services, commercial and noncommercial.
