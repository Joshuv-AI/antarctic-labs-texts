15 U.S.C. § 1266 — Hearing before report of criminal violation
Citation: us:usc:15:1266
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1266
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 30 (Hazardous Substances). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Before any violation of this chapter is reported by the Commission to any United States attorney for institution of a criminal proceeding, the person against whom such proceeding is contemplated shall be given appropriate notice and an opportunity to present his views, either orally or in writing, with regard to such contemplated proceeding.
