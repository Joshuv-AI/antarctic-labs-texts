48 C.F.R. § 732.406-73 — 732.406-73 LOC contract clause.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 732 (Contract Financing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If payment is to be provided by LOC, the contract must contain the clause in 752.232-70.
(b) Contracting officers must ensure that an appropriate (48 CFR) FAR payment clause is also inserted in the contract, in the event that the LOC is revoked pursuant to 732.406-74.
