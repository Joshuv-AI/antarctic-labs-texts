29 C.F.R. § 10.7 — Waiver of rights.
Citation: us:cfr:29:10.7
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-10.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 10 (Establishing A Minimum Wage For Contractors). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Workers cannot waive, nor may contractors induce workers to waive, their rights under Executive Order 13658 or this part.
