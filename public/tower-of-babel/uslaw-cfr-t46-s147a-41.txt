46 C.F.R. § 147A.41 — A.41 Person in charge of fumigation; flammable fumigants.
Citation: us:cfr:46:147A.41
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-147A.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 147A (Part 147A—Interim Regulations For Shipboard Fumigation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The person in charge of fumigation shall ensure that:
(1) Before the space that is to be fumigated is sealed, it is thoroughly cleaned, and all refuse, oily waste, and other combustible material is removed;
(2) Before fumigation, all fire fighting equipment, including sprinklers and fire pumps, is in operating condition; and
(3) Before and during fumigation, electrical circuits that are in the space that is fumigated are de-energized.
