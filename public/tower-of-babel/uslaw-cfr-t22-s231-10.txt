22 C.F.R. § 231.10 — Payment to USAID of excess amounts received by a Noteholder.
Citation: us:cfr:22:231.10
Creator: Agency For International Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-231.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 231 (Arab Republic Of Egypt Loan Guarantees Issued Under The Emergency Wartime Supplemental Appropriations Act Of 2003, Public Law 108-11—Standard Terms And Conditions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a Noteholder shall, as a result of USAID paying compensation under this Guarantee, receive an excess payment, it shall refund the excess to USAID.
