22 U.S.C. § 1650 — Appointments of representatives and panel members under Convention on the Settlement of Investment Disputes
Citation: us:usc:22:1650
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section1650
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 21A (Settlement Of Investment Disputes). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President may make such appointments of representatives and panel members as may be provided for under the convention.
