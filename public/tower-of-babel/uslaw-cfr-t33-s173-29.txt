33 C.F.R. § 173.29 — Notification to issuing authority.
Citation: us:cfr:33:173.29
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-173.29
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 173 (Vessel Numbering And Casualty And Accident Reporting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A person whose name appears as the owner of a vessel on a certificate of number shall, within 15 days, notify the issuing authority in a manner prescribed by the issuing authority of:
(a) Any change in his or her address;
(b) The theft or recovery of the vessel;
(c) The loss or destruction of a valid certificate of number;
(d) The transfer of all or part of his or her interest in the vessel; and
(e) The destruction or abandonment of the vessel.
