48 C.F.R. § 604.805 — 604.805 Storage, handling, and disposal of contract files.
Citation: us:cfr:48:604.805
Creator: Chapter 6—Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-604.805
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 604 (Administrative Matters). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Heads of contracting activities shall prescribe procedures for handling, storing, and disposing of contract files. Additional guidance on records management may be found in 5 FAM.
