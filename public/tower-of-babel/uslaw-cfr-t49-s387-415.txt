49 C.F.R. § 387.415 — Acceptance and revocation by the FMCSA.
Citation: us:cfr:49:387.415
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-387.415
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 387 (Minimum Levels Of Financial Responsibility For Motor Carriers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The FMCSA may at any time refuse to accept or may revoke its acceptance of any surety bond, certificate of insurance, qualifications as a self-insurer, or other security or agreement that does not comply with these rules or fails to provide adequate public protection.
