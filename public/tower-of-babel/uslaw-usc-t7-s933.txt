7 U.S.C. § 933 — Moneys in the Rural Electrification and Telephone Revolving Fund
Citation: us:usc:7:933
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section933
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 31 (Rural Electrification And Telephone Service); Subchapter III (Rural Electric And Telephone Direct Loan Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Moneys in the fund shall remain on deposit in the Treasury of the United States until disbursed.
