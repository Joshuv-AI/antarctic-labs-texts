9 C.F.R. § 557.19 — Specimens for laboratory examination and similar purposes.
Citation: us:cfr:9:557.19
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-557.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 557 (Importation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Importation of fish or fish product samples for trade show exhibition, laboratory examination, research, evaluative testing, trade show exhibition, or other scientific purposes are subject to the same conditions as imported meat or meat product specimens under § 327.19 of this chapter.
