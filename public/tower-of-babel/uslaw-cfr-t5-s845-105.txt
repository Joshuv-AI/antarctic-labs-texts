5 C.F.R. § 845.105 — Termination and suspension of collection actions.
Citation: us:cfr:5:845.105
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-845.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 845 (Federal Employees Retirement System—Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The termination or suspension of a collection action, other than waiver of an overpayment under subparts B and C of this part, are controlled exclusively by the Federal Claims Collection Standards, chapter II of title 4, Code of Federal Regulations.
