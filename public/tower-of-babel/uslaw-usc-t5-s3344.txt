5 U.S.C. § 3344 — Details; administrative law judges
Citation: us:usc:5:3344
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section3344
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart B (Employment And Retention); Chapter 33 (Examination, Selection, And Placement); Subchapter III (Details, Vacancies, And Appointments). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An agency as defined by section 551 of this title which occasionally or temporarily is insufficiently staffed with administrative law judges appointed under section 3105 of this title may use administrative law judges selected by the Office of Personnel Management from and with the consent of other agencies.
