14 C.F.R. § 189.5 — Limitation of liability.
Citation: us:cfr:14:189.5
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-189.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 189 (Use Of Federal Aviation Administration Communications System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The United States is not liable for any omission, error, or delay in transmitting or relaying, or for any failure to transmit or relay, any message accepted for transmission or relayed under this part, even if the omission, error, delay, or failure to transmit or relay is caused by the negligence of an employee of the United States.
