43 C.F.R. § 4300.51 — Will I be notified if another use, disposal, or withdrawal occurs on the land?
Citation: us:cfr:43:4300.51
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4300.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4300 (Grazing Administration; Alaska; Reindeer; General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. If there is a settlement, location, entry, disposal, or withdrawal on any lands described in your permit, BLM will notify you and will reduce your permit area by the amount of the area involved.
