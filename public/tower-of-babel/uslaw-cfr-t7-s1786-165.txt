7 C.F.R. § 1786.165 — Reporting.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1786.165
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1786.165
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1786 (Prepayment Of Rus Guaranteed And Insured Loans To Electric And Telephone Borrowers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Borrowers that no longer have any loans made or guaranteed by RUS and are considering applying for other financial assistance pursuant to the RE Act are encouraged to file the end-of-year operating report, RUS Form 7.
