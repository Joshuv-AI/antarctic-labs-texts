31 C.F.R. § 525.305 — Financial services.
Citation: us:cfr:31:525.305
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-525.305
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 525 (Burma Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term financial services, as used in § 525.202, includes loans, transfers, accounts, insurance, investments, securities, guarantees, foreign exchange, letters of credit, and commodity futures or options.
