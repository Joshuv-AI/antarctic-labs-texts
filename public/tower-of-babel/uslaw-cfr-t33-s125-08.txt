33 C.F.R. § 125.08 — Great Lakes.
Citation: us:cfr:33:125.08
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-125.08
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 125 (Identification Credentials For Persons Requiring Access To Waterfront Facilities Or Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term Great Lakes as used in the regulations in this subchapter shall include the Great Lakes and their connecting and tributary waters.
