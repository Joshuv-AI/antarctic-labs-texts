43 C.F.R. § 20.503 — Scope of authority.
Citation: us:cfr:43:20.503
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-20.503
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 20 (Employee Responsibilities And Conduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employes shall not engage in any conduct or activity which is in excess of his or her authority, or is otherwise contrary to any law or announced Departmental policy.
