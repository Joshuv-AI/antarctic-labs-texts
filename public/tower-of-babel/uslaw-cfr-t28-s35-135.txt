28 C.F.R. § 35.135 — Personal devices and services.
Citation: us:cfr:28:35.135
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-35.135
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 35 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Disability In State And Local Government Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part does not require a public entity to provide to individuals with disabilities personal devices, such as wheelchairs; individually prescribed devices, such as prescription eyeglasses or hearing aids; readers for personal use or study; or services of a personal nature including assistance in eating, toileting, or dressing.
