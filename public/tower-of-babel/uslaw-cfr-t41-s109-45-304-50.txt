41 C.F.R. § 109-45.304-50 — -45.304-50 Processing bids and awarding of contracts.
Citation: us:cfr:41:109-45.304-50
Creator: Chapter 109—Department Of Energy Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-109-45.304-50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 109-45 (45—Sale, Abandonment, Or Destruction Of Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The procedures established in 48 CFR 14.4 and 48 CFR 914.4 shall be made applicable to the execution, receipt, safeguarding, opening, abstraction, and evaluation of bids and awarding contracts, except that in evaluating bids and awarding contracts, disposal under conditions most advantageous to the Government based on high bids received shall be the determining factor.
