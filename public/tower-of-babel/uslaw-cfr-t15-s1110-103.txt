15 C.F.R. § 1110.103 — Disclosure to a certified person.
Citation: us:cfr:15:1110.103
Creator: National Technical Information Service, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-1110.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 1110 (Certification Program For Access To The Death Master File). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Disclosure by a Person certified under this part of Limited Access DMF to another Person certified under this part shall be deemed to satisfy the disclosing Person's obligation to ensure compliance with § 1110.102(a)(4)(i) through (iii).
