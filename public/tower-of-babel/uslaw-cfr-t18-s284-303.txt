18 C.F.R. § 284.303 — OCS blanket certificates.
Citation: us:cfr:18:284.303
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-284.303
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 284 (Certain Sales And Transportation Of Natural Gas Under The Natural Gas Policy Act Of 1978 And Related Authorities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Every OCS pipeline [as that term is defined in § 284.302(b)] is required to provide open-access, nondiscriminatory transportation service pursuant to a blanket transportation certificate issued under subpart G of this part.
