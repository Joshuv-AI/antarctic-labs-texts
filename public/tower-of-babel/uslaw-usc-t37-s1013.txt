37 U.S.C. § 1013 — Payment of compensation for victims of terrorism
Citation: us:usc:37:1013
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title37-section1013
Context: United States Code, Title 37 (Pay And Allowances Of The Uniformed Services); Chapter 19 (Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any benefit or payment pursuant to section 559 of this title, or section 1032 or 1095a or chapter 110 of title 10, shall be paid out of funds available to the Secretary concerned for military personnel.
