46 C.F.R. § 54.05-1 — -1 Scope (replaces UG-84).
Citation: us:cfr:46:54.05-1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-54.05-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 54 (Pressure Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The toughness tests of materials used in pressure vessels must be as required by this subpart in lieu of requirements in UG-84 of Section VIII of the ASME BPVC (incorporated by reference; see § 54.01-1).
