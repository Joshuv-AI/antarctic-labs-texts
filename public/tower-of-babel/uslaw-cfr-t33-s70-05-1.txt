33 C.F.R. § 70.05-1 — -1 General provisions.
Citation: us:cfr:33:70.05-1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-70.05-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 70 (Interference With Or Damage To Aids To Navigation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person shall take possession of or make use of for any purpose, or build upon, alter, deface, destroy, move, injure, obstruct by fastening vessels thereto or otherwise, or in any manner whatever impair the usefulness of any aid to navigation established and maintained by the United States.
