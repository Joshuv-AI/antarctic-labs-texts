36 C.F.R. § 63.5 — Federal Register publication of properties determined eligible.
Citation: us:cfr:36:63.5
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-63.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 63 (Determinations Of Eligibility For Inclusion In The National Register Of Historic Places). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to written notice to the Federal agency and the State Historic Preservation Officer, public notice of properties determined eligible for the National Register will be published in the Federal Register at regular intervals and in a cumulative annual edition usually issued in February. Determinations in accord with § 63.3 will be identified with an asterisk.
