49 C.F.R. § 200.1 — General.
Citation: us:cfr:49:200.1
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-200.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 200 (Informal Rules Of Practice For Passenger Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part prescribes procedures under which applications will be received and heard and by which rules and orders will be issued under subsection 402(e) and section 406 of the Rail Passenger Service Act (45 U.S.C. 562(e) and 566).
