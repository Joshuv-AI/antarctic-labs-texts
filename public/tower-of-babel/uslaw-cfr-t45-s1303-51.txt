45 C.F.R. § 1303.51 — Subordination of the federal interest.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1303.51
Creator: Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1303.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1303 (Financial And Administrative Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Only the responsible HHS official can subordinate federal interest to the rights of a lender or other third party. Subordination agreements must be in writing and the mortgage agreement or security agreement for which subordination is requested must comply with § 1303.49. When the amount of federal funds already contributed to the facility exceeds the amount to be provided by the lender seeking subordination, the federal interest may only be subordinated if the grant recipient can show that funding is not available without subordination of the federal interest.
