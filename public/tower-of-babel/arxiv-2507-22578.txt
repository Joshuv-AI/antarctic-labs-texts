Nonlocal conservation laws for the two-dimensional Euler equation in vorticity form
Oleg I. Morozov

Abstract
We combine the construction of the canonical conservation law and the nonlocal cosymmetry to derive a collection of nonlocal conservation laws for the two-dimensional Euler equation in vorticity form. For computational convenience and simplicity of presentation of the results we perform a complex rotation of the independent variables.

For a differential equation

\ell^{*}_{F}(p)&=&0.\end{array}\right.

Straightforward computations prove the following three assertions.

Proposition 1.

The horizontal differential 2-form

\Omega_{q,p}=\mathcal{D}(q)\,p\,dx\wedge dy+K_{1}\,dy\wedge dt+K_{2}\,dt\wedge dx,

\hskip 0.0ptK_{1}=u_{y}\,(q\,\mathcal{D}(p)+\mathcal{D}(q)\,p)-\mathcal{D}(u_{y})\,q\,p-\textstyle{\frac{1}{2}}\,(q_{t}\,p)_{y}-q_{y}\,(p_{t}-u_{x}\,p_{y})+u_{yy}\,q\,p_{x}

\hskip 0.0ptK_{2}=-u_{x}\,(q\,\mathcal{D}(p)+\mathcal{D}(q)\,p)+\mathcal{D}(u_{x})\,q\,p+\textstyle{\frac{1}{2}}\,(q_{t}\,p)_{x}+q\,(p_{tx}-u_{xx}\,p_{y})-u_{y}\,q_{x}\,p_{x},
