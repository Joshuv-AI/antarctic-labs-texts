7 C.F.R. § 4284.1041 — Application withdrawal.
Citation: us:cfr:7:4284.1041
Creator: Rural Business-Cooperative Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-4284.1041
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 4284 (Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

During the period between the submission of the application and award approval, the Applicant must notify the Agency in writing if the project is no longer viable or if the Applicant is no longer requesting financial assistance for the project. When the Applicant notifies the Agency, the application will be withdrawn from consideration for funding.
