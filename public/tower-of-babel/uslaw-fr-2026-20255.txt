DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Foreign-Trade Zones Board

[B-8-2026]


Foreign-Trade Zone (FTZ) 230; Authorization of Limited Production 
Activity; Qorvo US, Inc.; (Semiconductor Wafers, Modules, and Chips); 
Greensboro, North Carolina

    On January 15, 2026, Qorvo US, Inc. submitted a notification of 
proposed production activity to the FTZ Board for its facility within 
Subzone 230K, in Greensboro, North Carolina.
    The notification was processed in accordance with the regulations 
of the FTZ Board (15 CFR part 400), including notice in the Federal 
Register inviting public comment (91 FR 2725, January 22, 2026). On 
September 29, 2026, the applicant was notified of the FTZ Board's 
decision that further review of part of the proposed activity is 
warranted. The FTZ Board authorized the production activity described 
in the notification on a limited basis, subject to

[[Page 62690]]

the FTZ Act and the Board's regulations, including Sec.  400.14, and 
further subject to a two-year time limitation on the use of printed 
circuit boards as foreign status components.

    Dated: September 29, 2026.
Colin Agostisi,
Executive Secretary.
