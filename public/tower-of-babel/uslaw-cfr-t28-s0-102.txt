28 C.F.R. § 0.102 — Drug enforcement policy coordination.
Citation: us:cfr:28:0.102
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-0.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 0 (Organization Of The Department Of Justice). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator of the Drug Enforcement Administration shall report to the Attorney General, through the Deputy Attorney General or the Associate Attorney General, as directed by the Attorney General.
