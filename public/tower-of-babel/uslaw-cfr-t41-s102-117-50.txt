41 C.F.R. § 102-117.50 — -117.50 Coastwise laws.

Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-117 (117—Transportation Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Coastwise laws (46 U.S.C. chapter 551) require the use of U.S.-flag, coastwise-endorsed vessels for domestic water shipments between U.S. ports.
(b) For exceptions and further information, refer to regulations issued by the U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP) (19 CFR 4.80) and MARAD.
