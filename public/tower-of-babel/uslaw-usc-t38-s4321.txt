38 U.S.C. § 4321 — Assistance in obtaining reemployment or other employment rights or benefits
Citation: us:usc:38:4321
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section4321
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part III (Readjustment And Related Benefits); Chapter 43 (Employment And Reemployment Rights Of Members Of The Uniformed Services); Subchapter III (Procedures For Assistance, Enforcement, And Investigation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary (through the Veterans’ Employment and Training Service) shall provide assistance to any person with respect to the employment and reemployment rights and benefits to which such person is entitled under this chapter. In providing such assistance, the Secretary may request the assistance of existing Federal and State agencies engaged in similar or related activities and utilize the assistance of volunteers.
