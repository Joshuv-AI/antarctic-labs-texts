7 C.F.R. § 764.205 — Security requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:7:764.205
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-764.205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 764 (Direct Loan Making). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Down payment loan must:
(a) Be secured in accordance with §§ 764.103 through 764.106;
(b) Be secured by a lien on the property being acquired with the loan funds and junior only to the party financing the balance of the purchase price.
