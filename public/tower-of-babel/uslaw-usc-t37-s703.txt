37 U.S.C. § 703 — Allotments: members of Coast Guard
Citation: us:usc:37:703
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title37-section703
Context: United States Code, Title 37 (Pay And Allowances Of The Uniformed Services); Chapter 13 (Allotments And Assignments Of Pay). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Members of the Coast Guard may, under regulations prescribed by the Secretary of Homeland Security, make allotments from their pay and allowances.
