7 U.S.C. § 2238 — Use of field work funds for purchase of arms and ammunition
Citation: us:usc:7:2238
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2238
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 55 (Department Of Agriculture). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Funds available for field work in the Department of Agriculture may be used for the purchase of arms and ammunition whenever the individual purchase does not exceed $50, and for individual purchases exceeding $50, when such arms and ammunition cannot advantageously be supplied by the Secretary of the Army pursuant to section 7655 of title 10.
