46 C.F.R. § 315.7 — Transferred vessels and contracts.
Citation: us:cfr:46:315.7
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-315.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 315 (Agency Agreements And Appointment Of Agents). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The eligibility requirements of section 315.5(a) do not apply to a contractor managing vessels owned by the United States under a contract or contracts previously awarded by another federal agency if the contract, and the vessels managed under such contract, are subsequently transferred to MARAD, provided the period of performance of the transferred contract does not exceed the period of performance of the original contract, including options.
