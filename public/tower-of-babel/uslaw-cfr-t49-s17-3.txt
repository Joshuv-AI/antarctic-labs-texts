49 C.F.R. § 17.3 — What programs and activities of the Department are subject to these regulations?
Citation: us:cfr:49:17.3
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-17.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 17 (Intergovernmental Review Of Department Of Transportation Programs And Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary publishes in the Federal Register a list of the Department's programs and activities that are subject to these regulations and identifies which of these are subject to the requirements of section 204 of the Demonstration Cities and Metropolitan Development Act.
