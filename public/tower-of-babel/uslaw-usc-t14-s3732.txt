14 U.S.C. § 3732 — Applicability of this subchapter
Citation: us:usc:14:3732
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section3732
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 37 (Coast Guard Reserve); Subchapter II (Personnel). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This subchapter applies only to the Reserve; except that it does not apply to a temporary member of the Reserve.
