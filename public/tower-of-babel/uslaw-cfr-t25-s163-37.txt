25 C.F.R. § 163.37 — Forest management research.
Citation: us:cfr:25:163.37
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-163.37
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 163 (General Forestry Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary, with the consent of the authorized Indian representatives' is authorized to perform forestry research activities to improve the basis for determining appropriate land management activities to apply to Indian forest land.
