25 C.F.R. § 39.143 — What is a small high school?
Citation: us:cfr:25:39.143
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-39.143
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 39 (The Indian School Equalization Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For purposes of this part, a small high school:
(a) Is accredited under 25 U.S.C. 2001(b);
(b) Is staffed with highly qualified teachers;
(c) Operates any combination of grades 9 through 12;
(d) Offers high school diplomas; and
(e) Has an ADM of fewer than 100 students.
