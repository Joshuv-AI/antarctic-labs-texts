16 C.F.R. § 1507.5 — Pyrotechnic leakage.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1507.5
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1507.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1507 (Fireworks Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The pyrotechnic chamber in fireworks devices shall be sealed in a manner that prevents leakage of the pyrotechnic composition during shipping, handling, and normal operation.
