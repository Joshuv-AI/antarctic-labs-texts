Policy Review: BLM Manual 6320, Considering Lands With Wilderness 
Characteristics in the BLM Land Use Planning Process

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

ACTION: Notice of policy and procedural guidance.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Bureau of Land Management (BLM) is seeking public comment 
on potential updates to BLM Manual 6320--Considering Lands with 
Wilderness Characteristics in the BLM Land Use Planning Process and is 
interested in receiving information and recommendations regarding 
whether updates, clarifications, or other revisions to existing Manual 
6320 may be appropriate.

DATES: Send your comments on Manual 6320 on or before August 14, 2026. 
The BLM is not obligated to consider comments received after this date.

Please include the words ``Considering Lands with Wilderness 
Characteristics'' as an identifier in your message.

click the ``Search'' button. Click the ``Comment Now!'' button. 
Comments can also be mailed or hand delivered to: U.S. Department of

Room 5646, Washington DC 20240, Attention: Considering Lands with 
Wilderness Characteristics.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The BLM is seeking public comment on 
potential updates to BLM Manual 6320--Considering Lands with Wilderness 
Characteristics in the BLM Land Use Planning Process. The manual 
provides policy and guidance for evaluating and considering lands with 
wilderness characteristics during land use planning under section 202 
of the Federal Land Policy and Management Act of 1976 (FLPMA) and other 
applicable laws. Consistent with FLPMA and other applicable 
authorities, the BLM considers management of public lands identified as 
having wilderness characteristics in land use plans, amendments, or 
revisions. Lands with wilderness characteristics share similar 
conditions as designated wilderness or existing wilderness study areas; 
however, their management is derived from land use planning or project-
level management decisions. The BLM is interested in receiving 
information and recommendations regarding whether updates, 
clarifications, or other revisions to existing Manual 6320 may be 
appropriate.
    Before including your address, phone number, email address, or

ask us in your comment to withhold your personal identifying

able to do so.
