45 C.F.R. § 1801.22 — Interview of Finalists with panel.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1801.22
Creator: Harry S. Truman Scholarship Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1801.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1801 (Harry S. Truman Scholarship Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Foundation invites each Finalist to an interview with a regional review panel. Panels evaluate Truman Finalists primarily on:
(a) Leadership potential and communication skills;
(b) Likelihood of “making a difference” in public service; and
(c) Intellectual strength, analytical abilities, and prospects of performing well in graduate school.
