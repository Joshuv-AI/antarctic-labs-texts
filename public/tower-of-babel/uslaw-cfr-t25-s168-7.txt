25 C.F.R. § 168.7 — Kind of livestock.
Citation: us:cfr:25:168.7
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-168.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 168 (Grazing Regulations For The Hopi Partitioned Lands Area). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless determined otherwise by the Area Director for conservation purposes, the Hopi Tribe may determine, subject to the authorized carrying capacity, the kind of livestock that may be grazed by their tribal members on the range units within the Hopi Partitioned Land area.
