48 C.F.R. § 234.7000 — 234.7000 Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:48:234.7000
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-234.7000
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 234 (Major System Acquisition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart—
(a) Implements 10 U.S.C. 3455; and
(b) Requires a determination by the Secretary of Defense and a notification to Congress before acquiring a major weapon system as a commercial product.
