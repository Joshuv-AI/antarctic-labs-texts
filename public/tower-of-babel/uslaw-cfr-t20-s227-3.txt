20 C.F.R. § 227.3 — Reduction for railroad retirement family maximum.
Citation: us:cfr:20:227.3
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-227.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 227 (Computing Supplemental Annuities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the railroad retirement family maximum applies, and the reduction amount is higher than the spouse tier II rate, as shown in part 226 of this chapter, the initial supplemental annuity rate from § 227.2 is reduced by the smaller of—
(a) The difference between the total railroad retirement maximum reduction amount and the reduction in the spouse annuity; or
(b) The total supplemental annuity rate from § 227.2.
