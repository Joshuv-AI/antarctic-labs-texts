43 U.S.C. § 422k–1 — Loan contracts for deferment of repayment installments; amendment or supplementation
Citation: us:usc:43:422k–1
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section422k–1
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 12 (Reclamation And Irrigation Of Lands By Federal Government); Subchapter IV (Construction Of Small Projects). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A loan contract negotiated and executed pursuant to this subchapter may be amended or supplemented for the purpose of deferring repayment installments in accordance with the provisions of section 485b–1(b) of this title.
