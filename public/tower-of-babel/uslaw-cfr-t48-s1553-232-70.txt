48 C.F.R. § 1553.232-70 — 1553.232-70 EPA Form 1900-3, Assignee's Release.

Creator: Chapter 15—Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1553 (Forms). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As prescribed in 1532.805-70(a), the EPA Form 1900-3 is required to be submitted by the assignee for cost-reimbursement contracts prior to final payment under the contract.
