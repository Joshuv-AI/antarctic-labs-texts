14 C.F.R. § 25.779 — Motion and effect of cockpit controls.
Citation: us:cfr:14:25.779
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-25.779
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 25 (Airworthiness Standards: Transport Category Airplanes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Cockpit controls must be designed so that they operate in accordance with the following movement and actuation:
(a) Aerodynamic controls:
(1) Primary.
(2) Secondary.
(b) Powerplant and auxiliary controls:
(1) Powerplant.
(2) Auxiliary.
