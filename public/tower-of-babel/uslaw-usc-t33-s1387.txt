33 U.S.C. § 1387 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:33:1387
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section1387
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 26 (Water Pollution Prevention And Control); Subchapter VI (State Water Pollution Control Revolving Funds). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated to carry out the purposes of this subchapter—
(1)
$2,400,000,000 for fiscal year 2022;
(2)
$2,750,000,000 for fiscal year 2023;
(3)
$3,000,000,000 for fiscal year 2024; and
(4)
$3,250,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2025 and 2026.
