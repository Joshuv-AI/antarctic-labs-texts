42 C.F.R. § 600.615 — Deposit of Federal BHP payment.

Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-600.615

HHS will make quarterly deposits into the state's BHP trust fund based on the aggregate quarterly payment amounts described in § 600.610(c).
