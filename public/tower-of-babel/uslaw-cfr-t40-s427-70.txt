40 C.F.R. § 427.70 — Applicability; description of the asbestos floor tile subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:427.70
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-427.70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 427 (Asbestos Manufacturing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to discharges resulting from the process in which asbestos, polyvinyl chloride resin, chemical stabilizers, limestone, and other fillers are used in the manufacture of asbestos floor tile.
