22 U.S.C. § 2736d–1 — Donald M. Payne International Development Fellowship Program
Citation: us:usc:22:2736d–1
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section2736d–1
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 38 (Department Of State). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Authorization
There shall be in the Department a Donald M. Payne International Development Fellowship Program.
(b) Purpose
The program required by subsection (a) shall continue to provide financial assistance, mentoring, and professional development opportunities to individuals pursuing careers in international development.
(c) Administration
The program required by subsection (a) shall be administered by the Department, consistent with existing law and regulations.
