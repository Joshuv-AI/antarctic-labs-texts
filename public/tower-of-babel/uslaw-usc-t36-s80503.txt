36 U.S.C. § 80503 — Purposes
Citation: us:usc:36:80503
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section80503
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 805 (Gold Star Wives Of America). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purposes of the corporation are—
(1)
to operate in the public interest, as a nonpartisan and nonprofit organization, solely for patriotic, charitable, literary, educational, scientific, or civic improvement purposes; and
(2)
the purposes stated in its articles of incorporation that are not inconsistent with the purposes described in clause (1) of this section.
