46 C.F.R. § 111.05-9 — -9 Masts.
Citation: us:cfr:46:111.05-9
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-111.05-9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 111 (Electric Systems—General Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each nonmetallic mast and topmast must have a lightning-ground conductor in accordance with section 10 of IEC 60092-401:1980 (incorporated by reference; see § 110.10-1 of this subchapter).
