16 U.S.C. § 410uu–2 — Cooperative agreements
Citation: us:usc:16:410uu–2
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section410uu–2
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter LIX–S (Hopewell Culture National Historical Park). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may enter into a cooperative agreement with the Ohio Historical Society, the Archeological 1 Conservancy, and other public and private entities for consultation and assistance in the interpretation and management of the park.
