30 U.S.C. § 1501 — Congressional statement of findings
Citation: us:usc:30:1501
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section1501
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 27 (Geothermal Energy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Congress finds that—
(1)
domestic geothermal reserves can be developed into regionally significant energy sources promoting the economic health and national security of the Nation;
(2)
there are institutional and economic barriers to the commercialization of geothermal technology; and
(3)
Federal agencies should consider the use of geothermal energy in the Government’s buildings.
