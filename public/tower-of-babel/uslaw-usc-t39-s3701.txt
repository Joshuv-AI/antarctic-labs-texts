39 U.S.C. § 3701 — Purpose
Citation: us:usc:39:3701
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title39-section3701
Context: United States Code, Title 39 (Postal Service); Part IV (Mail Matter); Chapter 37 (Nonpostal Services). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purpose of this chapter is to enable the Postal Service to increase its net revenues through specific nonpostal products and services that are expressly authorized by this chapter.
