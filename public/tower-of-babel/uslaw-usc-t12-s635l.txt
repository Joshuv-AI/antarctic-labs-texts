12 U.S.C. § 635l — Authorization for appropriation of funds for losses
Citation: us:usc:12:635l
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section635l
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 6A (Export-Import Bank Of The United States); Subchapter II (Export Financing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are hereby authorized to be appropriated to the Secretary of the Treasury without fiscal year limitation $100,000,000 to cover the amount of any losses which are to be borne by the Secretary of the Treasury as provided in section 635k of this title.
