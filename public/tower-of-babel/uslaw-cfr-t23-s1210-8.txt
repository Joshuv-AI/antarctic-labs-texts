23 C.F.R. § 1210.8 — Period of availability of subsequently apportioned funds.
Citation: us:cfr:23:1210.8
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration And Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-1210.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 1210 (Operation Of Motor Vehicles By Intoxicated Minors). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Funds apportioned pursuant to § 1210.7 will remain available for expenditure until the end of the third fiscal year following the fiscal year in which the funds are apportioned.
