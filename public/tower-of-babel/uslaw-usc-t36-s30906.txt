36 U.S.C. § 30906 — Restrictions
Citation: us:usc:36:30906
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section30906
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 309 (Boy Scouts Of America). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Profit.—
The corporation may not operate for pecuniary profit to its members.
(b) Stocks and Dividends.—
The corporation may not issue stock or declare or pay a dividend.
