17 U.S.C. § 805 — General rule for voluntarily negotiated agreements
Citation: us:usc:17:805
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title17-section805
Context: United States Code, Title 17 (Copyrights); Chapter 8 (Proceedings By Copyright Royalty Judges). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any rates or terms under this title that—
(1)
are agreed to by participants to a proceeding under section 803(b)(3),
(2)
are adopted by the Copyright Royalty Judges as part of a determination under this chapter, and
(3)
are in effect for a period shorter than would otherwise apply under a determination pursuant to this chapter,
shall remain in effect for such period of time as would otherwise apply under such determination, except that the Copyright Royalty Judges shall adjust the rates pursuant to the voluntary negotiations to reflect national monetary inflation during the additional period the rates remain in effect.
