5 C.F.R. § 8.4 — Positions excepted from the application of this part.
Citation: us:cfr:5:8.4
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-8.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 8 (Appointments To Overseas Positions (Rule Viii)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part shall not apply to positions in Hawaii, Puerto Rico, the Virgin Islands, and Alaska, and on the Isthmus of Panama.
