33 C.F.R. § 333.42 — Filing requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:33:333.42
Creator: Corps Of Engineers, Department Of The Army, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-333.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 333 (Processing Of Department Of The Army Permits And 33 U.S.C. 408 Permissions, National Environmental Policy Act Implementing Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The District Engineer will file environmental impact statements together with comments and any responses with the Environmental Protection Agency (EPA), Office of Federal Activities for publication in the Federal Register.
