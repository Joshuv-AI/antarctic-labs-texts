42 U.S.C. § 3542 — Public notice and comment regarding demonstration programs not expressly authorized in law
Citation: us:usc:42:3542
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section3542
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 44 (Department Of Housing And Urban Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
No demonstration program not expressly authorized in law may be commenced by the Secretary of Housing and Urban Development until (1) a description of such demonstration program is published in the Federal Register, which description may be included in a notice of funding availability; and (2) there expires a period of sixty calendar days following the date of such publication, during which period the Secretary shall fully consider any public comments submitted with respect to such demonstration program.
(b)
Nothing in this section may be considered to authorize the conducting of any demonstration program by the Secretary of Housing and Urban Development.
