33 C.F.R. § 115.10 — Sufficiency of State authority for bridges.
Citation: us:cfr:33:115.10
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-115.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 115 (Bridge Locations And Clearances; Administrative Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An opinion of the attorney general of the State as to the sufficiency of State authority for the construction of a bridge is acceptable to the Coast Guard in doubtful cases.
