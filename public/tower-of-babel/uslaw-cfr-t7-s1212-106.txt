7 C.F.R. § 1212.106 — Referendum report.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1212.106
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Miscellaneous Commodities), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1212.106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1212 (Honey Packers And Importers Research, Promotion, Consumer Education And Industry Information Order). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise directed, the referendum agent shall prepare and submit to the Administrator a report on the results of the referendum, the manner in which it was conducted, the extent and kind of public notice given, and other information pertinent to the analysis of the referendum and its results.
