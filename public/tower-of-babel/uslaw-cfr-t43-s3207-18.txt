43 C.F.R. § 3207.18 — Can my lease be extended if it is eliminated from a unit?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3207.18
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3207.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3200 (Geothermal Resource Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If your lease is eliminated from a unit under § 3283.6, it is eligible for an extension if it meets the requirements for such extension.
