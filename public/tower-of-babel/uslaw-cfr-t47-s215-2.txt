47 C.F.R. § 215.2 — Assignment of responsibilities.
Citation: us:cfr:47:215.2
Creator: Office Of Science And Technology Policy And National Security Council
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-215.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 215 (Federal Government Focal Point For Electromagnetic Pulse (Emp) Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Executive Agent, NCS, shall be the focal point within the Federal Government for all EMP technical data and studies concerning telecommunications. It shall provide such data and the results of such studies to all appropriate agencies requesting them. It shall coordinate and approve EMP telecommunications tests and studies, and shall keep the National Security Advisor informed regarding such tests and studies being conducted and planned.
