16 U.S.C. § 673e — Cooperation of Secretaries of the Interior, Agriculture and Defense with State of California
Citation: us:usc:16:673e
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section673e
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 6 (Game And Bird Preserves; Protection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Interior, the Secretary of Agriculture, and the Secretary of Defense shall cooperate with the State of California in making the lands under their respective jurisdictions reasonably available for the preservation and grazing of Tule elk in such manner and to such extent as may be consistent with Federal law.
