14 C.F.R. § 194.201 — Alternate definition of cross-country time.
Citation: us:cfr:14:194.201
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-194.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 194 (Special Federal Aviation Regulation No. 120—Powered-Lift: Pilot Certification And Training; Operations Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Notwithstanding the cross-country time definitions in § 61.1(b) of this chapter, a person may log flight time in a powered-lift as cross-country time provided the time was acquired during a flight—
(a) That includes a point of landing that was at least a straight-line distance of more than 25 nautical miles from the original point of departure; and
(b) That involves the use of dead reckoning, pilotage, electronic navigation aids, radio aids, or other navigation systems to navigate to the landing point.
