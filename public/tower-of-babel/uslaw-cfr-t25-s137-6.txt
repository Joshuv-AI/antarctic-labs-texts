25 C.F.R. § 137.6 — Power development.
Citation: us:cfr:25:137.6
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-137.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 137 (Reimbursement Of Construction Costs, San Carlos Indian Irrigation Project, Arizona). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The cost of the power development at the Coolidge Dam is hereby fixed at $735,000. The net revenues derived from the operation of this power development shall be disposed of as required by the terms and conditions of the act of March 7, 1928 (45 Stat. 210) as supplemented or amended.
