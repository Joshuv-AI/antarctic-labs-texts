13 C.F.R. § 108.210 — Minimum capital requirements for NMVC Companies.
Citation: us:cfr:13:108.210
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-108.210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 108 (New Markets Venture Capital (“Nmvc”) Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must have Regulatory Capital of at least $5,000,000 and Leverageable Capital of at least $500,000 to become a NMVC Company.
