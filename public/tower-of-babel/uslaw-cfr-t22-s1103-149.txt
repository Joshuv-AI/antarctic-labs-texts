22 C.F.R. § 1103.149 — Program accessibility: Discrimination prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:22:1103.149
Creator: International Boundary And Water Commission, United States And Mexico, United States Section
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-1103.149
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 1103 (Enforcement Of Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Handicap In Programs Or Activities Conducted By International Boundary And Water Commission, United States And Mexico, United States Section). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise provided in § 1103.150, no qualified handicapped person shall, because the agency's facilities are inaccessible to or unusable by handicapped persons, be denied the benefits of, be excluded from participation in, or otherwise be subjected to discrimination under any program or activity conducted by the agency.
