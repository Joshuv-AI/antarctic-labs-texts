20 C.F.R. § 656.11 — Substitutions and modifications to applications.
Citation: us:cfr:20:656.11
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-656.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 656 (Labor Certification Process For Permanent Employment Of Aliens In The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Substitution or change to the identity of an alien beneficiary on any application for permanent labor certification, whether filed under this part or 20 CFR part 656 in effect prior to March 28, 2005, and on any resulting certification, is prohibited for any request to substitute submitted after July 16, 2007.
(b) Requests for modifications to an application will not be accepted for applications submitted after July 16, 2007.
