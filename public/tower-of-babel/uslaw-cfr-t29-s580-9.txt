29 C.F.R. § 580.9 — Commencement of proceeding.
Citation: us:cfr:29:580.9
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-580.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 580 (Civil Money Penalties—Procedures For Assessing And Contesting Penalties). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each administrative proceeding permitted under the Act and these regulations shall be commenced upon receipt of a timely request for hearing filed in accordance with § 580.6 of this subpart.
