46 C.F.R. § 404.107 — Ratemaking step 7: Calculate average weighting factors by area.
Citation: us:cfr:46:404.107
Creator: Coast Guard (Great Lakes Pilotage), Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-404.107
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 404 (Great Lakes Pilotage Ratemaking). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director calculates the average weighting factor for each area by computing the 10-year rolling average of weighting factors applied in that area, beginning with the year 2014. If less than 10 years of data are available, the Director calculates the average weighting factor using data from each year beginning with 2014.
