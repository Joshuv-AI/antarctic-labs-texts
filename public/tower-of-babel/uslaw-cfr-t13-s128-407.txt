13 C.F.R. § 128.407 — May SBA appeal a contracting officer's decision not to make a procurement available for award as a SDVOSB contract?
Citation: us:cfr:13:128.407
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-128.407
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 128 (Veteran Small Business Certification Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The SBA Administrator may appeal a contracting officer's decision not to make a particular requirement available for award as an SDVOSB sole source or a SDVOSB set-aside contract at or above the simplified acquisition threshold.
