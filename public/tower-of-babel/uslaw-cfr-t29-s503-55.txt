29 C.F.R. § 503.55 — Final decision of the Administrative Review Board.

Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026

Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The ARB's final decision will be issued within 90 days from the notice granting the petition and served upon all parties and the ALJ.
