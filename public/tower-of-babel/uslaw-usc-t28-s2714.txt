28 U.S.C. § 2714 — Investment of proceeds of attached property
Citation: us:usc:28:2714
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section2714
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part VI (Particular Proceedings); Chapter 173 (Attachment In Postal Suits). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When the property attached is sold on an interlocutory order or is producing revenue, the money arising from the sale or revenue shall be invested, under the order of the court, in securities of the United States. The accretions therefrom are subject to the order of the court.
