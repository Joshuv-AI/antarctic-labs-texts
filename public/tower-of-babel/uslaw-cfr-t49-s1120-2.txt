49 C.F.R. § 1120.2 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1120.2
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1120.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1120 (Use Of 1977-1978 Study Of Motor Carrier Platform Handling Factors). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In any proceeding requiring the development of platform handling times for distribution of platform expense, carriers may use the results of the national weight formula contained in the Board's study, entitled 1977-1978 Motor Carrier Platform Study, Statement 2S1-79.
