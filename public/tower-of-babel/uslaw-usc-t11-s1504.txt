11 U.S.C. § 1504 — Commencement of ancillary case
Citation: us:usc:11:1504
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title11-section1504
Context: United States Code, Title 11 (Bankruptcy); Chapter 15 (Ancillary And Other Cross-Border Cases); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A case under this chapter is commenced by the filing of a petition for recognition of a foreign proceeding under section 1515.
