7 C.F.R. § 319.8-9 — -9 Hull fiber and gin trash.
Citation: us:cfr:7:319.8-9
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-319.8-9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 319 (Foreign Quarantine Notices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Entry of hull fiber will be authorized under the same conditions as are applicable to waste under this subpart.
(b) Gin trash may be imported only under the provisions of § 319.8-20.
