12 C.F.R. § 1011.5 — Reimbursement.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1011.5
Creator: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1011.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1011 (Purchasers' Revocation Rights, Sales Practices And Standards (Regulation K)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a purchaser exercises rights under 15 U.S.C. 1703(b), (c), or (d), but cannot reconvey the lot in substantially similar condition, the developer may subtract from the amount paid by the purchaser, and otherwise due to the purchaser under 15 U.S.C. 1703, any diminished value in the lot caused by the acts of the purchaser.
