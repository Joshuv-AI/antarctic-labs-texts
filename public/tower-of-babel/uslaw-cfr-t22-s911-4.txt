22 C.F.R. § 911.4 — Effect of Board decision.
Citation: us:cfr:22:911.4
Creator: Foreign Service Grievance Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-911.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 911 (Implementation Disputes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The action of the Board shall be final and binding and shall be implemented by the parties, unless an exception is filed with the Foreign Service Labor Relations Board within 30 days after receipt of the Grievance Board action.
