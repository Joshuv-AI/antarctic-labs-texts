46 C.F.R. § 193.10-1 — -1 Application.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 193 (Fire Protection Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The provisions of this subpart, with the exception of § 193.10-90, shall apply to all vessels contracted for on or after March 1, 1968.
(b) Vessels contracted for prior to March 1, 1968, shall meet the requirements of § 193.10-90.
