41 C.F.R. § 301-11.23 — -11.23 Itemization requirements for actual expense reimbursement.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-11.23
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-11.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-11 (11—Subsistence Expenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees must itemize all expenses, including meals (with each meal itemized separately), for which they will be reimbursed under the actual expense method. Receipts are required for:
(a) Lodging, regardless of amount; and
(b) Any individual meal exceeding $75 in cost.
