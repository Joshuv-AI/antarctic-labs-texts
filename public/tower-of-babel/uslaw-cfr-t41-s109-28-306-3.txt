41 C.F.R. § 109-28.306-3 — -28.306-3 Limitations on use.
Citation: us:cfr:41:109-28.306-3
Creator: Chapter 109—Department Of Energy Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-109-28.306-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 109-28 (28—Storage And Distribution). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

DOE offices and designated contractors shall establish internal controls for ensuring that the use of CSC accounts is limited to the purchase of items for official Government use.
