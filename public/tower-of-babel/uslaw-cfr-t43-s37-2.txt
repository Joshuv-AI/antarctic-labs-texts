43 C.F.R. § 37.2 — Policy.
Citation: us:cfr:43:37.2
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-37.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 37 (Cave Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It is the policy of the Secretary that Federal lands be managed in a manner which, to the extent practical, protects and maintains significant caves and cave resources. The type and degree of protection will be determined through the agency resource management planning process with full public participation.
