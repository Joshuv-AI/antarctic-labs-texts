42 C.F.R. § 86.30 — Nature and purpose of direct traineeships.
Citation: us:cfr:42:86.30
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-86.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 86 (Grants For Education Programs In Occupational Safety And Health). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A direct traineeship is an award of funds directly from the Federal Government to an individual (herein called the “trainee”) for his subsistence and other expenses during a period in which he is acquiring training (a) in the occupational safety and health professions, (b) for research relating to occupational safety and health, or (c) for teaching in occupational safety and health.
