42 C.F.R. § 457.1214 — Conflict of interest safeguards.
Citation: us:cfr:42:457.1214
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-457.1214
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 457 (Allotments And Grants To States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The State must have in effect safeguards against conflict of interest in accordance with the terms of § 438.58 of this chapter, except that references to § 438.54(b) should be read to refer to the enrollment processes described in § 457.1210(a).
