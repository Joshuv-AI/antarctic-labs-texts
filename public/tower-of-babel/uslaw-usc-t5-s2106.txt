5 U.S.C. § 2106 — Member of Congress
Citation: us:usc:5:2106
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section2106
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart A (General Provisions); Chapter 21 (Definitions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purpose of this title, “Member of Congress” means the Vice President, a member of the Senate or the House of Representatives, a Delegate to the House of Representatives, and the Resident Commissioner from Puerto Rico.
