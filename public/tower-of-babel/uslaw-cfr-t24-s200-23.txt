24 C.F.R. § 200.23 — Projects in declining neighborhoods.
Citation: us:cfr:24:200.23
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-200.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 200 (Introduction To Fha Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Mortgage financing the repair, rehabilitation or construction of a project located in an older declining urban area shall be eligible for insurance pursuant to provisions of section 223(e) of the Act and such terms and conditions established by the Commissioner.
