16 C.F.R. § 1310.2 — Definition.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1310.2
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1310.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1310 (Ban Of Inclined Sleepers For Infants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Inclined sleeper for infants means a product with an inclined sleep surface greater than ten degrees that is intended, marketed, or designed to provide sleeping accommodations for an infant up to 1 year old.
