41 C.F.R. § 102-118.90 — -118.90 Supplemental bills.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-118.90
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-118.90
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-118 (118—Transportation Payment And Audit). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies must process, review, and verify supplemental billings using the same procedures as on an original billing. Disputes are managed in accordance with agency policy.
