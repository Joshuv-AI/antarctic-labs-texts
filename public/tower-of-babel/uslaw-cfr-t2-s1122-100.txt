2 C.F.R. § 1122.100 — Purpose of this subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:2:1122.100
Creator: Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1122.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1122 (National Policy Requirements: General Award Terms And Conditions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart provides:
(a) Direction to DoD Components on how to construct the four articles of national policy requirements for inclusion in the general terms and conditions of grants and cooperative agreements.
(b) Standard wording for national policy requirements that are more commonly applicable to DoD Components' grants and cooperative agreements.
