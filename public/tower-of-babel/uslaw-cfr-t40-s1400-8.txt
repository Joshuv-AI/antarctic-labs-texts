40 C.F.R. § 1400.8 — Access to off-site consequence analysis information by Federal government officials.
Citation: us:cfr:40:1400.8
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency And Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1400.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1400 (Distribution Of Off-Site Consequence Analysis Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator shall provide any Federal government official with the OCA information requested by the official for official use. The Administrator shall provide the OCA information to the official in electronic form, unless the official specifically requests the information in paper form. The Administrator may charge a fee to cover the cost of copying OCA information in paper form.
