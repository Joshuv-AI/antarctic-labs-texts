45 C.F.R. § 1637.3 — Prohibition.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1637.3
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1637.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1637 (Representation Of Prisoners). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A recipient may not participate in any civil litigation on behalf of a person who is incarcerated in a Federal, State or local prison, whether as a plaintiff or as a defendant, nor may a recipient participate on behalf of such an incarcerated person in any administrative proceeding challenging the conditions of incarceration.
