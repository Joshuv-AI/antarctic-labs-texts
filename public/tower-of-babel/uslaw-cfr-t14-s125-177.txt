14 C.F.R. § 125.177 — Control of engine rotation.
Citation: us:cfr:14:125.177
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-125.177
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 125 (Certification And Operations: Aircraft Having A Seating Capacity Of 20 Or More Passengers Or A Maximum Payload Capacity Of 6,000 Pounds Or More; And Rules Governing Persons On Board Such Aircraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Except as provided in paragraph (b) of this section, each airplane must have a means of individually stopping and restarting the rotation of any engine in flight.
(b) In the case of turbine engine installations, a means of stopping rotation need be provided only if the Administrator finds that rotation could jeopardize the safety of the airplane.
