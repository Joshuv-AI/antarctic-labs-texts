45 C.F.R. § 2556.605 — Who is eligible to apply to serve as a leader?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2556.605
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2556.605
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2556 (Volunteers In Service To America). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An individual is eligible to apply to serve as a leader if they have successfully completed any of the following:
(a) At least one year of service as a VISTA;
(b) At least one full term of service as a full-time AmeriCorps State and National member;
(c) At least one full term of service as a member of the AmeriCorps National Civilian Community Corps (NCCC); or
(d) At least one traditional term of service as a Peace Corps Volunteer.
