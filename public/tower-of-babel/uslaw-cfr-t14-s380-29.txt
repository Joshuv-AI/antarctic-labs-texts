14 C.F.R. § 380.29 — Charter contract.
Citation: us:cfr:14:380.29
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-380.29
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 380 (Public Charters). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The charter contract between the charter operator or foreign charter operator and the direct air carrier shall evidence a binding commitment on the part of the carrier to furnish the air transportation required for the trip or trips covered by the contract.
