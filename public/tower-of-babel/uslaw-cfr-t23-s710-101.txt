23 C.F.R. § 710.101 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:23:710.101
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-710.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 710 (Right-Of-Way And Real Estate). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The primary purpose of the requirements in this part is to ensure the prudent use of Federal funds under title 23, United States Code, in the acquisition, management, and disposal of real property. In addition to the requirements of this part, other real property related provisions apply and are found at 49 CFR part 24.
