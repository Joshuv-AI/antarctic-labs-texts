46 C.F.R. § 26.03-10 — -10 Signaling light.
Citation: us:cfr:46:26.03-10
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-26.03-10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 26 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All vessels of over 150 gross tons, when engaged on an international voyage, shall be equipped with an efficient daylight signaling lamp in accordance with the requirements of subchapter J (Electrical Engineering) of this chapter.
