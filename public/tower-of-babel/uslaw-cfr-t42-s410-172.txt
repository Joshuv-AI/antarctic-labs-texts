42 C.F.R. § 410.172 — Payment for partial hospitalization services in CMHCs: Conditions.
Citation: us:cfr:42:410.172
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-410.172
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 410 (Supplementary Medical Insurance (Smi) Benefits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Medicare Part B pays for partial hospitalization services furnished in a CMHC on behalf of an individual only if the following conditions are met:
(a) The CMHC files a written request for payment on the CMS form 1450 and in the manner prescribed by CMS; and
(b) The services are furnished in accordance with the requirements described in § 410.110.
