5 C.F.R. § 550.1408 — Prohibition against payment for unused compensatory time off.
Citation: us:cfr:5:550.1408
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-550.1408
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 550 (Pay Administration (General)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As provided by 5 U.S.C. 5550b(b), an individual may not receive payment under any circumstances for any unused compensatory time off he or she earned under this subpart. This prohibition against payment applies to surviving beneficiaries in the event of the individual's death.
