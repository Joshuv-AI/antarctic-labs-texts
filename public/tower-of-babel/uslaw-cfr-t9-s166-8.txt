9 C.F.R. § 166.8 — Vehicles used to transport garbage.
Citation: us:cfr:9:166.8
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-166.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 166 (Swine Health Protection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Vehicles used by a licensee to transport untreated garbage, except those that have also been used to treat the garbage so moved, shall not be used for hauling animals or treated garbage until cleaned and disinfected as set forth in § 166.14(c) of this part.
