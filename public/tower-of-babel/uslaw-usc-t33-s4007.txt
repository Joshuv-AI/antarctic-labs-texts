33 U.S.C. § 4007 — Effect on other Federal authority
Citation: us:usc:33:4007
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section4007
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 53 (Harmful Algal Bloom And Hypoxia Research And Control). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Authority preserved
Nothing in this chapter supersedes or limits the authority of any agency to carry out its responsibilities and missions under other laws.
(b) Regulatory authority
Nothing in this chapter may be construed as establishing new regulatory authority for any agency.
