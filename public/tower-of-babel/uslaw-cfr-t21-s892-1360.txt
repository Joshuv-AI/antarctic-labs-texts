21 C.F.R. § 892.1360 — Radionuclide dose calibrator.
Citation: us:cfr:21:892.1360
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-892.1360
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 892 (Radiology Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. A radionuclide dose calibrator is a radiation detection device intended to assay radionuclides before their administration to patients.
(b) Classification. Class II.
