7 U.S.C. § 6814 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:7:6814
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section6814
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 97 (Fresh Cut Flowers And Fresh Cut Greens Promotion And Information). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
There are authorized to be appropriated for each fiscal year such sums as are necessary to carry out this chapter.
(b) Administrative expenses
Funds appropriated under subsection (a) may not be used for the payment of the expenses or expenditures of the PromoFlor Council in administering a provision of an order.
