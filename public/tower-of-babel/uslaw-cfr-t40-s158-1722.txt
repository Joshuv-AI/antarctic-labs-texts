40 C.F.R. § 158.1722 — Scorpions.
Citation: us:cfr:40:158.1722
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-158.1722
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 158 (Data Requirements For Pesticides). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the pesticide product labeling makes a claim against scorpions, then testing is required using one of the following test species: Striped bark scorpion (Centruroides vittatus) or Arizona bark scorpion (Centrurioides sculpturatus).
