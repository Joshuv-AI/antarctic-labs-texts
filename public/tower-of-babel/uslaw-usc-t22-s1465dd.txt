22 U.S.C. § 1465dd — Assistance from other Government agencies
Citation: us:usc:22:1465dd
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section1465dd
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 18 (United States Information And Educational Exchange Programs); Subchapter V–B (Television Broadcasting To Cuba). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In order to assist the Broadcasting Board of Governors in carrying out the provisions of this subchapter, any agency or instrumentality of the United States may sell, loan, lease, or grant property (including interests therein) and may perform administrative and technical support and services at the request of the Board.
