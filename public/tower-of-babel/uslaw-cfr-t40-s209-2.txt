40 C.F.R. § 209.2 — Use of number and gender.
Citation: us:cfr:40:209.2
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-209.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 209 (Rules Of Practice Governing Proceedings Under The Noise Control Act Of 1972). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In these rules of practice, words in the singular number apply to the plural and words in the masculine gender apply to the feminine and vice versa.
