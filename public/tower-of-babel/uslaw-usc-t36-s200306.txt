36 U.S.C. § 200306 — Exclusive right to name, seals, emblems, and badges
Citation: us:usc:36:200306
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section200306
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 2003 (Sons Of Union Veterans Of The Civil War). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation and its subordinate divisions have the exclusive right to use the name “Sons of Union Veterans of the Civil War”. The corporation has the exclusive right to use and to allow others to use seals, emblems, and badges the corporation adopts.
