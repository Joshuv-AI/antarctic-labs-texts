14 C.F.R. § 89.105 — Remote identification requirement.
Citation: us:cfr:14:89.105
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-89.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 89 (Remote Identification Of Unmanned Aircraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise authorized by the Administrator or as provided in § 89.120, after September 16, 2023, no person may operate an unmanned aircraft within the airspace of the United States unless the operation meets the requirements of § 89.110 or § 89.115.
