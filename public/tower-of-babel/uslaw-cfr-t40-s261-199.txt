40 C.F.R. § 261.199 — Special requirements for incompatible materials.
Citation: us:cfr:40:261.199
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-261.199
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 261 (Identification And Listing Of Hazardous Waste). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Incompatible materials must not be placed in the same tank system.
(b) Hazardous secondary material must not be placed in a tank system that has not been decontaminated and that previously held an incompatible material.
