19 C.F.R. § 162.4 — Search for letters.
Citation: us:cfr:19:162.4
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-162.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 162 (Inspection, Search, And Seizure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Customs officer may search vessels for letters which may be on board or may have been conveyed contrary to law on board any vessel or on any post route, and shall seize such letters and deliver them to the nearest post office or detain them subject to the orders of the postal authorities.
