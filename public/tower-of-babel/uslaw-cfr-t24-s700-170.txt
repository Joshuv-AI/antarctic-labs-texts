24 C.F.R. § 700.170 — Reserve for supplemental adjustment.
Citation: us:cfr:24:700.170
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Housing And Urban Development (Housing Assistance Programs And Public And Indian Housing Programs)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-700.170
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 700 (Congregate Housing Services Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary concerned may reserve funds subject to section 802(o). Requests to utilize supplemental funds by the grantee shall be transmitted to the Secretary concerned in such form as may be required.
