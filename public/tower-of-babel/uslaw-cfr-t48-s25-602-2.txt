48 C.F.R. § 25.602-2 — 25.602-2 Buy American statute.
Citation: us:cfr:48:25.602-2
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-25.602-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 25 (Foreign Acquisition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided in 25.603, use only unmanufactured construction material mined or produced in the United States, as required by the Buy American statute or, if trade agreements apply, unmanufactured construction material mined or produced in a designated country may also be used.
