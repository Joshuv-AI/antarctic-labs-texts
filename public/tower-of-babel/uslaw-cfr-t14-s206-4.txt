14 C.F.R. § 206.4 — Exemption of air carriers for military transportation.
Citation: us:cfr:14:206.4
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-206.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 206 (Certificates Of Public Convenience And Necessity: Special Authorizations And Exemptions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Air carriers providing air transportation pursuant to a contract with the Department of Defense are hereby exempted from Chapter 415 of 49 U.S.C. Subtitle VII, and from part 221, §§ 207.4 and 208.32, of this chapter, with respect to those services.
