41 C.F.R. § 302-3.209 — -3.209 Effect on return travel and transportation to place of actual residence for violating the new service agreement.
Citation: us:cfr:41:302-3.209
Creator: Chapter 302—Relocation Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-302-3.209
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 302-3 (3—Relocation Allowance By Specific Type). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the employee violates the new service agreement, the Government will reimburse the employee for return travel and transportation to the employee's place of actual residence only if the employee did not receive all of their allowances under a previous service agreement in which they successfully completed their required period of service. The Government will then authorize the reimbursement cost for return travel and transportation expenses from the former post of duty to the place of actual residence.
