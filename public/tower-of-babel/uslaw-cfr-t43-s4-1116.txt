43 C.F.R. § 4.1116 — Status of notices of violation and orders of cessation pending review by the Office of Hearings and Appeals.
Citation: us:cfr:43:4.1116
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4.1116
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4 (Department Of The Interior Hearings And Appeals Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except where temporary relief is granted pursuant to section 525(c) or section 526(c) of the act, notices of violation and orders of cessation issued under the act shall remain in effect during the pendency of review before an administrative law judge or the Board.
