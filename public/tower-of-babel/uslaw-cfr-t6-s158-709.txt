6 C.F.R. § 158.709 — Exemption from other laws regarding deployment.
Citation: us:cfr:6:158.709
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security, Office Of The Secretary
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-6/section-158.709
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 6 (Domestic Security), Part 158 (Cybersecurity Talent Management System (Ctms)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of laws, among other similar laws, listed in §§ 158.405, 158.502, and 158.605 do not apply under CTMS, to the DHS-CS, or to talent management involving the individuals described in § 158.103.
