7 C.F.R. § 277.3 — Budgets and budget revision procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:7:277.3
Creator: Food And Nutrition Administration, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-277.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 277 (Payments Of Certain Administrative Costs Of State Agencies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The preparation, content, submittal, and revision requirements for the State SNAP Budget shall be as specified in § 272.2. The application for funds and budget requirements for the Food Distribution Program on Indian Reservations shall be as specified in § 283.9. State agencies must submit a budget to FNS as part of the State Plan each fiscal year. Upon approval of the budget by FNS, administrative funds will be provided.
