Abstract
Neutrino nucleus elastic scattering ( ν ​ A e ​ l \nu{A}_{el} ) with partial quantum-mechanical coherency has been observed in several nuclei with neutrinos from decay-at-rest pions. This interaction with reactor neutrinos where coherency is > > 95% has not yet been experimentally observed. We present new results on the studies of ν ​ A e ​ l \nu{A}_{el} cross section with an electro-cooled p-type point-contact germanium detector at the Kuo-Sheng Reactor Neutrino laboratory. A total of 242(357) kg ​ - ​ days \rm kg\mbox{-}days of Reactor ON(OFF) data at a detector threshold of 200 eV in ionization energy are analyzed. The Lindhard model parametrized by a single variable k k which characterizes the quenching function was used. Limit at 90% confidence level of ρ < 4.7 \rho{<}4.7 at the predicted value of k = 0.162 k{=}0.162 is derived, where ρ \rho is the ratio of the measured to standard model (SM) cross section. Conversely, k < 0.288 k{<}0.288 at the SM-value of ρ = 1 \rho{=}1 .



The elastic scattering of neutrinos
with a nucleus
A(Z,N)

(denoted by
\nu{A}_{el}
, also represented as CE
\nu
NS in the
literature)
Freedman 1974
;
Freedman et al. 1977
:

\nu~{+}~A(Z,N)~{\rightarrow}~\nu~{+}~A(Z,N)

(1)

is a fundamental electroweak neutral current process
in the Standard Model (SM).
Its cross section at low momentum transfer (
q^{2}
)
is increased by quantum-mechanical coherency effects
relative to those of other neutrino interactions.
At typical reactor neutrino energy (
{\rm E}_{\nu}
) where
individual nucleon effects can be ignored,
the differential and total cross sections for
\nu{A}_{el}

can be approximated
Kerman et al. 2016
by, respectively:

\displaystyle\left[\frac{d\sigma}{dq^{2}}\right]

\displaystyle\approx

\displaystyle\frac{N^{2}}{2}\left[\frac{G_{F}^{2}}{4\pi}\right]\left[1-\frac{q^{2}}{4{\rm E}_{\nu}^{2}}\right]~~~{\rm and}

\displaystyle\sigma

\displaystyle\approx

\displaystyle N^{2}\left[\frac{G_{F}^{2}{\rm E}_{\nu}^{2}}{4\pi}\right]

(2)

showing an enhancement by
N^{2}
, the neutron number squared.
Studies of
\nu{A}_{el}

Abdullah et al. 2022
;
Cadeddu et al. 2023

physics beyond the SM (BSM), various astrophysical processes,
and the
neutron density distributions.
It can be applied to
supernova neutrino detection and
real-time monitoring of nuclear reactors
with compact and transportable neutrino detectors.
There are several active experimental programs on
\nu{A}_{el}
,
with neutrinos from reactors or
from decay-at-rest pions (
{\rm DAR}\mbox{-}\pi

The coherency effects of

These deviations from the perfect coherency case (

Reactor
\nu{A}_{el}

The nuclear recoil energy, however, is less than a few keV
which translates to the measurable ionization energy of
T{<}500~{\rm eV}

in germanium (Ge) detectors.
This poses severe constraints to the detector requirements.

We report in this Letter a new limit on reactor
