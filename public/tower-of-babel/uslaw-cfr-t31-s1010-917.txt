31 C.F.R. § 1010.917 — Payment of expenses.
Citation: us:cfr:31:1010.917
Creator: Financial Crimes Enforcement Network, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-1010.917
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 1010 (General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Persons summoned under this chapter shall be paid the same fees and mileage for travel in the United States that are paid witnesses in the courts of the United States. The United States shall not be liable for any other expense incurred in connection with the production of books, papers, records, or other data under this chapter.
