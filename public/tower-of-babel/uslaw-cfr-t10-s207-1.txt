10 C.F.R. § 207.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:10:207.1
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-207.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 207 (Collection Of Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this subpart is to set forth the manner of enforcement regarding the collection of energy information which the Administrator is authorized to obtain by sections 11 (a) and (b) of ESECA.
