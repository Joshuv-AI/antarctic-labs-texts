16 U.S.C. § 496 — Disposition of funds
Citation: us:usc:16:496
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section496
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 2 (National Forests); Subchapter I (Establishment And Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All funds arising from the privileges granted under section 495 of this title shall be covered into the Treasury of the United States as provided by law.
