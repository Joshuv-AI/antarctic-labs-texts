10 U.S.C. § 2781 — Availability of appropriations: exchange fees; losses in accounts
Citation: us:usc:10:2781
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section2781
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Property); Chapter 165 (Accountability And Responsibility). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Amounts appropriated to the Department of Defense may be used for—
(1)
exchange fees; and
(2)
losses in the accounts of disbursing officials and agents in accordance with law.
