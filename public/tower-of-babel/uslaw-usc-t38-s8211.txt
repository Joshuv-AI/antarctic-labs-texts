38 U.S.C. § 8211 — Declaration of purpose
Citation: us:usc:38:8211
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section8211
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part VI (Acquisition And Disposition Of Property); Chapter 82 (Assistance In Establishing New State Medical Schools; Grants To Affiliated Medical Schools; Assistance To Health Manpower Training Institutions); Subchapter I (Pilot Program For Assistance In The Establishment Of New State Medical Schools). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purpose of this subchapter is to authorize the Secretary to implement a pilot program under which the Secretary may provide assistance in the establishment of new State medical schools at colleges or universities which are primarily supported by the States in which they are located if such schools are located in proximity to, and operated in conjunction with, Department medical facilities.
