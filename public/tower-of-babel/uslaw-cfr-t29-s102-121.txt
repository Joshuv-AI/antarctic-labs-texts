29 C.F.R. § 102.121 — Rules to be liberally construed.
Citation: us:cfr:29:102.121
Creator: National Labor Relations Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-102.121
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 102 (Rules And Regulations, Series 8). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Rules and Regulations in this part will be liberally construed to effectuate the purposes and provisions of the Act.
