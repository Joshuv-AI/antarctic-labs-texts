54 U.S.C. § 302703 — Apportionment of grant funds
Citation: us:usc:54:302703
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section302703
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 3027 (Historic Preservation Programs And Authorities For Indian Tribes And Native Hawaiian Organizations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In consultation with interested Indian tribes, other Native American organizations, and affected State Historic Preservation Officers, the Secretary shall establish and implement procedures for carrying out section 302902(c)(1)(A) of this title with respect to tribal programs that assume responsibilities under section 302702 of this title.
