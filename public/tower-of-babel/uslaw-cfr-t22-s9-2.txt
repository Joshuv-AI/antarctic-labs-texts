22 C.F.R. § 9.2 — Objective.
Citation: us:cfr:22:9.2
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-9.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 9 (Security Information Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The objective of the Department's classification program is to ensure that national security information is protected from unauthorized disclosure, but that it remains classified only to the extent and for such a period as is necessary.
