30 C.F.R. § 585.426 — When must I submit my request for renewal?
Citation: us:cfr:30:585.426
Creator: Bureau Of Ocean Energy Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-585.426
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 585 (Renewable Energy On The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You must request a renewal from BOEM:
(1) No later than 180 days before the termination date of your limited lease or grant.
(2) No later than two years before the termination date of the operations period of your commercial lease.
(b) You must submit to BOEM all information we request pertaining to your lease or grant and your renewal request.
