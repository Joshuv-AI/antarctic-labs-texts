10 U.S.C. § 8467 — Degree on graduation
Citation: us:usc:10:8467
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8467
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Education And Training); Chapter 853 (United States Naval Academy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Under regulations prescribed by the Secretary of the Navy, the Superintendent of the Naval Academy may confer the degree of bachelor of science upon graduates of the Academy.
