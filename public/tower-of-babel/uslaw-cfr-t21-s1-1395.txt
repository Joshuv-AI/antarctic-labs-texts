21 C.F.R. § 1.1395 — Under what circumstances may FDA revise or revoke modified requirements or an exemption?
Citation: us:cfr:21:1.1395
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1.1395
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1 (General Enforcement Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

FDA may revise or revoke modified requirements or an exemption if we determine that such revision or revocation is necessary to protect the public health.
