42 C.F.R. § 432.45 — Applicability of provisions in subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:42:432.45
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-432.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 432 (State Personnel Administration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The rates of FFP specified in this subpart C do not apply to State personnel who conduct survey activities and certify facilities for participation in Medicaid, as provided for under section 1902(a)(33)(B) of the Act.
