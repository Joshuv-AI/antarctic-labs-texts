29 U.S.C. § 12 — Director of bureau; appointment
Citation: us:usc:29:12
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section12
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 2 (Women’S Bureau). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Women’s Bureau shall be in charge of a director, a woman, to be appointed by the President.
