49 C.F.R. § 5.53 — General.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 5 (Administrative Rulemaking, Guidance, And Enforcement Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The requirements set forth in this subpart apply to all enforcement actions taken by each DOT operating administration (OA) and each component of the Office of the Secretary of Transportation (OST) with enforcement authority.
