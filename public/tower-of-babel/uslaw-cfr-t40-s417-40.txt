40 C.F.R. § 417.40 — Applicability; description of the glycerine concentration subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:417.40
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-417.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 417 (Soap And Detergent Manufacturing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to discharges resulting from the concentration of sweet water from saponification or fat splitting to approximately 60 to 80 percent crude glycerine content.
