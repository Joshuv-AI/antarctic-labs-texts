20 C.F.R. § 361.12 — Procedures for salary offset: Types of collection.
Citation: us:cfr:20:361.12
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-361.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 361 (Recovery Of Debts Owed To The United States Government By Government Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A debt will be collected in a lump sum or in installments. Collection will be effected in one lump sum collection unless the employee is financially unable to pay in one lump sum, or if the amount of the debt exceeds 15 percent of disposable pay. In these cases, deduction will be by installments.
