10 C.F.R. § 20.1701 — Use of process or other engineering controls.
Citation: us:cfr:10:20.1701
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-20.1701
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 20 (Standards For Protection Against Radiation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The licensee shall use, to the extent practical, process or other engineering controls (e.g., containment, decontamination, or ventilation) to control the concentration of radioactive material in air.
