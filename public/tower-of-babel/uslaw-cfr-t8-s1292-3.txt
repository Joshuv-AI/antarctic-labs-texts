8 C.F.R. § 1292.3 — Conduct for practitioners and recognized organizations—rules and procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:8:1292.3
Creator: Executive Office For Immigration Review, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-1292.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 1292 (Representation And Appearances). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Practitioners, as defined in § 1003.101(b) of this chapter, and recognized organizations are subject to the imposition of sanctions as provided in 8 CFR part 1003, subpart G, § 1003.101 et seq., and 8 CFR 292.3 (pertaining to practice before DHS).
