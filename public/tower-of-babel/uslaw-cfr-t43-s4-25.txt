43 C.F.R. § 4.25 — Oral argument and status conferences.
Citation: us:cfr:43:4.25
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4 (Department Of The Interior Hearings And Appeals Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director or the presiding officer or an Appeals Board may, in their discretion, order or grant upon a written request, an opportunity for oral argument or status conferences. An oral argument or status conference may be conducted by video, teleconferencing, or other suitable technology.
