39 C.F.R. § 3030.240 — General.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 3030 (Regulation Of Rates For Market Dominant Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless a specific exception applies, unused rate adjustment authority, on a class-by-class basis, shall be calculated for each rate adjustment filing. Unused rate adjustment authority shall be added to the schedule of banked rate authority in each instance, and be available for application to rate adjustments pursuant to the requirements of this subpart.
