20 C.F.R. § 618.410 — Applying for a job search allowance.
Citation: us:cfr:20:618.410
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-618.410
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 618 (Trade Adjustment Assistance Under The Trade Act Of 1974, As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Forms. To receive a job search allowance, an AAW must apply to the State, using the State's process.
(b) Submittal. An AAW must apply for a job search allowance before beginning a job search to be funded by such an allowance.
