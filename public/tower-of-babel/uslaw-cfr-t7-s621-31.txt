7 C.F.R. § 621.31 — Who may request assistance.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 621 (River Basin Investigations And Surveys). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any organization, group, or State or local government may request assistance.
