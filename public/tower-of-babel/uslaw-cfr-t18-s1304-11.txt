18 C.F.R. § 1304.11 — Little Tennessee River; date of formal submission.
Citation: us:cfr:18:1304.11
Creator: Tennessee Valley Authority
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-1304.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 1304 (Approval Of Construction In The Tennessee River System And Regulation Of Structures And Other Alterations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As regards structures on the Little Tennessee River, applications are deemed by TVA to be formally submitted within the meaning of section 26a of the Act, on that date upon which applicant has complied in good faith with all applicable provisions of § 1304.2.
