43 C.F.R. § 8.5 — Mineral rights.
Citation: us:cfr:43:8.5
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-8.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 8 (Joint Policies Of The Departments Of The Interior And Of The Army Relative To Reservoir Project Lands). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Mineral, oil and gas rights will not be acquired except where the development thereof would interfere with project purposes, but mineral rights not acquired will be subordinated to the Government's right to regulate their development in a manner that will not interfere with the primary purposes of the project, including public access.
