34 C.F.R. § 657.33 — What are the reporting requirements for grantee institutions and for individual fellows who receive funds under this program?
Citation: us:cfr:34:657.33
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-657.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 657 (Foreign Language And Area Studies Fellowships Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each institution of higher education, each member in a consortium of institutions of higher education, and each individual fellowship recipient under this program must submit performance reports, in such form and at such time as required by the Secretary.
