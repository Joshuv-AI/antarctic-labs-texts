5 C.F.R. § 2425.10 — Authority decision.
Citation: us:cfr:5:2425.10
Creator: Federal Labor Relations Authority, General Counsel Of The Federal Labor Relations Authority And Federal Service Impasses Panel
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-2425.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 2425 (Review Of Arbitration Awards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Authority shall issue its decision and order taking such action and making such recommendations concerning the award as it considers necessary, consistent with applicable laws, rules, or regulations.
