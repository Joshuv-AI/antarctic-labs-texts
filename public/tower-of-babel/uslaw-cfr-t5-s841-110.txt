5 C.F.R. § 841.110 — Garnishment of FERS payments.
Citation: us:cfr:5:841.110
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-841.110
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 841 (Federal Employees Retirement System—General Administration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

FERS payments are not subject to execution, levy, attachment, garnishment or other legal process except as expressly provided by Federal law.
