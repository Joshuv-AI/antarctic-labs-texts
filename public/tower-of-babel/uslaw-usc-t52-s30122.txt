52 U.S.C. § 30122 — Contributions in name of another prohibited
Citation: us:usc:52:30122
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title52-section30122
Context: United States Code, Title 52 (Voting And Elections); Chapter 301 (Federal Election Campaigns); Subchapter I (Disclosure Of Federal Campaign Funds). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No person shall make a contribution in the name of another person or knowingly permit his name to be used to effect such a contribution, and no person shall knowingly accept a contribution made by one person in the name of another person.
