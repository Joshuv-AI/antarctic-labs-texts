33 C.F.R. § 126.1 — What does this part apply to?
Citation: us:cfr:33:126.1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-126.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 126 (Handling Of Dangerous Cargo At Waterfront Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to waterfront facilities handling packaged and bulk-solid dangerous cargo and to vessels at those facilities.
