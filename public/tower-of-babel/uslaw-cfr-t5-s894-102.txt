5 C.F.R. § 894.102 — If I have a pre-existing dental or vision condition, may I join FEDVIP?
Citation: us:cfr:5:894.102
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-894.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 894 (Federal Employees Dental And Vision Insurance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. Pre-existing conditions do not exclude you from coverage under FEDVIP. The Administrator may not deny an individual the right to enroll solely because of a preexisting dental or vision condition.
