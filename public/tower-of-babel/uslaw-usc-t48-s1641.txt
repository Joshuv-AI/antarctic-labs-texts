48 U.S.C. § 1641 — Method of payment of official salaries
Citation: us:usc:48:1641
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section1641
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 12 (Virgin Islands [1954]); Subchapter VII (Fiscal Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The salaries and travel allowances of the Governor, Lieutenant Governor, the heads of the executive departments, other officers and employees of the government of the Virgin Islands, and the members of the legislature shall be paid by the government of the Virgin Islands at rates prescribed by the laws of the Virgin Islands.
