45 C.F.R. § 2519.110 — Who may apply for a grant?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2519.110
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2519.110
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2519 (Higher Education Innovative Programs For Community Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following entities may apply for a grant from the Corporation: (a) An institution of higher education.
(b) A consortium of institutions of higher education.
(c) A higher education partnership, as defined in § 2510.20 of this chapter.
