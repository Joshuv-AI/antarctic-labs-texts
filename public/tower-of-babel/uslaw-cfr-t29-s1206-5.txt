29 C.F.R. § 1206.5 — Decertification of representatives.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1206.5
Creator: National Mediation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1206.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1206 (Handling Representation Disputes Under The Railway Labor Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees who no longer wish to be represented may seek to decertify the current representative of a craft or class in a direct election. The employees must follow the procedure outlines in § 1203.2.
