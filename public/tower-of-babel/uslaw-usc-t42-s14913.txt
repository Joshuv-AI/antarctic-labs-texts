42 U.S.C. § 14913 — Responsibilities of the Attorney General
Citation: us:usc:42:14913
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section14913
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 143 (Intercountry Adoptions); Subchapter I (United States Central Authority). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In addition to such other responsibilities as are specifically conferred upon the Attorney General by this chapter, the central authority functions specified in Article 14 of the Convention (relating to the filing of applications by prospective adoptive parents to the central authority of their country of residence) shall be performed by the Attorney General.
