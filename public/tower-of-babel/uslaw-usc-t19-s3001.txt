19 U.S.C. § 3001 — Purposes
Citation: us:usc:19:3001
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section3001
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 18 (Implementation Of Harmonized Tariff Schedule). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purposes of this chapter are—
(1)
to approve the International Convention on the Harmonized Commodity Description and Coding System;
(2)
to implement in United States law the nomenclature established internationally by the Convention; and
(3)
to provide that the Convention shall be treated as a trade agreement obligation of the United States.
