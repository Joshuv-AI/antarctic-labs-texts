24 C.F.R. § 42.301 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:24:42.301
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-42.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 42 (Displacement, Relocation Assistance, And Real Property Acquisition For Hud And Hud-Assisted Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies only to CDBG grants under 24 CFR part 570, subparts D, F, and I (Entitlement grants, HUD-Administered Small Cities, and State programs); grants under 24 CFR part 570, subpart G (Urban Development Action Grants), and Loan Guarantees under 24 CFR part 570, subpart M; and assistance to State and local governments under 24 CFR part 92 (HOME program).
