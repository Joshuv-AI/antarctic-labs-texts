25 U.S.C. § 291 — Removal of Government property at schools
Citation: us:usc:25:291
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section291
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 7 (Education Of Indians). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Where there is Government property on hand at any of the Indian reservations or schools not required for the use or benefit of the Indians of reservations or said schools, the Secretary of the Interior is authorized to move such property to other Indian reservations or schools where it may be required.
