45 C.F.R. § 1613.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1613.1
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1613.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1613 (Restrictions On Legal Assistance With Respect To Criminal Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part is designed to ensure that Corporation funds will not be used to provide legal assistance with respect to criminal proceedings unless such assistance is authorized by this part.
