2 C.F.R. § 5800.930 — Debarring official.
Citation: us:cfr:2:5800.930
Creator: Election Assistance Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-5800.930
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 5800 (Nonprocurement Debarment And Suspension). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For the Commission, the debarring official for all nonprocurement transactions is the Commission's Contracting Officer. In the case of a vacancy in the position of the Contracting Officer, the alternate debarring official is the Chief Financial Officer.
