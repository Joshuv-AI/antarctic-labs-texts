42 U.S.C. § 300d–45 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:42:300d–45
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section300d–45
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter X (Trauma Care); Part D (Trauma Centers Operating In Areas Severely Affected By Drug-Related Violence). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purpose of carrying out this part, there are authorized to be appropriated $100,000,000 for fiscal year 2009, and such sums as may be necessary for each of fiscal years 2010 through 2015. Such authorization of appropriations is in addition to any other authorization of appropriations or amounts that are available for such purpose.
