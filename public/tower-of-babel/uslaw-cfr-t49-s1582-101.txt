49 C.F.R. § 1582.101 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1582.101
Creator: Transportation Security Administration, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1582.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1582 (Public Transportation And Passenger Railroad Security). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The requirements of this subpart apply to the following:
(a) Amtrak (also known as the National Railroad Passenger Corporation).
(b) Each owner/operator identified in Appendix A to this part.
(c) Each owner/operator described in § 1582.1(a)(1) through (3) that serves as a host railroad to a freight operation described in § 1580.101 of this subchapter or to a passenger train operation described in paragraph (a) or (b) of this section.
