30 C.F.R. § 756.18 — Required amendments to the Hopi Tribe's abandoned mine land reclamation plan.
Citation: us:cfr:30:756.18
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-756.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 756 (Indian Tribe Abandoned Mine Land Reclamation Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Pursuant to 30 CFR 884.15, the Hopi Tribe is required to submit to OSM by the date specified either a proposed amendment or a reasonable timetable, which is consistent with the Hopi Tribe's established administrative and legislative procedures, for submitting an amendment to the Hopi Tribe plan.
