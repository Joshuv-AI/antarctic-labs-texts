34 C.F.R. § 642.10 — What types of projects does the Secretary assist?
Citation: us:cfr:34:642.10
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-642.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 642 (Training Program For Federal Trio Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary assists projects that train the leadership personnel and staff of projects funded under the Federal TRIO Programs to enable them to operate those projects more effectively.
