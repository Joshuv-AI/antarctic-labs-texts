7 U.S.C. § 3601 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:7:3601
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section3601
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 67 (Implementation Of International Sugar Agreement, 1977). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For purposes of this chapter—
(1)
The term “Agreement” means the International Sugar Agreement, 1977, signed at New York City on December 9, 1977.
(2)
The term “sugar” has the same meaning as is given to such term in paragraph (12) of Article 2 of the Agreement.
(3)
The term “entry” means entry, or withdrawal from warehouse, for consumption in the customs territory of the United States.
