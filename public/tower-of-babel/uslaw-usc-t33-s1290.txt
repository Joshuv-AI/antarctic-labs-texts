33 U.S.C. § 1290 — Annual survey
Citation: us:usc:33:1290
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section1290
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 26 (Water Pollution Prevention And Control); Subchapter II (Grants For Construction Of Treatment Works). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator shall annually make a survey to determine the efficiency of the operation and maintenance of treatment works constructed with grants made under this chapter, as compared to the efficiency planned at the time the grant was made. The results of such annual survey shall be included in the report required under section 1375(a) of this title.
