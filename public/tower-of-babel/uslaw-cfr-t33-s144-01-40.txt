33 C.F.R. § 144.01-40 — -40 Emergency communications equipment.
Citation: us:cfr:33:144.01-40
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-144.01-40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 144 (Lifesaving Appliances). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

On manned platforms means of communication by radio and/or wire telephone shall be provided for contacting the shore or vessels in the vicinity for aid in the event of an emergency.
