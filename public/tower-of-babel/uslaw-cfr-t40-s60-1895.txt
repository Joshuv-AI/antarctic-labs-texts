40 C.F.R. § 60.1895 — If a semiannual report is required, when must I submit it?
Citation: us:cfr:40:60.1895
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-60.1895
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 60 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) For data collected during the first half of a calendar year, submit your semiannual report by August 1 of that year.
(b) For data you collected during the second half of the calendar year, submit your semiannual report by February 1 of the following year.
