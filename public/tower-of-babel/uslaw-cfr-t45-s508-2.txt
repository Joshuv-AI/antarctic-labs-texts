45 C.F.R. § 508.2 — Request for hearing.
Citation: us:cfr:45:508.2
Creator: Foreign Claims Settlement Commission Of The United States, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-508.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 508 (Hearings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Within 30 days after the Commission's notice of denial of a claim, or approval for a lesser amount than claimed, has been posted by the Commission, the claimant, if a hearing is desired, must notify the Commission in writing, and must set forth in full the reasons for requesting the hearing, including any statement of law or facts upon which the claimant relies.
