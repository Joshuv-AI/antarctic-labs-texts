26 U.S.C. § 1399 — No separate taxable entities for partnerships, corporations, etc.
Citation: us:usc:26:1399
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section1399
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 1 (Normal Taxes And Surtaxes); Subchapter V (Title 11 Cases). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except in any case to which section 1398 applies, no separate taxable entity shall result from the commencement of a case under title 11 of the United States Code.
