7 U.S.C. § 348 — Rules and regulations
Citation: us:usc:7:348
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section348
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 13 (Agricultural And Mechanical Colleges); Subchapter IV (Agricultural Extension Work Appropriation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Agriculture is authorized to make such rules and regulations as may be necessary for carrying out the provisions of this subchapter.
