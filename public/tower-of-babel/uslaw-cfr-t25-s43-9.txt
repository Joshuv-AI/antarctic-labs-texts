25 C.F.R. § 43.9 — Procedures for granting access.
Citation: us:cfr:25:43.9
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-43.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 43 (Maintenance And Control Of Student Records In Bureau Schools). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each educational institution shall establish appropriate procedures for granting a request by parents for access to the records of their children, or by eligible students for access to their own records within a reasonable period of time. In no case shall access be withheld more than forty-five (45) days after the request has been made.
