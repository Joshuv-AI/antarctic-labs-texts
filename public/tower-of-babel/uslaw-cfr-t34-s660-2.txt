34 C.F.R. § 660.2 — Who is eligible to apply for grants under this program?
Citation: us:cfr:34:660.2
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-660.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 660 (The International Research And Studies Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Public and private agencies, organizations, and institutions, and individuals are eligible to apply for grants under this part.
