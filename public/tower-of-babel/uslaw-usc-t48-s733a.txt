48 U.S.C. § 733a — Citizens; residence in island of citizens of United States
Citation: us:usc:48:733a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section733a
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 4 (Puerto Rico); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All citizens of the United States who have resided or who shall after March 4, 1927, reside in the island for one year shall be citizens of Puerto Rico.
