50 C.F.R. § 228.8 — Mailing address.
Citation: us:cfr:50:228.8
Creator: National Marine Fisheries Service, National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-228.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 228 (NOTICE AND HEARING ON SECTION 103(d) REGULATIONS). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless otherwise specified in the notice of hearing, all direct testimony shall be addressed to the Presiding Officer, c/o Assistant Administrator, National Marine Fisheries Service, 1315 East-West Highway, Silver Spring, MD 20910. All affidavits and exhibits shall be clearly marked with the docket number of the proceedings.
