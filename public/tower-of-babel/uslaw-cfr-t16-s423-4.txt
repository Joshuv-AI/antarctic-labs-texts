16 C.F.R. § 423.4 — Who is covered.
Citation: us:cfr:16:423.4
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-423.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 423 (Care Labeling Of Textile Wearing Apparel And Certain Piece Goods As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Manufacturers and importers of textile wearing apparel and certain piece goods are covered by this regulation. This includes any person or organization that directs or controls the manufacture or importation of covered products.
