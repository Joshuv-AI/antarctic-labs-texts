49 C.F.R. § 1242.49 — Equipment damaged (account XX-27-48).
Citation: us:cfr:49:1242.49
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1242.49
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1242 (Separation Of Common Operating Expenses Between Freight Service And Passenger Service For Railroads 1). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Separate common expenses according to distribution of common expenses in machinery, passenger and other revenue equipment, computer and data processing equipment and work and other non-revenue equipment accounts (accounts XX-27-40, XX-27-45, XX-27-46, and XX-27-47).
