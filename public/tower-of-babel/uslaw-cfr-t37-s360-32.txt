37 C.F.R. § 360.32 — Reinstatement of previously withdrawn claims.
Citation: us:cfr:37:360.32
Creator: Copyright Royalty Board, Library Of Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-360.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 360 (Filing Of Claims To Royalty Fees Collected Under Compulsory License). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Once a claimant has withdrawn a claim, that claim may be reinstated only by order of the Copyright Royalty Judges, on motion showing good cause and lack of prejudice to other claimants to the applicable year's royalty funds.
