20 C.F.R. § 402.115 — The FOIA Exemption 1: National defense and foreign policy.
Citation: us:cfr:20:402.115
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-402.115
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 402 (Availability Of Information And Records To The Public). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Exemption 1 protects from disclosure information specifically authorized under criteria established by an Executive order to be kept secret in the interest of national defense or foreign policy and are in fact properly classified pursuant to such Executive order.
