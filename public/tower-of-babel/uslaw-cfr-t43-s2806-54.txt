43 C.F.R. § 2806.54 — Rent for energy storage facilities that are not part of a solar or wind energy development facility.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2806.54
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2806.54
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2800 (Rights-Of-Way Under The Federal Land Policy And Management Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Rent for energy storage facilities that are not part of a solar or wind energy development facility will be determined pursuant to the linear rent formula set forth in § 2806.23. The BLM may determine your rent pursuant to § 2806.70 if we determine the linear rent schedule does not apply.
