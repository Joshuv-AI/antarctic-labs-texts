2 C.F.R. § 930.415 — Government participation.
Citation: us:cfr:2:930.415
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-930.415
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 930 (Other Transaction Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Technology Investment Agreements is used to carry out cooperative relationships between the Federal Government and the awardee(s) which require substantial involvement of the Government in the technical and/or management aspects of the research, development, and demonstration (RD&D) project.
