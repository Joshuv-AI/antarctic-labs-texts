30 C.F.R. § 926.20 — Approval of Montana abandoned mine land reclamation plan.
Citation: us:cfr:30:926.20
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-926.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 926 (Montana). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Montana Abandoned Mine Land Reclamation Plan, as submitted on June 16, 1980, and as revised on July 28, 1980, is approved effective November 24, 1980. Copies of the approved plan are available at:
(a) Montana Department of Environmental Quality, 1625 Eleventh Avenue, Helena, MT 59620-1601.
(b) Office of Surface Mining Reclamation and Enforcement, Casper Field Office, 100 East B Street, Room 2128, Casper, WY 82601-1918.
