29 U.S.C. § 2009 — Effect on other law and agreements
Citation: us:usc:29:2009
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section2009
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 22 (Employee Polygraph Protection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as provided in subsections (a), (b), and (c) of section 2006 of this title, this chapter shall not preempt any provision of any State or local law or of any negotiated collective bargaining agreement that prohibits lie detector tests or is more restrictive with respect to lie detector tests than any provision of this chapter.
