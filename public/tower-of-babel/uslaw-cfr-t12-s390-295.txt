12 C.F.R. § 390.295 — Low-rent housing.
Citation: us:cfr:12:390.295
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-390.295
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 390 (Regulations Transferred From The Office Of Thrift Supervision). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term low-rent housing means real estate which is, or which is being constructed, remodeled, rehabilitated, modernized, or renovated to be, the subject of an annual contributions contract for low-rent housing under the provisions of the United States Housing Act of 1937, as amended.
