33 C.F.R. § 327.6 — Legal adviser.
Citation: us:cfr:33:327.6
Creator: Corps Of Engineers, Department Of The Army, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-327.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 327 (Public Hearings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

At each public hearing, the district counsel or his designee may serve as legal advisor to the presiding officer. In appropriate circumstances, the district engineer may waive the requirement for a legal advisor to be present.
