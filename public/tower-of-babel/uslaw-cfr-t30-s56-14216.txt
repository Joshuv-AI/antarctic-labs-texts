30 C.F.R. § 56.14216 — Backpoling.
Citation: us:cfr:30:56.14216
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-56.14216
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 56 (Safety And Health Standards—Surface Metal And Nonmetal Mines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Backpoling of trolleys is prohibited except where there is inadequate clearance to reverse the trolley pole. Where backpoling is required, it shall be done only at the minimum tram speed of the trolley.
