10 U.S.C. § 1071 — Purpose of this chapter
Citation: us:usc:10:1071
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1071
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 55 (Medical And Dental Care). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purpose of this chapter is to create and maintain high morale in the uniformed services by providing an improved and uniform program of medical and dental care for members and certain former members of those services, and for their dependents.
