25 C.F.R. § 47.1 — What is the purpose of this part?
Citation: us:cfr:25:47.1
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-47.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 47 (Uniform Direct Funding And Support For Bureau-Operated Schools). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part contains the requirements for developing local educational financial plans that Bureau-operated schools need in order to receive direct funding from the Bureau of Indian Affairs under section 1127 of the Act.
