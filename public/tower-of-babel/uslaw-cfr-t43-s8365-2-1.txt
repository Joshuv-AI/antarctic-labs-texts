43 C.F.R. § 8365.2-1 — -1 Sanitation.
Citation: us:cfr:43:8365.2-1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-8365.2-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 8360 (Visitor Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

On developed recreation sites and areas, no person shall, unless otherwise authorized:
(a) Clean fish, game, other food, clothing or household articles at any outdoor hydrant, pump, faucet or fountain, or restroom water faucet;
(b) Deposit human waste except in toilet or sewage facilities provided for that purpose; or
(c) Bring an animal into such an area unless the animal is on a leash not longer than 6 feet and secured to a fixed object or under control of a person, or is otherwise physically restricted at all times.
