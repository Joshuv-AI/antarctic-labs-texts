33 C.F.R. § 106.140 — Maritime Security (MARSEC) Directive.
Citation: us:cfr:33:106.140
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-106.140
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 106 (Marine Security: Outer Continental Shelf (Ocs) Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All OCS facility owners or operators subject to this part must comply with any instructions contained in a MARSEC Directive issued under § 101.405 of this subchapter.
