7 C.F.R. § 996.15 — Positive lot identification.
Citation: us:cfr:7:996.15
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-996.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 996 (Minimum Quality And Handling Standards For Domestic And Imported Peanuts Marketed In The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Positive lot identification is a means of identifying those peanuts meeting outgoing quality regulations as defined in § 996.31 and relating the inspection certificate issued by the Inspection Service, as defined in § 996.10, to the lot covered so that there is no doubt that the peanuts in the lot are the same peanuts described on the inspection certificate.
