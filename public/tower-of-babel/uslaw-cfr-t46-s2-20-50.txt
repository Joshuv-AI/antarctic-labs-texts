46 C.F.R. § 2.20-50 — -50 Repairs or alterations in lifesaving or fire prevention equipment.
Citation: us:cfr:46:2.20-50
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-2.20-50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 2 (Vessel Inspections). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No repairs or alterations shall be made to any lifesaving or fire-detecting or fire-extinguishing equipment, except in an emergency, without advance notice to the Officer in Charge, Marine Inspection. See §§ 78.33-10 and 97.30-10 of this chapter.
