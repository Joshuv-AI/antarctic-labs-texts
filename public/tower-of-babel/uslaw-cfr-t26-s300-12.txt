26 C.F.R. § 300.12 — Fee for estate tax closing letter.
Citation: us:cfr:26:300.12
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-300.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 300 (User Fees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Applicability. This section applies to the request by a person described in paragraph (c) of this section for an estate tax closing letter from the IRS.
(b) Fee. The fee for issuing an estate tax closing letter is $76.
(c) Person liable for the fee. The person liable for the fee is the estate of the decedent or other person requesting, in accordance with applicable procedures and policies, an estate tax closing letter to be issued with respect to the estate.
(d) Applicability date. This section applies to requests for estate tax closing letters received by the IRS after October 26, 2026.
