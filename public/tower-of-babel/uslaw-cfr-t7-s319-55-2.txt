7 C.F.R. § 319.55-2 — -2 Application for permit.
Citation: us:cfr:7:319.55-2
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-319.55-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 319 (Foreign Quarantine Notices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Application for a permit to import from any country rice straw or rice hulls may be made to the Plant Protection and Quarantine Programs in accordance with §§ 319.7 through 319.7-5.
