7 C.F.R. § 2500.013 — Types of proposals.
Citation: us:cfr:7:2500.013
Creator: Office Of Advocacy And Outreach, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-2500.013
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 2500 (Oao Federal Financial Assistance Programs—General Award Administrative Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The type of proposal acceptable may vary by funding opportunity. The RFP will stipulate what will be required for submission to OAO in response to the funding opportunity.
