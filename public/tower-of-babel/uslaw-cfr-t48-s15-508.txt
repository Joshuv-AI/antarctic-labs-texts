48 C.F.R. § 15.508 — 15.508 Discovery of mistakes.
Citation: us:cfr:48:15.508
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-15.508
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 15 (Contracting By Negotiation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Mistakes in a contractor's proposal that are disclosed after award shall be processed substantially in accordance with the procedures for mistakes in bids at 14.407-4.
