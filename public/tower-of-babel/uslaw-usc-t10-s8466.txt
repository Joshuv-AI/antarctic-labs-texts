10 U.S.C. § 8466 — Course of study
Citation: us:usc:10:8466
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8466
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Education And Training); Chapter 853 (United States Naval Academy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The course at the Naval Academy is four years.
(b)
The Secretary of the Navy shall arrange the course so that classes will not be held on Sunday.
