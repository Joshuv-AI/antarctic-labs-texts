5 C.F.R. § 835.607 — Administrative charges.
Citation: us:cfr:5:835.607
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-835.607
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 835 (Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All administrative charges incurred in connection with the referral of the debts to the IRS will be assessed on the debt and thus increase the amount of the offset.
