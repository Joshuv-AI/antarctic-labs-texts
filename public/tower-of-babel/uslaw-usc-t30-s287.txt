30 U.S.C. § 287 — Extension of prospecting permits
Citation: us:usc:30:287
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section287
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 3A (Leases And Prospecting Permits); Subchapter IX (Potash). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any prospecting permit issued under this subchapter may be extended by the Secretary of the Interior for a period not exceeding two years, upon a showing of satisfactory cause.
