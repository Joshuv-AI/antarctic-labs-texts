36 C.F.R. § 51.13 — When will the Director determine if proposals are responsive?
Citation: us:cfr:36:51.13
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-51.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 51 (Concession Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director will determine if proposals are responsive or non-responsive prior to or as of the date of selection of the best proposal.
