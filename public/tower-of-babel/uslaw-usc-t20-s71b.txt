20 U.S.C. § 71b — Status of completed addition
Citation: us:usc:20:71b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section71b
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 3 (Smithsonian Institution, National Museums And Art Galleries); Subchapter II (National Gallery Of Art). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any building constructed under authority of section 71a of this title shall, upon completion, be a part of the National Gallery of Art.
