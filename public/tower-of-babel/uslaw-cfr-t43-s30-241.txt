43 C.F.R. § 30.241 — May I submit another petition for rehearing?
Citation: us:cfr:43:30.241
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-30.241
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 30 (Indian Probate Hearings Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No. Successive petitions for rehearing may not be filed by the same party or BIA in the same probate case.
