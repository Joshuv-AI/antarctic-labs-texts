42 C.F.R. § 423.900 — Basis and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:42:423.900
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-423.900
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 423 (Voluntary Medicare Prescription Drug Benefit). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Basis. This subpart is based on sections 1935(a) through (d) of the Act as amended by section 103 of the MMA.
(b) Scope. This subpart specifies State agency obligations for the Part D prescription drug benefit.
