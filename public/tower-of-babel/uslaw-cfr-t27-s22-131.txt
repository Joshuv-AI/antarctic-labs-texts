27 C.F.R. § 22.131 — General.
Citation: us:cfr:27:22.131
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-22.131
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 22 (Distribution And Use Of Tax-Free Alcohol). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person or permittee conducting recovery operations of tax-free alcohol shall be qualified by the terms of their permit to do so, under the provision of subpart D of this part. Restoration of recovered tax-free alcohol may only be accomplished on the permit premises or by the proprietor of a distilled spirits plant.
