49 C.F.R. § 234.255 — Gate arm and gate mechanism.
Citation: us:cfr:49:234.255
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-234.255
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 234 (Grade Crossing Safety). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each gate arm and gate mechanism shall be inspected at least once each month.
(b) Gate arm movement shall be observed for proper operation at least once each month.
(c) Hold-clear devices shall be tested for proper operation at least once every 12 months.
