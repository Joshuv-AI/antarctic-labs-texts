25 C.F.R. § 158.58 — Disposition of proceeds of partition sales.
Citation: us:cfr:25:158.58
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-158.58
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 158 (Osage Lands). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Owelty moneys due members of the Osage Tribe who do not have certificates of competency shall be paid into the Treasury of the United States and placed to the credit of the Indians upon the same conditions as attach to segregated shares of the Osage national fund.
