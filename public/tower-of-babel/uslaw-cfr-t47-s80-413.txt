47 C.F.R. § 80.413 — On-board station equipment records.
Citation: us:cfr:47:80.413
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-80.413
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 80 (Stations In The Maritime Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The licensee of an on-board station must keep equipment records which show:
(1) The ship name and identification of the on-board station;
(2) The number and type of repeater and mobile units used on-board the vessel; and
(3) The date and type of equipment which is added or removed from the on-board station.
