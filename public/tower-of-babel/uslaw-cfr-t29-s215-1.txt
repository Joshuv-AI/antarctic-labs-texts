29 C.F.R. § 215.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:29:215.1
Creator: Office Of Labor-Management Standards, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-215.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 215 (GUIDELINES, SECTION 5333(b), FEDERAL TRANSIT LAW). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of these guidelines is to provide information concerning the Department of Labor's administrative procedures in processing applications for assistance under the Federal Transit law, as codified at 49 U.S.C. chapter 53.
