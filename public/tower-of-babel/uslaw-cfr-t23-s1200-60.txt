23 C.F.R. § 1200.60 — Fiscal year 2013 Section 402 grants.
Citation: us:cfr:23:1200.60
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration And Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-1200.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 1200 (Uniform Procedures For State Highway Safety Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Highway safety grants apportioned under 23 U.S.C. 402 for fiscal year 2013 shall be governed by the applicable implementing regulations at the time of grant award.
