An RSA Cryptosystem over a Halidon Group Ring of a Dihedral Group
A. Telveenus

Abstract
The article explores the creation of a cryptosystem using a halidon group ring of a dihedral group. Due to the non-abelian nature of the group, constructing the cryptosystem is more challenging compared to an abelian group. The logic used to develop a decryption programme was also quite complex.
