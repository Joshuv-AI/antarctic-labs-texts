30 C.F.R. § 556.400 — When must I demonstrate that I am qualified to hold a lease on the OCS?
Citation: us:cfr:30:556.400
Creator: Bureau Of Ocean Energy Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-556.400
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 556 (Leasing Of Sulfur Or Oil And Gas And Financial Assurance Requirements In The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In order to bid on, own, hold, or operate a lease on the OCS, bidders, record title holders, and operating rights owners must first obtain a qualification number from BOEM.
