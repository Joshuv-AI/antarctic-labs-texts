6 C.F.R. § 126.7 — Function of TSOB Review Panel.
Citation: us:cfr:6:126.7
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security, Office Of The Secretary
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-6/section-126.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 6 (Domestic Security), Part 126 (Transportation Security Oversight Board Review Panel Process And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A TSOB Review Panel reviews an ALJ's decision regarding a Determination of Security Threat issued by the TSA Administrator and may affirm, modify, or reverse the ALJ's decision. The TSOB Review Panel also may remand the matter to the ALJ with instructions to address particular issues or consider additional testimony or evidence.
