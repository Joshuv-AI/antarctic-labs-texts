7 C.F.R. § 22.305 — Conformance with OMB Circular No. A-95.
Citation: us:cfr:7:22.305
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-22.305
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 22 (Rural Development Coordination). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The State and multicounty jurisdictional rural development planning process must conform to the review requirements expressed in OMB Circular No. A-95 under parts I, III, and IV as appropriate.
