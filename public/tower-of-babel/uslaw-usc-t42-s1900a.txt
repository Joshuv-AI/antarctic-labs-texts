42 U.S.C. § 1900a — Rules and regulations
Citation: us:usc:42:1900a
Creator: United States Congress

Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1900a
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 16B (Contracts For Scientific And Technological Research). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall prescribe such rules and regulations as he deems necessary to carry out the provisions of this chapter.
