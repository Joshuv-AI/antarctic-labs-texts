32 C.F.R. § 1802.11 — Prerequisites.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1802.11
Creator: National Counterintelligence Center
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1802.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1802 (Challenges To Classification Of Documents By Authorized Holders Pursuant To Section 1.9 Of Executive Order 12958). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Prior to reliance on this part, authorized holders are required to first exhaust such established administrative procedures for the review of classified information. Further information on these procedures is available from the point of contact, § 1802.3.
