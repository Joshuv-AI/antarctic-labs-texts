36 U.S.C. § 190106 — Exclusive right to name, seals, emblems, and badges
Citation: us:usc:36:190106
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section190106
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1901 (Reserve Officers Association Of The United States). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation and its subordinate departmental subdivisions and local chapters have the exclusive right to use the name “Reserve Officers Association of the United States” and seals, emblems, and badges the corporation adopts.
