29 C.F.R. § 401.14 — Officer.
Citation: us:cfr:29:401.14
Creator: Office Of Labor-Management Standards, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-401.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 401 (Meaning Of Terms Used In This Subchapter). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Officer means any constitutional officer, any person authorized to perform the functions of president, vice president, secretary, treasurer, or other executive functions of a labor organization, and any member of its executive board or similar governing body.
