34 C.F.R. § 303.116 — Public awareness program.
Citation: us:cfr:34:303.116
Creator: Office Of Special Education And Rehabilitative Services, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-303.116
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 303 (Early Intervention Program For Infants And Toddlers With Disabilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each system must include a public awareness program that—
(a) Focuses on the early identification of infants and toddlers with disabilities; and
(b) Provides information to parents of infants and toddlers through primary referral sources in accordance with § 303.301.
