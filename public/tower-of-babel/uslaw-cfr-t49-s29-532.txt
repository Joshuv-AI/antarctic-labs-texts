49 C.F.R. § 29.532 — May persons who are not Indians assert claims under FTCA?
Citation: us:cfr:49:29.532
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-29.532
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 29 (Tribal Transportation Self-Governance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any aggrieved person may assert claims for alleged torts arising from activities performed in carrying out compacts and funding agreements.
