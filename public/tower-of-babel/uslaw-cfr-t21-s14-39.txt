21 C.F.R. § 14.39 — Additional rules for a particular advisory committee.
Citation: us:cfr:21:14.39
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-14.39
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 14 (Public Hearing Before A Public Advisory Committee). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) In addition to these rules, an advisory committee may, with the concurrence of the designated Federal employee, adopt additional rules which are not inconsistent with this subpart or with other legal requirements.
(b) Any additional rules will be included in the minutes of the meeting when adopted and in the materials compiled under § 14.33 and will be available for public disclosure under § 14.65(c).
