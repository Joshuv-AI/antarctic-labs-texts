10 C.F.R. § 1048.3 — Unauthorized entry.
Citation: us:cfr:10:1048.3
Creator: Department Of Energy (General Provisions)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1048.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1048 (Trespassing On Strategic Petroleum Reserve Facilities And Other Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unauthorized entry into or upon an SPR facility or real property subject to this part, without authorization, is prohibited.
