43 U.S.C. § 325 — Resident citizenship of State as qualification for entry
Citation: us:usc:43:325
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section325
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 9 (Desert-Land Entries). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Excepting in the State of Nevada, no person shall be entitled to make entry of desert lands unless he be a resident citizen of the State or Territory in which the land sought to be entered is located.
