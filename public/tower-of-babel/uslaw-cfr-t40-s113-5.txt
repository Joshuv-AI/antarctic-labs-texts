40 C.F.R. § 113.5 — Exclusions.
Citation: us:cfr:40:113.5
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-113.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 113 (Liability Limits For Small Onshore Storage Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart does not apply to:
(a) Those facilities whose average daily oil throughout is more than their fixed oil storage capacity.
(b) Vehicles and rolling stock.
