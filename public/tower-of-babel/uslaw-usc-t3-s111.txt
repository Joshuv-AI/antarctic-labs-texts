3 U.S.C. § 111 — Expense allowance of Vice President
Citation: us:usc:3:111
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title3-section111
Context: United States Code, Title 3 (The President); Chapter 2 (Office And Compensation Of President). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There shall be paid to the Vice President in equal monthly installments an expense allowance of $20,000 per annum to assist in defraying expenses relating to or resulting from the discharge of his official duties, for which no accounting, other than for income tax purposes, shall be made by him.
