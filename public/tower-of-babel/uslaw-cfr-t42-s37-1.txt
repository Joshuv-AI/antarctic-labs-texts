42 C.F.R. § 37.1 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:42:37.1
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-37.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 37 (Specifications For Medical Examinations Of Coal Miners). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Under this subpart, coal mine operators are required to provide radiographic examinations to each current and new coal miner, using medical facilities approved by NIOSH in accordance with standards established in this subpart.
