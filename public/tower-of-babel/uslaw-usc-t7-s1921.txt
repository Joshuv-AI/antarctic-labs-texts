7 U.S.C. § 1921 — Congressional findings
Citation: us:usc:7:1921
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1921
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 50 (Agricultural Credit). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Congress finds that the statutory authority of the Secretary of Agriculture, hereinafter referred to in this chapter as the “Secretary,” for making and insuring loans to farmers and ranchers should be revised and consolidated to provide for more effective credit services to farmers.
