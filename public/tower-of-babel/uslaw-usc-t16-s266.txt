16 U.S.C. § 266 — Authorization of appropriations for acquisition of additional lands
Citation: us:usc:16:266
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section266
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter XXVIII (Cumberland Gap National Historical Park). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the acquisition of lands authorized in section 267 of this title, there are authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary, but not more than $427,500.
