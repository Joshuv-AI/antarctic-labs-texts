33 C.F.R. § 155.225 — Internal cargo transfer capability.
Citation: us:cfr:33:155.225
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-155.225
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 155 (Oil Or Hazardous Material Pollution Prevention Regulations For Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Oil tankers and offshore oil barges must carry suitable hoses and reducers for internal transfer of cargo to tanks or other spaces within the cargo block, unless the vessel's installed cargo piping system is capable of performing this function.
