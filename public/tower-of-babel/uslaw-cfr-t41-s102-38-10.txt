41 C.F.R. § 102-38.10 — -38.10 Conducting sales.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-38.10
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-38.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-38 (38—Sale Of Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Only an executive agency designated or authorized by the General Services Administration (GSA) may sell personal property, including on behalf of another agency. Only an authorized contracting officer may execute the sale award documents and bind the United States to the sales contract.
