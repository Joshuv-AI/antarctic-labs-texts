25 C.F.R. § 170.625 — What regulations apply to waivers?
Citation: us:cfr:25:170.625
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-170.625
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 170 (Tribal Transportation Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following regulations apply to waivers:
(a) For self-determination contracts, 25 CFR 900.140 through 900.148;
(b) For self-governance agreements, 25 CFR 1000.220 through 1000.232; and
(c) For direct service, 25 CFR 1.2.
