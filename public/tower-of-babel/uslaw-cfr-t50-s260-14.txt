50 C.F.R. § 260.14 — Issuance of certificates.
Citation: us:cfr:50:260.14
Creator: National Marine Fisheries Service, National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-260.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 260 (Inspection And Certification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Inspection Certificates and Certificates of Loading may be issued by an inspector authorized by the Agency to affix their signature to a certificate that has been prepared in accordance with the documented evidence in connection with the inspection service provided.
