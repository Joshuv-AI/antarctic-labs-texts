45 C.F.R. § 1616.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1616.1
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1616.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1616 (Attorney Hiring). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part is designed to promote a mutually beneficial relationship between a recipient and the local Bar and community, and to insure that a recipient will choose highly qualified attorneys for its staff.
