12 U.S.C. § 2408 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:12:2408
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section2408
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 25 (National Commission On Electronic Fund Transfers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated without fiscal year limitations such sums, not to exceed $2,000,000, as may be necessary to carry out the provisions of this chapter.
