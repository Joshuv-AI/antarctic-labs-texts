21 C.F.R. § 1250.79 — Garbage disposal.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1250.79
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1250.79
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1250 (Interstate Conveyance Sanitation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Water-tight, readily cleanable, nonabsorbent containers with close-fitting covers shall be used to receive and store garbage.
(b) Can washing and draining facilities shall be provided.
(c) Garbage cans shall be emptied daily and shall be thoroughly washed before being returned for use.
