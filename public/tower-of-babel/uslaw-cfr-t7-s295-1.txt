7 C.F.R. § 295.1 — General statement.
Citation: us:cfr:7:295.1
Creator: Food And Nutrition Administration, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-295.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 295 (Availability Of Information And Records To The Public). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part is issued in accordance with the regulations of the Secretary of Agriculture at 7 CFR 1.1-1.23, and appendix A, implementing the Freedom of Information Act (5 U.S.C. 552). The Secretary's regulations, as implemented by the regulations in this part, govern the availability of records of FNS to the public.
