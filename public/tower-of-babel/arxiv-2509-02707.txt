The splitting of Generalisations of the Fadell-Neuwirth Short exact sequence
Daciberg Lima Gonçalves, John Guaschi, Carolina de Miranda e Pereiro

Abstract
. We study some generalisations to mixed braid groups of the Fadell-Neuwirth short exact sequence and the possible splitting of this sequence. In certain cases, we determine conditions under which the projection from the mixed braid group B n 1 , … , n k ​ ( M ) B_{n_{1},\ldots,n_{k}}(M) to B n 1 , … , n k − q ​ ( M ) B_{n_{1},\ldots,n_{k-q}}(M) admits a section, where M M is either the torus or the Klein bottle, n 1 , … , n k , q ∈ ℕ n_{1},\ldots,n_{k},q\in\mathbb{N} , and 1 ≤ q ≤ k − 1 1\leq q\leq k-1 . For k ≥ 2 k\geq 2 and q = k − 1 q=k-1 , we show that this projection admits a section if and only if n 1 n_{1} divides n i n_{i} for all i = 2 , … , k i=2,\ldots,k . We present some partial conclusions in the case k ≥ 3 k\geq 3 and q = 1 q=1 . To obtain our results, we compute and make use of suitable mixed braid groups of M M , as well as certain key quotients that play a central rôle in our analysis.

Theorem 1.1
.

Let

2

Observe that Theorem

implies the result of

To prove that the condition of the statement of Theorem

is sufficient, we make use of the existence of a non-vanishing vector field on

to construct a geometric section on the level of the associated configuration spaces, which implies the existence of an algebraic section for (

1.3

The mixed braid groups defined above may be generalised to any number of factors. To do so, for
k,n_{1},\ldots,n_{k}\in\mathbb{N}
, let:

B_{n_{1},\ldots,n_{k}}(M)=\pi_{1}(D_{n_{1},\ldots,n_{k}}(M)),

(1.4)

1\longrightarrow\operatorname{\text{Ker}}({p_{\ast}})\longrightarrow B_{n_{1},\ldots,n_{k}}(M)\stackrel{{\scriptstyle p_{\ast}}}{{\longrightarrow}}B_{n_{1},\ldots,n_{k-q}}(M)\longrightarrow 1,

where

that forgets the last

may be identified with the group
B_{n_{k-q+1},\ldots,n_{k}}(M\setminus\{x_{1},\ldots,x_{n_{1}+\cdots+n_{k-q}}\})

1.4

1.1

or

Theorem 1.2
.

Let
M
be the
2
-torus or the Klein bottle. If
q=k-1
, with the above notation, the short exact sequence (

) splits if and only if

divides

.

The case

Theorem 1.3
.

Let
M
be the
2
-torus or the Klein bottle, let

, and let

.

(a)

If there exist

such that

, then the homomorphism
p_{\ast}\colon\thinspace B_{n_{1},\ldots,n_{k}}(M)\longrightarrow B_{n_{1},\ldots,n_{k-1}}(M)

(b)

If the homomorphism
p_{\ast}\colon\thinspace B_{n_{1},\ldots,n_{k}}(M)\longrightarrow B_{n_{1},\ldots,n_{k-1}}(M)

However, the following result shows that in one of the simplest such situations, where

,

,

,

, and

or

Theorem 1.4
.

Let
M
be either the
2
-torus or the Klein bottle, and let

. Then the projection

does not admit a section.
