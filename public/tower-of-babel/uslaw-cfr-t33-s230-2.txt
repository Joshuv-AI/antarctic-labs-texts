33 C.F.R. § 230.2 — Applicability.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 230 (Procedures For Implementing Nepa). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This regulation is applicable to the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers Civil Works program, except the permitting programs and the 33 U.S.C. 408 request for permission program covered in 33 CFR part 333.
