25 U.S.C. § 1831 — Purpose
Citation: us:usc:25:1831
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section1831
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 20 (Tribally Controlled Colleges And Universities Assistance); Subchapter II (Tribally Controlled College Or University Endowment Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the purpose of this subchapter to provide grants for the encouragement of endowment funds for the operation and improvement of tribally controlled colleges or universities.
