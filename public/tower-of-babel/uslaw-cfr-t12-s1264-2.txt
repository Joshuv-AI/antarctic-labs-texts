12 C.F.R. § 1264.2 — Bank authority to make advances to housing associates.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1264.2
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1264.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1264 (Federal Home Loan Bank Housing Associates). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Subject to the provisions of the Bank Act and part 1266 of this chapter, a Bank may make advances to an entity that is not a member of the Bank if the Bank has certified the entity as a housing associate under the provisions of this part.
