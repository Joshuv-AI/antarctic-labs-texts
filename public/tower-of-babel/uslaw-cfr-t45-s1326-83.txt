45 C.F.R. § 1326.83 — Use of gender and number.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1326.83
Creator: Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1326.83
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1326 (Developmental Disabilities Formula Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As used in this subpart, words importing the singular number may extend and be applied to several persons or things, and vice versa. Words importing either gender may be applied to the other gender or to organizations.
