40 C.F.R. § 141.1 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:40:141.1
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-141.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 141 (National Primary Drinking Water Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part establishes primary drinking water regulations pursuant to section 1412 of the Public Health Service Act, as amended by the Safe Drinking Water Act (Pub. L. 93-523); and related regulations applicable to public water systems.
