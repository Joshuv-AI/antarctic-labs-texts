7 C.F.R. § 1435.600 — General statement.

Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1435 (Sugar Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The provisions of this subpart will be applied when CCC determines that buying sugar is necessary to avoid forfeitures of sugar pledged as collateral for CCC sugar loans.
(b) This subpart will be applicable to:
(1) Any sugar seller who contractts with CCC to sell sugar; and
(2) Any bioenergy producer who contracts with CCC to purchase sugar for the production of bioenergy.
(c) This subpart will be applicable until the end of crop year 2026.
