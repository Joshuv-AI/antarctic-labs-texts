30 C.F.R. § 75.508-2 — -2 Changes in electric system map; recording.
Citation: us:cfr:30:75.508-2
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-75.508-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 75 (Mandatory Safety Standards—Underground Coal Mines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Changes made in the location, electrical rating or setting within the mine electrical system shall be recorded on the map of such system no later than the end of the next workday following completion of such changes.
