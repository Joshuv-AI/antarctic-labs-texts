10 C.F.R. § 611.203 — Eligibility.
Citation: us:cfr:10:611.203
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-611.203
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 611 (Advanced Technology Vehicles Manufacturer Assistance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In order to be eligible for an award, an applicant must be either—
(a) An automobile manufacturer that can demonstrate an improved fuel economy as specified in paragraph (b) of section 611.3, or
(b) A manufacturer of a qualifying component.
