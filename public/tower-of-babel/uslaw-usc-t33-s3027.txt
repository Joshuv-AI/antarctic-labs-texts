33 U.S.C. § 3027 — General qualification of officers for promotion to higher permanent grade
Citation: us:usc:33:3027
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section3027
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 43 (National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration Commissioned Officer Corps); Subchapter II (Appointment And Promotion Of Officers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No officer may be promoted to a higher permanent grade on the active list until the officer has passed a satisfactory mental and physical examination in accordance with regulations prescribed by the Secretary.
