46 C.F.R. § 151.03-39 — -39 Ocean.
Citation: us:cfr:46:151.03-39
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-151.03-39
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 151 (Barges Carrying Bulk Liquid Hazardous Material Cargoes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A designation for all vessels normally navigating the waters of any ocean or the Gulf of America more than 20 nautical miles offshore.
