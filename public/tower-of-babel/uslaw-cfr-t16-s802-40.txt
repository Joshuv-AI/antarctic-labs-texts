16 C.F.R. § 802.40 — Exempt formation of corporations or unincorporated entities.
Citation: us:cfr:16:802.40
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-802.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 802 (Exemption Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The formation of an entity is exempt from the requirements of the Act if the entity will be not-for-profit within the meaning of sections 501(c)(1)-(4), (6)-(15), (17)-(20) or (d) of the Internal Revenue Code.
