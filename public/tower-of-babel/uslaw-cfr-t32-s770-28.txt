32 C.F.R. § 770.28 — Entry restrictions.
Citation: us:cfr:32:770.28
Creator: Department Of The Navy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-770.28
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 770 (Rules Limiting Public Access To Particular Installations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each commander is responsible for the security of his/her command. Therefore, entry onto a command or into part of a command may be controlled by the commander through the imposition of such restrictions as may be required by attendant circumstances. Within the State of Hawaii, entry into a naval installation is not permitted without the permission of the responsible commander.
