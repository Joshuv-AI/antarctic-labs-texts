28 C.F.R. § 505.7 — Procedures for final disposition.
Citation: us:cfr:28:505.7
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-505.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 505 (Cost Of Incarceration Fee). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Before the inmate completes his or her sentence, Unit Team staff must review the status of the inmate's fee. Any unpaid amount will be referred for collection in accordance with Federal Claims Collection Standards (4 CFR Chapter II).
