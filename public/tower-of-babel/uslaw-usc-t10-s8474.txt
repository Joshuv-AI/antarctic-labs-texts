10 U.S.C. § 8474 — United States Naval Academy Museum Fund: references to Fund
Citation: us:usc:10:8474
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8474
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Education And Training); Chapter 853 (United States Naval Academy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any reference in a law, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the United States Naval Academy Museum Fund formerly maintained under this section shall be deemed to refer to the United States Naval Academy Gift and Museum Fund maintained under section 8473 of this title.
