22 U.S.C. § 3715b — Transfers from Fund for compensation benefits
Citation: us:usc:22:3715b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section3715b
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 51 (Panama Canal); Subchapter I (Administration And Regulations); Part 3 (Funds And Accounts); Subpart i (Funds). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Treasury shall, upon request of the Secretary of Labor, transfer funds from the Fund to the Employees’ Compensation Fund to reimburse the Employees’ Compensation Fund for the total cost of workers’ compensation benefits and other payments described in section 3715a(a) of this title that are provided on or after October 1, 1988.
