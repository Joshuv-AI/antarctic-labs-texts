43 C.F.R. § 3135.7 — What effect does a suspension of operations and production have on the term of my lease?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3135.7
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3135.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3130 (Oil And Gas Leasing: National Petroleum Reserve, Alaska). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Primary term. If BLM grants a suspension of operations and production for your lease, the suspension stops the running of the primary term of your lease for the period of the suspension.
(b) Extended term. If your lease is in its extended term, a suspension holds your lease in its extended term for the period of the suspension as if it were in production.
