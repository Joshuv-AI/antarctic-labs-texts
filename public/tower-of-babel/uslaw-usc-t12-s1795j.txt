12 U.S.C. § 1795j — Agent of Federal Reserve System
Citation: us:usc:12:1795j
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section1795j
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 14 (Federal Credit Unions); Subchapter III (Central Liquidity Facility). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The facility is authorized to act upon the request of the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System as an agent of the Federal Reserve System in matters pertaining to credit unions under such terms and conditions as may be established by the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System.
