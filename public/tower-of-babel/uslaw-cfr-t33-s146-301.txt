33 C.F.R. § 146.301 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:33:146.301
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-146.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 146 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to vessels, including MODUs, engaged in OCS activities other than U.S. vessels already required to report marine casualties under 46 CFR part 4 or subpart D of 46 CFR part 109.
