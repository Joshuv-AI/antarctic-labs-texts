48 C.F.R. § 904.7201 — 904.7201 Contract clause.
Citation: us:cfr:48:904.7201
Creator: Chapter 9—Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-904.7201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 904 (Administrative Matters). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 952.204-75, Public Affairs, in solicitations and contracts that require the contractor to release unclassified information related to efforts under its contract regarding DOE policies, programs, and activities.
