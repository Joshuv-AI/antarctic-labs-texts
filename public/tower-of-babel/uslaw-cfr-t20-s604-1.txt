20 C.F.R. § 604.1 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:20:604.1
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-604.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 604 (Regulations For Eligibility For Unemployment Compensation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this Part is to implement the requirements of Federal UC law that limit a State's payment of UC to individuals who are able to work and available for work. This regulation applies to all State UC laws and programs.
