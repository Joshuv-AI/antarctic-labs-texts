2.
Preliminaries

In this section we prove some preliminary results on local to global property of generalized Cohen-Macaulay property for invariant rings. We also study the relation between invariant rings of Sylow
p
-subgroups and rings of invariants of the group. Finally we study some change of rings problems.

Lemma 2.1
.

Let
(\mathcal{O},\mathfrak{m})
be a local ring and let
R=\bigoplus_{n\geq 0}R_{n}
be a graded Noetherian ring with
R_{0}=\mathcal{O}
. Assume that
R^{<c>}
is standard graded. Let
m
be a positive integer and assume that
R^{<cml>}
is Cohen-Macaulay for all
l\gg 0
. Then
R^{<cl>}
is Cohen-Macaulay for all
l\gg 0
.

Proof.

We may assume that
\mathcal{O}
is complete. Let
\mathfrak{M}
be the graded maximal ideal of
R
. Then

\mathfrak{M}^{<l>}
is the graded maximal ideal of
R^{<l>}
. Local cohomology behaves well with respect to the Veronese functor. In particular for all
i\geq 0
we have

\left(H^{i}_{\mathfrak{M}}(R)\right)^{<l>}=H^{i}_{\mathfrak{M}^{<l>}}(R^{<l>}).

For
i\geq 0
let
E_{i}=H^{i}_{\mathfrak{M}}(R)^{\vee}
be the Matlis dual of
H^{i}_{\mathfrak{M}}(R)
. Note that

E_{i}
is a finitely generated
R
-module. We note that as
R^{<c>}
is standard graded we have
(E_{i})_{cml}=(E_{i}^{<c>})_{ml}=0
for all
i\gg 0
if and only if
(E_{i})_{cl}=0
for all
l\gg 0
. It follows that

\left(H^{i}_{\mathfrak{M}}(R)\right)^{<cml>}=0
for
l\gg 0
if and only if
\left(H^{i}_{\mathfrak{M}}(R)\right)^{<cl>}=0
for
l\gg 0
. The result follows.
∎
