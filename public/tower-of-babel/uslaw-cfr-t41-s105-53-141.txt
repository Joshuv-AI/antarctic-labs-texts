41 C.F.R. § 105-53.141 — -53.141 Office of Policy Analysis.
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-53.141
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-53.141
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-53 (53—Statement Of Organization And Functions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Office of Policy Analysis, headed by the Associate Administrator for Policy Analysis, is responsible for providing analytical support, independent, objective information concerning management policies and programs, and technical and analytical assistance in the areas of policy analysis and resource allocation to the Administrator, senior officials, and organizations in GSA.
