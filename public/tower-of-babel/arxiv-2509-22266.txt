Antitoroidal magnets and anomalous Hall effect
Vladimir A. Zyuzin

Abstract
In this paper we introduce a theoretical model of a metallic magnetic system with an antitoroidal order. We introduce a mechanism of indirect interaction of conducting fermions with localized spins based on the tunneling processes of conducting fermions through the localized spins. We demonstrate that interaction of conducting fermions with an antitoroidal results in odd in momentum spin-momentum locking. The interaction resembles Rashba spin-orbit coupling but breaks the time-reversal symmetry. As a result, we show that a ferromagnet with the antitoroidal order is an insulator with anomalous Hall effect occurring without any spin-orbit coupling.

To conclude, in this paper we have presented a theoretical model of an antitoroidal magnet with a ferromagnetic order.
The antitoroidal order results in the momentum-dependent exchange interaction of conducting fermions. The interaction is shown to be odd in momentum Eq. (
Antitoroidal magnets and anomalous Hall effect
), and it does resemble standard Rashba spin-orbit coupling but with the difference that it breaks the time-reversal symmetry. We showed that the anomalous Hall effect is obatined in the model without any spin-orbit coupling. The anomalous Hall effect is shown to be consistent with the existence of chiral edge states.
