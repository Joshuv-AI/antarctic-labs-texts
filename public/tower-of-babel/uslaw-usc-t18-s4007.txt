18 U.S.C. § 4007 — Expenses of prisoners
Citation: us:usc:18:4007
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section4007
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part III (Prisons And Prisoners); Chapter 301 (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The expenses attendant upon the confinement of persons arrested or committed under the laws of the United States, as well as upon the execution of any sentence of a court thereof respecting them, shall be paid out of the Treasury of the United States in the manner provided by law.
