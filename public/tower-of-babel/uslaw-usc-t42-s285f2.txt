42 U.S.C. § 285f–2 — Research and research training regarding tuberculosis
Citation: us:usc:42:285f–2
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section285f–2
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter III (National Research Institutes); Part C (Specific Provisions Respecting National Research Institutes); Subpart 6 (National Institute Of Allergy And Infectious Diseases). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In carrying out section 285f of this title, the Director of the Institute shall conduct or support research and research training regarding the cause, diagnosis, early detection, prevention and treatment of tuberculosis.
