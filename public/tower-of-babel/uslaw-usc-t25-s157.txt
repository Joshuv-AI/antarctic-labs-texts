25 U.S.C. § 157 — Investments of stock required by treaties
Citation: us:usc:25:157
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section157
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 4 (Performance By United States Of Obligations To Indians); Subchapter III (Deposit, Care, And Investment Of Indian Moneys). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All investments of stock, that are or may be required by treaties with the Indians, shall be made under the direction of the President; and special accounts of the funds under such treaties shall be kept at the Treasury, and statements thereof be annually laid before Congress.
