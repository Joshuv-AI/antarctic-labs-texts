42 C.F.R. § 416.300 — Basis and scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:42:416.300
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-416.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 416 (Ambulatory Surgical Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Statutory basis. Section 1833(i)(2)(D)(iv) and (i)(7) of the Act authorizes the Secretary to implement a revised ASC payment system in a manner so as to provide for a 2.0 percentage point reduction in any annual update for an ASC's failure to report on quality measures in accordance with the Secretary's requirements.
(b) Scope. This subpart contains specific requirements and standards for the ASCQR Program.
