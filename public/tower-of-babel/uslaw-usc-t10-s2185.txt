10 U.S.C. § 2185 — Programs to be consistent with programs administered by the Department of Veterans Affairs
Citation: us:usc:10:2185
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section2185
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Training And Education); Chapter 110 (Educational Assistance For Members Held As Captives And Their Dependents). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Regulations prescribed to carry out this chapter shall provide that the programs under this chapter shall be consistent with the educational assistance programs under chapters 35 and 36 of title 38.
