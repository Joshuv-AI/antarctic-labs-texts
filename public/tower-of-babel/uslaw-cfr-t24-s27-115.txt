24 C.F.R. § 27.115 — Disposition of sales proceeds.
Citation: us:cfr:24:27.115
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-27.115
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 27 (Nonjudicial Foreclosure Of Multifamily And Single Family Mortgages). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The foreclosure commissioner will keep such records as will permit the Secretary to verify the costs claimed, and otherwise to enable the Secretary to audit the foreclosure commissioner's disposition of the sale proceeds.
