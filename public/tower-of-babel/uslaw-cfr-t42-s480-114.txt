42 C.F.R. § 480.114 — Limitation on data collection.
Citation: us:cfr:42:480.114
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-480.114
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 480 (Acquisition, Protection, And Disclosure Of Quality Improvement Organization Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A QIO or any agent, organization, or institution acting on its behalf, that is collecting information under authority of this part, must collect only that information which is necessary to accomplish the purposes of Title XI Part B of the Act in accordance with 44 U.S.C. Chapter 35, Coordination of Federal Reporting Services Information Policy.
