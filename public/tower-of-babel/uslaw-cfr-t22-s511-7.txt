22 C.F.R. § 511.7 — Investigations.
Citation: us:cfr:22:511.7
Creator: United States Agency For Global Media
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-511.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 511 (Federal Tort Claims Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board may request any other Federal agency to investigate a claim filed under section 2672 of the Act, or to conduct a physical or mental examination of the claimant and provide a report of such examination.
