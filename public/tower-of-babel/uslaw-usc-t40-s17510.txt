40 U.S.C. § 17510 — Cooperation
Citation: us:usc:40:17510
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section17510
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Chapter 175 (Federal Motor Vehicle Expenditure Control). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director of the Office of Management and Budget and the Administrator of General Services shall cooperate closely in the implementation of this chapter.
