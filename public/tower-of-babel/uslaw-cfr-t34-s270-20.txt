34 C.F.R. § 270.20 — How does the Secretary evaluate an application for a grant?
Citation: us:cfr:34:270.20
Creator: Office Of Elementary And Secondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-270.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 270 (Equity Assistance Center Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Secretary evaluates the application on the basis of the criteria in 34 CFR 75.210.
(b) The Secretary selects the highest ranking application for each geographic region to receive a grant.
