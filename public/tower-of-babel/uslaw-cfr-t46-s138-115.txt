46 C.F.R. § 138.115 — Compliance.
Citation: us:cfr:46:138.115
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-138.115
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 138 (Towing Safety Management System (Tsms)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Owners or managing operators selecting the TSMS option must obtain a TSMS certificate issued under § 138.305 at least 6 months before obtaining a Certificate of Inspection (COI) for any of their vessels covered by the TSMS certificate.
