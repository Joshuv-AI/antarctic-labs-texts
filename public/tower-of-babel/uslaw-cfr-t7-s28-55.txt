7 C.F.R. § 28.55 — Issuance of memoranda and certificates.
Citation: us:cfr:7:28.55
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-28.55
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 28 (Cotton Classing, Testing, And Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As soon as practicable after the classification of cotton has been completed by a Classing Office, there shall be issued a cotton class memorandum or certificate of the appropriate kind showing the results of such classification. Upon request from an applicant, classification results may be issued in preliminary form on record sheets.
