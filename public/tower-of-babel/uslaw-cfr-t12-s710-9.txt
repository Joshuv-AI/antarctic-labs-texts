12 C.F.R. § 710.9 — Federally insured state-chartered credit unions.
Citation: us:cfr:12:710.9
Creator: National Credit Union Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-710.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 710 (Voluntary Liquidation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A federal insured state credit union will notify the Regional Director in writing within three days after the board of directors' decision to liquidate is made. A balance sheet and income statement as of the previous month-end and a copy of any liquidation plan will be included with the notification to the Regional Director.
