46 C.F.R. § 113.40-5 — -5 General requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:46:113.40-5
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-113.40-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 113 (Communication And Alarm Systems And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The position of the rudder, if power-operated, must be shown at the principal steering station. If there is non-follow-up steering control at the alternative steering station, there must be a separate rudder angle indicator system for that station that is electrically independent from each other rudder angle indicator system.
