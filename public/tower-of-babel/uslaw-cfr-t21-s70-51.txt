21 C.F.R. § 70.51 — Advisory committee on the applicability of the anticancer clause.
Citation: us:cfr:21:70.51
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-70.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 70 (Color Additives). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All requests for and procedures governing any advisory committee on the anticancer clause shall be subject to the provisions of part 14 of this chapter, and particularly subpart H of that part.
