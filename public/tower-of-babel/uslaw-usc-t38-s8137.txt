38 U.S.C. § 8137 — State control of operations
Citation: us:usc:38:8137
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section8137
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part VI (Acquisition And Disposition Of Property); Chapter 81 (Acquisition And Operation Of Hospital And Domiciliary Facilities; Procurement And Supply; Enhanced-Use Leases Of Real Property); Subchapter III (State Home Facilities For Furnishing Domiciliary, Nursing Home, And Hospital Care). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as otherwise specifically provided, nothing in this subchapter shall be construed as conferring on any Federal officer or employee the right to exercise any supervision or control over the administration, personnel, maintenance, or operation of any State home for which facilities are constructed or acquired with assistance received under this subchapter.
