7 C.F.R. § 948.57 — Failure to nominate.
Citation: us:cfr:7:948.57
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-948.57
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 948 (Irish Potatoes Grown In Colorado). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If nominations are not made pursuant to the provisions of § 948.56 by the date provided therein, the Secretary may, without regard to nominations, select members and alternates on the basis of the representation provided for in this part.
