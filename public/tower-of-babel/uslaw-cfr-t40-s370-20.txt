40 C.F.R. § 370.20 — What are the reporting requirements of this part?
Citation: us:cfr:40:370.20
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-370.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 370 (Hazardous Chemical Reporting: Community Right-To-Know). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The reporting requirements of this part consist of SDS reporting and inventory reporting. If you are the owner or operator of a facility subject to the reporting requirements of this part, then you must comply with both types of reporting requirements. SDS reporting requirements are addressed in §§ 370.30 through 370.33. Inventory reporting requirements are addressed in §§ 370.40 through 370.45.
