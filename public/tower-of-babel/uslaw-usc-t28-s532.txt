28 U.S.C. § 532 — Director of the Federal Bureau of Investigation
Citation: us:usc:28:532
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section532
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part II (Department Of Justice); Chapter 33 (Federal Bureau Of Investigation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Attorney General may appoint a Director of the Federal Bureau of Investigation. The Director of the Federal Bureau of Investigation is the head of the Federal Bureau of Investigation.
