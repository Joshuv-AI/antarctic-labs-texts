Study of the mass spectra of doubly heavy Ξ Q ​ Q ′ \Xi_{QQ^{\prime}} and Ω Q ​ Q ′ \Omega_{QQ^{\prime}} baryons
Ji-Hai Pan, Ji-Si Pan

Abstract
LHCb Collaboration first observed a doubly charmed baryon Ξ c ​ c + ⁣ + \Xi^{++}_{cc} in the Λ c + ​ K − ​ π + ​ π + \Lambda^{+}_{c}K^{-}\pi^{+}\pi^{+} decay with a mass of 3621.40 ± 0.72 ± 0.27 3621.40\pm 0.72\pm 0.27 MeV. In this paper, we enumerated the mass spectra of the radial and orbital excited states for the doubly heavy Ξ Q ​ Q ′ \Xi_{QQ^{\prime}} and Ω Q ​ Q ′ \Omega_{QQ^{\prime}} baryons using the Regge trajectory model and the scaling rules. Our studies suggest that Ξ c ​ c + ⁣ + \Xi^{++}_{cc} can be grouped into the 1 ​ S 1S -wave state with the spin-parity quantum number J P = 1 / 2 + J^{P}=1/2^{+} . On the other hand, the mass of Ξ c ​ c \Xi_{cc} state with J P = 3 / 2 + J^{P}=3/2^{+} is predicted to be 3699.69 ± 4.59 3699.69\pm 4.59 MeV. We also predict the mass spectra of the unknown ground and excited states for the doubly heavy Ξ Q ​ Q ′ \Xi_{QQ^{\prime}} and Ω Q ​ Q ′ \Omega_{QQ^{\prime}} baryons, which provide useful references for the experimental test in the future.

The effective mass of quark

Generally, the effective mass of the quark is a concept in quantum chromodynamics (QCD) that arises from the confinement of quarks within hadrons
[

36

This variation arises because the quark effective mass is inherently dependent on the choice of radial and orbital quantum numbers. Exploring this phenomenon in baryon system presents an intriguing avenue for further research.

By including relativistic effects, one can obtain the effective masses

\displaystyle\frac{m_{1}}{\sqrt{1-v^{2}_{1}}},

\displaystyle\langle T_{i}\rangle=\frac{1}{2}\langle rV^{\prime}\rangle.

III
The spin-average masses of doubly heavy baryons

In the heavy-light quark picture, we consider the baryon system as a bound state of three constituent quarks under the strong interaction. The doubly heavy baryon can be done by viewing a QCD rotating-string tied to the heavy diquark
(QQ^{\prime})
with spin-1 as one end and to the light quark
(q)
with spin-1/2 as the other end, see Fig. 1. Based on this model, we make an attempt to investigate the Regge trajectory behavior of the hadronic system.

Figure 1:
Schematic diagram of doubly heavy baryon in the heavy-light quark picture.

For the orbital excitations of the doubly heavy baryons, the spin-average mass
\bar{M}

(9)

L=\frac{m_{QQ^{\prime}}v_{QQ^{\prime}}^{2}}{\sqrt{1-{{v_{QQ^{\prime}}^{2}}}}}+\frac{\alpha}{\omega^{2}}\int_{0}^{v_{QQ^{\prime}}}\frac{u^{2}du}{\sqrt{1-{u}^{2}}}+\frac{m_{q}v_{q}^{2}}{\sqrt{1-{{v_{q}^{2}}}}}+\frac{\alpha}{\omega^{2}}\int_{0}^{v_{q}}\frac{u^{2}du}{\sqrt{1-{u}^{2}}},

By introducing the coefficient

\displaystyle\alpha=\frac{k}{2\pi}(M_{QQ^{\prime}})^{\frac{1}{2}}.

(12)

In addition, the velocity of the quark in the doubly heavy baryon system is defined as

For the string ending at the heavy diquark we utilize the boundary condition

\displaystyle\frac{\alpha}{\omega}=\frac{m_{QQ^{\prime}}v_{QQ^{\prime}}}{1-v^{2}_{QQ^{\prime}}}\approx M_{QQ^{\prime}}v_{QQ^{\prime}}.

VII
The
\Omega_{QQ^{\prime}}
baryons

The doubly heavy
\Omega_{QQ^{\prime}}
baryons are regarded as an important and unique part of the baryons in heavy-light quark system, as they are composed of light strange quark
s
. Up to now, the doubly heavy
\Omega_{QQ^{\prime}}
baryons have not been reported yet by the experimental, and their nature are still unknown. Thus, the doubly heavy
\Omega_{QQ^{\prime}}
baryons were studied by using various approaches, we recommend interested readers to see Refs.
[
66
,
67
,
32
,
30
,
68
,
69
]

\Omega_{cc}
,
\Omega_{bc}
, and
\Omega_{bb}
baryons. The results of the masses are listed in Table
7
,
8
, and
9
for the
\Omega_{cc}
,
\Omega_{bc}
, and
\Omega_{bb}
baryons, respectively. The calculations are the same as for the the
\Xi_{QQ^{\prime}}
baryons, the uncertainties in the mass results of the
\Omega_{QQ^{\prime}}
baryon states are due to the errors in the experiment values as well as the uncertainties in determination of the parameters
a_{1}
,
a_{2}
,
b_{1}
, and
c_{1}
and other input parameters.
