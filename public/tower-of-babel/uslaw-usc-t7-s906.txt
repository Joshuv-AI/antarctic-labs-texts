7 U.S.C. § 906 — Funding for administrative expenses
Citation: us:usc:7:906
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section906
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 31 (Rural Electrification And Telephone Service); Subchapter I (Rural Electrification). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purpose of administering this chapter and for the purpose of making the studies, investigations, publications, and reports herein provided for, there is authorized to be appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, such sums as shall be necessary.
