25 C.F.R. § 217.2 — Authority and purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:25:217.2
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-217.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 217 (Management Of Tribal Assets Of Ute Indian Tribe, Uintah And Ouray Reservation, Utah, By The Tribe And The Ute Distribution Corp.). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In accordance with the Ute Partition Act approved August 27, 1954 (68 Stat. 868; 25 U.S.C. 677-677aa), as amended by the Act of August 2, 1956 (70 Stat. 936), and the Act of September 25, 1962 (76 Stat. 597), assets shall be managed jointly by the business committee and the board of directors. These regulations set out the procedures for exercising such joint management.
