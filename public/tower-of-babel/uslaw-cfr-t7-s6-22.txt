7 C.F.R. § 6.22 — Requirement for a license.
Citation: us:cfr:7:6.22
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-6.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 6 (Import Quotas And Fees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A person who seeks to enter, or cause to be entered an article as a commercial entry, shall obtain a license, in accordance with this subpart.
