24 C.F.R. § 350.10 — Liability of Federal Reserve Banks as Depositories.
Citation: us:cfr:24:350.10
Creator: Government National Mortgage Association, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-350.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 350 (Book-Entry Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Federal Reserve Banks as Depositories may rely on the information provided in a Transfer Message, and are not required to verify the information. The Federal Reserve Banks as Depositories shall not be liable for any action taken in accordance with the information set out in a Transfer Message, or evidence submitted in support thereof.
