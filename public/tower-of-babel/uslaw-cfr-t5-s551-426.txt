5 C.F.R. § 551.426 — Time spent in charitable activities.
Citation: us:cfr:5:551.426
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-551.426
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 551 (Pay Administration Under The Fair Labor Standards Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Time spent working for public or charitable purposes at an agency's request, or under an agency's direction or control, shall be considered hours of work. However, time spent voluntarily in such activities outside an employee's regular working hours is not hours of work.
