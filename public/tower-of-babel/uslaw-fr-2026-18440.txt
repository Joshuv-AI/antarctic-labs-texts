DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Center for Advancing Translational Sciences; Notice of 
Public Online Roundtable

ACTION: Notice; announcement of public forum.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The National Center for Advancing Translational Sciences 
(NCATS) will host a public online roundtable to share the latest 
updates on how NCATS is advancing translational science research and 
provide an opportunity for an interactive discussion with NCATS 
director Dr. Joni Rutter.

DATES: The public online forum will be available on October 9, 2026, 
from 12:00 p.m. to 1:00 p.m. EDT.

ADDRESSES: The public forum will be held online, at https://teams.microsoft.com/meet/254720356527980?p=p25BdPepvAOddo3GEA on the 
date indicated above.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Christopher Maurer, Management 
Analyst, National Center for Advancing Translational Sciences, NIH, 
[email protected] or 301-827-7280 OR Brittany Gibbons, Health 
Specialist, National Center for Advancing Translational Sciences, NIH, 
[email protected] or 240-401-8298.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The roundtable will also invite public 
discussion of proposed organizational changes at NCATS, including 
abolishing the Office of Drug Development Partnership Programs and the 
Office of Translational Medicine, revising the functional statement of 
the Office of Special Initiatives, and renaming the Chemical Genomics 
Branch as the Translational Models Branch. These changes are intended 
to consolidate program components within the NCATS Office of the 
Director and align one of the Center's intramural research branches 
with efforts to create non-animal models of disease. During the online 
forum, members of the public will be able to review the proposed 
changes and submit comments.
    The NIH Reform Act of 2006 (42 U.S.C.281 (d)(4)) requires public 
notice of proposed reorganization plans. This announcement and the 
public forum serve as that notice.

    Dated: September 2, 2026.
Joni L. Rutter,
Director, National Center for Advancing Translational Sciences.
