19 U.S.C. § 1304a — Technical assistance to U.S. Customs and Border Protection
Citation: us:usc:19:1304a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section1304a
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 4 (Tariff Act Of 1930); Part I (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Agriculture shall make available to U.S. Customs and Border Protection technical assistance related to the identification of produce represented as grown in the United States when it is not in fact grown in the United States.
