5 U.S.C. § 5519 — Crediting amounts received for certain Reserve or National Guard service
Citation: us:usc:5:5519
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section5519
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart D (Pay And Allowances); Chapter 55 (Pay Administration); Subchapter II (Withholding Pay). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An amount (other than a travel, transportation, or per diem allowance) received by an employee or individual for military service as a member of the Reserve or National Guard for a period for which he is granted military leave under section 6323(b) shall be credited against the pay payable to the employee or individual with respect to his civilian position for that period.
