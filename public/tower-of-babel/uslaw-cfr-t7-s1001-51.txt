7 C.F.R. § 1001.51 — Class I differential and price.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1001.51
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Milk), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1001.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1001 (Milk In The Northeast Marketing Area). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Class I differential shall be the differential established for Suffolk County, Massachusetts, which is reported in § 1000.52. The Class I price shall be the price computed pursuant to § 1000.50(a) for Suffolk County, Massachusetts.
