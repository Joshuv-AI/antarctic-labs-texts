31 U.S.C. § 793 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:31:793
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section793
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 7 (Government Accountability Office); Subchapter VII (Center For Audit Excellence). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary to carry out this subchapter.
