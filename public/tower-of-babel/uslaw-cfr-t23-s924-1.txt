23 C.F.R. § 924.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:23:924.1
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-924.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 924 (Highway Safety Improvement Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this regulation is to prescribe requirements for the development, implementation, and evaluation of a highway safety improvement program (HSIP) in each State.
