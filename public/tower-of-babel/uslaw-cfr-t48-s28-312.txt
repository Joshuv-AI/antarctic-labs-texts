48 C.F.R. § 28.312 — 28.312 Contract clause for insurance of leased motor vehicles.
Citation: us:cfr:48:28.312
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-28.312
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 28 (Bonds And Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.228-8, Liability and Insurance—Leased Motor Vehicles, in solicitations and contracts for the leasing of motor vehicles (see subpart 8.11).
