A Lie algebra associated with adjoint multiple zeta values
Takumi Anzawa

Abstract
. Jarossay (arXiv math.NT1412.5099) introduced adjoint multiple zeta values and, by using Racinet’s dual formulation of the generating series of multiple zeta values, found ℚ \mathbb{Q} -algebraic relations among them, referred to as the adjoint double shuffle relations . Additionally, Jarossay defined the affine scheme AdDMR 0 \mathrm{AdDMR}_{0} determined by the adjoint double shuffle relations and posed a question whether AdDMR 0 \mathrm{AdDMR}_{0} is isomorphic to Racinet’s double shuffle group DMR 0 \mathrm{DMR}_{0} (Publ. Math. Inst. Hautes Études Sci. (2002), no. 95). In this paper, we refine Jarossay’s question by introducing the condition referred to as the adjoint conditions, and, based on this refinement, we study the corresponding Lie algebraic aspect. Within this framework, we construct the Lie algebra associated with the adjoint double shuffle relations by imposing Hirose’s parity results.

When

MZVs were first studied by Euler and Goldbach.
They studied double zeta values (the case

-linear relations among MZVs which is known as the sum formula nowadays.
Around 1990, Hoffman

Brown

2

-linear combination of MZVs.

These relations arise from the combination of two kinds of product-to-sum relations, where the first one originates from the iterated integral expression, and the second one originates from the multiple series expressions.

Extended double shuffle relations

MZVs are known to satisfy two types of the product to sum relations among MZVs arising from their integral and series expressions.

\mathbb{Q}
-linear relations among MZVs, termed as double shuffle relations.

-linear relations among MZVs. In this subsection, we briefly discuss this framework in more general setting.

For a
