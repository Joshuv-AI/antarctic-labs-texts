46 C.F.R. § 8.300 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:46:8.300
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-8.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 8 (Vessel Inspection Alternatives). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart establishes options for vessel owners and operators to obtain required international convention certification through means other than those prescribed elsewhere in this chapter.
