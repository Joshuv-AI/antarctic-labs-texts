5 C.F.R. § 582.401 — Aggregate disposable earnings.
Citation: us:cfr:5:582.401
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-582.401
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 582 (Commercial Garnishment Of Federal Employees' Pay). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In accordance with the Consumer Credit Protection Act, the aggregate disposable earnings under this part are the employee-obligor's pay less those amounts excluded in accordance with § 582.103.
