42 U.S.C. § 660 — Civil action to enforce child support obligations; jurisdiction of district courts
Citation: us:usc:42:660
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section660
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 7 (Social Security); Subchapter IV (Grants To States For Aid And Services To Needy Families With Children And For Child-Welfare Services); Part D (Child Support And Establishment Of Paternity). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The district courts of the United States shall have jurisdiction, without regard to any amount in controversy, to hear and determine any civil action certified by the Secretary of Health and Human Services under section 652(a)(8) of this title. A civil action under this section may be brought in any judicial district in which the claim arose, the plaintiff resides, or the defendant resides.
