7 U.S.C. § 254 — Penalties for noncompliance
Citation: us:usc:7:254
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section254
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 10 (Warehouses). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If a person fails to comply with any requirement of this chapter (including regulations promulgated under this chapter), the Secretary may assess, on the record after an opportunity for a hearing, a civil penalty—
(1)
of not more than $25,000 per violation, if an agricultural product is not involved in the violation; or
(2)
of not more than 100 percent of the value of the agricultural product, if an agricultural product is involved in the violation.
