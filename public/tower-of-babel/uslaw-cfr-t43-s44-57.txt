43 C.F.R. § 44.57 — Can a unit of general local government appeal a rejection of a protest?
Citation: us:cfr:43:44.57
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-44.57
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 44 (Financial Assistance, Local Governments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any affected local government may appeal the Department's decision to reject a protest to the Interior Board of Land Appeals under 43 CFR part 4.
