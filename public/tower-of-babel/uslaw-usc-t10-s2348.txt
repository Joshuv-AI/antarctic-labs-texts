10 U.S.C. § 2348 — Inventories of supplies not to be increased
Citation: us:usc:10:2348
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section2348
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Property); Chapter 138 (Cooperative Agreements With Nato Allies And Other Countries); Subchapter I (Acquisition And Cross-Servicing Agreements). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Inventories of supplies for elements of the armed forces may not be increased for the purpose of transferring supplies under the authority of this subchapter.
