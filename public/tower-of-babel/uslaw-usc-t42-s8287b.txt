42 U.S.C. § 8287b — Reports
Citation: us:usc:42:8287b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section8287b
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 91 (National Energy Conservation Policy); Subchapter VII (Energy Savings Performance Contracts). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each Federal agency shall periodically furnish the Secretary of Energy with full and complete information on its activities under this subchapter, and the Secretary shall include in the report submitted to Congress under section 8260 1 of this title a description of the progress made by each Federal agency in—
(1)
including the authority provided by this subchapter in its contracting practices; and
(2)
achieving energy savings under contracts entered into under this subchapter.
