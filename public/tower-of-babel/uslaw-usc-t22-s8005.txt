22 U.S.C. § 8005 — Inoperability of determination and waivers
Citation: us:usc:22:8005
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section8005
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 87 (United States And India Nuclear Cooperation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A determination and any waiver under section 8003 of this title shall cease to be effective if the President determines that India has detonated a nuclear explosive device after December 18, 2006.
