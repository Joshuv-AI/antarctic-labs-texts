2 U.S.C. § 1902 — Compensation of Chief
Citation: us:usc:2:1902
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section1902
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 29 (Capitol Police); Subchapter I (Organization And Administration); Part A (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The annual rate of pay for the Chief of the Capitol Police shall be the amount equal to the annual rate of basic pay for level II of the Executive Schedule under section 5313 of title 5.
