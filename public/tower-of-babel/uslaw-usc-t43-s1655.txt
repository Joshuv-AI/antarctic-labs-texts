43 U.S.C. § 1655 — Roads and airports
Citation: us:usc:43:1655
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section1655
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 34 (Trans-Alaska Pipeline). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A right-of-way, permit, lease, or other authorization granted under section 1652(b) of this title for a road or airstrip as a related facility of the trans-Alaska pipeline may provide for the construction of a public road or airstrip.
