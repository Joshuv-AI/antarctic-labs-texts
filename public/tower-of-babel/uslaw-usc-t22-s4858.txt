22 U.S.C. § 4858 — Training to improve perimeter security at United States diplomatic missions abroad
Citation: us:usc:22:4858
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section4858
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 58 (Diplomatic Security); Subchapter IV (Diplomatic Security Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Training
It is the sense of Congress that the President should use the authority under chapter 8 of title II of the Foreign Assistance Act of 1961 [22 U.S.C. 2349aa et seq.] (relating to antiterrorism assistance) to improve perimeter security of United States diplomatic missions abroad.
