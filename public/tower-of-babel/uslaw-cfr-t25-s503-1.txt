25 C.F.R. § 503.1 — Purpose of this part.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 503 (Commission Information Collection Requirements Under The Paperwork Reduction Act: Omb Control Numbers And Expiration Dates). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part displays the control numbers and expiration dates assigned to information collection requirements of the National Indian Gaming Commission (NIGC, or the Commission) assigned by the Director of the Office of Management and Budget (OMB) pursuant to the Paperwork Reduction Act of 1980, 44 U.S.C. 3501 et seq.
