29 C.F.R. § 102.171 — Cost shifting.
Citation: us:cfr:29:102.171
Creator: National Labor Relations Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-102.171
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 102 (Rules And Regulations, Series 8). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Costs incurred by the Agency in connection with referral of debts for tax refund offset will be added to the debt and thus increase the amount of the offset. Such costs may include administrative costs and attorneys fees.
