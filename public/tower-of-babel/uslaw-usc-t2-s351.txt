2 U.S.C. § 351 — Establishment
Citation: us:usc:2:351
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section351
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 11 (Citizens’ Commission On Public Service And Compensation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is hereby established a commission to be known as the Citizens’ Commission on Public Service and Compensation (hereinafter referred to as the “Commission”).
