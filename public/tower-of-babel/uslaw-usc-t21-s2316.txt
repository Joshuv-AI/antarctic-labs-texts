21 U.S.C. § 2316 — Briefings on implementation
Citation: us:usc:21:2316
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title21-section2316
Context: United States Code, Title 21 (Food And Drugs); Chapter 28 (Sanctions With Respect To Foreign Traffickers Of Illicit Synthetic Opioids); Subchapter I (Sanctions With Respect To Foreign Opioid Traffickers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 90 days after December 20, 2019, and every 180 days thereafter until the date that is 5 years after December 20, 2019, the President, acting through the Secretary of State and the Director of National Intelligence, in coordination with the Secretary of the Treasury, shall provide to the appropriate congressional committees and leadership a comprehensive briefing on efforts to implement this subchapter.
