26 U.S.C. § 5690 — Definition of the term “person”
Citation: us:usc:26:5690
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section5690
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 51 (Distilled Spirits, Wines, And Beer); Subchapter J (Penalties, Seizures, And Forfeitures Relating To Liquors); Part IV (Penalty, Seizure, And Forfeiture Provisions Common To Liquors). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The term “person”, as used in this subchapter, includes an officer or employee of a corporation or a member or employee of a partnership, who as such officer, employee, or member is under a duty to perform the act in respect of which the violation occurs.
