20 C.F.R. § 416.1227 — When the resources excluded under a plan to achieve self-support begin to count.
Citation: us:cfr:20:416.1227
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-416.1227
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 416 (Supplemental Security Income For The Aged, Blind, And Disabled). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The resources that were excluded under the individual's plan will begin to be counted as of the first day of the month following the month in which any of these circumstances occur:
(a) Failing to follow the conditions of the plan:
(b) Abandoning the plan;
(c) Completing the time schedule outlined in the plan; or
(d) Reaching the goal as outlined in the plan.
