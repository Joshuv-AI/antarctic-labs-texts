20 C.F.R. § 418.3205 — What makes an application a claim for a subsidy?
Citation: us:cfr:20:418.3205
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-418.3205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 418 (Medicare Subsidies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We will consider your application a claim for the subsidy if:
(a) You, or someone acting on your behalf as described in § 418.3215, complete an application on a form prescribed by us;
(b) You, or someone acting on your behalf as described in § 418.3215, file the application with us pursuant to § 418.3220; and
(c) You are alive on the first day of the month in which the application is filed.
