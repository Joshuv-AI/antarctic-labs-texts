19 C.F.R. § 151.24 — Unlading facilities for bulk sugar.
Citation: us:cfr:19:151.24
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-151.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 151 (Examination, Sampling, And Testing Of Merchandise). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When dutiable sugar is to be imported in bulk, a full description of the facilities to be used in unlading the sugar shall be submitted to the Commissioner of Customs as far as possible in advance of the date of importation, and special instructions will be issued as to the methods to be applied in weighing and sampling such sugar.
