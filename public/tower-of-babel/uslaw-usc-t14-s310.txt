14 U.S.C. § 310 — Director of Coast Guard Investigative Service

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 3 (Composition And Organization); Subchapter II (Positions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In General.—
There shall be a Director of the Coast Guard Investigative Service.
(b) Chain of Command.—
The Director of the Coast Guard Investigative Service shall report directly to and be under the general supervision of the Commandant, acting through the Vice Commandant of the Coast Guard.
