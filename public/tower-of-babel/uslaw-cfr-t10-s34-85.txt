10 C.F.R. § 34.85 — Records of radiation surveys.
Citation: us:cfr:10:34.85
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-34.85
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 34 (Licenses For Industrial Radiography And Radiation Safety Requirements For Industrial Radiographic Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each licensee shall maintain a record of each exposure device survey conducted before the device is placed in storage as specified in § 34.49(c), if that survey is the last one performed in the workday. Each record must be maintained for 3 years after it is made.
