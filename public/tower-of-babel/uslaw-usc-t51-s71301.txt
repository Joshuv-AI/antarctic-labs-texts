51 U.S.C. § 71301 — Common docking system standard to enable rescue
Citation: us:usc:51:71301
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section71301
Context: United States Code, Title 51 (National And Commercial Space Programs); Chapter 713 (Cooperation For Safety Among Spacefaring Nations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In order to maximize the ability to rescue astronauts whose space vehicles have become disabled, the Administrator shall enter into discussions with the appropriate representatives of spacefaring nations who have or plan to have crew transportation systems capable of orbital flight or flight beyond low Earth orbit for the purpose of agreeing on a common docking system standard.
