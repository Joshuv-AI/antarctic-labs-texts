41 C.F.R. § 102-117.65 — -117.65 Determine mode.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-117.65
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-117.65
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-117 (117—Transportation Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Shipping urgency, origin, destination, and any special handling requirements determine which mode of transportation is selected. Each mode has unique requirements for documentation, liability, size, weight, and delivery time. HAZMAT, radioactive, and other specialized cargo may require special permits which may limit choices.
