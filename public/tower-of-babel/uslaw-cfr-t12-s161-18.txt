12 C.F.R. § 161.18 — Director.
Citation: us:cfr:12:161.18
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-161.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 161 (Definitions For Regulations Affecting All Savings Associations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The term director means any director, trustee, or other person performing similar functions with respect to any organization whether incorporated or unincorporated. Such term does not include an advisory director, honorary director, director emeritus, or similar person, unless the person is otherwise performing functions similar to those of a director.
