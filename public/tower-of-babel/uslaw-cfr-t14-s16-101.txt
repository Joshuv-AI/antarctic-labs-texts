14 C.F.R. § 16.101 — Basis for the initiation of agency action.
Citation: us:cfr:14:16.101
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-16.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 16 (Rules Of Practice For Federally-Assisted Airport Enforcement Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The FAA may initiate its own investigation of any matter within the applicability of this part without having received a complaint. The investigation may include, without limitation, any of the actions described in § 16.29(b).
