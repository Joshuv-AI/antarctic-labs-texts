46 C.F.R. § 127.630 — Electrical installation requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:46:127.630
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-127.630
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 127 (Construction And Arrangements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Electrical installations must comply with regulation II—1/42 of SOLAS, 1974, as amended (incorporated by reference, see § 125.180) in lieu of regulation II-1/43.
