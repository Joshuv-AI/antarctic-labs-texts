Foreign-Trade Zone (FTZ) 27, Notification of Proposed Production 
Activity; Rochester Electronics, LLC; (Semiconductor Products); 
Newburyport, Massachusetts

    The Massachusetts Port Authority, grantee of FTZ 27, submitted a 
notification of proposed production activity to the FTZ Board (the 
Board) on behalf of Rochester Electronics, LLC for Rochester's 
facilities in Newburyport, Massachusetts within FTZ 27. The 
notification conforming to the requirements of the Board's regulations 
(15 CFR 400.22) was received on September 3, 2026.
    Pursuant to 15 CFR 400.14(b), FTZ production activity would be 
limited to the specific foreign-status material(s)/component(s) and 
specific finished product(s) described in the submitted notification 
(summarized below) and subsequently authorized by the Board. The 
benefits that may stem from conducting production activity under FTZ 
procedures are explained in the background section of the Board's 
website--accessible via www.trade.gov/ftz.
    The proposed finished products include: Light-emitting diodes 
(LEDs); Semiconductor microprocessors; and Semiconductor 
microcontrollers (duty free).
    The proposed foreign-status materials/components include: Epoxy 
molding compound (epoxy cresol novolac resin) for plastic package 
encapsulation; Flexible plastic (PVC) tubes for component handling; 
Flexible plastic (PVC/polyethylene) pipes; Flexible plastic (PVC/
polyethylene) hoses; Plastic boxes for packaging and shipment of 
finished semiconductor devices; Plastic cases for packaging and 
shipment of finished semiconductor devices; Plastic crates for 
packaging and shipment of finished semiconductor devices; Gold bonding 
wire for semiconductor wire bonding; Bare printed circuit boards; 
unpackaged Semiconductor chips; Bare semiconductor die; Diffused 
monocrystalline silicon wafers bearing completed LED die, prior to 
singulation; Diffused monocrystalline silicon wafers, 100 mm (4-inch) 
to 200 mm (8-inch) diameter, bearing completed integrated circuit die, 
prior to singulation; Microprocessor integrated circuits used as 
inputs; Microcontroller integrated circuits used as inputs; Amplifier 
integrated circuits; Analog-to-digital converter integrated circuits; 
Lead frames for integrated circuits, copper alloy, supplied in strip 
form; and Lead frames for LEDs, copper alloy, supplied in strip form 
(duty rate ranges from duty-free to 6.1%).
    The request indicates that certain materials/components are subject 
to duties under section 301 of the Trade Act of 1974 (section 301), 
depending on the country of origin. The applicable section 301 decision 
requires subject merchandise to be admitted to FTZs in privileged 
foreign status as described in 19 CFR 146.41.
    Public comment is invited from interested parties. Submissions 
shall be addressed to the Board's Executive Secretary and sent to: 
[email protected]. The closing period for their receipt is November 3, 
2026.
    A copy of the notification will be available for public inspection 
in the ``Online FTZ Information System'' section of the Board's 
website.
