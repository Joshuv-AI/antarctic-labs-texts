48 C.F.R. § 703.104-4 — 703.104-4 Disclosure, protection, and marking of contractor bid or proposal information and source selection information.
Citation: us:cfr:48:703.104-4
Creator: Chapter 7—Agency For International Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-703.104-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 703 (Improper Business Practices And Personal Conflicts Of Interest). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A contracting officer may authorize the release of proprietary and/or source selection information outside the Government for evaluation purposes pursuant to (48 CFR) FAR 15.305(c) and (48 CFR) AIDAR 715.305(c).
