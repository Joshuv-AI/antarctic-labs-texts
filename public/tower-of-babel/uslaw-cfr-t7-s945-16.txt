7 C.F.R. § 945.16 — Container.
Citation: us:cfr:7:945.16
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-945.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 945 (Irish Potatoes Grown In Certain Designated Counties In Idaho, And Malheur County, Oregon). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Container means a sack, box, bag, crate, hamper, basket, carton, package, barrel, or any other type of receptacle used in the packaging, transportation, sale, or other handling of potatoes.
