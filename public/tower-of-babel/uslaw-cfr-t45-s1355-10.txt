45 C.F.R. § 1355.10 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1355.10
Creator: Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1355.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1355 (General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless otherwise specified, part 1355 applies to States and Indian Tribes and contains general requirements for Federal financial participation under titles IV-B and IV-E of the Social Security Act.
