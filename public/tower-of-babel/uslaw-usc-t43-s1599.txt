43 U.S.C. § 1599 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:43:1599
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section1599
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 32A (Colorado River Basin Salinity Control); Subchapter II (Measures Upstream From Imperial Dam). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this subchapter—
(a)
all terms that are defined in the Colorado River Compact shall have the meanings therein defined;
(b)
“Colorado River Basin States” means the States of Arizona, California, Colorado, Nevada, New Mexico, Utah, and Wyoming.
