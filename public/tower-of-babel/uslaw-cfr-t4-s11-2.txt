4 C.F.R. § 11.2 — Practice by attorneys.
Citation: us:cfr:4:11.2
Creator: Government Accountability Office
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-4/section-11.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 4 (Accounts), Part 11 (Recognition Of Attorneys And Other Representatives). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person who is a member in good standing of the bar of the Supreme Court of the United States or of the highest court of any State, territory, or the District of Columbia, and is not under any order of any court suspending, enjoining, restraining, disbarring, or otherwise restricting him in the practice of law, may represent others before the Government Accountability Office.
