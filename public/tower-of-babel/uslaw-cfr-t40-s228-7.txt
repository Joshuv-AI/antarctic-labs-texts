40 C.F.R. § 228.7 — Regulation of disposal site use.
Citation: us:cfr:40:228.7
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-228.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 228 (Criteria For The Management Of Disposal Sites For Ocean Dumping). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where necessary, disposal site use will be regulated by setting limitations on times of dumping and rates of discharge, and establishing a disposal site monitoring program.
