46 U.S.C. § 12307 — Regulations on numbering and fees
Citation: us:usc:46:12307
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section12307
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part H (Identification Of Vessels); Chapter 123 (Numbering Undocumented Vessels). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The authority issuing a number under this chapter may prescribe regulations and establish fees to carry out the intent of this chapter. The fees shall apply equally to residents and nonresidents of the State. A State issuing authority may impose only conditions for vessel numbering that are—
(1)
prescribed by this chapter or regulations of the Secretary about the standard numbering system; or
(2)
related to proof of payment of State or local taxes.
