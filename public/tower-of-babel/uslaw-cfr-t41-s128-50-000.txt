41 C.F.R. § 128-50.000 — -50.000 Scope of part.
Citation: us:cfr:41:128-50.000
Creator: Chapter 128—Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-128-50.000
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 128-50 (50—Seized Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part prescribes the policies for the storage and care of seized personal property; the preparation and maintenance of inventory records of its seized personal property; the conducting of periodic internal reviews; and the investigation of any discrepancy between the inventory records and the actual amount of its seized personal property.
