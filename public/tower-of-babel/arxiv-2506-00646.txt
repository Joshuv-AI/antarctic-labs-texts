F F -injectivity does not imply F F -fullness in normal domains
Alessandro De Stefani, Thomas Polstra, Austyn Simpson

Abstract
. We construct examples of noetherian three-dimensional local geometrically normal domains of prime characteristic which are F F -injective but not F F -full. Along the way, we find examples of two-dimensional local geometrically normal domains which are F F -injective but not F F -anti-nilpotent. A crucial theme of our constructions is the behavior of F F -injectivity along a purely inseparable finite base change.
