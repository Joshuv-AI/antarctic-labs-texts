7 U.S.C. § 2304 — Disclaimer of intention to prohibit normal dealing
Citation: us:usc:7:2304
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2304
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 56 (Unfair Trade Practices Affecting Producers Of Agricultural Products). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall prevent handlers and producers from selecting their customers and suppliers for any reason other than a producer’s membership in or contract with an association of producers, nor require a handler to deal with an association of producers.
