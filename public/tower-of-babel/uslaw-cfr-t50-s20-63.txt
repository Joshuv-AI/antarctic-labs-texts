50 C.F.R. § 20.63 — Species identification requirement.
Citation: us:cfr:50:20.63
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-20.63
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 20 (Migratory Bird Hunting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person shall import migratory game birds unless each such bird has one fully feathered wing attached, and such wing must remain attached while being transported between the port of entry and the personal abode of the possessor or between the port of entry and a migratory bird preservation facility.
