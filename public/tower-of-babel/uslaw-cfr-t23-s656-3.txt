23 C.F.R. § 656.3 — Policy.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 656 (Carpool And Vanpool Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Section 126(d) of the Surface Transportation Assistance Act of 1978 declares that special effort should be made to promote commuter modes of transportation which conserve energy, reduce pollution, and reduce traffic congestion.
