42 U.S.C. § 8628 — Limitation on use of grants for construction
Citation: us:usc:42:8628
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section8628
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 94 (Low-Income Energy Assistance); Subchapter II (Low-Income Home Energy Assistance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Grants made under this subchapter may not be used by the State, or by any other person with which the State makes arrangements to carry out the purposes of this subchapter, for the purchase or improvement of land, or the purchase, construction, or permanent improvement (other than low-cost residential weatherization or other energy-related home repairs) of any building or other facility.
