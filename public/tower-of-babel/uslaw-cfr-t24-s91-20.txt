24 C.F.R. § 91.20 — Exceptions.
Citation: us:cfr:24:91.20
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-91.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 91 (Consolidated Submissions For Community Planning And Development Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The HUD Field Office may grant a jurisdiction an exception from the submission deadline for plans and reports and from a requirement in the implementation guidelines for good cause, as determined by the field office and reported in writing to HUD Headquarters, unless the requirement is required by statute or regulation.
