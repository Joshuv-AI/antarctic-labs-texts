36 U.S.C. § 150907 — Principal office
Citation: us:usc:36:150907
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section150907
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1509 (National Council On Radiation Protection And Measurements). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The principal office of the corporation shall be in the District of Columbia or another place decided by the board of directors. However, the activities of the corporation may be conducted throughout the world.
