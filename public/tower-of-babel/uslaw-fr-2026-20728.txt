JUDICIAL CONFERENCE OF THE UNITED STATES


Committee on Rules of Practice and Procedure; Meeting of the 
Judicial Conference

AGENCY: Judicial Conference of the United States, Committee on Rules of 
Practice and Procedure.

ACTION: Notice of open meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Committee on Rules of Practice and Procedure will hold an 
in-person meeting on January 5, 2027 in Washington, DC. The meeting 
will be conducted in hybrid format with virtual attendance options 
available. The meeting is open to the public for observation but not 
participation. A meeting agenda and supporting materials will be posted 
at least 7 days in advance of the meeting at: https://www.uscourts.gov/forms-rules/records-rules-committees/agenda-books.

DATES: January 5, 2027 (meeting date) and December 29, 2026 
(registration deadline for in-person observation).

ADDRESSES: Requests to observe must be made in writing by emailing the 
request to: [email protected]. Please see the 
Supplementary Information section in

[[Page 64679]]

this notice for further instructions on observing the meeting.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Carolyn A. Dubay, Chief Counsel, Rules 
Committee Staff, Administrative Office of the U.S. Courts, Thurgood 
Marshall Federal Judiciary Building, One Columbus Circle NE, Suite 7-
300, Washington, DC 20544, Phone (202) 502-1820, 
[email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: To observe the meeting in person, 
individuals must contact the office listed above by 5 p.m. (eastern 
time) on December 29, 2026. After this deadline, only virtual 
observation is permitted. Virtual registration is available until the 
meeting date, provided it is completed before the projected end time.

(Authority: 28 U.S.C. 2073.)

    Dated: October 7, 2026.
Shelly L. Cox,
Management Analyst, Rules Committee Staff.
