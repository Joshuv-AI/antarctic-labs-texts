34 U.S.C. § 12443 — Eligible entities
Citation: us:usc:34:12443
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section12443
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 121 (Violent Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Subchapter III (Violence Against Women); Part I (Privacy Protections For Victims Of Domestic Violence, Dating Violence, Sexual Violence, And Stalking). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Entities eligible for grants under this part include—
(1)
jurisdictions or agencies within jurisdictions having authority or responsibility for developing or maintaining public databases, registries or victim notification systems;
(2)
nonprofit nongovernmental victim advocacy organizations having expertise regarding confidentiality, privacy, and information technology and how these issues are likely to impact the safety of victims;
(3)
States or State agencies;
(4)
local governments or agencies;
(5)
Indian tribal governments or tribal organizations;
(6)
territorial governments, agencies, or organizations; or
(7)
nonprofit nongovernmental victim advocacy organizations, including statewide domestic violence and sexual assault coalitions.
