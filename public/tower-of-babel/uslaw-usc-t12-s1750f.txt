12 U.S.C. § 1750f — Rules and regulations
Citation: us:usc:12:1750f
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 13 (National Housing); Subchapter X (National Defense Housing Insurance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized and directed to make such rules and regulations as may be necessary to carry out the provisions of this subchapter.
