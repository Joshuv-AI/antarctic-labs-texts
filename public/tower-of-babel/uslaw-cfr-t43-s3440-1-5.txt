43 C.F.R. § 3440.1-5 — -5 Compliance with Surface Mining Control and Reclamation Act.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3440.1-5
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3440.1-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3440 (Licenses To Mine). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Mining on a license to mine shall not commence without a permit issued by the Surface Mining Officer unless the operation is exempt from the permit requirements under 30 CFR 700.11.
