41 C.F.R. § 102-37.200 — -37.200 Reporting unneeded, usable property for disposal.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-37.200
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-37.200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-37 (37—Donation Of Surplus Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When reporting unneeded, usable property that is not required for transfer to another SASP, provide GSA with the:
(a) Description of each line item of property, current condition code, quantity, unit and total acquisition cost, State serial number, demilitarization code, and any special handling conditions;
(b) Date you received each line item of property listed; and
(c) Certification of reimbursement requested under § 102-37.215.
