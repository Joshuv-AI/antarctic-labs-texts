6 U.S.C. § 754 — Use of existing resources
Citation: us:usc:6:754
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title6-section754
Context: United States Code, Title 6 (Domestic Security); Chapter 2 (National Emergency Management); Subchapter II (Comprehensive Preparedness System); Part A (National Preparedness System). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In establishing the national preparedness goal and national preparedness system, the Administrator shall use existing preparedness documents, planning tools, and guidelines to the extent practicable and consistent with this Act.
