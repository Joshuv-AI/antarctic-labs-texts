31 C.F.R. § 224.1 — What does this part cover?
Citation: us:cfr:31:224.1
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-224.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 224 (Federal Process Agents Of Surety Corporations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part provides guidance on when a surety corporation must appoint a service of process agent and how the surety corporation complies with this requirement.
