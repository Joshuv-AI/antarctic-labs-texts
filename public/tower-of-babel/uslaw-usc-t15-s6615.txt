15 U.S.C. § 6615 — Applicability of State law
Citation: us:usc:15:6615
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section6615
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 92 (Year 2000 Computer Date Change). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to affect the applicability of any State law that provides stricter limits on damages and liabilities, affording greater protection to defendants in Y2K actions, than are provided in this chapter.
