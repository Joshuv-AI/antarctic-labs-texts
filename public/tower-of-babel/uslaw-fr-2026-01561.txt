DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Center for Complementary & Integrative Health; Amended 
Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Advisory Council for Complementary and Integrative Health, April 17, 
2026, 10:00 a.m. to April 17, 2026, 05:00 p.m., National Institutes of 
Health, DEM 2, 6707 Democracy Boulevard, Bethesda, MD 20892 which was 
published in the Federal Register on December 16, 2025, 90 FR 58257.
    This amendment reflects the new end time for the NACCIH Advisory 
Council Meeting, with the Closed Session ending at 11:30 a.m. and the 
Open Session starting at 12:00 p.m. The Open Session will be broadcast 
to the public. The meeting is partially Closed to the public.

    Dated: January 22, 2026.
Bruce A. George,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
