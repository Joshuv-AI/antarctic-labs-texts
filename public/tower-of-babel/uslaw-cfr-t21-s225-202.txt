21 C.F.R. § 225.202 — Formula, production, and distribution records.
Citation: us:cfr:21:225.202
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-225.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 225 (Current Good Manufacturing Practice For Medicated Feeds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Records shall be maintained identifying the formulation, date of mixing, and if not for own use, date of shipment. The records shall be adequate to facilitate the recall of specific batches of medicated feed that have been distributed. Such records shall be retained on the premises for 1 year following the date of last distribution.
