34 C.F.R. § 76.732 — Records related to performance.
Citation: us:cfr:34:76.732
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-76.732
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 76 (State-Administered Formula Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A grantee must keep records of significant project experiences and results.
(b) The grantee must use the records under paragraph (a) to—
(1) Determine progress in accomplishing project objectives;
(2) Inform periodic review and continuous improvement of the project plans; and
(3) Revise those project objectives, if necessary.
