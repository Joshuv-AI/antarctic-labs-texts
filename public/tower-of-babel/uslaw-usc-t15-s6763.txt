15 U.S.C. § 6763 — Federal funding prohibited
Citation: us:usc:15:6763
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section6763
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 93 (Insurance); Subchapter III (National Association Of Registered Agents And Brokers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Association may not receive, accept, or borrow any amounts from the Federal Government to pay for, or reimburse,1 the Association for, the costs of establishing or operating the Association.
