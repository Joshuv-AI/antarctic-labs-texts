49 C.F.R. § 1242.01 — Expenses solely related to freight service and passenger service.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1242.01
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1242.01
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1242 (Separation Of Common Operating Expenses Between Freight Service And Passenger Service For Railroads 1). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Uniform System of Accounts for Railroad Companies (49 CFR part 1201) requires that carriers assign directly to freight service or to passenger service, including allied services, the expenses, taxes, and purchased services incurred solely for the benefit of either freight or passenger service.
