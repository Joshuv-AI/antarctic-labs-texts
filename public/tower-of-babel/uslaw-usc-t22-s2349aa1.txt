22 U.S.C. § 2349aa–1 — Purposes

Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 32 (Foreign Assistance); Subchapter II (Military Assistance And Sales); Part VIII (Antiterrorism Assistance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Activities conducted under this part shall be designed—
(1)
to enhance the antiterrorism skills of friendly countries by providing training and equipment to deter and counter terrorism;
(2)
to strengthen the bilateral ties of the United States with friendly governments by offering concrete assistance in this area of great mutual concern; and
(3)
to increase respect for human rights by sharing with foreign civil authorities modern, humane, and effective antiterrorism techniques.
