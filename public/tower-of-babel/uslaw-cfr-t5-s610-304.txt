5 C.F.R. § 610.304 — Coverage.
Citation: us:cfr:5:610.304
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-610.304
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 610 (Hours Of Duty). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to regular employees of the Federal Government paid at daily, hourly, or piecework rates. This subpart does not apply to experts and consultants.
