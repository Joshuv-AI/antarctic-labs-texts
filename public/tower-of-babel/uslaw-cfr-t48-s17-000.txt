48 C.F.R. § 17.000 — 17.000 Scope of part.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 17 (Special Contracting Methods). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part prescribes policies and procedures for the acquisition of supplies and services through special contracting methods, including—
(a) Multi-year contracting;
(b) Options;
(c) Leader company contracting; and
(d) The use of reverse auctions to obtain competitive pricing.
