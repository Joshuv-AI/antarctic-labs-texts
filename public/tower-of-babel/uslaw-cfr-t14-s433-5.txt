14 C.F.R. § 433.5 — Operational restrictions on a reentry site.
Citation: us:cfr:14:433.5
Creator: Commercial Space Transportation, Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-433.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 433 (License To Operate A Reentry Site). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A license to operate a reentry site authorizes the licensee to offer use of the site to support reentry of a reentry vehicle for which the three-sigma footprint of the vehicle upon reentry is wholly contained within the site.
