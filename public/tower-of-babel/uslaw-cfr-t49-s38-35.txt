49 C.F.R. § 38.35 — Public information system.

Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 38 (Americans With Disabilities Act (Ada) Accessibility Specifications For Transportation Vehicles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Vehicles in excess of 22 feet in length, used in multiple-stop, fixed-route service, shall be equipped with a public address system permitting the driver, or recorded or digitized human speech messages, to announce stops and provide other passenger information within the vehicle.
