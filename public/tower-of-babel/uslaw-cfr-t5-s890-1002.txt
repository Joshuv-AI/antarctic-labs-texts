5 C.F.R. § 890.1002 — Use of terminology.
Citation: us:cfr:5:890.1002
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-890.1002
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 890 (Federal Employees Health Benefits Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless otherwise indicated, within this subpart the words “health care provider,” “provider,” and “he” mean a health care provider(s) of either gender or as a business entity, in either the singular or plural. The acronym “OPM” and the pronoun “it” connote the U.S. Office of Personnel Management.
