28 C.F.R. § 2.58 — Prior orders.
Citation: us:cfr:28:2.58
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-2.58
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 2 (Parole, Release, Supervision And Recommitment Of Prisoners, Youth Offenders, And Juvenile Delinquents). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any order of the United States Board of Parole entered prior to May 14, 1976, including, but not limited to, orders granting, denying, rescinding or revoking parole or mandatory release, shall be a valid order of the United States Parole Commission according to the terms stated in the order.
