If
q\in\mathbb{H}_{\mathbb{Z}_{p}},

q=q_{0}+q_{1}f_{1}+q_{2}f_{2}+q_{3}f_{3},
then

\overline{q}=q_{0}-q_{1}f_{1}-q_{2}f_{2}-q_{3}f_{3}

is called the
conjugate
of the element
q.
For
q\in\mathbb{H}_{\mathbb{Z}_{p}},
we consider the following elements:

\mathbf{t}\left(q\right)=q+\overline{q}\in\mathbb{H}_{\mathbb{Z}_{p}}

and

\,\mathbf{n}\left(q\right)=q\overline{q}=q_{0}^{2}+q_{1}^{2}+q_{2}^{2}+q_{3}^{2}\in\mathbb{H}_{\mathbb{Z}_{p}},

called the
trace
, respectively, the
norm
of the element
q\in\mathbb{H}_{\mathbb{Z}_{p}}
. Shortly, the norm and the trace of the
element
q
will be denoted
\mathbf{n}_{q}
and
\mathbf{t}_{q}
.  It   follows   that

\left(q+\overline{q}\right)q\,=q^{2}+\overline{q}q=q^{2}+\mathbf{n}\left(q\right)\cdot 1

and

q^{2}-\mathbf{t}\left(q\right)q+\mathbf{n}\left(q\right)=0,\forall q\in\mathbb{H}_{\mathbb{Z}_{p}},\,

therefore the generalized quaternion algebra is a
quadratic algebra
.

Since the field
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
is a finite field, due to the Wedderburn’s
Theorem, the quaternion algebra
\mathbb{H}_{\mathbb{Z}_{p}}
is allways a
non division algebra or a split algebra. Also, we must remark that the
multiplicative group
\left(\mathbb{Z}_{p}^{\ast},\cdot\right)
is a
cyclic group, for
p
a prime number.

Definition 1.
An element
x
in a ring
R
is called
nilpotent
if we can find a positive integer
n
such that
x^{n}=0
. The
number
n
, the smallest with this property, is called the
nilpotency index
. An element
x
in a ring
R
is called
k
-potent
, for
k>1
, a positive integer, if
k
is the smallest number such
that
x^{k}=x
. The number
k
is called the
k
-potency index
. For

k=2,
we have idempotent elements, for
k=3
, we have tripotent elements,
and so on.

In the paper [MS; 11], authors counted the number of zero divisors in
\mathbb{H}_{\mathbb{Z}_{p}},
that means the number of nonzero elements

such that
\mathbf{n}\left(x\right)=0

In the following we will count the number of

-potent elements in

Proposition 2.

Proposition 7.

With the above notations, the sets

A_{x}

and

B_{y}

have the same number of elements, namely

\left|A_{x}\right|=\left|B_{y}\right|=p\left(p+1\right).

Proof.
Since
\mathbf{t}_{x}\neq 0,\mathbf{t}_{y}\neq 0
, we have

\mathbf{t}_{x}=a\mathbf{t}_{y}

\mathbf{t}_{y}=b\mathbf{t}_{x}

\left|A_{x}\right|\leq\left|B_{y}\right|

Indeed,

has norm equal with zero. The map
\eta:B_{y}\rightarrow A_{x},\eta\left(r\right)=ar
is also an injective map, therefore
\left|B_{y}\right|\leq

\left|A_{x}\right|
. From

\left|A_{x}\right|=\left|B_{y}\right|
. From Remark 5, the number of elements with zero norm and nonzero trace is

results that we have
p-1

, all having the same number of

\mathbf{t}_{x}\in\{\widehat{1},\widehat{2},...,\widehat{p-1}\}
, therefore
\left|A_{x}\right|=\left|B_{y}\right|=p\left(p+1\right)
