22 U.S.C. § 7682 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:22:7682
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section7682
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 83 (United States Leadership Against Hiv/Aids, Tuberculosis, And Malaria); Subchapter V (International Financial Institutions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
There are authorized to be appropriated to the President such sums as may be necessary for the fiscal year 2004 and each fiscal year thereafter to carry out section 262p–8 of this title.
(b) Availability of funds
Amounts appropriated pursuant to subsection (a) are authorized to remain available until expended.
