Quantum Exclusion Process driven by Brownian Motions in terms of quantum Bernoulli noises
Authors: Suling Ren, Lixia Zhang
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
AbstractQuantum Bernoulli noises are the family of annihilation and creation operators on Bernoulli functionals, which satisfy a canonical anti-commutation relation in equal time. The paper is devoted to the study of Quantum Exclusion Process driven by Brownian Motions in terms of Quantum Bernoulli Noises. We first study the existence and uniqueness of regular solutions to the classical Quantum Exclusion Process driven by Brownian Motions. We then provide an explicit representation formula by separating the action on off-diagonal and diagonal operators, on which they are reduced to the Quantum Exclusion Process of classical Markov chains. Finally, we prove that the existence of regular invariant measures for Quantum Exclusion Process driven by Brownian Motions in terms of Quantum Bernoulli Noises.Subject terms: Pure mathematics, Quantum information

Introduction

Quantum Exclusion Process is a stochastic evolution equation in a complex Hilbert space, which arises in the study of open quantum systems and can be viewed as a stochastic and infinite-dimensional analog of the classical Quantum Exclusion Process. The theory of Quantum Exclusion Process provides a convenient mathematical model for describing the time evolution of the state of the system in physics and quantum probability1–4. In the past four decades, extensive research has been carried out on general Quantum Exclusion Process, and many in-depth results have been achieved5–7. Barchielli, Holevo, Paganoni, and Zucca examined a class of Quantum Exclusion Process whose coefficients involve bounded operators8,9. Holevo established the existence and uniqueness of the weak topology solution for a general Quantum Exclusion Process whose coefficients involve unbounded operators. Mora and Rebolledo extended Holevo’s work mentioned above to a more general case.

As a special class of Quantum Exclusion Process, Quantum Exclusion Process driven by Brownian Motions in terms of Quantum Bernoulli Noises can be viewed as quantum analogs of classical Quantum Exclusion Process in probability, which have received much attention in recent years. Quantum Bernoulli noises (QBN, for short) are the family of annihilation operators on Bernoulli functionals (Bernoulli annihilators, for short) and their adjoint ones, which satisfy a canonical anti-commutation relation (CAR) in equal time10. In recent years, these operators have begun to find applications in developing a discrete-time stochastic calculus in infinite dimensions. Indeed, Privault11 used the Bernoulli annihilators to define the gradients for Bernoulli functionals in 2008. In 2010, Nourdin et al.12 investigated normal approximation of Rademacher functionals (a special case of Bernoulli functionals) with the help of the Bernoulli annihilators. Recently, we have shown in13 that a wide class of quantum Markov semigroups can be constructed from QBN. As is known, quantum Markov semigroups are quantum analogues of the classical Markov semigroups in probability theory, which provide a mathematical model for describing the irreversible evolution of quantum systems interacting with the environment (see, e.g.4,14 and references therein). More recently, we15 have obtained a new relationship between the limiting probability distribution and its initial state from QBN.

Conclusions

To summarize, we inspired by Fagnola and Mora’s idea, we would like to apply QBN to study of mathematical properties of Quantum Exclusion Process allowing us to obtain information on physical phenomena. More specifically, The paper is devoted to the study of Quantum Exclusion Process driven by Brownian Motions associated with quantum Bernoulli noises in the space of square integrable complex-valued Bernoulli functionals. we were finally able to generalize the existence and uniqueness of regular solutions to nonlinear quantum exclusion Process despite the difficulties that rise from verifying the conditions satisfied by the reference operator. Furthermore, we provide an explicit representation formula by separating the action on off-diagonal and diagonal operators, on which they are reduced to the Quantum Exclusion Process of classical Markov chains. In the end, Eq. (2) allows us to prove the existence of a regular invariant measures for Quantum Exclusion Process.
