20 C.F.R. § 408.655 — When will we stop making your payments to a representative payee?
Citation: us:cfr:20:408.655
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-408.655
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 408 (Special Benefits For Certain World War Ii Veterans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To determine when we will stop representative payment for you, we follow the rules in § 404.2055 of this chapter.
