43 C.F.R. § 3283.8 — What must be filed with BLM to change the unit operator?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3283.8
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3283.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3280 (Geothermal Resources Unit Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To change the unit operator, the new operator must:
(a) Meet the qualification requirements of § 3281.11;
(b) Submit to BLM evidence of acceptable bonding under § 3214.13 of this chapter; and
(c) File with BLM written acceptance of the unit terms and obligations.
