33 U.S.C. § 985a — Cancellation of bonds issued under section 985
Citation: us:usc:33:985a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section985a
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 19 (Saint Lawrence Seaway). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, any bond issued under section 985 of this title, is hereby canceled together with the obligation to pay such bond.
