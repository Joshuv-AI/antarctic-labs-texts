48 C.F.R. § 32.1002 — 32.1002 Bases for performance-based payments.
Citation: us:cfr:48:32.1002
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-32.1002
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 32 (Contract Financing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Performance-based payments may be made on any of the following bases:
(a) Performance measured by objective, quantifiable methods.
(b) Accomplishment of defined events.
(c) Other quantifiable measures of results.
