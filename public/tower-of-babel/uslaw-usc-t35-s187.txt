35 U.S.C. § 187 — Nonapplicability to certain persons
Citation: us:usc:35:187
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title35-section187
Context: United States Code, Title 35 (Patents); Part II (Patentability Of Inventions And Grant Of Patents); Chapter 17 (Secrecy Of Certain Inventions And Filing Applications In Foreign Country). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The prohibitions and penalties of this chapter shall not apply to any officer or agent of the United States acting within the scope of his authority, nor to any person acting upon his written instructions or permission.
