42 U.S.C. § 10249 — Cooperation of other departments and agencies
Citation: us:usc:42:10249
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section10249
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 108 (Nuclear Waste Policy); Subchapter IV (Nuclear Waste Negotiator). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each department, agency, and instrumentality of the United States, including any independent agency, may furnish the Negotiator such information as he determines to be necessary to carry out his functions under this subchapter.
