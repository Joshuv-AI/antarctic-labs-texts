9 U.S.C. § 6 — Application heard as motion
Citation: us:usc:9:6
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title9-section6
Context: United States Code, Title 9 (Arbitration); Chapter 1 (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any application to the court hereunder shall be made and heard in the manner provided by law for the making and hearing of motions, except as otherwise herein expressly provided.
