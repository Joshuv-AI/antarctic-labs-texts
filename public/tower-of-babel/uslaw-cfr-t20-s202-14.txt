20 C.F.R. § 202.14 — Service incidental to railroad transportation.
Citation: us:cfr:20:202.14
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-202.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 202 (Employers Under The Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An organization, association, bureau or agency is performing a service in connection with or incidental to railroad transportation whenever it is engaged in the performance of functions which would normally be performed by the constituent employers in the absence of such organization, association, bureau, or agency.
