49 C.F.R. § 551.67 — Where and how may an agent be served?
Citation: us:cfr:49:551.67
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-551.67
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 551 (Procedural Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An agent may be served at the agent's office or usual place of residence, by registered or certified mail addressed to the agent with return receipt requested, or by any other manner authorized by law.
