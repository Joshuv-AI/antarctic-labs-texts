49 C.F.R. § 174.105 — Routing shipments, Division 1.1 or 1.2 (explosive) materials.
Citation: us:cfr:49:174.105
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-174.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 174 (Carriage By Rail). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Before a shipment of Division 1.1 or 1.2 (explosive) materials destined to a point beyond the lines of the initial carrier is accepted from the shipper, the initial carrier shall ascertain that the shipment can go forward by the route designated. To avoid delays en route, the initial carrier must be in possession of full rate information before forwarding the shipment.
