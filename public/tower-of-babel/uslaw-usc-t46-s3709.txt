46 U.S.C. § 3709 — Exemptions
Citation: us:usc:46:3709
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section3709
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part B (Inspection And Regulation Of Vessels); Chapter 37 (Carriage Of Liquid Bulk Dangerous Cargoes). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may exempt a vessel from the minimum requirements established by sections 3704–3706 of this title for segregated ballast, crude oil washing, and dedicated clean ballast if the Secretary decides that shore-based reception facilities are a preferred method of handling ballast and that adequate facilities are readily available.
