54 U.S.C. § 101101 — Authority to accept land, rights-of-way, buildings, other property, and money
Citation: us:usc:54:101101
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section101101
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 1011 (Donations); Subchapter I (Authority Of Secretary). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary in the administration of the Service may accept—
(1)
patented land, rights-of-way over patented land or other land, buildings, or other property within a System unit; and
(2)
money that may be donated for the purposes of the System.
