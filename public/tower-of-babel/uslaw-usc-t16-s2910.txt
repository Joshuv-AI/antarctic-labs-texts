16 U.S.C. § 2910 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:16:2910
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section2910
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 49 (Fish And Wildlife Conservation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated for purposes of making reimbursements under section 2905 of this title to States for the development and implementation of conservation plans and for administration of this chapter under section 2907 of this title not to exceed $5,000,000 for each of fiscal years 1992, 1993, 1994, 1995, 1996, and 1997.
