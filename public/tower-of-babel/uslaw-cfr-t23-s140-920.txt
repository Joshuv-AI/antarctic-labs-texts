23 C.F.R. § 140.920 — Lump sum payments.
Citation: us:cfr:23:140.920
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-140.920
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 140 (Reimbursement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where approved by FHWA, pursuant to 23 CFR 646.216(d)(3), reimbursement may be made as a lump sum payment, in lieu of actual costs.
