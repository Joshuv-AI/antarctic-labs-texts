10 U.S.C. § 3002 — Federal Acquisition Regulation
Citation: us:usc:10:3002
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section3002
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part V (Acquisition); Subpart A (General); Chapter 201 (Definitions); Subchapter I (Definitions Relating To Defense Acquisition System Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this part, the term “Federal Acquisition Regulation” means the Federal Acquisition Regulation issued pursuant to section 1303(a)(1) of title 41.
