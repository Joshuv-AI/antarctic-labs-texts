40 C.F.R. § 67.12 — Application for approval of programs.
Citation: us:cfr:40:67.12
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-67.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 67 (Epa Approval Of State Noncompliance Penalty Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A state that wishes to administer a section 120 program shall submit an application in writing to the Administrator describing its proposed program. All necessary supporting materials shall accompany the application.
