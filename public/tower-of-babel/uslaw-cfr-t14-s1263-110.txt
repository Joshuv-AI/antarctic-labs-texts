14 C.F.R. § 1263.110 — Intention to provide guidance.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1263 (Demand For Information Or Testimony Served On Agency Employees; Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part is intended to provide guidance for the internal operation of NASA and is not intended to, does not, and may not be relied upon to create any right of benefit—substantive or procedural—enforceable at law against the United States or NASA.
