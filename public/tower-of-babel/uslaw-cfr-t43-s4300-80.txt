43 C.F.R. § 4300.80 — How can I get a permit to cross reindeer over public lands?
Citation: us:cfr:43:4300.80
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4300.80
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4300 (Grazing Administration; Alaska; Reindeer; General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) BLM may issue a crossing permit free of charge when you file an application with BLM at least 30 days before the crossing is to begin. Lands crossed may include lands under a grazing permit.
(b) The application does not have to be on a BLM-approved form nor in a particular format, but it must show:
(1) The number of reindeer to be driven;
(2) The start date;
(3) The approximate period of time required for the crossing; and
(4) The land to be crossed.
(c) You must comply with applicable State and Federal laws on livestock quarantine and sanitation when crossing reindeer on public land.
