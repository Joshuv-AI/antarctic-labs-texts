22 U.S.C. § 5511 — Overseas Security Electronic Bulletin Board
Citation: us:usc:22:5511
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section5511
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 64 (United States Response To Terrorism Affecting Americans Abroad). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 60 days after November 16, 1990, the Secretary of State shall issue such rules and regulations as may be necessary to establish, under the Bureau of Consular Affairs, an electronic bulletin board accessible to the general public. Such bulletin board shall contain all information, updated daily, which is available on the Overseas Security Electronic Bulletin Board of the Bureau of Diplomatic Security.
