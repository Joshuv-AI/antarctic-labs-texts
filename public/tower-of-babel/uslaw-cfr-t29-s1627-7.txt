29 C.F.R. § 1627.7 — Transcriptions and reports.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1627.7
Creator: Equal Employment Opportunity Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1627.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1627 (Records To Be Made Or Kept Relating To Age: Notices To Be Posted). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Every person required to maintain records under the Act shall make such extension, recomputation or transcriptions of his records and shall submit such reports concerning actions taken and limitations and classifications of individuals set forth in records as the Commission or its authorized representative may request in writing.
