14 C.F.R. § 101.19 — Rapid deflation device.
Citation: us:cfr:14:101.19
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-101.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 101 (Moored Balloons, Kites, Amateur Rockets, And Unmanned Free Balloons). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person may operate a moored balloon unless it has a device that will automatically and rapidly deflate the balloon if it escapes from its moorings. If the device does not function properly, the operator shall immediately notify the nearest ATC facility of the location and time of the escape and the estimated flight path of the balloon.
