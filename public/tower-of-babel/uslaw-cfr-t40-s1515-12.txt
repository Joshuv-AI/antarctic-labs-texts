40 C.F.R. § 1515.12 — Will CEQ keep my request confidential?
Citation: us:cfr:40:1515.12
Creator: Council On Environmental Quality
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1515.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1515 (Freedom Of Information Act Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No. CEQ generally will not keep your request confidential. A FOIA request, including the requester's identity, is generally a matter of public record. CEQ publishes logs of requests and requesters at https://www.whitehouse.gov/ceq/foia/.
