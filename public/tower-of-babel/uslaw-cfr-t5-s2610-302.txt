5 C.F.R. § 2610.302 — Filing and service of documents.
Citation: us:cfr:5:2610.302
Creator: Office Of Government Ethics
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-2610.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 2610 (Implementation Of The Equal Access To Justice Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any application for an award or other pleading or document related to an application shall be filed and served on all parties to the proceeding in the same manner as other pleadings in the proceeding, except as provided in § 2610.202(b) for confidential financial information.
