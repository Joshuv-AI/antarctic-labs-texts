14 C.F.R. § 1259.502 — Application procedures.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1259 (National Space Grant College And Fellowship Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) All applicants for designation as Space Grant colleges and consortia shall apply for Space Grant fellowships.
(b) Applicants for Space Grant program or project grants (under § 1259.200) and for national needs grants (under § 1259.300) may also apply for Space Grant fellowships.
(c) There shall be a merit review selection for Space Grant fellowship awards.
