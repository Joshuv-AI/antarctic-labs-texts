10 U.S.C. § 12552 — Funeral honors functions at funerals for veterans
Citation: us:usc:10:12552
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section12552
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel Generally); Chapter 1215 (Miscellaneous Prohibitions And Penalties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Performance by a Reserve of funeral honors functions at the funeral of a veteran (as defined in section 1491(h) of this title) may not be considered to be a period of drill or training, but may be performed as funeral honors duty under section 12503 of this title.
