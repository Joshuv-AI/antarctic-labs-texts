12 C.F.R. § 223.55 — What are the standards under which the Board may grant exemptions from the requirements of section 23B?
Citation: us:cfr:12:223.55
Creator: Federal Reserve System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-223.55
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 223 (Transactions Between Member Banks And Their Affiliates (Regulation W)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board may prescribe regulations to exempt transactions or relationships from the requirements of section 23B and subpart F of this part if it finds such exemptions to be in the public interest and consistent with the purposes of section 23B.
