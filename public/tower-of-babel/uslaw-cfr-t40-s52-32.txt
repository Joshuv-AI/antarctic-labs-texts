40 C.F.R. § 52.32 — Sanctions following findings of SIP inadequacy.
Citation: us:cfr:40:52.32
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-52.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 52 (Approval And Promulgation Of Implementation Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For purposes of the SIP revisions required by § 51.120, EPA may make a finding under section 179(a) (1)-(4) of the Clean Air Act, 42 U.S.C. 7509(a) (1)-(4), starting the sanctions process set forth in section 179(a) of the Clean Air Act. Any such finding will be deemed a finding under § 52.31(c) and sanctions will be imposed in accordance with the order of sanctions and the terms for such sanctions established in § 52.31.
