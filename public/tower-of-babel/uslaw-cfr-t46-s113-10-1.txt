46 C.F.R. § 113.10-1 — -1 Approved equipment.
Citation: us:cfr:46:113.10-1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-113.10-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 113 (Communication And Alarm Systems And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each alarm annunciator, fire detector, test station, manual station, and vibrating bell must be approved under Subpart 161.002 of this chapter and meet the requirements of this subpart.
