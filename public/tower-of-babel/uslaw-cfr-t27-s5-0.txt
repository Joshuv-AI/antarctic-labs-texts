27 C.F.R. § 5.0 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:27:5.0
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-5.0
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 5 (Labeling And Advertising Of Distilled Spirits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part sets forth requirements that apply to the labeling and packaging of distilled spirits in containers, including requirements for label approval and rules regarding mandatory, regulated, and prohibited labeling statements. This part also sets forth requirements that apply to the advertising of distilled spirits.
