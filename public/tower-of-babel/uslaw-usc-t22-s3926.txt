22 U.S.C. § 3926 — Regulations; delegation of functions
Citation: us:usc:22:3926
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section3926
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 52 (Foreign Service); Subchapter II (Management Of Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Secretary may prescribe such regulations as the Secretary deems appropriate to carry out functions under this chapter.
(b)
The Secretary may delegate functions under this chapter which are vested in the Secretary to any employee of the Department or any member of the Service.
