14 C.F.R. § 302.415 — Hearing.
Citation: us:cfr:14:302.415
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-302.415
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 302 (Rules Of Practice In Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After the issues have been formulated, whether by the pleadings or otherwise, the administrative law judge shall give the parties reasonable written notice of the time and place of the hearings. Except as may be modified by the provisions of this subpart, the procedures in §§ 302.17 to 302.38 governing the conduct of oral evidentiary hearings will apply.
