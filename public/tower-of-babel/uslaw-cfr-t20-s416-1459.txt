20 C.F.R. § 416.1459 — Effect of dismissal of a request for a hearing before an administrative law judge.
Citation: us:cfr:20:416.1459
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-416.1459
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 416 (Supplemental Security Income For The Aged, Blind, And Disabled). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The dismissal of a request for a hearing is binding, unless it is vacated by an administrative law judge or the Appeals Council.
