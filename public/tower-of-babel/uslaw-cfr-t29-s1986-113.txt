29 C.F.R. § 1986.113 — Judicial enforcement.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1986.113
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1986.113
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1986 (Procedures For The Handling Of Retaliation Complaints Under The Employee Protection Provision Of The Seaman'S Protection Act (Spa), As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Whenever any person has failed to comply with a preliminary order of reinstatement or a final order, including one approving a settlement agreement issued under SPA, the Secretary may file a civil action seeking enforcement of the order in the United States district court for the district in which the violation was found to have occurred.
