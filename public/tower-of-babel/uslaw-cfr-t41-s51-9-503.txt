41 C.F.R. § 51-9.503 — -9.503 Effective date of new systems of records or alteration of an existing system of records.
Citation: us:cfr:41:51-9.503
Creator: Chapter 51—Committee For Purchase From People Who Are Blind Or Severely Disabled
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-51-9.503
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 51-9 (9—Privacy Act Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Systems of records proposed to be established or altered in accordance with the provision of the subpart shall be effective no sooner than 30 days from the publication of notice required by § 51-9.502.
