25 C.F.R. § 1000.685 — What funds are used to carry out inherent Federal functions?
Citation: us:cfr:25:1000.685
Creator: Office Of The Assistant Secretary, Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-1000.685
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 1000 (Annual Funding Agreements Under The Tribal Self-Government Act Amendments To The Indian Self-Determination And Education Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The funds for BIA to carry out inherent Federal functions are the funds to support functions that may not legally be delegated to an Indian Tribe if all Tribes were to assume responsibilities for all BIA programs that the Act permits.
