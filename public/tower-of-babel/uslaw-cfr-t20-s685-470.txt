20 C.F.R. § 685.470 — How can grantees request a waiver?
Citation: us:cfr:20:685.470
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-685.470
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 685 (National Farmworker Jobs Program Under Title I Of The Workforce Innovation And Opportunity Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To request a waiver, a grantee must submit to the Department a waiver plan that:
(a) Describes the goals of the waiver, the expected programmatic outcomes, and how the waiver will improve the provision of program activities;
(b) Is consistent with any guidelines the Department establishes;
(c) Describes the data that will be collected to track the impact of the waiver; and
(d) Includes a modified program plan reflecting the effect of the requested waiver.
