21 C.F.R. § 872.3690 — Tooth shade resin material.
Citation: us:cfr:21:872.3690
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-872.3690
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 872 (Dental Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. Tooth shade resin material is a device composed of materials such as bisphenol-A glycidyl methacrylate (Bis-GMA) intended to restore carious lesions or structural defects in teeth.
(b) Classification. Class II.
