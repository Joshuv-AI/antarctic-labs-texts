12 U.S.C. § 393 — Federal reserve banks as depositaries for Farm Credit System
Citation: us:usc:12:393
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section393
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 3 (Federal Reserve System); Subchapter XI (Depositaries And Fiscal Agents). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Federal Reserve banks are authorized to act as depositaries for and fiscal agents of any Federal land bank, Federal intermediate credit bank, bank for cooperatives, or other institutions of the Farm Credit System.
