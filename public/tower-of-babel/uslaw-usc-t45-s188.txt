45 U.S.C. § 188 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:45:188
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2024
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title45-section188
Context: United States Code, Title 45 (Railroads); Chapter 8 (Railway Labor); Subchapter II (Carriers By Air). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary for expenditure by the Mediation Board in carrying out the provisions of this chapter.
