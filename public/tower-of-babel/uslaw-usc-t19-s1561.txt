19 U.S.C. § 1561 — Public stores
Citation: us:usc:19:1561
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section1561
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 4 (Tariff Act Of 1930); Part IV (Transportation In Bond And Warehousing Of Merchandise). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any premises owned or leased by the Government and used for the storage of merchandise for the final release of which from customs custody a permit has not been issued shall be known as a “public store.”
