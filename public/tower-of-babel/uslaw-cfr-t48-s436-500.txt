48 C.F.R. § 436.500 — 436.500 Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:48:436.500
Creator: Chapter 4—Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-436.500
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 436 (Construction And Architect-Engineer Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes clauses for insertion in USDA solicitations and contracts for construction and for dismantling, demolition, or removal of improvements or structures. The contracting officer shall use the clauses as prescribed in contracts that exceed the simplified acquisition threshold. The contracting officer may use the clauses if the contract amount is expected to be at or below the simplified acquisition threshold.
