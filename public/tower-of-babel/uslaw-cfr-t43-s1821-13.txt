43 C.F.R. § 1821.13 — What if the specific program regulations conflict with these regulations?
Citation: us:cfr:43:1821.13
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-1821.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 1820 (Application Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If there is a conflict, the specific program regulations will govern and the conflicting portion of these regulations will not apply.
