39 C.F.R. § 960.8 — Official authorized to take final action under the Act.
Citation: us:cfr:39:960.8
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-960.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 960 (Equal Access To Justice Act In Postal Service Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Postal Service official who renders the final agency decision in a proceeding under § 952.26 or § 953.15, or the panel that renders the decision in an appeal before the Board of Contract Appeals under part 955 procedures, as the case may be, is authorized to take final action on matters pertaining to the Equal Access to Justice Act as applied to the proceeding.
