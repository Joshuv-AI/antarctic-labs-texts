Maker-Breaker total domination number
Athira Divakaran, Tijo James, Sandi Klavžar, Latha S Nair

Abstract
The Maker-Breaker total domination number, γ MBT ​ ( G ) \gamma_{\rm MBT}(G) , of a graph G G is introduced as the minimum number of moves of Dominator to win the Maker-Breaker total domination game, provided that he has a winning strategy and is the first to play. The Staller-start Maker-Breaker total domination number, γ MBT ′ ​ ( G ) \gamma_{\rm MBT}^{\prime}(G) , is defined analogously for the game in which Staller starts. Upper and lower bounds on γ MBT ​ ( G ) \gamma_{\rm MBT}(G) and on γ MBT ′ ​ ( G ) \gamma_{\rm MBT}^{\prime}(G) are provided and demonstrated to be sharp. It is proved that for any pair of integers ( k , ℓ ) (k,\ell) with 2 ≤ k ≤ ℓ 2\leq k\leq\ell , (i) there exists a connected graph G G with γ MB ​ ( G ) = k \gamma_{\rm MB}(G)=k and γ MBT ​ ( G ) = ℓ \gamma_{\rm MBT}(G)=\ell , (ii) there exists a connected graph G ′ G^{\prime} with γ MB ′ ​ ( G ′ ) = k \gamma_{\rm MB}^{\prime}(G^{\prime})=k and γ MBT ′ ​ ( G ′ ) = ℓ \gamma_{\rm MBT}^{\prime}(G^{\prime})=\ell , and (iii) there there exists a connected graph G ′′ G^{\prime\prime} with γ MBT ​ ( G ′′ ) = k \gamma_{\rm MBT}(G^{\prime\prime})=k and γ MBT ′ ​ ( G ′′ ) = ℓ \gamma_{\rm MBT}^{\prime}(G^{\prime\prime})=\ell . Here, γ MB \gamma_{\rm MB} and γ MB ′ \gamma_{\rm MB}^{\prime} are corresponding invariants for the Maker-Breaker domination game.

Introduction

Maker-Breaker game is a two-player game played on an arbitrary hypergraph by Maker and Breaker. The game belongs to the larger family of Maker-Breaker positional games introduced by Hales and Jewett
[
16
]
, and later by Erdős and Selfridge
[
10
]
. The game has been the subject of extensive study, both in general settings and specific cases, as detailed in the book
[
17
]
. See also the recent paper
[
23
]
and references therein.

Duchêne, Gledel, Parreau, and Renault
[
9
]
introduced a variation of the Maker-Breaker game called the
Maker-Breaker domination game
(or
MBD game
for short). The
Maker-Breaker domination game
is played on a graph
G=(V(G),E(G))
by two players named Dominator and Staller. These names align with the terminology used in the well-studied domination game
[
2
,
3
]
. The players alternately select unplayed vertices of
G
. Dominator’s goal is to occupy all vertices of some dominating set of
G
, while Staller prevents it from happening. Recent research on the game includes
[
1
,
6
]
.

The total domination game from
[
18
]
has received almost as much attention as the domination game, see for instance
[
20
,
21
,
24
]
. We would particularly like to highlight that recently Portier and Versteegen
[
22
]
proved the
3/4
-conjecture posed in
[
19
]
. Motivated by the total domination game, the
Maker-Breaker total domination game
(
MBTD game
for short) was introduced in
[
14
]
. The game is played just like the Maker-Breaker domination game, except that Dominator aims to occupy all the vertices of a total dominating set of
G
. In
[
14
]
, the outcome of the MBTD game was determined for cacti, and for Cartesian products of paths and cycles, while in
[
12
]
the focus was on cubic graphs. Let us also mention here a related game domination subdivision number studied by Favaron, Karami, and Sheikholeslami
[
11
]
.

A D-game is a MBD game or a MBTD in which Dominator takes the first turn, otherwise we speak of an
S-game
. There are four graph invariants associated with the MBD game
[
4
,
15
]
. The
Maker-Breaker domination number
,
\gamma_{\rm MB}(G)
, represents the minimum number of moves required for Dominator to win the D-game on
G
, assuming an optimal play by both players. Similarly, the
Staller-Maker-Breaker domination number
,
\gamma_{\rm SMB}(G)
, represents the minimum number of moves required for Staller to win the D-game. The parameters
\gamma_{\rm MB}^{\prime}(G)
and
\gamma_{\rm SMB}^{\prime}(G)
are defined for the S-game. The study in
[
4
]
focuses on
\gamma_{\rm SMB}(G)
and
\gamma_{\rm SMB}^{\prime}(G)
, providing, among other results, exact formulas for
\gamma_{\rm SMB}(G)
when
G
is a path. In
[
5
]
, trees
T
with
\gamma_{\rm SMB}(T)=k
were characterized for every positive integer
k
, and exact formulas for
\gamma_{\rm SMB}(G)
and
\gamma_{\rm SMB}^{\prime}(G)
were derived for caterpillars. The main result of
[
13
]
determined
\gamma_{\rm MB}
and
\gamma_{\rm MB}^{\prime}
for Cartesian products of
K_{2}
and a path. Additionally, in
[
8
]
, the MBD game was further explored on Cartesian products of paths, stars, and complete bipartite graphs.

In this paper we introduce the Maker-Breaker total domination number
\gamma_{\rm MBT}(G)
and the the Staller-start Maker-Breaker total domination number
\gamma_{\rm MBT}^{\prime}(G)
of a graph
G
. In Section
2
, we provide upper and lower bounds on
\gamma_{\rm MBT}
and on
\gamma_{\rm MBT}^{\prime}
, and demonstrate their sharpness. Then, in Section
3
, we relate Maker-Breaker domination numbers with Maker-Breaker total domination numbers. We prove that for any pair of integers
(k,\ell)
with
2\leq k\leq\ell
, (i) there exists a connected graph
G
with
\gamma_{\rm MB}(G)=k
and
\gamma_{\rm MBT}(G)=\ell
, (ii) there exists a connected graph
G^{\prime}
with
\gamma_{\rm MB}^{\prime}(G^{\prime})=k
and
\gamma_{\rm MBT}^{\prime}(G^{\prime})=\ell
, and (iii) there there exists a connected graph
G^{\prime\prime}
with
\gamma_{\rm MBT}(G^{\prime\prime})=k
and
\gamma_{\rm MBT}^{\prime}(G^{\prime\prime})=\ell
. In the rest of the section, additional definitions and concepts needed are given.

For
k\in\mathbb{N}
we will use the notation
[k]=\{1,\ldots,k\}
. Let
G=(V(G),E(G))
be a graph. The order of
G
is represented as
n(G)
. A
dominating set
of a graph
G
is a subset
D\subseteq V(G)
such that every vertex in
V(G)\setminus D
has at least one neighbor in
D
, and
D
is a
total dominating set
if every vertex in
V(G)
has a neighbor in
D
. The
domination number

\gamma(G)
is the minimum cardinality of a dominating set in
G
, and the
total domination number

\gamma_{t}(G)
is the minimum cardinality of a total dominating set in
G
. A subset of pairs
\{(u_{1},v_{1}),\ldots,(u_{k},v_{k})\}
of vertices in
V(G)
is a
pairing total dominating set
if all the vertices are distinct and for any selection of vertices
x_{i}\in\{u_{i},v_{i}\}
,
i\in[k]
, the set
\{x_{1},\dots,x_{k}\}
is a total dominating set of
G
.

The
outcome

o(G)
of the MBTD game played on
G
is

•

\mathcal{D}
, if Dominator has a winning strategy no matter who starts the game;

•

\mathcal{S}
, if Staller has a winning strategy no matter who starts the game;

•

\mathcal{N}
, if the first player has a winning strategy.

In an optimal strategy of Dominator (resp. Staller) in the MBTD game it is never an advantage
for him (resp. for her) to skip a move. This fact is known as
No-Skip-Lemma

[
14
, Lemma 2.1]
.

The
Maker-Breaker total domination number
,
\gamma_{\rm MBT}(G)
, of a graph
G
is the minimum number of moves of Dominator to win the MBTD game, provided that he has a winning strategy and is the first to play. If Dominator has no winning strategy in the D-game, then set
\gamma_{\rm MBT}(G)=\infty
. The
Staller-start Maker-Breaker total domination number
,
\gamma_{\rm SMB}^{\prime}(G)
, is defined analogously for the S-game.

2
Bounds on the MBTD numbers

In this section we demonstrate that straightforward upper and lower bounds on
\gamma_{\rm MBT}
and on
\gamma_{\rm MBT}^{\prime}
are sharp. The result dealing with upper bounds reads as follows.

Theorem 2.1

Let G be a graph.

(i)

If
\gamma_{\rm MBT}(G)<\infty
, then
\gamma_{\rm MBT}(G)\leq\left\lceil\frac{n(G)}{2}\right\rceil
.

(ii)

If
\gamma_{\rm MBT}^{\prime}(G)<\infty
, then
\gamma_{\rm MBT}^{\prime}(G)\leq\left\lfloor\frac{n(G)}{2}\right\rfloor
.

Moreover, for any
k>1
, there is a connected graph
H
with
\gamma_{\rm MBT}(H)=k=\left\lceil\frac{n(H)}{2}\right\rceil
, and there is a connected graph
H^{\prime}
with
\gamma_{\rm MBT}^{\prime}(H^{\prime})=k=\left\lfloor\frac{n(H^{\prime})}{2}\right\rfloor
.

Proof.
Let
G
be a graph with
\gamma_{\rm MBT}(G)<\infty
. Since Dominator and Staller play alternately and Dominator starts the game, he can select at most
\left\lceil\frac{n(G)}{2}\right\rceil
vertices, hence
\gamma_{\rm MBT}(G)\leq\left\lceil\frac{n(G)}{2}\right\rceil
. Analogous argument applies to the inequality (ii).

To prove sharpness of the bounds we give four constructions based on the parity of

Realization of parameters

In this section we relate Maker-Breaker domination numbers with Maker-Breaker total domination numbers and demonstrate that in all non-trivial cases the difference can be arbitrarily large.

\gamma_{\rm MB}(G)=1
