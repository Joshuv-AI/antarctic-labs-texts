22 C.F.R. § 128.17 — Availability of orders.
Citation: us:cfr:22:128.17
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-128.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 128 (Administrative Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All charging letters, debarment orders, and orders imposing civil penalties and probationary periods are available for public inspection in the Public Reading Room of the Department of State.
