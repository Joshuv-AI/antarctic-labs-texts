14 U.S.C. § 906 — Air Station Cape Cod Improvements
Citation: us:usc:14:906
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section906
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 9 (Administration); Subchapter I (Real And Personal Property). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may expend funds for the repair, improvement, restoration, or replacement of those federally or nonfederally owned support buildings, including appurtenances, which are on leased or permitted real property constituting Coast Guard Air Station Cape Cod, located on Massachusetts Military Reservation, Cape Cod, Massachusetts.
