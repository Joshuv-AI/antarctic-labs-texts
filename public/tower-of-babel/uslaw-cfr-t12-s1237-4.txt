12 C.F.R. § 1237.4 — Receivership following conservatorship; administrative expenses.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1237.4
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1237.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1237 (Conservatorship And Receivership). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a receivership immediately succeeds a conservatorship, the administrative expenses of the conservatorship shall also be deemed to be administrative expenses of the subsequent receivership.
