32 C.F.R. § 1609.4 — Oath of office.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1609.4
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1609.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1609 (Uncompensated Personnel). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Every person who undertakes to render voluntary uncompensated service in the administration of the Selective Service Law shall execute an Oath of Office and Waiver of Pay before he enters upon his duties.
