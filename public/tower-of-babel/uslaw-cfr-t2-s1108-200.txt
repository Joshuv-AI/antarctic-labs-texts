2 C.F.R. § 1108.200 — Fixed-amount subaward.
Citation: us:cfr:2:1108.200
Creator: Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1108.200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1108 (Definitions Of Terms Used In Subchapters A Through F Of This Chapter). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Fixed-amount subaward means a subaward:
(a) That a recipient or subrecipient makes to another entity at the next lower tier; and
(b) Under which the total amount to be paid to the other entity is based on performance and results, and not on the actual, allowable costs that entity incurs.
