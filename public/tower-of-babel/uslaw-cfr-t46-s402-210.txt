46 C.F.R. § 402.210 — Requirements and qualifications for registration.
Citation: us:cfr:46:402.210
Creator: Coast Guard (Great Lakes Pilotage), Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-402.210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 402 (Great Lakes Pilotage Rules And Orders). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Pursuant to § 401.210(a)(4), each applicant for an original registration at the time of application and each Applicant Trainee, Apprentice Pilot, United States Registered Pilot, and Temporary Registered Pilot is required to pass a physical examination in accordance with subpart C of 46 CFR part 10.
