49 C.F.R. § 803.3 — Authority to affix Seal.
Citation: us:cfr:49:803.3
Creator: National Transportation Safety Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-803.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 803 (Official Seal). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Seal shall be in the custody and control of the Chief Human Capital Officer of the Board.
(b) The Chief Human Capital Officer may delegate and authorize redelegations of this authority.
