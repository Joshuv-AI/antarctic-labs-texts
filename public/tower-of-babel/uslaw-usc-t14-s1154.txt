14 U.S.C. § 1154 — Procurement of buoy chain
Citation: us:usc:14:1154
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section1154
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 11 (Acquisitions); Subchapter III (Procurement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Except as provided in subsection (b), the Coast Guard may not procure buoy chain—
(1)
that is not manufactured in the United States; or
(2)
substantially all of the components of which are not produced or manufactured in the United States.
(b)
The Coast Guard may procure buoy chain that is not manufactured in the United States if the Secretary determines that—
(1)
the price of buoy chain manufactured in the United States is unreasonable; or
(2)
emergency circumstances exist.
