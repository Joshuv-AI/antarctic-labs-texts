25 C.F.R. § 33.8 — Realignment of area and agency offices.
Citation: us:cfr:25:33.8
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-33.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 33 (Transfer Of Indian Education Functions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Assistant Secretary—Indian Affairs shall implement Bureau of Indian Affairs Area Office and Agency Office reorganizations required to structure these offices consistent with education program activities to be undertaken at those levels.
