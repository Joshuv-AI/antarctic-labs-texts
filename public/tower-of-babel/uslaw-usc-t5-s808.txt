5 U.S.C. § 808 — Effective date of certain rules
Citation: us:usc:5:808
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section808
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part I (The Agencies Generally); Chapter 8 (Congressional Review Of Agency Rulemaking). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding section 801—
(1)
any rule that establishes, modifies, opens, closes, or conducts a regulatory program for a commercial, recreational, or subsistence activity related to hunting, fishing, or camping, or
(2)
any rule which an agency for good cause finds (and incorporates the finding and a brief statement of reasons therefor in the rule issued) that notice and public procedure thereon are impracticable, unnecessary, or contrary to the public interest,
shall take effect at such time as the Federal agency promulgating the rule determines.
