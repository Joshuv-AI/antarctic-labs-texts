48 C.F.R. § 3053.227-70 — 3053.227-70 Conveyance of invention rights acquired by the Government.
Citation: us:cfr:48:3053.227-70
Creator: Chapter 30—Department Of Homeland Security, Homeland Security Acquisition Regulation (Hsar)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-3053.227-70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 3053 (Forms). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following form is prescribed for including a means for contractors to report inventions made in the course of contract performance, as specified in (HSAR) 48 CFR 3027.305-4: DD Form 882, Report of Inventions and Subcontracts.
