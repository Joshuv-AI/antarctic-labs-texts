40 C.F.R. § 720.122 — Inspections.
Citation: us:cfr:40:720.122
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-720.122
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 720 (Premanufacture Notification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

EPA will conduct inspections under section 11 of the Act to assure compliance with section 5 of the Act and this rule, to verify that information submitted to EPA under this rule is true and correct, and to audit data submitted to EPA under this rule.
