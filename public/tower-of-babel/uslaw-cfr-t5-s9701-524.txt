5 C.F.R. § 9701.524 — Compilation and publication of data.
Citation: us:cfr:5:9701.524
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security Human Resources Management System (Department Of Homeland Security—Office Of Personnel Management)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-9701.524
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 9701 (Department Of Homeland Security Human Resources Management System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The HSLRB must maintain a file of its proceedings and copies of all available agreements and arbitration decisions and publish the texts of its impasse resolution decisions and the actions taken under § 9701.519.
(b) All files maintained under paragraph (a) of this section must be open to inspection and reproduction in accordance with 5 U.S.C. 552 and 552a. The HSLRB will establish rules in consultation with the Department for maintaining and making available for inspection sensitive information.
