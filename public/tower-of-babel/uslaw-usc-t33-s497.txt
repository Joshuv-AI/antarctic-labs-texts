33 U.S.C. § 497 — “Persons” defined
Citation: us:usc:33:497
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section497
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 11 (Bridges Over Navigable Waters); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The word “persons” as used in sections 491 to 494 and 495 to 498 of this title, shall be construed to import both the singular and the plural, as the case demands, and shall include municipalities, quasi-municipal corporations, corporations, companies, and associations.
