15 C.F.R. § 791.7 — Public disclosure of records.
Citation: us:cfr:15:791.7
Creator: Bureau Of Industry And Security, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-791.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 791 (Securing The Information And Communications Technology And Services Supply Chain). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Public requests for agency records related to this part will be processed in accordance with the Department of Commerce's Freedom of Information Act regulations, 15 CFR part 4, or other applicable law and regulation.
