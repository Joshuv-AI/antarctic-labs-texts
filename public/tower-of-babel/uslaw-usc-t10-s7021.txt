10 U.S.C. § 7021 — Army Reserve Forces Policy Committee
Citation: us:usc:10:7021
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7021
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization); Chapter 703 (Department Of The Army). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is in the Office of the Secretary of the Army an Army Reserve Forces Policy Committee. The functions, membership, and organization of that committee are set forth in section 10302 of this title.
