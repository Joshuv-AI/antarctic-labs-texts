29 C.F.R. § 4041A.41 — A.41 General rule.
Citation: us:cfr:29:4041A.41
Creator: Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-4041A.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 4041A (Part 4041A—Termination Of Multiemployer Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a plan's assets, excluding any claim of the plan for unpaid withdrawal liability, are sufficient to satisfy all obligations for nonforfeitable benefits provided under the plan, the plan sponsor may close out the plan in accordance with this subpart by distributing plan assets in full satisfaction of all nonforfeitable benefits under the plan.
