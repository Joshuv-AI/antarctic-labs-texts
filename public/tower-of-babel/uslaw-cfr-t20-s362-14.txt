20 C.F.R. § 362.14 — Finality of settlement.
Citation: us:cfr:20:362.14
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-362.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 362 (Employees' Personal Property Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, settlement of a claim under the Act and this part is final and conclusive.
