5 C.F.R. § 831.2009 — Lump sum payments which include contributions made to a retirement system for employees of a nonappropriated fund instrumentality.
Citation: us:cfr:5:831.2009
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-831.2009
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 831 (Retirement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A lump sum payment will include employee contributions and interest as provided under subpart G of part 847 of this chapter.
