17 C.F.R. § 275.222-2 — -2 Definition of “client” for purposes of the national de minimis standard.
Citation: us:cfr:17:275.222-2
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-275.222-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 275 (Rules And Regulations, Investment Advisers Act Of 1940). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For purposes of section 222(d)(2) of the Act (15 U.S.C. 80b-18a(d)(2)), an investment adviser may rely upon the definition of “client” provided by § 275.202(a)(30)-1, without giving regard to paragraph (b)(4) of that section.
