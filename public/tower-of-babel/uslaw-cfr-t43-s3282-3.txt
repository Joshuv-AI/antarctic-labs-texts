43 C.F.R. § 3282.3 — When must the unit operator submit an application for BLM approval of a proposed initial participating area?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3282.3
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3282.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3280 (Geothermal Resources Unit Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The unit operator must submit an application for BLM approval of a proposed participating area no later than:
(a) 60 days after receiving BLM's determination identified in § 3281.15(a)(3) that a unit well will produce or utilize in commercial quantities; or
(b) 30 days before the initiation of commercial operations, whichever occurs earlier.
