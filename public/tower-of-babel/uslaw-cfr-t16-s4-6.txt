16 C.F.R. § 4.6 — Cooperation with other agencies.
Citation: us:cfr:16:4.6
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-4.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 4 (Miscellaneous Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It is the policy of the Commission to cooperate with other governmental agencies to avoid unnecessary overlapping or duplication of regulatory functions.
