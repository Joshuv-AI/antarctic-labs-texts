7 C.F.R. § 295.3 — Informational and educational publications.
Citation: us:cfr:7:295.3
Creator: Food And Nutrition Administration, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-295.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 295 (Availability Of Information And Records To The Public). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

FNS publishes a wide variety of informational and educational periodicals, pamphlets, brochures, leaflets, guides, and educational aids explaining the operation of FNS food assistance programs. For more information concerning FNS publications and how to obtain them, write the Director, Public Information Staff, Food and Nutrition Service, USDA, 3101 Park Center Drive, Alexandria, VA 22302-1500.
