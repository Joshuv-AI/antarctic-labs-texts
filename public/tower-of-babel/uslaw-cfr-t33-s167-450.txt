33 C.F.R. § 167.450 — In the Santa Barbara Channel Traffic Separation Scheme: General.
Citation: us:cfr:33:167.450
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-167.450
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 167 (Offshore Traffic Separation Schemes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Traffic Separation Scheme in the Santa Barbara Channel is described in §§ 167.451 and 167.452. The geographic coordinates in §§ 167.451 and 167.452 are defined using North American Datum 1983 (NAD 83).
