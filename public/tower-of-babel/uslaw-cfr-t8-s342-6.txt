8 C.F.R. § 342.6 — Depositions.
Citation: us:cfr:8:342.6
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-342.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 342 (Administrative Cancellation Of Certificates, Documents, Or Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon good cause shown, the testimony of any witness may be taken by depositions, either orally or upon written interrogatories before a person having authority to administer oaths (affirmations), as may be designated by the naturalization examiner.
