15 U.S.C. § 1677 — Effect on State laws
Citation: us:usc:15:1677
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1677
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 41 (Consumer Credit Protection); Subchapter II (Restrictions On Garnishment). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This subchapter does not annul, alter, or affect, or exempt any person from complying with, the laws of any State
(1)
prohibiting garnishments or providing for more limited garnishment than are allowed under this subchapter, or
(2)
prohibiting the discharge of any employee by reason of the fact that his earnings have been subjected to garnishment for more than one indebtedness.
