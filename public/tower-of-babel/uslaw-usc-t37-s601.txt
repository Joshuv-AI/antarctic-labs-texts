37 U.S.C. § 601 — Applicability
Citation: us:usc:37:601
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title37-section601
Context: United States Code, Title 37 (Pay And Allowances Of The Uniformed Services); Chapter 11 (Payments To Mentally Incompetent Persons). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This chapter applies to—
(1)
members of a uniformed service who are on active duty (other than for training) or who are on a retired list of that service; and
(2)
members of the Fleet Reserve or Fleet Marine Corps Reserve.
