14 C.F.R. § 25.1149 — Propeller speed and pitch controls.
Citation: us:cfr:14:25.1149
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-25.1149
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 25 (Airworthiness Standards: Transport Category Airplanes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) There must be a separate propeller speed and pitch control for each propeller.
(b) The controls must be grouped and arranged to allow—
(1) Separate control of each propeller; and
(2) Simultaneous control of all propellers.
(c) The controls must allow synchronization of all propellers.
(d) The propeller speed and pitch controls must be to the right of, and at least one inch below, the pilot's throttle controls.
