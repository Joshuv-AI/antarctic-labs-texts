33 C.F.R. § 158.176 — Effect of suspension of a Certificate of Adequacy.
Citation: us:cfr:33:158.176
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-158.176
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 158 (Reception Facilities For Oil, Noxious Liquid Substances, And Garbage). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After the COTP notifies the person in charge and places a suspension order in effect, the COTP denies entry of ships to the port or terminal while the Certificate of Adequacy is suspended.
