10 C.F.R. § 1016.2 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:10:1016.2
Creator: Department Of Energy (General Provisions)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1016.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1016 (Safeguarding Of Restricted Data By Access Permittees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part apply to all persons who may require access to Retricted Data used, processed, stored, reproduced, transmitted, or handled in connection with an access permit.
