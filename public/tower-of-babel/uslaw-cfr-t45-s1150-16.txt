45 C.F.R. § 1150.16 — May I use the Endowment's failure to comply with these regulations as a defense?
Citation: us:cfr:45:1150.16
Creator: National Foundation On The Arts And The Humanities
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1150.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1150 (Collection Of Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No. The failure of the Endowment to comply with any standard prescribed in the Federal Claims Collection Standards or these regulations shall not be available to any debtor as a defense.
