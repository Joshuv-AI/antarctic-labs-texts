25 C.F.R. § 522.12 — Individually owned class II gaming operations operating on September 1, 1986.
Citation: us:cfr:25:522.12
Creator: National Indian Gaming Commission, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-522.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 522 (Submission Of Gaming Ordinance Or Resolution). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For licensing of individually owned gaming operations operating on September 1, 1986, under § 502.3(e) of this chapter, a tribal ordinance shall contain the same requirements as those in § 522.11(a) through (d).
