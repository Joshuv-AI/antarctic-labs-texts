43 C.F.R. § 3400.6 — Minimum comment period.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3400.6
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3400.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3400 (Coal Management: General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless otherwise required in Group 3400 of this title, a minimum period of 30 days shall be allowed for public review and comment where such review is required for Federal coal management program activities under Group 3400 of this title.
