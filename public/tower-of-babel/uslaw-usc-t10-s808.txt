10 U.S.C. § 808 — Art. 8. Apprehension of deserters
Citation: us:usc:10:808
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section808
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 47 (Uniform Code Of Military Justice); Subchapter II (Apprehension And Restraint). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any civil officer having authority to apprehend offenders under the laws of the United States or of a State, Commonwealth, possession, or the District of Columbia may summarily apprehend a deserter from the armed forces and deliver him into the custody of those forces.
