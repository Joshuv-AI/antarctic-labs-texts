36 U.S.C. § 80306 — Restrictions
Citation: us:usc:36:80306
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section80306
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 803 (Girl Scouts Of The United States Of America). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Profit.—
The corporation may not operate for profit.
(b) Political Activities.—
The corporation shall be nonpolitical and nonsectarian.
