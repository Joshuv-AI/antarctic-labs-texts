Principles governing agency collection procedures for reporting debts to the Government Accountability Office (GAO) or the Department of Justice are found in 31 CFR chapter IX and in the GAO Policy and Procedures Manual for Guidance of Federal Agencies (https://www.gao.gov/products/149099).
