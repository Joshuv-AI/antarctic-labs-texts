29 C.F.R. § 552.103 — Babysitting services in general.
Citation: us:cfr:29:552.103
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-552.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 552 (Application Of The Fair Labor Standards Act To Domestic Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term “babysitting services” is defined in § 552.4. Babysitting is a form of domestic service, and babysitters other than those working on a casual basis are entitled to the same benefits under the Act as other domestic service employees.
