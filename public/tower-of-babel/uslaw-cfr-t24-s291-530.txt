24 C.F.R. § 291.530 — Eligible firefighter/emergency medical technicians.
Citation: us:cfr:24:291.530
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-291.530
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 291 (Disposition Of Hud-Acquired And -Owned Single Family Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A person qualifies as a firefighter/emergency medical technician for the purposes of the GNND Sales Program if the person is:
(a) Employed full-time as a firefighter or emergency medical technician by a fire department or emergency medical services responder unit of the Federal Government, a State, unit of general local government, or an Indian tribal government; and
(b) The full-time employment in paragraph (a) of this section must, in the normal course of business, directly serve the locality where the home is located.
