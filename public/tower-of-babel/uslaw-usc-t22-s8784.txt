22 U.S.C. § 8784 — Rule of construction with respect to use of force against Iran and Syria
Citation: us:usc:22:8784
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section8784
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 94 (Iran Threat Reduction And Syria Human Rights); Subchapter VI (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this Act or the amendments made by this Act shall be construed as a declaration of war or an authorization of the use of force against Iran or Syria.
