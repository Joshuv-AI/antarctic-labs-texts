44 C.F.R. § 150.10 — Date of submission of nominations.
Citation: us:cfr:44:150.10
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-150.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 150 (Public Safety Awards To Public Safety Officers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nominations may only be submitted for acts, services, or contributions occurring within two years preceding the November 15 cut-off date described in § 150.3(c) of this part. However, nominations submitted prior to the February 28, 1985 cut-off date may be made for acts, services or contributions occurring on or after October 29, 1972 (two years before the effective date of the Act).
