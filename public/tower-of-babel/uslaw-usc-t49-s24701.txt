49 U.S.C. § 24701 — National rail passenger transportation system
Citation: us:usc:49:24701
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section24701
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part C (Passenger Transportation); Chapter 247 (Amtrak Route System). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Amtrak shall operate a national rail passenger transportation system which ties together existing and emergent regional rail passenger service and other intermodal passenger service.
