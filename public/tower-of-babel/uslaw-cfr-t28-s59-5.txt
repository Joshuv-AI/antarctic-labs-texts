28 C.F.R. § 59.5 — Functions and authorities of the Deputy Assistant Attorneys General.
Citation: us:cfr:28:59.5
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-59.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 59 (Guidelines On Methods Of Obtaining Documentary Materials Held By Third Parties). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The functions and authorities of the Deputy Assistant Attorneys General set out in this part may at any time be exercised by an Assistant Attorney General, the Associate Attorney General, the Deputy Attorney General, or the Attorney General.
