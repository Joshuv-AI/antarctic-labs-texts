33 C.F.R. § 149.410 — Location and number of fire extinguishers required for manned deepwater ports constructed before August 22, 2016.
Citation: us:cfr:33:149.410
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-149.410
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 149 (Deepwater Ports: Design, Construction, And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Manned deepwater ports constructed before August 22, 2016, must meet the following requirements:
(a) Previously installed extinguishers with extinguishing capacities smaller than what is required in table 149.409 of this subpart need not be replaced and may be continued in service so long as they are maintained in good condition to the satisfaction of the Officer in Charge, Marine Inspection.
(b) All new equipment and installations must meet the applicable requirements in this subpart for new vessels.
