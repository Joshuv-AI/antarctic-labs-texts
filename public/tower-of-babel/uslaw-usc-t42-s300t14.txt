42 U.S.C. § 300t–14 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:42:300t–14
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section300t–14
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter XIV (Health Resources Development); Part E (Program To Assist And Encourage Voluntary Discontinuance Of Unneeded Hospital Services And Conversion Of Unneeded Hospital Services To Other Health Services Needed By Community). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

To make payments under grants under sections 300t–12 and 300t–13 of this title there are authorized to be appropriated $30,000,000 for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1980, $50,000,000 for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1981, and $75,000,000 for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1982, except that in any fiscal year not more than 10 percent of the amount appropriated under this section may be obligated for grants under section 300t–13 of this title.
