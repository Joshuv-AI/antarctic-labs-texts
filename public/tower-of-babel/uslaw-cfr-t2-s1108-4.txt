2 C.F.R. § 1108.4 — Definitions of terms that vary depending on context.
Citation: us:cfr:2:1108.4
Creator: Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1108.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1108 (Definitions Of Terms Used In Subchapters A Through F Of This Chapter). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

DoDGARs definitions of some terms related to types of legal instruments (e.g., “contract”) and purposes for which they are used (e.g., “procurement” or “acquisition”) may vary, depending on the context. Appendix A to this part provides additional information about those terms and their definitions.
