5 C.F.R. § 847.441 — Election requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:5:847.441
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-847.441
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 847 (Elections Of Retirement Coverage By Current And Former Employees Of Nonappropriated Fund Instrumentalities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An employee who completed a qualifying move under § 847.402(b) may elect to be covered by a NAFI retirement system for all Federal service following the qualifying move.
(b) A survivor eligible for benefits under FERS may make an election under this section if the employee was otherwise eligible to make an election, but died before expiration of the time limit under § 847.304.
