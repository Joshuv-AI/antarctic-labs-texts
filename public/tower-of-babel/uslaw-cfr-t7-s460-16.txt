7 C.F.R. § 460.16 — Eligibility.
Citation: us:cfr:7:460.16
Creator: Federal Crop Insurance Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-460.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 460 (Disaster And Other Additional Payments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To be eligible for additional payment under ADD PAY II, the participant must be an AIP who administered one or more eligible crop insurance contracts for specialty crops for the 2022 or 2023 reinsurance years.
