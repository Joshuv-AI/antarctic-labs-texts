16 U.S.C. § 4262 — Purposes
Citation: us:usc:16:4262
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section4262
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 62A (Asian Elephant Conservation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purposes of this chapter are the following:
(1)
To perpetuate healthy populations of Asian elephants.
(2)
To assist in the conservation and protection of Asian elephants by supporting the conservation programs of Asian elephant range states and the CITES Secretariat.
(3)
To provide financial resources for those programs.
