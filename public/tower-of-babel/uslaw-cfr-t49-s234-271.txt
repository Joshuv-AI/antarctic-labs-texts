49 C.F.R. § 234.271 — Insulated rail joints, bond wires, and track connections.
Citation: us:cfr:49:234.271
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-234.271
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 234 (Grade Crossing Safety). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Insulated rail joints, bond wires, and track connections shall be inspected at least once every three months.
