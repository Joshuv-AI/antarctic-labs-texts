12 C.F.R. § 605.500 — Policy.
Citation: us:cfr:12:605.500
Creator: Farm Credit Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-605.500
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 605 (Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It is the policy of the Farm Credit Administration to act in matters relating to national security information in accordance with Executive Order 13292 and directives issued thereunder by the Information Security Oversight Office (ISOO).
