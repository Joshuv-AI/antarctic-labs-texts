12 U.S.C. § 2 — Comptroller of the Currency; appointment; term
Citation: us:usc:12:2
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section2
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 1 (The Comptroller Of The Currency). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Comptroller of the Currency shall be appointed by the President, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, and shall hold his office for a term of five years unless sooner removed by the President, upon reasons to be communicated by him to the Senate.
