Sensitivity of the redshifted 21 cm signal from the Dark Ages to parameters of primordial magnetic fields
B. Novosyadlyj, Yu. Kulinich, N. Fortuna, A. Rudakovskyi

Abstract
We analyze the impact of the decaying magnetic turbulence of primordial magnetic fields (PMFs) and ambipolar diffusion on the ionization and thermal history of the Dark Ages Universe ( 30 ≤ z ≤ 300 30\leq z\leq 300 ), and its imprint on the spectral profile of the global signal in the 21 cm hydrogen line. The heating function caused by decaying magnetic turbulence monotonically decreases after cosmological recombination, its amplitude strongly depends on the strength of the PMFs B 0 B_{0} and weakly depends on the spectral index of the initial power spectrum of PMFs n B n_{\rm B} . The heating function caused by ambipolar diffusion, in contrast, noticeably depends on the spectral index in the range − 3 ≲ n B ≲ − 1 -3\lesssim n_{\rm B}\lesssim-1 , but is subdominant in the Dark Ages epoch for PMF models with B 0 ≲ 0.5 B_{0}\lesssim 0.5 nG. We computed the ionization and thermal history of intergalactic gas from the cosmological recombination up to the end of the Dark Ages epoch for a range of PMF parameters 0.05 ≲ B 0 ≲ 0.5 0.05\lesssim B_{0}\lesssim 0.5 nG, − 2.9 ≲ n B ≲ 4 -2.9\lesssim n_{\rm B}\lesssim 4 and show the essentially distinguished thermal evolution from one in the Λ \Lambda CDM model. We also show that the profile of the redshifted 21 cm hydrogen line is very sensitive to the PMF parameters from this range and can be used for their constraints in conjunction with other observational data.

Comparison of results for PMFs models is not so unambiguous due to the authors’ use of different approximations in calculations of decaying turbulence and ambipolar diffusion, the cut-off scale of magnetic field at the time of recombination, taking into account its changes over time during the Dark Ages, etc. In the paper, we have described completely the PMFs model and all the parameters and approximations used in the calculations. 5
Conclusions

We analyzed the impact of primordial magnetic fields on the thermal history of the Universe in the Dark Ages due to the decaying magnetic turbulence and ambipolar diffusion in the low-ionized plasma, and showed that fields with
B_{0}\geq 0.05
nG reduce the depth of the 21 cm absorption line of the standard
\Lambda
CDM model, in which the brightness temperature in line is
\delta T_{\rm br}\approx-40
mK at frequency
\approx 16
MHz and the effective width
\approx 25
MHz. At
B_{0}\sim 0.25-0.3
nG, the absorption line becomes emission line in the low-frequency wing of the absorption line. In models with
n_{\rm B}=-2.9
and
0.05\leq B_{0}\leq 0.5
nG, the amplitude of the absorption-emission line ranges from
-40
to
+26
mK in the frequency range of
16-8
MHz, the width at half-maximum
25-20
MHz.
Thus, the global signal in the 21 cm line from Dark Ages is sensitive tPMFs with nearly scale-invariant power spectrum
n_{\rm B}=-2.9
when
B_{0}\gtrsim 0.05
nG.

We have found a sensitivity of the amplitude and profile shape of the 21 cm hydrogen spectral line formed during the Dark Ages to the value of the spectral index
n_{\rm B}
at fixed values of the field strength
B_{0}
. As
n_{\rm B}
increases from -2.9 to -2.0 in the case of PMFs with
B_{0}=0.1
nG, the absorption line becomes shallower from -35 to -32 mK at the same frequency
\sim 16
MHz. In the case of PMFs with
B_{0}=0.5
nG, the amplitude of the emission line increases from 26 to 47 mK.

Comparison with other gas heating mechanisms in models outside the standard
\Lambda
CDM model revealed similarities in profiles with the decaying dark matter model, indicating the need to use other signals from the early Universe to resolve this degeneracy.

Damping scale of PMFs at recombination epoch

The damping scale of PMFs at recombination epoch is defined in
Subramanian & Barrow 1998
as

\lambda_{D}=2\pi\sqrt{\frac{3}{5}}\frac{V_{\rm A}}{c}\lambda^{\gamma}_{\rm D}.

(16)

where the magnetohydrodynamic Alfv́en velocity in units of light one at this epoch according to
Kunze & Komatsu 2014
equals

\frac{V_{\rm A}}{c}=3.24\cdot 10^{-4}\left(\frac{B_{\lambda}}{\rm 1\,nG}\right)\left(\frac{k_{\rm D}}{k_{\lambda}}\right)^{\frac{n_{\rm B+3}}{2}}h^{-1},

(17)

and baryon-photon Silk damping scale
(
Silk 1968
;
Peebles & Yu 1970
;
Kaiser 1983
)
:

\lambda^{\gamma}_{\rm D}=5.7\omega_{m}^{-\frac{1}{4}}\omega_{b}^{-\frac{1}{2}}\left(\frac{x_{e}^{rec}}{0.1}\right)^{-\frac{1}{2}}\left(\frac{1+z_{\rm rec}}{1100}\right)^{-\frac{5}{4}}\,{\rm Mpc}.

(18)

This approximation well agree with approximation proposed in
Hu & White 1997
. So,

\displaystyle\frac{1}{k_{\rm D}}\equiv\frac{\lambda_{\rm D}}{2\pi}=2.04\cdot 10^{-3}\omega_{m}^{-\frac{1}{4}}\omega_{b}^{-\frac{1}{2}}\left(\frac{x_{e}^{rec}}{0.1}\right)^{-\frac{1}{2}}

\displaystyle\times\left(\frac{1+z_{\rm rec}}{1100}\right)^{-\frac{5}{4}}\left(\frac{B_{\lambda}}{\rm nG}\right)\left(\frac{k_{\rm D}}{k_{\lambda}}\right)^{\frac{n_{\rm B}+3}{2}}\frac{0.7}{h}\,{\rm Mpc}.

(19)

From this equation we obtain the damping scale of PMFs (
7
) at the epoch of cosmological recombination.
