14 C.F.R. § 25.751 — Main float buoyancy.
Citation: us:cfr:14:25.751
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-25.751
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 25 (Airworthiness Standards: Transport Category Airplanes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each main float must have—
(a) A buoyancy of 80 percent in excess of that required to support the maximum weight of the seaplane or amphibian in fresh water; and
(b) Not less than five watertight compartments approximately equal in volume.
