22 C.F.R. § 92.3 — Consular districts.
Citation: us:cfr:22:92.3
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-92.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 92 (Notarial And Related Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where consular districts have been established, the geographic limits of the district determine the area in which notarial acts can be performed by the notarizing officer. See § 92.41 (b) regarding authentication of the seals and signatures of foreign officials outside the consular district.
