42 U.S.C. § 2386 — Transfer of installations without charge
Citation: us:usc:42:2386
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2386
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 24 (Disposal Of Atomic Energy Communities); Subchapter VII (Municipalities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The transfer of any municipal installation authorized to be made under the provisions of this subchapter may be made without charge to the entity receiving the installation.
