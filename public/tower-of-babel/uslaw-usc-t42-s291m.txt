42 U.S.C. § 291m — State control of operations
Citation: us:usc:42:291m
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section291m
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter IV (Construction And Modernization Of Hospitals And Other Medical Facilities); Part D (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as otherwise specifically provided, nothing in this subchapter shall be construed as conferring on any Federal officer or employee the right to exercise any supervision or control over the administration, personnel, maintenance, or operation of any facility with respect to which any funds have been or may be expended under this subchapter.
