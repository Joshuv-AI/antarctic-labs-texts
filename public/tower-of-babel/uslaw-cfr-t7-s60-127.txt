7 C.F.R. § 60.127 — United States.
Citation: us:cfr:7:60.127
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-60.127
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 60 (Country Of Origin Labeling For Fish And Shellfish). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

United States means the 50 States, the District of Columbia, the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico, the U.S. Virgin Islands, American Samoa, Guam, the Northern Mariana Islands, and any other Commonwealth, territory, or possession of the United States, and the waters of the United States as defined in § 60.132.
