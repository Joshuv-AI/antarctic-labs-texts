40 C.F.R. § 62.10360 — Identification of plan.
Citation: us:cfr:40:62.10360
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-62.10360
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 62 (Approval And Promulgation Of State Plans For Designated Facilities And Pollutants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The State of South Dakota submitted a letter on June 7, 2021, certifying that there are no designated facilities subject to the emissions guidelines for existing hospital medical infectious waste incinerators under 40 CFR part 60, subpart Ce, operating within the State's jurisdiction.
