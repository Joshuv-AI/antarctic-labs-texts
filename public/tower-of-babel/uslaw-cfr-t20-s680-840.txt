20 C.F.R. § 680.840 — May funds provided to employers for work-based training and other work experiences be used to fill job openings as a result of a labor dispute?
Citation: us:cfr:20:680.840
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-680.840
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 680 (Adult And Dislocated Worker Activities Under Title I Of The Workforce Innovation And Opportunity Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No. Funds provided to employers for work-based training, as described in this subpart and in subpart A of this part, may not be used to directly or indirectly aid in the filling of a job opening which is vacant because the former occupant is on strike, or is being locked out in the course of a labor dispute, or the filling of which is otherwise an issue in a labor dispute involving a work stoppage.
