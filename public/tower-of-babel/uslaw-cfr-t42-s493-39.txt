42 C.F.R. § 493.39 — Notification requirements for laboratories issued a certificate of waiver.
Citation: us:cfr:42:493.39
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-493.39
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 493 (Laboratory Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Laboratories performing one or more tests listed in § 493.15 and no others must notify HHS or its designee—
(a) Before performing and reporting results for any test or examination that is not specified under § 493.15 for which the laboratory does not have the appropriate certificate as required in subpart C or subpart D of this part, as applicable; and
(b) Within 30 days of any change(s) in—
(1) Ownership;
(2) Name;
(3) Location; or
(4) Director.
