32 C.F.R. § 37.1280 — Equipment.
Citation: us:cfr:32:37.1280
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-37.1280
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 37 (Technology Investment Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Tangible property, other than real property, that has a useful life of more than one year and an acquisition cost of $5,000 or more per unit.
