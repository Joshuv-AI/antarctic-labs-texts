20 U.S.C. § 2503 — Administration by Office of Career Education
Citation: us:usc:20:2503
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section2503
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 45 (Career Education And Career Development); Subchapter I (Career Education And Development Programs And Activities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of this subchapter shall be carried out by the Secretary of Education through the Office of Career Education established pursuant to section 2612a(c) of this title.
