36 U.S.C. § 70707 — Misnomer not to affect transfer of property
Citation: us:usc:36:70707
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section70707
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 707 (Frederick Douglass Memorial And Historical Association). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A misnomer of the corporation does not affect any transfer of property to or from the corporation.
