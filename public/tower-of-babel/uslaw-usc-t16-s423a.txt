16 U.S.C. § 423a — Acceptance of donations of lands
Citation: us:usc:16:423a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section423a
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter LX (National Military Parks). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Interior is authorized to accept, on behalf of the United States, donations of lands, interests therein, or rights pertaining thereto required for the Petersburg National Battlefield.
