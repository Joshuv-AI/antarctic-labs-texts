43 U.S.C. § 36a — Acquisition of scientific or technical books, maps, etc., for library
Citation: us:usc:43:36a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section36a
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 2 (United States Geological Survey). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director of the United States Geological Survey, under the general supervision of the Secretary of the Interior, is authorized to acquire for the United States, by gift or devise, scientific or technical books, manuscripts, maps, and related materials, and to deposit the same in the library of the United States Geological Survey for reference and use as authorized by law.
