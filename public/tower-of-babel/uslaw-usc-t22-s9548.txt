22 U.S.C. § 9548 — Appropriate congressional committees defined
Citation: us:usc:22:9548
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section9548
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 102 (Countering Russian Influence In Europe And Eurasia); Subchapter II (Countering Russian Influence In Europe And Eurasia). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as otherwise provided, in this subchapter, the term “appropriate congressional committees” means—
(1)
the Committee on Foreign Relations, the Committee on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs, the Committee on Armed Services, the Committee on Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs, the Committee on Appropriations, and the Select Committee on Intelligence of the Senate; and
(2)
the Committee on Foreign Affairs, the Committee on Financial Services, the Committee on Armed Services, the Committee on Homeland Security, the Committee on Appropriations, and the Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence of the House of Representatives.
