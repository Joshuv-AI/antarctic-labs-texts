29 C.F.R. § 4065.3 — Filing requirement.
Citation: us:cfr:29:4065.3
Creator: Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-4065.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 4065 (Annual Report). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The requirement to report the occurrence of a reportable event under section 4043 of ERISA in the Annual Report is waived.
(b) Plan administrators shall file the Annual Report on IRS/DOL/PBGC Form 5500, 5500-C, 5500-K or 5500-R, as appropriate, in accordance with the instructions therein.
