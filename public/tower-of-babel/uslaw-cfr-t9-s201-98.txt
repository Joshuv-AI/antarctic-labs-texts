9 C.F.R. § 201.98 — Packers and dealers not to charge, demand, or collect commission, yardage, or other service charges.
Citation: us:cfr:9:201.98
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Fair Trade Practices Program), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-201.98
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 201 (Administering The Packers And Stockyards Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No packer or dealer shall, in connection with the purchase of livestock in commerce, charge, demand, or collect from the seller of the livestock any compensation in the form of commission, yardage, or other service charge unless the charge is for services mandated by law or statute and is not inconsistent with the provisions of the Act.
