5 C.F.R. § 1201.2 — Original jurisdiction.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1201.2
Creator: Merit Systems Protection Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1201.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1201 (Practices And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board's original jurisdiction includes the following cases:
(a) Actions brought by the Special Counsel under 5 U.S.C. 1214, 1215, and 1216;
(b) Requests, by persons removed from the Senior Executive Service for performance deficiencies, for informal hearings; and
(c) Actions taken against administrative law judges under 5 U.S.C. 7521.
