22 U.S.C. § 2276 — “Central American countries” defined
Citation: us:usc:22:2276
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section2276
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 32 (Foreign Assistance); Subchapter I (International Development); Part VI (Central America Democracy, Peace, And Development Initiative). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purposes of this part, the term “Central American countries” includes Belize, Costa Rica, El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras, Nicaragua, Panama, and regional programs which benefit such countries.
