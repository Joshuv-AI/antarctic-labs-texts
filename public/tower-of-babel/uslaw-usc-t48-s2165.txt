48 U.S.C. § 2165 — Limitation on jurisdiction and powers of court
Citation: us:usc:48:2165
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section2165
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 20 (Puerto Rico Oversight, Management, And Economic Stability); Subchapter III (Adjustments Of Debts). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Subject to the limitations set forth in subchapters I and II of this chapter, notwithstanding any power of the court, unless the Oversight Board consents or the plan so provides, the court may not, by any stay, order, or decree, in the case or otherwise, interfere with—
(1)
any of the political or governmental powers of the debtor;
(2)
any of the property or revenues of the debtor; or
(3)
the use or enjoyment by the debtor of any income-producing property.
