45 C.F.R. § 1176.4 — Establishing and converting part-time positions.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1176.4
Creator: National Foundation On The Arts And The Humanities
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1176.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1176 (Part-Time Career Employment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Position management and other internal reviews may indicate that positions may be either converted from full-time or initially established as part-time positions. Criteria listed in § 1176.3 may be used during these reviews. If a decision is made to convert to or to establish a part-time position, regular position management and classification procedures will be followed.
