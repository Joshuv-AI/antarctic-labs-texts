7 C.F.R. § 1730.29 — Grants and Grantees.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1730.29
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1730.29
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1730 (Electric System Operations And Maintenance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For the purposes of this part, the terms “borrower” shall include recipients of RUS electric program grants, and “applicant” shall include applicants for such grants. References to “security documents” shall, with respect to recipients of RUS electric program grants, include grant agreements and other grant-related documents.
