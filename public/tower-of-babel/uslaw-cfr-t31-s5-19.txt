31 C.F.R. § 5.19 — Will Treasury entities issue a refund if money is erroneously collected on a debt?
Citation: us:cfr:31:5.19
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-5.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 5 (Treasury Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Treasury entities shall promptly refund to a debtor any amount collected on a Treasury debt when the debt is waived or otherwise found not to be owed to the United States, or as otherwise required by law. Refunds under this part shall not bear interest unless required by law.
