10 U.S.C. § 7771 — Acceptance of donations: land for mobilization, training, supply base, or aviation field
Citation: us:usc:10:7771
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7771
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Procurement); Chapter 779 (Real Property). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Army may accept for the United States a gift of—
(1)
land that he considers suitable and desirable for a permanent mobilization, training, or supply station; and
(2)
land that he considers suitable and desirable for an aviation field, if the gift is from a citizen of the United States and its terms authorize the use of the property by the United States for any purpose.
