49 C.F.R. § 179.220-8 — -8 Tank heads.
Citation: us:cfr:49:179.220-8
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-179.220-8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 179 (Specifications For Tank Cars). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Tank heads of the inner container, inner container compartments and outer shell must be of approved contour, and may be flanged and dished or ellipsoidal for pressure on concave side.
(b) Flanged and dished heads must have main inside radius not exceeding 10 feet and inside knuckle radius must be not less than 3 3/4 inches for steel and alloy steel tanks nor less than 5 inches for aluminum alloy tanks.
(c) Ellipsoidal heads must be an ellipsoid of revolution in which the major axis must equal the diameter of the shell and the minor axis must be one-half the major axis.
