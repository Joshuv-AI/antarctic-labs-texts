11 U.S.C. § 350 — Closing and reopening cases
Citation: us:usc:11:350
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title11-section350
Context: United States Code, Title 11 (Bankruptcy); Chapter 3 (Case Administration); Subchapter III (Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
After an estate is fully administered and the court has discharged the trustee, the court shall close the case.
(b)
A case may be reopened in the court in which such case was closed to administer assets, to accord relief to the debtor, or for other cause.
