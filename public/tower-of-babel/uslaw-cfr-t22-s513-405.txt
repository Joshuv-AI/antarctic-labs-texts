22 C.F.R. § 513.405 — Causes for suspension.

Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Suspension may be imposed in accordance with the provisions of §§ 513.400 through 513.413 upon adequate evidence:
(1) To suspect the commission of an offense listed in § 513.305(a); or
(2) That a cause for debarment under § 513.305 may exist.
(b) Indictment shall constitute adequate evidence for purposes of suspension actions.
