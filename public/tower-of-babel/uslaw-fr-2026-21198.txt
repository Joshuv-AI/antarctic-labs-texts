Overview of This Information Collection

    Abstract: ATF provides law enforcement and investigation training 
to ATF employees and other federal, state, local, and international law 
enforcement personnel. Training covers numerous topics using both 
internal (ATF employees) and external technical experts from various 
disciplines. External instructors are non-ATF experts and may be from 
other law enforcement agencies, fire and rescue departments, 
professional organizations, or academia. Due to the nature of law 
enforcement and related training, ATF must maintain detailed records on 
the instructors' qualifications and expertise. An instructor's record 
includes their contact information, areas of expertise, educational and 
professional background, and professional vita or curriculum vitae. ATF 
must retain these records to maintain its Federal Law Enforcement 
Training Accreditation (FLETA) and to maintain a legally defensible 
training program. In some legal cases, it may be necessary to not only 
show that a specific individual received training, but also demonstrate 
that qualified instructors provided the training in a consistent 
manner. ATF uses the Adjunct Instructor Data Form, ATF Form 6140.3, to 
collect the necessary personally identifiable information (PII) from 
non-ATF employees to document and evaluate their qualifications to 
serve as an ATF instructor.
    Type of information collection: revising a previously approved

    Estimate of the total number of respondents and the amount of time 
estimated for an average respondent to respond: There are an estimated 
10 respondents associated with this collection per year, and it will 
take each respondent an average of approximately 30 minutes (0.50 
hours) per response to complete their responses.
    Estimate of the total annual burden (in hours) associated with the 
collection: The estimated annual public burden associated with this 
collection is five hours, which is equal to 10 (total respondents) * 1 
(# of responses per respondent) * 0.50 hours.
    Estimate of the total annual other cost burden associated with the 
collection, if applicable: 0; there are no costs.

Revisions to This Information Collection

    As part of this ICR renewal, ATF is making no revisions to this 
information collection, OMB 1140-0099. However, the annual hourly 
burden has decreased because the number of respondents went down from 
20 to 10 over the past three years. This in turn resulted in a 
decreased total annual burden from 10 to 5 hours. ATF is also making 
some minor edits to the form for plain writing compliance.
