40 C.F.R. § 60.5367c — c Is there an approval process for my state or Tribal plan?
Citation: us:cfr:40:60.5367c
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-60.5367c
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 60 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. The EPA will review your state or Tribal plan according to § 60.27a.
(a) The EPA will determine the completeness of your plan submission according § 60.27a(g).
(b) The EPA will act on your plan submission according to § 60.27a.
