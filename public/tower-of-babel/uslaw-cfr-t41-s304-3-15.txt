41 C.F.R. § 304-3.15 — -3.15 Reporting travel payments on financial disclosure reports.
Citation: us:cfr:41:304-3.15
Creator: Chapter 304—Payment Of Travel Expenses From A Non-Federal Source
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-304-3.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 304-3 (3—Employee Responsibility). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Generally, employees are not required to report travel payments on financial disclosure reports when payments are made to or on behalf of the agency, or payments are not considered personal gifts.
