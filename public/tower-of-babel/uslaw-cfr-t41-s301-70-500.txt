41 C.F.R. § 301-70.500 — -70.500 Governing policies and procedures for employee emergency travel.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-70.500
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-70.500
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-70 (70—Internal Policy And Procedure Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each agency must establish policies and procedures to determine—
(a) When the agency will authorize employee emergency travel under part 301-30 of this chapter;
(b) Who will determine if an employee's situation warrants payment for emergency travel expenses;
(c) When and by whom travel to an alternate location other than the official station or point of interruption will be authorized; and
(d) Who will determine when and if the definition of immediate family may be extended and to whom.
