21 C.F.R. § 880.5045 — Medical recirculating air cleaner.
Citation: us:cfr:21:880.5045
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-880.5045
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 880 (General Hospital And Personal Use Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. A medical recirculating air cleaner is a device used to remove particles from the air for medical purposes. The device may function by electrostatic precipitation or filtration.
(b) Classification. Class II (performance standards).
