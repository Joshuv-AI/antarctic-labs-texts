Abstract

A total dominating set exists if and only if every vertex has at least one in-neighbor. We call the orientation of such directed graphs valid. The total domination number of D D , denoted by γ t ​ ( D ) \gamma_{t}(D) , is the size of the smallest total dominating set of D D . Result 2.1

Let

Observation 2.3
.

Since an extremal orientation

Observation 2.4

Suppose that

Let us suppose on the contrary that there exists

Corollary 2.5
.

In an extremal orientation

every out-degree must be at most 2, i.e.

for every

.

Corollary 2.6

In an extremal orientation

Observation 2.7

A vertex
