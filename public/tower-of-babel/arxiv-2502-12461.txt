Contributions from the color-octet matrix elements to the B ¯ \overline{B} → {\to} π ​ π {\pi}{\pi} decays with QCD factorization approach
Yueling Yang, Bingbing Yang, Junfeng Sun

Abstract
Motivated by the π ​ π {\pi}{\pi} puzzle in nonleptonic B B decays in the time of high-precision and huge-data for particle physics, we restudy the B ¯ \overline{B} → {\to} π ​ π {\pi}{\pi} decays with QCD factorization approach. An additional contributions from the color-octet current matrix elements (COCME) which are unnoticed in almost all the previous calculation, are taken into consideration and parameterized by X X . It is found that (1) form factor F 0 B ​ π F_{0}^{B{\pi}} ≈ {\approx} 0.22 0.22 is favored by the available latest measurements. (2) The COCME contributions are helpful to interpret the experimental data on branching ratios rather than C ​ P CP asymmetries for the B ¯ \overline{B} → {\to} π ​ π {\pi}{\pi} decays.

II.3
The color-octet current matrix elements

As commented earlier in Section
I
,
except for the data-driven fit method,
we can not properly get a satisfactory theoretical explanation
for the measurement on branching ratio for the

\overline{B}^{0}

{\to}

{\pi}^{0}{\pi}^{0}
decay.
One major reason is that the
\overline{B}^{0}

{\to}

{\pi}^{0}{\pi}^{0}
decay induced by the internal
W^{\ast}

emission is color suppressed based on the argument that the
final pion must be color singlet.
The color suppression effect is incarnated by the amplitude in
Eq.(
24
) being in proportion to coefficient
a_{2}
.
It is easily seen from Eq.(
26
) that
(1) at the
{\alpha}_{s}^{0}
order, there is large interference
cancellation between Wilson coefficients
C_{2}
and
C_{1}
.
(2) at the
{\alpha}_{s}
order, the vertex correction contributions
V_{2}

are suppressed by both
{\alpha}_{s}
and the color factor
1/N
.
So considering comprehensively above-mentioned factors,
the expected value of branching ratio for the
\overline{B}^{0}

{\to}

{\pi}^{0}{\pi}^{0}
decay seems unlikely to be large.

To seek for a possible solution to the
{\pi}{\pi}
puzzle problem,
let’s reexamine the nonfactorizable contributions arising
from the hard gluon exchange interactions.
(1)

V_{2}
is in proportion to the large Wilson coefficient
C_{1}
.
(2)
The participation of the color-octet gluon would inevitably change
the color-structure of current-current operators.
An interesting idea is that the rebirth of color-octet HME
accompanied by large
C_{1}
might be helpful to the

{\pi}{\pi}
puzzle problem.

Using the identity relationship among the generators
T^{a}

(with
a

=

1
,
{\cdots}
,
8
) of the color
SU(3)
group,

T^{a}_{i,j}\,T^{a}_{k,l}\,=\,\frac{1}{2}\,{\delta}_{i,l}\,{\delta}_{k,j}-\frac{1}{2\,N}\,{\delta}_{i,j}\,{\delta}_{k,l},

(31)

where the subscript
i
,
j
,
k
,
l
are the color indices.
The four-quark operator can be expressed as,

\displaystyle(\bar{q}_{1,{\alpha}}\,{\Gamma}_{1}\,q_{2,{\beta}})\,(\bar{q}_{3,{\beta}}\,{\Gamma}_{2}\,q_{4,{\alpha}})

(32)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{N}\,(\bar{q}_{1,{\alpha}}\,{\Gamma}_{1}\,q_{2,{\alpha}})\,(\bar{q}_{3,{\beta}}\,{\Gamma}_{2}\,q_{4,{\beta}})

\displaystyle+

\displaystyle 2\,(\bar{q}_{1}\,{\Gamma}_{1}\,T^{a}\,q_{2})\,(\bar{q}_{3}\,{\Gamma}_{2}\,T^{a}\,q_{4}),

where
{\Gamma}_{1,2}
denotes Dirac current.
Take the HME of the
O_{1}
operator for example.
Using the Fierz rearrangement transformation,
there is

\displaystyle C_{1}\,{\langle}\,{\pi}^{0}\,{\pi}^{0}\,{|}\,O_{1}\,{|}\,\overline{B}^{0}\,{\rangle}

(33)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle C_{1}\,{\langle}\,{\pi}^{0}\,{\pi}^{0}\,{|}\,(\bar{u}_{\alpha}\,b_{\alpha})_{V-A}\,(\bar{d}_{\beta}\,u_{\beta})_{V-A}\,{|}\,\overline{B}^{0}\,{\rangle}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle C_{1}\,{\langle}\,{\pi}^{0}\,{\pi}^{0}\,{|}\,(\bar{u}_{\alpha}\,u_{\beta})_{V-A}\,(\bar{d}_{\beta}\,b_{\alpha})_{V-A}\,{|}\,\overline{B}^{0}\,{\rangle}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{C_{1}}{N}\,{\langle}\,{\pi}^{0}\,{\pi}^{0}\,{|}\,(\bar{u}_{\alpha}\,u_{\alpha})_{V-A}\,(\bar{d}_{\beta}\,b_{\beta})_{V-A}\,{|}\,\overline{B}^{0}\,{\rangle}

\displaystyle+

\displaystyle 2\,C_{1}\,{\langle}\,{\pi}^{0}\,{\pi}^{0}\,{|}\,(\bar{u}\,T^{a}\,u)_{V-A}\,(\bar{d}\,T^{a}\,b)_{V-A}\,{|}\,\overline{B}^{0}\,{\rangle}.

The first term in Eq.(
33
) is the color-singlet
current matrix element and corresponds to the second
and third terms of
a_{2}
in Eq.(
26
).
In almost all the practical calculation, the second term in Eq.(
33
),
called as the color-octet current matrix element (COCME),
is dropped because the color transparency scenario is routinely
used for most QCD situations.
We can imagine that before the formation of the color-neutral
point-like configuration, the meson composed of the colored quark
and colored antiquark might be regarded as a small color-dipole
at the interaction point.
In this paper, we will assume that
(1)
the COCME contributions are nonzero in
B
nonleptonic decays;
(2)
the chromatic interactions sourcing from the soft gluon exchange
between the color-octet currents alter only the color charge,
but are independent of the spin and flavor of the quarks;
(3)
the COCME contributions are nonperturbative, and should be
smaller than those of the color-singlet current-current
matrix elements.

In order to analyze the COCME contributions in
the decay amplitudes, we will parameterize the COCME contributions
by analogy with the color-singlet current matrix element
in the naive factorization (NF) approach
[
32
]
,
following the similar treatment with the PQCD approach in
Refs.
[
35
,
36
]
.

{\langle}\,{\pi}^{0}\,{\pi}^{0}\,{|}\,(\bar{u}_{\alpha}\,u_{\alpha})_{V-A}\,(\bar{d}_{\beta}\,b_{\beta})_{V-A}\,{|}\,\overline{B}^{0}\,{\rangle}\,=\,-i\,(m_{B}^{2}-m_{\pi}^{2})\,f_{\pi}\,F_{0}^{B{\pi}},

(34)

{\langle}\,{\pi}^{0}\,{\pi}^{0}\,{|}\,(\bar{u}\,T^{a}\,u)_{V-A}\,(\bar{d}\,T^{a}\,b)_{V-A}\,{|}\,\overline{B}^{0}\,{\rangle}\,=\,-i\,(m_{B}^{2}-m_{\pi}^{2})\,f_{\pi}\,F_{0}^{B{\pi}}\,X_{LL},

(35)

where the phenomenological parameter
X_{LL}
is introduced to
parameterize our ignorance about the COCME contributions
with the Dirac current configuration of

{\Gamma}_{1}\,{\otimes}\,{\Gamma}_{2}

=

(V-A)\,{\otimes}\,(V-A)
in Eq.(
32
),
and to show the relative magnitude and phase to the
color-singlet current matrix element.
Similarly, the COCME with other Dirac current configurations
are parameterized as,

{\langle}\,{\pi}^{0}\,{\pi}^{0}\,{|}\,(\bar{d}\,T^{a}\,d)_{V+A}\,(\bar{d}\,T^{a}\,b)_{V-A}\,{|}\,\overline{B}^{0}\,{\rangle}\,=\,-i\,(m_{B}^{2}-m_{\pi}^{2})\,f_{\pi}\,F_{0}^{B{\pi}}\,X_{RL},

(36)

-2\,{\langle}\,{\pi}^{0}\,{\pi}^{0}\,{|}\,(\bar{d}\,T^{a}\,d)_{S+P}\,(\bar{d}\,T^{a}\,b)_{S-P}\,{|}\,\overline{B}^{0}\,{\rangle}\,=\,+i\,(m_{B}^{2}-m_{\pi}^{2})\,f_{\pi}\,F_{0}^{B{\pi}}\,R\,X_{SP}.

(37)

After subtracting the common constant factors,
the parameters corresponding to different Dirac current
configurations seem to have similar origin.
In the calculation, we will assume that

X_{LL}

=

X_{RL}

=

X_{SP}

=

X

=

{|}X{|}\,e^{i\,{\delta}}

to reduce the number of parameters.
The quantity of
{\delta}
should be a strong phase,
and the same for the
B
and
\overline{B}
decays.

With the above convention, the decay amplitudes for the
the
\overline{B}

{\to}

{\pi}{\pi}
decays can be written as,

{\cal M}_{{\pi}{\pi}}\,=\,{\cal A}_{{\pi}{\pi}}+{\cal C}_{{\pi}{\pi}},

(38)

where
{\cal A}_{{\pi}{\pi}}
have been given in
Eq.(
22
), Eq.(
23
)
and Eq.(
24
).

{\cal C}_{{\pi}{\pi}}
denotes the amplitudes from
the COCME contributions, and has the general expression,

{\cal C}_{{\pi}{\pi}}\,=\,2\,X\,{\cal A}_{{\pi}{\pi}}(a_{i}\,{\to}\,C_{j}),

(39)

with
j

=

i

-

1
for even
i
, and

j

=

i

+

1
for odd
i
.

The small theoretical expectation of

{\cal A}_{CP}({\pi}^{-}{\pi}^{0})
brings a big challenge
to the experimental measurements.
Meanwhile, the observation of a large

{\cal A}_{CP}({\pi}^{-}{\pi}^{0})
is likely to imply the hint
of new physics beyond SM or a fresh physics breakthrough to be
explored.
All the current measurement precisions of

{\cal A}_{CP}({\pi}^{-}{\pi}^{0})
are still poor,
can not be used to make a meaningful and decisive judgment.
(b)
For the
\overline{B}^{0}

{\to}

{\pi}{\pi}
decays,
the amplitude
P

Considering the fact that the amplitude
T_{00}

{\propto}

a_{2}
is color suppressed, there are two interesting
expectations.
(i)
One is that
{\cal C}_{CP}({\pi}^{0}{\pi}^{0})

is sensitive to the COCME contributions, which is nicely
supported by the fit results.
The disagreements on
{\cal C}_{CP}({\pi}^{0}{\pi}^{0})

Of course, the current experimental errors of

{\cal C}_{CP}({\pi}^{0}{\pi}^{0})

A more reliable and higher precision measurement on

25
]
and

IV
Summary

The unitarity angle
{\alpha}
is an important observation
quantity in carefully checking the CKM picture of
CP

violation and precisely examining SM.
The
\overline{B}

{\to}

{\pi}{\pi}
decays play an essential role
in extracting and constraining the angle
{\alpha}
.
With the improvement of measurement precision in big data era,
the
{\pi}{\pi}
puzzle in
B
decays is exposed and attract
closer attention.
In this paper, we pick up the COCME contributions which are
rarely noticed by the traditional calculation, and restudy the

\overline{B}

{\to}

{\pi}{\pi}
decays with the QCDF approach.
By fitting with the available data using the least squares
method, the information of form factor
F_{0}^{B{\pi}}

and parameter
X
are obtained.
It is found that there is a close correlation between

F_{0}^{B{\pi}}
and
X
.
A small form factor
F_{0}^{B{\pi}}

{\approx}

0.22

is favored by both the fit and the recent lattice
simulation
[
37
]

and LCSR calculation
[
38
]
.
The COCME contributions can effectively enhance
branching ratio for the
\overline{B}^{0}

{\to}

{\pi}^{0}{\pi}^{0}
decay.
With the optimal parameters, the fit branching ratios
are in good agreement with the latest experimental data
within an error range.
The fit
CP
asymmetries are more or less inconsistent
with the current measurements, which need much more theoretical
and experimental endeavors to try the best together to
ease the tension.
