28 C.F.R. § 512.18 — Termination or suspension.
Citation: us:cfr:28:512.18
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-512.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 512 (Research). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director, Bureau of Prisons, may suspend or terminate a research project if it is believed that the project violates research policy or that its continuation may prove detrimental to the inmate population, the staff, or the orderly operation of the institution.
