45 C.F.R. § 2544.120 — What personal services from a volunteer may be solicited and accepted?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2544.120
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2544.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2544 (Solicitation And Acceptance Of Donations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A donation in the form of personal services from a volunteer may be solicited and accepted to assist the Corporation in carrying out its duties. However, volunteers may not perform an inherently governmental function.
