11 C.F.R. § 107.2 — Registration and reports by host committees and municipal funds.
Citation: us:cfr:11:107.2
Creator: Federal Election Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-11/section-107.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 11 (Federal Elections), Part 107 (Presidential Nominating Convention, Registration And Reports). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each host committee and municipal fund shall register and report in accordance with 11 CFR 9008.51. The reports shall contain the information specified in 11 CFR part 104.
