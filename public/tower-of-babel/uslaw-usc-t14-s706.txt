14 U.S.C. § 706 — United States Postal Service
Citation: us:usc:14:706
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section706
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 7 (Cooperation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Coast Guard facilities and personnel may be utilized for the transportation and delivery of mail matter during emergency conditions or at isolated locations under such arrangements as may be satisfactory to the Secretary and the United States Postal Service.
