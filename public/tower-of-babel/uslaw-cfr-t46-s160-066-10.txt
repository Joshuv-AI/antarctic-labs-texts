46 C.F.R. § 160.066-10 — -10 Expiration date.
Citation: us:cfr:46:160.066-10
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-160.066-10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 160 (Lifesaving Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each approved signal must have an expiration date marked on it. That date must not be more than forty-two months from date of manufacture.
