18 U.S.C. § 3556 — Order of restitution
Citation: us:usc:18:3556
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section3556
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part II (Criminal Procedure); Chapter 227 (Sentences); Subchapter A (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The court, in imposing a sentence on a defendant who has been found guilty of an offense shall order restitution in accordance with section 3663A, and may order restitution in accordance with section 3663. The procedures under section 3664 shall apply to all orders of restitution under this section.
