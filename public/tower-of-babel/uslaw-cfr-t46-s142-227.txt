46 C.F.R. § 142.227 — Fire axe.
Citation: us:cfr:46:142.227
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-142.227
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 142 (Fire Protection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each towing vessel must be equipped with at least one fire axe that is readily accessible for use from the exterior of the vessel.
