47 C.F.R. § 1.10016 — How do I apply for special temporary authority?
Citation: us:cfr:47:1.10016
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-1.10016
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 1 (Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Requests for Special Temporary Authority (STA) may be filed via ICFS for most services. We encourage you to file STA applications through ICFS as it will ensure faster receipt of your request.
(b) For specific information on the content of your request, refer to §§ 25.120 and 63.25 of this chapter.
