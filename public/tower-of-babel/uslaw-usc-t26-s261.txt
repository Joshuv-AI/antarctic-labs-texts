26 U.S.C. § 261 — General rule for disallowance of deductions
Citation: us:usc:26:261
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section261
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 1 (Normal Taxes And Surtaxes); Subchapter B (Computation Of Taxable Income); Part IX (Items Not Deductible). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In computing taxable income no deduction shall in any case be allowed in respect of the items specified in this part.
