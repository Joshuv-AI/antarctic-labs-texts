20 C.F.R. § 685.340 — What career services may grantees provide to eligible migrant and seasonal farmworkers?
Citation: us:cfr:20:685.340
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-685.340
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 685 (National Farmworker Jobs Program Under Title I Of The Workforce Innovation And Opportunity Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Grantees may provide the career services described in WIOA secs. 167(d) and 134(c)(2), and part 680 of this chapter to eligible MSFWs.
(b) Grantees may provide other services identified in the approved program plan.
(c) The delivery of career services to eligible MSFWs by the grantee and through the one-stop delivery system must be discussed in the required MOU between the Local WDB and the grantee.
