10 C.F.R. § 2.1209 — Findings of fact and conclusions of law.
Citation: us:cfr:10:2.1209
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-2.1209
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 2 (Agency Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each party shall file written post-hearing proposed findings of fact and conclusions of law on the contentions addressed in an oral hearing under § 2.1207 or a written hearing under § 2.1208 within 30 days of the close of the hearing or at such other time as the presiding officer directs. Proposed findings of fact and conclusions of law must conform to the format requirements in § 2.712(c).
