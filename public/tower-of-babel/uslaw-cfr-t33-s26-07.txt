33 C.F.R. § 26.07 — Communications.
Citation: us:cfr:33:26.07
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-26.07
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 26 (Vessel Bridge-To-Bridge Radiotelephone Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person may use the services of, and no person may serve as, a person required to maintain a listening watch under section 5 of the Act, 33 U.S.C. 1204, unless the person can communicate in the English language.
