24 C.F.R. § 200.300 — Nondiscrimination and fair housing policy.
Citation: us:cfr:24:200.300
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-200.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 200 (Introduction To Fha Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Federal Housing Administration programs shall be administered in accordance with:
(a) The nondiscrimination and fair housing requirements set forth in 24 CFR part 5, including the prohibition on inquiries regarding sexual orientation or gender identity set forth in 24 CFR 5.105(a)(2); and
(b) The affirmative fair housing marketing requirements in 24 CFR part 200, subpart M and 24 CFR part 108.
