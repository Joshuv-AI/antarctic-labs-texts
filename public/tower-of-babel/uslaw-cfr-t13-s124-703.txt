13 C.F.R. § 124.703 — Who is eligible to receive 7(j) assistance?
Citation: us:cfr:13:124.703
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-124.703
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 124 (8(a) BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT/SMALL DISADVANTAGED BUSINESS STATUS DETERMINATIONS). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following businesses are eligible to receive assistance from SBA through its service providers:
(a) Businesses which qualify as small under part 121 of this title, and which are located in urban or rural areas with a high proportion of unemployed or low-income individuals, or which are owned by such low-income individuals; and
(b) Businesses eligible to receive 8(a) contracts.
