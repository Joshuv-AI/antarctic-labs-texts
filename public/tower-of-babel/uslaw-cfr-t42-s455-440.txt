42 C.F.R. § 455.440 — National Provider Identifier.
Citation: us:cfr:42:455.440
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-455.440
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 455 (Program Integrity: Medicaid). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The State Medicaid agency must require all claims for payment for items and services that were ordered or referred to contain the National Provider Identifier (NPI) of the physician or other professional who ordered or referred such items or services.
