7 U.S.C. § 4819 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:7:4819
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section4819
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 79 (Pork Promotion, Research, And Consumer Information). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
There are authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary for the Secretary to carry out this chapter, subject to reimbursement from the Board under section 4809(c)(3)(B)(iv) of this title.
(b)
Sums appropriated to carry out this chapter shall not be available for payment of an expense or expenditure incurred by the Board in administering an order.
