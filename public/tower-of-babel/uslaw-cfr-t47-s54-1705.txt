47 C.F.R. § 54.1705 — Emergency Connectivity Fund eligible uses.
Citation: us:cfr:47:54.1705
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-54.1705
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 54 (Universal Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Eligible equipment and services purchased with Emergency Connectivity Fund support must be used primarily for educational purposes, as defined in § 54.1700.
