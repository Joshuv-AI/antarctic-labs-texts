48 C.F.R. § 828.106-71 — 828.106-71 Assisting service-disabled veteran-owned and veteran-owned small businesses in obtaining bonding.
Citation: us:cfr:48:828.106-71
Creator: Chapter 8—Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-828.106-71
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 828 (Bonds And Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

VA prime contractors are encouraged to assist SDVOSB concerns and VOSB concerns in obtaining subcontractor performance and payment bonds. Mentors are especially encouraged to assist their protégés in obtaining bid, payment, and performance bonds as prime contractors and bonds as subcontractors when bonds are required.
