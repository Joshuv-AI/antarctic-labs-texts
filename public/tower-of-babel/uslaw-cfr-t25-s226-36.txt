25 C.F.R. § 226.36 — Control devices.
Citation: us:cfr:25:226.36
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-226.36
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 226 (Leasing Of Osage Reservation Lands For Oil And Gas Mining). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In drilling operations in fields where high pressures, lost circulation, or other conditions exist which could result in blowouts, lessee shall install an approved gate valve or other controlling device which is in proper working condition for use until the well is completed. At all times preventative measures must be taken in all well operations to maintain proper control of subsurface strata.
