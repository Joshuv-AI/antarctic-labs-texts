49 C.F.R. § 1114.4 — Documents in Board's files.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1114.4
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1114.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1114 (Evidence; Discovery). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a party offers in evidence any matter contained in a report or other document open to public inspection in the files of the Board, such report or other document need not be made available at the hearing.
