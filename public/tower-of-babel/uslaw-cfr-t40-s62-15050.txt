40 C.F.R. § 62.15050 — What must I include in the notifications of achievement of my increments of progress?
Citation: us:cfr:40:62.15050
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-62.15050
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 62 (Approval And Promulgation Of State Plans For Designated Facilities And Pollutants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Your notification of achievement of increments of progress must include three items:
(a) Notification that the increment of progress has been achieved.
(b) Any items required to be submitted with the increment of progress (§§ 62.15065 through 62.15085).
(c) The notification must be signed by the owner or operator of the municipal waste combustion unit.
