6 C.F.R. § 115.110 — Coverage of DHS holding facilities.
Citation: us:cfr:6:115.110
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security, Office Of The Secretary
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-6/section-115.110
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 6 (Domestic Security), Part 115 (Sexual Abuse And Assault Prevention Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart B covers all DHS holding facilities. Standards found in subpart A of this part are not applicable to DHS facilities except ICE immigration detention facilities.
