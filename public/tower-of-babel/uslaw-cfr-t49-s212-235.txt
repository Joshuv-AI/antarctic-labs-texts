49 C.F.R. § 212.235 — Inapplicable qualification requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:49:212.235
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-212.235
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 212 (State Safety Participation Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Associate Administrator may determine that a specific requirement of this subpart is inapplicable to an identified position created by a State agency if it is not relevant to the actual duties of the position. The determination is made in writing.
