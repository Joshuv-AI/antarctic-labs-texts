12 C.F.R. § 329.103 — Calculation of available stable funding amount.
Citation: us:cfr:12:329.103
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-329.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 329 (Liquidity Risk Measurement Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An FDIC-supervised institution's ASF amount equals the sum of the carrying values of the FDIC-supervised institution's NSFR regulatory capital elements and NSFR liabilities, in each case multiplied by the ASF factor applicable in § 329.104 or § 329.107(c) and consolidated in accordance with § 329.109.
