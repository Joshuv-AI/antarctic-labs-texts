33 C.F.R. § 52.55 — Nonappearance.
Citation: us:cfr:33:52.55
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-52.55
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 52 (Board For Correction Of Military Records Of The Coast Guard). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An applicant who fails without good cause to appear in person or by counsel at the appointed date, time, and place for hearing, is deemed to have waived the right to a hearing. The application is then considered by the Board on the basis of all the material of record.
