48 C.F.R. § 49.108-1 — 49.108-1 Subcontractor's rights.
Citation: us:cfr:48:49.108-1
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-49.108-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 49 (Termination Of Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A subcontractor has no contractual rights against the Government upon the termination of a prime contract. A subcontractor may have rights against the prime contractor or intermediate subcontractor with whom it has contracted. Upon termination of a prime contract, the prime contractor and each subcontractor are responsible for the prompt settlement of the settlement proposals of their immediate subcontractors.
