7 C.F.R. § 1780.42 — Transfer of obligations.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1780.42
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1780.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1780 (Water And Waste Loans And Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An obligation of funds established for an applicant may be transferred to a different (substituted) applicant provided:
(a) The substituted applicant is eligible and has the authority to receive the assistance approved for the original applicant; and
(b) The need, purpose(s) and scope of the project for which RUS funds will be used remain substantially unchanged.
