21 C.F.R. § 556.530 — Pradofloxacin.
Citation: us:cfr:21:556.530
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-556.530
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 556 (Tolerances For Residues Of New Animal Drugs In Food). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Acceptable daily intake (ADI). The ADI for total residue of pradofloxacin is 2 µg/kg of body weight per day.
(b) Tolerances. The tolerances for pradofloxacin (marker residue) are:
(1) Cattle. Kidney (target tissue): 30 ppb.
(2) Swine. Kidney (target tissue): 1 ppm.
(c) Related conditions of use. See § 522.1860 of this chapter.
