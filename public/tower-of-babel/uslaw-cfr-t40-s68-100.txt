40 C.F.R. § 68.100 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:40:68.100
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-68.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 68 (Chemical Accident Prevention Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart designates substances to be listed under section 112(r)(3), (4), and (5) of the Clean Air Act, as amended, identifies their threshold quantities, and establishes the requirements for petitioning to add or delete substances from the list.
