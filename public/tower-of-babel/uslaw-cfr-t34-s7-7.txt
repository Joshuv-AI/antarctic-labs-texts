34 C.F.R. § 7.7 — Notice to employee of determination.
Citation: us:cfr:34:7.7
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-7.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 7 (Employee Inventions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The employee-inventor shall be notified in writing of the Department's determination of the rights to his invention and of his right of appeal, if any. Notice need not be given if the employee stated in writing that he would agree to the determination of ownership which was in fact made.
