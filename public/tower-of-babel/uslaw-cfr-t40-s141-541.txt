40 C.F.R. § 141.541 — What are significant changes to disinfection practice?
Citation: us:cfr:40:141.541
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-141.541
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 141 (National Primary Drinking Water Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Significant changes to disinfection practice include:
(a) Changes to the point of disinfection;
(b) Changes to the disinfectant(s) used in the treatment plant;
(c) Changes to the disinfection process; or
(d) Any other modification identified by the State.
