43 C.F.R. § 2200.0-2 — -2 Objective.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2200.0-2
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2200.0-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2200 (Exchanges: General Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The objective is to encourage and expedite the exchange of Federal lands for non-Federal lands, found to be in the public interest, in accordance with applicable statutory policies, standards and requirements.
