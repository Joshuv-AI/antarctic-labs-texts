44 U.S.C. § 1112 — Annual reports: type for reports of executive officers
Citation: us:usc:44:1112
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section1112
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 11 (Executive And Judiciary Printing And Binding). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The annual reports of executive officers shall be printed in the same type and form as the report of the head of the department which it accompanies, unless otherwise ordered by the Joint Committee on Printing.
