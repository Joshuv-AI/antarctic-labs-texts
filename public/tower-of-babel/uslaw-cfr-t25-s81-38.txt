25 C.F.R. § 81.38 — When are ballots counted?
Citation: us:cfr:25:81.38
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-81.38
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 81 (Secretarial Election Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The ballots will be counted under the supervision of the Secretarial Election Board, after the deadline established for receiving all ballots or closing of the polls, if polling sites are required by the tribe's governing document's amendment or adoption articles.
