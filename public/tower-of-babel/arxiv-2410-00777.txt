Abstract
We study the stability of a class of Caffarelli-Kohn-Nirenberg (CKN) interpolation inequality and establish a strong-form stability as following: inf v ∈ ℳ p , a , b ‖ u − v ‖ H b p ​ ‖ u − v ‖ L a p p − 1 ‖ u ‖ H b p ​ ‖ u ‖ L a p p − 1 ≤ C ​ δ p , a , b ​ ( u ) t , \inf_{v\in\mathcal{M}_{p,a,b}}\frac{\|u-v\|_{H_{b}^{p}}\|u-v\|_{L^{p}_{a}}^{p-1}}{\|u\|_{H^{p}_{b}}\|u\|_{L^{p}_{a}}^{p-1}}\leq C\delta_{p,a,b}(u)^{t}, where t = 1 t=1 for p = 2 p=2 and t = 1 p t=\frac{1}{p} for p > 2 p>2 , and δ p , a , b ​ ( u ) \delta_{p,a,b}(u) is deficit of the CKN. We also note that it is impossible to establish stability results for ∥ ⋅ ∥ H b p \|\cdot\|_{H_{b}^{p}} or ∥ ⋅ ∥ L a p \|\cdot\|_{L_{a}^{p}} separately. Moreover, we consider the second-order CKN inequalities and establish similar results for radial functions. Keywords: Stability; Caffarelli-Kohn-Nirenberg (CKN) inequality; Minimizers. 2020 Mathematics Subject Classification: Primary 46E35(Secondary 26D10); 35J61; 35B50.

Introduction

Recall the Caffarelli-Kohn-Nirenberg (CKN) inequality:

Theorem 1.1

(
[
3
]
)
.

For
N\geq 1
, let
s,p,q,a,b,c
and
\theta
satisfy the following conditions:

\displaystyle p,q\geq 1,\quad s>0,\quad 0\leq\theta\leq 1;

\displaystyle\frac{1}{p}+\frac{b}{N}>0,\quad\frac{1}{q}+\frac{a}{N}>0,\quad\frac{1}{s}+\frac{c}{N}>0;

\displaystyle\frac{1}{s}+\frac{c}{N}=\theta\left(\frac{1}{p}+\frac{b-1}{N}\right)+(1-\theta)\left(\frac{1}{q}+\frac{a}{N}\right);

\displaystyle c\leq\theta b+(1-\theta)a;

\displaystyle\frac{1}{s}\leq\frac{\theta}{p}+\frac{1-\theta}{q},\quad\text{if}\ \theta=0\ \text{or}\ \theta=1\ \text{or}\ \frac{1}{s}+\frac{c}{N}=\frac{1}{p}+\frac{b-1}{N}=\frac{1}{q}+\frac{a}{N}.

Then there exists some positive constant
S
such that

\big\||x|^{b}\nabla u\big\|_{L^{p}(\mathbb{R}^{N})}^{\theta}\ \big\||x|^{a}u\big\|_{L^{q}(\mathbb{R}^{N})}^{1-\theta}\geq S\big\||x|^{c}u\big\|_{L^{s}(\mathbb{R}^{N})}

(1.1)

holds for all
u\in C_{c}^{1}(\mathbb{R}^{N})
.

The largest
S
that satisfies (
1.1
) is its best constant, and a function that takes the equality is called a minimizer.

1.1
Brief history of the stability for CKN inequalities

We are concerned with the stability of it — can the
deficit
of
u
:

\delta(u)\coloneqq\||x|^{b}\nabla u\|_{L^{p}}^{\theta}\||x|^{a}u\|_{L^{q}}^{1-\theta}-S\||x|^{c}u\|_{L^{s}}

control some distance of
u
from the set of minimizers? Our quest to address this question unfolds in two primary ways: firstly, we need to figure out the best constant and the structure of the set of minimizers. Subsequently, we embark on identifying and employing suitable methods to estimate the distance.

The question on the stability of functional inequalities traced back to Brézis and Lieb
[
2
]
, and the initial result of the Sobolev inequalities (for the case
p=2
) was given by Bianchi and Egnell in
[
1
]
. The quantitative form of the
L^{p}
Sobolev inequality with
p\neq 2
was proved by
[
10
,
16
]
. For more detailed results about the Sobolev-type inequalities, see
[
17
,
6
,
19
,
23
,
26
,
15
,
9
,
18
]
.

When
\theta=1
, in the special case, only the term of derivative remains on the left, and this is known as weighted Sobolev inequality. Fortunately, some methods of studying the stability of the Sobolev inequality remain applicable in this case, and there are already many stability results. Chou and Chu
[
8
]
gave the best constant and minimizers for
p=2,\,(b,c)\neq(0,0)
. Based on their work, Wei and Wu
[
27
]
established some stability results in this case. For
p\neq 2
, the explicit best constant was given by Horiuchi in
[
20
]
. See Zhou and Zou
[
28
]
and the references therein for stability results in this case.

When it comes to the case
\theta\neq 1
, the inclusion of the interpolation terms in the CKN inequality makes it difficult to study. When
(a,b,c)=(0,0,0)
, it becomes the renowned Gagliardo-Nirenberg-Sobolev (GNS) inequality. Since there are no singular terms, we can use some rearrangement and symmetrization tricks to get its best constants and minimizers. Del Pino and Dolbeault
[
12
]
gave the best constant and minimizers for
p=2
, and
[
11
]
gave another proof by using mass transportation. Carlen and Figalli
[
4
]
used dimension reduction method to derive a stability result for the GNS inequality in
\mathbb{R}^{2}
by a result for Sobolev inequality in
\mathbb{R}^{4}
. Later, Nguyen
[
24
]
and Seuffert
[
25
]
generalized their work.

The case
\theta\neq 1,\,(a,b,c)\neq(0,0,0)
is perhaps the most challenging one. Until recently, stability results in this case had not been extensively explored. In
[
22
]
, McCurdy and Venkatraman gave a stability result for
(p,q,s)=(2,2,2),\,(a,b,c)=(1,0,0)
. Specifically, there exist constants
C_{1}>0,\,C_{2}(N)>0
such that for all
u\in W^{1,2}(\mathbb{R}^{N})
such that
|x|u\in L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{N})
, the following inequality holds:

\displaystyle\|\nabla u\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{N})}^{2}\||x|u\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{N})}^{2}-\frac{N^{2}}{4}\|u\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{N})}^{4}\geq C_{1}\|u\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{N})}d_{1}(u,E)^{2}+C_{2}(N)d_{1}(u,E)^{4},

where
E=\{ce^{-\alpha|x|^{2}}:c\in\mathbb{R},\,\alpha>0\}
is the set of minimizers, and

denotes the distance from
u
to
E
under
L^{2}
-norm. Fathi
[
14
]
gave a short proof with explicit constants
(C_{1},C_{2})=(\frac{1}{4},\frac{1}{16})
. Further advancements were made by Cazacu, Flynn, Lam and Lu
[
5
]
, which established an identity and used it to obtain the optimal constants
(C_{1},C_{2})=(N,1)
. Do, Flynn, Lam and Lu
[
13
]
followed their methods and generalized their result to the following
L^{p}
-CKN inequality:

Theorem 1.2

(
[
13
, Corollary 1.2]
)
.

Let
N\geq 1,\,p>1,\,b+1-a>0

. Then for all

\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}\frac{|\nabla u|^{p}}{|x|^{pb}}\,dx\right)^{1/p}\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}\frac{|u|^{p}}{|x|^{pa}}\,dx\right)^{\frac{p-1}{p}}\geq\frac{N-1-(p-1)a-b}{p}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}\frac{|u|^{p}}{|x|^{(p-1)a+b+1}}\,dx.

(1.2)

They also obtain the following weak stability results for some special
(p,a,b)

Theorem 1.3

(
[
13

)

Let

\displaystyle C(N,p,a,b)\inf_{v\in\mathcal{M}_{p,a,b}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}\frac{|u-v|^{p}}{|x|^{(p-1)a+b+1}}\,dx,

where its set of minimizers is

\mathcal{M}_{p,a,b}=\left\{k\exp\left(-\frac{\lambda}{b+1-a}|x|^{b+1-a}\right):k\in\mathbb{R},\,\lambda>0\right\}.

Let

\displaystyle\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}\frac{|\nabla u|^{2}}{|x|^{2b}}\,dx\right)^{1/2}\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}\frac{|u|^{2}}{|x|^{2a}}\,dx\right)^{1/2}-\frac{3b-a-N+3}{2}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}\frac{|u|^{2}}{|x|^{a+b+1}}\,dx

\displaystyle C(N,a,b)\inf_{v\in\mathcal{M}_{2,a,b}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}\frac{|u-v|^{2}}{|x|^{a+b+1}}\,dx,

where its set of minimizers is

1.2
Main results of the current paper

We begin by some notations. Let
N\geq 1
,
p>1
. The space
H^{p}_{b}(\mathbb{R}^{N})
is the closure of
C_{c}^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}^{N}\backslash\{0\})
under the norm

\|u\|_{H^{p}_{b}(\mathbb{R}^{N})}\coloneqq\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}|x|^{-pb}|\nabla u|^{p}\,dx\right)^{1/p},

and
L^{p}_{a}(\mathbb{R}^{N})
is the closure of
C_{c}^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}^{N}\backslash\{0\})
under the norm

\|u\|_{L^{p}_{a}(\mathbb{R}^{N})}\coloneqq\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}|x|^{-pa}|u|^{p}\,dx\right)^{1/p}.

The space
\mathcal{H}_{b,a}^{p,q}
is the closure of
C_{c}^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}^{N}\backslash\{0\})
under the norm

\|u\|_{\mathcal{H}_{b,a}^{p,q}(\mathbb{R}^{N})}\coloneqq\|\Delta u\|_{L_{b}^{p}(\mathbb{R}^{N})}+\|u\|_{H_{a}^{q}(\mathbb{R}^{N})}.

In the following sections, for the sake of simplifying notations, we will omit
\mathbb{R}^{N}
if there are no confusions.

We define the deficit of a function
u\in H^{p}_{b}\cap L^{p}_{a}
to be

\delta_{p,a,b}(u)=\frac{\|u\|_{H_{b}^{p}}\|u\|_{L_{a}^{p}}^{p-1}}{\|u\|_{L_{c}^{p}}^{p}}-S_{p,a,b},

(1.7)

where
c=\frac{(p-1)a+b+1}{p}
and
S_{p,a,b}
is the best constant of the CKN inequality. Similarly, we define the deficit of a function
u\in\mathcal{H}_{b,a}^{p,p}
to be

\sigma_{p,a,b}(u)\coloneqq\frac{\|\Delta u\|_{L_{b}^{p}}\|u\|_{H_{a}^{p}}^{p-1}}{\|u\|_{H_{c}^{p}}^{p}}-K_{p,a,b},

(1.8)

where
K_{p,a,b}
is the best constant of the second-order CKN inequality.

The first part of our paper focus on the CKN inequalities. Firstly, we will prove the following proposition, which means it is impossible to establish stability results for
\|\cdot\|_{H_{b}^{p}}
or
\|\cdot\|_{L_{a}^{p}}
separately.

Proposition 1.7
.

Assume
1<p<N
and
b+1\neq a
, then there do not exist universal constant
C>0
and
\alpha>0
such that

\displaystyle\left(\frac{\inf_{v\in\mathcal{M}_{p,a,b}}\|u-v\|_{H^{p}_{b}}}{\|u\|_{L^{p}_{c}}}\right)^{\alpha}\leq C\delta_{p,a,b}(u)

or

\displaystyle\left(\frac{\inf_{v\in\mathcal{M}_{p,a,b}}\|u-v\|_{L^{p}_{a}}}{\|u\|_{L^{p}_{c}}}\right)^{\alpha}\leq C\delta_{p,a,b}(u)

holds for all
u\in H^{p}_{b}\cap L^{p}_{a}
.

In light of this, our focus in this paper shifts towards providing a strong-form stability with respect to the interpolation terms. Specifically, we observe very different
behaviors for the cases of
p=2
and
p>2.

Theorem 1.8
.

Let
p=2
and
(a,b)
satisfy one of the following conditions:

(1)

0\leq b<\frac{N-2}{2}
,
a<\frac{Nb}{N-2}
and
a+b+1=\frac{2bN}{N-2}
;

(2)

\frac{N-2}{2}<b\leq N-2

2.1
Some observations for the CKN inequalities

•

\displaystyle\left\|\exp\left(-\frac{|x|^{b+1-a}}{b+1-a}\right)\right\|_{L^{p}_{c}}^{-1}

Then

\displaystyle\|v(k,\lambda)\|_{L^{p}_{a}}={}

\displaystyle|k|C_{1}\lambda^{-1/p}\left(V(\mathbb{S}^{N-1})\int_{0}^{\infty}r^{-pa+N-1}\exp\left(-\frac{pr^{b+1-a}}{b+1-a}\right)\,dr\right)^{1/p}

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle|k|\lambda^{-1/p}\left(\frac{b+1-a}{p}\right)^{1/p}\left(\frac{pS_{p,a,b}}{b+1-a}\right)^{1/p}

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle|k|\left(\frac{S_{p,a,b}}{\lambda}\right)^{1/p}.

When

and

satisfy condition (2) in Theorem

, then

.

Functions in

It is easy to see that the norms of

have the following relations (in both cases):

(2.1)

(2.2)

Proposition 2.1
.

Let
N\geq 1
,
p\geq 2
. Suppose
(p,a,b)

1.9
or Theorem
1.8
, then there exists
\delta_{0}>0

\delta_{p,a,b}(u)<\delta_{0}

\inf_{w\in\mathcal{M}_{p,a,b}}\|u-w\|_{L_{c}^{p}}.

(2.3)

Proof.

We first assume
(p,a,b)
satisfy conditions in Theorem

1.9
or condition (1) in Theorem
1.8
, then

pc=(p-1)a+b+1=\frac{pbN}{N-p}\in[0,N),

and so
|x|^{-c}\in L^{p}(\{|x|\leq 1\})
. Picking a minimizing sequence
\{v_{i}=v(k_{i},\lambda_{i})\}_{i=1}^{\infty}
, we show
\{k_{i}\},\,\{\lambda_{i}\},\,\{1/\lambda_{i}\}
are bounded. Since by (
1.3
)

\lim_{i\to\infty}\|u-v_{i}\|_{L^{p}_{c}}=\inf_{w\in\mathcal{M}}\|u-w\|_{L^{p}_{c}}\lesssim\delta_{p,a,b}(u)^{1/p}\|u\|_{L^{p}_{c}},

then

\delta_{p,a,b}(u)^{1/p}\|u\|_{L^{p}_{c}}\geq\lim_{i\to\infty}\|u-v(k_{i},\lambda_{i})\|_{L^{p}_{c}}\geq\limsup_{i\to\infty}|k_{i}|-\|u\|_{L^{p}_{c}}.

(2.4)

Thus
\{k_{i}\}
is bounded:
|k_{i}|\leq K
,
\forall\,i
, and we can pick a subsequence such that
k_{i}\to k_{0}\in\mathbb{R}
. If
\lambda_{i}\to 0
, for any
x\in\mathbb{R}^{N}\backslash\{0\}
, we have

|x|^{-c}|v_{i}(x)|=|k_{i}|C_{1}\lambda_{i}^{\alpha}|x|^{-c}\exp\left(-\lambda_{i}\frac{|x|^{b+1-a}}{b+1-a}\right)\leq KC_{1}\lambda_{i}^{\alpha}|x|^{-c}\to 0,\quad i\to\infty.

If
\lambda_{i}\to+\infty
, for any
x\in\mathbb{R}^{N}\backslash\{0\}
, choosing an integer

, we have

\displaystyle|x|^{-c}|v_{i}(x)|={}

\displaystyle|k_{i}|C_{1}\lambda_{i}^{\alpha}|x|^{-c}\exp\left(-\lambda_{i}\frac{|x|^{b+1-a}}{b+1-a}\right)

\displaystyle\leq{}

\displaystyle\lesssim{}

\displaystyle K\lambda_{i}^{-m+\alpha}|x|^{-m(b+1-a)-c}\to 0,\quad i\to\infty.

Hence, no matter
\lambda_{i}\to 0
or

pointwise for all

. Since

In the region

\displaystyle\delta_{0}\|u\|_{L^{p}_{c}}^{p}>\delta_{p,a,b}(u)\|u\|_{L^{p}_{c}}^{p}\gtrsim\lim_{i\to\infty}\|u-v_{i}\|_{L^{p}_{c}}^{p}\geq{}

\displaystyle\limsup_{i\to\infty}\int_{\epsilon\leq|x|\leq A}|x|^{-pc}|u-v_{i}|^{p}\,dx

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle\int_{\epsilon\leq|x|\leq A}|x|^{-pc}|u|^{p}\,dx>\frac{1}{2}\|u\|^{p}_{L^{p}_{c}},

which leads to a contradiction if
\delta_{0}\ll 1
. Thus,

are bounded, and we can pick a subsequence such that both

and so

showing that

attains the infimum.

Next, we assume
(p,a,b)
satisfy condition (2) in Theorem
1.8

and
\{k_{i}\}
is bounded. Next, we show

are bounded. If

If

3
Strong-form stability results for CKN inequalities

For the first part of this section, we show the deficit
\delta_{p,a,b}(u)
cannot control the
H^{p}_{b}
- or
L^{p}_{a}
-norms of
u
. For
u\in H^{p}_{b}\cap L^{p}_{a}
, define
\Phi_{\lambda}u(x)\coloneqq\lambda^{N/p-c}u(\lambda x)
, then

\displaystyle\|\Phi_{\lambda}u\|_{L^{p}_{c}}=\|u\|_{L^{p}_{c}};

\displaystyle\|\Phi_{\lambda}u\|_{L^{p}_{a}}=\lambda^{a-c}\|u\|_{L^{p}_{a}};

\displaystyle\|\Phi_{\lambda}u\|_{H^{p}_{b}}=\lambda^{b-c+1}\|u\|_{H^{p}_{b}},

implying that

\delta_{p,a,b}(\Phi_{\lambda}u)=\delta_{p,a,b}(u).

It is easy to see
\Phi_{\lambda^{-1}}(\Phi_{\lambda}u)=u
, and the set of minimizers is invariant under this transformation, i.e.,
\Phi_{\lambda}\mathcal{M}_{p,a,b}=\mathcal{M}_{p,a,b}
,
\forall\,\lambda>0
. Thus,

\displaystyle\inf_{v\in\mathcal{M}_{p,a,b}}\|\Phi_{\lambda}u-v\|_{H^{p}_{b}}=\inf_{v\in\mathcal{M}_{p,a,b}}\|\Phi_{\lambda}(u-v)\|_{H^{p}_{b}}=\lambda^{b-c+1}\inf_{v\in\mathcal{M}_{p,a,b}}\|u-v\|_{H^{p}_{b}}.

Proof of Proposition
1.7
.

If there exist
C>0
and
\alpha>0
such that for all
u\in H^{p}_{b}\cap L^{p}_{a}\backslash\{0\}
,

\displaystyle\left(\frac{\inf_{v\in\mathcal{M}_{p,a,b}}\|u-v\|_{H^{p}_{b}}}{\|u\|_{L^{p}_{c}}}\right)^{\alpha}\leq C\delta_{p,a,b}(u),

replacing
u
by
\Phi_{\lambda}u
we get

\displaystyle\left(\frac{\lambda^{b-c+1}\inf_{v\in\mathcal{M}_{p,a,b}}\|u-v\|_{H^{p}_{b}}}{\|u\|_{L^{p}_{c}}}\right)^{\alpha}\leq C\delta_{p,a,b}(\Phi_{\lambda}u)=C\delta_{p,a,b}(u),\quad\forall\,\lambda>0.

Since
b+1\neq a\implies b-c+1\neq 0
, taking
\lambda\to\infty
or
0
, we get a contradiction. Similarly, if

\left(\frac{\inf_{v\in\mathcal{M}_{p,a,b}}\|u-v\|_{L^{p}_{a}}}{\|u\|_{L^{p}_{c}}}\right)^{\alpha}\leq C\delta_{p,a,b}(u),

then

\displaystyle\left(\frac{\lambda^{a-c}\inf_{v\in\mathcal{M}_{p,a,b}}\|u-v\|_{L^{p}_{a}}}{\|u\|_{L^{p}_{c}}}\right)^{\alpha}\leq C\delta_{p,a,b}(\Phi_{\lambda}u)=C\delta_{p,a,b}(u),\quad\forall\,\lambda>0,

we also get a contradiction by letting
\lambda\to\infty
or
0
.
∎

These properties of transformation indicate that, to establish the stability results of the strong-forms, it is only possible to strengthen the deficit
\delta_{p,a,b}(u)
into
\tilde{\delta}_{p,a,b}(u)
, or reduce the sum of the
H^{p}_{b}
- and
L^{p}_{a}
-norms to their weighted product.

The case

Proof of Theorem
1.10
: the case
p=2
.

Write
S=S_{2,a,b}
and

. Thanks to Proposition

, there exists

\inf_{w\in\mathcal{M}_{2,a,b}}\|u-w\|_{L_{c}^{2}}

is attainable. If
\tilde{\delta}(u)\geq 2\delta_{0}

\displaystyle\inf_{v\in\mathcal{M}_{2,a,b}}\frac{\|u-v\|_{H_{b}^{2}}^{2}+\|u-v\|_{L^{2}_{a}}^{2}}{\|u\|_{H^{2}_{b}}^{2}+\|u\|_{L^{2}_{a}}^{2}}\leq 1\leq\epsilon_{1}^{-1}\tilde{\delta}(u),

and the statement holds.

Next, we assume
\tilde{\delta}(u)<2\delta_{0}
. We can choose
v\in\mathcal{M}_{2,a,b}
such that it attains
\inf_{w\in\mathcal{M}_{2,a,b}}\|u-w\|_{L_{c}^{2}}
. Assume
v(x)=v(k_{0},\lambda_{0})
, then by the minimality, we know
k_{0}\neq 0
and

\displaystyle\partial_{k}\big|_{k=k_{0}}\|u-v(k,\lambda)\|_{L^{2}_{c}}=0,

\displaystyle\partial_{\lambda}\big|_{\lambda=\lambda_{0}}\|u-v(k,\lambda)\|_{L^{2}_{c}}=0.

Since for

,

\partial_{k}v(k,\lambda)=\frac{v}{k},\quad\partial_{\lambda}v(k,\lambda)=\frac{\alpha}{\lambda}v-\frac{|x|^{b+1-a}}{b+1-a}v,

we get

\displaystyle\int|x|^{-2c}(u-v)\frac{v}{k_{0}}=0,\quad\int|x|^{-2c}(u-v)v\left(\frac{\alpha}{\lambda_{0}}-\frac{|x|^{b+1-a}}{b+1-a}\right)=0.

Hence, the following orthogonality conditions hold:

\displaystyle\int|x|^{-(a+b+1)}(u-v)v\,dx=0\implies\|v\|_{L^{2}_{c}}^{2}=\int|x|^{-(a+b+1)}uv\,dx,

(3.1)

\displaystyle\int|x|^{-2a}(u-v)v\,dx=0\implies\|v\|_{L^{2}_{a}}^{2}=\int|x|^{-2a}uv\,dx.

(3.2)

Since
v
satisfies equation (
2.2
), testing
v
by
u
, we obtain

\displaystyle\int|x|^{-2b}\nabla v\cdot\nabla u+\lambda_{0}^{2}\int|x|^{-2a}vu-2S\lambda_{0}\int|x|^{-(a+b+1)}vu=0

\displaystyle\implies{}

\displaystyle\int|x|^{-2b}\nabla u\cdot\nabla v=-\lambda_{0}^{2}\|v\|_{L^{2}_{a}}^{2}+2S\lambda_{0}\|v\|_{L^{2}_{c}}^{2}.

By (
2.1
), when

, we get the third orthogonality condition:

\displaystyle\int|x|^{-2b}\nabla u\cdot\nabla v\,dx=\|v\|_{H^{2}_{b}}^{2}

(3.3)

\displaystyle\implies{}

\displaystyle\int|x|^{-2b}(\nabla u-\nabla v)\cdot\nabla v\,dx=0.

By (
3.1
), (
3.2

for the

\displaystyle\|u-v\|_{H_{b}^{2}}^{2}+\|u-v\|_{L^{2}_{a}}^{2}={}

\displaystyle(\|u\|_{H_{b}^{2}}^{2}-\|v\|_{H_{b}^{2}}^{2})+(\|u\|_{L^{2}_{a}}^{2}-\|v\|_{L^{2}_{a}}^{2})

(3.4)

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle\|u\|_{H_{b}^{2}}^{2}+\|u\|_{L_{a}^{2}}^{2}-2S^{2}\|u\|_{L_{c}^{2}}^{2}+2S^{2}\|u-v\|_{L^{2}_{c}}^{2}

\displaystyle-\big(\|v\|_{H_{b}^{2}}^{2}+\|v\|_{L_{a}^{2}}^{2}-2S^{2}\|v\|_{L^{2}_{c}}^{2}\big)

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle\tilde{\delta}(u)\|u\|_{L^{2}_{c}}^{2}+2S^{2}\|u-v\|_{L^{2}_{c}}^{2}-\tilde{\delta}(v)\|v\|_{L^{2}_{c}}^{2}

\displaystyle\tilde{\delta}(u)\|u\|_{L^{2}_{c}}^{2}+2S^{2}\|u-v\|_{L^{2}_{c}}^{2}.

Hence,

\displaystyle\frac{\|u-v\|_{H^{2}_{b}}^{2}+\|u-v\|_{L^{2}_{a}}^{2}}{\|u\|_{H^{2}_{b}}^{2}+\|u\|_{L^{2}_{a}}^{2}}\lesssim{}

\displaystyle\tilde{\delta}(u)\frac{\|u\|_{L^{2}_{c}}^{2}}{\|u\|_{H^{2}_{b}}^{2}+\|u\|_{L^{2}_{a}}^{2}}\lesssim\tilde{\delta}(u),

\displaystyle\|u\|_{L^{p}_{a}}^{p}=\int|x|^{-pa}|u|^{p}\,dx={}

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{S}^{N-1}}\int_{0}^{\infty}r^{-pa+N-1}|u|^{p}\,drd\theta

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle l^{-1}\int_{\mathbb{S}^{N-1}}\int_{0}^{\infty}r^{\frac{p}{p-1}+N-1}|\tilde{u}|^{p}\,drd\theta

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle l^{-1}\|\tilde{u}\|_{L^{p}_{-1/(p-1)}}^{p}=l^{\frac{N(p-1)}{p}}\|u_{1}\|_{L^{p}_{-1/(p-1)}}^{p},

\displaystyle\|u\|_{L^{p}_{c}}^{p}=\int|x|^{-pc}|u|^{p}\,dx={}

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{S}^{N-1}}\int_{0}^{\infty}r^{-pc+N-1}|u|^{p}\,drd\theta

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle l^{-1}\int_{\mathbb{S}^{N-1}}\int_{0}^{\infty}r^{\frac{p}{p-1}+N-1}|\tilde{u}|^{p}\,drd\theta

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle l^{-1}\|\tilde{u}\|_{L^{p}_{0}}^{p}=l^{\frac{N(p-1)}{p}-1}\|u_{1}\|_{L^{p}_{0}}^{p}.

Note that

\displaystyle S_{p,a,b}={}

\displaystyle\frac{N-1-(p-1)a-b}{p}

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle\frac{N-\frac{Npb}{N-p}}{p}

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle\frac{N}{p}\cdot\frac{N-p-pb}{N-p}=lS(p,-\frac{1}{p-1},0),

we get

\displaystyle\delta_{p,a,b}(u)\geq l\delta_{p,-\frac{1}{p-1},0}(u_{1}),

(3.5)

\displaystyle\tilde{\delta}_{p,a,b}(u)\geq l\tilde{\delta}_{p,-\frac{1}{p-1},0}(u_{1}).

Note that, under this transform
u\mapsto u_{1}
, the set
\mathcal{M}_{p,a,b}
becomes
\mathcal{M}_{p,-\frac{1}{p-1},0}
, so it is enough to show Theorem
1.10
and
1.9
with
(a,b,c)=(-\frac{1}{p-1},0,0)
. In the following we will omit
a,b
if there are no confusions, and write

\displaystyle H^{p}=H^{p}_{0},\quad L^{p}=L^{p}_{0},\quad S=S_{p,-\frac{1}{p-1},0}=\frac{N}{p},

\displaystyle\delta(u)=\delta_{p,-\frac{1}{p-1},0}(u),\quad\tilde{\delta}(u)=\tilde{\delta}_{p,-\frac{1}{p-1},0}(u),\quad\mathcal{M}=\mathcal{M}_{p,-\frac{1}{p-1},0}

for convenience.

To prove Theorem
1.10
and
1.9
, we will use the following sharp inequalities on vectors proved in

]
.

Lemma 3.2

(
[
17
, Lemma 2.1 and 2.4]
)
.

Let
y,z\in\mathbb{R}^{n}
and
a,b\in\mathbb{R}
. Then, for any
\kappa>0
, there exist constants
c_{0}=c_{0}(p,\kappa)>0
and
c_{1}=c_{1}(p,\kappa)>0
such that the following statements hold:

(i)

For
p>2
,

\displaystyle|y+z|^{p}\geq{}

\displaystyle|y|^{p}+p|y|^{p-2}y\cdot z

\displaystyle+\frac{1-\kappa}{2}\Big(p|y|^{p-2}|z|^{2}+p(p-2)|w|^{p-2}\big(|y|-|y+z|\big)^{2}\Big)+c_{0}|z|^{p},

where

w=w(y,y+z)=\begin{cases}x,&\text{if}\ |y|\leq|y+z|,\\
(\frac{|y+z|}{|y|})^{\frac{1}{p-2}}(y+z),&\text{if}\ |y+z|\leq|y|.\end{cases}

(ii)

For
p>2
,

\displaystyle|a+b|^{p}\leq{}

\displaystyle|a|^{p}+p|a|^{p-2}ab+\left(\frac{p(p-1)}{2}+\kappa\right)|a|^{p-2}|b|^{2}+c_{1}|b|^{p}.

Thanks to these primary inequalities, the proof for
p=2
is valid as long as we can find some minimizer
v
satisfying the following orthogonality properties:

\displaystyle\int|\nabla v|^{p-2}\nabla v\cdot(\nabla u-\nabla v)=0,

(3.6)

\displaystyle\int|x|^{\frac{p}{p-1}}|v|^{p-2}v(u-v)=0,

\displaystyle\int|v|^{p-2}v(u-v)=0.

We hope
v\in\mathcal{M}
attaining
\inf_{w\in\mathcal{M}}\|u-w\|_{L^{p}}
can give us the desired result, but such minimizer only satisfies

\displaystyle\int|u-v|^{p-2}uv=0=\int|x|^{\frac{p}{p-1}}|u-v|^{p-2}uv,

and it is difficult to show it satisfies (
3.6
). To settle it, we pick another element of
\mathcal{M}
.

Definition 3.3
.

For
u\in H^{p}\cap L^{p}_{-1/(p-1)}
, set

P_{u}=\left\{v\in\mathcal{M}:\|v\|_{L^{p}}=1,\,\int|v|^{p-2}uv=\sup_{\stackrel{{\scriptstyle w\in\mathcal{M}}}{{\|w\|_{L^{p}}=1}}}\int|w|^{p-2}uw\right\}.

(3.7)

Proposition 3.4
.

There exists
\delta_{1}>0
such that for any
u\in H^{p}\cap L^{p}_{-1/(p-1)}
with
\delta(u)<\delta_{1}
, the following supremum is attainable:

\sup_{\stackrel{{\scriptstyle w\in\mathcal{M}}}{{\|w\|_{L^{p}}=1}}}\int|w|^{p-2}uw.

(3.8)

Proof.

Pick a minimizing sequence
\{v_{i}=v(k_{i},\lambda_{i})\}_{i=1}^{\infty}
, where
k_{i}\in\{1,-1\}
and
\lambda_{i}>0
. Let

\mu\coloneqq\sup_{\stackrel{{\scriptstyle w\in\mathcal{M}}}{{\|w\|_{L^{p}}=1}}}\int|w|^{p-2}uw.

If
\mu=0
, since at least one of
v(1,\lambda)
,
v(-1,\lambda)
makes
\int|v|^{p-1}vu\geq 0
, it is easily to attain it. Next we assume
\mu>0
. Since
\||v_{i}|^{p-1}v_{i}\|_{L^{p^{\prime}}}^{p^{\prime}}=\|v_{i}\|_{L^{p}}^{p}=1
, there exists
f\in L^{p^{\prime}}
s.t.
|v_{i}|^{p-2}v_{i}\rightharpoonup f
weakly in
L^{p^{\prime}}
. We recall the estimates of
|v_{i}(x)|
in Proposition
2.1
:

|v_{i}(x)|\lesssim\begin{cases}K\left(\sup\lambda_{i}\right)^{\alpha},&\text{If $\lambda_{i}\to 0$},\\
K|x|^{-m(b+1-a)}\left(\inf\lambda_{i}\right)^{-m+\alpha},&\text{If $\lambda_{i}\to\infty$}.\end{cases}

Hence, if
\lambda_{i}\to 0
or
\lambda_{i}\to+\infty
, then for any
0<\epsilon<A<+\infty
and any
\varphi\in C_{0}^{\infty}(\{\epsilon\leq|x|\leq A\})
,

\int f\varphi=\lim_{i\to\infty}\int|v_{i}|^{p-2}v_{i}\varphi=0\implies f=0\,\,\text{for a.e. $\epsilon\leq|x|\leq A$},

yielding that
f=0
a.e. and thus,

0=\int fu=\lim_{i\to\infty}\int|v_{i}|^{p-2}v_{i}u=\mu>0,

which is a contradiction. Thus, we can assume
k_{i}\equiv 1
and
\lambda_{i}\to\lambda_{0}\in\mathbb{R}_{+}
, and so
v_{i}\to v_{0}\coloneqq v(1,\lambda_{0})
pointwise. We must have
f=|v_{0}|^{p-2}v_{0}
a.e. and then

\int|v_{0}|^{p-2}v_{0}u=\lim_{i\to\infty}\int|v_{i}|^{p-2}v_{i}u=\sup_{\stackrel{{\scriptstyle w\in\mathcal{M}}}{{\|w\|_{L^{p}}=1}}}\int|w|^{p-2}uw

as we desire.
∎

Remark 3.5
.

Fix
p\geq 2
, for general
(a,b)
satisfying the conditions in Theorem
1.9
, if

.

Proposition 3.6
.

There exist
\delta_{2}\leq\min\{\delta_{0},\delta_{1}\}
and
C_{2}>0
depending only on
N,p,a,b
s.t. for any
u\in H^{p}\cap L^{p}_{-1/(p-1)}
with
\delta(u)<\delta_{2}
, we have

\sup_{v\in P_{u}}\frac{\|u-\mu_{v}v\|_{L^{p}}}{\|u\|_{L^{p}}}\leq C_{2}\left(\inf_{w\in\mathcal{M}}\frac{\|u-w\|_{L^{p}}}{\|u\|_{L^{p}}}\right)^{1/2}\lesssim\delta(u)^{1/(2p)},

(3.9)

where for
v\in P_{u}
,
\mu_{v}=\int|v|^{p-2}vu\geq 0
.

Proof.

For
u\in H^{p}\cap L^{p}_{-1/(p-1)}
with
\delta(u)<\min\{\delta_{0},\delta_{1}\}
, we can pick
v\in P_{u}
,
\mu=\int|v|^{p-2}vu
and
w
attaining
\inf_{w\in\mathcal{M}}\|u-w\|_{L^{p}}
. Since
\epsilon\coloneqq\|u-w\|_{L^{p}}\lesssim\delta(u)^{1/p}\ll 1
, we have

\big|\|w\|_{L^{p}}-1\big|\leq\|w-u\|_{L^{p}}=\epsilon.

(3.10)

From the fact that
v\in P_{u}
, we get

\displaystyle\|w\|_{L^{p}}^{p-1}\mu\geq{}

\displaystyle\int|w|^{p-2}wu

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle\int|w|^{p-2}w(u-w)+\|w\|_{L^{p}}^{p}

\displaystyle\geq{}

\displaystyle\|w\|_{L^{p}}^{p}-\|w\|_{L^{p}}^{p-1}\|u-w\|_{L^{p}},

and so

1\geq\int|v|^{p-2}vu=\mu\geq\|w\|_{L^{p}}-\epsilon.

(3.11)

By (
3.10
),

0\leq 1-\mu\leq\epsilon+1-\|w\|_{L^{p}}\leq 2\epsilon.

(3.12)

By (
3.10
), (
3.12
), we immediately get

\begin{split}&\left\|\frac{w}{\|w\|_{L^{p}}}-v\right\|_{L^{p}}\\
\geq{}&\|u-\mu v\|_{L^{p}}-\|\mu v-v\|_{L^{p}}-\|u-w\|_{L^{p}}-\left\|\frac{w}{\|w\|_{L^{p}}}-w\right\|_{L^{p}}\\
\geq{}&\|u-\mu v\|_{L^{p}}-|\mu-1|-\big|\|w\|_{L^{p}}-1\big|-\epsilon\\
\geq{}&\|u-\mu v\|_{L^{p}}-4\epsilon.\end{split}

(3.13)

Moreover,

\displaystyle 1\geq\int|v|^{p-2}v\frac{w}{\|w\|_{L^{p}}}={}

\displaystyle\int|v|^{p-2}v_{i}\left(\frac{w}{\|w\|_{L^{p}}}-u_{i}\right)+\mu

(3.14)

\displaystyle\geq{}

\displaystyle\mu-\|u-w\|_{L^{p}}-\left\|\frac{w}{\|w\|_{L^{p}}}-w\right\|_{L^{p}}

\displaystyle\geq{}

\displaystyle 1-4\epsilon>0,

if
\epsilon<1/4
. Thus,
v
and
w
have the same sign, and we can assume
v=v(1,\lambda)
and
w=v(\|w\|_{L^{p}},\rho)
. Now we have

\displaystyle\int|v|^{p-2}v\frac{w}{\|w\|_{L^{p}}}={}

\displaystyle C_{1}^{p}\lambda^{\frac{N(p-1)^{2}}{p^{2}}}\rho^{\frac{N(p-1)}{p^{2}}}\int\exp\left(-((p-1)\lambda+\rho)\frac{|x|^{p/(p-1)}}{p/(p-1)}\right)\,dx

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle C_{1}^{p}\left(\frac{\lambda^{\frac{p-1}{p}}\rho^{\frac{1}{p}}}{(1-\frac{1}{p})\lambda+\frac{1}{p}\rho}\right)^{\frac{N(p-1)}{p}}\int\exp\left(-(p-1)|x|^{p/(p-1)}\right)\,dx

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle\left(\frac{\lambda^{\frac{p-1}{p}}\rho^{\frac{1}{p}}}{(1-\frac{1}{p})\lambda+\frac{1}{p}\rho}\right)^{\frac{N(p-1)}{p}}=\left(\frac{t^{1/p}}{1-1/p+t/p}\right)^{\frac{N(p-1)}{p}},

where
t=\rho/\lambda
. By (
3.14
),

0\leq 1-\left(\frac{t^{1/p}}{1-1/p+t/p}\right)^{\frac{N(p-1)}{p}}\leq 4\epsilon.

(3.15)

It is easy to see
|t-1|\leq 1/2
as long as
\epsilon\ll 1
. Since

\lim_{x\to 1}\frac{1-x^{q}}{1-x}=\lim_{x\to 1}\frac{-qx^{q-1}}{-1}=q>0,\quad\forall\,q\in\mathbb{R}_{+},

(
3.15
) implies

1-\frac{t^{1/p}}{1-1/p+t/p}\lesssim\epsilon.

(3.16)

Finally, we calculate the distance of
\frac{w}{\|w\|_{L^{p}}}
and
v
:

\displaystyle\left\|\frac{w}{\|w\|_{L^{p}}}-v\right\|_{L^{p}}^{p}={}

\displaystyle C_{1}^{p}\int\left|\rho^{\frac{N(p-1)}{p^{2}}}e^{-\rho\frac{|x|^{p/(p-1)}}{p/(p-1)}}-\lambda^{\frac{N(p-1)}{p^{2}}}e^{-\lambda\frac{|x|^{p/(p-1)}}{p/(p-1)}}\right|^{p}\,dx

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle C_{1}^{p}\int\left|t^{\frac{N(p-1)}{p^{2}}}e^{-t\frac{|x|^{p/(p-1)}}{p/(p-1)}}-e^{-\frac{|x|^{p/(p-1)}}{p/(p-1)}}\right|^{p}\,dx

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle C_{1}^{p}\int_{\mathbb{S}^{N-1}}\int_{0}^{\infty}e^{-(p-1)r^{p/(p-1)}}r^{N-1}\left|1-t^{\frac{N(p-1)}{p^{2}}}e^{(1-t)\frac{r^{p/(p-1)}}{p/(p-1)}}\right|^{p}\,drd\theta

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle C_{1}^{p}V(\mathbb{S}^{N-1})\int_{0}^{\infty}e^{-(p-1)r^{p/(p-1)}}r^{N-1}|f_{r}(1)-f_{r}(t)|^{p}\,dx,

where

f_{r}(s)=s^{\frac{N(p-1)}{p^{2}}}e^{(1-s)\frac{r^{p/(p-1)}}{p/(p-1)}}.

(3.17)

For
|s-1|\leq 1/2
, we have

\displaystyle f_{r}^{\prime}(s)={}

\displaystyle f_{r}(s)\left(\frac{N(p-1)}{p^{2}s}-\frac{r^{p/(p-1)}}{p/(p-1)}\right)

\displaystyle\implies|f_{r}^{\prime}(s)|\leq{}

\displaystyle\left(\frac{2N(p-1)}{p^{2}}+\frac{r^{p/(p-1)}}{p/(p-1)}\right)|f_{r}(s)|

\displaystyle\leq{}

\displaystyle 2^{\frac{N(p-1)}{p^{2}}}\left(\frac{2N(p-1)}{p^{2}}+\frac{r^{p/(p-1)}}{p/(p-1)}\right)\exp\left(\frac{1}{2}\frac{r^{p/(p-1)}}{p/(p-1)}\right),

and so

|f_{r}(1)-f_{r}(t)|\leq|1-t|\sup_{s\in[1,t]}|f_{r}^{\prime}(s)|\lesssim|1-t|(1+r^{p/(p-1)})\exp\left(\frac{1}{2}\frac{r^{p/(p-1)}}{p/(p-1)}\right).

(3.18)

Hence, when
\epsilon\ll 1
, we have

\begin{split}\left\|\frac{w}{\|w\|_{L^{p}}}-v\right\|_{L^{p}}^{p}\lesssim{}&|1-t|^{p}\int_{0}^{\infty}e^{-\frac{p-1}{2}r^{p/(p-1)}}r^{N-1}(1+r^{p/(p-1)})^{p}\,dr\\
={}&O(|1-t|^{p}).\end{split}

(3.19)

Since

\lim_{x\to 1}\frac{1-\frac{1}{p}+\frac{x}{p}-x^{\frac{1}{p}}}{(1-x)^{2}}=\lim_{x\to 1}\frac{\frac{1}{p}\left(1-x^{\frac{1}{p}-1}\right)}{-2(1-x)}=\lim_{x\to 1}\frac{\frac{1}{p}\left(1-\frac{1}{p}\right)x^{\frac{1}{p}-2}}{2}=\frac{1}{2p}\left(1-\frac{1}{p}\right)>0,

(
3.16
) implies

\epsilon\gtrsim 1-\frac{t^{1/p}}{1-1/p+t/p}=O(|1-t|^{2})\implies|1-t|\lesssim\epsilon^{1/2}.

(3.20)

Taking (
3.19
) and (
3.20
) into (
3.13
), we get

\begin{split}&|1-t|\gtrsim\left\|\frac{w}{\|w\|_{L^{p}}}-v\right\|_{p}\gtrsim\|u-\mu v\|_{L^{p}}-4\epsilon\\
\implies{}&\|u-\mu v\|_{L^{p}}\lesssim 4\epsilon+|1-t|\lesssim\epsilon+\epsilon^{1/2}\lesssim\epsilon^{1/2},\end{split}

and we complete our proof.
∎

Proof of Theroem
1.10
: the case
p>2
.

We first prove it for
(a,b)=(-\frac{1}{p-1},0)
.
If
\tilde{\delta}(u)\geq p\delta_{2}
, then

\inf_{v\in\mathcal{M}}\frac{\|u-v\|_{H^{p}}^{p}+(p-1)\|u-v\|_{L^{p}_{-1/(p-1)}}^{p}}{\|u\|_{H^{p}}^{p}+(p-1)\|u\|_{L^{p}_{-1/(p-1)}}^{p}}\leq 1\lesssim\tilde{\delta}(u)^{1/p},

and we are done. Next, assume
\tilde{\delta}(u)<p\delta_{2}
, then
\delta(u)<\delta_{2}
, and
P_{u}\neq\varnothing
. Thus, we can pick

,
\mu=\int|v|^{p-2}vu\geq 0
. Since
\|v\|_{L^{p}}=1
, we may assume
v=v(1,\lambda)
. By the maximality of
v
,

\displaystyle 0={}

\displaystyle\frac{d}{d\lambda}\int|v(1,\lambda)|^{p-2}v(1,\lambda)u

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle(p-1)\int|v|^{p-2}u\partial_{\lambda}v

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle\frac{N(p-1)^{2}}{p^{2}\lambda}\int|v|^{p-2}uv-\frac{(p-1)^{2}}{p}\int|x|^{\frac{p}{p-1}}|v|^{p-2}uv.

Thus,

(3.21)

By (
2.2
), for any

and

By (

\begin{split}\int|\nabla v|^{p-2}\nabla v\cdot\nabla u={}&Sp\lambda^{p-1}\int|v|^{p-2}uv-(p-1)\lambda^{p}\int|x|^{\frac{p}{p-1}}|v|^{p-2}uv\\
={}&Sp\lambda^{p-1}\mu-(p-1)\lambda^{p}\frac{S\mu}{\lambda}=S\lambda^{p-1}\mu.\end{split}

(3.23)

Note that

Write

, then the following orthogonality conditions hold:

Applying (

\displaystyle+\frac{1-\kappa}{2}\bigg(\int p|\nabla\bar{v}|^{p-2}|\nabla u-\nabla\bar{v}|^{2}

\displaystyle\|\nabla\bar{v}\|_{L^{p}}^{p}+c_{0}\|\nabla u-\nabla\bar{v}\|_{L^{p}}^{p}.

(3.26)

Lastly, taking

and integrating over

\displaystyle\|u\|_{L^{p}}^{p}\leq{}

\displaystyle\|\bar{v}\|_{L^{p}}^{p}+p\int|\bar{v}|^{p-2}\bar{v}(u-\bar{v})

\displaystyle+\left(\frac{p(p-1)}{2}+\kappa\right)\int|\bar{v}|^{p-2}|u-\bar{v}|^{2}+c_{1}\|u-\bar{v}\|_{L^{p}}^{p}

\displaystyle\leq{}

\displaystyle\|\bar{v}\|_{L^{p}}^{p}+\left(\frac{p(p-1)}{2}+\kappa\right)\|\bar{v}\|_{L^{p}}^{p-2}\|u-\bar{v}\|_{L^{p}}^{2}+c_{1}\|u-\bar{v}\|_{L^{p}}^{p}.

Now (

\displaystyle\|\nabla u\|_{L^{p}}^{p}+(p-1)\|u\|_{L^{p}_{-1/(p-1)}}^{p}-Sp\|u\|_{L^{p}}^{p}

\displaystyle+c_{0}\Big(\|\nabla u-\nabla\bar{v}\|_{L^{p}}^{p}+(p-1)\|u-\bar{v}\|_{L^{p}_{-1/(p-1)}}^{p}\Big)

\displaystyle-\left(\frac{p(p-1)}{2}+\kappa\right)\|\bar{v}\|_{L^{p}}^{p-2}\|u-\bar{v}\|_{L^{p}}^{2}-c_{1}\|u-\bar{v}\|_{L^{p}}^{p}.

Proof of Theorem

.

Set
\lambda=\frac{\|u\|_{H^{p}_{b}}}{\|u\|_{L^{p}_{a}}}
and let
\tilde{u}(x)=u(\lambda^{-\frac{1}{b+1-a}}x)
, then
\|\tilde{u}\|_{H^{p}_{b}}=\|\tilde{u}\|_{L^{p}_{a}}
, and
\tilde{\delta}_{p,a,b}(\tilde{u})=p\delta_{p,a,b}(u)
. For any
v\in\mathcal{M}_{p,a,b}
,

\displaystyle\frac{\|\tilde{u}-v\|_{H_{b}^{p}}^{p}+(p-1)\|\tilde{u}-v\|_{L^{p}_{a}}^{p}}{\|\tilde{u}\|_{H^{p}_{b}}^{p}+(p-1)\|\tilde{u}\|_{L^{p}_{a}}^{p}}={}

\displaystyle\frac{\lambda^{-p+1}\|u-\tilde{v}\|_{H^{p}_{b}}^{p}+(p-1)\lambda\|u-\tilde{v}\|_{L^{p}_{a}}^{p}}{p\|\tilde{u}\|_{H^{p}_{b}}\|\tilde{u}\|_{L^{p}_{a}}^{p-1}}

\displaystyle\geq{}

\displaystyle\frac{\|u-\tilde{v}\|_{H^{p}_{b}}\|u-\tilde{v}\|_{L^{p}_{a}}^{p-1}}{\|u\|_{H_{b}^{p}}\|u\|_{L^{p}_{a}}^{p-1}},

where
\tilde{v}(x)=v(\lambda^{-\frac{1}{b+1-a}}x)
. By the symmetry of
\mathcal{M}_{p,a,b}
, we know
\tilde{v}\in\mathcal{M}_{p,a,b}
and hence, applying (
1.12
) to
\tilde{u}
we obtain

\inf_{v\in\mathcal{M}_{p,a,b}}\frac{\|u-v\|_{H^{p}_{b}}\|u-v\|_{L^{p}_{a}}^{p-1}}{\|u\|_{H^{p}_{b}}\|u\|_{L^{p}_{a}}^{p-1}}\lesssim\tilde{\delta}_{p,a,b}(\tilde{u})^{1/p}\lesssim\delta_{p,a,b}(u)^{1/p}.\qed

Proof of Theorem
1.11
.

For
p\geq 2
, there exists
m_{p}\in(0,1)
such that

\mathcal{R}_{p}(s,t)\geq m_{p}|s-t|^{p}.

From (
1.6
), when
pb+(p-1)N<0
(i.e.,
\epsilon=-1
), it holds that

\displaystyle\|\Delta u\|_{L_{b}^{p}}\|u\|_{H_{a}^{p}}^{p-1}-K_{p,a,b}\|u\|_{H_{c}^{p}}^{p}

\displaystyle\geq{}\frac{m_{p}}{p}\int\frac{1}{|x|^{pb}}\left|\lambda^{1/p}|x|^{b-a-1}x\cdot\nabla u+\lambda^{-\frac{p-1}{p}\Delta u}\right|^{p}\,dx

\displaystyle={}\frac{m_{p}}{p}\int\frac{\lambda^{1-p}}{|x|^{pb}}e^{-\frac{p\lambda|x|^{b-a+1}}{b-a+1}}\left|\nabla\cdot\left(e^{\frac{\lambda|x|^{b-a+1}}{b-a+1}}\nabla u\right)\right|^{p}\,dx.

When
u
is radial, set
w(r)=r^{-1}e^{\frac{\lambda r^{b-a+1}}{b-a+1}}u^{\prime}(r)
, the divergence term becomes

\nabla\cdot(w(r)x)=rw^{\prime}(r)+Nw(r)=r^{1-N}(r^{N}w(r))^{\prime}.

Thus, we obtain

\displaystyle\|\Delta u\|_{L_{b}^{p}}\|u\|_{H_{a}^{p}}^{p-1}-K_{p,a,b}\|u\|_{H_{c}^{p}}^{p}

\displaystyle\geq{}\frac{m_{p}}{p}\int\frac{\lambda^{1-p}}{|x|^{pb+p(N-1)}}e^{-\frac{p\lambda|x|^{b-a+1}}{b-a+1}}\Big|\nabla(|x|^{N}w(|x|))\Big|^{p}\,dx.

Hence, by setting
\mu=pb+p(N-1)
,
\gamma=b-a+1
,
m=\frac{p}{b-a+1}
and
\tilde{\lambda}=\lambda^{\frac{1}{b-a+1}}

4.1
, it follows that

\displaystyle\|\Delta u\|_{L_{b}^{p}}\|u\|_{H_{a}^{p}}^{p-1}-K_{p,a,b}\|u\|_{H_{c}^{p}}^{p}

\displaystyle\geq{}

\displaystyle C(N,p,a,b)\inf_{c}\int\frac{1}{|x|^{\frac{N\mu}{N-p}}}e^{-\frac{p\lambda|x|^{b-a+1}}{b-a+1}}\Big||x|^{N}w(|x|)-c\Big|^{p}\,dx

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle C(N,p,a,b)\inf_{c}\int\frac{1}{|x|^{\frac{N\mu}{N-p}+(N-1)p}}\Big|\nabla u(x)-c|x|^{1-N}e^{-\frac{\lambda|x|^{b-a+1}}{b-a+1}}\frac{x}{|x|}\Big|^{p}\,dx

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle C(N,p,a,b)\inf_{c}\int\frac{1}{|x|^{\frac{N\mu}{N-p}+(N-1)p}}|\nabla u-\nabla v(c,\lambda)|^{p}\,dx.

By our conditons for
(p,a,b)
