42 U.S.C. § 4335 — Efforts supplemental to existing authorizations
Citation: us:usc:42:4335
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section4335
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 55 (National Environmental Policy); Subchapter I (Policies And Goals). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The policies and goals set forth in this chapter are supplementary to those set forth in existing authorizations of Federal agencies.
