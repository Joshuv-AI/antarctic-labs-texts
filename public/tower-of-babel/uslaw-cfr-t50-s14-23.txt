50 C.F.R. § 14.23 — Live farm-raised fish and farm-raised fish eggs.
Citation: us:cfr:50:14.23
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-14.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 14 (Importation, Exportation, And Transportation Of Wildlife). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Live farm-raised fish and farm-raised fish eggs meet the definition of “bred in captivity” as stated in 50 CFR 17.3. Except for wildlife requiring a permit pursuant to parts 17 or 23 of this subchapter, live farm-raised fish and farm-raised fish eggs may be exported from any U.S. Customs port.
