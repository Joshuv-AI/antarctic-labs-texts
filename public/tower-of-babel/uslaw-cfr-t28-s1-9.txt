28 C.F.R. § 1.9 — Delegation of authority.
Citation: us:cfr:28:1.9
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-1.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 1 (Executive Clemency). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Attorney General may delegate to any officer of the Department of Justice any of his or her duties or responsibilities under §§ 1.1 through 1.8.
