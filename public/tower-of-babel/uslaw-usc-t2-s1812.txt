2 U.S.C. § 1812 — Care and superintendence of Capitol
Citation: us:usc:2:1812
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section1812
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 28 (Architect Of The Capitol); Subchapter II (General Powers And Duties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Architect of the Capitol shall on and after March 3, 1977, have the care and superintendence of the Capitol, including lighting. His office shall be in the Capitol Building.
