40 U.S.C. § 5105 — Assistance to authorities by Capitol employees
Citation: us:usc:40:5105
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section5105
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Part B (United States Capitol); Chapter 51 (United States Capitol Buildings And Grounds). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each individual employed in the service of the Federal Government in the United States Capitol or within the United States Capitol Grounds shall prevent, as far as may be in the individual’s power, a violation of a provision of this chapter or section 9, 9A, 9B, 9C, or 14 of the Act of July 31, 1946 (ch. 707, 60 Stat. 719, 720), and shall aid the police in securing the arrest and conviction of the individual violating the provision.
