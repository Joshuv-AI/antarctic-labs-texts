20 C.F.R. § 61.206 — Reports by employees and dependents.
Citation: us:cfr:20:61.206
Creator: Office Of Workers' Compensation Programs, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-61.206
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 61 (Claims For Compensation Under The War Hazards Compensation Act, As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Office may require a claimant to submit reports of facts materially affecting the claimant's entitlement to compensation under the Act. These may include reports of recurrence or termination of disability, of employment and earnings, or of a change in the marital or dependency status of a beneficiary.
