16 U.S.C. § 3315 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:16:3315
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section3315
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 52 (Salmon And Steelhead Conservation And Enhancement); Subchapter II (Coordinated Management Of Salmon And Steelhead). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated to the Secretary for the purposes of carrying out the provisions of this subchapter in fiscal years commencing after September 30, 1981, an aggregate amount of $3,000,000. Funds appropriated pursuant to this section remain available to the Secretary until expended.
