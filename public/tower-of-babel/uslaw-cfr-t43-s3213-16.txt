43 C.F.R. § 3213.16 — May BLM cancel my lease?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3213.16
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3213.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3200 (Geothermal Resource Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) BLM may cancel your lease if it was issued in error.
(b) If BLM cancels your lease because it was issued in error, the cancellation is effective when you receive it.
