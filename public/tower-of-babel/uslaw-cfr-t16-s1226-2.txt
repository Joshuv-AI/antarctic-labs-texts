16 C.F.R. § 1226.2 — Requirements for soft infant and toddler carriers.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1226.2
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1226.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1226 (Safety Standard For Soft Infant And Toddler Carriers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each soft infant and toddler carrier must comply with all applicable provisions of ASTM F2236-24, Standard Consumer Safety Specification for Soft Infant and Toddler Carriers, approved on July 1, 2024. The Director of the Federal Register approves this incorporation by reference in accordance with 5 U.S.C. 552(a) and 1 CFR part 51.
