48 C.F.R. § 19.202-3 — 19.202-3 Equal low bids.
Citation: us:cfr:48:19.202-3
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-19.202-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 19 (Small Business Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In the event of equal low bids (see 14.408-6), awards shall be made first to small business concerns which are also labor surplus area concerns, and second to small business concerns which are not also labor surplus area concerns.
