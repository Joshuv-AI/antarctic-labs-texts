34 C.F.R. § 101.4 — Suspension of rules.
Citation: us:cfr:34:101.4
Creator: Office For Civil Rights, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-101.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 101 (Practice And Procedure For Hearings Under Part 100 Of This Title). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon notice to all parties, the reviewing authority or the presiding officer, with respect to matters pending before them, may modify or waive any rule in this part upon determination that no party will be unduly prejudiced and the ends of justice will thereby be served.
