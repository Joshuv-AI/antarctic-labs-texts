50 U.S.C. § 4513 — Penalties
Citation: us:usc:50:4513
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section4513
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 55 (Defense Production); Subchapter I (Priorities And Allocations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any person who willfully performs any act prohibited, or willfully fails to perform any act required, by the provisions of this subchapter or any rule, regulation, or order thereunder, shall, upon conviction, be fined not more than $10,000 or imprisoned for not more than one year, or both.
