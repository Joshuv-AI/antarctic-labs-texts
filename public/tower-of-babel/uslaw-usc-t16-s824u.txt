16 U.S.C. § 824u — Prohibition on filing false information
Citation: us:usc:16:824u
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section824u
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 12 (Federal Regulation And Development Of Power); Subchapter II (Regulation Of Electric Utility Companies Engaged In Interstate Commerce). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No entity (including an entity described in section 824(f) of this title) shall willfully and knowingly report any information relating to the price of electricity sold at wholesale or the availability of transmission capacity, which information the person or any other entity knew to be false at the time of the reporting, to a Federal agency with intent to fraudulently affect the data being compiled by the Federal agency.
