33 U.S.C. § 1108 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:33:1108
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section1108
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 22 (Sea Grant Colleges And Marine Science Development); Subchapter I (Marine Resources And Engineering Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are hereby authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary to carry out this subchapter, but sums appropriated for any one fiscal year shall not exceed $1,200,000.
