32 C.F.R. § 21.525 — Who issues policy guidance for the DAADS?
Citation: us:cfr:32:21.525
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-21.525
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 21 (DoD GRANTS AND AGREEMENTS—GENERAL MATTERS). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Principal Deputy Assistant Secretary of Defense for Research and Engineering (PDASD(R&E)), or his or her designee, issues necessary policy guidance for the Defense Assistance Awards Data System.
