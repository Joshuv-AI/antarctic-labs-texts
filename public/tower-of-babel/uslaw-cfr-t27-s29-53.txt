27 C.F.R. § 29.53 — Identification of distilling apparatus.
Citation: us:cfr:27:29.53
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-29.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 29 (Stills And Miscellaneous Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) General. Each still or condenser manufactured will be identified by the manufacturer as follows:
(1) Name of manufacturer.
(2) Address of manufacturer.
(3) Manufacturer's serial number for the apparatus.
(b) Marking requirements. The apparatus will be identified in a legible and durable manner. The required identification marks will be placed on the apparatus in a location where they will not be obscured or concealed.
