15 U.S.C. § 1096 — Registration on supplemental register not used to stop importations
Citation: us:usc:15:1096
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1096
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 22 (Trademarks); Subchapter II (The Supplemental Register). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Registration on the supplemental register or under the Act of March 19, 1920, shall not be filed in the Department of the Treasury or be used to stop importations.
