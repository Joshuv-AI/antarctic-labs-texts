31 C.F.R. § 407.4 — Preservation of property.
Citation: us:cfr:31:407.4
Creator: Secret Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-407.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 407 (Regulations Governing Conduct In The Treasury Building And The Treasury Annex). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person shall, without proper authority, willfully destroy, damage, deface, or remove property or any part thereof, or any furnishings therein.
