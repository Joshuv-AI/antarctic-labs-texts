12 C.F.R. § 407.7 — Relationship to Freedom of Information Act.
Citation: us:cfr:12:407.7
Creator: Export-Import Bank Of The United States
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-407.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 407 (Regulations Governing Public Observation Of Eximbank Meetings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this part expands or limits the present rights of any person under part 404, except that the exemptions contained in § 407.2 shall govern in the case of any request made pursuant to part 404 to copy or inspect the transcripts, recordings or minutes described in § 407.6.
