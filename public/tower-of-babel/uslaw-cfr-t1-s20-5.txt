1 C.F.R. § 20.5 — Sources of information.
Citation: us:cfr:1:20.5
Creator: Administrative Committee Of The Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-1/section-20.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 1 (General Provisions), Part 20 (Handling Of The United States Government Manual Statements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-23 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Pertinent sources of information useful to the public, in areas of public interest such as employment, consumer activities, contracts, services to small business, and other topics of public interest should be provided with each agency statement. These sources of information shall plainly identify the places at which the public may obtain information or make submittals or requests.
