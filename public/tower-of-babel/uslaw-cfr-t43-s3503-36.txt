43 C.F.R. § 3503.36 — Are there any size or shape limitations on the lands I can apply for?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3503.36
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3503.36
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3500 (Leasing Of Solid Minerals Other Than Coal And Oil Shale). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Generally, a quarter-quarter section, a lot or a protraction block is the smallest subdivision for which you may apply. The lands must be in reasonably compact form.
