48 C.F.R. § 1915.106-70 — 1915.106-70 Key personnel and facilities.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1915.106-70
Creator: Chapter 19—Broadcasting Board Of Governors
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1915.106-70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1915 (Contracting By Negotiation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Whenever contractor selection has been substantially predicated on the contractor's possession of special capabilities (i.e., personnel and/or facilities) the contracting officer shall include the clause at 1952.215-70 in the awarded contract.
