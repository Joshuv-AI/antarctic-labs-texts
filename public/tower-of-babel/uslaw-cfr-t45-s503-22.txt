45 C.F.R. § 503.22 — Scope of application.
Citation: us:cfr:45:503.22
Creator: Foreign Claims Settlement Commission Of The United States, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-503.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 503 (Privacy Act And Government In The Sunshine Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this part 503, §§ 503.20 through 503.29, apply to meetings of the Commission, and do not apply to conferences or other gatherings of employees of the Commission who meet or join with others, except at meetings of the Commission to deliberate on or conduct official agency business.
