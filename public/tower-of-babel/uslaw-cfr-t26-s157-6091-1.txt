26 C.F.R. § 157.6091-1 — -1 Place for filing returns.
Citation: us:cfr:26:157.6091-1
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-157.6091-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 157 (Excise Tax On Structured Settlement Factoring Transactions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The return required by § 157.6011-1 (relating to returns of tax with respect to structured settlement factoring transactions) must be filed at the place specified in the forms and instructions provided by the Internal Revenue Service.
