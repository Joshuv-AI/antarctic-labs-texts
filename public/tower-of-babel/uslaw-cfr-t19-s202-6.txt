19 C.F.R. § 202.6 — Reports.
Citation: us:cfr:19:202.6
Creator: United States International Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-202.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 202 (Investigations Of Costs Of Production). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After the completion of its investigation, the Commission will incorporate its findings in a report, and the report will be transmitted to the President.
