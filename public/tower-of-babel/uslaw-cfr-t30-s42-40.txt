30 C.F.R. § 42.40 — Refunds.
Citation: us:cfr:30:42.40
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-42.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 42 (National Mine Health And Safety Academy). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An applicant may withdraw an application and receive a full refund of tuition fees provided that written notification to the Academy's Student Services Branch is mailed no later than 14 days before the course begins.
