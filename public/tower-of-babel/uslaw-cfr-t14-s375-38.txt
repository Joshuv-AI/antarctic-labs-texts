14 C.F.R. § 375.38 — Other foreign civil aircraft: Small unmanned aircraft operated exclusively as model aircraft.
Citation: us:cfr:14:375.38
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-375.38
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 375 (Navigation Of Foreign Civil Aircraft Within The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Foreign civil aircraft that are small unmanned aircraft used exclusively as model aircraft may be operated in the United States only when the individual:
(a) Completes the registration process in accordance with §§ 48.30, 48.100(b) and (c), 48.105, and 48.115 of this title;
(b) Identifies the aircraft in accordance with the aircraft marking requirements in §§ 48.200 and 48.205 of this title; and
(c) Complies with the requirements of Sec. 336 of Pub. L. 112-95 (Feb. 14, 2012).
