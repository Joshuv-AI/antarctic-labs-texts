9 C.F.R. § 147.21 — Sanitation procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:9:147.21
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-147.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 147 (Auxiliary Provisions On National Poultry Improvement Plan). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Sanitation must be maintained in a manner approved by the Administrator. Approved procedures for maintaining sanitation are listed in the NPIP Program Standards, as defined in § 147.51. Sanitation procedures may also be approved by the Administrator in accordance with § 147.53(d)(2).
