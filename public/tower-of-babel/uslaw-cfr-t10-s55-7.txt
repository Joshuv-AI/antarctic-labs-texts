10 C.F.R. § 55.7 — Additional requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:10:55.7
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-55.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 55 (Operators' Licenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission may, by rule, regulation, or order, impose upon any licensee such requirements, in addition to those established in the regulations in this part, as it deems appropriate or necessary to protect health and to minimize danger to life or property.
