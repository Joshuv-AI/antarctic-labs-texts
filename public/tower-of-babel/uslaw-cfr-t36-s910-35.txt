36 C.F.R. § 910.35 — Fine arts.
Citation: us:cfr:36:910.35
Creator: Pennsylvania Avenue Development Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-910.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 910 (General Guidelines And Uniform Standards For Urban Planning And Design Of Development Within The Pennsylvania Avenue Development Area). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Fine arts, including sculpture, paintings, decorative windows, bas-reliefs, ornamental fountains, murals, tapestries, and the like, should be included in each development. PADC encourages commissions for original works of art which are appropriate for the development. For information and guidance, a reasonable expenditure for fine arts is deemed to be one half of one percent of the total construction cost of the development.
