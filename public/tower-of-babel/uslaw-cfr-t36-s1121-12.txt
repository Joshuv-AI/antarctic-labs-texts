36 C.F.R. § 1121.12 — Fees.
Citation: us:cfr:36:1121.12
Creator: Architectural And Transportation Barriers Compliance Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1121.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1121 (Privacy Act Implementation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an individual or authorized representative requests copies of his or her record, he or she shall be charged ten cents per page, excluding the cost of any search for review of the record, in advance of receipt of the pages.
