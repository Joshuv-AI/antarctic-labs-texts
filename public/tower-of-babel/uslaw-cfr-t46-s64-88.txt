46 C.F.R. § 64.88 — Plan approval, construction, and inspection of cargo-handling systems.
Citation: us:cfr:46:64.88
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-64.88
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 64 (Marine Portable Tanks And Cargo Handling Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Plans for the cargo-handling system of a portable tank authorized under subpart 98.30 of this chapter must be approved by the Coast Guard in accordance with the requirements of § 56.01-10 of this subchapter. In addition, the cargo-handling system must be constructed and inspected in accordance with part 56 of this subchapter.
