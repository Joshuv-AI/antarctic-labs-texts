24 C.F.R. § 3280.507 — Comfort heat gain.

Year: 2026

Information necessary to calculate the home cooling load shall be provided as specified in this part.
(a) Transmission heat gains. Homes complying with this section shall meet the minimum heat loss transmission coefficients specified in § 3280.506(a).
