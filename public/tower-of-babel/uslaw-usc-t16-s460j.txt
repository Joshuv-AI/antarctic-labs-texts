16 U.S.C. § 460j — Disposition of proceeds
Citation: us:usc:16:460j
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section460j
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter LXVII (Cottage Site Developments And Uses In Reservoir Areas). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The proceeds from any sale made under this subchapter shall be covered into the Treasury of the United States as miscellaneous receipts.
