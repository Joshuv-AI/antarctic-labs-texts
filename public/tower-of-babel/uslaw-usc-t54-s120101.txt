54 U.S.C. § 120101 — Definition of National Heritage Area
Citation: us:usc:54:120101
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section120101
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 1201 (National Heritage Area System). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter, the term “National Heritage Area” means a component of the National Heritage Area System described in section 120102(b).
