42 C.F.R. § 435.903 — Adherence of local agencies to State plan requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:42:435.903
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-435.903
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 435 (Eligibility In The States, District Of Columbia, The Northern Mariana Islands, And American Samoa). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The agency must—
(a) Have methods to keep itself currently informed of the adherence of local agencies to the State plan provisions and the agency's procedures for determining eligibility; and
(b) Take corrective action to ensure their adherence.
