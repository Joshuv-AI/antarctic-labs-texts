50 U.S.C. § 3040a — Prohibition on collection and maintenance of information of United States persons based on first amendment-protected activities
Citation: us:usc:50:3040a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section3040a
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 44 (National Security); Subchapter I (Coordination For National Security). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No element of the intelligence community may collect or maintain information concerning a United States person (as defined in section 3039 of this title) solely for the purpose of monitoring an activity protected by the first amendment to the Constitution of the United States.
