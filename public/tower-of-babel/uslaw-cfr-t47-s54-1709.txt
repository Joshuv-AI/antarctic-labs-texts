47 C.F.R. § 54.1709 — Availability period of the Emergency Connectivity Fund.
Citation: us:cfr:47:54.1709
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-54.1709
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 54 (Universal Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Emergency Connectivity Fund was established by Congress in the United States Treasury through an appropriation of $7.171 billion, to remain available until September 30, 2030.
