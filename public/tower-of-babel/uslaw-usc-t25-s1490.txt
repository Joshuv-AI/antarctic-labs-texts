25 U.S.C. § 1490 — Maturity of loans
Citation: us:usc:25:1490
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section1490
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 17 (Financing Economic Development Of Indians And Indian Organizations); Subchapter II (Loan Guaranty And Insurance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The maturity of any loan guaranteed or insured hereunder shall not exceed thirty years.
