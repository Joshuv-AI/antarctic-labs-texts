47 C.F.R. § 2.811 — Transmitters operated under part 73 of this chapter.
Citation: us:cfr:47:2.811
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-2.811
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 2 (Frequency Allocations And Radio Treaty Matters; General Rules And Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Section 2.803(a) through (c) shall not be applicable to a transmitter operated in any of the Radio Broadcast Services regulated under part 73 of this chapter, provided the conditions set out in part 73 of this chapter for the acceptability of such transmitter for use under licensing are met.
