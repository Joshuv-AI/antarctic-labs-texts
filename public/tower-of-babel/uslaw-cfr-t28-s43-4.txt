28 C.F.R. § 43.4 — Annual reports.
Citation: us:cfr:28:43.4
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-43.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 43 (Recovery Of Cost Of Hospital And Medical Care And Treatment Furnished By The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The head of each Department or Agency concerned, or his designee, shall report annually to the Attorney General, by March 1, commencing in 1964, the number and dollar amount of claims asserted against, and the number and dollar amount of recoveries from third persons.
