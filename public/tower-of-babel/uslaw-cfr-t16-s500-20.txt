16 C.F.R. § 500.20 — Conspicuousness.
Citation: us:cfr:16:500.20
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-500.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 500 (Regulations Under Section 4 Of The Fair Packaging And Labeling Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The statement of net quantity of contents shall appear in conspicuous and easily legible boldface type or print in distinct contrast (by typography, layout, color, embossing, or molding) to other matter on the package; except that a statement of net quantity blown, embossed, or molded on a glass or plastic surface is permissible when all label information is so formed on the surface.
