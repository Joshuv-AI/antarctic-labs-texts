22 C.F.R. § 705.101 — Cross-reference to employee ethical conduct standards and financial disclosure regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:22:705.101
Creator: Us International Development Finance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-705.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 705 (Employee Ethical Conduct Standards And Financial Disclosure Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees of the US International Development Finance Corporation (DFC) should refer to the executive branch-wide Standards of Ethical Conduct at 5 CFR part 2635, the DFC regulation at 5 CFR 4301.101 which supplements the executive branch-wide standards, and the executive branch-wide financial disclosure regulation at 5 CFR part 2634.
