47 C.F.R. § 74.463 — Modulation requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:47:74.463
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-74.463
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 74 (Experimental Radio, Auxiliary, Special Broadcast And Other Program Distributional Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each new remote pickup broadcast station authorized to operate with a power output in excess of 3 watts shall be equipped with a device which will automatically prevent modulation in excess of the limits set forth in this subpart.
(b) If amplitude modulation is employed, modulation shall not exceed 100 percent on negative peaks.
(c) If frequency modulation is employed, emission shall conform to the requirements specified in § 74.462.
