16 C.F.R. § 801.3 — Activities in or affecting commerce.
Citation: us:cfr:16:801.3
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-801.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 801 (Coverage Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Section 7A(a)(1) is satisfied if any entity included within the acquiring person, or any entity included within the acquired person, is engaged in commerce or in any activity affecting commerce.
