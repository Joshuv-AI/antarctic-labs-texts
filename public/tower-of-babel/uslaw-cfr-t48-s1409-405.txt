48 C.F.R. § 1409.405 — 1409.405 Effect of listing.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1409 (Contractor Qualifications). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When a CO finds that a compelling reason exists to conduct business with a contractor listed on the EPLS, the HCA shall submit the determination and findings to the Director, PAM, for approval.
