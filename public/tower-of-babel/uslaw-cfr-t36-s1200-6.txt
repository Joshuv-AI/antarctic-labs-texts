36 C.F.R. § 1200.6 — Who is authorized to apply the official seals on documents or other materials?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1200.6
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1200.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1200 (Official Seals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Archivist of the United States (and the Archivist's designee) is the only individual authorized to apply NARA official seals, embossing seals, and replicas and reproductions of seals to appropriate documents, authentications, and other material. NARA accepts requests to use the official seals and approves or denies them based on the criteria identified in § 1200.10.
