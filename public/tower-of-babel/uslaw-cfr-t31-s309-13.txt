31 C.F.R. § 309.13 — Functions of Federal Reserve Banks.
Citation: us:cfr:31:309.13
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-309.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 309 (Issue And Sale Of Treasury Bills). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Federal Reserve Banks and Branches, as fiscal agents of the United States, are authorized to perform all such acts as may be necessary to carry out the provisions of this circular and of any public notice or notices issued in connection with any offering of Treasury bills.
