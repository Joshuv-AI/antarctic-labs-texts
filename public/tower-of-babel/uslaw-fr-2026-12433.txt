Proposed Extension of a Currently Approved Collection: 
Certification of Funeral Expenses Under the Longshore and Harbor 
Workers' Compensation Act; Correction

AGENCY: Office of Workers' Compensation Programs, Division of Longshore 
and Harbor Workers' Compensation (OWCP/DLHWC), Department of Labor.

ACTION: Notice; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This is a correction of a notice published June 9, 2026 which 
in error referenced Administration of the Longshore and Harbor Workers' 
Compensation Act. The collection of information is for Certification of 
Funeral Expenses (LS-265).

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Anjanette Suggs by telephone at 202- 
354-9660 or by email at [email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

Correction

    In the Federal Register of June 9, 2026, in FR Doc. 2026-11496, 
make the following corrections:
    1. On page 34837, in the first column, in the first sentence of the 
SUMMARY preamble caption, correct the information collection request 
title to read ``Certification of Funeral Expenses under the Longshore 
and Harbor Workers' Compensation Act (LS-265)''.
    2. On page 34838, in the first column, under ``Title of 
Collection:'', correct the title to read, ``Certification of Funeral 
Expenses under the Longshore and Harbor Workers' Compensation Act (LS-
265)''.

(Authority: 33 U.S.C. 909(a) 913 and 937)
