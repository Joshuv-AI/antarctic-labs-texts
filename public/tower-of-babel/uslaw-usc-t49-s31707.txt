49 U.S.C. § 31707 — Limitations on statutory construction
Citation: us:usc:49:31707
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section31707
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part B (Commercial); Chapter 317 (Participation In International Registration Plan And International Fuel Tax Agreement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Sections 31704 and 31705 of this title do not limit the amount of money a State may charge for registration of a commercial motor vehicle or the amount of any fuel use tax a State may impose.
