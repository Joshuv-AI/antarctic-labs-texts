30 U.S.C. § 1541 — Use of geothermal energy in Federal facilities
Citation: us:usc:30:1541
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section1541
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 27 (Geothermal Energy); Subchapter IV (Federal Facilities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The option of using geothermal energy or geothermal energy resources shall be considered fully in any new Federal building, facility, or installation which is located in a geothermal resource area as designated by the Secretary.
