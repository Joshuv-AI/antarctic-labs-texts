32 C.F.R. § 37.815 — May the Government withhold payments?
Citation: us:cfr:32:37.815
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-37.815
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 37 (Technology Investment Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Your TIA must provide that the administrative agreements officer may withhold payments in the circumstances described in 32 CFR 34.12(g), but not otherwise.
