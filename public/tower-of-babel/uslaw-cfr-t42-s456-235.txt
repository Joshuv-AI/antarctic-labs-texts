42 C.F.R. § 456.235 — Description of methods and criteria: Continued stay review dates; length of stay modification.
Citation: us:cfr:42:456.235
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-456.235
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 456 (Utilization Control). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The UR plan must describe—
(a) The methods and criteria, including norms if used, that the committee uses to assign initial and subsequent continued stay review dates under §§ 456.233 and 456.234 of this subpart; and
(b) The methods that the committee uses to modify an approved length of stay when the beneficiary's condition or treatment schedule changes.
