18 C.F.R. § 34.9 — Reports.
Citation: us:cfr:18:34.9
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-34.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 34 (Application For Authorization Of The Issuance Of Securities Or The Assumption Of Liabilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The applicant must file reports under § 131.43 and § 131.50 of this chapter no later than 30 days after the sale or placement of long-term debt or equity securities or the entry into guarantees or assumptions of liabilities pursuant to authority granted under this part.
