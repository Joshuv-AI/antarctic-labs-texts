50 U.S.C. § 1 — Creation, purpose, and composition of council
Citation: us:usc:50:1
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section1
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 1 (Council Of National Defense). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A Council of National Defense is established, for the coordination of industries and resources for the national security and welfare, to consist of the Secretary of the Army, the Secretary of the Navy, the Secretary of the Interior, the Secretary of Agriculture, the Secretary of Commerce, and the Secretary of Labor.
