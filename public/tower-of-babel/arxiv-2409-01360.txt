Exceptional zeros for Heegner points and p p -converse to the theorem of Gross–Zagier and Kolyvagin
Francesc Castella

Abstract
. We prove a p p -converse to the theorem of Gross–Zagier and Kolyvagin for elliptic curves E / 𝐐 E/\mathbf{Q} at primes p > 3 p>3 of multiplicative reduction. Two key ingredients in the argument are an extension to this setting of a p p -adic formula of Bertolini–Darmon–Prasanna obtained in our earlier work [ Cas18a ] , and an exceptional zero formula for Heegner points. By independent approaches different from ours, a similar p p -converse theorem was obtained by Skinner–Zhang [ SZ14 ] under additional ramification hypotheses on E ⁡ [ p ] E[p] , and by Venerucci [ Ven16b ] assuming finiteness of Ш ​ ( E / 𝐐 ) ​ [ p ∞ ] \Sha(E/\mathbf{Q})[p^{\infty}] .



1.
Introduction

1.1.
Main results

The main result of this note is the following
p
-converse to the theorem of Gross–Zagier and Kolyvagin for rational elliptic curves at primes
p
of multiplicative reduction.

Theorem 1.1
.

Let
E/\mathbf{Q}
be an elliptic curve with multiplicative reduction at the prime
p>3
. Assume that

(i)

E[p]
is irreducible as a
G_{\mathbf{Q}}:={\rm Gal}(\overline{\mathbf{Q}}/\mathbf{Q})
-module,

(ii)

E
has nonsplit multiplicative reduction at some prime
q\neq p
where
E[p]
is ramified,

(iii)

E(\mathbf{Q}_{p})[p]=0
.

Then

{\rm corank}_{\mathbf{Z}_{p}}{\rm Sel}_{p^{\infty}}(E/\mathbf{Q})=1\quad\Longrightarrow\quad{\rm ord}_{s=1}L(E,s)=1,

and so
{\rm rank}_{\mathbf{Z}}E(\mathbf{Q})=1
and
\#\Sha(E/\mathbf{Q})<\infty
.

As an important application, Theorem
1.1
allows us to extend the reach of
p
-adic conditions under which an elliptic curve
E
over
\mathbf{Q}
can be shown to have algebraic and analytic rank one (and hence to satisfy the Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer conjecture).

Corollary 1.2
.

Let
E/\mathbf{Q}
be an elliptic curve with multiplicative reduction at the prime
p>3
satisfying conditions (i)–(iii) in Theorem
1.1
. If the
p
-Selmer group
{\rm Sel}_{p}(E/\mathbf{Q})\subset{\rm H}^{1}(\mathbf{Q},E[p])
has order
p
, then

{\rm rank}_{\mathbf{Z}}E(\mathbf{Q})={\rm ord}_{s=1}L(E,s)=1

and
\#\Sha(E/\mathbf{Q})<\infty
; in particular, the Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer conjecture holds for
E
.

Proof.

This is immediate from Theorem
1.1
and the existence of the alternating Cassels–Tate pairing on the quotient of
{\rm Sel}_{p^{\infty}}(E/\mathbf{Q})
by its maximal divisible subgroup (see
[
BSZ14
, Thm. 9]
for details, and note that the argument also shows
\Sha(E/\mathbf{Q})[p^{\infty}]=0
).
∎

Remark
.

Conversely, under the hypotheses of Corollary
1.2
(in fact, just assuming
E(\mathbf{Q})[p]=0
), condition
{\rm Sel}_{p}(E/\mathbf{Q})\simeq\mathbf{Z}/p\mathbf{Z}
is necessary for the conclusion
of that result to hold, as one sees immediately from the
p
-descent exact sequence

0\rightarrow E(\mathbf{Q})/pE(\mathbf{Q})\rightarrow{\rm Sel}_{p}(E/\mathbf{Q})\rightarrow\Sha(E/\mathbf{Q})[p]\rightarrow 0.

We deduce Theorem
1.1
from the proof of a new variant of Perrin-Riou’s Heegner point main conjecture
[
PR87
]
reflecting the presence of “exceptional zeros” (in the sense of
[

]

Let

be an imaginary quadratic field of odd discriminant

\textrm{there exists an ideal $\mathfrak{N}\subset\mathcal{O}_{K}$ with $\mathcal{O}_{K}/\mathfrak{N}\simeq\mathbf{Z}/N\mathbf{Z}$}.

Let

be the anticyclotomic
\mathbf{Z}_{p}
-extension of

, let

, and put

where the limits are with respect to the corestriction and the multiplication-by-

, whose existence is granted by
[
BCDT01
]

give rise a collection of Heegner points

defined over the ring class field

of

Let

From the norm relations satisfied by the points

-adic class

These same norm relations imply, in the case where

We can then show that existence of a “derived” Heegner class

\Lambda

Theorem 1.3
.

Let

be an elliptic curve with multiplicative reduction at the prime

, and let

-D_{K}<-4

If

.

1.2.
Relation to previous works

Earlier results on the
p
-converse to the theorem of Gross–Zagier and Kolyvagin for multiplicative primes are due to Skinner–Zhang
[
SZ14
]
and independently Venerucci
[
Ven16b
]
, both obtained by methods markedly different from ours. The result of
[
SZ14
]
is deduced as a consequence of their proof of Kolyvagin’s nonvanishing conjecture for multiplicative primes
p
, and it requires additional ramification hypotheses on
E[p]
arising from the proof of that result; while the main result of
[
Ven16b
]
is subject to the hypothesis that
\#\Sha(E/\mathbf{Q})[p^{\infty}]<\infty
(but, compared to Theorem
1.1
, allows both

, and
E
to have split multiplicative reduction at the prime
q\|N
, with

, where
E[p]
is required to ramify).

We also refer the reader to
[
Büy12
]
,
[
RR21
,
Riv23
]
, and
[
Büy16
,
Ven16a
]
for other studies of “derivative” classes in the presence of exceptional zeros (for circular units, Rankin–Eisenstein classes, and Beilinson–Kato elements, respectively). In the spirit of in this note, it would be interesting to study the interplay between the results in those settings and the corresponding Iwasawa Main Conjectures.

2.
Exceptional zeros for Heegner points

In this section we study the behavior of
\Lambda
-adic systems of Heegner classes in the presence of “exceptional zeros”. The key new ingredient is the explicit reciprocity law of Theorem
2.2
in the split multiplicative case, involving a certain “derived”
\Lambda
-adic Heegner class.

The results in this section apply more generally to newforms of weight
2
, but for simplicity we shall restrict ourselves the case of rational elliptic curves.

2.1.
Setting

Let
E/\mathbf{Q}
be an elliptic curve of conductor
N
, let
f\in S_{2}(\Gamma_{0}(N))
be the newform associated to
E
, and let
p
be a prime of multiplicative reduction for
E
, so
p\|N
. Throughout we assume that
p>3
, but it should be noted that many of our arguments apply to any odd prime
p
.

Let
K
be an imaginary quadratic field of odd discriminant
-D_{K}<-4
and ring of integers
\mathcal{O}_{K}
such that (
Heeg
) holds.
In particular, the prime
p
splits in
K
, say

p=\mathfrak{p}\overline{\mathfrak{p}},

with
\mathfrak{p}
the prime of
K
above
p
induced by a fixed embedding
\imath_{p}:\overline{\mathbf{Q}}\hookrightarrow\overline{\mathbf{Q}}_{p}
. Fix once and for all an ideal
\mathfrak{N}
as in (
Heeg
) with
\mathfrak{p}\mid\mathfrak{N}
.

2.2.
Derived Heegner points

Let
K_{\infty}
be the anticyclotomic
\mathbf{Z}_{p}
-extension of
K
, and put

\Gamma={\rm Gal}(K_{\infty}/K)\simeq\mathbf{Z}_{p},\quad\quad\Lambda=\mathbf{Z}_{p}[\![\Gamma]\!].

Let
\alpha
be the
p
-th Fourier coefficient of
f
(so
\alpha=1
or
-1
according to whether
E
has split or nonsplit multiplicative reduction at
p
). For each
m
, let
H_{p^{m}}
denote the ring class field of
K
of conductor
p^{m}
, and put
H_{p^{\infty}}=\bigcup_{m>0}H_{p^{m}}
, which contains
K_{\infty}
with finite index.

Following
[
BD96
, §2.5]
, define the
regularized Heegner point

z_{m}\in E(H_{p^{m}})\otimes\mathbf{Z}_{p}
by

(2.1)

z_{m}:=\begin{cases}\alpha^{-m}\cdot y_{p^{m}}&\textrm{if $m>0$,}\\[1.99997pt]
u^{-1}(1-\alpha^{-1}\sigma)\cdot y_{1}&\textrm{if $m=0$,}\end{cases}

where
u:=\frac{1}{2}\#\mathcal{O}_{K}^{\times}
and
\sigma\in{\rm Gal}(H_{1}/K)
. A direct calculation using the norm relations satisfied by the points
y_{m}
(see [
op. cit.
, §2.4]) shows that the points
z_{m}
are norm-compatible. Thus taking their images under the Kummer map
E(H_{p^{m}})\otimes\mathbf{Z}_{p}\rightarrow{\rm Sel}(H_{m},T)
, where
T=\varprojlim_{r}E[p^{r}]
denotes the
p
-adic Tate module of
E
, we obtain the compatible family of cohomology classes

\mathbf{z}_{\infty}:=\{z_{m}\}_{m}\in\varprojlim_{m}{\rm Sel}(H_{p^{m}},T).

With a slight abuse of notation, we continue to denote by
\mathbf{z}_{\infty}
its natural image in
\check{S}_{p}
. On the other hand, put

\kappa_{f}:={\rm cor}_{H_{1}/K}(y_{0})\in{\rm Sel}(K,T),

and note that the Gross–Zagier formula
[
GZ86
]
yields the equivalence

(2.2)

\kappa_{f}\not\in{\rm Sel}(K,T)_{\rm tors}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad{\rm ord}_{s=1}L(E/K,s)=1,

where
{\rm Sel}(K,T)_{\rm tors}
denotes the torsion submodule of
{\rm Sel}(K,T)
.

Given a class
\mathfrak{Z}_{\infty}
in the kernel of the specialization map

{\rm pr}_{0}:\check{S}_{p}\rightarrow{\rm Sel}(K,T),

we refer the reader to
[
Cas18a
, Lem. 3.10]
for the definition of the “derivative” class
\mathfrak{Z}_{\infty}^{\prime}\in\check{S}_{p}
satisfying the relation

(2.3)

\mathfrak{Z}_{\infty}=(\gamma-1)\mathfrak{Z}_{\infty}^{\prime}

for
\gamma\in\Gamma
any topological generator. When
E(K)[p]=0
(so the
\Lambda
-module
\check{S}_{p}
is torsion-free),
\mathfrak{Z}_{\infty}^{\prime}
is uniquely determined by (
2.3
). We also note that letting
\kappa:\Gamma\mapsto 1+p\mathbf{Z}_{p}
be any fixed isomorphism, the (integral) normalization
p{\rm log}_{p}(\kappa(\gamma))^{-1}\mathfrak{Z}_{\infty}^{\prime}
is independent of
\gamma
.

A key role in this note will be played by the following “exceptional zero formula” for
\mathbf{z}_{\infty}
.

Theorem 2.1
.

Suppose
E
has split multiplicative reduction at
p
, and that
E(K)[p]=0
. Then
{\rm pr}_{0}(\mathbf{z}_{\infty})=0
,
and its derivative
\mathbf{z}_{\infty}^{\prime}\in\check{S}_{p}
is such that

{\rm pr}_{0}(\mathbf{z}_{\infty}^{\prime})=\mathscr{L}_{\mathfrak{p}}(f,K)\cdot\kappa_{f}

in
{\rm Sel}(K,T[1/p])
, where
\mathscr{L}_{\mathfrak{p}}(f,K)\in\mathbf{Q}_{p}
.

Proof.

By definition (
2.1
), we see that

\displaystyle{\rm pr}_{0}(\mathbf{z}_{\infty})={\rm cor}_{H_{1}/K}(z_{0})

\displaystyle=u^{-1}\sum_{\tau}y_{1}^{\tau}-u^{-1}\alpha^{-1}\sum_{\tau}y_{1}^{\sigma\tau}

\displaystyle=u^{-1}(1-\alpha^{-1})\sum_{\tau}y_{1}^{\tau},

where
\tau
runs over the elements in
{\rm Gal}(H_{1}/K)
. Since
\alpha=1
, this shows the first assertion.

The second assertion follows from a result of S. Molina
[
Mol19
, Thm. 7.5]
(see also Theorem 4.2.5.4 in
[
Dis20
]
for a reformulation closest to ours).
∎

Remark
.

The constant
\mathscr{L}_{\mathfrak{p}}(f,K)
appearing in Theorem
2.1
is a natural
\mathscr{L}
-invariant
attached to
E
and the anticyclotomic
\mathbf{Z}_{p}
-extension
K_{\infty}/K
(see
[
Cas18a
, (3.9)]
).
Moreover, as shown in
[
Dis20
]
, Theorem
2.1
can be recast as a special case of the conjectures of Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer type formulated by Bertolini–Darmon
[
BD96
]
in the case of rank one.

2.3.
Explicit reciprocity law

For
g\in S_{2r}(\Gamma_{0}(N_{g}))
a
p
-ordinary newform of weight
2r\geq 2
and level
N_{g}

coprime to
p
, one of the main results of
[
CH18
]
is an “explicit reciprocity law” expressing the
p
-adic
L
-function
L_{\mathfrak{p}}(g)
of Bertolini–Darmon–Prasanna
[
BDP13
]
as the image of a
\Lambda
-adic class (constructed from the
p
-adic étale Abel–Jacobi image of generalized Heegner cycles) under a Perrin-Riou big logarithm map. An extension to the multiplicative case of the
p
-adic Abel–Jacobi image computations of
[
BDP13
]
was obtained in
[
Cas18a
]
; in this section we deduce from this a corresponding extension of the aforementioned result from
[
CH18
]
.

Put

\mathbf{T}:=T\hat{\otimes}_{\mathbf{Z}_{p}}\Lambda

equipped with the diagonal
G_{K}
-action, where
G_{K}
acts on
\Lambda
through the inverse of the character
G_{K}\twoheadrightarrow\Gamma\hookrightarrow\Lambda^{\times}
. By Tate’s uniformization, the Tate module

fits into a
G_{\mathbf{Q}_{p}}
-equivariant short exact sequence

0\rightarrow\mathscr{F}^{+}T\rightarrow T\rightarrow\mathscr{F}^{-}T\rightarrow 0

with
\mathscr{F}^{\pm}T
free of rank
1
over
\mathbf{Z}_{p}
, and with the
G_{\mathbf{Q}_{p}}
-action on
\mathscr{F}^{-}T
given by the unramified character
{\rm unr}(\alpha)
sending a Frobenius element
{\rm Fr}_{p}\in G_{\mathbf{Q}_{p}}/I_{p}
to
\alpha
(and so
G_{\mathbf{Q}_{p}}
acts on
\mathscr{F}^{+}T
via
{\rm unr}(\alpha)^{-1}\varepsilon_{\rm cyc}
for the
p
-adic cyclotomic character
\varepsilon_{\rm cyc}
.). Put also

\mathscr{F}^{\pm}\mathbf{T}:=\mathscr{F}^{\pm}T\hat{\otimes}_{\mathbf{Z}_{p}}\Lambda.

Let
R_{0}=\hat{\mathbf{Z}}_{p}^{\rm nr}
denote the completion of the ring of integers of the maximal unramified extension of
\mathbf{Q}_{p}
, set
\Lambda_{R_{0}}=\Lambda\hat{\otimes}_{\mathbf{Z}_{p}}R_{0}
, and denote by

L_{\mathfrak{p}}(f)\in\Lambda_{R_{0}}

the
p
-adic Rankin
L
-function of Bertolini–Darmon–Prasanna
[
BDP13
]
, as extended in
[
Hsi14
,
Cas18a
]
to the
p
-multiplicative case.

2.3.1.
Split multiplicative case

It follows from its geometric construction, that
the image of
\mathbf{z}_{\infty}
under the restriction

(2.4)

{\rm loc}_{\mathfrak{p}}:\check{S}_{p}\rightarrow{\rm H}^{1}(K_{\mathfrak{p}},\mathbf{T})

lands in the image of the natural map
{\rm H}^{1}(K_{\mathfrak{p}},\mathscr{F}^{+}\mathbf{T})\rightarrow{\rm H}^{1}(K_{\mathfrak{p}},\mathbf{T})
(cf.
[
CH18
, p. 604]
), which is easily seen to be an injection (see e.g.
[
How07
, Lem. 2.4.4]
). In the following, we shall often write
\mathbf{Q}_{p}
and

interchangeably.

The next is the key new result in this note.

Theorem 2.2
.

Assume that
E
has split multiplicative reduction at
p
. Then there is a
\Lambda
-linear isomorphism

\tilde{\mathcal{L}}_{\mathfrak{p}}:{\rm H}^{1}(K_{\mathfrak{p}},\mathscr{F}^{+}\mathbf{T})\hat{\otimes}_{\mathbf{Z}_{p}}R_{0}\rightarrow\Lambda_{R_{0}}

such that

\tilde{\mathcal{L}}_{\mathfrak{p}}({\rm loc}_{\mathfrak{p}}(\mathbf{z}_{\infty}^{\prime}))=L_{\mathfrak{p}}(f)

up to a
p
-adic unit.

Proof.

For a (typically infinite) Galois extension
E_{\infty}
of
\mathbf{Q}_{p}
with Galois group
\mathcal{G}={\rm Gal}(E_{\infty}/\mathbf{Q}_{p})
, we denote by
\Lambda_{\mathcal{G}}^{\#}
the Iwasawa algebra
\mathbf{Z}_{p}[\![\mathcal{G}]\!]
equipped with the Galois action by the inverse of the tautological character

G_{\mathbf{Q}_{p}}\twoheadrightarrow\mathcal{G}\hookrightarrow\Lambda_{\mathcal{G}}^{\times};

and for any abelian extension
F
of
K
, let
F_{\mathfrak{p}}
denote the completion of
F
at the prime above
\mathfrak{p}
induced by our fixed embedding
\imath_{p}:\overline{\mathbf{Q}}\hookrightarrow\overline{\mathbf{Q}}_{p}
.

It follows easily from local class field theory, that
K_{\infty,\mathfrak{p}}
is contained in the
\mathbf{Z}_{p}^{\times}
-extension
F_{\infty}=\bigcup_{n\geq 0}F(\mathcal{F}[p^{n}])

Col79

Put

\mathcal{G}={\rm Gal}(F_{\infty}/F),\quad\quad\tilde{\mathcal{G}}={\rm Gal}(F_{\infty}/\mathbf{Q}_{p}).

As in
[
KLZ17

Let
\hat{\mathbf{Q}}_{p}^{\rm ur}=R_{0}[1/p]

, and let

-linear map

(2.5)

{\rm Col}_{\mathcal{T}}:{\rm H}^{1}(\mathbf{Q}_{p},\mathcal{T}(1)\hat{\otimes}_{\mathbf{Z}_{p}}\Lambda_{\tilde{\mathcal{G}}}^{\#})\hat{\otimes}_{\mathbf{Z}_{p}}R_{0}\rightarrow S^{-1}\Lambda_{\tilde{\mathcal{G}}}\hat{\otimes}_{\mathbf{Z}_{p}}\mathbf{D}(\mathcal{T})\hat{\otimes}_{\mathbf{Z}_{p}}R_{0},

where

generated by the elements in the kernel of the augmentation map

\mathcal{T}=\mathscr{F}^{+}T(-1),

(2.7)

\omega_{f}:\mathbf{D}(\mathcal{T})\rightarrow\mathbf{Z}_{p}

[
KLZ17

). Put

, and note that

\mathcal{T}(1)\simeq\mathbf{Z}_{p}({\rm unr}(\alpha)).

Hence from local Tate duality we see that corestriction for

{\rm H}^{1}(\mathbf{Q}_{p},\mathcal{T}(1)\hat{\otimes}_{\mathbf{Z}_{p}}\Lambda_{\tilde{\mathcal{G}}}^{\#})\otimes_{\Lambda_{\tilde{\mathcal{G}}}}\Lambda_{\Gamma_{\mathfrak{p}}}\simeq{\rm H}^{1}(\mathbf{Q}_{p},\mathcal{T}(1)\hat{\otimes}_{\mathbf{Z}_{p}}\Lambda_{\Gamma_{\mathfrak{p}}}^{\#}),

2.5
) and (

-linear map

\bigl\langle{\rm Col}_{\mathcal{T}}(-),\omega_{f}\bigr\rangle:{\rm H}^{1}(\mathbf{Q}_{p},\mathcal{T}(1)\hat{\otimes}_{\mathbf{Z}_{p}}\Lambda_{\Gamma_{\mathfrak{p}}}^{\#})\hat{\otimes}_{\mathbf{Z}_{p}}R_{0}\rightarrow(\gamma_{\mathfrak{p}}-1)^{-1}\Lambda_{\Gamma_{\mathfrak{p}}}\hat{\otimes}_{\mathbf{Z}_{p}}R_{0},

where
\gamma_{\mathfrak{p}}\in\Gamma_{\mathfrak{p}}

over

noting the isomorphism

as

It follows from the same argument as in

that

{\rm image}(\mathcal{L}_{\mathfrak{p}}^{(1)})=\begin{cases}(\gamma-1)^{-1}\Lambda_{R_{0}}&\textrm{if $\alpha=1$,}\\[1.99997pt]
\Lambda_{R_{0}}&\textrm{if $\alpha=-1$}.\end{cases}

Moreover, by construction for any
\mathbf{z}\in{\rm H}^{1}(K_{\mathfrak{p}},\mathscr{F}^{+}\mathbf{T})
the Laurent series
\mathcal{L}_{\mathfrak{p}}^{(1)}(\mathbf{z})\in(\gamma-1)^{-1}\Lambda_{R_{0}}
interpolates (a branch determined by
\omega_{f}
of) the Bloch–Kato logarithm and dual exponential maps over specializations of
\mathbf{z}
by characters of
\Gamma
(cf.
[
CH22
, Thm. 3.4]
; for
\epsilon

\mathbf{Z}_{p}
-basis of the
p

\mathcal{F}

op. cit.

Since

, but replacing the appeal in [

]
to the multiplicative case, we see that (

) sends the localized
\Lambda
-adic Heegner class

to

up to a

-adic unit. Hence the
\Lambda
-linear map

\mathcal{L}_{\mathfrak{p}}^{(2)}:=(\gamma-1)\cdot\mathcal{L}^{(1)}_{\mathfrak{p}}

is injective with image equal to

, and satisfies

\mathcal{L}_{\mathfrak{p}}^{(2)}({\rm loc}_{\mathfrak{p}}(\mathbf{z}_{\infty}))=(\gamma-1)\cdot L_{\mathfrak{p}}(f)

up to a

Since

3.
Iwasawa theory

We keep the setting of §
2.1
, and let
\mathbf{z}_{\infty}^{*}\in\check{S}_{p}
be the
\Lambda
-adic Heegner class defined by

\mathbf{z}_{\infty}^{*}:=\begin{cases}\mathbf{z}_{\infty}^{\prime}&\textrm{if $E$ has split multiplicative reduction at $p$,}\\[1.99997pt]
\mathbf{z}_{\infty}&\textrm{otherwise.}\end{cases}

In this section we describe the two ingredients we need from Iwasawa theory: some results on variants of the anticyclotomic Iwasawa Main Conjecture for
E/K
(one in terms of
L_{\mathfrak{p}}(f)
, and another in terms
\mathbf{z}_{\infty}^{*}
, which is new to this paper),
and a variant of Mazur’s control theorem.

3.1.
Anticyclotomic Main Conjectures

Similarly as in
[
JSW17
, §2.3]
and
[
Cas18a
, §2.1]
, define the anticyclotomic Selmer group

{\rm Sel}_{\mathfrak{p}}(K_{\infty},E[p^{\infty}]):={\rm ker}\biggl\{{\rm H}^{1}(K_{\infty},E[p^{\infty}])\rightarrow\prod_{w}\frac{{\rm H}^{1}(K_{\infty,w},E[p^{\infty}])}{{\rm H}_{\mathfrak{p}}^{1}(K_{\infty,w},E[p^{\infty}])}\biggr\},

where
w
runs over all finite primes of
K_{\infty}
, and we put

{\rm H}_{\mathfrak{p}}^{1}(K_{\infty,w},E[p^{\infty}]):=\begin{cases}{\rm H}^{1}(K_{\infty,w},E[p^{\infty}])&\textrm{if $w\mid\overline{\mathfrak{p}}$,}\\[1.99997pt]
0&\textrm{otherwise.}\end{cases}

Write
X_{\mathfrak{p}}
for the Pontryagin dual of
{\rm Sel}_{\mathfrak{p}}(K_{\infty},E[p^{\infty}])
. The following is one of the main results of
[
Cas18b
]
, in the final form given in
[
Cas24
]
.

Theorem 3.1
.

Assume that:

(i)

E[p]
is irreducible as a
G_{\mathbf{Q}}
-module,

(ii)

If
2
is nonsplit in
K
, then
2\|N
,

(iii)

E
has nonsplit multiplicative reduction at each prime
q\|N
which is nonsplit in
K
, and that there is at least one such prime
q
at which
E[p]
is ramified,

(iv)

E(\mathbf{Q}_{p})[p]=0
.

Then
X_{\mathfrak{p}}
is
\Lambda
-torsion and

{\rm char}_{\Lambda}(X_{\mathfrak{p}})=(L_{\mathfrak{p}}(f))

as ideals in
\Lambda_{R_{0}}
.

Proof.

This is
[
Cas18b
, Thm. 4.4]
,
in the final form given in
[
Cas24
, Thm. 1.1]
.
∎

As is well-known in Iwasawa theory, given an “explicit reciprocity law” expressing
L_{\mathfrak{p}}(f)
as the image under a Perrin-Riou big logarithm map of a
\Lambda
-adic system of cohomology classes
\mathfrak{Z}_{\infty}
, the main conjecture for
L_{\mathfrak{p}}(f)
can be alternatively stated (in favorable circumstances) in the form of a main conjecture without reference to
p
-adic
L
-functions (but rather
\mathfrak{Z}_{\infty}
instead) in the spirit of
[
PR87
, Conj. B]
. Here we explain the form this takes in our setting.

Put
\mathscr{F}^{+}E[p^{\infty}]:=\mathscr{F}^{+}T\otimes\mathbf{Q}_{p}/\mathbf{Z}_{p}
, which defines a
G_{\mathbf{Q}_{p}}
-invariant submodule of
E[p^{\infty}]
under the isomorphism
E[p^{\infty}]\simeq T\otimes\mathbf{Q}_{p}/\mathbf{Z}_{p}
.
Following
[
Gre89
,
Gre94
]
, define

\mathfrak{Sel}_{\rm Gr}(K_{\infty},E[p^{\infty}]):={\rm ker}\biggl\{{\rm H}^{1}(K_{\infty},E[p^{\infty}])\rightarrow\prod_{w\nmid p}{\rm H}^{1}(K_{\infty,w},E[p^{\infty}])\times\prod_{w\mid p}\frac{{\rm H}^{1}(K_{\infty,w},E[p^{\infty}])}{{\rm H}_{\rm Gr}^{1}(K_{\infty,w},E[p^{\infty}])}\biggr\},

where for
w\mid p
we put

{\rm H}^{1}_{\rm Gr}(K_{\infty,w},E[p^{\infty}]):={\rm ker}\bigl\{{\rm H}^{1}(K_{\infty,w},E[p^{\infty}])\rightarrow{\rm H}^{1}(I_{w},E[p^{\infty}]/\mathscr{F}^{+}E[p^{\infty}])\bigr\}

with
I_{w}\subset G_{K_{\infty,w}}
a decomposition group at
w
; and define the
strict Selmer group
by

{\rm Sel}_{\rm str}(K_{\infty},E[p^{\infty}]):={\rm ker}\biggl\{{\rm H}^{1}(K_{\infty},E[p^{\infty}])\rightarrow\prod_{w\nmid p}{\rm H}^{1}(K_{\infty,w},E[p^{\infty}])\times\prod_{w\mid p}\frac{{\rm H}^{1}(K_{\infty,w},E[p^{\infty}])}{{\rm H}_{\rm str}^{1}(K_{\infty,w},E[p^{\infty}])}\biggr\},

where for
w\mid p
we put

{\rm H}^{1}_{\rm str}(K_{\infty,w},E[p^{\infty}]):={\rm ker}\{{\rm H}^{1}(K_{\infty,w},E[p^{\infty}])\rightarrow{\rm H}^{1}(K_{\infty,w},E[p^{\infty}]/\mathscr{F}^{+}E[p^{\infty}])\}.

Then clearly
{\rm Sel}_{\rm str}(K_{\infty},E[p^{\infty}])\subset\mathfrak{Sel}_{\rm Gr}(K_{\infty},E[p^{\infty}])
, and there is a
\Lambda
-module pseudo-isomorphism

{\rm Sel}_{p^{\infty}}(E/K_{\infty})\sim{\rm Sel}_{\rm str}(K_{\infty},E[p^{\infty}])

(see e.g.
[
CG96
]
).
On the other hand, let
\check{S}_{\mathfrak{p}}
denote the subgroup of

{\rm H}^{1}(K,\mathbf{T})\simeq\varprojlim_{L}{\rm H}^{1}(L,T)

where
L
runs over the finite extensions of
K
contained in
K_{\infty}
, cut out by the local conditions that are everywhere orthogonal complements to
{\rm H}_{\mathfrak{p}}^{1}(K_{\infty,w},E[p^{\infty}])
under local Tate duality.

Proposition 3.2
.

Suppose
\mathbf{z}_{\infty}^{*}
is not
\Lambda
-torsion and the localization map

{\rm loc}_{\mathfrak{p}}:\check{S}_{p}\rightarrow{\rm H}^{1}(K_{\mathfrak{p}},\mathscr{F}^{+}\mathbf{T})

is nonzero. Then the following are equivalent:

(i)

X_{\mathfrak{p}}
and
\check{S}_{\mathfrak{p}}
are both
\Lambda
-torsion, with

{\rm char}_{\Lambda}(X_{\mathfrak{p}})\Lambda_{R_{0}}=\bigl(L_{\mathfrak{p}}\bigr(f)).

(ii)

X
and
\check{S}_{p}
both have
\Lambda
-rank one, with

{\rm char}_{\Lambda}(X_{\rm tors})={\rm char}_{\Lambda}\bigl(\check{S}_{p}/\Lambda\mathbf{z}_{\infty}^{*}\bigr)^{2}.

Proof.

As in the good
p
-ordinary case considered in
[
BCK21
, Thm. 5.1]
, this can be readily extracted from the arguments in
[
Cas17
, Appendix A]
, replacing the appeal to the explicit reciprocity law of
[
Cas17
, App. A.1]
(a special case of
[
CH18
, Thm. 5.7]
) by Theorem
2.2
and Corollary
2.3
above.
∎

Remark
.

The assertions in part (ii) of Proposition
3.2
are a natural extension of Perrin-Riou’s Heegner point main conjecture
[
PR87
, Conj. B]
to multiplicative primes
p
. Moreover, in the
split
multiplicative case, they might be viewed as a “primitive” of the following:

Conjecture 3.3
.

\mathfrak{X}
and
\check{S}_{p}
both have
\Lambda
-rank one, with

{\rm char}_{\Lambda}(\mathfrak{X}_{\rm tors})={\rm char}_{\Lambda}\bigl(\check{S}_{p}/\Lambda\mathbf{z}_{\infty}\bigr)^{2}.

Indeed, directly from the definitions we see that
\mathfrak{Sel}_{\rm Gr}(K_{\infty},E[p^{\infty}])/{\rm Sel}_{\rm str}(K_{\infty},E[p^{\infty}])
injects into

\displaystyle\prod_{w\mid p}{\rm ker}\bigl\{{\rm H}^{1}(K_{\infty,w},E[p^{\infty}]/\mathscr{F}^{+}E[p^{\infty}])\rightarrow{\rm H}^{1}(I_{w},E[p^{\infty}]/\mathscr{F}^{+}E[p^{\infty}])\bigr\}

\displaystyle\quad\simeq\prod_{w\mid p}{\rm H}^{1}(G_{K_{\infty,w}}/I_{w},(E[p^{\infty}]/\mathscr{F}^{+}E[p^{\infty}])^{I_{w}})\simeq\prod_{v\in\{\mathfrak{p},\overline{\mathfrak{p}}\}}(\mathbf{Q}_{p}/\mathbf{Z}_{p})/(\alpha-1)(\mathbf{Q}_{p}/\mathbf{Z}_{p})

(cf.
[
Ski16
, §3.2]
). Using this, it should be possible to show that

{\rm char}_{\Lambda}(\mathfrak{X}_{\rm tors})=\begin{cases}{\rm char}_{\Lambda}(X_{\rm tors})\cdot(\gamma-1)^{2}&\textrm{if $E$ has split multiplicative reduction at $p$,}\\[1.99997pt]
{\rm char}_{\Lambda}(X_{\rm tors})&\textrm{otherwise.}\end{cases}

On the other hand, since clearly

{\rm char}_{\Lambda}\bigl(\check{S}_{p}/\Lambda\mathbf{z}_{\infty}\bigr)={\rm char}_{\Lambda}\bigl(\check{S}_{p}/\Lambda\mathbf{z}_{\infty}^{\prime}\bigr)\cdot(\gamma-1),

we see that the assertions in part (ii) of Proposition
3.2
should be equivalent to Conjecture
3.3
in the nonsplit multiplicative case, and to a “primitive” version of the same conjecture – with the trivial zeros accounted for by the factor
(\gamma-1)^{2}
removed – in the split multiplicative case.

3.2.
Mazur’s control theorem

For every
n
, put
\Gamma_{n}
for the unique subgroup of
\Gamma
of index
p^{n}
, and set
K_{n}=(K_{\infty})^{\Gamma_{n}}
, so
{\rm Gal}(K_{n}/K)=\Gamma/\Gamma_{n}\simeq\mathbf{Z}/p^{n}\mathbf{Z}
.

Theorem 3.4
.

The natural restriction map

{\rm Sel}_{p^{\infty}}(E/K_{n})\rightarrow{\rm Sel}_{p^{\infty}}(E/K_{\infty})^{\Gamma_{n}}

has finite kernel and cokernel, of order bounded independently of
n
.

Proof.

Since we assume that
p
splits in
K
, this follows from
[
Gre99
, Prop. 3.7]
and the main result of
[
BSDGP96
]
(showing that
{\rm log}_{p}(q_{E})\neq 0
for the Tate period
q_{E}
of
E/\mathbf{Q}_{p}
).
∎

4.
Proof of main results

Proof of Theorem
1.3
.

It follows from Theorem 1.10 in
[
CV07
]
and the definition of
\mathbf{z}_{\infty}^{\prime}
that the
\Lambda
-adic Heegner class
\mathbf{z}_{\infty}^{*}
is non-torsion (both in the split and the nonsplit multiplicative case),
and by the same argument as in
[
BCK21
, Cor. 4.5]
, this yields
{\rm loc}_{\mathfrak{p}}(\check{S}_{p})\neq 0
. In view of Proposition
3.2
, the result now follows from Theorem
3.1
.
∎

Proof of Theorem
1.1
.

Choose an imaginary quadratic field
K
such that:

(i)

q
is ramified in
K
,

(ii)

if
N
is odd or divisible by
4
, then
2
splits in
K
,

(iii)

every prime factor
\ell\neq q
of
N
splits in
K
,

(iv)

L(E^{K},1)\neq 0
.

Note that condition (iii) implies in particular that
p
splits in
K
. The existence of such
K
(in fact, of infinitely many such
K
) is ensured by
[
FH95
, Thm. B.1]
.

By condition (iv) and Kolyvagin’s (or alternatively, Kato’s) work, we have
\#{\rm Sel}_{p^{\infty}}(E^{K}/\mathbf{Q})<\infty
, and so our corank one assumption implies that

(4.1)

{\rm corank}_{\mathbf{Z}_{p}}{\rm Sel}_{p^{\infty}}(E/K)=1.

Let
\gamma\in\Gamma
be a topological generator.
By Theorem
3.4
, it follows that
{\rm rank}_{\mathbf{Z}_{p}}(X/(\gamma-1)X)=1
, and so the first assertion of Theorem
1.3
implies that

{\rm char}_{\Lambda}(X_{\rm tors})\not\subset(\gamma-1).

By the equality of characteristic ideals in the same result, this shows that

{\rm char}_{\Lambda}(\check{S}_{p}/\Lambda\mathbf{z}_{\infty}^{*})\not\subset(\gamma-1),

and so (by another application of Theorem
3.4
, and noting that
{\rm rank}_{\mathbf{Z}_{p}}{\rm Sel}(K,T)=1
by (
4.1
))
\mathbf{z}_{\infty}^{*}
has non-torsion image under the projection

{\rm pr}_{0}:\check{S}_{p}\rightarrow{\rm Sel}(K,T).

By Theorem
2.1
in the split multiplicative case, and its proof in the nonsplit multiplicative case (so
(1-\alpha^{-1})\neq 0
),
{\rm pr}_{0}(\mathbf{z}_{\infty}^{*})
is a nonzero multiple of
\kappa_{f}
, and hence by the Gross–Zagier formula (see (
2.2
)) the above shows that
{\rm ord}_{s=1}L(E/K,s)=1
. Together with condition (iv) and the factorization
L(E/K,s)=L(E,s)L(E^{K},s)
, this yields the result.
∎
