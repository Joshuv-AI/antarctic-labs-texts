14 C.F.R. § 460.53 — Security.
Citation: us:cfr:14:460.53
Creator: Commercial Space Transportation, Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-460.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 460 (Human Space Flight Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An operator must implement security requirements to prevent any space flight participant from jeopardizing the safety of the flight crew or the public. A space flight participant may not carry on board any explosives, firearms, knives, or other weapons.
