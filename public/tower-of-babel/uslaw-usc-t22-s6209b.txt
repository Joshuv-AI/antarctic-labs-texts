22 U.S.C. § 6209b — Role of the Secretary of State in foreign policy guidance
Citation: us:usc:22:6209b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section6209b
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 71 (United States International Broadcasting). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

To assist the Agency in carrying out its functions, the Chief Executive Officer shall regularly consult with and seek from the Secretary of State guidance on foreign policy issues.
