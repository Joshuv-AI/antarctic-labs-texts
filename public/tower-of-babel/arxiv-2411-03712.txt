Probability Versions of Li-Yau Type Inequalities and Applications
Li-Juan Cheng, Feng-Yu Wang

Abstract
By using stochastic analysis, two probability versions of Li-Yau type inequalities are established for diffusion semigroups on a manifold possibly with (non-convex) boundary. The inequalities are explicitly given by the Bakry-Emery curvature-dimension, as well as the lower bound of the second fundamental form if the boundary exists. As applications, a number of global and local estimates are presented, which extend or improve existing ones derived for manifolds without boundary. Compared with the maximum principle technique developed in the literature, the probabilistic argument we used is more straightforward and hence considerably simpler.

1
Introduction

Since Li-Yau
[
14
]
established their famous parabolic Harnack inequality for the heat semigroup on Riemannian manifolds,
a number of extensions and refinements have been intensively made in the literature, which will be briefly recalled latter on.

The purpose of this paper is to provide probability versions of Li-Yau type inequalities for the diffusion semigroups on a complete Riemannian manifold possibly with a Neumann boundary,
which are formulated by expectations on functionals of the corresponding diffusion process, which are explicitly given by the Bakry-Emery curvature-dimension of the generator, the second fundamental form of the boundary if exists, and an adapted process
\ell_{s}
satisfying
\ell_{0}=1
and
\ell_{t}=1
, see Theorem
2.1
. With specific choices of the reference adapted process
\ell_{s}
, these inequalities imply new explicit gradient estimates on the heat semigroup, see Corollary
3.1
for global estimates and Corollary
4.2
for local estimates.

Compared with the maximum principle technique developed from
[
14
]
and adopted in substantial references, the martingale argument we used here considerably simplify the proof. The main idea of the study comes from Arnaudon-Thalmaier
[
2
]
, where some global and local gradient estimates on the heat semigroup is presented by using stochastic analysis on manifolds.

Before moving on, let us recall some existing results on Li-Yau type inequalities, which are derived on manifolds without boundary, and in most cases for the Laplacian without drift.
See
[
26
,
28
]
for extensions to manifolds with boundary.

Let
M
be an
m
-dimensional connected complete Riemannian manifold without boundary, let
L:=\Delta+Z
for some vector field
Z.
Assume that
for some constants
n\geq m
and
K\in\mathbb{R}
the following Bakry-Emery curvature-dimension condition holds:

(1.1)

{\rm Ric}_{Z}^{(n-m)}:={\rm Ric}_{Z}-\frac{Z\otimes Z}{n-m}\geq K,

where
{\rm Ric}_{Z}:={\rm Ric}-\nabla Z
is the Bakry-Emery curvature, and
{\rm Ric}
is the Ricci curvature. This condition
is equivalent to

(1.2)

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}L|\nabla f|^{2}-\langle\nabla Lf,\nabla f\rangle\geq K|\nabla f|^{2}+\frac{1}{n}(Lf)^{2},\quad f\in C^{\infty}(M).

When
Z=0
we may take
n=m
in (
1.1
), so that this condition reduces to
{\rm Ric}\geq K.

Consider a bounded positive solution to the heat equation

\partial_{t}u_{t}=Lu_{t},\ \ t\geq 0.

Li-Yau
[
14
]
proved that when
Z=0
and
{\rm Ric}\geq K
for some constant
K
,

\frac{|\nabla u_{t}|^{2}}{u_{t}^{2}}\leq\alpha\frac{\Delta u_{t}}{u_{t}}+\frac{nK^{-}\alpha^{2}}{2(\alpha-1)}+\frac{n\alpha^{2}}{2t},\ \ t>0,\alpha>1,

where
K^{-}:=\max\{-K,0\}
is the negative part of
K
.
In particular, when
K=0
(i.e.
{\rm Ric}\geq 0
) with
\alpha\downarrow 1
, this implies

\frac{|\nabla u_{t}|^{2}}{u_{t}^{2}}\leq\frac{\Delta u_{t}}{u_{t}}+\frac{n}{2t},

where the equality holds for
u_{t}
being the standard heat kernel on
M=\mathbb{R}^{n}
.

The above Li-Yau inequality has been extensively extended or refined.
For instances, by Davies
[
6
]
(for
Z=0
)

(1.3)

\displaystyle\frac{|\nabla u_{t}|^{2}}{u_{t}^{2}}\leq\alpha\frac{\Delta u_{t}}{u_{t}}+\frac{nK^{-}\alpha^{2}}{4(\alpha-1)}+\frac{n\alpha^{2}}{2t},\ \ \alpha>1,t>0;

by Yau
[
29
]
(for
Z=0
)

\frac{|\nabla u_{t}|^{2}}{u_{t}^{2}}\leq\frac{\Delta u_{t}}{u_{t}}+\sqrt{2nK^{-}}\sqrt{\frac{|\nabla u_{t}|^{2}}{u_{t}}+\frac{n}{2t}+2nK^{-}}+\frac{n}{2t},\ \ t>0,

which is then improved by Bakry-Qian
[
5
, (6)]

\frac{|\nabla u_{t}|^{2}}{u_{t}^{2}}\leq\frac{\Delta u_{t}}{u_{t}}+\sqrt{nK^{-}}\sqrt{\frac{|\nabla u_{t}|^{2}}{u_{t}^{2}}+\frac{n}{2t}+\frac{nK^{-}}{4}}+\frac{n}{2t},

by
[
5
, (54)]

(1.4)

\frac{|\nabla u_{t}|^{2}}{u_{t}^{2}}\leq\left(1+\frac{2}{3}K^{-}t\right)\frac{\Delta u_{t}}{u_{t}}+\frac{n}{2t}+\frac{nK^{-}}{2}\left(1+\frac{1}{3}K^{-}t\right),\ \ t>0;

and more recently by Bakry-Bolley-Gentil
[
3
]
(also for
Z\neq 0
)

(1.5)

\frac{4}{nK}\frac{Lu_{t}}{u_{t}}<1+\frac{\pi^{2}}{K^{2}t^{2}},

(1.6)

\frac{|\nabla u_{t}|^{2}}{u_{t}^{2}}\leq\frac{Lu_{t}}{u_{t}}-\frac{nK}{2}+\frac{n}{2}\Phi_{t}\Big(1-\frac{4}{nK}\frac{Lu_{t}}{u_{t}}\Big),\ \ t>0,

where

\Phi_{t}(r):=\begin{cases}K\sqrt{r}\coth(Kt\sqrt{r}),&\ \ \ \ r>0,\\
\frac{1}{t},&\ \ \ \ r=0,\\
K\sqrt{-r}\cot(Kt\sqrt{-r}),&\ \ \ \ -\frac{\pi^{2}}{K^{2}t^{2}}<r<0.\end{cases}

Moreover,
Li-Xu
[
13
]
proved (for
Z=0
)

(1.7)

\displaystyle\frac{|\nabla u_{t}|^{2}}{u_{t}^{2}}\leq\left(1+\frac{\sinh(K^{-}t)\cosh(K^{-}t)-K^{-}t}{\sinh^{2}(K^{-}t)}\right)\frac{\Delta u_{t}}{u_{t}}+\frac{nK^{-}}{2}\Big(1+\coth(K^{-}t)\Big),\ \ t>0,

see also Qian
[
17
,
18
]
for conditions on functions

a
and
c
such that

\displaystyle\frac{|\nabla u_{t}|^{2}}{u_{t}^{2}}\leq a(t)\frac{\Delta u_{t}}{u_{t}}+c(t),\ \ t>0.

All these inequalities are proved by using the technique of maximum principle developed in Li-Yau
[
14
]
.

In the next section, we present two probability versions of Li-Yau type inequalities for the heat semigroup generated by
L:=\Delta+Z
with Neumann boundary if
\partial M
exists, by using the diffusion process
X_{t}
generated by
L
with reflecting boundary if
\partial M
exists. The inequalities are explicitly given by
K
and
n
in (
1.1
) for some constant

and a function

, and the lower bound of the second fundamental form of
\partial M
if it exists. As applications, besides extensions of existing estimates to the case with boundary, some new global and local estimates are presented in Sections 3 and 4 respectively, where the curvature may be unbounded from below.

General results

\text{\rm{d}}X_{t}=Z(X_{t})\text{\rm{d}}t+\sqrt{2}\,U_{t}\circ\text{\rm{d}}B_{t}+N(X_{t})\text{\rm{d}}\mathscr{L}_{t},

For any

Proof.

(a) When

By (

\begin{split}I_{2}&=\mathbb{E}^{x}\bigg[\int_{0}^{t}(\ell_{s}^{2})^{\prime}\,\text{\rm{e}}^{\frac{2\alpha}{\alpha-1}K_{0}s}\,\mathbb{E}^{x}\Big(\text{\rm{e}}^{-2K_{0}s}\frac{|\nabla u_{t-s}|^{2}}{u_{t-s}}(X_{s})\Big)\text{\rm{d}}s\bigg]\\

By

and integration by parts formula, we derive

So,

and combining

) and (
