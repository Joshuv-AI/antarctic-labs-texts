36 C.F.R. § 401.6 — Approval by National Commission of Fine Arts.
Citation: us:cfr:36:401.6
Creator: American Battle Monuments Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-401.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 401 (Monuments And Memorials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A design for a memorial to be constructed at the expense of the United States Government must be approved by the National Commission of Fine Arts before the Commission can accept it.
