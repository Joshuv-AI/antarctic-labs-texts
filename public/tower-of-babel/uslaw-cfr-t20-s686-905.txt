20 C.F.R. § 686.905 — Are loss and damages that occur to persons or personal property of students at Job Corps centers eligible for reimbursement?
Citation: us:cfr:20:686.905
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-686.905
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 686 (The Job Corps Under Title I Of The Workforce Innovation And Opportunity Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, the Job Corps may pay students for valid claims under the procedures found in 29 CFR part 15, subpart D.
