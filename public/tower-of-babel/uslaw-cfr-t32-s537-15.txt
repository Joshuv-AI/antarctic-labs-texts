32 C.F.R. § 537.15 — Statutory authority for maritime claims and claims involving civil works of a maritime nature.
Citation: us:cfr:32:537.15
Creator: Department Of The Army
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-537.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 537 (Claims On Behalf Of The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Army Maritime Claims Settlement Act. The sections pertinent to maritime affirmative claims are set out at 10 U.S.C. 4803-4804.
(b) The Rivers and Harbors Act. The section of the Act pertinent to affirmative claims involving civil works of a maritime nature is set out at 33 U.S.C. 408.
