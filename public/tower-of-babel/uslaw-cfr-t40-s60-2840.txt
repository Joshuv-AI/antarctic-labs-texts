40 C.F.R. § 60.2840 — How do I comply with the increment of progress for submittal of a control plan?
Citation: us:cfr:40:60.2840
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-60.2840
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 60 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For your control plan increment of progress, you must satisfy the two requirements specified in paragraphs (a) and (b) of this section:
(a) Submit the final control plan, including a description of any devices for air pollution control and any process changes that you will use to comply with the emission limitations and other requirements of this subpart; and
(b) Maintain an onsite copy of the final control plan.
