20 U.S.C. § 1710 — Civil actions by Attorney General; notice of violations; certification respecting undertaking appropriate remedial action
Citation: us:usc:20:1710
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1710
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 39 (Equal Educational Opportunities And Transportation Of Students); Subchapter I (Equal Educational Opportunities); Part 3 (Enforcement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Attorney General shall not institute a civil action under section 1706 of this title before he—
(a)
gives to the appropriate educational agency notice of the condition or conditions which, in his judgment, constitute a violation of part 2 of this subchapter; and
(b)
certifies to the appropriate district court of the United States that he is satisfied that such educational agency has not, within a reasonable time after such notice, undertaken appropriate remedial action.
