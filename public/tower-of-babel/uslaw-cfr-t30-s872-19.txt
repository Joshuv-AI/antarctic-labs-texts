30 C.F.R. § 872.19 — Are there any restrictions on how Indian tribes may use Tribal share funds?
Citation: us:cfr:30:872.19
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-872.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 872 (Moneys Available To Eligible States And Indian Tribes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. You may only use Tribal share funds for:
(a) Coal reclamation under § 874.12 of this chapter;
(b) Water supply restoration under § 874.14 of this chapter;
(c) Noncoal reclamation under § 875.12 of this chapter that is requested under section 409(c) of SMCRA;
(d) Deposit into an acid mine drainage abatement and treatment fund under part 876 of this chapter;
(e) Land acquisition under § 879.11 of this chapter; and
(f) Maintenance of the AML inventory under section 403(c) of SMCRA.
