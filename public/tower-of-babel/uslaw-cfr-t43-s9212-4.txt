43 C.F.R. § 9212.4 — Penalties.
Citation: us:cfr:43:9212.4
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-9212.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 9210 (Fire Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person who knowingly and willfully violates the regulations at § 9212.1 of this title shall, upon conviction, be subject to a fine of not more than $1,000 or to imprisonment of not more than 12 months, or both.
