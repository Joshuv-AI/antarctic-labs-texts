Author Correction: Responses in organs, sperm, steroid hormones and CYP450 enzyme in male mice treated by quinestrol only or in conjunction with clarithromycin
Authors: Yu Ji, Yujie Wang, Yuhang Liu, Yutong Liu, Jiao Qin, Daohuan Yuan, Quansheng Liu
Published: 2024

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-024-78752-1, published online 09 November 2024

The original version of this Article contained errors.

The Keywords section was incorrect.

“Keywords Fertility control, Clarithromycin, Quinestrol, Fertility control”

now reads:

“Keywords Fertility control, Clarithromycin, Quinestrol, CYP3A4”

In addition, in the Materials and methods section, under the subheading ‘Statistical analysis’,

“The differences among groups in organ weights (liver, small intestine, kidney, spleen, testicular, epididymal, seminal vesicle), sperm density, serum LH and T concentrations, and small intestinal and liver CYP3A4 levels were analyzed using LSD multiple comparisons.”

now reads:

“A one-way analysis of variance (ANOVA) was used to examine the effects of different treatments on the weights of various organs (liver, small intestine, kidney, spleen, testicular, epididymal, seminal vesicle), sperm density, serum LH and T concentrations, and small intestinal and liver CYP3A4 levels. LSD multiple comparison tests were applied to assess differences between groups.”

The original Article has been corrected.
