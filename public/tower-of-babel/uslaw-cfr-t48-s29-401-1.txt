48 C.F.R. § 29.401-1 — 29.401-1 Indefinite-delivery contracts for leased equipment.
Citation: us:cfr:48:29.401-1
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-29.401-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 29 (Taxes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Insert the clause at 52.229-1, State and Local Taxes, in solicitations and contracts for leased equipment when—
(a) A fixed-price indefinite-delivery contract is contemplated;
(b) The contract will be performed wholly or partly in the United States or its outlying areas; and
(c) The place or places of delivery are not known at the time of contracting.
