32 C.F.R. § 757.1 — Scope of subpart A.
Citation: us:cfr:32:757.1
Creator: Department Of The Navy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-757.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 757 (Affirmative Claims Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Subpart A describes how to assert, administer, and collect claims for damage to or loss or destruction of Government property through negligence or wrongful acts.
