21 U.S.C. § 615 — Inspection of carcasses, meat of which is intended for export
Citation: us:usc:21:615
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title21-section615
Context: United States Code, Title 21 (Food And Drugs); Chapter 12 (Meat Inspection); Subchapter I (Inspection Requirements; Adulteration And Misbranding). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall also cause to be made a careful inspection of the carcasses and parts thereof of all amenable species, the meat of which, fresh, salted, canned, corned, packed, cured, or otherwise prepared, is intended and offered for export to any foreign country, at such times and places and in such manner as he may deem proper.
