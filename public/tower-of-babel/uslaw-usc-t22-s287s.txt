22 U.S.C. § 287s — Amendments to constitution of Organization involving new obligations
Citation: us:usc:22:287s
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section287s
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 7 (International Bureaus, Congresses, Etc.); Subchapter XVII (United Nations Educational, Scientific, And Cultural Organization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Unless Congress by law authorizes such action, neither the President nor any person or agency shall on behalf of the United States approve any amendment under article XIII of the constitution of the Organization involving any new obligation for the United States.
