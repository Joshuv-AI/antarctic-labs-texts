40 C.F.R. § 750.33 — Notice of proposed rulemaking.
Citation: us:cfr:40:750.33
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-750.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 750 (Procedures For Rulemaking Under Section 6 Of The Toxic Substances Control Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Rulemaking for PCB exemptions filed pursuant to § 750.31(a) shall begin with the publication of a notice of proposed rulemaking in the Federal Register. The notice shall state in summary form the required information described in § 750.31(c).
