10 U.S.C. § 8625 — Navy Reserve flag
Citation: us:usc:10:8625
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8625
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (General Administration); Chapter 861 (Secretary Of The Navy: Miscellaneous Powers And Duties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Navy shall prescribe a suitable flag to be known as the Navy Reserve flag. This flag may be flown by a seagoing merchant vessel if—
(1)
the vessel is documented under the laws of the United States;
(2)
the vessel has been designated by the Secretary, under such regulations as he prescribes, as suitable for service as a naval auxiliary in time of war; and
(3)
the master or commanding officer and at least half of the other licensed officers of the vessel are members of the Navy.
