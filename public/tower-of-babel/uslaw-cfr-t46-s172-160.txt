46 C.F.R. § 172.160 — Definitions.

Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 172 (Special Rules Pertaining To Bulk Cargoes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As used in this subpart—
(a) Length or L means the load line length (LLL).
(b) MARVS means the Maximum Allowable Relief Valve Setting of a cargo tank.
