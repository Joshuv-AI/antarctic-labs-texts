12 C.F.R. § 1202.12 — Is there anything else I need to know about FOIA procedures?
Citation: us:cfr:12:1202.12
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1202.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1202 (Freedom Of Information Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This FOIA regulation does not and shall not be construed to create any right or to entitle any person, as of right, to any service or to the disclosure of any record to which such person is not entitled under the FOIA. This regulation only provides procedures for requesting records under the FOIA.
