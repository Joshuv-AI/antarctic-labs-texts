2 U.S.C. § 1802 — Compensation
Citation: us:usc:2:1802
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section1802
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 28 (Architect Of The Capitol); Subchapter I (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The compensation of the Architect of the Capitol shall be at an annual rate which is equal to the annual rate of basic pay for level II of the Executive Schedule under section 5313 of title 5.
