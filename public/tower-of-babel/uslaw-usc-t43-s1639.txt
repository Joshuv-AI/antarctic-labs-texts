43 U.S.C. § 1639 — Construction with Alaska Native Claims Settlement Act
Citation: us:usc:43:1639
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section1639
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 33A (Implementation Of Alaska Native Claims Settlement And Alaska Statehood). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as specifically provided in this Act, (i) the provisions of the Alaska Native Claims Settlement Act [43 U.S.C. 1601 et seq.] are fully applicable to this Act, and (ii) nothing in this Act shall be construed to alter or amend any of such provisions.
