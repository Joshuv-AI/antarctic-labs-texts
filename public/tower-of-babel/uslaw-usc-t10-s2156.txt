10 U.S.C. § 2156 — Joint Forces Staff College: duration of principal course of instruction
Citation: us:usc:10:2156
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section2156
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Training And Education); Chapter 107 (Professional Military Education). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Duration.—
The duration of the principal course of instruction offered at the Joint Forces Staff College may not be less than 10 weeks of resident instruction.
(b) Definition.—
In this section, the term “principal course of instruction” means any course of instruction offered at the Joint Forces Staff College as Phase II joint professional military education.
