41 C.F.R. § 105-74.610 — -74.610 Controlled substance.
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-74.610
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-74.610
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-74 (74—Governmentwide Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Financial Assistance)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Controlled substance means a controlled substance in schedules I through V of the Controlled Substances Act (21 U.S.C. 812), and as further defined by regulation at 21 CFR 1308.11 through 1308.15.
