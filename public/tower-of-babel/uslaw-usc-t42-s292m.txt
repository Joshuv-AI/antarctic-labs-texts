42 U.S.C. § 292m — Repayment by Secretary of loans of deceased or disabled borrowers
Citation: us:usc:42:292m
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section292m
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter V (Health Professions Education); Part A (Student Loans); Subpart i (Insured Health Education Assistance Loans To Graduate Students). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If a borrower who has received a loan dies or becomes permanently and totally disabled (as determined in accordance with regulations of the Secretary), the Secretary shall discharge the borrower’s liability on the loan by repaying the amount owed on the loan from the account established under section 292i of this title.
