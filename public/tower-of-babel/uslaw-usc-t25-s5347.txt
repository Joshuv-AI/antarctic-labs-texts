25 U.S.C. § 5347 — Reimbursement to school districts for educating non-resident students
Citation: us:usc:25:5347
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section5347
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 46 (Indian Self-Determination And Education Assistance); Subchapter II (Contracts With States). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any school district educating Indian students who are members of recognized Indian tribes, who do not normally reside in the State in which such school district is located, and who are residing in Federal boarding facilities for the purposes of attending public schools within such district may, in the discretion of the Secretary of the Interior, be reimbursed by him for the full per capita costs of educating such Indian students.
