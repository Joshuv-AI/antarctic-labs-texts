41 C.F.R. § 102-33.160 — -33.160 Accounting for the use of Government aircraft.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-33.160
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-33.160
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-33 (33—Management Of Government Aircraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When accounting for the use of Government aircraft, including UAS, you must document all flights and keep this documentation for two years after the date of the flight. For each flight, record the:
(a) Aircraft's registration mark;
(b) Owner and operator;
(c) Purpose of the flight;
(d) Departure and destination points;
(e) Flight dates and times;
(f) Manifest; and
(g) Names of the pilots and crewmembers.
