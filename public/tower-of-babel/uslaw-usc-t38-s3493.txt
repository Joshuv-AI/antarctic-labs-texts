38 U.S.C. § 3493 — Effect on educational entitlement
Citation: us:usc:38:3493
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section3493
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part III (Readjustment And Related Benefits); Chapter 34 (Veterans’ Educational Assistance); Subchapter V (Special Assistance For The Educationally Disadvantaged). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The educational assistance allowance or cost of individualized tutorial assistance authorized by this subchapter shall be paid without charge to any period of entitlement the veteran may have earned pursuant to section 3461(a) of this title.
