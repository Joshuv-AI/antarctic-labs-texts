33 C.F.R. § 175.315 — Fixed fire extinguishing systems.
Citation: us:cfr:33:175.315
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-175.315
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 175 (Equipment Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A fixed fire extinguishing system must be of a type approved by the Coast Guard under 46 CFR part 162.
(b) A carbon dioxide system must be designed and installed in accordance with 46 CFR part 76, subpart 76.15.
(c) An automatic sprinkler system must be designed and installed in accordance with 46 CFR 25.30-15(c).
