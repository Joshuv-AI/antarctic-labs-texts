7 U.S.C. § 7473 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:7:7473
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section7473
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 101 (Agricultural Promotion); Subchapter IV (Kiwifruit). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated for each fiscal year such sums as are necessary to carry out this subchapter.
