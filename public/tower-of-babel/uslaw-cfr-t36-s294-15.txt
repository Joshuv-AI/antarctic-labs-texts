36 C.F.R. § 294.15 — Advisory committee review.
Citation: us:cfr:36:294.15
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-294.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 294 (Special Areas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A National Advisory Committee shall review each petition and provide advice and recommendations to the Secretary within 90 days of receipt of a completed petition. The committee will also provide advice and recommendations to the Secretary on any subsequent State-specific rulemakings.
