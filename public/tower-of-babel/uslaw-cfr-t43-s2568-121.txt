43 C.F.R. § 2568.121 — If an agency determines my allotment is inconsistent with the purposes of a CSU, what can I do if I disagree?
Citation: us:cfr:43:2568.121
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2568.121
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2560 (Alaska Occupancy And Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You may request reconsideration of a CSU manager's decision by sending a signed request to that manager.
(b) The request for reconsideration must be submitted in person or correctly addressed and postmarked to the CSU manager no later than 90 calendar days of when you received the decision.
(c) The request for reconsideration must include:
(1) The BLM case file number of the application and parcel, and
(2) Your reason(s) for filing the reconsideration, and any new pertinent information.
