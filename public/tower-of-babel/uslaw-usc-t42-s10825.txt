42 U.S.C. § 10825 — Technical assistance
Citation: us:usc:42:10825
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section10825
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 114 (Protection And Advocacy For Individuals With Mental Illness); Subchapter I (Protection And Advocacy Systems); Part B (Administrative Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall use not more than 2 percent of the amounts appropriated under section 10827 of this title to provide technical assistance to eligible systems with respect to activities carried out under this subchapter, consistent with requests by such systems for such assistance.
