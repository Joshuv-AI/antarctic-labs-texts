7 C.F.R. § 766.455 — Borrower acceptance of DBSA.
Citation: us:cfr:7:766.455
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-766.455
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 766 (Direct Loan Servicing—Special). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Subject to the 165-calendar day limitation in § 766.452(a)(8), the borrower must execute the appropriate Agency documents within 45 days after the borrower receives notification of Agency approval of DBSA, which will be within 30 days of having submitted a complete application.
