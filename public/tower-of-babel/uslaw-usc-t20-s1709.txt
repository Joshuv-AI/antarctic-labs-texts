20 U.S.C. § 1709 — Intervention by Attorney General
Citation: us:usc:20:1709
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1709
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 39 (Equal Educational Opportunities And Transportation Of Students); Subchapter I (Equal Educational Opportunities); Part 3 (Enforcement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whenever a civil action is instituted under section 1706 of this title by an individual, the Attorney General may intervene in such action upon timely application.
