12 U.S.C. § 5372 — Rule of construction
Citation: us:usc:12:5372
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section5372
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 53 (Wall Street Reform And Consumer Protection); Subchapter I (Financial Stability); Part C (Additional Board Of Governors Authority For Certain Nonbank Financial Companies And Bank Holding Companies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this Act shall be construed to limit or curtail the Corporation’s current authority to examine or bring enforcement actions with respect to any insured depository institution or institution-affiliated party.
