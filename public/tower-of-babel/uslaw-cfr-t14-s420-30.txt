14 C.F.R. § 420.30 — Launch site location review for permitted launch vehicles.
Citation: us:cfr:14:420.30
Creator: Commercial Space Transportation, Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-420.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 420 (License To Operate A Launch Site). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an applicant plans to use its proposed launch site solely for launches conducted under an experimental permit, the FAA will approve a launch site location if the FAA has approved an operating area under part 437 for launches from that site.
