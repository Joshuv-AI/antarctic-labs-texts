17 C.F.R. § 229.511 — (Item 511) Other expenses of issuance and distribution.
Citation: us:cfr:17:229.511
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-229.511
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 229 (Standard Instructions For Filing Forms Under Securities Act Of 1933, Securities Exchange Act Of 1934 And Energy Policy And Conservation Act Of 1975—Regulation S-K). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Furnish a reasonably itemized statement of all expenses in connection with the issuance and distribution of the securities to be registered, other than underwriting discounts and commissions. If any of the securities to be registered are to be offered for the account of security holders, indicate the portion of such expenses to be borne by such security holder.
