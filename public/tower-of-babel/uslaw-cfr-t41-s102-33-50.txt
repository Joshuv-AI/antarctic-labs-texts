41 C.F.R. § 102-33.50 — -33.50 Responsibilities when contracting to purchase or capital lease a Federal aircraft or to award a CAS contract.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-33.50
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-33.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-33 (33—Management Of Government Aircraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When purchasing, leasing, or awarding a CAS contract for a Federal aircraft, you must follow 48 CFR chapter 1, unless your agency is exempt.
