46 C.F.R. § 47.600 — Description of service.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 47 (Combination Load Lines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to deck cargo barges operating in International and Great Lakes service and meeting the conditions of assignment in § 47.610 of this subpart.
