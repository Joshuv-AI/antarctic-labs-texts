39 U.S.C. § 415 — Prohibition on restriction or elimination of services
Citation: us:usc:39:415
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title39-section415
Context: United States Code, Title 39 (Postal Service); Part I (General); Chapter 4 (General Authority). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Postal Service may not restrict, eliminate, or adversely affect any service provided by the Postal Service as a result of the payment of any penalty imposed under the Occupational Safety and Health Act of 1970 (29 U.S.C. 651 et seq.).
