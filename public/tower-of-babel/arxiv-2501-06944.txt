Structure theorem for log de Rham-Witt sheaves with vanishing
Fei Ren

Abstract
. The ℤ / p n \mathbb{Z}/p^{n} coefficient motivic cohomology with vanishing along an effective divisor D D is characterized by log de Rham-Witt sheaves with vanishing along the same divisor. In this paper, we prove an elegant structure theorem for log de Rham-Witt sheaves with vanishing along D D defined in [ RR24 ] , answering a question of Shuji Saito during the Mainz conference and a question of Yigeng Zhao during a short visit of the author last summer. Our structural result for the log forms also lays the foundation for the study of Milnor K K -theory with vanishing along D D in the paper to come.

Notations and conventions

Throughout the article,
k

X

For an effective Cartier divisor

)
, if

•

•

is a reduced divisor.

Note that

(1)

(2)

are allowed to be zero divisors, and

(3)

for any

, a

-divisibility decomposition of

always exists and is unique. (Hence we are allowed to use the article “the” when the length is specified.)

(4)

If
(
1.2.1
)
is the
p
-divisibility decomposition of length
n
, then

(1.2.2)

E=E_{0}+pE_{1}+\ldots+p^{n-2}E_{n-2}+p^{n-1}(E_{n-1}+pE_{n})

-divisibility decomposition of length

When

) is the

-divisibility decomposition of

For any given

-divisibility decomposition of length

as defined in
[

]

In this article, we will be only using

If we have

4.1.
A Hartog’s type lemma for Cohen-Macaulay abelian sheaves

Lemma 4.1
.

Let
X
be a locally noetherian equidimensional scheme, and

F
be a Cohen-Macaulay abelian sheaf on

(see
[

, Definition and Remark on p.238]

such that
\alpha\in F_{x}

Proof.

If

is an injective resolution of

, then

0\rightarrow\underline{\Gamma}_{\overline{\{x\}}}(I^{\bullet})\rightarrow I^{\bullet}\rightarrow j_{*}I^{\bullet}\rightarrow 0,

is a short exact of complexes, where

The long exact sequence it induces gives a four-term exact sequence of sheaves

Taking stalks at

is Cohen-Macauley, the Cousin complex

4.1.1
) into this sequence, we deduce that

, we get a left exact sequence

\Omega^{q-1}_{X}(\log A)=\Omega^{q-1}_{R_{0}[T]}=\bigoplus_{i\geq 0}\Omega^{q-1}_{R_{0}}\cdot T^{i}\oplus\bigoplus_{i\geq 1}\Omega^{q-2}_{R_{0}}\cdot T^{i}\operatorname{dlog}T,

\Omega^{q-1}_{X}(\log\widetilde{A})=\Omega^{q-1}_{R_{0}[T]}(\log T)=\bigoplus_{i\geq 0}\Omega^{q-1}_{R_{0}}\cdot T^{i}\oplus\bigoplus_{i\geq 0}\Omega^{q-2}_{R_{0}}\cdot T^{i}\operatorname{dlog}T.

Now suppose
\alpha\in\Omega^{q-1}_{X}(\log A)
.
Then there exist

a_{i}\in\Omega^{q-1}_{R_{0}},b_{i}\in\Omega^{q-2}_{R_{0}}
, among which only finitely many are nonzero, such that

\alpha=a_{0}+\sum_{i\geq 1}(a_{i}T^{i}+b_{i}T^{i}\operatorname{dlog}T).

Hence

d\alpha=\sum_{i\geq 0}T^{i}da_{i}+\sum_{i\geq 1}((-1)^{q-1}ia_{i}+db_{i})\cdot T^{i}\operatorname{dlog}T.

The condition
d\alpha\in T^{r}\Omega^{q}_{R_{0}[T]}=\bigoplus_{i\geq r}\Omega^{q}_{R_{0}}\cdot T^{i}\oplus\bigoplus_{i\geq r+1}\Omega^{q-1}_{R_{0}}\cdot T^{i}\operatorname{dlog}T

gives

da_{i}=0\text{ for all $i\in[0,r-1],$
\quad and
\quad$(-1)^{q-1}ia_{i}+db_{i}=0$ for all $i\in[1,r]$.}

In particular, the second equation above implies
db_{i}=0
when
p\mid i\in[1,r]
; and
da_{r}=0
(since
p\nmid r
by our assumption).
Let

\epsilon:=a_{0}+\sum_{p\mid i\in[1,r]}a_{i}T^{i}+\sum_{i\in[1,r]}b_{i}T^{i}\operatorname{dlog}T-\sum_{p\nmid i\in[1,r]}\frac{(-1)^{q-1}}{i}T^{i}db_{i}.

It is direct to check that

d\epsilon=da_{0}+\sum_{p\mid i\in[1,r]}T^{i}\left(da_{i}+db_{i}\operatorname{dlog}T\right)=0.

Moreover,

\alpha-\epsilon=\sum_{i\geq r+1}(a_{i}T^{i}+b_{i}T^{i}\operatorname{dlog}T)\in T^{r+1}\cdot\Omega^{q-1}_{X}(\log\widetilde{A}).

∎

4.3.
Log forms as the Frobenius invariant in
W_{n}\Omega^{q}_{X}(\log A)/\mathfrak{B}_{\infty}

The following lemma originates from a lemma by Kay Rülling.
Below we formulate and prove a log version.
We only need the
n=1
version of it, but nevertheless we record a
proof for general
n
.

Lemma 4.5
.

Let
X
be a connected smooth
k
-scheme and
A
be a simple normal crossing divisor on
X
.
Let
j:U:=X\setminus A\hookrightarrow X
be the open immersion.
Let
W_{n}\Omega^{*}_{X}(\log A)
be the de Rham-Witt complex associated to the log structure on
X
associated to
A
(relative to the trivial log structure on the base
k
) defined by Hyodo-Kato.
More explicitly (see
[
HK94
, Proposition 4.6]
),

W_{n}\Omega^{q}_{X}(\log A)=W_{n}\mathcal{O}_{X}\cdot j_{*}\operatorname{dlog}K^{M}_{q,U}+dW_{n}\mathcal{O}_{X}\cdot j_{*}\operatorname{dlog}K^{M}_{q-1,U}\subset j_{*}W_{n}\Omega^{q}_{U},

where
K^{M}_{q,U}
is the étale sheaf of Milnor
K
-theory on
U
.
Let

W_{n}\Omega^{q}_{X}(\log A)_{\log}:=W_{n}\Omega^{q}_{X}(\log A)\cap j_{*}W_{n}\Omega^{q}_{U,\log}.

Then the sequence

0\rightarrow W_{n}\Omega^{q}_{X}(\log A)_{\log}\rightarrow W_{n}\Omega^{q}_{X}(\log A)/\mathfrak{B}_{\infty}\xrightarrow{\overline{F}-1}W_{n}\Omega^{q}_{X}(\log A)/\mathfrak{B}_{\infty}\rightarrow 0

is exact on
X_{\operatorname{\acute{e}t}}.

Here

2
2

2

Note that by
[
Ill79
, I.3.11.4]
, for
n=1
, the image of
F^{j}dW_{j+1}\Omega^{q-1}
in
\Omega^{q}
is exactly
\mathfrak{B}_{j+1}\Omega^{q}
, so this definition recovers
\mathfrak{B}_{\infty}=\bigcup_{m\geq 1}\mathfrak{B}_{m}\Omega^{q}
as used previously.

\mathfrak{B}_{\infty}:=\bigcup_{j\geq 0}F^{j}(dW_{n+j}\Omega^{q-1}_{U}(\log A)),

and
\overline{F}
is induced from
F
via the restriction map.

Proof.

We first show that the map
\overline{F}
is well-defined.
By
[
HK94
, Theorem 4.4]
,

\operatorname{Ker}(R:W_{n+1}\Omega^{q}_{X}(\log A)\rightarrow W_{n}\Omega^{q}_{X}(\log A))=V^{n}(\Omega^{q}_{X}(\log A))+dV^{n}(\Omega^{q-1}_{X}(\log A)).

Hence
F:W_{n+1}\Omega^{q}_{U}\rightarrow W_{n}\Omega^{q}_{U}
induces a well-defined map

\overline{F}:W_{n}\Omega^{q}_{X}(\log A)\rightarrow W_{n}\Omega^{q}_{X}(\log A)/dV^{n-1}\Omega^{q-1}_{X}(\log A).

Since
dV^{n-1}\Omega^{q-1}_{X}(\log A)\subset dW_{n}\Omega^{q-1}_{X}(\log A)\subset\mathfrak{B}_{\infty}
, the map
F
further induces a well-defined map

W_{n}\Omega^{q}_{X}(\log A)/\mathfrak{B}_{\infty}\rightarrow W_{n}\Omega^{q}_{X}(\log A)/\mathfrak{B}_{\infty}

which we also denote by
\overline{F}
.

Clearly the sequence is a complex.
When
A=\emptyset
, the surjectivity of
\overline{F}-1
follows from
[
Ill79
, I, Proposition 3.26]
.
In general, we can run a similar (but way easier) argument as in the proof in
loc. cit.
.
For any
\alpha\in W_{n}\mathcal{O}_{X}\cdot j_{*}\operatorname{dlog}K^{M}_{q-1,U}
,
we have
\sum_{i=1}^{n}dV^{i}(R^{i-1}\alpha)\in W_{n+1}\Omega^{q}_{X}(\log A)

and it satisfies

d\alpha=(\overline{F}-1)(R\Big(\sum_{i=1}^{n}dV^{i}(R^{i-1}\alpha)\Big)).

It remains to show
W_{n}\mathcal{O}_{X}\cdot j_{*}\operatorname{dlog}K^{M}_{q,U}\subset(\overline{F}-1)(W_{n}\Omega^{q}_{X}(\log A))
étale locally.
Since
\overline{F}-1:W_{n}\mathcal{O}_{X}\rightarrow W_{n}\mathcal{O}_{X}
is surjective on
X_{\operatorname{\acute{e}t}}
and log forms are invariant under the endomorphism
\overline{F}
,
it follows that
W_{n}\mathcal{O}_{X}\cdot j_{*}\operatorname{dlog}K^{M}_{q,U}\subset(\overline{F}-1)(W_{n}\mathcal{O}_{X}\cdot j_{*}\operatorname{dlog}K^{M}_{q,U})
, and in particular , it lies in
(\overline{F}-1)(W_{n}\Omega^{q}_{X}(\log A))
as well.

We continue to prove the injectivity.
Let
\alpha\in W_{n}\Omega^{q}_{X}(\log A)_{\log}
be zero in
W_{n}\Omega^{q}_{X}(\log A)/\mathfrak{B}_{\infty}
.
Then by the definition of
\mathfrak{B}_{\infty}
,

\alpha=F^{m}d\beta
for some
\beta\in W_{n+m}\Omega^{q-1}_{X}(\log A)
.
It follows that

\displaystyle V^{n+m}(\alpha)

\displaystyle=V^{n+m}(F^{m}d\beta)=p^{m}V^{n}d\beta=p^{n+m}dV^{n}\beta=dV^{n}(p^{n+m}\beta)=0.

Let
\widetilde{\alpha}\in W_{2n+m}\Omega^{q}_{X}(\log A)_{\log}
be a lift of
\alpha
.
Then

0=V^{n+m}(\alpha)=V^{n+m}(F^{n+m}(\widetilde{\alpha}))=\underline{p}^{n+m}(\alpha),

where in the second equality we have used that
\widetilde{\alpha}
is a log form and hence
F(\widetilde{\alpha})=R(\widetilde{\alpha})
.
By the injectivity of
\underline{p}
, we deduce that
\alpha=0
as desired.

Next we prove the exactness in the middle.
Let
\alpha\in W_{n}\Omega^{q}_{X}(\log A)
and assume

\overline{F}(\alpha)-\alpha=F^{m}d\beta

for some
\beta\in W_{n+m}\Omega_{X}^{q-1}(\log A)

\gamma=\sum_{i=1}^{n+m}dV^{i}(R^{i-1}(\beta))\;\in W_{n+m+1}\Omega^{q}_{X}(\log A).

Then

d\beta=(\overline{F}-1)(R\gamma)

\overline{F}(\alpha-F^{m}R(\gamma))-(\alpha-F^{m}R(\gamma))=F^{m}d\beta-\overline{F}(F^{m}R\gamma)+F^{m}R(\gamma)=0.

By
[
CTSS83
, Lemme 2]
,
\alpha-F^{m}R(\gamma)
is a log form.
