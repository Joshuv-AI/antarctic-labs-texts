26 C.F.R. § 28.7701-1 — -1 Tax return preparer.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 28 (Imposition Of Tax On Gifts And Bequests From Covered Expatriates). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) In general. For the definition of the term tax return preparer, see § 301.7701-15 of this chapter.
(b) Applicability date. This section applies to returns and claims for refund filed, and advice provided, on or after January 14, 2025.
