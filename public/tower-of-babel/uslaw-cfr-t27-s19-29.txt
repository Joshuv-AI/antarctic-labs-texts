27 C.F.R. § 19.29 — Exemptions for national defense and disasters.
Citation: us:cfr:27:19.29
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-19.29
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 19 (Distilled Spirits Plants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Whenever TTB finds it is necessary to meet the requirements of national defense or necessary or desirable by reason of disaster, TTB may temporarily exempt the proprietor from any provisions of the internal revenue laws and the provisions of this part relating to distilled spirits, except those requiring the payment of tax.
