13 C.F.R. § 130.300 — Small Business Development Centers (SBDCs).
Citation: us:cfr:13:130.300
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-130.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 130 (Small Business Development Centers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Small Business Development Center Program is established under the statutory authority of the Small Business Act (15 U.S.C. 648) and administered through cooperative agreements issued to recipient organizations.
