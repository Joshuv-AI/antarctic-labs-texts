14 C.F.R. § 158.93 — Public agencies subject to reduction.
Citation: us:cfr:14:158.93
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-158.93
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 158 (Passenger Facility Charges (Pfc'S)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The funds apportioned under 49 U.S.C. 47114 to a public agency for a specific primary commercial service airport that it controls are reduced if—
(a) Such airport enplanes 0.25 percent or more of the total annual enplanements in the U.S., and
(b) The public agency imposes a PFC at such airport.
