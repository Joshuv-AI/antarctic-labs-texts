21 C.F.R. § 112.140 — Under this subpart, what requirements apply regarding records?
Citation: us:cfr:21:112.140
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-112.140
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 112 (Standards For The Growing, Harvesting, Packing, And Holding Of Produce For Human Consumption). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You must establish and keep records required under this subpart in accordance with the requirements of subpart O of this part.
(b) You must establish and keep documentation of the date and method of cleaning and sanitizing of equipment subject to this subpart used in:
(1) Growing operations for sprouts; and
(2) Covered harvesting, packing, or holding activities.
