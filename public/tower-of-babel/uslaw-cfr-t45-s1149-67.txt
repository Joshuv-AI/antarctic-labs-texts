45 C.F.R. § 1149.67 — Who represents the NEA on an appeal?
Citation: us:cfr:45:1149.67
Creator: National Foundation On The Arts And The Humanities
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1149.67
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1149 (Program Fraud Civil Remedies Act Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The authority head will designate the NEA's representative in the event of an appeal.
