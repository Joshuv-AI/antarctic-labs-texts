5 C.F.R. § 735.102 — What are the grounds for disciplinary action?
Citation: us:cfr:5:735.102
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-735.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 735 (Employee Responsibilities And Conduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee's violation of any of the regulations in subpart B of this part may be cause for disciplinary action by the employee's agency, which may be in addition to any penalty prescribed by law.
