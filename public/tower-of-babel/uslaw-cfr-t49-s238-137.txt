49 C.F.R. § 238.137 — Mixed consist; operating equipment with incompatible exterior side door systems.
Citation: us:cfr:49:238.137
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-238.137
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 238 (Passenger Equipment Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A train made up of equipment with incompatible exterior side door systems shall be operated within the constraints of each such door system.
(b) No later than December 6, 2018, each railroad shall adopt and comply with operating rules to provide for the safe use of equipment with incompatible exterior side door systems when utilized in a mixed consist.
