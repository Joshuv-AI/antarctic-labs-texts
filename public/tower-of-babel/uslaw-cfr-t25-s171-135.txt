25 C.F.R. § 171.135 — Where do I submit written information or requests?
Citation: us:cfr:25:171.135
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-171.135
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 171 (Irrigation Operation And Maintenance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Submit written information to us or make request of us in writing at the irrigation project servicing your farm unit.
