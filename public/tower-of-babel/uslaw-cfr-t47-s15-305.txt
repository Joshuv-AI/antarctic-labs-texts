47 C.F.R. § 15.305 — Equipment authorization requirement.
Citation: us:cfr:47:15.305
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-15.305
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 15 (Radio Frequency Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

PCS devices operating under this subpart shall be certified by the Commission under the procedures in subpart J of part 2 of this chapter before marketing. The application for certification must contain sufficient information to demonstrate compliance with the requirements of this subpart.
