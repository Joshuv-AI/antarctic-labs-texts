14 C.F.R. § 1206.304 — Expedited processing.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1206.304
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1206.304
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1206 (Procedures For Disclosure Of Records Under The Freedom Of Information Act (Foia)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A requester may ask for expedited processing of a request. However, information to substantiate the request must be included in accordance with § 1206.400, Criteria for Expedited Processing; otherwise, the request for expedited processing will be denied and processed in the simple or complex queue.
