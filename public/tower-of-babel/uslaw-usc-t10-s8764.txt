10 U.S.C. § 8764 — Devices and trophies: transfer to other agencies
Citation: us:usc:10:8764
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8764
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (General Administration); Chapter 875 (Disposal Of Obsolete Or Surplus Material). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Secretary of the Navy may, without reimbursement, transfer to the Secretary of the Treasury devices and trophies for the promotion of the sale of war bonds or victory bonds. The Secretary of the Treasury may sell or donate the devices and trophies for the promotion of the sale of such bonds.
(b)
The Secretary of the Navy may, without reimbursement, transfer to any agency of the United States devices and trophies for scientific, experimental, monumental, or display purposes.
