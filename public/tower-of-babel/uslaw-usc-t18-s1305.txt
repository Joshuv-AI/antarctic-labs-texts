18 U.S.C. § 1305 — Fishing contests
Citation: us:usc:18:1305
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section1305
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 61 (Lotteries). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of this chapter shall not apply with respect to any fishing contest not conducted for profit wherein prizes are awarded for the specie, size, weight, or quality of fish caught by contestants in any bona fide fishing or recreational event.
