31 C.F.R. § 212.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:31:212.1
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-212.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 212 (Garnishment Of Accounts Containing Federal Benefit Payments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to implement statutory provisions that protect Federal benefits from garnishment by establishing procedures that a financial institution must follow when served a garnishment order against an account holder into whose account a Federal benefit payment has been directly deposited.
