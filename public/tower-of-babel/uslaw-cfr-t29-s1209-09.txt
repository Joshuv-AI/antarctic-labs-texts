29 C.F.R. § 1209.09 — Requests for records under Freedom of Information Act.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1209.09
Creator: National Mediation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1209.09
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1209 (Public Observation Of National Mediation Board Meetings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Requests to review or obtain copies of agency records other than notices or records prepared under this part may be pursued in accordance with the Freedom of Information Act (5 U.S.C. 552). Part 1208 of the Board's Rules addresses the requisite procedures under that Act.
