34 C.F.R. § 226.11 — How does the Secretary evaluate an application?
Citation: us:cfr:34:226.11
Creator: Office Of Elementary And Secondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-226.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 226 (State Charter School Facilities Incentive Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Secretary evaluates an application on the basis of the criteria in § 226.12 and the competitive preference priorities in § 226.14.
(b) The Secretary informs applicants of the maximum possible score for each criterion and competitive preference priority in the application package or in a notice published in the Federal Register.
