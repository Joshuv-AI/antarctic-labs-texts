31 C.F.R. § 515.558 — Bunkering of Cuban vessels and fueling of Cuban aircraft by American-owned or controlled foreign firms.
Citation: us:cfr:31:515.558
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-515.558
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 515 (Cuban Assets Control Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Foreign firms owned or controlled by United States persons are authorized to engage in transactions ordinarily incident to the bunkering of vessels and to the fueling of aircraft owned or controlled by, or chartered to, Cuba or nationals thereof.
