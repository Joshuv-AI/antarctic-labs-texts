46 C.F.R. § 147.105 — Anesthetics, drugs, and medicines.
Citation: us:cfr:46:147.105
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-147.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 147 (Hazardous Ships' Stores). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Anesthetics, drugs, and medicines must be stowed and dispensed in accordance with the DHHS Publication No. (PHS) 84-2024.
