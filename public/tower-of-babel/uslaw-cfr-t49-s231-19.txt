49 C.F.R. § 231.19 — Definition of “Right” and “Left.”
Citation: us:cfr:49:231.19
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-231.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 231 (Railroad Safety Appliance Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Right or Left refers to side of person when facing end or side of car from ground.
