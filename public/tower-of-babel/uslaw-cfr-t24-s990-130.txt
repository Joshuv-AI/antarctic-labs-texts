24 C.F.R. § 990.130 — Ineligible units.
Citation: us:cfr:24:990.130
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-990.130
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 990 (The Public Housing Operating Fund Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Vacant units that do not fall within the definition of § 990.145 or § 990.150 are not eligible for operating subsidy under this part.
(b) Units that are eligible to receive an asset-repositioning fee, as described in § 990.190(h), are not eligible to receive operating subsidy under this subpart.
