54 U.S.C. § 306122 — Contracts for management of historic property
Citation: us:usc:54:306122
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section306122
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 3061 (Program Responsibilities And Authorities); Subchapter II (Lease, Exchange, Or Management Of Historic Property). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The head of any Federal agency having responsibility for the management of any historic property may, after consultation with the Council, enter into a contract for the management of the property. The contract shall contain terms and conditions that the head of the agency considers necessary or appropriate to protect the interests of the United States and ensure adequate preservation of the historic property.
