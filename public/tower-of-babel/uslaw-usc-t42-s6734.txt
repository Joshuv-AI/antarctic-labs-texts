42 U.S.C. § 6734 — Administration; rules; authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:42:6734
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section6734
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 80 (Public Works Employment); Subchapter II (Antirecession Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Secretary is authorized to prescribe, after consultation with the Secretary of Labor, such rules as may be necessary for the purpose of carrying out his functions under this subchapter. Such rules should be prescribed by the Secretary not later than ninety days of July 22, 1976.
(b)
There are authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary for the administration of this subchapter.
