14 C.F.R. § 1261.106 — Submission of claims.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1261.106
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1261.106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1261 (Processing Of Monetary Claims (General)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All claims shall be submitted in duplicate to the Administrator or designee on NASA Form 1204, “Employee's Claim for Damage to, or Loss of, Personal Property Incident to Service.”
