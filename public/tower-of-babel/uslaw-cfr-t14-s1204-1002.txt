14 C.F.R. § 1204.1002 — Responsibility.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1204.1002
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1204.1002
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1204 (Administrative Authority And Policy). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The NASA Center Directors and the Executive Director for Headquarters Operations are responsible for implementing the provisions of this subpart. In implementing this subpart, these officials will coordinate their action with appropriate officials of other affected agencies.
