16 U.S.C. § 938 — Notice of proposals
Citation: us:usc:16:938
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section938
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 15A (Great Lakes Fisheries). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

At least thirty days before approving a proposal to utilize a lamprey control measure or install a device in any stream, the United States Section shall cause notice of such proposal to be sent to the official agency having jurisdiction over fisheries in each of the States through which the stream flows.
