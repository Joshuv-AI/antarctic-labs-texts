7 U.S.C. § 2009l — Duties of Rural Economic and Community Development State Offices
Citation: us:usc:7:2009l
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2009l
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 50 (Agricultural Credit); Subchapter V (Rural Community Advancement Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In carrying out this subchapter, the Director of a Rural Economic and Community Development State Office shall—
(1)
to the maximum extent practicable, ensure that the State strategic plan referred to in section 2009c of this title is implemented;
(2)
coordinate community development objectives within the State;
(3)
establish links between local, State, and field office program administrators of the Department of Agriculture;
(4)
ensure that recipient communities comply with applicable Federal and State laws and requirements; and
(5)
integrate State development programs with assistance under this subchapter.
