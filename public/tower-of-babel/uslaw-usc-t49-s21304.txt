49 U.S.C. § 21304 — Willfulness requirement for penalties against individuals
Citation: us:usc:49:21304
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section21304
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part A (Safety); Chapter 213 (Penalties); Subchapter I (Civil Penalties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A civil penalty under this subchapter may be imposed against an individual only for a willful violation. An individual is deemed not to have committed a willful violation if the individual was following the direct order of a railroad carrier official or supervisor under protest communicated to the official or supervisor. The individual is entitled to document the protest.
