15 U.S.C. § 7311 — Additional applicability
Citation: us:usc:15:7311
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section7311
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 99 (National Construction Safety Team). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The authorities and restrictions applicable under this chapter to the Director and to Teams shall apply to the activities of the National Institute of Standards and Technology in response to the attacks of September 11, 2001.
