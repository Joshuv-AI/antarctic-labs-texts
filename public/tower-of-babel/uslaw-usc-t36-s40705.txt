36 U.S.C. § 40705 — Duty to maintain tax-exempt status
Citation: us:usc:36:40705
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section40705
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 407 (Corporation For The Promotion Of Rifle Practice And Firearms Safety); Subchapter I (Corporation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation shall be operated in a manner and for purposes that qualify the corporation for exemption from taxation under section 501(a) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (26 U.S.C. 501(a)) as an organization described in section 501(c)(3) of that Code (26 U.S.C. 501(c)(3)).
