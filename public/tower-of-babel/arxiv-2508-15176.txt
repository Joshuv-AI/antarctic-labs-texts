In general, a product of two

In the paper, we continue the study of mutually permutable product of finite groups by use of Sylow numbers. We focus our attention on the

Theorem 1.1
.

Suppose that

is a mutually permutable product of two

-solvable subgroups

(a)

(|G|,p-1)=1

(b)
either

For simplicity, we define the following real functions:

f(x)=\begin{cases}\log_{3}(x/2)&x>0,\\
\log_{3}(1/2)&x=0.\end{cases}

g(x)=\begin{cases}\log_{2}(x)&x>0,\\
0&x=0.\end{cases}

Theorem 1.2
.

Suppose that
G=AB
is a mutually permutable product of two solvable subgroups

and
B
. Then

(a)
the nilpotent length

(b)
the derived length

(1) The equality can be satisfied in Theorem
