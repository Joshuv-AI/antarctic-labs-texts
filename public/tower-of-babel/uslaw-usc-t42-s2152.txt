42 U.S.C. § 2152 — Policies contained in international arrangements
Citation: us:usc:42:2152
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2152
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 23 (Development And Control Of Atomic Energy); Subchapter X (International Activities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In the performance of its functions under this chapter, the Commission shall give maximum effect to the policies contained in any international arrangement made after August 30, 1954.
