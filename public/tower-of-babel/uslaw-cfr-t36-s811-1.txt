36 C.F.R. § 811.1 — Cross-references to employees' ethical conduct standards, financial disclosure and financial interests regulations and other conduct rules.
Citation: us:cfr:36:811.1
Creator: Advisory Council On Historic Preservation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-811.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 811 (Employee Responsibilities And Conduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees of the Advisory Council on Historic Preservation are subject to the executive branch-wide standards of ethical conduct, financial disclosure and financial interests regulations at 5 CFR Parts 2634, 2635 and 2640, as well as the executive branch-wide employee responsibilities and conduct regulations at 5 CFR Part 735.
