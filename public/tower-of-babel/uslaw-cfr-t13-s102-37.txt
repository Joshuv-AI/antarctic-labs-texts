13 C.F.R. § 102.37 — Training requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:13:102.37
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-102.37
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 102 (Record Disclosure And Privacy). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All employees should attend privacy training within one year of employment with SBA. All employees with Privacy Act responsibilities must attend Privacy Act training, whenever needed, that is offered by the SBA.
