18 C.F.R. § 153.13 — Emergency reconstruction.
Citation: us:cfr:18:153.13
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-153.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 153 (Applications For Authorization To Construct, Operate, Or Modify Facilities Used For The Export Or Import Of Natural Gas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of subpart F of part 157 of this chapter that permit reconstruction for the purpose of immediately restoring interrupted service for the protection of life or health or for maintenance of physical property in an emergency due to a sudden unanticipated loss of gas supply or capacity are applicable to facilities subject to section 3 of the Natural Gas Act.
