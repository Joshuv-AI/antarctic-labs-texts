16 U.S.C. § 460x–13 — Certificate of Secretary to interested person indicating prohibition from acquiring particular property by condemnation; contents
Citation: us:usc:16:460x–13
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section460x–13
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter LXXXII (Sleeping Bear Dunes National Lakeshore). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall furnish to any interested person requesting the same a certificate indicating, with respect to any property which the Secretary has been prohibited from acquiring by condemnation in accordance with provisions of this subchapter, that such authority is prohibited and the reasons therefor.
