A note on an inequality involving the sides and medians in a triangle
Peter Vassilev

Abstract
The main focus of the present paper is the following inequality ( b ​ c − a ) ​ m a + ( a ​ c − b ) ​ m b + ( a ​ b − c ) ​ m c ≥ 0 , \left(\sqrt{bc}-a\right)m_{a}+\left(\sqrt{ac}-b\right)m_{b}+\left(\sqrt{ab}-c\right)m_{c}\geq 0, where a , b , c a,b,c are the sides of a non-degenerate triangle and m a , m b , m c , m_{a},m_{b},m_{c}, the respective medians; which was conjectured to be true but had not been proved. We provide a proof. We also show that analogous inequality is true when the medians are replaced by the altitudes or the internal angle bisectors. Finally, we conclude with an open problem regarding the Cevians which would satisfy such inequality.

Introduction

In the 1980s the Bulgarian mathematician Georgi Boychev observed that the following inequality seems to hold for an arbitrary triangle with sides
a,b,c
and medians
m_{a},m_{b},m_{c}
:

\sqrt{bc}m_{a}+\sqrt{ac}m_{b}+\sqrt{ab}m_{c}\geq am_{a}+bm_{b}+cm_{c}

(1)

He made numerous numerical experiments which confirmed the conjectured relation, however, he was unable to find a definitive proof
1
1

1

We challenge the interested readers to test their ideas on possible proof before reading further
. He had communicated his conjecture in personal conversations with various colleagues including Mladen Vassilev-Missana, who relayed this story to us. Perhaps because such “Olympiad type problems” are not of great interest to the professional mathematicians or due to to the fact that it was not generally known to puzzle solvers, not many people undertook the task to find a proof.

Whatever the case may be, the problem, as far as we know, had remained unresolved to this day.

Before setting on finding a proof we made an investigation regarding similar problems. We have found the following inequality proposed by Kee-Wai Lau
[
1
]
:

\left({bc}-a^{2}\right)m_{a}+\left({ac}-b^{2}\right)m_{b}+\left({ab}-c^{2}\right)m_{c}\geq 0

(2)

which looks similar to (
1
). Its proof relies on the following inequality:

\begin{cases}2am_{a}\leq cm_{b}+bm_{c}\\
2bm_{b}\leq am_{c}+cm_{a}\\
2cm_{c}\leq am_{b}+bm_{a}\end{cases}

(3)

A purely algebraic proof is given by Marcin E. Kuczma in
[
2
]
. The same inequality is given by Dorin Andrica in
[
3
]
where geometrical proof is given. For other geometrical proofs see also
[
4
]
.

As it turns out (
3
) is also crucial for the proof of (
1
). In order to establish the proof we require some additional observations and finally the application of arithmetic mean - geometric mean inequality
2
2

2

In fact we only need a very particular case of it – namely that if the product of three positive numbers equals one, their sum is greater or equal to three.
.

Preliminary results

Lemma 1
.

Inequality (
1
) is true for any isosceles triangle.

Proof.

First we observe that (
1
) turns into identity for an equilateral triangle.

Thus, without loss of generality we can have either: 1)
a=b<c
or 2)
a<b=c.

Let us consider case 1). Then (
1
) becomes

2\sqrt{ac}m_{a}+am_{c}\geq 2am_{a}+cm_{c}

(4)

Dividing (
4
) by
c^{2},
and putting
x=\frac{a}{c}<1,
it is equivalent to:

\displaystyle 2\sqrt{x}\sqrt{2+x^{2}}+x\sqrt{4x^{2}-1}-2x\sqrt{2+x^{2}}-\sqrt{4x^{2}-1}

\displaystyle=2\sqrt{x}(1-\sqrt{x})\sqrt{2+x^{2}}+(\sqrt{x}-1)(\sqrt{x}+1)\sqrt{4x^{2}-1}

\displaystyle=(1-\sqrt{x})(2\sqrt{2x+x^{3}}-(\sqrt{x}+1)\sqrt{4x^{2}-1})\geq 0

But we have,

\displaystyle 2\sqrt{2x+x^{3}}-(\sqrt{x}+1)\sqrt{4x^{2}-1}\geq 2(\sqrt{2x+x^{3}}-\sqrt{4x^{2}-1})

\displaystyle=\frac{2({2x+x^{3}}-{4x^{2}+1})}{\sqrt{2x+x^{3}}+\sqrt{4x^{2}-1}}=\frac{2(1-x)(-x^{2}+3x+1)}{\sqrt{2x+x^{3}}+\sqrt{4x^{2}-1}}\geq 0

since
1>x.
Hence, the desired inequality is fulfilled.

Let us consider case 2). Then (
1
) becomes

cm_{a}+2\sqrt{ac}m_{c}\geq am_{a}+2cm_{c}

(5)

Putting
x=\frac{a}{c}<1,
after division by
c^{2},
(
5
) is equivalent to:

\sqrt{4-x^{2}}+2\sqrt{x}\sqrt{1+2x^{2}}\geq x\sqrt{4-x^{2}}+2\sqrt{1+2x^{2}}

Hence,

(1-x)\sqrt{4-x^{2}}-2\sqrt{1+2x^{2}}(1-\sqrt{x})\geq 0

Simplifying,

(1-\sqrt{x})\left((1+\sqrt{x})\sqrt{4-x^{2}}-2\sqrt{1+2x^{2}}\right)\geq 0

Using the fact that
1+\sqrt{x}\geq 1+x,
we have:

\displaystyle(1-\sqrt{x})\left((1+\sqrt{x})\sqrt{4-x^{2}}-2\sqrt{1+2x^{2}}\right)

\displaystyle\geq(1-\sqrt{x})\left((1+x)\sqrt{4-x^{2}}-2\sqrt{1+2x^{2}}\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{(1-\sqrt{x})\left((1+x)^{2}(4-x^{2})-4(1+2x^{2})\right)}{(1+x)\sqrt{4-x^{2}}+2\sqrt{1+2x^{2}}}

\displaystyle=\frac{(1-\sqrt{x})x\left(8-5x-2x^{2}-x^{3}\right)}{(1+x)\sqrt{4-x^{2}}+2\sqrt{1+2x^{2}}}\geq 0,

since
x<1.

∎

Lemma 2
.

If
a,b,c
are sides of a non-degenerate triangle, so are
\sqrt{a},\sqrt{b},\sqrt{c}.

Proof.

Without loss of generality let
a\leq b\leq c.
We have due to (
7
)

\sqrt{a+b}>\sqrt{c}.

It remains to prove that

\displaystyle\sqrt{a}+\sqrt{b}>\sqrt{a+b},\text{ i.e. }\frac{\sqrt{a}}{\sqrt{a+b}}+\frac{\sqrt{b}}{\sqrt{a+b}}>1.

Since we have
\sqrt{x}>x
for
0<x<1,

\sqrt{\frac{a}{a+b}}+\sqrt{\frac{b}{a+b}}>\frac{a}{a+b}+\frac{b}{a+b}=1.

∎

Lemma 3
.

If
a,b,c
are sides of triangle and
a<b<c,
we have

\sqrt{bc}m_{a}+\sqrt{ac}m_{b}\geq bm_{b}+cm_{c}

(9)

Proof.

We will show that

2\sqrt{ac}m_{b}+2\sqrt{bc}m_{a}\geq am_{b}+bm_{a}+cm_{a}+am_{b}

(10)

Due to (
3
) we have

am_{b}+bm_{a}+cm_{a}+am_{c}\geq 2bm_{b}+2cm_{c}

Hence, (
10
) (since
m_{b}>m_{c}
) together with (
3
) imply (
9

We rewrite (

2(\sqrt{ac}-a)m_{b}\geq(c+b-2\sqrt{bc})m_{a}

i.e.,

(2m_{b})\sqrt{a}(\sqrt{c}-\sqrt{a})\geq m_{a}(\sqrt{c}-\sqrt{b})^{2}

The last is obviously true since we have
2m_{b}>m_{b}+m_{c}>m_{a}
and by Lemma

\sqrt{a}>\sqrt{c}-\sqrt{b}
and
\sqrt{c}-\sqrt{a}>\sqrt{c}-\sqrt{b},

Lemma 4
.

Let
a<b<c
be sides of triangle and
m_{a}>m_{b}>m_{c}
its medians. Then we have

B>\max(A,C),

(11)

where
A=am_{a},B=bm_{b},C=cm_{c}.

Proof.

First we will show that
B>C.

We have to show that:

\sqrt{2a^{2}b^{2}+2b^{2}c^{2}-b^{4}}-\sqrt{2b^{2}c^{2}+2a^{2}c^{2}-c^{4}}>0

We obtain:

\displaystyle\sqrt{2a^{2}b^{2}+2b^{2}c^{2}-b^{4}}-\sqrt{2b^{2}c^{2}+2a^{2}c^{2}-c^{4}}

\displaystyle=\frac{2a^{2}b^{2}+2b^{2}c^{2}-b^{4}-2b^{2}c^{2}-2a^{2}c^{2}+c^{4}}{\sqrt{2a^{2}b^{2}+2b^{2}c^{2}-b^{4}}+\sqrt{2b^{2}c^{2}+2a^{2}c^{2}-c^{4}}}

\displaystyle=\frac{(c^{2}-b^{2})(c^{2}+b^{2}-2a^{2})}{\sqrt{2a^{2}b^{2}+2b^{2}c^{2}-b^{4}}+\sqrt{2b^{2}c^{2}+2a^{2}c^{2}-c^{4}}}>0

Next we will show that
B>A.

We have to show that:

\sqrt{2a^{2}b^{2}+2b^{2}c^{2}-b^{4}}-\sqrt{2a^{2}c^{2}+2a^{2}b^{2}-a^{4}}>0

We obtain:

\displaystyle\sqrt{2a^{2}b^{2}+2b^{2}c^{2}-b^{4}}-\sqrt{2a^{2}c^{2}+2a^{2}b^{2}-a^{4}}

\displaystyle=\frac{2a^{2}b^{2}+2b^{2}c^{2}-b^{4}-2a^{2}c^{2}-2a^{2}b^{2}+a^{4}}{\sqrt{2a^{2}b^{2}+2b^{2}c^{2}-b^{4}}+\sqrt{2a^{2}c^{2}+2a^{2}b^{2}-a^{4}}}

\displaystyle=\frac{(b^{2}-a^{2})(2c^{2}-b^{2}-a^{2})}{\sqrt{2a^{2}b^{2}+2b^{2}c^{2}-b^{4}}+\sqrt{2a^{2}c^{2}+2a^{2}b^{2}-a^{4}}}>0

Hence,
B>A
and
B>C,
i.e.,
B>\max(A,C).

∎

Main result

We are now ready to prove our main result.

Theorem 1
.

For any non-degenerate triangle (
1
) holds.

Proof.

From Lemma
1
we know the desired inequality is true for isosceles triangles. So without loss of generality we further assume that we have a scalene triangle with
a<b<c.

We give two equivalent forms of (
1

\left(\frac{\sqrt{c}m_{a}}{\sqrt{b}m_{b}}-1\right)B+\left(\frac{\sqrt{c}m_{b}}{\sqrt{a}m_{a}}-1\right)A+\left(\frac{\sqrt{ab}}{c}-1\right)C\geq 0

(12)

\left(\frac{\sqrt{c}m_{a}}{\sqrt{b}m_{b}}-1\right)B+\left(\frac{\sqrt{a}m_{b}}{\sqrt{c}m_{c}}-1\right)C+\left(\frac{\sqrt{b}m_{c}}{\sqrt{a}m_{a}}-1\right)A\geq 0

(13)

For any fixed triangle we have two possible cases 1)
A\geq C
or 2)
C>A.

Let 1) be fulfilled. We choose (
12
). By using the fact that
B\geq\max(A,C)
(see Lemma
4
) to diminish the value of the expression and then applying the arithmetic mean - geometric mean inequality we obtain:

\displaystyle\underbrace{\left(\frac{\sqrt{c}m_{a}}{\sqrt{b}m_{b}}-1\right)}_{\geq 0}B+\left(\frac{\sqrt{c}m_{b}}{\sqrt{a}m_{a}}-1\right)A+\underbrace{\left(\frac{\sqrt{ab}}{c}-1\right)}_{\leq 0}C

\displaystyle\geq\left(\frac{\sqrt{c}m_{a}}{\sqrt{b}m_{b}}-1\right)A+\left(\frac{\sqrt{c}m_{b}}{\sqrt{a}m_{a}}-1\right)A+\left(\frac{\sqrt{ab}}{c}-1\right)A

\displaystyle=\left(\frac{\sqrt{c}m_{a}}{\sqrt{b}m_{b}}+\frac{\sqrt{c}m_{b}}{\sqrt{a}m_{a}}+\frac{\sqrt{ab}}{{c}}-3\right)A\geq 0

Hence, (
1
) is valid.

Let 2) be fulfilled. We choose (
13
). We have two cases:

2)(i)
\frac{\sqrt{b}m_{c}}{\sqrt{a}m_{a}}-1\leq 0.
Then proceeding in a similar manner as above we obtain:

\displaystyle\underbrace{\left(\frac{\sqrt{c}m_{a}}{\sqrt{b}m_{b}}-1\right)}_{\geq 0}B+\left(\frac{\sqrt{a}m_{b}}{\sqrt{c}m_{c}}-1\right)C+\underbrace{\left(\frac{\sqrt{b}m_{c}}{\sqrt{a}m_{a}}-1\right)}_{\leq 0}A

\displaystyle\geq\left(\frac{\sqrt{c}m_{a}}{\sqrt{b}m_{b}}-1\right)C+\left(\frac{\sqrt{a}m_{b}}{\sqrt{c}m_{c}}-1\right)C+\left(\frac{\sqrt{b}m_{c}}{\sqrt{a}m_{a}}-1\right)C

\displaystyle=\left(\frac{\sqrt{c}m_{a}}{\sqrt{b}m_{b}}+\frac{\sqrt{a}m_{b}}{\sqrt{c}m_{c}}+\frac{\sqrt{b}m_{c}}{\sqrt{a}m_{a}}-3\right)C\geq 0

Hence, (

2)(ii)
\frac{\sqrt{b}m_{c}}{\sqrt{a}m_{a}}-1>0.

Then we have

\left(\frac{\sqrt{b}m_{c}}{\sqrt{a}m_{a}}-1\right)A=\sqrt{ab}m_{c}-am_{a}>0

But by Lemma

Lemma 5

For every triangle with sides

\displaystyle\left(\frac{\sqrt{b(a+c-b)}}{a+c}-\frac{\sqrt{a(b+c-a)}}{b+c}\right)\left(\frac{\sqrt{b(a+c-b)}}{a+c}+\frac{\sqrt{a(b+c-a)}}{b+c}\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{{b(a+c-b)}}{(a+c)^{2}}-\frac{{a(b+c-a)}}{(b+c)^{2}}=\frac{(b-a)(a+b+c)(ab-a^{2}+c^{2}-b^{2})}{(a+c)^{2}(b+c)^{2}}\geq 0

a\leq b\leq c.

bl_{b}\geq cl_{c}
is equivalent to

\frac{\sqrt{b(a+c-b)}}{a+c}-\frac{\sqrt{c(a+b-c)}}{a+b}\geq 0

This is true if

\displaystyle\left(\frac{\sqrt{b(a+c-b)}}{a+c}-\frac{\sqrt{c(a+b-c)}}{a+b}\right)\left(\frac{\sqrt{b(a+c-b)}}{a+c}+\frac{\sqrt{c(a+b-c)}}{a+b}\right)

Hence, the condition of the lemma is fulfilled.

Conclusion

We have provided a proof for an inequality which was conjectured to be true but had not been proved before. In our small quest for establishing the desired proof, we have stumbled upon similarly looking inequality and we have established that both inequalities may be extended to at least some other Cevians. We have thus given a new direction for investigation of the innumerable properties of triangles.
