30 C.F.R. § 75.400-1 — -1 Definitions.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 75 (Mandatory Safety Standards—Underground Coal Mines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The term coal dust means particles of coal that can pass a No. 20 sieve.
(b) The term float coal dust means the coal dust consisting of particles of coal that can pass a No. 200 sieve.
(c) The term loose coal means coal fragments larger in size than coal dust.
