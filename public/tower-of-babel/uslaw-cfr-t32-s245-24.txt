32 C.F.R. § 245.24 — Aircraft assigned an EATPL number 1 or 2.
Citation: us:cfr:32:245.24
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-245.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 245 (Plan For The Emergency Security Control Of Air Traffic (Escat)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Aircraft assigned an EATPL number 1 or 2 will not be delayed, diverted, or rerouted by Combatant Commanders. However, commanders may recommend that this traffic be rerouted to avoid critical or critically threatened areas.
