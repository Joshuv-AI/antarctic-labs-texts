38 U.S.C. § 1142 — Rates of peacetime death compensation

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part II (General Benefits); Chapter 11 (Compensation For Service-Connected Disability Or Death); Subchapter V (Peacetime Death Compensation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purposes of section 1141 of this title, the monthly rates of death compensation payable shall be those specified in section 1122 of this title.
