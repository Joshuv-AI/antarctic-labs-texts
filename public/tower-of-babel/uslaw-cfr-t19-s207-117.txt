19 C.F.R. § 207.117 — Review by Commission.
Citation: us:cfr:19:207.117
Creator: United States International Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-207.117
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 207 (Investigations Of Whether Injury To Domestic Industries Results From Imports Sold At Less Than Fair Value Or From Subsidized Exports To The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

On review, the parties may not present argument on any issue that is not set forth in the notice of review; and the Commission may affirm, reverse, modify, set aside or remand for further proceedings, in whole or in part, the initial determination of the administrative law judge. The Commission may make any findings or conclusions that in its judgment are proper based on the record in the proceeding.
