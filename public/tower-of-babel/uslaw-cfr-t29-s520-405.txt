29 C.F.R. § 520.405 — Must I notify my employees that I am applying for a certificate to employ messengers and/or learners at subminimum wages?
Citation: us:cfr:29:520.405
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-520.405
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 520 (Employment Under Special Certificate Of Messengers, Learners (Including Student-Learners), And Apprentices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon making application for a messenger and/or learner certificate or for renewal thereof, an employer shall post a copy of the first page of the completed application form in a conspicuous place in each department of the plant or establishment where he/she proposes to employ messengers and/or learners at subminimum wage rates. Such notice shall remain posted until the application is acted upon by the Administrator.
