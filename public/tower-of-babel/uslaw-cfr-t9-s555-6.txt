9 C.F.R. § 555.6 — Certificates.
Citation: us:cfr:9:555.6
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-555.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 555 (Transportation Of Fish Products In Commerce). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions in § 325.14 of this chapter regarding the filing of original certificates of unmarked inspected meat products delivered to carriers applies with respect to fish and fish products.
