Correction to ‘Casδ, an evolutionary transitional CRISPR system enables efficient genome editing across animals and plants’
Published: 2026

This is a correction to: Zhijia Yang, Meixia Yu, Peiyang Li, Zhuoyang Li, Yunpeng Teng, Yueheng Zhou, Meiqi Zhao, Chang Liu, Zilong Zhao, Zijian Wang, Jianrui Li, Yilin Jing, Yingnan Li, Haiming Zhao, Weibin Song, Chao Bian, Hainan Zhao, Jian Chen, Beibei Xin, Jinsheng Lai, Casδ, an evolutionary transitional CRISPR system enables efficient genome editing across animals and plants, Nucleic Acids Research, Volume 53, Issue 22, 11 December 2025, gkaf1358, https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkaf1358

The following changes have been made to the originally published paper.

Under the heading Protein expression and purification, text is corrected to read: “The resin was washed with 3 column volumes with 20 mM imidazole…” instead of: "The resin was washed with 30 column volumes with 20 mM imidazole…”.

Under the heading PAM depletion and validation assay, text is corrected to read: “Clones completely covered of 65536 random PAM combinations…” instead of: "Clones completely covered of 48 random PAM combinations…”.

Under the heading Casδ-1 only requires CRISPR (cr) RNA for dsDNA cleavage, text is corrected to read: “Here, we demonstrated that Casδ-1 possesses robust trans-activity, cleaving ssDNA substrates upon activation by either ssDNA or dsDNA triggers.” instead of: "Here, we demonstrated that Casδ-1 possesses robust trans-activity, cleaving both ssDNA and dsDNA substrates upon activation by either ssDNA or dsDNA triggers.”

In Supplementary Figure 3 legend, text is corrected to read: “(C and D) Trans-DNA cleavage activity analysis of Casδ-1 on ssDNA substrates with ssDNA and dsDNA activators.” instead of: "(C) Trans-DNA cleavage activity analysis of Casδ-1 on dsDNA substrate with ssDNA and dsDNA activators. (D) Trans-DNA cleavage activity analysis of Casδ-1 on ssDNA substrate with ssDNA and dsDNA activators.”

In the originally-published Table S1, the protein information and the direct repeat sequences were incorrect and have been corrected in a revised table in the Supplementary data file.
