32 C.F.R. § 1624.8 — Transfer for induction.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1624.8
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1624.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1624 (Inductions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director of Selective Service may direct that a registrant or registrants in a specified group of registrants be transferred for induction to such MEPS as he may designate.
