33 C.F.R. § 149.421 — What is the requirement for a previously approved fire detection and alarm system on a deepwater port?
Citation: us:cfr:33:149.421
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-149.421
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 149 (Deepwater Ports: Design, Construction, And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An existing fire detection and alarm system on a deepwater port need not meet the requirements in this subpart until the system needs replacing, provided it is periodically tested and maintained in good operational condition.
