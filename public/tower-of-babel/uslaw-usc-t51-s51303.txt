51 U.S.C. § 51303 — Asteroid resource and space resource rights
Citation: us:usc:51:51303
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section51303
Context: United States Code, Title 51 (National And Commercial Space Programs); Chapter 513 (Space Resource Commercial Exploration And Utilization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A United States citizen engaged in commercial recovery of an asteroid resource or a space resource under this chapter shall be entitled to any asteroid resource or space resource obtained, including to possess, own, transport, use, and sell the asteroid resource or space resource obtained in accordance with applicable law, including the international obligations of the United States.
