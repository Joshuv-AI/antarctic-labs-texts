42 C.F.R. § 93.209 — Departmental Appeals Board or DAB.
Citation: us:cfr:42:93.209
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-93.209
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 93 (Public Health Service Policies On Research Misconduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Departmental Appeals Board or DAB means the organization, within the HHS Office of the Secretary, established to conduct hearings and provide impartial review of disputed decisions made by HHS operating components.
