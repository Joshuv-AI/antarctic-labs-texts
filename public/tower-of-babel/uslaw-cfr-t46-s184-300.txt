46 C.F.R. § 184.300 — Ground tackle and mooring lines.
Citation: us:cfr:46:184.300
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-184.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 184 (Vessel Control And Miscellaneous Systems And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A vessel must be fitted with ground tackle and mooring lines necessary for the vessel to be safely anchored or moored. The ground tackle and mooring lines provided must be satisfactory for the size of the vessel, the waters on which the vessel operates, subject to the approval of the cognizant OCMI.
