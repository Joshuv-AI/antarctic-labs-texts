26 U.S.C. § 211 — Allowance of deductions
Citation: us:usc:26:211
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section211
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 1 (Normal Taxes And Surtaxes); Subchapter B (Computation Of Taxable Income); Part VII (Additional Itemized Deductions For Individuals). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In computing taxable income under section 63, there shall be allowed as deductions the items specified in this part, subject to the exceptions provided in part IX (section 261 and following, relating to items not deductible).
