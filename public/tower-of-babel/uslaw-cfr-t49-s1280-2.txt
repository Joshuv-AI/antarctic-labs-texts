49 C.F.R. § 1280.2 — Policy.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1280.2
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1280.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1280 (Handling Of National Security Information And Classified Material). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It is the policy of the Surface Transportation Board to act in accordance with Executive Order 12356, dated April 6, 1982, in matters relating to national security information.
