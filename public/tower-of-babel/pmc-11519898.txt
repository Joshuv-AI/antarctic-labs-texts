Auxiliary model nonlinear innovation least squares algorithm for identification ship 4-DOF via full-scale test data
Authors: Chunyu Song, Yinfu Li, Jianghua Sui
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
For the ship motion with large inertia coupled system identification modeling inaccuracy, the ship scale effect and the existence of partial unmeasured ship data problems. In this paper, an auxiliary model nonlinear innovation least squares identification algorithm is proposed. The new algorithm uses the output of the auxiliary model instead of the unmeasurable variables in the full-scale test data of the ship, and optimizes the error using the tangent function. Compared with the existing algorithm, the error of the improved algorithm decreases with the increase of time and continuously approaches to zero, which greatly improves the identification accuracy and convergence efficiency. The results show that the improved algorithm has significant identification accuracy and reliability. The identification method designed in this paper can be applied to the field of ship intelligent navigation engineering.Supplementary InformationThe online version contains supplementary material available at 10.1038/s41598-024-73081-9.

Introduction

The 98th meeting of the Maritime Safety Committee of the International Maritime Organization (IMO) proposed the concept of autonomous surface ships at sea, and suggested that relevant research institutions should invest in different levels of intelligent and automated autonomous ships as soon as possible1. Therefore, it is particularly important to carry out research on ship intelligent navigation system, which is the core key to ensure the navigation autonomy of intelligent ships. Therefore, as a disruptive technology, the ship intelligent navigation is a research hotspot in academia and industry, and it is urgent to carry out related research2.

However, the ship’s sea navigation is faced with the problems of large inertial coupled motion, multi-phase input energy dissipation, and rough sea condition interference at the same time, which makes its control system design extremely challenging and scientifically significant3,4.

Ship motion mathematical models are the core foundation of large ship maneuvering and control problems. Identification modeling is a method to research the mathematical model of the system on the basis of ship model or real ship maneuverability test data, which has the characteristics of high modeling accuracy and excellent generalization performance. And the method has received wide attention from scholars in related fields and has broad application prospects5. Fossen6 pointed out that the key bottleneck problems in ship motion identification modeling are dynamics model structure identification and ship scale effect. Sat7 research on the estimation of hydrodynamic derivatives in mathematical models of ship maneuverability using system identification techniques (extended Kalman filter and constrained least squares methods).

But the algorithm design did not consider the problem of multi-degree-of-freedom (MDOF) motion coupling effect of the ship, which makes it difficult to guarantee the overall accuracy of the model. In Addition, there are many researches on ship motion discrimination modeling, such as Ding first proposed a new theory of system identification8, which turns the error (innovation) information of a scalar into an error information vector, called the multi-innovation identification algorithm, and achieved good theoretical and applied results. Salid9 proposed a 4-DOF model to avoid over-identification. In reference10, the multi-innovation kalman filter algorithm was used to track the target in video images, and the convergence of the proposed algorithm was proven by the approximation theory. Xie11 applies the algorithm to the parameter identification of an underwater autonomous robot motion model. Bai12 is identified by locally weighted multi-innovation gradient iterative learning. Ding13 proposed a model-based auxiliary recursive least squares method for systems with irregular missing data. Perez14 research the model identification considering the actual measurement conditions of ship engineering. A two-step identification algorithm based on sensitivity weighting factor and unscented kalman filter is proposed.

However, traditional methods of ship motion control ignore the inaccurate modeling of large inertia coupled system identification; No optimization mechanism of actuator input energy consumption to meet comprehensive performance requirements has been developed. Hyeon15 also explored the aviation model identification technology. Zou16,17 used SVM method for ship model identification for the first time. Ding18–20 first proposed the idea of using the known outputs of the auxiliary model to replace some of the unmeasurable variables in the system, which in turn enables better ship motion maneuver identification.

Nevertheless, in most of these researches, the identification results are based on simulated data from ship motion models without interference, and the data from real ship tests are not accurate in sensor measurements. Zhang21–23 he proposed a self-organized multi-innovation ship identification modeling method. The mechanism of interactive coupling of hydrodynamic derivatives in large ship dynamics model was revealed, and a multi-degree-of-freedom model parameter correlation weight screening mechanism was constructed. Song24–26 proposed a nonlinear innovation ship identification modeling method based on real ship test data to realize the collaborative identification of structural and hydrodynamic derivatives in the mathematical model of ship motion, which solved the problem of limited innovation length of traditional ship maneuverability test data. In fact, using real ship test data for identification modeling research is a fundamental approach to solve the scale effect problem. However, there are currently limited resources for ships capable of performing real ship test measurements. In addition, both the accuracy and efficiency of identification need to be further improved.

Therefore, an auxiliary model nonlinear innovation least squares algorithm is proposed in this paper. Auxiliary model identification theory is a method which uses the measurable information of the system to establish an auxiliary model and uses the output of the auxiliary model to replace the unmeasurable variables of the system. And by choosing the parameters of the auxiliary model, the output of the auxiliary model approximates the unmeasurable variables in the ship. In addition, the innovation is optimized by using tangent function. The so-called innovation is the useful information that can improve the accuracy of parameter estimation or the accuracy of state estimation. At the same time, it is a significant theoretical value and practical significance to carry out research on the collaborative identification method of ship model structure and parameters based on real ship maneuverability test data.

System description and preliminaries

The Ship Maneuvering Mathematical Model Group (MMG) proposed a separate ship motion mathematical model. This model was designed to study the mutual interference between various parts in a propulsion and control system, based on the hydrodynamic characteristics of the hull, propeller, and rudder. Based on the MMG model and Newton’s rigid body mechanics, this paper established a mathematical model of ship motion with 4-DOF. The ship coordinate system is shown in Fig. 1.

Conclusion

This paper proposes a method nonlinear innovation least squares algorithm based on an auxiliary model can effectively realize the identification of structural and hydrodynamic derivative systems in the mathematical model of ship motion. Provide the basis for the identification and modeling of large inertial coupled system of ship motion. It solves the problem that the traditional ship maneuverability test data has partial unmeasurable and scale effect. The results show that the proposed algorithm improves the identification accuracy and efficiency. The above research can be widely applied to the identification modeling problems in engineering tasks such as intelligent ship design, large ship maneuvering simulator, and development of highly reliable intelligent navigation equipment.

In the follow-up work, the authors supplement and improve the proposed algorithm, and apply the algorithm to more ship types and further apply it to practical projects.
