Correction: Elucidating the growth-promoting factors of Bacillus amyloliquefaciens on rapeseed through metabolomics
Authors: Song Pei, Deng Yue, Yu Yaoying, Hu Zijin, Zhang Zhongmei, Yang Xiaoxiang, Zhang Lei, Liu Yong, Huang Xiaoqin
Published: 2025

Correction: BMC Plant Biol 25, 1507 (2025)

https://doi.org/10.1186/s12870-025-07538-y

Following publication of the original article [1], the authors found minor typo errors in the last sentence of Results section under subheading, Growth-promoting effect of Bam22 on B. napus. The changes are highlighted below in bold.

The incorrect sentence currently reads:

“In terms of fresh weight, both the above-ground biomass and root fresh weight of rapeseed seedlings treated with Bam22 showed significant differences compared to the control group after 7 and 35 days of inoculation, approximately 70.3% (shoot, 7 dpi), 39.4% (shoot, 35 dpi), 23.8% (root, 7 dpi), and 26.8% (root, 35 dpi), respectively. (Fig. 1c, h, i).”

The sentence should be corrected to:

“In terms of fresh weight, both the above-ground biomass and root fresh weight of rapeseed seedlings treated with Bam22 showed significant differences compared to the control group after 7 and 35 days of inoculation, approximately 70.3% (root, 7 dpi), 39.4% (root, 35 dpi), 23.8% (shoot, 7 dpi), and 26.8% (shoot, 35 dpi), respectively. (Fig. 1c, h, i).”

The corrections do not affect the conclusion of the article. The original article [1] has been corrected.
