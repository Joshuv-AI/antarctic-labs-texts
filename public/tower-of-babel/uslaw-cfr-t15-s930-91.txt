15 C.F.R. § 930.91 — Federal assistance.
Citation: us:cfr:15:930.91
Creator: National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-930.91
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 930 (Federal Consistency With Approved Coastal Management Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term “federal assistance” means assistance provided under a federal program to an applicant agency through grant or contractual arrangements, loans, subsidies, guarantees, insurance, or other form of financial aid.
