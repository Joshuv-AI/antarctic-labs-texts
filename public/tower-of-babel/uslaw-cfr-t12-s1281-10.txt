12 C.F.R. § 1281.10 — General.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1281.10
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1281.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1281 (Federal Home Loan Bank Housing Goals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Pursuant to the requirements of the Bank Act, as amended (12 U.S.C. 1430c), this subpart establishes:
(a) A prospective mortgage purchase housing goal;
(b) A small member participation housing goal;
(c) Requirements for measuring performance under the housing goals; and
(d) Procedures for monitoring and enforcing the housing goals.
