16 U.S.C. § 460ll–5 — Authorization of appropriations

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section460ll–5
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter XCVI (Rattlesnake National Recreation Area). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Effective October 1, 1981, there is hereby authorized to be appropriated such funds as may be necessary to carry out the purposes of this subchapter.
