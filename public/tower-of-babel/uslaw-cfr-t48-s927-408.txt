48 C.F.R. § 927.408 — 927.408 Cosponsored research and development activities.
Citation: us:cfr:48:927.408
Creator: Chapter 9—Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-927.408
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 927 (Patents, Data, And Copyrights). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Because of the Department of Energy's statutory duties to disseminate data first produced under its contracts for research, development, and demonstration, the provisions of FAR 27.408 do not apply to cosponsored or cost shared contracts.
