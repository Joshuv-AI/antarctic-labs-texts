22 C.F.R. § 151.4 — Minimum limits for motor vehicle insurance.
Citation: us:cfr:22:151.4
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-151.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 151 (Compulsory Liability Insurance For Diplomatic Missions And Personnel). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The insurance shall provide not less than $100,000 per person and $300,000 per incident for bodily injury liability and $100,000 per incident for property damage or $300,000 combined single limit for all bodily injury liability and property damage liability arising from a single incident, except where the Director of the Office of Foreign Missions grants a special exception.
