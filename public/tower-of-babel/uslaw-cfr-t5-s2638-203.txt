5 C.F.R. § 2638.203 — Collection of public financial disclosure reports required to be submitted to the Office of Government Ethics.
Citation: us:cfr:5:2638.203
Creator: Office Of Government Ethics
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-2638.203
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 2638 (Executive Branch Ethics Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The public financial disclosure reports of individuals, other than candidates for elected office and elected officials, whose reports are required by section 103 of the Act to be transmitted to the Office of Government Ethics will be transmitted through the executive branch-wide electronic filing system of the Office of Government Ethics, except in cases in which the Director determines that using that system would be impracticable.
