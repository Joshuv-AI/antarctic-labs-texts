7 C.F.R. § 57.134 — Accessibility of product.
Citation: us:cfr:7:57.134
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-57.134
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 57 (Inspection Of Eggs (Egg Products Inspection Act)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each product for which inspection service is required shall be so placed as to disclose fully its class, quality, quantity, and condition as the circumstances may warrant.
