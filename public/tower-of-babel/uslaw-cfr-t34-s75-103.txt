34 C.F.R. § 75.103 — Deadline date for preapplications.
Citation: us:cfr:34:75.103
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-75.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 75 (Direct Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If the Secretary invites or requires preapplications under a program, the application notice for the program sets a deadline date for preapplications.
(b) An applicant shall submit its preapplication in accordance with the procedures for applications in § 75.102(d).
