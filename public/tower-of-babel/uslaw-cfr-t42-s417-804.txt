42 C.F.R. § 417.804 — Cost apportionment.
Citation: us:cfr:42:417.804
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-417.804
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 417 (Health Maintenance Organizations, Competitive Medical Plans, And Health Care Prepayment Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The HCPP follows the cost apportionment principles specified in §§ 417.552 through 417.566, except for provisions on provider costs and provisions on departmental apportionment.
(b) The HCPP may use a method for reporting costs that is approved by CMS. CMS bases its approval on a finding that the method—
(1) Results in an accurate and equitable allocation of allowable costs; and
(2) Is justifiable from an administrative and cost efficiency standpoint.
