41 C.F.R. § 101-27.507 — -27.507 Transportation and other costs.
Citation: us:cfr:41:101-27.507
Creator: Chapter 101—Federal Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-101-27.507
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 101-27 (27—Inventory Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Transportation costs for the movement of material to GSA and handling costs for preparation and shipment shall be paid by the activity shipping the material to GSA.
