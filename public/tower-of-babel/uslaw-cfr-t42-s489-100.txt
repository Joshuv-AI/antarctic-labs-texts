42 C.F.R. § 489.100 — Definition.
Citation: us:cfr:42:489.100
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-489.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 489 (Provider Agreements And Supplier Approval). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For purposes of this part, advance directive means a written instruction, such as a living will or durable power of attorney for health care, recognized under State law (whether statutory or as recognized by the courts of the State), relating to the provision of health care when the individual is incapacitated.
