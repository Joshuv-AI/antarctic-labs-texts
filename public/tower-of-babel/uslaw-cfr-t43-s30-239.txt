43 C.F.R. § 30.239 — Does any distribution of the estate occur while a petition for rehearing is pending?
Citation: us:cfr:43:30.239
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-30.239
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 30 (Indian Probate Hearings Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The agencies must not initiate payment of claims or distribute any portion of the estate while the petition is pending, unless otherwise directed by the judge.
