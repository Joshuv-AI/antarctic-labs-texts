Enhanced Hantzsche Theorem
Michael H. Freedman

Abstract
. A closed 3-manifold M M may be described up to some indeterminacy by a Heegaard diagram 𝒟 \mathcal{D} . The question “Does M M smoothly embed in ℝ 4 \mathbb{R}^{4} ?” is equivalent to a property of 𝒟 \mathcal{D} which we call doubly unlinked (DU). This perspective leads to an enhancement of Hantzsche’s embedding obstruction.
