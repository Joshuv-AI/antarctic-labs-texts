47 C.F.R. § 32.4510 — Capital stock.
Citation: us:cfr:47:32.4510
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-32.4510
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 32 (Uniform System Of Accounts For Telecommunications Companies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) This account shall include the par value, stated amount, or in the case of no-par stock, the amount received for capital stock issued and outstanding.
(b) Subsidiary records shall be maintained so as to show separately each class of stock.
(c) This account shall be charged with the book amount of any stock retired.
