18 C.F.R. § 808.13 — Notice of violation.
Citation: us:cfr:18:808.13
Creator: Susquehanna River Basin Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-808.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 808 (Hearings And Enforcement Actions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When the Executive Director or his/her designee issues a Notice of Violation (NOV) to an alleged violator, such NOV will:
(a) List the violations that are alleged to have occurred.
(b) State a date by which the alleged violator shall respond to the NOV.
