22 U.S.C. § 6010 — “United States person” defined
Citation: us:usc:22:6010
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section6010
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 69 (Cuban Democracy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this chapter, the term “United States person” means any United States citizen or alien admitted for permanent residence in the United States, and any corporation, partnership, or other organization organized under the laws of the United States.
