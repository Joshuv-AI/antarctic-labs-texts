2 U.S.C. § 4701 — Subletting duties of employees of Senate or House
Citation: us:usc:2:4701
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section4701
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 47 (Congressional Ethics); Subchapter I (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No employee of Congress, either in the Senate or House, shall sublet to, or hire, another to do or perform any part of the duties or work attached to the position to which he was appointed.
