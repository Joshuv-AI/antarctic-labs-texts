36 C.F.R. § 401.7 — Cooperation with other than Government entities.
Citation: us:cfr:36:401.7
Creator: American Battle Monuments Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-401.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 401 (Monuments And Memorials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission has the discretion to cooperate with citizens of the United States, States, municipalities, or associations desiring to build war memorials outside the United States.
