43 C.F.R. § 3815.2 — Prospecting and mining.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3815.2
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3815.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3810 (Lands And Minerals Subject To Location). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All prospecting and mining operations shall be conducted in such manner as to cause no interference with the use of the surface of the land for stock driveway purposes, except such as may actually be necessary.
