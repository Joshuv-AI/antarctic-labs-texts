29 C.F.R. § 1925.2 — Safety and health standards.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1925.2
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1925.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1925 (Safety And Health Standards For Federal Service Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Every contractor and subcontractor shall comply with the safety and health standards published in 41 CFR part 50-204, including any matters incorporated by reference therein.
