46 C.F.R. § 140.660 — Vessel security.
Citation: us:cfr:46:140.660
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-140.660
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 140 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each towing vessel must be operated in compliance with:
(a) The Maritime Transportation Security Act of 2002 (46 U.S.C. Chapter 701); and
(b) 33 CFR parts 101 and 104, as applicable.
