46 C.F.R. § 310.11 — Cadet uniforms.
Citation: us:cfr:46:310.11
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-310.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 310 (Merchant Marine Training). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Cadet uniforms shall be supplied at the school in accordance with the uniform regulations of the School. Those regulations shall prescribe a distinctive insignia or device approved by the Maritime Administrator.
