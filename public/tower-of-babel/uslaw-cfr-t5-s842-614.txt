5 C.F.R. § 842.614 — Computation of partial annuity reduction.
Citation: us:cfr:5:842.614
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-842.614
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 842 (Federal Employees Retirement System—Basic Annuity). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a court order or the death of a current or former spouse results in providing less than the maximum permitted survivor reduction under § 842.613, the reduction in the employee's annuity will be 10 percent of the amount of the employee's annuity on which the survivor benefits will be computed (called the “base”).
