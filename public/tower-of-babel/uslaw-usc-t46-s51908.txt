46 U.S.C. § 51908 — Prohibition against unauthorized manufacture, sale, possession, or display of awards
Citation: us:usc:46:51908
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section51908
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part B (Merchant Marine Service); Chapter 519 (Merchant Marine Awards). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Prohibition.—
Except as authorized by this chapter or the Secretary of Transportation, a person may not manufacture, sell, possess, or display a decoration or medal provided for in this chapter.
(b) Civil Penalty.—
A person violating this section is liable to the United States Government for a civil penalty of not more than $2,000.
