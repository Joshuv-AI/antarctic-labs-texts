2 U.S.C. § 6540 — Adjustment of rate of compensation by Secretary of Senate
Citation: us:usc:2:6540
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section6540
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 65 (Senate Officers And Administration); Subchapter II (Secretary Of The Senate); Part A (Organization And Personnel). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any specific rate of compensation established by law, as such rate has been increased or may hereafter be increased by or pursuant to law, for any position under the jurisdiction of the Secretary shall be considered as the maximum rate of compensation for that position, and the Secretary is authorized to adjust the rate of compensation of an individual occupying any such position to a rate not exceeding such maximum rate.
