36 C.F.R. § 1223.24 — When can vital records be destroyed?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1223.24
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1223.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1223 (Managing Vital Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The disposition of vital records that are original records is governed by records schedules approved by NARA (see part 1225, Scheduling Records, of this subchapter). Agencies must not destroy original records that are not scheduled. Duplicate copies created and maintained for vital records purposes only may be destroyed when superseded or obsolete during the routine vital records cycle process.
