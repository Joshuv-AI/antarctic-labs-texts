7 C.F.R. § 82.9 — Inspection and certification of diversion.
Citation: us:cfr:7:82.9
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-82.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 82 (Clingstone Peach Diversion Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Removal of clingstone peach trees under the Program must be completed within 60 days of receipt of the Notification of Clingstone Peach Tree Removal form. When the removal of the clingstone peach trees is complete, the grower will notify CCPA on the Notification of Clingstone Peach Tree Removal form provided by CCPA. CCPA will certify that the trees approved for removal from the acreage have been removed and notify AMS to release payment.
