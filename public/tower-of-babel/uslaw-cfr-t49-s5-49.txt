49 C.F.R. § 5.49 — Reports to Congress and GAO.
Citation: us:cfr:49:5.49
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-5.49
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 5 (Administrative Rulemaking, Guidance, And Enforcement Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless otherwise determined in writing by OGC, it is the policy of the Department that upon issuing a guidance document determined to be “significant” within the meaning of section 5.37, the issuing OA or component of OST will submit a report to Congress and GAO in accordance with the procedures described in 5 U.S.C. 801 (the “Congressional Review Act”).
