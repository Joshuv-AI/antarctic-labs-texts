The Global Existence and Uniqueness of Maxwell-Chern-Simons-Higgs Equation in (2+1) Dimensions
Mulyanto, Ardian N. Atmaja, Fiki T. Akbar, Bobby E. Gunara

Abstract
In this paper, we show the global existence and uniqueness of classical solutions of the Maxwell-Chern-Simmons-Higgs system coupled to a neutral scalar with nontrivial scalar potential on (2+1) dimensional Minkowski spacetime. Our methods rely only on classical existence theorems, including energy estimates, the Sobolev inequality, and the choice of the Coulomb gauge condition. The equations are well-posed for finite initial data and the solution preserves any additional H s H^{s} regularity for s > 0 s>0 in the data.
