Introduction

The characteristics of fluids saturated by bi-hybrid nanomaterials [1] in channel made by walls with uniform slope is of broaden significance from applications point of view. Such setup is beneficial for thermal dynamics of heat exchanging devices, micro and nano-leveled systems. The oblique flow geometry attracted the researcher’s attention because of their configuration and forces exerted by the walls. Extending nanofluid’s study in oblique channel also enhances the applicability of flow transport regarding thermal applications. These fluids are much better for cooling and heating applications than common fluid classes. The reason of enriched thermal conductivity provided by the inclusion of nanoparticles [2] in fluid make the traditional classes more effective for thermal applications. This class also helpful to maintain the temperature at desired level due to the control of nanoparticles addition. Thus, these tinny nanomaterials can be considered as catalytic agent to control the model performance. Analysis of nanofluid problems is slanted walls channel is of wider interest for optimizing the engineering and industrial applications.

Implication of magnetic field on the physical models comprising the study of nanofluids affects the performance by producing extra forces in the system which alter the dynamics of heat transfer. This is due to nanoparticles [3] and fluid motion in the fluidic setup. Magnetic field from multiple orientation along with nanofluids is a potential motive for heat optimization in new cooling devices. Production of Lorentz forces according to the applied strength provides resistive force factor which is good for flow control and also influenced in the heat gradient. The geometric shape associated to magnetic field explores more fascinating applications of nanofluids in which flow through convergent/divergent channel is one of configuration. Orientation of applied field can behave contrarily on the fluid flow; thus, direction of acting magnetic field on the system is important. Transversely oriented magnetic field is a helping tool which produces eddy phenomena in the fluids, causes reduction in the energy consumption in industrial processes. Power generation and reactors section cooling working on this process to acquire the heat at different levels by applying multiple strength magnetic field.

The heating source and natural radiations [4] are potential ways to augment the functionality of nanofluids. The radiative thermal transmission is good for combustion processes and cope the heat problem in aircrafts, where the surfaces exhibiting dominant temperature. Modifying the energy model with these constraints are beneficial for heat dissipation from the surface. Impacts of radiations and heating parameters further enhances the proficiency of nanofluids in which the absorption of heat by the nanoparticles has critical role. Interaction of these quantities with nanoparticles provides excellent heat mechanism in nanofluids. Thus, exploration of nanofluids inclined walls channel is imperative from applications perspectives. Sari et al. [5], made efforts for heat transfer models with addition of magnetic forces. The unique configuration of oblique walls attracted the researchers and they show that MHD control the flow and weak effects reduces the backflow separation from the walls.

Dogonchi and Ganji [6], reported an approximate analytical investigation has been undertaken to examine the effects of key governing parameters, including the stretching/shrinking rate, thermal radiation, Reynolds number, Hartmann number, channel divergence angle, and nanoparticle solid volume fraction. The analysis demonstrates that both velocity and thermal distributions increase with a rise in the stretching parameter. Vinutha et al. [7] further examined that enhancement of local and external pollutant sources promotes concentration within divergent channels, whereas a decline is observed for convergent configurations. The inclusion of nanoparticles intensifies the surface drag force, suggesting significant implications for thermal regulation, mitigation of pollutant dispersion, and improved circulation in nanofluid systems. Further investigations have been made by various researchers (see Refs [8–11]). for the applications of nanofluids [12, 13] applications in engineering regarding the heat mechanism problems. The findings of Akinshilo et al. [14] indicate that an increase in the Reynolds number elevates the effective viscosity while reducing the velocity field. Similarly, heat transfer is suppressed at higher Darcy numbers due to reduced flow intensity, thereby diminishing internal friction. Comparison with previously published studies confirms the consistency and accuracy of the present results. Bég et al. [15] analyzed that in divergent channels, larger opening angles combined with higher Reynolds numbers induce a pronounced reduction in temperature, while the inverse trend is recorded for convergent channels.

Saadoon et al. [16] conducted numerical investigation and reveal that the integration of nanofluids in converge-diverge mini-channels markedly enhances the hydrothermal performance of heat sinks. Among the tested working fluids, Ag-H2O nanofluid demonstrates superior heat transfer efficiency over Fe3O4-H2O, achieving up to a 43.27% improvement in the Nusselt number at a nanoparticle volume concentration of 0.075%. The recent trends in the field of nanofluids explored potential results for heat transfer using multiple schemes (see the Refs [17–20]., and [21]). These studies highlighted different aspects of heat transport using nanofluids and their applications in industrial areas. Moreover, Ashraf [22] studied combined influence of absorption and Joule heating and shown that it plays a constructive role in controlling thermal transport, with ternary nanofluids containing blade-shaped nanoparticles exhibiting a significant enhancement in heat transfer performance. Jan et al. [23] observed that at increased solid volume fractions, distinct flow characteristics are observed: velocity diminishes in divergent channels while it intensifies in convergent channels. The influence of the Eckert number and porosity parameter is evident in the skin friction behavior, where an increase is recorded for divergent geometries and a reduction for convergent configurations, applicable to both nanofluids and hybrid nanofluids. Notably, hybrid nanocomposites exhibit more dominant thermophysical effects compared to conventional nanocomposites in both expanding and contracting channels.

Ullah et al. [24] shown that an increase in the couple stress parameter enhances resistance to wall shear stress and simultaneously promotes the transport of hybrid nanofluids, thereby improving the overall stability of the base fluid (blood). Moreover, numerical analysis confirms that variations in nanoparticle volume fraction yield a significant percentage rise in heat transfer capability. The study of Kurtulmuş et al. [25] reveals a substantial enhancement in thermal transport, albeit accompanied by a marked pressure drop. The maximum thermal performance factor (TPF) is achieved as 1.46 for M=0.5. Numerical simulations, conducted under identical parametric conditions, are in strong agreement with experimental findings, thereby validating the model predictions. Ramesh et al. [26] exercised the Runge-Kutta-Fehlberg (RKF) fourth-fifth order integration technique, and the obtained solutions are benchmarked against previously reported results. The physical implications of the governing constraints are illustrated through graphical interpretations, demonstrating a reduction in velocity within divergent channels and an enhancement within convergent channels at higher particle concentration. Furthermore, the porosity parameter and inertia coefficient are shown to amplify velocity near the channel walls, underscoring their critical role in regulating flow behavior.

Research gap

Despite extensive progress in heat transfer using nanofluids, the Jeffery Hamel flow (flow through converging/diverging channel) for ferrite nanomaterials (NiZnFe2O4-MnZnFe2O4)/C2H6O2 hybrid nanofluid insufficiently discussed, specifically by implementing data driven scheme. The previous attempts made using numerical approaches and provide limited insights into whether the ANN approach can precisely learn the combined framework for the parameters, thermal transport properties and the channel configuration. The function of adding NiZnFe2O4-MnZnFe2O4 in C2H6O2 with artificial intelligence based scheme provides a potential gap that has gained limited attention in converging diverging channel flows. Addressing this significant gap offers a systematic model for quantifying thermal response and assessing the ANN predictive ability rather than extending the traditional nanofluids simulation

Novelty

The novelty of this attempt is to develop integrated computational model for Jeffery Hamel and the contribution of NiZnFe2O4-MnZnFe2O4 in the heat mechanism of C2H6O2 by combining the distinct thermophysical characteristics of ferrite nanomaterial. The investigation prolongs the traditional Jeffery Hamel analysis by examining the influence of driving parameters like Reynolds, Eckert, hybrid nanomaterial concentration, and converging diverging parameter on the dynamics and associated heat transport in hybrid (NiZnFe2O4-MnZnFe2O4)/C2H6O2. Further, the methodological novelty falls in the addition of an ANN as a data driven model that trained using bvp4c to achieve the effective results between the varying parameters and the model thermal response. The proposed study develops an integrated approach in which physics based model and ANN used together to describe thermal transport in Jeffery Hamel flow of hybrid nanofluid. Thus, the framework adds new computational insights into the heat transport response to controlling governing parameters while allowing an effective framework for predicting output in investigated parametric domain

The characteristics of nanofluid laminar flow in the channel greatly changes when the parameters vary over certain ranges. This decides that how the flow control or thermal efficiency of the functional fluid can be maintained. Thus, this section decorates the physical results of the model for both convergent and divergent cases.

Figures 3 and 4 elaborate the physical scenarios of the velocity and temperature inside convergent and divergent channel. Increase in the concentration enhances the velocity in convergent while it reduces in divergent channel. However, the fluid attains optimum velocity about the center line . In divergent case, the decrement is examined as the concentration increases. Physically, the opening channel prolong the flow area as a moment the fluid layers flow towards the space provided due to the walls opening. Thus, it reduces the velocity. In contrast, the convergent channel enhances the velocity due to increase in the pressure because the force per unit area becomes stronger. This physical situation leads to the velocity enhancement. At high concentration , the fluid layers move back and hence produces backflow to some extent. Similarly, for stronger Re the velocity declines and more back movement of the layers is examined. Figure 4 highlights the temperature versus the parameters. Under this model, the efficiency drops for Eckert and it prominently increases for Re. For convergent case, the optimum changes in the temperature are noticed. At the walls, the temperature vanishes as per the implemented conditions and the fluid near the center attains maximum temperature.

Limitations and future work

The present research is limited to viscous incompressible model for hybrid nanofluid flow in Jeffery Hamel configuration in the absence of chemical reaction and hybrid nanomaterial and primary solvent are homogenous mixture. The results obtained under laminar flow regimes with specified ranges of the parameters. The future work on this topic could be extended for non-Newtonian, cross fluid, combining mass and bioconvective models, perpendicular and inclined influence of magnetic field, and advanced nanofluids. Further, extension could be made by using machine learning approaches for their effectiveness for Jeffery Hamel problems

Possible applications

The current research on theoretical heat transfer investigation in Jeffery Hamel flow provides essential into thermal transport in convergent divergent flows and the hybrid nanomaterial improved the fluid characteristics. These findings may be relevant to mining technology linking the heat exchangers, thermal management of machinery, mineral equipment processing and cooling where flow area is of converging diverging channel form. However, the potential uses should be viewed as potential engineering relevance, since the research model is theoretical instead of experimental
