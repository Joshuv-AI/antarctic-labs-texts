33 C.F.R. § 179.07 — Notice given by “more expeditious means”.
Citation: us:cfr:33:179.07
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-179.07
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 179 (Defect Notification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each manufacturer who gives notice by more expeditious means as provided for in 46 U.S.C. 4310(c)(1)(C), must give such notice in writing.
