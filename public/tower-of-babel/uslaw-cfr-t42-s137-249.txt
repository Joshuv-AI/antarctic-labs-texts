42 C.F.R. § 137.249 — Will retrocession adversely affect funding available for the retroceded program?
Citation: us:cfr:42:137.249
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-137.249
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 137 (Tribal Self-Governance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No, the Secretary shall provide no less than the same level of funding that would have been available if there had been no retrocession.
