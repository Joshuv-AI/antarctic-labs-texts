34 C.F.R. § 75.534 — Training grants—automatic increases for additional dependents.
Citation: us:cfr:34:75.534
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-75.534
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 75 (Direct Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary may increase a grant to cover the cost of additional dependents not specified in the notice of award under § 75.235 if—
(a) Allowances for dependents are authorized by applicable statutes and regulations and are allowable under the grant; and
(b) Appropriations are available to cover the cost.
