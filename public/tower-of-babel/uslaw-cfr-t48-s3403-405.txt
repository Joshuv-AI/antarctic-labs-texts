48 C.F.R. § 3403.405 — 3403.405 Misrepresentation or violations of the covenant against contingent fees.
Citation: us:cfr:48:3403.405
Creator: Chapter 34—Department Of Education Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-3403.405
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 3403 (Improper Business Practices And Personal Conflicts Of Interest). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any Departmental personnel who suspect or have evidence of attempted or actual exercise of improper influence, misrepresentation of a contingent fee arrangement, or other violation of the Covenant Against Contingent Fees, must report the matter promptly in accordance with the procedures in 3403.203.
