28 U.S.C. § 254 — Single-judge trials
Citation: us:usc:28:254
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section254
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part I (Organization Of Courts); Chapter 11 (Court Of International Trade). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as otherwise provided in section 255 of this title, the judicial power of the Court of International Trade with respect to any action, suit or proceeding shall be exercised by a single judge, who may preside alone and hold a regular or special session of court at the same time other sessions are held by other judges.
