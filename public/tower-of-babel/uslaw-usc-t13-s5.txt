13 U.S.C. § 5 — Questionnaires; number, form, and scope of inquiries
Citation: us:usc:13:5
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title13-section5
Context: United States Code, Title 13 (Census); Chapter 1 (Administration); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall prepare questionnaires, and shall determine the inquiries, and the number, form, and subdivisions thereof, for the statistics, surveys, and censuses provided for in this title.
