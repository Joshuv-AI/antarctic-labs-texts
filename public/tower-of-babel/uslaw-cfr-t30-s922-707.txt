30 C.F.R. § 922.707 — Exemption for coal extraction incident to government-financed highway or other construction.
Citation: us:cfr:30:922.707
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-922.707
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 922 (Michigan). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Part 707 of this chapter, Exemption for Coal Extraction Incident to Government-Financed Highway or Other Construction, shall apply to surface coal mining and reclamation operations.
