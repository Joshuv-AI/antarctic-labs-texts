30 U.S.C. § 1521 — Reservoir insurance program study
Citation: us:usc:30:1521
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section1521
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 27 (Geothermal Energy); Subchapter II (Study, Establishment, And Implementation Of Insurance Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall conduct a detailed study of the need for and feasibility of establishing a reservoir insurance and reinsurance program incorporating the terms, conditions, and provisions set forth in section 1522 of this title, and shall submit to the Congress within one year after June 30, 1980, a report on the results of such study including his findings and recommendations with respect thereto.
