48 C.F.R. § 8.001 — 8.001 General.
Citation: us:cfr:48:8.001
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-8.001
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 8 (Required Sources Of Supplies And Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Regardless of the source of supplies or services to be acquired, information technology acquisitions shall comply with capital planning and investment control requirements in 40 U.S.C. 11312 and OMB Circular A-130.
