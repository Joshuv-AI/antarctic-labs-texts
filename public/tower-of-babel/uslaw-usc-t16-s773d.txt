16 U.S.C. § 773d — Cooperation of Federal agencies
Citation: us:usc:16:773d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section773d
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 10 (Northern Pacific Halibut Fishing); Subchapter IV (Northern Pacific Halibut Act Of 1982). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any agency of the Federal Government is authorized upon request of the Commission, to cooperate in the conduct of scientific and other programs, and to furnish on a reimbursable basis, facilities and personnel for the purposes of assisting the Commission in carrying out its duties under the Convention. Such agency may accept reimbursement from the Commission.
