22 U.S.C. § 4853 — Security requirements for contractors
Citation: us:usc:22:4853
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section4853
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 58 (Diplomatic Security); Subchapter IV (Diplomatic Security Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 90 days after August 27, 1986, the Secretary of State shall issue regulations to—
(1)
strengthen the security procedures applicable to contractors and subcontractors involved in any way with any diplomatic construction or design project; and
(2)
permit a contractor or subcontractor to have access to any design or blueprint relating to such a project only in accordance with those procedures.
