40 U.S.C. § 3302 — Prohibition on construction of buildings except by Administrator of General Services
Citation: us:usc:40:3302
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section3302
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Part A (General); Chapter 33 (Acquisition, Construction, And Alteration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Only the Administrator of General Services may construct a public building. The Administrator shall construct a public building in accordance with this chapter.
