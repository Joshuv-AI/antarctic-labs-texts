36 C.F.R. § 1256.94 — Definition.
Citation: us:cfr:36:1256.94
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1256.94
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1256 (Access To Records And Donated Historical Materials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For the purposes of this subpart, “Audiovisual records” mean motion picture films, videotapes, and sound recordings, and other materials regardless of physical form or characteristics that were prepared for dissemination abroad.
