43 C.F.R. § 3284.10 — What must a unit operator do to prevent or compensate for drainage?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3284.10
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3284.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3280 (Geothermal Resources Unit Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The unit operator must take all necessary measures to prevent or compensate for drainage of geothermal resources from unitized land by wells on land not subject to the unit agreement (see §§ 3210.16 and 3210.17 of this chapter).
