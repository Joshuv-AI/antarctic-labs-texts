27 C.F.R. § 73.34 — When is an electronically submitted form considered timely filed?
Citation: us:cfr:27:73.34
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-73.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 73 (Electronic Signatures; Electronic Submission Of Forms). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you submit a form to our electronic document receiving system, the document will be considered filed on the date of the electronic postmark given by that system.
