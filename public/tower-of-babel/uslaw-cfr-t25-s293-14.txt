25 C.F.R. § 293.14 — When does a compact or amendment take effect?
Citation: us:cfr:25:293.14
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-293.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 293 (Class Iii Tribal-State Gaming Compacts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A compact or amendment, that is affirmatively approved or approved by operation of law, takes effect on the date that notice of its approval is published in the Federal Register.
(b) The notice of affirmative approval or approval by operation of law must be published in the Federal Register within 90 days from the date the compact or amendment is received by the Office of Indian Gaming.
