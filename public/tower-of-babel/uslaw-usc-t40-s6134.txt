40 U.S.C. § 6134 — Firearms, fireworks, speeches, and objectionable language in the Supreme Court Building and grounds
Citation: us:usc:40:6134
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section6134
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Part C (Federal Building Complexes); Chapter 61 (United States Supreme Court Building And Grounds); Subchapter IV (Prohibitions And Penalties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is unlawful to discharge a firearm, firework or explosive, set fire to a combustible, make a harangue or oration, or utter loud, threatening, or abusive language in the Supreme Court Building or grounds.
