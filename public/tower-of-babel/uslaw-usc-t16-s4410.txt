16 U.S.C. § 4410 — Revisions to Plan
Citation: us:usc:16:4410
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section4410
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 64 (North American Wetlands Conservation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall, in 1998 and at five-year intervals thereafter, undertake with the appropriate officials in Canada and Mexico to revise the goals and other elements of the Plan in accordance with the information required under section 4409 of this title and with the other provisions of this chapter.
