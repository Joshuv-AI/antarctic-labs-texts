18 C.F.R. § 367.4116 — Account 411.6, Gains from disposition of service company plant.
Citation: us:cfr:18:367.4116
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-367.4116
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 367 (Uniform System Of Accounts For Centralized Service Companies Subject To The Provisions Of The Public Utility Holding Company Act Of 2005, Federal Power Act And Natural Gas Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The service company must record in this account gains resulting from the settlement of asset retirement obligations related to service company plant in accordance with the accounting prescribed in General Instructions in § 367.22.
(b) Income taxes relating to losses, recorded in this account must be recorded in Account 409.1, Income Taxes, operating income (§ 367.4091).
