49 C.F.R. § 192.189 — Vaults: Drainage and waterproofing.
Citation: us:cfr:49:192.189
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-192.189
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 192 (Transportation Of Natural And Other Gas By Pipeline: Minimum Federal Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each vault must be designed so as to minimize the entrance of water.
(b) A vault containing gas piping may not be connected by means of a drain connection to any other underground structure.
(c) Electrical equipment in vaults must conform to the applicable requirements of Class 1, Group D, of the National Electrical Code, NFPA-70 (incorporated by reference, see § 192.7).
