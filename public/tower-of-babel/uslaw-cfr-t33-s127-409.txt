33 C.F.R. § 127.409 — Records.
Citation: us:cfr:33:127.409

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 127 (Waterfront Facilities Handling Liquefied Natural Gas And Liquefied Hazardous Gas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The operator must keep on file the following information:
(1) A description of the components tested under § 127.407.
(2) The date and results of the test under § 127.407.
(3) A description of any corrective action taken after the test.
(b) The information required by this section must be retained for 24 months.
