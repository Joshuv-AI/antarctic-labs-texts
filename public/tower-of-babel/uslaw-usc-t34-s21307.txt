34 U.S.C. § 21307 — Allocation of increased formula grant funds
Citation: us:usc:34:21307
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section21307
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 213 (Rape Survivor Child Custody). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Attorney General shall allocate an increase in the amount provided to a State under the covered formula grants under this chapter such that—
(1)
25 percent 1 the amount of the increase is provided under the program described in section 21301(1)(A) of this title; and
(2)
75 percent 1 the amount of the increase is provided under the program described in section 21301(1)(B) of this title.
