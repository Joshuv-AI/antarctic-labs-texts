31 C.F.R. § 585.301 — Blocked account; blocked property.
Citation: us:cfr:31:585.301
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-585.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 585 (Hong Kong-Related Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The terms blocked account and blocked property shall mean any account or property subject to the prohibitions in § 585.201 held in the name of a person whose property and interests in property are blocked pursuant to § 585.201, or in which such person has an interest, and with respect to which payments, transfers, exportations, withdrawals, or other dealings may not be made or effected except pursuant to a license or other authorization from OFAC expressly authorizing such action.
