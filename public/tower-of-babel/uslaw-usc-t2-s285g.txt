2 U.S.C. § 285g — Availability of applicable accounts of House
Citation: us:usc:2:285g
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section285g
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 9A (Office Of Law Revision Counsel). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Until such time as funds are appropriated by law to carry out the purpose of this chapter, the applicable accounts of the House of Representatives shall be available for such purpose.
