25 C.F.R. § 170.436 — How are public hearings for TTP planning and projects funded?
Citation: us:cfr:25:170.436
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-170.436
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 170 (Tribal Transportation Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Public hearings for a TTIP or a Tribe's LRTP are funded using the Tribe's funds as described in § 170.403.
