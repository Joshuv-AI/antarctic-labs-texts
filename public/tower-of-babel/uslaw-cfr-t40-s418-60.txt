40 C.F.R. § 418.60 — Applicability; description of the ammonium sulfate production subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:418.60
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-418.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 418 (Fertilizer Manufacturing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart apply to discharges resulting from the production of ammonium sulfate by the synthetic process and by coke oven by-product recovery. The provisions of this subpart do not apply to ammonium sulfate produced as a by-product of caprolactam production.
