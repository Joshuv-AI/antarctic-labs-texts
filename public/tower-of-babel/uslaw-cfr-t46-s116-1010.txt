46 C.F.R. § 116.1010 — Safety glazing materials.
Citation: us:cfr:46:116.1010
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-116.1010
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 116 (Construction And Arrangement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Glass and other glazing material used in windows must be of material that will not break into dangerous fragments if fractured.
