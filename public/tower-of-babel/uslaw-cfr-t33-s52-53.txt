33 C.F.R. § 52.53 — Witnesses.
Citation: us:cfr:33:52.53
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-52.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 52 (Board For Correction Of Military Records Of The Coast Guard). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) In any case in which the Chair has granted a hearing, the applicant shall have the right to present witnesses.
(b) It is the responsibility of the applicant to notify his or her witnesses and to ensure their appearance at the date, time, and place set for the hearing.
