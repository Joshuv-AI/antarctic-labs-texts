18 U.S.C. § 1661 — Robbery ashore
Citation: us:usc:18:1661
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section1661
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 81 (Piracy And Privateering). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever, being engaged in any piratical cruise or enterprise, or being of the crew of any piratical vessel, lands from such vessel and commits robbery on shore, is a pirate, and shall be imprisoned for life.
