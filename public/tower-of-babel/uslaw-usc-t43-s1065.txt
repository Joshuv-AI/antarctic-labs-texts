43 U.S.C. § 1065 — Summary removal of inclosures
Citation: us:usc:43:1065
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section1065
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 25 (Unlawful Inclosures Or Occupancy; Obstructing Settlement Or Transit). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President is authorized to take such measures as shall be necessary to remove and destroy any unlawful inclosure of any of the public lands mentioned in this chapter, and to employ civil or military force as may be necessary for that purpose.
