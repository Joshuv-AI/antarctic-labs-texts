2 U.S.C. § 5304 — Salaries of Representatives, Delegates, and Resident Commissioners elected for unexpired terms
Citation: us:usc:2:5304
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section5304
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 53 (House Of Representatives Members); Subchapter I (Member Pay). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The salaries of Representatives in Congress, Delegates from Territories, and Resident Commissioners, elected for unexpired terms, shall commence on the date of their election and not before.
