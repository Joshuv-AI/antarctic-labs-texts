16 U.S.C. § 79n — Authorization of appropriations for rehabilitation programs
Citation: us:usc:16:79n
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section79n
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter VII (Redwood National Park). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Effective on October 1, 1978, there are hereby authorized to be appropriated $33,000,000 to carry out the rehabilitation provisions of this Act.
