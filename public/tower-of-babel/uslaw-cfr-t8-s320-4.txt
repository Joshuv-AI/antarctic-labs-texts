8 C.F.R. § 320.4 — Who must appear for an interview on the application for citizenship?
Citation: us:cfr:8:320.4
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-320.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 320 (Child Born Outside The United States And Residing Permanently In The United States; Requirements For Automatic Acquisition Of Citizenship). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All applicants (and U.S. citizen parent(s) if application filed on behalf of a minor biological or adopted child) must appear for examination unless such examination is waived under the guidelines expressed in § 341.2 of this chapter.
