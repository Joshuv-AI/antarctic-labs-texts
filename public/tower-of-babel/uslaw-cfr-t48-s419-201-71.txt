48 C.F.R. § 419.201-71 — 419.201-71 Small business coordinators.
Citation: us:cfr:48:419.201-71
Creator: Chapter 4—Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-419.201-71
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 419 (Small Business Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Mission Area senior contracting official shall designate, in writing, small business coordinator(s). The number of coordinators shall be determined by the Mission Area senior contracting official and sufficient for the number of contracting officers or contracting offices.
