14 C.F.R. § 121.275 — Fire detectors.
Citation: us:cfr:14:121.275
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-121.275
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 121 (Operating Requirements: Domestic, Flag, And Supplemental Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Fire detectors must be made and installed in a manner that assures their ability to resist, without failure, all vibration, inertia, and other loads to which they may be normally subjected. Fire detectors must be unaffected by exposure to fumes, oil, water, or other fluids that may be present.
