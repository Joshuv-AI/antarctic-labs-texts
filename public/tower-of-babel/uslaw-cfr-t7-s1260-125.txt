7 C.F.R. § 1260.125 — Industry information.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1260.125
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Miscellaneous Commodities), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1260.125
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1260 (Beef Promotion And Research). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Industry information means information and programs that will lead to the development of new markets, marketing strategies, increased efficiency, and activities to enhance the image of the cattle industry.
