49 C.F.R. § 511.62 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:49:511.62
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-511.62
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 511 (Adjudicative Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Average fuel economy standard means an average fuel economy standard established by or pursuant to the Motor Vehicle Information and Cost Savings Act.
Insolvency means the inability to meet expenses when due.
Settlement means a compromise, modification, or remission of a civil penalty assessed under this part for a violation of an average fuel economy standard.
