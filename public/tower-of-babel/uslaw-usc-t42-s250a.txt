42 U.S.C. § 250a — Transfer of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:42:250a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section250a
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter II (General Powers And Duties); Part C (Hospitals, Medical Examinations, And Medical Care). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Attorney General may transfer to the Department of Health and Human Services such amounts as may be necessary for direct expenditures by that Department for medical relief for inmates of Federal penal and correctional institutions.
