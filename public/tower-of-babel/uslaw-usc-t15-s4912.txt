15 U.S.C. § 4912 — Fees and access
Citation: us:usc:15:4912
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section4912
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 75 (National Trade Data Bank). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall provide reasonable public services and access (including electronic access) to any information maintained as part of the Data Bank and may charge reasonable fees consistent with section 552 of title 5.
