12 C.F.R. § 261a.11 — a.11 Restrictions on disclosure.
Citation: us:cfr:12:261a.11
Creator: Federal Reserve System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-261a.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 261a (PART 261a—RULES REGARDING ACCESS TO PERSONAL INFORMATION UNDER THE PRIVACY ACT 1974). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We will not disclose any record about you contained in a system of records to any person or agency without your prior written consent unless the disclosure is authorized by 5 U.S.C. 552a(b).
