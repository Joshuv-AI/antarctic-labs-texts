37 C.F.R. § 11.301 — Meritorious claims and contentions.
Citation: us:cfr:37:11.301
Creator: United States Patent And Trademark Office, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-11.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 11 (Representation Of Others Before The United States Patent And Trademark Office). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A practitioner shall not bring or defend a proceeding, or assert or controvert an issue therein, unless there is a basis in law and fact for doing so that is not frivolous, which includes a good-faith argument for an extension, modification or reversal of existing law.
