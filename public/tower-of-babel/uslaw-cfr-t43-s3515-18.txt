43 C.F.R. § 3515.18 — Will I be notified when BLM is considering initiating an exchange that will affect my lease?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3515.18
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3515.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3500 (Leasing Of Solid Minerals Other Than Coal And Oil Shale). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. The notice you receive will:
(a) State why we believe an exchange would be in the public interest;
(b) Ask whether you are willing to negotiate for an exchange;
(c) Contain a description of the lands for which we would offer exchange terms; and
(d) Ask you to describe the lands on which you would accept a lease in exchange for your present holdings.
