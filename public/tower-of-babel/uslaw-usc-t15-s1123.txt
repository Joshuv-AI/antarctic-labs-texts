15 U.S.C. § 1123 — Rules and regulations for conduct of proceedings in Patent and Trademark Office
Citation: us:usc:15:1123
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1123
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 22 (Trademarks); Subchapter III (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director shall make rules and regulations, not inconsistent with law, for the conduct of proceedings in the Patent and Trademark Office under this chapter.
