47 C.F.R. § 214.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:47:214.1
Creator: Office Of Science And Technology Policy And National Security Council
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-214.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 214 (Procedures For The Use And Coordination Of The Radio Spectrum During A Wartime Emergency). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to provide guidance for the use of the radio spectrum in a period of war, or a threat of war, or a state of public peril or other wartime emergency.
