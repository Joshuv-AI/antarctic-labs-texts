LEGAL SERVICES CORPORATION


Sunshine Act Meetings

FEDERAL REGISTER CITATION OF PREVIOUS ANNOUNCEMENT:  91 FR 64693.

PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED TIME AND DATE OF THE MEETING: The Institutional 
Advancement Committee of the Legal Services Corporation Board of 
Directors will meet on October 21, 2026, 2:00 p.m. Eastern Time.

CHANGES IN THE MEETING: The maters to be discussed during the open 
session meeting of the Institutional Advancement Committee have 
changed. Matters to be discussed now also include endowment 
opportunities for planned giving; and considering and acting on a 
motion to ratify acceptance of an endowment fund.

CONTACT PERSON FOR MORE INFORMATION: Kimberly Little, Board and 
Executive Coordinator, at (202) 295-1500. Questions may also be sent by 
electronic mail to the Office of the Corporate Secretary at 
[email protected].
