40 C.F.R. § 1090.1565 — Requirements related to dispensing liquefied petroleum gas.
Citation: us:cfr:40:1090.1565
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1090.1565
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1090 (Regulation Of Fuels, Fuel Additives, And Regulated Blendstocks). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Except for pumps dedicated to heavy-duty vehicles, any pump installation used for dispensing liquefied petroleum gas into motor vehicles must have a nozzle that has no greater than 2.0 cm 3 dead space from which liquefied petroleum gas will be released when the nozzle disconnects from the vehicle.
(b) Determine the volume of the nozzle cavity using calculations based on the geometric shape of the nozzle, with an assumed flat surface where the nozzle face seals against the vehicle.
