10 C.F.R. § 1008.19 — Criminal penalties—improper disclosure.
Citation: us:cfr:10:1008.19
Creator: Department Of Energy (General Provisions)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1008.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1008 (Records Maintained On Individuals (Privacy Act)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Subsection (i)(1) of the Act provides that a Federal employee who willfully discloses information subject to the Privacy Act in violation of the Act or rules promulgated under it shall be guilty of a misdemeanor and fined up to $5,000.
