5 C.F.R. § 551.214 — Statutory exclusion.
Citation: us:cfr:5:551.214
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-551.214
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 551 (Pay Administration Under The Fair Labor Standards Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A customs officer who receives overtime pay under subsection (a) or premium pay under subsection (b) of 19 U.S.C. 267 and under 19 CFR 24.16 for time worked may not receive pay or other compensation for that work under any other provision of law.
