34 C.F.R. § 206.40 — What restrictions are there on expenditures?
Citation: us:cfr:34:206.40
Creator: Office Of Elementary And Secondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-206.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 206 (Special Educational Programs For Students Whose Families Are Engaged In Migrant And Other Seasonal Farmwork—High School Equivalency Program And College Assistance Migrant Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Funds provided under HEP or CAMP may not be used for construction activities, other than minor construction-related activities such as the repair or minor remodeling or alteration of facilities.
