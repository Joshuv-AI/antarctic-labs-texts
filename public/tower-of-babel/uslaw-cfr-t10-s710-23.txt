10 C.F.R. § 710.23 — Extensions of time by the manager.
Citation: us:cfr:10:710.23
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-710.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 710 (Procedures For Determining Eligibility For Access To Classified Matter And Special Nuclear Material Or Eligibility To Hold A Sensitive Position). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Manager may, for good cause shown, at the written request of the individual, extend the time for filing a written request for a hearing, and/or the time for filing a written answer to the matters contained in the notification letter. The Manager shall notify the Director, in writing, when such extensions have been approved.
