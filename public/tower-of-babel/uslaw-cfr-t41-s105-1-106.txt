41 C.F.R. § 105-1.106 — -1.106 Applicability.

Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-1 (1—Introduction). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Chapter 105 applies to the management of property and records and to certain other programs and activities of GSA. Unless otherwise specified, chapter 105 applies to activities outside as well as within the United States.
