20 U.S.C. § 1136c — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:20:1136c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1136c
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 28 (Higher Education Resources And Student Assistance); Subchapter VII (Graduate And Postsecondary Improvement Programs); Part A (Graduate Education Programs); Subpart 4 (Masters Degree Programs At Historically Black Colleges And Universities And Predominantly Black Institutions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Masters degree programs at historically Black colleges and universities
There are authorized to be appropriated to carry out section 1136a of this title such sums as may be necessary for fiscal year 2009 and each of the five succeeding fiscal years.
(b) Masters degree programs at predominantly Black institutions
There are authorized to be appropriated to carry out section 1136b of this title such sums as may be necessary for fiscal year 2009 and each of the five succeeding fiscal years.
