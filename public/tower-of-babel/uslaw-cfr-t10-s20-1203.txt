10 C.F.R. § 20.1203 — Determination of external dose from airborne radioactive material.
Citation: us:cfr:10:20.1203
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-20.1203
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 20 (Standards For Protection Against Radiation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Licensees shall, when determining the dose from airborne radioactive material, include the contribution to the deep-dose equivalent, lens dose equivalent, and shallow-dose equivalent from external exposure to the radioactive cloud (see appendix B to part 20, footnotes 1 and 2).
