29 C.F.R. § 23.250 — Frequency of pay.
Citation: us:cfr:29:23.250
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-23.250
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 23 (Increasing The Minimum Wage For Federal Contractors). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Wage payments to workers shall be made no later than one pay period following the end of the regular pay period in which such wages were earned or accrued. A pay period under Executive Order 14026 may not be of any duration longer than semi-monthly.
