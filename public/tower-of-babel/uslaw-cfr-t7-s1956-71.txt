7 C.F.R. § 1956.71 — Settling uncollectible recapture receivables.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1956.71
Creator: Rural Housing Service, Rural Business-Cooperative Service, And Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1956.71
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1956 (Debt Settlement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The settlement of uncollectible recapture receivables will be fully documented on a debt settlement form and retained in the case file.
