15 U.S.C. § 1679i — Statute of limitations
Citation: us:usc:15:1679i
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1679i
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 41 (Consumer Credit Protection); Subchapter II–A (Credit Repair Organizations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any action to enforce any liability under this subchapter may be brought before the later of—
(1)
the end of the 5-year period beginning on the date of the occurrence of the violation involved; or
(2)
in any case in which any credit repair organization has materially and willfully misrepresented any information which—
(A)
the credit repair organization is required, by any provision of this subchapter, to disclose to any consumer; and
(B)
is material to the establishment of the credit repair organization’s liability to the consumer under this subchapter,
the end of the 5-year period beginning on the date of the discovery by the consumer of the misrepresentation.
