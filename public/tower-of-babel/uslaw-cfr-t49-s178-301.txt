49 C.F.R. § 178.301 — Alternative requirements for the design, construction, inspection and testing of cargo tank motor vehicles.
Citation: us:cfr:49:178.301
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-178.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 178 (Specifications For Packagings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-28 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Notwithstanding the requirements of this subpart, cargo tank motor vehicles of Specification MC 331 and 338, and DOT 406, 407, or 412 may be designed, constructed, inspected, tested, and certified in accordance with ASME Code Section XII (IBR, see § 171.7 of this subchapter) in conjunction with the NBIC (IBR, see § 171.7 of this subchapter), and in accordance with the conditions and limitations of § 173.252 of this subchapter.
