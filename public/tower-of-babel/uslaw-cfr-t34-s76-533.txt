34 C.F.R. § 76.533 — Acquisition of real property; construction.
Citation: us:cfr:34:76.533
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-76.533
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 76 (State-Administered Formula Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No State or subgrantee may use its grant or subgrant for acquisition of real property or for construction unless specifically permitted by applicable statutes or implementing regulations for the program.
