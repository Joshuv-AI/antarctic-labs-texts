6 C.F.R. § 9.33 — Enforcement.
Citation: us:cfr:6:9.33
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security, Office Of The Secretary
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-6/section-9.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 6 (Domestic Security), Part 9 (Restrictions Upon Lobbying). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The head of each agency shall take such actions as are necessary to ensure that the provisions herein are vigorously implemented and enforced in that agency.
