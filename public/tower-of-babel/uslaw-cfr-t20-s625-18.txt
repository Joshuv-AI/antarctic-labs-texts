20 C.F.R. § 625.18 — Public access to Agreements.
Citation: us:cfr:20:625.18
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-625.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 625 (Disaster Unemployment Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The State agency of a State will make available to any individual or organization a true copy of the Agreement with the State for inspection and copying. Copies of an Agreement may be furnished on request to any individual or organization upon payment of the same charges, if any, as apply to the furnishing of copies of other records of the State agency.
