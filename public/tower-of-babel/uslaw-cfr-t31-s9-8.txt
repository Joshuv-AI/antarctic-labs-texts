31 C.F.R. § 9.8 — Emergency action.
Citation: us:cfr:31:9.8
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-9.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 9 (Effects Of Imported Articles On The National Security). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In emergency situations or when in his judgment national security interests require it, the Secretary may vary or dispense with any of the procedures set forth above and may formulate his views without following such procedures.
