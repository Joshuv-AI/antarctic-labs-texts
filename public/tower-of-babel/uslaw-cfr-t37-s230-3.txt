37 C.F.R. § 230.3 — Response to request.
Citation: us:cfr:37:230.3
Creator: U.S. Copyright Office, Library Of Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-230.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 230 (Requests For Reconsideration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A party opposing a request for a reconsideration may file a response to the request within 30 days of the date of service of the request. Such response shall be no more than 12 pages and shall meet the requirements set forth in § 220.5(b) of this subchapter.
