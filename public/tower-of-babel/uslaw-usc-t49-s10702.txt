49 U.S.C. § 10702 — Authority for rail carriers to establish rates, classifications, rules, and practices
Citation: us:usc:49:10702
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section10702
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part A (Rail); Chapter 107 (Rates); Subchapter I (General Authority). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A rail carrier providing transportation or service subject to the jurisdiction of the Board under this part shall establish reasonable—
(1)
rates, to the extent required by section 10707, divisions of joint rates, and classifications for transportation and service it may provide under this part; and
(2)
rules and practices on matters related to that transportation or service.
