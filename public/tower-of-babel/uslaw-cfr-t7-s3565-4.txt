7 C.F.R. § 3565.4 — Availability of assistance.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3565.4
Creator: Rural Housing Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3565.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3565 (Guaranteed Rural Rental Housing Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Agency's authority to enter into commitments, guarantee loans, or provide interest credits is limited to the extent that appropriations are available to cover the cost of the assistance. The Agency will notify the public of the availability of assistance, changes in application requirements, or changes in the fee structure.
