20 C.F.R. § 678.425 — What are the applicable career services that must be provided through the one-stop delivery system by required one-stop partners?
Citation: us:cfr:20:678.425
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-678.425
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 678 (Description Of The One-Stop Delivery System Under Title I Of The Workforce Innovation And Opportunity Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The applicable career services to be delivered by required one-stop partners are those services listed in § 678.430 that are authorized to be provided under each partner's program.
(b) One-stop centers provide services to individual customers based on individual needs, including the seamless delivery of multiple services to individual customers. There is no required sequence of services.
