A counterexample of the Fredholm of Toeplitz operator
Liu Hua, Zhang Xinyang

Abstract
. In this paper we study the essential spectra of the Toeplitz operator on the Hardy space H 1 H^{1} . We give a counterexample to show that the Toeplitz operator with symbol is not Fridholm, which give a counterexample to the conjecture by J.A. Virtanen J A in 2006.
