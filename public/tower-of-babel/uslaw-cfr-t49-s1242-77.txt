49 C.F.R. § 1242.77 — Administration (account XX-55-01).
Citation: us:cfr:49:1242.77
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1242.77
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1242 (Separation Of Common Operating Expenses Between Freight Service And Passenger Service For Railroads 1). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Separate common expenses in the same proportion as common expenses are separated in employees performing clerical and accounting functions, communication systems operations and loss and damage claims processing (accounts XX-55-76, XX-55-77 and XX-55-78).
