Treasurer's Report for Financial Year 2024
Authors: Aline Muyle
Published: 2025

This report covers the period from January the 1st to December the 31st 2024. In 2024, the SMBE organized its first annual meeting in Latin America (Puerto Vallarta, Mexico). To support this important milestone, the Society drew on previously accumulated assets, resulting in a net deficit of −$641,393.83 USD for the year.

Annual Meeting—Puerto Vallarta

The Puerto Vallarta meeting was the Society's first in Latin America. The SMBE Council prioritized inclusivity and representation, supporting more symposia than usual and subsidizing registration costs, particularly for researchers from the Global South.

As in previous years, the Society covered travel expenses to the annual meeting for MBE and GBE editors, SMBE council members, awardees, Local Organizing Committee members, SMBE staff, and 3 IDEA taskforce members. Caregiver awards were also granted to support researchers with caregiving responsibilities to foster their participation. Previously awarded but unused travel grants from 2020 to 2021 were finally honored in 2024.

Annual Meeting Costs included venue rental, childcare, logistics, symposia, website, hybrid setup, insurance, food, and PCO fees that were not covered by registration fees. The SMBE deliberately kept registration fees below actual costs to maximize accessibility.
