40 C.F.R. § 255.33 — Inclusion of Federal facilities and Native American Reservations.
Citation: us:cfr:40:255.33
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-255.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 255 (Identification Of Regions And Agencies For Solid Waste Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Major Federal facilities and Native American Reservations should be treated for the purposes of these guidelines as though they are incorporated municipalities, and the facility director or administrator should be considered the same as a locally elected official.
