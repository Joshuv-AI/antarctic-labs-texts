41 C.F.R. § 102-37.325 — -37.325 Property available for donation to donees with conditional eligibility.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-37.325
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-37.325
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-37 (37—Donation Of Surplus Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may only make available surplus property that the donee can use immediately. If property is provided to the donee with conditional eligibility, and the conditional eligibility lapses, the property must be returned to the SASP for redistribution or disposal.
