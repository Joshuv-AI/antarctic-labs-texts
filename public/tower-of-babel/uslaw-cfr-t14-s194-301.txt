14 C.F.R. § 194.301 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:14:194.301
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-194.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 194 (Special Federal Aviation Regulation No. 120—Powered-Lift: Pilot Certification And Training; Operations Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless otherwise specified by this part, persons operating powered-lift must continue to comply with rules applicable to all aircraft in parts 91, 135, and 136 of this chapter, as applicable to the operation. In addition, any sections or paragraphs within sections under parts 91 and 135 that refer to specific categories of aircraft and that are not referenced in the SFAR table to § 194.302 or § 194.306, do not apply to powered-lift.
