12 U.S.C. § 2293 — Budget and audit provisions of Government corporation control law applicable
Citation: us:usc:12:2293
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section2293
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 24 (Federal Financing Bank). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The budget and audit provisions of chapter 91 of title 31 shall be applicable to the Federal Financing Bank in the same manner as they are applied to the wholly owned Government corporations named in section 9101(3) of title 31.
