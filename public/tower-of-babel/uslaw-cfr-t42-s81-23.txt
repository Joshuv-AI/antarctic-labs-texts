42 C.F.R. § 81.23 — Guidelines for cancers for which primary site is unknown.
Citation: us:cfr:42:81.23
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 81 (Guidelines For Determining Probability Of Causation Under The Energy Employees Occupational Illness Compensation Program Act Of 2000). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) In claims for which the primary cancer site cannot be determined, but a site of metastasis is known, DOL will calculate probability of causation estimates for various likely primary sites. Table 1 of this paragraph (a) indicates the primary cancer site(s) DOL will use in NIOSH-IREP when the primary cancer site is unknown.
(b) DOL will select the site producing the highest estimate for probability of causation to adjudicate the claim.
