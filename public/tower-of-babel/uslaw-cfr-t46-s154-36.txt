46 C.F.R. § 154.36 — Correspondence and vessel information: Submission.
Citation: us:cfr:46:154.36
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-154.36
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 154 (Safety Standards For Self-Propelled Vessels Carrying Bulk Liquefied Gases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Correspondence to the Coast Guard and all vessel information submitted to the Coast Guard must be in English, except—
(a) IMO Certificates may be in French; and
(b) SOLAS Certificates may be in the official language of the flag administration.
