Applications of CFT determinant formulas in number theory
D. Levin &, H.-G. Shin †, A. Zuevsky ∗

Abstract
. In this note we show how to use the determinant representations for correlation functions in CFT to derive new determinant formulas for powers of the modular discriminant expressed via deformed elliptic functions with parameters. In particular, we obtain counterparts of Garvan’s formulas for the modular discriminant corresponding to the genus two Riemann surface case.

The most interesting example in this direction is represented

In computations of vertex operator algebra correlation functions

Their two-parameter natural generalizations were introduced in

For higher correlation

Various identities for powers of the

In the genus two case, the bosonization procedure provides us with

In this note, using the relations mentioned above,

1.1.
Torus correlation functions

For an automorphism

\mathcal{V}

2.

The modular discriminant is defined by

\Delta(\tau)=\eta(\tau)^{24}

\eta(\tau)

\eta(\tau)=q^{1/24}\prod_{n=1}^{\infty}(1-q^{n})

Recall that the Eisenstein series

where

is the
n

One finds
[

]
the relations:

E^{(1)}_{8}=\left(E^{(1)}_{4}(\tau)\right)^{2}

E^{(1)}_{10}(\tau)=E^{(1)}_{4}(\tau)E^{(1)}_{6}(\tau)

E^{(1)}_{12}(\tau)=\tfrac{441}{691}\left(E^{(1)}_{4}(\tau)\right)^{3}+\tfrac{250}{691}\left(E^{(1)}_{6}(\tau)\right)^{2}
