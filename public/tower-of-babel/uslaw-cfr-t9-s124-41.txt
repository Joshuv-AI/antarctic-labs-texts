9 C.F.R. § 124.41 — Notice of hearing.
Citation: us:cfr:9:124.41
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-124.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 124 (Patent Term Restoration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No later than ten days before the hearing, APHIS will notify the requesting party, the applicant, the petitioner, and any other interested person of the date, time, and location of the hearing.
