12 C.F.R. § 1070.4 — Records of the CFPB not to be otherwise disclosed.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1070.4
Creator: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1070.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1070 (Disclosure Of Records And Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided by this part, employees or former employees of the CFPB, or others in possession of a record of the CFPB that the CFPB has not already made public, are prohibited from disclosing such records, without authorization, to any person who is not an employee of the CFPB.
