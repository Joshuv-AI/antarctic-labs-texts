22 C.F.R. § 214.2 — Definition of advisory committee.
Citation: us:cfr:22:214.2
Creator: Agency For International Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-214.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 214 (Advisory Committee Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The term advisory committee is defined in section 3(2) of the Act.
(b) In general, this definition includes any committee, board, commission, council, conference, panel, task force, or other similar group, or any subcommittee or sub-group thereof, which is formed or utilized by the Agency for obtaining advice or recommendations, and which is not composed wholly of full-time Federal employees.
