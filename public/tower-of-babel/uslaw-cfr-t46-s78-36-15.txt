46 C.F.R. § 78.36-15 — -15 Shipboard stowage.
Citation: us:cfr:46:78.36-15
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-78.36-15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 78 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The approved buoyant work vests shall be stowed separately from the regular stowage of approved life preservers.
(b) The locations for the stowage of work vests shall be such as not to be easily confused with that for approved life preservers.
