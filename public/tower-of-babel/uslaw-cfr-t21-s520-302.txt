21 C.F.R. § 520.302 — Carnidazole tablets.

Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-520.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 520 (Oral Dosage Form New Animal Drugs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Specifications. Each tablet contains 10 milligrams of carnidazole.
(b) Sponsor. See 053923 in § 510.600(c) of this chapter.
(c) Conditions of use—(1) Amount. Adult pigeons: 1 tablet (10 milligrams); newly weaned pigeons: 1/2 tablet (5 milligrams).
(2) Indications for use. For treating trichomoniasis (canker) in ornamental and homing pigeons.
(3) Limitations. Not for use in pigeons intended for human food. Consult your veterinarian for assistance in the diagnosis, treatment, and control of parasitism or when severely ill birds do not respond to treatment.
