20 C.F.R. § 10.605 — What is reconsideration?
Citation: us:cfr:20:10.605
Creator: Office Of Workers' Compensation Programs, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-10.605
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 10 (Claims For Compensation Under The Federal Employees' Compensation Act, As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The FECA provides that the Director may review an award for or against compensation upon application by an employee (or his or her representative) who receives an adverse decision. The employee shall exercise this right through a request to the district office. The request, along with the supporting statements and evidence, is called the “application for reconsideration.”
