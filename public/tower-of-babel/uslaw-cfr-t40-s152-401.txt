40 C.F.R. § 152.401 — Inapplicability of fee provisions to applications filed prior to October 1, 1997.
Citation: us:cfr:40:152.401
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-152.401
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 152 (Pesticide Registration And Classification Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No fee required by this subpart U shall be levied with respect to any application filed during the period beginning on October 25, 1988, and ending on September 30, 1997. See FIFRA section 4(i)(7) (added to FIFRA by Pub. L. 100-532, October 25, 1988, 102 Stat. 2654).
