28 U.S.C. § 2346 — Certification of record on review
Citation: us:usc:28:2346
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section2346
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part VI (Particular Proceedings); Chapter 158 (Orders Of Federal Agencies; Review). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Unless the proceeding has been terminated on a motion to dismiss the petition, the agency shall file in the office of the clerk the record on review as provided by section 2112 of this title.
