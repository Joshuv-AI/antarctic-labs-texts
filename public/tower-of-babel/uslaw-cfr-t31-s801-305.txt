31 C.F.R. § 801.305 — Exception for air carriers.
Citation: us:cfr:31:801.305
Creator: Office Of Investment Security, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-801.305
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 801 (Pilot Program To Review Certain Transactions Involving Foreign Persons And Critical Technologies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No investment involving an air carrier, as defined in section 40102(a)(2) of title 49, United States Code, that holds a certificate issued under section 41102 of that title shall be a pilot program covered transaction.
