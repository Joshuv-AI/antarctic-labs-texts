Cohomology of Small Cartesian Closed Categories
Mirai Ikebuchi

Abstract
We show the isomorphism between the Quillen cohomology and the Baues-Wirsching cohomology of a cartesian closed category (CCC). This is an extension of the results of Dwyer-Kan for small categories and Jibladze-Pirashvili for small categories with finite products. These results implies that The Quillen cohomology of a CCC 𝐂 {\mathbf{C}} coincides with that of 𝐂 {\mathbf{C}} as a category with finite products, and also that of 𝐂 {\mathbf{C}} as a small category.

5
Baues–Wirsching cohomology

Let
{\mathbf{C}}
be a small category and
D
be a natural system on
{\mathbf{C}}
.

For
n>0
, we define
C^{n}_{\mathrm{BW}}({\mathbf{C}};D)
as the abelian group of all functions

f:N_{n}({\mathbf{C}})\to\bigcup_{g\in\mathrm{Mor}({\mathbf{C}})}D_{g}

such that
f(\lambda_{1},\dots,\lambda_{n})\in D_{\lambda_{1}\dots\lambda_{n}}
.
Here,
N({\mathbf{C}})
is the nerve of
{\mathbf{C}}
.

For
n=0
, let
C^{0}_{\mathrm{BW}}({\mathbf{C}};D)
be the abelian group of all functions

f:N_{0}({\mathbf{C}})=\mathrm{Ob}({\mathbf{C}})\to\bigcup_{A\in\mathrm{Ob}({\mathbf{C}})}D_{A}

such that
f(A)\in D_{A}
where
D_{A}=D_{1_{A}}
.
The addition in
C^{n}_{\mathrm{BW}}
is given by pointwise addition in
D_{f}
s.

Define the coboundary map
\delta:C^{n}_{\mathrm{BW}}\to C^{n+1}_{\mathrm{BW}}
as, for
n=0
,

(\delta f)(\lambda)=\lambda_{*}f(A)-\lambda^{*}f(B)\quad(\lambda:A\to B\in N_{0}({\mathbf{C}}))

and for
n>1
,

(\delta f)(\lambda_{1},\dots,\lambda_{n})=\lambda_{1*}f(\lambda_{2},\dots,\lambda_{n})+\sum_{i=1}^{n-1}(-1)^{i}f(\lambda_{1},\dots,\lambda_{i}\lambda_{i+1},\dots,\lambda_{n})+(-1)^{n}\lambda^{*}_{n}f(\lambda_{1},\dots,\lambda_{n-1}).

We can check
\delta\delta=0
, so
(C^{n}_{\mathrm{BW}}({\mathbf{C}};D),\delta)
forms a cochain complex.

Definition 5.1
.

[
8
]

The
n
-th
Baues–Wirsching cohomology group

H^{n}_{\mathrm{BW}}({\mathbf{C}};D)
is the
n
-th cohomology group of the cochain complex
(C^{\bullet}_{\mathrm{BW}}({\mathbf{C}};D),\delta)
.

It is known that the Baues–Wirsching cohomology is invariant under equivalences of categories in the following sense.

Proposition 5.2
.

[
8
]

For any two small categories
{\mathbf{C}}
,
{\mathbf{C}}^{\prime}
with equivalence
\phi:{\mathbf{C}}\to{\mathbf{C}}^{\prime}
and a natural system on
{\mathbf{C}}^{\prime}
,
\phi
induces an isomorphism

H^{n}_{\mathrm{BW}}({\mathbf{C}};\phi^{*}D)\cong H^{n}_{\mathrm{BW}}({\mathbf{C}}^{\prime};D)

for any
n\geq 0
where
\phi^{*}D
is the natural system given by
\phi^{*}D_{f}=D_{\phi(f)}
,
a_{*}=\phi(a)_{*}
,
b^{*}=\phi(b)^{*}
for
f,a,b\in\mathrm{Mor}({\mathbf{C}})
.

It is known that
H^{2}_{\mathrm{BW}}({\mathbf{C}};D)
classifies linear extensions of
{\mathbf{C}}
by
D
.

Proposition 5.3
.

[
8
]

Let
M(\mathbf{C};D)
be the set of equivalence classes of linear extensions of
{\mathbf{C}}
by
D
.
There is a natural bijection
M({\mathbf{C}};D)\cong H^{2}_{\mathrm{BW}}(\mathbf{C};D)
that maps the trivial linear extension to the zero in
H^{2}_{\mathrm{BW}}(\mathbf{C};D)
.

The Baues–Wirsching cohomology of
{\mathbf{C}}
can be written as an Ext over
\mathbf{Nat}_{\mathbf{C}}=\mathbf{Ab}^{\mathcal{F}{\mathbf{C}}}
.
Let
{\cal Z}_{\mathbf{C}}
be the natural system on
{\mathbf{C}}
such that for each morphism
f:X\to Y
in
{\mathbf{C}}
,
({\cal Z}_{\mathbf{C}})_{f}
is the free abelian group generated by
f
, and for each
a:X^{\prime}\to X
,
b:Y\to Y
,
a^{*}:({\cal Z}_{\mathbf{C}})_{f}\to({\cal Z}_{\mathbf{C}})_{fa}
and
b_{*}:({\cal Z}_{\mathbf{C}})_{f}\to({\cal Z}_{\mathbf{C}})_{bf}
are the isomorphisms sending the generator
f
to the generators
fa
and
bf
, respectively.

Proposition 5.4
.

[
8
]

For any natural system
D
on
{\mathbf{C}}
, there is an isomorphism

H^{n}_{\mathrm{BW}}({\mathbf{C}};D)\cong\Ext_{\mathbf{Nat}_{\mathbf{C}}}^{n}({\cal Z}_{\mathbf{C}},D)
.

It is proved that, for any
n\geq 2
,
H^{n}_{\mathrm{BW}}({\mathbf{C}};D)=0
for a free
{\mathbf{C}}
in
\mathbf{Cat}_{O}
and a natural system
D
on
{\mathbf{C}}

[
8
]
, and also for a free
{\mathbf{C}}
in
\mathbf{Law}_{S}
and a cartesian natural system
D
on
{\mathbf{C}}

[
12
]
.
We show that the same holds for a free
{\mathbf{C}}
in
\mathbf{CCC}_{S}
and a cartesian closed natural system
D
on
{\mathbf{C}}
.

Proposition 5.5
.

For any free
S
-sorted CCC
{\mathbf{C}}
and a cartesian closed natural system
D
on
{\mathbf{C}}
, we have
H^{n}_{\mathrm{BW}}({\mathbf{C}};D)=0
for
n\geq 2
.

Proof.

For any linear extension
D\to{\mathbf{E}}\xrightarrow{p}{\mathbf{C}}
, by Lemma
4.12
, we can equip
{\mathbf{E}}
with a structure of an
S
-sorted CCC.
Since
{\mathbf{C}}
is a free
S
-sorted CCC, we can construct a morphism
s:{\mathbf{C}}\to\mathbf{E}
in
\mathbf{CCC}_{S}
such that
ps=\mathrm{id}_{\mathbf{C}}
.
Therefore the extension splits, and by Proposition
5.4

H^{n}_{\mathrm{BW}}({\mathbf{C}};D)=0
for any
n\geq 2
.
∎

6
Equivalence

In
[
10
, Theorem 4, page 4.2]
, Quillen showed that any algebraic category
{\cal C}
has a simplicial model structure.
One of the important fact we use here is that, for any
X\in\mathrm{Ob}({\cal C})
, we can take a cofibrant replacement
Y_{\bullet}\to X
and
Y_{\bullet}
is degreewise free.
We call such
Y_{\bullet}\to X
a simplicial free resolution of
X
.
In this paper we take
{\cal C}=\mathbf{CCC}_{S}
.

Note that if
p:{\mathbf{C}}^{\prime}\to{\mathbf{C}}
is an
S
-sorted CCC over
{\mathbf{C}}
, then
p
induces a morphism
\mathcal{F}{\mathbf{C}}^{\prime}\to\mathcal{F}{\mathbf{C}}
, so any natural system
D
on
{\mathbf{C}}
can be considered as a natural system on
{\mathbf{C}}^{\prime}
.

Definition 6.1
.

Let
{\mathbf{C}}
be an
S
-sorted CCC and
D
be a cartesian closed natural system on
{\mathbf{C}}
.
Then the
n
-th
Quillen cohomology group
of
{\mathbf{C}}
with coefficients in
D
, written
HQ^{n}_{\mathbf{CCC}_{S}}({\mathbf{C}};D)
, is given as

H\!Q^{n}_{\mathbf{CCC}_{S}}({\mathbf{C}};D)=H^{n}(\mathrm{Der}^{\mathbf{CCC}_{S}}({\mathbf{F}}_{\bullet};D))

where
{\mathbf{F}}_{\bullet}
is a simplicial free resolution of
{\mathbf{C}}
in
\mathbf{CCC}_{S}
.

The goal of this paper is to show

H\!Q^{n}_{\mathbf{CCC}_{S}}({\mathbf{C}};D)\cong H^{n+1}_{\mathrm{BW}}({\mathbf{C}};D)

for any
n\geq 1
.

Let
{\mathbf{C}}
be an
S
-sorted CCC and
D
be a cartesian closed natural system on
{\mathbf{C}}
.
Let
p:{\mathbf{F}}\to{\mathbf{C}}
be an
S
-sorted CCC over
{\mathbf{C}}
and suppose that
{\mathbf{F}}
is freely generated by
\{f_{i}\}_{i\in I}
.

We define
\tilde{C}^{0}_{\mathrm{BW}}({\mathbf{F}};D)
as the subgroup of
\ker(\delta:C^{1}_{\mathrm{BW}}({\mathbf{F}};D)\to C^{2}_{\mathrm{BW}}({\mathbf{F}};D))
consisting of
\phi
such that
\phi(f_{i})=0
for
i\in I
.
Note that any
\phi\in\ker(\delta:C^{1}_{\mathrm{BW}}({\mathbf{F}};D)\to C^{2}_{\mathrm{BW}}({\mathbf{F}};D))
satisfies
\phi(a\circ b)=a_{*}\phi(b)+b^{*}\phi(a)
, so
\phi(a\circ b)
is determined by
\phi(a)
and
\phi(b)
.

For any
g_{i}:X\to X_{i}
(
i=1,2
) in
{\mathbf{F}}
, since
\phi(g_{i})=\phi(\pi_{i}\circ\ab<g_{1},g_{2}>)=\pi_{i*}\phi(\ab<g_{1},g_{2}>)+\ab<g_{1},g_{2}>^{*}\phi(\pi_{i})
and
\xi\mapsto(\pi_{1*}\xi,\pi_{2*}\xi)
is an isomorphism,

\phi(\ab<g_{1},g_{2}>)
is determined by
\phi(g_{i})
,
\phi(\pi_{i})
for
i=1,2
.
Similarly, for any
g:X\times Y\to Z
, since
\phi(g)=\phi(\expectationvalue{Y}{Z}\circ(\lambda g\times\mathrm{id}_{Y}))=\expectationvalue{Y}{Z*}\phi(\lambda g\times\mathrm{id}_{Y})+(\lambda g\times\mathrm{id}_{Y})^{*}\phi(\expectationvalue{Y}{Z})
,

\phi(\lambda g)
is determined by
\phi(g)
,
\phi(\expectationvalue{Y}{Z})
.
So,
\phi\in\ker(\delta:C^{1}_{\mathrm{BW}}({\mathbf{F}};D)\to C^{2}_{\mathrm{BW}}({\mathbf{F}};D))
is uniquely determined by
\phi(f_{i})
,
\phi(\pi_{i})
,
\phi(\expectationvalue{Y}{Z})
, and
\phi\in\tilde{C}_{\mathrm{BW}}^{0}({\mathbf{F}};D)
is uniquely determined by
\phi(\pi_{i})
,
\phi(\expectationvalue{Y}{Z})
.
(In particular,
\tilde{C}^{0}_{\mathrm{BW}}({\mathbf{F}};D)
is the same group for any free
S
-sorted CCC
{\mathbf{F}}
.)
Also, by Lemma
4.15
,
\phi\in\mathrm{Der}^{\mathbf{CCC}_{S}}({\mathbf{F}};D)
is determinded by
\phi(f_{i})
(
i\in I
), and from these observation, we get the following.

Lemma 6.2
.

\tilde{C}_{\mathrm{BW}}^{0}({\mathbf{F}};D)\oplus\mathrm{Der}^{\mathbf{CCC}_{S}}({\mathbf{F}};D)\cong\ker(\delta:C^{1}_{\mathrm{BW}}({\mathbf{F}};D)\to C^{2}_{\mathrm{BW}}({\mathbf{F}};D))
.

For
n>0
, let
\tilde{C}^{n}_{\mathrm{BW}}({\mathbf{F}};D)=C^{n}_{\mathrm{BW}}({\mathbf{F}};D)
. Then
\tilde{C}^{\bullet}_{\mathrm{BW}}({\mathbf{F}};D)
forms a cochain complex.
Then, we get
H^{1}\ab(\tilde{C}_{\mathrm{BW}}^{\bullet}({\mathbf{F}};D))\cong\mathrm{Der}^{\mathbf{CCC}_{S}}({\mathbf{F}};D)
and
H^{n}\ab(\tilde{C}_{\mathrm{BW}}^{\bullet}({\mathbf{F}};D))=0
for
n\neq 1
.

Remark 6.3
.

Even though the proof of
H\!Q^{n}_{\mathbf{CCC}_{S}}\cong H^{n+1}_{\mathrm{BW}}
we are going to give is quite similar to that for Lawvere theories in
[
12
]
, they claimed and used an incorrect proposition
\mathrm{Der}^{\mathbf{Law}_{S}}({\mathbf{F}};D)\cong\ker(\delta:C^{1}_{\mathrm{BW}}({\mathbf{F}};D)\to C^{0}_{\mathrm{BW}}({\mathbf{F}};D))
, which makes their proof invalid.
Our discussion above corrects their mistake.

Theorem 6.4
.

For
n>0
, an
S
-sorted cartesian closed category
{\mathbf{C}}
, and a cartesian closed natural system
D
on
{\mathbf{C}}
,

H\!Q^{n}_{\mathbf{CCC}_{S}}({\mathbf{C}};D)\cong H^{n+1}_{\mathrm{BW}}({\mathbf{C}};D).

Proof.

Let
\epsilon:{\mathbf{F}}_{\bullet}\to{\mathbf{C}}
be a simplicial free resolution in
\mathbf{CCC}_{I}/{\mathbf{C}}
.
For each two objects
X,Y
, the simplicial object
{\mathbf{F}}_{\bullet}
induces the simplicial set
\mathrm{Hom}_{{\mathbf{F}}_{\bullet}}(X,Y)
given by

\mathrm{Hom}_{{\mathbf{F}}_{\bullet}}(X,Y)_{k}=\mathrm{Hom}_{{\mathbf{F}}_{k}}(X,Y)
,
and
\epsilon
induces a weak equivalence from
\mathrm{Hom}_{{\mathbf{F}}_{\bullet}}(X,Y)
to
\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathbf{T}}(X,Y)
considered as a constant simplicial set.

Consider the double complex
\tilde{C}^{\bullet}_{\mathrm{BW}}({\mathbf{F}}_{\bullet};D)
and two spectral sequences
{}^{\prime}\!E^{pq}
,
{}^{\prime\prime}\!E^{pq}
converging to the cohomology of the total complex.
For
{}^{\prime}\!E^{pq}_{1}
, we have

{}^{\prime}\!E_{1}^{pq}=H^{q}\ab(\tilde{C}^{\bullet}_{\mathrm{BW}}({\mathbf{F}}_{p};D))=\begin{cases}\mathrm{Der}^{\mathbf{CCC}_{S}}({\mathbf{F}}_{p};D),&q=1,\\
0,&q\neq 1,\end{cases}

so,
{}^{\prime}\!E_{2}^{pq}\Rightarrow H\!Q^{p+q-1}({\mathbf{C}};D)
.

For
{}^{\prime\prime}\!E_{1}^{pq}
, we have

{}^{\prime\prime}\!E_{1}^{pq}=H^{q}\ab(\tilde{C}^{p}_{\mathrm{BW}}({\mathbf{F}}_{\bullet};D)).

For
Y_{0},\dots,Y_{p}\in\mathrm{Ob}({\mathbf{C}})=\mathrm{Ob}({\mathbf{F}}_{q})
, consider the simplicial set

S_{\bullet}^{Y_{0},\dots,Y_{p}}=\mathrm{Hom}_{{\mathbf{F}}_{\bullet}}(Y_{1},Y_{0})\times\dots\times\mathrm{Hom}_{{\mathbf{F}}_{\bullet}}(Y_{p},Y_{p-1}).

By the definition of Baues–Wirsching cochain complexes, for any
p>0
,

C^{p}_{\mathrm{BW}}({\mathbf{F}}_{q};D)\cong\prod_{Y_{0},\dots,Y_{p}}C^{q}\ab(S_{\bullet}^{Y_{0},\dots,Y_{p}};D_{(-)})

where the right-hand side is the product of cochain complexes of simplicial sets
S_{\bullet}^{Y_{0},\dots,Y_{p}}
with coefficients in
D_{f_{1}\dots f_{p}}
on the connected component of
S_{\bullet}^{Y_{0},\dots,Y_{p}}
corresponding to
(f_{1},\dots,f_{p})\in\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathbf{C}}(Y_{1},Y_{0})\times\dots\times\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathbf{C}}(Y_{p},Y_{p-1})
.

We have
H^{n}\ab(S_{\bullet}^{Y_{0},\dots,Y_{p}};D_{(-)})=0
for
n>0
since we have a weak equivalence between
{\mathbf{F}}_{\bullet}
and
{\mathbf{C}}
, a constant simplicial object.
Therefore,
{}^{\prime\prime}\!E_{1}^{pq}=0
for
p,q>0
.
For
p>0
and
q=0
, we have

{}^{\prime\prime}\!E_{1}^{p0}=\prod_{Y_{0},\dots,Y_{p+1}}H^{0}\ab(\mathrm{Hom}_{{\mathbf{F}}_{\bullet}}(Y_{1},Y_{0})\times\dots\times\mathrm{Hom}_{{\mathbf{F}}_{\bullet}}(Y_{p},Y_{p-1});D_{(-)})=C_{\mathrm{BW}}^{p}({\mathbf{C}};D).

For
p=0
,
{}^{\prime\prime}\!E_{1}^{0q}=0
since
\tilde{C}^{0}_{\mathrm{BW}}({\mathbf{F}}_{i};D)\to\tilde{C}^{0}_{\mathrm{BW}}({\mathbf{F}}_{i-1};D)
is an isomorphism.
Thus
{}^{\prime\prime}\!E_{2}^{pq}\Rightarrow H_{\mathrm{BW}}^{p}({\mathbf{C}};D)
.
∎

Note that if we have an

-sorted CCC

In other words,

In

Here, since Quillen showed in
[

]
that there is a spectral sequence

E^{pq}_{2}=\Ext^{p}_{({\cal C}/X)_{\mathrm{ab}}}\ab(H\!Q_{q}^{{\cal C}}(X),M)\Rightarrow H\!Q^{p+q}_{{\cal C}}(X;M)
