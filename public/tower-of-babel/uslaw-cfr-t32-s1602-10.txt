32 C.F.R. § 1602.10 — County.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1602.10
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1602.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1602 (Definitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The word county includes, where applicable, counties, independent cities, and similar subdivisions, such as the independent cities of Virginia and the parishes of Louisiana.
