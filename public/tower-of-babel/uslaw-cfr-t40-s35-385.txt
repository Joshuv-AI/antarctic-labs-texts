40 C.F.R. § 35.385 — Maximum federal share.
Citation: us:cfr:40:35.385
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-35.385
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 35 (State And Local Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

EPA may provide up to 75 percent of the approved work plan costs for the development or refinement of a wetlands protection and management program.
