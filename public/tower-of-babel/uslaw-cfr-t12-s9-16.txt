12 C.F.R. § 9.16 — Receivership or voluntary liquidation of bank.
Citation: us:cfr:12:9.16
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-9.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 9 (Fiduciary Activities Of National Banks). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the OCC appoints a receiver for an uninsured national bank, or if a national bank places itself in voluntary liquidation, the receiver or liquidating agent shall promptly close or transfer to a substitute fiduciary all fiduciary accounts, in accordance with OCC instructions and the orders of the court having jurisdiction.
