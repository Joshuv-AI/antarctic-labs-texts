5 C.F.R. § 610.408 — Use of credit hours.
Citation: us:cfr:5:610.408
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-610.408
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 610 (Hours Of Duty). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Members of the Senior Executive Service (SES) may not accumulate credit hours under an alternative work schedule. Any credit hours accumulated in the SES prior to December 1, 1993, must be used within 6 months of that date.
