24 C.F.R. § 5.1004 — System of award management.
Citation: us:cfr:24:5.1004
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-5.1004
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 5 (General Hud Program Requirements; Waivers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Applicants for HUD financial assistance that are subject to this subpart are required to register with the System of Award Management (SAM) and have an active registration in SAM in accordance with 2 CFR part 25, appendix A in order for HUD to obligate funds and for an awardee to receive an award of funds from HUD.
