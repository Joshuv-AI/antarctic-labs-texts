19 U.S.C. § 4087 — Compensation authority
Citation: us:usc:19:4087
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section4087
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 26 (Dominican Republic-Central America Free Trade); Subchapter III (Relief From Imports); Part B (Textile And Apparel Safeguard Measures). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For purposes of section 123 of the Trade Act of 1974 (19 U.S.C. 2133), any import relief provided by the President under this part shall be treated as action taken under chapter 1 of title II of that Act [19 U.S.C. 2251 et seq.].
