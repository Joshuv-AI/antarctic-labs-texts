12 C.F.R. § 602.9 — Current FOIA index.
Citation: us:cfr:12:602.9
Creator: Farm Credit Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-602.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 602 (Releasing Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

FCA will make a current index available for public inspection and copying, as required by the FOIA. We will give you an index for the cost of copying it. Because we rarely receive requests for an index, we have not published one in the Federal Register.
