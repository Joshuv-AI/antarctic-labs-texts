44 U.S.C. § 314 — Inks, glues, and other supplies furnished to other Government agencies: payment
Citation: us:usc:44:314
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section314
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 3 (Government Publishing Office). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Inks, glues, and other supplies manufactured by the Government Publishing Office in connection with its work may be furnished to departments and other establishments of the Government upon requisition, and payment made from appropriations available.
