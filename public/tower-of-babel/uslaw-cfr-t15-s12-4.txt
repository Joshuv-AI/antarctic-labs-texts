15 C.F.R. § 12.4 — Report to the Congress.
Citation: us:cfr:15:12.4
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-12.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 12 (Fair Packaging And Labeling). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Whenever the Secretary publishes a final determination under § 12.3(b)(4) or § 12.3(c)(5), he shall promptly report such determination to the Congress with a statement of the efforts that have been made under the voluntary standards program and his recommendation as to whether Congress should enact legislation providing regulatory authority to deal with the situation in question.
