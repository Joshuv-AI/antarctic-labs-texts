49 U.S.C. § 80109 — Liens under negotiable bills
Citation: us:usc:49:80109
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section80109
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Chapter 801 (Bills Of Lading). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A common carrier issuing a negotiable bill of lading has a lien on the goods covered by the bill for—
(1)
charges for storage, transportation, and delivery (including demurrage and terminal charges), and expenses necessary to preserve the goods or incidental to transporting the goods after the date of the bill; and
(2)
other charges for which the bill expressly specifies a lien is claimed to the extent the charges are allowed by law and the agreement between the consignor and carrier.
