45 C.F.R. § 1636.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1636.1
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1636.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1636 (Client Identity And Statement Of Facts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this rule is to ensure that, when an LSC recipient files a complaint in a court of law or otherwise initiates or participates in litigation against a defendant or engages in pre-complaint settlement negotiations, the recipient identifies the plaintiff it represents to the defendant and ensures that the plaintiff has a colorable claim.
