7 C.F.R. § 1737.10 — Initial contact.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1737.10
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1737.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1737 (Pre-Loan Policies And Procedures Common To Insured And Guaranteed Telecommunications Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Initial loan applicants seeking assistance should write the Rural Utilities Service, United States Department of Agriculture, Washington, DC 20250. A field representative will be assigned by RUS to visit the applicant and discuss its financial needs and eligibility. Existing borrowers initiate the contact directly with their assigned field representative. Borrowers consult with RUS field representatives and headquarters staff, as necessary.
