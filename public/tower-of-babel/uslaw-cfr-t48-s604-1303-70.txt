48 C.F.R. § 604.1303-70 — 604.1303-70 DOSAR contract clause.
Citation: us:cfr:48:604.1303-70
Creator: Chapter 6—Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-604.1303-70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 604 (Administrative Matters). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 652.204-70, Department of State Personal Identification Card Policy and Procedures, in solicitations and contracts that require contractor employees to perform on-site at a DOS location and/or that require contractor employees to have access to DOS information systems.
