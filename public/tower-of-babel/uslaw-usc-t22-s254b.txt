22 U.S.C. § 254b — Privileges and immunities of mission of nonparty to Vienna Convention
Citation: us:usc:22:254b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section254b
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 6 (Foreign Diplomatic And Consular Officers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

With respect to a nonparty to the Vienna Convention, the mission, the members of the mission, their families, and diplomatic couriers shall enjoy the privileges and immunities specified in the Vienna Convention.
