14 C.F.R. § 34.20 — Applicability.

Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-34.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 34 (Fuel Venting And Exhaust Emission Requirements For Turbine Engine Powered Airplanes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to all aircraft gas turbine engines of the classes specified beginning on the dates specified in § 34.21.
