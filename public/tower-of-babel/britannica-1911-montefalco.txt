MONTEFALCO, a town of the province of Perugia, Italy, 6 m.
S.W. of Foligno, situated on a hill, 1550 ft. above sea-level.
Pop. (1901), 3397 (town); 5726 (commune). Its churches contain
a number of pictures of the Umbrian school; S. Francesco
has good frescoes (scenes from the life of S. Francis) of 1452,
by Benozzo Gozzoli, in the choir. There is also a communal
picture-gallery in the picturesque Palezzo Comunale.
