16 U.S.C. § 718k — Use of fees collected for Federal migratory bird permits
Citation: us:usc:16:718k
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section718k
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 7 (Protection Of Migratory Game And Insectivorous Birds); Subchapter IV (Hunting And Conservation Stamp Tax). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

On and after October 21, 1998, all fees collected for Federal migratory bird permits shall be available to the Secretary, without further appropriation, to be used for the expenses of the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service in administering such Federal migratory bird permits, and shall remain available until expended.
