2 C.F.R. § 1130.500 — Purpose of PROP Article V.
Citation: us:cfr:2:1130.500
Creator: Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1130.500
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1130 (Property Administration: General Award Terms And Conditions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

PROP Article V specifies requirements for recipients' use and disposition of federally owned property. It implements the portion of OMB guidance in 2 CFR 200.312(a) that applies to disposition of federally owned property.
