On integrable structure of the null string in (anti-)de Sitter space D.V. Uvarov 1 1 1 E-mail: d_uvarov@ hotmail.com NSC Kharkiv Institute of Physics and Technology, 61108 Kharkiv, Ukraine


Abstract
Presently integrability turned the key property in the study of duality between superconformal gauge theories and strings in anti-de Sitter superspaces. Complexity of the study of integrable structure in string theory is caused by complicated dependence of background fields of the Type II supergravity multiplets, with which strings interact, on the superspace coordinates. This explains an interest to study of limiting cases, in which superstring equations simplify. In the present work considered is the limiting case of zero tension corresponding to null string. It is obtained the representation in the form of the Lax equation of null-string equations in (anti-)de Sitter space realized as coset manifold. Proposed is twistor interpretation of the Lagrangian of (null) string in anti-de Sitter space expressed in terms of group variables.



Keywords: tensionless string; (anti-)de Sitter space; classical integrability; Lax pair; twistor

3
Lax representation of null string equations in (anti-) de Sitter space

3.1
Group-theoretic description of geometry of (anti-)de Sitter space

Relations of the
so(2,D-1)
isometry algebra of the
D-
dimensional anti-de Sitter space and
so(1,D)
isometry algebra of the
D-
dimensional de Sitter space can be presented in the uniform way

[M_{ab},M_{cd}]=\eta_{ad}M_{bc}-\eta_{ac}M_{bd}-\eta_{bd}M_{ac}+\eta_{bc}M_{ad},

where
-\eta_{00}=\eta_{11}=\ldots=\eta_{D-1D-1}=1
and
\eta_{DD}=s
with
s=-1
for anti-de Sitter space and
+1
for de Sitter space. These relations in the form, in which generators of the
so(2,D-1)/so(1,D-1)
(
so(1,D)/so(1,D-1)
) quotient algebra are manifestly separated, are known as
(a)ds_{D}
algebra

\displaystyle\left[M_{Db^{\prime}},M_{Dd^{\prime}}\right]=-sM_{b^{\prime}d^{\prime}},\quad\left[M_{a^{\prime}b^{\prime}},M_{Dd^{\prime}}\right]=-\eta_{a^{\prime}d^{\prime}}M_{Db^{\prime}}+\eta_{b^{\prime}d^{\prime}}M_{Da^{\prime}},

\displaystyle\left[M_{a^{\prime}b^{\prime}},M_{c^{\prime}d^{\prime}}\right]=\eta_{a^{\prime}d^{\prime}}M_{b^{\prime}c^{\prime}}-\eta_{a^{\prime}c^{\prime}}M_{b^{\prime}d^{\prime}}-\eta_{b^{\prime}d^{\prime}}M_{a^{\prime}c^{\prime}}+\eta_{b^{\prime}c^{\prime}}M_{a^{\prime}d^{\prime}},\;a^{\prime},b^{\prime},c^{\prime},d^{\prime}=0,\ldots D-1.

Define Cartan 1-form with value in the
(a)ds_{D}
algebra

\mathcal{C}(d)=\mathscr{G}^{-1}d\mathscr{G}=2G^{Da^{\prime}}(d)M_{Da^{\prime}}+G^{a^{\prime}b^{\prime}}(d)M_{a^{\prime}b^{\prime}}\in(a)ds_{D},

(1)

where
\mathscr{G}\in SO(2,D-1)/SO(1,D-1)
for anti-de Sitter space and
\mathscr{G}\in SO(1,D)/SO(1,D-1)
for de Sitter space. By definition Cartan form obeys Maurer-Cartan equation that in terms of differential forms is written as
d\mathcal{C}(d)=\mathcal{C}(d)\wedge\mathcal{C}(d)
. It splits into equations for the Cartan forms associated with generators of the
so(2,D-1)/so(1,D-1)
(
so(1,D)/so(1,D-1)
) quotient algebra

dG^{Da^{\prime}}(d)=2G^{a^{\prime}}{}_{b^{\prime}}(d)\wedge G^{Db^{\prime}}(d)

(2)

and equations for the Cartan forms associated with the
so(1,D-1)
stability algebra generators

dG^{a^{\prime}b^{\prime}}(d)=-sG^{Da^{\prime}}(d)\wedge G^{Db^{\prime}}(d)+sG^{Db^{\prime}}(d)\wedge G^{Da^{\prime}}(d)+2G^{a^{\prime}}{}_{c^{\prime}}(d)\wedge G^{c^{\prime}b^{\prime}}(d).

(3)

The Cartan forms
G^{Da^{\prime}}(d)
are identified with the vielbein 1-form
E^{a^{\prime}}(d)=dX^{m^{\prime}}E^{a^{\prime}}_{m^{\prime}}(X)
of the (anti-)de Sitter space parametrized by (local) coordinates
X^{m^{\prime}}
and
G^{a^{\prime}b^{\prime}}(d)
is identified with its spin connection 1-form
\Omega^{a^{\prime}b^{\prime}}(d)=dX^{m^{\prime}}\Omega^{a^{\prime}b^{\prime}}_{m^{\prime}}(X)
:

E^{a^{\prime}}(d)=G^{Da^{\prime}}(d),\quad\Omega^{a^{\prime}b^{\prime}}(d)=2G^{a^{\prime}b^{\prime}}(d).

The coefficient +2 in the second equality follows from comparison of the Maurer-Cartan equation (
2
) with the zero torsion condition for the space-time vielbein

T^{a^{\prime}}(d)=dE^{a^{\prime}}(d)-\Omega^{a^{\prime}}{}_{b^{\prime}}(d)\wedge E^{b^{\prime}}(d)=0.

Identification of the Cartan forms with the vielbein and spin connection transforms the Maurer-Cartan Eq.(
3
) into definition of the curvature 2-form of the (anti-)de Sitter space

R^{a^{\prime}b^{\prime}}(d)=d\Omega^{a^{\prime}b^{\prime}}(d)-\Omega^{a^{\prime}}{}_{c^{\prime}}(d)\wedge\Omega^{c^{\prime}b^{\prime}}(d)=-2sE^{a^{\prime}}(d)\wedge E^{b^{\prime}}(d).

3.2
Lagrangian formulation of null string in (anti-)de Sitter space

The action of the null string in (anti-)de Sitter space

S=\int d^{2}\xi\mathscr{L}(\xi),\quad\mathscr{L}(\xi)=-\frac{1}{2}\rho^{i}\rho^{j}G^{Da^{\prime}}_{i}\eta_{a^{\prime}b^{\prime}}G^{Db^{\prime}}_{j},

(4)

where
\xi^{i}=(\tau,\sigma)
are local world-sheet coordinates, depends on the world-sheet projections of the Cartan forms associated with the quotient algebra generators
G^{Da^{\prime}}_{i}=\partial_{i}X^{m^{\prime}}(\xi)E^{a^{\prime}}_{m^{\prime}}(X(\xi))
. Induced world-sheet metric is defined as
g_{ij}(\xi)=G^{Da^{\prime}}_{i}\eta_{a^{\prime}b^{\prime}}G^{Db^{\prime}}_{j}=\partial_{i}X^{m^{\prime}}g_{m^{\prime}n^{\prime}}(X)\partial_{j}X^{n^{\prime}}
, where
g_{m^{\prime}n^{\prime}}(X)=E^{a^{\prime}}_{m^{\prime}}(X)\eta_{a^{\prime}b^{\prime}}E^{b^{\prime}}_{n^{\prime}}(X)
is metric tensor of the (anti-)de Sitter space and
\eta_{a^{\prime}b^{\prime}}
is tangent space Minkowski metric proportional to the Killing form of the
so(2,D-1)
(
so(1,D)
) algebra restricted to the subspace spanned by generators of the quotient algebra. Due to the presence of
\eta_{a^{\prime}b^{\prime}}
the space-time and induced metrics are invariant under local
SO(1,D-1)
symmetry generated by stability group action on the coset representative
\mathscr{G}^{\prime}=\mathscr{G}H
,
H\in SO(1,D-1)
. Induced metric is also invariant under global
SO(2,D-1)
(
SO(1,D)
) symmetry
\mathscr{G}^{\prime}H^{-1}=G\mathscr{G}
,
G\in SO(2,d-1)
(
SO(1,D)
) of (anti-)de Sitter space but the Cartan forms get transformed as follows from their definition (
1
)

\mathcal{C}^{\prime}(d)=H^{-1}\mathcal{C}(d)H+H^{-1}dH.

(5)

In particular,
G^{a^{\prime}b^{\prime}}(d)
transforms as 1-form connection that justifies its identification with the spin connection of (anti-)de Sitter space-time.

Lagrangian (
4
) also includes auxiliary world-sheet vector density
\rho^{i}(\xi)
of weight
w=-1/2
. (This vector density is proportional to the world-sheet zweibein components and enter formulations of both tensionless and tensile (super)strings proposed and examined in
[
6
]
,
[
7
]
,
[
8
]
,
[
11
]
.) Under world-sheet reparametrizations
\xi^{\prime}=\xi^{\prime}(\xi)
it transforms as

\rho^{\prime i}(\xi^{\prime})=J^{-w}\frac{\partial\xi^{\prime i}}{\partial\xi^{j}}\rho^{j}(\xi),\quad J=\left|\textrm{det}\frac{\partial\xi}{\partial\xi^{\prime}}\right|.

From algebraic equations resulting from variation of
\rho^{i}(\xi)

g_{ij}\rho^{j}=0

it follows that
\rho^{i}
is non-zero eigenvector of the induced metric
g_{ij}
with zero eigenvalue that amounts to vanishing of its determinant.

3.3
Dynamical equations of null string in (anti-)de Sitter space and their Lax representation

Equations for the space-time coordinate fields
X^{m^{\prime}}(\xi)
can be presented through the Cartan forms if one takes as variation parameters not
\delta X^{m^{\prime}}(\xi)
but
G^{Da^{\prime}}(\delta)=\delta X^{m^{\prime}}E^{a^{\prime}}_{m^{\prime}}
. The latter can be obtained from the Cartan forms
G^{Da^{\prime}}(d)
by applying the operator of formal substitution
i_{\delta}G^{Da^{\prime}}(d)=\delta X^{m^{\prime}}E^{a^{\prime}}_{m^{\prime}}
. One also has to use well-known Cartan formula for Lie derivative of a differential form
F(d)
:
\mathcal{L}F(d)=di_{\delta}F+i_{\delta}dF
. It equals variation of differential forms, which dependence on the space-time coordinates and their differentials does not change that holds for background fields. When applied to the Cartan forms
G^{Da^{\prime}}(d)
, one should substitute in the second summand the Maurer-Cartan equations (
2
). This results in the following equation

-\frac{\delta S}{\delta G^{D}{}_{a^{\prime}}(\delta)}=\rho^{i}\nabla_{i}(\rho^{j}G^{Da^{\prime}}_{j})+2\rho^{i}G_{i}^{a^{\prime}}{}_{b^{\prime}}\rho^{j}G^{Db^{\prime}}_{j}=0,

(6)

where
\nabla_{i}
is a covariant derivative on the world-sheet and vector density
\rho^{i}
is taken to be divergence-free
\nabla_{i}\rho^{i}=0
. Since
\rho^{i}
plays the role of zweibein on the null world sheet, this condition is an analogue of the covariant constancy condition for metric/zweibein on the non-singular world-sheet
[
50
]
.

We write Lagrangian equation (
6
) as the Lax equation

\rho^{i}\nabla_{i}L-[L,M]=0,

(7)

where

L=\rho^{i}G^{Da^{\prime}}_{i}M_{Da^{\prime}},\quad M=\rho^{i}G^{a^{\prime}b^{\prime}}_{i}M_{a^{\prime}b^{\prime}}

are components of the Lax pair. The Lax representation (
7
) has the same form as in the case of massless (super)particle
[
69
]
,
[
70
]
modulo substitution
\rho^{i}\nabla_{i}\rightarrow\frac{d}{d\tau}
, where
\tau
is (super)particle’s world-line parameter. Note that the Lax equation is invariant under local
SO(1,D-1)
stability group transformation that acts on the Lax pair components in the following way

L^{\prime}=H^{-1}LH,\quad M^{\prime}=H^{-1}MH+H^{-1}\rho^{i}\nabla_{i}H

in accordance with the transformation law (
5
) for the Cartan forms.

3.4
Lax represention of Hamiltonian equations of null string in (anti-)de Sitter space

In the original derivation of the null string Lagrangian in the formulation with auxiliary vector density (
4
) one takes tension-to-zero limit in the phase-space representation of the tensile string Lagrangian in order to make this limit non-singular. Below we present in the form of the Lax equation dynamical equations that follow from the null string Lagrangian expressed in terms of the phase-space variables. To this end introduce density of the canonical momentum conjugate to the space-time coordinates

p_{m^{\prime}}(\tau,\sigma)=-\rho^{\tau}\rho^{\tau}g_{m^{\prime}n^{\prime}}(X)\partial_{\tau}X^{n^{\prime}}-\rho^{\tau}\rho^{\sigma}g_{m^{\prime}n^{\prime}}(X)\partial_{\sigma}X^{n^{\prime}}.

(8)

It is convenient to introduce momentum density with the tangent-space index

p_{a^{\prime}}(\tau,\sigma)=E^{n^{\prime}}_{a^{\prime}}p_{n^{\prime}},

where
E^{n^{\prime}}_{a^{\prime}}
is inverse space-time vielbein:
E^{m^{\prime}}_{a^{\prime}}E_{m^{\prime}}^{b^{\prime}}=\delta_{a^{\prime}}^{b^{\prime}}
. Then Lagrangian of the null string is written in the form

\displaystyle\mathscr{L}(\tau,\sigma)=p_{m^{\prime}}\partial_{\tau}X^{m^{\prime}}-\mathscr{H}=p_{a^{\prime}}G^{Da^{\prime}}_{\tau}-\mathscr{H},

(9)

\displaystyle\mathscr{H}(\tau,\sigma)=\frac{1}{2(\rho^{\tau})^{2}}p_{a^{\prime}}\eta^{a^{\prime}b^{\prime}}p_{b^{\prime}}+\frac{\rho^{\sigma}}{\rho^{\tau}}p_{a^{\prime}}G^{Da^{\prime}}_{\sigma},\quad\rho^{\tau}\not=0.

As should be for dynamical systems invariant under reparametrization symmetry, density of the Hamiltonian is given by the sum of the first-class constraints

p^{2}=p_{a^{\prime}}\eta^{a^{\prime}b^{\prime}}p_{b^{\prime}}\approx 0,\quad p_{a^{\prime}}G^{Da^{\prime}}_{\sigma}\approx 0

(10)

that are generators of this symmetry, with arbitrary Lagrange multipliers. Their role is played by components of the world-sheet vector denstity
\rho^{i}
like in the case of tensile string
[
11
]
. Variation of the Lagrangian (
9
) on the phase-space variables gives two equations

\rho^{i}G^{Da^{\prime}}_{i}+\frac{1}{\rho^{\tau}}p^{a^{\prime}}=0,

(11)

\rho^{i}\nabla_{i}\left(\frac{1}{\rho^{\tau}}p^{a^{\prime}}\right)+2\rho^{i}G_{i}^{a^{\prime}}{}_{b^{\prime}}\frac{1}{\rho^{\tau}}p^{b^{\prime}}=0.

(12)

Eq. (
11
) determines momentum density and Eq. (
12
) is dynamical equation. In writing these equations it was taken into account that
(\rho^{\tau})^{-1}p^{a^{\prime}}
transforms under reparametrizations as a world-sheet scalar density of weight
w=-1/2
as follows from the definition of momentum density (
8
)

\frac{1}{\rho^{\tau}}p_{m^{\prime}}(\tau,\sigma)=-\rho^{i}g_{m^{\prime}n^{\prime}}(X)\partial_{i}X^{n^{\prime}}.

As is known covariant derivative of a tensor density
\phi
of weight
w
includes contribution of contracted Christoffel symbols

\nabla_{i}\phi^{\cdots}_{\cdots}=\partial_{i}\phi^{\cdots}_{\cdots}+w\Gamma^{j}_{ij}\phi^{\cdots}_{\cdots}+\ldots,

where dots stands for terms that are the same for tensor density and respective true tensor. In the case of the null string condition
\nabla_{i}\rho^{i}=0
determines projection of
\Gamma^{j}_{ij}
on
\rho^{i}
:
\rho^{i}\Gamma^{j}_{ij}=-2\partial_{i}\rho^{i}
. This relation allows writing Eq. (
12
) in another form

\partial_{i}\left(\frac{\rho^{i}}{\rho^{\tau}}p^{a^{\prime}}\right)+2\rho^{i}G_{i}^{a^{\prime}}{}_{b^{\prime}}\frac{1}{\rho^{\tau}}p^{b^{\prime}}=0.

Eq. (
12
) can be cast into the form of the Lax equation (
7
), in which in expression for the Lax component
L
substituted was Eq. (
11
)

L=\rho^{i}G^{Da^{\prime}}_{i}M_{Da^{\prime}}=-\frac{1}{\rho^{\tau}}p^{a^{\prime}}M_{Da^{\prime}}.

3.5
Lax representation of Lagrangian and Hamiltonian equations of tensile string in (anti-)de Sitter space

For study of tension-to-zero limit of the integrable structure of string in (anti-)de Sitter space it is of interest to compare obtained Lax representation for the null string equations with that in case of non-zero tension.

Tensile string counterpart of the null string action (
4
) is well known

S=\int d^{2}\xi\mathscr{L}(\xi),\quad\mathscr{L}(\xi)=-\frac{T}{2}\sqrt{-\gamma}\gamma^{ij}G^{Da^{\prime}}_{i}\eta_{a^{\prime}b^{\prime}}G^{Db^{\prime}}_{j}.

(13)

It includes non-sigular auxiliary world-sheet metric
\gamma_{ij}
with determinant
\gamma
and inverse metric
\gamma^{ij}
. It is also known representation of the string Lagrangian in terms of differential forms

\mathscr{L}(\xi)=\frac{T}{2}G^{Da^{\prime}}(d)\eta_{a^{\prime}b^{\prime}}\wedge\ast G^{Db^{\prime}},(d)

(14)

where the Hodge dual of a world-sheet 1-form
\alpha(d)=d\xi^{i}\alpha_{i}
is defined as

\ast\alpha(d)=-\sqrt{-\gamma}d\xi^{k}\varepsilon_{kj}\gamma^{ji}\alpha_{i}=-\frac{1}{\sqrt{-\gamma}}d\xi^{k}\gamma_{kj}\varepsilon^{ji}\alpha_{i},\quad\varepsilon^{ij}=-\varepsilon^{ji},\ \varepsilon^{ij}\varepsilon_{jk}=\delta^{i}_{k},\ \varepsilon^{\tau\sigma}=+1.

From the action (
13
) with Lagrangian expressed in terms of differential forms (
14
) there follow dynamical equations of the tensile string

d\ast G^{Da^{\prime}}(d)-2G^{a^{\prime}}{}_{b^{\prime}}(d)\wedge\ast G^{Db^{\prime}}(d)=0,

the form of which is similar to the Maurer-Cartan equation (
2
). This similarly underlies the Lax representation of these equations as the zero curvature condition

dL(d)-L(d)\wedge L(d)=0

(15)

of the Lax 1-form

L(d)=2L^{Da^{\prime}}(d)M_{Da^{\prime}}+L^{a^{\prime}b^{\prime}}M_{a^{\prime}b^{\prime}}\in(a)ds_{D},

where

L^{Da^{\prime}}(d)=\ell_{1}G^{Da^{\prime}}(d)+\ell_{2}\ast G^{Da^{\prime}}(d),\quad L^{a^{\prime}b^{\prime}}(d)=G^{a^{\prime}b^{\prime}}(d).

The dependence of functions
\ell_{1}
and
\ell_{2}
on the spectral parameter is determined by algebraic equation
\ell_{1}^{2}-\ell_{2}^{2}=1
that is the consequence of the zero curvature condition. Eq. (
15
) is invariant under gauge transformation of the Lax 1-form
L^{\prime}(d)=H^{-1}L(d)H+H^{-1}dH
,
H\in SO(1,D-1)
that allows to interpret it as trivial
SO(1,D-1)
connection. Let us also note that in the case, when the world-sheet reparametrizations and dilations are used to bring auxiliary metric equal to the two-dimensional Minkowski metric
\gamma_{ij}=\eta_{ij}=\textrm{diag}(-1,+1)
,
L^{Da^{\prime}}(d)
is related to
G^{Da^{\prime}}(d)
by the two-dimensional Lorentz transformation

L^{Da^{\prime}}_{i}=\Lambda_{i}{}^{j}G^{Da^{\prime}}_{j},\quad\Lambda=\left(\begin{array}[]{cc}\textrm{ch}\varphi&\textrm{sh}\varphi\\
\textrm{sh}\varphi&\textrm{ch}\varphi\end{array}\right)\in SO(1,1),

where
\varphi
is the spectral parameter and
\ell_{1}=\textrm{ch}\varphi
,
\ell_{2}=\textrm{sh}\varphi
.

In the canonical approach momentum density conjugate to the space-time coordinates is determined by the relation

-\frac{1}{T\sqrt{-\gamma}}p_{m^{\prime}}(\tau,\sigma)=\gamma^{\tau\tau}g_{m^{\prime}n^{\prime}}(X)\partial_{\tau}X^{n^{\prime}}+\gamma^{\tau\sigma}g_{m^{\prime}n^{\prime}}(X)\partial_{\sigma}X^{n^{\prime}}.

(16)

Like in the tensionless case there can be introduced tangent-space momentum density
p_{a^{\prime}}(\tau,\sigma)=E_{a^{\prime}}^{n^{\prime}}p_{n^{\prime}}
. In terms of it one can express two first-class constraints that generate reparametrizations of the tensile string world sheet

p_{a^{\prime}}\eta^{a^{\prime}b^{\prime}}p_{b^{\prime}}+T^{2}G^{Da^{\prime}}_{\sigma}\eta_{a^{\prime}b^{\prime}}G^{Db^{\prime}}_{\sigma}\approx 0,\quad p_{a^{\prime}}G^{Da^{\prime}}_{\sigma}\approx 0.

In the tenstion-to-zero limit they turn into the constraints of the null string (
10
). Lagrangian (
13
) in terms of canonical variables has the form

\mathscr{L}=p_{a^{\prime}}G^{Da^{\prime}}_{\tau}+\frac{\lambda}{2}(p^{2}+T^{2}G^{Da^{\prime}}_{\sigma}\eta_{a^{\prime}b^{\prime}}G^{Db^{\prime}}_{\sigma})+\rho p_{a^{\prime}}G^{Da^{\prime}}_{\sigma},

(17)

where
\lambda^{-1}=T\sqrt{-\gamma}\gamma^{\tau\tau}
and
\rho=\gamma^{\tau\sigma}(\gamma^{\tau\tau})^{-1}
play the role of Lagrange multipliers at the first-class constraints. From the string action with the Lagrangian (
17
) there follow two non-trivial equations

p^{a^{\prime}}=-\frac{1}{\lambda}(G^{Da^{\prime}}_{\tau}+\rho G^{Da^{\prime}}_{\sigma}),

(18)

\partial_{\tau}p^{a^{\prime}}+2G_{\tau}^{a^{\prime}}{}_{b^{\prime}}p^{b^{\prime}}+\partial_{\sigma}(\rho p^{a^{\prime}}+T^{2}\lambda G^{Da^{\prime}}_{\sigma})+2G_{\sigma}^{a^{\prime}}{}_{b^{\prime}}(\rho p^{b^{\prime}}+T^{2}\lambda G^{Db^{\prime}}_{\sigma})=0.

(19)

Eq. (
18
) is another form of the definition of string momentum (
16
). It is used to express components of the Lax connection
L(d)=d\tau L_{\tau}+d\sigma L_{\sigma}
in terms of canonical variables

\displaystyle-L^{Da^{\prime}}_{\tau}=\ell_{1}(\lambda p^{a^{\prime}}+\rho G^{Da^{\prime}}_{\sigma})+\ell_{2}\left(\frac{\rho}{T}p^{a^{\prime}}+T\lambda G^{Da^{\prime}}_{\sigma}\right),\quad L^{Da^{\prime}}_{\sigma}=\ell_{1}G^{Da^{\prime}}_{\sigma}+\ell_{2}\frac{1}{T}p^{a^{\prime}},

\displaystyle L^{a^{\prime}b^{\prime}}_{\tau}=G^{a^{\prime}b^{\prime}}_{\tau},\quad L^{a^{\prime}b^{\prime}}_{\sigma}=G^{a^{\prime}b^{\prime}}_{\sigma}.

Above components of the Lax connection enter the zero-curvature condition (
15
) that in component form reduces to single equation

\partial_{\tau}L_{\sigma}-\partial_{\sigma}L_{\tau}+[L_{\tau},L_{\sigma}]=0.

It holds on dynamical equations (
19
) and Maurer-Cartan equations (
2
) and (
3
) expressed in terms of the phase-space variables of the string

\displaystyle\partial_{\tau}G^{Da^{\prime}}_{\sigma}+\partial_{\sigma}(\lambda p^{a^{\prime}}+\rho G^{Da^{\prime}}_{\sigma})+2G_{\tau}^{a^{\prime}}{}_{b^{\prime}}G^{Db^{\prime}}_{\sigma}+2G_{\sigma}^{a^{\prime}}{}_{b^{\prime}}(\lambda p^{b^{\prime}}+\rho G^{Db^{\prime}}_{\sigma})=0,

\displaystyle\partial_{\tau}G^{a^{\prime}b^{\prime}}_{\sigma}-\partial_{\sigma}G^{a^{\prime}b^{\prime}}_{\tau}+2G_{\tau}^{a^{\prime}}{}_{c^{\prime}}G^{c^{\prime}b^{\prime}}_{\sigma}-2G_{\sigma}^{a^{\prime}}{}_{c^{\prime}}G^{c^{\prime}b^{\prime}}_{\tau}+2s\lambda(p^{a^{\prime}}G^{Db^{\prime}}_{\sigma}-p^{b^{\prime}}G^{Da^{\prime}}_{\sigma})=0.

4
Conclusion

Study of integrable structures allows unveiling hidden symmetries that underlie duality between gauge fields and strings in anti-de Sitter superspaces. In superspaces containing anti-de Sitter spaces of dimension
D<5
it is obscured by broken supersymmetries. Study of simplifying limits such as those of zero and infinite tension of superstrings can provide important information on the integrable structures involved in respective dualities.

Here we obtained the Lax representation of equations of the null string in
D-
dimensional (anti-)de Sitter space. For instance, for
D=4
anti-de Sitter space it has the same group-theoretic structure as contribution proportional to generators of its isometry algebra to bosonic limit of the Lax representation of equations of massless superparticle on the
OSp(4|6)/(SO(1,3)\times U(3))
supercoset manifold and in the
AdS_{4}\times\mathbb{CP}^{3}
superspace. Obtained Lax representation can be readily generalized to null superstrings on supercoset manifolds. Generalization to
AdS
superspaces containing supergroup manifolds, like the
AdS_{4}\times\mathbb{CP}^{3}
one, is non-trivial but can give certain information on the integrable structure of tensile superstrings in these superspaces as shows found earlier correspondence between structure of the Lax pair of massless superparticle and the Lax connection of two-dimensional sigma-model on supergroup manifolds.

Another result of this work is proposed twistor interpretation of the formulation of (null) strings in anti-de Sitter space in terms of group-theoretic variables.
