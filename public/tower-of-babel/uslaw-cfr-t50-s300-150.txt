50 C.F.R. § 300.150 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:50:300.150
Creator: International Fishing And Related Activities
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-300.150
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 300 (International Fisheries Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart regulates U.S. nationals fishing in the Russian fisheries and implements the Agreement between the Government of the United States of America and the Government of the Union of Soviet Socialist Republics on Mutual Fisheries Relations, signed May 31, 1988.
