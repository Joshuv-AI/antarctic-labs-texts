33 C.F.R. § 333.26 — Publication of the environmental impact statement.
Citation: us:cfr:33:333.26
Creator: Corps Of Engineers, Department Of The Army, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-333.26
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 333 (Processing Of Department Of The Army Permits And 33 U.S.C. 408 Permissions, National Environmental Policy Act Implementing Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The District Engineer will publish the entire environmental impact statement on a publicly available website. During the process of preparing the environmental impact statement, the District Engineer may publish a draft statement or other materials that in their judgment may assist in fulfilling their NEPA responsibilities.
