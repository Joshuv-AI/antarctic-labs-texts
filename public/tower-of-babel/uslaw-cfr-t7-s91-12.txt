7 C.F.R. § 91.12 — Record of filing time and laboratory tests.
Citation: us:cfr:7:91.12
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-91.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 91 (Services And General Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A record showing the date of receipt for each application for a laboratory service or an appeal of a laboratory service shall be maintained. In addition, the requested laboratory analyses shall be recorded at the time of sample receipt.
