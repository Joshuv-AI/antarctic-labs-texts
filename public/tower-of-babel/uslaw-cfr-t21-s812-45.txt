21 C.F.R. § 812.45 — Informing investigators.
Citation: us:cfr:21:812.45
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-812.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 812 (Investigational Device Exemptions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A sponsor shall supply all investigators participating in the investigation with copies of the investigational plan and the report of prior investigations of the device.
