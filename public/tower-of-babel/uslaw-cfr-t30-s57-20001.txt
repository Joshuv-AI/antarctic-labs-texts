30 C.F.R. § 57.20001 — Intoxicating beverages and narcotics.
Citation: us:cfr:30:57.20001
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-57.20001
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 57 (Safety And Health Standards—Underground Metal And Nonmetal Mines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Intoxicating beverages and narcotics shall not be permitted or used in or around mines. Persons under the influence of alcohol or narcotics shall not be permitted on the job.
