39 C.F.R. § 221.1 — The United States Postal Service.
Citation: us:cfr:39:221.1
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-221.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 221 (General Organization). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The United States Postal Service was established as an independent establishment within the executive branch of the government of the United States under the Postal Reorganization Act of August 12, 1970 (Pub. L. 91-375, 84 Stat. 719).
