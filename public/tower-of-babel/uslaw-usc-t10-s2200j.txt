10 U.S.C. § 2200j — Definitions

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Training And Education); Chapter 113 (Defense Civilian Training Corps). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter:
(1)
The term “program” means the Defense Civilian Training Corps program established under section 2200g.
(2)
The term “member of the program” means a student at an accredited civilian educational institution who is enrolled in the program.
