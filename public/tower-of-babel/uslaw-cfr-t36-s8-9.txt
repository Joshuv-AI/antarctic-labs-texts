36 C.F.R. § 8.9 — Posting of regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:36:8.9
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-8.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 8 (Labor Standards Applicable To Employees Of National Park Service Concessioners). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Concessioners shall post in a conspicuous place easily accessible to all employees copies of the regulations in this part in such form as the Director may approve.
