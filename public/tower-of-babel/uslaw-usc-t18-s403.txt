18 U.S.C. § 403 — Protection of the privacy of child victims and child witnesses
Citation: us:usc:18:403
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section403
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 21 (Contempts). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A knowing or intentional violation of the privacy protection accorded by section 3509 of this title is a criminal contempt punishable by not more than one year’s imprisonment, or a fine under this title, or both.
