16 C.F.R. § 1018.11 — Charters.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1018.11
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1018.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1018 (Advisory Committee Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) No advisory committee shall meet or take any action until its charter has been filed with the GSA Secretariat in accordance with the requirements of section 9(c) of the Federal Advisory Committee Act.
(b) The Advisory Committee Management officer shall have responsibility for the preparation and filing of charters.
