46 C.F.R. § 540.3 — Proof of financial responsibility, when required.
Citation: us:cfr:46:540.3
Creator: Federal Maritime Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-540.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 540 (Passenger Vessel Financial Responsibility). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person in the United States may arrange, offer, advertise or provide passage on a vessel unless a Certificate (Performance) has been issued to or covers such person.
