40 U.S.C. § 18104 — TTY installation by Congress
Citation: us:usc:40:18104
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section18104
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Chapter 181 (Telecommunications Accessibility For Hearing-Impaired And Speech-Impaired Individuals). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each House of Congress shall establish a policy under which Members of the House of Representatives and the Senate may obtain TTY’s for use in communicating with hearing-impaired and speech-impaired individuals, and for the use of hearing-impaired and speech-impaired employees.
