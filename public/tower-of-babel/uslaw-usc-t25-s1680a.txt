25 U.S.C. § 1680a — Contract health facilities
Citation: us:usc:25:1680a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section1680a
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 18 (Indian Health Care); Subchapter VI (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Service shall provide funds for health care programs and facilities operated by tribes and tribal organizations under contracts with the Service entered into under the Indian Self-Determination Act [25 U.S.C. 5321 et seq.]—
(1)
for the maintenance and repair of clinics owned or leased by such tribes or tribal organizations,
(2)
for employee training,
(3)
for cost-of-living increases for employees, and
(4)
for any other expenses relating to the provision of health services,
on the same basis as such funds are provided to programs and facilities operated directly by the Service.
