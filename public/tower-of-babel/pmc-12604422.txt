Correction: Novilysobacter viscosus sp. Nov. and Novilysobacter longmucuonensis sp. Nov., two Nov.l species capable of producing proteases and RiPPs
Authors: Dorji Phurbu, Xuan Zhang, Rui Wang, Zi-Xuan Liu, Yan-Yan Zheng, Ai-Hua Li
Published: 2025

Correction: BMC Microbiol 25, 448 (2025)

https://doi.org/10.1186/s12866-025-04178-y

Following publication of the original article [1], the authors noticed that on the online version, the title of the article is written incorrectly. The incorrect and correct title are shown below. The original article has been corrected.

Incorrect title:

Novilysobacter viscosus sp. Nov. and Novilysobacter longmucuonensis sp. Nov., two Nov.l species capable of producing proteases and RiPPs

Correct title:

Novilysobacter viscosus sp. nov. and Novilysobacter longmucuonensis sp. nov., two novel species capable of producing proteases and RiPPs
