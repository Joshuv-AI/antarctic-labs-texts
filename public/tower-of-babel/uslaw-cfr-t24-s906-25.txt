24 C.F.R. § 906.25 — Ownership interests that may be conveyed to a purchaser.
Citation: us:cfr:24:906.25
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-906.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 906 (Public Housing Homeownership Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A homeownership program may provide for sale to the purchasing family of any ownership interest that the PHA considers appropriate under the homeownership program, including but not limited to:
(a) Ownership in fee simple;
(b) A condominium interest;
(c) An interest in a limited dividend cooperative;
(d) A shared appreciation interest with a PHA providing financing; or
(e) A leasehold under a bona fide lease-purchase arrangement.
