FEDERAL RESERVE SYSTEM


Change in Bank Control Notices; Acquisitions of Shares of a Bank 
or Bank Holding Company; Correction

AGENCY: Federal Reserve System.

ACTION: Notice; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Federal Reserve System published a document in the Federal 
Register of October 22, 2026, concerning the list of notificants who 
applied under the Change in Bank Control Act to acquire shares of a 
bank or bank holding company. The document contained incorrect 
information under paragraph B.1.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

Correction

    In the Federal Register of October 22, 2026, in FR Doc. 2026-21625, 
on page 66703, in the third column, correct paragraph B.1 to read:
    1. The Jerome and Barbara Bushman Lifetime Bank Stock Revocable 
Trust dated December 5, 2023, as amended, Barbara A. Bushman, as 
trustee, all of Naples, Florida, and Tia G. Bushman, Plymouth, 
Minnesota, and Mitchell Bushman, Hatley, Wisconsin, individually, and 
as trustees; to join the Bushman Family Group, a group acting in 
concert, to retain voting shares of First Southeastern Banc Group, 
Inc., and thereby indirectly retain voting shares of First Southeast 
Bank, both of Harmony, Minnesota.

    Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System.
Michele Taylor Fennell,
Associate Secretary of the Board.
