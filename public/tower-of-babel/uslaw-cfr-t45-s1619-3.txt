45 C.F.R. § 1619.3 — Referral to the Corporation.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1619.3
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1619.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1619 (Disclosure Of Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a person requests information, not required to be disclosed by this part, that the Corporation may be required to disclose pursuant to part 1602 of this chapter implementing the Freedom of Information Act, the recipient shall either provide the information or inform the person seeking it how to request it from the Corporation.
