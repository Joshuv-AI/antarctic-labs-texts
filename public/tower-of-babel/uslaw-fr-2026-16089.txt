ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY

[FRL-13410-01-R10]


Proposed Reissuance of NPDES General Permit for Oil and Gas 
Exploration Facilities in Federal Waters of Cook Inlet (AKG51000)

AGENCY: Environmental Protection Agency (EPA).

ACTION: Notice of proposed reissuance of NPDES General Permit and 
request for public comment.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Environmental Protection Agency (EPA), Region 10, proposes 
to reissue the National Pollutant Discharge Elimination System (NPDES) 
General Permit for Oil and Gas Exploration Facilities in Federal Waters 
of Cook Inlet (draft GP). The draft GP proposes to authorize certain 
discharges of pollutants into the federal waters of Cook Inlet from oil 
and gas exploration facilities subject to limits and requirements 
designed to minimize pollution and protect water quality.

DATES: Comments must be received by September 21, 2026.

ADDRESSES: Comments and requests regarding the draft GP must be 
submitted to [email protected] with the subject line: Public 
Comments on AKG51000. The draft GP, Fact Sheet, and Ocean Discharge 
Criteria Evaluation are available on the EPA Region 10 website at 
https://www.epa.gov/npdes-permits/npdes-general-permit-oil-and-gas-exploration-facilities-federal-waters-cook-inlet.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Copies of the administrative record 
and additional information concerning the draft GP are also available 
upon request. Requests may be made to Abigail Conner at 
[email protected] or (206) 553-6358.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Please see the draft GP and Fact Sheet.

[[Page 51173]]

Other Legal Requirements

    This action is not a significant regulatory action and was 
therefore not submitted to the Office of Management and Budget (OMB) 
for review. Compliance with Endangered Species Act, Essential Fish 
Habitat, Paperwork Reduction Act, and other requirements are discussed 
in the fact sheet to the proposed permit.
    This notice is given pursuant to 40 CFR 124.10(c)(2)(i).
