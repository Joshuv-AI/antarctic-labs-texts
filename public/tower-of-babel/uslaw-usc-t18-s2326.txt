18 U.S.C. § 2326 — Enhanced penalties
Citation: us:usc:18:2326
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section2326
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 113A (Telemarketing And Email Marketing Fraud). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A person who is convicted of an offense under section 1028, 1029, 1341, 1342, 1343, 1344, or 1347 or section 1128B of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1320a–7b), or a conspiracy to commit such an offense, in connection with the conduct of telemarketing or email marketing—
(1)
shall be imprisoned for a term of up to 5 years in addition to any term of imprisonment imposed under any of those sections, respectively; and
(2)
in the case of an offense under any of those sections that—
(A)
victimized ten or more persons over the age of 55; or
(B)
targeted persons over the age of 55,
shall be imprisoned for a term of up to 10 years in addition to any term of imprisonment imposed under any of those sections, respectively.
