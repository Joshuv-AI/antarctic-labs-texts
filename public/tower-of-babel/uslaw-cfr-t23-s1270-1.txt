23 C.F.R. § 1270.1 — Scope.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 1270 (Open Container Laws). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part prescribes the requirements necessary to implement Section 154 of Title 23 of the United States Code which encourages States to enact and enforce open container laws.
