51 U.S.C. § 60132 — Availability of federally gathered unenhanced data
Citation: us:usc:51:60132
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section60132
Context: United States Code, Title 51 (National And Commercial Space Programs); Chapter 601 (Land Remote Sensing Policy); Subchapter IV (Research, Development, And Demonstration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In General.—
All unenhanced land remote sensing data gathered and owned by the United States Government, including unenhanced data gathered under the technology demonstration program carried out pursuant to section 60133 of this title, shall be made available to users in a timely fashion.
(b) Protection for Commercial Data Distributor.—
The President shall seek to ensure that unenhanced data gathered under the technology demonstration program carried out pursuant to section 60133 of this title shall, to the extent practicable, be made available on terms that would not adversely affect the commercial market for unenhanced data gathered by the Landsat 6 spacecraft.
