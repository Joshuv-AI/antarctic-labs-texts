40 U.S.C. § 607 — Records
Citation: us:usc:40:607
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section607
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Chapter 5 (Property Management); Subchapter VI (Motor Vehicle Pools And Transportation Systems). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator of General Services shall maintain an accurate record of the cost of establishing, maintaining, and operating each motor vehicle pool or system established under section 602 of this title.
