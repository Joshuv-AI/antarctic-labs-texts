Abstract
We report the first observation of the two-body baryonic decays B + → Σ c ​ ( 2455 ) + ⁣ + ​ Ξ ¯ c − B^{+}\to\Sigma_{c}(2455)^{++}\overline{\Xi}_{c}^{-} and B 0 → Σ c ​ ( 2455 ) 0 ​ Ξ ¯ c 0 B^{0}\to\Sigma_{c}(2455)^{0}\overline{\Xi}_{c}^{0} with significances of 7.3 ​ σ 7.3\,\sigma and 6.2 ​ σ 6.2\,\sigma , respectively, including statistical and systematic uncertainties. Baryonic
B

baryon–antibaryon pairs in the nonperturbative regime of quantum chromodynamics (QCD).
Over the past three decades, a number of such decays have been observed

;

These observations help elucidate the intricate kinematic and dynamical properties of baryonic
B

In 2003, the Belle experiment reported the first observation of a two-body baryonic decay, and measured the decay

B^{0}\to\overline{\Lambda}_{c}^{-}p

10^{-5}

Belle:2002gir

In 2006, Belle observed the double-charm decays

this result was later confirmed by the BaBar experiment

Nevertheless, their branching fractions differ by nearly two orders of magnitude,

Several possible mechanisms have been proposed to account for the large decay rates into pairs of charmed baryons,

Cheng:2009yz

Further measurements of

In summary, we report the first observation of the two-body baryonic decays

B^{+}\to\Sigma_{c}(2455)^{++}\overline{\Xi}_{c}^{-}
and
B^{0}\to\Sigma_{c}(2455)^{0}\overline{\Xi}_{c}^{0}
,
using electron-positron data samples that contain

772\times 10^{6}
and
387\times 10^{6}

\Upsilon(4S)
events collected by the Belle and
Belle II detectors, respectively. The branching fractions are measured to be

\mathcal{B}(B^{+}\to\Sigma_{c}(2455)^{++}\overline{\Xi}_{c}^{-})=(5.74\pm 1.11\pm 0.42^{+2.47}_{-1.53})\times 10^{-4}

and
\mathcal{B}(B^{0}\to\Sigma_{c}(2455)^{0}\overline{\Xi}_{c}^{0})=(4.83\pm 1.12\pm 0.37_{-0.60}^{+0.72})\times 10^{-4}
,
where the uncertainties are statistical, systematic, and from the absolute branching
fractions of
\overline{\Xi}_{c}^{-}
or
\overline{\Xi}_{c}^{0}
decays, respectively.
The observed branching fractions are an order of magnitude smaller than those predicted by the QCD sum rule
Chernyak:1990ag
,
but are consistent with the expectations of the diquark model
Ball:1990fw
.
Interestingly, these branching fractions are larger than those of their singly-charmed counterparts,

B^{+}\to\overline{\Sigma}_{c}(2455)^{0}p

and
B^{0}\to\overline{\Sigma}_{c}(2455)^{-}p
,
by one to two orders of magnitude
ParticleDataGroup:2024cfk
, although the
corresponding combinations of CKM matrix elements in their amplitudes have nearly equal magnitudes.
