47 C.F.R. § 15.315 — Conducted limits.
Citation: us:cfr:47:15.315
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-15.315
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 15 (Radio Frequency Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An unlicensed PCS device that is designed to be connected to the public utility (AC) power line must meet the limits specified in § 15.207.
