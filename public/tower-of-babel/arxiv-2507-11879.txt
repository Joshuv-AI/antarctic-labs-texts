Asymptotic Critical Radii in Random Geometric Graphs over Higher-dimensional Regions
Jie Ding, Xiang Wei, Shuai Ma

Abstract
This article presents the precise asymptotical distribution of two types of critical transmission radii, defined in terms of k − k- connectivity and the minimum vertex degree, for a random geometry graph distributed over a unit-volume region Ω ⊂ ℝ d ​ ( d ≥ 3 ) \Omega\subset\mathbb{R}^{d}(d\geq 3) .

4.1

Notice the fact that uniform
n
-point process
\chi_{n}

\mathcal{P}_{n}
distributed over region
\Omega
with intensity

the number of the points in

\Pr\left\{\mathcal{P}_{n}(A)=j\right\}=\frac{e^{-n|A|}(n|A|)^{j}}{j!},\quad j\geq 0.

Given the condition that there are exactly

If

can be well approximated by
