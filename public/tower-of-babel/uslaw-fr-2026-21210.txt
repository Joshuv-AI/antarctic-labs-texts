    Title of Collection: High School and Beyond 2022 (HS&B:22) High 
School Transcript Study Data Collection.
    OMB Control Number: 1850-0944.
    Type of Review: A revision of a currently approved collection.
    Respondents/Affected Public: Private Sector; State, Local, and 
Tribal Governments; Individuals and Households.
    Total Estimated Number of Annual Responses: 13,403.
    Total Estimated Number of Annual Burden Hours: 2,311.
    Abstract: The High School and Beyond Longitudinal Study of 2022 
(HS&B:22) is the sixth in a series of longitudinal studies at the high 
school level conducted by the National Center for Education Statistics 
(NCES), within the Institute of Education Sciences (IES) of the U.S. 
Department of Education. HS&B:22 is following a nationally 
representative sample of ninth grade students from when they started 
high school in the 2022-23 school year. The base-year data collection 
was conducted in the 2022-23 school year with ninth grade students, 
with a high school transcript collection planned for 2027. The original 
study design included a first follow-up data collection when the modal 
sample would have been in 12th grade. A field test was completed, but 
not the full-scale data collection. This request is to obtain clearance 
to conduct the high school transcript collection for

[[Page 65737]]

HS&B:22 sample members, which includes contacting schools and a subset 
of students that may not have finished at their base-year high school.
    NCES encourages the public to review the materials associated with 
this submission on regulations.gov and submit comments. Part A of this 
submission presents information on the basic design of the HS&B:22 High 
School Transcript Study (HSTS) data collection. Part B discusses the 
statistical methods employed. Appendix A provides the communication 
materials that will be used for school district, school, student, and 
parent data collection activities.
