22 C.F.R. § 12.2 — Claimants denied access to employees.
Citation: us:cfr:22:12.2
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-12.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 12 (Complaints Against Employees By Alleged Creditors). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Persons claiming to be creditors or collectors of debts or claims will be denied access to employees for the purpose of presenting or collecting claims during the hours set apart for the transaction of public business or while the employees concerned are on duty.
