Multiple breaks of log-concavity in the independence polynomials of trees
César Bautista-Ramos

Abstract
. We construct infinite families of trees whose independence polynomials violate log-concavity at an arbitrary number of indices. This affirmatively answers a question of D. Galvin.

Primary 05C05, 05C31, 05A15, 30C15; Secondary 05C76, 11B83

1.
Introduction

The conjecture that independence polynomials of trees are unimodal
[
1
]
was later strengthened to the more tractable conjecture that they are log-concave
[
9
]
. Several authors have since disproven this
[
7
,
6
,
4
,
10
]
. At the time of this writing, all known counterexamples in the literature exhibited only a single break in log-concavity. This observation led Galvin
[
4
]
to ask whether multiple breaks are possible.

In this note, we answer that question affirmatively. We construct infinite families of trees whose independence polynomials break log-concavity at an arbitrary number of indices (Theorem
1
).

Definition 2

Definition 4

Let

The relation

\Theta

\asymp

Proposition 1
.

Let
m
be a fixed positive integer. Let
A,B,C,D
be four sequences of eventually nonnegative functions of the positive integer variable

S_{A}(x)\asymp S_{C}(x)\text{ and }S_{B}(x)\asymp S_{D}(x)\pmod{x^{m}}

then

S_{A}(x)+S_{B}(x)\asymp S_{C}(x)+S_{D}(x)\text{ and }S_{A}(x)S_{B}(x)\asymp S_{C}(x)S_{D}(x)\pmod{x^{m}}.

The proof of this proposition is elementary, so we omit it. This proposition is used frequently in the proof of the following lemma.

Lemma 2

Let

This analysis reveals how the log-concavity of
I(TG_{m,t})
is broken multiple times.

Theorem 1
.

For each positive integer
m
and for every sufficiently large integer
t
, the log-concavity of the independence polynomial of the tree
TG_{m,t}
is broken at
m
indices.

Proof.

Let
0\leq k\leq 2(m-1)
. According to Lemma
3
,
c_{k}(m,t)\in\Theta(2^{u_{k}t})
, where
u_{k}=k+\lfloor k/2\rfloor
. Let us assume that
k
is even and write
k=2j
. Then,
u_{k}=3j
,
u_{k+1}=3j+1
,
u_{k+2}=3(j+1)
, and

c_{k}(m,t)c_{k+2}(m,t)\in\Theta(2^{(6j+3)t}),\quad c_{k+1}^{2}(m,t)\in\Theta(2^{(6j+2)t}),

which implies

c_{k}(m,t)c_{k+2}(m,t)-c_{k+1}^{2}(m,t)\in\Theta(2^{(6j+3)t}).

∎
