7 U.S.C. § 1704a — Agreements for use of foreign currencies; reports to Congress
Citation: us:usc:7:1704a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1704a
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 41 (Food For Peace); Subchapter II (Economic Assistance And Food Security). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Within sixty days after any agreement is entered into for the use of any foreign currencies, a full report thereon shall be made to the Senate and the House of Representatives of the United States and to the Committees on Agriculture and Appropriations thereof.
