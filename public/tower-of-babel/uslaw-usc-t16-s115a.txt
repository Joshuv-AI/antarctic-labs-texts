16 U.S.C. § 115a — Mineral resources; exploitation
Citation: us:usc:16:115a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section115a
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter XII (Mesa Verde National Park). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

After January 26, 1931, no permit, license, lease, or other authorization for the prospecting, development, or utilization of the mineral resources within the Mesa Verde National Park, Colorado, shall be granted or made.
