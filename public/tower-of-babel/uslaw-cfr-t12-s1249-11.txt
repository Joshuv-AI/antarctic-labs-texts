12 C.F.R. § 1249.11 — Maintenance of Enterprise Securities.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1249.11
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1249.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1249 (Book-Entry Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An Enterprise Security may be maintained in the form of a Definitive Enterprise Security or a Book-entry Enterprise Security. A Book-entry Enterprise Security shall be maintained in the Book-entry System.
