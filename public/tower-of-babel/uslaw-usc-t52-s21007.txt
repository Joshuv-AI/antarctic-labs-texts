52 U.S.C. § 21007 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:52:21007
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 52 (Voting And Elections); Chapter 209 (Election Administration Improvement); Subchapter II (Commission); Part D (Election Assistance); Subpart 1 (Requirements Payments). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
In addition to amounts transferred under section 20904(c) of this title, there are authorized to be appropriated for requirements payments under this subpart the following amounts:
(1)
For fiscal year 2003, $1,400,000,000.
(2)
For fiscal year 2004, $1,000,000,000.
(3)
For fiscal year 2005, $600,000,000.
(4)
For fiscal year 2010 and subsequent fiscal years, such sums as are necessary for purposes of making requirements payments to States to carry out the activities described in section 21001(b)(3) of this title.
(b) Availability
Any amounts appropriated pursuant to the authority of subsection (a) shall remain available without fiscal year limitation until expended.
