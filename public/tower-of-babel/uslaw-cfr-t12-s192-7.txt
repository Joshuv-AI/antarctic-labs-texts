12 C.F.R. § 192.7 — Electronic filing.
Citation: us:cfr:12:192.7
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-192.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 192 (Conversions From Mutual To Stock Form). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For Federal savings associations, the OCC encourages the electronic filing of all applications, notices, or other documents required by this part through http://www.banknet.gov/. The Comptroller's Licensing Manual describes the OCC's electronic filing procedures.
