41 C.F.R. § 101-5.104-5 — -5.104-5 Designating agency representatives.
Citation: us:cfr:41:101-5.104-5
Creator: Chapter 101—Federal Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-101-5.104-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 101-5 (5—Centralized Services In Federal Buildings And Complexes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The head of each agency receiving a GSA notice regarding a scheduled feasibility study will be requested to designate one or more officials at the location where the study will be made who may consult with authorized GSA representatives. Such information and assistance as is required or pertinent for an adequate review of the feasibility of the proposed centralized service shall be made available to GSA through the designated agency representatives.
