48 C.F.R. § 204.7604 — 204.7604 Solicitation provision.
Citation: us:cfr:48:204.7604
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-204.7604
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 204 (Administrative And Information Matters). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except for supplies or services exempted by DoDI 5000.79, use the provision at 252.204-7024, Notice on the Use of the Supplier Performance Risk System, in solicitations for supplies and services, including solicitations using FAR part 12 procedures for the acquisition of commercial products and commercial services.
