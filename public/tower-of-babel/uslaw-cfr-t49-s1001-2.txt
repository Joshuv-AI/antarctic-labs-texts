49 C.F.R. § 1001.2 — Certified copies of records.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1001.2
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1001.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1001 (Inspection Of Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Copies of and extracts from public records will be certified by the Records Officer. Persons requesting the Board to prepare such copies should clearly state the material to be copied, and whether it shall be certified. Charges will be made for certification and for the preparation of copies as provided in part 1002 of this chapter.
