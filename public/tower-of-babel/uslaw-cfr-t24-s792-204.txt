24 C.F.R. § 792.204 — Recordkeeping and reporting.
Citation: us:cfr:24:792.204
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Housing And Urban Development (Housing Assistance Programs And Public And Indian Housing Programs)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-792.204
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 792 (Public Housing Agency Section 8 Fraud Recoveries). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To permit HUD to audit amounts retained under this part, an PHA must maintain all records required by HUD, including:
(a) Amounts recovered on any judgment or repayment agreement;
(b) The nature of the judgment or repayment agreement; and
(c) The amount of the legal fees and expenses incurred in obtaining the judgment or repayment agreement and recovery.
