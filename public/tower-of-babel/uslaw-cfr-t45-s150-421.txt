45 C.F.R. § 150.421 — Appearance of counsel.
Citation: us:cfr:45:150.421
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-150.421
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 150 (Cms Enforcement In Group And Individual Insurance Markets). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any attorney who is to appear on behalf of a party must promptly file, with the ALJ, a notice of appearance.
