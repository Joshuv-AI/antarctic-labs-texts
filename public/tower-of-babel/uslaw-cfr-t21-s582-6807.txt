21 C.F.R. § 582.6807 — Sodium thiosulfate.
Citation: us:cfr:21:582.6807
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-582.6807
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 582 (Substances Generally Recognized As Safe). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Product. Sodium thiosulfate.
(b) Tolerance. 0.1 percent.
(c) Limitations, restrictions, or explanation. This substance is generally recognized as safe when used in salt in accordance with good manufacturing or feeding practice.
