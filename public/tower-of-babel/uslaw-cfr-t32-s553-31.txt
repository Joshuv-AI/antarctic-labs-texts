32 C.F.R. § 553.31 — Memorial and commemorative monuments (other than private headstones or markers).
Citation: us:cfr:32:553.31
Creator: Department Of The Army
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-553.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 553 (Army Cemeteries). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The placement of memorials or commemorative monuments in Arlington National Cemetery will be carried out in accordance with 38 U.S.C. 2409(b).
