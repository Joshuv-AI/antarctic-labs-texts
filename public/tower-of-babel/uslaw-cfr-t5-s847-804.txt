5 C.F.R. § 847.804 — How do employees make an election to use their NAFI service to qualify for an immediate retirement under CSRS or FERS?
Citation: us:cfr:5:847.804
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-847.804
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 847 (Elections Of Retirement Coverage By Current And Former Employees Of Nonappropriated Fund Instrumentalities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees electing to credit NAFI service under this subpart must:
(a) Inform the NAFI retirement plan that they are electing to credit NAFI service for CSRS or FERS retirement eligibility;
(b) Document the election on a form prescribed by OPM; and
(c) Submit the election with their application for immediate retirement.
