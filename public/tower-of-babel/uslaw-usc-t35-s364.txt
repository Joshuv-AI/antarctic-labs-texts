35 U.S.C. § 364 — International stage: Procedure
Citation: us:usc:35:364
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title35-section364
Context: United States Code, Title 35 (Patents); Part IV (Patent Cooperation Treaty); Chapter 36 (International Stage). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
International applications shall be processed by the Patent and Trademark Office when acting as a Receiving Office, International Searching Authority, or International Preliminary Examining Authority, in accordance with the applicable provisions of the treaty, the Regulations, and this title.
(b)
An applicant’s failure to act within prescribed time limits in connection with requirements pertaining to an international application may be excused as provided in the treaty and the Regulations.
