42 U.S.C. § 3194 — Administration, operation, and maintenance
Citation: us:usc:42:3194
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section3194
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 38 (Public Works And Economic Development); Subchapter V (Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall approve Federal assistance under this chapter only if the Secretary is satisfied that the project for which Federal assistance is granted will be properly and efficiently administered, operated, and maintained.
