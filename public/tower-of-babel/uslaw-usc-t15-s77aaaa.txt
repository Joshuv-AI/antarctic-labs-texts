15 U.S.C. § 77aaaa — Contrary stipulations void
Citation: us:usc:15:77aaaa
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section77aaaa
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 2A (Securities And Trust Indentures); Subchapter III (Trust Indentures). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any condition, stipulation, or provision binding any person to waive compliance with any provision of this subchapter or with any rule, regulation, or order thereunder shall be void.
