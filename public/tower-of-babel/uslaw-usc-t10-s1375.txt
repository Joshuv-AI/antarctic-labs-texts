10 U.S.C. § 1375 — Entitlement to commission: commissioned officers advanced on retired list
Citation: us:usc:10:1375
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1375
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 69 (Retired Grade). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A commissioned officer of the Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, or Space Force who is advanced on a retired list is entitled to a commission in the grade to which he is advanced.
