20 C.F.R. § 10.119 — What action will OWCP take with respect to information submitted by the employer?
Citation: us:cfr:20:10.119
Creator: Office Of Workers' Compensation Programs, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-10.119
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 10 (Claims For Compensation Under The Federal Employees' Compensation Act, As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

OWCP will consider all evidence submitted appropriately, and OWCP will inform the employee, the employee's representative, if any, and the employer of any action taken. Where an employer contests a claim within 30 days of the initial submittal and the claim is later approved, OWCP will notify the employer of the rationale for approving the claim.
