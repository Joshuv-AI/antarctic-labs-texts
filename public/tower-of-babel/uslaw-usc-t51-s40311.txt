51 U.S.C. § 40311 — Continuing emphasis
Citation: us:usc:51:40311
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section40311
Context: United States Code, Title 51 (National And Commercial Space Programs); Chapter 403 (National Space Grant College And Fellowship Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administration shall continue its emphasis on the importance of education to expand opportunities for Americans to understand and participate in the Administration’s aeronautics and space projects by supporting and enhancing science and engineering education, research, and public outreach efforts.
