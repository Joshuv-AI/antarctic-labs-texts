10 C.F.R. § 218.30 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:10:218.30
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-218.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 218 (Standby Mandatory International Oil Allocation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart establishes the administrative procedures applicable to supply orders. They shall be exclusive of any other procedures contained in this chapter, unless such other procedures are specifically made applicable hereto by this subpart.
