47 U.S.C. § 386 — Forfeitures

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 47 (Telecommunications); Chapter 5 (Wire Or Radio Communication); Subchapter III (Special Provisions Relating To Radio); Part III (Radio Installations On Vessels Carrying Passengers For Hire). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The following forfeitures shall apply to this part in addition to penalties and forfeitures provided by subchapter V of this chapter:
(a)
Any vessel of the United States that is navigated in violation of the provisions of this part or of the rules and regulations of the Commission made in pursuance thereof shall forfeit to the United States the sum of $5,000 recoverable by way of suit or libel. Each day during which such navigation occurs shall constitute a separate offense.
(b)
Every willful failure on the part of the master of a vessel of the United States to enforce or to comply with the provisions of this part or the rules and regulations of the Commission made in pursuance thereof shall cause him to forfeit to the United States the sum of $1,000.
