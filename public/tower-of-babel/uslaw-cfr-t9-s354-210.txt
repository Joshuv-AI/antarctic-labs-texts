9 C.F.R. § 354.210 — Minimum standards for sanitation, facilities, and operating procedures in official plants.
Citation: us:cfr:9:354.210
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-354.210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 354 (Voluntary Inspection Of Rabbits And Edible Products Thereof). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of §§ 354.210 to 354.247 shall apply with respect to inspection service in all official plants. The table set forth in § 354.247 indicates some of the types of material which may be used in the construction of equipment, utensils, and facilities for use in the plant.
