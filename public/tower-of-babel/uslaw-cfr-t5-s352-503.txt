5 C.F.R. § 352.503 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:5:352.503
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-352.503
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 352 (Reemployment Rights). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In this subpart:
(a) Act means the Foreign Assistance Act of 1961, as amended (22 U.S.C. 2151 et seq.); and
(b) Former position means the position that an employee was occupying at the time of his appointment to a position under authority of section 233(d) or section 625(b) of the Act.
