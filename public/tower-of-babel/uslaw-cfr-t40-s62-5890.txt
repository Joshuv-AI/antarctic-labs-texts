40 C.F.R. § 62.5890 — Identification of plan—negative declaration.
Citation: us:cfr:40:62.5890
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-62.5890
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 62 (Approval And Promulgation Of State Plans For Designated Facilities And Pollutants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

On June 21, 2017, the Minnesota Pollution Control Agency submitted a negative declaration letter to EPA certifying that there are no existing Other Solid Waste Incineration (OSWI) units in the State of Minnesota subject to the emissions guidelines at 40 CFR part 60, subpart FFFF.
