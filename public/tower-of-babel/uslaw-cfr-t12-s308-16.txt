12 C.F.R. § 308.16 — FDIC's right to conduct examination.
Citation: us:cfr:12:308.16
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-308.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 308 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing contained in this subpart limits in any manner the right of the FDIC to conduct any examination, inspection, or visitation of any institution or institution-affiliated party, or the right of the FDIC to conduct or continue any form of investigation authorized by law.
