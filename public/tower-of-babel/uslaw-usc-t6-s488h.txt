6 U.S.C. § 488h — Deadlines for regulations
Citation: us:usc:6:488h
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title6-section488h
Context: United States Code, Title 6 (Domestic Security); Chapter 1 (Homeland Security Organization); Subchapter VIII (Coordination With Non-Federal Entities; Inspector General; United States Secret Service; Coast Guard; General Provisions); Part J (Secure Handling Of Ammonium Nitrate). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary—
(1)
shall issue a proposed rule implementing this part not later than 6 months after December 26, 2007; and
(2)
issue a final rule implementing this part not later than 1 year after December 26, 2007.
