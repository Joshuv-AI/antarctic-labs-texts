7 C.F.R. § 4290.40 — How to read this part 4290.
Citation: us:cfr:7:4290.40
Creator: Rural Business-Cooperative Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-4290.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 4290 (Rural Business Investment Company (“Rbic”) Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Center Headings. Center headings are descriptive and are used for convenience only. They have no regulatory effect.
(b) Capitalizing defined terms. Terms defined in § 4290.50 have initial capitalization in this part 4290.
(c) “You.” The pronoun “you” as used in this part 4290 means a RBIC unless otherwise noted.
(d) Forms. All references in this part to forms, and instructions for their preparation, are to the current issue of such forms.
