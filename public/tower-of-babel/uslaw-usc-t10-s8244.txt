10 U.S.C. § 8244 — Enlisted members assigned to mess: basic allowance for subsistence paid to mess
Citation: us:usc:10:8244
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8244
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 827 (Rations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Under such regulations as the Secretary of the Navy prescribes, the basic allowance for subsistence of enlisted members of the naval service assigned to duty with and subsisting in an officers’ or other mess, afloat or ashore, may be paid to the mess to which they are assigned.
