48 C.F.R. § 2803.908-71 — 2803.908-71 Whistleblower Protection in Intelligence-Related Non-Disclosure Agreement.
Citation: us:cfr:48:2803.908-71
Creator: Chapter 28—Department Of Justice
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2803 (Improper Business Practices And Personal Conflicts Of Interest). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer shall ensure that any Intelligence-Related NDA that DOJ requires a Covered Individual to sign contains the required Whistleblower Protection Provision at JAR 2852.203-71.
