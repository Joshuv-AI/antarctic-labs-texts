18 U.S.C. § 3334 — General provisions
Citation: us:usc:18:3334
Creator: United States Congress

Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section3334
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part II (Criminal Procedure); Chapter 216 (Special Grand Jury). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of chapter 215, title 18, United States Code, and the Federal Rules of Criminal Procedure applicable to regular grand juries shall apply to special grand juries to the extent not inconsistent with sections 3331, 3332, or 3333 of this chapter.
