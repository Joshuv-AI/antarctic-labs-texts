40 C.F.R. § 142.64 — Variances and exemptions from the requirements of part 141, subpart H—Filtration and Disinfection.
Citation: us:cfr:40:142.64
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-142.64
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 142 (National Primary Drinking Water Regulations Implementation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) No variances from the requirements in part 141, subpart H are permitted.
(b) No exemptions from the requirements in § 141.72 (a)(3) and (b)(2) to provide disinfection are permitted.
