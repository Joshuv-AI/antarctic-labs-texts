Non-Uniform Dependence on Initial Data of Solutions to the logarithmically regularized 2D Euler equations
Xuan-Truong Vu

Abstract
. In this paper, we study the logarithmically regularized 2D Euler equations, which are derived by regularizing the Euler equation for the vorticity have been studied recently to get better understanding of the Euler equations. It was shown that the local well-posedness in the critical Sobolev space H 1 ​ ( ℝ 2 ) ∩ H − 1 ​ ( ℝ 2 ) H^{1}(\mathbb{R}^{2})\cap H^{-1}(\mathbb{R}^{2}) can be derived for γ > 1 2 \gamma>\dfrac{1}{2} , and the strong ill-posedness in this borderline space was yielded by Kwon for 0 ≤ γ ≤ 1 2 0\leq\gamma\leq\frac{1}{2} . In this paper, we show that the local well-posedness of the logarithmically regularized 2D Euler equations can be achieved in the subcritical space H s ​ ( 𝕋 2 ) H^{s}(\mathbb{T}^{2}) with s > 2 s>2 for γ ≥ 0 \gamma\geq 0 . Moreover, we prove that the data-to-solution map is not uniformly continuous in the Sobolev H s ​ ( 𝕋 2 ) H^{s}(\mathbb{T}^{2}) topology for any s > 2 s>2 .

We begin with an estimate of the analogue of the Biot-Savart operator for the equations in (

Lemma 2.1

Let

(2.1)

\|u_{T}\|_{{{H}}^{s}}\leq C\|\theta_{T}\|_{\dot{H}^{s-1}}

Proof.

We have

\displaystyle\|u_{T}\|_{\dot{H}^{s}}^{2}

\displaystyle=\|\nabla^{\perp}\Delta^{-1}T\,\theta_{T}\|_{\dot{H}^{s}}^{2}

\displaystyle=\int\limits_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}|\xi|^{2s}|\mathcal{F}(\nabla^{\perp}\Delta^{-1}T\,\theta_{T})(\xi)|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle=\int\limits_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}|\xi|^{2(s-1)}\left|\frac{(-\xi_{2},\xi_{1})}{|\xi|}T(|\xi|)\right|^{2}|\widehat{\theta}_{T}(\xi)|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq C\|\theta_{T}\|_{\dot{H}^{s-1}}^{2},

where in the second to last step we used Plancherel’s identity. With the same argument we get

\displaystyle\|u_{T}\|_{L^{2}}^{2}

\displaystyle\lesssim\|\theta_{T}\|_{\dot{H}^{-1}}^{2}\lesssim\|\theta_{T}\|_{\dot{H}^{s-1}}^{2}\lesssim\|\theta_{T}\|_{H^{s}}^{2}

Putting these estimates together, we get the desired estimate (
