7 C.F.R. § 945.89 — Personal liability.
Citation: us:cfr:7:945.89
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-945.89
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 945 (Irish Potatoes Grown In Certain Designated Counties In Idaho, And Malheur County, Oregon). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No member or alternate of the committee, nor any employee or agent thereof, shall be held personally responsible, either individually or jointly with others, in any way whatsoever, to any handler or to any person for errors in judgment, mistakes, or other acts, either of commission or omission, as such member, alternate, or employee, except for acts of dishonesty.
