25 C.F.R. § 11.447 — Maintaining a public nuisance.
Citation: us:cfr:25:11.447
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-11.447
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 11 (Courts Of Indian Offenses And Law And Order Code). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A person who permits his or her property to fall into such condition as to injure or endanger the safety, health, comfort, or property of his or her neighbors, is guilty of a violation.
