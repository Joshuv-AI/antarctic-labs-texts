24 C.F.R. § 965.503 — Categories for establishment of allowances.
Citation: us:cfr:24:965.503
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-965.503
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 965 (Pha-Owned Or Leased Projects—General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Separate allowances shall be established for each utility and for each category of dwelling units determined by the PHA to be reasonably comparable as to factors affecting utility usage.
