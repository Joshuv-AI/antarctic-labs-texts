36 C.F.R. § 1256.40 — What are general restrictions?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1256.40
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1256.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1256 (Access To Records And Donated Historical Materials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

General restrictions apply to certain kinds of information or classes of records, regardless of the record group to which the records have been allocated. These general restrictions may apply to records and materials not covered by the Freedom of Information Act. The general restrictions are listed and explained in §§ 1256.46 through 1256.62.
