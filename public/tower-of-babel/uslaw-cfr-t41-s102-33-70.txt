41 C.F.R. § 102-33.70 — -33.70 Life-limited parts requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-33.70
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-33.70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-33 (33—Management Of Government Aircraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When acquiring life-limited parts, you must:
(a) Identify and inspect the parts, ensuring that they have civil or military-certified documentation; and
(b) Mutilate and dispose of any expired life-limited parts.
