16 C.F.R. § 1020.2 — What is the definition of “small business”?
Citation: us:cfr:16:1020.2
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1020.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1020 (Small Business). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As used in this part, the term small business means any entity that is either a small business, small organization, or small governmental jurisdiction, as those terms are defined at 5 U.S.C. 601(3), (4), and (5), respectively.
