33 C.F.R. § 187.318 — Effect of missing or incorrect information.
Citation: us:cfr:33:187.318
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-187.318
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 187 (Vessel Identification System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise provided as prescribed by State law related to security interests in goods, a certificate of title or other record required or authorized by this subpart is effective even if it contains incorrect information or does not contain required information.
