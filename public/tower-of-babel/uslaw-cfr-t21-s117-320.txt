21 C.F.R. § 117.320 — Requirements for official review.
Citation: us:cfr:21:117.320
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-117.320
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 117 (Current Good Manufacturing Practice, Hazard Analysis, And Risk-Based Preventive Controls For Human Food). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All records required by this part must be made promptly available to a duly authorized representative of the Secretary of Health and Human Services for official review and copying upon oral or written request.
