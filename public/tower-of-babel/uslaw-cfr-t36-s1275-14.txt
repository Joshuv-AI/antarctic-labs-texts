36 C.F.R. § 1275.14 — Legal custody.
Citation: us:cfr:36:1275.14
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1275.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1275 (Preservation And Protection Of And Access To The Presidential Historical Materials Of The Nixon Administration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Archivist of the United States has or will obtain exclusive legal custody and control of all Presidential historical materials of the Nixon Administration held pursuant to the provisions of title I of the Presidential Recordings and Materials Preservation Act.
