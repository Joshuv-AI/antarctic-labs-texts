4 C.F.R. § 11.3 — Authority to represent in payment cases.
Citation: us:cfr:4:11.3
Creator: Government Accountability Office
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-4/section-11.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 4 (Accounts), Part 11 (Recognition Of Attorneys And Other Representatives). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In the prosecution of claims involving payments to be made by the United States, a proper power of attorney is required before an attorney or other representative may be recognized. A power of attorney from the principal may also be requested in other cases.
