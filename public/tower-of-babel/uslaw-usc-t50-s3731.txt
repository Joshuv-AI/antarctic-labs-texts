50 U.S.C. § 3731 — Prohibition on use of funds for specified purposes

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section3731
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 48 (Department Of Defense Cooperative Threat Reduction); Subchapter II (Restrictions And Limitations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
Cooperative Threat Reduction funds may not be obligated or expended for any of the following purposes:
(1)
Conducting any peacekeeping exercise or other peacekeeping-related activity.
(2)
Provision of housing.
(3)
Provision of assistance to promote environmental restoration.
(4)
Provision of assistance to promote job retraining.
(5)
Provision of assistance to promote defense conversion.
(b) Limitation with respect to conventional weapons
Cooperative Threat Reduction funds may not be obligated or expended for the elimination of—
(1)
conventional weapons; or
(2)
delivery vehicles of conventional weapons, unless such delivery vehicles could reasonably be used or adapted to be used for the delivery of chemical, nuclear, or biological weapons.
