34 C.F.R. § 104.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:34:104.1
Creator: Office For Civil Rights, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-104.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 104 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Handicap In Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to effectuate section 504 of the Rehabilitation Act of 1973, which is designed to eliminate discrimination on the basis of handicap in any program or activity receiving Federal financial assistance.
