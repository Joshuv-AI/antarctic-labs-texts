12 C.F.R. § 313.23 — Offset prior to completion of procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:12:313.23
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-313.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 313 (Procedures For Collection Of Corporate Debt, Criminal Restitution Debt, And Civil Money Penalty Debt). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The FDIC may collect a debt by administrative offset prior to the completion of the procedures described in § 313.25, if:
(a) Failure to offset a payment would substantially prejudice the FDIC's ability to collect the debt; and
(b) The time before the payment is to be made does not reasonably permit completion of the procedures described in § 313.25. Such prior offsetting shall be followed promptly by the completion of the procedures described in § 313.25.
