25 C.F.R. § 1000.2005 — Is the Secretary required to report on Self Governance?
Citation: us:cfr:25:1000.2005
Creator: Office Of The Assistant Secretary, Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-1000.2005
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 1000 (Annual Funding Agreements Under The Tribal Self-Government Act Amendments To The Indian Self-Determination And Education Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, on January 1 of each year, the Secretary will submit a report on self-governance to the Congress. The report will be based on:
(a) Information contained in funding agreements;
(b) Annual audit reports, and
(c) Data of the Secretary regarding the disposition of Federal funds.
