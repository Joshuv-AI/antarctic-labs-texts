22 C.F.R. § 1509.210 — To whom must I distribute my drug-free workplace statement?
Citation: us:cfr:22:1509.210
Creator: African Development Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-1509.210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 1509 (Governmentwide Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Financial Assistance)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must require that a copy of the statement described in § 1509.205 be given to each employee who will be engaged in the performance of any Federal award.
