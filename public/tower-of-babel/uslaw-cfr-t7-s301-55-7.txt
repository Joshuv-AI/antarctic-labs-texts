7 C.F.R. § 301.55-7 — -7 Assembly and inspection of regulated articles.
Citation: us:cfr:7:301.55-7
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-301.55-7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 301 (Domestic Quarantine Notices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Any person (other than a person authorized to issue limited permits under § 301.555(c)) who desires a certificate or limited permit to move a regulated article interstate must request an inspector 1 to examine the articles as far in advance of the desired interstate movement as possible, but no less than 48 hours before the desired interstate movement.
(b) The regulated article must be assembled at the place and in the manner the inspector designates as necessary to comply with this subpart.
