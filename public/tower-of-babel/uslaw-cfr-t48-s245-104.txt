48 C.F.R. § 245.104 — 245.104 Responsibility and liability for Government property.
Citation: us:cfr:48:245.104
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-245.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 245 (Government Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to the contract types listed at FAR 45.104, contractors are not held liable for loss of Government property under negotiated fixed-price contracts awarded on a basis other than submission of certified cost or pricing data.
