High-pressure stabilization of Mg 2 ​ IrH 7 \text{Mg}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\text{IrH}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{7}}} : Structural proximity to high- T c superconductivity
Shubham Sinha, Wencheng Lu, Mads F. Hansen, Michael J. Hutcheon, Trevor W. Bontke, Lewis J. Conway, Kapildeb Dolui, Chris J. Pickard, Christoph Heil, Piotr A. Guńka, Stella Chariton, Vitali Prakapenka, Liangzi Deng, Ching-Wu Chu, Matthew N. Julian, Rohit P. Prasankumar, Timothy A. Strobel

Abstract
Mg 2 ​ IrH 6 \text{Mg}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\text{IrH}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{6}}} is a metastable complex metal hydride with a predicted superconducting transition temperature as high as 170 K 170\text{\,}\mathrm{K} at ambient pressure. Following the synthesis of isomorphic, insulating Mg 2 ​ IrH 5 \text{Mg}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\text{IrH}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{5}}} at low pressure, higher-pressure studies were conducted to investigate the phase behavior and compound formation in this system. X-ray diffraction and Raman spectroscopic measurements indicate that cubic Mg 2 ​ IrH 7 \text{Mg}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\text{IrH}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{7}}} is stabilized above ca. 40 GPa and coexists with a related hexagonal hydride with likely composition near Mg 2 ​ IrH 5 \text{Mg}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\text{IrH}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{5}}} . Electrical transport measurements show that cubic Mg 2 ​ IrH 7 \text{Mg}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\text{IrH}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{7}}} is insulating, in agreement with ab initio predictions, and persists during room-temperature decompression until ∼ \sim 20 GPa 20\text{\,}\mathrm{G}\mathrm{P}\mathrm{a} before reverting back to cubic Mg 2 ​ IrH 5 \text{Mg}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\text{IrH}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{5}}} . The experimental results confirm ground-state structure predictions in the Mg–Ir–H system, and the formation of two nearly identical phases with surrounding compositions opens new opportunities to access superconducting Mg 2 ​ IrH 6 \text{Mg}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\text{IrH}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{6}}} through non-equilibrium processing pathways.

Finally, the formation of FCC
\text{Mg}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\text{IrH}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{7}}}
allows us to begin the exploration of synthetic pathways to superconducting
\text{Mg}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\text{IrH}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{6}}}
via hydrogen removal, as initially proposed by Dolui et al.
9

\text{Mg}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\text{IrH}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{6}}}
, diffusion of the non-covalent hydrogen from
\text{Mg}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\text{IrH}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{7}}}
represents a potential route to obtain this superconducting metastable phase. This possibility might be realized via the decompression of
\text{Mg}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\text{IrH}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{7}}}
given the local, low-energy minimum of
\text{Mg}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\text{IrH}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{6}}}
before reversion to
\text{Mg}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\text{IrH}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{5}}}
, and would be manifested by a change to metallic conductivity/superconductivity at some intermediate pressure.

sample equipped with electrical contacts was heated in ammonia borane near

and the presence of an expanded FCC lattice was verified by XRD, in addition to the presence of the strong
\sim

show insulating behavior with resistance reaching the M
\Omega

)—the expected behavior for FCC
\text{Mg}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\text{IrH}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{7}}}
, which is predicted to be a charge-balanced insulator (i.e., 2Mg

When temperature-dependent transport measurements were conducted on
\text{Mg}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\text{IrH}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{7}}}
at pressure steps during decompression, the sample remained insulating with an offset in the resistance magnitude across the
\sim

\text{Mg}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\text{IrH}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{5}}}

\text{Mg}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\text{IrH}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{6}}}

transition mechanism could produce valuable insights for the transient stabilization of superconducting
\text{Mg}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\text{IrH}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{6}}}

While metastable
\text{Mg}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\text{IrH}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{6}}}
remains elusive to date, the demonstration of two nearly identical structures is promising for its future synthesis. FCC
\text{Mg}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\text{IrH}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{5}}}
, and now,
\text{Mg}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\text{IrH}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{7}}}
are available as starting precursors with isostructural metal substructure arrangements, differing only by a single hydrogen atom per formula unit. Non-equilibrium processing methods such as implantation/irradiation, deposition, electro/mechanochemistry, or dynamic P/T cycling might serve as valuable routes to be explored to isolate this exceptional, dynamically stable phase.

IV
Conclusion

Following prior studies on FCC
\text{Mg}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\text{IrH}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{5}}}
,
Fm\bar{3}m

\text{Mg}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\text{IrH}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{7}}}
was found to form at higher pressures near 40 GPa. The Raman spectrum of
\text{Mg}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\text{IrH}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{7}}}
is characterized by an intense Ir–H stretching band near 1520 cm
-1
, diagnostic of [IrH
6
]
3-
complexes, clearly distinguishing this phase from
\text{Mg}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\text{IrH}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{5}}}
. Compared with FCC
\text{Mg}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\text{IrH}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{5}}}
,
\text{Mg}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\text{IrH}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{7}}}
has an expanded lattice and persists to ca. 20 GPa when decompressed at room temperature. In addition to FCC
\text{Mg}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\text{IrH}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{7}}}
, another hexagonal Mg
2
IrH
x
hydride was observed upon heating samples near 40 GPa. This structure crystallizes in the probable space group
P6_{3}mc
with the likely composition
\text{Mg}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\text{IrH}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{5}}}
based on formula-unit volume, and likely represents a hexagonal modification of disordered FCC
\text{Mg}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\text{IrH}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{5}}}
with a doubling of the
c
-axis. The thermodynamic stability, spectroscopic behavior and structural observations in this work are all strongly supported by
ab intio
calculations, providing further confidence in experimental interpretations and in using these tools to help guide materials discovery. While superconducting
\text{Mg}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\text{IrH}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{6}}}
was not observed as a spontaneous intermediate phase during the decompression of
\text{Mg}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\text{IrH}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{7}}}
, the successful formation of
\text{Mg}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{2}}}\text{IrH}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{7}}}
enables access to a structurally related compound that may serve as a useful precursor for future non-equilibrium synthesis approaches.
