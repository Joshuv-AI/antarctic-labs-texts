41 U.S.C. § 1701 — Cooperation with the Administrator
Citation: us:usc:41:1701
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title41-section1701
Context: United States Code, Title 41 (Public Contracts); Chapter 17 (Agency Responsibilities And Procedures). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

On the request of the Administrator, each executive agency shall—
(1)
make its services, personnel, and facilities available to the Office of Federal Procurement Policy to the greatest practicable extent for the performance of functions under this division; and
(2)
except when prohibited by law, furnish to the Administrator, and give the Administrator access to, all information and records in its possession that the Administrator may determine to be necessary for the performance of the functions of the Office.
