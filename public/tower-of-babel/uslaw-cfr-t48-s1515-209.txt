48 C.F.R. § 1515.209 — 1515.209 Solicitation provisions and contract clauses.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1515 (Contracting By Negotiation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 1552.215-75, Past Performance Information, or a clause substantially the same as 1552.215-75, in all competitively negotiated acquisitions with an estimated value in excess of the simplified acquisition threshold.
