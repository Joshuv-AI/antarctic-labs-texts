10 C.F.R. § 1014.5 — Authority to adjust, determine, compromise, and settle.
Citation: us:cfr:10:1014.5
Creator: Department Of Energy (General Provisions)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1014.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1014 (Administrative Claims Under Federal Tort Claims Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The General Counsel, the Deputy General Counsel, the Deputy General Counsel for Legal Services, the Assistant General Counsel for Legal Counsel, and any employees of the Department designated by the General Counsel to receive and act on tort claims at Headquarters and field locations are authorized to act on claims.
