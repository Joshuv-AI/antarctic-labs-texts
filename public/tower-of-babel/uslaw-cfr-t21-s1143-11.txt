21 C.F.R. § 1143.11 — Does not apply to foreign distribution.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1143.11
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1143.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1143 (Minimum Required Warning Statements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this part do not apply to a manufacturer or distributor of tobacco products that does not manufacture, package, or import tobacco products for sale or distribution within the United States.
