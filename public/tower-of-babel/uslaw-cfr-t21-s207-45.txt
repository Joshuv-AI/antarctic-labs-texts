21 C.F.R. § 207.45 — When, after initial registration of an establishment, must drug listing information be submitted?
Citation: us:cfr:21:207.45
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-207.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 207 (Requirements For Foreign And Domestic Establishment Registration And Listing For Human Drugs, Including Drugs That Are Regulated Under A Biologics License Application, And Animal Drugs, And The National Drug Code). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For each drug being manufactured, repacked, relabeled, or salvaged for commercial distribution at an establishment at the time of initial registration, drug listing information must be submitted no later than 3 calendar days after the initial registration of the establishment.
