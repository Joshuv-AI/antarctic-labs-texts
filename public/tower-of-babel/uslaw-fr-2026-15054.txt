Marine Mammals; File No. 29716

AGENCY: National Marine Fisheries Service (NMFS), National Oceanic and 
Atmospheric Administration (NOAA), Commerce.

ACTION: Notice; receipt of application.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Notice is hereby given that ABR, Inc. Environmental Research 
and Services, P.O. Box 80410, Fairbanks, AK 99708 (Responsible Party: 
Adrian Gall, Ph.D.), has applied in due form for a permit to conduct 
research on 4 pinniped species and 10 cetacean species.

DATES: Written comments must be received on or before August 26, 2026.

ADDRESSES: The application and related documents are available for 
review upon written request via email to [email protected].
    Written comments on this application should be submitted via email 
to [email protected]. Please include File No. 29716 in the 
subject line of the email comment.
    Those individuals requesting a public hearing should submit a 
written request via email to [email protected]. The request 
should set forth the specific reasons why a hearing on this application 
would be appropriate.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Amy Hapeman or Sara Young, (301) 427-
8401.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The subject permit is requested under the 
authority of the Marine Mammal Protection Act of 1972, as amended 
(MMPA; 16 U.S.C. 1361 et seq.), the regulations governing the taking 
and importing of marine mammals (50 CFR part 216), the Endangered 
Species Act of 1973, as amended (ESA; 16 U.S.C. 1531 et seq.), the 
regulations governing the taking, importing, and exporting of 
endangered and threatened species (50 CFR parts 222-226), and the Fur 
Seal Act of 1966, as amended (16 U.S.C. 1151 et seq.).
    The applicant proposes to evaluate the seasonal and annual 
distribution and abundance of marine mammals in the vicinity of a 
proposed barge site in the Tuxedni Bay and Tuxedni Channel, western 
Cook Inlet, Alaska. Target species include: endangered beluga 
(Delphinapterus leucas, Cook Inlet distinct population segment [DPS]); 
endangered fin (Balaenoptera physalus); endangered North Pacific right 
(Eubalaena japonica); gray (Eschrichtius robustus, including the 
endangered Western North Pacific stock); humpback (Megaptera 
novaeangliae, Hawaii, endangered Western North Pacific, and threatened 
Mexico DPSs), killer (Orcinus orca), and minke (B. acutorostrata) 
whales; Pacific white-sided dolphins (Lagenorhynchus obliquidens); 
harbor (Phocoena phocoena) and Dall's (Phocoenoides dalli) porpoise; 
harbor (Phoca vitulina) and Northern fur (Callorhinus ursinus) seals; 
and California (Zalophus californianus) and Steller (Eumetopias 
jubatus, endangered Western DPS) sea lions. Researchers would conduct 
vessel and aerial surveys (crewed and uncrewed) of marine mammals of 
all life stages for counts, photography/videography, and observations. 
See the application for numbers of animals requested by species. The 
permit is requested for 5 years and 3 months.
    In compliance with the National Environmental Policy Act of 1969 
(42 U.S.C. 4321 et seq.), an initial determination has been made that 
the activity proposed is categorically excluded from the requirement to 
prepare an environmental assessment or environmental impact statement.
    Concurrent with the publication of this notice in the Federal 
Register, NMFS is forwarding copies of the application to the Marine 
Mammal Commission and its Committee of Scientific Advisors.
