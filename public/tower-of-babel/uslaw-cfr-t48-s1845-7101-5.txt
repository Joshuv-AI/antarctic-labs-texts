48 C.F.R. § 1845.7101-5 — 1845.7101-5 Contractor's privileged financial and business information.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1845.7101-5
Creator: Chapter 18—National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1845.7101-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1845 (Government Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a transfer of property between contractors involves disclosing costs of a proprietary nature, the contractor shall furnish unit acquisition costs only on copies of shipping documents sent to the shipping and receiving NASA Centers.
