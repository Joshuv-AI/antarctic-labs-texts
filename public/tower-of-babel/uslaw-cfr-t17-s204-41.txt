17 C.F.R. § 204.41 — Non-waiver of rights.
Citation: us:cfr:17:204.41
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-204.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 204 (Rules Relating To Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee's involuntary payment of all or any portion of a debt being collected under 5 U.S.C. 5514 shall not be construed as a waiver of any rights that the employee may have under 5 U.S.C. 5514 or any other provision of contract or law, unless there are statutory or contractual provisions to the contrary.
