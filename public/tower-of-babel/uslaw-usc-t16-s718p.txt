16 U.S.C. § 718p — Authority to issue electronic duck stamps
Citation: us:usc:16:718p
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section718p
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 7 (Protection Of Migratory Game And Insectivorous Birds); Subchapter IV–A (Permanent Electronic Duck Stamp). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
The Secretary may authorize any State to issue electronic stamps in accordance with this subchapter.
(b) Consultation
The Secretary shall implement this section in consultation with State management agencies.
