40 C.F.R. § 75.45 — Daily quality assurance criteria.
Citation: us:cfr:40:75.45
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-75.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 75 (Continuous Emission Monitoring). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The owner or operator shall either demonstrate that daily tests equivalent to those specified in appendix B of this part can be performed on the alternative monitoring system or demonstrate and document that such tests are unnecessary for providing quality-assured data.
