24 C.F.R. § 2002.17 — Authority to release records or duplications.
Citation: us:cfr:24:2002.17
Creator: Office Of Inspector General, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-2002.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 2002 (Availability Of Information To The Public). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any Assistant Inspector General or an appointed designee is authorized to release any record (or duplication) pertaining to activities for which he or she has primary responsibility, unless disclosure is clearly inappropriate under this part. No authorized person may release records for which another officer has primary responsibility without the consent of the officer or his or her designee.
