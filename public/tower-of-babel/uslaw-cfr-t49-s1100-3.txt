49 C.F.R. § 1100.3 — Liberal construction.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1100.3
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1100.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1100 (General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The rules will be construed liberally to secure just, speedy and inexpensive determination of the issues presented.
