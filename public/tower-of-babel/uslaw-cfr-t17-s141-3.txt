17 C.F.R. § 141.3 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:17:141.3
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-141.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 141 (Salary Offset). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

These regulations are to be followed when:
(a) The Commission is owed a debt by an individual currently employed by another federal agency;
(b) The Commission is owed a debt by an individual who is a current employee of the Commission;
(c) The Commission employs an individual who owes a debt to another federal agency.
