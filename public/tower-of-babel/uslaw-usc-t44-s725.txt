44 U.S.C. § 725 — Statement of appropriations; “usual number”
Citation: us:usc:44:725
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section725
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 7 (Congressional Printing And Binding). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Of the statements of appropriations required to be prepared by section 105 of Title 2,1 there shall be printed, after the close of each regular session of Congress, the usual number of copies.
