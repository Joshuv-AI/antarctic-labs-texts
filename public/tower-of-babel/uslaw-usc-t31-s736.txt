31 U.S.C. § 736 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:31:736
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section736
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 7 (Government Accountability Office); Subchapter III (Personnel). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Amounts necessary to carry out this subchapter and subchapter IV of this chapter may be appropriated to the Comptroller General.
