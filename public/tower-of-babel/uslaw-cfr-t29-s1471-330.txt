29 C.F.R. § 1471.330 — What requirements must I pass down to persons at lower tiers with whom I intend to do business?
Citation: us:cfr:29:1471.330
Creator: Federal Mediation And Conciliation Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1471.330
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1471 (Governmentwide Debarment And Suspension (Nonprocurement)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Before entering into a covered transaction with a participant at the next lower tier, you must require that participant to—
(a) Comply with this subpart as a condition of participation in the transaction. You may do so using any method(s), unless § 1471.440 requires you to use specific methods.
(b) Pass the requirement to comply with this subpart to each person with whom the participant enters into a covered transaction at the next lower tier.
