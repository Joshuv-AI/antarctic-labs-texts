14 U.S.C. § 101 — Establishment of Coast Guard
Citation: us:usc:14:101
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section101
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 1 (Establishment And Duties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Coast Guard, established January 28, 1915, shall be a military service and a branch of the armed forces of the United States at all times.
