40 C.F.R. § 139.23 — Inert gas systems.
Citation: us:cfr:40:139.23
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-139.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 139 (Discharges Incidental To The Normal Operation Of Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

There are no additional discharge-specific requirements that apply to the discharge of washwater from an inert gas system and deck seal water when used as an integral part of that system.
