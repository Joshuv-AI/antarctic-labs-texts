49 C.F.R. § 38.87 — Public information system.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 38 (Americans With Disabilities Act (Ada) Accessibility Specifications For Transportation Vehicles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each vehicle shall be equipped with an interior public address system permitting transportation system personnel, or recorded or digitized human speech messages, to announce stations and provide other passenger information. Alternative systems or devices which provide equivalent access are also permitted.
