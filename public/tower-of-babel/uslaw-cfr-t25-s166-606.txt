25 C.F.R. § 166.606 — What happens to a bond if a violation occurs?
Citation: us:cfr:25:166.606
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-166.606
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 166 (Grazing Permits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We may apply the bond to remedy the violation, in which case we will require the permittee to submit a replacement bond of an appropriate amount.
