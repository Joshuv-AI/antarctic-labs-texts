46 C.F.R. § 32.56-55 — -55 Control spaces—T/ALL.
Citation: us:cfr:46:32.56-55
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-32.56-55
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 32 (Special Equipment, Machinery, And Hull Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Bulkheads and decks that separate control spaces from adjacent spaces must be “A” Class construction and insulated against fire. 46 CFR table 72.05-10(e) of the Passenger Vessel Regulations may be used as a guide.
