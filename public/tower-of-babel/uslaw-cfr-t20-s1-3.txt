20 C.F.R. § 1.3 — What rules are contained in this chapter?
Citation: us:cfr:20:1.3
Creator: Office Of Workers' Compensation Programs, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-1.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 1 (Performance Of Functions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The rules in this chapter are those governing the OWCP functions under the Federal Employees' Compensation Act, the War Hazards Compensation Act, the War Claims Act and the Energy Employees Occupational Illness Compensation Program Act of 2000.
