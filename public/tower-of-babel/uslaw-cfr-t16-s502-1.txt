16 C.F.R. § 502.1 — Scope of the regulations in this part.
Citation: us:cfr:16:502.1
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-502.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 502 (Regulations Under Section 5(C) Of The Fair Packaging And Labeling Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part establish requirements for labeling of consumer commodities with respect to use of package size characterizations, retail sale price representations, and common name and ingredient listing. Additionally, the regulations in this part establish criteria to prevent nonfunctional-slack-fill of packages containing consumer commodities.
