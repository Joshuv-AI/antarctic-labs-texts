18 C.F.R. § 725.5 — Council studies.
Citation: us:cfr:18:725.5
Creator: Water Resources Council
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-725.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 725 (Implementation Of Executive Orders 11988, Floodplain Management And 11990, Protection Of Wetlands). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All studies and appraisals performed by the Council pursuant to section 102 of Pub. L. 89-80 and any recommendations based on these activities shall include specific analyses for reflection of and opportunities to meet the objectives of E.O. 11988 and E.O. 11990. The Council's Floodplain Management Guidelines (43 FR 6030), E.O. 11988 and E.O. 11990 provide the basic evaluation tools for these analyses.
