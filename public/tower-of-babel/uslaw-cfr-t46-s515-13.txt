46 C.F.R. § 515.13 — Investigation of applicants.
Citation: us:cfr:46:515.13
Creator: Federal Maritime Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-515.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 515 (Licensing, Registration, Financial Responsibility Requirements And General Duties For Ocean Transportation Intermediaries). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission shall conduct an investigation of the applicant's qualifications for a license. Such investigations may address:
(a) The accuracy of the information submitted in the application;
(b) The integrity and financial responsibility of the applicant;
(c) The character of the applicant and its qualifying individual; and
(d) The length and nature of the qualifying individual's experience in handling ocean transportation intermediary duties.
