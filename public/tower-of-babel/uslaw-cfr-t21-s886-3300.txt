21 C.F.R. § 886.3300 — Absorbable implant (scleral buckling method).
Citation: us:cfr:21:886.3300
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-886.3300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 886 (Ophthalmic Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. An absorbable implant (scleral buckling method) is a device intended to be implanted on the sclera to aid retinal reattachment.
(b) Classification. Class II.
