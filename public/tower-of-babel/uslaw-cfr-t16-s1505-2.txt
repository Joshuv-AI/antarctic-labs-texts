16 C.F.R. § 1505.2 — Scope of part.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1505.2
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1505.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1505 (Requirements For Electrically Operated Toys Or Other Electrically Operated Articles Intended For Use By Children). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part sets forth the requirements whereby electrically operated toys and other electrically operated articles intended for use by children (as defined in § 1505.1(a)(1)) are not banned toys or banned articles under § 1500.18(b)(1) of this chapter.
