42 C.F.R. § 417.598 — Annual enrollment reconciliation.
Citation: us:cfr:42:417.598
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-417.598
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 417 (Health Maintenance Organizations, Competitive Medical Plans, And Health Care Prepayment Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

CMS's payment to an HMO or CMP may be subject to an enrollment reconciliation at least annually. CMS conducts this reconciliation as necessary to ensure that the payments made do not exceed or fall short of the appropriate per capita rate of payment for each Medicare enrollee of the HMO or CMP during the contract period. The HMO or CMP must submit any information or reports required by CMS to conduct the reconciliation.
