42 U.S.C. § 1997i — Disclaimer respecting standards of care
Citation: us:usc:42:1997i
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1997i
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 21 (Civil Rights); Subchapter I–A (Institutionalized Persons). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Provisions of this subchapter shall not authorize promulgation of regulations defining standards of care.
