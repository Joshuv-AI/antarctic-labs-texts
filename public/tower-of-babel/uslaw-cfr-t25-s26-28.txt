25 C.F.R. § 26.28 — What follow-up services are available after I become employed?
Citation: us:cfr:25:26.28
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-26.28
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 26 (Job Placement And Training Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As determined by the service provider, the following type of services may be available: Temporary housing, transportation to work for a limited period of time, work clothing, and childcare.
