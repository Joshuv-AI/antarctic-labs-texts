43 C.F.R. § 2886.11 — Who regulates activities within my right-of-way or TUP area?
Citation: us:cfr:43:2886.11
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2886.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2880 (Rights-Of-Way Under The Mineral Leasing Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After BLM has issued the grant or TUP, the head of the agency having administrative jurisdiction over the Federal lands involved will regulate your grant or TUP activities in conformance with the Act, appropriate regulations, and the terms and conditions of the grant or TUP. BLM and the other agency head may reach another agreement under 30 U.S.C. 185(c).
