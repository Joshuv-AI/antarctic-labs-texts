42 C.F.R. § 430.14 — Review of State plan material.
Citation: us:cfr:42:430.14
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-430.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 430 (Grants To States For Medical Assistance Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

CMS regional staff reviews State plans and plan amendments, discusses any issues with the Medicaid agency, and consults with central office staff on questions regarding application of Federal policy.
