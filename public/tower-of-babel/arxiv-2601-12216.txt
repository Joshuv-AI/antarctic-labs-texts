Abstract
. This paper examines the large-time behavior of solutions to a one-dimensional conservation law featuring a non-convex flux and an artificial heat flux term regulated by Cattaneo’s law, forming a 2 × \times 2 system of hyperbolic equations. Under the conditions of small wave strength and sufficiently small initial perturbations, we demonstrate the time-asymptotic stability of a composite wave that combines a degenerate Oleinik shock and a rarefaction wave. The proof utilizes the Oleinik entropy condition, the a-contraction method with time-dependent shifts, and weighted energy estimates. Keywords: Conservation law; Asymptotic stability; Composite wave; Relative entropy; Large-time behavior AMS classification code : 35B35, 35L65

3.

(3.2)

where
x_{0}(t,x)
is the unique intersection point with

-axis of the straight characteristic line of Burgers equation. Correspondingly, we construct the smooth approximate rarefaction wave
u^{R}(t,x):=u^{R}(t,x;u_{-},u_{+})
of

by

\displaystyle u^{R}(t,x;u_{-},u_{+}):=(\lambda)^{-1}\left(w^{R}(t,x;\lambda_{-},\lambda_{+})\right),

where
\lambda(u):=f^{\prime}(u),\ \lambda_{\pm}=\lambda(u_{\pm})=f^{\prime}(u_{\pm})
. It is easy to check that

satisfies the equation

\left\{\begin{array}[]{l}u_{t}^{R}+f\left(u^{R}\right)_{x}=0,\\
u^{R}(0,x)=(\lambda)^{-1}\left(\frac{\lambda_{+}+\lambda_{-}}{2}+\frac{\lambda_{+}-\lambda_{-}}{2}\tanh x\right)\rightarrow u_{\pm},\ \text{as }\ x\rightarrow\pm\infty.\end{array}\right.

We have the following lemma to estimate

, see

Lemma 3.1
.

Assume
u_{-}<u_{+}
and

on

and let
\delta_{R}:=\left|u_{+}-u_{-}\right|
be the rarefaction wave strength. Then it holds that

(1)

u_{-}<u^{R}(t,x)<u_{+},\ u_{x}^{R}(t,x)>0
, for

and

;

(2)

\left\|\partial_{x}^{k}u^{R}(t)\right\|_{L^{p}(\mathbb{R})}\leq C_{p}\min\left\{\delta_{R},t^{-1}\right\},\ \ k=2,3,4;\end{array}

(3)

\displaystyle\lim_{t\to\infty}\sup_{x\in\mathbb{R}}\left|u^{R}(t,x)-u^{r}\left(\frac{x}{t}\right)\right|=0,

that is, the approximate rarefaction wave

uniformly;

(4)
For all

\left|u^{R}(t,x)-u_{-}\right|\leq C\delta_{R}e^{-2\left|x-\lambda_{-}t\right|},&x\leq\lambda_{-}t.\end{array}

(5)
For all

\left|u^{R}(t,x)-u_{-}\right|\leq C_{\varepsilon}\delta_{R}^{\frac{2\varepsilon}{2+\varepsilon}}t^{-1+\varepsilon}e^{-\varepsilon\left|x-\lambda_{-}t\right|},\quad x\leq\lambda_{-}t.\end{array}

For all

3.2.
Reformulation of the problem

In order to simplify our analysis, we rewrite the system

through the coordinates transformation
(t,x)\to(t,\xi=x-\sigma t)

\begin{cases}u_{t}-\sigma u_{\xi}+f(u)_{\xi}=q_{\xi},\\
\tau q_{t}-\tau\sigma q_{\xi}+q=u_{\xi}.\end{cases}

Next, we analyze the composite wave

\left(u^{S}(x-\sigma t+\mathbf{X}(t))+u^{r}(\frac{x}{t})-u_{m},q^{S}(x-\sigma t+\mathbf{X}(t))+\left(u^{r}(\frac{x}{t})\right)_{x}\right).

Since the rarefaction wave

is only Lipschitz continuous. We replace

to address this problem for the subsequent stability analysis. Here the shift function

remains to be determined. Thus, the stability ansatz is defined by

\displaystyle\tilde{u}(t,\xi+\mathbf{X}(t)):=u^{S}\left(\xi+\mathbf{X}(t)\right)+u^{R}\left(1+t,\xi+\sigma t+\mathbf{X}(t)\right)-u_{m},

(3.5)

\displaystyle\tilde{q}(t,\xi+\mathbf{X}(t)):=q^{S}(\xi+\mathbf{X}(t))+\left(u^{R}\left(1+t,\xi+\sigma t+\mathbf{X}(t)\right)\right)_{\xi}.

(3.6)

For simplification, we denote that

\displaystyle\tilde{u}^{\mathbf{X}}(t,x):=\tilde{u}(t,x+\mathbf{X}(t)),

\displaystyle\ \tilde{q}^{\mathbf{X}}(t,x):=\tilde{q}(t,x+\mathbf{X}(t)),

\displaystyle\left(u^{S}\right)^{\mathbf{X}}(t,\xi):=u^{S}(t,\xi+\mathbf{X}(t)),

\displaystyle\ \left(u^{R}\right)^{\mathbf{X}}(t,\xi):=u^{R}(t,\xi+\mathbf{X}(t)).

Meanwhile, the ansatz

\begin{cases}\tilde{u}_{t}^{\mathbf{X}}-\sigma\tilde{u}_{\xi}^{\mathbf{X}}+f\left(\tilde{u}^{\mathbf{X}}\right)_{\xi}-\tilde{q}_{\xi}^{\mathbf{X}}=\dot{X}(t)\left(\left(u^{S}\right)_{\xi}^{\mathbf{X}}+\left(u^{R}\right)_{\xi}^{\mathbf{X}}\right)+F^{\mathbf{X}},\\
\tau\tilde{q}_{t}^{\mathbf{X}}-\tau\sigma\tilde{q}_{\xi}^{\mathbf{X}}+\tilde{q}^{\mathbf{X}}-\tilde{u}_{\xi}^{\mathbf{X}}=\tau\dot{X}(t)\tilde{q}_{\xi}^{\mathbf{X}}+\tau\left(u^{R}\right)_{t\xi}^{\mathbf{X}},\end{cases}

(3.7)

where the error term
F^{\mathbf{X}}(t,\xi)=F(t,\xi+\mathbf{X}(t))
is given by

F^{\mathbf{X}}=\left(f\left(\tilde{u}^{\mathbf{X}}\right)-f\left(\left(u^{S}\right)^{\mathbf{X}}\right)-f\left(\left(u^{R}\right)^{\mathbf{X}}\right)\right)_{\xi}-\left(u^{R}\right)_{\xi\xi}^{\mathbf{X}}.

(3.8)

We define the perturbation

\phi(t,\xi):=u(t,\xi)-\tilde{u}^{\mathbf{X}}(t,\xi),\ \ r(t,\xi):=q(t,\xi)-\tilde{q}^{\mathbf{X}}(t,\xi).

(3.9)

The perturbation

\left\{\begin{array}[]{l}\phi_{t}-\sigma\phi_{\xi}+\left(f\left(\phi+\tilde{u}^{\mathbf{X}}\right)-f\left(\tilde{u}^{\mathbf{X}}\right)\right)_{\xi}+\dot{\mathbf{X}}(t)\left(\left(u^{S}\right)_{\xi}^{\mathbf{X}}+\left(u^{R}\right)_{\xi}^{\mathbf{X}}\right)-r_{\xi}=-F^{\mathbf{X}},\\
\tau r_{t}-\tau\sigma r_{\xi}+r-\phi_{\xi}+\tau\dot{\mathbf{X}}(t)\tilde{q}_{\xi}^{\mathbf{X}}+\tau\left(u^{R}\right)_{t\xi}^{\mathbf{X}}=0,\end{array}\right.

(3.10)

with initial condition

\displaystyle\left(\phi(0,\xi),r(0,\xi)\right)=\left(\phi_{0}(\xi),r_{0}(\xi)\right)=\left(u_{0}(\xi)-\tilde{u}(0,\xi),q_{0}(\xi)-\tilde{q}(0,\xi)\right).

(3.11)

3.3.
Construction of weight function and shift function

There exist unique values

and

3.4.
The continuity arguments

Under the assumption

\displaystyle\varepsilon^{*}:=\min\left\{\frac{\varepsilon_{1}}{4},\sqrt{\frac{\varepsilon_{1}^{2}}{36C_{0}}-\delta_{R}^{\frac{8}{33}}}\right\},

By the Lemma

, we have

\sup_{0\leq t\leq T_{0},\ x\in\mathbb{R}}|u(t,x)|\leq C,

|\dot{\mathbf{X}}(t)|\leq Ct,\ \ \forall t\in[0,T_{0}].

Consequently, we can choose

\displaystyle\lVert\tilde{u}^{\mathbf{X}}-\tilde{u}\rVert_{H^{2}(\mathbb{R})}+\sqrt{\tau}\lVert\tilde{q}^{\mathbf{X}}-\tilde{q}\rVert_{H^{2}(\mathbb{R})}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{\varepsilon_{1}}{8}.

(3.18)

Let energy term

be defined as

\displaystyle E(t)=\sup_{0\leq s\leq t}\left\lVert\left(\phi,\sqrt{\tau}r\right)\right\rVert_{H^{2}(\mathbb{R})}.

From

and

\displaystyle E(T_{0})\leq\frac{\varepsilon_{1}}{2}+\frac{\varepsilon_{1}}{8}<\varepsilon_{1}.

We next turn to the maximal existence time:

\displaystyle T_{max}:=\sup\left\{t>0|E(t)\leq\varepsilon_{1}\right\}.

For now, we assume
E(0)\leq\varepsilon_{0}
. If
T_{max}<+\infty
, then

While it holds from Proposition
3.4
that

\displaystyle E(T_{max})\leq\sqrt{C_{0}\left(\|\phi_{0}\|_{H^{2}}^{2}+\tau\|r_{0}\|_{H^{2}}^{2}+\delta_{R}^{\frac{8}{33}}\right)}=\sqrt{C_{0}E(0)+C_{0}\delta_{R}^{\frac{8}{33}}}\leq\frac{\varepsilon_{1}}{4},

which contradicts

Thus, we have

. Together with Proposition

, this yields

\displaystyle\sup_{t\geq 0}

\displaystyle\left[\|\phi(t,\cdot)\|_{H^{2}}^{2}+\tau\|r(t,\cdot)\|_{H^{2}}^{2}\right]+\int_{0}^{t}\|r(s,\cdot)\|_{H^{2}}^{2}ds+\int_{0}^{t}\int_{\mathbb{R}}\|\phi_{\xi}\|_{H^{2}}^{2}d\xi ds

(3.19)

\displaystyle\leq C\left(\|\phi_{0}\|_{H^{2}}^{2}+\tau\|r_{0}\|_{H^{2}}^{2}+\delta_{R}^{\frac{8}{33}}\right).

In addition, we need to check that the initial energy

\displaystyle\small E(0)=

\displaystyle\left\|u_{0}-(u^{S}(x;u_{-},u_{m})+u^{R}(1,x;u_{m},u_{+})-u_{m})\right\|_{H^{2}(\mathbb{R})}

\displaystyle\left\|u_{0}-u^{S}(x;u_{-},u_{m})\right\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb{R}_{-})}+\left\|u^{R}(1,x;u_{m},u_{+})-u_{m}\right\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb{R}_{-})}

3.5.
Time-asymptotic behavior

To establish the time-asymptotic behavior
(\ref{time})
and

, we define

\displaystyle\lim_{t\to+\infty}\|\phi(t,\cdot)\|_{L^{\infty}(\mathbb{R})}\leq C\lim_{t\to+\infty}\|\phi(t,\cdot)\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb{R})}^{\frac{1}{2}}\|\phi_{\xi}(t,\cdot)\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb{R})}^{\frac{1}{2}}=0,

\displaystyle|\dot{\mathbf{X}}(t)|\leq C\|\phi\|_{L^{\infty}(\mathbb{R})}\to 0,\ as\ t\to+\infty.

\displaystyle\sup_{x\in\mathbb{R}}\left|u^{r}\left(\frac{x}{t};u_{m},u_{+}\right)-u^{R}\left(1+t,x+\mathbf{X}(t);u_{m},u_{+}\right)\right|

\displaystyle\sup_{x\in\mathbb{R}}\left|u^{r}\left(\frac{x}{t};u_{m},u_{+}\right)-u^{r}\left(\frac{x}{1+t};u_{m},u_{+}\right)\right|

\displaystyle+\sup_{x\in\mathbb{R}}\left|u^{r}\left(\frac{x}{1+t};u_{m},u_{+}\right)-u^{r}\left(\frac{x+\mathbf{X}(t)}{1+t};u_{m},u_{+}\right)\right|
