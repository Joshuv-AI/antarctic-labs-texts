30 C.F.R. § 203.77 — May I voluntarily give up relief if conditions change?
Citation: us:cfr:30:203.77
Creator: Bureau Of Safety And Environmental Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-203.77
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 203 (Relief Or Reduction In Royalty Rates). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, you may voluntarily give up relief by sending a letter to that effect to the BSEE Regional office for your region.
