10 C.F.R. § 70.35 — Commission action on applications to renew or amend.
Citation: us:cfr:10:70.35
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-70.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 70 (Domestic Licensing Of Special Nuclear Material). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In considering an application by a licensee to renew or amend his license, the Commission will apply the criteria set forth in § 70.23.
