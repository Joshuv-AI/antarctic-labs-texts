41 C.F.R. § 102-118.150 — -118.150 Requirement to audit.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-118.150
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-118.150
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-118 (118—Transportation Payment And Audit). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All transportation bills must undergo an audit unless the agency chooses to perform statistical sampling as established by the Comptroller General (31 U.S.C. 3521(b)).
