49 C.F.R. § 26.43 — Can recipients use set-asides or quotas as part of this program?
Citation: us:cfr:49:26.43
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-26.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 26 (Participation By Disadvantaged Business Enterprises In Department Of Transportation Financial Assistance Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You are not permitted to use quotas for DBEs on DOT-assisted contracts subject to this part.
(b) You may not set-aside contracts for DBEs on DOT-assisted contracts subject to this part, except that, in limited and extreme circumstances, you may use set-asides when no other method could be reasonably expected to redress egregious instances of discrimination.
