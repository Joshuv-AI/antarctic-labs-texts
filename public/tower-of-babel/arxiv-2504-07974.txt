A note on variants of Buchstab’s identity
Runbo Li

Abstract
. The author proves variants of Buchstab’s identity on sieve functions, refining the previous work on new iteration rules of Brady. The main tool used in the proof is a special form of combinatorial identities related to the binomial coefficients. As a by–product, the author obtains better inequalities of F κ ​ ( s ) F_{\kappa}(s) and f κ ​ ( s ) f_{\kappa}(s) for dimensions κ > 1 \kappa>1 .

Introduction

Let
\mathcal{A}
be a set of numbers,
\mathcal{A}_{d}=\{a:ad\in\mathcal{A}\}

\left|\left|\mathcal{A}_{d}\right|-\kappa^{\omega(d)}\frac{y}{d}\right|\leqslant 1.

(1)

Suppose that

and

(1+o(1))f_{\kappa}(s)y\prod_{p<z}\left(1-\frac{\kappa}{p}\right)\leqslant S(\mathcal{A},z)\leqslant(1+o(1))F_{\kappa}(s)y\prod_{p<z}\left(1-\frac{\kappa}{p}\right)

as large as possible and

as small as possible, given that (2) holds for all choices of
\mathcal{A}
satisfying (1). Selberg
[
3
]
has shown that

and

are continuous, monotone, and computable for

s
goes to infinity.

When
\kappa\leqslant 1
, the optimal estimates for
F_{\kappa}(s)
and
f_{\kappa}(s)
arise from Buchstab’s identity

S\left(\mathcal{A},z\right)=S\left(\mathcal{A},w\right)-\sum_{w\leqslant p<z}S\left(\mathcal{A}_{p},p\right)

(3)

for
w\leqslant z
. Simply let
w=2
, this becomes

S\left(\mathcal{A},z\right)=\left|\mathcal{A}\right|-\sum_{p<z}S\left(\mathcal{A}_{p},p\right).

(4)

This leads to the inequalities

\displaystyle s^{\kappa}F_{\kappa}(s)\leqslant

\displaystyle\ s^{\kappa}-\kappa\int_{t>s}t^{\kappa-1}\left(f_{\kappa}(t-1)-1\right)dt,

(5)

\displaystyle s^{\kappa}f_{\kappa}(s)\geqslant

\displaystyle\ s^{\kappa}-\kappa\int_{t>s}t^{\kappa-1}\left(F_{\kappa}(t-1)-1\right)dt.

(6)

Infinite iteration of these inequalities leads to the
\beta
–sieve.

However, there are better estimates for
F_{\kappa}(s)
and
f_{\kappa}(s)
when
\kappa>1
. Taking Selberg’s upper bound sieve as a starting point and using similar iteration rules, Diamond, Halberstam and Richert
[
2
]
developed their DHR–sieve.

In 2017, Brady mentioned and proved lots of new sieve iteration rules in his PhD thesis. One of his simplest upper bound sieve is

S\left(\mathcal{A},z\right)\leqslant S\left(\mathcal{A},w\right)-\frac{2}{3}\sum_{w\leqslant p_{1}<z}S\left(\mathcal{A}_{p_{1}},w\right)+\frac{1}{3}\sum_{w\leqslant p_{2}<p_{1}<z}S\left(\mathcal{A}_{p_{1}p_{2}},w\right).

He proved this inequality using a combinatorial identity

1-\frac{2}{3}n+\frac{1}{3}\left(\genfrac{}{}{0.0pt}{}{n}{2}\right)=\left(1-\frac{n}{2}\right)\left(1-\frac{n}{3}\right).

(7)

Clearly, this leads to an inequality of
F_{\kappa}(s)
:

s^{\kappa}F_{\kappa}(s)\leqslant t^{\kappa}F_{\kappa}(t)-\frac{2}{3}\int_{\frac{1}{t}}^{\frac{1}{s}}\frac{t^{\kappa}f_{\kappa}(t(1-x_{1}))}{x_{1}}dx_{1}+\frac{1}{3}\int_{\frac{1}{t}}^{\frac{1}{s}}\int_{\frac{1}{t}}^{x_{1}}\frac{t^{\kappa}F_{\kappa}(t(1-x_{1}-x_{2}))}{x_{1}x_{2}}dx_{2}dx_{1}.

(8)

In this note, we further develop his method and prove a series of generalized iteration rules.
