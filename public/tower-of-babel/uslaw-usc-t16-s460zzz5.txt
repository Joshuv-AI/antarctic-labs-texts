16 U.S.C. § 460zzz–5 — Management plan
Citation: us:usc:16:460zzz–5
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section460zzz–5
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter CXXXVII (Dominguez-Escalante National Conservation Area). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
Not later than 3 years after March 30, 2009, the Secretary shall develop a comprehensive management plan for the long-term protection and management of the Conservation Area.
(b) Purposes
The management plan shall—
(1)
describe the appropriate uses and management of the Conservation Area;
(2)
be developed with extensive public input;
(3)
take into consideration any information developed in studies of the land within the Conservation Area; and
(4)
include a comprehensive travel management plan.
