49 C.F.R. § 1552.1 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1552.1
Creator: Transportation Security Administration, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1552.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1552 (Flight Training Security Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part includes requirements for the following persons:
(a) Persons who provide flight training or flight training equipment governed by 49 U.S.C. subtitle VII, part A, to any individual.
(b) Persons who lease flight training equipment.
(c) Aliens who apply for or participate in flight training.
(d) U.S. citizens and U.S. nationals who participate in flight training.
