28 U.S.C. § 1922 — Witness fees before United States magistrate judges
Citation: us:usc:28:1922
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section1922
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part V (Procedure); Chapter 123 (Fees And Costs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The fees of more than four witnesses shall not be taxed against the United States, in the examination of any criminal case before a United States magistrate judge, unless their materiality and importance are first approved and certified to by the United States attorney for the district in which the examination is had.
