39 U.S.C. § 1201 — Definition
Citation: us:usc:39:1201
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title39-section1201
Context: United States Code, Title 39 (Postal Service); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 12 (Employee-Management Agreements). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this chapter, “guards” means—
(1)
maintenance guards who, on the effective date of this chapter, are in key position KP–5 under the provisions of former section 3514 of title 39; and
(2)
security guards, who may be employed in the Postal Service and whose primary duties shall include the exercise of authority to enforce rules to protect the safety of property, mail, or persons on the premises.
