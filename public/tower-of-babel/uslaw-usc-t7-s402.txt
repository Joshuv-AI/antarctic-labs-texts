7 U.S.C. § 402 — Chief of bureau; appointment and duties
Citation: us:usc:7:402
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section402
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 16 (Bureau Of Dairy Industry). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A Chief of the Bureau of Dairy Industry shall be appointed by the Secretary of Agriculture, who shall be subject to the general direction of the Secretary of Agriculture. He shall devote his time to the investigation of the dairy industry, and the dissemination of information for the promotion of the dairy industry.
