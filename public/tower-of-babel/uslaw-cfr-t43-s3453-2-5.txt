43 C.F.R. § 3453.2-5 — -5 Effect of partial assignment.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3453.2-5
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3453.2-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3450 (Management Of Existing Leases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A transfer of full record-title to only part of the lands, or any bed of the coal deposits therein, shall segregate the transferred and retained portions into separate and distinct leases or licenses, with the retained portion keeping the original serial number. The newly segregated lease or license shall be assigned a new serial number and shall contain the same terms and conditions as the original lease or license.
