43 C.F.R. § 1784.4-1 — -1 Calls for nominations.
Citation: us:cfr:43:1784.4-1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-1784.4-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 1780 (Cooperative Relations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except where otherwise provided, candidates for appointment to advisory committees are sought through public calls for public nominations. Such calls shall be published in the Federal Register and are made through media releases and systematic contacts with individuals and organizations interested in the use and management of public lands and resources.
