5 C.F.R. § 850.105 — Agency responsibility.
Citation: us:cfr:5:850.105
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-850.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 850 (Electronic Retirement Processing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies employing individuals whose retirement records or processing are affected by this part are responsible for counseling those individuals regarding their rights and benefits under CSRS, FERS, FEGLI, FEHB, or RFEHB.
