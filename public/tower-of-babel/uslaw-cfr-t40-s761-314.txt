40 C.F.R. § 761.314 — Chemical analysis of standard wipe test samples.
Citation: us:cfr:40:761.314
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-761.314
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 761 (POLYCHLORINATED BIPHENYLS (PCBs) MANUFACTURING, PROCESSING, DISTRIBUTION IN COMMERCE, AND USE PROHIBITIONS). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Perform the chemical analysis of standard wipe test samples in accordance with § 761.253. Report sample results in micrograms per 100 cm 2.
