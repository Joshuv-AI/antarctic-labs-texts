13 C.F.R. § 120.530 — Deferment of payment.
Citation: us:cfr:13:120.530
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-120.530
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 120 (Business Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

SBA may agree to defer payments on a business loan for a stated period of time, and use such other methods as it considers necessary and appropriate to help in the successful operation of the Borrower. This policy applies to all business loan programs, including 504 loans.
