5 C.F.R. § 301.201 — Appointments of United States citizens recruited overseas.
Citation: us:cfr:5:301.201
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-301.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 301 (Overseas Employment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When there is a shortage of eligible applicants, as defined at § 337.202 of this chapter, resulting from a competitive announcement that is open to applicants in the local overseas area, an agency may give an overseas limited appointment to a United States citizen recruited overseas for a position overseas.
