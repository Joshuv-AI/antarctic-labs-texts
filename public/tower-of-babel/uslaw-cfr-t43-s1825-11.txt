43 C.F.R. § 1825.11 — When are relinquishments effective?
Citation: us:cfr:43:1825.11
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-1825.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 1820 (Application Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Generally, BLM considers a relinquishment to be effective when it is received, along with any required fee, in the BLM office having jurisdiction of the lands being relinquished. However, the specific program regulations govern effectiveness of relinquishments.
