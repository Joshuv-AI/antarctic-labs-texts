25 C.F.R. § 13.16 — Technical assistance after disapproval.
Citation: us:cfr:25:13.16
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-13.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 13 (Tribal Reassumption Of Jurisdiction Over Child Custody Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a petition is disapproved, the Bureau shall immediately offer technical assistance to the tribal governing body for the purpose of overcoming the defect in the petition or plan that resulted in the disapproval.
