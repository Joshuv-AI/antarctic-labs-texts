49 C.F.R. § 1018.92 — Administrative charges.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1018.92
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1018.92
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1018 (Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In accordance with 49 CFR 1018.30, all administrative charges incurred in connection with the referral of the debts to the IRS shall be assessed on the debt and thus increase the amount of the offset.
