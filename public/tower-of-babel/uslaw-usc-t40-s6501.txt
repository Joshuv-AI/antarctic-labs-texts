40 U.S.C. § 6501 — Definition
Citation: us:usc:40:6501
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section6501
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Part C (Federal Building Complexes); Chapter 65 (Thurgood Marshall Federal Judiciary Building). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter, the term “Chief Justice” means the Chief Justice of the United States or the designee of the Chief Justice, except that when there is a vacancy in the office of the Chief Justice, the most senior associate justice of the Supreme Court shall be deemed to be the Chief Justice for purposes of this chapter until the vacancy is filled.
