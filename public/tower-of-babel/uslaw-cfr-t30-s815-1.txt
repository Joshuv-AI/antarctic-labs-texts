30 C.F.R. § 815.1 — Scope and purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:30:815.1
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-815.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 815 (Permanent Program Performance Standards—Coal Exploration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part sets forth performance standards required for coal exploration which substantially disturbs the natural land surface. At the discretion of the regulatory authority, coal exploration operations may be further required to comply with the applicable standards of 30 CFR parts 816 through 828.
