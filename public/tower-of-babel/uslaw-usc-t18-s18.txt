18 U.S.C. § 18 — Organization defined
Citation: us:usc:18:18
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section18
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 1 (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this title, the term “organization” means a person other than an individual.
