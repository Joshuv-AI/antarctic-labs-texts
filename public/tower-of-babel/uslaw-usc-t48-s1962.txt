48 U.S.C. § 1962 — Transition funding
Citation: us:usc:48:1962
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section1962
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 18 (Micronesia, Marshall Islands, And Palau); Subchapter II (Palau); Part B (Implementation Of Compact). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purposes of applying section 1905(c)(2) of this title to Palau, the terms “fiscal year 1987”, “fiscal year 1988”, and “fiscal year 1989” in section 104(c) of Public Law 99–658 shall be deemed to be the first, second, and third fiscal years, respectively, beginning after the effective date of the Compact.
