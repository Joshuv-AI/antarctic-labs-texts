32 C.F.R. § 855.3 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:32:855.3
Creator: Department Of The Air Force
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-855.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 855 (Civil Aircraft Use Of United States Air Force Airfields). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to all regular United States Air Force (USAF), Air National Guard (ANG), and United States Air Force Reserve (USAFR) installations with airfields. This part also applies to civil aircraft use of Air Force ramps at civil airports hosting USAF, ANG, and USAFR units.
