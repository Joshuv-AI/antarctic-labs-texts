17 C.F.R. § 155.10 — Exemptions.
Citation: us:cfr:17:155.10
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-155.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 155 (Trading Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise provided in this part, the Commission may, in its discretion and upon such terms and conditions as it deems appropriate, exempt any contract market or other person from any of the provisions of this part.
