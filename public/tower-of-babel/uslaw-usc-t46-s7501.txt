46 U.S.C. § 7501 — Duplicates
Citation: us:usc:46:7501
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section7501
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part E (Merchant Seamen Licenses, Certificates, And Documents); Chapter 75 (General Procedures For Licensing, Certification, And Documentation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
If a license, certificate of registry, or merchant mariner’s document issued under this part is lost as a result of a marine casualty, the holder shall be supplied with a duplicate without cost.
(b)
For any other loss, the holder may obtain a duplicate on payment of reasonable costs prescribed by regulation by the Secretary.
