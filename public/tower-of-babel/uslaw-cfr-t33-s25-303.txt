33 C.F.R. § 25.303 — Procedure.
Citation: us:cfr:33:25.303
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-25.303
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 25 (Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A claim shall be presented and processed in accordance with 28 CFR Part 14. Should there be a conflict between the provisions of 33 CFR Part 25, Subpart A and the Department of Justice regulations in 28 CFR Part 14, the Department of Justice regulations govern.
