39 U.S.C. § 3629 — Reduced rates for voter registration purposes
Citation: us:usc:39:3629
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title39-section3629
Context: United States Code, Title 39 (Postal Service); Part IV (Mail Matter); Chapter 36 (Postal Rates, Classes, And Services); Subchapter I (Provisions Relating To Market-Dominant Products). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Postal Service shall make available to a State or local voting registration official the rate for any class of mail that is available to a qualified nonprofit organization under section 3626 for the purpose of making a mailing that the official certifies is required or authorized by the National Voter Registration Act of 1993.
