5 U.S.C. § 7502 — Actions covered
Citation: us:usc:5:7502
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section7502
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart F (Labor-Management And Employee Relations); Chapter 75 (Adverse Actions); Subchapter I (Suspension For 14 Days Or Less). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This subchapter applies to a suspension for 14 days or less, but does not apply to a suspension under section 7521 or 7532 of this title or any action initiated under section 1215 of this title.
