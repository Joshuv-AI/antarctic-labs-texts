2 U.S.C. § 1867 — Advancement and reimbursement of expenses for flying American flags and providing certification services therefor
Citation: us:usc:2:1867
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section1867
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 28 (Architect Of The Capitol); Subchapter IV (Appropriations And Expenditures). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

On and after November 19, 1995, expenses, based on full cost recovery, for flying American flags and providing certification services therefor shall be advanced or reimbursed upon request of the Architect of the Capitol, and amounts so received shall be deposited into the Treasury.
