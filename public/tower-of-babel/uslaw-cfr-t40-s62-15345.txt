40 C.F.R. § 62.15345 — What must I do if I am out of compliance with these standards?
Citation: us:cfr:40:62.15345
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-62.15345
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 62 (Approval And Promulgation Of State Plans For Designated Facilities And Pollutants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must submit a semiannual report on any recorded emission or parameter level that does not meet the requirements specified in this subpart.
