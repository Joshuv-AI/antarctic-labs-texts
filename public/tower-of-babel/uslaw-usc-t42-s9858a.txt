42 U.S.C. § 9858a — Establishment of block grant program
Citation: us:usc:42:9858a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section9858a
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 105 (Community Services Programs); Subchapter II–B (Child Care And Development Block Grant). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized to make grants to States in accordance with the provisions of this subchapter.
