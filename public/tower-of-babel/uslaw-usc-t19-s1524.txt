19 U.S.C. § 1524 — Deposit of reimbursable charges
Citation: us:usc:19:1524
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section1524
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 4 (Tariff Act Of 1930); Part III (Ascertainment, Collection, And Recovery Of Duties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Receipts for any reimbursable charges or expenses which have been paid for out of any appropriation for collecting the revenue from customs shall be deposited as a refund to such appropriation instead of being covered into the Treasury as miscellaneous receipts, as provided by section 527 of this title.
