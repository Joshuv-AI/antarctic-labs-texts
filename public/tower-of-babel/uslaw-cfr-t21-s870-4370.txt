21 C.F.R. § 870.4370 — Roller-type cardiopulmonary bypass blood pump.
Citation: us:cfr:21:870.4370
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-870.4370
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 870 (Cardiovascular Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. A roller-type cardiopulmonary bypass blood pump is a device that uses a revolving roller mechanism to pump the blood through the cardiopulmonary bypass circuit during bypass surgery.
(b) Classification. Class II (performance standards).
