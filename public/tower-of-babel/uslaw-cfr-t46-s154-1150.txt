46 C.F.R. § 154.1150 — Distribution of dry chemical.
Citation: us:cfr:46:154.1150
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-154.1150
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 154 (Safety Standards For Self-Propelled Vessels Carrying Bulk Liquefied Gases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) All locations on the above deck cargo area and the cargo piping outside that cargo area must be protected by:
(1) At least two dry chemical hand hose lines; or
(2) At least one dry chemical hand hose line and one dry chemical monitor.
(b) At least one dry chemical storage unit and hand hose line or monitor must be at the after end of the cargo areas.
(c) Each cargo loading and discharge manifold must be protected by at least one dry chemical monitor.
