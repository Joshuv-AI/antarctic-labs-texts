32 C.F.R. § 516.45 — Records determined to be releasable.
Citation: us:cfr:32:516.45
Creator: Department Of The Army
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-516.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 516 (Litigation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the deciding official, after considering the factors set forth in § 536.44, determines that all or part of requested official records are releasable, copies of the records should be furnished to the requester.
