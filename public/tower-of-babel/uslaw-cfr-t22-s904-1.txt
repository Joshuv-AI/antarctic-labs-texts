22 C.F.R. § 904.1 — General.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 904 (Jurisdiction And Preliminary Determinations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board's jurisdiction extends to any grievance, and to any separation for cause proceeding initiated pursuant to section 610(a)(2) of the Act.
