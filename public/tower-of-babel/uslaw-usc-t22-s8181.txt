22 U.S.C. § 8181 — Authorization of appropriations

Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 88 (Nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty—United States Additional Protocol Implementation); Subchapter VIII (Authorization Of Appropriations).
