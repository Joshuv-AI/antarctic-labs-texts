21 C.F.R. § 1313.57 — Final order.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1313.57
Creator: Drug Enforcement Administration, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1313.57
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1313 (Importation And Exportation Of List I And List Ii Chemicals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As soon as practicable after the presiding officer has certified the record to the Administrator, the Administrator shall issue his order regarding the suspension of shipment. The order shall include the findings of fact and conclusions of law upon which the order is based. The Administrator shall serve one copy of his order upon each party in the hearing.
