43 U.S.C. § 1623 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:43:1623
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section1623
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 33 (Alaska Native Claims Settlement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary to carry out the provisions of this chapter.
