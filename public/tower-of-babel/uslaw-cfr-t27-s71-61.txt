27 C.F.R. § 71.61 — Notice of hearing.
Citation: us:cfr:27:71.61
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-71.61
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 71 (Rules Of Practice In Permit Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In case a request for a hearing is filed by the applicant within the required time, the appropriate TTB officer shall refer the matter to the administrative law judge and the administrative law judge shall set a time and place for a hearing and shall serve notice thereof upon the parties at least ten days in advance of the hearing date.
