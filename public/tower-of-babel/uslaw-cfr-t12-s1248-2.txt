12 C.F.R. § 1248.2 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1248.2
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1248.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1248 (Uniform Mortgage-Backed Securities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to:
(a) Enhance liquidity in the MBS marketplace, and to that end, enable adoption of the UMBS, by achieving sufficient similarity of cash flows on cohorts of TBA-eligible MBS such that investors will accept delivery of UMBS from either issuer in settlement of trades on the TBA market.
(b) Provide transparency and durability into the process for creating alignment.
