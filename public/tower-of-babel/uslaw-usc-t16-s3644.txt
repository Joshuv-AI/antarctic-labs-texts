16 U.S.C. § 3644 — Restriction on spending authority
Citation: us:usc:16:3644
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section3644
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 56A (Pacific Salmon Fishing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

New spending authority or authority to enter into contracts provided in this chapter shall be effective only to such extent, or in such amounts, as are provided in advance in appropriation Acts.
