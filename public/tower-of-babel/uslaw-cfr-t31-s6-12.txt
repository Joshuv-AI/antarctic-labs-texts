31 C.F.R. § 6.12 — Filing and service of documents.
Citation: us:cfr:31:6.12
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-6.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 6 (Applications For Awards Under The Equal Access To Justice Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any application for an award or other pleading or document related to an application shall be filed and served on all parties to the proceeding in the same manner as other pleadings in the proceeding.
