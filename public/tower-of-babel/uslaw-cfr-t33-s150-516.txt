33 C.F.R. § 150.516 — What are the periodic requirements for weight testing?
Citation: us:cfr:33:150.516
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-150.516
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 150 (Deepwater Ports: Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The operator must weight test, using the procedure outlined in 46 CFR 199.45(a)(1), each lifeboat, davit-launched liferaft, and rescue boat every time a fall is replaced or turned end-for-end.
