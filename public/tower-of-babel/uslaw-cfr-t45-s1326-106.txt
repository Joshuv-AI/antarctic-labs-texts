45 C.F.R. § 1326.106 — Exclusion from hearing for misconduct.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1326.106
Creator: Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1326.106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1326 (Developmental Disabilities Formula Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Disrespectful, disorderly, or rebellious language or contemptuous conduct, refusal to comply with directions, or continued use of dilatory tactics by any person at the hearing before a presiding officer shall constitute grounds for immediate exclusion of such person from the hearing by the presiding officer.
