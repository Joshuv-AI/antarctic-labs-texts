48 U.S.C. § 1421n — Applicability of Federal copyright laws
Citation: us:usc:48:1421n
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section1421n
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 8A (Guam); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The laws of the United States relating to copyrights, and to the enforcement of rights arising thereunder, shall have the same force and effect in Guam as in the continental United States.
