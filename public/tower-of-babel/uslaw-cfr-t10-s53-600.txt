10 C.F.R. § 53.600 — Construction and manufacturing—scope and purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:10:53.600
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-53.600
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 53 (Risk-Informed, Technology-Inclusive Regulatory Framework For Commercial Nuclear Plants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to those construction and manufacturing activities authorized by a construction permit (CP), combined license (COL), manufacturing license (ML), or limited work authorization (LWA) issued under this part.
