36 C.F.R. § 1202.24 — Will NARA ever request information about me from someone else?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1202.24
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1202.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1202 (Regulations Implementing The Privacy Act Of 1974). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

NARA will make every effort to gather information from you directly. When NARA solicits information about you from someone else, NARA will explain to that person the purpose for which the information will be used.
