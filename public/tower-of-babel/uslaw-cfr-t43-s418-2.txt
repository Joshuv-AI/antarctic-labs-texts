43 C.F.R. § 418.2 — How Project water may be used.
Citation: us:cfr:43:418.2
Creator: Bureau Of Reclamation, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-418.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 418 (Operating Criteria And Procedures For The Newlands Reclamation Project, Nevada). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Project water may be delivered only to serve valid water rights used for:
(a) Maintenance of wetlands and fish and wildlife including endangered and threatened species;
(b) Recreation;
(c) Irrigation of eligible land; and
(d) Domestic and other uses of Project water as defined by the decrees.
