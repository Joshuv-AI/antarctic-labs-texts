33 C.F.R. § 153.303 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:33:153.303
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-153.303
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 153 (Control Of Pollution By Oil And Hazardous Substances, Discharge Removal). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart apply to any owner or operator of a vessel or onshore or offshore facility from which a discharge of oil into coastal waters occurs who acts to remove or arranges for the removal of such discharges.
