49 C.F.R. § 79.5 — Investigation.
Citation: us:cfr:49:79.5
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-79.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 79 (Medals Of Honor). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Department of Transportation may make any investigation of an application that it deems appropriate, including the taking of testimony under oath or affirmation.
