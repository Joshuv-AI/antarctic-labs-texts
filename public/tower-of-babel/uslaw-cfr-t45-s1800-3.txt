45 C.F.R. § 1800.3 — Procedures for requests for access to individual records in a record system.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1800.3
Creator: Harry S. Truman Scholarship Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1800.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1800 (Privacy Act Of 1974). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An individual shall submit a request to the Deputy Executive Secretary of the Foundation to determine if a system of records named by the individual contains a record pertaining to the individual. The individual shall submit a request to the Deputy Executive Secretary of the Foundation which states the individual's desire to review his or her record.
