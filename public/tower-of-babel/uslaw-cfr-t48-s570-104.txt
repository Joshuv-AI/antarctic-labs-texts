48 C.F.R. § 570.104 — 570.104 Competition.
Citation: us:cfr:48:570.104
Creator: Chapter 5—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-570.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 570 (Acquiring Leasehold Interests In Real Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless the contracting officer uses the simplified procedures in subpart 570.2, the competition requirements of FAR part 6 apply to acquisition of leasehold interests in real property.
