7 C.F.R. § 868.13 — Regulations not applicable for certain purposes.
Citation: us:cfr:7:868.13
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Federal Grain Inspection Service, Fair Trade Practices Program), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-868.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 868 (General Regulations And Standards For Certain Agricultural Commodities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

These regulations do not apply to the inspection of grain under the United States Grain Standards Act, as amended (7 U.S.C. 71 et seq.) or the inspection of commodities under the United States Warehouse Act, as amended (7 U.S.C. 241 et seq.).
