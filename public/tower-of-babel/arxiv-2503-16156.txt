Electromagnetically-Induced Transparency Effect Improves Quantum-Battery Lifetime
Cheng-Ge Liu, Jin-Tian Zhang, Qing Ai

Abstract
Quantum battery (QB) is an application of quantum thermodynamics which uses quantum effects to store and transfer energy, overcoming the limitations of classical batteries and potentially improving performance. However, due to the interaction with the external environment, it will lead to decoherence and thus reduce the lifetime of QBs. Here, we propose suppressing the environmental dissipation in the energy-storage process of the QB by exploiting the electromagnetically-induced transparency (EIT) and bound states. By constructing a hybrid system composed of a four-level atom and a coupled-cavity array, two bound states are formed in the system when the energy of the QB is in the energy band of the cavity array. Due to the bound states and the EIT effect, the ambient dissipation is significantly suppressed, which improves the lifetime of the QB. In addition, we show that when the energy of the QB is in resonance with the cavity, the ergotropy of the QB reaches the maximum. Furthermore, there exists an optimal coupling strength between two neighbouring cavities which helps improve the performance of the QB. These discoveries may shed the light on the design of high-efficiency QBs.

;
Roßnagel et al. 2014
;
Linden et al. 2010
;

. The concept of quantum batteries (QBs) was first proposed by

The QBs utilize quantum effects such as entanglement

;

;

;

Farina et al. 2019

.

As known to all, electromagnetically induced transparency (EIT) is a quantum coherence phenomenon
Harris et al. 1990
, which is widely observed in atomic or molecular systems. When a probe field passes a medium with resonant two-level atoms, i.e., the ground state and a lossy excited state, the transmittance of the probe field will significantly decrease due to the absorption. However, if a control field is introduced to induce the transition between the lossy excited state and a metastable state, the probe field will pass the medium as if it is transparent. And thus that is why it is called EIT. There are typically three kinds of configurations for the EIT, i.e., the ladder type, V-type and
\Lambda
type
Boon et al. 1999
. In the EIT, there exists a dark state, i.e., the superposition of the ground state and the metastable state
Wang et al. 2018
. Due to the dark state, the probe field can pass the medium without absorption since the dark state is void of the lossy excited state. The dark state has been applied in coherent energy transfer of artificial light harvesting
Dong et al. 2012
and perfect state transfer in optomechanical systems
Wang and Clerk 2012
;
Khanaliloo et al. 2015
. Recently, remote charging and degradation suppression of the QB can be achieved when bound states form in the total system including the charger, the QB, and the electromagnetic field
Bai et al. 2019
;
Song et al. 2024
. In this work, inspired by these discoveries, we theoretically propose a QB scheme utilizing a hybrid system composed of a four-level atom as the QB and a coupled cavity array as the charger, which can be implemented with superconducting transmission line resonators
You and Nori 2003
;
Chiorescu et al. 2004
;
Wallraff et al. 2004
and a superconducting charge qubit
Sillanpää et al. 2007
;
You and Nori 2005
. By applying two coherent light beams to the four-level atom, we can realize a quasi-two-level system with a dark state and the ground state for the QB. We show that the interaction between the dark state of the QB and the coupled-cavity array gives rise to two bound states in the hybrid system. Remarkably, the bound states assisted by the dark state significantly suppresses the environmental dissipation, thereby enhancing the QB’s lifetime. Furthermore, our results indicate that when the dark state is resonant with the bare frequency of the cavity, the extractable work, i.e., ergotropy, reaches its maximum. Additionally, we also demonstrate that the coupling strength between two adjacent cavities can be optimized to increase the performance of the QB. Different from traditional dark-state QBs
Liu et al. 2019
;
Liu and Hanna 2024
;
Quach and Munro 2020
;
Santos et al. 2019
, we propose an charging and energy-storage mechanism that combines the EIT effect and the introduction of bound states. The EIT effect is introduced to effectively enhance the coherence between the QB and the environment, and the dark state and bound state formed by EIT effectively protect the excitation energy of the battery and the ergotropy of the QB is improved.

This article is structured as follows. In the next section, we will introduce our QB protocol. By using the dark state, we obtain the effective Hamiltonian of the QB interacting with the coupled-cavity array, as detailed derived in Appendix

We use analytical and numerical methods to verify that in the presence of two bound states, environmental dissipation can be significantly suppressed due to the combination of the bound states and the dark state. In Sec.
IV
, we summarize our main findings.

IV
Conclusion

In this paper, we propose a scheme to suppress the environmental dissipation of energy storage in QBs by using the bound states and the EIT. Under the effect of the EIT, we obtain a QB of an effective two-level system including the dark state and the ground state. This QB interacts with a coupled-cavity array to form a hybrid system. When the energy of the dark state is within the energy band of the cavity array, there are two bound states. We show that the energy of the bound states determine the population dynamic of the QB in the excited state, and thus results in the Rabi-like oscillations. Under the influence of the bound states and the EIT, we find that the dissipation of the environment is strongly suppressed. Furthermore, we demonstrate that there is a coupling strength of two cavities is which maximizes the extractable work of the QB. When the energy of the dark state is resonant with the frequency of the cavity, the extractable work of the QB reaches the maximum. Therefore, our scheme can significantly improves the lifetime of the QB and fabricate QBs with high extractable work.
