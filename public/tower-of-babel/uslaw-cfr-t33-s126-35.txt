33 C.F.R. § 126.35 — Primary responsibility.
Citation: us:cfr:33:126.35
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-126.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 126 (Handling Of Dangerous Cargo At Waterfront Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing contained in the rules, regulations, conditions, and designations in this part shall be construed as relieving the masters, owners, operators, and agents of vessels, docks, piers, wharves, or other waterfront facilities from their primary responsibility for the security of such vessels, docks, piers, wharves, or waterfront facilities.
