40 C.F.R. § 427.100 — Applicability; description of the vapor absorption subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:427.100
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-427.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 427 (Asbestos Manufacturing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to discharges resulting from the removal of volatilized organic materials from atmospheric emissions by means of wet scrubbers.
