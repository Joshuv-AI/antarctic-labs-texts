16 C.F.R. § 301.17 — Misrepresentation of origin of furs.
Citation: us:cfr:16:301.17
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-301.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 301 (Rules And Regulations Under Fur Products Labeling Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No misleading nor deceptive statements as to the geographical or zoological origin of the animal producing a fur shall be used directly or indirectly in labeling, invoicing or advertising furs or fur products.
