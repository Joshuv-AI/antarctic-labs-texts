42 U.S.C. § 6324 — State energy efficiency goals
Citation: us:usc:42:6324
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section6324
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 77 (Energy Conservation); Subchapter III (Improving Energy Efficiency); Part B (State Energy Conservation Plans). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each State energy conservation plan with respect to which assistance is made available under this part on or after August 8, 2005, shall contain a goal, consisting of an improvement of 25 percent or more in the efficiency of use of energy in the State concerned in calendar year 2012 as compared to calendar year 1990, and may contain interim goals.
