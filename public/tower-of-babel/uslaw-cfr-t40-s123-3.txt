40 C.F.R. § 123.3 — Coordination with other programs.
Citation: us:cfr:40:123.3
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-123.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 123 (State Program Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Issuance of State permits under this part may be coordinated with issuance of RCRA, UIC, NPDES, and 404 permits whether they are controlled by the State, EPA, or the Corps of Engineers. See § 124.4.
