45 C.F.R. § 2519.310 — Is a participant eligible to receive an AmeriCorps educational award?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2519.310
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2519.310
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2519 (Higher Education Innovative Programs For Community Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In general, no. However, certain positions in programs funded under this part may qualify as approved AmeriCorps positions. The Corporation will establish eligibility requirements for these positions as a part of the application package.
