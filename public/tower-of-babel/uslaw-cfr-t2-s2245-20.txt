2 C.F.R. § 2245.20 — Does this part apply to me?
Citation: us:cfr:2:2245.20
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-2245.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 2245 (Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Financial Assistance)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part and, through this part, pertinent portions of the OMB guidance in Subparts A through F of 2 CFR part 182 (see table at 2 CFR 182.115(b)) apply to you if you are a—
(a) Recipient of a Corporation grant or cooperative agreement; or
(b) A Corporation awarding official.
