25 U.S.C. § 116 — Indians 18 years of age to have right to receipt for annuity
Citation: us:usc:25:116
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section116
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 4 (Performance By United States Of Obligations To Indians); Subchapter II (Disbursement Of Moneys And Supplies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All Indians, when they shall arrive at the age of eighteen years, shall have the right to receive and receipt for all annuity money that may be due or become due to them, if not otherwise incapacitated under the regulations of the Indian Office.
