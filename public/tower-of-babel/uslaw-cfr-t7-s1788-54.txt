7 C.F.R. § 1788.54 — Compliance with contracts.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1788.54
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1788.54
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1788 (Rus Fidelity And Insurance Requirements For Electric And Telecommunications Borrowers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It is the responsibility of the borrower to determine, before the commencement of work, that the engineer, architect, and the contractor have insurance that complies with their contract requirements.
