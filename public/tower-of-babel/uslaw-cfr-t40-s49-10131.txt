40 C.F.R. § 49.10131 — Identification of plan.
Citation: us:cfr:40:49.10131
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-49.10131
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 49 (Indian Country: Air Quality Planning And Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This section and §§ 49.10132 through 49.10160 contain the implementation plan for the Hoh Indian Tribe of the Hoh Indian Reservation. This plan consists of a combination of Tribal rules and measures and Federal regulations and measures which apply within the Hoh Indian Reservation.
