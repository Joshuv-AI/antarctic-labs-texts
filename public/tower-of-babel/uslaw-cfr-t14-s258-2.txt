14 C.F.R. § 258.2 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:14:258.2
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-258.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 258 (Disclosure Of Change-Of-Gauge Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to the following:
(a) Direct air carriers and foreign air carriers that sell or issue tickets in the United States for scheduled passenger air transportation on change-of-gauge services or that operate such transportation; and
(b) Ticket agents doing business in the United States that sell or issue tickets for scheduled passenger air transportation on change-of-gauge services.
