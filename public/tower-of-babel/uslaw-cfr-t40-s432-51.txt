40 C.F.R. § 432.51 — Special definitions.

Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-432.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 432 (Meat And Poultry Products Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For the purpose of this subpart:
(a) Finished product means the final product, such as fresh meat cuts, hams, bacon or other smoked meats, sausage, luncheon meats, stew, canned meats or related products.
(b) Small processor means an operation that produces no more than 6000 lbs (2730 kg) per day of any type or combination of finished products.
