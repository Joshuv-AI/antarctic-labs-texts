18 U.S.C. § 3236 — Murder or manslaughter
Citation: us:usc:18:3236
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section3236
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part II (Criminal Procedure); Chapter 211 (Jurisdiction And Venue). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In all cases of murder or manslaughter, the offense shall be deemed to have been committed at the place where the injury was inflicted, or the poison administered or other means employed which caused the death, without regard to the place where the death occurs.
