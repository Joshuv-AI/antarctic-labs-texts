43 U.S.C. § 1511a — Cooperation and participation by Secretary of the Army with Federal, State, and local agencies
Citation: us:usc:43:1511a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section1511a
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 32 (Colorado River Basin Project); Subchapter II (Investigations And Planning). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Army, acting through the Chief of Engineers, is authorized to cooperate and participate with concerned Federal, State, and local agencies in preparing the general plan for the development of the water resources of the western United States authorized by the Colorado River Basin Project Act [43 U.S.C. 1501 et seq.].
