16 U.S.C. § 833o — Authority of Secretary of the Interior for acquisition of Indian land
Citation: us:usc:16:833o
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section833o
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 12C (Fort Peck Project). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Interior is authorized to perform any and all acts and to prescribe such regulations as he may deem appropriate to carry out the provisions of sections 833l to 833p of this title.
