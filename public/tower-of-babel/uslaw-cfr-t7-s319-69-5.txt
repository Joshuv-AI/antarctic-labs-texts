7 C.F.R. § 319.69-5 — -5 Types of organic decaying vegetative matter authorized for packing.
Citation: us:cfr:7:319.69-5
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-319.69-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 319 (Foreign Quarantine Notices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following types of organic decaying vegetative matter are authorized as safe for packing:
(a) Peat;
(b) Peat moss; and
(c) Osmunda fiber.
