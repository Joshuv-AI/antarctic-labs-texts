40 C.F.R. § 98.382 — GHGs to report.
Citation: us:cfr:40:98.382
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-98.382
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 98 (Mandatory Greenhouse Gas Reporting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Suppliers of coal-based liquid fuels must report the CO2 emissions that would result from the complete combustion or oxidation of fossil-fuel products (besides coal or crude oil) produced, used as feedstock, imported, or exported during the calendar year. Additionally, producers must report CO2 emissions that would result from the complete combustion or oxidation of any biomass co-processed with fossil fuel-based feedstocks.
