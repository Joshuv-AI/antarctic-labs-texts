39 C.F.R. § 4.3 — Postmaster General.
Citation: us:cfr:39:4.3
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-4.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 4 (Officials (Article Iv)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The appointment and role of the Postmaster General are described at 39 U.S.C. 202(c), 203. The Governors set the compensation and benefits of the Postmaster General by resolution, subject to 39 U.S.C. 1003(a) and 3686.
