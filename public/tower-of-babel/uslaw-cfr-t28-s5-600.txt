28 C.F.R. § 5.600 — Public examination of records.
Citation: us:cfr:28:5.600
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-5.600
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 5 (Administration And Enforcement Of Foreign Agents Registration Act Of 1938, As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Registration statements, informational materials, Dissemination Reports, and copies of political propaganda filed under section 4(a) of the Act, shall be available for public examination at the Registration Unit on official business days, during the posted hours of operation.
