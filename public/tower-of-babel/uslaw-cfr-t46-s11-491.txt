46 C.F.R. § 11.491 — National officer endorsements for service on offshore supply vessels.
Citation: us:cfr:46:11.491
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-11.491
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 11 (Requirements For Officer Endorsements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each officer endorsement for service on offshore supply vessels (OSVs) authorizes service on OSVs as defined in 46 U.S.C. 2101 and as interpreted under 46 U.S.C. 14104(b), subject to any restrictions placed on the MMC.
(b) For those officers who previously received a 500 GRT limitation on their national officer endorsement due to the definition of OSV existing before October 15, 2010, the limitation will be raised to 1,600 GRT to be consistent with other national officer endorsements.
