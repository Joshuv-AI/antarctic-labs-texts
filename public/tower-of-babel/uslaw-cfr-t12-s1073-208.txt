12 C.F.R. § 1073.208 — Refunds.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1073.208
Creator: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1073.208
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1073 (Procedures For Bureau Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Amounts recovered by administrative offset but later found not to be owed to the Government shall be promptly refunded. Unless required by law or contract, such refunds shall not bear interest.
