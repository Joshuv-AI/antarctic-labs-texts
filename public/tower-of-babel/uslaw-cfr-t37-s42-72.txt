37 C.F.R. § 42.72 — Termination of trial.
Citation: us:cfr:37:42.72
Creator: United States Patent And Trademark Office, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-42.72
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 42 (Trial Practice Before The Patent Trial And Appeal Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board may terminate a trial without rendering a final written decision, where appropriate, including where the trial is consolidated with another proceeding or pursuant to a joint request under 35 U.S.C. 317(a) or 327(a).
