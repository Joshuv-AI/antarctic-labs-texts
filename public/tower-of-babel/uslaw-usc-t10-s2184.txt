10 U.S.C. § 2184 — Termination of assistance
Citation: us:usc:10:2184
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section2184
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Training And Education); Chapter 110 (Educational Assistance For Members Held As Captives And Their Dependents). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Assistance under this chapter—
(1)
shall be discontinued for any person whose conduct or progress is unsatisfactory under standards consistent with those established under section 3524 of title 38; and
(2)
may not be provided for any person for more than 45 months (or the equivalent in other than full-time education or training).
