46 C.F.R. § 141.370 — Miscellaneous life saving requirements for towing vessels.
Citation: us:cfr:46:141.370
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-141.370
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 141 (Lifesaving). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Miscellaneous lifesaving requirements are summarized in Table 141.370 of this section. Equipment requirements are based on the area in which a towing vessel is operating, not the route for which it is certificated.
