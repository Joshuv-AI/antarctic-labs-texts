7 C.F.R. § 500.12 — Weapons and explosives.
Citation: us:cfr:7:500.12
Creator: Agricultural Research Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-500.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 500 (National Arboretum). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) No person while in or on USNA property shall carry firearms, other dangerous or deadly weapons, or explosives, either openly or concealed, except for authorized official purposes.
(b) No person while in or on the USNA shall ignite fireworks or other pyrotechnical devices.
