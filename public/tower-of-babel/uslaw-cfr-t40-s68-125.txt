40 C.F.R. § 68.125 — Exemptions.
Citation: us:cfr:40:68.125
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-68.125
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 68 (Chemical Accident Prevention Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agricultural nutrients. Ammonia used as an agricultural nutrient, when held by farmers, is exempt from all provisions of this part.
