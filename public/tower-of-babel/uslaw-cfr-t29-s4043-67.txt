29 C.F.R. § 4043.67 — Loan default.
Citation: us:cfr:29:4043.67
Creator: Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-4043.67
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 4043 (Reportable Events And Certain Other Notification Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Advance notice is required for an acceleration of payment, a default, a waiver, or an agreement to an amendment with respect to a loan agreement described in § 4043.34(a).
