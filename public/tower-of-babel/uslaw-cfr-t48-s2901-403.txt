48 C.F.R. § 2901.403 — 2901.403 Individual deviations.

Creator: Chapter 29—Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-2901.403
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2901 (Department Of Labor Acquisition Regulation System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Individual deviations affect only one contract action. Except for individual deviations referenced in FAR 1.405(e), the SPE is authorized to approve individual deviations from FAR provisions (see FAR 1.403) or from DOLAR provisions.
