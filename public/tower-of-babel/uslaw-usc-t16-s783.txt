16 U.S.C. § 783 — Punishment for violations of law; liability of vessels
Citation: us:usc:16:783
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section783
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 11 (Regulation Of Landing, Curing, And Sale Of Sponges Taken From Gulf Of Mexico And Straits Of Florida). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Every person, partnership, or association guilty of a violation of the provisions of this chapter shall be liable to a fine of not more than $500, and in addition such fine shall be a lien against the vessel or boat on which the offense is committed, and said vessel or boat shall be seized and proceeded against by process of libel in any court having jurisdiction of the offense.
