30 C.F.R. § 1219.103 — How will ONRR manage payments to Indian accounts?
Citation: us:cfr:30:1219.103
Creator: Office Of Natural Resources Revenue, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-1219.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 1219 (Distribution And Disbursement Of Royalties, Rentals, And Bonuses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

ONRR will transfer mineral revenues received from Indian leases to the appropriate Indian accounts that the Bureau of Indian Affairs (BIA) manages for allotted and Tribal revenues. These accounts are specifically designated Treasury accounts. ONRR will transfer these revenues to the Indian accounts at the earliest practicable date after such funds are received, but in no case later than the last business day of the month in which ONRR receives these revenues.
