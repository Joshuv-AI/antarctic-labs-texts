21 U.S.C. § 951 — Definitions

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 21 (Food And Drugs); Chapter 13 (Drug Abuse Prevention And Control); Subchapter II (Import And Export). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
For purposes of this subchapter—
(1)
The term “import” means, with respect to any article, any bringing in or introduction of such article into any area (whether or not such bringing in or introduction constitutes an importation within the meaning of the tariff laws of the United States).
(2)
The term “customs territory of the United States” has the meaning assigned to such term by general note 2 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States.
(b)
Each term defined in section 802 of this title shall have the same meaning for purposes of this subchapter as such term has for purposes of subchapter I.
