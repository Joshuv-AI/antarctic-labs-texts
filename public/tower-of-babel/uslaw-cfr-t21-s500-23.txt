21 C.F.R. § 500.23 — Thermally processed low-acid foods packaged in hermetically sealed containers.
Citation: us:cfr:21:500.23
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-500.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 500 (General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided in § 507.5(b) of this chapter, the provisions of parts 507 and 113 of this chapter apply to the manufacturing, processing, or packing of low-acid foods in hermetically sealed containers, and intended for use as food for animals.
