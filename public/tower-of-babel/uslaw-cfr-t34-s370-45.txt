34 C.F.R. § 370.45 — What limitation applies to the pursuit of legal remedies?
Citation: us:cfr:34:370.45
Creator: Office Of Special Education And Rehabilitative Services, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-370.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 370 (Client Assistance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A designated agency may not bring any class action in carrying out its responsibilities under this part.
