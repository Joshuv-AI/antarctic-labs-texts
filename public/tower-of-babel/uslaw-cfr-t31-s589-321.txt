31 C.F.R. § 589.321 — Interest.
Citation: us:cfr:31:589.321
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-589.321
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 589 (Ukraine-/Russia-Related Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise provided in this part, the term interest, when used with respect to property (e.g., “an interest in property”), means an interest of any nature whatsoever, direct or indirect.
