48 C.F.R. § 570.117-1 — 570.117-1 Federal leadership in environmental, energy, and economic performance.
Citation: us:cfr:48:570.117-1
Creator: Chapter 5—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-570.117-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 570 (Acquiring Leasehold Interests In Real Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In order to create a clean energy economy that will increase our Nation's prosperity, promote energy security, protect the interests of taxpayers, and safeguard the health of our environment, GSA will accomplish all sustainability requirements described in 570.117-2 that apply to lease acquisitions.
