7 C.F.R. § 2.400 — Deputy Chief Information Officer.
Citation: us:cfr:7:2.400
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-2.400
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 2 (Delegations Of Authority By The Secretary Of Agriculture And General Officers Of The Department). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Pursuant to § 2.89, the following delegation of authority is made by the Chief Information Officer to the Deputy Chief Information Officer, to be exercised only during the absence or unavailability of the Chief Information Officer: Perform all duties and exercise all powers which are now or which may hereafter be delegated to the Chief Information Officer.
