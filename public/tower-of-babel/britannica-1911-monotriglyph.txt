MONOTRIGLYPH, in architecture, the interval of the
intercolumniation of the Doric column, which is observed by the
intervention of one triglyph only between the triglyphs which
come over the axes of the columns. This is the usual arrangement,
but in the Propylaea at Athens there are two triglyphs
over the central intercolumniation, in order to give increased
width to the roadway, up which chariots and beasts of sacrifice
ascended.
