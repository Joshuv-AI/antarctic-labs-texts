31 C.F.R. § 3.4 — Approval of claims not in excess of $25,000.
Citation: us:cfr:31:3.4
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-3.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 3 (Claims Regulations And Indemnification Of Department Of Treasury Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Claims not exceeding $25,000 and not otherwise requiring consultation with the Department of Justice pursuant to 28 CFR 14.6(b) shall be approved, disapproved, or compromised by the head of the bureau or office or his designee, taking into consideration the recommendation of the legal division.
