49 U.S.C. § 41908 — Effect on foreign postal arrangements
Citation: us:usc:49:41908
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section41908
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part A (Air Commerce And Safety); Subpart ii (Economic Regulation); Chapter 419 (Transportation Of Mail). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This part does not—
(1)
affect an arrangement made by the United States Government with the postal administration of a foreign country related to the transportation of mail by aircraft; or
(2)
impair the authority of the United States Postal Service to make such an arrangement.
