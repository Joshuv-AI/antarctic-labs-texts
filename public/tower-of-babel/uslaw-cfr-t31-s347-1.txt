31 C.F.R. § 347.1 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:31:347.1
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-347.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 347 (Regulations Governing Retirement Savings Bonds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part apply to retirement savings bonds issued, on behalf of participants, to the IRA custodian for Treasury's retirement savings program and to the Auto-IRA custodians for state Auto-IRA programs.
