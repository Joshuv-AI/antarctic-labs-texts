36 C.F.R. § 1008.5 — Federal Register notices describing systems of records.
Citation: us:cfr:36:1008.5
Creator: Presidio Trust
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1008.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1008 (Requests Under The Privacy Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Privacy Act requires publication of a notice in the Federal Register describing each system of records subject to the Privacy Act. Such notice will be published prior to the establishment or a revision of the system of records. 5 U.S.C. 552a(e)(4).
