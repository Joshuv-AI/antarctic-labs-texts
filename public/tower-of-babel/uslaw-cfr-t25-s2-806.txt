25 C.F.R. § 2.806 — What must my Objection to the Statement of Performance contain?
Citation: us:cfr:25:2.806
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-2.806
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 2 (Appeals From Administrative Decisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Your Objection to the Statement of Performance must be in writing and contain all of the following:
(a) Your name, address, and telephone number;
(b) The statement date of the specific Statement of Performance that you are challenging;
(c) A copy of the Statement of Performance being challenged; and
(d) The Basis of Objection.
