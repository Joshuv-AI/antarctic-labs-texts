23 C.F.R. § 1225.10 — Apportionment of withheld funds after compliance.
Citation: us:cfr:23:1225.10
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration And Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-1225.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 1225 (Operation Of Motor Vehicles By Intoxicated Persons). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a State has not met the requirements of 23 U.S.C. 163 and § 1225.4 of this part by October 1, 2007, the funds withheld under § 1225.8 shall begin to lapse and will no longer be available for apportionment to the State, in accordance with appendix A of this part.
