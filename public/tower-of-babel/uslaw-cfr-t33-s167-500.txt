33 C.F.R. § 167.500 — In the approaches to Los Angeles-Long Beach Traffic Separation Scheme: General.
Citation: us:cfr:33:167.500
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-167.500
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 167 (Offshore Traffic Separation Schemes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Traffic Separation Scheme in the approaches to Los Angeles-Long Beach consists of three parts: a Precautionary Area, a Western Approach, and a Southern Approach. The specific areas in the approaches to Los Angeles-Long Beach are described in §§ 167.501 through 167.503. The geographic coordinates in §§ 167.501 through 167.503 are defined using North American Datum 1983 (NAD 83).
