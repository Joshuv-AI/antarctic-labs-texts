42 U.S.C. § 7385e — Certification of treatment of payments under other laws
Citation: us:usc:42:7385e
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section7385e
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 84 (Department Of Energy); Subchapter XVI (Energy Employees Occupational Illness Compensation Program); Part C (Treatment, Coordination, And Forfeiture Of Compensation And Benefits). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Compensation or benefits provided to an individual under this subchapter—
(1)
shall be treated for purposes of the internal revenue laws of the United States as damages for human suffering; and
(2)
shall not be included as income or resources for purposes of determining eligibility to receive benefits described in section 3803(c)(2)(C) of title 31 or the amount of such benefits.
