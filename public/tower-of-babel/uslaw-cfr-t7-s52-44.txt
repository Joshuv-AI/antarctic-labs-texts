7 C.F.R. § 52.44 — Inspection fees when charges for sampling have been collected.
Citation: us:cfr:7:52.44
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-52.44
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 52 (Processed Fruits And Vegetables, Processed Products Thereof, And Certain Other Processed Food Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For any lot of processed products from which a sample in drawn by a licensed sampler and the applicable sampling fee is collected, as provided in § 52.43, the fees for the other inspection services with respect to such lot shall not include charges for sampling.
