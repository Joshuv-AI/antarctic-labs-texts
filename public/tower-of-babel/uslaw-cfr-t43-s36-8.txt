43 C.F.R. § 36.8 — Administrative appeals.
Citation: us:cfr:43:36.8
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-36.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 36 (Transportation And Utility Systems In And Across, And Access Into, Conservation System Units In Alaska). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If any appropriate Federal agency disapproves a TUS application pursuant to § 36.7(a), the applicant may appeal the denial pursuant to section 1106(a) of ANILCA.
(b) There is no administrative appeal for a denial issued under the provisions of § 36.7(b).
