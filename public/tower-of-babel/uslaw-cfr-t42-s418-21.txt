42 C.F.R. § 418.21 — Duration of hospice care coverage—Election periods.
Citation: us:cfr:42:418.21
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-418.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 418 (Hospice Care). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Subject to the conditions set forth in this part, an individual may elect to receive hospice care during one or more of the following election periods:
(1) An initial 90-day period;
(2) A subsequent 90-day period; or
(3) An unlimited number of subsequent 60-day periods.
(b) The periods of care are available in the order listed and may be elected separately at different times.
