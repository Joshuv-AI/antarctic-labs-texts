22 C.F.R. § 71.6 — Services for distressed Americans.
Citation: us:cfr:22:71.6
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-71.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 71 (Protection And Welfare Of Citizens And Their Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Diplomatic or consular officers of the United States shall extend every possible aid and assistance within their power to distressed American citizens within their districts, but they shall not expend the funds nor pledge the credit of the Government of the United States for this purpose, except in the case of American seamen, or except as authorized by the Department of State.
