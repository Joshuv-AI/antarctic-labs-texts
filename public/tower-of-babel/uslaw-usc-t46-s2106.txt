46 U.S.C. § 2106 — Liability in rem
Citation: us:usc:46:2106
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section2106
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part A (General Provisions); Chapter 21 (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When a vessel is made liable in rem under this subtitle, the vessel may be libeled and proceeded against in the district court of the United States for any district in which the vessel is found.
