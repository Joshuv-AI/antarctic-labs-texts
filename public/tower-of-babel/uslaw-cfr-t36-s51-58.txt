36 C.F.R. § 51.58 — What actions may or must the concessioner take with respect to a leasehold surrender interest?
Citation: us:cfr:36:51.58
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-51.58
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 51 (Concession Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The concessioner:
(a) May encumber a leasehold surrender interest in accordance with this part, but only for the purposes specified in this part;
(b) Where applicable, must transfer in accordance with this part its leasehold surrender interest in connection with any assignment, termination or expiration of the concession contract; and
(c) May relinquish or waive a leasehold surrender interest.
