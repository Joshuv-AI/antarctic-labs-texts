2 U.S.C. § 4560 — Deductions by Chief Administrative Officer in disbursement of gratuity appropriations
Citation: us:usc:2:4560
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section4560
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 45 (Congressional Pay And Benefits); Subchapter II (House Of Representatives); Part B (Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Chief Administrative Officer of the House of Representatives is authorized, in the disbursement of gratuity appropriations, to make deductions of such amounts as may be due to or through his office or as may be due the House of Representatives.
