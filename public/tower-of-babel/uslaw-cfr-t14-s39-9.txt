14 C.F.R. § 39.9 — What if I operate an aircraft or use a product that does not meet the requirements of an airworthiness directive?
Citation: us:cfr:14:39.9
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-39.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 39 (Airworthiness Directives). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the requirements of an airworthiness directive have not been met, you violate § 39.7 each time you operate the aircraft or use the product.
