10 U.S.C. § 10112 — Air National Guard: when a component of the Air Force
Citation: us:usc:10:10112
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section10112
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization And Administration); Chapter 1003 (Reserve Components Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Air National Guard while in the service of the United States is a component of the Air Force.
