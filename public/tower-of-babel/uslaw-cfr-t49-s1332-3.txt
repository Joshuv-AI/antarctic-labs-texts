49 C.F.R. § 1332.3 — Manner of submitting contracts.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1332.3
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1332.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1332 (Filing Contracts For Surface Mail Transportation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The U.S. Postal Service will submit to the Board, upon request, a copy of the requested contract(s) or agreement(s). Such contract(s) or agreement(s) will be submitted by facsimile transmission or messenger service where feasible, and, where such services are not feasible, by the fastest available mail service.
