15 U.S.C. § 202 — Repairs to standards
Citation: us:usc:15:202
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section202
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 6 (Weights And Measures And Standard Time); Subchapter I (Weights, Measures, And Standards Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Such necessary repairs and adjustments shall be made to the standards furnished to the several States and Territories as may be requested by the governors thereof, and also to standard weights and measures that have been or may be supplied to United States customhouses and other offices of the United States under Act of Congress, when requested by the Secretary of Commerce.
