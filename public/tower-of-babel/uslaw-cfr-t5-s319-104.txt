5 C.F.R. § 319.104 — Applicable instructions.
Citation: us:cfr:5:319.104
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-319.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 319 (Employment In Senior-Level And Scientific And Professional Positions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Provisions in statute, Executive order, or regulations that relate in general to competitive and excepted service positions and employment apply to positions and employment under the SL and ST systems unless there is a specific provision to the contrary.
