20 U.S.C. § 7942 — Purpose
Citation: us:usc:20:7942
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section7942
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 70 (Strengthening And Improvement Of Elementary And Secondary Schools); Subchapter VIII (General Provisions); Part F (Uniform Provisions); Subpart 3 (Teacher Liability Protection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purpose of this subpart is to provide teachers, principals, and other school professionals the tools they need to undertake reasonable actions to maintain order, discipline, and an appropriate educational environment.
