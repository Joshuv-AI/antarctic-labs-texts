49 C.F.R. § 800.28 — Delegation to the Chief Financial Officer.
Citation: us:cfr:49:800.28
Creator: National Transportation Safety Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-800.28
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 800 (Administrative Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board delegates to the Chief Financial Officer the authority to settle claims for money damages of $2,500 or less against the United States arising under Section 2672 of 28 United States Code (the Federal Tort Claims Act) because of acts or omissions of Board employees.
