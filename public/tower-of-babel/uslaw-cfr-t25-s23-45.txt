25 C.F.R. § 23.45 — Subgrants.
Citation: us:cfr:25:23.45
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-23.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 23 (Indian Child Welfare Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A tribal government grantee may make a subgrant under subpart C of this part, provided that such subgrants are for the purpose for which the grant was made and that the grantee retains administrative and financial responsibility over the activity and the funds.
