Nanoarchitecture‐Integrated Hydrogel Boosts Angiogenesis–Osteogenesis–Neurogenesis Tripling for Infected Bone Fracture Healing (Adv. Sci. 43/2024)
Authors: Kangkang Zha, Weixian Hu, Yuan Xiong, Shengming Zhang, Meijun Tan, Pengzhen Bu, Yanzhi Zhao, Wenqian Zhang, Ze Lin, Yiqiang Hu, Mohammad‐Ali Shahbazi, Qian Feng, Guohui Liu, Bobin Mi
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
Infected Fracture Healing In article number 2406439, Qian Feng, Guohui Liu, Bobin Mi, and co‐workers develop a pH‐responsive nanoarchitecture‐integrated hydrogel to treat bacteria‐infected fracture through controlled release of humic acids, Zn2+, and Ga3+. This hydrogel promotes angiogenesis, osteogenesis, and neurogenesis. The enhanced neurogenesis further promotes angiogenesis and osteogenesis, forming a mutually supportive angiogenesis‐osteogenesis‐neurogenesis cycle at the fracture site, proving a promising whole‐course‐repair strategy for infected fracture healing.
