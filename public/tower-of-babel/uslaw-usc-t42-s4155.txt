42 U.S.C. § 4155 — Effective date of standards
Citation: us:usc:42:4155
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section4155
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 51 (Design And Construction Of Public Buildings To Accommodate Physically Handicapped). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Every building designed, constructed, or altered after the effective date of a standard issued under this chapter which is applicable to such building, shall be designed, constructed, or altered in accordance with such standard.
