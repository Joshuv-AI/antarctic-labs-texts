42 U.S.C. § 4908 — Imports
Citation: us:usc:42:4908
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section4908
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 65 (Noise Control). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Treasury shall, in consultation with the Administrator, issue regulations to carry out the provisions of this chapter with respect to new products imported or offered for importation.
