52 U.S.C. § 30144 — Partial invalidity
Citation: us:usc:52:30144
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title52-section30144
Context: United States Code, Title 52 (Voting And Elections); Chapter 301 (Federal Election Campaigns); Subchapter II (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If any provision of this Act, or the application thereof to any person or circumstance, is held invalid, the validity of the remainder of the Act and the application of such provision to other persons and circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
