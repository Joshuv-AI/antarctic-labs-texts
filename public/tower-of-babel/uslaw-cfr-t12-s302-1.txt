12 C.F.R. § 302.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:12:302.1
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-302.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 302 (Regulations Governing Bank Supervision). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The FDIC issues regulations and guidance as part of its supervisory function. This subpart reiterates the distinctions between regulations and guidance, as stated in the Statement Clarifying the Role of Supervisory Guidance (appendix A to this part) (Statement).
