1 C.F.R. § 20.4 — Description of program activities.
Citation: us:cfr:1:20.4
Creator: Administrative Committee Of The Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-1/section-20.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 1 (General Provisions), Part 20 (Handling Of The United States Government Manual Statements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-23 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Descriptions should clearly state the public purposes that the agency serves, and the programs that carry out those purposes.
(b) Descriptions of the responsibilities of individuals or of administrative units common to most agencies will not be accepted for publication in the Manual.
