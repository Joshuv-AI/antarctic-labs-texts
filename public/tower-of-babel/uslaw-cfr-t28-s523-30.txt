28 C.F.R. § 523.30 — What is educational good time sentence credit?
Citation: us:cfr:28:523.30
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-523.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 523 (Computation Of Sentence). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Educational good time sentence credit is authorized by District of Columbia (D.C.) Code § 24-221.01, and reduces the amount of time to serve under a term of imprisonment. In these rules, we refer to D.C. educational good time as “DCEGT.”
