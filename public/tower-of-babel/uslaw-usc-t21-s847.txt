21 U.S.C. § 847 — Additional penalties
Citation: us:usc:21:847
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title21-section847
Context: United States Code, Title 21 (Food And Drugs); Chapter 13 (Drug Abuse Prevention And Control); Subchapter I (Control And Enforcement); Part D (Offenses And Penalties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any penalty imposed for violation of this subchapter shall be in addition to, and not in lieu of, any civil or administrative penalty or sanction authorized by law.
