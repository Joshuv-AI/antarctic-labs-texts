22 C.F.R. § 150.3 — Severability.
Citation: us:cfr:22:150.3
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-150.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 150 (Diplomatic Agent-Level Immunity). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this part are separate and severable from one another. If any provision is stayed or determined to be invalid, it is the Department of State's intention that the remaining provisions shall continue in effect.
