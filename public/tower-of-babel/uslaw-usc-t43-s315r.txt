43 U.S.C. § 315r — Rental payments in advance in case of withdrawal of lands for war or national defense purposes
Citation: us:usc:43:315r
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section315r
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 8A (Grazing Lands); Subchapter I (Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In administering the provisions of section 315q of this title, payments of rentals may be made in advance.
