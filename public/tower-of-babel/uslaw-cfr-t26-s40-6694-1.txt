26 C.F.R. § 40.6694-1 — -1 Section 6694 penalties applicable to tax return preparer.

Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 40 (Excise Tax Procedural Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) In general. For general definitions regarding section 6694 penalties applicable to preparers of returns or claims for refund of any tax to which this part 40 applies, see § 1.6694-1 of this chapter.
(b) Effective/applicability date. This section is applicable to returns and claims for refund filed, and advice provided, after December 31, 2008.
