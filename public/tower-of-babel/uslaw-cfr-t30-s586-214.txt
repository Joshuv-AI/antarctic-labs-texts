30 C.F.R. § 586.214 — When will BOEM suspend an Alternate Use RUE?
Citation: us:cfr:30:586.214
Creator: Bureau Of Ocean Energy Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-586.214
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 586 (Alternate Uses Of Existing Facilities On The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) BOEM may suspend an Alternate Use RUE if:
(1) necessary to comply with judicial decrees; or
(2) necessary for reasons of national security or defense.
(b) A suspension will extend the term of your Alternate Use RUE grant for the period of the suspension.
