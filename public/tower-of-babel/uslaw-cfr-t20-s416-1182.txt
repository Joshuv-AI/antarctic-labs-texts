20 C.F.R. § 416.1182 — When we begin to count the income excluded under the plan.
Citation: us:cfr:20:416.1182
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-416.1182
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 416 (Supplemental Security Income For The Aged, Blind, And Disabled). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We will begin to count the earned and unearned income that would have been excluded under your plan in the month in which any of the following circumstances first exist:
(a) You fail to follow the conditions of your plan;
(b) You abandon your plan;
(c) You complete the time schedule outlined in the plan; or
(d) You reach your goal as outlined in the plan.
