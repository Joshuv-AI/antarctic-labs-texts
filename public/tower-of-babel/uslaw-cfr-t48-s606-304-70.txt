48 C.F.R. § 606.304-70 — 606.304-70 Acquisitions by overseas posts.
Citation: us:cfr:48:606.304-70
Creator: Chapter 6—Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-606.304-70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 606 (Competition Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Departmental Advocate for Competition is the approval authority for the purposes of FAR 6.304(a)(3). This authority is not redelegable. Any such justification must be transmitted through the Principal Officer at the overseas post.
