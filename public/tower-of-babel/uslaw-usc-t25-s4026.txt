25 U.S.C. § 4026 — Return of withdrawn funds
Citation: us:usc:25:4026
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section4026
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 42 (American Indian Trust Fund Management Reform); Subchapter II (Indian Trust Fund Management Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Subject to such conditions as the Secretary may prescribe, any Indian tribe which has withdrawn trust funds may choose to return any or all of the trust funds such tribe has withdrawn by notifying the Secretary in writing of its intention to return the funds to the control and management of the Secretary.
