31 C.F.R. § 316.11 — Reservation as to issue of bonds.
Citation: us:cfr:31:316.11
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-316.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 316 (Offering Of United States Savings Bonds, Series E). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary of the Treasury reserved the right to reject any application for purchase of Series E bonds, in whole or in part, and to refuse to issue, or permit to be issued hereunder, any such bonds in any case or any class or classes of cases if such action was deemed to be in the public interest. Any action in any such respect was final.
