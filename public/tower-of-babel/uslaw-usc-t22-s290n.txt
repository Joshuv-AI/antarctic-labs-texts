22 U.S.C. § 290n — Appointment of members of Border Health Commission
Citation: us:usc:22:290n
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section290n
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 7 (International Bureaus, Congresses, Etc.); Subchapter XXIX (United States-Mexico Border Health Commission). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 30 days after November 29, 1999, the President shall appoint the United States members of the United States-Mexico Border Health Commission, and shall attempt to conclude an agreement with Mexico providing for the establishment of such Commission.
