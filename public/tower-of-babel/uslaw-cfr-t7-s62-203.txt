7 C.F.R. § 62.203 — How to withdraw application for service.
Citation: us:cfr:7:62.203
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-62.203
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 62 (Agricultural Marketing Service Audit Verification And Accreditation Programs (Avaap)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An application for service may be withdrawn, all or in part, by the applicant at any time; Provided, That the applicant notifies the USDA service office in writing of its desire to withdraw the application for service and pays any expenses USDA has incurred in connection with such application.
