29 C.F.R. § 784.112 — Substantial amounts of nonaquatic products; enforcement policy.
Citation: us:cfr:29:784.112
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-784.112
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 784 (Provisions Of The Fair Labor Standards Act Applicable To Fishing And Operations On Aquatic Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As an enforcement policy in applying the principles stated in §§ 784.110 and 784.111, if more than 20 percent of a commodity consists of products other than aquatic products named in section 13(a)(5) or 13(b)(4), the commodity will be deemed to contain a substantial amount of such nonaquatic products.
