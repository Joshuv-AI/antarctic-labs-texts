45 C.F.R. § 30.6 — Form of payment.
Citation: us:cfr:45:30.6
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-30.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 30 (Claims Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Claims may be paid in the form of money or, when a contractual basis exists, the Department may demand the return of specific property or the performance of specific services.
