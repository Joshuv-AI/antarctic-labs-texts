19 C.F.R. § 18.45 — Supervision of exportation.
Citation: us:cfr:19:18.45
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-18.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 18 (Transportation In Bond And Merchandise In Transit). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of §§ 18.41 through 18.44 do not require the director of the port of actual exportation to verify that merchandise moving under cover of a TIR carnet is loaded on board the exporting carrier.
