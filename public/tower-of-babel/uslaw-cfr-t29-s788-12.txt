29 C.F.R. § 788.12 — Limitation of exemption to specific operations in which “number of employees * * * does not exceed eight.”
Citation: us:cfr:29:788.12
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-788.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 788 (Forestry Or Logging Operations In Which Not More Than Eight Employees Are Employed). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Regardless of his duties, no employee is exempt under section 13(a)(13) unless “the number of employees employed by his employer in such forestry or lumbering operations does not exceed eight.”
