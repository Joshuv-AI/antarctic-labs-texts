2 U.S.C. § 4916 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:2:4916
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section4916
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 49 (Congressional Pages); Subchapter II (House Of Representatives). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in sections 4914 to 4916 of this title, the term—
(1)
“academic year” means a regular school year, consisting of two semesters;
(2)
“page” means a page of the House of Representatives, but such term does not include a full time, permanent employee of the House of Representatives with supervisory responsibility for pages; and
(3)
“congressional page” means a page of the House of Representatives or the Senate.
