36 U.S.C. § 150504 — Membership
Citation: us:usc:36:150504
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section150504
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1505 (National Conference Of State Societies, Washington, District Of Columbia). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The membership of the corporation consists of the members of the State and territorial societies in the District of Columbia. Except as provided in this chapter, eligibility for membership in the corporation and the rights and privileges of members are as provided in the bylaws and regulations of the corporation.
