36 C.F.R. § 52.12 — How long will respondents have to submit a response?
Citation: us:cfr:36:52.12
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-52.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 52 (Visitor Experience Improvements Authority Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director will define the process and the timeline for responding and entering into negotiations in the request for proposals. The Director will not consider untimely responses.
