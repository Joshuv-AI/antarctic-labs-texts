43 C.F.R. § 3142.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3142.1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3142.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3140 (Leasing In Special Tar Sand Areas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart provides definitions and procedures for meeting the production in paying quantities and the diligent development requirements for tar sand in all combined hydrocarbon leases and tar sand leases.
