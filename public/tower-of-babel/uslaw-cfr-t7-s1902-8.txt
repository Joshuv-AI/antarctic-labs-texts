7 C.F.R. § 1902.8 — Authority to establish and administer supervised bank accounts.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1902.8
Creator: Rural Housing Service, Rural Business-Cooperative Service, And Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1902.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1902 (Supervised Bank Accounts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Servicing Officials are authorized to establish supervised bank accounts, deposit loan checks and other funds, countersign checks, close accounts, and execute all forms in connection with supervised bank account transactions and redelegate this authority to a person under their supervision who is considered capable of exercising such authority. State Directors will make written demand upon the bank for withdrawals outlined in § 1902.16.
