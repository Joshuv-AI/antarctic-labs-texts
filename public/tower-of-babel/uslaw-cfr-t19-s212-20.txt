19 C.F.R. § 212.20 — Filing and service of documents.
Citation: us:cfr:19:212.20
Creator: United States International Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-212.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 212 (Implementation Of The Equal Access To Justice Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any application for an award or other pleading or document related to an application shall be filed and served on all parties to the adversary adjudication in the same manner as other pleadings in the adversary adjudication, except as provided in § 212.11(b) for confidential financial information.
