32 U.S.C. § 111 — Suspension of certain provisions of this title
Citation: us:usc:32:111
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title32-section111
Context: United States Code, Title 32 (National Guard); Chapter 1 (Organization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In time of war, or of emergency declared by Congress, the President may suspend the operation of any provision of sections 307(e), 309, 310, and 323(d) and (e) 1 of this title with respect to the Army National Guard or the Air National Guard.
