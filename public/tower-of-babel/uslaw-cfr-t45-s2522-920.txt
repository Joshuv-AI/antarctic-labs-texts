45 C.F.R. § 2522.920 — Are there any exceptions to the qualifications requirements?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2522.920
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2522.920
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2522 (Americorps Participants, Programs, And Applicants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The qualifications requirements in § 2522.910 of this subpart do not apply to a member who is a K-12 student tutoring younger children in the school or after school as part of a structured, school-managed cross-grade tutoring program.
