32 C.F.R. § 2004.28 — Cost reports.
Citation: us:cfr:32:2004.28
Creator: Information Security Oversight Office, National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-2004.28
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 2004 (National Industrial Security Program (Nisp)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Agencies must annually report to the Director, ISOO, on their NISP implementation costs for the previous year.
(b) CSAs must annually collect information on NISP implementation costs incurred by entities under their cognizance and submit a report to the Director, ISOO.
