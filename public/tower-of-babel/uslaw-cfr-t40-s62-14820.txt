40 C.F.R. § 62.14820 — How must I monitor opacity for air curtain incinerators that burn 100 percent wood wastes, clean lumber, and/or yard waste?
Citation: us:cfr:40:62.14820
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-62.14820
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 62 (Approval And Promulgation Of State Plans For Designated Facilities And Pollutants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Use Method 9 of 40 CFR part 60, appendix A to determine compliance with the opacity limitation.
(b) Conduct an initial test for opacity as specified in § 60.8 no later than January 2, 2005.
(c) After the initial test for opacity, conduct annual tests no more than 12 calendar months following the date of your previous test.
