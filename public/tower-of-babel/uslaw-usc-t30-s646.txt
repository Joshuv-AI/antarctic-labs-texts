30 U.S.C. § 646 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:30:646
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section646
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 17 (Exploration Program For Discovery Of Minerals). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are hereby authorized to be appropriated, from any funds in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, such sums as may be necessary to carry out the provisions of this chapter.
