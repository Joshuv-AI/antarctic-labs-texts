33 C.F.R. § 106.135 — Alternative Security Program.
Citation: us:cfr:33:106.135
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-106.135
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 106 (Marine Security: Outer Continental Shelf (Ocs) Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An OCS facility owner or operator may use an Alternative Security Program approved under § 101.120 of this subchapter if:
(a) The Alternative Security Program is appropriate to that OCS facility;
(b) The OCS facility does not serve vessels on international voyages; and
(c) The Alternative Security Program is implemented in its entirety.
