21 U.S.C. § 862b — Sanctioning for testing positive for controlled substances
Citation: us:usc:21:862b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title21-section862b
Context: United States Code, Title 21 (Food And Drugs); Chapter 13 (Drug Abuse Prevention And Control); Subchapter I (Control And Enforcement); Part D (Offenses And Penalties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, States shall not be prohibited by the Federal Government from testing welfare recipients for use of controlled substances nor from sanctioning welfare recipients who test positive for use of controlled substances.
