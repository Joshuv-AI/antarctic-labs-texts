2 C.F.R. § 417.210 — Which nonprocurement transactions are covered transactions?
Citation: us:cfr:2:417.210
Creator: Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-417.210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 417 (Nonprocurement Debarment And Suspension). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All nonprocurement transactions, as defined in 2 CFR 180.970, are covered transactions unless listed in § 417.215.
