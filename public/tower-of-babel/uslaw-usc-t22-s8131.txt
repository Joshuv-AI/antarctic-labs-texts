22 U.S.C. § 8131 — Protection of confidentiality of information
Citation: us:usc:22:8131
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section8131
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 88 (Nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty—United States Additional Protocol Implementation); Subchapter III (Confidentiality Of Information). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Information reported to, or otherwise acquired by, the United States Government under this chapter or under the Additional Protocol shall be exempt from disclosure under section 552 of title 5.
