42 U.S.C. § 2297g–4 — Reports to Congress

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2297g–4
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 23 (Development And Control Of Atomic Energy); Subchapter VII (Decontamination And Decommissioning). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Within 3 years after October 24, 1992, and at least once every 3 years thereafter, the Secretary shall report to the Congress on progress under this subchapter. The 5th report submitted under this section shall contain recommendations of the Secretary for the reauthorization of the program and Fund under this division.
