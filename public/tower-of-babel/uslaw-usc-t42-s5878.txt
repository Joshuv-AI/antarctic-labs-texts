42 U.S.C. § 5878 — Information to Congressional committees
Citation: us:usc:42:5878
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section5878
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 73 (Development Of Energy Sources); Subchapter III (Miscellaneous And Transitional Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator shall keep the appropriate congressional committees fully and currently informed with respect to all of the Administration’s activities.
