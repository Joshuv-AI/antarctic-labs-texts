48 C.F.R. § 2817.204 — 2817.204 Contracts.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2817 (Special Contracting Methods). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Deviation requests to exceed the 5-year limitations specified in FAR 17.204(e) require advance approval from—
(a) The HCA or designee for individual contracts; and
(b) The SPE for classes of contracts.
