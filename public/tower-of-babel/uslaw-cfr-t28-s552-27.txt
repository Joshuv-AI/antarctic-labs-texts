28 C.F.R. § 552.27 — Documentation of use of force and application of restraints incidents.
Citation: us:cfr:28:552.27
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-552.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 552 (Custody). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Staff shall appropriately document all incidents involving the use of force, chemical agents, or less-than-lethal weapons. Staff shall also document, in writing, the use of restraints on an inmate who becomes violent or displays signs of imminent violence. A copy of the report shall be placed in the inmate's central file.
