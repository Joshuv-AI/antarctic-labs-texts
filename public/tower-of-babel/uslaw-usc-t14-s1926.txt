14 U.S.C. § 1926 — Cadet applicants; preappointment travel to Academy
Citation: us:usc:14:1926
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section1926
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 19 (Coast Guard Academy); Subchapter II (Cadets). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized to expend appropriated funds for selective preappointment travel to the Academy for orientation visits of cadet applicants.
