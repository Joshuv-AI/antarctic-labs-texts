43 C.F.R. § 4730.1 — Destruction.
Citation: us:cfr:43:4730.1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4730.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4700 (Protection, Management, And Control Of Wild Free-Roaming Horses And Burros). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as an act of mercy, no wild horse or burro shall be destroyed without the authorization of the authorized officer. Old, sick, or lame animals shall be destroyed in the most humane manner possible. Excess animals for which adoption demand does not exist shall be destroyed in the most humane and cost efficient manner possible.
