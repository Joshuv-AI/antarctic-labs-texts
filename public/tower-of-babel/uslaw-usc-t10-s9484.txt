10 U.S.C. § 9484 — Quartermaster and ordnance property: sales
Citation: us:usc:10:9484
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section9484
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Training); Chapter 957 (Schools And Camps). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Air Force may sell to a person attending a school or camp established under section 9481 of this title quartermaster and ordnance property necessary for his proper equipment. Sales under this section shall be for cash.
