50 C.F.R. § 27.62 — Search for and removal of objects of antiquity.
Citation: us:cfr:50:27.62
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-27.62
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 27 (Prohibited Acts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person shall search for or remove from national wildlife refuges objects of antiquity except as may be authorized by 43 CFR part 3.
