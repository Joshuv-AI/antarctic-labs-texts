49 C.F.R. § 244.3 — Preemptive effect.
Citation: us:cfr:49:244.3

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 244 (Regulations On Safety Integration Plans Governing Railroad Consolidations, Mergers, And Acquisitions Of Control). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Under 49 U.S.C. 20106, issuance of these regulations preempts any State law, regulation, or order covering the same subject matter, except an additional or more stringent law, regulation, or order that:
(a) Is necessary to eliminate or reduce an essentially local safety hazard;
(b) Is not incompatible with a law, regulation, or order of the United States Government; and
(c) Does not unreasonably burden interstate commerce.
