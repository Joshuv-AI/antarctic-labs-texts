45 C.F.R. § 2553.103 — Who develops the performance measures?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2553.103
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2553.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2553 (The Retired And Senior Volunteer Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) AmeriCorps may establish performance measures that will apply to RSVP projects, which sponsors will be responsible for meeting.
(b) An applicant is responsible for choosing its own project specific performance measures.
