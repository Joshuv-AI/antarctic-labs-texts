11 C.F.R. § 9004.1 — Major parties.
Citation: us:cfr:11:9004.1
Creator: Federal Election Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-11/section-9004.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 11 (Federal Elections), Part 9004 (Entitlement Of Eligible Candidates To Payments; Use Of Payments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The eligible candidates of each major party in a Presidential election shall be entitled to equal payments under 11 CFR part 9005 in an amount which, in the aggregate, shall not exceed $20,000,000 as adjusted by the Consumer Price Index in the manner described in 11 CFR 110.17(a).
