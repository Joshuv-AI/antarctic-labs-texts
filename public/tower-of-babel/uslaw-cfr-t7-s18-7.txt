7 C.F.R. § 18.7 — Reports.
Citation: us:cfr:7:18.7
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-18.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 18 (Equal Employment Opportunity In The State Cooperative Extension Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Within 6 months of the program going into effect and thereafter at least annually, the President shall submit a summary report to the Secretary on implementation and operation of the program. The Secretary may request additional reports as he deems advisable.
