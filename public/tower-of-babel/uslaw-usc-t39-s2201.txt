39 U.S.C. § 2201 — No postal equipment or supplies manufactured by convict labor
Citation: us:usc:39:2201
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title39-section2201
Context: United States Code, Title 39 (Postal Service); Part III (Modernization And Fiscal Administration); Chapter 22 (Convict Labor). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as provided in chapter 307 of title 18, the Postal Service may not make a contract for the purchase of equipment or supplies to be manufactured by convict labor.
