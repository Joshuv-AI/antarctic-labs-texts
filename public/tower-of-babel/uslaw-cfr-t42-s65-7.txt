42 C.F.R. § 65.7 — For what purposes may grant funds be spent?
Citation: us:cfr:42:65.7
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-65.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 65 (National Institute Of Environmental Health Sciences Hazardous Waste Worker Training). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Individuals receiving training shall be entitled only to the stipends and allowances included in a budget approved by the Director, taking into account the cost of living and such other factors as the needs of the program and the availability of funds.
