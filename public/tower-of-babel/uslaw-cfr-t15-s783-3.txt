15 C.F.R. § 783.3 — Reports containing information determined by BIS not to be required by the APR.
Citation: us:cfr:15:783.3
Creator: Bureau Of Industry And Security, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-783.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 783 (Civil Nuclear Fuel Cycle-Related Activities Not Involving Nuclear Materials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you submit a report and BIS determines that none of the information contained therein is required by the APR, BIS will not process the report and will notify you, either electronically or in writing, explaining the basis for its decision. BIS will not maintain any record of the report. However, BIS will maintain a copy of the notification.
