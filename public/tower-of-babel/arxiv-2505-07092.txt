1.
Introduction

One major problem in algebraic number theory (and thus in the theory of units of number fields which is related to all areas of algebraic number theory) is the computation of a
fundamental system of units.
Let
k
be a number field of degree
n
and
k^{+}
its maximal real subfield. Let
E_{k}
(resp.

k

\mathcal{O}_{k}
(resp.
\mathcal{O}_{k^{+}}

k
(resp.
k^{+}
). The computation of the unit group of
k
may
give answers on many questions of algebraic number theory such as the size of the

2
-class group, the length of the Hilbert
2
-class field tower of its subextensions and
the capitulation problem.
For quadratic fields, the problem
is easily solved. For quartic bicyclic fields, Kubota
[
13
]
gave
a method for finding a fundamental system of units.
Wada
[
14
]
generalized Kubota’s method, creating an algorithm for computing
fundamental units in any given multiquadratic field. However, in general, it is not easy to compute the unit group of a number field especially for number fields of degree more than
4
. In some of their recent works, Azizi, Chems-Eddin, El Hamam and Zekhnini used some technical computations to determine the unit group of some number fields
k
of degree
8
and

4

1.

It is known that
