33 U.S.C. § 883k — Acquisition of land for facilities
Citation: us:usc:33:883k
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section883k
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 17 (National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration); Subchapter II (Surveys). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For fiscal year 1990 and hereafter funds appropriated under this heading shall be available for acquisition of land for facilities.
