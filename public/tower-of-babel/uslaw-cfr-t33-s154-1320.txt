33 C.F.R. § 154.1320 — Response plan submission requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:33:154.1320
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-154.1320
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 154 (Facilities Transferring Oil Or Hazardous Material In Bulk). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An owner or operator of a facility that handles, stores, or transports other non-petroleum oils shall submit a response plan in accordance with the requirements of this subpart, and with all sections of subpart F of this part, except §§ 154.1045 and 154.1047, which apply to petroleum oils.
