43 C.F.R. § 3602.24 — When may I assign my materials sales contract?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3602.24
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3602.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3600 (Mineral Materials Disposal). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You may not assign the contract or any interest therein unless BLM approves the transfer in writing.
(b) BLM will not approve your proposed assignment of contract, unless—
(1) Your assignee—
(i) Furnishes a financial guarantee as required by § 3602.14; or
(ii) Obtains a written commitment from the previous surety that it will be bound by the assignment when BLM approves it; and
(2) The assignment contains all the terms and conditions in your contract.
