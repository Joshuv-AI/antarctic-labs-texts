5 C.F.R. § 1315.15 — Grant recipients.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1315.15
Creator: Office Of Management And Budget
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1315.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1315 (Prompt Payment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Recipients of Federal assistance may pay interest penalties if so specified in their contracts with contractors. However, obligations to pay such interest penalties will not be obligations of the United States. Federal funds may not be used for this purpose, nor may interest penalties be used to meet matching requirements of federally assisted programs.
