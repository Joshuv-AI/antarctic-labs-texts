46 C.F.R. § 532.6 — Notices.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 532 (Nvocc Negotiated Rate Arrangements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An NVOCC wishing to invoke an exemption pursuant to this part must indicate that intention to the Commission and the public by a prominent notice in its rules tariff.
