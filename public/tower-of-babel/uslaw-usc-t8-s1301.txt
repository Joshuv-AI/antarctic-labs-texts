8 U.S.C. § 1301 — Alien seeking entry; contents
Citation: us:usc:8:1301
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title8-section1301
Context: United States Code, Title 8 (Aliens And Nationality); Chapter 12 (Immigration And Nationality); Subchapter II (Immigration); Part VII (Registration Of Aliens). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No visa shall be issued to any alien seeking to enter the United States until such alien has been registered in accordance with section 1201(b) of this title.
