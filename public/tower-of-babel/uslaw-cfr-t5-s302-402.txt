5 C.F.R. § 302.402 — Reappointment.
Citation: us:cfr:5:302.402
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-302.402
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 302 (Employment In The Excepted Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An agency may reappoint a current or former nontemporary employee of the executive branch of the Federal Government who is a preference eligible to a position covered by this part without regard to the names of qualified applicants on the agency's priority reemployment, reemployment, or regular employment list.
