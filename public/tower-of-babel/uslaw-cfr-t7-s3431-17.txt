7 C.F.R. § 3431.17 — VMLRP service agreement offer.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3431.17
Creator: National Institute Of Food And Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3431.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3431 (Veterinary Medicine Loan Repayment Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary will make an offer to successful applicants to enter into an agreement with the Secretary to provide veterinary services under the VMLRP. As part of the offer, successful VMLRP applicants will be provided a specific period of time, as defined in the RFA, to secure an offer of employment or establish and/or maintain a veterinary practice in a veterinary shortage situation.
