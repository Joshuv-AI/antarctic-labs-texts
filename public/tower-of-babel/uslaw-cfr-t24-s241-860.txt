24 C.F.R. § 241.860 — Commissioner's right to require acceleration.
Citation: us:cfr:24:241.860
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-241.860
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 241 (Supplementary Financing For Insured Project Mortgages). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon receipt of notice of the failure of the borrower to comply with any covenant or obligation under the security instrument or note, or under the conventional note and mortgage, the Commissioner may require the lender to accelerate payment of the outstanding principal balance due.
