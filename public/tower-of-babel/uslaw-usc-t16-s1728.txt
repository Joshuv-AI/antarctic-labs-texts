16 U.S.C. § 1728 — Nondisplacement
Citation: us:usc:16:1728
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section1728
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 37 (Youth Conservation Corps And Public Lands Corps); Subchapter II (Public Lands Corps). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The nondisplacement requirements of section 12637 of title 42 shall be applicable to all activities carried out by the Public Lands Corps, to all activities carried out under this subchapter by a qualified youth or conservation corps, and to the selection and service of resource assistants.
