48 C.F.R. § 50.100 — 50.100 Definitions.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 50 (Extraordinary Contractual Actions And The Safety Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As used in this part—
Approving authority means an agency official or contract adjustment board authorized to approve actions under Pub. L. 85-804 and E.O. 10789.
Secretarial level means a level at or above the level of a deputy assistant agency head, or a contract adjustment board.
