20 U.S.C. § 6052 — Continuation of awards
Citation: us:usc:20:6052
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section6052
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 68 (National Education Reform); Subchapter IX (Educational Research And Improvement); Part F (Certain Multiyear Grants And Contracts). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
Notwithstanding any other provision of law, from funds appropriated under subsection (b), the Secretary—
(1)
shall continue to fund any multiyear grant or contract awarded under section 3141 and parts A and C of title XIII of the Elementary and Secondary Education Act of 1965 (as such provisions were in effect on the day preceding January 8, 2002), for the duration of that multiyear award in accordance with its terms; and
(2)
may extend, on a year-to-year basis, any multiyear grant or contract awarded under an authority described in paragraph (1) that expires after January 8, 2002, but before the enactment of successor authority to this subchapter.1
(b) Authorization of appropriations
There are authorized to be appropriated for each fiscal year such sums as may be necessary to carry out subsection (a).
