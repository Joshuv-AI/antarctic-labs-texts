22 U.S.C. § 4012 — Termination of appointments of consular agents and foreign national employees
Citation: us:usc:22:4012
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section4012
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 52 (Foreign Service); Subchapter VI (Promotion And Retention). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Secretary of State may terminate at any time the appointment of any consular agent in light of the criteria and procedures normally followed in the locality in similar circumstances.
(b)
The Secretary may terminate at any time the appointment of any foreign national employee in light of the criteria and procedures normally followed in the locality in similar circumstances.
