21 C.F.R. § 556.240 — Estradiol and related esters.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 556 (Tolerances For Residues Of New Animal Drugs In Food). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) [Reserved]
(b) Residues. Residues of estradiol are not permitted in excess of the following increments above the concentrations of estradiol naturally present in untreated animals:
(1) Cattle. (i) Muscle: 0.2 ppb.
(ii) Liver: 0.6 ppb.
(iii) Kidney: 1.2 ppb.
(iv) Fat: 1.2 ppb.
(2) [Reserved]
(c) Related conditions of use. See §§ 522.840, 522.850, 522.1940, 522.2343, 522.2477, and 522.2478 of this chapter.
