46 C.F.R. § 176.675 — Extension of examination intervals.
Citation: us:cfr:46:176.675
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-176.675
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 176 (Inspection And Certification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The intervals between drydock examinations and internal structural examinations specified in § 176.600 of this part may be extended by the cognizant OCMI or Commandant.
