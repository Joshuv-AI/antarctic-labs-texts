21 C.F.R. § 1250.44 — Drinking utensils and toilet articles.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1250.44
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1250.44
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1250 (Interstate Conveyance Sanitation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) No cup, glass, or other drinking utensil which may be used by more than one person shall be provided on any conveyance unless such cup, glass, or drinking utensil shall have been thoroughly cleaned and subjected to effective bactericidal treatment after each individual use.
(b) Towels, combs, or brushes for common use shall not be provided.
