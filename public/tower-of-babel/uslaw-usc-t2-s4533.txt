2 U.S.C. § 4533 — Single per annum gross rates of pay for employees
Citation: us:usc:2:4533
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section4533
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 45 (Congressional Pay And Benefits); Subchapter II (House Of Representatives); Part A (Amount And Type). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whenever the rate of pay of an employee whose pay is disbursed by the Chief Administrative Officer of the House of Representatives is fixed or adjusted on or after the effective date of this section, that rate, as so fixed or adjusted, shall be a single per annum gross rate.
