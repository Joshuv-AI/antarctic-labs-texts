29 U.S.C. § 766 — Definition of covered school
Citation: us:usc:29:766
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section766
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 16 (Vocational Rehabilitation And Other Rehabilitation Services); Subchapter II (Research And Training). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this subchapter, the term “covered school” means an elementary school or secondary school (as such terms are defined in section 7801 of title 20) or an institution of higher education.
