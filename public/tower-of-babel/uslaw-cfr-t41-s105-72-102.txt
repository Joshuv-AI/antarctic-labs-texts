41 C.F.R. § 105-72.102 — -72.102 Effect on other issuances.
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-72.102
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-72.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-72 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For awards subject to this regulation, all administrative requirements of codified program regulations, program manuals, handbooks and other nonregulatory materials which are inconsistent with the requirements of this regulation shall be superseded, except to the extent they are required by statute, or authorized in accordance with the deviations provision in § 105-72.103.
