7 C.F.R. § 7.27 — Political activity.
Citation: us:cfr:7:7.27
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-7.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 7 (Selection And Functions Of Farm Service Agency State And County Committees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Permitted and prohibited political activities, with respect to any State committee member, county committee member, county executive director, or any other county employee, will be determined in accordance with applicable policies specified in FSA handbooks and directives.
