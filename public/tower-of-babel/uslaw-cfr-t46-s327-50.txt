46 C.F.R. § 327.50 — Statute of limitations for other admiralty claims and claim requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:46:327.50
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-327.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 327 (Seamen'S Claims; Administrative Action And Litigation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A civil suit must be filed within the statute of limitations of the specific admiralty claim. The start date for such statute of limitations determinations must be the Accrual Date.
