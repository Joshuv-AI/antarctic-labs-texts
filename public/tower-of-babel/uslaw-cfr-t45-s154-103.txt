45 C.F.R. § 154.103 — Applicability.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 154 (Health Insurance Issuer Rate Increases: Disclosure And Review Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) In general. The requirements of this part apply to health insurance issuers offering health insurance coverage in the individual market and small group market.
(b) Exceptions. The requirements of this part do not apply to—
(1) Grandfathered health plan coverage as defined in § 147.140 of this subchapter;
(2) Excepted benefits as described in section 2791(c) of the PHS Act; and
(3) For coverage effective on or after July 1, 2018, student health insurance coverage as defined in § 147.145 of this subchapter.
