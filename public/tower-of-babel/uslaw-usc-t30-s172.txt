30 U.S.C. § 172 — Certain Alabama lands subject to homestead entry
Citation: us:usc:30:172
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section172
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 3 (Lands Containing Coal, Oil, Gas, Salts, Asphaltic Materials, Sodium, Sulphur, And Building Stone); Subchapter IX (Disposal Of Alabama Lands As Agricultural Lands). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All lands designated as agricultural in the reclassification of the public lands of Alabama by the Secretary of the Interior under authority of Act March 27, 1906 (chapter 1347, section 1, Thirty-fourth Statutes, page 88), shall be subject to homestead entry as such.
