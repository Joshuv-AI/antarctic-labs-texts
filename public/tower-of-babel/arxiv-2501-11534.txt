2.
Identities of RB-algebras of degree 3 and 4

A linear map
R:A\rightarrow A
is called a Baxter operator with weight
\lambda
if it satisfies the following identity

(1)

R(a)R(b)=R(aR(b)+bR(a)+\lambda ab),

for any
a,b\in A.

In this section we establish the following facts.

Let
A
be Baxter algebra with Baxter operator
R.
Then the algebra
AR=(A,\circ)
satisfies the following identities

(2)

(x\circ a)\circ b=(x\circ b)\circ a,

(3)

a\circ([x,y]\circ b)-(a,x,y\circ b)+(a,y,x\circ b)=0,

for any
a,b,x,y,\in A,
where
[a,b]=a\circ b-b\circ a.

Proof.
Relation (
2
) is evident

(x\circ a)\circ b=(x\,R(a))\,R(b)=(x\,R(b))\,R(a)=(x\circ b)\circ a.

Let us prove (
3
). We have

a\circ([x,y]\circ b)-(a,x,y\circ b)+(a,y,x\circ b)=

a\circ((x\circ y)\circ b)-a\circ((y\circ x)\circ b)-

a\circ(x\circ(y\circ b))+(a\circ x)\circ(y\circ b)+

a\circ(y\circ(x\circ b))-(a\circ y)\circ(x\circ b)=

a\,R((xR(y))R(b))-a\,R((yR(x))R(b))-a\,R(x\,R(y\,R(b)))+(a\,R(x))\,R(y\,R(b))

+a\,R(y\,R(x\,R(b)))-(a\,R(y))\,R(x\,R(b))=

a(Q_{1}+Q_{2}+Q_{3}),

where

Q_{1}=R(x)\,R(y\,R(b))-R((yR(x))R(b)),

Q_{2}=R((xR(y))R(b))-R(y)\,R(x\,R(b)),

Q_{3}=-R(x\,R((y\,R(b)))+R(y\,R(x\,R(b))).

By (
1
) we have

Q_{1}=

R(x)\,R(y\,R(b))-R(R(x)(y\,R(b)))=

R(x\,R(y\,R(b)))+\lambda\,R(x\,y\,R(b)),

Q_{2}=

R((x\,R(b))\,R(y))-R(y)\,R(x\,R(b))=

-R(y\,R(x\,R(b)))-\lambda\,R(y\,x\,R(b)).

Thus,

Q_{1}+Q_{2}=R(x\,R(y\,R(b)))-R(y\,R(x\,R(b)))=-Q_{3},

and,

a\circ([x,y]\circ b)-(a,x,y\circ b)+(a,y,x\circ b)=Q_{1}+Q_{2}+Q_{3}=0.

Identity (
3
) is established.

\square

In this section we establish the following facts.

Let
A

R:A\rightarrow A.
Let us define polynomial

A

\langle a,b,c\rangle=\{a,\{b,c\}\}-\{\{a,b\},c\},

\{a,b\}=a\circ b+b\circ a,

f(a,b,c,u,v)=-f(b,a,c,u,v),

f(a,b,c,u,v)=-f(a,b,c,v,u),

Proof.
Let us define
