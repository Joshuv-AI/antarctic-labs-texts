43 C.F.R. § 23.4 — Application for permission to conduct exploration operations.
Citation: us:cfr:43:23.4
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-23.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 23 (Surface Exploration, Mining And Reclamation Of Lands). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person shall, in any manner or by any means which will cause the surface of lands to be disturbed, explore, test, or prospect for minerals (other than oil and gas) subject to disposition under the mineral leasing acts without first filing an application for, and obtaining, a permit, lease or contract which authorizes such exploring, testing, or prospecting.
