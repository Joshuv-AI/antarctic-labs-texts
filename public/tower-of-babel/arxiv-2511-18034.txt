A q q -analogue of the Koecher-Leshchiner generating function of odd zeta values
Roberto Tauraso

Abstract
. In the 1980s, Koecher and, independently, Leshchiner found an elegant formula for the generating function of odd zeta values. In this short note, we derive a q q -analogue of this formula, which provides a q q -version of the accelerated series for ζ ⁡ ( 3 ) \zeta(3) used by Apéry in his famous proof of irrationality.



1991 Mathematics Subject Classification
Primary 11B65, 05A19; Secondary 05A15, 05A19, 05A30, 11M06.

1.
Introduction

In June 1978, during a talk, the French mathematician Roger Apéry outlined a rather unexpected proof establishing that
\zeta(3)
is an irrational number
[
1
]
(see also the survey paper
[
8
]
).
A fundamental component of his approach was identity

(1)

\zeta(3)=\frac{5}{2}\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^{k-1}}{k^{3}\binom{2k}{k}}.

This accelerated series for
\zeta(3)
was actually first found by Markov in 1890. It follows from
[
7
, (14)]
, which is rewritten below in a compact mode, by letting
a=0
,

\sum_{k=a+1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{k^{3}}=\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^{k-1}(\frac{5}{2}k^{2}+3ak+a^{2})}{k^{5}\binom{2k}{k}\binom{a+k}{a}^{4}}

After Apéry’s proof, several other related identities were either established or revived. Here, we will focus on a generating function identity for odd zeta values obtained by
Koecher
[
5
]
and, independently, by Leshchiner
[
6
]

(see also
[
9
, (1-2)]
):

(2)

\sum_{r=0}^{\infty}\zeta(2r+3)x^{2r}=\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{k(k^{2}-x^{2})}=\frac{1}{2}\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^{k-1}}{k^{3}\binom{2k}{k}}\cdot\frac{5k^{2}-x^{2}}{k^{2}-x^{2}}\cdot\prod_{m=1}^{k-1}\Big(1-\frac{x^{2}}{m^{2}}\Big),

which implies, for
r\geq 0
,

\zeta(2r+3)=\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^{k-1-r}}{k^{3}\binom{2k}{k}}\Big(\frac{5}{2}H_{k-1}(\{2\}^{r})+2\sum_{j=1}^{r}\frac{(-1)^{j}}{k^{2j}}H_{k-1}(\{2\}^{r-j})\Big)

where

H_{k}(\{2\}^{s})=\sum_{1\leq j_{1}<\dots<j_{s}\leq k}1/(j_{1}^{2}\cdots j_{s}^{2})
.

For even zeta values, Bailey, Borwein, and Bradley using an
experimental approach discovered and then formally proved
[
3
, Theorem 1.1]
that

\sum_{r=0}^{\infty}\zeta(2r+2)x^{2r}=\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{k^{2}-x^{2}}=\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{3}{k^{2}\binom{2k}{k}}\cdot\frac{\prod_{m=1}^{k-1}\big(1-\frac{4x^{2}}{m^{2}}\big)}{\prod_{m=1}^{k}\big(1-\frac{x^{2}}{m^{2}}\big)}.

Later in
[
4
, Theorem 1]
), Kh. Hessami Pilehrood and T. Hessami Pilehrood proved the interesting
q
-analogue,

(3)

\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{q^{k}}{[k]^{2}_{q}-x^{2}q^{2k}}=\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{q^{k^{2}}(1+2q^{k})}{[k]^{2}_{q}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{2k}{k}_{q}}\cdot\frac{\prod_{m=1}^{k-1}\big(1-\frac{x^{2}(1+q^{m})^{2}}{[m]_{q}^{2}}\big)}{\prod_{m=1}^{k}\big(1-\frac{x^{2}q^{2m}}{[m]_{q}^{2}}\big)}.

In particular, for
x=0
, we get

\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{q^{k^{2}}(1+2q^{k})}{[k]^{2}_{q}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{2k}{k}_{q}}=\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{q^{k}}{[k]^{2}_{q}}.

Is there a
q
-analogue for the generating fuction of the odd zeta values (
2
)?

In
[
4
, (19) and (20)]
the authors gave some
q
-version for the Markov-Apéry formula (
1
) for
\zeta(3)
. In this short note we provide a general
q
-version of (
2
):

\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{q^{k}+q^{2k}}{[k]_{q}([k]^{2}_{q}-x^{2}q^{k})}=\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}

\displaystyle\frac{(-1)^{k-1}q^{\binom{k+1}{2}}}{[k]^{3}_{q}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{2k}{k}_{q}}

\displaystyle\cdot\frac{(1+3q^{k}+q^{2k})[k]^{2}_{q}-x^{2}q^{2k}}{[k]^{2}_{q}-x^{2}q^{k}}\cdot\prod_{m=1}^{k-1}\left(1-\frac{x^{2}q^{m}}{[m]^{2}_{q}}\right).

which is equivalent, after expanding the series with respect to the powers
x^{2r}
, to the family of identities

(4)

\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{q^{(r+1)k}+q^{(r+2)k}}{[k]^{2r+3}_{q}}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^{k-1+r}q^{{\binom{k+1}{2}}}}{[k]^{3}_{q}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{2k}{k}_{q}}

\displaystyle\cdot\Big((1+3q^{k}+q^{2k})H_{q,k-1}(\{2\}^{r})+(1+q^{k})^{2}\sum_{j=1}^{r}\frac{(-1)^{j}q^{kj}}{[k]^{2j}_{q}}H_{q,k-1}(\{2\}^{r-j})\Big)

where for
s\geq 1
,
H_{q,k}(\{2\}^{s})
is the multiple harmonic
q
-sum

\sum_{1\leq j_{1}<\dots<j_{s}\leq k}\frac{q^{j_{1}+\dots+j_{s}}}{[j_{1}]_{q}^{2}\cdots[j_{s}]_{q}^{2}}

and
H_{q,k}(\{2\}^{0})=1
. It is worth noting that for
r=0
, the
q
-series (
4
) yields a
q
-analogue of (
1
),

\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{q^{k}+q^{2k}}{[k]^{3}_{q}}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^{k-1}q^{{\binom{k+1}{2}}}(1+3q^{k}+q^{2k})}{[k]^{3}_{q}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{2k}{k}_{q}}.

2.
Proof of (
4
)

We first introduce some notations. The
q
-analogue of a positive integer
n
is

[n]_{q}=\frac{1-q^{n}}{1-q}=\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}q^{j},

and the
q
-factorial
[n]_{q}!
is defined as
\prod_{j=1}^{n}[j]_{q}
.
The
q
-binomial coefficient is given by

\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n}{k}_{q}=\frac{[n]_{q}!}{[k]_{q}![n-k]_{q}!}.

We will show the following finite form of (
4
):

\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{N}\frac{q^{(r+1)k}+q^{(r+2)k}}{[k]^{2r+3}_{q}}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=1}^{N}\frac{(-1)^{k-1+r}q^{{\binom{k+1}{2}}}}{[k]^{3}_{q}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{2k}{k}_{q}}

\displaystyle\cdot\Big((1+3q^{k}+q^{2k})H_{q,k-1}(\{2\}^{r})+(1+q^{k})^{2}\sum_{j=1}^{r}\frac{(-1)^{j}q^{kj}}{[k]^{2j}_{q}}H_{q,k-1}(\{2\}^{r-j})\Big)

\displaystyle-\sum_{k=1}^{N}\frac{(-1)^{k-1+r}q^{-\binom{k}{2}+Nk+k}}{[k]_{q}^{3}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{N}{k}_{q}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{N+k}{k}_{q}}H_{q,k-1}(\{2^{r}\}).

Then the claim is easily obtained by letting
N\to\infty
.

The definition of
H_{q,k}(\{2^{s}\})
leads to the recurrence relation

H_{q,k}(\{2^{s}\})-H_{q,k-1}(\{2^{s}\})=\frac{q^{k}}{[k]_{q}^{2}}H_{q,k-1}(\{2^{s-1}\})

which implies

q^{n-k}H_{q,k}(\{2^{s}\})+\frac{[n+k]_{q}[n-k]_{q}}{[k]_{q}^{2}}H_{q,k-1}(\{2^{s}\})=\frac{[n]_{q}^{2}}{[k]_{q}^{2}}H_{q,k-1}(\{2^{s}\})+\frac{q^{n}}{[k]_{q}^{2}}H_{q,k-1}(\{2^{s-1}\}).

We multiply both sides of the above equality by

(-1)^{k}q^{-\binom{n-k+1}{2}}\frac{([k]_{q}!)^{2}}{\prod_{j=n-k}^{n+k}[j]_{q}}.

Then the left-hand side becomes

\text{LHS}=(-1)^{k}q^{-\binom{n-k}{2}}\frac{([k]_{q}!)^{2}}{\prod_{j=n-k}^{n+k}[j]_{q}}H_{q,k}(\{2^{s}\})-(-1)^{k-1}q^{-\binom{n-(k-1)}{2}}\frac{([k-1]_{q}!)^{2}}{\prod_{j=n-(k-1)}^{n+k-1}[j]_{q}}H_{q,k-1}(\{2^{s}\})

while the right-hand side equals

\text{RHS}=(-1)^{k}q^{-\binom{n-k+1}{2}}\frac{([k-1]_{q}!)^{2}}{\prod_{j=n-k}^{n+k}[j]_{q}}\Big([n]_{q}^{2}H_{q,k-1}(\{2^{s}\})+q^{n}H_{q,k-1}(\{2^{s-1}\})\Big).

Summing both sides for
k=1,\dots,n-1
, the LHS telescopes and simplifies to

\text{LHS}=\frac{(-1)^{n-1}(1+q^{n})}{[n]_{q}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{2n}{n}_{q}}H_{q,n-1}(\{2^{s}\})-\frac{q^{-\binom{n}{2}}}{[n]}H_{q,0}(\{2^{s}\})

(note that
[2n]_{q}=(1+q^{n})[n]_{q}
). Conversely, on the other side, we find

\text{RHS}=\sum_{k=1}^{n-1}(-1)^{k}q^{-\binom{n-k+1}{2}}\frac{([k-1]_{q}!)^{2}}{\prod_{j=n-k}^{n+k}[j]_{q}}\Big([n]_{q}^{2}H_{q,k-1}(\{2^{s}\})+q^{n}H_{q,k-1}(\{2^{s-1}\})\Big).

After multiplying both sides by

\frac{(-1)^{s}q^{\binom{n}{2}+(r-s)n}(1+q^{n})}{[n]_{q}^{2r-2s}},

we get

\text{LHS}=\frac{(-1)^{s+n-1}q^{\binom{n}{2}+(r-s)n}(1+q^{n})^{2}}{[n]_{q}^{2r-2s+1}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{2n}{n}_{q}}H_{q,n-1}(\{2^{s}\})-\frac{q^{(r-s)n}+q^{(r-s+1)n}}{[n]^{2r-2s+1}}H_{q,0}(\{2^{s}\})

and

\displaystyle\text{RHS}=q^{\binom{n}{2}}

\displaystyle(1+q^{n})\sum_{k=1}^{n-1}(-1)^{k}q^{-\binom{n-k+1}{2}}\frac{([k-1]_{q}!)^{2}}{\prod_{j=n-k}^{n+k}[j]_{q}}

\displaystyle\cdot\left(\frac{(-1)^{s}q^{(r-s)n}}{[n]_{q}^{2r-2s-2}}H_{q,k-1}(\{2^{s}\})-\frac{(-1)^{s-1}q^{(r-s+1)n}}{[n]_{q}^{2r-2s}}H_{q,k-1}(\{2^{s-1}\})\right).

The next step is to sum both sides for
s=0,\dots,r-1
. We obtain

LHS

\displaystyle=\sum_{s=0}^{r-1}\frac{(-1)^{n-1+s}q^{\binom{n}{2}+(r-s)n}(1+q^{n})^{2}}{[n]_{q}^{2r-2s+1}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{2n}{n}_{q}}H_{q,n-1}(\{2^{s}\})-\frac{q^{rn}+q^{(r+1)n}}{[n]_{q}^{2r+1}}

\displaystyle=\frac{(-1)^{r+n-1}q^{\binom{n}{2}}(1+q^{n})^{2}}{\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{2n}{n}_{q}}\sum_{j=1}^{r}\frac{(-1)^{j}q^{nj}}{[n]_{q}^{2j+1}}H_{q,n-1}(\{2^{r-j}\})-\frac{q^{rn}+q^{(r+1)n}}{[n]_{q}^{2r+1}},

and the RHS telescopes,

\displaystyle\text{RHS}=q^{\binom{n}{2}}

\displaystyle(1+q^{n})\sum_{k=1}^{n-1}(-1)^{k}q^{-\binom{n-k+1}{2}}\frac{([k-1]_{q}!)^{2}}{\prod_{j=n-k}^{n+k}[j]_{q}}

\displaystyle\cdot\sum_{s=0}^{r-1}\left(\frac{(-1)^{s}q^{(r-s)n}}{[n]_{q}^{2r-2s-2}}H_{q,k-1}(\{2^{s}\})-\frac{(-1)^{s-1}q^{(r-s+1)n}}{[n]_{q}^{2r-2s}}H_{q,k-1}(\{2^{s-1}\})\right)

\displaystyle=(-1)^{r-1}q^{\binom{n}{2}}(1+q^{n})\sum_{k=1}^{n-1}(-1)^{k}q^{-\binom{n-k+1}{2}}\frac{([k-1]_{q}!)^{2}}{\prod_{j=n-k}^{n+k}[j]_{q}}q^{n}H_{q,k-1}(\{2^{r-1}\})

\displaystyle=(-1)^{r-1}(1+q^{n})\sum_{k=1}^{n-1}(-1)^{k}q^{-\binom{k}{2}+nk}\frac{([k-1]_{q}!)^{2}}{\prod_{j=n-k}^{n+k}[j]_{q}}H_{q,k-1}(\{2^{r-1}\}).

Then we proceed by summing both sides for
n=1,\dots,N
.
After interchanging the order of summation, the RHS can be written as

RHS

\displaystyle=(-1)^{r-1}\sum_{n=1}^{N}(1+q^{n})\sum_{k=1}^{n-1}(-1)^{k}q^{-\binom{k}{2}+nk}\frac{([k-1]_{q}!)^{2}}{\prod_{j=n-k}^{n+k}[j]_{q}}H_{q,k-1}(\{2^{r-1}\})

\displaystyle=(-1)^{r-1}\sum_{k=1}^{N-1}\frac{(-1)^{k}q^{-\binom{k}{2}}}{[k]_{q}^{2}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{2k}{k}_{q}}H_{q,k-1}(\{2^{r-1}\})\cdot[2k]_{q}!\sum_{n=k+1}^{N}\frac{(1+q^{n})q^{nk}}{\prod_{j=n-k}^{n+k}[j]_{q}}

\displaystyle=(-1)^{r-1}\sum_{k=1}^{N-1}\frac{(-1)^{k}q^{-\binom{k}{2}}}{[k]_{q}^{2}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{2k}{k}_{q}}H_{q,k-1}(\{2^{r-1}\})\cdot[2k]_{q}!\sum_{n=k+1}^{N}\frac{1}{[k]_{q}}\left(\frac{q^{nk}}{\prod_{j=n-k}^{n+k-1}[j]_{q}}-\frac{q^{(n+1)k}}{\prod_{j=n+1-k}^{n+1+k-1}[j]_{q}}\right),

so that the RHS telescopes again

RHS

\displaystyle=(-1)^{r-1}\sum_{k=1}^{N-1}\frac{(-1)^{k}q^{-\binom{k}{2}}}{[k]_{q}^{3}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{2k}{k}_{q}}H_{q,k-1}(\{2^{r-1}\})\cdot[2k]_{q}!\left(\frac{q^{k(k+1)}}{\prod_{j=1}^{2k}[j]_{q}}-\frac{q^{(N+1)k}}{\prod_{j=N-k+1}^{N+k}[j]_{q}}\right)

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=1}^{N-1}\frac{(-1)^{k-1+r}q^{\binom{k+1}{2}+k}}{[k]_{q}^{3}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{2k}{k}_{q}}H_{q,k-1}(\{2^{r-1}\})-\sum_{k=1}^{N-1}\frac{(-1)^{k-1+r}q^{-\binom{k}{2}+Nk+k}}{[k]_{q}^{3}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{N}{k}_{q}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{N+k}{k}_{q}}H_{q,k-1}(\{2^{r-1}\})

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=1}^{N}\frac{(-1)^{k-1+r}q^{\binom{k+1}{2}+k}}{[k]_{q}^{3}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{2k}{k}_{q}}H_{q,k-1}(\{2^{r-1}\})-\sum_{k=1}^{N}\frac{(-1)^{k-1+r}q^{-\binom{k}{2}+Nk+k}}{[k]_{q}^{3}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{N}{k}_{q}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{N+k}{k}_{q}}H_{q,k-1}(\{2^{r-1}\}).

Finally, putting the LHS and the RHS back together, we have, for
r\geq 1
,

\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{N}

\displaystyle\frac{q^{rn}+q^{(r+1)n}}{[n]_{q}^{2r+1}}=-\sum_{n=1}^{N}\frac{(-1)^{n+r}q^{\binom{n}{2}}(1+q^{n})^{2}}{\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{2n}{n}_{q}}\sum_{j=1}^{r}\frac{(-1)^{j}q^{nj}}{[n]_{q}^{2j+1}}H_{q,n-1}(\{2^{r-j}\})

\displaystyle+\sum_{k=1}^{N}\frac{(-1)^{k+r}q^{\binom{k+1}{2}+k}}{[k]_{q}^{3}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{2k}{k}_{q}}H_{q,k-1}(\{2^{r-1}\})-\sum_{k=1}^{N}\frac{(-1)^{k+r}q^{-\binom{k}{2}+Nk+k}}{[k]_{q}^{3}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{N}{k}_{q}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{N+k}{k}_{q}}H_{q,k-1}(\{2^{r-1}\})

which is the desired finite form of (
4
) after replacing
r
with
r+1
.

\square
