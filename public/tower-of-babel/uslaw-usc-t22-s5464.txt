22 U.S.C. § 5464 — Economic and commercial officers at United States embassies and missions in Poland and Hungary
Citation: us:usc:22:5464
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section5464
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 63 (Support For East European Democracy (Seed)); Subchapter V (Additional Seed Program Actions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the sense of the Congress that, to the extent practicable—
(1)
the United States Embassy in Budapest, Hungary, should be assigned one additional economic and commercial officer;
(2)
the United States Embassy in Warsaw, Poland, should be assigned one additional economic officer and one additional commercial officer;
(3)
the United States Trade Center in Warsaw, Poland, should be assigned one additional economic and commercial officer; and
(4)
the United States mission in Krakow, Poland, should be assigned one additional economic and commercial officer.
