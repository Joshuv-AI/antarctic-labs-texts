24 C.F.R. § 3285.501 — Home installation manual supplements.
Citation: us:cfr:24:3285.501
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-3285.501
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 3285 (Model Manufactured Home Installation Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Supplemental instructions for optional equipment or features must be approved by the DAPIA as not taking the home out of conformance with the requirements of this part, or part 3280 of this chapter, and included with the manufacturer installation instructions.
