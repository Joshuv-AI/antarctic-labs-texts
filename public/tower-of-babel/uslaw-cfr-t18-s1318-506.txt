18 C.F.R. § 1318.506 — Causes of action.
Citation: us:cfr:18:1318.506
Creator: Tennessee Valley Authority
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-1318.506
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 1318 (Implementation Of The National Environmental Policy Act Of 1969). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part establishes the procedures by which TVA will typically fulfill the requirements of NEPA. Minor deviations do not establish that TVA failed to comply with NEPA and do not create a substantive right or cause of action for violation of NEPA.
