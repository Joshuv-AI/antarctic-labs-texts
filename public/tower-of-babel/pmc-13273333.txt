Correction for McDonald et al., Can auxiliary indicators improve COVID-19 forecasting and hotspot prediction?
Published: 2026

Correction for “Can auxiliary indicators improve COVID-19 forecasting and hotspot prediction?” by Daniel J. McDonald, Jacob Bien, Alden Green, Addison J. Hu, Nat DeFries, Sangwon Hyun, Natalia L. Oliveira, James Sharpnack, Jingjing Tang, Robert Tibshirani, Valérie Ventura, Larry Wasserman, and Ryan J. Tibshirani, which published December 13, 2021; 10.1073/pnas.2111453118 (Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. U.S.A. 118, e2111453118).

The editors note that editor R.R. received unrestricted gifts from Facebook and Google.org and coauthored with D.J.M., J.B., A.G., A.J.H., N.D., S.H., N.L.O., J.S., J.T., R.T., V.V., L.W., and R.J.T. within the 48 months prior to editing this paper. These associations fall within the journal’s competing interest policy and therefore should have been disclosed. The article has been updated to include this information.
