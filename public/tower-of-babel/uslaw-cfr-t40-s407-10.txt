40 C.F.R. § 407.10 — Applicability; description of the apple juice subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:407.10
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-407.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 407 (Canned And Preserved Fruits And Vegetables Processing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to discharges resulting from the processing of apples into apple juice or apple cider. When a plant is subject to effluent limitations covering more than one subcategory, the plant discharge limitation shall be set by proration limitations for each subcategory based on the total raw material covered by each subcategory.
