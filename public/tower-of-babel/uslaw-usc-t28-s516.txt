28 U.S.C. § 516 — Conduct of litigation reserved to Department of Justice
Citation: us:usc:28:516
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section516
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part II (Department Of Justice); Chapter 31 (The Attorney General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as otherwise authorized by law, the conduct of litigation in which the United States, an agency, or officer thereof is a party, or is interested, and securing evidence therefor, is reserved to officers of the Department of Justice, under the direction of the Attorney General.
