35 U.S.C. § 385 — Effect of international design application
Citation: us:usc:35:385
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title35-section385
Context: United States Code, Title 35 (Patents); Part V (The Hague Agreement Concerning International Registration Of Industrial Designs); Chapter 38 (International Design Applications). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An international design application designating the United States shall have the effect, for all purposes, from its filing date determined in accordance with section 384, of an application for patent filed in the Patent and Trademark Office pursuant to chapter 16.
