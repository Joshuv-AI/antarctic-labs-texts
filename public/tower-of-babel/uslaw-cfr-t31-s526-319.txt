31 C.F.R. § 526.319 — Wrongful detention.
Citation: us:cfr:31:526.319
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-526.319
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 526 (Hostages And Wrongful Detention Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term wrongful detention means a detention that the Secretary of State has determined to be wrongful consistent with section 302(a) of the Robert Levinson Hostage Recovery and Hostage-taking Accountability Act (22 U.S.C. 1741 et seq.).
