46 C.F.R. § 58.20-1 — -1 Scope.

Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 58 (Main And Auxiliary Machinery And Related Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The regulations in this subpart apply to fixed refrigeration systems for air conditioning, refrigerated spaces, cargo spaces, and reliquefaction of low temperature cargo installed on vessels.
(b) The regulations in this subpart do not apply to small self-contained units.
