16 C.F.R. § 1630.5 — Labeling.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1630.5
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1630.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1630 (Standard For The Surface Flammability Of Carpets And Rugs (Ff 1-70)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the carpet or rug has had a fire-retardant treatment or is made of fibers which have had a fire-retardant treatment, it shall be labeled with the letter “T” pursuant to conditions established by the Consumer Product Safety Commission.
