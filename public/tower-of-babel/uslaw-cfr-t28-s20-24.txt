28 C.F.R. § 20.24 — State laws on privacy and security.
Citation: us:cfr:28:20.24
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-20.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 20 (Criminal Justice Information Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where a State originating criminal history record information provides for sealing or purging thereof, nothing in these regulations shall be construed to prevent any other State receiving such information, upon notification, from complying with the originating State's sealing or purging requirements.
