10 U.S.C. § 20503 — Removal of officer: action by Secretary upon recommendation of retention board
Citation: us:usc:10:20503
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section20503
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Space Force); Chapter 2011 (Separation Of Officers For Substandard Performance Of Duty Or For Certain Other Reasons). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Air Force may remove an officer from space force active status if the removal of such officer from space force active status is recommended by a retention board convened under section 20502 of this title.
