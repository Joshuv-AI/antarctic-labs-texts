16 U.S.C. § 715k–3 — Authorization of appropriations for the preservation of wetlands and other waterfowl habitat
Citation: us:usc:16:715k–3
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section715k–3
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 7 (Protection Of Migratory Game And Insectivorous Birds); Subchapter III (Migratory Bird Conservation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In order to promote the conservation of migratory waterfowl and to offset or prevent the serious loss of important wetlands and other waterfowl habitat essential to the preservation of such waterfowl, there is authorized to be appropriated for the period beginning on July 1, 1961, and ending when all amounts authorized to be appropriated have been expended, not to exceed $200,000,000.
