34 C.F.R. § 663.22 — How does the J. William Fulbright Foreign Scholarship Board select fellows?
Citation: us:cfr:34:663.22
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-663.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 663 (Fulbright-Hays Faculty Research Abroad Fellowship Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The J. William Fulbright Foreign Scholarship Board selects fellows on the basis of the Secretary's recommendations and the information described in § 663.20(e) from binational commissions or United States diplomatic missions.
