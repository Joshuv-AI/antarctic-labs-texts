10 U.S.C. § 7152 — General officers: title of office
Citation: us:usc:10:7152
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7152
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 715 (Appointments In The Regular Army). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An officer holding an appointment as a general officer in the Regular Army may be called a general officer in the Regular Army. In addition, a general officer of the Regular Army in the Medical Corps, Dental Corps, Veterinary Corps, Judge Advocate General’s Corps, or the Chaplains, may be called a general officer of that branch.
