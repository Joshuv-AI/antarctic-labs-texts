2 C.F.R. § 175.100 — Purpose of this part.
Citation: us:cfr:2:175.100
Creator: Office Of Management And Budget Government-Wide Guidance For Federal Financial Assistance
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-175.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 175 (Award Term For Trafficking In Persons). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part establishes a Federal award term for grants and cooperative agreements to implement the requirements in 22 U.S.C. 7104(g); 22 U.S.C. 7104a; 22 U.S.C. 7104b; and 22 U.S.C. 7104c.
