31 C.F.R. § 370.35 — Does the Bureau of the Fiscal Service accept all electronically signed transaction requests?
Citation: us:cfr:31:370.35
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-370.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 370 (Electronic Transactions And Funds Transfers Relating To United States Securities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An electronic signature will not be accepted if it has not been accomplished through a method that has been approved for specific purposes by us.
