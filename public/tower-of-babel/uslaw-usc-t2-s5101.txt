2 U.S.C. § 5101 — Employment of administrative assistants for Speaker and House Majority and Minority Leaders; compensation; appropriations
Citation: us:usc:2:5101
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section5101
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 51 (House Of Representatives Leadership); Subchapter I (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Speaker, the majority leader, and the minority leader of the House of Representatives are each authorized to employ an administrative assistant, who shall receive basic compensation at a rate not to exceed $8,000 a year. There is authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary for the payment of such compensation.
