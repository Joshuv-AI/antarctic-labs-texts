Petition for Exemption

    Docket No.: FAA-2025-0301.
    Petitioner: Helicopter Consultants of Maui dba Blue Hawaiian 
Helicopters.
    Section of 14 CFR Affected: Sec.  136.75(d)(1).
    Description of Relief Sought: The petitioner is seeking an 
exemption from Sec.  136.75(d)(1) to be able to fly below the 1,500-
foot minimum altitude above the surface in specific areas on the 
islands of O[revaps]ahu, Maui, Moloka[revaps]i and Hawai[revaps]i (the 
Big Island) in the State of Hawaii. Several of these areas include 
specific Razor Back ridge crossings. Petitioner is seeking this relief 
from Sec.  136.75(d)(1) because a lower altitude in the listed areas of 
the islands of the State of Hawai[revaps]i combined with the correct 
equipment and training is a safer altitude to conduct air tours in 
these areas that will also limit the impact to the public.
