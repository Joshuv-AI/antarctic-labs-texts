25 C.F.R. § 170.915 — May Tribal employment taxes or fees be included in a TTP project budget?
Citation: us:cfr:25:170.915
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-170.915
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 170 (Tribal Transportation Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. The cost of Tribal employment taxes or fees may be included in the budget for a TTP project.
