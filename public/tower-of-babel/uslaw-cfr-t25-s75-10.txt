25 C.F.R. § 75.10 — Where application forms may be obtained.
Citation: us:cfr:25:75.10
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-75.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 75 (Revision Of The Membership Roll Of The Eastern Band Of Cherokee Indians, North Carolina). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Application forms will be supplied by the Tribal Enrollment Office of the Eastern Band of Cherokee Indians, Council House, Cherokee, N.C. 28719, upon request, either in person or by mail.
