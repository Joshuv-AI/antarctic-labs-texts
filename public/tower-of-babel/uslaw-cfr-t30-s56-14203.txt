30 C.F.R. § 56.14203 — Application of belt dressing.
Citation: us:cfr:30:56.14203
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-56.14203
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 56 (Safety And Health Standards—Surface Metal And Nonmetal Mines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Belt dressings shall not be applied manually while belts are in motion unless a pressurized-type applicator is used that allows the dressing to be applied from outside the guards.
