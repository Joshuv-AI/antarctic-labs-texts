19 C.F.R. § 208.7 — Report to the President.
Citation: us:cfr:19:208.7
Creator: United States International Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-208.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 208 (Investigations Of United States-Mexico Cross-Border Long-Haul Trucking Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In its report to the President, the Commission will include the following:
(a) The determination made and an explanation of the basis for the determination;
(b) If the determination is affirmative or if the Commission is equally divided in its determination, the recommendation of members of the Commission who agreed to the affirmative determination for the action that is necessary to address the material harm or threat of material harm found, and an explanation of the basis for the recommendation.
(c) Any dissenting or separate views by members of the Commission regarding the determination.
