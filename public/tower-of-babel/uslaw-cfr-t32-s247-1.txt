32 C.F.R. § 247.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:32:247.1
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-247.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 247 (Department Of Defense Newspapers, Magazines And Civilian Enterprise Publications). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part implements DoD Directive 5122.10 1 and implements policy, assigns responsibilities, and prescribes procedures concerning authorized DoD Appropriated Funded (APF) newspapers and magazines, and Civilian Enterprise (CE) newspapers, magazines, guides, and installation maps in support of the DoD Internal Information Program.
