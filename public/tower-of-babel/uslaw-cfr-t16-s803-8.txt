16 C.F.R. § 803.8 — Foreign language documents.
Citation: us:cfr:16:803.8
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-803.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 803 (Transmittal Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Documentary materials or information in a foreign language required to be submitted at the time of filing a Notification and Report Form and in response to a request for additional information or documentary material must be submitted with verbatim English language translations. All verbatim translations must be accurate and complete.
