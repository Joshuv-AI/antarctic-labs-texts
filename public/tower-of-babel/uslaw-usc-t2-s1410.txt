2 U.S.C. § 1410 — Other judicial review prohibited
Citation: us:usc:2:1410
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section1410
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 24 (Congressional Accountability); Subchapter IV (Administrative And Judicial Dispute-Resolution Procedures). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as expressly authorized by sections 1407, 1408, and 1409 of this title, the compliance or noncompliance with the provisions of this chapter and any action taken pursuant to this chapter shall not be subject to judicial review.
