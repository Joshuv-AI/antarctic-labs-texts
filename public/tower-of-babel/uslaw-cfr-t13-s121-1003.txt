13 C.F.R. § 121.1003 — Where should a size protest be filed?
Citation: us:cfr:13:121.1003
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-121.1003
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 121 (Small Business Size Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A protest involving a government procurement or sale must be filed with the contracting officer for the procurement or sale, who must forward the protest to the SBA Government Contracting Area Office serving the area in which the headquarters of the protested concern is located, regardless of the location of any parent company or affiliates.
