54 U.S.C. § 103103 — Amounts provided by private entities for utility services
Citation: us:usc:54:103103
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section103103
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 1031 (Appropriations And Accounting). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, amounts provided to the Service by private entities for utility services shall be credited to the appropriate account and remain available until expended.
