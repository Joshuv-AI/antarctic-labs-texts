26 U.S.C. § 3124 — Estimate of revenue reduction
Citation: us:usc:26:3124
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section3124
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 21 (Federal Insurance Contributions Act); Subchapter C (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary at intervals of not longer than 3 years shall estimate the reduction in the amount of taxes collected under this chapter by reason of the operation of section 3121(b)(9) and shall include such estimate in his annual report.
