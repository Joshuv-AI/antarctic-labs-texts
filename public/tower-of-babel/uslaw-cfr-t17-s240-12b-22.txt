17 C.F.R. § 240.12b-22 — b-22 Disclaimer of control.
Citation: us:cfr:17:240.12b-22
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-240.12b-22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 240 (General Rules And Regulations, Securities Exchange Act Of 1934). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the existence of control is open to reasonable doubt in any instance, the registrant may disclaim the existence of control and any admission thereof; in such case, however, the registrant shall state the material facts pertinent to the possible existence of control.
