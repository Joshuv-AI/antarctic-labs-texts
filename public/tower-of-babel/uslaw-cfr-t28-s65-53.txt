28 C.F.R. § 65.53 — Confidentiality of information.
Citation: us:cfr:28:65.53
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-65.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 65 (Emergency Federal Law Enforcement Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Section 812 of title I of the Omnibus Crime Control and Safe Streets Act of 1968 (as amended and implemented by 28 CFR part 20) shall apply with respect to information, including criminal history information and criminal intelligence systems operating with the support of Federal law enforcement assistance.
