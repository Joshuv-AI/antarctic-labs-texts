SUMMARY: DOT is correcting a notice published on September 29, 2025, 
issue of the Federal Registry entitled ``Senior Executive Service 
Performance Review Board Membership''. This notice adds additional 
individuals that may be selected to serve on one or more Departmental 
PRBs.

Office of Human Resource Management (202) 366-2478.

additional individuals that may be selected to serve on one or more 
Departmental PRBs.
