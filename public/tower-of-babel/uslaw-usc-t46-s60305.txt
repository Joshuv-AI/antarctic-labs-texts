46 U.S.C. § 60305 — Vessels in distress
Citation: us:usc:46:60305
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section60305
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 603 (Tonnage Taxes And Light Money). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A vessel is exempt from tonnage taxes and light money when it enters because it is in distress.
