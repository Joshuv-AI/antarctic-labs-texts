25 C.F.R. § 900.252 — In an emergency reassumption, what is the Secretary required to do?
Citation: us:cfr:25:900.252
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior, And Indian Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-900.252
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 900 (Contracts Under The Indian Self-Determination And Education Assistance Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Immediately rescind, in whole or in part, the contract;
(b) Assume control or operation of all or part of the program; and
(c) Give written notice to the contractor and the Indian tribes or tribal organizations served.
