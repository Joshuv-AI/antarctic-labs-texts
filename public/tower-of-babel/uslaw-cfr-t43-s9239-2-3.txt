43 C.F.R. § 9239.2-3 — -3 Responsibility for execution of law.
Citation: us:cfr:43:9239.2-3
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-9239.2-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 9230 (Trespass). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The execution of this law devolves primarily upon the officers of the Department of Justice, but as it is the purpose to free the public lands from unlawful enclosures and obstructions, it is deemed incumbent upon the officers of the Department of the Interior to furnish the officers of the Department of Justice with the evidence necessary to a successful prosecution of the law.
