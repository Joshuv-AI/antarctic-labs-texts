36 U.S.C. § 130506 — Exclusive right to name and emblems
Citation: us:usc:36:130506
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section130506
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1305 (Little League Baseball, Incorporated). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation has the exclusive right to use and to allow others to use the names “Little League” and “Little Leaguer” and the official Little League emblem or any colorable simulation of that emblem. This section does not affect any vested rights.
