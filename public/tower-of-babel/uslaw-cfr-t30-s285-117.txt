30 C.F.R. § 285.117 — Severability.
Citation: us:cfr:30:285.117
Creator: Bureau Of Safety And Environmental Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-285.117
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 285 (Renewable Energy And Alternate Uses Of Existing Facilities On The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a court holds any provisions of this subpart or their applicability to any persons or circumstances invalid, the remainder of the provisions and their applicability to any persons or circumstances will not be affected.
