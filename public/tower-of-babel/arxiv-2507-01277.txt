The electromagnetic form factors of Σ \Sigma and Σ 0 → Λ \Sigma^{0}\to\Lambda transition in the timelike region
Cheng Chen, Bing Yan, Bing-Wei Long, Ju-Jun Xie

Abstract
We investigate the e + ​ e − → Σ ​ Σ ¯ e^{+}e^{-}\to\Sigma\bar{\Sigma} and e + ​ e − → Λ ​ Σ ¯ 0 e^{+}e^{-}\to\Lambda\bar{\Sigma}^{0} reactions within the extended vector meson dominance model. In addition to the ground state mesons ρ \rho and ω \omega , we consider the contributions of the excited states ρ ⁡ ( 3 ​ D ) \rho(3D) , ω ⁡ ( 3 ​ D ) \omega(3D) , ϕ ⁡ ( 3 ​ D ) \phi(3D) , and ρ ⁡ ( 6 ​ D ) \rho(6D) . It is found that the current experimental data on the Σ \Sigma electromagnetic form factors in timelike region can be well reproduced. And the ϕ ⁡ ( 3 ​ D ) \phi(3D) resonance is essential to the near threshold enhancement of the cross section for the e + ​ e − → Σ ​ Σ ¯ e^{+}e^{-}\to\Sigma\bar{\Sigma} reaction. Furthermore, in the e + ​ e − → Λ ​ Σ ¯ 0 e^{+}e^{-}\to\Lambda\bar{\Sigma}^{0} reaction, the ρ ⁡ ( 3 ​ D ) \rho(3D) is important to get a good fit for the experimental results.

Introduction

The electromagnetic form factors (EMFFs) of baryons encode crucial information about their electromagnetic structure
Denig and Salme 2013
;
Pacetti et al. 2015
;
Punjabi et al. 2015

q^{2}
, of the interacted virtual photon. Hence, investigating the EMFFs of baryons is essential for gaining deeper insight into their fundamental structure. The region where
q^{2}<0
is referred to as the spacelike region, while
q^{2}>0
is known as the timelike region. In the spacelike region, EMFFs are real and relate to the charge and magnetic distribution of baryons. In contrast, EMFFs in the timelike region are generally complex. Interestingly, an analytical study revealed that EMFFs in the timelike region tend to become real at large four-momentum transfer squared
Mangoni et al. 2021
. Furthermore, Ref.
Kuraev et al. 2012
proposed that, in the timelike region, these EMFFs are connected to the time evolution of baryons’ electromagnetic distributions. On the experimental side, measuring the EMFFs of unstable baryons, such as hyperon
\Sigma
,
\Lambda
, and
\Xi
, through electron-baryon scattering in the spacelike region is challenging due to their short lifetimes. Consequently, it is more practical to study baryon EMFFs in the timelike region using electron-positron annihilation reactions. II
Formalism

For the
e^{+}e^{-}\to\Sigma\bar{\Sigma}
reaction, the interaction vertex of
\gamma^{*}\Sigma\bar{\Sigma}
can be parameterized to two independent form factors
F_{1}(q^{2})
and
F_{2}(q^{2})
, were called the Dirac and Pauli form factors, respectively, according to the electromagnetic current conservation and
CP
invariance. Therefore, the matrix of
\Sigma\bar{\Sigma}
created by electromagnetic current from the vacuum can be expressed as

\displaystyle\!\!\!\!\!\!\braket{\Sigma\bar{\Sigma}|J_{\mu}^{em}|0}=

\displaystyle\!\!\!\!\!\!\bar{u}_{\Sigma}(p_{\Sigma},s_{\Sigma})\left[\gamma_{\mu}F_{1}(q^{2})+i\frac{F_{2}(q^{2})}{2M_{\Sigma}}\sigma_{\mu\nu}q^{\nu}\right]v_{\bar{\Sigma}}(p_{\bar{\Sigma}},s_{\bar{\Sigma}}),

(1)

where
q
is four-momentum of the virtual photon
\gamma^{*}
, and
M_{\Sigma}
is the mass of
\Sigma
hyperon. Then the electric
G_{E}
and magnetic form factor
G_{M}
can be obtained by combining the Pauli and Dirac form factors,

\displaystyle G_{E}(q^{2})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle F_{1}(q^{2})+\tau F_{2}(q^{2}),

(2)

\displaystyle G_{M}(q^{2})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle F_{1}(q^{2})+F_{2}(q^{2}),

(3)

with
\tau=q^{2}/(4M_{\Sigma}^{2})
. Within the
G_{M}
and
G_{E}
, the total cross section of the
e^{+}e^{-}\to\Sigma\bar{\Sigma}
reaction in the center of mass frame is obtained as

\displaystyle\sigma(e^{+}e^{-}\to\Sigma\bar{\Sigma})=\frac{4\pi\alpha_{em}^{2}\beta C}{3s}\left(|G_{M}|^{2}+\frac{1}{2\tau}|G_{E}|^{2}\right),

(4)

where
s=q^{2}
is the invariant mass squared of the
e^{+}e^{-}
system,
\alpha_{em}=e^{2}/4\pi=1/137.036
is the electromagnetic fine structure constant, and
\beta=\sqrt{1-4M^{2}_{\Sigma}/s}
is the velocity of
\Sigma
hyperon.
The factor
C
is the
S
-wave Sommerfeld-Gamow factor originating from the Coulomb interaction of the final
\Sigma\bar{\Sigma}

Sakharov 1948
,

\displaystyle C=\begin{cases}\frac{y}{1-e^{-y}},&\text{for}\ \Sigma^{+},\Sigma^{-},\\
&\\
1,&\text{for}\ \Sigma^{0},\end{cases}

(5)

and here
y=2\pi\alpha_{em}M_{\Sigma}/(\beta\sqrt{s})
. Thus, for charged
\Sigma^{+}
and
\Sigma^{-}
, the total cross section of their responding is non-zero at the reaction threshold. Moreover, the Coulomb interaction just affects the reaction threshold about a few MeV, and it decreases rapidly and exponentially to a constant 1.

According to the total cross section as in Eq. (
4
), we can extract the so-called effective form factor
|G_{\rm eff}|
that is

\displaystyle|G_{\rm eff}(q^{2})|=\sqrt{\frac{2\tau|G_{M}(q^{2})|^{2}+|G_{E}(q^{2})|^{2}}{1+2\tau}}.

(6)

Within the one-photon exchange approximation, the effective form factor stands for the effective coupling strength between photon and hyperons, varying with the center mass of energy, and also characterizes the degree of
\Sigma
hyperons deviation from the point particle. Moreover, we can also obtain the differential cross section distribution parameter
\alpha
in the reaction

\displaystyle\alpha=\frac{s|G_{M}|^{2}-4M_{\Sigma}^{2}|G_{E}|^{2}}{s|G_{M}|^{2}+4M_{\Sigma}^{2}|G_{E}|^{2}},

(7)

which only depends on the ratio of the absolute values of EMFFs and satisfies
-1\leq\alpha\leq 1
.

In the timelike region,
G_{E}
and
G_{M}
are complex, and their relative phase
\Delta\Phi
is an observable quantity, which can be extracted from the spin polarization of the final
hyperons
Ablikim et al. 2019b
;
Ablikim et al. 2022b
;
Ablikim et al. 2022c
;
Ablikim et al. 2023b
;
Ablikim et al. 2020b
;
Ablikim et al. 2024d
;
Ablikim et al. 2024e
;
Ablikim et al. 2024f
;
Ablikim et al. 2022d
;
Ablikim et al. 2023d
;
Liu et al. 2023

\displaystyle G_{E}/G_{M}=|G_{E}/G_{M}|e^{i\Delta\Phi},

(8)

and the spin polarization distribution along the direction
\hat{p}\times\hat{k}
(
\hat{k}
is the momentum direction of electron, and
\hat{p}
is the momentum direction of
\Sigma
hyperon in the center mass frame) is given by
Fäldt and Kupsc 2017

\displaystyle P_{y}(\theta)=\frac{\sqrt{1-\alpha^{2}}\sin\theta\cos\theta}{1+\alpha\cos^{2}\theta}\sin\Delta\Phi,

where the

IV
SUMMARY

In this work, we study the electromagnetic form factors of
\Sigma
and
\Lambda-\bar{\Sigma}^{0}
transition form factors in the timelike region using a phenomenological extending vector meson dominance model. In the reaction
e^{+}e^{-}\to\Sigma\bar{\Sigma}
, in addition to ground mesons
\rho
,
\omega
and
\phi
, we take account into additional vector meson excited states
\rho(3D)
,
\omega(3D)
,
\phi(3D)
and
\rho(6D)
. We split into two scenarios according to the contribution of
\rho(3D)
and
\omega(3D)
. With these vector mesons, both scenarios can successfully describe the total cross section of the
\Sigma
isospin triplet state, as well as the electromagnetic ratio
|G_{E}/G_{M}|
and relative phase
\Delta\Phi
of
\Sigma^{+}
. Our results show a significant enhancement of the total cross section around the center-of-mass energy of 2.5 GeV compared to only the ground state vector mesons, which is mainly due to the effect of
\phi(3D)
. This also gives a natural explanation for the cross section recently measured by BESIII. Furthermore, we find that the electromagnetic ratio of
\Sigma^{+}
exhibits a decreasing oscillatory behavior, a feature very similar to that in
\Lambda_{c}^{+}
. This non-monotonic behavior is very relevant to the vector meson resonance above the reaction threshold.

In the process of
e^{+}e^{-}\to\Lambda\bar{\Sigma}^{0}
, we have considered the contributions from
\rho
and
\rho(3D)
states. Our analysis finds that the
\rho(3D)
state plays a crucial role in the threshold enhancement of the total cross section. Notably, despite the
\rho(3D)
lying below the reaction threshold, it significantly impacts the electromagnetic form factors above the threshold. This effect is particularly pronounced in the ratio
|G_{E}/G_{M}|
and its associated
\Delta\Phi
across a wide energy range.

Finally, we emphasize that in the
e^{+}e^{-}\to\Sigma\bar{\Sigma}
and
\Lambda\bar{\Sigma}^{0}
process, its cross section enhancement, the non-monotonic structure of the ratio
|G_{E}/G_{M}|
and the non-zero relative phase
\Delta\Phi
can be naturally explained by the vector meson dominance model. In particular, the contribution of vector meson resonance states above the reaction threshold is important.

\displaystyle P=q,\qquad Q=p_{1}-p_{2}.

(A12)

The proton momentum in the
\Sigma^{+}
rest frame is expressed as:

\displaystyle\vec{l}_{1}^{\prime}

\displaystyle=|\vec{l}_{1}^{\prime}|(\sin\theta_{p}\cos\phi_{p},\sin\theta_{p}\sin\phi_{p},\cos\theta_{p}),

(A13)

and the antiproton momentum in the
\bar{\Sigma}^{-}
rest frame is:

\displaystyle\vec{l}_{2}^{\prime}

\displaystyle=|\vec{l}_{2}^{\prime}|(\sin\theta_{\bar{p}}\cos\phi_{\bar{p}},\sin\theta_{\bar{p}}\sin\phi_{\bar{p}},\cos\theta_{\bar{p}}),

(A14)

where
\Omega_{p}=(\theta_{p},\phi_{p})
and
\Omega_{\bar{p}}=(\theta_{\bar{p}},\phi_{\bar{p}})
represent the solid angles.

Our primary objective is to calculate
|\mathcal{M}|^{2}
. The leptonic tensor is defined as:

\displaystyle L^{\mu\nu}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{4}\sum_{\rm spins}\bar{v}(k_{2})\gamma^{\mu}u(k_{1})\bar{u}(k_{1})\gamma^{\nu}v(k_{2})

(A15)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle k_{1}^{\mu}k_{2}^{\nu}+k_{1}^{\nu}k_{2}^{\mu}-\frac{1}{2}sg^{\mu\nu}.

In this definition,
\frac{1}{4}\sum_{\rm spins}
signifies the summation and averaging over the initial electron and positron spins. Additionally, the lepton masses are neglected in this calculation.

The hadronic tensor is defined as

\displaystyle H_{\mu\nu}=\sum_{\rm spins}H_{\mu}H_{\nu}^{\dagger}=\mathrm{Tr}\left[(\not{p}_{1}+M)(a^{*}-b^{*}\gamma^{5})(\not{l}_{1}+m)\right.

\displaystyle\qquad(a+b\gamma^{5})(\not{p}_{1}+M)\Gamma_{\mu}(\not{p}_{2}-M)(\bar{a}+\bar{b}\gamma^{5})(\not{l}_{2}-m)

\displaystyle\qquad\left.(\bar{a}^{*}-\bar{b}^{*}\gamma^{5})(\not{p}_{2}-M)\bar{\Gamma}_{\nu}\right],

(A16)

where the summation
\sum_{\rm spins}
represents the spin sum over all final protons and antiprotons, and
m
denotes the proton mass. The quantity
\bar{\Gamma}_{\nu}
is defined as

\displaystyle\bar{\Gamma}_{\nu}=\gamma^{0}\Gamma_{\nu}^{\dagger}\gamma^{0}.

(A17)

We can simplify the hadronic tensor
H^{\mu\nu}
by introducing the following definitions. Let

\displaystyle X

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(\not{p}_{1}+M)(a^{*}-b^{*}\gamma^{5})(\not{l}_{1}+m)(a+b\gamma^{5})(\not{p}_{1}+M)

(A18)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(\not{p}_{1}+M)\left[2p_{1}\cdot l_{1}(|a|^{2}+|b|^{2})+2Mm(|a|^{2}-|b|^{2})\right.

\displaystyle\left.-2(ab^{*}+a^{*}b)\gamma^{5}(M\not{l}_{1}+p_{1}\cdot l_{1})\right]

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle R(\not{p}_{1}+M)\left[1+S\gamma^{5}\left(\not{l}_{1}+\frac{p_{1}\cdot l_{1}}{M}\right)\right],

and

\displaystyle Y

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(\not{p}_{2}-M)(\bar{a}+\bar{b}\gamma^{5})(\not{l}_{2}-m)(\bar{a}^{*}-\bar{b}^{*}\gamma^{5})(\not{p}_{2}-M)

(A19)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(\not{p}_{2}-M)\left[2p_{2}\cdot l_{2}(|\bar{a}|^{2}+|\bar{b}|^{2})+2Mm(|\bar{a}|^{2}-|\bar{b}|^{2})\right.

\displaystyle\left.-2(\bar{a}\bar{b}^{*}+\bar{a}^{*}\bar{b})\gamma^{5}(M\not{l}_{2}-p_{2}\cdot l_{2})\right]

\displaystyle\bar{R}(\not{p}_{2}-M)\left[1+\bar{S}\gamma^{5}\left(\not{l}_{2}-\frac{p_{2}\cdot l_{2}}{M}\right)\right],

\displaystyle R

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 2p_{1}\cdot l_{1}(|a|^{2}+|b|^{2})+2Mm(|a|^{2}-|b|^{2})

(A20)

\displaystyle|a|^{2}[(M+m)^{2}-\mu^{2}]+|b|^{2}[(M-m)^{2}-\mu^{2}],\qquad

\displaystyle\bar{R}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 2p_{2}\cdot l_{2}(|\bar{a}|^{2}+|\bar{b}|^{2})+2Mm(|\bar{a}|^{2}-|\bar{b}|^{2})

(A21)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle|\bar{a}|^{2}[(M+m)^{2}-\mu^{2}]+|\bar{b}|^{2}[(M-m)^{2}-\mu^{2}],

with
\mu
the mass of
\pi_{0}
, and

\displaystyle S

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\frac{2M(ab^{*}+a^{*}b)}{R},

(A22)

\displaystyle\bar{S}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\frac{2M(\bar{a}\bar{b}^{*}+\bar{a}^{*}\bar{b})}{\bar{R}}.

(A23)

It is evident that the quantities
\frac{p_{1}\cdot l_{1}}{M}

\frac{p_{2}\cdot l_{2}}{M}

\Sigma^{+}
and
\bar{\Sigma}^{-}

\displaystyle H_{1}=\frac{p_{1}\cdot l_{1}}{M},\qquad H_{2}=\frac{p_{2}\cdot l_{2}}{M}.

(A24)

Furthermore, the parameters
S
and
\bar{S}

\Sigma^{+}\to p+\pi^{0}
and
\bar{\Sigma}^{-}\to\bar{p}+\pi^{0}

\displaystyle\alpha_{\Sigma^{+}}=S|\vec{l}_{1}^{\prime}|,\qquad\alpha_{\bar{\Sigma}^{-}}=\bar{S}|\vec{l}_{2}^{\prime}|.

Next, we decompose the hadronic tensor

\displaystyle H^{\mu\nu}=R\bar{R}\left[H^{\mu\nu}_{I}+SH^{\mu\nu}_{S}+\bar{S}H^{\mu\nu}_{\bar{S}}+S\bar{S}H^{\mu\nu}_{S\bar{S}}\right].

The first component is given by

\displaystyle H^{\mu\nu}_{I}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\mathrm{Tr}\left[(\not{p}_{1}+M)\Gamma^{\mu}(\not{p}_{2}-M)\bar{\Gamma}^{\nu}\right]

(A27)

\displaystyle 4|G_{M}|^{2}\left(p_{1}^{\mu}p_{2}^{\nu}+p_{1}^{\nu}p_{2}^{\mu}-\frac{1}{2}sg^{\mu\nu}\right)

\displaystyle+2(G_{M}F_{2}^{*}+G_{M}^{*}F_{2})(p_{1}^{\mu}-p_{2}^{\mu})(p_{1}^{\nu}-p_{2}^{\nu})

\displaystyle+2\left(\frac{s}{4M^{2}}-1\right)|F_{2}|^{2}(p_{1}^{\mu}-p_{2}^{\mu})(p_{1}^{\nu}-p_{2}^{\nu})

\displaystyle 4|G_{M}|^{2}\left(p_{1}^{\mu}p_{2}^{\nu}+p_{1}^{\nu}p_{2}^{\mu}-\frac{1}{2}sg^{\mu\nu}\right)

\displaystyle+\frac{2}{\tau-1}(|G_{E}|^{2}-|G_{M}|^{2})Q^{\mu}Q^{\nu}.

Contracting

with
L_{\mu\nu}

\displaystyle H^{\mu\nu}_{I}L_{\mu\nu}

\displaystyle s^{2}(1+\cos^{2}\theta)|G_{M}|^{2}+4sM^{2}|G_{E}^{2}|\sin^{2}\theta

(A29)

\displaystyle\alpha=\frac{s|G_{M}|^{2}-4M^{2}|G_{E}|^{2}}{s|G_{M}|^{2}+4M^{2}|G_{E}|^{2}}.

(A30)

The second component is given by

\displaystyle H^{\mu\nu}_{S}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\mathrm{Tr}\left[(\not{p}_{1}+M)\gamma^{5}\left(\not{l}_{1}+H_{1}\right)\Gamma^{\mu}(\not{p}_{2}-M)\bar{\Gamma}^{\nu}\right]

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-4i|G_{M}|^{2}(M\varepsilon^{\rho\sigma\mu\nu}({p_{1}}_{\rho}+{p_{2}}_{\rho}){l_{1}}_{\sigma}+H_{1}\varepsilon^{\rho\sigma\mu\nu}{p_{1}}_{\rho}{p_{2}}_{\sigma})

\displaystyle+G_{M}^{*}\frac{F_{2}}{2M}4i\varepsilon^{\alpha\beta\gamma\nu}{p_{1}}_{\alpha}{p_{2}}_{\beta}{l_{1}}_{\gamma}(p_{1}^{\mu}-p_{2}^{\mu})

\displaystyle-4i(M\varepsilon^{\rho\sigma\mu\nu}({p_{1}}_{\rho}+{p_{2}}_{\rho}){l_{1}}_{\sigma}+H_{1}\varepsilon^{\rho\sigma\mu\nu}{p_{1}}_{\rho}{p_{2}}_{\sigma})

\displaystyle+\frac{2i}{M(\tau-1)}\left(|G_{M}|^{2}-|G_{E}||G_{M}|\cos(\Delta\Phi)\right)

\displaystyle\times{p_{1}}_{\alpha}{p_{2}}_{\beta}{l_{1}}_{\gamma}(\varepsilon^{\alpha\beta\gamma\mu}Q^{\nu}-\varepsilon^{\alpha\beta\gamma\nu}Q^{\mu})

\displaystyle\times{p_{1}}_{\alpha}{p_{2}}_{\beta}{l_{1}}_{\gamma}(\varepsilon^{\alpha\beta\gamma\mu}Q^{\nu}+\varepsilon^{\alpha\beta\gamma\nu}Q^{\mu}).

\displaystyle H^{\mu\nu}_{S}L_{\mu\nu}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{8Ep}{M(\tau-1)}|G_{E}||G_{M}|\cos\theta\sin(\Delta\Phi)

(A32)

\displaystyle\times\varepsilon^{\alpha\beta\gamma\mu}{p_{1}}_{\alpha}{p_{2}}_{\beta}{l_{1}}_{\gamma}({k_{1}}_{\mu}-{k_{2}}_{\mu}).

To evaluate the term

\Sigma^{+}

\displaystyle p_{1}^{\prime}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(M,0,0,0),

(A33)

\displaystyle p_{2}^{\prime}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left(\frac{E^{2}+p^{2}}{M},0,0,-\frac{2Ep}{M}\right),

(A34)

\displaystyle k_{1}^{\prime}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle E\left(\frac{E-p\cos\theta}{M},\sin\theta,0,\frac{E\cos\theta-p}{M}\right),

(A35)

\displaystyle k_{2}^{\prime}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle E\left(\frac{E+p\cos\theta}{M},-\sin\theta,0,\frac{-E\cos\theta-p}{M}\right),\qquad

(A36)

where
p_{1}^{\prime}
,
p_{2}^{\prime}
,
k_{1}^{\prime}
, and

represent the four-momenta of the proton, antiproton, electron, and positron in the
\Sigma^{+}
rest frame, respectively. By applying Lorentz invariance, we find that

\displaystyle\varepsilon^{\alpha\beta\gamma\mu}{p_{1}}_{\alpha}{p_{2}}_{\beta}{l_{1}}_{\gamma}({k_{1}}_{\mu}-{k_{2}}_{\mu})

(A37)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-M(\vec{p}_{2}^{\prime}\times(\vec{k}_{1}^{\prime}-\vec{k}_{2}^{\prime}))\cdot\vec{l}_{1}^{\prime}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 4E^{2}p\sin\theta(\frac{1}{\sin\theta}\hat{p}\times\hat{k})\cdot\vec{l}_{1}^{\prime}.

After simplifying the expressions, the final result is

\displaystyle H^{\mu\nu}_{S}L_{\mu\nu}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle sD(s)\sqrt{1-\alpha^{2}}\sin\theta\cos\theta\sin\Delta\Phi

(A38)

\displaystyle\times\left[\frac{1}{\sin\theta}(\hat{p}\times\hat{k})\cdot\vec{l}_{1}^{\prime}\right].

For the third component
H^{\mu\nu}_{\bar{S}}
, its explicit form is given by

\displaystyle H^{\mu\nu}_{\bar{S}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\mathrm{Tr}\left[(\not{p}_{1}+M)\Gamma_{\mu}(\not{p}_{2}-M)\gamma^{5}\left(\not{l}_{2}-H_{2}\right)\bar{\Gamma}_{\nu}\right]

(A39)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-4i|G_{M}|^{2}(M\varepsilon^{\rho\sigma\mu\nu}({p_{1}}_{\rho}+{p_{2}}_{\rho}){l_{2}}_{\sigma}-H_{2}\varepsilon^{\rho\sigma\mu\nu}{p_{1}}_{\rho}{p_{2}}_{\sigma})

\displaystyle-G_{M}\frac{F_{2}^{*}}{2M}4i\varepsilon^{\alpha\beta\gamma\mu}{p_{1}}_{\alpha}{p_{2}}_{\beta}{l_{2}}_{\gamma}(p_{1}^{\nu}-p_{2}^{\nu})

\displaystyle+G_{M}^{*}\frac{F_{2}}{2M}4i\varepsilon^{\alpha\beta\gamma\nu}{p_{1}}_{\alpha}{p_{2}}_{\beta}{l_{2}}_{\gamma}(p_{1}^{\mu}-p_{2}^{\mu})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-4i(M\varepsilon^{\rho\sigma\mu\nu}({p_{1}}_{\rho}+{p_{2}}_{\rho}){l_{2}}_{\sigma}-H_{2}\varepsilon^{\rho\sigma\mu\nu}{p_{1}}_{\rho}{p_{2}}_{\sigma})

\displaystyle+\frac{2i}{M(\tau-1)}\left(|G_{M}|^{2}-|G_{E}||G_{M}|\cos(\Delta\Phi)\right)

\displaystyle\times{p_{1}}_{\alpha}{p_{2}}_{\beta}{l_{2}}_{\gamma}(\varepsilon^{\alpha\beta\gamma\mu}Q^{\nu}-\varepsilon^{\alpha\beta\gamma\nu}Q^{\mu})

\displaystyle+\frac{2}{M(\tau-1)}|G_{E}||G_{M}|\sin(\Delta\Phi)

\displaystyle\times{p_{1}}_{\alpha}{p_{2}}_{\beta}{l_{2}}_{\gamma}(\varepsilon^{\alpha\beta\gamma\mu}Q^{\nu}+\varepsilon^{\alpha\beta\gamma\nu}Q^{\mu}).

By applying a similar approach, we derive the result for
H^{\mu\nu}_{\bar{S}}L_{\mu\nu}

\displaystyle H^{\mu\nu}_{\bar{S}}L_{\mu\nu}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle sD(s)\sqrt{1-\alpha^{2}}\sin\theta\cos\theta\sin(\Delta\Phi)

(A40)

\displaystyle\times\left[\frac{1}{\sin\theta}(\hat{p}\times\hat{k})\cdot\vec{l}_{2}^{\prime}\right].

The final component,
H^{\mu\nu}_{S\bar{S}}
, is the most intricate to compute among the components discussed. It is expressed as:

\displaystyle H^{\mu\nu}_{S\bar{S}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\mathrm{Tr}\left[(\not{p}_{1}+M)\gamma^{5}\left(\not{l}_{1}+H_{1}\right)\Gamma^{\mu}\right.

(A41)

\displaystyle\left.\times(\not{p}_{2}-M)\gamma^{5}\left(\not{l}_{2}-H_{2}\right)\bar{\Gamma}^{\nu}\right]

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle|G_{M}|^{2}A^{\mu\nu}+\frac{|F_{2}|^{2}}{4M^{2}}BQ^{\mu}Q^{\nu}

\displaystyle-\frac{G_{M}F_{2}^{*}}{2M}T^{\mu\nu}_{1}-\frac{G_{M}^{*}F_{2}}{2M}T^{\mu\nu}_{2},

with

\displaystyle A^{\mu\nu}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\mathrm{Tr}\left[(\not{p}_{1}+M)\gamma^{5}\left(\not{l}_{1}+H_{1}\right)\gamma^{\mu}\right.

(A42)

\displaystyle\left.\times(\not{p}_{2}-M)\gamma^{5}\left(\not{l}_{2}-H_{2}\right)\gamma^{\nu}\right]

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-4\left[(l_{1}\cdot l_{2}+H_{1}H_{2})(p_{1}^{\mu}p_{2}^{\nu}+p_{1}^{\nu}p_{2}^{\mu}-\frac{1}{2}sg^{\mu\nu})\right.

\displaystyle+\left.\frac{1}{2}s(l_{1}^{\mu}l_{2}^{\nu}+l_{1}^{\nu}l_{2}^{\mu})+P\cdot l_{1}P\cdot l_{2}g^{\mu\nu}\right],

\displaystyle B

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\mathrm{Tr}\left[(\not{p}_{1}+M)\gamma^{5}\left(\not{l}_{1}+H_{1}\right)(\not{p}_{2}-M)\gamma^{5}\left(\not{l}_{2}-H_{2}\right)\right]

(A43)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 4\left[(-\frac{1}{2}s+2M^{2})l_{1}\cdot l_{2}+(\frac{1}{2}s+2M^{2})H_{1}H_{2}\right.

\displaystyle+\left.P\cdot l_{1}P\cdot l_{2}-2M(H_{1}P\cdot l_{2}+H_{2}P\cdot l_{1})\right],

and

\displaystyle T_{1}^{\mu\nu}

\displaystyle=

(A44)

\displaystyle\times\left.(\not{p}_{2}-M)\gamma^{5}\left(\not{l}_{2}-H_{2}\right)\right]Q^{\nu}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 4\left[(Ml_{1}\cdot l_{2}-H_{2}p_{2}\cdot l_{1})p_{1}^{\mu}\right.

\displaystyle-\left.(Ml_{1}\cdot l_{2}-H_{1}p_{1}\cdot l_{2})p_{2}^{\mu}\right.

\displaystyle-\left.(Mp_{1}\cdot l_{2}-H_{2}p_{1}\cdot p_{2})l_{1}^{\mu}\right.

\displaystyle+\left.(Mp_{2}\cdot l_{1}-H_{1}p_{1}\cdot p_{2})l_{2}^{\mu}\right]Q^{\nu},

\displaystyle T_{2}^{\mu\nu}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\mathrm{Tr}\left[(\not{p}_{1}+M)\gamma^{5}\left(\not{l}_{1}+H_{1}\right)\right.

(A45)

\displaystyle\left.(\not{p}_{2}-M)\gamma^{5}\left(\not{l}_{2}-H_{2}\right)\gamma^{\nu}\right]Q^{\mu}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 4\left[(Ml_{1}\cdot l_{2}-H_{2}p_{2}\cdot l_{1})p_{1}^{\nu}\right.

\displaystyle-\left.(Ml_{1}\cdot l_{2}-H_{1}p_{1}\cdot l_{2})p_{2}^{\nu}\right.

\displaystyle-\left.(Mp_{1}\cdot l_{2}-H_{2}p_{1}\cdot p_{2})l_{1}^{\nu}\right.

\displaystyle+\left.(Mp_{2}\cdot l_{1}-H_{1}p_{1}\cdot p_{2})l_{2}^{\nu}\right]Q^{\mu},

Substituting these into the expression for
H^{\mu\nu}_{S\bar{S}}
, the terms involving
T_{1}^{\mu\nu}
and
T_{2}^{\mu\nu}
can be expressed as:

\displaystyle-\frac{G_{M}F_{2}^{*}}{2M}T^{\mu\nu}_{1}-\frac{G_{M}^{*}F_{2}}{2M}T^{\mu\nu}_{2}

(A46)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{2}{M(\tau-1)}\left(|G_{M}|^{2}-|G_{E}||G_{M}|\cos(\Delta\Phi)\right)T_{S}^{\mu\nu}

\displaystyle T_{S}^{\mu\nu}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{4}(T_{1}^{\mu\nu}+T_{2}^{\mu\nu})

(A47)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(H_{2}P\cdot l_{1}+H_{1}P\cdot l_{2})(p_{1}^{\mu}p_{2}^{\nu}+p_{1}^{\nu}p_{2}^{\mu})

\displaystyle-2H_{2}P\cdot l_{1}p_{1}^{\mu}p_{1}^{\nu}-2H_{1}P\cdot l_{2}p_{2}^{\mu}p_{2}^{\nu}

\displaystyle-(MP\cdot l_{2}-\frac{1}{2}sH_{2})(Q^{\mu}l_{1}^{\nu}+Q^{\nu}l_{1}^{\mu})

\displaystyle+2M(l_{1}\cdot l_{2}+H_{1}H_{2})Q^{\mu}Q^{\nu},

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{4}(T_{1}^{\mu\nu}-T_{2}^{\mu\nu})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(H_{2}P\cdot l_{1}-H_{1}P\cdot l_{2})(p_{1}^{\mu}p_{2}^{\nu}-p_{1}^{\nu}p_{2}^{\mu})

The next step involves the contraction of
A^{\mu\nu}
and
T_{S}^{\mu\nu}
with

l_{1}^{\prime}
and

\Sigma^{+}
and
\bar{\Sigma}^{-}

\displaystyle l_{1}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{H_{1}}{M}(E,\vec{p})+\frac{\eta_{1}}{M}(p,E\hat{p})+(0,\vec{l}_{1\perp}^{\prime}),

(A49)

\displaystyle l_{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{H_{2}}{M}(E,-\vec{p})+\frac{\eta_{2}}{M}(-p,E\hat{p})+(0,\vec{l}_{2\perp}^{\prime}),

(A50)

where

\displaystyle\eta_{1}=\hat{p}\cdot\vec{l}_{1}^{\prime},\qquad\eta_{2}=\hat{p}\cdot\vec{l}_{2}^{\prime},

(A51)

and

\displaystyle\vec{l}_{1\perp}^{\prime}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\vec{l}_{1}^{\prime}-\eta_{1}\hat{p},

(A52)

\displaystyle\vec{l}_{2\perp}^{\prime}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\vec{l}_{2}^{\prime}-\eta_{2}\hat{p}.

(A53)

In addition, we also have defined the following shorthand

\displaystyle\zeta_{1}=\vec{l}_{1\perp}^{\prime}\cdot\hat{k},\qquad\zeta_{2}=\vec{l}_{2\perp}^{\prime}\cdot\hat{k},\qquad\gamma=\vec{l}_{1\perp}^{\prime}\cdot\vec{l}_{2\perp}^{\prime}.

(A54)

With these definitions in place, we can proceed to derive the following results.

\displaystyle l_{1}\cdot l_{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{H_{1}H_{2}}{M^{2}}(E^{2}+p^{2})-\frac{2E^{2}}{M^{2}}\eta_{1}\eta_{2}+\frac{2EH_{2}}{M^{2}}p\eta_{1}

(A55)

\displaystyle-\frac{2EH_{1}}{M^{2}}p\eta_{2}-(\gamma-\eta_{1}\eta_{2}),

\displaystyle P\cdot l_{1}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{2E^{2}H_{1}}{M}+\frac{2E}{M}p\eta_{1},

(A56)

\displaystyle P\cdot l_{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{2E^{2}H_{2}}{M}-\frac{2E}{M}p\eta_{2},

(A57)

and

\displaystyle l_{1}\cdot k_{1}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{EH_{1}}{M}(E-p\cos\theta)-E\zeta_{1}

\displaystyle+\frac{E}{M}(p-E\cos\theta)\eta_{1},

\displaystyle\frac{EH_{1}}{M}(E+p\cos\theta)+E\zeta_{1}

(A59)

\displaystyle+\frac{E}{M}(p+E\cos\theta)\eta_{1},

\displaystyle l_{2}\cdot k_{1}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{EH_{2}}{M}(E+p\cos\theta)-E\zeta_{2}

\displaystyle-\frac{E}{M}(p+E\cos\theta)\eta_{2},

\displaystyle\frac{EH_{2}}{M}(E-p\cos\theta)+E\zeta_{2}

\displaystyle(l_{1}^{\mu}l_{2}^{\nu}+l_{1}^{\nu}l_{2}^{\mu})L_{\mu\nu}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle s(\gamma-\zeta_{1}\zeta_{2})-\frac{sE}{M}(\eta_{2}\zeta_{1}+\eta_{1}\zeta_{2})\cos\theta

\displaystyle-\frac{sH_{1}H_{2}}{M^{2}}p^{2}\sin^{2}\theta+\frac{sE^{2}}{M^{2}}\eta_{1}\eta_{2}\sin^{2}\theta

\displaystyle-\frac{sEH_{2}p}{M^{2}}\eta_{1}\sin^{2}\theta+\frac{sEH_{1}p}{M^{2}}\eta_{2}\sin^{2}\theta

and

\displaystyle(p_{1}^{\mu}l_{1}^{\nu}+p_{1}^{\nu}l_{1}^{\mu})L_{\mu\nu}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{sH_{1}}{M}p^{2}\sin^{2}\theta+\frac{sE}{M}p\eta_{1}\sin^{2}\theta

(A63)

\displaystyle-sp\zeta_{1}\cos\theta,

\displaystyle(p_{2}^{\mu}l_{2}^{\nu}+p_{2}^{\nu}l_{2}^{\mu})L_{\mu\nu}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{sH_{2}}{M}p^{2}\sin^{2}\theta-\frac{sE}{M}p\eta_{2}\sin^{2}\theta

(A64)

\displaystyle+sp\zeta_{2}\cos\theta.

Furthermore, due to the condition
(p_{1}^{\mu}+p_{2}^{\mu})L_{\mu\nu}=0

and

are the spin four-vector of

\displaystyle s_{1}(\vec{p}_{1},\vec{n}_{1})=\frac{\vec{n}_{1}\cdot\hat{p}_{1}}{M}(|\vec{p}_{1}|,E\hat{p}_{1})+(0,\vec{n}_{1\perp}),

(A94)

\displaystyle s_{2}(\vec{p}_{2},\vec{n}_{2})=\frac{\vec{n}_{2}\cdot\hat{p}_{2}}{M}(|\vec{p}_{2}|,E\hat{p}_{2})+(0,\vec{n}_{2\perp}).

(A95)

Here,

\displaystyle\vec{n}_{1}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(\sin\theta_{1}\cos\phi_{1},\sin\theta_{1}\sin\phi_{1},\cos\theta_{1}),

(A96)

\displaystyle\vec{n}_{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(\sin\theta_{2}\cos\theta_{2},\sin\theta_{2}\sin\theta_{2},\cos\theta_{2}),

(A97)

and

\displaystyle p_{1}\cdot s_{1}=0,\qquad p_{2}\cdot s_{2}=0.

(A98)

Then, the differential cross section for the reaction is given by

\displaystyle\mathrm{d}\sigma_{s_{1}s_{2}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{4}\frac{1}{2s}|\mathcal{M}_{s_{1}s_{2}}|^{2}\frac{p}{16\pi^{2}\sqrt{s}}\ \mathrm{d}\Omega

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\mathrm{Tr}\left[(\not{p}_{1}+M)\frac{1}{2}(1+\gamma^{5}\not{s}_{1})\Gamma^{\mu}\right.

\displaystyle\left.\times(\not{p}_{2}-M)\frac{1}{2}(1-\gamma^{5}\not{s}_{2})\bar{\Gamma}^{\nu}\right].

\displaystyle\mathcal{F}_{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sin\theta\cos\theta(\cos\theta_{2}\sin\theta_{1}\cos\phi_{1}+\cos\theta_{1}\sin\theta_{2}\cos\phi_{2}),

\displaystyle\mathcal{F}_{3}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sin\theta\cos\theta\sin\theta_{1}\sin\phi_{1},

\displaystyle\mathcal{F}_{4}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sin\theta\cos\theta\sin\theta_{2}\sin\phi_{2},

\displaystyle\mathcal{F}_{5}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\cos^{2}\theta,

\displaystyle\mathcal{F}_{6}

\displaystyle\cos\theta_{1}\cos\theta_{2}-\sin^{2}\theta\sin\theta_{1}\sin\theta_{2}\sin\phi_{1}\sin\phi_{2}.

N_{s}

\displaystyle\frac{1}{N_{s}}\sum_{\pm s_{1}\pm s_{2}}\int G^{\mu\nu}_{s_{1}s_{2}}L_{\mu\nu}\,\mathrm{d}\Omega=1.

\displaystyle\frac{\mathrm{d}\sigma_{s_{1}s_{2}}}{\mathrm{d}\Omega}=\frac{\pi\beta\alpha_{em}^{2}}{s^{2}}D(s)\left(1+\frac{\alpha}{3}\right)W_{s_{1}s_{2}}.
