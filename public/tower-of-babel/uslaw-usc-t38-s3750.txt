38 U.S.C. § 3750 — Incorporation of other provisions by the Secretary
Citation: us:usc:38:3750
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section3750
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part III (Readjustment And Related Benefits); Chapter 37 (Housing And Small Business Loans); Subchapter IV (Small Business Loans). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may provide that the provisions of sections of other subchapters of this chapter that are not otherwise applicable to loans made or guaranteed under this subchapter shall be applicable to loans made or guaranteed under this subchapter. The Secretary shall exercise authority under the preceding sentence by regulations prescribed after publication in the Federal Register and a period of not less than thirty days for public comment.
