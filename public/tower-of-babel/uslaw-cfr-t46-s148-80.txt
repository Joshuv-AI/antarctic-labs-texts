46 C.F.R. § 148.80 — Supervision of cargo transfer.
Citation: us:cfr:46:148.80
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-148.80
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 148 (Carriage Of Bulk Solid Materials That Require Special Handling). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The master must ensure that cargo transfer operations are supervised by a responsible person as defined in § 148.3 of this part.
