5 C.F.R. § 551.217 — Exemption of Border Patrol agents.
Citation: us:cfr:5:551.217
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-551.217
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 551 (Pay Administration Under The Fair Labor Standards Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Border Patrol agent (as defined in 5 U.S.C. 5550(a)(2) and 5 CFR 550.1603) is exempt from the minimum wage and the hours of work and overtime pay provisions of the Act.
