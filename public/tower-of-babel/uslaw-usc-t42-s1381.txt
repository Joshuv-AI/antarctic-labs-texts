42 U.S.C. § 1381 — Statement of purpose; authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:42:1381
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1381
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 7 (Social Security); Subchapter XVI (Supplemental Security Income For Aged, Blind, And Disabled). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purpose of establishing a national program to provide supplemental security income to individuals who have attained age 65 or are blind or disabled, there are authorized to be appropriated sums sufficient to carry out this subchapter.
