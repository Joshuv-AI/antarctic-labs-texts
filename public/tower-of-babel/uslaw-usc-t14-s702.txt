14 U.S.C. § 702 — State Department
Citation: us:usc:14:702
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section702
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 7 (Cooperation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Coast Guard, through the Secretary, may exchange information, through the Secretary of State, with foreign governments and suggest to the Secretary of State international collaboration and conferences on all matters dealing with the safety of life and property at sea, other than radio communication.
