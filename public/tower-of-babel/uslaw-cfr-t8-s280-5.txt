8 C.F.R. § 280.5 — Mitigation or remission of fines.
Citation: us:cfr:8:280.5
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-280.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 280 (Imposition And Collection Of Fines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In any case in which mitigation or remission of a fine is authorized by the Immigration and Nationality Act, the party served with Notice of Intention to Fine may apply in writing to the district director or the Associate Commissioner for Examinations, or the Director for the National Fines Office for such mitigation or remission.
