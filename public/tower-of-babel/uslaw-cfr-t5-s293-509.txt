5 C.F.R. § 293.509 — Use of existing Employee Medical Folders upon transfer or reemployment.
Citation: us:cfr:5:293.509
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-293.509
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 293 (Personnel Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The requirements of § 293.306, regarding the use of existing OPFs, apply to the use of existing EMFs upon the employee's transfer to or reemployment in a new employing agency.
