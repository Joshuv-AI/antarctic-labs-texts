46 C.F.R. § 16.240 — Serious marine incident testing requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:46:16.240
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-16.240
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 16 (Chemical Testing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The marine employer must ensure that all persons directly involved in a serious marine incident are chemically tested for evidence of dangerous drugs and alcohol in accordance with the requirements of subpart 4.06 of this chapter.
