16 C.F.R. § 1502.22 — Unavailability of presiding officer.

Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1502.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1502 (Procedures For Formal Evidentiary Public Hearing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If the presiding officer is unable to act for any reason, the Commission will assign the powers and duties to another presiding officer. The substitution will not affect the hearing, except as the new presiding officer may order.
(b) Any motion based on the substitution must be made within 10 days.
