6 C.F.R. § 9.20 — Reporting.
Citation: us:cfr:6:9.20
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security, Office Of The Secretary
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 6 (Domestic Security), Part 9 (Restrictions Upon Lobbying). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No reporting is required with respect to payments of reasonable compensation made to regularly employed officers or employees of a person.
