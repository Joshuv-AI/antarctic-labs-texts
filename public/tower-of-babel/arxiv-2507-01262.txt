Structure studies of 257 Db through combined α \alpha , γ \gamma and internal-conversion-electron spectroscopy
P. Brionnet, A. Lopez-Martens, O. Dorvaux, Z. Asfari, M.L. Chelnokov, V.I. Chepigin, H. Faure, B. Gall, K. Hauschild, A.V. Isaev, I.N. Izosimov, A.A. Kuznetsova, O.N. Malyshev, R.S Mukhin, J. Abstract
This work reports on the study of the decay properties along the 257 Db decay chain using the GABRIELA setup. The first observation of a high-K isomer in 257 Db is presented. In addition, an unreported α \alpha -decay branch in 249 Md has been evidenced, allowing to constrain the differences in energy of the α \alpha -decaying levels in 249 Md, 253 Lr and 257 Db. Finally, the combination of the observed fine structure α \alpha -decay from the high-spin state in 257 Db with the first observation the internal decay in 253 Lr requires a revision of level and decay scheme. In particular, a change of parity for the high-spin state from 9/2 + to 9/2 - in the 257 Db is suggested, and the implications of such a change are also discussed.

In conclusion, a new study of the decay properties of

In order to explain the observed double-humped structure of the

-
[505] state, originating from the same

-
[514] ground state of

[514] state are difficult to explain, though some arguments have been put forward, in particular a relative positioning of spherical high-
