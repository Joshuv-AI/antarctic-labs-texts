7 C.F.R. § 1434.14 — Loss or damage.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1434.14
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1434.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1434 (Nonrecourse Marketing Assistance Loans And Loan Deficiency Payments For Honey). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The producer is responsible for any loss in quantity or quality of the honey pledged as collateral for a loan. CCC will not assume any loss in quantity or quality of the loan collateral.
