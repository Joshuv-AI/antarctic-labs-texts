5 C.F.R. § 531.402 — Employee coverage.
Citation: us:cfr:5:531.402
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-531.402
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 531 (Pay Under The General Schedule). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Except as provided in paragraph (b) of this section, this subpart applies to employees who—
(1) Are classified and paid under the General Schedule;
(2) Occupy permanent positions; and
(3) Are paid less than the maximum rate of their grade.
(b) This subpart does not apply to any employee who is appointed by the President, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate.
