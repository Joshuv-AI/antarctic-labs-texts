13 C.F.R. § 134.401 — Scope of the rules in this subpart D.
Citation: us:cfr:13:134.401
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-134.401
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 134 (Rules Of Procedure Governing Cases Before The Office Of Hearings And Appeals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The rules of practice in this subpart D apply to all appeals to OHA from:
(a) Denials of 8(a) BD program admission based solely on a negative finding(s) of social disadvantage, economic disadvantage, ownership or control pursuant to § 124.206 of this title;
(b) Early graduation pursuant to §§ 124.302 and 124.304;
(c) Termination pursuant to §§ 124.303 and 124.304;
(d) Denials of requests to issue a waiver pursuant to § 124.515; and
(e) Suspensions pursuant to § 124.305(a).
