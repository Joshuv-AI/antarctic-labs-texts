5 C.F.R. § 332.404 — Order of selection from certificates.
Citation: us:cfr:5:332.404
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-332.404
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 332 (Recruitment And Selection Through Competitive Examination). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An appointing officer, with sole regard to merit and fitness, shall select any eligible candidate certified for appointment on a certificate of eligibles, except the hiring manager may not pass over a preference eligible to select a lower standing non-preference eligible on the certificate unless the agency complies with pass over procedures in accordance with § 332.406.
