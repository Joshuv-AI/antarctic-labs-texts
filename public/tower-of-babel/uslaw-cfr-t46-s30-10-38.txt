46 C.F.R. § 30.10-38 — -38 Lightweight—TB/ALL.
Citation: us:cfr:46:30.10-38
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-30.10-38
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 30 (General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term lightweight means the displacement of a vessel in metric tons without cargo, oil fuel, lubricating oil, ballast water, fresh water, feedwater in tanks, consumable stores, and persons and their effects.
