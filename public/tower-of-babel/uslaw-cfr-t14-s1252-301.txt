14 C.F.R. § 1252.301 — Notice to subrecipients.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1252.301
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1252.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1252 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Age In Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where a recipient passes on Federal financial assistance from NASA to subrecipients, the recipient shall provide the subrecipient written notice of their obligations under these regulations.
