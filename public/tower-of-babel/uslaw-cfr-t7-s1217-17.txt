7 C.F.R. § 1217.17 — Nominal size or count.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1217.17
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Miscellaneous Commodities), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1217.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1217 (Softwood Lumber Research, Promotion, Consumer Education And Industry Information Order). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nominal size or count means the size by which softwood lumber is known and sold in the marketplace that differs from actual or net size and is based on the thickness and width of a board when it is first cut from a log, or rough cut, prior to drying and planing.
