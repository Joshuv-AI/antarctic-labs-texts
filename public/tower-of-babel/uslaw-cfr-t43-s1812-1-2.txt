43 C.F.R. § 1812.1-2 — -2 Inquiries.
Citation: us:cfr:43:1812.1-2
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-1812.1-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 1810 (Introduction And General Guidance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person other than officers or employees of the Department of the Interior shall direct any inquiry to any employee of the Bureau with respect to any matter pending before it other than to the head of the unit in which the matter is pending, to a superior officer, or to an employee of the unit authorized by the unit head to answer inquiries.
