42 C.F.R. § 93.212 — Falsification.
Citation: us:cfr:42:93.212
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-93.212
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 93 (Public Health Service Policies On Research Misconduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Falsification means manipulating research materials, equipment, or processes, or changing or omitting data or results such that the research is not accurately represented in the research record.
