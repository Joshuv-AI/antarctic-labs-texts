25 U.S.C. § 2209 — Trusteeship title of United States for any Indian or Indian tribe
Citation: us:usc:25:2209
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section2209
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 24 (Indian Land Consolidation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Title to any land acquired under this chapter by any Indian or Indian tribe shall be taken in trust by the United States for that Indian or Indian tribe.
