34 U.S.C. § 10154 — Review of applications
Citation: us:usc:34:10154
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section10154
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 101 (Justice System Improvement); Subchapter V (Bureau Of Justice Assistance Grant Programs); Part A (Edward Byrne Memorial Justice Assistance Grant Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Attorney General shall not finally disapprove any application (or any amendment to that application) submitted under this part without first affording the applicant reasonable notice of any deficiencies in the application and opportunity for correction and reconsideration.
