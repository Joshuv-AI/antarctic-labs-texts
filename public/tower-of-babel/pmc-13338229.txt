Study on the main causes of oil and gas pipeline accidents based on improved TOPSIS method
Authors: Xuefeng Chen, Wentao Chen, Yifu Zhang, Yuling Wang
Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
Oil and gas pipeline accidents not only caused casualties, economic losses and environmental pollution, but also brought great panic to the public. In order to prevent and reduce oil and gas pipeline accidents, it is necessary to study the main causes of oil and gas pipeline accidents. Its purpose is provide targeted safety measures for accident prevention and control. In this article, the main causes of oil and gas pipeline accidents based on improved TOPSIS method are studied. Firstly, a causal network of oil and gas pipeline accidents based on complex network theory is constructed. Secondly, by analyzing network importance evaluation indicators of the nodes, the determination of the main causes of oil and gas pipeline accidents are transformed into the multi-attribute decision-making evaluation of the importance of accident causal network nodes. Thirdly, a main causes determination method for oil and gas pipeline accidents based on improved TOPISIS method is proposed. The proposed method combines complex network theory, multi-attribute decision theory and improved TOPSISI method, and can address the issue of the significant impact of the correlation between node importance evaluation indicators in the causal network of oil and gas pipeline accidents on decision-making results. Subsequently, the method is applied to the study of main causes and preventive measures of an oil and gas pipelines in central China. Based on the mentioned analysis, targeted safety measures are put forward to prevent oil and gas pipeline accidents. Therefore, this study can provide decision-making basis for oil and gas pipeline accidents safety management.Supplementary InformationThe online version contains supplementary material available at 10.1038/s41598-026-51648-y.

Introduction

With the rapid development of the industry, the number of oil and gas pipelines is increasing year by year. However, due to corrosion, design defect, operation err, third party damage and natural disaster, the pipelines accidents often happen1. In order to prevent and reduce accidents, many accident cause analysis methods are widely used in the oil and gas pipeline industry. Its purpose is to explore the pipeline accidents, pipeline failure modes and accidents causes as well as their relationships, thereby providing decision-making basis for pipeline accident prevention and control.

In recent years, many accident cause analysis methods have been proposed in the oil and gas pipeline industry. For instance, Lin and Chien constructed a causal cycle of production, safety and control modules to form a complex system to explore the root cause of the natural gas pipeline accidents2. Liu et al. employed K-means clustering and co-occurrence network to identify the influencing factors of pipeline accidents and their causal dependence. It provided a new method for accident analysis and prevention3. Li et al. propose a dynamic risk modeling of urban natural gas pipeline accidents using Stochastic Petri net (SPN). An SPN model of an accident evolution process is constructed based on the discrete events in an accident flowchart, and the critical places and transitions are evaluated through this model4. Xiao and Xiong applied advanced data interpolation techniques to create a complete gas transmission pipeline data set and evaluate the impact of environmental factors on pipeline failure prediction5. However, the current accident cause analysis method mainly focuses on independent causes of oil and gas pipeline accidents and accident evolution process of oil and gas pipelines, often ignoring the inherent correlation between these causal events. This cannot fully reflect the real causes for oil and gas pipelines accidents. This leads to the deterioration of the quality of current accident main cause analysis method for oil and gas pipelines. The current accident cause analysis method cannot provide targeted safety management strategies for oil and gas pipelines.

In this article, the main causes of oil and gas pipeline accidents based on improved TOPSIS method are studied. The purpose of this study is to rectify the lacunas as addressed above and enrich and develop the current cause analysis method for oil and gas pipeline accidents.The proposed method can also help managers and workers to develop safety management strategies for oil and gas pipelines.

Analysis of causes in oil and gas pipeline accidents

Analysis of causes of oil and gas pipeline accidents in some databases

In order to prevent and reduce oil and gas pipeline accidents, developed countries have established oil and gas pipeline accident database, such as Pipeline and Hazardous Materials Safety Administration (PHMSA) database, European Gas Incident Group (EGIG) database and Canada’s National Energy Commission (NEB) database6.

Analysis of causes of oil and gas pipeline accidents in PHMSA database

The causes for statistical failure of PHMSA can be summarized into seven categories: corrosion failure, natural force cause, excavation damage, material/welding failure, other external force damage, incorrect operation and other incident cause7. Between 1994 and 2018, a total of 3557 onshore natural gas pipeline accidents are recorded in PHMSA database. The quantity and proportion are presented in Figs. 1 and 2. From Figs. 1 and 2, material/welding failure is the primary cause of gas pipeline accidents in the United States, followed by corrosion failure, excavation damage, other incident cause, natural force cause, other external force damage and incorrect operation.

Analysis of causes of oil and gas pipeline accidents in EGIG database

The causes for statistical failure of EGIG can be summarized into six categories: external interference, construction defect/material failure, corrosion, ground movement, hot tap made by error, other and unknown8. Complex network theory

Complex network theory is to use the fundamental graph theory to study complex systems. Its purpose is to describe the complex interrelationships between complex system components15. The components of a complex system are represented by nodes, and the interrelationships between components are represented by edges. The characteristic parameters of complex networks are degree, degree distribution, average path length, clustering coefficient and betweenness. The characteristic parameters of complex network are introduced as follow.

Determination of main causes of oil and gas pipeline accidents based on improved TOPSIS method

The determination of the main causes of oil and gas pipeline accidents causal network is to treat each node in the network as a scheme to be evaluated, and regard network nodes importance evaluation indicators as the multiple attributes indicators of each scheme to be evaluated. Therefore, the importance evaluation of network nodes is transformed into a multi-attribute decision-making problem for each scheme to be evaluated18.

In this article, TOPSIS method is used for multi-attribute decision-making of network node importance evaluation. The basic principle of TOPSIS method is to rank the closeness between limited evaluation scheme and ideal scheme. The optimal evaluation scheme is the one that is closest to the positive ideal scheme and farthest from the negative ideal scheme. Furthermore, the weighted mahalanobis distance are used instead of Euclidean distance in traditional TOPSIS methods19. The purpose is to address the issue of the significant impact of the correlation between node importance evaluation indicators in the causal network of oil and gas pipeline accidents on decision-making results. Application study

Brief introduction of pipeline enterprise

By the end of 2024, an oil and gas pipeline enterprise in central China had a mileage of 3,322.43 km, a natural gas supply capacity of 19.854 billion cubic meters per year and a one-time pipeline transportation capacity of 11 million tons per year. In recent years, due to corrosion, design defect, operation err, third party damage and natural disaster, the pipelines accidents often happen. Therefore, in order to effectively prevent and reduce accidents, it is necessary to study the main causes of oil and gas pipeline accidents in this enterprise, so as to provide targeted safety measures for oil and gas pipeline accidents prevention and control .

Conclusions

Oil and gas pipeline accidents not only caused casualties, economic losses and environmental pollution, but also brought great panic to the public. In order to prevent and reduce oil and gas pipeline accidents, it is necessary to study the main causes of oil and gas pipeline accidents. In this article, the main causes of oil and gas pipeline accidents based on improved TOPSIS method are studied. Firstly, a causal network of oil and gas pipeline accidents based on complex network theory is constructed. Secondly, by analyzing network importance evaluation indicators of the nodes, the determination of the main causes of oil and gas pipeline accidents are transformed into the multi-attribute decision-making evaluation of the importance of accident causal network nodes.Thirdly, a main causes determination method for oil and gas pipeline accidents based on improved TOPISIS method is proposed. The proposed method combines complex network theory, multi-attribute decision theory and improved TOPSISI method, and can address the issue of the significant impact of the correlation between node importance evaluation indicators in the causal network of oil and gas pipeline accidents on decision-making results.

The method is applied to the study of main causes of an oil and gas pipelines in central China. Its results demonstrates that the importance ranking of the causes are inner protective layer failure, outer protective layer damage, poor daily maintenance management, improper operation, third party construction defects, unclear identification, irresponsible inspection, etc. Subsequently, targeted safety measures are proposed to prevent oil and gas pipeline accidents. This study can be used to analyze the oil and gas pipeline accident main causes and providing decision-making basis for safety measures to prevent oil and gas pipeline accidents.
