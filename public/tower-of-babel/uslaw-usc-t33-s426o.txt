33 U.S.C. § 426o — Great Lakes material disposal
Citation: us:usc:33:426o
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section426o
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 9 (Protection Of Navigable Waters And Of Harbor And River Improvements Generally); Subchapter I (In General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In planning and implementing any navigation project (including maintenance thereof) on the Great Lakes and adjacent waters, the Secretary shall consult and cooperate with concerned States in selecting disposal areas for dredged material which is suitable for beach nourishment.
