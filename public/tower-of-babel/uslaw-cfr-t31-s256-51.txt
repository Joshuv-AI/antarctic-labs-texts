31 C.F.R. § 256.51 — Does Fiscal Service report Judgment Fund payments to the IRS as income to the payee on IRS Form 1099?
Citation: us:cfr:31:256.51
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-256.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 256 (Obtaining Payments From The Judgment Fund And Under Private Relief Bills). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No, Fiscal Service does not report Judgment Fund payments as potential taxable income to the IRS. Fiscal Service does not have sufficient information about the payment to determine if a Form 1099 must be issued or to prepare such a form when required. To the extent any Form 1099 needs to be issued, it is the responsibility of the agency submitting the payment request to do so.
