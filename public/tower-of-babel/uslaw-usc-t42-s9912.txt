42 U.S.C. § 9912 — Office of Community Services
Citation: us:usc:42:9912
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section9912
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 106 (Community Services Block Grant Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Office
The Secretary shall carry out the functions of this chapter through an Office of Community Services, which shall be established in the Department of Health and Human Services. The Office shall be headed by a Director.
(b) Grants, contracts, and cooperative agreements
The Secretary shall carry out functions of this chapter through grants, contracts, or cooperative agreements.
