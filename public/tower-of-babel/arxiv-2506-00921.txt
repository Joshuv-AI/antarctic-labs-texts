Girth and Laplacian eigenvalue distribution
Leyou Xu, Bo Zhou

Abstract
Let G G be a connected graph of order n n with girth g g . For k = 1 , … , min ⁡ { g − 1 , n − g } k=1,\dots,\min\{g-1,n-g\} , let n ⁡ ( G , k ) n(G,k) be the number of Laplacian eigenvalues (counting multiplicities) of G G that fall inside the interval [ n − g − k + 4 , n ] [n-g-k+4,n] . We prove that if g ≥ 4 g\geq 4 , then n ⁡ ( G , k ) ≤ n − g . n(G,k)\leq n-g. Those graphs achieving the bound for k = 1 , 2 k=1,2 are determined. We also determine the graphs G G with g = 3 g=3 such that n ⁡ ( G , k ) = n − 1 , n − 2 , n − 3 n(G,k)=n-1,n-2,n-3 . Keywords: Laplacian eigenvalue distribution, girth, cycle AMSC: 05C50, 15A18, 05C38

Proof of Theorem
3
.

From Lemma
9
and the fact that the
number of connected components of
\overline{G}
is equal to

\displaystyle m_{G}(n)=n-3

\displaystyle\Leftrightarrow\sigma_{L}(G)=\{n^{[n-3]},a,b,0\}\mbox{ with some $a,b$ with $n>a\geq b>0$}

\displaystyle\Leftrightarrow\sigma_{L}(\overline{G})=\{n-b,n-a,0^{[n-2]}\}\mbox{ with some $a,b$ with $n>a\geq b>0$}

\displaystyle\Leftrightarrow\overline{G}\cong(n-3)K_{1}\cup K_{3},(n-3)K_{1}\cup P_{3},(n-4)K_{1}\cup 2K_{2}

\displaystyle\Leftrightarrow G\cong K_{n-3}\vee 3K_{1},K_{n-3}\vee(K_{1}\cup K_{2}),K_{n-4}\vee 2K_{2}.

The Item (i) follows by noting that
m_{K_{n}}(n)=n-1
and
m_{K_{n}-e}(n)=n-2
.

In the following, we prove Item (ii).

It is trivial that
m_{G}[n-1,n]=n-1
if and only if
G\cong K_{n}
. Suppose that
G\ncong K_{n}
. Let

be the diameter of

. Then

, and

.

, where

Suppose that

for any

, where
