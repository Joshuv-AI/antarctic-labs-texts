12 C.F.R. § 1217.8 — Statute of limitations.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1217.8
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1217.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1217 (Program Fraud Civil Remedies Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The statute of limitations for commencing a hearing under this part shall be tolled:
(a) If the hearing is commenced in accordance with 31 U.S.C. 3803(d)(2)(B) within 6 years after the date on which the claim or statement is made; or
(b) If the parties agree to such tolling.
