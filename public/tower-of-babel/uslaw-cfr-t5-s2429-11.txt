5 C.F.R. § 2429.11 — Interlocutory appeals.

Creator: Federal Labor Relations Authority, General Counsel Of The Federal Labor Relations Authority And Federal Service Impasses Panel
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-2429.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 2429 (Miscellaneous And General Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as set forth in part 2423, the Authority and the General Counsel ordinarily will not consider interlocutory appeals.
