18 U.S.C. § 1117 — Conspiracy to murder
Citation: us:usc:18:1117
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section1117
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 51 (Homicide). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If two or more persons conspire to violate section 1111, 1114, 1116, or 1119 of this title, and one or more of such persons do any overt act to effect the object of the conspiracy, each shall be punished by imprisonment for any term of years or for life.
