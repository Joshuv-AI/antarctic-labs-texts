44 U.S.C. § 3107 — Authority of Comptroller General
Citation: us:usc:44:3107
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section3107
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 31 (Records Management By Federal Agencies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Chapters 21, 25, 27,1 29, and 31 of this title do not limit the authority of the Comptroller General of the United States with respect to prescribing accounting systems, forms, and procedures, or lessen the responsibility of collecting and disbursing officers for rendition of their accounts for settlement by the Government Accountability Office.
