9 C.F.R. § 201.70 — Restriction or limitation of competition between packers and dealers prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:9:201.70
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Fair Trade Practices Program), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-201.70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 201 (Administering The Packers And Stockyards Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each packer and dealer engaged in purchasing livestock, in person or through employed buyers, shall conduct his buying operations in competition with, and independently of, other packers and dealers similarly engaged.
