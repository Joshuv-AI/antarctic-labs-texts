15 C.F.R. § 922.161 — Boundary.
Citation: us:cfr:15:922.161
Creator: National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-922.161
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 922 (National Marine Sanctuary Program Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The sanctuary consists of an area of approximately 2,872 square nautical miles (nmi 2) (3,803 sq. mi.) of coastal and ocean waters, and the submerged lands thereunder, surrounding the Florida Keys in Florida. Appendix I to this subpart sets forth the precise Sanctuary boundary.
