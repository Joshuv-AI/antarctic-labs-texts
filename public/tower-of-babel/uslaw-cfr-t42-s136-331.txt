42 C.F.R. § 136.331 — Selection.
Citation: us:cfr:42:136.331
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-136.331
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 136 (Indian Health). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Secretary, acting through the Service, shall determine the individuals who receive Indian Health Scholarships.
(b) Priority shall be given to applicants who are Indians.
