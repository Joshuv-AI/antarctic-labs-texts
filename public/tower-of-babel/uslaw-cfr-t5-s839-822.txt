5 C.F.R. § 839.822 — Which employer is responsible for submitting the employee and employer retirement deductions and contributions and correcting my records if I had different employers?
Citation: us:cfr:5:839.822
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-839.822
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 839 (Correction Of Retirement Coverage Errors Under The Federal Erroneous Retirement Coverage Corrections Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Your current or most recent employer will be responsible for this purpose. Even if that employer was not involved in the retirement coverage error, it must issue corrected records for the entire period of the retirement coverage error.
