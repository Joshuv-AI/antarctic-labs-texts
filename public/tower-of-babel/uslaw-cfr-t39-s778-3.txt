39 C.F.R. § 778.3 — What categories of facility project actions of the Postal Service are subject to these regulations?

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 778 (Intergovernmental Review Of Postal Service Facility Actions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Postal Service publishes in the Federal Register a list of its categories of facility project actions that are subject to these regulations.
