47 C.F.R. § 3.44 — Time to achieve settlements.
Citation: us:cfr:47:3.44
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-3.44
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 3 (Authorization And Administration Of Accounting Authorities In Maritime And Maritime Mobile-Satellite Radio Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All maritime telecommunications accounts should be timely paid in accordance with applicable ITU Regulations, Article 66 and International Telecommunication Regulations (Melbourne, 1988). Accounting authorities are deemed to be responsible for remitting, in a timely manner, all valid amounts due to foreign administrations or their agents.
