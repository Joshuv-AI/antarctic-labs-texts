OFFICE OF PERSONNEL MANAGEMENT


Senior Executive Service--Performance Review Board

AGENCY: Office of Personnel Management.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

[[Page 61882]]

SUMMARY: Notice is hereby given of the appointment of members of the 
OPM Performance Review Board.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Kimberly Sylke, Office of the Chief 
Human Capital Officer, Office of Personnel Management, 1900 E Street 
NW, Washington, DC 20415, (202) 936-3339.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Section 4314(c)(1) through (5) of Title 5, 
U.S.C., requires each agency to establish, in accordance with 
regulations prescribed by the Office of Personnel Management, one or 
more SES performance review boards. The board reviews and evaluates the 
initial appraisal of a senior executive's performance by the supervisor 
and considers recommendations to the appointing authority regarding the 
performance of the senior executive. The following have been designated 
as members of the Fiscal Year 2026 Performance Review Board of the U.S. 
Office of Personnel Management:

Kurt Dykstra, General Counsel (Chair)
Christina Bonarrigo, Senior Advisor to the Director
Marina Golovkina, White House Liaison
Veronica Hinton, Associate Director, Workforce Policy and Innovation
Joseph Knouff, Suitability Director
Katie McGuiness, Chief Financial Officer
Tyshawn Thomas, Chief Human Capital Officer

Office of Personnel Management.
Alexys Stanley,
Federal Register Liaison.
