40 C.F.R. § 370.2 — Who do the terms you, I, and your refer to in this part?
Citation: us:cfr:40:370.2
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-370.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 370 (Hazardous Chemical Reporting: Community Right-To-Know). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Throughout this part the terms you, I, and your refer to the owner or operator of a facility.
