On automatic boundedness of some operators in ordered Banach spaces


Abstract
We study order-to-weak continuous operators from an ordered Banach space to a normed space. It is proved that under rather mild conditions every order-to-weak continuous operator is bounded.

2
Main results

It is established in
[
7
, Theorem 2.8]
that every order-to-norm bounded operator from
an OBS with closed generating cone to a NS is bounded (see also
[
6
, Theorem 2.1]
for the Banach lattice case).
Accordingly to
[
7
, Theorem 2.4]
, each order bounded operator from an OVS to an OVS with generating cones is ru-continuous.
Combining these two results and keeping in mind that order intervals in an OBS with normal cone are bounded, we obtain the following automatic
boundedness conditions of order bounded operators:

(*)

{\cal L}_{ob}(X,Y)\subseteq\text{\rm L}(X,Y)
whenever
X
and
Y
are OBSs with generating cones such that
X_{+}
is closed and
Y_{+}
is normal.

Another case of automatic boundedness of operators concerns the order continuity.
Namely, as every order continuous operator from an Archimedean OVS with generating cone to an OVS is order bounded
[
7
, Corollary 2.2]
, we have:

(**)

{\cal L}_{oc}(X,Y)\subseteq\text{\rm L}(X,Y)
whenever
X
and
Y
are OBSs with generating cones such that
X_{+}
is closed and
Y_{+}
is normal.

Condition
X_{+}-X_{+}=X
is essential in both (*) and (**) since each operator
T:X\to X
is order bounded and order continuous whenever
X_{+}=\{0\}

An inspection of
[

tells us that each infinite dimensional Banach space

has a non-closed generating Archimedean cone so that
every operator
T:X\to X
is order continuous (and hence order bounded by
[
7
, Corollary 2.2]
).

It is worth nothing that the order-to-norm and order-to-weak continuous operators on VLs were defined in

without specifying the class of (just linear or continuous linear) operators.
Automatic boundedness of such operators was investigated in recent paper
