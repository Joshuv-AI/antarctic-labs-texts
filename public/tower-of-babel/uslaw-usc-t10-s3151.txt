10 U.S.C. § 3151 — Joint policy requirement
Citation: us:usc:10:3151
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section3151
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part V (Acquisition); Subpart A (General); Chapter 209 (Operational Contract Support); Subchapter I (Joint Policies On Requirements Definition, Contingency Program Management, And Contingency Contracting). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Defense, in consultation with the Chairman of the Joint Chiefs of Staff, shall develop joint policies for requirements definition, contingency program management, and contingency contracting during combat operations and post-conflict operations.
