32 C.F.R. § 842.45 — Scope of this subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:32:842.45
Creator: Department Of The Air Force
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-842.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 842 (Administrative Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart tells how to settle and pay claims against the United States presented by inhabitants of foreign countries for property damage, personal injury, or death caused by military and civilian members of the U.S. Armed Forces in foreign countries.
