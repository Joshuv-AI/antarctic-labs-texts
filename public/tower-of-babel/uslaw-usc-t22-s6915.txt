22 U.S.C. § 6915 — Expenditure of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:22:6915
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section6915
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 77 (United States-China Relations); Subchapter II (Congressional-Executive Commission On The People’S Republic Of China). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For each fiscal year for which an appropriation is made to the Commission, the Commission shall issue a report to the Congress on its expenditures under that appropriation.
