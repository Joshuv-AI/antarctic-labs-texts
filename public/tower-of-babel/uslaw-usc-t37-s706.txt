37 U.S.C. § 706 — Allotments: commissioned officers of the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration
Citation: us:usc:37:706
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title37-section706
Context: United States Code, Title 37 (Pay And Allowances Of The Uniformed Services); Chapter 13 (Allotments And Assignments Of Pay). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Under regulations prescribed by the Secretary of Commerce, commissioned officers of the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration may make allotments or assignments of their pay.
