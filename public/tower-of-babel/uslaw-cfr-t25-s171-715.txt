25 C.F.R. § 171.715 — How do I obtain an Annual Assessment Waiver?
Citation: us:cfr:25:171.715
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-171.715
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 171 (Irrigation Operation And Maintenance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For your land to be granted an Annual Assessment Waiver, you must:
(a) Send us a request in writing to have your land granted an Annual Assessment Waiver;
(b) Submit your request prior to the bill due date for the year for which you are requesting the Annual Assessment Waiver; and
(c) Receive our approval in writing.
