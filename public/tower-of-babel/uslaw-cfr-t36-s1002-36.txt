36 C.F.R. § 1002.36 — Gambling.
Citation: us:cfr:36:1002.36
Creator: Presidio Trust
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1002.36
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1002 (Resource Protection, Public Use And Recreation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Gambling in any form, or the operation of gambling devices, is prohibited.
(b) This regulation applies, regardless of land ownership, on all lands and waters within the boundaries of the area administered by the Presidio Trust that are under the legislative jurisdiction of the United States.
