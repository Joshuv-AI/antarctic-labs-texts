5 C.F.R. § 847.802 — Who may elect to use NAFI service to qualify for immediate retirement under CSRS or FERS?
Citation: us:cfr:5:847.802
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-847.802
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 847 (Elections Of Retirement Coverage By Current And Former Employees Of Nonappropriated Fund Instrumentalities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

CSRS and FERS employees may elect to credit NAFI service for retirement purposes under this subpart if:
(a) They separate for retirement on or after December 28, 2001;
(b) They do not otherwise qualify for immediate retirement; and
(c) They have enough otherwise creditable civilian service to qualify for deferred retirement.
