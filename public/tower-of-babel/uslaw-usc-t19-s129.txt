19 U.S.C. § 129 — Discriminating duties
Citation: us:usc:19:129
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section129
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 3 (The Tariff And Related Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No part of the additional or discriminating duty imposed by law on merchandise on account of its importation in foreign vessels shall be allowed to be drawback, but the whole shall be retained.
