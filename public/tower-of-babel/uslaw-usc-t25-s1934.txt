25 U.S.C. § 1934 — “Indian” defined for certain purposes
Citation: us:usc:25:1934
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section1934
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 21 (Indian Child Welfare); Subchapter II (Indian Child And Family Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purposes of sections 1932 and 1933 of this title, the term “Indian” shall include persons defined in section 1603(c) 1 of this title.
