42 U.S.C. § 9904 — Establishment of block grant program
Citation: us:usc:42:9904
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section9904
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 106 (Community Services Block Grant Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized to establish a community services block grant program and make grants through the program to States to ameliorate the causes of poverty in communities within the States.
