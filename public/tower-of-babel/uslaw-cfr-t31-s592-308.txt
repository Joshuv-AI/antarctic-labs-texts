31 C.F.R. § 592.308 — Participant.
Citation: us:cfr:31:592.308
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-592.308
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 592 (Rough Diamonds Control Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term Participant means a state, customs territory, or regional economic integration organization identified by the Secretary of State as one for which rough diamonds are controlled through the Kimberley Process Certification Scheme.
