A note on abelian envelopes
Anya Nordskova

Abstract
. This is a short note bridging the gap between two notions of universal abelian categories associated to exact categories, namely, Rump’s quotient categories and Bodzenta-Bondal’s abelian envelopes. The established connection allows us to draw several easy conclusions, in particular, we answer some (basic) questions raised by Bodzenta and Bondal in [ 2 ] .



1.
Introduction

In
[
13
]
Rump defines the
left (resp. right) quotient category

Q_{l}(\EuScript E)
(resp.
Q_{r}(\EuScript E)
) for any exact category
\EuScript E
. More generally, he considers left (right) exact categories, which are defined by imposing only half of the axioms of the usual definition, see Section
2
for details. The category
Q_{l}(\EuScript E)
comes with an exact full embedding
i_{l}:\EuScript E\to Q_{l}(\EuScript E)
and is
left abelian
(a natural “one-sided” generalisation of the notion of abelian categories). It was shown in
[
13
]
that
Q_{l}(\EuScript E)
together with
i_{l}
is universal among left abelian categories
\EuScript A
and right exact functors
\EuScript E\to\EuScript A
, see Theorem
2.6
for the precise statement. In
[
2
]
Bodzenta and Bondal defined
right (resp. left) abelian envelopes

\EuScript A_{r}(\EuScript E)
(resp.
\EuScript A_{l}(\EuScript E)
) of exact categories. By definition, a right abelian envelope
\EuScript A_{r}(\EuScript E)
of an exact category
\EuScript E
is an abelian category together with a right exact functor
\EuScript E\to\EuScript A_{r}(\EuScript E)
, satisfying a universal property for all abelian categories
\EuScript A
and right exact functors
\EuScript E\to\EuScript A
(Definition
3.1
). Unlike Rump’s quotient categories, abelian envelopes do not always exist.

Even though the definitions look quite similar, the precise relation between the two notions is not immediately clear. For any exact category
\EuScript E
, it is evident (see
[
2
]
) from the two universal properties that
\EuScript A_{r}(\EuScript E)
exists and coincides with
Q_{l}(\EuScript E)
if the latter is abelian. The main conclusion of this note is that the converse also holds, i.e. if
\EuScript A_{r}(\EuScript E)
exists then
Q_{l}(\EuScript E)
is abelian and they coincide. We make this connection via the explicit description of right abelian envelopes obtained by Bodzenta and Bondal. Namely, they show that
\EuScript A_{r}(\EuScript E)
, if exists, is isomorphic to the subcategory of compact objects
{\mathrm{Lex}}(\EuScript E)^{c}
of left exact contravariant functors from
\EuScript E
to abelian groups
{\mathrm{Ab}}
. Here we observe that
{\mathrm{Lex}}(\EuScript E)^{c}
is always left abelian and together with the Yoneda embedding
i:\EuScript E\to{\mathrm{Lex}}(\EuScript E)^{c}
it satisfies the universal property of
Q_{l}(\EuScript E)
for any exact category
\EuScript E
. It follows in particular that the universal functor
i_{R}:\EuScript E\to\EuScript A_{r}(\EuScript E)
is always fully faithful and exact (not only right exact), which answers the questions in Remark 4.6 and after Example 4.7 in
[
2
]
.

It is worth noting that although abelian envelopes of
[
2
]
essentially turn out to be a special case of Rump’s quotient categories, this special case has many advantages, e.g. when considering derived categories (some of this is also discussed in
[
14
]
). We end the note with some simple applications and examples.

1.1.
Conventions.

Throughout the note we will assume that all categories are skeletally small. All general “one-sided” (e.g. about right abelian envelopes) statements have natural dual counterparts which we do not mention.

2.
Preliminaries and Rump’s quotient categories

We recall the definitions of left exact and left abelian categories.

Definition 2.1
.

A
left exact category
is an additive category with a distinguished class of cokernels (
deflations
) satisfying the following properties:

1)

The class of deflations is closed under compositions and contains identity morphisms.

2)

The pullback of a deflation along any morphism exists and is again a deflation.

3)

If the composition
A\xrightarrow{f}B\xrightarrow{g}C
is a deflation and
g
has a kernel, then
g
is a deflation.

Kernels of deflations will be referred to as
inflations
. A kernel-cokernel pair consisting of an inflation and a deflation is called a
conflation
.

Definition 2.2
.

An additive category
\mathcal{A}
with cokernels is said to be
left abelian
if for every
f:A\to B
,
g:D\to B
with
cg=0
,
c:={\mathrm{coker}}(f)
, there exists a cokernel
d:E\to D
such that
gd
factors through
f
.

3.
Connection to abelian envelopes of Bodzenta and Bondal

Now we recall the definition of right abelian envelopes of exact categories.

Definition 3.1

(Bodzenta-Bondal,
[
2
]
)
.

Let
\EuScript E
be an exact category. A
right abelian envelope
of
\EuScript E
is an abelian category
\EuScript A_{r}(\EuScript E)
together with a right exact functor
i_{R}:\EuScript E\to\EuScript A_{r}(\EuScript E)
such that for any abelian category
\mathcal{B}
there is an equivalence of categories:

(-)\circ i_{R}:{\mathrm{Rex}}(\EuScript A_{r}(\EuScript E),\mathcal{B})\xrightarrow{\cong}{\mathrm{Rex}}(\EuScript E,\mathcal{B})

Left abelian envelopes are defined dually. Unlike the quotient categories of Rump, right/left abelian envelopes do not always exist.

Definition 3.2
.

An object
X
of a category
\mathcal{C}
is
compact
(also
finitely presented
in the literature) if
Hom_{\mathcal{C}}(X,-)
commutes with all filtered colimits.

The following facts were established in
[
2
]
:

Proposition 3.3

(
[
2
]
, Lemma 4.5 and the discussion after the definition of envelopes)
.

Let
\EuScript E
be an exact category.

1)

If the left quotient category
Q_{l}(\EuScript E)
is abelian, then the right abelian envelope
\EuScript A_{r}(\EuScript E)
exists and coincides with
Q_{l}(\EuScript E)
.

2)

Let
{\mathrm{Lex}}(\EuScript E):={\mathrm{Lex}}(\EuScript E^{op},{\mathrm{Ab}})
be the category of contravariant left exact functors from
\EuScript E
to the category
{\mathrm{Ab}}
of abelian groups. Denote by
{\mathrm{Lex}}(\EuScript E)^{c}
the subcategory of compact objects in
{\mathrm{Lex}}(\EuScript E)
. If
\EuScript A_{r}(\EuScript E)
exists, then it is equivalent to
{\mathrm{Lex}}(\EuScript E)^{c}
. Moreover, the functor
i_{R}
is faithful.

It is remarked, however, that the authors do not know whether the functor
i_{R}
in 2) composed with the natural embedding of compact objects
{\mathrm{Lex}}(\EuScript E)^{c}\hookrightarrow{\mathrm{Lex}}(\EuScript E)
is the Yoneda embedding
Y_{\EuScript}E:\EuScript E\hookrightarrow{\mathrm{Lex}}(\EuScript E)
. Indeed, this follows from their proof only provided that
i_{R}
is full, but this is also stated as unknown. In addition, it is natural to ask if
\EuScript A_{r}(\EuScript E)
always exists when the category
{\mathrm{Lex}}(\EuScript E)^{c}
is abelian. We answer these questions by establishing a more precise connection between Bodzenta-Bondal’s abelian envelopes and Rump’s quotient categories:

Theorem 3.4
.

Let
\EuScript E
be an exact category. Then the category
{\mathrm{Lex}}(\EuScript E)^{c}
together with the Yoneda embedding
Y_{\EuScript}E
is equivalent to the left quotient category
Q_{l}(\EuScript E)
.

Note that it is claimed in particular that the Yoneda embedding
Y_{\EuScript}E:\EuScript E\to{\mathrm{Lex}}(\EuScript E)^{c}
maps
\EuScript E
to compact objects in
{\mathrm{Lex}}(\EuScript E)
(this will be explained later). We conclude:

Corollary 3.5
.

The following are equivalent for any exact category
\EuScript E
.

1)

\EuScript A_{r}(\EuScript E)
exists.

2)

Q_{l}(\EuScript E)
is abelian.

3)

{\mathrm{Lex}}(\EuScript E)^{c}
is abelian.

In this case the universal functor
i_{R}:\EuScript E\to\EuScript A_{r}(\EuScript E)
is the Yoneda embedding
Y_{\EuScript}E:\EuScript E\to{\mathrm{Lex}}(\EuScript E)^{c}
. In particular,
i_{R}
is exact and fully faithful.

Remark 3.6
.

1)

Theorem
3.4
claims that
\mathop{\mathrm{mod}}\nolimits(\EuScript E)/{\mathrm{def}}(\EuScript E)\cong{\mathrm{Lex}}(\EuScript E)^{c}
for any exact category
\EuScript E
. One can also show this directly. Let us sketch the argument in the case when
\mathop{\mathrm{mod}}\nolimits(\EuScript E)
is abelian (equivalently, when
\EuScript E
has
weak kernels
, see the discussion after Definition
3.13
). Recall that
{\mathrm{Lex}}(\EuScript E)
is the quotient of the category of all additive contravariant functors
{\mathrm{Mod}}(\EuScript E)
from
\EuScript E
to
{\mathrm{Ab}}
by the Serre subcategory of

{\mathrm{Eff}}(\EuScript E)
, see e.g.
[
8
]
. By the discussion after Theorem
3.9
, any compact object in both
{\mathrm{Mod}}(\EuScript E)
and
{\mathrm{Lex}}(\EuScript E)
is the cokernel of a morphism in
\EuScript E
. Hence the localisation functor, which is an equivalence on
\EuScript E
, induces an exact essentially surjective functor
{Q:\mathop{\mathrm{mod}}\nolimits(\EuScript E)\cong({\mathrm{Mod}}{\EuScript E})^{c}\to{\mathrm{Lex}}(\EuScript E)^{c}}
. It is known that
{\mathrm{Eff}}(\EuScript E)\cap\mathop{\mathrm{mod}}\nolimits(\EuScript E)={\mathrm{def}}(\EuScript E)
(e.g.
[
5
]
, Proposition 3.20 and
[

]
, Lemma 9). Thus the kernel of

is
{\mathrm{def}}(\EuScript E)
.

2)

Let
{\mathrm{lex}}(\EuScript E)
be the full subcategory of
\mathop{\mathrm{mod}}\nolimits(\EuScript E)
consisting of left exact functors. We warn the reader that
{\mathrm{lex}}(\EuScript E)
is not necessarily equivalent to
{\mathrm{Lex}}(\EuScript E)^{c}
even when
\EuScript E
has weak kernels. To be more precise, in this case

is the subcategory of
{\mathrm{def}}(\EuScript E)

3)

Note an immediately consequence of the fact that the universal functors

are exact and not only right and left exact respectively. Namely, if

and

We will need some basic facts about Grothendieck categories and compact objects in the proof of Theorem

Definition 3.7
.

A category
\mathcal{A}
is

if it has filtered colimits, the full subcategory of compact objects

is skeletally small and every object of

is a filtered colimit of compact objects. A

is an abelian category which has a generator and small colimits and satisfies (AB5), i.e. small filtered colimits of exact sequences are exact.

Remark 3.8
.

An abelian category is locally finitely presented if and only if it is a Grothendieck category with a generating set of compact objects (see

3)

Let
\EuScript E
be an additive category with the split exact structure. Then
\EuScript E
has a right abelian envelope if and only if it is left coherent.

Proof.

1)

Indeed, this is an immediate consequence of Theorem
3.14
, since adding more deflations can only create more Ext-kernels.

2)

A weak kernel is always an Ext-kernel, so left coherent categories are left Ext-coherent. Another way to see it is by observing that if
\EuScript E
is left-coherent, then
Q_{l}(\EuScript E)
is abelian as a quotient of the abelian category
\mathop{\mathrm{mod}}\nolimits(\EuScript E)
by a Serre subcategory.

3)

Observe that for
\EuScript E
endowed with the split exact structure, Ext-kernels are exactly the same as weak kernels.

∎
