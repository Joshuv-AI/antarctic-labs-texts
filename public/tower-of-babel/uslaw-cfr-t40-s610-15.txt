40 C.F.R. § 610.15 — Eligibility for participation.
Citation: us:cfr:40:610.15
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-610.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 610 (Fuel Economy Retrofit Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Participation in an evaluation program initiated under § 610.12(c) will be available to any person or company who agrees to follow the procedures set forth in these protocols. Failure to conform to any aspect of these protocols, without the approval of the Adminstrator, may be interpreted as withdrawal from participation in the program.
