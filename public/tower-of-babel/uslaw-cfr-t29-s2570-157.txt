29 C.F.R. § 2570.157 — Allocation of burden of proof.
Citation: us:cfr:29:2570.157
Creator: Employee Benefits Security Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-2570.157
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 2570 (Procedural Regulations Under The Employee Retirement Income Security Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For purposes of a final decision under § 2570.158 (Decision of the Administrative Law Judge) or § 2570.159 (Review by the Secretary), the petitioner shall have the burden of proof as to whether it meets 29 CFR 2510.3-40.
