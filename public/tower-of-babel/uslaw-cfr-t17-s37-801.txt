17 C.F.R. § 37.801 — Additional sources for compliance.
Citation: us:cfr:17:37.801
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-37.801
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 37 (Swap Execution Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A swap execution facility may refer to the guidance and/or acceptable practices in appendix B of this part to demonstrate to the Commission compliance with the requirements of § 37.800.
