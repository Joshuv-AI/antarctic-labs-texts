29 C.F.R. § 1926.856 — Removal of walls, floors, and material with equipment.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1926.856
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1926.856
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1926 (Safety And Health Regulations For Construction). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Mechanical equipment shall not be used on floors or working surfaces unless such floors or surfaces are of sufficient strength to support the imposed load.
(b) Floor openings shall have curbs or stop-logs to prevent equipment from running over the edge.
(c) Cranes, derricks, and other mechanical equipment. Employers must meet the requirements specified in subparts N, O, and CC of this part.
