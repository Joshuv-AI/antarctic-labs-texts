54 U.S.C. § 320104 — Jurisdiction of States in acquired land
Citation: us:usc:54:320104
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section320104
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 3201 (Policy And Administrative Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be held to deprive any State, or political subdivision of a State, of its civil and criminal jurisdiction in and over land acquired by the United States under this chapter.
