46 U.S.C. § 14503 — Certificate of measurement
Citation: us:usc:46:14503
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section14503
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part J (Measurement Of Vessels); Chapter 145 (Regulatory Measurement); Subchapter I (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Secretary shall prescribe the certificate to be issued as evidence of a vessel’s measurement under this chapter.
(b)
The certificate shall be maintained as required by the Secretary.
