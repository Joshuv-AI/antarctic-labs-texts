5 C.F.R. § 844.104 — Administrative review of OPM decisions.
Citation: us:cfr:5:844.104
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-844.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 844 (Federal Employees' Retirement System—Disability Retirement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any individual whose rights or interests under FERS are affected by an initial decision of OPM may request OPM to review its decision under § 841.306.
