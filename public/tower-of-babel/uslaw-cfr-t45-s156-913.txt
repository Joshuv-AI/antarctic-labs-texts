45 C.F.R. § 156.913 — Settlement.
Citation: us:cfr:45:156.913
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-156.913
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 156 (Health Insurance Issuer Standards Under The Affordable Care Act, Including Standards Related To Exchanges). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

HHS has exclusive authority to settle any issue or any case, without the consent of the ALJ at any time before or after the ALJ's decision.
