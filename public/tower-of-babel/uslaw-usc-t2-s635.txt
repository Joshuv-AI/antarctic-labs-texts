2 U.S.C. § 635 — Permissible revisions of concurrent resolutions on the budget
Citation: us:usc:2:635
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section635
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 17A (Congressional Budget And Fiscal Operations); Subchapter I (Congressional Budget Process). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

At any time after the concurrent resolution on the budget for a fiscal year has been agreed to pursuant to section 632 of this title, and before the end of such fiscal year, the two Houses may adopt a concurrent resolution on the budget which revises or reaffirms the concurrent resolution on the budget for such fiscal year most recently agreed to.
