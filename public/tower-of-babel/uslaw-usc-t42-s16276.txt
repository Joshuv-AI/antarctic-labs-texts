42 U.S.C. § 16276 — Security of nuclear facilities
Citation: us:usc:42:16276
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section16276
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 149 (National Energy Policy And Programs); Subchapter IX (Research And Development); Part E (Nuclear Energy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall conduct a research and development program on cost-effective technologies for increasing—
(1)
the safety of nuclear facilities from natural phenomena; and
(2)
the security of nuclear facilities from deliberate attacks.
