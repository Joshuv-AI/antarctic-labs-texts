54 U.S.C. § 300315 — Preservation or historic preservation
Citation: us:usc:54:300315
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section300315
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 3003 (Definitions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this division, the term “preservation” or “historic preservation” includes—
(1)
identification, evaluation, recordation, documentation, curation, acquisition, protection, management, rehabilitation, restoration, stabilization, maintenance, research, interpretation, and conservation;
(2)
education and training regarding the foregoing activities; or
(3)
any combination of the foregoing activities.
