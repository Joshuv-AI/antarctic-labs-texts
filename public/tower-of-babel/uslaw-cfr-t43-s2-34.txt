43 C.F.R. § 2.34 — Will a submitter be notified of a FOIA lawsuit?
Citation: us:cfr:43:2.34
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2 (Freedom Of Information Act; Records And Testimony). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you file a lawsuit seeking to compel the disclosure of confidential information, the bureau must promptly notify the submitter.
