16 U.S.C. § 6451 — Establishment
Citation: us:usc:16:6451
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section6451
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 83A (Coral Reef Conservation, Management, And Assistance); Subchapter I (United States Coral Reef Task Force). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is established a task force to lead, coordinate, and strengthen Federal Government actions to better preserve, conserve, and restore coral reef ecosystems, to be known as the “United States Coral Reef Task Force” (in this subchapter referred to as the “Task Force”).
