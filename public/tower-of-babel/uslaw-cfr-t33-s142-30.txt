33 C.F.R. § 142.30 — Head protection.
Citation: us:cfr:33:142.30
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-142.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 142 (Workplace Safety And Health). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Personnel in areas where there is a hazard of falling objects or of contact with electrical conductors shall wear a head protector meeting the specifications of ANSI Z89.1, for the hazard involved.
(b) Each head protector must be marked with the information specified by ANSI Z89.1 for that type of protector and for the hazard involved.
