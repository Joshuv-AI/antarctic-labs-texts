45 C.F.R. § 4.4 — Acknowledgement of mailed process.
Citation: us:cfr:45:4.4
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-4.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 4 (Service Of Process). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Department will not provide a receipt or other acknowledgement of process received, except for a return receipt associated with certified mail and, where required, the acknowledgement specified by Rule 4(c)(2)(C) of the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure.
