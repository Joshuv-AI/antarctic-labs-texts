11 C.F.R. § 9032.11 — State.
Citation: us:cfr:11:9032.11
Creator: Federal Election Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-11/section-9032.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 11 (Federal Elections), Part 9032 (Definitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

State means each State of the United States, Puerto Rico, American Samoa, the Virgin Islands, the District of Columbia, and Guam.
