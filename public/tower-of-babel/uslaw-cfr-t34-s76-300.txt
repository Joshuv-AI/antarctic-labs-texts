34 C.F.R. § 76.300 — Contact the State for procedures to follow.
Citation: us:cfr:34:76.300
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-76.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 76 (State-Administered Formula Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An applicant for a subgrant can find out the procedures it must follow by contacting the State agency that administers the program.
