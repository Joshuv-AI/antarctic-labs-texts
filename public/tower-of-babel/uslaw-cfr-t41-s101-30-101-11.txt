41 C.F.R. § 101-30.101-11 — -30.101-11 Recorded data.
Citation: us:cfr:41:101-30.101-11
Creator: Chapter 101—Federal Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-101-30.101-11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 101-30 (30—Federal Catalog System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Recorded data means the data which are associated with a national stock number and are recorded on microfilm or magnetic computer tape at the Defense Logistics Center (DLSC), Battle Creek, MI 49016.
