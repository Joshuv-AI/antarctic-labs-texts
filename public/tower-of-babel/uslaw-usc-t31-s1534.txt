31 U.S.C. § 1534 — Adjustments between appropriations
Citation: us:usc:31:1534
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section1534
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 15 (Appropriation Accounting); Subchapter III (Transfers And Reimbursements). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
An appropriation available to an agency may be charged at any time during a fiscal year for the benefit of another appropriation available to the agency to pay costs—
(1)
when amounts are available in both the appropriation to be charged and the appropriation to be benefited; and
(2)
subject to limitations applicable to the appropriations.
(b)
Amounts paid under this section are charged on a final basis during, or as of the close of, the fiscal year to the appropriation benefited. The appropriation charged under subsection (a) of this section shall be appropriately credited.
