54 U.S.C. § 302505 — Eligibility and responsibility of certified local government
Citation: us:usc:54:302505
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section302505
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 3025 (Certification Of Local Governments). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any local government—
(1)
that is certified under this chapter shall be eligible for funds under section 302902(c)(4) of this title; and
(2)
that is certified, or making efforts to become certified, under this chapter shall carry out any responsibilities delegated to it in accordance with such terms and conditions as the Secretary considers necessary or advisable.
