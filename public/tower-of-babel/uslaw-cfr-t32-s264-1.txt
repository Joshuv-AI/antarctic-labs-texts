32 C.F.R. § 264.1 — Purpose and cancellation.
Citation: us:cfr:32:264.1
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-264.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 264 (International Interchange Of Patent Rights And Technical Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to restate Department of Defense policy concerning the international interchange for defense purposes of patent rights and technical information. DoD Directive 2000.3, “Technical Property Interchange Agreements”, dated April 15, 1954, is hereby superseded and cancelled. Delegation published at 19 FR 2523 is cancelled.
