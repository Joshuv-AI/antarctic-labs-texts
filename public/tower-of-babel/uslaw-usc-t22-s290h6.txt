22 U.S.C. § 290h–6 — Government corporation control provisions applicable
Citation: us:usc:22:290h–6
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section290h–6
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 7 (International Bureaus, Congresses, Etc.); Subchapter XXIII (United States African Development Foundation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Foundation shall be subject to the provisions of chapter 91 of title 31 applicable to wholly owned Government corporations.
