38 U.S.C. § 5320 — Threshold for reporting debts to consumer reporting agencies
Citation: us:usc:38:5320
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section5320
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part IV (General Administrative Provisions); Chapter 53 (Special Provisions Relating To Benefits). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall prescribe regulations that establish the minimum amount of a claim or debt, arising from a benefit administered by the Under Secretary for Benefits or Under Secretary for Health, that the Secretary will report to a consumer reporting agency under section 3711 of title 31.
