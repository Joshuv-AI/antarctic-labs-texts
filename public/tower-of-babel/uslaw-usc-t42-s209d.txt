42 U.S.C. § 209d — Appointment of osteopaths as commissioned officers
Citation: us:usc:42:209d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section209d
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter I (Administration And Miscellaneous Provisions); Part A (Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Graduates of colleges of osteopathy whose graduates are eligible for licensure to practice medicine or osteopathy in a majority of the States of the United States, or approved by a body or bodies acceptable to the Secretary, shall be eligible, subject to the other provisions of this Act, for appointment as commissioned medical officers in the Public Health Service.
