42 U.S.C. § 16061 — Hybrid vehicles
Citation: us:usc:42:16061
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section16061
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 149 (National Energy Policy And Programs); Subchapter VII (Vehicles And Fuels); Part B (Hybrid Vehicles, Advanced Vehicles, And Fuel Cell Buses); Subpart 1 (Hybrid Vehicles). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall accelerate efforts directed toward the improvement of batteries and other rechargeable energy storage systems, power electronics, hybrid systems integration, and other technologies for use in hybrid vehicles.
