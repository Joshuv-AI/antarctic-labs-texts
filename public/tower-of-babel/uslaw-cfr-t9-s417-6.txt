9 C.F.R. § 417.6 — Inadequate HACCP Systems.
Citation: us:cfr:9:417.6
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-417.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 417 (Hazard Analysis And Critical Control Point (Haccp) Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A HACCP system may be found to be inadequate if:
(a) The HACCP plan in operation does not meet the requirements set forth in this part;
(b) Establishment personnel are not performing tasks specified in the HACCP plan;
(c) The establishment fails to take corrective actions, as required by § 417.3 of this part;
(d) HACCP records are not being maintained as required in § 417.5 of this part; or
(e) Adulterated product is produced or shipped.
