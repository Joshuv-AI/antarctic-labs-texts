Correction to ‘Generation of precise and accurate engineered circRNAs using enzymatic ligation’
Published: 2026

This is a correction to: Amrita Singh, Adela Dujsikova, Noah Mueller, Y Grace Chen, Generation of precise and accurate engineered circRNAs using enzymatic ligation, Nucleic Acids Research, Volume 54, Issue 9, 22 May 2026, gkag405, https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkag405

During the final proof stage, outdated versions of Figures 1 and 2 were inadvertently supplied. To improve alignment between the figures and the corresponding text, the authors reorganized several figure panels. Specifically, the previous Fig. 1D and Fig. 1E have been moved to Supplementary Fig. 1 and Fig. 1D, respectively, while the previous Fig. 2A-C and Fig. 2D-G are now presented as Fig. 1E-G and Fig. 2A-D, respectively.

This change does not affect the results, discussion and conclusions presented in the article. The published article has been updated.
