46 U.S.C. § 53719 — Best practices
Citation: us:usc:46:53719
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section53719
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part C (Financial Assistance Programs); Chapter 537 (Loans And Guarantees); Subchapter I (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary or Administrator shall ensure that all standard documents and agreements that relate to loan guarantees made pursuant to this chapter are reviewed and updated every four years to ensure that such documents and agreements meet the current commercial best practices to the extent permitted by law.
