23 C.F.R. § 810.100 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:23:810.100
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-810.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 810 (Mass Transit And Special Use Highway Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of the regulations in this subpart is to implement 23 U.S.C. 137, 142(a)(1), 142(b), and 149, which authorize various highway public mass transportation improvements and special use highway facilities as Federal-aid highway projects.
