21 C.F.R. § 892.1710 — Mammographic x-ray system.
Citation: us:cfr:21:892.1710
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-892.1710
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 892 (Radiology Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. A mammographic x-ray system is a device intended to be used to produce radiographs of the breast. This generic type of device may include signal analysis and display equipment, patient and equipment supports, component parts, and accessories.
(b) Classification. Class II.
