16 U.S.C. § 460h — Costs of surveys or relocation of boundaries
Citation: us:usc:16:460h
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section460h
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter LXVII (Cottage Site Developments And Uses In Reservoir Areas). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The costs of any surveys or the relocation of boundary markers necessary as an incident of a conveyance or other property transfer under this subchapter shall be borne by the grantee.
