38 U.S.C. § 310 — Chief Information Officer
Citation: us:usc:38:310
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section310
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part I (General Provisions); Chapter 3 (Department Of Veterans Affairs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Chief Information Officer for the Department is designated pursuant to section 3506(a)(2) of title 44.
(b)
The Chief Information Officer performs the duties provided for chief information officers of executive agencies under chapter 35 of title 44 and subtitle III of title 40.
