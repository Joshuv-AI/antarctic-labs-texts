16 U.S.C. § 7708 — Territorial participation
Citation: us:usc:16:7708
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section7708
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 96 (North Pacific Fisheries Convention). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of State shall ensure participation in the Commission and its subsidiary bodies by the Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands, American Samoa, and Guam to the extent allowed under United States law.
