29 C.F.R. § 101.32 — Investigation of charges; withdrawal of charges; dismissal of charges and appeals to Board.
Citation: us:cfr:29:101.32
Creator: National Labor Relations Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-101.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 101 (Statements Of Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

These matters are handled as described in §§ 101.4 to 101.7, inclusive. Cases involving violation of paragraph (4)(D) of section 8(b) in which it is deemed appropriate to seek injunctive relief of a district court pursuant to section 10(1) of the Act are given priority over all other cases in the office except other cases under section 10(1) of the Act and cases of like character.
