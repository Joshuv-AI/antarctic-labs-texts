19 C.F.R. § 177.27 — Oral discussion of issues.
Citation: us:cfr:19:177.27
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-177.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 177 (Administrative Rulings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any party authorized to request a ruling under the provisions of § 177.23 may request an opportunity for oral discussion of the issues presented in the request. The oral discussion of issues will be governed by the provisions of § 177.4.
