31 C.F.R. § 343.5 — Taxation.
Citation: us:cfr:31:343.5
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-343.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 343 (Regulations Governing The Offering Of United States Mortgage Guaranty Insurance Company Tax And Loss Bonds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Tax and loss bonds will be exempt from all taxation now or hereafter imposed on the principal by any state or any possession of the United States or of any local taxing authority.
