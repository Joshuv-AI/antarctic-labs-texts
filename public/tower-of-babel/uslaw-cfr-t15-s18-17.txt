15 C.F.R. § 18.17 — Reply.
Citation: us:cfr:15:18.17
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-18.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 18 (Attorney'S Fees And Other Expenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Within 15 calendar days after service of an answer, the applicant may file a reply. If the reply is based on any alleged facts not already in the record of the proceeding, the applicant shall include with the reply either supporting affidavits or a request for further proceedings under § 18.20.
