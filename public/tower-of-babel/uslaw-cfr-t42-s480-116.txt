42 C.F.R. § 480.116 — Notice to individuals and institutions under review.
Citation: us:cfr:42:480.116
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-480.116
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 480 (Acquisition, Protection, And Disclosure Of Quality Improvement Organization Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The QIO must establish and implement procedures to provide patients, practitioners, and institutions under review with the following information—
(a) The title and address of the person responsible for maintenance of QIO information;
(b) The types of information that will be collected and maintained;
(c) The general rules governing disclosure of QIO information; and
(d) The procedures whereby patients, practitioners, and institutions may obtain access to information about themselves.
