7 C.F.R. § 765.103 — Transfer and assignment of Agency liens.
Citation: us:cfr:7:765.103
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-765.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 765 (Direct Loan Servicing—Regular). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Agency may assign its lien to the new lender when the borrower is graduating and all FLP debt will be paid in full.
