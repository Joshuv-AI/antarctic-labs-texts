13 C.F.R. § 127.352 — What is the process for becoming a third-party certifier?
Citation: us:cfr:13:127.352
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-127.352
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 127 (Women-Owned Small Business Federal Contract Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

SBA will periodically hold open solicitations. All entities that believe they meet the criteria to act as a third-party certifier will be free to respond to the solicitation.
