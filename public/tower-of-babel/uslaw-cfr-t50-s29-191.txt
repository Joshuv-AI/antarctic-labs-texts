50 C.F.R. § 29.191 — What enforcement actions can the Service take?
Citation: us:cfr:50:29.191
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-29.191
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 29 (Land Use Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you engage in a prohibited act:
(a) The Service may suspend and/or revoke your approved operations permit and your authorization for operations as set forth at § 29.43 and § 29.44; and/or
(b) All prohibited acts are subject to the penalty provisions set forth at § 28.31 of this subchapter.
