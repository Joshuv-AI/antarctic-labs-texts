16 C.F.R. § 1512.13 — Requirements for front fork.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1512.13
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1512.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1512 (Requirements For Bicycles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The front fork shall be tested for strength by application of at least 39.5 J (350 in-lb) of energy in accordance with the fork test, § 1512.18(k)(1), without visible evidence of fracture. Sidewalk bicycles need not meet this requirement.
