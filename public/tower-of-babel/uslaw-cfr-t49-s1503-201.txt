49 C.F.R. § 1503.201 — Reports of violations.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1503.201
Creator: Transportation Security Administration, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1503.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1503 (Investigative And Enforcement Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Any person who knows of a violation of a TSA requirement should report it to appropriate personnel of any TSA office.
(b) TSA will review each report made under this section, together with any other information TSA may have that is relevant to the matter reported, to determine the appropriate response, including additional investigation or administrative or legal enforcement action.
