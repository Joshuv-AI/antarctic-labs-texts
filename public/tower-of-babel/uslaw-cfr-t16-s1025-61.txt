16 C.F.R. § 1025.61 — Who may make appearances.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1025.61
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1025.61
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1025 (Rules Of Practice For Adjudicative Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A party or participant may appear in person, or by a duly authorized officer, partner, regular employee, or other agent of the party or participant, or by counsel or other duly qualified representative, in accordance with § 1025.65.
