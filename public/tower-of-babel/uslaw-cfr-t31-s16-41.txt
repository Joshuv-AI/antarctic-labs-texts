31 C.F.R. § 16.41 — Stay pending appeal.
Citation: us:cfr:31:16.41
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-16.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 16 (Regulations Implementing The Program Fraud Civil Remedies Act Of 1986). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An initial decision is stayed automatically pending disposition of a motion for reconsideration or of an appeal to the authority head.
(b) No administrative stay is available following a final decision of the authority head.
