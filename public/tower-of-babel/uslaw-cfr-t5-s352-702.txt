5 C.F.R. § 352.702 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:5:352.702
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-352.702
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 352 (Reemployment Rights). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In this subpart:
(a) Agency means the Bureau of Indian Affairs and the Indian Health Service. For reemployment purposes, the Public Health Service shall be considered the agency to which Indian Health Service employees may return.
(b) Competitive area is the same as defined in § 351.402 of this title.
(c) Tribal organization is defined in section 4(c) of the Indian Self-Determination Act (88 Stat. 2204).
