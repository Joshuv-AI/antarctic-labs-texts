Compactification of reductive group schemes
Ayan Nath

Abstract
. Let G G be an isotrivial reductive group over a scheme S S . We construct a smooth projective S S -scheme containing G G as a fiberwise-dense open subscheme equipped with left and right actions of G G which extend the translation actions of G G on itself. This verifies a conjecture of Česnavičius [ 3 ] . When G G is adjoint, we recover fiberwise the wonderful compactification. Finally, we give an example of a non-isotrivial torus admitting no equivariant compactification.
