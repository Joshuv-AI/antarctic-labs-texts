33 C.F.R. § 158.400 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:33:158.400
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-158.400
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 158 (Reception Facilities For Oil, Noxious Liquid Substances, And Garbage). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this subpart is to supply the criteria for determining the adequacy of reception facilities for garbage at ports and terminals that receive ships and to comply with the Act and Regulation 7 of Annex V to MARPOL 73/78.
