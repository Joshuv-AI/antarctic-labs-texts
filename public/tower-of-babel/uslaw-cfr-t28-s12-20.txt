28 C.F.R. § 12.20 — Filing of registration statement.
Citation: us:cfr:28:12.20
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-12.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 12 (Registration Of Certain Persons Having Knowledge Of Foreign Espionage, Counterespionage, Or Sabotage Matters Under The Act Of August 1, 1956). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Registration statements shall be filed in duplicate with the National Security Division, Department of Justice, Washington, DC 20530. Filing may be made in person or by mail, and shall be deemed to have taken place upon the receipt thereof by the Division.
