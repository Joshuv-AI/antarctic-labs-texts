7 U.S.C. § 940h — Bonding requirements
Citation: us:usc:7:940h
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section940h
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 31 (Rural Electrification And Telephone Service); Subchapter III (Rural Electric And Telephone Direct Loan Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall review the bonding requirements for all programs administered by the Rural Utilities Service under this chapter to ensure that bonds are not required if—
(1)
the interests of the Secretary are adequately protected by product warranties; or
(2)
the costs or conditions associated with a bond exceed the benefit of the bond.
