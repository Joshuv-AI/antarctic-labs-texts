36 C.F.R. § 228.45 — Qualifications of applicants.
Citation: us:cfr:36:228.45
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-228.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 228 (Minerals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The authorized officer may require applicants for prospecting permits, negotiated contracts, or free-use permits or bidders for the sale of mineral materials to furnish information necessary to determine their ability to perform the obligations of the contract or permit.
