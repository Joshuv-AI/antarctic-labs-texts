50 U.S.C. § 455 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:50:455
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section455
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 16 (Defense Industrial Reserves). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated such sums as the Congress may from time to time determine to be necessary to enable the Secretary to carry out the provisions of this chapter.
