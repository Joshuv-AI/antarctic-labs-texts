34 U.S.C. § 10388 — General regulatory authority
Citation: us:usc:34:10388
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 101 (Justice System Improvement); Subchapter XVI (Public Safety And Community Policing; “Cops On The Beat”). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Attorney General may promulgate regulations and guidelines to carry out this subchapter.
