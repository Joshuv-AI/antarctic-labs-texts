26 U.S.C. § 7406 — Disposition of judgments and moneys recovered
Citation: us:usc:26:7406
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section7406
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 76 (Judicial Proceedings); Subchapter A (Civil Actions By The United States). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All judgments and moneys recovered or received for taxes, costs, forfeitures, and penalties shall be paid to the Secretary as collections of internal revenue taxes.
