24 C.F.R. § 17.5 — Investigations.
Citation: us:cfr:24:17.5
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-17.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 17 (Administrative Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Department may investigate, or may request any other Federal agency to investigate, a claim filed under this subpart.
