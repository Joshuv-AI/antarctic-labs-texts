36 C.F.R. § 703.3 — Administration responsibilities.
Citation: us:cfr:36:703.3
Creator: Library Of Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-703.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 703 (Disclosure Or Production Of Records Or Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The administration of this part shall be the responsibility of the Chief, Office Systems Services (OSS), Library of Congress, 101 Independence Avenue, SE., Washington, DC 20540-9440, and to that end, the Chief may promulgate such supplemental rules or guidelines as may be necessary.
