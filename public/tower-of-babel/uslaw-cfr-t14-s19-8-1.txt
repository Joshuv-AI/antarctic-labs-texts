14 C.F.R. § 19-8.1 — 19-8.1 Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:14:19-8.1
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-19-8.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 241 (Uniform System Of Accounts And Reports For Large Certificated Air Carriers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to set forth required data that certain air carriers must submit to the Department, either themselves or via third party vendors, so that information on air carriers' ticket pricing, passenger volumes, and trip routings can be made available to consumers of air transportation.
