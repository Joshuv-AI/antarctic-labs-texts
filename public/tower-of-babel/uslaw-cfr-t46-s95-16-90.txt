46 C.F.R. § 95.16-90 — -90 Installations contracted for prior to July 9, 2012.
Citation: us:cfr:46:95.16-90
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-95.16-90
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 95 (Fire Protection Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Installations contracted for prior to July 9, 2012, must meet the requirements of this subpart unless previously approved existing arrangements, materials, and facilities are:
(a) Maintained in good condition to the satisfaction of the Officer in Charge, Marine Inspection; and
(b) Subjected to no more than minor repairs or alterations implemented to the same standards as the original installation.
