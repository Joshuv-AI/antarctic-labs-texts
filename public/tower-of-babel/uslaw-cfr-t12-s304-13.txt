12 C.F.R. § 304.13 — Reduced reporting.
Citation: us:cfr:12:304.13
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-304.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 304 (Forms, Instructions, And Reports). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A covered depository institution may file the FFIEC 051 version of the report of condition, or any successor thereto, which shall provide for reduced reporting for the reports of condition for the first and third calendar quarters for a year.
