20 C.F.R. § 1011.220 — How will VETS verify a HIRE Vets Medallion Award application?
Citation: us:cfr:20:1011.220
Creator: Office Of The Assistant Secretary For Veterans' Employment And Training Service, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-1011.220
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 1011 (Hire Vets Medallion Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

VETS will verify all information provided by an employer in its application to the extent that such information is relevant in determining whether or not such employer meets the criteria to receive a HIRE Vets Medallion Award or in determining the appropriate level of HIRE Vets Medallion Award for that employer to receive. VETS will verify this information by reviewing all information provided as part of the application.
