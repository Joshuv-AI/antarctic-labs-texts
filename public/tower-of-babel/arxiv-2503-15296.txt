Constructing the antimagic labelings for double stars union paths on three vertices
Wei-Tian Li, Po-Wen Yang

Abstract
For a graph on m m edges, a bijective function between the edge set of the graph and { 1 , 2 , … , m } \{1,2,\ldots,m\} is an antimagic labeling provided that when adding the labels of the edges incident to the same vertex, the sums are pairwise distinct. Hartsfield and Ringel conjectured that every connected graph has antimagic labeling. On the other hand, it is known that for any graph G G , the disjoint union of G G and many P 3 P_{3} , a path on 3 vertices, is not antimagic. In this paper, we determined the exact number of P 3 P_{3} ’s such that the disjoint union of a double star with the number of P 3 P_{3} ’s is antimagic. In addition, we provide some examples of ( 1 , 1 ) (1,1) -antimagic labelings. That is, the antimagic labelings have vertex sums 1 through the number of vertices of the graphs.

as the initial term and

This concept was introduced by Bodendiek and Walther in 1993
[

.
A necessary condition for which a graph

m

-antimagic labeling is

2an+n(n-1)d=2m(m+1),

(12)

given by Bodendiek and Walther
[

]
.
They also showed that the paths
P_{2k+1}
have the
(k,1)
-antimagic labeling and also the cycles

have the

-antimagic labelings for

.

-antimagic labelings, see

4
