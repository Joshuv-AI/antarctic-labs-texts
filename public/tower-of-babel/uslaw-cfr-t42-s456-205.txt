42 C.F.R. § 456.205 — UR committee required.
Citation: us:cfr:42:456.205
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-456.205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 456 (Utilization Control). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The UR plan must—
(a) Provide for a committee to perform UR required under this subpart;
(b) Describe the organization, composition, and functions of this committee; and
(c) Specify the frequency of meetings of the committee.
