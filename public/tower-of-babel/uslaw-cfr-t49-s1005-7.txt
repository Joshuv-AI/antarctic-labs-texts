49 C.F.R. § 1005.7 — Weight as a measure of loss.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1005.7
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1005.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1005 (Principles And Practices For The Investigation And Voluntary Disposition Of Loss And Damage Claims And Processing Salvage). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where weight is used as a measure of loss in rail transit of scrap iron and steel and actual tare and gross weights are determined at origin and destination, the settlement of claims shall be based upon a comparison of net weights at origin and destination.
