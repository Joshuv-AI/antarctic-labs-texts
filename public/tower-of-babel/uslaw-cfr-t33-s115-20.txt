33 C.F.R. § 115.20 — Permit bonds.
Citation: us:cfr:33:115.20
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-115.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 115 (Bridge Locations And Clearances; Administrative Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When compensatory works or the removal of temporary structures should be required of the permittee, or in other unusual cases when there is reason to anticipate that the permittee may fail to carry out parts of the work that are against his interest, an additional condition will be included in the permit requiring the permittee to furnish a bond insuring compliance with the permit requirements.
