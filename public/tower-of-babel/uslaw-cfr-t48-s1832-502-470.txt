48 C.F.R. § 1832.502-470 — 1832.502-470 NASA contract clause.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1832.502-470
Creator: Chapter 18—National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1832.502-470
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1832 (Contract Financing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer may insert a clause substantially as stated at 1852.232-82, Submission of Requests for Progress Payments, in fixed-price solicitations and contracts that provide for progress payments. The recipient of the requests and number of copies may be changed as required.
