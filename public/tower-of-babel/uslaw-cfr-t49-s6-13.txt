49 C.F.R. § 6.13 — Delegations of authority.
Citation: us:cfr:49:6.13
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-6.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 6 (Implementation Of Equal Access To Justice Act In Agency Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary of Transportation delegates to the head of each operating administration of this Department the authority to take final action, other than rulemaking, on matters pertaining to the Act in actions that require section 554 proceedings. The head of each operating administration may redelegate this authority.
