15 C.F.R. § 23.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:15:23.1
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-23.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 23 (Use Of Penalty Mail In The Location And Recovery Of Missing Children). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

These regulations are intended to comply with 39 U.S.C. 3220(a)(2), and the Office of Juvenile Justice and Delinquency Prevention (OJJDP) guidelines (50 FR 46622), to assist in the location and recovery of missing children through the use of penalty mail.
