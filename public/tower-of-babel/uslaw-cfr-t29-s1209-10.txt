29 C.F.R. § 1209.10 — Capacity of public observers.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1209.10
Creator: National Mediation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1209.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1209 (Public Observation Of National Mediation Board Meetings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The public may attend open Board meetings for the sole purpose of observation. Observers may not participate in meetings unless expressly invited or otherwise interfere with the conduct and disposition of agency business. When a portion of a meeting is closed to the public, observers will leave the meeting room upon request to enable discussion of the exempt matter therein under consideration.
