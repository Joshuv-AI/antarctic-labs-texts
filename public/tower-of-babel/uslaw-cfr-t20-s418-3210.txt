20 C.F.R. § 418.3210 — What is a prescribed application for a subsidy?
Citation: us:cfr:20:418.3210
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-418.3210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 418 (Medicare Subsidies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you choose to apply with SSA, you must file for the subsidy on an application prescribed by us. A prescribed application may include a printed form, an application our employees complete on computer screens, or an application available online on our Internet Web site (www.socialsecurity.gov). See § 418.3220 for places where an application for the subsidy may be filed and when it is considered filed.
