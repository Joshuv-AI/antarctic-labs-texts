18 U.S.C. § 2724 — Civil action

Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 123 (Prohibition On Release And Use Of Certain Personal Information From State Motor Vehicle Records). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Cause of Action.—
A person who knowingly obtains, discloses or uses personal information, from a motor vehicle record, for a purpose not permitted under this chapter shall be liable to the individual to whom the information pertains, who may bring a civil action in a United States district court.
(b) Remedies.—
The court may award—
(1)
actual damages, but not less than liquidated damages in the amount of $2,500;
(2)
punitive damages upon proof of willful or reckless disregard of the law;
(3)
reasonable attorneys’ fees and other litigation costs reasonably incurred; and
(4)
such other preliminary and equitable relief as the court determines to be appropriate.
