42 C.F.R. § 86.21 — Applicability of 45 CFR part 74.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 86 (Grants For Education Programs In Occupational Safety And Health). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of 45 CFR part 74, establishing uniform administrative requirements and cost principles, shall apply to all grants under this part to States and local governments as those terms are defined in subpart A of that part 74.
