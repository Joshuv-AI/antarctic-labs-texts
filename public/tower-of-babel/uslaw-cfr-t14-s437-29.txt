14 C.F.R. § 437.29 — Hazard analysis.
Citation: us:cfr:14:437.29
Creator: Commercial Space Transportation, Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-437.29
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 437 (Experimental Permits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An applicant must perform a hazard analysis that complies with § 437.55(a).
(b) An applicant must provide to the FAA all the results of each step of the hazard analysis required by paragraph (a) of this section.
