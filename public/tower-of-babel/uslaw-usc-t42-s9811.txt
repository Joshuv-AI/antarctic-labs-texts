42 U.S.C. § 9811 — Limitation on assistance
Citation: us:usc:42:9811
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section9811
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 105 (Community Services Programs); Subchapter I (Community Economic Development); Part B (Special Rural Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No financial assistance shall be provided under this part unless the Secretary determines that—
(1)
any cooperative association receiving assistance has a minimum of fifteen active members, a majority of which are low-income rural persons;
(2)
adequate technical assistance is made available and committed to the programs being supported;
(3)
such financial assistance will materially further the purposes of this part; and
(4)
the applicant is fulfilling or will fulfill a need for services, supplies, or facilities which is otherwise not being met.
