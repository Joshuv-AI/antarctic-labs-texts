40 C.F.R. § 440.40 — Applicability; description of the mercury ore subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:440.40
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-440.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 440 (Ore Mining And Dressing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of subpart D are applicable to discharges from (a) mines, either open-pit or underground, that produce mercury ores; and (b) mills beneficiating mercury ores by gravity separation methods or by froth-flotation methods.
