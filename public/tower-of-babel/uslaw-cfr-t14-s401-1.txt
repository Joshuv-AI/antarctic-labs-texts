14 C.F.R. § 401.1 — The Office of Commercial Space Transportation.
Citation: us:cfr:14:401.1
Creator: Commercial Space Transportation, Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-401.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 401 (Organization And Definitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Office of Commercial Space Transportation, referred to in these regulations as the “Office,” is a line of business within the Federal Aviation Administration and is located in the Federal Aviation Administration Headquarters, 800 Independence Avenue, SW., Room 331, Washington, DC 20591.
