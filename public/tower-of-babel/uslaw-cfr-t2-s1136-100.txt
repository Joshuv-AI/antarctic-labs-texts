2 C.F.R. § 1136.100 — Purpose of OAR Article I.
Citation: us:cfr:2:1136.100
Creator: Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1136.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1136 (Other Administrative Requirements: General Award Terms And Conditions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

OAR Article I sets forth requirements for recipients to maintain current information about themselves in the data system the Federal Government specifies as the repository for standard information about its business partners, currently the System for Award Management. The article thereby implements OMB guidance in:
(a) 2 CFR 200.113 and 200.210(b)(1)(iii);
(b) 2 CFR part 25; and
(c) The portion of 2 CFR 200.300(b) that cites 2 CFR part 25 and the System for Award Management).
