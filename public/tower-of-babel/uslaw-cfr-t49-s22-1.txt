49 C.F.R. § 22.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:49:22.1
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-22.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 22 (Short-Term Lending Program (Stlp)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of the DOT OSDBU STLP is to provide financial assistance in the form of short-term loans from Participating Lenders that are guaranteed by DOT OSDBU, to DBEs and SDBs for the execution of DOT funded and supported transportation-related contracts.
