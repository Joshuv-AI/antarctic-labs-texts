14 C.F.R. § 400.1 — Basis.
Citation: us:cfr:14:400.1
Creator: Commercial Space Transportation, Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-400.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 400 (Basis And Scope). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The basis for the regulations in this chapter is the Commercial Space Launch Act of 1984, and applicable treaties and international agreements to which the United States is party.
