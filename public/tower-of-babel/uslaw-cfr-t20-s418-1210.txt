20 C.F.R. § 418.1210 — What is not a major life-changing event?
Citation: us:cfr:20:418.1210
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-418.1210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 418 (Medicare Subsidies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We will not consider events other than those described in § 418.1205 to be major life-changing events. Certain types of events are not considered major life-changing events for the purposes of this subpart, such as:
(a) Events that affect your expenses, but not your income; or
(b) Events that result in the loss of dividend income because of the ordinary risk of investment.
