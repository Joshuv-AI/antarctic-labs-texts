Reaction processes of muon-catalyzed fusion in the muonic molecule 𝒅 ​ 𝒅 ​ 𝝁 \displaystyle dd\mu studied with the tractable 𝑻 \displaystyle T -matrix model
Qian Wu, Zhu-Fang Cui, Masayasu Kamimura

Abstract
Muon-catalyzed fusion has recently regained significant attention due to experimental and theoretical developments being performed. The present authors [Phys. Rev. C 109 054625 (2024)] proposed the tractable T \displaystyle T -matrix model based on the Lippmann-Schwinger equation to approximate the elaborate two- and three-body coupled-channel (CC) calculations [Kamimura, Kino, and Yamashita, Phys. Rev. C 107 , 034607 (2023)] for the nuclear reaction processes in the muonic molecule d ​ t ​ μ \displaystyle dt\mu , ( d t μ ) J = 0 → 4 He + n + μ + 17.6 MeV \displaystyle(dt\mu)_{J=0}\to\!^{4}{\rm He}+n+\mu+17.6\,{\rm MeV} . The T \displaystyle T -matrix model well reproduced almost all of the results generated by the CC work. In the present paper, we apply this model to the nuclear reaction processes in the d ​ d ​ μ \displaystyle dd\mu molecule, ( d d μ ) J = 1 → 3 He + n + μ + 3.27 \displaystyle(dd\mu)_{J=1}\to\!^{3}{\rm He}+n+\mu+3.27\, MeV or t + p + μ + 4.03 \displaystyle t+p+\mu+4.03\, MeV, in which the fusion takes place via the p \displaystyle p -wave d \displaystyle d - d \displaystyle d relative motion. Recently, significantly different p \displaystyle p -wave astrophysical S ⁡ ( E ) \displaystyle S(E) factors of the reaction d + d → 3 He + n \displaystyle d+d\to\!^{3}{\rm He}+n or t + p \displaystyle t+p at E ≃ 1 \displaystyle E\!\simeq\!1 keV to 1 MeV have been reported experimentally and theoretically by five groups. Employing many sets of nuclear interactions that can reproduce those five cases of p \displaystyle p -wave S ⁡ ( E ) \displaystyle S(E) factors, we calculate the fusion rate of the ( d ​ d ​ μ ) J = 1 \displaystyle(dd\mu)_{J=1} molecule using three kinds of methods where results are consistent with each other. We also derive the He 3 {}^{3}{\rm He} - μ \displaystyle\mu sticking probability and the absolute values of the energy and momentum spectra of the emitted muon. The violation of charge symmetry in the p \displaystyle p -wave d \displaystyle d - d \displaystyle d reaction and the d ​ d ​ μ \displaystyle dd\mu fusion reaction is discussed. Information on the emitted 2.45-MeV neutrons and 1 keV-dominant muons should be useful for the application of d ​ d ​ μ \displaystyle dd\mu fusion.
