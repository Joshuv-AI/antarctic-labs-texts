13 C.F.R. § 120.171 — Compliance with child support obligations.
Citation: us:cfr:13:120.171
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-120.171
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 120 (Business Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any holder of 50% or more of the ownership interest in the recipient of an SBA loan must certify that he or she is not more than 60 days delinquent on any obligation to pay child support arising under:
(a) An administrative order;
(b) A court order;
(c) A repayment agreement between the holder and a custodial parent; or
(d) A repayment agreement between the holder and a State agency providing child support enforcement services.
