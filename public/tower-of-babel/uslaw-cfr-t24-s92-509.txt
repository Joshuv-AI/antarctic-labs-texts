24 C.F.R. § 92.509 — Performance reports.
Citation: us:cfr:24:92.509
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-92.509
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 92 (Home Investment Partnerships Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Management reports. Each participating jurisdiction must submit management reports on its HOME Investment Partnerships Program in such format and at such time as HUD may prescribe.
(b) Annual performance report. For annual performance report requirements, see 24 CFR part 91.
