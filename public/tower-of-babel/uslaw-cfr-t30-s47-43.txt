30 C.F.R. § 47.43 — Label alternatives.
Citation: us:cfr:30:47.43
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-47.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 47 (HAZARD COMMUNICATION (HazCom)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The operator may use signs, placards, process sheets, batch tickets, operating procedures, or other label alternatives for individual, stationary process containers, provided that the alternative—
(a) Identifies the container to which it applies,
(b) Communicates the same information as required on the label, and
(c) Is readily available throughout each work shift to miners in the work area.
