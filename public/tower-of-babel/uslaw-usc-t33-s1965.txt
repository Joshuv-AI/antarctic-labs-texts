33 U.S.C. § 1965 — Volunteer status
Citation: us:usc:33:1965
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section1965
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 33A (Marine Debris Research, Prevention, And Reduction); Subchapter II (Marine Debris Foundation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Commerce may accept, without regard to the civil service classification laws, rules, or regulations, the services of the Foundation, the Board, and the officers and employees of the Board, without compensation from the Department of Commerce, as volunteers in the performance of the functions authorized in this title.1
