24 C.F.R. § 906.43 — Where a PHA is to submit a homeownership program for HUD approval.
Citation: us:cfr:24:906.43
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-906.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 906 (Public Housing Homeownership Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A PHA must submit its proposed homeownership program together with supporting documentation, in a format prescribed by HUD, to the Special Applications Center with a copy to the appropriate HUD field office.
