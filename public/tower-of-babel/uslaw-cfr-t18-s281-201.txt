18 C.F.R. § 281.201 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:18:281.201
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-281.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 281 (Natural Gas Curtailment Under The Natural Gas Policy Act Of 1978). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this subpart is to implement section 401 of the NGPA in order to provide that effective November 1, 1979, the curtailment plans of interstate pipelines protect, to the maximum extent practicable, deliveries of natural gas for essential agricultural uses and for high-priority uses in accordance with the provisions of this subpart.
