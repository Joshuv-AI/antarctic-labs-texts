10 C.F.R. § 1022.23 — Applicant responsibilities.
Citation: us:cfr:10:1022.23
Creator: Department Of Energy (General Provisions)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1022.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1022 (Compliance With Floodplain And Wetland Environmental Review Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

DOE may require applicants for any use of real property (e.g., license, easement, lease, transfer, or disposal), permits, certificates, loans, grants, contract awards, allocations, or other forms of assistance or other entitlement related to activities in a floodplain or wetland to provide information necessary for DOE to comply with this part.
