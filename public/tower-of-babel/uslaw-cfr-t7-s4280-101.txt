7 C.F.R. § 4280.101 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:7:4280.101
Creator: Rural Business-Cooperative Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-4280.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 4280 (Loans And Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart contains the procedures and requirements for providing financial assistance under the Rural Energy for America Program (REAP) through grants to purchase and install a Renewable Energy System (RES) or make Energy Efficiency Improvements (EEI).
