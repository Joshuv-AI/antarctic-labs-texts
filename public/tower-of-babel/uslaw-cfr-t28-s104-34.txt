28 C.F.R. § 104.34 — Publication of awards.
Citation: us:cfr:28:104.34
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-104.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 104 (September 11Th Victim Compensation Fund). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Special Master reserves the right to publicize the amounts of some or all of the awards, but shall not publish the name of the claimants or victims that received each award. If published, these decisions would be intended by the Special Master as general guides for potential claimants and should not be viewed as precedent binding on the Special Master or her staff.
