47 C.F.R. § 1.926 — Application processing; initial procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:47:1.926
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-1.926
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 1 (Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Applications are assigned file numbers and service codes in order to facilitate processing. Assignment of a file number to an application is for administrative convenience and does not constitute a determination that the application is acceptable for filing. Purpose and service codes appear on the Commission forms.
