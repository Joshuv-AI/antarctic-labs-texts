10 C.F.R. § 851.12 — Implementation.
Citation: us:cfr:10:851.12
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-851.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 851 (Worker Safety And Health Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Contractors must implement the requirements of this part.
(b) Nothing in this part precludes a contractor from taking any additional protective action that is determined to be necessary to protect the safety and health of workers.
