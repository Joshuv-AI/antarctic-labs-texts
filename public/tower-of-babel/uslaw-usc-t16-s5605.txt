16 U.S.C. § 5605 — Rulemaking
Citation: us:usc:16:5605
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section5605
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 76 (Northwest Atlantic Fisheries Convention). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall promulgate regulations as may be necessary to carry out the purposes and objectives of the Convention and this chapter. Any such regulation may be made applicable, as necessary, to all persons and all vessels subject to the jurisdiction of the United States, wherever located.
