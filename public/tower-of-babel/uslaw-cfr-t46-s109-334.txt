46 C.F.R. § 109.334 — Working over water.
Citation: us:cfr:46:109.334
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-109.334
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 109 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The master or person in charge shall insure that each person working over the water is wearing a life preserver or a buoyant work vest.
