Grand variable Herz-Morrey-Hardy Spaces and applications to the boundedness of integral operators
Babar Sultan 1 ∗ {}^{1^{*}} , Amjad Hussain 1 and Mehvish Sultan 2

Abstract
. In this paper, the concept of grand variable Herz-Morrey-Hardy spaces are introduced. We also establish the atomic characterization of these spaces. As an application the authors investigate the continuity of a few singular integral operators. We also obtain the boundedness of commutators of homogeneous fractional integrals and their Lipschitz estimate on these spaces by applying the characterization.

4.

Let
\mathrm{S}^{n-1}
denote the unit sphere in
\mathbb{R}^{n}(n\geq 2)

is defined by

T_{\Omega,\sigma}g(x)=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\frac{\Omega(x-y)}{|x-y|^{n-\sigma}}g(y)dy.

\left[b,T_{\Omega,\sigma}\right]g(x)=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\frac{\Omega(x-y)}{|x-y|^{n-\sigma}}(b(x)-b(y))g(y)dy

In this paper we study the boundedness of the commutator
\left[b,T_{\Omega,\sigma}\right]

Let us first recall that the space
\operatorname{BMO}\left(\mathbb{R}^{n}\right)

\left\|g\right\|_{*}=\sup_{Q}\frac{1}{|Q|}\int_{Q}\left|g(x)-g_{Q}\right|dx<\infty

A nonnegative locally integrable function

such that
