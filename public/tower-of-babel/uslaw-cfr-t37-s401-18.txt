37 C.F.R. § 401.18 — Severability.
Citation: us:cfr:37:401.18
Creator: National Institute Of Standards And Technology, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-401.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 401 (Rights To Inventions Made By Nonprofit Organizations And Small Business Firms Under Government Grants, Contracts, And Cooperative Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this part are separate and severable from one another. If any provision is stayed or determined to be invalid, the remaining provisions shall remain in effect.
