21 C.F.R. § 571.110 — Procedure for objections and hearings.
Citation: us:cfr:21:571.110
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-571.110
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 571 (Food Additive Petitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Objections and hearings relating to food additive regulations under section 409(c), (d), or (h) of the act shall be governed by part 12 of this chapter.
