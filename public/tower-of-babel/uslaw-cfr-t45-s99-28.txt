45 C.F.R. § 99.28 — Record for decision.
Citation: us:cfr:45:99.28
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-99.28
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 99 (Procedure For Hearings For The Child Care And Development Fund). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The transcript of testimony, exhibits, and all papers and requests filed in the proceedings, except the correspondence section of the docket, including rulings and any recommended or initial decision, shall constitute the exclusive record for decision.
