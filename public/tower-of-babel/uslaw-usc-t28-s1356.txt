28 U.S.C. § 1356 — Seizures not within admiralty and maritime jurisdiction
Citation: us:usc:28:1356
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section1356
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part IV (Jurisdiction And Venue); Chapter 85 (District Courts; Jurisdiction). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The district courts shall have original jurisdiction, exclusive of the courts of the States, of any seizure under any law of the United States on land or upon waters not within admiralty and maritime jurisdiction, except matters within the jurisdiction of the Court of International Trade under section 1582 of this title.
