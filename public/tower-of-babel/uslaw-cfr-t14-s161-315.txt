14 C.F.R. § 161.315 — Receipt of complete application.
Citation: us:cfr:14:161.315
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-161.315
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 161 (Notice And Approval Of Airport Noise And Access Restrictions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) When a complete application has been received, the FAA will notify the applicant by letter that the FAA intends to act on the application.
(b) The FAA will publish notice of the proposed restriction in the Federal Register, inviting interested parties to file comments on the application within 30 days after publication of the Federal Register notice.
