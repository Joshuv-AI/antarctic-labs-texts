7 C.F.R. § 502.15 — Exceptions.
Citation: us:cfr:7:502.15
Creator: Agricultural Research Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-502.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 502 (Conduct On Beltsville Agriculture Research Center Property, Beltsville, Maryland). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator, Agricultural Research Service, may in individual cases, make prior, written exceptions to the rules and regulations in this part, if a determination is made that the exception is not adverse to the public interest.
