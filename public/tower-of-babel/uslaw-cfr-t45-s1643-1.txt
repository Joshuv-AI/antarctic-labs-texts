45 C.F.R. § 1643.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1643.1
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1643.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1643 (Restriction On Assisted Suicide, Euthanasia, And Mercy Killing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part is intended to ensure that recipients do not use any LSC funds for any assisted suicide, euthanasia or mercy killing activities prohibited by this part.
