20 C.F.R. § 61.202 — Time limitations for filing notice and claim.
Citation: us:cfr:20:61.202
Creator: Office Of Workers' Compensation Programs, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-61.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 61 (Claims For Compensation Under The War Hazards Compensation Act, As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The time limitation provisions found in 5 U.S.C. 8119 apply to the filing of claims under section 101(a) of the War Hazards Compensation Act. The Office may waive the time limitations if it finds that circumstances beyond the claimant's control prevented the filing of a timely claim.
