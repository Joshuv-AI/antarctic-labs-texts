32 C.F.R. § 2400.22 — Freedom of Information Act and Privacy Act requests.
Citation: us:cfr:32:2400.22
Creator: Office Of Science And Technology Policy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-2400.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 2400 (Regulations To Implement E.O. 12356; Office Of Science And Technology Policy Information Security Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Office of Science and Technology Policy shall process requests for declassification that are submitted under the provisions of the Freedom of Information Act, as amended, or the Privacy Act of 1974, in accordance with the provisions of those Acts.
