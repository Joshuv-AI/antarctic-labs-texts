38 U.S.C. § 5501A — Beneficiaries’ rights in mental competence determinations
Citation: us:usc:38:5501A
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section5501A
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part IV (General Administrative Provisions); Chapter 55 (Minors, Incompetents, And Other Wards). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may not make an adverse determination concerning the mental capacity of a beneficiary to manage monetary benefits paid to or for the beneficiary by the Secretary under this title unless such beneficiary has been provided all of the following, subject to the procedures and timelines prescribed by the Secretary for determinations of incompetency:
(1)
Notice of the proposed adverse determination and the supporting evidence.
(2)
An opportunity to request a hearing.
(3)
An opportunity to present evidence, including an opinion from a medical professional or other person, on the capacity of the beneficiary to manage monetary benefits paid to or for the beneficiary by the Secretary under this title.
(4)
An opportunity to be represented at no expense to the Government (including by counsel) at any such hearing and to bring a medical professional or other person to provide relevant testimony at any such hearing.
