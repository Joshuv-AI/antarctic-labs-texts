33 U.S.C. § 153 — Penalty for use of unlawful towline
Citation: us:usc:33:153
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section153
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 3 (Navigation Rules For Harbors, Rivers, And Inland Waters Generally); Subchapter I (Preliminary). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The master of the towing vessel shall be liable to the suspension or revocation of his license for any willful violation of regulations issued pursuant to section 152 of this title in the manner prescribed for incompetency, misconduct, or unskillfulness.
