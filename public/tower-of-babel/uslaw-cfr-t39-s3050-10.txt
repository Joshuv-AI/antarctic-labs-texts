39 C.F.R. § 3050.10 — Analytical principles to be applied in the Postal Service's annual periodic reports to the Commission.
Citation: us:cfr:39:3050.10
Creator: Postal Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-3050.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 3050 (Periodic Reporting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In its annual periodic reports to the Commission, the Postal Service shall use only accepted analytical principles. With respect to its submissions under § 3050.26, however, the Postal Service may elect to use an analytical principle prior to its acceptance by the Commission.
