7 U.S.C. § 2009aa–12 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:7:2009aa–12
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2009aa–12
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 50 (Agricultural Credit); Subchapter VI (Delta Regional Authority). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
There is authorized to be appropriated to the Authority to carry out this subchapter $40,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2025 through 2029, to remain available until expended.
(b) Administrative expenses
Not more than 5 percent of the amount appropriated under subsection (a) for a fiscal year shall be used for administrative expenses of the Authority.
