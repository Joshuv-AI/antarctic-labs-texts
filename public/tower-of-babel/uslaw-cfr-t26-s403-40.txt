26 C.F.R. § 403.40 — Place of filing.
Citation: us:cfr:26:403.40
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-403.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 403 (Disposition Of Seized Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The petition should be filed in triplicate with the District Director for the internal revenue district in which the property was seized.
