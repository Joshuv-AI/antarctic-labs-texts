7 C.F.R. § 94.100 — General.
Citation: us:cfr:7:94.100
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-94.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 94 (Poultry And Egg Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Analyses for voluntary egg product samples may be requested to certify that specifications regarding stated identity, quality, and wholesomeness are met; to test routinely for the presence of Salmonella; and to ensure laboratory quality control with testing activities.
