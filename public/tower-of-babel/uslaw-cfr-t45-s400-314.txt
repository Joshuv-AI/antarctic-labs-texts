45 C.F.R. § 400.314 — Priority in provision of services.
Citation: us:cfr:45:400.314
Creator: Office Of Refugee Resettlement, Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-400.314
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 400 (Refugee Resettlement Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A State must plan its targeted assistance program and allocate its targeted assistance funds in such a manner that services are provided to refugees in the following order of priority, except in certain individual extreme circumstances:
(a) Cash assistance recipients, particularly long-term recipients;
(b) Unemployed refugees who are not receiving cash assistance; and
(c) Employed refugees in need of services to retain employment or to attain economic independence.
