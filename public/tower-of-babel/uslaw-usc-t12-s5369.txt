12 U.S.C. § 5369 — Avoiding duplication
Citation: us:usc:12:5369
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section5369
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 53 (Wall Street Reform And Consumer Protection); Subchapter I (Financial Stability); Part C (Additional Board Of Governors Authority For Certain Nonbank Financial Companies And Bank Holding Companies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Board of Governors shall take any action that the Board of Governors deems appropriate to avoid imposing requirements under this part that are duplicative of requirements applicable to bank holding companies and nonbank financial companies under other provisions of law.
