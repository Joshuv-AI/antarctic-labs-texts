49 C.F.R. § 244.15 — Subjects to be addressed in a Safety Integration Plan not involving an amalgamation of operations.
Citation: us:cfr:49:244.15
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-244.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 244 (Regulations On Safety Integration Plans Governing Railroad Consolidations, Mergers, And Acquisitions Of Control). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an applicant does not propose an amalgamation of operations conducted on properties subject to the transaction, the applicant shall not be required to file a Safety Integration Plan unless directed to do so by FRA.
