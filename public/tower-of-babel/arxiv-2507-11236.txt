Improved sampling algorithms and Poincaré inequalities for non-log-concave distributions

Abstract

(2) The second moment of μ \mu is finite, i.e. , 𝐄 X ∼ μ ​ [ ‖ X ‖ 2 ] ≤ M \mathbf{E}_{X\sim\mu}\left[\|X\|^{2}\right]\leq M for some M < ∞ M<\infty . Recently, He and Zhang (COLT, 2025) showed that the query complexity of sampling from this family of distributions can be as large as ( L ​ M d ​ ε ) Ω ⁡ ( d ) \left(\frac{LM}{d\varepsilon}\right)^{\Omega(d)} where ε \varepsilon is the desired accuracy in total variation distance, and this indicates that the Poincaré constant can be arbitrarily large 1 1 1 Otherwise, a bounded Poincaré constant can imply samplers with 𝗉𝗈𝗅𝗒 ⁡ ( 1 / ε ) \mathsf{poly}(1/\varepsilon) queries (Chewi, Erdogdu, Li, Shen and Zhang (FoCM, 2024)), contradicting the lower bound in the work of He and Zhang (COLT, 2025). .
