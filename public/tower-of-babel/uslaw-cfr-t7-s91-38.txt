7 C.F.R. § 91.38 — Additional fees for appeal of analysis.
Citation: us:cfr:7:91.38
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-91.38
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 91 (Services And General Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The applicant for appeal sample testing will be charged a fee based on the formulas in § 91.37.
(b) The appeal fee will not be waived for any reason if analytical testing was completed in addition to the original analysis.
