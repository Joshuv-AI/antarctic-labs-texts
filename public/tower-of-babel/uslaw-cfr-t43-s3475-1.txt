43 C.F.R. § 3475.1 — Lease form.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3475.1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3475.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3470 (Coal Management Provisions And Limitations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Leases shall be issued on a standard form approved by the Director. The authorized officer may modify those provisions of the standard form which are not required by statute or regulations and may add such additional stipulations and conditions as he/she deems appropriate.
