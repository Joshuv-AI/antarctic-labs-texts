40 C.F.R. § 1515.18 — What happens if CEQ grants my request in full or in part?
Citation: us:cfr:40:1515.18
Creator: Council On Environmental Quality
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1515.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1515 (Freedom Of Information Act Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Once you have paid the fees that are due under subpart E of this part (if any), CEQ will promptly provide you with a copy of the records you requested, except for the records or portions of records we have determined to withhold under § 1515.16. We will follow the procedures in § 1515.19 with respect to those records or portions of records.
