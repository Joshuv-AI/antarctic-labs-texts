12 C.F.R. § 390.312 — Subordinated debt security.
Citation: us:cfr:12:390.312
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-390.312
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 390 (Regulations Transferred From The Office Of Thrift Supervision). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term subordinated debt security means any unsecured note, debenture, or other debt security issued by a State savings association and subordinated on liquidation to all claims having the same priority as account holders or any higher priority.
