48 C.F.R. § 12.103 — 12.103 Commercially available off-the-shelf (COTS) items.
Citation: us:cfr:48:12.103
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-12.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 12 (Acquisition Of Commercial Products And Commercial Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Commercially available off-the-shelf (COTS) items are defined in 2.101. Unless indicated otherwise, all of the policies that apply to commercial products also apply to COTS items. Section 12.505 lists the laws that are not applicable to COTS items (in addition to 12.503 and 12.504).
