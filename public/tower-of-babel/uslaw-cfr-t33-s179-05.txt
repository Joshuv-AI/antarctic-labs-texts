33 C.F.R. § 179.05 — Manufacturer discovered defects.
Citation: us:cfr:33:179.05
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-179.05
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 179 (Defect Notification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each manufacturer who is required to furnish a notice of a defect or failure to comply with a standard or regulation under 46 U.S.C. 4310(b), shall furnish that notice within 30 days after the manufacturer discovers or acquires information of the defect or failure to comply.
