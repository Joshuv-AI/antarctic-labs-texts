46 C.F.R. § 137.120 — Responsibility for compliance.
Citation: us:cfr:46:137.120
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-137.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 137 (Vessel Compliance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The owner and managing operator must ensure that the towing vessel is in compliance with this subchapter and other applicable laws and regulations at all times.
(b) Non-conformities and deficiencies must be corrected in a timely manner.
