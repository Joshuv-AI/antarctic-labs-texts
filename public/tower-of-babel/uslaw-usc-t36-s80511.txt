36 U.S.C. § 80511 — Liability for acts of officers and agents
Citation: us:usc:36:80511
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section80511
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 805 (Gold Star Wives Of America). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation is liable for the acts of its officers and agents acting within the scope of their authority and in accordance with the laws of the States in which it carries on its activities.
