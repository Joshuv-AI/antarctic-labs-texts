11 C.F.R. § 4.3 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:11:4.3
Creator: Federal Election Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-11/section-4.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 11 (Federal Elections), Part 4 (Public Records And The Freedom Of Information Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part implement the provisions of the Freedom of Information Act, 5 U.S.C. 552, with respect to the availability of records for inspection and copying.
