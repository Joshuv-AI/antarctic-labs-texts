34 C.F.R. § 76.653 — Needs, number of students, and types of services.
Citation: us:cfr:34:76.653
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-76.653
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 76 (State-Administered Formula Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A subgrantee shall determine the following matters on a basis comparable to that used by the subgrantee in providing for participation of public school students:
(a) The needs of students enrolled in private schools.
(b) The number of those students who will participate in a project.
(c) The benefits that the subgrantee will provide under the program to those students.
