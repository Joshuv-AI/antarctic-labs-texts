10 U.S.C. § 2343 — Waiver of applicability of certain laws
Citation: us:usc:10:2343
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section2343
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Property); Chapter 138 (Cooperative Agreements With Nato Allies And Other Countries); Subchapter I (Acquisition And Cross-Servicing Agreements). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Sections 4651, 3201, 3351, 3352(a), 3352(c), 3701 through 3708, and 3841 of this title and section 6306 of title 41 shall not apply to acquisitions made under the authority of section 2341 of this title or to agreements entered into under section 2342 of this title.
