48 C.F.R. § 34.102 — 34.102 Policy.
Citation: us:cfr:48:34.102
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-34.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 34 (Major System Acquisition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It is the policy of the Government, as required by section 126 of Public Law 102-558, to pay for any testing and qualification required for the use or incorporation of the industrial resources manufactured or developed with assistance provided under Title III of the Defense Production Act of 1950.
