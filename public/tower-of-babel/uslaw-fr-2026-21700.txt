Privacy Act of 1974; System of Records

AGENCY: Office of Information and Technology (OIT), Department of 
Veterans Affairs (VA).

ACTION: Rescindment of a system of records.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Pursuant to the Privacy Act of 1974, notice is hereby given 
that VA is rescinding an existing system of records titled, ``Federal 
Case Management Tool (FCMT)--VA'' (202VA005Q). FCMT is a web-based 
application that supports VA and the Department of War with the 
management and tracking of veteran and service member beneficiaries at 
all levels of the continuum of care.

DATES: Comments on this rescindment notice must be received no later 
than November 23, 2026.
    This rescindment is effective as of November 23, 2026 unless 
modified by a subsequent notice incorporating any comments received.

ADDRESSES: Comments may be submitted through www.regulations.gov under 
docket number VA-2026-VACO-0001 or mailed to VA Privacy Service 
(005X6F), 810 Vermont Avenue NW, Washington, DC 20420. Comments should 
indicate that they are submitted in response to ``Federal Case 
Management Tool (FCMT)--VA'' (202VA005Q). Comments received will be 
available at www.regulations.gov for public viewing, inspection, or 
copies.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Freda Perry, Project Manager, Federal 
Case Management Tool (FCMT), Product Delivery Service (005S), OIT, 
[email protected] or Riley Ross, Acting Director, Education Veterans 
Readiness and Employment Product Line--EVREPL (FCMT), Product Delivery 
Service (005S), OIT, [email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The FCMT is not being decommissioned; only 
the system of record notice is being rescinded. Although this system 
uses information collected by VA, it does not meet the criteria of a 
Privacy Act System of Records, as defined by the Privacy Act of 1974. 
All information used by FCMT is covered under various other VA Systems 
of Records.
