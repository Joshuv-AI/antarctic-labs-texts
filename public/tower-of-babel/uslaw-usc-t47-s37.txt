47 U.S.C. § 37 — Violations; punishment
Citation: us:usc:47:37
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title47-section37
Context: United States Code, Title 47 (Telecommunications); Chapter 2 (Submarine Cables). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever knowingly commits, instigates, or assists in any act forbidden by section 34 of this title shall be guilty of a misdemeanor and shall be fined not more than $5,000, or imprisoned for not more than one year, or both.
