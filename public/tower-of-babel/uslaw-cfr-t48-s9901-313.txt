48 C.F.R. § 9901.313 — 9901.313 Public hearings.
Citation: us:cfr:48:9901.313
Creator: Chapter 99—Cost Accounting Standards Board, Office Of Federal Procurement Policy, Office Of Management And Budget
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-9901.313
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 9901 (Rules And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Public hearings to assist the Board in the development and explanation of cost accounting standards and interpretive rulings may be held to the extent the Board in its sole discretion deems desirable. Notice of such hearings shall be given by publication in the Federal Register.
