25 C.F.R. § 115.807 — Will OTFM consult with tribes about investments of tribal trust funds?
Citation: us:cfr:25:115.807
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-115.807
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 115 (Trust Funds For Tribes And Individual Indians). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon the request of a tribe, OTFM will consult with the tribe annually to develop investment strategies to accommodate the cash flow needs of the tribe.
