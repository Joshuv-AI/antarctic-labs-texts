15 U.S.C. § 2230 — Surplus and excess Federal equipment
Citation: us:usc:15:2230
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section2230
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 49 (Fire Prevention And Control). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator shall make publicly available, including through the Internet, information on procedures for acquiring surplus and excess equipment or property that may be useful to State and local fire, emergency, and hazardous material handling service providers.
