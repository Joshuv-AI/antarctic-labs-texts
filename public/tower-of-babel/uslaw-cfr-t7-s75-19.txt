7 C.F.R. § 75.19 — Seed lot inspection.
Citation: us:cfr:7:75.19
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-75.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 75 (Provisions For Inspection And Certification Of Quality Of Agricultural And Vegetable Seeds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A lot inspection shall be made by obtaining a representative sample from a specified quantity of seed identified with a distinguishing mark or number to appear on all containers in the lot, and performing such test(s) as may be requested by the applicant. The identification mark or number must be approved by the inspector and will appear on the certificate to be issued.
