49 C.F.R. § 244.21 — Compliance and Enforcement.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 244 (Regulations On Safety Integration Plans Governing Railroad Consolidations, Mergers, And Acquisitions Of Control). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) After the Surface Transportation Board has approved a transaction subject to this part, a railroad implementing a transaction subject to this part shall operate in accordance with the Safety Implementation Plan approved by FRA until the properties involved in the transaction are completely integrated into the form contemplated in the Surface Transportation Board's approval of the transaction.
(b) FRA may exercise any or all of its enforcement remedies authorized by the Federal railroad safety laws if a railroad fails to comply with paragraph (a) of this section or to execute any measure contained in a Safety Implementation Plan approved by FRA.
