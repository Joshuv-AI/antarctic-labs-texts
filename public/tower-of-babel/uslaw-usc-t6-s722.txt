6 U.S.C. § 722 — Urban Search and Rescue Response System

Context: United States Code, Title 6 (Domestic Security); Chapter 2 (National Emergency Management); Subchapter I (Personnel Provisions); Part B (Emergency Management Capabilities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
There is in the Agency a system known as the Urban Search and Rescue Response System.
(b) Authorization of appropriations
There is authorized to be appropriated to carry out the system for fiscal year 2008, an amount equal to the amount appropriated for the system for fiscal year 2007 and an additional $20,000,000.
