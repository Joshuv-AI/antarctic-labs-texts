25 U.S.C. § 4243 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:25:4243
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section4243
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 43 (Native American Housing Assistance And Self-Determination); Subchapter VIII (Housing Assistance For Native Hawaiians). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated to the Department of Housing and Urban Development for grants under this subchapter such sums as may be necessary for each of fiscal years 2001, 2002, 2003, 2004, and 2005.
