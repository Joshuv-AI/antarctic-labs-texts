16 U.S.C. § 7128 — Termination of authority
Citation: us:usc:16:7128
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section7128
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 90 (Secure Rural Schools And Community Self-Determination); Subchapter II (Special Projects On Federal Land). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
The authority to initiate projects under this subchapter shall terminate on September 30, 2028.
(b) Deposits in Treasury
Any project funds not obligated by September 30, 2029, shall be deposited in the Treasury of the United States.
