10 U.S.C. § 9231 — Command: chaplains
Citation: us:usc:10:9231
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section9231
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 925 (Rank And Command). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An officer designated as a chaplain has rank without command.
