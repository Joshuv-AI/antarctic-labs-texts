18 C.F.R. § 701.314 — Exemptions.
Citation: us:cfr:18:701.314
Creator: Water Resources Council
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-701.314
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 701 (Council Organization). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No Council records system or systems are exempted from the provisions of 5 U.S.C. 552a as permitted under certain conditions by 5 U.S.C. 552a(j) and (k).
