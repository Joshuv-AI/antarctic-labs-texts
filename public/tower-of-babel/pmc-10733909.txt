Authors: Kun Qiao, Shun Arakaki, Mitsuharu Suzuki, Ken-ichi Nakayama
Published: 2023

Abstract:

The obtained device exhibited a current on/off ratio of 9.63 × 105, which is the largest value reported for rGO-based VOFETs. The vertical electron mobility of the PTCDI-C8 layer estimated by the space-charge-limited current technique was 1.14 × 10–3 cm2/(V s). However, it was not the main limiting factor for the current density in this device.
