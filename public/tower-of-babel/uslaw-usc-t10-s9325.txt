10 U.S.C. § 9325 — Computation of years of service: voluntary retirement; enlisted members
Citation: us:usc:10:9325
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section9325
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 941 (Retirement For Length Of Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
For the purpose of determining whether an enlisted member of the Air Force or the Space Force may be retired under section 9314 or 9317 of this title, his years of service are computed by adding all active service in the armed forces.
(b)
Time required to be made up under section 972(a) of this title may not be counted in computing years of service under subsection (a).
