26 U.S.C. § 5753 — Disposal of forfeited, condemned, and abandoned tobacco products, and cigarette papers and tubes
Citation: us:usc:26:5753
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section5753
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 52 (Tobacco Products And Cigarette Papers And Tubes); Subchapter F (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If it appears that any forfeited, condemned, or abandoned tobacco products, or cigarette papers and tubes, when offered for sale, will not bring a price equal to the tax due and payable thereon, and the expenses incident to the sale thereof, such articles shall not be sold for consumption in the United States but shall be disposed of in accordance with such regulations as the Secretary shall prescribe.
