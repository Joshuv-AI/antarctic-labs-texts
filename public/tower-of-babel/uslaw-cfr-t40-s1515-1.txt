40 C.F.R. § 1515.1 — What is the purpose of the rules in this part?
Citation: us:cfr:40:1515.1
Creator: Council On Environmental Quality
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1515.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1515 (Freedom Of Information Act Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) This part explains how you, a member of the public, may request copies of records from the Council on Environmental Quality (CEQ) under the Freedom of Information Act (FOIA). You can find the text of the FOIA at 5 U.S.C. 552.
(b) Nothing in this part entitles you to any service or to the disclosure of any record to which you are not entitled under the FOIA.
