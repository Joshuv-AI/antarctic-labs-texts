46 C.F.R. § 46.05-10 — -10 Foreign voyage.
Citation: us:cfr:46:46.05-10
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-46.05-10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 46 (Subdivision Load Lines For Passenger Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A foreign voyage for the purpose of marking passenger vessels with subdivision load lines is a voyage by sea between a port under the jurisdiction of the United States and a port of a foreign country, its colonies, territories, or protectorates, or conversely (a voyage exclusively on the Great Lakes excepted).
