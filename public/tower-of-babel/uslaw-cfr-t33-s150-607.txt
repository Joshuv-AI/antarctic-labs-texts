33 C.F.R. § 150.607 — What are the general safe working requirements?
Citation: us:cfr:33:150.607
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-150.607
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 150 (Deepwater Ports: Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) All equipment, including machinery, cranes, derricks, portable power tools, and, most importantly, safety gear must be used in a safe manner and in accordance with the manufacturer's recommended practice, unless otherwise stated in this subchapter.
(b) All machinery and equipment must be maintained in proper working order or removed.
