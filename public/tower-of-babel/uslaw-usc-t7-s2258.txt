7 U.S.C. § 2258 — Purchase of newspapers
Citation: us:usc:7:2258
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2258
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 55 (Department Of Agriculture). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Department of Agriculture is authorized to subscribe for such newspapers as may be necessary to carry out its authorized work.
