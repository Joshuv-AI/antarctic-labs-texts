48 C.F.R. § 1303.101-2 — 1303.101-2 Solicitation and acceptance of gratuities by government personnel.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1303.101-2
Creator: Chapter 13—Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1303.101-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1303 (Improper Business Practices And Personal Conflicts Of Interest). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Suspected violations of the prohibition on soliciting and accepting gratuities shall be reported to the Office of the Inspector General in accordance with DAO 207-10, Inspector General Investigations.
(b) To obtain legal advice regarding the solicitation and acceptance of gratuities, contact the Office of the Assistant General Counsel for Administration, Ethics Law and Program Division.
