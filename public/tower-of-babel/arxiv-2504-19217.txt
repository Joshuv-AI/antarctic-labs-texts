Abstract
. Invoking a rather simple semigroup approach, we refine the recent qualitative properties of Euclidean heat content obtained by van den Berg and Gittins.



2020 Mathematics Subject Classification
Primary: 35K05; Secondary: 35K20, 35K15.

In this paper we refine quantitatively the qualitative properties (in particular, the convexity and monotonicity obtained by van den Berg and Gittins in
[
BG24
]
) of heat content

H_{\Omega}(t)=\int_{\Omega}\int_{\Omega}p_{t}(x,y)dxdy,\quad t\geq 0,

where
\Omega
is a non-empty open set in
{\mathbb{R}}^{m}
and

p_{t}(x,y)=\frac{1}{(4\pi t)^{\frac{m}{2}}}e^{-\frac{|x-y|^{2}}{4t}}

is the standard heat kernel in
{\mathbb{R}}^{m}
. Using the heat semigroup

e^{t\Delta}f(x)=\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{m}}p_{t}(x,y)f(y)dy

and the
L^{2}({\mathbb{R}}^{m})
pairing for real valued functions

(f,g)=\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{m}}f(x)g(x)dx,

we can rewrite the heat content into the following convenient form

H_{\Omega}(2t)=(e^{2t\Delta}\chi_{\Omega},\chi_{\Omega})=(e^{t\Delta}\chi_{\Omega},e^{t\Delta}\chi_{\Omega}).

(0.1)

Via this formulation it is immediate that the heat content
H_{\Omega}(t)
is decreasing and convex.

We can also express the second order derivative of heat content in a transparent way

\frac{d^{2}}{dt^{2}}H_{\Omega}(2t)=4(\Delta e^{t\Delta}\chi_{\Omega},\Delta e^{t\Delta}\chi_{\Omega}).

(0.2)

Notice that

\Delta(e^{t\Delta}\chi_{\Omega})(x)=\int_{\Omega}\partial_{t}p_{t}(x,y)dy,

whereas the time derivative of heat kernel satisfies

\partial_{t}p_{t}(x,y)=\frac{1}{t}\left[-\frac{m}{2}+\frac{|x-y|^{2}}{4t}\right]p_{t}(x,y).

(0.3)

Suppose
\text{{\rm diam}}\,(\Omega)<\infty
. Therefore, for
t\geq(\text{{\rm diam}}\,(\Omega))^{2}
, as
|x-y|\leq\text{{\rm diam}}\,(\Omega)
,

\partial_{t}p_{t}(x,y)\leq-\frac{2m-1}{4}\frac{p_{t}(x,y)}{t}.

(0.4)

Hence, integrating (
0.4
) with respect to
(x,y)\in\Omega\times\Omega
gives

\frac{d}{dt}H_{\Omega}(t)\leq-\frac{2m-1}{4}\frac{H_{\Omega}(t)}{t},\quad t\geq{(\text{{\rm diam}}\,(\Omega))^{2}}.

(0.5)

Remark 0.1
.

(
0.5
) improves the monotonicity result of van den Berg and Gittins
[
BG24
]

\frac{d}{dt}H_{\Omega}(t)\leq-\frac{4m^{2}+4m-7}{8(m+2)e^{\frac{1}{4}}}\frac{H_{\Omega}(t)}{t},\quad t\geq{(\text{{\rm diam}}\,(\Omega))^{2}}.

Meanwhile, inserting (
0.4
) into (
0.2
), we get

\frac{d^{2}}{dt^{2}}H_{\Omega}(2t)\geq 4\left(\frac{2m-1}{4}\right)^{2}\frac{H_{\Omega}(2t)}{t^{2}},\quad t\geq(\text{{\rm diam}}\,(\Omega))^{2},

Equivalently, this can be written as

\frac{d^{2}}{dt^{2}}H_{\Omega}(t)\geq\left(\frac{2m-1}{2}\right)^{2}\frac{H_{\Omega}(t)}{t^{2}},\quad t\geq 2{(\text{{\rm diam}}\,(\Omega))^{2}}.

(0.6)

Remark 0.2
.

(
0.6
) improves, with essentially the same large-time condition, the convexity result of van den Berg and Gittins
[
BG24
]

(they considered the
\partial^{2}_{tt}p_{t}(x,y)
version of (
0.3
))

\frac{d^{2}}{dt^{2}}H_{\Omega}(t)\geq\frac{4m^{2}+4m-7}{16}\frac{H_{\Omega}(t)}{t^{2}},\quad t\geq{(\text{{\rm diam}}\,(\Omega))^{2}}.

Furthermore, using the heat kernel lower bound

p_{t}(x,y)\geq\frac{e^{-\frac{1}{8}}}{(4\pi t)^{\frac{m}{2}}},\quad t\geq 2{(\text{{\rm diam}}\,(\Omega))^{2}},

we get

H_{\Omega}(t)\geq e^{-\frac{1}{8}}\frac{|\Omega|^{2}}{(4\pi t)^{\frac{m}{2}}},

(0.7)

which in conjunction with (
0.6
) gives

\frac{d^{2}}{dt^{2}}H_{\Omega}(t)\geq\left(\frac{2m-1}{2}\right)^{2}\frac{e^{-\frac{1}{8}}}{(4\pi)^{\frac{m}{2}}}\frac{|\Omega|^{2}}{t^{\frac{m}{2}+2}},\quad t\geq 2{(\text{{\rm diam}}\,(\Omega))^{2}}.

(0.8)

Integrating this inequality over the interval
[t,+\infty)
, with
t\geq 2{(\text{{\rm diam}}\,(\Omega))^{2}}
, gives

\frac{d}{dt}H_{\Omega}(t)\leq-\frac{1}{\frac{m}{2}+1}\left(\frac{2m-1}{2}\right)^{2}\frac{e^{-\frac{1}{8}}}{(4\pi)^{\frac{m}{2}}}\frac{|\Omega|^{2}}{t^{\frac{m}{2}+1}},\quad t\geq 2{(\text{{\rm diam}}\,(\Omega))^{2}}.

(0.9)

This (except for
m=1
) is sharper than the resulted estimate by merely combining (
0.5
) and (
0.7
).
Since
H_{\Omega}(t)
is convex, its first derivative is increasing and continuous, hence

\frac{d}{dt}H_{\Omega}(t)\leq-\frac{1}{\frac{m}{2}+1}\left(\frac{2m-1}{2}\right)^{2}\frac{e^{-\frac{1}{8}}}{(4\pi)^{\frac{m}{2}}}\frac{|\Omega|^{2}}{(2{(\text{{\rm diam}}\,(\Omega))^{2}})^{\frac{m}{2}+1}},\quad t\leq 2{(\text{{\rm diam}}\,(\Omega))^{2}}.

(0.10)

By similar reasoning (i.e.,
\frac{d^{3}}{dt^{3}}H_{\Omega}(t)\leq 0
, verified again via (
0.1
)), we deduce from (
0.8
)

\frac{d^{2}}{dt^{2}}H_{\Omega}(t)\geq\left(\frac{2m-1}{2}\right)^{2}\frac{e^{-\frac{1}{8}}}{(4\pi)^{\frac{m}{2}}}\frac{|\Omega|^{2}}{(2{(\text{{\rm diam}}\,(\Omega))^{2}})^{\frac{m}{2}+2}},\quad t\leq 2{(\text{{\rm diam}}\,(\Omega))^{2}}.

(0.11)

Remark 0.3
.

In the small-time case for
{\mathbb{R}}^{m}
, there is no strict-convexity result in
[
BG24
]
.

In summary, we have obtained the following quantitatively refined properties (listed in the order of
[
BG24
, Theorem 3]
) of the heat content:
(
0.6
), (
0.8
)&(
0.11
), (
0.9
)&(
0.10
), (
0.5
).
The novelty lies in using the semigroup formulation (
0.1
) of the heat content.
