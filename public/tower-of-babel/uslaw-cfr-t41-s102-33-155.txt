41 C.F.R. § 102-33.155 — -33.155 Recovering operating cost.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-33.155
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-33.155
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-33 (33—Management Of Government Aircraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Under 31 U.S.C. 1535 and other statutes, you may need to recover aircraft operating costs for supporting other agencies. The method for setting rates will depend on the statutory authorities under which you acquired and operate your aircraft:
(1) The variable cost recovery rate; or
(2) The full cost recovery rate.
(b) See the U.S. Government Aircraft CAG for definitions.
