26 U.S.C. § 3212 — Determination of compensation
Citation: us:usc:26:3212
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section3212
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 22 (Railroad Retirement Tax Act); Subchapter B (Tax On Employee Representatives). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The compensation of an employee representative for the purpose of ascertaining the tax thereon shall be determined in the same manner and with the same effect as if the employee organization by which such employee representative is employed were an employer as defined in section 3231(a).
