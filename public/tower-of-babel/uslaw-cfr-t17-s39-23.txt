17 C.F.R. § 39.23 — Antitrust considerations.
Citation: us:cfr:17:39.23
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-39.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 39 (Derivatives Clearing Organizations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless necessary or appropriate to achieve the purposes of the Act, a derivatives clearing organization shall not adopt any rule or take any action that results in any unreasonable restraint of trade, or impose any material anticompetitive burden.
