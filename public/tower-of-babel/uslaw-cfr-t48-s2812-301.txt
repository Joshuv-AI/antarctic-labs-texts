48 C.F.R. § 2812.301 — 2812.301 Solicitation provisions and contract clauses for the acquisition of commercial items.
Citation: us:cfr:48:2812.301
Creator: Chapter 28—Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-2812.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2812 (Acquisition Of Commercial Items). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Contracting officers shall include the provisions and clauses at JAR 2852.212-4 in all solicitations and contracts for the acquisition of commercial items that require FAR 52.212-4, Contract Terms and Conditions—Commercial Items.
