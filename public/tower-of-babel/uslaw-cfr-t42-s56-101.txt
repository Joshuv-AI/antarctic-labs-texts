42 C.F.R. § 56.101 — Applicability.

Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-56.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 56 (Grants For Migrant Health Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations of this subpart are applicable to all grants authorized by section 319 of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 247d).
