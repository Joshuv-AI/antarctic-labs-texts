28 C.F.R. § 61.8 — Classified proposals.
Citation: us:cfr:28:61.8
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-61.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 61 (Procedures For Implementing The National Environmental Policy Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an environmental document includes classified matter, a version containing only unclassified material shall be prepared unless the head of the office, board, bureau or division determines that preparation of an unclassified version is not feasible.
