27 C.F.R. § 18.37 — Change in stockholders.
Citation: us:cfr:27:18.37
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-18.37
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 18 (Production Of Volatile Fruit-Flavor Concentrate). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The proprietor shall submit changes in the list of stockholders furnished under the provisions of § 18.25 annually on May 1. When the sale or transfer of capital stock results in a change of control or management of the business, the proprietor shall comply with the provisions of § 18.35.
