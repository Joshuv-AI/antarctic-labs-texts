34 C.F.R. § 650.1 — What is the Jacob K. Javits Fellowship Program?
Citation: us:cfr:34:650.1
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-650.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 650 (Jacob K. Javits Fellowship Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Under the Jacob K. Javits Fellowship Program the Secretary awards fellowships to students of superior ability selected on the basis of demonstrated achievement, financial need, and exceptional promise, for study at the doctoral level in selected fields of the arts, humanities, and social sciences.
(b) Students awarded fellowships under this program are called Jacob K. Javits Fellows.
