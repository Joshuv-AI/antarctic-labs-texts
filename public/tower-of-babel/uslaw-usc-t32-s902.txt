32 U.S.C. § 902 — Homeland defense activities: funds
Citation: us:usc:32:902
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title32-section902
Context: United States Code, Title 32 (National Guard); Chapter 9 (Homeland Defense Activities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Defense may provide funds to a Governor to employ National Guard units or members to conduct homeland defense activities that the Secretary determines to be necessary and appropriate for participation by the National Guard units or members, as the case may be.
