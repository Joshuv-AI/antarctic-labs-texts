22 U.S.C. § 4502 — “Institution of higher education” and “Advisory Committee” defined
Citation: us:usc:22:4502
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section4502
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 55 (Research And Training For Eastern Europe And Independent States Of Former Soviet Union). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this chapter—
(1)
the term “institution of higher education” has the same meaning given such term in section 1001 of title 20; and
(2)
the term “Advisory Committee” means the Advisory Committee for Studies of Eastern Europe and the Independent States of the Former Soviet Union established by section 4503(a) of this title.
