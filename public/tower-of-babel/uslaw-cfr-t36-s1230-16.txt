36 C.F.R. § 1230.16 — How does NARA handle allegations of unlawful or accidental removal, defacing, alteration, or destruction?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1230.16
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1230.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1230 (Unlawful Or Accidental Removal, Defacing, Alteration, Or Destruction Of Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon receiving any credible information that records are at risk of actual, impending, or threatened damage, alienation, or unauthorized destruction, NARA will contact the agency as follows:
(a) If the threat has not yet resulted in damage, removal, or destruction, NARA will contact the agency by phone promptly and follow up in writing within five business days.
(b) If records have allegedly been damaged, removed, or destroyed, NARA will notify the agency in writing promptly with a request for a response within 30 days.
