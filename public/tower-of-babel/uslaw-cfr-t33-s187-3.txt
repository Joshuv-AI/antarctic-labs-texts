33 C.F.R. § 187.3 — What vessels are affected by this part?
Citation: us:cfr:33:187.3
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-187.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 187 (Vessel Identification System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Only vessels numbered or titled by a participating State are affected by this part. Vessels documented under 46 U.S.C. chapter 121 and 46 CFR parts 67 and 68 are not affected.
