36 C.F.R. § 1002.21 — Smoking.
Citation: us:cfr:36:1002.21
Creator: Presidio Trust
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1002.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1002 (Resource Protection, Public Use And Recreation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Board may designate a portion of the area administered by the Presidio Trust, or all or a portion of a building, structure or facility as closed to smoking when necessary to protect resources, reduce the risk of fire, or prevent conflicts among visitor use activities. Smoking in an area or location so designated is prohibited.
(b) Smoking is prohibited within all caves and caverns.
