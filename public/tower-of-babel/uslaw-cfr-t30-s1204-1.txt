30 C.F.R. § 1204.1 — What is the purpose of this part?
Citation: us:cfr:30:1204.1
Creator: Office Of Natural Resources Revenue, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-1204.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 1204 (Alternatives For Marginal Properties). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part explains how you as a lessee or designee of a Federal onshore or Outer Continental Shelf (OCS) oil and gas lease may obtain prepayment or accounting and auditing relief for production from certain marginal properties. This part does not apply to production from Indian leases, even if the Indian lease is within an agreement that qualifies as a marginal property.
