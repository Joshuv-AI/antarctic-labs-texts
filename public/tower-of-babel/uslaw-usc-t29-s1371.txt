29 U.S.C. § 1371 — Penalty for failure to timely provide required information
Citation: us:usc:29:1371
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section1371
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 18 (Employee Retirement Income Security Program); Subchapter III (Plan Termination Insurance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation may assess a penalty, payable to the corporation, against any person who fails to provide any notice or other material information required under this subtitle, subtitle A, B, or C, or section 1083(k)(4) or 1085a(g)(4) of this title,,1 or any regulations prescribed under any such subtitle or such section, within the applicable time limit specified therein. Such penalty shall not exceed $1,000 for each day for which such failure continues.
