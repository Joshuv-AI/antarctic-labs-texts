7 C.F.R. § 948.1 — Secretary.
Citation: us:cfr:7:948.1
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-948.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 948 (Irish Potatoes Grown In Colorado). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Secretary means the Secretary of Agriculture of the United States, or any officer or employee of the Department of Agriculture to whom authority has heretofore been delegated, or to whom authority hereafter may be delegated, to act in his stead.
