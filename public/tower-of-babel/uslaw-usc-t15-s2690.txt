15 U.S.C. § 2690 — Relationship to other Federal law
Citation: us:usc:15:2690
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section2690
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 53 (Toxic Substances Control); Subchapter IV (Lead Exposure Reduction). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this subchapter shall affect the authority of other appropriate Federal agencies to establish or enforce any requirements which are at least as stringent as those established pursuant to this subchapter.
