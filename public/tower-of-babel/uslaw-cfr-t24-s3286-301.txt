24 C.F.R. § 3286.301 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:24:3286.301
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-3286.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 3286 (Manufactured Home Installation Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this subpart D is to establish the requirements for a person to qualify to provide the training required under subpart C of this part. This training is required for manufactured home installers who want to be licensed in accordance with the HUD-administered installation program.
