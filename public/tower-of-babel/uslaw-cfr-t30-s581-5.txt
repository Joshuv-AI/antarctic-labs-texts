30 C.F.R. § 581.5 — False statements.
Citation: us:cfr:30:581.5
Creator: Bureau Of Ocean Energy Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-581.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 581 (Leasing Of Minerals Other Than Oil, Gas, And Sulphur In The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Under the provisions of 18 U.S.C. 1001, it is a crime punishable by up to 5 years imprisonment or a fine of $10,000, or both, for anyone knowingly and willfully to submit or cause to be submitted to any Agency of the United States any false or fraudulent statement(s) to any matters within the Agency's jurisdiction.
