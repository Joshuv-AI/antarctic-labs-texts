50 U.S.C. § 1704 — Authority to issue regulations
Citation: us:usc:50:1704
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section1704
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 35 (International Emergency Economic Powers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President may issue such regulations, including regulations prescribing definitions, as may be necessary for the exercise of the authorities granted by this chapter.
