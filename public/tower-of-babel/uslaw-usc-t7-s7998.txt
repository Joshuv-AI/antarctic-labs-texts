7 U.S.C. § 7998 — Estimates of net farm income
Citation: us:usc:7:7998
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section7998
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 106 (Commodity Programs); Subchapter VI (Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In each issuance of projections of net farm income, the Secretary shall include (as determined by the Secretary)—
(1)
an estimate of the net farm income earned by commercial producers in the United States; and
(2)
an estimate of the net farm income attributable to commercial producers of each of the following:
(A)
Livestock.
(B)
Loan commodities.
(C)
Agricultural commodities other than loan commodities.
