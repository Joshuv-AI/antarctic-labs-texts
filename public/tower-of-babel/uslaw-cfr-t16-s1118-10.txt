16 C.F.R. § 1118.10 — Remedies for failure to permit authorized investigations.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1118.10
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1118.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1118 (Investigations, Inspections And Inquiries Under The Consumer Product Safety Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In the event a person or firm fails to comply with any investigative process authorized by these rules, the Commission may seek appropriate action within its authority under the Consumer Product Safety Act (15 U.S.C. 2051, et seq.)
