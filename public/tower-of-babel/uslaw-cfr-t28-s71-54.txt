28 C.F.R. § 71.54 — Collection and compromise of liabilities imposed by Agency.
Citation: us:cfr:28:71.54
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-71.54
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 71 (Implementation Of The Provisions Of The Program Fraud Civil Remedies Act Of 1986). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Assistant Attorney General of the Civil Division is authorized to initiate actions to collect assessments and civil penalties imposed under the Program Fraud Civil Remedies Act of 1986, and, subsequent to the filing of a petition for judicial review pursuant to section 3805 of the Act, to defend such actions and/or to approve settlements and compromises of such liability.
