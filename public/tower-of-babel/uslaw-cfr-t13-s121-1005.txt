13 C.F.R. § 121.1005 — How must a protest be filed with the contracting officer?
Citation: us:cfr:13:121.1005
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-121.1005
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 121 (Small Business Size Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A protest must be delivered to the contracting officer by hand, telegram, mail, facsimile, Federal Express or other overnight delivery service, e-mail, or telephone. If a protest is made by telephone, the contracting officer must later receive a confirming letter either within the 5-day period in § 121.1004(a)(1) or postmarked no later than one day after the date of the telephone protest.
