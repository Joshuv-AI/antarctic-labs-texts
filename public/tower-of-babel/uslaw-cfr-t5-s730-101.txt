5 C.F.R. § 730.101 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:5:730.101
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-730.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 730 (Notification Of Post-Employment Restrictions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part implements 5 U.S.C. 7302, which requires agencies to provide written notice to senior executives and other individuals covered by 18 U.S.C. 207(c)(2)(A)(ii) that they are subject to certain post-employment conflict-of-interest restrictions in 18 U.S.C. 207(c).
