40 C.F.R. § 57.110 — Reimbursement of State or local agency.
Citation: us:cfr:40:57.110
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-57.110
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 57 (Primary Nonferrous Smelter Orders). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As a condition of issuing an NSO, any issuing agency may require the smelter operator to pay a fee to the State or local agency sufficient to defray the issuing agency's expenses in issuing and enforcing the NSO.
