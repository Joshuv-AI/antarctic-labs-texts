45 C.F.R. § 2522.207 — How do I determine an individual's eligibility to serve in a covered position?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2522.207
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2522.207
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2522 (Americorps Participants, Programs, And Applicants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To determine an individual's eligibility to serve in a covered position, you must follow the procedures in part 2540 of this chapter.
