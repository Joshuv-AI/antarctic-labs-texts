26 U.S.C. § 139I — Continuation coverage premium assistance
Citation: us:usc:26:139I
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section139I
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 1 (Normal Taxes And Surtaxes); Subchapter B (Computation Of Taxable Income); Part III (Items Specifically Excluded From Gross Income). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In the case of an assistance eligible individual (as defined in subsection (a)(3) of section 9501 of the American Rescue Plan Act of 2021), gross income does not include any premium assistance provided under subsection (a)(1) of such section.
