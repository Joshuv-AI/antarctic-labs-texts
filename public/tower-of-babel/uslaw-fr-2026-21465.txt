Submission for OMB Review; Comment Request

AGENCY: Office of the Under Secretary of Defense for Personnel and 
Readiness (OUSD(P&R)), Department of Defense (DoD).

ACTION: 30-Day information collection notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The DoD (referred to herein as ``the Department'', 
``Department of War'' or ``DoW'') has submitted to the Office of 
Management and Budget (OMB) for clearance the following proposal for 
collection of information under the provisions of the Paperwork 
Reduction Act.

DATES: Consideration will be given to all comments received by November 
19, 2026.

ADDRESSES: Written comments and recommendations for the proposed 
information collection should be sent within 30 days of publication of 
this notice to www.reginfo.gov/public/do/PRAMain. Find this particular 
information collection by selecting ``Currently under 30-day Review--
Open for Public Comments'' or by using the search function.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Mr. Reginald Lucas, (571) 372-7574, 
[email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: 
    Title; Associated Form; and OMB Number: Pure Power; OMB Control 
Number 0704-MFPP.
    Type of Request: New.
    Number of Respondents: 17.
    Responses per Respondent: 128.
    Annual Responses: 2,176.
    Average Burden per Response: 2 minutes.
    Annual Burden Hours: 72.5.
    Needs and Uses: This study is designed to assess the unique social, 
emotional and behavioral needs of military-connected youth, directly 
aligning with the administration's focus on service member readiness, 
retention, and optimized quality of life for their families.
    Affected Public: Individuals and households.
    Frequency: On occasion.
    Respondent's Obligation: Voluntary.
    DOD Clearance Officer: Mr. Reginald Lucas.
    Requests for copies of the information collection proposal should 
be sent to Mr. Lucas at [email protected].
