25 C.F.R. § 166.222 — Are there standard permit forms?
Citation: us:cfr:25:166.222
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-166.222
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 166 (Grazing Permits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. Standard permit forms, including bid forms, permit forms, and permit modification forms are available at our agency offices.
