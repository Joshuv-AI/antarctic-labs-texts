25 C.F.R. § 171.300 — Does BIA restrict my water use?
Citation: us:cfr:25:171.300
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-171.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 171 (Irrigation Operation And Maintenance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You must not interfere with or alter our service to you without our prior written authorization; and
(b) You must only use water we deliver for authorized uses. We may withhold services if you use water for any other purpose.
