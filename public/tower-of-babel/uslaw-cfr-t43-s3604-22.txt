43 C.F.R. § 3604.22 — What conditions and restrictions pertain to my free use permit?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3604.22
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3604.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3600 (Mineral Materials Disposal). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You must not barter or sell mineral materials that you obtain under a free use permit.
(b) You must not remove mineral materials before BLM issues you a permit or after your permit expires.
(c) BLM may incorporate other conditions and restrictions into your free use permit.
