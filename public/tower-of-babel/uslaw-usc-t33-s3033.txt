33 U.S.C. § 3033 — Suspension during war or emergency
Citation: us:usc:33:3033
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section3033
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 43 (National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration Commissioned Officer Corps); Subchapter II (Appointment And Promotion Of Officers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In time of emergency declared by the President or by the Congress, and in time of war, the President is authorized, in the President’s discretion, to suspend the operation of all or any part of the provisions of law pertaining to promotion of commissioned officers of the Administration.
