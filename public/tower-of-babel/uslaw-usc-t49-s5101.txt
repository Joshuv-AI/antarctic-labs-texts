49 U.S.C. § 5101 — Purpose
Citation: us:usc:49:5101
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section5101
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Chapter 51 (Transportation Of Hazardous Material). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purpose of this chapter is to protect against the risks to life, property, and the environment that are inherent in the transportation of hazardous material in intrastate, interstate, and foreign commerce.
