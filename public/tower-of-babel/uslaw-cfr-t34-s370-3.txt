34 C.F.R. § 370.3 — Who is eligible for services and information under the CAP?
Citation: us:cfr:34:370.3
Creator: Office Of Special Education And Rehabilitative Services, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-370.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 370 (Client Assistance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Any client or client-applicant is eligible for the services described in § 370.4.
(b) Any individual with a disability is eligible to receive information on the services and benefits available to individuals with disabilities under the Act and title I of the ADA.
