7 C.F.R. § 1.818 — Additional documents to be filed with hearing clerk.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1.818
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1.818
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1 (Administrative Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to the documents or papers required or authorized by the foregoing provisions of this subpart to be filed with the hearing clerk, the hearing clerk shall receive for filing and shall have custody of all papers, reports, records, orders, and other documents which relate to the administration of any order and which the Secretary is required to issue or to approve.
