48 C.F.R. § 219.502-1 — 219.502-1 Requirements for setting aside acquisitions.
Citation: us:cfr:48:219.502-1
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-219.502-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 219 (Small Business Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Do not set aside acquisitions—
(1) For supplies that were developed and financed, in whole or in part, by Canadian sources under the U.S.-Canadian Defense Development Sharing Program; or
(2) Excluded from procurement center representative review (see 219.402(c)(iii)).
