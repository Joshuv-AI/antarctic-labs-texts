42 U.S.C. § 10200 — Study of research and development needs for monitored retrievable storage proposal
Citation: us:usc:42:10200
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section10200
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 108 (Nuclear Waste Policy); Subchapter II (Research, Development, And Demonstration Regarding Disposal Of High-Level Radioactive Waste And Spent Nuclear Fuel). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 6 months after January 7, 1983, the Secretary shall submit to the Congress a report describing the research and development activities the Secretary considers necessary to develop the proposal required in section 10161(b) of this title with respect to a monitored retrievable storage facility.
