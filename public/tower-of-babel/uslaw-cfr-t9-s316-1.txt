9 C.F.R. § 316.1 — Authorization required to make devices bearing official marks.
Citation: us:cfr:9:316.1
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-316.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 316 (Marking Products And Their Containers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No brand manufacturer, printer or other person shall cast, print, lithograph, or otherwise make or cause to be made any device containing any official mark or simulation thereof without prior written authority therefor from the Administrator as provided for in part 317 of this subchapter.
