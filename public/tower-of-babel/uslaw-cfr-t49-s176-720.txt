49 C.F.R. § 176.720 — Requirements for carriage of INF cargo in international transportation.
Citation: us:cfr:49:176.720
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-176.720
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 176 (Carriage By Vessel). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to all other applicable requirements of this subchapter, a vessel carrying INF cargo (see § 176.2, under INF cargo definition) in international transportation must meet the requirements of the INF Code contained in the IMDG Code (IBR, see § 171.7 of this subchapter).
