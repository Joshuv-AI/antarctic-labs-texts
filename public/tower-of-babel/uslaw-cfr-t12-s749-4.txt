12 C.F.R. § 749.4 — Format for vital records preservation.
Citation: us:cfr:12:749.4
Creator: National Credit Union Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-749.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 749 (Vital Records Preservation Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Preserved vital records may be in any format that can be used to reconstruct the credit union's vital records. The format used must accurately reflect the information in the record, remain accessible to all persons entitled to access by statute, regulation, or rule of law, and be capable of reproduction by transmission, printing, or otherwise.
