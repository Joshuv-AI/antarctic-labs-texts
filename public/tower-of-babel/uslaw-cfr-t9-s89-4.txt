9 C.F.R. § 89.4 — Watering.
Citation: us:cfr:9:89.4
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-89.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 89 (Statement Of Policy Under The Twenty-Eight Hour Law). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Livestock should be furnished an ample supply of potable water. Water treated with chemicals for industrial or boiler use, or taken from streams or ponds containing sewage, mud, or other objectionable matter should not be used. Troughs and other receptacles should be clean. In cold weather, the water should be free from ice.
