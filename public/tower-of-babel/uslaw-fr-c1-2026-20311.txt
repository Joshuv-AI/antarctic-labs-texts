Electronic Comments

     Use the Commission's internet comment form (https://www.sec.gov/comments/4-933/potential-designation-chartered-financial-analyst-designation-qualifying-natural-persons-accredited); or
     Send an email to [email protected]. Please include 
File Number 4-933 on the subject line. To submit a comment to more than 
one file, please include each file number on the subject line.

Paper Comments

     Send paper comments to Vanessa A. Countryman, Secretary, 
Securities and Exchange Commission, 100 F Street NE, Washington, DC 
20549-1090.

to more than one file, please refer to each file number. This file

the Commission process and review your comments more efficiently, 
please use only one method of submission. The Commission will post all 
submitted comments on its website (https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/public-comments/4-933). Do not include personally 
identifiable information in submissions; you should submit only 
information that you wish to make available publicly. The Commission

material that is obscene or subject to copyright protection.''
