20 C.F.R. § 655.136 — Withdrawal of an Application for Temporary Employment Certification and job order.
Citation: us:cfr:20:655.136
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-655.136
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 655 (Temporary Employment Of Foreign Workers In The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The employer may withdraw an Application for Temporary Employment Certification and the related job order at any time before the CO makes a determination under § 655.160. However, the employer is still obligated to comply with the terms and conditions of employment contained in the Application for Temporary Employment Certification and job order with respect to all workers recruited in connection with that application and job order.
(b) To request withdrawal, the employer must submit a request in writing to the NPC identifying the Application for Temporary Employment Certification and job order and stating the reason(s) for the withdrawal.
