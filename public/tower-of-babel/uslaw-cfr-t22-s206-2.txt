22 C.F.R. § 206.2 — Production or disclosure prohibited unless approved by the General Counsel.
Citation: us:cfr:22:206.2
Creator: Agency For International Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-206.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 206 (Testimony By Employees And The Production Of Documents In Proceedings Where A.I.D. Is Not A Party). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No employee or former employee of the Agency shall, in response to a demand of a court or other authority set forth in § 206.1(d), produce any material or disclose any information described in § 206.1(a) without the approval of the General Counsel or his designee.
