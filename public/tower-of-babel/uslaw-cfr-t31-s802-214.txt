31 C.F.R. § 802.214 — Excepted real estate foreign state.
Citation: us:cfr:31:802.214
Creator: Office Of Investment Security, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-802.214
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 802 (Regulations Pertaining To Certain Transactions By Foreign Persons Involving Real Estate In The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term excepted real estate foreign state means, until February 13, 2023, a foreign state that meets the criteria in paragraph (a) of this section, and beginning on February 13, 2023, a foreign state that meets both the criteria in paragraphs (a) and (b) of this section:
(a) Is identified by the Committee as an eligible foreign state and
(b) Is a foreign state for which the Committee has made a determination under § 802.1001(a).
