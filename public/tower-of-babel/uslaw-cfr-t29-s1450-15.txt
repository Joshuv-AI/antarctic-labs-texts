29 C.F.R. § 1450.15 — Referrals to the Department of Justice or the General Accounting Office.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1450.15
Creator: Federal Mediation And Conciliation Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1450.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1450 (Collections Of Claims Owed The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Referrals to the Department of Justice or the General Accounting Office shall be made in accordance with the standards set forth in part 105 of the Federal Claims Collection Standards (4 CFR part 105).
