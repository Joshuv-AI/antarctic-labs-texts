21 C.F.R. § 862.1085 — Angiotensin I and renin test system.
Citation: us:cfr:21:862.1085
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-862.1085
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 862 (Clinical Chemistry And Clinical Toxicology Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. An angiotensin I and renin test system is a device intended to measure the level of angiotensin I generated by renin in plasma. Angiotensin I measurements are used in the diagnosis and treatment of certain types of hypertension.
(b) Classification. Class II.
