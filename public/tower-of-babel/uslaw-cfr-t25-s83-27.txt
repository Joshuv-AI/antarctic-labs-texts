25 C.F.R. § 83.27 — What are technical assistance reviews?
Citation: us:cfr:25:83.27
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-83.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 83 (Procedures For Federal Acknowledgment Of Indian Tribes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Technical assistance reviews are preliminary reviews for OFA to tell the petitioner where there appear to be evidentiary gaps for the criteria that will be under review in that phase and to provide the petitioner with an opportunity to supplement or revise the documented petition.
