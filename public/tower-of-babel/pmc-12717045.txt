Author Correction: Inactivation of cytidine triphosphate synthase 1 prevents fatal auto-immunity in mice
Authors: Claire Soudais, Romane Schaus, Camille Bachelet, Norbert Minet, Sara Mouasni, Cécile Garcin, Caique Lopes Souza, Pierre David, Clara Cousu, Hélène Asnagli, Andrew Parker, Paul Palmquist-Gomes, Fernando E Sepulveda, Sébastien Storck, Sigolène M Meilhac, Alain Fischer, Emmanuel Martin, Sylvain Latour
Published: 2025

Correction to: Nature Communications 10.1038/s41467-024-45805-y, published online 04 March 2025

In the version of the article initially published, due to a figure preparation mistake in Fig. 7d, the left-hand image in the “Liver” row was a duplicate of the right-hand image. The figure is now amended in the HTML and PDF versions of the article.
