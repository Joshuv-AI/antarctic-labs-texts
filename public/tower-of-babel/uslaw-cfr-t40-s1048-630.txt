40 C.F.R. § 1048.630 — What are the provisions for exempting engines used solely for competition?
Citation: us:cfr:40:1048.630
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1048.630
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1048 (Control Of Emissions From New, Large Nonroad Spark-Ignition Engines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We may grant you an exemption from the standards and requirements of this part for a new engine on the grounds that it is to be used solely for competition under the provisions of 40 CFR 1054.620. The requirements of this part do not apply to engines that we exempt for use solely for competition.
