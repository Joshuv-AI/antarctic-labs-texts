49 C.F.R. § 238.127 — Low-location emergency exit path marking.
Citation: us:cfr:49:238.127
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-238.127
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 238 (Passenger Equipment Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

On or after January 28, 2015, low-location emergency exit path marking shall be provided in each passenger car in accordance with the minimum requirements specified in APTA PR-PS-S-004-99, Rev. 2, “Standard for Low-Location Exit Path Marking,” Authorized October 7, 2007, or an alternative standard providing at least an equivalent level of safety, if approved by FRA pursuant to § 238.21. The incorporation by reference of this APTA standard was approved by the Director of the Federal Register in accordance with 5 U.S.C. 552(a) and 1 CFR Part 51.
