18 C.F.R. § 367.4120 — Account 412, Cost and expenses of construction or other services.
Citation: us:cfr:18:367.4120
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-367.4120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 367 (Uniform System Of Accounts For Centralized Service Companies Subject To The Provisions Of The Public Utility Holding Company Act Of 2005, Federal Power Act And Natural Gas Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This account must include expenditures related to the performance of construction or service contracts, under which the service company undertakes projects to construct physical property for associate or non-associate companies (see General Instructions § 367.24, Construction and service contracts for other companies) and the cost of services performed for others not provided for elsewhere.
