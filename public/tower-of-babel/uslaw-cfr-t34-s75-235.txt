34 C.F.R. § 75.235 — The notification of grant award.
Citation: us:cfr:34:75.235
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-75.235
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 75 (Direct Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) To make a grant, the Secretary issues a notification of grant award and sends it to the grantee.
(b) The notification of grant award sets the amount of the grant award and establishes other specific conditions, if any.
