42 C.F.R. § 71.47 — Special provisions relating to airports: Office and isolation facilities.
Citation: us:cfr:42:71.47
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-71.47
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 71 (Foreign Quarantine). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each U.S. airport which receives international traffic shall provide without cost to the Government suitable office, isolation, and other exclusive space for carrying out the Federal responsibilities under this part.
