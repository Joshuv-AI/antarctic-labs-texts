Retraction Note: Successive use of microorganisms to remove chromium from wastewater
Authors: Amina Elahi, Iqra Arooj, Dilara A Bukhari, Abdul Rehman
Published: 2026

Retraction Note: Applied Microbiology and Biotechnology (2020) 104:3729–3743

10.1007/s00253-020-10533-y

The Editor-in-Chief has retracted this article. Concerns have been raised regarding overlap with a previously published article by different authors [1], including the republication of Figure 1 without obtaining appropriate permission. Additionally, Figure 2 is previously published as Figure 6 in [2] and it is not clear if permission to republish this figure was obtained.

The authors have not responded to correspondence regarding this retraction.
