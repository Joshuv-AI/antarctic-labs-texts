33 C.F.R. § 127.1405 — Repairs.
Citation: us:cfr:33:127.1405
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-127.1405
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 127 (Waterfront Facilities Handling Liquefied Natural Gas And Liquefied Hazardous Gas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each operator of a waterfront facility handling LHG must ensure that—
(a) Equipment is repaired so that—
(1) The equipment continues to meet the applicable requirements in this subpart;
(2) Safety is not compromised; and
(b) Welding and cutting meet NFPA 51B (incorporated by reference, see § 127.003).
