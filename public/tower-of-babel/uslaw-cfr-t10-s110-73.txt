10 C.F.R. § 110.73 — Availability of NRC records.
Citation: us:cfr:10:110.73
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-110.73
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 110 (Export And Import Of Nuclear Equipment And Material). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Commission records under this part will be made available to the public only in accordance with part 9 of this chapter.
(b) Proprietary information provided under this part may be protected under Part 9 and § 2.390(b), (c), and (d) of this chapter.
