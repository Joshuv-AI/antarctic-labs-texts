45 C.F.R. § 2522.205 — To whom must I apply eligibility criteria relating to criminal history?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2522.205
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2522.205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2522 (Americorps Participants, Programs, And Applicants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must apply eligibility criteria relating to criminal history to individuals specified in 45 CFR 2540.201.
