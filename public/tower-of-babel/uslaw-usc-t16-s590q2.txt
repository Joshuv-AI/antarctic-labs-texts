16 U.S.C. § 590q–2 — Voluntary relinquishment of allotments
Citation: us:usc:16:590q–2
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section590q–2
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 3B (Soil Conservation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the Secretary may provide for the reduction or cancellation of any allotment or base when the owner of the farm states in writing that he has no further use of such allotment or base.
