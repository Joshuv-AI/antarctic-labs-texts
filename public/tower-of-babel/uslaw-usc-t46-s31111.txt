46 U.S.C. § 31111 — Claims by nationals of foreign countries
Citation: us:usc:46:31111
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section31111
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 311 (Suits Involving Public Vessels). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A national of a foreign country may not maintain a civil action under this chapter unless it appears to the satisfaction of the court in which the action is brought that the government of that country, in similar circumstances, allows nationals of the United States to sue in its courts.
