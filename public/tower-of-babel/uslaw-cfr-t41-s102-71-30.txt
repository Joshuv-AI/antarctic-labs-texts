41 C.F.R. § 102-71.30 — -71.30 Automatic sprinkler systems.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-71.30
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-71.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-71 (71—Real Property Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The performance objective of the automatic sprinkler system is that it must be capable of protecting human lives. Sprinklers must be capable of controlling the spread of fire and its effects beyond the room of origin. A functioning sprinkler system must activate prior to the onset of flashover.
