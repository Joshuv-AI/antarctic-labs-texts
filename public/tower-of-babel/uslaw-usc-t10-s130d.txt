10 U.S.C. § 130d — Treatment under Freedom of Information Act of certain confidential information shared with State and local personnel
Citation: us:usc:10:130d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section130d
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization And General Military Powers); Chapter 3 (General Powers And Functions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Confidential business information and other sensitive but unclassified homeland security information in the possession of the Department of Defense that is shared, pursuant to section 892 of the Homeland Security Act of 2002 (6 U.S.C. 482), with State and local personnel (as defined in such section) shall not be subject to disclosure under section 552 of title 5 by virtue of the sharing of such information with such personnel.
