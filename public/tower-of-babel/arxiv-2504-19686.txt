Four-Fermion Deformations on Line Defects in QED 4 with Yukawas
Alessandra D’Alise, Giulia Muco, Francesco Sannino

Abstract
We elucidate the dynamics induced by a Wilson Line of charge q q and electron charge e e in Q ​ E ​ D 4 QED_{4} coupled to a real scalar via Yukawa interactions. We observe that there is a range of the fixed product e 2 ​ q e^{2}q with e → 0 e\rightarrow 0 and q → ∞ q\rightarrow\infty for which four-fermion operators play a dominant role for the dynamics of the theory. We uncover the fixed-point dynamics of the theory and analyze their critical behavior.

Fermionic
QED_{4}

Let us now focus on the example of fermionic QED in four space-time dimensions with metric
\eta_{\mu\nu}=\text{diag}(1,-\vec{1})

q
fixed in space at the position
\vec{x}=0

\mathcal{L}=-\frac{1}{4e^{2}}F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu}+i\bar{\psi}\not{D}\psi-q\delta^{3}(\vec{x})\delta^{\mu}_{0}A_{\mu}(x)

\hat{\Delta}(\alpha^{\dagger}\alpha)=1-2\nu<1

\frac{\partial}{\partial\log{\mu}}\Bigl(\frac{\beta}{\alpha}\Bigr)=0\ \ \Rightarrow\ \ \frac{\partial}{\partial f}\Bigl(\frac{\beta}{\alpha}\Bigr)\beta_{f}-r_{0}\frac{\partial}{\partial r_{0}}\Bigl(\frac{\beta}{\alpha}\Bigr)=0

and
\displaystyle{\beta_{f}=\frac{\partial}{\partial\log{\mu}}f}
is its beta function, which we find by solving eq. (

\beta_{f}=-2f[\nu-f(m+\nu)].

5
Conclusions

In this work we first reviewed the defect induced conformal physics of QED
4
in the presence of an infinitely heavy state of charge
q
. We then moved to investigate the dynamics in the presence of Yukawa interactions spurred by the addition of a real scalar field. We observed the emergence of relevant four-fermion operators which trigger an RG flow on the Wilson line. This occurs for a specific range of
e^{2}q
once fixed
y/e
. We discover a new structure of unstable fixed points going beyond the case of ordinary QED
4
. Beyond four-fermion operators the Fermi statistics only allows the possible effect of eight-fermion operators in the smaller range
3/8<\nu<1/2
which will be analyzed elsewhere. Future directions for this work include applying the framework to the study of interacting field theories, such as asymptotically safe theories
[
7
]
. We added a series of appendices in which we report the details of our computations.
