Service of Process; Further Change

AGENCY: Department of State.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This rule provides an additional change in the address for 
service of process for summonses, complaints, or other legal documents 
directed to the Department of State or to any Department employee or 
former employee in connection with Federal or State litigation.

DATES: This rule is effective January 16, 2026.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Alice Kottmyer, Attorney Adviser, 
Office of Management, [email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: For background and Regulatory Analyses, 
please see the final rule at 91 FR 1245 on January 13, 2026. This rule 
provides a further modification to Sec.  172.3.
