5 C.F.R. § 630.505 — Restoration after appeal.
Citation: us:cfr:5:630.505
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-630.505
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 630 (Absence And Leave). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When an employee is restored to an agency as a result of an appeal, the agency shall reestablish his leave account as a credit or charge as it was at the time of separation.
