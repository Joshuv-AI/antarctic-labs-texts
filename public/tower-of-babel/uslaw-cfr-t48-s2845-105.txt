48 C.F.R. § 2845.105 — 2845.105 Contractors' property management system compliance.
Citation: us:cfr:48:2845.105
Creator: Chapter 28—Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-2845.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2845 (Government Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contractor's records for Government-furnished property may be kept as a separate account in the bureau's internal property management system, in which case the contracting officer or formally designated property administrator shall serve as custodian of the account.
