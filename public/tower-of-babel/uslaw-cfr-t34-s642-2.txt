34 C.F.R. § 642.2 — Who are eligible applicants?
Citation: us:cfr:34:642.2
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-642.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 642 (Training Program For Federal Trio Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following are eligible to apply for a grant to carry out a Training Program project:
(a) Institutions of higher education.
(b) Public and private nonprofit agencies and organizations.
