In their intuitionistic epistemic logics, Artemov and Protopopescu

Considering that knowledge is the product of verifications, they read formulas of the form

According to them, the reflection principle
{\square}p{\rightarrow}p

{\square}p{\rightarrow}{\neg}{\neg}p

In their article, among other things, Artemov and Protopopescu prove the completeness of their intuitionistic epistemic logics with respect to their appropriate relational semantics.

Modal logic is the study of two modal operators: a box
\square

The above-mentioned articles being only about the box, it is natural to ask whether a diamond can be added to the intuitionistic epistemic logics put forward by Artemov and Protopopescu.
