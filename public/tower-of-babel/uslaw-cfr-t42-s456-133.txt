42 C.F.R. § 456.133 — Subsequent continued stay review dates.
Citation: us:cfr:42:456.133
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-456.133
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 456 (Utilization Control). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The UR plan must provide that—
(a) The committee assigns subsequent continued stay review dates in accordance with §§ 456.128 and 456.134(a);
(b) The committee assigns a subsequent review date each time it decides under § 456.135 that the continued stay is needed; and
(c) The committee ensures that each continued stay review date it assigns is recorded in the beneficiary's record.
