44 U.S.C. § 1105 — Form and style of work for departments
Citation: us:usc:44:1105
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section1105
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 11 (Executive And Judiciary Printing And Binding). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director of the Government Publishing Office shall determine the form and style in which the printing or binding ordered by a department is executed, and the material and the size of type used, having proper regard to economy, workmanship, and the purposes for which the work is needed.
