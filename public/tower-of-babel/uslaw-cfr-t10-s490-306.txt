10 C.F.R. § 490.306 — Vehicle operation requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:10:490.306
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-490.306
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 490 (Alternative Fuel Transportation Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The alternative fueled vehicles acquired pursuant to section 490.302 of this part shall be operated solely on alternative fuels, except when these vehicles are operating in an area where the appropriate alternative fuel is unavailable.
