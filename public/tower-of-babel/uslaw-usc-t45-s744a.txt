45 U.S.C. § 744a — End of Conrail commuter service obligation
Citation: us:usc:45:744a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2024
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title45-section744a
Context: United States Code, Title 45 (Railroads); Chapter 16 (Regional Rail Reorganization); Subchapter III (Consolidated Rail Corporation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of law or contract, Conrail shall be relieved of any legal obligation to operate commuter service on January 1, 1983.
