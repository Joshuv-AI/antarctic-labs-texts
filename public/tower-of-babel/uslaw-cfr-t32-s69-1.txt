32 C.F.R. § 69.1 — Purpose.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 69 (School Boards For Department Of Defense Domestic Dependent Elementary And Secondary Schools). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part prescribes policies and procedures for the establishment and operation of elected School Boards for schools operated by the Department of Defense (DoD) under 10 U.S.C. 2164, 32 CFR part 345, and Public Law 92-463.
