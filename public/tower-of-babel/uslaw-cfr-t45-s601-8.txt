45 C.F.R. § 601.8 — Access to classified materials.
Citation: us:cfr:45:601.8
Creator: National Science Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-601.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 601 (Classification And Declassification Of National Security Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person may be given access to classified information unless that person has been determined to be trustworthy and unless access is essential to the accomplishment of lawful and authorized Government purposes.
