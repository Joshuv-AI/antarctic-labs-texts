13 U.S.C. § 22 — Qualifications of permanent personnel
Citation: us:usc:13:22
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title13-section22
Context: United States Code, Title 13 (Census); Chapter 1 (Administration); Subchapter II (Officers And Employees). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All permanent officers and employees of the Bureau shall be citizens of the United States.
