36 C.F.R. § 271.6 — Review of licenses.
Citation: us:cfr:36:271.6
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-271.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 271 (Use Of “Smokey Bear” Symbol). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Chief will cooperate with the Association of State Foresters and the Advertising Council, and for this purpose may review with these organizations from time to time the nature and status of licenses granted under these regulations in this part.
