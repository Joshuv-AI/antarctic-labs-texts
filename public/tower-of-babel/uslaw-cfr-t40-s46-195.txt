40 C.F.R. § 46.195 — Project period.
Citation: us:cfr:40:46.195
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-46.195
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 46 (Fellowships). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Based on the “Date Fellow Will Enter on Duty” which you enter on the Activation Notice (see § 46.185(a)), EPA will establish the project period for the fellowship. If you incur costs before the date of the fellowship award, the date on the Activation Notice must reflect that fact (see also § 46.170(b)).
