29 U.S.C. § 14 — Assistant director of bureau; appointment; duties
Citation: us:usc:29:14
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section14
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 2 (Women’S Bureau). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There shall be in the Women’s Bureau an assistant director, to be appointed by the Secretary of Labor, who shall perform such duties as shall be prescribed by the director and approved by the Secretary of Labor.
