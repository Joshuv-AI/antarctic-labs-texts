10 U.S.C. § 12403 — Army and Air National Guard of the United States: members; status in which ordered into Federal service
Citation: us:usc:10:12403
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section12403
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel Generally); Chapter 1211 (National Guard Members In Federal Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Members of the Army National Guard of the United States ordered to active duty shall be ordered to duty as Reserves of the Army. Members of the Air National Guard of the United States ordered to active duty shall be ordered to duty as Reserves of the Air Force.
