10 C.F.R. § 490.603 — Prohibited acts.
Citation: us:cfr:10:490.603
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-490.603
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 490 (Alternative Fuel Transportation Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It is unlawful for any person to violate any provision of sections 501, 503(b), 507, 514 of the Act, or any regulations issued under such sections.
