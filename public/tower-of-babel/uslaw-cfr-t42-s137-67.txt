42 C.F.R. § 137.67 — How may a Self-Governance Tribe use interest earned on statutorily mandated grant funds?
Citation: us:cfr:42:137.67
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-137.67
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 137 (Tribal Self-Governance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Interest earned on such funds must be used to enhance the grant program including allowable administrative costs.
