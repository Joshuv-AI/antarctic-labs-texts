7 U.S.C. § 1736l — Consultation on grain marketing
Citation: us:usc:7:1736l
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1736l
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 41 (Food For Peace); Subchapter IV (General Authorities And Requirements). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Congress encourages the Secretary of Agriculture, in coordination with other appropriate Federal departments and agencies, to continue to consult with representatives of other major grain exporting nations toward the goal of establishing more orderly marketing of grain and achieving higher farm income for producers of grain.
