Maps from knots to 2 2 -links, chord diagrams, and a way to enhance Vassiliev invariants.
Vassily Olegovich Manturov

Abstract
In the present paper, we discuss a way of generalising Vassiliev knot invariants and weight systems to framed chord diagrams having framing 0 0 and 1 1 .



Keywords: knot, link, Vassiliev invariant,
chord diagram, weight system,
parity

AMS MSC: 57M25,57M27,81T18

1
Introduction

It is well known that symbols of the
Vassiliev knot invariants give rise
to
weight systems
, i.e.,
linear functions on chord diagrams
satisfying the 4-term and the 1-term
relation. There is a
framed

counterpart of chord diagrams,
i.e., diagrams having
odd

and
even
chords, with 4T-relations
modified accordingly
[
7
]
.

Framed chord diagrams seem to be
more interesting and richer than
usual one and it would be interesting
to apply them to some knot theories.

In the present paper we address the
question about the geometric meaning
of such relations.

We derive them from the Vassiliev invariants
of knots in
3
-manifolds having
non-trivial first
{\mathbb{Z}}_{2}
-cohomology
and discuss how this techniques
can be appled to classical knots
in
{\mathbb{R}}^{3}
.

Throughout the paper, all knots and links are assumed oriented, unless specified otherwise.

The paper is organised as follows.
In Section 2, we define various linear
spaces and algebras on chord diagrams.
In Section 3, we discuss Vassiliev’s
invariants for knots in various
3-manifolds, in particular,
in complements to knots in
{\mathbb{R}}^{3}
.
Our main result (Theorem
1
)
the symbol of any good Vassiliev invariant considered
as a function on framed chord diagrams
gives rise to a function satisfying the framed
4T
-relation (and
1T
-relation)
where framing comes from the
{\mathbb{Z}}_{2}
-homology
of the ambient manifold. It is
known that in
{\mathbb{R}}^{3}
weight systems satisfying
the 1T-relation give rise to Vassiliev
invariants. This is realised
by the Kontsevich integral.
We address the same question in the framed
case.
We
formulate the program for
further research: how we can use
a 2-cable for a knot
K
to
get framing for chord diagrams
corresponding to a singular knot.

The Kontsevich integral

(1)

In (
