42 C.F.R. § 403.768 — Excluded services.
Citation: us:cfr:42:403.768
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-403.768
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 403 (Special Programs And Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to items and services excluded in § 409.49 of this chapter, items and services are also excluded if they are provided by:
(a) A HHA that is not a RNHCI.
(b) A supplier who is not providing RNHCI designated items under arrangement with a RNHCI.
(c) A nurse who is not providing RNHCI home nursing services under arrangement with a RNHCI.
