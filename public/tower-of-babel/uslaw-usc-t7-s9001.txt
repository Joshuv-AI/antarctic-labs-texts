7 U.S.C. § 9001 — Definition of Secretary of Agriculture
Citation: us:usc:7:9001
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section9001
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 115 (Agricultural Commodity Policy And Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this Act, the term “Secretary” means the Secretary of Agriculture.
