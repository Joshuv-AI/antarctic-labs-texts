29 C.F.R. § 783.8 — “Person”.
Citation: us:cfr:29:783.8
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-783.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 783 (Application Of The Fair Labor Standards Act To Employees Employed As Seamen). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As used in the Act (including definition of “enterprise” set forth below in § 783.9), “person” is defined as meaning “an individual, partnership, association, corporation, business trust, legal representative, or any organized group of persons” (Act, section 3(a)).
