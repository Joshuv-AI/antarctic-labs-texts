    Title: Designation of Work Study Site Supervisors, VA Form 22-
10219a.
    OMB Control Number: 2900-NEW. https://www.reginfo.gov/public/do/PRASearch (Once at this link, you can enter the OMB Control Number to 
find the historical versions of this Information Collection).
    Type of Review: New Collection.
    Abstract: The VA Work Study program allows Veterans and other VA 
beneficiaries who are enrolled at least \3/4\ time (75% Rate of 
Pursuit) in school to earn pay by working part-time jobs related to VA 
services. These jobs are available at locations such as approved 
colleges and training facilities, VA Regional Offices, VA cemeteries, 
and other approved sites. Each approved

[[Page 63673]]

work site is required to designate a Primary and at least one Secondary 
Work Site Supervisor. Similar to designated VA School Certifying 
officials (SCO) at schools and training facilities approved to certify 
training for VA students to receive GI Bill benefits, the designated 
Work Study Site Supervisors are the only individuals authorized to 
provide and receive information pertaining to approved VA Work Study 
students and who have the authority to submit VA Work Study forms on a 
student's behalf.
    VA Form 22-10219, Department of Veterans Affairs Work Study Site 
Application captures this information when a work site initially 
applies for VA approval. The VA Form 22-10219 is not practical for 
collecting subsequent changes to the Work Site Supervisors and would 
require a work site to provide redundant and unneeded information when 
updates to the Work Site Supervisors are needed. Therefore, the VA Work 
Study Team currently provides a locally produced template to capture 
this information when updates are needed to the listed site 
supervisors. When a work site needs to update its Site Supervisor 
information, staff currently must request a template by email and 
manually submit changes to VA, which is an inefficient manual process. 
Additionally, in some instances a Work Site cannot certify Work Study 
Students for payment during this process, and the delay may cause 
financial hardship, making it difficult for students to cover essential 
expenses.
    VA Form 22-10219a, Designation of Work Study Site Supervisors, has 
been patterned after the form used to collect designated VA School 
Certifying Officials, VA Form 22-8794, as the concepts are very 
similar. Incorporation of VA Form 22-10219a, Designation of Work Study 
Site Supervisors, will allow work sites to download the form online and 
submit it to VA, enabling faster communication between work sites and 
VA. This digital process will not only provide quicker processing of 
work study site supervisor designations, reducing payment delays, but 
will streamline record-keeping as well. Additionally, an official form 
is required to lay the groundwork for future automation of the process.
    Affected Public: Individuals, households and educational 
Institutions.
