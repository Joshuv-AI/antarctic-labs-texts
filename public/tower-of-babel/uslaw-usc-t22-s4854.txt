22 U.S.C. § 4854 — Qualifications of persons hired for diplomatic construction program
Citation: us:usc:22:4854
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section4854
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 58 (Diplomatic Security); Subchapter IV (Diplomatic Security Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In carrying out the diplomatic construction program referred to in section 4851(a) of this title, the Secretary of State shall employ as professional staff (by appointment, contract, or otherwise) only those persons with a demonstrated specialized background in the fields of construction, construction law, or contract management. In filling such positions, the Secretary shall actively recruit women and members of minority groups.
