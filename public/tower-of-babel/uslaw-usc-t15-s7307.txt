15 U.S.C. § 7307 — National Construction Safety Team report
Citation: us:usc:15:7307
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section7307
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 99 (National Construction Safety Team). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 90 days after completing an investigation, a Team shall issue a public report which includes—
(1)
an analysis of the likely technical cause or causes of the building failure investigated;
(2)
any technical recommendations for changes to or the establishment of evacuation and emergency response procedures;
(3)
any recommended specific improvements to building standards, codes, and practices; and
(4)
recommendations for research and other appropriate actions needed to help prevent future building failures.
