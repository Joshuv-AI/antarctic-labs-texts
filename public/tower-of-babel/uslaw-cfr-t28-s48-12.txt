28 C.F.R. § 48.12 — Ex parte communications.
Citation: us:cfr:28:48.12
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-48.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 48 (Newspaper Preservation Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person shall communicate on any matter related to these proceedings with the administrative law judge, the Attorney General or anyone having decisional responsibility, except as provided in these regulations.
