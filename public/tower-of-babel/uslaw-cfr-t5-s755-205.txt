5 C.F.R. § 755.205 — Finality of appeal decision.
Citation: us:cfr:5:755.205
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-755.205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 755 (Appeal Procedures For Recoupment Of Awards, Bonuses, Or Relocation Expenses Awarded Or Approved For All Employees Of The Department Of Veterans Affairs (Va)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Pursuant to 38 U.S.C. 723(b)(2), the OPM appeal decision is final; no further administrative review is available within OPM.
