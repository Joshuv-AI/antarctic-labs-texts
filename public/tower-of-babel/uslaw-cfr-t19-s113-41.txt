19 C.F.R. § 113.41 — Entry made prior to production of documents.
Citation: us:cfr:19:113.41
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-113.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 113 (Cbp Bonds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When entry is made prior to the production of a required document, the importer must indicate in the “Missing Documents” box (box 16) on CBP Form 7501, or its electronic equivalent, the missing document, whether the importer gives a bond or stipulates to produce the document.
