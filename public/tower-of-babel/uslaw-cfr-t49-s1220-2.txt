49 C.F.R. § 1220.2 — Protection and storage of records.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1220.2
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1220.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1220 (Preservation Of Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The company shall protect records subject to this part from fires, floods, and other hazards, and safeguard the records from unnecessary exposure to deterioration from excessive humidity, dryness, or lack of ventilation.
(b) The company shall notify the Board if prescribed records are substantially destroyed or damaged before the term of the prescribed retention periods.
