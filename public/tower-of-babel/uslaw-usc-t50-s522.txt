50 U.S.C. § 522 — Acquisition of lands; advance payments for construction
Citation: us:usc:50:522
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section522
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 20 (Wind Tunnels); Subchapter II (Air Engineering Development Center). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

To accomplish the purposes of this subchapter, the Secretary of the Air Force is authorized to acquire lands and rights pertaining thereto, or other interest therein, including the temporary use thereof, by donation, purchase, exchange of Government-owned lands, or otherwise, and construction under this subchapter may be prosecuted without regard to section 3324(a) and (b) of title 31.
