10 U.S.C. § 8780 — Heat and light for Young Men’s Christian Association buildings
Citation: us:usc:10:8780
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8780
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (General Administration); Chapter 877 (Quarters, Utilities, And Services). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Navy may furnish, without charge, heat and light to any building of the Young Men’s Christian Association located at a naval activity.
