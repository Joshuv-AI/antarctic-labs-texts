41 C.F.R. § 301-10.304 — -10.304 Reimbursement for POV parking at common carrier terminal.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-10.304
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-10.304
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-10 (10—Transportation Expenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The agency may reimburse the parking fee as an allowable transportation expense, not exceeding the cost of using one of the following to/from the terminal, as determined by the agency: a taxi, transportation network company (TNC), or innovative mobility technology company.
