22 U.S.C. § 1928d — Auditing and accounting
Citation: us:usc:22:1928d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section1928d
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 24 (Mutual Security Program); Subchapter III (Economic Assistance); Part D (Special Assistance And Other Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The certificate of the Chairman of the House delegation and the Senate delegation of the NATO Parliamentary Assembly shall on and after July 11, 1956, be final and conclusive upon the accounting officers in the auditing of the accounts of the United States Group of the NATO Parliamentary Assembly.
