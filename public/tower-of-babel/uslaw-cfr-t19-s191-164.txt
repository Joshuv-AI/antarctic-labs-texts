19 C.F.R. § 191.164 — Return to Customs custody.
Citation: us:cfr:19:191.164
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-191.164
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 191 (Drawback). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

There is no time limit for the return to Customs custody of distilled spirits, wine, or beer subject to refund of taxes under the provisions of this subpart.
