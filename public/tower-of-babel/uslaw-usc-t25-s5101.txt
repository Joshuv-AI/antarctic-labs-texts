25 U.S.C. § 5101 — Allotment of land on Indian reservations
Citation: us:usc:25:5101
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section5101
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 45 (Protection Of Indians And Conservation Of Resources). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

On and after June 18, 1934, no land of any Indian reservation, created or set apart by treaty or agreement with the Indians, Act of Congress, Executive order, purchase, or otherwise, shall be allotted in severalty to any Indian.
