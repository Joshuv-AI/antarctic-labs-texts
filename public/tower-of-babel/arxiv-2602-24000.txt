Electromagnetic form factors and structure of the T b ​ b T_{bb} tetraquark
Sasa Prelovsek

Abstract
We present the first lattice QCD calculation of electromagnetic form factors of a tetraquark, focusing on the T b ​ b = b ​ b ​ u ¯ ​ d ¯ T_{bb}=bb\bar{u}\bar{d} with quantum numbers I ⁡ ( J P ) = 0 ​ ( 1 + ) I(J^{P})=0(1^{+}) . The electromagnetic current probes the charge monopole, magnetic dipole and the electric quadrupole distributions within the tetraquark. From it, we find evidence that its structure consists of a compact heavy diquark [ b ​ b ] [bb] in spin one, color-antitriplet configuration, and a light antidiquark [ u ¯ ​ d ¯ ] [\bar{u}\bar{d}] in spin zero, color-triplet configuration. The computations were performed on a single CLS ensemble with N f = 2 + 1 N_{f}=2+1 dynamical quarks at a lattice spacing a ≈ 0.064 a\approx 0.064 fm and with a pion mass m π ≈ 290 m_{\pi}\approx 290 MeV.

Studying the composition of hadrons in terms of their elastic electromagnetic (EM) form factors offers the advantage of having a clear physical interpretation. These form factors are encoded in EM matrix elements, defined in the infinite-volume spacetime continuum as

\displaystyle\mathcal{M}_{EM}^{\mu}=\langle h(p_{2},\lambda_{2})|\hat{\jmath}^{\mu}_{EM,cont}(x=0)|h(p_{1},\lambda_{1})\rangle,\hskip 28.45274pt\hat{\jmath}^{\mu}_{EM,cont}\equiv\sum_{q}e_{q}\bar{q}\gamma^{\mu}q,

(1)

where
e_{q}
is the electric charge of quark
q
and
h(p_{1(2)},\lambda_{1(2)})
is the ingoing(outgoing) state
h
with momentum
p_{1(2)}
and helicity
\lambda_{1(2)}
. The vector current and consequently the matrix element (
1

F(Q^{2})=\sum_{q}F_{q}(Q^{2})
and probe the distributions of individual quarks inside a hadron. Lorentz-covariant parametrization of eq. (
1
) for (pseudo)scalars gives a single charge form factor
F_{C}

\displaystyle\mathcal{M}_{EM}^{\mu}=(p_{1}+p_{2})^{\mu}F_{C}(Q^{2}),

(2)

hadrons we get

\displaystyle\mathcal{M}_{EM}^{\mu}=-(

\displaystyle+\frac{(\varepsilon_{2}^{*}\cdot q)(\varepsilon_{1}\cdot q)}{2m^{2}}(p_{1}+p_{2})^{\mu}F_{3}(Q^{2}),

(3)

0&1&0\\[1.0pt]
1&-1&(1+\eta)\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}F_{1}(Q^{2})\\[1.0pt]

5
Summary and conclusions

In this study, the first lattice QCD calculation and analysis of electromagnetic form factors of the
T_{bb}
tetraquark was presented. The simulations were performed on a single CLS ensemble with an unphysical pion mass
m_{\pi}\approx 290
MeV using
b-
quarks implemented with a relativistic heavy quark action tuned to reproduce the physical
B
and
B^{*}
masses and dispersion relations. Following this, we measured the binding energy of the
T_{bb}
to be
m_{T_{bb}}-(m_{B}+m_{B^{*}})=-64(10)
MeV.

Having established the existence of a
T_{bb}
bound state in our lattice setup, we computed its matrix elements containing local heavy and light vector currents, enabling us to determine the separate contributions to the electromagnetic form factors from the light and heavy (anti)quarks. This approach allowed us to extract the charge distributions within the tetraquark, the magnetic dipole moment and the electric quadrupole moment, as well as their flavor decompositions. We found that the available lattice data, combined with the Pauli principle applied to heavy and light (anti)quark pairs is sufficient to completely describe the internal structure of the
T_{bb}
. Rather uniquely, it is composed as a bound state consisting of a compact heavy diquark
[bb]
in a color-antitriplet with spin one, and a light antidiquark
[\bar{u}\bar{d}]
in a color-triplet in spin zero.
