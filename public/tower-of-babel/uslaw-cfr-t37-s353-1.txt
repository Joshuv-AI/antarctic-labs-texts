37 C.F.R. § 353.1 — When granted.
Citation: us:cfr:37:353.1
Creator: Copyright Royalty Board, Library Of Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-353.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 353 (Rehearing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A motion for rehearing may be filed by any participant in the relevant proceeding. The Copyright Royalty Judges may grant rehearing upon a showing that any aspect of the determination may be erroneous.
