9 C.F.R. § 145.7 — Specific provisions for participating dealers.
Citation: us:cfr:9:145.7
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-145.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 145 (National Poultry Improvement Plan For Breeding Poultry). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Dealers in hatching eggs, newly hatched poultry, or started poultry shall comply with the provisions in this part (within the NPIP Program Standards document, Program Standard C applies to hatcheries; alternatives to the program standards may also be approved by the Administrator under § 147.53 of this subchapter).
