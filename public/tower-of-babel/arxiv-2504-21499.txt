On L α L^{\alpha} -flatness of Erdős-Littlewood’s polynomials.
el Houcein el Abdalaoui ⋆

Abstract
. It is shown that Erdős-Littlewood’s polynomials are not L α L^{\alpha} -flat when α > 2 \alpha>2 is an even integer (and hence for any α ≥ 4 \alpha\geq 4 ). This provides a partial solution to an old problem posed by Littlewood. Consequently, we obtain a positive answer to the analogous Erdös-Newman conjecture for polynomials with coefficients ± 1 \pm 1 ; that is, there is no ultraflat sequence of polynomials from the class of Erdős–Littlewood polynomials. Our proof is short and simple. It relies on the classical lemma for L p L^{p} norms of the Dirichlet kernel, the Marcinkiewicz-Zygmund interpolation inequalities, and the p p -concentration theorem due to A. Bonami and S. Révész.
