4 C.F.R. § 2.6 — Veterans' preference.
Citation: us:cfr:4:2.6
Creator: Government Accountability Office
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-4/section-2.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 4 (Accounts), Part 2 (Purpose And General Provision). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) GAO will provide preference, for any individual who would be a preference eligible in the executive branch, in a manner and to an extent consistent with preference eligibles in the executive branch.
(b) Appeals from preference decisions will be heard by the GAO Personnel Appeals Board.
