30 C.F.R. § 285.823 — Will BSEE reimburse me for my expenses related to inspections?
Citation: us:cfr:30:285.823
Creator: Bureau Of Safety And Environmental Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-285.823
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 285 (Renewable Energy And Alternate Uses Of Existing Facilities On The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon request, BSEE will reimburse you for food, quarters, and transportation that you provide for our representatives while they inspect your lease or grant facilities and associated activities. You must send us your reimbursement request within 90 days of the inspection.
