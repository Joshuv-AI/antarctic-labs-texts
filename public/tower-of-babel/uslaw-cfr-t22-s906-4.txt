22 C.F.R. § 906.4 — Hearing panels and members.
Citation: us:cfr:22:906.4
Creator: Foreign Service Grievance Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-906.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 906 (Hearings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless the Board and the parties agree otherwise, all hearings shall be held before a panel of at least three members.
