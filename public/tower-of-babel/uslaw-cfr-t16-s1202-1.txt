16 C.F.R. § 1202.1 — Scope and effective date.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1202.1
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1202.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1202 (Safety Standard For Matchbooks). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Scope. This part 1202, a consumer product safety standard, prescribes the safety requirements, including labeling requirements, for the matchbook. This part 1202 applies to all matchbooks manufactured in or imported into the United States after its effective date.
(b) Effective date. The effective date shall be May 4, 1978.
