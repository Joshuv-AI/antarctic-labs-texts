7 C.F.R. § 613.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:7:613.1
Creator: Natural Resources Conservation Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-613.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 613 (Plant Materials Centers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part provides NRCS policy on the operation of PMCs. The Centers have responsibilities for assembling, testing, releasing, and providing for the commercial production and use of plant materials and plant materials technology for programs of soil, water, and related resource conservation and development.
