15 U.S.C. § 634a — Office of Advocacy within Small Business Administration; Chief Counsel for Advocacy
Citation: us:usc:15:634a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section634a
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 14A (Aid To Small Business). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is established within the Small Business Administration an Office of Advocacy. The management of the Office shall be vested in a Chief Counsel for Advocacy who shall be appointed from civilian life by the President, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate.
