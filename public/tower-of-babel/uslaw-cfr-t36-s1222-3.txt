36 C.F.R. § 1222.3 — What standards are used as guidance for this part?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1222.3
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1222.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1222 (Creation And Maintenance Of Federal Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

These regulations conform with guidance provided in ISO 15489-1:2001, Information and documentation—Records management. Paragraphs 7.1 (Principles of records management programmes), 7.2 (Characteristics of a record), 8.3.5 (Conversion and migration), 8.3.6 (Access, retrieval and use), and 9.6 (Storage and handling) apply to records creation and maintenance.
