33 C.F.R. § 384.5 — What is the Corps of Engineers' obligation with respect to Federal interagency coordination?
Citation: us:cfr:33:384.5
Creator: Corps Of Engineers, Department Of The Army, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-384.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 384 (Intergovernmental Review Of Department Of The Army Corps Of Engineers Programs And Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Responsible Corps officials, to the extent practicable, consult with and seek advice from all other substantially affected Federal departments and agencies in an effort to assure full coordination between such agencies and the Corps of Engineers regarding programs and activities covered under these regulations.
