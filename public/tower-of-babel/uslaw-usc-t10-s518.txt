10 U.S.C. § 518 — Temporary enlistments
Citation: us:usc:10:518
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section518
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 31 (Enlistments). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Temporary enlistments may be made only in the Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, Space Force, or Coast Guard, as the case may be, without specification of component.
