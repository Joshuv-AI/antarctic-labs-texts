54 U.S.C. § 320101 — Declaration of national policy
Citation: us:usc:54:320101
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section320101
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 3201 (Policy And Administrative Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is declared that it is a national policy to preserve for public use historic sites, buildings, and objects of national significance for the inspiration and benefit of the people of the United States.
