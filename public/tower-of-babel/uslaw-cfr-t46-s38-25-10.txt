46 C.F.R. § 38.25-10 — -10 Safety relief valves—TB/ALL.
Citation: us:cfr:46:38.25-10
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-38.25-10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 38 (Liquefied Flammable Gases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The cargo tank safety relief valves shall be inspected at least once in every 2 years.
(b) The safety relief valve discs must be lifted from their seats in the presence of a marine inspector by either liquid, gas, or vapor pressure at least once every 5 years to determine the accuracy of adjustment and, if necessary, must be reset.
