10 U.S.C. § 986 — Policy regarding identification of gender or personal pronouns in official correspondence
Citation: us:usc:10:986
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section986
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 49 (Miscellaneous Prohibitions And Penalties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Defense may not require or prohibit a member of the armed forces or a civilian employee of the Department of Defense to identify the gender or personal pronouns of such member or employee in any official correspondence of the Department.
