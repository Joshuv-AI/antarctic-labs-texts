32 C.F.R. § 553.45 — Eligibility for interment in U.S. Disciplinary Barracks Cemetery at Fort Leavenworth.
Citation: us:cfr:32:553.45
Creator: Department Of The Army
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-553.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 553 (Army Cemeteries). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Military prisoners who die while in Military custody and are not claimed by the person authorized to direct disposition of remains or other persons legally authorized to dispose of remains are permitted to be interred in the U.S. Disciplinary Barracks Cemetery. All decisions for interment in the U.S.D.B. Cemetery will be made by the Executive Director, ANMC.
(b) Other persons approved by the Executive Director.
