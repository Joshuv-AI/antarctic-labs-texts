36 U.S.C. § 306 — National bird

Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part A (Observances And Ceremonies); Chapter 3 (National Anthem, Motto, Floral Emblem, March, Tree, And Bird). The bald eagle (Haliaeetus leucocephalus) is the national bird.
