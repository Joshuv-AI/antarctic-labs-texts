14 C.F.R. § 29.77 — Landing Decision Point (LDP): Category A.
Citation: us:cfr:14:29.77
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-29.77
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 29 (Airworthiness Standards: Transport Category Rotorcraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The LDP is the last point in the approach and landing path from which a balked landing can be accomplished in accordance with § 29.85.
(b) Determination of the LDP must include the pilot recognition time interval following failure of the critical engine.
