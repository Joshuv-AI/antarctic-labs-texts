47 C.F.R. § 27.904 — Geographic partitioning and spectrum disaggregation.
Citation: us:cfr:47:27.904
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-27.904
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 27 (Miscellaneous Wireless Communications Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An entity that acquires a portion of a 1670-1675 MHz band licensee's geographic area or spectrum subject to a geographic partitioning or spectrum disaggregation agreement under § 27.15 must function as a 1670-1675 MHz licensee and is subject to the obligations and restrictions on the 1670-1675 MHz license as set forth in this subpart.
