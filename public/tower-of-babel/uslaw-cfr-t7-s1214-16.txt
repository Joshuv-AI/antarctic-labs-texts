7 C.F.R. § 1214.16 — Produce.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1214.16
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Miscellaneous Commodities), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1214.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1214 (Christmas Tree Promotion, Research, And Information Order). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Produce means to engage in the cutting and selling of Christmas trees for the holiday market.
