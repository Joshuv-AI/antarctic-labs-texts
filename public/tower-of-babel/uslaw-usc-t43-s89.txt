43 U.S.C. § 89 — Monthly returns of district land offices
Citation: us:usc:43:89
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section89
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 4 (District Land Offices). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Interior or such officer as he may designate shall make to the Secretary of the Treasury monthly returns of the moneys received in district land offices, and pay over such money pursuant to his instructions.
