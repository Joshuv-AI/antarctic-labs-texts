7 C.F.R. § 1738.301 — General.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1738 (Rural Broadband Loans, Loan/Grant Combinations, And Loan Guarantees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) To be eligible for a loan guarantee, the Applicant must submit an application that meets the requirements in this part along with the requirements as stated in 7 CFR part 4279, subparts A and B, as well as any additional requirements published in the Federal Register.
(b) The Agency may approve Rural Broadband Program loan guarantees in excess of $10 million but less than $25 million when the project meets one of the priorities in § 1738.105(a)(3)(i).
(c) The lender will service the loan in accordance with 7 CFR part 4287, subpart B.
(d) Any reference to priorities in 7 CFR part 4279 or 4287 shall have the meaning as stated in § 1738.105 and any reference to Administrator or Agency shall have the meaning as defined in § 1738.2.
