40 C.F.R. § 455.30 — Applicability; description of the metallo-organic pesticide chemicals manufacturing subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:455.30
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-455.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 455 (Pesticide Chemicals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to discharges resulting from the manufacture of metallo-organic active ingredients containing mercury, cadmium, arsenic, or copper. The manufacture of intermediates used to manufacture the active ingredients are excluded from coverage by this subpart.
