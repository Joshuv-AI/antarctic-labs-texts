Implementation of the Executive Order Entitled ``Zero-Based 
Regulatory Budgeting To Unleash American Energy''; Correction

AGENCY: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission.

ACTION: Correcting amendment.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (FERC) published a 
direct final rule in the Federal Register of October 21, 2025, revising 
its regulations to insert a conditional sunset date into certain 
regulations in response to Executive Order 14270, ``Zero-Based 
Regulatory Budgeting to Unleash American Energy.'' The document 
contained an error. This document corrects the regulations.

[[Page 5291]]


DATES: This correction is effective February 6, 2026.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Karin Herzfeld, Office of the General 
Counsel, Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, 888 First Street NE, 
Washington, DC 20426, (202) 502-8459, [email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: In FR Doc. 2025-19607 (193 FERC ] 61,002) 
(90 FR 48397, October 21, 2025), FERC added a conditional sunset date 
to Sec.  157.202 at paragraph (b)(2)(ii)(H) in error. FERC is removing 
paragraph (b)(2)(ii)(H).

    Administrative practice and procedure, Natural gas, Reporting and
