43 C.F.R. § 4300.54 — Can BLM increase the size of the land in my permit?
Citation: us:cfr:43:4300.54
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4300.54
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4300 (Grazing Administration; Alaska; Reindeer; General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. BLM may increase the area on its own initiative or by your request if BLM determines that the area is too small for the number of reindeer you are grazing. BLM will give you at least 30 days' notice of this action.
