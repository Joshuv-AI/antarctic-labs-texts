49 C.F.R. § 192.193 — Valve installation in plastic pipe.
Citation: us:cfr:49:192.193
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-192.193
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 192 (Transportation Of Natural And Other Gas By Pipeline: Minimum Federal Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each valve installed in plastic pipe must be designed so as to protect the plastic material against excessive torsional or shearing loads when the valve or shutoff is operated, and from any other secondary stresses that might be exerted through the valve or its enclosure.
