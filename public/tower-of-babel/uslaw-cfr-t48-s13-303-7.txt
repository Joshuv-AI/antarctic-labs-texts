48 C.F.R. § 13.303-7 — 13.303-7 Completion of BPAs.
Citation: us:cfr:48:13.303-7
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-13.303-7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 13 (Simplified Acquisition Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An individual BPA is considered complete when the purchases under it equal its total dollar limitation, if any, or when its stated time period expires.
