12 U.S.C. § 2279a–3 — Capitalization
Citation: us:usc:12:2279a–3
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section2279a–3
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 23 (Farm Credit System); Subchapter VII (Restructuring Of System Institutions); Part A (Merger Of Banks Within A District). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In accordance with section 2154a of this title, each merged bank shall provide, through bylaws and subject to Farm Credit Administration regulations, for the capitalization of the bank and the manner in which bank stock shall be issued, held, transferred, and retired and bank earnings distributed.
