7 C.F.R. § 1901.501 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1901.501
Creator: Rural Housing Service, Rural Business-Cooperative Service, And Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1901.501
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1901 (Program-Related Instructions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes policies and procedures for Rural Development certificates of beneficial ownership and insured notes. This subpart is inapplicable to Farm Service Agency, Farm Loan Programs.
