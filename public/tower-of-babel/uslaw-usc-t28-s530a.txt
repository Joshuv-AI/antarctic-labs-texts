28 U.S.C. § 530A — Authorization of appropriations for travel and related expenses and for health care of personnel serving abroad
Citation: us:usc:28:530A
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section530A
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part II (Department Of Justice); Chapter 31 (The Attorney General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be used from appropriations, for any fiscal year, for the Department of Justice, such sums as may be necessary—
(1)
for travel and related expenses of employees of the Department of Justice serving abroad and their families, to be payable in the same manner as applicable with respect to the Foreign Service under paragraphs (2), (3), (5), (6), (8), (9), (11), and (15) of section 901 of the Foreign Service Act of 1980, and under the regulations issued by the Secretary of State; and
(2)
for health care for such employees and families, to be provided under section 904 of that Act.
