25 C.F.R. § 18.202 — How does a tribe request approval for a tribal probate code amendment?
Citation: us:cfr:25:18.202
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-18.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 18 (Tribal Probate Codes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To amend a tribal probate code, the tribe must follow the same procedures as for submitting a tribal probate code to the Department for approval.
