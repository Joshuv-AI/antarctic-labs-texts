Construction Standards--Advisory Committee on Construction Safety

AGENCY: Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA), Labor.

ACTION: Final rule; Correction

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Occupational Safety and Health Administration is 
correcting an error in the docket number listed in its final rule on 
Construction Standards--Advisory Committee on Construction Safety and 
Health, which was published in the Federal Register on July 1, 2025 (90 
FR 27996).

DATES: Effective July 9, 2025.
