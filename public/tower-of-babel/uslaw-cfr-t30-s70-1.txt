30 C.F.R. § 70.1 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:30:70.1
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-70.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 70 (Mandatory Health Standards—Underground Coal Mines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part 70 sets forth mandatory health standards for each underground coal mine subject to the Federal Mine Safety and Health Act of 1977, as amended.
