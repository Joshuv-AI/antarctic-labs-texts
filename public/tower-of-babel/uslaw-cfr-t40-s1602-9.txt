40 C.F.R. § 1602.9 — Fees.
Citation: us:cfr:40:1602.9
Creator: Chemical Safety And Hazard Investigation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1602.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1602 (Protection Of Privacy And Access To Individual Records Under The Privacy Act Of 1974). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The CSB will charge fees for duplication of records under the Privacy Act in the same way in which it charges duplication fees under the FOIA (see part 1601, subpart D of this chapter). No search or review fee will be charged for any record.
