25 C.F.R. § 61.15 — Special instructions.
Citation: us:cfr:25:61.15
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-61.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 61 (Preparation Of Rolls Of Indians). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To facilitate the work of the Director or Superintendent, the Assistant Secretary may issue special instructions not inconsistent with the regulations in this part 61.
