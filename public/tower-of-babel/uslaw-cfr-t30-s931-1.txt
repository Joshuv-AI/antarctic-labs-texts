30 C.F.R. § 931.1 — Scope.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 931 (New Mexico). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part contains all rules applicable only within New Mexico that have been adopted under the Surface Mining Control and Reclamation Act of 1977.
