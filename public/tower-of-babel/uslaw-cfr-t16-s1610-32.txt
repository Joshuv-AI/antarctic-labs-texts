16 C.F.R. § 1610.32 — General requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1610.32
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1610.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1610 (Standard For The Flammability Of Clothing Textiles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No article of wearing apparel or fabric subject to the Act and regulations shall be marketed or handled if such article or fabric, when tested according to the procedures prescribed in section 4(a) of the Act (16 CFR 1609), is so highly flammable as to be dangerous when worn by individuals.
