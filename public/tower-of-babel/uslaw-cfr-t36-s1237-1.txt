36 C.F.R. § 1237.1 — What is the applicability and scope of this part?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1237.1
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1237.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1237 (Audiovisual, Cartographic, And Related Records Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies must manage audiovisual, cartographic, and related records in accordance with parts 1220-1235. This part prescribes additional policies and procedures for managing audiovisual, cartographic, and related records to ensure adequate and proper documentation and authorized, timely, and appropriate disposition.
