25 U.S.C. § 3745 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:25:3745
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section3745
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 39 (American Indian Agricultural Resource Management); Subchapter III (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) General authorization
There are authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary to carry out the purposes of this chapter.
(b) Funding source
The activities required under subchapter II may only be funded from appropriations made pursuant to this chapter. To the greatest extent possible, such activities shall be coordinated with activities funded from other sources.
