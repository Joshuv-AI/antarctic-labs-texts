12 C.F.R. § 1806.100 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1806.100
Creator: Community Development Financial Institutions Fund, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1806.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1806 (Bank Enterprise Award Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of the Bank Enterprise Award (BEA) Program is to provide grants to Insured Depository Institutions that provide financial and technical assistance to Community Development Financial Institutions and increase their activities in Distressed Communities.
