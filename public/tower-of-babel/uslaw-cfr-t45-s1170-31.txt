45 C.F.R. § 1170.31 — Discrimination prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1170.31
Creator: National Foundation On The Arts And The Humanities
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1170.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1170 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Handicap In Federally Assisted Programs Or Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No qualified handicapped person shall, because recipient's facilities are inaccessible to or unusable by handicapped persons, be denied the benefits of, be excluded from participation in, or otherwise be subjected to discrimination under any program or activity to which this part applies.
