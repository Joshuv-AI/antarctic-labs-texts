7 C.F.R. § 869.103 — Amendments to license.
Citation: us:cfr:7:869.103
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Federal Grain Inspection Service, Fair Trade Practices Program), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-869.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 869 (Regulations For The United States Warehouse Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

AMS will issue an amended license upon:
(a) Receipt of forms prescribed and furnished by AMS outlining the requested changes to the license;
(b) Payment of applicable licensing and examination fees;
(c) Receipt of bonding or other financial assurance if required in the applicable licensing agreement; and
(d) Receipt of a report on the examination of the proposed facilities pending inclusion or exclusion, if determined necessary by AMS.
