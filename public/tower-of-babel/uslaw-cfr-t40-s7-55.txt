40 C.F.R. § 7.55 — Separate or different aid, benefits, or services.
Citation: us:cfr:40:7.55
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-7.55
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 7 (Nondiscrimination In Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Assistance From The Environmental Protection Agency). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Recipients shall not deny a qualified handicapped person an opportunity equal to that afforded others to participate in or benefit from the aid, benefit, or service in the program or activity receiving EPA assistance. Recipients shall administer programs or activities in the most integrated setting appropriate to the needs of qualified handicapped persons.
