36 C.F.R. § 1254.44 — How long may I use a microfilm reader?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1254.44
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1254.44
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1254 (Using Records And Donated Historical Materials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Use of the microfilm readers in the National Archives Building is on a first-come-first served basis.
(b) Archival operations directors at our regional archives may permit reservations for use of microfilm readers and set time limits on use to meet local circumstances.
