16 U.S.C. § 7933 — State authority for fish and wildlife
Citation: us:usc:16:7933
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section7933
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 98 (Sportsmen’S Access To Federal Land); Subchapter III (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this title— 1
(1)
authorizes the Secretary of Agriculture or the Secretary to require Federal licenses or permits to hunt and fish on Federal land; or
(2)
enlarges or diminishes the responsibility or authority of States with respect to fish and wildlife management.
