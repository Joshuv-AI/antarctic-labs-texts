42 U.S.C. § 10263 — Functions
Citation: us:usc:42:10263
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section10263
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 108 (Nuclear Waste Policy); Subchapter V (Nuclear Waste Technical Review Board). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Board shall evaluate the technical and scientific validity of activities undertaken by the Secretary after December 22, 1987, including—
(1)
site characterization activities; and
(2)
activities relating to the packaging or transportation of high-level radioactive waste or spent nuclear fuel.
