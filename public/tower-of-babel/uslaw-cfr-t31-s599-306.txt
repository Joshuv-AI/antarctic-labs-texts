31 C.F.R. § 599.306 — Foreign person.
Citation: us:cfr:31:599.306
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-599.306
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 599 (Illicit Drug Trade Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term foreign person means any citizen or national of a foreign state (including any such individual who is also a citizen or national of the United States, provided such individual does not reside in the United States) or any entity not organized under the laws of the United States.
