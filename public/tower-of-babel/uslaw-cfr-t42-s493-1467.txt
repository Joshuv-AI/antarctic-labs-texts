42 C.F.R. § 493.1467 — Condition: Laboratories performing high complexity testing; cytology general supervisor.
Citation: us:cfr:42:493.1467
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-493.1467
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 493 (Laboratory Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For the subspecialty of cytology, the laboratory must have a general supervisor who meets the qualification requirements of § 493.1469 of this subpart, and provides supervision in accordance with § 493.1471 of this subpart.
