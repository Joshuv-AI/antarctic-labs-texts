36 C.F.R. § 13.936 — What is prohibited?
Citation: us:cfr:36:13.936
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-13.936
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 13 (National Park System Units In Alaska). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) No one may operate a motor vehicle on the restricted section of the Park road without a valid permit.
(b) No one may use a motor home, camper or trailer to transport guests to a lodge or other business in Kantishna.
(c) No one may transfer or accept transfer of a Denali Park road permit without the superintendent's approval.
