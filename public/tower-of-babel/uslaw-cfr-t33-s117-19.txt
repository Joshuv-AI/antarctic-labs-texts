33 C.F.R. § 117.19 — Signalling when two or more vessels are approaching a drawbridge.
Citation: us:cfr:33:117.19
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-117.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 117 (Drawbridge Operation Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When two or more vessels are approaching the same drawbridge at the same time, or nearly the same, time, whether from the same or opposite directions, each vessel shall signal independently for the opening of the draw and the drawtender shall reply in turn to the signal of each vessel. The drawtender need not reply to signals by vessels accumulated at the bridge for passage during a scheduled open period.
