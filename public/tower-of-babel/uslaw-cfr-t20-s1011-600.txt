20 C.F.R. § 1011.600 — What are the record retention requirements for the HIRE Vets Medallion Award?
Citation: us:cfr:20:1011.600
Creator: Office Of The Assistant Secretary For Veterans' Employment And Training Service, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-1011.600
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 1011 (Hire Vets Medallion Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Applicants must retain a record of all information used to support an application for the HIRE Vets Medallion Award for 2 years from the date of application.
