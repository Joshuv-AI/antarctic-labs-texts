46 C.F.R. § 201.9 — Suspension, amendment, etc., of rules.
Citation: us:cfr:46:201.9
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-201.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 201 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part may, from time to time, be suspended, amended, or revoked, in whole or in part. Notice of any such action will be published in the Federal Register. Also, any regulation in this part may be waived by the Administration or the Presiding Officer to prevent undue hardship in any particular case.
