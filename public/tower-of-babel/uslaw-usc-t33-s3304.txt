33 U.S.C. § 3304 — Limitations on statutory construction
Citation: us:usc:33:3304
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section3304
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 46 (National Levee Safety Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed as—
(1)
creating any liability of the United States or its officers or employees for the recovery of damages caused by an action or failure to act; or
(2)
relieving an owner or operator of a levee of a legal duty, obligation, or liability incident to the ownership or operation of a levee.
