41 C.F.R. § 102-42.35 — -42.35 Reimbursement.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-42.35
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-42.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-42 (42—Utilization, Donation, And Disposal Of Foreign Gifts And Decorations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All transfers of gifts and decorations to Federal agencies or donations through SASPs are conducted without reimbursement for the property itself. However, the employing agency may require the receiving agency to reimburse all or a portion of the direct costs incurred by the employing agency for packing, preparation for shipment, loading, and transportation.
