45 C.F.R. § 17.3 — Precautions to be taken.
Citation: us:cfr:45:17.3
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-17.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 17 (Release Of Adverse Information To News Media). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The issuing organization shall take reasonable precautions to assure that information released is accurate and that its release fulfills an authorized purpose.
