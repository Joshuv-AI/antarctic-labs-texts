28 C.F.R. § 506.1 — What is the purpose of individual inmate commissary accounts?
Citation: us:cfr:28:506.1
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-506.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 506 (Inmate Commissary Account). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of individual inmate commissary accounts is to allow the Bureau to maintain inmates' monies while they are incarcerated. Family, friends, or other sources may deposit funds into these accounts.
