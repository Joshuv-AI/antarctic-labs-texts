14 C.F.R. § 142.45 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:14:142.45
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-142.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 142 (Training Centers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes the personnel and flight training equipment requirements for a certificate holder that is training to meet the requirements of part 61 of this chapter.
