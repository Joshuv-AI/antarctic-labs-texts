12 C.F.R. § 1272.6 — Examinations.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1272.6
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1272.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1272 (New Business Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this part shall limit in any manner the right of FHFA to conduct any examination of any Bank relating to its implementation of an NBA, including pre- or post-implementation safety and soundness examinations, or review of contracts or other agreements between the Bank and any other party.
