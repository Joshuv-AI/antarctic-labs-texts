42 C.F.R. § 402.17 — Settlement.
Citation: us:cfr:42:402.17
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-402.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 402 (Civil Money Penalties, Assessments, And Exclusions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

CMS or OIG has exclusive authority to settle any issues or case, without the consent of the ALJ or the Secretary, at any time before a final decision by the Secretary. Thereafter, the General Counsel has the exclusive authority.
