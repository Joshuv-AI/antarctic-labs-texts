7 C.F.R. § 631.14 — Contract violations.
Citation: us:cfr:7:631.14
Creator: Natural Resources Conservation Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-631.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 631 (Great Plains Conservation Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Contract violations, determinations and appeals will be handled in accordance with the terms of the contract and attachments thereto. Violations involving fraud are to be handled in accordance with current USDA regulations.
