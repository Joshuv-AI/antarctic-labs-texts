48 C.F.R. § 9.409 — 9.409 Contract clause.
Citation: us:cfr:48:9.409
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-9.409
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 9 (Contractor Qualifications). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.209-6, Protecting the Government's Interest when Subcontracting with Contractors Debarred, Suspended, Proposed for Debarment, or Voluntarily Excluded, in solicitations and contracts where the contract value exceeds $45,000.
