49 C.F.R. § 1106.5 — Waiver.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1106.5
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1106 (Procedures For Surface Transportation Board Consideration Of Safety Integration Plans In Cases Involving Railroad Consolidations, Mergers, And Acquisitions Of Control). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The SIP requirements established by this part may be waived or modified by the Board where a railroad shows that relief is warranted or appropriate.
