50 U.S.C. § 2463 — Sharing of technology with Department of Defense
Citation: us:usc:50:2463
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section2463
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 41 (National Nuclear Security Administration); Subchapter V (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator shall, in cooperation with the Secretary of Defense, establish procedures and programs to provide for the sharing of technology, technical capability, and expertise between the Administration and the Department of Defense to further national security objectives.
