7 U.S.C. § 361i — Power to amend, repeal, etc., reserved
Citation: us:usc:7:361i
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section361i
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 14 (Agricultural Experiment Stations); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Congress may at any time, amend, suspend, or repeal any or all of the provisions of sections 361a to 361i of this title.
