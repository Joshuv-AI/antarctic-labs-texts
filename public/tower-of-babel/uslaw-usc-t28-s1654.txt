28 U.S.C. § 1654 — Appearance personally or by counsel
Citation: us:usc:28:1654
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section1654
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part V (Procedure); Chapter 111 (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In all courts of the United States the parties may plead and conduct their own cases personally or by counsel as, by the rules of such courts, respectively, are permitted to manage and conduct causes therein.
