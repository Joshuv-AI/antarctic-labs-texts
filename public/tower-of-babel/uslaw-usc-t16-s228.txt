16 U.S.C. § 228 — Buildings on privately owned lands
Citation: us:usc:16:228
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section228
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter XXIV (Grand Canyon National Park). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Where privately owned lands within the said park lie within three hundred feet of the rim of the Grand Canyon no building, tent, fence, or other structure shall be erected on the park lands lying between said privately owned lands and the rim.
