46 U.S.C. § 105 — Consular officer
Citation: us:usc:46:105
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section105
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 1 (Definitions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this title, the term “consular officer” means an officer or employee of the United States Government designated under regulations to issue visas.
