12 C.F.R. § 1209.21 — Conduct of examination.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1209.21
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1209.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1209 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this part limits or constrains in any manner any duty, authority, or right of FHFA to conduct or to continue any examination, investigation, inspection, or visitation of any regulated entity or entity-affiliated party.
