40 C.F.R. § 370.60 — How does a person obtain SDS information about a specific facility?
Citation: us:cfr:40:370.60
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-370.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 370 (Hazardous Chemical Reporting: Community Right-To-Know). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person may obtain an SDS for a specific facility by writing to the LEPC and asking for it.
(a) If the LEPC has the SDS it must provide it to the person making the request.
(b) If the LEPC does not have the SDS it must request it from the facility's owner or operator.
