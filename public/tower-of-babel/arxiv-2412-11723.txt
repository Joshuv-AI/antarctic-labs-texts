Constant case of the Grothendieck–Serre conjecture in mixed characteristic
I.Panin, A.Stavrova

Abstract
Let D D be a DVR of mixed characteristic. Let 𝐆 \mathbf{G} be a reductive D D -group scheme. Then the Grothendieck–Serre conjecture is true for the D D -group scheme 𝐆 \mathbf{G} and any geometrically regular local D D -algebra R R . Also we prove a version of Lindel–Ojanguren–Gabber’s geometric presentation lemma in the DVR context.
