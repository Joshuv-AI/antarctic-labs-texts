10 U.S.C. § 10142 — Ready Reserve
Citation: us:usc:10:10142
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section10142
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization And Administration); Chapter 1005 (Elements Of Reserve Components). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Ready Reserve consists of units or Reserves, or both, liable for active duty as provided in sections 12301 and 12302 of this title.
(b)
The authorized strength of the Ready Reserve is 2,900,000.
