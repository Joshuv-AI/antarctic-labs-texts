5 U.S.C. § 902 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:5:902
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section902
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part I (The Agencies Generally); Chapter 9 (Executive Reorganization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purpose of this chapter—
(1)
“agency” means—
(A)
an Executive agency or part thereof; and
(B)
an office or officer in the executive branch;
but does not include the Government Accountability Office or the Comptroller General of the United States;
(2)
“reorganization” means a transfer, consolidation, coordination, authorization, or abolition, referred to in section 903 of this title; and
(3)
“officer” is not limited by section 2104 of this title.
