7 C.F.R. § 985.67 — Agents.
Citation: us:cfr:7:985.67
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-985.67
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 985 (Marketing Order Regulating The Handling Of Spearmint Oil Produced In The Far West). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary may, by designation in writing, name any officer or employee of the United States or name any agency or division in the U.S. Department of Agriculture, to act as the Secretary's agent or representative in connection with any of the provisions of this part.
