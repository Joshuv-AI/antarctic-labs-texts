46 C.F.R. § 13.305 — Proof of service for Tank Barge-PIC.
Citation: us:cfr:46:13.305
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-13.305
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 13 (Certification Of Tank Vessel Personnel). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Provide evidence in a method prescribed by the Coast Guard of proof of service from the owner or operator of a terminal; the owner or operator of a tank barge; the owner, operator, or Master of a tank vessel; or the employer of shore-based PICs. The evidence must contain the information required by § 13.127(a), excluding paragraph (a)(4)(vii).
