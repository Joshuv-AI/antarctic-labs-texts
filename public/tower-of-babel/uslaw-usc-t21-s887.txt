21 U.S.C. § 887 — Coordination and consolidation of post-seizure administration
Citation: us:usc:21:887
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title21-section887
Context: United States Code, Title 21 (Food And Drugs); Chapter 13 (Drug Abuse Prevention And Control); Subchapter I (Control And Enforcement); Part E (Administrative And Enforcement Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Attorney General and the Secretary of the Treasury shall take such action as may be necessary to develop and maintain a joint plan to coordinate and consolidate post-seizure administration of property seized under this subchapter, subchapter II, or provisions of the customs laws relating to controlled substances.
