10 C.F.R. § 960.3-2-2-1 — -2-2-1 Evaluation of all potentially acceptable sites.
Citation: us:cfr:10:960.3-2-2-1
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-960.3-2-2-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 960 (General Guidelines For The Preliminary Screening Of Potential Sites For A Nuclear Waste Repository). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

First, in considering sites for nomination, each of the potentially acceptable sites shall be evaluated on the basis of the disqualifying conditions specified in the technical guidelines of subparts C and D, in accordance with the application requirements set forth in appendix III of this part. This evaluation shall support a finding by the DOE that such sites is not disqualified.
