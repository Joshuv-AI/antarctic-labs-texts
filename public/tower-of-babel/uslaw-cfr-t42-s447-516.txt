42 C.F.R. § 447.516 — Upper limits for drugs furnished as part of services.
Citation: us:cfr:42:447.516
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-447.516
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 447 (Payments For Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The upper limits for payment for prescribed drugs in this subpart also apply to payment for drugs provided as part of skilled nursing facility services and intermediate care facility services and under prepaid capitation arrangements.
