42 U.S.C. § 263a–6 — Fees
Citation: us:usc:42:263a–6
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section263a–6
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter II (General Powers And Duties); Part F (Licensing Of Biological Products And Clinical Laboratories); Subpart 2 (Clinical Laboratories). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may require the payment of fees for the purpose of, and in an amount sufficient to cover the cost of, administering sections 263a–1 to 263a–7 of this title. A State operating a program under section 263a–2 of this title may require the payment of fees for the purpose of, and in an amount sufficient to cover the costs of, administering its program.
