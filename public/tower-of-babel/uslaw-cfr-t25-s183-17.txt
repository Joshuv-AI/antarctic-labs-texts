25 C.F.R. § 183.17 — If expenditures under this part lead to a claim or cause of action, who is liable?
Citation: us:cfr:25:183.17
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-183.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 183 (Use And Distribution Of The San Carlos Apache Tribe Development Trust Fund And San Carlos Apache Tribe Lease Fund). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Tribe may be liable. The United States must not be liable for any claim or cause of action arising from the Tribe's use or expenditure of monies distributed from the Trust Fund or the Lease Fund.
