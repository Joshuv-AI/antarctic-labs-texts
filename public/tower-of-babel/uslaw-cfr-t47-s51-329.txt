47 C.F.R. § 51.329 — Notice of network changes: Methods for providing notice.
Citation: us:cfr:47:51.329
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-51.329
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 51 (Interconnection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An incumbent LEC may provide the required notice to the public of network changes through publicly accessible industry fora, industry publications, or the incumbent LEC's website.
(b) Until the planned change is implemented, an incumbent LEC must keep the notice available for public inspection, and amend the notice to keep the information complete, accurate and up-to-date.
