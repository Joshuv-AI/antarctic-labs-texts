36 C.F.R. § 1280.4 — Children under the age of 14.
Citation: us:cfr:36:1280.4
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1280.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1280 (Use Of Nara Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We admit children under the age of 14 to NARA facilities only if they are accompanied by an adult who will supervise them at all times. The director of a NARA facility may authorize a lower age limit for unaccompanied children to meet special circumstances (e.g., students who have been given permission to conduct research without adult supervision).
