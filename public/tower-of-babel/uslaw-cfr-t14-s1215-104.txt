14 C.F.R. § 1215.104 — Apportionment and assignment of services.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1215.104
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1215.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1215 (Tracking And Data Relay Satellite System (Tdrss)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No user may apportion, assign, or otherwise convey to any third party its TDRSS service. Each user may obtain service only through contractual agreement with the Associate Administrator for Space Operations.
