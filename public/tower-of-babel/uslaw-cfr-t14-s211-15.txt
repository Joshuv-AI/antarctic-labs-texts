14 C.F.R. § 211.15 — Statements of fact.
Citation: us:cfr:14:211.15
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-211.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 211 (Applications For Permits To Foreign Air Carriers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The applicant shall include only significant and relevant facts in an application. Each application shall contain adequate information with respect to the evidence required in subpart C of this part. The application may contain other information and data the applicant considers necessary to explain particular circumstances.
