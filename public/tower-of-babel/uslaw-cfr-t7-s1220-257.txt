7 C.F.R. § 1220.257 — OMB control numbers.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1220.257
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Miscellaneous Commodities), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1220.257
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1220 (Soybean Promotion, Research, And Consumer Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The control number assigned to the information collection requirements by the Office of Management and Budget pursuant to the Paperwork Reduction Act, Public Law 96-511, is OMB number 0581-0093, except Board member nominee information sheets are assigned OMB number 0505-0001.
