16 C.F.R. § 1052.2 — Notice of opportunity for oral presentation.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1052.2
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1052.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1052 (Procedural Regulations For Informal Oral Presentations In Proceedings Before The Consumer Product Safety Commission). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission will publish in the Federal Register notice of opportunity for an oral presentation in each instance. The notice shall be sufficiently in advance of the oral presentation to allow interested persons to participate. If the oral presentation involves a proposed rule, the notice of opportunity may be in the notice proposing the rule or in a later, separate Federal Register notice.
