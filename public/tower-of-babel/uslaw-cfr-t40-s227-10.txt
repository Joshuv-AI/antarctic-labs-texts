40 C.F.R. § 227.10 — Hazards to fishing, navigation, shorelines or beaches.
Citation: us:cfr:40:227.10
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-227.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 227 (Criteria For The Evaluation Of Permit Applications For Ocean Dumping Of Materials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Wastes which may present a serious obstacle to fishing or navigation may be dumped only at disposal sites and under conditions which will insure no unacceptable interference with fishing or navigation.
(b) Wastes which may present a hazard to shorelines or beaches may be dumped only at sites and under conditions which will insure no unacceptable danger to shorelines or beaches.
