14 C.F.R. § 460.65 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:14:460.65
Creator: Commercial Space Transportation, Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-460.65
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 460 (Human Space Flight Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to:
(a) An applicant for a license or permit under this chapter who proposes to have a government astronaut without a safety-critical role on board a vehicle.
(b) An operator licensed or permitted under this chapter who has a government astronaut without a safety-critical role on board a vehicle.
