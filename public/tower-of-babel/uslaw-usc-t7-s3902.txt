7 U.S.C. § 3902 — Research program on cancer in animals and birds
Citation: us:usc:7:3902
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section3902
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 70 (Animal Cancer Research). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Agriculture shall conduct a program of basic research on cancer in animals and birds at appropriate facilities within the Department of Agriculture or by grants to other qualified research facilities.
