15 U.S.C. § 6005 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:15:6005
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section6005
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 86 (Children’S Bicycle Helmet Safety). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration to carry out the grant program authorized by this chapter, there are authorized to be appropriated $2,000,000 for fiscal year 1995, $3,000,000 for fiscal year 1996, and $4,000,000 for fiscal year 1997.
