48 U.S.C. § 914 — Issuance of bonds and obligations
Citation: us:usc:48:914
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section914
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 4 (Puerto Rico); Subchapter VII (Low Rent Housing Projects And Elimination Of Sub-Standard Housing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The legislature may authorize such authorities to issue bonds or other obligations with such security as the legislature may provide and may provide for the disposition of the proceeds of such bonds and all receipts and revenues of such authorities.
