2.1.
General properties of the scheme
\mathscr{F}_{E}

For all
[L]\in\Pic^{0}(C)

Using a standard argument, we define a scheme structure on

Proposition 3

Let

Proof.

Let

and

Set

Looking at the projection

Proposition 4

Let

Remark 12

(Nonreduced Brill–Noether loci)
.

On any smooth projective curve
C
of genus at least
2
, one can construct stable vector bundles
E
of rank
2
and degree
2g-1

for which the scheme
B_{1}(E)
has a nonreduced component (of codimension
2
in
\Pic^{0}(C)
). The scheme
\mathscr{F}_{E}
is then singular in codimension
2
.
