22 C.F.R. § 213.5 — Subdivision of claims not authorized.
Citation: us:cfr:22:213.5
Creator: Agency For International Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-213.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 213 (Claims Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

USAID will not subdivide a claim to avoid the $100,000 limit on the Agency's authority to compromise a claim, suspend collection action on a claim, or terminate collection action on a claim. A debtor's liability that arises from a particular transaction or contract is a single claim.
