19 C.F.R. § 132.4 — Quota quantity entry limits.
Citation: us:cfr:19:132.4
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-132.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 132 (Quotas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

At the opening of the quota no importer shall be permitted to present entries or withdrawals for consumption of quota-class merchandise for a quantity in excess of the quantity admissible under the applicable quota.
