14 C.F.R. § 121.251 — Engine accessory section diaphragm.
Citation: us:cfr:14:121.251
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-121.251
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 121 (Operating Requirements: Domestic, Flag, And Supplemental Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless equivalent protection can be shown by other means, a diaphragm that complies with § 121.247 must be provided on air-cooled engines to isolate the engine power section and all parts of the exhaust system from the engine accessory compartment.
