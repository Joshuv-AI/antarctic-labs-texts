39 U.S.C. § 501 — Establishment
Citation: us:usc:39:501
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title39-section501
Context: United States Code, Title 39 (Postal Service); Part I (General); Chapter 5 (Postal Regulatory Commission). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Postal Regulatory Commission is an independent establishment of the executive branch of the Government of the United States.
