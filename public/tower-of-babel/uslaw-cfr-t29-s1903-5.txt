29 C.F.R. § 1903.5 — Entry not a waiver.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1903.5
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1903.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1903 (Inspections, Citations And Proposed Penalties). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any permission to enter, inspect, review records, or question any person, shal not imply or be conditioned upon a waiver of any cause of action, citation, or penalty under the Act. Compliance Safety and Health Officers are not authorized to grant any such waiver.
