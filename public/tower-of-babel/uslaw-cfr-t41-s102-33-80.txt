41 C.F.R. § 102-33.80 — -33.80 Management responsibilities when hiring CAS.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-33.80
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-33.80
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-33 (33—Management Of Government Aircraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When hiring CAS, you must:
(a) Establish agency-specific Flight Program Standards as applicable and require compliance with these standards in your contracts and agreements;
(b) Account for the cost of your aircraft and services hired as CAS;
(c) Account for the use of your aircraft hired as CAS; and
(d) Report the cost and usage data for your CAS hires.
