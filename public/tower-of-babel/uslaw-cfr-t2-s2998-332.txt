2 C.F.R. § 2998.332 — What requirements must I pass down to persons at lower tiers with whom I intend to do business?
Citation: us:cfr:2:2998.332
Creator: Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-2998.332
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 2998 (Nonprocurement Debarment And Suspension). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You, as a participant, must include a term or condition in lower-tier transactions requiring lower-tier participants to comply with subpart C of the OMB guidance in 2 CFR part 180, as supplemented by this subpart.
