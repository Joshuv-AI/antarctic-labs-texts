47 C.F.R. § 22.721 — Geographic area authorizations.
Citation: us:cfr:47:22.721
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-22.721
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 22 (Public Mobile Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Eligible persons may apply for a paging geographic area authorization in the Rural Radiotelephone Service, on the channel pairs listed in § 22.725, by following the procedures and requirements set forth in § 22.503 for paging geographic area authorizations.
