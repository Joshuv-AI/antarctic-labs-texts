16 U.S.C. § 2444 — Severability
Citation: us:usc:16:2444
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section2444
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 44A (Antarctic Marine Living Resources Convention). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If any provision of this chapter or the application of this chapter to any person or circumstance is held invalid, neither the remainder of this chapter nor the application of that provision to other persons or circumstances shall be affected thereby.
