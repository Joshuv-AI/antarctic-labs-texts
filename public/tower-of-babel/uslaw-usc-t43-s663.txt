43 U.S.C. § 663 — Restriction of sites to inclusion of necessary lands
Citation: us:usc:43:663
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section663
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 15 (Appropriation Of Waters; Reservoir Sites). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Reservoir sites located or selected and to be located and selected shall be restricted to and shall contain only so much land as is actually necessary for the construction and maintenance of reservoirs; excluding so far as practicable lands occupied by actual settlers at the date of the location of said reservoirs.
