30 C.F.R. § 570.2 — Application of this part.
Citation: us:cfr:30:570.2
Creator: Bureau Of Ocean Energy Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-570.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 570 (Nondiscrimination In The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to any contract or subcontract entered into by a lessee or by a contractor or subcontractor of a lessee after the effective date of these regulations to provide goods, services, facilities, or property in an amount of $10,000 or more in connection with any activity related to the exploration for or development and production of oil, gas, or other minerals or materials in the OCS under the Act.
