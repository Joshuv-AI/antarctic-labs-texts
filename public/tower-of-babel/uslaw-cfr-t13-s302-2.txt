13 C.F.R. § 302.2 — Procedures in disaster areas.
Citation: us:cfr:13:302.2
Creator: Economic Development Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-302.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 302 (General Terms And Conditions For Investment Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When non-statutory EDA administrative or procedural conditions for Investment Assistance awards under PWEDA cannot be met by an Eligible Applicant as the result of a disaster, EDA may waive such conditions.
