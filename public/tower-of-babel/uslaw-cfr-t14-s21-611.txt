14 C.F.R. § 21.611 — Issuance.
Citation: us:cfr:14:21.611
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-21.611
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 21 (Certification Procedures For Products And Articles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the FAA finds that the applicant complies with the requirements of this subchapter, the FAA issues a TSO authorization to the applicant (including all TSO deviations granted to the applicant).
