42 U.S.C. § 1594d — Appropriations; use of quarters allowances
Citation: us:usc:42:1594d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1594d
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 9 (Housing Of Persons Engaged In National Defense); Subchapter XI (Housing For Military Personnel). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
There are authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary to carry out the provisions of sections 1594, 1594a, 1594b, and 1594c of this title.
(b)
Any funds heretofore or hereafter authorized to be expended by any of the military departments or the Coast Guard for the payment of allowances for quarters for military personnel may be used for the purposes specified in subsection (a) above.
