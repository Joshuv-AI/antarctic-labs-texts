11 C.F.R. § 9002.10 — Presidential election.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 11 (Federal Elections), Part 9002 (Definitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Presidential election means the election of Presidential and Vice Presidential electors.
