5 C.F.R. § 892.303 — Can I pay my premiums directly by check under the premium conversion plan?
Citation: us:cfr:5:892.303
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-892.303
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 892 (Federal Flexible Benefits Plan: Pre-Tax Payment Of Health Benefits Premiums). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No, your employer must take your contribution to your FEHB premium from your salary to qualify for pre-tax treatment.
