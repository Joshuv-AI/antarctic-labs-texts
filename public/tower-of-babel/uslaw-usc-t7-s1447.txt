7 U.S.C. § 1447 — Price support levels for other nonbasic agricultural commodities
Citation: us:usc:7:1447
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1447
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 35A (Price Support Of Agricultural Commodities); Subchapter III (Nonbasic Agricultural Commodities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized to make available through loans, purchases, or other operations price support to producers for any nonbasic agricultural commodity not designated in sections 1446, 1446a, and 1446d 1 of this title at a level not in excess of 90 per centum of the parity price for the commodity.
