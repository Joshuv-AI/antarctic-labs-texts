14 C.F.R. § 107.47 — Flight restrictions in the proximity of certain areas designated by notice to airmen.
Citation: us:cfr:14:107.47
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-107.47
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 107 (Small Unmanned Aircraft Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A person acting as a remote pilot in command must comply with the provisions of §§ 91.137 through 91.145 and 99.7 of this chapter.
