20 C.F.R. § 61.402 — Protection, release, inspection and copying of records.
Citation: us:cfr:20:61.402
Creator: Office Of Workers' Compensation Programs, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-61.402
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 61 (Claims For Compensation Under The War Hazards Compensation Act, As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The protection, release, inspection and copying of the records shall be accomplished in accordance with the rules, guidelines and provisions contained in 29 CFR parts 70 and 70a and the annual notice of systems of records and routine uses as published in the Federal Register.
