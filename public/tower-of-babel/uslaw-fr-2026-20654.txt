SUMMARY: The Institute of Museum and Library Services (IMLS), as part 
of its continuing effort to reduce paperwork and respondent burden, 
conducts a pre-clearance consultation program to provide the general 
public and Federal agencies with an opportunity to comment on proposed 
and/or continuing collections of information in accordance with the 
Paperwork Reduction Act. This program helps to ensure that requested 
data can be provided in the desired format, reporting burden (time and 
financial resources) is minimized, collection instruments are clearly 
understood, and the impact of collection requirements on respondents 
can be properly assessed. The purpose of this notice is to communicate 
the agency's intent to request clearance for a new IMLS grant program 
(the final name is to be determined) designed to support small history 
museums, small historical societies, and small historic houses/sites 
that celebrate and preserve America's history and values, with an 
emphasis on amplifying the stories of their local communities. A copy 
of the proposed information collection request can be obtained by 
contacting the individual listed below in the ADDRESSES section of this 
notice.

I. Background

    IMLS is the primary source of Federal support for the Nation's 
libraries and museums. The agency advances, supports, and empowers 
America's museums, libraries, and related organizations through grant 
making, research, and policy development. For more information about 
IMLS, visit www.imls.gov.

II. Current Actions

    This action will seek clearance for a Notice of Funding Opportunity 
for an IMLS Grants for Local History program for three years. This 
grant program is being offered as a special initiative with funding 
from the Museums for America program.
    The IMLS Grants for Local History program supports eligible small 
history museums, small historical societies, and small historic houses/
sites in celebrating and preserving America's history and values, with 
an emphasis on amplifying the stories of their local communities.
    The goal of the program is to amplify the stories of local 
communities through interpretation of their history or conservation of 
objects related to their history and to celebrate their connections to 
the broader American story. This goal is met through the objectives of 
(1) serving the learning needs and interests of the community and (2) 
improving the stewardship of collections in small history museums, 
small historical societies, and small historic houses/sites.
    Interpretation could happen through, for example, oral histories, 
genealogical research, lecture series, publications, living-history 
demonstrations, and special events such as reenactments or 
commemorations that relate local stories to wide-ranging American 
history.
    Collections stewardship could happen through conservation treatment 
and rehousing of local history collections (for example, textiles, 
artwork, documents, archaeological material, military artifacts) that 
are connected to the broader American story.
