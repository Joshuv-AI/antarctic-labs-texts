33 C.F.R. § 167.101 — In the approaches to Narragansett Bay, RI, and Buzzards Bay, MA: Precautionary areas.
Citation: us:cfr:33:167.101
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-167.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 167 (Offshore Traffic Separation Schemes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A precautionary area is established with a radius of 5.4 miles and is centered upon geographical position 41°06.00′ N, 71°23.30′ W.
(b) A precautionary area is established with a radius of 3.55 miles and is centered upon geographical position 41°25.60′ N, 71°23.30′ W.
