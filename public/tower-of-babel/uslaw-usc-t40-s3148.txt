40 U.S.C. § 3148 — Application of this subchapter to certain contracts
Citation: us:usc:40:3148
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section3148
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Part A (General); Chapter 31 (General); Subchapter IV (Wage Rate Requirements). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This subchapter applies to a contract authorized by law that is made without regard to section 6101(b) to (d) of title 41, or on a cost-plus-a-fixed-fee basis or otherwise without advertising for proposals, if this subchapter otherwise would apply to the contract.
