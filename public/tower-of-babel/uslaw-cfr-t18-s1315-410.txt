18 C.F.R. § 1315.410 — Enforcement.
Citation: us:cfr:18:1315.410
Creator: Tennessee Valley Authority
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-1315.410
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 1315 (New Restrictions On Lobbying). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The head of each agency shall take such actions as are necessary to ensure that the provisions herein are vigorously implemented and enforced in that agency.
