14 C.F.R. § 25.303 — Factor of safety.
Citation: us:cfr:14:25.303
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-25.303
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 25 (Airworthiness Standards: Transport Category Airplanes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless otherwise specified, a factor of safety of 1.5 must be applied to the prescribed limit load which are considered external loads on the structure. When a loading condition is prescribed in terms of ultimate loads, a factor of safety need not be applied unless otherwise specified.
