48 C.F.R. § 2104.7001 — 2104.7001 Designation of authorized personnel.
Citation: us:cfr:48:2104.7001
Creator: Chapter 21—Office Of Personnel Management, Federal Employees Group Life Insurance Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-2104.7001
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2104 (Administrative Matters). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contractor shall notify the contracting officer in writing of the name(s), title(s), and address(es) of the individual(s) authorized to act on behalf of the contractor regarding a LIFAR Program contract. The notice shall include any restriction(s) upon the authority of the individual(s). Any change to the notice must also be provided to the contracting officer in writing.
