20 C.F.R. § 688.610 — What are the recordkeeping requirements for YouthBuild programs?
Citation: us:cfr:20:688.610
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-688.610
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 688 (Provisions Governing The Youthbuild Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Grantees must follow the recordkeeping requirements specified in the Uniform Administrative Requirements, at 29 CFR 95.53 and 97.42, as appropriate.
(b) Grantees must maintain such additional records related to the use of buildings constructed or rehabilitated with YouthBuild funds as specified in the grant agreement or in the Department's guidance.
