28 U.S.C. § 1874 — Actions on bonds and specialties
Citation: us:usc:28:1874
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section1874
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part V (Procedure); Chapter 121 (Juries; Trial By Jury). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In all actions to recover the forfeiture annexed to any articles of agreement, covenant, bond, or other specialty, wherein the forfeiture, breach, or nonperformance appears by default or confession of the defendant, the court shall render judgment for the plaintiff for such amount as is due. If the sum is uncertain, it shall, upon request of either party, be assessed by a jury.
