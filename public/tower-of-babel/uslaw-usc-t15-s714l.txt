15 U.S.C. § 714l — Interest of Members of Congress
Citation: us:usc:15:714l
Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 15 (Economic Recovery); Subchapter II (Commodity Credit Corporation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of section 6306(a) of title 41 shall apply to all contracts or agreements of the Corporation, except contracts or agreements of a kind which the Corporation may enter into with farmers participating in a program of the Corporation.
