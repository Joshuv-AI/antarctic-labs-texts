10 U.S.C. § 4653 — Prohibition on use of funds to relieve economic dislocations
Citation: us:usc:10:4653
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section4653
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part V (Acquisition); Subpart H (Contract Management); Chapter 363 (Prohibition And Penalties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
In order to help avoid the uneconomic use of Department of Defense funds in the procurement of goods and services, the Congress finds that it is necessary to prohibit the use of such funds for certain purposes.
(b)
No funds appropriated to or for the use of the Department of Defense may be used to pay, in connection with any contract awarded by the Department of Defense, a price differential for the purpose of relieving economic dislocations.
