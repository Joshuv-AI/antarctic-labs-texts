48 U.S.C. § 1952 — Fiscal procedures assistance
Citation: us:usc:48:1952
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section1952
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 18 (Micronesia, Marshall Islands, And Palau); Subchapter II (Palau); Part B (Implementation Of Compact). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Upon request of the Government of Palau, the Secretary of the Interior shall provide assistance to the Government of Palau to develop and promulgate regulations for the effective expenditure of funds received pursuant to this joint resolution, Public Laws 99–658 [48 U.S.C. 1931 et seq.] and 99–239 [48 U.S.C. 1901 et seq., 2001 et seq.], or any other Act of Congress.
