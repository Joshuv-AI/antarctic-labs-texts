33 U.S.C. § 2709 — Contribution
Citation: us:usc:33:2709
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section2709
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 40 (Oil Pollution); Subchapter I (Oil Pollution Liability And Compensation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A person may bring a civil action for contribution against any other person who is liable or potentially liable under this Act or another law. The action shall be brought in accordance with section 2717 of this title.
