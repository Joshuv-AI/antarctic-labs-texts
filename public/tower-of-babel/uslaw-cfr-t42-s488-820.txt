42 C.F.R. § 488.820 — Available sanctions.
Citation: us:cfr:42:488.820
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-488.820
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 488 (Survey, Certification, And Enforcement Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to termination of the provider agreement, the following alternative sanctions are available:
(a) Civil money penalties.
(b) Suspension of payment for all new admissions.
(c) Temporary management of the HHA.
(d) Directed plan of correction, as set out at § 488.850.
(e) Directed in-service training, as set out at § 488.855.
