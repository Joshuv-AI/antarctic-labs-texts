49 C.F.R. § 555.15 — Time period for exemptions.
Citation: us:cfr:49:555.15
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-555.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 555 (Temporary Exemption From Motor Vehicle Safety And Bumper Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Subject to § 555.16, each temporary exemption granted by the Administrator under this subpart shall be in effect for a period of three years from the effective date. The Administrator shall identify each exemption by a unique number.
