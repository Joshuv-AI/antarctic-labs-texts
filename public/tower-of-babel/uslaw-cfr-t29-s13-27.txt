29 C.F.R. § 13.27 — Timing of pay.
Citation: us:cfr:29:13.27
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-13.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 13 (Establishing Paid Sick Leave For Federal Contractors). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contractor shall compensate an employee for time during which the employee used paid sick leave no later than one pay period following the end of the regular pay period in which the paid sick leave was used.
