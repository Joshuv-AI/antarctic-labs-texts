12 C.F.R. § 1074.3 — Implementation of the Statement Clarifying the Role of Supervisory Guidance.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1074.3
Creator: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1074.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1074 (Rulemaking And Guidance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Statement describes the official policy of the Bureau with respect to the use of supervisory guidance in the supervisory process. The Statement is binding on the Bureau.
