16 C.F.R. § 682.4 — Relation to other laws.
Citation: us:cfr:16:682.4
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-682.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 682 (Disposal Of Consumer Report Information And Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in the rule in this part shall be construed:
(a) To require a person to maintain or destroy any record pertaining to a consumer that is not imposed under other law; or
(b) To alter or affect any requirement imposed under any other provision of law to maintain or destroy such a record.
