44 U.S.C. § 2120 — Online access of founding fathers documents
Citation: us:usc:44:2120
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section2120
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 21 (National Archives And Records Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Archivist may enter into a cooperative agreement to provide online access to the published volumes of the papers of—
(1)
George Washington;
(2)
Alexander Hamilton;
(3)
Thomas Jefferson;
(4)
Benjamin Franklin;
(5)
John Adams;
(6)
James Madison; and
(7)
other prominent historical figures, as determined appropriate by the Archivist of the United States.
