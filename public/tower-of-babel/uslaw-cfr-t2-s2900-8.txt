2 C.F.R. § 2900.8 — Cost sharing or matching.
Citation: us:cfr:2:2900.8
Creator: Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-2900.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 2900 (Uniform Administrative Requirements, Cost Principles, And Audit Requirements For Federal Awards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to the guidance set forth in 2 CFR 200.306(b), for Federal awards from the Department of Labor, the non-Federal entity accounts for funds used for cost sharing or match within their accounting systems as the funds are expended.
