28 U.S.C. § 3005 — Application of chapter to judgments
Citation: us:usc:28:3005
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section3005
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part VI (Particular Proceedings); Chapter 176 (Federal Debt Collection Procedure); Subchapter A (Definitions And General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This chapter shall not apply with respect to a judgment on a debt if such judgment is entered more than 10 years before the effective date of this chapter.
