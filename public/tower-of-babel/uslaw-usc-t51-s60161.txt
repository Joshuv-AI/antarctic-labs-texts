51 U.S.C. § 60161 — Prohibition
Citation: us:usc:51:60161
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section60161
Context: United States Code, Title 51 (National And Commercial Space Programs); Chapter 601 (Land Remote Sensing Policy); Subchapter VI (Prohibition Of Commercialization Of Weather Satellites). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Neither the President nor any other official of the Government shall make any effort to lease, sell, or transfer to the private sector, or commercialize, any portion of the weather satellite systems operated by the Department of Commerce or any successor agency.
