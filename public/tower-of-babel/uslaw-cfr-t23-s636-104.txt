23 C.F.R. § 636.104 — Does this part apply to all Federal-aid design-build projects?
Citation: us:cfr:23:636.104
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-636.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 636 (Design-Build Contracting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this part apply to all Federal-aid design-build projects within the highway right-of-way or linked to a Federal-aid highway project (i.e., the project would not exist without another Federal-aid highway project). Projects that are not located within the highway right-of-way, and not linked to a Federal-aid highway project may utilize State-approved procedures.
