48 C.F.R. § 1419.810 — 1419.810 SBA appeals.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1419.810
Creator: Chapter 14—Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1419.810
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1419 (Small Business Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Assistant Secretary of Policy Management and Budget, without the power of redelegation, is authorized to issue the decision on an SBA appeal of a Contracting Officer's Section 8(a) decision.
