49 C.F.R. § 570.52 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:49:570.52
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-570.52
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 570 (Vehicle In Use Inspection Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to establish criteria for the inspection of motor vehicles through State inspection programs, in order to reduce deaths and injuries attributable to failure or inadequate performance of the motor vehicle systems covered by this part.
