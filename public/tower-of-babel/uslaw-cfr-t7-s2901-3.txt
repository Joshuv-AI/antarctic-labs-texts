7 C.F.R. § 2901.3 — Oral presentation.
Citation: us:cfr:7:2901.3
Creator: Office Of Energy Policy And New Uses, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-2901.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 2901 (Administrative Procedures For Adjustments Of Natural Gas Curtailment Priority). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person seeking an adjustment under this part 2901 shall be given an opportunity to make an oral presentation of data, views and arguments in support of the request for an adjustment, provided that a request to make an oral presentation is submitted in writing with the request for the adjustment. An official of the Department of Agriculture shall preside at such oral presentation.
