15 U.S.C. § 9711 — Gap analysis on availability of snow-related data to assess and predict flood and flood impacts
Citation: us:usc:15:9711
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section9711
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 121 (Flood Level Observation, Operations, And Decision Support). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
The Administrator, in consultation with the Department of Agriculture, the Department of the Interior, and the Army Corps of Engineers, shall conduct an analysis of gaps in the availability of snow-related data to assess and predict floods and flood impacts, including data on the following:
(1)
Snow water equivalent.
(2)
Snow depth.
(3)
Snowpack temperature.
(4)
Snow and mixed-phase precipitation.
(5)
Snow melt.
(6)
Rain-snow line.
(b) Report
Not later than 180 days after December 27, 2022, the Administrator shall submit to the Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation of the Senate and the Committee on Science, Space, and Technology of the House of Representatives a report on—
(1)
the findings of the gap analysis required by subsection (a); and
(2)
opportunities for additional collaboration among Federal agencies to collect snow-related data to better assess and predict floods and flood impacts.
