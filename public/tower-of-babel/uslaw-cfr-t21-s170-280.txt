21 C.F.R. § 170.280 — Submission of a supplement.
Citation: us:cfr:21:170.280
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-170.280
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 170 (Food Additives). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If circumstances warrant, you may submit a supplement to a filed GRAS notice after we respond to your notice by letter in accordance with § 170.265(b)(1) or cease to evaluate your notice in accordance with § 170.265(b)(3).
