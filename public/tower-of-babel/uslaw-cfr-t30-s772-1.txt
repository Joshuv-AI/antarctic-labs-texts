30 C.F.R. § 772.1 — Scope and purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:30:772.1
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-772.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 772 (Requirements For Coal Exploration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part establishes the requirements and procedures applicable to coal exploration operations on all lands except for Federal lands subject to the requirements of 43 CFR parts 3480-3487.
