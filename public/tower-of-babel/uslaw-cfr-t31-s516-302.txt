31 C.F.R. § 516.302 — Effective date.
Citation: us:cfr:31:516.302
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-516.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 516 (Cuba Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term effective date refers to the effective date of the applicable prohibitions and directives contained in this part, and, with respect to a person whose property and interests in property are blocked pursuant to § 516.201, the earlier of the date of actual or constructive notice that such person's property and interests in property are blocked.
