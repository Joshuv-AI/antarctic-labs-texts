26 U.S.C. § 7484 — Change of incumbent in office
Citation: us:usc:26:7484
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section7484
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 76 (Judicial Proceedings); Subchapter D (Court Review Of Tax Court Decisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When the incumbent of the office of Secretary changes, no substitution of the name of his successor shall be required in proceedings pending before any appellate court reviewing the action of the Tax Court.
