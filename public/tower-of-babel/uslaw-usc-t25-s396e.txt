25 U.S.C. § 396e — Officials authorized to approve leases
Citation: us:usc:25:396e
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section396e
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 12 (Lease, Sale, Or Surrender Of Allotted Or Unallotted Lands). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Interior may, in his discretion, authorize superintendents or other officials in the Indian Service to approve leases for oil, gas, or other mining purposes covering any restricted Indian lands, tribal or allotted.
