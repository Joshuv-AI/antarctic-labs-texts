29 C.F.R. § 35.35 — Prohibition against intimidation or retaliation.
Citation: us:cfr:29:35.35
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-35.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 35 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Age In Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance From The Department Of Labor). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A recipient may not engage in acts of intimidation or retaliation against any person who:
(a) Attempts to assert a right protected by the Act or this part; or
(b) Cooperates in any mediation, investigation, hearing or other part of CRC's investigation, conciliation, and enforcement process.
