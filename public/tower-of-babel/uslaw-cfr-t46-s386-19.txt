46 C.F.R. § 386.19 — Dogs and other animals.
Citation: us:cfr:46:386.19
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-386.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 386 (Regulations Governing Public Buildings And Grounds At The United States Merchant Marine Academy). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Persons are prohibited from bringing dogs and other animals on to the Academy premises, except for authorized purposes and except for seeing eye or other guide dogs, or pets approved in writing by the Superintendent or a designee of the Superintendent.
