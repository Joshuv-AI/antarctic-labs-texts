22 U.S.C. § 2294 — Authorizations of appropriations for Development Fund for Africa
Citation: us:usc:22:2294
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section2294
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 32 (Foreign Assistance); Subchapter I (International Development); Part X (Development Fund For Africa). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Funds appropriated to carry out this part are authorized to be made available until expended. It is the sense of the Congress that the authority of this subsection 1 should be used to extend the period of availability of those funds whenever appropriate to improve the quality of assistance provided under section 2293 of this title.
