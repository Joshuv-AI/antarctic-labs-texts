44 U.S.C. § 2907 — Records centers and centralized microfilming or digitization services
Citation: us:usc:44:2907
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section2907
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 29 (Records Management By The Archivist Of The United States And By The Administrator Of General Services). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Archivist may establish, maintain, and operate records centers and centralized microfilming or digitization services for Federal agencies.
