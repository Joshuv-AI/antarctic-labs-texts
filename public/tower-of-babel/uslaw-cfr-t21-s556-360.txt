21 C.F.R. § 556.360 — Lincomycin.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 556 (Tolerances For Residues Of New Animal Drugs In Food). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Acceptable daily intake (ADI). The ADI for total residue of lincomycin is 25 µg/kg of body weight per day.
(b) Tolerances. The tolerances for lincomycin are:
(1) Chickens. Edible tissues (excluding eggs): Not required.
(2) Swine. (i) Liver: 0.6 ppm.
(ii) Muscle: 0.1 ppm.
(3) Honey. 750 ppb.
(c) Related conditions of use. See §§ 520.1263b, 522.1260, and 558.325 of this chapter.
