7 C.F.R. § 22.303 — Cooperation with Federal Regional Councils.
Citation: us:cfr:7:22.303
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-22.303
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 22 (Rural Development Coordination). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

States are urged to establish and maintain close and cooperative relationships with the Federal Regional Councils which will be in a position to assist the States and multicounty jurisdictions in the identification and application of available resources. States may authorize direct communications and liaison between the Regional Councils and multicounty jurisdictions within States.
