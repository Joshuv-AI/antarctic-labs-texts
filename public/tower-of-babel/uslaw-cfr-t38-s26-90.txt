38 C.F.R. § 26.90 — Purpose of this subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:38:26.90
Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-26.90
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 26 (National Environmental Policy Act Implementing Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart provides the VA procedures for complying with NEPA during emergencies, how VA will handle sensitive or classified information, and how VA will consider the international effects of VA actions.
