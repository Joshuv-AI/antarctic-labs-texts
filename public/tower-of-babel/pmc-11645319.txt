New Properties of Holomorphic Sobolev–Hardy Spaces
Authors: William Gryc, Loredana Lanzani, Jue Xiong, Yuan Zhang
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
AbstractWe give new characterizations of the optimal data space for the Lp(bD,σ)-Neumann boundary value problem for the ∂¯ operator associated to a bounded, Lipschitz domain D⊂C. We show that the solution space is embedded (as a Banach space) in the Dirichlet space and that for p=2, the solution space is a reproducing kernel Hilbert space.Keywords: Lipschitz domain, Sobolev–Hardy space, Neumann problem, ∂¯, Reproducing kernel Hilbert space

A bounded domain D⊂C with boundary bD is called a Lipschitz domain if there are finitely many rectangles {Rj}j=1m with sides parallel to the coordinate axes, angles {θj}j=1m, and Lipschitz functions ϕj:R→R such that the collection {e-iθjRj}j=1m covers bD and (eiθjD)∩Rj={x+iy:y>ϕj(x),x∈(aj,bj)} for some aj<bj<∞.
