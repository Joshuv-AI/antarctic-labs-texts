43 U.S.C. § 894 — Adjustment of land grants to railroads
Citation: us:usc:43:894
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section894
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 21 (Grants In Aid Of Railroads And Wagon Roads). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Interior is authorized and directed as of March 3, 1887, to adjust, in accordance with the decisions of the Supreme Court, each of the railroad land grants made by Congress to aid in the construction of railroads and theretofore unadjusted.
