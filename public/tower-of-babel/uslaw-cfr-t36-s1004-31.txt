36 C.F.R. § 1004.31 — Hitchhiking.
Citation: us:cfr:36:1004.31
Creator: Presidio Trust
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1004.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1004 (Vehicles And Traffic Safety). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Hitchhiking or soliciting transportation is prohibited except in designated areas and under conditions established by the Board.
