10 C.F.R. § 460.101 — Climate zones.

Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-460.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 460 (Energy Conservation Standards For Manufactured Homes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Manufactured homes subject to the requirements of this subpart must comply with the requirements applicable to one or more of the climate zones set forth in figure 1 to § 460.101 and table 1 to § 460.101.
