7 U.S.C. § 2705 — Findings and issuance of orders
Citation: us:usc:7:2705
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2705
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 60 (Egg Research And Consumer Information). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

After notice and opportunity for hearing as provided in section 2704 of this title, the Secretary shall issue an order if he finds, and sets forth in such order, upon the evidence introduced at such hearing, that the issuance of such order and all the terms and conditions thereof will tend to effectuate the declared policy of this chapter.
