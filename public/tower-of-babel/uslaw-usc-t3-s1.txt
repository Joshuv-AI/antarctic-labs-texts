3 U.S.C. § 1 — Time of appointing electors
Citation: us:usc:3:1
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title3-section1
Context: United States Code, Title 3 (The President); Chapter 1 (Presidential Elections And Vacancies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The electors of President and Vice President shall be appointed, in each State, on election day, in accordance with the laws of the State enacted prior to election day.
