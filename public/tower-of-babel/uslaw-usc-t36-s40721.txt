36 U.S.C. § 40721 — Responsibility of corporation

Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 407 (Corporation For The Promotion Of Rifle Practice And Firearms Safety); Subchapter II (Civilian Marksmanship Program).
