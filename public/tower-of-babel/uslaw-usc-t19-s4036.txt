19 U.S.C. § 4036 — Regulations
Citation: us:usc:19:4036
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section4036
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 26 (Dominican Republic-Central America Free Trade); Subchapter II (Customs Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Treasury shall prescribe such regulations as may be necessary to carry out—
(1)
subsections (a) through (n) of section 4033 of this title;
(2)
the amendment made by section 204; 1 and
(3)
any proclamation issued under section 4033(o) of this title.
