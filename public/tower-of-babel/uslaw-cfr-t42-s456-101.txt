42 C.F.R. § 456.101 — UR plan required for inpatient hospital services.
Citation: us:cfr:42:456.101
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-456.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 456 (Utilization Control). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A State plan must provide that each hospital furnishing inpatient services under the plan has in effect a written UR plan that provides for review of each beneficiary's need for the services that the hospital furnishes him.
(b) Each written hospital UR plan must meet the requirements under §§ 456.101 through 456.145.
