7 C.F.R. § 57.965 — Returned U.S. inspected and marked products; not importations.
Citation: us:cfr:7:57.965
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-57.965
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 57 (Inspection Of Eggs (Egg Products Inspection Act)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Products that have been inspected by the Department and so marked, and which are returned from foreign countries are not importations within the meaning of this part. Such returned shipments shall be reported to the Administrator by letter.
