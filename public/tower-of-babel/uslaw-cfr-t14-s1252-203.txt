14 C.F.R. § 1252.203 — Special benefits for children and the elderly.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1252.203
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1252.203
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1252 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Age In Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a recipient operating a program or activity provides special benefits to the elderly or to children, such use of age distinctions shall be presumed to be necessary to the normal operation of the program or activity, notwithstanding the provision of § 1252.10.
