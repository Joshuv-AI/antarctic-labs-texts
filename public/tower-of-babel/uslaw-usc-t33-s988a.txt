33 U.S.C. § 988a — Waiver of collection of charges or tolls
Citation: us:usc:33:988a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section988a
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 19 (Saint Lawrence Seaway). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Notwithstanding section 988 of this title or any other provision of law, the Corporation shall not collect any charge or toll established pursuant to section 988 of this title with respect to a commercial vessel (as defined in section 4462(a)(4) of title 26).
(b)
The Corporation will maintain a record of the annual amount of each charge or toll that would have been collected with respect to each such commercial vessel if it were not for paragraph (a) of this section.
