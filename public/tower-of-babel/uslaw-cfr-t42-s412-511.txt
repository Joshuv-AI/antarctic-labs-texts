42 C.F.R. § 412.511 — Reporting and recordkeeping requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:42:412.511
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-412.511
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 412 (Prospective Payment Systems For Inpatient Hospital Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A long-term care hospital participating in the prospective payment system under this subpart must meet the requirement of §§ 412.22(e)(3) and 412.22(h)(6) to report co-located status, if applicable, and the recordkeeping and cost reporting requirements of §§ 413.20 and 413.24 of this subchapter.
