5 C.F.R. § 6301.101 — General.

Creator: Department Of Education
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 6301 (Supplemental Standards Of Ethical Conduct For Employees Of The Department Of Education). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In accordance with 5 CFR 2635.105, the regulations in this part apply to employees of the Department of Education and supplement the Standards of Ethical Conduct for Employees of the Executive Branch contained in 5 CFR part 2635.
