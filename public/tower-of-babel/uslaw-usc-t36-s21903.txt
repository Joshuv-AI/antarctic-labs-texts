36 U.S.C. § 21903 — Powers
Citation: us:usc:36:21903
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section21903
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 219 (The American National Theater And Academy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation may—
(1)
adopt a constitution, bylaws, and regulations;
(2)
adopt and alter a corporate seal;
(3)
establish and maintain offices and buildings to conduct its activities;
(4)
establish State and territorial organizations and local branches;
(5)
acquire, own, lease, encumber, and transfer property as necessary to carry out the purposes of the corporation, subject to its constitution and instructions of donors;
(6)
sue and be sued; and
(7)
do any other act necessary and proper to carry out the purposes of the corporation.
