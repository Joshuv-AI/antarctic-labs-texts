7 C.F.R. § 789.55 — Compliance conflicts.
Citation: us:cfr:7:789.55
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-789.55
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 789 (Agriculture Priorities And Allocations System (Apas)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If compliance with any provision of the Defense Production Act and other applicable laws, this part, or an official action would prevent a person from filling a rated order or from complying with another provision of the Defense Production Act and other applicable laws, this part, or an official action, the person must immediately notify USDA for resolution of the conflict.
