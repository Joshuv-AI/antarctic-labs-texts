30 C.F.R. § 1219.416 — When will ONRR disburse funds to Gulf producing States and coastal political subdivisions?
Citation: us:cfr:30:1219.416
Creator: Office Of Natural Resources Revenue, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-1219.416
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 1219 (Distribution And Disbursement Of Royalties, Rentals, And Bonuses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

ONRR will disburse GOMESA revenues as soon as authorized and practicable within the fiscal year following the year that we collect qualified OCS revenues (Phase I).
