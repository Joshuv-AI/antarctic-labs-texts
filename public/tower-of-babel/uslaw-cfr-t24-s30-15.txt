24 C.F.R. § 30.15 — Application of other remedies.
Citation: us:cfr:24:30.15
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-30.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 30 (Civil Money Penalties: Certain Prohibited Conduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A civil money penalty may be imposed in addition to other administrative sanctions or any other civil remedy or criminal penalty.
