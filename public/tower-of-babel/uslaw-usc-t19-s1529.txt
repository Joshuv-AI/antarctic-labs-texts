19 U.S.C. § 1529 — Collection of fees on behalf of other agencies
Citation: us:usc:19:1529
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section1529
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 4 (Tariff Act Of 1930); Part III (Ascertainment, Collection, And Recovery Of Duties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Customs Service shall be reimbursed from the fees collected for the cost and expense, administrative and otherwise, incurred in collecting any fees on behalf of any government 1 agency for any reason.
