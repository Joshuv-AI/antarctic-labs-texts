25 C.F.R. § 309.21 — What are examples of dolls and toys that are Indian products?
Citation: us:cfr:25:309.21
Creator: Indian Arts And Crafts Board, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-309.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 309 (Protection Of Indian Arts And Crafts Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Dolls, toys, and related items made by an Indian, including, but not limited to, no face dolls, corn husk dolls, patchwork and palmetto dolls, reindeer horn dolls, lacrosse sticks, stick game articles, gambling sticks, gaming dice, miniature cradle boards, and yo-yos, are Indian products.
