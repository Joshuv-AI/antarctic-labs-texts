25 U.S.C. § 1660h — Health information technology
Citation: us:usc:25:1660h
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section1660h
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 18 (Indian Health Care); Subchapter IV (Health Services For Urban Indians). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary, acting through the Service, may make grants to urban Indian organizations under this subchapter for the development, adoption, and implementation of health information technology (as defined in section 300jj of title 42), telemedicine services development, and related infrastructure.
