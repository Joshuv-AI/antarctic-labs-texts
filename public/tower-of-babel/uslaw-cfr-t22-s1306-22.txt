22 C.F.R. § 1306.22 — Discharge.
Citation: us:cfr:22:1306.22
Creator: Millennium Challenge Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-1306.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 1306 (Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Once a debt has been closed out for accounting purposes and collection has been terminated, the debt is discharged. MCC must report discharged debt as income to the debtor to the Internal Revenue Service per 26 U.S.C. 6050P and 26 CFR 1.6050P-1.
