46 U.S.C. § 11112 — Master’s lien for wages
Citation: us:usc:46:11112
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section11112
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part G (Merchant Seamen Protection And Relief); Chapter 111 (Protection And Relief). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The master of a documented vessel has the same lien against the vessel for the master’s wages and the same priority as any other seaman serving on the vessel.
