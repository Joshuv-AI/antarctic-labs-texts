10 C.F.R. § 140.18 — Special provisions applicable to licensees furnishing financial protection in whole or in part in the form of adequate resources.
Citation: us:cfr:10:140.18
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-140.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 140 (Financial Protection Requirements And Indemnity Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In any case where a licensee undertakes to maintain financial protection in the form specified in § 140.14(a)(2) for all or part of the financial protection required by this part, the Commission may require such licensee to file with the Commission such financial information as the Commission determines to be appropriate for the purpose of determining whether the licensee is maintaining financial protection as required by this part.
