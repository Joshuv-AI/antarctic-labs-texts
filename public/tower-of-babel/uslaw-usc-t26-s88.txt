26 U.S.C. § 88 — Certain amounts with respect to nuclear decommissioning costs
Citation: us:usc:26:88
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section88
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 1 (Normal Taxes And Surtaxes); Subchapter B (Computation Of Taxable Income); Part II (Items Specifically Included In Gross Income). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In the case of any taxpayer who is required to include the amount of any nuclear decommissioning costs in the taxpayer’s cost of service for ratemaking purposes, there shall be includible in the gross income of such taxpayer the amount so included for any taxable year.
