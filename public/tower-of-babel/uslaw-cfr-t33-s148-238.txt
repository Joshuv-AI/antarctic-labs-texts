33 C.F.R. § 148.238 — Who are the parties to a formal hearing?
Citation: us:cfr:33:148.238
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-148.238
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 148 (Deepwater Ports: General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The parties to a formal hearing are:
(a) The applicant;
(b) The Commandant (CG-5P); and
(c) Any person intervening in the proceedings.
