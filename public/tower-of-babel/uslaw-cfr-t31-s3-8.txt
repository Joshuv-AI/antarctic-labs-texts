31 C.F.R. § 3.8 — Statute of limitations.
Citation: us:cfr:31:3.8
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-3.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 3 (Claims Regulations And Indemnification Of Department Of Treasury Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Claims under this subpart must be presented in writing to the Department within 2 years after the claim accrued.
