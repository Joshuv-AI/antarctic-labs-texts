12 C.F.R. § 364.100 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:12:364.100
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-364.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 364 (Standards For Safety And Soundness). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Section 39 of the Federal Deposit Insurance Act requires the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation to establish safety and soundness standards. Pursuant to section 39, this part establishes safety and soundness standards by guideline.
