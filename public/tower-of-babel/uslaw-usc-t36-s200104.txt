36 U.S.C. § 200104 — Restrictions
Citation: us:usc:36:200104
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section200104
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 2001 (Society Of American Florists And Ornamental Horticulturists). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Profit.—
The corporation may not operate for profit.
(b) Use of Earnings.—
Earnings generated by the corporation may be used only for the purposes provided in section 200102 of this title.
(c) Use of Property.—
Property held by the corporation, and the proceeds from the property, may be used only for the purposes provided in section 200102 of this title.
(d) Parks in the District of Columbia.—
The corporation may not occupy any park in the District of Columbia.
