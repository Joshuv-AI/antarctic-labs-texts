10 U.S.C. § 20262 — Retirement: selective early retirement
Citation: us:usc:10:20262
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Space Force); Chapter 2005 (Officers); Subchapter V (Continuation On Active Duty And Selective Early Retirement; Other Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Sections 638 and 638a of this title shall apply to the retirement of officers on the Space Force officer list in the same manner as to officers of the Regular Air Force.
