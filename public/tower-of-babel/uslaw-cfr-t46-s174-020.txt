46 C.F.R. § 174.020 — Alternate intact stability criterion.
Citation: us:cfr:46:174.020
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-174.020
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 174 (Special Rules Pertaining To Specific Vessel Types). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A barge need not comply with § 174.015 and subparts C and E of part 170 of this chapter if it has the following characteristics:
(a) The weather deck is watertight.
(b) The barge's hull proportions fall within any one of the ratios in categories (A) through (D) in Table 174.020.
(c) The maximum cargo height is 30 feet (9.25 meters) or a value equal to the depth of the barge amidships, whichever is less.
