34 C.F.R. § 692.53 — What requirements must a State satisfy to receive SLEAP Program funds?
Citation: us:cfr:34:692.53
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-692.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 692 (Leveraging Educational Assistance Partnership Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To receive SLEAP Program funds for any fiscal year, a State must—
(a) Participate in the LEAP Program;
(b) Meet the requirements in § 692.60; and
(c) Have a program that satisfies the requirements in § 692.21(a), (b), (d), (e), (f), (g), (j), and (k).
