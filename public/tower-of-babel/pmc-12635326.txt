Author Correction: Analysis wheat wild relatives Thinopyrum intermedium and Roegneria kamoji genomes reveal different polyploid evolution paths
Authors: Silong Sun, Naixiu Che, Liyang Chen, Yongchao Hao, Yongchang Xu, Qingyang Chen, Wenyang Ge, Shuangshuang Qin, Wenxiu Zhang, Chunmei Dong, Jianqiang Li, Yuxin Liu, Fangfang Yan, Long Han, Zhangwei Liu, Liang Zhang, Chengzhi Jiang, Zujun Yang, Hongwei Wang, Lingrang Kong
Published: 2025

Correction to: Nature Communications 10.1038/s41467-025-63007-y, published online 18 August 2025.

In the version of the article initially published, the subgenome labels were missing from the bottom row of Fig. 2b and two labels (“Pse. Libanotica” and “H. marinum”) were missing from Fig. 2c. Fig. 2 has now been corrected in the HTML and PDF versions of the article.
