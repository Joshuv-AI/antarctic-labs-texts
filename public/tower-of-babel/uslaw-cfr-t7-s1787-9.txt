7 C.F.R. § 1787.9 — Waivers.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1787.9
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1787.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1787 (The “Buy American” Requirement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Under limited circumstances the Administrator may waive the Buy American requirement with respect to a specific contract entered into between an RUS Borrower and a third party which will be paid for with loan funds, subject to §§ 1787.10 through 1787.14.
