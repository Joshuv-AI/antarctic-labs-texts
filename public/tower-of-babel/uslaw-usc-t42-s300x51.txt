42 U.S.C. § 300x–51 — Opportunity for public comment on State plans
Citation: us:usc:42:300x–51
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section300x–51
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter XVII (Block Grants); Part B (Block Grants Regarding Mental Health And Substance Use); Subpart iii (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A funding agreement for a grant under section 300x or 300x–21 of this title is that the State involved will make the plan required in section 300x–1 of this title, and the plan required in section 300x–32 of this title, respectively, public within the State in such manner as to facilitate comment from any person (including any Federal or other public agency) during the development of the plan (including any revisions) and after the submission of the plan to the Secretary.
