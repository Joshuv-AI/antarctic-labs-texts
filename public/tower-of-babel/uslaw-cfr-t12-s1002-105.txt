12 C.F.R. § 1002.105 — Covered financial institutions and exempt institutions.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1002.105
Creator: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1002.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1002 (Equal Credit Opportunity Act (Regulation B)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Financial institution means any partnership, company, corporation, association (incorporated or unincorporated), trust, estate, cooperative organization, or other entity that engages in any financial activity.
(b) Covered financial institution means a financial institution, other than a Farm Credit System lender, that originated at least 1,000 covered credit transactions for small businesses in each of the two preceding calendar years.
