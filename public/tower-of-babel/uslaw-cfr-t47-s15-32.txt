47 C.F.R. § 15.32 — Test procedures for CPU boards and computer power supplies.
Citation: us:cfr:47:15.32
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-15.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 15 (Radio Frequency Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Power supplies and CPU boards used with personal computers and for which separate authorizations are required to be obtained shall be tested in accordance with the specific procedures published or otherwise authorized by the Commission.
