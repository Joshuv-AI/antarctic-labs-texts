52 U.S.C. § 10702 — “State” defined

Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 52 (Voting And Elections); Chapter 107 (Right To Vote At Age Eighteen). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this chapter, the term “State” includes the District of Columbia.
