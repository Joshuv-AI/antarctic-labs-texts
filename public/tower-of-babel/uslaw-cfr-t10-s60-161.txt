10 C.F.R. § 60.161 — Training and certification program.
Citation: us:cfr:10:60.161
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-60.161
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 60 (Disposal Of High-Level Radioactive Wastes In Geologic Repositories). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

DOE shall establish a program for training, proficiency testing, certification and requalification of operating and supervisory personnel.
