42 C.F.R. § 136.334 — Publication of a list of recipients.
Citation: us:cfr:42:136.334
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-136.334
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 136 (Indian Health). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary, acting through the Service, will publish annually in the Federal Register a list of recipients of Indian Health Scholarships, including the name of each recipient, tribal affiliation if applicable, and school.
