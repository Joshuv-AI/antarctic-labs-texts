32 C.F.R. § 1602.3 — Aliens and nationals.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1602.3
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1602.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1602 (Definitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The term alien means any person who is not a citizen or national of the United States.
(b) The term national of the United States means:
(1) A citizen of the United States, or
(2) A person, though not a citizen of the United States, who owes allegiance to the United States.
