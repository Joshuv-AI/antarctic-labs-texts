40 C.F.R. § 52.1681 — Control strategy: Lead.
Citation: us:cfr:40:52.1681
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-52.1681
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 52 (Approval And Promulgation Of Implementation Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As part of the attainment demonstration for lead, the State of New York has committed to rate all sources of lead or lead compound emissions with either an “A” or “B” environmental rating pursuant to 6 NYCRR Part 212.
