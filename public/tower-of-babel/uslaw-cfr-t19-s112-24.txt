19 C.F.R. § 112.24 — Issuance of license.
Citation: us:cfr:19:112.24
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-112.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 112 (Carriers, Cartmen, And Lightermen). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The port director shall issue a customhouse cartage and lighterage license on Customs Form 3857 provided he is satisfied that:
(a) The character, qualifications, and experience of the applicant and fitness of his equipment are satisfactory.
(b) The applicant has complied with all the requirements of § 112.22.
