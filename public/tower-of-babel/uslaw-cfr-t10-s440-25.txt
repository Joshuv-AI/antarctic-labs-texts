10 C.F.R. § 440.25 — Reports.
Citation: us:cfr:10:440.25
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-440.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 440 (Weatherization Assistance For Low-Income Persons). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

DOE may require any recipient of financial assistance under this part to provide, in such form as may be prescribed, such reports or answers in writing to specific questions, surveys, or questionnaires as DOE determines to be necessary to carry out its responsibilities or the responsibilities of the Secretary of Health and Human Services under this part.
