12 U.S.C. § 3336 — Procedures for establishing appraisal standards and requiring use of certified and licensed appraisers
Citation: us:usc:12:3336
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section3336
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 34A (Appraisal Subcommittee Of Federal Financial Institutions Examination Council). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Appraisal standards and requirements for using State certified and licensed appraisers in federally related transactions pursuant to this chapter shall be prescribed in accordance with procedures set forth in section 553 of title 5, including the publication of notice and receipt of written comments or the holding of public hearings with respect to any standards or requirements proposed to be established.
