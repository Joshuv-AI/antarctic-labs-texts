22 U.S.C. § 4505 — Applications; payments to eligible institutions
Citation: us:usc:22:4505
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section4505
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 55 (Research And Training For Eastern Europe And Independent States Of Former Soviet Union). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Any institution seeking funding under this chapter shall prepare and submit an application to the Secretary of State once each fiscal year. Each such application shall—
(1)
provide a description of the purposes for which the payments will be used in accordance with section 4504 of this title; and
(2)
provide such fiscal control and such accounting procedures as may be necessary (A) to ensure a proper accounting of Federal funds paid under this chapter, and (B) to ensure the verification of the costs of the continuing education and research programs conducted under this chapter.
(b)
Payments under this chapter may be made in installments, in advance, or by way of reimbursement, with necessary adjustments on account of overpayments and underpayments.
