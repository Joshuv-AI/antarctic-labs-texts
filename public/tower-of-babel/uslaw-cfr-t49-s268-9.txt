49 C.F.R. § 268.9 — Eligible participants.
Citation: us:cfr:49:268.9
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-268.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 268 (Magnetic Levitation Transportation Technology Deployment Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any State, or any authority designated by one or more State(s) to carry out the preconstruction planning activities under the Maglev Deployment Program is eligible to participate in the Maglev Deployment Program.
