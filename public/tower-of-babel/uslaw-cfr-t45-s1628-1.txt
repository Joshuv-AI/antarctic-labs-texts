45 C.F.R. § 1628.1 — Purpose
Citation: us:cfr:45:1628.1
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1628.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1628 (Recipient Fund Balances). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to set out the Corporation's policies and procedures applicable to recipient fund balances. The Corporation's fund balance policies are intended to ensure the timely expenditure of LSC funds for the effective and economical provision of high quality legal assistance to eligible clients.
