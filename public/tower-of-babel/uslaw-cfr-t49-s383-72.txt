49 C.F.R. § 383.72 — Implied consent to alcohol testing.
Citation: us:cfr:49:383.72
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-383.72
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 383 (Commercial Driver'S License Standards; Requirements And Penalties). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person who holds a CLP or CDL or is required to hold a CLP or CDL is considered to have consented to such testing as is required by any State or jurisdiction in the enforcement of item (4) of Table 1 to § 383.51 of this subpart and § 392.5(a)(2) of this subchapter. Consent is implied by driving a commercial motor vehicle.
