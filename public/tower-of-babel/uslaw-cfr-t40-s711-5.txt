40 C.F.R. § 711.5 — Chemical substances for which information must be reported.
Citation: us:cfr:40:711.5
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-711.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 711 (Tsca Chemical Data Reporting Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any chemical substance that is in the Master Inventory File at the beginning of a submission period described in § 711.20, unless the chemical substance is specifically excluded by § 711.6.
