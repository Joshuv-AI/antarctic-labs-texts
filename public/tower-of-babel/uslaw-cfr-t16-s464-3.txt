16 C.F.R. § 464.3 — Misleading fees prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:16:464.3
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-464.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 464 (Rule On Unfair Or Deceptive Fees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In any offer, display, or advertisement for a covered good or service it is an unfair and deceptive practice and a violation of this part for any business to misrepresent any fee or charge, including: the nature, purpose, amount, or refundability of any fee or charge; and the identity of the good or service for which the fee or charge is imposed.
