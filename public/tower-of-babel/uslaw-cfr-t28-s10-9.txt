28 C.F.R. § 10.9 — Requirements for supplemental registration statement.
Citation: us:cfr:28:10.9
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-10.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 10 (Registration Of Certain Organizations Carrying On Activities Within The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The rules and regulations in this part with respect to registration statements submitted to the Attorney General under section 2 of the said act shall apply with equal force and effect to supplemental registration statements required thereunder to be filed with the Attorney General.
