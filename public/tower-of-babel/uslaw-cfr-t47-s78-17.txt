47 C.F.R. § 78.17 — Amendment of applications.
Citation: us:cfr:47:78.17
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-78.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 78 (Cable Television Relay Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any application may be amended as a matter of right prior to the adoption date of any final action taken by the Commission with respect to the application. If a petition to deny has been filed, the amendment shall be served on the petitioner.
