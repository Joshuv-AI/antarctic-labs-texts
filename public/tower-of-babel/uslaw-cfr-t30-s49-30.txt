30 C.F.R. § 49.30 — Requirements for small coal mines.
Citation: us:cfr:30:49.30
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-49.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 49 (Mine Rescue Teams). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

At mines with 36 or fewer underground employees, mine rescue team members shall be knowledgeable about the operations and ventilation of each covered mine.
