49 C.F.R. § 38.33 — Fare box.
Citation: us:cfr:49:38.33
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-38.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 38 (Americans With Disabilities Act (Ada) Accessibility Specifications For Transportation Vehicles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where provided, the farebox shall be located as far forward as practicable and shall not obstruct traffic in the vestibule, especially wheelchairs or mobility aids.
