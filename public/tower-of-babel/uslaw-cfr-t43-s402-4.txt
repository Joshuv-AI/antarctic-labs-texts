43 C.F.R. § 402.4 — Citizenship requirement.
Citation: us:cfr:43:402.4
Creator: Bureau Of Reclamation, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-402.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 402 (Sale Of Lands In Federal Reclamation Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Before patent may be issued to a purchaser under the regulations in this subpart, he must furnish satisfactory evidence that he is a citizen of the United States.
