42 U.S.C. § 4124 — Applicability of fiscal controls
Citation: us:usc:42:4124
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section4124
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 50 (National Flood Insurance); Subchapter IV (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of chapter 91 of title 31 shall apply to the program authorized under this chapter to the same extent as they apply to wholly owned Government corporations.
