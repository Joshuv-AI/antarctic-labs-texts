8 C.F.R. § 334.1 — Filing of application for naturalization.
Citation: us:cfr:8:334.1
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-334.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 334 (Application For Naturalization). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person who is an applicant under sections 316, 319, 324, 325, 327, 328, 329, or 330 of the Act and the corresponding parts of this chapter, may apply for naturalization in accordance with the procedures prescribed in this chapter in accordance with the instructions on the form.
