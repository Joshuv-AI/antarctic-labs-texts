46 C.F.R. § 108.427 — International shore connection.
Citation: us:cfr:46:108.427
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-108.427
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 108 (Design And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A fire main system on a unit in international service must have—
(a) At least one international shore connection that meets ASTM F 1121 (incorporated by reference, see § 105.01-3).
(b) A cutoff valve and check valve for each connection; and
(c) Facilities available enabling the connection to be used on either side of the unit.
