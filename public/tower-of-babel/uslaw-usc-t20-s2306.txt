20 U.S.C. § 2306 — Special rule
Citation: us:usc:20:2306
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 44 (Career And Technical Education). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In the case of a local community in which no employees are represented by a labor organization, for purposes of this chapter, the term “representatives of employees” shall be substituted for “labor organization”.
