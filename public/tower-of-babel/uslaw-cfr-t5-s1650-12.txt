5 C.F.R. § 1650.12 — Single payment.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1650.12
Creator: Federal Retirement Thrift Investment Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1650.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1650 (Methods Of Withdrawing Funds From The Thrift Savings Plan). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Provided that, in the case of a partial distribution, the amount elected is not less than $1,000, a participant can elect a distribution of all or a portion of his or her account balance in a single payment.
