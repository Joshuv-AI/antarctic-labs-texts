7 C.F.R. § 1210.322 — Term of office.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1210.322
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Miscellaneous Commodities), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1210.322
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1210 (Watermelon Research And Promotion Plan). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The term of office of Board members shall be three years.
(b) Except in the case of mid-term vacancies, the term of office shall begin on January 1, or such other date as may be recommended by the Board and approved by the Secretary.
(c) Board members shall serve during the term of office for which they are selected and have qualified, and until their successors are selected and have qualified.
(d) No person shall serve more than two successive terms of office.
