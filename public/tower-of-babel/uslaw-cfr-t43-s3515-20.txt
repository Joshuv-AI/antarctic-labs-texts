43 C.F.R. § 3515.20 — May I exchange preference rights?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3515.20
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3515.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3500 (Leasing Of Solid Minerals Other Than Coal And Oil Shale). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. To have a preference right that can be exchanged, you must have timely submitted a preference right lease application. If you have demonstrated a right to a lease, BLM may, in lieu of issuing the preference right lease, negotiate for the selection of appropriate lands to exchange and establish lease terms for those lands.
