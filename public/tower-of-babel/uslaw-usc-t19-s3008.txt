19 U.S.C. § 3008 — Import and export statistics
Citation: us:usc:19:3008
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section3008
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 18 (Implementation Of Harmonized Tariff Schedule). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Commerce shall compile, and make publicly available, the import and export trade statistics of the United States. Such statistics shall be conformed to the nomenclature of the Convention.
