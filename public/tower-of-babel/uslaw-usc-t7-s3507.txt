7 U.S.C. § 3507 — Regulations

Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 66 (Agricultural Foreign Investment Disclosure). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 90 days after October 14, 1978, the Secretary shall prescribe regulations for purposes of carrying out the provisions of this chapter.
