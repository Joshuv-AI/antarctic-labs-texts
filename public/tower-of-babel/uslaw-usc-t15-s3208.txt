15 U.S.C. § 3208 — Relationship to other applicable law
Citation: us:usc:15:3208
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section3208
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 59 (Retail Policies For Natural Gas Utilities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter prohibits any State regulatory authority or nonregulated gas utility from adopting, pursuant to State law, any standard or rule affecting gas utilities which is different from any standard established by this chapter.
