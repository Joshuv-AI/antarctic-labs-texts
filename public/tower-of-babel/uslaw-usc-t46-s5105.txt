46 U.S.C. § 5105 — Load line surveys
Citation: us:usc:46:5105
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section5105
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part C (Load Lines Of Vessels); Chapter 51 (Load Lines). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Secretary may provide for annual, renewal, and other load line surveys.
(b)
In conducting a load line survey, the Secretary shall consider whether—
(1)
the hull and fittings of the vessel—
(A)
are adequate to protect the vessel from the sea; and
(B)
meet other requirements the Secretary may prescribe by regulation;
(2)
the strength of the hull is adequate for all loading conditions;
(3)
the stability of the vessel is adequate for all loading conditions;
(4)
the topsides of the vessel are arranged and constructed to allow rapid overboard drainage of deck water in heavy weather; and
(5)
the topsides of the vessel are adequate in design, arrangement, and equipment to protect crewmembers performing outside tasks necessary for safe operation of the vessel.
