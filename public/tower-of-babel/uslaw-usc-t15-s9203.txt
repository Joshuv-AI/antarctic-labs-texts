15 U.S.C. § 9203 — NIST support for research and standards on generative adversarial networks
Citation: us:usc:15:9203
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section9203
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 117 (Identifying Outputs Of Generative Adversarial Networks). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
The Director of the National Institute of Standards and Technology shall support research for the development of measurements and standards necessary to accelerate the development of the technological tools to examine the function and outputs of generative adversarial networks or other technologies that synthesize or manipulate content.
(b) Outreach
The Director of the National Institute of Standards and Technology shall conduct outreach—
(1)
to receive input from private, public, and academic stakeholders on fundamental measurements and standards research necessary to examine the function and outputs of generative adversarial networks; and
(2)
to consider the feasibility of an ongoing public and private sector engagement to develop voluntary standards for the function and outputs of generative adversarial networks or other technologies that synthesize or manipulate content.
