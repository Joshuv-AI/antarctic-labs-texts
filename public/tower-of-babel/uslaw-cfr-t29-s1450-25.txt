29 C.F.R. § 1450.25 — Non-waiver of rights by payments.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1450.25
Creator: Federal Mediation And Conciliation Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1450.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1450 (Collections Of Claims Owed The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee's involuntary payment of all or any portion of a debt being collected under 5 U.S.C. 5514 shall not be construed as a waiver of any rights which the employee may have under 5 U.S.C. 5514 or any other provision of contract or law, unless statutory or contractual provisions provide to the contrary.
