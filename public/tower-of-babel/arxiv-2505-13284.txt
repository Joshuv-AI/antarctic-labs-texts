Abstract
We present the logic IBV, which is an intuitionistic version of BV, in the sense that its restriction to the MLL connectives is exactly IMLL, the intuitionistic version of MLL. For this logic we give a deep inference proof system and show cut elimination. We also show that the logic obtained from IBV by dropping the associativity of the new non-commutative seq-connective is an intuitionistic variant of the recently introduced logic NML. For this logic, called INML, we give a cut-free sequent calculus.

\Delta
) can contain formulas of the shape

, which are decomposed in
\Gamma_{1}
and
\Gamma_{2}
(resp.
\Delta_{1}
and

∎

The logic
\mathsf{INML}
is indeed the same logic as we would obtain by removing associativity of
\triangleleft
from
\mathsf{IBV}
.

Theorem 7.3

Let
A
be a formula.
Then
\mathord{\vdash_{\mathsf{INML}}\,}A
iff
\mathord{\vdash_{\mathsf{IBV}\setminus\{\mathsf{asso_{R}^{\triangleleft}},\mathsf{asso_{L}^{\triangleleft}}\}}\,}A
.

Proof

Most of the work has already been done in Lemmas
3
and
4
. It remains to show that (i) Lemma
3
also applies to the rule
\triangleleft
in (
11
), and that
\mathsf{asso_{R}^{\triangleleft}}
,
\mathsf{asso_{L}^{\triangleleft}}
are not needed for this, and (ii) that Lemma
4
also applies to the
\mathsf{q}\mathord{\downarrow}
-,
\mathsf{sq}
-, and
\mathsf{ref}
-rules, when we use
\mathsf{INML}
instead of
\mathsf{IMLL}
.
Details in
Appendix

0.C
.
∎

Unsurprisingly, we have for
\mathsf{INML}
the same conservativity results as we proved for
\mathsf{IBV}
in the previous two sections.

Theorem 7.4

\mathsf{INML}
is a conservative extension of
\mathsf{IMLL}
.

Proof

It suffices to remark that all rules in
\mathsf{INML}
have the subformula property, so any proof of a
\triangleleft
-free formula in
\mathsf{INML}
can only use rules in
\mathsf{IMLL}
.
∎

We now would like to show that
\mathsf{NML}
is conservative over
\mathsf{INML}
, but we encounter the same problems with the units as in
\mathsf{BV}
. We therefore consider only unit-free formulas, and the unit-free version of
\mathsf{NML}
, that we call
\mathsf{NML^{-}}
, and which is shown in

Theorem 7.5

Let
A
be a unit-free formula.
We have
\mathord{\vdash_{\mathsf{INML}}\,}A
iff
\mathord{\vdash_{\mathsf{NML^{-}}}\,}{A^{\flat}}
.

Proof

This follows in the same way as (unit-free)
\mathsf{MLL}_{\mathsf{mix}}
is conservative over unit-free
\mathsf{IMLL}
.
See
Appendix

0.C
for details.
∎

We conclude this section by observing that the cut restricted to associativity of
\triangleleft
is enough to obtain from
\mathsf{INML}
a sequent calculus for
\mathsf{IBV}
.
To this end, we introduce the following
associative-cut
rules:

\Gamma\vdash(A\triangleleft B)\triangleleft C

A\triangleleft(B\triangleleft C),\Delta\vdash D

\mathsf{a\mathchar 45\relax cut_{L}}\;

\Gamma\vdash D

\Gamma\vdash A\triangleleft(B\triangleleft C)

(A\triangleleft B)\triangleleft C,\Delta\vdash D

\mathsf{a\mathchar 45\relax cut_{R}}\;

\Gamma\vdash D

(12)

Theorem 7.6

Let
A
be a formula.
Then
\mathord{\vdash_{\mathsf{IBV}}\,}A
iff
\mathord{\vdash_{\mathsf{INML}\cup\{\mathsf{a\mathchar 45\relax cut_{L}},\mathsf{a\mathchar 45\relax cut_{R}}\}}\,}A
.

Proof

As before, it suffices to extend Lemmas
3
and
4
.
Note that for simulating
\mathsf{a\mathchar 45\relax cut_{L}}
and
\mathsf{a\mathchar 45\relax cut_{R}}
in
\mathsf{IBV}
, we use
\mathsf{i}_{\uparrow}^{\bullet}
and then apply Corollary
3
.
∎

Remark 7

A sequent calculus for
\mathsf{BV}
could be obtained by adding to the cut-free sequent system
\mathsf{NML}
with the unit
from

the one-side version of the rule
\mathsf{a\mathchar 45\relax cut_{R}}

8
Conclusion and Future Work

In this paper, we have defined two logics,
\mathsf{IBV}
and
\mathsf{INML}
, which are the intuitionistic counterparts of the non-commutative logics
\mathsf{BV}
and
\mathsf{NML}
, respectively.
In order to show that both systems are conservative extensions of the multiplicative intuitionistic linear logic
\mathsf{IMLL}
,
for
\mathsf{IBV}
we have implemented the first splitting proof for an intuitionistic system, and for
\mathsf{INML}
we have shown that cut-elimination holds for the sequent system
\mathsf{INML}
.
