The period map from commutative to noncommutative deformations
Samuel A. Moore

Abstract
. We study the period map from infinitesimal deformations of a scheme X X over a perfect field k k to those of the associated k k -linear ∞ \infty -category QC ⁡ ( X ) \QC(X) . For quasicompact, smooth, and separated X X , we identify the corresponding map on tangent fibres with the dual HKR map R ​ Γ ​ ( X , T X ) ​ [ 1 ] → HH ∙ ⁡ ( X / k ) ​ [ 2 ] \mathrm{R}\Gamma(X,\mathrm{T}_{X})[1]\to\HH^{\bullet}(X/k)[2] , and give conditions for injectivity on homotopy groups. 1.3.
Outline of proof

Let us briefly outline the proof of
Theorem
A
. If
\mathrm{sqz}_{k}(V)
denotes the square-zero extension of
k
by some
V\in\Mod_{k\geq 0}
, then it is known that

\Omega\mathrm{Def}_{X,\Lambda}(\mathrm{sqz}_{k}(V))\simeq\mathrm{Map}_{\QC(X)}(\mathrm{L}_{X/k},\mathscr{O}_{X}\otimes_{k}V),

where we have taken the basepoint at the trivial deformation. We will construct similar equivalences

\Omega\mathrm{CatDef}^{\land}_{\QC(X),\Lambda}(\mathrm{sqz}_{k}(V))\simeq\mathrm{Map}_{\Fun_{k}(\QC(X),\QC(X))}(\mathrm{id},\mathrm{id}\otimes_{k}V[1])\simeq\mathrm{Map}_{\QC(X)}(\underline{\mathrm{HH}}_{\bullet}[-1],\mathscr{O}_{X}\otimes_{k}V),

where the first map is a variant of the usual
Atiyah class
construction (which sends a deformation of a sheaf to an
\mathrm{Ext}^{1}
class).

We can view the right sides of these equivalences as functors on
\QC(X)_{\geq 0}
evaluated at sheaves of the form
\mathscr{O}_{X}\otimes_{k}V
. We show that the ‘looped’ period map accordingly extends to a transformation

h\colon\mathrm{Map}_{\QC(X)}(\mathrm{L}_{X/k},-)\longrightarrow\mathrm{Map}_{\QC(X)}(\underline{\mathrm{HH}}_{\bullet}[-1],-)

of functors on
\mathrm{QC}(X)_{\geq 0}
. A result of Căldăraru linking the ‘universal Atiyah class’ to the HKR map allows us to identify the map
\underline{\mathrm{HH}}_{\bullet}\to\mathrm{L}_{X/k}[1]
classifying
h
. The other results then follow by further analysis of the dual HKR map and some facts on formal moduli problems.

4.3.
