20 C.F.R. § 801.104 — Operational rules.
Citation: us:cfr:20:801.104
Creator: Benefits Review Board, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-801.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 801 (Establishment And Operation Of The Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Deputy Secretary of Labor may promulgate such rules and regulations as may be necessary or appropriate for effective operation of the Benefits Review Board as an independent quasi-judicial body in accordance with the provisions of the statute.
