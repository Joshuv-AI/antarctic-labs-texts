45 C.F.R. § 1631.17 — Maintenance.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1631.17
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1631.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1631 (Purchasing And Property Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A recipient must maintain real estate acquired with LSC funds:
(a) In an efficient operating condition; and
(b) In compliance with state and local government property standards and building codes.
