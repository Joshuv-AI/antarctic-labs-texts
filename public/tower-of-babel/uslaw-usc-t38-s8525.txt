38 U.S.C. § 8525 — Court actions
Citation: us:usc:38:8525
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section8525
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part VI (Acquisition And Disposition Of Property); Chapter 85 (Disposition Of Deceased Veterans’ Personal Property); Subchapter II (Death While Patient Of Department Facility). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If necessary to obtain such assets the Secretary, through the Secretary’s authorized attorneys, may bring and prosecute appropriate actions at law or other legal proceedings, the costs and expenses thereof to be paid as are other administrative expenses of the Department.
