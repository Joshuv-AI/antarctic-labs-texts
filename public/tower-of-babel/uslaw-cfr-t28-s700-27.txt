28 C.F.R. § 700.27 — Other rights and services.
Citation: us:cfr:28:700.27
Creator: Office Of Independent Counsel
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-700.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 700 (Production Or Disclosure Of Material Or Information Of The Office Of Independent Counsel). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this subpart shall be construed to entitle any person, as of right, to any service or to the disclosure of any record to which such person is not entitled under 5 U.S.C. 552a.
