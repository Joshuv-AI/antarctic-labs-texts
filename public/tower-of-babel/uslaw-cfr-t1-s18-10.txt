1 C.F.R. § 18.10 — Illustrations, tabular material, and forms.
Citation: us:cfr:1:18.10
Creator: Administrative Committee Of The Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-1/section-18.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 1 (General Provisions), Part 18 (Preparation And Transmittal Of Documents Generally). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-23 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If it is necessary to publish a form or illustration, a clear and legible original form or illustration, or a clear and completely legible reproduction approximately 8 1/2 by 11 inches, shall be included in the original document and each certified copy.
(b) A document that includes tabular material may be assigned to the deferred publication schedule. See § 17.7.
