18 C.F.R. § 11.8 — Adjustment of annual charges.
Citation: us:cfr:18:11.8
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-11.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 11 (Annual Charges Under Part I Of The Federal Power Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All annual charges imposed under this subpart continue in effect as fixed unless changed as authorized by law.
