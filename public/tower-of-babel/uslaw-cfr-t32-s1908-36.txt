32 C.F.R. § 1908.36 — Notification of decision and right of further appeal.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1908.36
Creator: Central Intelligence Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1908.36
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1908 (Public Requests For Mandatory Declassification Review Of Classified Information Pursuant To Sec. 3.5 Of Executive Order 13526). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Executive Secretary of the Agency Release Panel shall promptly prepare and communicate the final Agency decision to the requester, NARA, or the particular Presidential Library. That correspondence shall include a notice, if applicable, that an appeal of the decision may be made to the Interagency Security Classification Appeals Panel (ISCAP) established pursuant to section 5.3 of the Order.
