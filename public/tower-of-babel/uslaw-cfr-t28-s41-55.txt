28 C.F.R. § 41.55 — Preemployment inquiries.
Citation: us:cfr:28:41.55
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-41.55
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 41 (Implementation Of Executive Order 12250, Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Handicap In Federally Assisted Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A recipient may not conduct a preemployment medical examination or make a preemployment inquiry as to whether an applicant is a handicapped person or as to the nature or severity of a handicap except under the circumstances described in 28 CFR 42.513.
