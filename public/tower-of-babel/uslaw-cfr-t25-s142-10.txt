25 C.F.R. § 142.10 — How is freight to be prepared?
Citation: us:cfr:25:142.10
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-142.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 142 (Alaska Resupply Operation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All freight must be prepared in accordance with industry standards, unless otherwise specified, for overseas shipment, including any pickup, delivery, staging, sorting, consolidating, packaging, crating, boxing, containerizing, and marking that may be deemed necessary by the Manager.
