18 U.S.C. § 1792 — Mutiny and riot prohibited
Citation: us:usc:18:1792
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section1792
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 87 (Prisons). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever instigates, connives, willfully attempts to cause, assists, or conspires to cause any mutiny or riot, at any Federal penal, detention, or correctional facility, shall be imprisoned not more than ten years or fined under this title, or both.
