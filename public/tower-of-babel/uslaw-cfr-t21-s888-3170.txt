21 C.F.R. § 888.3170 — Elbow joint radial (hemi-elbow) polymer prosthesis.

Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-888.3170
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 888 (Orthopedic Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. An elbow joint radial (hemi-elbow) polymer prosthesis is a device intended to be implanted made of medical grade silicone elastomer used to replace the proximal end of the radius.
(b) Classification. Class II.
