18 C.F.R. § 385.709 — Other types of decisions (Rule 709).
Citation: us:cfr:18:385.709
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-385.709
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 385 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In lieu of an initial decision under Rule 708, the Commission may order any type of decision as provided by 5 U.S.C. 557(b), or permit waiver of the initial decision as provided by Rule 710.
