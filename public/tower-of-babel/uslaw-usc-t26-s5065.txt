26 U.S.C. § 5065 — Territorial extent of law
Citation: us:usc:26:5065
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section5065
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 51 (Distilled Spirits, Wines, And Beer); Subchapter A (Gallonage And Occupational Taxes); Part I (Gallonage Taxes); Subpart E (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of this part imposing taxes on distilled spirits, wines, and beer shall be held to extend to such articles produced anywhere within the exterior boundaries of the United States, whether the same be within an internal revenue district or not.
