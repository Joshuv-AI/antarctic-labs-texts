21 C.F.R. § 330.11 — NDA deviations from applicable monograph.
Citation: us:cfr:21:330.11
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-330.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 330 (Over-The-Counter (Otc) Human Drugs Which Are Generally Recognized As Safe And Effective And Not Misbranded). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A new drug application requesting approval of an OTC drug deviating in any respect from a monograph that has become final shall be in the form required by § 314.50 of this chapter, but shall include a statement that the product meets all conditions of the applicable monograph except for the deviation for which approval is requested and may omit all information except that pertinent to the deviation.
