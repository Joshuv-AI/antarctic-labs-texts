22 C.F.R. § 309.24 — Bankruptcy.
Citation: us:cfr:22:309.24
Creator: Peace Corps
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-309.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 309 (Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Peace Corps generally terminates collection activity on debts that have been discharged in bankruptcy unless otherwise provided for by bankruptcy law. The CFO will seek legal advice by the General Counsel's office if there is the belief that any claims or offset may have survived the discharge of a debtor.
