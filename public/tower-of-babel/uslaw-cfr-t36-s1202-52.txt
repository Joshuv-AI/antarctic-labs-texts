36 C.F.R. § 1202.52 — How do I pay?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1202.52
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1202.52
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1202 (Regulations Implementing The Privacy Act Of 1974). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must pay by check or money order. Make your check or money order payable to the National Archives and Records Administration and send it to the NARA Privacy Act Officer, Room 3110, 8601 Adelphi Road, College Park, MD 20740-6001.
