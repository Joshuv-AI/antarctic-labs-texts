34 C.F.R. § 673.2 — Applicability of regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:34:673.2
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-673.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 673 (General Provisions For The Federal Perkins Loan Program, Federal Work-Study Program, And Federal Supplemental Educational Opportunity Grant Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The participating institution is responsible for administering these programs in accordance with the regulations in this part and the applicable program regulations in 34 CFR parts 674, 675, and 676.
