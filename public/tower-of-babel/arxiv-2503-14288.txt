Symmetric versus genuine symmetric forms in Hermitian K K -theory
Marco Schlichting

Abstract
. We show that for finite dimensional regular Noetherian rings that contain a field or are smooth over a Dedekind domain, the comparison map from the Hermitian K K -theory of genuine symmetric forms to that of symmetric forms is an isomorphism in degrees ≥ − 1 \geq-1 and a monomorphism in degree − 2 -2 . In particular, the spaces of Hermitian K K -theory of genuine symmetric forms and the symplectic K K -theory space are homotopy invariant for such rings.

Theorem 1.1

Let

Even if the involution on

As a corollary of Theorem

Theorem 1.2
.

Let

If

The hermitian

-theory space of genuine symmetric forms

Some low degree computations

All rings in this article are assumed to be commutative.

Lemma 2.1
.

Let
R
be a local ring. Then

GW^{[2]}_{1}(R)=GW^{[1]}_{0}(R)=L^{gs}_{-1}(R)=L^{gs}_{-2}(R)=0

Proof.

Recall that for local rings, the symplectic

is

Now, the lemma follows from the exact sequence of the fundamental sequence (

and the surjection

.
∎
