27 C.F.R. § 71.49 — Applications for basic permits.
Citation: us:cfr:27:71.49
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-71.49
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 71 (Rules Of Practice In Permit Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If, upon examination of any application (including a renewal application) for a basic permit, the appropriate TTB officer has reason to believe that the applicant is not entitled to such permit he shall issue a citation for the contemplated disapproval of the application.
