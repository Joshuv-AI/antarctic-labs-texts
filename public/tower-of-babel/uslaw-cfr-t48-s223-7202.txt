48 C.F.R. § 223.7202 — 223.7202 Preaward responsibilities.
Citation: us:cfr:48:223.7202

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 223 (Environment, Sustainable Acquisition, And Material Safety). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When an acquisition involves AA&E, technical or requirements personnel shall specify in the purchase request—
(a) That AA&E is involved; and
(b) Which physical security requirements of DoD Manual 5100.76 apply.
