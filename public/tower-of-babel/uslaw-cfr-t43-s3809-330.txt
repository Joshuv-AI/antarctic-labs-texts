43 C.F.R. § 3809.330 — May I modify my notice?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3809.330
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3809.330
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3800 (Mining Claims Under The General Mining Laws). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Yes, you may submit a notice modification at any time during operations under a notice.
(b) BLM will review your notice modification the same way it reviewed your initial notice under §§ 3809.311 and 3809.313.
