7 U.S.C. § 1361 — Application of subpart
Citation: us:usc:7:1361
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1361
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 35 (Agricultural Adjustment Act Of 1938); Subchapter II (Loans, Parity Payments, Consumer Safeguards, Marketing Quotas, And Marketing Certificates); Part C (Administrative Provisions); Subpart i (Publication And Review Of Quotas). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This subpart shall apply to the publication and review of farm marketing quotas established for corn, wheat, cotton, and rice, established under part B of this subchapter.
