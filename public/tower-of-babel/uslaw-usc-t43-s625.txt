43 U.S.C. § 625 — Release of unentered land from lien on noncompletion of irrigation project
Citation: us:usc:43:625
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section625
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 13 (Federal Lands Included In State Irrigation Districts). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Interior may, upon the expiration of ten years from the date of his approval of said map and plan of any irrigation district, release from the lien authorized by this chapter any unentered land or lands upon which final certificate has not issued, for which irrigation works have not been constructed and water of such district made available for the land.
