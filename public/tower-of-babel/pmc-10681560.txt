Clinical analysis of prophylactic uterine artery embolization combined with double balloon catheter for second-trimester pregnancy termination in cases of complete placenta previa: A retrospective study
Authors: Hao Li, Wenxing Cheng, Jiao Wen, Jing Peng, Shiyao Wu, Yun Zhao, Weina Xia
Published: 2023

ABSTRACT
Complete placenta previa often causes significant bleeding in a short period during second-trimester pregnancy termination. This can destabilize the mother’s circulation, threatening her life. Furthermore, the condition is complicated by an immature cervix, making it a challenging clinical problem. The aim of this study was to investigate the effect of prophylactic uterine artery embolization (UAE) combined with double balloon catheter (DBC) deal with those cases. A total of 7 patients who underwent pregnancy termination in the second-trimester in Maternal and Child Health Hospital of Hubei Province between March 1st, 2021 and August 31st, 2023 were retrospectively analyzed in this study. All patients were diagnosed with complete placenta previa status and placenta accreta spectrum, and were treated with prophylactic UAE combined with DBC, and/or dilation and evacuation. All the patients received mifepristone, and 5 of them underwent medical termination with ethacridine lactate. Following prophylactic UAE combined with DBC, 6 patients underwent dilation and evacuation, which was monitored by ultrasound. And one patient experienced natural delivery of their fetus and placenta. Only one patient (patient 3) developed an intrapartum fever after prolonged duration of 18 hours from ethacridine to UAE and 56 hours from UAE to DBC. The amount of intrapartum hemorrhage ranged from 20 mL to 300 mL. The combined therapy of prophylactic UAE and DBC is a preferred option for patients with complete placenta previa undergoing second-trimester pregnancy termination. The use of dilation and evacuation may depend on the cervical condition, bleeding, or infection.

1. Introduction

Complete placenta previa is defined as an abnormality of the placental location where placental parenchyma completely covers the internal uterine ostium, which occasionally causes severe postpartum hemorrhage and is frequently complicated by placenta accreta spectrum (PAS).[1] Following the implementation of the universal two-child policy in October 2015 and the three-child policy on May 2021 in China,[2] there has been an increase in pregnancies among older women. Fetal mortality, congenital anomalies and placental previa were connected with maternal age.[3–5] So, the termination in the second-trimester with complete placenta previa is increasing with the change in family planning policy in China. Mifepristone combined with ethacridine lactate or misoprostol are medicine methods used to induct second-trimester termination in pregnant women, regardless of the presence of placenta previa in China. However, the risk of prenatal bleeding is higher in cases of placenta previa.[6–8] At the same time, the pregnant women have an immature cervix during the second-trimester.[9,10] Clinically, prophylactic uterine artery embolization (UAE) can be used to treat antenatal or intrapartum hemorrhage,[11,12] and double balloon catheter (DBC) is an effective method for cervical ripening.[13,14] Using DBC for cervical ripening followed by prophylactic UAE may be a promising strategy for second-trimester pregnancy termination in cases of complete placenta previa.

With the aim for investigating the method of prophylactic UAE and DBC, we analyzed 7 patients with complete placental previa who underwent pregnancy termination in the second-trimester from March 1st, 2021 to August 31st, 2023 in our birth center.

2. Methods

2.1. Ethical approval

The study protocol was retrospective research, and the study protocol was approved by the Ethics Committee of Maternal and Child Health Hospital of Hubei Province ([2023] IEC [084]). All pregnant women requiring termination of pregnancy in our work signed informed consent.

2.2. Participants

This study was conducted from March 1st, 2021 to August 31st, 2023 at our birth center, a tertiary-care teaching hospital located in Wuhan city, central China. All patients were diagnosed with complete placenta previa status and PAS. The reasons for terminating pregnancy included intrauterine death or fetal malformation in the second-trimester.

2.3. Data sources

This retrospective research was based on the data collected from the birth center of a tertiary-level public hospital in Wuhan city, Hubei province, China, with annual number of newborn babies of around 25,000 in recent 5 years. The data were collected from the hospital’s information system from March 2021 to June 2023. Inclusion criteria included: (1) gestation of weeks: from 14 + 0 w to 27 + 6 w; (2) the placenta covers the internal cervix completely by trans-vaginal ultrasound; (3) UAE, DBC and/or dilation and evacuation step-by-step; (4) delivery in our birth center. Exclusion criteria included: (1) gestation of weeks: <14 + 0 w or more than 27 + 6 w; (2) the placenta was normal or the edge of the placenta reached the cervix; (3) absence of UAE or DBC treatment. There were only 7 cases were diagnosed with complete placenta previa status and PAS by ultrasound and magnetic resonance imaging.

2.4. The methods of labor induction

The placentas of all cases were routinely examined by trans-vaginal ultrasound after admission. At first, oral mifepristone (50 mg, Bid*3d) was applied in all termination cases in the second-trimester. Next, ethacridine lactate (100 mg) was injected into the amniotic cavity under ultrasound guidance if necessary. Then, prophylactic UAE was used after medical induction, and DBC was applied subsequently. Dilation and evacuation were performed based on the cervical condition, bleeding or infection indices as needed.

UAE[15]: Patients were placed in the supine position. The inguinal area was disinfected and draped. Lidocaine was then administered for local anesthesia prior to surgery. The surgeon punctured the right femoral artery according to Seldinger method, inserted the 5F catheter sheath and catheter into the left uterine artery for arterial subtraction, perfused Gentamicin 80,000 units, and embolized with gelatin sponge. The right uterine artery was cannulated, perfused and embolized as well. After the operation, the catheter and sheath were pulled out before applying the local pressure bandage. The right lower limb was immobilized for 6 hours, and perioperative antibiotics were given to prevent infection.

DBC[10]: The patient emptied the bladder and took the lithotomy position. Then the obstetricians gently placed the speculum into the vagina, disinfected the cervix, inserted the DBC into the cervix till both balloons entered the cervical canal, injected 40 mL of normal saline into the “U” balloon, pulled the “V” balloon out of the cervical external orifice and injected 40 mL of normal saline into it. At last, 2 balloons were added normal saline till the volume of both balloons reached 80 mL (Fig. 1). If the patient was unbearable for 80 mL of normal saline, then 10 to 60 mL of normal saline should be drawn out from both balloons. The DBC duration varied between 12 and 24 hours according to the cervical condition.

Double balloon catheter and placenta previa on ultrasound: U balloon: the balloon in the cervix in the double balloon catheter; V balloon: the balloon in the vagina in the double balloon catheter.

2.5. Observational indexes

The observation indexes included maternal age, gravidity, parity, body mass index (BMI), gestational age, the history of cesarean section, placenta position, the reasons for induction, method of labor induction, induction time (the time from the beginning to UAE, the time from UAE to DBC, the time from DBC to curettage or delivery). Other factors such as antenatal bleeding, postpartum hemorrhage, curettage, manual removal of placenta, puerperal morbidity, inpatient days, and routine blood work (including white blood cell, hemoglobin, C-reactive protein on admission and on discharge) were also considered.

2.6. Statistical analysis

The data analysis was conducted using SPSS Statistics 28.0 software. Continuous variables were directly expressed as a range. Categorical variables were expressed as number (n).

They underwent complex second-trimester termination procedures involving UAE combined with DBC, with or without dilation and evacuation under ultrasound monitoring. The prepregnant BMI ranged from 21.3 to 24.1 kg/m2, and the BMI on admission ranged from 22.3 to 25.2 kg/m2. The number of gestations, parity ranged from 1 to 7. Among them, 4 cases had previous history of one, 1, 2 and 3 cesarean delivery (CD), 2 cases had previous history of 1 and 2 vaginal delivery, 1 cases had previous history of 1 ectopic pregnancy, 2 cases had previous history of 1 and 4 artificial abortions. 4. Discussion

With the introduction of the three-child policy in China, an increasing number of women with a history of CD or vaginal delivery are opting for another pregnancy.[16] However, as maternal age increases, there is a higher incidence of fetal malformations and intrauterine death.[3,4] Patients presenting with complete placenta previa and PAS, who require an induced abortion during the second-trimester, face a high risk of substantial hemorrhage and infection. These risks are largely attributable to the immature cervical condition resulting from extended induction time during delivery.[8,17] Hysterotomy can be a relatively safe and effective approach for patients with complete placental previa and PAS, particularly for those beyond 16 weeks of gestation.[7] However, this procedure can potentially impact future pregnancy. It is challenging to implement vaginal delivery for the termination of pregnancy during the second trimester for patients with complete placental previa and PAS.

In China, conservative treatment for second-trimester termination includes medical abortion methods using mifepristone, misoprostol and Ethacridine Lactate.[8,18] These drugs play a role in softening and dilating the cervix, thus promoting fetal delivery.[19] However, these drugs for induced labor not only have an uncontrollable induction time but also carry a low success rate for abortion. Our previous observation found that the success rate of the combined therapy by mifepristone and misoprostol/ethacridine lactate was 88.2% (30/34) for termination of second-trimester pregnancy with placenta previa, 4 cases showed prenatal hemorrhage up to 400 mL and underwent emergency UAE + DBC. Placental edge crossed the cervical ostia is 1.7 cm is the optimal cutoff value for the diagnosis of prenatal hemorrhage, and its sensitivity and specificity are 75.0% and 86.7% respectively.[8]

Prophylactic UAE is widely used to prevent bleeding in cases of abnormally invasive placenta with placenta previa, cesarean scar pregnancy, and large fibroids.[20–22] The use of prophylactic UAE for the termination of second-trimester pregnancy with placenta previa is controversial.[7,12] Wang et al[12] investigated 15 patients with placenta previa who underwent second-trimester pregnancy termination by prophylactic UAE. Their findings revealed no significant improvement in outcomes of second-trimester abortion including abortion rates, bleeding volume and induction-to-abortion time. Hu Q et al[7] observed 20 cases in which planned hysterotomy was performed after prophylactic UAE for patients with placenta previa and previous CD, who were undergoing second-trimester. Among them, 3 were required to undergo hysterectomy due to life-threatening bleeding. In our observation, prophylactic UAE was effective in all 7 cases to prevent antenatal and intrapartum hemorrhage.

Handling an immature cervix in the second-trimester presents another challenge. DBC serves as a viable alternative method for inducing labor with unfavorable cervix conditions, which has been a commonly used induction for the term pregnancies,[23] prolonged pregnancies,[24] vaginal birth after CD,[25] high risk of uterine hyperstimulation,[26] and oligohydramnios.[27] DBC in labor carries a low risk of uterine hyperstimulation and leads to high maternal satisfaction.[13] Regarding the unfavorable cervix in the second-trimester, the duration of DBC ranges from 12 hours to 24 hours, which promotes cervical ripening.[10] In our study, only patient 3, who underwent UAE + DBC, experienced antenatal high fever (38.5 °C), albeit with no sepsis. The reason for this might be the prolonged duration from ethacridine to UAE and from UAE to DBC (18 hours, 56 hours, respectively), and duration of DBC was too short (16 hours) for adequate pain relief by pethidine. The effect of pain relief through neuraxial analgesia proved effective when the duration from UAE to DBC was zero hours.

Dilation and evacuation is one of the most common invasive surgeries and can be performed on a pregnant or nonpregnant patient and be either diagnostic or therapeutic around the world.[7,28–30] Patients seeking elective termination or managing a missed, incomplete, or inevitable abortion with <14 weeks of estimated gestational age could opt for these surgical procedures or medical management. Dilation and evacuation is generally not recommended for patients with an estimated gestational age >14 weeks,[28] as these procedures are associated with pain, hemorrhage, DIC and other side effects.[31–34] Foley catheter proved to be an effective alternative to osmotic dilators for cervical preparation before second-trimester dilation and evacuation.[35] In our study, only 1 case (patient 2) experienced natural delivery of the placenta and fetal following spontaneous detachment of DBC, while the remaining 6 cases all opted for dilation and evacuation after 16 to 24 hours duration of DBC due to antenatal hemorrhage. Does prenatal bleeding reoccur after UAE? Wang et al[36] observed that patients with CSP who underwent curettage within 24 hours after UAE had a 5.0% risk of intraoperative bleeding. However, this risk increased to 19.4% for those with a treatment interval longer than 72 hours. Therefore, the fetus and placenta should be removed within 24 hours to prevent bleeding and/or infection. These findings are consistent with our study.

5. Conclusion

The adjuvant therapy of prophylactic UAE combined with DBC is a preferred choice for patients with complete placenta previa undergoing pregnancy termination in the second-trimester. The use of dilation and evacuation should be based on the cervical condition, bleeding, or infection.

6. Limitation

Studies have shown that UAE is associated with premature ovarian failure, and when combined with dilation and curettage, can be linked to Asherman syndrome.[37] Jitsumori et al suggest that previous UAE is a significant risk factor for placenta accreta spectrum.[38] As our sample size was small, further multi-center research is needed to explore the effects of different management strategies on patients’ subsequent fertility.
