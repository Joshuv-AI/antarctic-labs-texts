47 C.F.R. § 90.439 — Inspection of stations.
Citation: us:cfr:47:90.439
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-90.439
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 90 (Private Land Mobile Radio Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All stations and records of stations in these services shall be made available for inspection at any reasonable time and any time while the station is in operation upon reasonable request of an authorized representative of the Commission.
