46 C.F.R. § 128.320 — Exhaust systems.
Citation: us:cfr:46:128.320
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-128.320
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 128 (Marine Engineering: Equipment And Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No diesel-engine exhaust system need meet the material requirements in § 58.10-5(d)(1)(i) of this chapter if the installation is certified as required by § 128.220(c) of this part.
