43 C.F.R. § 429.23 — How does Reclamation determine use fees?
Citation: us:cfr:43:429.23
Creator: Bureau Of Reclamation, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-429.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 429 (Use Of Bureau Of Reclamation Land, Facilities, And Waterbodies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The use fee is based on a valuation or by competitive bidding. Use fees may be adjusted as deemed appropriate by Reclamation to reflect current conditions, as provided in the use authorization.
