10 U.S.C. § 582 — Warrant officer active-duty list: exclusions
Citation: us:usc:10:582
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section582
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 33A (Appointment, Promotion, And Involuntary Separation And Retirement For Members On The Warrant Officer Active-Duty List). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Warrant officers in the following categories are not subject to this chapter:
(1)
Reserve warrant officers—
(A)
on active duty as authorized under section 115(a)(1)(B) or 115(b)(1) of this title, or excluded from counting for active duty end strengths under section 115(i) of this title; or
(B)
on full-time National Guard duty.
(2)
Retired warrant officers on active duty (other than retired warrant officers who were recalled to active duty before February 1, 1992, and have served continuously on active duty since that date).
(3)
Students enrolled in the Army Physician’s Assistant Program.
