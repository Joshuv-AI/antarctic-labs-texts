SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

Agenda

Wednesday, October 21, 2026

    The Charter Halibut Management Committee will meet to make 
recommendations on management measures to analyze for the 2027 season. 
First, the Alaska Department of Fish and Game (ADF&G) will review the 
final charter halibut harvest and effort numbers for 2025 and 
preliminary harvest and effort numbers for 2026. Then the committee 
will discuss the development of the 2027 management measures for ADF&G 
analysis. The meeting will conclude with any other business. Public 
testimony will then be taken at the chair's discretion, and the meeting 
will conclude with other business and upcoming meetings. The agenda is 
subject to change, and the latest version will be posted prior to the 
meeting, along with meeting materials, https://meetings.npfmc.org/Meeting/Details/7156.
