16 C.F.R. § 1242.9 — Severability.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1242.9
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1242.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1242 (Safety Standard For Nursing Pillows). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this part are separate and severable from one another. If any provision is stayed or determined to be invalid, it is the Commission's intention that the remaining provisions shall continue in effect.
