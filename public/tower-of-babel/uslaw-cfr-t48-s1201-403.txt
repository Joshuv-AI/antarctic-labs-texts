48 C.F.R. § 1201.403 — 1201.403 Individual deviations.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1201.403
Creator: Chapter 12—Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1201.403
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1201 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The head of the contracting activity (HCA), or designee with a rank that is no lower than that of a Senior Executive Service (SES) official, may authorize individual deviations to the FAR and TAR, unless FAR 1.405(e) applies.
