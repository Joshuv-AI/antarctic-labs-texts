36 C.F.R. § 61.5 — Grants to State programs.
Citation: us:cfr:36:61.5
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-61.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 61 (Procedures For State, Tribal, And Local Government Historic Preservation Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each State with an approved State program is eligible for grants-in-aid from the Historic Preservation Fund (HPF).
(b) The National Park Service (NPS) will administer HPF matching grants-in-aid in accordance with the Act, OMB Circular A-133 and 43 CFR part 12, and related guidance that NPS issues. Failure by a State program to meet these requirements is cause for comment and appropriate action by the Secretary.
