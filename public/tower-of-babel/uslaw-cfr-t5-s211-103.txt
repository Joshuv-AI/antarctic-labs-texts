5 C.F.R. § 211.103 — Administration of preference.
Citation: us:cfr:5:211.103
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-211.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 211 (Veteran Preference). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies are responsible for making all preference determinations except for preference based on a common law marriage. Such a claim must be referred to OPM's General Counsel for decision.
