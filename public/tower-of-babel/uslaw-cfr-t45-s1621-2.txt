45 C.F.R. § 1621.2 — Grievance Committee.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1621.2
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1621.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1621 (Client Grievance Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The governing body of a recipient shall establish a grievance committee or committees, composed of lawyer and client members of the governing body, in approximately the same proportion in which they are on the governing body.
