20 U.S.C. § 1140h — Rule of construction
Citation: us:usc:20:1140h
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1140h
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 28 (Higher Education Resources And Student Assistance); Subchapter VII (Graduate And Postsecondary Improvement Programs); Part D (Programs To Provide Students With Disabilities With A Quality Higher Education); Subpart 2 (Transition Programs For Students With Intellectual Disabilities Into Higher Education). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this subpart shall be construed to reduce or expand—
(1)
the obligation of a State or local educational agency to provide a free appropriate public education, as defined in section 1401 of this title; or
(2)
eligibility requirements under any Federal, State, or local disability law, including the Americans with Disabilities Act of 1990 (42 U.S.C. 12101 et seq.), the Rehabilitation Act of 1973 (29 U.S.C. 701 et seq.), or the Developmental Disabilities Assistance and Bill of Rights Act of 2000 (42 U.S.C. 15001 et seq.).
