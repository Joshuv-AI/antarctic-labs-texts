31 C.F.R. § 353.38 — Payment during lifetime of owner of beneficiary bond.
Citation: us:cfr:31:353.38
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-353.38
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 353 (Regulations Governing Definitive United States Savings Bonds, Series Ee And Hh). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A savings bond registered in beneficiary form will be paid to the registered owner during his or her lifetime upon surrender with an appropriate request. Upon payment (as determined in § 353.43) the beneficiary will cease to have any interest in the bond.
