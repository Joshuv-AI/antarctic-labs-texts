46 C.F.R. § 154.1160 — Monitor coverage of system.
Citation: us:cfr:46:154.1160
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-154.1160
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 154 (Safety Standards For Self-Propelled Vessels Carrying Bulk Liquefied Gases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The coverage of each dry chemical system monitor under § 154.1150 must not exceed:
(a) 10 m (32.8 ft.) at 10 kg/sec (22 lb/sec);
(b) 30 m (98.4 ft.) at 25 kg/sec (55 lb/sec);
(c) 40 m (131.2 ft.) at 45 kg/sec (99 lb/sec);
(d) An interpolation between 10 m (32.8 ft.) at 10 kg/sec (22 lb/sec) and 30 m (98.4 ft.) at 25 kg/sec (55 lb/sec); or
(e) An interpolation between 30 m (98.4 ft.) at 25 kg/sec (55 lb/sec) and 40 m (131.2 ft.) at 45 kg/sec (99 lb/sec).
