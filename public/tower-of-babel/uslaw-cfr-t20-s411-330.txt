20 C.F.R. § 411.330 — How will SSA evaluate an EN's performance?
Citation: us:cfr:20:411.330
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-411.330
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 411 (The Ticket To Work And Self-Sufficiency Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) We will periodically review the results of the work of each EN to ensure effective quality assurance in the provision of services by ENs.
(b) In conducting such a review, we will solicit and consider the views of the individuals the EN serves and the PM which monitors the EN.
(c) ENs must make the results of these periodic reviews available to disabled beneficiaries to assist them in choosing among available ENs.
