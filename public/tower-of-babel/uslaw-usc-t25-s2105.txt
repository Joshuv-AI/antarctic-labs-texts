25 U.S.C. § 2105 — Effect of other provisions
Citation: us:usc:25:2105
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section2105
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 23 (Development Of Tribal Mineral Resources). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall affect, nor shall any Minerals Agreement approved pursuant to this chapter be subject to or limited by, sections 396a to 396g of this title, or any other law authorizing the development or disposition of the mineral resources of an Indian or Indian tribe.
