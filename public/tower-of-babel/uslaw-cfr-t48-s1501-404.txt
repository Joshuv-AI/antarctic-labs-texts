48 C.F.R. § 1501.404 — 1501.404 Class deviations.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1501.404
Creator: Chapter 15—Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1501.404
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1501 (General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Requests for class deviations to the FAR and the EPAAR shall be submitted to the HCA for processing in accordance with FAR 1.404 and this section. Requests shall include the same type of information prescribed in 1501.403 for individual deviations.
