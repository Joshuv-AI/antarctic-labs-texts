50 C.F.R. § 20.64 — Foreign export permits.
Citation: us:cfr:50:20.64
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-20.64
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 20 (Migratory Bird Hunting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person shall import, possess or transport, any migratory game birds killed in a foreign country unless such birds are accompanied by export permits, tags, or other documentation required by applicable foreign laws or regulations.
