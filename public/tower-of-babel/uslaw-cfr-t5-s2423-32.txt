5 C.F.R. § 2423.32 — Burden of proof before the Administrative Law Judge.
Citation: us:cfr:5:2423.32
Creator: Federal Labor Relations Authority, General Counsel Of The Federal Labor Relations Authority And Federal Service Impasses Panel
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-2423.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 2423 (Unfair Labor Practice Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The General Counsel shall present the evidence in support of the complaint and have the burden of proving the allegations of the complaint by a preponderance of the evidence. The Respondent shall have the burden of proving any affirmative defenses that it raises to the allegations in the complaint.
