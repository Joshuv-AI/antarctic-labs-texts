49 C.F.R. § 29.206 — May the Department request and obtain an extension of time of the 45-day review period?
Citation: us:cfr:49:29.206
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-29.206
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 29 (Tribal Transportation Self-Governance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Department may request an extension of time before the expiration of the 45-day review period. The Tribe may either grant or deny the Department's request for an extension. Any grant of extension of time must be in writing and signed by a person authorized by the Tribe to grant the extension before the expiration of the 45-day review period.
