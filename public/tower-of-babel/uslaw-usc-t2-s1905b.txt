2 U.S.C. § 1905b — Notification of obligation
Citation: us:usc:2:1905b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section1905b
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 29 (Capitol Police); Subchapter I (Organization And Administration); Part A (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Beginning on March 15, 2022, the Chief of the United States Capitol Police shall provide written notice to the Committee on Appropriations of the Senate and the Committee on Appropriations of the House of Representatives before any obligation of funds under section 1905(a)(2) of this title that equals or exceeds $100,000.
(b)
This section shall apply with respect to fiscal year 2022 and each fiscal year thereafter.
