12 C.F.R. § 617.7525 — May FCA use other enforcement actions?
Citation: us:cfr:12:617.7525
Creator: Farm Credit Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-617.7525
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 617 (Borrower Rights). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

FCA may issue a distressed loan restructuring directive in addition to, or instead of, any other action allowed by law, including cease and desist proceedings, civil money penalties, or the granting or conditioning of any application or other requests by the System institution.
