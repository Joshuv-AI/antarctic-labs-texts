Towards a parameter-free analysis of the QCD chiral phase transition and its universal critical behavior
Sabarnya Mitra 1, Frithjof Karsch 1

Abstract
To quantify the universal properties of chiral phase transition in (2+1)-flavor QCD, we use an improved, renormalized order parameter for the chiral symmetry breaking. We construct ratios of this divergence-free order parameter from its values for different pairs of light quark masses. From this, we determine in a parameter-independent manner, the chiral phase transition temperature T c T_{c} and the associated critical exponent δ \delta of the universality class. We present first numerical results of these calculations on N τ = 8 N_{\tau}=8 lattices, with staggered fermions.

With light quark mass

4
Conclusion and Outlook

We have demonstrated in this work, that as a function of
T
, the re-scaled order parameter
M
exhibits a unique intersection point for different values of light quark mass
m_{\ell}
, or equivalently
H
for small enough values of
m_{\ell}
where the sub-leading contributions are negligible. We also showed that evaluating its ratios allows determining
T_{c}
and critical exponent
\delta
, without having the need to make any apriori assumption about the universality class. However, we still need to improve computational precision by incorporating more
T,H
data points close to critical point, besides checking the finite-volume effects and taking the continuum limit. This is to estimate

\delta
with accuracy good enough to distinguish between
U(2)\times U(2)
and
O(4)
universality classes.
