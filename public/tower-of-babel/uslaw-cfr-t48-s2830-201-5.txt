48 C.F.R. § 2830.201-5 — 2830.201-5 Waiver.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2830 (Cost Accounting Standards Administration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The SPE is the agency head for the purposes of FAR 30.201-5. Pursuant to FAR 30.201-5, this authority may not be delegated to any official in the agency below the senior contract policymaking level.
