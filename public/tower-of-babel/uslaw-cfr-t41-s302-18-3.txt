41 C.F.R. § 302-18.3 — -18.3 Factors GSA will consider in approving a request for a relocation expenses test program.
Citation: us:cfr:41:302-18.3
Creator: Chapter 302—Relocation Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-302-18.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 302-18 (18—Relocation Expenses Test Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following factors will be considered:
(a) Potential savings to the Government.
(b) Application of results to other agencies.
(c) Feasibility of successful implementation.
(d) Number of tests, if any, already authorized to the same activity.
(e) Whether the request meets the requirements of § 302-18.2.
(f) Other agency requests under consideration at the time of submission.
(g) Whether the proposed test is duplicative of any existing test programs.
