11 C.F.R. § 9032.8 — Political committee.
Citation: us:cfr:11:9032.8
Creator: Federal Election Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-11/section-9032.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 11 (Federal Elections), Part 9032 (Definitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Political committee means any committee, club, association, organization or other group of persons (whether or not incorporated) which accepts contributions or incurs qualified campaign expenses for the purpose of influencing, or attempting to influence, the nomination of any individual for election to the office of President of the United States.
