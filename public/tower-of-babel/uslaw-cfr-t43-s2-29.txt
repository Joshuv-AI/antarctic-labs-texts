43 C.F.R. § 2.29 — When will the bureau not notify a submitter of a request for their possibly confidential information?
Citation: us:cfr:43:2.29
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2.29
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2 (Freedom Of Information Act; Records And Testimony). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The notice requirements of § 2.28 of this subpart will not apply if:
(a) The information has been lawfully published or officially made available to the public;
(b) Disclosure of the information is required or prohibited by a statute other than the FOIA or by a regulation (other than this part) issued in accordance with the requirements of Executive Order 12600; or
(c) The bureau has exercised due diligence to notify the submitter, but its efforts were unsuccessful.
