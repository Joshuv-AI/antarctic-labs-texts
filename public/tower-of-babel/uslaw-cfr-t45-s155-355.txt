45 C.F.R. § 155.355 — Right to appeal.
Citation: us:cfr:45:155.355
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-155.355
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 155 (Exchange Establishment Standards And Other Related Standards Under The Affordable Care Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Individual appeals. The Exchange must include the notice of the right to appeal and instructions regarding how to file an appeal in any eligibility determination notice issued to the applicant in accordance with § 155.310(g), § 155.330(e)(1)(ii), or § 155.335(h)(1)(ii).
