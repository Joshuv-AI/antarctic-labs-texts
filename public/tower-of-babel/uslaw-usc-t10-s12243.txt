10 U.S.C. § 12243 — Warrant officers: suspension of laws for promotion or mandatory retirement or separation during war or emergency
Citation: us:usc:10:12243
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section12243
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel Generally); Chapter 1207 (Warrant Officers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In time of war, or of emergency declared after May 29, 1954, by Congress or the President, the President may suspend the operation of any provision of law relating to promotion, or mandatory retirement or separation, of permanent reserve warrant officers of any armed force.
