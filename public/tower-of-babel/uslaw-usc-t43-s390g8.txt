43 U.S.C. § 390g–8 — Interstate transfer of water from Arkansas
Citation: us:usc:43:390g–8
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section390g–8
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 12 (Reclamation And Irrigation Of Lands By Federal Government); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No funds authorized to be appropriated by sections 390g to 390g–8 of this title shall be used for any activities associated with:
(1)
the interstate transfer of water from the State of Arkansas; or
(2)
the study or demonstration of the potential for the interstate transfer of water from the State of Arkansas.
