33 C.F.R. § 80.715 — Savannah River.
Citation: us:cfr:33:80.715
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-80.715
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 80 (Colregs Demarcation Lines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A line drawn from the southernmost tank on Hilton Head Island charted in approximate position latitude 32°06.7′ N., longitude 80°49.3′ W., to Bloody Point Range Rear Light; thence to Tybee Light.
