10 U.S.C. § 195 — Defense Automated Printing Service: applicability of Federal printing requirements
Citation: us:usc:10:195
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section195
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization And General Military Powers); Chapter 8 (Defense Agencies And Department Of Defense Field Activities); Subchapter I (Common Supply And Service Activities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Defense Automated Printing Service shall comply fully with the requirements of section 501 of title 44 relating to the production and procurement of printing, binding, and blank-book work.
