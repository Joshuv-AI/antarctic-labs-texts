42 C.F.R. § 457.120 — Public involvement in program development.
Citation: us:cfr:42:457.120
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-457.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 457 (Allotments And Grants To States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A State plan must include a description of the method the State uses to—
(a) Involve the public in both the design and initial implementation of the program;
(b) Ensure ongoing public involvement once the State plan has been implemented; and
(c) Ensure interaction with Indian Tribes and organizations in the State on the development and implementation of the procedures required at § 457.125.
