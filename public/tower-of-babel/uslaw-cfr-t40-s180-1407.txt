40 C.F.R. § 180.1407 — Gluconobacter cerinus strain BC18B and Hanseniaspora uvarum strain BC18Y; exemptions from the requirement of a tolerance.
Citation: us:cfr:40:180.1407
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-180.1407
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 180 (Tolerances And Exemptions For Pesticide Chemical Residues In Food). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Exemptions from the requirement of tolerances are established for residues of Gluconobacter cerinus strain BC18B and Hanseniaspora uvarum strain BC18Y in or on all food commodities when used in accordance with label directions and good agricultural practices.
