46 C.F.R. § 131.935 — Prevention of oil pollution.
Citation: us:cfr:46:131.935
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-131.935
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 131 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each vessel must be operated in compliance with—
(a) Section 311 of the Federal Water Pollution Control Act, as amended (33 U.S.C. 1321); and
(b) 33 CFR parts 151, 155, and 156.
