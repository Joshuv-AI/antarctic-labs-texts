40 C.F.R. § 279.59 — Management of residues.
Citation: us:cfr:40:279.59
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-279.59
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 279 (Standards For The Management Of Used Oil). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Owners and operators who generate residues from the storage, processing, or re-refining of used oil must manage the residues as specified in § 279.10(e).
