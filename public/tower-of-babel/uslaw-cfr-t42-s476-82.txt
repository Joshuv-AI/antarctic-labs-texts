42 C.F.R. § 476.82 — Continuation of functions not assumed by QIOs.
Citation: us:cfr:42:476.82
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-476.82
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 476 (Quality Improvement Organization Review). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any of the duties and functions under Part B of Title XI of the Act for which a QIO has not assumed responsibility under its contract with CMS must be performed in the manner and to the extent otherwise provided for under the Act or in regulations.
