22 U.S.C. § 5801 — Definition of independent states
Citation: us:usc:22:5801
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section5801
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 67 (Freedom For Russia And Emerging Eurasian Democracies And Open Markets Support). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For purposes of this Act, the terms “independent states of the former Soviet Union” and “independent states” mean the following: Armenia, Azerbaijan, Belarus, Georgia, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Moldova, Russia, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan, Ukraine, and Uzbekistan.
