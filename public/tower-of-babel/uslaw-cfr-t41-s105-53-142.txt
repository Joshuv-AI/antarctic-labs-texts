41 C.F.R. § 105-53.142 — -53.142 Office of Public Affairs.
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-53.142
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-53.142
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-53 (53—Statement Of Organization And Functions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Office of Public Affairs, headed by the Associate Administrator for Public Affairs, is responsible for the planning, implementation, and coordination of GSA public information and public events and employee communication activities, and managing and operating the Consumer Information Center.
