52 U.S.C. § 10312 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:52:10312
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title52-section10312
Context: United States Code, Title 52 (Voting And Elections); Chapter 103 (Enforcement Of Voting Rights). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are hereby authorized to be appropriated such sums as are necessary to carry out the provisions of chapters 103 to 107 of this title.
