31 C.F.R. § 250.1 — Scope of regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:31:250.1
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-250.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 250 (Payment On Account Of Awards Of The Foreign Claims Settlement Commission Of The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part govern payment by the Department of the Treasury on awards made and certified to the Secretary of the Treasury by the Foreign Claims Settlement Commission under the International Claims Settlement Act of 1949, as amended (22 U.S.C. 1621 et seq.), and Title II of the War Claims Act of 1948 (50 U.S.C. App. 2017 et seq.).
