Correction to Supporting Information for Huang et al., Stochastic game dynamics under demographic fluctuations
Published: 2026

Correction to Supporting Information for “Stochastic game dynamics under demographic fluctuations,” by Weini Huang, Christoph Hauert, and Arne Traulsen, which published July 6, 2015; 10.1073/pnas.1418745112 (Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. U.S.A. 112, 9064–9069).

The authors note that “the diffusion approximation in SI Appendix, Eqs. S4–S12 appeared incorrectly. The original submission confounded the scaling of the system size expansion and that of the population density/size. Both used the symbol M. This resulted in a wrong scaling of the competition term in SI Appendix, Eq. S7. We decided to split this scaling term into two and use V for the system size expansion and M to scale the population densities.” Equations 1 to 12 in SI Appendix, section 2 have been corrected accordingly and Equations 13 and 14 have been added. In addition, a typo was fixed in section 3 [E_2 instead of E_3 in (iii) on p. 7]. Finally, an additional reference, reference 4, has been added. The authors note: “At the time of working on this project, we were aware of the work by Constable and McKane through personal communications but weren’t able to include the reference because their paper appeared only a little later.” The SI Appendix has been updated.
