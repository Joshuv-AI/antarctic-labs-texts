36 C.F.R. § 1220.16 — What recorded information must be managed in accordance with the regulations in Subchapter B?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1220.16
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1220.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1220 (Federal Records; General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The requirements in Subchapter B apply to documentary materials that meet the definition of Federal records. See also Part 1222 of this subchapter.
