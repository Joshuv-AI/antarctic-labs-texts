42 U.S.C. § 8630 — Renewable fuels
Citation: us:usc:42:8630
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section8630
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 94 (Low-Income Energy Assistance); Subchapter II (Low-Income Home Energy Assistance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In providing assistance pursuant to this subchapter, a State, or any other person with which the State makes arrangements to carry out the purposes of this subchapter, may purchase renewable fuels, including biomass.
