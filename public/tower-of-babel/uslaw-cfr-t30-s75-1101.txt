30 C.F.R. § 75.1101 — Deluge-type water sprays, foam generators; main and secondary belt-conveyor drives.
Citation: us:cfr:30:75.1101
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-75.1101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 75 (Mandatory Safety Standards—Underground Coal Mines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Deluge-type water sprays or foam generators automatically actuated by rise in temperature, or other no less effective means approved by the Secretary of controlling fire, shall be installed at main and secondary belt-conveyor drives.
