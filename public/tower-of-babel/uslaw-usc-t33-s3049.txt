33 U.S.C. § 3049 — Recall to active duty
Citation: us:usc:33:3049
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section3049
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 43 (National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration Commissioned Officer Corps); Subchapter III (Separation And Retirement Of Officers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of chapter 39 of title 10 relating to recall of retired officers to active duty, including the limitations on such recalls, shall apply to commissioned officers of the Administration.
