2 C.F.R. § 2700.930 — Debarring official (SBA supplement to government-wide definition at 2 CFR 180.930).
Citation: us:cfr:2:2700.930
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-2700.930
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 2700 (Nonprocurement Debarment And Suspension). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For SBA, the debarring official for financial assistance programs is the Director of the Office of Credit Risk Management; for all other programs, the debarring official is the Associate General Counsel for Procurement Law.
