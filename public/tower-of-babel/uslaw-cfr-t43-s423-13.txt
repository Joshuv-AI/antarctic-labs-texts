43 C.F.R. § 423.13 — How will Reclamation establish periodic and regular closures?
Citation: us:cfr:43:423.13
Creator: Bureau Of Reclamation, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-423.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 423 (Public Conduct On Bureau Of Reclamation Facilities, Lands, And Waterbodies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Reclamation facilities, lands, or waterbodies that are closed periodically and regularly, regardless of the date of the initial closure, must be noticed as provided in § 423.12(a) only once, and at any time the schedule of closure is changed.
