6 U.S.C. § 214 — Separate budget request for Customs
Citation: us:usc:6:214
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title6-section214
Context: United States Code, Title 6 (Domestic Security); Chapter 1 (Homeland Security Organization); Subchapter IV (Border, Maritime, And Transportation Security); Part B (U.S. Customs And Border Protection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President shall include in each budget transmitted to Congress under section 1105 of title 31 a separate budget request for U.S. Customs and Border Protection.
