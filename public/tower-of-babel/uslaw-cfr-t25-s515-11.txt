25 C.F.R. § 515.11 — Penalties.
Citation: us:cfr:25:515.11
Creator: National Indian Gaming Commission, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-515.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 515 (Privacy Act Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person who makes a false statement in connection with any request for access to a record, or an amendment thereto, under this part, is subject to the penalties prescribed in 18 U.S.C. 494 and 495.
