14 C.F.R. § 382.72 — Must carriers allow a service animal to accompany a passenger with a disability?
Citation: us:cfr:14:382.72
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-382.72
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 382 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Disability In Air Travel). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must allow a service animal to accompany a passenger with a disability. You must not deny transportation to a service animal based on the animal's breed or type or on the basis that its carriage may offend or annoy carrier personnel or persons traveling on the aircraft.
