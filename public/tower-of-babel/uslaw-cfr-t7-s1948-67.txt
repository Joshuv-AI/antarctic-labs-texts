7 C.F.R. § 1948.67 — Procedure for designation.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1948.67
Creator: Rural Housing Service, Rural Business-Cooperative Service, And Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1948.67
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1948 (Rural Development). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Local governments may request the Governor of the State in which they are located to designate an area served by them as an energy impacted area.
(b) The Governor will define the geographic area of a designated area consistent with the nature of the impact and the socio-economic integration of the area.
(c) The Governor may designate an area as an energy impacted area based on the criteria contained in this subpart.
