33 C.F.R. § 149.135 — What should be marked on the cargo transfer system alarm switch?
Citation: us:cfr:33:149.135
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-149.135
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 149 (Deepwater Ports: Design, Construction, And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each switch for activating an alarm, and each audio or visual device for signaling an alarm, must be identified by the words “Oil Transfer Alarm” or “Natural Gas Transfer Alarm” in red letters at least 1 inch high on a yellow background.
