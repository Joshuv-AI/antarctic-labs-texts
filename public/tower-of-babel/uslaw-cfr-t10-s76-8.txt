10 C.F.R. § 76.8 — Information collection requirements: OMB approval not required.
Citation: us:cfr:10:76.8
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-76.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 76 (Certification Of Gaseous Diffusion Plants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The information collection requirements contained in this part of limited applicability apply to a wholly-owned instrumentality of the United States and affect fewer than ten respondents. Therefore, Office of Management and Budget clearance is not required pursuant to the Paperwork Reduction Act (44 U.S.C. 3501 et seq.).
