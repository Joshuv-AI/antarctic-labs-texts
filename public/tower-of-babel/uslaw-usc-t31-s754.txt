31 U.S.C. § 754 — Action by the Comptroller General
Citation: us:usc:31:754
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section754
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 7 (Government Accountability Office); Subchapter IV (Personnel Appeals Board). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When the Comptroller General has authority, the Comptroller General promptly shall carry out action the General Accounting Office Personnel Appeals Board 1 orders under section 753 of this title.
