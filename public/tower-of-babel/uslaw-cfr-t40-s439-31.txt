40 C.F.R. § 439.31 — Special definitions.

Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 439 (Pharmaceutical Manufacturing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For the purpose of this subpart:
(a) Chemical synthesis means using one or a series of chemical reactions in the manufacturing process of a specified product.
(b) Product means any pharmaceutical product manufactured by chemical synthesis.
