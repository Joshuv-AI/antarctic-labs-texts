12 C.F.R. § 238.152 — Credit exposure limits.
Citation: us:cfr:12:238.152
Creator: Federal Reserve System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-238.152
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 238 (Savings And Loan Holding Companies (Regulation Ll)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

General limit on aggregate net credit exposure. No covered company may have an aggregate net credit exposure to any counterparty that exceeds 25 percent of the tier 1 capital of the covered company.
