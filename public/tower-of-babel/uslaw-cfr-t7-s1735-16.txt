7 C.F.R. § 1735.16 — Minimum loan amount.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1735.16
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1735.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1735 (General Policies, Types Of Loans, Loan Requirements—Telecommunications Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Recognizing plant costs, the borrower's cost of system design, and RUS's administrative costs, RUS will not consider applications for loans of less than $50,000.
