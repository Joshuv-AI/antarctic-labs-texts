14 U.S.C. § 3731 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:14:3731
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section3731
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 37 (Coast Guard Reserve); Subchapter II (Personnel). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this subchapter—
(1)
“Reserve officer” means a commissioned officer in the Reserve, except an officer excluded by section 3732 of this title or a commissioned warrant officer; and
(2)
“discharged” means released from an appointment as a Reserve officer.
