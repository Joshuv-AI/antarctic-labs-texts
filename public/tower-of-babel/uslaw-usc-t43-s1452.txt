43 U.S.C. § 1452 — Deputy Secretary of the Interior; appointment
Citation: us:usc:43:1452
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section1452
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 31 (Department Of The Interior). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The position of Deputy Secretary is established in the Department of the Interior with appointment thereto by the President, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate.
