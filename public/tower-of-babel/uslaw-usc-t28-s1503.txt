28 U.S.C. § 1503 — Set-offs
Citation: us:usc:28:1503
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section1503
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part IV (Jurisdiction And Venue); Chapter 91 (United States Court Of Federal Claims). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The United States Court of Federal Claims shall have jurisdiction to render judgment upon any set-off or demand by the United States against any plaintiff in such court.
