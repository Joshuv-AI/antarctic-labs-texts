14 C.F.R. § 260.7 — Affirmative acceptance of an offer of alternative compensation.
Citation: us:cfr:14:260.7
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-260.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 260 (Refunds For Airline Fare And Ancillary Service Fees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A covered carrier must not deem a consumer to have accepted an offer for travel credits, vouchers, or other compensation in lieu of a refund under § 260.6(c) unless the consumer affirmatively agrees to the alternative form of compensation.
