22 U.S.C. § 2659 — State statutes to be procured
Citation: us:usc:22:2659
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section2659
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 38 (Department Of State). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of State shall procure from time to time such of the statutes of the several States as may not be in his office.
