49 C.F.R. § 800.38 — Consideration of comments received.
Citation: us:cfr:49:800.38
Creator: National Transportation Safety Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-800.38
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 800 (Administrative Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All timely comments are considered before final action is taken on a rulemaking proposal. Late filed comments may be considered to the extent practicable.
