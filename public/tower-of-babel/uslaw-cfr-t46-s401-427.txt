46 C.F.R. § 401.427 — Charge on past due accounts.
Citation: us:cfr:46:401.427
Creator: Coast Guard (Great Lakes Pilotage), Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-401.427
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 401 (Great Lakes Pilotage Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A charge of two percent (2%) per month must be paid on the opening monthly balance on accounts remaining unpaid over thirty (30) days after the billing date.
