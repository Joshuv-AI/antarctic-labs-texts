49 C.F.R. § 380.711 — Equipment.
Citation: us:cfr:49:380.711
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-380.711
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 380 (Special Training Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) All vehicles used in the behind-the-wheel training must comply with applicable Federal and State safety requirements.
(b) Training vehicles must be in the same group and type that driver-trainees intend to operate for their CDL skills test.
