25 C.F.R. § 67.2 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:25:67.2
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-67.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 67 (Preparation Of A Roll Of Independent Seminole Indians Of Florida). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part govern the compilation of a roll of persons who meet the requirements specified in section 7 of the Act who will be eligible to share in the distribution of a portion of the judgment funds awarded the Seminole Indians in Dockets 73, 151, and 73-A of the Indian Claims Commission.
