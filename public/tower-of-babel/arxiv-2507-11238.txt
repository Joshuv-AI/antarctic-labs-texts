Complexity of some modal logics of density (extended version)
Philippe Balbiani, Olivier Gasquet

Abstract
By using a selective filtration argument, we prove that the satisfiability problem of the unimodal logic of density is in 𝐄𝐗𝐏𝐓𝐈𝐌𝐄 \mathbf{EXPTIME} . By using a tableau-like approach, we prove that the satisfiability problem of the bimodal logic of weak density is in 𝐏𝐒𝐏𝐀𝐂𝐄 \mathbf{PSPACE} .



Introduction

For modal logics defined by
grammar axioms
of the form
\langle a_{1}\rangle\ldots\langle a_{m}\rangle p\rightarrow\langle b_{1}\rangle\ldots\langle b_{n}\rangle p
the satisfiability problem is known to be undecidable in general
[
4
]
while for some specific
grammar logics
, the satisfiability problem is known to be decidable in
\mathbf{NEXPTIME}

[
7
]
and even in
\mathbf{PSPACE}
, for instance the well-known
\mathbf{K}
,
\mathbf{KT}
,
\mathbf{K}4
or
\mathbf{S4}
, or also a logic like
\mathbf{K}+\lozenge p\leftrightarrow\lozenge\lozenge p

[
3
]
.

In this paper, we study the complexity of some modal logics defined by axioms of the form
\langle a\rangle p\rightarrow\langle b\rangle\langle c\rangle p
.
Firstly, by using a selective filtration argument, we prove that the satisfiability problem of the unimodal logic of density is in
\mathbf{EXPTIME}
.
Secondly, by using a tableau-like approach, we prove that the satisfiability problem of the bimodal logic of weak density is in
\mathbf{PSPACE}
.
Let
\mathbf{KDe}
be the modal logic
\mathbf{K}+\lozenge p\rightarrow\lozenge\lozenge p
.
In Section
1
, we prove that
\mathbf{KDe}
is
\mathbf{PSPACE}
-hard.
In Section
2
, we prove that
\mathbf{KDe}
is in
\mathbf{EXPTIME}
. Then, let
\mathbf{KDe_{a,b}}
be the modal logic
\mathbf{K}+\Diamond_{a}p\rightarrow\Diamond_{a}\Diamond_{b}p
, the rest of the paper is devoted to prove it to be
\mathbf{PSPACE}
-complete.

Despite their apparent simplicity, the exact complexity of satisfiability of density as well as that of multimodal logics with more complex weak forms of density remains open in full generality. Still, we showed that both selective filtration and our
