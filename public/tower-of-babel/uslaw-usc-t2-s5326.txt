2 U.S.C. § 5326 — “Member of the House” defined
Citation: us:usc:2:5326
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section5326
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 53 (House Of Representatives Members); Subchapter II (Employees). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in section 5324 of this title the phrase “Member of the House” shall mean a Representative, Representative-elect, Delegate, Delegate-elect, Resident Commissioner, or Resident Commissioner-elect.
