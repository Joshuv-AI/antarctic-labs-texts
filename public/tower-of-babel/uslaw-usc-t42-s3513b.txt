42 U.S.C. § 3513b — Working capital fund; availability for common personnel support services
Citation: us:usc:42:3513b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section3513b
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 43 (Department Of Health And Human Services); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Working Capital Fund of the Department of Health and Human Services shall on and after August 10, 1971, be available for expenses necessary for common personnel support services in the Washington area.
