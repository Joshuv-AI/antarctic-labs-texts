33 C.F.R. § 156.225 — Designation of lightering zones.
Citation: us:cfr:33:156.225
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-156.225
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 156 (Oil And Hazardous Material Transfer Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The District Commander is delegated the authority to designate lightering zones and their operating requirements, where they are necessary for safety or environmental protection. When a lightering zone has been designated, lightering and STS Operations in a given geographic area may only be conducted within the designated lightering zone.
