20 C.F.R. § 703.105 — Copies of forms of policies to be submitted with application.
Citation: us:cfr:20:703.105
Creator: Office Of Workers' Compensation Programs, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-703.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 703 (Insurance Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

With each application for authority to write insurance there shall be submitted for the approval of the Office copies of the forms of policies which the applicant proposes to issue in writing insurance under the LHWCA, or its extensions, to which shall be attached the appropriate endorsement to be used in connection therewith.
