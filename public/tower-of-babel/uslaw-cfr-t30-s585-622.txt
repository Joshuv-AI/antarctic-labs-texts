30 C.F.R. § 585.622 — How do I submit my COP?
Citation: us:cfr:30:585.622
Creator: Bureau Of Ocean Energy Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-585.622
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 585 (Renewable Energy On The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You must submit your COP to BOEM pursuant to § 585.111.
(b) You may submit information and a request for any project easement as part of your original COP submission or as a revision to your COP.
