1 C.F.R. § 8.2 — Orderly development.
Citation: us:cfr:1:8.2
Creator: Administrative Committee Of The Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-1/section-8.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 1 (General Provisions), Part 8 (Code Of Federal Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-23 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To assure orderly development of the Code of Federal Regulations along practical lines, the Director of the Federal Register may establish new titles in the Code and rearrange existing titles and subordinate assignments. However, before taking an action under this section, the Director shall consult with each agency directly affected by the proposed change.
