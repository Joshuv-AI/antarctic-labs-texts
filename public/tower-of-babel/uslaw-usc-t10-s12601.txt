10 U.S.C. § 12601 — Compensation: Reserve on active duty accepting from any person
Citation: us:usc:10:12601
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section12601
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel Generally); Chapter 1217 (Miscellaneous Rights And Benefits). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any Reserve who, before being ordered to active duty, was receiving compensation from any person may, while he is on that duty, receive compensation from that person.
