36 C.F.R. § 1002.11 — Picnicking.
Citation: us:cfr:36:1002.11
Creator: Presidio Trust
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1002.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1002 (Resource Protection, Public Use And Recreation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Picnicking is allowed, except in designated areas closed in accordance with § 1001.5 of this chapter. The Board may establish conditions for picnicking in areas where picnicking is allowed. Picnicking in violation of established conditions is prohibited.
