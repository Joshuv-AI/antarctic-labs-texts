12 C.F.R. § 1235.6 — Access to records.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1235.6
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1235.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1235 (Record Retention For Regulated Entities And Office Of Finance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each regulated entity and the Office of Finance shall make its records available promptly upon request by FHFA, at a location and in a form and manner acceptable to FHFA.
