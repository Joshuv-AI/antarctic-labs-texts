7 C.F.R. § 20.1 — General.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 20 (Export Sales Reporting Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations of this part 20 are issued under section 404 of the Agricultural Trade Act of 1978, as amended, to implement the export sales reporting requirements of section 602 of the Agricultural Trade Act of 1978, as amended.
