10 C.F.R. § 9.41 — Other rights and services.
Citation: us:cfr:10:9.41
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-9.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 9 (Public Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this subpart will be construed to entitle any person, as of right, to any service or to the disclosure of any record to which such person is not entitled under the FOIA.
