Projection Cascades of models of log del Pezzo surfaces
Muhammad Imran Qureshi

Abstract
. We introduce the notion of type-I projection cascade for a biregular model (infinite series) of log del Pezzo surfaces. We study the existence of type-I projection cascades for known classes of models of log del Pezzo surfaces, such that their images under their anti-canonical embeddings in some weighted projective space, can be described as codimension 4 and codimension 3 varieties. We obtain two cascades of length three and four cascades of length two, where each projection gives rise to a well-formed and quasismooth biregular model in lower codimension.



1.
Introduction

1.1.
Cascades of log del Pezzo surfaces and graded rings

An algebraic surface
X
with an ample anti-canonical class is called the del Pezzo surface. They are among one of the most studied mathematical structures in algebraic geometry. There are a total of 10 deformation families of smooth del Pezzo surfaces:
\mathbb{P}^{1}\times\mathbb{P}^{1},\mathbb{P}^{2},
and blow ups of
\mathbb{P}^{2}
in
d
general points for
1\leq d\leq 8:
forming a so called cascade of smooth del Pezzo surfaces. A singular del Pezzo surface is
usually called a log del Pezzo surface or an orbifold del Pezzo surface if
the singularities are cyclic quotient singularities; we use both terms interchangeably. The construction and classification
of log del Pezzo surfaces have been the subject of great interest over the years from various perspectives, see
[
JK01
,
CPS10
,
CS13
,
CH17
,
CK17
,
Qur19
,
CP20
,
Qur20
]
.

A log del Pezzo surface with large
anti-canonical linear system can be blown up successively to obtain a chain
of del Pezzo surfaces, called a cascade of del Pezzo surfaces
[
RS04
]
.
The blow up can be considered as a projection from a weighted projective space of a higher dimension to a weighted projective space of a lower dimension. In favorable cases, this leads to explicit descriptions of the images of
log del Pezzo surfaces under their anti-canonical embeddings in some weighted projective space, as low codimension Gorenstein varieties.

The cascade of log del Pezzo surfaces obtained by
blowing up
\mathbb{P}(1,1,3)
in
1\leq d\leq 8
points was studied by Reid and Suzuki in
[
RS04
]
. They explicitly described the Gorenstein construction
of these varieties in low codimension cases. In particular, they showed that del the Pezzo surfaces for
d
equal to
6,7,
and
8
have descriptions in terms of codimension 3 variety in
\mathbb{P}^{5}(w_{i})
defined by the maximal Pfaffians of
5\times 5
skew-symmetric matrix of general forms , codimension 2 complete intersection variety in
\mathbb{P}^{4}(w_{i})
and a hypersurface in
\mathbb{P}^{3}(w_{i})
respectively. Moreover, they are connected by type-I Gorenstein projection
[
PR04
]

from a smooth coordinate point of the ambient weighted projective space
\mathbb{P}(w_{i}),
followed by a deformation of the image of projection to a generic member of some
family, forming what we call a type-I projection cascade for log del Pezzo
surfaces, see Definition
1.2
. Also,
the case
d=5
can be described as a codimension 4 variety that can
be obtained as an unprojection of the Pfaffian del Pezzo surface that corresponds
to
d=6
.

1.2.
Aim and results

The aim of this paper is to study the type-I projection cascades for biregular models
of log del Pezzo surfaces, introduced in
[
Qur19
]
, obtained via algorithmic
developed in
[
Qur17
]
. For this purpose we
introduce the notion of type-I projection cascade for biregular
models of log del Pezzo surfaces and study if our biregular models from
[
Qur19
,
Qur24
]
may form a type-I projection cascade. A biregular model of log del Pezzo surfaces is an infinite series of such
surfaces parameterized by the positive integers, formally defined
as follows.

Definition 1.1
.

[
Qur19
]

A
biregular model of log del Pezzo surfaces

\mathbb{M}
is an infinite series of families of del Pezzo surfaces satisfying the following conditions.

(i)

A family of wellformed and quasismooth log del Pezzo surfaces exists for each parameter
r(n)
for all
n\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}
, where
r
is a linear function of

n
.

(ii)

The
ambient weighted projective space
\mathbb{P}(w_{i})
that contains the image of log del Pezzo surface under their (sub) anti-canonical embedding must
contain at least
one weight to be
r
, and rest of the weights
w_{i}
and the volume
(-K_{X})^{2}
are functions of
r
.

A simple example of a biregular model is an index
r+2
del Pezzo surface

\mathbb{P}(1,1,r)
that contains an orbifold point of type
\frac{1}{r}(1,1)
,
where
r=n\in\mathbb{Z}_{+}
.

Definition 1.2
.

A
type-I projection cascade of biregular models of log del
Pezzo surfaces
is a collection
\{\mathbb{M}_{1},\ldots,\mathbb{M}_{k}\}
of biregular models of log del Pezzo surface such that for any surface

in
\mathbb{M}_{1}
there exist a collection of surfaces
\{X_{i}^{r(n)}:X_{i}^{r(n)}\in\mathbb{M}_{i},2\leq i\leq k\}

(i)

X_{1}^{r(n)}
has image under their anti-canonical embedding described as a subvariety of
\mathbb{P}(1^{l},a_{1},a_{2},\ldots,a_{s})
where
l\geq k
and

del Pezzo surface

under their anti-canonical embedding is a subvariety of
\mathbb{P}(1^{l-i+1},a_{1},a_{2},\ldots,a_{s})

X_{i+1}^{r(n)}

(ii)

There exist type-I projection from a smooth coordinate point

3.1.6.

This is a one step cascade which starts at a codimension 4
\mathbb{P}^{2}\times\mathbb{P}^{2}
model
\mathbb{P}_{12}

\mathbb{PF}_{14}
. In model
\mathbb{P}_{12}
each surface
S

\mathbb{P}(1,2,r^{2},r+1,r+2,2r-1)=\mathbb{P}(x_{0},a,b,c,d,e,f).

The equations of
X
are given by the
2\times 2
minors of following matrix, where
F_{r+1}
and
F_{2r}

r+1
and
2r

\begin{pmatrix}x_{0}&a&b\\

p_{x_{0}}

F_{2r}

\widetilde{X}

\widetilde{X}\subset\mathbb{P}(2,r^{2},r+1,r+2,2r-1)

p_{x_{0}}

N

\Tom_{1}
format
[
BKR12
]

N

D

I_{D}:=<d,e,f,F_{2r}>

\frac{1}{r}

p_{x_{0}}

\widetilde{X}

\mathbb{P}^{2}\times\mathbb{P}^{2}

\mathbb{P}_{12}

\mathbb{Q}

\widetilde{X}

\mathbb{PF}_{14}

A

(2,3)

r+1

(4,5)
has degree
2r
in the matrix
N
