31 U.S.C. § 6206 — Data consistency and uniformity of data elements
Citation: us:usc:31:6206
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section6206
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 62 (Consolidated Federal Funds Report). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director shall designate a single organizational unit to provide for data consistency and uniform reporting of data elements.
