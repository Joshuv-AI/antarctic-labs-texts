40 C.F.R. § 428.40 — Applicability; description of the latex rubber subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:428.40
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-428.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 428 (Rubber Manufacturing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to discharges of pollutants resulting from the manufacture of latex rubber.
