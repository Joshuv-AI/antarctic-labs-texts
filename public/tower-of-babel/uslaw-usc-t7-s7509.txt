7 U.S.C. § 7509 — Relationship to other programs
Citation: us:usc:7:7509
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section7509
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 102 (Emergency Food Assistance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Section 2013(b) of this title shall not apply with respect to the distribution of commodities under this chapter.
(b)
Except as otherwise provided in section 7504 of this title, none of the commodities distributed under this chapter shall be sold or otherwise disposed of in commercial channels in any form.
