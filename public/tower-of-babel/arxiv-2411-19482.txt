Abstract
As we all know, the k k -ary n n -cube is a highly efficient interconnect network topology structure. It is also a concept of great significance, with a broad range of applications spanning both mathematics and computer science. In this paper, we study the existence of Hamiltonian cycles passing through prescribed matchings in k k -ary n n -cubes, and obtain the following result. For n ≥ 5 n\geq 5 and k ≥ 4 k\geq 4 , every matching with at most 4 ​ n − 20 4n-20 edges is contained in a Hamiltonian cycle in the k k -ary n n -cube.

Basic definitions and results

The vertex set and edge set of a graph
G
are denoted by
V(G)
and
E(G)
, respectively. Let
F\subseteq E(G)
and
U\subseteq V(G)
, and let
H,H^{\prime}
be two subgraphs of
G
. The notation
V(F)
represents the set of all vertices incident with edges in
F
. Denote
G-F
as the resulting graph by deleting from
G
the edges in
F
, and denote
G-U
as the resulting graph by deleting from
G
the vertices in
U
together with all the edges incident with
U
. Let
H+H^{\prime}
represent the graph with the vertex set
V(H)\cup V(H^{\prime})
and edge set
E(H)\cup E(H^{\prime})
, and let
H+F
denote the graph with the vertex set
V(H)\cup V(F)
and edge set
E(H)\cup F
.

In a graph
G
, the distance between vertices
u
and
x
is denoted by
d_{G}(u,x)
, and the distance of a vertex
u
and an edge
xy
is defined by
d_{G}(u,xy)=\min\{d_{G}(u,x),d_{G}(u,y)\}
. For a set
M\subseteq E(G)
, we say that a subgraph
H
of
G

passes through

M
, if
E(M)\subseteq E(H)
and
V(M)\subseteq V(H)
.

A
u,v
-path
is a path with endpoints
u
and
v
, denoted by
P_{u,v}
when we specify a particular such path. A spanning subgraph of
G
is called a
spanning
m
-path
if it consists of
m
vertex-disjoint paths. A spanning 1-path is a spanning or Hamiltonian path. Let
P_{x,y}=x,\ldots,v_{i},\ldots,v_{j},\ldots,y
be a path. The notation
P_{x,y}[v_{i},v_{j}]
denotes the subpath
v_{i},\ldots,v_{j}
of
P_{x,y}
joining
v_{i}
and
v_{j}
. For
u\in V(P_{x,y})\setminus\{x\}
, a neighbor
u^{\prime}
of
u
on
P_{x,y}
is called
closer to

x
than
u
if
u^{\prime}
lies on the subpath
P_{x,y}[x,u]
.

Note that
Q_{n}^{k}
is bipartite if and only if
k
is even. When
k
is even, the
parity
of a vertex
u=\alpha_{1}\cdots\alpha_{n}
in
Q_{n}^{k}
is defined by
p(u)=\sum_{i=1}^{n}\alpha_{i}
(mod 2), and a set
\{u_{1},u_{2},\ldots,u_{2n-1},u_{2n}\}
of distinct vertices is
balanced
in
Q_{n}^{k}
if the number of odd vertices equals to the number of even vertices. For even
k
,
p(u)\neq p(v)
if
uv\in E(Q_{n}^{k})
, and the condition “
p(u)\neq p(v)
” is necessary for the existence of a spanning
u,v
-path in
Q_{n}^{k}
.

Next, let us introduce several lemmas.

Lemma 2.1
.

[
18
]
For
n\geq 2
and odd
k\geq 3
, let
U\subseteq V(Q_{n}^{k})
with
|U|\leq 2n-3
. If
x,y\in V(Q_{n}^{k})\setminus U
are distinct, then there is a spanning
x,y
-path in
Q_{n}^{k}-U
.

Lemma 2.2
.

[
12
]
For
n\geq 2
and even
k\geq 4
, if
x,y
are vertices of different parities in
Q_{n}^{k}
, then there is a spanning
x,y
-path in
Q_{n}^{k}
.

Lemma 2.3
.

[
15
]
For
n\geq 3
and even
k\geq 4
, if
u,x,y
are distinct vertices in
Q_{n}^{k}
such that
p(u)\neq p(x)=p(y)
, then there is a spanning
x,y
-path in
Q_{n}^{k}-u
.

Lemma 2.4
.

[
15
]
For
n\geq 3
and
k\geq 3
, let
x,y
be distinct vertices in
Q_{n}^{k}
and whenever
k
is even,
p(x)\neq p(y)
. If
uv\in E(Q_{n}^{k})
and
\{u,v\}\neq\{x,y\}
, then there is a spanning
x,y
-path passing through
uv
in
Q_{n}^{k}
.

Lemma 2.5
.

[
15
]
For
n\geq 3
and
k\geq 3
, let
M
be a matching in
Q_{n}^{k}
with
|M|\leq\max\{0,2n-7\}
. If
uu^{\prime}
and
vv^{\prime}
are two disjoint edges in
Q_{n}^{k}
such that
\{u,v\}\cap V(M)=\emptyset
and
u^{\prime}v^{\prime}\notin M
, then there is a spanning 2-path
P_{u,u^{\prime}}+P_{v,v^{\prime}}
passing through
M
in
Q_{n}^{k}
.

Let
d\in\{1,\ldots,n\}
. An edge in
Q_{n}^{k}
is an
d
-edge
if its endpoints differ in the
d
th position. Let
E_{d}
denote the set of all
d
-edges in
Q_{n}^{k}
. Let
Q_{n-1}^{k,d}[i]
be the
(n-1)
-dimensional subcube of
Q_{n}^{k}
induced by all the vertices with the
d
th position being
i
where
i\in\{0,1,\ldots,k-1\}
, abbreviated as
Q[i]
. Obviously,
Q_{n}^{k}-E_{d}=Q[0]+\cdots+Q[k-1]
. We say that
Q_{n}^{k}
splits into
(n-1)
-dimensional subcubes
Q[0],\ldots,Q[k-1]
by
E_{d}
. For every vertex
u_{i}=\alpha_{1}\cdots\alpha_{d-1}i

\alpha_{d+1}\cdots\alpha_{n}
in
Q[i]
, let
u_{j}
represent the vertex
\alpha_{1}\cdots\alpha_{d-1}j

\alpha_{d+1}\cdots\alpha_{n}
in
Q[j]
where
0\leq j\leq k-1
.

In the following definitions, they are all defined under the premise of modulo
k
. Let
p,q
be two integers with
0\leq p\leq q\leq k-1
. Denote
E_{d}(p,p+1)
as the set of all

-edges joining

and

. Let
E_{d}(p,q)=\bigcup_{i=p}^{q-1}E_{d}(i,i+1)
when

, and let
E_{d}(p,q)=\emptyset
when

. Let

, abbreviated as

, represent the graph with the vertex set
\bigcup_{i=p}^{q}V(Q[i])
and edge set
(\bigcup_{i=p}^{q}E(Q[i]))\cup E_{d}(p,q)
. Then
Q[p,p]=Q[p]
and
Q[0,k-1]=Q_{n}^{k}-E_{d}(k-1,0)
. Given
M\subseteq E(Q_{n}^{k})
, let
M[p,q]=M\cap E(Q[p,q])
, and we briefly write
M[p,p]
as

.

Lemma 2.6
.

[
15
]
For
n\geq 3
and
k\geq 3
, if
x,y
are distinct vertices in
Q_{n-1}^{k,d}[p,q]
and

when

is even, then there is a spanning path

in
Q[p,q]
such that
E(P_{x,y})\cap E(Q[q])
forms a spanning path or 2-path in
Q[q]
.

Lemma 2.7
.

[
15
]
For
n\geq 3
and
k\geq 3
, let
M
be a matching in
Q_{n-1}^{k,d}[p,q]
such that
|M_{i}|\leq 2n-4
for every

and
|M\cap E_{d}(i,i+1)|\leq 1
for every
i\in\{p,\ldots,q-1\}
when
q>p
. If

, then there is a spanning

-path

passing through

in
Q[p,q]
such that
E(P_{x,y})\cap E(Q[q])
forms a spanning path in
Q[q]
and
E(P_{x,y})\cap E(Q[p])
forms a spanning 2-path in
Q[p]
.

Lemma 2.8
.

[
15
]
For
n\geq 4
and
k\geq 3
, let
x,y,u,v
be distinct vertices in
Q_{n-1}^{k,d}[p,q]
and whenever
k
is even,
p(x)\neq p(y)
and
p(u)\neq p(v)
. If
x,y,u,v\in V(Q[p])
or

and

in

3
Spanning
m
-paths in
Q_{n}^{k}

In this section, we testify some Lemmas about spanning
m
-paths which are applied in the constructions of Hamiltonian cycles in our result. In the following lemmas, we always assume
k\geq 4
.

Lemma 3.1
.

For
n\geq 4
, let
x,y
be distinct vertices in
Q_{n-1}^{k,d}[p,q]
and whenever
k
is even,
p(x)\neq p(y)
. Let
M
be a matching in
Q_{n-1}^{k,d}[p,q]
such that
|M|\leq 1
and
xy\notin M
. If
x,y\in V(Q[p])
or
x\in V(Q[p])
and
y\in V(Q[q])
, then there is a spanning
x,y
-path passing through
M
in
Q[p,q]
such that
E(P_{x,y})\cap E(Q[q])
forms a spanning path or 2-path in
Q[q]
.

Proof.

Note that
0\leq p\leq q\leq k-1
. If
M=\emptyset
, the result holds by Lemma
2.6
. If
|M|=1
, we prove the result by induction on
q-p
. When
q=p
, it holds by Lemma
2.4
. Now consider
q>p
, and suppose that the lemma holds for all
Q[p^{\prime},q^{\prime}]
with
q^{\prime}-p^{\prime}<q-p
.

Case 1.

x,y\in V(Q[p])
.

By Lemma
2.4
there is a spanning path
P_{x,y}^{\prime}
passing through
M_{p}
in
Q[p]
. If
M\cap E_{d}(p,p+1)=\emptyset
, since
|E(P_{x,y}^{\prime})|-|M|\geq k^{n-1}-1>1
, choose an edge
s_{p}s_{p}^{\prime}\in E(P_{x,y}^{\prime})\setminus M
such that
s_{p+1}s_{p+1}^{\prime}\notin M
. If
M\cap E_{d}(p,p+1)\neq\emptyset
, let
M=\{s_{p}s_{p+1}\}
and
s_{p}^{\prime}
be a neighbor of
s_{p}
on
P_{x,y}^{\prime}
. Since
|M[p+1,q]|\leq 1
, by Lemma
2.7
there is a spanning path
P_{s_{p+1},s_{p+1}^{\prime}}
passing through
M[p+1,q]
in
Q[p+1,q]
. Hence,
P_{x,y}=P_{x,y}^{\prime}+P_{s_{p+1},s_{p+1}^{\prime}}+\{s_{p}s_{p+1},s_{p}^{\prime}s_{p+1}^{\prime}\}-\{s_{p}s_{p}^{\prime}\}
is the desired spanning path in
Q[p,q]
.

Case 2.

x\in V(Q[p])
and
y\in V(Q[q])
.

If there exists an integer
j\in\{p,\ldots,q-1\}
such that
M\cap E_{d}(j,j+1)=\emptyset
, let
s_{j}\in V(Q[j])
such that
s_{j}\neq x
,
s_{j+1}\neq y
and
p(s_{j+1})=p(x)\neq p(y)=p(s_{j})
when
k
is even. By the induction hypothesis there exist spanning paths
P_{x,s_{j}}
passing through
M[p,j]
in
Q[p,j]
and
P_{s_{j+1},y}
passing through
M[j+1,q]
in
Q[j+1,q]
, respectively. Hence,
P_{x,y}=P_{x,s_{j}}+P_{s_{j+1},y}+\{s_{j}s_{j+1}\}
is the desired spanning path in
Q[p,q]
.

Otherwise,
p+1=q
and
|M\cap E_{d}(p,p+1)|=1
. Let
M\cap E_{d}(p,p+1)=\{s_{p}s_{p+1}\}
. If
y\neq s_{p+1}
, since
s_{p}
has
2(n-1)\geq 6
neighbors in
Q[p]
, we may choose a neighbor
s_{p}^{\prime}
of
s_{p}
in
Q[p]
such that
s_{p+1}^{\prime}\neq y
. Since
|V(Q[p+1])|=k^{n-1}\geq 7
, we may choose a vertex
t_{p+1}\in V(Q[p+1])\setminus\{y,s_{p+1},s_{p+1}^{\prime}\}
such that
t_{p}\neq x
,
xt_{p}\neq s_{p}s_{p}^{\prime}
and
p(x)\neq p(t_{p})
when
k
is even. Then
s_{p+1},s_{p+1}^{\prime},y,t_{p+1}
are distinct and
p(s_{p+1})\neq p(s_{p+1}^{\prime})
,
p(y)\neq p(t_{p+1})
when
k
is even. By the induction hypothesis there is a spanning path
P_{x,t_{p}}
passing through
s_{p}s_{p}^{\prime}
in
Q[p]
, and by Lemma
2.8
there is a spanning 2-path
P_{s_{p+1},s_{p+1}^{\prime}}+P_{y,t_{p+1}}
in
Q[p+1]
. Hence,
P_{x,y}=P_{x,t_{p}}+P_{s_{p+1},s_{p+1}^{\prime}}+P_{y,t_{p+1}}+\{s_{p}s_{p+1},s_{p}^{\prime}s_{p+1}^{\prime},t_{p}t_{p+1}\}-\{s_{p}s_{p}^{\prime}\}
is the desired spanning path in
Q[p,q]
. If
y=s_{p+1}
, now
x\neq s_{p}
. Choose a vertex
t_{p}\in V(Q[p])\setminus\{x,s_{p}\}
such that
p(t_{p})\neq p(x)
when
k
is even. So
t_{p+1}\neq y
. By Lemma
2.6
there is a spanning path
P_{x,t_{p}}
in
Q[p]
. Denote by
s_{p}^{\prime}
the neighbor of
s_{p}
on
P_{x,t_{p}}
that is closer to
x
. Then
y,s_{p+1}^{\prime},t_{p+1}
are distinct and
p(y)\neq p(s_{p+1}^{\prime})=p(t_{p+1})
when
k
is even. By Lemma
2.1
or Lemma
2.3
there is a spanning path
P_{s_{p+1}^{\prime},t_{p+1}}
in
Q[p+1]-y
. Now
P_{x,t_{p}}+P_{s_{p+1}^{\prime},t_{p+1}}+\{s_{p}s_{p+1},s_{p}^{\prime}s_{p+1}^{\prime},t_{p}t_{p+1}\}-\{s_{p}s_{p}^{\prime}\}
is the desired spanning path in
Q[p,q]
.
∎

Lemma 3.2
.

For
n\geq 4
, let
x,y,u,v
be distinct vertices in
Q_{n-1}^{k,d}[p,q]
where

and whenever
k
is even,
p(x)\neq p(y)
and

. If

If
|M_{p}|=1
, since
|\{x,y,u,v\}\cap V(M)|\leq 1
, we may assume
\{u,v\}\cap V(M)=\emptyset
. By Lemma
2.4
there exists a spanning path
P_{x,y}^{\prime}
passing through
M_{p}
in
Q[p]
. Let
u_{p}^{\prime},v_{p}^{\prime}
be neighbors of
u,v
on
P_{x,y}^{\prime}
such that
u_{p}^{\prime}\notin V(P_{x,y}[u,v])
and
v_{p}^{\prime}\notin V(P_{x,y}[u,v])
. Note that
\{uu_{p}^{\prime},vv_{p}^{\prime}\}\cap M=\emptyset
, and
u_{p}^{\prime}\neq v_{p}^{\prime}
, and
p(u_{p}^{\prime})\neq p(v_{p}^{\prime})
when
k
is even. By Lemma
2.6
there is a spanning path
P_{u_{p+1}^{\prime},v_{p+1}^{\prime}}
in
Q[p+1,q]
. Let
P_{x,y}=P_{x,y}^{\prime}-V(P_{x,y}^{\prime}[u,v])+P_{u_{p+1}^{\prime},v_{p+1}^{\prime}}+\{u_{p}^{\prime}u_{p+1}^{\prime},v_{p}^{\prime}v_{p+1}^{\prime}\}
and

. Now
P_{x,y}+P_{u,v}
is the desired spanning 2-path in

.

Otherwise,
|M_{p}|=0
. Then by Lemma
2.8
there is a spanning 2-path
P_{x,y}^{\prime}+P_{u,v}^{\prime}
in
Q[p]
. If
M\cap E_{d}(p,p+1)=\emptyset
, let
s_{p}s_{p}^{\prime}\in E(P_{x,y}^{\prime}+P_{u,v}^{\prime})
such that
s_{p+1}s_{p+1}^{\prime}\notin M_{p+1}
. If
M\cap E_{d}(p,p+1)\neq\emptyset
, let
M\cap E_{d}(p,p+1)=\{s_{p}s_{p+1}\}
and
s_{p}s_{p}^{\prime}
be an edge on
P_{x,y}^{\prime}+P_{u,v}^{\prime}
. Note that

, by Lemma
2.7
there exists a spanning path
P_{s_{p+1},s_{p+1}^{\prime}}
passing through
M[p+1,q]
in
Q[p+1,q]
. Without loss of generality, we may assume
s_{p}s_{p}^{\prime}\in E(P_{x,y}^{\prime})
. Let
P_{x,y}=P_{x,y}^{\prime}+P_{s_{p+1},s_{p+1}^{\prime}}+\{s_{p}s_{p+1},s_{p}^{\prime}s_{p+1}^{\prime}\}-\{s_{p}s_{p}^{\prime}\}
and
P_{u,v}=P_{u,v}^{\prime}
. Now
P_{x,y}+P_{u,v}
is the desired spanning 2-path in
Q[p,q]
.
∎

Lemma 3.3
.

For
n\geq 4
, let
x,y
be distinct vertices in
Q_{n}^{k}
and whenever
k
is even,
p(x)\neq p(y)
. If
M
is a matching in
Q_{n}^{k}
with
|M|\leq 2
and
xy\notin M
, then there is a spanning
x,y
-path passing through
M
in
Q_{n}^{k}
.

Proof.

When
|M|\leq 1
, the result holds by Lemma
2.4
. Next consider
|M|=2
. Since
n\geq 4
, we may split
Q_{n}^{k}
into subcubes
Q[0],\ldots,Q[k-1]
such that
M\cap E_{d}=\emptyset
. By symmetry we may assume
x\in V(Q[0])
and
y\in V(Q[i])
, where
0\leq i\leq k-1
.

Case 1.

i=0
.
