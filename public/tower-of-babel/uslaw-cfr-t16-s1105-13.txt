16 C.F.R. § 1105.13 — Noncompensable costs.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1105.13
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1105.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1105 (Contributions To Costs Of Participants In Development Of Consumer Product Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The items of cost toward which the Commission will not contribute include:
(a) Costs for the acquisition of any interest in land or buildings;
(b) Costs for the payment of items in excess of the participant's actual cost; and
(c) Costs determined not to be allowable under generally accepted accounting principles and practices or part 1-15, Federal Procurement Regulations (41 CFR part 1-15).
