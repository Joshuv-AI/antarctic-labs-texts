31 U.S.C. § 5103 — Legal tender
Citation: us:usc:31:5103
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section5103
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 51 (Coins And Currency); Subchapter I (Monetary System). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

United States coins and currency (including Federal reserve notes and circulating notes of Federal reserve banks and national banks) are legal tender for all debts, public charges, taxes, and dues. Foreign gold or silver coins are not legal tender for debts.
