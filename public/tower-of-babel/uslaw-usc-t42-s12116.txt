42 U.S.C. § 12116 — Regulations
Citation: us:usc:42:12116
Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 126 (Equal Opportunity For Individuals With Disabilities); Subchapter I (Employment). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 1 year after July 26, 1990, the Commission shall issue regulations in an accessible format to carry out this subchapter in accordance with subchapter II of chapter 5 of title 5.
