5 C.F.R. § 1601.31 — Applicability.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1601 (Participants' Choices Of Tsp Funds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to investment elections made under subpart B of this part, fund reallocations and fund transfers made under subpart C of this part, and fund transfers made under subpart F of this part.
