42 U.S.C. § 7508 — Guidance documents
Citation: us:usc:42:7508
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section7508
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 85 (Air Pollution Prevention And Control); Subchapter I (Programs And Activities); Part D (Plan Requirements For Nonattainment Areas); Subpart 1 (Nonattainment Areas In General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator shall issue guidance documents under section 7408 of this title for purposes of assisting States in implementing requirements of this part respecting the lowest achievable emission rate. Such a document shall be published not later than nine months after August 7, 1977, and shall be revised at least every two years thereafter.
