31 C.F.R. § 375.3 — What is the role of the Federal Reserve Bank of New York in this process?
Citation: us:cfr:31:375.3
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-375.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 375 (Marketable Treasury Securities Redemption Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As fiscal agent of the United States, the Federal Reserve Bank of New York performs various activities necessary to conduct a redemption operation under this part. These activities may include but are not limited to:
(a) Accepting and reviewing tenders;
(b) Calculating redemption operation results;
(c) Issuing notices of redemptions;
(d) Accepting deliveries of Treasury securities at settlement; and
(e) Processing the Treasury payment for securities delivered at settlement.
