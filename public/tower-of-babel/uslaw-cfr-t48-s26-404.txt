48 C.F.R. § 26.404 — 26.404 Contract clause.
Citation: us:cfr:48:26.404
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-26.404
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 26 (Other Socioeconomic Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Insert the clause at 52.226-6, Promoting Excess Food Donation to Nonprofit Organizations, in solicitations and contracts greater than $35,000 for the provision, service, or sale of food in the United States.
