2 C.F.R. § 1130.200 — Purpose of PROP Article II.
Citation: us:cfr:2:1130.200
Creator: Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1130.200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1130 (Property Administration: General Award Terms And Conditions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) PROP Article II prescribes standards for:
(1) Insurance coverage for real property and equipment acquired or improved under awards;
(2) The system that a recipient uses to manage both equipment that is acquired or improved in whole or in part under awards and federally owned property.
(b) It thereby implements OMB guidance in 2 CFR 200.310 and 200.313(d)(1) through (4), and partially implements 2 CFR 200.313(b).
