21 C.F.R. § 1315.03 — Personal use exemption.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1315.03
Creator: Drug Enforcement Administration, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1315.03
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1315 (Importation And Production Quotas For Ephedrine, Pseudoephedrine, And Phenylpropanolamine). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A person need not register as an importer, file an import declaration, and obtain an import quota if both of the following conditions are met:
(a) The person purchases scheduled listed chemical products at retail and imports them for personal use, by means of shipping through any private or commercial carrier or the Postal Service.
(b) In any 30-day period, the person imports no more than 7.5 grams of ephedrine base, 7.5 grams of pseudoephedrine base, and 7.5 grams of phenylpropanolamine base in scheduled listed chemical products.
