37 C.F.R. § 222.1 — Applicability of the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure and Federal Rules of Evidence.
Citation: us:cfr:37:222.1
Creator: U.S. Copyright Office, Library Of Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-222.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 222 (Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The rules of procedure and evidence governing proceedings before the Copyright Claims Board (Board) are set forth in this subchapter. The Board is not bound by the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure or the Federal Rules of Evidence.
