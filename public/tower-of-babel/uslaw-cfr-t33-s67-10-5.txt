33 C.F.R. § 67.10-5 — -5 Location requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:33:67.10-5
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-67.10-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 67 (Aids To Navigation On Artificial Islands And Fixed Structures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The sound signal required by §§ 67.20-10, 67.25-10, and 67.30-10 must:
(a) Be located on the structure so that the sound signal produced is audible over 360° in a horizontal plane at all ranges up to and including the required rated range; and
(b) Be located at least 10 feet but not more than 150 feet above mean high water.
