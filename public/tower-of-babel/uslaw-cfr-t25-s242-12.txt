25 C.F.R. § 242.12 — Authority to lease.
Citation: us:cfr:25:242.12
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-242.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 242 (Commercial Fishing On Red Lake Indian Reservation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Band, with the approval of the Secretary, may execute a lease or permit on its fisheries plant and hatchery at Redby, Minnesota, to the Association.
