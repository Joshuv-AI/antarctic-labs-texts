44 C.F.R. § 296.20 — Prerequisite to compensation.
Citation: us:cfr:44:296.20
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-296.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 296 (Hermit'S Peak/Calf Canyon Fire Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In order to receive compensation under the Act, a claimant must be an Injured Person who suffered an injury as a result of the Hermit's Peak/Calf Canyon Fire and sustained damages.
