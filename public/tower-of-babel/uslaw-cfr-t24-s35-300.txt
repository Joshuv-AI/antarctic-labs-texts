24 C.F.R. § 35.300 — Purpose and applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:24:35.300
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-35.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 35 (Lead-Based Paint Poisoning Prevention In Certain Residential Structures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this subpart D is to establish procedures to eliminate as far as practicable lead-based paint hazards in a residential property that receives more than $5,000 annually per project in project-based assistance on or after September 15, 2000, under a program administered by a Federal agency other than HUD.
