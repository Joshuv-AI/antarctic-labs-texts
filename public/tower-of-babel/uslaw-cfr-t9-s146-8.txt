9 C.F.R. § 146.8 — Terminology and classification; slaughter plants.
Citation: us:cfr:9:146.8
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-146.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 146 (National Poultry Improvement Plan For Commercial Poultry). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Participating slaughter plants shall be designated as “U.S. H5/H7 Avian Influenza Monitored.” All Official State Agencies shall be notified by the Service of additions, withdrawals, and changes in classification.
