40 C.F.R. § 1066.920 — Enclosure calibrations.
Citation: us:cfr:40:1066.920
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1066.920
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1066 (Vehicle-Testing Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Enclosures for evaporative and refueling emissions must meet the calibration specifications described in 40 CFR 86.116-94 and 86.117-96.
