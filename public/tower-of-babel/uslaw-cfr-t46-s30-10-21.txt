46 C.F.R. § 30.10-21 — -21 Flammable or inflammable—TB/ALL.
Citation: us:cfr:46:30.10-21
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-30.10-21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 30 (General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The words flammable and inflammable are interchangeable or synonymous terms for the purpose of the regulations in this subchapter.
