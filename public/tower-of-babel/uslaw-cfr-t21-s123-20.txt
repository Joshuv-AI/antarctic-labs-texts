21 C.F.R. § 123.20 — General.
Citation: us:cfr:21:123.20
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-123.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 123 (Fish And Fishery Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart augments subpart A of this part by setting forth specific requirements for processing fresh or frozen molluscan shellfish, where such processing does not include a treatment that ensures the destruction of vegetative cells of microorganisms of public health concern.
