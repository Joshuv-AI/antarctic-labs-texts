48 C.F.R. § 29.401-2 — 29.401-2 Construction contracts performed in North Carolina.
Citation: us:cfr:48:29.401-2
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-29.401-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 29 (Taxes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.229-2, North Carolina State and Local Sales and Use Tax, in solicitations and contracts for construction to be performed in North Carolina. If the requirement is for vessel repair to be performed in North Carolina, the clause shall be used with its Alternate I.
