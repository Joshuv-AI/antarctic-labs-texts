20 C.F.R. § 218.29 — Pay for time lost.
Citation: us:cfr:20:218.29
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-218.29
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 218 (Annuity Beginning And Ending Dates). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Pay for time lost because of personal injury must be credited to an actual period of time lost. The annuity can begin no earlier than the day after that period ends.
