19 C.F.R. § 144.4 — Allowance for abandoned, destroyed, or exported merchandise.
Citation: us:cfr:19:144.4
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-144.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 144 (Warehouse And Rewarehouse Entries And Withdrawals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Allowance in duties shall be made for merchandise in warehouse which is abandoned or destroyed in accordance with § 158.43 of this chapter or exported in accordance with § 144.37.
