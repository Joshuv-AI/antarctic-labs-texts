29 C.F.R. § 18.406 — Habit; routine practice.
Citation: us:cfr:29:18.406
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-18.406
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 18 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure For Administrative Hearings Before The Office Of Administrative Law Judges). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Evidence of the habit of a person or of the routine practice of an organization, whether corroborated or not and regardless of the presence of eyewitnesses, is relevant to prove that the conduct of the person or organization on a particular occasion was in conformity with the habit or routine practice.
