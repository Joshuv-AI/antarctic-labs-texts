31 C.F.R. § 359.49 — What is the issue date of a book-entry Series I savings bond?
Citation: us:cfr:31:359.49
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-359.49
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 359 (Offering Of United States Savings Bonds, Series I). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The issue date of a book-entry Series I savings bond is the first day of the month in which the security posts to the current holdings of the account owner.
