5 C.F.R. § 330.709 — Establishing ICTAP selection priority.
Citation: us:cfr:5:330.709
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-330.709
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 330 (Recruitment, Selection, And Placement (General)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

ICTAP selection priority for a specific vacancy begins when:
(a) The ICTAP eligible submits all required application materials, including proof of eligibility, within agency-established timeframes; and
(b) The agency determines the eligible is well-qualified for the vacancy.
