16 U.S.C. § 753 — Cooperative work
Citation: us:usc:16:753
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section753
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 9 (Fish And Wildlife Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

On and after July 2, 1942, cooperative work conducted by the United States Fish and Wildlife Service shall be subject to the provisions of the Act of July 24, 1919 [7 U.S.C. 2220, 2279i].
