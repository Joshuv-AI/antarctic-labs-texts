10 U.S.C. § 12681 — Reserves: discharge authority
Citation: us:usc:10:12681
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section12681
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel Generally); Chapter 1221 (Separation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Subject to other provisions of this title, reserve commissioned officers may be discharged at the pleasure of the President. Other Reserves may be discharged under regulations prescribed by the Secretary concerned.
