47 C.F.R. § 51.401 — State authority.
Citation: us:cfr:47:51.401
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-51.401
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 51 (Interconnection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A state commission shall determine whether a telephone company is entitled, pursuant to section 251(f) of the Act, to exemption from, or suspension or modification of, the requirements of section 251 of the Act. Such determinations shall be made on a case-by-case basis.
