16 U.S.C. § 590z–9 — Powers and duties of Secretaries of the Interior and Agriculture; rules and regulations
Citation: us:usc:16:590z–9
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section590z–9
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 3C (Water Conservation); Subchapter II (Conservation And Utilization Projects). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Interior and the Secretary of Agriculture are authorized to perform any and all Acts 1 and to make such rules and regulations as may be necessary and proper for the purpose of carrying out their respective functions under this subchapter and for the purpose of carrying the provisions of this subchapter into full force and effect.
