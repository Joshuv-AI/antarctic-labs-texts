10 C.F.R. § 71.89 — Opening instructions.
Citation: us:cfr:10:71.89
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-71.89
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 71 (Packaging And Transportation Of Radioactive Material). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Before delivery of a package to a carrier for transport, the licensee shall ensure that any special instructions needed to safely open the package have been sent to, or otherwise made available to, the consignee for the consignee's use in accordance with 10 CFR 20.1906(e).
