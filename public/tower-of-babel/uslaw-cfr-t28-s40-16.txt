28 C.F.R. § 40.16 — Denial of certification.
Citation: us:cfr:28:40.16
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-40.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 40 (Standards For Inmate Grievance Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the Attorney General finds that the grievance procedure is not in substantial compliance with the standards promulgated herein or is no longer fair and effective, the Attorney General shall deny certification and inform the applicant in writing of the area or areas in which the grievance procedure or the application is deemed inadequate.
