A quasilinear elliptic equation with absorption term and Hardy potential
Marie-Françoise Bidaut-Véron, Huyuan Chen

Abstract
Here we study the positive solutions of the equation − Δ p ​ u + μ ​ u p − 1 | x | p + | x | θ ​ u q = 0 , x ∈ ℝ N \ { 0 } , -\Delta_{p}u+\mu\frac{u^{p-1}}{\left|x\right|^{p}}+\left|x\right|^{\theta}u^{q}=0,\qquad x\in\mathbb{R}^{N}\backslash\left\{0\right\}, where Δ p u = d i v ( | ∇ u | p − 2 ∇ u ) \Delta_{p}u={div}(\left|\nabla u\right|^{p-2}\nabla u) and 1 < p ⁡ < N , q > ​ p − 1 , μ , θ ∈ ℝ . 1<p<N,q>p-1,\mu,\theta\in\mathbb{R}. We give a complete description of the existence and the asymptotic behaviour of the solutions near the singularity 0 , 0, or in an exterior domain. We show that the global solutions ℝ N \ { 0 } \mathbb{R}^{N}\backslash\left\{0\right\} are radial and give their expression according to the position of the Hardy coefficient μ \mu with respect to the critical exponent μ 0 = − ( N − p p ) p . \mu_{0}=-(\frac{N-p}{p})^{p}. Our method consists into proving that any nonradial solution can be compared to a radial one, then making exhaustive radial study by phase-plane techniques. Our results are optimal, extending the known results when μ = 0 \mu=0 or p = 2 p=2 , with new simpler proofs. They make in evidence interesting phenomena of nonuniqueness when θ + p = 0 \theta+p=0 , and of existence of locally constant solutions when moreover p > 2 p>2 .

Introduction

Here we study the positive solutions
u
in a domain
\Omega
of
\mathbb{R}^{N}
of the quasilinear equation with a Hardy term

-\Delta_{p}u+\mu\frac{u^{p-1}}{\left|x\right|^{p}}+\left|x\right|^{\theta}u^{q}=0,

(1.1)

where
\Delta_{p}
is the
p
-Laplace operator
u\longmapsto\Delta_{p}u={div}(\left|\nabla u\right|^{p-2}\nabla u)
and

1<p<N,\qquad q>p-1,\qquad\mu,\theta\in\mathbb{R}.

We consider the problem of the isolated singularities at
0
with
\Omega=B_{r_{0}}\backslash\left\{0\right\}
(a priori estimates, description of
the asymptotic behaviour of the solutions, existence of local solutions of
any possible type), the problem in an exterior domain with
\Omega=\mathbb{R}^{N}\backslash\overline{B_{r_{0}}}
(same questions), and the global
problem in
\mathbb{R}^{N}\backslash\left\{0\right\}
(existence of
solutions, radiality or nonradiality of the possible solutions).

The case
p=2
and
q>1,\theta=0
was studied by Guerch and Veron
[
18
]
, who gave the precise behaviour near
0
of the solutions of any sign
of equation

-\Delta u+\mu\frac{u}{\left|x\right|^{2}}+g(u)=0

(1.2)

and isotropy results when
g(u)
has a power-like growth. They extended many
properties the solutions of the classical problem

-\Delta u+u^{q}=0

object of an impressive number of articles, starting to the pionneer papers

[
7
]
,
[
29
]
, and also
[
6
]
.It is clear that problem (
1.2
) is deeply linked to the Hardy operator

v\longmapsto\mathcal{L}_{2,\mu}v=-\Delta v+\mu\frac{v}{\left|x\right|^{2}}

for which the value
\mu=\mu_{0}=-(\frac{N-2}{2})^{2}
plays an essential
role, due to the Hardy inequality in bounded
\Omega

\int_{\Omega}\left|\nabla u\right|^{2}dx\geq(\frac{N-2}{2})^{2}\int_{\Omega}\frac{u^{2}}{\left|x\right|^{2}}dx,\qquad\forall u\in W_{0}^{1,2}(\Omega).

In
[
9
]
, Cirstea who considered the positive solutions of

-\Delta u+\mu\frac{u}{\left|x\right|^{2}}+b(x)g(u)=0,

where
g(u)/u^{q}
and
b(x)/\left|x\right|^{\theta}
may have a
logarithmic behaviour, giving a precise behaviour near
0
, in the case
\mu\geq-(\frac{N-2}{2})^{2}
, and near
\infty
by Kelvin transform. In the
case of equation

-\Delta u+\mu\frac{u}{\left|x\right|^{2}}+\left|x\right|^{\theta}u^{q}=0

(1.3)

with
\mu<-(\frac{N-2}{2})^{2}
and
\theta+2>0
, Wei and Du
[
30

gave a precise behaviour near

, and a uniqueness result of global
solutions in
\mathbb{R}^{N}\backslash\left\{0\right\}
. The problem of
existence and radiality of global solutions was then solved in
[
11
]

for any value of
\theta
, by using thin techniques of supersolutions and
subsolutions.

The case
p=2
and
q<1
was treated in
[
5
]
, showing in particular
the existence of many nonradial solutions, with possible dead cores.

In case
p>1
, the quasilinear equation where
\mu=0,\theta=0,q>p-1
,

-\Delta_{p}u+u^{q}=0

(1.4)

was studied by
[
15
]
and
[
28
]
. Concerning the behaviour of any
solution

in

, it was shown that
three eventualities occur when

: either
\lim_{\left|x\right|\longrightarrow 0}\left|x\right|^{\frac{p}{q+1-p}}

, or

and the singularity is removable if

In case of quasilinear equation (

v\longmapsto\mathcal{L}_{p,\mu}v=-\Delta_{p}v+\mu\frac{v^{p-1}}{\left|x\right|^{p}}

(1.5)

\int_{\Omega}\left|\nabla u\right|^{p}dx\geq(\frac{N-p}{p})^{p}\int_{\Omega}\frac{\left|u\right|^{p}}{\left|x\right|^{p}}dx,\qquad\forall u\in W_{0}^{1,p}(\Omega).

For a precise study of the Hardy operator, we refer for exemple to
[

Here our purpose is to extend to equation (

Our results are

Main results

2.1
Parameters of the study

\bullet
We first consider the equation

\mathcal{L}_{p,\mu}u=0

(2.1)

and search of the form
u(x)=C\left|x\right|^{-S},
with

then

\mathcal{L}_{p,\mu}u=0\Longleftrightarrow\varphi(S)=0;

(2.2)

where

the function

\varphi(\frac{N-p}{p})=-(\frac{N-p}{p})^{p}-\mu=\mu_{0}-\mu

In any case

S_{1}>0,\quad\quad S_{2}\leq\frac{N-p}{p}\leq S_{1}.

(2.4)

If

we obtain two roots

If
\mu_{0}<\mu<0

If
\mu=0
,

. If
\mu=\mu_{0}
, we find only one root

,
corresponding to solutions

Note that for
\mu=\mu_{0},
equation (
2.1
) admits also radial
local positive solutions with a logarithmic behaviour :

\lim_{x\longrightarrow 0}\left|x\right|^{\frac{N-p}{p}}\left|\ln\left|x\right|\right|^{-\frac{2}{p}}u(x)=C_{1}>0,\qquad\text{or }\mathbf{\ }\lim_{\left|x\right|\longrightarrow\infty}\left|x\right|^{\frac{N-p}{p}}\left|\ln\left|x\right|\right|^{-\frac{2}{p}}u(x)=C_{2}>0,

(explicit and well known when

) see

[
19
]

and a short proof at Lemma
4.5
below.

\bullet
Next we consider equation (
1.1
), which takes the
form

\mathcal{L}_{p,\mu}u+\left|x\right|^{\theta}u^{q}=0

(2.5)

and search a particular power solution of the form
u^{\ast}(x)=a^{\ast}\left|x\right|^{-\lambda}.
If it exists, then necessarily
\lambda=\gamma
, defined at (
1.8
) and

u^{\ast}(x)=a^{\ast}\left|x\right|^{-\gamma}\text{ and }(a^{\ast})^{q+1-p}=\varphi(\gamma)>0,

(2.6)

and the existence of

only depends on the position of
\gamma
with
respect to
S_{1},S_{2}.\vskip 12.0pt plus 4.0pt minus 4.0pt

\bullet
In the sequel we divide our analysis into 5 assumptions:

\left\{\begin{array}[]{cc}\text{(}\mathcal{H}_{1}\text{):}&\mu\geq\mu_{0}\quad\text{and }\gamma>S_{1},\\
\text{(}\mathcal{H}_{2}\text{)}\text{: }&\mu\geq\mu_{0}\quad\text{and }\gamma<S_{2},\\
\text{(}\mathcal{H}_{3}\text{)}\text{:}&\mu>\mu_{0}\quad\text{and }S_{2}\leq\gamma\leq S_{1},\\

\text{(}\mathcal{H}_{5}\text{)}\text{:}&\mu<\mu_{0}.\end{array}\right.

(2.7)

Then the solution

exists exclusively in cases (
\mathcal{H}_{1}
),(
\mathcal{H}_{2}
) (
\mathcal{H}_{5}
).

We note that
\gamma

has the sign of

p+\theta.
These different cases do not involve the sign of
\gamma.
but a main
difference is the behaviour of the solution
u^{\ast}
, when it exists:
when
p+\theta>0
, then

is singular, decreasing from
\infty

as

to

\infty

When

, that means
\gamma=0
, equation (
1.1
) takes the
form

(2.8)

so that for
\mu<0,\mu\neq\mu_{0}
, the function
u^{\ast}\equiv\left|\mu\right|^{\frac{1}{q+1-p}}
is a
constant solution
. This case has a specific interest. Indeed there hold phenomena of

of solutions in a neighborhood of

possibly global, see Remark

, we show the

, but not identically

or
\infty

\mu_{0}\leq\mu<0
, or (
\mathcal{H}_{5}

5.1
The strong maximum principle

Theorem 5.1

(Strong Maximum Principle) Let

be any nonnegative

solution of (
1.1

(resp. in
\mathbb{R}^{N}\backslash\overline{B_{r_{0}}}

Proof.
If
\mu\leq 0
, then setting
C_{1}=\max_{x\in\overline{\omega}}\left|x\right|^{\theta}
, and

If
\mu>0

C_{2}=\max_{x\in\overline{\omega}}\left|x\right|^{-p}
and
\beta_{2}(u)=\mu C_{2}u^{p-1}+u^{q}

-\Delta_{p}u+\beta_{2}(u)\geq 0.

Then the result comes from the Strong Maximum Principle of
[
27

, since in any case
\int_{0}^{1}(s\beta_{i}(s))^{-\frac{1}{p}}ds=\infty
.
