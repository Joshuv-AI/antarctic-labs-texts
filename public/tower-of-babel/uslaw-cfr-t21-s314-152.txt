21 C.F.R. § 314.152 — Notice of withdrawal of approval of an application or abbreviated application for a new drug.
Citation: us:cfr:21:314.152
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-314.152
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 314 (Applications For Fda Approval To Market A New Drug). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the Food and Drug Administration withdraws approval of an application or abbreviated application for a new drug, FDA will publish a notice in the Federal Register announcing the withdrawal of approval. If the application or abbreviated application was withdrawn for grounds described in § 314.150(a) or § 314.151, the notice will announce the removal of the drug from the list of approved drugs published under section 505(j)(6) of the act and shall satisfy the requirement of § 314.162(b).
