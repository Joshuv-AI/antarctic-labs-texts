DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Air Force


Notice Is Given of the Names of Members of the Performance Review 
Board for the Department of the Air Force

AGENCY: Department of the Air Force.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY:  Notice is given of the names of members of the 2026 
Performance Review Board for the Department of the Air Force.

DATES: June 22, 2026.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:  Please direct any written comments or 
requests for information to Ms. Jacquelyn Salkeld, Department of the 
Air Force Civilian Senior Executive Management Office, SAF/MRL, 1660 
Air Force Pentagon, Washington, DC 20330-1040, (Primary POC: PH: 703-
693-6447; or via email at [email protected]).

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Pursuant to 5 U.S.C. 4314(c)(1-5), the 
Department of the Air Force announces the appointment of members to the 
Air Force's Senior Executive Service Performance Review Board. 
Appointments are made by the authorizing official. Each board member 
shall review and evaluate performance scores provided by the Senior 
Executive's rater/immediate supervisor. Performance standards must be 
applied consistently across the Air Force. The board will make final 
recommendations to the authorizing official relative to the performance 
of the executive.
    The members of the 2025 Performance Review Board for the Air Force 
are:

1. Honorable Richard Anderson (Chair), Assistant Secretary of the Air 
Force for Manpower and Reserve Affairs
2. General Shawn Bratton (Co-Chair), Vice Chief, Space Operations, 
United States Space Force
3. General John Lamontagne (Co-Chair), Vice Chief, United States Air 
Force
4. LTG Richard Zellman, Deputy Commander, United States Space Command
5. Mr. William Bailey, Principal Deputy Assistant Secretary, 
Acquisition, Technology & Logistics, Office of the Secretary of the Air 
Force
6. Ms. Shannon McGuire, Principal Deputy General Counsel for the 
Department of the Air Force
7. Mr. John Stoneburg, Executive Director to the Assistant Secretary of 
the Air Force for Manpower and Reserve Affairs
8. Mr. Edwin Oshiba, Administrative Assistant, Office of the Secretary 
of the Air Force
9. Ms. Katharine Kelley, Deputy Chief of Space Operations for Human 
Capital, United States Space Force
10. Acting, Assistant Deputy Chief of Staff for Manpower, Personnel and 
Services
11. Ms. Kathy Watern, Executive Director, Air Force Materiel Command
12. Mr. Sean Dalton (Legal Advisor), Deputy General Counsel for Fiscal, 
Ethics, and Administrative Law
13. Ms. Avis McAllister (Legal Advisor), Associate General Counsel of 
the Air Force
