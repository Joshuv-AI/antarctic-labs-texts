46 U.S.C. § 30906 — Venue

Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 309 (Suits In Admiralty Against The United States). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In General.—
A civil action under this chapter shall be brought in the district court of the United States for the district in which—
(1)
any plaintiff resides or has its principal place of business; or
(2)
the vessel or cargo is found.
(b) Transfer.—
On a motion by a party, the court may transfer the action to any other district court of the United States.
