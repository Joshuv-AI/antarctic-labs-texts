7 U.S.C. § 4915 — Separability
Citation: us:usc:7:4915
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section4915
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 80 (Watermelon Research And Promotion). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If any provision of this chapter or the application thereof to any person or circumstances is held invalid, the validity of the remainder of this chapter and the application of such provision to other persons and circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
