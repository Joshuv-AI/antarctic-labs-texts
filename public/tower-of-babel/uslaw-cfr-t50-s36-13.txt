50 C.F.R. § 36.13 — Subsistence fishing.
Citation: us:cfr:50:36.13
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-36.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 36 (Alaska National Wildlife Refuges). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Fish may be taken by local rural residents for subsistence uses in compliance with applicable State and Federal law. To the extent consistent with the provisions of this part and other Federal law, applicable State laws and regulations governing the taking of fish which are now or will hereafter be in effect are hereby incorporated by reference as a part of these regulations.
