25 C.F.R. § 115.410 — What types of identification will the BIA or OTFM accept as “verifiable photo identification”?
Citation: us:cfr:25:115.410
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-115.410
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 115 (Trust Funds For Tribes And Individual Indians). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

BIA or OTFM will accept the following forms of identification as “verifiable photo identification”:
(a) A valid driver's license;
(b) A government-issued photo identification card, such as a passport, security badge, etc.; or
(c) A tribal photo identification card.
