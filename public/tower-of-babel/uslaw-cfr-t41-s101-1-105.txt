41 C.F.R. § 101-1.105 — -1.105 Authority for FPMR System.
Citation: us:cfr:41:101-1.105
Creator: Chapter 101—Federal Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-101-1.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 101-1 (1—Introduction). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The FPMR system is prescribed by the Administrator of General Services under authority of the Federal Property and Administrative Services Act of 1949, 63 Stat. 377, as amended, and other laws and authorities specifically cited in the text.
