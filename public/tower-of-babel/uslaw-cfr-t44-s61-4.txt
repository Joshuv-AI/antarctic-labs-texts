44 C.F.R. § 61.4 — Properties in violation of law, regulation, or ordinance.
Citation: us:cfr:44:61.4
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-61.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 61 (Insurance Coverage And Rates). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No new flood insurance or renewal of flood insurance policies will be written for properties declared by a duly constituted State or local zoning or other authority to be in violation of any floodplain, mudslide (i.e., mudflow), or flood-related erosion area management or control law, regulation, or ordinance.
