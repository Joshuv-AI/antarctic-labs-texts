Abstract
. In this note, we show that the p p -adic periods of motives introduced recently by Ancona and Frăţilă (“André periods”) reduce to the classically studied notion in the case of Mixed Tate motives in connection with p p -adic multiple zeta values and p p -adic multiple polylogarithms. We also connect André periods with Coleman integration by observing that the Frobenius-fixed de Rham paths of Besser and Vologodsky come from motivic paths in characteristic p p (unconditionally in the mixed Tate setting, conditionally in general). We use this to realize special values of p p -adic multiple polylogarithms as André periods in a concrete way. Keywords: p p -Adic periods, p p -adic integration, mixed Tate motives 2020 Mathematics Subject Classification: 14F30, 14C15, 11G55

5.
Frobenius-fixed paths

Our goal here is to explain the statement that the Frobenius-fixed paths of Besser and Vologodsky are motivic. This section presumes some familiarity with the formalism of “algebraic geometry in a Tannakian category”
[
17
]
and the unipotent fundamental group
[
16
]
.

5.1.

Let
\overline{X}\to Z
be smooth and proper and let
X\subset\overline{X}
be the complement of a snc divisor. Consider points
x,y\in X(\hat{\mathcal{O}}_{\mathfrak{p}})
with values in the formal completion of the local ring at
\mathfrak{p}
(
1.2
). Let
\operatorname{UnVIC}(X_{K_{\mathfrak{p}}})
be the
K_{\mathfrak{p}}
-Tannakian category of unipotent vector bundles with integrable connection, and let
\omega_{x}
,
\omega_{y}
be the fiber functors

\operatorname{UnVIC}(X_{K_{\mathfrak{p}}})\to\operatorname{\bf{Vect}}K_{\mathfrak{p}}

associated to
x,y
. There’s a Frobenius action on
\operatorname{Isom}^{\otimes}(\omega_{x},\omega_{y})
; by Besser
[
3
]
and Vologodsky
[
25
]
,
\operatorname{Isom}^{\otimes}(\omega_{x},\omega_{y})
contains a unique Frobenius-fixed
K_{\mathfrak{p}}
-point
p^{\mathrm{Cr}}
.

5.2.

When
X
is of mixed Tate type,
x,y
are
Z
-points of
X
, and

\omega_{x},\omega_{y}:\operatorname{UnVIC}(X_{K})\rightrightarrows\operatorname{\bf{Vect}}K

are the associated fiber functors over

, the path torsor
\operatorname{Isom}^{\otimes}(\omega_{x},\omega_{y})
is motivic. Thus, there’s a prounipotent group object
\pi_{1}^{\mathrm{un}}(X,x)
in
\operatorname{MTM}(Z)
, a torsor object
\pi_{1}^{\mathrm{un}}(X,x,y)
, and an isomorphism

{\rm{dR}}^{*}\pi_{1}^{\mathrm{un}}(X,x,y)=\operatorname{Isom}^{\otimes}(\omega_{x},\omega_{y}).

We may then consider the specialization

, a torsor object in
\operatorname{MTM}(k)
, as well as the crystalline realization of the latter
\mathrm{Cr}^{*}s_{\mathfrak{p}}^{*}\pi_{1}^{\mathrm{un}}(X,x,y)
, which carries the structure of a torsor under a prounipotent group in the category of
\phi
-modules over
K_{\mathfrak{p}}^{0}
. The Berthelot isomorphism

{\rm{dR}}^{*}\pi_{1}^{\mathrm{un}}(X,x,y)_{K_{\mathfrak{p}}}=\mathrm{Cr}^{*}s_{\mathfrak{p}}^{*}\pi_{1}^{\mathrm{un}}(X,x,y)_{K_{\mathfrak{p}}}

is compatible with the Frobenius actions. (Indeed, in the mixed Tate setting, this may be taken as a

of the Frobenius action on the left hand side; the arguments in Besser
[
3
]
go through without the need to compare this definition with the one given there.)

Proposition 5.3
.

In the situation and the notation of paragraph
5
, there exists a unique morphism of affine scheme objects in
\operatorname{MTM}(k)

\mathbbm{1}\to s_{\mathfrak{p}}^{*}\pi_{1}^{\mathrm{un}}(X,x,y)

whose crystalline realization maps via the Berthelot isomorphism
5
(*) to the Frobenius-fixed path
p^{\mathrm{Cr}}
.

Proof.

Any torsor object under a prounipotent group object in a semisimple Tannakian category is trivial, i.e. admits a point, as in (**). The crystalline realization is a map of
\phi
-modules, hence corresponds to a
\phi
-fixed point. Uniqueness then follows from the faithfulness of the realization functor.
∎

6.

-Adic multiple polylogarithms

Finally, in this section, we construct certain functions

6.1.

Let

[

shows, on the one hand, that the de Rham path torsor
