7 C.F.R. § 614.17 — Judicial review.
Citation: us:cfr:7:614.17
Creator: Natural Resources Conservation Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-614.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 614 (Nrcs Appeal Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A participant must receive a final determination from NAD pursuant to 7 CFR part 11 prior to seeking judicial review in any U.S. District Court of competent jurisdiction.
