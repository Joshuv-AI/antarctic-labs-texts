22 C.F.R. § 1509.510 — What actions will the Federal Government take against a recipient determined to have violated this part?
Citation: us:cfr:22:1509.510
Creator: African Development Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-1509.510
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 1509 (Governmentwide Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Financial Assistance)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a recipient is determined to have violated this part, as described in § 1509.500 or § 1509.505, the African Development Foundation may take one or more of the following actions—
(a) Suspension of payments under the award;
(b) Suspension or termination of the award; and
(c) Suspension or debarment of the recipient under 22 CFR part 1508, for a period not to exceed five years.
