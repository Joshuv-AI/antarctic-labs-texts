28 C.F.R. § 74.9 — Conditions of acceptance of payment.
Citation: us:cfr:28:74.9
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-74.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 74 (Civil Liberties Act Redress Provision). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each eligible individual will be deemed to have accepted payment if, after receiving notification of eligibility from the Redress Administrator, the eligible individual does not refuse payment in the manner described in § 74.11.
(b) Acceptance of payment shall be in full satisfaction of all claims arising out of the acts described in § 74.3(a)(4).
