29 C.F.R. § 810.520 — Confidentiality.
Citation: us:cfr:29:810.520
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-810.520
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 810 (High-Wage Components Of The Labor Value Content Requirements Under The United States-Mexico-Canada Agreement Implementation Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator shall, to the full extent of the law, protect the confidentiality of any person who provides information to the Department in confidence in the course of a verification or otherwise under this subpart.
