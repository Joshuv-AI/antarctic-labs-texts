16 U.S.C. § 5108 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:16:5108
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 71 (Atlantic Coastal Fisheries Cooperative Management). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
To carry out this chapter, there are authorized to be appropriated $10,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2001 through 2005.
(b) Cooperative statistics program
Amounts authorized under subsection (a) may be used by the Secretary to support the Commission’s cooperative statistics program.
