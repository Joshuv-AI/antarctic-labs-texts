7 U.S.C. § 950aaa — Purpose
Citation: us:usc:7:950aaa
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section950aaa
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 31A (Telemedicine And Distance Learning Services In Rural Areas). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purpose of this chapter is to encourage and improve telemedicine services and distance learning services in rural areas through the use of telecommunications, computer networks, and related advanced technologies by students, teachers, medical professionals, and rural residents.
