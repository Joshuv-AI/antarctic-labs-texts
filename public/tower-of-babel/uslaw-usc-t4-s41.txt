4 U.S.C. § 41 — Seal of the United States
Citation: us:usc:4:41
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title4-section41
Context: United States Code, Title 4 (Flag And Seal, Seat Of Government, And The States); Chapter 2 (The Seal). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The seal heretofore used by the United States in Congress assembled is declared to be the seal of the United States.
