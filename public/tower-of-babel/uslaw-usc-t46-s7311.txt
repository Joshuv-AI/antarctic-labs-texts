46 U.S.C. § 7311 — Able seamen—sail
Citation: us:usc:46:7311
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section7311
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part E (Merchant Seamen Licenses, Certificates, And Documents); Chapter 73 (Merchant Mariners’ Documents). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For service on a sailing school vessel on any waters, an individual may be rated as able seaman—sail if the individual has at least 6 months’ service on deck on sailing school vessels, oceanographic research vessels powered primarily by sail, or equivalent sailing vessels operating on the oceans or navigable waters of the United States (including the Great Lakes).
