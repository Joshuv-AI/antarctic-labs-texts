48 C.F.R. § 233.215-70 — 233.215-70 Additional contract clause.
Citation: us:cfr:48:233.215-70
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-233.215-70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 233 (Protests, Disputes, And Appeals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Use the clause at 252.233-7001, Choice of Law (Overseas), in solicitations and contracts when contract performance will be outside the United States and its outlying areas, unless otherwise provided for in a government-to-government agreement.
