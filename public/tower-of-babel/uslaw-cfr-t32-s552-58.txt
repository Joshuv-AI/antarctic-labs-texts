32 C.F.R. § 552.58 — Other transactions.
Citation: us:cfr:32:552.58
Creator: Department Of The Army
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-552.58
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 552 (Regulations Affecting Military Reservations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Commercial transactions with other than individuals (such as nonappropriated fund activities) are restricted to the office of the custodian of the specific fund activity. Business will be conducted during normal duty hours.
