33 U.S.C. § 2221 — Cost limitations on projects
Citation: us:usc:33:2221
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section2221
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 36 (Water Resources Development); Subchapter I (Cost Sharing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Beginning in fiscal year 2006 and thereafter, agreements proposed for execution by the Assistant Secretary of the Army for Civil Works or the United States Army Corps of Engineers after November 19, 2005, pursuant to section 560 of this title; section 561 1 of this title; the Civil Functions Appropriations Act, 1936, Public Law 75–208 1 ; section 1962d–5a of title 42; sections 2214, 2231, and 2232 of this title; section 426i–1 1 of this title; section 701b–13 1 of this title; and any other specific project authority, shall be limited to total credits and reimbursements for all applicable projects not to exceed $100,000,000 in each fiscal year.
