31 C.F.R. § 566.202 — Evasions; attempts; causing violations; conspiracies.
Citation: us:cfr:31:566.202
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-566.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 566 (Hizballah Financial Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Any transaction on or after the effective date that evades or avoids, has the purpose of evading or avoiding, causes a violation of, or attempts to violate any of the prohibitions set forth in this part is prohibited.
(b) Any conspiracy formed to violate any of the prohibitions set forth in this part is prohibited.
