34 C.F.R. § 361.39 — State-imposed requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:34:361.39
Creator: Office Of Special Education And Rehabilitative Services, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-361.39
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 361 (State Vocational Rehabilitation Services Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The designated State unit must, upon request, identify those regulations and policies relating to the administration or operation of its vocational rehabilitation program that are State-imposed, including any regulations or policy based on State interpretation of any Federal law, regulation, or guideline.
