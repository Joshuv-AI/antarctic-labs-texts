29 U.S.C. § 663 — Representation in civil litigation
Citation: us:usc:29:663
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section663
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 15 (Occupational Safety And Health). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as provided in section 518(a) of title 28 relating to litigation before the Supreme Court, the Solicitor of Labor may appear for and represent the Secretary in any civil litigation brought under this chapter but all such litigations shall be subject to the direction and control of the Attorney General.
