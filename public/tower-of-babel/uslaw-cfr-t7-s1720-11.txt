7 C.F.R. § 1720.11 — Servicing.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1720.11
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1720.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1720 (Guarantees For Bonds And Notes Issued For Utility Infrastructure Purposes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary, or other agent of the Secretary on his or her behalf, shall have the right to service the guaranteed bond, and periodically inspect the facilities, assets, books, and accounts of the guaranteed lender or the collateral agent to ascertain compliance with the provisions of the RE Act and the bond documents.
