45 C.F.R. § 617.6 — Information requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:45:617.6
Creator: National Science Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-617.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 617 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Age In Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance From Nsf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each recipient shall:
(a) Make available upon request to NSF information necessary to determine whether the recipient is complying with the Act.
(b) Permit reasonable access by NSF or its designee to the books, records, accounts, and other recipient facilities and sources of information to the extent necessary to determine whether a recipient is complying with the Act.
