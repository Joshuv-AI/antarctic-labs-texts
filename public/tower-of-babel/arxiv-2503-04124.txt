Tight upper bounds on the hop domination number of triangle-free graphs
Shinya Fujita

Abstract
For a graph G G , a subset S S of V ⁡ ( G ) V(G) is a hop dominating set of G G if every vertex not in S S has a 2 2 -step neighbor in S S . The hop domination number , γ h ​ ( G ) \gamma_{h}(G) , of G G is the minimum cardinality of a hop dominating set of G G . In this paper, we show that for a connected triangle-free graph G G with n ≥ 15 n\geq 15 vertices, if δ ⁡ ( G ) ≥ 2 \delta(G)\geq 2 , then γ h ​ ( G ) ≤ 2 ​ n 5 \gamma_{h}(G)\leq\frac{2n}{5} , and the bound is tight. We also give some tight upper bounds on γ h ​ ( G ) \gamma_{h}(G) for triangle-free graphs G G that contain a Hamiltonian path or a Hamiltonian cycle. Keywords: Hop dominating set, hop domination number, dominating set, domination number, triangle-free graph

The dominating set problem in graph theory has been a topic of interest for many researchers and is related to network coverage and control problems, applied in various fields like communication networks, social networks, and more (see

For a graph

The

of a hop dominating set of

holds for the complete graph

The concept of hop dominating set was originally introduced by Natarajan and Ayyaswamy in
[
11
]
. Henning and Rad
[
9
]
further explored this concept: Indeed, they showed that a connected graph

if and only if

(that is, the graph obtained from

by deleting one edge), thereby answering a question posed by Natarajan and Ayyaswamy in
[
11
]
; and moreover, they gave probabilistic upper bounds for the hop domination number of a graph and also showed that the decision problems on the hop dominating set problems are NP-complete for planar bipartite graphs and planar chordal graphs. More computational results were given by Henning, Pal, Pradhan
[
8
]
along this line.
Furthermore, they found an important relationship between the hop domination number and the total domination number of a triangle-free graph:

Theorem 1.1
.

[
9
]

If

\gamma_{h}(G)\leq\gamma_{t}(G)
.

Considering the complete

. They further showed that, if

, a large clique in a graph

An open problem

It would be a natural extension to consider giving a sharp upper bound on the hop domination number for a graph with a large girth. So we propose the following open problem.

Problem 1
.

Let
r
be an integer with
4\leq r
.
Determine the least value
f(n,r)
such that the following proposition can be true:

Proposition 4.1
.

If
G
is a connected graph of order sufficiently large
n
with
\delta(G)\geq 2
and girth at least
r
, then
\gamma_{h}(G)\leq f(n,r)
.

Considering a large odd cycle, we see that
f(n,r)\geq\lceil n/3\rceil
. In this paper, we showed that
f(n,4)=\frac{2}{5}n
. For
r\geq 5
, it is open.
