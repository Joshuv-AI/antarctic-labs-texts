27 C.F.R. § 18.26 — Powers of attorney.
Citation: us:cfr:27:18.26
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-18.26
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 18 (Production Of Volatile Fruit-Flavor Concentrate). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The proprietor shall execute and file a power of attorney on form TTB F 5000.8 for every person authorized to sign or to act on behalf of the proprietor. (Not required for persons whose authority is furnished in the application.)
