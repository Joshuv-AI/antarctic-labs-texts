7 C.F.R. § 1223.100 — General.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1223 (Pecan Promotion, Research, And Information Order). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Referenda to determine whether eligible pecan producers and importers favor the issuance, amendment, suspension, or termination of the Pecan Promotion, Research, and Information Order shall be conducted in accordance with this subpart.
