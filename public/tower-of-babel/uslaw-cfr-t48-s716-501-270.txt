48 C.F.R. § 716.501-270 — 716.501-270 Partner vetting—indefinite-delivery contracts.
Citation: us:cfr:48:716.501-270
Creator: Chapter 7—Agency For International Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-716.501-270
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 716 (Types Of Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a task order or delivery order under an indefinite-delivery contract has the potential to be subject to vetting, then the contract itself will be subject to the applicable procedures and requirements for partner vetting in (48 CFR) AIDAR 704.70.
