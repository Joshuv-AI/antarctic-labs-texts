48 C.F.R. § 49.105-4 — 49.105-4 Cleanup of construction site.
Citation: us:cfr:48:49.105-4
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-49.105-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 49 (Termination Of Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In the case of terminated construction contracts, the contracting officer shall direct action to ensure the cleanup of the site, protection of serviceable materials, removal of hazards, and other action necessary to leave a safe and healthful site.
