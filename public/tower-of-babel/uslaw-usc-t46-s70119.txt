46 U.S.C. § 70119 — Civil penalty
Citation: us:usc:46:70119
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section70119
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 701 (Port Security); Subchapter I (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In General.—
Any person that violates this chapter or any regulation under this chapter shall be liable to the United States for a civil penalty of not more than $25,000 for each day during which the violation continues.
(b) Continuing Violations.—
The maximum amount of a civil penalty for a violation under this section shall not exceed $50,000.
