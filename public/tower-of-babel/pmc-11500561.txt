Comment on “A bibliometric and knowledge-map study on the treatment of hematological malignancies with CAR-T cells from 2012 – 2023”
Authors: Hua Zhao, Jiayue Xu, Kun Xu, Yuejun Zhou
Published: 2024

Dear Editor,

We have read a recent article1 titled ‘A bibliometric and knowledge-map study on the treatment of hematological malignancies with CAR-T cells from 2012 to 2023’ in Human Vaccines & Immunotherapeutics with great interest. In this study, Qing Huang et al. further improved Miao et al.’ s bibliometrics study2 in the whole field of CAR T cells, focusing on the relevant studies of CAR T therapy for hematological malignancies (excluding literatures related to solid tumors). Six emerging themes are identified that are the prevailing research hotspots, which may provide more targeted guidance and valuable references for researchers in the field of CAR-T cell therapy for hematological malignancies. However, there are several figures in the paper that may require further confirmation.

First, in the analysis of “countries and institutions,” it is written that “The University of Pennsylvania emerged as the most prolific research institution, having published 279 articles, followed by Pennsylvania Medicine (n = 250) and the University of Texas System (n = 250),” but according to Table 1, the number of articles published by the University of Texas system should be 233.
