25 C.F.R. § 583.4 — Are motions permitted?
Citation: us:cfr:25:583.4
Creator: National Indian Gaming Commission, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-583.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 583 (Appeals From Approvals Or Disapprovals Of Management Contracts Or Amendments To Management Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Management contract and amendment appeals are summary proceedings. Only motions for an extension of time under § 580.4(f) of this subchapter, motions to supplement the record under § 581.5 of this subchapter, and motions for reconsideration under § 581.6 of this subchapter, are permitted.
