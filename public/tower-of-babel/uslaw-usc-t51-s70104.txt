51 U.S.C. § 70104 — Definition of Space Launch System
Citation: us:usc:51:70104
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section70104
Context: United States Code, Title 51 (National And Commercial Space Programs); Chapter 701 (Use Of Space Launch System Or Alternatives). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter, the term “Space Launch System” means the Space Launch System authorized under section 302 of the National Aeronautics and Space Administration Authorization Act of 2010 (42 U.S.C. 18322).
