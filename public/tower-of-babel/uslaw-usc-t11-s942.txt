11 U.S.C. § 942 — Modification of plan
Citation: us:usc:11:942
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title11-section942
Context: United States Code, Title 11 (Bankruptcy); Chapter 9 (Adjustment Of Debts Of A Municipality); Subchapter III (The Plan). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The debtor may modify the plan at any time before confirmation, but may not modify the plan so that the plan as modified fails to meet the requirements of this chapter. After the debtor files a modification, the plan as modified becomes the plan.
