7 C.F.R. § 869.101 — Financial records and reporting requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:7:869.101
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Federal Grain Inspection Service, Fair Trade Practices Program), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-869.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 869 (Regulations For The United States Warehouse Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Warehouse operators must maintain complete, accurate, and current financial records that must be available to AMS for review or audit at AMS's request as may be prescribed in the applicable licensing agreement.
(b) Warehouse operators must, annually, present a financial statement as may be prescribed in the applicable licensing agreement to AMS.
