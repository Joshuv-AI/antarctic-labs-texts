43 C.F.R. § 3830.100 — How do I appeal a final decision by BLM?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3830.100
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3830.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3830 (Administration Of Mining Claims And Sites; General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you are adversely affected by a BLM decision under parts 3830-3839, you may appeal the decision in accordance with parts 4 and 1840 of this title.
