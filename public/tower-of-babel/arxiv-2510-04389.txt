How large is the braid monodromy of low-genus Lefschetz fibrations?
Faye Jackson

Abstract
. Given a genus g g smooth Lefschetz fibration π : M → S 2 \pi:M\to S^{2} with singular locus Δ ⊆ S 2 \Delta\subseteq S^{2} , we describe the subgroup Br ⁡ ( π ) \Br(\pi) of the spherical braid group Mod ⁡ ( S 2 , Δ ) \Mod(S^{2},\Delta) consisting of braids admitting a lift to a fiber-preserving diffeomorphism of M M . We develop general methods for showing that the index [ Mod ( S 2 , Δ ) : Br ( π ) ] [\Mod(S^{2},\Delta):\Br(\pi)] is infinite. As an application of our methods, we prove that [ Mod ( S 2 , Δ ) : Br ( π ) ] = ∞ [\Mod(S^{2},\Delta):\Br(\pi)]=\infty when g = 1 g=1 , when π \pi is expressible as a self-fiber sum when g ≥ 2 g\geq 2 , or when π \pi is a holomorphic genus g = 2 g=2 Lefschetz fibration whose vanishing cycles are nonseparating. In the genus g = 1 g=1 case, we relate the subgroup Br ⁡ ( π ) \Br(\pi) to the action of Mod ⁡ ( S 2 , Δ ) \Mod(S^{2},\Delta) on the SL 2 \SL_{2} -character variety for S 2 ∖ Δ S^{2}\setminus\Delta and provide an alternate proof of the first application via recent work of Lam–Landesman–Litt [ LLL23 ] .
