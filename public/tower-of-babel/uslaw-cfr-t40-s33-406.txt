40 C.F.R. § 33.406 — May a recipient designate a lead agency for fair share objective negotiation purposes?
Citation: us:cfr:40:33.406
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-33.406
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 33 (Participation By Disadvantaged Business Enterprises In United States Environmental Protection Agency Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an Indian Tribal, State or local Government has more than one agency that receives EPA financial assistance, the agencies within that Government may designate a lead agency to negotiate MBE and WBE fair share objectives with EPA to be used by each of the agencies. Each agency must otherwise negotiate with EPA separately its own MBE and WBE fair share objectives.
