46 C.F.R. § 15.818 — Global Maritime Distress and Safety System (GMDSS) At-sea Maintainer.
Citation: us:cfr:46:15.818
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-15.818
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 15 (Manning Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Every person employed or engaged to maintain GMDSS equipment at sea, when the service of a person so designated is used to meet the maintenance requirements of SOLAS Regulation IV/15 (incorporated by reference, see § 15.103), must provide documentary evidence that they are competent to maintain GMDSS equipment at sea.
