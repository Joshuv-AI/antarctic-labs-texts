40 C.F.R. § 52.1436 — Significant deterioration of air quality.
Citation: us:cfr:40:52.1436
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-52.1436
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 52 (Approval And Promulgation Of Implementation Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The requirements of sections 160 through 165 of the Clean Air Act are met except as noted in paragraphs (a) and (b) of this section. The EPA is retaining § 52.21 except paragraph (a)(1) as part of the Nebraska SIP for the following types of sources:
(a) Sources proposing to construct on Indian lands in Nebraska; and,
(b) Enforcement of permits issued by EPA prior to the July 28, 1983, delegation of authority to Nebraska.
