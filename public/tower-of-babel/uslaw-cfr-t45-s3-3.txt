45 C.F.R. § 3.3 — Compliance.
Citation: us:cfr:45:3.3
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-3.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 3 (Conduct Of Persons And Traffic On The National Institutes Of Health Federal Enclave). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A person must comply with the regulations in this part; with all official signs; and with the lawful directions or orders of a police officer or other authorized person, including traffic and parking directions.
