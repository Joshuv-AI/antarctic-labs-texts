5 C.F.R. § 293.506 — Ownership of the Employee Medical Folder.
Citation: us:cfr:5:293.506
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-293.506
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 293 (Personnel Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The EMF of each employee in a position subject to civil service rules and regulations is part of the records of the Office. When the EMF also contains occupational medical records created during employment in a position not subject to the civil service (e.g., with the Postal Service), the EMF is then part of the records of both the Office and the employing agency.
