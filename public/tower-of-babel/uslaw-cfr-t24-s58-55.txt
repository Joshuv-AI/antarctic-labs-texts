24 C.F.R. § 58.55 — Notice of intent to prepare an EIS.
Citation: us:cfr:24:58.55
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-58.55
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 58 (Environmental Review Procedures For Entities Assuming Hud Environmental Responsibilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As soon as practicable after the responsible entity decides to prepare an EIS, it must publish a NOI/EIS, using the HUD recommended format and disseminate it in the same manner as required by 40 CFR parts 1500 through 1508.
