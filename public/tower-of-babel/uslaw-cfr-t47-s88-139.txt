47 C.F.R. § 88.139 — DFMS administrator fees.
Citation: us:cfr:47:88.139
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-88.139
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 88 (Uncrewed Aircraft System Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A DFMS Administrator may charge users a reasonable fee for services provided, including usage-based fees for frequency assignments.
(b) The Commission, upon request, will review the fees and can require changes in those fees if they are found to be excessive.
