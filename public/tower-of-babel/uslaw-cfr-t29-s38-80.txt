29 C.F.R. § 38.80 — Lack of jurisdiction.
Citation: us:cfr:29:38.80
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-38.80
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 38 (Implementation Of The Nondiscrimination And Equal Opportunity Provisions Of The Workforce Innovation And Opportunity Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If CRC does not have jurisdiction over a complaint, the Director must:
(a) Notify the complainant in writing and explain why the complaint falls outside the coverage of the nondiscrimination and equal opportunity provisions of WIOA or this part; and
(b) Where possible, transfer the complaint to an appropriate Federal, State or local authority.
