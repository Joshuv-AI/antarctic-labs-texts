40 C.F.R. § 35.4195 — Are there certain people my group cannot select to be our technical advisor, grant administrator, or other contractor under the grant?
Citation: us:cfr:40:35.4195
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-35.4195
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 35 (State And Local Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Your group may not hire the following:
(a) The person(s) who wrote the specifications for the “contract” and/or who helped screen or select the contractor;
(b) In the case of a technical advisor, a person or entity doing work for the Federal or State government or any other entity at the same NPL site for which your group is seeking a technical advisor; and
(c) Any person who is on the List of Parties Excluded from Federal Procurement or NonProcurement Programs.
