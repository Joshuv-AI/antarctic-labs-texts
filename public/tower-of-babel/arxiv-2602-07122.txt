Revisiting an infinitely nested radical
Aung Phone Maw

Abstract
We revisit an infinitely nested radical by Ramanujan. Utilizing the full strength of his method, we shall arrive at some new infinitely nested radicals. Keywords: Infinitely nested radical, Iteration



1
Introduction

Ramanujan posed the following problem in the Journal of Indian Mathematical Society in 1911, the problem (
[
1
, p. 323]
) is to find the value of :

\sqrt{1+2\sqrt{1+3\sqrt{1+4\sqrt{1+\cdots}}}}.

The value of which is simply
3
. The following algebraic relation is used by Ramanujan to arrive at the solution of the above infinitely nested radical :

x^{2}=n^{2}+an+ax+(x-n-a)(x+n).

Ramanujan used this relation to obtain a general infinitely nested radical and then obtaining the value of (
1
) as a particular case (see
[
2
, p. 108]
).
Utilizing the full strength of his method we shall evaluate the following infinitely nested radicals :

3=\sqrt{1^{2}+(5+1^{2}-4)\sqrt{3^{2}+(5+2^{2}-8)\sqrt{5^{2}+(5+3^{2}-12)\sqrt{7^{2}+\cdots}}}}

3=\sqrt{2^{2}+(3+1^{2}-3)\sqrt{4^{2}+(3+2^{2}-6)\sqrt{6^{2}+(3+3^{2}-9)\sqrt{8^{2}+\cdots}}}}

9=\sqrt[3/2]{8+3+1\ \sqrt[3/2]{8+6+2\ \sqrt[3/2]{8+9+3\ \sqrt[3/2]{8+12+4\ \sqrt[3/2]{8+15+\cdots}}}}}

1=\sqrt{\sqrt{2}-1+(\sqrt{2}-1)\sqrt{\sqrt{3}-1+(\sqrt{3}-1)\sqrt{\sqrt{4}-1+(\sqrt{4}-1)\sqrt{\cdots}}}}.

2
Three Identities

Let
a_{1},a_{2},...
and
n_{1},n_{2},..
be two sequences, then by (
1
), for all
x
and for each
i\in\mathbb{N}
we have :

x^{2}=n_{i}^{2}+a_{i}n_{i}+a_{i}x+(x-n_{i}-a_{i})(x+n_{i}).

Thus for all
x
and for all
i\in\mathbb{N}
we have :

F(x)^{2}=n_{i}^{2}+a_{i}n_{i}+a_{i}x+(x-n_{i}-a_{i})F(x+n_{i}),

with
F(x)=x
. Therefore, we have the following formal iteration :

\displaystyle F(x)

\displaystyle=\sqrt{n_{1}^{2}+a_{1}n_{1}+a_{1}x+(x-n_{1}-a_{1})F(x+n_{1})}

\displaystyle=\sqrt{n_{1}^{2}+a_{1}n_{1}+a_{1}x+(x-n_{1}-a_{1})\sqrt{n_{2}^{2}+a_{2}n_{2}+a_{2}(x+n_{1})+(x+n_{1}-n_{2}-a_{2})F(x+n_{2})}}

\displaystyle=\sqrt{n_{1}^{2}+a_{1}n_{1}+a_{1}x+(x-n_{1}-a_{1})\sqrt{n_{2}^{2}+a_{2}n_{2}+a_{2}(x+n_{1})+(x+n_{1}-n_{2}-a_{2})\sqrt{n_{3}^{2}+a_{3}n_{3}+\cdots}}}

Thus :

Proposition 1
.

Let
a_{1},a_{2},...
and
n_{1},n_{2},..
be two sequences, formally, we have :

x=\sqrt{n_{1}^{2}+a_{1}n_{1}+a_{1}x+(x-n_{1}-a_{1})\sqrt{n_{2}^{2}+a_{2}n_{2}+a_{2}(x+n_{1})+(x+n_{1}-n_{2}-a_{2})\sqrt{n_{3}^{2}+a_{3}n_{3}+\cdots}}},

for all
x
.

Another identity for degree three can be obtained by the following algebraic relation:

x^{3}=2n^{3}+3n^{2}x+(x-2n)(x+n)^{2}

Thus for a sequence
n_{1},n_{2},...
and
G(x)=x^{2}
we have the following formal iteration :

\displaystyle G(x)

\displaystyle=\sqrt[3/2]{2n_{1}^{3}+3n_{1}^{2}x+(x-2n_{1})G(x+n_{1})}

\displaystyle=\sqrt[3/2]{2n_{1}^{3}+3n_{1}^{2}x+(x-2n_{1})\sqrt[3/2]{2n_{2}^{3}+3n_{2}^{2}(x+n_{1})+(x+n_{1}-2n_{2})G(x+n_{2})}}

\displaystyle=\sqrt[3/2]{2n_{1}^{3}+3n_{1}^{2}x+(x-2n_{1})\sqrt[3/2]{2n_{2}^{3}+3n_{2}^{2}(x+n_{1})+(x+n_{1}-2n_{2})\sqrt[3/2]{2n_{3}^{3}+3n_{3}^{2}(x+n_{1}+n_{2})+\cdots}}}

Therefore :

Proposition 2
.

Let
n_{1},n_{2},...
be a sequence, formally, we have :

x^{2}=\sqrt[3/2]{2n_{1}^{3}+3n_{1}^{2}x+(x-2n_{1})\sqrt[3/2]{2n_{2}^{3}+3n_{2}^{2}(x+n_{1})+(x+n_{1}-2n_{2})\sqrt[3/2]{2n_{3}^{3}+3n_{3}^{2}(x+n_{1}+n_{2})+\cdots}}},

for all
x
.

Finally, we consider the following relation :

x^{2}=a\sqrt{x^{2}+n}-n+(\sqrt{x^{2}+n}-a)\sqrt{x^{2}+n},

Thus, with
H(x)=x
and sequences
a_{1},a_{2},...
and
n_{1},n_{2},...
, formally we have :

\displaystyle H(x)

\displaystyle=\sqrt{a_{1}\sqrt{x^{2}+n_{1}}-n_{1}+(\sqrt{x^{2}+n_{1}}-a_{1})H(\sqrt{x^{2}+n_{1}})}

\displaystyle=\sqrt{a_{1}\sqrt{x^{2}+n_{1}}-n_{1}+(\sqrt{x^{2}+n_{1}}-a_{1})\sqrt{a_{2}\sqrt{x^{2}+n_{1}+n_{2}}-n_{2}+(\sqrt{x^{2}+n_{1}+n_{2}}-a_{2})H(\sqrt{x^{2}+n_{1}+n_{2}})}}

\displaystyle=\sqrt{a_{1}\sqrt{x^{2}+n_{1}}-n_{1}+(\sqrt{x^{2}+n_{1}}-a_{1})\sqrt{a_{2}\sqrt{x^{2}+n_{1}+n_{2}}-n_{2}+(\sqrt{x^{2}+n_{1}+n_{2}}-a_{2})\sqrt{\cdots}}}.

Therefore :

Proposition 3
.

Let
a_{1},a_{2},...
and
n_{1},n_{2},..
be two sequences, formally, we have:

x=\sqrt{a_{1}\sqrt{x^{2}+n_{1}}-n_{1}+(\sqrt{x^{2}+n_{1}}-a_{1})\sqrt{a_{2}\sqrt{x^{2}+n_{1}+n_{2}}-n_{2}+(\sqrt{x^{2}+n_{1}+n_{2}}-a_{2})\sqrt{\cdots}}},

for all
x
.

Now we turn to particular cases. Put
x=3,a_{1}=a_{2}=\cdots=0
and
n_{i}=2i-1,
for all
i\in\mathbb{N}
in
Proposition 1
, then since
3+(\sum_{i=1}^{n-1}(2i-1))-(2n-1)=3+(n-1)^{2}-2n+1=5+n^{2}-4n
, for each
n\in\mathbb{N}
, we have :

Corollary 4
.

3=\sqrt{1^{2}+(5+1^{2}-4)\sqrt{3^{2}+(5+2^{2}-8)\sqrt{\cdots\sqrt{(2n-1)^{2}+(5+n^{2}-4n)\sqrt{\cdots}}}}}.

Next, put
x=3,a_{1}=a_{2}=\cdots=0
and
n_{i}=2i
for all
i\in\mathbb{N}
in
Proposition 1
. Since
3+\sum_{i=1}^{n-1}2i-2n=3+n^{2}-n-2n=n^{2}-3(n-1)
, we have :

Corollary 5
.

3=\sqrt{2^{2}+1^{2}\sqrt{4^{2}+(2^{2}-3)\sqrt{\cdots\sqrt{(2n)^{2}+(n^{2}-3(n-1))\sqrt{\cdots}}}}}.

In
Proposition 2
, let
x=3,n_{1}=n_{2}=\cdots=1,
to get

Corollary 6
.

9=\sqrt[3/2]{8+3+1\ \sqrt[3/2]{8+6+2\ \sqrt[3/2]{\cdots\sqrt[3/2]{8+3n+n\ \sqrt[3/2]{\cdots}}}}}.

Finally, let
x=1,a_{1}=a_{2}=\cdots=1
and
n_{1}=n_{2}=...=1
in
Proposition 3
to get:

Corollary 7
.

1=\sqrt{\sqrt{2}-1+(\sqrt{2}-1)\sqrt{\sqrt{3}-1+(\sqrt{3}-1)\sqrt{\sqrt{4}-1+(\sqrt{4}-1)\sqrt{\cdots}}}}.
