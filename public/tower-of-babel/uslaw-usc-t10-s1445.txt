10 U.S.C. § 1445 — Correction of administrative deficiencies
Citation: us:usc:10:1445
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1445
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 73 (Annuities Based On Retired Or Retainer Pay); Subchapter I (Retired Serviceman’S Family Protection Plan). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whenever he considers it necessary, the Secretary concerned may, under regulations prescribed under section 1444(a) of this title, correct any election, or any change or revocation of an election, under this subchapter when he considers it necessary to correct an administrative error. Except when procured by fraud, a correction under this section is final and conclusive on all officers of the United States.
