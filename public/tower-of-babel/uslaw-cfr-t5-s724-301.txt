5 C.F.R. § 724.301 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:5:724.301
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-724.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 724 (Implementation Of Title Ii Of The Notification And Federal Employee Antidiscrimination And Retaliation Act Of 2002). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart implements Title II of the Notification and Federal Employee Antidiscrimination and Retaliation Act of 2002 concerning the obligation of Federal agencies to report on specific topics concerning Federal Antidiscrimination Laws and Whistleblower Protection Laws applicable to them covering employees, former employees, and applicants for Federal employment.
