40 C.F.R. § 96.80 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:40:96.80
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-96.80
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 96 (Nox Budget Trading Program And Cair Nox And So2 Trading Programs For State Implementation Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A unit that is in the State, is not a NOX Budget unit under § 96.4, vents all of its emissions to a stack, and is operating, may qualify, under this subpart, to become a NOX Budget opt-in source. A unit that is a NOX Budget unit, is covered by a retired unit exemption under § 96.5 that is in effect, or is not operating is not eligible to become a NOX Budget opt-in source.
