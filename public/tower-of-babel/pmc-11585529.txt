Introduction

Renal cell carcinoma (RCC) is one of urological malignancies with high mortality and increasing incidence [1]. 85% of kidney cancers are RCC [2], and approximately 70% of these have clear cell histology (ccRCC) [3, 4]. The bad news is that even with targeted therapy, the 5-year survival rate for patients with metastatic RCC is still less than 10% [5, 6]. Surgical treatment is effective for stage IV patients. For example, lymph nodes suspected of metastatic disease on CT may be proliferative rather than involving tumors; therefore, the presence of minimal regional gonadopathy does not preclude surgery. For patients whose tumors cannot be surgically removed, the NCCN team recommends tissue sampling to confirm the diagnosis of renal cell carcinoma and guide subsequent treatment based on histological findings. Systemic therapy is generally recommended for patients with recurrent multiple metastases or tumors that cannot be removed by surgery [7]. One of the main drugs for the first line therapy of metastatic RCC is sunitinib [8]. Sunitinib play its function by inhibiting receptor tyrosine kinase and suppressing vascular endothelial growth factor receptor [9]. However, 20% of patients with advanced RCC are resistant to sunitinib therapy [10]. An improved understanding of mechanisms underlying sunitinib resistance will continue to promote the development of a novel therapeutic strategy in RCC.

Programmed cell death plays a critical role in maintaining normal physiological homeostasis, the abnormal changes of cell death would lead to the progression of various diseases, especially cancer [11]. Increasing numbers of evidence showed that programmed cell death, including apoptosis, necroptosis, autophagy, and ferroptosis are widely involved in kidney disease progression [12]. Ferroptosis is iron-dependent oxidative cell death which is companied by the lethal accumulation of lipid-based reactive oxygen species (ROS) [12, 13]. Ferroptosis regulates disease progression mainly via polyunsaturated fatty acid metabolism, glutamine catabolic pathways, and nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide phosphate (NADPH/H+) [14]. Increasing numbers of studies have reported that ferroptosis plays an important role in chemoresistance [15]. CAF-secreted miR-522 suppresses ferroptosis and promotes acquired chemo-resistance in gastric cancer [15].

More and more researches have shown the contribution of ferroptosis in the pathogenesis of RCC and indicated that some agents can induce ferroptosis in RCC cells. KLF2 inhibits cancer cell migration and invasion by regulating ferroptosis through GPX4 in clear cell renal cell carcinoma [16]. Everolimus accelerates Erastin and RSL3-induced ferroptosis in renal cell carcinoma [17]. Ferroptosis-related gene CHAC1 is a valid indicator for the poor prognosis of kidney renal clear cell carcinoma [18].

Recent researches have confirmed that ferroptosis inducers or activators could help to overcome RCC sunitinib resistance. Curcumin combined with sunitinib suppresses the proliferation of sunitinib-resistant RCC cells by inducing ferroptosis via upregulating the ADAMTS18 [19]. Artesunate inhibits the growth of sunitinib-resistant renal cell carcinoma cells through cell cycle arrest and induction of ferroptosis [20]. However, the specific function and mechanism of ferroptosis in RCC sunitinib has not been fully elucidated.

Circular RNAs (circRNAs) are a newly found type of non-coding RNAs with special circular structure. The circRNA circular loops are covalently connected and with high stability [21]. It has been reported that circRNAs consist of many microRNA response elements and could act as microRNA sponges [22]. circRNAs exerts their function mainly through playing as competitive endogenous RNA (ceRNA) and regulating the expression of target genes at the post-transcriptional level [23]. cirRNAs are widely involved in the regulation of target gene, modification of chromatin, packaging of genome, and genomic imprinting [24]. With rapid development in high-throughput sequencing and bioinformatics, more and more circRNAs are found to be highly conserved and abnormally expressed in various diseases, including cancers [25–28]. circRNAs widely participated in the occurrence development, and prognosis of cancers [24, 29].

In our research, we identified a novel circRNA Hsa_circ_0072732 from circRNA datasets (GSE108735 and GSE100186). We focused on the function and mechanism of Hsa_circ_0072732 in ferroptosis-induced RCC sunitinib resistance.

Material and methods

Cell lines and cell culture

The renal carcinoma cells 769-P, 786-O, Caki-1, OSRC-2, A‐498, ACHN, OS‐RC‐2, and normal renal epithelial cell line KiMA were all purchased from the American Type Culture Collection (ATCC). All cells were cultured in complete DMEM (Gibco) media with 10% FBS (Gibco), 100 U/ml penicillin, and 100 mg/ml streptomycin.

Transfections

All the cells were transfected with good viability. The RCC cells were transfected with small interfering RNA (siRNAs) for hsa_circ_0072732, overexpression vector, miR-548 mimics, miR-548 inhibitor, and negative control using Lipofectamine 3000 following the instruction of the manufactory. The cells were collected for experiments after 24-h transfection. All the plasmids and small RNAs were designed and synthesized by GenePharma (Shanghai, China).

RNA extraction

TRIzol reagent (Life Technologies, Carlsbad, CA) was used to extract RNA from RCC cells following the instruction of manufactory. The values of A260/A280 were measured to determine the purity of extracted RNAs. The extracted RNAs were stored in a − 80 °C freezer until use.

Quantitative reverse transcription polymerase chain reaction (qRT‐PCR)

RCC total RNAs were reverse-transcribed into complementary DNAs (cDNAs) using a High-Capacity cDNA Reverse Transcription Kit (Qiagen) and amplified using SYBR Green Master Mix (TaKaRa, Dalian, China). The relative expression levels were calculated by the 2−△△Ct methods and normalized by GADPH or U6 small nuclear noncoding RNA (U6).

Dual-luciferase reporter assay

The sequence of Hsa_circ_0072732 in RCC cells were cloned into luciferase report pGL3 (Promega, Shanghai, China) as Hsa_circ_0072732-WT. The sequence of Hsa_circ_0072732 with the mutation of the miR-548b-3p were synthesized and cloned into pGL3 as mutated Hsa_circ_0072732-Mut. The sequence of SLC7A11 3ʹUTR in RCC cells were cloned into luciferase report pGL3(Promega, Shanghai, China) as SLC7A11-WT. The sequence of SLC7A11 3ʹUTR with the mutation of the miR-548b-3p binding site were synthesized and cloned into pGL3 as mutated SLC7A11-Mut. 5 × 104 cells were seeded in 24-well plates in triplicate and transfected with indicated plasmids and miRNA mimics. Luciferase reporter assays were performed using Dual-luciferase reporter assay kits (Promega, Madison, WI) according to the manufacturer’s instructions 48 h after transfection. Renilla luciferase activity was used as the internal control.

RNase R assay

10 μg total RNA was incubated with 5units of RNase R (Epicentre Biotechnologies, Madison, WI). Then qRT-PCR was used to detect the changes of RNA levels with treatment of RNase R.

Subcellular fractionation location

Collected 1 × 106 RCC cells with high viability. Then, a Cytoplasmic & Nuclear RNA Purification Kit (NORGEN, Thorold, ON, Canada) was applied to extract the nuclear and cytoplasmic RNA fractions, respectively. qRT‐PCR was used to detect the levels of RNA in cytoplasm and nuclear.

Western blot

The total protein of RCC cells were extracted using Total Protein Extraction Kit (KeyGEN, Nanjing, China). BCA protein assay kit (KeyGEN, Nanjing, China) was used to detect the concentration of extracted protein. 30ug proteins were loaded and separated by electrophoresis. The proteins were transferred on to the PVDF membranes. The membranes were blocked with PBS containing 5% non-fat dry milk overnight. HRP-conjugated secondary antibodies (1:3000; Kangcheng Inc., Shanghai, China) were used to incubate the membrane for 1 h at 37 °C. Chemiluminescence chromogenic substrate (Beyotime, Beijing, China) and a ChemiDoc Imaging System (BioRad, Hercules, CA, USA) were used to visualize the signal of the protein.

RNA pull down

Has_circ_0072732 and miR-548 biotinylated probes were designed and synthesized by GenePharma (Suzhou, China). RCC cells were lysed and sonicated in a 4 °C water bath for 45 min. 40 μl lysate was stored for RNA input. The probes were incubated with beads to form probes-coated beads. Put probes-coated beads into 100 μl lysates and incubate together for 12 h at 4 °C. Wash the beads three times with wash buffer, elute the RNA complexes from the beads. RNA was purified by TRIzol reagent (Invitrogen, Carlsbad, CA, USA), and reverse transcribed to cDNA. qRT-PCR was used for further analysis.

GEO data availability

The sequencing data are obtained from the NCBI’s Gene Expression Omnibus (GSE100186 and GSE108735). Gene Expression Omnibus, https∶/http://ww.ncbinlm.nih.gov/geo. GSE100186: eight renal clear cell carcinoma tissues from CCRCC surgery were distributed into two experimental groups: tumor tissue group and normal tissue group, then performed circRNAs chips by Arraystar Human circRNAs chip (Arraystar). GSE108735: seven paired frozen carcinoma tissues as well as normal tissues from patients with renal cell carcinoma were used for circRNA profiling by the second generation of RNA sequencing.

Statistical analysis

Statistical analyses were analyzed and graphs were constructed using GraphPad Prism 5. Student’s t-test was used for the comparison of differences between the two groups. One-way ANOVA followed by Turkey’s post-hoc test was applied for multiple groups.

Results

Hsa_circ_0072732 was upregulated in RCC

To investigate the function of circRNAs in RCC progression, two circRNAs datasets (GSE108735 and GSE100186) were analyzed. hsa_ circ_0006528, hsa_circ_0072732, hsa_circ_0003146, hsa_circ_0072954, and hsa_circ_0002484 were identified to be upregulated in both datasets (Fig. 1A). Similar results were obtained in reported research [30]. We identified the expression of Hsa_circ_0072732 in RCC cells and normal renal epithelial cell line KiMA. Results showed that Hsa_circ_0072732 was highly expressed in RCC cells (Fig. 1B). The half-life of Hsa_circ_0072732 was detected by the transcription inhibitor actinomycin D. Actinomycin D assay results showed that Hsa_circ_0072732 had a longer half-life than its linear isoform (Fig. 1C, D). A processive 3′ to 5ʹ exoribonuclease RNase R was used to digest the RNAs and detect the stability of RNAs. The results showed that Hsa_circ_0072732 was significantly resistant to RNase R (Fig. 1E, F). To investigate the function of sunitinib, we constructed RCC Hsa_circ_0072732 silence cells (Fig. 2A, B). CCK8 assays were used to detect the influence of Hsa_circ_0072732 on RCC proliferation and sunitinib resistance (Fig. 2C, D). Cell death was also detected by CCK8 assay (Fig. 2E, F). The results showed that silence of Hsa_circ_0072732 did not influence the proliferation of RCC (Fig. 2C, D), while silence of Hsa_circ_0072732 inhibited the growth rate (Fig. 2C, D) and increased cell death (Fig. 2E, F) with treated of sunitinib in RCC cells. Hsa_circ_0072732 acts as microRNA sponges for miR-548b-3p

It has been well reported that circRNA acts its function mainly through sponging and regulating the expression of microRNAs in cytoplasm [31, 32]. Firstly, we detected the location of Hsa_circ_0072732 in RCC cells. The results showed that Hsa_circ_0072732 mainly exists in cytoplasm suggesting that Hsa_circ_0072732 might exerts its function by microRNAs sponges (Fig. 3A, B). We applied the computational algorithm in circular RNA interactome. miR-548b-3p, miR-1183, miR-1197, miR-1238, miR-1303, miR-186, miR-203, miR-330-5p, miR-326, miR-217 were predicted to potentially bind to Hsa_circ_0072732. qRT-PCR was used to detect the levels of 10 predicted microRNAs in RCC NC and si- Hsa_circ_0072732 cells (Fig. 3C, D). The results showed that the silence of Hsa_circ_0072732 significantly upregulated the expression of miR-548b-3p in both RCC cell lines (Fig. 3C, D). Rescue experiments also were applied and confirmed the results (Fig. 3E, F). Luciferase activity assays were also performed to confirm the interaction between miR-548b-3p and Hsa_circ_0072732. The results showed that miR-548b-3p could significantly decrease the activity of pGL3-Hsa_circ_0072732-3ʹUTR, while the mutation of the binding sites between miR-548b-3p and Hsa_circ_0072732 abrogated the influence on the activity of pGL3-Hsa_circ_0072732-3ʹUTR (Fig. 3G, H). Discussion

RCC is one of the prevalent urinary system malignant tumors. Sunitinib was used to treat gastrointestinal tumors and renal cell carcinoma by inhibiting tyrosine kinase activity. Although sunitinib is reported to be effectual for some patients with advanced RCC, a certain number of patients are primarily resistant to sunitinib or acquired resistance with the treatment of sunitinib [33].The research of RCC progression and sunitinib resistance is of important value for RCC therapy.

In this research, our results showed that Hsa_circ_0072732 was highly expressed in RCC cells. Further investigations showed that silence Hsa_circ_0072732 could increase its sensitivity to sunitinib. Our research suggests a new target for RCC sunitinib therapy.

Recently, a large number of researchers have shown that circRNA could act as microRNA sponges to regulate the target genes to participate in biological activity. The regulatory mechanisms of circRNA in RCC chemoresistance remain unclear. In our research, we found that Hsa_circ_0072732 was highly expressed in two circRNA datasets (GSE108735 and GSE100186). The expression of Hsa_circ_0072732 was further confirmed to be highly expressed in RCC cells (Fig. 1A). Silence of Hsa_circ_0072732 significantly decreased the tumor growth in RCC treated with sunitinib (Fig. 2) by increasing ferroptosis.

Ferroptosis belongs to programmed cell deaths which is different from apoptosis, necrosis, or autophagy. Ferroptosis is iron-dependent and induced by lipid-reactive oxygen species. Solute carrier family 7 member 11 (SLC7A11) plays important roles in cystine uptake and oxidative reactions. SLC7A11 could maintain cell survival under oxidative stress. Abnormal increase in SLC7A11 expression induced ferroptosis inhibition in various cancers. In recent research, SLC7A11 has been shown as a promising target in lung cancer treatment [34]. The SLC7A11 transporter could export intracellular glutamate and import extracellular cystine, which contribute to cysteine for GSH synthesis [35]. SLC7A11 is upregulated to promote the proliferation, migration, and invasion of RCC cancer cells by impairing ferroptosis [36].

Interestingly, more and more researchers have uncovered that circRNAs also play an important role in ferroptosis regulation. Nevertheless, there is a lack of information on the effects of circRNAs on ferroptosis in RCC. Our research uncovered a new pathway to regulate SLC7A11. Our results showed that Hsa_circ_0072732 could regulate the RCC ferroptosis through SLC7A11. Ferroptosis is intimately related to RCC metabolic disturbances in ROS and MDA [37]. Metabolic processes in tumors are very vigorous with abundant reactive oxygen species [38]. RCC contains affluent membranous organelles and is sensitive to ferroptosis inducers [14]. What’s more, ferroptosis is shown to be closely connected with chemoresistance in tumors [14, 15, 39–41].

In our research, we did not include clinical data. In our future work, we will collect enough patient tissue and confirm our findings at the clinical level. We want to know the influence of ferroptosis activators or inhibitors on Hsa_circ_0072732-induced sunitinib resistance. Ferroptosis activator might be a promising drug in combination with sunitinib by inhibiting Hsa_circ_0072732-induced resistance. We also plan to confirm our findings in more RCC cell lines and other types of cancers. We hope to clearly investigate the function of Hsa_circ_0072732 /miR-548b-3p/ SLC7A11 pathway.

One of the effective strategies for early RCC is surgery. Resistance leads to poor prognosis, although chemotherapy benefits advanced RCC patients [42]. Sunitinib is a multi-target tyrosine kinase inhibitor that achieves the goal by blocking the activity of various tyrosine kinases involved in tumor cell proliferation and angiogenesis. Specifically, sunitinib can inhibit a variety of receptor tyrosine kinases, including vascular endothelial growth factor receptor 1, 2, and 3 (VEGFR1, VEGFR2, VEGFR3), platelet-derived growth factor receptor-alpha and -beta (PDGFR-alpha and PDGFR-beta), and colony-stimulating factor receptor 1 (CSF-1R). Sunitinib is the only FDA-approved VEGF TKI in resected high-risk ccRCC based on the positive phase III S-TRAC trial [43, 44]. Sunitinib has been a mainstay of treatment for advanced RCC for several years, newer agents and combinations have emerged as potential alternatives. The National Comprehensive Cancer Network (NCCN) guidelines currently recommend several first-line treatment options for advanced RCC, including sunitinib, pazopanib, cabozantinib, nivolumab plus ipilimumab, and pembrolizumab plus axitinib [45].

In the clinical treatment of RCC, sunitinib as a first-line treatment has been shown to significantly improve progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS) in patients [46, 47]. In recent years, with the development of tumor immunotherapy, the combination of sunitinib and other drugs has also received widespread attention [48]. One of the key limitations of our study is that we did not directly compare sunitinib to other systemic treatments for RCC, such as immune checkpoint inhibitors (ICI). Recent studies have shown that ICI, particularly in combination with tyrosine kinase inhibitors (TKI) like sunitinib, can improve outcomes in patients with advanced RCC [49, 50]. However, the research also showed that the addition of standard doses of sunitinib or pazopanib to nivolumab resulted in a high incidence of high-grade toxicities limiting the future development of either combination regimen [51, 52]. While the findings suggest that the success of combination regimens based on immune checkpoint inhibitors and antiangiogenic drugs may be dependent on the careful selection of the antiangiogenic component and dose [53]. Regarding the combination of ICI and sunitinib, there is no large-scale clinical trial data to support its use as the standard treatment. However, studies have shown that the combination of immunotherapy and targeted therapies may have some potential. The specific efficacy and safety of this combination therapy need to be validated by further clinical studies [49].

Regarding the limitations of our research, several factors might have influenced the results. First, there is a potential for selection bias that patients included in the study may not be representative of the overall RCC population. Second, our research relied on data from two databases, which may have resulted in an incomplete presentation of patient data. Third, the lack of follow-up data was not included in our research. Finally, there may be unmeasured factors that could have influenced the results, such as patient adherence to treatment and the use of concomitant medications.

More and more research has shown that ferroptosis participates in RCC progression and chemoresistance [39]. Targeting ferroptosis-related regulators would offer a promising therapeutic strategy for RCC. In our research, Hsa_circ_0072732 contributed to sunitinib chemoresistance by impairing ferroptosis. Targeting Hsa_circ_0072732 or ferroptosis would be a promising strategy for RCC sunitinib therapy.

Conclusion

In our research, we found that Hsa_circ_0072732 was significantly upregulated in RCC cells. Hsa_circ_0072732 contributed to sunitinib chemoresistance by impairing ferroptosis. Hsa_circ_0072732 exerts its function mainly by acting as sponges for miR-548b-3p and regulating the expression SLC7A11. Our research suggests that ferroptosis is involved in sunitinib resistance targeting ferroptosis is a promising way for RCC treatment.
