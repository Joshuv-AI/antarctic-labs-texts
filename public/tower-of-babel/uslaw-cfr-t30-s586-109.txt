30 C.F.R. § 586.109 — When must I notify BOEM if an action has been filed alleging that I am insolvent or bankrupt?
Citation: us:cfr:30:586.109
Creator: Bureau Of Ocean Energy Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-586.109
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 586 (Alternate Uses Of Existing Facilities On The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must notify BOEM within 3-business days after you learn of any action filed alleging that you are insolvent or bankrupt.
