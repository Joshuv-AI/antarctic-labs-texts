Correction: Synergistic design of gadolinium-doped polyaniline/MWCNT hybrid nanocomposites for high-performance supercapacitors
Authors: J Dominic, Victor Stupin, S Chitra Devi, S Arunkumar, B Gowri Shannkari, Ekaterina Silina, Shiva Prasad Kollur
Published: 2026

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-025-30564-7, published online 08 December 2025

The original version of this Article contained errors:

The SI units in the results and discussion were incorrect as a result, the sentence under the subsection “SEM and EDAX analysis”

“The analysis reveals that the K-series energy peaks for carbon, nitrogen and chlorine at approximately 0.3 eV, 0.4 eV and 2.6 eV, respectively. These elements are primarily attributed to the polyaniline matrix and possibly residual chloride from the metal salt precursor used during synthesis. Furthermore, the presence of gadolinium is clearly evidenced by the appearance of L-series peaks at around 1.2 eV and 6.0 eV."

now reads:

“The analysis reveals that the K-series energy peaks for carbon, nitrogen and chlorine at approximately 0.3 keV, 0.4 keV and 2.6 keV, respectively. These elements are primarily attributed to the polyaniline matrix and possibly residual chloride from the metal salt precursor used during synthesis. Furthermore, the presence of gadolinium is clearly evidenced by the appearance of L-series peaks at around 1.2 keV and 6.0 keV."

Also in Table 1 ΔS (J mol-1 K-1) was incorrectly given as ΔS (kJ mol-1).

Under the “Materials and Methods” section, the sentence “To map and verify the elemental identification of the composite, particularly the presence of carbon (C), nitrogen (N), chlorine (Cl) and erbium (Er)” now reads, “To map and verify the elemental identification of the composite, particularly the presence of carbon (C), nitrogen (N), chlorine (Cl) and gadolinium (Gd).

The original Article has been corrected.
