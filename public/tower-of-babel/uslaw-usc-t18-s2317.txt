18 U.S.C. § 2317 — Sale or receipt of livestock
Citation: us:usc:18:2317
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section2317
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 113 (Stolen Property). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever receives, conceals, stores, barters, buys, sells, or disposes of any livestock, moving in or constituting a part of interstate or foreign commerce, knowing the same to have been stolen, shall be fined under this title or imprisoned not more than five years, or both.
