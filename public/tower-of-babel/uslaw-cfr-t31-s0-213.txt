31 C.F.R. § 0.213 — Government issued charge cards.
Citation: us:cfr:31:0.213
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-0.213
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 0 (Department Of The Treasury Employee Rules Of Conduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Employees shall not make improper purchases with government contractor-issued charge cards.
(b) Employees shall timely pay undisputed amounts owed on government contractor-issued travel charge cards.
