7 U.S.C. § 4905 — Regulations
Citation: us:usc:7:4905
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section4905
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 80 (Watermelon Research And Promotion). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may issue such regulations as may be necessary to carry out the provisions of this chapter and the powers vested in the Secretary under this chapter.
