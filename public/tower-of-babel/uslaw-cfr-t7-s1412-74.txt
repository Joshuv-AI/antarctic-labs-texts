7 C.F.R. § 1412.74 — Failure to make election.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1412.74
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1412.74
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1412 (Agriculture Risk Coverage And Price Loss Coverage). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If all producers on a farm do not make a unanimous election during the period specified in § 1412.72, that farm will not have a valid election and any producer on the farm is not eligible for 2026 ARC or PLC enrollment or payments.
(b) If a valid election is not made for a farm in the 2026 crop year, FSA will not make any payments with respect to the farm for the 2026 crop year and the producers on the farm will, subject to § 1412.71(f), default to the same coverage for each covered commodity on the farm for the 2020 through 2025 crop years as was applicable for the 2015 through 2018 crop years or 2027 through 2031 crop years as was applicable for the 2025 crop year.
