On S S - J J -Noetherian Rings
Tushar Singh, Ajim Uddin Ansari, Shiv Datt Kumar

Abstract
Let R R be a commutative ring with identity, S ⊆ R S\subseteq R be a multiplicative set and J J be an ideal of R R . In this paper, we introduce the concept of S S - J J -Noetherian rings, which generalizes both J J -Noetherian rings and S S -Noetherian rings. We study several properties and charaterizations of this new class of rings. For instance, we prove Cohen’s-type theorem for S S - J J -Noetherian rings. Among other results, we establish the existence of S S -primary decomposition in S S - J J -Noetherian rings as a generalization of classical Lasker-Noether theorem.

A ring
