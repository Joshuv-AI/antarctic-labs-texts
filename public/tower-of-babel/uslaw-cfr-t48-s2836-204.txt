48 C.F.R. § 2836.204 — 2836.204 Disclosure in solicitations of the magnitude of construction projects.
Citation: us:cfr:48:2836.204
Creator: Chapter 28—Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-2836.204
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2836 (Construction And Architect-Engineer Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For construction projects over $10,000,000, present the magnitude in ranges as follows:
(a) Between $10,000,001 and $25,000,000;
(b) Between $25,000,001 and $100,000,000;
(c) Between $100,000,001 and $250,000,000;
(d) Between $250,000,001 and $500,000,000; and
(e) Over $500,000,000.
