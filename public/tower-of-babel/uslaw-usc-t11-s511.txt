11 U.S.C. § 511 — Rate of interest on tax claims
Citation: us:usc:11:511
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title11-section511
Context: United States Code, Title 11 (Bankruptcy); Chapter 5 (Creditors, The Debtor, And The Estate); Subchapter I (Creditors And Claims). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
If any provision of this title requires the payment of interest on a tax claim or on an administrative expense tax, or the payment of interest to enable a creditor to receive the present value of the allowed amount of a tax claim, the rate of interest shall be the rate determined under applicable nonbankruptcy law.
(b)
In the case of taxes paid under a confirmed plan under this title, the rate of interest shall be determined as of the calendar month in which the plan is confirmed.
