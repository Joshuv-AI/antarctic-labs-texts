49 U.S.C. § 14505 — State tax
Citation: us:usc:49:14505
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section14505
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part B (Motor Carriers, Water Carriers, Brokers, And Freight Forwarders); Chapter 145 (Federal-State Relations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A State or political subdivision thereof may not collect or levy a tax, fee, head charge, or other charge on—
(1)
a passenger traveling in interstate commerce by motor carrier;
(2)
the transportation of a passenger traveling in interstate commerce by motor carrier;
(3)
the sale of passenger transportation in interstate commerce by motor carrier; or
(4)
the gross receipts derived from such transportation.
