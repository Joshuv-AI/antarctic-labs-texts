48 C.F.R. § 14.302 — 14.302 Bid submission.
Citation: us:cfr:48:14.302
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-14.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 14 (Sealed Bidding). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Bids shall be submitted so that they will be received in the office designated in the invitation for bids not later than the exact time set for opening of bids.
