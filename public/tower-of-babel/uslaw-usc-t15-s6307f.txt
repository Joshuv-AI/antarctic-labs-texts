15 U.S.C. § 6307f — Required disclosures for judges and referees
Citation: us:usc:15:6307f
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section6307f
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 89 (Professional Boxing Safety). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A judge or referee shall not be entitled to receive any compensation, directly or indirectly, in connection with a boxing match until it provides to the boxing commission responsible for regulating the match in a State a statement of all consideration, including reimbursement for expenses, that will be received from any source for participation in the match.
