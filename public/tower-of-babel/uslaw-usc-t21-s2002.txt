21 U.S.C. § 2002 — Records, audit, and report
Citation: us:usc:21:2002
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title21-section2002
Context: United States Code, Title 21 (Food And Drugs); Chapter 25 (Miscellaneous Anti-Drug Abuse Provisions); Subchapter I (Anti-Doping Agency). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Records
The United States Anti-Doping Agency shall keep correct and complete records of account.
(b) Report
The United States Anti-Doping Agency shall submit an annual report to Congress which shall include—
(1)
an audit conducted and submitted in accordance with section 10101 of title 36; and
(2)
a description of the activities of the agency.
