20 C.F.R. § 408.624 — How do we investigate a representative payee applicant?
Citation: us:cfr:20:408.624
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-408.624
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 408 (Special Benefits For Certain World War Ii Veterans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Before selecting an individual or organization as your representative payee, we investigate him or her following the rules in § 404.2024 of this chapter.
