45 C.F.R. § 213.4 — Suspension of rules.
Citation: us:cfr:45:213.4
Creator: Office Of Family Assistance (Assistance Programs), Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-213.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 213 (Practice And Procedure For Hearings To States On Conformity Of Public Assistance Plans To Federal Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon notice to all parties, the Administrator or the presiding officer, with respect to matters pending before him and within his jurisdiction, may modify or waive any rule in this part upon determination that no party will be unduly prejudiced and the ends of justice will thereby be served.
