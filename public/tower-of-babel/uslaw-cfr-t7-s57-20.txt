7 C.F.R. § 57.20 — Inspection in accordance with methods prescribed or approved.
Citation: us:cfr:7:57.20
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-57.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 57 (Inspection Of Eggs (Egg Products Inspection Act)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Inspection of eggs shall be rendered pursuant to these regulations and under such conditions and in accordance with such methods as may be prescribed or approved by the Administrator.
