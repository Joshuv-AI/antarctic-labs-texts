14 C.F.R. § 158.81 — General.
Citation: us:cfr:14:158.81
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-158.81
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 158 (Passenger Facility Charges (Pfc'S)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart contains the procedures for termination of PFCs or loss of Federal airport grant funds for violations of this part or 49 U.S.C. 40117. This subpart does not address the circumstances under which the authority to collect PFCs may be terminated for violations of 49 U.S.C. 47523 through 47528.
