16 U.S.C. § 1684 — Annual reports
Citation: us:usc:16:1684
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section1684
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 36 (Forest And Rangeland Renewable Resources Planning); Subchapter IV (Wood Residue Utilization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall make annual reports to the Congress on the programs authorized by this subchapter. These reports shall be submitted with the reports required under section 1606(c) of this title.
