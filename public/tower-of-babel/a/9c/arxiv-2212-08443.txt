Comment on ‘Comment on ‘Affine density, von Neumann dimension and a problem of Perelomov”
Luís Daniel Abreu

Abstract
. In the Letter ‘Comment on ‘Affine density, von Neumann dimension and a problem of Perelomov’ https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2211.04879 , by Prof. J. L. Romero, it is claimed that the main theorem of Ref2 := [Adv. Math. 407, Article ID 108564, 22 p. (2022)] is included in the prior research survey Ref1 := [Expo. Math., 40(2), 265-301, 2022]. We provide some background, and demonstrate with hard facts that this claim is false , as well as other claims in the Letter which, in most cases, either result from wrong mathematical reasoning or objectively differ from what is written in the published material. The author might be interested in publicly retracting and apologising for his comments.

Background on the wavelet transform

Consider the unitary representation of the affine group
G_{a}=\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{R}^{+}\equiv\mathbb{C}^{+}
, acting on the Hardy space
H^{2}\left(\mathbb{C}^{+}\right)

\pi(z)\psi(t):=\frac{1}{\sqrt{y}}\psi\left(\frac{t-x}{y}\right),\quad z=x+iy\in\mathbb{C}^{+}\text{.}

The continuous wavelet transform
of a function
f
with respect to a
wavelet
\psi
is defined, for every
z=x+iy\in\mathbb{C}^{+}

(2.1)

W_{\psi}f(z)=\left\langle f,\pi({z})\psi\right\rangle_{H^{2}\left(\mathbb{C}^{+}\right)}\text{.}

[
12

\alpha>0

in terms of the Laguerre polynomials as follows

(2.2)

(\mathcal{F}\psi_{n}^{\alpha})(\xi):=\xi^{\frac{\alpha}{2}}e^{-\xi}L_{n}^{\alpha}(2\xi),\quad L_{n}^{\alpha}(t)=\frac{t^{-\alpha}e^{t}}{n!}\left(\frac{d}{dt}\right)^{n}(e^{-t}t^{\alpha+n}),\text{ }\xi>0\text{.}

We will write
d\mu^{+}(z)=y^{-2}d\mu_{\mathbb{C}^{+}}(z)
, where
d\mu_{\mathbb{C}^{+}}(z)\
is the Lebesgue measure in
\mathbb{C}^{+}
. The image
of

W_{\psi_{n}^{\alpha}}:H^{2}\left(\mathbb{C}^{+}\right)\rightarrow L^{2}(\mathbb{C}^{+},d\mu^{+})

k_{\psi_{n}^{\alpha}}(z,w)=\frac{1}{C_{\psi_{n}^{\alpha}}}W_{\psi_{n}^{\alpha}}\psi_{n}^{\alpha}({w}^{-1}.z),
(where
.
stands for the product on
G_{a}

\quad k_{\psi_{n}^{\alpha}}(z,z)=\frac{\|\psi_{n}^{\alpha}\|_{2}^{2}}{C_{\psi_{n}^{\alpha}}}

A further remark about the projective representations. For groups with
trivial Lie algebra cohomology as the case of the whole
SL(2,\mathbb{R})
,
one could think about using Bargmann’s theorem
[
9
]
to lift the
projective representations to representations in the universal cover, where
they were classified by Pukanszky
[
27
]
and Sally
[
30
]
. But
this is
not possible
to do for the
restriction of the
projective representation to an arbitrary Fuchsian group
. Indeed, in
Theorem A1 in
[
20
, Appendix A]
, Jones proved that, for a Fuchsian
group associated with a Riemann surface of genus
g
, the cohomology
obstruction only vanishes (a requirement to lift the representation) if
\alpha
is an integer multiple of
\frac{1}{g-1}
. Thus, for a Fuchsian
group of genus
g=2
, this requires
\alpha
to be an integer and we are
back in the holomorphic discrete series. Thus, there is no possibility of
assuring unitary equivalences between the results of Theorem 2.1 by general
results. The action of the projective representation (
3.1
) must
be considered in its explicit form, acting on each Fuchsian group, in its
associated space
\mathcal{W}_{\psi_{n}^{\alpha}}(\mathbb{C}^{+})
. Thus,
from any mathematical perspective, (
3.1
) is a
new
projective representation of
PSL(2,R)
