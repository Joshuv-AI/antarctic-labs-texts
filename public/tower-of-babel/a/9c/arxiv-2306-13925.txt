A Robin boundary condition problem of sand transport equations] An existence and homogenization results of degenerate parabolic sand problem transport equations with robin boundary condition


Abstract
This paper focuses on the theoretical study of degenerate parabolic sand transport equations in a non periodic domain with Robin boundary condition. We give existence and uniqueness results for the models which is also homogenized. Finally some corrector results are given.

Homogenization and corrector results

3.1
On Two-Scale convergence

In this subsection we are going to recall the notion of Two-Scale convergence.

Definition 3.1

A sequence of functions
(z^{\epsilon})
in
L^{\infty}([0,T),L^{2}(\Omega))
is said to Two-Scale converge to

U\in L^{\infty}([0,T),L^{\infty}_{\#}(\mathbb{R},L^{2}(\Omega)))

if for every
\psi\in\mathcal{C}([0,T),\mathcal{C}_{\#}(\mathbb{R},\mathcal{C}(\Omega)))
we have

\displaystyle\lim_{\epsilon\rightarrow 0}\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{T}z^{\epsilon}(t,x)\psi(t,\frac{t}{\epsilon},x)dt\,dx=\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{T}\int_{0}^{1}U(t,\theta,x)\psi(t,\theta,x)d\theta\,dt\,dx.

(3.1)

In
[
1
]
and
[
40
]
, the following theorem is given.

Theorem 3.1

If a sequence
(z^{\epsilon})
is bounded in
L^{\infty}([0,T),L^{2}({\mathbb{T}}^{2})),
there exists a subsequence still denoted
(z^{\epsilon})
and a function
U\in L^{\infty}([0,T),L^{\infty}_{\#}(\mathbb{R},L^{2}(\Omega)))

such that

\displaystyle z^{\epsilon}\longrightarrow U\,\,\textrm{Two-Scale}.

(3.2)
