Introduction

The main theme of this paper

\operatorname{Var}(\mathfrak{C}):=\{\mathfrak{p}\in\operatorname{Spec}(R):R_{\mathfrak{p}}\emph{isnotnormal}\},

Lemma 2.3

Suppose

(S_{2})

If
\operatorname{pd}_{R}(\mathfrak{C})<\infty

Proof.

Let
\mathfrak{p}

\operatorname{Spec}(R)
of height one.
Recall that
\mathfrak{C}=\operatorname{Hom}_{R}(\overline{R},R)

\overline{R}

\operatorname{Hom}_{R}(\overline{R},-)_{\mathfrak{p}}=\operatorname{Hom}_{R_{\mathfrak{p}}}(\overline{R}_{\mathfrak{p}},-_{\mathfrak{p}})=\operatorname{Hom}_{R_{\mathfrak{p}}}(\overline{R_{\mathfrak{p}}},-_{\mathfrak{p}}).

From this,
(\mathfrak{C}_{R})_{\mathfrak{p}}=\mathfrak{C}_{R_{\mathfrak{p}}}

of
(\mathfrak{C}_{R})_{\mathfrak{p}}
is finite. Recall that

\operatorname{pd}_{R_{\mathfrak{p}}}(\frac{R_{\mathfrak{p}}}{(\mathfrak{C}_{R})_{\mathfrak{p}}})\leq 0pt(R_{\mathfrak{p}})\leq\dim(R_{\mathfrak{p}})=1,

is not subset of any proper principal ideal. In sum,
\mathfrak{C}_{R_{\mathfrak{p}}}={R_{\mathfrak{p}}}
.
This implies

{R_{\mathfrak{p}}}
is normal. By dimension consideration,

{R_{\mathfrak{p}}}
is regular. Following Fact
2.2
,

R
is normal.
∎

Let us drop the
(S_{2})
condition.

Observation 2.4
.

If
\operatorname{pd}_{R}(\mathfrak{C})<\infty

Proof.

We processed by induction on

\mathfrak{m}

and analytically unramified behaved well with respect to localization. It turns out that
\mathfrak{C}_{R_{\mathfrak{p}}}=R_{\mathfrak{p}}
for all

\frac{{R}}{\mathfrak{C}}
is of finite length. Suppose on the way of contradiction that
\frac{{R}}{\mathfrak{C}}\neq 0

2.3

Since
