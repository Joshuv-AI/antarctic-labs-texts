Asymptotically Consistent Measures of General Quantum Resources: Discord, Non-Markovianity, and Non-Gaussianity
Kohdai Kuroiwa, Hayata Yamasaki

Abstract
Quantum resource theories provide a unified framework to quantitatively analyze inherent quantum properties as resources for quantum information processing. So as to investigate the best way for quantifying resources, desirable axioms for resource quantification have been extensively studied through axiomatic approaches. However, a conventional way of resource quantification by resource measures with such desired axioms may contradict rates of asymptotic transformation between resourceful quantum states due to an approximation in the transformation. In this paper, we establish an alternative axiom, asymptotic consistency of resource measures, and we investigate asymptotically consistent resource measures , which quantify resources without contradicting the rates of the asymptotic resource transformation. We prove that relative entropic measures are consistent with the rates for a broad class of resources, i.e. , all convex finite-dimensional resources, e.g. , entanglement, coherence, and magic, and even some nonconvex or infinite-dimensional resources such as quantum discord, non-Markovianity, and non-Gaussianity. These results show that consistent resource measures are widely applicable to the quantitative analysis of various inherent quantum-mechanical properties.

Introduction

Quantum resource theories (QRTs)
Chitambar and Gour 2019
;

provide a unified framework for quantitatively analyzing quantum properties, such as entanglement
Horodecki et al. 2009
and magic

;
Howard and Campbell 2017
;

,
which underlies the advantage of quantum information processing over classical information processing.
General frameworks to reveal the universal properties of quantum resources have been widely studied
Horodecki and Oppenheim 2013
;
Brandão and Gour 2015
;
Korzekwa et al. 2019
;
Liu et al. 2019a
;
Vijayan et al. 2020
;
Liu et al. 2019b
;

;
Takagi and Regula 2019
;
Fang and Liu 2020
;
Regula et al. 2020
;
Sparaciari et al. 2020
;

,
and quantification of resources is one of the major focuses of QRTs

;

;
Bromley et al. 2018
;
Anshu et al. 2018
;
Uola et al. 2019
;
Gonda and Spekkens 2019
;
Regula et al. 2021
;
Lami et al. 2021
.
To quantify resources, we use real-valued functions of states called
resource measures
.
Resource measures can quantify resources without contradicting one-shot convertibility of resources; that is, the resource amount quantified by a resource measure does not increase when we transform a resource by free operations without error.
For example,
the relative entropy of resource
, that is, the relative entropy between a given state and its closest free state, is conventionally used in various QRTs
Vedral et al. 1997
;
Veitch et al. 2014
;
Baumgratz et al. 2014
;
Brandão et al. 2013
;
Modi et al. 2010
;
Ibinson et al. 2008
;
Genoni et al. 2008
;
Lami and Shirokov 2021
.
With various resource measures proposed, desirable properties of resource measures have been extensively studied through axiomatic approaches
Vidal 2000
;
Horodecki 2001
;
Donald et al. 2002
;
Synak-Radtke and Horodecki 2006
;
Girard et al. 2014
;
Regula 2017
;
Kuroiwa and Yamasaki 2020
to seek the best resource measure.

On the other hand, it is known that resources are not necessarily comparable in terms of the exact convertibility.
For example, in the QRT of magic for qutrits, there are two classes of states impossible to be exactly converted to each other using free operations
Veitch et al. 2014
.
Moreover, resource measures with conventionally adopted axioms,
i.e.,
asymptotic continuity and additivity, may contradict rates of asymptotic state conversion
Kuroiwa and Yamasaki 2020
, where many copies of a given state are converted by free operations into many copies of a target state within a vanishingly small but nonzero error.
Therefore, it is vital to investigate properties of resource measures associated with the asymptotic state conversion in addition to the exact conversion.
While these two concepts, resource measures and asymptotic state conversion, were previously discussed separately,
we proposed in Ref.
Kuroiwa and Yamasaki 2020
a concept of
asymptotically consistent resource measures
, or
consistent resource measures
for short, which quantifies resources without contradicting the rates of the asymptotic state conversion as well as the exact conversion.
Reference
Kuroiwa and Yamasaki 2020
also shows that a relative entropic measure called
the regularized relative entropy of resource
serves as a consistent resource measure in QRTs with particular restrictions, namely, convex and finite-dimensional QRTs with a full-rank free state.

However, physically well-motivated resources do not necessarily satisfy these restrictions.
For example, the sets of states that have no quantum discord,
e.g.
, classical-classical states and classical-quantum states, are not convex
Bera et al. 2017
.
In addition, the set of quantum Markov chains
Accardi and Frigerio 1983
and that of Gaussian states
Weedbrook et al. 2012
are also known to be nonconvex.
Moreover, the Gaussian states are defined on an infinite-dimensional state space.
Therefore, when we regard these properties as quantum resources, the existing technique for proving the consistency of the entropic measure is no longer directly applicable
Kuroiwa and Yamasaki 2020
;
Weis and Knauf 2012
,
and a substantial breakthrough is needed for further development of general QRTs that cover such nonconvex or infinite-dimensional resources.

In this paper, we investigate the regularized relative entropy of resource as a prospective candidate for a consistent resource measure even for the general classes of resources.
We prove the consistency of the regularized relative entropy of resource in
all
finite-dimensional convex QRTs even without full-rank free states.
Moreover, as physically well-motivated nonconvex or infinite-dimensional resources, we study discord, non-Markovianity, and non-Gaussianity.
Even though the proof technique of the previous work cannot be straightforwardly applied due to the nonconvexity and the infinite-dimensionality,
we analyze the consistency of the regularized relative entropy of resource for these three resources.
A more detailed overview of our results is at the beginning of Sec.
III
.
Here, we emphasize that the concept of consistent resource measures accomplishes the fundamental and physically intuitive property that resources do not increase under free operations in both asymptotic and one-shot state conversions.
Even though the regularized relative entropy of resource is a measure asymptotically defined, which may not be extracted with one-shot manipulation,
the regularized relative entropy still qualifies as a valid resource measure with monotonicity under one-shot state conversion.

Our results show the existence of a consistent resource measure even for physically important classes of nonconvex or infinite-dimensional resources, namely, discord, non-Markovianity, and non-Gaussianity,
which are not covered in our initial work
Kuroiwa and Yamasaki 2020
introducing consistent resource measures.
Our analysis should be an essential first step for the breakthrough in general QRTs to broadly cover the general class of resources.
We believe that our investigation for consistent resource measures leads to further understandings of quantifications of resources useful for studying a larger class of physically well-motivated quantum phenomena.

II
Consistent resource measure

In this section, we provide a brief review of consistent resource measures.
For more details, see Sections 6.3 and 6.4 of Ref.
Kuroiwa and Yamasaki 2020
.
Throughout this paper, we let
\mathcal{D}(\mathcal{H})
denote the set of states on a quantum system
\mathcal{H}
, and we consider a subset
\mathcal{S}(\mathcal{H})\subseteq\mathcal{D}(\mathcal{H})
to be the set of states of interest in QRTs.
In a QRT, free operations are defined as a subclass of quantum operations (linear completely positive and trace-preserving maps) that is closed under composition and tensor product, and includes the identity map and the (partial) trace
Chitambar and Gour 2019
;
Kuroiwa and Yamasaki 2020
.
Free states on
\mathcal{H}
are states in
\mathcal{S}(\mathcal{H})
into which an arbitrary state can be converted by free operations.
To begin with, we provide a definition of a resource measure.

Definition 1

(Resource Measure)
.

A resource measure
R_{\mathcal{H}}
is a family of real functions from
\mathcal{S}\left(\mathcal{H}\right)
to
\mathbb{R}
satisfying
monotonicity
:
for any states
\phi\in\mathcal{S}(\mathcal{H})
and
\psi\in\mathcal{S}(\mathcal{H}^{\prime})
,

\exists\mathcal{N}:\mathrm{free\,\,operation\,\,s.t.\,\,}\mathcal{N}(\phi)=\psi\Rightarrow R_{\mathcal{H}}(\phi)\geqq R_{\mathcal{H}^{\prime}}(\psi).

We may omit the subscript of
R_{\mathcal{H}}
to write
R
for brevity when the system
\mathcal{H}
is obvious.

In Ref.
Kuroiwa and Yamasaki 2020
, a concept of
consistency
of a resource measure is introduced, which we investigate in this paper.
As implied in the definition, a resource measure
R
quantifies resources consistently with the exact state conversion under the free operations.
By also considering the consistency with the rate of the asymptotic state conversion, we give the definition of a consistent resource measure.

Definition 2

(Asymptotically Consistent Resource Measure
Kuroiwa and Yamasaki 2020
)
.

For quantum systems
\mathcal{H}
and
\mathcal{H}^{\prime}
, a resource measure
R
is called
asymptotically consistent
, or
consistent
for short, if for any states
\phi\in\mathcal{S}\left(\mathcal{H}\right)
and
\psi\in\mathcal{S}\left(\mathcal{H}^{\prime}\right)
, it holds that

R_{\mathcal{H}^{\prime}}\left(\psi\right)r\left(\phi\to\psi\right)\leqq R_{\mathcal{H}}\left(\phi\right).

(1)

In Eq. (
1
),
r\left(\phi\to\psi\right)
is the rate of the asymptotic state conversion between
\phi
and
\psi
definied as

\displaystyle r\left(\phi\to\psi\right)\coloneqq

\displaystyle\inf\Big\{r\geqq 0:\exists\left(\mathcal{N}_{n}:\mathrm{free\,\,operation}\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}},

\displaystyle\liminf_{n\to\infty}\left\|\mathcal{N}_{n}\left(\phi^{\otimes n}\right)-\psi^{\otimes\left\lceil rn\right\rceil}\right\|_{1}=0\Big\},

where
\|\cdot\|_{1}
is the trace norm, and
\lceil{}\cdots{}\rceil
is the ceiling function.

We here note that from the definition (
1
), consistent resource measures are weakly additive
Kuroiwa and Yamasaki 2020
;
that is,
R(\phi^{\otimes n})=nR(\phi)
for all states
\phi
and all positive integers
n
.
In Ref.
Kuroiwa and Yamasaki 2020
, a sufficient condition for the
regularization

R^{\infty}
of a resource measure
R
, defined as
R^{\infty}(\phi)\coloneqq\lim_{n\to\infty}R(\phi^{\otimes n})/n
, to be consistent was provided.
The regularization of a resource measure also serves as a resource measure.
The sufficient condition consists of the following conventionally considered properties for a resource measure:

1.

Asymptotic continuity
:
For any sequence of positive integers
{\left(n_{i}\right)}_{i\in\mathbb{N}}
, and any sequences of states
{\left(\phi_{n_{i}}\in\mathcal{S}\left(\mathcal{H}^{\otimes n_{i}}\right)\right)}_{i}
and
{\left(\psi_{n_{i}}\in\mathcal{S}\left(\mathcal{H}^{\otimes n_{i}}\right)\right)}_{i}
satisfying
\lim_{i\to\infty}\left\|\phi_{n_{i}}-\psi_{n_{i}}\right\|_{1}=0
,
it holds that
\lim_{i\to\infty}|R_{\mathcal{H}^{\otimes n_{i}}}(\phi_{n_{i}})-R_{\mathcal{H}^{\otimes n_{i}}}(\psi_{n_{i}})|/n_{i}=0
.

2.

Subadditivity
:
For any states
\phi
and
\psi
, it holds that

R\left(\phi\otimes\psi\right)\leqq R\left(\phi\right)+R\left(\psi\right)
.

Note that, in Ref.
Ferrari et al. 2021
, another sufficient condition for consistency of resource measures is given;
in this paper, we investigate the sufficient condition shown in the following lemma
Kuroiwa and Yamasaki 2020
.

Lemma 3

(Sufficient Condition for Consistency)
.

The regularization
R^{\infty}
of a resource measure
R
is consistent if
R
is asymptotically continuous and subadditive.

From this lemma, a promising way for constructing a consistent resource measure is to consider a subadditive resource measure and then to check its asymptotic continuity.
As a subadditive resource measure, we here consider the relative entropy of resource
R_{\textup{R}}
, which is widely studied in various QRTs
Vedral et al. 1997
;
Veitch et al. 2014
;
Baumgratz et al. 2014
;
Brandão et al. 2013
;
Modi et al. 2010
;
Ibinson et al. 2008
;
Genoni et al. 2008
.
For our purpose, it may not be suitable to use non-subadditive measures such as robustness-based measures
Vidal and Tarrach 1999
;
Steiner 2003
;
Piani et al. 2016
;
Howard and Campbell 2017
;
Regula 2017
.

Definition 4

(Relative Entropy of Resource)
.

The relative entropy of resource
R_{\textup{R}}
is defined as

R_{\textup{R}}(\phi)\coloneqq\min_{\psi:\mathrm{free}}\relent(\phi\|\psi)

(2)

for all states
\phi
, where
\relent(\cdot\|\cdot)
is the relative entropy
\relent(\rho||\sigma)\coloneqq\tr(\rho\log_{2}\rho-\rho\log_{2}\sigma)
.

The regularization of the relative entropy of resource
R^{\infty}_{R}
is called
the regularizaed relative entropy of resource
.
Reference
Brandão and Plenio 2010
gives an operational interpretation of the regularlized relative entropy of resource;
the regularized relative entropy of resource is considered as the optimal rate of asymptotic hypothesis testing where one aims to distinguish a given state and its closest free state.

III
Main results

In this section, we investigate the consistency of the regularized relative entropy of resource for general resources of physical importance.
Before we go through further details, we briefly overview the backgrounds and our contributions.

In Ref.
Kuroiwa and Yamasaki 2020
, it was shown that the regularized relative entropy of resource is consistent in all finite-dimensional convex QRTs with a full-rank free state.
The proof was based on asymptotic continuity of the relative entropy of resource in such QRTs
Synak-Radtke and Horodecki 2006
;
Winter 2016
.

Here, we consider relaxing the assumption.
First, in Sec.
III.1
, we study the necessity of full-rank free states.
Indeed, by refining the definition of consistency, we show that the regularized relative entropy of resource is consistent in finite-dimensional convex QRTs even without full-rank free states.
Next, we investigate physically well-motivated nonconvex or infinite-dimensional resources.
We will overview the physical significance of these resources at the beginning of each section.
For nonconvex resources, namely, discord (Sec.
III.2
) and non-Markovianity (Sec.
III.3
),
despite the existence of a counterexample for asymptotic continuity of the relative entropy of resource
Weis and Knauf 2012
,
we prove that the regularized relative entropy of resource is consistent.
Moreover, we analyze an infinite-dimensional resource, non-Gaussianity, in Sec.
III.4
.
Contrary to the statement in the previous work
Genoni et al. 2008
, we prove that the relative entropy of non-Gaussianity is not continuous even with a reasonable energy constraint.
On the other hand, if we take the convex hull of the set of Gaussian states, we show that the relative entropy of non-Gaussianity is asymptotically continuous under an appropriate energy constraint;
therefore, the regularized relative entropy can be employed as a consistent resource measure under the energy constraint in this convex but infinite-dimensional QRT.

III.1
Finite dimensional convex QRTs

In this section, we show that the regularized relative entropy of resource is indeed consistent in all finite-dimensional convex QRTs even without full-rank free states.
This result contributes to removing an arguably artificial restriction of imposing full-rankness of free states in considering general QRTs.
Here, the problem is that if no full-rank free state exists, the regularized relative entropy of resource
R_{\textup{R}}
in Eq. (
2
) may become infinite, and the definition of consistency may become an indeterminate form.
To resolve these problems, we consider the inequality (
1
) to be also true
when
R(\phi)=\infty
or
when
R(\phi)<\infty
,
R(\psi)=\infty
, and
r(\phi\to\psi)=0
.
If the set of free states contains a full-rank state, the regularized relative entropy of resource becomes consistent as shown in the previous work.
As shown in the Supplemental Materials, we show that these two cases cover all the cases where
R
diverges to infinity.
Therefore, under this refined definition to take infinities into account,
the regularized relative entropy of resources can be consistent even in the QRTs without full-rank free states.

III.2
Discord

In this section, we investigate consistent resource measures for discord.
Discord is conceptually understood as a form of quantum correlation; it is defined as the difference between total (quantum and classical) correlation and classical correlation
Bera et al. 2017
.
While entanglement is widely appreciated as a quantum correlation enhancing various quantum information processing tasks
Horodecki et al. 2009
,
it has been revealed that discord serves as a resource for several tasks even without the existence of entanglement
Datta et al. 2008
;
Dakić et al. 2012
;
Li et al. 2012
;
Madhok and Datta 2013
;
Gu et al. 2012
;
Pirandola 2014
;
Weedbrook et al. 2016
.
In addition, discord also has operational meanings in several tasks
Madhok and Datta 2011
;
Cavalcanti et al. 2011
.
From such operational significance of discord, it is essential to establish a consistent way for quantitative analysis of discord.

To analyze discord for this purpose, one may consider a QRT of discord by choosing, as free operations, a class of operations that preserves a set of states with zero discord.
As explained in Sec.
II
, each choice of free operations determines the corresponding set of free states.
For discord, there have been three kinds of sets of states conventionally considered as free states.
Here, we only consider the bipartite case for simplicity of presentation, while it is also possible to consider multipartite cases
Modi et al. 2010
.
Hereafter, we consider two parties
A
and
B
with finite-dimensional quantum systems
\mathcal{H}^{A}
and
\mathcal{H}^{B}
respectively.
Then, let
D
denote the dimension of the composite system
\mathcal{H}^{A}\otimes\mathcal{H}^{B}
.
The first set of free states is the set of classical-classical states

\mathrm{c}\mhyph\mathrm{c}\coloneqq\left\{\sum_{k}p_{k}\ket{a_{k}}\bra{a_{k}}^{A}\otimes\ket{b_{k}}\bra{b_{k}}^{B}\right\}
,
where
\{\ket{a_{k}}^{A}\}
is an orthonormal basis of
\mathcal{H}^{A}
and
\{\ket{b_{k}}^{B}\}
is an orthonormal basis of
\mathcal{H}^{B}
.
Note that these bases are not necessarily fixed to some standard bases in contrast to the QRT of coherence
Streltsov et al. 2017
.
The second one is the set of quantum-classical states

\mathrm{q}\mhyph\mathrm{c}\coloneqq\left\{\sum_{k}p_{k}\rho_{k}^{A}\otimes\ket{b_{k}}\bra{b_{k}}^{B}\right\}
,
and the last one is the set of classical-quantum states
\mathrm{c}\mhyph\mathrm{q}
, where the roles of
A
and
B
in

\mathrm{q}\mhyph\mathrm{c}
are interchanged.
Due to the symmetry of the definitions of
\mathrm{q}\mhyph\mathrm{c}
and
\mathrm{c}\mhyph\mathrm{q}
, we only consider
\mathrm{c}\mhyph\mathrm{c}
and
\mathrm{q}\mhyph\mathrm{c}
in the rest of this section.
Since the bases
\{\ket{a_{k}}^{A}\}
and
\{\ket{b_{k}}^{B}\}
are not fixed but arbitrary, it is clear that none of these three sets are convex.
Then, for each of these sets of free states, discord can be analyzed in the framework of QRTs.
Due to the nonconvexity of these sets, however, these QRTs of discord are nonconvex QRTs.

Based on these free states, we can define the relative entropy of discord as

D^{\mathcal{{F}}}_{\textup{rel}}(\phi^{AB})\coloneqq\min_{\psi^{AB}\in\mathcal{F}}\relent(\phi^{AB}\|\psi^{AB}),

(3)

where
\mathcal{F}
is either
\mathrm{c}\mhyph\mathrm{c}
or
\mathrm{q}\mhyph\mathrm{c}
.
Indeed, we derive a simple expression of the relative entropic measure for any choice of free states.
In more detail, for all states
\phi^{AB}\in\mathcal{D}(\mathcal{H}^{A}\otimes\mathcal{H}^{B})
, it holds that

~D^{\mathcal{F}}_{\textup{rel}}(\phi^{AB})=\min_{\chi^{\mathcal{F}}_{\phi}}\entropy(\chi^{\mathcal{F}}_{\phi})-\entropy(\phi^{AB}),

(4)

with the von Neumann entropy
\entropy(\rho)\coloneqq-\tr(\rho\log_{2}\rho)
.
The minimization in Eq. (
4
) is taken over the free states

\chi^{\mathrm{c}\mhyph\mathrm{c}}_{\phi}\coloneqq\sum_{k}(\bra{a_{k},b_{k}})\phi^{AB}(\ket{a_{k},b_{k}})\ket{a_{k},b_{k}}\bra{a_{k},b_{k}}^{AB}
or

\chi^{\mathrm{q}\mhyph\mathrm{c}}_{\phi}\coloneqq\sum_{k}(\mathbbm{1}^{A}\otimes\bra{b_{k}})\phi^{AB}(\mathbbm{1}^{A}\otimes\ket{b_{k}})\otimes\ket{b_{k}}\bra{b_{k}}^{B}

with the identity operator
\mathbbm{1}
.
See also Ref.
Modi et al. 2010
for derivation of this expression in the case
\mathcal{F}=\mathrm{c}\mhyph\mathrm{c}
.

With this simplified expression of the relative entropy of discord, we prove that this relative entropic measure is asymptotically continuous by repeatedly using the Fannes-Audenaert inequality
Audenaert 2007
on the asymptotic continuity of the von Neumann entropy.
Indeed, we show that for all states
\phi^{AB},\psi^{AB}\in\mathcal{D}(\mathcal{H}^{A}\otimes\mathcal{H}^{B})
, it holds that

\displaystyle|D^{\mathcal{F}}_{\textup{rel}}(\phi^{AB})-D^{\mathcal{F}}_{\textup{rel}}(\psi^{AB})|

(5)

\displaystyle\leqq\|\phi^{AB}-\psi^{AB}\|_{1}\log_{2}D+2h_{2}\left(\frac{\|\phi^{AB}-\psi^{AB}\|_{1}}{2}\right),

where
h_{2}
is the binary entropy function defined as
h_{2}(x)\coloneqq-x\log_{2}x-(1-x)\log_{2}(1-x)
for
x\in[0,1]
.
Then, Eq. (
5
) implies asymptotic continuity of the relative entropy of discord.
The rigorous proof is shown in the Supplemental Materials.
Considering Lemma
3
and Eq. (
5
) together,
we conclude that the regularization of the relative entropy of discord is consistent for all the choices of free states.

On the other hand, we also prove that another discord measure,
measurement-based quantum discord

Ollivier and Zurek 2001
,
is subadditive and asymptotically continuous.
Therefore, we can also employ its regularization as a consistent resource measure for discord.
The detailed definition and proofs are shown in the Supplemental Materials.

III.3
Non-Markovianity

In this section, we prove that the regularized relative entropy of non-Markovianity is consistent.
The quantum Markov chain is a quantum extension of the classical Markov chain, originally formulated in Ref.
Accardi and Frigerio 1983
.
In the classical case, a sequence of random variables
XYZ
is said to be a Markov chain if
Z
conditioned on
Y
is independent of
X
, which is indeed equivalent to
I(X:Z|Y)=0
, where
I(X:Z|Y)
is the conditional mutual information
Wyner 1978
.
Analogously, a quantum state
\phi^{ABC}\in\mathcal{D}(\mathcal{H}^{A}\otimes\mathcal{H}^{B}\otimes\mathcal{H}^{C})
is said to be a quantum Markov chain if
C
conditioned on
B
is independent of
A

Accardi and Frigerio 1983
, which is indeed equivalent to the condition
I(A:C|B)_{\phi}=0

Hayden et al. 2004
in terms of the quantum conditional mutual information
Cerf and Adami 1998
.
Non-Markovianity serves as a resource in various tasks.
For example, while a classical non-Markov chain enables the secret key agreement
Maurer and Wolf 1999
,
quantum non-Markovianity is exploited as a resource in the quantum one-time pad
Sharma et al. 2020
,
which is a protocol to ensure secure communication between two parties.

The QRT of non-Markovianity has been established in Refs.
Wakakuwa 2017
;
Wakakuwa 2021
to analyze this quantum version of the Markov property in the QRT framework.
Hereafter, we consider the tripartite system
\mathcal{H}^{A}\otimes\mathcal{H}^{B}\otimes\mathcal{H}^{C}
with dimension
D
in total.
The set of quantum Markov chains
\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{Markov}}
is defined as
\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{Markov}}\coloneqq\{\psi\in\mathcal{D}(\mathcal{H}^{A}\otimes\mathcal{H}^{B}\otimes\mathcal{H}^{C}):I(A:C|B)_{\psi}=0\}
.
Here, note that
\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{Markov}}
is not convex
Wakakuwa 2017
; the QRT of non-Markovianity is a nonconvex QRT.
Then, the relative entropy of non-Markovianity is defined as

\Delta(\phi^{ABC})\coloneqq\min_{\psi\in\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{Markov}}}\relent(\phi^{ABC}\|\psi^{ABC}).

(6)

We prove that the relative entropy of non-Markovianity is asymptotically continuous.
We show that for all states
\phi,\psi\in\mathcal{D}(\mathcal{H}^{A}\otimes\mathcal{H}^{B}\otimes\mathcal{H}^{C})
satisfying
\|\phi-\psi\|_{1}/2\leqq 1/3
,
it holds that

\displaystyle|\Delta(\phi^{ABC})-\Delta(\psi)^{ABC}|

(7)

\displaystyle\leqq 2(\|\phi^{ABC}-\psi^{ABC}\|_{1}\log_{2}D+h_{2}(\|\phi^{ABC}-\psi^{ABC}\|_{1})).

Then, Eq. (
7
) implies that the relative entropy of non-Markovianity is asymptotically continuous.
Our proof is based on a simple expression of the relative entropy of non-Markovianity, in a similar form to Eq. (
4
), as a gap of two von Neumann entropies
Ibinson et al. 2008
.
Then, we explicitly show asymptotic continuity of the relative entropy of non-Markovianity using this characterization.
In the proof,
we further decompose the characterization and repeatedly exploit Fannes-Audenaert inequality
Audenaert 2007
.
See the Supplemental Materials for more details of the proof.
Therefore, from Lemma
3
and Eq. (
7
), it follows that the regularized relative entropy of non-Markovianity is consistent.

III.4
Non-Gaussianity

In this section, we investigate the consistency of the relative entropic measure in the QRT of non-Gaussianity
Takagi and Zhuang 2018
;
Albarelli et al. 2018
;
Lami et al. 2018
;
Yamasaki et al. 2020a
.
Gaussian quantum information
Weedbrook et al. 2012
, based on Gaussian states and Gaussian operations, plays a central role in continuous-variable (CV) quantum information processing
since Gaussian states and operations are experimentally implementable with high-precision control in quantum-optical setups
Walls and Milburn 2007
.
In addition, despite the infinite-dimensionality, the theoretical analysis of Gaussian states is relatively tractable due to the fact that their characterization functions are in Gaussian forms.
Indeed, various quantum information processing protocols, such as quantum teleportation
Vaidman 1994
;
Braunstein and Kimble 1998
;
Ralph and Lam 1998
, noisy quantum cloning
Cerf and Iblisdir 2000
;
Lindblad 2000
, quantum illumination
Tan et al. 2008
, quantum reading
Pirandola 2011
, and quantum key distribution
Grosshans and Grangier 2002
;
Grosshans et al. 2003
;
Weedbrook et al. 2004
;
Weedbrook et al. 2006
;
Matsuura et al. 2021
, can be implemented by Gaussian states and operations.
However, it was shown that non-Gaussianity is an essential resource for several tasks including entanglement distillation
Eisert et al. 2002
;
Giedke and Ignacio Cirac 2002
;
Fiurášek 2002
;
Zhang and van
Loock 2010
, quantum error correction
Niset et al. 2009
, and universal quantum computation using CV systems
Lloyd and Braunstein 1999
;
Bartlett and Sanders 2002
;
Ohliger et al. 2010
;
Menicucci et al. 2006
;
Yamasaki et al. 2020b
.
Non-Gaussianity also reveals the implementation cost of CV fault-tolerant quantum computation
Yamasaki et al. 2020a
;
Yamasaki et al. 2020b
.

Here, we briefly review the basic concepts for the QRT of non-Gaussianity. For further details, see Refs.
Braunstein and van
Loock 2005
;
Weedbrook et al. 2012
.
Consider an
N
-mode bosonic system and define the real quadratic field operators
\hat{x}\coloneqq(\hat{q}_{1},\hat{p}_{1},\ldots,\hat{q}_{N},\hat{p}_{N})
.
A quantum state
\phi
is described by its Wigner characteristic function
\chi(\xi,\phi)=\tr[\phi\hat{D}(\xi)]
.
Here,
\xi=(\xi_{1},\ldots,\xi_{2N})\in\mathbb{R}^{2N}
is a real-valued vector, and
\hat{D}(\xi)\coloneqq\exp(i\hat{x}^{\mathrm{T}}\Omega\xi)
is the Weyl displacement operator with
\Omega\coloneqq i\bigoplus_{k=1}^{N}Y
, where
Y
is the Pauli-
Y
matrix.

A quantum state
\psi
is a Gaussian state if its characteristic function
\chi(\xi,\psi)
is expressed as a Gaussian function whose form is determined only by the mean and covariance matrix of
\hat{x}
with respect to
\psi

Braunstein and van
Loock 2005
;
Weedbrook et al. 2012
.
We define
\mathcal{G}
as the set of Gaussian states.
Since the sum of Gaussian functions is not necessarily Gaussian, the set
\mathcal{G}
is not convex.
The relative entropy of non-Gaussianity is defined as

\delta[\phi]\coloneqq\min_{\psi\in\mathcal{G}}\relent(\phi\|\psi).

(8)

It is known that the relative entropy of non-Gaussianity can be written as

\delta[\phi]=\entropy(\phi_{\textup{G}})-\entropy(\phi)
, where
\phi_{\textup{G}}
is the Gaussification of
\phi
, that is, the Gaussian state with the same mean and covariance matrix as
\phi

Genoni et al. 2008
.
Despite this simple characterization, we cannot directly apply the same strategy for asymptotic continuity as in the QRTs of discord and non-Markovianity because Gaussian states are defined on an infinite-dimensional state space.

While continuity of the relative entropy of non-Gaussianity is claimed in Ref.
Genoni et al. 2008
, we discover a counterexample showing that the relative entropy of non-Gaussianity is not continuous even under a reasonable energy constraint.
Note that this discovery may also affect some of the arguments in other existing literature citing Ref.
Genoni et al. 2008
.
Consider a single-mode system with Hamiltonian
H=\hbar\omega a^{\dagger}a
.
Take two Fock-diagonal states
\rho=\epsilon\ket{E/\epsilon}\bra{E/\epsilon}+(1-\epsilon)\ket{0}\bra{0}
and
\sigma=\ket{0}\bra{0}
with a small positive number
\epsilon
and fixed positive number
E
.
Then, these states satisfy the energy constraint
\tr(\rho H)\leqq E
and
\tr(\sigma H)\leqq E
.
However, while
\|\rho-\sigma\|_{1}\approx\epsilon
, we have
|\delta[\rho]-\delta[\sigma]|\approx(E+1)\log_{2}(E+1)-E\log_{2}E
even for infinitesimal
\epsilon
.
Therefore, the relative entropy of non-Gaussianity is not continuous.
A more detailed discussion and proof are shown in the Supplemental Materials.

We also show that we can avoid this discontinuity of the relative entropy of non-Gaussianity, by considering the convex QRT of non-Gaussianity
Takagi and Zhuang 2018
;
Albarelli et al. 2018
.
In the convex QRT of non-Gaussianity, we take, as free operations, Gaussian operations that may be conditioned on outcomes of homodyne detections.
That is, we use the convex hull of the set of Gaussian operations as free operations.
The set of free states is the convex hull of the set of Gaussian states
\mathrm{conv}(\mathcal{G})
.
Then, we consider the following modified version of relative entropy of non-Gaussianity, which we call the relative entropy of convex non-Gaussianity:

\delta_{\textup{conv}}[\phi]\coloneqq\min_{\psi\in\mathrm{conv}(\mathcal{G})}\relent(\phi\|\psi).

(9)

Here, to avoid discontinuity due to the infinite dimensionality of the state space, we prove asymptotic continuity under an energy constraint.
Hereafter, let
H
be the Hamiltonian of a given system.
We suppose that the smallest eigenvalue of
H
is fixed to zero.
Then, let
E\geqq 0
represent an upper bound of the energy of the system.
Let
\mathcal{D}_{H,E}(\mathcal{H})\coloneqq\{\rho\in\mathcal{D}(\mathcal{H}):\tr(H\rho)\leqq E\}
denote a subset of the set of density operators that contains all states satisfying the energy constraint, and we here take the set of states of interest as
\mathcal{S}(\mathcal{H})=\mathcal{D}_{H,E}(\mathcal{H})
.
The relative entropy of convex non-Gaussianity
\delta_{\mathrm{conv}}
is indeed asymptotically continuous in the following sense:
if the Hamiltonian
H
satisfies the condition from Ref.
Shirokov 2018

\lim_{\lambda\to\infty}[\tr(\mathrm{e}^{-\lambda H})]^{\lambda}=0,

(10)

for any sequences
\{\phi_{n}\in\mathcal{D}_{H_{n},nE}(\mathcal{H})\}_{n}
and
\{\psi_{n}\in\mathcal{D}_{H_{n},nE}(\mathcal{H})\}_{n}
such that
\lim_{n\to\infty}\|\phi_{n}-\psi_{n}\|_{1}\to 0

with the Hamiltonian
H_{n}=H\otimes\mathbbm{1}\otimes\cdots\otimes\mathbbm{1}+\mathbbm{1}\otimes H\otimes\mathbbm{1}\otimes\cdots\otimes\mathbbm{1}+\cdots+\mathbbm{1}\otimes\cdots\otimes\mathbbm{1}\otimes H
,
then it holds that

\lim_{n\to\infty}|\delta_{\textup{conv}}[\phi_{n}]-\delta_{\textup{conv}}[\psi_{n}]|/n=0
.

Here, we note that the assumption (
10
), on which asymptotic continuity is based, is satisfied by representative choices of Hamiltonians such as those of harmonic oscillators representing light.
Our proof of the asymptotic continuity is based on a modified version of Proposition 3 in Ref.
Shirokov 2018
showing that
for the Hamiltonian
H
satisfying Eq. (
10
)
and for all states
\phi,\psi\in\mathcal{D}_{H,E}(\mathcal{H})
such that
\|\phi-\psi\|\leqq\epsilon\leqq 1/2
,
it holds that
|\delta_{\textup{conv}}[\phi]-\delta_{\textup{conv}}[\psi]|\to 0
as
\epsilon\to 0
.
Then, letting
r^{(H,E)}\left(\phi\to\psi\right)
denote the asymptotic conversion rate under the energy constraint,
we have the following inequality showing the consistency of
\delta^{\infty}_{\mathrm{conv}}

~\delta_{\textup{conv}}^{\infty}[\psi]r^{(H,E)}\left(\phi\to\psi\right)\leqq\delta_{\textup{conv}}^{\infty}[\phi].

(11)

See the Supplemental Materials for the rigorous statement and proof.
Therefore, from Eq. (
11
), it follows that the regularized relative entropy of convex non-Gaussianity is consistent in terms of the asymptotic state conversion under the energy constraint.

IV
Conclusion

We investigated the relative entropy of resource for general resources, including discord, non-Markovianity, and non-Gaussianity
to show the existence of asymptotically consistent resource measures in QRTs of these resources.
In contrast with the analysis in our initial paper
Kuroiwa and Yamasaki 2020
, which showed the consistency of the regularized relative entropy of resource
by assuming the finite dimensionality, convexity, and existence of full-rank free states,
we here showed that the regularized relative entropy of resource is consistent in all finite-dimensional convex QRTs even
without assuring the existence of full-rank free states
.
Furthermore, we proved that the regularized relative entropy of resource serves as a consistent resource measure for some physically well-motivated
nonconvex
quantum resources, namely, discord and non-Markovianity.
For an
infinite-dimensional
resource, non-Gaussianity, we showed that the relative entropy of non-Gaussianity is indeed discontinuous even though the continuity is argued in the previous research
Genoni et al. 2008
.
At the same time, by considering the convex hull of Gaussian states, we proved that the convex relative entropy of non-Gaussianity is asymptotically continuous under an energy constraint and that its regularization is consistent.
Thus, we disclosed the consistency of the regularized relative entropy of resource for general resources beyond those covered in the previous work.
These results pave the way for the quantification of resources consistent with the asymptotic state conversion, which fuels further investigation of resources not exactly comparable with each other.
The results in this paper establish a theoretical foundation for quantitative studies of a wide variety of quantum resources that may not satisfy restrictive mathematical assumptions such as convexity and finite-dimensionality.

SII
Finite dimensional convex QRTs

In this section, we argue that the regularized relative entropy of resource
R_{\textup{R}}
is consistent in all finite-dimensional convex QRTs even without full-rank free states.

Before going through the detailed analysis, we here summarize our proof.
From the previous work
Kuroiwa and Yamasaki 2020
, the regularized relative entropy of resource is consistent in finite-dimensional convex QRTs with full-rank free states.
Hereafter, suppose that the set of free states does not contain full-rank states; that is,
the support of free states is strictly smaller than the whole system.
Applying the same discussion as that with a full-rank free state, we show that the relative entropy of resource is asymptotically continuous on the support of free states. (See Theorem
S3
.)
Then, by Sufficient Condition for Consistency (Lemma 3) in the main article, the regularized relative entropy of resource is consistent on the support of free states.
Next, consider the case where
\phi
is not in the support of free states.
Proving that
\phi^{\otimes n}
is also not in the support of free states (Lemma
S2
), we observe that
R^{\infty}_{R}(\phi)=\infty
, and
R^{\infty}_{R}
is consistent in terms of the refined definition of the consistency shown in the main text.
Finally, suppose that
\phi
is in the support and
\psi
is not; that is,
R^{\infty}_{R}(\phi)<\infty
and
R^{\infty}_{R}(\psi)=\infty
.
By using the same reasoning, we have that
\psi^{\otimes n}
is not in the support of free states.
Then, using the fact that free operations do not transfer a state from inside to outside of the support of free states (Lemma
S4
), we show that we have
r(\phi\to\psi)=0
(Lemma
S5
and Theorem
S6
) in this case.
Therefore, even without full-rank free states, the regularization of the relative entropy of resource is consistent in finite-dimensional convex QRTs under the refined definition of the consistent resource measures given in the main text.

Now, we provide detailed proof.
We first show that the regularized relative entropy of resource is consistent on the support of free states.
Here, we let
\mathcal{F}(\mathcal{H})\subseteq\mathcal{S}(\mathcal{H})
denote the set of free states on the system
\mathcal{H}
.
We define the support of
\mathcal{F}(\mathcal{H})
, denoted by
\mathrm{supp}(\mathcal{F}(\mathcal{H}))
, as the smallest Hilbert space containing the supports of all free states in
\mathcal{F}(\mathcal{H})
;
that is,

\mathrm{supp}(\mathcal{F}(\mathcal{H}))\coloneqq\mathrm{span}\left\{\ket{\psi}\in\mathcal{H}:\exists\phi\in\mathcal{F}(\mathcal{H})\,\,\mathrm{s.t.}\,\,\ket{\psi}\in\mathrm{supp}(\phi)\right\}.

(S5)

Lemma S2
.

Let
\mathcal{H}
be a finite-dimensional quantum system.
Then, for any positive integer
n>0
,
\mathrm{supp}(\mathcal{F}(\mathcal{H}^{\otimes n}))=\mathrm{supp}(\mathcal{F}(\mathcal{H}))^{\otimes n}
.

Proof.

The proof is by induction.
Let
D>0
be the dimension of the system
\mathcal{H}
and let
0<L\leqq D
be the dimension of
\mathrm{supp}(\mathcal{F}(\mathcal{H}))
.
Let
\{\ket{1},\ldots,\ket{L},\ket{L+1},\ldots,\ket{D}\}
be an orthonormal basis of
\mathcal{H}
so that
\{\ket{1},\ldots,\ket{L}\}
spans
\mathrm{supp}(\mathcal{F}(\mathcal{H}))
.
For the proof by induction, suppose that
\mathrm{supp}(\mathcal{F}(\mathcal{H}^{\otimes n-1}))=\mathrm{supp}(\mathcal{F}(\mathcal{H}))^{\otimes n-1}
.
Here, take any state
\phi_{n}
in
\mathcal{F}(\mathcal{H}^{\otimes n})
.
This state
\phi_{n}
can be expressed as

\phi_{n}\coloneqq\sum_{i_{1},j_{1},\ldots,i_{n},j_{n}=1,\ldots,D}\lambda_{i_{1}j_{1},\cdots,i_{n}j_{n}}\ket{i_{1}\cdots i_{n}}\bra{j_{1}\cdots j_{n}}.

(S6)

Let
\mathcal{T}_{m}
be the partial trace over
m
th system.
Since
\mathcal{T}_{m}
is a free operation,
\mathcal{T}_{m}(\phi_{n})\in\mathcal{F}(\mathcal{H}^{\otimes n-1})
for all
1\leqq m\leqq n
.
Since
\mathrm{supp}(\mathcal{F}(\mathcal{H}^{\otimes n-1}))=\mathrm{supp}(\mathcal{F}(\mathcal{H}))^{\otimes n-1}
,

\mathcal{T}_{m}(\phi_{n})
can be expressed only by elements from
\{\ket{s_{1},s_{2},\ldots,s_{n-1}}:s_{1},\ldots,s_{n-1}=1,\ldots,L\}
.
Now, take
m=1
.
Then,

\mathcal{T}_{1}(\phi_{n})\coloneqq\sum_{i_{2},j_{2},\ldots,i_{n},j_{n}=1,\ldots,D}\left(\sum_{i_{1}}\lambda_{i_{1}i_{1}i_{2}j_{2}\cdots i_{n}j_{n}}\right)\ket{i_{2}\cdots i_{n}}\bra{j_{2}\cdots j_{n}}.

(S7)

Since
\mathcal{T}_{1}(\phi_{n})
must not consist of any state in
\{\ket{L+1},\ldots,\ket{D}\}
,
if either one of
i_{2},\ldots,i_{n}
is in
\{L+1,\ldots,D\}
,
it holds that

\sum_{i_{1}}\lambda_{i_{1}i_{1}i_{2}i_{2}\cdots i_{n}i_{n}}=0.

(S8)

Since
\lambda_{i_{1}i_{1}i_{2}i_{2}\cdots i_{n}i_{n}}\geqq 0
for all
i_{1}
, in this case,
\lambda_{i_{1}i_{1}i_{2}i_{2}\cdots i_{n}i_{n}}=0
for all
i_{1}
.
Therefore,
\phi_{n}
does not contain a state from
\{L+1,\ldots,D\}
in all systems except the first one.
The same argument also applies to arbitrary choice of
m\in\{1,2,\ldots,n\}
.
Therefore, we have that
\phi_{n}
does not contain a state from
\{L+1,\ldots,D\}
in all systems.
Hence, it follows that
\phi_{n}
does not contain a state from
\{L+1,\ldots,D\}
, which implies that
\{\ket{s_{1},s_{2},\ldots,s_{n}}:s_{1},\ldots,s_{n}=1,\ldots,L\}
spans
\mathcal{F}(\mathcal{H}^{\otimes n})
;
that is,
\mathrm{supp}(\mathcal{F}(\mathcal{H}^{\otimes n}))=\mathrm{supp}(\mathcal{F}(\mathcal{H}))^{\otimes n}
.
∎

Theorem S3
.

Let
\phi
and
\psi
be states such that
R_{\textup{R}}^{\infty}(\phi)<\infty
and
0<R_{\textup{R}}^{\infty}(\psi)<\infty
.
Then, it holds that
r(\phi\to\psi)<\infty
.
Moreover,
R_{\textup{R}}^{\infty}
is consistent for these states; that is,
R_{\textup{R}}^{\infty}(\psi)r(\phi\to\psi)\leqq R_{\textup{R}}^{\infty}(\phi)
.

Proof.

By Lemma
S2
, we have
R_{\textup{R}}(\phi),R_{\textup{R}}(\psi)<\infty
, and thus
\phi
and
\psi
are in the support of free states.
Here, we note that the relative entropy of resource is asymptotically continuous on the support of free states using the same argument of the proof in Refs.
Synak-Radtke and Horodecki 2006
;
Winter 2016
.

We first prove that
r(\phi\to\psi)<\infty
. The proof is by contradiction.
Assume that
r(\phi\to\psi)=\infty
.
Then, we can take a positive number

~C>\frac{R_{\textup{R}}^{\infty}(\phi)}{R_{\textup{R}}^{\infty}(\psi)},

(S9)

and for an arbitrarily small
\epsilon>0
, there exists sufficiently large
n
and a free operation
\mathcal{N}_{n}
such that

\left\|\mathcal{N}_{n}(\phi^{\otimes n})-\psi^{\otimes\lceil Cn\rceil}\right\|_{1}\leqq\epsilon.

(S10)

Using asymptotic continuity of
R_{\textup{R}}
on the support of free states, we have

R_{\textup{R}}(\phi^{\otimes n})\geqq R_{\textup{R}}(\psi^{\otimes\lceil Cn\rceil})-\lceil Cn\rceil\epsilon.

(S11)

Then, dividing this inequality by
\lceil Cn\rceil
, we have

\frac{R_{\textup{R}}(\phi^{\otimes n})}{Cn}\geqq\frac{R_{\textup{R}}(\phi^{\otimes n})}{\lceil Cn\rceil}\geqq\frac{R_{\textup{R}}(\psi^{\otimes\lceil Cn\rceil})}{\lceil Cn\rceil}-\epsilon.

(S12)

Taking the limit
n\to\infty
and
\epsilon\to 0
, we have

R_{\textup{R}}^{\infty}(\phi)\geqq CR_{\textup{R}}^{\infty}(\psi),

(S13)

which contradicts the assumption (
S9
).

Next, we prove the consistency.
Since
r(\phi\to\psi)<\infty
, for an arbitrarily small
\delta>0
,
r\coloneqq r(\phi\to\psi)-\delta
is an achievable rate.
Then, for an arbitrarily small
\epsilon>0
, there exists sufficiently large
n
and a free operation
\mathcal{N}_{n}
such that

\left\|\mathcal{N}_{n}(\phi^{\otimes n})-\psi^{\otimes\lceil rn\rceil}\right\|_{1}\leqq\epsilon.

(S14)

Using asymptotic continuity of
R_{\textup{R}}
on the support of free states, we have

R_{\textup{R}}(\phi^{\otimes n})\geqq R_{\textup{R}}(\psi^{\otimes\lceil rn\rceil})-\lceil Cn\rceil\epsilon.

(S15)

Then, dividing this inequality by
\lceil rn\rceil
, we have

\frac{R_{\textup{R}}(\phi^{\otimes n})}{rn}\geqq\frac{R_{\textup{R}}(\phi^{\otimes n})}{\lceil rn\rceil}\geqq\frac{R_{\textup{R}}(\psi^{\otimes\lceil rn\rceil})}{\lceil rn\rceil}-\epsilon.

(S16)

Taking the limit
n\to\infty
and
\epsilon\to 0
, we have

R_{\textup{R}}^{\infty}(\phi)\geqq rR_{\textup{R}}^{\infty}(\psi).

(S17)

Since we can take
\delta
arbitrarily small,
R_{\textup{R}}
is consistent.
∎

Then, we show that the regularized relative entropy of resource quantifies resources consistently with the asymptotic conversion rate even for resources out of the support of free states.

Lemma S4
.

Let
\mathcal{H}
and
\mathcal{H}^{\prime}
be quantum systems.
Suppose
\phi\in\mathcal{D}(\mathcal{H})
is a state such that
\mathrm{supp}(\phi)\subseteq\mathrm{supp}(\mathcal{F}(\mathcal{H}))
.
Then, for an arbitrary free operation
\mathcal{N}
from a system
\mathcal{H}
to a system
\mathcal{H}^{\prime}
, it holds that
\mathrm{supp}(\mathcal{N}(\phi))\subseteq\mathrm{supp}(\mathcal{F}(\mathcal{H}^{\prime}))
.

Proof.

Since
\mathrm{supp}(\phi)\subseteq\mathrm{supp}(\mathcal{F}(\mathcal{H}))
,
there exists a free state
\psi
such that

\relent(\phi\|\psi)<\infty.

(S18)

Using the monotonicity of the relative entropy, it holds that

\relent(\mathcal{N}(\phi)\|\mathcal{N}(\psi))\leqq\relent(\phi\|\psi)<\infty.

(S19)

Since
\mathcal{N}(\psi)
is also a free state, we have
\mathrm{supp}(\mathcal{N}(\phi))\subseteq\mathrm{supp}(\mathcal{N}(\psi))\subseteq\mathrm{supp}(\mathcal{F}(\mathcal{H}^{\prime}))
.
∎

Lemma S5
.

Let
\mathcal{H}
and
\mathcal{H}^{\prime}
be quantum systems.
Let
\phi\in\mathcal{D}(\mathcal{H})
such that
\mathrm{supp}(\phi)\subseteq\mathrm{supp}(\mathcal{F}(\mathcal{H}))
,
and let
\psi\in\mathcal{D}(\mathcal{H}^{\prime})
such that
\mathrm{supp}(\psi)\not\subseteq\mathrm{supp}(\mathcal{F}(\mathcal{H}^{\prime}))
.
Then, the asymptotic conversion rate
r(\phi\to\psi)
is zero.

Proof.

The proof is by contradiction.
Suppose that
r(\phi\to\psi)>0
.
Then, for some
r>0
, it holds that for an arbitrarily small
\epsilon
, there exists sufficiently large
n
and a free operation
\mathcal{N}_{n}
such that

\left\|\mathcal{N}_{n}(\phi^{\otimes n})-\psi^{\otimes\lceil rn\rceil}\right\|_{1}<\epsilon.

(S20)

Let
\mathcal{T}
denote the partial trace over all systems other than the first one.
Then, using the monotonicity of the trace norm, we have

\left\|\mathcal{T}\circ\mathcal{N}_{n}(\phi^{\otimes n})-\psi\right\|_{1}\leqq\left\|\mathcal{N}_{n}(\phi^{\otimes n})-\psi^{\otimes\lceil rn\rceil}\right\|_{1}

(S21)

for any
n>0
and any free operation
\mathcal{N}_{n}
.
From Lemma
S4
,
\mathcal{T}\circ\mathcal{N}_{n}(\phi^{\otimes n})
is also in the support of
\mathcal{F}(\mathcal{H}^{\prime})
.
On the other hand,
\mathrm{supp}(\psi)\not\subseteq\mathrm{supp}(\mathcal{F}(\mathcal{H}^{\prime}))
.
Therefore,
\left\|\mathcal{T}\circ\mathcal{N}_{n}(\phi^{\otimes n})-\psi\right\|_{1}
can be lower-bounded by

D_{\psi}\coloneqq\min_{\sigma}\|\sigma-\psi\|_{1}>0,

(S22)

where the minimization is taken over all states
\sigma
in the support of
\mathcal{F}(\mathcal{H}^{\prime})
.
If we take
\epsilon
smaller than
D_{\psi}
, it always holds that

\epsilon\leqq\left\|\mathcal{N}_{n}(\phi^{\otimes n})-\psi^{\otimes\lceil rn\rceil}\right\|_{1},

(S23)

which contradicts to the assumption of
r(\phi\to\psi)>0
.
∎

Theorem S6
.

Let
\mathcal{H}
and
\mathcal{H}^{\prime}
be quantum systems.
Let
\phi\in\mathcal{D}(\mathcal{H})
such that
R_{\textup{R}}^{\infty}(\phi)<\infty
,
and let
\psi\in\mathcal{D}(\mathcal{H}^{\prime})
such that
R_{\textup{R}}^{\infty}(\psi)=\infty
.
Then, it holds that
r(\phi\to\psi)=0
.

Proof.

From Lemma
S2
, since
R_{\textup{R}}^{\infty}(\phi)<\infty
, we have
\mathrm{supp}(\phi)\subseteq\mathrm{supp}(\mathcal{F}(\mathcal{H}))
.
Since
R_{\textup{R}}^{\infty}(\psi)=\infty
, it holds that
\mathrm{supp}(\psi)\not\subseteq\mathrm{supp}(\mathcal{F}(\mathcal{H}^{\prime}))
.
Then, by Lemma
S5
, we have
r(\phi\to\psi)=0
.

SIII
Discord

In this section, we investigate consistent resource measures in the resource theory of discord.
We basically adopt the notation in Ref.
Bera et al. 2017
.
In Sec
SIII.1
, we study consistency of the regularized relative entropy of discord.
We first consider classical-classical states as free states in Sec.
SIII.1.1
, and we show that the regularized relative entropy of discord is consistent for this choice of free states.
Then, in Sec.
SIII.1.2
, we consider quantum-classical states as free states, and we also show that the regularized relative entropy of discord is consistent in this case.

SIII.1
Relative Entropy of Discord

In this section, we investigate the relative entropy of discord for two choices of free states, namely, classical-classical states and quantum-classical states.
We prove that the regularized relative entropy of discord is consistent for both of these two cases.

SIII.1.1
Classical-Classical States

Here, we formally define the set of classical-classical states.

Definition S7
.

Let
\mathcal{H}^{A}
and
\mathcal{H}^{B}
be finite-dimensional quantum systems.
Then, we define a set
\mathrm{c}\mhyph\mathrm{c}
of classical states on
\mathcal{H}^{A}\otimes\mathcal{H}^{B}
as

\mathrm{c}\mhyph\mathrm{c}\coloneqq\left\{\sum_{k}p_{k}\ket{a_{k}}\bra{a_{k}}^{A}\otimes\ket{b_{k}}\bra{b_{k}}^{B}\right\},

(S24)

where
\ket{a_{k}}^{A}
and
\ket{b_{k}}^{B}
are orthonormal bases of systems
\mathcal{H}^{A}
and
\mathcal{H}^{B}
respectively,
and
p_{k}
is a probability distribution; that is,
\mathrm{c}\mhyph\mathrm{c}
is the set of the mixture of local classical states.

Remark 1
.

\mathrm{c}\mhyph\mathrm{c}
is not convex.

The relative entropy of discord for
\mathrm{c}\mhyph\mathrm{c}
is defined as follows.

Definition S8
.

Let
\mathcal{H}^{A}
and
\mathcal{H}^{B}
be finite-dimensional quantum systems.
For a quantum state
\phi^{AB}\in\mathcal{D}(\mathcal{H}^{A}\otimes\mathcal{H}^{B})
,
define the relative entropy of discord
D^{\mathrm{c}\mhyph\mathrm{c}}_{\textup{rel}}
as

D^{\mathrm{c}\mhyph\mathrm{c}}_{\textup{rel}}(\phi^{AB})\coloneqq\min_{\psi^{AB}\in\mathrm{c}\mhyph\mathrm{c}}\relent(\phi^{AB}\|\psi^{AB}).

(S25)

Then, we derive a simple characterization of the relative entropy of discord for
\mathrm{c}\mhyph\mathrm{c}
.

Proposition S9
.

Let
\mathcal{H}^{A}
and
\mathcal{H}^{B}
be finite-dimensional quantum systems.
For a quantum state
\phi^{AB}\in\mathcal{D}(\mathcal{H}^{A}\otimes\mathcal{H}^{B})
,
it holds that

D^{\mathrm{c}\mhyph\mathrm{c}}_{\textup{rel}}(\phi^{AB})=\min_{\ket{a_{k}}^{A},\ket{b_{k}}^{B}}\entropy\left(\sum_{k}(\bra{a_{k}}\otimes\bra{b_{k}})\phi^{AB}(\ket{a_{k}}\otimes\ket{b_{k}})\ket{a_{k}}\bra{a_{k}}^{A}\otimes\ket{b_{k}}\bra{b_{k}}^{B}\right)-\entropy(\phi^{AB}),

(S26)

where the minimization is taken over all orthonormal bases of
\mathcal{H}^{A}
and
\mathcal{H}^{B}
.

Proof.

Take a classical-classical state

\psi^{AB}\coloneqq\sum_{k}p_{k}\ket{a_{k}}\bra{a_{k}}^{A}\otimes\ket{b_{k}}\bra{b_{k}}^{B}.

(S27)

such that
\mathrm{supp}(\phi^{AB})\subseteq\mathrm{supp}(\sigma^{AB})
.
Then, it holds that

\displaystyle\relent(\phi^{AB}\|\psi^{AB})

\displaystyle=-\entropy(\phi^{AB})-\tr[\phi^{AB}\log_{2}\psi^{AB}]

(S28)

\displaystyle=-\entropy(\phi^{AB})-\tr\left[\phi^{AB}\log_{2}\left(\sum_{k}p_{k}\ket{a_{k}}\bra{a_{k}}^{A}\otimes\ket{b_{k}}\bra{b_{k}}^{B}\right)\right]

(S29)

\displaystyle=-\entropy(\phi^{AB})-\sum_{k}\tr\left[\phi^{AB}\left(\log_{2}p_{k}\ket{a_{k}}\bra{a_{k}}^{A}\otimes\ket{b_{k}}\bra{b_{k}}^{B}\right)\right]

(S30)

\displaystyle=-\entropy(\phi^{AB})-\sum_{k}\log_{2}p_{k}(\bra{a_{k}}\otimes\bra{b_{k}})\phi^{AB}(\ket{a_{k}}\otimes\ket{b_{k}}).

(S31)

Now, define
q_{k}\coloneqq(\bra{a_{k}}\otimes\bra{b_{k}})\phi^{AB}(\ket{a_{k}}\otimes\ket{b_{k}})
.
Then, we have

\displaystyle\relent(\phi^{AB}\|\psi^{AB})

\displaystyle=-\entropy(\phi^{AB})-\sum_{k}q_{k}\log_{2}p_{k}

(S32)

\displaystyle=-\entropy(\phi^{AB})-\sum_{k}q_{k}\log_{2}q_{k}+\left(\sum_{k}q_{k}\log_{2}q_{k}-\sum_{k}q_{k}\log_{2}p_{k}\right)

(S33)

\displaystyle\geqq-\entropy(\phi^{AB})-\sum_{k}q_{k}\log_{2}q_{k}

(S34)

\displaystyle=-\entropy(\phi^{AB})+\entropy\left(\sum_{k}(\bra{a_{k}}\otimes\bra{b_{k}})\phi^{AB}(\ket{a_{k}}\otimes\ket{b_{k}})\ket{a_{k}}\bra{a_{k}}^{A}\otimes\ket{b_{k}}\bra{b_{k}}^{B}\right).

(S35)

The inequality (
S34
) follows from the positivity of the relative entropy, and it is saturated by taking
p_{k}=q_{k}
for all
k
.
Therefore, the minimum of
\mathrm{D}(\phi^{AB}\|\psi^{AB})
can be achieved by choosing optimal
\ket{a_{k}}^{A}
and
\ket{b_{k}}^{B}
,
which implies (
S26
).
∎

Then, applying Proposition
S9
, we prove the asymptotic continuity of
D^{\mathrm{c}\mhyph\mathrm{c}}_{\textup{rel}}
.

Theorem S10
.

Let
\mathcal{H}^{A}
and
\mathcal{H}^{B}
be finite-dimensional quantum systems with dimension
D=\mathrm{dim}(\mathcal{H}^{A}\otimes\mathcal{H}^{B})
.
The relative entropy of discord
D^{\mathrm{c}\mhyph\mathrm{c}}_{\textup{rel}}
is asymptotically continuous; i.e.,
it holds that

|D^{\mathrm{c}\mhyph\mathrm{c}}_{\textup{rel}}(\phi^{AB})-D^{\mathrm{c}\mhyph\mathrm{c}}_{\textup{rel}}(\psi^{AB})|\leqq\|\phi^{AB}-\psi^{AB}\|_{1}\log_{2}D+2h_{2}\left(\frac{\|\phi^{AB}-\psi^{AB}\|_{1}}{2}\right),

(S36)

where
h_{2}
is the binary entropy function.

Proof.

By Proposition
S9
, it holds that

\displaystyle|D^{\mathrm{c}\mhyph\mathrm{c}}_{\textup{rel}}(\phi^{AB})-D^{\mathrm{c}\mhyph\mathrm{c}}_{\textup{rel}}(\psi^{AB})|

\displaystyle\leqq|\entropy(\phi^{AB})-\entropy(\psi^{AB})|

\displaystyle+\Bigg|\entropy\left(\sum_{k}(\bra{a_{k}}\otimes\bra{b_{k}})\phi^{AB}(\ket{a_{k}}\otimes\ket{b_{k}})\ket{a_{k}}\bra{a_{k}}^{A}\otimes\ket{b_{k}}\bra{b_{k}}^{B}\right)

\displaystyle\quad-\entropy\left(\sum_{k}(\bra{a_{k}}\otimes\bra{b_{k}})\psi^{AB}(\ket{a_{k}}\otimes\ket{b_{k}})\ket{a_{k}}\bra{a_{k}}^{A}\otimes\ket{b_{k}}\bra{b_{k}}^{B}\right)\Bigg|,

where
\ket{a_{k}}^{A}
and
\ket{b_{k}}^{B}
is optimal orthonormal bases for
\psi
.
Here, we assume that

\displaystyle\min_{\ket{a_{k}},\ket{b_{k}}}

\displaystyle\entropy\left(\sum_{k}(\bra{a_{k}}\otimes\bra{b_{k}})\phi^{AB}(\ket{a_{k}}\otimes\ket{b_{k}})\ket{a_{k}}\bra{a_{k}}^{A}\otimes\ket{b_{k}}\bra{b_{k}}^{B}\right)

\displaystyle\geqq\min_{\ket{a_{k}},\ket{b_{k}}}\entropy\left(\sum_{k}(\bra{a_{k}}\otimes\bra{b_{k}})\psi^{AB}(\ket{a_{k}}\otimes\ket{b_{k}})\ket{a_{k}}\bra{a_{k}}^{A}\otimes\ket{b_{k}}\bra{b_{k}}^{B}\right).

Otherwise, we can switch the roles of
\phi^{AB}
and
\psi^{AB}
to apply the same argument.

Now, it holds that

\displaystyle\left\|\sum_{k}(\bra{a_{k}}\otimes\bra{b_{k}})\phi^{AB}(\ket{a_{k}}\otimes\ket{b_{k}})\ket{a_{k}}\bra{a_{k}}^{A}\otimes\ket{b_{k}}\bra{b_{k}}^{B}-\sum_{k}(\bra{a_{k}}\otimes\bra{b_{k}})\psi^{AB}(\ket{a_{k}}\otimes\ket{b_{k}})\ket{a_{k}}\bra{a_{k}}^{A}\otimes\ket{b_{k}}\bra{b_{k}}^{B}\right\|_{1}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k}\left\|\left((\bra{a_{k}}\otimes\bra{b_{k}})\left(\phi^{AB}-\psi^{AB}\right)(\ket{a_{k}}^{A}\otimes\ket{b_{k}}^{B})\right)\ket{a_{k}}\bra{a_{k}}^{A}\otimes\ket{b_{k}}\bra{b_{k}}^{B}\right\|_{1}

\displaystyle=\left\|\sum_{k}\left((\bra{a_{k}}\otimes\bra{b_{k}})\left(\phi^{AB}-\psi^{AB}\right)(\ket{a_{k}}^{A}\otimes\ket{b_{k}}^{B})\right)\ket{a_{k}}\bra{a_{k}}^{A}\otimes\ket{b_{k}}\bra{b_{k}}^{B}\right\|_{1}

\displaystyle\leqq\|\phi^{AB}-\psi^{AB}\|_{1}.

Therefore, using Lemma
S1
(Fannes-Audenaert inequality), we have

|D^{\mathrm{c}\mhyph\mathrm{c}}_{\textup{rel}}(\phi^{AB})-D^{\mathrm{c}\mhyph\mathrm{c}}_{\textup{rel}}(\psi^{AB})|\leqq\|\phi^{AB}-\psi^{AB}\|_{1}\log_{2}D+2h_{2}\left(\frac{\|\phi^{AB}-\psi^{AB}\|_{1}}{2}\right).

(S37)

∎

SIII.1.2
Quantum-Classical States

First, we formally define the set of classical-classical states.

Definition S11
.

Let
\mathcal{H}^{A}
and
\mathcal{H}^{B}
be finite-dimensional quantum systems.
Then, we define a set
\mathrm{q}\mhyph\mathrm{c}
of quantum-classical states on
\mathcal{H}^{A}\otimes\mathcal{H}^{B}
as

\mathrm{q}\mhyph\mathrm{c}\coloneqq\left\{\sum_{l}p_{l}\rho_{l}^{A}\otimes\ket{l}\bra{l}^{B}\right\},

(S38)

where and
\ket{l}^{B}
are orthonormal bases of systems
\mathcal{H}^{A}
and
\mathcal{H}^{B}
respectively,
and
p_{l}
is a probability distribution.

Remark 2
.

\mathrm{q}\mhyph\mathrm{c}
is not convex.

Then, we define the relative entropy of discord for
\mathrm{q}\mhyph\mathrm{c}
.

Definition S12
.

Let
\mathcal{H}^{A}
and
\mathcal{H}^{B}
be finite-dimensional quantum systems.
For a quantum state
\phi^{AB}\in\mathcal{D}(\mathcal{H}^{A}\otimes\mathcal{H}^{B})
,
define the relative entropy of discord
D^{\mathrm{q}\mhyph\mathrm{c}}_{\textup{rel}}
as

D^{\mathrm{q}\mhyph\mathrm{c}}_{\textup{rel}}(\phi^{AB})\coloneqq\min_{\psi^{AB}\in\mathrm{q}\mhyph\mathrm{c}}\relent(\phi^{AB}\|\psi^{AB}).

(S39)

Here, we give a simple characterization of the relative entropy of discord for
\mathrm{q}\mhyph\mathrm{c}
.

Proposition S13
.

Let
\mathcal{H}^{A}
and
\mathcal{H}^{B}
be finite-dimensional quantum systems.
For a quantum state
\phi^{AB}\in\mathcal{D}(\mathcal{H}^{A}\otimes\mathcal{H}^{B})
,
it holds that

D^{\mathrm{q}\mhyph\mathrm{c}}_{\textup{rel}}(\phi^{AB})=\min_{\ket{b_{k}}^{B}}\entropy\left(\sum_{k}(\mathbbm{1}^{A}\otimes\bra{b_{k}}^{B})\phi^{AB}(\mathbbm{1}^{A}\otimes\ket{b_{k}}^{B})\otimes\ket{b_{k}}\bra{b_{k}}^{B}\right)-\entropy(\phi^{AB}),

(S40)

where the minimization is taken over all orthonormal bases of
\mathcal{H}^{B}
.

Proof.

Take a quantum-classical state

\psi^{AB}\coloneqq\sum_{k}p_{k}\rho^{A}_{k}\otimes\ket{b_{k}}\bra{b_{k}}^{B}.

(S41)

such that
\mathrm{supp}(\phi^{AB})\subseteq\mathrm{supp}(\psi^{AB})
.
Then, it holds that

\displaystyle\relent(\phi^{AB}\|\psi^{AB})

(S42)

\displaystyle=-\entropy(\phi^{AB})-\tr[\phi^{AB}\log_{2}\psi^{AB}]

(S43)

\displaystyle=-\entropy(\phi^{AB})-\tr\left[\phi^{AB}\log_{2}\left(\sum_{k}p_{k}\rho^{A}_{k}\otimes\ket{b_{k}}\bra{b_{k}}^{B}\right)\right]

(S44)

\displaystyle=-\entropy(\phi^{AB})-\sum_{k}\tr\left[\phi^{AB}\left(\log_{2}\rho^{A}_{k}\otimes\ket{b_{k}}\bra{b_{k}}^{B}\right)\right]-\sum_{k}\tr\left[\phi^{AB}\left(\mathbbm{1}_{\mathrm{supp}(\rho^{A}_{k})}\otimes\log_{2}p_{k}\ket{b_{k}}\bra{b_{k}}^{B}\right)\right]

(S45)

\displaystyle=-\entropy(\phi^{AB})-\sum_{k}\tr\left[(\mathbbm{1}^{A}\otimes\bra{b_{k}}^{B})\phi^{AB}(\mathbbm{1}^{A}\otimes\ket{b_{k}}^{B})\log_{2}\rho^{A}_{k}\right]-\sum_{k}\tr[(\mathbbm{1}^{A}\otimes\bra{b_{k}}^{B})\phi^{AB}(\mathbbm{1}^{A}\otimes\ket{b_{k}}^{B})]\log_{2}p_{k}.

(S46)

Now, write

\displaystyle q_{k}\coloneqq\tr\left[(\mathbbm{1}^{A}\otimes\bra{b_{k}}^{B})\phi^{AB}(\mathbbm{1}^{A}\otimes\ket{b_{k}}^{B})\right],

(S47)

\displaystyle\phi^{A}_{k}\coloneqq\frac{(\mathbbm{1}^{A}\otimes\bra{b_{k}}^{B})\phi^{AB}(\mathbbm{1}^{A}\otimes\ket{b_{k}}^{B})}{q_{k}}.

(S48)

Then, we have

\displaystyle\relent(\phi^{AB}\|\psi^{AB})

\displaystyle=-\entropy(\phi^{AB})-\sum_{k}q_{k}\tr\left[\phi^{A}_{k}\log_{2}\rho^{A}_{k}\right]-\sum_{k}q_{k}\log_{2}p_{k}

(S49)

\displaystyle=-\entropy(\phi^{AB})-\sum_{k}q_{k}\log_{2}q_{k}+\left(\sum_{k}q_{k}\log_{2}q_{k}-\sum_{k}q_{k}\log_{2}p_{k}\right)+\sum_{k}q_{k}\entropy(\phi^{A}_{k})+\sum_{k}q_{k}\relent(\phi^{A}_{k}\|\rho^{A}_{k})

(S50)

\displaystyle\geqq-\entropy(\phi^{AB})-\sum_{k}q_{k}\log_{2}q_{k}+\sum_{k}q_{k}\entropy(\phi^{A}_{k})

(S51)

\displaystyle=-\entropy(\phi^{AB})+\entropy\left(\sum_{k}q_{k}\phi^{A}_{k}\otimes\ket{b_{k}}\bra{b_{k}}^{B}\right)

(S52)

\displaystyle=\entropy\left(\sum_{k}(\mathbbm{1}^{A}\otimes\bra{b_{k}}^{B})\rho(\mathbbm{1}^{A}\otimes\ket{b_{k}}^{B})\otimes\ket{b_{k}}\bra{b_{k}}^{B}\right)-\entropy(\phi^{AB}).

(S53)

The inequality (
S51
) follows from the positivity of the relative entropy, and it is saturated by taking
p_{k}=q_{k}
and
\rho^{A}_{k}=\phi^{A}_{k}
for all
k
.
Therefore, the minimum of
\relent(\phi^{AB}\|\psi^{AB})
can be achieved by choosing optimal
\ket{b_{k}}^{B}
in (
S53
),
which implies (
S40
).
∎

Using Proposition
S13
, we prove the asymptotic continuity of
D^{\mathrm{q}\mhyph\mathrm{c}}_{\textup{rel}}
.

Theorem S14
.

Let
\mathcal{H}^{A}
and
\mathcal{H}^{B}
be finite-dimensional quantum systems with dimension
D=\mathrm{dim}(\mathcal{H}^{A}\otimes\mathcal{H}^{B})
.
The relative entropy of discord
D^{\mathrm{q}\mhyph\mathrm{c}}_{\textup{rel}}
is asymptotically continuous; i.e.,
it holds that

|D^{\mathrm{q}\mhyph\mathrm{c}}_{\textup{rel}}(\phi^{AB})-D^{\mathrm{q}\mhyph\mathrm{c}}_{\textup{rel}}(\psi^{AB})|\leqq\|\phi^{AB}-\psi^{AB}\|_{1}\log_{2}D+2h_{2}\left(\frac{\|\phi^{AB}-\psi^{AB}\|_{1}}{2}\right),

(S54)

where
h_{2}
is the binary entropy function.

Proof.

By Proposition
S13
, it holds that

\displaystyle|D^{\mathrm{q}\mhyph\mathrm{c}}_{\textup{rel}}(\phi^{AB})-D^{\mathrm{q}\mhyph\mathrm{c}}_{\textup{rel}}(\psi^{AB})|

\displaystyle\leqq|\entropy(\phi^{AB})-\entropy(\psi^{AB})|

\displaystyle+\Bigg|\entropy\left(\sum_{k}(\mathbbm{1}^{A}\otimes\bra{b_{k}}^{B})\phi^{AB}(\mathbbm{1}^{A}\otimes\ket{b_{k}}^{B})\otimes\ket{b_{k}}\bra{b_{k}}^{B}\right)

\displaystyle\quad-\entropy\left(\sum_{k}(\mathbbm{1}^{A}\otimes\bra{b_{k}}^{B})\psi^{AB}(\mathbbm{1}^{A}\otimes\ket{b_{k}}^{B})\otimes\ket{b_{k}}\bra{b_{k}}^{B}\right)\Bigg|,

where
\ket{b_{k}}^{B}
is an optimal orthonormal basis for
\psi^{AB}
.
Here, we assume that

\min_{\ket{b_{k}}^{B}}\entropy\left(\sum_{k}(\mathbbm{1}^{A}\otimes\bra{b_{k}}^{B})\phi^{AB}(\mathbbm{1}^{A}\otimes\ket{b_{k}}^{B})\otimes\ket{b_{k}}\bra{b_{k}}^{B}\right)\geqq\min_{\ket{b_{k}}}\entropy\left(\sum_{k}(\mathbbm{1}^{A}\otimes\bra{b_{k}}^{B})\psi^{AB}(\mathbbm{1}^{A}\otimes\ket{b_{k}}^{B})\otimes\ket{b_{k}}\bra{b_{k}}^{B}\right).

Otherwise, we can switch the roles of
\phi^{AB}
and
\psi^{AB}
to apply the same argument.
Now, it holds that

\displaystyle\left\|\sum_{k}(\mathbbm{1}^{A}\otimes\bra{b_{k}}^{B})\phi^{AB}(\mathbbm{1}^{A}\otimes\ket{b_{k}}^{B})\otimes\ket{b_{k}}\bra{b_{k}}^{B}-\sum_{k}(\mathbbm{1}^{A}\otimes\bra{b_{k}}^{B})\psi^{AB}(\mathbbm{1}^{A}\otimes\ket{b_{k}}^{B})\otimes\ket{b_{k}}\bra{b_{k}}^{B}\right\|_{1}

(S55)

\displaystyle=\sum_{k}\left\|(\mathbbm{1}^{A}\otimes\bra{b_{k}}^{B})\left(\phi^{AB}-\psi^{AB}\right)(\mathbbm{1}^{A}\otimes\ket{b_{k}}^{B})\right\|_{1}

(S56)

\displaystyle=\left\|\sum_{k}(\mathbbm{1}^{A}\otimes\bra{b_{k}}^{B})\left(\phi^{AB}-\psi^{AB}\right)(\mathbbm{1}^{A}\otimes\ket{b_{k}}^{B})\right\|_{1}

(S57)

\displaystyle\leqq\|\phi^{AB}-\psi^{AB}\|_{1}.

(S58)

Therefore, using Lemma
S1
(Fannes-Audenaert inequality), we have

|D^{\mathrm{q}\mhyph\mathrm{c}}_{\textup{rel}}(\phi^{AB})-D^{\mathrm{q}\mhyph\mathrm{c}}_{\textup{rel}}(\psi^{AB})|\leqq\|\phi^{AB}-\psi^{AB}\|_{1}\log_{2}D+2h_{2}\left(\frac{\|\phi^{AB}-\psi^{AB}\|_{1}}{2}\right).

(S59)

∎

SIII.2
Measurement-Based Quantum Discord

In this section, we show that another measure for discord,
measurement-based quantum discord
, also satisfies subadditivity and asymptotic continuity; that is, its regularization is consistent.

Definition S15

(Measurement-Based Quantum Discord
Ollivier and Zurek 2001
)
.

Let
\mathcal{H}^{A}
and
\mathcal{H}^{B}
be finite-dimensional quantum systems.
For a quantum state
\phi^{AB}
, the measurement-based quantum discord for the measurement on
B
is defined as

D^{\leftarrow}(\phi^{AB})\coloneqq I(A:B)_{\phi}-J(A|B)_{\phi}.

(S60)

In the above definition,
I(A:B)_{\phi}
is the quantum mutual information defined as
I(A:B)_{\phi}\coloneqq\entropy(\phi^{A})+\entropy(\phi^{B})-\entropy(\phi^{AB})
, and
J(A|B)_{\phi}
is the classical correlation function
Henderson and Vedral 2001
of
\phi^{AB}
defined as

J(A|B)_{\phi}\coloneqq\entropy(\phi^{A})-\min_{\{M_{k}^{B}\}_{k}}\entropy(A|\{M_{k}^{B}\}_{k})_{\phi}

(S61)

where
\{M_{k}^{B}\}_{k}
forms a measurement on system
B
; that is,
\sum_{k}(M_{k}^{B})^{\dagger}M_{k}^{B}=\mathbbm{1}^{B}
holds, and

\entropy(A|\{M_{k}^{B}\}_{k})_{\phi}\coloneqq\sum_{k}p_{k}\entropy(\phi^{A|k})

(S62)

is the conditional entropy with measurement
\{M_{k}^{B}\}_{k}

with probability
p_{k}\coloneqq\tr((\mathbbm{1}_{A}\otimes(M_{k}^{B})^{\dagger}M_{k}^{B})\phi^{AB})
and
post-measurement state
\phi^{A|k}\coloneqq\tr_{B}((\mathbbm{1}^{A}\otimes M_{k}^{B})\phi^{AB}(\mathbbm{1}^{A}\otimes(M_{k}^{B})^{\dagger}))/p_{k}
for measurement outcome
k
.

In a similar manner, we can define the measurement-based quantum discord for the measurement on
A
as

D^{\rightarrow}(\phi^{AB})\coloneqq I(A:B)_{\phi}-J(B|A)_{\phi}.

(S63)

Remark 3
.

The measurement-based quantum discord is not symmetric; that is,
D^{\leftarrow}\neq D^{\rightarrow}
in general
Ollivier and Zurek 2001
.

Hereafter, we only consider
D^{\leftarrow}
as the measurement-based quantum discord.
The same argument applies to
D^{\rightarrow}
by interchanging the roles of
A
and
B
.
We here show that
D^{\leftarrow}
is subadditive and asymptotically continuous; in particular, the regularization of
D^{\leftarrow}
is consistent.

Proposition S16
.

The measurement-based quantum discord
D^{\leftarrow}
is subadditive; that is, for quantum states
\phi^{AB}
and
\psi^{A^{\prime}B^{\prime}}
, it holds that

D^{\leftarrow}(\phi^{AB}\otimes\psi^{A^{\prime}B^{\prime}})\leqq D^{\leftarrow}(\phi^{AB})+D^{\leftarrow}(\psi^{A^{\prime}B^{\prime}}).

(S64)

Proof.

Let
\{M_{k}^{B}\}_{k}
and
\{N_{l}^{B^{\prime}}\}_{l}
be optimal measurements achieving
D^{\leftarrow}(\phi^{AB})
and
D^{\leftarrow}(\psi^{A^{\prime}B^{\prime}})
respectively.
Then, it holds that

\displaystyle D^{\leftarrow}(\phi^{AB}\otimes\psi^{A^{\prime}B^{\prime}})

\displaystyle=I(AA^{\prime}:BB^{\prime})_{\phi\otimes\psi}-J(AA^{\prime}|BB^{\prime})_{\phi\otimes\psi}

(S65)

\displaystyle=\entropy(\phi^{B}\otimes\psi^{B^{\prime}})-\entropy(\phi^{AB}\otimes\psi^{A^{\prime}B^{\prime}})+\min_{\{O_{j}^{BB^{\prime}}\}_{j}}\entropy(AA^{\prime}|\{O_{j}^{BB^{\prime}}\}_{j})_{\phi\otimes\psi}

(S66)

\displaystyle=(\entropy(\phi^{B})-\entropy(\phi^{AB}))+(\entropy(\psi^{B^{\prime}})-\entropy(\psi^{A^{\prime}B^{\prime}}))+\min_{\{O_{j}^{BB^{\prime}}\}_{j}}\entropy(AA^{\prime}|\{O_{j}^{BB^{\prime}}\}_{j})_{\phi\otimes\psi}.

(S67)

Here, consider measurement
\{M_{k}^{B}\otimes N_{l}^{B^{\prime}}\}_{k,l}
.
This is a suboptimal mesurement for
\min_{\{O_{j}^{BB^{\prime}}\}_{j}}\entropy(AA^{\prime}|\{O_{j}^{BB^{\prime}}\}_{j}^{\prime})_{\phi\otimes\psi}
.
Probability
p_{k,l}
for measurement outcome
(k,l)
of this measurement is

\displaystyle p_{k,l}

\displaystyle=\tr((\mathbbm{1}^{A}\otimes M_{k}^{B}\otimes\mathbbm{1}^{A^{\prime}}\otimes N_{l}^{B^{\prime}})(\phi^{AB}\otimes\psi^{A^{\prime}B^{\prime}})(\mathbbm{1}^{A}\otimes(M_{k}^{B})^{\dagger}\otimes\mathbbm{1}^{A^{\prime}}\otimes(N_{l}^{B^{\prime}})^{\dagger}))

(S68)

\displaystyle=\tr((\mathbbm{1}^{A}\otimes M_{k}^{B})\phi^{AB}(\mathbbm{1}^{A}\otimes(M_{k}^{B})^{\dagger}))\times\tr((\mathbbm{1}^{A^{\prime}}\otimes N_{l}^{B^{\prime}})\psi^{A^{\prime}B^{\prime}}(\mathbbm{1}^{A^{\prime}}\otimes(N_{l}^{B^{\prime}})^{\dagger}))

(S69)

\displaystyle\eqqcolon q_{k}\times r_{l}

(S70)

and post-measurement state
\rho^{AA^{\prime}|k,l}
for outcome
(k,l)
is

\displaystyle\rho^{AA^{\prime}|k,l}

\displaystyle=\frac{\tr_{BB^{\prime}}((\mathbbm{1}^{A}\otimes M_{k}^{B}\otimes\mathbbm{1}^{A^{\prime}}\otimes N_{l}^{B^{\prime}})(\phi^{AB}\otimes\psi^{A^{\prime}B^{\prime}})(\mathbbm{1}^{A}\otimes(M_{k}^{B})^{\dagger}\otimes\mathbbm{1}^{A^{\prime}}\otimes(N_{l}^{B^{\prime}})^{\dagger}))}{p_{k,l}}

(S71)

\displaystyle=\frac{\tr_{B}((\mathbbm{1}^{A}\otimes M_{k}^{B})\phi^{AB}(\mathbbm{1}^{A}\otimes(M_{k}^{B})^{\dagger}))}{q_{k}}\otimes\frac{\tr_{B^{\prime}}((\mathbbm{1}^{A^{\prime}}\otimes N_{l}^{B^{\prime}})\psi^{A^{\prime}B^{\prime}}(\mathbbm{1}^{A^{\prime}}\otimes(N_{l}^{B^{\prime}})^{\dagger}))}{r_{l}}

(S72)

\displaystyle\eqqcolon\phi^{A|k}\otimes\psi^{A^{\prime}|l}.

(S73)

Then, it holds that

\displaystyle\min_{\{O_{j}^{BB^{\prime}}\}_{j}}\entropy(AA^{\prime}|\{O_{j}^{BB^{\prime}}\}_{j})_{\phi\otimes\psi}

\displaystyle\leqq\sum_{k,l}p_{k,l}\entropy(\rho^{AA^{\prime}|k,l})

(S74)

\displaystyle=\sum_{k,l}q_{k}r_{l}\entropy(\phi^{A|k}\otimes\psi^{A^{\prime}|l})

(S75)

\displaystyle=\left(\sum_{k}q_{k}\entropy(\phi^{A|k})\right)+\left(\sum_{l}r_{l}\entropy(\psi^{A^{\prime}|l})\right).

(S76)

Therefore, it follows that

\displaystyle D^{\leftarrow}(\phi^{AB}\otimes\psi^{A^{\prime}B^{\prime}})

(S79)

where the last equality (

Theorem S17

Non-Markovianity

In this section, we investigate a consistent resource measure in the resource theory of non-Markovianity.
We adopt the notation in Ref.
Ibinson et al. 2008
.
A Markov state is defined as a state whose quantum conditional mutual information is zero.

Definition S18
.

Let
\mathcal{H}^{A}
,
\mathcal{H}^{B}
and
\mathcal{H}^{C}
be finite-dimensional quantum systems.
Define the set of quantum Markov state
\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{Markov}}
as

\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{Markov}}\coloneqq\left\{\psi^{ABC}\in\mathcal{D}\left(\mathcal{H}^{A}\otimes\mathcal{H}^{B}\otimes\mathcal{H}^{C}\right):I(A:C|B)_{\psi}=0\right\}.

(S105)

Remark 4
.

\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{Markov}}
is not convex.

Here, we define the relative entropy of non-Markovianity.

Definition S19
.

Let
\mathcal{H}^{A}
,
\mathcal{H}^{B}
and
\mathcal{H}^{C}
be finite-dimensional quantum systems.
For a quantum state
\phi^{ABC}\in\mathcal{D}\left(\mathcal{H}^{A}\otimes\mathcal{H}^{B}\otimes\mathcal{H}^{C}\right)
,
define the relative entropy of non-Markovianity
\Delta
as

\Delta(\phi^{ABC})\coloneqq\min_{\psi^{ABC}\in\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{Markov}}}\relent(\phi^{ABC}\|\psi^{ABC}).

(S106)

The following lemma shows a simple characterization of the relative entropy of non-Markovianity.

Lemma S20

(
Ibinson et al. 2008
)
.

Let
\mathcal{H}^{A}
,
\mathcal{H}^{B}
and
\mathcal{H}^{C}
be finite-dimensional quantum systems.
For all states
\phi^{ABC}\in\mathcal{D}(\mathcal{H}^{A}\otimes\mathcal{H}^{B}\otimes\mathcal{H})
, it holds that

\Delta(\phi^{ABC})=\min_{b_{j}^{L},b_{j}^{R}}\left[\entropy\left(\bigoplus_{j}q_{j}\omega(\phi)^{Ab_{j}^{L}}_{j}\otimes\omega(\phi)^{b_{j}^{R}C}_{j}\right)\right]-\entropy(\phi^{ABC}),

(S107)

where
the minimum is taken over decompositions
\mathcal{H}^{B}=\bigoplus_{j}\mathcal{H}_{b_{j}^{L}}\otimes\mathcal{H}_{b_{j}^{R}}

\displaystyle\omega(\phi)^{Ab_{j}^{L}}_{j}\coloneqq\tr_{b_{j}^{R}C}\omega(\phi)^{Ab_{j}^{L}b_{j}^{R}C}_{j},

(S108)

\displaystyle\omega(\phi)^{b_{j}^{R}C}_{j}\coloneqq\tr_{Ab_{j}^{L}}\omega(\phi)^{Ab_{j}^{L}b_{j}^{R}C}_{j},

(S109)

and

\displaystyle q(\phi)_{j}\coloneqq\tr[(\mathbbm{1}^{AC}\otimes P_{j})\phi(\mathbbm{1}^{AC}\otimes P_{j})],

(S110)

\displaystyle\omega(\phi)^{Ab_{j}^{L}b_{j}^{R}C}_{j}\coloneqq\frac{(\mathbbm{1}^{AC}\otimes P_{j})\phi(\mathbbm{1}^{AC}\otimes P_{j})}{q(\phi)_{j}},

(S111)

with the projector
P_{j}
onto
\mathcal{H}_{b_{j}^{L}}\otimes\mathcal{H}_{b_{j}^{R}}

Then, using Lemma

Theorem S21
.

Let
\mathcal{H}^{A}
,
\mathcal{H}^{B}
and
\mathcal{H}^{C}

\displaystyle|\Delta(\phi^{ABC})-\Delta(\psi^{ABC})|

\displaystyle\left\|\bigoplus_{j}q(\phi)_{j}\omega(\phi)^{Ab_{j}^{L}}_{j}\otimes\omega(\phi)^{b_{j}^{R}C}_{j}-\bigoplus_{j}q(\psi)_{j}\omega(\psi)^{Ab_{j}^{L}}_{j}\otimes\omega(\psi)^{b_{j}^{R}C}_{j}\right\|_{1}

SV
Non-Gaussianity

In this section, we investigate a consistent resource measure in the resource theory of non-Gaussianity.
In the following, we consider an infinite-dimensional Hilbert space.
Here, we recall the basic concepts of Gaussian quantum information
Braunstein and van
Loock 2005
;
Weedbrook et al. 2012
.
Consider an
N
-mode bosonic system and define real quadratic field operators
\hat{x}\coloneqq(\hat{q}_{1},\hat{p}_{1},\ldots,\hat{q}_{N},\hat{p}_{N})
.
A quantum state
\phi
is described by its Wigner characteristic function
\chi(\xi,\phi)=\tr[\phi\hat{D}(\xi)]
.
Here,
\xi=(\xi_{1},\ldots,\xi_{2N})\in\mathbb{R}^{2N}
is a real-valued vector, and
\hat{D}(\xi)\coloneqq\exp(i\hat{x}^{\mathrm{T}}\Omega\xi)
is the Weyl displacement operator with
\Omega\coloneqq i\bigoplus_{k=1}^{N}Y
, where
Y
is the Pauli-
Y
matrix.
Then, a quantum state
\psi
is a Gaussian state if its characteristic function
\chi(\xi,\psi)
is expressed as a Gaussian function whose form is determined only by the mean and covariance matrix of
\hat{x}
with respect to
\psi

\chi(\xi,\psi)=\exp\left(-\frac{1}{2}\xi^{\mathrm{T}}(\Omega\Lambda\Omega^{\mathrm{T}})\xi-i(\Omega\bar{x})^{\mathrm{T}}\xi\right),

(S130)

where
\bar{x}
is the mean of
\hat{x}
with respect to the state
\psi
and
\Lambda
is its covariance matrix defined as
\bar{x}_{i}\coloneqq\braket{\hat{x}_{i}}\coloneqq\tr(\hat{x}_{i}\phi)
and
\Lambda{ij}\coloneqq\braket{\hat{x}_{i}\hat{x}_{j}+\hat{x}_{j}\hat{x}_{i}}/2-\braket{\hat{x}_{i}}\braket{\hat{x}_{j}}
for all
i,j\in\{1,2,\ldots,2n\}
.

SV.1
Nonconvex

We first provide the definition of the relative entropy of non-Gaussianity.
Note that since the sum of two Gaussian functions is not necessarily Gaussian, the set of Gaussian states
\mathcal{G}
is not convex.

Definition S22
.

Let
\mathcal{H}
be an infinite-dimensional Hilbert space.
Let
\phi\in\mathcal{D}(\mathcal{H})
be a state.
Define the relative entropy of non-Gaussianity
\delta
as

\delta[\phi]\coloneqq\min_{\psi\in\mathcal{G}}\relent(\phi\|\psi),

(S131)

where
\mathcal{G}
is the set of the Gaussian states.

It is known that the relative entropy of non-Gaussianity can be written as

\delta[\phi]\coloneqq\entropy(\phi_{\textup{G}})-\entropy(\phi),

(S132)

where
\phi_{\textup{G}}\in\mathcal{G}
is the Gaussification of
\phi
, which has the same mean and covariance matrix as
\phi

Genoni et al. 2008
.

In the following theorem, we show that the relative entropy of non-Gaussianity is not continuous even under a reasonable energy constraint by a counterexample.

Theorem S23
.

Let
\mathcal{H}
be a single-mode space.
The relative entropy of
\delta[\cdot]
is not continuous; that is,
there exists
\epsilon>0
such that for any
0<\epsilon^{\prime}\leq 1
,
there exist states
\rho,\sigma\in\mathcal{D}(\mathcal{H})

with energy constraint
\tr(H\rho),\tr(H\sigma)\leqq E
for a given Hamiltonian
H=\hbar\omega a^{\dagger}a
and some positive real number
E

such that

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\|\rho-\sigma\|_{1}\leqq\epsilon^{\prime},

(S133)

\displaystyle|\delta[\rho]-\delta[\sigma]|>\epsilon.

(S134)

Proof.

Let
0<\alpha\leqq 1
be a real number.
Define an integer

m\coloneqq\left\lfloor\frac{E}{\alpha\hbar\omega}\right\rfloor,

(S135)

where
\lfloor{}\cdots{}\rfloor
is the floor function.
Then, define
\rho
and
\omega
as

\displaystyle\rho\coloneqq\alpha\ket{m}\bra{m}+\left(1-\alpha\right)\ket{0}\bra{0},

(S136)

\displaystyle\sigma\coloneqq\ket{0}\bra{0}.

(S137)

Then, it holds that

\frac{1}{2}\|\rho-\sigma\|_{1}=\alpha.

(S138)

Moreover, it holds that

\displaystyle\tr(H\rho)=\alpha\hbar\omega m\leqq E,

(S139)

\displaystyle\tr(H\sigma)=0\leqq E.

(S140)

Since the Gaussification
\omega_{G}\in\mathcal{G}
of a Fock-diagonal state

\omega=\sum_{l=0}^{\infty}p_{l}\ket{l}\bra{l}

(S141)

is given as

\omega_{\textup{G}}=\sum_{l=0}^{\infty}\frac{n^{l}}{(n+1)^{l+1}}\ket{l}\bra{l}

(S142)

with
n=\tr{a^{\dagger}a\omega}=\sum_{l=0}^{\infty}lp_{l}

Marian et al. 2013
;
Marian and Marian 2013
, it holds that

\displaystyle\delta(\omega)

\displaystyle=\entropy(\omega_{\textup{G}})-\entropy(\omega)

(S143)

\displaystyle=-\sum_{l=0}^{\infty}\frac{n^{l}}{(n+1)^{l+1}}\log_{2}\frac{n^{l}}{(n+1)^{l+1}}-\entropy(\omega)

(S144)

\displaystyle=(n+1)\log_{2}(n+1)-n\log_{2}n-\entropy(\omega)

(S145)

\displaystyle=(n+1)\log_{2}(n+1)-n\log_{2}n+\sum_{l=0}^{\infty}p_{l}\log_{2}p_{l}.

(S146)

Therefore, it holds that

|\delta[\rho]-\delta[\sigma]|=\left(\alpha m+1\right)\log_{2}\left(\alpha m+1\right)-\alpha m\log_{2}(\alpha m)-h_{2}\left(\alpha\right)

(S147)

Define a function
f(x)\coloneqq(x+1)\log_{2}(x+1)-x\log_{2}x
so that
we can write

|\delta[\rho]-\delta[\sigma]|=f(\alpha m)-h_{2}(\alpha).

(S148)

Since
f^{\prime}(x)=\log_{2}(1+1/x)>0
,
f
is an increasing function.
In addition, it holds that

\alpha m=\alpha\left\lfloor\frac{E}{\alpha\hbar\omega}\right\rfloor>\frac{E}{\hbar\omega}-\alpha.

(S149)

Therefore, it holds that

|\delta[\rho]-\delta[\sigma]|>f\left(\frac{E}{\hbar\omega}-\alpha\right)-h_{2}(\alpha)\eqqcolon g(\alpha).

(S150)

Since
f
is increasing,
g(0)=f(E/\hbar\omega)>f(0)=0
.
Therefore, since
g
is continuous, there must be a real number
0<\alpha_{0}\leqq 1/2
such that

g(\alpha_{0})>0
.

Fix such an
\alpha_{0}
.
We prove that we can take
\epsilon\coloneqq g(\alpha_{0})
.
For a given
0<\epsilon^{\prime}\leqq 1
, define two states
\rho
and
\sigma
as

\displaystyle\rho\coloneqq\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\epsilon^{\prime}\ket{m}\bra{m}+\left(1-\epsilon^{\prime}\right)\ket{0}\bra{0}&(0<\epsilon^{\prime}\leqq\alpha_{0})\\
\alpha_{0}\ket{m}\bra{m}+\left(1-\alpha_{0}\right)\ket{0}\bra{0}&(\epsilon^{\prime}\geqq\alpha_{0})\end{array}\right.

\displaystyle\sigma\coloneqq\ket{0}\bra{0}.

(S153)

Then, it holds that

\frac{1}{2}\left\|\rho-\sigma\right\|_{1}\leqq\epsilon^{\prime}.

(S154)

Since
f
is increasing and since
h_{2}(x)
is increasing in
0<x\leqq 1/2
,

g(\alpha)=f\left(E/\hbar\omega-\alpha\right)-h_{2}(\alpha)
is decreasing in
0<\alpha\leqq 1/2
.
Therefore, it holds that

|\delta[\rho]-\delta[\sigma]|>f\left(\frac{E}{\hbar\omega}-\alpha_{0}\right)-h_{2}(\alpha_{0})=g(\alpha_{0})=\epsilon,

(S155)

which implies that
\delta
is discontinuous.
∎

SV.2
Convex

Here, we adopt another framework of the QRT of non-Gaussianity where we consider the convex hull of the set of Gaussian states as the set of free states
Takagi and Zhuang 2018
;
Albarelli et al. 2018
.
We consider the following modified version of relative entropy of non-Gaussianity,
which we call the relative entropy of convex non-Gaussianity.

Definition S24
.

Let
\mathcal{H}
be an infinite-dimensional Hilbert space.
Let
\phi\in\mathcal{D}(\mathcal{H})
be a state.
Define the relative entropy of non-Gaussianity
\delta_{\mathrm{conv}}
as

\delta_{\textup{conv}}[\phi]\coloneqq\inf_{\psi\in\mathrm{conv}(\mathcal{G})}\relent(\phi\|\psi),

(S156)

where the infimum can be replaced with the minimum under the energy constraint that we discuss in the following.

Here, we prove that
\delta_{\textup{conv}}
is asymptotically continuous if a given Hamiltonian satisfies a certain condition (See Eq. (
S158
)).
Hereafter, we suppose that the smallest eigenvalue of the Hamiltonian
H
is fixed to zero.
To discuss asymptotic continuity under energy constraint, we consider the following subset of density operators.

Definition S25
.

Let
H
be a Hamiltonian of the system
\mathcal{H}
.
Let
E
be a positive number corresponding to an upper bound of the energy of the system such that
E\geqq 0
.
Then, we define
\mathcal{D}_{H,E}(\mathcal{H})\subset\mathcal{D}(\mathcal{H})
as a subset of the set of density operators that contains all density operators
\rho
satisfying
\tr(H\rho)\leqq E
;
that is,
\mathcal{D}_{H,E}(\mathcal{H})\coloneqq\{\rho\in\mathcal{D}(\mathcal{H}):\tr(H\rho)\leqq E\}
.

The core of the proof is the uniform continuity of
\delta_{\textup{conv}}
, which is shown in Ref.
Shirokov 2018
in a general form.
One of the main components of the proof is the following Gibbs state defined with a Hamiltonian
H
and an energy
E

\gamma(H,E)\coloneqq\frac{\mathrm{e}^{-\beta(H,E)H}}{Z(H,E)},

(S157)

where
Z(H,E)\coloneqq\tr(\mathrm{e}^{-\beta(H,E)H})
and
\beta(H,E)
is the solusion of
\tr[\mathrm{e}^{-\beta H}(H-E)]=0
.
To show asymptotic continuity of
\delta_{\mathrm{conv}}
, we assume a certain condition given in the following lemma.

Lemma S26

(
Shirokov 2018
)
.

Let
H
be a Hamiltonian of the system
\mathcal{H}
.
Let
E
be a positive number corresponding to an upper bound of the energy of the system such that
E\geqq 0
.
Then,
H
satisfies the condition

\lim_{\lambda\to 0}\left[\tr(\mathrm{e}^{-\lambda H})\right]^{\lambda}=0,

(S158)

if and only if it holds that
H(\gamma(H,E))=o(\sqrt{E})
.

In the following proposition, we reformulate the statement of Proposition 3 in Ref.
Shirokov 2018
for
\delta_{\textup{conv}}
.

Proposition S27
.

Let
H
be a Hamiltonian of the system
\mathcal{H}
.
Let
E
be a positive constant corresponding to an upper bound of the energy of the system.
Suppose that the condition (
S158
) is satisfied.
Then, the relative entropy of convex non-Gaussianity
\delta_{\textup{conv}}
is uniformly continuous on
\mathcal{D}_{H,E}(\mathcal{H})
; that is,
it holds that

|\delta_{\textup{conv}}[\phi]-\delta_{\textup{conv}}[\psi]|\leqq\sqrt{2\epsilon}\entropy\left(\gamma(H,E/\epsilon)\right)+g_{2}(\sqrt{2\epsilon})

(S159)

for any
\phi,\psi\in\mathcal{D}_{H,E}(\mathcal{H})
such that
\|\phi-\psi\|_{1}\leqq\epsilon\leqq 1/2
, where
g_{2}(x)\coloneqq(x+1)\log_{2}(x+1)-x\log_{2}x=(x+1)h_{2}(x/(x+1))
defined in
x\in[0,1]
.

Using this proposition, we prove asymptotic continuity in the following theorem,

Theorem S28
.

Let
H
be a Hamiltonian satisfying the condition (
S158
).
Let
E
be a positive constant.
Then, the relative entropy of convex non-Gaussianity
\delta_{\textup{conv}}
is asymptotically continuous on
\mathcal{D}_{H,E}(\mathcal{H})
; that is,
for any sequences
\{\phi_{n}\in\mathcal{D}_{H_{n},nE}(\mathcal{H})\}_{n}
and
\{\psi_{n}\in\mathcal{D}_{H_{n},nE}(\mathcal{H})\}_{n}
such that
\lim_{n\to\infty}\|\phi_{n}-\psi_{n}\|_{1}\to 0

where the Hamiltonian
H_{n}=H\otimes\mathbbm{1}\otimes\cdots\otimes\mathbbm{1}+\mathbbm{1}\otimes H\otimes\mathbbm{1}\otimes\cdots\otimes\mathbbm{1}+\cdots+\mathbbm{1}\otimes\cdots\otimes\mathbbm{1}\otimes H
satisfies the condition (
S158
),
it holds that

\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{|\delta_{\textup{conv}}[\phi_{n}]-\delta_{\textup{conv}}[\psi_{n}]|}{n}=0.

(S160)

Proof.

Using Proposition
S27
, it holds that

|\delta_{\textup{conv}}[\phi_{n}]-\delta_{\textup{conv}}[\psi_{n}]|\leqq\sqrt{2\epsilon}\entropy\left(\gamma(H_{n},nE/\epsilon)\right)+g_{2}(\sqrt{2\epsilon}).

(S161)

Since
\entropy\left(\gamma(H_{n},nE/\epsilon)\right)=\entropy\left(\gamma(H,E/\epsilon)^{\otimes n}\right)=n\entropy\left(\gamma(H,E/\epsilon)\right)
, we have

\frac{|\delta_{\textup{conv}}[\phi_{n}]-\delta_{\textup{conv}}[\psi_{n}]|}{n}\leqq\sqrt{2\epsilon}\entropy\left(\gamma(H,E/\epsilon)\right)+\frac{1}{n}g_{2}(\sqrt{2\epsilon}).

(S162)

∎

Remark 6

(Tighter bound for asymptotic continuity)
.

In fact, a tighter (and almost optimal) uniform-continuity bound was proposed in Ref.
Shirokov 2020
(Theorem 1),
and we may use this bound instead of Proposition
S27
.
However, since the assumptions needed to derive the bound are more complicated for technical reasons,
we adopt the bound in Proposition
S27
in this paper.
One may rewrite the statements of Proposition
S27
and Theorem
S28
using the tighter bound
with this modification of the assumptions, but we here use the bound in Ref.
Shirokov 2020
for simplicity of analysis.

Here, we consider the energy-constrained version of the state conversion
and show that the regularized relative entropy of convex non-Gaussianity

\delta_{\textup{conv}}^{\infty}[\phi]\coloneqq\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{\delta_{\textup{conv}}[\phi^{\otimes n}]}{n}

(S163)

becomes a consistent resource measure under the energy-constrained state conversion.

Definition S29
.

Let
H_{1}
and
H_{2}
be Hamiltonians of the system
\mathcal{H}_{1}
and
\mathcal{H}_{2}
respectively.
Let
E_{1}
and
E_{2}
be some positive constants.
Let
\mathcal{O}^{(\mathrm{EC})}(\mathcal{H}_{1}^{(H_{1},E_{1})}\to\mathcal{H}_{2}^{(H_{2},E_{2})})
denote the energy-constrained subset of the free operations; that is,
an opeartion
\mathcal{N}\in\mathcal{O}^{(\mathrm{EC})}(\mathcal{H}_{1}^{(H_{1},E_{1})}\to\mathcal{H}_{2}^{(H_{2},E_{2})})
is a map from
\mathcal{D}_{H_{1},E_{1}}(\mathcal{H}_{1})
to
\mathcal{D}_{H_{2},E_{2}}(\mathcal{H}_{2})
.

Then, consider a quantum system
\mathcal{H}
with a Hamiltonian
H
. Let
E
be a positive constant corresponding to an upper bound of the energy of the system.
For the
m
-fold quantum system
\mathcal{H}^{\otimes m}
, we adopt the following Hamiltonian
H_{m}\coloneqq H\otimes\mathbbm{1}\otimes\cdots\otimes\mathbbm{1}+\mathbbm{1}\otimes H\otimes\mathbbm{1}\otimes\cdots\otimes\mathbbm{1}+\cdots+\mathbbm{1}\otimes\cdots\otimes\mathbbm{1}\otimes H
.
Define the set of achievable rates under the energy-constrained free operations as

\displaystyle\mathcal{R}^{(H,E)}\left(\phi\to\psi\right)

(S164)

\displaystyle\coloneqq\Big\{r\geqq 0:\exists\left(\mathcal{N}_{n}\in\mathcal{O}^{(\mathrm{CE})}\left((\mathcal{H}^{\otimes n})^{(H_{n},nE)}\to(\mathcal{H}^{\otimes\left\lceil rn\right\rceil})^{(H_{\lceil rn\rceil},\lceil rn\rceil E)}\right):n\in\mathbb{N}\right),\liminf_{n\to\infty}\left\|\mathcal{N}_{n}\left(\phi^{\otimes n}\right)-\psi^{\otimes\left\lceil rn\right\rceil}\right\|_{1}=0\Big\},

where
\phi^{\otimes 0}\coloneqq 1
.
With respect to this achievable rate set, an asymptotic state conversion rate
r^{H,E}\left(\phi\to\psi\right)
is defined as

r^{(H,E)}\left(\phi\to\psi\right)\coloneqq\sup\mathcal{R}^{(H,E)}\left(\phi\to\psi\right).

(S165)

Theorem S30
.

Let
H
be a Hamiltonian satisfying the condition (
S158
).
Let
E
be a positive constant.
For states
\phi,\psi\in\mathcal{D}_{H,E}(\mathcal{H})
, it holds that

\delta_{\textup{conv}}^{\infty}[\psi]r^{(H,E)}\left(\phi\to\psi\right)\leqq\delta_{\textup{conv}}^{\infty}[\phi].

(S166)

Proof.

Fix a positive number
\epsilon>0
.
For an arbitrary positive number
\epsilon^{\prime}>0
, take
r\coloneqq r^{(H,E)}\left(\phi\to\psi\right)-\epsilon^{\prime}
.
There exists a sequence of the energy-constrained free operations
\left(\mathcal{N}_{n}:n\in\mathbb{N}\right)
such that

\left\|\mathcal{N}_{n}(\phi^{\otimes n})-\psi^{\otimes\lceil rn\rceil}\right\|_{1}<\epsilon.

(S167)

holds for an infinitely large number of
n
.
Therefore, it holds that

\displaystyle\frac{\delta_{\textup{conv}}[\phi^{\otimes n}]}{n}

\displaystyle\geqq\frac{\delta_{\textup{conv}}[\mathcal{N}(\phi^{\otimes n})]}{n}

(S168)

\displaystyle\geqq\frac{\delta_{\textup{conv}}[\psi^{\otimes{\lceil rn\rceil}}]}{n}-\sqrt{2\epsilon}\entropy\left(\gamma(H,E/\epsilon)\right)-\frac{1}{n}g_{2}(\sqrt{2\epsilon})

\displaystyle=\frac{\lceil rn\rceil}{n}\frac{\delta_{\textup{conv}}[\psi^{\otimes{\lceil rn\rceil}}]}{\lceil rn\rceil}-\sqrt{2\epsilon}\entropy\left(\gamma(H,E/\epsilon)\right)-\frac{1}{n}g_{2}(\sqrt{2\epsilon})

\displaystyle\geqq r\frac{\delta_{\textup{conv}}[\psi^{\otimes{\lceil rn\rceil}}]}{\lceil rn\rceil}-\sqrt{2\epsilon}\entropy\left(\gamma(H,E/\epsilon)\right)-\frac{1}{n}g_{2}(\sqrt{2\epsilon}).

Since we can take arbitrarily small
\epsilon
and
\epsilon^{\prime}
, it holds that

\frac{\delta_{\textup{conv}}[\phi^{\otimes n}]}{n}\geqq r^{(H,E)}\left(\phi\to\psi\right)\frac{\delta_{\textup{conv}}[\psi^{\otimes{\lceil rn\rceil}}]}{\lceil rn\rceil}

(S169)

for an infinitely large subset of
n
. Taking limit
n\to\infty
, we have

\delta_{\textup{conv}}^{\infty}[\phi]\geqq r^{(H,E)}\left(\phi\to\psi\right)\delta_{\textup{conv}}^{\infty}[\psi].

(S170)

∎
