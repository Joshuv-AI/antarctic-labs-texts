First Steps in Twisted Rabinowitz–Floer Homology
Yannis Bähni

Abstract
Rabinowitz–Floer homology is the Morse–Bott homology in the sense of Floer associated with the Rabinowitz action functional introduced by Kai Cieliebak and Urs Frauenfelder in 2009. In our work, we consider a generalisation of this theory to a Rabinowitz–Floer homology of a Liouville automorphism. As an application, we show the existence of noncontractible periodic Reeb orbits on quotients of symmetric star-shaped hypersurfaces. In particular, our theory applies to lens spaces.

Definition 2.1 (Free Twisted Loop Space).

Let
\varphi\in\Diff(M)
be a diffeomorphism of a smooth manifold
M
. Define the
free twisted loop space of
M
and
\varphi
by

\mathscr{L}_{\varphi}M\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=\mathinner{\{\gamma\in C^{\infty}(\mathbb{R},M)\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}\gamma(t+1)=\varphi(\gamma(t))\>\forall t\in\mathbb{R}\}}.

Let
(M,\omega)
be a symplectic manifold and
\varphi\in\Symp(M,\omega)
. Given a twisted loop
\gamma\in\mathscr{L}_{\varphi}M
and
\varepsilon_{0}>0
, we say that a curve

\mathinner{(-\varepsilon_{0},\varepsilon_{0})}\to\mathscr{L}_{\varphi}M,\hskip 22.99988pt\varepsilon\mapsto\gamma_{\varepsilon}

starting at
\gamma
is
smooth
, iff the induced variation

\mathbb{R}\times\mathinner{(-\varepsilon_{0},\varepsilon_{0})}\to M,\hskip 22.99988pt(t,\varepsilon)\mapsto\gamma_{\varepsilon}(t)

is smooth. Since
\gamma_{\varepsilon}(t+1)=\varphi(\gamma_{\varepsilon}(t))
holds for all
\varepsilon\in\mathinner{(-\varepsilon_{0},\varepsilon_{0})}
and
t\in\mathbb{R}
, we call such a variation a
twisted variation
. Then the infinitesimal variation

\delta\gamma\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=\frac{\partial\gamma_{\varepsilon}}{\partial\varepsilon}\bigg|_{\varepsilon=0}\in\mathfrak{X}(\gamma),

satisfies

\delta\gamma(t+1)=D\varphi(\delta\gamma(t))\hskip 22.99988pt\forall t\in\mathbb{R}.

Lemma 2.1.

Let
(M,\omega)
be a symplectic manifold and let
\varphi\in\Symp(M,\omega)
be of finite order. Let
\gamma\in\mathscr{L}_{\varphi}M
and let
X\in\mathfrak{X}(\gamma)
be such that

X(t+1)=D\varphi(X(t))\hskip 22.99988pt\forall t\in\mathbb{R}.

Then there exists a twisted variation of
\gamma
such that
\delta\gamma=X
.

Proof.

As
\varphi
is assumed to be of finite order, there exists a
\varphi
-invariant
\omega
-compatible almost complex structure
J
on
M
by
[
MS17
, Lemma 5.5.6]
. With respect to the induced Riemannian metric

m_{J}\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=\omega(J\cdot,\cdot),

the symplectomorphism
\varphi
is an isometry. Define the exponential variation

\mathbb{R}\times\mathinner{(-\varepsilon_{0},\varepsilon_{0})}\to M,\hskip 22.99988pt\gamma_{\varepsilon}(t)\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=\exp^{\nabla_{J}}_{\gamma(t)}(\varepsilon X(t)),

for
\varepsilon_{0}>0
sufficiently small and
\nabla_{J}
denoting the Levi–Civita connection associated with
m_{J}
. Such an
\varepsilon_{0}>0
does exist by naturality of geodesics
[
Lee18
, Corollary 5.14]
. Then we compute

\displaystyle\gamma_{\varepsilon}(t+1)

\displaystyle=\exp^{\nabla_{J}}_{\gamma(t+1)}(\varepsilon X(t+1))

\displaystyle=\exp^{\nabla_{J}}_{\varphi(\gamma(t))}(D\varphi(\varepsilon X(t)))

\displaystyle=\varphi\mathinner{\bigl(\exp^{\nabla_{J}}_{\gamma(t)}(\varepsilon X(t))\bigr)}

\displaystyle=\varphi(\gamma_{\varepsilon}(t))

by naturality of the exponential map
[
Lee18
, Proposition 5.20]
.
∎

Definition 2.3 (Twisted Hamiltonian Function).

Let
(M,\omega)
be a symplectic manifold and
\varphi\in\Symp(M,\omega)
. A function
H\in C^{\infty}(M\times\mathbb{R})
is said to be a

, iff

\varphi^{*}H_{t+1}=H_{t}\hskip 22.99988pt\forall t\in\mathbb{R}.

We denote the space of all twisted Hamiltonian functions by
C^{\infty}_{\varphi}(M\times\mathbb{R})
and the subspace of all autonomous twisted Hamiltonian functions by

Recall, that an exact symplectic manifold is by definition a pair
(M,\lambda)
such that
(M,d\lambda)
is a symplectic manifold. An exact symplectomorphism of an exact symplectic manifold
(M,\lambda)
is a diffeomorphism
\varphi\in\Diff(M)
such that
\varphi^{*}\lambda-\lambda

Definition 2.4 (Perturbed Twisted Rabinowitz Action Functional).

Let
(M,\lambda)
be an exact symplectic manifold and
\varphi\in\Diff(M)
an exact symplectomorphism with
\varphi^{*}\lambda-\lambda=df
. For

\mathscr{A}^{(H,F)}_{\varphi}(\gamma,\tau)\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=\int_{0}^{1}\gamma^{*}\lambda-\tau\int_{0}^{1}H_{t}(\gamma(t))dt-\int_{0}^{1}F_{t}(\gamma(t))dt-f(\gamma(0)).

If

and

\mathscr{A}^{H}_{\varphi}
for

.

Definition 3.1 (Liouville Domain).

A

is defined to be a compact connected exact symplectic manifold
(W,\lambda)
with connected boundary such that the Liouville vector field

Let

\mu

of

Let

If
(H_{\sigma})_{\sigma\in I}

The proof follows from the same adiabatic argument as in
[
CF09
, p. 275–277]
. Crucial is that
[
CF09
, Theorem 3.6]
remains true in our setting as well as the genericity of the Morse–Bott condition. Indeed, if
