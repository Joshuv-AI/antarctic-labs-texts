Introduction

In this paper a problem of the Kolmogorov widths of an intersection of an arbitrary family of finite-dimensional balls in a mixed norm is studied. The obtained estimates can be applyed in solving the problem of the widths of an intersection of weighted Besov classes with a strong singularity at a point, or an intersection of Besov classes with dominating mixed smoothness.
