On detecting the trivial rational 3 3 -tangle
Bo-hyun Kwon

Abstract
. An important issue in classifying the rational 3 3 -tangle is how to know whether or not the given tangle is the trivial rational 3-tangle called ∞ \infty -tangle. The author [ 2 ] provided a certain algorithm to detect the ∞ \infty -tangle. In this paper, we give a much simpler method to detect the ∞ \infty -tangle by using the bridge arc replacement . We hope that this method can help prove many application problems such as a classification of 3 3 -bridge knots.
