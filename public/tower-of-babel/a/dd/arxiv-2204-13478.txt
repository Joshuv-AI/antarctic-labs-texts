Observation of the γ ​ γ → τ ​ τ \gamma\gamma\to\tau\tau process in Pb+Pb collisions and constraints on the τ \tau -lepton anomalous magnetic moment with the ATLAS detector


Abstract
This Letter reports the observation of τ \tau -lepton pair production in ultraperipheral lead–lead collisions, Pb+Pb → Pb ​ ( γ ​ γ → τ ​ τ ) ​ Pb \text{Pb+Pb}\to\text{Pb}(\gamma\gamma\to\tau\tau)\text{Pb} , and constraints on the τ \tau -lepton anomalous magnetic moment, a τ a_{\tau} . The dataset corresponds to an integrated luminosity of 1.44 nb -1 of LHC Pb+Pb collisions at s NN = 5.02 \sqrt{s_{{}_{\text{NN}}}}=5.02 TeV recorded by the ATLAS experiment in 2018. Selected events contain one muon from a τ \tau -lepton decay, an electron or charged-particle track(s) from the other τ \tau -lepton decay, little additional central-detector activity, and no forward neutrons. The γ ​ γ → τ ​ τ \gamma\gamma\to\tau\tau process is observed in Pb+Pb collisions with a significance exceeding 5 standard deviations, and a signal strength of μ τ ​ τ = 1.03 − 0.05 + 0.06 \mu_{\tau\tau}=1.03^{+0.06}_{-0.05} assuming the Standard Model value for a τ a_{\tau} . To measure a τ a_{\tau} , a template fit to the muon transverse-momentum distribution from τ \tau -lepton candidates is performed, using a dimuon ( γ ​ γ → μ ​ μ \gamma\gamma\to\mu\mu ) control sample to constrain systematic uncertainties. The observed 95% confidence-level interval for a τ a_{\tau} is − 0.057 < a τ < 0.024 -0.057<a_{\tau}<0.024 .

Uncertainties in the muon momentum scale and resolution follow those in Ref.

Uncertainties in the reconstruction, identification, and energy calibration of electrons and photons are evaluated in accord with Ref.

Uncertainties in the topocluster reconstruction efficiency and energy calibration are estimated using

In summary,
\tau

\text{{}$\text{Pb}$+{}$\text{Pb}$ }\to\text{Pb}(\gamma\gamma\to\tau\tau)\text{Pb}

5\sigma
in 1.44 nb
-1
of
\sqrt{s_{{}_{\text{NN}}}}=5.02

TeV
data at the LHC.
The observed event yield is compatible with that expected from the SM prediction within uncertainties.
The events are used to set constraints on the
\tau
-lepton anomalous magnetic moment, corresponding to
-0.057<a_{\tau}<0.024
at 95% CL.
The measurement precision is limited by statistical uncertainties.
This result introduces the use of hadron-collider data to test electromagnetic properties of the
\tau
-lepton, and the results are competitive with existing lepton-collider constraints.
