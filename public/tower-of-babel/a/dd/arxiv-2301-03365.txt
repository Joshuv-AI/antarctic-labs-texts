LOCALIZED BOUNDED BELOW APPROXIMATE SCHAUDER FRAMES ARE FINITE UNIONS OF APPROXIMATE RIESZ SEQUENCES
K. MAHESH KRISHNA

Abstract




Abstract
: Based on the truth of Feichtinger conjecture by Marcus, Spielman and Srivastava
[Ann. of Math. (2), 2015]
and from the localized version by Gröchenig
[Adv. Comput. Math., 2003]
, we introduce the notion of localization of approximate Schauder frames (ASFs) and approximate Riesz sequences (ARSs). We show that localized bounded below ASFs are finite unions of ARSs.

Keywords
: Feichtinger conjecture, Frame, Riesz sequence, Localization.

Mathematics Subject Classification (2020)
: 42C15, 46A45, 46B45.

Conjecture 1.1

\displaystyle 0<\inf_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\|\tau_{n}\|.

2.
Localized bounded below ASFs are finite unions of ARSs

We consider the following most general notion of approximate Schauder frames. In the entire paper,
\mathcal{X}
is a separable Banach space and
\mathcal{X}^{*}
is its dual.

Definition 2.1
.

[
11
,
7
,
21
]

Let
\{\tau_{n}\}_{n}
be a collection in
\mathcal{X}
and
\{f_{n}\}_{n}
be a collection in
\mathcal{X}^{*}
. The pair
(\{f_{n}\}_{n},\{\tau_{n}\}_{n})
is said to be an
approximate Schauder frame
(we write ASF) for
\mathcal{X}
if
the
frame operator

\displaystyle S_{f,\tau}:\mathcal{X}\ni x\mapsto S_{f,\tau}x\coloneqq\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}f_{n}(x)\tau_{n}\in\mathcal{X}

is a well-defined bounded linear invertible operator.

We use the following notion of ‘bounded below’ for ASFs.

Definition 2.2
.

An ASF
(\{f_{n}\}_{n},\{\tau_{n}\}_{n})
for
\mathcal{X}
is said to be
bounded below
if

\displaystyle\inf_{n\in\mathbb{N}}|f_{n}(\tau_{n})|>0.

Motivated from the definition of localization of frames
[
24
,
20
]
, we introduce the following notion.

Definition 2.3
.

An ASF
(\{f_{n}\}_{n},\{\tau_{n}\}_{n})
for
\mathcal{X}
is said to be
intrinsically/self localized
if there exist
s>1
and
A>0
such that

\displaystyle|f_{n}(\tau_{m})|\leq\frac{A}{(1+|n-m|)^{s}},\quad\forall n,m\in\mathbb{N}.

Two notions of Riesz sequences for Banach spaces exist in literature, see

[
15
,
1
]
and
[
32
]
. Here we define another.

Definition 2.4
.

An ASF
(\{f_{n}\}_{n},\{\tau_{n}\}_{n})
for
\mathcal{X}
is said to be an
approximate Riesz sequence
(we write ARS) if there exists a finite partition
Q_{1},\dots,Q_{N}
of
\mathbb{N}
such that

\displaystyle\mathbb{N}=\bigcup_{j=1}^{N}Q_{j}

and for each
1\leq j\leq N
,

\displaystyle\inf_{n\in Q_{j}}\left(|f_{n}(\tau_{n})|-\sum_{m\in Q_{j},m\neq n}|f_{n}(\tau_{m})|\right)>0.

Note that for Hilbert spaces, if
\{f_{n}\}_{n}
is determined by
\{\tau_{n}\}_{n}
(Riesz representation), then Definition
2.4
is equivalent (due to positivity) to the definition of Riesz sequence (see
[
23
]
). We now formulate the following conjecture (some other are formulated in
[
31
]
).

Conjecture 2.5
.

Every bounded below ASF can be partitioned as a finite union of ARBs.

We now prove Conjecture
2.5
for intrinsically localized ASFs with the help of following result.

Theorem 2.6
.

[
23
]

(i)

For every
s>1
,

\displaystyle D_{s}\coloneqq\sup_{x\in\mathbb{R}}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{(1+|n-x|)^{s}}<\infty.

(ii)

For every
s>1
, there exists a
C_{s}>0
(which does not depend on
\delta
) such that

\displaystyle\sup_{m\in\mathbb{N}}\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N},n\neq m}\frac{1}{(1+|n-m|)^{s}}\leq\frac{C_{s}}{\delta^{s}},

whenever

\displaystyle\inf_{n,m\in\mathbb{N},n\neq m}|n-m|\geq\delta.

Theorem 2.7
.

Conjecture
2.5
holds for intrinsically localized bounded below ASFs.

Proof.

Our proof is highly motivated from
[
23
]
. Let
(\{f_{n}\}_{n},\{\tau_{n}\}_{n})
be an intrinsically localized bounded below ASF for
\mathcal{X}
.
Define

\displaystyle C\coloneqq\inf_{n\in\mathbb{N}}|f_{n}(\tau_{n})|>0.

Since
(\{f_{n}\}_{n},\{\tau_{n}\}_{n})
intrinsically localized there exist
s>1
and
A>0
such that

\displaystyle|f_{n}(\tau_{m})|\leq\frac{A}{(1+|n-m|)^{s}},\quad\forall n,m\in\mathbb{N}.

Let
C_{s}
be the constant as in Theorem
2.6
. Choose natural number
M
such that

\displaystyle\frac{AC_{s}}{M^{s}}\leq\frac{C}{2}.

We now partition
\mathbb{N}
into
Q_{1},\dots,Q_{N}
such that

\displaystyle\mathbb{N}\coloneqq\bigcup_{j=1}^{N}Q_{j}

and for each
1\leq j\leq N
,

\displaystyle\inf_{n,m\in Q_{j},n\neq m}|n-m|\geq M.

(Note that there are infinitely many partitions of
\mathbb{N}
of this type.) Let
1\leq j\leq N
. Then using (ii) in Theorem
2.6

\displaystyle\sup_{n\in Q_{j}}\sum_{m\in Q_{j},m\neq n}|f_{n}(\tau_{m})|\leq A\sup_{n\in Q_{j}}\sum_{m\in Q_{j},m\neq n}\frac{1}{(1+|n-m|)^{s}}\leq A\frac{C_{s}}{M^{s}}\leq\frac{C}{2}.

Therefore for each fixed
1\leq j\leq N

\displaystyle\inf_{n\in Q_{j}}\left(|f_{n}(\tau_{n})|-\sum_{m\in Q_{j},m\neq n}|f_{n}(\tau_{m})|\right)

\displaystyle=\inf_{n\in Q_{j}}|f_{n}(\tau_{n})|-\sup_{n\in Q_{j}}\sum_{m\in Q_{j},m\neq n}|f_{n}(\tau_{m})|

\displaystyle\geq C-\frac{C}{2}=\frac{C}{2}>0.

Since
j
was arbitrary, we get the theorem.
∎
