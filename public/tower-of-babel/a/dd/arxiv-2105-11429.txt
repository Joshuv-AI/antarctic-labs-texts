Introduction

Let
k
be a field and
R=k[x_{1},\ldots,x_{n}]
be a polynomial ring in
n

be a homogeneous ideal of

is defined as
I^{(s)}=\displaystyle{\bigcap_{P\in\operatorname{Ass}I}(I^{s}R_{P}\cap R)}
. Geometrically, the symbolic powers are important since they capture, all the polynomials that vanish with a given multiplicity. We refer
[
4
]
to the reader to analyse the background results of symbolic powers of ideals. By definition it is clear that
I^{s}\subseteq I^{(s)}
for all

but the reverse containment may fail. It is always an interesting problem to find the necessary and sufficient condition for holding the reverse containment. There is no such criteria to be known when the equality
I^{s}=I^{(s)}
holds for any arbitrary ideal. But for certain classes of ideals such as prime or radical ideals, there are equivalent conditions given by Hochster in
[
6
]
and by Li and Swanson in
[
7
]
.
In this paper we compare the ordinary and symbolic powers of edge ideals of weighted oriented graphs.

Let
D=(V(D),E(D),w)
be a weighted oriented graph with the vertex set
V(D)=\{x_{1},\ldots,x_{n}\},
the edge set
E(D)
consists of ordered pairs of the form
(x_{i},x_{j})
which represents a directed edge from the vertex
x_{i}
to the vertex
x_{j}
and the weight function
w:V(D)\longrightarrow\mathbb{N}
.
The weight of a vertex
x_{i}\in V(D)
is
w(x_{i})
denoted by
w_{i}
or
w_{x_{i}}.
If a vertex
x_{i}
of
D
is a source (i.e., has only arrows leaving
x_{i}
), we set
w_{i}=1
. The edge ideal of
D
is denoted by
I(D)
and is defined as

I(D)=(x_{i}x_{j}^{w_{j}}|(x_{i},x_{j})\in E(D)).
Let
G=(V(G),E(G))
be the underlying graph of
D
whose
vertex set is

and edge set is

The edge ideal of

In general, even for monomial ideals comparison of ordinary and symbolic powers is a difficult problem. For simple graphs, by
[
10
, Theorem 5.9]
, we know that all the ordinary and symbolic powers
of edge ideal coincide if and only if the graph is bipartite. But there is no such result for weighted oriented graphs. As the edge ideal of weighted oriented graph depends upon both the orientation of edges and weights on vertices, it is actually difficult to get the necessary and sufficient condition, even for a particular class of weighted oriented graphs. Recently in
[
2
]
, the authors characterize the
weighted oriented complete graphs and weighted oriented complete bipartite graphs for the equality of all the ordinary and symbolic powers of their edge ideals. If a simple graph
G
contains an induced odd cycle of length
2n+1
, we know that
{I(G)}^{(n+1)}\neq{I(G)}^{n+1}
. We prove that the same is true for weighted oriented graphs, if
D
contains an induced odd cycle
D^{\prime}
of length
2n+1
with the condition “
V(D^{\prime})\setminus N_{D}^{+}(V^{+}(D))
contains one vertex which is not source in
D^{\prime}
, otherwise, it contains a vertex which is source in
D^{\prime}
with trivial weight in
D
”, then
{I(D)}^{(n+1)}\neq{I(D)}^{n+1}
(see Proposition
3.1
). As one of its application, we characterize the weighted oriented graph having each edge in some induced odd cycle of it, for the equality of ordinary and symbolic powers of its edge ideal in Theorem
3.3
. Also we characterize the weighted naturally oriented unicyclic graphs with unique odd cycles and weighted naturally oriented even cycles for the equality of ordinary and symbolic powers of their edge ideals (see Theorem
3.11
, Corollary
3.19
and Proposition
3.20
, respectively).

Let
D^{\prime}
be the weighted oriented graph obtained from
D
after replacing the weights of vertices with non-trivial weights which are sinks, by trivial weights. If we assume all vertices of
D
with non-trivial weights are sinks, then
D^{\prime}=G
and in
[
8
]
, we proved that the symbolic powers of
I(D)
and
I(G)
behave in a similar way. In this paper, even if we do not assume all vertices of
D
with non-trivial weights are sinks, we show that the symbolic powers of
I(D)
and
I(D^{\prime})
behave in a similar way (see Theorem
4.4
). We prove that the symbolic defects of edge ideals of
D
and
D^{\prime}
are same in Proposition
4.5
and so we have
{{I(D^{\prime})}}^{(s)}={I(D^{\prime})}^{s}
if and only if
{I(D)}^{(s)}={I(D)}^{s}
for each

As an instant application of this result, we characterize
the weighted oriented even cycles of length
4
which are not naturally oriented for the equality of all the ordinary and symbolic powers of their edge ideals (see Proposition
4.10
).
Finally in Theorem
4.12

symbolic powers of edge ideal of any weighted oriented star graph.

In this section, we compare the ordinary and symbolic powers of edge ideals of weighted oriented graphs containing induced odd cycles and weighted naturally oriented even cycles.

In
[

weighted oriented graphs under certain condition.

Proposition 3.1

be a weighted oriented graph.
Let

be an induced odd cycle with underlying graph
