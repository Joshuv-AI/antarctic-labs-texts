Logarithmic Dieudonné theory
Kazuya Kato

Abstract
We develop the theory of logarithmic p p -divisible groups and the theory of logarithmic finite locally free commutative group schemes.

Finite logarithmic group objects ; definitions

Throughout this paper, let
X
be an fs log scheme (
[
4
]
§1) whose underlying scheme is locally Noetherian.

Let

(resp.
X^{\log}_{\mathrm{\acute{e}t}}
, resp.

]

Thus these sites are the same as categories, but the topologies are different.

1.1
.

We denote by
(\operatorname{fin}/X)_{c}
the category of finite locally free commutative group schemes over the underlying scheme of
X
.
(The notation ”c” means ”
c

with the inverse images of the log structure of

, and by considering the sheaf on

(
[
4
]
3.1) represented by them, we regard
(\operatorname{fin}/X)_{c}
as a full subcategory of the category of sheaves of abelian groups on
X^{\log}_{\mathrm{fl}}
.

1.2
.

We define four categories

\ref{1.2}.1\quad\quad(\operatorname{fin}/X)_{d}\subset(\operatorname{fin}/X)_{r}\subset(\operatorname{fin}/X)_{e}\subset(\operatorname{fin}/X)_{f}

which are full subcategories of the category of sheaves of abelian groups on
X^{\log}_{\mathrm{fl}}
. (The letters

are taken from the properties of the categories or from the properties of the objects of the categories; ”is stable under the Cartier
d
uality”,
”is
r
epresentable”,
”is classical log
e
tale locally”, ”is classical log
f

(\operatorname{fin}/X)_{c}

The definitions of
(\operatorname{fin}/X)_{r}
,
(\operatorname{fin}/X)_{e}

(\operatorname{fin}/X)_{f}
are as follows. (The definition of

1.3
.

Definition.
Let
G
be a sheaf of abelian groups on
X^{\log}_{\mathrm{fl}}
.

(1)

belongs to

if there is a covering
Y\to X
in

(
[

]
2.3) such that the pullback of

to

belongs to
(\operatorname{fin}/Y)_{c}
.

(2)
G
belongs to
(\operatorname{fin}/X)_{r}
if it belongs to
(\operatorname{fin}/X)_{f}
and it is represented by an fs log scheme over

.

(3)
G
belongs to
(\operatorname{fin}/X)_{e}
if there is a covering
Y\to X
in
X^{\log}_{\mathrm{\acute{e}t}}
such that the pullback of
G
to
Y^{\log}_{\mathrm{fl}}
belongs to
(\operatorname{fin}/Y)_{r}
.

Proposition 1.4
.

Let
G
be a sheaf of abelian groups on
X^{\log}_{\mathrm{fl}}
. Then
G
belongs to
(\operatorname{fin}/X)_{r}
if and only if

is represented by an fs log scheme over

which is log flat, finite and of Kummer type.

This follows from
[
4
]
2.7(1) and from the log flat decent theory for properties of morphisms (
[
4
]
§7).

1.5
.

To define
(\operatorname{fin}/X)_{d}
, we need some preparation on Cartier duality.

For an object
G
of
(\operatorname{fin}/X)_{f}
, the sheaf
\mathcal{H}om(G,{\bf G}_{m})
on
X^{\log}_{\mathrm{fl}}
also belongs to
(\operatorname{fin}/X)_{f}
and

G\overset{\cong}{\to}\mathcal{H}om(\mathcal{H}om(G,{\bf G}_{m}),{\bf G}_{m}).

Indeed, working locally for the log flat topology, these are reduced to the classical Cartier duality. As we will see later in
1.8
.2,
(\operatorname{fin}/X)_{r}
is not stable under Cartier duality.

1.6
.

We define
(\operatorname{fin}/X)_{d}
to be the full subcategory of
(\operatorname{fin}/X)_{r}
consisting of objects
G
such that
\mathcal{H}om(G,{\bf G}_{m})
also belongs to
(\operatorname{fin}/X)_{r}
.

Remark 1.7
.

We will see in
2.5
that
(\operatorname{fin}/X)_{e}
is stable under
\mathcal{H}om(G,{\bf G}_{m})
.

Finite logarithmic group objects ; basic results

If

is quasi-compact,

is killed by some non-zero integer. In fact, since there is a covering

for the log flat topology such that

to

,

Proposition 2.1
.

Let
G
be an object of
(\operatorname{fin}/X)_{f}
and assume that
G
is killed by an integer which is invertible on
X
. Then
G
belongs to
(\operatorname{fin}/X)_{d}
.

Corollary 2.2
.

If the underlying scheme of

is a
{\mathbb{Q}}
-scheme, the categories
(\operatorname{fin}/X)_{d}
,
(\operatorname{fin}/X)_{r}
,
(\operatorname{fin}/X)_{e}
and

coincide.

Proposition 2.3
.

Let

be an exact sequence of sheaves of abelian
groups on

. If
G^{\prime}
and

belong to

(resp.
(\operatorname{fin}/X)_{r}
, resp.

, resp.

.
(The similar statement for

Proposition 2.4
.

Let

be a sheaf of abelian groups on

. Then the following three conditions (i) - (iii) are equivalent.

(i)
G
belongs to
(\operatorname{fin}/X)_{e}
.

(ii) Log étale locally on

, there exists a sequence

for

(iii) Classical étale locally on

, there exists a subgroup sheaf

belong to
(\operatorname{fin}/X)_{c}
.

Corollary 2.5
.

(\operatorname{fin}/X)_{e}
is stable under the Cartier duality.

Take a covering

Lemma 2.8
.

Let

be as in the hypothesis of

be an object of

is connected. Then,

It is sufficient to show that the log structure of

is the inverse image of that of

. Let

be the closed point of

of

defines a homomorphism

5.1
