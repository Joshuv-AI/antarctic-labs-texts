In this paper, we report the successful growth of superconducting infinite-layer Nd
0.8

NiO

The thickness and out-plane x-ray diffraction (XRD) pattern of the prepared films were examined using a SmartLab x-ray diffractometer. The superconductivity was confirmed by transport measurements using a Quantum Design Physical Property Measurement System (PPMS) with a standard four-probe configuration. The wire connection was made by melting indium.

Currently, using the PLD growth and soft chemical reduction procedure introduced above, we manage to produce at least one superconducting sample out of the ten films, and we are continuing our optimization of the growth condition to improve even higher the reproducibility. The growth window is extremely narrow probably because the formation energy of the desired 113 phase differs only slightly from that of the secondary phase.
