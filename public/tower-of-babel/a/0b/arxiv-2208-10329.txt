Study of the ω \omega and ω 3 \omega_{3} , ρ \rho and ρ 3 \rho_{3} and the newly observed ω \omega -like state X ⁡ ( 2220 ) X(2220)
Ya-Rong Wang 1,2, Ting-Yan Li 1,2, Zheng-Yuan Fang 1,2, Hao Chen 1,2,3, Cheng-Qun Pang 1,2,3

Abstract
We study the excited states of ω \omega and ω 3 \omega_{3} by comparison with the ρ \rho and ρ 3 \rho_{3} families, and discuss the possibility of X ⁡ ( 2220 ) X(2220) as ω \omega excitation by analyzing the mass spectra and strong decay behaviors. In addition, we predict the masses and widths of ω ⁡ ( 2 ​ D ) \omega(2D) and ω 3 \omega_{3} and ρ 3 ​ ( 4 ​ D ) \rho_{3}(4D) , ρ 3 ​ ( 1 ​ G ) \rho_{3}(1G) , ω 3 \omega_{3} and ρ 3 ​ ( 2 ​ G ) \rho_{3}(2G) and ω 3 ​ ( 3 ​ G ) \omega_{3}(3G) and ρ 3 ​ ( 3 ​ G ) \rho_{3}(3G) . The abundant information of their two-body strong decays predicted in this work will be helpful to further study of these ω \omega and ω 3 \omega_{3} and ρ \rho and ρ 3 \rho_{3} states in experiment and theory.

II
Models employed in the work

The modified GI quark model, Regge trajectory, and the QPC model can be used to calculate the mass spectra and the two-body strong decays of the meson family, respectively. Before discussing the results, the above models will be introduced briefly here.

II.1
Regge trajectory

It is efficient to investigate a light-meson spectrum by determining the Regge trajectory
Chew and Frautschi 1962
;
Anisovich et al. 2000a
.
The following relation is satisfied by the masses and radial quantum numbers of the light mesons that belong to the same meson family:

\displaystyle M^{2}=M_{0}^{2}+(n-1)\mu^{2},

(2.1)

where
M_{0}
represents the mass of the ground state,
n
is the radial quantum number of the corresponding meson with the mass
M
, and
\mu^{2}
is the trajectory slope.
The Regge trajectories are a great method to help us decide the approximate mass range of a particle and it lays a good foundation for our next step.
This method can approximately hold even if it is not as strict as the Regge trajectory as a function of the particle spin, it can still approximately hold since at most one or two excitations are known for each quantum number. And we will calculate the mass spectrum using the MGI model, which obviously takes into account spin breaking.

state as well as
\omega
and
\omega_{3}
and
\rho
and
\rho_{3}
states. We adopt a new way to get the value of
\gamma
in
\omega
and
\omega_{3}
system. Following is the main work.

1.

The newly observed state
X(2220)
should be a
3^{3}D_{1}

\omega
state.

2.

The
\omega(782)
,
\omega(1420)
and
\omega(1960)
states can be interpreted as the
\omega(1^{3}S_{1})
,
\omega(2^{3}S_{1})
and
\omega(3^{3}S_{1})
.
\omega(2240)
,
\omega(2330)
and
\omega(2290)
can be the candidates of
\omega(4^{3}S_{1})
.

\rho(1450)
,

and

are the first, second and third excited states of
\rho(770)
as shown in Fig.
1
.

3.

\omega(2D)
, by our prediction, are
2047.6
MeV and
212\pm 36.1
MeV.
\omega(2205)
and
X(2220)
are the
\omega(3D)
states.

4.

\omega_{3}(1945)
,

and
\rho_{3}(1990)
,

.

5.

The masses of
\omega_{3}(4D)
and
