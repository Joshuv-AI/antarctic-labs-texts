On a generalized Brauer group in mixed characteristic cases, II
Makoto Sakagaito

Abstract
As an extension of an author’s previous paper ( [ 20 , Theorem 1.1] ), we prove the Gersten-type conjecture for the mod p p étale motivic cohomology over a local ring of a smooth scheme over the spectrum of a discrete valuation ring of mixed characteristic ( 0 , p ) (0,p) . We also prove the ℙ 1 \mathbb{P}^{1} -homotopy invariance for the generalized Brauer group which is a generalization of a result due to Saltman ( [ 22 ] ) in mixed characteristic cases.
