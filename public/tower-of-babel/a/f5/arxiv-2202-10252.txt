Four-Family 𝒩 = 1 {\cal N}=1 Supersymmetric Pati-Salam Models from Intersecting D6-Branes
Tianjun Li, Rui Sun, Chi Zhang

Abstract
We investigate the construction of four-family 𝒩 = 1 {\cal N}=1 supersymmetric Pati-Salam models from Type IIA 𝕋 6 / ℤ 2 × ℤ 2 \mathbb{T}^{6}/{\mathbb{Z}_{2}\times\mathbb{Z}_{2}} orientifold with intersecting D D 6-branes. Utilizing the deterministic algorithm introduced in Ref. heCompleteSearchSupersymmetric2021 , we obtain 274 274 types of models with three rectangular tori and distinct gauge coupling relations at string scale, while 6 6 types of models with two rectangular tori and one titled torus. In both cases, there exists a class of models with gauge coupling unification at string scale. In particular, for the models with two rectangular tori, one tilted torus and gauge coupling unification, the gaugino condensations are allowed, and thus supersymmetry breaking and moduli stabilization are possible for further phenomenological study.

3.1
T-Duality and its Variations

In string theory, if two models are related by T-duality, these models are considered equivalent. By applying T-duality, one can tremendously simplify the process of searching inequivalent models. Before the discussion of T-duality, we first point out two obvious symmetries that relate equivalent models.

1.

Two models are equivalent, if they are related by a permutation of three
\mathbbm{T}^{2}
; and

2.

two
D6\mathchar 45\relax

\mathbbm{T}^{2}
are in opposite signs, while are the same on the third
\mathbbm{T}^{2}

The above two symmetries are known as the
D6\mathchar 45\relax

Then, follow the convention of
cveticSupersymmetricPatiSalamModels2004

Type I duality transformation acts on arbitrary two
\mathbbm{T}^{2}

j

k

\mathbbm{T}^{2}

\begin{split}(n^{j}_{a},l^{j}_{a})\mapsto(-l^{j}_{a},n^{j}_{a})\,,\\
(n^{k}_{a},l^{k}_{a})\mapsto(l^{k}_{a},-n^{k}_{a})\,,\end{split}

(25)

when Type I T-duality applies. Recall the definitions in Equation (
8
), Type I T-duality only makes an exchange between

(B_{a},\tilde{B}_{a})
,
(C_{a},\tilde{C}_{a})

\mathbbm{T}^{2}

As for Type II T-duality, it acts on all three different
\mathbbm{T}^{2}

j

k

\begin{split}(1,2)\times(-1,0)\times(-1,1)&\xrightarrow{\text{Type II T-duality}}(-1,2)\times(0,-1)\times(1,-1)\\

\end{split}

The

\begin{split}(1,0)\times(1,-2)\times(1,1)&\xrightarrow{\text{DSEP}}(1,-2)\times(1,0)\times(1,1)\\
&\xrightarrow{\text{DSEP}}(1,-2)\times(-1,0)\times(-1,-1)\,.\\
\end{split}

5
Discussions and Conclusions

Utilizing the deterministic algorithm, we obtain various classes of four-family supersymmetric models from intersecting
D6\mathchar 45\relax
branes on
{\mathbbm{T}^{6}}/{\mathbbm{Z}_{2}\times\mathbbm{Z}_{2}}
orientifold, with and without tilted torus.
In total, there are
274
physical independent four-family supersymmetric models without tilted torus, and
6
physical independent four-family supersymmetric models with the third torus to be the tilted one, without loss of generality.

For models without tilted torus, Model
14
represents the model with gauge coupling unification at the string scale. Models
15
and
16
are the rare models without any
\mathrm{USp}
group in the hidden sectors, with tadpole cancellation conditions satisfied. Models
22
-
26
are with at least two confining
\mathrm{USp}
groups. Thus gaugino condensation can be triggered to break the supersymmetry and stabilize the moduli.
Moreover, there are Models
27
-
35
with wrapping numbers absolutely larger than
5
which was not reached for three family supersymmetric Pati-Salam models as discussed in
heCompleteSearchSupersymmetric2021
.

For models with one tilted torus, Models
37
and
38
satisfy the tadpole cancellation conditions without any filler branes. Models such as Models
39
and
40
are related with
b
and
c
stacks of branes swapping. Model
36
represents the models with exact gauge coupling unification at the string scale, with two confining
\mathrm{USp}
groups allowing gaugino condensation as well. This class of models would be ideal for further phenomenology model buildings (such as in
He:2021kbj
) as gaugino condensation can be triggered to break the supersymmetry and stabilize the moduli, while gauge coupling unified at string scale.
