Curvature of Metrics on Semple Jet bundles
Mohammad Reza Rahmati

Abstract
. We discuss the Morse estimates for the curvature of several metrics on Semple weighted projective bundle over a projective variety. Following Demailly works on holomorphic Morse inequalities we show an analogue of his results along the Green-Griffiths conjecture for invariant jets.
