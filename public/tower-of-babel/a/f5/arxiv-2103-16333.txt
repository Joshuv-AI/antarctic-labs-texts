Global weak solutions for compressible Navier-Stokes-Vlasov-Fokker-Planck system
Hai-Liang Li, Ling-Yun Shou

Abstract
The one-dimensional compressible Navier-Stokes-Vlasov-Fokker-Planck system with density dependent viscosity and drag force coefficients is investigated in the present paper. The existence, uniqueness, and regularity of global weak solution to the initial value problem for general initial data are established in spatial periodic domain. Moreover, the long time behavior of the weak solution is analyzed. It is shown that as the time grows, the distribution function of the particles converges to the global Maxwellian, and both the fluid velocity and the macroscopic velocity of the particles converge to the same speed.



Key words:
Fluid-particle model; Compressible Navier-Stokes-Vlasov-Fokker-Planck; Hypoellipticity; Global existence; Large time behavior

&\quad\quad\quad\quad+\int_{0}^{1}\big[\rho|u|^{2}+\rho u\mathcal{I}(n)+\rho^{\gamma}\big](y,\tau)dy+\int_{0}^{1}(\mu(\rho)u_{y})(y,\tau)dy\Big)d\tau.\end{split}

To employ Lemma

below, we need to estimate

\left\{\begin{split}&\big|\mathcal{I}\big(\rho u+\rho\mathcal{I}(n)\big)(x,t)\big|\leq\|\rho(t)\|_{L^{1}(\mathbb{T})}^{\frac{1}{2}}\|(\sqrt{\rho}u)(t)\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb{T})}+\|\rho(t)\|_{L^{1}(\mathbb{T})}\|n(t)\|_{L^{1}(\mathbb{T})},\\

&\big|\int_{0}^{1}\big[\rho|u|^{2}+\rho u\mathcal{I}(n)+\rho^{\gamma}\big](y,t)dy\big|\\
&\quad\leq\|(\sqrt{\rho}u)(t)\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb{T})}^{2}+\|n(t)\|_{L^{1}(\mathbb{T})}\|\rho(t)\|_{L^{1}(\mathbb{T})}^{\frac{1}{2}}\|(\sqrt{\rho}u)(t)\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb{T})}+\|\rho^{\gamma}(t)\|_{L^{1}(\mathbb{T})}.\end{split}\right.

(2.13)

Since it holds

\left\{\begin{split}&\mu(\rho)\leq 2,\quad\text{if}~0\leq\rho\leq 1,\\
&\mu(\rho)\leq\beta\theta(\rho)+2,\quad\text{if}~\rho\geq 1,\end{split}\right.

we also have

\displaystyle\Big|\int_{0}^{1}(\mu(\rho)u_{y})(y,t)dy\Big|

\displaystyle\quad=1+\frac{1}{4}\Big(\int_{\{y\in(0,1)|\rho(y,t)\leq 1\}}+\int_{\{y\in(0,1)|\rho(y,t)\geq 1\}}\Big)\mu(\rho)(y,t)dy\int_{0}^{1}(\mu(\rho)|u_{y}|^{2})(y,t)dy

\displaystyle\quad\leq 1+\int_{0}^{1}(\mu(\rho)|u_{y}|^{2})(y,t)dy+\frac{\beta}{4}\sup_{x\in\{y\in(0,1)|\rho(y,t)\geq 1\}}\theta(\rho)(x,t)\int_{0}^{1}(\mu(\rho)|u_{y}|^{2})(y,t)dy.

(2.14)

By virtue of (
2.1
), (
2.4
) and (

)-(

\begin{split}|F(\mathcal{X}^{x,t}(t_{2}),t_{2})-F(\mathcal{X}^{x,t}(t_{1}),t_{1})|\leq C_{2}+\frac{\beta C_{1}^{2}}{4}\int_{0}^{T}\sup_{x\in\{y\in(0,1)|\rho(y,t)\geq 1\}}\theta(\rho)(x,t)dt+C_{3}(t_{2}-t_{1}),\end{split}

(2.15)

where

\left\{\begin{split}&C_{2}:=2\|\rho_{0}\|_{L^{1}(\mathbb{T})}^{\frac{1}{2}}C_{1}+2\|\rho_{0}\|_{L^{1}(\mathbb{T})}\|n_{0}\|_{L^{1}(\mathbb{T})}+C_{1},\\
&C_{3}:=\|\rho_{0}\|_{L^{1}(\mathbb{T})}C_{1}+C_{1}^{2}+\|n_{0}\|_{L^{1}(\mathbb{T})}\|\rho_{0}\|_{L^{1}(\mathbb{T})}^{\frac{1}{2}}C_{1}+C_{1}+1.\end{split}\right.

Thus, we apply Lemma
6.1

and

to have

\begin{split}&\sup_{x\in\{y\in(0,1)|\rho(y,t)\geq 1\}}\theta(\rho)(x,t)\leq\max\Big\{\sup_{x\in\mathbb{T}}\theta(\rho_{0}),\theta(C_{3}^{\frac{1}{\gamma}})\Big\}+C_{2}+\frac{\beta C_{1}^{2}}{4}\int_{0}^{T}\sup_{x\in\{y\in(0,1)|\rho(y,t)\geq 1\}}\theta(\rho)(x,t)dt,\end{split}

which together with the Gr
{\rm{\ddot{o}}}

(\ref {basicCNSVFP})_{5}
. The proof of Lemma

is completed.

(\ref {m1})_{1}

(2.24)

-

(2.29)

Multiplying (

(2.51)

(2.52)

Lemma 6.10
.

For any
T>0

\left\{\begin{split}&f\in L^{\infty}(0,T;L^{1}_{2}\cap L^{\infty}(\mathbb{T}\times\mathbb{R})),\quad f_{v}\in L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathbb{T}\times\mathbb{R})),\\
&\rho\in L^{\infty}(0,T;L^{\infty}(\mathbb{T})),\quad u\in L^{2}(0,T;L^{\infty}(\mathbb{T})),\quad G\in L^{1}(0,T;L^{1}(\mathbb{T}\times\mathbb{R})).\end{split}\right.

If
f
solves the equation

\begin{split}f_{t}+vf_{x}+(\rho(u-v)f)_{v}-\rho f_{vv}=G\quad\text{in}~\mathcal{D}^{\prime}(\mathbb{T}\times\mathbb{R}\times(0,T)),\end{split}

(6.4)

then
f
is a renormalized solution to
(\ref {lfp})
; i.e., for any
b(s)\in C^{2}(\mathbb{R})
, it holds

\begin{split}&(b(f))_{t}+v(b(f))_{x}+(\rho(u-v)b(f))_{v}-(\rho b(f))_{vv}\\
&\quad\quad+\rho(b(f)-b^{\prime}(f)f)+b^{\prime\prime}(f)|f_{v}|^{2}=b^{\prime}(f)G\quad\text{in}~\mathcal{D}^{\prime}(\mathbb{T}\times\mathbb{R}\times(0,T)).\end{split}

(6.5)

Proof.

Denote

f^{\varepsilon}:=J^{\varepsilon}\ast f,\quad J^{\varepsilon}(x,v):=J_{1}^{\varepsilon}(x)J_{2}^{\varepsilon}(v),

where
J_{1}^{\varepsilon}(x)
and
J_{2}^{\varepsilon}(v)
are the Friedrichs mollifier for
\varepsilon>0
with respect to the variables
x
and
v
respectively. Applying
J^{\varepsilon}\ast
to the equation (
6.4
), we obtain

\begin{split}&(f^{\varepsilon})_{t}+v(f^{\varepsilon})_{x}+(\rho(u-v)f^{\varepsilon})_{v}-\rho(f^{\varepsilon})_{vv}-G\ast J^{\varepsilon}\\
&\quad=v(f^{\varepsilon})_{x}-(vf)\ast J^{\varepsilon}+[(\rho(u-v)f^{\varepsilon}-(\rho(u-v)f)\ast J^{\varepsilon}]_{v}+[(\rho f_{v})\ast J^{\varepsilon}-\rho(f^{\varepsilon})_{v}]_{v}.\end{split}

(6.6)

Multiplying (
6.6
) by
b^{\prime}(f^{\varepsilon})
and using the fact
-b^{\prime}(f^{\varepsilon})(f^{\varepsilon})_{vv}=-b(f^{\varepsilon})_{vv}+b^{\prime\prime}(f^{\varepsilon})|(f^{\varepsilon})_{v}|^{2}
, we have

\begin{split}&b(f^{\varepsilon})_{t}+vb(f^{\varepsilon})_{x}+(\rho(u-v)b(f^{\varepsilon}))_{v}-\rho b(f^{\varepsilon})_{vv}-b^{\prime}(f^{\varepsilon})G\ast J^{\varepsilon}\\
&\quad\quad-\rho(b^{\prime}(f^{\varepsilon})f^{\varepsilon}-b(f^{\varepsilon}))+\rho b^{\prime\prime}(f^{\varepsilon})|(f^{\varepsilon})_{v}|^{2}=\mathcal{R}_{1}^{\varepsilon}+(\mathcal{R}_{2}^{\varepsilon})_{v}+\mathcal{R}_{3}^{\varepsilon}+(\mathcal{R}^{\varepsilon}_{4})_{v}+\mathcal{R}^{\varepsilon}_{5},\end{split}

(6.7)

where
\mathcal{R}_{i}^{\varepsilon}
,
i=1,...,5
, are given as

\begin{split}&\mathcal{R}_{1}^{\varepsilon}:=b^{\prime}(f^{\varepsilon})[v(f^{\varepsilon})_{x}-(vf_{x})\ast J^{\varepsilon}],\\
&\mathcal{R}_{2}^{\varepsilon}:=b^{\prime}(f^{\varepsilon})[\rho(u-v)f^{\varepsilon}-(\rho(u-v)f)\ast J^{\varepsilon}],\\
&\mathcal{R}_{3}^{\varepsilon}:=-b^{\prime\prime}(f^{\varepsilon})(f^{\varepsilon})_{v}[(\rho(u-v)f)\ast J^{\varepsilon}-\rho(u-v)f^{\varepsilon}],\\
&\mathcal{R}_{4}^{\varepsilon}:=b^{\prime}(f^{\varepsilon})[(\rho f_{v})\ast J^{\varepsilon}-\rho(f^{\varepsilon})_{v}],\\
&\mathcal{R}_{5}^{\varepsilon}:=-b^{\prime\prime}(f^{\varepsilon})(f^{\varepsilon})_{v}[(\rho f_{v})\ast J^{\varepsilon}-\rho(f^{\varepsilon})_{v}].\end{split}

Since it holds as
\varepsilon\rightarrow 0
that

\left\{\begin{split}&f^{\varepsilon}\rightarrow f\quad\text{in $L^{\infty}(0,T;L^{1}_{2}\cap L^{2}_{1}(\mathbb{T}\times\mathbb{R}))$},\\
&(f^{\varepsilon})_{v}\rightarrow f_{v}\quad\text{in $L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathbb{T}\times\mathbb{R}))$},\\
&G\ast J^{\varepsilon}\rightarrow G\quad\text{in $L^{1}(0,T;L^{1}(\mathbb{T}\times\mathbb{R}))$},\end{split}\right.

one can conclude (
6.5
) provided that the right-hand side of (
6.7
) tends to
0
as
\varepsilon\rightarrow 0
in the sense of distributions. To show it, we first have

\begin{split}&|R_{1}^{\varepsilon}|=\Big|b^{\prime}(f^{\varepsilon})\int_{\mathbb{T}\times\mathbb{R}}(J^{\varepsilon})_{x}(x-x_{1},x-v_{1})(v-v_{1})f(x_{1},v_{1},t)dv_{1}dx_{1}\Big|\\
&\quad~~=\Big|b^{\prime}(f^{\varepsilon})\int_{\{|x-x_{1}|\leq\varepsilon,|v-v_{1}|\leq\varepsilon\}}\frac{v-v_{1}}{\varepsilon}J_{x_{1}}^{\varepsilon}(x-x_{1},v-v_{1})(f(x_{1},v_{1},t)-f(x,v,t))dv_{1}dx_{1}\Big|\\
&\quad~~\leq C\sup_{h\in(0,\varepsilon]}\int_{\mathbb{T}\times\mathbb{R}}|J_{x_{1}}^{\varepsilon}(x-x_{1},v-v_{1})||f(x_{1},v_{1},t)-f(x_{1}+h,v_{1}+h,t)|dv_{1}dx_{1},\end{split}

which together with the Young inequality of convolution type gives rise to

\begin{split}&\|R_{1}^{\varepsilon}\|_{L^{1}(0,T;L^{1}(\mathbb{T}\times\mathbb{R}))}\leq C\sup_{h\in(0,\varepsilon]}\|f(\cdot+h,\cdot+h,\cdot)-f(\cdot,\cdot,\cdot)\|_{L^{1}(0,T;L^{1}(\mathbb{T}\times\mathbb{R}))}\rightarrow 0,\quad\text{as}~\varepsilon\rightarrow 0.\end{split}

Due to
f\in L^{\infty}(0,T;L^{2}_{1}(\mathbb{T}\times\mathbb{R}))
and
\rho(u-v)f\in L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathbb{T}\times\mathbb{R}))

\left\{\begin{split}&\|R_{2}^{\varepsilon}\|_{L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathbb{T}\times\mathbb{R}))}\\

&\quad\quad+C\big(1+\|u\|_{L^{2}(0,T;L^{\infty}(\mathbb{T}))}\big)\|f^{\varepsilon}-f\|_{L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}_{1}(\mathbb{T}\times\mathbb{R}))}\rightarrow 0,\\
&\|R_{3}^{\varepsilon}\|_{L^{1}(0,T;L^{1}(\mathbb{T}\times\mathbb{R}))}\\
&\quad\leq C\|(f^{\varepsilon})_{v}\|_{L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathbb{T}\times\mathbb{R}))}\|\rho(u-v)f-(\rho(u-v)f)\ast J^{\varepsilon}\|_{L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathbb{T}\times\mathbb{R}))}\\
&\quad\quad+C\big(1+\|u\|_{L^{2}(0,T;L^{\infty}(\mathbb{T}))}\big)\|(f^{\varepsilon})_{v}\|_{L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathbb{T}\times\mathbb{R}))}\|f^{\varepsilon}-f\|_{L^{\infty}(0,T;L^{2}_{1}(\mathbb{T}\times\mathbb{R}))}\rightarrow 0.\end{split}\right.
