Maclaurin’s series expansions for positive integer powers of inverse (hyperbolic) sine and related functions, specific values of partial Bell polynomials, and two applications Thanks: *Corresponding author Thanks: This paper was typeset usingAmS- L a T e X
Bai-Ni Guo, Dongkyu Lim*, Feng Qi*

Abstract
. In the paper, the authors establish Maclaurin’s series expansions and series identities for positive integer powers of the inverse sine function, for positive integer powers of the inverse hyperbolic sine function, for the composite of incomplete gamma functions with the inverse hyperbolic sine function, for positive integer powers of the inverse tangent function, and for positive integer powers of the inverse hyperbolic tangent function, in terms of the first kind Stirling numbers and binomial coefficients, apply the newly established Maclaurin’s series expansion for positive integer powers of the inverse sine function to derive a closed-form formula for specific values of partial Bell polynomials and to derive a series representation of the generalized logsine function, and deduce several combinatorial identities involving the first kind Stirling numbers. Some of these results simplify and unify some known ones. All of these newly established Maclaurin’s series expansions of positive integer powers of the inverse (hyperbolic) sine and tangent functions can be used to derive infinite series representations of the circular constant Pi and of positive integer powers of Pi.

2.
Maclaurin’s series expansion for positive integer powers of inverse sine function

In
[
16
, Remarks 5.2 to 5.5]
, there is a review and a survey of special cases of Maclaurin’s series expansions of
(\arcsin t)^{\ell}
for
\ell\in\mathbb{N}
. In
[
16

, general expressions for Maclaurin’s series expansions of
(\arcsin t)^{2\ell-1}
and
(\arcsin t)^{2\ell}
for
\ell\in\mathbb{N}

In this section, we discover a nice, simpler, and general expression of Maclaurin’s series expansions of the functions
(\arcsin t)^{m}
and
\frac{(\arcsin t)^{m}}{\sqrt{1-t^{2}}\,}
for
m\in\mathbb{N}
and derive two combinatorial identities for finite sums involving the first kind Stirling numbers
s(n,k)
.

Theorem 2.1
.

For
m\in\mathbb{N}
and
|t|<1
, the function
\bigl(\frac{\arcsin t}{t}\bigr)^{m}
, whose value at
t=0
is defined to be

\biggl(\frac{\arcsin t}{t}\biggr)^{m}=1+\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}(-1)^{k}\frac{Q(m,2k;2)}{\binom{m+2k}{m}}\frac{(2t)^{2k}}{(2k)!},

(2.1)

where

Q(m,k;\alpha)=\sum_{\ell=0}^{k}\binom{m+\ell-1}{m-1}s(m+k-1,m+\ell-1)\biggl(\frac{m+k-\alpha}{2}\biggr)^{\ell}

(2.2)

for
m,k\in\mathbb{N}
and
\alpha\in\mathbb{R}
such that
m+k\neq\alpha
and

is generalized by (
1.1
).

First proof.

In

\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{\bigl(\ti a+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)_{k/2}}{\bigl(\ti a+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)_{-k/2}}\frac{(-\ti x)^{k}}{k!}=\frac{2}{\sqrt{4-x^{2}}\,}\exp\biggl(2a\arcsin\frac{x}{2}\biggr),

in terms of the gamma function

2n\geq k\in\mathbb{N}
, we have

\bell_{2n,k}\biggl(0,\frac{1}{3},0,\frac{9}{5},0,\frac{225}{7},\dotsc,\frac{1+(-1)^{k+1}}{2}\frac{[(2n-k)!!]^{2}}{2n-k+2}\biggr)\\
=(-1)^{n+k}\frac{(4n)!!}{(2n+k)!}\sum_{q=1}^{k}(-1)^{q}\binom{2n+k}{k-q}Q(q,2n;2),

(3.1)

where
Q(q,2n;2)
is given by (
2.2
).

Proof.

It is well known that the power series expansion

\arcsin t=\sum_{\ell=0}^{\infty}[(2\ell-1)!!]^{2}\frac{t^{2\ell+1}}{(2\ell+1)!},\quad|t|<1

is valid, where
(-1)!!=1
.
This implies that

\displaystyle\bell_{2n,k}\biggl(0,\frac{1}{3},0,\frac{9}{5},0,\frac{225}{7},\dotsc,\frac{1+(-1)^{k+1}}{2}\frac{[(2n-k)!!]^{2}}{2n-k+2}\biggr)

\displaystyle=\bell_{2n,k}\biggl(\frac{(\arcsin t)^{\prime\prime}|_{t=0}}{2},\frac{(\arcsin t)^{\prime\prime\prime}|_{t=0}}{3},\frac{(\arcsin t)^{(4)}|_{t=0}}{4},\dotsc,\frac{(\arcsin t)^{(2n-k+2)}|_{t=0}}{2n-k+2}\biggr).

Employing the formula

\bell_{n,k}\biggl(\frac{x_{2}}{2},\frac{x_{3}}{3},\dotsc,\frac{x_{n-k+2}}{n-k+2}\biggr)=\frac{n!}{(n+k)!}\bell_{n+k,k}(0,x_{2},x_{3},\dotsc,x_{n+1})

in
[
10
, p. 136]
, we acquire

\displaystyle\bell_{2n,k}\biggl(0,\frac{1}{3},0,\frac{9}{5},0,\frac{225}{7},\dotsc,\frac{1+(-1)^{k+1}}{2}\frac{[(2n-k)!!]^{2}}{2n-k+2}\biggr)

\displaystyle=\frac{(2n)!}{(2n+k)!}\bell_{2n+k,k}\bigl(0,(\arcsin t)^{\prime\prime}|_{t=0},(\arcsin t)^{\prime\prime\prime}|_{t=0},\dotsc,(\arcsin t)^{(2n+1)}|_{t=0}\bigr).

Making use of the formula

\frac{1}{k!}\Biggl(\sum_{m=1}^{\infty}x_{m}\frac{t^{m}}{m!}\Biggr)^{k}=\sum_{n=k}^{\infty}\bell_{n,k}(x_{1},x_{2},\dotsc,x_{n-k+1})\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

for
k\in\mathbb{N}_{0}
in
[
10
, p. 133]
yields

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\bell_{n+k,k}(x_{1},x_{2},\dotsc,x_{n+1})\frac{k!n!}{(n+k)!}\frac{t^{n+k}}{n!}

\displaystyle=\Biggl(\sum_{m=1}^{\infty}x_{m}\frac{t^{m}}{m!}\Biggr)^{k},

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{\bell_{n+k,k}(x_{1},x_{2},\dotsc,x_{n+1})}{\binom{n+k}{k}}\frac{t^{n+k}}{n!}

\displaystyle=\Biggl(\sum_{m=1}^{\infty}x_{m}\frac{t^{m}}{m!}\Biggr)^{k},

\displaystyle\bell_{n+k,k}(x_{1},x_{2},\dotsc,x_{n+1})

\displaystyle=\binom{n+k}{k}\lim_{t\to 0}\frac{\td^{n}}{\td t^{n}}\Biggl[\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}x_{m+1}\frac{t^{m}}{(m+1)!}\Biggr]^{k},

\displaystyle\bell_{2n+k,k}(x_{1},x_{2},\dotsc,x_{2n+1})

\displaystyle=\binom{2n+k}{k}\lim_{t\to 0}\frac{\td^{2n}}{\td t^{2n}}\Biggl[\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}x_{m+1}\frac{t^{m}}{(m+1)!}\Biggr]^{k}.

Setting
x_{1}=0
and
x_{m}=(\arcsin t)^{(m)}|_{t=0}
for
m\geq 2
gives

\displaystyle\quad\bell_{2n+k,k}\bigl(0,(\arcsin t)^{\prime\prime}|_{t=0},(\arcsin t)^{\prime\prime\prime}|_{t=0},\dotsc,(\arcsin t)^{(2n+1)}|_{t=0}\bigr)

\displaystyle=\binom{2n+k}{k}\frac{\td^{2n}}{\td t^{2n}}\Biggl[\frac{1}{t}\sum_{m=2}^{\infty}(\arcsin t)^{(m)}|_{t=0}\frac{t^{m}}{m!}\Biggr]^{k}

\displaystyle=\binom{2n+k}{k}\frac{\td^{2n}}{\td t^{2n}}\biggl(\frac{\arcsin t-t}{t}\biggr)^{k}

\displaystyle=\binom{2n+k}{k}\frac{\td^{2n}}{\td t^{2n}}\sum_{q=0}^{k}(-1)^{k-q}\binom{k}{q}\biggl(\frac{\arcsin t}{t}\biggr)^{q}

\displaystyle=\binom{2n+k}{k}\sum_{q=1}^{k}(-1)^{k-q}\binom{k}{q}\frac{\td^{2n}}{\td t^{2n}}\biggl(\frac{\arcsin t}{t}\biggr)^{q}.

By virtue of the series expansion (
2.1
) in Theorem
2.1
, we obtain

\lim_{t\to 0}\frac{\td^{2n}}{\td t^{2n}}\biggl(\frac{\arcsin t}{t}\biggr)^{q}=(-1)^{n}\frac{2^{2n}}{\binom{2n+q}{q}}Q(q,2n;2)

for
n\geq q\in\mathbb{N}
. In conclusion, we arrive at

\displaystyle\quad\bell_{2n,k}\biggl(0,\frac{1}{3},0,\frac{9}{5},0,\frac{225}{7},\dotsc,\frac{1+(-1)^{k+1}}{2}\frac{[(2n-k)!!]^{2}}{2n-k+2}\biggr)

\displaystyle=\frac{(-1)^{n}(2n)!}{(2n+k)!}\binom{2n+k}{k}\sum_{q=1}^{k}\binom{k}{q}\frac{(-1)^{k-q}2^{2n}}{\binom{2n+q}{q}}Q(q,2n;2)

\displaystyle=(-1)^{n+k}\frac{(4n)!!}{(2n+k)!}\sum_{q=1}^{k}(-1)^{q}\binom{2n+k}{k-q}Q(q,2n;2).

The proof of Theorem
3.1
is complete.
∎

4.
Application to series representation of generalized logsine function

In
[
16
, Section 4 and Remark 5.7]
, two series representations for generalized logsine function
\ls_{j}^{(k)}(\theta)
defined by (
1.4
) were established by two cases
\ls_{j}^{(2\ell-1)}(\theta)

.

\displaystyle\ls_{j}^{(k)}(\theta)

\displaystyle=(\ln 2)^{j}\biggl(\frac{2\sin\frac{\theta}{2}}{\ln 2}\biggr)^{k+1}\Biggl[k!\sum_{q=1}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^{q+1}}{(k+2q)!}\biggl(2\sin\frac{\theta}{2}\biggr)^{2q}Q(k+1,2q;2)

\displaystyle\times\sum_{\ell=0}^{j-k-1}\binom{j-k-1}{\ell}\biggl(\frac{\ln\sin\frac{\theta}{2}}{\ln 2}\biggr)^{\ell}\sum_{p=0}^{\ell}\frac{(-1)^{p}\langle\ell\rangle_{p}}{(k+2q+1)^{p+1}\bigl(\ln\sin\frac{\theta}{2}\bigr)^{p}}

\displaystyle-\sum_{\ell=0}^{j-k-1}\binom{j-k-1}{\ell}\biggl(\frac{\ln\sin\frac{\theta}{2}}{\ln 2}\biggr)^{\ell}\sum_{p=0}^{\ell}\frac{(-1)^{p}\langle\ell\rangle_{p}}{(k+1)^{p+1}\bigl(\ln\sin\frac{\theta}{2}\bigr)^{p}}\Biggr],

where the falling factorial
\langle z\rangle_{k}
is defined by (
2.9
) and

2.2
).

Proof.

In
[
11
, p. 308]
, it was derived that

\ls_{j}^{(k)}(\theta)=-2^{k+1}\int_{0}^{\sin(\theta/2)}\frac{(\arcsin x)^{k}}{\sqrt{1-x^{2}}\,}\ln^{j-k-1}(2x)\td x

and

. Applying the series expansion (
2.19
) in Corollary
2.1
to (
4.2
) gives

\displaystyle\ls_{j}^{(k)}(\theta)

\displaystyle=\sum_{q=1}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^{q+1}}{{(2q)!}}\frac{2^{2q+k+1}}{\binom{2q+k}{k}}Q(k+1,2q;2)\int_{0}^{\sin(\theta/2)}x^{2q+k}\ln^{j-k-1}(2x)\td x

\displaystyle\quad-2^{k+1}\int_{0}^{\sin(\theta/2)}x^{k}\ln^{j-k-1}(2x)\td x.

Making use of the formula

\int x^{n}\ln^{m}x\td x=x^{n+1}\sum_{p=0}^{m}(-1)^{p}\langle m\rangle_{p}\frac{\ln^{m-p}x}{(n+1)^{p+1}},\quad m,n\in\mathbb{N}_{0}

in
[

, p. 238, 2.722]
results in

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\sin(\theta/2)}x^{2q+k}\ln^{j-k-1}(2x)\td x=\int_{0}^{\sin(\theta/2)}x^{2q+k}(\ln 2+\ln x)^{j-k-1}\td x

\displaystyle=\sum_{\ell=0}^{j-k-1}\binom{j-k-1}{\ell}(\ln 2)^{j-k-\ell-1}\int_{0}^{\sin(\theta/2)}x^{2q+k}(\ln x)^{\ell}\td x

\displaystyle=\sum_{\ell=0}^{j-k-1}\binom{j-k-1}{\ell}(\ln 2)^{j-k-\ell-1}\biggl(\sin\frac{\theta}{2}\biggr)^{2q+k+1}\sum_{p=0}^{\ell}(-1)^{p}\langle\ell\rangle_{p}\frac{\bigl(\ln\sin\frac{\theta}{2}\bigr)^{\ell-p}}{(2q+k+1)^{p+1}}

and

\displaystyle\ls_{j}^{(k)}(\theta)

\displaystyle=\sum_{q=1}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^{q+1}}{{(2q)!}}\frac{2^{2q+k+1}}{\binom{2q+k}{k}}Q(k+1,2q;2)

\displaystyle\quad\times\sum_{\ell=0}^{j-k-1}\binom{j-k-1}{\ell}(\ln 2)^{j-\ell}\biggl(\frac{\sin\frac{\theta}{2}}{\ln 2}\biggr)^{2q+k+1}\sum_{p=0}^{\ell}(-1)^{p}\langle\ell\rangle_{p}\frac{\bigl(\ln\sin\frac{\theta}{2}\bigr)^{\ell-p}}{(2q+k+1)^{p+1}}

\displaystyle\quad-2^{k+1}\sum_{\ell=0}^{j-k-1}\binom{j-k-1}{\ell}(\ln 2)^{j-\ell}\biggl(\frac{\sin\frac{\theta}{2}}{\ln 2}\biggr)^{k+1}\sum_{p=0}^{\ell}(-1)^{p}\langle\ell\rangle_{p}\frac{\bigl(\ln\sin\frac{\theta}{2}\bigr)^{\ell-p}}{(k+1)^{p+1}}

\displaystyle=(\ln 2)^{j}k!\biggl(\frac{2\sin\frac{\theta}{2}}{\ln 2}\biggr)^{k+1}\sum_{q=1}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^{q+1}}{(2q+k)!}\biggl(2\sin\frac{\theta}{2}\biggr)^{2q}Q(k+1,2q;2)

\displaystyle\quad\times\sum_{\ell=0}^{j-k-1}\binom{j-k-1}{\ell}\biggl(\frac{\ln\sin\frac{\theta}{2}}{\ln 2}\biggr)^{\ell}\sum_{p=0}^{\ell}\frac{(-1)^{p}\langle\ell\rangle_{p}}{(2q+k+1)^{p+1}\bigl(\ln\sin\frac{\theta}{2}\bigr)^{p}}

\displaystyle\quad-(\ln 2)^{j}\biggl(\frac{2\sin\frac{\theta}{2}}{\ln 2}\biggr)^{k+1}\sum_{\ell=0}^{j-k-1}\binom{j-k-1}{\ell}\biggl(\frac{\ln\sin\frac{\theta}{2}}{\ln 2}\biggr)^{\ell}\sum_{p=0}^{\ell}\frac{(-1)^{p}\langle\ell\rangle_{p}}{(k+1)^{p+1}\bigl(\ln\sin\frac{\theta}{2}\bigr)^{p}}.

5.
Maclaurin’s series expansions for positive integer powers of inverse hyperbolic sine function and for incomplete gamma function

In this section, by similar methods and arguments used in Section
2
, we discover Maclaurin’s series expansions of the function
\bigl(\frac{\arcsinh t}{t}\bigr)^{m}
for
m\in\mathbb{N}
, of the function
\Gamma(m,\arcsinh t)
for

, and of the exponential function

, while we present three series identities involving

for

and the quantity

for

Theorem 5.1
.

For
m\in\mathbb{N}
and
|t|<\infty
, the function
\bigl(\frac{\arcsinh t}{t}\bigr)^{m}
, whose value at
t=0

\biggl(\frac{\arcsinh t}{t}\biggr)^{m}=1+\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{Q(m,2k;2)}{\binom{m+2k}{m}}\frac{(2t)^{2k}}{(2k)!},

where

is defined by (
2.2
).

The inverse hyperbolic sine function
\arcsinh t
satisfies the series identities

\displaystyle\sum_{\ell=0}^{\infty}(-1)^{\ell}(\ell+1)\frac{(\arcsinh t)^{\ell+2}}{(\ell+2)!}

(5.2)

\displaystyle\sum_{\ell=0}^{\infty}(-1)^{\ell}(\ell+1)(\ell+2)\frac{(\arcsinh t)^{\ell+3}}{(\ell+3)!}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{3}t^{3}+\frac{1}{8}\sum_{k=3}^{\infty}Q(3,k-2;3)\frac{(2t)^{k+1}}{(k+1)!},

and, for

,

\sum_{\ell=0}^{\infty}(-1)^{\ell}\binom{\ell+m}{m}\frac{(\arcsinh t)^{\ell+m+1}}{(\ell+m+1)!}=\frac{1}{2^{m+1}}\sum_{k=m}^{\infty}Q(m+1,k-m;3)\frac{(2t)^{k+1}}{(k+1)!},

(5.4)

where
Q(m,k;3)
is defined by (
2.2
).

The exponential function
\te^{\arcsinh t}
of the inverse hyperbolic sine function
\arcsinh t
has Maclaurin’s series expansion

(5.5)

where extended binomial coefficient

is defined by (
2.8
).

Proof.

In
[
17
, pp. 210–211, (10.49.32) and (10.49.35)]

\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{(a)_{k/2}}{(a+1)_{-k/2}}\frac{x^{k}}{k!}=\exp\Bigl(2a\arcsinh\frac{x}{2}\Bigr)

(5.6)

and

\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{(a)_{k/2}}{(a)_{-k/2}}\frac{x^{k}}{k!}=\frac{2}{\sqrt{4+x^{2}}\,}\exp\Bigl[(2a-1)\arcsinh\frac{x}{2}\Bigr].

(5.7)

In (
5.6
) and (
5.7
), replacing
x
by
2x
, utilizing extended Pochhammer symbol in (
2.5
), and employing extended binomial coefficient in (
2.8
) result in

\te^{2a\arcsinh x}=a\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{\Gamma\bigl(a+\frac{k}{2}\bigr)}{\Gamma\bigl(a+1-\frac{k}{2}\bigr)}\frac{(2x)^{k}}{k!}=1+a\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\binom{a-1+\frac{k}{2}}{k-1}\frac{(2x)^{k}}{k}

(5.8)

and

\frac{\exp[(2a-1)\arcsinh x]}{\sqrt{1+x^{2}}\,}=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{\Gamma\bigl(a+\frac{k}{2}\bigr)}{\Gamma\bigl(a-\frac{k}{2}\bigr)}\frac{(2x)^{k}}{k!}=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\binom{a-1+\frac{k}{2}}{k}(2x)^{k}.

(5.9)

Integrating on both sides of (
5.9
) with respect to
x\in(0,t)
produces

\frac{\exp[(2a-1)\arcsinh t]-1}{2a-1}=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\binom{a-1+\frac{k}{2}}{k}\frac{2^{k}t^{k+1}}{k+1}.

(5.10)

By virtue of the formula (
2.11
), we obtain

\displaystyle\binom{a-1+\frac{k}{2}}{k-1}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{(k-1)!}\sum_{\ell=0}^{k-1}s(k-1,\ell)\biggl(a-1+\frac{k}{2}\biggr)^{\ell}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{(k-1)!}\sum_{\ell=0}^{k-1}s(k-1,\ell)\sum_{m=0}^{\ell}\binom{\ell}{m}\biggl(\frac{k-2}{2}\biggr)^{\ell-m}a^{m}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{(k-1)!}\sum_{m=0}^{k-1}\Biggl[\sum_{\ell=m}^{k-1}\binom{\ell}{m}s(k-1,\ell)\biggl(\frac{k-2}{2}\biggr)^{\ell-m}\Biggr]a^{m}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{(k-1)!}\sum_{m=0}^{k-1}Q(m+1,k-m-1;2)a^{m}

and

\displaystyle\binom{a-1+\frac{k}{2}}{k}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{k!}\sum_{\ell=0}^{k}s(k,\ell)\biggl(a-1+\frac{k}{2}\biggr)^{\ell}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{k!}\sum_{\ell=0}^{k}s(k,\ell)\sum_{m=0}^{\ell}\binom{\ell}{m}\biggl(\frac{k-2}{2}\biggr)^{\ell-m}a^{m}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{k!}\sum_{m=0}^{k}\Biggl[\sum_{\ell=m}^{k}\binom{\ell}{m}s(k,\ell)\biggl(\frac{k-2}{2}\biggr)^{\ell-m}\Biggr]a^{m}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{k!}\sum_{m=0}^{k}Q(m+1,k-m;3)a^{m}

for
k\geq 3
. Substituting these two finite sums into the right hand sides of (
5.8
) and (
5.10
) gives

\displaystyle 1+a\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\binom{a-1+\frac{k}{2}}{k-1}\frac{(2x)^{k}}{k}=1+2xa+2x^{2}a^{2}+a\sum_{k=3}^{\infty}\binom{a-1+\frac{k}{2}}{k-1}\frac{(2x)^{k}}{k}

\displaystyle=1+2xa+2x^{2}a^{2}+\sum_{k=3}^{\infty}\Biggl[\sum_{m=0}^{k-1}Q(m+1,k-m-1;2)a^{m+1}\Biggr]\frac{(2x)^{k}}{k!}

\displaystyle=1+\Biggl[2x+\sum_{k=3}^{\infty}Q(1,k-1;2)\frac{(2x)^{k}}{k!}\Biggr]a+\Biggl[2x^{2}+\sum_{k=3}^{\infty}Q(2,k-2;2)\frac{(2x)^{k}}{k!}\Biggr]a^{2}

\displaystyle+\sum_{m=3}^{\infty}\Biggl[\sum_{k=m}^{\infty}Q(m,k-m;2)\frac{(2x)^{k}}{k!}\Biggr]a^{m}

and

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\binom{a-1+\frac{k}{2}}{k}\frac{2^{k}t^{k+1}}{k+1}=t-\frac{1}{2}t^{2}+\sum_{k=3}^{\infty}Q(1,k;3)\frac{2^{k}t^{k+1}}{(k+1)!}

\displaystyle+\Biggl[t^{2}-\frac{2}{3}t^{3}+\sum_{k=3}^{\infty}Q(2,k-1;3)\frac{2^{k}t^{k+1}}{(k+1)!}\Biggr]a

\displaystyle+\Biggl[\frac{2}{3}t^{3}+\sum_{k=3}^{\infty}Q(3,k-2;3)\frac{2^{k}t^{k+1}}{(k+1)!}\Biggr]a^{2}+\sum_{m=3}^{\infty}\Biggl[\sum_{k=3}^{\infty}Q(m+1,k-m;3)\frac{2^{k}t^{k+1}}{(k+1)!}\Biggr]a^{m}.

On the other hand, the left hand sides of (
5.8
) and (
5.10

\te^{2a\arcsinh x}=\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\frac{(2\arcsinh x)^{m}}{m!}a^{m}

and

\displaystyle\frac{\exp[(2a-1)\arcsinh t]-1}{2a-1}

\displaystyle=\sum_{m=1}^{\infty}\frac{(2a-1)^{m-1}(\arcsinh t)^{m}}{m!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\frac{(\arcsinh t)^{m+1}}{(m+1)!}\sum_{q=0}^{m}(-1)^{m-q}\binom{m}{q}(2a)^{q}

\displaystyle=\sum_{q=0}^{\infty}\Biggl[\sum_{m=q}^{\infty}(-1)^{m-q}\binom{m}{q}\frac{(\arcsinh t)^{m+1}}{(m+1)!}\Biggr](2a)^{q}

\displaystyle=\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\Biggl[\sum_{\ell=m}^{\infty}(-1)^{\ell-m}\binom{\ell}{m}\frac{(\arcsinh t)^{\ell+1}}{(\ell+1)!}\Biggr](2a)^{m}.

Accordingly, equating coefficients of
a^{m}
for
m\in\mathbb{N}_{0}
, we obtain

\displaystyle 2\arcsinh x

\displaystyle=2x+\sum_{k=3}^{\infty}Q(1,k-1;2)\frac{(2x)^{k}}{k!},

\displaystyle\frac{(2\arcsinh x)^{2}}{2!}

\displaystyle=2x^{2}+\sum_{k=3}^{\infty}Q(2,k-2;2)\frac{(2x)^{k}}{k!},

\displaystyle\frac{(2\arcsinh x)^{m}}{m!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=m}^{\infty}Q(m,k-m;2)\frac{(2x)^{k}}{k!},

\displaystyle\sum_{\ell=0}^{\infty}(-1)^{\ell}\frac{(\arcsinh t)^{\ell+1}}{(\ell+1)!}

\displaystyle=t-\frac{t^{2}}{2}+\sum_{k=3}^{\infty}Q(1,k;3)\frac{2^{k}t^{k+1}}{(k+1)!}

\displaystyle\Biggl[\sum_{\ell=1}^{\infty}(-1)^{\ell-1}\binom{\ell}{1}\frac{(\arcsinh t)^{\ell+1}}{(\ell+1)!}\Biggr]2

\displaystyle=t^{2}-\frac{2}{3}t^{3}+\sum_{k=3}^{\infty}Q(2,k-1;3)\frac{2^{k}t^{k+1}}{(k+1)!},

\displaystyle\Biggl[\sum_{\ell=2}^{\infty}(-1)^{\ell-2}\binom{\ell}{2}\frac{(\arcsinh t)^{\ell+1}}{(\ell+1)!}\Biggr]2^{2}

\displaystyle=\frac{2}{3}t^{3}+\sum_{k=3}^{\infty}Q(3,k-2;3)\frac{2^{k}t^{k+1}}{(k+1)!},

\displaystyle\Biggl[\sum_{\ell=m}^{\infty}(-1)^{\ell-m}\binom{\ell}{m}\frac{(\arcsinh t)^{\ell+1}}{(\ell+1)!}\Biggr]2^{m}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=3}^{\infty}Q(m+1,k-m;3)\frac{2^{k}t^{k+1}}{(k+1)!}

for
m\geq 3
. Reformulating these seven series expansions and series identities arrives at

\displaystyle\frac{\arcsinh x}{x}

\displaystyle=1+\sum_{k=3}^{\infty}Q(1,k-1;2)\frac{(2x)^{k-1}}{k!}

\displaystyle=1+\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{Q(1,2k;2)}{\binom{2k+1}{1}}\frac{(2x)^{2k}}{(2k)!}+\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{Q(1,2k+1;2)}{\binom{2k+2}{1}}\frac{(2x)^{2k+1}}{(2k+1)!}

\displaystyle=1+\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{Q(1,2k;2)}{\binom{2k+1}{1}}\frac{(2x)^{2k}}{(2k)!},

\displaystyle\biggl(\frac{\arcsinh x}{x}\biggr)^{2}

\displaystyle=1+\frac{1}{2x^{2}}\sum_{k=3}^{\infty}Q(2,k-2;2)\frac{(2x)^{k}}{k!}

\displaystyle=1+\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{2Q(2,k;2)}{(k+2)(k+1)}\frac{(2x)^{k}}{k!}

\displaystyle=1+\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{Q(2,2k;2)}{\binom{2k+2}{2}}\frac{(2x)^{2k}}{(2k)!}+\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{Q(2,2k-1;2)}{\binom{2k+1}{2}}\frac{(2x)^{2k-1}}{(2k-1)!}

\displaystyle=1+\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{Q(2,2k;2)}{\binom{2k+2}{2}}\frac{(2x)^{2k}}{(2k)!},

\displaystyle\biggl(\frac{\arcsinh x}{x}\biggr)^{m}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{Q(m,k;2)}{\binom{m+k}{m}}\frac{(2x)^{k}}{k!}

\displaystyle=1+\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{\binom{m+2k}{m}}Q(m,2k;2)\frac{(2x)^{2k}}{(2k)!}+\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{Q(m,2k-1;2)}{\binom{m+2k-1}{m}}\frac{(2x)^{2k-1}}{(2k-1)!}

\displaystyle=1+\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{\binom{m+2k}{m}}Q(m,2k;2)\frac{(2x)^{2k}}{(2k)!},

\displaystyle\sum_{\ell=0}^{\infty}(-1)^{\ell}\frac{(\arcsinh t)^{\ell+1}}{(\ell+1)!}

\displaystyle=t-\frac{t^{2}}{2}+\sum_{k=3}^{\infty}Q(1,k;3)\frac{2^{k}t^{k+1}}{(k+1)!}

\displaystyle=t-\frac{t^{2}}{2}+\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}Q(1,2k+1;3)\frac{2^{2k+1}t^{2k+2}}{(2k+2)!}+\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}Q(1,2k+2;3)\frac{2^{2k+2}t^{2k+3}}{(2k+3)!},

and

\displaystyle\sum_{\ell=0}^{\infty}(-1)^{\ell}\binom{\ell+1}{1}\frac{(\arcsinh t)^{\ell+2}}{(\ell+2)!}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2}t^{2}-\frac{1}{3}t^{3}+\frac{1}{2^{2}}\sum_{k=3}^{\infty}Q(2,k-1;3)\frac{(2t)^{k+1}}{(k+1)!},

\displaystyle\sum_{\ell=0}^{\infty}(-1)^{\ell}\binom{\ell+2}{2}\frac{(\arcsinh t)^{\ell+3}}{(\ell+3)!}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{6}t^{3}+\frac{1}{2^{3}}\sum_{k=3}^{\infty}Q(3,k-2;3)\frac{(2t)^{k+1}}{(k+1)!},

\displaystyle\sum_{\ell=0}^{\infty}(-1)^{\ell}\binom{\ell+m}{m}\frac{(\arcsinh t)^{\ell+m+1}}{(\ell+m+1)!}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2^{m+1}}\sum_{k=3}^{\infty}Q(m+1,k-m;3)\frac{(2t)^{k+1}}{(k+1)!}

for
m\geq 3
. Consequently, from the first three equations and the last three equations above, we conclude Maclaurin’s series expansion (
5.1
) and the series identities (
5.2
), (
5.3
), and (
5.4
).

In the fourth formula above, by virtue of (
2.11
), we obtain

Q(1,2k+1;3)=(2k+1)!\binom{\frac{2k-1}{2}}{2k+1}

(5.11)

and

Q(1,2k+2;3)=0.

(5.12)

These two combinatorial identities imply

\sum_{\ell=0}^{\infty}(-1)^{\ell}\frac{(\arcsinh t)^{\ell+1}}{(\ell+1)!}=t-\frac{t^{2}}{2}+\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}2^{2k+1}\binom{\frac{2k-1}{2}}{2k+1}\frac{t^{2k+2}}{2k+2}.

Furthermore, since

\sum_{\ell=0}^{\infty}(-1)^{\ell}\frac{(\arcsinh t)^{\ell+1}}{(\ell+1)!}=-\sum_{\ell=1}^{\infty}\frac{(-\arcsinh t)^{\ell}}{\ell!}=1-\sum_{\ell=0}^{\infty}\frac{(-\arcsinh t)^{\ell}}{\ell!}=1-\te^{-\arcsinh t},

we acquire

1-\te^{-\arcsinh t}=t-\frac{t^{2}}{2}+\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}2^{2k+1}\binom{\frac{2k-1}{2}}{2k+1}\frac{t^{2k+2}}{2k+2}.

Replacing
t
by
-t
in the above equation leads to Maclaurin’s series expansion (
5.5
).
The proof of Theorem
5.1
is complete.
∎

Corollary 5.1
.

For
m\geq 2
, the composite
\Gamma(m,\arcsinh t)
has Maclaurin’s series expansions

\displaystyle\Gamma(2,\arcsinh t)

\displaystyle=1-\frac{1}{2}t^{2}+\frac{1}{3}t^{3}-\frac{1}{4}\sum_{k=3}^{\infty}Q(2,k-1;3)\frac{(2t)^{k+1}}{(k+1)!},

(5.13)

\displaystyle\Gamma(3,\arcsinh t)

\displaystyle=2-\frac{1}{3}t^{3}-\frac{1}{8}\sum_{k=3}^{\infty}Q(3,k-2;3)\frac{(2t)^{k+1}}{(k+1)!},

(5.14)

and, for
m\geq 3
,

\Gamma(1+m,\arcsinh t)=m!-\frac{m!}{2^{m+1}}\sum_{k=m}^{\infty}Q(m+1,k-m;3)\frac{(2t)^{k+1}}{(k+1)!}.

(5.15)

where the quantities
Q(m,k;3)
are given by (
2.2
) and the incomplete gamma function
\Gamma(a,x)
is defined by (
1.5
).

Proof.

In
[
14
, p. 908, 8.352.2]
and
[
19
, Theorem 3]
, there is the formula

\Gamma(1+m,x)=m!\te^{-x}\sum_{k=0}^{m}\frac{x^{k}}{k!},\quad m=0,1,2,\dotsc.

Hence, it follows that

\displaystyle(-1)^{m}\biggl[1-\frac{\Gamma(1+m,x)}{m!}\biggr]

\displaystyle=(-1)^{m}\Biggl(1-\te^{-x}\sum_{k=0}^{m}\frac{x^{k}}{k!}\Biggr)

\displaystyle=(-1)^{m}\Biggl[1-\te^{-x}\Biggl(\te^{x}-\sum_{k=m+1}^{\infty}\frac{x^{k}}{k!}\Biggr)\Biggr]

\displaystyle=(-1)^{m}\te^{-x}\sum_{k=m+1}^{\infty}\frac{x^{k}}{k!}

\displaystyle=(-1)^{m}\Biggl[\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}(-1)^{k}\frac{x^{k}}{k!}\Biggr]\Biggl[\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{x^{k+m+1}}{(k+m+1)!}\Biggr]

\displaystyle=(-1)^{m}x^{m+1}\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\Biggl[\sum_{\ell=0}^{k}\frac{(-1)^{\ell}}{\ell!}\frac{1}{(k-\ell+m+1)!}\Biggr]x^{k}

\displaystyle=(-1)^{m}\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\Biggl[\sum_{\ell=0}^{k}(-1)^{\ell}\binom{k+m+1}{\ell}\Biggr]\frac{x^{k+m+1}}{(k+m+1)!}

\displaystyle=(-1)^{m}\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^{k}(k+1)}{k+m+1}\binom{k+m+1}{k+1}\frac{x^{k+m+1}}{(k+m+1)!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=m}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^{k}(k-m+1)}{k+1}\binom{k+1}{k-m+1}\frac{x^{k+1}}{(k+1)!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=m}^{\infty}(-1)^{k}\binom{k}{m}\frac{x^{k+1}}{(k+1)!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}(-1)^{k+m}\binom{k+m}{m}\frac{x^{k+m+1}}{(k+m+1)!}

for
m\in\mathbb{N}_{0}
, where we used the combinatorial identity

\sum_{\ell=0}^{k}(-1)^{\ell}\binom{k+m+1}{\ell}=\frac{(-1)^{k}(k+1)}{k+m+1}\binom{k+m+1}{k+1}=(-1)^{k}\binom{k+m}{k}

which can be derived from the identity

\sum_{k=0}^{n}(-1)^{k}\binom{x}{k}=(-1)^{n}\binom{x-1}{n}=\prod_{k=1}^{n}\biggl(1-\frac{x}{k}\biggr)

(5.16)

in
[
48
, p. 18, (1.5)]
.
Substituting this result into the series identities (
5.2
), (
5.3
), and (

), (
5.14
), and (

).
The proof of Corollary

6.
Maclaurin’s series expansions for positive integer powers of inverse (hyperbolic) tangent function

In this section, we discuss Maclaurin’s series expansions of the inverse tangent function
\arctan t
and the inverse hyperbolic tangent function
\arctanh t
.

6.1.
Maclaurin’s series expansion for positive integer powers of inverse tangent function

It is well known that

\arctan t=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}(-1)^{k}\frac{t^{2k+1}}{2k+1},\quad|t|<1.

In
[
20
, pp. 152–153, (820) and (821)]
, there are Maclaurin’s series expansions

\displaystyle\frac{(\arctan t)^{2}}{2!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}(-1)^{k}\Biggl(\sum_{\ell=0}^{k}\frac{1}{2\ell+1}\Biggr)\frac{t^{2k+2}}{2k+2}

\displaystyle=\frac{t^{2}}{2}-\biggl(1+\frac{1}{3}\biggr)\frac{t^{4}}{4}+\biggl(1+\frac{1}{3}+\frac{1}{5}\biggr)\frac{t^{6}}{6}-\dotsm

and

\displaystyle\frac{(\arctan t)^{3}}{3!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}(-1)^{k}\Biggl(\sum_{\ell_{2}=0}^{k}\frac{1}{2\ell_{2}+2}\sum_{\ell_{1}=0}^{\ell_{2}}\frac{1}{2\ell_{1}+1}\Biggr)\frac{t^{2k+3}}{2k+3}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2}\frac{t^{3}}{3}-\biggl[\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{4}\biggl(1+\frac{1}{3}\biggr)\biggr]\frac{t^{5}}{5}+\biggl[\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{4}\biggl(1+\frac{1}{3}\biggr)+\frac{1}{6}\biggl(1+\frac{1}{3}+\frac{1}{5}\biggr)\biggr]\frac{t^{7}}{7}-\dotsm

for
|t|<1
.
What is the general expression of Maclaurin’s series expansion of
(\arctan t)^{n}
for
n>3
and
|t|<1
? We guess that it should be

\displaystyle\frac{(\arctan t)^{n}}{n!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}(-1)^{k}\Biggl(\sum_{\ell_{n-1}=0}^{k}\frac{1}{2\ell_{n-1}+n-1}\sum_{\ell_{n-2}=0}^{\ell_{n-1}}\frac{1}{2\ell_{n-2}+n-2}\dotsm\sum_{\ell_{2}=0}^{\ell_{3}}\frac{1}{2\ell_{2}+2}\sum_{\ell_{1}=0}^{\ell_{2}}\frac{1}{2\ell_{1}+1}\Biggr)\frac{t^{2k+n}}{2k+n}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}(-1)^{k}\Biggl(\prod_{m=1}^{n-1}\sum_{\ell_{m}=0}^{\ell_{m+1}}\frac{1}{2\ell_{m}+m}\Biggr)\frac{t^{2k+n}}{2k+n}

(6.1)

for
|t|<1
and all
n\in\mathbb{N}
with
\ell_{n}=k
, where the product is understood to be
1
if the starting index exceeds the finishing index. For example, when
n=4,5,6
, we have

\displaystyle\frac{(\arctan t)^{4}}{4!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}(-1)^{k}\Biggl(\sum_{\ell_{3}=0}^{k}\frac{1}{2\ell_{3}+3}\sum_{\ell_{2}=0}^{\ell_{3}}\frac{1}{2\ell_{2}+2}\sum_{\ell_{1}=0}^{\ell_{2}}\frac{1}{2\ell_{1}+1}\Biggr)\frac{t^{2k+4}}{2k+4},

\displaystyle\frac{(\arctan t)^{5}}{5!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}(-1)^{k}\Biggl(\sum_{\ell_{4}=0}^{k}\frac{1}{2\ell_{4}+4}\sum_{\ell_{3}=0}^{\ell_{4}}\frac{1}{2\ell_{3}+3}\sum_{\ell_{2}=0}^{\ell_{3}}\frac{1}{2\ell_{2}+2}\sum_{\ell_{1}=0}^{\ell_{2}}\frac{1}{2\ell_{1}+1}\Biggr)\frac{t^{2k+5}}{2k+5},

and

\frac{(\arctan t)^{6}}{6!}=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}(-1)^{k}\Biggl(\sum_{\ell_{5}=0}^{k}\frac{1}{2\ell_{5}+5}\sum_{\ell_{4}=0}^{\ell_{5}}\frac{1}{2\ell_{4}+4}\sum_{\ell_{3}=0}^{\ell_{4}}\frac{1}{2\ell_{3}+3}\sum_{\ell_{2}=0}^{\ell_{3}}\frac{1}{2\ell_{2}+2}\sum_{\ell_{1}=0}^{\ell_{2}}\frac{1}{2\ell_{1}+1}\Biggr)\frac{t^{2k+6}}{2k+6}

for
|t|<1
.

In
[
1
, p. 122, 6.42.3]
, there is Maclaurin’s series expansion

(\arctan x)^{p}=p!\sum_{k_{0}=1}^{\infty}(-1)^{k_{0}-1}\frac{x^{2k_{0}+p-2}}{2k_{0}+p-2}\prod_{\alpha=1}^{p-1}\Biggl(\sum_{k_{\alpha}=1}^{k_{\alpha-1}}\frac{1}{2k_{\alpha}+p-\alpha-2}\Biggr)

(6.2)

for
p\in\mathbb{N}
. The Maclaurin’s series expansion (
6.2
) was proved in
[
46
]
and is obviously equivalent to (
6.1
). Hence, Maclaurin’s series expansion (
6.1
) is true.

By the way, in appearance, Maclaurin’s series expansion (
6.1
) is more beautiful than (
6.2
).

6.2.
Maclaurin’s series expansion for positive integer powers of inverse hyperbolic tangent function

It is also well known that

\arctanh t=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{t^{2k+1}}{2k+1},\quad|t|<1.

Motivated by the difference between (
2.1
) and (
5.1
), basing on (
6.1
), we further guess that

\displaystyle\frac{(\arctanh t)^{n}}{n!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\Biggl(\sum_{\ell_{n-1}=0}^{k}\frac{1}{2\ell_{n-1}+n-1}\sum_{\ell_{n-2}=0}^{\ell_{n-1}}\frac{1}{2\ell_{n-2}+n-2}\dotsm\sum_{\ell_{2}=0}^{\ell_{3}}\frac{1}{2\ell_{2}+2}\sum_{\ell_{1}=0}^{\ell_{2}}\frac{1}{2\ell_{1}+1}\Biggr)\frac{t^{2k+n}}{2k+n}

(6.3)

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\Biggl(\prod_{m=1}^{n-1}\sum_{\ell_{m}=0}^{\ell_{m+1}}\frac{1}{2\ell_{m}+m}\Biggr)\frac{t^{2k+n}}{2k+n}

for
|t|<1
and all
n\in\mathbb{N}
with
\ell_{n}=k
, where the product is understood to be
1
if the starting index exceeds the finishing index. For example, when
n=2,3,4,5,6
, we have

\displaystyle\frac{(\arctanh t)^{2}}{2!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\Biggl(\sum_{\ell=0}^{k}\frac{1}{2\ell+1}\Biggr)\frac{t^{2k+2}}{2k+2},

\displaystyle\frac{(\arctanh t)^{3}}{3!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\Biggl(\sum_{\ell_{2}=0}^{k}\frac{1}{2\ell_{2}+2}\sum_{\ell_{1}=0}^{\ell_{2}}\frac{1}{2\ell_{1}+1}\Biggr)\frac{t^{2k+3}}{2k+3},

\displaystyle\frac{(\arctanh t)^{4}}{4!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\Biggl(\sum_{\ell_{3}=0}^{k}\frac{1}{2\ell_{3}+3}\sum_{\ell_{2}=0}^{\ell_{3}}\frac{1}{2\ell_{2}+2}\sum_{\ell_{1}=0}^{\ell_{2}}\frac{1}{2\ell_{1}+1}\Biggr)\frac{t^{2k+4}}{2k+4},

\displaystyle\frac{(\arctanh t)^{5}}{5!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\Biggl(\sum_{\ell_{4}=0}^{k}\frac{1}{2\ell_{4}+4}\sum_{\ell_{3}=0}^{\ell_{4}}\frac{1}{2\ell_{3}+3}\sum_{\ell_{2}=0}^{\ell_{3}}\frac{1}{2\ell_{2}+2}\sum_{\ell_{1}=0}^{\ell_{2}}\frac{1}{2\ell_{1}+1}\Biggr)\frac{t^{2k+5}}{2k+5},

and

\frac{(\arctanh t)^{6}}{6!}=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\Biggl(\sum_{\ell_{5}=0}^{k}\frac{1}{2\ell_{5}+5}\sum_{\ell_{4}=0}^{\ell_{5}}\frac{1}{2\ell_{4}+4}\sum_{\ell_{3}=0}^{\ell_{4}}\frac{1}{2\ell_{3}+3}\sum_{\ell_{2}=0}^{\ell_{3}}\frac{1}{2\ell_{2}+2}\sum_{\ell_{1}=0}^{\ell_{2}}\frac{1}{2\ell_{1}+1}\Biggr)\frac{t^{2k+6}}{2k+6}

for
|t|<1
.

Proof of Maclaurin’s series expansion (
6.3
).

Imitating the proof of (
6.2
) in
[
46
]
, we now give a proof of Maclaurin’s series expansion (
6.3
).

It is clear that

\arctanh t=\int_{0}^{t}\frac{\td x}{1-x^{2}}=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\int_{0}^{t}x^{2k}\td x=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{t^{2k+1}}{2k+1}

and

\displaystyle(\arctanh t)^{2}

\displaystyle=2\int_{0}^{t}\frac{\arctanh x}{1-x^{2}}\td x

\displaystyle=2\int_{0}^{t}\Biggl(\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{x^{2k+1}}{2k+1}\Biggr)\Biggl(\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}x^{2k}\Biggr)\td x

\displaystyle=2\int_{0}^{t}\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{2k+1}\Biggl(\sum_{\ell=0}^{\infty}x^{2\ell+2k+1}\Biggr)\td x

\displaystyle=2\int_{0}^{t}\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{2k+1}\Biggl(\sum_{\ell=k}^{\infty}x^{2\ell+1}\Biggr)\td x

\displaystyle=2!\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{2k+1}\Biggl(\sum_{\ell=k}^{\infty}\frac{t^{2\ell+2}}{2\ell+2}\Biggr)

\displaystyle=2!\sum_{\ell=0}^{\infty}\Biggl(\sum_{k=0}^{\ell}\frac{1}{2k+1}\Biggr)\frac{t^{2\ell+2}}{2\ell+2}.

If Maclaurin’s series expansion (
6.3
) is true, then

\displaystyle(\arctanh t)^{n+1}

\displaystyle=(n+1)\int_{0}^{t}\frac{(\arctanh x)^{n}}{1-x^{2}}\td x

\displaystyle=(n+1)!\int_{0}^{t}\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\Biggl(\prod_{m=1}^{n-1}\sum_{\ell_{m}=0}^{\ell_{m+1}}\frac{1}{2\ell_{m}+m}\Biggr)\frac{x^{2k+n}}{2k+n}\sum_{\ell=0}^{\infty}x^{2\ell}\td x

\displaystyle=(n+1)!\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\Biggl(\prod_{m=1}^{n-1}\sum_{\ell_{m}=0}^{\ell_{m+1}}\frac{1}{2\ell_{m}+m}\Biggr)\frac{1}{2k+n}\sum_{\ell=0}^{\infty}\int_{0}^{t}x^{2\ell+2k+n}\td x

\displaystyle=(n+1)!\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\Biggl(\prod_{m=1}^{n-1}\sum_{\ell_{m}=0}^{\ell_{m+1}}\frac{1}{2\ell_{m}+m}\Biggr)\frac{1}{2k+n}\sum_{\ell=0}^{\infty}\frac{x^{2\ell+2k+n+1}}{2\ell+2k+n+1}

\displaystyle=(n+1)!\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{2k+n}\Biggl(\sum_{\ell_{n-1}=0}^{k}\frac{1}{2\ell_{n-1}+n-1}\prod_{m=1}^{n-2}\sum_{\ell_{m}=0}^{\ell_{m+1}}\frac{1}{2\ell_{m}+m}\Biggr)\sum_{\ell=k}^{\infty}\frac{x^{2\ell+n+1}}{2\ell+n+1}

\displaystyle=(n+1)!\sum_{\ell=0}^{\infty}\sum_{k=0}^{\ell}\frac{1}{2k+n}\Biggl(\sum_{\ell_{n-1}=0}^{k}\frac{1}{2\ell_{n-1}+n-1}\prod_{m=1}^{n-2}\sum_{\ell_{m}=0}^{\ell_{m+1}}\frac{1}{2\ell_{m}+m}\Biggr)\frac{x^{2\ell+n+1}}{2\ell+n+1}

\displaystyle=(n+1)!\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\Biggl(\prod_{m=1}^{n}\sum_{\ell_{m}=0}^{\ell_{m+1}}\frac{1}{2\ell_{m}+m}\Biggr)\frac{t^{2k+n+1}}{2k+n+1},

where
\ell_{n+1}=k
.
By induction, Maclaurin’s series expansion (
6.3
) is thus proved.
∎

Remark 7.17
.

It is common knowledge that

\arcsin t+\arccos t=\frac{\pi}{2},\quad|t|<1.

This means that

\displaystyle(\arccos t)^{m}

\displaystyle=\biggl(\frac{\pi}{2}-\arcsin t\biggr)^{m}

\displaystyle=\sum_{q=0}^{m}(-1)^{q}\binom{m}{q}\biggl(\frac{\pi}{2}\biggr)^{m-q}(\arcsin t)^{q}

\displaystyle=\biggl(\frac{\pi}{2}\biggr)^{m}+\sum_{q=1}^{m}(-1)^{q}\binom{m}{q}\biggl(\frac{\pi}{2}\biggr)^{m-q}t^{q}\Biggl[1+\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^{k}}{\binom{q+2k}{q}}Q(q,2k;2)\frac{(2t)^{2k}}{(2k)!}\Biggr]

\displaystyle=\biggl(\frac{\pi}{2}\biggr)^{m}+\sum_{q=1}^{m}(-1)^{q}\binom{m}{q}\biggl(\frac{\pi}{2}\biggr)^{m-q}t^{q}

\displaystyle\quad+\sum_{q=1}^{m}\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\binom{m}{q}\biggl(\frac{\pi}{2}\biggr)^{m-q}\frac{(-1)^{q+k}}{\binom{q+2k}{q}}Q(q,2k;2)\frac{2^{2k}t^{q+2k}}{(2k)!}

\displaystyle=\biggl(\frac{\pi}{2}\biggr)^{m}+\sum_{q=1}^{m}(-1)^{q}\binom{m}{q}\biggl(\frac{\pi}{2}\biggr)^{m-q}t^{q}

\displaystyle\quad+\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\sum_{q=1}^{m}\binom{m}{q}\biggl(\frac{\pi}{2}\biggr)^{m-q}\frac{(-1)^{q+k}}{\binom{q+2k}{q}}Q(q,2k;2)\frac{2^{2k}t^{q+2k}}{(2k)!}

\displaystyle=\biggl(\frac{\pi}{2}\biggr)^{m}+\sum_{q=1}^{m}(-1)^{q}\binom{m}{q}\biggl(\frac{\pi}{2}\biggr)^{m-q}t^{q}

\displaystyle\quad+\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}(-4)^{k}\sum_{q=1}^{m}(-1)^{q}q!\binom{m}{q}\biggl(\frac{\pi}{2}\biggr)^{m-q}Q(q,2k;2)\frac{t^{q+2k}}{(q+2k)!}

\displaystyle=\biggl(\frac{\pi}{2}\biggr)^{m}+\sum_{q=1}^{m}(-1)^{q}\binom{m}{q}\biggl(\frac{\pi}{2}\biggr)^{m-q}t^{q}

\displaystyle\quad+\sum_{p=3}^{\infty}\Biggl[\sum_{q+2k=p}^{q,k\in\mathbb{N}}(-4)^{k}(-1)^{q}q!\binom{m}{q}\biggl(\frac{\pi}{2}\biggr)^{m-q}Q(q,p-q;2)\Biggr]\frac{t^{p}}{p!}
