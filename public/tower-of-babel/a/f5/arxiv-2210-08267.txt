Two-dimensional Penta-NiPS Sheets: Two Stable Polymorphs
Klichchupong Dabsamut, Thanasee Thanasarnsurapong, Intuon Chatratin, Tosapol Maluangnont, Sirichok Jungthawan, Adisak Boonchun

Abstract
The discovery of new and stable two-dimensional (2D) materials with exotic properties is essential for technological advancement. Inspired by the recently reported penta -PdPSe, we proposed penta -NiPS as a new member of the penta -2D materials based on first-principles calculations. The penta -NiPS monolayer is stable in two polymorphs including the α \alpha phase with identical structure as penta -PdPSe, and the newly proposed β \beta phase with rotated sublayers. Comprehensive analyses indicated that both phases are thermodynamically, dynamically, mechanically, and thermally stable. The penta -NiPS is a soft material with 2D Young’s modulus of E a E_{a} = 208 Nm -1 and E b E_{b} = 178 Nm -1 for the α \alpha phase and E a E_{a} = 184 Nm -1 and E b E_{b} = 140 Nm -1 for the β \beta phase. Interestingly, the α \alpha - penta -NiPS showed nearly zero Poisson’s ratios along the in-plane direction, where its dimensions would be maintained when being extended. For electronic application, we demonstrated that penta -NiPS is the wide band gap semiconductors with an indirect band gap of 2.35 eV for α \alpha phase, and 2.20 eV for β \beta phase.

Computational Method

Our calculations are based on density functional theory (DFT) and the plane-wave basis projector augmented wave (PAW)
Blöchl 1994
method, as defined in the Vienna Ab-initio Simulation Package (VASP)
Kresse and Furthmüller 1996
;
Kresse and Furthmüller 1996
. The kinetic energy cutoff of 500 eV is used for the plane-wave expansion. Perdew-Burke-Ernzerhof (PBE)
Perdew et al. 1996
functional is imposed for geometry optimization. In order to accurately describe layered material, the long-range van der Waals (vdW) interactions were taken into account using the DFT-D2 functional proposed by Grimme.
Grimme 2006
The structures are optimized until total Hellmann-Feynman forces are decreased to 0.02 eV/Åand the energy convergence is set to
10^{-6}
eV. The centered k-point grid within
7\times 7\times 1
is employed for first Brillouin
zone integration within the Monkhorst-Pack
Monkhorst and Pack 1976
scheme. A vacuum layer of 16 Å is included along the direction perpendicular to a crystal layer to avoid the interaction between the layer and its neighboring image. A PHONOPY package
Togo and Tanaka 2015
over
3\times 3\times 1
supercell with 500 eV of energy cutoff and a single k-point at
\Gamma
is used to calculate phonon spectra to verify the dynamic stability. Ab initio molecular dynamics (AIMD) simulations is employed to confirm thermodynamic stability. To perform electronic band structure, since DFT within the standard approximation severely underestimates band gaps, we use the Heyd-Scuseria-Ernzerhof (HSE06)
Heyd et al. 2003
;
Heyd et al. 2006
hybrid functional to correct not only the band gap but also the band width.
