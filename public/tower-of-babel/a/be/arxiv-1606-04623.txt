K ¯ \bar{K} -induced formation of the f 2 ​ ( 1270 ) f_{2}(1270) and f 2 ′ ( 1525 ) f_{2}^{{}^{\prime}}(1525) resonances on proton targets
Yin Huang, Ju-jun Xie, Jun He, Xurong Chen, Hong-fei Zhang

Abstract
We investigate the productiong of f 2 ​ ( 1270 ) f_{2}(1270) and f 2 ′ ( 1525 ) f_{2}^{{}^{\prime}}(1525) mesons in the K − ​ p → Λ ​ f 2 ​ ( 1270 ) K^{-}p\to\Lambda{}f_{2}(1270) , K − p → Λ f 2 ′ ( 1525 ) K^{-}p\to\Lambda{}f^{{}^{\prime}}_{2}(1525) and K − ​ p → K + ​ K − ​ Λ K^{-}p\to{}K^{+}K^{-}\Lambda reactions within an effective Lagrangian approach. For K − ​ p → f 2 ​ Λ K^{-}p\to{}f_{2}\Lambda reaction, by considering the contributions from the t t -channel K + K^{+} exchange and u u -channel nucleon pole, we get a fairly good description of the experimental measurements about the total and differential cross sections. Based on the studies of the K − ​ p → f 2 ​ Λ K^{-}p\to{}f_{2}\Lambda reaction, we investigate K − ​ p → K + ​ K − ​ Λ K^{-}p\to{}K^{+}K^{-}\Lambda reaction including the contributions from the f 2 ​ ( 1270 ) f_{2}(1270) and f 2 ′ ​ ( 1525 ) f^{\prime}_{2}(1525) mesons decaying into K + ​ K − K^{+}K^{-} pair. The total cross sections and invariant mass distribution of the K ​ p → K + ​ K − ​ Λ Kp\to K^{+}K^{-}\Lambda reaction are predicted. The results can be tested in future experiments and therefore offer new clues on the nature of these tensor states.

INTRODUCTION

Since the charmonium-like resonance
XYZ
and the new baryonic

P_{c}

Aaij:2015tga
states are observed, a lot of experiments
spring up to study the exotic states from Belle, BaBar, BESIII,
LHCb, CDF, D0 and other collaborations. And people
believe that the traditional convention, the meson is made up
of quark and antiquark as well as baryon is made
up of three quarks, is broken. Furthermore, it is not surprising to find
out that many low-lying states, even those long believed to be
conventional
q\bar{q}
(or
qqq
) states, may have large components
of other nature. These observation has attracted a lot of attention
from the theoretical side. Various explanations of these states have
been proposed, such as molecules, muti-quark states,
kinematic effects, or mixtures of components of different
nature. Nevertheless, up to now none of them has
been accepted unanimously.

Indeed, it has been shown that many of the lowlying
mesonic states can be understood not only as
q\bar{q}
states but
also as meson-meson molecules, dynamically generated in
the so-called unitary approaches. For example,
f_{2}(1270),f_{2}^{{}^{\prime}}(1525)
and

are
listed in the Particle Data Group
K.A. Olive
as the lightest tensor
states and correspond to the

On the other hand, these states have been studied tensor
mesons in the unitary approach based on the hidden-gauge Lagrangians.
It was found that the

,
f^{\prime}(1525)
, and
K^{*}_{2}(1430)

generated from vector-meson-vector-meson interactions
Geng:2008gx
.
Within this picture, the branching ratios into pseudoscalar-pseudoscalar and
vector-vector final states are all consistent with data
Geng:2008gx
.
In Ref.
Nagahiro:2008um
, the two-photon decay widths of the

f_{0}(1370)

Furthermore, the ratios of the

into
\gamma{}f_{2}(1270)
and
\gamma{}f_{2}^{{}^{\prime}}(1525)

The agreement with experimental data turns out to be quite good in general,
providing support to the underlying assumption that these states contain large
meson-meson components.

Assuming that the

IV
SUMMARY

In this work, we perform a calculation of the
f_{2}
[
\equiv f_{2}(1270)

f_{2}^{{}^{\prime}}(1525)
] tenser meson production in the
K^{-}p\to{}f_{2}\Lambda
and

K^{-}p\to{}K^{-}K^{+}\Lambda
reaction within the effective
Lagrangian method. For the
K^{-}p\to{}f_{2}\Lambda
reaction,
by considering the contributions from the
t
-channel
K^{+}
exchange and

u
-channel nucleon pole, we get a fairly good description of the experimental
total cross section data. Our model shown the
differential cross section
d\sigma/dt
as function as
-t
,
and get a good description of the experimental differential cross section data.

Basing on our results of
K^{-}p\to{}f_{2}\Lambda
reaction,
we have studied the
K^{-}p\to{}K^{-}K^{+}\Lambda
reaction. In this case, we have
considered the contributions from only the
f_{2}(1270)
and
f^{{}^{\prime}}_{2}(1525)
mesons.
The invariant mass distribution for the Dalitz
process
K^{-}p\to{}K^{-}K^{+}\Lambda
shows an obvious peak at
M^{2}_{K^{+}K^{-}}\approx 1.63

GeV and
M^{2}_{K^{+}K^{-}}\approx 2.33
GeV, respectively, which can be checked by further
experiment.
