The Poincaré-Birkhof-Witt Theorem for Poisson algebras.
The (classical) Poincaré-Birkhof-Witt Theorem states that for each Lie algebra
{\cal G}
there is a natural isomorphism of graded algebras,

{\rm gr}\,U({\cal G})\simeq{\rm Sym}({\cal G}),

where
{\rm gr}\,U({\cal G})
is the associated graded algebra of the universal enveloping algebra
U({\cal G})
of the Lie algebra
{\cal G}
and
{\rm Sym}({\cal G})
is the symmetric algebra of
{\cal G}
. For a
smooth
Poisson algebra
{\cal P}
, a
similar result holds
[
17
, Theorem 3.1]
:

{\rm gr}\,{\cal U}({\cal P})\simeq{\rm Sym}_{\cal P}(\Omega_{\cal P}),

where
{\rm gr}\,{\cal U}({\cal P})
is the associated graded algebra of the Poisson enveloping algebra
{\cal U}({\cal P})
of the Poisson algebra
{\cal P}
and
{\rm Sym}_{\cal P}(\Omega_{\cal P})
is the symmetric algebra of the
{\cal P}
-module
\Omega_{\cal P}
of Kähler differentials of the associative algebra
{\cal P}
. In fact,
[
17
, Theorem 3.1]
holds in sightly more general situation, namely, for the universal enveloping algebra of the Lie-Reinhatr algebra. The pair
({\cal P},\Omega_{\cal P})
is an example of a Lie-Reinhart algebra and its universal enveloping algebra
V({\cal P},\Omega_{\cal P})
is isomorphic to the Poisson enveloping algebra (PEA)
{\cal U}({\cal P})
,
[
17
, Section 2, p.197]
(see also
[
6
]
).
Recently, it was proven that the PBW Theorem holds for certain
singular
Poisson hypersurfaces,
[
9
, Theorem 3.7]
.
One of the main results of this paper, Theorem
3.5
