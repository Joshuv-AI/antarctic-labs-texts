The m m -step Solvable Hom-Form of Birational Anabelian Geometry for Number Fields
Alberto Corato, Mohamed Saïdi

Abstract
In 1981, Uchida proved a conditional version of the Hom-form of the Grothendieck birational anabelian conjecture for number fields. In this paper we prove an m m -step solvable conditional version of the Grothendieck birational anabelian conjecture for number fields whereby our conditions are slightly weaker than the ones in Uchida’s theorem. Furthermore, as in Uchida’s work, we show that our result is unconditional in the case where the number field relating to the domain of the given homomorphism is ℚ \mathbb{Q} .

Introduction

For two algebraic number fields
K
and
L
, an isomorphism between their separable closure
\overline{K}
and
\overline{L}
induces an isomorphism between their absolute Galois groups
G_{K}=G(\overline{K}/K)
and
G_{L}=G(\overline{L}/L)
. A well known result by Neukirch and Uchida (
[
5
]
,
[
4
]
,
[
10
]
) shows that
G_{K}
determines the isomorphy type of
K
as follows:

Neukirch-Uchida
.

Let
\sigma:G_{K}\to G_{L}
be an isomorphism of profinite groups. Then, there exists a unique isomorphism of fields
\tau:\overline{K}\xrightarrow{\sim}\overline{L}
such that for all
g\in G_{K}
we have
\sigma(g)=\tau g\tau^{-1}
. Furthermore,
\tau
restricts to an isomorphism
K\xrightarrow{\sim}L
.

In
[
10
]
, Uchida also shows that in the above theorem the absolute Galois group can be replaced by its maximal pro-solvable quotient
G^{\sol}
.

A more recent result, by Saïdi and Tamagawa
[
8
]
, shows that in the statement of the Neukirch-Uchida theorem, one can replace
G_{K}
by its
m
-step maximal solvable quotient
G_{K}^{m}
, as follows:

Saïdi-Tamagawa
.

Let
m\geq 2
be an integer, and let
\sigma_{m+3}:G_{K}^{m+3}\to G_{L}^{m+3}
be an isomorphism of profinite groups. Consider the isomorphism of profinite groups
\sigma_{m}:G_{K}^{m}\to G_{L}^{m}
naturally induced by
\sigma_{m+3}
. Then, there is a unique isomorphism of fields
\tau_{m}:K_{m}\to L_{m}
such that for all
g\in G_{K}^{m}
we have
\sigma_{m}(g)=\tau_{m}g\tau_{m}^{-1}
. Furthermore,
\tau_{m}
restricts to an isomorphism
K\xrightarrow{\sim}L
.

Another noteworthy result by Uchida (
[
11
]
) shows that one can replace the isomorphisms in the Neukirch-Uchida theorem with homomorphisms. More precisely, one can obtain the following conditional result:

Conditional Hom-Form
.

Let
\sigma:G_{K}\to G_{L}
be a homomorphism of profinite groups, and assume that for each decomposition group at a finite prime
D_{\mathfrak{p}}\subset G_{K}
, there exists a decomposition group at a finite prime
D_{\mathfrak{q}}\subset G_{L}
such that
\sigma(D_{\mathfrak{p}})\subseteq D_{\mathfrak{q}}
and
\sigma(D_{\mathfrak{p}})
is open in
D_{\mathfrak{q}}
. Then,
\sigma(G_{K})
is open in
G_{L}
, and there exists a unique embedding of fields
\tau:\overline{L}\hookrightarrow\overline{K}
such that for all
g\in G_{K}
we have
\tau\sigma(g)=g\tau
. Furthermore,
\tau
restricts to an embedding
L\hookrightarrow K
.

Uchida also proved the following unconditional result:

Hom-Form over
\mathbb{Q}
.

Let
\sigma:G_{\mathbb{Q}}\to G_{L}
be a homomorphism of profinite groups such that
\sigma(G_{\mathbb{Q}})
is open in
G_{L}
. Then,
L=\mathbb{Q}
,
\sigma
is an isomorphism, and there exists an an automorphism
\tau:\overline{\mathbb{Q}}\to\overline{\mathbb{Q}}
such that
\tau\sigma(g)=g\tau
for all
g\in G_{\mathbb{Q}}^{m}
.

Uchida’s results provide a conditional version of the Hom-Form of the Birational Anabelian Geometry of Number Fields (
[
11
]
, Conjecture)

Hom-Form of the Birational Anabelian Geometry of Number Fields (Conjecture)
.

Let
\sigma:G_{K}\to G_{L}
be a homomorphism of profinite groups such that
\sigma(G_{K})
is open in
G_{L}
. Then, there exists a unique embedding of fields
\tau:\overline{L}\hookrightarrow\overline{K}
such that for all
g\in G_{K}
we have
\tau\sigma(g)=g\tau
. Furthermore,
\tau
restricts to an embedding
L\hookrightarrow K
.

Our goal in this paper is to refine Uchida’s results to a sharper version, analogous to the relation between Saïdi and Tamagawa’s results on the Isom-Form with respect to the Neukirch-Uchida theorem. To do this, we will use the theory established by Saïdi and Tamagawa in
[
8
]
, and prove such a version under some condition on a continuous homomorphism between
G_{K}^{m}
and
G_{L}^{m}
which can be detected group-theoretically.

We will use an object introduced by Saïdi and Tamagawa,
(\star_{l})
-subgroups (see Definition
1.4
), which consists of a generalisation of
l
-Sylow subgroups of decomposition groups which can be detected group-theoretically, but requiring an additional abelian “step” of information. We will show that, as long as our homomorphism satisfies some condition on these
(\star_{l})
-subgroups, we are able to construct a map between the primes of the fields
K
and
L
. By working on this map, we can show that it satisfies some inequality between the norm of primes.

With some additional result which we obtain from the map of primes, we are then able to show that a homomorphism
\sigma_{m}:G_{K}^{m}\to G_{L}^{m}
satisfying our desired condition induces an embedding of
L_{m}
in
K_{m}
compatible with
\sigma_{m}
.

To show this embedding of fields is unique, we will add another condition, which can also be expressed entirely in terms of group theory, to our homomorphism.

The following statement (see Theorem
6.1
for the proof) will be the main result of this paper:

Conditional
m
-step Hom-Form of the Birational Anabelian Geometry of Number Fields
.

Let
m\geq 2
be an integer, and
\sigma_{m+3}:G_{K}^{m+3}\to G_{L}^{m+3}
a homomorphism of profinite groups. Consider the homomorphism of profinite groups
\sigma_{m}:G_{K}^{m}\to G_{L}^{m}
induced by
\sigma_{m+3}
. Assume that
\sigma_{m}
restricts to an injection on every
(\star_{l})
-subgroup of
G_{K}^{m}
for every prime number
l
, and that
\sigma_{m+3}(G_{K}^{m+3})\supseteq G_{L}^{m+3}[m-1]
.

Then, there exists a unique embedding of fields
\tau:L_{m}\hookrightarrow K_{m}
such that for all
g\in G_{K}^{m}
we have
\tau\sigma_{m}(g)=g\tau
. Furthermore,
\tau
restricts to an embedding
L\hookrightarrow K
.

The condition that
(\star_{l})
-subgroups are mapped injectively is a sharper variation of the condition Uchida uses in his result (cf. Remark
6.4
).

We will then also give a variation on the Conditional
m
-step Hom-Form which follows immediately from the above but is a proper sharpening of Uchida’s result. In particular we will show that, for a possibly large integer
m
which is dependent on the fields
K
and
L
, the condition we require for uniqueness is automatically satisfied (see Theorem
6.2
).

The last result we prove in this paper is a study of a specific case of our previous result, namely we look at what happens when
K=\mathbb{Q}
and we have a homomorphism
\sigma_{m}:G_{\mathbb{Q}}^{m}\to G_{L}^{m}
where
m
is an integer
\geq 3
. We show that any homomorphism
\sigma_{m-2}:G_{\mathbb{Q}}^{m-2}\to G_{L}^{m-2}
induced from
\sigma_{m}
is an isomorphism and
L=\mathbb{Q}
. In particular, the condition on
l
-decomposition like group we requested in the main result holds. We then have the following unconditional result (see Theorem
7.6
):

m
-step Hom-Form over
\mathbb{Q}
.

Let
m\geq 2
be an integer, and
\sigma_{m+3}:G_{\mathbb{Q}}^{m+3}\to G_{L}^{m+3}
a homomorphism of profinite groups. Consider the homomorphism of profinite groups
\sigma_{m}:G_{\mathbb{Q}}^{m}\to G_{L}^{m}
induced by
\sigma_{m+3}
. Then,
L=\mathbb{Q}
,
\sigma_{m}
is an isomorphism, and there exists a unique automorphism
\tau:\mathbb{Q}_{(m)}\to\mathbb{Q}_{(m)}
such that, for all
g\in G_{\mathbb{Q}}^{m}
, we have
\sigma_{m}(g)=\tau g\tau^{-1}
.

Lemma 2.1

Proposition 4.4

Now, observe that

is a totally imaginary extension (and

),
s=[L(\zeta_{2^{n}}):L]/2+1=[K^{\prime\prime}:K]/2+1

Proposition 7.4
.

\sigma_{m}
has trivial kernel, and so it is an isomorphism

Proof.

Let us denote by
\Lambda
the subfield of
K_{m}
corresponding to the kernel of
\sigma_{m}
.
Let
L^{\prime}
be a finite Galois extension of
L
contained in
L_{m}
, and let
K^{\prime}
be the finite Galois extension of
K
contained in
\Lambda
corresponding to it by
\sigma_{m}
. Observe first that
K^{\prime}
and
L^{\prime}
have the same degree over
\mathbb{Q}
.

We may take an odd prime
p\in S
that splits completely in
L^{\prime}/L
. Observe that the image of
D_{p}
by the map
G_{K}^{m}\twoheadrightarrow G(K^{\prime}/K)
must be trivial by Proposition
7.3
, that is
p
splits completely in
K^{\prime}
. It then follows there is an embedding
K^{\prime}\hookrightarrow L^{\prime}
by Proposition 13.9 in
[
6
]
, however equality between degrees shows this is in fact an isomorphism, we may also construct the set
\mathfrak{A}_{L^{\prime}}
of such isomorphisms, which is finite.

The inverse limit over all finite Galois subextensions
L^{\prime}
of
L_{m}/L
of the family of sets
\mathfrak{A}_{L^{\prime}}
is non-empty as all the
\mathfrak{A}_{L^{\prime}}
are finite and non-empty. Then, this shows that there is an isomorphism
\alpha:\Lambda\xrightarrow{\sim}L_{m}
.

Assume then
\Lambda\subsetneq K_{m}
. Then,
K_{m}/\Lambda
is a non-trivial extension and

. Thus, we may define using

.
∎
