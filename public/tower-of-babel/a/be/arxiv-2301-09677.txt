Study of the generalized von mangoldt function defined by L-additive function
Es-said En-naoui

Abstract
The main object of this paper is to study the generalized von mangoldt function using the L-additive function, which can help us give many result about the classical arithmetic function.

Introduction

In this work, we address two major result: Dirichlet product of generalized von Mangoldt function with an arithmetic function
f
and dirichlet serie of many arithmetics function completely additive .

In this article, we study the generalized function von Mangoldt by the L-additive function to find alternative proof for many series expansions that depend on the arithmetic function additive and completely additive.
The methods readily generalize, and can be applied to other L-additive functions. Our principal result are that :

\sum\limits_{n\geq 1}\frac{\Lambda_{f}(n)}{n^{s}}=\sum\limits_{p}\frac{f(p)}{h_{f}(p)p^{s}-h_{f}(p)}

where
f
is L-additive function with
h_{f}
is nonzero-valued .

Definition 1.4

(L-additive function)

We say that an arithmetic function

The property (

For example, the arithmetic derivative

\delta(mn)=n\delta(m)+m\delta(n)

for all positive integers

is completely multiplicative.
Further, all completely additive functions

are L-additive with
h_{f}(n)=1

n
is completely additive since

{\rm ld}(mn)={\rm ld}(m)+{\rm ld}(n).

\Lambda_{f}(n)={\begin{cases}\frac{f(p)}{h_{f}(p)}&{\text{if }}n=p^{k}{\text{ for some prime }}p{\text{ and integer }}k\geq 1,\\
0&{\text{otherwise.}}\end{cases}}

(6)

then we have this result :

Theorem 2.1
.

If
n\geq 1
then we have :

f(n)=h_{f}(n)\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}d|n\end{subarray}}\Lambda_{f}(d)

That mean by using dirichlet convolution :
f=h_{f}\ast h_{f}\Lambda_{f}

Proof.

If
n=p_{1}^{\alpha_{1}}\ldots p_{s}^{\alpha_{s}}
then we have :

\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}d|n\end{subarray}}\Lambda_{f}(d)=\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}i=1\end{subarray}}^{s}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}k=1\end{subarray}}^{i}\Lambda_{f}(p_{i}^{k})=\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}i=1\end{subarray}}^{s}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}k=1\end{subarray}}^{i}\frac{f(p_{i})}{h_{f}(p_{i})}=\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}i=1\end{subarray}}^{s}\frac{if(p_{i})}{h_{f}(p_{i})}=\frac{f(n)}{h_{f}(n)}

as claimed

∎

Theorem 2.2
.

for every positive integer
n
we have :

{\displaystyle\Lambda_{f}(n)=\sum_{d\mid n}\frac{\mu\left(\frac{n}{d}\right)f\left(d\right)}{h_{f}\left(d\right)}}=-\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}d|n\end{subarray}}\frac{\mu(d)f(d)}{h_{f}(d)}

That is we have :

\Lambda_{f}=\mu\ast\frac{f}{h_{f}}=-1\ast\frac{\mu f}{h_{f}}

Proof.

By Theorem (
2.1
) applying Mobius inversion, we have
\Lambda_{f}=\mu\ast\frac{f}{h_{f}}
, Note that :

\displaystyle\sum_{d\mid n}\frac{\mu\left(\frac{n}{d}\right)f\left(d\right)}{h_{f}\left(d\right)}

\displaystyle=\sum_{d\mid n}\mu(d)\frac{f\left(\frac{n}{d}\right)}{h_{f}\left(\frac{n}{d}\right)}=\sum\limits_{d|n}\mu(d)\frac{h_{f}(d)}{h_{f}(n)}\bigg(\frac{h_{f}(d)f(n)-h_{f}(n)f(d)}{h_{f}^{2}(d)}\bigg)

\displaystyle=\sum\limits_{d|n}\frac{\mu(d)h^{2}_{f}(d)f(n)}{h_{f}(n)h^{2}_{f}(d)}-\frac{\mu(d)h_{f}(n)h_{f}(d)f(d)}{h_{f}(n)h^{2}_{f}(d)}

\displaystyle=\frac{f(n)}{h_{f}(n)}\sum\limits_{d|n}\mu(d)-\sum\limits_{d|n}\frac{\mu(d)f(d)}{h_{f}(d)}

\displaystyle=\frac{f(n)e(n)}{h_{f}(n)}-\sum\limits_{d|n}\frac{\mu(d)f(d)}{h_{f}(d)}

\displaystyle=-\sum\limits_{d|n}\frac{\mu(d)f(d)}{h_{f}(d)}

Hence
\Lambda_{f}(n)=-1\ast\frac{\mu f}{h_{f}}
. This completes the proof .
∎

Theorem 2.4

Theorem 2.7

\displaystyle=\sum\limits_{d|n}g(d)\frac{f(d)}{h_{f}(d)}h\left(\frac{n}{d}\right)+\sum\limits_{d|n}g(d)h\left(\frac{n}{d}\right)\frac{f(\frac{n}{d})}{h_{f}(\frac{n}{d})}

4
the generalized von mangoldt functions in terms of the Dirichlet serie

Above we have seen that many fundamental properties of the generalized von mangoldt function. We complete this article by changing our point of view slightly and demonstrate that generalized von mangoldt function can also be studied in terms of the Dirichlet series .

Dirichlet product defined in (
4
) occurs naturally in the study of Dirichlet series such as the Riemann zeta function. It describes the multiplication of two Dirichlet series in terms of their coefficients:

\bigg(\sum\limits_{n\geq 1}\frac{\big(f*g\big)(n)}{n^{s}}\bigg)=\bigg(\sum\limits_{n\geq 1}\frac{f(n)}{n^{s}}\bigg)\bigg(\sum\limits_{n\geq 1}\frac{g(n)}{n^{s}}\bigg)

(41)

with Riemann zeta function or is defined by :

\zeta(s)=\sum\limits_{n\geq 1}\frac{1}{n^{s}}

These functions are widely studied in the literature (see, e.g.,
[
2
,
3
,
4
]
).

For later convenience we introduce the prime zeta function, described in Froberg (1968) (see, e.g.,
[
6
]
), denoted by
P(s)

P(s)=\sum\limits_{p}\frac{1}{p^{s}}

In the next of this section we will use this notation :
