Essential norm of composition operators harmonic Bloch spaces
Y. Estaremi, S. Esmaeili and A. Ebadian

Abstract
. In this paper we characterize essential norm of composition operators on the spaces of Harmonic Bloch functions. These results extends the similar results that were proven for composition operators on Bloch spaces.

1.
Introduction

The essential norm
\|T\|_{e}
of a continuous linear operator
T
between Banach spaces
X
and
Y
is defined as the distance from
T
to the space of compact operators from
X
to
Y
.
The essential norm of composition operators have been studied on analytic function spaces in
[
13
,
12
,
5
,
10
,
11
,
3
,
9
,
4
]
.

Let
D
be the open unit disk in the complex plane. Let
\varphi
be an analytic self-map of
D
, i. e., an analytic function
\varphi
in
D
such that
\varphi(D)\subset D
. The composition operator
C_{\varphi}
induced by such
\varphi
is the linear map on the spaces of all harmonic functions on the unit disk defined by

C_{\varphi}f=fo\varphi.

The main goal of this paper is to compute the essential norm of
C_{\varphi}
in terms of an asymptotic bound involving the quantity

\frac{(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}}{(1-|\varphi(z)|^{2})^{\alpha}}|\varphi^{{}^{\prime}}(z)|.

Further, we obtain an other essential norm formula for composition operators on
HB(\alpha)
for any
0<\alpha<\infty
in terms of
\varphi^{n}
, where,
\varphi^{n}
means the
n
-th power of
\varphi
.

Let
D
be the open unit disk in the complex plane. For a continuously differentiable complex-valued
f(z)=u(z)+i\upsilon(z),

z=x+iy,
we use the common notation for its formal derivatives:

f_{z}=\frac{1}{2}(f_{x}-if_{y}),

f_{\bar{z}}=\frac{1}{2}(f_{x}+if_{y}).

A twice continuously differentiable complex-valued function
f=u+i\upsilon
on
D
is called a harmonic function if and only if the real-valued function
u
and
\upsilon
satisfy Laplace’s equation
\Delta u=\Delta\upsilon=0
.

A direct calculation shows that the Laplacian of
f
is

\Delta f=4f_{z\bar{z}}.

Thus for functions
f
with continuous second partial derivatives, it is clear that
f
is harmonic if ana only if
\Delta f=0.

We consider complex-valued harmonic function
f
defined in a simply connected domain
D\subset C.
The function
f
has a canonical decomposition
f=h+\bar{g},
where
h
and
g
are analytic in
D

[
6
]
.
A planar complex-valued harmonic function
f
in
D
is called a harmonic Bloch function if and only if

\beta_{f}=\sup_{z,w\in D,z\neq w}\frac{|f(z)-f(w)|}{\varrho(z,w)}<\infty.

Here
\beta_{f}
is the Lipschitz number of
f
and

\displaystyle\varrho(z,w)=\arctan h|\frac{z-w}{1-\bar{z}w}|,

denotes the hyperbolic distance between
z
and
w
in
D
, where here
\rho(z,w)
is the pseudo-hyperbolic distance on
D
.
In
[
2
]
Colonna proved that

\displaystyle\beta_{f}=\sup_{z\in D}(1-|z|^{2})[|f_{z}(z)|+|f_{\bar{z}}(z)|].

Moreover, the set of all harmonic Bloch mappings, denoted by the symbol
HB(1)
or
HB
, forms a complex Banach space with the norm
\|.\|
given by

\|f\|_{HB(1)}=|f(0)|+\sup_{z\in D}(1-|z|^{2})[|f_{z}(z)|+|f_{\bar{z}}(z)|].

Now we define the Harmonic
\alpha
-Bloch space
HB(\alpha)
.

Definition 1.1
.

For
\alpha\in(0,\infty)
, the Harmonic
\alpha
-Bloch space
HB(\alpha)
consists of complex-valued harmonic function
f
defined on
D
such that

|||f|||_{HB(\alpha)}=\sup_{z\in D}(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}[|f_{z}(z)|+|f_{\bar{z}}(z)|]<\infty,

and the harmonic little
\alpha
-Bloch space
HB_{0}(\alpha)
consists of all function in
HB(\alpha)
such that

\lim_{|z|\rightarrow 1}(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}[|f_{z}(z)|+|f_{\bar{z}}(z)|]=0.

Obviously, when
\alpha=1
, we have
|||f|||_{HB(\alpha)}=\beta_{f}
. Each
HB(\alpha)
is a Banach space with the norm given by

\displaystyle\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}

\displaystyle=|f(0)|+\sup_{z\in D}(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}[|f_{z}(z)|+|f_{\bar{z}}(z)|],

and
HB_{0}(\alpha)
is a closed subspace of
HB(\alpha)
.

2.
Main results

In this section we characterize essential norm of composition operator
C_{\varphi}
on
HB(\alpha)
.

Here we recall the next lemma from
[
14
]
that we need it in the sequel.

Lemma 2.1
.

[
14
]
If
\alpha>0
,
n\in N
,
0<x<1
and

H_{n,\alpha}(x)=x^{n-1}(1-x^{2})^{\alpha},

then
H_{n,\alpha}
has the following properties:

a)

\max_{0\leq x\leq 1}H_{n,\alpha}(x)=

H_{n,\alpha}(r_{n})=\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}1,&\hbox{n=1;}\\
$$\par$$(\frac{2\alpha}{n-1+2\alpha})^{\alpha}$$$$(\frac{n-1}{n-1+2\alpha})^{\frac{(n-1)}{2}},$$$$&n\geq 2.$$\par$$\end{array}\right.

In which

r_{n}=\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}0,&\hbox{n=1;}\\
$$\par$$(\frac{n-1}{n-1+2\alpha})^{\frac{1}{2}},$$$$&n\geq 2.$$\par$$\end{array}\right.

b) For
n\geq 1,H_{n,\alpha}
is increasing on
[0,r_{n}]
and decreasing on
[r_{n},1]
.

c) For
n\geq 1,H_{n,\alpha}
is decreasing on
[r_{n},r_{n+1}],
and so

\min_{x\in[r_{n},r_{n+1}]}H_{n,\alpha}(x)=H_{n,\alpha}(r_{n+1})=(\frac{2\alpha}{n+2\alpha})^{\alpha}(\frac{n}{n+2\alpha})^{\frac{(n-1)}{2}}.

Consequently,

\lim_{n\rightarrow\alpha}n^{\alpha}\min_{x\in[r_{n},r_{n+1}]}H_{n,\alpha}(x)=(\frac{2\alpha}{e})^{\alpha}.

By using the Lemma
2.1
we can find a lower bound for the essential norm of
C_{\varphi}:HB(\alpha)\rightarrow HB(\alpha)
.

Theorem 2.2
.

Let
0<\alpha<\infty
,
\varphi
be an analytic self-map of the unit disk
D
and
C_{\varphi}
be a bounded operator from
HB(\alpha)
into
HB(\alpha)
. Then

\|C_{\varphi}\|_{e}\geq\lim_{s\rightarrow 1}\sup_{|\varphi(z)|>s}\frac{(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}}{(1-|\varphi(z)|^{2})^{\alpha}}|\varphi^{{}^{\prime}}(z)|.

Proof.

Let
n\in\mathbb{N}
and consider the function
z^{n}+\bar{z}^{n}
. By the Lemma
2.1
we have

\displaystyle\|z^{n}+\bar{z}^{n}\|_{HB(\alpha)}

\displaystyle=\max_{z\in D}2n|z|^{n-1}(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}

\displaystyle=2n(\frac{2\alpha}{n-1+2\alpha})^{\alpha}(\frac{n-1}{n-1+2\alpha})^{\frac{(n-1)}{2}},

in which the maximum is attained at any point on the circle with radius
r_{n}=(\frac{n-1}{n-1+2\alpha})^{\frac{(n-1)}{2}}
.

Let
f_{n}=\frac{z^{n}+\bar{z}^{n}}{\|z^{n}+\bar{z}^{n}\|_{HB(\alpha)}}
. Then
f_{n}
converges to
0
weakly in
HB(\alpha)
. In particular, if
K
is any compact operator on
HB(\alpha)
, then
\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}\|Kf_{n}\|_{HB(\alpha)}=0
. For

D_{n}=\{z\in D:r_{n}\leq|\varphi(z)|\leq r_{n+1}\},

we have

\displaystyle\min_{z\in D_{n}}(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}[|(f_{n})_{z}(z)|+|(f_{n})_{\bar{z}}(z)|]

\displaystyle=(1-|r_{n+1}|^{2})^{\alpha}[|h_{n}^{\prime}(z)|+|g_{n}^{\prime}(z)|]

\displaystyle=(\frac{n-1+2\alpha}{n+2\alpha})^{\alpha}(\frac{n^{2}(2\alpha-1)n}{n^{2}(2\alpha-1)n-2\alpha}).

It is easy to see that this minimum tends to
1
when
n\rightarrow\infty
. Also for each
n\geq 2
the minimum is attained at any point of the circle centered at the origin and of radius
r_{n+1}
. For any compact operator
K
on
HB(\alpha)
, we get that

\displaystyle\|C_{\varphi}-K\|

\displaystyle\geq\limsup_{n\rightarrow\infty}\|(C_{\varphi}-K)f_{n}\|_{HB(\alpha)}

\displaystyle\geq\limsup_{n\rightarrow\infty}(\|(C_{\varphi})f_{n}\|_{HB(\alpha)}-\|(Kf_{n}\|_{HB(\alpha)})

\displaystyle=\limsup_{n\rightarrow\infty}\|(C_{\varphi})f_{n}\|_{HB(\alpha)}.

Thus we obtain the followings:

\displaystyle\|C_{\varphi}\|_{e}

\displaystyle=\inf_{K}\|C_{\varphi}-K\|

\displaystyle\geq\limsup_{n\rightarrow\infty}\|(C_{\varphi})f_{n}\|_{HB(\alpha)}

\displaystyle=\limsup_{n\rightarrow\infty}\sup_{z\in D}\frac{(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}}{(1-|\varphi(z)|^{2})^{\alpha}}|\varphi^{{}^{\prime}}(z)|(1-|\varphi(z)|^{2})^{\alpha}[|h_{n}^{{}^{\prime}}(\varphi(z))|+|g_{n}^{{}^{\prime}}(\varphi(z))|]

\displaystyle\geq\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}\sup_{\varphi(z)\in D_{n}}\frac{(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}}{(1-|\varphi(z)|^{2})^{\alpha}}|\varphi^{{}^{\prime}}(z)|\min_{\varphi(z)\in D_{n}}(1-|\varphi(z)|^{2})^{\alpha}[|h_{n}^{{}^{\prime}}(\varphi(z))|+|g_{n}^{{}^{\prime}}(\varphi(z))|]

\displaystyle\geq\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}\sup_{\varphi(z)\in D_{n}}\frac{(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}}{(1-|\varphi(z)|^{2})^{\alpha}}|\varphi^{\prime}(z)|\min_{w\in D_{n}}(1-|w|^{2})^{\alpha}[|h_{n}^{{}^{\prime}}(w)|+|g_{n}^{{}^{\prime}}(w)|],

Since

\limsup_{n\rightarrow\infty}\min_{w\in D_{n}}(1-|w|^{2})^{\alpha}[|h_{n}^{{}^{\prime}}(w)|+|g_{n}^{{}^{\prime}}(w)|]=1,

then we conclude that

\|C_{\varphi}\|_{e}\geq\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}\sup_{\varphi(z)\in D_{n}}\frac{(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}}{(1-|\varphi(z)|^{2})^{\alpha}}|\varphi^{{}^{\prime}}(z)|.

∎

We need some Lemmas to obtain some upper bounds for the essential norm of
C_{\varphi}
. For
r\in(0,1)
, let
K_{r}f(z)=f(rz)
. Then by Theorems 2. 6 and 2.7 of
[
7
]
we get that
K_{r}
is a compact operator on the spaces
HB(\alpha)
and
HB_{0}(\alpha)
for any positive number
\alpha
, with
\|K_{r}\|\leq 1
.

Lemma 2.3
.

For
0<\alpha<1
, there exists a sequence
\{r_{k}\}
,
0<r_{k}<1
, tending to
1
, such that the compact operator
L_{n}=(\frac{1}{n})\sum_{k=1}^{n}K_{r_{k}}
on
HB_{0}(\alpha)
satisfies:

a) For every
t\in[0,1)
we have

\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}\sup_{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\leq 1}\sup_{|z|\leq t}[|((I-L_{n})f)_{z}(z)|+|((I-L_{n})f)_{\bar{z}}(z)|]=0.

b)

\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}\sup_{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\leq 1}\sup_{z\in D}|(I-L_{n})f(z)|=0.

c)

\limsup_{n\rightarrow\infty}\|I-L_{n}\|\leq 1.

Proof.

In order to prove the Lemma we need to prove that, for any
t
,
0<t<1
, and any
\varepsilon>0
, there is an
n>0
such that, for any
n>N,

\sup_{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\leq 1}\sup_{|z|\leq t}[|((I-L_{n})f)_{z}(z)|+|((I-L_{n})f)_{\bar{z}}(z)|]<\varepsilon,

\sup_{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\leq 1}\sup_{z\in D}|(I-L_{n})f(z)|<\varepsilon,

and

\|I-L_{n}\|\leq 1+2\varepsilon.

Let
S_{HB_{0}(\alpha)}
denote the unit ball of
HB_{0}(\alpha)
and the positive number
s_{1}
such that
t<s_{1}<1
. Since
S_{HB_{0}(\alpha)}
is a relatively compact subset with respect to the topology
\tau
of the uniform convergence on compact subsets of the unit disk and
(I-K_{r})f
tends to
0
with respect to
\tau
, then
((I-K_{r})f)^{{}^{\prime}}
tends to
0
. And so there is
0<r_{1}<1
such that

\displaystyle\sup_{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\leq 1}\sup_{|z|\leq s_{1}}(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}[|((I-K_{r_{1}})f)_{z}(z)|+|((I-K_{r_{1}})f)_{\bar{z}}(z)|]

\displaystyle<\min\{\varepsilon,\varepsilon(1-|s_{1}|^{2})^{\alpha}\}.

Since
K_{r_{1}}
is compact, then
K_{r_{1}}S_{HB_{0}(\alpha)}
is relatively compact and we can find
s_{2}>s_{1}
such that

\sup_{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\leq 1}\sup_{|z|>s_{2}}(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}[|(K_{r_{1}}f)_{z}(z)|+|(K_{r_{1}}f)_{\bar{z}}(z)|]<\varepsilon.

And so we get that

\sup_{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\leq 1}\sup_{|z|>s_{2}}(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}[|((I-K_{r_{1}})f)_{z}(z)|+|((I-K_{r_{1}})f)_{\bar{z}}(z)|]<1+\varepsilon.

By repeating this method we can find the sequences
\{s_{k}\}
and
\{r_{k}\}
satisfying

\displaystyle\sup_{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\leq 1}\sup_{|z|\leq s_{k}}(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}[|((I-K_{r_{k}})f)_{z}(z)|+|((I-K_{r_{k}})f)_{\bar{z}}(z)|]

\displaystyle<\min\{\varepsilon,\varepsilon(1-|s_{1}|^{2})^{\alpha}\}

and

\sup_{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\leq 1}\sup_{|z|>s_{k+1}}(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}[|((I-K_{r_{k}})f)_{z}(z)|+|((I-K_{r_{k}})f)_{\bar{z}}(z)|]<1+\varepsilon.

If we take
n>\frac{1}{\varepsilon}
and set
L_{n}=(\frac{1}{n})\sum_{k=1}^{n}K_{r_{k}}
, the by using the fact that
(1-|s_{1}|^{2})^{\alpha}\}\leq(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}
for
|z|\leq s_{1}
, we have

\displaystyle\sup_{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\leq 1}\sup_{|z|\leq t}[|((I-L_{n})f)_{z}(z)|+|((I-L_{n})f)_{\bar{z}}(z)|]

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{n}\sum_{k=1}^{n}\sup_{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\leq 1}\sup_{|z|\leq s_{1}}[|((I-K_{r_{k}})f)_{z}(z)|+|((I-K_{r_{k}})f)_{\bar{z}}(z)|]

\displaystyle<\varepsilon.

This gives (a). Now we prove (c). We know that for
n\geq 1
and
0<\alpha<1
,

\displaystyle\|I-L_{n}\|

\displaystyle=\sup_{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\leq 1}\|(I-L_{n})f\|_{HB(\alpha)}

\displaystyle=\sup_{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\leq 1}\sup_{|z|\leq t}(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}[|((I-L_{n})f)_{z}(z)|+|((I-L_{n})f)_{\bar{z}}(z)|]

\displaystyle+\sup_{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\leq 1}\sup_{|z|>t}(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}[|((I-L_{n})f)_{z}(z)|+|((I-L_{n})f)_{\bar{z}}(z)|]

\displaystyle=I_{1}+I_{2}.

In which

I_{1}=\sup_{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\leq 1}\sup_{|z|\leq t}(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}[|((I-L_{n})f)_{z}(z)|+|((I-L_{n})f)_{\bar{z}}(z)|]<\varepsilon

and

I_{2}=\sup_{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\leq 1}\sup_{s_{l}<|z|<s_{l+1},k\neq l}(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}[|((I-L_{n})f)_{z}(z)|+|((I-L_{n})f)_{\bar{z}}(z)|]+

\sup_{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\leq 1}\sup_{|z|>s_{k+1}}(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}[|((I-L_{n})f)_{z}(z)|+|((I-L_{n})f)_{\bar{z}}(z)|].

For
k=l
,
s_{l}<|z|<s_{l+1}
and
\|f\|\leq 1
, we have

(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}[|((I-k_{r_{l}})f)_{z}(z)|+|((I-k_{r_{l}})f)_{\bar{z}}(z)|]\leq\|I-k_{r_{l}}\|\leq\|I\|+\|k_{r_{l}}\|\leq 2,

except possibly for
k\neq l
. However, in this case we get that

(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}[|((I-k_{r_{l}})f)_{z}(z)|+|((I-k_{r_{l}})f)_{\bar{z}}(z)|]\leq 1+\varepsilon

and so

\displaystyle(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}[|((I-L_{n})f)_{z}(z)|+|((I-L_{n})f)_{\bar{z}}(z)|]

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{n}\sum_{k\neq l}(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}[|((I-k_{r_{k}})f)_{z}(z)|+|((I-k_{r_{k}})f)_{\bar{z}}(z)|]

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{n}(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}[|((I-k_{r_{l}})f)_{z}(z)|+|((I-k_{r_{l}})f)_{\bar{z}}(z)|]

\displaystyle<\frac{n-1}{n}(1+\varepsilon)+\frac{2}{n}

\displaystyle<1+2\varepsilon.

Therefore we get (c). The proof of the property (b) is exactly as in the proof of the Lemma 1 of
[
11
]
.

Also for the case

Lemma 2.5
.

For
\alpha>1
, there is a sequence
\{r_{k}\}
with
0<r_{k}<1
, tending to
1
. Also, the compact operator
L_{n}=(\frac{1}{n})\sum_{k=1}^{n}K_{r_{k}}
on

has the followings properties:

a) For any
s\in[0,1)
,

\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}\sup_{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\leq 1}\sup_{|z|\leq s}[|((I-L_{n})f)_{z}(z)|+|((I-L_{n})f)_{\bar{z}}(z)|]=0.

b) For any
t\in[0,1)
,

\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}\sup_{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\leq 1}\sup_{|z|\leq t}|(I-L_{n})f(z)|=0.

c)
\limsup_{n\rightarrow\infty}\|I-L_{n}\|\leq 1

Proof.

The proof of (a) and (c) is the same as the proof of the Lemma
2.3
. Hence we prove (b). For harmonic function
f=h+\bar{g}
we have

\displaystyle|(I-L_{n})f(z)|

\displaystyle\leq|(I-L_{n})h(z)|+|(I-L_{n})g(z)|

\displaystyle\leq\int_{D}\frac{(1-|w|^{2})^{1+\alpha}}{|w||1-\bar{z}w|^{2+\alpha}}[|((I-L_{n})h)^{{}^{\prime}}(z)|+|((I-L_{n})g)^{{}^{\prime}}(z)|]dA(w)

\displaystyle=\int_{D}\frac{(1-|w|^{2})^{1+\alpha}}{|w||1-\bar{z}w|^{2+\alpha}}[|((I-L_{n})f)_{w}(z)|+|((I-L_{n})f)_{w}(z)|]dA(w).

For each
t\in[0,1)
and any
\varepsilon>0
we can find
s\in[0,1)
sufficiently close to
1
such that

\frac{1-s}{s(1-t)^{2+\alpha}}<\varepsilon.

Hence we get that

\displaystyle I_{1}

\displaystyle=\sup_{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\leq 1}\sup_{|z|\leq t}\int_{D\setminus D_{s}}\frac{(1-|w|^{2})^{1+\alpha}}{|w||1-\bar{z}w|^{2+\alpha}}[|((I-L_{n})f)_{w}(z)|+|((I-L_{n})f)_{w}(z)|]dA(w)

\displaystyle\leq\sup_{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\leq 1}\|(I-L_{n})f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\sup_{|z|\leq t}|\int_{D\setminus D_{s}}\frac{(1-|w|^{2})^{1+\alpha}}{|w||1-\bar{z}w|^{2+\alpha}}dA(w)

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{s(1-t)^{2+\alpha}}(1-s)<\varepsilon,

when
s
is sufficiently close to
1
. By (a) we can find
N>0
such that for all
n>N
,

\sup_{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\leq 1}\sup_{|z|\leq s}[|((I-L_{n})f)_{z}(z)|+|((I-L_{n})f)_{\bar{z}}(z)|]<\varepsilon,

and so we conclude that

\displaystyle I_{2}

\displaystyle=\sup_{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\leq 1}\sup_{|z|\leq t}\int_{D_{s}}\frac{(1-|w|^{2})^{1+\alpha}}{|w||1-\bar{z}w|^{2+\alpha}}[|((I-L_{n})f)_{w}(z)|+|((I-L_{n})f)_{w}(z)|]dA(w)

for some

. Therefore by combining the above inequalities, we get that for

,

\displaystyle\sup_{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\leq 1}\sup_{|z|\leq t}|(I-L_{n})f(z)|

\displaystyle\leq\sup_{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\leq 1}\sup_{|z|\leq t}\int_{D}\frac{(1-|w|^{2})^{1+\alpha}}{|w||1-\bar{z}w|^{2+\alpha}}[|((I-L_{n})f)_{w}(z)|+|((I-L_{n})f)_{w}(z)|]dA(w)

\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}\sup_{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\leq 1}\sup_{|z|\leq t}|(I-L_{n})f(z)|=0.

Now we can find an upper bound for the essential norm of
C_{\varphi}:HB(\alpha)\rightarrow HB(\alpha)
.

Theorem 2.6
.

Let
\alpha>0
and
C_{\varphi}:HB(\alpha)\rightarrow HB(\alpha)
be bounded. Then we have

\|C_{\varphi}\|_{e}\leq\lim_{s\rightarrow 1}\sup_{|\varphi(z)|>s}\frac{(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}}{(1-|\varphi(z)|^{2})^{\alpha}}|\varphi^{{}^{\prime}}(z)|.

Proof.

Let
L_{n}
be a sequence of operators given in Lemma
2.5
. Since each
L_{n}
is a compact operator from
HB(\alpha)
to
HB(\alpha)
, then
C_{\varphi}L_{n}
is compact and we have

\displaystyle\|C_{\varphi}\|_{e}

\displaystyle\leq\|C_{\varphi}-C_{\varphi}L_{n}\|

\displaystyle=\|C_{\varphi}(I-L_{n})\|

\displaystyle=\sup_{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\leq 1}\|C_{\varphi}(I-L_{n})f\|_{HB(\alpha)}

\displaystyle\leq\sup_{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\leq 1}|(I-L_{n})f(\varphi(0))|

\displaystyle+\sup_{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\leq 1}\sup_{z\in D}(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}|\varphi^{{}^{\prime}}(z)|[|((I-L_{n})f)_{z}(\varphi(z))|+[|((I-L_{n})f)_{\bar{z}}(\varphi(z))|].

By the Lemma
2.5
we can suppose the term
\sup_{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\leq 1}|(I-L_{n})f(\varphi(0))|
sufficiently small for some large enough
n
.
Now we need only consider the term

\sup_{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\leq 1}\sup_{z\in D}(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}|\varphi^{{}^{\prime}}(z)|[|((I-L_{n})f)_{z}(\varphi(z))|+[|((I-L_{n})f)_{\bar{z}}(\varphi(z))|].

For every
0<s<1
, we bound this expression from above by

\displaystyle\sup_{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\leq 1}\sup_{|\varphi(z)|\leq s}(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}|\varphi^{{}^{\prime}}(z)|[|((I-L_{n})f)_{z}(\varphi(z))|+[|((I-L_{n})f)_{\bar{z}}(\varphi(z))|]

\displaystyle+\sup_{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\leq 1}\sup_{|\varphi(z)|>s}(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}|\varphi^{{}^{\prime}}(z)|[|((I-L_{n})f)_{z}(\varphi(z))|+[|((I-L_{n})f)_{\bar{z}}(\varphi(z))|].

Since
C_{\varphi}
is bounded from
HB(\alpha)
into
HB(\alpha)
, then we conclude that

(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}|\varphi^{{}^{\prime}}(z)|\leq(1-|\varphi(z)|^{2})^{\alpha}\sup_{z\in D}(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}|\varphi^{{}^{\prime}}(z)|<\infty,

and so

\sup_{|\varphi(z)|\leq s}(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}|\varphi^{{}^{\prime}}(z)|<\infty.

Hence by the Lemma
2.5
we see that

\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}\sup_{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\leq 1}\sup_{|\varphi(z)|\leq s}(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}|\varphi^{{}^{\prime}}(z)|[|((I-L_{n})f)_{z}(\varphi(z))|+[|((I-L_{n})f)_{\bar{z}}(\varphi(z))|]=0.

Easily we find that the term

\sup_{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\leq 1}\sup_{|\varphi(z)|>s}\frac{(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}|\varphi^{{}^{\prime}}(z)|}{(1-|\varphi(z)|^{2})^{\alpha}}(1-|\varphi(z)|^{2})^{\alpha}[|((I-L_{n})f)_{z}(\varphi(z))|+[|((I-L_{n})f)_{\bar{z}}(\varphi(z))|]

is bounded above by

\displaystyle\sup_{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\leq 1}\|(I-L_{n})f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\sup_{|\varphi(z)|>s}\frac{(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}|\varphi^{{}^{\prime}}(z)|}{(1-|\varphi(z)|^{2})^{\alpha}}

\displaystyle=\|I-L_{n}\|\sup_{|\varphi(z)|>s}\frac{(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}}{(1-|\varphi(z)|^{2})^{\alpha}}|\varphi^{{}^{\prime}}(z)|.

Therefore we have

\displaystyle\limsup_{n\rightarrow\infty}\sup_{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\leq 1}\sup_{|\varphi(z)|>s}(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}|\varphi^{{}^{\prime}}(z)|[|((I-L_{n})f)_{z}(\varphi(z))|+[|((I-L_{n})f)_{\bar{z}}(\varphi(z))|]

\displaystyle\leq\sup_{|\varphi(z)|>s}\frac{(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}}{(1-|\varphi(z)|^{2})^{\alpha}}|\varphi^{{}^{\prime}}(z)|,

and so we get that

\|C_{\varphi}\|_{e}\leq\sup_{|\varphi(z)|>s}\frac{(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}}{(1-|\varphi(z)|^{2})^{\alpha}}|\varphi^{{}^{\prime}}(z)|.

Consequently by taking the limit as
s\rightarrow 1
, we get the result.
∎

Recall that the composition operator
C_{\varphi}:HB(\alpha)\rightarrow HB(\alpha)
is said to be bounded below, if there exists
\varepsilon>0
such that

|||C_{\varphi}f|||_{HB(\alpha)}\geq\varepsilon|||f|||_{HB(\alpha)},

for any
f\in HB(\alpha)
. In the next theorem we characterize boundedness of below of
C_{\varphi}
on
HB(\alpha)
.

Theorem 2.8
.

The composition operator
C_{\varphi}
is bounded below on
HB(\alpha)
if and only if there exists
\delta>0
such that
\|C_{\varphi}(f)\|_{HB(\alpha)}\geq\delta\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}
for
f\in HB(\alpha)
.

Proof.

First we assume that
C_{\varphi}
is bounded below on
HB(\alpha)
, so there exists a
\delta>0
such that
|||go\varphi|||_{HB(\alpha)}\geq\delta|||g|||_{HB(\alpha)}
for
g\in HB(\alpha)
. Let
g(z)=f(z)-f(\varphi(0))
for
f\in HB(\alpha)
. Hence
g\in HB(\alpha)
,
g(\varphi(0))=0
,
\|g\|_{HB(\alpha)}=\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}
and
\|go\varphi\|_{HB(\alpha)}=\|fo\varphi\|_{HB(\alpha)}
. Therefore

\displaystyle\|C_{\varphi}(f)\|_{HB(\alpha)}

\displaystyle=\|fo\varphi\|_{HB(\alpha)}

\displaystyle=|||go\varphi|||_{HB(\alpha)}

\displaystyle\geq\delta\|g\|_{HB(\alpha)}=\delta\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}.

Conversely, suppose that there exists
\delta>0
such that
\|C_{\varphi}(f)\|_{HB(\alpha)}\geq\delta\|f\|_{HB(\alpha}
for all
f\in HB(\alpha)
. Then for every
f\in HB(\alpha)
, we have

\displaystyle|f(0)|

\displaystyle\leq|f(\varphi(0))|+\int_{o}^{\varphi(0)}[|(f)_{z}(z)|+|(f)_{\bar{z}}(z)|]|dz|

\displaystyle=|f(\varphi(0))|+\int_{o}^{\varphi(0)}(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}[|(f)_{z}(z)|+|(f)_{\bar{z}}(z)|]\frac{|dz|}{(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}}

\displaystyle\leq|f(\varphi(0))|+\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\int_{o}^{\varphi(0)}\frac{|dz|}{(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}}.

If
\alpha=1
, then

|f(0)|\leq|f(\varphi(0))|+\frac{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}}{2}\ln\frac{1+|\varphi(0)|}{1-|\varphi(0)|}

and so

\displaystyle|||f|||_{HB(\alpha)}

\displaystyle=|f(0)|+\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{|||fo\varphi|||_{HB(\alpha)}}{\delta}(1+\frac{1}{2}ln\frac{1+|\varphi(0)|}{1-|\varphi(0)|}).

Since,
\|fo\varphi\|_{HB(\alpha)}\geq\delta\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}
.
As the same way if
\alpha\neq 1
,

\displaystyle|f(0)|

\displaystyle\leq|f(\varphi(0))|+\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\int_{o}^{\varphi(0)}\frac{|dz|}{(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}}

\displaystyle\leq|f(\varphi(0))|+\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\int_{o}^{\varphi(0)}\frac{|dz|}{(1-|z|)^{\alpha}},

so

\displaystyle|f(0)|

\displaystyle\leq|f(\varphi(0))|+\frac{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}}{-\alpha+1}[(1-|\varphi(0)|)^{-\alpha+1}-1]|||f|||_{HB(\alpha)}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{|||fo\varphi|||_{HB(\alpha)}}{\delta}(1+\frac{1}{-\alpha+1}[(1-|\varphi(0)|)^{-\alpha+1}-1]).

By these observations we get that
C_{\varphi}
is bounded below.
[
3
]

∎

Here we give an equivalent condition for boundedness of
C_{\varphi}:HB(\alpha)\rightarrow HB(\alpha)
.

Theorem 2.9
.

Let
0<\alpha<\infty
and
\varphi
be an analytic self-map of the unit disk
D
. Then
C_{\varphi}:HB(\alpha)\rightarrow HB(\alpha)
is bounded if and only if

\sup_{n\in N}n^{\alpha-1}\|\varphi^{n}+\bar{\varphi}^{n}\|_{HB(\alpha)}<\infty.

Proof.

Let
n\in N
and
z^{n}+\bar{z}^{n}
. By Theorem
2.8
we have

\displaystyle\|z^{n}+\bar{z}^{n}\|_{HB(\alpha)}

\displaystyle=\max_{z\in D}2n|z|^{n-1}(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}

\displaystyle=2n(\frac{2\alpha}{n-1+2\alpha})^{\alpha}(\frac{n-1}{n-1+2\alpha})^{\frac{(n-1)}{2}},

where the maximum is attained at any point on the circle with radius
r_{n}=(\frac{n-1}{n-1+2\alpha})^{\frac{(n-1)}{2}}
. Therefore

\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}n^{\alpha-1}\|z^{n}+\bar{z}^{n}\|_{HB(\alpha)}=2(\frac{2\alpha}{e}).

And so there exists a constant
K>0
, independent of
n
, such that
\|z^{n}+\bar{z}^{n}\|_{HB(\alpha)}\leq Kn^{1-\alpha}
. For
f_{n}=\frac{z^{n}+\bar{z}^{n}}{\|z^{n}+\bar{z}^{n}\|_{HB(\alpha)}}
we get that
|||f_{n}|||_{HB(\alpha)}=\|f_{n}\|_{HB(\alpha)}=1
. Hence we have

\displaystyle\infty

\displaystyle>\|C_{\varphi}\|

\displaystyle\geq\|C_{\varphi}f_{n}\|_{HB(\alpha)}

\displaystyle=\|f_{n}o\varphi\|_{HB(\alpha)}

\displaystyle\geq\frac{1}{K}n^{\alpha-1}\|\varphi^{n}+\bar{\varphi}^{n}\|_{HB(\alpha)}.

Conversely, by the assumption obviously we have
\|\varphi+\bar{\varphi}\|_{HB(\alpha)}<\infty
. If
\sup_{z\in D}|\varphi(z)|<1
, then we can find
0<r<1
such that
\sup_{z\in D}|\varphi(z)|<r
. In this case easily we get that
C_{\varphi}:HB(\alpha)\rightarrow HB(\alpha)
is bounded. Now we assume that
\sup_{z\in D}|\varphi(z)|=1
and put for any
n\geq 1

D_{n}=\{z\in D:r_{n}\leq|\varphi(z)|\leq r_{n+1}\},

where
r_{n}
is given as before. Let
m
be the smallest positive integer such that
D_{m}\neq\varnothing
. Since
\sup_{z\in D}|\varphi(z)|=1
,
D_{n}
is not empty for every integer
n\geq m
, and
D=\bigcup_{n=m}^{\infty}D_{n}
. Then for every
n\geq m
we conclude that

\displaystyle\min_{z\in D_{n}}2n^{\alpha}|\varphi(z)|^{n-1}(1-|\varphi(z)|^{2})^{\alpha}

\displaystyle\geq 2n^{\alpha}H_{n,\alpha}(r_{n+1})

\displaystyle=2(\frac{2\alpha n}{n+2\alpha})^{\alpha}(\frac{n}{n+2\alpha})^{\frac{(n-1)}{2}},

where
H_{n,\alpha}(x)=x^{n-1}(1-x^{2})^{\alpha}
. Thus

\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}\min_{z\in D_{n}}2n^{\alpha}|\varphi(z)|^{n-1}(1-|\varphi(z)|^{2})^{\alpha}\geq 2(\frac{2\alpha}{e}).

Therefore, there exists
\delta>0
such that, for any
n\geq m,

\min_{z\in D_{n}}2n^{\alpha}|\varphi(z)|^{n-1}(1-|\varphi(z)|^{2})^{\alpha}\geq\delta.

Also for every
f\in HB(\alpha)
we have

\displaystyle\|C_{\varphi}f\|_{HB(\alpha)}

\displaystyle=\sup_{z\in D}(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}|\varphi^{{}^{\prime}}(z)|[|f_{z}(z)|+|f_{\bar{z}}(z)|]

\displaystyle=\sup_{n\geq m}\sup_{z\in D_{n}}(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}|\varphi^{{}^{\prime}}(z)|[|f_{z}(z)|+|f_{\bar{z}}(z)|]

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{\delta}\sup_{n\geq m}\sup_{z\in D_{n}}n^{\alpha-1}(1-|\varphi(z)|^{2})^{\alpha}[|f_{z}(z)|+|f_{\bar{z}}(z)|]2|(\varphi^{n})^{{}^{\prime}}(z)|(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{\delta}\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\sup_{n\geq 1}n^{\alpha-1}\|\varphi^{n}+\bar{\varphi}^{n}\|_{HB(\alpha)}.

This implies that
C_{\varphi}
is bounded on
HB(\alpha)
.
∎

It is well-known that the composition operator
C_{\varphi}
is bounded on the Bloch space
HB
for any analytic self-map
\varphi
of
D
. Hence, if we let
\alpha=1
in Theorem

, we get the following corollary.

Corollary 2.10
.

For any analytic self-map
\varphi
of the unit disk
D
,

\sup_{n\in N}\|\varphi^{n}+\bar{\varphi}^{n}\|_{HB}<\infty.

Theorem 2.11
.

Let
0<\alpha<\infty
and
\varphi
be an analytic self-map of the unit disk
D
. Then the essential norm of the composition operator
C_{\varphi}:HB(\alpha)\rightarrow HB(\alpha)
is

\|C_{\varphi}\|_{e}=\frac{1}{2}(\frac{e}{2\alpha})^{\alpha}\limsup_{n\rightarrow\infty}n^{\alpha-1}\|\varphi^{n}+\bar{\varphi}^{n}\|_{HB(\alpha)}.

Proof.

Let
n\in N
and
z^{n}+\bar{z}^{n}
. By Theorem
2.9
we have

\displaystyle\|z^{n}+\bar{z}^{n}\|_{HB(\alpha)}

\displaystyle=\max_{z\in D}2n|z|^{n-1}(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}

\displaystyle=2n(\frac{2\alpha}{n-1+2\alpha})^{\alpha}(\frac{n-1}{n-1+2\alpha})^{\frac{(n-1)}{2}},

where the maximum is attained at any point on the circle with radius

r_{n}=(\frac{n-1}{n-1+2\alpha})^{\frac{(n-1)}{2}}.

Thus we have

\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}n^{\alpha-1}\|z^{n}+\bar{z}^{n}\|_{HB(\alpha)}=2(\frac{2\alpha}{e}).

Let
f_{n}=\frac{z^{n}+\bar{z}^{n}}{\|z^{n}+\bar{z}^{n}\|_{HB(\alpha)}}
and so
\|f_{n}\|_{HB(\alpha)}=1
. Also easily we get that
\{f_{n}\}
converges to
0
weakly in
HB(\alpha)
. If
K
is a compact operator on
HB(\alpha)
, then
\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}\|Kf_{n}\|_{HB(\alpha)}=0
.
Therefore

\displaystyle\|C_{\varphi}-K\|

\displaystyle\geq\limsup_{n\rightarrow\infty}\|(C_{\varphi}-K)f_{n}\|_{HB(\alpha)}

\displaystyle\geq\limsup_{n\rightarrow\infty}(\|(C_{\varphi})f_{n}\|_{HB(\alpha)}-\|(Kf_{n}\|_{HB(\alpha)})

\displaystyle=\limsup_{n\rightarrow\infty}\|(C_{\varphi})f_{n}\|_{HB(\alpha)}.

By taking the
\inf
on both sides of this inequality over all compact operator
K
, for
C_{\varphi}:HB(\alpha)\rightarrow HB(\alpha)
, we obtain the that

\displaystyle\|C_{\varphi}\|_{e}

\displaystyle=\inf_{K}\|C_{\varphi}-K\|

\displaystyle\geq\limsup_{n\rightarrow\infty}\|(C_{\varphi})f_{n}\|_{HB(\alpha)}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2}(\frac{e}{2\alpha})^{\alpha}\limsup_{n\rightarrow\infty}n^{\alpha-1}\|\varphi^{n}+\bar{\varphi}^{n}\|_{HB(\alpha)}.

If

\limsup_{n\rightarrow\infty}n^{\alpha-1}\|\varphi^{n}+\bar{\varphi}^{n}\|_{HB(\alpha)}=\infty,

then
C_{\varphi}
is not bounded on
HB(\alpha)
. Now suppose that

\limsup_{n\rightarrow\infty}n^{\alpha-1}\|\varphi^{n}+\bar{\varphi}^{n}\|_{HB(\alpha)}<\infty.

Hence
C_{\varphi}
is a bounded operator from
HB(\alpha)
to
HB(\alpha)
and, clearly,

\|\varphi+\bar{\varphi}\|_{HB(\alpha)}<\infty
.
Notice that if
\sup_{Z\in D}|\varphi(z)|<1
, then
C_{\varphi}
is compact and we have the conditions of theorem. Hence, in the sequel we assume that
\sup_{Z\in D}|\varphi(z)|=1
. Let
L_{n}
be a sequence of operators as we used in the last results. Since each
L_{n}
is compact as an operator on
HB(\alpha)
, so is
C_{\varphi}L_{n}
. And we have

\displaystyle\|C_{\varphi}\|_{e}

\displaystyle=\inf_{C_{\varphi}L_{n}iscompact}\|C_{\varphi}-C_{\varphi}L_{n}\|

\displaystyle\leq\limsup_{n\rightarrow\infty}\|C_{\varphi}-C_{\varphi}L_{n}\|

\displaystyle\leq\limsup_{n\rightarrow\infty}\sup_{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\leq 1}|(I-L_{n})f(\varphi(0))|

\displaystyle+\limsup_{n\rightarrow\infty}\sup_{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\leq 1}\sup_{z\in D}(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}|\varphi^{{}^{\prime}}(z)|[|((I-L_{n})f)_{z}(\varphi(z))|+[|((I-L_{n})f)_{\bar{z}}(\varphi(z))|].

Consequently, we have

\limsup_{n\rightarrow\infty}\sup_{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\leq 1}|(I-L_{n})f(\varphi(0))|=0.

Now we need only consider the term

J=\sup_{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\leq 1}\sup_{z\in D}(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}|\varphi^{{}^{\prime}}(z)|[|((I-L_{n})f)_{z}(\varphi(z))|+[|((I-L_{n})f)_{\bar{z}}(\varphi(z))|].

For every
n\geq 1
put

D_{n}=\{z\in D:r_{n}\leq|\varphi(z)|\leq r_{n+1}\},

where
r_{n}
is as given in the Lemma 2.16. Let
m
be the smallest positive integer such that
D_{m}\neq\varnothing
. Since
\sup_{z\in D}|\varphi(z)|=1
,
D_{n}
is not empty for every integer
n\geq m
and
D=\bigcup_{n=m}^{\infty}D_{n}
. Now we divide
J
into two parts:

\displaystyle J

\displaystyle=\sup_{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\leq 1}\sup_{m\leq k\leq N-1}\sup_{z\in D_{k}}(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}|\varphi^{{}^{\prime}}(z)|[|((I-L_{n})f)_{z}(\varphi(z))|+[|((I-L_{n})f)_{\bar{z}}(\varphi(z))|]

\displaystyle+\sup_{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\leq 1}\sup_{k\geq N}\sup_{z\in D_{k}}(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}|\varphi^{{}^{\prime}}(z)|[|((I-L_{n})f)_{z}(\varphi(z))|+[|((I-L_{n})f)_{\bar{z}}(\varphi(z))|]

\displaystyle=J_{1}+J_{2}.

For
J_{1}
we have

\displaystyle\limsup_{n\rightarrow\infty}J_{1}

\displaystyle=\limsup_{n\rightarrow\infty}\sup_{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\leq 1}\sup_{m\leq k\leq N-1}\sup_{z\in D_{k}}(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}|\varphi^{{}^{\prime}}(z)|[|((I-L_{n})f)_{z}(\varphi(z))|

\displaystyle+[|((I-L_{n})f)_{\bar{z}}(\varphi(z))|]

\displaystyle=\limsup_{n\rightarrow\infty}\sup_{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\leq 1}\sup_{r_{m}\leq|\varphi(z)|\leq r_{N-1}}[|((I-L_{n})f)_{z}(\varphi(z))|+[|((I-L_{n})f)_{\bar{z}}(\varphi(z))|]=0.

Write the function under three supremum signs in
J_{2}
as

\frac{(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}|\varphi^{{}^{\prime}}(z)|[|((I-L_{n})f)_{z}(\varphi(z))|+[|((I-L_{n})f)_{\bar{z}}(\varphi(z))|k^{\alpha}|\varphi(z)|^{k-1}(1-|\varphi(z)|^{2})^{\alpha}}{k^{\alpha}|\varphi(z)|^{k-1}(1-|\varphi(z)|^{2})^{\alpha}}.

It is easy to see that

\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}[2(\frac{2\alpha k}{k+2\alpha})^{\alpha}(\frac{k}{k+2\alpha})^{\frac{(k-1)}{2}}]^{-1}=\frac{1}{2}(\frac{e}{2\alpha})^{\alpha}.

Hence for every
\varepsilon>0
, we can choose
N>m+1
large enough such that for any

,

[2(\frac{2\alpha k}{k+2\alpha})^{\alpha}(\frac{k}{k+2\alpha})^{\frac{(k-1)}{2}}]^{-1}<\frac{1}{2}(\frac{e}{2\alpha})^{\alpha}+\varepsilon.

For such
N
we have

\displaystyle J_{2}

\displaystyle\leq[\frac{1}{2}(\frac{e}{2\alpha})^{\alpha}+\varepsilon]\sup_{\|f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\leq 1}\|(I-L_{n})f\|_{HB(\alpha)}\sup_{k\geq N}\sup_{z\in D_{k}}2k^{\alpha-1}|(\varphi^{k})^{{}^{\prime}}(z)|(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}

\displaystyle\leq[\frac{1}{2}(\frac{e}{2\alpha})^{\alpha}+\varepsilon]\|I-L_{n}\|\sup_{k\geq N}k^{\alpha-1}\|\varphi^{k}+\bar{\varphi}^{k}\|_{HB(\alpha)}.

Thus by the Theorem
2.9
we obtain

\limsup_{k\rightarrow\infty}J_{2}\leq[\frac{1}{2}(\frac{e}{2\alpha})^{\alpha}+\varepsilon]\sup_{k\geq N}k^{\alpha-1}\|\varphi^{k}+\bar{\varphi}^{k}\|_{HB(\alpha)}.

Hence for any
N
sufficiently large we have

\displaystyle\|C_{\varphi}\|_{e}\leq\limsup_{n\rightarrow\infty}J_{2}\leq[\frac{1}{2}(\frac{e}{2\alpha})^{\alpha}+\varepsilon]\sup_{k\geq N}k^{\alpha-1}\|\varphi^{k}+\bar{\varphi}^{k}\|_{HB(\alpha)}.

Thus we have

\|C_{\varphi}\|_{e}\leq[\frac{1}{2}(\frac{e}{2\alpha})^{\alpha}+\varepsilon]\limsup_{k\rightarrow\infty}k^{\alpha-1}\|\varphi^{k}+\bar{\varphi}^{k}\|_{HB(\alpha)}.

Since
\varepsilon
is an arbitrary positive number, therefore we get

\|C_{\varphi}\|_{e}\leq\frac{1}{2}(\frac{e}{2\alpha})^{\alpha}\limsup_{k\rightarrow\infty}k^{\alpha-1}\|\varphi^{k}+\bar{\varphi}^{k}\|_{HB(\alpha)}.

This completes the proof.
∎
