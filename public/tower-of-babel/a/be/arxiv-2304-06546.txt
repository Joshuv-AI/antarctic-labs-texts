KG-oscillators in cosmic string rainbow gravity spacetime and a non-uniform magnetic field

In the cosmic string rainbow gravity spacetime background (
3

(8)

in Eq. (
7

\left\{\tilde{E}^{2}+g_{{}_{1}}\left(y\right)^{2}\left[\partial_{r}^{2}+\frac{1}{r}\partial_{r}-\frac{\left(\ell-eA_{\varphi}\right)^{2}}{\alpha^{2}\,r^{2}}\right]\right\}\psi\left(r\right)=0,

IV
Concluding remarks

In a recent paper
R40.1
, we have studied PDM KG-Coulomb particles in cosmic string rainbow gravity and a uniform magnetic field
\mathbf{B}=\mathbf{\nabla}\times\mathbf{A}=\frac{1}{2}B_{\circ}\,\hat{z}
(introduced by the electromagnetic vector potential
A_{\varphi}=\frac{1}{2}B_{\circ}r

g_{{}_{1}}\left(y\right)=\sqrt{1-\epsilon y^{2}}
;
y=E/E_{p}

g_{{}_{0}}\left(y\right)=1

g_{{}_{0}}\left(y\right)=1

R6

R61

R42

Under such fine tuning settings, we have studied KG-oscillators in the cosmic string rainbow gravity spacetime (

R40.1
