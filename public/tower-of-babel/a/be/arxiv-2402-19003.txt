Introduction

In this paper, all groups are finite and we write
Irr(G)
and
nl(G)
for the set of irreducible characters and the set of non-linear irreducible characters of
G
respectively.
c(G)
denotes the nilpotency class of
G
. For the terminologies not defined here follow the Isaacs’ book
[
4
]
and Rotman’s book
[
6
]
. An important question in group theory is what information can we get from the set of character degrees about the structure of the group. There are many research articles attempting to answer this question by examining the groups whose irreducible characters have some distinctive properties like ”the group has only two character degrees”, ”the centres of the irreducible characters form a chain with respect to inclusion” etc. In
[
1
]
, A. Nenciu obtained various characterizations of the finite groups for which
\chi(1)^{2}=|G:Z(\chi)|
for all characters
\chi\in Irr(G)
and
\{Z(\chi):\chi\in Irr(G)\}

is a chain, where
Z(\chi)=\{g\in G:|\chi(g)|=\chi(1)\}
. In
[
8
]
Mark L. Lewis obtained the characterizations of the groups for which
\{Z(\chi):\chi\in Irr(G)\}

is a chain with respect to inclusion and obtained some information regarding the structure of these groups. In this paper we examine the GVZ-groups having two character degrees. The concept of GVZ-group first appeared in
[
10
]
under the name ”groups of Ono type”. The term GVZ-group was introduced by A. Nenciu.

Definition 1.1
.

[
1
]

A nonabelian group
G
is called a generalized VZ-group (GVZ for short) if for all
\chi\in Irr(G)
we have
\chi(g)=0
for all
g\in G\setminus Z(\chi)
.

By Corollary 2.30
[
4
]
we get that if
G
is a GVZ-group then
\chi(1)^{2}=|G:Z(\chi)|
. If
G
is a GVZ-group with two character degrees then it follows that
|Z(\chi)|
is constant for all
\chi\in nl(G)
. In
[
8
]
Mark L. Lewis obtained various results about the factor groups for a group where centres of the irreducible characters form a chain. In this paper, we examine the GVZ-groups having two character degrees. It is to be noted that if a GVZ-group has only two character degrees, then the centres of non-linear irreducible characters of the group are of equal size. We obtain the following results.

Theorem 1.1
.

Let
G
be a GVZ-group with two character degrees. Then for any
\chi\in nl(G)
, the groups

G/Z(\chi)
,
G/Z(G)
and
G^{\prime}/[Z(\chi),G]

are elementary Abelian and
exp\ G/Z(\chi)=exp\ G/Z(G)=exp\ G^{\prime}/[Z(\chi),G]
.

Theorem 1.2
.

Let
G
be a GVZ group with two character degrees.
Let
N
be a normal subgroup of
G
such that
G^{\prime}\not\subseteq N
. Then
\chi\in nl(G/N)
if and only if
N[Z(\chi),G]<NG^{\prime}
.

We also obtain a characterization of GVZ-groups with two character degrees by the orders of the centres of some factor groups of the group.

Theorem 1.3
.

Let
G
be a GVZ-group. Then the following conditions are equivalent:

(i)

|cd(G)|=2
.

(ii)

There is a collection
\{X_{1},X_{2},\ldots,X_{n}\}
of normal subgroups of
G
of equal order such that every normal subgroup
N
of
G
with
G^{\prime}\not\subseteq N
and
[Z(\chi),G]\subseteq N
for some
\chi\in\ nl(G)
satisfies
Z(G/N)=X_{i}/N
for some
1\leq i\leq n
.

(iii)

For any two normal subgroups
N
and
M
of
G
such that
G^{\prime}\not\subseteq N,M
and
[Z(\chi),G]\subseteq N
,
[Z(\phi),G]\subseteq M
for some
\chi,\phi\in\ nl(G)
if we define
Z_{N}/N=Z(G/N)
,
Z_{M}/M=Z(G/M)
then
|Z_{N}|=|Z_{M}|
.

Moreover the collection
\{X_{1},X_{2},\ldots,X_{n}\}
of normal subgroups in (ii) is the collection of centres of non-linear irreducible characters of
G
.

The characterizations of
p
-groups from the set of character degrees
are of special interest. For example the finite
p
-groups with two character degrees where all the non-linear irreducible characters are faithful have been studied in
[
7
]
and
[
3
]
. The finite
p
- groups
G
having character degree set
\{1,|G:Z(G)|^{\frac{1}{2}}\}
have been characterized in
[
2
]
. Generalizing this result we obtain the following theorem.

Theorem 1.4
.

Let
G
be a non-Abelian
p
group with
|cd(G)|=2
.Then the following conditions are equivalent:

(i)

G
is a GVZ group.

(ii)

G/[Z(\chi),G]
is isoclinic to a semi extraspecial
p
-group for any
\chi\in nl(G)
.

(iii)

Z(G/N)=Z(\chi)/N
for any normal subgroup
N
of
G
such that
G^{\prime}\not\leq N
and
[Z(\chi),G]\leq N
for some
\chi\in\ nl(G)
.

2
Preliminaries

The aim of this section is to prove some essential results which will be applied in the proofs of our main results. We state the following lemma.

Lemma 2.1
.

Let
G
be a GVZ-group with two character degrees. Then the nilpotency class of
G
is two.

Proof.

From Theorem B in
[
13
]
we get that if
G
is a GVZ-group then
c(G)\leq|cd(G)|
.Since
|cd(G)|=2
and
G
is a non-Abelian group it follows that
c(G)=2
.
∎

In the following Lemma we get an essential condition that the centres of two non-linear irreducible characters of a GVZ-group with two character degrees are equal.

Lemma 2.2
.

Let
G
be a GVZ group with two character degrees. Let
\phi,\chi\in nl(G)
. If
[Z(\phi),G]\leq ker\chi
, then
Z(\phi)=Z(\chi)
.

Proof.

Suppose
[Z(\phi),G]\leq ker\chi
.
Then
\frac{Z(\phi)ker\chi}{\ker\chi}\subseteq Z(\frac{G}{ker\chi})=\frac{Z(\chi)}{ker\chi}
.

This gives
Z(\phi)\subseteq Z(\chi)
. Since
|Z(\chi)|
is constant for all
\chi\in nl(G)
, we get
Z(\phi)=Z(\chi)
.
∎

Corollary 2.1
.

Let
G
be a GVZ group with two character degrees. Let
N\unlhd G

and
\chi\in nl(G)
be such that
G^{\prime}\not\subseteq N
and
[Z(\chi),G]\leq N
. Then
N\leq Z(\chi)
.

Proof.

Let
\phi\in nl(G)
be such that
N\leq ker\phi
. Then
N\leq Z(\phi)
and since
[Z(\chi),G]\leq ker\phi
, it follows that
Z(\chi)=Z(\phi)
. Thus we get that

N\leq Z(\chi)
.

∎

The following lemma gives a necessary and sufficient condition to find the centres of the non-linear irreducible characters of a GVZ-group with two character degrees.

Lemma 2.3
.

Let
G
be a GVZ group with two character degrees.
Let
\{X_{1},X_{2},\ldots,X_{n}\}
be the collection of centres of non-linear irreducible characters. Let
\chi\in nl(G)
. Then
Z(\chi)=X_{i}
if and only if
[X_{i},G]\leq ker\chi
.

Proof.

If
Z(\chi)=X_{i}
, then
[X_{i},G]=[Z(\chi),G]\leq ker\chi
. Conversely if
[X_{i},G]\leq ker\chi
then
X_{i}ker\chi/ker\chi\subseteq Z(G/ker\chi)=Z(\chi)/ker\chi
. Since
|X_{i}|=|Z(\chi)|
, it follows that
Z(\chi)=X_{i}
.

∎

In the next lemma we obtain a necessary and sufficient condition for a character
\chi\in Irr(G)
to be linear.

Lemma 2.4
.

Let
\chi\in Irr(G)
. Then
\chi
is a linear character if and only if
[Z(\chi),G]=G^{\prime}
.

Proof.

If
\chi
is a linear character then
[Z(\chi),G]=[G,G]=G^{\prime}
.

Conversely suppose that
[Z(\chi),G]=G^{\prime}
where
\chi\in Irr(G)
. Let
\Psi
be the representation affording the character
\chi
.
Let
g\in G^{\prime}=[Z(\chi),G]
so that
g=g_{1}g_{2}\ldots g_{t}
. For each
1\leq i\leq t
let
g_{i}=x_{i}^{-1}y_{i}^{-1}x_{i}y_{i}
where
x_{i}\in Z(\chi)
and
y_{i}\in G
.
Then
\Psi(x_{i})=\alpha_{i}I
for some
\alpha_{i}\in\mathbb{C}
.
Now
\Psi(g_{i})=\Psi(x_{i}^{-1}y_{i}^{-1}x_{i}y_{i})

=\Psi(x_{i})^{-1}\Psi(y_{i})^{-1}\Psi(x_{i})\Psi(y_{i})

=\alpha_{i}^{-1}I\Psi(y_{i})^{-1}\alpha_{i}I\Psi(y_{i})=I
.
Thus
\Psi(g)=I
and hence

g\in ker\Psi=ker\chi
. This shows that
G^{\prime}\subseteq ker\chi
. Hence
\chi
is a linear character.
∎

Since
[Z(\chi),G]<G^{\prime}
for any
\chi\in nl(G)
it makes sense to find the centre of the quotient group
G/[Z(\chi),G]
.

Lemma 2.5
.

Let
\chi\in Irr(G)
. Then
Z(G/[Z(\chi),G])=Z(\chi)/[Z(\chi),G]
.

Proof.

Z(\chi)/[Z(\chi),G]\leq Z(G/[Z(\chi),G])
is obvious.

Let
Z/[Z(\chi),G]=Z(G/[Z(\chi),G])
. Let
\Psi
be the representation affording the character
\chi
.

Let
g[Z(\chi),G]\in Z(G/[Z(\chi),G])
.Then
for any
x\in G
,
gx[Z(\chi),G]=xg[Z(\chi),G]
and hence
[g,x]\in[Z(\chi),G]\leq ker\chi=ker\Psi
.
This gives that
\Psi([g,x])=I,\forall x\in G
and therefore

\Psi(g)\Psi(x)=\Psi(x)\Psi(g),\forall x\in G
.
By Lemma 2.25 of
[
4
]
we get
\Psi(g)=\alpha I
for some
\alpha\in\mathbb{C}
.
By Lemma 2.27 of
[
4
]
we get that
g\in Z(\chi)
. Hence we conclude that
Z(G/[Z(\chi),G])\leq Z(\chi)/[Z(\chi),G]
. This proves the result.
∎

In the following lemma we find the centre of a quotient group of certain type of GVZ-group with two character degrees .

Lemma 2.6
.

Let
G
be a GVZ group with two character degrees. Let
N\unlhd G
be such that
G^{\prime}\not\subseteq N
but
[Z(\chi),G]\leq N
for some
\chi\in nl(G)
, then
Z(G/N)=Z(\chi)/N
.

Proof.

Let
Z/N=Z(G/N)
. Then
Z=\cap_{\theta\in Irr(G/N)}Z(\theta)
. For every
\theta\in Irr(G/N)
,
N\leq ker\theta
. Then
[Z(\chi),G]\leq N\leq ker\theta
and hence
Z(\chi)=Z(\theta)
. Thus
Z=\cap_{\theta\in Irr(G/N)}Z(\theta)=Z(\chi)
.

∎

Isoclinicism is an equivalence relation of groups and was first introduced by P. Hall

For a non-abelian

3
Main Results

In the following theorem we determine the structure of some of the factor groups of a GVZ-group with two character degrees.

Theorem 3.1
.

Let
G
be a GVZ-group with two character degrees. Then for any
\chi\in nl(G)
, the groups
G/Z(\chi)
,
G/Z(G)
and
G^{\prime}/[Z(\chi),G]

are elementary Abelian and
exp\ G/Z(\chi)=exp\ G/Z(G)=exp\ G^{\prime}/[Z(\chi),G]
.

Proof.

We get that
c(G)=2
. Therefore,
1\neq G^{\prime}\subseteq Z(G)
. Since the irreducible character
\chi
is non-linear, we get that
[Z(\chi),G]\not\subseteq G^{\prime}\subseteq Z(G)
and hence we can find a prime
p
and a normal subgroup
N
of
G
such that
[Z(\chi),G]\subseteq N\subseteq G^{\prime}
and
|G^{\prime}:N|=p
. Since
Z(G/N)=Z(\chi)/N
and
G/Z(\chi)\cong(G/N)/(Z(\chi)/N)
, we can assume that
N=1
.
Then
|G^{\prime}|=p
. Since the nilpotency class of
G
is
2
, by Lemma 4.4
[
5
]
we get that exp
G/Z(G)=p
and hence

G/Z(G)
is elementary Abelian. Since
G/Z(\chi)\cong(G/Z(G))/(Z(\chi)/Z(G))
, it follows that
G/Z(\chi)
is an elementary Abelian group for any
\chi\in nl(G)
.

Let
\chi_{1},\chi_{2}\in nl(G)
.
Suppose
G/Z(\chi_{1})
is an elementary Abelian
p_{1}
-group and
G/Z(\chi_{2})
is an elementary Abelian
p_{2}
-group.
Now for any
x\in G
,
x^{p_{1}}\in Z(\chi_{1})\subseteq Z(\chi_{1})Z(\chi_{2})
and this shows that
G/Z(\chi_{1})Z(\chi_{2})
is an elemantary Abelian
p_{1}
-group. Again
x^{p_{2}}\in Z(\chi_{2})\subseteq Z(\chi_{1})Z(\chi_{2})
and this shows
G/Z(\chi_{1})Z(\chi_{2})
is an elemantary Abelian
p_{2}
-group. Hence
p_{1}=p_{2}
. This proves that
exp\ G/Z(\chi)=p
for all
\chi\in nl(G)
.

Hence for any
x\in G
, we get that
x^{p}\in Z(\chi)
for all
\chi\in nl(G)
. This in turn gives that
x^{p}\in\cap_{\chi\in nl(G)}Z(\chi)=Z(G)
. Thus we get that the group
G/Z(G)
is elementary Abelian and
exp\ G/Z(G)=p
.

Since
G^{\prime}/[Z(\chi),G]=(G/[Z(\chi),G])^{\prime}
, it is enough to assume that
[Z(\chi),G]=1
. The group
G^{\prime}
is generated by the elements of the form
[x,y]
,
x,y\in G
. Since the nilpotency class of
G
is
2
and
x^{p}\in Z(G)
for all
g\in G
, we get that
[x,y]^{p}=[x^{p},y]=1
. Thus exponent of every element of
G^{\prime}
is
p
and consequently
G^{\prime}
is an elementary Abelian group.

∎

From Lemma
2.4
we get that
\chi\in Irr(G)
is a non-linear character if and only if
[Z(\chi),G]<G^{\prime}
. In the following theorem we get a similar result for the irreducible characters of the factor groups of a GVZ-group with two character degrees.

Theorem 3.2
.

Let
G
be a GVZ group with two character degrees. Then
\chi\in nl(G/N)
if and only if
N[Z(\chi),G]<NG^{\prime}
.

Proof.

First we assume that there is a
\chi\in nl(G/N)
. Suppose
N[Z(\chi),G]=NG^{\prime}
. By Lemma
2.3
we get that
[Z(\chi),G]\leq ker\chi
. Again
N\leq ker\chi
.Thus
G^{\prime}\leq NG^{\prime}=N[Z(\chi),G]\leq ker\chi
. This gives that
\chi
is a linear character. This contradiction proves that
N[Z(\chi),G]<NG^{\prime}
.

Conversely assume that
N[Z(\chi),G]<NG^{\prime}
for
\chi\in Irr(G/N)
. We choose a non-principal irreducible character
\lambda\in Irr(NG^{\prime}/N[Z(\chi),G])
. If possible assume that all the irreducible constituent characters of
\lambda^{G}
are linear. Then
G^{\prime}\subseteq ker(\lambda^{G})\subseteq ker\lambda
and hence
NG^{\prime}\subseteq ker\lambda
. This gives that
\lambda
is the principal character of the group
NG^{\prime}/N[Z(\chi),G]
. Thus there exists a non-linear irreducible character
\phi
of
G
which is a constituent of
\lambda^{G}
. Then
N[Z(\chi),G]\leq ker\lambda\leq ker\phi
. The fact
[Z(\chi),G]\leq ker\phi
gives that

Z(\phi)=Z(\chi)
. Since
\phi
is a non-linear irreducible character we must get that
\chi
is a non-linear irreducible character of
G/N
.
∎

In the following theorem we characterize the GVZ-groups with two character degrees by the orders of the centres of some factor groups of the group.

Theorem 3.3
.

Let
G
be a a GVZ group. Then the following conditions are equivalent:

(i)

|cd(G)|=2
.

(ii)

There is a collection
\{X_{1},X_{2},\ldots,X_{n}\}
of normal subgroups of
G
of equal order such that every normal subgroup
N
of
G
with
G^{\prime}\not\subseteq N
and
[Z(\chi),G]\subseteq N
for some
\chi\in\ nl(G)
satisfies
Z(G/N)=X_{i}/N
for some
1\leq i\leq n
.

(iii)

For any two normal subgroups
N
and
M
of
G
such that
G^{\prime}\not\subseteq N,M
and
[Z(\chi),G]\subseteq N
,
[Z(\phi),G]\subseteq M
for some
\chi,\phi\in\ nl(G)
if we define
Z_{N}/N=Z(G/N)
,
Z_{M}/M=Z(G/M)
then
|Z_{N}|=|Z_{M}|
.

Moreover the collection
\{X_{1},X_{2},\ldots,X_{n}\}
of normal subgroups in (ii) is the collection of centres of non-linear irreducible characters of
G
.

Proof.

(i)\Rightarrow(ii)

Since
G
is a GVZ-group with
|cd(G)|=2
, we get that
|Z(\chi)|
is constant for all
\chi\in nl(G)
. Let
\{X_{1},X_{2},\ldots,X_{n}\}
be the collection of centres of non-linear irreducible characters of
G
. Let
N
be a normal subgroup of
G
such that
G^{\prime}\not\subseteq N
and
[Z(\chi),G]\subseteq N
for some
\chi\in\ nl(G)
. Let
Z(\chi)=X_{i}
. Then by Lemma
2.6

Z(G/N)=Z(\chi)/N=X_{i}/N
.

(ii)\Rightarrow(iii)

Let
N
and
M
be normal subgroups of
G
such that
G^{\prime}\not\subseteq N,M
and
[Z(\chi),G]\subseteq N
,
[Z(\phi),G]\subseteq M
for some
\chi,\phi\in\ nl(G)
. If
Z_{N}/N=Z(G/N)
,
Z_{M}/M=Z(G/M)
we get that
Z_{N}=X_{i}
and
Z_{M}=X_{j}
for some
i,j
. Since
|X_{i}|=|X_{j}|
we get that
|Z_{N}|=|Z_{M}|
.

(iii)\Rightarrow(i)

Let
\chi,\phi\in\ nl(G)
.
Then
G^{\prime}\not\subseteq ker\chi,ker\phi
and
[Z(\chi),G]\leq ker\chi
,
[Z(\phi),G]\leq ker\phi
. Since
Z(\chi)/ker\chi=Z(G/ker\chi)
and
Z(\phi)/ker\phi=Z(G/ker\phi)
, it follows that
|Z(\chi)|=|Z(\phi)|
. Since
G
is a GVZ-group it follows that
\chi(1)^{2}=|\frac{G}{Z(\chi)}|
is constant and hence we get that
|cd(G)|=2
.

For every
\chi\in nl(G)
, we get that
G^{\prime}\not\subseteq ker\chi
and
[Z(\chi),G]\subseteq ker\chi
. Thus by (ii) we get that
Z(\chi)/ker\chi=Z(G/ker\chi)=X_{i}/ker\chi
for some
1\leq i\leq n
and hence
Z(\chi)=X_{i}
. This shows that
\{X_{1},X_{2},\ldots,X_{n}\}
is the collection of the centres of the non-linear irreducible characters of
G
.

∎

In our last theorem we obtain some characterizations of non-Abelian
p
-groups which are GVZ-groups with two character degrees. The results of this theorem generalize the results of Theorem
2.1
.

Theorem 3.4
.

Let
G
be a non-Abelian
p

|cd(G)|=2
. Then the following conditions are equivalent:

(i)

G
is a GVZ group.

(ii)

G/[Z(\chi),G]
is isoclinic to a semi extraspecial
p
-group for any
\chi\in nl(G)
.

(iii)

Z(G/N)=Z(\chi)/N
for any normal subgroup
N
of
G
such that
G^{\prime}\not\leq N
and
[Z(\chi),G]\leq N
for some
\chi\in\ nl(G)
.

Proof.

(i)\Rightarrow(ii)

G
is a GVZ-group with two character degrees. Let

\overline{G}=G/[Z(\chi),G]
. By Lemma
2.5
we get that
\overline{G}
is a GVZ-group with
cd(\overline{G})=\{1,|\frac{\overline{G}}{Z(\overline{G})}|^{\frac{1}{2}}\}
. Hence by Theorem

we get that
G/[Z(\chi),G]
is isoclinic to a semi extraspecial
p
-group.

(ii)\Rightarrow(i)
Let
\chi\in nl(G)
and

\overline{G}=G/[Z(\chi),G]
. Since
\overline{G}
is isoclinic to a semi-extraspecial
p
-group, it follows from Theorem
2.1
that
cd(\overline{G})=\{1,|\overline{G}:Z(\overline{G})|^{\frac{1}{2}}\}
.Since
Z(G/[Z(\chi),G])=Z(\chi)/[Z(\chi),G]
, it follows that
cd(\overline{G})=\{1,|G:Z(\chi)|^{\frac{1}{2}}\}
. Since
|cd(G)|=2
we get that
cd(G)=\{1,|G:Z(\chi)|^{\frac{1}{2}}\}
. By Corollary 2.30 of
[
4
]
it follows that
G
is a GVZ group.

(i)\Rightarrow(iii)

Let
N
be a normal subgroup of
G
such that
G^{\prime}\not\leq N
and
[Z(\chi),G]\leq N
where
\chi\in\ nl(G)
.
