Extended weakly symmetric manifolds
1 K. K. Baishya, 2 S. K. Jana, 3 M. R. Bakshi, 4 M. Pain and 4 H. Kundu

Abstract
. In this work, we have introduced and studied some basic geometric properties of extended weakly symmetric spaces. After classification of this structure we have also established the existence of such a space by presenting a non-trivial example.

Introduction

The modern trend of Mathematics is abstraction, generalization, extension
and then applications. Keeping this in our mind we have ideated the notion
of ‘Extended weakly symmetric manifolds’, which extends the notion of
‘Generalized weakly symmetric manifolds’

‘Hyper-generalized weakly symmetric manifolds’

An
n

extended
weakly symmetric manifold
(which will be abbreviated hereafter as
(EWS)_{n}

(1.1)

\displaystyle(\nabla_{X_{5}}R)(X_{1},X_{2},X_{3},X_{4})=A(X_{5})R(X_{1},X_{2},X_{3},X_{4})

\displaystyle+B(X_{1})R(X_{5},X_{2},X_{3},X_{4})+\bar{B}(X_{2})R(X_{1},X_{5},X_{3},X_{4})

\displaystyle+D(X_{3})R(X_{1},X_{2},X_{5},X_{4})+\bar{D}(X_{4})R(X_{1},X_{2},X_{3},X_{5})

\displaystyle+\alpha(X_{5})H(X_{1},X_{2},X_{3},X_{4})+\beta(X_{1})H(X_{5},X_{2},X_{3},X_{4})

\displaystyle+\bar{\beta}(X_{2})H(X_{1},X_{5},X_{3},X_{4})+\gamma(X_{3})H(X_{1},X_{2},X_{5},X_{4})

\displaystyle+\bar{\gamma}(X_{4})H(X_{1},X_{2},X_{3},X_{5})+\theta(X_{5})G(X_{1},X_{2},X_{3},X_{4})

\displaystyle+\phi(X_{1})G(X_{5},X_{2},X_{3},X_{4})+\bar{\phi}(X_{2})G(X_{1},X_{5},X_{3},X_{4})

\displaystyle+\psi(X_{3})G(X_{1},X_{2},X_{5},X_{4})+\bar{\psi}(X_{4})G(X_{1},X_{2},X_{3},X_{5}),

\displaystyle q(X_{1},X_{4})p(X_{2},X_{3})-q(X_{1},X_{3})p(X_{2},X_{4})

\displaystyle+p(X_{1},X_{4})q(X_{2},X_{3})-p(X_{1},X_{3})q(X_{2},X_{4})

2.
Some basic results

Proposition 2.1
.

Let
K
be a generalized curvature tensor and
A
,
B
be two 1-forms. Then

\displaystyle A(X_{5})K(X_{1},X_{2},X_{3},X_{4})+B(X_{5})K(X_{1},X_{4},X_{2},X_{3})

\displaystyle=\left\{A(X_{5})-\frac{1}{2}B(X_{5})\right\}K(X_{1},X_{2},X_{3},X_{4}).

Lemma 2.1
.

Let
K
be a generalized curvature tensor on a
semi-Riemannian manifold
M
. If

(2.1)

A(X_{1})K(X_{2},X_{5},X_{3},X_{4})+A(X_{2})K(X_{1},X_{5},X_{3},X_{4})=0

then either
A=0
or
K=0
.

Proof: Let
A\neq 0
. So there exists a vector field
\xi
such
that
A(\xi)\neq 0
. Then putting
X_{1}=X_{2}=\xi
in (
2.1
), we get

A(\xi)K(\xi,X_{5},X_{3},X_{4})=0\ \ \Rightarrow\ \ K(\xi,X_{5},X_{3},X_{4})=0.

Now putting
X_{1}=\xi
in (
2.1
), we get

A(\xi)K(X_{2},X_{5},X_{3},X_{4})+A(X_{2})K(\xi,X_{5},X_{3},X_{4})=0,

which implies
K(X_{2},X_{5},X_{3},X_{4})=0
. This completes the proof.

Proposition 2.2
.

If on a semi-Riemannian manifold
M

(2.2)

\displaystyle A(X_{1})R(X_{2},X_{5},X_{3},X_{4})+A(X_{2})R(X_{1},X_{5},X_{3},X_{4})

\displaystyle+B(X_{1})G(X_{2},X_{5},X_{3},X_{4})+B(X_{2})G(X_{1},X_{5},X_{3},X_{4})=0

holds, then either
M
is of constant curvature or
A=B=0
.

Proof: Contracting (
2.2
) over
X_{1}
and
X_{2}
, we get

R(X_{A},X_{5},X_{3},X_{4})+G(X_{B},X_{5},X_{3},X_{4})=0,

where
X_{A},X_{B}
denote the associated vector fields of
A
and
B

respectively. Again contraction over
X_{1}
and
X_{3}
on (
2.2
),
we obtain

\displaystyle\ \ B(X_{2})\{g(X_{4},X_{5})-ng(X_{4},X_{5})\}-A(X_{2})S(X_{4},X_{5})

\displaystyle\hskip 22.76228pt+R(X_{A},X_{4},X_{2},X_{5})+G(X_{B},X_{4},X_{2},X_{5})=0

\displaystyle\Rightarrow A(X_{2})S(X_{4},X_{5})+(n-1)B(X_{2})g(X_{4},X_{5})=0

\displaystyle\Rightarrow rA(X_{2})+n(n-1)B(X_{2})=0.

Now, using this value in (
2.2
), we get

\displaystyle A(X_{1})\left[R(X_{2},X_{5},X_{3},X_{4})-\frac{r}{n(n-1)}G(X_{2},X_{5},X_{3},X_{4})\right]

\displaystyle+A(X_{2})\left[R(X_{1},X_{5},X_{3},X_{4})-\frac{r}{n(n-1)}G(X_{1},X_{5},X_{3},X_{4})\right]=0.

Now by virtue of the Lemma

, one can easily bring out either
A=0
or
M
is of constant curvature. Moreover for the case of
A=0
, we
have
B=-\frac{r}{n(n-1)}A=0
.

Proposition 2.3
.

Let us suppose that on a semi-Riemannian manifold
M

(2.3)

\displaystyle A(X_{1})R(X_{2},X_{5},X_{3},X_{4})+A(X_{2})R(X_{1},X_{5},X_{3},X_{4})

\displaystyle+B(X_{1})H(X_{2},X_{5},X_{3},X_{4})+B(X_{2})H(X_{1},X_{5},X_{3},X_{4})

\displaystyle+D(X_{1})G(X_{2},X_{5},X_{3},X_{4})+D(X_{2})G(X_{1},X_{5},X_{3},X_{4})=0

holds, where
H=g\wedge S
. Then either

is of constant curvature or
conformally flat or
A=B=D=0
or
A=\frac{r}{n}B+D=0
.

Proof: Contracting (
2.3
) over
X_{1}
and
X_{2}
, we get

R(X_{A},X_{5},X_{3},X_{4})+H(X_{B},X_{5},X_{3},X_{4})+G(X_{D},X_{5},X_{3},X_{4})=0,

where
X_{A},X_{B},X_{D}
denote the associated vector fields of
A
,
B
and
D

respectively. Again contracting (
2.3
) over
X_{1}
and
X_{3}
,
we obtain

\displaystyle D(X_{2})(1-n)g(X_{4},X_{5})+B(X_{2})\{(2-n)S(X_{4},X_{5})-rg(X_{4},X_{5})\}-A(X_{2})S(X_{4},X_{5})

\displaystyle+R(X_{A},X_{4},X_{2},X_{5})+H(X_{B},X_{4},X_{2},X_{5})+G(X_{D},X_{4},X_{2},X_{5})=0,

which gives

(2.4)

\displaystyle\ \{A(X_{2})+(n-2)D(X_{2})\}S(X_{4},X_{5})=\{(1-n)B(X_{2})-rD(X_{2})\}g(X_{4},X_{5}).

Now two cases arise

Case 1: Let
A+(n-2)D\neq 0
. Then the manifold becomes Einstein and hence (

\displaystyle A(X_{1})R(X_{2},X_{5},X_{3},X_{4})+\left\{\frac{r}{n}B(X_{1})+D(X_{1})\right\}G(X_{2},X_{5},X_{3},X_{4})

\displaystyle+A(X_{2})R(X_{1},X_{5},X_{3},X_{4})+\left\{\frac{r}{n}B(X_{2})+D(X_{2})\right\}G(X_{1},X_{5},X_{3},X_{4})=0.

Thus using Proposition
2.3

Case 2: Let

2.4

nB+D=0

in terms of

(2.5)

\displaystyle D(X_{1})C(X_{2},X_{5},X_{3},X_{4})+D(X_{2})C(X_{1},X_{5},X_{3},X_{4})=0.

Thus using Lemma

M
is of conformally
flat or
D=0
. Moreover then
A=(2-n)D=0
and
B=-\frac{1}{n}D=0

R(X_{1},X_{2},X_{3},X_{4})=-R(X_{2},X_{1},X_{3},X_{4})

\displaystyle\{B(X_{1})-\bar{B}(X_{1})\}R(X_{2},X_{5},X_{3},X_{4})+\{B(X_{2})-\bar{B}(X_{2})\}R(X_{1},X_{5},X_{3},X_{4})

\displaystyle(\nabla_{X_{5}}R)(X_{1},X_{2},X_{3},X_{4})=A(X_{5})R(X_{1},X_{2},X_{3},X_{4})

\displaystyle+D(X_{3})R(X_{1},X_{2},X_{5},X_{4})+D(X_{4})R(X_{1},X_{2},X_{3},X_{5})

\displaystyle+\alpha(X_{5})H(X_{1},X_{2},X_{3},X_{4})+\beta(X_{1})H(X_{5},X_{2},X_{3},X_{4})

\displaystyle+\beta(X_{2})H(X_{1},X_{5},X_{3},X_{4})+\gamma(X_{3})H(X_{1},X_{2},X_{5},X_{4})

\displaystyle+\gamma(X_{4})H(X_{1},X_{2},X_{3},X_{5})+\theta(X_{5})G(X_{1},X_{2},X_{3},X_{4})

\displaystyle+\phi(X_{1})G(X_{5},X_{2},X_{3},X_{4})+\phi(X_{2})G(X_{1},X_{5},X_{3},X_{4})

\displaystyle+\psi(X_{3})G(X_{1},X_{2},X_{5},X_{4})+\psi(X_{4})G(X_{1},X_{2},X_{3},X_{5}).

Now contracting (
3.1

(3.2)

\displaystyle(\nabla_{X_{5}}S)(X_{2},\ X_{3})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle f_{1}(X_{5})S(X_{2},\ X_{3})+f_{2}(X_{2})S(X_{5},\ X_{3})+f_{3}(X_{3})S(X_{2},X_{5})

\displaystyle-\beta(LX_{2})g(X_{5},\ X_{3})-\gamma(LX_{3})g(X_{5},\ X_{2}),
