Connection matrices on the Siegel-Jacobi upper half space and extended Siegel-Jacobi upper half space
Elena Mirela Babalic, Stefan Berceanu

Abstract
. The inverse of the metric matrices on the Siegel-Jacobi upper half space 𝒳 n J {{\mathcal{X}}}^{J}_{n} , invariant to the restricted real Jacobi group G n J ​ ( ℝ ) 0 G^{J}_{n}(\mathbb{R})_{0} and extended Siegel-Jacobi 𝒳 ~ n J \tilde{{{\mathcal{X}}}}^{J}_{n} upper half space, invariant to the action of the real Jacobi G n J ​ ( ℝ ) G^{J}_{n}(\mathbb{R}) , are presented. The results are relevant for Berezin quantization of the manifolds 𝒳 n J {{\mathcal{X}}}^{J}_{n} and 𝒳 ~ n J \tilde{{\mathcal{X}}}^{J}_{n} . Explicit calculations in the case n = 2 n=2 are given.
