Superconducting Orbital Magnetoelectric Effect and its Evolution across the Superconductor-Normal Metal Phase Transition
Wen-Yu He, K. T. Law

Abstract
Superconducting magnetoelectric effect, which is the current-induced magnetization in a superconductor, mainly focused on the spin magnetization in previous studies, but ignore the effect of the orbital magnetic moments carried by the paired Bloch electrons. In this work, we show that orbital magnetic moments in superconductors can induce large orbital magnetization in the presence of a current. We constructed a unified description for the current-induced spin and orbital magnetization across the superconductor-normal metal phase transition. We find that in a superconductor with uniform pairing, the current-induced magnetization at a given current density is the same as that in its normal metal state, while with the nonuniform superconducting pairing, the current-induced magnetization exhibits an abrupt change in magnitude near the superconductor-normal metal phase transition. Importantly, our theory predicts the orbital magnetoelectric effect in superconducting twisted bilayer graphene which has paired Bloch electrons with large orbital magnetic moments and negligible spin-orbit coupling. We propose that the measurement of the current-induced orbital magnetoelectric effect can be used to detect the possible nonuniform pairings in twisted bilayer graphene and other newly discovered superconductors with non-trivial Berry curvatures.

Bogliubov-de Gennes Hamiltonian and the Green’s Function

The generic Hamiltonian that can describe the normal state of a system takes the form

\displaystyle\mathcal{H}_{0}=\sum_{\nu,\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}c^{\dagger}_{\nu,\bm{k}}H_{0,\nu\nu^{\prime}}c_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}},

(S1)

with
c^{\dagger}_{\nu,\bm{k}}\left(c_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)
being the creation (or annihilation) operator,
H_{0,\nu\nu^{\prime}}\left(\bm{k}\right)
being the element of the Hamiltonian matrix
H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)
, and
\nu=1,2,3,\dots
denoting the spin and orbital index of the system. The Bogliubov-de Gennes Hamiltonian that can describe the superconductivity state of a system is

\displaystyle\mathcal{H}=\frac{1}{2}\sum_{\bm{k}}\begin{pmatrix}c^{\dagger}_{\bm{k}}&c_{-\bm{k}}\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)&\hat{\Delta}\left(\bm{k}\right)\\
\hat{\Delta}^{\dagger}\left(\bm{k}\right)&-H^{\ast}_{0}\left(-\bm{k}\right)\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}c_{\bm{k}}\\
c^{\dagger}_{-\bm{k}}\end{pmatrix},

(S2)

where
c_{\bm{k}}=\left[c_{1,\bm{k}},c_{2,\bm{k}},c_{3,\bm{k}},\dots\right]^{\textrm{T}}
,
c^{\dagger}_{\bm{k}}=\left[c^{\dagger}_{1,\bm{k}},c^{\dagger}_{2,\bm{k}},c^{\dagger}_{3,\bm{k}},\dots\right]
, and
\hat{\Delta}\left(\bm{k}\right)
is the pairing matrix. We know that the normal state Hamiltonian
H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)
can be diagonalized by an unitary transformation
U\left(\bm{k}\right)
as

\displaystyle U\left(\bm{k}\right)H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)U^{\dagger}\left(\bm{k}\right)=\textrm{diag}\left[\xi_{1,\bm{k}},\xi_{2,\bm{k}},\dots,\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right],

(S3)

with
U^{\dagger}\left(\bm{k}\right)U\left(\bm{k}\right)=1
and the corresponding eigen states
\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}=U_{\nu\nu^{\prime}}\left(\bm{k}\right)c_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}
,
\phi^{\dagger}_{\nu,\bm{k}}=c^{\dagger}_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}U^{\ast}_{\nu^{\prime}\nu}\left(\bm{k}\right)
. Applying the unitary transformation to the Bogliubov-de Gennes Hamiltonian then yields

\displaystyle\begin{pmatrix}U_{\bm{k}}&0\\
0&U^{\ast}_{-\bm{k}}\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)&\hat{\Delta}_{\bm{k}}\\
\hat{\Delta}^{\dagger}_{\bm{k}}&-H^{\ast}_{0}\left(-\bm{k}\right)\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}U^{\dagger}_{\bm{k}}&0\\
0&U^{\textrm{T}}_{-\bm{k}}\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}U_{\bm{k}}H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)U^{\dagger}_{\bm{k}}&U_{\bm{k}}\hat{\Delta}_{\bm{k}}U^{\textrm{T}}_{-\bm{k}}\\
U^{\ast}_{-\bm{k}}\hat{\Delta}^{\dagger}_{\bm{k}}U^{\dagger}_{\bm{k}}&-U^{\ast}_{-\bm{k}}H^{\ast}_{0}\left(-\bm{k}\right)U^{\textrm{T}}_{-\bm{k}}\end{pmatrix}.

(S4)

Importantly, after the unitary transformation, we obtain

\displaystyle U_{\bm{k}}H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)U^{\dagger}_{\bm{k}}=\textrm{diag}\left[\xi_{1,\bm{k}},\xi_{2,\bm{k}},...,\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right],\quad\quad\quad-U^{\ast}_{-\bm{k}}H_{0}^{\ast}\left(-\bm{k}\right)U^{\textrm{T}}_{-\bm{k}}=\textrm{diag}\left[-\xi_{1,-\bm{k}},-\xi_{2,-\bm{k}},...,-\xi_{\nu,-\bm{k}}\right].

(S5)

In such eigen-band basis
\left[\phi^{\dagger}_{1,\bm{k}},\phi^{\dagger}_{2,\bm{k}},...,\phi^{\dagger}_{\nu,\bm{k}},\phi_{1,-\bm{k}},\phi_{2,-\bm{k}},...,\phi_{\nu,-\bm{k}}\right]
, the intraband pairing
\left\langle\phi_{\nu,-\bm{k}}\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right\rangle
gives an energetically favourable pairing phase, while other interband pairing will require extra attractive interaction to overcome the energy (momentum) difference
Sigrist
. In the energetically favourable pairing phase, the pairing matrix respects
H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)\hat{\Delta}_{\bm{k}}-\hat{\Delta}_{\bm{k}}H^{\ast}_{0}\left(-\bm{k}\right)=0

Sigrist
, and the pairing matrix is therefore diagonalized simultaneously by the unitary transformation as

\displaystyle U_{\bm{k}}\hat{\Delta}_{\bm{k}}U^{\textrm{T}}_{-\bm{k}}=\textrm{diag}\left[\Delta_{1,\bm{k}},\Delta_{2,\bm{k}},...,\Delta_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right],\quad\quad\quad U^{\ast}_{-\bm{k}}\hat{\Delta}_{\bm{k}}U^{\dagger}_{\bm{k}}=\textrm{diag}\left[\Delta^{\ast}_{1,\bm{k}},\Delta^{\ast}_{2,\bm{k}},...,\Delta^{\ast}_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right].

(S6)

As a result, the Bogliubov-de Gennes Hamiltonian in the eigen-band basis takes the form

\displaystyle\begin{pmatrix}U_{\bm{k}}&0\\
0&U^{\ast}_{-\bm{k}}\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)&\hat{\Delta}_{\bm{k}}\\
\hat{\Delta}^{\dagger}_{\bm{k}}&-H^{\ast}_{0}\left(-\bm{k}\right)\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}U^{\dagger}_{\bm{k}}&0\\
0&U^{\textrm{T}}_{-\bm{k}}\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}\xi_{1,\bm{k}}&0&0&0&\Delta_{1,\bm{k}}&0&0&0\\
0&\xi_{2,\bm{k}}&0&0&0&\Delta_{2,\bm{k}}&0&0\\
0&0&\ddots&0&0&0&\ddots&0\\
0&0&0&\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}&0&0&0&\Delta_{\nu,\bm{k}}\\
\Delta^{\ast}_{1,\bm{k}}&0&0&0&-\xi_{1,-\bm{k}}&0&0&0\\
0&\Delta^{\ast}_{2,\bm{k}}&0&0&0&-\xi_{2,-\bm{k}}&0&0\\
0&0&\ddots&0&0&0&\ddots&0\\
0&0&0&\Delta^{\ast}_{\nu,\bm{k}}&0&0&0&-\xi_{\nu,-\bm{k}}\end{pmatrix}.

(S7)

We define the Green’s function for the Bogliubov-de Gennes Hamiltonian as

\displaystyle G^{-1}_{0}\left(\bm{k},i\omega_{n}\right)=\begin{pmatrix}i\omega_{n}-H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)&-\hat{\Delta}_{\bm{k}}\\
-\hat{\Delta}_{\bm{k}}&i\omega_{n}+H^{\ast}_{0}\left(-\bm{k}\right)\end{pmatrix},\quad\textrm{and}\quad G_{0}\left(\bm{k},i\omega_{n}\right)=\begin{pmatrix}G_{e}\left(\bm{k},i\omega_{n}\right)&F\left(\bm{k},i\omega_{n}\right)\\
F^{\dagger}\left(\bm{k},i\omega_{n}\right)&G_{h}\left(\bm{k},i\omega_{n}\right)\end{pmatrix}

(S8)

and then we apply the unitary transformation to the Green’s function:

\displaystyle\begin{pmatrix}U_{\bm{k}}&0\\
0&U^{\ast}_{-\bm{k}}\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}G_{e}\left(\bm{k},i\omega_{n}\right)&F\left(\bm{k},i\omega_{n}\right)\\
F^{\dagger}\left(\bm{k},i\omega_{n}\right)&G_{h}\left(\bm{k},i\omega_{n}\right)\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}U^{\dagger}_{\bm{k}}&0\\
0&U^{\textrm{T}}_{-\bm{k}}\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}U_{\bm{k}}G_{e}\left(\bm{k},i\omega_{n}\right)U^{\dagger}_{\bm{k}}&U_{\bm{k}}F\left(\bm{k},i\omega_{n}\right)U^{\textrm{T}}_{-\bm{k}}\\
U^{\ast}_{-\bm{k}}F^{\dagger}\left(\bm{k},i\omega_{n}\right)U^{\dagger}_{\bm{k}}&U^{\ast}_{-\bm{k}}G_{h}\left(\bm{k},i\omega_{n}\right)U^{\textrm{T}}_{-\bm{k}}\end{pmatrix},

(S9)

which gives

\displaystyle U_{\bm{k}}G_{e}\left(\bm{k},i\omega_{n}\right)U^{\dagger}_{\bm{k}}

\displaystyle=\begin{pmatrix}-\frac{i\omega_{n}+\xi_{1,\bm{k}}}{\omega_{n}^{2}+\xi^{2}_{1,\bm{k}}+\Delta_{1,\bm{k}}\Delta^{\ast}_{1,\bm{k}}}&0&0&0\\
0&-\frac{i\omega_{n}+\xi_{2,\bm{k}}}{\omega_{n}^{2}+\xi^{2}_{2,\bm{k}}+\Delta_{2,\bm{k}}\Delta^{\ast}_{2,\bm{k}}}&0&0\\
0&0&...&0\\
0&0&0&-\frac{i\omega_{n}+\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}{\omega_{n}^{2}+\xi^{2}_{\nu,\bm{k}}+\Delta_{\nu,\bm{k}}\Delta^{\ast}_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\end{pmatrix},

(S10)

\displaystyle U_{\bm{k}}F\left(\bm{k},i\omega_{n}\right)U^{\textrm{T}}_{-\bm{k}}

\displaystyle=\begin{pmatrix}-\frac{\Delta_{1,\bm{k}}}{\omega_{n}^{2}+\xi^{2}_{1,\bm{k}}+\Delta_{1,\bm{k}}\Delta^{\ast}_{1,\bm{k}}}&0&0&0\\
0&-\frac{\Delta_{2,\bm{k}}}{\omega_{n}^{2}+\xi^{2}_{2,\bm{k}}+\Delta_{2,\bm{k}}\Delta^{\ast}_{2,\bm{k}}}&0&0\\
0&0&...&0\\
0&0&0&-\frac{\Delta_{\nu,\bm{k}}}{\omega_{n}^{2}+\xi^{2}_{\nu,\bm{k}}+\Delta_{\nu,\bm{k}}\Delta^{\ast}_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\end{pmatrix},

(S11)

\displaystyle U^{\ast}_{-\bm{k}}F^{\dagger}\left(\bm{k},i\omega_{n}\right)U^{\dagger}_{\bm{k}}

\displaystyle=\begin{pmatrix}-\frac{\Delta^{\ast}_{1,\bm{k}}}{\omega_{n}^{2}+\xi^{2}_{1,\bm{k}}+\Delta_{1,\bm{k}}\Delta^{\ast}_{1,\bm{k}}}&0&0&0\\
0&-\frac{\Delta^{\ast}_{2,\bm{k}}}{\omega_{n}^{2}+\xi^{2}_{2,\bm{k}}+\Delta_{2,\bm{k}}\Delta^{\ast}_{2,\bm{k}}}&0&0\\
0&0&...&0\\
0&0&0&-\frac{\Delta^{\ast}_{\nu,\bm{k}}}{\omega_{n}^{2}+\xi^{2}_{\nu,\bm{k}}+\Delta_{\nu,\bm{k}}\Delta^{\ast}_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\end{pmatrix},

(S12)

\displaystyle U^{\ast}_{-\bm{k}}G_{h}\left(\bm{k},i\omega_{n}\right)U^{\textrm{T}}_{-\bm{k}}

\displaystyle=\begin{pmatrix}-\frac{i\omega_{n}-\xi_{1,\bm{k}}}{\omega_{n}^{2}+\xi^{2}_{1,\bm{k}}+\Delta_{1,\bm{k}}\Delta^{\ast}_{1,\bm{k}}}&0&0&0\\
0&-\frac{i\omega_{n}-\xi_{2,\bm{k}}}{\omega_{n}^{2}+\xi^{2}_{2,\bm{k}}+\Delta_{2,\bm{k}}\Delta^{\ast}_{2,\bm{k}}}&0&0\\
0&0&...&0\\
0&0&0&-\frac{i\omega_{n}-\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}{\omega_{n}^{2}+\xi^{2}_{\nu,\bm{k}}+\Delta_{\nu,\bm{k}}\Delta^{\ast}_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\end{pmatrix}.

(S13)

II
Linear Response Theory for the current-induced Magnetization

Applying current introduces a perturbation

\displaystyle\delta\mathcal{H}=

\displaystyle-\sum_{\bm{k},\bm{q}}c^{\dagger}_{\bm{k}-\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}c_{\bm{k}+\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}\left[\frac{e}{\hbar}\partial_{k_{i}}H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)A_{i}\left(-\bm{q},t\right)-\frac{e^{2}}{2\hbar^{2}}\partial^{2}_{k_{i}k_{j}}H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)A_{i}\left(\bm{q},t\right)A_{j}\left(-\bm{q},t\right)\right]

(S14)

to the Bogliubov-de Gennes Hamiltonian, so the free-energy density of the system gets changed. The change of free-energy density can be obtained through expanding the electromagnetic gauge fields, and it can be expressed as

\displaystyle\Delta F=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\sum_{\bm{q},m}A_{i}\left(-\bm{q},-i\omega_{m}\right)\Pi_{ij}\left(\bm{q},i\omega_{m}\right)A_{j}\left(\bm{q},i\omega_{m}\right)

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{2}\sum_{\bm{q},m}B_{i}\left(-\bm{q},-i\omega_{m}\right)T^{\textrm{s}}_{ij}\left(\bm{q},i\omega_{m}\right)A_{j}\left(\bm{q},i\omega_{m}\right)+\frac{1}{2}\sum_{\bm{q},m}A_{i}\left(-\bm{q},-i\omega_{m}\right)\tilde{T}_{ij}^{\textrm{s}}\left(\bm{q},i\omega_{m}\right)B_{j}\left(\bm{q},i\omega_{m}\right).

(S15)

Here, the terms
T^{\textrm{s}}_{ij}\left(\bm{q},i\omega_{m}\right)
and
\tilde{T}^{\textrm{s}}_{ij}\left(\bm{q},i\omega_{m}\right)
, where

\displaystyle T_{ij}^{\textrm{s}}\left(\bm{q},i\omega_{m}\right)=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\beta V}\sum_{\bm{k},n}\textrm{tr}\left[G_{e}\left(\bm{k}-\frac{1}{2}\bm{q},i\omega_{n}\right)\frac{1}{2}\mu_{\textrm{B}}g\sigma_{i}G_{e}\left(\bm{k}+\frac{1}{2}\bm{q},i\omega_{n}+i\omega_{m}\right)\frac{e}{\hbar}\frac{\partial H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)}{\partial k_{j}}\right.

\displaystyle\left.-F\left(\bm{k}-\frac{1}{2}\bm{q},i\omega_{n}\right)\frac{1}{2}\mu_{\textrm{B}}g\sigma_{i}^{\ast}F^{\dagger}\left(\bm{k}+\frac{1}{2}\bm{q},i\omega_{n}+i\omega_{m}\right)\frac{e}{\hbar}\frac{\partial H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)}{\partial k_{j}}\right],

(S16)

\displaystyle\tilde{T}_{ij}^{\textrm{s}}\left(\bm{q},i\omega_{m}\right)=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\beta V}\sum_{\bm{k},n}\textrm{tr}\left[G_{e}\left(\bm{k}-\frac{1}{2}\bm{q},i\omega_{n}\right)\frac{e}{\hbar}\frac{\partial H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)}{\partial k_{i}}G_{e}\left(\bm{k}+\frac{1}{2}\bm{q},i\omega_{n}+i\omega_{m}\right)\frac{1}{2}\mu_{\textrm{B}}g\sigma_{j}\right.

\displaystyle\left.-F\left(\bm{k}-\frac{1}{2}\bm{q},i\omega_{n}\right)\frac{e}{\hbar}\frac{\partial H^{\ast}_{0}\left(-\bm{k}\right)}{-\partial k_{i}}F^{\dagger}_{0}\left(\bm{k}+\frac{1}{2}\bm{q},i\omega_{n}+i\omega_{m}\right)\frac{1}{2}\mu_{\textrm{B}}g\sigma_{j}\right].

(S17)

give the spin magnetoelectric susceptibility in both the superconductivity and normal metal regions, while the polarization tensor
\Pi_{ij}\left(\bm{q},i\omega_{m}\right)
, where

\displaystyle\Pi_{ij}\left(\bm{q},i\omega_{m}\right)=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\beta V}\sum_{\bm{k},n}\textrm{tr}\left[G_{e}\left(\bm{k},i\omega_{n}\right)\frac{e^{2}}{\hbar^{2}}\frac{\partial^{2}H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)}{\partial k_{i}\partial k_{j}}+G_{e}\left(\bm{k}-\frac{1}{2}\bm{q},i\omega_{n}\right)\frac{e}{\hbar}\frac{\partial H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)}{\partial k_{i}}G_{e}\left(\bm{k}+\frac{1}{2}\bm{q},i\omega_{n}+i\omega_{m}\right)\frac{e}{\hbar}\frac{\partial H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)}{\partial k_{j}}\right.

\displaystyle\left.+F\left(\bm{k}-\frac{1}{2}\bm{q},i\omega_{n}\right)\frac{e}{\hbar}\frac{\partial H^{\ast}_{0}\left(-\bm{k}\right)}{\partial k_{i}}F^{\dagger}\left(\bm{k}+\frac{1}{2}\bm{q},i\omega_{n}+i\omega_{m}\right)\frac{e}{\hbar}\frac{\partial H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)}{\partial k_{j}}\right],

(S18)

is responsible for the current and current-induced orbital magnetization in both the superconductivity and normal metal regions.

II.1
Polarization Tensor, Current, and the Current-induced Orbital Magnetization

Substituting Eq.

,
S11
,
S12
,
S13
into Eq.

, we can obtain the polarization tensor
\Pi_{ij}\left(\bm{q},i\omega_{m}\right)
, where

\displaystyle\Pi_{ij}\left(\bm{q},i\omega_{m}\right)=\Pi_{ij}^{\textrm{sta}}\left(\bm{q}\right)+\Pi_{ij}^{\textrm{dyn}}\left(\bm{q},i\omega_{m}\right),

with the static part
\Pi^{\textrm{sta}}_{ij}\left(\bm{q}\right)

\displaystyle\Pi^{\textrm{sta}}_{ij}\left(\bm{q}\right)=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2V}\frac{e^{2}}{\hbar^{2}}\sum_{\bm{k},\nu}\left[\frac{\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}+\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}{\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}f\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)+\frac{\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}-\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}{\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}f\left(-\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)\right]\bra{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\partial^{2}_{k_{i}k_{j}}H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)\ket{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}

\displaystyle-\frac{1}{2V}\frac{e^{2}}{\hbar^{2}}\sum_{\bm{k},\nu,\nu^{\prime}}\left[\left(1-\frac{\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}-\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}\xi_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}+\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}+\Delta_{\nu,\bm{k}-\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}\Delta^{\ast}_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}+\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}}{\epsilon_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}+\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}-\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}}\right)\frac{1-f\left(\epsilon_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}+\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}\right)-f\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}-\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}\right)}{\epsilon_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}+\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}+\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}-\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}}\right.

\displaystyle\left.-\left(1+\frac{\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}-\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}\xi_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}+\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}+\Delta_{\nu,\bm{k}-\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}\Delta^{\ast}_{\bm{k}+\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}}{\epsilon_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}+\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}-\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}}\right)\frac{f\left(\epsilon_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}+\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}\right)-f\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}-\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}\right)}{\epsilon_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}+\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}-\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}-\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}}\right]

\displaystyle\bra{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}-\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}}\partial_{k_{i}}H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)\ket{\phi_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}+\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}}\bra{\phi_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}+\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}}\partial_{k_{j}}H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)\ket{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}-\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}},

(S19)

and the dynamic part
\Pi^{\textrm{dyn}}_{ij}\left(\bm{q},i\omega_{m}\right)
, where

\displaystyle\Pi_{ij}^{\textrm{dyn}}\left(\bm{q},i\omega_{m}\right)=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{4V}\sum_{\bm{k},\nu,\nu^{\prime}}\left\{\left(1-\frac{\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}-\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}\xi_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}+\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}+\Delta_{\nu,\bm{k}-\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}\Delta^{\ast}_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}+\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}}{\epsilon_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}+\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}-\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}}\right)\left[\frac{i\omega_{m}}{i\omega_{m}+\epsilon_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}+\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}+\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}-\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}}+\frac{i\omega_{m}}{i\omega_{m}-\left(\epsilon_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}+\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}+\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}-\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}\right)}\right]\right.

\displaystyle\left.\frac{1-f\left(\epsilon_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}+\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}\right)-f\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}-\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}\right)}{\epsilon_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}+\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}+\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}-\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}}\right.

\displaystyle\left.-\left(1+\frac{\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}-\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}\xi_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}+\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}+\Delta_{\nu,\bm{k}-\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}\Delta^{\ast}_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}+\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}}{\epsilon_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}+\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}-\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}}\right)\left[\frac{i\omega_{m}}{i\omega_{m}-\left(\epsilon_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}+\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}-\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}-\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}\right)}+\frac{i\omega_{m}}{i\omega_{m}+\epsilon_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}+\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}-\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}-\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}}\right]\right.

\displaystyle\left.\frac{f\left(\epsilon_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}+\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}\right)-f\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}-\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}\right)}{\epsilon_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}+\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}-\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}-\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}}\right\}\bra{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}-\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}}\frac{e}{\hbar}\partial_{k_{i}}H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)\ket{\phi_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}+\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}}\bra{\phi_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}+\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}}\frac{e}{\hbar}\partial_{k_{j}}H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)\ket{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}-\frac{1}{2}\bm{q}}}.

(S20)

The static and dynamic part of the polarization tensor can then be expanded in terms of
\bm{q}
to the linear order as

\displaystyle\Pi_{ij}^{\textrm{sta}}\left(\bm{q}\right)=

\displaystyle\Pi_{ij}^{\textrm{sta},\left(0\right)}+Q^{\textrm{sta}}_{ijl}q_{l}+\mathcal{O}\left(\bm{q}^{2}\right),\quad\Pi_{ij}^{\textrm{dyn}}\left(\bm{q},i\omega_{m}\right)=\Pi_{ij}^{\textrm{dyn},\left(0\right)}+Q^{\textrm{dyn}}_{ijl}q_{l}+\mathcal{O}\left(\bm{q}^{2}\right),

(S21)

with

\displaystyle Q^{\textrm{sta}}_{ijl}=\lim_{\bm{q}\rightarrow 0}\frac{\Pi^{\textrm{sta}}_{ij}\left(\bm{q}\right)-\Pi^{\textrm{sta}}_{ij}\left(0\right)}{q_{l}},\quad Q^{\textrm{dyn}}_{ijl}=\lim_{\bm{q}\rightarrow 0}\frac{\Pi^{\textrm{dyn}}_{ij}\left(\bm{q}\right)-\Pi^{\textrm{dyn}}_{ij}\left(0\right)}{q_{l}}.

(S22)

The intra-band contribution to
\Pi_{ij}^{\textrm{sta},\left(0\right)}
is

\displaystyle\Pi^{\textrm{sta},\left(0\right),\textrm{intra}}_{ij}=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2V}\frac{e^{2}}{\hbar^{2}}\sum_{\bm{k},\nu}\bra{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\partial^{2}_{k_{i}k_{j}}H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)\left[\frac{\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}+\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}{\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}f\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)+\frac{\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}-\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}{\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}f\left(-\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)\right]

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{V}\frac{e^{2}}{\hbar^{2}}\sum_{\bm{k},\nu}\bra{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\partial_{k_{i}}H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)\ket{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\bra{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\partial_{k_{j}}H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)\ket{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\frac{df\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}

\displaystyle\approx\frac{1}{V}\frac{e^{2}}{\hbar^{2}}\sum_{\bm{k},\nu}\left[\bra{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\partial^{2}_{k_{i}k_{j}}H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)\ket{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}f\left(\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)+\bra{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\partial_{k_{i}}H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)\ket{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\bra{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\partial_{k_{j}}H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)\ket{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\frac{df\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\right].

For the inter-band contribution, we assume that the pairing gap
|\Delta_{\nu,\bm{k}}|
is much smaller than the inversion symmetry breaking-induced band splitting, so the inter-band term can be approximated by that used in the normal metal phase with
\Delta_{\nu,\bm{k}}=0

\displaystyle\Pi^{\textrm{sta},\left(0\right),\textrm{inter}}_{ij}=

\displaystyle-\frac{1}{2V}\frac{e^{2}}{\hbar^{2}}\sum_{\bm{k},\nu\neq\nu^{\prime}}\left[\left(1-\frac{\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}\xi_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}+\Delta_{\nu,\bm{k}}\Delta^{\ast}_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}}{\epsilon_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\right)\frac{1-f\left(\epsilon_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}\right)-f\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{\epsilon_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}+\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\right.

\displaystyle\left.-\left(1+\frac{\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}\xi_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}+\Delta_{\nu,\bm{k}}\Delta^{\ast}_{\bm{k}}}{\epsilon_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\right)\frac{f\left(\epsilon_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}\right)-f\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{\epsilon_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}-\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\right]\bra{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\partial_{k_{i}}H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)\ket{\phi_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}}\bra{\phi_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}}\partial_{k_{j}}H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)\ket{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}

\displaystyle\approx

\displaystyle\frac{1}{V}\frac{e^{2}}{\hbar^{2}}\sum_{\bm{k},\nu\neq\nu^{\prime}}\frac{f\left(\xi_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}\right)-f\left(\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{\xi_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}-\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\bra{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\partial_{k_{i}}H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)\ket{\phi_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}}\bra{\phi_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}}\partial_{k_{j}}H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)\ket{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}.

(S23)

We also know from the gauge invariance that

\displaystyle\frac{1}{V}\frac{e^{2}}{\hbar^{2}}\sum_{\bm{k},\nu\neq\nu^{\prime}}\frac{f\left(\xi_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}\right)-f\left(\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{\xi_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}-\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\bra{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\partial_{k_{i}}H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)\ket{\phi_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}}\bra{\phi_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}}\partial_{k_{j}}H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)\ket{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\frac{1}{V}\frac{e^{2}}{\hbar^{2}}\sum_{\bm{k},\nu}\left[\bra{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\partial_{k_{i}}H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)\ket{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\bra{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\partial_{k_{j}}H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)\ket{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\frac{df\left(\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}+\bra{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\partial^{2}_{k_{i}k_{j}}H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)\ket{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}f\left(\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)\right],

(S24)

so eventually we obtain the
\Pi_{ij}^{\textrm{sta},\left(0\right)}
, where

\displaystyle\Pi_{ij}^{\textrm{sta},\left(0\right)}=

\displaystyle\Pi^{\textrm{sta},\left(0\right),\textrm{intra}}_{ij}+\Pi^{\textrm{sta},\left(0\right),\textrm{inter}}_{ij}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{V}\frac{e^{2}}{\hbar^{2}}\sum_{\nu,\bm{k}}\bra{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\partial_{k_{i}}H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)\ket{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\bra{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\partial_{k_{j}}H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)\ket{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\left[\frac{df\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}-\frac{df\left(\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\right]

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{e^{2}}{\hbar^{2}}\frac{1}{\left(2\pi\right)^{d}}\int_{\textrm{BZ}}d\bm{k}\sum_{\nu}v_{\nu,\bm{k},i}v_{\nu,\bm{k},j}\left[\frac{df\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}-\frac{df\left(\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\right],

(S25)

with the group velocity
\bm{v}_{\nu,\bm{k}}=\nabla_{\bm{k}}\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}=\bra{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\nabla_{\bm{k}}H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)\ket{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}
. For the term
\Pi_{ij}^{\textrm{dyn},\left(0\right)}
that is
i\omega_{m}
dependent, we consider the dominant intra-band contribution and take the analytic continuation
i\omega_{m}\rightarrow\hbar\omega+i\hbar\tau^{-1}
to get

\displaystyle\Pi_{ij}^{\textrm{dyn},\left(0\right)}=\frac{e^{2}}{\hbar^{2}}\frac{i\omega\tau}{1-i\omega\tau}\sum_{\nu}\int_{\textrm{BZ}}v_{\nu,\bm{k},i}v_{\nu,\bm{k},j}\frac{df\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\frac{d\bm{k}}{\left(2\pi\right)^{d}}.

(S26)

The intra-band contribution to
Q^{\textrm{sta}}_{ijl}

Moore
;
JingMa
is

\displaystyle Q^{\textrm{sta},\textrm{intra}}_{ijl}=

\displaystyle\frac{i}{V}\frac{e}{\hbar}\sum_{\nu,\bm{k}}\frac{df\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\left(\partial_{k_{i}}\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}\epsilon_{dlj}m_{\nu,\bm{k},d}-\partial_{k_{j}}\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}\epsilon_{dli}m_{\nu,\bm{k},d}\right),

(S27)

with
\bm{m}_{\nu,\bm{k}}=\frac{ie}{2\hbar}\bra{\partial_{\bm{k}}\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\times\left[H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)-\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right]\ket{\partial_{\bm{k}}\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}
being the orbital magnetic moment. For the inter-band contribution, given that the pairing gap
|\Delta_{\nu,\bm{k}}|
is much smaller than the band splitting, it can also be approximated by that used in the normal metal phase in a way that is similar to Eq.
II.1
. We know that the summation of the intra-band and inter-band static terms in the normal metal phase gives
Q_{ijl}^{\textrm{sta}}=\frac{e^{2}}{\hbar^{2}}\int_{\textrm{BZ}}\frac{d\bm{k}}{\left(2\pi\right)^{d}}\sum_{\nu}2f\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)\nabla_{\bm{k}}\cdot\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\bm{\Omega}_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)
, which is confirmed to be zero
Moore
;
JingMa
. As a result, the inter-band contribution to
Q^{\textrm{sta}}_{ijl}
is

\displaystyle Q^{\textrm{sta},\textrm{inter}}_{ijl}=

\displaystyle-\frac{i}{V}\frac{e}{\hbar}\sum_{\nu,\bm{k}}\frac{df\left(\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\left(\partial_{k_{i}}\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}\epsilon_{dlj}m_{\nu,\bm{k},d}-\partial_{k_{j}}\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}\epsilon_{dli}m_{\nu,\bm{k},d}\right).

(S28)

For the dynamic part
Q_{ijl}^{\textrm{dyn}}
, we consider the dominant intra-band term and use the analytical continuation
i\omega_{m}\rightarrow\hbar\omega+i\hbar\tau^{-1}
as well, and then we can obtain

\displaystyle Q_{ijl}^{\textrm{dyn}}=

\displaystyle-\frac{1}{V}\frac{e}{\hbar}\frac{\omega\tau}{1-i\omega\tau}\sum_{\bm{k},\nu}\frac{df\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\left(\partial_{k_{i}}\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}\epsilon_{dlj}m_{\nu,\bm{k},d}-\partial_{k_{j}}\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}\epsilon_{dli}m_{\nu,\bm{k},d}\right).

(S29)

Eventually, the polarization tensor
\Pi_{ij}\left(\bm{q},\omega\right)
can be approximated:

\displaystyle\Pi_{ij}\left(\bm{q},\omega\right)\approx

\displaystyle\Pi_{ij}^{\textrm{sta},\left(0\right)}+Q^{\textrm{sta}}_{ijl}q_{l}+\Pi_{ij}^{\textrm{dyn},\left(0\right)}+Q^{\textrm{dyn}}_{ijl}q_{l}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{e^{2}}{\hbar^{2}}\int_{\textrm{BZ}}\frac{d\bm{k}}{\left(2\pi\right)^{d}}\sum_{\nu}v_{\nu,\bm{k},i}v_{\nu,\bm{k},j}\left[\frac{df\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}-\frac{df\left(\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\right]+\frac{e^{2}}{\hbar^{2}}\frac{i\omega\tau}{1-i\omega\tau}\int_{\textrm{BZ}}\frac{d\bm{k}}{\left(2\pi\right)^{d}}\sum_{\nu}v_{\nu,\bm{k},i}v_{\nu,\bm{k},j}\frac{df\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}

\displaystyle+\frac{i}{V}\frac{e}{\hbar}\sum_{\nu,\bm{k}}\left(v_{\nu,\bm{k},i}\epsilon_{dlj}m_{\nu,\bm{k},d}-v_{\nu,\bm{k},j}\epsilon_{dli}m_{\nu,\bm{k},d}\right)\left[\frac{df\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}-\frac{df\left(\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\right]

\displaystyle-\frac{1}{V}\frac{e}{\hbar}\frac{\omega\tau}{1-i\omega\tau}\sum_{\nu,\bm{k}}\left(v_{\nu,\bm{k},i}\epsilon_{dlj}m_{\nu,\bm{k},d}-v_{\nu,\bm{k},j}\epsilon_{dli}m_{\nu,\bm{k},d}\right)\frac{df\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}.

(S30)

From Eq.
II
, we can then obtain the current density:

\displaystyle J_{i}=

\displaystyle-\frac{\partial\Delta F}{\partial A_{i}}=-\frac{e^{2}}{\hbar^{2}}\sum_{\nu}\int_{\textrm{BZ}}v_{\nu,\bm{k},i}v_{\nu,\bm{k},j}\left\{\frac{\tau}{1-i\omega\tau}\frac{df\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}E_{j}+\left[\frac{df\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}-\frac{df\left(\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\right]A_{j}\right\},

(S31)

Interestingly, we notice that the free energy density related to
Q^{\textrm{sta}}_{ijl}
and
Q^{\textrm{dyn}}_{ijl}
can be further simplified as

\displaystyle\Delta F_{Q}=

\displaystyle-\frac{i}{V}\frac{e}{\hbar}\sum_{\nu,\bm{k},\bm{q},m}\frac{1}{2}A_{i}\left(-\bm{q},-i\omega_{m}\right)\left[\frac{df\left(\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}-\frac{df\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\right]\left(v_{\nu,\bm{k},i}m_{\nu,\bm{k},d}\epsilon_{dlj}-v_{\nu,\bm{k},j}m_{\nu,\bm{k},d}\epsilon_{dli}\right)q_{l}A_{j}\left(\bm{q},i\omega_{m}\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\frac{1}{2}\sum_{\nu,\bm{q},\omega}B_{i}\left(-\bm{q},-\omega\right)\frac{e}{\hbar}\int_{\textrm{BZ}}\frac{d\bm{k}}{\left(2\pi\right)^{d}}m_{\nu,\bm{k},i}v_{\nu,\bm{k},j}\left[\frac{df\left(\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}-\frac{df\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\right]A_{j}\left(\bm{q},\omega\right)

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{2}\sum_{\nu,\bm{q},\omega}B_{i}\left(-\bm{q},-\omega\right)\frac{e}{\hbar}\frac{i\omega\tau}{1-i\omega\tau}\int_{\textrm{BZ}}\frac{d\bm{k}}{\left(2\pi\right)^{d}}m_{\nu,\bm{k},i}v_{\nu,\bm{k},j}\frac{df\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}A_{j}\left(\bm{q},\omega\right)

\displaystyle-\frac{1}{2}\sum_{\nu,\bm{q},\omega}A_{i}\left(-\bm{q},\omega\right)\frac{e}{\hbar}\int_{\textrm{BZ}}\frac{d\bm{k}}{\left(2\pi\right)^{d}}v_{\nu,\bm{k},i}m_{\nu,\bm{k},j}\left[\frac{df\left(\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}-\frac{df\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\right]B_{j}\left(\bm{q},\omega\right)

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{2}\sum_{\nu,\bm{q},\omega}A_{i}\left(-\bm{q},-\omega\right)\frac{e}{\hbar}\frac{i\omega\tau}{1-i\omega\tau}\int_{\textrm{BZ}}\frac{d\bm{k}}{\left(2\pi\right)^{d}}v_{\nu,\bm{k},i}m_{\nu,\bm{k},j}\frac{df\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}B_{j}\left(\bm{q},\omega\right).

(S32)

Then, we derive the current-induced orbital magnetization:

\displaystyle m_{i}=-\frac{\partial\Delta F}{\partial B_{i}}=-\frac{e}{\hbar}\sum_{\nu}\int_{\textrm{BZ}}m_{\nu,\bm{k},i}v_{\nu,\bm{k},j}\left\{\frac{\tau}{1-i\omega\tau}\frac{df\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}E_{j}+\left[\frac{df\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}-\frac{df\left(\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\right]A_{j}\right\}.

(S33)

Here the electric field has been expressed in terms of the vector gauge potential as
\bm{E}=i\omega\bm{A}
.

II.2
Spin Magnetoelectric Susceptibility Calculation

The spin magnetoelectric susceptibility
T^{\textrm{s}}_{ij}
is calculated as:

\displaystyle T^{\textrm{s}}_{ij}=

\displaystyle-\frac{1}{\beta V}\sum_{\bm{k},n,\nu,\nu^{\prime}}\frac{\left(\omega_{n}^{2}+\omega_{n}\omega_{m}-\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}\xi_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}-\Delta_{\nu,\bm{k}}\Delta^{\ast}_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}\right)\bra{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\frac{1}{2}\mu_{\textrm{B}}\sigma_{i}\ket{\phi_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}}\bra{\phi_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}}\frac{e}{\hbar}\partial_{k_{j}}H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)\ket{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}}{\left[\omega_{n}^{2}+\xi^{2}_{\nu,\bm{k}}+\Delta^{\ast}_{\nu,\bm{k}}\Delta_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right]\left[\left(\omega_{n}+\omega_{m}\right)^{2}+\xi^{2}_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}+\Delta^{\ast}_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}\Delta_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}\right]}.

(S34)

We further sum over the Matsubara frequency and decompose the spin magnetoelectric susceptibility into the static
\left(i\omega_{m}=0\right)
and dynamic
\left(i\omega_{m}\neq 0\right)
parts as

\displaystyle T^{\textrm{s}}_{ij}=

\displaystyle T^{\textrm{s},\textrm{sta}}_{ij}+T^{\textrm{s},\textrm{dyn}}_{ij}\left(i\omega_{m}\right),

(S35)

with

\displaystyle T^{\textrm{s},\textrm{sta}}_{ij}=

\displaystyle-\frac{1}{V}\sum_{\bm{k},\nu,\nu^{\prime}}\left[\frac{1}{2}\left(1-\frac{\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}\xi_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}+\Delta_{\nu,\bm{k}}\Delta^{\ast}_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}}{\epsilon_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\right)\frac{1-f\left(\epsilon_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}\right)-f\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{\epsilon_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}+\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}-\frac{1}{2}\left(1+\frac{\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}\xi_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}+\Delta_{\nu,\bm{k}}\Delta^{\ast}_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}}{\epsilon_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\right)\frac{f\left(\epsilon_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}\right)-f\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{\epsilon_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}-\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\right]

\displaystyle\bra{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\frac{1}{2}\mu_{\textrm{B}}g\sigma_{i}\ket{\phi_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}}\bra{\phi_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}}\frac{e}{\hbar}\partial_{k_{j}}H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)\ket{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}},

(S36)

and

\displaystyle T^{\textrm{s},\textrm{dyn}}_{ij}\left(i\omega_{m}\right)=

\displaystyle-\frac{1}{V}\sum_{\bm{k},\nu,\nu^{\prime}}\left\{\frac{1}{4}\left(1-\frac{\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}\xi_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}+\Delta_{\nu,\bm{k}}\Delta^{\ast}_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}}{\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\epsilon_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}}\right)\left[\frac{i\omega_{m}}{i\omega_{m}+\epsilon_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}+\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}+\frac{i\omega_{m}}{i\omega_{m}-\left(\epsilon_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}+\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}\right]\frac{1-f\left(\epsilon_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}\right)-f\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{\epsilon_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}+\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\right.

\displaystyle\left.\frac{1}{4}\left(1+\frac{\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}\xi_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}+\Delta_{\nu,\bm{k}}\Delta^{\ast}_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}}{\epsilon_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\right)\left[\frac{i\omega_{m}}{i\omega_{m}-\left(\epsilon_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}-\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}+\frac{i\omega_{m}}{i\omega_{m}+\epsilon_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}-\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\right]\frac{f\left(\epsilon_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}\right)-f\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{\epsilon_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}-\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\right\}

\displaystyle\bra{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\frac{1}{2}\mu_{\textrm{B}}g\sigma_{i}\ket{\phi_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}}\bra{\phi_{\nu^{\prime},\bm{k}}}\frac{e}{\hbar}\partial_{k_{j}}H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)\ket{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}.

(S37)

The intra-band contribution to
T^{\textrm{s},\textrm{sta}}_{ij}
is

\displaystyle T^{\textrm{s},\textrm{sta},\textrm{intra}}_{ij}=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{V}\sum_{\bm{k},\nu}\frac{df\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\bra{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\frac{1}{2}\mu_{\textrm{B}}g\sigma_{i}\ket{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\bra{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\frac{e}{\hbar}\partial_{k_{i}}H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)\ket{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}.

(S38)

For the inter-band contribution, similarly, we assume that the pairing gap
|\Delta_{\nu,\bm{k}}|
is much smaller than the band splitting so that it can be approximated by that in the normal metal phase. We also know that the summation of the intra-band and inter-band terms in the static limit will vanish, so the inter-band contribution can be written as

\displaystyle T^{\textrm{s},\textrm{sta},\textrm{inter}}_{ij}=

\displaystyle-\frac{1}{V}\sum_{\bm{k},\nu}\frac{df\left(\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\bra{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\frac{1}{2}\mu_{\textrm{B}}g\sigma_{i}\ket{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\bra{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\frac{e}{\hbar}\partial_{k_{j}}H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)\ket{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}.

(S39)

We know that for a Bloch state, the spin magnetic moment is
\bm{S}_{\nu,\bm{k}}=\bra{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\frac{1}{2}\mu_{\textrm{B}}g\bm{\sigma}\ket{\phi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}
, so the static part of the spin magnetoelectric susceptibility can be calculated as

\displaystyle T^{\textrm{s},\textrm{sta}}_{ij}=

\displaystyle T^{\textrm{s},\textrm{sta},\textrm{intra}}_{ij}+T^{\textrm{s},\textrm{sta},\textrm{inter}}_{ij}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\frac{e}{\hbar}\int_{\textrm{BZ}}\frac{d\bm{k}}{\left(2\pi\right)^{d}}\sum_{\nu}S_{\nu,\bm{k},i}v_{\nu,\bm{k},j}\left[\frac{df\left(\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}-\frac{df\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\right].

(S40)

\tilde{T}^{\textrm{s},\textrm{sta}}_{ij}
can be obtained similarly following the above procedure:

\displaystyle\tilde{T}^{\textrm{s},\textrm{sta}}_{ij}=

\displaystyle-\frac{e}{\hbar}\int_{\textrm{BZ}}\frac{d\bm{k}}{\left(2\pi\right)^{d}}\sum_{\nu}v_{\nu,\bm{k},i}S_{\nu,\bm{k},j}\left[\frac{df\left(\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}-\frac{df\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\right].

(S41)

For the dynamic part that is
i\omega_{m}
dependent, we consider the dominant intra-band contribution and obtain

\displaystyle T^{\textrm{s},\textrm{dyn}}_{ij}\left(i\omega_{m}\right)=

\displaystyle\frac{i\omega\tau}{1-i\omega\tau}\frac{e}{\hbar}\int_{\textrm{BZ}}\frac{d\bm{k}}{\left(2\pi\right)^{d}}\sum_{\nu}S_{\nu,\bm{k},i}v_{\nu,\bm{k},j}\frac{df\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}.

(S42)

\tilde{T}^{\textrm{s},\textrm{dyn}}_{ij}\left(i\omega_{m}\right)
can be obtained similarly following the above procedure as well:

\displaystyle\tilde{T}^{\textrm{s},\textrm{dyn}}_{ij}\left(i\omega_{m}\right)=

\displaystyle\frac{i\omega\tau}{1-i\omega\tau}\frac{e}{\hbar}\int_{\textrm{BZ}}\frac{d\bm{k}}{\left(2\pi\right)^{d}}\sum_{\nu}v_{\nu,\bm{k},i}S_{\nu,\bm{k},j}\frac{df\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}.

(S43)

Eventually, we get the spin magnetoelectric susceptibility as

\displaystyle T_{ij}^{\textrm{s}}=

\displaystyle-\frac{e}{\hbar}\int_{\textrm{BZ}}\frac{d\bm{k}}{\left(2\pi\right)^{d}}\sum_{\nu}S_{\nu,\bm{k},i}v_{\nu,\bm{k},j}\left[\frac{df\left(\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}-\frac{df\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\right]+\frac{i\omega\tau}{1-i\omega\tau}\frac{e}{\hbar}\int_{\textrm{BZ}}\frac{d\bm{k}}{\left(2\pi\right)^{d}}\sum_{\nu}S_{\nu,\bm{k},i}v_{\nu,\bm{k},j}\frac{df\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}},

(S44)

\displaystyle\tilde{T}_{ij}^{\textrm{s}}=

\displaystyle-\frac{e}{\hbar}\int_{\textrm{BZ}}\frac{d\bm{k}}{\left(2\pi\right)^{d}}\sum_{\nu}v_{\nu,\bm{k},i}S_{\nu,\bm{k},j}\left[\frac{df\left(\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}-\frac{df\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\right]+\frac{i\omega\tau}{1-i\omega\tau}\frac{e}{\hbar}\int_{\textrm{BZ}}\frac{d\bm{k}}{\left(2\pi\right)^{d}}\sum_{\nu}v_{\nu,\bm{k},i}S_{\nu,\bm{k},j}\frac{df\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}.

(S45)

II.3
Current-induced Total Magnetization

Now we are ready to write down the expression for the total magnetization induced by current:

\displaystyle M_{i}=

\displaystyle-\frac{\partial\Delta F}{\partial B_{i}}=-\frac{e}{\hbar}\int_{\textrm{BZ}}\frac{d\bm{k}}{\left(2\pi\right)^{d}}\sum_{\nu}M_{\nu,\bm{k},i}v_{\nu,\bm{k},j}\left\{\frac{\tau}{1-i\omega\tau}\frac{df\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}E_{j}+\left[\frac{df\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}-\frac{df\left(\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\right]A_{j}\right\},

(S46)

which is induced by the current density

\displaystyle J_{i}=

\displaystyle-\frac{\partial\Delta F}{\partial A_{i}}=-\frac{e^{2}}{\hbar^{2}}\int_{\textrm{BZ}}\frac{d\bm{k}}{\left(2\pi\right)^{d}}\sum_{\nu}v_{\nu,\bm{k},i}v_{\nu,\bm{k},j}\left\{\frac{\tau}{1-i\omega\tau}\frac{df\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}E_{j}+\left[\frac{df\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}-\frac{df\left(\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\right]A_{j}\right\}.

(S47)

The total magnetic moment
\bm{M}_{\nu,\bm{k}}=\bm{m}_{\nu,\bm{k}}+\bm{S}_{\nu,\bm{k}}
includes both the orbital magnetic moment
\bm{m}_{\nu,\bm{k}}
and the spin magnetic moment
\bm{S}_{\nu,\bm{k}}
.

As a result, at given current density
\bm{J}
, the induced magnetization becomes
M_{i}=\alpha_{ik}J_{k}
, where the susceptibility
\alpha_{ik}
is

\displaystyle\alpha_{ik}=\gamma_{ij}\left(\tilde{v}^{-1}\right)_{jk},

(S48)

with

\displaystyle\gamma_{ij}=-\frac{e}{\hbar}\sum_{\nu}\int_{\textrm{BZ}}M_{\nu,\bm{k},i}v_{\nu,\bm{k},j}\left[\frac{1}{1-i\omega\tau}\frac{df\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{{k}}}}-\frac{df\left(\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\right]\frac{d\bm{k}}{\left(2\pi\right)^{d}},

(S49)

and

\displaystyle\tilde{v}_{jk}=-\frac{e^{2}}{\hbar^{2}}\sum_{\nu}\int_{\textrm{BZ}}v_{\nu,\bm{k},i}v_{\nu,\bm{k},j}\left[\frac{1}{1-i\omega\tau}\frac{df\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}-\frac{df\left(\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\right]\frac{d\bm{k}}{\left(2\pi\right)^{d}}.

(S50)

At the zero temperature
T=0
, we know
\frac{df\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}=0
, so the two tensors
\gamma_{ij}
,
\tilde{v}_{ij}
become

\displaystyle\gamma_{ij}=

\displaystyle\frac{e}{\hbar}\sum_{\nu}\int_{\textrm{BZ}}M_{\nu,\bm{k},i}v_{\nu,\bm{k},j}\frac{df\left(\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\frac{d\bm{k}}{\left(2\pi\right)^{d}}\sim\frac{e}{\hbar}\sum_{\nu}\oint M_{\nu,\bm{k}_{\textrm{F}},i}v_{\nu,\bm{k}_{\textrm{F}},j}d\bm{k}_{\textrm{F}},

(S51)

and

\displaystyle\tilde{v}_{ij}=

\displaystyle\frac{e^{2}}{\hbar^{2}}\sum_{\nu}\int_{\textrm{BZ}}v_{\nu,\bm{k},i}v_{\nu,\bm{k},j}\frac{df\left(\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\frac{d\bm{k}}{\left(2\pi\right)^{d}}\sim\frac{e^{2}}{\hbar^{2}}\sum_{\nu}\oint v_{\nu,\bm{k}_{\textrm{F}},i}v_{\nu,\bm{k}_{\textrm{F}},j}d\bm{k}_{\textrm{F}}.

(S52)

Near the critical temperature
T_{\textrm{c}}
, we consider the limit
\omega\tau\ll 1
, so the tensor
\gamma_{ij}
,
\tilde{v}_{ij}
can have the taylor expansion in terms of the pairing
\Delta_{\nu,\bm{k}}
as

\displaystyle\gamma_{ij}\approx

\displaystyle\frac{e}{\hbar}\sum_{\nu}\int_{\textrm{BZ}}M_{\nu,\bm{k},i}v_{\nu,\bm{k},j}\left[\frac{df\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}-\frac{df\left(\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\right]\frac{d\bm{k}}{\left(2\pi\right)^{d}}

\displaystyle\approx

\displaystyle\frac{e}{\hbar}\sum_{\nu}\int_{\textrm{BZ}}M_{\nu,\bm{k},i}v_{\nu,\bm{k},j}\Delta^{2}_{\nu,\bm{k}}\frac{d^{2}f\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\epsilon^{2}_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\frac{d\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}{d\left(\Delta^{2}_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}|_{\Delta_{\nu,\bm{k}}=0}

\displaystyle\sim

\displaystyle\frac{e}{\hbar}\sum_{\nu}\oint M_{\nu,\bm{k}_{\textrm{F}},i}v_{\nu,\bm{k}_{\textrm{F}},j}\Delta^{2}_{\nu,\bm{k}_{\textrm{F}}}d\bm{k}_{\textrm{F}},

(S53)

and

\displaystyle\tilde{v}_{ij}\approx

\displaystyle\frac{e^{2}}{\hbar^{2}}\sum_{\nu}\int_{\textrm{BZ}}v_{\nu,\bm{k},i}v_{\nu,\bm{k},j}\left[\frac{df\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}-\frac{df\left(\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\right]\frac{d\bm{k}}{\left(2\pi\right)^{d}}

\displaystyle\approx

\displaystyle\frac{e^{2}}{\hbar^{2}}\sum_{\nu}\int_{\textrm{BZ}}v_{\nu,\bm{k},i}v_{\nu,\bm{k},j}\Delta^{2}_{\nu,\bm{k}}\frac{d^{2}f\left(\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\epsilon^{2}_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\frac{d\epsilon_{\nu,\bm{k}}}{d\left(\Delta^{2}_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}|_{\Delta_{\nu,\bm{k}}=0}

\displaystyle\sim

\displaystyle\frac{e^{2}}{\hbar^{2}}\sum_{\nu}\oint v_{\nu,\bm{k}_{\textrm{F}},i}v_{\nu,\bm{k}_{\textrm{F}},j}\Delta^{2}_{\nu,\bm{k}_{\textrm{F}}}d\bm{k}_{\textrm{F}},

(S54)

with
\bm{k}_{\textrm{F}}
being the wave vector on the Fermi surfaces. At
T>T_{\textrm{c}}
, the pairing gap
\Delta_{\nu,\bm{k}}
is zero, so the tensors
\gamma_{ij}
,
\tilde{v}_{ij}
are

\displaystyle\gamma_{ij}=

\displaystyle\frac{e}{\hbar}\sum_{\nu}\int_{\textrm{BZ}}M_{\nu,\bm{k},i}v_{\nu,\bm{k},j}\frac{df\left(\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\frac{d\bm{k}}{\left(2\pi\right)^{d}}\sim\frac{e}{\hbar}\sum_{\nu}\oint M_{\nu,\bm{k}_{\textrm{F}},i}v_{\nu,\bm{k}_{\textrm{F}},j}d\bm{k}_{\textrm{F}},

(S55)

and

\displaystyle\tilde{v}_{ij}=

\displaystyle\frac{e^{2}}{\hbar^{2}}\sum_{\nu}\int_{\textrm{BZ}}v_{\nu,\bm{k},i}v_{\nu,\bm{k},j}\frac{df\left(\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}\right)}{d\xi_{\nu,\bm{k}}}\frac{d\bm{k}}{\left(2\pi\right)^{d}}\sim\frac{e^{2}}{\hbar^{2}}\sum_{\nu}\oint v_{\nu,\bm{k}_{\textrm{F}},i}v_{\nu,\bm{k}_{\textrm{F}},j}d\bm{k}_{\textrm{F}}.

(S56)

III
Continuum Model for the Twisted Bilayer Graphene

In the monolayer graphene, the primitive lattice vectors and the corresponding reciprocal primitive lattice vectors are

\displaystyle\bm{a}^{0}_{1}=

\displaystyle\sqrt{3}\left(\frac{1}{2},\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)d,\quad\quad\quad\bm{a}^{0}_{2}=\sqrt{3}\left(-\frac{1}{2},\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)d,

(S57)

\displaystyle\bm{b}^{0}_{1}=

\displaystyle\frac{4\pi}{3d}\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2},\frac{1}{2}\right),\quad\quad\quad\bm{b}^{0}_{2}=\frac{4\pi}{3d}\left(-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2},\frac{1}{2}\right),

(S58)

with
d=1.42\AA
. We also define the vector that links the origin of the unit cell to its respective sublattice
\alpha=A,B
atom to be
\bm{\delta}_{A}=\bm{0}
,
\bm{\delta}_{B}=d\left(0,1\right)
. The Dirac points localize at the Brillouin zone corner
\bm{K}_{\pm}=\frac{4\pi}{3d}\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2},\frac{1}{2}\right)
. Given the uniaxial strain tensor
\bm{\mathcal{E}}
along the zig-zag direction of one graphene layer, where

\displaystyle\bm{\mathcal{E}}=\varepsilon\begin{pmatrix}-1&0\\
0&\nu_{\textrm{poi}}\end{pmatrix},

(S59)

with the Poison’s ratio
\nu_{\textrm{poi}}=0.165
, we know that the uniaxial strain will deform both the real and reciprocal space as

\displaystyle\tilde{\bm{r}}=\left(1+\bm{\mathcal{E}}\right)\bm{r},\quad\quad\quad\tilde{\bm{k}}=\left(1-\bm{\mathcal{E}}^{\textrm{T}}\right)\bm{k}_{0}.

(S60)

As a result, the strain changes the position of the Dirac points in the reciprocal space to be

\displaystyle\tilde{\bm{K}}_{\eta}=\left(1-\bm{\mathcal{E}}^{\textrm{T}}\right)\bm{K}_{\eta}-\eta\bm{A}_{\textrm{strain}},

(S61)

with the valley index
\eta=\pm 1
and the strain induced effective gauge field
\bm{A}_{\textrm{strain}}=\frac{\beta}{d}\left(\mathcal{E}_{xx}-\mathcal{E}_{yy},-2\mathcal{E}_{xy}\right)
,
\beta=1.57
. The Hamiltonian for the bottom layer graphene at the valley
\eta
then becomes

\displaystyle\tilde{\mathcal{H}}_{\textrm{b}}=\sum_{\bm{k},s,\eta}c^{\dagger}_{\textrm{b},s,\eta}\left(\bm{k}\right)h_{\textrm{b},\eta}\left(\bm{k}\right)c_{\textrm{b},s,\eta}\left(\bm{k}\right)=\sum_{\bm{k},s,\eta}c^{\dagger}_{\textrm{b},s,\eta}\left[\eta\hbar v_{\textrm{F}}\hat{\bm{R}}_{-\frac{\theta}{2}}\left(1+\bm{\mathcal{E}}^{\textrm{T}}\right)\left(\bm{k}+\eta\bm{A}_{\textrm{strain}}\right)\cdot\bm{\sigma}+\Delta\sigma_{z}\right]c_{\textrm{b},s,\eta}\left(\bm{k}\right),

(S62)

with the spin index
s=\uparrow,\downarrow
, the rotation matrix
\hat{\bm{R}}_{-\frac{\theta}{2}}=\cos\frac{\theta}{2}+i\sigma_{y}\sin\frac{\theta}{2}
, and the momentum
\bm{k}=\bm{k}-\left(1-\bm{\mathcal{E}}^{\textrm{T}}\right)\bm{K}_{\eta}
. The top layer graphene has the Hamiltonian

\displaystyle\mathcal{H}_{\textrm{t}}=\sum_{\bm{k},s,\eta}c^{\dagger}_{\textrm{t},s,\eta}\left(\bm{k}\right)h_{\textrm{t},\eta}\left(\bm{k}\right)c_{\textrm{t},s,\eta}=\sum_{\bm{k},s,\eta}c^{\dagger}_{\textrm{t},s,\eta}\left(\bm{k}\right)\eta\hbar v_{\textrm{F}}\hat{\bm{R}}_{\frac{\theta}{2}}\bm{k}\cdot\bm{\sigma}c_{\textrm{t},s,\eta}\left(\bm{k}\right).

(S63)

Then, we consider the tunneling matrix element from the bottom layer to the top layer
Neto1
;
Neto2
;
MacDonald
to be

\displaystyle\tilde{T}^{\alpha,\beta}_{\hat{\bm{R}}_{-\frac{\theta}{2}}\tilde{\bm{K}}_{\eta}+\bm{k},\hat{\bm{R}}_{\frac{\theta}{2}}\bm{K}_{\eta}+\bm{k}^{\prime}}=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{3}t_{\perp}\left[\delta_{\hat{\bm{R}}_{-\frac{\theta}{2}}\tilde{\bm{K}}_{\eta}+\bm{k},\hat{\bm{R}}_{\frac{\theta}{2}}\bm{K}_{\eta}+\bm{k}^{\prime}}+e^{i\tilde{\bm{b}}_{2}\cdot\left(\tilde{\bm{\delta}}_{\alpha}-\tilde{\bm{\delta}}_{\beta}\right)}\delta_{\hat{R}_{-\frac{\theta}{2}}\left(\tilde{\bm{K}}_{\eta}+\tilde{\bm{b}}_{2}\right)+\bm{k},\hat{\bm{R}}_{\frac{\theta}{2}}\left(\bm{K}_{\eta}+\bm{b}_{2}+\right)+\bm{k}^{\prime}}\right.

\displaystyle\left.+e^{-i\tilde{b}_{1}\cdot\left(\tilde{\bm{\delta}}_{\alpha}-\tilde{\bm{\delta}}_{\beta}\right)}\delta_{\hat{R}_{-\frac{\theta}{2}}\left(\tilde{\bm{K}}_{\eta}-\tilde{\bm{b}}_{1}\right)+\bm{k},\hat{\bm{R}}_{\frac{\theta}{2}}\left(\bm{K}_{\eta}-\bm{b}_{1}\right)+\bm{k}^{\prime}}\right],

(S64)

so the strain deformed interlayer Hamiltonian becomes

\displaystyle\tilde{\mathcal{H}}_{\textrm{int}}=

\displaystyle\sum_{\bm{k},s,\eta}c^{\dagger}_{\textrm{b},s,\eta}\left(\bm{k}\right)\left[\tilde{T}_{\eta\tilde{\bm{k}}_{\textrm{b}}}\delta_{\bm{k}^{\prime}-\bm{k},\eta\tilde{\bm{k}}_{\textrm{b}}}+\tilde{T}_{\eta\tilde{\bm{k}}_{\textrm{tr}}}\delta_{\bm{k}^{\prime}-\bm{k},\eta\tilde{\bm{k}}_{\textrm{tr}}}+\tilde{T}_{\eta\tilde{\bm{k}}_{\textrm{tl}}}\delta_{\bm{k}^{\prime}-\bm{k},\eta\tilde{\bm{k}}_{\textrm{tl}}}\right]c_{\textrm{t},s,\eta}\left(\bm{k}^{\prime}\right)+h.c.,

(S65)

where

\displaystyle\tilde{T}_{\eta\tilde{\bm{k}}_{\textrm{b}}}=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{3}t_{\perp}\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\
1&1\end{pmatrix},

(S66)

\displaystyle\tilde{T}_{\eta\tilde{\bm{k}}_{\textrm{tr}}}=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{3}t_{\perp}\begin{pmatrix}1&e^{-i\eta\frac{2\pi}{3}\left(1+\sqrt{3}\mathcal{E}_{xx}\mathcal{E}_{xy}+\sqrt{3}\mathcal{E}_{xy}\mathcal{E}_{yy}-\mathcal{E}^{2}_{xy}-\mathcal{E}^{2}_{yy}\right)}\\
e^{i\eta\frac{2\pi}{3}\left(1+\sqrt{3}\mathcal{E}_{xx}\mathcal{E}_{xy}+\sqrt{3}\mathcal{E}_{xy}\mathcal{E}_{yy}-\mathcal{E}^{2}_{xy}-\mathcal{E}^{2}_{yy}\right)}&1\end{pmatrix},

(S67)

\displaystyle\tilde{T}_{\eta\tilde{\bm{k}}_{\textrm{tl}}}=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{3}t_{\perp}\begin{pmatrix}1&e^{i\eta\frac{2\pi}{3}\left(1-\sqrt{3}\mathcal{E}_{xx}\mathcal{E}_{xy}-\sqrt{3}\mathcal{E}_{xy}\mathcal{E}_{yy}-\mathcal{E}^{2}_{xy}-\mathcal{E}^{2}_{yy}\right)}\\
e^{-i\eta\frac{2\pi}{3}\left(1-\sqrt{3}\mathcal{E}_{xx}\mathcal{E}_{xy}-\sqrt{3}\mathcal{E}_{xy}\mathcal{E}_{yy}-\mathcal{E}^{2}_{xy}-\mathcal{E}^{2}_{yy}\right)}&1\end{pmatrix},

(S68)

with

\displaystyle\tilde{\bm{k}}_{\textrm{b}}=

\displaystyle\hat{\bm{R}}_{-\frac{\theta}{2}}\left(1-\bm{\mathcal{E}}^{\textrm{T}}\right)\bm{K}_{+}-\hat{\bm{R}}_{\frac{\theta}{2}}\bm{K}_{+}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\frac{4\pi}{3\sqrt{3}d}\begin{pmatrix}\mathcal{E}_{xx}\cos\frac{\theta}{2}+\mathcal{E}_{xy}\sin\frac{\theta}{2},&\left(2-\mathcal{E}_{xx}\right)\sin\frac{\theta}{2}+\mathcal{E}_{xy}\cos\frac{\theta}{2}\end{pmatrix}

(S69)

\displaystyle\tilde{\bm{k}}_{\textrm{tr}}=

\displaystyle\hat{\bm{R}}_{-\frac{\theta}{2}}\left(1-\bm{\mathcal{E}}^{\textrm{T}}\right)\left(\bm{K}_{+}+\bm{b}_{2}\right)-\hat{\bm{R}}_{\frac{\theta}{2}}\left(\bm{K}_{+}+\bm{b}_{2}\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{2\pi}{9d}\begin{pmatrix}\left(\sqrt{3}\mathcal{E}_{xx}-3\mathcal{E}_{xy}\right)\cos\frac{\theta}{2}+\left(6+\sqrt{3}\mathcal{E}_{xy}-3\mathcal{E}_{yy}\right)\sin\frac{\theta}{2},&-\left(3\mathcal{E}_{yy}-\sqrt{3}\mathcal{E}_{xy}\right)\cos\frac{\theta}{2}+\left(2\sqrt{3}+3\mathcal{E}_{xy}-\sqrt{3}\mathcal{E}_{xx}\right)\sin\frac{\theta}{2}\end{pmatrix}

(S70)

\displaystyle\tilde{\bm{k}}_{\textrm{tl}}=

\displaystyle\hat{\bm{R}}_{-\frac{\theta}{2}}\left(1-\bm{\mathcal{E}}^{\textrm{T}}\right)\left(\bm{K}_{+}-\bm{b}_{1}\right)-\hat{\bm{R}}_{\frac{\theta}{2}}\left(\bm{K}_{+}-\bm{b}_{1}\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{2\pi}{9d}\begin{pmatrix}\left(\sqrt{3}\mathcal{E}_{xx}+3\mathcal{E}_{xy}\right)\cos\frac{\theta}{2}-\left(6-\sqrt{3}\mathcal{E}_{xy}-3\mathcal{E}_{xx}\right)\sin\frac{\theta}{2},&\left(3\mathcal{E}_{yy}+\sqrt{3}\mathcal{E}_{xy}\right)\cos\frac{\theta}{2}+\left(2\sqrt{3}-3\mathcal{E}_{xy}-\sqrt{3}\mathcal{E}_{xx}\right)\sin\frac{\theta}{2}\end{pmatrix}.

(S71)

The interlayer hopping strength is taken as
t_{\perp}=0.33
eV. As the uniaxial strain deforms the Moiré superlattice, the reciprocal primitive lattice vector for the strained Moiré superlattice can be obtained as

\displaystyle\tilde{\bm{b}}_{1}^{m}=

\displaystyle\tilde{\bm{k}}_{\textrm{b}}-\tilde{\bm{k}}_{\textrm{tl}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{2\pi}{3d}\left(-\left(\sqrt{3}\mathcal{E}_{xx}+\mathcal{E}_{xy}\right)\cos\frac{\theta}{2}+\left(2-\sqrt{3}\mathcal{E}_{xy}-\mathcal{E}_{yy}\right)\sin\frac{\theta}{2},-\left(\mathcal{E}_{yy}+\sqrt{3}\mathcal{E}_{xy}\right)\cos\frac{\theta}{2}-\left(2\sqrt{3}-\mathcal{E}_{xy}-\sqrt{3}\mathcal{E}_{xx}\right)\sin\frac{\theta}{2}\right),

(S72)

\displaystyle\tilde{\bm{b}_{2}}^{m}=

\displaystyle\tilde{\bm{k}}_{\textrm{tr}}-\tilde{\bm{k}}_{b}

(S73)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{2\pi}{3d}\left(\left(\sqrt{3}\mathcal{E}_{xx}-\mathcal{E}_{xy}\right)\cos\frac{\theta}{2}+\left(2+\sqrt{3}\mathcal{E}_{xy}-\mathcal{E}_{yy}\right)\sin\frac{\theta}{2},-\left(\mathcal{E}_{yy}-\sqrt{3}\mathcal{E}_{xy}\right)\cos\frac{\theta}{2}+\left(2\sqrt{3}+\mathcal{E}_{xy}-\sqrt{3}\mathcal{E}_{xx}\right)\sin\frac{\theta}{2}\right),

(S74)

and the primitive lattice vectors
\tilde{\bm{a}}_{1}=\left(\tilde{a}_{1x},\tilde{a}_{1y}\right)
,
\tilde{\bm{a}}_{2}=\left(\tilde{a}_{2x},\tilde{a}_{2y}\right)
are obtained by

\displaystyle\begin{pmatrix}\tilde{a}_{1x}&\tilde{a}_{2x}\\
\tilde{a}_{1y}&\tilde{a}_{2y}\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}\tilde{b}^{m}_{1x}&\tilde{b}^{m}_{1y}\\
\tilde{b}^{m}_{2x}&\tilde{b}^{m}_{2y}\end{pmatrix}^{-1}\begin{pmatrix}2\pi&0\\
0&2\pi\end{pmatrix}.

(S75)

Finally, the Hamiltonian for the twisted bilayer graphene aligned with boron nitride substrate is written as

\displaystyle\mathcal{H}=

\displaystyle\tilde{\mathcal{H}_{\textrm{b}}}+\mathcal{H}_{\textrm{t}}+\tilde{\mathcal{H}}_{\textrm{int}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{\bm{k},s,\eta}A^{\dagger}_{s,\eta}\left(\bm{k}\right)h_{\eta}\left(\bm{k}\right)A_{s,\eta}\left(\bm{k}\right),

(S76)

where
A_{s,\eta}\left(\bm{k}\right)
has infinite components representing the series of states
c_{\textrm{b},s,\eta}\left(\bm{k}\right)
,
c_{\textrm{t},s,\eta}\left(\bm{k}^{\prime}\right)
with
\bm{k}-\bm{k}^{\prime}=\eta\bm{k}_{\textrm{b}},\eta\bm{k}_{\textrm{tr}},\eta\bm{k}_{\textrm{tl}}
. The Hamiltonian matrix
h_{\eta}\left(\bm{k}\right)
in the truncated basis
\left[a_{\textrm{b},s,\eta}\left(\bm{k}\right),a_{\textrm{t},s,\eta}\left(\bm{k}+\eta\bm{k}_{\textrm{b}}\right),a_{\textrm{t},s,\eta}\left(\bm{k}+\eta\bm{k}_{\textrm{tr}}\right),a_{\textrm{t},s,\eta}\left(\bm{k}+\eta\bm{k}_{tl}\right)\right]^{\textrm{T}}
then has the form

\displaystyle h_{\eta}\left(\bm{k}\right)=\begin{pmatrix}h_{\textrm{b},\eta}\left(\bm{k}\right)&\tilde{T}_{\eta\tilde{\bm{k}}_{\textrm{b}}}&\tilde{T}_{\eta\tilde{\bm{k}}_{\textrm{tr}}}&\tilde{T}_{\eta\tilde{\bm{k}}_{\textrm{tl}}}\\
\tilde{T}_{\eta\tilde{\bm{k}}_{\textrm{b}}}^{\dagger}&h_{\textrm{t},\eta}\left(\bm{k}+\eta\tilde{\bm{k}}_{\textrm{b}}\right)&0&0\\
\tilde{T}_{\eta\tilde{\bm{k}}_{\textrm{tr}}}^{\dagger}&0&h_{\textrm{t},\eta}\left(\bm{k}+\eta\tilde{\bm{k}}_{\textrm{tr}}\right)&0\\
\tilde{T}_{\eta\tilde{\bm{k}}_{\textrm{tl}}}^{\dagger}&0&0&h_{\textrm{t},\eta}\left(\bm{k}+\eta\tilde{\bm{k}}_{\textrm{tl}}\right)\end{pmatrix}.

(S77)

We consider 42 sites in the hexagonal reciprocal lattice so that
h_{\eta}\left(\bm{k}\right)
is an 84
\times
84 matrix in the calculation for the energy dispersion
\xi_{\nu,s,\eta,\bm{k}}
. For a specific band with index
\nu
, the Bogliubov de-Gennes Hamiltonian in the eigen-band basis
\left[\phi^{\dagger}_{\nu,\uparrow,\eta,\bm{k}},\phi^{\dagger}_{\nu,\downarrow,\eta,\bm{k}},\phi_{\nu,\downarrow,-\eta,-\bm{k}},\phi_{\nu,\uparrow,-\eta,-\bm{k}}\right]
can be written as

\displaystyle\begin{pmatrix}H_{0}\left(\bm{k}\right)&\hat{\Delta}\left(\bm{k}\right)\\
\hat{\Delta}^{\dagger}\left(\bm{k}\right)&-H_{0}^{\ast}\left(-\bm{k}\right)\end{pmatrix}=

\displaystyle\begin{pmatrix}\xi_{\nu,\uparrow,\eta,\bm{k}}&0&\Delta_{\bm{k}}&0\\
0&\xi_{\nu,\downarrow,\eta,\bm{k}}&0&-\Delta_{\bm{k}}\\
\Delta_{\bm{k}}&0&\xi_{\nu,\downarrow,-\eta,-\bm{k}}&0\\
0&-\Delta_{\bm{k}}&0&\xi_{\nu,\uparrow,-\eta,-\bm{k}}\end{pmatrix},

(S78)

with the singlet pairing
\Delta_{\bm{k}}=\Delta_{0}+\lambda\Delta_{0}\left\{\cos\left(\bm{k}\cdot\tilde{\bm{a}}_{1}\right)+\cos\left(\bm{k}\cdot\tilde{\bm{a}}_{2}\right)+\cos\left[\bm{k}\cdot\left(\tilde{\bm{a}}_{1}-\tilde{\bm{a}}_{2}\right)\right]\right\}
.
