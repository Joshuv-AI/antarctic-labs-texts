Representations of Toroidal and Full toroidal Lie algebras over polynomial algebras Thanks: ⋆ Corresponding author
Santanu Tantubay, Priyanshu Chakraborty

Abstract
. Toroidal Lie algebras are n n variable generalizations of affine Kac-Moody Lie algebras. Full toroidal Lie algebra is the semidirect product of derived Lie algebra of toroidal Lie algebra and Witt algebra, also it can be thought of n n -variable generalization of Affine-Virasoro algebras. Let 𝔥 ~ \tilde{\mathfrak{h}} be a Cartan subalgebra of a toroidal Lie algebra as well as full toroidal Lie algebra without containing the zero-degree central elements. In this paper, we classify the module structure on U ⁡ ( 𝔥 ~ ) U(\tilde{\mathfrak{h}}) for all toroidal Lie algebras as well as full toroidal Lie algebras which are free U ⁡ ( 𝔥 ~ ) U(\tilde{\mathfrak{h}}) -modules of rank 1. These modules exist only for type A l ​ ( l ≥ 1 ) A_{l}(l\geq 1) , C l ​ ( l ≥ 2 ) C_{l}(l\geq 2) toroidal Lie algebras and the same is true for full toroidal Lie algebras. Also, we determined the irreducibility condition for these classes of modules for both the Lie algebras.

Introduction

In order to understand the module category of any algebras, one of the initial steps is to classify its irreducible objects. Lie algebra is a fundamental subject in mathematics. Lie algebra theory also plays an important role in physics. The classification of simple modules over finite dimensional simple complex Lie algebra seems wild; for

For example, simple weight modules with finite-dimensional weight spaces are classified for simple finite-dimensional Lie algebras, there are two types of such modules: parabolically induced modules and cuspidal module. Parabolically induced modules include simple finite-dimensional modules (

2.
Notations and Preliminaries

Throughout this paper,
\mathbb{Z}
,
\mathbb{C}
, and
\mathbb{C}^{*}
denote the sets of integers, complex numbers, and nonzero complex numbers, respectively. For a Lie algebra
\mathfrak{a}

\mathfrak{a}
as
\mathcal{U}(\mathfrak{a})

\mathbb{C}

In this section, we shall introduce the notions of affine Kac-Moody algebras, toroidal Lie algebra and full toroidal Lie algebra.

2.1.

Suppose
\mathfrak{g}=\mathfrak{g}(X_{l})
is a finite-dimensional simple Lie algebra with a fixed Cartan subalgebra
\mathfrak{h}
of type
X_{l}
, where
X
is one of the types
A,B,\cdots G
. Let
\{x_{i},y_{i},h_{i}:1\leq i\leq l\}
be a set of Chevalley generators for
\mathfrak{g}
, where
l=\hbox{dim}(\mathfrak{h})
. For type
\mathfrak{g}=\mathfrak{g}(A_{l})
or type
\mathfrak{g}=\mathfrak{g}(C_{l})
, we take a basis
\{H_{i}:1\leq i\leq l\}
of
\mathfrak{h}
by

[H_{i},x_{j}]=\delta_{ij}x_{i},\;[H_{i},y_{j}]=-\delta_{ij}y_{j},\;\forall\;1\leq i,j\leq l.

2.2.
Affine Kac-Moody algebras.

Let
\mathfrak{g}
be a simple finite-dimensional Lie algebra. Let us fix a Cartan subalgebra
\mathfrak{h}
of
\mathfrak{g}
. Suppose that
\Delta
is the set of roots of
\mathfrak{g}
and
\Delta_{+}
is the set of positive roots of
\mathfrak{g}
. Then
\mathfrak{g}=\mathfrak{h}\oplus\displaystyle{\bigoplus_{\alpha\in\Delta}}\mathfrak{g}_{\alpha}
is the root space decomposition of
\mathfrak{g}
with respect to
\mathfrak{h}
. Assume that
(-,-)
is the Killing form on
\mathfrak{g}
.

Let
\mathbb{C}[t^{\pm 1}]
be the Laurent polynomial ring over
\mathbb{C}
. Then the algebra
\widehat{\mathfrak{g}}
is a central extension of loop algebra
\mathfrak{g}\otimes\mathbb{C}[t^{\pm 1}]
, i.e.
\widehat{\mathfrak{g}}=\mathfrak{g}\otimes\mathbb{C}[t^{\pm 1}]\oplus\mathbb{C}{\bf{k}}
with the Lie brackets

(2.1)

[x(m),y(n)]=[x,y](m+n)+m(x,y)\delta_{m+n,0}{\bf{k}},\quad[\widehat{\mathfrak{g}},{\bf{k}}]=0,

for all
x,y\in\mathfrak{g},m,n\in\mathbb{Z}
, where
x(m)=x\otimes t^{m}
.

Moreover, we can define the affine Kac-Moody algebra
\widetilde{\mathfrak{g}}:=\widehat{\mathfrak{g}}\oplus\mathbb{C}d=\mathfrak{g}\otimes\mathbb{C}[t^{\pm 1}]\oplus\mathbb{C}{\bf{k}}\oplus\mathbb{C}d
, with above Lie brackets and

[d,x(m)]=mx(m).

Let
\widetilde{\mathfrak{h}}=\mathfrak{h}\oplus\mathbb{C}d
be a Cartan subalgebra of Kac-Moody Lie algebra
\widetilde{\mathfrak{g}}
.

2.3.
Toroidal Lie algebras

Let
A=\mathbb{C}[t_{1}^{\pm 1},\cdots,t_{n}^{\pm 1}]
be the Laurent polynomial ring in
n
commuting variables
t_{1},\cdots,t_{n}
over
\mathbb{C}
. Assume
\Omega_{A}
is the free
A
-module with basis
{K_{1},\dots,K_{n}}
and
dA
is the subspace spanned by the elements
\sum_{i=1}^{n}m_{i}t^{m}K_{i},\;m\in\mathbb{Z}^{n}
.
Then
L(\mathfrak{g})=\mathfrak{g}\otimes A
with the brackets
[x\otimes t^{r},y\otimes t^{s}]=[x,y]\otimes t^{r+s}
is known as the loop algebra of
\mathfrak{g}
by
A
. Define
\mathcal{K}_{A}=\Omega_{A}/dA
, then it is well known that (See
[
35
]
,
[
36
]
)
\widetilde{L(\mathfrak{g})}=L(\mathfrak{g})\oplus\mathcal{K}_{A}
is the universal central extension of
L(\mathfrak{g})
with the Lie brackets

(2.2)

[x(a),y(b)]=[x,y](a+b)+(x,y)\sum_{i=1}^{n}a_{1}t^{a+b}K_{i},\;[L(\mathfrak{g}),\mathcal{K}_{A}]=0

Let
D=\oplus_{i=1}^{n}\mathbb{C}d_{i}
be a
n
dimensional vectorspace. We define the toroidal Lie algebra
\widetilde{\mathfrak{g}}_{n}=\widetilde{L(\mathfrak{g})}\oplus D
with the Lie brackets (
2.2
) and
[d_{i},x(a)]=a_{i}x(a),\;[d_{i},d_{j}]=0
, where
x(a)\in\widetilde{L(\mathfrak{g})}
and
1\leq i,j\leq n
. Then it is easy to see that
\widetilde{\mathfrak{g}}_{1}=\widetilde{\mathfrak{g}}
.
We define a Cartan subalgebra of toroidal Lie algebra by
\widetilde{\mathfrak{h}}_{n}=\mathfrak{h}\oplus D
.

2.4.
Full toroidal Lie algebras (
[
34
]
)

Let
Der(A)
be the space of all derivations on
A
. It has a basis consisting of elements of the form
\{t^{r}d_{i}:r\in\mathbb{Z}^{n},\;1\leq i\leq n\}
. Now define
D(u,r)=\sum_{i=1}^{n}u_{i}t^{r}d_{i}
, where
u\in\mathbb{C}^{n}
and
r\in\mathbb{Z}^{n}
. We see that
Der(A)
is a Lie algebra, with the Lie brackets
[D(u,r),D(v,s)]=D(w,r+s)
, where
w=(u,s)v-(v,r)u
,
u,v\in\mathbb{C}^{n},\;r,s\in\mathbb{Z}^{n}
. This Lie algebra is known as Witt algebra. Every derivation
d
of
A
can be naturally extended to a derivation on the tensor product
\mathfrak{g}\otimes A
by
d(x\otimes f)=x\otimes df
, where
x\in\mathfrak{g}
,
f\in A
and
d
has a unique natural extension to
\mathcal{K}_{A}
by

t^{r}d_{i}(t^{s}K_{j})=s_{i}t^{(r+s)}K_{j}+\delta_{ij}\sum_{p=1}^{n}r_{p}t^{r+s}K_{p}.

It is well known that
Der(A)
admits two non-trivial
2
-cocyles with values in
\mathcal{K}_{A}
(See
[
2
]
):

\phi_{1}(t^{r}d_{i},t^{s}d_{j})=-s_{i}r_{j}\sum_{p=1}^{n}r_{p}t^{r+s}K_{p},

\phi_{2}(t^{r}d_{i},t^{s}d_{j})=r_{i}s_{j}\sum_{p=1}^{n}r_{p}t^{r+s}K_{p}.

Let
\phi
be an arbitrary Linear combination of
\phi_{1}
and
\phi_{2}
. Then there is a corresponding Lie algebra

\mathcal{L}=\mathfrak{g}\otimes A\oplus\mathcal{K}_{A}\oplus Der(A)

with the Lie brackets
2.2
and the following:

[t^{r}d_{i},t^{s}K_{j}]=s_{i}t^{r+s}K_{j}+\delta_{ij}\sum_{p=1}^{n}r_{p}t^{r+s}K_{p}

[D(u,r),D(v,s)]=D(w,r+s)+\phi(D(u,r),D(w,s))

[D(u,r),x(s)]=(u,s)x(r+s).

This lie algebra is known as full toroidal Lie algebra.

2.5.

Let
\mathfrak{a}
be any Lie algebra over
\mathbb{C}
and
\mathfrak{b}
be any finite-dimensional abelian subalgebra of
\mathfrak{a}
. Denote
\mathcal{M}(\mathfrak{a},\mathfrak{b})
by the full subcategory of
U(\mathfrak{a})
-modules consisting of objects whose restriction to

is a free module of rank

\mathcal{M}(\mathfrak{a},\mathfrak{b})=\{M\in U(\mathfrak{a})-\hbox{Mod}|\hbox{Res}_{U(\mathfrak{b})}^{U(\mathfrak{a})}M\cong_{U(\mathfrak{b})}U(\mathfrak{b})\}.

4.
Cartan Free modules over

We see that
\widetilde{\mathfrak{h}}_{n}
is an abelian Lie algebra, so
\mathcal{U}(\widetilde{\mathfrak{h}}_{n})=\mathbb{C}[H_{1},\dots,H_{l},d_{1},\dots,d_{n}]

\mathbb{C}[H_{1},\dots,H_{l},d_{1},\dots,d_{n}]

The

Suppose

is not simple as

-module and

\widetilde{\mathfrak{g}}_{n}
-module of
\widetilde{M}_{\widetilde{\mathfrak{g}}_{n}}(\mathbf{\lambda},M_{\mathfrak{g}})

Conversely assume
M_{\mathfrak{g}}
is a simple
\mathfrak{g}
-module. Let
W
be a nonzero submodule of
M_{\mathfrak{g}}\otimes\mathcal{P}_{D}

Claim:
There exists

\hbox{deg}_{d_{j}}(w)=0
for all

Let
w_{1}\in W
be a non-zero element with a minimal degree in
