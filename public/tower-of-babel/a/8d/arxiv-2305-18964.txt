Abstract
. L. Soukup formulated an abstract framework in his introductory paper for proving theorems about uncountable graphs by subdividing them by an increasing, continuous chain of elementary submodels. The applicability of this method relies on the preservation of a certain property (that varies from problem to problem) by the subgraphs obtained by subdividing the graph by an elementary submodel. He calls the properties that are preserved “well-reflecting”. The aim of this paper is to investigate the possibility of weakening the assumption “well-reflecting” in L. Soukup’s framework. Our motivation is to gain a better understanding of a class of problems in infinite graph theory where a weaker form of well-reflection naturally occurs.

Clearly

It follows from Observation

Proposition 3.13

Proof.

It is immediate from Corollary

3.2.
Finding a club of intersectable and subtractable elementary submodels

In the following lemmas, we have a
\kappa

\kappa

Lemma 3.14

For every uncountable regular cardinal
\kappa
and every increasing, continuous sequence

of elementary submodels satisfying (

), (

) and
(

\{\alpha<\kappa:\ M_{\alpha}\text{ is subtractable}\}\in\mathsf{club}(\kappa).

Proof.

Let
M:=\bigcup_{\alpha<\kappa}M_{\alpha}
. For every

, let

be a club of
\kappa
such that
M_{\alpha}
is
G
-subtractable for

(see Definition

). For
\alpha<\kappa
let

Note that

is a club because

is the intersection of less than
\kappa

Then

\beta<\alpha

\alpha\in C_{\beta}\subseteq C_{G}
. But then, by the definition of

M_{\alpha}

is

is subtractable.

∎

Lemma 3.15
.

Let
\kappa
be an uncountable regular cardinal and let

\left\langle M_{\alpha}:\ \alpha<\kappa\right\rangle
be an increasing, continuous sequence of elementary submodels
satisfying (

), (
2b
) and
(
2c
) of Definition
3.5
. If

\{\alpha<\kappa:\ M_{\alpha}\text{ is subtractable and intersectable}\}\in\mathsf{club}(\kappa).

Proof.

By applying Lemma

, we pick a club

of
\kappa
such that

is subtractable for each

is unbounded in

and

is intersectable and subtractable for

.

.
But then

Lemma 3.16
.

Let
\kappa
be an uncountable regular cardinal and let

\left\langle M_{\alpha}:\ \alpha<\kappa\right\rangle

satisfying (

), (

) and
(

) of Definition

. If

It is enough to show that

. Let

be given. We need to show that

of successor ordinals with limit

. Then

is an increasing (and continuous)

.

3.3.

It remains to construct decomposable submodels of a given size
\kappa
containing a prescribed set

second requirement stronger and demand the existence of a decomposition in which every member contains

For every set

of size

.

Suppose now that

\mathsf{cf}(\lambda)>\omega

As we have already seen (right after Observation

),
\Phi=\Psi
is implied by the
conjunction of Propositions

3.13
and
3.17
. The first usage of the approachability assumption was at
the induction step of Proposition
3.17
from
\aleph_{1}
to
\aleph_{2}
.
Therefore it remains to justify in ZFC that every
G\in\Phi
of size
\aleph_{2}
is in
\Psi
.

4.
Graphs of size
\aleph_{2}

Let
G\in\Phi
with
\left|G\right|=\aleph_{2}
be given. First of all, we claim that there is an increasing, continuous
sequence
\left\langle G_{\alpha}:\ \alpha<\omega_{2}\right\rangle
of subsets of
G
exhausting
G
such that
G_{\alpha}
is
G
-subtractable and smaller than
\aleph_{2}
for each
\alpha
. Indeed, let
\left\langle M_{\alpha}:\ \alpha<\omega_{2}\right\rangle
be
an
increasing, continuous
and
\in
-increasing sequence of elementary submodels containing
G
with
\aleph_{1}=\left|M_{\alpha}\right|\subseteq M_{\alpha}

for each
\alpha<\omega_{2}
. By Lemma
3.14
we can assume that
M_{\alpha}
is subtractable for every
\alpha<\omega_{2}
by
switching to a subsequence corresponding to a suitable club of
\omega_{2}
. Then
G_{\alpha}:=G\cap M_{\alpha}
is as
desired.

Let

\left\langle M^{\prime}_{\alpha}:\ \alpha<\omega_{2}\right\rangle
be an increasing, continuous
and
\in
-increasing sequence of elementary submodels with
G\in M^{\prime}_{0}
such that

\aleph_{1}=\left|M^{\prime}_{\alpha}\right|\subseteq M^{\prime}_{\alpha}
and
M^{\prime}_{\alpha}
is intersectable for every
\alpha\in\omega_{2}\setminus\mathsf{acc}(\omega_{2})
.
The existence of
intersectable elementary submodels
M
with
\aleph_{1}=\left|M\right|\subseteq M
containing a prescribed set was
shown in ZFC.
Therefore the
sequence

\left\langle M^{\prime}_{\alpha}:\ \alpha<\omega_{2}\right\rangle
can be constructed by a straightforward transfinite
recursion by taking: an intersectable elementary submodel
M_{0}^{\prime}
with
\aleph_{1}=\left|M^{\prime}_{0}\right|\subseteq M^{\prime}_{0}
containing
G
, union at limit steps and an intersectable elementary submodel
M^{\prime}_{\alpha+1}
with
\aleph_{1}=\left|M^{\prime}_{\alpha+1}\right|\subseteq M^{\prime}_{\alpha+1}

containing
M^{\prime}_{\alpha}
in successor steps.

We claim that
M^{\prime}_{\alpha}
is
G
-subtractable for each
\alpha<\omega_{2}
. Let
\alpha
be fixed. By elementarity we
can find a
\left\langle G_{\beta}:\ \beta<\omega_{2}\right\rangle\in M^{\prime}_{0}
as described in the first paragraph. Since
G_{\beta}

is
G
-subtractable for every
\beta<\omega_{2}
, it is enough to
show that there is a
\beta<\omega_{2}
with
G\cap M^{\prime}_{\alpha}=G_{\beta}
. Let
\beta:=\min\{\gamma<\omega_{2}:(G\setminus G_{\gamma})\cap M^{\prime}_{\alpha}=\varnothing\}
. Then
G\cap M^{\prime}_{\alpha}\subseteq G_{\beta}
is immediate from the
definition. Let
\gamma<\beta
and take an
e\in(G\setminus G_{\gamma})\cap M^{\prime}_{\alpha}
. The smallest ordinal
\delta
with
e\in(G_{\delta+1}\setminus G_{\delta})
is at least
\gamma
and is definable from
\left\langle G_{\beta}:\ \beta<\omega_{2}\right\rangle
and
e
, thus
\delta\in M^{\prime}_{\alpha}
. But then
\delta+1\in M^{\prime}_{\alpha}
and therefore
G_{\delta+1}\in M^{\prime}_{\alpha}
. It follows by Fact
2.4
that
G_{\delta+1}\subseteq M^{\prime}_{\alpha}

and hence
G_{\gamma+1}\subseteq M^{\prime}_{\alpha}
. Since
\gamma<\beta
was arbitrary, this means
G_{\beta}\subseteq M^{\prime}_{\alpha}
which concludes the proof of
G\cap M^{\prime}_{\alpha}=G_{\beta}
.

It follows from the
G
-subtractablity of
M^{\prime}_{\alpha}
that
G\setminus M^{\prime}_{\alpha}\in\Phi
. Since
M^{\prime}_{\alpha+1}
is intersectable and
G\setminus M^{\prime}_{\alpha}\in M^{\prime}_{\alpha+1}
, it is in
particular
G\setminus M^{\prime}_{\alpha}
-intersectable. Since
M^{\prime}_{0}
is also intersectable and hence
G
-intersectable, we can apply
Observation
3.7
to
conclude that
M^{\prime}:=\bigcup_{\alpha<\omega_{2}}M^{\prime}_{\alpha}
is
G
-intersectable. But then
G=G\cap M^{\prime}\in\Psi
.
