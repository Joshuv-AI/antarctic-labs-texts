Universal Stochastic Equations of Monitored Quantum Dynamics
Zhenyu Xiao, Tomi Ohtsuki, Kohei Kawabata

Abstract
We investigate the monitored quantum dynamics of Gaussian mixed states and derive the universal Fokker-Planck equations that govern the stochastic time evolution of entire density-matrix spectra, obtaining their exact solutions. From these equations, we reveal an even-odd effect in purification dynamics: whereas entropy exhibits exponential decay for an even number N N of complex fermions, algebraic decay with divergent purification time occurs for odd N N as a manifestation of dynamical criticality. Additionally, we identify the universal fluctuations of entropy in the chaotic regime, serving as a non-unitary counterpart of the universal conductance fluctuations in mesoscopic electronic transport phenomena. Furthermore, we elucidate and classify the universality classes of non-unitary quantum dynamics based on fundamental symmetry. We also validate the universality of these analytical results through extensive numerical simulations across different types of models.

Symmetry classification
.—Symmetric space of the quantum trajectory
M_{0:t}

greatly influences the time evolution of its singular-value spectrum, as also noticed in the study of quantum transport
Beenakker 1997
;
Evers and Mirlin 2008
.
In the dynamics studied above, the unitary part
U_{t}\in{\rm U}(N)
imposes no symmetry constraint on
M_{0:t}
.
Therefore, the dynamical generator
L_{\rm eff}
defined by
M_{0:t}\equiv e^{L_{\rm eff}t}
is a generic non-Hermitian matrix without any symmetry and hence belongs to class A (see Table
1
)
Altland and Zirnbauer 1997
;
Bernard and LeClair 2002
;
Kawabata et al. 2019
.

As an exemplary symmetry class different from class A, we study
the monitored dynamics of
2N
Majorana fermions

Fava et al. 2023
.
A generic Majorana quadratic Hamiltonian is
\mathcal{H}=\sum_{i,j}H_{ij}\psi_{i}\psi_{j}
(
H^{{\dagger}}=H
,
H^{\rm T}=-H
) with Majorana fermions
\psi_{i}
’s (
\psi_{i}=\psi_{i}^{{\dagger}}
,
\{\psi_{i},\psi_{j}\}=2\delta_{i,j}
).
Gaussian Majorana unitary operators satisfy

e^{-\text{i}\mathcal{H}\Delta t}\psi_{i}e^{\text{i}\mathcal{H}\Delta t}=\sum\psi_{j}(U_{t})_{ji}
and
U_{t}=e^{-4\text{i}H\Delta t}\in{\rm SO}(2N)
,
and Gaussian measurements satisfy
M_{t}^{\rm T}=M_{t}^{-1}
.
Consequently, the quantum trajectory
M_{0:t}
, comprised of the product of
U_{t}
’s and
M_{t}
’s, satisfies symmetry
M_{0:t}^{\rm T}=M_{0:t}^{-1}
, and hence the non-Hermitian dynamical generator respects
L_{\rm eff}^{\rm T}=-L_{\rm eff}
and belongs to class D
Altland and Zirnbauer 1997
;
Bernard and LeClair 2002
;
Kawabata et al. 2019
.
Due to this symmetry, the singular values of
M_{0:t}
come in
(e^{-z_{n}},e^{z_{n}})
pairs (
z_{n}\geq 0
), leading to

\displaystyle\langle\Delta z_{n}(t)\rangle_{E}

\displaystyle=\frac{4\left(\mu_{n}+\nu_{n}\right)}{2N-1}\gamma\Delta t,

(15)

\displaystyle\langle\Delta z_{n}(t)\Delta z_{m}(t)\rangle_{E}

\displaystyle=\frac{4\delta_{mn}}{2N-1}\gamma\Delta t,

(16)

\mu_{n}=\sum_{m\neq n}\left(\coth(z_{n}-z_{m})+\coth(z_{n}+z_{m})\right),\,\nu_{n}=\tanh z_{n}\,.

(17)

Discussion
.—We establish the Fokker-Planck equations that universally govern the monitored dynamics of free fermions.
Our formula (
10
) relating the purification time
\tau_{P}
to the Lyapunov exponents
\eta_{n}
’s facilitates efficient numerical analysis of measurement-induced phase transitions
unp
.
Due to the single-particle nature, it differs from those in Refs.
Gullans and Huse 2020a
;
Zabalo et al. 2022
;
Mochizuki and Hamazaki 2025
, and the resulting Fokker-Planck equation (
7
) also contrasts with many-body formulations
Schomerus 2022
;
Bulchandani et al. 2024
;
De Luca et al. 2023
;
Gerbino et al. 2024
.
We elucidate that this distinction enriches the monitored quantum dynamics, leading to the even-odd effect of the purification dynamics.
Moreover, we uncover the universal entropy fluctuations within the chaotic regime, serving as a non-unitary counterpart of the universal conductance fluctuations
Stone 1985
;
Al’tshuler 1985
;
Lee and Stone 1985
;
Imry 1986
;
Al’tshuler and Shklovskiĭ 1986
;
Lee et al. 1987
;
Washburn and Webb 1986
.
It warrants further investigation to incorporate the full-counting statistics of other observables.
In this respect, the charge fluctuations should also exhibit similar behavior since they are of the same order as Rényi entropy for fermionic Gaussian states
Potter 2014
;
Burmistrov et al. 2017
.
Application of our approach to quantum control and engineering
Wiseman and Milburn 2009
also deserves further research.
