Penalization method for reflected BDSDEs with two-sided jumps and driven by Lévy process
Mohamed Marzougue

Abstract
In this paper, we prove the existence and uniqueness of the solution to reflected backward doubly stochastic differential equations driven by Teugels martingales associated with a Lévy process where the barrier process is not necessarily right continuous by approximating such equations by a new version of penalization method.



1
Introduction

After Pardoux and Peng
[
32
]
have introduced the notion of non-linear backward stochastic differential equations (BSDEs in short) in a Brownian setting, the authors introduced a new kind of BSDEs in
[
33
]
, that is a class of backward doubly stochastic differential equations (BDSDEs in short) with two different directions of stochastic integrals with respect to two independent Brownian motions. Precisely, these equations take the form

Y_{t}=\xi+\int_{t}^{T}f(s,Y_{s},Z_{s})ds+\int_{t}^{T}g(s,Y_{s},Z_{s})dB_{s}-\int_{t}^{T}Z_{s}dW_{s},\quad 0\leq t\leq T.

(1.1)

where the integral driven by
dW_{s}
is the standard forward stochastic Itô’s integral and the integral driven by
dB_{s}
is the backward stochastic Kunita-Itô’s integral. The authors established the existence and uniqueness of solution to BDSDE (
1.1
) under the square integrability on the data
(\xi,f,g)
with Lipschitzian drivers
f
and
g
. As application, the authors gave a probabilistic representation for a class of quasi-linear stochastic partial differential equations. Following this, Ren et al.
[
37
]
have considered BDSDEs driven by Teugels martingales associated with a Lévy process satisfying some moment condition and an independent Brownian motion. Later, there are several works which have focused on developing the theory of BDSDEs in different direction (see for instance
[
4
,
13
,
14
,
29
,
30
,
31
,
38
,
39
]
).

As a variation of BDSDEs, Bahlali et al.
[
7
]
were introduced reflected BDSDEs (RBDSDEs in short) where an additional nondecreasing process
K
is added to the standard BDSDEs (
1.1
) in order to keep the
Y
-component of the solution above a certain lower continuous process, called barrier (or obstacle), and to do so in a minimal fashion. The authors have studied the case when the coefficient
f
is continuous, and they proved the existence of a minimal and maximal solutions in a Brownian setting. Following this, Ren
[
36
]
has considered RBDSDEs driven by Teugels martingales associated with a Lévy process, in which the barrier process is right continuous with left limits. We stress that the theory of RBDSDEs has been extended to the case where the barrier is not necessarily continuous and/or a larger filtration than the Brownian filtration, or by weakening the assumptions on the coefficients, by several authors, we quote
[
3
,
5
,
2
,
18
,
22
,
36
]
and references therein. In all of the mentioned works, the barrier has been assumed to be at least right continuous. Recently, Berrhazi et al.
[
8
]
considered RBDSDEs when the barrier is not necessarily right-continuous by inspiring on the work of Grigorova et al.
[
15
]
which is the first one dealing with right upper semi-continuous barrier reflected BSDEs. For more developments on Reflected BSDEs when the barrier is not necessarily right-continuous, we refer to
[
1
,
6
,
16
,
19
,
23
,
25
,
26
]
. More recently, Marzougue and Sagna
[
27
]
extended the work of Berrhazi et al.
[
8
]
to the case when the noise is driven also by an independent Poisson random measure under the so-called stochastic Lipschitz condition on the drivers.

The penalization method is one of useful tools to establish the existence result for RBSDEs (e.g.,
[
12
,
17
,
21
]
) and RBDSDEs (e.g.,
[
2
]
). Very recently, Marzougue
[
24
]
has proved a new monotonic limit theorem for regulated processes (in which the trajectories have just left and right limits), from that proved by Peng
[
34
]
. As application, the author has established the existence theorem of RBSDEs when the barrier has regulated trajectories (not necessarily right-continuous) by means of penalization method.

Motivated by the Marzougue’s monotonic limit theorem, we can now approximate the solution of RBDSDEs with regulated barrier by a modified penalized schema. To this end, we mainly consider, in this paper, the problem of RBDSDEs driven by Teugels martingales associated with a Lévy process (RBDSDEL in short), and we prove the existence and uniqueness of the solution of such equations by means of penalization method.

The paper is organized as follows: In section
2
we give some notations and preliminaries, and we formulate our problem. Section
3
is devoted to establish the well-posedness of our problem which corresponds to a class of RBDSDEL where the barrier process is not necessarily right-continuous by means of penalization method.

2
Background

2.1
Preliminaries and notations

Let
T
strictly positive real number and let
(\Omega,\mathcal{F},\mathbb{P},(\mathcal{F}_{t})_{t\leq T},(B_{t})_{t\leq T},(\mathfrak{L}_{t})_{t\leq T})
be a complete Brownian-Lévy space in
\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{R}^{\ast}
, with Lévy measure
\nu
, i.e.
(\Omega,\mathcal{F},\mathbb{P})
is a complete probability space,
(B_{t})_{t\leq T}
is a standard Brownian motion in
\mathbb{R}
and
(\mathfrak{L}_{t})_{t\leq T}
is a
\mathbb{R}^{\ast}
-valued pure-jump Lévy process independent of
(B_{t})_{t\leq T}
, which corresponds to a standard Lévy measure
\nu
satisfying the following conditions:

(i)

\int_{\mathbb{R}}(1\wedge x^{2})\nu(dx)<+\infty,

(ii)

\int_{(-\varepsilon,\varepsilon)^{c}}e^{\lambda|x|}\nu(dx)<+\infty
for each
\varepsilon>0
and some
\lambda>0
.

For each
t\leq T
, we define

\mathcal{F}_{t}\triangleq\mathcal{F}_{t,T}^{B}\vee\mathcal{F}_{0,t}^{L},

where for any process
(\eta_{t})_{t\leq T}
;
\mathcal{F}_{s,t}^{\eta}=\sigma\{\eta_{r}-\eta_{s};\;s\leq r\leq t\}\vee\mathcal{N}
and
\mathcal{N}
denotes the class of
\mathbb{P}
-null sets of
\mathcal{F}
.
Note that the collection
(\mathcal{F}_{t})_{t\leq T}
is neither increasing nor decreasing, so it does not constitute a filtration. However,
(\mathcal{G}_{t})_{t\leq T}
defined as
\mathcal{G}_{t}\triangleq\mathcal{F}_{T}^{B}\vee\mathcal{F}_{t}^{L}
is a filtration which contains
(\mathcal{F}_{t})_{t\leq T}
.

We denote by
\mathfrak{L}_{t-}=\lim_{s\nearrow t}\mathfrak{L}_{s}
and
\Delta\mathfrak{L}_{t}=\mathfrak{L}_{t}-\mathfrak{L}_{t-}
. The power-jumps of the Lévy process
\mathfrak{L}
are defined by
\mathfrak{L}_{t}^{(1)}=\mathfrak{L}_{t}\quad\mbox{and}\quad\mathfrak{L}_{t}^{(i)}=\sum_{0<s\leq t}(\Delta\mathfrak{L}_{s})^{i},\ i\geq 2
. Define
Y_{t}^{(i)}=\mathfrak{L}_{t}^{(i)}-\mathbb{E}[\mathfrak{L}_{t}^{(i)}]=\mathfrak{L}_{t}^{(i)}-t\mathbb{E}[\mathfrak{L}_{1}^{(i)}]
for all
i\geq 1
, the so-called Teugels martingales. We associate with the Lévy process
\mathfrak{L}
the family of processes
(H^{(i)})_{i\geq 1}
defined by
H_{t}^{(i)}=\sum_{j=1}^{i}\alpha_{ij}Y_{t}^{(j)}
.
The martingales
H^{(i)}
, called the orthonormalized
i
th-power-jump processes, are strongly orthogonal and its predictable quadratic variation process
is
\langle H^{(i)},H^{(j)}\rangle_{t}=\delta_{ij}t
. For more details on Teugels martingales, one can see Bertoin
[
9
]
and also Nualart and Schoutens
[
28
]
.

We will denote by

1.

|.|
the Euclidian norm on
\mathbb{R}^{d}
,

2.

\mathcal{T}_{[t,T]}
the set of all stopping times
\tau
with values in
[t,T]
,

3.

\mathcal{P}
(resp.
\mathcal{O}(\mathbb{R}^{d})
) the predictable (resp. Optional)
\sigma
-algebra on
\Omega\times[0,T]
(resp. on
\mathbb{R}^{d}
).

Let us introduce some spaces:

1.

\mathcal{S}^{2}
is the space of
\mathbb{R}
-valued and optional processes
(Y_{t})_{t\leq T}
such that

\|Y\|_{\mathcal{S}^{2}}^{2}=\mathbb{E}\left[\operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{\tau\in\mathcal{T}_{[0,T]}}|Y_{\tau}|^{2}\right]<+\infty.

2.

\mathcal{H}^{2}
is the space of
\mathbb{R}^{d}
-valued and predictable processes
(Z_{t})_{t\leq T}
such that

\|Z\|_{\mathcal{H}^{2}}^{2}=\mathbb{E}\left[\int_{0}^{T}|Z_{t}|^{2}dt\right]<+\infty.

3.

\ell^{2}=\left\{z=(z_{t})_{t\geq 0};\ \|z\|_{\ell^{2}}=\left(\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}|z_{t}^{(k)}|^{2}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}<+\infty\right\}
.

4.

\mathcal{H}^{2}(\ell^{2})
: the space of the
\mathcal{F}_{t}
–predictable and
\ell^{2}
-valued processes
(Z_{t})_{t\leq T}
such that

\|Z\|^{2}_{\mathcal{H}^{2}(\ell^{2})}:=\mathbb{E}\left[\int_{0}^{T}\|Z_{t}\|_{\ell^{2}}^{2}dt\right]=\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\mathbb{E}\left[\int_{0}^{T}|Z_{t}^{(k)}|^{2}dt\right]<+\infty.

5.

\mathcal{B}^{2}:=\mathcal{S}^{2}\times\mathcal{H}^{2}(\ell^{2})
is a Banach space endowed with the norm

\|(Y,Z)\|^{2}_{\mathcal{B}^{2}}=\|Y\|_{\mathcal{S}^{2}}^{2}+\|Z\|^{2}_{\mathcal{H}^{2}(\ell^{2})}.

Remark 1
.

Let
\beta>0
. There exists an equivalent norm
\|(.,.)\|_{\mathcal{B}^{2}_{\beta}}
to the norm
\|(.,.)\|_{\mathcal{B}^{2}}
, defined in the Banach space
\mathcal{B}^{2}_{\beta}:=\mathcal{S}^{2}_{\beta}\times\mathcal{H}^{2}_{\beta}(\ell^{2})
as

\|(Y,Z)\|^{2}_{\mathcal{B}^{2}_{\beta}}=\|Y\|_{\mathcal{S}^{2}_{\beta}}^{2}+\|Z\|^{2}_{\mathcal{H}^{2}_{\beta}(\ell^{2})}=\mathbb{E}\left[\operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{\tau\in\mathcal{T}_{[0,T]}}e^{\beta\tau}|Y_{\tau}|^{2}\right]+\mathbb{E}\left[\int_{0}^{T}e^{\beta t}\|Z_{t}\|_{\ell^{2}}^{2}dt\right].

Definition 2
.

A pair of functions
(f,g)
is said to be a pair of Lipschitz drivers if

1.

f,g:\Omega\times[0,T]\times\mathbb{R}\times\ell^{2}\longrightarrow\mathbb{R}
are progressively measurable.

2.

\mathbb{E}\int_{0}^{T}(|f(t,0,0)|^{2}+|g(t,0,0)|^{2})dt<+\infty
.

3.

There exists some nonnegative constants
L
and
0<\alpha<\frac{1}{2}
such that
\forall(t,y,z,y^{\prime},z^{\prime})\in[0,T]\times\mathbb{R}\times\ell^{2}\times\mathbb{R}\times\ell^{2}

|f(t,y,z)-f(t,y^{\prime},z^{\prime})|\leq L\left(|y-y^{\prime}|+\|z-z^{\prime}\|_{\ell^{2}}\right)

and

|g(t,y,z)-g(t,y^{\prime},z^{\prime})|^{2}\leq L|y-y^{\prime}|^{2}+\alpha\|z-z^{\prime}\|_{\ell^{2}}^{2}.

Definition 3
.

We say that a function
\mathcal{Y}:[0,T]\rightarrow\mathbb{R}
has regulated trajectories (or làdlàg) if
\mathcal{Y}
has a
left limit in each point of
]0,T]
, and a right limit in each point of
[0,T[
.
For a process
\mathcal{Y}
with regulated trajectories, we denote

1.

\mathcal{Y}_{t-}=\lim\limits_{s\nearrow t}\mathcal{Y}_{s}
the left-hand limit of
\mathcal{Y}
at
t\in]0,T]
,
(\mathcal{Y}_{0-}=\mathcal{Y}_{0})
,
\mathcal{Y}_{-}:=(\mathcal{Y}_{t-})_{t\leq T}
and
\Delta\mathcal{Y}_{t}:=\mathcal{Y}_{t}-\mathcal{Y}_{t-}
the size of the left jump of
\mathcal{Y}
at
t
.

2.

\mathcal{Y}_{t+}=\lim\limits_{s\searrow t}\mathcal{Y}_{s}
the right-hand limit of
\mathcal{Y}
at
t\in[0,T[
,
(\mathcal{Y}_{T+}=\mathcal{Y}_{T})
,
\mathcal{Y}_{+}:=(\mathcal{Y}_{t+})_{t\leq T}
and
\Delta_{+}\mathcal{Y}_{t}:=\mathcal{Y}_{t+}-\mathcal{Y}_{t}
the size of the right jump of
\mathcal{Y}
at
t
.

3.

For all
t\leq T
,
\mathcal{Y}_{t}=\mathcal{Y}^{\ast}_{t}+\sum_{s<t}\Delta_{+}\mathcal{Y}_{s}
where
\mathcal{Y}^{\ast}
is the right-continuous part of the process
\mathcal{Y}
and
\sum_{s<t}\Delta_{+}\mathcal{Y}_{s}
stands its purely jumping part
consisting of right jumps such that
\sum_{s<t}|\Delta_{+}\mathcal{Y}_{s}|<+\infty
a.s.

2.2
Problem’s formulation

Let us now announce the definition of reflected BDSDEs driven by Teugels martingales associated with a pure-jump Lévy process. Let
\xi\in\mathcal{S}^{2}
. For all
t\leq T
, we define
\hat{\xi}_{t}:=\limsup\limits_{s\uparrow t,\;s<t}\xi_{s}
.
\hat{\xi}
is predictable (see Theorem 90, page 225 in
[
10
]
). It is left upper semi-continuous and is called the left upper semi-continuous envelope of
\xi
(cf, Definition
10
in Appendix).

Definition 4

(RBDSDELs)
.

Let
(f,g)
be a pair of Lipschitz drivers and
\xi
be an irregular barrier belongs to
\mathcal{S}^{2}
. The triple of processes
(Y,Z,K)
is said to be solution to RBDSDEL associated with parameters
(f,g,\xi)
if

\displaystyle\hskip-14.22636pt(i)\;(Y,Z,K)\in\mathcal{S}^{2}\times\mathcal{H}^{2}(\ell^{2})\times\mathcal{S}^{2},

\displaystyle\hskip-14.22636pt(ii)\;Y_{t}=\xi_{T}+\int_{t}^{T}f(s,Y_{s},Z_{s})ds+\int_{t}^{T}g(s,Y_{s},Z_{s})dB_{s}+K_{T}-K_{t}-\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\int_{t}^{T}Z_{s}^{(k)}dH_{s}^{(k)}\qquad t\leq T,

\displaystyle\hskip-14.22636pt(iii)\;Y_{t}\geq\xi_{t}\quad\forall t\leq T,

\displaystyle\hskip-14.22636pt(iv)\;K\mbox{ is a nondecreasing predictable process with regulated trajectories}

\displaystyle\quad\mbox{ such that }K_{0}=0,\;\;\mathbb{E}[K_{T}]<+\infty\mbox{ and }\quad\displaystyle\int_{0}^{T}(Y_{t-}-\hat{\xi}_{t})dK^{\ast}_{t}+\sum_{t<T}(Y_{t}-\xi_{t})\Delta_{+}K_{t}=0\;a.s.

Remark 5
.

If
(Y,Z,K,C)
is a solution to RBSDELs associated with parameters
(f,g,\xi)
, then the process
Y
has regulated trajectories. Moreover, the process
\left(Y_{t}+\int_{0}^{t}f(s,Y_{s},Z_{s})ds\right)_{t\leq T}
is an optional strong supermartingale.

Proposition 6
.

Let
(Y,Z)\in\mathcal{S}^{2}\times\mathcal{H}^{2}(\ell^{2})
where
Y
has a regulated trajectories. Then the process
\left(\sum\limits_{k=1}^{\infty}\int_{0}^{t}Y_{s-}Z_{s}^{(k)}dH_{s}^{(k)}\right)_{t\leq T}
is a martingale.

3
The main result: Existence and uniqueness of the solution

3.1
The uniqueness

Proposition 7
.

Let
(f,g)
be a pair of Lipschitz drivers and
\xi
be a completely irregular barrier. The RBDSDEL associated with parameters
(f,g,\xi)
has at most one solution.

Proof.

Let us consider two solutions
(Y,Z,K,)
and
(Y^{\prime},Z^{\prime},K^{\prime})
of RBDSDEL associated with parameters
(f,g,\xi)
. Denote
\overline{\Re}=\Re-\Re^{\prime}
for
\Re\in\{Y,Z,K\}
. By applying the Proposition
13
to
|\overline{Y}_{t}|^{2}
, we get

\displaystyle|\overline{Y}_{t}|^{2}+\int_{t}^{T}\|\overline{Z}_{s}\|_{\ell^{2}}^{2}ds

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 2\int_{t}^{T}\overline{Y}_{s}(f(s,Y_{s},Z_{s})-f^{\prime}(s,Y^{\prime}_{s},Z^{\prime}_{s}))ds+2\int_{t}^{T}\overline{Y}_{s}(g(s,Y_{s},Z_{s})-g^{\prime}(s,Y^{\prime}_{s},Z^{\prime}_{s}))dB_{s}

\displaystyle+2\int_{t}^{T}\overline{Y}_{s-}d\overline{K}^{\ast}_{s}-2\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\int_{t}^{T}\overline{Y}_{s-}\overline{Z}_{s}^{(k)}dH_{s}^{(k)}+\int_{t}^{T}|g(s,Y_{s},Z_{s})-g^{\prime}(s,Y^{\prime}_{s},Z^{\prime}_{s})|^{2}ds

\displaystyle-\sum_{t<s\leq T}|\Delta_{-}\overline{Y}_{s}|^{2}-\sum_{t\leq s<T}|\Delta_{+}\overline{Y}_{s}|^{2}+2\sum_{t\leq s<T}\overline{Y}_{s}\Delta_{+}\overline{K}_{s}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle(3L+\varrho L^{2})\int_{t}^{T}|\overline{Y}_{s}|^{2}ds+\left(\frac{1}{\varrho}+\alpha\right)\int_{t}^{T}\|\overline{Z}_{s}\|_{\ell^{2}}^{2}ds+2\int_{t}^{T}\overline{Y}_{s-}d\overline{K}^{\ast}_{s}+2\sum_{t\leq s<T}\overline{Y}_{s}\Delta_{+}\overline{K}_{s}

\displaystyle+2\int_{t}^{T}\overline{Y}_{s}(g(s,Y_{s},Z_{s})-g^{\prime}(s,Y^{\prime}_{s},Z^{\prime}_{s}))dB_{s}-2\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\int_{t}^{T}\overline{Y}_{s-}\overline{Z}_{s}^{(k)}dH_{s}^{(k)}

for some
\varrho>0
, where we have used the Lipschitz property of
(f,g)
. Thanks to the Skorokhod condition on
K
, we have

\displaystyle\int_{t}^{T}\overline{Y}_{s-}d\overline{K}^{\ast}_{s}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int_{t}^{T}(Y_{s-}-\hat{\xi}_{s})dK^{\ast}_{s}-\int_{t}^{T}(Y^{\prime}_{s-}-\hat{\xi}_{s})dK^{\ast}_{s}+\int_{t}^{T}(Y^{\prime}_{s-}-\hat{\xi}_{s})dK^{{}^{\prime}\ast}_{s}-\int_{t}^{T}(Y_{s-}-\hat{\xi}_{s})dK^{{}^{\prime}\ast}_{s}\leq 0

and

\displaystyle\sum_{t\leq s<T}\overline{Y}_{s}\Delta_{+}\overline{K}_{s}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{t\leq s<T}(Y_{s}-\xi_{s})\Delta_{+}K_{s}-\sum_{t\leq s<T}(Y^{\prime}_{s}-\xi_{s})\Delta_{+}K_{s}+\sum_{t\leq s<T}(Y^{\prime}_{s}-\xi_{s})\Delta_{+}K^{\prime}_{s}-\sum_{t\leq s<T}(Y_{s}-\xi_{s})\Delta_{+}K^{\prime}_{s}\leq 0.

Then for
\varrho>\frac{1}{1-\alpha}
we obtain

\mathbb{E}|\overline{Y}_{t}|^{2}+\mathbb{E}\int_{t}^{T}\|\overline{Z}_{s}\|_{\ell^{2}}^{2}ds\leq C_{{}_{L,\alpha}}\mathbb{E}\int_{t}^{T}|\overline{Y}_{s}|^{2}ds

where
C_{{}_{L,\alpha}}
is a positive constant which depends on
L
and
\alpha
. Consequently, according to Gronwall’s lemma we obtain
Y=Y^{\prime}
,
Z=Z^{\prime}
and thus
K=K^{\prime}
.
∎

3.2
The existence via penalization method

Let us first consider the special case when the coefficient
g
does not depend on the solution. We put
g(t,y,z):=\widetilde{g}(t)
such that
\mathbb{E}\int_{0}^{T}|\widetilde{g}(t)|^{2}dt<+\infty
.

Lemma 8
.

Let
f
be a Lipschitz driver and
\xi
be a completely irregular barrier. Then the RBDSDEL associated with parameters
(f,\widetilde{g},\xi)
admits a unique solution.

Proof.

We rely on a penalization method by approximating the irregular barrier
\xi
, which has been introduced by Klimsiak et al.
[
19
]
and later by Marzougue
[
24
]
. For each
n\in\mathbb{N}
, we consider the following penalized version of BDSDEL

\displaystyle Y^{n}_{t}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\xi_{T}+\int_{t}^{T}f(s,Y^{n}_{s},Z^{n}_{s})ds+\int_{t}^{T}\widetilde{g}(s)dB_{s}-\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\int_{t}^{T}Z_{s}^{(k),n}dH_{s}^{(k)}

(3.1)

\displaystyle+n\int_{t}^{T}(Y^{n}_{s}-\xi_{s})^{-}ds+\sum_{t\leq\sigma_{n,i}<T}(Y^{n}_{\sigma_{n,i}+}-\xi_{\sigma_{n,i}})^{-}

with specially defied arrays of stopping times
\{\sigma_{n,i}\}
exhausting right-side jumps of
\xi
.
We define
\{\sigma_{n,i}\}
inductively. We fist set

\displaystyle\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\sigma_{1,0}=0,&\hbox{}\\
\sigma_{1,i}=\inf\{t>\sigma_{1,i-1}\;\;|\;\;\Delta_{+}\xi_{t}<-1\}\wedge T,&\hbox{\quad$i=1,...,k_{1}$ for $k_{1}\in\mathbb{N}$.}\end{array}\right.

Next, for
n\in\mathbb{N}
and given array
\{\sigma_{n,i}\}
we set

\displaystyle\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\sigma_{n+1,0}=0,&\hbox{}\\
\sigma_{n+1,i}=\inf\{t>\sigma_{n+1,i-1}\;\;|\;\;\Delta_{+}\xi_{t}<-\frac{1}{n+1}\}\wedge T,&\hbox{\quad$i=1,...,j_{n+1}$}\end{array}\right.

with
j_{n+1}
is chosen so that
\mathbb{P}(\sigma_{n+1,j_{n+1}}<T)\rightarrow 0
as
n\rightarrow+\infty
and

\sigma_{n+1,i}=\sigma_{n+1,j_{n+1}}\vee\sigma_{n,i-j_{n+1}-1},\quad i=j_{n+1}+1,...,k_{n+1},\quad k_{n+1}=j_{n+1}+k_{n}+1.

According to the work of Ren et al.
[
37
]
, on each interval
(\sigma_{n,i-1},\sigma_{n,i}]
,
i=1,...,k_{n}+1
with
\sigma_{n,k_{n}+1}=T
, there exists a unique process
(Y^{n},Z^{n})
solution of the following BDSDEL

\displaystyle Y^{n}_{t}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\xi_{\sigma_{n,i}}\vee Y^{n}_{\sigma_{n,i}+}+\int_{t}^{\sigma_{n,i}}f(s,Y^{n}_{s},Z^{n}_{s})ds+\int_{t}^{\sigma_{n,i}}\widetilde{g}(s)dB_{s}

(3.2)

\displaystyle+n\int_{t}^{\sigma_{n,i}}(Y^{n}_{s}-\xi_{s})^{-}ds-\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\int_{t}^{T}Z_{s}^{(k),n}dH_{s}^{(k)}\qquad t\in(\sigma_{n,i-1},\sigma_{n,i}]

with the convention
Y^{n}_{T}=\xi_{T}
and
Y^{n}_{0}=\xi_{0}\vee Y^{n}_{0+}
. On the other hand, the BDSDEL (
3.1
) can be written as

Y^{n}_{t}=\xi_{T}+\int_{t}^{T}f(s,Y^{n}_{s},Z^{n}_{s})ds+\int_{t}^{T}\widetilde{g}(s)dB_{s}+K^{n}_{T}-K^{n}_{t}-\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\int_{t}^{T}Z_{s}^{(k),n}dH_{s}^{(k)}

(3.3)

where

K^{n}_{t}:=K^{n,\ast}_{t}+\sum_{0\leq s<t}\Delta_{+}K^{n}_{s}=n\int_{0}^{t}(Y^{n}_{s}-\xi_{s})^{-}ds+\sum_{0\leq\sigma_{n,i}<t}(Y^{n}_{\sigma_{n,i}+}-\xi_{\sigma_{n,i}})^{-}.

It remains to establish the convergence of the sequence
(Y^{n},Z^{n},K^{n})_{n\geq 1}
to the solution of the RBDSDEL associated with parameters
(f,\widetilde{g},\xi)
. For this end, we divided the proof into four steps:

Step 1: A priori estimate.

There exists a positive constant
\mathcal{C}_{{}_{\beta,L}}
independent on
n
such that for all
\beta
large enough

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{\tau\in\mathcal{T}_{[0,T]}}e^{\beta\tau}|Y^{n}_{\tau}|^{2}+\mathbb{E}\int_{0}^{T}e^{\beta s}|Y^{n}_{s}|^{2}ds+\mathbb{E}\int_{0}^{T}e^{\beta s}\|Z^{n}_{s}\|_{\ell^{2}}^{2}ds+\mathbb{E}|K^{n}_{T}|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\mathcal{C}_{{}_{\beta,L}}\left(\mathbb{E}\operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{\tau\in\mathcal{T}_{[0,T]}}e^{2\beta\tau}|\xi_{\tau}|^{2}+\mathbb{E}\int_{0}^{T}e^{\beta s}|f(s,0,0)|^{2}ds+\mathbb{E}\int_{0}^{T}e^{\beta s}|\widetilde{g}(s)|^{2}ds\right).

Indeed, by applying the Proposition
13
to
e^{\beta t}|Y^{n}_{t}|^{2}
, we have

\displaystyle e^{\beta t}|Y^{n}_{t}|^{2}+\beta\int_{t}^{T}e^{\beta s}|Y^{n}_{s}|^{2}ds+\int_{t}^{T}e^{\beta s}\|Z^{n}_{s}\|_{\ell^{2}}^{2}ds

(3.4)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle e^{\beta T}|\xi_{T}|^{2}+2\int_{t}^{T}e^{\beta s}Y^{n}_{s}f(s,Y^{n}_{s},Z^{n}_{s})ds+2\int_{t}^{T}e^{\beta s}Y^{n}_{s}\widetilde{g}(s)dB_{s}

\displaystyle+2\int_{t}^{T}e^{\beta s}Y^{n}_{s-}dK^{n,\ast}_{s}-2\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\int_{t}^{T}e^{\beta s}Y^{n}_{s-}Z_{s}^{(k),n}dH_{s}^{(k)}+\int_{t}^{T}e^{\beta s}|\widetilde{g}(s)|^{2}ds

\displaystyle-\sum_{t<s\leq T}e^{\beta s}|\Delta Y^{n}_{s}|^{2}-\sum_{t\leq s<T}e^{\beta s}(|Y^{n}_{s+}|^{2}-|Y^{n}_{s}|^{2}).

Observe that

Moreover, according to the Lipschitz property of

\displaystyle 2\int_{t}^{T}e^{\beta s}Y^{n}_{s}f(s,Y^{n}_{s},Z^{n}_{s})ds

\displaystyle\varrho_{{}_{2}}\int_{t}^{T}e^{\beta s}|Y^{n}_{s}|^{2}ds+\frac{1}{\varrho_{{}_{2}}}\int_{t}^{T}e^{\beta s}\left|f(s,Y^{n}_{s},Z^{n}_{s})\right|^{2}ds

\displaystyle\left(\varrho_{{}_{2}}+\frac{3L^{2}}{\varrho_{{}_{2}}}\right)\int_{t}^{T}e^{\beta s}|Y^{n}_{s}|^{2}ds+\frac{3L^{2}}{\varrho_{{}_{2}}}\int_{t}^{T}e^{\beta s}\|Z^{n}_{s}\|_{\ell^{2}}^{2}ds

for some

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{t}e^{\beta s}Y^{n}_{s-}dK^{n,\ast}_{s}+\sum_{s<t}e^{\beta s}Y^{n}_{s}\Delta_{+}K^{n}_{s}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{t}e^{\beta s}Y^{n}_{s-}dK^{n}_{s}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{t}e^{\beta s}Y^{n}_{s}n(Y^{n}_{s}-\xi_{s})^{-}ds

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{t}e^{\beta s}\xi_{s}n(Y^{n}_{s}-\xi_{s})^{-}ds-\int_{0}^{t}e^{\beta s}n((Y^{n}_{s}-\xi_{s})^{-})^{2}ds

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{\varrho_{{}_{3}}}{2}\operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{\tau\in\mathcal{T}_{[0,T]}}e^{2\beta\tau}|\xi_{\tau}|^{2}+\frac{1}{2\varrho_{{}_{3}}}\left|K^{n}_{T}\right|^{2},

\displaystyle\left.+\varrho_{{}_{3}}\mathbb{E}\operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{\tau\in\mathcal{T}_{[0,T]}}e^{2\beta\tau}|\xi_{\tau}|^{2}+\frac{1}{\varrho_{{}_{3}}}\mathbb{E}\left|K^{n}_{T}\right|^{2}\right)

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\int_{0}^{T}e^{\beta s}|Y^{n}_{s}|^{2}ds+\mathbb{E}\int_{0}^{T}e^{\beta s}\|Z^{n}_{s}\|_{\ell^{2}}^{2}ds+\mathbb{E}|K^{n}_{T}|^{2}

Step 3: Strong convergence result.

The main result of this section is the following:

Theorem 9
.

Let
(f,g)
be a pair of Lipschitz drivers and
\xi
be a completely irregular barrier. Then the RBDSDEL associated with parameters
(f,g,\xi)
admits a unique solution.

Proof.

Denote
\mathfrak{B}^{2}_{\beta}:=\mathcal{H}^{2}_{\beta}\times\mathcal{H}^{2}_{\beta}(\ell^{2})
the Banach space endowed with the norm

\|(Y,Z)\|^{2}_{\mathfrak{B}^{2}_{\beta}}=\mathbb{E}\int_{0}^{T}e^{\beta t}|Y_{t}|^{2}dt+\mathbb{E}\int_{0}^{T}e^{\beta t}\|Z_{t}\|_{\ell^{2}}^{2}dt.

Given
(y,z)\in\mathfrak{B}^{2}_{\beta}
and consider the following RBDSDEL

Y_{t}=\xi+\int_{t}^{T}f(s,Y_{s},Z_{s})ds+\int_{t}^{T}g(s,y_{s},z_{s})dB_{s}+K_{T}-K_{t}-\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\int_{t}^{T}Z_{s}^{(k)}dH_{s}^{(k)}.

(3.7)

By the Lipschitz property on
g
, we have
\mathbb{E}\int_{0}^{T}|g(t,y_{t},z_{t})|^{2}dt<+\infty
. Then, from Lemma
8
, the RBDSDEL (
3.7
) admits a unique solution.
Next, we define a mapping
\Phi
from
\mathfrak{B}^{2}_{\beta}
into itself such that for any
(y,z)
and
(y^{\prime},z^{\prime})
in
\mathfrak{B}^{2}_{\beta}
,
\Phi(y,z)=(Y,Z)
and
\Phi(y^{\prime},z^{\prime})=(Y^{\prime},Z^{\prime})
where
(Y,Z,K)
and
(Y^{\prime},Z^{\prime},K^{\prime})
are the solutions of the RBDSDEL associated with parameters
(f,g(.,y,z),\xi)
and
(f,g(.,y^{\prime},z^{\prime}),\xi)
respectively. Set
\bar{\Re}=\Re-\Re^{\prime}
for
\Re\in\{Y,Z,K,y,z\}
, and we put
\bar{f}_{t}=f(t,Y_{t},Z_{t})-f(t,Y^{\prime}_{t},Z^{\prime}_{t})
and
\bar{g}_{t}=g(t,y_{t},z_{t})-g(t,y^{\prime}_{t},z^{\prime}_{t})
for all
t\leq T
. By applying the Proposition
13
to
e^{\beta t}|\bar{Y}_{t}|^{2}
we have

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}e^{\beta t}|\bar{Y}_{t}|^{2}+\beta\mathbb{E}\int_{t}^{T}e^{\beta s}|\bar{Y}_{s}|^{2}ds+\mathbb{E}\int_{t}^{T}e^{\beta s}\|\bar{Z}_{s}\|_{\ell^{2}}^{2}ds

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 2\mathbb{E}\int_{t}^{T}e^{\beta s}\bar{Y}_{s}\bar{f}_{s}ds+2\mathbb{E}\int_{t}^{T}e^{\beta s}\bar{Y}_{s-}d\bar{K}^{\ast}_{s}+\mathbb{E}\int_{t}^{T}e^{\beta s}|\bar{g}_{s}|^{2}ds

\displaystyle-\mathbb{E}\sum_{t<s\leq T}e^{\beta s}|\Delta\bar{Y}_{s}|^{2}-\mathbb{E}\sum_{t\leq s<T}e^{\beta s}|\Delta_{+}\bar{Y}_{s}|^{2}+2\mathbb{E}\sum_{t\leq s<T}e^{\beta s}\bar{Y}_{s}\Delta_{+}\bar{K}_{s}.

Thanks to the Skorokhod condition on
K
, we have
\int_{t}^{T}e^{\beta s}\bar{Y}_{s-}d\bar{K}^{\ast}_{s}\leq 0

and

\displaystyle\sum_{t\leq s<T}e^{\beta s}\bar{Y}_{s}\Delta_{+}\bar{K}_{s}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{t\leq s<T}e^{\beta s}(Y_{s}-\xi_{s})\Delta_{+}K_{s}-\sum_{t\leq s<T}e^{\beta s}(Y^{\prime}_{s}-\xi_{s})\Delta_{+}K_{s}

\displaystyle+\sum_{t\leq s<T}e^{\beta s}(Y^{\prime}_{s}-\xi_{s})\Delta_{+}K^{\prime}_{s}-\sum_{t\leq s<T}e^{\beta s}(Y_{s}-\xi_{s})\Delta_{+}K^{\prime}_{s}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle 0.

Moreover, from the Lipschitz property on
f
and
g
we deduce for some
\varrho_{{}_{4}}>0

\displaystyle 2\int_{t}^{T}e^{\beta s}\bar{Y}_{s}\bar{f}_{s}ds

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle 2\int_{t}^{T}e^{\beta s}L\bar{Y}_{s}(\bar{Y}_{s}+\|\bar{Z}_{s}\|_{\ell^{2}})ds

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle 2L(1+L)\int_{t}^{T}e^{\beta s}|\bar{Y}_{s}|^{2}ds+\frac{1}{2}\int_{t}^{T}e^{\beta s}\|\bar{Z}_{s}\|_{\ell^{2}}^{2}ds

and

\int_{t}^{T}e^{\beta s}|\bar{g}_{s}|^{2}ds\leq L\int_{t}^{T}e^{\beta s}|\bar{y}_{s}|^{2}ds+\alpha\int_{t}^{T}e^{\beta s}\|\bar{z}_{s}\|_{\ell^{2}}^{2}ds.

Then

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\left(\Lambda_{1}\int_{t}^{T}e^{\beta s}|\bar{Y}_{s}|^{2}ds+\frac{1}{2}\int_{t}^{T}e^{\beta s}\|\bar{Z}_{s}\|_{\ell^{2}}^{2}ds\right)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle 2\alpha\mathbb{E}\left(\Lambda_{1}\int_{t}^{T}e^{\beta s}|\bar{y}_{s}|^{2}ds+\frac{1}{2}\int_{t}^{T}e^{\beta s}\|\bar{z}_{s}\|_{\ell^{2}}^{2}ds\right)

where
\Lambda_{1}=\frac{L}{2\alpha}
and
\beta
is chosen such that
\beta>\Lambda_{1}+2L(1+L)
. Consequently, the mapping
\Phi
is a contraction and then has a unique fixed point
(Y,Z)
which actually belongs to
\mathfrak{B}^{2}_{\beta}
. Moreover, there exists
K\in\mathcal{S}^{2}

(K_{0}=0)
such that
(Y,Z,K)
is the unique solution of the RBDSDEL associated with parameters
(f,g,\xi)
.
∎

In this section we summarize the principal tools used in our proofs throughout the paper.

Definition 10
.

Let
\tau\in\mathcal{T}_{[0,T]}
. An optional process
(\xi_{t})_{t\leq T}
is said to be left upper-semicontinuous along stopping times at the stopping time
\tau
if for all nondecreasing sequence of stopping times
(\tau_{n})_{n\geq 0}
such that
\tau_{n}\uparrow\tau
a.s.,
\xi_{\tau}\geq\limsup_{n\rightarrow+\infty}\xi_{\tau_{n}}
a.s. The process
(\xi_{t})_{t\leq T}
is said to be left upper-semicontinuous along stopping times if it is left upper-semicontinuous along stopping times at each
\tau\in\mathcal{T}_{[0,T]}
.

[
28
]
))

Every random variable
M
in
\mathbb{L}^{2}(\Omega,\mathcal{G})
has a representation of the form

M=\mathbb{E}[M]+\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\int_{0}^{T}Z_{s}^{(k)}dH_{s}^{(k)}

where
\left\{Z_{s}^{(k)};\;\;k=1,...\infty\right\}
are predictable.

[

]

Let
\tilde{Y}
be a strong optional supermartingale of class(D). There exists a unique uniformly integrable locale martingale

K

Proposition 13

(Itô’s formula for regulated processes)
.

Let
Y
be a semimartingale with regulated trajectories and
F
be a twice continuously differentiable function on

. Then, almost surely, for each
n\in\mathbb{N}
and all
t\geq 0
,

\displaystyle F(Y_{t})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle F(Y_{0})+\sum_{k=1}^{n}\int_{0}^{t}D^{k}F(Y_{s-})dY^{\ast,k}_{s}+\frac{1}{2}\sum_{k,l=1}^{n}\int_{0}^{t}D^{k}D^{l}F(Y_{s-})d[Y^{\ast,k},Y^{\ast,l}]^{c}_{s}

\displaystyle+\sum_{0<s\leq t}\left[F(Y_{s})-F(Y_{s-})-\sum_{k=1}^{n}D^{k}F(Y_{s-})\Delta Y^{k}_{s}\right]+\sum_{0\leq s<t}\left[F(Y_{s+})-F(Y_{s})\right],

In what follows a special comparison theorem for the solutions to BDSDEs without reflection.

Proposition 14

(BDSDE’s comparison theorem)
.

Let
(Y^{i},Z^{i})
be a solution of the following BDSDE (associated with parameters
(\xi^{i},f^{i},g)
)

Y^{i}_{t}=\xi^{i}+\int_{t}^{T}f^{i}(s,Y^{i}_{s},Z^{i}_{s})ds+\int_{t}^{T}\widetilde{g}(s)dB_{s}-\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\int_{t}^{T}Z_{s}^{(k),i}dH_{s}^{(k)}

where
f^{i}
are Lipschitz drivers for
i\in\{1,2\}
,
\mathbb{E}\int_{0}^{T}|\widetilde{g}(t)|^{2}dt<+\infty
and
\mathbb{E}|\xi|^{2}<+\infty
.
We suppose that
\xi^{1}\leq\xi^{2}
,
f^{1}(t,y,z)\leq f^{2}(t,y,z)

\forall(t,y,z)\in[0,T]\times\mathbb{R}\times\ell^{2}
and

\zeta^{k}_{t}=\frac{f^{1}\left(t,Y^{2}_{t},\tilde{Z}^{(k-1)}_{t}\right)-f^{1}\left(t,Y^{2}_{t},\tilde{Z}^{(k)}_{t}\right)}{Z^{(k),1}_{t}-Z^{(k),2}_{t}}\mathds{1}_{\{Z^{(k),1}_{t}-Z^{(k),2}_{t}\neq 0\}},

where

\tilde{Z}^{(k)}_{t}=\left(Z^{(1),2}_{t},Z^{(2),2}_{t},\ldots,Z^{(k),2}_{t},Z^{(k+1),1}_{t},\ldots,Z^{(d),1}_{t}\right)

such that

\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\zeta_{t}^{k}\Delta H_{t}^{(k)}>-1\qquad dt\otimes d\mathbb{P}-\mbox{a.s.}

(4.1)

Then
\forall t\leq T
,
Y^{1}_{t}\leq Y^{2}_{t}
a.s.

Proof.

Define
\widehat{\Re}=\Re^{1}-\Re^{2}
for
\Re\in\{Y,Z,\xi\}
. Then the process
(\widehat{Y},\widehat{Z})
satisfies the following equation

\widehat{Y}_{t}=\widehat{\xi}+\int_{t}^{T}\left(p_{s}\widehat{Y}_{s}+\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\zeta^{k}_{s}\widehat{Z}_{s}^{(k)}+u_{s}\right)ds-\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\int_{t}^{T}\widehat{Z}_{s}^{(k)}dH_{s}^{(k)}

where

1.

p_{t}=\displaystyle\frac{f^{1}(t,Y^{1}_{t},Z^{1}_{t})-f^{1}(t,Y^{2}_{t},Z^{1}_{t})}{Y^{1}_{t}-Y^{2}_{t}}\mathds{1}_{\{Y^{1}_{t}-Y^{2}_{t}\neq 0\}}
;

2.

u_{t}=f^{1}(t,Y^{2}_{t},Z^{2}_{t})-f^{2}(t,Y^{2}_{t},Z^{2}_{t})
.

Since the solutions of BDSDE are square integrable then thanks to Theorem 37 page 84 in Protter
[
35
]
, for
0\leq s\leq t\leq T
, the following linear SDE

\Gamma_{s,t}=1+\int_{s}^{t}\Gamma_{s,r-}d\mathcal{X}_{r}

with

\mathcal{X}_{t}=\int_{0}^{t}p_{s}ds+\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\int_{0}^{t}\zeta_{s}^{k}dH_{s}^{(k)}

admits a unique solution of the form

\Gamma_{s,t}=\exp\left(\mathcal{X}_{t}-\mathcal{X}_{s}\right)\times\prod_{s\leq r\leq t}\left(1+\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\zeta_{r}^{k}\Delta H_{r}^{(k)}\right)\exp\left(-\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\zeta_{r}^{k}\Delta H_{r}^{(k)}\right).

This solution is strictly positive according to the assumption (
4.1
). Now, by applying Itô’s formula to
\Gamma_{s,t}\widehat{Y}_{t}
we get

\displaystyle\Gamma_{s,t}\widehat{Y}_{t}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\Gamma_{s,T}\widehat{\xi}-\int_{t}^{T}\Gamma_{s,r-}d\widehat{Y}_{r}-\int_{t}^{T}\widehat{Y}_{r}d\Gamma_{s,r}-\int_{t}^{T}d[\Gamma,\widehat{Y}]_{r}

(4.2)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\Gamma_{s,T}\widehat{\xi}+\int_{t}^{T}\Gamma_{s,r}\left(p_{r}\widehat{Y}_{r}+\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\zeta^{k}_{r}\widehat{Z}_{r}^{(k)}+u_{r}\right)dr-\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\int_{t}^{T}\Gamma_{s,r-}\widehat{Z}_{r}^{(k)}dH_{r}^{(k)}

\displaystyle-\int_{t}^{T}\Gamma_{s,r}p_{r}\widehat{Y}_{r}dr-\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\int_{t}^{T}\Gamma_{s,r-}\widehat{Y}_{r}\zeta^{k}_{r}dH_{r}^{(k)}-\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\int_{t}^{T}\Gamma_{s,r}\zeta^{k}_{r}\widehat{Z}_{r}^{(k)}d[H^{(k)},H^{(k)}]_{r}.

But,

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\left(\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\int_{t}^{T}\Gamma_{s,r}\zeta^{k}_{r}\widehat{Z}_{r}^{(k)}d[H^{(k)},H^{(k)}]_{r}|\mathcal{G}_{t}\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\left(\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\int_{t}^{T}\Gamma_{s,r}\zeta^{k}_{r}\widehat{Z}_{r}^{(k)}d\langle H^{(k)},H^{(k)}\rangle_{r}|\mathcal{G}_{t}\right)

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\left(\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\int_{t}^{T}\Gamma_{s,r}\zeta^{k}_{r}\widehat{Z}_{r}^{(k)}dr|\mathcal{G}_{t}\right).

Thus, by taking the conditional expectation w.r.t
\mathcal{G}_{t}
on both sides of the equality (

\Gamma_{s,t}\widehat{Y}_{t}=\mathbb{E}\left(\Gamma_{s,T}\widehat{\xi}+\int_{t}^{T}\Gamma_{s,r}u_{r}dr|\mathcal{G}_{t}\right)\leq 0

in view of
\Gamma_{s,r}>0
,
\widehat{\xi}\leq 0
and

\forall t\leq T

Y_{t}^{1}\leq Y_{t}^{2}
a.s.
