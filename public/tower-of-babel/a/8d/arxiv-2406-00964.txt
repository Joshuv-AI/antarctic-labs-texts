1.
Introduction

The aim of this paper is to present a new formulation of the vertex operators
of the elliptic quantum toroidal algebra (EQTA)
U_{t_{1},t_{2},p}({\mathfrak{gl}}_{1,tor})
by combining its representations and the notions of the elliptic stable envelopes for the instanton moduli space
{\mathcal{M}}(n,r)

The EQTA

is an elliptic quantum group associated with the toroidal algebra of type
{\mathfrak{gl}}_{1}
formulated in
[
32

and

and

[
32
]

It is defined by generators and relations in the same scheme as the elliptic quantum group (EQG)

associated with the affine Lie algebra

[

19
,
7

31

and equipped with
a Hopf algebroid structure given by the Drinfeld comultiplication
\Delta^{D}
, i.e. a comultiplication formula in terms of the Drinfeld generators or their generating functions called the elliptic currents.
Two types of vertex operators, the type I and the type II dual, have been constructed as intertwining operators of
U_{t_{1},t_{2},p}({\mathfrak{gl}}_{1,tor})
-modules w.r.t.
\Delta^{D}
and shown to give a realization of the generating function
T(u)
of the affine quiver
W
-algebra associated with the Jordan quiver
\Gamma(\widehat{A}_{0})
[
20
]

At the same time, the elliptic currents of
U_{t_{1},t_{2},p}({\mathfrak{gl}}_{1,tor})
gives the screening currents of it.
In addition, it has turned out that these vertex operators and

realize the refined topological vertices
[

16

relevant to calculate the instanton partition functions of the 5d and 6d lift of the 4d

[

However their relations to the elliptic stable envelopes and to the vertex functions of the corresponding quiver variety were missing.

The notion of stable envelopes was initiated in
[
35
,
41
]
. They provide a good basis of the equivariant cohomology and the
\mathrm{K}
-theory of Nakajima quiver varieties

[
37

38

R
-matrices satisfying the Yang-Baxter equation (YBE). Regarding such
R
-matrix as universal one i.e.
a
L
-operator which generate a quantum group, the transition property can be understood as
a Gauss decomposition of the
L
-operator in terms of the stable envelopes.
Hence the stable envelopes provide a new geometric formulation of quantum groups as well as quantum integrable systems associated with the quiver varieties.
The elliptic version of stable envelopes (ESE)
[
1
]
for the equivariant elliptic cohomology
\mathrm{E}_{T}(X)

is more fascinating. They depend on both the equivariant and the Kähler parameters and provide the elliptic dynamical
R
-matrix as their transition matrix. There these two parameters play a role of the spectral and the dynamical parameters, respectively.

An typical example is the case
X=T^{*}fl
, the cotangent bundle to the partial flag variety. The ESE’s for
\mathrm{E}_{T}(T^{*}fl)
have been identified with the elliptic weight functions of the
A

[

29
,
43

-matrices of type
A
[
17
,
12
,
18
,
24

In
[
28

29

-modules w.r.t the standard comultiplication given in terms of the
L
-operator instead of the Drinfeld comultiplication. Basically such vertex operators are operator valued integrals, the so called screened vertex operators, possessing basic elliptic weight functions i.e. basic elliptic stable envelopes, as their integration kernels. Then a composition of them produces more general ESEs
corresponding to general configurations in
T^{*}fl
via the shuffle product formula
[
29

-operator
of

22

L
-operators.

In addition, for the same case

-theoretic vertex function

to

-hypergeometric integral containing the elliptic weight function inside the integral and satisfies certain difference equations. The
monodromy matrix of it is then given by a transition matrix of the weight functions. In
[
1

Motivated by such nice relationship between representation theory of

3.1.
The instanton moduli space

Let

be the moduli space of framed rank

{\mathbb{P}}^{2}

means a choice of isomorphism

\displaystyle{\mathcal{S}}|_{{\mathbb{P}}}\ \to\ {\mathcal{O}}^{\oplus r}_{L_{\infty}},

where

{\mathbb{P}}^{2}

The space
{\mathcal{M}}(n,r)
is isomorphic to the Nakajima quiver variety associated to the quiver consisting of one vertex and one loop with the vertex dimension
n
and the framing dimension

One has a natural action of
G=GL(r)\times GL(2)
on

T
be the maximal torus of
G
and set

\displaystyle A=T\cap GL(r).

The parameters
t_{1},t_{2}
are identified with the generators of the character group of

. Note also that the rank 1 case is isomorphic to the Hilbert scheme of
n
-points on
{\mathbb{C}}^{2}

\displaystyle{\mathcal{M}}(n,1)\cong\mathrm{Hilb}^{n}({\mathbb{C}}^{2}).

We also have the following isomorphism for the fixed points w.r.t

\displaystyle{\mathcal{M}}(n,r)^{A}=\coprod_{n_{1}+\cdots+n_{r}=n}\mathrm{Hilb}^{n_{1}}({\mathbb{C}}^{2})\times\cdots\times\mathrm{Hilb}^{n_{r}}({\mathbb{C}}^{2}).

Let us consider the case

, the Hilbert scheme

,

We denote the coordinate on

\displaystyle t_{1}=u\hbar^{1/2},\qquad t_{2}=u^{-1}\hbar^{1/2}.

The

Let

\displaystyle{\mathcal{P}}_{n}=\{\ \lambda\in{\mathcal{P}}\ |\ |\lambda|=n\ \}.

(3.1)

n

\displaystyle a<b\ \Leftrightarrow\ \rho_{a}<\rho_{b}\qquad a,b\in\lambda
