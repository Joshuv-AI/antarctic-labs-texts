Abstract
. Let K K be an ( n − 1 ) (n-1) -dimensional piecewise linear sphere on [ m ] [m] , where m ≤ n + 4 m\leq n+4 . There are a canonical action of m m -dimensional torus T m T^{m} on the moment-angle complex 𝒵 K \mathcal{Z}_{K} , and a canonical action of ℤ 2 m \mathbb{Z}_{2}^{m} on the real moment-angle complex ℝ ​ 𝒵 K \mathbb{R}\mathcal{Z}_{K} , where ℤ 2 \mathbb{Z}_{2} is the additive group with two elements. Introduction

Let

We define the

(\underline{X},\underline{Y})^{K}\coloneqq\bigcup_{\sigma\in K}\left\{(x_{1},\ldots,x_{m})\in X^{m}\mid x_{i}\in Y\text{ when }i\notin\sigma\right\}.

Here,

The

of

(\underline{D^{2}},\underline{S^{1}})^{K}

of

We observe that the

on
\mathcal{Z}_{K}
.
Additionally, there is an

-action on the pair
(D^{1},S^{0})

For clarity and consistency in our terminology throughout this paper, we treat

as the additive group

This, then, yields the canonical

It is noteworthy that when an

Such spaces are commonly referred to as

Consequently, understanding which subtori

The Buchstaber number

.
Similarly, taking a subgroup

The real Buchstaber number

The determination of (real) Buchstaber numbers is challenging.

In the class of PL spheres, no examples have been known where

In light of this observation, one may ask whether

Problem 1.1

Let

Given a subgroup of

In other words, this asks whether every small cover (or real topological toric manifold) is induced from some quasitoric manifold (or topological toric manifold, respectively).
The lifting problem was initially proposed by Zhi Lü at the toric topology conference held in Osaka in 2011, as documented in
[

However, significant advances in resolving this problem have been elusive.
This paper aims to make a contribution by providing meaningful results to the lifting problem, and more broadly to Problem

Theorem 1.2
.

Let

Then, any subgroup of

freely acting on
\mathcal{Z}_{K}
.

Let

\mathbb{Z}_{2}^{m}

Then,

To address this question, we categorize our approach into two distinct cases: the case where

.
In Section

It demonstrates that Theorem

holds for the case where

Subsequent sections will focus on the case

Here, we establish the theorem for this case employing a method we call

This method was firstly introduced by Choi and Park in

Additionally, the combinatorial structure of a binary matroid of rank

2.
The case

Let
K
be an
(n-1)
-dimensional simplicial complex on
[m]=\{1,2,\ldots,m\}
,
H\subset T^{m}
a subtorus of dimension

.
After choosing a basis, it can be written as

(2.1)

H=\{(e^{2\pi i(s_{11}\phi_{1}+\dots+s_{1r}\phi_{r})},\ldots,e^{2\pi i(s_{m1}\phi_{1}+\dots+s_{mr}\phi_{r})})\in T^{m}\mid\phi_{j}\in\mathbb{R},j=1,...,r\},

We define an

.
Additionally, the

The following proposition was proved for polytopal simplicial complexes in

Proposition 2.1
.

Let

be a simplicial complex. Then the subtorus (

) acts freely on
\mathcal{Z}_{K}
if and only if for any facet

of

defined above gives a monomorphism

to a direct summand.

The latter condition is equivalent to

having an

submatrix whose determinant is

.
A similar argument holds for

\mathbb{R}\mathcal{Z}_{K}
.
Then the mod

reduction of the matrix

on

Theorem 2.2
.

Let

be an
(n-1)
-dimensional simplicial complex on
[m]
, and

Then any rank

\mathbb{Z}_{2}^{m}
acting freely on

freely acting on
\mathcal{Z}_{K}

Proof.

Assume an

over

represents the freely acting subgroup of

Define the

Remark 4.6
