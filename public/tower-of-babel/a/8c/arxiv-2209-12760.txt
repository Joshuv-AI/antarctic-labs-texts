On Minimal Sums of Tensor Product of Sequences in Separable Hilbert Spaces
Abdelkrim Bourouihiya, Samir Kabbaj

Abstract
. It is known that the tensor product of two sequences, in the tensor product of two separable Hilbert spaces, is a frame if and only if each component of that product is a frame. This paper proposes a sort of generalization of the aforementioned result by dealing with sequences S that are finite minimal sums of tensor products of a finite number of sequences. We prove that S is a Bessel sequence if and only if it is a sum for which each term is the tensor product of Bessel sequences. We also state necessary conditions for S to be a frame. For dimensions d greater than one, we deduce several results on Gabor systems generated by finite rank square integrable functions. Surprisingly, the one dimensional versions of some of these results are extremely difficult to prove or disapprove.

(iii)

Proof.

Let

\displaystyle\forall k=1,2,\quad F_{k}=Ass(\{\bigotimes_{j=1}^{d}e_{j,n_{j}}\},\mathcal{F}_{k})=\bigotimes_{j=1}^{d}F_{j,k}

(i)

Gabor Systems generated by Finite Rank Windows

Using Theorem
1.3
, we can deduce the following proposition for Gabor systems.

Proposition 4.1
.

Let
d>1
. Let
g
be a rank
r
function in
L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{d})
, i.e.,

(4.1)

\displaystyle g=\sum_{k=1}^{r}\bigotimes_{j=1}^{d}g_{j,k},

where,
\forall j\in\{1,\dots,d\}

\mathcal{G}(g,\prod_{j=1}^{d}\Lambda_{j})
is a Bessel sequence in

if and only if
\mathcal{G}(g_{j,k},\Lambda_{j})
is a Bessel sequence in

for each
(j,k)\in\{1,\dots,d\}\times\{1,\dots,r\}
.

Remark 4.2
.

If
\mathcal{F}
is a sum of
r
independent sequences in
\mathcal{H}
, the fact that
\mathcal{F}
is a Bessel sequence does not necessary implies that each term in
\mathcal{F}
is a Bessel sequence. In addition, if
\mathcal{F}
is assumed to be a Bessel sequence, it is extremely difficult to prove whether each term in
\mathcal{F}
is a Bessel sequence or not. In particular, the one dimensional version of Proposition

does not always hold.

The following density theorem for general rectangular Gabor systems is a particular case of the density theorem for lattices that is proved in
[
5
]
.

Theorem 4.3
.

Let

and let

(i)

If
\mathcal{G}(g,a,b)

(ii)

\mathcal{G}(g,a,b)
is a Riesz basis if and only if

is a frame and

For the rest of this section, we assume that

On the one hand, if
\prod_{j=1}^{d}a_{j}b_{j}\leq 1

is a frame

Lemma 4.6
.

Let
g\in L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{d})\setminus\{0\}
.

(i)

If
\mathcal{G}(g,a,b)
be a frame with the frame bounds
A
and
B
, then, for all positive integers

\mathcal{G}\big(g,(a_{1}/u_{1},\dots a_{d}/u_{d}),(b_{1}/v_{1},\dots b_{d}/v_{d})\big)

u_{1}\dots u_{d}A
and
v_{1}\dots v_{d}B
.

(ii)

If
\mathcal{G}(g,a,b)
is a Bessel sequence, then so is
\mathcal{G}(g,\langle u,a\rangle,\langle v,b\rangle)
for all
u,v\in\mathbb{Q}^{d}
such that
u_{1},v_{1},\dots u_{d},v_{d}>0
.

Proof.

For the sake of notation simplicity, we prove the lemma for
d=1
.

(i)

Assume that
\mathcal{G}(g,a,b)
is a frame in
L^{2}(\mathbb{R})
with the frame bounds
A
and
B
and let
(u,v)
be a pair of positive integers. Hence,
\mathcal{G}(M_{jb/v}T_{ia/u}g,a,b)
is a frame with the frame bounds
A
and
B
,
\forall(i,j)\in\{0,\dots,u-1\}\times\{0,\dots,v-1\}
, and so, for each
f\in L^{2}(\mathbb{R})
, we have

\displaystyle A\|f\|^{2}\leq\sum_{m,n\in\mathbb{Z}}\mid\langle f,M_{(nv+j)b/v}T_{(mu+i)a/u}g\rangle\mid^{2}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle B\|f\|^{2}.

Hence,

\displaystyle uvA\|f\|^{2}\leq\sum_{m,n\in\mathbb{Z}}\sum_{i,j=0}^{u-1,v-1}\mid\langle f,M_{(nv+j)b/v}T_{(mu+i)a/u}g\rangle\mid^{2}\leq uvB\|f\|^{2}.

The last inequality proves that
\mathcal{G}(g,a/u,b/v)
is a frame.

(ii)

Let
u_{1},u_{2},v_{1},v_{2}
be positive integers. As we did in Statement (i), if

is a Bessel sequence, then so is
