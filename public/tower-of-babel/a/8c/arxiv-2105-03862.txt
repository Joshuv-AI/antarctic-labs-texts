Introduction

This paper concerns the following initial boundary value problem with the time delay

\begin{cases}|u_{t}|^{\rho}u_{tt}-\Delta u-\Delta u_{tt}+\int_{0}^{t}g(t-s)\Delta u(s)ds\\
\quad+\mu_{1}u_{t}(x,t)+\mu_{2}u_{t}(x,t-\tau)=b|u|^{p-2}u&(x,t)\in\Omega\times(0,\infty),\\
u_{t}(x,t-\tau)=f_{0}(x,t-\tau)&(x,t)\in\Omega\times(0,\tau),\\
u(x,0)=u_{0}(x),~u_{t}(x,0)=u_{1}(x)&x\in\Omega,\\
u(x,t)=0&(x,t)\in\partial\Omega\times[0,\infty),\end{cases}

\frac{bc}{p}-N_{1}b>0
.

In what follows, we will discuss in two cases.

Case 1:
G
is linear
Multiplying (
3.10
) by
\zeta(t)
, using
(\mathbf{H_{1}})
and (
2.4
), one gives

\begin{split}\zeta(t)F^{\prime}(t)&\leq-m\zeta(t)E(t)+c\zeta(t)\int_{t_{1}}^{t}g(s)\|\nabla u(t)-\nabla u(t-s)\|_{2}^{2}ds\\
&\leq-m\zeta(t)E(t)-c\int_{t_{1}}^{t}g^{\prime}(s)\|\nabla u(t)-\nabla u(t-s)\|_{2}^{2}ds\\
&\leq-m\zeta(t)E(t)-cE^{\prime}(t),\end{split}

which implies

(\zeta(t)F(t)+cE(t))^{\prime}\leq-m\zeta(t)E(t)\quad\text{for }t\geq t_{1}.

Integrating the above inequality over
(t_{1},t)
, and using the fact that
\zeta(t)F(t)+cE(t)
is equivalent to
E(t)
, one has

E(t)\leq k_{1}e^{-k_{2}\int_{t_{1}}^{t}\zeta(s)ds}\quad\text{for }t\geq t_{1},

here
k_{1}
and
k_{2}
are constants.

Case 2:
G
is nonlinear
Define a functional

H(t)=L(t)+I_{4}(t).

Taking the combination of Lemma
3.1
and the non-negativity of
E(t)
obtained by Lemma
2.4
with the definition of
I_{4}(t)
in (
2.35
)
, it is not difficult to get the non-negativity of
H(t)
. It follows from (
3.6
) and (
2.36
) that for some
m_{1}>0
and
t\geq t_{1}
,

\begin{split}H^{\prime}(t)&=L^{\prime}(t)+I^{\prime}_{4}(t)\\
&\leq-C_{1}\|u_{t}\|_{\rho+2}^{\rho+2}-C_{2}\|\nabla u_{t}\|_{2}^{2}-(1-l)\|\nabla u\|_{2}^{2}\\
&\quad+N_{1}b\|u\|_{p}^{p}-\frac{1}{4}(g\circ\nabla u)(t)-2N_{3}\int_{0}^{1}\|z(x,\kappa,t)\|_{2}^{2}d\kappa\leq-m_{1}E(t).\end{split}

Integrating the above inequality over
(t_{1},t)
yields

m_{1}\int_{t_{1}}^{t}E(s)ds\leq H(t_{1})-H(t)\leq H(t_{1}),

which implies

\int_{0}^{\infty}E(s)ds<+\infty.

(3.11)

Define

\lambda(t)=p\int_{t_{1}}^{t}\|\nabla u(t)-\nabla u(t-s)\|_{2}^{2}ds,

by using (
2.8
), then we give

\begin{split}\lambda(t)&\leq 2p\int_{0}^{t}\Big(\|\nabla u(t)\|_{2}^{2}+\|\nabla u(t-s)\|_{2}^{2}\Big)ds\\
&\leq\frac{8p\mathcal{D}}{l}\int_{0}^{t}\Big(E(t)+E(t-s)\Big)ds\leq\frac{16p\mathcal{D}}{l}\int_{0}^{t}E(t-s)ds\\
&=\frac{16p\mathcal{D}}{l}\int_{0}^{t}E(s)ds\leq\int_{0}^{\infty}E(s)ds<+\infty.\end{split}

Thus, we can choose
p
so small that for
t\geq t_{1}
,

\lambda(t)<1.

(3.12)

It is direct that

G(\theta z)\leq\theta G(z)\quad\text{for }0\leq\theta\leq 1\text{ and }z\in(0,r],

(3.13)

since
G
is strictly convex on
(0,r]
and
G(0)=0
. Based on (
1.3
), (
3.12
), (
3.13
) and Jensen’s inequality, one gives

\begin{split}I(t)&=\frac{1}{p\lambda(t)}\int_{t_{1}}^{t}\lambda(t)(-g^{\prime}(s))p\|\nabla u(t)-\nabla u(t-s)\|_{2}^{2}ds\\
&\geq\frac{1}{p\lambda(t)}\int_{t_{1}}^{t}\lambda(t)\zeta(s)G(g(s))p\|\nabla u(t)-\nabla u(t-s)\|_{2}^{2}ds\\
&\geq\frac{\zeta(t)}{p\lambda(t)}\int_{t_{1}}^{t}\overline{G}(\lambda(t)g(s))p\|\nabla u(t)-\nabla u(t-s)\|_{2}^{2}ds\\
&\geq\frac{\zeta(t)}{p}\overline{G}\Big(p\int_{t_{1}}^{t}g(s)\|\nabla u(t)-\nabla u(t-s)\|_{2}^{2}ds\Big),\end{split}

which yields

\int_{t_{1}}^{t}g(s)\|\nabla u(t)-\nabla u(t-s)\|_{2}^{2}ds\leq\frac{1}{p}\overline{G}^{-1}\Big(\frac{pI(t)}{\zeta(t)}\Big),

(3.14)

where
G
has an extension
\overline{G}
which is a strictly increasing and strictly convex
C^{2}
function on
(0,+\infty)
as in Remark 2.1
[
6
]
.
Therefore, (
3.10
) becomes

F^{\prime}(t)\leq-mE(t)+\frac{c}{p}\overline{G}^{-1}\Big(\frac{pI(t)}{\zeta(t)}\Big).

(3.15)

Let us define the functional

F_{1}(t)=\overline{G}^{\prime}\Big(\frac{r_{1}E(t)}{E(0)}\Big)F(t)+E(t)

with
0<r_{1}<r,
then
F_{1}
is equivalent to
E
and

\begin{split}F_{1}^{\prime}(t)&=\frac{r_{1}E^{\prime}(t)}{E(0)}\overline{G}^{\prime\prime}\Big(\frac{r_{1}E(t)}{E(0)}\Big)F(t)+\overline{G}^{\prime}\Big(\frac{r_{1}E(t)}{E(0)}\Big)F^{\prime}(t)+E^{\prime}(t)\\
&\leq-mE(t)\overline{G}^{\prime}\Big(\frac{r_{1}E(t)}{E(0)}\Big)+\frac{c}{p}\overline{G}^{-1}\Big(\frac{pI(t)}{\zeta(t)}\Big)\overline{G}^{\prime}\Big(\frac{r_{1}E(t)}{E(0)}\Big)+E^{\prime}(t)\end{split}

(3.16)

by using (
3.15
), (
2.4
),
G^{\prime}>0
and
G^{\prime\prime}>0
. Let
\overline{G}^{*}
be the convex conjugate of
G
in the sense of Young in
[
1
]
, which is given by

\overline{G}^{*}(s)=s(\overline{G}^{\prime})^{-1}(s)-\overline{G}\Big[(\overline{G}^{\prime})^{-1}(s)\Big]

(3.17)

and it satisfies the following Young’s inequality

AB\leq\overline{G}^{*}(A)+\overline{G}(B).

(3.18)

Choosing

A=\overline{G}^{\prime}\Big(\frac{r_{1}E(t)}{E(0)}\Big)\text{ and }B=\overline{G}^{-1}\Big(\frac{pI(t)}{\zeta(t)}\Big),

then using (
3.18
), (
3.17
) and the non-negativity of
\overline{G},
(
3.16
) becomes

\begin{split}F_{1}^{\prime}(t)&\leq-mE(t)\overline{G}^{\prime}\Big(\frac{r_{1}E(t)}{E(0)}\Big)+\frac{c}{p}\overline{G}^{-1}\Big(\frac{pI(t)}{\zeta(t)}\Big)\overline{G}^{\prime}\Big(\frac{r_{1}E(t)}{E(0)}\Big)+E^{\prime}(t)\\
&\leq-mE(t)\overline{G}^{\prime}\Big(\frac{r_{1}E(t)}{E(0)}\Big)+\frac{c}{p}\overline{G}^{*}\Big(\overline{G}^{\prime}\Big(\frac{r_{1}E(t)}{E(0)}\Big)\Big)+c\frac{I(t)}{\zeta(t)}+E^{\prime}(t)\\
&\leq-mE(t)\overline{G}^{\prime}\Big(\frac{r_{1}E(t)}{E(0)}\Big)+\frac{c}{p}\frac{r_{1}E(t)}{E(0)}\overline{G}^{\prime}\Big(\frac{r_{1}E(t)}{E(0)}\Big)\\
&\quad-\overline{G}\Big(\frac{r_{1}E(t)}{E(0)}\Big)+c\frac{I(t)}{\zeta(t)}+E^{\prime}(t)\\
&\leq-mE(t)\overline{G}^{\prime}\Big(\frac{r_{1}E(t)}{E(0)}\Big)+\frac{c}{p}\frac{r_{1}E(t)}{E(0)}\overline{G}^{\prime}\Big(\frac{r_{1}E(t)}{E(0)}\Big)+c\frac{I(t)}{\zeta(t)}+E^{\prime}(t).\end{split}

(3.19)

Note that
(
2.4
)
implies

I(t)\leq\int_{t_{1}}^{t}-g^{\prime}(s)\|\nabla u(t)-\nabla u(t-s)\|_{2}^{2}ds\leq-2E^{\prime}(t),

then one has

\zeta(t)F_{1}^{\prime}(t)\leq-m\zeta(t)E(t)G^{\prime}\Big(\frac{r_{1}E(t)}{E(0)}\Big)+\frac{c}{p}\zeta(t)\frac{r_{1}E(t)}{E(0)}G^{\prime}\Big(\frac{r_{1}E(t)}{E(0)}\Big)-cE^{\prime}(t)

(3.20)

by multiplying (
3.19
) by
\zeta(t)
and by using the fact

\overline{G}^{\prime}\Big(\frac{r_{1}E(t)}{E(0)}\Big)=G^{\prime}\Big(\frac{r_{1}E(t)}{E(0)}\Big).

Define the functional
F_{2}(t)=\zeta(t)F_{1}(t)+cE(t)
which is equivalent to
E(t)
, which means

\gamma_{1}F_{2}(t)\leq E(t)\leq\gamma_{2}F_{2}(t)

(3.21)

for some
\gamma_{1}
and
\gamma_{2}
. Under a suitable choice of
r_{1}
and for a positive constant
k
, we have

F_{2}^{\prime}(t)\leq-k\zeta(t)\frac{E(t)}{E(0)}G^{\prime}\Big(\frac{r_{1}E(t)}{E(0)}\Big)=-k\zeta(t)G_{2}\Big(\frac{E(t)}{E(0)}\Big)

(3.22)

with
G_{2}(t)=tG^{\prime}(r_{1}t)
. Obviously,
G_{2}
and
G_{2}^{\prime}
are positive in
(0,1]
since

G_{2}^{\prime}(t)=G^{\prime}(r_{1}t)+r_{1}t^{2}G^{\prime\prime}(r_{1}t)

and the convexity of
G
in
(0,r].

(
3.22
) and (
3.21
) imply

\Big(\frac{\gamma_{1}F_{2}(t)}{E(0)}\Big)^{\prime}\leq-k\zeta(t)\frac{\gamma_{1}}{E(0)}G_{2}\Big(\frac{E(t)}{E(0)}\Big)\leq-k_{3}\zeta(t)G_{2}\Big(\frac{\gamma_{1}F_{2}(t)}{E(0)}\Big)

(3.23)

with
k_{3}=k\frac{\gamma_{1}}{E(0)}
. Setting
R(t)=\frac{\gamma_{1}F_{2}(t)}{E(0)}
, and then integrating (
3.23
) over
(t_{1},t)
, one has

\int_{t_{1}}^{t}-\frac{R^{\prime}(s)}{G_{2}(R(s))}ds\geq\int_{t_{1}}^{t}k_{3}\zeta(s)ds.

Since
r_{1}R(t_{1})<r
, we have

G_{1}(r_{1}R(t))=\int_{r_{1}R(t)}^{r_{1}R(t_{1})}\frac{1}{sG^{\prime}(s)}ds\geq k_{3}\int_{t_{1}}^{t}\zeta(s)ds.

It is noted that
G_{1}
is strictly decreasing function on
(0,r]
and
\lim_{t\to 0}G_{1}(t)=+\infty
in Theorem
3.3
, then

R(t)\leq\frac{1}{r_{1}}G_{1}^{-1}\Big(k_{3}\int_{t_{1}}^{t}\zeta(s)ds\Big).

Since
R(t)
is equivalent to
E(t)
, further one obtains

E(t)\leq k_{4}G_{1}^{-1}\Big(k_{3}\int_{t_{1}}^{t}\zeta(s)ds\Big)

with
k_{4}=\frac{1}{r_{1}}.

This completes the proof of this theorem.

4.
Possible generalizations

Our results can be generalized to the following initial boundary value problem with the time-varying delay
\tau(t)

\begin{cases}|u_{t}|^{\rho}u_{tt}-\Delta u-\Delta u_{tt}+\int_{0}^{t}g(t-s)\Delta u(s)ds\\
\quad+\mu_{1}u_{t}(x,t)+\mu_{2}u_{t}(x,t-\tau(t))=b|u|^{p-2}u&(x,t)\in\Omega\times(0,\infty),\\
u_{t}(x,t-\tau(0))=f_{0}(x,t-\tau(0))&(x,t)\in\Omega\times(0,\tau(0)),\\
u(x,0)=u_{0}(x),~u_{t}(x,0)=u_{1}(x)&x\in\Omega,\\
u(x,t)=0&(x,t)\in\partial\Omega\times[0,\infty),\end{cases}

equipped with the following assumptions in addition to
(\mathbf{H_{1}})
and
(\mathbf{H_{2}})
:

(A_{1})
the function
\tau\in W^{2,\infty}([0,T])
, for all
T>0
such that

0<\tau_{0}\leq\tau(t)\leq\tau_{1}\quad\text{for all }t>0,

\tau^{\prime}(t)\leq d<1\quad\text{for all }t>0,

where
\tau_{0}
and
\tau_{1}
are positive numbers.

(A_{2})
the coefficients of delay and dissipation satisfy

|\mu_{2}|\leq\frac{2(1-d)}{2-d}\mu_{1}.

The above assumptions in fact are given in
[
6
]
. To be more precise, we need to define the new energy functional

\begin{split}E(t)&=\frac{1}{\rho+2}\|u_{t}\|_{\rho+2}^{\rho+2}+\frac{1}{2}\Big(1-\int_{0}^{t}g(s)ds\Big)\|\nabla u\|_{2}^{2}+\frac{1}{2}(g\circ\nabla u)(t)\\
&\quad+\frac{1}{2}\|\nabla u_{t}\|_{2}^{2}+\frac{\xi}{2}\tau(t)\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}z^{2}(x,\kappa,t)d\kappa dx-\frac{b}{p}\|u\|_{p}^{p},\end{split}

where
\xi
satisfies

\frac{|\mu_{2}|}{1-d}\leq\xi\leq 2\mu_{1}-|\mu_{2}|.

We also need to replace
I_{3}(t)
in Lemma
2.9
by

I_{3}(t)=\int_{0}^{1}e^{-2\tau(t)\kappa}\|z(x,\kappa,t)\|_{2}^{2}d\kappa,

further, one has

I_{3}^{\prime}(t)\leq-2I_{3}(t)-\frac{(1-d)e^{-2\tau_{1}}}{\tau_{1}}\|z(x,1,t)\|_{2}^{2}+\frac{1}{\tau_{0}}\|u_{t}\|_{2}^{2}

as shown in Lemma 2.8 of
[
6
]
. Based on the above changes, we believe that it is possible to get the similar results in Theorem
3.3
by following the steps in this paper.

In addition, we have a conjecture that our results can also be extended to the following initial boundary value problem:

\begin{cases}|u_{t}|^{\rho}u_{tt}-\Delta u-\Delta u_{tt}+\int_{0}^{t}g(t-s)\Delta u(s)ds\\
\quad+\mu_{1}|u_{t}(x,t)|^{m-2}u_{t}(x,t)\\
\quad+\mu_{2}|u_{t}(x,t-\tau)|^{m-2}u_{t}(x,t-\tau)=b|u|^{p-2}u&(x,t)\in\Omega\times(0,\infty),\\
u_{t}(x,t-\tau)=f_{0}(x,t-\tau)&(x,t)\in\Omega\times(0,\tau),\\
u(x,0)=u_{0}(x),~u_{t}(x,0)=u_{1}(x)&x\in\Omega,\\
u(x,t)=0&(x,t)\in\partial\Omega\times[0,\infty).\end{cases}
