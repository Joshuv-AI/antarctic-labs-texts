Abstract
. Let 𝐆 \mathbf{G} be a reductive algebraic group over a non-archimedean local field F F of characteristic zero and let G = 𝐆 ⁡ ( F ) G=\mathbf{G}(F) be the group of F F -rational points. 4.2.
Classifying irreducible representations of the asymptotic Hecke algebra

Now,

Proposition 4.3
.

Let
{\mathfrak{d}}=[{\mathbf{M}},\delta]
be a Harish-Chandra block, let
K\subset G
be a compact open subgroup. For any
\chi\delta\in\Psi(M)\delta
, let
\mathfrak{m}_{W_{[\delta]}\chi}
be the corresponding maximal ideal of
\Cent({\mathcal{J}}(G,K)^{\mathfrak{d}})={\mathcal{O}}(\Psi(M)\delta)^{W_{[\delta]}}

{\mathcal{J}}(G,K)^{\mathfrak{d}}/\mathfrak{m}_{W_{[\delta]}\chi}
has irreducible representations
I_{Q}^{G}(\pi\otimes\chi^{\prime})^{K}
, where
\mathbf{Q}=\mathbf{L}\ltimes\mathbf{U}^{\prime}\subset{\mathbf{G}}
is a parabolic subgroup containing
{\mathbf{M}}
such that
\chi^{\prime}
is strictly positive with respect to
\mathbf{Q}
and
\pi\in\Irr_{t}(L)^{[{\mathbf{M}},\delta]}
, so that
\pi\otimes\chi^{\prime}
is a direct summand of
I_{M}^{L}(\delta\otimes\chi)
. Moreover, the surjection

{\mathcal{J}}(G,K)^{\mathfrak{d}}/\mathfrak{m}_{W_{[\delta]}\chi}\to\bigoplus\End_{\mathbb{C}}(I_{Q}^{G}(\pi\otimes\chi^{\prime})^{K})

induces an isomorphism on the cocenters.

Proof.

First let us consider the completion
{\mathcal{J}}^{\land}
of
{\mathcal{J}}(G,K)^{\mathfrak{d}}
at
\mathfrak{m}_{W_{[\delta]}\chi}
.

Fix a parabolic
{\mathbf{P}}\in{\mathcal{P}}({\mathbf{M}})
such that
\chi
is non-strictly positive with respect to
{\mathbf{P}}

2

4.1
says nothing unless
\chi
is non-strictly positive with respect to
{\mathbf{P}}^{\prime}\in{\mathcal{P}}({\mathbf{M}})
. Now there is a minimal parabolic
\mathbf{Q}=\mathbf{L}\ltimes\mathbf{U}^{\prime}
containing all
{\mathbf{P}}^{\prime}\in{\mathcal{P}}({\mathbf{M}})
such that
\chi
is non-strictly positive with respect to
{\mathbf{P}}^{\prime}
. Now
\chi=\chi_{1}\chi_{2}
where
\chi_{1}\in\Psi_{u}(M)
and
\chi_{2}\in\Psi(L)
is strictly positive with respect to
\mathbf{Q}
. Thus the intertwining operators may be written as

J_{P^{\prime}|P}=I_{Q}^{G}(J_{P^{\prime}\cap L|P\cap L})\colon I_{Q}^{G}(I_{P\cap L}^{L}(\delta\otimes\chi_{1})\otimes\chi_{2})=I_{P}^{G}(\delta\otimes\chi)\to I_{P}^{G}(\delta\otimes\chi).

By Lemma
2.5
, the intertwining operators

are regular at
\chi_{1}

is also regular. Thus we have an isomorphism

{\mathcal{J}}^{\land}\simeq\big(\widehat{\mathcal{O}}_{\mathfrak{m}_{\chi}}\otimes\End_{\mathbb{C}}(I_{P}^{G}(V_{[\delta]})^{K})\big)^{W_{[\delta]}},

where now

is a subgroup of

be a finite covering of

(closely related to the group
\widetilde{R}
considered in
[
Art93

\mathfrak{m}_{\chi}
the
\widetilde{W}_{[\delta]}\times{\mathcal{H}}(G,K)
-representation

splits as:

I_{P}^{G}(\delta\otimes\chi)^{K}=\bigoplus_{i}\rho_{i}\boxtimes I_{Q}^{G}(\pi_{i}\otimes\chi_{2})^{K}

for tempered representations
\pi_{i}
of
L
, direct summands of
I_{P\cap L}^{L}(\delta\otimes\chi_{1})

We can lift the decomposition to

\widehat{\mathcal{O}}_{\mathfrak{m}_{\chi}}\otimes I_{P}^{G}(V_{[\delta]})^{K}=\bigoplus_{i}\widehat{\rho}_{i}\boxtimes I_{Q}^{G}(V_{[\pi_{i}]}\otimes\chi_{2})^{K},

\widehat{\mathcal{O}}_{\mathfrak{m}_{\chi}}
-semi-linear representations of

5.
Trace Paley-Wiener theorem for the asymptotic Hecke algebra

Let us recall the trace Paley-Wiener theorem for
\mathcal{H}(G)
:

Proposition 5.1

(
[
BDK86
, Theorem 1.2]
)
.

Let
R(G)
be the Grothendieck group of admissible finite-length
G
-modules and let
\Hom_{\reg}(R(G),{\mathbb{C}})
be the vector space of homomorphisms
\varphi\colon R(G)\to{\mathbb{C}}
such that:

•

there exists a compact open subgroup
K
of
G
such that
f([\pi])=0
for all representations
\pi\in\Rep(G)
with no
K
-fixed vectors; and

•

for any parabolic subgroup
\mathbf{P}=\mathbf{M}\ltimes\mathbf{U}\subset\mathbf{G}
and representation
\sigma\in\Rep(M)
, the function
f([I_{P}^{G}(\sigma\otimes\chi)])
is a regular function of
\chi\in\Psi(M)
.

Then trace provides an isomorphism
{\mathcal{H}}/[{\mathcal{H}},{\mathcal{H}}]\simeq\Hom_{\reg}(R(G),{\mathbb{C}})
.

We prove an analogous statement for the asymptotic Hecke algebra
\mathcal{J}(G)
:

Theorem 5.2
.

Let
R(\mathcal{J})
be the Grothendieck group of finite-length
\mathcal{J}
-modules and let
\Hom_{\reg}(R(\mathcal{J}),{\mathbb{C}})
be the set of homomorphisms
\varphi\colon R(\mathcal{J})\to{\mathbb{C}}
such that:

•

there exists a compact open subgroup
K
of
G
such that
f([\pi])=0
for all representations
\pi\in\mathcal{J}\mathrm{-mod}
with no
K
-fixed vectors; and

•

for any parabolic subgroup
\mathbf{P}=\mathbf{M}\ltimes\mathbf{U}\subset\mathbf{G}
and
\sigma\in\Rep^{t}(M)
, the function
f([I_{P}^{G}(\sigma\otimes\chi)])
extends to a regular function of
\chi\in\Psi(M)
.

Proof of Theorem
5.2
.

First of all, we can show that for
f\in{\mathcal{J}}
if
f([I_{P}^{G}(\sigma\otimes\chi)])=0
for any standard triple
({\mathbf{P}},\sigma,\chi)
then
f\in[{\mathcal{J}},{\mathcal{J}}]
.
It suffices to prove the proposition when

where
{\mathfrak{d}}=[{\mathbf{M}},\delta]
is a Harish-Chandra block and
K\subset G
is a compact open subgroup. It suffices to check that
f\mod\mathfrak{m}_{\chi}
is in
[{\mathcal{J}}(G,K)^{\mathfrak{d}}/\mathfrak{m}_{\chi},{\mathcal{J}}(G,K)^{\mathfrak{d}}/\mathfrak{m}_{\chi}]
, since
{\mathcal{J}}(G,K)^{\mathfrak{d}}
is a finitely generated
{\mathcal{O}}(\Psi(M)\delta)
-module. This is shown in Proposition
4.3
.

Thus
{\mathcal{J}}/[{\mathcal{J}},{\mathcal{J}}]\to\Hom(R({\mathcal{J}}),{\mathbb{C}})
is injective. The image is in
\Hom_{\reg}(R({\mathcal{J}}),{\mathbb{C}})
. But by Proposition
4.7
we have
R({\mathcal{J}})\simeq R(G)
, which gives an isomorphism

\Hom_{\reg}(R({\mathcal{J}}),{\mathbb{C}})\simeq\Hom_{\reg}(R(G),{\mathbb{C}}).

Thus

∎
