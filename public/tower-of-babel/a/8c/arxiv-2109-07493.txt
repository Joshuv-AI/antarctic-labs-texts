Higgs Branches of Argyres-Douglas theories as Quiver Varieties
Anindya Dey

Abstract
We present a general prescription for constructing 3d 𝒩 = 4 {\cal N}=4 Lagrangians for the IR SCFTs that arise from the circle reduction of a large class of Argyres-Douglas theories. The resultant Lagrangian gives a realization of the Higgs branch of the 4d SCFT as a quiver variety, up to a set of decoupled interacting SCFTs with empty Higgs branches. As representative examples, we focus on the families ( A p − N − 1 , A N − 1 ) (A_{p-N-1},A_{N-1}) and D p ​ ( S ​ U ​ ( N ) ) D_{p}(SU(N)) . The Lagrangian in question is generically a non-ADE-type quiver gauge theory involving only unitary gauge nodes with fundamental and bifundamental hypermultiplets, as well as hypermultiplets which are only charged under the U ⁡ ( 1 ) U(1) subgroups of certain gauge nodes. Our starting point is the Lagrangian 3d mirror of the circle-reduced Argyres-Douglas theory, which can be read off from the class 𝒮 {\cal S} construction. Using the toolkit of the S S -type operations, developed in Dey:2020hfe , we show that the mirror of the 3d mirror for any Argyres-Douglas theory in the aforementioned families is guaranteed to be a Lagrangian theory of the above type, up to some decoupled free sectors. We comment on the extension of this procedure to other families of Argyres-Douglas theories. In addition, for the case of D p ​ ( S ​ U ​ ( N ) ) D_{p}(SU(N)) theories, we compare these 3d Lagrangians to the ones found in Closset:2020afy and propose that the two are related by an IR duality. We check the proposed IR duality at the level of the three-sphere partition function for specific examples. In contrast to the 3d Lagrangians in Closset:2020afy , which are linear chains involving unitary-special unitary nodes, we observe that the Coulomb branch global symmetries are manifest in the 3d Lagrangians that we find.

1.1.1
AD theories and 3d mirrors from the class
{\cal S}
construction

Argyres-Douglas (AD) theories constitute a large class of 4d
{\cal N}=2
SCFTs for which the Coulomb branch operators
have fractional scaling dimensions. The first examples of such theories were realized as special points on the Coulomb
branch of Lagrangian theories in 4d
Argyres:1995jj
;
Argyres:1995xn
.
Over the years, various important subclasses of such theories have been constructed
via the geometric engineering technique
Cecotti:2010fi
;
Cecotti:2013lda
of compactifying Type IIB superstring theory on certain singular Calabi-Yau
hypersurfaces, as well as by the class
{\cal S}
construction
Gaiotto:2009hg
;
Xie:2012hs
. As is well known, the latter involves
compactifying a 6d (2,0) theory (which has an ADE classification) on a Riemann sphere with either a single irregular puncture,
or a single irregular puncture with a single regular puncture
Xie:2012hs

1
1

1

Certain AD theories can also be realized by the
compactification of twisted ADE theories on a Riemann surface with only regular punctures, as pointed out in
Beem:2020pry
.
.

One can compactify a given 4d
{\cal N}=2
SCFT to 3d by putting the theory on
\mathbb{R}^{3}\times S^{1}
, and flowing to
the deep IR. The resultant 3d
{\cal N}=4
SCFT may or may not have a Lagrangian description, but is
known to have a Lagrangian 3d mirror for a large subset of class
{\cal S}
theories.
Given the class
{\cal S}
construction of a 4d
{\cal N}=2
SCFT, the basic idea is to show that the associated
Hitchin moduli space (which is the Coulomb branch of the
theory on
\mathbb{R}^{3}\times S^{1}
) in the IR limit is isomorphic to the Higgs branch of a 3d
{\cal N}=4
quiver gauge theory.
The aforementioned 3d quiver gauge theory can be explicitly built from the class
{\cal S}
data in the following generic
steps:

•

To every puncture on the Riemann surface, one associates a quiver gauge theory, which can be read off
from the singularity data of the puncture.

•

Given the set of quivers associated with the Riemann surface with punctures, one glues them together,
following certain rules which depend on the specific punctures involved.

The 3d mirrors for 4d
{\cal N}=2
SCFTs, which arise from 6d
A_{N-1}
(2,0) theories compactified on
a Riemann surface with regular punctures, was found in
boalch2007quivers
;
Benini:2010uu
.
For an important subclass of irregular punctures, which appear
in the realization of AD theories, the analogous result was first obtained in
boalch2008irregular
and
was extended in subsequent papers
Xie:2012hs
;
Xie:2013jc
;
Wang:2018gvb
.
For a recent review of the subject, we would like to refer the reader to
Xie:2021ewm
.

In this work, we would be primarily interested in the 3d mirrors of the AD theories of the following families:

(A_{p-N-1},D_{N-1})
and
D_{p}(SU(N))

while the second is realized by a single irregular puncture and a single regular puncture. The 3d mirror for each family

S
-type operations and 3d Lagrangians for AD theories

In
Dey:2020hfe

starting from a well-defined set of basic dualities. Let us briefly summarize the construction. We consider a
class of 3d SCFTs (which we refer to as class
{\cal U}
) with UV Lagrangians for which the Higgs branch
global symmetry has a subgroup
G^{\rm sub}_{\rm global}=\prod_{\gamma}U(M_{\gamma})\subset G_{H}
.

Given a UV Lagrangian
X
in class
{\cal U}
, one can define a map which acts on
X
to give a generically
different theory
X^{\prime}
, i.e.

{\cal O}^{\alpha}_{\bm{{\cal P}}}:X[\bm{\widehat{A}}]\mapsto X^{\prime}[\bm{\widehat{B}}],

(1)

where the phrase “different theory” implies that
X
and
X^{\prime}
flow to different SCFTs in the IR. The precise definition
of the map
{\cal O}^{\alpha}_{\bm{{\cal P}}}
is reviewed in Section
2
. In (
1
),
\widehat{\bm{A}}
and
\widehat{\bm{B}}

collectively represent the background fields associated with the global symmetries of the theories
X
and
X^{\prime}
respectively.
We refer to the above map as an “elementary
S
-type operation”. The name arises from the fact that in
the special case, where
G^{\rm sub}_{\rm global}=U(1)
, and
{\cal O}^{\alpha}_{\bm{{\cal P}}}

is a gauging operation of the said
U(1)

S
generator of
Witten’s
SL(2,\mathbb{Z})
action

on a 3d CFT.

Suppose the theory
X[\widehat{\bm{A}}]
has a 3d mirror
Y[\widehat{\bm{A}}]
, such that both theories
have Lagrangian realizations. For a given elementary
S
-type operation
{\cal O}^{\alpha}_{\bm{{\cal P}}}
on

define a dual operation
\widetilde{{\cal O}}^{\alpha}_{\bm{{\cal P}}}
on

\widetilde{{\cal O}}^{\alpha}_{\bm{{\cal P}}}:Y[\widehat{\bm{A}}]\mapsto Y^{\prime}[\widehat{\bm{B}}],

(2)

which maps it to a different theory
Y^{\prime}[\widehat{\bm{B}}]
, such that the pair of theories
(X^{\prime}[\widehat{\bm{B}}],Y^{\prime}[\widehat{\bm{B}}])
are 3d mirrors.
The four theories
X[\widehat{\bm{A}}],Y[\widehat{\bm{A}}]

Given the recipe for finding the dual of the
S

We first define a conveniently small subset of mirror pairs, which are well understood from the String Theory

(X,Y)

We will pick the set of basic dualities to be the set of mirror pairs involving linear (A-type)

Gaiotto-Witten sense
Gaiotto:2008ak
. Mirror symmetry for this class of theories has been studied in a lot of detail using the Type IIB
Hanany-Witten brane construction
Hanany:1996ie
on the one hand and QFT approaches on the other.
In particular, it was shown in
Gaiotto:2008ak
that the mirror of a good linear quiver is also a good linear quiver.
Using the construction outlined above, one can then construct mirror pairs
involving quivers of arbitrary shapes.

In this paper, we will use this construction to determine 3d
{\cal N}=4
Lagrangians for families of AD theories which have Lagrangian
3d mirrors. Let this Lagrangian 3d mirror be
X^{\prime}
(see Fig.
1
above). The strategy is to show that
X^{\prime}
can be engineered
from a good linear quiver
X
by a sequence of
S
-type operations. Since
X
has a 3d mirror
Y
, which is also a good linear
quiver, the 3d mirror of
X^{\prime}
can be obtained by a sequence of dual
S
-type operations on the theory
Y
. If the resultant theory

Y^{\prime}
is Lagrangian, then it gives a Lagrangian realization for the 3d SCFT obtained from the circle-reduction of the AD theory.
As mentioned above, for a completely generic quiver gauge theory
X^{\prime}
, the theory
Y^{\prime}
is not Lagrangian. However, for the AD theories under
consideration,
X^{\prime}
can be engineered from a linear quiver
X
by a very special class of
S
-type operations, for which
the theory
Y^{\prime}
is guaranteed to have a Lagrangian description.

1.1.3
Another 3d Lagrangian for a
D_{p}(SU(N))
theory

In
Closset:2020afy
;
Giacomelli:2020ryy
, the authors derived 3d Lagrangians for
D_{p}(SU(N))
theories, by making use of a partially weakly-coupled description of the 4d
{\cal N}=2
SCFTs. We briefly summarize the results relevant for our paper below.

The
D_{p}(SU(N))
theories are specified by the SW curve:

x^{N}+z^{p}+\sum_{i,j\geq 0}u_{ij}\,z^{i}\,x^{j}=0,\quad\lambda_{\rm SW}=\frac{x\,dz}{z},

(3)

where the scaling dimension of the SW differential is
[\lambda_{\rm SW}]=1
, and this implies
that the other scaling dimensions are given as

\displaystyle[x]=1,\quad[z]=\frac{N}{p},\quad[u_{ij}]=N-j-\frac{N}{p}\,i.

(4)

If
p,N
are coprime, then
D_{p}(SU(N))
is an isolated SCFT, i.e. it has no conformal manifold. There is
another isolated SCFT that will be important in this story:
{\cal D}_{q}(N,N-n)
, which has the following SW curve

\displaystyle x^{N}+x^{N-n}\,z^{q}+\sum_{i,j\geq 0}u_{ij}\,z^{i}\,x^{j}=0,\quad\lambda_{\rm SW}=\frac{x\,dz}{z},

\displaystyle\implies\,\frac{x^{N}}{z^{q}}+x^{N-n}+\sum_{i,j\geq 0}v_{ij}\,z^{i}\,x^{j}=0.

(5)

If
n,q
are chosen such that
n=N/{\rm GCD}(p,N)
and
q=p/{\rm GCD}(p,N)
, then
{\cal D}_{q}(N,N-n)
is an
isolated SCFT, as can be seen by counting the number of deformations of scaling dimension zero. These theories
have a global symmetry
SU(N)\times U(1)\times SU(N-n)
.

Given an SCFT
D_{p}(SU(N))
, the monomial
x^{N-n}z^{q}
in the SW curve corresponds to a marginal deformation. Also, the
monomials
x^{j}z^{q}
with
j=0,1,\ldots,N-n-1
correspond to integer scaling dimensions

\Delta_{qj}=N-n,N-n-1,\ldots,1
. Leaving out the mass deformation, the remaining deformations correspond
to CB operators of a conformally gauged
SU(N-n)
factor of the 4d SCFT. Together with the marginal deformation
term, these terms in the SW curve can be organized as follows (after appropriate rescaling):

x^{N-n}z^{q}+\sum^{N-n-2}_{j=0}\,u_{qj}x^{j}z^{q}=:(x^{N-n}+\sum^{N-n}_{j=2}\,u_{j}x^{N-n-j})\,z^{q}=:P_{N-n}(x)\,z^{q}.

(6)

The SW curve can then be written as

\displaystyle x^{N}+\ldots+P_{N-n}(x)\,z^{q}+\ldots+z^{p}=0,\implies\frac{x^{N}}{z^{q}}+\ldots+P_{N-n}(x)+\ldots+z^{p-q}=0.

(7)

In this form, the above SW curve represents a
{\cal D}_{q}(N,N-n)
system and a
D_{p-q}(SU(N-n))
system with the diagonal

SU(N-n)
flavor symmetry being conformally gauged. This allows us to write a partially weakly-coupled description of the
SCFT as follows:

D_{p}(SU(N))={\cal D}_{q}(N,N-n)\longleftarrow SU(N-n)\longrightarrow D_{p-q}(SU(N-n)).

(8)

Applying this argument iteratively, one can write the following partially weakly coupled description:

\displaystyle D_{p}(SU(N))={\cal D}_{q}(N,N-n)\longleftarrow{SU(N-n)}\longrightarrow{\cal D}_{q}(N-n,N-2n)\longleftarrow{SU(N-2n)}

\displaystyle\longrightarrow{\cal D}_{q}(N-2n,N-3n)\longleftarrow{SU(N-3n)}\longrightarrow{\cal D}_{q}(N-3n,N-4n)\longleftarrow{SU(N-4n)}

\displaystyle\longrightarrow{\cal D}_{q}(N-4n,N-5n)\longleftarrow\ldots\longleftarrow{SU(n)}\longrightarrow{\cal D}_{q}(n,0),

(9)

where
{\cal D}_{q}(n,0)=D_{q}(SU(n))
. This allows one to write the SCFT
D_{p}(SU(N))
in terms of a set of isolated SCFTs with
conformally-gauged special unitary flavor subgroups.

The isolated SCFT
{\cal D}_{q}(N,K)
can be realized as the low energy
effective field theory at the origin of the Coulomb branch of a 4d Lagrangian theory, with appropriately tuned flavor
masses. The 4d Lagrangian theory in question is a linear chain of special unitary gauge groups with
N
fundamental flavors at one end,
and
K
fundamental flavors at the other. For example, with
K=N-1
, i.e.
n=N/{\rm GCD}(p,N)=1
, the 4d Lagrangian is given as

{\cal D}_{q}(N,N-1)=[N]-SU(N)_{1}-SU(N)_{2}-\ldots-SU(N)_{q-1}-[N-1].

(10)

The case for general
(N,K)
can be found in
Closset:2020afy
. Given the description in (
1.1.3
) and the above realization of the SCFTs

{\cal D}_{q}(N,K)
, the 3d Lagrangian can be obtained as follows:

1.

The 3d limit of a given isolated SCFT
{\cal D}_{q}(N,K)
is obtained by studying the low energy effective theory of
the dimensionally reduced linear quiver at the origin of the Coulomb branch, with the flavor masses turned on.
Let us call these 3d theories
{\cal T}_{i}
, where
i=1,\ldots,m-1
. The theory
{\cal T}_{i}
associated with an isolated SCFT
{\cal D}_{q}(N,K)

turns out to be a linear quiver with unitary gauge nodes and Higgs branch global symmetry
SU(N)\times U(1)\times U(K)
.

2.

The 4d vector multiplets of the conformally-gauged
SU
factors become 3d vector multiplets under
dimensional reduction.

3.

Given the set of 3d theories
\{{\cal T}_{i}\}
, the 3d limit of
D_{p}(SU(N))
is then given by a linear chain of unitary and
special unitary nodes with bifundamental and fundamental hypers:

[D_{p}(SU(N))]_{3d}:{\cal T}_{1}-SU(N-n)-{\cal T}_{2}-SU(N-2n)-\ldots-SU(n)-{\cal T}_{m-1}.

(11)

In certain cases, the 3d theory can have ugly nodes, but one can always reduce the theory to a quiver of the form (
11
)
and a set of free hypermultiplets.

In Section
3.2
, we note that the 3d Lagrangian in (
11
) and the 3d Lagrangian constructed using
S
-type operations
are different quiver gauge theories, i.e. they have different gauge groups and matter content. We propose that the two 3d Lagrangians are
related by an IR duality, and perform certain checks of the proposed duality for specific examples in Section
4
.

3d Lagrangians for other families of Argyres-Douglas theories:
The construction using the
S
-type operation, as summarized
in Fig.
1
, can be deployed whenever the 3d mirror of the AD theory reduced on a circle has a Lagrangian description.
In the generic case, where the 3d mirror can only be engineered from a linear quiver with unitary gauge groups by a non-Abelian

S
-type operation, there is no guarantee that the dual operation will produce a Lagrangian theory.
However, for families of AD theories for which the aforementioned 3d mirror can be engineered from a linear quiver
by a sequence of elementary Abelian
S
-type operations, the mirror of the 3d mirror is guaranteed to be Lagrangian.
This in turn implies that the 3d SCFT obtained by the circle-reduction of the associated AD theory is guaranteed to have a
Lagrangian description. Aside from the two infinite families –
(A_{p-N-1},A_{N-1})
and
D_{p}(SU(N)
– that we study in
the present work, there are other infinite families for which the 3d mirrors have the same property and can therefore be treated
in an analogous fashion. These include the families
(A_{s},D_{(s+1)p+2})
and
D^{N-1}_{p}(SU(N))
(for
p\gtreqless N-1
).
In the first case, the 3d Lagrangians can be worked out in a fashion very similar to the
(A_{p-N-1},A_{N-1})
theories,
while in the second case, the construction closely mimics the
D_{p}(SU(N))
theories.
It would be interesting to have a complete classification of the AD theories for which the 3d mirrors have
the above property, and construct the associated 3d Lagrangians. We leave this to a future work.

4
An infinite class of IR dualities

From Section
3.2.1
and Section
3.2.2
, we showed that the 3d SCFT obtained by compactifying
D_{p}(SU(N)
on a
circle has a Lagrangian description in terms of a quiver gauge theory. The gauge group of the theory in question has
only unitary factors, and involves a set of Abelian hypermultiplets in addition to fundamental and bifundamental
hypers. Comparing our results with
Closset:2020afy
suggests that we have an IR duality for every
D_{p}(SU(N)
, where the
dual theories belong to the following classes of quiver gauge theory respectively:

•

Theory
{\cal T}
: A linear chain of unitary and special unitary gauge groups with fundamentals and bifundamentals.

•

Theory
{\cal T}^{\vee}
: A generically non-linear quiver involving only unitary gauge groups, decorated with Abelian matter
in addition to fundamental/bifundamental hypers.

In this section, we present a couple of simple examples of this conjectured duality, and check that the three-sphere partition
function of the dual theories do agree.

SU(N)
with
2N-1
