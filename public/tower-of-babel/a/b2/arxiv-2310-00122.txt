Throughout the paper we let

In this work we consider trajectories which are allowed to enter

Again under the assumption of ergodicity

Clearly for any

3.1.
Hausdorff dimension of limsup sets

The exceptional sets we study in this paper are of the form
A=\limsup\limits_{N\rightarrow\infty}A_{N}
, that is

A=\bigcap\limits_{N\geq 1}\bigcup\limits_{n\geq N}A_{n}

for a sequence of subsets

.

First, we recall the definition of the Hausdorff dimension.
Let
A
be a subset of a metric space
Y
. For any
\rho,\beta>0
, we define

{\mathcal{H}}_{\rho}^{\beta}(A)=\inf\left\{\sum_{I\in\mathcal{U}}\operatorname{diam}(I)^{\beta}:\mathcal{U}\text{ is a cover of A by balls of diameter }<\rho\right\}.

Then, the
\beta
-dimensional Hausdorff measure of
A
is defined to be

{\mathcal{H}}^{\beta}(A)=\lim_{\rho\rightarrow 0}{\mathcal{H}}_{\rho}^{\beta}(A).

Definition 3.1
.

The Hausdorff dimension of a subset
A
of a metric space
Y
is equal to

\dim(A)=\inf\left\{\beta\geq 0:{\mathcal{H}}^{\beta}(A)=0\right\}=\sup\left\{\beta\geq 0:{\mathcal{H}}^{\beta}(A)=\infty\right\}.

Let
\left\{A_{N}\right\}_{N\geq 1}

.
Suppose there exist constants

\dim\left(\limsup\limits_{N\rightarrow\infty}A_{N}\right)\leq\liminf\limits_{N\rightarrow\infty}\alpha_{N}.

Proof of Theorem

Proof of Theorem

.

Let
O
be an open subset of
X
,
x\in X
, and let
\delta>0
. In view of countable stability of Hausdorff dimension, it suffices to show that for any
0<r\leq r_{2}
, we have

\codim S_{x,\delta}(O)\gg\frac{\mu({\sigma_{r}}O)\cdot{\phi\left(\mu({\sigma_{r}}O),\sqrt{1-\delta}\right)}}{\log\frac{1}{r}}

where

is as in (
4.1
) and
\phi
is as in (
1.4
). In order to prove the above statement, it is evident that it suffices to demonstrate that for any
0<r\leq r_{2}/5
we have

{\codim S_{x,\delta}(O)\gg\frac{\mu({\sigma_{5r}}O)\cdot{\phi\left(\mu({\sigma_{5r}}O),\sqrt{1-\delta}\right)}}{\log\frac{1}{5r}}.}

(6.1)

If
\mu({\sigma_{5r}}O)=0
, then the above statement follows immediately. So in this proof we assume always that
\mu({\sigma_{5r}}O)>0
.
We start with the following combinatorial lemma:

Lemma 6.1
.

Let
m={m}(n)\leq n
be a function of
n
such that
\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}\frac{m}{n}=z
for some fixed constant
0<z<1
. Then

{n\choose m}=o(1){B\big(\tfrac{m}{n}\big)}^{n},

where

{B(z):=\left(\frac{1}{z}\right)^{z}\left(\frac{1}{1-z}\right)^{1-z}.}

(6.2)

Proof.

Note that
\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}\frac{m}{n}=z<1
implies that both
m
and
n-m
tend to infinity as
n
goes to infinity; moreover,
\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}\frac{n-m}{n}=1-z
. Hence, by using Stirling’s approximation we have:

\displaystyle{n\choose m}=\frac{n!}{m!(n-m)!}

\displaystyle=(1+o(1))\frac{\sqrt{2\pi n}(\frac{n}{e})^{n}}{\sqrt{2\pi m}(\frac{m}{e})^{m}+\sqrt{2\pi(n-m)}(\frac{n-m}{e})^{n-m}}

\displaystyle=(1+o(1))\sqrt{\frac{n}{2\pi m(n-m)}}\left(\frac{n}{m}\right)^{m}\left(\frac{n}{n-m}\right)^{n-m}

\displaystyle=o(1)\left(\frac{n}{m}\right)^{m}\left(\frac{n}{n-m}\right)^{n-m}

\displaystyle=o(1){B\big(\tfrac{m}{n}\big)}^{n},

where the third equality above follows from the fact that

\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}\frac{n}{2\pi m(n-m)}=\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}\frac{1}{2\pi n}\cdot\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}\frac{n}{m}\cdot\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}\frac{n}{n-m}=0\cdot\frac{1}{z}\cdot\frac{1}{1-z}=0.

∎

Now given
0<\varepsilon<\delta

{z:=1-\frac{1-\delta}{1-\varepsilon}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\varepsilon=1-\frac{1-\delta}{1-z}=\frac{\delta-z}{1-z}.}

(6.3)

, applied with

, then implies that there exists

{{N\choose\left\lceil zN\right\rceil}\leq B\left(\frac{\lceil zN\rceil}{N}\right)^{N}\quad\text{ for all }N\geq N_{0}.}

(6.4)

and

as in (

), and let

. By combining Corollary

for any

,

\displaystyle A_{x,\delta}(NT,O^{c})\text{ can be covered with at most }

(6.5)

\displaystyle C_{1}e^{LN{{\lambda_{\max}}}T}\cdot\beta_{N}^{N}\text{ balls in $P$ of radius }re^{-{{\lambda_{\max}}}NT},

where

\beta_{N}:={B\left(\frac{\lceil zN\rceil}{N}\right)}\cdot\left(\frac{C(T,O)}{\varepsilon}\right)^{\frac{\lceil zN\rceil}{N}}\left(1+c_{0}e^{-{{\lambda_{\min}}}T}\right)^{1-\frac{\lceil zN\rceil}{N}}.

Note that we have

{\lim_{N\rightarrow\infty}\beta_{N}=B(z)\cdot\left(\frac{C(T,O)}{\varepsilon}\right)^{z}\left(1+c_{0}e^{-{{\lambda_{\min}}}T}\right)^{1-z}}
