A hook formula for eigenvalues of k k -point fixing graph
Mahdi Ebrahimi

Abstract
Let S n S_{n} denote the symmetric group on n n letters. The k k -point fixing graph ℱ ⁡ ( n , k ) \mathcal{F}(n,k) is defined to be the graph with vertex set S n S_{n} and two vertices g , h g,h of ℱ ⁡ ( n , k ) \mathcal{F}(n,k) are joined by an edge, if and only if g ​ h − 1 gh^{-1} fixes exactly k k points. Ku, Lau and Wong [Cayley graph on symmetric group generated by elements fixing k k points, Linear Algebra Appl. 471 (2015) 405-426] obtained a recursive formula for the eigenvalues of ℱ ⁡ ( n , k ) \mathcal{F}(n,k) . In this paper, we use objects called excited diagrams defined as certain generalizations of skew shapes and derive an explicit formula for the eigenvalues of Cayley graph ℱ ⁡ ( n , k ) \mathcal{F}(n,k) . Then we apply this formula and show that the eigenvalues of ℱ ⁡ ( n , k ) \mathcal{F}(n,k) are in the interval [ − | S ⁡ ( n , k ) | n − k − 1 , | S ⁡ ( n , k ) | ] [\frac{-|S(n,k)|}{n-k-1},|S(n,k)|] , where S ⁡ ( n , k ) S(n,k) is the set of elements σ \sigma of S n S_{n} such that σ \sigma fixes exactly k k points.

A partition is a weakly decreasing finite sequence of positive integers

A generalization of branching rule is as follows:

Lemma 2.2

Suppose

[\lambda]\downarrow S_{m}=\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}\mu\vdash m\\

Thus using branching rule,

\displaystyle[\lambda]\downarrow S_{m}

\displaystyle=\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}\tilde{\mu}\vdash m+1\\
\tilde{\mu}\subseteq\lambda\end{subarray}}f^{\lambda/\tilde{\mu}}[\tilde{\mu}]\downarrow S_{m}

\displaystyle=\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}\tilde{\mu}\vdash m+1\\

The standard Young tableaux and skew shapes are central objects in enumerative and algebraic combinatorics.
A

.
Analogously, if
