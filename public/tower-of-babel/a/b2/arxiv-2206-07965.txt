Global Gevery regulartiy and analyticity of a weakly dissipative Camassa-Holm equation
Zhiying Meng and, Zhaoyang Yin

Abstract
This work is concerned with the Gevrey regularity and analyticity of the solution to a weakly dissipative Camassa-Holm system. We first demonstrate the local Gevery regularity and analyticity of this equation. Then, we disscuss the continuity of the data-to-solution map. Finally, we obtain the global Gevery regularity of this system in Gevery class G σ G_{\sigma} with σ ≥ 1 \sigma\geq 1 in time.



Keywords
: A weakly dissipative Camassa-Holm equation; analyticity; Gevery class; global
Gevrey regularity

T^{\infty}=\frac{1}{2^{2\sigma+8}C^{\prime}(e^{-\sigma}\sigma^{\sigma}+2)(1+\|u^{\infty}_{0}\|_{G^{1}_{\sigma,s}})^{4}},~T^{n}=\frac{1}{2^{2\sigma+8}C^{\prime}(e^{-\sigma}\sigma^{\sigma}+2)(1+\|u^{n}_{0}\|_{G^{1}_{\sigma,s}})^{4}}.

Owing to
\|u_{0}^{n}-u_{0}^{\infty}\|_{G^{1}_{\sigma,s}}\to 0
as
n\to\infty,
there exists a constant
N
such that if
n\geq N
, we obtain

\displaystyle\|u_{0}^{n}\|_{G^{1}_{\sigma,s}}\leq\|u_{0}^{\infty}\|_{G^{1}_{\sigma,s}}+1.

(4.1)

Set

\displaystyle T=\frac{1}{2^{2\sigma+8}C^{\prime}(e^{-\sigma}\sigma^{\sigma}+2)(2+\|u^{n}_{0}\|_{G^{1}_{\sigma,s}})^{4}}.

(4.2)

This means that
T\leq\min\{T^{n},~T^{\infty}\}
for any
n\geq N.
A similar argument based on the proof of Theorem
3.1
, we claim that
T^{n}
and
T^{\infty}
are the existence time of the solutions
u^{n}
and
u^{\infty}
corresponding to
u_{0}^{n}
and
u_{0}^{\infty}

respectively.Therefore, we see that for any
n\geq N,

\displaystyle u^{\infty}(t,x)

\displaystyle=u_{0}^{\infty}+\int_{0}^{t}F(\tau,u^{\infty}(t,\tau))d\tau,~0\leq t<\frac{T(1-\delta)^{\sigma}}{2^{\sigma}-1},

(4.3)

\displaystyle u^{n}(t,x)

\displaystyle=u_{0}^{n}~+\int_{0}^{t}F(\tau,u^{n}(t,\tau))d\tau,~~0\leq t<\frac{T(1-\delta)^{\sigma}}{2^{\sigma}-1},

(4.4)

with
F
is given in Theorem
3.1
.Thus, for any
0\leq t\leq\frac{T(1-\delta)^{\sigma}}{2^{\sigma}-1}
and
0\leq\delta\leq 1,
we can obtain

\displaystyle\|u^{n}(t)-u^{\infty}(t)\|_{\delta}\leq\|u_{0}^{n}(t)-u_{0}^{\infty}(t)\|_{\delta}+\int_{0}^{t}\|F(u^{n}(\tau))-F(u^{\infty}(\tau))\|_{\delta}d\tau.

(4.5)

Define that

\delta(t)=\frac{1}{2}(1+\delta)+(\frac{1}{2})^{2+\frac{1}{\sigma}}\Big([(1-\delta)^{\sigma}-\frac{t}{T}]^{\frac{1}{\sigma}}-[(1-\delta)^{\sigma}+(2^{\sigma+1}-1)\frac{t}{T}]^{\frac{1}{\sigma}}\Big).

Making use of Lemma
2.12
, we know that
\delta<\delta(t)<1.
One can get from (
4.5
) that

\displaystyle\|F(u^{n}(\tau))-F(u^{\infty}(\tau))\|_{\delta}\leq\frac{L\|u^{n}(\tau)-u^{\infty}(\tau)\|_{\delta(\tau)}}{(\delta(\tau)-\delta)^{\sigma}},

(4.6)

where
L=2^{4}{C^{\prime}(e^{-\sigma}\sigma^{\sigma}+2)}(1+\|u_{0}\|_{1})^{4}.
Hence, we can deduce that

\displaystyle\|u^{n}(t)-u^{\infty}(t)\|_{\delta}\leq\|u_{0}^{n}(t)-u_{0}^{\infty}(t)\|_{\delta}+L\int_{0}^{t}\frac{\|u^{n}(\tau)-u^{\infty}(\tau)\|_{\delta(\tau)}}{(\delta(\tau)-\delta)^{\sigma}}d\tau.

(4.7)

It follows from Lemma
2.12
that

\displaystyle\|u^{n}(t)-u^{\infty}(t)\|_{\delta}\leq\|u_{0}^{n}(t)-u_{0}^{\infty}(t)\|_{\delta}+\frac{T2^{2\delta+3}\|u^{n}(t)-u^{\infty}(t)\|_{E_{T}}}{(1-\delta)^{\sigma}}\sqrt{\frac{T(1-\delta)^{\sigma}}{T(1-\delta)^{\sigma}-t}}.

(4.8)

Combining (
4.2
) and
LT2^{{2\sigma+3}}<\frac{1}{2}
. Therefore, we obtain

\displaystyle\|u^{n}(t)-u^{\infty}(t)\|_{\delta}\leq\|u_{0}^{n}(t)-u_{0}^{\infty}(t)\|_{\delta}+\frac{\|u^{n}(t)-u^{\infty}(t)\|_{E_{T}}}{2(1-\delta)^{\sigma}}\sqrt{\frac{T(1-\delta)^{\sigma}}{T(1-\delta)^{\sigma}-t}},

which implies

\displaystyle\|u^{n}(t)-u^{\infty}(t)\|_{E_{T}}(1-\delta)^{\sigma}\sqrt{1-\frac{t}{T(1-\delta)^{\sigma}}}

\displaystyle\leq\|u_{0}^{n}-u_{0}^{\infty}\|_{\delta}(1-\delta)^{\sigma}\sqrt{1-\frac{t}{T(1-\delta)^{\sigma}}}+\frac{1}{2}\|u^{n}(t)-u^{\infty}(t)\|_{E_{T}}

\displaystyle\leq\|u_{0}^{n}-u_{0}^{\infty}\|_{1}+\frac{1}{2}\|u^{n}(t)-u^{\infty}(t)\|_{E_{T}}.

In view of the right hand side of the above estimate is independent of
t
and
\delta.
By taking the supermum over
0<\delta<1,~0<t<\frac{T(1-\delta)^{\sigma}}{2^{\sigma}-1},
we see that

\displaystyle\|u^{n}(t)-u^{\infty}(t)\|_{E_{T}}\leq\|u_{0}^{n}-u_{0}^{\infty}\|_{1}+\frac{1}{2}\|u^{n}(t)-u^{\infty}(t)\|_{E_{T}},

which means that

\displaystyle\|u^{n}(t)-u^{\infty}(t)\|_{E_{T}}\leq 2\|u^{n}(t)-u^{\infty}(t)\|_{E_{T}}.

This verifies the above inequality is valid for any
n\geq N.

∎

5
Global Gevery regularity and analyticity

In this section, we focus on the global Gevery regularity of solution to the system (
1.2
).
Before stating the main result of this section, we introduce some lemmas.

Lemma 5.1
.

[
17
]

Let
\delta\geq 0,~\sigma\geq 1
and
s>1.
Then, for any
\xi,\eta\in\mathbb{R}
, we have

\displaystyle|(1+\xi^{2})^{\frac{s}{2}}e^{\delta(1+\xi^{2})^{\frac{1}{2\sigma}}}-(1+\eta^{2})^{\frac{s}{2}}e^{\delta(1+\eta^{2})^{\frac{1}{2\sigma}}}|

\displaystyle\leq C_{s}|\xi-\eta|\Big((1+|\xi-\eta|^{2})^{\frac{s-1}{2}}+(1+|\eta|^{2})^{\frac{s-1}{2}}

\displaystyle~~~+\delta[(1+|\xi-\eta|^{2})^{\frac{s-1}{2}+\frac{1}{2\sigma}}+(1+|\eta|^{2})^{\frac{s-1}{2}+\frac{1}{2\sigma}}]e^{\delta(1+|\xi-\eta|^{2})^{\frac{1}{2\sigma}}}\Big).

Lemma 5.2
.

[
17
]

Let
\delta\geq 0,~\sigma\geq 1
and
s>1+\frac{d}{2}.
Suppose that
u\in G^{\delta}_{\sigma,\frac{1}{2\sigma}}(\mathbb{R}),~v\in G^{\delta}_{\sigma,\frac{1}{2\sigma}}(\mathbb{R}).
Then, we have

\displaystyle|\langle\Lambda^{s}e^{\delta{\Lambda}^{\frac{1}{\sigma}}}(u\cdot v),\Lambda^{s}e^{\delta{\Lambda}^{\frac{1}{\sigma}}}v\rangle|

\displaystyle\leq C\|\Lambda^{s}u\|\|\Lambda^{s}v\|^{2}+C\delta\Big(\|\Lambda^{s}e^{\delta{\Lambda}^{\frac{1}{\sigma}}}u\|\|\Lambda^{s+\frac{1}{\sigma}}e^{\delta{\Lambda}^{\frac{1}{\sigma}}}v\|^{2}

\displaystyle~~~+\|\Lambda^{s+\frac{1}{\sigma}}e^{\delta{\Lambda}^{\frac{1}{\sigma}}}u\|\|\Lambda^{s+\frac{1}{\sigma}}e^{\delta{\Lambda}^{\frac{1}{\sigma}}}v\|\|\Lambda^{s}e^{\delta{\Lambda}^{\frac{1}{\sigma}}}v\|\Big),

where
\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle
represents the scalar product of
L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{d})
, and
\|\cdot\|:=\|\cdot\|_{L^{2}}
,
\Lambda^{s}
denotes for the inverse Fourier transform of
(1+|\xi|^{2})^{\frac{s}{2}}\hat{f}.

Lemma 5.3
.

[
17
]

For all
\delta\geq 0,~\sigma\geq 1,~s\in\mathbb{R}
and
l>0.
Let
u\in G^{\delta}_{\sigma,\frac{1}{2\sigma}}(\mathbb{R}).
Then we have the following estimate

\displaystyle\|u\|_{G^{\delta}_{\sigma,s}}\leq\sqrt{e}\|u\|_{H^{s}}+(2\delta)^{\frac{l}{2}}\|u\|_{G^{\delta}_{\sigma,s+\frac{l}{2\sigma}}}.

We can now state the main result of this section.

Theorem 5.4
.

Given
s>\frac{3}{2},~\sigma\geq 1.
Let
u_{0}\in G_{\sigma}(\mathbb{R}).
Assume that
|\alpha|+\Gamma|+\|u_{0}\|_{H^{s}}+\frac{|\beta|}{3}\|u_{0}\|^{2}_{H^{s}}+\frac{|\gamma|}{4}\|u_{0}\|^{3}_{H^{s}}\leq\lambda\epsilon.
Then there exists a unique global solution of system (
1.2
) in Gevery class
\sigma,
namely, for any
t\geq 0,~u(t,\cdot)
is of Gevery class
\sigma.

Proof.

We aim to get the global priori estimate of
u
in the time-dependent space
G^{\delta(t)}_{\sigma,s}.
One can apply the Fourier Galerkin approximating method to construct local solutions in
G^{\delta(t)}_{\sigma,s},
globalize the
result by the later estimate (
5.9
).

According to the definition of the Gevery class, we see that
G^{\delta}_{\sigma,s}\hookrightarrow G^{\delta-\epsilon}_{\sigma,\infty}
for any
\epsilon>0
. Notice that
u_{0}\in G_{\sigma}(\mathbb{R}).
In general, we can suppose that
u_{0}\in G^{1}_{\sigma,s}.
In the following, we will claim that there exists a
\delta(t)
such that the solutions remain belong to the Gevery class
G_{\sigma}.
For any
t\in[0,T],
it follows from the system (
1.2
) that

\displaystyle\frac{d}{dt}\|u\|^{2}_{G^{\delta(t)}_{\sigma,s}}

\displaystyle=\frac{d}{dt}\int(1+|\xi|^{2})^{s}e^{2\delta(t)|\xi|^{\frac{1}{\sigma}}}\hat{u}(\xi)\bar{\hat{u}}(\xi)d\xi

\displaystyle=2\dot{\delta}(t)\int(1+|\xi|^{2})^{s}|\xi|^{\frac{1}{\sigma}}e^{2\delta(t)|\xi|^{\frac{1}{\sigma}}}\hat{u}(\xi)\bar{\hat{u}}(\xi)d\xi

\displaystyle~~~+\textbf{Re}\int(1+|\xi|^{2})^{s}e^{2\delta(t)|\xi|^{\frac{1}{\sigma}}}\Big(-\widehat{uu_{x}}-\Gamma\hat{u}-\lambda\hat{u}-\widehat{P_{x}}\Big)(\xi)\bar{\hat{u}}(\xi)d\xi,

(5.1)

with
Re
stands the real part of a complex number.

Taking advantage of Lemma
5.2
, we get

\displaystyle|\int(1+|\xi|^{2})^{s}e^{2\delta(t)|\xi|^{\frac{1}{\sigma}}}\widehat{uu_{x}}(\xi)\bar{\hat{u}}(\xi)d\xi|

\displaystyle=|\langle\Lambda^{s}e^{\delta(t)\Lambda^{\frac{1}{\sigma}}}(uu_{x}),\Lambda^{s}e^{\delta(t)\Lambda^{\frac{1}{\sigma}}}u\rangle|

\displaystyle\leq C\Big(\|u\|^{3}_{H^{s}}+\delta(t)\|u\|_{G^{\delta(t)}_{\sigma,s}}\|u\|^{2}_{G^{\delta(t)}_{\sigma,s+\frac{1}{2\sigma}}}\Big).

(5.2)

Likewise, one can get the following estimates

\displaystyle|\int(1+|\xi|^{2})^{s}e^{2\delta(t)(1+|\xi|^{2})^{\frac{1}{2\sigma}}}\widehat{-P_{x}}(\xi)\bar{\hat{u}}(\xi)d\xi|

\displaystyle=|\langle\Lambda^{s}e^{\delta(t)\Lambda^{\frac{1}{\sigma}}}\Lambda^{-2}\Big(\partial_{x}(-h(u)-u^{2}-\frac{1}{2}u_{x}^{2})\Big),\Lambda^{s}e^{\delta(t)\Lambda^{\frac{1}{\sigma}}}u\rangle|

\displaystyle\leq\|\Lambda^{s-1}e^{\delta(t)\Lambda^{\frac{1}{\sigma}}}\Big(\partial_{x}(-h(u)-u^{2}-\frac{1}{2}u_{x}^{2})\Big)\|\|\Lambda^{s}e^{\delta(t)\Lambda^{\frac{1}{\sigma}}}u\|

\displaystyle\leq C\Big(\|u\|^{2}_{G^{\delta(t)}_{\sigma,s}}+\|u\|^{3}_{G^{\delta(t)}_{\sigma,s}}+\|u\|^{4}_{G^{\delta(t)}_{\sigma,s}}+\|u\|^{5}_{G^{\delta(t)}_{\sigma,s}}\Big).

(5.3)

Combining Lemma
5.3
with
l=1,~l=\frac{2}{3},~l=\frac{1}{2}
and
l=\frac{2}{5},
we obtain

\displaystyle\|u\|^{2}_{G^{\delta(t)}_{\sigma,s}}\leq C\Big(\|u\|^{2}_{H^{s}}+\delta(t)\|u\|^{2}_{G^{\delta(t)}_{\sigma,s+\frac{1}{2\sigma}}}\Big),

\displaystyle\|u\|^{3}_{G^{\delta(t)}_{\sigma,s}}\leq C\Big(\|u\|^{3}_{H^{s}}+\delta(t)\|u\|^{3}_{G^{\delta(t)}_{\sigma,s+\frac{1}{3\sigma}}}\Big)\leq C\Big(\|u\|^{3}_{H^{s}}+\delta(t)\|u\|_{G^{\delta(t)}_{\sigma,s}}\|u\|^{2}_{G^{\delta(t)}_{\sigma,s+\frac{1}{2\sigma}}}\Big),

\displaystyle\|u\|^{4}_{G^{\delta(t)}_{\sigma,s}}\leq C\Big(\|u\|^{4}_{H^{s}}+\delta(t)\|u\|^{4}_{G^{\delta(t)}_{\sigma,s+\frac{1}{4\sigma}}}\Big)\leq C\Big(\|u\|^{4}_{H^{s}}+\delta(t)\|u\|^{2}_{G^{\delta(t)}_{\sigma,s}}\|u\|^{2}_{G^{\delta(t)}_{\sigma,s+\frac{1}{2\sigma}}}\Big),

\displaystyle\|u\|^{5}_{G^{\delta(t)}_{\sigma,s}}\leq C\Big(\|u\|^{5}_{H^{s}}+\delta(t)\|u\|^{5}_{G^{\delta(t)}_{\sigma,s+\frac{1}{5\sigma}}}\Big)\leq C\Big(\|u\|^{5}_{H^{s}}+\delta(t)\|u\|^{3}_{G^{\delta(t)}_{\sigma,s}}\|u\|^{2}_{G^{\delta(t)}_{\sigma,s+\frac{1}{2\sigma}}}\Big).

(5.4)

By the same token, it yields

\displaystyle|\int(1+|\xi|^{2})^{s}e^{2\delta(t)(1+|\xi|^{2})^{\frac{1}{2\sigma}}}(-\Gamma\hat{u}-\lambda\hat{u})(\xi)\bar{\hat{u}}(\xi)d\xi|\leq\Big(\|u\|^{2}_{H^{s}}+C\delta(t)\|u\|^{2}_{G^{\delta(t)}_{\sigma,s+\frac{1}{2\sigma}}}\Big).

(5.5)

Plugging (
5
)-(
5.5
) into (
5
), we obtain

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\frac{d}{dt}\|u\|^{2}_{G^{\delta(t)}_{\sigma,s}}

\displaystyle\leq\Big(\dot{\delta}(t)+C\delta(t)(1+\|u\|_{G^{\delta(t)}_{\sigma,s}})^{3}\Big)\|u\|^{2}_{G^{\delta(t)}_{\sigma,s+\frac{1}{2\sigma}}}+C(\underbrace{1+\|u\|_{H^{s}}}_{b(t)})^{5},

which implies

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\frac{d}{dt}(1+\|u\|_{G^{\delta(t)}_{\sigma,s}})^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\Big(\dot{\delta}(t)+C\delta(t)(1+\|u\|_{G^{\delta(t)}_{\sigma,s}})^{3}\Big)(1+\|u\|_{G^{\delta(t)}_{\sigma,s+\frac{1}{2\sigma}}})^{2}+Cb^{5}(t).

For any
t\in[0,T_{0}],
we assume that

\displaystyle(1+\|u\|_{G^{\delta(t)}_{\sigma,s}})^{2}\leq 4f^{2}(t),

(5.6)

where
f^{2}(t):=2(1+\|u_{0}\|_{G^{\delta_{0}}_{\sigma,s}})^{2}+2C\int_{0}^{t}b^{5}(t^{\prime})dt^{\prime}

and take

\displaystyle\dot{\delta}(t)=-8C\delta(t)f^{3}(t).

(5.7)

Noting that
B^{s}_{2,2}=H^{s},

Lemma
2.13
guarantees that the existence of global classical solution
u\in\mathcal{C}(\mathbb{R}^{+};H^{s}).
Hence, it follows from (
5.7
) that

\displaystyle\delta(t)=\delta_{0}{\rm\exp}\Big(-8C\int_{0}^{t}f^{3}(t^{\prime})dt^{\prime}\Big)>0,~\forall~t\in[0,\infty),

(5.8)

where
0<\delta_{0}<1
.

Combining (
5.6
) and (
5.7
), we get for any
[0,T_{0}]

\displaystyle(1+\|u(t)\|_{G^{\delta(t)}_{\sigma,s}})^{2}\leq f^{2}(t).

(5.9)

Applying Theorem
3.1
, we have the unique global solution on time interval
[0,T_{0}].
Moreover, we get

\displaystyle(1+\|u(T_{0})\|_{G^{\delta(T_{0})}_{\sigma,s}})^{2}\leq f^{2}(T_{0}).

(5.10)

Using (
5.6
)-(
5.7
) and (
5.10
) again, then there exists a
T_{1}>T_{0}
such that
(1+\|u\|_{G^{\delta(t)}_{\sigma,s}})^{2}\leq 2f^{2}(t)
on
[T_{0},T_{1}].
Therefore, we have
\sup_{t\in[T_{0},T_{1}]}(1+\|u\|_{G^{\delta(t)}_{\sigma,s}})^{2}\leq f^{2}(t).
Moreover, we conclude that

\sup_{t\in[0,T_{1}]}(1+\|u(t)\|_{G^{\delta(t)}_{\sigma,s}})^{2}\leq f^{2}(t).

Repeating the bootstrap argument, we see that the global existence of strong solution of the system (
1.2
).
