Introduction

In 2012, the author compiled a subset of the following inequalities for a researcher in randomized algorithms. One might think of said inequalities as a very quick reference, with access to primary and secondary resources listed either within the section or alongside the inequality of interest. In the intervening years, some of the original sources and their respective links have vanished, leading the author to consider a companion document with proofs for select inequalities within this list. Though the author would refute the completeness of this collection for more advanced researchers, he nonetheless believes it may serve some interest. It is important
