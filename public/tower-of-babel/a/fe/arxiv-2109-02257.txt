The Multipartite Ramsey numbers m j ​ ( n ​ K 2 , C 7 ) m_{j}(nK_{2},C_{7})
Yaser Rowshan 1

Abstract
. Assume that K j × n K_{j\times n} be a complete, multipartite graph consisting of j j partite sets and n n vertices in each partite set. For given graphs G 1 G_{1} and G 2 G_{2} , the multipartite Ramsey number (M-R-number) m j ​ ( G 1 , G 2 ) m_{j}(G_{1},G_{2}) is the smallest integer t t such that any subgraph G G of the K j × t K_{j\times t} , either G G contains a copy of G 1 G_{1} or its complement relative to K j × t K_{j\times t} contains a copy of G 2 G_{2} . C. J. Jayawardene, E. T. Baskoro et al. ( 2016 ) (2016) gave the size of M-R-numbe m j ​ ( n ​ K 2 , C 7 ) m_{j}(nK_{2},C_{7}) for j ≥ 2 j\geq 2 and n ≤ 6 n\leq 6 . Y. Rowshan et al. ( 2021 ) (2021) gave the size of M-R-number m j ​ ( n ​ K 2 , C 7 ) m_{j}(nK_{2},C_{7}) for j = 2 , 3 , 4 j=2,3,4 and n ≥ 2 n\geq 2 . In this article we compute the size of M-R-number m j ​ ( n ​ K 2 , C 7 ) m_{j}(nK_{2},C_{7}) , for each j ≥ 5 j\geq 5 and n ≥ 2 n\geq 2 .
