Common ( π \pi , π \pi ) Band Folding and Surface Reconstruction in FeAs-Based Superconductors
Yongqing Cai 1,2,♯ , Tao Xie 1,♯ , Huan Yang 1,2,♯ , Dingsong Wu 1,2 , Jianwei Huang 1,2 , Wenshan Hong 1,2 , Lu Cao 1,2 , Chang Liu 1,2 , Cong Li 1,2 , Yu Xu 1,2 , Qiang Gao 1,2 , Taimin Miao 1,2 , Guodong Liu 1,2,3 , Shiliang Li 1,2,3 , Li Huang 1 , Huiqian Luo 1,2,3 , Zuyan Xu 4 , Hongjun Gao 1,2,3,5 , Lin Zhao 1,2,3∗ and X. Abstract
High resolution angle-resolved photoemission spectroscopy (ARPES) measurements are carried out on CaKFe 4 As 4 , KCa 2 Fe 4 As 4 F 2 and (Ba 0.6 K 0.4 )Fe 2 As 2 superconductors. Clear evidence of band folding between the Brillouin zone center and corners with a ( π \pi , π \pi ) wave vector has been found from the measured Fermi surface and band structures in all the three kinds of superconductors. A dominant 2 × 2 \sqrt{2}\,\times\,\sqrt{2} surface reconstruction is observed on the cleaved surface of CaKFe 4 As 4 by scanning tunneling microscopy (STM) measurements. We propose that the commonly observed 2 × 2 \sqrt{2}\,\times\,\sqrt{2} reconstruction in the FeAs-based superconductors provides a general scenario to understand the origin of the ( π \pi , π \pi ) band folding. Our observations provide new insights in understanding the electronic structure and superconductivity mechanism in iron-based superconductors. PACS: 74. 70. -b, 74. 25. Jb, 79. 60. -i, 71. 20. -b
