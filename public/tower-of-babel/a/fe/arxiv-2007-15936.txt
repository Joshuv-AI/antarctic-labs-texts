1.1.

This subsection details a non-standard notation scheme the author

Definition 7

For each

(1.2)

\displaystyle H_{0}\left(x\right)

(1.3)

\displaystyle H_{1}\left(x\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{qx+1}{2}

We also call
H_{0}
and

Given an integer

H_{0}
and

Example 1
.

Classically, one of the key statistics of the Collatz map (or, for
that matter,
T_{q}
) is the

. Given an integer

n
under
H

H

For this example, let us fix

\displaystyle H\left(3\right)

\displaystyle=H_{1}\left(3\right)=\frac{3\times 3+1}{2}=5

\displaystyle H^{\circ 2}\left(3\right)

\displaystyle=H\left(5\right)=H_{1}\left(5\right)=\frac{3\times 5+1}{2}=8

\displaystyle H^{\circ 3}\left(3\right)

H^{\circ 3}\left(3\right)=H_{0}\left(H_{1}\left(H_{1}\left(3\right)\right)\right)

(1.4)

H^{\circ k}\left(n\right)=\left(H_{j_{1}}\circ H_{j_{2}}\circ\cdots\circ H_{j_{k}}\right)\left(n\right)

Since

\displaystyle n

\displaystyle=H^{\circ k}\left(n\right)

\displaystyle\Updownarrow

(1.5)

\displaystyle n

\displaystyle n

\displaystyle=\left(H_{j_{1}}\circ H_{j_{2}}\circ\cdots\circ H_{j_{k}}\right)\left(n\right)

\displaystyle\Updownarrow

\displaystyle n

\displaystyle=an+b

\displaystyle\Updownarrow

\displaystyle n

Definition 10

(1.45)

(1.47)

3.28
)
is small in absolute value. The import of Baker’s Theorem is that
it allows one to obtain lower bounds on expressions such as
\left|2^{m}-3^{n}\right|
,
where
m
and
n
are positive integers, and it was for applications
such as these in conjunction with the study of Diophantine equations
that Baker earned the Fields Medal. See
[
6
]

[
8
]
.
