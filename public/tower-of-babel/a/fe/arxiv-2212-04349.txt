SISO-OFDM and MISO-OFDM Counterparts for “Wideband Waveforming for Integrated Data and Energy Transfer: Creating Extra Gain Beyond Multiple Antennas and Multiple Carriers”
Zhonglun Wang, Jie Hu, Kun Yang

Abstract
In this article, we proposethe SISO-OFDM and MISO-OFDM based IDET systems, which are the counterparts of our optimal wideband waveforming strategy in [ 1 ] . The fair-throughput and the sum-throughput optimisation problems are formulated. By exploiting the FP based alternating algorithm, we obtain the optimal fair-throughput and sum-throughput for both the SISO-OFDM and MISO-OFDM.

SISO-OFDM for IDET in Wideband Channel

I-A

System Model

We have
J
receivers and
N
subcarriers in the system.
Both the transmitter and the receivers are equipped with a single antenna.
There are a number of resolvable paths existing in the channel generated by the wideband OFDM signal. Denote the channel length of the
j
-th receiver as
L_{j}
. Then the wideband channel vector is expressed as

\displaystyle\mathbf{h}_{j}=\left[\underbrace{h_{j,0},\cdots,h_{j,l},\cdots,h_{j,L_{j}-1}}_{{L_{j}}},\underbrace{0,\cdots,0}_{L-L_{j}}\right],

(1)

where we define the maximum channel length as
L=\max_{j}L_{j}
.

The symbol of the
j
-th receiver in the
k
-th subcarrier is denoted as
S_{j,k}
. All the receivers multiplex all the subcarriers together, while the multiplexed symbol in the
k
-th subcarrier can be expressed as

\displaystyle S_{k}=\sum_{j=1}^{J}S_{j,k}.

(2)

By invoking the inverse fast Fourier transform (IFFT). Moreover, the
n
-th symbol in the time-domain can be expressed as

\displaystyle s_{n}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{N}}\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}S_{k}e^{j2\pi nk/N},\forall k=0,1,\cdots,N-1.

(3)

In order to eliminate the inter-symbol interference (ISI) and gurantee the orthogonality of the subcarriers for the
j
-th receiver, we need to add the cyclic prefix (CP), whose period should be longer than the delay spread
\left(L_{j}-1\right)
at least. Therefore, we add
\left(L-1\right)
CP symbols to make sure we can remove the ISI for all the receivers. Therefore, the transmit symbols are sequentially expressed as

\displaystyle x_{0}=s_{0},

\displaystyle\text{\ \ \ }\vdots

\displaystyle x_{N-1}=s_{N-1}

\displaystyle\text{\ \ \ }\vdots

\displaystyle x_{N+L-2}=s_{N-2}.

(4)

We denote the transmit symbol vector
\mathbf{x}=\left[x_{0},\cdots,x_{N+L-2}\right]
.
Without considering the tail part of an OFDM symbol affecting the head part of the next symbol, the received symbols of the
j
-th receiver are expressed as

\displaystyle\mathbf{r}_{j}

\displaystyle=\mathbf{h}_{j}*\mathbf{x}=\begin{bmatrix}&h_{j,0}&0&\cdots&0&0&\cdots&0\\
&\vdots&\vdots&\cdots&\vdots&0&\cdots&0\\
&h_{j,L-1}&h_{j,L-2}&\cdots&h_{j,0}&0&\cdots&0\\
&0&h_{j,L-1}&\cdots&h_{j,1}&h_{j,0}&\cdots&0\\
&\vdots&\vdots&\vdots&\vdots&\vdots&\vdots&\vdots\\
&0&0&\cdots&0&0&\cdots&h_{j,0}\\
&\vdots&\vdots&\vdots&\vdots&\vdots&\vdots&\vdots\\
&0&0&\cdots&0&0&\cdots&h_{j,L-1}\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}&s_{0}\\
&\vdots\\
&s_{L-1}\\
&\vdots\\
&s_{N-1}\\
&s_{0}\\
&\vdots\\
&s_{L-2}\end{bmatrix}+\mathbf{z}_{r,j},

(5)

where
\mathbf{z}_{r,j}
is the circularly symmetric complex white Gaussian noise introduced by the receive antenna with a zeros mean and a variance of
\sigma_{0}^{2}
.
The
L
to
(L+N-1)
elements of the
\mathbf{r}_{j}
are the circular convolution between the channel vector
\mathbf{h}_{j}
and the OFDM symbol vector
\mathbf{s}
. After removing the CP, the input signals to the information decoder for the
j
-th receiver can be expressed as

\displaystyle\mathbf{r}_{ID,j}

\displaystyle=\sqrt{1-\rho_{j}}\left[y_{j,0},\cdots,y_{j,N-1}\right]+\sqrt{1-\rho_{j}}\mathbf{z}_{j}+\mathbf{v}_{j}

\displaystyle=\sqrt{1-\rho_{j}}\begin{bmatrix}&h_{j,L-1}&h_{j,L-2}&\cdots&h_{j,0}&0&\cdots&0\\
&0&h_{j,L-1}&\cdots&h_{j,1}&h_{j,0}&\cdots&0\\
&\vdots&\vdots&\vdots&\vdots&\vdots&\vdots&\vdots\\
&0&0&\cdots&h_{j,L-1}&h_{j,L-2}&\cdots&0\\
&\vdots&\vdots&\vdots&\vdots&\vdots&\vdots&\vdots\\
&0&0&\cdots&0&0&\cdots&h_{j,0}\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}&s_{0}\\
&s_{1}\\
&\vdots\\
&s_{N-1}\end{bmatrix}

\displaystyle\text{\ \ \ }+\sqrt{1-\rho_{j}}\mathbf{z}_{j}+\mathbf{v}_{j}

\displaystyle=\sqrt{1-\rho_{j}}\mathbf{h}_{j}\otimes\mathbf{s}+\sqrt{1-\rho_{j}}\mathbf{z}_{j}+\mathbf{v}_{j}.

(6)

where
\mathbf{z}_{j}=\left[z_{r,j,L-1},\cdots,z_{r,j,N+L-2}\right]
and
\mathbf{v}_{j}\sim\mathcal{CN}\left({0},\sigma_{cov}^{2}\mathbf{I}\right)
is the passband-to-baseband conversion noise.

Then we decode the OFDM symbol by exploiting the orthogonality between the subcarriers, which has the same form as the Fast-Fourier-Transformation (FFT). The
k
-th decoding symbol for the
j
-th receiver in the frequency domain can be expressed as

\displaystyle Y_{j,k}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\sqrt{N}}\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}\mathbf{r}_{ID,j}

\displaystyle=\sqrt{N}\sqrt{1-\rho_{j}}H_{j,k}S_{k}+\sqrt{1-\rho_{j}}Z_{j,k}+V_{j,k},

(5)

where
H_{j,k}
and
S_{k}
are the FFT of the
h_{j,n}
and
s_{n}
, which can be expressed as

\displaystyle H_{j,k}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\sqrt{N}}\sum_{n=0}^{L-1}{h_{j,n}}e^{-j2\pi nk/N},

(6)

\displaystyle S_{k}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\sqrt{N}}\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}{s_{n}}e^{-j2\pi nk/N},

(7)

\displaystyle Z_{j,k}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\sqrt{N}}\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}{z_{j,k}}e^{-j2\pi nk/N},

(8)

\displaystyle V_{j,k}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\sqrt{N}}\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}{v_{j,k}}e^{-j2\pi nk/N}.

(9)

The noise
Z_{j,k}
and
V_{j,k}
have the same distribution with
z_{j,k}
and
v_{j,k}
, respectively
[
3
]
.

Then the signal-to-interference-plus-noise ratio (SINR)
\gamma_{j,k}
can be expressed as

\displaystyle\gamma_{j,k}\left(\mathbf{p},\rho_{j}\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{(1-\rho_{j})N\left|H_{j,k}\right|^{2}\mathbb{E}\left[S_{j,k}S_{j,k}^{\dagger}\right]}{(1-\rho_{j})\sum_{j^{\prime}\neq j}N\left|H_{j^{\prime},k}\right|^{2}\mathbb{E}\left[S_{j^{\prime},k}S_{j^{\prime},k}^{\dagger}\right]+(1-\rho_{j})\sigma_{0}^{2}+\sigma_{cov}^{2}}

\displaystyle=\frac{(1-\rho_{j})N\left|H_{j,k}\right|^{2}p_{j,k}}{(1-\rho_{j})\sum_{j^{\prime}\neq j}N\left|H_{j^{\prime},k}\right|^{2}p_{j^{\prime},k}+(1-\rho_{j})\sigma_{0}^{2}+\sigma_{cov}^{2}},

(10)

where
\mathbf{p}=\left[[p_{1,0},\cdots,p_{1,N-1}]^{T},\cdots,[p_{J,0},\cdots,p_{J,N-1}]^{T}\right]
.
The throughput
R_{j}\left(\mathbf{p},\rho_{j}\right)
is expressed as

\displaystyle R_{j}\left(\mathbf{p},\rho_{j}\right)=\frac{B}{N}\cdot\frac{N}{N+L-1}\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}\log_{2}\left(1+\gamma_{j,k}\left(\mathbf{p},\rho_{j}\right)\right),\text{\ bit/s}.

(11)

Let the average transmit power of each symbol
s_{n}
be
P_{tx}^{OFDM}
. According to the Perseval’s theorem
[
2
]
, we can obtain

\displaystyle\frac{1}{{N}}\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}\left|S_{k}\right|^{2}=P_{tx}^{OFDM},

(12)

\displaystyle\frac{1}{{N}}\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}\left|s_{n}\right|^{2}=P_{tx}^{OFDM},

(13)

\displaystyle\frac{1}{{N+L-1}}\sum_{n=0}^{N+L-2}\left|x_{n}\right|^{2}=P_{tx}^{OFDM}.

(14)

Therefore, the power constraints for the OFDM based IDET system can be expressed as

\displaystyle 0\leq\sum_{k=0}^{M-1}\mathbb{E}\left[S_{k}S_{k}^{\dagger}\right]=\sum_{k=0}^{M-1}\sum_{j=1}^{J}\mathbb{E}\left[S_{j,k}S_{j,k}^{\dagger}\right]=\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}\sum_{j=1}^{J}p_{j,k}\leq NP_{tx}^{OFDM}.

(15)

The RF signal for the energy harvester of the
j
-th receiver can be expressed as

\displaystyle\mathbf{r}_{EH,j}=\sqrt{\rho_{j}}\left[\mathbf{r}_{CP,j}^{T},\mathbf{r}_{OS,j}^{T}\right]^{T},

(16)

where
\mathbf{r}_{CP,j}
is the received CP symbol with considering the tail part of an OFDM symbol affecting the head part of the next symbol and
r_{OS,j}
is the receive OFDM symbol.
The power for the energy harvester of the
j
-th receiver is
P_{EH,j}=\frac{{\rho_{j}}}{N+L-1}\left(E_{CP,j}+E_{OS,j}\right)
, where
E_{CP,j}
is the energy of the received CP symbols and
E_{OS,j}
is the energy of the received OFDM symbols. The received CP symbols can be expressed

\left\{\begin{aligned} r_{CP,j,0}&=h_{j,0}s_{0}+h_{j,L-1}s_{0}^{\prime}+h_{j,L-2}s_{1}^{\prime}+\cdots+h_{j,1}s_{L-2}^{\prime},\\
r_{CP,j,1}&=h_{j,1}s_{0}+h_{j,2}s_{1}+h_{j,L-1}s_{1}^{\prime}+\cdots+h_{j,2}s_{L-2}^{\prime},\\
&\text{\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ }\vdots\\
r_{CP,j,n}&=h_{j,n}s_{0}+\cdots+h_{j,0}s_{n}+h_{j,L-1}s_{n}^{\prime}+\cdots+h_{j,n+1}s_{L-2},\\
&\text{\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ }\vdots\\
r_{CP,j,L-2}&=h_{j,L-2}s_{0}+h_{j,L-3}s_{1}+\cdots+h_{j,0}s_{L-2}+h_{j,L-1}s_{L-2}^{\prime}.\end{aligned}\right.

According to Theorem 1 and Theorem 2, the energy carried by the received CP can be expressed as

\displaystyle E_{CP,j}

\displaystyle=\mathbb{E}\left[\sum_{n=0}^{L-2}\left|r_{CP,j,n}\right|\right]

\displaystyle=\mathbb{E}\left[\sum_{n=0}^{L-2}\left|\left(\sum_{i=0}^{n}h_{j,i}s_{n-i}+\sum_{i=n}^{L-2}h_{j,L-1+n-i}s_{i}^{\prime}+z_{r,j,n}\right)\right|^{2}\right]

\displaystyle=\left(L-1\right)P_{tx}^{OFDM}\sum_{n=0}^{L-1}\left|h_{j,n}\right|^{2}+\left(L-1\right)\sigma_{0}^{2}.

(17)

The energy carried by the received OFDM symbols

can be expressed as

\displaystyle E_{OS,j}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=L-1}^{N+L-2}\left|r_{j,n}\right|^{2}

\displaystyle=N\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}\left|H_{j,k}\right|^{2}\left|S_{k}\right|^{2}+\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}\left|Z_{j,k}\right|^{2}

\displaystyle=N\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}\sum_{j=1}^{J}p_{j,k}\left|H_{j,k}\right|^{2}+N\sigma_{0}^{2}.

(18)

Then the receive power for the energy harvester of the
j
-th receiver can be reformulated as

\displaystyle P_{EH,j}\left(\mathbf{p},\rho_{j}\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{\rho_{j}}{N+L-1}\left(E_{CP,j}+E_{OS,j}\right).

(19)

The RF-DC conversion is determined by the charateristics of the diode. Given an input RF power of

\displaystyle i_{j}^{SO}(\mathbf{p},\rho_{j})\approx k_{0}+k_{1}P_{EH}(\mathbf{p},\rho_{j})+k_{2}P_{EH}^{2}(\mathbf{p},\rho_{j}),

(20)

where the circuit related constants satisfies

s_{n}
; (b) DSW up-sampling symbol
s_{DSW,n}^{\mathcal{D}}
.

Assume
s_{DSW,n\mathcal{D}}^{\mathcal{D}}\sim\mathcal{CN}\left(0,P_{tx}^{DSW}\right)
, for
\forall n=0,\cdots,N+L-2
. We can obtain the average power of each symbol
P_{tx}^{DSW}
as

\displaystyle P_{tx}^{DSW}

\displaystyle=\mathbb{E}\left[\left|s_{DSW,n\mathcal{D}}^{\mathcal{D}}\right|^{2}\right]=\mathcal{D}\overline{P.}

(36)

Similarly, the average transmit power for the OFDM can be expressed as

\displaystyle\overline{P}=\frac{1}{N+L-1}\sum_{n=0}^{N+L-2}\mathbb{E}\left[\left|s_{n}\right|^{2}\right].

(37)

As mentioning in
s_{n}\sim\mathcal{CN}\left(0,P_{tx}^{OFDM}\right)
, the average transmit power of each OFDM symbol
P_{tx}^{OFDM}
can be expressed as

\displaystyle P_{tx}^{OFDM}=\mathbb{E}\left[\left|s_{n}\right|^{2}\right]=\overline{P}.

(38)

Finally, for comparing the DSW based IDET scheme and the OFDM based IDET scheme fairly, we must let the ratio between the average power of the OFDM symbols and the average power of the non-zero DSW symbols satisfy
P_{tx}^{DSW}/P_{tx}^{OFDM}=\mathcal{D}
.

II
MISO OFDM

II-A

System Model

We have an OFDM transmitter with
M
antennas and
J
single antenna receivers. The number of the OFDM subcarriers is denoted as
N
. Assume the wideband resolvable channel from the
m
-th transmit antenna to the
j
-th receiver as
\mathbf{h}_{j,m}=\left[h_{j,m,0}\cdots,h_{j,m,L}\right]
, where the channel length for all links is
L\leq N
. The vector
\mathbf{H}_{j,k}=[H_{j,1,k},\cdots,H_{j,M,k}]
is defined as the FFT channel in the
k
-th subcarrier between the transmitter and the
j
-th receiver, where the
m
-th element
H_{j,m,k}
is expressed as

\displaystyle H_{j,m,k}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{N}}\sum_{n=0}^{L-1}h_{j,m,n}e^{-j2\pi nk/N},\forall k=0,\cdots,N-1.

(39)

The
k
-th symbol of the
j
-th receiver in the
m
-th antenna is
S_{j,m,k}
, which satisfies
S_{j,1,k}=\cdots=S_{j,M,k}
and
S_{j,m,k}\sim\mathcal{CN}(0,1)
. Moreover, the optimal beamforming vector of the
j
-th receiver for the
k
-th subcarrier is defined as
\mathbf{W}_{j,k}=[W_{j,1,k},\cdots,W_{j,M,k}]^{T}
, which satisfies
\|\mathbf{W}_{j,k}\|_{2}^{2}\leq p_{j,k}
. The
k
-th symbol of the
m
-th antenna
S_{m,k}
is expressed as

\displaystyle S_{m,k}=\sum_{j=1}^{J}W_{j,m,k}S_{j,m,k}.

(40)

Then time-domain symbol
s_{m,n}
is expressed as

\displaystyle s_{m,n}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{N}}\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}S_{m,k}e^{j2\pi nk/N},\forall n=0,\cdots,N-1.

(41)

In order to eliminate the inter-symbol interference (ISI) and gurantee the orthogonality of the subcarriers for the
j
-th receiver, we also need to add the CP whose period is longger the delay spread
\left(L_{j}-1\right)
at least. Therefore, we add
\left(L-1\right)
CP symbols to make sure we can remove the ISI for all the receivers. The transmit symbols are then expressed as

\left\{\small\begin{aligned} x_{m,0}&=s_{m,0},\\
&\vdots\\
x_{m,N-1}&=s_{m,N-1},\\
x_{m,N}&=s_{m,0},\\
\vdots\\
x_{m,N+L-2}&=s_{m,L-2}.\end{aligned}\right.

The received symbol of the
j
-th receiver
\mathbf{r}_{j}
is expressed as

\displaystyle\mathbf{r}_{j}

\displaystyle=\sum_{m=1}^{M}\mathbf{h}_{j,m}*\mathbf{x}_{m}+\mathbf{z}_{j}

\displaystyle=\sum_{m=1}^{M}\begin{bmatrix}&h_{j,m,0}&0&\cdots&0&0&\cdots&0\\
&\vdots&\vdots&\cdots&\vdots&\vdots&\cdots&\vdots\\
&h_{j,m,L-1}&h_{j,m,L-2}&\cdots&h_{j,m,0}&0&\cdots&0\\
&0&h_{j,m,L-1}&\cdots&h_{j,m,1}&h_{j,m,0}&\cdots&0\\
&\vdots&\vdots&\vdots&\vdots&\vdots&\vdots&\vdots\\
&0&0&\cdots&0&0&\cdots&h_{j,m,0}\\
&\vdots&\vdots&\vdots&\vdots&\vdots&\vdots&\vdots\\
&0&0&\cdots&0&0&\cdots&h_{j,m,L-1}\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}&s_{m,0}\\
&\vdots\\
&s_{m,L-1}\\
&\vdots\\
&s_{m,N-1}\\
&s_{m,0}\\
&\vdots\\
&s_{m,L-2}\end{bmatrix}+\begin{bmatrix}&z_{j,0}\\
&\vdots\\
&z_{j,L-1}\\
&\vdots\\
&z_{j,N-1}\\
&z_{j,N}\\
&\vdots\\
&z_{j,N+L-2}\end{bmatrix},

where
\mathbf{z}_{j}\sim\mathcal{CN}(0,\sigma_{0}^{2}\mathbf{I})
is the antenna noise. By removing the CP in the receivers, the information symbol of the
j
-th receiver
\mathbf{r}_{ID,j}
is expressed as

\displaystyle\mathbf{r}_{ID,j}=

\displaystyle\sqrt{1-\rho_{j}}\sum_{m=1}^{M}\mathbf{h}_{j,m}\otimes\mathbf{s}_{m}+\sqrt{1-\rho_{j}}\widetilde{\mathbf{z}}_{j}+\widetilde{\mathbf{v}}_{j}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sqrt{1-\rho_{j}}\sum_{m=1}^{M}\begin{bmatrix}&h_{j,m,L-1}&h_{j,m,L-2}&\cdots&h_{j,m,0}&0&\cdots&0\\
&0&h_{j,m,L-1}&\cdots&h_{j,m,1}&h_{j,m,0}&\cdots&0\\
&\vdots&\vdots&\vdots&\vdots&\vdots&\vdots&\vdots\\
&0&0&\cdots&h_{j,m,L-1}&h_{j,m,L-2}&\cdots&0\\
&\vdots&\vdots&\vdots&\vdots&\vdots&\vdots&\vdots\\
&0&0&\cdots&0&0&\cdots&h_{j,m,0}\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}&s_{m,0}\\
&s_{m,1}\\
&\vdots\\
&s_{m,N-1}\end{bmatrix}

\displaystyle+\sqrt{1-\rho_{j}}\widetilde{\mathbf{z}}_{j}+\widetilde{\mathbf{v}}_{j},

(42)

where
\widetilde{\mathbf{z}}_{j}
is the antenna noise and
\widetilde{\mathbf{v}}_{j}\sim\mathcal{CN}(0,\sigma_{cov}^{2}\mathbf{I})
is the RF-to-baseband conversion noise. Then we decode the OFDM symbols by exploiting the orthogonality between the subcarriers, which has the same form with the DFT. The
k
-th decoding symbol of the
j
-th receiver
Y_{j,k}
is expressed as

\displaystyle Y_{j,k}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\sqrt{N}}\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}r_{ID,j,n}e^{-j2\pi nk/N}

\displaystyle=\sqrt{N}\sqrt{1-\rho_{j}}\sum_{m=1}^{M}H_{j,m,k}S_{m,k}+\sqrt{1-\rho_{j}}Z_{j,k}+V_{j,k}.

\displaystyle=\sqrt{N}\sqrt{1-\rho_{j}}\sum_{m=1}^{M}\sum_{j=1}^{J}H_{j,m,k}S_{j,m,k}+\sqrt{1-\rho_{j}}Z_{j,k}+V_{j,k}.

(43)

Moreover, the average SINR
\gamma_{j,k}
is expressed as

\displaystyle\gamma_{j,k}\left(\mathbf{W},\rho_{j}\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{N(1-\rho_{j})\mathbf{H}_{j,k}\mathbf{W}_{j,k}\mathbf{W}_{j,k}^{\dagger}\mathbf{H}_{j,k}^{\dagger}}{N(1-\rho_{j})\sum_{j^{\prime}\neq j}\mathbf{H}_{j,k}\mathbf{W}_{j^{\prime},k}\mathbf{W}_{j^{\prime},k}^{\dagger}\mathbf{H}_{j,k}^{\dagger}+(1-\rho_{j})\sigma_{0}^{2}+\sigma_{cov}^{2}}

(44)

where
\mathbf{W}=\left[[\mathbf{W}_{1,0},\cdots,\mathbf{W}_{1,N-1}]^{T},\cdots,[\mathbf{W}_{J,0},\cdots,\mathbf{W}_{J,N-1}]^{T}\right]
.
Finally, the throughput of the
j
-th receiver
R_{j}
is expressed as

\displaystyle R_{j}\left(\mathbf{W},\rho_{j}\right)=\frac{B}{N}\frac{N}{N+L-1}\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}\log_{2}(1+\gamma_{j,k}\left(\mathbf{W},\rho_{j}\right)),\text{bit/s}.

(45)

Considering the tail part of an OFDM symbol affecting the the head part of the next symbol, the CP
\{r_{CP,j,m,k}\}
can be expressed as

\left\{\begin{aligned} r_{CP,j,0}&=\sum_{m=1}^{M}h_{j,m,0}s_{m,0}+\sum_{m=1}^{M}h_{j,m,L-1}s_{m,0}^{\prime}+\sum_{m=1}^{M}h_{j,m,L-2}s_{m,1}^{\prime}+\cdots+\sum_{m=1}^{M}h_{j,m,1}s_{m,L-2}^{\prime},\\
r_{CP,j,1}&=\sum_{m=1}^{M}h_{j,m,1}s_{m,0}+\sum_{m=1}^{M}h_{j,m,2}s_{m,1}+\sum_{m=1}^{M}h_{j,m,L-1}s_{m,1}^{\prime}+\cdots+\sum_{m=1}^{M}h_{j,m,2}s_{m,L-2}^{\prime},\\
&\text{\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ }\vdots\\
r_{CP,j,n}&=\sum_{m=1}^{M}h_{j,m,n}s_{m,0}+\cdots+\sum_{m=1}^{M}h_{j,m,0}s_{m,n}+\sum_{m=1}^{M}h_{j,m,L-1}s_{m,n}^{\prime}+\cdots+\sum_{m=1}^{M}h_{j,m,n+1}s^{\prime}_{m,L-2},\\
&\text{\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ }\vdots\\
r_{CP,j,L-2}&=\sum_{m=1}^{M}h_{j,m,L-2}s_{m,0}+\sum_{m=1}^{M}h_{j,m,L-3}s_{m,1}+\cdots+\sum_{m=1}^{M}h_{j,m,0}s_{m,L-2}+\sum_{m=1}^{M}h_{j,m,L-1}s_{m,L-2}^{\prime}.\end{aligned}\right.

The energy
E_{CP,j}
harvested by the
j
-th receiver from CP is expressed as

\displaystyle E_{CP,j}=

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\left[\sum_{n=0}^{L-2}\left|r_{CP,j,n}\right|^{2}\right]

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\left[\sum_{n=0}^{L-2}\left|\sum_{i=0}^{n}\sum_{m=1}^{M}h_{j,m,i}s_{m,n-i}+\sum_{i=n}^{L-2}\sum_{m=1}^{M}h_{j,m,L-1+n-i}s^{\prime}_{m,i}\right|^{2}\right]+\sum_{n=0}^{L-2}\mathbb{E}\left[\left|z_{j,n}\right|^{2}\right]

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{L-2}\sum_{i=0}^{n}\mathbb{E}\left[\left|\sum_{m=1}^{M}h_{j,m,i}s_{m,n-i}\right|^{2}\right]+\sum_{n=0}^{L-2}\sum_{i=n}^{L-2}\mathbb{E}\left[\left|\sum_{m=1}^{M}h_{j,m,L-1+n-i}s_{m,i}\right|^{2}\right]+\sum_{n=0}^{L-2}\mathbb{E}\left[\left|z_{j,n}\right|^{2}\right]

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{L-2}\sum_{i=0}^{n}\mathbb{E}\left[\frac{1}{N}\left|\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}\sum_{j^{\prime}=1}^{J}S_{j^{\prime},m,k}e^{j2\pi(n-i)k/N}\sum_{m=1}^{M}h_{j,m,i}W_{j^{\prime},m,k}\right|^{2}\right]

\displaystyle+\sum_{n=0}^{L-2}\sum_{i=n}^{L-2}\mathbb{E}\left[\frac{1}{N}\left|\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}\sum_{j^{\prime}=1}^{J}S^{\prime}_{j^{\prime},m,k}e^{j2\pi ik/N}\sum_{m=1}^{M}h_{j,m,L-1+n-i}W_{j^{\prime},m,k}\right|^{2}\right]+\sum_{n=0}^{L-2}\mathbb{E}\left[\left|z_{j,n}\right|^{2}\right]

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{L-2}\sum_{i=0}^{L-1}\left[\frac{1}{N}\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}\sum_{j^{\prime}=1}^{J}\left|\sum_{m=1}^{M}h_{j,m,i}W_{j^{\prime},m,k}\right|^{2}\right]+\sum_{n=0}^{L-2}\mathbb{E}\left[\left|z_{j,n}\right|^{2}\right]

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{L-2}\sum_{i=0}^{L-1}\left[\frac{1}{N}\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}\sum_{j^{\prime}=1}^{J}\sum_{m=1}^{M}\left|h_{j,m,i}\right|^{2}\left|\sum_{m=1}^{M}W_{j^{\prime},m,k}\right|^{2}\right]+\sum_{n=0}^{L-2}\mathbb{E}\left[\left|z_{j,n}\right|^{2}\right]

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{N}\sum_{n=0}^{L-2}\sum_{i=0}^{L-1}\left[\left|\sum_{m=1}^{M}h_{j,m,i}\right|^{2}\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}\sum_{j^{\prime}=1}^{J}\left|\sum_{m=1}^{M}W_{j^{\prime},m,k}\right|^{2}\right]+\sum_{n=0}^{L-2}\mathbb{E}\left[\left|z_{j,n}\right|^{2}\right]

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{M^{2}}{N}\left[\sum_{n=0}^{L-2}\sum_{i=0}^{L-1}\sum_{m=1}^{M}\left|h_{j,m,i}\right|^{2}\right]\left[\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}\sum_{j^{\prime}=1}^{J}\sum_{m=1}^{M}\left|W_{j^{\prime},m,k}\right|^{2}\right]+\sum_{n=0}^{L-2}\mathbb{E}\left[\left|z_{j,n}\right|^{2}\right]

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle M^{2}(L-1)P_{tx}^{OFDM}\sum_{i=0}^{L-1}\sum_{m=1}^{M}\left|h_{j,m,i}\right|^{2}+(L-1)\sigma_{0}^{2}.

(46)

The energy
E_{OS,j}
harvested from the OFDM symbols is expressed as

\displaystyle E_{OS,j}=

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\left[\sum_{n=L-1}^{N+L-2}\left|r_{j,n}\right|^{2}\right]

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle N\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}\mathbb{E}\left[\left|\sum_{m=1}^{M}H_{j,m,k}S_{m,k}\right|^{2}\right]+\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}\mathbb{E}\left[\left|Z_{j,k}\right|^{2}\right]

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle N\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}\mathbb{E}\left[\left|\sum_{j^{\prime}=1}^{J}\sum_{m=1}^{M}H_{j,m,k}W_{j^{\prime},m,k}S_{j^{\prime},m,k}\right|^{2}\right]+\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}\mathbb{E}\left[\left|Z_{j,k}\right|^{2}\right]

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle N\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}\sum_{j^{\prime}=1}^{J}\mathbf{H}_{j,k}\mathbf{W}_{j^{\prime},k}\mathbf{W}_{j^{\prime},k}^{\dagger}\mathbf{H}_{j,k}^{\dagger}+N\sigma_{0}^{2}

(47)

The total RF power harvested by the
j
-th receiver is formulated as

\displaystyle P_{EH,j}\left(\mathbf{W},\rho_{j}\right)=

\displaystyle\frac{\rho_{j}}{N+L-1}\left(E_{CP,j}+E_{OS,j}\right)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{\rho_{j}}{N+L-1}\left(M^{2}(L-1)P_{tx}^{OFDM}\sum_{i=0}^{L-1}\sum_{m=1}^{M}\left|h_{j,m,i}\right|^{2}+N\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}\sum_{j^{\prime}=1}^{J}\mathbf{H}_{j,k}\mathbf{W}_{j^{\prime},k}\mathbf{W}_{j^{\prime},k}^{\dagger}\mathbf{H}_{j,k}^{\dagger}\right)+\rho_{j}\sigma_{0}^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\widetilde{P}_{EH,j}\left(\mathbf{W},\rho_{j}\right),

(48)

where
\widetilde{P}_{EH,j}\left(\mathbf{W},\rho_{j}\right)
is the upper bound of
P_{EH,j}\left(\mathbf{W},\rho_{j}\right)
. Given an input power of
\widetilde{P}_{EH,j}(\mathbf{W},\rho_{j})
, the output DC is expressed as

\displaystyle i_{j}^{MO}(\mathbf{W},\rho_{j})\approx k_{0}+k_{1}\widetilde{P}_{EH,j}(\mathbf{W},\rho_{j})+k_{2}\widetilde{P}^{2}_{EH,j}(\mathbf{W},\rho_{j}).

(49)
