A Metric Lower Bound Estimate for Geodesics in the Space of Kähler Potentials
Jingchen Hu

Abstract
In this paper we establish a positive lower bound estimate for the second smallest eigenvalue of the complex Hessian of solutions to a degenerate complex Monge-Ampère equation. As a consequence, we find that in the space of Kähler potentials any two points close to each other in C 2 C^{2} norm can be connected by a geodesic along which the associated metrics do not degenerate.

Introduction

1.1
Backgroud and Subject

In this paper we will be interested in the solution to degenerate complex Monge-Ampère equations, which play important roles in Kähler geometry.

For a compact Kähler manifold
\mathcal{V}
with a metric
\omega_{0}
, we define the following space of Kähler potentials,

\displaystyle\mathcal{H}=\{\varphi\in C^{\infty}(\mathcal{V})|\omega_{0}+\sqrt{-1}\partial\overline{\partial}\varphi>0\}.

(1.1)

Then a Riemannian metric can be introduced on this infinite dimensional space, for
\psi_{1},\psi_{2}\in T_{\varphi}\mathcal{H}

\displaystyle<\psi_{1},\psi_{2}>_{\varphi}=\int_{\mathcal{V}}\psi_{1}\psi_{2}\left(\omega_{0}+\sqrt{-1}\partial\overline{\partial}\varphi\right)^{n}.

(1.2)

Under this metric (
1.2

\Phi:[0,1]\times\mathcal{V}\rightarrow\mathbb{R}

\Phi(t,\ast)\in\mathcal{H}
for each

\displaystyle\Phi_{tt}=\Phi_{t{\overline{\beta}}}g^{\alpha{\overline{\beta}}}\Phi_{\alpha t},\ \ \ \ \ \

\displaystyle\text{in\ \ \ \ }[0,1]\times\mathcal{V}.

(1.3)

where
g_{\alpha{\overline{\beta}}}=\omega_{0,\alpha{\overline{\beta}}}+\Phi_{\alpha{\overline{\beta}}}
.
If we define
\mathcal{S}=\{\zeta\in\mathbb{C}|0<\text{Re}(\zeta)<1\}

\displaystyle\left(\Omega_{0}+\sqrt{-1}\partial\overline{\partial}\Phi\right)^{n+1}=0,\ \ \ \ \ \

\displaystyle\text{in\ \ \ \ }\mathcal{S}\times\mathcal{V}.

(1.4)

\displaystyle\text{on\ \ }\{\text{Re}(\zeta)=1\}\times\mathcal{V};

\displaystyle\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \Phi=\varphi_{0},
