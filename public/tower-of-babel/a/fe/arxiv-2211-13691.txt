Open Superstring First Mass Level Effective Lagrangian: Massive Spin- 3 / 2 3/2 Fields in an Electromagnetic Background
Karim Benakli, Cassiano A. Daniel, Wenqi Ke

Abstract
We derive fully explicit equations of motion, and the associated set of constraints, describing the propagation in a flat space-time of a charged spin- 3 / 2 3/2 massive state in a constant electromagnetic background. For this purpose, we provide the Lagrangian for the physical fermionic fields in the first massive level of the open superstring. We first write a compact Lagrangian, allowing a simple derivation of the equations of motion and constraints. Then another one is given that yields directly a decoupled system of equations, though the fields of different spins look coupled at the level of the Lagrangian.



I
INTRODUCTION

The description of the propagation of massive spin-3/2 charged states in an electromagnetic background is an old problem dating back to the 1930’s
Dirac 1936
;
Fierz and Pauli 1939
. An important obstacle is the appearance of superluminal propagation which leads to a loss of causality
Velo and Zwanziger 1969
. The difficulty of writing causal equations of motion and a Lagrangian is illustrated, for example, in the work of
Deser et al. 2000
where it has been shown that a large class of modifications of the minimal theory fail to restore causality. However, a solution to the problem is in principle possible. Indeed, in
Porrati and Rahman 2009
, an ansatz for a Lagrangian has been proposed whose coefficients can be obtained recursively, order by order in the field strength
F_{mn}
of the electromagnetic background. In a previous work
Benakli et al. 2021
, we generalised to the charged case the computation in
Berkovits and Leite 1999
;
Berkovits and Leite 1997
of the superspace Lagrangian and the equations of motion of the first massive level of the open superstring. We have thus generalised the work of
Argyres and Nappi 1989
;
Porrati et al. 2011
;
Klishevich 2000
for the bosonic case. Here we will obtain explicit expressions for the Lagrangian of fermionic physical states and their equations of motion.
The details of the derivation of our results will be given elsewhere
Benakli et al. tion
.

The first massive level of open superstrings has on-shell 12 complex fermionic degrees of freedom of mass
M
. Eight of them correspond to massive spin-3/2 fields denoted
(\lambda_{mj},\chi_{mj})
and four to spin-1/2 ones
\psi_{j},\gamma_{j}
, with
j=1,2
. They carry total charges
Q=q_{0}+q_{\pi}
where
q_{0}
and
q_{\pi}
are the two charges at the string end-points. The covariant derivative is

\displaystyle D_{n}=(\partial-iQF\cdot X)_{n}

(1)

where
X^{n}
is a space-time coordinate. The stringy origin of these states manifests itself in the dependence on
F_{mn}
through the matrix
Fradkin and Tseytlin 1985
;
Abouelsaood et al. 1987

\displaystyle\epsilon=\frac{\Lambda^{2}}{\pi}\left[\arctanh\left(\frac{\pi q_{0}F}{\Lambda^{2}}\right)+\arctanh\left(\frac{\pi q_{\pi}F}{\Lambda^{2}}\right)\right]

(2)

where
\Lambda
is the fundamental (string) scale of the theory and the dressed form of the covariant derivative
Abouelsaood et al. 1987
;
Argyres and Nappi 1989

\displaystyle\mathfrak{D}_{m}=-\mathrm{i}\,\mathfrak{M}_{mn}\,D^{n},\qquad\left[\mathfrak{D}_{m},\mathfrak{D}_{n}\right]=\mathrm{i}\epsilon_{mn}

(3)

with the matrix
\mathfrak{M}
satisfying:

\displaystyle\mathfrak{M}\cdot\mathfrak{M}^{T}=\frac{\epsilon}{QF}

(4)

As noted in
Argyres and Nappi 1989
for the case of the bosonic open string, the consistency of the Lagrangian and the derivation of the equations of motion use the antisymmetric property of
\epsilon_{mn}
but nowhere the explicit dependence of
\epsilon_{mn}
on
F_{mn}
. Therefore, our analysis continues to hold if we take everywhere the quantum field theory limit
\epsilon_{mn}\rightarrow QF_{mn}
and
\mathfrak{D}_{m}\rightarrow D_{m}
.

Four-component notation:

Let’s express the spin-1/2 and spin-3/2 in four components, introducing

\Phi_{1}\equiv\begin{pmatrix}\gamma_{1\alpha}\\

IV
CONCLUSIONS

One also expects to have a Lagrangian of the fermionic fields in the first massive level of the open superstring that reduces, in absence of an electromagnetic background, to a sum of the usual Rarita-Schwinger and Dirac Lagrangians. We have indeed obtained such a Lagrangian, but we have chosen not to present it here because it is a long expression that is not very enlightening. It contains three parts: the first one is the sum of Rarita-Schwinger and Dirac Lagrangians, up to replacing the covariant derivatives by partial derivatives, whereas the others represent additional kinetic plus the mass terms, and couplings between spin-
3/2
and spin-
1/2
. The last two pieces disappear with vanishing electromagnetic field. This will be provided in
Benakli et al. tion
.

We have found fully explicit equations of motion and constraints for the massive charged spin-3/2 field in a constant electromagnetic background. It is interesting to note that in the case of
Deser et al. 2000
the
\gamma
-trace constraint is modified. As a result, it disappears for a particular value of the electromagnetic field strength. This signals
the presence of the Velo-Zwanziger problem. Here, (
20
) is the same form as in the case without electromagnetic background and is always present, as it is in the proposal of
Porrati and Rahman 2009
. The Lagrangian as derived by string field theory in
Benakli et al. 2021
involves the presence of spin-1/2 fields to enforce the constraints.
