Generic proving of replica symmetry breaking
Mihailo Stojnic

Abstract




Abstract

We study the replica symmetry breaking (rsb) concepts on a generic level through the prism of recently introduced interpolating/comparison mechanisms for bilinearly indexed (bli) random processes. In particular,
[
93
]
introduced a
fully lifted
(fl) interpolating mechanism and
[
92
]
developed its a
stationarized fully lifted
(sfl) variant. Here, we present a sfl
matching
mechanism that shows that the results obtained in
[
93
,
92
]
completely correspond to the ones obtained by a statistical physics replica tool with the replica symmetry breaking (rsb) form suggested by Parisi in
[
62
,
63
,
64
]
. The results are very generic as they allow to handle pretty much all bilinear models at once. Moreover, given that the results of
[
93
,
92
]
are extendable to many other forms, the concepts presented here automatically extend to any such forms as well.

Index Terms: Random processes; Replica symmetry breaking; Interpolation; Stationarization
.

2.1
Mathematical description of bilinear Hamiltonian based models

As we are focused on a statistical physics approach to studying general bilinear models, the following Hamiltonian is of key interest

{\cal H}(G)={\bf y}^{T}G{\bf x},

(1)

where

Instead of dealing directly with the partition function,

A connection between the replica symmetry breaking (rsb) predictions and random processes comparisons in the bilinear Hamiltonian based models is studied. In
