Abstract
We discuss a class of bow varieties which can be viewed as Taub-NUT deformations of moduli spaces of instantons on noncommutative ℝ 4 {\mathbb{R}}^{4} . Via the generalized Legendre transform, we find the Kähler potential on each of these spaces.

[
16

k

[

The complex manifold

A

On a spectral curve

{\rm Pic}^{0}(S)\to\operatorname{Jac}^{g-1}(S)

As shown in
