Abstract
The balanced hypercube B ​ H n BH_{n} , a variant of the hypercube, is a novel interconnection network for massive parallel systems. It is known that the balanced hypercube remains Hamiltonian after deleting at most 4 ​ n − 5 4n-5 faulty edges if each vertex is incident with at least two edges in the resulting graph for all n ≥ 2 n\geq 2 . In this paper, we show that there exists a fault-free Hamiltonian cycle in B ​ H n BH_{n} for n ≥ 2 n\geq 2 with | F | ≤ 5 ​ n − 7 \left|F\right|\leq 5n-7 if the degree of every vertex in B ​ H n − F BH_{n}-F is at least two and there exists no f 4 f_{4} -cycles in B ​ H n − F BH_{n}-F , which improves some known results. Key words: Interconnection network; Balanced hypercubes; Hamiltonian cycle; Fault-tolerance



1.
Introduction

In parallel and distributed systems, interconnection network plays an important role in their performance and specific functions. The topological structure of an interconnection network is usually modeled by a simple undirected graph, where vertices represent processors and edges represent communication link between processors. The
n
-dimensional hypercube
Q_{n}
is one of the most efficient interconnection network models, which has numerous good properties such as high symmetry, large connectivity and recursive structure. The balanced hypercube,
BH_{n}
, is a variant of the hypercube, which not only retains most of the excellent properties of
Q_{n}
, but also possesses some outstanding features that
Q_{n}
does not have. For instance, the diameter of
BH_{n}
is less than the hypercube with the same number of vertices for odd
n
. Moreover, for each vertex of
BH_{n}
, there is a unique vertex (backup vertex) with the same neighborhood, implying that if a vertex fails then tasks running on it can be easily transferred to its backup vertex without modifying routing. With such good properties,
BH_{n}
has received much attention. Embedding properties, especially Hamiltonian path and cycle embedding of
BH_{n}
have been studied in
[
1
,
2
,
4
,
8
,
21
,
23
,
26
,
12
]
. Reliability indices under different theoretical model of
BH_{n}
have been determined in
[
11
,
22
,
24
]
. Some other topological issues of
BH_{n}
have been investigated in
[
13
,
25
,
19
]
.

A graph
G
is called
Hamiltonian
if there is a Hamiltonian cycle in
G
. A bipartite graph
G=(X\cup Y,E)
is
Hamiltonian laceable
if there is a Hamiltonian path between any two vertices
x
and
y
in different partite sets. When designing and selecting the desired topology for an interconnected network, the Hamiltonian property is an important indicator for evaluating network performance
[
7
]
. Hamiltonian properties of the hypercube and its variants, as well as other famous networks have been widely studied
[
3
,
5
,
6
,
15
,
16
]
.

In practical application, fault is inevitable in a large network, so it is necessary to consider fault-tolerance. A graph
G
is
k
edge fault-tolerance Hamiltonian
if
G-F
remains Hamiltonian for any
F\subset E(G)
with
\left|F\right|\leq k
. And a bipartite graph
G
is
k
edge fault-tolerance Hamiltonian laceable
if
G-F
remains Hamiltonian laceable for any
F\subset E(G)
with
\left|F\right|\leq k
. In
[
26
]
, Zhou et al. proved that
BH_{n}
is
2n-2
edge fault-tolerant Hamiltonian laceable for
n\geq 2
. Recently, Li et al.
[
9
]
further showed that there exists a Hamiltonian cycle in
BH_{n}-F
for
n\geq 2
with
\left|F\right|\leq 4n-5
if the degree of every vertex in
BH_{n}-F
is at least two. They also showed that the upper bound
4n-5
of the number of faulty edges can be tolerated is optimal. Actually, if a set
F
of
4n-4
edges is deleted from
BH_{n}
, then there exists no Hamiltonian cycles as a cycle of length four with a pair of nonadjacent vertices whose degrees are both two may occur in

(see Fig.

-cycle
. Thus, a natural question arises: what is the upper bound of the number of faulty edges can be tolerated if

-cycle is forbidden in

? Motivated by this, Liu and Wang
[
10
]
showed that the hypercube still has a Hamiltonian cycle in

if
\delta(Q_{n}-F)\geq 2
(
|F|\leq 3n-8
) and there exists no

-cycles.

In this paper, we show that there exists a fault-free Hamiltonian cycle in
BH_{n}
for

with
\left|F\right|\leq 5n-7
if the degree of every vertex in
BH_{n}-F
is at least two and there exists no
f_{4}
-cycles in
BH_{n}-F
.
