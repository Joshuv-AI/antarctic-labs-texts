Uniqueness of P ⁡ ( f ) P(f) and [ P ⁡ ( f ) ] ( k ) [P(f)]^{(k)} concerning weakly weighted sharing Thanks: This work was supported by Institutional Post Doctoral Fellowship of Indian Institution of Technology Bhubaneswar.
Molla Basir Ahamed

Abstract
. In this paper, with the help of the idea of weakly weighted sharing introduced by Lin -Lin [Kodai Math. J., 29(2006), 269-280], we study the uniqueness of a polynomial expression P ⁡ ( f ) P(f) and [ P ⁡ ( f ) ] ( k ) [P(f)]^{(k)} of a meromorphic function f f sharing a small function. The main results significantly improved the result of Liu - Gu [Kodai Math. J., 27(3)(2004), 272-279]. This research work explores certain condition under which the polynomial P ⁡ ( f ) P(f) can be reduced to a non-zero monomial, and as a consequence, the specific form of the function f f is obtained. By some constructive examples it has been shown that some conditions in the main results can not be removed and some of the inequalities are sharp. 2010 Mathematics Subject Classification. 30D35. Keywords and phrases. Meromorphic function, derivatives, shared values, small functions, weakly-weighted sahring.

Regarding finding the class of the meromorphic functions which satisfies
