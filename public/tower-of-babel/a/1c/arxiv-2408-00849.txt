Existence of BV solutions for 2 × 2 \displaystyle 2\times 2 hyperbolic balance laws for L ∞ \displaystyle L^{\infty} initial data
Boris Haspot, Animesh Jana

Abstract
We prove the existence of BV solutions for 2 × 2 \displaystyle 2\times 2 system of hyperbolic balance laws in one space dimension. The flux is assumed to have two genuinely nonlinear characteristic fields. We consider a general force which may possibly depend on time and space variable as well. To prove the existence, we assume the initial data to be small in L ∞ \displaystyle L^{\infty} . Furthermore, we also study qualitative behavior for entropy solutions to hyperbolic system of balance laws.
