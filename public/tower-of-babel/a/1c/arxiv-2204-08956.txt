The cross section of the process e + ​ e − → Λ ​ Λ ¯ e^{+}e^{-}\to\Lambda\bar{\Lambda} in the vicinity of charmonium ψ ⁡ ( 3770 ) \psi(3770) including three-gluon and D D -meson loop contributions
Yu.M. Bystritskiy, A. I. Ahmadov

Abstract
The total cross section of the process e + ​ e − → Λ ​ Λ ¯ e^{+}e^{-}\to\Lambda\bar{\Lambda} is calculated within the energy range close to the mass of ψ ⁡ ( 3770 ) \psi(3770) charmonium state. Two different contributions were considered: the D D -meson loop and the three gluon charmonium annihilation one. Both of them contribute noticeably and in sum fairly reproduce the data. Large relative phase for these contributions are generated with respect to the pure electromagnetic mechanism. As a by product the fit for the electromagnetic form factor of Λ \Lambda -hyperon is obtained for the large momentum transferred region.



I
Introduction

The bound state of a pair of charmed quarks are the one of the
most clear and simple system which allows one to study the details of
a confinement mechanism and to refine the conjectures of Quantum Chormodynamics (QCD).
During few recent decades these states are under intensive experimental and theoretical study

Asner:2008nq
;
Kussner:2020yfa
;
Nerling:2021bxo
;
Yuan:2021wpg
.
The special interest is focused to the electron–positron annihilation
processes with the production of different mesonic and baryonic final states
thus giving us the clear way to produce and to study the charmonium in pure
J^{PC}=1^{--}
state.
The binary final states (i.e. with two particles finally produced) give the possibility
to further simplify the consideration of the processes with charmonium in the
intermediate state
DM2:1987riy
.

Besides it was shown a long time ago that these processes are the excellent
way to measure the electromagnetic form factors of the particles produced

Cabibbo:1960zza
;
Cabibbo:1961sz
.
There is enormous set of the measurements of the electromagnetic form factors
of the proton, for example, by the BaBar Collaboration
BaBar:2013ves
;
BaBar:2013ukx

or by the BES III Collaboration
BESIII:2015axk
.
But the other baryons are also under study:

\Lambda^{0}
and
\Sigma^{0}
baryons

;
Dobbs:2017hyd
,

\Sigma^{\pm}
baryons
BESIII:2020uqk
,

\Xi^{0}
,
\Xi^{-}
and
\Omega^{-}
baryons

.
For a recent review of the situation on
\Lambda\bar{\Lambda}

a review

.
A lot of interest paid to the near threshold behaviour of these form factors

BESIII:2017hyw
;
BESIII:2020ktn
which demonstrates non-trivial enhancement effect.
Many theoretical ideas to explain this effect were proposed
(see, for example,
Haidenbauer:2006dm
;

).

In this paper we want to consider the process of electron-positron annihilation
into a pair of
\Lambda
-hyperons which was recently precisely measured

\psi(3770)
charmonium

The charmonium

II
Born approximation

We consider the process of electron-positron annihilation into a pair of
\Lambda
-baryons:

\displaystyle e^{+}(q_{+})+e^{-}(q_{-})\to\Lambda(p_{1})+\bar{\Lambda}(p_{2}),

(1)

where quantities in parenthesis are the 4-momenta of the corresponding particles.
The cross section for the process has the standard way:

\displaystyle d\sigma=\frac{1}{8s}\sum_{\text{spins}}\left|{\cal M}\right|^{2}\,d\Phi_{2},

(2)

where the summation of the amplitude square
\left|{\cal M}\right|^{2}
runs over all possible initial and final particles spin states.
We systematically neglect the mass of the electron
m_{e}
in this paper.
The phase volume of final particles
d\Phi_{2}
has the form:

\displaystyle d\Phi_{2}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\left(2\pi\right)^{2}}\delta\left(q_{+}+q_{-}-p_{1}-p_{2}\right)\frac{d{\bf p_{1}}}{2E_{1}}\frac{d{\bf p_{2}}}{2E_{2}}=

\displaystyle=\frac{\left|{\bf p}\right|}{2^{4}\pi^{2}\sqrt{s}}\,d\Omega_{\Lambda}=\frac{\beta}{2^{4}\pi}\,d\cos\theta_{\Lambda},

(3)

where
d\Omega_{\Lambda}=d\phi_{\Lambda}\,d\cos\theta_{\Lambda}
and

\phi_{\Lambda}
and
\theta_{\Lambda}
are the azimuthal and the polar angles of the final
\Lambda
-baryon momentum
in the center-of-mass reference frame (center of mass system, c.m.s),
i.e.
\theta_{\Lambda}
is the angle between 3-momenta of the initial electron
{\bf q_{-}}
and the final
\Lambda
-baryon

{\bf p_{1}}
(see Fig.
1
)
and
\left|{\bf p}\right|\equiv\left|{\bf p_{1}}\right|=\left|{\bf p_{2}}\right|=\sqrt{s}\beta/2
is
the modulus of 3-momenta of the final
\Lambda
or
\bar{\Lambda}
.
Here the quantity
\beta=\sqrt{1-4M_{\Lambda}^{2}/s}
is the final
\Lambda
-baryon velocity,
with
M_{\Lambda}
being the mass of
\Lambda
-baryon.

\theta_{\Lambda}

) in

In Born approximation (see Fig.
2
) the amplitude
{\cal M}={\cal M}_{B}
in
(
2
) describes the electron–positron pair annihilation into
virtual photon, which then produces
\Lambda\bar{\Lambda}
pair. The amplitude
{\cal M}_{B}
corresponding to this
process has the form:

\displaystyle{\cal M}_{B}=\frac{1}{s}J^{e\bar{e}\to\gamma}_{\mu}(q)\,J_{\gamma\to\Lambda\bar{\Lambda}}^{\mu}(q),

(4)

where
s=q^{2}=\left(q_{+}+q_{-}\right)^{2}=\left(p_{1}+p_{2}\right)^{2}

is the total invariant mass squared of the lepton pair (
q
is the momentum of the
intermediate photon).
The quantities
J^{e\bar{e}\to\gamma}_{\mu}
and
J^{\gamma\to\Lambda\bar{\Lambda}}_{\mu}

are electromagnetic currents:

\displaystyle J^{e\bar{e}\to\gamma}_{\mu}(q)

\displaystyle=-e\left[\bar{v}(q_{+})\gamma_{\mu}u(q_{-})\right],

(5)

\displaystyle J^{\gamma\to\Lambda\bar{\Lambda}}_{\mu}(q)

\displaystyle=e\left[\bar{u}(p_{1})\Gamma_{\mu}(q)v(p_{2})\right],

(6)

where
e
is the modulus of electron charge
e=\sqrt{4\pi\alpha}
with
\alpha
being
the fine structure constant
Zyla:2020zbs
. In general the vertex
of the photon with the
\Lambda
-baryon has the form:

\displaystyle\Gamma_{\mu}(q)

\displaystyle=F_{1}(q^{2})\gamma_{\mu}-\frac{F_{2}(q^{2})}{4M_{\Lambda}}\left(\gamma_{\mu}{\hat{q}}-{\hat{q}}\gamma_{\mu}\right).

(7)

where we use the notation
{\hat{a}}\equiv a_{\mu}\gamma^{\mu}
.
Here the functions
F_{1,2}(q^{2})
are the
\Lambda
-baryon
electromagnetic form factors normalized as
F_{1}(0)=0
and
F_{2}(0)=\mu_{\Lambda}
,
where
\mu_{\Lambda}
is the
\Lambda
-baryon anomalous magnetic moment.

It was shown in
Tomasi-Gustafsson:2020vae
that the non-trivial structure of the baryon starts
to manifest itself even at relatively low
q^{2}
and thus one must take into account
these effects of the structure.
Since experimental data at the moment do not allow one to extract the electric
G_{E}

and the magnetic
G_{M}
form factors of a baryons separately we utilize the
assumption that
\left|G_{E}\right|=\left|G_{M}\right|
, i.e.
F_{2}(q^{2})=0
.
Then the total cross section in Born approximation has the form:

\displaystyle\sigma_{B}(s)=\frac{2\pi\alpha^{2}}{3s}\beta\left(3-\beta^{2}\right)\left|F_{1}\left(s\right)\right|^{2}.

(8)

The form factor
F_{1}
is chosen to have pQCD inspired form
Lepage:1979za
;
Lepage:1980fj
,
which takes into account the running of the QCD coupling constant
\alpha_{s}
:

\displaystyle F_{1}(s)=\frac{C}{s^{2}\log^{2}\left(s/\Lambda_{\mbox{\tiny QCD}}^{2}\right)},

(9)

where
\Lambda_{\mbox{\tiny QCD}}
is the QCD scale and the constant
C
should be fitted on the experimental data
for baryon–antibaryon production in a wide energy range.

For proton in our energy region this fit was done in
Ablikim:2014jrz

giving
C=(62.6\pm 4.1)~\mbox{GeV}^{4}
(using
\Lambda_{\mbox{\tiny QCD}}=300~\mbox{MeV}
).
This fit qualitatively agrees with the more recent result of paper
Bianconi:2015owa
where
this constant was fitted to be equal to
C=72~\mbox{GeV}^{4}
with
\Lambda_{\mbox{\tiny QCD}}=520~\mbox{MeV}
.

).
The curve errors origin from the form factor constant (

In our case of
\Lambda\bar{\Lambda}
pair production we fix this constant
using the whole range of BES-III measurement
BESIII:2021ccp
presented
in Fig.
3
.
Fitting the Born cross section from (
8
) with respect to this
data gives us the following parameter:

\displaystyle C=\left(43.1\pm 1.4\right)~\mbox{GeV}^{4},

(10)

which we use further for
\Lambda
-baryon electromagnetic form factor (
9
).
We note that this expression for the form factor
F_{1}
with
the constant
C
from (
10
) works for relatively large momentum transfered
q^{2}
.
It does not pretend to work near threshold, since there are many delicate
features playing important role there, such as Coulomb-like
enhancement factor
Haidenbauer:2014kja
;
Amoroso:2021slc
or
the manifestation of wavy nature of baryon stabilization after its
emerging from the vacuum
Tomasi-Gustafsson:2020vae
.

III
The quarkonium
\psi(3770)
intermediate state

The main task of our work is to describe the effect of the charmonium resonance
\psi(3770)

excitation in the process (
1
).
As one can see in Fig.
3
the experimental point for the cross section
at
\sqrt{s}=M_{\psi}
(where
M_{\psi}
is the
\psi(3770)
mass)
flies higher the Born cross section curve.
In
BESIII:2021ccp
there is a fit of this point with the use of some
phenomenologically inspired formula (see eq. (3) in
BESIII:2021ccp
).
Further we develop the model (based on our previous calculations

Ahmadov:2013ova
;
Bystritskiy:2021frx
) which reveals the underlying mechanism for this point
to be upstairs.

In the region of charmonium resonance
\psi(3770)
excitation one should take into account
the additional contribution to the amplitude:

\displaystyle{\cal M}={\cal M}_{B}+{\cal M}_{\psi},

(11)

where
{\cal M}_{\psi}
takes into account the mechanism with charmonium
\psi(3770)

in the intermediate state (see Fig.
2
) which is enhanced by the
Breit-Wigner factor:

\displaystyle{\cal M}_{\psi}=\frac{g^{\mu\nu}-q^{\mu}q^{\nu}/M_{\psi}^{2}}{s-M_{\psi}^{2}+iM_{\psi}\,\Gamma_{\psi}}J^{e\bar{e}\to\psi}_{\mu}(q)J^{\psi\to\Lambda\bar{\Lambda}}_{\nu}(q),

(12)

where
\Gamma_{\psi}
is the total decay width of
\psi(3770)
resonance and

J^{e\bar{e}\to\psi}_{\mu}
and
J^{\psi\to\Lambda\bar{\Lambda}}_{\mu}
are the currents which describe the transition
of lepton pair into
\psi(3770)
resonance and the transition of the
\psi(3770)
resonance into

\Lambda\bar{\Lambda}
pair correspondingly.
Following
Ahmadov:2013ova
we assume that
J^{e\bar{e}\to\psi}_{\mu}
has the same structure as

J^{e\bar{e}\to\gamma}_{\mu}
from (
5
), i.e.

\displaystyle J^{e\bar{e}\to\psi}_{\mu}(q)=g_{e}\,\left[\bar{v}(q_{+})\gamma_{\mu}u(q_{-})\right],

(13)

where the constant
g_{e}=F_{1}^{\psi\to e\bar{e}}(M_{\psi}^{2})
is the value of the form factor of the
vertex
\psi\to e\bar{e}
at the
\psi(3770)
mass-shell
(here we follow the same approximation as in the Born case and
assume that
F_{2}^{\psi\to e\bar{e}}(M_{\psi}^{2})=0
).
We fix this constant
g_{e}
via total decay width of
\psi\to e^{+}e^{-}
which is known to be equal to

\Gamma_{\psi\to e^{+}e^{-}}=261~\mbox{eV}

Zyla:2020zbs
:

\displaystyle g_{e}=\sqrt{\frac{12\pi\Gamma_{\psi\to e^{+}e^{-}}}{M_{\psi}}}=1.6\cdot 10^{-3}.

(14)

We neglect a possible imaginary part of the vertex
g_{e}
since it
was shown in
Kuraev:2013swa
that it is small, less then 10 % of the real part.
We also note a mistake in our paper
Bystritskiy:2021frx
where this constant is claimed to be
related with the
\psi\to p\bar{p}
decay.

The amplitude
{\cal M}_{\psi}
from (
12
) interferes with the Born one
{\cal M}_{B}
giving
the standard interference contribution to the cross section:

\displaystyle d\sigma_{int}=\frac{1}{8s}\sum_{\text{spins}}2\,\mbox{Re}\left[{\cal M}_{B}^{+}{\cal M}_{\psi}\right]\,d\Phi_{2},

(15)

which leads to the following form of the interference contribution to
the total cross section:

\displaystyle\sigma_{int}(s)=\mbox{Re}\left(\frac{S_{i}(s)}{s-M_{\psi}^{2}+iM_{\psi}\,\Gamma_{\psi}}\right),

(16)

where
S_{i}(s)
contains all the dynamics of the transformation of charmonium into

\Lambda\bar{\Lambda}
pair and has the following explicit form:

\displaystyle S_{i}(s)=\frac{eg_{e}\beta}{48\pi s}\int\limits_{-1}^{1}d\cos\theta_{\Lambda}\sum_{s^{\prime}}\left(J_{\gamma\to\Lambda\bar{\Lambda}}^{\alpha}\right)^{*}J^{\psi\to\Lambda\bar{\Lambda}}_{\alpha}.

(17)

The subscript index
i
in the expression above denotes the type of mechanism of this transformation.
Since the mass of
\psi(3770)
is higher than the threshold of
D
-meson pair
production it is natural to expect that the
D
-meson loop
will be the main mechanism in this reaction (see Fig.
4
)
and we consider it below in Section
IV
.
However we need also to consider the OZI-violating three gluon mechanism (see Fig.
5
)
which we briefly recall in Section
V
.

Having the interference contribution (
16
) with the total
relative phase between Born amplitude
{\cal M}_{B}
and the charmonium contribution one
{\cal M}_{\psi}

we can restore the total cross section using the procedure
described in
Bystritskiy:2021frx
(see eqs. (15) and (16) there).

IV
D-meson loop mechanism

Figure 4:
D
-meson loop mechanism.

Here we follow exactly to our previous calculations
Ahmadov:2013ova
;
Bystritskiy:2021frx

\Lambda\bar{\Lambda}

The
D
-meson loop mechanism (presented in Fig.
4
)
contribute to the interference of a charmonium state with the Born amplitude (see (
17
)) as:

\displaystyle S_{D}\left(s\right)

\displaystyle=\alpha_{D}\left(s\right)\,Z_{D}\left(s\right),

(18)

where

\displaystyle\alpha_{D}\left(s\right)=\frac{\alpha~g_{e}}{2^{4}\,3\pi^{2}}\beta\,F_{1}(s),

\displaystyle Z_{D}\left(s\right)=\frac{1}{s}\int\frac{dk}{i\pi^{2}}\times

\displaystyle\times\frac{SpD(s,k^{2})}{\left(k^{2}-M_{\Xi}^{2}\right)\left((k-p_{1})^{2}-M_{D}^{2}\right)\left((k+p_{2})^{2}-M_{D}^{2}\right)}\times

\displaystyle\quad\times G_{\psi D\bar{D}}(s,(k+p_{2})^{2},(k-p_{1})^{2})\times

\displaystyle\quad\times G_{\Lambda D\Xi}\left(k^{2},(k-p_{1})^{2}\right)G_{\Lambda D\Xi}\left(k^{2},(k+p_{2})^{2}\right),

(19)

and
SpD(s,k^{2})
is the trace of
\gamma
-matrices over the baryon line:

\displaystyle SpD(s,k^{2})=

\displaystyle=\mbox{Sp}\left[({\hat{p}_{1}}+M_{\Lambda})\gamma_{5}({\hat{k}}+M_{\Xi})\gamma_{5}({\hat{p}_{2}}-M_{\Lambda})({\hat{k}}-M_{\Lambda})\right]=

\displaystyle=2\left(\left(k^{2}\right)^{2}+k^{2}\left(s-2\left(M_{D}^{2}+M_{\Lambda}M_{\Xi}\right)\right)-\right.

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\quad\left.\frac{}{}-sM_{\Lambda}M_{\Xi}+c_{D}\right),

(20)

\displaystyle c_{D}=M_{D}^{4}+2M_{\Lambda}M_{\Xi}M_{D}^{2}+2M_{\Xi}M_{\Lambda}^{3}-M_{\Lambda}^{4}.

(21)

The quantities
G_{\psi D\bar{D}}
and
G_{D\Xi\Lambda}
in (
19
) are
the form factors for the vertexes
\psi\to D\bar{D}
and
D\to\Xi\Lambda
.

The details of the calculation of quantity
Z_{D}\left(s\right)
can be found in
Bystritskiy:2021frx
,
but here we just recall that technically we calculate imaginary part of this quantity
and then restore the real part of it by using the dispersion relation with one subtraction
at
q^{2}=0
. First we need to mention that the subtraction constant here is also vanish since
the
\Lambda
-hyperon (which is the
uds
quarks state) do not have open charm and thus
the vertex
\psi\to\Lambda\bar{\Lambda}
at

Next, cutting the diagram by
D
-meson propagators we get the vertex

\psi\to D\bar{D}
with the only dependence over charmonium
virtuality
q^{2}=s
since
D
-meson legs are on-mass-shell:

\displaystyle G_{\psi D\bar{D}}\left(s,M_{D}^{2},M_{D}^{2}\right)=g_{\psi D\bar{D}}\,\frac{M_{\psi}^{2}}{s}\,\frac{\log\left(M_{\psi}^{2}/\Lambda_{D}^{2}\right)}{\log\left(s/\Lambda_{D}^{2}\right)},

(22)

where scale
\Lambda_{D}
we fix on the characteristic value of the reaction
\Lambda_{D}=2M_{D}

and the constant
g_{\psi D\bar{D}}
is fixed by the
\psi\to D\bar{D}

\displaystyle g_{\psi D\bar{D}}

\displaystyle\equiv G_{\psi D\bar{D}}(M_{\psi}^{2},M_{D}^{2},M_{D}^{2})=

\displaystyle=4\,\sqrt{\frac{3\pi\,\Gamma_{\psi\to D\bar{D}}}{M_{\psi}\,\beta_{D}^{3}}}=18.4,

(23)

where
\beta_{D}=\sqrt{1-4M_{D}^{2}/M_{\psi}^{2}}
is the
D
-meson velocity in this decay.

Next we consider function
G_{\Lambda D\Xi}\left(k^{2},p^{2}\right)
from (
19
).
Again the only dependence left in the imaginary part of
Z_{D}
is
the off-mass-shellness of
\Xi
baryon in
t
-channel since

;

\displaystyle G_{\Lambda DP}(k^{2},M_{D}^{2})=\frac{f_{D}\,g_{DN\Lambda}}{m_{u}+m_{c}},\qquad k^{2}<0,

(24)

where
f_{D}\approx 180~\mbox{MeV}

\displaystyle\frac{g_{DN\Lambda}}{\sqrt{4\pi}}=1.9\pm 0.6.

(25)

V
Three gluon mechanism

Figure 5:
Three gluon mechanism.

The three gluon mechanism first was considered in
Ahmadov:2013ova
and
in
Bystritskiy:2021frx
it was refined and some misprints and minor mistakes were corrected.
So here we just present the final formulae for its contribution to the
interference of a charmonium state with Born amplitude (see (
17
)):

\displaystyle S_{3g}\left(s\right)=\alpha_{3g}\left(s\right)\,Z_{3g}\left(s\right),

(28)

where

\displaystyle\alpha_{3g}\left(s\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{\alpha\,\alpha_{s}^{3}}{2^{3}\,3}g_{e}\,g_{col}\,\phi\,\beta\,F_{1}\left(s\right)\,G_{\psi}(s),

(29)

\displaystyle Z_{3g}\left(s\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{4}{\pi^{5}s}\int\frac{dk_{1}}{k_{1}^{2}}\frac{dk_{2}}{k_{2}^{2}}\frac{dk_{3}}{k_{3}^{2}}\times

\displaystyle\times\frac{Sp3g~\delta\left(q-k_{1}-k_{2}-k_{3}\right)}{(\left(p_{1}-k_{1}\right)^{2}-M_{\Lambda}^{2})(\left(p_{2}-k_{3}\right)^{2}-M_{\Lambda}^{2})},

(30)

where the quantity
g_{col}=(1/4)\left<\Lambda\right|d^{ijk}~T^{i}T^{j}T^{k}\left|\Lambda\right>=15/2
is the color factor
and
Sp3g
is the product of traces over the
\Lambda
-hyperon and the
c
-quark lines:

\displaystyle Sp3g

\displaystyle=\mbox{Sp}\left[\hat{Q}_{\alpha\beta\gamma}({\hat{p}_{c}}+m_{c})\gamma^{\mu}({\hat{p}_{\bar{c}}}-m_{c})\right]\times

\displaystyle\times\mbox{Sp}\left[({\hat{p}_{1}}+M_{\Lambda})\gamma^{\alpha}({\hat{p}_{1}}-{\hat{k}_{1}}+M_{\Lambda})\gamma^{\beta}\right.\times

\displaystyle\qquad\times\left.(-{\hat{p}_{2}}+{\hat{k}_{3}}+M_{\Lambda})\gamma^{\gamma}({\hat{p}_{2}}-M_{\Lambda})\gamma_{\mu}\right],

with

\displaystyle\hat{Q}_{\alpha\beta\gamma}

\displaystyle=\frac{\gamma_{\gamma}(-{\hat{p}_{\bar{c}}}+{\hat{k}_{3}}+m_{c})\gamma_{\beta}({\hat{p}_{c}}-{\hat{k}_{1}}+m_{c})\gamma_{\alpha}}{((p_{\bar{c}}-k_{3})^{2}-m_{c}^{2})((p_{c}-k_{1})^{2}-m_{c}^{2})}+

\displaystyle+\left[\text{gluon permutations}\right],

(31)

where the permutations over gluon vertices are performed in the gray block in Fig.
5
.

The quantity
\phi
in (
29
) is related to the charmonium wave function

\psi\left({\bf r}\right)
as:

\displaystyle\phi=\frac{\left|\psi\left({\bf r}={\bf 0}\right)\right|}{M_{\psi}^{3/2}}=\frac{\alpha_{s}^{3/2}}{3\sqrt{3\pi}},

(32)

where
\alpha_{s}
is the QCD coupling constant. We should note that this three gluon mechanism
is very sensitive to this quantity, since it
depends on its value in a rather high degree (see eqs. (
29
) and (
32
)).
We use the value
\alpha_{s}(M_{c})=0.28
which is expected
by the QCD evolution of
\alpha_{s}
from the
b
-quark scale to the

c
-quark scale, i.e. to
s\sim M_{c}^{2}
.
We note that this value differs from the one for the

J/\psi
charmonium which tends to feel much smaller value of parameter
\alpha_{s}(M_{c})=0.19

Chiang:1993qi

The factor
G_{\psi}(s)
in (

\displaystyle\left|G_{\psi}(s)\right|=\frac{C_{\psi}}{s^{2}\log^{2}\left(s/\Lambda^{2}\right)}.

For the constant

in the case of proton–antiproton

since gluons to not feel the flavour

VII
Conclusion

We considered the process of the electron–positron annihilation into
a
\Lambda\bar{\Lambda}
-pair in the vicinity of charmonium
\psi(3770)
resonance.
Besides the Born mechanism, which is the pure QED, there are two contributions
related with the intermediate charmonium
\psi(3770)
state.
One of them is the
D
-meson loop and the other is the three gluon mechanism.

It was shown that both mechanisms contribute noticeably and give much of the final result.
The total sum of them gives rather good agreement with the experimental point at

\sqrt{s}=M_{\psi}
. It is also important that our curve reproduce
the tendency of the experimental points at left and at right shoulders with respect to
the central point.
It is worth to notice again that we do not use any fitting procedure in this calculation.
The parameters were fixed for the proton-antiproton production channel in the paper
Bystritskiy:2021frx
.

It is extremely desirable to make a precise scan over the energy region around charmonium

\psi(3770)
resonance with the small steps. This could support the conclusion
that in charmonium decay the phase of
\psi\to p\bar{p}
and
\psi\to\Lambda\bar{\Lambda}

vertexes are large (
\phi_{\psi}\sim 200^{\circ}
) and can be precisely measured in this channels.
We showed this large phase generation in a set of papers
Ahmadov:2013ova
;
Kuraev:2013swa
;
Bystritskiy:2021frx
.

In the future we plan to consider another binary final states production processes
induced by the charmonium annihilation. Polarized effects are also can be considered
since the formalism is ready and the data are present
BESIII:2021cvv
.
