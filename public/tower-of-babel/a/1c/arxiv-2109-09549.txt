On Some Properties of K K - type Block Matrices in the context of Complementarity Problem
A. Dutta, A. K. Das

Abstract
In this article we introduce K K -type block matrices which include two new classes of block matrices namely block triangular K K -matrices and hidden block triangular K K -matrices. We show that the solution of linear complementarity problem with K K -type block matrices can be obtained by solving a linear programming problem. We show that block triangular K K -matrices satisfy least element property. We prove that hidden block triangular K K -matrices are Q 0 Q_{0} and processable by Lemke’s algorithm. The purpose of this article is to study properties of K K -type block matrices in the context of the solution of linear complementarity problem. Keywords: Z Z -matrix, Hidden Z Z -matrix, linear programming problem, linear complementary problem, semi-sublattice, P P -matrix, Q 0 Q_{0} -matrix. AMS subject classifications: 90C33, 90C51, 15A39, 15B99.

Theorem 3.1
.

Let

-matrix. Then LCP

is processable by Lemke’s algorithm.

Proof.

Let

4
Conclusion

In this article, we introduce the class of block triangular
K
-matrix and the class of hidden block triangular
K
-matrix in the context of solution of linear complementarity problem. We call these two classes jointly as
K
-type block matricces. We show that the linear complementarity problem with
K
-type block matrix is solvable by linear program. The linear complementarity problem with block triangular
K
-matrix is also processable by Lemke’s algorithm as well as criss-cross method. We show that the hidden block triangular
K
-matrix is a
Q_{0}
-matrix.
