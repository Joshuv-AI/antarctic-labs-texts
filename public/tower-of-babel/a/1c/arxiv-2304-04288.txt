Distance matrix of enhanced power graphs of finite groups


Abstract
The enhanced power graph of a group G G is the graph 𝒢 E ​ ( G ) \mathcal{G}_{E}(G) with vertex set G G and edge set { ( u , v ) : u , v ∈ ⟨ w ⟩ , for some w ∈ G } \{(u,v):u,v\in\langle w\rangle,~\mbox{for some}~w\in G\} . In this paper, we compute the spectrum of the distance matrix of the enhanced power graph of non-abelian groups of order p ​ q pq , dihedral groups, dicyclic groups, elementary abelian groups El ⁡ ( p n ) \mathrm{El}(p^{n}) and the non-cyclic abelian groups El ⁡ ( p n ) × El ⁡ ( q m ) \mathrm{El}(p^{n})\times\mathrm{El}(q^{m}) and El ⁡ ( p n ) × ℤ m \mathrm{El}(p^{n})\times\mathbb{Z}_{m} , where p p and q q are distinct primes. For the non-cyclic abelian group El ⁡ ( p n ) × El ⁡ ( q m ) \mathrm{El}(p^{n})\times\mathrm{El}(q^{m}) , we also compute the spectrum of the adjacency matrix of its enhanced power graph and the spectrum of the adjacency and the distance matrix of its power graph.

Introduction

The study of graphs associated with various algebraic structures has been a topic of increasing interest during the last couple of decades. Cayley graphs are one of the well-known classes of graphs arising from algebraic structures. Many different types of graphs, such as power graph
[
1
,
2
]
and enhanced power graph
[
3
]
of a group has been introduced to explore the properties of algebraic structures using graph theory. It is important to notice that these graphs represent some combinatorial properties of the underlying group. Research on combinatorial properties of groups and semigroups originates from Bernhard Neumann’s theorem
[
4
]
answering a question of Paul Erdós. In
[
5
]
, a brief theory of applications of combinatorial properties of groups is given. We are interested in studying power graphs and enhanced power graphs as they represent some combinatorial properties of groups. Also, these graphs help us to
characterize the algebraic structures with isomorphic graphs and realize
the interdependence between the algebraic structures and the corresponding graphs.
The concept of power graph was introduced in the context of semigroup theory by Kelarev and Quin
[
2
]
.

Definition 1
.

The
power graph

\mathcal{P}(G)
of a group
G
is a simple graph with vertex set
G
and two vertices
a
and
b
are adjacent if and only if one of them is some power of the other, i.e., either
b^{k}=a
or
a^{k}=b
, for some
k\in\mathbb{N}
.

Another closely related graph in this context is the enhanced power graph of a group which is introduced by Alipour et al.

[
3
]
.

Definition 2
.

The
enhanced power graph

\mathcal{G}_{E}(G)
of a group
G
is the graph with vertex set
G,
and two vertices
a
and
b
are adjacent if and only if

a,b\in\langle c\rangle
, for some
c\in G
.

Various properties of the enhanced power graph of finite groups have been studied in detail. Aalipour et al.
[
3
]

characterized finite groups for which the power and the enhanced power graphs are equal.
Besides, Zahirovic et al.
[
6
]
proved that finite groups with isomorphic enhanced power graphs have isomorphic directed power graphs.
Bera et al.
[
7
]
studied the completeness, dominatability, and many other interesting properties of the enhanced power graph.
The metric dimension
of enhanced power graphs of finite groups was studied by Ma and She
[
8
]
.

In the last decade, many researchers have studied matrices related to the power and enhanced power graph of finite groups. Chattopadhyay and Panigrahi
[
9
]
studied the Laplacian spectra of power
graphs of finite cyclic groups and the dihedral groups. Mehranian et al.
[
10
]
computed the adjacency spectrum of the power graph of cyclic groups, dihedral groups, elementary abelian groups of prime power order and the Mathieu group.
Hamzeh and Ashrafi
[
11
]
investigated adjacency and Laplacian spectra of power
graphs of the cyclic, dihedral and quaternion groups.

Wani and Shrivastav
[
12
]
studied the spectrum of the distance matrix of power graphs of cyclic groups, dihedral groups of order
2p^{n}
, dicyclic groups of order
4p^{n}
, and groups of order
pq
, where

p
and
q
are distinct primes.
However, they have not described the full spectrum explicitly for some of these graphs. In this paper, we give the full spectrum of the distance matrix of the power graph of these groups.
The main aim of this paper is to study the distance spectra of the enhanced power graphs of finite groups. In Section
2
, we develop some backgrounds and state some earlier known results, which are crucial for the forthcoming sections. In Section
3
, we study the distance spectra of enhanced power graphs of groups of order
pq
, dihedral groups, and dicyclic groups. We conclude the paper by considering certain classes of finite abelian groups viz.

\mathrm{El}(p^{n})\times\mathrm{El}(q^{m})
and

\mathrm{El}(p^{n})\times\mathbb{Z}_{m}
in Section
4
and
5
.

definitions and notations.

For a vertex

if there exists a path between for any pair of vertices

and

The distance between two vertices

3.

All vectors are considered to be column vectors unless stated otherwise. We use

4.

A partition of a set

X
such that every element of

is in exactly one of these subsets.

5.

Suppose

is a real symmetric matrix whose rows and columns are indexed by

. Let
\{X_{1},X_{2},\dots,X_{m}\}
be a partition of

.
Let

[
13

If the row sum of each block

6.

Suppose
\Gamma

If a partition

T=(t_{ij})
. Throughout this paper, we use the above discussion as the definition of an equitable partition of a graph.

7.

For a simple graph
\Gamma
with vertex set
V=\{1,2,\dots,n\}
, the matrix
D(\Gamma):=(d_{ij})

\Gamma
, where

d_{ij}:=\begin{cases}0&~\mbox{if}~i=j,\\
d(i,j)&~\mbox{otherwise}.\end{cases}

Suppose
\text{diam}(\Gamma)\leq 2

denotes the constant row sum of

t^{D}_{ij}:=\begin{cases}2|V_{i}|-2-|N(u)\cap V_{i}|&~\mbox{if}~i=j\\
2|V_{j}|-|N(u)\cap V_{j}|&~\mbox{otherwise.}\end{cases}

(1)

In this paper, the matrix
T^{D}:=(t^{D}_{ij})
is called the
distance quotient matrix
of
\Gamma
.

8.

For a matrix

, the characteristic polynomial is denoted by

. We denote the characteristic polynomial of the adjacency and distance matrix of a graph

9.

For a graph

Note that the graph

3
Groups of order
pq
, Dihedral groups, and Dicyclic groups

In this section, we study the distance spectra of enhanced power graphs of groups of order
pq
, dihedral groups, and dicyclic groups.
We start with the non-abelian groups of order
pq
, where
p
and
q
are distinct primes. This is because the enhanced power graph of any abelian group of order
pq
is complete.

Theorem 3
.

Let
p,q
be primes with
p<q
and
G_{pq}
denote the unique non-abelian group of order
pq
, when it exists. Then, the characteristic polynomial of the distance matrix of
\mathcal{G}_{E}(G_{pq})
is

\displaystyle\phi_{D}(\mathcal{G}_{E}(G_{pq}),x)

\displaystyle=(x+1)^{pq-q-2}(x+p)^{q-1}\bigg[x^{3}+x^{2}(-2pq+p+q+2)+

\displaystyle x(-2pq^{2}-2pq+2q^{2}+2p+1)-(pq^{2}+pq-p-q^{2})\bigg].

Proof.

By Sylow Theorem, there is a unique
q
-Sylow subgroup of
G_{pq}
, say
H_{1}
.
As
G_{pq}
is non-cyclic, the number of p-Sylow subgroups
of
G_{pq}
is
q
. Suppose
H_{2},\dotsc,H_{q+1}
are the p-Sylow subgroups
of
G_{pq}
. It is easy to see that
H_{j}

2\leq j\leq q+1
have a trivial intersection and hence no two non-identity elements of distinct
H_{j}

\mathcal{G}_{E}(G_{pq})
. Moreover, since
G_{pq}
is non-cyclic, no non-identity element of
H_{1}
is adjacent to a non-identity element of
H_{j}
in
\mathcal{G}_{E}(G_{pq})
, for all
2\leq j\leq q+1
. Finally, since each
H_{j}
,
1\leq j\leq q+1
induces a complete subgraph in
\mathcal{G}_{E}(G_{pq})
, we have

\mathcal{G}_{E}(G_{pq})\cong K_{1,q+1}[K_{1},K_{q-1},\underbrace{K_{p-1},\dotsc,K_{p-1}}_{q}].

(2)

Suppose
T^{D}
is the distance quotient matrix of
\mathcal{G}_{E}(G_{pq})
corresponding to the equitable partition given in (
2
). Then

T^{D}=\left[\begin{array}[]{cccc}0&q-1&(p-1)\mathbf{1}_{q}^{\prime}\\
1&q-2&(2p-2)\mathbf{1}_{q}^{\prime}\\
\mathbf{1}_{q}&(2q-2)\mathbf{1}_{q}&2(p-1)J_{q}-pI_{q}\end{array}\right].

Using Theorem
2
, we get

\displaystyle\phi_{D}(\mathcal{G}_{E}(G_{pq}),x)

\displaystyle=\phi(T^{D},x)\frac{\phi_{D}(K_{q-1},x)}{(x-(q-2))}\prod_{i=1}^{q}\frac{\phi_{D}(K_{p-1},x)}{(x-(p-2))}

(3)

\displaystyle=\phi(T^{D},x)(x+1)^{q-2}(x+1)^{(p-2)q}

\displaystyle=\phi(T^{D},x)(x+1)^{pq-q-2}.

We now determine the characteristic polynomial of
T^{D}
. We know that

\displaystyle\phi(T^{D},x)

\displaystyle={\rm det}(xI_{q+2}-T^{D})

(4)

\displaystyle=\left|\begin{array}[]{cccc}x&1-q&(1-p)\mathbf{1}_{q}^{\prime}\\
-1&x-(q-2)&(2-2p)\mathbf{1}_{q}^{\prime}\\
-\mathbf{1}_{q}&(2-2q)\mathbf{1}_{q}&(x+p)I_{q}-2(p-1)J_{q}\end{array}\right|.

Using elementary row and column operations, we have

\displaystyle\phi(T^{D},x)

\displaystyle=\left|\begin{array}[]{cccc}x&1-q&(1-p)\mathbf{1}_{q}^{\prime}\\
-1&x-(q-2)&(2-2p)\mathbf{1}_{q}^{\prime}\\
0&-(x+q)\mathbf{1}_{q}&(x+p)I_{q}\end{array}\right|

(5)

\displaystyle=x\left|\begin{array}[]{cc}x-q+2&(2-2p)\mathbf{1}_{q}^{\prime}\\
-(x+q)\mathbf{1}_{q}&(x+p)I_{q}\end{array}\right|+\left|\begin{array}[]{cc}1-q&(1-p)\mathbf{1}_{q}^{\prime}\\
-(x+q)\mathbf{1}_{q}&(x+p)I_{q}\end{array}\right|

\displaystyle=x(x-q+2)(x+p)^{q}+xq(2-2p)(x+q)(x+p)^{q-1}+(1-q)(x+p)^{q}

\displaystyle+q(x+q)(1-p)(x+p)^{q-1}

\displaystyle=(x+p)^{q-1}[x^{3}+x^{2}(-2pq+p+q+2)+x(-2pq^{2}-2pq+2q^{2}+2p+1)-

\displaystyle(pq^{2}+pq-p-q^{2})].

From (
3
) and (
5
), the proof is complete.
∎

Corollary 1
.

Let
p,q
be primes with
p<q
and
G_{pq}
denote the unique non-abelian group of order
pq
, when it exists. The distance matrix
D
of
\mathcal{G}_{E}(G_{pq})
is non-singular.

Proof.

As
q>1
, we have
q^{2}+q-1>q^{2}
and hence
p(q^{2}+q-1)>q^{2}.
Therefore, setting
x=0
in Theorem
3
, we see that

{\rm det}(D)=p^{q-1}[p(q^{2}+q-1)-q^{2}],

which is clearly non-zero. The proof is complete.
∎

By
[
3

the power graph and the enhanced power graph for a finite group

G
has prime power order. Thus, for a non-cyclic group
G

pq

\mathcal{P}(G_{pq})
and
\mathcal{G}_{E}(G_{pq})

Next, we compute the distance spectra of the enhanced power graph of the dihedral group
D_{2n}
. For
n\geq 3
, the

D_{2n}
of order

is defined

D_{2n}=\langle a,b:a^{n}=b^{2}=e,ab=ba^{-1}\rangle.

Theorem 4
.

For any positive integer

is

\phi_{D}(\mathcal{G}_{E}(D_{2n}),x)=(x+2)^{n-1}(x+1)^{n-2}(x^{3}-(3n-4)x^{2}-(2n^{2}+4n-5)x-n^{2}-2n+2).

Proof.

It is easy to verify that

V(\mathcal{G}_{E}(D_{2n}))=\langle e\rangle\cup\langle a\rangle^{*}\cup\langle ab\rangle^{*}\cup\dotsc\cup\langle a^{n-1}b\rangle^{*}

is an equitable partition for the vertex set of
\mathcal{G}_{E}(D_{2n})
. This yields,

\mathcal{G}_{E}(D_{2n})\cong K_{1,n+1}[K_{1},K_{n-1},\underbrace{K_{1},\dotsc,K_{1}}_{n}].

By Theorem
2
, we get

\phi_{D}(\mathcal{G}_{E}(D_{2n}),x)=\phi(T^{D},x)(x+1)^{2n-n-2},

where

T^{D}=\left[\begin{array}[]{cccc}0&n-1&\mathbf{1}^{\prime}_{n}\\
1&n-2&\mathbf{1}^{\prime}_{n}\\
\mathbf{1}_{n}&(2n-2)\mathbf{1}_{n}&2J_{n}-2I_{n}\end{array}\right].

Proceeding similarly as in the proof of Theorem
3

\phi(T^{D},x)=(x+2)^{n-1}\big(x^{3}-(3n-4)x^{2}-(2n^{2}+4n-5)x-n^{2}-2n+2\big).

This completes the proof.
∎

1-p&(1-p)(q-1)-1\end{array}\right]
and
\left[\begin{array}[]{cc}(p-1)(1-q)-1&1-q\\
(p^{n}-1)(1-q)&-q\end{array}\right]
, respectively.

5
The finite abelian group
\mathrm{El}(p^{n})\times\mathbb{Z}_{m}

We conclude the paper by determining the distance spectra of the enhanced power graph of finite abelian group
\mathrm{El}(p^{n})\times\mathbb{Z}_{m}
, where
n\geq 2
and
\text{gcd}(p,m)=1
.

Theorem 11
.

Let
G=\mathrm{El}(p^{n})\times\mathbb{Z}_{m}
, where
m
is a positive integer such that
\text{gcd}(m,p)=1
. Suppose
\alpha=\frac{p^{n}-1}{p-1}
, then

\phi_{D}(\mathcal{G}_{E}(G),x)
is

(x+mp-m+1)^{\alpha-1}(x+1)^{(mp-m-1)\alpha+m-1}(x^{2}+(mp+2-2mp^{n})x+m^{2}(p^{n}-p)-2mp^{n}+mp+1).

Proof.

By
[
19
, Corollary 4.2]
, the dominating vertices of the graph

\mathcal{G}_{E}(G)
are precisely the vertices of the form
(e,a)
, where
e
is the identity of
\mathrm{El}(p^{n})
and
a\in\mathbb{Z}_{m}.

From Theorem 3.2 of
[
20
]
, we know that the number of connected components of
\mathcal{G}_{E}(\mathrm{El}(p^{n})\setminus\{e\})
is
\frac{p^{n}-1}{p-1}
. Let
C_{1},\dots,C_{\alpha}
be the components of
\mathcal{G}_{E}(\mathrm{El}(p^{n})\setminus\{e\})
. Since
\text{gcd}(p,m)=1
, the sets

V_{1}:=\{(e,a):a\in\mathbb{Z}_{m}\},~\mbox{and}~V_{i}:=\{(b,a):b\in C_{i-1},a\in\mathbb{Z}_{m}\},~\mbox{for}~2\leq i\leq\alpha+1,

form an equitable partition for
\mathcal{G}_{E}(G)
. It is clear that for each
2\leq i\leq\alpha+1
,
V_{i}
is a complete graph on
m(p-1)
vertices.
Thus,

\mathcal{G}_{E}(G)\cong K_{1,\alpha}[K_{m},\underbrace{K_{m(p-1)},\dotsc,K_{m(p-1)}}_{\alpha}].

(16)

Suppose
T^{D}
is the distance quotient matrix of
\mathcal{G}_{E}(G)
corresponding to the equitable partition given in (
16
). Then

T^{D}=\left[\begin{array}[]{cccc}m-1&m(p-1)\mathbf{1}_{\alpha}^{\prime}\\
m\mathbf{1}_{\alpha}&2m(p-1)J_{\alpha}-(mp-m+1)I_{\alpha}\end{array}\right].

By Theorem
2
, we have

\displaystyle\phi_{D}(\mathcal{G}_{E}(G),x)

\displaystyle=\phi(T^{D},x)(x+1)^{(mp-m-1)\alpha+(m-1)}.

We now compute the characteristic polynomial of
T^{D}
. Since
p
is a prime and
n\geq 2
,
2mp^{n}-mp-m-1
is not an eigenvalue of
T^{D}
. Thus,

\displaystyle\phi(T^{D},x)

\displaystyle={\rm det}(xI_{\alpha+1}-T^{D})

\displaystyle=\left|\begin{array}[]{cccc}x-m+1&-(p-1)m\mathbf{1}_{\alpha}^{\prime}\\
-m\mathbf{1}_{\alpha}&(x+mp-m+1)I_{\alpha}-2m(p-1)J_{\alpha}\end{array}\right|

\displaystyle=\frac{m}{x-(2mp^{n}-mp-m-1)}{\rm det}(T^{\prime}),

where

T^{\prime}=\left[\begin{array}[]{cccc}\displaystyle\frac{(x-m+1)(x-2mp^{n}+mp+m+1)}{m}&-m(p-1)\mathbf{1}_{\alpha}^{\prime}\\
-(x-2mp^{n}+mp+m+1)\mathbf{1}_{\alpha}&(x+mp-m+1)I_{\alpha}-2m(p-1)J_{\alpha}\end{array}\right].

Replacing the first column of
T^{\prime}
by the sum of all the columns, the determinant of
T^{\prime}
is equal to

\displaystyle\left|\begin{array}[]{cccc}\displaystyle\frac{(x-m+1)(x-2mp^{n}+mp+m+1)}{m}-m(p^{n}-1)&-m(p-1)\mathbf{1}_{\alpha}^{\prime}\\
\mathbf{0}&\hskip-8.53581pt(x+mp-m-1)I_{\alpha}-2m(p-1)J_{\alpha}\end{array}\right|.

Hence,

\phi(T^{D},x)=(x^{2}+(mp+2-2mp^{n})x+m^{2}(p^{n}-p)-2mp^{n}+mp+1)(x+mp-m+1)^{\alpha-1}.

The proof is complete.
∎

Setting
m=1
in Theorem
11
, we immediately have the distance spectra of the enhanced power graph of elementary abelian groups. This is stated below.

Theorem 12
.

Suppose
\alpha=\frac{p^{n}-1}{p-1}
. Then

\phi_{D}(\mathcal{G}_{E}(\mathrm{El}(p^{n})),x)=(x+p)^{\alpha-1}(x+1)^{(p-2)\alpha}(x^{2}-(2p^{n}-p-2)x-(p^{n}-1)).

In particular, the eigenvalues of
\mathcal{P}(\mathrm{El}(p^{n}))
are
-1
with multiplicity
(n-2)\alpha
,
-p
with multiplicity
\alpha-1
and two simple eigenvalues
\lambda_{1,2}=\frac{(2p^{n}-p-2)\pm\sqrt{(2p^{n}-p-2)^{2}+4(p^{n}-1)}}{2}.
