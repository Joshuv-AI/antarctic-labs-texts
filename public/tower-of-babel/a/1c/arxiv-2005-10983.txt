Rotating black hole with a probe string in Horndeski Gravity
F. F. Santos 1

Abstract
In this work, we present the effect of a probe string on the complexity of a black hole according to the CA (Complexity equals action) conjecture on Horndeski’s gravity. In our system, we consider a particle moving on the boundary of black hole spacetime in ( 2 + 1 2+1 )-dimensions. To obtain a dual description, we need to insertion a fundamental string on the bulk spacetime. The effect of this string is given by the Nambu-Goto term. For the Nambu-Goto term, we can analyze the time development of this system, which is affected by the parameters of Horndeski’s gravity. In our case, we show some interesting complexity properties for this gravity.

Black hole solutions and probe string

Let us consider for Horndeski’s gravity that the string is moving in the spacetime of the BTZ black hole

\displaystyle S_{NG}=-T_{s}\int{d\sigma^{2}\sqrt{-detg_{ind}}},

(19)

where
T_{s}
is the fundamental string tension and the horizon is determined by
f(r)=0

Besides, we can calculate the time derivative of the NG action, which is obtained by integrating the square root of the determinant of the induced metric, and write that

\displaystyle\frac{dS_{NG}}{dt}=T_{s}\int^{r_{+}}_{r_{-}}{d\sigma\sqrt{\xi^{{}^{\prime}2}(\sigma)(v\sigma^{2}-J)^{2}}},

\displaystyle\mathcal{L}=T_{s}\xi^{{}^{\prime}}(\sigma)(v\sigma^{2}-J),

\displaystyle\frac{d}{d\sigma}\frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial\xi^{{}^{\prime}}(\sigma)}-\frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial\xi(\sigma)}=0

V
Conclusion

In this work, we show the effects of the probe string on a BTZ black hole, a solution of Horndeski’s theory without any restrictions on the parameters. We have seen that although the string is stationary, it can provide maximum growth in complexity, which is obtained for
c_{\xi}<0
along with the parameters of Horndeski’s theory, see figure
1
. However, for
c_{\xi}>0
as shown in figure
2
the growth of the action with the black hole mass, which has its behavior influenced by the parameters of Horndeski’s theory where the complexity increases with the black hole mass giving us an idea of how complex the physical system. As we showed in the calculation of NG’s action on the remaining flame, the peak is located at
v=0
, where this behavior derives from the expansion of time. We can perceive a decrease in complexity, as shown in figure
1
, with the growth of the action with the the black hole mass, which is a monotonically decreasing function of the black hole mass. One might think that this is because complexity defines how complex the physical system is. So, if complexity decreases, we have less information from the physical system. So, this is a bigger system and has bigger information.
