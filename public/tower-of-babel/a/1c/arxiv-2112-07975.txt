Solving Linear Tensor Equations II: Including Parity Odd Terms in 4 4 -dimensions
Damianos Iosifidis

Abstract
In this letter, focusing in 4 4 -dimensions, we extend our previous results of solving linear tensor equations. In particular, we consider a 30 30 parameter linear tensor equation for the unknown tensor components N α ​ μ ​ ν N_{\alpha\mu\nu} in terms of the known (sources) components B α ​ μ ​ ν B_{\alpha\mu\nu} . Our extension now consists in including also the parity even linear terms in N α ​ μ ​ ν N_{\alpha\mu\nu} (and the associated traces) formed by contracting the latter with the 4 4 -dimensional Levi-Civita pseudo-tensor. Assuming a generic non-degeneracy condition and following a step by step procedure we show how one can solve explicitly for the unknown tensor field components N α ​ μ ​ ν N_{\alpha\mu\nu} and consequently derive its unique and exact solution in terms of the components B α ​ μ ​ ν B_{\alpha\mu\nu} .
