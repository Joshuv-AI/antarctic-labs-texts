A note on cycle lengths in graphs of chromatic number five and six
Qingyi Huo

Abstract
In this note, we prove that every non-complete ( k + 1 ) (k+1) -critical graph contains cycles of all lengths modulo k k , where k = 4 , 5 k=4,5 . Together with a result in [ 7 ] , this completely gives an affirmative answer to the question of Moore and West on graphs of given chromatic number.
