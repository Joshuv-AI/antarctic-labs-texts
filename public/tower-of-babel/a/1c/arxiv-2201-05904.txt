Darboux Wronskian solutions of Ito typed coupled KdV equation with exact solitonic solutions and conserved densities Journal: Nuclear Physics B
Irfan Mahmood, Hira Sohail, and Allah Ditta

Abstract
In this article, we derive the Darboux solutions of Ito type coupled KdV equation in Darboux framework which is associated with Hirota Satsuma systems. Then we generalise N N -fold Darboux transformations in terms of Wronskians. We also derive the exact multi-solitonic solutions for the coupled field variables of that system in the background of zero seed solutions. The last section encloses the derivation of continuity equation with several conserved densities through its Riccati equation.

\displaystyle\partial_{x}(f_{0},f_{1})^{T}=A(f_{0},f_{1})^{T},

\lambda-u-\beta q&0\end{bmatrix}

Now combining equation (

4\lambda^{2}-2\lambda u-v^{2}-2u^{2}-\frac{uv^{2}}{2\lambda}-u_{xx}&u_{x}\end{bmatrix},

(8)

This section is devoted to construct the equation of continuity which is associated with CNW equation (

For this purpose, let define a quantity

Now take the time derivative of

(57)

and the space derivative of equation(
56
