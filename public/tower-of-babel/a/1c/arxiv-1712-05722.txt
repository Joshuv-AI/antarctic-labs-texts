Radiative Seesaw Model and DAMPE Excess from Leptophilic Gauge Symmetry
Zhi-Long Han 1, Weijian Wang 2, Ran Ding 3

Abstract
In the light of the e + + e − e^{+}+e^{-} excess observed by DAMPE experiment, we propose an anomaly-free radiative seesaw model with an alternative leptophilic U ​ ( 1 ) X U(1)_{X} gauge symmetry. In the model, only right-handed leptons are charged under U ​ ( 1 ) X U(1)_{X} symmetry. The tiny Dirac neutrino masses are generated at one-loop level and charged leptons acquire masses though the type-I seesaw-like mechanism with heavy intermediate fermions. In order to cancel the anomaly, irrational U ​ ( 1 ) X U(1)_{X} charge numbers are assigned to some new particles. After the spontaneous breaking of U ​ ( 1 ) X U(1)_{X} symmetry, the dark Z 2 Z_{2} symmetry could appear as a residual symmetry such that the stability of inert particles with irrational charge numbers are guaranteed, naturally leading to stable DM candidates. We show that the Dirac fermion DM contained in the model can explain the DAMPE excess. Meanwhile, experimental constraints from DM relic density, direct detection, LEP and anomalous magnetic moments are satisfied.

pp\to\ell^{+}\ell^{-}Z^{\prime}\to\ell^{+}\ell^{-}\ell^{+}\ell^{-}
is promising at LHC
Bell:2014tta
. As shown in next section, the DAMPE excess favors heavy
Z^{\prime}\lesssim 3\penalty\ {\rm TeV}
. In this case, the
Z^{\prime}
can hardly be detected at LHC, but are within the reach of the
3\penalty\ {\rm TeV}
CLIC in the
e^{+}e^{-}\to Z^{\prime}\to\mu^{+}\mu^{-}
channel
Kara:2011xw
.

II.6
LHC Signature

In this subsection, we qualitatively discuss possible signatures of new particles at LHC. Since decays of
\eta_{1}
scalars and
\Psi
depend on their masses, the resulting signatures would be different. Considering the mass spectrum
m_{\Psi}<m_{\eta_{1}}
, the decay mode of
\eta_{1}
scalars are
\eta_{1}^{0}\to\ell^{-}\Psi^{+}
,
\eta^{\pm}\to\nu\Psi^{\pm}
, and decay modes of
\Psi
are

\Psi^{\pm}\to\ell^{\pm}Z,\ell^{\pm}h,\nu W^{\pm}
. The promising signature would be
pp\to\Psi^{+}\Psi^{-}\to\ell^{+}\ell^{-}ZZ
, leading to same signature as charged fermion in type-III seesaw
Li:2009mw
. In the opposite case
m_{\Psi}>m_{\eta_{1}}
, the decay mode of
\eta_{1}
scalars are
\eta_{1}^{0}\to\ell^{+}\ell^{-}
,
\eta_{1}^{\pm}\to\ell^{\pm}\nu
, and new decay modes of exotic charged fermion
\Psi^{\pm}\to\ell^{\pm}\eta_{1}^{0},\nu\eta_{1}^{\pm}
are also possible. Note that
\eta_{1}
scalars are responsible for charged fermion mass, hence
\eta_{1}^{0}\to\tau^{+}\tau^{-}
and
\eta_{1}^{\pm}\to\tau^{\pm}\nu
are the dominant decay mode. The promising signature would be
pp\to\eta_{1}^{0}\eta_{1}^{0*}\to\tau^{+}\tau^{-}\tau^{+}\tau^{-}
,
pp\to\eta_{1}^{\pm}\eta_{1}^{0}\to\tau^{\pm}\nu\tau^{+}\tau^{-}
, similar as the lepton-specific 2HDM
Abe:2015oca
.

For the mass of scalar singlet
m_{H}\sim 500\penalty\ {\rm GeV}
with not too small mixing angle
\alpha\sim 0.1
, the promising signature would be
gg\to H\to W^{+}W^{-},ZZ,hh
at LHC
Robens:2016xkb
. Provided
m_{\Psi}<m_{H,\eta_{1}}
, the new decay channel
H\to\ell^{\pm}\Psi^{\mp}
is also allowed. Then, the new signature
gg\to H\to\ell^{\pm}\Psi^{\mp}
with
\Psi^{\pm}
further decaying into
\ell^{\pm}Z,\nu W^{\pm}
is a good way to probe the corresponding Yukawa coupling
y_{2}\bar{E}_{R}\Psi_{L}\sigma
introduced in this model.

As for the inert scalars, the most promising signature in principle would be
pp\to\eta_{2}^{+}\eta_{2}^{-}\to\ell^{+}F_{1}+\ell^{-}\bar{F}_{1}
, i.e.,
\ell^{+}\ell^{-}+\cancel{E}_{T}
, for fermion DM at LHC
Ding:2016wbd
. But actually, this dilepton signature is suppressed dramatically by heavy mass of the inert charge scalar
m_{\eta_{2}^{\pm}}\sim 10\penalty\ {\rm TeV}
in our consideration
Guella:2016dwo
, thus it is hard to probe at LHC. Similarly, the mono-
j
signature
pp\to\eta_{2}^{0}\eta_{2}^{0*}j\to\nu\bar{\nu}F_{1}\bar{F}_{1}j
, i.e.,
j+\cancel{E}_{T}
, is also challenging at LHC.

III
DAMPE Dark Matter

Motivated by recent DAMPE excess around
1.5\penalty\ {\rm TeV}
, we focus on DM phenomenon in this section. Here, we consider the lightest Dirac fermion
F_{1}
as DM candidate. The relevant interactions mediated by the new gauge boson
Z^{\prime}
for DM and leptons are

\mathcal{L}_{Z^{\prime}}\supset g^{\prime}Z^{\prime}_{\mu}\big(Q_{E_{R}}\bar{E}_{R}\gamma^{\mu}E_{R}+Q_{\nu_{R}}\bar{\nu}_{R}\gamma^{\mu}\nu_{R}+Q_{F_{L}}\bar{F}_{L1}\gamma^{\mu}F_{L1}+Q_{F_{R}}\bar{F}_{R1}\gamma^{\mu}F_{R1}\big),

(24)

with mass of gauge boson
Z^{\prime}
given by
m_{Z^{\prime}}\simeq g^{\prime}nv_{\sigma}
.
In the following numerical calculation, we will take
n=1/3
for illustration. Therefore, we have
Q_{E_{R}}=1
,
Q_{\nu_{R}}=2/3
,
Q_{F_{L}}=(\sqrt{11}+1)/6
, and
Q_{F_{R}}=(\sqrt{11}-1)/6
.

The dominant annihilation channels for DM
F_{1}
are

\bar{F}_{1}F_{1}\to\bar{\ell}\ell,\bar{\nu}\nu,Z^{\prime}Z^{\prime}.

(25)

Provided
m_{Z^{\prime}}>m_{F_{1}}
, then the annihilation channel
\bar{F}_{1}F_{1}\to Z^{\prime}Z^{\prime}
is not allowed kinematically. Hence,
\bar{F}_{1}F_{1}\to\bar{\ell}\ell,\bar{\nu}\nu
become dominant, which would be able to interpret the DAMPE
e^{+}+e^{-}
excess when
m_{F_{1}}\sim 1.5\penalty\ {\rm TeV}
.

III.1
Constraints

In this part, we summarize some relevant constraints for DAMPE DM. To research the DM phenomenon, we implement this model into
FeynRules

Alloul:2013bka
package. Then, for DM relic density, we require the results calculated by
micrOMEGAs4.3.5

Belanger:2014vza
in
1\sigma
range of Planck measurements:
\Omega h^{2}=0.1199\pm 0.0027

Ade:2015xua

As for direct detection, the leptophilic
Z^{\prime}
will mediate DM-electron scattering at tree level, with the corresponding cross section constrained by XENON100, i.e.,
\sigma_{e}<10^{-34}\text{cm}^{2}
Aprile:2015ade
. Because of XENON100 sensitive to axial-vector couplings, the analytical expression for axial-vector DM-electron scattering is given by

Kopp:2009et

\sigma_{e}=3(g_{F}^{a}g_{\ell}^{a})^{2}\frac{m_{e}^{2}}{\pi m_{Z^{\prime}}^{4}}\approx 3(g_{F}^{a}g_{\ell}^{a})^{2}\left(\frac{m_{Z^{\prime}}}{10\penalty\ {\rm GeV}}\right)^{-4}\times 3.1\times 10^{-39}\text{cm}^{2},

(26)

where
g_{F}^{a}=g^{\prime}(Q_{F_{R}}-Q_{F_{L}})/2=-g^{\prime}/6
and
g_{\ell}^{a}=g^{\prime}Q_{E_{R}}/2=g^{\prime}/2
. For
g^{\prime}\sim 0.1
,
m_{Z^{\prime}}\sim 3\penalty\ {\rm TeV}
, the predicted value is far below current experimental bound. Instead, we consider the loop induced DM-nucleus scattering with the cross section calculated as
Kopp:2009et

\sigma_{N}=\frac{\alpha^{2}Z^{2}\mu_{N}^{2}}{9\pi^{3}A^{2}m_{Z^{\prime}}^{4}}\sum_{\ell=e,\mu,\tau}\left(g_{F}^{v}g_{\ell}^{v}\log\frac{m_{\ell}^{2}}{\mu^{2}}\right)^{2},

(27)

where
\mu_{N}=m_{N}m_{F_{1}}/(m_{N}+m_{F_{1}})
is the reduced DM-nucleus mass,
g_{F}^{v}=g^{\prime}(Q_{F_{R}}+Q_{F_{L}})/2=g^{\prime}\sqrt{11}/6
,
g_{\ell}^{v}=g^{\prime}Q_{E_{R}}/2=g^{\prime}/2
and
\mu=m_{Z^{\prime}}/\sqrt{g_{F}^{v}g_{\ell}^{v}}
is the cut-off scale. Since current most strict direct detection constraint is performed by PandaX
Cui:2017nnn
, we take
Z=54
,
A=131
and
m_{N}=131\penalty\ {\rm GeV}
for the target nucleus¡¯ charge, mass number and mass respectively.

The leptophilic
Z^{\prime}
will contribute to anomalous magnetic moments of leptons
Fayet:2007ua

\Delta a_{\ell}\simeq\frac{g^{\prime 2}}{12\pi^{2}}\frac{m_{\ell}^{2}}{m_{Z^{\prime}}^{2}}

(28)

For an universal gauge-lepton coupling, the precise measurement of
\Delta a_{\mu}=(27.8\pm 8.8)\times 10^{-10}

Bennett:2006fi
set a stringent bound, i.e.,

g^{\prime}\lesssim 5\times 10^{-3}m_{Z^{\prime}}/1{\rm GeV}

Z^{\prime}
at LEP in terms of four-fermion operators provide a much more stringent bound:
g^{\prime}\lesssim 2\times 10^{-4}m_{Z^{\prime}}/1{\rm GeV}

LEP:2003aa

IV
Conclusion

In this paper, we propose an anomaly-free radiative seesaw model with an alternative leptophilic
U(1)_{X}
gauge symmetry. Under the
U(1)_{X}
symmetry, only right-handed leptons are charged. Charged leptons acquire mass via the type-I seesaw-like mechanism with heavy intermediate fermions added also for anomaly-free cancellation. Meanwhile, tiny neutrino masses are generated at one-loop level with DM candidate in the loop.

Provided all other particles are heavy enough, the dominant annihilation channel for DM
F_{1}
is
\bar{F}_{1}F_{1}\to\bar{\ell}\ell,\bar{\nu}\nu
mediated by the new leptophilic gauge boson
Z^{\prime}
.
Motivated by the observed DAMPE
e^{+}+e^{-}
excess around
1.5\penalty\ {\rm TeV}
, we fix
m_{F_{1}}=1.5\penalty\ {\rm TeV}
while consider possible constraints from relic density, direct detection and collider searches. Under all these constraints, a benchmark points, i.e.,
m_{Z^{\prime}}=2950\penalty\ {\rm GeV}
, is chosen from the viable region
m_{Z^{\prime}}\in[2810,3000]\penalty\ {\rm GeV}
. After fitting to the observed spectrum, we find that the DAMPE excess can be explained by a nearby subhalo with a distance of
0.1
(
0.3
) kpc and the viral mass
3\times 10^{7}

M_{\odot}
(
3\times 10^{8}

M_{\odot}
).
