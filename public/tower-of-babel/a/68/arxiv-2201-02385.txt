Direct C ​ P CP violation of three bodies decay process from the resonance effect
Gang Lü 1 , Yan-Lin Zhao , Liang-Chen Liu 1 , Xin-Heng Guo 2

Abstract
The physical state of ρ \rho - ω \omega - ϕ \phi mesons can be mixed by the unitary matrix. The decay processes of ω → π + ​ π − \omega\rightarrow\pi^{+}\pi^{-} and ϕ → π + ​ π − \phi\rightarrow\pi^{+}\pi^{-} are from the isospin symmetry breaking. The ρ − ω \rho-\omega , ρ − ϕ \rho-\phi and ω − ϕ \omega-\phi interferences lead to resonance contribution to produce the strong phases. The C ​ P CP asymmetry is considered from above isospin symmetry breaking due to the new strong phase for the first order. It has been found the C ​ P CP asymmetry can be enhanced greatly for the decay process of B 0 → π + π − η ( ′ ) B^{0}\rightarrow\pi^{+}\pi^{-}\eta^{(^{\prime})} when the invariant masses of the π + ​ π − \pi^{+}\pi^{-} pairs are in the area around the ω \omega resonance range and the ϕ \phi resonance range in perturbative QCD. We also discuss the possibility to search the predicted C ​ P CP violation at the LHC.
