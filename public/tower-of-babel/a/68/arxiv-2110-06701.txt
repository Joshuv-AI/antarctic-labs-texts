Lemma 3.4

5.

CR
-Submanifolds in Kahler Manifolds

By making use of the Gauss equation, we construct a new general inequality for
\mathcal{D}_{T}
-minimal warped product
CR
-submanifolds in arbitrary Kahler manifolds. This inequality generalizes all inequalities in [
[
7
]
].

Now, we present the main theorem of this article.

Theorem 5.1
.

Let
\varphi:M^{n}=N_{T}\times_{f}N_{\perp}\longrightarrow\tilde{M}^{m}
be an isometric immersion of a warped product
CR
-submanifold
M^{n}
into a Kahler manifold
\tilde{M}^{m}
. Then, we have

(i)

\frac{1}{2}||h||^{2}\geq\tilde{\tau}(T_{x}M^{n})-\tilde{\tau}(T_{x}N_{T})-\tilde{\tau}(T_{x}N_{\perp})-\frac{n_{2}\Delta f}{f}.

(ii)

The equality in (i) holds identically if and only if
N_{T}
,
N_{\perp}
and
M^{n}
are totally geodesic, totally umbilical and minimal submanifolds in
\tilde{M}^{m}
, respectively.

Proof.

Via (
2.21
), we first have

||h||^{2}=-2\tau(T_{x}M^{n})+2\tilde{\tau}(T_{x}M^{n})+n^{2}||\vec{H}||^{2}.

In view of Lemma
3.3
, the above equation takes the following form

||h||^{2}=2\tilde{\tau}(T_{x}M^{n})-2\tilde{\tau}(T_{x}N_{T})-2\tilde{\tau}(T_{x}N_{\perp})-2\frac{n_{2}\Delta f}{f}+n^{2}||\vec{H}||^{2}

-2\left(\sum_{r=n+1}^{m}\sum_{1\leq a<b\leq n_{1}}\bigl(h_{aa}^{r}h_{bb}^{r}-(h_{ab}^{r})^{2}\bigr)\right)

~~~~~~~-2\left(\sum_{r=n+1}^{m}\sum_{n_{1}+1\leq A<B\leq n}\bigl(h_{AA}^{r}h_{BB}^{r}-(h_{AB}^{r})^{2}\bigr)\right).

This is equivalent to

||h||^{2}=2\tilde{\tau}(T_{x}M^{n})-2\tilde{\tau}(T_{x}N_{T})-2\tilde{\tau}(T_{x}N_{\perp})-2\frac{n_{2}\Delta f}{f}+n^{2}||\vec{H}||^{2}

-\left(\sum_{r=n+1}^{m}\sum_{1\leq a\neq b\leq n_{1}}\bigl(h_{aa}^{r}h_{bb}^{r}-(h_{ab}^{r})^{2}\bigr)\right)

(5.1)

~~~~~~~-\left(\sum_{r=n+1}^{m}\sum_{n_{1}+1\leq A\neq B\leq n}\bigl(h_{AA}^{r}h_{BB}^{r}-(h_{AB}^{r})^{2}\bigr)\right).

Since
\varphi
is
\mathcal{D}_{T}
-minimal immersion, then

-\left(\sum_{r=n+1}^{m}\sum_{1\leq a\neq b\leq n_{1}}\bigl(h_{aa}^{r}h_{bb}^{r}-(h_{ab}^{r})^{2}\bigr)\right)=

\sum_{r=n+1}^{m}\sum_{1\leq a\neq b\leq n_{1}}(h_{ab}^{r})^{2}-\sum_{r=n+1}^{m}\sum_{1\leq a\neq b\leq n_{1}}h_{aa}^{r}h_{bb}^{r}=

\overbrace{\sum_{r=n+1}^{m}\sum_{1\leq a\neq b\leq n_{1}}(h_{ab}^{r})^{2}+\left(\sum_{r=n+1}^{m}\bigl((h_{11}^{r})^{2}+\cdots+(h_{n_{1}n_{1}}^{r})^{2}\bigr)\right)}

\overbrace{-\left(\sum_{r=n+1}^{m}\bigl((h_{11}^{r})^{2}+\cdots+(h_{n_{1}n_{1}}^{r})^{2}\bigr)\right)-\sum_{r=n+1}^{m}\sum_{1\leq a\neq b\leq n_{1}}h_{aa}^{r}h_{bb}^{r}}.

By means of the binomial theorem, we deduce that

\overbrace{\sum_{r=n+1}^{m}\sum_{1\leq a\neq b\leq n_{1}}(h_{ab}^{r})^{2}+\left(\sum_{r=n+1}^{m}\bigl((h_{11}^{r})^{2}+\cdots+(h_{n_{1}n_{1}}^{r})^{2}\bigr)\right)}=\sum_{r=n+1}^{m}\sum_{a,b=1}^{n_{1}}(h_{ab}^{r})^{2},

and

\overbrace{-\left(\sum_{r=n+1}^{m}\bigl((h_{11}^{r})^{2}+\cdots+(h_{n_{1}n_{1}}^{r})^{2}\bigr)\right)-\sum_{r=n+1}^{m}\sum_{1\leq a\neq b\leq n_{1}}h_{aa}^{r}h_{bb}^{r}}=

-\sum_{r=n+1}^{m}(h_{11}^{r}+\cdots+h_{n_{1}n_{1}}^{r})^{2}.

Next, by combining the last three equations together we obtain

(5.2)

-\left(\sum_{r=n+1}^{m}\sum_{1\leq a\neq b\leq n_{1}}\bigl(h_{aa}^{r}h_{bb}^{r}-(h_{ab}^{r})^{2}\bigr)\right)=\sum_{r=n+1}^{m}\sum_{a,b=1}^{n_{1}}(h_{ab}^{r})^{2}-\sum_{r=n+1}^{m}(h_{11}^{r}+\cdots+h_{n_{1}n_{1}}^{r})^{2}.

2.1
, the second term in the right hand side vanishes whenever
\varphi
is
\mathcal{D}_{T}
-minimal, consequently (

) reduces to

-\left(\sum_{r=n+1}^{m}\sum_{1\leq a\neq b\leq n_{1}}\bigl(h_{aa}^{r}h_{bb}^{r}-(h_{ab}^{r})^{2}\bigr)\right)=\sum_{r=n+1}^{m}\sum_{a,b=1}^{n_{1}}(h_{ab}^{r})^{2}.

Combining (

) and (

+\sum_{r=n+1}^{m}\sum_{a,b=1}^{n_{1}}(h_{ab}^{r})^{2}

-\left(\sum_{r=n+1}^{m}\sum_{n_{1}+1\leq A\neq B\leq n}\bigl(h_{AA}^{r}h_{BB}^{r}-(h_{AB}^{r})^{2}\bigr)\right).

Theorem 6.4
.

Let
M_{\mu}
be a measurable manifold with a measure

over a quantity

\int_{M_{\mu}}=lim_{g\mapsto id}\sum_{x\in M_{\mu}}\mu(x)X(gx)

where

is an element of the manifold, here, the covering basis of it and

Proof.

Consider two values of a quantity
X

X(gx)
and
X(x)
for any manifold covering
x

g
will now tend to the identity transform. Thus,

will be infinitesimal in the case when the function is smooth.
In non-smooth case, the transformation
g
will shift the covering
x
from the singularity apart by appropriate choice of it. Since the manifold
M_{\mu}
is measurable, we can define a measure on it and can also compute a measure-weighted sum over
