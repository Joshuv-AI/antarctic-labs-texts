\displaystyle\quad=\int_{a}^{r}dx\,\frac{|f(x)|^{2}}{4(x-a)^{2}}+2^{-1}\big[(x-a)^{-1/2}f(x)\big]^{2}\big|_{x=a}^{r}

\displaystyle\quad\geq\int_{a}^{r}dx\,\frac{|f(x)|^{2}}{4(x-a)^{2}}-2^{-1}\lim_{x\downarrow a}\big[(x-a)^{-1/2}f(x)\big]^{2}

\displaystyle\quad=\int_{a}^{r}dx\,\frac{|f(x)|^{2}}{4(x-a)^{2}},\quad r\in(a,\infty)\cup\{\infty\},

(B.13)

employing the estimate (
B.8
) with
f(a)=0
.

Corollary B.4
.

(B.15)

In particular, if

(B.16)
