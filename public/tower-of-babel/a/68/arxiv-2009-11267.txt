A Categorical Quantum Toroidal Action On The Hilbert Schemes
Yu Zhao

Abstract
. We categorify the commutation of Nakajima’s Heisenberg operators P ± 1 P_{\pm 1} and their infinitely many counterparts in the quantum toroidal algebra U q 1 , q 2 ​ ( g ​ l 1 ¨ ) U_{q_{1},q_{2}}(\ddot{gl_{1}}) acting on the Grothendieck groups of Hilbert schemes from [ 11 , 34 , 26 , 29 ] . By combining our result with [ 29 ] , one obtains a geometric categorical U q 1 , q 2 ​ ( g ​ l 1 ¨ ) U_{q_{1},q_{2}}(\ddot{gl_{1}}) action on the derived category of Hilbert schemes. Our main technical tool is a detailed geometric study of certain nested Hilbert schemes of triples and quadruples, through the lens of the minimal model program, by showing that these nested Hilbert schemes are either canonical or semi-divisorial log terminal singularities.

1.4.
Categorical Heisenberg Actions

Khovanov

]
defined the Heisenberg category through graphical calculus. Cautis-Licata
[

constructed a categorical Heisenberg action on the derived category of Hilbert schemes of points of the minimal resolution of the type ADE singularities. Krug

In this paper, for any complex

has cohomological degree

Definition 3.3

Given a two term complex of locally free sheaves

\displaystyle S^{k}(U):=\{\wedge^{k}W\cdots\to\cdots\to W\otimes S^{k-1}V\to S^{k}(V)\}

Definition 3.4

Let

be a two term complex of locally free sheaves over a scheme

such that

has rank

and
V
has rank

. Let

is the projection morphism. We define

to be the projectivization of

over
X
, denoted by

if

is resolved by the Koszul complex:

When

Lemma 3.5

(Categorical Projection Lemma)
.

If

is the projectivization of

over

in

, then the tensor contraction

(3.4)

and

is quasi-isomorphic to its cone.

Proof.

\mathcal{O}_{Z}(k)
is quasi-isomorphic to the complex

\{\cdots\to\wedge^{j}\rho^{*}W\otimes\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}_{X}(V)}(-j+k)\to\cdots\to\rho^{*}W\otimes\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}_{X}(V)}(k-1)\to\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}_{X}(V)}(k)\to 0\}.

\displaystyle F_{0}=\{\cdots\to\wedge^{k+v+1}\rho^{*}W\otimes\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}_{X}(V)}(-v-1)\to\wedge^{k+v}\rho^{*}W\otimes\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}_{X}(V)}(-v)\}[-v-k+1]

induces a morphism of

with the cone quasi-isomorphic to

.

By Exercise III.8.4 of Hartshorne
[

]
,

(3.6)

R\rho_{*}(\mathcal{O}(j))=\begin{cases}S^{j}(V)&j\geq 0\\
0&-v<j<0\\

and thus

Hence
