Three-Dimensional Eigenvalues of Harmonic Oscillator- and Coulomb-type Potentials from One-Dimensional Generalized Morse Potential: Perturbative Analyse based on Generalized Laguerre Polynomials
Altuğ Arda

Abstract
We present perturbative energy eigenvalues (up to second order) of Coulomb- and harmonic oscillator-type fields within a perturbation scheme. We have the required unperturbed eigenvalues ( E n ( 0 ) E_{n}^{(0)} ) analytically obtained by using similarities between the expressions obtained from unperturbed Hamiltonian(s) for two fields and obtained from the ones for one-dimensional generalized Morse field. We use the Langer transformation for this aim. We need the diagonal and non-diagonal matrix elements of unperturbed and perturbed Hamiltonians to get energy eigenvalues perturbatively, which are obtained with help of some recursion identities or some integrals of generalized Laguerre polynomials having analytical results. Keywords: perturbation scheme, generalized Laguerre polynomials, Langer transformation, energy eigenvalues, harmonic oscillator field, Coulomb filed, Morse field

II
Unperturbative Solutions

The time-independent Schrödinger equation is an eigenvalue equation for a non-relativistic particle subjected to the potential
V(\vec{r\,})

\displaystyle\left[-\,\frac{1}{2}\,\vec{\nabla\,}^{2}+V(\vec{r\,})\right]\Psi_{n\ell}(\vec{r\,})=E_{n}^{(0)}\Psi_{n\ell}(\vec{r\,})\,,

(1)

where
E_{n}^{(0)}
are eigenvalues of the equation,
\Psi_{n\ell}
is corresponding eigenfunction, and the pair of
(n,\ell)
are understood as quantum numbers. One expects that energy eigenvalues are real (positive or negative) for bound-states of the particle. Writing the eigenfunction as
\Psi_{n\ell}(\vec{r\,})=r^{-1}\psi_{n\ell}(r)Y_{n\ell}(\theta,\vartheta)
gives the radial part of the whole equation

\displaystyle\left[-\,\frac{1}{2}\,\frac{d^{2}}{dr^{2}}+V(r)+\,\frac{\ell(\ell+1)}{2r^{2}}\right]\psi_{n\ell}(r)=E_{n}^{(0)}\psi_{n\ell}(r)\,,

(2)

which is valid for spherically symmetric potential
V(r)
with
\int_{0}^{\infty}dr|\psi(r)|^{2}=1
for bound-state solutions.

We use a generic form for the potential
NC

\displaystyle V(r)=sr^{\mu}+\,\frac{\nu}{r^{2}}\,,

(3)

which corresponds to harmonic oscillator- and Coulomb-like potentials in below.

Within the perturbation theory, the total Hamiltonian is routinely divided into two parts

\displaystyle h(s)=h_{0}+h_{1}\,,

(4)

where the extended unperturbed Hamiltonian is

\displaystyle h_{0}=\,-\,\frac{1}{2}\,\frac{d^{2}}{dr^{2}}+sr^{\mu}+\,\frac{\nu}{r^{2}}\,,

(5)

\displaystyle h_{1}=(Z-s)r^{\mu}+h^{\prime}\,.

(6)

with the screening parameter

denotes initial perturbing term
GFC

Our aim is to study the perturbative effects on the solutions. So, we need first to obtain the eigenvalues of extended unperturbed Hamiltonian (
h_{0}

The Schrödinger equation for the one-dimensional generalized Morse potential is

\displaystyle\left[-\,\frac{1}{2}\,\frac{d^{2}}{dx^{2}}+V_{1}e^{-\alpha x}+V_{2}e^{-2\alpha x}-\varepsilon_{n}\right]\phi_{n}(x)=0\,\,\,(\alpha>0)\,,

(7)

where
\varepsilon_{n}
is energy eigenvalues and normalization of the corresponding eigenfunctions is
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}dx|\phi_{n}(x)|^{2}=1
.

Because of behaviour of the wave function in limits, which also requires
V_{2}>0

\phi=y^{\sqrt{-2\varepsilon_{n}\,}/\alpha}e^{-y/2}f(y)
with a new variable
y=(2\sqrt{2V_{2}\,}/\alpha)e^{-\alpha x}
we obtain a Kummer’s-type equation

\displaystyle\left[y\,\frac{d^{2}}{dy^{2}}+\left(\frac{2\sqrt{-2\varepsilon_{n}\,}}{\alpha}+1-y\right)\frac{d}{dy}-\left(\frac{V_{1}}{2\sqrt{V_{2}\,}\alpha}+\frac{\sqrt{-2\varepsilon_{n}\,}}{\alpha}+\frac{1}{2}\right)\right]f(y)=0\,,

(8)

\displaystyle\phi_{n}(y)=A_{n}y^{\sqrt{-2\varepsilon_{n}\,}/\alpha}e^{-y/2}L_{n}^{(2\sqrt{-2\varepsilon_{n}\,}/\alpha)}(y)\,,

(9)

with normalization constant
A_{n}
. The potential parameter
V_{1}
should be negative to have finite solutions

\displaystyle\varepsilon_{n}=-\,\frac{V_{1}^{2}}{4V_{2}}\left[1-\,\frac{\sqrt{2V_{2}\,}\alpha}{|V_{1}|}\,N\right]^{2}\,.

\displaystyle\psi_{n\ell}=e^{-\Gamma\alpha x}g\,,\,\,e^{-\Gamma\alpha x/2}=\sqrt{\frac{r}{r_{0}}\,}\,,\,\,(r_{0}>0,\,\Gamma>0)\,,

IV
Conclusions

We present the energy eigenvalues with first- and second-order corrections within perturbation theory for three-dimensional harmonic oscillator- and Coulomb-like potentials, separately. We give some integrals of generalized Laguerre polynomials with analytical result. The unperturbed bound state eigenvalues and the corresponding eigenfunctions of the above potentials are obtained from the ones of one-dimensional generalized Morse potential by the help of some similarities and using the Langer transformation. The effect of screening parameter
s
on perturbative corrections is discussed.
