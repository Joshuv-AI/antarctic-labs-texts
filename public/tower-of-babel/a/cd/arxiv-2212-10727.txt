Introduction and statement of main results

Let

\Big({\mathbb{Z}}\vec{u}\oplus{\mathbb{Z}}\vec{v}\Big)

The summation ranges over all the lattice points except for the origin
0

The function

E_{f}(L)

f

The most physically related and widely used potential is the Lennard-Jones potential.
It takes the form (see e.g.

\displaystyle f(|\cdot|^{2}):=4\varepsilon\Big((\frac{\sigma}{|\cdot|})^{12}-(\frac{\sigma}{|\cdot|})^{6}\Big),

It is considered an archetype model for simple yet realistic intermolecular interactions and is not only of fundamental importance in computational chemistry and soft-matter physics, but also for the modeling of real substances. \displaystyle\mathcal{E}_{LJ}=2\varepsilon\sigma^{12}\Big(\zeta(6,z)-b\zeta(3,z)\Big),\;\;b=\frac{1}{\sigma^{6}}.

Remark 1.1
.

By the relation in (

Remark 1.2
.

We find that there admits two minimizers for the first threshold

up to rotations and translations. This is a new phenomenon mathematically
and we do not know any potential physical implications.

Remark 1.3
.

Our method of proofs can be easily adopted to treat the more general Lenard-Jones potential cases:

\displaystyle f(|\cdot|^{2}):=4\varepsilon\Big((\frac{\sigma}{|\cdot|})^{2s_{1}}-(\frac{\sigma}{|\cdot|})^{2s_{2}}\Big),s_{1}>s_{2}>1.

, followed by its corresponding fundamental region.

We first fix the parametrization of a lattice in
\mathbb{R}^{2}

\mathbf{a}_{1},\mathbf{a}_{2}

we fix the area of the lattice to be

|\mathbf{a}_{1}\wedge\mathbf{a}_{2}|=1

Up to rotation and translation, one can set
\mathbf{a}_{1}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{y}}(1,0)

In this way,

Next we introduce another group related to the functionals

The following lemma characterizes the fundamental symmetries of the theta functions

Lemma 2.1
.

For any

, any

and

\ \zeta(s;\gamma(z))=\zeta(s;z)

Let

\displaystyle\mathcal{W}_{b}(\alpha;z):=\zeta(\alpha;z)-b\zeta(2\alpha;z).
