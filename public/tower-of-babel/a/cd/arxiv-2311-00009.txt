Application of Fermion Quantum Number 𝑭 F and Unit Electroweak Charge 𝑭 𝟎 F_{0} in the Electroweak Theory
Xin-Hua Ma

Abstract
After hypercharge Y Y , isospin I I , isospin projection I 3 I_{3} , and four flavour quantum numbers ( S S , C C , B ∗ B^{*} and T T ), weak isospin I w ​ 3 I_{w3} and weak hypercharge Y w Y_{w} in the electroweak theory are substituted by fermion quantum number F F and unit electroweak charge F 0 F_{0} , both of which are conserved in electromagnetic interaction, weak interaction and strong interaction. Electrical charge Q Q and neutral current weak charge Q Z Q_{Z} in electroweak interaction can be expressed by mixture of F F and F 0 F_{0} via the Weinberg angle θ W \theta_{W} . Electroweak interaction is separated into two parts: one is neutral current electroweak interaction which has no change of particle types with only exchange of neutral bosons, and the other is charged current weak interaction which has change of particle types, i.e., decays.

4
Conclusion and Discussion

Both fermion quantum number
F
and unit electroweak charge
F_{0}
are conserved in electromagnetic interaction, weak interaction and strong interaction. Not only hypercharge
Y
, isospin
I
, isospin projection
I_{3}
and four flavour quantum numbers (
S
,
C
,
B^{*}
and
T
), but also weak isospin
I_{w3}
and weak hypercharge
Y_{w}
in the electroweak theory are substituted by
F
and
F_{0}
in simpler method of additive quantum numbers. Indistinguishability of fermions results in
F
conservation, and indistinguishability of relative phases of wave functions of fermions results in
F_{0}
conservation. It can be analogical to the fact that indistinguishability of protons and neutrons results in
I_{3}
conservation in nuclei, and indistinguishability of relative phases of wave functions of charged particles results in
Q
conservation in electromagnetic interaction.

More profoundly, by combining electromagnetic interaction and neutral current weak interaction into neutral current electroweak interaction, electroweak charge
\mathbf{Q_{eZ}}
can be deduced from electroweak fermion quantum number
\mathbf{F_{eZ}}
via
\theta_{W}
. Then electroweak interaction is separated into two parts: one is neutral current electroweak interaction which has no change of particle types with only exchange of neutral bosons, and the other is charged current weak interaction which has change of particle types, i.e., decays.

In fact, generality among electromagnetic interaction, neutral current weak interaction and strong interaction is that flavours of fermions are not changed (including fermion pair production), and the interactions happen between fermions via exchanging neutral bosons (photon,
Z^{0}
and gluon respectively). On the the other hand, in charged current weak interaction, including all types of decays and their equivalent reactions, flavours of fermions are changed, which means charged current weak interaction happens inside fermions via emitting or absorbing charged bosons (
W^{\pm}
). It is interesting that the selection rules of charged current weak interaction are determined by conservation of
F

[
7
]
, meanwhile electroweak fermion quantum number
\mathbf{F_{eZ}}
(including
F
and
F_{0}
) determines electroweak charge
\mathbf{Q_{eZ}}
(including
Q
,
Q_{Z}
) of neutral current electroweak interaction. Maybe we should recombine different interactions in the unified theory into the flavour-unchanged part and the flavour-changed part where
\mathbf{F_{eZ}}
should perform a function.
