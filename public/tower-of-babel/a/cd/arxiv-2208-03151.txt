C ​ P C\!P violation in B B mesons
D. Manuzzi on behalf of the LHCb collaboration, with results from the Belle and Belle II collaborations

Abstract
This proceeding reports a selection of recent experimental results concerning C ​ P C\!P violation in the sectors of B 0 {B}^{0} and B ± {B}^{\pm} mesons. They were published within the last two years by the Belle , Belle II and LHCb collaborations. The first set of measurements is related to the determination of the angles of the Unitarity Triangle. The second is connected to another crucial test of the SM: the isospin sum rule of B → K ​ π B\rightarrow K\pi decays. Thirdly, studies of C ​ P C\!P violation in the decays charged B B mesons to charmless three-body final states are presented. Finally, prospects for the future experimental upgrades of this sector are summarised.

V
Prospects

Many of the

B

This is one of the motivations leading to the foreseen upgrades of the
LHCb
and
Belle II
detectors.

LHCb
is expected to increase its dataset to 23
\text{\,fb}^{-1}
before the end of 2026, touching 50
\text{\,fb}^{-1}
within 2032.
In the same period, the target of
Belle II
is collecting a total of 50
\text{\,ab}^{-1}
.
Several studies have already started for the next phase, setting the
LHCb
goal to 300
\text{\,fb}^{-1}
and the
Belle II

\text{\,ab}^{-1}
.
