Yaser Rowshan 1, MOSTAFA GHOLAMI 1

Abstract
. For bipartite graphs G G and H H and a positive integer m m , the m m -bipartite Ramsey number B ​ R m ​ ( G , H ) BR_{m}(G,H) of G G and H H is the smallest integer n n , such that every red-blue coloring of K m , n K_{m,n} results in a red G G or a blue H H . Zhenming Bi, Gary Chartrand and Ping Zhang in [ 1 ] evaluate this numbers for all positive integers m m when G = K 2 , 2 G=K_{2,2} and H ∈ { K 2 , 3 , K 3 , 3 } H\in\{K_{2,3},K_{3,3}\} , especially in a long and hard argument they showed that B ​ R 5 ​ ( K 2 , 2 , K 3 , 3 ) = B ​ R 6 ​ ( K 2 , 2 , K 3 , 3 ) = 12 BR_{5}(K_{2,2},K_{3,3})=BR_{6}(K_{2,2},K_{3,3})=12 and B ​ R 7 ​ ( K 2 , 2 , K 3 , 3 ) = B ​ R 8 ​ ( K 2 , 2 , K 3 , 3 ) = 9 BR_{7}(K_{2,2},K_{3,3})=BR_{8}(K_{2,2},K_{3,3})=9 . In this article, by a short and easy argument we determine the exact value of B ​ R m ​ ( K 2 , 2 , K 3 , 3 ) BR_{m}(K_{2,2},K_{3,3}) for each m ≥ 1 m\geq 1 .

BR_{m}(K_{2,2},K_{3,3})=\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\text{does not exist},&~~~if~~~m=2,3,\\
