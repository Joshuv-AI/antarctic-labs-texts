1
Introduction

As mentioned in the Abstract, we will take up the following problem to study.

\displaystyle\begin{split}(-\Delta)^{s}u+u&=\lambda|u|^{-\gamma-1}u+P(x)|u|^{p-1}u,~\text{in}~\mathbb{R}^{N}\setminus\Omega\\
N_{s}u(x)&=0,~\text{in}~\Omega.\end{split}

(
P
)

The function
P
is a continuous function such that

(P_{1}):~~~~~~P(x)\geq\tilde{P}>0~\text{in}~\mathbb{R}^{N}\setminus\Omega

and

\underset{|x|\rightarrow\infty}{\lim}P(x)=\tilde{P}.

The “nonlocal normal derivative”
N_{s}
was first introduced by Dipierro et al.
[
1
]
. The authors in
[
1
]
proved that as
s\rightarrow 1^{-}
, the classical Neumann boundary condition is recovered in the following sense.

\displaystyle\underset{s\rightarrow 1^{-}}{\lim}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}\setminus\Omega}vN_{s}u

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int_{\partial\Omega}v\frac{\partial u}{\partial\nu}

where
\nu
is an outward drawn normal to the boundary
\partial\Omega
. Elliptic problems considered in a exterior domain is a rarity in the literature. However, when we traced through the literature pertaining to the exterior domain problem, we found a few seminal works. One of them is due to Benci and Cerami
[
6
]
who considered the problem (
P
) with
s=1
,
V(x)\equiv 1
,
\lambda=0
,
Q(x)\equiv 1
and with a zero Dirichlet boundary condition. The authors in
[
6
]
showed that it does not have a ground state solution. Thanks to the article due to Esteban
[
2
]
who proved that the same problem with Neumann condition has a ground state solution to the following problem.

\displaystyle\begin{split}-\Delta u+u&=|u|^{p-1}u,~\text{in}~\mathbb{R}^{N}\setminus\Omega\\
\frac{\partial u}{\partial\nu}&=0,~\text{on}~\partial\Omega.\end{split}

(1.1)

A noteworthy work is due to Cao
[
3
]
who studied the existence of positive solution to (
1.1
) under the assumption that

P(x)\geq\tilde{P}-Ce^{-a|x|}|x|^{-m}~~\text{as}~~|x|\rightarrow\infty

together with the condition in
(P_{1})
. Here
a=\frac{2(p+1)}{p-1}
,
m>N-1
and
C>0
. Continuing in this article
[
3
]
, Cao further proved the existence of sign changing solution (also know as a
nodal
solution) under the additional assumption that

P(x)\geq\tilde{P}+Ce^{-\frac{p|x|}{p+1}}|x|^{-m}~~\text{as}~~|x|\rightarrow\infty

together with the condition in
(P_{1})
with
0<m<\frac{N-1}{2}
. Alves et al.
[
7
]
proved that the results found in
[
3
]
also holds true for the
p
-Laplacian operator and for a larger class of nonlinearity. The problem with
N=2
and a nonlinearity of critical growth has also been considered by Alves in
[
8
]
.

Off-late, the fractional Laplacian operator gained a mileage as far as attention is concerned as it naturally arises in many different contexts, viz. optimization, thin obstacle problem, finance, crystal dislocation, conservation laws, limits of quantum mechanics, material science and water waves to name a few. Interested readers may refer to the articles [13,19,20,31,32] and the references therein. We drew motivation from the work due to of Alves
[
9
]
to study the problem (
P
). To the best of our knowledge, there is no article in the literature that addresses the problem (
P
) driven by a singularity in an exterior domain and a Neumann boundary condition. We will first prove the existence of a nonnegative ground state solution to (
P
). Capitalising on this proof, we will further show that the problem has infinitely many bounded solutions for a finite range of
\lambda
. The main result concerning the existence of a ground state solution is as follows.

Theorem 1.1
.

{main_thm}

Suppose
p\in(1,2_{s}^{*}-1)
and
(P_{1})
holds, then (
P
) has positive ground state solution. Further, there exists
\lambda_{0}>0
such that (
P
) has infinitely many bounded solutions whenever
\lambda\in(0,\lambda_{0})
.

Preliminaries

The section introduces the readers to some well known function spaces besides considering the following limiting problem.

\displaystyle\begin{split}(-\Delta)^{s}u+u&=\lambda|u|^{-\gamma-1}u+\tilde{P}|u|^{p-1}u,~\text{in}~\mathbb{R}^{N}\\
u&\in H^{s}(\mathbb{R}^{N}).\end{split}

(
P_{\infty}
)

The operator
(-\Delta)^{s}
is defined as follows.

\displaystyle\begin{split}(-\Delta)^{s}u(x)&=C_{N,s}P.V.\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}\frac{u(x)-u(y)}{|x-y|^{N+2s}}dy\end{split}

(2.1)

where
C_{N,s}=2^{2s-1}\pi^{-\frac{N}{2}}\frac{\Gamma(\frac{N+2s}{2})}{|\Gamma(-s)|}
. We will denote
H^{s}(\mathbb{R}^{N})
to be the fractional Sobolev space equipped with the norm

\|u\|=\left(\frac{1}{2}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{2}}{|x-y|^{N+2s}}dydx+\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}|u|^{2}dx\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}.

Let
D\subset\mathbb{R}^{N}
be a smooth domain. We now define the fractional Sobolev space pertaining to an exterior domain as follows.

H_{D}^{s}=\left\{u:\mathbb{R}^{N}\rightarrow\mathbb{R}~\text{measurable}:\frac{1}{2}\iint_{\mathbb{R}^{2N}\setminus(D^{c})^{2}}\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{2}}{|x-y|^{N+2s}}dydx+\int_{D}|u|^{2}dx<\infty\right\}

where
D^{c}=\mathbb{R}^{N}\setminus D
.
This space is equipped with the norm

\|u\|_{s}=\left(\frac{1}{2}\iint_{\mathbb{R}^{2N}\setminus(D^{c})^{2}}\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{2}}{|x-y|^{N+2s}}dydx+\int_{D}|u|^{2}dx\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}.

This space
H_{D}^{s}
is a Hilbert space with an inner product
\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle_{H_{D}^{s}}
given by

\langle u,v\rangle_{H_{D}^{s}}=\frac{1}{2}\iint_{\mathbb{R}^{2N}\setminus(D^{c})^{2}}\frac{(u(x)-u(y))(v(x)-v(y))}{|x-y|^{N+2s}}dydx+\int_{D}uvdx.

We now state a few embedding results pertaining to the space
H_{D}^{s}
which can be found in
[
5
]
.

Lemma 2.1
.

{emb}

1.

Let
H^{s}(D)
be the classical fractional Sobolev space equipped with the norm

\|u\|_{H^{s}(D)}^{2}=\frac{1}{2}\int_{D}\int_{D}\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{2}}{|x-y|^{N+2s}}dydx+\int_{D}|u|^{2}dx.

Since
D\times D\subset\mathbb{R}^{2N}\setminus(D^{c})^{2}
, then the embedding

is continuous.

2.

The embedding

is continuous.

3.

Since

is continuously for every

, by

we have

H_{D}^{s}\hookrightarrow L^{p}(D)~\text{for all}~p\in\left[2,\frac{2N}{N-2s}\right].

4.

If

is bounded, we have the compact embedding

H_{D}^{s}\hookrightarrow L^{p}(D)~\text{for all}~p\in\left[1,\frac{2N}{N-2s}\right).

Definition 2.2

(Palais Smale condition
[
10
]
)
.

{PS}

Let

Definition 2.3

(Mountain pass theorem of Ambrosetti and Rabinowitz
[

]
)

{MP_thm}

Let

be a

functional satisfying

. Let

,

Then

Lemma 2.4
.

{existence_positive_soln}

Let

,

\displaystyle(-\Delta)^{s}u+u

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\lambda u^{-\gamma},~\text{in}~\mathbb{R}^{N}\setminus\Omega

\displaystyle>

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 0,~\text{in}~\Omega

Proof.

We follow the proof in
[

\displaystyle E(u)=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{1}{2}\iint_{\mathbb{R}^{2N}\setminus(\Omega^{c})^{2}}\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{2}}{|x-y|^{N+2s}}dydx+\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}\setminus\Omega}|u|^{2}dx\right)-\frac{\lambda}{1-\gamma}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}\setminus\Omega}|u|^{1-\gamma}dx

Second, we have the decomposition

\displaystyle\begin{split}E^{\prime}(tv_{0})|_{t=\epsilon}=&\epsilon\left(\frac{1}{2}\iint_{\mathbb{R}^{2N}\setminus(\Omega^{c})^{2}}\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{2}}{|x-y|^{N+2s}}dydx+\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}\setminus\Omega}|u|^{2}dx\right)-\lambda\epsilon^{-\gamma}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}\setminus\Omega}|u|^{1-\gamma}dx<0\end{split}

Lemma 3.2

{MP_geometry}

Proof.

\frac{1}{2}\|u\|_{H_{\Omega^{c}}^{s}}^{2}-\frac{\lambda C_{2}}{1-\gamma}\|u\|_{H_{\Omega^{c}}^{s}}^{1-\gamma}>0

&\leq\left(\lambda\int_{\Omega^{\prime}}|u|^{\beta}|u|^{p}dx\right)\frac{(\beta+1)^{2}}{4\beta}\\
&\leq\lambda\beta C^{\prime\prime}\|u\|_{\alpha^{*}}^{p}\|u^{\beta}\|_{t};~\text{since by using the H\"{o}lder's inequality}.\end{split}

(3.33)

Let us denote the set

. Let

- obtained from the Egorov’s theorem - where

\{u_{n}\}

in

. Further, define

n

. Now consider
