Large upper critical fields and dimensionality crossover of superconductivity in infinite-layer nickelate La 0.8 Sr 0.2 NiO 2 Preprint: La0.8Sr0.2NiO2
Wei Wei, Wenjie Sun, Yue Sun, Yongqiang Pan, Gangjian Jin, Feng Yang, Yueying Li, Zengwei Zhu, Yuefeng Nie, Zhixiang Shi

Abstract
The recently emerging superconductivity in infinite-layer nickelates, with isostructure and isoelectron of cuprates, provides a new platform to explore the pairing mechanism of high-temperature superconductors. In this work, we studied the upper critical field ( H c2 H_{\rm{c2}} ) of a high-quality La 0.8 Sr 0.2 NiO 2 thin film with superconducting transition temperature, T c T_{\rm{c}} = 18.8 K, using high magnetic field up to 56 T. A very large H c2 H_{\rm{c2}} , ∼ \sim 40 T for H H ∥ \Arrowvert c c and ∼ \sim 52 T for H H ∥ \Arrowvert a ​ b ab , was confirmed, which suggests that infinite-layer nickelates also have great application potential. The anisotropy of H c2 H_{\rm{c2}} monotonically decreases from ∼ \sim 10 near T c T_{\rm{c}} to ∼ \sim 1.5 at 2 K. Angle dependence of H c2 H_{\rm{c2}} confirms the crossover of superconductivity from two-dimensional (2D) to three-dimensional (3D) as the temperature decreases. We discussed that the interstitial orbital effect causes the weakening of anisotropy. The observed abnormal upturning of H c2 H_{\rm{c2}} at low temperatures is found to be a universal behavior independent of film quality and rare earth elements. Therefore, it should not be the Fulde-Ferrell-Larkin-Ovchinnikov (FFLO) state due to the fact that it is in the dirty limit and insensitive to disorder.
