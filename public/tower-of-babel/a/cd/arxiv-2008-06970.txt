Lifts on complex manifolds
Mohammad Nazrul Islam Khan

Abstract
In this article, we study higher-order complete lifts of the tensor field of type (1,1) and find some theorems on Nijenhuis tensor. We also consider almost complex structure on complex manifold M M and defined extended almost complex structures on M k {}^{k}M . Moreover, we study Lie derivative concerning higher-order lifts and find some basic results on the almost analytic complex vector concerning an extended almost complex structure on M k {}^{k}M .

2.1

Let

{}^{k}M

The extended properties about vertical and complete lifts of complex functions are given as follows:

\displaystyle i)~~(f+g)^{v^{r}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle f^{v^{r}}+g^{v^{r}},(f.g)^{v^{r}}=f^{v^{r}}.g^{v^{r}}

\displaystyle ii)~~(f+g)^{c^{r}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle f^{c^{r}}+g^{c^{r}},(f.g)^{c^{r}}=\sum^{r}_{j=0}C^{r}_{j}f^{c^{r-j}v^{j}}.g^{c^{j}v^{r-j}},

(2.6)

and

X^{c^{k}}=C^{r}_{j}(Z^{0i})^{v^{k-r}c^{r}}\frac{\partial}{\partial z^{ri}}+C^{r}_{j}(\bar{Z}^{0i})^{v^{k-r}c^{r}}\frac{\partial}{\partial\bar{z}^{ri}}.

The extended properties about vertical and complete lifts of complex vector field are given as follows:

\displaystyle i)~~(X+Y)^{v^{r}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle X^{v^{r}}+Y^{v^{r}},(X+Y)^{c^{r}}=X^{c^{r}}+Y^{c^{r}}

\displaystyle ii)~~X^{v^{k}}(f^{c^{k}})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(Xf)^{v^{k}},~~~X^{c^{k}}(f^{c^{k}})=(Xf)^{c^{k}}

\displaystyle iii)~~~~(fX)^{v^{r}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle f^{v^{r}}.X^{v^{r}},(fX)^{c^{r}}=\sum^{r}_{j=0}C^{r}_{j}f^{c^{r-j}v^{j}}.X^{c^{j}v^{r-j}},

\displaystyle 0,~~X^{c^{r}}(f^{c^{r}})=(Xf)^{c^{r}},~X^{c^{r}}(f^{v^{r}})=X^{v^{r}}(f^{c^{r}})=(Xf)^{v^{r}}

\displaystyle v)~~[X^{v^{r}},Y^{v^{r}}]

\displaystyle 0,[X^{c^{r}},Y^{c^{r}}]=[X,Y]^{c^{r}},[X^{v^{r}},Y^{c^{r}}]=[X^{c^{r}},Y^{v^{r}}]=[X,Y]^{v^{r}}

Theorem 3.1

Let

\displaystyle(FG)^{c^{k}}X^{c^{k}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle((FG)X)^{c^{k}}=(F(GX))^{c^{k}}=F^{c^{k}}(GX)^{c^{k}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle F^{c^{k}}G^{c^{k}}X^{c^{k}}=(F^{c^{k}}G^{c^{k}})X^{c^{k}}

\displaystyle\Rightarrow(FG)^{c^{k}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle F^{c^{k}}G^{c^{k}}.

(3.2)

for arbitrary complex vector field

Theorem 3.2

where

Taking lifts of both sides of equation (

\displaystyle(N_{F})^{c^{k}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle([FX,FY]+F^{2}[X,Y]-F[FX,Y]-F[X,FY])^{c^{k}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle[F^{c^{k}}X^{c^{k}},F^{c^{k}}Y^{c^{k}}]+(F^{c^{k}})^{2}[X^{c^{k}},Y^{c^{k}}]-F^{c^{k}}[F^{c^{k}}X^{c^{k}},Y^{c^{k}}]-F^{c^{k}}[X^{c^{k}},F^{c^{k}}Y^{c^{k}}]

\displaystyle(F^{2}+I)^{c^{k}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 0

\displaystyle(F^{2})^{c^{k}}+I

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 0

\displaystyle(F^{2})^{c^{k}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-I\Rightarrow(F^{c^{k}})^{2}=-I
