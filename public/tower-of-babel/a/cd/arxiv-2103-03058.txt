Monotonicity of the over-rotation intervals for bimodal maps
Sourav Bhattacharya, Alexander Blokh

Abstract
. We show that the connectedness of the set of parameters for which the over-rotation interval of a bimodal interval map is constant. In other words, the over-rotation interval is a monotone function of a bimodal interval map.

INTRODUCTION

One-dimensional combinatorial dynamics started when O. M. Sharkovsky proved his theorem on
the coexistence of periods of cycles for interval maps. To state it, we recall the (transitive)

Sharkovsky order
of the set natural numbers:

3\succ\mkern-14.0mu_{s}\;5\succ\mkern-14.0mu_{s}\;7\succ\mkern-14.0mu_{s}\;\dots\succ\mkern-14.0mu_{s}\;2\cdot 3\succ\mkern-14.0mu_{s}\;2\cdot 5\succ\mkern-14.0mu_{s}\;2\cdot 7\succ\mkern-14.0mu_{s}\;\\
\dots\succ\mkern-14.0mu_{s}\;2^{2}\cdot 3\succ\mkern-14.0mu_{s}\;2^{2}\cdot 5\succ\mkern-14.0mu_{s}\;2^{2}\cdot 7\succ\mkern-14.0mu_{s}\;\dots\succ\mkern-14.0mu_{s}\;2^{2}\succ\mkern-14.0mu_{s}\;2\succ\mkern-14.0mu_{s}\;1.

In what follows by the
period
we mean the
minimal
period.
Below
I
always denotes a closed interval.

Theorem 1.1

(
[

]
and

]

If

Theorem

invariant under topological conjugacy of interval maps. It can be defined as follows.

Let

The set of

Theorem 1.2

(

Suppose that

Definition

Definition 2.5
