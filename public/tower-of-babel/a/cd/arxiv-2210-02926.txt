Abstract
. We show that every skew-symmetric 6 × 6 6\times 6 matrix of linear forms with vanishing Pfaffian is congruent to one of finitely many types of matrices, each of which is characterised by a specific pattern of zeroes (and some other linear relations) among its entries. Such matrices are for example important for compactifying moduli spaces of stable rank 2 2 vector bundles with Chern classes c 1 = 0 , c 2 = 2 c_{1}=0,c_{2}=2 on cubic threefolds.



In this paper we prove that a skew-symmetric

In some cases the invariant

Definition 4.5
.

Let
V,W
be
\mathbb{C}
vector spaces. After choosing bases of
V
and
W
we have a
1:1
correspondence
between
\dim W\times\dim W
matrices of linear forms over
\mathbb{P}(V)
and elements of

\mathrm{Hom}(V,W^{*}\otimes W^{*}).

In this situation we call
\mathbb{P}(W)
the space of
generalized rows
.
Considering the natural isomorphisms

\mathrm{Hom}(V,W^{*}\otimes W^{*})\cong W^{*}\otimes W^{*}\otimes V^{*}\cong\mathrm{Hom}(W,V^{*}\otimes W^{*})

we see that (after choosing bases for
V
and
W

1:1

\dim W\times\dim W
matrices of linear forms over
\mathbb{P}(V)
and
\dim V\times\dim W

matrices of linear forms over
\mathbb{P}(W)

M\mapsto\widehat{M}.

and call
\widehat{M}
the

of

We define the
