Ground states of one-dimensional dipolar lattice bosons at unit filling
Mateusz Ła̧cki, Henning Korbmacher, Gustavo A. Domínguez-Castro, Jakub Zakrzewski, Luis Santos

Abstract
Recent experiments on ultracold dipoles in optical lattices open exciting possibilities for the quantum simulation of extended Hubbard models. When considered in one dimension, these models present at unit filling a particularly interesting ground-state physics, including a symmetry-protected topological phase known as Haldane insulator. We show that the tail of the dipolar interaction beyond nearest-neighbors, which may be tailored by means of the transversal confinement, does not only modify quantitatively the Haldane insulator regime and lead to density waves of larger periods, but results as well in unexpected insulating phases. These insulating phases may be topological or topologically trivial, and are characterized by peculiar correlations of the site occupations. These phases may be realized and observed in state-of-the-art experiments.



I
Introduction

Ultra cold quantum systems in optical lattices and tweezer arrays offer extraordinary possibilities for the quantum simulation of many-body lattice models
Lewenstein et al. 2007
;
Bloch et al. 2008
;
Lewenstein et al. 2012
. Whereas these systems are typically characterized
by on-site interactions, with the exception of relatively weak super-exchange terms
Trotzky et al. 2008
, recent experiments on dipolar systems, including
magnetic atoms
Chomaz et al. 2022
, polar molecules
Bohn et al. 2017
, and Rydberg gases
Browaeys and Lahaye 2020
, open
the possibility to simulate lattice models with significant inter-site interactions. Extended Hubbard models
Dutta et al. 2015
have been already realized using magnetic atoms
Baier et al. 2016
;
Su et al. 2023
, including the very recent observation of density-wave patterns
Su et al. 2023
.

One-dimensional extended Bose-Hubbard models (EBHMs)
are particularly interesting at unit filling, since they
present in addition to
the superfluid (SF), Mott insulator (MI) and density-wave (DW) phases, the so-called Haldane insulator (HI)
Dalla Torre et al. 2006
;
Rossini and Fazio 2012
;
Ejima et al. 2014
, a symmetry protected topological phase, characterized by a string order, and a doubly-degenerate entanglement spectrum
Pollmann et al. 2010
. This phase is directly
linked to the Haldane phase of spin-
1

chains
den Nijs and Rommelse 1989
;
Kennedy and Tasaki 1992
, since for strong-enough on-site interactions, EBHMs may be mapped to spin-
1
models
Dalla Torre et al. 2006
. Recently it was shown
Fraxanet et al. 2022
that topological properties can persist at the HI-DW phase transition.

Although major features of the physics of polar lattice gases may be understood using models with only nearest-neighbor interactions,
for sufficiently large dipole moments, the tail of the dipolar interactions beyond nearest neighbors, which is typically assumed as decaying as
1/r^{3}
, with
r
the inter-site distance, may become relevant
Dalla Torre et al. 2006
;
Biedroń et al. 2018
;
Kraus et al. 2020
.
The dipolar tail leads to quantitative modifications of the HI parameter region, but it does not compromise its existence
Dalla Torre et al. 2006
. In addition, it is expected that strong-enough interactions beyond nearest neighbors should lead to DWs with longer integer spatial periods.

In this work, we show that, surprisingly, the dipolar tail
results as well in the appearance of unexpected insulating phases. For the usually considered
1/r^{3}
dipolar tail, we
find at low on-site interactions a topologically trivial spatially disordered insulator (TTI). Moreover, by properly choosing the transversal confinement it is possible to taylor a milder decay of the dipolar tail
Wall and Carr 2013
;
Korbmacher et al. 2023a
;
Korbmacher et al. 2023b
, which results not only in an enhanced TTI region, but also in an additional topological insulator (TI) phase, characterized by a doubly-degenerate entanglement spectrum. This phase resembles the HI, but it cannot be trivially mapped to an integer spin chain. We show that the TTI and TI phases present peculiar correlations of the site occupations that we characterize using generalized string correlations, which may be measured using quantum gas microscope techniques
Gross and Bakr 2021
.
We characterize the corresponding phase transitions. In particular, the TTI (TI) phases undergo, for large-enough dipolar strength, a second-order transition
belonging to the 3-state (4-state) Potts universality class into a density wave with a period of 3 (4) sites. These phases may be realized and observed in state-of-the-art experiments.

The paper is organized as follows. In Sec.
II
we introduce the extended Hubbard model that characterizes the polar lattice gas. Section
III
reviews the phase diagram for the nearest-neighbor model.
Section
IV
discusses the phase diagram for polar lattice gases, and in particular the properties of the TTI and TI phases, as well as the involved phase transitions. Finally, we conclude in Sec.
V
.

II
Model

We consider dipolar bosons of mass
m
in a 1D optical lattice
V_{0}\sin^{2}(\pi z/a)
, where
V_{0}
is the lattice depth and

is the lattice spacing, of length
L

\frac{1}{2}m\omega_{\perp}^{2}(x^{2}+y^{2})

\displaystyle\hat{H}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-t\sum_{i}\left(\hat{b}^{\dagger}_{i+1}\hat{b}_{i}+\mathrm{H.c.}\right)

(1)

\displaystyle+

\displaystyle\frac{U}{2}\sum_{i}\hat{n}_{i}\left(\hat{n}_{i}\!-1\right)+\sum_{i}\sum_{j>0}V_{j}\hat{n}_{i}\hat{n}_{i+j},

where

\hat{b}^{\dagger}_{i}
(
\hat{b}_{i}

i

\hat{n}_{i}=\hat{b}^{\dagger}_{i}\hat{b}_{i}
the corresponding number operator, and

characterizes the on-site interactions.

The dipole-induced inter-site interactions are given by
V_{j}=VG_{j}(B)

,
where
V
is the interaction strength
between nearest-neighboring sites, and the decay of the dipolar tail is given by

G_{j}(B)=f(\sqrt{B}j)/f(\sqrt{B}),

(2)

with

f(\xi)=2\xi-\sqrt{2\pi}(1+\xi^{2})e^{\xi^{2}/2}\mathrm{erfc}(\xi/\sqrt{2}),

(3)

where

B=\frac{\pi^{2}\zeta}{2}(1-\frac{\zeta}{2\sqrt{s}})^{-1},

(4)

with
\zeta=\hbar\omega_{\perp}/E_{R}
,
s=V_{0}/E_{R}
, and
E_{R}=\frac{\pi^{2}\hbar^{2}}{2ma^{2}}

Although the dipolar tail eventually decays for large

1/j^{3}

\beta_{\text{eff}}
, with
V_{2}/V=1/2^{\beta_{\text{eff}}}

Korbmacher et al. 2023a
;
Korbmacher et al. 2023b
.
The case

corresponds to the usually assumed

). With lower

\beta_{\text{eff}}=1
(corresponding to
B\approx 0.297

In the following, we evaluate the ground state at unit filling using the infinite density-matrix renormalization group (iDMRG) technique
McCulloch 2008
, with bond dimensions up to
\chi=1200
,
and a unit cell of
12
sites, truncating the inter-site interactions
V_{j}
at
20
sites. Since we are interested in regimes of low
U/t
values, we consider in our numerical simulations up to
5
particles per site (larger occupations do not modify our conclusions). We assume that the lattice is deep-enough, such that we can neglect the effect of density-assisted hopping
Sowiński et al. 2012
;
Dutta et al. 2015
;
Kraus et al. 2022
as well as the effects due to higher bands
Ła̧cki et al. 2013
;
Hughes et al. 2023
.

NEAREST-NEIGHBOR MODEL

For the case of nearest-neighbor interactions,
V_{j}=V\delta_{j,1}

phases

(we do not

C_{\text{SP}}(i,j)=\langle\hat{b}^{\dagger}_{i}\hat{b}_{j}\rangle

, Néel, and Haldane phases of spin-

0

2

The MI displays finite parity order

\mathcal{O}_{P}=\lim_{|i-j|\rightarrow\infty}\langle e^{i\pi\sum_{i<k<j}\delta\hat{n}_{k}}\rangle.

(5)

with
\delta\hat{n}_{i}=\hat{n}_{i}-1
, and vanishing string order

\mathcal{O}_{S}=\lim_{|i-j|\rightarrow\infty}\langle\delta\hat{n}_{i}e^{i\pi\sum_{i<k<j}\delta\hat{n}_{k}}\delta\hat{n}_{j}\rangle.

(6)

The 2DW phase, occurring at sufficiently large

, shows a staggered density-density correlation revealed by a peak at momentum
k=\pi

in its structure factor

S(k)=\frac{1}{L^{2}}\sum_{i,j}e^{ik(i-j)}\langle\hat{n}_{i}\hat{n}_{j}\rangle,

(7)

with
L
the number of sites. Finally, the HI phase, occurring at intermediate
U/t
ad
V/t

{\cal O}_{S}
and vanishing
{\cal O}_{P}

The HI is further characterized
by its entanglement spectrum

\rho_{A}=\mathrm{Tr}_{B}\rho

, i.e.
P_{\Lambda}=0

P_{\Lambda}=\sqrt{\sum_{i}\left(\lambda_{2i}-\lambda_{2i+1}\right)^{2}},

(8)

where the sum is over all eigenvalues of

IV
POLAR LATTICE GASES

The dipolar tail modifies quantitatively the extension of the HI parameter region (panels (b) and (c) of Fig.
1
), but does not compromise its existence
Dalla Torre et al. 2006
. At large
V/t
, additional DW phases appear, with period
\ell=3
(3DW),
4

k=2\pi/\ell

S(k)

\ell
DW and
(\ell+1)
DW phases are separated from each other by a first-order phase transition at
(U/V)_{\ell}=2\sum_{s>0}\left[nG_{s\ell}-(\ell+1)G_{s(\ell+1)}\right]

Interestingly, for low
U/t
values, the dipolar tail results in additional phases
which we term TTI (topologically-trivial insulator) and TI (topological insulator),
which to the best of our knowledge have remained up to now unnoticed.
The TTI phase is apparent even for the case of an unmodified
1/j^{3}
dipolar tail (Fig.
1
(b)). Decreasing
\beta_{\textrm{eff}}
enhances the
TTI region and reveals the TI phase, see Fig.
1
(c).
We discuss below the nature of these unexpected phases.

IV.1
Topologically Trivial Insulator phase

IV.2
Topological Insulator phase

Remarkably, whereas reducing
V/t
induces the melting of the 3DW into the TTI, melting of the 4DW leads to a qualitatively different (TI) phase. As for the TTI, the TI phase is
an insulator with exponentially-decaying

and has no

however, the

and

IV.3
Phase transitions

Whereas the direct transitions occurring at large-enough
U/t
and
V/t
between MI and 2DW and between the
\ell

U/t

are second-order. These transitions are characterized by the behavior of the von Neumann entanglement entropy

at criticality, which we obtain from our iDMRG calculations, and which up to a constant acquires the form

S_{vN}(\chi)=\frac{c}{6}\log\xi_{\chi},

(11)

where
\chi
is the bond dimension,
\xi_{\chi}
is the

correlation length, and

is the central charge. As it is well known, the MI-HI transition is in the Luttinger liquid universality class with
c=1

;
Ejima et al. 2014
. Our results show that the HI-TTI, and TTI-TI transitions are characterized as well by

(see Fig.

;
Ejima et al. 2014

c=1/2
. In contrast, the TTI-3DW transition is well described by the minimal conformal field theory with
c=4/5
, corresponding to the 3-state Potts universality class, whereas the TI-4DW transition
is characterized by
c=1
, corresponding to the 4-state Potts universality class

V
Conclusions

For one-dimensional polar lattice gases at unit filling, the long-range tail of the dipolar interaction does not only result, as expected, in a quantitative modication of the Haldane-insulator parameter region, and in the existence of density waves with larger integer spatial periods, but also in the unexpected appearance of additional insulating phases. These spatially disordered phases present peculiar correlations of site occupations, and may be either topological (TI) or topologically trivial (TTI).
For a
1/r^{3}
dipolar tail, the TTI is found for
2\lesssim V/t\lesssim 5
, readily accessible in on-going erbium experiments
Su et al. 2023
. The observation of the TI demands softening the transversal confinement
to modify the decay of the dipolar tail. Since this softening reduces
V/t
, the observation of the TI phase may demand the use of Dy in UV lattices, or the use of polar molecules, which should be both available in the near future.
