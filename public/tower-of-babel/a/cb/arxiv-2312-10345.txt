Multi-Target Two-way Integrated Sensing and Communications with Full Duplex MIMO Radios
Muhammad Talha 1 , Besma Smida 1 , Md Atiqul Islam 2 , and George C. Alexandropoulos 3

Abstract
In this paper, we propose a multiple input multiple output (MIMO) Full-Duplex Integrated Sensing and Communication System consisting of multiple targets, a single downlink, and a single uplink user. We employed signal-to-interference plus noise ratio (SINR) as the performance metric for radar, downlink, and uplink communication. We use a communication-centric approach in which communication waveform is used for both communication and sensing of the environment. We develop a sensing algorithm capable of estimating direction of arrival (DoA) , range, and velocity of each target. We also propose a joint optimization framework for designing A/D transmit and receive beamformers to improve radar, downlink, and uplink SINRs while minimizing self-interference (SI) leakage. We also propose a null space projection (NSP) based approach to improve the uplink rate. Our simulation results, considering Orthogonal Frequency Division Multiplexing (OFDM) waveform, show accurate radar parameter estimation with improved downlink and uplink rate.

II-A

Channel Models

For
DL
communication, it is assumed that there are
L
scatterers present in the environment, which help to realize different
line of sight (LOS)
multipaths in

channel. As
mmWave

paths are neglected. Hence, the

\mathbf{H}_{\text{DL}}=\sum_{l=1}^{L}\alpha_{l}\mathbf{a}_{M_{u}}(\theta_{l})\mathbf{a}_{N_{b}}^{\text{H}}(\theta_{l}),

(2)

where
\alpha_{l}\in\mathbb{C}
and
\theta_{l}\in[-90\degree,90\degree]
is the channel coefficient and angular direction of
l
-th path respectively. Moreover,
\mathbf{a}(\theta)
is the
uniform linear array (ULA)

\mathbf{a}_{N_{b}}(\theta)=\left[1,e^{j\frac{2\pi}{\lambda}d\sin(\theta)},\cdots,e^{j\frac{2\pi}{\lambda}(N_{b}-1)d\sin(\theta)}\right]^{\text{T}},

where
\lambda
is the signal wavelength and
d
is the inter-element spacing of the antenna array.
For
UL
communication, only one direct

path is assumed. i.e.,

\mathbf{H}_{\text{UL}}=\beta\mathbf{a}_{M_{b}}(\phi)\mathbf{a}_{N_{u}}^{\text{H}}(\phi),

(3)

where
\beta
is the
UL
channel coefficient and
\phi
is the angular direction of user.

For sensing operation, it is assumed that there are
M
passive radar targets,
L

DL
scatterers, and a single LOS reflection from
UL
user is considered. Hence total
K
sensing targets are assumed i.e.,
K=M+L+1
. The purpose of the sensing operation is to estimate the angular direction (Direction of Arrival (DoA)), range, and velocity of each radar target. The range and velocity of each target
k
correspond to time delay
\tau_{k}
and Doppler frequency
f_{D,k}
respectively, such that
\tau_{k}=\frac{2d_{k}}{c}
and
f_{D,k}=2v_{k}f_{c}/c
, where

v_{k}
is the velocity of
k
-th target and

is the carrier frequency. Hence the radar channel at
p
-th subcarrier and
q
-th OFDM symbol can be given as

\mathbf{H}_{\text{Rad}}^{p,q}=\sum_{k=1}^{K}\alpha_{k}e^{j2\pi\left(qT_{s}f_{D,k}-p\tau_{k}\Delta f\right)}\mathbf{a}_{M_{b}}(\theta_{k})\mathbf{a}_{N_{b}}^{\text{H}}(\theta_{k}),

(4)

where
\alpha_{k}
is the reflection coefficient of the
k
-th radar target,
\Delta f
is the subcarrier spacing and
T_{s}=\frac{1}{\Delta f}+T_{cp}
is the total symbol duration (including the cyclic prefix) and
\theta_{k}
is the angular direction of the
k
-th target. The two-way propagation delay due to the range of the target translates to a phase shift across each subcarrier and the Doppler shift due to the moving velocity translates to a phase shift across each OFDM symbol. Moreover, in order to be consistent with
UL
channel, it is assumed that
\theta_{K}=\phi
.

III
Sensing Parameter Estimation

In this section, we present the parameter estimation of the sensing targets utilizing the received signal at the BS node
b
. We estimate the DoA, range, and velocity of the targets using both reflected and uplink signals at the BS RX.

First, we estimate the
DoAs
of targets including the direction of the DL and UL user using
MUltiple SIgnal Classification (MUSIC)

[
18
]
parameter estimation algorithm. After receiving the
PQ
snapshots of the reflected signal, we calculate the sample covariance matrix of (
7
). As the covariance matrix is positive semi-definite, after eigenvalue decomposition, all the eigenvalues will be real and non-negative. Now assuming
K<M_{b}^{\text{RF}}
, the subspace corresponding to the first
K
eigenvalues will be signal space while the remaining subspace will correspond to noise subspace. In order to find the
DoAs
,
\widehat{\theta_{k}},\forall k
, we calculate the vectors orthogonal to noise subspace by sweeping through all the angles in angular range.

Second, we estimate the range and velocity of the radar targets, including DL and UL users, by finding out the delay and Doppler shift associated with the
K
estimated DoAs. We formulate a reference signal in the
k
th target direction as

\begin{split}\mathbf{g}_{b,k}^{p,q}=\mathbf{a}_{M_{b}}(\widehat{\theta}_{k})\mathbf{a}_{N_{b}}^{\rm H}(\widehat{\theta}_{k})\mathbf{x}_{b}^{p,q}.\end{split}

(9)

The reference signal
\mathbf{g}_{b,k}^{p,q}
in the direction of
\widehat{\theta}_{k}
and the RX signal
\widetilde{\mathbf{y}}_{p,q}
in (
7
) are utilized to derive quotient that includes the effect of delay and Doppler shift as

\begin{split}z_{b,k}^{p,q}=\frac{1}{M_{b}}\sum\limits_{i=1}^{M_{b}}[\mathbf{W}_{b}^{\rm RF}\widetilde{\mathbf{y}}_{b}^{p,q}]_{i}/[\mathbf{g}_{b}^{p,q}]_{i},\,\,\forall p,q.\end{split}

(10)

Now, we estimate the quantized delay and Doppler shift associated with
k
th radar target as

\begin{split}({n}^{*},{m}^{*})\!=\!\underset{n,m}{\text{arg max}}\Big|\!\sum\limits_{p=0}^{P-1}\!\Big(\!\!\sum\limits_{q=0}^{Q-1}z_{b}^{p,q}e^{-j2\pi\frac{qm}{Q}}\Big)e^{j2\pi\frac{pn}{P}}\Big|^{2}\end{split}

(11)

where
n=0,\cdots,P-1
and
m=-Q/2,\cdots,Q/2-1
. Finally, the estimated delay and Doppler frequency are expressed as
\widehat{\tau}_{k}=\frac{{n}^{*}}{P\Delta f}
and
\widehat{f}_{D,k}=\frac{{m}^{*}}{QT_{s}}
, respectively.

IV
Proposed ISAC Optimization Framework

In this section, we propose an optimization framework for designing A/D beamformers such that the DL rate, sensing performance and UL rate get maximized while constraining
SI

We consider a time division duplexing communication protocol, where the

(12)

where
\widehat{\mathbf{H}}_{\text{Rad}}=\sum_{i-1}^{K-1}\mathbf{a}_{M_{b}}(\widehat{\theta}_{i})\mathbf{a}_{N_{b}}^{\text{H}}(\widehat{\theta}_{i})+\mathbf{a}_{M_{b}}(\widehat{\phi})\mathbf{a}_{N_{b}}^{\text{H}}(\widehat{\phi})
,
\widehat{\widetilde{\mathbf{H}}}_{b,b}=(\mathbf{W}_{b}^{\text{RF}})^{\text{H}}\mathbf{H}_{b,b}\mathbf{V}_{b}^{\text{RF}}
and
\mathbf{C}_{b}
. It is to be noted that the

, because we consider

where
\widehat{\mathbf{H}}_{\text{DL}}=\sum_{i=1}^{L}\mathbf{a}_{M_{u}}(\widehat{\theta}_{i})\mathbf{a}_{N_{b}}^{\text{H}}(\widehat{\theta}_{i})

Moreover, for

\gamma_{\text{UL}}=\frac{||\left(\mathbf{W}_{b}^{\text{BB}}\right)^{\text{H}}\left(\mathbf{W}_{b}^{\text{RF}}\right)^{\text{H}}\widehat{\mathbf{H}}_{\text{UL}}\mathbf{V}_{u}^{\text{BB}}||_{\text{2}}^{2}}{\Sigma_{\text{UL}}}

(14)

where

VI
Conclusion

In this article, we presented
FD-ISAC
communication system with multiple targets and
UL
and
DL
communication users. We proposed a complete framework for maximizing the
DL
and
UL
rate performance while also estimating the targets’ radar parameters (
DoA
, range and velocity). We optimize the A/D beamformers at both
TX
and
RX
of
BS
and users to increase the corresponding
SINRs
and to minimize the
SI
leakage power at
BS
. Our results demonstrated accurate radar parameter estimation and increased
DL
and
UL
rates. Moreover, our proposed algorithm manages to provide a high
DL
rate even at high
SI
leakage.
