Geometric Upper Critical Dimensions of the Ising Model
Sheng Fang, Zongzheng Zhou, Youjin Deng

Abstract
The upper critical dimension of the Ising model is known to be d c = 4 d_{c}=4 , above which critical behavior is regarded as trivial. We hereby argue from extensive simulations that, in the random-cluster representation, the Ising model simultaneously exhibits two upper critical dimensions at ( d c = 4 , d p = 6 ) (d_{c}=4,d_{p}=6) , and critical clusters for d ≥ d p d\geq d_{p} , except the largest one, are governed by exponents from percolation universality. We predict a rich variety of geometric properties and then provide strong evidence in dimensions from 4 to 7 and on complete graphs. Our findings significantly advance the understanding of the Ising model, which is a fundamental system in many branches of physics.

Models–
The Hamiltonian of the Ising model reads

{\cal H}=-K\sum_{i\sim j}S_{i}S_{j}\;,\hskip 14.22636pt(S_{i}=\pm 1)

(1)

By the FK transformation, it can be mapped onto the

with the partition function

{\cal Z}=\sum_{A\subseteq G}p^{|A|}(1-p)^{|E\backslash A|}Q^{c(A)}\;,

(2)

G\equiv(V,E)

A\subseteq G
,

|A|

respectively represent the number of occupied bonds and of clusters,
and the bond probability is

We simulate the FK-Ising model on

We also simulate bond percolation in 7D at criticality

Evidence for
d_{c}=4
from finite-size scaling–
In the spin representation,

It is now believed

with exponent

Y. Deng 2017

Z. Zhou, J. Grimm, S. Fang, Y. Deng, and T. M.

Conclusion–
Based on a combination of extensive simulations
from
d=4
to 7 and insights from RG theory,
and rigorous and numerical results for CG,
we propose that, in the FK random-cluster representation,
the Ising model simultaneously has
two upper critical dimensions at
(d_{c}=4,d_{p}=6)
.
Besides being an answer for the long-standing debate, dated back to 1970s, this picture provides
a counter-intuitive and advanced understanding
for the Ising model, which is probably the most fundamental
system in statistical and condensed-matter physics.
Note that the scenario of two upper critical dimensions was also proposed in the field-theoretical treatment of the
\rm{CP}^{1}
model
Nahum et al. 2013
.

In FK-Ising clusters for
d\geq 4

Two pronounced features can be seen: 1), as long as

two-scale properties, two scaling windows and two configuration sectors;
and 2), for

are in percolation universality, unexpected from the first sight.
Interestingly, while the geometric properties are very sophisticated,
critical behaviors in the spin representation are much simpler:
no percolation-like behaviors exist and the upper critical dimension
d_{p}=6
cannot be seen.
