1.
Introduction

In 1997, L. van Hamme
[
21
]
proposed many conjectural
p
-adic supercongruences
motivated by corresponding Ramanujan-type series for
1/\pi
. For example, he conjectured the supercongruence

\sum_{n=0}^{(p-1)/2}\frac{6n+1}{256^{n}}\binom{2n}{n}^{3}\equiv(-1)^{(p-1)/2}p\ ({\rm{mod}}\ p^{4})

(1.1)

for any prime
p>3
, inspired by the Ramanujan series (cf.
[
14
]
)

\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{6n+1}{256^{n}}\binom{2n}{n}^{3}=\frac{4}{\pi}.

The congruence
(
1.1
)
was confirmed by L. Long
[
8
]
in 2011.

In 2011 Z.-W. Sun
[
16
]
formulated many conjectural supercongruences involving Bernoulli numbers or Euler numbers. Recall that the Bernoulli numbers
B_{0},B_{1},\ldots
and the Euler numbers

E_{0},E_{1},\ldots
are defined by

\frac{x}{e^{x}-1}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}B_{n}\frac{x^{n}}{n!}\ (|x|<2\pi)\ \text{and}\ \frac{2}{e^{x}+e^{-x}}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}E_{n}\frac{x^{n}}{n!}\ \left(|x|<\frac{\pi}{2}\right)

respectively. For example, he conjectured the congruence

\sum_{n=0}^{p-1}\frac{6n+1}{256^{n}}\binom{2n}{n}^{3}\equiv(-1)^{(p-1)/2}p-p^{3}E_{p-3}\ ({\rm{mod}}\ p^{4})

(1.2)

for any prime
p>3
. This was later confirmed by G.-S. Mao and C.-W. Wen
[
11
, Th. 1.2]
.

In 2019 Z.-W. Sun
[
18
, Conj. 22]
conjectured that for any prime
p>3
and positive odd integer
m
we have

\displaystyle\frac{16^{m-1}}{(pm)^{4}\binom{m-1}{\frac{m-1}{2}}^{3}}\bigg(\sum_{n=0}^{\frac{pm-1}{2}}\frac{6n+1}{256^{n}}\binom{2n}{n}^{3}-(-1)^{(p-1)/2}p\sum_{r=0}^{\frac{m-1}{2}}\frac{6r+1}{256^{r}}\binom{2r}{r}^{3}\bigg)

\displaystyle\equiv(-1)^{(p-1)/2}\frac{7}{24}B_{p-3}\ ({\rm{mod}}\ p).

In this paper we confirm this in the case
m=1
. Namely, we establish the following result.

Theorem 1.1
.

Let
p>3
be a prime. Then

\sum_{n=0}^{(p-1)/2}\frac{6n+1}{256^{n}}\binom{2n}{n}^{3}\equiv p(-1)^{(p-1)/2}+(-1)^{(p-1)/2}\frac{7}{24}p^{4}B_{p-3}\ ({\rm{mod}}\ p^{5}).

(1.3)

Another similar congruence modulo
p^{5}
states that

\sum_{n=0}^{p-1}\frac{3n+1}{16^{n}}\binom{2n}{n}^{3}\equiv p+\frac{7}{6}p^{4}\ ({\rm{mod}}\ p^{5})

for any prime
p>3
, which was conjectured by Sun
[
16
]
in 2011 and
confirmed by C. Wang and D.-W. Hu
[
22
]
in 2020.

In the next section, we provide some known lemmas.
We will use the WZ method to prove Theorem
1.1
in Section 3.

2.
Some known lemmas

In 1862 J. Wolstenholme
[
23
]

\binom{2p-1}{p-1}\equiv 1\ ({\rm{mod}}\ p^{3})

for any prime
p>3

[
2

Lemma 2.1

(Glaisher
[
2
]
)
.

For any prime
p>3
, we have

\binom{2p-1}{p-1}\equiv 1-\frac{2}{3}p^{3}B_{p-3}\ ({\rm{mod}}\ p^{4}).

(2.1)

Remark 2.2
.

For modern references about (
2.1
), the reader may consult
[
12
]
and
[
4
]
.

In 1895, F. Morley
[
13
]
got the following fundamental congruence:

\binom{p-1}{(p-1)/2}\equiv(-1)^{(p-1)/2}4^{p-1}\ ({\rm{mod}}\ p^{3})

for any prime
p>3
. This was refined by L. Carlitz
[
1
]
in 1953.

Lemma 2.3

(Carlitz
[
1
]
)
.

For each odd prime
p
, we have

(-1)^{(p-1)/2}\binom{p-1}{(p-1)/2}\equiv 4^{p-1}+\frac{p^{3}}{12}B_{p-3}\ ({\rm{mod}}\ p^{4}).

We also need the following result of E. Lehmer established in 1938.

Lemma 2.4

(E. Lehmer
[
7
]
)
.

For any prime
p>3
, we have

\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}\frac{1}{k}\equiv-2\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}\frac{1}{2k-1}\equiv-2q_{p}(2)+p\,q_{p}(2)^{2}\ ({\rm{mod}}\ p^{2}),

(2.2)

where
q_{p}(2)
denotes the Fermat quotient
(2^{p-1}-1)/p
.

Let
a_{1},a_{2},\ldots,a_{m}
be integers. For any integer
n\geq m
, we define the alternating multiple harmonic sum

H(a_{1},a_{2},\ldots,a_{m};n):=\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}1\leq k_{1}<k_{2}<\ldots<k_{m}\leq n\end{subarray}}\prod_{i=1}^{m}\frac{\mbox{sign}(a_{i})^{k_{i}}}{k_{i}^{|a_{i}|}},

and call
m
and
\sum_{i=1}^{m}|a_{i}|
its
depth
and
weight
respectively. For convenience, we simply write
H_{n}
to stand for
H(1;n)
.

We need the following known results as lemmas.

Lemma 2.5

(
[
6
]
)
.

Let
a,r\in\mathbb{Z}^{+}=\{1,2,3,\ldots\}
, For any prime
p>ar+2
, we have

\displaystyle H(\{a\}^{r};p-1)\equiv\begin{cases}(-1)^{r}\frac{a(ar+1)}{2(ar+2)}p^{2}B_{p-ar-2}\ &\ ({\rm{mod}}\ p^{3})\qquad\text{if}\ ar\ \text{is odd},\\
(-1)^{r-1}\frac{a}{ar+1}pB_{p-ar-1}\ &\ ({\rm{mod}}\ p^{2})\qquad\text{if}\ ar\ \text{is even}.\end{cases}

Lemma 2.6

(
[
15
]
)
.

For any
a\in\mathbb{Z}^{+}
and prime
p>a+2
, we have

\displaystyle H\left(a;\frac{p-1}{2}\right)

\displaystyle\equiv\begin{cases}-2q_{p}(2)+pq_{p}(2)^{2}-\frac{2}{3}p^{2}q_{p}(2)^{3}-\frac{7}{12}p^{2}B_{p-3}\ ({\rm{mod}}\ p^{3})&\text{if}\ a=1,\\
-\frac{2^{a}-2}{a}B_{p-a}\ ({\rm{mod}}\ p)&\text{if $a>1$ is odd},\\
\frac{a(2^{a+1}-1)}{2(a+1)}pB_{p-a-1}\ ({\rm{mod}}\ p^{2})&\text{if $a$ is even}.\end{cases}

Lemma 2.7

(
[
6
]
)
.

For any
a,b\in\mathbb{Z}^{+}
and any prime
p>a+b+1
, we have

H(a,b;p-1)\equiv\frac{(-1)^{b}}{a+b}\binom{a+b}{a}B_{p-a-b}\ ({\rm{mod}}\ p).

Lemma 2.8

(
[
19
]
)
.

For any prime
p>3
, we have

\displaystyle H(1,-2;p-1)

\displaystyle\equiv H(-1,2;p-1)\equiv H(2,-1;p-1)

\displaystyle\equiv H(-2,1;p-1)\equiv\frac{1}{4}B_{p-3}\ ({\rm{mod}}\ p)

(

)

Let

H\left(a,b;\frac{p-1}{2}\right)\equiv\frac{B_{p-a-b}}{2(a+b)}\left((-1)^{b}\binom{a+b}{a}+2^{a+b}-2\right)\ ({\rm{mod}}\ p).

3.
Proof of Theorem
1.1

We will use the following WZ pair appeared in
[
3
]
to prove Theorem
1.1
. For
n,k\in\mathbb{N}=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}
, we define

F(n,k)=\frac{(6n-2k+1)}{2^{8n-2k}}\frac{\binom{2n}{n}\binom{2n+2k}{n+k}\binom{2n-2k}{n-k}\binom{n+k}{n}}{\binom{2k}{k}}

and

G(n,k)=\frac{n^{2}\binom{2n}{n}\binom{2n+2k}{n+k}\binom{2n-2k}{n-k}\binom{n+k}{n}}{2^{8n-2k-4}(2n+2k-1)\binom{2k}{k}}.

Clearly
F(n,k)=G(n,k)=0
if
n<k
. It is easy to check that

F(n,k-1)-F(n,k)=G(n+1,k)-G(n,k)

(3.1)

for all
n\in\mathbb{N}
and
k\in\mathbb{Z}^{+}
.

Summing (
3.1
) over
n\in\{0,\ldots,(p-1)/2\}
we get

\sum_{n=0}^{(p-1)/2}F(n,k-1)-\sum_{n=0}^{(p-1)/2}F(n,k)=G\left(\frac{p+1}{2},k\right)-G(0,k)=G\left(\frac{p+1}{2},k\right).

Furthermore, summing both side of the above identity over
k\in\{1,\ldots,(p-1)/2\}
, we obtain

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{(p-1)/2}F(n,0)=F\left(\frac{p-1}{2},\frac{p-1}{2}\right)+\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}G\left(\frac{p+1}{2},k\right).

(3.2)

Lemma 3.1
.

Let
p>3
be a prime. Then

\displaystyle F\left(\frac{p-1}{2},\frac{p-1}{2}\right)

\displaystyle\equiv(-1)^{\frac{p-1}{2}}p\left(1-pq_{p}(2)+p^{2}q_{p}(2)^{2}-p^{3}q_{p}(2)^{3}-\frac{7}{12}p^{3}B_{p-3}\right)\ ({\rm{mod}}\ p^{5}).

Proof.

By the definition of
F(n,k)
, we have

\displaystyle F\left(\frac{p-1}{2},\frac{p-1}{2}\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{2p-1}{2^{3p-3}}\binom{2p-2}{p-1}\binom{p-1}{(p-1)/2}=\frac{p\binom{2p-1}{p-1}\binom{p-1}{(p-1)/2}}{2^{3p-3}}.

This, together with Lemma
2.1
, Lemma
2.3
and the equality
2^{p-1}=1+pq_{p}(2)
, yields that

\displaystyle F\left(\frac{p-1}{2},\frac{p-1}{2}\right)\equiv\frac{p(1-\frac{2}{3}p^{3}B_{p-3})(-1)^{(p-1)/2}(4^{p-1}+\frac{1}{12}p^{3}B_{p-3})}{(1+pq_{p}(2))^{3}}

\displaystyle\equiv(-1)^{\frac{p-1}{2}}p\left(1-pq_{p}(2)+p^{2}q_{p}(2)^{2}-p^{3}q_{p}(2)^{3}-\frac{7}{12}p^{3}B_{p-3}\right)\ ({\rm{mod}}\ p^{5}).

This concludes the proof.

Lemma 3.2
.

For any prime
p>3
, we have

\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}\frac{(p/2-k)}{(p+1-2k)(p+2k)}

\displaystyle\equiv\frac{1}{2}q_{p}(2)-\frac{p}{4}q_{p}(2)^{2}-2pq_{p}(2)+\frac{1}{6}p^{2}q_{p}(2)^{3}

\displaystyle+4p^{2}q_{p}(2)+p^{2}q_{p}(2)^{2}+\frac{7}{48}p^{2}B_{p-3}\ ({\rm{mod}}\ p^{3}).

Proof.

In view of Lemma
2.4

\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}\frac{1}{k(2k-1)}

\displaystyle=2\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}\frac{1}{2k-1}-H_{(p-1)/2}\equiv 4q_{p}(2)-2pq_{p}(2)^{2}\ ({\rm{mod}}\ p^{2})

(3.3)

and

\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}\frac{1}{k(2k-1)^{2}}

\displaystyle=H_{(p-1)/2}-2\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}\frac{1}{2k-1}+2\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}\frac{1}{(2k-1)^{2}}

\displaystyle\equiv-4q_{p}(2)+\frac{1}{2}H(2;(p-1)/2)

\displaystyle\equiv-4q_{p}(2)\ ({\rm{mod}}\ p).

(3.4)

It is easy to see that

\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}\frac{(p/2-k)}{(p+1-2k)(p+2k)}

\displaystyle\equiv-\frac{1}{4}H_{(p-1)/2}-\frac{p}{2}\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}\frac{1}{k(2k-1)}-p^{2}\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}\frac{1}{k(2k-1)^{2}}.

Then we immediately obtain the desired result by Lemma
2.4
, (
3
) and (
3

Lemma 3.3
.

For any prime
p>3
, we have

\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}\frac{(p/2-k)H_{k}}{(p+1-2k)(p+2k)}

\displaystyle\equiv 2q_{p}(2)-q_{p}(2)^{2}-6pq_{p}(2)+2pq_{p}(2)^{2}+pq_{p}(2)^{3}+\frac{7}{12}pB_{p-3}\ ({\rm{mod}}\ p^{2}).

\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}\frac{H_{2k}}{k}

\displaystyle=H(1,1;p-1)+H(1,-1;p-1)+\frac{1}{2}H(2;(p-1)/2)

\displaystyle\equiv q_{p}(2)^{2}-pq_{p}(2)^{3}+\frac{7}{24}pB_{p-3}\ ({\rm{mod}}\ p^{2}).

(3.5)

Noting

\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}\frac{H_{k}}{k}

\displaystyle\equiv 2q_{p}(2)^{2}-2pq_{p}(2)^{3}+\frac{7}{6}pB_{p-3}\ ({\rm{mod}}\ p^{2}).

(3.6)

It is easy to see that

\displaystyle H_{(p+1)/2-k}

\displaystyle\equiv\frac{2}{p+1-2k}+2pH(2;2k)-\frac{p}{2}H(2;k)+H_{(p-1)/2}+2H_{2k}-H_{k}\ ({\rm{mod}}\ p^{2}).

This, together with (

\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}\frac{H_{(p+1)/2-k}}{k}

\displaystyle\equiv-8q_{p}(2)+4q_{p}(2)^{2}+4pq_{p}(2)^{2}+8pq_{p}(2)

\displaystyle-4pq_{p}(2)^{3}-\frac{7}{3}pB_{p-3}\ ({\rm{mod}}\ p^{2})

(3.7)

and

\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}\frac{H_{(p+1)/2-k}}{k(2k-1)}

\displaystyle\equiv\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}\frac{H_{k}}{k(2k-1)}

\displaystyle\equiv-\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}\frac{H_{(p+1)/2-k}}{k}-\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}\frac{H_{k}}{k}

Lemma 3.7
.

For any prime
p>3
, we have

\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}\frac{(p/2-k)H_{2k}^{2}}{(p+1-2k)(p+2k)}\equiv q_{p}(2)-q_{p}(2)^{2}+\frac{1}{6}q_{p}(2)^{3}+\frac{1}{3}B_{p-3}\ ({\rm{mod}}\ p).

Proof.

Replacing
k
by
(p+1)/2-j
in (
3
), we have

\sum_{j=1}^{(p-1)/2}\frac{1}{(2j-1)j^{2}}\equiv 8q_{p}(2)\ ({\rm{mod}}\ p),

and in view of
[
10
, Lemma 2.4, (3.12)]
and (ii), we can deduce that

\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}\frac{H_{2k-2}}{k^{2}}\equiv-8q_{p}(2)+\frac{5}{2}B_{p-3}\ ({\rm{mod}}\ p).

This, together with (
3.10
) and (
3
), yields that

\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}\frac{(p/2-k)H_{2k}^{2}}{(p+1-2k)(p+2k)}

\displaystyle\equiv-\frac{1}{4}\left(\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}\frac{H_{2k}H_{2k-2}}{k}-\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}\frac{H_{2k-2}}{k(2k-1)}-\frac{1}{2}\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}\frac{H_{2k-2}}{k^{2}}\right)

\displaystyle\equiv q_{p}(2)-q_{p}(2)^{2}+\frac{1}{6}q_{p}(2)^{3}+\frac{1}{3}B_{p-3}\ ({\rm{mod}}\ p).

This ends the proof.

Lemma 3.8
.

Let
p>3
be a prime. Then

\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}\frac{(p/2-k)H(2;2k)}{(p+1-2k)(p+2k)}\equiv q_{p}(2)-\frac{3}{16}B_{p-3}\ ({\rm{mod}}\ p),

\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}\frac{(p/2-k)H(2;k)}{(p+1-2k)(p+2k)}\equiv 4q_{p}(2)-\frac{7}{8}B_{p-3}\ ({\rm{mod}}\ p).

Proof.

In view of
[
10
, (2.3)]
, (
3
) and Lemma
2.6
, we have

\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}\frac{(p/2-k)H(2;2k)}{(p+1-2k)(p+2k)}

\displaystyle\equiv\frac{1}{4}\left(\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}\frac{H(2;2k)}{k}-\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}\frac{1}{k(2k-1)^{2}}-\frac{1}{4}\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}\frac{1}{k^{3}}\right)

\displaystyle\equiv q_{p}(2)-\frac{3}{16}B_{p-3}\ ({\rm{mod}}\ p).

It is easy to see that

\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}\frac{(p/2-k)H(2;k)}{(p+1-2k)(p+2k)}

\displaystyle\equiv\frac{1}{2}\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}\frac{H(2;(p+1)/2-k)}{p-2k}

\displaystyle\equiv\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}\frac{H(2;2k-2)}{k}-\frac{1}{4}\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}\frac{H(2;k-1)}{k}.

Observe that

\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}\frac{H(2;2k-2)}{k}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}\frac{H(2;2k)}{k}-\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}\frac{1}{k(2k-1)^{2}}-\frac{1}{4}\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}\frac{1}{k^{3}}

\displaystyle\equiv 4q_{p}(2)-\frac{3}{4}B_{p-3}\ ({\rm{mod}}\ p)

and

\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}\frac{H(2;k-1)}{k}=\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}\frac{H(2;k)}{k}-\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}\frac{1}{k^{3}}\equiv\frac{1}{2}B_{p-3}\ ({\rm{mod}}\ p).

So

\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}\frac{(p/2-k)H(2;k)}{(p+1-2k)(p+2k)}\equiv 4q_{p}(2)-\frac{7}{8}B_{p-3}\ ({\rm{mod}}\ p).

Therefore the proof of Lemma
3.8
is complete.

Lemma 3.9
.

For any primes
p>3
, we have

\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}G\left(\frac{p+1}{2},k\right)

\displaystyle\equiv(-1)^{\frac{p-1}{2}}p^{2}\left(q_{p}(2)-pq_{p}(2)^{2}+p^{2}q_{p}(2)^{3}+\frac{7}{8}p^{3}B_{p-3}\right)\ ({\rm{mod}}\ p^{5}).

Proof.

For any complex number
a
, let
(a)_{0}=1
and
(a)_{n}=a(a+1)\ldots(a+n-1)

for
n\in\mathbb{Z}^{+}
.
By the definition of
G(n,k)
, we have

\displaystyle G(n,k)

\displaystyle=\frac{n^{2}\binom{2n}{n}\binom{2n+2k}{n+k}\binom{2n-2k}{n-k}\binom{n+k}{n}}{2^{8n-4-2k}(2n+2k-1)\binom{2k}{k}}=\frac{n^{2}\binom{2n}{n}\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)_{n+k}\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)_{n-k}\binom{n}{k}}{2^{4n-4-2k}n!^{2}(2n+2k-1)\binom{2k}{k}}

\displaystyle=\frac{n^{2}\binom{2n}{n}\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)_{n}\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)_{n-1}\left(\frac{1}{2}+n\right)_{k}\binom{n}{k}}{2^{4n-4-2k}n!^{2}\left(\frac{1}{2}+n-k\right)_{k-1}(2n+2k-1)\binom{2k}{k}}

\displaystyle=\frac{n\binom{2n}{n}^{2}\binom{2n-2}{n-1}\left(\frac{1}{2}+n\right)_{k}\binom{n}{k}}{2^{8n-6-2k}n!^{2}\left(\frac{1}{2}+n-k\right)_{k-1}(2n+2k-1)\binom{2k}{k}},

(3.14)

where we have used the equalities

\frac{\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)_{n+k}}{(n+k)!}=\frac{\binom{2n+2k}{n+k}}{4^{n+k}},

\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)_{n+k}=\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)_{n}\left(\frac{1}{2}+n\right)_{k}

and

\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)_{n-k}\left(\frac{1}{2}+n-k\right)_{k-1}=\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)_{n-1}\ \ (1\leq k\leq n).

It is easy to check that

\displaystyle\frac{\left(\frac{p}{2}+1\right)_{k}}{\left(\frac{p}{2}-k\right)_{k}}

\displaystyle\equiv\frac{k!\left(1+\frac{p}{2}H_{k}+\frac{p^{2}}{4}\sum_{1\leq i<j\leq k}\frac{1}{ij}\right)}{(-1)^{k}k!(\left(1-\frac{p}{2}H_{k}+\frac{p^{2}}{4}\sum_{1\leq i<j\leq k}\frac{1}{ij}\right)}

\displaystyle\equiv(-1)^{k}\left(1+pH_{k}+\frac{p^{2}}{2}H_{k}^{2}\right)\ ({\rm{mod}}\ p^{3}).

In view of
[
17
, (4.4)]
, we have the following congruence modulo
p^{3}

\displaystyle\frac{\binom{(p-1)/2}{k}(-4)^{k}}{\binom{2k}{k}}\equiv 1-p\sum_{j=1}^{k}\frac{1}{2j-1}+\frac{p^{2}}{2}\left(\left(\sum_{j=1}^{k}\frac{1}{2j-1}\right)^{2}-\sum_{j=1}^{k}\frac{1}{(2j-1)^{2}}\right)

\displaystyle=1-p\left(H_{2k}-\frac{1}{2}H_{k}\right)+\frac{p^{2}}{2}\left(\left(H_{2k}-\frac{1}{2}H_{k}\right)^{2}-H(2;2k)+\frac{1}{4}H(2;k)\right).

By (
3
), we have the following congruence modulo
p^{5}

\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}G\left(\frac{p+1}{2},k\right)\equiv\frac{(p+1)^{2}\binom{p+1}{(p+1)/2}^{2}\binom{p-1}{(p-1)/2}}{2^{4p-1}}\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}\frac{(p/2-k)}{(p+1-2k)(p+2k)}

\displaystyle\cdot\left(1+\frac{3p}{2}H_{k}-pH_{2k}+\frac{9p^{2}}{8}H_{k}^{2}-\frac{3p^{2}}{2}H_{k}H_{2k}+\frac{p^{2}}{2}H_{2k}^{2}-\frac{p^{2}}{2}\left(H(2;2k)-\frac{H(2;k)}{4}\right)\right).

In view of Lemmas

\displaystyle\equiv\frac{(p+1)^{2}\binom{p+1}{\frac{p+1}{2}}^{2}\binom{p-1}{\frac{p-1}{2}}}{2^{4p-1}}\left(\frac{1}{2}q_{p}(2)-\frac{3}{2}pq_{p}(2)^{2}+3p^{2}q_{p}(2)^{3}+\frac{7}{16}p^{2}B_{p-3}\right)

\displaystyle\equiv\frac{2p^{2}\binom{p-1}{(p-1)/2}^{3}}{2^{4p-4}}\left(\frac{1}{2}q_{p}(2)-\frac{3}{2}pq_{p}(2)^{2}+3p^{2}q_{p}(2)^{3}+\frac{7}{16}p^{2}B_{p-3}\right)
