Congruences concerning quadrinomial coefficients
Mohammed Mechacha

Abstract
In this paper, we establish congruences (mod p 2 p^{2} ) involving the quadrinomial coefficients ( n ​ p − 1 p − 1 ) 3 \dbinom{np-1}{p-1}_{3} and ( n ​ p − 1 p − 1 2 ) 3 . \dbinom{np-1}{\frac{p-1}{2}}_{3}. This is an analogue of congruences involving the trinomial coefficients ( n ​ p − 1 p − 1 ) 2 \dbinom{np-1}{p-1}_{2} and ( n ​ p − 1 p − 1 2 ) 2 \dbinom{np-1}{\frac{p-1}{2}}_{2} due to Elkhiri and Mihoubi. 2010 Mathematics Subject Classification. — 11B65, 11A07, 05A10.

Introduction and statements of results

Several mathematicians studied, for a prime number
p
, congruences modulo powers of
p
involving the binomial coefficients
\dbinom{2p-1}{p-1}

\dbinom{p-1}{\frac{p-1}{2}}

In

described some properties and congruences involving the trinomial coefficients
\dbinom{n}{k}_{2}
defined by

\left(1+x+x^{2}\right)^{n}=\overset{2n}{\underset{k=0}{\sum}}\binom{n}{k}_{2}x^{k}.

In

Elkhiri
and

[

studied congruences modulo
p^{2}
for the trinomial coefficients
\dbinom{np-1}{p-1}_{2}
and
\dbinom{np-1}{\frac{p-1}{2}}_{2}.
More precisely, they proved the following result.

Theorem

(
[
5
]
, Theorem 1)
.

Let
p\geq 5
be a prime number and
n
be a positive integer. We have

\hskip-51.21504pt\binom{np-1}{p-1}_{2}\equiv\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\hskip-2.84526pt1+npq_{p}(3)\hskip 2.84526pt(\mathrm{mod}\hskip 0.85358ptp^{2})&\text{if}\hskip 4.2679ptp\equiv 1\,(\mathrm{mod}\hskip 0.85358pt3),\\
\hskip-2.84526pt-1-npq_{p}(3)\hskip 2.84526pt(\mathrm{mod}\hskip 0.85358ptp^{2})&\text{if}\hskip 4.2679ptp\equiv 2\,(\mathrm{mod}\hskip 0.85358pt3),\end{array}\right.

and

\binom{np-1}{\frac{p-1}{2}}_{2}\equiv\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\hskip-2.84526pt1+np\Big(2q_{p}(2)+\frac{1}{2}q_{p}(3)\Big)\hskip 2.84526pt(\mathrm{mod}\hskip 0.85358ptp^{2})&\text{if}\hskip 4.2679ptp\equiv 1\,(\mathrm{mod}\hskip 0.85358pt6),\\
\hskip-2.84526pt-\frac{1}{2}npq_{p}(3)\hskip 2.84526pt(\mathrm{mod}\hskip 0.85358ptp^{2})&\text{if}\hskip 4.2679ptp\equiv 5\,(\mathrm{mod}\hskip 0.85358pt6),\end{array}\right.

where
q_{p}(a)
is the
Fermat
quotient defined, for
a\in\mathbb{Z}-p\mathbb{Z}
, by
q_{p}(a)=\frac{a^{p-1}-1}{p}.

Analogously, one defines the quadrinomial coefficients
\binom{n}{k}_{3}
by

\left(1+x+x^{2}+x^{3}\right)^{n}=\overset{3n}{\underset{k=0}{\sum}}\binom{n}{k}_{3}x^{k}.

In this paper, we study congruences modulo
p^{2}
for the quadrinomial coefficients
\dbinom{np-1}{p-1}_{3}
and
\dbinom{np-1}{\frac{p-1}{2}}_{3}.
Our purpose is to establish the following analogous result.

Theorem A
.

Let
p\geq 5
be a prime number and
n
be a positive integer. We have

(1.1)

\hskip-51.21504pt\binom{np-1}{p-1}_{3}\equiv\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\hskip-2.84526pt1+2npq_{p}(2)\hskip 2.84526pt(\mathrm{mod}\hskip 0.85358ptp^{2})&\text{if}\hskip 4.2679ptp\equiv 1\,(\mathrm{mod}\hskip 0.85358pt4),\\
\hskip-2.84526pt-\frac{1}{2}npq_{p}(2)\hskip 2.84526pt(\mathrm{mod}\hskip 0.85358ptp^{2})&\text{if}\hskip 4.2679ptp\equiv 3\,(\mathrm{mod}\hskip 0.85358pt4),\end{array}\right.

and

(1.2)

\binom{np-1}{\frac{p-1}{2}}_{3}\equiv\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\hskip-2.84526pt1+np\Big(\frac{13}{4}q_{p}(2)+\chi_{p}\Big)\hskip 2.84526pt(\mathrm{mod}\hskip 0.85358ptp^{2})&\text{if}\hskip 4.2679ptp\equiv 1\,(\mathrm{mod}\hskip 0.85358pt8),\\
\hskip-2.84526pt-1-np\Big(\frac{13}{4}q_{p}(2)+\chi_{p}\Big)\hskip 2.84526pt(\mathrm{mod}\hskip 0.85358ptp^{2})&\text{if}\hskip 4.2679ptp\equiv 3\,(\mathrm{mod}\hskip 0.85358pt8),\\
\hskip-2.84526pt-np\Big(\frac{1}{4}q_{p}(2)-\chi_{p}\Big)\hskip 2.84526pt(\mathrm{mod}\hskip 0.85358ptp^{2})&\text{if}\hskip 4.2679ptp\equiv 5\,(\mathrm{mod}\hskip 0.85358pt8),\\
\hskip-2.84526ptnp\Big(\frac{1}{4}q_{p}(2)-\chi_{p}\Big)\hskip 2.84526pt(\mathrm{mod}\hskip 0.85358ptp^{2})&\text{if}\hskip 4.2679ptp\equiv 7\,(\mathrm{mod}\hskip 0.85358pt8),\end{array}\right.

where
\chi_{p}:=P_{p-(\frac{2}{p})}/p
is the
Pell
quotient and
(P_{n})_{n}
is the Pell sequence (OEIS A000129).

Using this theorem we obtain the following proposition, which is an analogue of Proposition 3 of

Proposition B

Let

n

(1.3)

\hskip-2.84526pt-\frac{1}{4}npq_{2}(2)\hskip 2.84526pt(\mathrm{mod}\hskip 0.85358ptp^{2})&\text{if}\hskip 4.2679ptp\equiv 3\,(\mathrm{mod}\hskip 0.85358pt4),\end{array}\right.

and

(1.4)

\overset{\frac{p-1}{2}}{\underset{k=0}{\sum}}\binom{np-1}{k}_{3}\equiv\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\hskip-2.84526pt1+\frac{3}{2}np\Big(2q_{p}(2)+\chi_{p}\Big)\hskip 2.84526pt(\mathrm{mod}\hskip 0.85358ptp^{2})&\text{if}\hskip 4.2679ptp\equiv 1\,(\mathrm{mod}\hskip 0.85358pt8),\\

\hskip-2.84526pt-\frac{1}{4}np\Big(q_{p}(2)+2\chi_{p}\Big)\hskip 2.84526pt(\mathrm{mod}\hskip 0.85358ptp^{2})&\text{if}\hskip 4.2679ptp\equiv 7\,(\mathrm{mod}\hskip 0.85358pt8).\end{array}\right.

\displaystyle\equiv-4q_{p}(2)-2\chi_{p}\hskip 2.84526pt(\mathrm{mod}\hskip 0.85358ptp),\,p\geq 5,

Lemma 2.2
.

Let
p\geq 5
be a prime number.

1.
If
p\equiv 1\,(\mathrm{mod}\hskip 0.85358pt4)
then

(2.4)

\displaystyle\hskip 8.53581pt\sum\limits_{k=0}^{\frac{p-5}{4}}\frac{1}{4k+1}\equiv\frac{3}{4}q_{p}(2)\,(\mathrm{mod}\hskip 0.85358ptp),

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{k=0}^{\frac{p-1}{4}}\frac{1}{4k+2}\equiv 1-\frac{1}{4}q_{p}(2)\,(\mathrm{mod}\hskip 0.85358ptp),

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{k=0}^{\frac{p-1}{4}}\frac{1}{4k+3}\equiv\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{4}q_{p}(2)\,(\mathrm{mod}\hskip 0.85358ptp).

2.
If
p\equiv 3\,(\mathrm{mod}\hskip 0.85358pt4)
then

(2.5)

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{k=0}^{\frac{p-3}{4}}\frac{1}{4k+1}\equiv\frac{1}{4}q_{p}(2)\,(\mathrm{mod}\hskip 0.85358ptp),

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{k=0}^{\frac{p-3}{4}}\frac{1}{4k+2}\equiv-\frac{1}{4}q_{p}(2)\,(\mathrm{mod}\hskip 0.85358ptp),

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{k=0}^{\frac{p-7}{4}}\frac{1}{4k+3}\equiv\frac{3}{4}q_{p}(2)\,(\mathrm{mod}\hskip 0.85358ptp).

Proof.

i.
Assume that
p\equiv 1\,(\mathrm{mod}\hskip 0.85358pt4).
We have

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{k=0}^{\frac{p-5}{4}}\frac{1}{4k+1}

\displaystyle=\sum\limits_{j=1}^{\frac{p-1}{4}}\frac{1}{4(\frac{p-1}{4}-j)+1}=\sum\limits_{j=1}^{\frac{p-1}{4}}\frac{1}{p-4j},

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{k=0}^{\frac{p-1}{4}}\frac{1}{4k+2}

\displaystyle=\sum\limits_{j=\frac{p-1}{4}}^{\frac{p-1}{2}}\frac{1}{4(\frac{p-1}{2}-j)+2}=\sum\limits_{j=\frac{p-1}{4}}^{\frac{p-1}{2}}\frac{1}{2p-4j}

and

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{k=0}^{\frac{p-1}{4}}\frac{1}{4k+3}

\displaystyle=\sum\limits_{j=\frac{p+3}{4}}^{\frac{p+1}{2}}\frac{1}{4(j-\frac{p+3}{4})+3}=\sum\limits_{j=\frac{p+3}{4}}^{\frac{p+1}{2}}\frac{1}{4j-p},

so that

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{k=0}^{\frac{p-5}{4}}\frac{1}{4k+1}

\displaystyle\equiv-\frac{1}{4}\sum\limits_{j=1}^{\frac{p-1}{4}}\frac{1}{j}=-\frac{1}{4}H_{[\frac{p}{4}]}=\frac{3}{4}q_{p}(2)\,(\mathrm{mod}\hskip 0.85358ptp),

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{k=0}^{\frac{p-1}{4}}\frac{1}{4k+2}

\displaystyle\equiv-\frac{1}{4}\sum\limits_{j=\frac{p-1}{4}}^{\frac{p-1}{2}}\frac{1}{j}=-\frac{1}{p-1}-\frac{1}{4}\sum\limits_{j=1}^{\frac{p-1}{2}}\frac{1}{j}+\frac{1}{4}\sum\limits_{j=1}^{\frac{p-1}{4}}\frac{1}{j}

\displaystyle\equiv 1-\frac{1}{4}\sum\limits_{j=1}^{\frac{p-1}{2}}\frac{1}{j}+\frac{1}{4}\sum\limits_{j=1}^{\frac{p-1}{4}}\frac{1}{j}=1-\frac{1}{4}H_{[\frac{p}{2}]}+\frac{1}{4}H_{[\frac{p}{4}]}=1-\frac{1}{4}q_{p}(2)\,(\mathrm{mod}\hskip 0.85358ptp)

and

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{k=0}^{\frac{p-1}{4}}\frac{1}{4k+3}

\displaystyle\equiv\frac{1}{4}\sum\limits_{j=\frac{p+3}{4}}^{\frac{p+1}{2}}\frac{1}{j}=\frac{1}{2p+2}+\frac{1}{4}\sum\limits_{j=1}^{\frac{p-1}{2}}\frac{1}{j}-\frac{1}{4}\sum\limits_{j=1}^{\frac{p-1}{4}}\frac{1}{j}

\displaystyle\equiv\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{4}\sum\limits_{j=1}^{\frac{p-1}{2}}\frac{1}{j}-\frac{1}{4}\sum\limits_{j=1}^{\frac{p-1}{4}}\frac{1}{j}=\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{4}H_{[\frac{p}{2}]}-\frac{1}{4}H_{[\frac{p}{4}]}=\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{4}q_{p}(2)\,(\mathrm{mod}\hskip 0.85358ptp).

ii.
Assume now that
p\equiv 3\,(\mathrm{mod}\hskip 0.85358pt4).
From the equalities

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{k=0}^{\frac{p-3}{4}}\frac{1}{4k+1}

\displaystyle=\sum\limits_{j=\frac{p+1}{4}}^{\frac{p-1}{2}}\frac{1}{4(j-\frac{p-3}{4}-1)+1}=\sum\limits_{j=\frac{p+1}{4}}^{\frac{p-1}{2}}\frac{1}{4j-p},

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{k=0}^{\frac{p-3}{4}}\frac{1}{4k+2}

\displaystyle=\sum\limits_{j=\frac{p+1}{4}}^{\frac{p-1}{2}}\frac{1}{4(\frac{p-1}{2}-j)+2}=\sum\limits_{j=\frac{p+1}{4}}^{\frac{p-1}{2}}\frac{1}{2p-4j}

and

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{k=0}^{\frac{p-7}{4}}\frac{1}{4k+3}

\displaystyle=\sum\limits_{j=\frac{p+3}{4}}^{\frac{p+1}{2}}\frac{1}{4(\frac{p-7}{4}-j+1)+3}=\sum\limits_{j=1}^{\frac{p-3}{4}}\frac{1}{p-4j},

we deduce the congruences

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{k=0}^{\frac{p-3}{4}}\frac{1}{4k+1}

\displaystyle\equiv\frac{1}{4}\sum\limits_{j=\frac{p+1}{4}}^{\frac{p-1}{2}}\frac{1}{j}=\frac{1}{4}H_{[\frac{p}{2}]}-\frac{1}{4}H_{[\frac{p}{4}]}=\frac{1}{4}q_{p}(2)\,(\mathrm{mod}\hskip 0.85358ptp),

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{k=0}^{\frac{p-3}{4}}\frac{1}{4k+2}

\displaystyle\equiv-\frac{1}{4}\sum\limits_{j=\frac{p+1}{4}}^{\frac{p-1}{2}}\frac{1}{j}=-\frac{1}{4}H_{[\frac{p}{2}]}+\frac{1}{4}H_{[\frac{p}{4}]}=-\frac{1}{4}q_{p}(2)\,(\mathrm{mod}\hskip 0.85358ptp)

and

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{k=0}^{\frac{p-7}{4}}\frac{1}{4k+3}

\displaystyle\equiv-\frac{1}{4}\sum\limits_{j=1}^{\frac{p-3}{4}}\frac{1}{j}=-\frac{1}{4}H_{[\frac{p}{4}]}=\frac{3}{4}q_{p}(2)\,(\mathrm{mod}\hskip 0.85358ptp).

∎

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{k=0}^{\frac{p-1}{8}}\frac{1}{4k+1}\equiv

\displaystyle-\frac{1}{4}\sum\limits_{j=\frac{p-1}{8}}^{\frac{p-1}{4}}\frac{1}{j}=\frac{2}{p-1}-\frac{1}{4}\sum\limits_{j=1}^{\frac{p-1}{4}}\frac{1}{j}+\frac{1}{4}\sum\limits_{j=1}^{\frac{p-1}{8}}\frac{1}{j}

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{k=0}^{\frac{p-1}{8}}\frac{1}{4k+3}\equiv

\displaystyle-\sum\limits_{j=\frac{p-9}{8}}^{\frac{p-5}{4}}\frac{1}{-4j-2}=-\sum\limits_{j=0}^{\frac{p-1}{4}}\frac{1}{4j+2}+\sum\limits_{j=0}^{\frac{p-1}{8}}\frac{1}{4j+2}+\frac{1}{p+1}-\frac{2}{p-5}-\frac{p+3}{2}

\displaystyle\equiv

\displaystyle-\frac{1}{4}\sum\limits_{j=0}^{\frac{p-1}{4}}\frac{1}{4j+2}+\sum\limits_{j=0}^{\frac{p-1}{8}}\frac{1}{4j+2}+\frac{11}{15}=\frac{2}{5}+\frac{1}{4}H_{[\frac{p}{2}]}+\frac{1}{4}H_{[\frac{p}{4}]}-\frac{1}{4}H_{[\frac{p}{8}]}

\displaystyle=\frac{2}{5}-\frac{1}{4}q_{p}(2)+\frac{1}{2}\chi_{p}\,(\mathrm{mod}\hskip 0.85358ptp).

ii.
Assume that
p\equiv 3\,(\mathrm{mod}\hskip 0.85358pt8).
From the equalities

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{k=0}^{\frac{p-3}{8}}\frac{1}{4k+1}=

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{j=\frac{p-3}{8}}^{\frac{p-3}{4}}\frac{1}{4(\frac{p-7}{4}-j+1)+1}=\sum\limits_{j=\frac{p-3}{8}}^{\frac{p-3}{4}}\frac{1}{p-4j-2},

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{k=0}^{\frac{p-3}{8}}\frac{1}{4k+2}=

\displaystyle\frac{2}{p+1}+\frac{1}{2}\sum\limits_{j=1}^{\frac{p-3}{4}}\frac{1}{j}-\frac{1}{4}\sum\limits_{j=1}^{\frac{p-3}{8}}\frac{1}{j}

and

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{k=0}^{\frac{p-3}{8}}\frac{1}{4k+3}=

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{j=\frac{p-3}{8}}^{\frac{p-3}{4}}\frac{1}{4\big(\frac{p-3}{4}-j\big)+3}=\sum\limits_{j=\frac{p-3}{8}}^{\frac{p-3}{4}}\frac{1}{p-4j},

\displaystyle=-\binom{np-1}{\frac{p+1}{2}}_{3}-\binom{np-1}{\frac{p+3}{2}}_{3}+np\sum\limits_{k=0}^{\frac{p-3}{8}}\frac{1}{4k+3}

\displaystyle=np\sum\limits_{k=0}^{\frac{p-3}{8}}\frac{1}{4k+1}

\displaystyle=-\frac{1}{4}np\Big(q_{p}(2)-2\chi(p)\Big).
