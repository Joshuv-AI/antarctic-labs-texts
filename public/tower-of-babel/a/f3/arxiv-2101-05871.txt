Abstract
. We consider the Hénon equation ( P α P_{\alpha} ) − Δ ​ u = | x | α ​ | u | p − 1 ​ u in B N , u = 0 on ∂ B N , -\Delta u=|x|^{\alpha}|u|^{p-1}u\ \ \textrm{in}\ \ B^{N},\quad u=0\ \ \textrm{on}\ \ \partial B^{N}, where B N ⊂ ℝ N B^{N}\subset\mathbb{R}^{N} is the open unit ball centered at the origin, N ≥ 3 N\geq 3 , p > 1 p>1 and α > 0 \alpha>0 is a parameter. We show that, after a suitable rescaling, the two-dimensional Lane-Emden equation − Δ ​ w = | w | p − 1 ​ w in ​ B 2 , w = 0 on ​ ∂ B 2 , -\Delta w=|w|^{p-1}w\quad\text{in}\ B^{2},\quad w=0\quad\text{on}\ \partial B^{2}, where B 2 ⊂ ℝ 2 B^{2}\subset\mathbb{R}^{2} is the open unit ball, is the limit problem of ( P α ), as α → ∞ \alpha\to\infty , in the framework of radial solutions. Introduction

The qualitative analysis of solutions of partial differential equations is a very important field of research, and it turns out that the Hénon equation
[
15
]
is an excellent prototype for the study of some fundamental problems in this subject. For example, the symmetry of least energy solutions and least energy nodal solutions
[

and some concentration phenomena

[

[
1
]
are presented for autonomous problems and their proofs cannot be adapted to the nonautonomous case, which includes the Hénon equation. Indeed, a conjecture on the symmetry breaking of the least energy nodal solutions of the superlinear subcritical Hénon equation remained opened for at least 14 years, since the paper
[
5
]
, was partially solved in
[
20
]
for the case of

(see also
[

,
4
,

,
18
,

,

with new information on the asymptotic profile and Morse indices of the radial solutions of the Hénon equation, for both positive and nodal solutions.

Energy levels and their asymptotic estimates

Set

\varphi_{\alpha}(u):=\frac{1}{2}\int_{B^{N}}|\nabla u|^{2}dx-\frac{1}{p+1}\int_{B^{N}}|x|^{\alpha}|u|^{p+1}dx.

Since the embedding

v(t)=u(r)
, where
r=t^{\theta}
and
\theta=\frac{N}{\alpha+N}\in(0,1]
. Of course
v
has
m
nodal sets,
v_{i}(t)=u_{i}(r)
for each
i=1,\cdots,m
, and

\displaystyle\int_{B^{N}}|x|^{\alpha}|u_{i}(x)|^{p+1}dx=\theta\int_{B^{N}}|v_{i}|^{p+1}dx,\qquad\int_{B^{N}}|\nabla u_{i}(x)|^{2}dx=\theta^{-1}\int_{B^{N}}|\nabla v_{i}(x)|^{2}|x|^{(2-N)(1-\theta)}dx.

Then

\frac{\left(\displaystyle\int_{B^{N}}|\nabla u_{i}|^{2}\right)^{\frac{p+1}{p-1}}}{\left(\displaystyle\int_{B^{N}}|x|^{\alpha}|u_{i}|^{p+1}\right)^{\frac{2}{p-1}}}=\frac{1}{\theta^{\frac{p+3}{p-1}}}\frac{\left(\displaystyle\int_{B^{N}}|\nabla v_{i}|^{2}|x|^{(2-N)(1-\theta)}\right)^{\frac{p+1}{p-1}}}{\left(\displaystyle\int_{B^{N}}|v_{i}|^{p+1}\right)^{\frac{2}{p-1}}}

which, by (
2.3
), yields

(2.4)

{C}_{\alpha,m}=\frac{\left(\frac{1}{2}-\frac{1}{p+1}\right){R}_{\theta,m}}{\theta^{\frac{p+3}{p-1}}}=\left(\frac{1}{2}-\frac{1}{p+1}\right)\left(\frac{\alpha+N}{N}\right)^{\frac{p+3}{p-1}}{R}_{\theta,m}\,,

where

{R}_{\rho,m}:=\inf_{v\in H_{m}}\sum_{i=1}^{m}\frac{\left(\int_{B^{N}}|\nabla v_{i}|^{2}|x|^{(2-N)(1-\rho)}\right)^{\frac{p+1}{p-1}}}{\left(\int_{B^{N}}|v_{i}|^{p+1}\right)^{\frac{2}{p-1}}},\quad\rho\in[0,1].

Since
\rho\mapsto{R}_{\rho,m}
is non-increasing and
0<{R}_{1,m}<{R}_{0,m}<\infty
, we infer that

C_{1}\left(\frac{\alpha+N}{N}\right)^{\frac{p+3}{p-1}}\leq{C}_{\alpha,m}\leq C_{2}\left(\frac{\alpha+N}{N}\right)^{\frac{p+3}{p-1}}\quad\forall\alpha>\alpha_{p},

where
C_{1}=\left(\frac{1}{2}-\frac{1}{p+1}\right)\displaystyle\lim_{\rho\to 1^{-}}{R}_{\rho,m}
and
C_{2}=\left(\frac{1}{2}-\frac{1}{p+1}\right)\displaystyle\lim_{\rho\to 0^{+}}{R}_{\rho,m}
. The strict monotonicity of
\alpha\mapsto{C}_{\alpha,m}
follows immediately from (
2.4
).
∎

3.
Estimates for the
L^{\infty}
-norms

In this section we present asymptotic estimates for
\|u_{\alpha}\|_{\infty}
. We recall that, by
[
14
, Lemma 5.2]
,
u_{\alpha}(0)=\displaystyle\max_{x\in B^{N}}u_{\alpha}(x)=\|u_{\alpha}\|_{\infty}
. Moreover, by Proposition
2.3
, there exist constants
C_{1},C_{2}>0
, that do not depend on
\alpha
, such that

(3.1)

C_{1}\left(\frac{\alpha+N}{N}\right)^{\frac{p+3}{p-1}}\leq\int_{B^{N}}|\nabla u_{\alpha}|^{2}=\int_{B^{N}}|x|^{\alpha}|u_{\alpha}|^{p+1}\leq C_{2}\left(\frac{\alpha+N}{N}\right)^{\frac{p+3}{p-1}},\quad\forall\alpha>\alpha_{p}.

We start with the bound from below.

Lemma 3.1
.

There exists a constant
C>0
, that does not depend of
\alpha
, such that

\|u_{\alpha}\|_{\infty}\geq C\left(\frac{\alpha+N}{N}\right)^{\frac{2}{p-1}},\quad\forall\alpha>\alpha_{p}.

Proof.

For each
\alpha>\alpha_{p}
, with
\theta=\frac{N}{\alpha+N}
, consider the function

\widetilde{u}_{\alpha}(y):=\theta^{\frac{2}{p-1}}u_{\alpha}\left(\theta^{\frac{1}{2-N}}y\right),\quad y\in\Omega_{\alpha}:=B\left(0,\theta^{\frac{1}{N-2}}\right).

Since
\|\widetilde{u}_{\alpha}\|_{\infty}=\left(\frac{N}{\alpha+N}\right)^{\frac{2}{p-1}}\|u_{\alpha}\|_{\infty}
, it suffices to show that
\|\widetilde{u}_{\alpha}\|_{\infty}\geq C
. Performing the change of variables
x=\theta^{\frac{1}{2-N}}y
, we infer that

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega_{\alpha}}|\nabla\widetilde{u}_{\alpha}(y)|^{2}dy=\left(\frac{N}{\alpha+N}\right)^{\frac{p+3}{p-1}}\int_{B^{N}}|\nabla u_{\alpha}(x)|^{2}dx,

whence, by (
3.1
),

(3.2)

C_{1}\leq\int_{\Omega_{\alpha}}|\nabla\widetilde{u}_{\alpha}|^{2}dy\leq C_{2},\quad\forall\alpha>\alpha_{p}.

In addition, since
u_{\alpha}
solves (

P
α

),
\widetilde{u}_{\alpha}
satisfies

(3.3)

-\Delta\widetilde{u}_{\alpha}(y)=\theta^{\frac{-2(N-1)}{N-2}}\left|\theta^{\frac{1}{2-N}}y\right|^{\alpha}|{\widetilde{u}_{\alpha}(y)}|^{p-1}\widetilde{u}_{\alpha}(y),\quad y\in\Omega_{\alpha},\quad\widetilde{u}_{\alpha}=0\ \text{on}\ \partial\Omega_{\alpha}.

Consequently, by Hölder’s inequality, (
3.2
) and (
3.3
),

(3.4)

\displaystyle 0<C_{1}

\displaystyle\leq\int_{\Omega_{\alpha}}|\nabla\widetilde{u}_{\alpha}|^{2}dy=\int_{\Omega_{\alpha}}\theta^{\frac{-2(N-1)}{N-2}}\left|\theta^{\frac{1}{2-N}}y\right|^{\alpha}|{\widetilde{u}_{\alpha}}|^{p+1}dy

\displaystyle\leq\|{\widetilde{u}_{\alpha}}\|_{\infty}^{p-1}\int_{\Omega_{\alpha}}\theta^{\frac{-2(N-1)}{N-2}}\left|\theta^{\frac{1}{2-N}}y\right|^{\alpha}|{\widetilde{u}_{\alpha}}|^{2}dy

\displaystyle\leq\|{\widetilde{u}_{\alpha}}\|_{\infty}^{p-1}\left(\int_{\Omega_{\alpha}}\!\!\!\!\!\theta^{\frac{-2(N-1)}{N-2}}\left|\theta^{\frac{1}{2-N}}y\right|^{\alpha}dy\right)^{\frac{p-1}{p+1}}\left(\int_{\Omega_{\alpha}}\!\!\!\!\!\theta^{\frac{-2(N-1)}{N-2}}\left|\theta^{\frac{1}{2-N}}y\right|^{\alpha}|{\widetilde{u}_{\alpha}}|^{p+1}dy\right)^{\frac{2}{p+1}}

\displaystyle\leq(C_{2})^{\frac{2}{p+1}}\|{\widetilde{u}_{\alpha}}\|_{\infty}^{p-1}\left(\int_{\Omega_{\alpha}}\!\!\!\!\!\theta^{\frac{-2(N-1)}{N-2}}\left|\theta^{\frac{1}{2-N}}y\right|^{\alpha}dy\right)^{\frac{p-1}{p+1}}.

Note that, since
\theta=\frac{N}{\alpha+N}
,

(3.5)

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega_{\alpha}}\theta^{\frac{-2(N-1)}{N-2}}\left|\theta^{\frac{1}{2-N}}y\right|^{\alpha}dy=\omega_{N-1}\theta^{\frac{2(N-1)+\alpha}{2-N}}\int_{0}^{\theta^{\frac{1}{N-2}}}t^{\alpha+N-1}dt=\frac{\omega_{N-1}}{N}.

Therefore, from (
3.4
) and (
3.5
), there exists
C>0
, independent of
\alpha
, such that

\|{\widetilde{u}_{\alpha}}\|_{\infty}\geq C,\quad\forall\alpha>\alpha_{p},

which proves the lemma.
∎

To prove the reverse estimate, for each
\alpha>\alpha_{p}
, consider

(3.6)

v_{\alpha}(t):=\left(\frac{2}{\alpha+2}\right)^{\frac{2}{p-1}}u_{\alpha}\left(t^{\frac{2}{\alpha+2}}\right),\quad t\in[0,1].

Then by
[
9
, Proposition 4.2]
,
v_{\alpha}
satisfies

(3.7)

\left\{\begin{array}[]{l}-(t^{M_{\alpha}-1}v_{\alpha}^{\prime})^{\prime}=t^{M_{\alpha}-1}|v_{\alpha}|^{p-1}v_{\alpha}\quad\textrm{in}\ \ (0,1),\\
v_{\alpha}(1)=v_{\alpha}^{\prime}(0)=0,\end{array}\right.

where
M_{\alpha}:=\frac{2(\alpha+N)}{\alpha+2}\in(2,N)
. Then, integrating from
0
to
t
,

(3.8)

v_{\alpha}^{\prime}(t)=-\frac{1}{t^{M_{\alpha}-1}}\int_{0}^{t}s^{M_{\alpha}-1}|v_{\alpha}(s)|^{p-1}v_{\alpha}(s)ds\quad\forall t\in(0,1].

Moreover, performing the change of variables
t\leftrightarrow r
, where
r=t^{\frac{2}{\alpha+2}}
, we obtain

(3.9)

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{1}|v_{\alpha}^{\prime}(t)|^{2}t^{M_{\alpha}-1}dt=\left(\frac{2}{\alpha+2}\right)^{\frac{p+3}{p-1}}\int_{0}^{1}|u_{\alpha}^{\prime}(r)|^{2}r^{N-1}dr.

Lemma 3.2
.

There exist
\alpha_{0}>\alpha_{p}
and
C>0
, that do not depend on
\alpha
, such that

\|u_{\alpha}\|_{\infty}\leq C\left(\frac{\alpha+2}{2}\right)^{\frac{2}{p-1}}\ \ \forall\,\alpha\geq\alpha_{0}.

Proof.

By (
3.6
), it is enough to show that

v_{\alpha}(0)=\|v_{\alpha}\|_{\infty}\leq C,\ \ \forall\,\alpha\geq\alpha_{0}.

From (
3.1
) and (
3.9
), there exists a constant
C_{0}>0
independent of
\alpha
such that

(3.10)

\int_{0}^{1}|v_{\alpha}^{\prime}(t)|^{2}t^{M_{\alpha}-1}dt\leq C_{0}\quad\forall\alpha>\alpha_{p}.

Fix
M>2
, with
p<\frac{M}{M-2}
, and take
\alpha_{0}>0
so that
M_{\alpha}\leq M
for
\alpha\geq\alpha_{0}
. By contradiction, suppose that
\|v_{\alpha}\|_{\infty}
is not bounded in
[\alpha_{0},\infty)
. Then there exists a sequence
\alpha_{n}\geq\alpha_{0}
such that
\|v_{\alpha_{n}}\|_{\infty}\to\infty
as
n\to\infty
. Set

\overline{v}_{\alpha}(s)=\frac{1}{\|v_{\alpha}\|_{\infty}}v_{\alpha}\left(\|v_{\alpha}\|_{\infty}^{1-p}s\right),\quad\text{for}\ s\in\left[0,\|v_{\alpha}\|_{\infty}^{p-1}\right],\quad\text{and}\ \ t=\|v_{\alpha}\|_{\infty}^{1-p}s.

Then, since
M\geq M_{\alpha_{n}}
, from (
3.10
) and
(M-2)p-M<0
, we infer that

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\|v_{\alpha_{n}}\|_{\infty}^{p-1}}|\overline{v}_{\alpha_{n}}^{\prime}(s)|^{2}s^{M-1}ds

\displaystyle=\|v_{\alpha_{n}}\|_{\infty}^{(M-2)p-M}\int_{0}^{1}|v_{\alpha_{n}}^{\prime}(t)|^{2}t^{M-1}dt

\displaystyle\leq\|v_{\alpha_{n}}\|_{\infty}^{(M-2)p-M}\int_{0}^{1}|v_{\alpha_{n}}^{\prime}(t)|^{2}t^{M_{\alpha_{n}}-1}dt\leq C_{0}\|v_{\alpha_{n}}\|_{\infty}^{(M-2)p-M}\to 0.

Hence
\overline{v}_{\alpha_{n}}\to 0\ \text{in}\ \mathcal{D}^{1,2}_{M},

where

\mathcal{D}^{1,2}_{M}:=\left\{w\in L^{2^{*}_{M}}_{M}(0,\infty):\quad\begin{aligned} &\text{$w$ has first order weak derivative and}\\
&\|w\|_{\mathcal{D}^{1,2}_{M}}^{2}:=\int_{0}^{\infty}|w^{\prime}(t)|^{2}t^{M-1}dt<\infty\end{aligned}\right\}

and

L^{2^{*}_{M}}_{M}(0,\infty):=\left\{v:[0,\infty)\to\mathbb{R}\ \text{measurable}:\int_{0}^{\infty}|v(s)|^{\frac{2M}{M-2}}s^{M-1}ds<\infty\right\}.

Since, by Sobolev’s inequality, the inclusion
\mathcal{D}^{1,2}_{M}\subset L^{2^{*}_{M}}_{M}(0,\infty)
is continuous, up to a subsequence,
\overline{v}_{\alpha_{n}}\to 0
a.e. in
[0,\infty)
.

On the other hand,

\overline{v}_{\alpha_{n}}(0)=\|\overline{v}_{\alpha_{n}}\|_{\infty}=1,

and thus, the sequence of functions
(\overline{v}_{\alpha_{n}})
is uniformly bounded.
Moreover, by (
3.8
),

|\overline{v}_{\alpha_{n}}^{\prime}(s)|=\|v_{\alpha_{n}}\|_{\infty}^{-p}|v_{\alpha_{n}}^{\prime}\left(\|v_{\alpha_{n}}\|_{\infty}^{1-p}s\right)|\leq\|v_{\alpha_{n}}\|_{\infty}^{-p}\|v_{\alpha_{n}}\|_{\infty}^{p}=1.

In particular, the sequence of functions
(\overline{v}_{\alpha_{n}})
is equicontinuous.
Consequently, by Arzelà-Ascoli theorem, up to a subsequence,
\overline{v}_{\alpha_{n}}\to\overline{v}
uniformly in
[0,1]
with
\|\overline{v}\|_{\infty}=1
, which leads us to a contradiction.
∎

.

Notice that, unlike Lemma

, the conclusion of the previous lemma is not true for all

(

Remark 4.2

Proof of Theorem

. Part (iii)
.

By (

Next for simplicity, we denote by

,

.

,
\|v_{\alpha}\|_{\infty}\leq C

since

6.

and

Here we analyze the spectrum of some linear operators. More precisely, we derive asymptotic expansions of the negative eigenvalues of the linearized operators associated to the Hénon equation (

P

In addition, from
