Superposition of configurations and scanning
Andreas Stavrou

Abstract
. We endow the cohomology of configuration spaces of a manifold with a product arising from superposing configurations. We prove that, under the scanning isomorphism, this product corresponds to the cup-product of the section space of the standard scanning bundle of the manifold.

1.
Symmetric products and multivalued functions

Given a based space
(X,e)
, the
symmetric product

\SP(X)
is the free unital abelian monoid on

with identity

, topologised as the quotient

\SP(X)\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=\Big(\bigsqcup_{n\geq 1}X^{n}/\mathfrak{S}_{n}\Big)\Big/\sim

where

A

We give an alternative definition of the superposition product appearing in

be the one point compactification of

Proof.
