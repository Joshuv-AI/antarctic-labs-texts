1.
Introduction

Throughout this paper, the symbol
\mathbb{M}_{n}(\mathbb{C})
refers to the set consisting of complex matrices with size
n
.
If
\langle x,Ax\rangle\geq 0
for each vector
x\in\mathbb{C}^{n}
, then we can say that a Hermitian matrix
A
is positive semidefinite.
If
\langle x,Ax\rangle>0
for each vector
x\in\mathbb{C}^{n}-\{0\}
, then we can say that a Hermitian matrix
A
is positive definite.
We use the symbol
\mathbb{P}_{n}
for the set consisting of positive definite matrices in
\mathbb{M}_{n}(\mathbb{C})
.
We use the symbol
{\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|.\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|}
for unitarily invariant norms on the space
\mathbb{M}_{n}(\mathbb{C})
.

For all
A,B\in\mathbb{P}_{n},
the matrix
A\sharp B
is called the geometric mean of
A
and
B
given by

\displaystyle A\sharp B=A^{{}^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}}\left(A^{{}^{\frac{-1}{{}_{2}}}}BA^{{}^{\frac{-1}{{}_{2}}}}\right)^{{}^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}}A^{{}^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}}.

(1.1)

The matrix
A\sharp_{t}B
is called the
t
-geometric mean of
A
and
B
given by

\displaystyle A\sharp_{t}B=A^{{}^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}}\left(A^{{}^{\frac{-1}{{}_{2}}}}BA^{{}^{\frac{-1}{{}_{2}}}}\right)^{t}A^{{}^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}},~\hskip 2.84544pt\forall~t\in[0,1].

(1.2)

The matrix
|A|
is called the absolute value of
A
defined as
|A|=(A^{*}A)^{{}^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}}.

It is well known that the subadditivity relation

\displaystyle f(a+b)\leq f(a)+f(b),

(1.3)

holds for any
a,b\geq 0
and for any nonnegative concave functions
f
on
[0,\infty).

Bourin and Uchiyama
[
6
, Theorem 1.1]
discovered a noncommutative version of this inequality for all positive semidefinite matrices. This version states that if
A,B\in\mathbb{M}_{n}(\mathbb{C})
are both positive semidefinite matrices, then for all unitarily invariant norms, we have

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|f\left(A+B\right)\right|\right|\right|\leq\left|\left|\left|f\left(A\right)+f\left(B\right)\right|\right|\right|.

(1.4)

This result prompted Bourin
[
5
]
to pose the following question.

Question 1.1
.

If
A,B\in\mathbb{M}_{n}(\mathbb{C})
are positive semidefinite matrices, is it true that

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|A^{p+q}+B^{p+q}\right|\right|\right|\leq\left|\left|\left|(A^{p}+B^{p})(A^{q}+B^{q})\right|\right|\right|,~\forall~p,q>0?

Hayajneh and Kittaneh
[
10
]
gave an affirmative answer to this question for the Hilbert-Schmidt and trace norms. Audenaert
[
2
]
gave an affirmative answer to this question by proving

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|\sum_{i=1}^{m}\left(A_{i}B_{i}\right)\right|\right|\right|\leq\left|\left|\left|\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_{i}^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}B_{i}^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}\right)^{2}\right|\right|\right|\leq\left|\left|\left|\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_{i}\right)\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}B_{i}\right)\right|\right|\right|,

(1.5)

for any
A_{i},B_{i}\in\mathbb{P}_{n},~i=1,\cdots,m
such that
A_{i}B_{i}=B_{i}A_{i}
and for all unitarily invariant norms.
This result also confirms a conjecture that Hayajneh and Kittaneh proved in
[
10
]
. Additionally, Lin
[
12
]
gave a different proof for inequality (
1.5
).

Hayajneh and Kittaneh
[
11
]
provided a generalization of inequality (
1.5
) by proving

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|\sum_{i=1}^{m}\left(A_{i}B_{i}\right)\right|\right|\right|\leq\left|\left|\left|\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_{i}^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}B_{i}^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}\right)^{2}\right|\right|\right|\leq\left|\left|\left|\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_{i}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}B_{i}\right)\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_{i}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}\right|\right|\right|,\hskip 8.5359pt

(1.6)

for any
A_{i},B_{i}\in\mathbb{P}_{n},~i=1,\cdots,m,
such that
A_{i}B_{i}=B_{i}A_{i}
and for all unitarily invariant norms.

Dinh, Ahsani, and Tam
[
1
, Theorem
3.1
]
made a noncommutative version of inequality (
1.6
). According to this version, if
A_{i},B_{i}\in\mathbb{P}_{n}
for all
i=1,\cdots,m,
then for all unitarily invariant norms, we have

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|\sum_{i=1}^{m}\left(A_{i}\sharp B_{i}\right)^{2}\right|\right|\right|\leq\left|\left|\left|\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_{i}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}B_{i}\right)\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_{i}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}\right|\right|\right|.~~~

(1.7)

Dinh
[
7
]
has generalized inequality (
1.7
). This generalization states that if
A_{i},B_{i}\in\mathbb{P}_{n}
for all
i=1,\cdots,m
and if
t\in[0,1],

p>0
and
r\geq 1
, then

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|\sum_{i=1}^{m}\left(A_{i}\sharp_{t}B_{i}\right)^{r}\right|\right|\right|\leq\left|\left|\left|\left(\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_{i}\right)^{\frac{(1-t)rp}{2}}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}B_{i}\right)^{trp}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_{i}\right)^{\frac{(1-t)rp}{2}}\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}\right|\right|\right|,\hskip 8.5359pt

(1.8)

for all unitarily invariant norms.

Inequality (
1.6
) prompted also the authors in
[
1
, page 787]
to provide the following conjecture.

Conjecture 1.2
.

If
A_{i},B_{i}\in\mathbb{P}_{n}
for all

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|\sum_{i=1}^{m}\left(A_{i}^{2}\sharp B_{i}^{2}\right)\right|\right|\right|\leq\left|\left|\left|\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_{i}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}B_{i}\right)\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_{i}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}\right|\right|\right|,

,

Lemma 2.7
.

Let
Y\in\mathbb{M}_{n}(\mathbb{C})
and let

Then

|||(Y^{*}Y)^{a}|||=|||(YY^{*})^{a}|||,

for all unitarily invariant norms.

Proof.
Let
Y\in\mathbb{M}_{n}(\mathbb{C})

Thus
|||(Y^{*}Y)^{a}|||=|||(YY^{*})^{a}|||.

This completes the proof.

Two matrices

are unitarily equivalence if there is a unitary matrix
U\in\mathbb{M}_{n}
such that

, and we write
A\cong B
.

Lemma 2.10
.

Let
A_{i},B_{i}\in\mathbb{P}_{n}
for all
i=1,\cdots,m
and let
Z=[Z_{ij}]
be the block matrix such that
Z_{ij}=B_{i}^{{}^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}}\left(\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{m}A_{k}\right)B_{j}^{{}^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}}
for all
i,j=1,\cdots,m
. Then

Z\cong\left(\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_{i}\right)^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}B_{i}\right)\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_{i}\right)^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}\right)\oplus 0.

Proof.
Let

K=\left[\begin{array}[]{ccccc}B_{1}^{{}^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}}&0&\cdots&0\\
\vdots&\vdots&\ddots&\vdots\\
B_{m}^{{}^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}}&0&\cdots&0\end{array}\right]\left[\begin{array}[]{ccc}\left(\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_{i}\right)^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}&0\\
0&0\end{array}\right].

Then

Z=KK^{*}\cong K^{*}K=\left(\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_{i}\right)^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}B_{i}\right)\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_{i}\right)^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}\right)\oplus 0.

This completes the proof.
\Box

3.
Main Results

In this section, we introduce our main results. Lemma
2.2
and Lemma
3.1
play an important role in proving Lemma
3.2
. In the proof of the following lemma, we need to use Lemma
2.1
.

Lemma 3.1
.

Let
A,B\in\mathbb{P}_{n}
and let
U\in\mathbb{M}_{n}(\mathbb{C})
be a unitary. Then for all unitarily invariant norms and for all
q\geq 1
, we have

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|~|AUB|^{q}~\right|\right|\right|\leq\left|\left|\left|~\left|A^{q}UB^{q}\right|~\right|\right|\right|.

Proof.
Let
A,B\in\mathbb{P}_{n}
and let
U\in\mathbb{M}_{n}(\mathbb{C})
be a unitary.
We are to prove that for all unitarily invariant norms and for all
q\geq 1
, we have

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|~|AUB|^{q}~\right|\right|\right|\leq\left|\left|\left|~\left|A^{q}UB^{q}\right|~\right|\right|\right|.

To see this, note that

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|~|AUB|^{q}~\right|\right|\right|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|~(BU^{*}AAUB)^{\frac{q}{{}_{2}}}~\right|\right|\right|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|~(BU^{*}A^{2}UB)^{\frac{q}{{}_{2}}}~\right|\right|\right|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|~(B^{q}(U^{*}A^{2}U)^{q}B^{q})^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}~\right|\right|\right|\hskip 8.5359pt\text{(by Lemma \ref{Lem14})}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|~(B^{q}U^{*}A^{2q}UB^{q})^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}~\right|\right|\right|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|~((A^{q}UB^{q})^{*}(A^{q}UB^{q}))^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}~\right|\right|\right|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|~|A^{q}UB^{q}|~\right|\right|\right|.

So for all unitarily invariant norms and for all
q\geq 1
, we have

\left|\left|\left|~|AUB|^{q}~\right|\right|\right|\leq\left|\left|\left|~|A^{q}UB^{q}|~\right|\right|\right|.

This completes the proof.
\Box

The following lemma plays an important role in proving Theorem

.

0&&A_{m}^{{}^{\frac{s-1}{{}_{2}}}}U_{m}B_{m}^{{}^{\frac{s-1}{{}_{2}}}}\end{array}$}\right],

then for all unitarily invariant norms, we have

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|~|X|^{q}~\right|\right|\right|\leq\left|\left|\left|\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{m}\left|A_{i}^{{}^{\frac{s}{{}_{2}}}}U_{i}B_{i}^{{}^{\frac{s}{{}_{2}}}}\right|~~\right|\right|\right|.

Proof.
Let
A_{i},B_{i}\in\mathbb{P}_{n}
and let
U_{i}\in\mathbb{M}_{n}(\mathbb{C})
be a unitary matrix for all
i=1,\cdots,m.
Suppose that
s>1
and
q>1
such that
\frac{1}{s}+\frac{1}{q}=1\text{,~i.e.,~}(s-1)q=s.
Let
X\in\mathbb{M}_{nm}(\mathbb{C})
be given by

X=\left[\scalebox{0.9}{$\begin{array}[]{ccc}A_{1}^{{}^{\frac{s-1}{{}_{2}}}}U_{1}B_{1}^{{}^{\frac{s-1}{{}_{2}}}}&&0\\
&\ddots&\\
0&&A_{m}^{{}^{\frac{s-1}{{}_{2}}}}U_{m}B_{m}^{{}^{\frac{s-1}{{}_{2}}}}\end{array}$}\right].

We are to prove that for all unitarily invariant norms, we have

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|~|X|^{q}~\right|\right|\right|\leq\left|\left|\left|\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{m}\left|A_{i}^{{}^{\frac{s}{{}_{2}}}}U_{i}B_{i}^{{}^{\frac{s}{{}_{2}}}}\right|~~\right|\right|\right|.

Now, note that

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|~|X|^{q}~\right|\right|\right|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|~\left|\left[\scalebox{0.9}{$\begin{array}[]{ccc}A_{1}^{{}^{\frac{s-1}{{}_{2}}}}U_{1}B_{1}^{{}^{\frac{s-1}{{}_{2}}}}&&0\\
&\ddots&\\
0&&A_{m}^{{}^{\frac{s-1}{{}_{2}}}}U_{m}B_{m}^{{}^{\frac{s-1}{{}_{2}}}}\end{array}$}\right]\right|^{q}~\right|\right|\right|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|~\left|\left[\scalebox{0.9}{$\begin{array}[]{ccc}A_{1}^{{}^{\frac{s-1}{{}_{2}}}}&&0\\
&\ddots&\\
0&&A_{m}^{{}^{\frac{s-1}{{}_{2}}}}\end{array}$}\right]\left[\scalebox{0.9}{$\begin{array}[]{ccc}U_{1}&&0\\
&\ddots&\\
0&&U_{m}\end{array}$}\right]\left[\scalebox{0.9}{$\begin{array}[]{ccc}B_{1}^{{}^{\frac{s-1}{{}_{2}}}}&&0\\
&\ddots&\\
0&&B_{m}^{{}^{\frac{s-1}{{}_{2}}}}\end{array}$}\right]\right|^{q}~\right|\right|\right|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|~\left|\left[\scalebox{0.9}{$\begin{array}[]{ccc}A_{1}^{{}^{\frac{s-1}{{}_{2}}}}&&0\\
&\ddots&\\
0&&A_{m}^{{}^{\frac{s-1}{{}_{2}}}}\end{array}$}\right]^{q}\left[\scalebox{0.9}{$\begin{array}[]{ccc}U_{1}&&0\\
&\ddots&\\
0&&U_{m}\end{array}$}\right]\left[\scalebox{0.9}{$\begin{array}[]{ccc}B_{1}^{{}^{\frac{s-1}{{}_{2}}}}&&0\\
&\ddots&\\
0&&B_{m}^{{}^{\frac{s-1}{{}_{2}}}}\end{array}$}\right]^{q}\right|~\right|\right|\right|

(by Lemma
3.1
)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|~\left[\scalebox{0.9}{$\begin{array}[]{ccc}\left|A_{1}^{{}^{\frac{(s-1)q}{{}_{2}}}}U_{1}B_{1}^{{}^{\frac{(s-1)q}{{}_{2}}}}\right|&&0\\
&\ddots&\\
0&&\left|A_{m}^{{}^{\frac{(s-1)q}{{}_{2}}}}U_{m}B_{m}^{{}^{\frac{(s-1)q}{{}_{2}}}}\right|\end{array}$}\right]~\right|\right|\right|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|~\left[\scalebox{0.9}{$\begin{array}[]{ccc}\left|A_{1}^{{}^{\frac{s}{{}_{2}}}}U_{1}B_{1}^{{}^{\frac{s}{{}_{2}}}}\right|&&0\\
&\ddots&\\
0&&\left|A_{m}^{{}^{\frac{s}{{}_{2}}}}U_{m}B_{m}^{{}^{\frac{s}{{}_{2}}}}\right|\end{array}$}\right]~\right|\right|\right|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{m}\left|A_{i}^{{}^{\frac{s}{{}_{2}}}}U_{i}B_{i}^{{}^{\frac{s}{{}_{2}}}}\right|~~\right|\right|\right|.\hskip 8.5359pt\text{(by Lemma \ref{Lem10})}

So for all unitarily invariant norms, we have
\left|\left|\left|~|X|^{q}~\right|\right|\right|\leq\left|\left|\left|\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{m}\left|A_{i}^{{}^{\frac{s}{{}_{2}}}}U_{i}B_{i}^{{}^{\frac{s}{{}_{2}}}}\right|~~\right|\right|\right|.

This completes the proof.
\Box

Theorem
3.3
and Theorem
3.4
are the main ingredients to get our main result, namely, Theorem
3.6
.

Theorem 3.3
.

Let
A_{i},B_{i}\in\mathbb{P}_{n}
for all
i=1,\cdots,m
and let
Z=[Z_{ij}]
be the block matrix such that
Z_{ij}=B_{i}^{{}^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}}\left(\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{m}A_{k}\right)B_{j}^{{}^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}}
for all
i,j=1,\cdots,m
. Then for all unitarily invariant norms and for all
s\geq 1
, we have

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{m}\left(A_{i}^{s}\sharp B_{i}^{s}\right)\right|\right|\right|\leq\left|\left|\left|Z^{{}^{\frac{s}{2}}}\right|\right|\right|.

(3.6)

Proof.
Let
A_{i},B_{i}\in\mathbb{P}_{n}
for all
i=~1,\cdots,m
and let
s>1
. Then, using Lemma
2.3
, we have
A_{i}^{s}\sharp B_{i}^{s}=A_{i}^{{}^{\frac{s}{{}_{2}}}}U_{i}B_{i}^{{}^{\frac{s}{{}_{2}}}}
for some unitary
U_{i}\in\mathbb{M}_{n}(\mathbb{C})
and for all
i=1,\cdots,m.
Let
X,Y\in\mathbb{M}_{mn}(\mathbb{C})
be given by

\displaystyle X=\left[\scalebox{0.8}{$\begin{array}[]{ccc}B_{1}^{{}^{\frac{s-1}{{}_{2}}}}U_{1}^{*}A_{1}^{{}^{\frac{s-1}{{}_{2}}}}&&0\\
&\ddots&\\
0&&B_{m}^{{}^{\frac{s-1}{{}_{2}}}}U_{m}^{*}A_{m}^{{}^{\frac{s-1}{{}_{2}}}}\end{array}$}\right]\mathrm{~and~}Y=\left[\scalebox{0.8}{$\begin{array}[]{ccccc}B_{1}^{{}^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}}&0&\cdots&0\\
\vdots&\vdots&\ddots&\vdots\\
B_{m}^{{}^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}}&0&\cdots&0\end{array}$}\right]\left[\scalebox{0.9}{$\begin{array}[]{ccc}A_{1}^{{}^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}}&\cdots&A_{m}^{{}^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}}\\
0&\cdots&0\\
\vdots&\ddots&\vdots\\
0&\cdots&0\end{array}$}\right].

We are to prove that
\left|\left|\left|\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{m}\left(A_{i}^{s}\sharp B_{i}^{s}\right)\right|\right|\right|\leq~\left|\left|\left|Z^{{}^{\frac{s}{2}}}\right|\right|\right|
for all unitarily invariant norms.
To see this, note that

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|\sum_{i=1}^{m}\left(A_{i}^{s}\sharp B_{i}^{s}\right)\right|\right|\right|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|\sum_{i=1}^{m}\left(A_{i}^{{}^{\frac{s}{{}_{2}}}}U_{i}B_{i}^{{}^{\frac{s}{{}_{2}}}}\right)\right|\right|\right|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}\left(A_{i}^{{}^{\frac{s}{{}_{2}}}}U_{i}B_{i}^{{}^{\frac{s}{{}_{2}}}}\right)\right)\oplus 0\right|\right|\right|\hskip 8.5359pt

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|\left[\begin{array}[]{ccc}\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{m}\left(A_{i}^{{}^{\frac{s}{{}_{2}}}}U_{i}B_{i}^{{}^{\frac{s}{{}_{2}}}}\right)&0\\
0&0\end{array}\right]\right|\right|\right|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|\left[\scalebox{0.9}{$\begin{array}[]{ccc}A_{1}^{{}^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}}&\cdots&A_{m}^{{}^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}}\\
0&\cdots&0\\
\vdots&\ddots&\vdots\\
0&\cdots&0\end{array}$}\right]\left[\scalebox{0.9}{$\begin{array}[]{ccc}A_{1}^{{}^{\frac{s-1}{{}_{2}}}}U_{1}B_{1}^{{}^{\frac{s-1}{{}_{2}}}}&&0\\
&\ddots&\\
0&&A_{m}^{{}^{\frac{s-1}{{}_{2}}}}U_{m}B_{m}^{{}^{\frac{s-1}{{}_{2}}}}\end{array}$}\right]\left[\scalebox{0.9}{$\begin{array}[]{ccccc}B_{1}^{{}^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}}&0&\cdots&0\\
\vdots&\vdots&\ddots&\vdots\\
B_{m}^{{}^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}}&0&\cdots&0\end{array}$}\right]\right|\right|\right|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|\left[\scalebox{0.9}{$\begin{array}[]{ccc}A_{1}^{{}^{\frac{s-1}{{}_{2}}}}U_{1}B_{1}^{{}^{\frac{s-1}{{}_{2}}}}&&0\\
&\ddots&\\
0&&A_{m}^{{}^{\frac{s-1}{{}_{2}}}}U_{m}B_{m}^{{}^{\frac{s-1}{{}_{2}}}}\end{array}$}\right]\left[\scalebox{0.9}{$\begin{array}[]{ccccc}B_{1}^{{}^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}}&0&\cdots&0\\
\vdots&\vdots&\ddots&\vdots\\
B_{m}^{{}^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}}&0&\cdots&0\end{array}$}\right]\left[\scalebox{0.9}{$\begin{array}[]{ccc}A_{1}^{{}^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}}&\cdots&A_{m}^{{}^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}}\\
0&\cdots&0\\
\vdots&\ddots&\vdots\\
0&\cdots&0\end{array}$}\right]\right|\right|\right|

(by Lemma
2.5
)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|XY\right|\right|\right|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle|||~|X|^{q}~|||^{{}^{\frac{1}{q}}}|||~|Y|^{s}~|||^{{}^{\frac{1}{s}}}\hskip 8.5359pt\text{(by (Lemma \ref{Lem3}))}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{m}\left|A_{i}^{{}^{\frac{s}{{}_{2}}}}U_{i}B_{i}^{{}^{\frac{s}{{}_{2}}}}\right|~~\right|\right|\right|^{\frac{1}{q}}|||~|Y|^{s}~|||^{\frac{1}{s}}\hskip 8.5359pt\text{(by Lemma \ref{The2})}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{m}\left(A_{i}^{s}\sharp B_{i}^{s}\right)\right|\right|\right|^{{}^{\frac{1}{q}}}|||~|Y|^{s}~|||^{{}^{\frac{1}{s}}}.

This shows that

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|\sum_{i=1}^{m}\left(A_{i}^{s}\sharp B_{i}^{s}\right)\right|\right|\right|\leq\left|\left|\left|\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{m}\left(A_{i}^{s}\sharp B_{i}^{s}\right)\right|\right|\right|^{{}^{\frac{1}{q}}}|||~|Y|^{s}~|||^{{}^{\frac{1}{s}}}.

Thus

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{m}\left(A_{i}^{{}^{s}}\sharp B_{i}^{{}^{s}}\right)\right|\right|\right|^{1-\frac{1}{q}}\leq|||~|Y|^{s}~|||^{{}^{\frac{1}{s}}}.

But
1-\frac{1}{q}=\frac{1}{s},
so for all unitarily invariant norms, we have

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{m}\left(A_{i}^{s}\sharp B_{i}^{s}\right)\right|\right|\right|\leq|||~|Y|^{s}~|||.

(3.11)

Now, for all unitarily invariant norms, note that

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|~|Y|^{s}~\right|\right|\right|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|~(Y^{*}Y)^{{}^{\frac{s}{{}_{2}}}}~\right|\right|\right|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|~(YY^{*})^{{}^{\frac{s}{{}_{2}}}}~\right|\right|\right|\hskip 8.5359pt\text{(by Lemma \ref{Lem12})}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|Z^{{}^{\frac{s}{2}}}\right|\right|\right|.\hskip 8.5359pt\text{(by (Lemma \ref{Lem4}))}

So for all unitarily invariant norms, we have

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|~|Y|^{s}~\right|\right|\right|=\left|\left|\left|Z^{{}^{\frac{s}{2}}}\right|\right|\right|.

(3.12)

From (
3.11
) and (
3.12
), we get

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{m}\left(A_{i}^{s}\sharp B_{i}^{s}\right)\right|\right|\right|\leq\left|\left|\left|Z^{{}^{\frac{s}{2}}}\right|\right|\right|,

for all unitarily invariant norms. So we are done. For the case
s=1
, using (
3
) and (
3.12
), we have

\left|\left|\left|\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_{i}\sharp B_{i}\right|\right|\right|\leq\left|\left|\left|Y\right|\right|\right|=\left|\left|\left|Z^{{}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right|\right|\right|,

for all unitarily invariant norms. This completes the proof.
\Box

Theorem 3.4
.

Let
A_{i},B_{i}\in\mathbb{P}_{n}
for all
i=1,\cdots,m
. Let
Z=[Z_{ij}]
be the block matrix such that
Z_{ij}=B_{i}^{{}^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}}\left(\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{m}A_{k}\right)B_{j}^{{}^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}}
for all
i,j=1,\cdots,m
. Then for all unitarily invariant norms and for all
s\geq 2,
we have

\left|\left|\left|Z^{{}^{\frac{s}{{}_{2}}}}\right|\right|\right|\leq\left|\left|\left|\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_{i}\right)^{\frac{s}{{}_{4}}}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}B_{i}\right)^{\frac{s}{{}_{2}}}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_{i}\right)^{\frac{s}{{}_{4}}}\right|\right|\right|.

Proof.
Let
A_{i},B_{i}\in\mathbb{P}_{n}
for all
i=1,\cdots,m
and let
s\geq 2
. Let
Z=[Z_{ij}]
be the block matrix such that
Z_{ij}=B_{i}^{{}^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}}\left(\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{m}A_{k}\right)B_{j}^{{}^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}}
for all
i,j=1,\cdots,m
.
We have

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|Z^{{}^{\frac{s}{2}}}\right|\right|\right|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|\left(\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_{i}\right)^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}B_{i}\right)\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_{i}\right)^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}\right)^{\frac{s}{{}_{2}}}\right|\right|\right|\hskip 8.5359pt\text{(by (Lemma \ref{Lem5}))}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_{i}\right)^{\frac{s}{{}_{4}}}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}B_{i}\right)^{\frac{s}{{}_{2}}}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_{i}\right)^{\frac{s}{{}_{4}}}\right|\right|\right|.

\displaystyle\hskip 71.13188pt\left(\text{by Lemma \ref{Lem14} and because $\frac{s}{2}\geq 1$}\right)

So for all unitarily invariant norms, we have

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|Z^{{}^{\frac{s}{{}_{2}}}}\right|\right|\right|\leq\left|\left|\left|\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_{i}\right)^{\frac{s}{{}_{4}}}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}B_{i}\right)^{\frac{s}{{}_{2}}}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_{i}\right)^{\frac{s}{{}_{4}}}\right|\right|\right|.

This completes the proof.
\Box

We combine Theorem
3.3
and Theorem
3.4
to get the following corollary.

Corollary 3.5
.

Let
A_{i},B_{i}\in\mathbb{P}_{n}
for all
i=1,\cdots,m
. Let
Z=[Z_{ij}]
be the block matrix such that
Z_{ij}=B_{i}^{{}^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}}\left(\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{m}A_{k}\right)B_{j}^{{}^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}}
for all
i,j=1,\cdots,m
. Then for all unitarily invariant norms and for all
s\geq 2,
we have

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{m}\left(A_{i}^{s}\sharp B_{i}^{s}\right)\right|\right|\right|\leq\left|\left|\left|Z^{{}^{\frac{s}{{}_{2}}}}\right|\right|\right|\leq\left|\left|\left|\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_{i}\right)^{\frac{s}{{}_{4}}}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}B_{i}\right)^{\frac{s}{{}_{2}}}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_{i}\right)^{\frac{s}{{}_{4}}}\right|\right|\right|.

Proof.
Using Theorem
3.3
and Theorem
3.4
, we are done.
\Box

Now, let us prove our main theorem using Corollary
3.5
.

Theorem 3.6
.

Let
A_{i},B_{i}\in\mathbb{P}_{n}
for all
i=1,\cdots,m
and let
p>0
and
r\geq 1
such that
rp\geq 1
. Let
Z=[Z_{ij}]
be the block matrix such that
Z_{ij}=B_{i}^{{}^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}}\left(\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{m}A_{k}\right)B_{j}^{{}^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}}
for all
i,j=1,\cdots,m
. Then for all unitarily invariant norms and for all
s\geq 2,
we have

\left|\left|\left|\sum_{i=1}^{m}\left(A_{i}^{s}\sharp B_{i}^{s}\right)^{r}\right|\right|\right|\leq\left|\left|\left|Z^{{}^{\frac{sr}{{}_{2}}}}\right|\right|\right|\leq\left|\left|\left|\left(\left(\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_{i}\right)^{\frac{srp}{{}_{4}}}\left(\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{m}B_{i}\right)^{\frac{srp}{{}_{2}}}\left(\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_{i}\right)^{\frac{srp}{{}_{4}}}\right)^{\frac{1}{{}_{p}}}\right|\right|\right|.

Proof.
Let
A_{i},B_{i}\in\mathbb{P}_{n}
for all
i=1,\cdots,m
and let
s\geq 2
,
p>0
and
r\geq 1
such that
rp\geq 1
. Let
Z=[Z_{ij}]
be the block matrix such that
Z_{ij}=B_{i}^{{}^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}}\left(\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{m}A_{k}\right)B_{j}^{{}^{\frac{1}{{}_{2}}}}
for all
i,j=1,\cdots,m
. We are to prove that for all unitarily invariant norms, we have

\left|\left|\left|\sum_{i=1}^{m}\left(A_{i}^{s}\sharp B_{i}^{s}\right)^{r}\right|\right|\right|\leq\left|\left|\left|Z^{{}^{\frac{sr}{{}_{2}}}}\right|\right|\right|\leq\left|\left|\left|\left(\left(\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_{i}\right)^{\frac{srp}{{}_{4}}}\left(\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{m}B_{i}\right)^{\frac{srp}{{}_{2}}}\left(\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_{i}\right)^{\frac{srp}{{}_{4}}}\right)^{\frac{1}{{}_{p}}}\right|\right|\right|.

Using Corollary
3.5
and the fact that
s\geq 2
, we have

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{m}\left(A_{i}^{s}\sharp B_{i}^{s}\right)\right|\right|\right|\leq\left|\left|\left|Z^{{}^{\frac{s}{{}_{2}}}}\right|\right|\right|\leq\left|\left|\left|\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_{i}\right)^{\frac{s}{{}_{4}}}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}B_{i}\right)^{\frac{s}{{}_{2}}}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_{i}\right)^{\frac{s}{{}_{4}}}\right|\right|\right|.

From Lemma
2.6
and the fact that
r\geq 1
, we get

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|\left(\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{m}\left(A_{i}^{s}\sharp B_{i}^{s}\right)\right)^{r}\right|\right|\right|\leq\left|\left|\left|\left(Z^{{}^{\frac{s}{{}_{2}}}}\right)^{r}\right|\right|\right|\leq\left|\left|\left|\left(\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_{i}\right)^{\frac{s}{{}_{4}}}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}B_{i}\right)^{\frac{s}{{}_{2}}}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_{i}\right)^{\frac{s}{{}_{4}}}\right)^{r}\right|\right|\right|.\hskip 14.22636pt

(3.13)

Using Lemma
2.8
and since the function
f:[0,\infty)\longrightarrow[0,\infty)
given by
f(x)=x^{r}

is convex, it follows that

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|\sum_{i=1}^{m}\left(A_{i}^{s}\sharp B_{i}^{s}\right)^{r}\right|\right|\right|\leq\left|\left|\left|\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_{i}^{s}\sharp B_{i}^{s}\right)^{r}\right|\right|\right|.

(3.14)

Now, using (
3.13
) and (
3.14
), we have

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|\sum_{i=1}^{m}\left(A_{i}^{s}\sharp B_{i}^{s}\right)^{r}\right|\right|\right|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_{i}^{s}\sharp B_{i}^{s}\right)^{r}\right|\right|\right|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|\left(Z^{{}^{\frac{s}{{}_{2}}}}\right)^{r}\right|\right|\right|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|\left(\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_{i}\right)^{\frac{s}{{}_{4}}}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}B_{i}\right)^{\frac{s}{{}_{2}}}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_{i}\right)^{\frac{s}{{}_{4}}}\right)^{r}\right|\right|\right|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|\left(\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_{i}\right)^{\frac{s}{{}_{4}}}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}B_{i}\right)^{\frac{s}{{}_{2}}}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_{i}\right)^{\frac{s}{{}_{4}}}\right)^{\frac{rp}{{}_{p}}}\right|\right|\right|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\left|\left|\left|\left(\left(\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_{i}\right)^{\frac{srp}{{}_{4}}}\left(\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{m}B_{i}\right)^{\frac{srp}{{}_{2}}}\left(\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_{i}\right)^{\frac{srp}{{}_{4}}}\right)^{\frac{1}{{}_{p}}}\right|\right|\right|.

(by Lemma
2.1
and because
pr\geq 1
)

This shows that

\left|\left|\left|\sum_{i=1}^{m}\left(A_{i}^{s}\sharp B_{i}^{s}\right)^{r}\right|\right|\right|\leq\left|\left|\left|Z^{{}^{\frac{sr}{{}_{2}}}}\right|\right|\right|\leq\left|\left|\left|\left(\left(\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_{i}\right)^{\frac{srp}{{}_{4}}}\left(\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{m}B_{i}\right)^{\frac{srp}{{}_{2}}}\left(\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_{i}\right)^{\frac{srp}{{}_{4}}}\right)^{\frac{1}{{}_{p}}}\right|\right|\right|,

for every unitarily invariant norms.
This completes the proof.
\Box

.

for all

\left|\left|\left|\sum_{i=1}^{m}\left(A_{i}^{s}\sharp B_{i}^{s}\right)^{r}\right|\right|\right|\leq\left|\left|\left|\left(\left(\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_{i}\right)^{\frac{(1-t)srp}{{}_{2}}}\left(\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{m}B_{i}\right)^{tsrp}\left(\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{m}A_{i}\right)^{\frac{(1-t)srp}{{}_{2}}}\right)^{\frac{1}{{}_{p}}}\right|\right|\right|,
