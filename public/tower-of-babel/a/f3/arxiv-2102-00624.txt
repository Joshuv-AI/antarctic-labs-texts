A canonical connection on bundles on Riemann surfaces and Quillen connection on the theta bundle
Indranil Biswas, Jacques Hurtubise

Abstract
. We investigate the symplectic geometric and also the differential geometric aspects of the moduli space of connections on a compact connected Riemann surface X X . Fix a theta characteristic K X 1 / 2 K^{1/2}_{X} on X X ; it defines a theta divisor on the moduli space ℳ {\mathcal{M}} of stable vector bundles on X X of rank r r degree zero. Given a vector bundle E ∈ ℳ E\,\in\,{\mathcal{M}} lying outside the theta divisor, we construct a natural holomorphic connection on E E that depends holomorphically on E E . Using this holomorphic connection, we construct a canonical holomorphic isomorphism between the following two: (1) the moduli space 𝒞 \mathcal{C} of pairs ( E , D ) (E,\,D) , where E ∈ ℳ E\,\in\,{\mathcal{M}} and D D is a holomorphic connection on E E , and (2) the space Conn ⁡ ( Θ ) {\rm Conn}(\Theta) given by the sheaf of holomorphic connections on the line bundle on ℳ \mathcal{M} associated to the theta divisor. The above isomorphism between 𝒞 \mathcal{C} and Conn ⁡ ( Θ ) {\rm Conn}(\Theta) is symplectic structure preserving, and it moves holomorphically as X X runs over a holomorphic family of Riemann surfaces.

4.
The two isomorphisms of torsors coincide

The following theorem is the main result proved here.

Theorem 4.1
.

The restriction of the isomorphism

G
in

Proof.

In view of the first condition in both Theorem

and Lemma

,

\Gamma_{0}\,:\,{\mathcal{C}}^{0}\,\longrightarrow\,T^{*}{\mathcal{M}}^{0}\,,\ \ z\,\longmapsto\,F(z)-G(z)\,.

In other words,

This map
\Gamma_{0}
is holomorphic because both

and

\alpha\,\in\,\widehat{\varphi}^{-1}(E)\,\subset\,{\mathcal{C}}^{0}\,,

\widehat{\varphi}

is the projection in (
3.12

\Gamma_{0}(\alpha+\nu)\,=\,F(\alpha+\nu)-G(\alpha+\nu)\,=\,F(\alpha)-G(\alpha)+2r\cdot\nu-2r\cdot\nu\,=\,\Gamma_{0}(\alpha)\,.

\Gamma_{0}\,=\,\Gamma\circ\widehat{\varphi}

where

is the projection in (

5.

Let

be a connected complex manifold, and let

, parametrized by

This means that

Let

(5.1)

be the holomorphic fiber bundle over

relative holomorphic connections
on
\Theta_{\mathcal{T}}

\text{Conn}(\Theta)
