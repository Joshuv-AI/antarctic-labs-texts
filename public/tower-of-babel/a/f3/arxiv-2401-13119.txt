A comparison theorem between Voevodsky’s motives and motives with modulus in positive characteristic with rational coefficients
Keiho Matsumoto

Abstract
. In this note, without the assumption of resolution of singularities, we prove an equivalence between the category of motives with modulus in positive characteristic with rational coefficients 𝐌𝐃𝐌 eff ⁡ ( k , ℚ ) \operatorname{\mathbf{MDM}}^{\operatorname{eff}}(k,\mathbb{Q}) and the Voevodsky’s category of motives 𝐃𝐌 eff ⁡ ( k , ℚ ) \operatorname{\mathbf{DM}}^{\operatorname{eff}}(k,\mathbb{Q}) , which has been philosophically predicted from the beginning in the study of motives with modulus.

Let
