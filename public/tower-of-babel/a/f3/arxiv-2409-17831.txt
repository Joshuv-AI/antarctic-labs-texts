\displaystyle\mathrm{Pr}\!\left[S>(1+\delta)\mu\right]

\displaystyle<\!\left(\frac{e^{\delta}}{(1+\delta)^{1+\delta}}\right)^{\mu}.

Theorem C.3

(Theorem 6
[
LM24
]
)
.

Let
X_{1},\ldots,X_{m}
be i.i.d Bernoulli random variables with mean at most
\mu
and let
S=\sum_{i\in[m]}X_{i}
. Then, for any integer
\tau>\mu m
, we have that

\displaystyle\operatorname{\mathbb{E}}[S-\min\{S,\tau\}]\leq\!\left(\frac{\mu m}{\tau-\mu m}\right)^{2}.
