On the Lattice of Boundaries and the Entropy Spectrum of Hyperbolic Groups
Samuel Dodds

Abstract
. Let Γ \Gamma be a non-elementary hyperbolic group and μ \mu be a probability on Γ \Gamma . We study the μ \mu -proximal, stationary actions, also known as boundary actions, of Γ \Gamma . In particular, we are interested in the spectrum of Furstenberg entropies of ( Γ , μ ) (\Gamma,\mu) -boundaries, and the lattice-theoretic and topological structure of the set ℬ ​ ℒ ​ ( Γ , μ ) \mathcal{BL}(\Gamma,\mu) of boundaries. We prove that all hyperbolic groups have infinitely many distinct boundaries, which attain an infinite set of distinct entropies. Additionally, for simple random walks on non-abelian free groups F d F_{d} , we establish that there are infinitely many boundaries whose entropy is greater than 1 2 − ϵ \frac{1}{2}-\epsilon times the entropy of Poisson boundary, when the rank d d is large. General results of independent interest about the order-theoretic and continuity properties of Furstenberg entropy for countable groups are attained along the way. This includes the result that under mild assumptions, the spectrum of boundary entropies ℋ bound ​ ( Γ , μ ) \mathcal{H}_{\text{bound}}(\Gamma,\mu) is closed.

Let
\Gamma

[
10

[

There are typically many stationary boundaries associated to a single measured group
(\Gamma,\mu)

is an (order-theoretic) lattice. The order is given by

(\partial_{P}(\Gamma,\mu),\nu_{P})
and the minimal element is always the trivial boundary
(\ast,\delta_{\ast})

Entropy is a key tool in the study of stationary spaces. The particular variety of entropy which is most adapted to this situation is that of

: if

is a stationary

-probability space, then set

Furstenberg entropy is decreasing under factors, and strictly decreasing under non-trivial factors between boundaries. The entropy of the trivial boundary is 0, and the entropy of the Poisson boundary

is finite under the assumption that

. We will take this as granted for the remainder of the paper.

For each measured group
