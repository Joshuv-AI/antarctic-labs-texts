Sanjay More, Anil Khairnar and B. N. Waphare

Abstract




2010 Mathematics Subject Classification
Primary 16W10; Secondary 16D25

Abstract:

S. K. Berberian raised the open problem “Can every weakly Rickart
*
-ring be
embedded in a Rickart
*
-ring? with preservation of right projections?” Berberian has given a partial solution to this problem. Khairnar and Waphare raised a similar problem for p.q.-Baer
*

*
-rings, weakly p.q.-Baer
*
-rings, projections, central cover.
