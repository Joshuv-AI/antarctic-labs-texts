Central Weyl involutions on Fano–Mukai fourfolds of genus 10 10
Mikhail Zaidenberg

Abstract
. It is known that every Fano–Mukai fourfold X X of genus 10 is acted upon by an involution τ \tau which comes from the center of the Weyl group of the simple algebraic group of type G 2 {\rm G}_{2} , see [ PZ18 , PZ22 ] . This involution is uniquely defined up to conjugation in the group Aut ⁡ ( X ) \operatorname{{Aut}}(X) . In this note we describe the set of fixed points of τ \tau and the surface scroll swept out by the τ \tau -invariant lines.

Introduction

Let
V
be a Fano–Mukai fourfold of genus 10 over
{\mathbb{C}}
, that is, a smooth
Fano fourfold of index 2 and of degree 18. A classification of the automorphism groups

In particular, every

[

10

\operatorname{{Aut}}^{0}(V)

The main result of the present note is the following theorem.

Theorem 0.1
.

Let
V
be a Fano–Mukai fourfold of genus
10
half-anticanonically embedded in
{\mathbb{P}}^{12}
,
and let
\tau\in\operatorname{{Aut}}(V)\setminus\operatorname{{Aut}}^{0}(V)

Then
\tau\in G

The fixed point set

is a union of two disjoint smooth rational sextic curves

and

such that

\langle E^{+}\rangle={\mathbb{P}}^{5},\quad N_{E^{+}/{\mathbb{P}}^{5}}={\mathcal{O}}_{{\mathbb{P}}^{1}}(8)^{\oplus 2}\oplus{\mathcal{O}}_{{\mathbb{P}}^{1}}(9)^{\oplus 2}\quad\text{and}\quad\langle E^{-}\rangle={\mathbb{P}}^{6},\quad N_{E^{-}/{\mathbb{P}}^{6}}={\mathcal{O}}_{{\mathbb{P}}^{1}}(8)^{\oplus 5}.

Furthermore,

in

verifying the following.

(i)

Each ruling of
\Pi
is
\tau
-invariant and each

\tau

(ii)

\tau

In the notation of Subsection

We show that the first normal bundle is almost balanced and the second is balanced. More precisely, we have the following

Actually, the first equality is well known. Indeed, let

N_{\mathcal{C}/{\mathbb{P}}^{n}}=\bigoplus_{i=1}^{n-1}{\mathcal{O}}_{{\mathbb{P}}^{1}}(d+b_{i})\quad\text{where}\quad b_{i}\geq 2\quad\text{and}\quad\sum_{i=1}^{n-1}b_{i}=2d-2,
