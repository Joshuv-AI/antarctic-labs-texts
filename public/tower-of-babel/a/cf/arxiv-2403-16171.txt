q q -Deformed glueballs spectrum in AdS/QCD correspondence
Fabiano F. Santos 1 and F. A. Brito 2,3

Abstract
This work presents the application of the q q -algebra in the glueballs spectrum. This algebra is implemented through Jackson derivatives in a Schrödinger-like equation resulting from the gravity fluctuations around the braneworld scenario in five dimensions. In our prescription, we consider a four-dimensional AdS 4 brane, living in AdS 5 bulk, that is also known for describing locally localized gravity via quasi-zero mode. At the appropriate limit, this background leads to confinement and allows us to find the shape of the q q -deformed glueball spectrum in the AdS 5 /QCD 4 correspondence. The introduction of the q q -deformation provides us with a richer glueball spectrum.
