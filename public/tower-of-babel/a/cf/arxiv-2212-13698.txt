Abstract
A new iteration method is represented to study the interior L p L_{p} regularity for Stokes systems both in divergence form and in non-divergence form. By the iteration, we improve the integrability of derivatives of solutions for Stokes systems step by step; after infinitely many steps, L p L_{p} regularity is achieved; and in each step, the maximal function method is used where solutions and their derivatives are involved simultaneously in each scale. The Hölder continuity of the coefficients in spatial variables is assumed to compensate the different scalings between the solutions and their derivatives.

Introduction

We consider Stokes systems both in divergence form and in non-divergence form that are the following

\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\vskip 5.69054ptu_{t}+\mathcal{L}_{1}u+\nabla\pi=\mbox{div}f~~\mbox{in}~~\Omega_{T},\\
\vskip 5.69054pt\mbox{div}~u=g~~\mbox{in}~~\Omega_{T}\end{array}\right.

(1.1)

and

\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\vskip 5.69054ptu_{t}+\mathcal{L}_{2}u+\nabla\pi=f~~\mbox{in}~~\Omega_{T},\\
\vskip 5.69054pt\mbox{div}~u=g~~\mbox{in}~~\Omega_{T}\end{array}\right.

(1.2)

respectively, with

\mathcal{L}_{1}:=-\mbox{div}\Big(A(x,t)\nabla\Big)=:-D_{i}\Big(a^{ij}(x,t)D_{j}\Big)

and

\mathcal{L}_{2}:=-A(x,t)D^{2}=:-a^{ij}(x,t)D_{ij},

where
1\leq i,j\leq n
with
n\geq 2
,
\Omega
is a bounded domain in
\mathbb{R}^{n}
and
\Omega_{T}=\Omega\times(-T,0]
is a cylinder with

.
The summation convention is assumed throughout the paper.

Both Stokes systems in the form (
1.1
) and (
1.2

[
1
]
) and also from Stokes systems on manifolds (c.f. e.g.
[
3

[
11

Serrin’s counterexample (cf.
[
12
]
and Remark 1.3 (iii) in the following) indicates that the interior regularity of Stokes systems is hard and will be essentially different from parabolic systems. The purpose of this paper is to investigate the interior
L_{p}
regularity of them. For (
1.1
), we shall improve the integrability of
Du
, that is, we will start from
Du\in L_{p_{0}}
and arrive at
Du\in L_{p}
under the assumption that

,

and

for some
1<p_{0}<p<\infty
. Here

is needed to provide the higher integrability in time direction, which can not be removed (cf. Remark 1.3 (iii) below) and this reveals that Stokes systems have very weak interior regularity in time direction. A new iteration method is designed so that the integrability of
Du
is improved step by step and after infinitely many steps, the
L_{p}
regularity (integrability) is achieved. In each step, the maximal function method is used and we gain the regularity from both parabolic equations and elliptic equations. Indeed,
\nabla\times u
(
see the notation list at the end of this section
) satisfies a parabolic equation and
Du
can be estimated by
\nabla\times u
and
u
via an elliptic equation. Thus,
u
and
\nabla\times u
should be involved simultaneously, but unfortunately they have different scalings. To overcome this difficulty, we introduce a new and important quantity
\Theta
which connects
u
and
Du
and whose scaling is between
u
and
Du
. The Hölder continuity of the coefficients
a^{ij}
in the spatial variables is assumed to compensate the different scalings of these quantities. For (1.2), we will improve the integrability of
D^{2}u
from
L_{p_{0}}
to

for some

The interior

regularity of Stokes systems has not been well developed yet.
As for constant coefficients case, Hu, Li and Wang
[
6
]
establish interior

regularity for
\Delta u
, that is,
\Delta u\in L_{p}
is attained under the assumption
u\in W^{2,1}_{2}
and righthand side
f\in L_{p}
with
p>2
; and if
u\in L_{p}
is further assumed,
D^{2}u\in L_{p}
can be obtained.
Observe that in
[
6
]
since the coefficients of the Stokes system are constant already, we do not need to transform it to a new system with coefficients depending only on

as what is done in this paper. Consequently in
[
6
]
, while using maximal function method, in each scale, only
\Delta u
and
f
are involved which have the same scaling. Therefore, the iteration argument designed in this paper is not needed there. In
[
10

, Meyers improved integrability of the first derivatives of solutions of elliptic equations in divergence form from
L_{2}
to
L_{p}
for some

which may be very closed to 2, where the righthand sides of the equations are in the space
W^{-1}_{p}
and no continuity of the coefficients is needed.
By this technique,
Kaplick
\acute{y}
and Wolf
[
7
]
generalize Meyers’ result to Stokes systems in divergence form and they actually show that under the assumption
u\in L_{\infty}(0,T;L_{2}(\Omega))\cap L_{2}(0,T;W^{1}_{2}(\Omega))
, there exists some

with

such that

. We should mention here that under their assumption,

by Sobolev embedding theorem and this implies that the upper bound of

For a priori

estimates, Solonnikov
[
13
]
is the first important contribution which demonstrates a global a priori

[
14

further develops the result to the case for variable coefficients that are continuous and belong to

are assumed for the systems in divergence form and in non-divergence form respectively, and the assumptions are essentially used in the proofs.

In this paper, we make the following assumptions for the coefficient matrix

.

(i) In Theorem 1.1, since

(ii) If we strengthen the assumption to

in Theorem 1.2, the Condition (

to obtain (

(iii) Let

be a harmonic function in

and

with

,

. Then

is a pair of solution of (1.1) or (1.2) with

(cf.
[
12
]
and
[
