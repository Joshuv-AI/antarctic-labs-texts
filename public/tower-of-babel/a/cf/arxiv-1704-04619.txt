Two-step Runge-Kutta methods for Retarded Functional Differential Equations Department of Control systems, Siberian State Aerospace University, Krasnoyarsk, Russia, e-mail: e-mail: tuzov@sibsau.ru


Abstract
This paper presents a class of two-step Runge-Kutta (TSRK) methods for the numerical solution of Retarded Functional Differential Equations (RFDEs). A convergence theorem is formulated and proved. Explicit methods up to order five are constructed. To avoid order reduction for mildly stiff problems the uniform stage order of the methods is chosen to be closed to uniform order. Keywords : Functional differential equations; two-step Runge-Kutta methods; A-methods; order conditions. AMS Subject Classification: 65Q05
