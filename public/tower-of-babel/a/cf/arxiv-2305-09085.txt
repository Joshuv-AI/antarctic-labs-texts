REGULARITY AND DECAY OF GLOBAL SOLUTIONS FOR THE 4D NAVIER-STOKES EQUATIONS POSED ON SMOOTH DOMAINS. Thanks: † Corresponding author Keywords: Navier-Stokes equations; Lipschitz and Smooth Domains; Decay in Bounded Domains
N. A. Larkin & M. V. Padilha †

Abstract
. We consider an initial-boundary value problem for the 4D Navier-Stokes equations posed on bounded smooth domains. We prove the existence and uniqueness of regular solutions as well as their exponential decay and additional regularity properties have been established assuming restrictions on initial data.



1.
Introduction

In this work, we study an initial-boundary value problem for the 4D Navier-Stokes equations,

\displaystyle u_{t}-\nu\Delta u+(u\cdot\nabla)u+\nabla p=0,\;\;\mbox{in}\;\;\Omega\times{\mathbb{R}},

(1.1)

\displaystyle\nabla\cdot u=0\;\;\mbox{in}\;\;\Omega,\;\;\;u|_{\partial\Omega}=0,

(1.2)

\displaystyle u(x,0)=u_{0}(x),

(1.3)

where
x=(x_{1},x_{2},x_{3},x_{4})\in\Omega\subset{\mathbb{R}}^{4}
and
\Omega
is a bounded smooth domain.

[
10

This work oriented mathematicians to study existence, uniqueness and decay of the energy of solutions to the multi-dimensional Navier-Stokes equations

. The most advanced results were established in the two-dimensional case, where the existence of weak and regular global solutions, uniqueness and decay of the energy for bounded and unbounded Lipschitz domains were established without additional conditions of smallness for the initial data, see

[

Chapter III,
\lx@sectionsign 3.3
, Theorem 3.1;
[

Theorems 4.2, 4.5, 4.6. The situation is more complicated for three dimensions: to prove uniqueness and regularity of global solutions, some authors considered small initial data, see

[

, Chapter III, Theorems 3.2, 3.5, 3.6; while the others studied initial boundary value problems in “thin” domains, see
[

. In
[

, regularity, uniqueness and exponential decay of the energy has been proved making use of connection between the geometric sizes of a domain and initial data without involving the first eigenvalue of the projector operator of the Laplacian that was for a long time the standard approach (see

The existence of weak and strong global solutions to (

Our goal here is to consider the 4D Navier-Stokes system posed on smooth domains. Earlier, the problem (1.1)-(1.3) has been considered posed on parallelepipeds, bounded and unbounded
