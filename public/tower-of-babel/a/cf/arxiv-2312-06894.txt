Schwarz-Pick lemma for ( α , β ) (\alpha,\beta) -harmonic functions in the unit disc
Miloš Arsenović, Jelena Gajić

Abstract
. We obtain Schwarz–Pick lemma for ( α , β ) (\alpha,\beta) -harmonic functions u u in the disc, where α \alpha and β \beta are complex parameters satisfying ℜ ⁡ α + ℜ ⁡ β > − 1 \Re\alpha+\Re\beta>-1 . We prove sharp estimate of ‖ D ​ u ​ ( 0 ) ‖ \|Du(0)\| for such functions in terms of L p L^{p} norm of the boundary function. Also, we give asymptotically sharp estimate of ∥ D ​ u ​ ( z ) ∥ \lVert Du(z)\rVert . Estimates of ∥ D ​ u ​ ( z ) ∥ \lVert Du(z)\rVert are extended to higher order derivatives. Our results extend earlier results for α \alpha -harmonic and T α T_{\alpha} -harmonic functions.

1.
Introduction and notation

The open unit disc in the complex plane is denoted by
\mathbb{D}
and its boundary by
\partial\mathbb{D}=\mathbb{T}
.
We normalize standard measure on
\mathbb{T}
, so we have
m(\mathbb{T})=1
.
We will use standard first order defferential operators

\partial_{z}=\frac{\partial}{\partial z}=\frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{\partial}{\partial x}-i\frac{\partial}{\partial y}\right)\quad\mbox{and}\quad\partial_{\overline{z}}=\frac{\partial}{\partial\overline{z}}=\frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{\partial}{\partial x}+i\frac{\partial}{\partial y}\right).

The derivative of a
C^{1}
complex valued function
f
on a domain
\Omega\subset\mathbb{C}
at a point
z
in
\Omega
is
denoted by
Df(z)
. The norm of this linear map on
\mathbb{R}^{2}
is denoted by
\|Df(z)\|
and we have

(1.1)

\lVert Df(z)\rVert=\sup\{|Df(z)\zeta|:|\zeta|=1\}=|f_{z}(z)|+|f_{\overline{z}}(z)|.

Let, for
\alpha,\beta\in\mathbb{C}

L_{\alpha,\beta}=(1-|z|^{2})\left((1-|z|^{2})\frac{\partial^{2}}{\partial z\partial\overline{z}}+\alpha z\frac{\partial}{\partial z}+\beta\overline{z}\frac{\partial}{\partial\overline{z}}-\alpha\beta\right)

be a second order uniformly elliptic linear partial differential operator in the unit disc
\mathbb{D}
. These operators have been introduced and studied in higher dimensions by Geller
[
4
]
and Ahern et al.
[
1
]
and in the planar case in
[
8
]
. They are degenerate near the boundary of the unit disc (the unit ball in the multidimensional case, see
[
1
]
), except for the special case
\alpha=\beta=0
when
L_{\alpha,\beta}=4^{-1}(1-|z|^{2})^{2}\Delta
, where
\Delta
is the Laplace operator with respect to
x
and
y
variables.

A function
u
in
C^{2}(\mathbb{D})
is said to be
(\alpha,\beta)
-harmonic if
L_{\alpha,\beta}u=0
. The vector space of all such functions is denoted by

h_{\alpha,\beta}(\mathbb{D})
. If
\alpha>-1
the
(0,\alpha)
-harmonic
functions are called
\alpha
-harmonic and if
\alpha\in\mathbb{R}
the
(\frac{\alpha}{2},\frac{\alpha}{2})
-harmonic
functions are called
T_{\alpha}
-harmonic functions. These one parameter cases appeared in
[
13
,
6
,
10
,
9
,
11
,
7
]
and
[
12
]
.

Colonna
[
2
]
obtained Scwarz-Pick lemma for complex-valued harmonic function. More precisely, if
f
is a complex-valued harmonic functions from
\mathbb{D}
into itself, then

\lVert Df(z)\rVert\leq\frac{4}{\pi}\frac{1}{1-|z|^{2}},\qquad z\in\mathbb{D}.

The following results can be interpreted as Schwarz-Pick lemma for
(0,\alpha)
-harmonic functions.

Theorem 1.1
.

(
[
11
]
, Theorem 1.1.) Suppose that
f\in C(\overline{\mathbb{D}})
is an
\alpha
-harmonic function in
\mathbb{D}
with
\alpha>-1
, and that

\sup_{z\in\overline{\mathbb{D}}}|f(z)|\leq M,
where

is a constant. Then for

\lVert Df(z)\rVert\leq{M(\alpha+2)}\frac{\Gamma(\alpha+1)}{\Gamma^{2}\left(\frac{\alpha}{2}+1\right)}\frac{1}{1-|z|}\leq{2M(\alpha+2)}\frac{\Gamma(\alpha+1)}{\Gamma^{2}\left(\frac{\alpha}{2}+1\right)}\frac{1}{1-|z|^{2}}.

In particular, if
f
maps
\mathbb{D}
into
\mathbb{D},
then

\lVert Df(z)\rVert\leq{2(\alpha+2)}\frac{\Gamma(\alpha+1)}{\Gamma^{2}\left(\frac{\alpha}{2}+1\right)}\frac{1}{1-|z|^{2}}.

(

(1+\alpha)2^{1+\alpha}\lVert\varphi\rVert_{\infty}\frac{1}{1-|z|^{2}},&\alpha\geq 0.\end{array}\right.

Lemma 3.1

Theorem 3.2

Let

\sup_{\|\varphi\|_{p}\leq 1}\lVert DP_{\alpha,\beta}[\varphi](0)\rVert=\frac{|c_{\alpha,\beta}|}{2\pi}\left(\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}\left||\alpha+1|+|\beta+1|e^{2it}\right|^{q}dt\right)^{\frac{1}{q}},

Proof.

Using (

\displaystyle\sup_{\|\varphi\|_{p}\leq 1}\lVert DP_{\alpha,\beta}[\varphi](0)\rVert=

\displaystyle|c_{\alpha,\beta}|\sup_{\|\varphi\|_{p}\leq 1}I(\varphi)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{|c_{\alpha,\beta}|}{2\pi}\max_{|\eta|=1}\sup_{\|\varphi\|_{p}\leq 1}\left|\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}e^{-it}\varphi(e^{it})\left(\lambda_{1}+\lambda_{2}\frac{\overline{\eta}}{\eta}e^{2it}\right)dt\right|

\displaystyle\frac{|c_{\alpha,\beta}|}{2\pi}\max_{|\eta|=1}\left(\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}\left|\lambda_{1}+\lambda_{2}\frac{\overline{\eta}}{{\eta}}e^{2it}\right|^{q}dt\right)^{\frac{1}{q}}

Proposition 3.3
.

For all
\varphi\in L^{\infty}(\mathbb{T})
we have

\lVert DP_{\alpha,\beta}[\varphi](0)\rVert\leq D(\alpha,\beta)\lVert\varphi\rVert_{\infty}

\alpha>0,
by Legandre duplication formula

\Gamma(2z)=2^{2z-1}\pi^{-\frac{1}{2}}\Gamma(z)\Gamma(z+1/2),\Re z>0

estimate (
3.5
) gives

\displaystyle\lVert DP_{0,\alpha}[\varphi](z)\rVert\leq

\displaystyle\frac{(\alpha+1)2^{\alpha+1}\pi^{-\frac{1}{2}}\Gamma\left(\frac{\alpha}{2}+\frac{1}{2}\right)}{\Gamma\left(\frac{\alpha}{2}+1\right)}\cdot\frac{\lVert\varphi\rVert_{\infty}}{1-|z|^{2}}

(3.6)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{(\alpha+1)2^{\alpha+1}B\left(\frac{\alpha}{2}+\frac{1}{2},\frac{1}{2}\right)}{\pi}\cdot\frac{\lVert\varphi\rVert_{\infty}}{1-|z|^{2}}.

The function
g(\alpha)=B\left(\frac{\alpha}{2}+\frac{1}{2},\frac{1}{2}\right)/\pi
is strictly decreasing for
\alpha\in(0,+\infty)
and
g(0)=1.

Therefore
B\left(\frac{\alpha}{2}+\frac{1}{2},\frac{1}{2}\right)<\pi
and hence, the constant
\pi^{-1}(\alpha+1)2^{\alpha+1}B\left(\frac{\alpha}{2}+\frac{1}{2},\frac{1}{2}\right)
in (
3
) is strictly smaller than the constant

(\alpha+1)2^{\alpha+1}
appearing in Lemma
1.2
.

Lemma 3.6
.

Let
u_{\alpha,\beta}
be as in (
2.1
) for some
\alpha,\beta\in\mathbb{C}
. Then for
k\geq 0
and
l\geq 0
we have

\partial^{k}\overline{\partial}^{l}u_{\alpha,\beta}(z)=P_{k,l}\left(\frac{1}{1-|z|^{2}},\frac{1}{1-z},\frac{1}{1-\overline{z}},f_{1}(z),\ldots,f_{N}(z)\right)u_{\alpha,\beta}(z),

where
N=N_{k,l},
the functions
f_{1},\ldots,f_{N_{k,l}}
are
C^{\infty}
on
\mathbb{C}
and
P_{k,l}
is a polynomial whose total degree in
(1-|z|^{2})^{-1},(1-z)^{-1},(1-\overline{z})^{-1}
is equal to
k+l
. In other words,

P_{k,l}=P_{k,l}\left(\frac{1}{1-|z|^{2}},\frac{1}{1-z},\frac{1}{1-\overline{z}}\right)

is polynomial over an algebra
C^{\infty}(\mathbb{C})
and
\deg P_{k,l}=k+l
.

Proof.

We proceed by induction on
n=k+l
. The case
n=1
is Lemma
2.4
from
[
8
]
. Now
let
n>1
and assume that Lemma holds for
n-1.
Let

u=(1-|z|^{2})^{-1},v=(1-z)^{-1},w=(1-\overline{z})^{-1}
. Then

\displaystyle\partial^{k+1}\overline{\partial}^{l}u_{\alpha,\beta}(z)=

\displaystyle\partial\left(P_{k,l}\left(\frac{1}{1-|z|^{2}},\frac{1}{1-z},\frac{1}{1-\overline{z}},f_{1}(z),\ldots,f_{N}(z)\right)u_{\alpha,\beta}(z)\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left(\frac{\partial P_{k,l}}{\partial u}\frac{\overline{z}}{(1-|z|^{2})^{2}}+\frac{\partial P_{k,l}}{\partial v}\frac{1}{(1-z)^{2}}+\sum_{i=1}^{N}\frac{\partial P_{k,l}}{\partial f_{i}}\frac{\partial f_{i}}{\partial z}\right.

\displaystyle+

\displaystyle\left.P_{k,l}\frac{\alpha+1}{1-z}-P_{k,l}\frac{(\alpha+\beta+1)\overline{z}}{1-|z|^{2}}\right)u_{\alpha,\beta}(z)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle P_{k+1,l}u_{\alpha,\beta}(z)

and similarly

\displaystyle\partial^{k}\overline{\partial}^{l+1}u_{\alpha,\beta}(z)=

\displaystyle\overline{\partial}\left(P_{k,l}\left(\frac{1}{1-|z|^{2}},\frac{1}{1-z},\frac{1}{1-\overline{z}},f_{1}(z),\ldots,f_{N}(z)\right)u_{\alpha,\beta}(z)\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left(\frac{\partial P_{k,l}}{\partial u}\frac{{z}}{(1-|z|^{2})^{2}}+\frac{\partial P_{k,l}}{\partial w}\frac{1}{(1-\overline{z})^{2}}+\sum_{i=1}^{N}\frac{\partial P_{k,l}}{\partial f_{i}}\frac{\partial f_{i}}{\partial\overline{z}}\right.

\displaystyle+

\displaystyle\left.P_{k,l}\frac{\beta+1}{1-\overline{z}}-P_{k,l}\frac{(\alpha+\beta+1)z}{1-|z|^{2}}\right)u_{\alpha,\beta}(z)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle P_{k,l+1}u_{\alpha,\beta}(z).

Clearly
P_{k+1,l},P_{k,l+1}
are the polynomials whose total degree in
(1-|z|^{2})^{-1},(1-z)^{-1},(1-\overline{z})^{-1}
is equal to
k+l+1
.
∎

Remark 3.7
.

Ako je
k=l

P_{k,l}=P_{k,l}\left(\frac{1}{1-|z|^{2}},\frac{1}{1-z},\frac{1}{1-\overline{z}}\right)
polinom po tim promjenljivim sa konstantnim koeficijentima. (nema ni

Proposition 3.8
.

Let
u_{\alpha,\beta}
be as in (
2.1
) for some
\alpha,\beta\in\mathbb{C}
. Then

(3.7)

\left|\partial^{k}\overline{\partial}^{l}u_{\alpha,\beta}(z)\right|\leq C_{\alpha,\beta,k,l}\frac{|u_{\alpha,\beta}(z)|}{(1-|z|^{2})^{k+l}}\quad z\in\mathbb{D}.

Proof.

The proposition follows from Lemma
3.6
and the fact that
\frac{1}{2}(1-|z|^{2})\leq 1-|z|\leq|1-z|=|1-\overline{z}|
for all
z\in\mathbb{D}
.
∎

The next theorem extends Theorem
3.5
to higher order derivatives.

Theorem 3.9
.

Let
\varphi\in L^{p}(\mathbb{T}),1\leq p\leq+\infty
. Then

(3.8)

\left|\partial^{k}\overline{\partial}^{l}u(z)\right|\leq C_{\alpha,\beta,k,l,p}\frac{\lVert\varphi\rVert_{p}}{(1-|z|^{2})^{k+l+\frac{1}{p}}},\qquad u=P_{\alpha,\beta}[\varphi].

Proof.

By Proposition
3.8
we have

\left|\partial^{k}\overline{\partial}^{l}u(z)\right|=\left|c_{\alpha,\beta}\int_{\mathbb{T}}\partial^{k}\overline{\partial}^{l}u_{\alpha,\beta}(z\overline{\zeta})\varphi(\zeta)dm(\zeta)\right|\leq C_{\alpha,\beta,k,l}\int_{\mathbb{T}}\frac{|u_{\alpha,\beta}(z\overline{\zeta})|}{(1-|z|^{2})^{k+l}}|\varphi(\zeta)|dm(\zeta),\qquad z\in\mathbb{D}.

If
p=+\infty
, then

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{T}}\frac{|u_{\alpha,\beta}(z\overline{\zeta})|}{(1-|z|^{2})^{k+l}}|\varphi(\zeta)|dm(\zeta)\leq

\displaystyle\frac{\lVert\varphi\rVert_{\infty}}{(1-|z|^{2})^{k+l}}\int_{\mathbb{T}}|u_{\alpha,\beta}(z\overline{\zeta})|dm(\zeta)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle e^{\frac{\pi}{2}|\Im\alpha-\Im\beta|}\frac{\Gamma(\Re\alpha+\Re\beta+1)}{\Gamma^{2}\left(\frac{\Re\alpha+\Re\beta+2}{2}\right)}\frac{\lVert\varphi\rVert_{\infty}}{(1-|z|^{2})^{k+l}},

and, if
p=1,
using estimate (
3.4
), we have

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{T}}\frac{|u_{\alpha,\beta}(z\overline{\zeta})|}{(1-|z|^{2})^{k+l}}|\varphi(\zeta)|dm(\zeta)\leq

\displaystyle\frac{C_{\alpha,\beta}}{(1-|z|^{2})^{k+l+1}}\int_{\mathbb{T}}|\varphi(\zeta)|dm(\zeta)=\frac{C_{\alpha,\beta}}{(1-|z|^{2})^{k+l+1}}\lVert\varphi\rVert_{L^{1}(\mathbb{T})}.

For
1<p<\infty
using H
\ddot{o}
lder inequality and Lemma
3.1
, we obtain

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{T}}\frac{|u_{\alpha,\beta}(z\overline{\zeta})|}{(1-|z|^{2})^{k+l}}|\varphi(\zeta)|dm(\zeta)\leq

\displaystyle\frac{\lVert\varphi\rVert_{L^{p}(\mathbb{T})}}{(1-|z|^{2})^{k+l}}\left(\int_{\mathbb{T}}|u_{\alpha,\beta}(z\overline{\zeta})|^{q}dm(\zeta)\right)^{\frac{1}{q}}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{C_{\alpha,\beta,p}\lVert\varphi\rVert_{L^{p}(\mathbb{T})}}{(1-|z|^{2})^{k+l}}\left(1-|z|^{2}\right)^{\frac{1}{q}-1}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{C_{\alpha,\beta,p}\lVert\varphi\rVert_{L^{p}(\mathbb{T})}}{(1-|z|^{2})^{k+l+\frac{1}{p}}}

where
C_{\alpha,\beta,p}=e^{\frac{\pi}{2}|\Im\alpha-\Im\beta|}\left(\frac{\Gamma(q(\Re\alpha+\Re\beta+2)-1)}{\Gamma^{2}\left(\frac{q(\Re\alpha+\Re\beta+2)}{2}\right)}\right)^{1-\frac{1}{p}}
.
∎
