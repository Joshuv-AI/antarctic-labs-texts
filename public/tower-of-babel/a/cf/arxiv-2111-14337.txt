Event-triggered bipartite consensus for multiagent system with general linear dynamics: an integral-type event triggered control
Nhan-Phu Chung, Thanh-Son Trinh, Woocheol Choi

Abstract
In this paper, we propose an integral based event-triggering controller for bipartite consensus of the multi-agent systems whose dynamics are described by general linear system. We prove that the system achieves the bipartite consensus in asymptotic regime and there is a positive minimum inter-event time (MIET) between two consecutive triggering times of each agent. The proof of the asymptotive stability involves a novel argument used to bound the norm of the difference between the true state and its estimated state (for each time) by an integration of its square. Numerical results are provided supporting the effectiveness of the proposed controller.

In certain practical problems, some agents collaborate while others are in another competitive group. There are two common issues in designing the event-triggered controls for the consensus problems. One is to guarantee that the controlled system actually achieves the consensus in asymptotic regime or in a prescribed time. The other one is to find a lower bound for the difference between two consecutive triggering times. The larger the lower bound is, the better the system is in saving the energy for the communications. These two issues could be in conflict with each other, and so it is important to design a balanced controller satisfying both the two properties. In particular, it is a non-trivial issue to prove that the controlled system has a positive minimum inter-event time (MIET) between two consecutive triggering times while the system achieves the consensus. Let
A\in M_{n}(\mathbb{R})

. Consider a group of

\displaystyle\dot{x}_{i}(t)=Ax_{i}(t)+Bu_{i}(t),\mbox{ }i\in\mathcal{V}=\{1,\dots,N\},

The event-triggered consensus protocol is given as

\displaystyle u_{i}(t)

\displaystyle=-K\sum_{j=1}^{N}|w_{ij}|(\hat{x}_{i}(t)-{\rm sgn}(w_{ij})\hat{x}_{j}(t)),

Definition II.3
.

We say that an agent

]

As
(L_{D}\otimes I_{n})\delta(t)=(L_{D}\otimes I_{n})z(t)
, combining with
\beta_{max}<\dfrac{1}{2\|L_{D}\|^{2}}
we obtain

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{t}\|e_{z}(s)\|^{2}ds

\displaystyle\leq\dfrac{2\beta_{max}\|L_{D}\|^{2}}{1-2\beta_{max}\|L_{D}\|^{2}}\int_{0}^{t}\|\delta(s)\|^{2}ds

(9)

\displaystyle+

\displaystyle\frac{N\beta_{max}}{1-2\beta_{max}\|L_{D}\|^{2}}.

(10)

Combining (
8
) with (
10
) we get that

\displaystyle V(t)-V(0)\leq

\displaystyle-\beta\int_{0}^{t}\|\delta(s)\|^{2}ds+\frac{N\beta_{max}}{c(1-2\beta_{max}\|L_{D}\|^{2})}

\displaystyle+\dfrac{2\beta_{max}\|L_{D}\|^{2}}{c(1-2\beta_{max}\|L_{D}\|^{2})}\int_{0}^{t}\|\delta(s)\|^{2}ds

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-c_{1}\int_{0}^{t}\|\delta(s)\|^{2}ds+c_{2}.

∎

Lemma III.3
.

Suppose that
\dot{y}(t)=Ay(t)
for
t\geq t_{k}^{i}
. Then we have

\|y(T)\|_{2}^{2}\leq\|y(t_{k}^{i})\|_{2}^{2}+2\|A\|\int_{t_{k}^{i}}^{T}\|y(t)\|_{2}^{2}dt

for all
T\geq t_{k}^{i}
, where
\|A\|:=\max_{v\in\mathbb{R}^{n}\setminus\{0\}}\frac{\|Av\|}{\|v\|}
.

Proof.

We find

\begin{split}\frac{d}{dt}\|y(t)\|^{2}&=2\langle y^{\prime}(t),~y(t)\rangle\\
&=2\langle Ay(t),~y(t)\rangle\\
&\leq 2\|A\|\|y(t)\|^{2}.\end{split}

Integrating this inequality over
[t_{k}^{i},T]
we obtain

\int_{t_{k}^{i}}^{T}\frac{d}{dt}\|y(t)\|^{2}dt\leq\int_{t_{k}^{i}}^{T}2\|A\|\|y(t)\|^{2}dt.

This completes the proof.
∎

Lemma III.4
.

For every
i
the function
\|\hat{z}_{i}(t)-z_{i}(t)\|
is uniformly bounded for
t\geq 0
.

Proof.

From Lemma
III.2
there exists
M>0
such that
\|z_{i}(t)-\bar{z}(t)\|\leq M
for every
t>0
and every
i=1,\dots,N
. Therefore to prove
\|\hat{z}_{i}(t)-z_{i}(t)\|
is bounded, it suffices to show that
\|\hat{z}_{i}(t)-\bar{z}(t)\|
is bounded.

Since
P
is a positive define matrix, so is
I_{N}\otimes P
. Hence,
V(t)=\delta^{T}(t)(I_{N}\otimes P)\delta(t)\geq 0
. Therefore, from Lemma
III.2
we get that

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{t}\|\delta(s)\|^{2}ds\leq\frac{V(0)+c_{2}}{c_{1}}.

(11)

Since (
11
) and (
10
), for every
i\in\{1,\ldots,N\}
, we obtain that

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{t}\|e_{z,i}(s)\|^{2}ds\leq\int_{0}^{t}\|e_{z}(s)\|^{2}ds\leq c_{3},

(12)

where
c_{3}:=(2\beta_{\max}\|L_{D}\|^{2}(V(0)+c_{2}))/(c_{1}(1-2\beta_{\max}\|L_{D}\|^{2}))+c_{2}
.

Furthermore, for every
t\geq 0
we have that

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{t}\|\hat{z}_{i}(s)-\overline{z}(s)\|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq 2\int_{0}^{t}\|\hat{z}_{i}(s)-z_{i}(s)\|^{2}ds+2\int_{0}^{t}\|z_{i}(s)-\overline{z}(s)\|^{2}ds

\displaystyle=2\int_{0}^{t}\|e_{z,i}(s)\|^{2}ds+2\int_{0}^{t}\|\delta_{i}(s)\|^{2}ds.

From (
11
) and (
12
) for every
t\geq 0
, we obtain that

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{t}\|\hat{z}_{i}(s)-\overline{z}(s)\|^{2}\leq 2\left(c_{3}+\frac{V(0)+c_{2}}{c_{1}}\right).

(13)

Assume that
\|\hat{z}_{i}(t)-\overline{z}(t)\|
is not bounded. Then there exists
M_{1}>M^{2}+4\|A\|(c_{3}+(V(0)+c_{2})/c_{1})
such that
\|\hat{z}_{i}(T)-\overline{z}(T)\|>M_{1}
for some
T\in[t^{i}_{k},t^{i}_{k+1})
. Next, we define
y(t):=\hat{z}_{i}(t)-\overline{z}(t)
. Then
\dot{y}(t)=Ay(t)
. Therefore, by Lemma
III.3
and (
13
)

\displaystyle M_{1}

\displaystyle<\|\hat{z}_{i}(T)-\overline{z}(T)\|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\|\hat{z}_{i}(t^{i}_{k})-\overline{z}(t^{i}_{k})\|^{2}+2\|A\|\int_{t^{i}_{k}}^{T}\|\hat{z}_{i}(t)-\overline{z}(t)\|^{2}dt

\displaystyle=\|z_{i}(t^{i}_{k})-\overline{z}(t^{i}_{k})\|^{2}+2\|A\|\int_{t^{i}_{k}}^{T}\|\hat{z}_{i}(t)-\overline{z}(t)\|^{2}dt

\displaystyle\leq M^{2}+4\|A\|\left(c_{3}+\frac{V(0)+c_{2}}{c_{1}}\right)

\displaystyle<M_{1}.

This is a contradiction. Hence, we get that
\|e_{z,i}(t)\|
is bounded for every
i=1,\ldots,N
.
∎

Theorem III.5
.

Let a multi-agent system as (
1
) such that the pair
(A,B)
is stabilizable, and the graph
\mathcal{G}=(\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E},\mathcal{W})
is connected and structurally balanced. Let
P\in M_{n}(\mathbb{R})
be a positive matrix being a solution of (
5
). We consider the event-triggered consensus protocol as (
2
) with the control matrix
K:=B^{T}P
. The event triggered times are determined by (
3
) and (
4
). Then the system achieves consensus. Furthermore, the multi-agent system has a positive minimum inter-event time, i.e. there exists
\tau>0
such that
t^{i}_{k+1}-t^{i}_{k}\geq\tau
for every
i=1,\dots,N
,
k\in\mathbb{N}
.

Proof.

As
P
is a positive matrix so is
I_{N}\otimes P
. Therefore
V(t)\geq 0
for every
t
. Hence from Lemma
III.2
we get that
\int_{0}^{t}\|\delta(s)\|^{2}ds\leq\dfrac{V(0)}{c_{1}}
and therefore
\lim_{t\to\infty}\int_{0}^{t}\|\delta(s)\|^{2}ds
is finite. Let
\kappa_{1}
be the smallest eigenvalue of
I_{N}\otimes P
. Then
V(0)\geq V(t)=\delta^{T}(t)(I_{N}\otimes P)\delta(t)\geq\kappa_{1}\delta^{T}(t)\delta(t)
. Hence
\delta(t)
is bounded. On the other hand, as

\displaystyle\dot{\delta(t)}=(I_{N}\otimes A-L_{D}\otimes BK)\delta(t)-(L_{D}\otimes BK)e_{z}(t),

applying Lemma
III.4
we get that
\dot{\delta}(t)
is also bounded. Applying Barbalat’s lemma
[
40
, Lemma 8.2]
, we get that
\lim_{t\to\infty}\delta(t)=0
and hence
\lim_{t\to\infty}\|z_{i}(t)-z_{j}(t)\|=0
for every
i,j
.

Now we prove that the system has positive minimum inter-event time. We note that

\begin{split}\frac{d}{dt}\|e_{z,i}(t)\|^{2}=&2\langle e_{z,i}(t),~\dot{e}_{z,i}(t)\rangle\\
\leq&2\|e_{z,i}(t)\|\|\dot{e}_{z,i}(t)\|\\
\leq&2\|BK\|\|e_{z,i}(t)\|\hbox{$\left\|\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}\sum_{j=1}^{N}w_{ij}(\hat{z}_{i}(t)-\hat{z}_{j}(t))\hbox{$\left\|\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}\\
&+2\|A\|\|e_{z,i}(t)\|^{2}\\
\leq&\hbox{$\left\|\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}\sum_{j=1}^{N}w_{ij}(\hat{z}_{i}(t)-\hat{z}_{j}(t))\hbox{$\left\|\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}^{2}\\
&+(2\|A\|+\|BK\|^{2})\|e_{z,i}(t)\|^{2},\end{split}

where we used (
7
) in the second inequality.
We then have

\begin{split}\|e_{z,i}(t)\|^{2}&\leq\int_{t_{k}^{i}}^{t}e^{(2\|A\|+\|BK\|^{2})(t-s)}\hbox{$\left\|\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}\sum_{j=1}^{N}w_{ij}(\hat{z}_{i}(s)-\hat{z}_{j}(s))\hbox{$\left\|\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}^{2}ds\\
&\leq e^{(2\|A\|+\|BK\|^{2})(t-t_{k}^{i})}\int_{t_{k}^{i}}^{t}\hbox{$\left\|\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}\sum_{j=1}^{N}w_{ij}(\hat{z}_{i}(s)-\hat{z}_{j}(s))\hbox{$\left\|\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}^{2}ds.\end{split}

We rewrite the above inequality by

\|e_{z,i}(s)\|^{2}\leq e^{(2\|A\|+\|BK\|^{2})(s-t_{k}^{i})}\int_{t_{k}^{i}}^{s}\hbox{$\left\|\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}\sum_{j=1}^{N}w_{ij}(\hat{z}_{i}(r)-\hat{z}_{j}(r))\hbox{$\left\|\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}^{2}dr.

Integrating this, we find

\begin{split}&\int_{t_{k}^{i}}^{t}\|e_{z,i}(s)\|^{2}ds\\
&\leq\int_{t_{k}^{i}}^{t}e^{(2\|A\|+\|BK\|^{2})(s-t_{k}^{i})}\int_{t_{k}^{i}}^{s}\hbox{$\left\|\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}\sum_{j=1}^{N}w_{ij}(\hat{z}_{i}(r)-\hat{z}_{j}(r))\hbox{$\left\|\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}^{2}drds\\
&\leq\int_{t_{k}^{i}}^{t}e^{(2\|A\|+\|BK\|^{2})(s-t_{k}^{i})}\int_{t_{k}^{i}}^{t}\hbox{$\left\|\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}\sum_{j=1}^{N}w_{ij}(\hat{z}_{i}(r)-\hat{z}_{j}(r))\hbox{$\left\|\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}^{2}drds\\
&=\hbox{$\left(\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}\int_{t_{k}^{i}}^{t}e^{(2\|A\|+\|BK\|^{2})(s-t_{k}^{i})}ds\hbox{$\left)\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}\int_{t_{k}^{i}}^{t}\hbox{$\left\|\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}\sum_{j=1}^{N}w_{ij}(\hat{z}_{i}(r)-\hat{z}_{j}(r))\hbox{$\left\|\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}^{2}dr.\end{split}

From this and the triggering condition, we see that

\int_{t_{k}^{i}}^{t_{k+1}^{i}}e^{(2\|A\|+\|BK\|^{2})(s-t_{k}^{i})}ds\geq\beta_{i}.

The proof is done.
∎

V
Conclusion

In this work, we designed an integral based event-triggered controller for bipartite consensus of the general linear system. We rigorously proved that the bipartite consensus is achieved and there is a positive MIET for the controlled system. The numerical simulation was provided supporting the theoretical results.
