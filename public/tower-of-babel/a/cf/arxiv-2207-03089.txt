Twisted Koecher-Maass series of the Ikeda type lift for the exceptional group of type E 7 , 3 E_{7,3} Thanks: The first author is partially supported by JSPS KAKENHI Grant Number No. 21K03152. The second author is partially supported by NSERC grant #482564.
Hidenori Katsurada and Henry H. Kim

Abstract
. We compute the twisted Koecher-Maass series of the first and second kind of the Ikeda type lift for the exceptional group of type E 7 , 3 E_{7,3} . As an application, we obtain their rationality result.

11F67, 11F55, 11E45

1.
Introduction

Let
{\mathfrak{J}}_{\mathbb{Q}}
be the exceptional Jordan algebra consisting of

3\times 3
matrices with entries in the Cayley numbers, and
{\mathcal{M}}^{\prime}
the group scheme over
{\mathbb{Z}}
of type
E_{6,2}
. (See Section
2
for the definitions.) Let
{\mathfrak{T}}
be the exceptional domain in
{\mathbb{C}}^{27}
. Let
{\bf G}
be a connected reductive group of type
GE_{7,3}
. For a cusp form

F(Z)=\sum_{T\in{\mathfrak{J}}({\mathbb{Z}})_{>0}}a_{F}(T)\exp(2\pi\sqrt{-1}(T,Z))

of weight
k
(
k
even) with respect to
{\bf G}({\mathbb{Z}})
and a Dirichlet character
\chi
,
we define the twisted Koecher-Maass series
K^{(2)}(s,F,\chi)
of the second kind as

K^{(2)}(s,F,\chi)=\sum_{T\in{\mathfrak{J}}(\mathbb{Z})_{>0}/{\mathcal{M}}^{\prime}({\mathbb{Z}})}\frac{\chi(\det T)a_{F}(T)}{\epsilon(T)\det(T)^{s}},

where for
T\in{\mathfrak{J}}({\mathbb{Z}})_{>0}
,
\epsilon(T)=\#({\mathcal{U}}_{T}({\mathbb{Z}}))
, and
{\mathcal{U}}_{T}
denotes the group scheme of type
F_{4}
. (See Section
5
.)
For a positive integer
N
let

\phi_{N}:{\mathcal{M}}^{\prime}({\mathbb{Z}})\longrightarrow{\mathcal{M}}^{\prime}({\mathbb{Z}}/N{\mathbb{Z}})
be the homomorphism induced by the natural surjection
\pi_{N}:{\mathbb{Z}}\longrightarrow{\mathbb{Z}}/N{\mathbb{Z}}
, and put
{\mathcal{M}}^{\prime}(N;{\mathbb{Z}})=\mathrm{Ker}(\phi_{N})
.
We define the twisted Koecher-Maass series
K^{(1)}(s,F,\chi)
of the first kind as

K^{(1)}(s,F,\chi)=\sum_{T\in{\mathfrak{J}}(\mathbb{Z})_{>0}/{\mathcal{M}}^{\prime}(N;{\mathbb{Z}})}\frac{\chi(\mathrm{Tr}(T))a_{F}(T)}{\epsilon_{N}(T)\det(T)^{s}},

where
\epsilon_{N}(T)=\#({\mathcal{M}}^{\prime}(N;{\mathbb{Z}})\cap{\mathcal{U}}_{T}({\mathbb{Z}}))
.

For a cuspidal Hecke eigenform
f\in S_{2k-8}(SL_{2}({\mathbb{Z}}))
(
2k\geq 20
), let
F_{f}
be the Ikeda type lift which is a cuspidal Hecke eigenform of weight
2k
with respect to
{\bf G}({\mathbb{Z}})
constructed by Kim and Yamauchi (
[
8
]
). In this paper we give explicit formulas of
K^{(1)}(s,F_{f},\chi)
and
K^{(2)}(s,F_{f},\chi)
.

Theorem 1.1
.

Let
\displaystyle c={5!7!11!\over(2\pi)^{28}}
and
L(s,\pi_{f},\chi)
be the
L
-function of the cuspidal automorphic representation
\pi_{f}
attached to
f
twisted by
\chi
. Then

\displaystyle K^{(2)}(s,F_{f},\chi)=c\zeta(2)\zeta(6)\zeta(8)\zeta(12)\times\prod_{i=1}^{3}L(s-k-9/2+4i-3,\pi_{f},\chi).

Theorem 1.2
.

Let
\chi
be a primitive Dirichlet character mod
N
. Suppose that
\chi
is not a quadratic character. Let
l=\mathrm{GCD}(3,\phi(N))
with
\phi
is the Euler phi-function, and let
u_{0}
be a primitive
l
-th root of unity mod
N
.

(1)

Suppose that
\chi(u_{0})\not=1
. Then
K^{(1)}(s,F_{f},\chi)=0
.

(2)

Suppose that
\chi(u_{0})=1
and fix a character
\widetilde{\chi}
such that
\chi=\widetilde{\chi}^{3}
(cf. Lemma
4.11
). Then

\displaystyle K^{(1)}(s,F_{f},\chi)=c\zeta(2)\zeta(6)\zeta(8)\zeta(12)d_{N}\sum_{\eta\in{\mathcal{D}}_{N}}J(\overline{\widetilde{\chi}\eta},\overline{\widetilde{\chi}\eta},\overline{\widetilde{\chi}\eta})\prod_{i=1}^{3}L(s-k-9/2+4i-3,\pi_{f},\overline{\widetilde{\chi}\eta}),

where
d_{N}=N^{64}\prod_{p|N}(1-p^{-2})(1-p^{-6})(1-p^{-8})(1-p^{-12})
,

{\mathcal{D}}_{N}=\{\eta\ |\ \eta\text{ is a Dirichlet character }mod\ N\text{ such that }\eta^{l}=1\},

and
J(\xi,\xi,\xi)
is the generalized Jacobi sum (cf. Section 4).
In particular, if
N
is odd, we have

\displaystyle K^{(1)}(s,F_{f},\chi)=c\zeta(2)\zeta(6)\zeta(8)\zeta(12)d_{N}\sum_{\eta\in{\mathcal{D}}_{N}}{W(\overline{\widetilde{\chi}\eta})^{3}\over W(\bar{\chi})}\prod_{i=1}^{3}L(s-k-9/2+4i-3,\pi_{f},\overline{\widetilde{\chi}\eta}),

where
W(\xi)
is the Gauss sum of the Dirichlet character
\xi
.

As an application, we obtain the rationality result for
K^{(1)}(m,F_{f},\chi)
and
K^{(2)}(m,F_{f},\chi)
for
9\leq m\leq 2k-9
(Theorems 7.2 and 7.3).

In the case of Siegel modular forms, Choie-Kohnen
[
2
]
studied the twisted Koecher-Maass series of the first kind and proved the analytic continuation and the functional equation, and studied their special values.
On the other hand, the first named author
[
4
]
studied the twisted Koecher-Maass series of both the first kind and the second kind, and gave explicit formulas for them associated with the Duke-Imamoglu-Ikeda lift.

Our method of proving the above two theorems is similar to that used in
[
4
]
. However, unlike the Siegel case, we cannot use explicit matrix decompositions in the exceptional group. It is one of the obstacles we need to overcome.
This paper is organized as follows. In Section 2, we briefly review modular forms on the exceptional domain. Unlike in previous papers
[
1
,
8
]
, we need to consider the exceptional similitude group
GE_{7,3}
. We use the definition of
GE_{7,3}
in
[
9
]
.

In Section 3, we prove that the twisted Koecher-Maass series of the first kind for any cusp forms has analytic continuation and the functional equation. In Section 4, we obtain the relationship between the twisted Koecher-Maass series of the first kind and the second kind.

In Section 5, we briefly review the mass formula and local density
\beta_{p}(T)
from
[
5
]
, where we used them to compute the Rankin-Selberg series
R(s,F_{f},F_{f})
for the Ikeda type lift
F_{f}
. In Section 6, we review the Ikeda type lift. By construction, the Fourier coefficients of
F_{f}
are expressed in terms of a product of the local Siegel series. Therefore, using the mass formula in Section 5, we can express the twisted Koecher-Maass series of the second kind
as an Euler product:

K^{(2)}(s,F_{f},\chi)=c\prod_{p}H_{p}(\alpha_{p},\chi(p)p^{-s+k+9/2}),

where
c
is in Theorem
1.1
,
\alpha_{p}
is the
p
-th Satake parameter for
f
, and
H_{p}(X,t)
is a certain power series involving the Siegel series and the local density. It is remarkable that
K^{(2)}(s,F_{f},\chi)
has an Euler product since
K^{(2)}(s,F,\chi)
does not have an Euler product for a general cusp form
F
. In Section 7, we prove Theorems
1.1
and
1.2
, and obtain rationality result of the twisted Koecher-Maass series.

3.
Twisted Koecher-Maass series of the first kind

From now on put

for

Let
F
be a cusp form of weight
k
(
k

\mathfrak{T}
with respect to
\Gamma

F(Z)=\sum_{T\in\mathfrak{J}(\mathbb{Z})_{>0}}a_{F}(T){\bf e}((T,Z)).

Recall the twisted Koecher-Maass series
K^{(1)}(s,F,\chi)
of the first kind for a Dirichlet character
\chi

K^{(1)}(s,F,\chi)=\sum_{T\in{\mathfrak{J}}(\mathbb{Z})_{>0}/{\mathcal{M}}^{\prime}(N;{\mathbb{Z}})}\frac{\chi(\mathrm{Tr}(T))a_{F}(T)}{\epsilon_{N}(T)\det(T)^{s}}.

In this section we prove the analytic continuation and the functional equation of
K^{(1)}(s,F,\chi)
.

Recall that
d^{*}Y=det(Y)^{-9}dY
is the invariant measure in
R_{3}^{+}(\mathbb{R})
.
Let
\mathcal{R}
be a fundamental domain for the action of
{\mathcal{M}}(\mathbb{Z})
on
R_{3}^{+}(\mathbb{R})
.
Let
\mathcal{R}_{N}
be a fundamental domain for the action of
{\mathcal{M}}(N;{\mathbb{Z}})
on
R_{3}^{+}(\mathbb{R})
. We may take

\mathcal{R}_{N}=\bigcup_{a=1}^{r}m_{a}\mathcal{R},

where
\{m_{1},...,m_{r}\}
is a set of representatives for
{\mathcal{M}}^{\prime}({\mathbb{Z}})/{\mathcal{M}}^{\prime}(N;{\mathbb{Z}})
.
We prove

Theorem 3.1
.

For
Re(s)>9+\frac{k}{2}
,
K^{(1)}(s,F,\chi)
converges absolutely, and in this region we have the integral representation

\Lambda(s,F,\chi)=\pi^{12}(2\pi)^{-3s}N^{3s}\Gamma(s)\Gamma(s-4)\Gamma(s-8)K^{(1)}(s,F,\chi)=N^{3s}\int_{\mathcal{R}_{N}}F_{\chi}(\sqrt{-1}Y)\det(Y)^{s}\,d^{*}Y.

It has the analytic continuation to all of
\mathbb{C}
, and satisfies the functional equation

\Lambda(k-s,F,\chi)=(-1)^{\frac{3k}{2}}\frac{W(\chi)^{2}}{N}\Lambda(s,F,\bar{\chi}).

Proof.

By Hecke’s bound,
a_{F}(T)\ll det(T)^{\frac{k}{2}}
. Hence

K^{(1)}(s,F,\chi)\ll\sum_{T\in{\mathfrak{J}}(\mathbb{Z})_{>0}/{\mathcal{M}}(N;{\mathbb{Z}})}\frac{\det(T)^{\frac{k}{2}-Re(s)}}{\epsilon_{N}(T)}.

By integral test, the series is majorized by

\int_{R_{3}^{+}(\mathbb{R})\atop\det(Y)\geq 1}\det(Y)^{\frac{k}{2}-Re(s)+9}\,d^{*}Y,

which converges for
Re(s)>9+\frac{k}{2}
.

We have
F_{\chi}(\sqrt{-1}Y)=\sum_{T\in\mathfrak{J}(\mathbb{Z})_{>0}}\chi({\rm tr}(T))a_{F}(T)e^{-2\pi(T,Y)}
. Since

a_{F}(uT)=a_{F}(T)
for
u\in\mathcal{U}_{T}(\mathbb{Z})\cap{\mathcal{M}}(N;{\mathbb{Z}})
,

\displaystyle N^{3s}\int_{\mathcal{R}_{N}}F_{\chi}(\sqrt{-1}Y)\det(Y)^{s}\,d^{*}Y=N^{3s}\sum_{T\in\mathfrak{J}(\mathbb{Z})_{>0}}\chi({\rm tr}(T))a_{F}(T)\int_{\mathfrak{R}_{N}}e^{-2\pi(T,Y)}\det(Y)^{s}\,d^{*}Y

\displaystyle\phantom{xxx}=N^{3s}\sum_{T\in\mathfrak{J}(\mathbb{Z})_{>0}/{\mathcal{M}}(N;{\mathbb{Z}})}\frac{\chi({\rm tr}(T))a_{F}(T)}{\epsilon_{N}(T)}\sum_{u\in\mathcal{U}_{T}(\mathbb{Z})\cap{\mathcal{M}}(N;{\mathbb{Z}})}\int_{\mathcal{R}_{N}}e^{-2\pi(T,Y)}\det(Y)^{s}\,d^{*}Y

\displaystyle\phantom{xxx}=N^{3s}\sum_{T\in\mathfrak{J}(\mathbb{Z})_{>0}/{\mathcal{M}}(N;{\mathbb{Z}})}\frac{\chi({\rm tr}(T))a_{F}(T)}{\epsilon_{N}(T)}\int_{R_{3}^{+}(\mathbb{R})}e^{-2\pi(T,Y)}\det(Y)^{s}\,d^{*}Y

\displaystyle\phantom{xxx}=N^{3s}\pi^{12}(2\pi)^{-3s}\Gamma(s)\Gamma(s-4)\Gamma(s-8)\sum_{T\in T\in\mathfrak{J}(\mathbb{Z})_{>0}/{\mathcal{M}}(N;{\mathbb{Z}})}\frac{\chi({\rm tr}(T))a_{F}(T)}{\epsilon_{N}(T)}\det(T)^{-s}.

Here we use the fact
[
1
, page 538]
that for
Re(s)>8
,

\int_{R_{3}^{+}(\mathbb{R})}e^{-2\pi(T,Y)}\det(Y)^{s}\,d^{*}Y=\pi^{12}(2\pi)^{-3s}\Gamma(s)\Gamma(s-4)\Gamma(s-8)\det(T)^{-s}.

For the functional equation, we write

\Lambda(s,F)=N^{3s}\int_{\mathcal{R}_{N}\atop\det(Y)\leq N^{-3}}F_{\chi}(\sqrt{-1}Y)\det(Y)^{s}\,d^{*}Y+N^{3s}\int_{\mathcal{R}_{N}\atop det(Y)\geq N^{-3}}F_{\chi}(\sqrt{-1}Y)\det(Y)^{s}\,d^{*}Y.

Notice that
\det((N^{2}Y)^{-1})=N^{-6}\det(Y)^{-1}
. Use the change of variables
Y\longmapsto(N^{2}Y)^{-1}
, and
the functional equation (
2.2
):

\displaystyle N^{3s}\int_{\mathcal{R}_{N}\atop\det(Y)\leq N^{-3}}F_{\chi}(\sqrt{-1}Y)\det(Y)^{s}\,d^{*}Y=N^{3s}\int_{\mathcal{R}_{N}\atop\det(Y)\geq N^{-3}}F_{\chi}(\sqrt{-1}N^{-2}Y^{-1})\det(Y)^{-s}\,d^{*}Y

\displaystyle\phantom{xxxxxxxxxxx}=\int_{\mathcal{R}_{N}\atop\det(Y)\geq N^{-3}}(-1)^{\frac{3k}{2}}N^{3k-3s}\frac{W(\chi)^{2}}{N}F_{\bar{\chi}}(\sqrt{-1}Y)\det(Y)^{k-s}\,d^{*}Y.

Hence

The analytic continuation and the functional equation follow from this.
∎

Proposition 4.1
.
