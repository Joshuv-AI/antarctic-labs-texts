Inflection-Point Sgoldstino Inflation in no-Scale Supergravity
Constantinos Pallis Laboratory of Physics, Faculty of Engineering, Aristotle University of Thessaloniki, GR-541 24 Thessaloniki, GREECE e-mail address: kpallis@gen.auth.gr

Abstract
Abstract: We propose a modification of no-scale supergravity models which incorporates sgoldstino stabilization and supersymmetry (SUSY) breaking with a tunable cosmological constant by introducing a Kähler potential which yields a kinetic pole of order one. The resulting scalar potential may develop an inflection point, close to which an inflationary period can be realized for subplanckian field values consistently with the observational data. For central value of the spectral index n s n_{\rm s} , the necessary tuning is of the order of 10 − 6 10^{-6} , the tensor-to-scalar ratio r r is tiny whereas the running of n s n_{\rm s} , a s a_{\rm s} , is around − 3 ⋅ 10 − 3 -3\cdot 10^{-3} . Our proposal is compatible with high-scale SUSY and the results of LHC on the Higgs boson mass. PACs numbers: 12.60.Jv, 04.65.+e Published in Phys. Lett. B 843 , 138018 (2023)

Introduction

One of the most tantalizingly evasive problems in Particle Physics
is the explanation of the large hierarchies existing in the
fundamental scales of the modern theories. Two of these scales are
related to the cosmological problems of inflation and
Dark
Energy
(
DE
) whereas a third one is related to the scale
of
Supersymmetry
(
SUSY
) breaking. The first of
the scales above, is expected to be less than
10^{4}~{\mbox{\rm EeV}}
, the
second one is related to the present acceleration of the Universe
and is really very tiny (
\Lambda\sim 1~{\mbox{\rm meV}}
) whereas the scale
of the SUSY partners,
\widetilde{m}
, is continuously pushed more and more
beyond the
TeV
region due to the lack of any positive signal at

In a set of recent papers

aforementioned scales (

interconnected within the framework of no-scale

Supergravity

old
;

VI
Conclusions

Taking advantage from the self-stabilized no-scale models of SUSY
breaking, established in Ref.
ns89
;
de
, we proposed a variant
which incorporates IPI – i.e., inflection-point inflation –
realized by the sgoldstino. The inflationary model results to an
adjustable
n_{\rm s}
, a small
r
and a sizable
a_{\rm s}
of the order of

-10^{-3}
. Linking our model to MSSM we showed that SUSY may be
broken at a dS vacuum, providing the correct DE density parameter
– at the cost of a fine-tuned parameter – and a SUSY mass scale

\widetilde{m}\sim 100~{\mbox{\rm EeV}}
which is consistent with the Higgs boson mass
measured at LHC. Needless to say, the stability of the electroweak
vacuum up to the Planck scale is automatically assured within our
framework.

It would be interesting to investigate if the intermediate-scale
lightest neutralino with mass
M_{1}
in the interval

T_{\rm rh}<M_{1}<T_{\rm max}
is a good cold dark matter candidate
adapting the non-equilibrium production
kolb
. Here,
T_{\rm max}
is the maximal temperature during reheating and may be as
high as
10^{3}T_{\rm rh}

kolb
. Also, baryogenesis via non-thermal
leptogensis
ntlepto
may be activated even without direct
coupling of the sgoldstino to right-handed neutrinos. Our model,
most probably, does not belong to the string swampland
vafa

but it is amenable to modifications
sevilla
which may
render it more friendly with the string ultraviolet completions.
