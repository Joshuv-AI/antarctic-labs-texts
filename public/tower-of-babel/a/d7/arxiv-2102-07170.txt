Lines in affine toric varieties
Shulim Kaliman

Abstract
. We prove that up to automorphisms of the target the affine line 𝔸 1 {\mathbb{A}}^{1} admits a unique embedding into the regular part of an affine simplicial toric variety of dimension at least 4 which is smooth in codimension 2. This is an analog of the well-known result on the existence of a linearization of any polynomial embedding 𝔸 1 ↪ 𝔸 n {\mathbb{A}}^{1}\hookrightarrow{\mathbb{A}}^{n} for n ≥ 4 n\geq 4 .

The paper is organized as follows. In Section 2 we survey the technique developed in

Definition 7.4
.

Let
C_{1}
and
C_{2}
be smooth curves
in a smooth quasi-affine variety

with defining ideals

and

in

We suppose also that

are closed in an affine variety containing

Let

\omega
(i.e.,
\omega
is a nonvanishing section of the canonical bundle on

each conormal bundle
\frac{I_{j}}{I_{j}^{2}}
of

in

By
[

there is a neighborhood

of

in

is a strict complete intersection
given by

where

and

. That is, for

Consider an isomorphism

Up to the induced isomorphism

These values can be viewed as polynomials in

\left[\frac{\partial\varphi(u_{l,1})}{\partial u_{s,2}}\right]_{l,s=1}^{n-1}
.
Since the normal bundle

is trivial, the existence of
\omega
implies the existence of a volume form on

.
Fix volume forms
\omega_{j}
on

. Choose a section
