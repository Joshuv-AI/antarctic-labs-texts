Kolmogorov widths of a finite‐dimensional balls intersection
A.A. Vasil’eva

Abstract




1
Introduction

When studying the problem of estimating the widths of Sobolev weight classes with restrictions on derivatives in various
L_{p}
-metrics
[
1
]
, the question arose about the widths of intersections of two finite-dimensional balls. Their order estimates were obtained in
[
2
]
. Here we generalize this result to the intersection of an arbitrary family of balls.

Let
X
be a normed space,
M\subset X
,
n\in\mathbb{Z}_{+}
. We denote by
{\cal L}_{n}(X)
the family of subspaces in
X

of dimension at most
n
. The Kolmogorov
n
-width of the set
M
in the space
X
is defined as follows:

d_{n}(M,\,X)=\inf_{L\in{\cal L}_{n}(X)}\sup_{x\in M}\inf_{y\in L}\|x-y\|.

Given
N\in\mathbb{N}
,
1\leqslant p\leqslant\infty
, we denote by
l_{p}^{N}
the space
\mathbb{R}^{N}
with the norm

\|(x_{1},\,\dots,\,x_{N})\|_{p}=\left\{\begin{array}[]{l}\left(\sum\limits_{i=1}^{N}|x_{i}|^{p}\right)^{1/p},\quad\text{if }1\leqslant p<\infty,\\
\max_{1\leqslant i\leqslant N}|x_{i}|,\quad\text{if }p=\infty.\end{array}\right.

By
B_{p}^{N}
we denote the unit ball in the space
l_{p}^{N}
.

Let
A
be a non‐empty set,
1\leqslant p_{\alpha}\leqslant\infty
,
\nu_{\alpha}>0
for every
\alpha\in A
and
p_{\alpha}\neq p_{\beta}
for
\alpha\neq\beta
, and let

\displaystyle M=\cap_{\alpha\in A}\nu_{\alpha}B_{p_{\alpha}}^{N}.

(1)

In this paper we obtain order estimates of the widths
d_{n}(M,\,l_{q}^{N})
for
n\leqslant N/2
.

The explicit values of
d_{n}(B_{p}^{N},\,l_{q}^{N})
were obtained by Pietsch and Stesin
[
3
,
4
]
for
p\geqslant q
, and by Kolmogorov, Petrov, Smirnov
[
5
]
and Stechkin
[
6
]
for
p=1
,
q=2
. For
p<q<\infty
, Gluskin
[
9
,
10
]
obtained order estimates of
d_{n}(B_{p}^{N},\,l_{q}^{N})
. The case
q=\infty
was investigated by Kashin
[
7
,
8
]
, Gluskin and Garnaev
[
11
]
; for
p\geqslant 2
, order estimates were obtained, and for
1\leqslant p<2
, the upper and the lower estimates differ by a logarithmic multiplier. For details, see
[
12
]
.

Galeev
[
13
]
obtained order estimates for

d_{n}(M,\,l_{q}^{2n})
, where
M
is defined by (
1
),
A\subset\mathbb{R}
,
\nu_{\alpha}=(2n)^{‐\alpha}
. This result was applied for estimating the widths of intersections of some function classes
[
13
,
14
]
.

Given
\alpha
,
\beta\in A
, we set

\displaystyle\varkappa_{\alpha,\,\beta}=\left\{\begin{array}[]{l}\left(\frac{\nu_{\beta}}{\nu_{\alpha}}\right)^{\frac{p_{\alpha}p_{\beta}}{p_{\alpha}‐p_{\beta}}},\quad\text{if }\alpha\neq\beta,\\
1,\quad\text{if }\alpha=\beta.\end{array}\right.

Then

\displaystyle\nu_{\alpha}\varkappa_{\alpha,\beta}^{‐1/p_{\alpha}}=\nu_{\beta}\varkappa_{\alpha,\beta}^{‐1/p_{\beta}}.

(4)

Notice that
\varkappa_{\alpha,\beta}=\varkappa_{\beta,\alpha}
.

Suppose that

\displaystyle 1\leqslant\varkappa_{\alpha,\beta}\leqslant N,\quad\alpha,\,\beta\in A

(5)

(in §3 it will be shown how to reduce the general case to this case).
Then
\{\nu_{\alpha}:\alpha\in A\}
is bounded and separated from zero.

Now we formulate the main result of this paper. First we give notation for order equalities and inequalities.

Let
X
,
Y
be sets, and let
f_{1}
,
f_{2}:\ X\times Y\rightarrow\mathbb{R}_{+}
.
We write
f_{1}(x,\,y)\underset{y}{\lesssim}f_{2}(x,\,y)
(or

f_{2}(x,\,y)\underset{y}{\gtrsim}f_{1}(x,\,y)
) if for any

y\in Y
there exists
c(y)>0
such that
f_{1}(x,\,y)\leqslant c(y)f_{2}(x,\,y)
for every
x\in X
;
f_{1}(x,\,y)\underset{y}{\asymp}f_{2}(x,\,y)
if
f_{1}(x,\,y)\underset{y}{\lesssim}f_{2}(x,\,y)
and
f_{2}(x,\,y)\underset{y}{\lesssim}f_{1}(x,\,y)
.

We denote

\displaystyle K=\{(\nu_{\alpha},\,1/p_{\alpha})\}_{\alpha\in A}.

(6)

.

Let
n,\,N\in\mathbb{N}
,
n\leqslant N/2
, and let
M
be defined by (
1
). Suppose that (
5
) holds.

1.

Let
p_{\alpha}\geqslant q
for every
\alpha\in A
. Then

\displaystyle d_{n}(M,\,l_{q}^{N})\asymp\inf_{\alpha\in A}\nu_{\alpha}N^{1/q‐1/p_{\alpha}}.

(7)

2.

Let
q\leqslant 2
,
p_{\alpha}\leqslant q
for any
\alpha\in A
. Then

\displaystyle d_{n}(M,\,l_{q}^{N})\asymp\inf_{\alpha\in A}\nu_{\alpha}.

(8)

3.

Let
q\leqslant 2
,
A_{1}=\{\alpha\in A:\;p_{\alpha}>q\}\neq\varnothing
,
A_{2}=\{\alpha\in A:\;p_{\alpha}<q\}\neq\varnothing
,
A_{1}^{\prime}=\{\alpha\in A:\;p_{\alpha}\geqslant q\}
,
A_{2}^{\prime}=\{\alpha\in A:\;p_{\alpha}\leqslant q\}
. Then

\displaystyle d_{n}(M,\,l_{q}^{N})\asymp\inf\{\nu_{\alpha}\varkappa_{\alpha,\beta}^{1/q‐1/p_{\alpha}}:\;\alpha\in A_{1}^{\prime},\;\beta\in A_{2}^{\prime}\}.

(9)

4.

Let
2<q<\infty
, and let
p_{\alpha}\leqslant 2
for every
\alpha\in A
. Then

\displaystyle d_{n}(M,\,l_{q}^{N})\underset{q}{\asymp}\inf_{\alpha\in A}\nu_{\alpha}\min\{1,\,n^{‐1/2}N^{1/q}\}.

(10)

5.

Let
2<q<\infty
,
A_{1}^{\prime}=\{\alpha\in A:\;p_{\alpha}\geqslant q\}
,
A_{2}^{\prime}=\{\alpha\in A:\;2\leqslant p_{\alpha}\leqslant q\}
,
A_{3}^{\prime}=\{\alpha\in A:\;p_{\alpha}\leqslant 2\}
. Suppose that
A\not\subset A_{1}^{\prime}
,
A\not\subset A_{3}^{\prime}
. Let
K
be defined by (
6

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{c}d_{n}(M,\,l_{q}^{N})\underset{q}{\asymp}\Phi(n,\,N,\,q,\,K):=\\
=\min\{\Phi_{1}(n,\,N,\,q,\,K),\,\Phi_{2}(n,\,N,\,q,\,K),\,\Phi_{3}(n,\,N,\,q,\,K)\},\end{array}

where

\displaystyle\Phi_{1}(n,\,N,\,q,\,K)=\inf\{\nu_{\alpha}\varkappa_{\alpha,\beta}^{1/q‐1/p_{\alpha}}:\;\alpha\in A_{1}^{\prime},\;\beta\in A_{2}^{\prime}\cup A_{3}^{\prime}\},

(13)

\displaystyle\Phi_{2}(n,\,N,\,q,\,K)=\inf\left\{\nu_{\alpha}(\min\{1,\,n^{‐1/2}N^{1/q}\})^{\frac{1/p_{\alpha}‐1/q}{1/2‐1/q}},\;\alpha\in A_{2}^{\prime}\right\},

(14)

\displaystyle\Phi_{3}(n,\,N,\,q,\,K)=\min\{\nu_{\alpha}\varkappa_{\alpha,\beta}^{1/2‐1/p_{\alpha}}\min\{1,\,n^{‐1/2}N^{1/q}\}:\;\alpha\in A_{1}^{\prime}\cup A_{2}^{\prime},\;\beta\in A_{3}^{\prime}\}

(15)

(the infimum of the empty set is
+\infty
).

Remark 1
.

If
p_{\alpha}=q
, we have

\nu_{\alpha}\varkappa_{\alpha,\beta}^{1/q‐1/p_{\alpha}}=\nu_{\alpha}=\nu_{\alpha}(\min\{1,\,n^{‐1/2}N^{1/q}\})^{\frac{1/p_{\alpha}‐1/q}{1/2‐1/q}};

if
p_{\beta}=q
, then

\nu_{\alpha}\varkappa_{\alpha,\beta}^{1/q‐1/p_{\alpha}}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle(\ref{kappa_ab})}}{{=}}\nu_{\beta}\varkappa_{\alpha,\beta}^{1/q‐1/p_{\beta}}=\nu_{\beta}=\nu_{\beta}(\min\{1,\,n^{‐1/2}N^{1/q}\})^{\frac{1/p_{\beta}‐1/q}{1/2‐1/q}}.

If
p_{\alpha}=2
, we have

\nu_{\alpha}(\min\{1,\,n^{‐1/2}N^{1/q}\})^{\frac{1/p_{\alpha}‐1/q}{1/2‐1/q}}=

=\nu_{\alpha}\min\{1,\,n^{‐1/2}N^{1/q}\}=\nu_{\alpha}\varkappa_{\alpha,\beta}^{1/2‐1/p_{\alpha}}\min\{1,\,n^{‐1/2}N^{1/q}\};

if
p_{\beta}=2
, then

\nu_{\alpha}\varkappa_{\alpha,\beta}^{1/2‐1/p_{\alpha}}\min\{1,\,n^{‐1/2}N^{1/q}\}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle(\ref{kappa_ab})}}{{=}}\nu_{\beta}\varkappa_{\alpha,\beta}^{1/2‐1/p_{\beta}}\min\{1,\,n^{‐1/2}N^{1/q}\}=

=\nu_{\beta}\min\{1,\,n^{‐1/2}N^{1/q}\}=\nu_{\beta}(\min\{1,\,n^{‐1/2}N^{1/q}\})^{\frac{1/p_{\beta}‐1/q}{1/2‐1/q}}.

Theorem 2
.

Let
n,\,N\in\mathbb{N}
,
n\leqslant N‐1
, let
M
be defined by (
1
),
p_{\alpha}\geqslant 2
for every
\alpha\in A
. Suppose that (

\displaystyle d_{n}(M,\,l_{\infty}^{N})\asymp\inf\left\{\nu_{\alpha}\min\left(1,\,n^{‐1}\log\frac{2N}{n}\right)^{1/p_{\alpha}}:\;\alpha\in A\right\}.

2
Proof of the main results

First we formulate some well‐known results.

Theorem A
.

[
10
]
Let
1\leqslant p\leqslant q<\infty
,

0\leqslant n\leqslant N/2
.

1.

Let
1\leqslant q\leqslant 2
. Then
d_{n}(B_{p}^{N},\,l_{q}^{N})\underset{p,q}{\asymp}1
.

2.

Let
2<q<\infty
,
\lambda_{pq}=\min\left\{1,\,\frac{1/p-1/q}{1/2-1/q}\right\}
. Then

d_{n}(B_{p}^{N},\,l_{q}^{N})\underset{p,q}{\asymp}\min\{1,\,n^{-1/2}N^{1/q}\}^{\lambda_{pq}}.

Theorem B
.

[
3
,
4
]
Let
1\leqslant q\leqslant p\leqslant\infty
,
0\leqslant n\leqslant N
. Then

\displaystyle d_{n}(B_{p}^{N},\,l_{q}^{N})=(N-n)^{1/q-1/p}.

(17)

Theorem C
.

[
11
]

Let
p\geqslant 2
,
n\leqslant N‐1
. Then

\displaystyle d_{n}(B_{p}^{N},\,l_{\infty}^{N})\asymp\min\{1,\,n^{-1/p}\log^{1/p}(1+N/n)\}.

(18)

Given

for

,

for

,

is the group of permutations of

,

.

For

, the lower estimates for

[

]
.

Theorem D
.

[
9
]
Let

\displaystyle d_{n}(V_{k},\,l_{q}^{N})\underset{q}{\gtrsim}k^{1/q}.

,

,

The following result was obtained by Gluskin
[

[

,

,

It is clear that if

, the right‐hand side of (
62
) equals to
\nu_{\alpha_{*}}n^{‐1/2}N^{1/q}
; if
p_{\beta_{*}}=2
, we have
\nu_{\alpha_{*}}\varkappa_{\alpha_{*},\beta_{*}}^{1/2‐1/p_{\alpha_{*}}}n^{‐1/2}N^{1/q}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle(\ref{kappa_ab})}}{{=}}\nu_{\beta_{*}}n^{‐1/2}N^{1/q}
. Thus, we have arrived at the case already considered.

Further we suppose that
p_{\alpha_{*}}>2
,
p_{\beta_{*}}<2
.

Let
k=\lfloor\varkappa_{\alpha_{*},\beta_{*}}\rfloor
.

We show that

\displaystyle\nu_{\alpha_{*}}k^{‐1/p_{\alpha_{*}}}V_{k}\subset 2M.

(63)

It suffices to check that

\displaystyle\nu_{\alpha_{*}}\varkappa_{\alpha_{*},\beta_{*}}^{1/p_{\gamma}‐1/p_{\alpha_{*}}}\leqslant\nu_{\gamma},\quad\gamma\in A.

(64)

If
\gamma\in A_{1}^{\prime}\cup A_{2}^{\prime}
, we have

\nu_{\alpha_{*}}\varkappa_{\alpha_{*},\beta_{*}}^{1/2‐1/p_{\alpha_{*}}}n^{‐1/2}N^{1/q}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle(\ref{5}),(\ref{8}),(\ref{phi_last})}}{{\leqslant}}\nu_{\gamma}\varkappa_{\gamma,\beta_{*}}^{1/2‐1/p_{\gamma}}n^{‐1/2}N^{1/q};

this together with (
4
) yields that
\nu_{\beta_{*}}\varkappa_{\alpha_{*},\beta_{*}}^{1/2‐1/p_{\beta_{*}}}\leqslant\nu_{\beta_{*}}\varkappa_{\gamma,\beta_{*}}^{1/2‐1/p_{\beta_{*}}}
. Since
p_{\beta_{*}}<2
, it implies that
\varkappa_{\alpha_{*},\beta_{*}}\geqslant\varkappa_{\gamma,\beta_{*}}
. Hence, by
p_{\gamma}\geqslant 2>p_{\beta_{*}}
we get

\nu_{\alpha_{*}}\varkappa_{\alpha_{*},\beta_{*}}^{1/p_{\gamma}‐1/p_{\alpha_{*}}}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle(\ref{kappa_ab})}}{{=}}\nu_{\beta_{*}}\varkappa_{\alpha_{*},\beta_{*}}^{1/p_{\gamma}‐1/p_{\beta_{*}}}\leqslant\nu_{\beta_{*}}\varkappa_{\gamma,\beta_{*}}^{1/p_{\gamma}‐1/p_{\beta_{*}}}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle(\ref{kappa_ab})}}{{=}}\nu_{\gamma}.

If
\gamma\in A_{3}^{\prime}
, we have

\nu_{\alpha_{*}}\varkappa_{\alpha_{*},\beta_{*}}^{1/2‐1/p_{\alpha_{*}}}n^{‐1/2}N^{1/q}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle(\ref{5}),(\ref{8}),(\ref{phi_last})}}{{\leqslant}}\nu_{\alpha_{*}}\varkappa_{\alpha_{*},\gamma}^{1/2‐1/p_{\alpha_{*}}}n^{‐1/2}N^{1/q}.

Since
p_{\alpha_{*}}>2
, it yields that
\varkappa_{\alpha_{*},\beta_{*}}\leqslant\varkappa_{\alpha_{*},\gamma}
. Recall that
p_{\alpha_{*}}>2\geqslant p_{\gamma}
. Hence,

\nu_{\alpha_{*}}\varkappa_{\alpha_{*},\beta_{*}}^{1/p_{\gamma}‐1/p_{\alpha_{*}}}\leqslant\nu_{\alpha_{*}}\varkappa_{\alpha_{*},\gamma}^{1/p_{\gamma}‐1/p_{\alpha_{*}}}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle(\ref{kappa_ab})}}{{=}}\nu_{\gamma}.

This completes the proof of (
64
).

Now we show that

\displaystyle n\geqslant\varkappa_{\alpha_{*},\beta_{*}}^{1‐2/q}N^{2/q}.

(65)

If
\alpha_{*}\in A_{1}^{\prime}
, then (
65
) follows from

\nu_{\alpha_{*}}\varkappa_{\alpha_{*},\beta_{*}}^{1/2‐1/p_{\alpha_{*}}}n^{‐1/2}N^{1/q}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle(\ref{5}),(\ref{6}),(\ref{phi_last})}}{{\leqslant}}\nu_{\alpha_{*}}\varkappa_{\alpha_{*},\beta_{*}}^{1/q‐1/p_{\alpha_{*}}}.

If
\alpha_{*}\in A_{2}
, then (
65
) follows from

\nu_{\alpha_{*}}\varkappa_{\alpha_{*},\beta_{*}}^{1/2‐1/p_{\alpha_{*}}}n^{‐1/2}N^{1/q}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle(\ref{5}),(\ref{7}),(\ref{phi_last})}}{{\leqslant}}\nu_{\alpha_{*}}(n^{‐1/2}N^{1/q})^{\frac{1/p_{\alpha_{*}}‐1/q}{1/2‐1/q}}.

Hence,

d_{n}(M,\,l_{q}^{N})\stackrel{{\scriptstyle(\ref{nu_a_k_vk_2m})}}{{\gtrsim}}d_{n}(\nu_{\alpha_{*}}k^{‐1/p_{\alpha_{*}}}V_{k},\,l_{q}^{N})\stackrel{{\scriptstyle(\ref{kq0}),(\ref{ngevrkab12qn2q})}}{{\underset{q}{\gtrsim}}}

\gtrsim\nu_{\alpha_{*}}k^{1/2‐1/p_{\alpha_{*}}}n^{‐1/2}N^{1/q}\asymp\nu_{\alpha_{*}}\varkappa_{\alpha_{*},\beta_{*}}^{1/2‐1/p_{\alpha_{*}}}n^{‐1/2}N^{1/q}.

6.

Let
n\leqslant N^{2/q}
,
\Phi(n,\,N,\,q,\,K)=\nu_{\alpha_{*}}\varkappa_{\alpha_{*},\beta_{*}}^{1/2‐1/p_{\alpha_{*}}}
,
\alpha_{*}\in A_{1}^{\prime}\cup A_{2}
,
\beta_{*}\in A_{3}
. If
\alpha_{*}\in A_{1}^{\prime}
, it follows from (
5
), (
13
) that
\nu_{\alpha_{*}}\varkappa_{\alpha_{*},\beta_{*}}^{1/2‐1/p_{\alpha_{*}}}\leqslant\nu_{\alpha_{*}}\varkappa_{\alpha_{*},\beta_{*}}^{1/q‐1/p_{\alpha_{*}}}
; therefore,
\varkappa_{\alpha_{*},\beta_{*}}\leqslant 1
. By (
5
), we get
\varkappa_{\alpha_{*},\beta_{*}}=1
and
\Phi(n,\,N,\,q,\,K)=\nu_{\alpha_{*}}\varkappa_{\alpha_{*},\beta_{*}}^{1/q‐1/p_{\alpha_{*}}}
. We have arrived at the case already considered. If
\alpha_{*}\in A_{2}
, it follows from (
5
), (
14
) that
\nu_{\alpha_{*}}\varkappa_{\alpha_{*},\beta_{*}}^{1/2‐1/p_{\alpha_{*}}}\leqslant\nu_{\alpha_{*}}
. Hence,
\varkappa_{\alpha_{*},\beta_{*}}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle(\ref{kappa_n})}}{{=}}1
and
\Phi(n,\,N,\,q,\,K)=\nu_{\alpha_{*}}
. We have arrived at the case already considered.

7.

Let
n\leqslant N^{2/q}
,
p_{\alpha_{*}}=2
,
\Phi(n,\,N,\,q,\,K)=\nu_{\alpha_{*}}
. We prove that
\nu_{\alpha_{*}}\leqslant\nu_{\alpha}
for all
\alpha\in A
. If
\alpha\in A_{2}
, it follows from (
5

14
). If

\nu_{\alpha}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle(\ref{kappa_ab})}}{{=}}\nu_{\alpha_{*}}\varkappa_{\alpha_{*},\alpha}^{1/p_{\alpha}‐1/p_{\alpha_{*}}}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle(\ref{kappa_n})}}{{\geqslant}}\nu_{\alpha_{*}}
. If

by (

\varkappa_{\alpha,\alpha_{*}}=1

Hence,

, and by Theorem
A

d_{n}(M,\,l_{q}^{N})\geqslant\nu_{\alpha_{*}}d_{n}(B_{1}^{N},\,l_{q}^{N})\underset{q}{\gtrsim}\nu_{\alpha_{*}}.

Proof of Theorem
2
.

The upper estimate follows from Theorem

.

Let us prove the lower estimate. As in Theorem

, we may assume that the set
K

\displaystyle\nu_{\alpha_{*}}\left\lceil n\log^{‐1}\frac{2N}{n}\right\rceil^{‐1/p_{\alpha_{*}}}\leqslant\nu_{\alpha}\left\lceil n\log^{‐1}\frac{2N}{n}\right\rceil^{‐1/p_{\alpha}},\quad\alpha\in A.

(66)

be the constant from Theorem

,

. Notice that if

3
The case of arbitrary
\nu_{\alpha}
.

Notice that if
\inf_{\alpha\in A}\nu_{\alpha}=0
, then
\cap_{\alpha\in A}\nu_{\alpha}B^{N}_{p_{\alpha}}=\{0\}
. Therefore, further we assume that
\inf_{\alpha\in A}\nu_{\alpha}>0
.

We set
Z=\{1/p_{\alpha}:\;\alpha\in A\}\subset[0,\,1]
; for
z=1/p_{\alpha}
we set
\nu(z)=\nu_{\alpha}
. Then
M=\cap_{z\in Z}\nu(z)B^{N}_{1/z}
, and (
5
) is equivalent to

1\leqslant\frac{\nu(z)}{\nu(w)}\leqslant N^{z‐w},\quad z,\,w\in Z,\quad z\geqslant w.

Let
\nu:Z\rightarrow(0,\,\infty)
be an arbitrary function, and let
\inf_{z\in Z}\nu(z)>0
. We construct the function
\nu_{**}:Z\rightarrow(0,\,\infty)
such that

\displaystyle 1\leqslant\frac{\nu_{**}(z)}{\nu_{**}(w)}\leqslant N^{z‐w},\quad z,\,w\in Z,\quad z\geqslant w,

(67)

\displaystyle M=\cap_{z\in Z}\nu_{**}(z)B^{N}_{1/z}.

(68)

Let

\nu_{*}(z)=\inf\{\nu(w):w\geqslant z\},\quad z\in Z.

Then
\nu_{*}(z)\leqslant\nu(z)
and the function
\nu_{*}
is non‐decreasing. We show that

\displaystyle M=\cap_{z\in Z}\nu_{*}(z)B^{N}_{1/z}.

(69)

Since
\nu_{*}(z)\leqslant\nu(z)
for all
z\in Z
, it suffices to check that

\displaystyle\nu_{*}(w)B_{1/w}^{N}\supset\cap_{z\in Z}\nu(z)B^{N}_{1/z},\quad w\in Z.

(70)

Let
w_{n}\geqslant w
,
\nu(w_{n})\underset{n\to\infty}{\to}\nu_{*}(w)
. Then

\nu_{*}(w)B_{1/w}^{N}\supset\cap_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\nu(w_{n})B^{N}_{1/w}\supset\cap_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\nu(w_{n})B^{N}_{1/w_{n}},

which implies (
70
).

Now we set

\nu_{**}(z)=\inf\{\nu_{*}(w)\cdot N^{z‐w}:\;w\leqslant z\}.

Notice that

\displaystyle\nu_{**}(z)\leqslant\nu_{*}(z),\quad z\in Z.

(71)

We show that
\nu_{**}
is a non‐decreasing function. Indeed, let
z_{1}\leqslant z_{2}
. If
w\leqslant z_{1}
, then
\nu_{*}(w)\cdot N^{z_{1}‐w}\leqslant\nu_{*}(w)\cdot N^{z_{2}‐w}
; hence,
\nu_{**}(z_{1})\leqslant\inf\{\nu_{*}(w)\cdot N^{z_{2}‐w}:\;w\leqslant z_{1}\}
. If
z_{1}\leqslant w\leqslant z_{2}
, by the monotonicity of the function
\nu_{*}
we get

\nu_{*}(w)\cdot N^{z_{2}‐w}\geqslant\nu_{*}(z_{1})\geqslant\nu_{**}(z_{1}).

Therefore,
\nu_{**}(z_{2})\geqslant\nu_{**}(z_{1})
.

Now we prove that

\displaystyle\frac{\nu_{**}(z_{2})}{\nu_{**}(z_{1})}\leqslant N^{z_{2}‐z_{1}},\quad z_{1},\;z_{2}\in Z,\quad z_{1}\leqslant z_{2}.

(72)

This yields (
67
). For every
w\leqslant z_{1}
we have

\nu_{**}(z_{2})\leqslant\nu_{*}(w)\cdot N^{z_{2}‐w}=\nu_{*}(w)\cdot N^{z_{1}‐w}\cdot N^{z_{2}‐z_{1}};

taking infimum over
w\leqslant z_{1}
, we get (
72
).

Now we show that

\displaystyle\cap_{z\in Z}\nu_{**}(z)B^{N}_{1/z}=\cap_{z\in Z}\nu_{*}(z)B^{N}_{1/z}.

(73)

By (
71
), it suffices to check that for every
w\in Z

\displaystyle\nu_{**}(w)B^{N}_{1/w}\supset\cap_{z\in Z}\nu_{*}(z)B^{N}_{1/z}.

(74)

Let
w_{n}\leqslant w
,
\nu_{*}(w_{n})\cdot N^{w‐w_{n}}\underset{n\to\infty}{\to}\nu_{**}(w)
. Then

\nu_{**}(w)B^{N}_{1/w}\supset\cap_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\nu_{*}(w_{n})N^{w‐w_{n}}B_{1/w}^{N}\supset\cap_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\nu_{*}(w_{n})B_{1/w_{n}}^{N}.

Hence, we obtain (
74
).

Now, (
69
) and (
73
) imply (
68
).
