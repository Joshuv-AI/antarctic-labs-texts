Decomposition of some classes of regular graphs into cycles and paths of length eight
Cecily Sahai. C, Sampath Kumar. S, Arputha Jose. T

Abstract
. In this paper, we examine the necessary and sufficient conditions for the existence of a ( 8 , p , q ) (8;p,q) -decomposition of λ \lambda -fold tensor product and wreath product of complete graphs.

\Box
