A non-singular bouncing cosmology in f ⁡ ( R , T ) f(R,T) gravity
J. K. Singh, Shaily, Akanksha Singh, Aroonkumar Beesham, Hamid Shabani

Abstract
We investigate a bounce realization in the framework of higher order curvature in f ⁡ ( R , T ) f(R,T) modified theory of gravity. We perform a detailed analysis of the cosmological parameters to explain the contraction phase, the bounce phase, and the expansion phase. Furthermore, we observe a violation of the null energy condition, instability of the model, and a singularity upon deceleration at the bouncing point, which are the supporting results for a bouncing cosmology. The outcome of the slow roll parameters is satisfactory to understand the inflation era and the equation of state parameter exhibits a ghost condensate behavior of the model near the bounce. Additionally, we discuss the stability of the model using linear perturbations in the Hubble parameter as well as the energy density.
