Abstract
At present the gravitational waves detectors achieve the sensitivity to detect the length variation ( δ ​ L \delta L ), 𝒪 ≈ 10 − 17 − 10 − 21 \mathcal{O}\approx 10^{-17}-10^{-21} meter. Recently a more stringent upperbound on the dimensionless parameter β 0 \beta_{0} , bearing the effect of generalized uncertainty principle has been given which corresponds to the intermediate length scale l i ​ m = β 0 ​ l p ​ l ∼ 10 − 23 ​ m l_{im}=\sqrt{\beta_{0}}l_{pl}\sim 10^{-23}m . Hence it becomes quite obvious to search for the generalized uncertainty principle by observing the response of the vibrations of phonon modes in such resonant detectors in the near future. Therefore, we calculate the resonant frequencies and transition rates induced by the incoming gravitational waves on these detectors in the generalized uncertainty principle framework.
