The Arinkin-Gaitsgory temperedness conjecture
Joakim Færgeman, Sam Raskin

Abstract
. Arinkin and Gaitsgory defined a category of tempered D D -modules on Bun G \operatorname{Bun}_{G} that is conjecturally equivalent to the category of quasi-coherent (not ind-coherent!) sheaves on LocSys G ˇ \operatorname{LocSys}_{\check{G}} . However, their definition depends on the auxiliary data of a point of the curve; they conjectured that their definition is independent of this choice. Beraldo has outlined a proof of this conjecture that depends on some technology that is not currently available. Here we provide a short, unconditional proof of the Arinkin-Gaitsgory conjecture.

Relation to work of Beraldo

1.2.1.

A strategy of proof for Theorem

We describe the ingredients for his approach below.

1.2.2.

Roughly speaking, Beraldo’s approach proceeds as follows.

Beraldo has explained that

\mathsf{QCoh}(\operatorname{LocSys}_{\check{G}})

[

1.2.3.

Unfortunately, the factorizable derived Satake theorem has been

and again more recently by Justin Campbell and the second
author, where it is currently work in progress. In particular, at the time we

publicly in written form.
So the full derivation of the action of Beraldo’s

This remains an open problem, for which Beraldo’s suggestion of

1.3.2.
