Jackson-Stechkin type inequalities for differentiable functions in weighted Orlicz spaces
Ramazan Akgün ⋆

Abstract
. In the present work some Jackson Stechkin type direct theorems of trigonometric approximation are proved in Orlicz spaces with weights satisfying some Muckenhoupt’s A p A_{p} condition. To obtain refined version of the Jackson type inequality we prove an extrapolation theorem, Marcinkiewicz multiplier theorem and Littlewood Paley type results. As a consequence refined inverse Marchaud type inequalities are obtained. By means of a realization result we find an equivalence between the fractional order weighted modulus of smoothness and the classical weighted Peetre’s K K -functional.

The present work is devoted to obtain some Jackson type direct theorems of
trigonometric approximation in weighted Orlicz spaces
L_{W}^{\varphi}

some singular integrals and maximal operators are bounded in the weighted
Lebesgue spaces
L_{W}^{p}
(
[
10

monograph
[
10
]

weights. In this study we will focus on those Muckenhoupt weights that in
A_{p\left(\varphi\right)}
where
p\left(\varphi\right)
is the indice
(
[
17
]
) of
\varphi
:

\frac{1}{p\left(\varphi\right)}:=\inf\left\{p:p>0,\text{ }\varphi^{p}\text{ is quasiconvex}\right\}.

(1.1)

A function
\varphi
is called
Young function
if
\varphi
is
even, continuous function, nonnegative in
\mathbb{R}:=\left(-\infty,+\infty\right)
, increasing on
\mathbb{R}^{+}:=\left(0,\infty\right)

such that

\varphi\left(0\right)=0\text{,\quad}\underset{x\rightarrow\infty}{\lim}\varphi\left(x\right)=\infty\text{.}

A function

function
\Phi

\Phi\left(x\right)\leq\varphi\left(x\right)\leq\Phi\left(Cx\right)\text{,\quad}\forall x\geq 0\text{.}

Detailed information of quasiconvex functions and their applications see

[

22

The Jackson type theorem relates the best approximation error
E_{n}\left(f\right)_{\varphi,W}
from above with the modulus of smoothness. There is
a lot of way of defining the modulus of smoothness. Generally speaking the
modulus of smoothness are defined by means of the operations such as the
usual translation operator
f\left(\cdot\right)\rightarrow f\left(\cdot+h\right)
, (
h\in\mathbb{R}
), or the average operator
f\left(\cdot\right)\rightarrow\frac{1}{h}\int\nolimits_{0}^{h}f\left(\cdot+t\right)dt
and so on. Usual translation operator
f\left(\cdot\right)\rightarrow f\left(\cdot+h\right)
is, in general, not bounded for weighted spaces of
functions. For overcoming this difficulty we will consider the Steklov
average operator

f\left(\cdot\right)\rightarrow\mathcal{A}_{h}f\left(\cdot\right):=\frac{1}{h}\int\nolimits_{-h/2}^{h/2}f\left(\cdot+t\right)dt

and the Muckenhoupt weights. Considering weighted Lebesgue space
L_{W}^{p}

with Muckenhoupt weight
W\in A_{p}
, (
1<p<\infty
), in 1986, E. A.
Gadjieva (
[
15
]
) defined the modulus of smoothness which is
constructed by means of the operator

\sigma_{h}f\left(\cdot\right):=\frac{1}{2h}\int\nolimits_{-h}^{h}f\left(\cdot+t\right)dt,\text{\quad}h\in\mathbb{R}^{+}.

(1.2)

For the same spaces
L_{W}^{p}
,
W\in A_{p}
, (
1<p<\infty
), another
approach is given by N. X. Ky, see e.g.,
[
26
,
27
,
28
]
. The Steklov
mean satisfies the inequality
\left|\mathcal{A}_{h}f\left(x\right)\right|\leq Mf(x)
almost everywhere on
\mathsf{T}:=[0,2\pi]
, where
M
is the Hardy-Littlewood maximal function. Considering the boundedness of
the Hardy-Littlewood maximal function
M
, author and Israfilov (
[
8
]
) was considered the following modulus of smoothness in weighted Orlicz
spaces

\underset{\underset{i=1,2,...,r}{0<h_{i}\leq\delta}}{\sup}\left\|\prod\limits_{i=1}^{r}\left(I-\sigma_{{}_{h_{i}}}\right)f\right\|_{\varphi,W}\text{,\quad}r\in\mathbb{N},

where
\left\|\cdot\right\|_{\varphi,W}
is some norm in weighted
Orlicz space.

In this work we will consider more natural weighted modulus of smoothness

\Omega_{r}\left(f,\delta\right)_{\varphi,W}:=\underset{0<h\leq\delta}{\sup}\left\|\left(I-\mathcal{A}_{{}_{h}}\right)^{r}f\right\|_{\varphi,W}\text{,\quad}r\in\mathbb{R}^{+}

of fractional order
r\in\mathbb{R}^{+}
. We obtain in
Theorem

5.2
that if

(1.3)

then

2.
Orlicz spaces with Muckenhoupt weights

A function
\omega:\mathsf{T}\mathbb{\rightarrow}\left[0,\infty\right]

will be called weight if
\omega
is measurable and positive a.e. on
\mathsf{T}.
A 2
\pi
-periodic weight
\omega
belongs to the Muckenhoupt
class
A_{p}
,
1\leq p<\infty
, if

\left(meas(J)\right)^{-1}\omega\left(J\right)\lesssim Cessinf_{x\in J}\omega\left(x\right)\text{, a.e. on }\mathsf{T}\text{,\quad}\left(p=1\right)\text{,}

(2.1)

C:=\sup\limits_{J}\frac{\omega\left(J\right)}{meas(J)}\left(\frac{\left[\omega\left(J\right)\right]^{\frac{1}{1-p}}}{meas(J)}\right)^{p-1}<\infty\text{,\quad}\left(1<p<\infty\right)

(2.2)

with some finite constant independent of
J
, where
J
is any subinterval
of
\mathsf{T}
and
\omega(A):=\int_{A}\omega(t)dt
for
A\subset\mathsf{T}
. The least constant
C
in (
2.1
) and (
2.2
) will be denoted
by
\left[\omega\right]_{p}
for
1\leq p<\infty.

Let

the class of Lebesgue measurable functions

satisfying the condition

\int_{\mathsf{T}}\varphi\left(C\left|f\left(x\right)\right|\right)W\left(x\right)dx<\infty

for some
C\in\mathbb{R}^{+}
. The collection of functions in

becomes a normed space with the Orlicz

\left\|f\right\|_{\varphi,W}:=\sup\left\{\int\limits_{\mathsf{T}}\left|f\left(x\right)g\left(x\right)\right|W\left(x\right)dx:\int\limits_{\mathsf{T}}\tilde{\varphi}\left(\left|g\right|\right)W\left(x\right)dx\leq 1\right\}

\tilde{\varphi}\left(y\right):=\sup_{x\geq 0}\left\{xy-\varphi\left(x\right)\right\},\quad y\geq 0,

(2.3)

is the
complementary function
of
\varphi
. The Banach space
L_{W}^{\varphi}
is called

. For a weight
W
, taking
\varphi_{p}:=\varphi\left(x,p\right):=x^{p}
,
1\leq p<\infty
, we
obtain the classical weighted Lebesgue space

. If
\varphi
is quasiconvex and
\tilde{\varphi}
is its

xy\leq\varphi\left(x\right)+\tilde{\varphi}\left(y\right)\text{,\quad}x,y\geq 0\text{.}

(2.4)

We define the

\left\|f\right\|_{\left(\varphi\right),\omega}:=\inf\left\{\tau>0:\int\limits_{\mathsf{T}}\varphi\left(\frac{\left|f\left(x\right)\right|}{\tau}\right)W\left(x\right)dx\leq 1\right\}\text{.}

There exist (

16

]
. We will use its one dimensional 2
\pi
-periodic version: Let a

(8.6)

where

Using (1) and (3) of Lemma 6.1.6 of

,

\displaystyle n^{-2k}\left\|\sum\limits_{j=1}^{n}j^{2k}A_{j}\left(\cdot+\frac{k\pi}{j},T_{n}\right)\right\|_{\varphi,W}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle n^{-2k}\left\|\sum\limits_{j=1}^{n}j^{2k}\left(\cos k\pi A_{j}\left(\cdot,T_{n}\right)-\sin k\pi A_{j}\left(\cdot,\widetilde{T_{n}}\right)\right)\right\|_{\varphi,W}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\left\|\sum\limits_{j=1}^{n}\left(\frac{j}{2n}\right)^{2k}A_{j}\left(\cdot,T_{n}\right)\right\|_{\varphi,W}

\displaystyle+\left\|\sum\limits_{j=1}^{n}\left(j/2n\right)^{2k}A_{j}\left(\cdot,\widetilde{T_{n}}\right)\right\|_{\varphi,W}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\left\|\sum\limits_{j=1}^{n}\left(\frac{\left(j/2n\right)^{2}}{\left(1-\frac{\sin j/2n}{j/2n}\right)}\right)^{k}\left(1-\frac{\sin j/2n}{j/2n}\right)^{k}A_{j}\left(\cdot,T_{n}\right)\right\|_{\varphi,W}+

\displaystyle+\left\|\sum\limits_{j=1}^{n}\left(\frac{\left(j/2n\right)^{2}}{\left(1-\frac{\sin j/2n}{j/2n}\right)}\right)^{k}\left(1-\frac{\sin j/2n}{j/2n}\right)^{k}A_{j}\left(\cdot,\widetilde{T_{n}}\right)\right\|_{\varphi,W}.

Using Marcinkiewicz type multiplier Theorem
6.5
we have

\displaystyle n^{-2k}\left\|T_{n}^{\left(2k\right)}\right\|_{\varphi,W}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\left\|\sum\limits_{j=1}^{n}\left(1-\frac{\sin j/2n}{j/2n}\right)^{k}A_{j}\left(\cdot,T_{n}\right)\right\|_{\varphi,W}+

\displaystyle+\left\|\sum\limits_{j=1}^{n}\left(1-\frac{\sin j/2n}{j/2n}\right)^{k}A_{j}\left(\cdot,\widetilde{T_{n}}\right)\right\|_{\varphi,W}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left\|\sum\limits_{j=1}^{n}\left(1-\frac{\sin j/2n}{j/2n}\right)^{k}A_{j}\left(\cdot,T_{n}\right)\right\|_{\varphi,W}+

\displaystyle+\left\|\left(\sum\limits_{j=1}^{n}\left(1-\frac{\sin j/2n}{j/2n}\right)^{k}A_{j}\left(\cdot,T_{n}\right)\right)^{\thicksim}\right\|_{\varphi,W}.

In the last step we used the linearity property of conjugate operator. Thus
from boundedness of conjugate (see e.g., (
4.6
)) operator we get

\displaystyle n^{-2k}\left\|T_{n}^{\left(2k\right)}\right\|_{\varphi,W}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\left\|\sum\limits_{j=1}^{n}\left(1-\frac{\sin j/2n}{j/2n}\right)^{k}A_{j}\left(\cdot,T_{n}\right)\right\|_{\varphi,W}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\left\|(I-\mathcal{A}_{1/n})^{k}T_{n}\right\|_{\varphi,W}\lesssim\Omega_{k}\left(T_{n},1/n\right)_{\varphi,W}.

∎
