Chen Zhao

Abstract
. Given a proper holomorphic surjective morphism f : X → Y f:X\rightarrow Y from a compact Kähler manifold to a compact Kähler manifold, and a Nakano semipositive holomorphic vector bundle E E on X X , we prove Kollár type vanishing theorems on cohomologies with coefficients in R q ​ f ∗ ​ ( ω X ​ ( E ) ) ⊗ F R^{q}f_{\ast}(\omega_{X}(E))\otimes F , where F F is a k k -positive vector bundle on Y Y . Let

Let

2.1.
Hermitian vector bundle

Let
(M,ds_{M}^{2})
be a complex manifold of dimension
n

ds_{M}^{2}
.
Let
(F,h_{F})
be a holomorphic vector bundle of rank
r
on
M
endowed with a Hermitian
metric
h_{F}
and let
(F^{\ast},h_{F}^{\ast})
be its dual Hermitian bundle.
Let
A^{p,q}(M,F)
be the space of
F
-valued smooth
(p,q)
-forms on
M

A_{0}^{p,q}(M,F)
be its subspace with compact support.
Let
\ast:A^{p,q}(M,F)\rightarrow A^{n-q,n-p}(M,F)
be the Hodge star operator relative to
ds_{M}^{2}
and let
\sharp_{F}:A^{p,q}(M,F)\to A^{q,p}(M,F^{\ast})
be the anti-isomorphism induced by
h_{F}
. Denote by
\langle-,-\rangle
the pointwise inner product on
A^{p,q}(M,F)
. These operators are related by

(2.1)

\displaystyle\langle\alpha,\beta\rangle{\rm vol}_{ds_{M}^{2}}=\alpha\wedge\ast\sharp_{F}\beta.

Let

(2.2)

\displaystyle(\alpha,\beta):=\int_{M}\langle\alpha,\beta\rangle{\rm vol}_{ds_{M}^{2}}

and
\|\alpha\|:=\sqrt{(\alpha,\alpha)}
.
Let
\nabla=D^{\prime}+\bar{\partial}
be the Chern connection relative to
h_{F}
. Let

\bar{\partial}^{\ast}_{h_{F}}=-\ast D^{\prime}\ast
and
D^{\prime\ast}_{h_{F}}=-\ast\bar{\partial}\ast
be the formal adjoints of
\bar{\partial}
and
D^{\prime}
respectively.

Denote by
\Theta_{h_{F}}=\nabla^{2}
the curvature of
(F,h_{F})
.
Locally we write

\Theta_{h_{F}}=\sqrt{-1}\sum_{i,j}\omega_{ij}e_{i}\otimes e_{j}^{\ast}

where
\omega_{ij}\in A^{1,1}_{M}
,
(e_{1},\dots,e_{r})
is an orthogonal local frame of
F
and
(e^{\ast}_{1},\dots,e^{\ast}_{r})
is the dual frame.

Definition 2.1
.

[
7
]

•

A tensor

is said to be

•

F
is called

-positive

\sqrt{-1}\Theta_{h_{F}}(F)(u,u)>0
for any nonzero

of rank
\leq m
. In this case, we write
\Theta_{h_{F}}(F)>_{m}0
(or
F>_{m}0
).

•

F
is called
Griffiths positive
if
m=1
, and
Nakano positive
if
m\geq\min\{n,r\}
.

•

F
is called
Nakano semipositive
, if the bilinear form

\displaystyle\theta(u_{1},u_{2}):=\sum_{i,j}\omega_{i,j}(u_{1i},\overline{u_{2j}}),\quad u_{l}=\sum_{i}u_{li}\otimes e_{i}\in T_{M}\otimes F,\quad l=1,2

is semi-positive definite.

\displaystyle\mathscr{D}_{Y}^{p,0}(E)\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\bar{\partial}}}{{\to}}\mathscr{D}_{Y}^{p,1}(E)\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\bar{\partial}}}{{\to}}\cdots\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\bar{\partial}}}{{\to}}\mathscr{D}_{Y}^{p,m}(E),

Let

\dim X=n+m

\dim Y=m

be a Nakano semipositive holomorphic vector bundle on

. As
[

, we fix a Kähler metric

on

such that the following conditions hold.

(1)

For every point

of

of

on

.

R^{q}f^{o}_{\ast}(\omega_{X^{o}/{Y^{o}}}(E))\simeq R^{q}f_{\ast}(\omega_{X/Y}(E))|_{Y^{o}}
is locally free. Here,

is a Kähler metric on

as described in §2.3, with

being its associated Kähler form.

3.1.
Mourougane-Takayama’s Hodge metric on

This section provides a review of Mourougane-Takayama’s construction of a Hodge metric on
R^{q}f^{o}_{\ast}(\omega_{{X^{o}}/{Y^{o}}}(E))
with Nakano semipositive curvature
[
28
]
. For more details, see
[
27
,
28
,
29
]
.

Define the sheaf
\mathscr{H}_{f}^{n+m,q}(E,h)
associated with the proper submersion
f:X\to Y
as described in §2.3. It follows from Theorem
2.5
that there exists a natural isomorphism

\tau:R^{q}f_{\ast}(\omega_{X}(E))\simeq\mathscr{H}_{f}^{n+m,q}(E).

Denote

\tau^{o}:=\tau|_{Y^{o}}:R^{q}f^{o}_{\ast}(\omega_{X^{o}}(E))\simeq\mathscr{H}_{f}^{n+m,q}(E)|_{Y^{o}}=:\mathscr{H}_{f^{o}}^{n+m,q}(E).

Let
y\in Y^{o}
and let
W\simeq\{t=(t_{1},\dots,t_{m})\in\mathbb{C}^{m}\mid\|t\|<1\}
be holomorphic coordinates centered at
y

Let
X^{o}_{W}
denote
(f^{o})^{-1}(W)
and let
dt
denote
dt_{1}\wedge\cdots\wedge dt_{m}
.
Take a trivialization
\mathscr{O}_{W}\xrightarrow{\sim}\Omega_{W}^{m}
given by

This trivialization induces an isomorphism of sheaves
\Omega_{{X^{o}_{W}}/{W}}^{n}\simeq\Omega_{{X^{o}_{W}}/{W}}^{n}\otimes(f^{o})^{\ast}\Omega_{W}^{m}\simeq\omega_{X^{o}_{W}}
via

\alpha^{q}_{W}:R^{q}f^{o}_{\ast}(\omega_{{X^{o}}/{Y^{o}}}(E))|_{W}\xrightarrow{\simeq}R^{q}f^{o}_{\ast}(\omega_{X^{o}}(E))|_{W}.

We also have an injection
\Omega_{{X^{o}_{W}}/W}^{n-q}\rightarrow\Omega_{X^{o}_{W}}^{n+m-q}
by
\sigma\mapsto\sigma\wedge dt
.
This injection induces the injection

\beta_{W}:f^{o}_{\ast}(\Omega_{{X^{o}}/{Y^{o}}}^{n-q}(E))|_{W}\rightarrow f^{o}_{\ast}(\Omega_{X^{o}}^{n+m-q}(E))|_{W}.

Notice that for every
u\in\mathscr{H}^{n+m,q}(X^{o}_{W},E)

\sigma_{u}\in H^{0}(X^{o}_{W},\Omega_{{X^{o}_{W}}/W}^{n-q}(E))

or
[
28

is well-defined and injective, and thus yields a homomorphism

\delta^{q}_{W}:\mathscr{H}_{f^{o}}^{n+m,q}(E)|_{W}\rightarrow f^{o}_{\ast}(\Omega_{{X^{o}}/{Y^{o}}}^{n-q}(E))|_{W},

where

\ast=\beta_{W}\circ\delta^{q}_{W}:\mathscr{H}_{f^{o}}^{n+m,q}(E)|_{W}\rightarrow f^{o}_{\ast}(\Omega_{X^{o}/{Y^{o}}}^{n-q}(E))|_{W}\rightarrow f^{o}_{\ast}(\Omega_{X^{o}}^{n+m-q}(E))|_{W}.

Then the composition map

S_{W}^{q}:R^{q}f^{o}_{\ast}(\omega_{X^{o}/{Y^{o}}}(E))|_{W}\xrightarrow{\alpha^{q}_{W}}R^{q}f^{o}_{\ast}(\omega_{X^{o}}(E))|_{W}\xrightarrow{\tau^{o}}\mathscr{H}_{f^{o}}^{n+m,q}(E)|_{W}\xrightarrow{\delta_{W}^{q}}f^{o}_{\ast}(\Omega_{{X^{o}}/{Y^{o}}}^{n-q}(E))|_{W}

is injective.

For every

and every pair of vectors

The induced metric

is independent of the choice of the coordinate

and thus defines a global Hermitian metric on the bundle

(
[
28
, Lemma 5.2]
). This Hermitian metric

is then called the

on
R^{q}f^{o}_{\ast}(\omega_{X^{o}/{Y^{o}}}(E))
.

Theorem 3.1
.

(
[
28
, Theorem 1.1]
)

\sqrt{-1}\Theta_{h}(R^{q}f^{o}_{\ast}(\omega_{X^{o}/{Y^{o}}}(E)))
is Nakano semipositive.

Now we define

as the associated
L^{2}
-Dolbeault complex
with respect to
ds_{Y}^{2}
and

Theorem 3.2

\mathscr{D}_{Y}^{m,\bullet}(R^{q}f^{o}_{\ast}(\omega_{X^{o}/{Y^{o}}}(E)))

is a fine resolution of
R^{q}f_{\ast}(\omega_{X}(E))
for every

.

Theorem 3.5

4.
Applications to Kollár type vanishing theorems

To establish the main vanishing theorem, it is necessary to introduce the following estimate.

Lemma 4.1
.

[
7
]

Let
(M,\tilde{\omega})
be a complete Kähler manifold of dimension
n
,
\omega
another Kähler metric,
possibly non-complete, and
E
a
m
-semi-positive vector bundle of rank
r
on
M
. Let
g\in L_{(2)}^{n,q}(X,E)
be such that
D^{\prime\prime}g=0
and

\int_{M}\langle A_{q}^{-1}g,g\rangle dV<+\infty

with respect to
\omega
, where
A_{q}=[i\Theta(E),\Lambda]
in bidegree
(n,q)
and
q\geq 1
,

m\geq\min\{n-q+1,r\}
. Then there exists
f\in L_{(2)}^{n,q-1}(M,E)
such that
D^{\prime\prime}f=g
and

\|f\|^{2}\leq\int_{M}\langle A_{q}^{-1}g,g\rangle dV.

Theorem 4.2
.

Let
f:X\rightarrow Y
be a proper holomorphic surjective morphism from a compact Kähler manifold to a compact Kähler manifold. Let
E
be a Nakano semipositive vector bundle on
X
and let
(F,h_{F})
be a
k
-positive Hermitian vector bundle on
Y
of rank
r
. Then

H^{i}(Y,R^{q}f_{\ast}(\omega_{X}(E))\otimes F)=0,\quad\forall i\geq 1,k\geq\min\{\dim_{\mathbb{C}}Y-i+1,r\}.

Proof.

First, we assert that

R^{q}f_{\ast}\omega_{X}(E)\otimes F\rightarrow\mathscr{D}^{\dim_{\mathbb{C}},\bullet}_{Y}(R^{q}f^{o}_{\ast}(\omega_{X^{o}/{Y^{o}}}(E\otimes f^{\ast}F)))

is a fine resolution for each
q
.
Since the problem is local, we consider an arbitrary point
y\in Y
and let
V
be an open neighborhood around
y
in
Y
so that
F|_{V}\simeq\mathscr{O}_{V}^{\oplus r}
and the metric
h_{F}
is quasi-isometric to the trivial metric.
Consequently,
E\otimes f^{\ast}F
is Nakano semipositive on
V
. By applying Theorem
1.3
, we confirm the validity of the claim.

Thus there exists an isomorphism

H^{i}(Y,R^{q}f_{\ast}(\omega_{X}(E))\otimes F)\simeq H^{\dim_{\mathbb{C}}Y,i}_{(2)}(Y^{o},R^{q}f_{\ast}(\omega_{X/Y}(E\otimes f^{\ast}(F)))|_{Y^{o}};ds_{Y}^{2},h\otimes h_{F}),\quad\forall i,

where

is the Hodge metric on

and

R^{q}f_{\ast}(\omega_{X}(E\otimes f^{\ast}(F)))|_{Y^{o}}\simeq R^{q}f^{o}_{\ast}(\omega_{X^{o}/Y^{o}}(E))\otimes F|_{Y^{o}}.

Since
F
is
k
-positive, the Hermitian operator
[\sqrt{-1}\Theta_{h_{F}}(F),\Lambda]
is positive on

\Lambda^{\dim_{\mathbb{C}}Y,i}T^{\ast}_{Y}\otimes F
for each

(
[
7
, Chap VII, Lemma (7.2)]
).

is compact, by applying Theorem

we can conclude that
[\sqrt{-1}\Theta_{h\otimes h_{F}}(R^{q}f^{o}_{\ast}(\omega_{X^{o}/Y^{o}}(E))\otimes F),\Lambda]
has a positive lower bound on

(\Lambda^{\dim_{\mathbb{C}}Y,i}T^{\ast}_{Y}\otimes R^{q}f^{o}_{\ast}(\omega_{X^{o}/Y^{o}}(E))\otimes F)
for each
i\geq 1,k\geq\min\{\dim_{\mathbb{C}}Y-i+1,r\}
. Additionally, the compactness of

implies that there is a globally defined complete Kähler metric on

, as shown in
[

. By applying Lemma

, we obtain the result that

H^{\dim_{\mathbb{C}}Y,i}_{(2)}(Y^{o},R^{q}f_{\ast}(\omega_{X/Y}(E\otimes f^{\ast}(F)))|_{Y^{o}})=0,\quad i>0.

Therefore, the theorem is proven.
∎
