A complete characterization of spectra of the Randić matrix of level-wise regular trees Corresponding author.
Punit Vadher

Abstract
Let G G be a simple finite connected graph with vertex set V ⁡ ( G ) = { v 1 , v 2 , … , v n } V(G)=\{v_{1},v_{2},\ldots,v_{n}\} . Denote the degree of vertex v i v_{i} by d i d_{i} for all 1 ≤ i ≤ n 1\leq i\leq n . The Randić matrix of G G , denoted by R ​ ( G ) = [ r i , j ] \mbox{{R}}(G)=[r_{i,j}] , is the n × n n\times n matrix whose ( i , j ) (i,j) -entry r i , j r_{i,j} is r i , j = 1 / d i ​ d j r_{i,j}=1/\sqrt{d_{i}d_{j}} if v i v_{i} and v j v_{j} are adjacent in G G and 0 otherwise. A tree is a connected acyclic graph. A level-wise regular tree is a tree rooted at one vertex r r or two (adjacent) vertices r r and r ′ r^{\prime} in which all vertices with the minimum distance i i from r r or r ′ r^{\prime} have the same degree m i m_{i} for 0 ≤ i ≤ h 0\leq i\leq h , where h h is the height of T T . In this paper, we give a complete characterization of the eigenvalues with their multiplicity of the Randić matrix of level-wise regular trees. We prove that the eigenvalues of the Randić matrix of a level-wise regular tree are the eigenvalues of the particular tridiagonal matrices, which are formed using the degree sequence ( m 0 , m 1 , … , m h − 1 ) (m_{0},m_{1},\ldots,m_{h-1}) of level-wise regular trees. Keywords: Graph spectrum, Randić matrix, Level-wise regular tree. AMS Subject Classification (2020): 15A18, 05C50, 05C05.

In this paper, we completely characterize all the eigenvalues (with their multiplicity) of the Randić matrix of the level-wise regular trees, a large class of trees. In fact, we prove that the eigenvalues of the Randić matrix of level-wise regular trees are the eigenvalues of the leading principal submatrices of three symmetric tridiagonal matrices of order equal to the height of level-wise regular trees plus one. This paper is organised as follows: We define the necessary terms, notations and also recall some well-known results of spectral theory in section

Concluding Remarks

The results presented in this paper reduce the computation of the eigenvalues of the Randić matrix of level-wise regular trees of size equal to the order (given in (
1
)) of level-wise regular trees to specific tridiagonal matrices (described in Theorem
3.1
) of size equal to the height of level-wise regular trees plus one. For examples, the level-wise regular trees given in Examples
2.2
and
2.3
are of order 41 and 56, respectively (and hence their Randić matrices of size
41\times 41
and
56\times 56
, respectively), but the eigenvalues for both trees can be calculated using matrices of size
4\times 4
(refer Examples
3.6
and
3.7
). Hence, the Randić index (which is the sum of the squares of the eigenvalues of Randić matrix) and the Randić energy (which is the sum of the absolute values of the eigenvalues of Randić matrix) can be easily calculated for level-wise regular trees.
