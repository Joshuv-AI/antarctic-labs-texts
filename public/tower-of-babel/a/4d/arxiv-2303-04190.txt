Multivariate growth and cogrowth
Rostislav Grigorchuk, Jean-François Quint, Asif Shaikh

Abstract
We investigate a multivariate growth series Γ L ​ ( 𝐳 ) , 𝐳 ∈ ℂ d \Gamma_{L}(\mathbf{z}),\mathbf{z}\in\mathbb{C}^{d} associated with a regular language L L over an alphabet of cardinality d . d. Our focus is on languages coming from subgroups of the free group and from subshifts of finite type. We develop a mechanism for computing the rate of growth φ L ​ ( 𝐫 ) \varphi_{L}(\mathbf{r}) of L L in the direction 𝐫 ∈ ℝ d \mathbf{r}\in\mathbb{R}^{d} . Using the concave growth condition (CG) introduced by the second author in [ 18 ] and the results of Convex Analysis we represent ψ L ​ ( 𝐫 ) = log ⁡ ( φ L ​ ( 𝐫 ) ) \psi_{L}(\mathbf{r})=\log\left(\varphi_{L}(\mathbf{r})\right) as a support function of a convex set that is a closure of the Relog image of the domain of absolute convergence of Γ L ​ ( 𝐳 ) \Gamma_{L}(\mathbf{z}) . This allows us to compute ψ L ​ ( 𝐫 ) \psi_{L}(\mathbf{r}) in some important cases, like a Fibonacci language or a language of freely reduced words representing elements of a free group F 2 F_{2} . Also we show that the methods of the Large deviation theory can be used as an alternative approach. Finally, we suggest some open problems directed on the possibility of extensions of the results of the first author from [ 13 ] on multivariate cogrowth.

The study of asymptotic properties of a sequence
\{\gamma_{n}\}_{n\geq 0}

Regular languages, their growth series and the condition (E)

We begin this section with recall of definition of finite automaton and language accepted by it. A finite automaton
\mathscr{A}
is given by a quintuple
\left(Q,\Sigma,\kappa,q_{0},\mathscr{F}\right),
where

\Sigma

is a special state called the initial state and the set
\mathscr{F}\subset Q
is nonempty set whose elements are called final states. It is convenient to visualize
\mathscr{A}
as a labeled directed graph
\Theta_{\mathscr{A}}
with the vertex set

\kappa(q,a_{i})=s

. Multiple edges and loops are allowed. The graph
\Theta_{\mathscr{A}}
is called the diagram of

The example of these diagrams are presented by Figures

and
8

is deterministic and complete automaton, i.e. given any

A word

A language

The important feature of this definition is the uniqueness of the path

for each

by a nonempty subset

Now, we recall some basic notions of LDT, namely, the notion of the rate function
I
and the Large Deviation Principal (LDP). A rate function is a lower semi-continuous function

defined on a topological space

is a closed subset of

A

is a rate function for which all level sets

are compact. A sequence

of Borel measures on

satisfies LDP if, for every Borel subset

-\displaystyle\inf_{\mathbf{x}\in B^{\circ}}I(x)\leq\displaystyle\liminf_{n\rightarrow\infty}\frac{1}{n}\log\mu_{n}(B)\leq\limsup_{n\rightarrow\infty}\frac{1}{n}\log\mu_{n}(B)\leq-\inf_{\mathbf{x}\in\overline{B}}I(\mathbf{x}),

where
B^{\circ},\overline{B}
are, respectively, the interior and the closure set of the set
B
.
