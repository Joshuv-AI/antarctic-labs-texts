Finite energy Navier-Stokes flows with unbounded gradients induced by localized flux in the half-space
Kyungkeun Kang, Baishun Lai, Chen-Chih Lai, Tai-Peng Tsai

Abstract
For the Stokes system in the half space, Kang [Math. Ann. 2005] showed that a solution generated by a compactly supported, Hölder continuous boundary flux may have unbounded normal derivatives near the boundary. In this paper we first prove explicit global pointwise estimates of the above solution, showing in particular that it has finite global energy and its derivatives blow up everywhere on the boundary away from the flux. We then use the above solution as a profile to construct solutions of the Navier-Stokes equations which also have finite global energy and unbounded normal derivatives due to the flux. Our main tool is the pointwise estimates of the Green tensor of the Stokes system proved by us in [ 11 ] (arXiv:2011.00134). We also examine the Stokes flows generated by dipole bumps boundary flux, and identify the regions where the normal derivatives of the solutions tend to positive or negative infinity near the boundary. Key words : Stokes system, Navier-Stokes equations, half space, unbounded derivatives, boundary flux, dipole bumps, everywhere blow-up, Green tensor 2010 Mathematics Subject Classifications : 35Q30, 76D03, 76D05, 76D07

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{1}\frac{-\log u}{(b+u)^{m}}du

Proof.

\displaystyle\geq\left(\frac{m}{(m+1){d}}\right)^{n-2}s^{\frac{n-1}{2}}(4\pi)^{\frac{n-1}{2}}\left(1-2^{\frac{n-1}{4}}e^{-\frac{{d}^{2}}{8m^{2}s}}\right),

(3.39)

\displaystyle K(x^{\prime},s)

\displaystyle\leq\left(\frac{m}{(m-1){d}}\right)^{n-2}s^{\frac{n-1}{2}}(4\pi)^{\frac{n-1}{2}}+\frac{C}{d^{n-2}}s^{\frac{n-1}{2}}e^{-\frac{{d}^{2}}{8m^{2}s}}.

(3.40)

where
{d}=\left|x^{\prime}\right|>0
, and
C=C(n)>0
is some absolute constant.

Remark.
For the application to the proof of Theorem
1.1
, it is crucial that the two leading constants
(\frac{m}{m+1})^{n-2}(4\pi)^{\frac{n-1}{2}}
and
(\frac{m}{m-1})^{n-2}(4\pi)^{\frac{n-1}{2}}
are close for large
m
. In particular, we cannot absorb the second terms in (
3.39
) and (
3.40
) to the first terms.

Proof.

Due to the scaling property
K(\mu x^{\prime},\mu^{2}s)=\mu K(x^{\prime},s)
and that of (
3.39
) and (
3.40
), it suffices to compute for the case
s=1
.
We split
\Sigma
into three disjoint regions
\mathcal{A}
,
\mathcal{B}
and
\mathcal{C}
defined by

\mathcal{A}=\left\{z^{\prime}\in\Sigma:\left|x^{\prime}-z^{\prime}\right|<\frac{{d}}{m}\right\},\quad\mathcal{B}=\left\{z^{\prime}\in\Sigma:\left|z^{\prime}\right|<\frac{(m-1)d}{m}\right\},

and

\mathcal{C}=\Sigma\setminus(\mathcal{A}\cup\mathcal{B})
,
and divide the integral as

K(x^{\prime},1)=\int_{\Sigma}\frac{e^{-\frac{|x^{\prime}-z^{\prime}|^{2}}{4}}}{\left|z^{\prime}\right|^{n-2}}dz^{\prime}=\int_{\mathcal{A}}\cdots+\int_{\mathcal{B}}\cdots+\int_{\mathcal{C}}\cdots:=K_{\mathcal{A}}+K_{\mathcal{B}}+K_{\mathcal{C}}.

Since
\left|z^{\prime}\right|<(1-1/m){d}
in
\mathcal{B}
, we have
|x^{\prime}-z^{\prime}|\geq\frac{d}{m}
and, with
m\geq 2
,

\begin{split}K_{\mathcal{B}}&=\left(\int_{|z^{\prime}|<\frac{d}{2}}+\int_{\frac{d}{2}<|z^{\prime}|<d-\frac{d}{m}}\right)\frac{e^{-\frac{|x^{\prime}-z^{\prime}|^{2}}{4}}}{\left|z^{\prime}\right|^{n-2}}dz^{\prime}\\
&\leq e^{-\frac{{d}^{2}}{16}}\int_{|z^{\prime}|<\frac{d}{2}}\frac{dz^{\prime}}{\left|z^{\prime}\right|^{n-2}}+(d/2)^{2-n}\int_{\frac{d}{2}<|z^{\prime}|<d-\frac{d}{m}}e^{-\frac{{d}^{2}}{8m^{2}}}e^{-\frac{|x^{\prime}-z^{\prime}|^{2}}{8}}dz^{\prime}\\
&\leq Cde^{-\frac{{d}^{2}}{16}}+Cd^{2-n}e^{-\frac{{d}^{2}}{8m^{2}}}\\
&\leq Cd^{2-n}e^{-\frac{{d}^{2}}{8m^{2}}}.\end{split}

Since
(1-1/m){d}\leq\left|z^{\prime}\right|
in both
\mathcal{A}
and
\mathcal{C}
, we have

\begin{split}K_{\mathcal{A}}+K_{\mathcal{C}}&\leq\left(\frac{m}{(m-1){d}}\right)^{n-2}\int_{\mathcal{A}\cup\mathcal{C}}e^{-\frac{|x^{\prime}-z^{\prime}|^{2}}{4}}dz^{\prime}\leq\left(\frac{m}{(m-1){d}}\right)^{n-2}\int_{\Sigma}e^{-\frac{|w^{\prime}|^{2}}{4}}dw^{\prime},\end{split}

and

\int_{\Sigma}e^{-|w^{\prime}|^{2}/4}dw^{\prime}=\int_{\Sigma}(4\pi)^{\frac{n-1}{2}}\Gamma^{\prime}(w^{\prime},1)\,dw^{\prime}=(4\pi)^{\frac{n-1}{2}}.

Above
\Gamma^{\prime}(w^{\prime},t)
is the heat kernel on
\Sigma\times(0,\infty)
.
Summing up the above estimates, we obtain the upper bound of
K
in (
3.40
).

For the lower bound (
3.39
), we note that
\left|z^{\prime}\right|\leq(1+1/m){d}
in
\mathcal{A}
. Thus

K(x^{\prime},1)\geq K_{\mathcal{A}}\geq\left(\frac{m}{(m+1){d}}\right)^{n-2}\int_{|w^{\prime}|<\frac{d}{m}}e^{-\frac{|w^{\prime}|^{2}}{4}}dw^{\prime},

while

\begin{split}\int_{|w^{\prime}|<\frac{{d}}{m}}e^{-\frac{|w^{\prime}|^{2}}{4}}dw^{\prime}&=\int_{\Sigma}e^{-\frac{|w^{\prime}|^{2}}{4}}dw^{\prime}-\int_{|w^{\prime}|>\frac{{d}}{m}}e^{-\frac{|w^{\prime}|^{2}}{4}}dw^{\prime}\\
&\geq\int_{\Sigma}e^{-\frac{|w^{\prime}|^{2}}{4}}dw^{\prime}-e^{-\frac{{d}^{2}}{8m^{2}}}\int_{\Sigma}e^{-\frac{|w^{\prime}|^{2}}{8}}dw^{\prime}\\
&=(4\pi)^{\frac{n-1}{2}}-e^{-\frac{{d}^{2}}{8m^{2}}}(4\pi\sqrt{2})^{\frac{n-1}{2}},\end{split}

using

\int_{\Sigma}e^{-|w^{\prime}|^{2}/8}dw^{\prime}=\int_{\Sigma}(4\pi\sqrt{2})^{\frac{n-1}{2}}\Gamma^{\prime}(w^{\prime},\sqrt{2})\,dw^{\prime}=(4\pi\sqrt{2})^{\frac{n-1}{2}}.

This shows (
3.39
).
∎

We are now ready to prove Theorem
1.1
.

Proof of Theorem
1.1
.

Step 1
. Hölder continuity.

Note that the boundary value
\phi\in C^{2a,a}(\mathbb{R}^{n-1}\times[0,2])
. Let
b=a
if
0<a<1/2
, and
b
can be any number in
(0,1/2)
when
a=1/2
. We claim that, since
\phi(\xi^{\prime},s)=g(\xi^{\prime})\,h(s)\,e_{n}
and
g\in C^{1}_{c}(B_{1}^{\prime})
,

R^{\prime}\phi\in C^{2b,b}(\mathbb{R}^{n-1}\times[0,2]),

(3.41)

where
R^{\prime}=(R^{\prime}_{1},\ldots,R^{\prime}_{n-1})
is
(n-1)
-dimensional Riesz transforms on
\mathbb{R}^{n-1}
. (If
n=2
,
R^{\prime}
is the Hilbert transform on
\mathbb{R}
.)
Indeed, it is known that homogeneous Hölder continuity is preserved by the Riesz transforms, i.e.
R^{\prime}g\in\dot{C}^{2b}(\mathbb{R}^{n-1})
(see e.g. Chang-Jin
[
3
, Proposition 4.1]
and related reference therein) if
0<2b<1
.
Being locally Hölder continuous,
R^{\prime}g
is locally bounded.
If
\left|\xi^{\prime}\right|>2
, then

\left|R_{i}g(\xi^{\prime})\right|=\left|\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n-1}}c\frac{\xi^{\prime}_{i}-y^{\prime}_{i}}{|\xi^{\prime}-y^{\prime}|^{n}}g(y^{\prime})dy^{\prime}\right|{\ \lesssim\ }\int_{B_{1}^{\prime}}\frac{1}{|\xi^{\prime}|^{n-1}}\left|g(y^{\prime})\right|dy^{\prime}\lesssim\frac{\left\|g\right\|_{L^{1}}}{|\xi^{\prime}|^{n-1}}.

The above shows (
3.41
).
By
[
3
, Theorem 1.2]
, we have
\hat{v}\in C^{2b,b}(\overline{\mathbb{R}^{n}_{+}}\times[0,2])
.

Step 2
. Finite global energy in
\mathbb{R}^{n}_{+}\times(0,2)
.

We first show that
\hat{v}(t)\in L^{2}_{x}(\mathbb{R}^{n}_{+})
,
n\geq 3
for
t\in[0,2]
.
With the aid of the estimate (
3.3
) in Proposition
3.1
, we have

\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}_{+}}|\hat{v}(x,t)|^{2}\,dx\leq C\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}_{+}}\frac{N^{2}_{1}}{{\langle x\rangle}^{2(n-1)}}\,dx\lesssim\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{(\rho^{2}+1)^{\frac{n-1}{2}}}d\rho<\infty

for
n\geq 3
. The last integral diverges if
n=2
.
On the other hand, by the estimate (
3.4
) of the gradient of
\hat{v}
for
n\geq 3
,

|\partial_{x_{j}}\hat{v}_{i}(x,t)|\leq CN_{2}\bigg[\frac{1}{{\langle x\rangle}^{n}}+\frac{\sigma\mathrm{L\mkern-0.5muN}}{{\langle x\rangle}^{n-1}(x^{2}_{n}+1)^{a}}\bigg],\qquad\sigma=\delta_{i<n}\delta_{jn},

(3.42)

where

\mathrm{L\mkern-0.5muN}=(x_{n}^{2}+|t-1|)^{a-\frac{1}{2}}+\delta_{a=\frac{1}{2}}\log\left(2+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{2}+|t-1|}\right).

It is clear that
1/{{\langle x\rangle}^{n}}
is square integrable in
\mathbb{R}^{n}_{+}
, uniformly in
t
, and thus it suffices to estimate the second term of the right-hand side in (
3.42
), in the case
\sigma=1
.
Firstly, we consider the case that
a<1/2
. Using
x_{n}^{2}+|t-1|\leq x_{n}^{2}+1
, we obtain

\begin{split}&\int_{0}^{2}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}_{+}}\frac{(x_{n}^{2}+|t-1|)^{2a-1}}{{\langle x\rangle}^{2(n-1)}(x^{2}_{n}+1)^{2a}}\,dxdt\lesssim\int_{0}^{2}\int_{\mathbb{R}_{+}}\frac{1}{(x_{n}^{2}+|t-1|)}\,dx_{n}dt\\
&\lesssim\int_{0}^{2}\frac{1}{|t-1|^{\frac{1}{2}}}\,dt<\infty.\end{split}

(3.43)

In the case
a=1/2
, we have

\int_{0}^{2}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}_{+}}\frac{\log\left(2+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{2}+|t-1|}\right)^{2}}{{\langle x\rangle}^{2(n-1)}(x^{2}_{n}+1)}\,dxdt\lesssim\int_{0}^{2}\int_{\mathbb{R}_{+}}\frac{\log\left(2+\frac{1}{|t-1|}\right)^{2}}{(x^{2}_{n}+1)}\,dx_{n}dt<\infty.

(3.44)

Collecting the above estimates, we get (
1.8
), i.e., the solution is of finite energy when
n\geq 3
.

Step 3
. Pressure estimate.

Let
n\geq 2
. We now derive the pointwise estimate (
1.9
) for the pressure
\hat{p}
. From (
3.2
), we have

\begin{split}\hat{p}(x,t)=&~2\partial_{n}^{2}\int_{\Sigma}E(x-\xi^{\prime})\phi_{n}(\xi^{\prime},t)\,d\xi^{\prime}+2\int_{\Sigma}E(x-\xi^{\prime})\partial_{t}\phi_{n}(\xi^{\prime},t)\,d\xi^{\prime}\\
&-4(\partial_{t}-\Delta_{x^{\prime}})\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\int_{\Sigma}\partial_{n}A(x-\xi^{\prime},t-\tau)\phi_{n}(\xi^{\prime},\tau)\,d\xi^{\prime}d\tau\\
&=:I+II+III.\end{split}

Since

I=2\sum_{i=1}^{n-1}\int_{\Sigma}E(x-\xi^{\prime})\partial_{i}^{2}\phi(\xi^{\prime},t)\,d\xi^{\prime},

|I|{\ \lesssim\ }h(t)\sum_{i=1}^{n}\int_{B_{1}^{\prime}}|E(x-\xi^{\prime})|\,d\xi^{\prime}.

For
|x|<2
,

\int_{B_{1}^{\prime}}|E(x-\xi^{\prime})|\,d\xi^{\prime}{\ \lesssim\ }1.

For
|x|\geq 2
, then
|x-\xi^{\prime}|\geq|x|-|\xi^{\prime}|\geq 1
so that
\log|x-\xi^{\prime}|>0
for
|\xi^{\prime}|\leq 1
, and

\begin{split}\int_{B_{1}^{\prime}}|E(x-\xi^{\prime})|\,d\xi^{\prime}{\ \lesssim\ }\begin{cases}\int_{B_{1}^{\prime}}\frac{1}{|x-\xi^{\prime}|^{n-2}}\,d\xi^{\prime}{\ \lesssim\ }\frac{1}{|x|^{n-2}}&\quad n\geq 3,\\
\int_{B_{1}^{\prime}}\log|x-\xi^{\prime}|\,d\xi^{\prime}{\ \lesssim\ }\log(|x|+1)&\quad n=2.\end{cases}\end{split}

Thus,

|I|{\ \lesssim\ }h(t)\left(\mathbbm{1}_{n\geq 3}\,\frac{1}{{\langle x\rangle}^{n-2}}+\mathbbm{1}_{n=2}\log(|x|+1)\right).

For
II
, from the same computation as for
I
we have

\begin{split}|II|&{\ \lesssim\ }|h^{\prime}(t)|\left(\mathbbm{1}_{n\geq 3}\,\frac{1}{{\langle x\rangle}^{n-2}}+\mathbbm{1}_{n=2}(1+\log(|x|+1))\right)\\
&{\ \lesssim\ }\frac{\chi_{\frac{1}{4}\leq t\leq 1}(t)}{(1-t)^{1-a}}\left(\mathbbm{1}_{n\geq 3}\,\frac{1}{{\langle x\rangle}^{n-2}}+\mathbbm{1}_{n=2}\log(|x|+2)\right).\end{split}

(3.45)

Note that the estimate of
I
is dominated by that of
II
.

For
III
, we consider the three cases:
t<\frac{1}{4}
,
t\in[\frac{1}{4},1)
and
t\geq 1
.

If
t<\frac{1}{4}
, since
\mathop{\mathrm{supp}}h\subset[\frac{1}{4},1]
and
A(x,t)=0
for
t<0
, we have
t-\tau<0
for
t<\frac{1}{4}
and
\tau\geq\frac{1}{4}
. So

\begin{split}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\int_{\Sigma}\partial_{n}A(x-\xi^{\prime},t-\tau)\phi_{n}(\xi^{\prime},\tau)\,d\xi^{\prime}d\tau&=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\int_{\Sigma}\partial_{n}A(x-\xi^{\prime},t-\tau)g(\xi^{\prime})h(\tau)\,d\xi^{\prime}d\tau\\
&=\int_{\frac{1}{4}}^{\infty}\int_{\Sigma}\partial_{n}A(x-\xi^{\prime},t-\tau)g(\xi^{\prime})h(\tau)\,d\xi^{\prime}d\tau=0,\end{split}

and thus
III=0
.

If
t\in[\frac{1}{4},1)
, integrating by parts we have

\begin{split}|III|&{\ \lesssim\ }\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\int_{\Sigma}|\partial_{n}A(x-\xi^{\prime},t-\tau)|\left[|\Delta_{\xi^{\prime}}g(\xi^{\prime})|h(\tau)+|g(\xi^{\prime})||h^{\prime}(\tau)|\right]d\xi^{\prime}d\tau\\
&{\ \lesssim\ }\int_{\frac{1}{4}}^{t}\int_{B_{1}^{\prime}}\frac{1}{\sqrt{t-\tau}(|x-\xi^{\prime}|+\sqrt{t-\tau})^{n-1}}\,h(\tau)\,d\xi^{\prime}d\tau\\
&\quad+\int_{\frac{1}{4}}^{t}\int_{B_{1}^{\prime}}\frac{1}{\sqrt{t-\tau}(|x-\xi^{\prime}|+\sqrt{t-\tau})^{n-1}}\,|h^{\prime}(\tau)|\,d\xi^{\prime}d\tau=:III_{1}+III_{2}.\end{split}

Since

\begin{split}&\int_{B_{1}^{\prime}}\frac{1}{(|x-\xi^{\prime}|+\sqrt{t-\tau})^{n-1}}\,d\xi^{\prime}\\
&{\ \lesssim\ }\begin{cases}\int_{0}^{|x|+1}\frac{r^{n-2}}{(t+\sqrt{t-\tau})^{n-1}}\,d\xi^{\prime}{\ \lesssim\ }\frac{|x|+1}{|x|+\sqrt{t-\tau}+1}\left(1+\log_{+}\frac{|x|+1}{\sqrt{t-\tau}}\right)&\qquad|x|<2,\\
\frac{1}{(|x|+\sqrt{t-\tau})^{n-1}}&\qquad|x|\geq 2,\end{cases}\\
&{\ \lesssim\ }\frac{\log\left(2+\frac{1}{\sqrt{t-\tau}}\right)}{(|x|+\sqrt{t-\tau}+1)^{n-1}},\end{split}

we have, using the change of variables
u=\sqrt{t-\tau}
,

\begin{split}|III_{1}|&{\ \lesssim\ }\int_{\frac{1}{4}}^{t}\frac{1}{\sqrt{t-\tau}}\,\frac{\log\left(2+\frac{1}{\sqrt{t-\tau}}\right)}{(|x|+\sqrt{t-\tau}+1)^{n-1}}\,(1-\tau)^{a}\,d\tau\\
&{\ \lesssim\ }\int_{0}^{\sqrt{t-\frac{1}{4}}}\frac{\log\left(2+\frac{1}{u}\right)}{(|x|+u+1)^{n-1}}\,du{\ \lesssim\ }\frac{1}{{\langle x\rangle}^{n-1}},\end{split}

and

\begin{split}|III_{2}|&{\ \lesssim\ }\int_{\frac{1}{4}}^{t}\frac{1}{\sqrt{t-\tau}}\,\frac{\log\left(2+\frac{1}{\sqrt{t-\tau}}\right)}{(|x|+\sqrt{t-\tau}+1)^{n-1}}\,\frac{1}{(1-\tau)^{1-a}}\,d\tau\\
&{\ \lesssim\ }\frac{1}{{\langle x\rangle}^{n-1}}\int_{0}^{\sqrt{t-\frac{1}{4}}}\log\left(2+\frac{1}{u}\right)\,\frac{1}{(u^{2}+1-t)^{1-a}}\,du{\ \lesssim\ }\frac{1}{(1-t)^{1-a}{\langle x\rangle}^{n-1}}.\end{split}

Therefore, for
t\in[\frac{1}{4},1)

|III|{\ \lesssim\ }\frac{1}{(1-t)^{1-a}{\langle x\rangle}^{n-1}}.

(3.46)

If
t\geq 1
, integrating by parts we have

\begin{split}|III|&{\ \lesssim\ }\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\int_{\Sigma}|\partial_{n}A(x-\xi^{\prime},t-\tau)|\left[|\Delta_{\xi^{\prime}}g(\xi^{\prime})|h(\tau)+|g(\xi^{\prime})||h^{\prime}(\tau)|\right]d\xi^{\prime}d\tau\\
&{\ \lesssim\ }\int_{\frac{1}{4}}^{1}\int_{B_{1}^{\prime}}\frac{1}{\sqrt{t-\tau}(|x-\xi^{\prime}|+\sqrt{t-\tau})^{n-1}}\,h(\tau)\,d\xi^{\prime}d\tau\\
&\quad+\int_{\frac{1}{4}}^{1}\int_{B_{1}^{\prime}}\frac{1}{\sqrt{t-\tau}(|x-\xi^{\prime}|+\sqrt{t-\tau})^{n-1}}\,|h^{\prime}(\tau)|\,d\xi^{\prime}d\tau=:III_{1}+III_{2},\end{split}

where, using the change of variables
u=\sqrt{t-\tau}
,

\begin{split}|III_{1}|&{\ \lesssim\ }\int_{\frac{1}{4}}^{1}\frac{1}{\sqrt{t-\tau}}\,\frac{\log\left(2+\frac{1}{\sqrt{t-\tau}}\right)}{(|x|+\sqrt{t-\tau}+1)^{n-1}}\,(1-\tau)^{a}\,d\tau\\
&{\ \lesssim\ }\int_{\sqrt{t-1}}^{\sqrt{t-\frac{1}{4}}}\frac{\log\left(2+\frac{1}{u}\right)}{(|x|+u+1)^{n-1}}\,du{\ \lesssim\ }\frac{1}{{\langle x\rangle}^{n-1}},\end{split}

and

\begin{split}|III_{2}|&{\ \lesssim\ }\int_{\frac{1}{4}}^{1}\frac{1}{\sqrt{t-\tau}}\,\frac{\log\left(2+\frac{1}{\sqrt{t-\tau}}\right)}{(|x|+\sqrt{t-\tau}+1)^{n-1}}\,\frac{1}{(1-\tau)^{1-a}}\,d\tau\\
&{\ \lesssim\ }\frac{1}{{\langle x\rangle}^{n-1}}\int_{t-1}^{t-\frac{1}{4}}\frac{1}{\sqrt{s}}\,\log\left(2+\frac{1}{\sqrt{s}}\right)\frac{1}{(1-t+s)^{1-a}}\,ds,\qquad s=t-\tau,\\
&{\ \lesssim\ }\frac{1}{{\langle x\rangle}^{n-1}}\int_{t-1}^{t-\frac{1}{4}}\frac{1}{s}\,\frac{1}{(1-t+s)^{1-a}}\,ds\\
&=\frac{1}{{\langle x\rangle}^{n-1}}\int_{0}^{\frac{3}{4}}\frac{1}{(u+t-1)u^{1-a}}\,du,\qquad u=1-t+s,\\
&{\ \lesssim\ }\frac{1}{{\langle x\rangle}^{n-1}}\,\frac{(3/4)^{a}}{(t-1/4)^{a}}\,\frac{1}{(t-1)^{1-a}}\sim\frac{1}{(t-1)^{1-a}{\langle x\rangle}^{n-1}}.\end{split}

Therefore, for
t\geq 1

|III|{\ \lesssim\ }\frac{1}{(t-1)^{1-a}{\langle x\rangle}^{n-1}}.

(3.47)

Combining (
3.46
) and (
3.47
), for all
t

|III|{\ \lesssim\ }\frac{\chi_{t\geq\frac{1}{4}}(t)}{|t-1|^{1-a}{\langle x\rangle}^{n-1}}.

(3.48)

The pointwise estimate (
1.9
) follows from (
3.45
) and (
3.48
).

Step 4
. Lower bound of velocity gradient: Set up.

By (
3.7
) of Remark
3.2
, we have
|\partial_{j}\hat{v}_{i}(x,1)-\sigma\partial_{n}I_{2}(x,1)|\leq C{\langle x\rangle}^{-n}
where
\sigma=\delta_{i<n}\delta_{jn}
. In particular,
|\partial_{j}\hat{v}_{i}(x,1)|
may blow up as
x_{n}\to 0_{+}
only if
\sigma=1
, i.e.,
i<n=j
.
We will show that
|\partial_{n}\hat{v}_{i}(x,1)|
is indeed unbounded when
i<n
.
It suffices to estimate
\partial_{n}I_{2}(x,1)
given in (
3.23
). After integration by parts,

\partial_{n}I_{2}(x,1)=-4\int_{0}^{1}\int_{\Sigma}\partial_{n}^{2}B(x-\xi^{\prime},s)\,\partial_{i}\phi_{n}(\xi^{\prime},1-s)\,d\xi^{\prime}ds,\quad\text{for }i<n.

(3.49)

Recall
K(x^{\prime},t)
defined in Lemma
3.3
,

K(x^{\prime},t):=\int_{\Sigma}\frac{e^{-\frac{|x^{\prime}-z^{\prime}|^{2}}{4t}}}{\left|z^{\prime}\right|^{n-2}}dz^{\prime},\qquad x^{\prime}\in\mathbb{R}^{n-1}.

By the definition (
2.10
) of
B
, it follows that

\begin{split}4B(x-\xi^{\prime},s)&=e^{-\frac{x_{n}^{2}}{4s}}\frac{C_{n}}{s^{\frac{n}{2}}}\,K(x^{\prime}-\xi^{\prime},s),\\
4\partial_{n}^{2}B(x-\xi^{\prime},s)&=e^{-\frac{x_{n}^{2}}{4s}}\left(-\frac{1}{2}+\frac{x_{n}^{2}}{4s}\right)\frac{C_{n}}{s^{\frac{n+2}{2}}}\,K(x^{\prime}-\xi^{\prime},s),\end{split}

where
C_{n}=4(4\pi)^{-n/2}[n(n-2)|B_{1}|]^{-1}>0
.
By our assumption (
1.4
)–(
1.6
) on the boundary value, we have

\partial_{i}\phi_{n}(\xi^{\prime},1-s)=\partial_{i}g(\xi^{\prime})\,s^{a},\quad(0\leq a\leq\tfrac{1}{2}).

(3.50)

Therefore we have

\partial_{n}I_{2}(x,1)=C_{n}\int_{0}^{1}\int_{\Sigma}e^{-\frac{x_{n}^{2}}{4s}}\left(\frac{1}{2}-\frac{x_{n}^{2}}{4s}\right)\frac{1}{s^{\frac{n+2}{2}}}\,K(x^{\prime}-\xi^{\prime},s)\,\partial_{i}g(\xi^{\prime})s^{a}\,d\xi^{\prime}ds.

(3.51)

To get a lower bound of
\partial_{n}I_{2}
, we choose
g
in a product form as in (
1.10
).

For fixed
i
,
denote the reflection
x^{\prime}_{*}=(x_{1},\ldots,-x_{i},\ldots,x_{n-1})
which differs from
x^{\prime}
in the sign of the
i
th component
x_{i}
. For our choice of
g
we have

\partial_{n}I_{2}(x_{*}^{\prime},x_{n},1)=-\partial_{n}I_{2}(x,1).

(3.52)

That is,
\partial_{n}I_{2}(x,1)
is odd in
x_{i}
for the fixed
i
in (
3.51
).
It is because

\begin{split}&\int_{\Sigma}K(x^{\prime}-\xi^{\prime},s)\,\partial_{i}g(\xi^{\prime})\,d\xi^{\prime}=\int_{\Sigma}K(x^{\prime}_{*}-\xi^{\prime}_{*},s)\,\partial_{i}g(\xi^{\prime})\,d\xi^{\prime}\\
&=\int_{\Sigma}K(x^{\prime}_{*}-\eta^{\prime},s)\,\partial_{i}g(\eta^{\prime}_{*})\,d\eta^{\prime}=-\int_{\Sigma}K(x^{\prime}_{*}-\eta^{\prime},s)\,\partial_{i}g(\eta^{\prime})\,d\eta^{\prime}.\end{split}

Above
\eta^{\prime}=\xi^{\prime}_{*}
, and we used
\partial_{i}g(\eta^{\prime}_{*})=-\partial_{i}g(\eta^{\prime})
in the last equality.

In what follows we assume

|x_{i}|=\max_{1\leq j\leq n-1}|x_{j}|\quad\text{and}\quad x_{i}<0.

(3.53)

The case
x_{i}>0
follows from (
3.52
) with a sign change.

Step 5
. Key estimates.

Decompose
\partial_{n}I_{2}
in (
3.51
) as

\begin{split}\frac{\partial_{n}I_{2}(x,1)}{C_{n}}&=\int_{0}^{x^{2}_{n}/2}\int_{\Sigma}e^{-\frac{x_{n}^{2}}{4s}}s^{a-1-\frac{n}{2}}K(x^{\prime}-\xi^{\prime},s)\left(-\frac{1}{2}+\frac{x_{n}^{2}}{4s}\right)\left[(\partial_{i}g)_{-}-(\partial_{i}g)_{+}\right](\xi^{\prime})\,d\xi^{\prime}ds\\
&\quad+\int_{x^{2}_{n}/2}^{1}\int_{\Sigma}e^{-\frac{x_{n}^{2}}{4s}}s^{a-1-\frac{n}{2}}\,K(x^{\prime}-\xi^{\prime},s)\left(\frac{1}{2}-\frac{x_{n}^{2}}{4s}\right)\left[(\partial_{i}g)_{+}-(\partial_{i}g)_{-}\right](\xi^{\prime})\,d\xi^{\prime}ds.\end{split}

By Lemma
3.3
with a large enough
m
that will be chosen later,

\begin{split}\frac{\partial_{n}I_{2}(x,1)}{C_{n}(4\pi)^{\frac{n-1}{2}}}&\geq\int_{0}^{x^{2}_{n}/2}\int_{\Sigma}e^{-\frac{x_{n}^{2}}{4s}}s^{a-1-\frac{n}{2}}\left(-\frac{1}{2}+\frac{x_{n}^{2}}{4s}\right)s^{\frac{n-1}{2}}\\
