Abstract
Erdős-Ginzburg-Ziv theorem says that if there are 2 ​ n − 1 2n-1 number is given, then there are n n numbers such that their sum is divided by n n . We will connect this theorem with the Ramsey theoretic large sets and will prove an infinitary version of this theorem. In our proof we will use the methods of ultrafilters. But one may proceed using methods of Topological dynamics.

\beta S

The basis for the topology is

Since
\beta S

Theorem 2

Let

\langle x_{i,1}\rangle_{i=1}^{\infty}

\langle x_{i,2}\rangle_{i=1}^{\infty}

\left(F_{i,j}\right)_{i=1}^{\infty}\subseteq\langle x_{i,j}\rangle_{i=1}^{\infty}

,

\mathbb{N}

\mid F_{i,j}\mid=n
, for each
i\in\mathbb{N}
,

2.

\sum_{t\in F_{i,j}}t\equiv 0\,\left(\mod\,n\right)
for each
i\in\mathbb{N}
,

3.

\left(F_{i_{1},j_{1}}+z_{i_{1},j_{1}}\right)+\left(F_{i_{2},j_{2}}+z_{i_{2},j_{2}}\right)+\cdots+\left(F_{i_{k},j_{k}}+z_{i_{k},j_{k}}\right)\subseteq B

.

Proof.

Let
p\in\beta\mathbb{N}
be a minimal idempotent such that
B\in p
.
As
B\in p,

we have
B^{*}=\left\{x\in B:-x+B\in p\right\}\in p
and so for
x\in B^{*}
,

-x+B^{*}\in p
. As
B^{*}\in p,
it is piecewise syndetic. So there
exists a finite set
G
such that
\cup_{t\in G}\left(-t+B^{*}\right)

is a thick set. Let
\mid G\mid=r
, hence for the finite set
E=\left\{\langle x_{i,1}\rangle_{i=1}^{\left(2n-1\right)r},\langle x_{i,2}\rangle_{i=1}^{\left(2n-1\right)r},,\ldots,\langle x_{i,m}\rangle_{i=1}^{\left(2n-1\right)r}\right\}

Hence for each

\left(2n-1\right)

\langle x_{i,j}\rangle_{i=1}^{\left(2n-1\right)r}

z_{1,j}=x+s

B^{*}

n

by

\mid F_{i,j}\mid=n

\sum_{t\in F_{i,j}}t\equiv 0\,\left(\mod\,n\right)

\left(F_{i_{1},j_{1}}+z_{i_{1},j_{1}}\right)+\left(F_{i_{2},j_{2}}+z_{i_{2},j_{2}}\right)+\cdots+\left(F_{i_{k},j_{k}}+z_{i_{k},j_{k}}\right)\subseteq B^{*}

Let
