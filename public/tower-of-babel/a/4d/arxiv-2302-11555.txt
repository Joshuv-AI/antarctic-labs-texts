On the Sausage Catastrophe in 4 4 Dimensions
Ji Hoon Chun

Abstract
The Sausage Catastrophe of J. Wills (1983) is the observation that in d = 3 d=3 and d = 4 d=4 , the densest packing of n n spheres in ℝ d \mathbb{R}^{d} is a sausage for small values of n n and jumps to a full-dimensional packing for large n n without passing through any intermediate dimensions. Let n d ∗ n_{d}^{*} be the smallest value of n n for which the densest packing of n n spheres in ℝ d \mathbb{R}^{d} is full-dimensional and N d ∗ N_{d}^{*} be the smallest value of N N for which the densest packing of N N spheres in ℝ d \mathbb{R}^{d} is full-dimensional for all N ≥ N d ∗ N\geq N_{d}^{*} . We extend the work of Gandini and Zucco (1992) to obtain new upper bounds of n 4 ∗ ≤ 338,196 n_{4}^{*}\leq 338,\!196 and N 4 ∗ ≤ 516,946 N_{4}^{*}\leq 516,\!946 . Some lengthy and repetitive components of the proof of the latter result were obtained using interval arithmetic.

The notations

,

,

,

, and

Definition 1.2
.

Let

\delta\left(C\right):=\limsup_{r\rightarrow\infty}\frac{\left|\left\{\mathbf{x}\in C\,\middle|\,\mathbf{x}+B^{d}\subseteq r\left[-1,1\right]^{d}\right\}\right|\kappa_{d}}{\vol\left(r\left[-1,1\right]^{d}\right)}.

For a given

A packing

is called a

of cardinality

and the

Let

(see

]

The situation in three and four dimensions is more complicated. The
