r {r} -hyperideals and generalizations of r {r} -hyperideals in Krasner hyperrings
Peng Xu, Melis Bolat, Elif Kaya, Serkan Onar, Bayram Ali Ersoy, Kostaq Hila

Abstract
. In this study, we examine some properties of r r -hyperideals in the commutative Krasner hyperrings. Some properties of p ​ r pr -hyperideals are also studied. The relation between prime hyperideals and r r -hyperideals is investigated. We show that the image and the inverse image of an r r -hyperideal is also an r r -hyperideal. We also introduce a generalization of r r -hyperideals and we prove some properties of them.

Theorem 3.1
.

Let
\Re

are equivalent:

a)

is an

-hyperideal.

b)

c)
N=(N:a),
for any

d)

where

is a hyperideal of

Proof.

(
a\Rightarrow b)
Let
N
be an
r
-hyperideal and
a
be a regular element.
Suppose that
x\in

(a\cdot\Re)\cap N.
Then
x\in a\cdot\Re
and
x\in N.

Thus,
x=a\cdot a^{{}^{\prime}},
for
a^{\prime}\in\Re.
Since
x=a\cdot a^{{}^{\prime}}\in N
,
ann(a)=0
and
N
is an
r
-hyperideal,
a^{\prime}\in N.

Hence,
a\cdot a^{\prime}\in a\cdot N
and
(a\cdot\Re)\cap N\subseteq a\cdot N.

Therefore, for every
a\in\Re,

a\cdot N\subseteq(a\cdot\Re)\cap N,

(a\cdot\Re)\cap N=a\cdot N.

(b\Rightarrow c)
We know that
N\subseteq(N:a),\
for every
a\in\Re.
Let

a
be regular,
a\notin N
and
x\in(N:a).
Hence
ann(a)=0
and
x\cdot a\in N.
From (b),
a\cdot x\in(a\cdot\Re)\cap N
and
a\cdot x\in a\cdot N.
This
implies that
a\cdot x=a\cdot y,
for
y\in N.
Since
ann(a)=0,
then
x=y\in N.
Hence,
x\in N.
Thus,
(N:a)\subseteq N.

(c\Rightarrow d)
Let
S
be the set of regular elements and
\varphi:\Re\rightarrow Q(\Re)
be natural homomorphism. We know that
N\subseteq\varphi^{-1}(M)=M^{c}
, for

. Suppose

\varphi^{-1}(M).
Since

From (c),

(d\Rightarrow a)
Let

and

We have

and since

is regular, then there exists

which is the

Thus

Hence,

-hyperideal.
∎
