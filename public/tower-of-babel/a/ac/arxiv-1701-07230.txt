Stefan Hoffelner

Abstract
We show that under the assumption of the existence of the canonical inner model with one Woodin cardinal M 1 M_{1} , there is a model of 𝖹𝖥𝖢 \mathsf{ZFC} in which NS ω 1 \hbox{NS}_{\omega_{1}} is ℵ 2 \aleph_{2} -saturated and Δ 1 \Delta_{1} -definable with ω 1 \omega_{1} as a parameter which answers a question of Sy-David Friedman and Liuzhen Wu. We also show that starting from an arbitrary universe with a Woodin cardinal, there is a model with NS ω 1 \hbox{NS}_{\omega_{1}} saturated and Δ 1 \Delta_{1} -definable with a ladder system C → \vec{C} and a full Suslin tree T T as parameters. Both results rely on a new coding technique whose presentation is the main goal of this article.

2.3

Definition 15

A

-sequence, or a ladder system, is a sequence

, such
that for every
\alpha

For three subsets

This enables us to define:

Definition 16
.

For a triple of subset of natural numbers

Next we want to define how suitable countable subsets of ordinals can be used to code reals. First we fix a ladder system

for the rest of this section.

-sequence of independent Suslin trees.
If we recall the proof of the main theorem, we see that the parameter
\vec{T}^{0}
can be replaced by
M_{1}|\omega_{1}
as the latter can compute such an independent
\omega
-sequence of Suslin trees.

The second parameter in the statement of the theorem, namely the ladder system
\vec{C}
can be replaced by
M_{1}|\omega_{1}
as well, for
M_{1}|\omega_{1}
can compute a canonical ladder system with the help of the
M_{1}
-wellorder of the reals. Thus, if we start with
M_{1}
as the ground model, we end up with a universe where
\hbox{NS}_{\omega_{1}}
is saturated and
\Delta_{1}(M_{1}|\omega_{1})
-definable, and one can use
(\ast)
to replace
the parameter
M_{1}|\omega_{1}
with just
\omega_{1}
.
We finally obtained a proof of the main theorem of this paper.

Theorem 65
.

Suppose that the canonical inner model with one Woodin cardinal,
M_{1}
exists. Then there is a model in which
\hbox{NS}_{\omega_{1}}
is saturated and
\Delta_{1}(\omega_{1})
-definable.
