Note on Klein-Nishina effect in strong-field QED: the case of nonlinear Compton scattering
U. Hernandez Acosta 1,2 , B. Kämpfer 1,3

Abstract
Suitably normalized differential probabilities of one-photon emission in external electromagnetic fields are compared to quantify the transit of nonlinear Compton scattering to linear Compton scattering, described by the Klein-Nishina formula, and to constant crossed field treatment. The known Klein-Nishina suppression at large energies is further enforced by increasing field intensity. In view of the Ritus-Narozhny conjecture, we demonstrate that different paths in the field intensity vs. energy plane towards large values of the quantum non-linearity parameter χ \chi facilitate significantly different asymptotic dependencies, both in the Klein-Nishina regime and the constant crossed field regime and in between.

Appendix D
\omega^{\prime}
and
\Theta^{\prime}
differential distributions

After the concise consideration of the invariant differential cross section
d\sigma^{\mathrm{IPA}}/du

and its dependencies, we turn to the differential cross section
d\sigma^{\mathrm{IPA}}/d\omega^{\prime}
,
where
\omega^{\prime}=\nu^{\prime}m
is the energy (scaled frequency) of the
out
photon.
One cannot directly employ
d\sigma^{\mathrm{IPA}}/du
,
Eq. (
7
), to get
d\sigma^{\mathrm{IPA}}/d\omega^{\prime}
,
instead one must use a differently weighted summation, in general,

\displaystyle\frac{d\sigma^{\mathrm{IPA}}}{d\omega^{\prime}}=-\frac{2\pi\alpha^{2}}{p\cdot k}\sum_{n=1}^{N\to\infty}\Theta(u_{n}-u(n,\nu^{\prime}))\frac{1}{m\kappa_{n}}\mathcal{F}_{n}(\hat{z}_{n}),

(20)

where
\hat{z}_{n}
refers to the definition Eq. (
9
) with
u\to u(n,\nu^{\prime}):=\frac{n\nu-\nu^{\prime}}{\kappa_{n}-n\nu+\nu^{\prime}}
,
see
Kampfer:2020cbx
;
Harvey:2009ry
for details.
The quantity
\kappa_{n}:=n\nu-\frac{1}{2}e^{\zeta}+\frac{1}{2}(1+a_{0}^{2})e^{-\zeta}

with electron rapidity
\zeta=\mathrm{Arcosh}E_{e^{-}}/m
can be positive or negative,
meaning read or blue shift. Here,
\nu:=\omega/m
.
