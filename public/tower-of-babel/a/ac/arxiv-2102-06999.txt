Comment on “The phase diagram of the multi-matrix model with ABAB-interaction from functional renormalization”
Carlos I. Pérez-Sánchez

Abstract
. Recently, [JHEP 20 131 (2020)] obtained (a similar, scaled version of) the ( a , b a,b )-phase diagram derived from the Kazakov–Zinn-Justin solution of the Hermitian two-matrix model with interactions − Tr ⁡ { a 4 ​ ( A 4 + B 4 ) + b 2 ​ A ​ B ​ A ​ B } , -\Tr\Big\{\frac{a}{4}(A^{4}+B^{4})+\frac{b}{2}ABAB\Big\}\,, starting from Functional Renormalization. We comment on something unexpected: the phase diagram of [JHEP 20 131 (2020)] is based on a β b \beta_{b} -function that does not have the one-loop structure of the Wetterich-Morris Equation. This raises the question of how to reproduce the phase diagram from a set of β \beta -functions that is, in its totality, consistent with Functional Renormalization. A non-minimalist, yet simple truncation that could lead to the phase diagram is provided. Additionally, we identify the ensemble for which the result of op. cit. would be entirely correct.
