Functional equations for supersingular abelian varieties over 𝐙 p 2 \mathbf{Z}_{p}^{2} -extensions
Cédric Dion

Abstract
. Let K K be an imaginary quadratic field and K ∞ K_{\infty} be the 𝐙 p 2 \mathbf{Z}_{p}^{2} -extension of K K . Answering a question of Ahmed and Lim, we show that the Pontryagin dual of the Selmer group over K ∞ K_{\infty} associated to a supersingular polarized abelian variety admits an algebraic functional equation. The proof uses the theory of Γ \Gamma -system developed by Lai, Longhi, Tan and Trihan. We also show the algebraic functional equation holds for Sprung’s chromatic Selmer groups of supersingular elliptic curves along K ∞ K_{\infty} . Résumé. Soit K K un corps quadratique imaginaire et K ∞ K_{\infty} l’unique 𝐙 p 2 \mathbf{Z}_{p}^{2} -extension de K K . Répondant à une question d’Ahmed et Lim, nous montrons que le dual de Pontryagin du groupe de Selmer sur K ∞ K_{\infty} associé à une variété abélienne supersingulière et polarisée admet une équation fonctionnelle algébrique. La preuve utilise la théorie des Γ \Gamma -systèmes développée par Lai, Longhi, Tan et Trihan. Nous démontrons aussi qu’une équation fonctionnelle algébrique tient pour les groupes de Selmer chromatiques de Sprung sur K ∞ K_{\infty} de courbes elliptiques supersingulières.

1.
Introduction

Fix an odd prime number
p
. Let
V
be a finite dimensional
\mathbf{Q}_{p}
-vector space with a continuous
{\mathrm{Gal}}(\overline{\mathbf{Q}_{p}}/\mathbf{Q}_{p})
-action. Suppose that
V
is ordinary at
p
in the sense of
[
11
]
and choose
T
a
{\mathrm{Gal}}(\overline{\mathbf{Q}_{p}}/\mathbf{Q}_{p})
-stable
\mathbf{Z}_{p}
-lattice inside
V
. Let
A=V/T
. Let
{\mathbf{Q}}_{\infty}
denote the cyclotomic
\mathbf{Z}_{p}
-extension of
{\mathbf{Q}}
with Galois group
\Gamma
. Write
S_{A}({\mathbf{Q}}_{\infty})
for the Greenberg Selmer group of
A
over
{\mathbf{Q}}_{\infty}
as defined in
[
11
, Page 98]
. Then,
S_{A}({\mathbf{Q}}_{\infty})
is a module over the Iwasawa algebra
\Lambda=\mathbf{Z}_{p}\llbracket\Gamma\rrbracket
. Let
\iota:\Lambda\to\Lambda
be the involution induced by sending
\sigma\in\Gamma
to
\sigma^{-1}
. For a
\Lambda
-module
M
we define the
\Lambda
-module
M^{\iota}
as the same set as
M
but with action given by
\lambda\cdot m=\iota(\lambda)\cdot m
for all
m\in M
and
\lambda\in\Lambda
. Under the assumption that
S_{A}({\mathbf{Q}}_{\infty})
is
\Lambda
-cotorsion (the Pontryagin dual
S_{A}({\mathbf{Q}}_{\infty})^{\vee}
of
S_{A}({\mathbf{Q}}_{\infty})
is a torsion
\Lambda
-module) and that the values of the associated
L
-functions
L_{V}(1)
and
L_{V^{\ast}}(1)
are critical values, Greenberg proved
[
11
, Theorem 2]
that
S_{A}({\mathbf{Q}}_{\infty})
and
S_{A^{\ast}}({\mathbf{Q}}_{\infty})^{\iota}
have the same characteristic ideal where
A^{\ast}=\mathrm{Hom}(V,\mathbf{Q}_{p}(1))/\mathrm{Hom}(T,\mathbf{Z}_{p}(1))
. He further makes the following conjecture
[
11
, Section 8 equation (66)]
:

Conjecture 1.1
.

One should expect that
S_{A}({\mathbf{Q}}_{\infty})^{\vee}\sim S_{A^{\ast}}({\mathbf{Q}}_{\infty})^{\vee,\iota}
when
V
is
p
-critical.

The symbol
\sim
denotes pseudo-isomorphisms, i.e.,
\Lambda
-homomorphisms with finite kernels and cokernels. When
A=E[p^{\infty}]
where
E
is an elliptic curve over
{\mathbf{Q}}
with good ordinary reduction or multiplicative reduction at
p
, conjecture
1.1
can be deduced from
[
11
, Theorem 2]
. Such a result should be seen as an algebraic analogue of the familiar functional equation
L_{p}(E,T)=(*)\cdot L_{p}(E,\frac{1}{1+T}-1)
for the
p
-adic
L
-function associated to
E
where
(*)
is an explicit factor.

Over the years, algebraic functional equations have been proven for Selmer groups attached to many kinds of Galois representations over various extensions. Let
K
be a number field unramified at
p
and let
T
be a
\mathbf{Z}_{p}
-lattice with a continuous
{\mathrm{Gal}}(\overline{{\mathbf{Q}}}/{\mathbf{Q}})
-action and let
K_{\infty}
be a
\mathbf{Z}_{p}^{d}
-extension of
K
. In the case where
T
is the
p
-adic Tate module of an elliptic curve over
{\mathbf{Q}}
with supersingular reduction at primes above
p
with
a_{p}=0
and
K_{\infty}={\mathbf{Q}}_{\infty}
is the cyclotomic
\mathbf{Z}_{p}
-extension of
{\mathbf{Q}}
, we can consider Kobayashi’s
\pm
-Selmer groups
{\mathrm{Sel}}_{\pm}(E/{\mathbf{Q}}_{\infty})
. In this setting, Kim
[
14
]
proved an analogue of conjecture
1.1
, namely that
{\mathrm{Sel}}_{\pm}(E/{\mathbf{Q}}_{\infty})^{\vee}\sim{\mathrm{Sel}}_{\pm}(E/{\mathbf{Q}}_{\infty})^{\vee,\iota}
by adapting Greenberg’s technique. When
K_{\infty}
is a
\mathbf{Z}_{p}^{d}
-extension with
d\geq 2
, Kim constructed multi-signed Selmer groups
{\mathrm{Sel}}_{\vec{s}}(E/K_{\infty})
generalizing Kobayashi’s plus and minus Selmer groups. In
[
1
]
, Ahmed and Lim proved that
{\mathrm{Sel}}_{\vec{s}}(E/K_{\infty})^{\vee}\sim{\mathrm{Sel}}_{\vec{s}}(E/K_{\infty})^{\vee,\iota}
. Their result is actually more general, they allow mixed reduction type at the primes above
p
.

For example of results when
T
does not come from an elliptic curve, one can look at the work of Lei and Ponsinet
[
18
]
. Suppose that
T
is crystalline at primes above
p
and satisfies some technical conditions. Büyükboduk and Lei
[
6
]
constructed multi-signed Selmer groups
{\mathrm{Sel}}_{I}(T/K_{\infty})
for
T
over the cyclotomic
\mathbf{Z}_{p}
-extension of
K
by making use of
p
-adic Hodge theory. The functional algebraic equation for
{\mathrm{Sel}}_{I}(T/K_{\infty})
was then proven in
[
18
]
.

Let now
A
be an abelian variety defined over a number field
K
with potentially ordinary reduction at every places of a finite ramification locus
S
. Let
K_{\infty}/K
be a
\mathbf{Z}_{p}^{d}
-extension and let
{\mathrm{Sel}}_{p^{\infty}}(A/K_{\infty})
be the
p^{\infty}
-Selmer group of
A
over
K_{\infty}
defined by means of flat cohomology
[
16

. Suppose that
{\mathrm{Sel}}_{p^{\infty}}(A/K_{\infty})^{\vee}
is torsion over the Iwasawa algebra
\mathbf{Z}_{p}\llbracket{\mathrm{Gal}}(K_{\infty}/K)\rrbracket
. Then, by
[
16
, Proposition 4.3.4]
,
{\mathrm{Sel}}_{p^{\infty}}(A/K_{\infty})^{\vee}\sim{\mathrm{Sel}}_{p^{\infty}}(A^{t}/K_{\infty})^{\vee,\iota}
where
A^{t}
is the dual abelian variety. This result is a byproduct of a much more general theory developed in
[
16
]
that can be used to prove functional equation for Iwasawa modules as long as they are part of a
\Gamma
-system
.
In the introduction of
[
1
]
, Ahmed and Lim ask whether or not the machinery of
\Gamma
-systems can be used to study non-ordinary motives with Hodge-Tate weights
0
and
1
over
\mathbf{Z}_{p}^{d}
-extensions of number fields in a manner similar to what they did for supersingular elliptic curves. In this paper, we give a partial answer to this question. Partial in the sense that we restrict ourselves to
\mathbf{Z}_{p}^{2}
-extension of quadratic imaginary fields, we do not treat the case of mixed reduction type and we only consider motives arising from abelian varieties satisfying additional conditions.

To state our result, we need to introduce more notation. Let
K
be an imaginary quadratic field where
(p)={\mathfrak{p}}{\mathfrak{p}}^{c}
splits. Let
K_{\infty}
be the unique
\mathbf{Z}_{p}^{2}
-extension of
K
with Galois group
\Gamma\cong\mathbf{Z}_{p}^{2}
. Let
A
be a polarized abelian variety defined over
K
of dimension
g
with supersingular reduction at both primes over
p
. Let
\mathbb{D}_{{\mathrm{cris}},{\mathfrak{p}}}(T)
(resp.
\mathbb{D}_{{\mathrm{cris}},{\mathfrak{p}}^{c}}(T)
) be the Dieudonné module of the
p
-adic Tate module of
A
viewed as a representation of
G_{K_{\mathfrak{p}}}
(resp.
G_{K_{{\mathfrak{p}}^{c}}}
). Let
\{v_{{\mathfrak{p}},1},\ldots,v_{{\mathfrak{p}},2g}\}
be a basis of
\mathbb{D}_{{\mathrm{cris}},{\mathfrak{p}}}(T)
such that
\{v_{{\mathfrak{p}},1},\ldots,v_{{\mathfrak{p}},g}\}
generates
\mathrm{Fil}^{0}\mathbb{D}_{{\mathrm{cris}},{\mathfrak{p}}}(T)
. A basis with this property is called Hodge-compatible. The matrix of the Frobenius operator
C_{\varphi,{\mathfrak{p}}}
acting on
\mathbb{D}_{{\mathrm{cris}},{\mathfrak{p}}}(T)
with respect to the chosen basis takes the form

C_{\varphi,{\mathfrak{p}}}=C_{\mathfrak{p}}\left[\begin{array}[]{c|c}I_{g}&0\\
\hline\cr 0&\frac{1}{p}I_{g}\end{array}\right]

for some
C_{\mathfrak{p}}\in\mathrm{GL}_{2g}(\mathbf{Z}_{p})
since the Hodge-Tate weights of
T
are
0
and
1
. Let
I
be the subset
\{1,\ldots,2g\}\subseteq\{1,\ldots,4g\}
and
I^{c}
its complement. We can construct Selmer groups
{\mathrm{Sel}}_{I}(A[p^{\infty}]/K_{\infty})
and
{\mathrm{Sel}}_{I^{c}}(A^{t}[p^{\infty}]/K_{\infty})
(see section
3.1
) depending on
I
and the choice of Hodge-compatible bases for both
\mathbb{D}_{{\mathrm{cris}},{\mathfrak{p}}}(T)
and
\mathbb{D}_{{\mathrm{cris}},{\mathfrak{p}}^{c}}(T)
. We remark that both bases may be chosen independently, as no relation between them is required in the construction of the Selmer groups.

Theorem A
.

Suppose that
{\mathrm{Sel}}_{I}(A[p^{\infty}]/K_{\infty})^{\vee}
is torsion over
\mathbf{Z}_{p}\llbracket\Gamma\rrbracket
. Suppose that the matrices
C_{\varphi,{\mathfrak{p}}}
and
C_{\varphi,{\mathfrak{p}}^{c}}
are block anti-diagonal and that
p
does not divide the class number of
K
. Then,

{\mathrm{Sel}}_{I}(A[p^{\infty}]/K_{\infty})^{\vee}\sim{\mathrm{Sel}}_{I^{c}}(A^{t}[p^{\infty}]/K_{\infty})^{\vee,\iota}.

Let us briefly explain the steps of the proof. Since we assume
{\mathrm{Sel}}_{I}(A[p^{\infty}]/K_{\infty})^{\vee}
to be torsion, there is a pseudo-isomorphism

{\mathrm{Sel}}_{I}(A[p^{\infty}]/K_{\infty})^{\vee}\to\bigoplus_{i=1}^{m}\mathbf{Z}_{p}\llbracket\Gamma\rrbracket/(\xi_{i}^{r_{i}})

where each
\xi_{i}
is irreducible and
r_{i}
are non-negative integers. Let

[{\mathrm{Sel}}_{I}(A[p^{\infty}]/K_{\infty})^{\vee}]\mathrel{\mathchoice{\vbox{\hbox{$\displaystyle:$}}}{\vbox{\hbox{$\textstyle:$}}}{\vbox{\hbox{$\scriptstyle:$}}}{\vbox{\hbox{$\scriptscriptstyle:$}}}{=}}\bigoplus_{i=1}^{m}\mathbf{Z}_{p}\llbracket\Gamma\rrbracket/(\xi_{i}^{r_{i}})

and define
[{\mathrm{Sel}}_{I}(A[p^{\infty}]/K_{\infty})^{\vee}]_{\text{si}}
as the sum over the
\xi_{i}
which are simple element (to be defined in section
4
) and
[{\mathrm{Sel}}_{I}(A[p^{\infty}]/K_{\infty})^{\vee}]_{\text{ns}}
as its complement. The proof of theorem A goes in two steps. We first show that
[{\mathrm{Sel}}_{I}(A[p^{\infty}]/K_{\infty})^{\vee}]_{\text{ns}}=[{\mathrm{Sel}}_{I^{c}}(A^{t}[p^{\infty}]/K_{\infty})^{\vee}]_{\text{ns}}^{\iota}
(theorem
5.14
) by using the machinery of
\Gamma
-system as in
[
16
]
and
[
1
]
. For the second step, namely showing that
[{\mathrm{Sel}}_{I}(A[p^{\infty}]/K_{\infty})^{\vee}]_{\text{si}}=[{\mathrm{Sel}}_{I^{c}}(A^{t}[p^{\infty}]/K_{\infty})^{\vee}]_{\text{si}}^{\iota}
(see corollary
5.14
), we can not use the arguments of
[
1
]
since unlike elliptic curves, abelian varieties are not necessarily self-dual. To circumvent this problem, the main difficulty will be to construct a
\mathbf{Z}_{p}\llbracket\Gamma\rrbracket
-homomorphism

{\mathrm{Sel}}_{I}(A[p^{\infty}]/K_{\infty})\to{\mathrm{Sel}}_{I^{c}}(A^{t}[p^{\infty}]/K_{\infty})

in order to put ourselves in a situation where we can apply
[
16
, Corollary 4.3.2]
. To do that, we need to analyze the behaviour of the basis of
\mathbb{D}_{{\mathrm{cris}},{\mathfrak{p}}}(T)
when we hit it with an isogeny
\alpha:A\to A^{t}
. This is done in section
5.2
.

Finally, we show the functional equation in the case when
A=E
is an elliptic curve over
K
with supersingular reduction at both primes above
p
and the Selmer groups considered are Sprung’s
\sharp/\flat
-
\sharp/\flat
Selmer groups. Since for elliptic curves
E^{t}=E
, we are able to mostly follow the argument in
[
1
]
. However, one still need to show that the local conditions defining those Selmer groups are their own orthogonal complement with respect to the Tate pairing in order to use the theory of
\Gamma
-systems. This is done via a comparison theorem relating multi-signed Coleman maps to
\sharp/\flat
-Coleman maps. We obtain:

Theorem B
.

Let
\star,\circ\in\{\sharp,\flat\}
and let
{\mathrm{Sel}}^{\star\circ}(E/K_{\infty})
be the chromatic Selmer group defined in section
6
. Then,

{\mathrm{Sel}}^{\star\circ}(E/K_{\infty})^{\vee}\sim{\mathrm{Sel}}^{\star\circ}(E/K_{\infty})^{\vee,\iota}.

Lemma 3.12

For any

\Lambda(\Gamma_{p})

is contained in a free

-module of rank

, with finite index. By the definition of the regulator map

, Definition 4.6]

\mathrm{Im}(\mathcal{L}_{T,{\mathfrak{q}}})=\mathrm{Im}(\mathcal{L}_{T,\mathbf{Q}_{p}})\widehat{\otimes}_{\mathbf{Z}_{p}}S_{F_{\infty}/\mathbf{Q}_{p}}.

By the decomposition of proposition

and of (

Let

\displaystyle((x_{n}),(y_{n}))

Lemma 3.13

Lemma 3.14
.

Let

and

Proof.

Again, we can follow the exact same arguments as in
[
18

. Let

-vector space inside

and

For

Proposition 6.8

The proof for
