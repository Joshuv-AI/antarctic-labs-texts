Area (or entropy) products for Newman-Unti-Tamburino class of black holes
Parthapratim Pradhan

Abstract
We compute area (or entropy) product formula for Newman-Unti-Tamburino (NUT) class of black holes. Specifically, we derive the area product of outer horizon and inner horizon ( ℋ ± {\mathcal{H}}^{\pm} ) for Taub-NUT, Euclidean Taub-NUT black hole, Reissner-Nordström–Taub-NUT black hole, Kerr-Taub-NUT black hole and Kerr-Newman-Taub-NUT black hole under the formalism developed very recently by Wu et al. [ 1 ] [PRD 100, 101501(R) (2019)]. The formalism is that a generic four dimensional Taub-NUT spacetime should be described completely in terms of three or four different types of thermodynamic hairs. They are defined as the Komar mass ( M = m M=m ), the angular momentum ( J n = m ​ n J_{n}=m\,n ), the gravitomagnetic charge ( N = n N=n ), the dual (magnetic) mass ( M ~ = n ) (\tilde{M}=n) . After incorporating this formalism, we show that the area (or entropy) product of both the horizons for NUT class of black holes are mass-independent . Consequently, the area product of ℋ ± {\mathcal{H}}^{\pm} for these black holes are universal . Which was previously known in the literature that the area product of said black holes are mass-dependent . Finally, we can say that this universality is solely due to the presence of new conserved charges J N = M ​ N J_{N}=M\,N which is closely analogue to the Kerr like angular momentum J = a ​ M J=a\,M .

Area product formula of Taub-NUT BH

First, we consider the Lorentzian Taub-NUT BH
[

\displaystyle ds^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-{\cal B}(r)\,\left(dt+2n\,\cos\theta\,d\phi\right)^{2}+\frac{dr^{2}}{{\cal B}(r)}+\left(r^{2}+n^{2}\right)\left(d\theta^{2}+\sin^{2}\theta d\phi^{2}\right)~,

(3)

where the function
{\cal B}(r)

\displaystyle{\cal B}(r)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{r^{2}+n^{2}}\left[r^{2}-n^{2}-2mr\right]

(4)

Under new formalism

for NUT class of BHs, the global

\displaystyle m

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle M~\mbox{(Komar mass)}

\displaystyle n

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle N~\mbox{(gravitomagnetic charge)}

\displaystyle m\,n

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle J_{n}~\mbox{(angular momentum)}~.

(5)

Taking cognizance of Eq. (
5

can be re-written as

\displaystyle ds^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-{\cal B}(r)\,\left(dt+2N\,\cos\theta d\phi\right)^{2}+\frac{dr^{2}}{{\cal B}(r)}+\left(r^{2}+N^{2}\right)\left(d\theta^{2}+\sin^{2}\theta d\phi^{2}\right)~,

(6)

where the function

is given by

\displaystyle{\cal B}(r)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{r^{2}+N^{2}}\left(r^{2}-N^{2}-2Mr\right)

\displaystyle r_{\pm}=M\pm\sqrt{M^{2}+N^{2}}\,\,\mbox{and}\,\,r_{+}>r_{-}

(8)

r_{+}

r_{-}

\displaystyle{\cal A}_{\pm}

\displaystyle\int^{2\pi}_{0}\int^{\pi}_{0}\sqrt{g_{\theta\theta}\,g_{\phi\phi}}{\mid}_{r=r_{\pm}}d\theta\,d\phi=8\pi\left[M^{2}+N^{2}\pm\sqrt{M^{4}+J_{n}^{2}}\right]~.

\displaystyle{\cal A}_{+}{\cal A}_{-}

\displaystyle(8\pi)^{2}\left(J_{n}^{2}+N^{4}\right)~.

J_{n}=m\,n=M\,N=J_{N}

Except this new input the area product of NUT class of BHs never be universal.

The BH temperature of both the horizons are computed via surface gravity on

T_{\pm}=\frac{\kappa_{\pm}}{2\pi}=\frac{{\cal B}^{\prime}(r_{\pm})}{4\pi\left(r_{\pm}^{2}+N^{2}\right)}=\frac{r_{\pm}-M}{2\pi\left(r_{\pm}^{2}+N^{2}\right)}=\frac{1}{4\pi\,r_{\pm}},

Area product formula of Reissner-Nordström–Taub-NUT BH

\displaystyle P

\displaystyle\equiv

\displaystyle a\,\sin^{2}\theta-2N\,\cos\theta~.

\displaystyle r_{\pm}

\displaystyle M\pm\sqrt{M^{2}-a^{2}+N^{2}}

\displaystyle\int^{2\pi}_{0}\int^{\pi}_{0}\sqrt{g_{\theta\theta}\,g_{\phi\phi}}\,d\theta\,d\phi

\displaystyle 8\pi\left[(M^{2}+N^{2})\pm\sqrt{M^{4}+J_{N}^{2}-J^{2}}\right]~.

\displaystyle{\cal A}_{+}{\cal A}_{-}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(8\pi)^{2}\left(J^{2}+J_{N}^{2}+N^{4}\right)~.

(28)

Conclusions

It has recently been stated that a generic 4D Taub-NUT BH could be completely
described in terms of three or four different types of thermodynamic hairs. They must be defined
as the Komar mass (
M=m
), the angular momentum (
J_{n}=m\,n
), the gravitomagnetic charge (
N=n
),
the dual (magnetic) mass
(\tilde{M}=n)
. Under these circumstances, we
demonstrated that the area (or entropy) product
of multi-horizons for NUT class of BH is
mass-independent
.
That means the mass-independent features of NUT class of BHs are interestingly
shown to be
universal
which was earlier known as “mass-dependent”
characteristics in the literature.

We have explicitly checked here for Lorentzian Taub-NUT BH, Euclidean Taub-NUT BH, Reissner-Nordström–Taub-NUT BH,
Kerr-Taub-NUT BH and Kerr-Newman-Taub-NUT BH. In each case, we showed that the area product of
both physical horizons is universal in nature after the inclusion of angular momentum like parameter

J_{N}=M\,N
with the generic parameters
M
and
N
as a global conserved charges. This universal feature
of area (or entropy) products that we have derived for NUT class of BHs further provide any hints
for potential explanation of the microscopic structure of the BHs in four dimensions.
