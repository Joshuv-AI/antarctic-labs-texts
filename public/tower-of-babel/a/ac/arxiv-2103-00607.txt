Graphs with constant adjacency dimension
Mohsen Jannesari

Abstract
For a set W W of vertices and a vertex v v in a graph G G , the k k -vector r 2 ​ ( v | W ) = ( a G ​ ( v , w 1 ) , … , a G ​ ( v , w k ) ) r_{2}(v|W)=(a_{G}(v,w_{1}),\ldots,a_{G}(v,w_{k})) is the adjacency representation of v v with respect to W W , where W = { w 1 , … , w k } W=\{w_{1},\ldots,w_{k}\} and a G ​ ( x , y ) a_{G}(x,y) is the minimum of 2 2 and the distance between the vertices x x and y y . The set W W is an adjacency resolving set for G G if distinct vertices of G G have distinct adjacency representations with respect to W W . The minimum cardinality of an adjacency resolving set for G G is its adjacency dimension . It is clear that the adjacency dimension of an n n -vertex graph G G is between 1 1 and n − 1 n-1 . The graphs with adjacency dimension 1 1 and n − 1 n-1 are known. All graphs with adjacency dimension 2 2 , and all n n -vertex graphs with adjacency dimension n − 2 n-2 are studied in this paper. In terms of the diameter and order of G G , a sharp upper bound is found for adjacency dimension of G G . Also, a sharp lower bound for adjacency dimension of G G is obtained in terms of order of G G . Using these two bounds, all graphs with adjacency dimension 2, and all n n -vertex graphs with adjacency dimension n − 2 n-2 are characterized.

Lemma 3.1

Let

Proof.

Let

The members of

Therefore,

In the next example we construct a family of graphs with

Example 3.2

Let

, we introduce a construction for graphs with adjacency dimension

Hence,
