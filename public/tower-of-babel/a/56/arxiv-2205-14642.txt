On an approximation of average cost per unit time impulse control of Markov processes
Łukasz Stettner

Abstract
In this paper we consider impulse control of continuous time Markov processes with average cost per unit time functional. This problem is approximated using impulse control problems stopped at the first exit time from increasing sequence of open sets. We find solution to Bellman equation corresponding to the original problem and show that stopped impulse control problems approximate optimal value of the cost functional.

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}_{x}\left\{\int_{0}^{\tau_{\cal{O}}\wedge\tau}e^{-\alpha s}\left(f(X_{s})+\beta(w_{\alpha}^{\beta}(X_{s})-G(X_{s}))^{+}\right)ds+1_{\tau<\tau_{\cal{O}}}e^{-\alpha\tau}w_{\alpha}^{\beta}(X_{\tau})+\right.

(68)

\displaystyle\left.1_{\tau_{\cal{O}}\leq\tau}e^{-\alpha\tau_{\cal{O}}}H(X_{\tau_{\cal{O}}})\right\}.

By Theorem 4.3 we know that

\tau_{\alpha}^{*}=\inf\left\{s\geq 0:w_{\alpha}(X_{s})\geq G(X_{s})\ or\ X_{s}\notin\cal{O}\right\}

(69)

is an optimal time for
w_{\alpha}
. Let
\tau^{\epsilon}=\inf\left\{s\geq 0:w(X_{s})+\epsilon\geq G(X_{s})\ or\ X_{s}\notin\cal{O}\right\}
. When

we have that whenever

which means that
\tau^{\epsilon}\leq\tau_{\alpha}^{*}
. Consequently from (

we obtain

w_{\alpha}^{\beta}(x)=\mathbb{E}_{x}\left\{\int_{0}^{\tau_{\cal{O}}\wedge\tau^{\epsilon}}e^{-\alpha s}f(X_{s})ds+1_{\tau^{\epsilon}<\tau_{\cal{O}}}e^{-\alpha\tau^{\epsilon}}w_{\alpha}^{\beta}(X_{\tau^{\epsilon}})+1_{\tau_{\cal{O}}\leq\tau^{\epsilon}}e^{-\alpha\tau_{\cal{O}}}H(X_{\tau_{\cal{O}}})\right\}.

Letting in the last equation

we finally obtain

w(x)=\mathbb{E}_{x}\left\{\int_{0}^{\tau_{\cal{O}}\wedge\tau^{\epsilon}}f(X_{s})ds+1_{\tau^{\epsilon}<\tau_{\cal{O}}}w(X_{\tau^{\epsilon}})+1_{\tau_{\cal{O}}\leq\tau^{\epsilon}}H(X_{\tau_{\cal{O}}})\right\}.

It remains now to notice that

(X_{s})

w(x)=\mathbb{E}_{x}\left\{\int_{0}^{\tau_{\cal{O}}\wedge\tau^{*}}f(X_{s})ds+1_{\tau^{*}<\tau_{\cal{O}}}w(X_{\tau^{*}})+1_{\tau_{\cal{O}}\leq\tau^{*}}H(X_{\tau_{\cal{O}}})\right\}.

(72)

completing this way the proof of optimality of

.

We finally formulate an ergodic stopping result

Theorem 14
.

Under (
(A.1)
) let
f,w\in C(E)
and
\mu(f)>0
. Then

z(x):=\inf_{\tau}\mathbb{E}_{x}\left\{\int_{0}^{\tau}f(X_{s})ds+w(X_{\tau})\right\}

is continuous and an optimal stopping time
\tau^{*}
is of the form

\tau^{*}=\inf\left\{s\geq 0:z(X_{s})\geq w(X_{s})\right\}.

(74)

Furthermore for any stopping time
\sigma

z(x)=\inf_{\tau}\mathbb{E}_{x}\left\{\int_{0}^{\tau\wedge\sigma}f(X_{s})ds+1_{\tau<\sigma}w(X_{\tau})+1_{\sigma\leq\tau}z(X_{\sigma})\right\}.

Proof.

By (
(A.1)
) we can write

Since

(77)

To show continuity of

(78)

where

is a family of stopping times taking values in
\left\{0,\delta,\ldots,n\delta,\ldots\right\}
.
Consider Bellman equation corresponding to (
78

r
such that

r(x)=(\mu(f)\delta+P_{\delta}r(x))\wedge g(x):=T_{\delta}r(x).

(79)

Notice that by (

) function

Consider two sequences
(\underline{r}_{n})

of functions approximating solutions to (

\underline{r}_{1}(x)=T_{\delta}(0)(x)
,
\underline{r}_{2}(x)=T_{\delta}\underline{r}_{1}(x)

\bar{r}_{2}(x)=T_{\delta}\bar{r}_{1}(x)

\underline{r}_{n+1}(x)=T_{\delta}\underline{r}_{n}(x)

\bar{r}_{n+1}(x)=T_{\delta}\bar{r}_{n}(x)
for positive integer

\underline{r}_{1}(x)\geq 0

\bar{r}_{1}(x)\leq g(x)

\bar{r}_{n+1}(x)\leq T_{\delta}\bar{r}_{n}(x)

\underline{r}_{n}(x)

is a nonincreasing sequence of continuous functions. Let
\underline{r}(x)=\lim_{n\to\infty}\underline{r}_{n}(x)
and
\bar{r}(x)=\lim_{n\to\infty}\bar{r}_{n}(x)

\underline{r}
is lower semicontinuous while
\bar{r}
is upper semicontinuous. Moreover one can show that

\underline{r}_{n}(x)=\inf_{\tau\in{\cal T}_{\delta}}\mathbb{E}_{x}\left\{\mu(f)(\tau\wedge n\delta)+1_{\tau<n\delta}g(X_{\tau})\right\}

(80)

and

\bar{r}_{n}(x)=\inf_{\tau\in{\cal T}_{\delta}}\mathbb{E}_{x}\left\{\mu(f)(\tau\wedge n\delta)+g(X_{\tau\wedge n\delta})\right\}.

(81)

In both problems (
80

81

\tau

so that letting

, by Fatou lemma we have

(83)

Therefore choosing

and since by (

\mathbb{P}_{x}\left\{\tau\geq n\delta\right\}\leq{g(x)\over\mu(f)n\delta}

and therefore also of

\underline{r}(x)=\bar{r}(x)
is continuous and coincides with

.
Let now
\tau
a
\varepsilon
-optimal stopping time for

\displaystyle 0\leq v_{\delta}(x)-v(x)\leq\varepsilon+\mu(f)\delta+

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}_{x}\left\{w(X_{\tau_{\delta}})-w(X_{\tau})+q(X_{\tau})-q(X_{\tau_{\delta}})\right\}\leq
