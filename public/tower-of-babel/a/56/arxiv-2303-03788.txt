Abstract
. We prove that for λ = ℶ ω \lambda=\beth_{\omega} or just λ \lambda strong limit singular of cofinality ℵ 0 \aleph_{0} , if there is a universal member in the class 𝐊 λ lf \mathbf{K}^{\rm lf}_{\lambda} of locally finite groups of cardinality λ \lambda , then there is a canonical one (parallel to special models for elementary classes, which is the replacement of universal homogeneous ones and saturated ones in cardinals λ = λ < λ \lambda=\lambda^{<\lambda} ). For this, we rely on the existence of enough indecomposable such groups, as proved in ` ​ ` . {{`}{`}}. Density of indecomposable locally finite groups”. We also more generally deal with the existence of universal members in general classes for such cardinals.
