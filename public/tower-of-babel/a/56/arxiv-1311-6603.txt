Matching and geometry of 2-step nilpotent Lie groups
Babak Hasanzadeh

Abstract
In this paper we study contact structure on 2-step nilpotent, Heisenberg type Lie groups. We decompose this Lie groups to center and orthogonal complement, then investigate properties of both orthogonal Lie subgroups. Finally, we provide a connection between matchings in groups and field extensions and 2-step nilpotent Lie groups. Keywords :Contact structure; 2-step nilpotent; Lie group; nonsingular; skew adjoint; semi-symmetric.
