On the derived category of a toric stack bundle
Qian Chao, Jiun-Cheng Chen, Hsian-Hua Tseng

Abstract
. We establish some properties of the derived category of torus-equivariant coherent sheaves on a split toric stack bundle. Our main result is a semi-orthogonal decomposition of such a category.

\mathbb{C}

Spanning classes

This Section contains some results on spanning classes. First, we have the following Lemma from
[
3

.

Lemma 3.1
.

The collection of
\mathbb{T}
-equivariant line bundles

(3.1)

\{\mathcal{L}\in Pic_{\mathbb{T}}(\mathfrak{X})\}

is a spanning class of
D^{b}_{\mathbb{T}}(\mathfrak{X})
.

Proof.

By the definition of spanning class, we need to show that for an object
M
of
D^{b}_{\mathbb{T}}(\mathfrak{X})
, if

(3.2)

{\mathrm{H}om}_{D^{b}_{\mathbb{T}}(\mathfrak{X})}(\mathcal{L},M[r])=0

for all
\mathcal{L}\in Pic_{\mathbb{T}}(\mathfrak{X})
,
r\in\mathbb{Z}
, then
M=0
.

We assume that
M
is a nonzero coherent sheaf. By
2
2

2

The argument in the proof of
[
2
, Theorem 4.6]
extends to the
\mathbb{T}
-equivariant setting.

[
2
, Theorem 4.6]
, there is a surjection

(3.3)

\bigoplus_{i}\mathcal{L}_{i}\to M.

where
\{\mathcal{L}_{i}\}
are
\mathbb{T}
-equivariant line bundles on
\mathfrak{X}
. So for some
i
, there is a nonzero section
\mathcal{O}_{\mathfrak{X}}\to M\otimes\mathcal{L}_{i}^{-1}
. But by assumption
0={\mathrm{H}om}_{D^{b}_{\mathbb{T}}(\mathfrak{X})}(\mathcal{L}_{i},M[0])={\mathrm{H}om}_{D^{b}_{\mathbb{T}}(\mathfrak{X})}(\mathcal{O}_{\mathfrak{X}},M\otimes\mathcal{L}^{-1})

The following result gives a spanning class for split toric stack bundles. Recall that
L
is an ample line bundle on
B
.

Proposition 3.2
.

The collection

(3.4)

\{\phi^{*}L^{\otimes i}\otimes\tilde{\mathcal{L}}\,\,|\,\,i\in\mathbb{Z},\mathcal{L}\in Pic_{\mathbb{T}}(\mathfrak{X})\}

is a spanning class of
D^{b}_{\mathbb{T}}(\mathfrak{E})
.

Proof.

By the definition of spanning class, we need to show that for an object
M
of
D^{b}_{\mathbb{T}}(\mathfrak{E})
, if

(3.5)

{\mathrm{H}om}_{D^{b}_{\mathbb{T}}(\mathfrak{E})}(\phi^{*}L^{\otimes i}\otimes\tilde{\mathcal{L}},M[r])=0

for all
i\in\mathbb{Z}
,
\mathcal{L}\in Pic_{\mathbb{T}}(\mathfrak{X})
,
r\in\mathbb{Z}
, then
M=0
.

We may assume that
M
is a coherent sheaf. We have

{\mathrm{H}om}_{D^{b}_{\mathbb{T}}(\mathfrak{E})}(\phi^{*}L^{\otimes i}\otimes\tilde{\mathcal{L}},M[r])={\mathrm{H}om}_{D^{b}_{\mathbb{T}}(\mathfrak{E})}(\mathcal{O}_{\mathfrak{E}},\phi^{*}L^{\otimes-i}\otimes\tilde{\mathcal{L}}^{-1}\otimes M[r]).

By assumption, we have

{\mathrm{H}om}_{D^{b}_{\mathbb{T}}(\mathfrak{E})}(\mathcal{O}_{\mathfrak{E}},\tilde{\mathcal{L}}^{-1}\otimes M[r])=0

for all
\mathcal{L}\in Pic_{\mathbb{T}}(\mathfrak{X})
. Since
D^{b}_{\mathbb{T}}(\mathfrak{X})
is generated by
\mathcal{L}\in Pic_{\mathbb{T}}(\mathfrak{X})

[
2
, Theorem 4.6]
, it follows that
M|_{\mathfrak{X}_{b_{0}}}=0
for any
b_{0}\in B
. If in addition
\phi_{*}(M)\neq 0
, then together this implies
M=0
.

Suppose
\phi_{*}(M)\neq 0
. Let
k\in\mathbb{Z}
be sufficiently large so that
\phi_{*}(M)\otimes L^{\otimes k}\neq 0
. We calculate

(3.6)

\phi_{*}(M\otimes\phi^{*}L^{\otimes k})|_{b_{0}}=(\phi_{*}(M)\otimes L^{\otimes k})|_{b_{0}}=H^{0}(X_{b_{0}},M|_{\mathfrak{X}_{b_{0}}})=0,

which is a contradiction unless

.
∎

4.
Semi-orthogonal decomposition

Theorem 4.1
.

The following is a semi-orthogonal decomposition of
D^{b}_{\mathbb{T}}(\mathfrak{E})
:

(4.1)

(\phi^{*}D^{b}(B)\otimes\tilde{E}_{a})_{a\in\mathbb{Z}^{m}}.

Proof.

Since (
3.5
) is a spanning class of
D^{b}_{\mathbb{T}}(\mathfrak{E})
, the subcategory of
D^{b}_{\mathbb{T}}(\mathfrak{E})
generated by (
4.1
) is the whole
D^{b}_{\mathbb{T}}(\mathfrak{E})
.

It remains to show semi-orthogonality, namely

(4.2)

{\mathrm{H}om}_{D^{b}_{\mathbb{T}}(\mathfrak{E})}(\phi^{*}M_{1}\otimes\tilde{E}_{a},\phi^{*}M_{2}\otimes\tilde{E}_{b})=0

for
M_{1},M_{2}
coherent sheaves on
B
and
a>b
.

Since
B
is smooth and projective, every coherent sheaf of
B
can be resolved by a (finite) complex of direct sums of line bundles.
It suffices to consider the case where
M_{i}=L_{i},i=1,2
are line bundles on
B
. It is also clear that we can assume
M_{1}
is the trivial line bundle. Thus we need to compute

{\mathrm{H}om}_{D^{b}_{\mathbb{T}}(\mathfrak{E})}(\tilde{E}_{a},\phi^{*}L_{2}\otimes\tilde{E}_{b}).

We adapt the main idea from
[
3
]
.
We use
(I,\overline{p})
(resp.
(J,\overline{q})
) to label the object
\tilde{E}_{a}
(resp.
\tilde{E}_{b}
) as mentioned in the previous section. It is more convenient for our proof.

As
\mathfrak{E}\to B
is Zariski locally trivial, we have a Zariski-open cover
\{B_{i}\}
of
B
such that

\mathfrak{E}|_{B_{i}}
is isomorphic to
[(U_{\Sigma}\times B_{i})/G]
; note that
G
acts trivially on
B_{i}
.
Let
j_{\Sigma\times B_{i}}:U_{\Sigma_{i}}\times B_{i}\to\mathbb{C}^{n}\times B_{i}
be the natural open embedding.
For each
\tilde{\mathcal{O}}_{I,\overline{p}}
, set
\tilde{\mathcal{O}}^{U_{\Sigma}\times B_{i}}_{I,\overline{p}}:=j^{*}_{\Sigma\times B_{i}}(\tilde{\mathcal{O}}_{I,\overline{p}}).

We first verify that

(4.3)

{\mathrm{H}om}_{[U_{\Sigma}\times B_{i}/G]}(\tilde{\mathcal{O}}^{U_{\Sigma}\times B_{i}}_{I,\overline{p}},\phi^{*}L_{2}\otimes\tilde{\mathcal{O}}^{U_{\Sigma}\times B_{i}}_{J,\overline{q}})^{\mathbb{T}}=0

when

(I,\overline{p})>(J,\overline{q})
. Note again that
(I,\overline{p})>(J,\overline{q})
means that

\overline{p}>\overline{q}
lexicographically or
\overline{p}=\overline{q}
and
|I|>|J|
.

We resolve the (shifted) sheaves
\tilde{\mathcal{O}}^{\Sigma\times B_{i}}_{I,\overline{p}}
and

\tilde{\mathcal{O}}^{\Sigma\times B_{i}}_{J,\overline{q}}
by Koszul complexes as in
[
3
]
.
Note that the argument for the
[(\mathbb{C}^{n}\times B_{i})/(\mathbb{C}^{*})^{n}]
case does not directly apply to the general
(\mathbb{C}^{*})^{n}
-invariant
U_{\Sigma}\times B_{i}
. However, we do still have a local-to-global spectral sequence as we can cover
U_{\Sigma_{i}}\times B_{i}
by
(\mathbb{C}^{*})^{n}
-invariant affine open sets, e.g.
\mathbb{C}^{n}
with some unions of coordinate hyper-planes.

Consider the
E_{2}
terms of the following local-to-global spectral sequence as in
[
3
, Proposition 2.4]
:

(4.4)

E_{2}^{r,s}=\bigoplus_{I\setminus(I\cap J)\subset S\subset I,|S|=s}H^{r-s}([(U_{\Sigma}\times B_{i})/(\mathbb{C}^{*})^{n}],\phi^{*}L_{2}\otimes\tilde{\mathcal{O}}^{U_{\Sigma}\times B_{i}}_{I\cup J}(\overline{p}-\overline{q}+\overline{\chi_{S}})).

Following
[
3
, Lemma 2.3]
, the only possible non-zero terms are when
r=s
. This implies that the spectral sequence degenerates at the
E_{2}
page. This implies

(4.5)

\begin{split}&{\mathrm{E}xt}^{r}_{U_{\Sigma}\times B_{i}/(\mathbb{C}^{*})^{n}}(\mathcal{O}^{U_{\Sigma}\times B_{i}}_{I,\overline{p}},\phi^{*}L_{2}\otimes\mathcal{O}^{U_{\Sigma}\times B_{i}}_{J,\overline{q}})\\
&\\
=&\bigoplus_{I\setminus(I\cap J)\subset S\subset I}H^{r-|S|}([(U_{\Sigma}\times B_{i})/(\mathbb{C}^{*})^{n}],\phi^{*}L_{2}\otimes\tilde{\mathcal{O}}^{U_{\Sigma}\times B_{i}}_{I\cup J}(\overline{p}-\overline{q}+\overline{\chi_{S}})).\end{split}

Since we assume
(I,\overline{p})>(J,\overline{q})
, every term on the right-hand side is actually zero.
This shows the left-hand side is zero. Letting
r=0
, we obtain

{\mathrm{H}om}_{[U_{\Sigma}/(\mathbb{C}^{*})^{n}\times B_{i}]}(\tilde{\mathcal{O}}^{U_{\Sigma}\times B_{i}}_{I,\overline{p}},\phi^{*}L_{2}\otimes\tilde{\mathcal{O}}^{U_{\Sigma}\times B_{i}}_{J,\overline{q}})=0.

The last step is a standard argument. Consider the composition of the global section functor
\Gamma
and
\mathcal{H}om(\phi^{*}M_{1}\otimes\tilde{E}_{a},-)
. The Grothendieck spectral sequence for their composition has
E^{p,q}_{2}=H^{p}(\mathfrak{E},\mathcal{E}xt^{q}(\phi^{*}M_{1}\otimes\tilde{E}_{a},\phi^{*}M_{2}\otimes\tilde{E}_{b}))
. We have shown
E^{p,q}_{2}
=0. This shows (
4.2
) is valid, as desired. ∎

5.
Fourier-Mukai equivalence

Let
\mathfrak{X}_{\pm}
be two smooth toric stacks related by a single crepant wall-crossing, as in

\mathbf{FM}=(\tilde{\pi}_{+})_{*}(\tilde{\pi}_{-})^{*}:D^{b}_{\mathbb{T}}(\mathfrak{E}_{-})\to D^{b}_{\mathbb{T}}(\mathfrak{E}_{+})

Proof.

We calculate

(5.2)

\mathbf{FM}(\phi_{-}^{*}M\otimes\tilde{\mathcal{L}})=\phi_{+}^{*}M\otimes\mathbf{FM}(\tilde{\mathcal{L}}).

Let

be an open cover of

To get the global case that

to the
\mathbb{T}
-equivariant setting.
Denote the right adjoint of
\mathbf{FM}

\mathbf{G}

It sufficies to show that

We first show

for
all
a\in D^{b}_{\mathbb{T}}(\mathfrak{E}_{-})
.
For each

, we have a distinguished triangle

In order to show

, it suffices to show that

.

Consider the collection of spanning class in Proposition

be any element in this class. Although

Taking

. Since

, it follows that

.

.
