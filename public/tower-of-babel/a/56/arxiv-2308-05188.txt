On Mahler’s inequality and small integral generators of totally complex number fields
Murray Child, Martin Widmer

Abstract
. We improve Mahler’s lower bound for the Mahler measure in terms of the discriminant and degree for a specific class of polynomials: complex monic polynomials of degree d ≥ 2 \mathchar 29028\mathchar 12821\mathchar 28722 such that all roots with modulus greater than some fixed value r ≥ 1 \mathchar 29042\mathchar 12821\mathchar 28721 occur in equal modulus pairs. We improve Mahler’s exponent 1 2 ​ d − 2 {{\mathchar 28721\over\mathchar 28722\mathchar 29028\mathchar 8704\mathchar 28722}} on the discriminant to 1 2 ​ d − 3 {{\mathchar 28721\over\mathchar 28722\mathchar 29028\mathchar 8704\mathchar 28723}} . Moreover, we show that this value is sharp, even when restricting to minimal polynomials of integral generators of a fixed not totally real number field. An immediate consequence of this new lower bound is an improved lower bound for integral generators of number fields, generalising a simple observation of Ruppert from imaginary quadratic to totally complex number fields of arbitrary degree.
