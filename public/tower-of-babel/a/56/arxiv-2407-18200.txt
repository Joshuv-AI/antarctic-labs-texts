Abstract
This paper investigates federated learning (FL) in a multi-hop communication setup, such as in constellations with inter-satellite links. In this setup, part of the FL clients are responsible for forwarding other client’s results to the parameter server. Instead of using conventional routing, the communication efficiency can be improved significantly by using in-network model aggregation at each intermediate hop, known as incremental aggregation (IA) . Prior works [ 1 ] have indicated diminishing gains for IA under gradient sparsification. Here we study this issue and propose several novel correlated sparsification methods for IA. Numerical results show that, for some of these algorithms, the full potential of IA is still available under sparsification without impairing convergence. We demonstrate a 15 × \times improvement in communication efficiency over conventional routing and a 11 × \times improvement over state-of-the-art (SoA) sparse IA.

II-A

Sparse Incremental Aggregation

Observe from
1

\sum_{k=1}^{K}D_{k}\bm{g}_{k}^{t}

\bm{\gamma}_{k}^{t}=\sum_{i=k}^{K}D_{i}\bm{g}_{i}^{t}

This is then transmitted to the PS via the next hop. Applying this approach in each node
k

and forwards it to node

Our goal is to combine

[
8
,
9
]

The primary motivation for sparsification and

2

3

4

4

k

\|\bm{\gamma}_{k}^{t}\|_{0}=\|\bm{\Gamma}_{k}^{t}\|_{0}+\|\bm{\Lambda}_{k}^{t}\|_{0}

\|\bm{\Gamma}_{k}^{t}\|_{0}
is deterministically
Q_{G}

\|\bm{\Lambda}_{k}^{t}\|_{0}

\bm{\Lambda}_{k}^{t}

\omega

\lceil\log_{2}d\rceil

\bm{\gamma}_{k}^{t}

\bm{\Gamma}_{k}^{t}

\omega

4

\sum\nolimits_{k=1}^{K}\left(\omega\mathds{E}\left[\|\bm{\Gamma}_{k}^{t}\|_{0}\right]+\left(\omega+\lceil\log_{2}d\rceil\right)\mathds{E}\left[\|\bm{\Lambda}_{k}^{t}\|_{0}\right]\right)\\
=K\omega Q_{G}+\left(\omega+\lceil\log_{2}d\rceil\right)\sum\nolimits_{k=1}^{K}\mathds{E}\left[\|\bm{\Lambda}_{k}^{t}\|_{0}\right].

(7)

Figure

As expected, the communication cost for SIA and RE-SIA is significantly higher than for the other algorithms. The TC-SIA approach shows the same quadratic growth, but at a much reduced rate. TCS
transmitting
Q_{G}
fewer indices.

VII
Conclusions

We have considered efficient communication for
FL
in
MH

SoA
approach for collecting intermediate
FL
results in such a system is
IA

[
5
,
7
]
, which shows diminishing returns under gradient sparsification
[
1
]
. We have developed four novel algorithms to address this issue and evaluated their performance numerically. These results show a distinct advantage of
Algorithm

3
