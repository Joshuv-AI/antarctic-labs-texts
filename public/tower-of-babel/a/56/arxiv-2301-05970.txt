Hawking radiation and entropy of a BTZ black hole with minimum length
M. A. Anacleto, F. A. Brito, E. Passos, José L. Paulino, A. T. N. Silva, J. Spinelly

Abstract
In this paper we consider a BTZ black hole with minimum length which has been introduced through the probability density of the ground state of the hydrogen atom. We analyzed the effect of the minimum length by calculating the thermodynamic quantities such as temperature and entropy and verified the stability of the black hole by computing the specific heat capacity.

II
BTZ Black Hole with minimum length

In this section we incorporate minimum length corrections for the BTZ metric by modifying the mass density of the black hole through the probability density of hydrogen in two dimensions.
Thus, we will investigate Hawking radiation, entropy and the stability of the BTZ black hole.

II.1
Mass Density - Probability Density

In our model, based on the work
Miao:2016ipk
, we consider the probability density of the ground state of the hydrogen atom in two dimensions
Yang1991
as the mass density for the BTZ black hole

\displaystyle\rho(r)=\frac{M}{a^{2}\pi}\exp\left(\frac{-4r}{a}\right),

(1)

where
M
is the total mass of the BTZ black hole and
a
is the minimum length parameter in the model.

In order to solve Einstein’s equations, in addition to knowing the energy-momentum tensor, we must write the metric in a way that suits the symmetry of the distribution. Thus, in the present case, the line element describing the three-dimensional spacetime must be of the type.

\displaystyle ds^{2}=-A(r)dt^{2}+B(r)dr^{2}+r^{2}d\phi^{2}.

(2)

Therefore, taking this into account and using the fact that due to the conservation law
\nabla_{\nu}T^{\mu\nu}=0
,
the energy-momentum tensor is given by
T^{\mu}_{\nu}=diag(-\rho,p_{r},p_{\perp})
, where radial and tangential pressure are described by

{p}_{r}=-{\rho}
and
{p}_{\bot}=-{\rho}rd{\rho}/dr
, respectively, follows from Einstein’s equations that

\frac{1}{2B^{2}r}\frac{dB}{dr}-\Lambda=2\pi\rho,

(3)

and

\frac{1}{2BAr}\frac{dA}{dr}+\Lambda=-2\pi\rho\ .

(4)

By solving this system of equations, we obtain

\displaystyle A=\frac{\alpha}{B},

(5)

and

\displaystyle B=\frac{1}{-2\mathcal{M}-\Lambda r^{2}+\beta}=\frac{1}{f(r)}\ ,

(6)

where
\alpha
and
\beta
are constants and
\mathcal{M}
is the spread mass distribution contained in a region of radius
r
, given by

\displaystyle\mathcal{M}(r)=\int^{r}_{0}\rho(r)2\pi rdr=M\left[1-\frac{(4r+a)}{a}\exp\left(\frac{-4r}{a}\right)\right].

(7)

Now, making
\alpha=1
,
\beta=M
and
\Lambda=-1/l^{2}
, we obtain

\displaystyle ds^{2}=-f(r)dt^{2}+f^{-1}(r)dr^{2}+r^{2}d\varphi^{2},

(8)

where

\displaystyle f(r)=-2\mathcal{M}(r)+\frac{r^{2}}{l^{2}}+M=-M+\frac{r^{2}}{l^{2}}+\left[\frac{(8Mr+2a)}{a}\exp\left(\frac{-4r}{a}\right)\right].

(9)

Note that in the limit
r/a\rightarrow\infty
, we recover the nonrotating BTZ black hole metric.

The event horizon of the BTZ black hole is obtained when we make
f(r)=0
.
Thus, we have

\displaystyle r_{H}^{2}=r_{h}^{2}\left[1-2\left(\frac{l^{2}}{r^{2}_{h}}+\frac{4r_{H}}{a}\right)e^{-4r_{H}/a}\right].

(10)

where
r_{h}=\sqrt{Ml^{2}}
is the event horizon for the nonrotating BTZ black hole in the absence of the minimum length.
Now, solving the above expression by iteration and keeping terms up to first order in
e^{-4r_{h}/a}
, we obtain

\displaystyle r_{H}=r_{h}\left[1-2\left(\frac{l^{2}}{r^{2}_{h}}+\frac{4r_{H}}{a}\right)e^{-4r_{H}/a}\right]^{1/2}=r_{h}\left[1-\left(\frac{l^{2}}{r^{2}_{h}}+\frac{4r_{h}}{a}\right)e^{-4r_{h}/a}\right]+{\cal O}\left(e^{-4r_{h}/a}\right)^{2}.

(11)

The Hawking temperature of the nonrotating BTZ black hole with minimum length is given by

\displaystyle T_{H}=\frac{1}{4\pi}\frac{\partial f}{\partial r}{\Big|}_{r_{H}}=\frac{r_{H}}{2\pi l^{2}}\left[1-\frac{16Ml^{2}}{a^{2}}e^{-4r_{H}/a}\right].

(12)

In terms of
r_{h}
we can write the Hawking temperature as follows

\displaystyle T_{H}=\frac{r_{h}}{2\pi l^{2}}-\frac{1}{2\pi l^{2}}\left[\frac{l^{2}}{r_{h}}+\frac{4r^{2}_{h}}{a}+\frac{16r^{3}_{h}}{a^{2}}\right]e^{-4r_{h}/a}+{\cal O}\left(e^{-4r_{h}/a}\right)^{2}.

(13)

Note that the first term is the Hawking temperature of the BTZ black hole (
T_{h}=r_{h}/2\pi l^{2}
) and the second term is the correction due to the minimum length.

From the condition
f(r)=0
, we can obtain the mass of the BTZ black hole which is given by

\displaystyle M=\frac{r^{2}_{H}}{l^{2}}\left[1-2\left(\frac{l^{2}}{r^{2}_{h}}+\frac{4r_{H}}{a}\right)\exp\left(-\frac{4r_{H}}{a}\right)\right]^{-1},

(14)

or in terms of
r_{h}
, we have

\displaystyle M=\frac{r^{2}_{h}}{l^{2}}+\left(e^{-4r_{h}/a}\right)^{2}.

(15)

Next, in order to compute the entropy of a BTZ black hole with minimum length we consider the following equation:

\displaystyle S=\int\frac{dM}{T_{H}}=\int\frac{1}{T_{H}}\frac{\partial M}{\partial r_{h}}dr_{h},

(16)

By replacing (
13
) and (
15
) in (
16
), we find

\displaystyle S

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int\frac{2\pi l^{2}}{r_{h}}\left[1+\left(\frac{l^{2}}{r^{2}_{h}}+\frac{4r_{h}}{a}+\frac{16r^{2}_{h}}{a^{2}}\right)e^{-4r_{h}/a}+\cdots\right]\left(\frac{2r_{h}}{l^{2}}\right)dr_{h},

(17)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 4\pi r_{h}-\frac{4l^{2}}{a}{\mbox{E}i}\left(\frac{-4r_{h}}{a}\right)-\left[\frac{3a}{4}+\frac{l^{2}}{r_{h}}+3r_{h}+\frac{4r^{2}_{h}}{a}\right]e^{-4r_{h}/a}+\cdots.

(18)

Now we calculate the correction of the specific heat capacity which is given by

\displaystyle C

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{\partial M}{\partial T}=\frac{\partial M}{\partial r_{h}}\left(\frac{\partial T}{\partial r_{h}}\right)^{-1},

(19)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 4\pi r_{h}\left[1+\left(R_{2}-\frac{4R_{1}}{a}\right)e^{-4r_{h}/a}\right],

(20)

where

\displaystyle R_{1}=\frac{l^{2}}{r_{h}}+\frac{4r^{2}_{h}}{a}+\frac{16r^{3}_{h}}{a^{2}},

(21)

\displaystyle R_{2}=-\frac{l^{2}}{r^{2}_{h}}+\frac{8r_{h}}{a}+\frac{48r^{2}_{h}}{a^{2}}.

(22)

Note that, considering the dominant term in (
19

a\ll 1
, we have

\displaystyle C\approx 4\pi r_{h}\left[1-\frac{64r^{3}_{h}}{a^{3}}e^{-4r_{h}/a}\right].

(23)

Hence the condition that cancels the specific heat capacity is given by

\displaystyle\frac{r^{3}_{h}}{e^{4r_{h}/a}}=\left(\frac{a}{4}\right)^{3}.

(24)

Now, we apply the above condition to the Hawking temperature expression (
13

\displaystyle T_{max}\approx\frac{1}{2\pi l^{2}}\left(r_{h}-\frac{a}{4}\right)=T_{H}-\frac{a}{8\pi l^{2}},

(25)

which is the maximum temperature of the remnant of the black hole.
On the other hand, for
r_{h}/a\ll 1
, from condition (
24
), we find
r_{h}=3a/8
.
Thus, the result in (
25
) becomes

\displaystyle{T_{max}\approx\frac{a}{16\pi l^{2}},}

(26)

with a minimum mass given by

\displaystyle M_{min}=\frac{9a^{2}}{64l^{2}}.

(27)

II.2
Smeared Distribution on a Collapsed Shell

Here, based on the work
Miao:2016ipk
, we introduce the effect of a minimum length considering a mass distribution given by

\displaystyle\rho(r)=\frac{100Ma^{2}_{0}r^{2}}{2\pi(a^{2}_{0}+5r^{2})^{3}},

(37)

where
M
is the total mass of the BTZ black hole and
a_{0}
being the minimum length parameter.
The smeared mass distribution function can be determined by applying the following relationship:

\displaystyle\mathcal{M}_{0}(r)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int^{r}_{0}\rho(r)2\pi rdr=\frac{25Ma^{2}_{0}r^{4}}{(a^{3}_{0}+5a_{0}r^{2})^{2}},

(38)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle M-\frac{2Ma^{2}_{0}}{5r^{2}}+{\cal O}(a^{4}_{0}).

(39)

Next, we incorporate the minimum length effect into the metric at the BTZ black hole by applying the above mass to construct the line element given by

\displaystyle ds^{2}=-f(r)dt^{2}+f^{-1}(r)dr^{2}+r^{2}{(d\varphi+N^{\varphi}dt)}^{2},

(40)

where

\displaystyle f(r)=-M+\frac{r^{2}}{l^{2}}+\frac{J^{2}}{4r^{2}}+\frac{4Ma^{2}_{0}}{5r^{2}},

(41)

and

\displaystyle N^{\varphi}=-\frac{J}{2r^{2}}.

(42)

Note that the parameter
a_{0}
simulates the effect of an “effective angular momentum” on the metric.

Let us now consider the BTZ black hole in the nonrotating regime (
J=0
). Thus, the metric becomes

\displaystyle ds^{2}=-f(r)dt^{2}+f^{-1}(r)dr^{2}+r^{2}d\varphi^{2},

(43)

where

\displaystyle f(r)=-M+\frac{r^{2}}{l^{2}}+\frac{4Ma^{2}_{0}}{5r^{2}}.

(44)

By making
f(r)=0
, we find the event horizons given by

\displaystyle r^{2}_{+}=\frac{r^{2}_{h}}{2}\left[1+\sqrt{1-\frac{4\tilde{a}^{2}}{r^{2}_{h}}}\right]=r^{2}_{h}\left(1-\frac{\tilde{a}^{2}}{r^{2}_{h}}+\cdots\right),\quad\Rightarrow\quad r_{+}=r_{h}-\frac{\tilde{a}^{2}}{2r_{h}}+\cdots,

(45)

\displaystyle r^{2}_{-}=\frac{r^{2}_{h}}{2}\left[1-\sqrt{1-\frac{4\tilde{a}^{2}}{r^{2}_{h}}}\right]=\tilde{a}^{2}+\cdots,\quad\Rightarrow\quad r_{-}=\tilde{a}+\cdots,

(46)

where
r_{h}=\sqrt{Ml^{2}}
and
\tilde{a}^{2}=4a^{2}_{0}/5
.

In this case, for the Hawking temperature we obtain

\displaystyle T_{H}=\frac{f^{\prime}(r_{+})}{4\pi}=\frac{2r_{+}}{4\pi l^{2}}\left(1-\frac{r^{2}_{h}\tilde{a}^{2}}{r^{4}_{+}}\right),

(47)

which in terms of
r_{h}
becomes

\displaystyle T_{H}=\frac{r_{h}}{2\pi l^{2}}-\frac{3\tilde{a}^{2}}{4\pi l^{2}r_{h}}+\cdots.

(48)

The mass of the BTZ black hole is

M=\frac{r^{2}_{+}}{l^{2}}\left(1+\frac{r^{2}_{h}\tilde{a}^{2}}{r^{4}_{+}}\right)=\frac{r^{2}_{h}}{l^{2}}+{\cal O}(\tilde{a}^{4}).

(49)

For entropy we obtain

\displaystyle S

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int\frac{1}{T_{H}}\frac{\partial M}{\partial r_{h}}dr_{h}=4\pi\int\frac{l^{2}}{r_{h}}\left[1+\frac{3\tilde{a}^{2}}{2r^{2}_{h}}+\cdots\right]\frac{r_{h}}{l^{2}}dr_{h}

(50)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 4\pi r_{h}-\frac{6\pi\tilde{a}^{2}}{r_{h}}+\cdots.

(51)

In this case the correction for the specific heat capacity is

\displaystyle C

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{\partial M}{\partial r_{+}}\left(\frac{\partial T}{\partial r_{+}}\right)^{-1},

(52)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 4\pi r_{+}\left(1-\frac{\tilde{a}^{2}}{r^{2}_{+}}+\cdots\right)

(53)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 4\pi r_{h}\left(1-\frac{3\tilde{a}^{2}}{2r^{2}_{h}}+\cdots\right).

(54)

For
r_{h}=\sqrt{3}\tilde{a}/\sqrt{2}
the specific heat vanishes. Thus, we have a minimum radius given by

\displaystyle r_{min}=\sqrt{l^{2}M_{min}}=\frac{\sqrt{3}\tilde{a}}{\sqrt{2}}=\frac{\sqrt{6}a_{0}}{\sqrt{5}},

(55)

and a minimum mass given by

\displaystyle M_{min}=\frac{3\tilde{a}^{2}}{2l^{2}}=\frac{6a_{0}^{2}}{5l^{2}}.

(56)

In this case, we find
T_{rem}=0
for the temperature of the black hole remnant.

II.3
Lorentzian-Type Distribution

In this section, based on the work
Anacleto:2020efy
, we will analyze the effect of minimum length considering the following mass distribution:

\displaystyle\rho(r)=\frac{16Mb}{\pi(4r+b)^{3}},

(57)

where
b
is the minimum length parameter.
Now we determine the mass distribution given by

\displaystyle\mathcal{M}_{b}(r)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int^{r}_{0}\rho(r)2\pi rdr=\frac{16Mr^{2}}{(b+4r)^{2}},

(58)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle M-\frac{Mb}{2r}+\frac{3Mb^{2}}{16r^{2}}+{\cal O}(b^{3}).

(59)

From there, we can get the line element as follows:

\displaystyle ds^{2}=-f(r)dt^{2}+f^{-1}(r)dr^{2}+r^{2}d\varphi^{2},

(60)

where

\displaystyle f(r)=-M+\frac{Mb}{2r}+\frac{r^{2}}{l^{2}}-\frac{3Mb^{2}}{16r^{2}}.

(61)

Note that by applying the above distribution new terms arise in the metric function (
61
).
The second term,
{Mb}/{2r}
, is a term of type Schwarzschild and the last term,
{3Mb^{2}}/{16r^{2}}
, corresponds to an effective angular momentum term.
In our calculations we will consider the situation where
J=0
, so the effect of angular momentum will be simulated by the last term of the metric function above.

The horizons are determined by solving the quartic equation

\displaystyle r^{4}-l^{2}Mr^{2}-\frac{3l^{2}Mb^{2}}{16}+\frac{l^{2}Mb}{2}r=0.

(62)

The equation can be rewritten as follows
Anacleto:2020efy
;
Visser:2012wu
:

\displaystyle(r^{2}-r^{2}_{+})(r^{2}-r^{2}_{-})+\frac{l^{2}Mb}{2}r=0,

(63)

where
r_{+}
and
r_{-}
are given by

\displaystyle r^{2}_{\pm}=\frac{l^{2}M}{2}\left(1\pm\sqrt{1+\frac{3b^{2}}{4l^{2}M}}\right)=\begin{cases}r^{2}_{+}=r^{2}_{h}+\dfrac{3b^{2}}{16}+\cdots,\\
\\
r^{2}_{-}=-\dfrac{3b^{2}}{16}+\cdots.\end{cases}

(64)

In order to solve the equation (
63
) perturbatively, we write in the form

\displaystyle r^{2}=r^{2}_{\pm}-\frac{r^{2}_{h}br}{2(r^{2}-r^{2}_{\mp})},

(65)

where
r_{h}=\sqrt{l^{2}M}
.
Hence, in the first approximation to the outer radius we have

\displaystyle\tilde{r}^{2}_{+}\approx r^{2}_{+}+\frac{r^{2}_{h}br_{+}}{2(r^{2}_{-}-r^{2}_{+})},

(66)

or

\displaystyle\tilde{r}_{+}=r_{+}+\frac{r^{2}_{h}b}{4(r^{2}_{-}-r^{2}_{+})}+\cdots,

(67)

and for the radius of the inner horizon we find

\displaystyle\tilde{r}^{2}_{-}\approx r^{2}_{-}-\frac{r^{2}_{h}br_{-}}{2(r^{2}_{-}-r^{2}_{+})},

(68)

and so for
\tilde{r}_{-}
, we have

\displaystyle\tilde{r}_{-}=r_{-}-\frac{r^{2}_{h}b}{4(r^{2}_{-}-r^{2}_{+})}+\cdots.

(69)

The mass of the BTZ black hole is given by

\displaystyle M

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{\tilde{r}_{+}^{2}}{l^{2}}+\frac{\tilde{r}_{+}b}{2l^{2}}-\frac{3b^{2}}{16l^{2}}+\cdots.

(70)

For the Hawking temperature we have

\displaystyle{\tilde{T}}_{H}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{\tilde{r}_{+}}{2\pi l^{2}}\left(1-\frac{r^{2}_{h}b}{4\tilde{r}^{3}_{+}}+\frac{3r^{2}_{h}b^{2}}{16\tilde{r}^{4}_{+}}\right),

(71)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\dfrac{1}{2\pi l^{2}}\left(r_{h}-\dfrac{b}{2}-\dfrac{b^{2}r_{h}}{8}+\dfrac{9b^{2}}{32r_{h}}\right).

(72)

III
Conclusions

In this paper, we have explored the effect of minimum length on BTZ black hole metrics by analyzing its thermodynamic properties.
We have implemented the minimum length contribution through a smeared mass distribution. In addition, we also introduce the minimum length through a collapsed shell mass density and a Lorentzian-like distribution with new terms being generated for the metric function.
With regard to the thermodynamic variables, we verified that the behavior of temperature, entropy and specific heat capacity indicates that there exists a minimum mass for the black hole. We have found that the modified black hole is stable, and the specific heat capacity vanishes for
r_{h}=r_{min}
, which signalizes the presence
of remnants as the final stage of BTZ solution with minimum length. Moreover, in the situation where the mass is null, we have found non-zero results for thermodynamic quantities due to the minimum length effect.
