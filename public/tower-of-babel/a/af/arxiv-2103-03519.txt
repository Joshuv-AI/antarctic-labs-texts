The dc-Josephson effect with more than four superconducting leads Thanks: Institute of Engineering Univ. Grenoble Alpes
Régis Mélin

Abstract
By definition, the p p -terminal dc-Josephson current is sensitive to the superconducting phase variables of p p terminals. In the paper, we establish protocol for direct detection of the p p -terminal dc-Josephson effect with p ≥ 3 p\geq 3 in a device containing N N superconducting leads S 1 , S 2 , … , S N S_{1},\,S_{2},\,...,\,S_{N} having the phase variables φ 1 , φ 2 , … , φ N \varphi_{1},\,\varphi_{2},\,...,\,\varphi_{N} . The calculated signal χ ( N ) {\chi}^{(N)} can be probed in microwave experiments, and it corresponds to the higher-order nonlocal inverse inductance obtained from differentiating the current I 1 I_{1} through S 1 S_{1} with respect to the remaining N − 2 N-2 independent phase differences φ 2 − φ N , φ 3 − φ N , … , φ N − 1 − φ N \varphi_{2}-\varphi_{N},\,\varphi_{3}-\varphi_{N},\,...,\varphi_{N-1}-\varphi_{N} . We find that the values p ≤ N − 2 p\leq N-2 do not contribute to χ ( N ) {\chi}^{(N)} , and that χ ( N ) ≠ 0 {\chi}^{(N)}\neq 0 implies evidence for the p = N − 1 p=N-1 or the p = N p=N -terminal dc-Josephson currents. For N = 4 N=4 superconducting leads, we demonstrate that χ ( 4 ) ≠ 0 {\chi}^{(4)}\neq 0 implies evidence for the p = 3 p=3 or p = 4 p=4 dc-Josephson effect, irrespective of the p = 2 p=2 -terminal dc-Josephson current. Thus, we provide a way to demonstrate the dc-Josephson effect with more than three terminals ( i.e. with p ≥ 3 p\geq 3 ) in a device containing more than four superconducting leads ( i.e. with N ≥ 4 N\geq 4 ). The predicted χ ( 4 ) {\chi}^{(4)} is “yes or no” answer to the p ≥ 3 p\geq 3 dc-Josephson effect, i.e. for N = 4 N=4 , nonvanishingly small χ ( 4 ) ≠ 0 \chi^{(4)}\neq 0 implies the p = 3 p=3 or p = 4 p=4 -terminal dc-Josephson effect and vanishingly small χ ( 4 ) = 0 \chi^{(4)}=0 implies absence of the p = 3 p=3 and p = 4 p=4 -terminal dc-Josephson effect. The paper can be viewed as generalizing the recently considered φ \varphi -junctions in Andreev molecules to arbitrary number N N of the superconducting leads, and it relies on basic properties of the dc-Josephson effect that are not directly related to nontrivial topology and Weyl point singularities.

In this subsection, we evaluate the Fourier transform of the ABS
