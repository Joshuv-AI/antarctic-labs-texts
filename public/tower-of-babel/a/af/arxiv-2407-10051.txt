The weight distributions of some linear
codes derived from Kloosterman sums

Abstract




Abstract:

Linear codes with few weights have applications in data storage systems, secret
sharing schemes, and authentication codes. In this paper, some kinds of
p
-ary linear
codes with few weights are constructed by use of the given defining set, where
p
is a prime. Their weight distributions are determined based on Kloosterman sums over finite fields. In addition, some linear codes we given is minimal.

Keywords
: linear code, weight distribution, Kloosterman sum, minimal.

MSC
: 94A24; 94B05

1
Introduction

Let
q=p^{m}
and
F_{q}
denote the finite field with
q
elements, where
p
is a prime and
m
is a positive integer. An
[n,k,d]
linear code
C
over
F_{p}
is a
k
-dimensional subspace of
F^{n}_{p}
with minimum (Hamming) distance
d
. Let
A_{i}
denote the number of codewords with Hamming weight
i
in a code
C
of length
n
. The weight enumerator of
C
is defined by
1+A_{1}z+A_{2}z^{2}+\cdots+A_{n}z^{n}
. Accordingly, the sequence
(1,A_{1},\ldots,A_{n})
is called the weight distribution of
C
. A code
C
is said to be a
t
-weight code if the number of nonzero
A_{i}
in
(1,A_{1},\ldots,A_{n})
is equal to
t
. Linear codes with few weights are important in many aspects, like as secret sharing schemes
[
2
]
and association schemes
[
3
]
.
As an important class of linear codes, minimal linear codes have applications in secret sharing schemes
[
12
]
. Therefore, the constructions of linear codes with few weights
and minimal linear codes have attracted much attention in coding theory.

In
[
4
]
, Ding et al. proposed a new construction of linear codes by use of defining sets and obtained a class of two-weight or three-weight linear codes. Let
D_{0}=\{d_{1},d_{2},\ldots,d_{n}\}\subseteq F_{q}^{*}
. A linear code from defining set
D_{0}
is defined by

C_{D_{0}}=\{c(a)=(Tr_{1}^{m}(ax))_{x\in D_{0}}:a\in F_{q}\},

where
Tr^{m}_{1}(\cdot)
is the absolute trace function from
F_{q}
to
F_{p}
.
Later, many linear codes with few weights have been constructed from different
D_{0}
and functions like trace function
[
6
,
14
]
, bent function
[
15
]
and plateaued function
[
13
]
for example.

In
[
9
]
, Li et al. extended Ding’s construction and defined a
p
-ary linear code by

C_{D}=\{c(a,b)=(Tr_{1}^{m}(ax+by))_{(x,y)\in D}:a,b\in F_{q}\}

(1.1)

where
D=\{(x_{1},y_{1}),(x_{2},y_{2}),\ldots,(x_{n},y_{n})\}\subseteq(F_{q}\times F_{q})\setminus\{(0,0)\}
. They employed exponential sums to investigate the weight distribution of the linear code
C_{D}
, where
D=\{(x,y)\in(F_{q}\times F_{q})\setminus\{(0,0)\}:Tr(x^{N_{1}}+y^{N_{2}})=0\}
and
N_{1},N_{2}\in\{1,2,p^{\frac{m}{2}+1}\}
. In
[
8
]
, Jian et al. extended the results in
[
9
]
and considered the linear codes
C_{D}
where
D=\{(x,y)\in(F_{q}\times F_{q})\setminus\{(0,0)\}:Tr(x^{N_{1}}+y^{p^{u}+1})=0\}
, where
N_{1}\in\{1,2\}
,
p
is odd prime and
u
is a positive integer satisfied some conditions. In this paper, we obtain the complete weight distribution of a class of linear code
{C}_{D}
of the form (
1.1
) with a given defining set
D
by using Kloosterman sums. And we obtain two classes of linear codes which are minimal constructed by deleting codewords from the original linear code
{C}_{D}
.

The rest of this paper is organized as follows. In Section 2, we review some basic notations and basic results needed in the subsequent sections. In Section 3, we mainly present the complete weight distributions of three kinds of linear codes. In Section 4, we give the explicit proofs of the main results in Section 3. In Section 5, we conclude this paper.

Preliminaries

Let
q=p^{m}
and
m=2t
, where
p
is a prime,
m,t
are positive integers and
t>2
.
Throughout this paper, we adopt the following notations unless otherwise stated:

•

Tr_{l_{1}}^{l_{2}}(x)
is the trace function from the finite field
F_{p^{l_{2}}}
to
F_{p^{l_{1}}}
for any positive integer
l_{1}
and
l_{2}
, where
l_{1}
divides
l_{2}
.

•

\xi_{p}=e^{2\pi\sqrt{-1}/p}
is primitive
p
-th root of complex unity.

•

\chi
is the canonical additive character on
F_{q}
, i.e.,
\chi(x)=\xi_{p}^{Tr_{1}^{m}(x)}
for any
x\in F_{q}
.

•

For any positive integer
l
,
\chi_{l}
is the canonical additive character on
F_{p^{l}}
.

Let
\alpha
be a generator of
F^{*}_{q}
,
\beta=\alpha^{p^{t}-1},
and then
order_{q}(\beta)=p^{t}+1
, where
order_{q}(\cdot)
represents the order of any element in the multiplication group
F^{*}_{q}
.
Let
\Delta=\left<\beta\right>=\{\alpha^{j(p^{t}-1)}:0\leq j\leq p^{t}\}
,
\Gamma=\{\alpha^{j}:0\leq j\leq p^{t}\}
, and then
\Delta=\{v^{p^{t}-1}:v\in\Gamma\}.

It is easy to know that
order_{q}(\alpha^{p^{t}+1})=p^{t}-1.
Let
F^{*}_{p^{t}}=\left<\alpha^{p^{t}+1}\right>
be a subgroup of
F_{q}^{*}
and then
\Gamma
is the set of the coset representative elements of
F_{p^{t}}^{*}
of
F_{q}^{*}
. Thus, for each
x\in F^{*}_{q}
, it has a unique decomposition as
x=uv,
where
u\in F^{*}_{p^{t}}
and
v\in\Gamma
.

With the above notations, we will give some useful results for later use.
The weights of the linear codes presented in this paper will be determined by the Kloosterman sums over finite field. For any
a\in F_{p^{l}}
, the Kloosterman sum of canonical additive character
\chi_{l}

over
F_{p^{l}}
at the point
a
is defined by

K_{l}(a)=\sum\limits_{x\in F_{p^{l}}^{*}}\chi_{l}(ax+\frac{1}{x}),

where
l
is a positive integer.
The following results about Kloosterman sum of canonical additive character will be useful in the sequel.

Lemma 2.1
.

[
11
, Theorem 5.45]
For any positive integer
l
and
a\in F^{*}_{p^{l}},

|K_{l}(a)|\leq 2\sqrt{p^{l}}
.

Combining the proof of
[
10
, Theorem 3]
about Kloosterman sum with
[
7
, Theorem 1]
, we summarize the result as follows with our notations in this paper and give a brief proof.

Lemma 2.2
.

For any
a\in F_{p^{t}}^{*}\subseteq F_{q}^{*},
we have

\sum\limits_{x\in\Delta}\chi(ax)=-K_{t}(a^{2}).

Proof.

By the proof in
[
10
, Theorem 3]
, for
p=2
, we have

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{x\in\Delta}\chi(ax)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-1-\sum\limits_{\gamma\in a^{-1}F_{2^{t}}\setminus a^{-1}F_{2}}\chi_{t}(a^{2}\gamma+\frac{1}{\gamma})-\chi_{t}(a^{2}a^{-1}+\frac{1}{a^{-1}})+1

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-K_{t}(a^{2}).

By Theorem 1 in
[
7
]
and
a^{p^{t}-1}=1
as
a\in F_{p^{t}}^{*},
for
p>2
, we have

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{x\in\Delta}\chi(ax)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{j=0}^{p^{t}}\chi(a\alpha^{j(p^{t}-1)})=\sum\limits_{j=0}^{p^{t}}\chi_{t}(Tr_{t}^{m}(a\alpha^{j(p^{t}-1)}))

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\sum\limits_{x\in F_{p^{t}}^{*}}\chi_{t}(x+\frac{a^{p^{t}+1}}{x})=-\sum\limits_{x\in F_{p^{t}}^{*}}\chi_{t}(a^{2}x+\frac{a^{p^{t}-1}}{x})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\sum\limits_{x\in F_{p^{t}}^{*}}\chi_{t}(a^{2}x+\frac{1}{x})=-K_{t}(a^{2}).

∎

Lemma 2.3
.

[
11
, Theorem 5.46]

For any
a\in F_{q}^{*},

K_{t}(a)=-\sum\limits_{i=0}^{\lfloor\frac{t}{2}\rfloor}(-1)^{t-i}\frac{t}{t-i}\binom{t-i}{i}p^{i}(K_{1}(a))^{t-2i}.

In addition, the known Lemmas
2.4
,
2.5
and
2.6
are important in the proofs of our main results.

Lemma 2.4
.

[
14
, Lemma 1]

For any given
a\in F^{*}_{q},
there is one and only one
v_{a}\in\Gamma
such that
Tr_{t}^{m}(av_{a})=0
.

Lemma 2.5
.

[
5
, p.259]

For an
[n,k,d]
code
C
over
F_{p}
with weight distribution
(1,A_{1},\ldots,A_{n}),
suppose that the weight distribution of its dual
code is
(1,A^{\bot}_{1},\ldots,A^{\bot}_{n}),
then the first two Pless power moments are

\sum\limits_{j=0}^{n}A_{j}=p^{k},

and

\sum\limits_{j=0}^{n}jA_{j}=p^{k-1}(pn-n-A^{\bot}_{1}).

Lemma 2.6
.

[
11
]

The orthogonal property of nontrivial additive character
\psi
over finite field
E
is given by

\sum\limits_{x\in E}\psi(zx)=\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}0,&z\not=0,\\
|E|,&z=0.\end{array}\right.

In this section, we will give proofs of Theorem

3.1

Lemma 4.1
.

Let

n=|\{(x,y)\in(F_{q}\times F_{q})\setminus\{(0,0)\}:Tr_{1}^{m}(x+y^{p^{t}-1})=0\}|,

then
n=p^{2m-1}-1.

Proof.

n
can be expressed in terms of character sums as

\displaystyle n

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{x,y\in F_{q}}\frac{1}{p}\sum\limits_{z\in F_{p}}\xi_{p}^{zTr_{1}^{m}(x+y^{p^{t}-1})}-1

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{q^{2}}{p}-1+\frac{1}{p}\sum\limits_{z\in F^{*}_{p}}\sum\limits_{x\in F_{q}}\chi(zx)\sum\limits_{y\in F_{q}}\chi(zy^{p^{t}-1})

\displaystyle{=}

\displaystyle p^{2m-1}-1.~\ {\rm(by~Lemma~\ref{character})}

∎

Lemma 4.2
.

Let

N(a,b)=|\{(x,y)\in(F_{q}\times F_{q})\setminus\{(0,0)\}:Tr_{1}^{m}(x+y^{p^{t}-1})=0,Tr_{1}^{m}(ax+by)=0\}|,

then

N(a,b)=\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}p^{2m-2}-1,&a\not\in F_{p}^{*},\\
p^{2m-2}-1+p^{m-2}(p-1)+p^{m-2}(p^{t}-1)S,&a\in F_{p}^{*},b=0,\\
p^{2m-2}-1+(p^{m-2}+p^{m+t-2})(p-1)-p^{m-2}S,&a\in F_{p}^{*},Tr_{1}^{m}(v_{b}^{p^{t}-1})=0,\\
p^{2m-2}-1+p^{m-2}(p-1)-p^{m+t-2}-p^{m-2}S,&a\in F_{p}^{*},Tr_{1}^{m}(v_{b}^{p^{t}-1})\not=0,\end{array}\right.

where

is the only one element in
\Gamma
such that

due to
Lemma
2.4
.

Proof.

N(a,b)
can be expressed in terms of character sums as

\displaystyle N(a,b)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{p^{2}}\sum\limits_{x,y\in F_{q}}\sum\limits_{z_{1}\in F_{p}}\xi_{p}^{z_{1}Tr_{1}^{m}(x+y^{p^{t}-1})}\sum\limits_{z_{2}\in F_{p}}\xi_{p}^{z_{2}Tr_{1}^{m}(ax+by)}-1

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{p^{2}}\sum\limits_{x,y\in F_{q}}(1+\sum\limits_{z_{1}\in F^{*}_{p}}\xi_{p}^{z_{1}Tr_{1}^{m}(x+y^{p^{t}-1})})(1+\sum\limits_{z_{2}\in F^{*}_{p}}\xi_{p}^{z_{2}Tr_{1}^{m}(ax+by)})-1

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{q^{2}}{p^{2}}-1+\frac{1}{p^{2}}\sum\limits_{x,y\in F_{q}}\sum\limits_{z_{1}\in F^{*}_{p}}\chi(z_{1}x+z_{1}y^{p^{t}-1})\sum\limits_{z_{2}\in F^{*}_{p}}\chi(z_{2}ax+z_{2}by)

\displaystyle+

\displaystyle\frac{1}{p^{2}}\sum_{x,y\in F_{q}}\sum\limits_{z_{1}\in F^{*}_{p}}\chi(z_{1}x+z_{1}y^{p^{t}-1})+\frac{1}{p^{2}}\sum\limits_{x,y\in F_{q}}\sum\limits_{z_{2}\in F^{*}_{p}}\chi(z_{2}ax+z_{2}by)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{q^{2}}{p^{2}}-1+\frac{1}{p^{2}}\sum\limits_{x,y\in F_{q}}\sum\limits_{z_{1}\in F^{*}_{p}}\chi(z_{1}x+z_{1}y^{p^{t}-1})\sum\limits_{z_{2}\in F^{*}_{p}}\chi(z_{2}ax+z_{2}by)~\ {\rm(by~Lemma~\ref{character})}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{q^{2}}{p^{2}}-1+\frac{1}{p^{2}}\sum\limits_{z_{1},z_{2}\in F^{*}_{p}}\sum\limits_{x\in F_{q}}\chi((z_{1}+z_{2}a)x)\sum\limits_{y\in F_{q}}\chi(z_{1}y^{p^{t}-1}+z_{2}by)
