Congruence Properties of Indices of Triangular Numbers Multiple of Other Triangular Numbers
Vladimir PLETSER

Abstract
. It is known that, for any positive non-square integer multiplier k k , there is an infinity of multiples of triangular numbers which are triangular numbers. We analyze the congruence properties of the indices ξ \xi of triangular numbers that are multiples of other triangular numbers. We show that the remainders in the congruence relations of ξ \xi modulo k k come always in pairs whose sum always equal ( k − 1 ) \left(k-1\right) , always include 0 and ( k − 1 ) \left(k-1\right) , and only 0 and ( k − 1 ) \left(k-1\right) if k k is prime, or an odd power of a prime, or an even square plus one or an odd square minus one or minus two. If the multiplier k k is twice the triangular number of n n , the set of remainders includes also n n and ( n 2 − 1 ) \left(n^{2}-1\right) and if k k has integer factors, the set of remainders include multiples of a factor following certain rules. Finally, algebraic expressions are found for remainders in function of k k and its factors. Several exceptions are noticed and superseding rules exist between various rules and expressions of remainders. This approach allows to eliminate in numerical searches those ( k − υ ) \left(k-\upsilon\right) values of ξ i \xi_{i} that are known not to provide solutions, where υ \upsilon is the even number of remainders. The gain is typically in the order of k / υ k/\upsilon , with υ ≪ k \upsilon\ll k for large values of k k .

Proposition 1

For
