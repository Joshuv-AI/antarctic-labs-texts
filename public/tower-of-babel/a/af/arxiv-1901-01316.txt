Abstract
. In this paper we investigate some strong convergence theorems for partial sums with respect to Vilenkin system.

42C10.

Key words and phrases:
Vilenkin system, partial sums, Fejér means, martingale Hardy space, strong convergence.

1.
Introduction

It is well-known (for details see e.g.
[
10
]
and
[
14
]
) that
Vilenkin system does not form basis in the space
L_{1}\left(G_{m}\right).

Moreover, there is a function in the Hardy space
H_{1}\left(G_{m}\right),

such that the partial sums of
f
are not bounded in
L_{1}
-norm.
However, subsequence
S_{M_{n}}
of partial sums are bounded from the
martingale Hardy space
H_{1}\left(G_{m}\right)
to the Lebesgue space
L_{1}\left(G_{m}\right):

(1)

\left\|S_{M_{k}}f\right\|_{H_{1}}\leq c\left\|f\right\|_{H_{1}}\text{ \ \ \ }(k\in\mathbb{N}).

Moreover, we have the follwing norm equivalence:

(2)

\left\|f\right\|_{H_{1}}\equiv\left\|\sup_{n}\left|S_{M_{n}}f\right|\right\|_{1}.

Moreover, Gát
[
8
]
proved the following strong convergence result for
all
f\in H_{1}:

\underset{n\rightarrow\infty}{\lim}\frac{1}{\log n}\overset{n}{\underset{k=1}{\sum}}\frac{\left\|S_{k}f-f\right\|_{1}}{k}=0,

where
S_{k}f
denotes the
k
-th partial sum of the Vilenkin-Fourier series
of
f.

It follows that there exists an absolute constant
c,
such that

(3)

\frac{1}{\log n}\overset{n}{\underset{k=1}{\sum}}\frac{\left\|S_{k}f\right\|_{1}}{k}\leq c\left\|f\right\|_{H_{1}}\text{ \ }\left(n=2,3...\right)

and

\underset{n\rightarrow\infty}{\lim}\frac{1}{\log n}\overset{n}{\underset{k=1}{\sum}}\frac{\left\|S_{k}f\right\|_{1}}{k}=\left\|f\right\|_{H_{1}},

for all
f\in H_{1}.

Analogical result for the trigonometric system was proved by Smith
[
20
]
, for the Walsh-Paley system by Simon
[
18
]
.

If partial sums of Vilenkin-Fourier series was bounded from
H_{1}
to
L_{1}
we also would have:

(4)

\underset{n\in\mathbf{\mathbb{N}}_{+}}{\sup}\frac{1}{n}\underset{m=1}{\overset{n}{\sum}}\left\|S_{m}f\right\|_{1}\leq c\left\|f\right\|_{H_{1}}.

but as it was present above that boundednes of partial sums does not hold from
H_{1}
to
L_{1}
, However, we have inequality (
3
).

On the other hand, in one-dimensional, Fujji
[
6
]
and Simon
[
17
]

proved that maximal operator Fejér means is bounded from
H_{1}
to
L_{1}
. It follows that

(5)

\underset{n\in\mathbf{\mathbb{N}}_{+}}{\sup}\left\|\frac{1}{n}\underset{m=1}{\overset{n}{\sum}}S_{m}f\right\|_{1}<c\left\|f\right\|_{H_{1}}.

So, natural question has arised that if inequality (
4
) holds true, which would be generalization of inequality (
5
) or we have negative answer on this problem.

In this paper we prove that there exists a function
f\in H_{1}
such that

\underset{n\in\mathbf{\mathbb{N}}_{+}}{\sup}\frac{1}{n}\underset{m=1}{\overset{n}{\sum}}\left\|S_{m}f\right\|_{1}=\infty.

This paper is organized as follows: in order not to disturb our discussions
later on some definitions and notations are presented in Section 2. For the proofs of the main results we
need some auxiliary Lemmas. These results are presented in Section 3. The formulation and detailed proof of main results can be found in Section 4.

2.
Definitions and Notations

Let
\mathbb{N}_{+}
denote the set of the positive integers,
\mathbb{N}:=\mathbb{N}_{+}\cup\{0\}.

Let
m:=(m_{0,}m_{1},\dots)
denote a sequence of the positive integers not
less than 2.

Denote by

Z_{m_{k}}:=\{0,1,\dots,m_{k}-1\}

the additive group of integers modulo
m_{k}.

Define the group
G_{m}
as the complete direct product of the group
Z_{m_{j}}
with the product of the discrete topologies of
Z_{m_{j}}

,
s.

The direct product
\mu
of the measures

\mu_{k}\left(\{j\}\right):=1/m_{k}\text{ \qquad}(j\in Z_{m_{k}})

is the Haar measure on
G_{m_{\text{ }}}
with
\mu\left(G_{m}\right)=1.

If
\sup_{n\in\mathbb{N}}m_{n}<\infty
, then we call
G_{m}
a bounded
Vilenkin group. If the generating sequence
m
is not bounded then
G_{m}

is said to be an unbounded Vilenkin group.
In this paper we discuss
bounded Vilenkin groups only.

The elements of
G_{m}
are represented by sequences

x:=(x_{0},x_{1},\dots,x_{k},\dots)\qquad\left(\text{ }x_{k}\in Z_{m_{k}}\right).

It is easy to give a base for the neighbourhood of
G_{m}

I_{0}\left(x\right):=G_{m},

I_{n}(x):=\{y\in G_{m}\mid y_{0}=x_{0},\dots,y_{n-1}=x_{n-1}\}\text{ }(x\in G_{m},\text{ }n\in\mathbb{N})

Denote
I_{n}:=I_{n}\left(0\right)
for
n\in\mathbb{N}
and
\overline{I_{n}}:=G_{m}

\backslash

I_{n}

.

Let

e_{n}:=\left(0,\dots,0,x_{n}=1,0,\dots\right)\in G_{m}\qquad\left(n\in\mathbb{N}\right).

If we define the so-called generalized number system based on
m
in the
following way:

M_{0}:=1,\text{ \qquad}M_{k+1}:=m_{k}M_{k\text{ }}\ \qquad(k\in\mathbb{N})

then every
n\in\mathbb{N}
can be uniquely expressed as
n=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}n_{j}M_{j}
where
n_{j}\in Z_{m_{j}}

~(j\in\mathbb{N})
and only a finite number of
n_{j}`
s differ from zero. Let
\left|n\right|:=\max

\{j\in\mathbb{N};

n_{j}\neq 0\}.

For the natural number
n=\sum_{j=1}^{\infty}n_{j}M_{j},
we define

\delta_{j}=signn_{j}=sign\left(\ominus n_{j}\right),\text{ \ \ \ \ }\delta_{j}^{\ast}=\left|\ominus n_{j}-1\right|\delta_{j},

\ominus

a_{k}\oplus b_{k}=\left(a_{k}+b_{k}\right)
mod
m_{k}.

We define functions
v

v^{\ast}
by

v\left(n\right)=\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\left|\delta_{j+1}-\delta_{j}\right|+\delta_{0},\text{ \ }v^{\ast}\left(n\right)=\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\delta_{j}^{\ast},

Next, we introduce on
G_{m}
an orthonormal system which is called the
Vilenkin system.

At first define the complex valued function
r_{k}\left(x\right):G_{m}\rightarrow\mathbb{C},
the generalized Rademacher functions as

r_{k}\left(x\right):=\exp\left(2\pi\imath x_{k}/m_{k}\right)\text{
\qquad}\left(\imath^{2}=-1,\text{ }x\in G_{m},\text{ }k\in\mathbb{N}\right).

Now define the Vilenkin system
\psi:=(\psi_{n}:n\in\mathbb{N})
on
G_{m}
as:

\psi_{n}\left(x\right):=\prod_{k=0}^{\infty}r_{k}^{n_{k}}\left(x\right)\text{ \qquad}\left(n\in\mathbb{N}\right).

Specially, we call this system the Walsh-Paley one if
m\equiv 2.

The norm (or quasi norm) of the space
L_{p}(G_{m})
is defined by

\left\|f\right\|_{p}:=\left(\int_{G_{m}}\left|f(x)\right|^{p}d\mu(x)\right)^{1/p}\qquad\left(0<p<\infty\right).

The Vilenkin system is orthonormal and complete in
L_{2}\left(G_{m}\right)\,
(for details see e.g.
[
1
,
26
]
).

If

partial sums of the Fourier series, Fejér means, Dirichlet kernels
with respect to the Vilenkin system in the usual manner:

\displaystyle:

4.
Main Result

Theorem 1
.

There exists a martingale
f\in H_{1},
such that

\sup_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\frac{1}{n}\overset{n}{\underset{k=1}{\sum}}\left\|S_{k}f\right\|_{1}=\infty.

5.
Proof of the Theorem

Proof of Theorem
1
.

Let
\left\{\alpha_{k}:k\in\mathbb{N}\right\}
be an increasing sequence of the positive integers such that

(8)

\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{\alpha_{k}^{1/2}}<c<\infty.

Let

f=\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{a_{k}}{\alpha_{k}^{1/2}},

where

a_{k}=D_{M_{\alpha_{k}+1}}-D_{M_{{}_{\alpha_{k}}}}.

It is evident that

S_{M_{n}}f=\sum_{\left\{k;\text{ }\alpha_{k}<n\right\}}\frac{a_{k}}{\alpha_{k}^{1/2}},

and

\left|S_{M_{n}}f\right|\leq\sum_{\left\{k;\text{ }\alpha_{k}<n\right\}}\frac{\left|a_{k}\right|}{\alpha_{k}^{1/2}}\leq\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{\left|a_{k}\right|}{\alpha_{k}^{1/2}},

It follows that

\sup_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\left|S_{M_{n}}f\right|\leq\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{\left|a_{k}\right|}{\alpha_{k}^{1/2}}.

Since (see equality (
6
))

\left\|a_{k}\right\|\leq 2,\ \ \text{fol all}\ \ k\in\mathbb{N}.

by combining (
2
) and (
8
) we get that

\displaystyle\left\|f\right\|_{H_{1}}\leq c\left\|\sup_{k\in\mathbb{N}}\left|S_{M_{k}}f\right|\right\|_{1}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle c\left\|\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{\left|a_{k}\right|}{\alpha_{k}^{1/2}}\right\|\leq c\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{\left\|a_{k}\right\|}{\alpha_{k}^{1/2}}\leq 2c\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{\alpha_{k}^{1/2}}\leq c<\infty.

Moreover,

(9)

\widehat{f}(j)=\left\{\begin{array}[]{l}\frac{1}{\alpha_{k}^{1/2}},\,\,\text{\ \ \ \ \thinspace\thinspace}j\in\left\{M_{\alpha_{k}},...,M_{\alpha_{k}+1}-1\right\},\text{ }k\in\mathbb{N}\\
0\text{ },\text{ \thinspace\qquad\thinspace\thinspace\thinspace\thinspace\thinspace}j\notin\bigcup\limits_{k=1}^{\infty}\left\{M_{\alpha_{k}},...,M_{\alpha_{k}+1}-1\right\}.\text{ }\end{array}\right.

Let
M_{\alpha_{k}}\leq j<M_{\alpha_{k+1}}.
Since

D_{j+M_{\alpha_{k}}}=D_{M_{\alpha_{k}}}+\psi_{{}_{M_{\alpha_{k}}}}D_{j},\text{ \qquad when \thinspace\thinspace}j<M_{\alpha_{k}},

if we apply (
9
) we obtain that

(10)

\displaystyle S_{j}f

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle S_{M_{\alpha_{k}}}f+\sum_{v=M_{\alpha_{k}}}^{j-1}\widehat{f}(v)\psi_{v}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle S_{M_{\alpha_{k}}}f+\sum_{v=M_{\alpha_{k}}}^{j-1}\widehat{f}(v)\psi_{v}

(11)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle S_{M_{\alpha_{k}}}f+\frac{M_{\alpha_{k}}^{1/p-1}}{\alpha_{k}^{1/2}}\sum_{v=M_{\alpha_{k}}}^{j-1}\psi_{v}

(12)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle S_{M_{\alpha_{k}}}f+\frac{M_{\alpha_{k}}^{1/p-1}}{\alpha_{k}^{1/2}}\left(D_{j}-D_{M_{\alpha_{k}}}\right)

(13)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle S_{M_{\alpha_{k}}}f+\frac{M_{\alpha_{k}}^{1/p-1}}{\alpha_{k}^{1/2}}\psi_{M_{\alpha_{k}}}D_{j-M_{\alpha_{k}}}

(14)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle III_{1}+III_{2}.

In view of (
1
) we can write that

(15)

\displaystyle\left\|III_{1}\right\|_{1}\leq\left\|S_{M_{\alpha_{k}}}f\right\|_{1}\leq c\left\|f\right\|_{H_{1}}.

By combining Lemma
1
and (
15
) we get that

\displaystyle\left\|S_{n}f\right\|_{1}\geq\left\|III_{2}\right\|_{1}-\left\|III_{1}\right\|_{1}\geq\frac{cv\left({n-M_{\alpha_{k}}}\right)}{\alpha_{k}^{1/2}}-c\left\|f\right\|_{H_{1}}.

Hence, according to Lemma
2
we can conclude that

\displaystyle\underset{n\in\mathbf{\mathbb{N}}_{+}}{\sup}\frac{1}{n}\underset{k=1}{\overset{n}{\sum}}\left\|S_{k}f\right\|_{1}

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{M_{\alpha_{k}+1}}\underset{\left\{M_{\alpha_{k}}\leq l\leq 2M_{\alpha_{k}}\right\}}{\sum}\left\|S_{l}f\right\|_{1}

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{M_{\alpha_{k}+1}}\underset{\left\{M_{\alpha_{k}}\leq l\leq 2M_{\alpha_{k}}\right\}}{\sum}\left(\frac{v\left(l-M_{\alpha_{k}}\right)}{\alpha_{k}^{1/2}}-c\left\|f\right\|_{H_{1}}\right)

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\frac{c}{\alpha_{k}^{1/2}M_{\alpha_{k}}}\underset{l=1}{\overset{M_{\alpha_{k}}-1}{\sum}}v\left(l\right)-c\left\|f\right\|_{H_{1/2}}^{1/2}

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle c\alpha_{k}^{1/2}\rightarrow\infty,\text{ as \ }k\rightarrow\infty.

The proof is complete.
