Abstract
. This note consists of two parts. The first part (§1 and §2) is a partial review of the works by van Meer and Stokman (2010), van Meer (2011) and Stokman (2014) which established a bispectral analogue of the Cherednik correspondence between quantum affine Knizhnik-Zamolodchikov equations and the eigenvalue problems of Macdonald type. In this review we focus on the rank one cases, i.e., on the reduced type A 1 A_{1} and the non-reduced type ( C 1 ∨ , C 1 ) (C_{1}^{\vee},C_{1}) , to which the associated Macdonald-Koornwinder polynomials are the Rogers polynomials and the Askey-Wilson polynomials, respectively. We give detailed computations and formulas that may be difficult to find in the literature. The second part (§3) is a complement of the first part, and is also a continuation of our previous study (Y.-Y., 2022) on the parameter specialization of Macdonald-Koornwinder polynomials, where we found four types of specialization of the type ( C 1 ∨ , C 1 ) (C_{1}^{\vee},C_{1}) parameters (which could be called the Askey-Wilson parameters) to recover the type A 1 A_{1} . In this note, we show that among the four specializations there is only one which is compatible with the bispectral correspondence discussed in the first part.

Let us begin by recalling on the original Cherednik’s correspondence. We refer to

for an exposition of this correspondence. In

\GL_{n}
. Let
H=H(k)
be the affine Hecke algebra of type
\GL_{n}
with complex parameter

be the algebraic torus associated with the weight lattice

q
-difference equations for functions of the torus variable

-module

q
-difference operators of Macdonald type.

Below we explain the correspondence for the type
\GL_{n}

\Lambda=\mathbb{Z}^{n}
and put
t=(t_{1},\dotsc,t_{n}),\gamma=(\gamma_{1},\dotsc,\gamma_{n})\in T

For a nonzero complex parameter
q\in\mathbb{C}
, which will be called the quantum parameter, let
\mathrm{SOL}_{\textup{Mac}}(k,q)_{\gamma}
be the eigenspace of the Macdonald-Ruijsenaars
q
-difference operators of type
\GL_{n}
, i.e.,

\mathrm{SOL}_{\textup{Mac}}(k,q)_{\gamma}\coloneqq\left\{f(t)\in\mathcal{M}(T)\mid L_{p}^{t}f(t)=p(\gamma)f(t),\ \forall p\in\mathbb{C}[T]^{\mathfrak{S}_{n}}\right\},

where
\mathcal{M}(T)
is the set of meromorphic functions on
T
, and

denotes the Macdonald-Ruijsenaars

-difference operator
[

]
associated to each symmetric polynomial

is given by

(0.1.1)

\displaystyle L_{e}^{t}\coloneqq\sum_{i=1}^{n}\prod_{j\neq i}\frac{kt_{i}-k^{-1}t_{j}}{t_{i}-t_{j}}T_{q,t_{i}}.

Here we used the

(T_{q,t_{i}}f)(t_{1},\dots,t_{n})=f(t_{1},\dots,qt_{i},\dots,t_{n}),\quad f(t)\in\mathcal{M}(T).

Moreover, let
\mathrm{SOL}_{\textup{qKZ}}(k,q)_{\gamma}
be the QAKZ equations of type
\GL_{n}
, i.e.,

\mathrm{SOL}_{\textup{qKZ}}(k,q)_{\gamma}\coloneqq\left\{f(t)\in H_{0}^{\mathcal{M}(T)}\mid C^{\gamma}_{\tu(\lambda)}(t)f(q^{-\lambda}t)=f(t),\ \lambda\in\Lambda\right\},

where
H_{0}=H_{0}(k)
is the finite Hecke algebra of type

and
H_{0}^{\mathcal{M}(T)}\coloneqq\mathcal{M}(T)\otimes_{\mathbb{C}}H_{0}
.
We omit the precise definition of the
q
-difference operators

and
(C^{\vee}_{1},C_{1})
in

1

section

2
, respectively.
Cherednik’s correspondence for the type

is now described as

(0.1.2)

\displaystyle\chi_{+}\colon\mathrm{SOL}_{\textup{qKZ}}(k,q)_{\gamma}\longrightarrow\mathrm{SOL}_{\textup{Mac}}(k,q)_{\gamma}.

A bispectral analogue of Cherednik’s correspondence is investigated by van Meer and Stokman
