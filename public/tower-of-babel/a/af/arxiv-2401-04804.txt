Existence of weak solutions for the kinetic models of motion of myxobacteria with alignment and reversals
Patrick Murphy, Oleg Igoshin, Misha Perepelitsa, Ilya Timofeyev

Abstract
. In this paper, we consider three non-linear kinetic partial differential equations that emerge in the modeling of motion of rod-shaped cells such as myxobacteria. This motion is characterized by nematic alignment with neighboring cells, orientation reversals from cell polarity switching, orientation diffusion, and transport driven by chemotaxis. Our primary contribution lies in establishing the existence of weak solutions for these equations. Our analytical approach is based on the application of the classical averaging lemma from the kinetic theory, augmented by a novel version where the transport operator is substituted with a uni-directional diffusion operator.



1.
Introduction

Many rod-shaped bacteria are known to move on surfaces and self-organize into various spatial patterns due to mechanical interactions and biochemical signaling between cells. For instance, myxobacteria - bacteria studied for their multicellular interactions can form dynamic patterns of clusters, streams, swirls, and aggregates.
Motivated by the problem of modeling the motion of a single layer of myxobacteria on a flat surface, we consider a mean-filed equation for

(1.2)

v_{f}(x,t){}={}\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}f(x,\theta_{1},t)\sin(2(\theta_{1}-\theta))\,d\theta_{1},

\tau_{\pi}f(x,\theta,t){}={}f(x,\theta+\pi,t),

The right-hand side of equation (

The left-hand side represents the active part: transport in the direction of orientation of the cell’s longer axis. The model is based on the kinetic model of Peruani et al.
[
19

Alongside equation (

In the first case, the kinetic equation is

\partial_{t}f{}-{}\,\text{div}\,\left(e(\theta)\otimes e(\theta)\nabla f\right){}=-{}\partial_{\theta}(f\Psi(v_{f})){}+{}\partial^{2}_{\theta\theta}f.

As in the classical Keller-Segel model, chemotaxis is expressed as a transport term to the “active” part of the kinetic equation.
