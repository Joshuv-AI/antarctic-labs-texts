Twists of Albanese varieties over function fields with large ranks
Abolfazl Mohajer, Sajad Salami

Abstract
. In this paper we construct abelian varieties of large Mordell-Weil rank over function fields. We achieve this by using a generalization of the notion of Prym variety to higher dimensions and a structure theorem for the Mordell-Weil group of abelian varieties over function fields proven in our previous works. We consider abelian and dihedral covers of the projective space and apply the above results to the twists of their Albanese varieties.

. Further, let

be smooth projective varieties over

with function fields

respectively. Let

be a

-Galois covering. To such a covering we have associated in
[
8
]
an abelian varietiy

, the so-called

, that generalizes the notion of Prym variety in the case of coverings of curves. The group

is also the Galois group of the Galois field extension

, the twist of

(i.e., we identify

). By the results of
[
8
]
there is an isomorphism for

in terms of the Prym variety. Furthermore,
[
8
]

In this paper, on the one hand we consider abelian covers

of the

L^{\prime}_{1}={L^{\prime}}^{{\mathbb{Z}}_{2}}=k(x_{1},\dots,x_{m},u_{1}u_{2},\dots,u_{1}u_{m})
. Setting

.
L^{\prime}_{2}={L}^{{\mathbb{Z}}_{n}}=k(t_{1},\dots,t_{m},z_{1}^{n-1}z_{2},\dots,z_{1}^{n-1}z_{m})
and so

1,\dots,m-1
. Suppose
C_{a}

\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}f(x_{1})U^{2}=f(x)\\

Remark 3.3
.

By similar arguments as in the proof of Theorem
