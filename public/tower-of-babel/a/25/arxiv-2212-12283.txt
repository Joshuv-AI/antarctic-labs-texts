Ξ \Xi -nuclear constraints from Ξ − \Xi^{-} emulsion capture events
\firstname Eliahu \lastname Friedman \fnsep, \firstname Avraham \lastname Gal \fnsep

Abstract
All five KEK and J-PARC two-body Ξ − \Xi^{-} + A Z → \to Λ A ′ {}^{A^{\prime}}_{~\Lambda} Z’+ Λ A ′′ {}^{A^{\prime\prime}}_{~\Lambda} Z” capture events in light emulsion nuclei, including KISO and IBUKI in 14 N, are consistent with Coulomb-assisted 1 ​ p Ξ − 1p_{\Xi^{-}} nuclear states. The underlying Ξ \Xi -nuclear potential is strongly attractive, with nuclear-matter depth V Ξ V_{\Xi} larger than 20 MeV. The recent 14 N capture events KINKA and IRRAWADDY assigned by J-PARC E07 to 1 ​ s Ξ − 1s_{\Xi^{-}} nuclear states, and implying considerably shallower V Ξ V_{\Xi} , have also another interpretation as 1 ​ p Ξ 0 1p_{\Xi^{0}} nuclear states.
