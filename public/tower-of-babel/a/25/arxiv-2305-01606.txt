is an embedding of algebras with involutions.
Then
\xi\circ\varepsilon_{F_{(K,L,{\mathcal{A}})}}
is an embedding

(F_{(K,L,{\mathcal{A}})}L,\phi_{F_{(K,L,{\mathcal{A}})}})\hookrightarrow({\mathcal{A}}_{F_{(K,L,{\mathcal{A}})}},\tau_{{F_{(K,L,{\mathcal{A}})}}})
of algebras with involution.

∎

The following construction of the field
F_{(K,S,A)}
is an adaptation of the construction from
[
10
]
for algebras with involutions.
We give the details below for the reader’s convenience.

Proposition 7
.

Let
F
be a field of characteristic
\neq 2,3
;
F/K
a quadratic field
extension,
\sigma
the non-trivial
K
-automorphism of
F
,
A\subset Alg_{3}(F/K)
and

S\subset Ext_{3}(F/K)
. Then there exists a field extension
F_{(K,S,A)}/F
and
a subfield
K_{(S,{\mathcal{A}})}\subset F_{(K,S,A)}
such that
[F_{(K,S,A)}:K_{(S,A)}]=2
and

(1)
F_{(K,S,A)}=K_{(S,A)}F
and the non-trivial
K_{(S,A)}
-automorphism, denoted by
\sigma_{F_{(K,S,A)}}
, of
F_{(K,S,A)}
extends
\sigma
;

(2) the homomorphism
\text{\rm res}_{F_{(K,S,A)}/F}:{{}_{3}\text{\rm Br}(F)}\longrightarrow{{}_{3}\text{\rm Br}(F_{(K,S,A)})}
is injective;

(3) for any field extension
L^{\prime}
of
F
of degree 3,
F_{(K,L,{\mathcal{A}})}
and
L^{\prime}
are linearly disjoint over
F
;

(4) for any
{\mathcal{A}}\in A
with an
F/K
-involution
\tau
and
L\in S
with a
K
-automorphism
\phi
of order 2 extending
\sigma
, there is an embedding
(F_{(K,S,A)}L,\phi_{F_{(K,S,A)}})\hookrightarrow({\mathcal{A}}_{F_{(K,S,A)}},\tau_{{F_{(K,S,A)}}})
of algebras with involution.

Proof
. Let
{\mathcal{P}}:=\{(L,{\mathcal{D}})|L\in S\mbox{ and }{\mathcal{D}}\in A\}
be the set of pairs.
Let also
<
be a well-ordering on
\mathcal{P}
and let
t_{0}=(L_{0},{\mathcal{D}}_{0})
denote its least element.
Set
E_{t_{0}}:=F_{(K,L_{0},{\mathcal{D}}_{0})}
and
T_{0}:=K_{(L_{0},{\mathcal{D}}_{0})}
, where the fields
F_{(K,L_{0},{\mathcal{D}}_{0})}
and
K_{(L_{0},{\mathcal{D}}_{0})}
are constructed in
Lemma
6
. For
t=(L,{\mathcal{D}})\in{\mathcal{P}}
, set

E^{<t}:=\bigcup_{t^{\prime}<t}E_{t^{\prime}},T^{<t}:=\bigcup_{t^{\prime}<t}T_{t^{\prime}},T_{t}:={T^{<t}}_{(E^{<t}L,{\mathcal{D}}_{E^{<t}})},\mbox{ and }E_{t}:={E^{<t}}_{(T^{<t},E^{<t}L,{\mathcal{D}}_{E^{<t}})},

where the fields
E_{t}
and
T_{t}
are obtained by applying Lemma
6
to the quadratic field extension

E^{<t}/T^{<t}
, the field extension
E^{<t}L/E^{<t}
of degree 3, the automorphism
\phi_{E^{<t}}
of
E^{<t}L
extending the automorphism
\phi
of
L

and the
E^{<t}
-algebra
{\mathcal{D}}_{E^{<t}}
.
We also define

F_{(K,S,A)}:=\bigcup_{t\in\mathcal{P}}E_{t}
and
K_{(S,A)}:=\bigcup_{t\in\mathcal{P}}T_{t}
.

By Lemma
6
,
E_{t}=T_{t}F
and
[E_{t}:T_{t}]=2
for any
t\in\mathcal{P}
. Then
F_{(K,S,A)}=K_{(S,A)}F
,
[F_{(K,S,A)}:K_{(S,A)}]=2
and the non-trivial
K_{(S,A)}
-automorphism of
F_{(K,S,A)}
extends
\sigma
.

By Lemma
6
and transfinite induction,
the homomorphism
\text{\rm res}_{F_{(K,S,A)}/F}:{{}_{3}\text{\rm Br}(F)}\longrightarrow{{}_{3}\text{\rm Br}(F_{(K,S,A)})}
is injective and
for any field extension
L^{\prime}
of
F
of degree 3,
F_{(K,S,A)}
and
L^{\prime}
are linearly disjoint over
F
.

Finally, let
{\mathcal{A}}\in A
with an
F/K
-involution
\tau
,
L\in S
with an automorphism
\phi
of order 2 extending
\sigma
and
t=(L,{\mathcal{A}})
.
By Lemma
6
, there is an embedding
(E_{t}L,\phi_{E_{t}})\hookrightarrow({\mathcal{A}}_{E_{t}},\tau_{E_{t}})
of algebras with involution.
Moreover, as in the proof of Lemma
6
, this embedding induces
the embedding

(F_{(K,S,A)}L,\phi_{F_{(K,S,A)}})\hookrightarrow({\mathcal{A}}_{F_{(K,S,A)}},\tau_{{F_{(K,S,A)}}})

of algebras with involution.
∎

Theorem 8
.

Let
F
be a field of characteristic
\neq 2,3
,

F/K
a quadratic field
extension,
\sigma
the non-trivial
K
-automorphism of
F
,
A\subset Alg_{3}(F/K)
. Then there exists a field extension
F_{A}/F
and
a subfield
K_{A}\subset F_{A}
such that
[F_{A}:K_{A}]=2
and

(1)
F_{A}=K_{A}F
and the non-trivial
K_{A}
-automorphism, denoted by
\sigma_{F_{A}}
, of
F_{A}
extends
\sigma
;

(2) the homomorphism
\text{\rm res}_{F_{A}/F}:{{}_{3}\text{\rm Br}(F)}\longrightarrow{{}_{3}\text{\rm Br}(F_{A})}
is injective;

(3) for any central simple

F
-algebra
{\mathcal{B}}
of degree
3
with an
F/K
-involution
\theta
, the algebra
{\mathcal{B}}_{F_{A}}

has an
F_{A}/K_{A}
-involution
\theta_{F_{A}}
extending
\theta
;

(4) if
L\in Ext_{3}(F_{A}/K_{A})
with a
K_{A}
-automorphism
\phi
of order 2 extending
\sigma_{F_{A}}
,
then there is an embedding
(L,\phi)\hookrightarrow({\mathcal{A}}_{F_{A}},\tau_{F_{A}})
of algebras with involution for any
{\mathcal{A}}\in A
with an
F/K
-involution
\tau
.

Proof
.
Let
F_{0}:=F
and
K_{0}:=K
. We recursively define
F_{i}
and
K_{i}
,
i\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{>0}
, to be the fields

{F_{i-1}}_{(K_{i-1},Ext_{3}(F_{i-1}/K_{i-1}),\text{\rm res}_{F_{i-1}/F}(A))}
and
{K_{i-1}}_{(Ext_{3}(F_{i-1}/K_{i-1}),\text{\rm res}_{F_{i-1}/F}(A))}

constructed by applying Proposition
7
to the quadratic field extension
F_{i-1}/K_{i-1}
, the set

\text{\rm res}_{F_{i-1}/F}(A)\subset Alg_{3}(F_{i-1}/K_{i-1})
and
the set
Ext_{3}(F_{i-1}/K_{i-1})
.

Let
F_{A}:=\bigcup_{i\geq 0}F_{i}
and
K_{A}:=\bigcup_{i\geq 0}K_{i}
.
Hence
F_{A}=K_{A}F
and the non-trivial
K_{A}
-automorphism
\sigma_{F_{A}}
of
F_{A}
extends
\sigma
.
Therefore, for any central simple
F
-algebra
{\mathcal{B}}
of degree
3
with an
F/K
-involution
\theta
,
the
F_{A}/K_{A}
-involution
\theta_{F_{A}}
extends
\theta
.

By induction and Proposition
7
,

\text{\rm res}_{F_{A}/F}:{{}_{3}\text{\rm Br}(F)}\longrightarrow{{}_{3}\text{\rm Br}(F_{A})}
is injective.

Assume that
{\mathcal{A}}\in A
with an
F/K
-involution
\tau
and
L\in Ext_{3}(F_{A}/K_{A})
with an automorphism
\phi
of order two
extending
\sigma_{F_{A}}
.
Then there exists
i\geq 0
and a field extension
L^{\prime}
of
F_{i}
of degree 3 such that

L=F_{A}L^{\prime}
and
\phi_{i}:=\phi_{|L^{\prime}}
is a
K_{i}
-automorphism of order two. This means that
L^{\prime}\in Ext_{3}(F_{i}/K_{i})
. By Proposition
7
,
there is an embedding

(F_{i+1}L^{\prime},{\phi_{i}}_{F_{i+1}})\hookrightarrow({\mathcal{A}}_{{F_{i+1}}},\tau_{F_{i+1}})

of algebras with involution.
As in the proof of Lemma

, this embedding can be extended to an embedding

of algebras with involution.

∎

As a corollary to Theorem
8
, we obtain the following

Corollary 9
.

There exists a field
E
and a subfield
T\subset E
with
[E:T]=2
such that there is an infinite set
B

of pairwise non-isomorphic division
E
-algebras of degree 3 with
E/T
-involution and
such that for any field extension
L/E
of degree 3 with an automorphism
\phi
of order 2 extending the non-trivial
T
-automorphism of

,
there is an embedding
(L,\phi)\hookrightarrow({\mathcal{A}},\tau)
of algebras with involution for any
{\mathcal{A}}\in B
with an
E/T
-involution
\tau
.

Proof
.
Let
\xi_{3}
be a primitive 3th root of unity,
K=\mathbb{Q}(\xi_{3})(x,y,z)
, the purely transcendental extension of the field
\mathbb{Q}(\xi_{3})
and

F=K(\sqrt{2})
. Then for

F

(z,\frac{x+\sqrt{2}y^{i}}{x-\sqrt{2}y^{i}})_{3}

8

for the infinite set

,

∎

Rephrasing the previous corollary in the language of algebraic groups, we obtain the following

Corollary 10

There exists a field
T
such that there are infinitely many (pairwise non-isomorphic) outer forms of type
A_{2}
over

infinite genus.
