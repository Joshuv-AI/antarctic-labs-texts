Abstract
. We classify all normal Schubert varieties in the affine Grassmannian of a semisimple group over an arbitrary field with special attention to small positive characteristic. The proof is elementary and relies on tangent space calculations for quasi-minuscule Schubert varieties, a refined Levi lemma in positive characteristic and the classification of minimal degenerations.



1.
Introduction

Schubert varieties in classical flag varieties are known to be normal by work of Ramanan–Ramanathan
[
RR85
]
, Seshadri
[
Ses87
]
, Andersen
[
And85
]
and Mehta–Srinivas
[
MS87
]
.
The normality of Schubert varieties in Kac–Moody affine flag varieties is due to Kumar
[
Kum87
]
, Mathieu
[
Mat88
]
and Littelmann
[
Lit98
]
.
For affine flag varieties, interpreted as moduli spaces of torsors over an algebraic disc together with a trivialization over the punctured one, Faltings
[
Fal03
]
proves normality of Schubert varieties for all split, simply connected groups.
More generally, Pappas–Rapoport
[
PR08
]
and Lourenço
[
Lou
]
show normality whenever the characteristic of the ground field is either zero or big enough in comparison to the group.

For a semisimple group
G
over an algebraically closed field
k
, a special case of
[
PR08
, Theorem 0.3]
shows normality of Schubert varieties in the affine Grassmannian
\mathop{\rm Gr}\nolimits_{G}
whenever
{\rm char}(k)\nmid\#\pi_{1}(G)
where
\pi_{1}(G)
is the algebraic fundamental group of
G
.
For almost simple groups
G
the number
\#\pi_{1}(G)
divides the connection index of the Dynkin type of
G
from
[
Bou68
, Tables]
, and agrees with it whenever
G
is simple.
The normality results along with other techniques are the basis for finer geometric properties such as having rational singularities, and are used in geometric representation theory or in the reduction to positive characteristic of Shimura varieties.

When
{\rm char}(k)\mid\#\pi_{1}(G)
the work of Haines, Lourenço and the second named author
[
HLR22
]
shows that almost all Schubert varieties in
\mathop{\rm Gr}\nolimits_{G}
are non-normal.
For example, if
{\rm char}(k)\mid n
, then only finitely many Schubert varieties in the affine Grassmannian for
G=\mathrm{PGL}_{n,k}
are normal.
The results of
[
HLR22
]
allow to classify all normal Schubert varieties for groups of small rank such as
G=\mathrm{PGL}_{2,k}
for
{\rm char}(k)=2
, or
G=\mathrm{PGL}_{3,k}
for
{\rm char}(k)=3
.
In the present manuscript we classify normal Schubert varieties in
\mathop{\rm Gr}\nolimits_{G}
for all semisimple groups
G
.

1.1.
Results

Let

Fix a maximal torus

Let

is an orbit closure in the affine Grassmannian

By

,

]
.

•

The proof of ?THM?
is elementary in that no cohomological and representation-theoretic methods are used.
In fact, most cases are handled by the following proposition which extends

Proposition 1.2

(Section

)
.

Assume

Let

\mathop{\rm Gr}\nolimits_{G,\leq\mu}

In other words each connected component of

contains small, non-normal Schubert varieties and all bigger Schubert varieties are non-normal as well.
The remaining cases are classified using the transversal slice and a refinement in positive characteristic of the Levi lemma from Malkin–Ostrik–Vybornov
[

]
, see Sections

and

.
The refinement in ?THM?
is necessarily more complicated as normality of Schubert varieties is sensitive to the isogeny type of

The methods lead in ?THM?
to the classification of non-normal minimal degeneration singularities, which is of independent interest to us.
Even though many Schubert varieties are non-normal when

it turns out that most minimal degeneration singularities are still normal, see ?THM?

So, somewhat surprisingly the combination of ?THM?

Regarding the normal locus of Schubert varieties in Dynkin type

2.
Schubert varieties

Let
G
be a connected reductive group over an algebraically closed field
k
.
For a
k
-algebra
R
we denote by
R[\hskip-1.42262pt[{\varpi}]\hskip-1.42262pt]
, respectively
R(\hskip-1.99168pt({\varpi})\hskip-1.99168pt)
the ring of formal power series, respectively Laurent series in the formal variable
\varpi
.
The
loop group

LG
(respectively,
positive loop group
or
jet group

L^{+}G
) is the group-valued functor on the category of
k
-algebras
R
defined by
LG(R)=G(R(\hskip-1.99168pt({\varpi})\hskip-1.99168pt))
(respectively,
L^{+}G(R)=G(R[\hskip-1.42262pt[{\varpi}]\hskip-1.42262pt])
).
Then,
L^{+}G\subset LG
is a subgroup functor.
The affine Grassmannian is the étale quotient

(2.1)

\mathop{\rm Gr}\nolimits_{G}\hskip 2.84544pt\mathrel{{\mathop{=}\limits}}\hskip 2.84544ptLG/L^{+}G,

which is representable by an ind-projective ind-scheme over
k
.
The affine Grassmannian is equipped with a left action by
L^{+}G
.
One has
L^{+}G=\lim_{i\geq 0}G_{i}
where
G_{i}(R)=G(R[\varpi]/(\varpi^{i+1}))
is the group of
i
-jets.
Each
G_{i}
is a smooth, affine
k
-group scheme with connected fibers.

2.1.
Definition

Fix a maximal torus
T
contained in a Borel subgroup
B
in
G
.
We denote by
\langle\operatorname{-},\operatorname{-}\rangle
the natural paring between the characters
X^{*}(T)
and cocharacters
X_{*}(T)
.
The sum of the
B
-positive roots is denoted
2\rho\in X^{*}(T)
.
Let
(X_{*}(T)^{+},\leq)
be the partially ordered monoid of
B
-dominant cocharacters equipped with the Bruhat partial order:
for
\lambda,\mu\in X_{*}(T)^{+}
one has
\lambda\leq\mu
if and only if
\mu-\lambda
is a sum of
B
-positive coroots with
{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}
-coefficients.

The map
\mu\mapsto\mu(\varpi)
identifies
X_{*}(T)^{+}
with a subset of
\mathop{\rm Gr}\nolimits_{G}(k)
.
For each
\mu\in X_{*}(T)^{+}
, the Schubert variety
\mathop{\rm Gr}\nolimits_{G,\leq\mu}
(respectively,
L^{+}G
-orbit
\mathop{\rm Gr}\nolimits_{G,\mu}
) is defined as the scheme-theoretic image (respectively, sheaf-theoretic image) of the orbit map
L^{+}G\to\mathop{\rm Gr}\nolimits_{G}
,
g\mapsto g\cdot\mu(\varpi)
.
Then,
\mathop{\rm Gr}\nolimits_{G,\leq\mu}
is representable by a projective
k
-variety of relative dimension
\langle 2\rho,\mu\rangle
.
Each orbit
\mathop{\rm Gr}\nolimits_{G,\mu}
is representable by a smooth
k
-variety.
The inclusion
\mathop{\rm Gr}\nolimits_{G,\mu}\subset\mathop{\rm Gr}\nolimits_{G,\leq\mu}
is open.
On the underlying sets one has
\mathop{\rm Gr}\nolimits_{G,\leq\mu}=\sqcup_{\lambda\in X_{*}(T)^{+}:\lambda\leq\mu}\mathop{\rm Gr}\nolimits_{G,\lambda}
.
The left-
L^{+}G
-action on each Schubert variety factors through the group of
i
-jets
L^{+}G\to G_{i}
for some
i\geq 0
.

2.2.
Normality

We denote by
\pi_{1}(G)
the algebraic fundamental group of
G
which is isomorphic to the quotient of
X_{*}(T)
by the coroot lattice.
In particular,
\pi_{1}(G)
is a finite group whenever
G
is semisimple.
The following theorem shows that most Schubert varieties are normal:

Theorem 2.1

(
[
PR08
, Theorem 0.3]
)
.

Assume either
{\rm char}(k)=0
, or
{\rm char}(k)>0
and
{\rm char}(k)\nmid\#\pi_{1}(G_{\rm der})
where
G_{\rm der}
is the derived group of
G
.
Then, the Schubert variety
\mathop{\rm Gr}\nolimits_{G,\leq\mu}
is normal for all
\mu\in X_{*}(T)^{+}
.

In addition, normal Schubert varieties are known to be Cohen–Macaulay with only rational singularities.
The proof of ?THM?
is by reduction to the simply connected cover
G_{\rm sc}\to G_{\rm der}
, which is an étale isogeny if and only if
{\rm char}(k)\nmid\#\pi_{1}(G_{\rm der})
.
The remaining cases where
{\rm char}(k)\mid\#\pi_{1}(G_{\rm der})
reduce to almost simple, semisimple groups.
In contrast to ?THM?
almost all Schubert varieties are non-normal in these cases:

Theorem 2.2

(
[
HLR22
, Theorem 1.1]
)
.

Assume
G
is almost simple, semisimple and
{\rm char}(k)\mid\#\pi_{1}(G)
.
Then, the Schubert variety
\mathop{\rm Gr}\nolimits_{G,\leq\mu}
is normal for only finitely many
\mu\in X_{*}(T)^{+}
.

The non-normal Schubert varieties are known to be geometrically unibranch and regular in codimension
1
, but neither S2 nor seminormal.
The methods in
[
HLR22
]
allow to give an explicit bound on the number of normal Schubert varieties in
\mathop{\rm Gr}\nolimits_{G}
based on the following result:

Proposition 2.3

(
[
HLR22
, Corollary 6.2]
)
.

Assume
G
is almost simple, semisimple and
p\mid\#\pi_{1}(G)
.
Then, the Schubert variety
\mathop{\rm Gr}\nolimits_{G,\leq\mu_{\rm qm}}
is non-normal where
\mu_{\rm qm}\in X_{*}(T)^{+}
is quasi-minuscule.

-basis of

of fundamental coweights in the notation of
[

, Tables]

The following is a special case of Stembridge’s classification of minimal degenerations:

Lemma 7.7
.

The normal Schubert varieties in
\mathop{\rm Gr}\nolimits_{G}
are precisely the minuscule ones and
\mathop{\rm Gr}\nolimits_{G,\leq\mu}
for all
\mu\in\{2\omega_{1}^{\vee},\omega_{3}^{\vee},\omega_{5}^{\vee},2\omega_{6}^{\vee}\}
.

Proof.

As in case
D
above one checks the Bruhat partial order in Figure
2
using the Stembridge classification.
So, the Schubert varieties for all dominant cocharacters not listed in the lemma are non-normal.

Now let
\mu\in\{\omega_{5}^{\vee},2\omega_{6}^{\vee}\}
and
\lambda=\omega_{1}^{\vee}
.
Then,
I_{\lambda,\mu}=\{\alpha_{2}^{\vee},\ldots,\alpha_{6}^{\vee}\}
.
Hence, the Levi subgroup
M_{I_{\lambda,\mu}}
is of type
D_{5}
. In particular,
3\nmid\#\pi_{1}(M_{\rm der})
and so the transversal slice
\mathop{\rm Gr}\nolimits^{\lambda}_{G,\leq\mu}
is normal.
The assertion for
\mu\in\{\omega_{3}^{\vee},2\omega_{1}^{\vee}\}
follows similarly.
∎
