Existence and stability results for a coupled system of Hilfer fractional Langevin equation with non local integral boundary value conditions.
Khalid HILAL a , Ahmed KAJOUNI a and Hamid LMOU a

Abstract
This paper deals with the existence and uniqueness of solution for a coupled system of Hilfer fractional Langevin equation with non local integral boundary value conditions. The novelty of this work is that it is more general than the works based on the derivative of Caputo and Riemann-Liouville, because when β = 0 \beta=0 we find the Riemann-Liouville fractional derivative and when β = 1 \beta=1 we find the Caputo fractional derivative. Initially, we give some definitions and notions that will be used throughout the work, after that we will establish the existence and uniqueness results by employing the fixed point theorems. Finaly, we investigate different kinds of stability such as Ulam-Hyers stability, generalized Ulam-Hyers stability.

\displaystyle=\displaystyle\frac{(b-a)^{p_{1}+p_{2}}}{\Gamma(p_{1}+p_{2}+1)}\mathcal{L}_{2}+\frac{(b-a)^{\delta_{1}+p_{2}-1}}{|\Lambda|\Gamma(\delta_{1}+p_{2})}

\displaystyle\times\Bigg[|\Phi_{1}|\Bigg(|\lambda_{1}|\displaystyle\sum\limits_{j=1}^{m}|\omega_{j}|\frac{(\xi_{j}-a)^{\alpha_{2}+\sigma_{j}}}{\Gamma(\alpha_{2}+\sigma_{j}+1)}+\displaystyle\sum\limits_{j=1}^{m}|\omega_{j}|\frac{(\xi_{j}-a)^{\alpha_{1}+\alpha_{2}+\sigma_{j}}}{\Gamma(\alpha_{1}+\alpha_{2}+\sigma_{j}+1)}\mathcal{L}_{1}\Bigg)

\displaystyle+|\Phi_{3}|\displaystyle\sum\limits_{i=1}^{n}|\mu_{i}|\frac{(\eta_{i}-a)^{p_{1}+p_{2}+\nu_{i}}}{\Gamma(p_{1}+p_{2}+\nu_{i}+1)}\mathcal{L}_{2}\Bigg],

(81)

C_{2}=\displaystyle\frac{(b-a)^{p_{1}+p_{2}}}{\Gamma(p_{1}+p_{2}+1)}.

(82)

It follows that

\begin{cases}\displaystyle\|\tilde{x}-x\|_{\mathcal{K}}-\frac{B_{1}}{1-A_{1}}\|\tilde{y}-y\|_{\mathcal{K}}\leq\frac{C_{1}}{1-A_{1}}\varepsilon_{1}.\\
\displaystyle\|\tilde{y}-y\|_{\mathcal{K}}-\frac{B_{2}}{1-A_{2}}\|\tilde{x}-x\|_{\mathcal{K}}\leq\frac{C_{2}}{1-A_{2}}\varepsilon_{2}.\end{cases}

(83)

Then
(79)
can be writing as matrices as follows

\displaystyle\begin{pmatrix}1&-\frac{B_{1}}{1-A_{1}}\\
-\frac{B_{2}}{1-A_{2}}&1\end{pmatrix}\displaystyle\begin{pmatrix}\|\tilde{x}-x\|_{\mathcal{K}}\\
\|\tilde{y}-y\|_{\mathcal{K}}\end{pmatrix}\leq\displaystyle\begin{pmatrix}\frac{C_{1}}{1-A_{1}}\varepsilon_{1}\\
\frac{C_{2}}{1-A_{2}}\varepsilon_{2}\end{pmatrix},

(84)

by simple computations, the above inequality becomes

\displaystyle\begin{pmatrix}\|\tilde{x}-x\|_{\mathcal{K}}\\
\|\tilde{y}-y\|_{\mathcal{K}}\end{pmatrix}\leq\displaystyle\begin{pmatrix}\frac{1}{\Delta}&\frac{B_{1}}{\Delta(1-A_{1})}\\
\frac{B_{2}}{\Delta(1-A_{2})}&\frac{1}{\Delta}\end{pmatrix}\times\displaystyle\begin{pmatrix}\frac{C_{1}}{1-A_{1}}\varepsilon_{1}\\
\frac{C_{2}}{1-A_{2}}\varepsilon_{2}\end{pmatrix},

(85)

where
\Delta=1-\displaystyle\frac{B_{1}B_{2}}{(1-A_{1})(1-A_{2})}\neq 0
, this leads to

\displaystyle\|\tilde{x}-x\|_{\mathcal{K}}\leq\frac{C_{1}}{\Delta(1-A_{1})}\varepsilon_{1}+\frac{B_{1}C_{2}}{\Delta(1-A_{1})(1-A_{2})}\varepsilon_{2}.

(86)

\displaystyle\|\tilde{y}-y\|_{\mathcal{K}}\leq\frac{B_{2}C_{1}}{\Delta(1-A_{1})(1-A_{2})}\varepsilon_{1}+\frac{C_{2}}{\Delta(1-A_{2})}\varepsilon_{2}.

(87)

We get

\displaystyle\|\tilde{x}-x\|_{\mathcal{K}}+\|\tilde{y}-y\|_{\mathcal{K}}\leq\frac{C_{1}(1-A_{2})+B_{2}C_{1}}{\Delta(1-A_{1})(1-A_{2})}\varepsilon_{1}+\frac{C_{2}(1-A_{1})+B_{1}C_{2}}{\Delta(1-A_{1})(1-A_{2})}\varepsilon_{2},

(88)

for
\varepsilon=max(\varepsilon_{1};\varepsilon_{2})
and
\lambda=\displaystyle\frac{C_{1}(1-A_{2})+B_{2}C_{1}+C_{2}(1-A_{1})+B_{1}C_{2}}{\Delta(1-A_{1})(1-A_{2})}
,
we obtain

\|(\tilde{x},\tilde{y})-(x,y)\|_{\mathcal{K}\times\mathcal{K}}\leq\lambda\varepsilon.

(89)

This proves that the Hilfer coupled system
(2)
, is U-H stable.

Moreover, by setting
\varphi(\varepsilon)=\lambda\varepsilon
with
\varphi(0)=0
we get

\|(\tilde{x},\tilde{y})-(x,y)\|_{\mathcal{K}\times\mathcal{K}}\leq\varphi(\varepsilon).

(90)

This shows that the Hilfer coupled system
(2)
is G-H-U stable.
