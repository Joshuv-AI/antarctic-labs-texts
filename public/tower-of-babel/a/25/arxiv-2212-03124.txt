Morse Index Stability for Critical Points to Conformally Invariant Lagrangians
Francesca Da Lio, Matilde Gianocca, Tristan Rivière

Abstract




Abstract :
We prove the upper-semi-continuity of the Morse index plus nullity of critical points to general conformally invariant Lagrangians in dimension 2 under weak convergence. Precisely we establish that the sum of the Morse indices and the nullity of an arbitrary sequence of weakly converging critical points to a general conformally invariant Lagrangians of maps from an arbitrary closed surface into an arbitrary closed smooth manifold passes to the limit in the following sense : it is asymptotically bounded from above by the sum of the Morse indices plus the nullity of the weak limit and the bubbles, while it was well known that the sum of the Morse index of the weak limit with the Morse indices of the bubbles is asymptotically bounded from above by the Morse indices of the weakly converging sequence. The main result is then extended to the case of sequences of maps from sequences of domains degenerating to a punctured Riemann surface assuming that the lengths of the images by the maps of the collars associated to this degeneration stay below some critical length.

Keywords.
Prescribed mean curvature surfaces, minimal surfaces, Morse index theory, harmonic maps, conformally invariant variational problems.

of

in the form

The harmonic part is decomposed as follows

\mathfrak{h}_{k}=\mathfrak{h}_{k}^{+}+\mathfrak{h}_{k}^{-}+\mathfrak{h}_{k}^{0}\ \ \mbox{ where }\ \ \mathfrak{h}_{k}^{+}=\Re\sum_{n>0}h_{n,k}z^{n}\ \ ,\ \ \mathfrak{h}_{k}^{-}=\Re\sum_{n<0}h_{n,k}z^{n}\ \ \mbox{ and }\ \ \mathfrak{h}_{k}^{0}=\mathfrak{h}_{0,k}+C_{\eta,k}^{0}\,\log|z|\ .

An important fact in the proof of the main result is to prove that the “

energy quantization” is implying the following control of the constant

C_{\eta,k}^{0}=o_{\eta,k}\left(\frac{1}{\log\frac{\eta^{2}}{\delta_{k}}}\right)

II.1
The Hessian to the Dirichlet Energy :
\mathbf{D^{2}{\mathfrak{L}}}
.

The second derivative of the Dirichlet energy at harmonic maps has been computed for several decades already (see for instance
[
32
]
). More generally, the second derivative of
{\mathfrak{L}}
at critical points can be found in
[
9
]
but for normal perturbations only. These computations moreover are involving the intrinsic Riemann tensors of
{\mathcal{N}}^{n}
. We present below a general computation which involves the
extrinsic tensor given by the second fundamental form of
{\mathcal{N}}^{n}
instead and which has the advantage of being relatively easily computable for a given sub-manifold.

We first consider the case
\omega=0
that is the case of the Dirichlet energy and harmonic maps.

II.1.1
The harmonic map case.

Let
u
be an harmonic map from
(\Sigma,h)
into
{\mathcal{N}}^{n}
. We consider a smooth perturbation of
u
of the form
u_{t}=\pi_{N}(u+t\,\phi)
where
\phi
is a smooth map in
C^{\infty}(\Sigma,{\mathbb{R}}^{m})

and
\pi_{N}
is the orthogonal projection onto
{\mathcal{N}}^{n}
(which is smooth in a tubular neighborhood of
{\mathcal{N}}^{n}
). We denote

w:=\frac{du_{t}}{dt}(0)=d\pi_{u}\phi\ .

Observe that

d\pi_{z}
for
z\in{\mathcal{N}}^{n}
coincide with the matrix orthogonal projection
P_{z}
of
{\mathbb{R}}^{m}
onto
T_{z}{\mathcal{N}}^{n}
. We have moreover in
{\mathbb{R}}^{m}

d\pi_{u+t\,\phi}=d\pi_{\pi(u+t\,\phi)}
. This gives finally

\frac{du}{dt}(t)=P_{u_{t}}\phi\ .

(II.1)

which gives

\frac{d^{2}u}{dt^{2}}(0)=d_{w}P_{u}\phi\ .

(II.2)

We first compute the

\frac{dE(u_{t})}{dt}=\int_{\Sigma}du_{t}\cdot_{h}d\frac{du_{t}}{dt}\ dvol_{h}

(II.3)

The harmonicity of
u
is equivalent to

\forall\ \phi\in C^{\infty}(\Sigma,{\mathbb{R}}^{m})\quad\quad 0=\frac{dE(u)}{dt}(0)=2\,\int_{\Sigma}du\cdot_{h}d(P_{u}\phi)\ dvol_{h}=2\,\int_{\Sigma}\Delta_{h}u\cdot_{h}P_{u}\phi\ dvol_{h}

(II.4)

Using the symmetry of the matrix
P_{u}
we deduce that this is equivalent to

P_{u}\left[\Delta_{h}u\right]=0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\Delta_{h}u=-\,d[P_{u}]\cdot_{h}du

(II.5)

where we have used
P_{u}du=du
and where we recall that
\Delta_{h}
denotes the positive Laplace Beltrami operator. Combining (
II.2
) and (
II.6
) gives

\begin{array}[]{l}\displaystyle\frac{d^{2}E(u_{t})}{dt^{2}}(0)=\int_{\Sigma}d\frac{du_{t}}{dt}\cdot_{h}d\frac{du_{t}}{dt}\ dvol_{h}+\int_{\Sigma}du_{t}\cdot_{h}d\frac{d^{2}u_{t}}{dt^{2}}\ dvol_{h}\\[14.22636pt]
\displaystyle=\int_{\Sigma}dw\cdot_{h}dw\ dvol_{h}+\int_{\Sigma}\left<\Delta_{h}u,d_{w}P_{u}\phi\right>\ dvol_{h}\\[14.22636pt]
\end{array}

(II.6)

where
<\cdot,\cdot>
denotes the scalar multiplication in
{\mathbb{R}}^{m}
. Since
P_{u}[P_{u}\phi]=P_{u}\phi
taking the differential along
w
gives

d_{w}P_{u}\phi=d_{w}P_{u}w+P_{u}d_{w}P_{u}\phi\ .

(II.7)

Since
P_{u}[\Delta_{h}u]=0
we deduce

\left<\Delta_{h}u,d_{w}P_{u}\phi\right>=\left<\Delta_{h}u,d_{w}P_{u}w\right>=-\left<d[P_{u}]\cdot_{h}du,d_{w}P_{u}\,w\right>\ .

(II.8)

Combining (
II.6
) and (
II.8
) gives finally

Q_{u}(w)=\int_{\Sigma}|dw|_{h}^{2}\ dvol_{h}-\int_{\Sigma}\left<d[P_{u}]\cdot_{h}du,d_{w}P_{u}\,w\right>\ dvol_{h}

(II.9)

Recall that for any extensions in an
{\mathbb{R}}^{n}
neighborhood of a point
z\in{\mathcal{N}}^{n}
of a pair of vectors
X,Y
in
T_{z}{\mathcal{N}}^{n}
one has

{\mathbb{I}}_{z}(X,Y)=d_{Y}X-P_{z}(d_{Y}X)

Choosing an extension such that
P_{z}(X)=X
for all
z\in{\mathcal{N}}^{n}
this gives

{\mathbb{I}}_{z}(X,Y)=d_{Y}(P_{z}X)-P_{z}(d_{Y}X)=d_{Y}P_{z}X\ .

(II.10)

We finally obtain

Q_{u}(w)=\int_{\Sigma}|dw|_{h}^{2}\ dvol_{h}-\int_{\Sigma}\left<{\mathbb{I}}_{u}(du,du)_{h},{\mathbb{I}}_{u}(w,w)\right>\ dvol_{h}

(II.11)

We shall denote by
S_{u}(du)_{h}
the map from
\Sigma
into the space of symmetric matrices real
m\times m
matrices such that

\forall x\in\Sigma\quad\forall X,Y\in T_{u(x)}{\mathcal{N}}^{n}\quad\left<S_{u}(du)_{h}\,X,Y\right>=\left<{\mathbb{I}}_{u}(du,du)_{h},{\mathbb{I}}_{u}(X,Y)\right>\ .

(II.12)

Observe that with this definition one has

|S_{u}(du)_{h}|\leq C\,|du|^{2}_{h}\ .

(II.13)

where
C>0
is independent of
u
and
h
.

In the case of the sphere
{\mathcal{N}}^{n}=S^{n}
for instance one has
P_{u}=I_{n+1}-u\otimes u
which implies

dP_{u}=-\left[du\otimes u-u\otimes du\right]\quad\mbox{ and }\quad d_{w}P_{u}=-w\otimes u-u\otimes w\ .

(II.14)

This gives

d[P_{u}]\cdot_{h}du=-\,u\,|du|^{2}_{h}\quad\mbox{ and }\quad d_{w}P_{u}\,w=-u\,|w|^{2}\ .

(II.15)

Combining (
II.9
) and (
II.14

Q_{u}(w)=\int_{\Sigma}|dw|_{h}^{2}\ dvol_{h}-\int_{\Sigma}|du|^{2}_{h}\,|w|^{2}\ dvol_{h}\ .

(II.16)

\displaystyle\leq C\,\frac{\delta^{2}}{\rho^{4}\eta^{2}}\int_{A(\eta,\delta)}|\nabla\mathfrak{f}^{-}|^{2}

(D.9)

Combining (

) gives (

). This concludes the proof of lemma

Appendix E
Weighted Wente Estimates

The following lemma is giving a Wente type estimate with weight. The weight
f
in the r.h.s. of (
E.2
)might not be optimal but is sufficient for later purposes.

Lemma E.1
.

Let
\varphi
be a solution of

\left\{\begin{array}[]{l}-\Delta\varphi=\partial_{x_{1}}a\ \partial_{x_{2}}b-\partial_{x_{2}}a\ \partial_{x_{1}}b\quad\quad~~\mbox{in $B_{1}(0)$}\\[14.22636pt]
\varphi=0\quad\quad~~\mbox{on $\quad\partial B_{1}(0)$}\end{array}\right.

(E.1)

Then

\int_{B(0,1)}|x|^{2}|\nabla\varphi|^{2}\leq C\ \int_{B_{1}(0)}f(|x|)|\nabla b|^{2}\ dx^{2}\ \int_{B_{1}(0))}|\nabla a|^{2}\ dx^{2}\ ,

(E.2)

where

f(r)=r^{2}\log^{2}\left(1+\frac{1}{r}\right)\log\left(1+\log\frac{1}{r}\right)\ ,

(E.3)

and
C>0
is a universal constant.

\Box

In order to prove lemma
E.1
we need to decompose the Jacobian
\nabla^{\perp}b\cdot\nabla a
into a countable sum of Jacobians supported each on a dyadic annulus of the form
A_{k}:=B_{2^{-k}}(0)\setminus B_{2^{-k-1}}(0)
. We will thus first prove the following lemma

Lemma E.2
.

Let
b
be in
W^{1,2}(B_{1}(0),{\mathbb{R}})
. There exists
b_{k}
such that

i)

\mbox{supp}(\nabla b_{k})\ \subset A_{k}\cup A_{k+1}\

(E.4)

ii)

\nabla b=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\nabla b_{k}

(E.5)

where the convergence of the partial sum to the series has to be understood in
L^{2}

iii)

\int_{B_{1}(0)}|\nabla b_{k}|^{2}\ dx^{2}\leq C\,\int_{A_{k}\cup A_{k+1}}|\nabla b|^{2}\ dx^{2}

(E.6)

where
C
is a universal constant.

\Box

Proof of lemma
E.2
.
Let
\chi
be a smooth compactly supported function in
{\mathbb{R}}_{+}
such that

\begin{array}[]{l}\displaystyle\chi\equiv 1\quad\mbox{ in }[0,1/2]\quad\mbox{ and }\quad\chi\equiv 0\quad\mbox{ in }[1,+\infty)\end{array}

For any
\rho<1
we write
\chi_{\rho}(x):=\chi(|x|/\rho)
. With this notation we have in particular

\mbox{Supp}(\nabla\chi_{\rho})\subset B_{\rho}\setminus B_{\rho/2}\ .

For any
\rho<1/2
and any

such that supp
(\nabla c)\subset B_{2\rho}
we proceed to the following decomposition

\nabla c:=\underbrace{\nabla\bigg(\chi_{\rho}\ ({c}-\overline{c}_{\rho})\bigg)}_{\textbf{(I) }\text{supported in }B_{\rho}}+\underbrace{\nabla\bigg((1-\chi_{\rho})(c-\overline{c}_{\rho})\bigg)}_{\textbf{(II) }\text{supported in }B_{2\rho}\setminus B_{\rho/2}}.

(E.7)

where we take

\overline{c}_{\rho}:=\frac{1}{|B_{\rho}\setminus B_{\rho/2}|}\int_{B_{\rho}\setminus B_{\rho/2}}c(x)\ dx

(E.8)

Because of Poincaré inequality we have

(E.9)

We define inductively

b^{0}:=b\quad\mbox{ , }\quad b^{k}:=T_{2^{-k}}(b^{k-1})\quad\mbox{ for }k\geq 1\quad\mbox{ and }\quad b_{k}:=b^{k}-b^{k+1}\quad\mbox{ for }k\geq 0\ .

(E.10)

By definition we have

\mbox{Supp}(\nabla b^{k})\subset B_{2^{-k}}\quad\mbox{ and }\quad\nabla b^{k}=\nabla b^{k-1}=\nabla b\quad\mbox{ in }\quad B_{2^{-k-1}}\quad.

(E.11)

This gives for

\mbox{Supp}(\nabla b_{k})\subset B_{2^{-k}}\setminus B_{2^{-k-2}}

(E.12)

Combining (
E.9
) and (
E.11
) gives moreover

\begin{array}[]{l}\displaystyle\int_{B_{2^{-k}}\setminus B_{2^{-k-1}}}|\nabla b_{k}|^{2}\ dx^{2}\leq 2\,\int_{B_{2^{-k}}\setminus B_{2^{-k-1}}}|\nabla b^{k}|^{2}+|\nabla b^{k+1}|^{2}\ dx^{2}\\[14.22636pt]
\displaystyle=2\,\int_{B_{2^{-k}}\setminus B_{2^{-k-1}}}|\nabla T_{2^{-k}}(b^{k-1})|^{2}\ dx^{2}\leq C\ \int_{B_{2^{-k}}\setminus B_{2^{-k-1}}}|\nabla b^{k-1}|^{2}\ dx^{2}\\[14.22636pt]
\displaystyle=C\ \int_{B_{2^{-k}}\setminus B_{2^{-k-1}}}|\nabla b|^{2}\ dx^{2}\end{array}

(E.13)

and

\begin{array}[]{l}\displaystyle\int_{B_{2^{-k-1}}\setminus B_{2^{-k-2}}}|\nabla b_{k}|^{2}\ dx^{2}\leq 2\,\int_{B_{2^{-k-1}}\setminus B_{2^{-k-2}}}|\nabla b^{k}|^{2}+|\nabla b^{k+1}|^{2}\ dx^{2}\\[14.22636pt]
\displaystyle=2\,\int_{B_{2^{-k-1}}\setminus B_{2^{-k-2}}}|\nabla b|^{2}+|\nabla T_{2^{-k}}(b^{k})|^{2}\ dx^{2}\leq C\,\int_{B_{2^{-k-1}}\setminus B_{2^{-k-2}}}|\nabla b|^{2}+|\nabla b^{k}|^{2}\ dx^{2}\\[14.22636pt]

(E.14)

Combining (
E.12
), (
E.13
) and (

), (
E.5
) and (
E.6

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C\int_{D^{2}}|\nabla b|^{2}|x|^{2}\log^{2}\left(1+\frac{1}{|x|}\right)\ \int_{D^{2}}|\nabla a|^{2}

(E.64)

Case 4.3:

(i<k-1,j>k+2)

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}2^{-2k}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}j>k+2\\
i<k-1\end{subarray}}\int_{A_{k}}\nabla\varphi_{i}\cdot\nabla\varphi_{j}

(E.66)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}2^{-2k}\sum_{i<k-2}\bigg(\int_{A_{k}}\nabla\varphi_{i}|^{2}\bigg)^{1/2}\bigg(\sum_{j>k+2}\bigg(\int_{A_{k}}|\nabla\varphi_{j}|^{2}\bigg)^{1/2}\bigg)

(E.67)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}2^{-2k}\bigg(\sum_{i<k-2}2^{-k}2^{2i}\bigg(\int_{\tilde{A}_{i}}|\nabla b_{i}|^{2}2^{-2i}\bigg)^{1/2}\bigg)\sum_{j>k+2}2^{k}\bigg(\int_{\tilde{A}_{j}}|\nabla b_{j}|^{2}2^{-2j}\bigg)^{1/2}\int_{D^{2}}|\nabla a|^{2}

(E.68)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}2^{-2k}\bigg(\sum_{i<k-2}\frac{2^{4i}}{i^{2}}\bigg)^{1/2}\bigg(\sum_{i<k-2}\int_{\tilde{A}_{i}}|\nabla b_{i}|^{2}2^{-2i}\log^{2}(1+2^{i})\bigg)^{1/2}

(E.69)

\displaystyle\bigg(\sum_{j>k+2}\frac{1}{j^{2}}\bigg)^{1/2}\bigg(\sum_{j>k+2}\int_{\tilde{A}_{j}}|\nabla b_{j}|^{2}2^{-2j}\log^{2}(1+2^{j})\bigg)^{1/2}\int_{D^{2}}|\nabla a|^{2}

(E.70)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\bigg(\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}2^{-4k}\sum_{i<k-2}\frac{2^{4i}}{i^{2}}\bigg)^{1/2}\bigg(\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\bigg(\sum_{j>k+2}\frac{1}{j^{2}}\bigg)\sum_{j>k+2}\int_{\tilde{A}_{j}}|\nabla b_{j}|^{2}2^{-2j}\log^{2}(1+2^{j})\bigg)^{1/2}

(E.71)

\displaystyle\bigg(\sum_{i<\infty}\int_{\tilde{A}_{i}}|\nabla b_{i}|^{2}2^{-2i}\log^{2}(1+2^{i})\bigg)^{1/2}\int_{D^{2}}|\nabla a|^{2}

(E.72)

With the estimate

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\bigg(\sum_{j>k+2}\frac{1}{j^{2}}\bigg)\sum_{j>k+2}\int_{\tilde{A}_{j}}|\nabla b_{j}|^{2}2^{-2j}\log^{2}(1+2^{j})

(E.74)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{k+1}\sum_{j>k+2}\int_{\tilde{A}_{j}}|\nabla b_{j}|^{2}2^{-2j}\log^{2}(1+2^{j})

(E.75)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{j=1}^{\infty}\int_{\tilde{A}_{j}}|\nabla b_{j}|^{2}2^{-2j}\log^{2}(1+2^{j})\sum_{k=1}^{j}\frac{1}{k+1}

(E.76)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\sum_{j=1}^{\infty}\int_{\tilde{A}_{j}}|\nabla b_{j}|^{2}2^{-2j}\log^{2}(1+2^{j})\log(1+j)

(E.77)

we obtain the desired result. Collecting all the estimates above give the weighted Wente inequality
E.2
and lemma
E.1
is proved.
\Box

Appendix F
Morrey decrease of Annulus Energies for Solutions to Wente type Equation.

We start with some preliminary results.

Lemma F.1
.

Let
a,b\in W^{1,2}(B_{1}(0))
and
\varphi
be a solution of

-\Delta\varphi=\partial_{x_{1}}a\ \partial_{x_{2}}b-\partial_{x_{2}}a\ \partial_{x_{1}}b\quad\mbox{in $B_{1}(0)$}

(F.1)

Then the following estimate hold.

\displaystyle\int_{B_{\frac{1}{2}}(0)\setminus B_{\frac{1}{4}}(0)}|\nabla\varphi|^{2}\ dx^{2}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{2}{3}\int_{B_{1}(0)\setminus B_{\frac{1}{2}}(0)}|\nabla\varphi|^{2}+C\ \int_{B_{1}(0)}|\nabla a|^{2}\ dx^{2}\ \int_{B_{1}(0)}f(|x|)\ |\nabla b|^{2}\ dx^{2}

(F.2)

where
f
is given by (
E.3
) and
C>0
is a universal constant.

\Box

Proof of Lemma
F.1
.

We decompose
\varphi=\sigma+\xi
with
\sigma,

\xi

satisfying respectively

\left\{\begin{array}[]{cc}\displaystyle\Delta\sigma=0&~~\mbox{in $B_{1}(0)$}\\[14.22636pt]
\displaystyle\sigma=\varphi&~~\mbox{on $\partial B_{1}(0)$}\end{array}\right.~~\quad\mbox{and}\quad~~~\left\{\begin{array}[]{cc}\displaystyle\Delta\xi=\Delta\varphi&~~\mbox{in $B_{1}(0)$}\\[14.22636pt]
\xi=0&~~\mbox{on $\partial B_{1}(0)$}\end{array}\right.

(F.3)

Since
\sigma
is harmonic the monotonicity formula for harmonic functions gives

\displaystyle\int_{B_{\frac{1}{2}}(0)}|\nabla\sigma|^{2}\ dx^{2}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{4}\int_{B_{1}(0)}|\nabla\sigma|^{2}\ dx^{2}

(F.4)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{4}\int_{B_{1}(0)\setminus B_{\frac{1}{2}}(0)}|\nabla\sigma|^{2}\ dx^{2}+\frac{1}{4}\int_{B_{\frac{1}{2}}(0)}|\nabla\sigma|^{2}\ dx^{2}\ .

From (
F.4
) it follows that

\int_{B_{\frac{1}{2}}(0)}|\nabla\sigma|^{2}\ dx^{2}\leq\frac{1}{3}\int_{B_{1}(0)\setminus B_{\frac{1}{2}}(0)}|\nabla\sigma|^{2}\ dx^{2}

(F.5)

We also have by the Dirichlet principle that

\int_{B_{1}(0)}|\nabla\sigma|^{2}\ dx^{2}\leq\int_{B_{1}(0)}|\nabla\varphi|^{2}\ dx^{2}

(F.6)

As far as the function
\xi
is concerned we can apply the weighted Wente Lemma
E.1
and deduce that

\int_{B_{1}(0)\setminus B_{\frac{1}{4}}(0)}|\nabla\xi|^{2}\ dx^{2}\leq C\int_{B_{1}(0)}|\nabla a|^{2}\ dx^{2}\ \int_{B_{1}(0)}\ f(|x|)\ |\nabla b|^{2}\ dx^{2}\ .

(F.7)

Next we have combining the previous inequalities

\begin{array}[]{l}\displaystyle\int_{B_{\frac{1}{2}}(0)\setminus B_{\frac{1}{4}}(0)}|\nabla\varphi|^{2}\ dx^{2}=\displaystyle\int_{B_{\frac{1}{2}}(0)\setminus B_{\frac{1}{4}}(0)}|\nabla\xi+\nabla\sigma|^{2}\ dx^{2}\\[14.22636pt]
\displaystyle\quad\leq\displaystyle\frac{3}{2}\ \int_{B_{\frac{1}{2}}(0)\setminus B_{\frac{1}{4}}(0)}|\nabla\sigma|^{2}\ dx^{2}+3\,\int_{B_{\frac{1}{2}}(0)\setminus B_{\frac{1}{4}}(0)}|\nabla\xi|^{2}\ dx^{2}\\[14.22636pt]
\displaystyle\quad\leq\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\int_{B_{1}(0)\setminus B_{\frac{1}{2}}(0)}|\nabla\sigma|^{2}+3\,\int_{B_{\frac{1}{2}}(0)\setminus B_{\frac{1}{4}}(0)}|\nabla\xi|^{2}\ dx^{2}\\[14.22636pt]
\displaystyle\quad\leq\displaystyle\frac{2}{3}\int_{B_{1}(0)\setminus B_{\frac{1}{2}}(0)}|\nabla\varphi|^{2}+9\,\int_{B_{1}(0)\setminus B_{\frac{1}{4}}(0)}|\nabla\xi|^{2}\ dx^{2}\\[14.22636pt]
\displaystyle\quad\leq\displaystyle\frac{2}{3}\int_{B_{1}(0)\setminus B_{\frac{1}{2}}(0)}|\nabla\varphi|^{2}+C\ \int_{B_{1}(0)}|\nabla a|^{2}\ dx^{2}\ \int_{B_{1}(0)}\ f(|x|)\ |\nabla b|^{2}\ dx^{2}\ .\end{array}

(F.8)

This concludes the proof of lemma
F.1
.
\Box

Lemma F.2
.

Under the hypothesis of lemma
F.1
, for any
\alpha\in(0,2)
there exists
C_{\alpha}>0
such that for any
k\in{\mathbb{N}}

\displaystyle{\int_{A_{k+1}}}|\nabla\varphi|^{2}\ dx^{2}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\gamma^{k+1}{\int_{A_{0}}}|\nabla\varphi|^{2}\ dx^{2}+C_{\alpha}\ \int_{B_{1}(0)}|\nabla a|^{2}\ dx^{2}\ \sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\gamma^{|n-k|}\int_{A_{n}}|\nabla b|^{2}\ dx^{2}\ .

(F.9)

where
\gamma=\max(2^{-\alpha},\frac{2}{3})
and we are making use of the notation
A_{k}:=B_{2^{-k}}(0)\setminus B_{2^{-k-1}}(0)
.
\Box

Proof of Lemma
F.2
.
By rescaling and iterating the estimate (
F.8
) we get for any
k\geq 0

\displaystyle{\int_{A_{k+1}}}|\nabla\varphi|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{2}{3}{\int_{A_{k}}}|\nabla\varphi|^{2}+C\ \int_{B_{1}(0)}|\nabla a|^{2}\ dx^{2}\int_{B_{2^{-k}}(0)}f(2^{k}|x|)\,|\nabla b|^{2}\ dx^{2}

(F.10)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{2}{3}\left[\frac{2}{3}{\int_{A_{k-1}}}|\nabla\varphi|^{2}+C\int_{B_{1}(0)}|\nabla a|^{2}\ dx^{2}\ \int_{B_{2^{-k+1}}(0)}f(2^{k-1}|x|)\ |\nabla b|^{2}\ dx^{2}\right]

\displaystyle+

\displaystyle C\int_{B_{1}(0)}|\nabla a|^{2}\ dx^{2}\ \int_{B_{2^{-k}}(0)}f(2^{k}|x|)\ |\nabla b|^{2}\ dx^{2}

\displaystyle\ldots

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{k+1}{\int_{A_{0}}}|\nabla\varphi|^{2}+C\ \int_{B_{1}(0)}|\nabla a|^{2}\ dx^{2}\ \sum_{j=0}^{k}\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{j}\int_{B_{2^{-k+j}}(0)}\ f(2^{k-j}|x|)\ |\nabla b|^{2}\ dx^{2}\ .

We observe that
f(r)=o(r^{\alpha})
as
r\to 0
for every
\alpha\in(0,2).
We have

\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{k}\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{j}\int_{B_{2^{-k+j}}(0)}\,2^{\alpha(k-j)}|x|^{\alpha}|\nabla b|^{2}\ dx^{2}

(F.11)

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{k}\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{j}\sum_{n=k-j}^{\infty}\int_{A_{n}}2^{\alpha(k-j-n)}|\nabla b|^{2}\ dx^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{k}\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{j}2^{\alpha(k-j)}\sum_{n=k-j}^{\infty}2^{-n\alpha}\int_{A_{n}}|\nabla b|^{2}\ dx^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{i=0}^{k}\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{k-i}\ 2^{\alpha i}\ \sum_{n=i}^{\infty}2^{-n\alpha}\int_{A_{n}}|\nabla b|^{2}\ dx^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{k}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}2^{-n\alpha}\,\sum_{i=0}^{\inf(n,k)}\left(2^{\alpha}\frac{3}{2}\right)^{i}\ \int_{A_{n}}|\nabla b|^{2}\ dx^{2}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{k}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}2^{-n\alpha}\left(2^{\alpha}\frac{3}{2}\right)^{\inf(n,k)}\int_{A_{n}}|\nabla b|^{2}\ dx^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{k}\sum_{n=0}^{k}2^{-n\alpha}\left(2^{\alpha}\frac{3}{2}\right)^{n}\int_{A_{n}}|\nabla b|^{2}\ dx^{2}

\displaystyle+

\displaystyle\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{k}\sum_{n=k+1}^{\infty}2^{-n\alpha}\left(2^{\alpha}\frac{3}{2}\right)^{k}\int_{A_{n}}|\nabla b|^{2}\ dx^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{k}\left(\frac{3}{2}\right)^{n-k}\int_{A_{n}}|\nabla b|^{2}\ dx^{2}+\sum_{n=k+1}^{\infty}2^{(k-n)\alpha}\int_{A_{n}}|\nabla b|^{2}\ dx^{2}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\gamma^{|n-k|}\int_{A_{n}}|\nabla b|^{2}\ dx^{2}\ .

Then the estimate (
F.9
) holds. This concludes the proof of the Lemma
F.2
.
\Box

Appendix G
A lemma on weighted series

Lemma G.1
.

Let
\gamma\in(0,1)
. For any
s_{1}<s_{2}
in
{\mathbb{N}}
for any pair of sequences of non negative numbers
(a_{n})_{n\in{\mathbb{N}}}
and
(b_{n})_{n\in{\mathbb{N}}}
satisfying for
s_{1}\leq k\leq s_{2}

a_{k}\leq b_{k}+\varepsilon_{0}\,\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\gamma^{|n-k|}a_{n}\ .

(G.1)

for some
\varepsilon_{0}>0
. Then for any
\gamma<\mu<1
there exists
C_{\mu,\gamma}>0
such that

\displaystyle\sum_{\ell=s_{1}}^{s_{2}}\mu^{|\ell-k|}\,a_{\ell}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\sum_{\ell=s_{1}}^{s_{2}}\mu^{|\ell-k|}b_{\ell}+C_{\mu,\gamma}\ \varepsilon_{0}\ \sum_{\ell=0}^{\infty}\mu^{|\ell-k|}\,a_{\ell}

(G.2)

\Box

Proof of lemma
G.1
.

From (
G.1
) we get:

\sum_{\ell=s_{1}}^{s_{2}}\mu^{|\ell-k|}a_{\ell}\leq\sum_{\ell=s_{1}}^{s_{2}}\mu^{|\ell-k|}b_{\ell}+\varepsilon_{0}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}a_{n}\left[\sum_{\ell=s_{1}}^{s_{2}}\gamma^{|n-\ell|}\mu^{|\ell-k|}\right]

(G.3)

Case 1.

s_{1}\leq n<k

\displaystyle\sum_{\ell=s_{1}}^{s_{2}}\gamma^{|n-\ell|}\mu^{|\ell-k|}=\sum_{\ell=s_{1}}^{n}\gamma^{|n-\ell|}\mu^{|\ell-k|}

(G.4)

\displaystyle+

\displaystyle\sum_{\ell=n}^{k}\gamma^{|n-\ell|}\mu^{|\ell-k|}+\sum_{\ell=k+1}^{s_{2}}\gamma^{|n-\ell|}\mu^{|\ell-k|}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{\ell=s_{1}}^{n}\gamma^{n-\ell}\mu^{k-\ell}+\sum_{\ell=n}^{k}\gamma^{\ell-n}\mu^{k-\ell}+\sum_{\ell=k+1}^{s_{2}}\gamma^{\ell-n}\mu^{\ell-k}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\gamma^{n}\mu^{k}\sum_{\ell=s_{1}}^{n}(\mu\gamma)^{-\ell}+\gamma^{-n}\mu^{k}\sum_{\ell=n}^{k}(\mu^{-1}\gamma)^{\ell}+\gamma^{-n}\mu^{-k}\sum_{\ell=k+1}^{\infty}(\mu\gamma)^{\ell}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\left[\frac{(\mu\gamma)^{-s_{1}+n}}{1-\mu\gamma}+\frac{1}{1-\mu^{-1}\gamma}\right]\,\mu^{k-n}+\frac{\mu\gamma}{1-\mu\gamma}\,\gamma^{k-n}\leq C_{\mu,\gamma}\,\mu^{k-n}.

Case 2.

n\leq s_{1}

\displaystyle\sum_{\ell=s_{1}}^{s_{2}}\gamma^{|n-\ell|}\mu^{|\ell-k|}=\sum_{\ell=s_{1}}^{k}\gamma^{|n-\ell|}\mu^{|\ell-k|}+\sum_{\ell=k+1}^{s_{2}}\gamma^{|n-\ell|}\mu^{|\ell-k|}

(G.5)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{\ell=s_{1}}^{k}\gamma^{\ell-n}\mu^{k-\ell}+\sum_{\ell=k+1}^{s_{2}}\gamma^{\ell-n}\mu^{\ell-k}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\gamma^{-n}\mu^{k}\sum_{\ell=n}^{k}(\mu^{-1}\gamma)^{\ell}+\gamma^{-n}\mu^{-k}\sum_{\ell=k+1}^{\infty}(\mu\gamma)^{\ell}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{1-\mu^{-1}\gamma}\,\mu^{k-n}+\frac{\mu\gamma}{1-\mu\gamma}\,\gamma^{k-n}\leq C_{\mu,\gamma}\,\mu^{k-n}.

Case 3.

k\leq n\leq s_{2}

\displaystyle\sum_{\ell=s_{1}}^{s_{2}}\gamma^{|n-\ell|}\mu^{|\ell-k|}=\sum_{\ell=s_{1}}^{k}\gamma^{n-\ell}\mu^{k-\ell}+\sum_{\ell=k+1}^{n}\gamma^{n-\ell}\mu^{\ell-k}+\sum_{\ell=n+1}^{s_{2}}\gamma^{\ell-n}\mu^{\ell-k}

(G.6)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{\gamma^{n-k}}{1-\gamma\,\mu}+\frac{\mu^{n-k}}{1-\gamma\mu^{-1}}+\frac{\mu^{n-k}}{1-\gamma\,\mu}\leq C_{\mu,\gamma}\,\mu^{n-k}.

Case 4.

s_{2}\leq n

\displaystyle\sum_{\ell=s_{1}}^{s_{2}}\gamma^{|n-\ell|}\mu^{|\ell-k|}=\sum_{\ell=s_{1}}^{k}\gamma^{n-\ell}\mu^{k-\ell}+\sum_{\ell=k+1}^{s_{2}}\gamma^{n-\ell}\mu^{\ell-k}

(G.7)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{\gamma^{n-k}}{1-\gamma\,\mu}+\frac{\gamma^{n-s_{2}}\,\mu^{-k+s_{2}}}{1-\gamma\mu^{-1}}\leq C_{\mu,\gamma}\,\mu^{n-k}.

Combining all the above cases and (
G.3
) gives (
G.2
) and lemma
G.1
is proved.
\Box
