On a matrix element representation of special functions associated with toric varieties Contribution to Proceedings of the Nankai Symposium on Mathematical Dialogue 2021.
A.A. Gerasimov, D.R. Lebedev, S.V. Oblezin

Abstract
. We develop representation theory approach to the study of special functions associated with toric varieties. In particular we show that the corresponding special functions are given by matrix elements of certain non-reductive Lie algebras.



With toric manifolds and their duals one can naturally associate generalized
hypergeometric functions introduced by Gelfand-Kapranov-Zelevinski

[
GKZ
]
. These functions arise in description of quantum cohomology
of toric manifolds and its mirror dual (see e.g.
[
Ba
]
).
In this note we consider a
special instance of these functions associated with toric varieties
obtained by Hamiltonian reduction of
\mathbb{C}^{N}
(see e.g.
[
Au
]
).
Precisely let us
fix the standard symplectic structure on
\mathbb{C}^{N}

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\Omega=\frac{\imath}{2}\sum_{i=1}^{N}dz_{i}\wedge d\bar{z}_{i}=\frac{1}{2}\sum_{i=1}^{N}d|z_{i}|^{2}\wedge d{\mathop{\rm Arg}}(z_{i}),\end{array}

and consider the Hamiltonian action of the compact torus

T^{n}=(S^{1})^{n}
on
\mathbb{C}^{N}
. The corresponding toric manifold

Z=\mathbb{C}^{N}//T^{n}
is the Hamiltonian reduction under action of

T^{n}
. The torus
T^{N}=(S^{1})^{N}
and hence its
subtorus
T^{n}\subset T^{N}
act on
Z
with the following momentum
maps

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\mathbb{C}^{N}\longrightarrow{\rm Lie}(T^{N})^{*},\qquad\mathbb{C}^{N}\longrightarrow{\rm Lie}(T^{n})^{*}.\end{array}

Define a natural projection

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}m\,:\quad{\rm Lie}(T^{N})^{*}\longrightarrow{\rm Lie}(T^{n})^{*}\,,\end{array}

which is dual to the inclusion
T^{n}\subset T^{N}
. Let
\{E_{i},\,i\in I\}
indexed by
I=\{1,\ldots,N\}
be a basis in

{\rm Lie}(T^{N})^{*}
and let
\{e_{\alpha},\,{\alpha}\in A\}
indexed by
A=\{1,\ldots,n\}
be a basis in
{\rm Lie}(T^{n})^{*}
. Then we have
explicitly

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}m(E_{j})=\sum_{{\alpha}=1}^{n}e_{\alpha}m_{j}^{\alpha},\qquad j\in I\,.\end{array}

Let
\{v_{k},\,k\in\overline{A}\}
indexed by
\overline{A}=\{1,\ldots,N-n\}
be a
basis in the kernel
{\rm Ker}(m)
of (
On a matrix element representation of special functions
associated with toric varieties
):

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}v_{k}=\sum\limits_{i=1}^{N}v_{k}^{i}E_{i}\in{\rm Lie}(T^{N})^{*},\qquad\sum_{i=1}^{N}m^{\alpha}_{i}v_{k}^{i}\,=\,0,\qquad{\alpha}\in A,\quad k\in\overline{A}\,.\end{array}

Let
Z_{1},\ldots,Z_{N}
be cohomology classes dual to the coordinate
hyperplane divisors in
Z
given by intersects of the coordinate
hyperplanes in
\mathbb{C}^{N}
with
Z
. Then in the quantum cohomology

QH^{*}(Z,q)
the following relations hold (see
[
Ba
]
):

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\prod_{j=1}^{N}\,Z_{j}^{m_{j}^{\alpha}}=q_{\alpha},\qquad{\alpha}\in A,\end{array}

where
q_{\alpha}=e^{x^{\alpha}}
are quantum deformation parameters.
These relations are deformations of the classical relations
for
q_{{\alpha}}=0
corresponding to the sets of hyperplanes with zero
intersections.

The
T^{N}
-equivariant mirror dual to the toric manifold
Z
is
defined by the data
(Y_{q},\mathcal{F},\omega_{q})
where
Y_{q}
is an affine
space given by the set of solutions of (
On a matrix element representation of special functions
associated with toric varieties
),
\mathcal{F}
is
given by

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\mathcal{F}(t,\lambda)=\sum_{i=1}^{N}(-t_{i}+\imath\lambda_{i}\ln t_{i}),\qquad\omega_{q}=\prod_{{\alpha}=1}^{n}\delta(f_{\alpha})\,\frac{dt_{1}\wedge\cdots\wedge dt_{N}}{t_{1}\cdots t_{N}},\end{array}

where

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}f_{\alpha}=e^{-x^{\alpha}}\prod_{j=1}^{N}\,t_{j}^{m_{j}^{\alpha}}-1,\qquad\qquad{\alpha}\in A.\end{array}

The special function associated with these data is then given by the
following period

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\Psi_{\lambda_{1},\ldots,\lambda_{N}}(q_{1},\cdots,q_{n})=\int\limits_{\mathbb{R}^{N}_{+}}\!\!\omega_{q}\,\,\,\prod_{i=1}^{N}t_{i}^{\imath\lambda_{i}}e^{-t_{i}}\,.\end{array}

The integral representation (
On a matrix element representation of special functions
associated with toric varieties
) allows interpretation
as a matrix element of a suitable Lie algebra. Namely, consider the
semi-direct sum
\mathcal{L}_{N}
of abelian Lie algebra
(\mathfrak{gl}_{1})^{N}
and
the Heisenberg algebra of dimension
2N+1
defined by the set generators

\{\mathcal{E}_{i},\mathcal{F}_{i},\mathcal{H}_{i},\,i\in I\}
, central element
\mathcal{C}
and relations

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}[\mathcal{E}_{i},\mathcal{H}_{j}]=\delta_{ij}\mathcal{E}_{i},\qquad[\mathcal{F}_{i},\mathcal{H}_{j}]=-\delta_{ij}\mathcal{F}_{i},\qquad[\mathcal{E}_{i},\mathcal{F}_{j}]=\mathcal{C}\delta_{ij},\qquad i,j\in I.\end{array}

We fix a generic irreducible representation
\pi_{\lambda,c}
in a
suitable space of functions in
t=(t_{1},\cdots,t_{N})\in\mathbb{R}_{+}^{N}

with images of the generators in this representation given by

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}E_{i}=c\partial_{t_{i}},\qquad F_{i}=t_{i},\qquad H_{i}=t_{i}\partial_{t_{i}}+\imath\lambda_{i},\qquad C=c,\end{array}

where
\lambda_{i}\in\mathbb{R},\,\,c\in\mathbb{R}_{+}
. In the following for
convenience we take
c=1
.
Define left and right vectors by the following conditions

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}E_{i}|\psi_{R}\rangle=-|\psi_{R}\rangle,\qquad i\in I\,;\\
\sum_{i=1}^{N}v_{k}^{i}H_{i}|\psi_{L}\rangle=0,\,\,k\in\overline{A},\qquad\prod_{j=1}^{N}F_{j}^{m_{j}^{\alpha}}|\psi_{L}\rangle=|\psi_{L}\rangle,\,\,{\alpha}\in A,\end{array}

where
\{v_{k},k\in\overline{A}\}
are given by (
On a matrix element representation of special functions
associated with toric varieties
). Now introduce the
following matrix element

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\Psi_{\lambda}(e^{y}):=\Psi_{\lambda_{1},\ldots,\lambda_{N}}(e^{y^{1}},\ldots,e^{y^{N}})=\langle\psi_{L}|e^{\sum\limits_{j=1}^{N}y^{j}H_{j}}|\psi_{R}\rangle,\end{array}

with the non-degenerate pairing
\langle\,,\,\rangle
given by

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\langle a\,,\,b\rangle\,:=\,\int\limits_{\mathbb{R}_{+}^{N}}\prod_{i=1}^{N}d^{\times}t_{i}\,\,\overline{a(t)}\,b(t),\qquad d^{\times}t_{i}=\frac{dt_{i}}{t_{i}}\,.\end{array}

Theorem 1

The matrix element (
On a matrix element representation of special functions
associated with toric varieties
) allows the following
integral representation

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\Psi_{\lambda}(e^{y})=\int\limits_{\mathbb{R}_{+}^{N}}\prod_{i=1}^{N}d^{\times}t_{i}\,\prod_{{\alpha}=1}^{n}\delta\Big(e^{-\sum\limits_{j=1}^{N}y^{j}m_{j}^{\alpha}}\,\prod_{j=1}^{N}t_{j}^{m_{j}^{\alpha}}-1\Big)\,\,\prod_{j=1}^{N}t_{j}^{\imath\lambda_{j}}\,e^{-t_{j}}\,\,,\end{array}

and satisfies the equations:

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\Big\{\prod_{j=1}^{N}\prod_{k=0}^{m_{j}^{{\alpha}}-1}(\imath\lambda_{j}+k-\partial_{y^{j}})-e^{\sum\limits_{j=1}^{N}y^{j}m_{j}^{\alpha}}\Big\}\,\Psi_{\lambda}(e^{y})=0,\qquad{\alpha}\in A,\end{array}

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\sum_{j=1}^{N}v_{k}^{j}\partial_{y^{j}}\cdot\Psi_{\lambda}(e^{y})=0,\qquad k\in\overline{A}\,,\end{array}

with
v_{k},\,k\in\overline{A}
defined in (
On a matrix element representation of special functions
associated with toric varieties
).

Proof
. Explicitly for the vectors
\psi_{L,R}
we have

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}|\psi_{R}\rangle=e^{-\sum\limits_{i=1}^{N}t_{i}},\qquad|\psi_{L}\rangle=\prod_{i=1}^{N}t_{i}^{-\imath\lambda_{i}}\,\,\prod_{{\alpha}=1}^{n}\delta\Big(\prod_{j=1}^{N}t_{j}^{m_{j}^{\alpha}}-1\Big).\end{array}

Substitution of (
On a matrix element representation of special functions
associated with toric varieties
) into (
On a matrix element representation of special functions
associated with toric varieties
) after a simple
change of variables leads to (
1
).
For the second assertion, consider the following polynomials of
generators acting trivially in representation
\pi_{\lambda,c}

defined by (
On a matrix element representation of special functions
associated with toric varieties
)

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\mathcal{C}_{\alpha}=\prod_{j=1}^{N}(F_{j}E_{j})^{m_{j}^{\alpha}}-\prod_{j=1}^{N}C^{m_{j}^{{\alpha}}}(H_{j}-\imath\lambda_{j})^{m_{j}^{\alpha}},\qquad{\alpha}\in A.\end{array}

Insertion of these expressions into the matrix element
(
On a matrix element representation of special functions
associated with toric varieties
) then leads to the first set of equations (
1
).
In addition we have the following set of equations (
1
)
obtained by inserting the linear combinations
\sum\limits_{j=1}^{N}v_{k}^{j}H_{j}
into the matrix element (
On a matrix element representation of special functions
associated with toric varieties
) and taking into
account the defining relation (
On a matrix element representation of special functions
associated with toric varieties
).
\Box

Corollary 1

The matrix element (
On a matrix element representation of special functions
associated with toric varieties
) effectively depends on a
smaller number of variables
x^{\alpha}=\sum_{i=1}^{N}y^{i}m_{i}^{\alpha}
,
\alpha\in A
and allows the following representation

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\Psi_{\lambda_{1},\ldots,\lambda_{N}}(e^{x_{1}},\ldots,e^{x_{n}})=\int\limits_{\mathbb{R}_{+}^{N}}\prod_{i=1}^{N}d^{\times}t_{i}\,\prod_{{\alpha}=1}^{n}\delta\Big(e^{-x^{\alpha}}\,\prod_{j=1}^{N}t_{j}^{m_{j}^{\alpha}}-1\Big)\,\,\,\prod_{j=1}^{N}t_{j}^{\imath\lambda_{j}}\,e^{-t_{j}}\,.\end{array}

This function coincides with the mirror period
(
On a matrix element representation of special functions
associated with toric varieties
) and solves the set of equations for
{\alpha}\in A

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\Big\{\prod_{j=1}^{N}\prod_{k=0}^{m_{j}^{{\alpha}}-1}(\imath\lambda_{j}+k-\sum_{{\beta}=1}^{n}m_{j}^{\beta}\partial_{x^{\beta}})-e^{x^{\alpha}}\Big\}\,\Psi_{\lambda_{1},\ldots,\lambda_{N}}(e^{x_{1}},\ldots,e^{x_{n}})=0.\end{array}

A simple example of the proposed construction is provided by mirror
correspondence for complex projective space

Z=\mathbb{P}^{\ell}
. Projective vector space allows the standard
Hamiltonian reduction representation

\mathbb{P}^{\ell}=\mathbb{C}^{\ell+1}//S^{1}
, where the action is defined as

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}S^{1}\,:\quad\mathbb{C}^{\ell+1}\longrightarrow\mathbb{C}^{\ell+1},\qquad e^{\imath{\theta}}:\,(z_{1},\ldots,z_{\ell+1})\longmapsto(z_{1}\,e^{\imath{\theta}},\ldots,z_{\ell+1}\,e^{\imath{\theta}}).\end{array}

Therefore we have
N=\ell+1
,
n=1
and the relation matrix is given
by
||m_{j}^{\alpha}||=(1,\ldots,1)
. The resulting representation for the
mirror period (
On a matrix element representation of special functions
associated with toric varieties
) reads

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\Psi_{\lambda_{1},\ldots,\lambda_{\ell+1}}(e^{x})=\int\limits_{\mathbb{R}_{+}^{N}}\prod_{i=1}^{\ell+1}d^{\times}t_{i}\,\,\delta\Big(e^{-x}\prod_{j=1}^{\ell+1}t_{j}-1\Big)\,\,\prod_{j=1}^{\ell+1}t_{j}^{\imath\lambda_{j}}\,e^{-t_{j}}.\end{array}

By Theorem 1, (
On a matrix element representation of special functions
associated with toric varieties
) coincides with the following matrix element
of the Lie algebra

\mathcal{L}_{\ell+1}=(\mathfrak{gl}_{1})^{\ell+1}\uplus\mathcal{H}_{2(\ell+1)}^{c}
in
the generic representation (
On a matrix element representation of special functions
associated with toric varieties
):

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\Psi_{\lambda_{1},\ldots,\lambda_{\ell+1}}(e^{x})=\langle\psi_{L}|e^{x\sum\limits_{i=1}^{\ell+1}a_{i}H_{i}}|\psi_{R}\rangle,\qquad\sum_{i}a_{i}=1,\end{array}

where the left and right vectors are defined by

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}E_{i}|\psi_{R}\rangle=-|\psi_{R}\rangle,\qquad i\in\{1,\ldots,\ell+1\}\,;\\
(H_{k}-H_{\ell+1})|\psi_{L}\rangle=0,\quad k\in\{1,\ldots,\ell\},\qquad F_{1}\cdots F_{\ell+1}|\psi_{L}\rangle=|\psi_{L}\rangle.\end{array}

Particular choice of
\{a_{i},\,i\in I\}
is irrelevant due to
(
On a matrix element representation of special functions
associated with toric varieties
). The defining equations for (
On a matrix element representation of special functions
associated with toric varieties
) are obtained
by using the following operator acting by zero in the representation
(
On a matrix element representation of special functions
associated with toric varieties
):

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\mathcal{C}_{\ell+1}=\prod_{i=1}^{\ell+1}F_{i}E_{i}-C^{\ell+1}\prod_{i=1}^{\ell+1}(H_{i}-\imath\lambda_{i}).\end{array}

The resulting equation

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\Big\{\prod_{i=1}^{\ell+1}(\imath\lambda_{i}-\partial_{x})-e^{x}\Big\}\,\Psi_{\lambda}(e^{x})=0,\end{array}

is in agreement with the proper
equivariant counting of holomorphic maps of
\mathbb{P}^{1}
to
\mathbb{P}^{\ell}

(see e.g.
[
GLO
]
).
