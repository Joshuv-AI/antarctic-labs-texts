All graphs considered here are simple, connected, finite and undirected unless otherwise specified.
For any graph

A

of

’s

[
6

A graph

Crossing-critical graphs give insight into structural properties of the crossing

Let

denote the set of

Richter and Thomassen
[

Salazar
[

Lomelí and Salazar
[

showed that,

In this paper, we further improve

[

Lemma 1

Let
d_{1},d_{2},\cdots,d_{5}
be integers with

3\leq d_{1}\leq d_{2}\leq\cdots\leq d_{5}
.
Then, the following statements hold:

(1).

if
\sum_{i=1}^{3}\frac{1}{d_{i}}>\frac{1}{2}
, then
\sum_{i=1}^{2}(d_{i}-2)\leq 10
;

(2).

if
\sum_{i=1}^{4}\frac{1}{d_{i}}>1
, then
\sum_{i=1}^{3}(d_{i}-2)\leq 5
; and

(3).

if
\sum_{i=1}^{5}\frac{1}{d_{i}}>\frac{3}{2}
, then
\sum_{i=1}^{4}(d_{i}-2)\leq 4
.

\Box

Proof
.
(1) Note that the conclusion holds for

(d_{1},d_{2},d_{3})=(3,11,11)
.

Suppose that
\sum_{i=1}^{2}(d_{i}-2)\geq 11
.
Then,
d_{2}\geq 8
.
Let
d_{1}=3+\alpha_{1}
and
d_{2}=8+\alpha_{2}
, where
\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2}\geq 0

As
\sum_{i=1}^{2}(d_{i}-2)\geq 11

which implies that

a contradiction with

(2) and (3) can be proved similarly.

\Box

Proposition 1

For any planar graph
G
with
\delta(G)\geq 3
,
there exists a cycle
C
in
G

\mu(C)\leq 10

Proof
.
For any face

the weight of

\omega(f)
, is defined as follows:

\omega(f)=\sum_{v\thicksim f}\frac{1}{d_{G}(v)},

where

means that

is a vertex on the boundary of
f

We have
\omega(f)\leq\ell(f)/3
, where
\ell(f)
denotes
the length of the boundary of

|V(G)|=\sum_{f\in F(G)}\omega(f)

2|E(G)|=\sum_{f\in F(G)}\ell(f)

By Euler’s polyhedron formula,

implying that

holds for some face

.
As

,

implying that
\ell(f_{0})\leq 5
.
Let
C
denote the boundary of face

.
Since
\delta(G)\geq 3
,

must be a cycle of

.

First consider the case that
\ell(f_{0})=3
.
The inequality

,

,

(1),

In the case

\omega(f)>\ell(f_{0})/2-1

By Lemma

\Box

Theorem 1

Let
G
be a graph with
n

cr(G)\leq\frac{3sk(G)^{2}+(4n-17)sk(G)}{6}.

.
If
sk(G)=0
, then
G
is planar and
cr(G)=0
.

Now assume that
sk(G)=t>0
.
By definition, there exists a set
E_{0}
of edges in
G

with
|E_{0}|=t
such that
G\setminus E_{0}
is planar.

Let
G_{1}
denote the subgraph

and
D_{1}
be a planar drawing of

Theorem 2

Let
G
be a graph in
\mathscr{M}_{k}
with minimum degree
\delta
.
If
G
contains a cycle

with
\mu(C)=s
, then,

cr(G)\leq\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\displaystyle 2k+\frac{s-5}{2},&\mbox{if }\delta=3;\\
\displaystyle 2k-sk(G)+\frac{\delta(s-\delta+2)}{2(\delta-2)},&\mbox{if }\delta\geq 4.\end{array}\right.

Proof
.
As
\mu(C)=s
, there exists some vertex
v
in
C
such that

|E(P)|=|V(C)|-1\leq\frac{1}{\delta-2}\sum_{u\in V(C)\setminus\{v\}}(d_{G}(u)-2)=\frac{s}{\delta-2}.

Let
e
be an edge of
C
with ends
v
and
w

and
P
denote the path of
C\setminus e
. As
G
is
k
-crossing-critical,
cr(G\setminus e)\leq k-1
.
Let
D
be a drawing of
G\setminus e
with at most
k-1
crossings.
Note that edges in
P
may cross each other in the drawing

.
We regard the drawing of

as a planar graph
H
with vertices of degrees
2 and 4. Let
P^{\prime}
be a shortest path in

joining
v
and
w
. There are two ways of reconnecting

and

P^{\prime}
, one for each side of
P^{\prime}
.

Let

denotes the number of crossings of edges of
P
in
D
. It is not hard to verify that the total number of crossings in these two drawings
of

is at most

\displaystyle\lambda

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{u\in V(C)\setminus\{v,w\}}(d_{G}(u)-2)+2r_{D}(P)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle s-(\delta-2)+2r_{D}(P)

Therefore, one of the two drawings for

crosses
at most
\lambda/2
edges of
D
.
Note that
r_{D}(P)\leq k-1
,
implying that

cr(G)\leq\frac{\lambda}{2}+k-1\leq\frac{s-\delta+2}{2}+2k-2=\frac{s-\delta}{2}+2k-1.

If
\delta=3

.

Now we consider the case that

. Removing from

) of

\displaystyle cr(G)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{\lambda}{2}+k-1

(4.2)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{s-\delta+2}{2}+r_{D}(P)+k-1

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{s-\delta+2}{2}+k-1+\frac{s}{\delta-2}+k-sk(G).

The result holds.
