Extensions of the symmetry algebra and Lax representations for the two-dimensional Euler equation
Oleg I. Morozov

Abstract
. We find the twisted extensions of the symmetry algebra of the 2D Euler equation in the vorticity form and use them to construct new Lax representation for this equation. Then we generalize this result by considering the transformation Lie–Rinehart algebras generated by finite-dimensional subalgebras of the symmetry algebra and derive a family of Lax representations for the Euler equation. The family depends on functional parameters and contains a non-removable spectral parameter.

The Lax representation

The Lax representation (

In the series of recent papers

]
–

In the present paper we apply the approach of

At the special choices of the parameters the obtained Lax representations get the form (

4.1.

5.
Concluding remarks

We have shown that the technique of twisted extensions of Lie algebras combined with the transformation Lie–Rinehart algebras
is useful for constructing new Lax representations of the 2D Euler equation, including the Lax representations with functional and non-removable parameters. We note that the results of the paper provide a new example of twisted extensions of Lie algebras that are not of the Kac–Moody type. We hope that our method will be applicable to other equations with non-trivial second twisted cohomology groups of the symmetry algebras that are of importance in fluid dynamics, climate modeling, and magnetohydrodynamics.
Likewise, it is interesting to check whether the obtained Lax representations can be implemented to study the Euler equation, in particular, to derive nonlocal symmetries and conservation laws thereof, see

[
40
,
3
,
15
,
4
,
21
,
22
,
14
,
18
]
and references therein. We intend to address these issues in the future work.
