Unconstrained Massless Higher Spin Supermultiplet in AdS 4 Preprint: IPM/P-2021/001
Mojtaba Najafizadeh

Abstract
We consider unconstrained formulation of the higher spin gauge theory in anti-de Sitter (AdS) spacetime, given by actions Segal:2001qq ; Najafizadeh:2018cpu , and provide on-shell supersymmetry transformations for the 𝒩 = 1 \mathcal{N}=1 unconstrained massless higher spin supermultiplet in four-dimensional AdS 4 . Such an irreducible supermultiplet ( Φ 1 , Φ 2 , Ψ 1 , Ψ 2 ) (\mathrm{\Phi}_{1},\mathrm{\Phi}_{2}\,;\mathrm{\Psi}_{1},\mathrm{\Psi}_{2}) contains a pair of bosonic fields, with opposite parity, which are generating functions (infinite collection of totally symmetric real tensor fields of all integer rank s = 0 , 1 , … , ∞ s=0,1,\ldots,\infty ), as well as two fermionic fields, which have opposite signs of the AdS radius, that are spinorial generating functions (infinite tower of totally symmetric Majorana spinor-tensor fields of all half-integer spin s = 1 2 , 3 2 , … , ∞ s={\scriptstyle\frac{1}{2}},{\scriptstyle\frac{3}{2}},\ldots,\infty ).

To make clear simplicity of this formalism, let us focus on the Segal action

The Fronsdal action describes an arbitrary totally symmetric tensor field

•

At the level of equations of motion, the Fronsdal and Segal equations are equivalent and can be conveniently converted to each other

;

•

In the Segal action, there is a derivative of the Dirac delta function which at first glance it may seem complicated. However, it makes simple calculations due to the property

•

The Fronsdal action leads to the Euler-Lagrange equation which, in comparison to the spin-two case, is an Einstein-like equation which in turn reduces to a Ricci-like equation. However, the Euler-Lagrange equation of the Segal action directly leads to a Ricci-like equation, that is why the form of its action seems to be simpler than the Fronsdal one and consequently fewer calculations are needed.

Unconstrained higher spins in flat space

0&\mathrm{\Delta}_{-\ell}\,\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}\mathbf{\Psi}_{{}_{1}}\\
\mathbf{\Psi}_{{}_{2}}\end{pmatrix}\,+\,{\footnotesize\hbox{G.T.}}\,,

(64)

where

\displaystyle\mathrm{\Delta}_{+\ell}

\displaystyle=2\,\Big[\,\bar{\varepsilon}_{{}_{2}}\,\gamma^{a}\,\varepsilon_{{}_{1}}\,\nabla_{a}\,+\,\tfrac{\ell}{2}\,\bar{\varepsilon}_{{}_{2}}\,\gamma^{ab}\,\varepsilon_{{}_{1}}\,M_{ab}\,\Big]\,,\qquad\qquad\mathrm{\Delta}_{-\ell}=2\,\Big[\,\bar{\chi}_{{}_{2}}\,\gamma^{a}\,\chi_{{}_{1}}\,\nabla_{a}\,-\,\tfrac{\ell}{2}\,\bar{\chi}_{{}_{2}}\,\gamma^{ab}\,\chi_{{}_{1}}\,M_{ab}\,\Big]\,.

(65)

This illustrates, unlike the flat space case (
30
), if one rotates the fermionic column vector, then the SUSY algebra does not hold (recall the SUSY-like transformations). This in turn comes from the fact that here two fermionic fields
\mathbf{\Psi}_{{}_{1}},\mathbf{\Psi}_{{}_{2}}
are respectively related to two Killing spinors
\varepsilon,\chi
, having opposite sign of the AdS radius. However, by taking the flat spacetime limit (
\ell\rightarrow 0
) two Killing spinors become identical to each other
\varepsilon=\chi
, such that
(\lim_{\ell\rightarrow 0}\mathrm{\Delta}_{\pm\ell})=\mathrm{\Delta}_{0}
, and thus (
64
) results in (
30
). To be more precise, by taking the limit
\ell\rightarrow 0
, the SUSY-like transformations (
57
) will then satisfy the SUSY algebra and reduce to (
32
) with
\theta=0
, while the supersymmetry transformations (
60
) lead to (
32
) with
\theta=\pi/4
.

IV
Conclusions and outlook

In this work, we took into account the unconstrained formalism of the higher spin gauge field theory given by the bosonic
Segal:2001qq
and fermionic
Najafizadeh:2018cpu
actions in 4-dimensional flat Minkowski and anti-de Sitter spacetimes. For the reader’s convenience, we first reviewed flat spacetime case independently, which was already discussed by taking a limit from the continuous spin gauge theory in
Najafizadeh:2019mun
. The Segal supermultiplet was included a complex bosonic field and a Dirac spinor field given by the generating functions. Supersymmetry transformations (
14
) were found so as the supersymmetry action (
13
) (a sum of the complex bosonic higher spin action and the Dirac higher spin action) left invariant and the supersymmetry algebra was closed on-shell. In order to compare our results with the AdS space case, we decomposed the complex and Dirac fields and presented supersymmetry transformations (
21
),(
22
) in terms of two Majorana fields, and two real bosonic fields which have opposite parity. Employing a matrix notation, supersymmetry transformations were given in a rotated basis as well (
32a
).

Afterwards, we considered a generalization of the supersymmetric unconstrained higher spin formalism to the anti-de Sitter space case. In this case, we took into account the bosonic
Segal:2001qq
and fermionic
Najafizadeh:2018cpu
higher spin actions in 4-dimensional AdS
4
space and realized that the supermultiplet should comprise of two bosonic real higher spin fields (with opposite parity) as well as a pair of Majorana-spinor higher spin fields which have opposite sign of the AdS radius. Having opposite radii as well as Majorana Killing spinor equations (
51
), (
52
) with
\ell_{1}=-\,\ell_{2}=\ell
were necessary to find supersymmetry transformations. We illustrated that the supersymmetry action (
56
) (containing four actions) is invariant under SUSY-like transformations (
58
), but the SUSY algebra can not be closed. To close the algebra, we rotated SUSY-like transformations and obtained supersymmetry transformations (
60
) leaving invariant the supersymmetry action (
59
). The algebra closes on-shell up to spinor gauge transformations. We demonstrated that in flat spacetime (
\ell\,\rightarrow\,0
), two Killing spinors became identical and thus supersymmetry transformations (
60
) will reproduce supersymmetry transformations in a rotated basis (
32a
).

Let us briefly discuss the possible further works related to obtained results. As we mentioned, Segal formulation is one of unconstrained higher spin formalism which is local and covariant, and gave us so simple results in context of supersymmetry. There are other unconstrained higher spin formulations in the literature (see e.g.
Gates:1996xs
;
Francia:2007qt
;
Buchbinder:2007ak
;
Buchbinder:2008ss
;
Buchbinder:2007vq
;
Chekmenev:2019ayr
and references therein) that examining of their supersymmetry may be interesting. There is also a different formulation in which similar infinite sets of higher spin fields appear
Sorokin:2017irs
. Supersymmetric higher spin models constructed in hyperspace
Bandos:1999qf
;
Bandos:2004nn
;
Florakis:2014kfa
;
Florakis:2014aaa
describe
infinite-dimensional higher spin supermultiplets and thus differ from the conventional higher spin supermultiplets in this work and
Najafizadeh:2019mun
. Cubic interaction vertices for the
\mathcal{N}=1
arbitrary spin massless supermultiplets were discussed in
Metsaev:2019dqt
;
Metsaev:2019aig
and it is interesting to study such interactions using Segal formulation (see
Metsaev:2020gmb
;
Metsaev:2021bjh
for interacting massive and massless arbitrary spin fields and
\mathcal{N}=2
supermultiplets in 3d flat space). One may generalize Segal formulation to massive higher spins in which on-shell/off-shell supersymmetry transformations will probably take a simple form as the massless ones, discussed in this work. In this regards, we note that recently an off-shell description of massive supermultiplets was found for the first time for half-integer supermultiplets
Koutrolikos:2020tel
.

Appendix B
Wess-Zumino multiplet in AdS
4

In this appendix, we briefly review the Wess-Zumino model in AdS
4
in a way that is base of what we followed to find SUSY transformations in this work, and thus may be helpful for reader. The Wess-Zumino model in anti-de Sitter space was first formulated using superspace techniques in
Ivanov:1979ft
, and then studied in the framework of off-shell component formalism in
Burges:1985qq
.

Let us consider a free massless real scalar field
A(x)
, and a free massless Majorana field
\psi(x)
in a general curved background given by actions
7
7

7

Majorana spinor is real and has half as many degrees of freedom in comparison to the Dirac spinor, thus the overall factor of 1/2 compared to the Dirac action appears.

S_{A}=-\,\frac{1}{2}\int\,d^{4}x\,{\sqrt{-\,g}}\,\left(\,\nabla^{\mu}A~\nabla_{\mu}A+\,\tfrac{R}{6}\,A^{2}\right)\,,\qquad\quad S_{\psi}=-\,\frac{1}{2}\int\,d^{4}x\,{\sqrt{-\,g}}\,\left(\,\overline{\psi}\,\gamma^{\mu}\,D_{\mu}\psi\,\right)\,,

(72)

where
R
is the scalar curvature,
\nabla_{\mu}\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=\partial_{\mu}
is the “covariant derivative”,
D_{\mu}\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=\partial_{\mu}+\frac{1}{4}\,w_{\mu}^{\,~ab}\,\gamma_{ab}
is the “spinorial covariant derivative”, and
\overline{\psi}=\psi^{\dagger}\,i\,\gamma^{0}
. Now let us consider the Wess-Zumino multiplet in AdS
4
, which is the maximally symmetric solution of Einstein’s equations, in which the scalar curvature is
R=-\,12\,\ell^{\,2}
. Due to the equality of degrees of freedom in a multiplet, one should add a free massless pseudo-scalar field

, and thus the free action of the on-shell massless Wess-Zumino multiplet in anti-de Sitter space should have form

(73)

To begin our method, let us rewrite the latter action, by using integration by parts and using flat indices, in the following form

S=\frac{1}{2}\int\,d^{4}x~e\left[\,A\left(\,\square_{{}_{AdS}}+2\,\ell^{\,2}\right)A+B\left(\,\square_{{}_{AdS}}+2\,\ell^{\,2}\right)B-\,\overline{\psi}\,\gamma^{a}D_{a}\psi\,\right]\,,

(74)

where
e\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=\hbox{det}\,e^{a}_{\mu}
such that
e^{a}_{\mu}
stands for vielbein of AdS
4
space,
\square_{{}_{AdS}}\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=\nabla^{\mu}\,\nabla_{\mu}=\nabla^{a}\,\nabla_{a}+w_{a}^{~\,ab}\,\nabla_{b}
while
\nabla_{a}\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=\partial_{a}
, and

(75)

in which we have kept variation of bosonic fields which are on the left-hand-side, as well as variation of fermionic field which is in the right-hand-side, while other variations will appear in dotted terms. By this approach, we will see later that the remnant variations (denoted by the dotted terms in the latter) would be Hermitian conjugation of previous terms, which will appear in the final step. In this sense, it is enough to demonstrate that existing terms in (
75
) cancel each other by choosing suitable supersymmetry transformations. For this purpose, we consider variation of bosonic fields as ones in the flat space case
