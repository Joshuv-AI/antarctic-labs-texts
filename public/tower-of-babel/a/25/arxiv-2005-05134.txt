On oriented planar trees with three leaves
Jack Morava

Abstract
. This elementary note proposes candidates for interesting continuous piecewise-smooth ‘Riemannian’ metrics on the moduli spaces of rooted geodesic trees embedded in the Poincaré disk. A related digression observes the existence of an apparently hitherto unrecognized abelian topological group structure on the real projective line. This is an early draft. The symbol ⋆ \star indicates an illustration to be supplied in a later version.

A finite set of points on the boundary of the

not necessarily binary tree, with one point chosen as root and the rest
as endpoints of its leaves. Alternatively, the dual

The simplest nontrivial case (rooted trees with three leaves)

inherits a very nice Riemannian metric from its Kähler structure - which
is however not unique, or necessarily well-behaved with respect to permutations

0.3
The purpose of this note is to suggest that pulling back (a
variant of) the product metric along a generalized Albanese map

{\overline{M}}_{0,n+1}({\mathbb{R}})\to{\overline{M}}_{0,3+1}({\mathbb{R}})^{\binom{n}{3}}

provides the moduli space with a continuous, though only piecewise smooth,

\Sigma_{n}

of the metric geometry of these spaces to the study of such generalized
travelling salesman problems.
