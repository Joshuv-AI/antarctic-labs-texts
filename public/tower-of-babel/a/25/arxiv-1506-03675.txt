A note on the pressure of strong solutions to the Stokes system in bounded and exterior domains
Jörg Wolf

Abstract
We consider the Stokes problem in an exterior domain Ω ⊂ ℝ n \Omega\subset\mathbb{R}^{n} with an external force 𝒇 ∈ L s ( 0 , T ; 𝑾 k , r ( Ω ) ) ( k ∈ ℕ , 1 < r < ∞ ) \boldsymbol{f}\in L^{s}(0,T;\boldsymbol{W}^{k,\,r}(\Omega))\,(k\in\mathbb{N},1<r<\infty) . In the present paper we show that in contrast to 𝒖 \boldsymbol{u} the boundary regularity of the pressure can be improved according to the differentiability of 𝒇 \boldsymbol{f} up to order k k . In particular, this implies that the pressure is smooth with respect to x ∈ Ω x\in\Omega if 𝒇 \boldsymbol{f} is smooth with respect to x ∈ Ω x\in\Omega .



Keywords
Stokes equations, exterior domain, boundary regularity

Mathematics subject classification
35Q30, 76D03.

Introduction

Let
\Omega\subset\mathbb{R}^{n}\,(n\in\mathbb{N},n\geq 2)

\displaystyle=0\quad\mbox{in}\quad Q

\displaystyle\partial_{t}\boldsymbol{u}-\Delta\boldsymbol{u}

\displaystyle=-\nabla p+\boldsymbol{f}\quad\mbox{in}\quad Q,

(3)

\displaystyle\boldsymbol{u}

\displaystyle=0\quad\mbox{on}\quad\partial\Omega\times(0,T),\quad\lim_{|x|\to\infty}\boldsymbol{u}(x,\cdot)=0,

\displaystyle=0\quad\mbox{in}\quad\Omega,

denotes the unknown velocity of the fluid,

the given external force. The Stokes problem
has been extensively studied in the past. In particular, for the case

to (

4

, and
\nabla p\in\boldsymbol{L}^{p}(Q)
.
By using the semi group approach, similar results have been obtained in

[
5
]
,
[
6
]
,
[
3
]
. For the corresponding estimates on the pressure we
refer to
[

By standard arguments from the regularity theory of parabolic equations one gets the

\boldsymbol{u}

in time and space.
However, if
\boldsymbol{f}
is only smooth in

\boldsymbol{u}
is smooth in

\boldsymbol{f}\in L^{s}(0,T;\boldsymbol{W}^{k,\,q}(\Omega))

Note that our result relies essentially on the fact that the initial data is zero.
In general our result may not be true as there is a counter-example
obtained in
[
9

. More precisely, there exists an initial data, and
a solution
\boldsymbol{u},p
to the Stokes system
such that

is continuous as

, while

\displaystyle=\,\,\mbox{closure of $\mathscr{C}^{\infty}_{0,\sigma}(\Omega)$ w.r.t. the norm $\|\cdot\|_{W^{k,q}}$},

\displaystyle\|f\|_{L^{\infty}(0,T;X)}

\displaystyle=\operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{t\in(0,T)}\|f(t)\|_{X}<+\infty\quad\mbox{if}\quad q=+\infty.

Proof of Theorem 1

Proof

1^{\circ}
