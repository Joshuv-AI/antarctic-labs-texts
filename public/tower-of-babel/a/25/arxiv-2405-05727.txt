Introduction

One of the most famous open problem in number theory is the Goldbach’s conjecture, which states that any even integers can be written as the sum of two primes. Since the original conjecture is so hard, mathematicians try to consider the problem of writing a large even integer as a sum of a prime and a number with few prime factors. Let
