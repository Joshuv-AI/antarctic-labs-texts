Non-abelian extensions of relative Rota-Baxter Lie algebras and Wells type exact sequences
Qinxiu Sun, QianWen Zhu

Abstract
. In this paper, we explore non-abelian extensions of relative Rota-Baxter Lie algebras and classify the non-abelian extensions by introducing the non-abelian second cohomology group. We also study the inducibility of a pair of automorphisms about a non-abelian extension of relative Rota-Baxter Lie algebras and derive the Wells type exact sequences. Finally, we investigate the inducibility problem of pairs of derivations about an abelian extension of relative Rota-Baxter Lie algebras and give an exact sequence of Wells type.

1.
Introduction

Rota-Baxter operators were originally appeared in Baxter’s study of the fluctuation theory in probability
[
3
]
and further
was developed by Rota
[
33
]
. They play an important role in the Connes-Kreimer’s algebraic approach
[
8
]
to
the renormalization in perturbative quantum field theory. Besides, Rota-Baxter operators and
noncommutative symmetric functions and Hopf algebras have close relationship
[
42
,
16
]
.
Recently it has found there are close connections between Rota-Baxter operators and
double Poisson algebras
[
20
]
. In the context of Lie algebra,
a Rota-Baxter operator was introduced independently in the 1980s as the operator form
of the classical Yang-Baxter equation that plays important roles in both mathematics
and mathematical physics such as integrable systems and quantum groups
[
6
,
34
]
. B. A.
Kupershmidt developed a more general notion, an
\mathcal{O}
-operator on a Lie algebra (later
also called a generalized Rota-Baxter operator or a relative Rota-Baxter operator) in his
study of the classical Yang-Baxter equation and related integrable systems
[
28
]
.

\mathcal{O}
-operators were known to be helpful in providing solutions of the CYBE
in the semidirect product Lie algebra and give rise to pre-Lie algebras
larger Lie algebra
[
1
]
. Inspired by Poisson structures, Uchino

[
40
]
introduced the notion of generalized Rota-Baxter operators
on associative algebras. A relative Rota-Baxter Lie (resp. associative)
algebra is a triple consisting of a Lie (resp. associative) algebra, a representation and a relative Rota-Baxter
operator. In
[
11
,
29
]
, the authors construct a suitable algebra which characterize relative Rota-Baxter Lie
(resp. associative) algebras as its Maurer-Cartan elements. Subsequently, representations and cohomology
of relative Rota-Baxter Lie (resp. associative, Leibniz) algebras are explicitly studied in
[
10
,
9
,
25
]
.

Extensions are useful mathematical objects to understand the
underlying structures. The non-abelian extension is a relatively
general one among various extensions (e.g. central extensions,
abelian extensions, non-abelian extensions etc.). Non-abelian
extensions were first explored by Eilenberg and Maclane
[
17
]
,
which yielded to the low dimensional non-abelian cohomology group. This theory was subsequently extended to
various kinds of algebras, such as Lie (super)algebras, Lie 2-algebras, Lie Yagamuti algebras,
(relative) Rota-Baxter groups, Rota-Baxter Lie algebras and
Rota-Baxter Leibniz algebras, see
[
7
,
12
,
18
,
21
,
22
,
24
,
30
,
4
]
and references therein .
The cohomology and abelian extensions of relative Rota-Baxter Lie algebras were considered
in
[
25
]
. But the non-abelian extension
of relative Rota-Baxter Lie algebras is still absent. This is the first motivation for writing this paper.

Another interesting study related to extensions of algebraic structures is given by the inducibility (extensibility)
of pairs of automorphisms and derivations. When a pair of automorphisms is inducible? This problem was first
considered by Wells
[
41
]
for abstract groups and further studied in
[
26
,
32
]
.
Since then, several authors have studied this subject further, see
[
21
,
22
,
23
,
30
]
and references therein.
While, the extensibility problem of a pair of derivations on abelian extensions was investigated in
[
14
,
36
,
38
,
13
]
.
As byproducts, the Wells short exact sequences were obtained for
various kinds of algebras
[
12
,
14
,
21
,
22
,
23
,
26
,
30
,
36
]
, which connected the relative automorphism groups and
the non-abelian second cohomology groups. Inspired by these results, we study inducibility of a pair of automorphisms on a non-abelian
extension of relative Rota-Baxter Lie algebras and derive the analogue of the Wells short exact sequences.
This is another motivation for writing the present paper.
Moreover, we explore the inducibility of pairs of derivations and develop
the Wells exact sequences for an abelian extensions of relative Rota-Baxter Lie algebras.

The paper is organized as follows. In Section 2, we recall some basic information
about the relative Rota-Baxter Lie algebra and its cohomology theory.
In Section 3, we investigate non-abelian extensions and classify the non-abelian
extensions using the non-abelian second cohomology groups. In Section 4, we study
the inducibility problem of a pair of automorphisms about a non-abelian extension of relative Rota-Baxter Lie algebras.
In Section 5, we develop Wells type exact sequences in the context of non-abelian
extensions of relative Rota-Baxter Lie algebras. Finally,
we discuss the inducibility problem of pairs of derivations about an abelian extensions of relative Rota-Baxter Lie algebras.

k
be a field. Unless otherwise

2.
Preliminaries on relative Rota-Baxter Lie algebras

We begin with recalling some basic notions of relative Rota-Baxter Lie algebras following
[
29
,
25
]
.

A Rota-Baxter Lie algebra is a Lie algebra
(A,[\ ,\ ]_{A})
with a linear map
T:A\longrightarrow A
, satisfying

[T(x),T(y)]_{A}=T([T(x),y]_{A}+[x,T(y)]_{A}),~~\forall~x,y\in A.

Definition 2.1
.

A relative Rota-Baxter Lie algebra is a triple
((A,[\ ,\ ]_{A}),(V,\rho),T)
, where
(A,[\ ,\ ]_{A})
is a Lie algebra,
\rho:A\longrightarrow gl(V)
is a representation of
A
on the
vector space
V
and
T:V\longrightarrow A
is a relative
Rota-Baxter operator, i.e.,

[T(u),T(v)]_{A}=T(\rho(T(u))v-\rho(T(v))u),~~\forall~u,v\in V.

((A,[\ ,\ ]_{A}),(V,\rho),T)
is called abelian if
(A,[\ ,\ ]_{A})
is an abelian Lie algebra and

(V,\rho)
is a trivial representation of
(A,[\ ,\ ]_{A})
, i.e.,

[x,y]_{A}=0
and
\rho(x)v=0
for all
x,y\in A,v\in V
.

Let
((A,[\ ,\ ]_{A}),(V,\rho),T)
and
((A^{\prime},[\ ,\ ]_{A^{\prime}}),(V^{\prime},\rho^{\prime}),T^{\prime})
be two
relative Rota-Baxter Lie algebras. A homomorphism from
((A,[\ ,\ ]_{A}),(V,\rho),T)
to
((A^{\prime},[\ ,\ ]_{A^{\prime}}),(V^{\prime},\rho^{\prime}),T^{\prime})
consisting of a Lie algebra homomorphism
\varphi:A\longrightarrow A^{\prime}

and a linear map
\phi:V\longrightarrow V^{\prime}
such that

(1)

T^{\prime}\phi=\varphi T,

(2)

\phi\rho(x)(v)=\rho^{\prime}(\varphi(x))(\phi(v)),~~\forall~x\in A,v\in V.

Denote by
\mathrm{Aut}(\mathcal{A})
the set of all automorphisms of the relative Rota-Baxter Lie algebra

\mathcal{A}=((A,[\ ,\ ]_{A}),(V,\rho),T)
. It is clear that
\mathrm{Aut}(\mathcal{A})
is a group.

Definition 2.2
.

A representation of a relative Rota-Baxter Lie algebra
((A,[\ ,\ ]_{A}),(V,\rho),T)
on a 2-term complex of
vector spaces
M\xrightarrow{S}B
is a triple

(\rho_{B},\rho_{M},\mu)
, where
\rho_{B}:A\rightarrow\mathrm{gl}(B)
and
\rho_{M}:A\rightarrow\mathrm{gl}(M)
are representations of the Lie
algebra
A
on the vector spaces
B
and
M

respectively, and
\mu:V\rightarrow\mathrm{Hom}(B,M)

is a linear map such that for all
x\in A,v\in V
,

(3)

\mu(\rho(x)v)=\rho_{M}(x)\mu(v)-\mu(v)\rho_{B}(x),

(4)

\rho_{B}(T(v))S=S\rho_{M}(T(v))+S\mu(v)S.

Proposition 2.3
.

Let
\mathcal{B}=(M\xrightarrow{S}B,\rho_{B},\rho_{M},\mu)
be a representation of
a relative Rota-Baxter Lie algebra
\mathcal{A}=((A,[\ ,\ ]_{A}),(V,\rho),T)
. Then

((A\oplus B,[\ ,\ ]_{\ltimes}),(V\oplus M,\rho_{\ltimes}),T_{\ltimes})

is a relative Rota-Baxter Lie algebra, where
[\ ,\ ]_{\ltimes}
is the Lie bracket defined by

[x+a,y+b]_{\ltimes}=[x,y]_{A}+\rho_{B}(x)b-\rho_{B}(y)a,~~\forall~x,y\in A,a,b\in B,

the representation
\rho_{\ltimes}:A\oplus B\longrightarrow\mathrm{gl}(V\oplus M)
is given by

\rho_{\ltimes}(x+a)(v+m)=\rho(x)v+\rho_{M}(x)m-\mu(v)a,~~\forall~x\in A,a\in B,v\in V,m\in M,

and the relative Rota-Baxter operator
T_{\ltimes}:V\oplus M\longrightarrow A\oplus B
is defined by

T_{\ltimes}(v+m)=T(v)+S(m),~~\forall~v\in V,m\in M.

This relative Rota-Baxter Lie algebra is called the semidirect product of
\mathcal{A}=((A,[\ ,\ ]_{A}),(V,\rho),T)
and representation

\mathcal{B}=(M\xrightarrow{S}B,\rho_{B},\rho_{M},\mu)
. Denote it simply by
\mathcal{A}\ltimes\mathcal{B}
.

Definition 2.4
.

Let
\mathcal{A}=((A,[\ ,\ ]_{A}),(V,\rho),T)
be a relative Rota-Baxter Lie algebra. A derivation
d_{\mathcal{A}}=(d_{A},d_{V})
on
\mathcal{A}

consists of linear maps
d_{A}\in\mathrm{Hom}(A,A)
and
d_{V}\in\mathrm{Hom}(V,V)

such that
d_{A}
is a derivation on
A
and the following equalities are satisfied:

(5)

Td_{V}=d_{A}T,

(6)

d_{V}(\rho(x)v)=\rho(x)d_{V}(v)+\rho(d_{A}(x))v,~~\forall~x\in A,~v\in V.

Denote the set of all derivations on
\mathcal{A}=((A,[\ ,\ ]_{A}),(V,\rho),T)
by
\mathrm{Der}(\mathcal{A})
. It is easy to check that
\mathrm{Der}(\mathcal{A})
is a Lie algebra.

Next, let us recall the cohomology theory of relative Rota-Baxter Lie
algebras investigated in
[
25

Let
(M\xrightarrow{S}B,\rho_{B},\rho_{M},\mu)=\mathcal{B}
be a representation
of a relative Rota-Baxter Lie algebra

Define the
n

C^{n}(\mathcal{A},\mathcal{B})

(7)

\mathcal{C}^{n}(\mathcal{A},\mathcal{B})=\left\{\begin{aligned} &\mathrm{Hom}(A,B)\oplus\mathrm{Hom}(V,M),&n=1,\\

The coboundary

are defined respectively by

\displaystyle(\delta f)_{B}(x_{1},\cdot\cdot\cdot,x_{n+1})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{n+1}(-1)^{i+1}\rho_{B}(x_{i})f_{B}(x_{1},\cdot\cdot\cdot,\hat{x}_{i},\cdot\cdot\cdot,x_{n+1})

(9)

\displaystyle+\sum_{1\leq i<j\leq n+1}(-1)^{i+j}f_{B}([x_{i},x_{j}]_{A},x_{1},\cdot\cdot\cdot,\hat{x}_{i},\cdot\cdot\cdot,\hat{x}_{j},\cdot\cdot\cdot,x_{n+1}),

\displaystyle(\delta f)_{M}(x_{1},\cdot\cdot\cdot,x_{n},v)

\displaystyle\sum_{1\leq i<j\leq n}(-1)^{i+j}f_{M}([x_{i},x_{j}]_{A},x_{1},\cdot\cdot\cdot,\hat{x}_{i},\cdot\cdot\cdot,\hat{x}_{j},\cdot\cdot\cdot,x_{n},v)

\displaystyle-(-1)^{n-1}\mu(v)f_{B}(x_{1},\cdot\cdot\cdot,\hat{x}_{i},\cdot\cdot\cdot,x_{n})

(10)

\displaystyle(h_{T}f)(v_{1},\cdot\cdot\cdot,v_{n})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(-1)^{n}f_{B}(Tv_{1},\cdot\cdot\cdot,Tv_{n})+\sum_{i=1}^{n}(-1)^{i+1}Sf_{M}(Tv_{1},\cdot\cdot\cdot,Tv_{i-1},Tv_{i+1},\cdot\cdot\cdot,Tv_{n},v_{i}),

for any
x_{1},\cdot\cdot\cdot,x_{n}\in A
and

v_{1},\cdot\cdot\cdot,v_{n},v\in V
.

Associated to the cochain complex

(\mathcal{C}^{*}(\mathcal{A},\mathcal{B}),\mathcal{D_{R}})

n

Denote the quotient by

(13)

\mathcal{H}^{n}(\mathcal{A},\mathcal{B})=\left\{\begin{aligned} &\mathcal{Z}^{1}(\mathcal{A},\mathcal{B}),&n=1,\\
&\mathcal{Z}^{n}(\mathcal{A},\mathcal{B})/\mathcal{B}^{n}(\mathcal{A},\mathcal{B}),&n\geq 2,\end{aligned}\right.

which is called the

-cohomology group of

\mathcal{A}
with
coefficients in the representation
\mathcal{B}
.

We interpret the 1-cocycyle and 2-cocycle.

For all
\varphi=(\varphi_{B},\varphi_{M})\in\mathcal{C}^{1}(\mathcal{A},\mathcal{B})
, write

\mathcal{D_{R}}(\varphi_{B},\varphi_{M})=((\delta\varphi)_{B},(\delta\varphi)_{M},h_{T}(\varphi))

By direct calculation, for all

Thus,

(18)

Write
\mathcal{D_{R}}(f,\theta)=((\delta f)_{B},(\delta f)_{M},\partial\theta+h_{T}(f))

for all

with

By direct computations, for all

\displaystyle(\delta f)_{B}(x,y,z)=

\displaystyle\rho_{B}(x)f_{B}(y,z)+\rho_{B}(z)f_{B}(x,y)-\rho_{B}(y)f_{B}(x,z)

(19)

&(\partial\theta+h_{T}f)(v_{1},v_{2})=0,~\forall~x,y,z\in A,v,v_{1},v_{2}\in V.\end{aligned}\right.\right\}.

3.
Non-abelian
extensions and non-abelian 2-cocycles of relative Rota-Baxter Lie algebras

In this section, we are devoted to considering non-abelian
extensions and non-abelian 2-cocycles of relative Rota-Baxter Lie algebras. More precisely,
we define the non-abelian second cohomology group and show that
the non-abelian extensions are classified by the non-abelian second cohomology groups.

Definition 3.1
.

Let
((A,[\ ,\ ]_{A}),(V,\rho),T)=\mathcal{A}
and
((B,[\ ,\ ]_{B}),(M,\nu_{M}),S)=\mathcal{B}
be two
relative Rota-Baxter Lie algebras.
A non-abelian 2-cocycle on
\mathcal{A}
with values in

\mathcal{B}
is a sextuple
(\omega,\varpi,\chi,\mu,\rho_{B},\rho_{M})

of maps such that
\omega:A\otimes A\longrightarrow B,~\varpi:A\otimes V\longrightarrow M
are bilinear
and
\rho_{B}:A\longrightarrow\mathfrak{gl}(B),\rho_{M}:A\longrightarrow\mathfrak{gl}(M)~\mu:V\longrightarrow\mathrm{Hom}(B,M),~\chi:V\longrightarrow B
are linear,
and the following equations are satisfied for all

x,y,z\in A,~a\in B,~v,v_{1},v_{2}\in V,~m\in M
,

(20)

\omega(x,y)+\omega(y,x)=0,

(21)

\rho_{B}(x)\rho_{B}(y)a-\rho_{B}(y)\rho_{B}(x)a-\rho_{B}([x,y]_{A})a=[\omega(x,y),a]_{B},

(22)

\rho_{B}(x)\omega(y,z)+\rho_{B}(y)\omega(z,x)+\rho_{B}(z)\omega(x,y)=\omega([x,y]_{A},z)+\omega([y,z]_{A},x)+\omega([z,x]_{A},y),

(23)

\varpi(x,\rho(y)v)-\varpi(y,\rho(x)v)-\varpi([x,y]_{A},v)=\rho_{M}(y)\varpi(x,v)-\rho_{M}(x)\varpi(y,v)-\mu(v)\omega(x,y),

(24)

\rho_{M}(x)\rho_{M}(y)m-\rho_{M}(y)\rho_{M}(x)m=\rho_{M}([x,y]_{A})m+\nu_{M}(\omega(x,y))m,

(25)

\rho_{M}(x)\mu(v)a-\mu(\rho(x)v)a+\nu_{M}(a)\varpi(x,v)=\mu(v)\rho_{B}(x)a,

(26)

\rho_{M}(x)\nu_{M}(a)m-\nu_{M}(a)\rho_{M}(x)m=\nu_{M}(\rho_{B}(x)a)m,

(27)

\rho_{B}(Tv)S(m)+[\chi(v),S(m)]_{B}=S(\rho_{M}(Tv)m+\nu_{M}(\chi(v))m+\mu(v)S(m)),

(28)

\displaystyle\rho_{B}(Tv_{1})\chi(v_{2})-\rho_{B}(Tv_{2})\chi(v_{1})+[\chi(v_{1}),\chi(v_{2})]_{B}+\omega(Tv_{1},Tv_{2})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle S(\varpi(Tv_{1},v_{2})-\varpi(Tv_{2},v_{1}))+\chi(\rho(Tv_{1})v_{2}-\rho(Tv_{2})v_{1})+S(\mu(v_{1})\chi(v_{2})-\mu(v_{2})\chi(v_{1})).

Definition 3.2
.

Let
(\omega,\varpi,\chi,\mu,\rho_{B},\rho_{M})
and

(\omega^{\prime},\varpi^{\prime},\chi^{\prime},\mu^{\prime},\rho^{{}^{\prime}}_{B},\rho^{{}^{\prime}}_{M})
be two
non-abelian 2-cocycles on
((A,[\ ,\ ]_{A}),(V,\rho),T)=\mathcal{A}
with values in

((B,[\ ,\ ]_{B}),(M,\nu_{M}),S)=\mathcal{B}
. They are said to be equivalent if there
exist linear maps
\zeta:A\longrightarrow B
and
\eta:V\longrightarrow M

such that for all

(30)

\displaystyle\rho_{B}(x)a-\rho^{{}^{\prime}}_{B}(x)a=[\zeta(x),a]_{B},

(31)

(32)

(33)

.
By Eq. (

.
According to Eqs. (
