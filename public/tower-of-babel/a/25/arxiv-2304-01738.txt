Quantum 3 ​ j 3j -symbols for U q ​ ( 𝔰 ​ 𝔩 3 ) U_{q}(\mathfrak{sl}_{3})
Ayaz Ahmed, P. Ramadevi, Shoaib Akhtar

Abstract
We propose an algebraic expression for U q ​ ( 𝔰 ​ 𝔩 3 ) U_{q}(\mathfrak{sl}_{3}) quantum 3 ​ j 3j symbols (quantum Clebsch-Gordan coefficients) appearing in the decomposition of tensor product of symmetric representations. Our compact form will be useful to write the spectral parameter dependent R R -matrix elements for any bi-partite vertex model whose edges carry states of the symmetric representations.

Introduction

In the context of quantum theory of addition of two angular momentum, we have seen the natural appearance of Clebsch-Gordan coefficients (Wigner
3j

In the development of quantum inverse scattering method, it is seen that quantising a classical system leads to quantum deformation in some structures of the system. In the work of Kulish

[

The closed form expression for q-CG coefficients of

4
Conclusion

In this paper, we focused on the tensor product decomposition of symmetric representation (
1
) and defined the linear independent weight states within every irreducible representation
\boldsymbol{\Lambda}^{(s)}
. Using the
U_{q}(\mathfrak{sl}_{2})
q-CG for the highest weight states (
24
), we could systematically obtain an algebraic expression for
U_{q}(\mathfrak{sl}_{3})
q-CG coefficients (
3.1
) for states (black dotted) in the weight diagram.

Further, the multiple states in the inner shell of the weight diagram can be shown to be linearly independent but not orthogonal. After performing Gram-Schmidt orthogonalisations, the q-CG coefficients for those states (indicated as red, green …concentric circles) can be obtained. These q-CG coefficients
are tabulated in appendix
B
for symmetric representations whose Young diagram presentation is up to two and three boxes. Further, the Mathematica program will enable computing for other symmetric representation. This compiled data of

U_{q}(\mathfrak{sl}_{3})
q-CG coefficients is useful to obtain R-matrix elements of new
bi-partite vertex models
[
7
,
8
]
.

It is still a challenging problem to attempt q-CG for a most general tensor product decomposition

(n_{1},m_{1})\otimes(n_{2},m_{2})=\oplus_{i}(N_{i},M_{i})~,

where some of the irreducible representation may occur more than once. Unlike
(n,0)
states where every state occurs only once, the general
(n_{1},m_{1})
states will have multiplicity. We hope to pursue them in future.
