T-Model Higgs Inflation in Supergravity
Constantinos Pallis

Abstract
We focus on a simple, natural and predictive T model of inflation in Supergravity employing as inflaton the Higgs field which leads to the spontaneous breaking of a U ​ ( 1 ) B − L U(1)_{B-L} symmetry at the SUSY GUT scale. We use a renormalizable superpotential, fixed by a U ⁡ ( 1 ) U(1) R R symmetry, and a Kähler potential which parameterizes the Kähler manifold S ​ U ​ ( 2 , 1 ) / ( S ​ U ​ ( 2 ) × U ⁡ ( 1 ) ) × ( S ​ U ​ ( 2 ) / U ⁡ ( 1 ) ) SU(2,1)/(SU(2)\times U(1))\times(SU(2)/U(1)) with scalar curvature ℛ K = − 6 / N + 2 / N 0 {\cal R}_{K}=-6/N+2/{N_{0}} where 0 < N 0 < 6 0<{N_{0}}<6 . The spectral index n s n_{\rm s} turns out to be close to its present central observational value and the tensor-to-scalar ratio r r increases with N ≲ 36 N\lesssim 36 . The model can be nicely linked to MSSM offering an explanation of the magnitude of the μ \mu parameter consistently with phenomenological data. It also allows for baryogenesis via non-thermal leptogenesis with gravitino as light as 1 ​ TeV 1~{\mbox{\rm TeV}} .
