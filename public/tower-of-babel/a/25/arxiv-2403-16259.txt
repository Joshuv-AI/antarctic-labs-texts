On the effective generation of direct images of pluricanonical bundles in mixed characteristic
Hirotaka Onuki

Abstract
. We present an effective global generation result for direct images of pluricanonical bundles in mixed characteristic. This is a mixed characteristic analog of Ejiri’s theorem [ Eji24 ] in positive characteristic and the theorem of Popa and Schnell [ PS14 ] regarding their Fujita-type conjecture in characteristic zero. As an application, we establish a weak positivity statement for the relative canonical sheaf of a smooth morphism in mixed characteristic.



1.
Introduction

A special case of Fujita’s freeness conjecture states that for a globally generated ample line bundle
\mathcal{L}
on an
n
-dimensional nonsingular projective variety
X
over
\mathbb{C}
, the adjoint bundle

\omega_{X}\otimes\mathcal{L}^{l}

is globally generated for all
l\geq n+1
.
While Fujita’s freeness conjecture itself is still unsolved, this special case follows from an application of the Castelnuovo-Mumford regularity and the Kodaira vanishing theorem.
An analogous result was shown in positive characteristic by using Serre vanishing and the Frobenius morphism instead of Kodaira vanishing (see
[
Smi97
,
Har03
,
Kee08
]
).
Very recently, a mixed characteristic analog was established in
[
BMP+23
,
HLS22
]
, which relies on the recent development of commutative algebra in mixed characteristic.

Popa and Schnell
[
PS14
]
generalized the above-stated case of Fujita’s freeness conjecture to the relative setting: for a surjection
f\colon X\to Y
between nonsingular projective varieties over
\mathbb{C}
with
\dim Y=n
and a globally generated ample line bundle
\mathcal{L}
on
Y
, the direct image

f_{*}\omega_{X}^{m}\otimes\mathcal{L}^{l}

of the pluricanonical bundle is globally generated for all
m\geq 1
and
l\geq m(n+1)
.
More generally, they proved the global generation of

f_{*}\mathcal{O}_{X}(m(K_{X}+\Delta))\otimes\mathcal{L}^{l}

for a log canonical pair
(X,\Delta)
.

On the other hand, an analogous statement in positive characteristic has a counterexample with
m=1
: for the fibration
g\colon X\to\mathbb{P}^{1}
of Moret-Bailly
[
MB81
]
over an algebraically closed field of characteristic
p>0
, the sheaf

g_{*}\omega_{X}\otimes\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^{1}}(2)\cong\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^{1}}(-1)\oplus\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^{1}}(p)

is not globally generated (see also
[
SZ20
, Theorem 3.16]
).
Therefore, we need to impose some conditions to obtain a global generation result for direct images of pluricanonical bundles in positive characteristic. Ejiri
[
Eji24
,
Eji23a
]
established such a result with the assumption that
m
is sufficiently large and the canonical divisor of
X
is
f
-ample:

Theorem 1.1

(
[
Eji24
, Theorem 6.11 (1)]
,
[
Eji23a
, Theorem 3.3]
)
.

Let
k
be an algebraically closed field of characteristic
p>0
and
f\colon X\to Y
a surjection between projective varieties over
k
with
X
normal.
Let
\Delta
be an effective
\mathbb{Q}
-divisor on
X
such that
K_{X}+\Delta
is
\mathbb{Q}
-Cartier with index
r\geq 1
not divisible by
p
.
Suppose that
V\subset Y
is an open set and
U\coloneqq f^{-1}(V)
is the preimage of
V
.
Assume in addition that the following two conditions hold.

\bullet

(K_{X}+\Delta)|_{U}
is
f|_{U}
-ample.

\bullet

(U,\Delta|_{U})
is
F
-pure. This assumption is satisfied, for example, if
U
is regular,
\Supp\Delta|_{U}
is SNC, and all the coefficients of
\Delta|_{U}
are at most
1
.

Let
\mathcal{L}
be a globally generated ample line bundle on
Y
, and set
n\coloneqq\dim Y
.
Then

f_{*}\mathcal{O}_{X}(m(K_{X}+\Delta))\otimes\mathcal{L}^{l}

is globally generated over
V
for all sufficiently large multiples
m
of
r
and for all
l\geq m(n+1)
.

Our main theorem is the following mixed characteristic analog of Ejiri’s theorem.

Theorem A

(see
Theorem
5.3
and
Remark
5.4
)
.

Let
(R,{\mathfrak{m}_{R}})
be a complete DVR of mixed characteristic
(0,p)
and
f\colon X\to Y
a surjection between integral flat projective
R
-schemes with
X
normal.
Let
\Delta
be an effective
\mathbb{Q}
-divisor on
X
such that
K_{X}+\Delta
is
\mathbb{Q}
-Cartier with index
r\geq 1
.
Suppose that
V\subset Y
is an open set and
U\coloneqq f^{-1}(V)
is the preimage of
V
.
Assume in addition that the following two conditions hold.

\bullet

(K_{X}+\Delta)|_{U}
is
f|_{U}
-ample.

\bullet

For every
x\in U
,
(\widehat{\mathcal{O}_{X,x}},\Delta|_{\widehat{\mathcal{O}_{X,x}}})
is
BCM
-regular (see
Definition
3.9
for the definition) or KLT respectively if the residue characteristic of
\mathcal{O}_{X,x}
is
p
or
0
.
This assumption is satisfied, for example, if
U
is regular,
\lfloor\Delta|_{U}\rfloor=0
and
\Supp\Delta|_{U}
is SNC.

Let
\mathcal{L}
be a globally generated ample line bundle on
Y
.
Set
n
to be the dimension of the special fiber
Y_{{\mathfrak{m}_{R}}}
of
Y
.
Then

f_{*}\mathcal{O}_{X}(m(K_{X}+\Delta))\otimes\mathcal{L}^{l}

is globally generated over
V
for all sufficiently large multiples
m
of
r
and for all
l\geq m(n+1)
.

Here,
R
is assumed to be a DVR only for the simplicity of the conditions on the singularities of
X
. Indeed, we have a similar theorem when
R
is of higher dimension (see
Theorem
5.3
for the details).

We also treat the case
m=1
. We show a global generation result for
f_{*}\omega_{X}\otimes\mathcal{L}^{l}
under mild conditions on singularities (see
Corollary
4.8
for the details).
When
R
is of higher dimension, we obtain a similar result with more restrictive assumptions on singularities (see
Theorem
4.19
for the details).

1.1.
Applications

Popa and Schnell
[
PS14
]
utilized their theorem to reprove Viehweg’s weak positivity theorem
[
Vie83
]
: if
f\colon X\to Y
is a surjection between nonsingular projective varieties over
\mathbb{C}
, then

f_{*}\omega_{X/Y}^{m}

is weakly positive for every
m\geq 1
.
Building upon
[
PS14
]
, Dutta and Murayama
[
DM19
]
obtained a weak positivity theorem for log canonical pairs.
Patakfalvi
[
Pat14
]
and Ejiri
[
Eji17
]
,
[
Eji24
, Theorem 6.11 (2)]
proved a similar result in positive characteristic.

We obtain the following weak positivity result in mixed characteristic by using
Theorem
A
.

Theorem B

(
Theorem
6.3
)
.

Under the assumption of
Theorem
A
, suppose further that
X
and
Y
are regular,
f
is smooth,
\lfloor\Delta|_{U}\rfloor=0
, and the morphism
\Supp\Delta|_{U}\to V
induced by
f
is smooth.
Then
K_{X/Y}+\Delta
is weakly positive over
U
.

The smoothness conditions in
Theorem
B
are imposed to ensure that the fiber products of copies of
X
over
Y
also have only mild singularities.

Theorem
B
is enough for many applications, although it is weaker than the weak positivity of
f_{*}\mathcal{O}_{X}(m(K_{X/Y}+\Delta))
.

As a consequence of
Theorem
B
, we can show that under the assumption of
Theorem
B
, the direct image

f_{*}\mathcal{O}_{X}(m(K_{X/Y}+\Delta))\otimes\omega_{Y}\otimes\mathcal{L}^{n+1}

is globally generated over
V
(see
Proposition
6.4
).
This is a mixed characteristic analog of Fujino’s theorem
[
Fuj23
, Theorem 1.7]
in characteristic zero and Ejiri’s theorem
[
Eji24
, Theorem 1.9]
in positive characteristic.

Using
Theorem
B
, we prove a result on the smooth descent of the positivity of the anticanonical divisor in mixed characteristic, although it can also be shown without
Theorem
B
(see
Proposition
6.7
for the details).
The equal characteristic case of it was proven by
[
KMM92
, Corollary 2.9]
(see also
[
Deb01
, Corollary 3.15]
,
[
FG14
]
and
[
Eji19
]
).

1.2.
Outline of the proof

We describe the key lemma for the proof of
Theorem
A
.

Hacon, Lamarche, and Schwede
[
HLS22
]
defined the
\bm{+}
-test ideal

\tau_{+}(\mathcal{O}_{X},\Delta)\subset\mathcal{O}_{X}\text{,}

which is a mixed characteristic analog of the test ideal
\tau(\mathcal{O}_{X},\Delta)
in positive characteristic.
They obtained a useful global generation theorem involving the
\bm{+}
-test ideal
[
HLS22
, Theorem B]
.
We give a natural generalization of the
\bm{+}
-test ideal to the relative setting: for a line bundle
\mathcal{M}
on
X
, we define a subsheaf

\mathbf{B}^{0}f_{*}\left(\tau_{+}\left(\mathcal{O}_{X},\Delta\right)\otimes\mathcal{M}\right)\subset f_{*}\left(\tau_{+}\left(\mathcal{O}_{X},\Delta\right)\otimes\mathcal{M}\right)\text{,}

which is a mixed characteristic analog of the Frobenius stable direct image
S^{0}f_{*}(\sigma(X,\Delta)\otimes\mathcal{M})
in
[
HX15
]
.

The key to the proof of
Theorem
A
is the following global generation result for
\mathbf{B}^{0}f_{*}\left(\tau_{+}\left(\mathcal{O}_{X},\Delta\right)\otimes\mathcal{M}\right)
, which generalizes
[
HLS22
, Theorem B]
.

Theorem C

(
Theorem
4.3
)
.

Suppose that
f
,
X
,
Y
,
\mathcal{L}
, and
\Delta
are as in
Theorem
A
.
Let
\mathcal{M}=\mathcal{O}_{X}(M)
be a line bundle on
X
.
Suppose that
V^{\prime}\subset Y
is an open set,
U^{\prime}\coloneqq f^{-1}(V^{\prime})
is the preimage of
V^{\prime}
, and
M-K_{X}-\Delta
is semiample over
U^{\prime}
and big.
Then

\mathbf{B}^{0}f_{*}\left(\tau_{+}\left(\mathcal{O}_{X},\Delta\right)\otimes\mathcal{M}\right)\otimes\mathcal{L}^{n}

is globally generated over
V^{\prime}
.

Theorem
C
is regarded as a mixed characteristic analog of
[
Eji23b
, Theorem 1.2]
, which is a global generation theorem for the Frobenius stable direct image.

2.
Preliminaries

Throughout this article,
(R,{\mathfrak{m}_{R}})
denotes a complete Noetherian local domain of residue characteristic
p>0
.

2.1.
Duality

Let
E\coloneqq E_{R}(R/{\mathfrak{m}_{R}})
be the injective hull of the residue field. The Matlis duality functor
({-})^{\vee}\coloneqq\Hom_{R}({-},E)
gives the anti-equivalence between the Noetherian
R
-modules and the Artinian
R
-modules
[
dJ
, Tag 08Z9]
. Fix a normalized dualizing complex
\omega^{\bullet}_{R}
of
R
. For a quasi-projective
R
-scheme
\pi\colon X\to\Spec R
, define
\omega^{\bullet}_{X}\coloneqq\pi^{!}\omega^{\bullet}_{R}
and
\omega_{X}\coloneqq H^{-{\dim X}}(\omega^{\bullet}_{X})
.

2.2.
Absolute integral closures

Consider a normal integral projective
R
-scheme
X
. Fix an algebraic closure
\overline{K(X)}
of the function field
K(X)
of
X
. The
absolute integral closure
of
X
, denoted by
\nu\colon X^{+}\to X
, is the normalization of
X
in
\overline{K(X)}
. By a limit over all finite covers of
X
, we mean that over all finite covers
g\colon X^{\prime}\twoheadrightarrow X
equipped with an inclusion
K\left(X^{\prime}\right)\subset\overline{K(X)}
.

In this paper, we often consider a surjection
f\colon X\to Y
to an integral projective
R
-scheme
Y
.
We can typically assume
R=H^{0}(Y,\mathcal{O}_{Y})
, replacing
\Spec R
with the image of
Y\to\Spec R
.

2.3.
Base loci

Definition 2.1
.

Let
X
be a projective
R
-scheme and
U
an open set in
X
. A coherent sheaf
\mathcal{F}
on
X
is said to be
globally generated over
U
if the natural map
H^{0}(X,\mathcal{F})\otimes_{R}\mathcal{O}_{X}\to\mathcal{F}
is surjective on
U
.

Definition 2.2
.

Let
X
be a normal integral projective
R
-scheme and
D
a
\mathbb{Q}
-Cartier divisor on
X
with Cartier index
r
. The
stable base locus

\mathbf{B}(D)
of
D
is defined to be
\bigcap_{m\geq 1}{\Bs(mrD)}_{\mathrm{red}}
.
The
augmented base locus
and the
restricted base locus
of
D
are defined by
\mathbf{B}_{+}(D)\coloneqq\bigcap_{A}\mathbf{B}(D-A)
and
\mathbf{B}_{-}(D)\coloneqq\bigcup_{A}\mathbf{B}(D+A)
respectively, where the intersection and the union are taken over all ample
\mathbb{Q}
-divisors
A
on
X
.

Definition 2.3
.

Let
X
be a normal integral projective
R
-scheme and
U
an open subset of
X
. A
\mathbb{Q}
-Cartier divisor
D
on
X
is said to be
weakly positive over
U
if
\mathbf{B}_{-}(D)\cap U=\emptyset
.

See
[
BMP+23
, §2.5]
for the definitions of several other positivities of a divisor in mixed characteristic.

3.
\bm{+}
-test ideals and
\bm{+}
-stable direct images

The aim of this section is to extend the definition of the
\bm{+}
-test ideal by Hacon, Lamarche, and Schwede
[
HLS22
]

This extension, which we call the
\bm{+}
-stable direct image, is used in the proof of

3.1.
\bm{+}
-stable sections and
\bm{+}
-test ideals

We review the definitions of the space of the
\bm{+}
-stable sections
[
BMP+23
]
(cf.
[
TY23
]
) and the
\bm{+}
-test ideal of
[
HLS22
]

Definition 3.1

(
[
BMP+23
, Section 4]
)
.

Let
X
be a normal integral projective
R
-scheme,
M
a (Weil) divisor on
X
and
B\geq 0
a
\mathbb{Q}
-divisor on
X
.

\bullet

Define the submodule
\mathbf{B}^{0}(X,B,\mathcal{O}_{X}(K_{X}+M))\subset H^{0}(X,\mathcal{O}_{X}(K_{X}+M))
to be

\bigcap_{g\colon X^{\prime}\to X}\im\left(H^{0}\left(X^{\prime},\mathcal{O}_{X^{\prime}}\left(K_{X^{\prime}}+\lceil g^{*}(M-B)\rceil\right)\right)\to H^{0}\left(X,\mathcal{O}_{X}(K_{X}+M)\right)\right)\text{,}

where
g\colon X^{\prime}\to X
runs over all finite surjections from normal integral schemes. The sections in
\mathbf{B}^{0}\left(X,B,\mathcal{O}_{X}(K_{X}+M)\right)
are called the
\bm{+}
-stable sections
of
H^{0}(X,\mathcal{O}_{X}(K_{X}+M))
.

\bullet

Let
D=\sum_{i}D_{i}
be a reduced divisor on
X
with
D_{i}
the irreducible components. Fix an integral closed subscheme
D_{i}^{+}\subset X^{+}
lying over
D_{i}
, or equivalently, fix an integral closed subscheme
D_{i,X^{\prime}}
for each finite cover

such that for every finite cover
h\colon X^{\prime\prime}\to X^{\prime}
,
D_{i,X^{\prime\prime}}
lies over
D_{i,X^{\prime}}
. Define the submodule
\mathbf{B}^{0}_{D}(X,D+B,\mathcal{O}_{X}(K_{X}+M))\subset H^{0}(X,\mathcal{O}_{X}(K_{X}+M))
to be

\displaystyle\bigcap_{g\colon X^{\prime}\to X}\im

\displaystyle\left(\bigoplus_{i}H^{0}\left(X^{\prime},\mathcal{O}_{X^{\prime}}\left(K_{X^{\prime}}+D_{i,X^{\prime}}+\lceil g^{*}(M-B-D)\rceil\right)\right)\right.

\displaystyle\left.\to H^{0}\left(X,\mathcal{O}_{X}\left(K_{X}+M\right)\right)\right)\text{,}

where
g\colon X^{\prime}\to X
runs over all finite surjections from normal integral schemes.

Definition 3.2

(
[
HLS22
, Definition 4.3, 4.14, 4.4]
)
.

Let
X
be a normal integral projective

-scheme.

\bullet

Let

\mathbb{Q}
-divisor on

. Suppose that

\bm{+}
-test submodule

\tau_{+}(\omega_{X},B)\coloneqq\mathcal{J}_{i}\otimes\mathcal{L}^{-i}\subset\omega_{X}

.

.

\bullet

Let

\mathbb{Q}
-divisor on

\tau_{+}(\omega_{X},B)\coloneqq\tau_{+}(\omega_{X},B+H)\otimes\mathcal{O}_{X}(H)\text{,}

. This definition is independent of the choice of

.

\bullet

Let
\Delta\geq 0

-divisor on

. Define

-test ideal

.

\bullet

Let

be a
\mathbb{Q}
-divisor on

. Suppose that
\mathcal{L}
is a very ample line bundle on

\mathrm{adj}_{+}^{D}\left(\omega_{X}(D),B\right)\coloneqq\mathcal{N}\otimes\mathcal{L}^{-i}\subset\omega_{X}(D)

Definition 3.3
.

Let
X
be a normal integral projective
R
-scheme,
M
a divisor on
X
and

\mathbb{Q}
-divisor on

. Define

.

Remark 3.4

(
[
BMP+23
, Remark 4.3]
)
.

Suppose that

is not integral, but normal and equidimensional. Then we can still define the

-stable sections and the

into the connected components.

Definition 3.5

(
[
HLS22
, Section 5]
)
.

Let
U
be a normal integral quasi-projective
R
-scheme and
B
a
\mathbb{Q}
-Cartier divisor on
U
.

\bullet

Suppose that
U
is affine and

is
\mathbb{Q}
-trivial. Take

and
\overline{B}
such that

Remark 3.6
.

Let
X
be a normal integral projective
R
-scheme,
U
an open set in
X
and
\Delta\geq 0
a
\mathbb{Q}
-divisor on
X
.
Suppose that

, and

Definition 3.7

(
[
BMP+23
, Definition 6.1]
)
.

Suppose that
X
is a normal integral projective

-scheme and

is a
\mathbb{Q}
-divisor on

.
Then

Remark 3.8

(cf.
[
BMP+23
, Corollary 6.11]
)
.

If
(X,B)
is globally
\bm{+}
-regular, then
\mathbf{B}^{0}(X,B,\omega_{X}\otimes\mathcal{M})=H^{0}(X,\omega_{X}\otimes\mathcal{M})
for any line bundle
\mathcal{M}
on
X
. In particular,
\tau_{+}(\omega_{X})=\omega_{X}
. Indeed, for every finite cover
g\colon X^{\prime}\to X
, applying the functor
\sHom_{\mathcal{O}_{X}}({-},\omega_{X}\otimes\mathcal{M})
to the map
\mathcal{O}_{X}\to g_{*}\mathcal{O}_{X^{\prime}}(\lfloor g^{*}B\rfloor)
, we see that the map

H^{0}\left(X^{\prime},\mathcal{O}_{X^{\prime}}\left(K_{X^{\prime}}+\lceil g^{*}(M-B)\rceil\right)\right)\to H^{0}\left(X,\mathcal{O}_{X}(K_{X}+M)\right)

is surjective, which gives the assertion.

Definition 3.9

(
[
MS21
, Definition 6.2, Definition 6.9]
)
.

Let
(A,\mathfrak{m})
be a complete normal local domain of mixed characteristic
(0,p)
and of dimension
d
. Let
\Delta\geq 0
be a
\mathbb{Q}
-divisor on
\Spec A
such that
K_{A}+\Delta
is
\mathbb{Q}
-Cartier. We may assume that
K_{A}
is effective. We can write

for some
n>0
and
f\in A
. We say that
(A,\Delta)
is
BCM
-regular
if for every perfectoid big Cohen-Macaulay

-algebra

, the natural map

H_{\mathfrak{m}}^{d}(A)\to H_{\mathfrak{m}}^{d}(B)\stackrel{{\scriptstyle f^{1/n}}}{{\to}}H_{\mathfrak{m}}^{d}(B)

is injective.

Remark 3.10
.

Assume that
(R,{\mathfrak{m}_{R}})
is a complete DVR of mixed characteristic.
Let
X
be a normal integral flat quasi-projective
R
-scheme, and let
\Delta\geq 0
be a
\mathbb{Q}
-divisor on
X
such that
K_{X}+\Delta
is
\mathbb{Q}
-Cartier.
Suppose that
x\in X
is a point. Set
A\coloneqq\widehat{\mathcal{O}_{X,x}}
, the completion of
\mathcal{O}_{X,x}
at the maximal ideal. Assume either of the following.

\bullet

The residue characteristic of
A
is
p
and
(A,\Delta|_{A})
is

Lemma 3.11

Let

and nef line bundle

We follow the argument of
[

It is enough to prove the assertion in the case

We may thus replace

with its high enough power. For a coherent sheaf

Define

[

Theorem 4.3
.

We work in
Setting
3.13
. Let
B
be a
\mathbb{Q}
-Cartier divisor on
X
. Suppose that
V\subset Y
is an open set,
U\coloneqq f^{-1}(V)
, and
-B
is semiample over
U
and big. Let
\mathcal{L}
be a globally generated ample line bundle on
Y
. Then

\mathbf{B}^{0}f_{*}\tau_{+}\left(\omega_{X},B\right)\otimes\mathcal{L}^{n}

is globally generated over
V
by
\mathbf{B}^{0}(X,B,\omega_{X}\otimes f^{*}\mathcal{L}^{n})
.

Proof.

Let
j\gg 0
. We take the normalized blowup
\mu\colon X^{\prime}\to X
along
\Bs(-jB)
; we see that

\mu^{*}(-jB)=M^{\prime}+F^{\prime}\text{,}

where
M^{\prime}
is the free part and
F^{\prime}
is the fixed part. Since
-B
is big, we see that
M^{\prime}
is also big. It follows from
Corollary
4.2
that

\mathbf{B}^{0}(f\circ\mu)_{*}\tau_{+}\left(\omega_{X^{\prime}},-\frac{M^{\prime}}{j}\right)\otimes\mathcal{L}^{n}

is globally generated by
\mathbf{B}^{0}(X^{\prime},-M^{\prime}/j,\omega_{X^{\prime}}\otimes(f\circ\mu)^{*}\mathcal{L}^{n})
.
By
[
HLS22
, Proposition 3.9]
, we see that

\mathbf{B}^{0}(X^{\prime},-M^{\prime}/j,\omega_{X^{\prime}}\otimes(f\circ\mu)^{*}\mathcal{L}^{n})\subset\mathbf{B}^{0}(X,B,\omega_{X}\otimes f^{*}\mathcal{L}^{n})\text{.}

We have morphisms

\displaystyle\mathbf{B}^{0}(f\circ\mu)_{*}\tau_{+}\left(\omega_{X^{\prime}},-\frac{M^{\prime}}{j}\right)\otimes\mathcal{L}^{n}

\displaystyle\hookrightarrow{}

\displaystyle\mathbf{B}^{0}(f\circ\mu)_{*}\tau_{+}\left(\omega_{X^{\prime}},-\frac{M^{\prime}}{j}-\frac{F^{\prime}}{j}\right)\otimes\mathcal{L}^{n}

\displaystyle\to{}

\displaystyle\mathbf{B}^{0}f_{*}\tau_{+}\left(\omega_{X},B\right)\otimes\mathcal{L}^{n}\text{,}

which are isomorphic over
V
by
Lemma
3.28
and
Proposition
3.27
.
Thus we obtain the assertion.
∎

We give a variant of
Proposition
4.1
based on a similar argument:

Proposition 4.4
.

Let
\mathcal{L}=\mathcal{O}_{X}(L)
be a globally generated ample line bundle on
Y
. Assume that for all
i>0
and
a\geq 0
,

\varprojlim_{g\colon X^{\prime}\to X}H^{i}\left(Y,f_{*}g_{*}\omega_{X^{\prime}}\otimes\mathcal{L}^{a+1}\right)=0\text{,}

where
g\colon X^{\prime}\to X
runs over all generically finite projective morphism from a normal integral scheme
X^{\prime}
. Then

\mathbf{B}^{0}f_{*}\tau_{+}\left(\omega_{X}\right)\otimes\mathcal{L}^{n}

is globally generated by
\mathbf{B}^{0}(X,\omega_{X}\otimes f^{*}\mathcal{L}^{n+1})
.

Proof.

The proof is similar to that of
Proposition
4.1
. Form the exact sequence

0\to\mathcal{G}_{g,n+1}\to\dots\to\mathcal{G}_{g,1}\to\mathcal{G}_{g,0}\to 0,\quad\mathcal{G}_{g,i}\coloneqq\left(f_{*}g_{*}\omega_{X^{\prime}}\otimes\mathcal{L}^{m+1-i}\right)^{{\oplus}\binom{n+1}{i}}

for every

and
m\geq n+1
. Noting the exactness of the inverse limit
[
BMP+23
, Lemma 4.10]
, we see that an argument similar to that of
Proposition
4.1
works.
∎

We observe that the vanishing in the assumption of
Proposition
4.4
holds in equal characteristic
p\geq 0
:

Remark 4.5
.

Suppose that
X
is a normal projective variety over a perfect field of characteristic
p\geq 0
.
Then for all
i>0
and
a\geq 0
,

\varprojlim_{g\colon X^{\prime}\to X}H^{i}\left(Y,f_{*}g_{*}\omega_{X^{\prime}}\otimes\mathcal{L}^{a+1}\right)=0\text{,}

where
g\colon X^{\prime}\to X
runs over all generically finite morphism from a normal projective variety
X^{\prime}
.

Proof.

It suffices to show that for every generically finite morphism
g_{1}\colon X_{1}\to X
from a normal projective variety
X_{1}
, there exists a generically finite morphism
g\colon X^{\prime}\to X_{1}
from a normal projective variety
X^{\prime}
such that

H^{i}\left(Y,f_{*}g_{1,*}g_{*}\omega_{X^{\prime}}\otimes\mathcal{L}^{a+1}\right)=0\text{.}

Replacing
X
with
X_{1}
, we may assume that
X_{1}=X
.

If
k
is of positive characteristic, then we set
g
to be the iterated Frobenius
F^{e}
(

) and obtain the equation by Serre vanishing.
If
k
is of characteristic zero, then we take a desingularization

and use Kollár’s vanishing
[
Kol86
, Theorem 2.1]
.
∎

4.1.
Global generation of
f_{*}\omega_{X}\otimes\mathcal{L}^{l}

Using

, we investigate the global generation of the direct image sheaf
f_{*}\omega_{X}\otimes\mathcal{L}^{l}
.
To this end, we also study conditions on the singularities.

Corollary 4.6
.

We work in
Setting
3.13
. Assume that
f
is generically finite and
X
is globally
\bm{+}
-regular. Let
\mathcal{L}
be a globally generated ample line bundle on
Y
. Then

f_{*}\omega_{X}\otimes\mathcal{L}^{l}

is globally generated for

.

Proof.

Since
f
is generically finite,
f^{*}\mathcal{L}
is big and semiample. The conclusion follows from

4.2
and
Remark
3.16
.
∎

Corollary 4.7
.

We work in
Setting
3.13
. Assume that
f
is generically finite. Let
\mathcal{L}
be a globally generated ample line bundle on
Y
. Define
V
to be the maximal open subset of
Y
satisfying
\mathbf{B}^{0}f_{*}\tau_{+}\left(\omega_{X}\right)|_{V}=f_{*}\tau_{+}(\omega_{X})|_{V}
. Then
V
contains the locus over which
f
is finite, and

f_{*}\tau_{+}(\omega_{X})\otimes\mathcal{L}^{l}

is globally generated over
V
for
l\geq n+1
.

Proof.

Let
X\xrightarrow{\mu}X^{\prime}\xrightarrow{g}Y
be the Stein factorization of
f
. Then we see that
\mathbf{B}^{0}f_{*}\tau_{+}\left(\omega_{X}\right)=\mathbf{B}^{0}g_{*}\tau_{+}\left(\omega_{X^{\prime}}\right)=g_{*}\tau_{+}(\omega_{X^{\prime}})
by
Proposition
3.27
.
Let
V^{\prime}\subset Y
be the locus over which
f
is finite.
Since
g_{*}\tau_{+}(\omega_{X^{\prime}})|_{V^{\prime}}=f_{*}\tau_{+}(\omega_{X})|_{V^{\prime}}
, we have
V^{\prime}\subset V
.

By
Corollary
3.22
, we have the inclusion

\mathbf{B}^{0}f_{*}\tau_{+}\left(\omega_{X}\right)\otimes\mathcal{L}^{l}\subset f_{*}\tau_{+}(\omega_{X})\otimes\mathcal{L}^{l}\text{,}

which is an isomorphism on
V
.
By
Corollary
4.2
,
\mathbf{B}^{0}f_{*}\tau_{+}\left(\omega_{X}\right)\otimes\mathcal{L}^{l}
is globally generated over
V
, and thus the assertion follows.
∎

To deduce the global generation of

For a

Proof.

We may assume that

Take a compactification of

and divisor

Lemma 4.15
.

Let
(A,\mathfrak{m})
be a
d
-dimensional Noetherian normal local ring that has a dualizing complex. Suppose that
A
has a regular finite cover
A\hookrightarrow B
. Then the following are equivalent.

(1)

H^{d}_{\mathfrak{m}}(A)\to H^{d}_{\mathfrak{m}}\left(A^{+}\right)
is injective.

(2)

H^{d}_{\mathfrak{m}}(A)\to H^{d}_{\mathfrak{m}}(B)
is injective.

(3)

\Tr\colon\omega_{B}\to\omega_{A}
is surjective.

Proof.

H^{d}_{\mathfrak{m}}(B)\to H^{d}_{\mathfrak{m}}(B^{+})\cong H^{d}_{\mathfrak{m}}(A^{+})
is injective by the direct summand theorem. Thus (1) and (2) are equivalent. By Matlis duality, (2) and (3) are equivalent.
∎

The lemma below is similar to

Lemma 4.16
.

Suppose that

\mathbf{B}^{0}_{D}(X,D+B,\omega_{X}(D)\otimes\mathcal{L})\to\mathbf{B}^{0}(D,B|_{D},\omega_{D}\otimes\mathcal{L})\text{.}

Proof.

Let
\mathcal{N}\coloneqq\mathcal{O}_{X^{+}}(\nu^{*}(B-L))

Theorem 4.18
.

Let
X
be a normal integral projective
R
-scheme. If
\mathcal{O}_{X,x}
satisfies

4.12
for every closed point
x\in X
, then
\tau_{+}(\omega_{X})=\omega_{X}

Proof.

We prove
\tau_{+}(\omega_{X})_{x}=\omega_{X,x}
for each closed point

. By assumption, there is a regular sequence

\mathcal{O}_{X,x}/(a_{1},\dots,a_{r})

4.11

.

Suppose that

. Since
\mathcal{O}_{X,x}

, by

, we have a neighborhood

of

is surjective. By

, we obtain
\Tr(g_{*}\tau_{+}(\omega_{U^{\prime}}))=\tau_{+}(\omega_{U})
. Now, we have
\tau_{+}(\omega_{U^{\prime}})=\omega_{U^{\prime}}

, Proposition 4.24]
. Therefore, we deduce that

\tau_{+}(\omega_{U})=\Tr(g_{*}\tau_{+}(\omega_{U^{\prime}}))=\Tr(g_{*}\omega_{U^{\prime}})=\omega_{U}\text{.}

\tau_{+}(\omega_{X})_{x}=\omega_{X,x}
.

Suppose that

. Let

be a sufficiently small affine neighborhood of

. Then we see that

is a normal prime divisor. Set
\overline{D}
to be the closure of

in
X
. Replacing
X
with a blowup of it, we may assume that
\overline{D}
is also a normal prime divisor. Indeed, the normalization morphism
{\overline{D}}^{\nu}\to\overline{D}
is the blowup along a subscheme

of
\overline{D}
, and the blowup of

along

is the desired replacement. We obtain

\im\left(\tau_{+}\left(\omega_{X},-\overline{D}\right)\to\omega_{\overline{D}}\right)\supseteq\tau_{+}\left(\omega_{\overline{D}}\right)

from

.
Therefore, since

by the induction hypothesis, we deduce that

\im(\tau_{+}(\omega_{U})\otimes\mathcal{O}_{U}(D)\to\omega_{D})_{x}=\im(\tau_{+}(\omega_{U},-D)\to\omega_{D})_{x}=\omega_{D,x}\text{.}

Now, since

(\omega_{U}(D)\otimes\mathcal{O}_{D})^{**}\cong\omega_{D}\text{.}

Since
\omega_{U}

(S_{2})

{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces(\tau_{+}(\omega_{U})\otimes\mathcal{O}_{U}(D))_{x}}

{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\omega_{D,x}}

l\gg 0
,

\mathbf{B}_{+}\left(\frac{1}{l}(K_{X}+\Delta)+f^{*}K_{Y}\right)\cap U=\emptyset\text{.}

Therefore we deduce that

\mathbf{B}_{+}\left((K_{X/Y}+\Delta)+\left(\frac{1}{l}(K_{X}+\Delta)+f^{*}K_{Y}\right)\right)\cap U=\emptyset\text{.}

The left-hand side equals
\mathbf{B}_{+}(K_{X}+\Delta)\cap U
, and the assertion follows.
∎

Remark 6.6
.

By
Proposition
6.5
, if
K_{Y}
is big, then
K_{X}+\Delta
is big.
This is regarded as a special case of the Iitaka conjecture in mixed characteristic.

However, this assertion can readily be seen from the analogous result in characteristic zero (see, for example,
[
KP17
, Theorem 1.2]
) since bigness can be checked on the generic fiber over
R
.
The interest of
Proposition
6.5
is that it applies also to points of positive characteristic.

We prove that images of Fano schemes under smooth morphisms are again Fano.

Proposition 6.7
.

Suppose that
f\colon X\to Y
is a smooth surjection between regular integral projective
R
-schemes. Let
N
be a
\mathbb{Q}
-divisor on
Y
. Then we have the following.

(1)

If
-K_{X}-f^{*}N
is nef, so is
-K_{Y}-N
.

(2)

If
-K_{X}-f^{*}N
is ample, so is
-K_{Y}-N
.

We present two proofs of
Proposition
6.7
: one is based on
Theorem
6.3
, and the other relies on a result in positive characteristic.
First, we give a lemma for the former proof.

Lemma 6.8
.

Let
f\colon X\to Y
be a surjection between normal integral projective
R
-schemes. Suppose that
f
is smooth over a neighborhood of a closed point
y\in Y
. Let
\mathcal{A}
be an ample line bundle over
Y
.

Then for every
m\gg 0
, there is an effective divisor
D\in\left\lvert\mathcal{A}^{m}\right\rvert
such that
\Supp D
contains no components of
{X}_{y}
and
D\hookrightarrow X\to Y
is smooth over a neighborhood of
y
.

Proof.

Let
m\gg 0
. We see that the map

\alpha\colon H^{0}(X,\mathcal{A}^{m})\to H^{0}\left({X}_{y},\mathcal{A}^{m}|_{{X}_{y}}\right)

is surjective.

If the residue field
\kappa(y)
of
y
is infinite, then by the Bertini theorem, we obtain a divisor
D_{y}\in\left\lvert\mathcal{A}^{m}|_{{X}_{y}}\right\rvert
that is smooth over
\kappa(y)
. If the residue field
\kappa(y)
is finite, we can take such
D_{y}
by Poonen’s Bertini theorem
[
Poo04
]
and
m\gg 0
.

By the surjectivity of
\alpha
, we get a divisor
D
satisfying
D|_{{X}_{y}}=D_{y}
, which is the desired divisor.
∎

Proof of
Proposition
6.7
.

We show that (
1
) implies (
2
). Assume that
-K_{X}-f^{*}N
is ample. Take an ample
\mathbb{Q}
-divisor
L
on
Y
such that
-K_{X}-f^{*}N-f^{*}L
is ample. It follows from (
1
) that
-K_{Y}-N-L
is nef, and consequently that
-K_{Y}-N
is ample.

It only remains to show (
1
). Assume that
-K_{X}-f^{*}N
is nef. Suppose by contradiction that
-K_{Y}-N
is not nef. Then
f^{*}(-K_{Y}-N)
is not nef, and hence there exists a closed point
y\in f(\mathbf{B}_{-}(f^{*}(-K_{Y}-N)))
. Take an ample divisor
A
on
X
. For some
0<\epsilon\ll 1
, we have

(6.8.1)

\begin{split}y&\in f(\mathbf{B}_{-}(f^{*}(-K_{Y}-N)+\epsilon A))\\
&=f(\mathbf{B}_{-}(K_{X/Y}+(-K_{X}-f^{*}N+\epsilon A)))\text{.}\end{split}

Since
-K_{X}-f^{*}N
is nef,
-K_{X}-f^{*}N+\epsilon A
is ample. From
Lemma
6.8
, it follows that for every
m\gg 0
, there exists a divisor
D
on
X
such that

D\sim_{\mathrm{lin}}m(-K_{X}-f^{*}N+\epsilon A)

and
D
is smooth over a neighborhood
V
of
y
. Set
\Delta\coloneqq D/m
.
We see that the divisor
K_{X}+\Delta\sim_{\mathbb{Q}}-f^{*}N+\epsilon A
is
f
-ample. Applying
Theorem
6.3
, we obtain

f(\mathbf{B}_{-}(K_{X/Y}+\Delta))\cap V=\emptyset\text{,}

a contradiction to
(
6.8.1
)
.
∎

Proposition
6.7

Proposition
6.7

1
) by using a similar result in positive characteristic (see, for example,
[
Deb01

) implies (

Corollary 6.9
.

Suppose that
f\colon X\to Y
is a smooth surjection between regular integral projective
R
-schemes.
If
-K_{X}
is nef and big, so is
-K_{Y}
.

Proof.

Assume that
-K_{X}
is nef and big. Then
-K_{Y}
is nef by
Proposition
6.7
. Since bigness can be checked on the generic fiber over
R
, it follows from
[
FG12
, Theorem 1.1]
that
-K_{Y}
is big.
∎
