Electromagnetic Power Radiated by an Accelerating Point Charge
Jerrold Franklin

Abstract
We derive the electromagnetic power radiated by an accelerating point charge, with the acceleration and velocity in the result being taken at the present time in the motion of the accelerating charge. This contrasts with the usual derivation which calculates the power radiated through the surface of a large sphere, and gives the radiated power in terms of the acceleration and velocity at an arbitrary retarded time.

2
Electromagnetic Power Emitted

by an Accelerating Point Charge

The electric and magnetic fields appearing in Eq. (
3

\displaystyle{\bf E(r},t)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left\{\frac{q({\bf\hat{r}}_{r}-{\bf v}_{r})}{r_{r}^{2}\gamma_{r}^{2}(1-{\bf\hat{r}}_{r}\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}{\bf v}_{r})^{3}}\right\}+\left\{\frac{{\bf\hat{r}}_{r}\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}[({\bf\hat{r}}_{r}-{\bf v}_{r})\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}{\bf a}_{r}]}{r_{r}(1-{\bf\hat{r}}_{r}\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}{\bf v}_{r})^{3}}\right\},

(4)

\displaystyle{\bf B(r},t)

\displaystyle{\bf{\hat{r}}}_{r}\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}{\bf E(r},t),

(6)

The radius vector,

The retarded radius,

{\bf r}_{r}{\bf-r=\langle v\rangle}(t_{r}-t)=-r_{r}{\bf\langle v\rangle},

t_{r}

t

3

R_{\rm rad}

R_{\rm rad}

R_{\rm rad}\rightarrow 0

2

\displaystyle{\bf a^{\prime}_{\perp}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle{\bf a_{\perp}}\gamma^{2}.

3
Conclusion

Our main conclusion is that the power radiated by an accelerating point charge
is given by

\displaystyle\frac{dW_{\rm rad}}{dt}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{2}{3}\gamma^{6}[a^{2}-({\bf v\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}a)}^{2}].

(15)

This result has the same form as Liénard’s relativistic extension of Larmor’s formula, but is given here
with all variables at the present time, and not at an arbitrary retarded time, with abitrary acceleration and velocity.
