Asymptotic profile of ground states for the Schrödinger-Poisson-Slater equation
Zeng Liu, Vitaly Moroz

Abstract
. We study the Schrödinger-Poisson-Slater equation − Δ ​ u + u + λ ⁡ ( I 2 ∗ | u | 2 ) ​ u = | u | p − 2 ​ u in ℝ 3 , -\Delta u+u+\lambda(I_{2}*|u|^{2})u=|u|^{p-2}u\quad\text{in ${\mathbb{R}}^{3}$}, where p ∈ ( 3 , 6 ) p\in(3,6) and λ > 0 \lambda>0 . By using direct variational analysis based on the comparison of the ground state energy levels, we obtain a characterization of the limit profile of the positive ground states for λ → ∞ \lambda\to\infty .

1.
Introduction

We are concerned with the asymptotic profiles of positive ground state solutions of a Schrödinger-Poisson-Slater equation

(
P_{\lambda}
)

-\Delta u+u+\lambda(I_{2}*|u|^{2})u=|u|^{p-2}u\quad\text{in ${\mathbb{R}}^{3}$},

where
p>2
and
\lambda>0
. Here
I_{2}(x):=(4\pi|x|)^{-1}
is the Riesz potential and
*
denotes the standard convolution in
{\mathbb{R}}^{3}

By a
ground state
solution of (

P
λ

weak solution

which has a minimal energy amongst all nontrivial solutions of (

P
λ

\mathcal{I}_{\lambda}(u_{0})\leq\mathcal{I}_{\lambda}(u)
for any solution

where

The energy functional

transforms after the rescaling (

\widetilde{\mathcal{I}}_{\lambda}(v):=\frac{1}{2}\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{3}}|\nabla v|^{2}dx+\frac{\lambda^{\frac{p-2}{4(3-p)}}}{2}\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{3}}|v|^{2}dx+\frac{1}{4}\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{3}}(I_{2}*|v|^{2})|v|^{2}dx-\frac{1}{p}\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{3}}|v|^{p}dx.

We denote by

\widetilde{\mathcal{I}}_{\infty}(v):=\frac{1}{2}\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{3}}|\nabla v|^{2}dx+\frac{1}{4}\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{3}}(I_{2}*|v|^{2})|v|^{2}dx-\frac{1}{p}\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{3}}|v|^{p}dx

the energy that corresponds to (

If

. Since

, small perturbation arguments in the spirit of the Lyapunov-Schmidt reduction are not directly applicable to the family

in the limit
\lambda\to\infty
. Using direct variational analysis based on the comparison of the ground state energy levels for two problems, we establish the following result.

Theorem 1.1
.

Let
3<p<6
. Then for any sequence
\{\lambda_{n}\}
with
\lambda_{n}\to\infty
as
n\to\infty
, there exists
\{\xi_{\lambda_{n}}\}\subset{\mathbb{R}}^{3}
such that the rescaled family of ground states of
(P_{\lambda})

v_{\lambda_{n}}(x):={\lambda_{n}}^{\frac{1}{2(3-p)}}u_{{\lambda_{n}}}({\lambda_{n}}^{\frac{p-2}{4(3-p)}}(x+\xi_{\lambda_{n}}))

converges in
E({\mathbb{R}}^{3})
to a positive ground state solution
\overline{v}_{\infty}
of the formal limit equation (
1.3
).
Moreover,
\lambda^{\frac{p-2}{4(3-p)}}\|v_{\lambda}\|_{2}^{2}\to 0
as
\lambda\to\infty
.

We remark that our strategy is quite different form
[
R10
]
, and follows from the ideas of
[
MM14
,
LM21
]
. In
[
R10
]
the author studied the profile of the radial global minimizer for
p\in(18/7,3)
as
\lambda\to 0
. Since for
p\in(18/7,3)
the energy functional
\widetilde{\mathcal{I}}_{\infty}
is coercive, the approach of
[
R10
]
is not applicable for
p\in(3,6)
. Note that Theorem
1.1
remains valid also for the radial ground state solutions since we can work in the radially symmetric settings step by step.

Our results do not rely and do not require the uniqueness or non-degeneracy of the ground-states of (
1.3
). We also point out that since

, it is crucial to know that the ground state solution

of the formal limit equation (

, and hence belongs to

. This plays an essential role in the comparison of ground state energy levels between (
1.2
) and (
1.3
).

Remark 1.1
.

Our method can be adapted to show that for
3<p<6
and for any sequence
\{\lambda_{n}\}
with
\lambda_{n}\to 0
as

, there exists
\{\xi_{\lambda_{n}}\}\subset{\mathbb{R}}^{3}

However, since both

and the local limit problem are well-posed in

this also can be seen via small perturbation arguments.

We begin by describing some properties of the space

Proposition 2.1

Let us define, for any

\|u\|_{E}=\Big(\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{3}}|\nabla u|^{2}dx+\Big(\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{3}}(I_{2}*|u|^{2})|u|^{2}dx\Big)^{\frac{1}{2}}\Big)^{\frac{1}{2}}.

Then,

is a norm, and
(E({\mathbb{R}}^{3}),\|\cdot\|_{E})
is a uniformly convex Banach space. Moreover,

is
dense in

and also
C^{\infty}_{0}({\mathbb{R}}^{3})
is dense in
E_{r}({\mathbb{R}}^{3})

Let us define
\phi_{u}=I_{2}*|u|^{2}

Proposition 2.2

Given a sequence

in

u_{n}\to u
in

and

Moreover,
u_{n}\rightharpoonup u
in
E({\mathbb{R}}^{3})
if and only if
u_{n}\rightharpoonup u
in
D^{1,2}({\mathbb{R}}^{3})
and
\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{3}}(I_{2}*|u|^{2})|u|^{2}dx
is bounded. In such case,

\phi_{u_{n}}\rightharpoonup\phi_{u}
in
D^{1,2}({\mathbb{R}}^{3})
.

It is also proved in

,

, and the inclusion is compact for

then we can easily check that for any

\frac{1}{2}\|u\|^{4}_{E}\leq M(u)\leq\|u\|^{2}_{E},\quad\text{if either}\ \|u\|_{E}\leq 1\quad\text{or}\ M(u)\leq 1.

Asymptotic profiles of the rescaled ground state

It is well known that in

]

is a ground state of (

) and corresponding to the rescaled minimization problem

(3.1)

m_{\lambda}=\inf_{u\in\mathscr{P}_{\lambda}}\widetilde{\mathcal{I}}_{\lambda}(u),\quad\mathscr{P}_{\lambda}:=\{u\in H^{1}(\mathbb{R}^{3})\setminus\{0\}:\mathcal{P}_{\lambda}(u)=0\},

\mathcal{P}_{\lambda}(u)=\frac{3}{2}\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{3}}|\nabla u|^{2}dx+\frac{\lambda^{\frac{p-2}{4(3-p)}}}{2}\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{3}}|u|^{2}dx+\frac{3}{4}\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{3}}(I_{2}*|u|^{2})|u|^{2}dx-\frac{2p-3}{p}\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{3}}|u|^{p}dx.

Observe that if
u\in H^{1}({\mathbb{R}}^{3})
is a critical point of
\widetilde{\mathcal{I}}_{\lambda}
, then

since
\mathcal{P}_{\lambda}(u)=0

\langle\widetilde{\mathcal{I}}^{\prime}_{\lambda}(u),u\rangle=0
and the Pohožaev type identity.
For each

, set

(3.2)

u_{t}(x):=t^{2}u(tx).

Then

(3.3)

f_{u}(t):=\widetilde{\mathcal{I}}_{\lambda}(u_{t})\\
=\frac{t^{3}}{2}\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{3}}|\nabla u|^{2}dx+\frac{\lambda^{\frac{p-2}{4(3-p)}}t}{2}\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{3}}|u|^{2}dx+\frac{t^{3}}{4}\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{3}}(I_{\alpha}*|u|^{2})|u|^{2}dx-\frac{t^{2p-3}}{p}\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{3}}|u|^{p}dx.

.

We now turn our attention to study the asymptotic profile of the rescaled ground state

. As we mentioned in the introduction, we use direct variational analysis based on the comparison of the ground state energy levels for two problems, we begin by studying the convergence of

Lemma 3.2
.

Assume that
p\in(3,6)
. Then
0<m_{\lambda}-m_{\infty}\to 0
as
\lambda\to\infty
.

Proof.

First, we use
v_{\lambda}
with
\lambda>0
as a test function for
\mathcal{P}_{\infty}
. We obtain

\mathcal{P}_{\infty}(v_{\lambda})=\mathcal{P}_{\lambda}(v_{\lambda})-\frac{\lambda^{\frac{p-2}{4(3-p)}}}{2}\|v_{\lambda}\|_{2}^{2}=-\frac{\lambda^{\frac{p-2}{4(3-p)}}}{2}\|v_{\lambda}\|_{2}^{2}<0.

Hence there exists a unique
t_{\lambda}\in(0,1)
such that
t^{2}_{\lambda}v_{\lambda}(t_{\lambda}x)\in\mathscr{P}_{\infty}
, and we have

(3.5)

m_{\infty}\leq\widetilde{\mathcal{I}}_{\infty}(t^{2}_{\lambda}v_{\lambda}(t_{\lambda}x))=\frac{t_{\lambda}^{3}(p-3)}{2p-3}\|\nabla v_{\lambda}\|_{2}^{2}+\frac{(p-3)t_{\lambda}^{3}}{2(2p-3)}\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{3}}(I_{2}*|v_{\lambda}|^{2})|v_{\lambda}|^{2}dx<\widetilde{\mathcal{I}}_{\lambda}(v_{\lambda}(x))=m_{\lambda},

which means
m_{\infty}<m_{\lambda}
.

To show that
m_{\lambda}\to m_{\infty}
as
\lambda\to\infty
we shall use
v_{\infty}
as a test function for
\mathcal{P}_{\lambda}
. Note that the ground state
v_{\infty}
of (
1.3
) has an exponential decay at infinity, hence
v_{\infty}\in L^{2}({\mathbb{R}}^{3})
(see Remark
3.1
).

Since
\mathcal{P}_{\lambda}(v_{\infty})=\frac{\lambda^{\frac{p-2}{4(3-p)}}}{2}\|v_{\infty}\|_{2}^{2}>0
, there exists
\overline{t}_{\lambda}>1
such that
\overline{t}^{2}_{\lambda}v_{\lambda}(\overline{t}_{\lambda}x)\in\mathscr{P}_{\lambda}
, i.e.,

\frac{3\overline{t}_{\lambda}^{3}}{2}\|\nabla v_{\infty}\|_{2}^{2}+\frac{\lambda^{\frac{p-2}{4(3-p)}}\overline{t}_{\lambda}}{2}\|v_{\infty}\|^{2}_{2}+\frac{3\overline{t}_{{\varepsilon}}^{3}}{4}\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{3}}(I_{\alpha}*|v_{\infty}|^{2})|v_{\infty}|^{2}dx=\frac{(2p-3)\overline{t}_{\lambda}^{2p-3}}{p}\|v_{\infty}\|_{p}^{p}.

This, combined with
\mathcal{P}_{\infty}(v_{\infty})=0
and
v_{\infty}\in L^{2}({\mathbb{R}}^{N})
, implies that

\frac{(2p-3)(\overline{t}_{\lambda}^{2p-6}-1)}{p}\|v_{\infty}\|_{p}^{p}=\frac{\lambda^{\frac{p-2}{4(3-p)}}\overline{t}^{-2}_{\lambda}}{2}\|v_{\infty}\|^{2}_{2}.

Therefore,
\overline{t}_{\lambda}\to 1
as
\lambda\to\infty
. Moreover,

\overline{t}_{\lambda}\leq 1+C\lambda^{\frac{p-2}{4(3-p)}},

where
C>0
is independent of
\lambda
.
Thus we have

\displaystyle m_{\lambda}\leq\widetilde{\mathcal{I}}_{\lambda}(t^{2}_{\lambda}v_{\lambda}(t_{\lambda}x))\leq

\displaystyle\widetilde{\mathcal{I}}_{\infty}(v_{\infty})+C(\overline{t}_{\lambda}^{3}-1)+\frac{\lambda^{\frac{p-2}{4(3-p)}}\overline{t}_{\lambda}}{2}\|v_{\infty}\|^{2}_{2}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle m_{\infty}+C\lambda^{\frac{p-2}{4(3-p)}}.

This, together with (
3.5
), means that
m_{\lambda}-m_{\infty}\to 0
as
\lambda\to\infty
.

Corollary 3.2

Let

\|\nabla v_{\lambda}\|_{2}^{2},\quad\lambda^{\frac{p-2}{4(3-p)}}\|v_{\lambda}\|_{2}^{2},\quad\|v_{\lambda}\|_{q}^{q},\quad\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{3}}(I_{2}*|v_{\lambda}|^{2})|v_{\lambda}|^{2}dx,

are uniformly bounded as
\lambda\to\infty

Proof.

From

and Lemma
3.2
we have

m_{\infty}+o(1)=m_{\lambda}=\widetilde{\mathcal{I}}_{\lambda}(v_{\lambda}(x))\\
=\frac{p-3}{2p-3}\|\nabla v_{\lambda}\|_{2}^{2}+\frac{p-2}{2p-3}\lambda^{\frac{p-2}{4(3-p)}}\|v_{\lambda}\|_{2}^{2}+\frac{p-3}{2(2p-3)}\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{3}}(I_{2}*|v_{\lambda}|^{2})|v_{\lambda}|^{2}dx.

Therefore,

\|\nabla v_{\lambda}\|_{2}^{2},\quad\lambda^{\frac{p-2}{4(3-p)}}\|v_{\lambda}\|_{2}^{2},\quad\|v_{\lambda}\|_{q}^{q},\quad\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{3}}(I_{2}*|v_{\lambda}|^{2})|v_{\lambda}|^{2}dx,

are uniformly bounded as
\lambda\to\infty
.
∎

Lemma 3.3
.

Let
p\in(3,6)
. Then
\lambda^{\frac{p-2}{4(3-p)}}\|v_{\lambda}\|_{2}^{2}\to 0
as

.

Proof.

Lemma
3.2
implies that there exists a unique
t_{\lambda}\in(0,1)

t_{\lambda}^{2}v_{\lambda}(t_{\lambda}x)\in\mathscr{P}_{\infty}
. Indeed, assume that
t_{\lambda}\to t_{\infty}<1

\lambda\to\infty
. Then by (
3.5
) we have, as
\lambda\to\infty
,

(3.6)

\displaystyle m_{\infty}

\displaystyle\leq\widetilde{\mathcal{I}}_{\infty}(t^{2}_{\lambda}v_{\lambda}(t_{\lambda}x))=\frac{t_{\lambda}^{3}(p-3)}{2p-3}\|\nabla v_{\lambda}\|_{2}^{2}+\frac{t_{\lambda}^{3}(p-3)}{2(2p-3)}\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{3}}(I_{2}*|v_{\lambda}|^{2})|v_{\lambda}|^{2}dx

which is a contradiction. Therefore

as

. Using

again, we see that

\displaystyle 0=

Proof of Theorem
1.1
.

From Corollary
3.2
, Lemma
3.3
and (
3.6
), we see that
\{t_{\lambda}^{2}v_{\lambda}(t_{\lambda}x)\}\subset\mathscr{P}_{\infty}
is a minimizing sequence for
m_{\infty}
which is bounded in
E({\mathbb{R}}^{3})
and
t_{\lambda}\to 1
as
\lambda\to\infty
. For any sequence
\lambda_{n}\to\infty
, up to subsequence, it follows from Proposition
2.2
that
\{t_{\lambda_{n}}^{2}v_{\lambda_{n}}(t_{\lambda_{n}}x)\}
converges weakly in
E({\mathbb{R}}^{3})
.
Applying the Concentration-Compactness principle [cf.
[
S96
, Chapter I, Theorem 4.9]
] to
t_{\lambda}^{2}v_{\lambda}(t_{\lambda}x)
and following the proof of
[
AP08
]
, we conclude that there exists
\{\xi_{\lambda_{n}}\}\subset{\mathbb{R}}^{3}
and
\overline{v}_{\infty}\in E({\mathbb{R}}^{3})
such that

t_{\lambda_{n}}^{2}v_{\lambda_{n}}(t_{\lambda_{n}}(x-\xi_{\lambda_{n}}))\to\overline{v}_{\infty}\quad\text{in}\ L^{q}({\mathbb{R}}^{3}),\ q\in(3,6).

By using nonlocal Brezis-Lieb Lemma
[
MMS16
]
*Proposition 4.7, we obtain that

\lim_{\lambda_{n}\to\infty}\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{3}}(I_{2}*|t_{\lambda_{n}}^{2}v_{\lambda_{n}}(t_{\lambda_{n}}x)|^{2})|t_{\lambda_{n}}^{2}v_{\lambda_{n}}(t_{\lambda_{n}}x)|^{2}dx=\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{3}}(I_{2}*|\overline{v}_{\infty}|^{2})|\overline{v}_{\infty}|^{2}dx,

which means that
\overline{v}_{\infty}\neq 0
since by Corollary
3.1
the sequence
\{M(t_{\lambda}^{2}v_{\lambda}(t_{\lambda}x))\}
has a positive lower bound. It follows from the weakly lower semi-continuity of the norm that
0=\mathcal{P}_{\infty}(t_{\lambda}^{2}v_{\lambda}(t_{\lambda}x))\geq\mathcal{P}_{\infty}(\overline{v}_{\infty})
, then there exists a unique
t_{\infty}\in(0,1]
such that
t_{\infty}^{2}\overline{v}_{\infty}(t_{\infty}x)\in\mathscr{P}_{\infty}
. Note that
\{t_{\lambda_{n}}^{2}v_{\lambda_{n}}(t_{\lambda_{n}}(x-\xi_{\lambda_{n}}))\}
is also a bounded minimizing sequence for
m_{\infty}
, we have

\displaystyle m_{\infty}+o(1)

\displaystyle=\widetilde{\mathcal{I}}_{\infty}(t_{\lambda_{n}}^{2}v_{\lambda_{n}}(t_{\lambda_{n}}(x-\xi_{\lambda_{n}})))

\displaystyle=\frac{t_{\lambda_{n}}^{3}(p-3)}{2p-3}\|\nabla v_{\lambda_{n}}\|_{2}^{2}+\frac{t_{\lambda_{n}}^{3}(p-3)}{2(2p-3)}\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{3}}(I_{2}*|v_{\lambda_{n}}|^{2})|v_{\lambda_{n}}|^{2}dx

\displaystyle\geq\frac{(p-3)}{2p-3}\|\nabla v_{\infty}\|_{2}^{2}+\frac{(p-3)}{2(2p-3)}\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{3}}(I_{2}*|v_{\infty}|^{2})|v_{\infty}|^{2}dx

\displaystyle\geq\frac{t_{\infty}^{3}(p-3)}{2p-3}\|\nabla v_{\infty}\|_{2}^{2}+\frac{t_{\infty}^{3}(p-3)}{2(2p-3)}\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{3}}(I_{2}*|v_{\infty}|^{2})|v_{\infty}|^{2}dx

\displaystyle=\widetilde{\mathcal{I}}_{\infty}(t_{\infty}^{2}\overline{v}_{\infty}(t_{\infty}x))

\displaystyle\geq m_{\infty},

which implies that
t_{\infty}=1
and
t_{\lambda_{n}}^{2}v_{\lambda_{n}}(t_{\lambda_{n}}(x-\xi_{\lambda_{n}}))\to\overline{v}_{\infty}\ \text{in}\ D^{1}({\mathbb{R}}^{3})

v_{\lambda_{n}}(\cdot-\xi_{\lambda_{n}})\to\overline{v}_{\infty}\ \text{in}\ E({\mathbb{R}}^{3})
since

as

. Hence, by Lemma

,

).
∎
