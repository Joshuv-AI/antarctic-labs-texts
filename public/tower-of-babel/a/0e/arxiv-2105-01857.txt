Magnetic Properties of NbSi 2 N 4 , VSi 2 N 4 , and VSi 2 P 4 Monolayers
Md. Rakibul Karim Akanda, Roger K. Lake

Abstract
The recent demonstration of MoSi 2 N 4 and its exceptional stability to air, water, acid, and heat has generated intense interest in this family of two-dimensional (2D) materials. Among these materials, monolayers of NbSi 2 N 4 , VSi 2 N 4 , and VSi 2 P 4 are semiconducting, easy-plane ferromagnets with negligible in-plane magnetic anisotropy. They thus satisfy a necessary condition for exhibiting a dissipationless spin superfluid mode. The Curie temperatures of monolayer VSi 2 P 4 and VSi 2 N 4 are determined to be above room temperature based on Monte Carlo and density functional theory calculations. The magnetic moments of VSi 2 N 4 can be switched from in-plane to out-of-plane by applying tensile biaxial strain or electron doping.
