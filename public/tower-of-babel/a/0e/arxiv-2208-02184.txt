Capacity of the range of random walk: The law of the iterated logarithm
Amir Dembolabel=e1]adembo@stanford.edu, Izumi Okadalabel=e2]iokada@math.s.chiba-u.ac.jp

Abstract
We establish both the lim sup \limsup and the lim inf \liminf law of the iterated logarithm ( lil ), for the capacity of the range of a simple random walk in any dimension d ≥ 3 d\geq 3 . While for d ≥ 4 d\geq 4 , the order of growth in n n of such lil at dimension d d matches that for the volume of the random walk range in dimension d − 2 d-2 , somewhat surprisingly this correspondence breaks down for the capacity of the range at d = 3 d=3 . We further establish such lil for the Brownian capacity of a 3 3 -dimensional Brownian sample path and novel, sharp moderate deviations bounds for the capacity of the range of a 4 4 -dimensional simple random walk.
