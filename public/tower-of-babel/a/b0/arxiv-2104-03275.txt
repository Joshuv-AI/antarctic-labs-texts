Muon g − 2 g-2 in a two-Higgs-doublet model with a type-II seesaw mechanism
Chuan-Hung Chen, Cheng-Wei Chiang, Takaaki Nomura

Abstract
We study the two-Higgs-doublet model with type-II seesaw mechanism. In view of constraints from the Higgs data, we consider the aligned two-Higgs-doublet scheme and its effects on muon anomalous magnetic dipole moment, a μ a_{\mu} , including both one-loop and two-loop Barr-Zee type diagrams. Thanks to a sizable trilinear scalar coupling, the Barr-Zee type diagrams mediated by the Higgs triplet fields have a dominant effect on a μ a_{\mu} . In particular, unlike the usual two-Higgs-doublet models that require exotic Higgs bosons light in mass, the masses of the corresponding particles in the model are of 𝒪 ⁡ ( 100 ) {\cal O}(100) GeV. The doubly-charged Higgs boson presents a different decay pattern from the usual Higgs triplet model and thus calls for a new collider search strategy, such as multi- τ \tau searches at the LHC.

To achieve the above-mentioned goals, we consider the 2HDM with type-II seesaw mechanism

The A2HDS has the interesting feature that it reduces to various 2HDM types by taking proper limits on the alignment parameters. With a small vacuum expectation value (VEV) induced by electroweak symmetry breaking from the two Higgs doublets, the Higgs triplet in the model provides Majorana mass to neutrinos through the so-called type-II seesaw mechanism

It is found that rather than a simple combination of the 2HDM and the type-II seesaw model (also called the Higgs triplet model or HTM), the model presents several interesting features:

1.

The coupling between the heavier neutral Higgs boson in the 2HDM and the doubly-charged Higgs boson in the HTM can significantly enhance the muon
g-2

2.

The Higgs triplet VEV is now determined by three lepton number-violating parameters instead of just one in the simple HTM. As a result of the extra freedom, these parameters are not necessarily of the same order as the Higgs triplet VEV
Chen:2014xva

3.

With a sizable Higgs triplet VEV, the doubly-charged Higgs boson shows a richer decay pattern. As a result, the doubly-charged Higgs boson can evade the recent ATLAS lower bound of 350 GeV in pair production
Aad:2021lzu

The most general Yukawa couplings in the model are given by:

\displaystyle-{\cal L}_{Y}

\displaystyle=\bar{Q}_{L}Y^{d}_{1}D_{R}\Phi_{1}+\bar{Q}_{L}Y^{d}_{2}D_{R}\Phi_{2}+\bar{Q}_{L}Y^{u}_{1}U_{R}\tilde{\Phi}_{1}+\bar{Q}_{L}Y^{u}_{2}U_{R}\tilde{\Phi}_{2}

With the assumed VEVs of

\displaystyle X_{f}

\displaystyle Y^{f}_{1}

\displaystyle=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{c_{\beta}v}\frac{1}{1+\xi_{f}t_{\beta}}M_{f}\,.

III
One- and two-loop muon
g-2

The electromagnetic interaction of a lepton can be written as:

\overline{\ell}(p^{\prime})\Gamma^{\mu}\ell(p)=\overline{\ell}(p^{\prime})\left[\gamma^{\mu}F_{1}(k^{2})+\frac{i\sigma^{\mu\nu}k_{\nu}}{2m_{\ell}}F_{2}(k^{2})\right]\ell(p)\,.

(25)

The lepton anomalous magnetic dipole moment is then defined by

a_{\ell}=\frac{g_{\ell}-2}{2}=F_{2}(0)\,.

(26)

Since the magnetic moment is associated with dipole operator, the lepton
g-2
originates from radiative quantum corrections. In the model, the one-loop corrections from new physics are induced by the mediation of
H
,
A^{0}
, and
H^{\pm}
, where the associated Feynman diagrams are shown in Fig.
1
(a) and (b).
Moreover, it is known that the two-loop Barr-Zee type diagrams can have important contributions to the magnetic dipole moment due to a large coupling enhancement
Barr:1990vd
;
Chang:2000ii
. The potentially large two-loop diagrams mediated by heavy fermions, including top, bottom, and
\tau
, are shown in Fig.
1
(c). The essential mechanism contributing to the muon
g-2
in the model is the two-loop with Barr-Zee type diagram mediated by the charged scalars, including
\delta^{++}
,
\delta^{+}
, and
H^{+}
, as shown in Fig.
1
(d). In addition to the lepton Yukawa coupling, such diagrams further enjoy the enhancement of the electric charges associated with the charged scalars.

Figure 1:

g-2
, where
f
in plot (c) includes top (bottom) quark and
\tau
lepton.

The one-loop corrections to the anomalous magnetic dipole moment in the 2HDM have been studied long time ago
Leveille:1977rc
;
Haber:1978jt
;
Krawczyk:1996sm
;
Dedes:2001nx
. Using the Yukawa couplings shown in Eq. (
21
), the muon
g-2
from Fig.
1
(a) and (b) can be expressed as:

\displaystyle\Delta a^{1,H/A^{0}}_{\mu}

\displaystyle=\frac{m^{2}_{\mu}}{8\pi^{2}v^{2}}\left[(y^{H}_{\ell})^{2}\int^{1}_{0}\frac{r^{\mu}_{H}x^{2}(2-x)}{1-x(1-r^{\mu}_{H}x)}-(y^{A^{0}}_{\ell})^{2}\int^{1}_{0}\frac{r^{\mu}_{A^{0}}x^{3}}{1-x(1-r^{\mu}_{A^{0}}x)}\right]\,,

\displaystyle\Delta a^{1,H^{\pm}}_{\mu}

\displaystyle=\frac{m^{2}_{\mu}\zeta^{2}_{\ell}}{8\pi^{2}v^{2}}\int^{1}_{0}\frac{r^{\mu}_{H^{\pm}}x^{2}(1-x)}{1-x(1-r^{\mu}_{H^{\pm}}x)}\,,

(27)

where
r^{f}_{B}=m^{2}_{f}/m^{2}_{B}
with
B=H,~A^{0},~H^{\pm}
and the Yukawa couplings
y^{H,A^{0}}_{f}
are defined as:

y^{H}_{f}=s_{\beta-\alpha}\zeta_{f}-c_{\beta-\alpha}\,,~~y^{A^{0}}_{f}=-s_{f}\zeta_{f}\,.

(28)

From the expressions, it can be seen that the induced muon
g-2
is proportional to
m^{4}_{\mu}
. One of the four factors of
m_{\mu}
comes from the definition in Eq. (
25
), another comes from the mass insertion for chirality flip, and the rest two enter through the two Yukawa interaction vertices, each of which is proportional to the muon mass. Thus, if the intermediate scalar mass is of
{\cal O}(100)
GeV, the resulting muon
g-2
is far below
10^{-9}
. To get
\Delta a_{\mu}
up to
\sim 10^{-9}
, the mediating particle has to be as light as tens of GeV. This was the observation previously found for the 2HDM in the literature.

Following the results shown in Ref.
Ilisie:2015tra
, the two-loop Barr-Zee type diagrams with fermion and charged scalars can be written as:

\displaystyle\Delta a^{2,f}_{\mu}

\displaystyle=\sum_{f=t,b,\tau}\frac{\alpha_{\rm em}m^{2}_{\mu}N^{f}_{C}Q^{2}_{f}}{4\pi^{3}v^{2}}\left[y^{H}_{f}y^{H}_{\ell}J^{H}_{f}\left(r^{f}_{H}\right)+y^{A^{0}}_{f}y^{A^{0}}_{\ell}J^{A^{0}}_{f}\left(r^{f}_{A^{0}}\right)\right]\,,

\displaystyle\Delta a^{2,S}_{\mu}

\displaystyle=\sum_{S=\delta^{++},\delta^{+},H^{+}}\frac{\alpha_{\rm em}Q^{2}_{S}r^{\mu}_{H}}{8\pi^{3}}\zeta_{\ell}\lambda_{HS^{*}S}\,J_{S}(r^{S}_{H})\,,

(29)

where
N^{f}_{C}
is the number of color for the fermion
f
,
Q_{P}
(
P=f,~S
) is the electric charge of the particle, and the loop functions are given by:

\displaystyle J^{H}_{f}(z)

\displaystyle=\frac{z}{2}\int^{1}_{0}\frac{2x(1-x)-1}{z-x(1-x)}\ln\left(\frac{z}{x(1-x)}\right)\,,

\displaystyle J^{A^{0}}_{f}(z)

\displaystyle=\frac{z}{2}\int^{1}_{0}\frac{1}{z-x(1-x)}\ln\left(\frac{z}{x(1-x)}\right)\,,

\displaystyle J_{S}(z)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2}\int^{1}_{0}\frac{x(1-x)}{z-x(1-x)}\ln\left(\frac{z}{x(1-x)}\right)\,.

(30)

The two-loop results are proportional to
m^{2}_{\mu}
because there is only one muon Yukawa coupling involved. It can be seen that when
y^{H/A}_{u,d}
are strictly bounded by the experimental data, their contributions become subleading, and
\Delta a^{2,S}_{\mu}
is the dominant effect.

Numerical Analysis

IV.1
Parameter choice

Among the parameters in the Yukawa and scalar sectors, most relevant ones for the muon

Before a numerical analysis, we first need to find the allowed parameter space for the model. All potential constraints from experimental measurements, including various flavor physics processes, Higgs data, and electroweak precision observables, and theoretical bounds, such as perturbative unitarity and positivity of the scalar potential, have to be taken into account. Recently, such a global fit, considering the theoretical constraints has been done in the A2HDS

and the latter has

\displaystyle\zeta_{u}=0.1,~\zeta_{d}=10,~\zeta_{\ell}=-100\,.

V
Summary

In this work, we have studied an extension of the Standard Model only in the scalar sector, with the addition of one Higgs doublet and one complex Higgs triplet, rending a two-Higgs-doublet model (2HDM) with the type-II seesaw mechanism. For the 2HDM part, we consider the aligned two-Higgs-doublet scheme (A2HDS) to avoid undesired flavor-changing neutral currents induced by the two Higgs doublet fields and to satisfy the current Higgs data constraints. The Higgs triplet field obtains a small vacuum expectation value (VEV) induced by the electroweak symmetry breaking and gives Majorana mass to neutrinos through Yukawa couplings.

We have examined how the model can accommodate the measured muon
g-2
deviation. Simple 2HDMs usually require CP-even and -odd Higgs bosons (
H
and
A^{0}
) to be sufficiently light (about a few
\times{\cal O}(10)
GeV) and rely on the contributions of Barr-Zee type diagrams to account for the muon
g-2
anomaly,
\Delta a_{\mu}
. In our model, the Barr-Zee type diagrams get additional contributions from a large
H\delta^{++(+)}\delta^{--(-)}
coupling, an enhanced coupling between charged leptons and the charged Higgs boson (
H^{\pm}
) in 2HDM, and the electric charges of the charged Higgs bosons (
\delta^{\pm}
and
\delta^{\pm\pm}
) from the Higgs triplet field, independent of the mass of CP-odd Higgs boson. In fact, the mass of the exotic Higgs bosons is allowed to have a wider range, up to a few hundred GeV.

Owing to the new interactions with the other charged Higgs and
W
bosons, the doubly-charged Higgs boson presents a different decay pattern than the usual Higgs triplet model. With the assumed Higgs triplet VEV,
v_{\Delta}\sim 5\times 10^{-4}
GeV, the doubly-charged Higgs boson may dominantly decay into like-sign charged Higgs bosons in the 2HDM rather than like-sign
W
bosons, when the magnitudes of the trilinear couplings
\mu_{1,2,3}
are greater than
10^{-3}~(10^{-2})
GeV for both (one of the) charged Higgs bosons being on-shell.
Therefore, pair productions of the doubly-charged Higgs bosons will lead to the signature of 4
\tau
-leptons and missing energy at the LHC.

In this appendix, we show the full mass matrices at tree level for scalar, pseudoscalar, and charged Higgs bosons. Since
A^{0}
and
H^{\pm}
are the physical states in the 2HDM, it is useful to show the scalar mass matrices in terms of the
(G^{0},A^{0},\eta^{0})
and
(G^{+},H^{+},\delta^{+})
bases when the Higgs triplet field
\Delta
is introduced. Since the CP-even
H^{0}_{1}
and
H^{0}_{2}
scalars mix, we show the mass matrix in the basis of the
(h,H)
states, defined in Eq. (
6
).

From the scalar potentials given in Eqs. (
8
)
-
(
10
) and the Higgs basis in Eq. (
4
), the mass matrix for the CP-odd components
G^{0}
,
A^{0}
and
\eta^{0}
is given by

\frac{1}{2}\begin{pmatrix}G^{0}~A^{0}~\eta^{0}\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}m^{2}_{G^{0}G^{0}}&m^{2}_{G^{0}A^{0}}&m_{G^{0}\eta^{0}}^{2}\\
m^{2}_{G^{0}A^{0}}&m_{A^{0}A^{0}}^{2}&m_{A^{0}\eta^{0}}^{2}\\
m_{G^{0}\eta^{0}}^{2}&m_{A^{0}\eta^{0}}^{2}&m_{\eta^{0}\eta^{0}}^{2}\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}G^{0}\\
A^{0}\\
\eta^{0}\end{pmatrix},

(48)

where the mass matrix elements

\displaystyle m^{2}_{G^{0}G^{0}}=

\displaystyle\frac{3v_{\Delta}}{\sqrt{2}}\left(c^{2}_{\beta}\mu_{1}+s^{2}_{\beta}\mu_{2}+c_{\beta}s_{\beta}\mu_{3}\right)\,,

\displaystyle m^{2}_{G^{0}A^{0}}=

\displaystyle-\sqrt{2}v_{\Delta}\left[s_{2\beta}(\mu_{1}-\mu_{2})-c_{2\beta}\mu_{3}\right]\,,

\displaystyle m^{2}_{G^{0}\eta^{0}}=

\displaystyle-\sqrt{2}v\left(c^{2}_{\beta}\mu_{1}+s^{2}_{\beta}\mu_{2}+c_{\beta}s_{\beta}\mu_{3}\right)\,,

\displaystyle m_{A^{0}A^{0}}^{2}\equiv

\displaystyle m^{2}_{A^{0}}=\frac{m^{2}_{12}}{s_{\beta}c_{\beta}}-\lambda_{5}v^{2}-\frac{v^{2}}{2}\left(\lambda_{6}t^{-1}_{\beta}+\lambda_{7}t_{\beta}\right)+2\sqrt{2}s^{2}_{\beta}v_{\Delta}\mu_{1}

\displaystyle+\frac{c^{2}_{\beta}v_{\Delta}\mu_{3}}{\sqrt{2}t_{\beta}}(1-t^{2}_{\beta})^{2}-\frac{v^{2}_{\Delta}\bar{\lambda}_{12}}{2}(s_{2\beta}+c^{3}_{\beta}+s^{3}_{\beta})\,,

\displaystyle m_{A^{0}\eta^{0}}^{2}=

\displaystyle\frac{v}{\sqrt{2}}\left[s_{2\beta}(\mu_{1}-\mu_{2})-c_{2\beta}\mu_{3}\right]\,,

\displaystyle m_{\eta^{0}\eta^{0}}^{2}\equiv

\displaystyle m^{2}_{\eta^{0}}=m^{2}_{\Delta}+\left(\lambda_{\Delta 1}+\lambda_{\Delta 2}\right)v^{2}_{\Delta}+\frac{\bar{\lambda}_{8}c^{2}_{b}+\bar{\lambda}_{9}s^{2}_{\beta}}{2}v^{2}+\frac{\bar{\lambda}_{12}s_{2\beta}}{2}v^{2}\,,

(49)

with
\bar{\lambda}_{8}=\lambda_{8}+\lambda^{\prime}_{8}
and
\bar{\lambda}_{9}=\lambda_{9}+\lambda^{\prime}_{9}
.
From Eq. (
12
), it is known that
c^{2}_{\beta}\mu_{1}+s^{2}_{\beta}\mu_{2}+c_{\beta}s_{\beta}\mu_{3}\sim{\cal O}(v_{\Delta})
. Neglecting term of
{\cal O}(v_{\Delta})
, one can see that
m^{2}_{G^{0}G^{0}}
,
m^{2}_{G^{0}A^{0}}
, and
m^{2}_{G^{0}\eta^{0}}
are negligibly small. Thus, to a good approximation,
G^{0}
represents the neutral Goldstone boson. Moreover, if we further demand that the factor
s_{2\beta}(\mu_{1}-\mu_{2})-c_{2\beta}\mu_{3}
in
m^{2}_{A^{0}\eta^{0}}
vanish,
A^{0}
and
\eta^{0}
decouple from each other. To understand the correlations among
\mu_{1}
,
\mu_{2}
, and
\mu_{3}
under the conditions:

\displaystyle\begin{cases}c^{2}_{\beta}\mu_{1}+s^{2}_{\beta}\mu_{2}+c_{\beta}s_{\beta}\mu_{3}&=\epsilon_{\Delta}\,,\\
s_{2\beta}(\mu_{1}-\mu_{2})-c_{2\beta}\mu_{3}&=0\,,\end{cases}

(50)

where
\epsilon_{\Delta}
is an parameter of
{\cal O}(v_{\Delta})
, we solve and obtain:

\mu_{2}=\mu_{1}t^{-2}_{\beta}+\epsilon_{\Delta}\left(1-t^{-2}_{\beta}\right)\,,~~\mu_{3}=-2t^{-1}_{\beta}(\mu_{1}-\epsilon_{\Delta})\,.

(51)

For
\tan\beta=1
, we have
\mu_{2}\simeq\mu_{1}
and
\mu_{3}\simeq-2\mu_{1}
. For large
\tan\beta
, they can be approximated as:

\mu_{2}\simeq\epsilon_{\Delta}+\mu_{1}t^{-2}_{\beta}\,,~~\mu_{3}\simeq-2\mu_{1}t^{-1}_{\beta}\,.

(52)

It is seen that the
\mu_{2,3}
scale is determined by
\mu_{1}
and
t_{\beta}
.

Similarly, the mass matrix for
G^{+}
,
H^{+}
and
\delta^{+}
is given by:

(G^{-}H^{-}\delta^{-})\left(\begin{array}[]{ccc}m^{2}_{G^{-}G^{+}}&m^{2}_{G^{-}H^{+}}&m^{2}_{G^{-}\delta^{+}}\\
m^{2}_{G^{-}H^{+}}&m^{2}_{H^{-}H^{+}}&m^{2}_{H^{-}\delta^{+}}\\
m^{2}_{G^{-}\delta^{+}}&m^{2}_{H^{-}\delta^{+}}&m^{2}_{\delta^{-}\delta^{+}}\\
\end{array}\right)\left(\begin{array}[]{c}G^{+}\\
H^{+}\\
\delta^{+}\\
\end{array}\right)\,,

(53)

where the mass matrix elements

\displaystyle m^{2}_{G^{-}G^{+}}

\displaystyle=\sqrt{2}v_{\Delta}\left(c^{2}_{\beta}\mu_{1}+s^{2}_{\beta}\mu_{2}+c_{\beta}s_{\beta}\mu_{3}\right)-\frac{v^{2}_{\Delta}}{2}(c^{2}_{\beta}\lambda^{\prime}_{8}+s^{2}_{\beta}\lambda^{\prime}_{9}+\lambda^{\prime}_{12}s_{2\beta})\,,

\displaystyle m^{2}_{G^{-}H^{+}}

\displaystyle=-\frac{v_{\Delta}}{\sqrt{2}}(s_{2\beta}(\mu_{1}-\mu_{2})-c_{2\beta}\mu_{3})+\frac{\lambda^{\prime}_{8}-\lambda^{\prime}_{9}}{2}c_{\beta}s_{\beta}v^{2}_{\Delta}-\frac{\lambda^{\prime}_{12}}{2}c_{2\beta}v^{2}_{\Delta}\,,

\displaystyle m^{2}_{G^{-}\delta^{+}}

\displaystyle=-v\left(c^{2}_{\beta}\mu_{1}+s^{2}_{\beta}\mu_{2}+c_{\beta}s_{\beta}\mu_{3}\right)+\frac{v_{\Delta}v}{2\sqrt{2}}\left(c^{2}_{\beta}\lambda^{\prime}_{8}+s^{2}_{\beta}\lambda^{\prime}_{9}+s_{2\beta}\lambda^{\prime}_{12}\right)\,,

\displaystyle m^{2}_{H^{-}H^{+}}

\displaystyle\equiv m^{2}_{H^{\pm}}=\frac{m^{2}_{12}}{c_{\beta}s_{\beta}}-\frac{\lambda_{4}+\lambda_{5}}{2}v^{2}-\frac{\lambda_{6}v^{2}}{2}t^{-1}_{\beta}-\frac{\lambda_{7}v^{2}}{2}t_{\beta}\,,

\displaystyle+\sqrt{2}v_{\Delta}\left(s^{2}_{\beta}\mu_{1}-c^{2}_{\beta}\mu_{2}\right)+\frac{\mu_{3}v_{\Delta}}{\sqrt{2}c_{\beta}s_{\beta}}\left(c^{4}_{\beta}+s^{4}_{\beta}\right)-\frac{v^{2}_{\Delta}}{2}\left(\lambda^{\prime}_{8}s^{2}_{\beta}+\lambda^{\prime}_{9}c^{2}_{\beta}\right)

\displaystyle-\frac{v^{2}_{\Delta}}{2}\left(s_{2\beta}\lambda_{12}+(c^{3}_{\beta}+s^{3}_{\beta})\bar{\lambda}_{12}\right)\,,

\displaystyle m^{2}_{H^{-}\delta^{+}}

\displaystyle=\frac{v}{2}\left(s_{2\beta}(\mu_{1}-\mu_{2})-c_{2\beta}\mu_{3}\right)-\frac{\lambda^{\prime}_{8}-\lambda^{\prime}_{9}}{2\sqrt{2}}c_{\beta}s_{\beta}vv_{\Delta}-\frac{\lambda^{\prime}_{12}}{2\sqrt{2}}s^{2}_{\beta}vv_{\Delta}\,,

\displaystyle m^{2}_{\delta^{-}\delta^{+}}

\displaystyle\equiv m^{2}_{\delta^{\pm}}=m^{2}_{\Delta}+\frac{2\lambda_{9}+\lambda^{\prime}_{9}}{4}s^{2}_{\beta}v^{2}+\frac{2\lambda_{8}+\lambda^{\prime}_{8}}{4}c^{2}_{\beta}v^{2}+\frac{2\lambda_{12}+\lambda^{\prime}_{12}}{4}s_{2\beta}v^{2}\,.

(54)

Analogous to the case of CP-odd scalar mass matrix,
m^{2}_{G^{-}G^{+}}
,
m^{2}_{G^{-}H^{+}}
, and
m^{2}_{G^{-}\delta^{+}}
vanish if we drop the
{\cal O}(v_{\Delta})
terms. Therefore,
G^{\pm}
can be approximated as the charged Goldstone bosons. If we further demand
\mu_{3}=t_{2\beta}(\mu_{1}-\mu_{2})
,
m^{2}_{H^{-}\delta^{+}}
also vanishes, and
H^{\pm}
and
\delta^{\pm}
decouple each other and are approximately the physical states.

Next, we discuss the CP-even scalars. In 2HDMs, the physical
(H,h)
states and the
(\Phi^{0}_{1},\Phi^{0}_{2})
states are related by:

\begin{pmatrix}H\\
h\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}c_{\alpha}&s_{\alpha}\\
-s_{\alpha}&c_{\alpha}\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}\Phi^{0}_{1}\\
\Phi^{0}_{2}\end{pmatrix}\,,

(55)

where
\alpha
is the mixing angle for CP-even scalars. To derive the mass matrix in the
(h,H,\delta^{0})
states, it is more convenient to start from the basis of
(\Phi^{0}_{1},\Phi^{0}_{2})
. Once the mass eigenvalues and eigenstates of
h
and
H
in the 2HDM are obtained, we then include
\delta^{0}
to form a three-component basis
(h,H,\delta^{0})
. As we will explicitly see below, these will be approximately the physical states as long as
{\cal O}(v_{\Delta})
terms are ignored.

The
2\times 2
mass matrix for
(\Phi^{0}_{1},\Phi^{0}_{2})
is:

\frac{1}{2}\begin{pmatrix}\Phi^{0}_{1}~\Phi^{0}_{2}\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}m^{2}_{\phi_{1}\phi_{1}}&m^{2}_{\phi_{1}\phi_{2}}\\
m^{2}_{\phi_{1}\phi_{2}}&m^{2}_{\phi_{2}\phi_{2}}\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}\Phi^{0}_{1}\\
\Phi^{0}_{2}\end{pmatrix}\,,

(56)

where the matrix elements

\displaystyle m^{2}_{\phi_{1}\phi_{1}}

\displaystyle=\left(m^{2}_{12}+\frac{v_{\Delta}\mu_{3}}{\sqrt{2}}\right)t_{\beta}+\lambda_{1}v^{2}c^{2}_{\beta}+\frac{3\lambda_{6}v^{2}}{2}c_{\beta}s_{\beta}-\frac{\lambda_{7}v^{2}}{2}s^{2}_{\beta}t_{\beta}\,,

\displaystyle m^{2}_{\phi_{1}\phi_{2}}

\displaystyle=-\left(m^{2}_{12}+\frac{v_{\Delta}\mu_{3}}{\sqrt{2}}\right)+\lambda_{345}v^{2}c_{\beta}s_{\beta}+\frac{3v^{2}}{2}\left(c^{2}_{\beta}\lambda_{6}+s^{2}_{\beta}\lambda_{7}\right)\,,

\displaystyle m^{2}_{\phi_{2}\phi_{2}}

\displaystyle=\left(m^{2}_{12}+\frac{v_{\Delta}\mu_{3}}{\sqrt{2}}\right)t^{-1}_{\beta}+\lambda_{2}v^{2}s^{2}_{\beta}-\frac{\lambda_{6}v^{2}}{2}c^{2}_{\beta}t^{-1}_{\beta}+\frac{3\lambda_{7}v^{2}}{2}c_{\beta}s_{\beta}\,.

(57)

Using the parametrization in Eq. (
55
), we obtain the eigenvalues of Eq. (
55
) as:

\displaystyle m^{2}_{h,H}=\frac{m^{2}_{\phi_{1}\phi_{1}}+m^{2}_{\phi_{2}\phi_{2}}}{2}\pm\frac{1}{2}\left[\left(m^{2}_{\phi_{1}\phi_{1}}-\phi^{2}_{\phi_{2}\phi_{2}}\right)^{2}+4(m^{2}_{\phi_{1}\phi_{2}})^{2}\right]^{1/2}\,,

(58)

and the mixing angle is determined by:

\tan{2\alpha}=\frac{2m^{2}_{\phi_{1}\phi_{2}}}{m^{2}_{\phi_{1}\phi_{1}}-m^{2}_{\phi_{2}\phi_{2}}}\,.

(59)

Using the rotational matrix,

\begin{pmatrix}-s_{\alpha}&c_{\alpha}&0\\
c_{\alpha}&s_{\alpha}&0\\
0&0&1\end{pmatrix}\,,

(60)

we can transform the basis from
(\Phi^{0}_{1},\Phi^{0}_{2},\delta^{0})
to
(h,H,\delta^{0})
, and the transformed mass matrix is:

\frac{1}{2}\left(h~H~\delta^{0}\right)\begin{pmatrix}m^{2}_{h}&0&m_{h\delta^{0}}^{2}\\
0&m_{H}^{2}&m_{H\delta^{0}}^{2}\\
m_{h\delta^{0}}^{2}&m_{H\delta^{0}}^{2}&m_{\delta^{0}\delta^{0}}^{2}\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}h\\
H\\
\delta^{0}\end{pmatrix}\,,

(61)

where the additional elements

\displaystyle m_{h\delta^{0}}^{2}=

\displaystyle-\frac{v}{\sqrt{2}}\left[(\mu_{1}+\mu_{2})s_{\beta-\alpha}-(\mu_{1}-\mu_{2})s_{\beta+\alpha}+\mu_{3}c_{\beta+\alpha}\right]

\displaystyle-\frac{v_{\Delta}v}{2}\left[(\bar{\lambda}_{8}+\bar{\lambda}_{9})s_{\beta-\alpha}+(\bar{\lambda}_{8}-\bar{\lambda}_{9})s_{\beta+\alpha}-2\bar{\lambda}_{12}c_{\beta+\alpha}\right]\,,

\displaystyle m_{H\delta^{0}}^{2}=

\displaystyle-\frac{v}{\sqrt{2}}\left[(\mu_{1}+\mu_{2})c_{\beta-\alpha}+(\mu_{1}-\mu_{2})c_{\beta+\alpha}+\mu_{3}s_{\beta+\alpha}\right]

\displaystyle+\frac{v_{\Delta}v}{2}\left[(\bar{\lambda}_{8}+\bar{\lambda}_{9})c_{\beta-\alpha}+(\bar{\lambda}_{8}-\bar{\lambda}_{9})c_{\beta+\alpha}+2\bar{\lambda}_{12}s_{\beta+\alpha}\right]\,,

\displaystyle m_{\delta^{0}\delta^{0}}^{2}=

\displaystyle m^{2}_{\Delta}+3(\lambda_{\Delta 1}+\lambda_{\Delta 2})v^{2}_{\Delta}+\frac{v^{2}}{2}\left(c^{2}_{\beta}\bar{\lambda}_{8}+s^{2}_{\beta}\bar{\lambda}_{9}+s_{2\beta}\bar{\lambda}_{12}\right)\,.

(62)

In the model,
m^{2}_{h\delta^{0}}
and
m^{2}_{H\delta^{0}}
are generally not small. Nevertheless, if we apply the conditions in Eq. (
51
),
m^{2}_{h\delta^{0}}
and
m^{2}_{H\delta^{0}}
can be rewritten as:

\displaystyle m^{2}_{h\delta^{0}}=

\displaystyle-\sqrt{2}v\epsilon_{\Delta}s_{\beta-\alpha}-\frac{v_{\Delta}v}{2}\left[(\bar{\lambda}_{8}+\bar{\lambda}_{9})s_{\beta-\alpha}+(\bar{\lambda}_{8}-\bar{\lambda}_{9})s_{\beta+\alpha}-\bar{\lambda}_{12}c_{\beta+\alpha}\right]\,,

\displaystyle m_{H\delta^{0}}^{2}=

\displaystyle-\sqrt{2}v\epsilon_{\Delta}s_{\beta+\alpha}+\frac{v_{\Delta}v}{2}\left[(\bar{\lambda}_{8}+\bar{\lambda}_{9})c_{\beta-\alpha}+(\bar{\lambda}_{8}-\bar{\lambda}_{9})c_{\beta+\alpha}+\bar{\lambda}_{12}s_{\beta+\alpha}\right]\,,

(63)

both being
\sim O(v_{\Delta})
. Comparing to the dominant diagonal elements
m^{2}_{h,H,\delta^{0}}
, the mixing effects among
h
,
H
, and
\delta^{0}
are thus small. Therefore, to the leading order in
v_{\Delta}
, we will neglect such mixing and
h
and
H
decouple from
\delta^{0}
.

Appendix B
Scalar potential and trilinear couplings in the Higgs basis

The scalar potential in terms of the Higgs basis
H_{1,2}
can be written as:

V=V(H_{1},H_{2})+V(\Delta)+V(H_{1},H_{2},\Delta)\,,

(64)

where each term is more explicitly given by

\displaystyle V(H_{1},H_{2})=

\displaystyle Y_{1}H^{\dagger}_{1}H_{1}+Y_{2}H^{\dagger}_{2}H_{2}+Y_{3}(H^{\dagger}_{1}H_{2}+\mbox{H.c.})+\frac{1}{2}\Lambda_{1}(H^{\dagger}_{1}H_{1})^{2}

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{2}\Lambda_{2}(H^{\dagger}_{2}H_{2})^{2}+\Lambda_{3}H^{\dagger}_{1}H_{1}H^{\dagger}_{2}H_{2}+\Lambda_{4}H^{\dagger}_{1}H_{2}H^{\dagger}_{2}H_{1}+\left[\frac{1}{2}\Lambda_{5}(H^{\dagger}_{1}H_{2})^{2}\right.

\displaystyle\left.+\Lambda_{6}(H^{\dagger}_{1}H_{1})(H^{\dagger}_{2}H_{1})+\Lambda_{7}(H^{\dagger}_{2}H_{2})(H^{\dagger}_{2}H_{1})+\mbox{H.c.}\right]\,,

(65)

\displaystyle V(\Delta)=

\displaystyle M^{2}_{\Delta}Tr\Delta^{\dagger}\Delta+\Lambda_{\Delta 1}(Tr\Delta^{\dagger}\Delta)^{2}+\Lambda_{\Delta 2}Tr(\Delta^{\dagger}\Delta)^{2}\,,

(66)

\displaystyle V(H_{1},H_{2},\Delta)=

\displaystyle\left(W_{1}H^{T}_{1}i\tau_{2}\Delta^{\dagger}H_{1}+W_{2}H^{T}_{2}i\tau_{2}\Delta^{\dagger}H_{2}+W_{3}H^{T}_{1}i\tau_{2}\Delta^{\dagger}H_{2}+\mbox{H.c.}\right)

\displaystyle+\left[\Lambda_{8}H^{\dagger}_{1}H_{1}+\Lambda_{9}H^{\dagger}_{2}H_{2}+(\Lambda_{12}H^{\dagger}_{1}H_{2}+\mbox{H.c.})\right]Tr\Delta^{\dagger}\Delta

\displaystyle+\Lambda^{\prime}_{8}H^{\dagger}_{1}\Delta\Delta^{\dagger}H_{1}+\Lambda^{\prime}_{9}H^{\dagger}_{2}\Delta\Delta^{\dagger}H_{2}+(\Lambda^{\prime}_{12}H^{\dagger}_{1}\Delta\Delta^{\dagger}H_{2}+\mbox{H.c.})\,.

(67)

Using the representations of
H_{1,2}
and
\Delta
in Eq. (
5
), the minimum conditions for the the VEVs of
H_{1,2}
and
\Delta
can be obtained as:

\displaystyle Y^{2}_{1}+\frac{\Lambda^{2}_{1}}{2}v^{2}

\displaystyle=\sqrt{2}W_{1}v_{D}-\frac{\bar{\Lambda}_{8}}{2}v^{2}_{D}\,,

\displaystyle Y^{2}_{3}+\frac{\Lambda^{2}_{6}}{2}v^{2}

\displaystyle=\frac{W_{3}}{\sqrt{2}}v_{D}-\frac{\bar{\Lambda}_{12}}{2}v^{2}_{D}\,,

\displaystyle M^{2}_{\Delta}+\frac{\bar{\Lambda}_{8}}{2}v^{2}

\displaystyle=\frac{v^{2}}{\sqrt{2}}\frac{W_{1}}{v_{\Delta}}-(\Lambda_{\Delta 1}+\Lambda_{\Delta 2})v^{2}_{\Delta}\,,

(68a)

with
\bar{\Lambda}_{8}=\Lambda_{8}+\Lambda^{\prime}_{8}
and
\bar{\Lambda}_{12}=\Lambda_{12}+\Lambda^{\prime}_{12}
.

From Eqs. (
65
)–(
67
), the doubly-charged Higgs mass is obtained as:

m^{2}_{\delta^{\pm\pm}}=m^{2}_{\Delta}+\frac{\Lambda_{8}v^{2}}{2}+\Lambda_{\Delta 1}v^{2}_{\Delta}\,.

(69)

The mass splittings in the Higgs triplet are:

\displaystyle m^{2}_{\delta^{\pm}}-m^{2}_{\delta^{\pm\pm}}

\displaystyle=\frac{\Lambda_{\Delta 2}v^{2}_{\Delta}}{2}+\frac{\Lambda^{\prime}_{8}v^{2}}{4}\,,

\displaystyle m^{2}_{\delta^{0}}-m^{2}_{\delta^{\pm\pm}}

\displaystyle=(2\Lambda_{\Delta 1}+3\Lambda_{\Delta 2})v^{2}_{\Delta}+\frac{\Lambda^{\prime}_{8}v^{2}}{2}\,.

(70)

The trilinear couplings of the neutral scalars to the charged Higgs scalars with
c_{\beta-\alpha}=0
are given by:

{\cal L}_{H_{i}SS}=-v\left[\lambda_{H_{i}\delta^{--}\delta^{++}}H_{i}\delta^{--}\delta^{++}+\lambda_{H_{i}\delta^{-}\delta^{+}}H_{i}\delta^{-}\delta^{+}+\lambda_{H_{i}H^{-}H^{+}}H_{i}H^{-}H^{+}\right]\,,

(71)

where the couplings:

\displaystyle\lambda_{h\delta^{--}\delta^{++}}

\displaystyle=\Lambda_{8}\,,~\Lambda_{H\delta^{--}\delta^{++}}=-\Lambda_{12}\,,

\displaystyle\lambda_{h\delta^{-}\delta^{+}}

\displaystyle=\Lambda_{8}+\frac{\Lambda^{\prime}_{8}}{2}\,,~\lambda_{H\delta^{-}\delta^{+}}=-\Lambda_{12}-\frac{\Lambda^{\prime}_{12}}{2}\,,

\displaystyle\lambda_{hH^{-}H^{+}}

\displaystyle=\Lambda_{3}\,,~\lambda_{HH^{-}H^{+}}=-\Lambda_{7}\,.

(72)
