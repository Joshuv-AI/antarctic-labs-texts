Loops in AdS: From the Spectral Representation to Position Space II
Dean Carmi

Abstract
We continue the study of AdS loop amplitudes in the spectral representation and in position space. We compute the finite coupling 4-point function in position space for the large- N N conformal Gross Neveu model on A ​ d ​ S 3 AdS_{3} . The resummation of loop bubble diagrams gives a result proportional to a tree-level contact diagram. We show that certain families of fermionic Witten diagrams can be easily computed from their companion scalar diagrams. Thus, many of the results and identities of Carmi:2019ocp are extended to the case of external fermions. We derive a spectral representation for ladder diagrams in AdS. Finally, we compute various bulk 2-point correlators, extending the results of Carmi:2019ocp .

Introduction

Two very important problems in physics are: understanding quantum gravity and understanding strongly coupled (non-perturbative) quantum field theories. These two problems are beautifully connected to each other via the gauge-gravity duality, in which Anti-de Sitter (AdS) space plays a unique role. Therefore computing observables for quantum field theories defined on AdS space gives important insights about the two questions mentioned above.

\displaystyle g_{4}(z,\bar{z})=\frac{-\pi iz\bar{z}}{\bar{z}-z}\int d\nu\frac{i\nu(i\nu+1)(i\nu-1)}{\sinh(\pi\nu)}\Big[\frac{\hat{z}^{2}-1}{\frac{i\nu}{2}}\partial_{\hat{z}}-\frac{\hat{\bar{z}}^{2}-1}{\frac{i\nu}{2}}\partial_{\hat{\bar{z}}}+(\hat{\bar{z}}-\hat{z})\Big]Q_{\frac{i\nu}{2}}(\hat{z})Q_{\frac{i\nu}{2}}(\hat{\bar{z}})

(137)

Now we use the following identity for Legendre functions:

\displaystyle w_{z}Q_{\frac{\beta}{2}}=\frac{\beta}{2}(\frac{\beta}{2}+1)Q_{\frac{\beta}{2}}

\displaystyle\text{where}\ \ \ \ w_{z}\equiv-\frac{d}{d\hat{z}}(1-\hat{z}^{2})\frac{d}{d\hat{z}}

(138)

Eq.
137
becomes:

\displaystyle g_{4}(z,\bar{z})=\mathcal{P}_{1}\int d\nu\frac{(i\nu+1)}{\sinh(\pi\nu)}Q_{\frac{i\nu}{2}}(\hat{z})Q_{\frac{i\nu}{2}}(\hat{\bar{z}})+\mathcal{P}_{2}\int d\nu\frac{(i\nu+1)^{2}}{\sinh(\pi\nu)}Q_{\frac{i\nu}{2}}(\hat{z})Q_{\frac{i\nu}{2}}(\hat{\bar{z}})

(139)

Where we have defined the differential operators:

\displaystyle\mathcal{P}_{1}=-\pi i\frac{z\bar{z}}{\bar{z}-z}(\hat{\bar{z}}-\hat{z})(4w_{z}+3)+4\pi i\frac{z\bar{z}}{\bar{z}-z}\Big[(\hat{z}^{2}-1)\partial_{\hat{z}}-(\hat{\bar{z}}^{2}-1)\partial_{\hat{\bar{z}}}\Big]

\displaystyle\mathcal{P}_{2}\equiv 3\pi i\frac{z\bar{z}}{\bar{z}-z}(\hat{\bar{z}}-\hat{z})-2\pi i\frac{z\bar{z}}{\bar{z}-z}\Big[(\hat{z}^{2}-1)\partial_{\hat{z}}-(\hat{\bar{z}}^{2}-1)\partial_{\hat{\bar{z}}}\Big]

(140)

Using the residue theorem on Eq.
139
gives:

\displaystyle g_{4}(z,\bar{z})=\pi\mathcal{P}_{1}\bigg[\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(2n+2)Q_{n+\frac{1}{2}}(\hat{z})Q_{n+\frac{1}{2}}(\hat{\bar{z}})-\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(2n+3)Q_{n+1}(\hat{z})Q_{n+1}(\hat{\bar{z}})\bigg]

\displaystyle+\pi\mathcal{P}_{2}\bigg[\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(2n+2)^{2}Q_{n+\frac{1}{2}}(\hat{z})Q_{n+\frac{1}{2}}(\hat{\bar{z}})-\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(2n+3)^{2}Q_{n+1}(\hat{z})Q_{n+1}(\hat{\bar{z}})\bigg]

\displaystyle=\pi\mathcal{P}_{1}S_{6}+\pi\mathcal{P}_{2}S_{7}+\pi\mathcal{P}_{2}Q_{0}(\hat{z})Q_{0}(\hat{\bar{z}})

(141)

where we defined:

\displaystyle S_{6}\equiv\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(2n+2)Q_{n+\frac{1}{2}}(\hat{z})Q_{n+\frac{1}{2}}(\hat{\bar{z}})-\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(2n+3)Q_{n+1}(\hat{z})Q_{n+1}(\hat{\bar{z}})

\displaystyle S_{7}\equiv\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(2n+2)^{2}Q_{n+\frac{1}{2}}(\hat{z})Q_{n+\frac{1}{2}}(\hat{\bar{z}})-\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(2n+1)^{2}Q_{n}(\hat{z})Q_{n}(\hat{\bar{z}})

(142)

These sums can be computed analytically. The
S_{6}
sum is equal to (see Eq. 5.9 of
Carmi:2019ocp
):

\displaystyle S_{6}=\Big[2Q_{\frac{1}{2}}(\hat{z})Q_{\frac{1}{2}}(\hat{\bar{z}})+\frac{\frac{3}{2}}{(\hat{z}-\hat{\bar{z}})}\Big(Q_{\frac{1}{2}}(\hat{z})Q_{\frac{3}{2}}(\hat{\bar{z}})-Q_{\frac{3}{2}}(\hat{z})Q_{\frac{1}{2}}(\hat{\bar{z}})\Big)\Big]

\displaystyle-\Big[3Q_{1}(\hat{z})Q_{1}(\hat{\bar{z}})+\frac{2}{(\hat{z}-\hat{\bar{z}})}\Big(Q_{1}(\hat{z})Q_{2}(\hat{\bar{z}})-Q_{2}(\hat{z})Q_{1}(\hat{\bar{z}})\Big)\Big]

(143)

The sum
S_{7}
is equal to (see Eq. 4.18 of
Carmi:2019ocp
):

\displaystyle S_{7}=\frac{1}{128\pi^{2}}g_{4}^{(contact)}(\hat{z},\hat{\bar{z}})

(144)

where
g_{4}^{(contact)}(\hat{z},\hat{\bar{z}})
is defined as the scalar tree-level contact diagram in
AdS_{3}
with external scaling dimensions
\Delta_{i}=(1,1,\frac{3}{2},\frac{3}{2})
.

To summarize, we have managed to compute analytically the (regularized) 4-point correlator of the
O(N)
model on
AdS_{5}
, with scaling dimension
\Delta=2
:

\displaystyle\boxed{g_{4}(z,\bar{z})=\pi\mathcal{P}_{1}S_{6}+\pi\mathcal{P}_{2}S_{7}+\pi\mathcal{P}_{2}Q_{0}(\hat{z})Q_{0}(\hat{\bar{z}})}

(145)

where all of these functions were explicitly defined and computed above.

with external scaling dimensions
a\equiv\frac{\Delta_{2}-\Delta_{1}}{2}=0
and

A simplification arises from the fact that the

conformal block with

and

{}_{2}F_{1}

\displaystyle\mathcal{K}^{\Delta_{i}}_{\beta}(z,\bar{z})=\frac{z\bar{z}}{z-\bar{z}}\Big(z^{\frac{\beta}{2}}{}_{2}F_{1}(\frac{\beta}{2},\frac{\beta+1}{2},\beta,z)\Big)\times\Big(\bar{z}^{\frac{\beta}{2}-1}{}_{2}F_{1}(\frac{\beta-2}{2},\frac{\beta-1}{2},\beta-2,\bar{z})\Big)-(z\leftrightarrow\bar{z})

where the cross-ratios are

and

, and in the last line we defined:

\displaystyle Z\equiv\frac{\sqrt{z\bar{z}}}{(1+\sqrt{1-z})(1+\sqrt{1-\bar{z}})}\ \ \ ,\ \ \ \widetilde{Z}\equiv\frac{2\sqrt{z}}{1+\sqrt{1-z}}\big(4Z\big)^{-2}\frac{z\bar{z}}{z-\bar{z}}-(z\leftrightarrow\bar{z})

The spectral representation of a 4-point function (see e.g Eq.

) simplifies when plugging
\Delta_{2}=\Delta_{1}
and
\Delta_{3}=\Delta_{4}+1
:

\displaystyle g_{4}(z,\bar{z})=\pi 2^{3-d}\int d\nu\tilde{F}_{\nu}\frac{\Gamma_{\frac{d}{2}+i\nu-1}}{4^{i\nu}\Gamma_{i\nu}}(\Gamma_{\Delta_{1}-\frac{d}{4}+\frac{i\nu}{2}}\Gamma_{\Delta_{1}-\frac{d}{4}-\frac{i\nu}{2}}\Gamma_{\Delta_{4}+\frac{1}{2}-\frac{d}{4}+\frac{i\nu}{2}}\Gamma_{\Delta_{4}+\frac{1}{2}-\frac{d}{4}-\frac{i\nu}{2}})\mathcal{K}^{\Delta_{i}}_{\frac{d}{2}+i\nu}

Putting
d=4
, and using Eq.
D

\displaystyle g_{4}(z,\bar{z})=2\pi\sqrt{\frac{u}{v}}Z\widetilde{Z}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}d\nu\ i\nu\tilde{F}_{\nu}(\Gamma_{\Delta_{1}-1+\frac{i\nu}{2}}\Gamma_{\Delta_{1}-1-\frac{i\nu}{2}}\Gamma_{\Delta_{4}-\frac{1}{2}+\frac{i\nu}{2}}\Gamma_{\Delta_{4}-\frac{1}{2}-\frac{i\nu}{2}})Z^{i\nu}

(149)

Rewriting this using a gamma function identity,
\Gamma_{x}\Gamma_{1-x}=\frac{\pi}{\sin(\pi x)}
, gives:

\displaystyle g_{4}(z,\bar{z})=\sqrt{\frac{u}{v}}Z\widetilde{Z}\int d\nu\frac{2\pi^{3}\tilde{F}_{\nu}}{\sin\pi(\Delta_{1}-1-\frac{i\nu}{2})\sin\pi(\Delta_{4}-\frac{1}{2}-\frac{i\nu}{2})}\frac{\Gamma_{\Delta_{4}-\frac{1}{2}+\frac{i\nu}{2}}}{\Gamma_{-\Delta_{4}+\frac{3}{2}+\frac{i\nu}{2}}}\frac{\Gamma_{\Delta_{1}-1+\frac{i\nu}{2}}}{\Gamma_{2-\Delta_{1}+\frac{i\nu}{2}}}Z^{i\nu}

Now we close the contour and pick up poles from

\displaystyle g_{4}(z,\bar{z})\ni\frac{8\pi^{3}\sqrt{\frac{u}{v}}Z\widetilde{Z}}{\cos\pi(\Delta_{1}-\Delta_{4})}\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\frac{\Gamma_{\Delta_{1}+\Delta_{4}-\frac{3}{2}+m}}{\Gamma_{\Delta_{4}-\Delta_{1}+\frac{3}{2}+m}}\frac{\Gamma_{2\Delta_{4}-1+m}}{\Gamma_{m+1}}\tilde{F}_{2\Delta_{4}-1+2m}Z^{2\Delta_{4}-1+2m}

\displaystyle+\Big(\Delta_{1}\leftrightarrow\Delta_{4}+\frac{1}{2}\Big)

\tilde{F}_{\nu}

Double-discontinuity

We can also take the double-discontinuity of the 4-point correlator in Eq.

Where we use

\displaystyle dDisc_{s}\big[\mathcal{K}^{\Delta_{i}}_{\frac{d}{2}+i\nu}(z,\bar{z})\big]=\sin\pi\big(\frac{\Delta_{1}+\Delta_{2}}{2}-\frac{i\nu+\frac{d}{2}}{2}\big)\sin\pi\big(\frac{\Delta_{3}+\Delta_{4}}{2}-\frac{i\nu+\frac{d}{2}}{2}\big)\mathcal{K}^{\Delta_{i}}_{\frac{d}{2}+i\nu}(z,\bar{z})
