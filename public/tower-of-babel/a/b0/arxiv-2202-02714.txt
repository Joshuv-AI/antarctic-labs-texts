Painlevé-type asymptotics for the defocusing Hirota equation in transition region
Weikang XUN, Luman JU, Engui FAN

Abstract
We consider the Cauchy problem for the classical Hirota equation on the line with decaying initial data. Based on the spectral analysis of the Lax pair of the Hirota equation, we first expressed the solution of the Cauchy problem in terms of the solution of a Riemann-Hilbert problem. Further we apply nonlinear steepest descent analysis to obtain the long-time asymptotics of the solution in the critical transition region | x t − α 2 3 ​ β | ​ t 2 / 3 ≤ M |\frac{x}{t}-\frac{\alpha^{2}}{3\beta}|t^{2/3}\leq M , M M is a positive constant. Our result shows that the long time asymptotics of the Hirota equation can be expressed in terms of the solution of Painlevé II \mathrm{II} equation. Keywords: Hirota equation, steepest descent method, Painlevé II \mathrm{II} equation, long-time asymptotics. Subject Classification: 35Q51; 35Q15; 37K15; 35C20.

2
Riemann-Hilbert problem

We consider the Cauchy problem for defocusing Hirota equation

\displaystyle iu_{t}+\alpha(u_{xx}-2|u|^{2}u)+i\beta(u_{xxx}-6|u|^{2}u_{x})=0,\ t>0,x\in\mathbb{R},

(2.1)

\displaystyle u(x,0)=u_{0}(x)\in\mathcal{S}\mathbb{(R)},

where
\alpha\in\mathbb{R}
,
\beta\in\mathbb{R}^{+}
. The Lax pair corresponding to (
2.1

\phi_{x}=P\phi,\qquad\phi_{t}=Q\phi,

(2.2)

where

\displaystyle\phi=\left(\begin{array}[]{lr}\phi_{1}\\
\phi_{2}\end{array}\right),\quad P=-ik\sigma_{3}+U,\quad U=\begin{pmatrix}0&u\\
\overline{u}&0\end{pmatrix},

(2.3)

\displaystyle Q=-4i\beta\sigma_{3}k^{3}-2i\alpha\sigma_{3}k^{2}+V,\quad V=V_{2}k^{2}+V_{1}k+V_{0},

\displaystyle V_{2}=4\beta U,\quad V_{1}=\begin{pmatrix}-2i\beta|u|^{2}&2i\beta u_{x}+2\alpha u\\
-2i\beta\overline{u}_{x}+2\alpha\overline{u}&2i\beta|u|^{2}\end{pmatrix},

\displaystyle V_{0}=\begin{pmatrix}-i\alpha|u|^{2}+\beta(-u\overline{u}_{x}+u_{x}\overline{u})&i\alpha u_{x}-\beta(u_{xx}-2|u|^{2}u)\\
-i\alpha\overline{u}_{x}+\beta(-\overline{u}_{xx}+2|u|^{2}\overline{u})&i\alpha|u|^{2}-\beta(-u\overline{u}_{x}+u_{x}\overline{u})\end{pmatrix},

and

denotes the complex conjugate of

\displaystyle\sigma_{1}=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\
1&0\end{pmatrix},\quad\sigma_{2}=\begin{pmatrix}0&i\\
-i&0\end{pmatrix},\quad\sigma_{3}=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\
0&-1\end{pmatrix}.

Considering the asymptotic property of

\phi=\Phi e^{[-ikx-(4i\beta k^{3}+2i\alpha k^{2})t]\sigma_{3}},

\displaystyle\Phi_{x}+ik[\sigma_{3},\Phi]=U\Phi,

(2.5)

\displaystyle\Phi_{t}+(2i\alpha k^{2}+4i\beta k^{3})[\sigma_{3},\Phi]=V\Phi.

We first define two solutions of the spectral problem

\displaystyle\Phi_{-}(x,t,k)=\mathrm{I}+\int_{-\infty}^{x}e^{-ik(x-y)\hat{\sigma}_{3}}U(y,t)\Phi_{-}(y,t,k)dy,

(2.6)

\displaystyle\Phi_{+}(x,t,k)=\mathrm{I}-\int^{\infty}_{x}e^{-ik(x-y)\hat{\sigma}_{3}}U(y,t)\Phi_{+}(y,t,k)dy.

s_{21}(k)&s_{22}(k)\end{pmatrix},\quad\theta(k)=4\beta k^{3}+2\alpha k^{2}+k\xi,\quad\xi=\frac{x}{t}.

Moreover, we have the critical symmetry

In this work, we have investigated the long time asymptotic solution of the Cauchy problem for defocusing Hirota equation with decaying data in the special transition region
|\frac{x}{t}-\frac{\alpha^{2}}{3\beta}|t^{2/3}\leq M
,
M
is a positive constant. Based on the Riemann-Hilbert prblem which is established in
[
17
]
, we perform the nonliear steepest descent method to analysis the asymptotic properties of the solution in the left and right transition regions, respectively. What is meaningful is that we find the solution of defocusing Hirota equation can be approximated in terms of the real-valued solution of Painlevé
\mathrm{II}
equation with the error
O(t^{-\frac{2}{3}})
. What’s more, as for the focusing Hirota equation, we can derive the asymptotic formula of the solution in transition regions by nonlinear steepest descent method. It’s worth noting that the result is similar to that of defocusing case except that other errors caused by soliton asymptotics.
