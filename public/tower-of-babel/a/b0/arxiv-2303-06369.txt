The product of Lindelöf groups and ℝ \mathbb{R} -factorizability
Evgeny Reznichenko

Abstract
Lindelöf topological groups 𝖦 𝟣 \mathsf{{G}_{1}} , 𝖧 𝟣 \mathsf{{H}_{1}} , 𝖦 𝟤 \mathsf{{G}_{2}} , 𝖧 𝟤 \mathsf{{H}_{2}} are constructed in such a way that the products of 𝖦 𝟣 × 𝖧 𝟣 \mathsf{{G}_{1}}\times\mathsf{{H}_{1}} and 𝖦 𝟤 × 𝖧 𝟤 \mathsf{{G}_{2}}\times\mathsf{{H}_{2}} are not ℝ \mathbb{R} -factorizable groups and (1) the group 𝖦 𝟣 × 𝖧 𝟣 \mathsf{{G}_{1}}\times\mathsf{{H}_{1}} is not pseudo- ℵ 1 \aleph_{1} -compact; (2) the group 𝖦 𝟤 × 𝖧 𝟤 \mathsf{{G}_{2}}\times\mathsf{{H}_{2}} is a separable not normal group and contains a discrete closed subset of the cardinality 2 ω 2^{\omega} .

Lemma 1

If a separately continuous function

is separately
\mathsf{TS}_{cnw}
-factorizable, then
\varphi(X)
has a countable network, where

\varphi:X\to C_{p}(Y),\ \varphi(x)(y)=f(x,y).

Proof.

Since

Y_{*}

g_{Y}:Y\to Y_{*}

\psi:X_{*}\to C_{p}(Y),\ \psi(x_{*},y)=h(x_{*},g_{Y}(y))

\varphi=g_{X}\circ\psi

Proposition 2

(1)

\varphi(x)(y)=\begin{cases}0,&\text{if }y\geq x,\\

The uncountable space
