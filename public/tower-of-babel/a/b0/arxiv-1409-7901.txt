Interface control of ferroelectricity in LaNiO 3 -BaTiO 3 superlattices
Yin-Zhong Wu 1,3, , Hai-Shuang Lu 2 , Tian-Yi Cai 2 & Sheng Ju 2,

Abstract
LaNiO 3 -BaTiO 3 superlattices with different types of interfaces are studied from first-principles density-functional theory. It is revealed that the ferroelectricity in the superlattice with (NiO 2 ) - /(BaO) 0 interfaces is enhanced from that of the superlattice with (LaO) + /(TiO 2 ) 0 interfaces. The origin lies at the polar discontinuity at the interface, which makes the holes localized within the (NiO 2 ) - /(BaO) 0 interface, but drives a penetration of electrons into BaTiO 3 component near (LaO) + /(TiO 2 ) 0 interface. Our calculations demonstrate an effective avenue to the robust ferroelectricity in BaTiO 3 ultrathin films.
