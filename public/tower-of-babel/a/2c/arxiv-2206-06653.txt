C*-ALGEBRAIC SCHOENBERG CONJECTURE
K. MAHESH KRISHNA

Abstract




Abstract
: Based on Schoenberg conjecture
[Amer. Math. Monthly., 1986]
/Malamud-Pereira theorem
[J. Math. Anal. Appl, 2003]
,
[Trans. Amer. Math. Soc., 2005]
we formulate the following conjecture which we call C*-algebraic Schoenberg Conjecture.

C*-algebraic Schoenberg Conjecture : Let
\mathcal{A}
be a C*-algebra. Let
d\in\mathbb{N}\setminus\{1\}
,
P(z)\coloneqq(z-a_{1})(z-a_{2})\cdots(z-a_{d})
be a polynomial over
\mathcal{A}
with
a_{1},a_{2},\dots,a_{d}\in\mathcal{A}
. If
P^{\prime}
can be written as
P^{\prime}(z)=d(z-b_{1})(z-b_{2})\cdots(z-b_{d-1})
on
\mathcal{A}
with
b_{1},b_{2},\dots,b_{d-1}\in\mathcal{A}
, then

\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{d-1}b_{k}b_{k}^{*}\leq\frac{1}{d^{2}}\left[\sum_{j=1}^{d}a_{j}\right]\left[\sum_{j=1}^{d}a_{j}\right]^{*}+\frac{d-2}{d}\sum_{j=1}^{d}a_{j}a_{j}^{*}

and

\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{d-1}b_{k}^{*}b_{k}\leq\frac{1}{d^{2}}\left[\sum_{j=1}^{d}a_{j}\right]^{*}\left[\sum_{j=1}^{d}a_{j}\right]+\frac{d-2}{d}\sum_{j=1}^{d}a_{j}^{*}a_{j}.

We show that C*-algebraic Schoenberg conjecture holds for degree 2 C*-algebraic polynomials over C*-algebras.

Keywords
: C*-algebra, Schoenberg conjecture.

\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{d-1}|b_{k}|^{2}\leq\frac{1}{d^{2}}\left|\sum_{j=1}^{d}a_{j}\right|^{2}+\frac{d-2}{d}\sum_{j=1}^{d}|a_{j}|^{2}.

Conjecture 1.2

(de Bruin-Sharma Conjecture/Cheung-Ng Theorem)
Let

\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{d-1}|b_{k}|^{4}\leq\frac{2}{d^{2}}\left(\sum_{j=1}^{d}|a_{j}|^{2}\right)^{2}+\frac{d-4}{d}\sum_{j=1}^{d}|a_{j}|^{4}.

Theorem 1.3
.

[

(Kushel-Tyaglov Theorem)
Let
P(z)\coloneqq(z-a_{1})(z-a_{2})\cdots(z-a_{d})
be a degree
d\geq 2
polynomial over
\mathbb{C}
. Let
b_{1},b_{2},\dots,b_{d-1}
be zeros of
P^{\prime}

\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{d-1}|b_{k}|^{4}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{d-6}{d}\sum_{j=1}^{d}|a_{j}|^{4}+\frac{1}{d^{2}}\left(\sum_{j=1}^{d}|a_{j}|^{2}\right)^{2}+\frac{1}{d^{2}}\left|\sum_{j=1}^{d}a_{j}^{2}-\frac{1}{d^{2}}\left(\sum_{k=1}^{d}a_{k}\right)^{2}\right|^{2}

2.
C*-algebraic Schoenberg conjecture

Let
\mathcal{A}
be a C*-algebra. Given
P(z)\coloneqq(z-a_{1})(z-a_{2})\cdots(z-a_{d})
for all
z\in\mathcal{A}
with
a_{1},a_{2},\dots,a_{d}\in\mathcal{A}
, we define

\displaystyle P^{\prime}(z)=\sum_{j=1}^{d}(z-a_{1})\cdots\widehat{(z-a_{j})}\cdots(z-a_{d}),\quad\forall z\in\mathcal{A}

where the term with cap is missing.
We state C*-algebraic version of Conjecture
1.1
as follows.

Conjecture 2.1
.

(C*-algebraic Schoenberg Conjecture)
Let
\mathcal{A}
be a C*-algebra. Let
d\in\mathbb{N}\setminus\{1\}
,
P(z)\coloneqq(z-a_{1})(z-a_{2})\cdots(z-a_{d})
be a polynomial over
\mathcal{A}
with
a_{1},a_{2},\dots,a_{d}\in\mathcal{A}
. If
P^{\prime}
can be written as
P^{\prime}(z)=d(z-b_{1})(z-b_{2})\cdots(z-b_{d-1})
on
\mathcal{A}
with
b_{1},b_{2},\dots,b_{d-1}\in\mathcal{A}
,
then

\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{d-1}b_{k}b_{k}^{*}\leq\frac{1}{d^{2}}\left[\sum_{j=1}^{d}a_{j}\right]\left[\sum_{j=1}^{d}a_{j}\right]^{*}+\frac{d-2}{d}\sum_{j=1}^{d}a_{j}a_{j}^{*}

and

\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{d-1}b_{k}^{*}b_{k}\leq\frac{1}{d^{2}}\left[\sum_{j=1}^{d}a_{j}\right]^{*}\left[\sum_{j=1}^{d}a_{j}\right]+\frac{d-2}{d}\sum_{j=1}^{d}a_{j}^{*}a_{j}.

Theorem 2.2
.

Conjecture
2.1
holds for C*-algebraic polynomials of degree 2.

Proof.

Let
\mathcal{A}
be a C*-algebra and
P(z)\coloneqq(z-a)(z-b)
be a degree 2 polynomial over
\mathcal{A}
,
a,b\in\mathcal{A}
. Then the zero of
P^{\prime}
is
c=\frac{a+b}{2}
. Therefore

\displaystyle cc^{*}=\frac{(a+b)(a^{*}+b^{*})}{4}=\frac{(a+b)(a^{*}+b^{*})}{4}+\frac{2-2}{2}(aa^{*}+bb^{*})

and

\displaystyle c^{*}c=\frac{(a^{*}+b^{*})(a+b)}{4}=\frac{(a^{*}+b^{*})(a+b)}{4}+\frac{2-2}{2}(a^{*}a+b^{*}b).

Therefore both inequalities in Conjecture
2.1
hold with equality.
∎

We state the C*-algebraic version of Conjecture
1.2
as follows.

Conjecture 2.3
.

(C*-algebraic de Bruin-Sharma Conjecture)
Let
\mathcal{A}
be a C*-algebra,
d\in\mathbb{N}\setminus\{1\}
and let
P(z)\coloneqq(z-a_{1})(z-a_{2})\cdots(z-a_{d})
be a polynomial over
\mathcal{A}
with
a_{1},a_{2},\dots,a_{d}\in\mathcal{A}
. Assume that
P^{\prime}
can be written as
P^{\prime}(z)\coloneqq d(z-b_{1})\cdots(z-b_{d-1})
on
\mathcal{A}
with
b_{1},b_{2},\dots,b_{d-1}\in\mathcal{A}
. If

\displaystyle\sum_{j=1}^{d}a_{j}=0,

then

\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{d-1}(b_{k}b_{k}^{*})^{2}\leq\frac{2}{d^{2}}\left(\sum_{j=1}^{d}a_{j}a_{j}^{*}\right)^{2}+\frac{d-4}{d}\sum_{j=1}^{d}(a_{j}a_{j}^{*})^{2}

and

\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{d-1}(b_{k}^{*}b_{k})^{2}\leq\frac{2}{d^{2}}\left(\sum_{j=1}^{d}a_{j}^{*}a_{j}\right)^{2}+\frac{d-4}{d}\sum_{j=1}^{d}(a_{j}^{*}a_{j})^{2}.

It is clear that Conjecture
2.3
holds for C*-algebraic polynomials of degree 2. We next formulate a strengthening of Conjecture
2.3
based on Theorem
1.3
.

Conjecture 2.4
.

(C*-algebraic Kushel-Tyaglov Conjecture)
Let
\mathcal{A}
be a C*-algebra,
n\in\mathbb{N}\setminus\{1\}
and let
P(z)\coloneqq(z-a_{1})(z-a_{2})\cdots(z-a_{d})
be a polynomial over
\mathcal{A}
with
a_{1},a_{2},\dots,a_{d}\in\mathcal{A}
. Assume that
P^{\prime}
can be written as
P^{\prime}(z)\coloneqq d(z-b_{1})\cdots(z-b_{d-1})
on
\mathcal{A}
with
b_{1},b_{2},\dots,b_{d-1}\in\mathcal{A}
. Then

\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{d-1}(b_{k}b_{k}^{*})^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{d-6}{d}\sum_{j=1}^{n}(a_{j}a_{j}^{*})^{2}+\frac{1}{d^{2}}\left(\sum_{j=1}^{d}a_{j}a_{j}^{*}\right)^{2}+\frac{1}{d^{2}}\left[\sum_{j=1}^{d}a_{j}^{2}-\frac{1}{d^{2}}\left(\sum_{k=1}^{d}a_{k}\right)^{2}\right]\left[\sum_{j=1}^{d}a_{j}^{2}-\frac{1}{d^{2}}\left(\sum_{k=1}^{d}a_{k}\right)^{2}\right]^{*}

\displaystyle+\frac{2}{d}\sum_{j=1}^{d}a_{j}\left[a_{j}+\frac{1}{d}\sum_{k=1}^{d}a_{k}\right]\left[a_{j}+\frac{1}{d}\sum_{k=1}^{d}a_{k}\right]^{*}a_{j}^{*}-\frac{4}{d^{3}}\sum_{j=1}^{d}a_{j}\left[\sum_{k=1}^{d}a_{k}\right]\left[\sum_{k=1}^{d}a_{k}\right]^{*}a_{j}^{*}

and

\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{d-1}(b_{k}^{*}b_{k})^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{d-6}{d}\sum_{j=1}^{d}(a_{j}^{*}a_{j})^{2}+\frac{1}{d^{2}}\left(\sum_{j=1}^{d}a_{j}^{*}a_{j}\right)^{2}+\frac{1}{d^{2}}\left[\sum_{j=1}^{d}a_{j}^{2}-\frac{1}{d^{2}}\left(\sum_{k=1}^{d}a_{k}\right)^{2}\right]^{*}\left[\sum_{j=1}^{d}a_{j}^{2}-\frac{1}{d^{2}}\left(\sum_{k=1}^{d}a_{k}\right)^{2}\right]

\displaystyle+\frac{2}{d}\sum_{j=1}^{n}a_{j}^{*}\left[a_{j}+\frac{1}{d}\sum_{k=1}^{d}a_{k}\right]^{*}\left[a_{j}+\frac{1}{d}\sum_{k=1}^{d}a_{k}\right]a_{j}-\frac{4}{d^{3}}\sum_{j=1}^{d}a_{j}^{*}\left[\sum_{k=1}^{d}a_{k}\right]^{*}\left[\sum_{k=1}^{d}a_{k}\right]a_{j}.
