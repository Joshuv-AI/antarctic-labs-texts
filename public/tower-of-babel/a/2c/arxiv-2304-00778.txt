Introduction

In the present paper, we aim at investigating local regularity for the minimizers of the following functional:

\displaystyle\mathcal{E}(u;\Omega):=\int_{\Omega}(F(x,Du)-f(x)u)\,dx+\iint_{\mathcal{C}_{\Omega}}|u(x)-u(y)|^{q}K_{sq}(x,y)\,dxdy,

(1.1)

where
\Omega\subset\mathbb{R}^{N}
(
N\geq 2
) is a bounded domain and

\mathcal{C}_{\Omega}:=(\mathbb{R}^{N}\times\mathbb{R}^{N})\setminus((\mathbb{R}^{N}\times\Omega)\times(\mathbb{R}^{N}\times\Omega)).

The Carathéodory function
F:\Omega\times\mathbb{R}^{N}\rightarrow\mathbb{R}
satisfies

\displaystyle\Lambda^{-1}|\xi|^{p}\leq F(x,\xi)\leq\Lambda|\xi|^{p}

(1.2)

for some
\Lambda>1
. The symmetric kernel
K_{sq}:\mathbb{R}^{N}\times\mathbb{R}^{N}\rightarrow\mathbb{R}
is a measurable function taking the form

\displaystyle K_{sq}(x,y)=\frac{a(x,y)}{|x-y|^{N+sq}}

(1.3)

with
a(x,y)=a(y,x)
satisfying

\displaystyle a_{0}\leq a(x,y)\leq A_{0}\quad\text{for all }x,y\in\mathbb{R}^{N}\times\mathbb{R}^{N},

(1.4)

where the constants
a_{0}\geq 0
and
A_{0}>0
. In addition,

1<p\leq sq,\quad s\in(0,1).

(1.5)

In order to state the definition of minimizers, we need to define a function space

\mathcal{A}(\Omega):=\left\{v:\mathbb{R}^{N}\rightarrow\mathbb{R}~\Big|~v|_{\Omega}\in L^{p}(\Omega)\text{ and }\mathcal{E}(v;\Omega)<\infty\right\},

and recall the tail space

L_{sq}^{q-1}\left(\mathbb{R}^{N}\right):=\left\{v\in L_{\operatorname{loc}}^{q-1}\left(\mathbb{R}^{N}\right)\Big|\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}\frac{|v(x)|^{q-1}}{1+|x|^{N+sq}}dx<\infty\right\},

with a corresponding nonlocal tail defined by

\displaystyle\operatorname{Tail}(v;x_{0},r)

\displaystyle:=\left(r^{sq}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}\backslash B_{r}\left(x_{0}\right)}\frac{|v(x)|^{q-1}}{\left|x-x_{0}\right|^{N+sq}}dx\right)^{\frac{1}{q-1}}\quad\text{for }r>0,x_{0}\in\mathbb{R}^{N}.

From these definitions, it is easy to deduce that
\operatorname{Tail}(v;x_{0},r)
is well-defined for any

v\in L^{q-1}_{sq}(\mathbb{R}^{N})
.

Now we are in a position to present the definition of minimizers to (
1.1
) as follows.

Definition 1.1.

We say that
u\in\mathcal{A}(\Omega)
is a minimizer of
\mathcal{E}
if

\displaystyle\mathcal{E}(u;\Omega)\leq\mathcal{E}(v;\Omega)

for any measurable function
v:\mathbb{R}^{N}\rightarrow\mathbb{R}
with
v=u
a.e. in
\mathbb{R}^{N}\backslash\Omega
.

coming subsection.

We now recall a classical iteration lemma.

Lemma 1.3
.

(
[

Let

be a sequence of positive

. Moreover,

\displaystyle\mathchoice{{\vbox{\hbox{$\textstyle-$ }}\kern-7.83337pt}}{{\vbox{\hbox{$\scriptstyle-$ }}\kern-6.11674pt}}{{\vbox{\hbox{$\scriptscriptstyle-$ }}\kern-5.48965pt}}{{\vbox{\hbox{$\scriptscriptstyle-$ }}\kern-5.31259pt}}\!\int_{B_{r}}\left|\frac{f}{r^{s}}\right|^{q}\,dx

\displaystyle\leq C\mathchoice{{\vbox{\hbox{$\textstyle-$ }}\kern-7.83337pt}}{{\vbox{\hbox{$\scriptstyle-$ }}\kern-6.11674pt}}{{\vbox{\hbox{$\scriptscriptstyle-$ }}\kern-5.48965pt}}{{\vbox{\hbox{$\scriptscriptstyle-$ }}\kern-5.31259pt}}\!\int_{B_{r}}\left|\frac{f-(f)_{B_{r}}}{r^{s}}\right|^{q}dx+C\left|\frac{(f)_{Br}}{r^{s}}\right|^{q}

\displaystyle\leq C\left(\mathchoice{{\vbox{\hbox{$\textstyle-$ }}\kern-7.83337pt}}{{\vbox{\hbox{$\scriptstyle-$ }}\kern-6.11674pt}}{{\vbox{\hbox{$\scriptscriptstyle-$ }}\kern-5.48965pt}}{{\vbox{\hbox{$\scriptscriptstyle-$ }}\kern-5.31259pt}}\!\int_{B_{r}}\left|\frac{f-(f)_{B_{r}}}{r^{s}}\right|^{p_{q}^{*}}\,dx\right)^{\frac{q}{p_{q}^{*}}}+C\left|\frac{(f)_{B_{r}}}{r^{s}}\right|^{q}

\displaystyle\leq Cr^{(1-s)q}\left(\mathchoice{{\vbox{\hbox{$\textstyle-$ }}\kern-7.83337pt}}{{\vbox{\hbox{$\scriptstyle-$ }}\kern-6.11674pt}}{{\vbox{\hbox{$\scriptscriptstyle-$ }}\kern-5.48965pt}}{{\vbox{\hbox{$\scriptscriptstyle-$ }}\kern-5.31259pt}}\!\int_{B_{r}}|Df|^{p}\,dx\right)^{\frac{q}{p}}+C\left|\frac{(f)_{B_{r}}}{r^{s}}\right|^{q},

where

p_{q}^{*}=\begin{cases}p^{*}&\text{ for }1<p<N,\\
q&\text{ for }p\geq N.\end{cases}

In a similar way, we get

\displaystyle\mathchoice{{\vbox{\hbox{$\textstyle-$ }}\kern-7.83337pt}}{{\vbox{\hbox{$\scriptstyle-$ }}\kern-6.11674pt}}{{\vbox{\hbox{$\scriptscriptstyle-$ }}\kern-5.48965pt}}{{\vbox{\hbox{$\scriptscriptstyle-$ }}\kern-5.31259pt}}\!\int_{B_{r}}\left|\frac{f}{r}\right|^{p}dx

\displaystyle\leq C\mathchoice{{\vbox{\hbox{$\textstyle-$ }}\kern-7.83337pt}}{{\vbox{\hbox{$\scriptstyle-$ }}\kern-6.11674pt}}{{\vbox{\hbox{$\scriptscriptstyle-$ }}\kern-5.48965pt}}{{\vbox{\hbox{$\scriptscriptstyle-$ }}\kern-5.31259pt}}\!\int_{B_{r}}\left|\frac{f-(f)_{B_{r}}}{r}\right|^{p}\chi_{\{f\neq 0\}}\,dx+C\left|\frac{(f)_{Br}}{r}\right|^{p}

\displaystyle\leq C\left(\frac{|\operatorname{supp}f|}{\left|B_{r}\right|}\right)^{\sigma}\left(\mathchoice{{\vbox{\hbox{$\textstyle-$ }}\kern-7.83337pt}}{{\vbox{\hbox{$\scriptstyle-$ }}\kern-6.11674pt}}{{\vbox{\hbox{$\scriptscriptstyle-$ }}\kern-5.48965pt}}{{\vbox{\hbox{$\scriptscriptstyle-$ }}\kern-5.31259pt}}\!\int_{B_{r}}\left|\frac{f-(f)_{B_{r}}}{r}\right|^{p_{q}^{*}}dx\right)^{\frac{p}{p_{q}^{*}}}+C\left|\frac{(f)_{B_{r}}}{r}\right|^{p}

\displaystyle\leq C\left(\frac{|\operatorname{supp}f|}{\left|B_{r}\right|}\right)^{\sigma}\mathchoice{{\vbox{\hbox{$\textstyle-$ }}\kern-7.83337pt}}{{\vbox{\hbox{$\scriptstyle-$ }}\kern-6.11674pt}}{{\vbox{\hbox{$\scriptscriptstyle-$ }}\kern-5.48965pt}}{{\vbox{\hbox{$\scriptscriptstyle-$ }}\kern-5.31259pt}}\!\int_{B_{r}}|Df|^{p}dx+C\left|\frac{(f)_{B_{r}}}{r}\right|^{p}.

It follows from the Hölder inequality that

\displaystyle L_{0}\left|\frac{(f)_{B_{r}}}{r^{s}}\right|^{q}+\left|\frac{(f)_{B_{r}}}{r}\right|^{p}

\displaystyle\leq L_{0}r^{-sq}\left(\frac{|\operatorname{supp}f|}{\left|B_{r}\right|}\right)^{q-1}\mathchoice{{\vbox{\hbox{$\textstyle-$ }}\kern-7.83337pt}}{{\vbox{\hbox{$\scriptstyle-$ }}\kern-6.11674pt}}{{\vbox{\hbox{$\scriptscriptstyle-$ }}\kern-5.48965pt}}{{\vbox{\hbox{$\scriptscriptstyle-$ }}\kern-5.31259pt}}\!\int_{B_{r}}|f|^{q}dx+r^{-p}\left(\frac{|\operatorname{supp}f|}{\left|B_{r}\right|}\right)^{p-1}\int_{B_{r}}|f|^{p}dx

\displaystyle\leq\left(\frac{|\operatorname{supp}f|}{\left|B_{r}\right|}\right)^{p-1}\mathchoice{{\vbox{\hbox{$\textstyle-$ }}\kern-7.83337pt}}{{\vbox{\hbox{$\scriptstyle-$ }}\kern-6.11674pt}}{{\vbox{\hbox{$\scriptscriptstyle-$ }}\kern-5.48965pt}}{{\vbox{\hbox{$\scriptscriptstyle-$ }}\kern-5.31259pt}}\!\int\left|\frac{f}{r}\right|^{p}+L_{0}\left|\frac{f}{r^{s}}\right|^{q}\,dx.

A combination of the above estimates results in the claim.
∎

2
Caccioppoli estimates

This section is devoted to establishing the Caccioppoli estimates that encodes all the information needed to show (local) regularity of minimizers including boundedness, Hölder continuity and Harnack estimates.

Lemma 2.1

(Caccioppoli inequality)
.

Let
B_{2R}\equiv B_{2R}(x_{0})\subset\subset\Omega
. Suppose that
u\in\mathcal{A}(\Omega)
is a minimizer to (
1.1
). Then there exists a positive constant

only depending on

such that for any

\displaystyle\leq\frac{C}{(r-\rho)^{p}}\int_{B_{r}}w_{\pm}^{p}dx+\frac{C}{(r-\rho)^{q}}\int_{B_{r}}\int_{B_{r}}a(x,y)\frac{|w_{\pm}(x)+w_{\pm}(y)|^{q}}{|x-y|^{N+(s-1)q}}\,dxdy

\displaystyle\quad+\frac{Cr^{N+sq}}{(r-\rho)^{N+sq}}\int_{B_{r}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}\backslash B_{\rho}}a(x,y)\frac{w_{\pm}^{q-1}(x)w_{\pm}(y)}{\left|x-x_{0}\right|^{N+sq}}dxdy+C\int_{B_{r}}|f|w_{\pm}\,dx,

where
w_{\pm}=(u-k)_{\pm}
with a level

.

Proof.

We only give the sketch of proof, because that is similar to
[
18
, Proposition 7.5]
. Choose any
\rho_{1},r_{1}
satisfying
\rho\leq\rho_{1}<r_{1}\leq r
. Let
\phi\in C_{0}^{\infty}\left(B_{(r_{1}+\rho_{1})/2}\right)
be a cut-off function satisfying
0\leq\phi\leq 1
,
\phi\equiv 1
in
B_{\rho_{1}}
and

. Using

as a test function in (
1.1
), we can see

\displaystyle 0\leq\int_{\Omega}\big(F(x,Dv)-F(x,Du)\big)dx+\int_{B_{r_{1}}}\int_{B_{r_{1}}}a(x,y)\big(|v(x)-v(y)|^{q}-|u(x)-u(y)|^{q}\big)d\mu

\displaystyle\quad+2\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}\backslash B_{r_{1}}}\int_{B_{r_{1}}}a(x,y)\big(|v(x)-v(y)|^{q}-|u(x)-u(y)|^{q}\big)d\mu+\int_{B_{r_{1}}}(v-u)f(x)dx

\displaystyle=:I_{1}+I_{2}+I_{3}+I_{4}.

(2.1)

We estimate terms
I_{1}
,
I_{2}
,
I_{3}
and
I_{4}
in (
2
) separately. For
I_{1}
, we obtain

\displaystyle I_{1}\leq C\int_{B_{r_{1}}\backslash B_{\rho_{1}}}\left|Dw_{+}\right|^{p}dx+C\int_{B_{r}}\left|\frac{w_{+}}{r_{1}-\rho_{1}}\right|^{p}dx-\Lambda^{-1}\int_{B_{r_{1}}}\left|Dw_{+}\right|^{p}dx.

The details can be found in
[
10
, Page 7]
. Following the computations in
[
18
, P4819–4821]
, we know that

\displaystyle I_{2}+I_{3}

\displaystyle\leq-\frac{1}{C}\int_{B_{\rho_{1}}}\int_{B_{\rho_{1}}}a(x,y)\left|w_{+}(x)-w_{+}(y)\right|^{q}d\mu

\displaystyle\quad-\frac{1}{C}\int_{B_{\rho_{1}}}w_{+}(x)\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}\frac{a(x,y)w^{q-1}_{-}(y)}{|x-y|^{N+sq}}dy\right)dx

\displaystyle\quad+C\iint_{(B_{r_{1}}\times B_{r_{1}})\backslash(B_{\rho_{1}}\times B_{\rho_{1}})}a(x,y)\left|w_{+}(x)-w_{+}(y)\right|^{q}d\mu

\displaystyle\quad+\frac{C}{(r_{1}-\rho_{1})^{q}}\int_{B_{r_{1}}}\int_{B_{r_{1}}}a(x,y)\frac{|w_{+}(x)+w_{+}(y)|^{q}}{|x-y|^{N+(s-1)q}}\,dxdy

\displaystyle\quad+\frac{Cr^{N+sq}}{(r_{1}-\rho_{1})^{N+sq}}\int_{B_{r}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}\backslash B_{\rho}}\frac{a(x,y)w_{+}^{q-1}(x)w_{+}(y)}{\left|x-x_{0}\right|^{N+sq}}dxdy

with some universal
C\geq 1
. For the term
I_{4}
, it is easy to see

\displaystyle(v-u)f(x)=\phi(x)w_{+}(x)f(x),

which directly gives the estimate of
I_{4}
as below,

\displaystyle I_{4}\leq\int_{B_{r}}w_{+}(x)|f(x)|dx.

We can conclude from the estimates of
I_{1}
–
I_{4}
that

\displaystyle\int_{B_{\rho_{1}}}\int_{B_{\rho_{1}}}a(x,y)\left|w_{+}(x)-w_{+}(y)\right|^{q}d\mu+\int_{B_{\rho_{1}}}\left|Dw_{+}\right|^{p}dx

\displaystyle\quad+\int_{B_{\rho_{1}}}w_{+}(x)\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}\frac{a(x,y)w^{q-1}_{-}(y)}{|x-y|^{N+sq}}dy\right)dx

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C\bigg(\int_{B_{r_{1}}\backslash B_{\rho_{1}}}\int_{B_{r_{1}}\backslash B_{\rho_{1}}}a(x,y)\left|w_{+}(x)-w_{+}(y)\right|^{q}d\mu+\int_{B_{r_{1}}\backslash B_{\rho_{1}}}\left|Dw_{+}\right|^{p}dx

\displaystyle\quad+\frac{1}{(r_{1}-\rho_{1})^{q}}\int_{B_{r_{1}}}\int_{B_{r_{1}}}a(x,y)\frac{|w_{+}(x)+w_{+}(y)|^{q}}{|x-y|^{N+(s-1)q}}\,dxdy+\frac{1}{(r_{1}-\rho_{1})^{p}}\int_{B_{r}}w_{+}^{p}dx

\displaystyle\quad+\frac{r^{N+sq}}{(r_{1}-\rho_{1})^{N+sq}}\int_{B_{r}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}\backslash B_{\rho}}\frac{a(x,y)w_{+}^{q-1}(x)w_{+}(y)}{\left|x-x_{0}\right|^{N+sq}}dxdy+\int_{B_{r}}w_{+}(x)|f(x)|dx\bigg).

(2.2)

Let us define

\displaystyle\Phi(t)=

\displaystyle\int_{B_{t}}\int_{B_{t}}a(x,y)\left|w_{+}(x)-w_{+}(y)\right|^{q}d\mu+\int_{B_{t}}\left|Dw_{+}\right|^{p}dx

\displaystyle+\int_{B_{t}}w_{+}(x)\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}\frac{a(x,y)w^{q-1}_{-}(y)}{|x-y|^{N+sq}}dy\right)dx,\qquad t>0.

Then it follows by (

\displaystyle\Phi\left(\rho_{1}\right)

\displaystyle\leq C\big(\Phi\left(r_{1}\right)-\Phi\left(\rho_{1}\right)\big)+\frac{C}{(r_{1}-\rho_{1})^{q}}\int_{B_{r_{1}}}\int_{B_{r_{1}}}a(x,y)\frac{|w_{+}(x)+w_{+}(y)|^{q}}{|x-y|^{N+(s-1)q}}\,dxdy

\displaystyle+\frac{C}{(r_{1}-\rho_{1})^{p}}\int_{B_{r}}w_{+}^{p}dx+\frac{Cr^{N+sq}}{(r_{1}-\rho_{1})^{N+sq}}\int_{B_{r}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}\backslash B_{\rho}}\frac{a(x,y)w_{+}^{q-1}(x)w_{+}(y)}{\left|x-x_{0}\right|^{N+sq}}dxdy

\displaystyle+C\int_{B_{r}}w_{+}(x)|f(x)|dx

with

.
This allows us to apply the the technical lemma

to arrive at the desired estimate.
