On β \beta -function of N = 2 N=2 supersymmetric integrable sigma-models
Mikhail Alfimov, Ivan Kalinichenko, and Alexey Litvinov

Abstract
We study regularization scheme dependence of Kähler ( N = 2 N=2 ) supersymmetric sigma models. At the one-loop order the metric β \beta function is the same as in non-supersymmetric case and coincides with the Ricci tensor. First correction in MS scheme is known to appear in the fourth loop Grisaru:1986px ; Grisaru:1986dk . We show that for certain integrable Kähler backgrounds, such as complete T − T- dual of η \eta -deformed ℂ ​ ℙ ​ ( n ) \mathbb{CP}(n) sigma models, there is a scheme in which the fourth loop contribution vanishes.

Two-dimensional sigma models

Let us remind basic concepts of

Withing this scaling the

\beta_{ij}^{(L)}\sim\hbar^{L-1}\,.

(9)

For bosonic sigma-models first four coefficients

;

;

-\frac{1}{2}R_{imnk}R_{jpq}^{\hskip 8.19447ptk}R^{mqnp}-\frac{3}{8}R_{iklj}R^{kmnp}R^{l}_{\,\,mnp}\,.\end{multlined}\end{gathered}
