Looking for the exotic X 0 ​ ( 2866 ) X_{0}(2866) and its J P = 1 + J^{P}=1^{+} partner in the B ¯ 0 → D ( ∗ ) + ​ K − ​ K ( ∗ ) ​ 0 \bar{B}^{0}\to D^{(*)+}K^{-}K^{(*)0} reactions
L. R. Dai, R. Molina, E. Oset

Abstract
We propose two reactions, B ¯ 0 → K 0 ​ D + ​ K − \bar{B}^{0}\to K^{0}D^{+}K^{-} and B ¯ 0 → K ∗ 0 ​ D ∗ ⁣ + ​ K − \bar{B}^{0}\to K^{*0}D^{*+}K^{-} , already measured at Belle, to look into the J P = 0 + J^{P}=0^{+} , X 0 ​ ( 2866 ) X_{0}(2866) state and a 1 + 1^{+} partner of molecular D ∗ ​ K ¯ ∗ D^{*}\bar{K}^{*} nature, by looking at the D + ​ K − D^{+}K^{-} and D ∗ ⁣ + ​ K − D^{*+}K^{-} invariant mass distributions, respectively. Very clear peaks over the background are predicted and the branching ratios for the production of these states are evaluated to facilitate the task of determining the needed statistics for their observation. The fact that the K ∗ 0 ​ K − K^{*0}K^{-} mass distribution from accumulation of events of different reactions is already available, indicates that one is not far away from having the needed statistics to see peaks in the D ( ∗ ) + ​ K − D^{(*)+}K^{-} mass distributions, so far not yet analyzed.

We have chosen two reactions, already performed by the Belle collaboration

of Ref.

we have selected two, the
\bar{B}^{0}\to K^{*0}D^{*+}K^{-}
and
\bar{B}^{0}\to K^{0}D^{+}K^{-}

1^{+}
and
0^{+}

first case the signal of the
1^{+}
state stems from the original
\bar{B}^{0}\to K^{*0}D^{*+}K^{*-}
reaction, followed by
D^{*+}K^{*-}
interaction
to give the

. In the second case, the signal for the

state comes from the

\bar{B}^{0}\to K^{0}D^{*+}K^{*-}
with posterior

interaction to produce the
0^{+}
state, which decays lately into

relate the mass distributions of the signal and the background, finding very clear peaks for the
1^{+}
and

we have argued that in the case of the

These reactions, already measured at Belle
belle
, would need somewhat more statistics to show the

peaks clearly. To facilitate the task
of determining the needed statistics, we have performed the integrated widths and we find

\displaystyle\Gamma(\bar{B}^{0}\to K^{*0}1^{+};1^{+}\to D^{*+}K^{-})/\Gamma(\bar{B}^{0}\to K^{*0}D^{*+}K^{-})=1.25\times 10^{-1}\,,

\displaystyle\Gamma(\bar{B}^{0}\to K^{0}0^{+};0^{+}\to D^{+}K^{-})/\Gamma(\bar{B}^{0}\to K^{0}D^{+}K^{-})=1.24\times 10^{-1}\,.

Together with the branching ratios of the backgrounds of

related to the existence of the
