Moduli of codimension two linear sections of subadjoint varieties
Yingqi Liu

Abstract
. Let G \displaystyle G be a simple algebraic group of type F 4 \displaystyle F_{4} , E 6 \displaystyle E_{6} , E 7 \displaystyle E_{7} or E 8 \displaystyle E_{8} , and let 𝔤 \displaystyle\mathfrak{g} be its Lie algebra. The adjoint variety X a ​ d ⊆ ℙ ​ 𝔤 \displaystyle X_{ad}\subseteq\mathbb{P}\mathfrak{g} is defined as the unique closed orbit of the adjoint action of G \displaystyle G on ℙ ​ 𝔤 \displaystyle\mathbb{P}\mathfrak{g} . X a ​ d \displaystyle X_{ad} is a Fano contact manifold covered by lines in ℙ ​ 𝔤 \displaystyle{\mathbb{P}}{\mathfrak{g}} . The subadjoint variety S ⊆ ℙ ​ W \displaystyle S\subseteq{\mathbb{P}}W is denoted by the variety of lines on X a ​ d \displaystyle X_{ad} through a fixed point x \displaystyle x , where W ⊆ T x ​ X \displaystyle W\subseteq T_{x}X is taken as the contact hyperplane. It follows from a result in representation theory of Vinberg that the GIT quotient space of codimension two linear sections of S \displaystyle S is isomorphic to the weighted projective space ℙ ⁡ ( 1 , 3 , 4 , 6 ) \displaystyle{\mathbb{P}}(1,3,4,6) . In this note, we investigate the problem of finding a geometric interpretation of the above isomorphism. As a main result, for each 𝔤 \displaystyle{\mathfrak{g}} of the above type, we construct a natural open embedding of the GIT quotient space of nonsingular codimension two linear sections of S \displaystyle S into ℙ ⁡ ( 1 , 3 , 4 , 6 ) \displaystyle{\mathbb{P}}(1,3,4,6) whose complement is a fixed hypersurface of degree 12. The key ingredient of our construction is to apply a correspondence of Bahargava and Ho which relates the above moduli problem to a moduli problem on curves of genus one.
