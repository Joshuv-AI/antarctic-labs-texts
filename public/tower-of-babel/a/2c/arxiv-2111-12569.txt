Rigorous approaches for spin glass and Gaussian spin glass with P-wise interactions
Linda Albanese, Andrea Alessandrelli

Abstract
Purpose of this paper is to face up to P-spin glass and Gaussian P-spin model, i.e. spin glasses with polynomial interactions of degree P > 2 P>2 . We consider the replica symmetry and first step of replica simmetry breaking assumptions and we solve the models via transport equation and Guerra’s interpolating technique, showing that we reach the same results. Thus, using rigorous approaches, we recover the same expression for quenched statistical pressure and self-consistency equation in both assumption found with other techniques, including the well-known replica trick technique. At the end, we show that for P = 2 P=2 the Gaussian P-spin glass model is intrinsecally RS.

P-spin glass model

In this section we deal with P-spin glass model, namely Sherrington-Kirkpatrick (SK) model with

1.1
Generalities

Definition 1
.

Let
P\in\mathbb{N}
and
\bm{\sigma}\in\{-1;+1\}^{N}
be a configuration of N spins, the Hamiltonian of the P-spin glass model is defined as

H_{N}^{(P)}(\bm{\sigma}|\bm{J})\coloneqq-\dfrac{1}{P!}\displaystyle\sum\limits_{i_{1},\cdots,i_{P}=1}^{N,\cdots,N}\,J_{i_{1},\cdots,i_{P}}\sigma_{i_{1}}\cdots\sigma_{i_{P}}

(1)

where the P-wise quenched couplings
\bm{J}=\{J_{i_{1},\cdots,i_{P}}\}_{i_{1},\cdots,i_{P}=1,\cdots,N}
are given by

J_{i_{1},...,i_{P}}\coloneqq\,\dfrac{J_{0}}{N^{P-1}}+J\sqrt{\dfrac{2}{N^{P-1}}}z_{i_{1},\cdots,i_{P}}

(2)

with
J_{0}\in\mathbb{R}^{+}
and
z_{i_{1},\cdots,i_{P}}
i.i.d. standard random variables
z_{i_{1},\cdots,i_{P}}\sim N(0;1)
.

Definition 2
.

The partition function related to the Hamiltonian (
1
) is given by

\begin{array}[]{lll}Z_{N}^{(P)}(\beta,\bm{J})&\coloneqq\sum_{\bm{\sigma}}\exp\left[-\beta H_{N}^{(P)}(\bm{\sigma}|\bm{J})\right]\,,\end{array}

(3)

where
\beta\in\mathbb{R}^{+}
is the inverse temperature in proper units such that for
\beta\to 0
the probability distribution for the neural configuration is uniformly spread while for
\beta\to\infty
it is sharply peaked at the minima of the energy function (
1
).

We introduce the
Boltzmann average
induced by the partition function (
3
), denoted with
\omega_{\bm{J}}
and, for an arbitrary observable
O(\bm{\sigma})
, defined as

\omega_{\bm{J}}(O(\bm{\sigma})):=\frac{\sum_{\bm{\sigma}}O(\bm{\sigma})e^{-\beta H_{N}(\bm{\sigma}|\bm{J})}}{Z_{N}(\beta,\bm{J})}.

(4)

This can be further averaged over the realization of the
J_{i_{1},\cdots,i_{P}}
’s (also referred to as
quenched average
) to get

\langle O(\bm{\sigma})\rangle\coloneqq\mathbb{E}\omega_{\bm{J}}(O(\bm{\sigma})).

(5)

Definition 3
.

The intensive quenched pressure of the P-spin glass model (
1
) is defined as

\mathcal{A}_{N}^{(P)}(\beta,\bm{J})\coloneqq\frac{1}{N}\mathbb{E}\ln Z_{N}^{(P)}(\beta,\bm{J}),

(6)

and its thermodynamic limit, assuming its existence, is referred to as

\mathcal{A}^{(P)}(\beta,\bm{J})\coloneqq\lim_{N\to\infty}\mathcal{A}^{(P)}_{N}(\beta,\bm{J}).

(7)

Remark 1
.

The existence of the thermodynamical limit of free energy density is proved for P-spin glass model in Guerra and Toninelli’s work
GuerraTon
.

Remark 2
.

We stress that the quenched statistical pressure
\mathcal{A}^{(P)}
is equivalent to free energy, namely

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}^{(P)}(\beta,\bm{J})=-{\beta}f^{(P)}(\beta,\bm{J})=-{\beta}\left(E(\beta,\bm{J})-\frac{S(\beta,\bm{J})}{\beta}\right)

(8)

where
E
is the internal energy and S the entropy of the system.

In order to solve the model we want to find out an explicit expression for the quenched pressure (
7
) in terms of the natural order parameters of the theory, namely the magnetization
m
and the two-replica overlap
q_{12}
, defined in the following

Definition 4
.

The order parameters used to describe the macroscopic behavior of the model are the standard ones
Amit
;
Coolen
, namely, the magnetization

m\coloneqq\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\sigma_{i}\,,

(9)

and the two-replica overlap, introduced as

q_{12}\coloneqq\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\sigma_{i}^{(1)}\sigma_{i}^{(2)}\,.

(10)

1.2
Resolution via Guerra’s interpolation

The purpose of this section is to solve the P-spin glass model through Guerra’s interpolating technique. To do so, we compute the derivative w.r.t. the interpolating parameter
t
and we apply the Fundamental theorem of Calculus. In the end, we find the expression of statistical pressure in the approximation of replica symmetry (RS) and first step of replica symmetry breaking (RSB).

1.2.1
RS solution

Definition 5
.

Under the replica-symmetry assumption, the order parameters, in the thermodynamic limit, self-average around their mean values and their distributions get delta-peaked at their equilibrium value (denoted with a bar), independently of the replica considered, namely

\displaystyle\lim_{N\to\infty}\langle(m-\bar{m})^{2}\rangle=0

\displaystyle\Rightarrow

\displaystyle\lim_{N\to\infty}\langle m\rangle=\bar{m},

(11)

\displaystyle\lim_{N\to\infty}\langle(q_{12}-\bar{q})^{2}\rangle=0

\displaystyle\Rightarrow

\displaystyle\lim_{N\to\infty}\langle q_{12}\rangle=\bar{q}.

(12)

For the generic order parameter
X
this can be rewritten as
\langle(\Delta X)^{2}\rangle\xrightarrow{N\to\infty}0
,
where

\Delta X\coloneqq X-\bar{X},

and, clearly, the RS approximation also implies that, in the thermodynamic limit,
\langle\Delta X\Delta Y\rangle=0
for any generic pair of order parameters
X,Y
. Moreover in the thermodynamic limit, we have
\langle(\Delta X)^{k}\rangle\rightarrow 0
for
k\geq 2
.

Definition 6
.

Given the interpolating parameter
t\in[0,1]
,
A,\ \psi\in\mathbb{R}
and
z_{i}\sim\mathcal{N}(0,1)
for
i=1,\ldots,N
standard Gaussian variables i.i.d., the Guerra’s interpolating partition function is given as

\begin{array}[]{lll}\mathcal{Z}^{(P)}_{N}(t)&\coloneqq&\displaystyle\sum\limits_{\{\bm{\sigma}\}}\exp{}\Bigg[t\dfrac{\beta^{{}^{\prime}}J_{0}N}{2}m^{P}(\bm{\sigma})+(1-t)N\psi\,m(\bm{\sigma})+\\
\\
&&+\sqrt{t}\beta^{{}^{\prime}}J\sqrt{\dfrac{1}{2N^{P-1}}}\displaystyle\sum\limits_{i_{1},\cdots,i_{{}_{P}}=1}^{N,\cdots,N}z_{i_{1}\cdots,i_{{}_{P}}}\sigma_{i_{1}}\cdots\sigma_{{}_{P}}+\\
\\
&&+A\sqrt{1-t}\,\displaystyle\sum\limits_{i=1}^{N}z_{i}\sigma_{i}\,,\end{array}

(13)

where
\beta^{{}^{\prime}}=2\beta/P!
.

Definition 7
.

The interpolating pressure for the P-spin glass model (
1
), at finite
N
, is introduced as

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}^{(P)}_{N}(t)

\displaystyle\coloneqq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{N}\mathbb{E}\left[\ln\mathcal{Z}^{(P)}_{N}(t)\right],

(14)

where the expectation
\mathbb{E}
is now meant over
z_{i_{1},\cdots,i_{P}}
and
z_{i}
, in the thermodynamic limit,

\mathcal{A}^{(P)}(t)\coloneqq\lim_{N\to\infty}\mathcal{A}^{(P)}_{N}(t).

(15)

By setting
t=1
the interpolating pressure recovers the original one (
6
), that is
A_{N}^{(P)}(\beta,J_{0},J)=\mathcal{A}^{(P)}_{N}(t=1)
.

Remark 3
.

The interpolating structure implies an interpolating measure, whose related Boltzmann factor reads as

\mathcal{B}(\bm{\sigma};t)\coloneqq\exp\left[\beta\mathcal{H}(\bm{\sigma};t)\right];

(16)

In this way the partition function is written as
\mathcal{Z}_{N}(t)=\sum_{\bm{\sigma}}\mathcal{B}(\bm{\sigma};t)
.

A generalized average follows from this generalized measure as

\omega_{t}(O(\bm{\sigma}))\coloneqq\sum_{\bm{\sigma}}O(\bm{\sigma})\mathcal{B}(\bm{\sigma};t)

(17)

and

\langle O(\bm{\sigma})\rangle_{t}\coloneqq\mathbb{E}[\omega_{t}(O(\bm{\sigma}))].

(18)

where
\mathbb{E}
denotes the average over
z_{i_{1},\cdots,i_{P}}
and
\{z_{i}\}_{i=1,\cdots,N}
.

Of course, when
t=1
the standard Boltzmann measure and related average is recovered.
Hereafter, in order to lighten the notation, we will drop the subindices
t
.

Lemma 1
.

The
t
derivative of interpolating pressure is given by

\small\begin{array}[]{lll}\dfrac{d\mathcal{A}^{(P)}(t)}{dt}\coloneqq&\dfrac{\beta^{{}^{\prime}}J_{0}}{2}\left(\langle m^{P}\rangle-\dfrac{2\psi}{\beta^{{}^{\prime}}J_{0}}\langle m\rangle\right)-\dfrac{\beta^{\prime}\,{}^{2}J^{2}}{4}\left(\langle q_{12}^{P}\rangle-\dfrac{2A^{2}}{\beta^{\prime}\,{}^{2}J^{2}}\langle q_{12}\rangle\right)\\
\\
&+\dfrac{\beta^{\prime}\,{}^{2}J^{2}}{4}-\dfrac{A^{2}}{2}\end{array}

(19)

Proof.

Deriving equation (
14
) with respect to
t
, we get

\footnotesize\begin{array}[]{lll}\dfrac{d\mathcal{A}^{(P)}(t)}{dt}&=&\dfrac{\beta^{{}^{\prime}}J_{0}}{2}\langle m^{P}\rangle-\psi\langle m\rangle+\\
\\
&&+\dfrac{\beta^{{}^{\prime}}J}{2N\sqrt{t}}\sqrt{\dfrac{1}{2N^{P-1}}}\mathbb{E}\left[\sum\limits_{\bm{i}}z_{\bm{i}}\omega(\sigma_{i_{1}}\cdots\sigma_{i_{P}})\right]+\\
\\
&&-\dfrac{1}{2N\sqrt{1-t}}\mathbb{E}\left[A\sum\limits_{i}z_{i}\omega(\sigma_{i})\right]\,.\end{array}

(20)

Now, using the Stein’s lemma (also known as Wick’s theorem) on standard Gaussian variable
z_{i}
and
z_{\bm{i}}
, which is for a standard Gaussian variable
J
, i.e.
J\sim N(0,1)
, and for a generic
function
f(J)
for which
\mathbb{E}zf(z)
and
\mathbb{E}\partial_{J}f(J)
both exist, then

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\left(Jf(J)\right)=\mathbb{E}\left(\frac{\partial f(J)}{\partial J}\right)\,,

(21)

we may rewrite the second and the third member of (
20
) as

\footnotesize\begin{array}[]{lll}\dfrac{\beta^{{}^{\prime}}J}{2N\sqrt{t}}\sqrt{\dfrac{1}{2N^{P-1}}}\mathbb{E}\left[\sum\limits_{\bm{i}}z_{\bm{i}}\omega(\sigma_{i_{1}}\cdots\sigma_{i_{P}})\right]-\dfrac{1}{2N\sqrt{1-t}}\mathbb{E}\left[A\sum\limits_{i}z_{i}\omega(\sigma_{i})\right]=D_{1}+D_{2}\,.\end{array}

Let’s investigate those three terms:

\footnotesize\begin{array}[]{lll}D_{1}&=\dfrac{\beta^{{}^{\prime}}J}{2N\sqrt{t}}\sqrt{\dfrac{1}{2N^{P-1}}}\mathbb{E}\left[\sum\limits_{\bm{i}}\partial_{z_{\bm{i}}}\omega(\sigma_{i_{1}}\cdots\sigma_{i_{P}})\right]\\
\\
&=\dfrac{\beta^{\prime}\,{}^{2}J^{2}}{4N^{P}}\left(\sum\limits_{\bm{i}}\mathbb{E}\left[\omega((\sigma_{i_{1}}\cdots\sigma_{i_{P}})^{2})\right]-\sum\limits_{\bm{i}}\mathbb{E}\left[\omega(\sigma_{i_{1}}\cdots\sigma_{i_{P}})^{2}\right]\right)\\
\\
&=\dfrac{\beta^{\prime}\,{}^{2}J^{2}}{4}\Big[1-\langle q_{12}^{P}\rangle\Big]\,;\end{array}

(22)

\footnotesize\begin{array}[]{lll}D_{2}&=-\dfrac{1}{2N\sqrt{1-t}}A\sum\limits_{i}\mathbb{E}\left[\partial_{z_{i}}\omega(\sigma_{i})\right]\\
\\
&=-\dfrac{1}{2N}A^{2}\left(\sum\limits_{i}\mathbb{E}\left[\omega(\sigma^{2}_{i})\right]-\sum\limits_{i}\mathbb{E}\left[\omega(\sigma_{i})^{2}\right]\right)\\
\\
&=-\dfrac{1}{2}A^{2}\Big[1-\langle q_{12}\rangle\Big]\,.\end{array}

(23)

Rearranging together (
22
) and (
23
) we obtain the thesis.
∎

Proposition 1
.

In the thermodynamic limit (

) and under RS assumption, applying the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus and using the suitable values of

and

\begin{array}[]{lll}\mathcal{A}^{(P)}(\beta,J_{0},J)&=&\ln{2}+\left\langle\ln{\cosh{\left[\dfrac{P}{2}\beta^{{}^{\prime}}J_{0}\bar{m}^{{}^{P-1}}+z\beta^{{}^{\prime}}J\sqrt{\dfrac{P}{2}\bar{q}^{{}^{P-1}}}\right]}}\right\rangle_{z}+\\
\\
&&-\dfrac{P-1}{2}\beta^{{}^{\prime}}J_{0}\bar{m}^{P}+\dfrac{\beta^{\prime}\,{}^{2}J^{2}}{4}\left(1-P\bar{q}^{P-1}+(P-1)\bar{q}^{P}\right)\,,\end{array}

\begin{array}[]{lll}\bar{m}=\left\langle\tanh{\left[\dfrac{P}{2}\beta J_{0}\bar{m}^{{}^{P-1}}+z\beta J\sqrt{\dfrac{P}{2}\bar{q}^{{}^{P-1}}}\right]}\right\rangle_{z}\,,\\

\bar{q}=\left\langle\tanh{}^{2}{\left[\dfrac{P}{2}\beta J_{0}\bar{m}^{{}^{P-1}}+z\beta J\sqrt{\dfrac{P}{2}\bar{q}^{{}^{P-1}}}\right]}\right\rangle_{z}\,.\end{array}

Proof.

Using the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus:

\footnotesize\mathcal{A}^{(P)}=\mathcal{A}^{(P)}(t=1)=\mathcal{A}^{(P)}(t=0)+\int\limits_{0}^{1}\,\partial_{s}\mathcal{A}^{(P)}(s)\Big|_{s=t}\,dt

\footnotesize\begin{array}[]{lll}\mathcal{A}^{(P)}(t=0)&=&\dfrac{1}{N}\mathbb{E}\ln{}\Bigg\{\displaystyle\sum\limits_{\{\bm{\sigma}\}}\exp{}\Bigg[N\psi\,m(\bm{\sigma})+A\,\displaystyle\sum\limits_{i=1}^{N}z_{i}\sigma_{i}\Bigg]\Bigg\}\,,\\

1.2.2
1RSB solution

In this subsection we turn to the solution of the P-spin glass model via the Guerra’s interpolating technique, restricting the description at the first step of RSB.

Definition 8
.

In the first step of replica-symmetry breaking, the distribution of the two-replica overlap

2
Gaussian P-Spin Glass

In this section we face with Gaussian P-spin glass model, namely spin glass model with Gaussian spins with
P
-wise interactions. Several works describe the principal aspects of this model, either in our assumption
BenArous
;
Bovier3
or in the so-called spherical version, where the Gaussian spin are on a spherical surface
crisanti
;
PRL
;
TalaP
.
We stress that two assumptions on Gaussian spins are equivalent, as shown in
AdrianoGauss
.
We deepen the model through Guerra’s interpolating technique and transport equation for RS and 1RSB assumptions.

2.1
Generalities

Definition 13
.

Let
P\in\mathbb{N}
and
z_{i}\in\mathbb{R},\;i=1,2,\cdots,N
be a configuration of N gaussian spins, the Hamiltonian of the P-spin Gaussian model is defined as

H_{N}^{(P)}(\bm{z}|\bm{J})\coloneqq-\dfrac{1}{P!}\displaystyle\sum\limits_{i_{1},\cdots,i_{P}=1}^{N,\cdots,N}\,\mathcal{J}_{i_{1},\cdots,i_{P}}z_{i_{1}}\cdots z_{i_{P}}

(107)

where the P-wise quenched couplings
\bm{\mathcal{J}}=\{\mathcal{J}_{i_{1},\cdots,i_{P}}\}_{i_{1},\cdots,i_{P}=1,\cdots,N}
are given by

\mathcal{J}_{i_{1},...,i_{P}}\coloneqq\,\sqrt{\dfrac{2}{N^{P-1}}}J_{i_{1},\cdots,i_{P}}

(108)

with
J_{i_{1},\cdots,i_{P}}
i.i.d. standard random variables drawn from
P(J_{i_{1},\cdots,i_{P}})=N(0;1)
.

Definition 14
.

The partition function related to the Hamiltonian (
107
) is given by

\begin{array}[]{lll}Z_{N}(\beta,\bm{J})&\coloneqq\displaystyle\int d\mu(z)\exp\left[-\beta H_{N}^{(P)}(z|\bm{J})\right]=\mathbb{E}_{z}\,e^{-\beta H_{N}^{(P)}(z|\bm{J})}\,,\end{array}

(109)

where,
d\mu(z)=\prod_{i}(2\pi)^{-1/2}e^{-\sum_{i}z_{i}^{2}/2}
and
\beta\in\mathbb{R}^{+}
is the inverse temperature in proper units.

As early pointed out for
instance in
Berlin
, unfortunately these kind of models need to be regularized; in
fact, the right side of (
109
) is not always well defined as the P-wise interactions bridges soft spins which are both Gaussian distributed. It will be clear soon that a good definition is

\begin{array}[]{lll}Z_{N}(\beta,\bm{J},\lambda)&\coloneqq\mathbb{E}_{z}\,\exp\left[-\beta H_{N}^{(P)}\left(z|\bm{J}\right)-\left(\dfrac{\beta}{P!}\right)^{2}\dfrac{1}{N^{P-1}}\left(\displaystyle\sum\limits_{i=1}^{N}z_{i}^{2}\right)^{P}+\dfrac{\lambda}{2}\displaystyle\sum\limits_{i=1}^{N}z_{i}^{2}\right]\,,\end{array}

(110)

where the first additional term is needed for convergence of the integral over the Gaussian measure
d\mu(z)
. Instead, the new parameter
\lambda
, within the last term of (
110
), is just to modify the variance of the soft spins, as in several applications this can sensibly vary.

We introduce the
Boltzmann average
induced by the partition function (
109
), denoted with
\omega_{\bm{J}}
and, for an arbitrary observable
O(\bm{\sigma})
, defined as

\omega_{\bm{J}}(O(\bm{\sigma})):=\frac{\mathbb{E}_{z}O(\bm{\sigma})e^{-\beta H_{N}(\bm{\sigma}|\bm{J})}}{Z_{N}(\beta,\bm{J},\lambda)}.

(111)

This can be further averaged over the realization of the
J_{i_{1},\cdots,i_{P}}
’s (also referred to as
quenched average
) to get

\langle O(\bm{\sigma})\rangle\coloneqq\mathbb{E}\omega_{\bm{J}}(O(\bm{\sigma})).

(112)

Definition 15
.

The intensive quenched pressure of the Gaussian P-spin glass model (
107
) is defined as

\mathcal{A}_{N}(\beta,\lambda)\coloneqq\frac{1}{N}\mathbb{E}\ln\mathcal{Z}_{N}(\beta,\bm{J},\lambda),

(113)

and its thermodynamic limit, assuming its existence, is referred to as

\mathcal{A}(\beta,\lambda)\coloneqq\lim_{N\to\infty}\mathcal{A}_{N}(\beta,\lambda).

(114)

In order to solve the model we want to find out an explicit expression for the quenched pressure (
114
) in terms of the natural order parameter of the theory, namely the two-replica overlap
q_{12}
, defined in the following

Definition 16
.

The order parameter used to describe the macroscopic behavior of the model is the two-replica overlap, introduced as

q_{12}\coloneqq\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}z_{i}^{(1)}z_{i}^{(2)}\,.

(115)

2.2
Resolution via Guerra’s interpolation

Mirroring P-spin glass model, the purpose of this section is to solve the Gaussian P-spin glass model via Guerra’s interpolating technique. To do so, we recover the expressions of statistical pressure (which is equivalent to free energy) and self consistency equations in the approximation of replica symmetry (RS) and first step of replica symmetry breaking (RSB).

2.2.1
RS solution

Using the same definition for
q_{12}
presented for the P-spin glass model (def.
5
), we can introduce the Guerra’s interpolating partition function of the Gaussian P-spin glass model.

Definition 17
.

Given the interpolating parameter

and
\tilde{J}_{i}\sim\mathcal{N}(0,1)
for

\footnotesize\begin{array}[]{lll}\mathcal{Z}^{(P)}_{N}(t)&\coloneqq&\mathbb{E}_{z}\exp{}\Bigg[\beta^{{}^{\prime}}\sqrt{t}\sqrt{\dfrac{1}{2N^{P-1}}}\displaystyle\sum\limits_{i_{1},\cdots,i_{P}=1}^{N,\cdots,N}J_{i_{1},\cdots,i_{P}}z_{i_{1}}\cdots z_{i_{P}}-t\dfrac{\beta^{\prime}\,{}^{2}}{4N^{P-1}}\left(\displaystyle\sum\limits_{i=1}^{N}z_{i}^{2}\right)^{P}\\

(116)

where

The interpolating pressure for the Gaussian P-spin glass model (

By setting

Remark 12

The interpolating structure implies an interpolating measure, whose related Boltzmann factor reads as

Lemma 5
