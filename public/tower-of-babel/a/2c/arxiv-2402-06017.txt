Meirav Amram, Cheng Gong, Jia-Li Mo

Abstract
This paper considers some algebraic surfaces that can deform to planar Zappatic stable surfaces with a unique singularity of type E n E_{n} . We prove that the Galois covers of these surfaces are all simply connected of general type, for n ≥ 4 n\geq 4 . As an application, we prove that such surfaces do not exist for n > 30 n>30 .

At the same time, some researchers also started to discuss Zappatic surfaces of type

Theorem 1

If a smooth projective algebraic surface deforms to a Zappatic surface of type

n\geq 4

We also discuss the existence of the above deformation, and have the following corollary:

Corollary 2

If

An interesting question is about the existence of the above deformation between

30

It is important to state that as our findings show, our goals were met and the mathematical community no longer needs to face the challenges that confronted us. The use of the results of the general case for such deformations can be spread to other studies about invariants of classification of algebraic surfaces. In addition, our work is dedicated to the significant development that consists of the original research; the original research was about deformations of specific cases and examples, while our contribution is for general cases and giving proofs about existence of deformations.
