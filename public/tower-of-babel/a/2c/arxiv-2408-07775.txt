Introduction

Given
n\in\mathbb{N}
and
s\in\mathbb{R}
, let
\dot{H}^{s}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
be the homogeneous Sobolev space of fractional order
s
defined as

\dot{H}^{s}(\mathbb{R}^{n}):=\left\{u\in\mathcal{S}^{\prime}(\mathbb{R}^{n}):\mathcal{F}u\in L^{1}_{\text{loc}}(\mathbb{R}^{n}),\,\|u\|_{\dot{H}^{s}(\mathbb{R}^{n})}:=\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}|\xi|^{2s}|\mathcal{F}u(\xi)|^{2}\text{d}\xi\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}<\infty\right\}

where
\mathcal{F}u
is the Fourier transform of
u
, and
\mathcal{S}^{\prime}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
is the space of tempered distributions, i.e., the continuous dual space of the Schwartz space
\mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
.
As shown in
[
4
]
,
\dot{H}^{s}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
is a Hilbert space if and only if
s<\frac{n}{2}
. Moreover, if
u\in\mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
, then

\|u\|_{\dot{H}^{s}(\mathbb{R}^{n})}^{2}=\left\|(-\Delta)^{s\over 2}u\right\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{n})}^{2}=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}u(-\Delta)^{s}u\quad\text{where }\mathcal{F}\left((-\Delta)^{s}u\right)(\xi):=|\xi|^{2s}\hat{u}(\xi).

The space
\dot{H}^{s}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
with
s<\frac{n}{2}
is realized as the completion of
\mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
under the norm
\|\cdot\|_{\dot{H}^{s}(\mathbb{R}^{n})}
.

For any
s\in(0,\frac{n}{2})
, there is an optimal constant
S_{n,s}>0
depending only on
n
and
s
such that

(1.1)

S_{n,s}\|u\|_{L^{p+1}(\mathbb{R}^{n})}\leq\|u\|_{\dot{H}^{s}(\mathbb{R}^{n})}\quad\text{for all }u\in\dot{H}^{s}(\mathbb{R}^{n})\quad\text{where }p:=\frac{n+2s}{n-2s},

referred to as the fractional Sobolev inequality.
Lieb
[
45
]
proved that the set of the extremizers of (
1.1
) consists of non-zero constant multiples of the functions (often called the bubbles)

(1.2)

U[z,\lambda](x)=\alpha_{n,s}\left(\frac{\lambda}{1+\lambda^{2}|x-z|^{2}}\right)^{n-2s\over 2}\quad\text{for }x\in\mathbb{R}^{n}

where
\alpha_{n,s}:=2^{n-2s\over 2}[\Gamma({n+2s\over 2})/\Gamma({n-2s\over 2})]^{n-2s\over 4s}
.

According to the standard theory of calculus of variations, an extremizer of (
1.1
) always solves

(1.3)

(-\Delta)^{s}u=\mu|u|^{p-1}u\quad\text{in }\mathbb{R}^{n},\quad u\in\dot{H}^{s}(\mathbb{R}^{n})

where
\mu\in\mathbb{R}
is a Lagrange multiplier. Chen et al.
[
16
]
classified all positive solutions to (
1.3
), showing that they must assume the form in (
1.2
) up to a constant multiple.
Furthermore, Dávila et al.
[
23
]
deduced that if
s\in(0,1)
, then the solution space of a linearized equation of (
1.3
)

(1.4)

(-\Delta)^{s}Z-p\,U[z,\lambda]^{p-1}Z=0\quad\text{in }\mathbb{R}^{n},\quad Z\in L^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}^{n}).\

is spanned by

Z^{a}[z,\lambda]=\left.\frac{1}{\lambda}\frac{\partial U[\bar{z},\lambda]}{\partial\bar{z}^{a}}\right|_{\bar{z}=z}\quad\text{for }a=1,\ldots,n\quad\text{and}\quad Z^{n+1}[z,\lambda]=\lambda\left.\frac{\partial U[z,\bar{\lambda}]}{\partial\bar{\lambda}}\right|_{\bar{\lambda}=\lambda},

where
\bar{z}=(\bar{z}_{1},\ldots,\bar{z}_{n})\in\mathbb{R}^{n}
.
In
[
44
, Lemma 5.1]
, Li and Xiong extended this non-degeneracy theorem to all
s\in(0,\frac{n}{2})
.
The condition
Z\in L^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
in
[
44
, Lemma 5.1]
can be replaced with
Z\in\dot{H}^{s}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
, which is the content of Lemma
A.1
below.

For a further understanding of (
1.1
), one can naturally consider its quantitative stability, as proposed by Brezis and Lieb
[
8
]
.
Bianchi and Egnell
[
7
]
proved the existence of a constant
C_{\text{BE}}>0
depending only on

such that

(1.5)

\inf_{z\in\mathbb{R}^{n},\,\lambda>0,\,c\in\mathbb{R}}\left\|u-cU[z,\lambda]\right\|_{\dot{H}^{1}(\mathbb{R}^{n})}^{2}\leq C_{\text{BE}}\left(\|u\|_{\dot{H}^{1}(\mathbb{R}^{n})}^{2}-S_{n,s}^{2}\|u\|_{L^{p+1}(\mathbb{R}^{n})}^{2}\right)

for any
u\in\dot{H}^{1}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
. Later, their result was generalized by Chen et al.
[

]
, who found a constant

depending only on

and

such that

(1.6)

\inf_{z\in\mathbb{R}^{n},\,\lambda>0,\,c\in\mathbb{R}}\left\|u-cU[z,\lambda]\right\|_{\dot{H}^{s}(\mathbb{R}^{n})}^{2}\leq C_{\text{CFW}}\left(\|u\|_{\dot{H}^{s}(\mathbb{R}^{n})}^{2}-S_{n,s}^{2}\|u\|_{L^{p+1}(\mathbb{R}^{n})}^{2}\right)

for any

.

Theorem A
.

Suppose that
n\in\mathbb{N}
,
\nu\in\mathbb{N}
,
s\in(0,\frac{n}{2})
,
p=\frac{n+2s}{n-2s}
, and
S_{n,s}>0
is the constant in (
1.1
).
Let
\{u_{m}\}_{m\in\mathbb{N}}
be a sequence of non-negative functions in
\dot{H}^{s}(\mathbb{R}^{n})

such that
\left(\nu-\frac{1}{2}\right)S_{n,s}^{n}\leq\|u_{m}\|_{\dot{H}^{s}(\mathbb{R}^{n})}^{2}\leq\left(\nu+\frac{1}{2}\right)S_{n,s}^{n}
. If it satisfies

\left\|(-\Delta)^{s}u_{m}+u_{m}^{p}\right\|_{\dot{H}^{-s}(\mathbb{R}^{n})}\to 0\quad\text{as }k\to\infty,

then there exist a sequence
\{(z_{1,m},\ldots,z_{\nu,m})\}_{m\in\mathbb{N}}
of
\nu
-tuples of points in
\mathbb{R}^{n}
and a sequence
\{(\lambda_{1,m},\ldots,\lambda_{\nu,m})\}_{m\in\mathbb{N}}
of
\nu
-tuples of positive numbers such that

\left\|u_{m}-\sum_{i=1}^{\nu}U[z_{i,m},\lambda_{i,m}]\right\|_{\dot{H}^{s}(\mathbb{R}^{n})}\to 0\quad\text{as }m\to\infty.

In addition, let
U_{i,m}:=U[z_{i,m},\lambda_{i,m}]
for
i=1,\ldots,\nu
.
Then there exists
m_{0}\in\mathbb{N}
such that the sequence
\{(U_{1,m},\ldots,U_{\nu,m})\}_{m\geq m_{0}}
of
\nu
-tuples of bubbles is
\delta
-interacting in the following sense: If we define the quantity

(1.7)

q_{ij}=q(z_{i},z_{j},\lambda_{i},\lambda_{j})=\left(\frac{\lambda_{i}}{\lambda_{j}}+\frac{\lambda_{i}}{\lambda_{j}}+\lambda_{i}\lambda_{j}|z_{i}-z_{j}|^{2}\right)^{-{n-2s\over 2}}\quad\text{for }i,j=1,\ldots,\nu,

then

\max_{\begin{subarray}{c}i,j=1,\ldots,\nu,\\
i\neq j\end{subarray}}q\left(z_{i,m},z_{j,m},\lambda_{i,m},\lambda_{j,m}\right)\leq\delta\quad\text{for all }m\geq m_{0}.

If

[
55
]

In this paper, we establish sharp quantitative stability estimates of the above decomposition provided any
n\in\mathbb{N}
and

Theorem 1.1
.

Let
n\in\mathbb{N}
,
\nu\in\mathbb{N}
,
s\in(0,\frac{n}{2})
, and
p=\frac{n+2s}{n-2s}
.
There exist a small constant
\delta>0
and a large constant
C>0
depending only on
n
,
s
, and
\nu
such that the following statement holds: If
u\in\dot{H}^{s}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
satisfies

(1.8)

\left\|u-\sum_{i=1}^{\nu}U[\tilde{z}_{i},\tilde{\lambda}_{i}]\right\|_{\dot{H}^{s}(\mathbb{R}^{n})}\leq\delta

for some
\delta
-interacting family
\{U[\tilde{z}_{i},\tilde{\lambda}_{i}]\}_{i=1}^{\nu}
,
then there is a family
\{U[z_{i},\lambda_{i}]\}_{i=1}^{\nu}
of bubbles such that

(1.9)

\left\|u-\sum_{i=1}^{\nu}U[z_{i},\lambda_{i}]\right\|_{\dot{H}^{s}(\mathbb{R}^{n})}\leq C\begin{cases}\Gamma(u)&\text{for }\nu=1,\\
\Gamma(u)&\text{for }2s<n<6s\text{ and }\nu\geq 2,\\
\Gamma(u)|\log\Gamma(u)|^{1\over 2}&\text{for }n=6s\text{ and }\nu\geq 2,\\

Furthermore, estimate (

and

in the sense that the power of
\Gamma(u)
(
|\log\Gamma(u)|
, respectively) cannot be substituted with a larger (smaller, resp.) one.

By (

(2.14)

\displaystyle\lesssim\left\|(-\Delta)^{s\over 2}\chi\right\|_{L^{n\over s}(\mathbb{R}^{n})}+1\lesssim 1.

Here, we applied a fractional Leibniz rule (see e.g.
[

) for the first inequality, and (

) and (

) for the second inequality.
Also, we employed the Hausdorff-Young inequality, the assumption that

, and

\displaystyle\mathord{{\raise 0.49991pt\hbox{$\displaystyle\ \int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\left\{\lambda_{i,m}^{-{n-2s\over 2}}\sigma_{m}\big(\lambda_{i,m}^{-1}y+z_{i,m}\big)\right\}^{p-1}\hat{\varrho}_{i,m}\psi\textup{d}y$}}}

\displaystyle\mathord{{\raise 0.49991pt\hbox{$\displaystyle=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}U[0,1]^{p-1}\hat{\varrho}_{i,m}\psi\textup{d}y+O\left(\max_{\alpha\in\left\{{p+1\over p},{p^{2}-1\over p}\right\}}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}k=1,\ldots,\nu,\\

because if we set

\displaystyle\lesssim\begin{cases}\left({\lambda_{k,m}\over\lambda_{i,m}}\right)^{\alpha(n-2s)\over 2}=o(1)&\mathord{{\raise 0.49991pt\hbox{$\displaystyle\text{if }\lim_{m\to\infty}\genfrac{}{}{0.4pt}{}{\lambda_{k,m}}{\lambda_{i,m}}=0,$}}}\\
\left({\lambda_{k,m}\over\lambda_{i,m}}\right)^{{\alpha(n-2s)\over 2}-n}+\left({\lambda_{k,m}\over\lambda_{i,m}}\right)^{-{\alpha(n-2s)\over 2}}=o(1)&\mathord{{\raise 0.49991pt\hbox{$\displaystyle\text{if }\lim_{m\to\infty}\genfrac{}{}{0.4pt}{}{\lambda_{k,m}}{\lambda_{i,m}}=\infty,$}}}\\

Writing

\tilde{\varrho}_{i,m}(y)=\lambda_{i,m}^{-{n-2s\over 2}}\varrho_{m}\big(\lambda_{i,m}^{-1}y+z_{i,m}\big)\quad\text{and}\quad\mathcal{F}(D^{s,\eta}\psi)(\xi)=i^{-|\eta|}\partial^{\eta}_{\xi}(|\xi|^{s})(\mathcal{F}\psi)(\xi)

where

\displaystyle\left|\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\mathcal{R}_{i,m}\psi\textup{d}y\right|

\displaystyle\leq\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}|\tilde{\varrho}_{i,m}(y)|\left|\left[(-\Delta)^{s}(\chi_{m}\psi)\right](y)-\chi_{m}(y)\left[(-\Delta)^{s}\psi\right](y)\right|\textup{d}y

\displaystyle\lesssim r_{m}^{-\frac{3s}{2}}+r_{m}^{-1}=o(1).

Here, the empty summation

is understood as zero for

.

Accordingly, by taking the limit

on (

(2.17)

(-\Delta)^{s}\hat{\varrho}_{i,\infty}-\mu_{\infty}U[0,1]^{p-1}\hat{\varrho}_{i,\infty}=0\quad\text{in }\mathbb{R}^{n}

where

. From (

), (

), the fact that

is an extremizer of (

), and Lemma

,
we conclude that

\int_{B_{i,m}}U_{i,m}^{p-1}\varrho_{m}^{2}\textup{d}x\leq\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}U[0,1]^{p-1}\hat{\varrho}_{i,m}^{2}\textup{d}y\lesssim\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}U[0,1]^{p}\hat{\varrho}_{i,m}\textup{d}y\right)^{p\over p-1}\to 0\quad\text{as }m\to\infty,

which reads (
2.13

Step 3.
Finally, we prove that

\lim_{m\to\infty}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\sigma_{m}^{p-1}\varrho_{m}^{2}\textup{d}x=0.

3.
Quantitative stability estimate for dimension

(1)

In this section, we establish Theorem

assuming that

. From now on, we always assume that
\nu\geq 2
.

The following two weighted

-norms were devised in

(for the case

) to capture the precise pointwise behavior of the bubbles,
which is crucial on determining the optimal exponents of

Definition 3.1
.

Recall the number
\mathscr{R}>0
in (
2.3
) and write

(3.2)

\begin{cases}\displaystyle v_{i}^{\textup{in}}(x):=\lambda_{i}^{n+2s\over 2}\frac{\mathscr{R}^{2s-n}}{\left\langle y_{i}\right\rangle^{4s}}\mathbf{1}_{\{|y_{i}|<\mathscr{R}\}}(x),\quad v_{i}^{\textup{out}}(x):=\lambda_{i}^{n+2s\over 2}\frac{\mathscr{R}^{-4s}}{|y_{i}|^{n-2s}}\mathbf{1}_{\{|y_{i}|\geq\mathscr{R}\}}(x),\\
\displaystyle w_{i}^{\textup{in}}(x):=\lambda_{i}^{n-2s\over 2}\frac{\mathscr{R}^{2s-n}}{\left\langle y_{i}\right\rangle^{2s}}\mathbf{1}_{\{|y_{i}|<\mathscr{R}\}}(x),\quad w_{i}^{\textup{out}}(x):=\lambda_{i}^{n-2s\over 2}\frac{\mathscr{R}^{-4s}}{|y_{i}|^{n-4s}}\mathbf{1}_{\{|y_{i}|\geq\mathscr{R}\}}(x)\end{cases}

for

and

, and

(3.3)

\begin{cases}\displaystyle v_{i}^{\textup{in}}(x):=\lambda_{i}^{4s}\frac{\mathscr{R}^{-4s}}{\left\langle y_{i}\right\rangle^{4s}}\mathbf{1}_{\{|y_{i}|<\mathscr{R}^{2}\}}(x),\quad v_{i}^{\textup{out}}(x):=\lambda_{i}^{4s}\frac{\mathscr{R}^{-2s}}{|y_{i}|^{5s}}\mathbf{1}_{\{|y_{i}|\geq\mathscr{R}^{2}\}}(x),\\
\displaystyle w_{i}^{\textup{in}}(x):=\lambda_{i}^{2s}\frac{\mathscr{R}^{-4s}}{\left\langle y_{i}\right\rangle^{2s}}\mathbf{1}_{\{|y_{i}|<\mathscr{R}^{2}\}}(x),\quad w_{i}^{\textup{out}}(x):=\lambda_{i}^{2s}\frac{\mathscr{R}^{-2s}}{|y_{i}|^{3s}}\mathbf{1}_{\{|y_{i}|\geq\mathscr{R}^{2}\}}(x)\end{cases}

and

Clearly, the norms

and

depend on the choice of

and
\lambda_{i}\in(0,\infty)

If we keep using

By utilizing the above norms, we will derive (

) for all

and small

Step 1.
Let
\sigma=\sum_{i=1}^{\nu}U[z_{i},\lambda_{i}]=\sum_{i=1}^{\nu}U_{i}
be such that

\|u-\sigma\|_{\dot{H}^{s}(\mathbb{R}^{n})}=\inf_{(\tilde{z}_{1},\ldots,\tilde{z}_{\nu},\,\tilde{\lambda}_{1},\ldots,\tilde{\lambda}_{\nu})\in\mathbb{R}^{n\nu}\times(0,\infty)^{\nu}}\left\|u-\sum_{i=1}^{\nu}U[\tilde{z}_{i},\tilde{\lambda}_{i}]\right\|_{\dot{H}^{s}(\mathbb{R}^{n})}\leq\delta.

We also set
\rho=u-\sigma\in\dot{H}^{s}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
and
Z_{i}^{a}=Z^{a}[z_{i},\lambda_{i}]
for

.
Because of (

), the family
\{U_{i}\}_{i=1,\ldots,\nu}
is
\delta^{\prime}
-interacting for some
\delta^{\prime}>0

as

. The function

(3.4)

(-\Delta)^{s}\rho-\left[|\sigma+\rho|^{p-1}(\sigma+\rho)-\sigma^{p}\right]=\left(\sigma^{p}-\sum_{i=1}^{\nu}U_{i}^{p}\right)+\left[(-\Delta)^{s}u-|u|^{p-1}u\right]

in
\mathbb{R}^{n}
and

(3.5)

\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}(-\Delta)^{s\over 2}\rho\,(-\Delta)^{s\over 2}Z_{i}^{a}\textup{d}x=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\rho U_{i}^{p-1}Z_{i}^{a}\textup{d}x=0\quad\text{for all }i=1,\ldots,\nu\text{ and }a=1,\ldots,n+1.

Consider the equation

(3.6)

Definition 4.3
.

Let
\preceq
be a partial order on a set
\mathcal{T}
, and
\prec
the corresponding strict partial order on
\mathcal{T}
.

- A partially ordered set
(\mathcal{T},\preceq)
is called a directed tree if for each
t\in\mathcal{T}
, the set
\{s\in\mathcal{T}:s\preceq t\}
is well-ordered by the relation
\preceq

- A root is the least element of the set
\{s\in\mathcal{T}:s\preceq t\}
for some
t\in\mathcal{T}
.

- A rooted tree is a directed tree with roots; a rooted forest is a disjoint union of rooted trees.

- A descendant of

\displaystyle\lesssim(C^{*})^{2s}\theta^{-\frac{n-4s}{2s}}\sum_{i=1}^{\nu}\int_{\mathcal{A}_{i,m;\varepsilon}}\frac{1}{|x-\omega|^{n-2s}}v_{i,m}^{\textup{in}}(\omega)\textup{d}\omega

\displaystyle\ +\left\{(C^{*})^{2s}\theta+L_{0}^{-2s}+o(1)\right\}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\frac{1}{|x-\omega|^{n-2s}}\mathcal{V}_{m}(\omega)\textup{d}\omega.

Hence, by applying (

) and possibly increasing the value of

, we achieve

\mathcal{I}_{\textup{Neck},m;\varepsilon}(x)\leq C(C^{*})^{2s}\theta^{-\frac{n-4s}{2s}}\sum_{i=1}^{\nu}\int_{\mathcal{A}_{i,m;\varepsilon}}\frac{1}{|x-\omega|^{n-2s}}v_{i,m}^{\textup{in}}(\omega)\textup{d}\omega+\frac{\zeta}{6}\mathcal{W}_{m}(x)

for any

and
\theta\in(0,1)
small. Moreover,

(4.45)

\displaystyle\int_{\mathcal{A}_{i,m;\varepsilon}}\frac{1}{|x-\omega|^{n-2s}}v_{i,m}^{\textup{in}}(\omega)\textup{d}\omega

\displaystyle\leq\sum_{j\in\mathcal{D}(i)}\underbrace{\int_{B(z_{j,m},\frac{\varepsilon}{\lambda_{i,m}})\setminus\cup_{k\in\mathcal{D}(i)}B(z_{k,m},\frac{L_{0}}{\lambda_{k,m}})}\frac{1}{|x-\omega|^{n-2s}}\frac{\lambda_{i,m}^{\frac{n+2s}{2}}\mathscr{R}_{m}^{2s-n}}{\left\langle\lambda_{i,m}(\omega-z_{i,m})\right\rangle^{4s}}\mathbf{1}_{B(z_{i,m},\frac{\mathscr{R}_{m}}{\lambda_{i,m}})}(\omega)\textup{d}\omega}_{=:\mathcal{J}_{i,m;\varepsilon}(x)}

for
x\in\mathbb{R}^{n}
. We will estimate
\mathcal{J}_{i,m;\varepsilon}(x)
by considering three separate cases.

Case 1:
Fixing any

, we assume that

for some

.

Letting

, and recalling

\displaystyle\mathcal{J}_{i,m;\varepsilon}(x)

\displaystyle\leq\int_{B(z_{j,m},\frac{\varepsilon}{\lambda_{i,m}})}\frac{1}{|x-\omega|^{n-2s}}\frac{\lambda_{i,m}^{\frac{n+2s}{2}}\mathscr{R}_{m}^{2s-n}}{\left\langle\lambda_{i,m}(\omega-z_{i,m})\right\rangle^{4s}}\mathbf{1}_{B(z_{i,m},\frac{\mathscr{R}_{m}}{\lambda_{i,m}})}(\omega)\textup{d}\omega

\displaystyle\ \varepsilon^{n}\lambda_{i,m}^{\frac{n-2s}{2}}\mathscr{R}_{m}^{2s-n}\frac{1}{|\tilde{y}_{ij,m}|^{n-2s}}

\displaystyle\lesssim\varepsilon^{n}\left[(L^{\prime})^{4s-n}\frac{\lambda_{i,m}^{\frac{n-2s}{2}}\mathscr{R}_{m}^{2s-n}}{\left\langle y_{i,m}\right\rangle^{2s}}\mathbf{1}_{\{|y_{i,m}|<\mathscr{R}_{m}\}}+(L^{\prime})^{-2s}\mathscr{R}_{m}^{6s-n}\frac{\lambda_{i,m}^{\frac{n-2s}{2}}\mathscr{R}_{m}^{-4s}}{|y_{i,m}|^{n-4s}}\mathbf{1}_{\{|y_{i,m}|\geq\mathscr{R}_{m}\}}\right]

\displaystyle\leq\varepsilon^{n}\mathcal{W}_{m}(x).

Case 2:
We assume that
\frac{2\varepsilon}{\lambda_{i,m}}\leq|x-z_{j,m}|\leq\frac{L^{\prime}}{\lambda_{i,m}}
for some
j\in\mathcal{D}(i)
.

As in (

), we compute

\displaystyle\mathcal{J}_{i,m;\varepsilon}(x)

\displaystyle\leq\lambda_{i,m}^{\frac{n-2s}{2}}\mathscr{R}_{m}^{2s-n}\int_{B(0,\varepsilon)}\frac{1}{|\tilde{y}_{ij,m}-\tilde{\omega}_{ij,m}|^{n-2s}}\frac{\textup{d}\tilde{\omega}_{ij,m}}{\left\langle\tilde{\omega}_{ij,m}+z_{ij,m}\right\rangle^{4s}}

\displaystyle\lesssim\varepsilon^{2s-n}\lambda_{i,m}^{\frac{n-2s}{2}}\mathscr{R}_{m}^{2s-n}\int_{B(0,\varepsilon)}\textup{d}\tilde{\omega}_{ij,m}\lesssim\varepsilon^{2s}\lambda_{i,m}^{\frac{n-2s}{2}}\mathscr{R}_{m}^{2s-n}

Case 3:
We assume that
|x-z_{j,m}|\leq\frac{2\varepsilon}{\lambda_{i,m}}
for some
j\in\mathcal{D}(i)
.

We calculate

\displaystyle\mathcal{J}_{i,m;\varepsilon}(x)

\displaystyle\leq\int_{B(x,\frac{3\varepsilon}{\lambda_{i,m}})}\frac{1}{|x-\omega|^{n-2s}}\frac{\lambda_{i,m}^{\frac{n+2s}{2}}\mathscr{R}_{m}^{2s-n}}{\left\langle\lambda_{i,m}(\omega-z_{i,m})\right\rangle^{4s}}\mathbf{1}_{B(z_{i,m},\frac{\mathscr{R}_{m}}{\lambda_{i,m}})}(\omega)\textup{d}\omega

\displaystyle\leq\lambda_{i,m}^{\frac{n+2s}{2}}\mathscr{R}_{m}^{2s-n}\int_{B(x,\frac{3\varepsilon}{\lambda_{i,m}})}\frac{1}{|x-\omega|^{n-2s}}\textup{d}\omega\lesssim\varepsilon^{2s}\lambda_{i,m}^{\frac{n-2s}{2}}\mathscr{R}_{m}^{2s-n}.

Because

for

From the above analysis for the three cases and (

)–(

\displaystyle\mathcal{I}_{\textup{Neck},m;\varepsilon}(x)

\displaystyle\leq C(C^{*})^{2s}\theta^{-\frac{n-4s}{2s}}\sum_{i=1}^{\nu}\sum_{j\in\mathcal{D}(i)}\mathcal{J}_{i,m;\varepsilon}(x)+\frac{\zeta}{6}\mathcal{W}_{m}(x)

for all

, provided

small and

large.

Now, by inserting (

), (

), and (

). Consequently, the contradictory inequality (

) holds for all

). This completes the proof of Proposition

.

Definition 5.1

As before, the derivation of (

Step 1.
Assume that

,

Lemma 5.2
.

There exists a constant

,

\left\|\sigma^{p}-\sum_{i=1}^{\nu}U_{i}^{p}\right\|_{**}\leq C\mathscr{Q}

By elementary calculus, (

Proof.

Substep 1.
We claim that there is a constant

,

, and
\nu

\sum_{i=1}^{\nu}\sum_{a=1}^{n+1}|c_{i}^{a}|\leq C\left(\|h\|_{**}+\mathscr{Q}\|f\|_{*}\right).

Appendix A
Auxiliary results

A.1.
Non-degeneracy result

We prove the non-degeneracy of the bubble
U[z,\lambda]
, which is a minor variation of ones in
[
23
,
44
]
. We believe that it is of practical use elsewhere.

Lemma A.1
.

Let
n\in\mathbb{N}
and
s\in(0,\frac{n}{2})
. If
Z\in\dot{H}^{s}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
solves

(-\Delta)^{s}Z-p\,U[0,1]^{p-1}Z=0\quad\text{in }\mathbb{R}^{n},

then
Z\in\textnormal{span}\{Z^{0}[0,1],Z^{1}[0,1],\ldots,Z^{n+1}[0,1]
}
.

Proof.

As in (
4.2
), it holds that

(A.1)

Z=\Phi_{n,s}\ast\left(p\,U[0,1]^{p-1}Z\right)\quad\text{in }\mathbb{R}^{n}.

It view of
[
44
, Lemma 5.1]
, it suffices to know that
Z\in L^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
. Its verification can be done in the following way:

-

If
n\geq 6s
, we apply the iteration process in Substep 1 of the proof of Proposition
3.3
to (
A.1
).

-

If
2s<n<6s
, then the HLS inequality and Hölder’s inequality imply

\hskip 30.0pt\|Z\|_{L^{t^{*}}(\mathbb{R}^{n})}\lesssim\left\|\frac{|Z|}{\left\langle\,\cdot\,\right\rangle^{4s}}\ast\frac{1}{|\cdot|^{n-2s}}\right\|_{L^{t^{*}}(\mathbb{R}^{n})}\lesssim\left\|\frac{|Z|}{\left\langle\,\cdot\,\right\rangle^{4s}}\right\|_{L^{\zeta_{2}}(\mathbb{R}^{n})}\lesssim\|Z\|_{L^{t}(\mathbb{R}^{n})}\left\|\frac{1}{\left\langle\,\cdot\,\right\rangle^{4s}}\right\|_{L^{\zeta_{1}}(\mathbb{R}^{n})}

for
t=\frac{2n}{n-2s}
,
\zeta_{1}\in(\frac{n}{2s},\frac{2n}{6s-n})
,
\zeta_{2}=\frac{t\zeta_{1}}{\zeta_{1}+t}
, and
t^{*}=\frac{n\zeta_{2}}{n-2s\zeta_{2}}\in(\frac{2n}{n-2s},\infty)
.
This means that
Z\in L^{\tilde{t}}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
for all
\tilde{t}\geq\frac{2n}{n-2s}
. From (
4.6
), we conclude that
Z\in L^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
. ∎

A.2.
Removability of singularity

We derive a result on the removability of singularities of a solution to an integral equation, which will be used in the proof of Lemma
4.6
.

Lemma A.2
.

Suppose that
n\in\mathbb{N}
,
s\in(0,\frac{n}{2})
,
\alpha\in(0,n)
, and
\beta>2s
.
Given any
N\in\mathbb{N}
, let
\mathfrak{y}_{1},\ldots,\mathfrak{y}_{N}
be distinct points in
\mathbb{R}^{n}
. If
f
and
V
are functions such that

(A.2)

\begin{cases}f=\Phi_{n,s}\ast(Vf)&\text{in }\mathbb{R}^{n}\setminus\{\mathfrak{y}_{1},\ldots,\mathfrak{y}_{N}\},\\
\displaystyle|f(y)|\leq C\left(1+\sum_{i=1}^{N}\frac{1}{|y-\mathfrak{y}_{i}|^{\alpha}}\right),\,|V(y)|\leq\frac{C}{\left\langle y\right\rangle^{\beta}}&\text{for }y\in\mathbb{R}^{n}\setminus\{\mathfrak{y}_{1},\ldots,\mathfrak{y}_{N}\}\end{cases}

for some
C>0
, then
f\in L^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
.

Proof.

Let
C^{**}=1+\max_{i=1,\ldots,N}|\mathfrak{y}_{i}|
. By (
A.2
),
f
is clearly bounded in the set
B(0,4C^{**})^{c}
.

Suppose that
y\in B(0,4C^{**})\setminus\{\mathfrak{y}_{1},\ldots,\mathfrak{y}_{N}\}
, it holds that

(A.3)

\displaystyle|f(y)|

\displaystyle\lesssim\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\frac{1}{|y-\omega|^{n-2s}}\frac{\textup{d}\omega}{\left\langle\omega\right\rangle^{\beta}}+\sum_{i=1}^{N}\int_{B(0,2C^{**})^{c}}\frac{1}{|y-\omega|^{n-2s}}\frac{\textup{d}\omega}{|\omega|^{\alpha+\beta}}

\displaystyle+\sum_{i=1}^{N}\int_{B(0,2C^{**})}\frac{1}{|y-\omega|^{n-2s}}\frac{\textup{d}\omega}{|\omega-\mathfrak{y}_{i}|^{\alpha}}

\displaystyle\lesssim\frac{1+\log(2+|y|)\mathbf{1}_{\{\beta=n\}}}{\left\langle y\right\rangle^{\min\{\beta,n\}-2s}}+\sum_{i=1}^{N}\int_{B(0,2C^{**})}\frac{1}{|y-\omega|^{n-2s}}\frac{\textup{d}\omega}{|\omega-\mathfrak{y}_{i}|^{\alpha}}

where the integrals on the first line were computed as in (
4.3
).

We shall estimate the rightmost integral in (
A.3
).
Fix any non-negative
\zeta\in(\alpha-2s,\min\{\alpha,n-2s\}]
.
By handling the cases
\{\omega\in\mathbb{R}^{n}:|\omega-y|<\frac{1}{2}|y-\mathfrak{y}_{i}|\}
,
\{\omega\in\mathbb{R}^{n}:|\omega-\mathfrak{y}_{i}|<\frac{1}{2}|y-\mathfrak{y}_{i}|\}
, and
\{\omega\in\mathbb{R}^{n}:\min\{|\omega-y|,|\omega-\mathfrak{y}_{i}|\}\geq\frac{1}{2}|y-\mathfrak{y}_{i}|\}
separately, one can derive

(A.4)

\frac{1}{|y-\omega|^{n-2s}}\frac{1}{|\omega-\mathfrak{y}_{i}|^{\alpha}}\leq\frac{c}{|y-\mathfrak{y}_{i}|^{\zeta}}\left(\frac{1}{|\omega-y|^{n-2s+\alpha-\zeta}}+\frac{1}{|\omega-\mathfrak{y}_{i}|^{n-2s+\alpha-\zeta}}\right)

where
c>0
is determined by
n
,
s
,
\alpha
, and
\zeta
. By (
A.4
) and the estimate

\displaystyle\int_{B(0,2C^{**})}\frac{\textup{d}\omega}{|\omega-\mathfrak{y}_{i}|^{n-2s+\alpha-\zeta}}

\displaystyle\lesssim\int_{\left\{|\omega-\mathfrak{y}_{i}|<\frac{|\mathfrak{y}_{i}|}{2}\right\}}\frac{\textup{d}\omega}{|\omega-\mathfrak{y}_{i}|^{n-2s+\alpha-\zeta}}+\frac{1}{|\mathfrak{y}_{i}|^{n-2s+\alpha-\zeta}}\int_{\left\{|\omega|<\frac{|\mathfrak{y}_{i}|}{2}\right\}}\textup{d}\omega

\displaystyle\quad+\int_{\left\{\frac{|\mathfrak{y}_{i}|}{2}\leq|\omega|<2C^{**}\right\}\cap\left\{|\omega-\mathfrak{y}_{i}|\geq\frac{|\mathfrak{y}_{i}|}{2}\right\}}\frac{\textup{d}\omega}{|\omega|^{n-2s+\alpha-\zeta}}

\displaystyle\lesssim|\mathfrak{y}_{i}|^{\zeta-(\alpha-2s)}+|\mathfrak{y}_{i}|^{\zeta-(\alpha-2s)}+(C^{**})^{\zeta-(\alpha-2s)}\lesssim(C^{**})^{\zeta-(\alpha-2s)},

we deduce

\int_{B(0,2C^{**})}\frac{1}{|y-\omega|^{n-2s}}\frac{\textup{d}\omega}{|\omega-\mathfrak{y}_{i}|^{\alpha}}\lesssim\frac{c(C^{**})^{\zeta-(\alpha-2s)}}{|y-\mathfrak{y}_{i}|^{\zeta}}

provided
|y|<4C^{**}
.

Therefore,

|f(y)|\leq C^{\prime}\left(1+\sum_{i=1}^{N}\frac{1}{|y-\mathfrak{y}_{i}|^{\zeta}}\right)\quad\text{for }y\in\mathbb{R}^{n}\setminus\{\mathfrak{y}_{1},\ldots,\mathfrak{y}_{N}\}

where
C^{\prime}>0
is determined by
n
,
s
,
\alpha
,
\beta
,
\zeta
,
C^{**}
, and
C
in (
A.2
).
Feeding back this information into (
A.3
), we can iterate the above process until we get
f\in L^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
.
∎
