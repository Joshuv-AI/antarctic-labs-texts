Improved Bounds on the Span of L ⁡ ( 1 , 2 ) L(1,2) -edge Labeling of Some Infinite Regular Grids Preliminary version of this paper was presented at the 18th Cologne-Twente Workshop on Graphs and Combinatorial Optimization (CTW 2020), September 14-16, 2020 Bandopadhyay et al. 2020
Susobhan Bandopadhyay, Sasthi C. Ghosh, Subhasis Koley

Abstract
For two given nonnegative integers h h and k k , an L ⁡ ( h , k ) L(h,k) -edge labeling of a graph G G is the assignment of labels { 0 , 1 , ⋯ , n } \{0,1,\cdots,n\} to the edges so that two edges having a common vertex are labeled with difference at least h h and two edges not having any common vertex but having a common edge connecting them are labeled with difference at least k k . The span λ h , k ′ ​ ( G ) \lambda^{\prime}_{h,k}{(G)} is the minimum n n such that G G admits an L ⁡ ( h , k ) L(h,k) -edge labeling. Here our main focus is on finding λ 1 , 2 ′ ​ ( G ) \lambda^{\prime}_{1,2}{(G)} for L ⁡ ( 1 , 2 ) L(1,2) -edge labeling of infinite regular hexagonal ( T 3 T_{3} ), square ( T 4 T_{4} ), triangular ( T 6 T_{6} ) and octagonal ( T 8 T_{8} ) grids. It was known that 7 ≤ λ 1 , 2 ′ ​ ( T 3 ) ≤ 8 7\leq\lambda^{\prime}_{1,2}{(T_{3})}\leq 8 , 10 ≤ λ 1 , 2 ′ ​ ( T 4 ) ≤ 11 10\leq\lambda^{\prime}_{1,2}{(T_{4})}\leq 11 , 16 ≤ λ 1 , 2 ′ ​ ( T 6 ) ≤ 20 16\leq\lambda^{\prime}_{1,2}{(T_{6})}\leq 20 and 25 ≤ λ 1 , 2 ′ ​ ( T 8 ) ≤ 28 25\leq\lambda^{\prime}_{1,2}{(T_{8})}\leq 28 . Here we settle two long standing open questions i.e. λ 1 , 2 ′ ​ ( T 3 ) \lambda^{\prime}_{1,2}{(T_{3})} and λ 1 , 2 ′ ​ ( T 4 ) \lambda^{\prime}_{1,2}{(T_{4})} . We show λ 1 , 2 ′ ​ ( T 3 ) = 7 \lambda^{\prime}_{1,2}{(T_{3})}=7 , λ 1 , 2 ′ ​ ( T 4 ) = 11 \lambda^{\prime}_{1,2}{(T_{4})}=11 . We also improve the bound for T 6 T_{6} and T 8 T_{8} and prove λ 1 , 2 ′ ​ ( T 6 ) ≥ 18 \lambda^{\prime}_{1,2}{(T_{6})}\geq 18 , λ 1 , 2 ′ ​ ( T 8 ) ≥ 26 \lambda^{\prime}_{1,2}{(T_{8})}\geq 26 .

Introduction

Channel assignment problem
(CAP) is one of the fundamental problems in wireless communication where frequency channels are assigned to transmitters such that interference can not occur. The objective of the CAP is to minimize the span of frequency spectrum. In 1980, Hale
Hale 1980

Definition 1
.

For two non-negative integers
h
and
k

-vertex labeling of a graph

is a function
\mathbf{f}:V\xrightarrow{}\{0,1,\cdots,n\},\forall v\in V
such that
|\mathbf{f}(u)-\mathbf{f}(v)|\geq h
when

and
|\mathbf{f}(u)-\mathbf{f}(v)|\geq k
when
d(u,v)=2

and
v
,

is
k^{\prime}

k^{\prime}

and

The

-vertex labeling. In 1992 Griggs and Yeh

Definition 2

For two non-negative integers
h
