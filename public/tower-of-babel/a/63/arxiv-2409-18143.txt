The L 1 L^{1} -relaxed area of the graph of the vortex map: optimal upper bound
Giovanni Bellettini, Alaa Elshorbagy, Riccardo Scala

Abstract
We compute an upper bound for the value of the L 1 L^{1} -relaxed area of the graph of the vortex map u : B l ​ ( 0 ) ⊂ ℝ 2 → ℝ 2 u:{\rm B}_{l}(0)\subset\mathbb{R}^{2}\to\mathbb{R}^{2} , u ⁡ ( x ) := x / | x | u(x):=x/|x| , x ≠ 0 x\neq 0 , for all values of l > 0 l>0 . Together with a previously proven lower bound, this upper bound turns out to be optimal. Interestingly, for the radius l l in a certain range, in particular l l not too large, a Plateau-type problem, having as solution a sort of catenoid constrained to contain a segment, has to be solved.



Key words:
Area functional, minimal surfaces, Plateau problem,
relaxation, Cartesian currents.

In order to prove our main result, the analysis

The paper is organized as follows: in Sections

In Section
