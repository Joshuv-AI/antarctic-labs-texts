1.
Introduction

The aim of this paper is to investigate the fractional heat semigroup of Schrödinger operators

L:=-\Delta+V(x),

where
-\Delta
denotes the Laplace operator:
\Delta=\sum\limits^{n}_{i=1}{\partial^{2}}/{\partial x_{i}^{2}}
and
V
is a nonnegative potential belonging to the reverse Hölder class
B_{q}
:

Definition 1.1
.

A nonnegative locally
L^{q}
integrable function
V
on
\mathbb{R}^{n}

is said to belong to
B_{q},1<q<\infty,
if there exists
C>0

such that the reverse Hölder inequality

(1.1)

\Big(\frac{1}{|B|}\int_{B}V^{q}(x)dx\Big)^{1/q}\leq\frac{C}{|B|}\int_{B}V(x)dx

holds for every ball
B
in
\mathbb{R}^{n}.

For the special case:
V=0
,
L=-\Delta
, the fractional heat semigroup can be defined via the Fourier transform:

(1.2)

\big({e^{-t(-\Delta)^{\alpha}}(f)}\big)^{\wedge}(\xi):=e^{-t|\xi|^{2\alpha}}\widehat{f}(\xi),\ \alpha\in(0,1].

In the literature, the fractional heat semigroup
\{e^{-t(-\Delta)^{\alpha}}\}_{t>0}
has been widely used in the study of
partial differential equations, harmonic analysis, potential theory and modern probability theory. For example, the semigroup
\{e^{-t(-\Delta)^{\alpha}}\}_{t>0}
is
usually applied to construct the linear part of solutions to fluid equations in
the mathematic physics, e.g. the generalized
Naiver-Stokes equation, the quasi-geostrophic equation, the generalized MHD equations. In the field of probability theory, the researchers use

\{e^{-t(-\Delta)^{\alpha}}\}_{t>0}
to describe some kind of Markov processes with jumps. For further information and the related applications of fractional heat semigroups
\{e^{-t(-\Delta)^{\alpha}}\}_{t>0}
, we refer the reader to
[
1
,
12
,
18
,
33
]
.

Denote by
K_{\alpha,t}(\cdot,\cdot)
the integral kernel of
e^{-t(-\Delta)^{\alpha}}
, i.e.,

(1.3)

K_{\alpha,t}(x)=(2\pi)^{-n/2}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}e^{ix\cdot\xi-t|\xi|^{2\alpha}}d\xi

and denote by
K^{\theta}_{\alpha,t}(\cdot)
the integral kernel of

(-\Delta)^{\theta/2}e^{-t(-\Delta)^{\alpha}}
. In
[
24
]
, by an
invariant derivative technique and the Fourier analysis method, Miao-Yuan-Zhang concluded that
the kernels
K_{\alpha,t}
and
K^{\theta}_{\alpha,t}
satisfy the following pointwise estimates, respectively (cf.
[
24
, Lemmas 2.1 and 2.2]
),

\left\{\begin{aligned} K_{\alpha,t}(x)&\lesssim\frac{t}{(t^{{1}/{2\alpha}}+|x|)^{n+2\alpha}}\quad\forall\ (x,t)\in\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n+1};\\
K_{\alpha,t}^{\theta}(x)&\lesssim\frac{1}{(t^{1/2\alpha}+|x|)^{n+\theta}}\quad\forall\ (x,t)\in\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n+1}.\end{aligned}\right.

Compared with
-\Delta
, for arbitrary Schrödinger operator
L
with the non-negative potential
V
, the fractional heat
semigroup
\{e^{-tL^{\alpha}}\}_{t>0},\alpha\in(0,1)
, can not be defined via (
1.2
). Also, it is obvious that the methods in
[
24
]
are
invalid for the estimation of the integral kernels of

\{e^{-t{L}^{\alpha}}\}_{t>0}
. In this paper, by the functional calculus, we observe that the integral kernel of the Poisson semigroup associated with
L

can be defined as

(1.4)

P^{L}_{t}(x,y)=\int^{\infty}_{0}\frac{e^{-u}}{\sqrt{u}}K^{L}_{t^{2}/4u}(x,y)du=\int^{\infty}_{0}\frac{te^{-t^{2}/4s}}{2s^{3/2}}K^{L}_{s}(x,y)ds,

where
K^{L}_{s}(\cdot,\cdot)
denotes the integral kernel of
e^{-sL}
, i.e.,

e^{-sL}(f)(x):=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}K^{L}_{s}(x,y)f(y)dy.

Recall that
K^{L}_{t}(\cdot,\cdot)
is a positive, symmetric function on
\mathbb{R}^{n}\times\mathbb{R}^{n}
, and
satisfies
\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}K^{L}_{t}(x,y)dy\leq 1
. Generally, for
\alpha>0
, the subordinative formula (cf.
[
12
]
)
indicates that there exists a continuous function
\eta^{\alpha}_{t}(\cdot)
on
(0,\infty)
such that

(1.5)

K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)=\int^{\infty}_{0}\eta^{\alpha}_{t}(s)K^{L}_{s}(x,y)ds.

The identity (
1.5
) enables us to estimate
K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(\cdot,\cdot)
via the heat kernel
K^{L}_{t}(\cdot,\cdot)
. Let

\rho(\cdot)
be the auxiliary function defined by (
2.2
) below. In Propositions
3.1
and
3.2
, we can obtain the following pointwise estimates of
K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(\cdot,\cdot)
:
for every
N>0
, there exists a constant
C_{N}
such that

(1.6)

\Big|K^{{L}}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)\Big|\leq\frac{C_{N}t}{(t^{1/2\alpha}+|x-y|)^{n+2\alpha}}\Big(1+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N},

and for every
N>0
,
0<\delta^{\prime}<\min\{1,2-n/q\}
and all
|h|\leq t^{1/\alpha}
, there exists a constant
C_{N}
such that

(1.7)

\displaystyle\Big|K^{{L}}_{\alpha,t}(x+h,y)-K^{{L}}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)\Big|\leq\frac{C_{N}t({|h|}/{t^{1/2\alpha}})^{\delta^{\prime}}}{(t^{1/2\alpha}+|x-y|)^{n+2\alpha}}\Big(1+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}.

Based on the estimates (
1.6
) and (
1.7
), we consider the regularity properties of
K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(\cdot,\cdot)
.
Let
\nabla_{x,t}
denote the gradient operator on
\mathbb{R}^{n}
, that is,
\nabla_{x,t}=(\nabla_{x},{\partial}/{\partial t})
, where

\nabla_{x}=(\partial/\partial x_{1},\partial/\partial x_{2},\ldots,\partial/\partial x_{n})
. We obtain an energy estimate of the solution to the equation:

(1.8)

\partial_{t}u+Lu=\partial_{t}u-\Delta u+Vu=0.

By the fundamental solution of
-\Delta
, we prove that, for any
N>0
, there exists a constant
C_{N}
such that

|\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{t}(x,y)|\leq\left\{\begin{aligned} &\frac{C_{N}}{t^{(n+1)/2}}e^{-c|x-y|^{2}/t}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N},&\quad\sqrt{t}\leq|x-y|;\\
&\frac{C_{N}}{|x-y|t^{n/2}}e^{-c|x-y|^{2}/t}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N},&\quad\sqrt{t}\geq|x-y|;\\
&\frac{C_{N}}{t^{(n+1)/2}}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N},&\quad\forall\ t,x,y.\end{aligned}\right.

A direct computation, together with the subordinative formula, gives

|\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)|\leq\frac{C_{N}t^{1-1/2\alpha}}{(t^{1/2\alpha}+|x-y|)^{n+2\alpha}}\Big(1+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(1+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N},

see Proposition
3.6
.
By a similar method, we obtain the Hölder regularity of
\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(\cdot,\cdot)
, i.e., for
|h|<|x-y|/4
and
\delta^{\prime}=1-n/q
,

|\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x+h,y)-\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)|\leq C_{N}\Big(\frac{|h|}{t^{1/2\alpha}}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}\frac{1}{t^{1/2\alpha}}\frac{t}{(t^{1/2\alpha}+|x-y|)^{n+2\alpha}},

see Proposition
3.9
.

In Section
3.3
, we focus on the time-fractional derivatives of
K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(\cdot,\cdot)
. Recently, there has been an increasing interest in fractional calculus since time-fractional operators are proved to be very useful for modeling purpose. For example, the following fractional heat equations

(1.9)

\partial^{\beta}_{t}u(x,t)=\Delta u(x,t)

are used to describe heat propagation in inhomogeneous media. It is known that as opposed to the classical heat equation, the equation (
1.9
) is known to exhibit sub diffusive behaviour and is related with anomalous diffusions or diffusions in non-homogeneous media, with random fractal structures. Recall that the fractional derivative of
K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(\cdot,\cdot)
is defined as

(1.10)

\partial_{t}^{\beta}K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x,y):=\frac{e^{-i\pi(m-\beta)}}{\Gamma(m-\beta)}\int^{\infty}_{0}\partial^{m}_{t}K^{L}_{\alpha,t+u}(x,y)u^{m-\beta}\frac{du}{u},\ \beta>0\text{ and }m=[\beta]+1.

In Section
3.1
, we first obtain the regularity estimates of

denoted by
\widetilde{D}_{\alpha,t}^{L,m}(\cdot,\cdot)
, see Proposition
3.3
.
Then the desired estimates of
\partial_{t}^{\beta}K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(\cdot,\cdot)
can be deduced from (
1.10
) and Proposition
3.3
, see Propositions
3.11
-
3.13
, respectively.

Preliminaries

2.1.
The Schrödinger operator

Let
L=-\Delta+V
be the Schrödinger differential operator
on
\mathbb{R}^{n},n\geq 3.
Throughout the paper, we will assume that

V
is a nonzero, nonnegative potential, and belongs to the reverse Hölder class
B_{q},q>n/2,
which is defined in Definition
1.1
.
By Hölder’s inequality, we can get
B_{q_{1}}\subset B_{q_{2}}

for
q_{1}\geq q_{2}>1
. One remarkable feature about the class
B_{q}

is that if
V\in B_{q}
for some
q>1
, then there exists

\varepsilon>0
, which depends only on
n
and the constant
C
in
(
1.1
), such that
V\in B_{q+\varepsilon}
. It is also well known that
if
V\in B_{q},q>1
then
V(x)dx
is a doubling measure. Namely,
for any
r>0,x\in\mathbb{R}^{n}
,

(2.1)

\int_{B(x,2r)}V(y)dy\leq C_{0}\int_{B(x,r)}V(y)dy.

The auxiliary function
m(x,V)
is defined by

(2.2)

\frac{1}{m(x,V)}:=\sup\Big\{r>0:\ \frac{1}{r^{n-2}}\int_{B(x,r)}V(y)dy\leq 1\Big\}.

Clearly,
0<m(x,V)<\infty
for every
x\in\mathbb{R}^{n}
and if

r={1}/{m(x,V)}
, then

\frac{1}{r^{n-2}}\int_{B(x,r)}V(y)dy=1.
For simplicity, we
denote
{1}/{m(x,V)}
by
\rho(x)
in the proof sometime.
We state some
properties of
m(x,V)
which will be used in the proofs of main results.

Lemma 2.1
.

(
[
25
, Lemma 1.2]
)
There exists a
constant
C>0
such that for every
0<r<R<\infty
and

, we have

\frac{1}{r^{n-2}}\int_{B(y,r)}V(x)dx\leq C\Big(\frac{r}{R}\Big)^{2-n/q}\frac{1}{R^{n-2}}\int_{B(y,R)}V(x)dx.

Lemma 2.2
.

(
[

For every constant

, there exists a constant

.

(

2.2.
Fractional heat kernels associated with
L

In this section, we first state some backgrounds on the fractional heat semigroup and the fractional heat kernel associated with
L
.
For the case
V\neq 0
, the fractional heat semigroup associated with
L
can not be defined via the Fourier multiplier method (
1.2
) as
the Laplace operator. We strike out on a new path and introduce the fractional heat semigroup via the subordinative formula.

The Schrödinger operator
L
can be seen as the generator of the semigroup

\{e^{-tL}\}_{t>0}
, i.e.,

L(f):=\lim_{t\rightarrow 0}\frac{f-e^{-tL}f}{t},

where the limit is in
L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
.

L
is a self-adjoint, positive definite operator. The integral kernels of the semigroups
\{e^{-tL}\}_{t>0}
are denoted by
K^{L}_{t}(\cdot,\cdot)
.
It is easy to verify that the kernel
K^{L}_{t}(\cdot,\cdot)
satisfies

\left\{\begin{aligned} &{\rm(i)}\ K^{L}_{t}(x,y)\geq 0,\ x,y\in\mathbb{R}^{n};\\
&{\rm(ii)}\ K^{L}_{t}(x,y)=K^{L}_{t}(y,x);\\
&{\rm(iii)}\ K^{L}_{s+t}(x,y)=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}K^{L}_{s}(x,z)K^{L}_{t}(z,y)dz;\\
&{\rm(iv)}\ \lim\limits_{t\rightarrow 0+}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}K^{L}_{t}(x,y)f(y)dy=f(x)\quad\forall\ f\in L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{n}).\end{aligned}\right.

For
\alpha\in(0,1)
, the fractional power of
L
, denoted by
L^{\alpha}
, is defined as

L^{\alpha}(f)=\frac{1}{\Gamma(-\alpha)}\int^{\infty}_{0}\Big(e^{-t\sqrt{L}}f(x)-f(x)\Big)\frac{dt}{t^{1+2\alpha}}\quad\forall\ f\in L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{n}).

Here
\{e^{-t\sqrt{L}}\}_{t>0}
denotes the Poisson semigroup related to
L
with the kernel
P^{L}_{t}(\cdot,\cdot)
defined as

\displaystyle P^{L}_{t}(x,y)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int^{\infty}_{0}\frac{e^{-u}}{\sqrt{u}}K^{L}_{t^{2}/4u}(x,y)du=\int^{\infty}_{0}\frac{e^{-t^{2}/4s}}{2s^{3/2}}K^{L}_{s}(x,y)ds.

By the subordinative formula, we know that there exists a non-negative continuous function
\eta^{\alpha}_{t}(\cdot)
satisfying (cf.
[
12
]
)

(2.4)

\left\{\begin{aligned} &\eta^{\alpha}_{t}(s)=\frac{1}{t^{1/\alpha}}\eta^{\alpha}_{1}(s/t^{1/\alpha});\\
&\eta^{\alpha}_{t}(s)\lesssim\frac{t}{s^{1+\alpha}}\ \forall\ s,t>0;\\
&\int^{\infty}_{0}s^{-\gamma}\eta^{\alpha}_{1}(s)ds<\infty,\ \gamma>0;\\
&\eta^{\alpha}_{t}(s)\simeq\frac{t}{s^{1+\alpha}}\,\ \forall\,\ s\geq t^{1/\alpha}>0,\end{aligned}\right.

such that
K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(\cdot,\cdot)
can be expressed as

(2.5)

K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)=\int^{\infty}_{0}\eta^{\alpha}_{t}(s)K^{{L}}_{s}(x,y)ds,

see
[
12
]
for some examples of
\eta^{\alpha}_{t}(\cdot)
. The function
\eta^{\alpha}_{t}(\cdot)
plays an important role in the estimate of the fractional heat kernel
K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(\cdot,\cdot)
.
Take
\alpha=1/2
for example: by (
1.4
), we can see that
\eta^{1/2}_{t}(s)=\frac{t}{2s^{3/2}}e^{-t^{2}/4s}\ \forall\ s,t>0.
It is
easy to verify that such
\eta^{1/2}_{t}(\cdot)
satisfies conditions in (
2.4
). For the special case
L=-\Delta
, a direct computation gives

P^{-\Delta}_{t}(x,y)=\int^{\infty}_{0}\frac{e^{-t^{2}/4s}t}{2s^{3/2}}s^{-n/2}e^{-|x-y|^{2}/s}ds=\frac{c_{n}t}{(t^{2}+|x-y|^{2})^{(n+1)/2}},

which coincides with the classical Poisson kernel obtained via (
1.3
).

2.3.
Campanato type spaces associated with
L

The Campanato type space associated with
L

is defined as follows.

Definition 2.11
.

The space
BMO^{\gamma}_{L}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
,
0<\gamma\leq 1
, is defined as the set of all locally integrable functions
f
satisfying
there exists a constant
C
such that

(2.6)

\sup_{B}\frac{1}{|B|^{1+\gamma/n}}\int_{B}|f(x)-f(B,V)|dx\leq C<\infty,

where the supremum is taken over all balls
B
centered at
x_{B}
with radius
r_{B}
, and

f(B,V):=\begin{cases}f_{B},\ r_{B}<\rho(x_{B});\\
0,\ r_{B}\geq\rho(x_{B}).\end{cases}

The norm
\|f\|_{BMO^{\gamma}_{L}}
is defined as the infimum of the constants
C
such that (
2.6
) above
holds.

Proposition 2.12
.

(
[
23
, Proposition 4.3]
)

Let
B=B(x,r)
with
r<\rho(x)
. If
f\in BMO^{\gamma}_{L}(\mathbb{R}^{n}),0<\gamma\leq 1
, then there exists a constant
C_{\gamma}
such that

|f_{B}|\leq C_{\gamma}(\rho(x))^{\gamma}\|f\|_{BMO^{\gamma}_{L}}
.

The space
BMO^{\gamma}_{L}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
is equivalent to the following Lipschitz type space related to
L
.

Definition 2.13
.

For
0<\gamma\leq 1
, a continuous function
f
defined on
\mathbb{R}^{n}
belongs to the space
C^{0,\gamma}_{L}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
if

\sup_{x,y\in\mathbb{R}^{n}}\frac{|f(x)-f(y)|}{|x-y|^{\gamma}}<\infty\text{ and }\sup_{x\in\mathbb{R}^{n}}\frac{|f(x)|}{\rho(x)^{\gamma}}<\infty.

Proposition 2.14
.

(
[
23
, Proposition 4.6]
)

If
0<\gamma\leq 1
, then the spaces
BMO^{\gamma}_{L}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
and
C^{0,\gamma}_{L}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
are equal and their norms are
equivalent.

It is well known that Hardy spaces
H^{p}(\mathbb{R}^{n}),0<p\leq 1
, are the predual spaces of Campanato spaces (cf.
[
10
]
). In 2000s, such dual relationship was extended to function spaces associated with operators, see
[
3
,
7
,
9
,
15
,
22
,
30
]
. For a Schrödinger operator
L
, the following Hardy type spaces
H^{p}_{L}(\mathbb{R}^{n}),0<p\leq 1
, were introduced in
[
6
,
8
]
.

Definition 2.15
.

For
0<p\leq 1
, an integrable function
f
is an element of the Hardy type space
H^{p}_{L}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
if the maximal function

T^{\ast}(f)(x):=\sup_{s>0}|T^{L}_{s}(f)(x)|

belongs to
L^{p}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
. The quasi-norm in
H^{p}_{L}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
is defined by

\|f\|_{H^{p}_{L}}:=\|T^{\ast}(f)\|_{L^{p}}.

Let
{\delta_{0}}=\min\{1,2-n/q\}
and
n/(n+\delta_{0})<p\leq 1
. An atom of
H^{p}_{L}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
associated with a ball
B(x_{B},r_{B})

is a function
a
such that

\left\{\begin{aligned} &\text{ supp }a\subseteq B(x_{B},r_{B}),\ r_{B}\leq\rho(x_{B});\\
&\|a\|_{L^{\infty}}\leq|B(x_{B},r_{B})|^{-1/p};\\
&\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}a(x)dx=0,\ r_{B}<\rho(x_{B})/4.\end{aligned}\right.

In
[
8
]
, Dziubański and Zienkiewicz obtained the following atomic characterization of
H^{p}_{L}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
.

Proposition 2.16
.

(
[
8
, Theorem 1.13]
)

Let
n/(n+\delta_{0})<p\leq 1
.
f\in H^{p}_{L}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
if and only if
f=\sum_{j}\lambda_{j}a_{j}
, where
\{a_{j}\}
are
H^{p}_{L}
-atoms and
\sum_{j}|\lambda_{j}|^{p}<\infty
.

Theorem 2.17
.

(
[
23
, Theorem 4.5]
)

Let
0\leq\gamma<1
. Then the dual space of
H^{n/(n+\gamma)}_{L}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
is
BMO^{\gamma}_{L}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
.
More precisely, any continuous linear functional
\Phi
over
H^{n/(n+\gamma)}_{L}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
can be represented as

\Phi(a)=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}f(x)a(x)dx

for some function
f\in BMO^{\gamma}_{L}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
and all
H^{n/(n+\gamma)}_{L}
-atoms
a
. Moreover, the operator norm
\|\Phi\|_{op}\sim\|f\|_{BMO^{\gamma}_{L}}
.

Lemma 2.18
.

(
[
9
, Lemma 7]
)

Let
q_{t}(x,y)
be a function of
x,y\in\mathbb{R}^{n}
,
t>0
. Assume that for every
N>0
, there exists a constant
C_{N}
such that for some
\gamma^{\prime}\geq\gamma
,

|q_{t}(x,y)|\leq C_{N}(1+t/\rho(x)+t/\rho(y))^{-N}t^{-n}(1+|x-y|/t)^{-(n+\gamma^{\prime})}.

Then for every
H^{n/(n+\gamma)}_{L}
-atom
g
supported on
B(x_{0},r)
, there exists
C_{N,x_{0},r}>0
such that

\sup_{t>0}\Big|\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}q_{t}(x,y)g(y)dy\Big|\leq C_{N,x_{0},r}(1+|x|)^{-(n+\gamma^{\prime})},\ x\in\mathbb{R}^{n}.

3.
Regularities on fractional heat semigroups associated with
L

The aim of this section is to estimate the regularities of the fractional heat kernel
K_{\alpha,t}^{L}(\cdot,\cdot)
. By use of (
1.5
), we first estimate
\partial_{t}^{m}K_{\alpha,t}^{L}(\cdot,\cdot),m\geq 1.
Then, via the solution to (
1.8
), we investigate the spatial gradient of
K_{\alpha,t}^{L}(\cdot,\cdot)
. At last, we obtain the estimation of the time-fractional derivatives of
K_{\alpha,t}^{L}(\cdot,\cdot)
.

3.1.
Regularities of the fractional heat kernel

We first investigate the regularities of
K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(\cdot,\cdot)
.

Proposition 3.1
.

Let
\alpha\in(0,1)
. For every
N>0
, there exists a constant
C_{N}
such that

\Big|K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)\Big|\leq\frac{C_{N}t}{(t^{1/2\alpha}+|x-y|)^{n+2\alpha}}\Big(1+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}.

Proof.

By Proposition
2.7
, we use (
2.3
), (
2.4
) and (
2.5
) to obtain

\displaystyle\Big|K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)\Big|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\int^{\infty}_{0}\frac{t}{s^{1+\alpha}}\Big|K^{L}_{s}(x,y)\Big|ds

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{N}\int^{\infty}_{0}\frac{te^{-c|x-y|^{2}/s}}{s^{1+\alpha+n/2}}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{s}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{s}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}ds.

Taking the change of variable
s=t^{1/\alpha}u
, we have

\displaystyle\Big|K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)\Big|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{N}\int^{\infty}_{0}\frac{t^{1+1/\alpha}}{(t^{1/\alpha}u)^{1+\alpha}}(t^{1/\alpha}u)^{-n/2}e^{-\frac{c|x-y|^{2}}{t^{1/\alpha}u}}\Big(1+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}\sqrt{u}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(1+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}\sqrt{u}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}du

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{C_{N}}{t^{n/2\alpha}}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}\int^{\infty}_{0}\frac{1}{u^{1+\alpha}}u^{-n/2-N}e^{-\frac{c|x-y|^{2}}{t^{1/\alpha}u}}du.

Let
\frac{|x-y|^{2}}{t^{1/\alpha}u}=r^{2}
. Then

\displaystyle\Big|K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)\Big|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{N}t^{-n/2\alpha}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}\int^{\infty}_{0}\Big(\frac{|x-y|^{2}}{t^{1/\alpha}r^{2}}\Big)^{-1-\alpha-n/2-N}e^{-cr^{2}}\frac{|x-y|^{2}}{t^{1/\alpha}r^{3}}dr

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{N}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}\frac{t^{1+N/\alpha}}{|x-y|^{2\alpha+n+2N}}\int^{\infty}_{0}r^{2\alpha+n+2N-1}e^{-cr^{2}}dr

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{N}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}\frac{t^{1+N/\alpha}}{|x-y|^{n+2\alpha+2N}},

which gives

(3.1)

\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{N}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{N}\Big|K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)\Big|\leq\frac{C_{N}t^{1+N/\alpha}}{|x-y|^{n+2\alpha+2N}}.

On the other hand, using the change of variables again, we obtain

\displaystyle\Big|K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)\Big|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{N}\int^{\infty}_{0}s^{-n/2}\eta^{\alpha}_{1}\Big(\frac{s}{t^{1/\alpha}}\Big)\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{s}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{s}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}ds

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{N}\int^{\infty}_{0}(t^{1/\alpha}\tau)^{-n/2}\frac{1}{t^{1/\alpha}}\eta^{\alpha}_{1}(\tau)\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{\tau}t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{\tau}t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}t^{1/\alpha}d\tau

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{N}t^{-n/2\alpha}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}\int^{\infty}_{0}\tau^{-n/2-N}\eta^{\alpha}_{1}(\tau)d\tau.

The above estimate implies that

(3.2)

\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{N}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{N}\Big|K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)\Big|\leq\frac{C_{N}}{t^{n/2\alpha}}.

Now, combining (
3.1
) and (
3.2
), we have

\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{N}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{N}\Big|K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)\Big|\leq C_{N}\min\Big\{\frac{t^{1+N/\alpha}}{|x-y|^{n+2\alpha+2N}},\ t^{-n/2\alpha}\Big\},

which, together with the arbitrariness of
N
, indicates that

\Big|K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)\Big|\leq\frac{C_{N}t}{(t^{1/2\alpha}+|x-y|)^{n+2\alpha}}\Big(1+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}.

This completes the proof of Proposition
3.1
.
∎

Proposition 3.2
.

Let
\alpha\in(0,1)
. For any
N>0
, there exists a constant
C_{N}>0
such that for every
0<\delta^{\prime}<\delta_{0}=\min\{1,2-n/q\}
and all
|h|\leq t^{1/2\alpha}
,

\displaystyle|K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x+h,y)-K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{C_{N}(|h|/t^{1/2\alpha})^{\delta}t}{(t^{1/2\alpha}+|x-y|)^{n+2\alpha}}\Big(1+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}.

Proof.

The proof is similar to that of Proposition
3.1
. We first assume that
|h|<|x-y|/2
. By the subordinative formula (
2.5
), we can use Proposition
2.8
to get

\displaystyle\Big|K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x+h,y)-K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)\Big|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{N}\int^{\infty}_{0}\frac{t}{s^{1+\alpha}}s^{-n/2}e^{-c|x-y|^{2}/s}\Big(|h|/\sqrt{s}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{s}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{s}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}ds

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{N}\Big(\frac{|h|}{t^{1/2\alpha}}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}\frac{t^{1+N/\alpha+\delta^{\prime}/2\alpha}}{|x-y|^{2\alpha+n+2N+\delta^{\prime}}}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{N}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}\frac{t^{1+N/\alpha}|h|^{\delta^{\prime}}}{|x-y|^{2\alpha+n+2N+\delta^{\prime}}},

which implies

(3.3)

\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{N}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{N}\Big|K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x+h,y)-K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)\Big|\leq\frac{C_{N}t^{1+N/\alpha}|h|^{\delta^{\prime}}}{|x-y|^{2\alpha+n+2N+\delta^{\prime}}}.

On the other hand, letting
\tau=s/t^{1/\alpha}
, we have

\displaystyle\Big|K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x+h,y)-K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)\Big|

\displaystyle\leq C_{N}\int^{\infty}_{0}s^{-n/2}\frac{1}{t^{1/\alpha}}\eta^{\alpha}_{1}(s/t^{1/\alpha})\Big(\frac{|h|}{\sqrt{s}}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{s}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{s}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}ds

\displaystyle\leq C_{N}\int^{\infty}_{0}(t^{1/\alpha}\tau)^{-n/2}\eta^{\alpha}_{1}(\tau)\Big(\frac{|h|}{\sqrt{\tau}t^{1/2\alpha}}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{\tau}t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{\tau}t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}d\tau

\displaystyle\leq C_{N}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}t^{-n/2\alpha-\delta^{\prime}/2\alpha}|h|^{\delta^{\prime}}\int^{\infty}_{0}\tau^{-n/2-N-\delta^{\prime}/2}\eta^{\alpha}_{1}(\tau)d\tau.

This gives

(3.4)

\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{N}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{N}\Big|K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x+h,y)-K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)\Big|\leq C_{N}t^{-n/2\alpha}\Big(\frac{|h|}{t^{1/2\alpha}}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}.

The estimates (
3.3
) and (
3.4
) indicate that

\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{N}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{N}\Big|K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x+h,y)-K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)\Big|\leq C_{N}\min\Bigg\{\frac{t^{1+N/\alpha}|h|^{\delta^{\prime}}}{|x-y|^{2\alpha+n+2N+\delta^{\prime}}},\ t^{-n/2\alpha}\Big(\frac{|h|}{t^{1/2\alpha}}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}\Bigg\}.

Due to the arbitrariness of
N
, we have

\Big|K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x+h,y)-K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)\Big|\leq\frac{C_{N}t}{(t^{1/2\alpha}+|x-y|)^{n+2\alpha}}\Big(\frac{|h|}{t^{1/2\alpha}}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}\Big(1+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}.

This proves Proposition
3.2
under the assumption
|h|<|x-y|/2
.

Now we prove this proposition for the case
|h|<t^{1/2\alpha}
. For
|h|<|x-y|/2<t^{1/2\alpha}
or
|h|<t^{1/2\alpha}<|x-y|/2
, the desired estimate can be deduced from (
3.3
) and (
3.4
). It remains to consider the case
|x-y|/2<|h|<t^{1/2\alpha}
. We split

\displaystyle\Big|K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x+h,y)-K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)\Big|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle S_{1}+S_{2},

where

\left\{\begin{aligned} S_{1}&:=\int_{|h|<\sqrt{s}}\eta^{\alpha}_{t}(s)\Big|K_{\alpha,s}^{L}(x+h,y)-K_{\alpha,s}^{L}(x,y)\Big|ds;\\
S_{2}&:=\int_{|h|\geq\sqrt{s}}\eta^{\alpha}_{t}(s)\Big|K_{\alpha,s}^{L}(x+h,y)-K_{\alpha,s}^{L}(x,y)\Big|ds.\end{aligned}\right.

For
S_{1}
, since
|h|<\sqrt{s}
, we can follow the procedure of (
3.4
) to deduce that

\displaystyle S_{1}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\int_{|h|<\sqrt{s}}s^{-n/2}\frac{1}{t^{1/\alpha}}\eta^{\alpha}_{1}(s/t^{1/\alpha})\Big(\frac{|h|}{\sqrt{s}}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{s}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{s}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}ds

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\int^{\infty}_{0}(t^{1/\alpha}\tau)^{-n/2}\eta^{\alpha}_{1}(\tau)\Big(\frac{|h|}{\sqrt{\tau}t^{1/2\alpha}}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{\tau}t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{\tau}t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}d\tau

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}t^{-n/2\alpha}\Big(\frac{|h|}{t^{1/2\alpha}}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}\int^{\infty}_{0}\tau^{-n/2-N-\delta^{\prime}/2}\eta^{\alpha}_{1}(\tau)d\tau

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}t^{-n/2\alpha}\Big(\frac{|h|}{t^{1/2\alpha}}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}.

We further divide
S_{2}
into
S_{2}=S_{2,1}+S_{2,2}
, where

\left\{\begin{aligned} S_{2,1}&:=\int_{|h|\geq\sqrt{s}}\eta^{\alpha}_{t}(s)\Big|K_{\alpha,s}^{L}(x,y)\Big|ds;\\
S_{2,2}&:=\int_{|h|\geq\sqrt{s}}\eta^{\alpha}_{t}(s)\Big|K_{\alpha,s}^{L}(x+h,y)\Big|ds.\end{aligned}\right.

Noticing
|h|>\sqrt{s}
, for
\delta^{\prime}>0
, it follows from Proposition
3.1
that

\displaystyle S_{2,1}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\int_{|h|\geq\sqrt{s}}s^{-n/2}\frac{1}{t^{1/\alpha}}\eta^{\alpha}_{1}(s/t^{1/\alpha})\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{s}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{s}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}ds

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\infty}s^{-n/2}\frac{1}{t^{1/\alpha}}\eta^{\alpha}_{1}(s/t^{1/\alpha})\Big(\frac{|h|}{\sqrt{s}}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{s}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{s}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}ds

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\int^{\infty}_{0}(t^{1/\alpha}\tau)^{-n/2}\eta^{\alpha}_{1}(\tau)\Big(\frac{|h|}{\sqrt{\tau}t^{1/2\alpha}}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{\tau}t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{\tau}t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}d\tau

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}t^{-n/2\alpha}\Big(\frac{|h|}{t^{1/2\alpha}}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}.

For
S_{2,2}
, similarly, we use Proposition
3.1
again to deduce that

\displaystyle S_{2,2}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\int_{|h|\geq\sqrt{s}}s^{-n/2}\frac{1}{t^{1/\alpha}}\eta^{\alpha}_{1}(s/t^{1/\alpha})\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{s}}{\rho(x+h)}\Big)^{-2N}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{s}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-2N}ds

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\infty}s^{-n/2}\frac{1}{t^{1/\alpha}}\eta^{\alpha}_{1}(s/t^{1/\alpha})\Big(\frac{|h|}{\sqrt{s}}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{s}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-2N}ds

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\int^{\infty}_{0}(t^{1/\alpha}\tau)^{-n/2}\eta^{\alpha}_{1}(\tau)\Big(\frac{|h|}{\sqrt{\tau}t^{1/2\alpha}}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{\tau}t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-2N}d\tau

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-2N}t^{-n/2\alpha}\Big(\frac{|h|}{t^{1/2\alpha}}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}},

which, together with the arbitrariness of
N
, indicates that

S_{2,2}\lesssim\Big(1+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-2N}t^{-n/2\alpha}\Big(\frac{|h|}{t^{1/2\alpha}}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}.

Because
|x-y|/2<t^{1/2\alpha}
, by Lemma
2.3
, it holds

m(y,V)\geq c\frac{m(x,V)}{(1+|x-y|m(x,V))^{k_{0}/(1+k_{0})}}\gtrsim\frac{m(x,V)}{(1+t^{1/2\alpha}m(x,V))^{k_{0}/(1+k_{0})}},

which gives

\displaystyle\Big(1+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle{\Big(1+{t^{1/2\alpha}}{m(y,V)}\Big)^{-N}}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\frac{(1+t^{1/2\alpha}m(x,V))^{k_{0}N/(1+k_{0})}}{\Big((1+t^{1/2\alpha}m(x,V))^{k_{0}/(1+k_{0})}+t^{1/2\alpha}{{m(x,V)}}\Big)^{N}}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\Big(1+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N^{\prime}}.

The estimates for
S_{1}
and
S_{2}
, together with
|x-y|/2<t^{1/2\alpha}
, imply that

\displaystyle\Big|K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x+h,y)-K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)\Big|

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle t^{-n/2\alpha}\Big(\frac{|h|}{t^{1/2\alpha}}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}\Big(1+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(1+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\frac{(|h|/t^{1/2\alpha})^{\delta}t}{(t^{1/2\alpha}+|x-y|)^{n+2\alpha}}\Big(1+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}.

∎

For
m\in\mathbb{Z}^{+}
and
t>0
, define

\widetilde{D}_{\alpha,t}^{L,m}(\cdot,\cdot)=t^{m}\partial^{m}_{t}K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(\cdot,\cdot).

We can obtain the following estimates about the kernel
\widetilde{D}_{\alpha,t}^{L,m}(\cdot,\cdot)
.

Proposition 3.3
.

Let
\alpha\in(0,1)
,
m\in\mathbb{Z}_{+}
and
\delta=\min\{2\alpha,\delta_{0}\}
, where
\delta_{0}
appears in Proposition
2.8
.

(i)
For any
N>0
, there exists a constant
C_{N}>0
such that

|\widetilde{D}_{\alpha,t}^{L,m}(x,y)|\leq\frac{C_{N}t^{m}}{(t^{1/2\alpha}+|x-y|)^{n+2\alpha m}}\Big(1+\frac{{t^{1/2\alpha}}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{{t^{1/2\alpha}}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}.

(ii)
Let
0<\delta^{\prime}\leq\delta
. For any
N>0
, there exists a constant
C_{N}>0
such that for all
|h|\leq t^{1/2\alpha}
,

|\widetilde{D}_{\alpha,t}^{L,m}(x+h,y)-\widetilde{D}_{\alpha,t}^{L,m}(x,y)|\leq C_{N}\Big(\frac{|h|}{{t^{1/2\alpha}}}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}\frac{t^{m}}{(t^{1/2\alpha}+|x-y|)^{n+2\alpha m}}\Big(1+\frac{{t^{1/2\alpha}}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{{t^{1/2\alpha}}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}.

(iii)
Let
0<\delta^{\prime}\leq\delta
. For any
N>0
, there exists a constant
C_{N}>0
such that

\Big|\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\widetilde{D}_{\alpha,t}^{L,m}(x,y)dy\Big|\leq C_{N}\frac{({t^{1/2\alpha}}/\rho(x))^{\delta^{\prime}}}{(1+{t^{1/2\alpha}}/\rho(x))^{N}}.

Proof.

For (i), since
\eta^{\alpha}_{t}(s)=\frac{1}{t^{1/\alpha}}\eta_{1}^{\alpha}(s/t^{1/\alpha})
,

\displaystyle K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int^{\infty}_{0}\frac{1}{t^{1/\alpha}}\eta_{1}^{\alpha}(s/t^{1/\alpha})K^{L}_{s}(x,y)ds=\int^{\infty}_{0}\eta^{\alpha}_{1}(\tau)K^{L}_{t^{1/\alpha}\tau}(x,y)d\tau.

Hence

\displaystyle\Big|\frac{\partial^{m}}{\partial t^{m}}K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)\Big|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\Big|\frac{\partial^{m}}{\partial t^{m}}\Big(\int^{\infty}_{0}\eta^{\alpha}_{1}(\tau)K^{L}_{t^{1/\alpha}\tau}(x,y)d\tau\Big)\Big|=\Big|\int^{\infty}_{0}\eta^{\alpha}_{1}(\tau)\frac{\partial^{m}}{\partial t^{m}}K^{L}_{t^{1/\alpha}\tau}(x,y)d\tau\Big|.

By (i) of Proposition
2.10
and the higher-order derivative formula of the composite function, we can get

\displaystyle\Big|\frac{\partial^{m}}{\partial t^{m}}K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)\Big|

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\sum^{m}_{i=1}\Big|\int^{\infty}_{0}\eta^{\alpha}_{1}(\tau)t^{i/\alpha-m}\tau^{i}\frac{\partial^{i}}{\partial s^{i}}K^{L}_{s}(x,y)\Big|_{s=t^{1/\alpha}\tau}d\tau\Big|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{N}t^{-m}\int^{\infty}_{0}\eta^{\alpha}_{1}(\tau)(t^{1/\alpha}\tau)^{-n/2}e^{-c|x-y|^{2}/t^{1/\alpha}\tau}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{\tau}t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{\tau}t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}d\tau.

Notice that
\eta_{1}^{\alpha}(\tau)\leq{C}/{\tau^{1+\alpha}}
. By changing of variables, we obtain

\displaystyle\Big|\frac{\partial^{m}}{\partial t^{m}}K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)\Big|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{C_{N}}{t^{m+n/2\alpha}}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}\int^{\infty}_{0}\tau^{-1-\alpha-n/2-N}e^{-c|x-y|^{2}/t^{1/\alpha}\tau}d\tau

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{C_{N}t^{1+N/\alpha-m}}{|x-y|^{2\alpha+n+2M}}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}.

On the other hand,

\displaystyle\Big|\frac{\partial^{m}}{\partial t^{m}}K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)\Big|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\Big|\int^{\infty}_{0}\eta^{\alpha}_{1}(\tau)\frac{\partial^{m}}{\partial t^{m}}K^{L}_{t^{1/\alpha}\tau}(x,y)d\tau\Big|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{N}t^{-m}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}\int^{\infty}_{0}\eta^{\alpha}_{1}(\tau)(\tau t^{1/\alpha})^{-n/2}\tau^{-N}d\tau

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{C_{N}}{t^{m+n/2\alpha}}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}.

Finally, we have proved that for arbitrary
N>0
,

\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{N}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{N}\Big|\frac{\partial^{m}}{\partial t^{m}}K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)\Big|\leq C_{N}\min\Big\{\frac{t^{1+N/\alpha-m}}{|x-y|^{2\alpha+n+2N}},\ \frac{1}{t^{m+n/2\alpha}}\Big\},

which gives

|\widetilde{D}_{\alpha,t}^{L,m}(x,y)|\leq\frac{C_{N}t^{m}}{(t^{1/2\alpha}+|x-y|)^{n+2\alpha m}}\Big(1+\frac{{t^{1/2\alpha}}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{{t^{1/2\alpha}}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}.

For (ii), via the subordinative formula (
2.5
), we can complete the proof by using (ii) of Proposition
2.10
. We omit the details.

For (iii), it is easy to see that
e^{-sL^{\alpha}}(f)(x)=\int^{\infty}_{0}\eta^{\alpha}_{s}(\tau)e^{-\tau L}(f)(x)d\tau.
Hence

\displaystyle\frac{\partial^{m}}{\partial t^{m}}K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)=\frac{\partial^{m}}{\partial t^{m}}\Big(\int^{\infty}_{0}\eta^{\alpha}_{1}(\tau)K^{L}_{\tau t^{1/\alpha}}(x,y)d\tau\Big)=C_{m}\sum^{m}_{i=1}\int^{\infty}_{0}\eta^{\alpha}_{1}(\tau)Q^{L}_{\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}\tau},i}(x,y)d\tau.

It follows from (iii) of Proposition
2.10
that

\displaystyle\Big|\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\widetilde{D}_{\alpha,t}^{L,m}(x,y)dy\Big|

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\int^{\infty}_{0}\eta^{\alpha}_{1}(\tau)\Big|\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}Q^{L}_{\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}\tau},i}(x,y)dy\Big|d\tau

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{N}\int^{\infty}_{0}\eta^{\alpha}_{1}(\tau)\frac{(\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}\tau}/\rho(x))^{\delta^{\prime}}}{(1+\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}\tau}/\rho(x))^{N}}d\tau.

If
t^{1/2\alpha}>\rho(x)
, then

\displaystyle\Big|\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\widetilde{D}_{\alpha,t}^{L,m}(x,y)dy\Big|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{N}\rho(x)^{N-\delta^{\prime}}t^{(\delta^{\prime}-N)/2\alpha}\int^{\infty}_{0}\eta_{1}^{\alpha}(\tau)\tau^{(\delta^{\prime}-N)/2}d\tau

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{C_{N}({t^{1/2\alpha}}/\rho(x))^{\delta^{\prime}}}{(1+{t^{1/2\alpha}}/\rho(x))^{N}}.

If
t^{1/2\alpha}\leq\rho(x)
, then

\displaystyle\Big|\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\widetilde{D}_{\alpha,t}^{L,m}(x,y)dy\Big|\leq C_{N}\int^{\infty}_{0}\eta_{1}^{\alpha}(\tau)(\sqrt{t^{1/\alpha}\tau}/\rho(x))^{\delta^{\prime}}d\tau\leq\frac{C_{N}({t^{1/2\alpha}}/\rho(x))^{\delta^{\prime}}}{(1+{t^{1/2\alpha}}/\rho(x))^{N}}.

∎

3.2.
Estimation on the spatial gradient

In this section, we investigate the spatial gradient of
K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(\cdot,\cdot)
,
\alpha>0
. For the special case
\alpha=1/2
, i.e., the Poisson kernel, the regularity estimates have been obtained in
[
5
, Lemma 3.9]

Lemma 3.4
.

Suppose that
V\in B_{q}
for some
q>n
. For every
N>0
, there exist constants
C_{N}>0
and
c>0
such that
for all
x,y\in\mathbb{R}^{n}
and
t>0
, the kernels
K_{t}^{L}(\cdot,\cdot)
satisfy the following estimates:

|\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{t}(x,y)|\leq\left\{\begin{aligned} \frac{C_{N}}{t^{(n+1)/2}}e^{-c|x-y|^{2}/t}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N},\ \sqrt{t}\leq|x-y|;\\
\frac{C_{N}}{|x-y|t^{n/2}}e^{-c|x-y|^{2}/t}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N},\ \sqrt{t}>|x-y|.\end{aligned}\right.

Proof.

Let
\Gamma_{0}(\cdot,\cdot)
be the fundamental solution of
-\Delta
in
\mathbb{R}^{n}
, i.e.,

\Gamma_{0}(x,y)=-\frac{1}{n(n-2)\omega(n)}\frac{1}{|x-y|^{n-2}},\ n\geq 3,

where
\omega(n)
denotes the area of the unit sphere in
\mathbb{R}^{n}
. Fix
t>0
and
x_{0},y_{0}\in\mathbb{R}^{n}
. Assume that
u(\cdot,\cdot)
is a solution to the equation

\partial_{t}u+Lu=\partial_{t}u+(-\Delta)u+Vu=0.

Let
\eta\in C^{\infty}_{0}(B(x_{0},2R))
with some
R>0
such that

\eta=1\text{ on }B(x_{0},3R/2)
,

|\nabla\eta|\leq C/R
and
|\nabla^{2}\eta|\leq C/R^{2}.

Noticing that
\partial_{t}u+Lu=0
, we can obtain

\displaystyle-\Delta(u\eta)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\sum^{n}_{i=1}\Big(\frac{\partial^{2}u}{\partial x_{i}^{2}}\cdot\eta+2\frac{\partial u}{\partial x_{i}}\frac{\partial\eta}{\partial x_{i}}+u\cdot\frac{\partial^{2}\eta}{\partial x_{i}^{2}}\Big)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\Delta u\cdot\eta-2\nabla u\cdot\nabla\eta-u\cdot\Delta\eta

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-(\partial_{t}u)\eta-Vu\eta-2\nabla u\nabla\eta-u\nabla\eta,

which, together with integration by parts, gives

\displaystyle-\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\Gamma_{0}(x,y)\nabla u(y,t)\cdot\nabla\eta(y)dy=-\sum^{n}_{i=1}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\Gamma_{0}(x,y)\frac{\partial u(y,t)}{\partial y_{i}}\frac{\partial\eta(y)}{\partial y_{i}}dy

\displaystyle\quad=\sum^{n}_{i=1}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\Big(\frac{\partial}{\partial y_{i}}\Gamma_{0}(x,y)\Big)\frac{\partial\eta(y)}{\partial x_{i}}u(y,t)dy+\sum^{n}_{i=1}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\Gamma_{0}(x,y)\Big(\frac{\partial^{2}\eta(y)}{\partial x_{i}^{2}}\Big)u(y,t)dy

\displaystyle\quad=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\nabla_{y}\Gamma_{0}(x,y)\cdot\nabla\eta(y)u(y,t)dy+\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\Gamma_{0}(x,y)\cdot\Delta\eta(y)u(y,t)dy.

Then we can get

\displaystyle u(x,t)\eta(x)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\Gamma_{0}(x,y)\Big\{-V(y)u(y,t)\eta(y)-\eta(y)\partial_{t}u(y,t)-2\nabla u(y,t)\cdot\nabla\eta(y)-u(y,t)\Delta\eta(y)\Big\}dy

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\Gamma_{0}(x,y)\Big\{-V(y)u(y,t)\eta(y)-\eta(y)\partial_{t}u(y,t)+\Delta\eta(y)\cdot u(y,t)\Big\}dy

\displaystyle+2\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\nabla_{y}\Gamma_{0}(x,y)\nabla\eta(y)u(y,t)dy.

Notice that it follows from Lemma
2.5
that

\int_{B(x_{0},2R)}\frac{V(y)}{|x-y|^{n-1}}dy\leq\frac{C}{R^{n-1}}\int_{B(x_{0},2R)}V(y)dy\leq\frac{C}{R}\Big(1+\frac{R}{\rho(x_{0})}\Big)^{m_{0}},\ m_{0}>1.

Thus for
x\in B(x_{0},R)
, it holds

\displaystyle|\nabla_{x}u(x,t)|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle|\nabla_{x}(u(x,t)\eta(x))|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\int_{B(x_{0},2R)}\frac{V(y)|u(y,t)||\eta(y)|}{|x-y|^{n-1}}dy+\int_{B(x_{0},2R)}\frac{|\partial_{t}u(y,t)||\eta(y)|}{|x-y|^{n-1}}dy+\frac{C}{R^{n+1}}\int_{B(x_{0},2R)}|u(y,t)|dy

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{C}{R}\sup_{B(x_{0},2R)}|u(y,t)|\Big\{\Big(1+\frac{R}{\rho(x_{0})}\Big)^{m_{0}}+1\Big\}+CR\sup_{B(x_{0},R)}|\partial_{t}u(y,t)|.

Take
u(x,t)=K^{L}_{t}(x,y_{0})
and
R<\min\{|x_{0}-y_{0}|/8,\ \rho(x_{0})\}
. We obtain that

|\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{t}(x_{0},y_{0})|\leq\frac{C}{R}\sup_{B(x_{0},2R)}|K^{L}_{t}(x,y_{0})|\Big\{\Big(1+\frac{R}{\rho(x_{0})}\Big)^{m_{0}}+1\Big\}+R\sup_{B(x_{0},2R)}|\partial_{t}K_{t}^{L}(x,y_{0})|.

If
x\in B(x_{0},2R)
, then
|x-y_{0}|\sim|x_{0}-y_{0}|
. Also
\rho(x)\sim\rho(x_{0})
for
|x-x_{0}|<2R<2\rho(x_{0})
.
It follows from Propositions
2.7
and
2.10
that for any
N>0
there exists a constant
C_{N}
such that

(3.5)

\left\{\begin{aligned} &\sup_{x\in B(x_{0},2R)}|K^{L}_{t}(x,y_{0})|\leq\frac{C_{N}}{t^{n/2}}e^{-c|x_{0}-y_{0}|^{2}/t}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x_{0})}+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(y_{0})}\Big)^{-N};\\
&\sup_{x\in B(x_{0},2R)}|t\partial_{t}K^{L}_{t}(x,y_{0})|\leq\frac{C_{N}}{t^{n/2}}e^{-c|x_{0}-y_{0}|^{2}/t}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x_{0})}+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(y_{0})}\Big)^{-N}.\end{aligned}\right.

Finally, it can be deduced from (
3.5
) that

\displaystyle|\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{t}(x_{0},y_{0})|

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\frac{C_{N}}{R}t^{-n/2}e^{-c|x_{0}-y_{0}|^{2}/t}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x_{0})}+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(y_{0})}\Big)^{-N}\Big\{1+\frac{R^{2}}{t}\Big\}.

The rest of the proof is divided into three cases.

Case 1:
R>\sqrt{t}
. For this case,
\sqrt{t}<R<\min\{|x_{0}-y_{0}|/8,\ \rho(x_{0})\}
.
We split

|\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{t}(x_{0},y_{0})|\leq C_{N}(M_{1}+M_{2}),

where

\left\{\begin{aligned} &M_{1}:=\frac{\sqrt{t}}{R}\frac{1}{t^{(n+1)/2}}e^{-c|x_{0}-y_{0}|^{2}/t}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x_{0})}+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(y_{0})}\Big)^{-N};\\
&M_{2}:=\frac{\sqrt{t}}{R}\frac{1}{t^{(n+1)/2}}e^{-c|x_{0}-y_{0}|^{2}/t}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x_{0})}+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(y_{0})}\Big)^{-N}\frac{R^{2}}{t}.\end{aligned}\right.

It is obvious that

M_{1}\lesssim\frac{1}{t^{(n+1)/2}}e^{-c|x_{0}-y_{0}|^{2}/t}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x_{0})}+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(y_{0})}\Big)^{-N}.

Similarly, for the term
M_{3}
, we can also get

\displaystyle M_{2}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\frac{1}{t^{(n+1)/2}}e^{-c|x_{0}-y_{0}|^{2}/t}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x_{0})}+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(y_{0})}\Big)^{-N}\frac{R^{2}}{t}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\frac{1}{t^{(n+1)/2}}e^{-c|x_{0}-y_{0}|^{2}/t}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x_{0})}+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(y_{0})}\Big)^{-N}\frac{|x_{0}-y_{0}|^{2}}{t}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\frac{1}{t^{(n+1)/2}}e^{-c|x_{0}-y_{0}|^{2}/t}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x_{0})}+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(y_{0})}\Big)^{-N}.

Case 2:
0<R\leq\sqrt{t}<\min\{|x_{0}-y_{0}|/8,\ \rho(x_{0})\}
. We write

|\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{t}(x_{0},y_{0})|\leq\frac{C_{N}}{t^{(n+1)/2}}e^{-c|x_{0}-y_{0}|^{2}/t}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x_{0})}+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(y_{0})}\Big)^{-N}\Big\{\frac{\sqrt{t}}{R}+\frac{R}{\sqrt{t}}\Big\}.

Because
R<\sqrt{t}<\min\{|x_{0}-y_{0}|/8,\ \rho(x_{0})\}
, taking the infimum for
R
yields

|\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{t}(x_{0},y_{0})|\leq\frac{C_{N}}{t^{(n+1)/2}}e^{-c|x_{0}-y_{0}|^{2}/t}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x_{0})}+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(y_{0})}\Big)^{-N}.

Case 3:
0<R<\min\{|x_{0}-y_{0}|/8,\ \rho(x_{0})\}<\sqrt{t}
.
Similarly, we can see that

\displaystyle|\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{t}(x_{0},y_{0})|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x_{0})}+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(y_{0})}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{\sqrt{t}}{R}+\frac{R}{\sqrt{t}}\Big).

Since
0<R<\min\{|x_{0}-y_{0}|/8,\ \rho(x_{0})\}<\sqrt{t}
, the function
\sqrt{t}/R+R/\sqrt{t}
is decreasing and with the infimum at

R=\min\{|x_{0}-y_{0}|/8,\ \rho(x_{0})\}
. Then

(3.6)

\displaystyle|\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{t}(x_{0},y_{0})|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{C_{N}}{t^{(n+1)/2}}e^{-c|x_{0}-y_{0}|^{2}/t}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x_{0})}+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(y_{0})}\Big)^{-N}

\displaystyle\times\Bigg\{\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\min\{|x_{0}-y_{0}|/8,\ \rho(x_{0})\}}+\frac{\min\{|x_{0}-y_{0}|/8,\ \rho(x_{0})\}}{\sqrt{t}}\Bigg\}.

Case 3.1:
\rho(x_{0})\leq|x_{0}-y_{0}|/8
.
Since
N
is arbitrary, we can deduce from (
3.6
) that

\displaystyle|\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{t}(x_{0},y_{0})|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{C_{N}}{t^{(n+1)/2}}e^{-c|x_{0}-y_{0}|^{2}/t}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x_{0})}+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(y_{0})}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x_{0})}+\frac{\rho(x_{0})}{\sqrt{t}}\Big)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{C_{N}}{t^{(n+1)/2}}e^{-c|x_{0}-y_{0}|^{2}/t}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x_{0})}+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(y_{0})}\Big)^{-N}.

Case 3.2:
\rho(x_{0})>|x_{0}-y_{0}|/8
.
For this case, by (
3.6
) again, it holds

\displaystyle|\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{t}(x_{0},y_{0})|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{C_{N}}{t^{(n+1)/2}}e^{-c|x_{0}-y_{0}|^{2}/t}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x_{0})}+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(y_{0})}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{\sqrt{t}}{|x_{0}-y_{0}|}+\frac{|x_{0}-y_{0}|}{\sqrt{t}}\Big)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{C_{N}}{t^{(n+1)/2}}e^{-c|x_{0}-y_{0}|^{2}/t}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x_{0})}+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(y_{0})}\Big)^{-N}

\displaystyle+\frac{C_{N}}{|x_{0}-y_{0}|t^{n/2}}e^{-c|x_{0}-y_{0}|^{2}/t}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x_{0})}+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(y_{0})}\Big)^{-N}.

Finally, we obtain the following estimates:

\displaystyle|\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{t}(x_{0},y_{0})|

\displaystyle\leq\left\{\begin{aligned} &\frac{C_{N}}{t^{(n+1)/2}}e^{-c|x_{0}-y_{0}|^{2}/t}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x_{0})}+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(y_{0})}\Big)^{-N},\ \sqrt{t}\leq\min\Big\{|x_{0}-y_{0}|/8,\ \rho(x_{0})\Big\};\\
&\frac{C_{N}}{t^{n/2}}e^{-c|x_{0}-y_{0}|^{2}/t}(\frac{1}{\sqrt{t}}+\frac{1}{|x_{0}-y_{0}|})\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x_{0})}+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(y_{0})}\Big)^{-N},\ \sqrt{t}>\min\Big\{|x_{0}-y_{0}|/8,\ \rho(x_{0})\Big\}.\end{aligned}\right.

Then if
\sqrt{t}\geq|x_{0}-y_{0}|/8
,

|\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{t}(x_{0},y_{0})|\leq\frac{C_{N}}{|x_{0}-y_{0}|t^{n/2}}e^{-c|x_{0}-y_{0}|^{2}/t}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x_{0})}+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(y_{0})}\Big)^{-N}.

This proves Lemma
3.4
.
∎

Lemma 3.5
.

Suppose that
V\in B_{q}
for some
q>n
. For every
N>0
, there exists a constant
C_{N}>0
such that
for all
x,y\in\mathbb{R}^{n}
and
t>0
, the semigroup kernels

satisfy the following estimate:

|\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{t}(x,y)|\leq\frac{C_{N}}{t^{(n+1)/2}}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}.

Proof.

Assume that
u(\cdot,\cdot)

equation

\partial_{t}u+Lu=\partial_{t}u+(-\Delta)u+Vu=0.

Similar to Lemma
3.4
, we can prove that

|\nabla_{x}u(x,t)|\leq\frac{C}{R}\sup_{B(x_{0},2R)}|u(y,t)|\Big\{\Big(1+\frac{R}{\rho(x_{0})}\Big)^{m_{0}}+1\Big\}+CR\sup_{B(x_{0},R)}|\partial_{t}u(y,t)|.

Take
u(x,t)=K^{L}_{t}(x,y_{0})
for fixed
y_{0}
, and let
R\in(0,\min\{\rho(x_{0}),\sqrt{t}\})
. It can be deduced from Propositions
2.7
and
2.10
that

\sup\limits_{x\in B(x_{0},2R)}\Big\{|K^{L}_{t}(x,y_{0})|+|t\partial_{t}K^{L}_{t}(x,y_{0})|\Big\}\leq\frac{C_{N}}{t^{n/2}}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x_{0})}+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(y_{0})}\Big)^{-N}.

This, together with
R<\rho(x_{0})
, implies that

(3.7)

\displaystyle|\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{t}(x_{0},y_{0})|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{C_{N}}{R}\frac{1}{t^{n/2}}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x_{0})}+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(y_{0})}\Big)^{-N}\Big\{1+\frac{R^{2}}{t}\Big\}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{C_{N}}{t^{(n+1)/2}}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x_{0})}+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(y_{0})}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{\sqrt{t}}{R}+\frac{R}{\sqrt{t}}\Big).

If
\sqrt{t}\leq\rho(x_{0})
, taking the infimum for
R
on both sides of (
3.7
) reaches

\displaystyle|\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{t}(x_{0},y_{0})|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{C_{N}}{t^{(n+1)/2}}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x_{0})}+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(y_{0})}\Big)^{-N}.

If
\sqrt{t}>\rho(x_{0})
, note that the function
h(t):=R/\sqrt{t}+\sqrt{t}/R
is decreasing on
R\in(0,\rho(x_{0}))
. Taking the infimum again, we get

\displaystyle|\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{t}(x_{0},y_{0})|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{C_{N}}{t^{(n+1)/2}}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x_{0})}+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(y_{0})}\Big)^{-N}\Big\{\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x_{0})}+\frac{\rho(x_{0})}{\sqrt{t}}\Big\}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{C_{N}}{t^{(n+1)/2}}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x_{0})}+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(y_{0})}\Big)^{-N}.

This completes the proof of Lemma
3.5
.
∎

Now we give the gradient estimate of
K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(\cdot,\cdot)
.

Proposition 3.6
.

Suppose
\alpha>0
and
V\in B_{q}
for some
q>n
. For every
N>0
, there exists a constant
C_{N}>0
such that for all
x,y\in\mathbb{R}^{n}
and
t>0
,

|t^{1/2\alpha}\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)|\leq\frac{C_{N}t}{(t^{1/2\alpha}+|x-y|)^{n+2\alpha}}\Big(1+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}.

Proof.

The subordinate formula gives

\displaystyle\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int^{\infty}_{0}\eta^{\alpha}_{t}(s)\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{s}(x,y)ds,

which, together with Lemma
3.4
, implies that

\big|\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)\big|\leq C_{N}(L_{1}+L_{2}),

where

\left\{\begin{aligned} &L_{1}:=\int^{|x-y|^{2}}_{0}\eta^{\alpha}_{t}(s)\frac{1}{s^{(n+1)/2}}e^{-c|x-y|^{2}/s}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{s}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{\sqrt{s}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}ds;\\
&L_{2}:=\int^{\infty}_{|x-y|^{2}}\eta^{\alpha}_{t}(s)\frac{1}{s^{n/2}|x-y|}e^{-c|x-y|^{2}/s}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{s}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{\sqrt{s}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}ds.\end{aligned}\right.

For
L_{1}
, letting
s=t^{1/\alpha}u
, we can get

\displaystyle L_{1}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\int^{\infty}_{0}\frac{t}{(t^{1/\alpha}u)^{1+\alpha}}(t^{1/\alpha}u)^{-(n+1)/2}e^{-\frac{c|x-y|^{2}}{t^{1/\alpha}u}}\Big(1+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}\sqrt{u}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(1+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}\sqrt{u}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}t^{1/\alpha}du

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}t^{-(n+1)/2\alpha}\int^{\infty}_{0}u^{-(n+1)/2-(1+\alpha+N)}e^{-\frac{c|x-y|^{2}}{t^{1/\alpha}u}}du

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}\frac{t^{1+N/\alpha}}{|x-y|^{2\alpha+2N+n+1}}.

Similarly, for the term
L_{2}
, a change of variables yields

\displaystyle L_{2}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{|x-y|}\int^{\infty}_{0}\frac{t}{(t^{1/\alpha}u)^{1+\alpha}}(t^{1/\alpha}u)^{-n/2}e^{-\frac{c|x-y|^{2}}{t^{1/\alpha}u}}\Big(1+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}\sqrt{u}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(1+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}\sqrt{u}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}t^{1/\alpha}du

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{t^{n/2\alpha}|x-y|}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}\int^{\infty}_{0}u^{-n/2-(1+\alpha+N)}e^{-\frac{c|x-y|^{2}}{t^{1/\alpha}u}}du

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{|x-y|t^{n/2\alpha}}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}\int^{\infty}_{0}\Big(\frac{t^{1/\alpha}r^{2}}{|x-y|^{2}}\Big)^{1+\alpha+N+n/2}e^{-cr^{2}}\frac{|x-y|^{2}}{t^{1/\alpha}r^{3}}dr

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}\frac{t^{1+N/\alpha}}{|x-y|^{2\alpha+2N+n+1}}.

The estimates for
L_{1}
and
L_{2}
indicate that

\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{N}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{N}|\nabla K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)|\leq\frac{C_{N}t^{1+N/\alpha}}{|x-y|^{2\alpha+2N+n+1}}.

On the other hand, by Lemma
3.5
and changing variable
\tau=s/t^{1/\alpha}
, we obtain

\displaystyle|\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{N}\int^{\infty}_{0}s^{-(n+1)/2}\frac{1}{t^{1/\alpha}}\eta^{\alpha}_{1}(\frac{s}{t^{1/\alpha}})\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{s}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{s}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}ds

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{N}\int^{\infty}_{0}(t^{1/\alpha}\tau)^{-(n+1)/2}\eta^{\alpha}_{1}(\tau)\Big(1+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}\sqrt{\tau}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(1+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}\sqrt{\tau}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}d\tau

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{C_{N}}{t^{(n+1)/2\alpha}}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}.

Finally, we obtain

\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{N}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{N}|\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)|\leq C_{N}\min\Big\{t^{-(n+1)/2\alpha},\ \frac{t^{1+N/\alpha}}{|x-y|^{n+1+2N+2\alpha}}\Big\}.

The arbitrariness of
N
indicates that

|\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)|\leq\frac{C_{N}}{t^{1/2\alpha}}\frac{t}{(t^{1/2\alpha}+|x-y|)^{n+2\alpha}}\Big(1+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(1+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}.

∎

Below we estimate the Lipschitz continuity of
|\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{t}(\cdot,\cdot)|
.

Lemma 3.7
.

Suppose that
\alpha>0
and
V\in B_{q}
for some
q>n
. Let
\delta^{\prime}=1-n/q
. For every
N>0
, there exist constants
C_{N}>0
and
c>0
such that for all
x,y\in\mathbb{R}^{n}
,

t>0
and
|h|<|x-y|/4
,

\displaystyle|\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{t}(x+h,y)-\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{t}(x,y)|

\displaystyle\quad\leq\left\{\begin{aligned} &\frac{C_{N}}{t^{(n+1)/2}}\Big(\frac{|h|}{\sqrt{t}}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}e^{-c|x-y|^{2}/t}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N},\ \sqrt{t}\leq|x-y|;\\
&\frac{C_{N}}{t^{n/2}|x-y|}\Big(\frac{|h|}{|x-y|}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}e^{-c|x-y|^{2}/t}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N},\ \sqrt{t}\geq|x-y|.\end{aligned}\right.

Proof.

The proof is similar to that of Lemma
3.4
. Let
\Gamma_{0}(\cdot,\cdot)
be the fundamental solution of
-\Delta
in
\mathbb{R}^{n}
.
Assume that
\partial_{t}u+(-\Delta)u+Vu=0
. Let

\eta\in C^{\infty}_{0}(B(x_{0},2R))
such that
\eta=1
on
B(x_{0},3R/2)
,
|\nabla\eta|\leq C/R
and
|\nabla^{2}\eta|\leq C/R^{2}
. It is easy to see that

(-\Delta)(u\eta)=(-\Delta u)\eta-2\nabla u\cdot\nabla\eta+u\cdot(-\Delta\eta).

Similar to the proof of Lemma
3.4
, an integration by parts implies that

\displaystyle-\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\Gamma_{0}(x,y)\nabla u(y,t)\cdot\nabla\eta(y)dy=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\nabla_{y}\Gamma_{0}(x,y)\nabla\eta(y)u(y,t)dy+\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\Gamma_{0}(x,y)\Delta\eta(y)u(y,t)dy,

which yields

\displaystyle u(x,t)\eta(x)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\Gamma_{0}(x,y)\Big\{(-\partial_{t}u(y,t))\eta(y)-V(y)u(y,t)\eta(y)+u(y,t)\Delta\eta(y)\Big\}dy

\displaystyle+2\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\nabla_{y}\Gamma_{0}(x,y)u(y,t)\cdot\nabla\eta(y)dy.

Then for
x\in B(x_{0},R)
,
u(x,t)=u(x,t)\eta(x)
and

\displaystyle\nabla_{x}^{2}u(x,t)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\nabla^{2}_{x}\Big\{\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\Gamma_{0}(x,y)\Big\{(-\partial_{t}u(y,t))\eta(y)-V(y)u(y,t)\eta(y)+u(y,t)\Delta\eta(y)\Big\}dy

\displaystyle+2\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\nabla_{y}\Gamma_{0}(x,y)u(y,t)\cdot\nabla\eta(y)dy\Big\},

which gives
\|\nabla^{2}_{x}u(\cdot,t)\|_{q}\leq\sum\limits^{4}_{i=1}\|S_{i}(\cdot,t)\|_{q}
, where

\left\{\begin{aligned} S_{1}(x,t)&:=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}|\nabla^{2}_{x}\Gamma_{0}(x,y)|\cdot|\eta(y)|\cdot|\partial_{t}u(y,t)|dy;\\
S_{2}(x,t)&:=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}|\nabla^{2}_{x}\Gamma_{0}(x,y)|\cdot|\Delta\eta(y)|\cdot|u(y,t)|dy;\\
S_{3}(x,t)&:=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}|\nabla^{2}_{x}\nabla_{y}\Gamma_{0}(x,y)|\cdot|\nabla\eta(y)|\cdot|u(y,t)|dy;\\
S_{4}(x,t)&:=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}|\nabla^{2}_{x}\Gamma_{0}(x,y)|\cdot|\eta(y)|V(y)|\cdot|u(y,t)|dy.\end{aligned}\right.

Now we estimate the terms
\|S_{i}(\cdot,t)\|_{q},i=1,2,3,4
, separately. For the term
\|S_{1}(\cdot,t)\|_{q}
, because
\nabla^{2}_{x}\Gamma_{0}(\cdot,\cdot)
is a Calderón-Zygmund kernel,
we have

\displaystyle\|S_{1}(\cdot,t)\|_{q}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\Big\|\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}|\eta(y)||\partial_{t}u(y,t)||\nabla_{x}^{2}\Gamma_{0}(\cdot,y)|dy\Big\|_{q}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\Big\{\sup_{y\in B(x_{0},2R)}|\partial_{t}u(y,t)|\Big\}\Big\|\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}|\eta(y)||\nabla_{x}^{2}\Gamma_{0}(\cdot,y)|dy\Big\|_{q}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\|\eta\|_{q}\Big\{\sup_{y\in B(x_{0},2R)}|\partial_{t}u(y,t)|\Big\}.

The estimate of
S_{2}
is similar to that of
S_{1}
. Noting that
\eta=1
on
B(x_{0},3R/2)
, we can get

\displaystyle S_{2}(x,t)\lesssim\int_{B(x_{0},2R)\setminus B(x_{0},3R/2)}\frac{|u(y,t)||\Delta\eta(y)|}{|x-y|^{n}}dy\lesssim\Big\{\sup\limits_{y\in B(x_{0},2R)}|u(y,t)|\Big\}\int_{B(x_{0},2R)\setminus B(x_{0},3R/2)}\frac{|\Delta\eta(y)|}{|x-y|^{n}}dy.

For
3R/2<|y-x_{0}|<2R
and
x\in B(x_{0},R)
, a direct computation gives
|x-y|\sim R
and

\displaystyle S_{2}(x,t)

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\frac{\sup_{y\in B(x_{0},2R)}|u(y,t)|}{R^{n}}\int_{B(x_{0},2R)\setminus B(x_{0},3R/2)}|\Delta\eta(y)|dy

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\frac{\sup_{y\in B(x_{0},2R)}|u(y,t)|}{R^{n+2}}\int_{B(x_{0},2R)}dy\lesssim\frac{\sup_{B(x_{0},2R)}|u(y,t)|}{R^{2}}.

Also

\displaystyle\|S_{2}(\cdot,t)\|_{q}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\Big\{\sup_{y\in B(x_{0},2R)}|u(y,t)|\Big\}\|\Delta\eta\|_{q}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\Big\{\sup_{y\in B(x_{0},2R)}|u(y,t)|\Big\}\Big(\int_{B(x_{0},2R)\setminus B(x_{0},3R/2)}|\Delta\eta(y)|^{q}dy\Big)^{1/q}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle R^{n/q-2}\Big\{\sup_{y\in B(x_{0},2R)}|u(y,t)|\Big\}.

Following the same procedure, we apply the Young inequality to obtain

\displaystyle\|S_{3}(\cdot,t)\|_{q}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\Big\{\sup_{y\in B(x_{0},2R)}|u(y,t)|\Big\}\Big\|\int_{B(x_{0},2R)\setminus B(x_{0},3R/2)}|\nabla^{2}_{x}\nabla_{y}\Gamma_{0}(\cdot,y)|\cdot|\nabla\eta(y)|dy\Big\|_{q}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\Big\{\sup_{B(x_{0},2R)}|u(y,t)|\Big\}\|\nabla\eta\|_{q}\cdot\Big\|\nabla^{2}_{x}\nabla_{y}\Gamma_{0}(x,\cdot)\chi_{B(x_{0},2R)\setminus B(x_{0},3R/2)}\Big\|_{1}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\Big\{\sup_{y\in B(x_{0},2R)}|u(y,t)|\Big\}R^{n/q-1}\Big(\int_{B(x_{0},2R)\setminus B(x_{0},3R/2)}\frac{dy}{|x-y|^{n+1}}\Big)

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle R^{n/q-2}\Big\{\sup_{y\in B(x_{0},2R)}|u(y,t)|\Big\}.

At last, for the term
S_{4}
, by Lemma
2.5
and the condition
V\in B_{q}
,
we can obtain, via the
L^{p}
-boundedness of the operator with the kernel
\nabla^{2}_{x}\Gamma_{0}(\cdot,\cdot)
, that

\displaystyle\|S_{4}(\cdot,t)\|_{q}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\Big\|V(\cdot)|u(\cdot,t)||\eta(\cdot)|\Big\|_{q}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\Big\{\sup_{y\in B(x_{0},2R)}|u(y,t)|\Big\}\Big(\int_{B(x_{0},2R)}V^{q}(y)dy\Big)^{1/q}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\Big\{\sup_{y\in B(x_{0},2R)}|u(y,t)|\Big\}R^{n/q}\Big(\frac{1}{|B(x_{0},2R)|}\int_{B(x_{0},2R)}V^{q}(y)dy\Big)^{1/q}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\Big\{\sup_{y\in B(x_{0},2R)}|u(y,t)|\Big\}R^{n/q-1}\Big(\frac{1}{R^{n-1}}\int_{B(x_{0},2R)}V(y)dy\Big)

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\Big\{\sup_{y\in B(x_{0},2R)}|u(y,t)|\Big\}R^{n/q-2}\Big(1+\frac{R}{\rho(x_{0})}\Big)^{m_{0}}.

The estimates for
\|S_{i}(\cdot,t)\|_{q},i=1,2,3,4
, indicate that

|\nabla^{2}_{x}u(x,t)|\lesssim R^{n/q-2}\Big\{\Big(1+\frac{R}{\rho(x_{0})}\Big)^{m_{0}}+1\Big\}\Big\{\sup_{y\in B(x_{0},2R)}|u(y,t)|\Big\}+R^{n/q}\Big\{\sup_{y\in B(x_{0},2R)}|\partial_{t}u(y,t)|\Big\}.

Let
u(x_{0},t)=K^{L}_{t}(x_{0},y_{0})
. Then

\displaystyle|\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{t}(x_{0}+h,y_{0})-\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{t}(x_{0},y_{0})|

\displaystyle\lesssim|h|^{1-n/q}\Big(\int_{B(x_{0},R)}|\nabla^{2}_{x}K_{t}^{L}(x,y_{0})|^{q}dx\Big)^{1/q}

\displaystyle\lesssim\Big(\frac{|h|}{\sqrt{t}}\Big)^{1-n/q}\Big(\frac{\sqrt{t}}{R}\Big)^{1-n/q}\frac{1}{R}\Big\{\Big(1+\frac{R}{\rho(x_{0})}\Big)^{m_{0}}+1\Big\}\Big\{\sup_{x\in B(x_{0},2R)}|K_{t}^{L}(x,y_{0})|+\frac{R^{2}}{t}\sup_{x\in B(x_{0},2R)}|t\partial_{t}K^{L}_{t}(x,y_{0})|\Big\}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{C_{N}}{R}\Big(\frac{|h|}{\sqrt{t}}\Big)^{1-n/q}\Big(\frac{\sqrt{t}}{R}\Big)^{1-n/q}\Big\{\Big(1+\frac{R}{\rho(x_{0})}\Big)^{m_{0}}+1\Big\}\Big\{\sup_{x\in B(x_{0},2R)}\frac{1}{t^{n/2}}e^{-c|x-y_{0}|^{2}/t}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(y_{0})}\Big)^{-N}\Big\}

\displaystyle\quad+C_{N}\frac{R^{2}}{t}\Big\{\sup_{x\in B(x_{0},2R)}\frac{1}{t^{n/2}}e^{-c|x-y_{0}|^{2}/t}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(y_{0})}\Big)^{-N}\Big\}.

Take
0<R<\min\{\rho(x_{0}),|x_{0}-y_{0}|/8\}
. If
x\in B(x_{0},2R)
, then
|x-x_{0}|<2\rho(x_{0})
, that is,
\rho(x)\sim\rho(x_{0})
.
Also, if
x\in B(x_{0},2R)
,
|x-x_{0}|<2R<|x_{0}-y_{0}|/4
, which means that
|x-y_{0}|\sim|x_{0}-y_{0}|
. We can get

(3.8)

\displaystyle|\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{t}(x_{0}+h,y_{0})-\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{t}(x_{0},y_{0})|

\displaystyle\quad\leq\frac{C_{N}}{t^{(n+1)/2}}\Big(\frac{|h|}{\sqrt{t}}\Big)^{1-n/q}e^{-c|x_{0}-y_{0}|^{2}/t}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x_{0})}\Big)^{-N}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(y_{0})}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{\sqrt{t}}{R}\Big)^{1-n/q}\Big(\frac{\sqrt{t}}{R}+\frac{R}{\sqrt{t}}\Big),

Define a function
F(x)=x^{1-n/q}(x+{1}/{x}),\ x>0
. Then
we can see that for
x>\sqrt{n/(2q-n)}
,
F^{\prime}(x)>0
, i.e.,
F
is increasing, which means that the function
f(R):=F(\sqrt{t}/R)
is decreasing
for
R\in(0,\sqrt{(2q-n)t/n})
.
Below we divide the rest of the proof into two cases.

Case 1:
0<R<\min\{\rho(x_{0}),|x_{0}-y_{0}|\}<\sqrt{t}
. We further divide the discussion into two subcases.

Case 1.1:
0<R<\rho(x_{0})<\sqrt{t}<\sqrt{(2q-n)t/n}
, i.e.,
\rho(x_{0})\leq|x_{0}-y_{0}|
.

Taking the infimum on both sides of (
3.8
), we can get

\Big(\frac{\sqrt{t}}{R}\Big)^{1-n/q}\Big(\frac{\sqrt{t}}{R}+\frac{R}{\sqrt{t}}\Big)\lesssim\Big(\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x_{0})}\Big)^{1-n/q}\Big(\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x_{0})}+\frac{\rho(x_{0})}{\sqrt{t}}\Big)\lesssim\Big(\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x_{0})}\Big)^{1-n/q}\Big(\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x_{0})}+1\Big),

where we have used the fact that
\rho(x_{0})<\sqrt{t}
. This gives

\displaystyle|\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{t}(x_{0}+h,y_{0})-\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{t}(x_{0},y_{0})|

\displaystyle\leq\frac{C_{N}}{t^{(n+1)/2}}\Big(\frac{|h|}{\sqrt{t}}\Big)^{1-n/q}e^{-c|x_{0}-y_{0}|^{2}/t}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x_{0})}\Big)^{-N}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(y_{0})}\Big)^{-N}\Big\{\Big(\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x_{0})}\Big)^{1-n/q}\Big(\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x_{0})}+1\Big)\Big\}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{C_{N}}{t^{(n+1)/2}}\Big(\frac{|h|}{\sqrt{t}}\Big)^{1-n/q}e^{-c|x_{0}-y_{0}|^{2}/t}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x_{0})}\Big)^{-N}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(y_{0})}\Big)^{-N}.

Case 1.2:
0<R<|x_{0}-y_{0}|<\sqrt{t}
, i.e.,
\rho(x_{0})>|x_{0}-y_{0}|
.
By taking the infimum on
f(R)
, we can get

\displaystyle\Big(\frac{\sqrt{t}}{R}\Big)^{1-n/q}\Big(\frac{\sqrt{t}}{R}+\frac{R}{\sqrt{t}}\Big)

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\Big(\frac{\sqrt{t}}{|x_{0}-y_{0}|}\Big)^{1-n/q}\Big(\frac{\sqrt{t}}{|x_{0}-y_{0}|}+\frac{|x_{0}-y_{0}|}{\sqrt{t}}\Big)

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\Big(\frac{\sqrt{t}}{|x_{0}-y_{0}|}\Big)^{1-n/q}\Big(\frac{\sqrt{t}}{|x_{0}-y_{0}|}+1\Big).

Then we obtain

\displaystyle|\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{t}(x_{0}+h,y_{0})-\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{t}(x_{0},y_{0})|

\displaystyle\leq\frac{C_{N}}{t^{(n+1)/2}}\Big(\frac{|h|}{\sqrt{t}}\Big)^{1-n/q}\Big(\frac{\sqrt{t}}{|x_{0}-y_{0}|}\Big)^{1-n/q}e^{-c|x_{0}-y_{0}|^{2}/t}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(x_{0})}\Big)^{-N}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{t}}{\rho(y_{0})}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{\sqrt{t}}{|x_{0}-y_{0}|}+1\Big).

It is easy to see that

\displaystyle|\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{t}(x_{0}+h,y_{0})-\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{t}(x_{0},y_{0})|

Proposition 3.9
.

Suppose that
\alpha>0
and
V\in B_{q}
for some
q>n
. Let
\delta^{\prime}=1-n/q
. For every
N>0
, there exists a constant
C_{N}>0
such that for all
x,y\in\mathbb{R}^{n}
and

t>0
, the fractional heat kernels
K_{\alpha,t}^{L}(\cdot,\cdot)
satisfy the following estimate: for
|h|<|x-y|/4
,

|\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x+h,y)-\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)|\leq C_{N}\Big(\frac{|h|}{t^{1/2\alpha}}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}\frac{1}{t^{1/2\alpha}}\frac{t}{(t^{1/2\alpha}+|x-y|)^{n+2\alpha}}.

Proof.

By the subordinative formula and Lemma
3.7
, we can get

\displaystyle|\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x+h,y)-\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)|\leq C_{N}\int^{\infty}_{0}\frac{t}{s^{1+\alpha}}\Big|\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{t}(x+h,y)-\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{t}(x,y)\Big|ds\leq C_{N}(M_{6}+M_{7}),

where

\left\{\begin{aligned} M_{6}&:=\int^{|x-y|}_{0}\frac{t}{s^{1+\alpha}}\Big(\frac{|h|}{\sqrt{s}}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}\frac{1}{s^{(n+1)/2}}e^{-c|x-y|^{2}/s}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{s}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{s}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}ds;\\
M_{7}&:=\int_{|x-y|}^{\infty}\frac{t}{s^{1+\alpha}}\Big(\frac{|h|}{|x-y|}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}\frac{1}{s^{n/2}|x-y|}e^{-c|x-y|^{2}/s}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{s}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{s}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}ds.\end{aligned}\right.

We first estimate
M_{6}
and apply change of variables to obtain

\displaystyle M_{6}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\int^{\infty}_{0}\frac{t}{s^{1+\alpha}}\Big(\frac{|h|}{\sqrt{s}}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}\frac{1}{s^{(n+1)/2}}e^{-c|x-y|^{2}/s}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{s}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{s}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}ds

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle t^{-(n+1)/2\alpha}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{|h|}{t^{1/2\alpha}}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}\int^{\infty}_{0}u^{-N-1-\alpha-(n+1)/2-\delta^{\prime}/2}e^{-c|x-y|^{2}/t^{1/\alpha}u}du

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle t^{-(n+1)/2\alpha}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{|h|}{t^{1/2\alpha}}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}\int^{\infty}_{0}\Big(\frac{r^{2}t^{1/\alpha}}{|x-y|^{2}}\Big)^{N+1+\alpha+(n+1)/2+\delta^{\prime}/2}e^{-cr^{2}}\frac{|x-y|^{2}}{t^{1/\alpha}r^{3}}dr

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{|h|}{|x-y|}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}\frac{t^{1+N/\alpha}}{|x-y|^{2\alpha+2N+n+1}}.

Similarly, for
M_{7}
, we have

\displaystyle M_{7}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\frac{1}{|x-y|}\int^{\infty}_{0}\frac{t}{s^{1+\alpha}}\Big(\frac{|h|}{\sqrt{s}}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}\frac{1}{s^{n/2}}e^{-c|x-y|^{2}/s}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{s}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{s}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}ds

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{|h|}{|x-y|}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}\frac{t^{1+N/\alpha}}{|x-y|^{2\alpha+2N+n+1}},

which gives

|\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x+h,y)-\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)|\leq\frac{C_{N}t^{1+N/\alpha}}{|x-y|^{2\alpha+2N+n+1}}\Big(\frac{|h|}{|x-y|}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}.

On the other hand, we can deduce from Lemma
3.8
that

\displaystyle|\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x+h,y)-\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)|

\displaystyle\leq C_{N}\int^{\infty}_{0}s^{-(n+1)/2}\frac{1}{t^{1/\alpha}}\eta^{\alpha}_{1}\Big(\frac{s}{t^{1/\alpha}}\Big)\Big(\frac{|h|}{\sqrt{s}}\Big)^{\beta}\Big(1+\frac{\sqrt{s}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{\sqrt{s}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}ds

\displaystyle\leq C_{N}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{|h|}{t^{1/2\alpha}}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}\frac{1}{t^{(n+1)/2\alpha}}.

Finally, the arbitrariness of

indicates that

\displaystyle\Big(1+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{N}\Big(1+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{N}|\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x+h,y)-\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)|

\displaystyle\quad\leq C_{N}\min\Bigg\{\Big(\frac{|h|}{|x-y|}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}\frac{t^{1+N/\alpha}}{|x-y|^{2\alpha+2N+n+1}},\ \Big(\frac{|h|}{t^{1/2\alpha}}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}\frac{1}{t^{(n+1)/2\alpha}}\Bigg\},

which proves Proposition
3.9
.

∎

Proposition 3.10
.

Assume that
V\in B_{q}
for some
q>n

\alpha\in(0,1/2-n/2q)

\displaystyle L_{s,1}(x)

\displaystyle\lesssim

Similarly, a direct calculus gives, together with changing variable:

\displaystyle L_{s,2}(x)

3.3.
Estimation on time-fractional derivatives

In this section we give some gradient estimate for the fractional heat kernel associated with the variable
t
.
Define an operator

D_{\alpha,t}^{L,\beta}(f)=t^{\beta}\partial^{\beta}_{t}e^{-tL^{\alpha}}f,\ \ \alpha\in(0,1)\ \&\ \beta>0.

Denote by
D_{\alpha,t}^{L,\beta}(\cdot,\cdot)
the integral kernel of
D_{\alpha,t}^{L,\beta}
. Then we can get the following proposition.

Proposition 3.11
.

Let
\alpha\in(0,1)
and
\beta>0
. For every
N>0
, there exists a constant
C_{N}>0
such that

(3.14)

|D_{\alpha,t}^{L,\beta}(x,y)|\leq\frac{C_{N}t^{\beta}}{(t^{1/2\alpha}+|x-y|)^{n+2\alpha\beta}}\Big(1+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}.

Proof.

The following two cases are considered.

Case 1:
\beta\in(0,1)
.

It is easy to see that

\displaystyle t^{\beta}\partial_{t}^{\beta}e^{-tL^{\alpha}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle c_{\beta}t^{\beta}\int^{\infty}_{0}\partial_{t}e^{-(t+s)L^{\alpha}}\frac{ds}{s^{\beta}}=c_{\beta}t^{\beta}\int^{\infty}_{0}(-L)^{\alpha}e^{-(t+s)L^{\alpha}}\frac{ds}{s^{\beta}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle c_{\beta}t^{\beta}\int^{\infty}_{0}(t+s)(-L)^{\alpha}e^{-(t+s)L^{\alpha}}\frac{ds}{(t+s)s^{\beta}},

which, together with Proposition
3.3
, gives

\displaystyle\Big|D^{L,\beta}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)\Big|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{N}t^{\beta}\int^{\infty}_{0}\frac{1}{((t+s)^{1/2\alpha}+|x-y|)^{n+2\alpha}}\Big(1+\frac{(t+s)^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(1+\frac{(t+s)^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}\frac{ds}{s^{\beta}}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{N}t^{\beta}\int^{\infty}_{0}\frac{1}{(t+s)^{n/2\alpha+1}}\Big(\frac{(t+s)^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{(t+s)^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}\frac{ds}{s^{\beta}}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{N}t^{\beta}\rho(x)^{N}\rho(y)^{N}\int^{\infty}_{0}(t+s)^{-n/2\alpha-N/\alpha-1}s^{-\beta}ds

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{C_{N}}{t^{n/2\alpha}}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}.

One the other hand, since
e^{-tL^{\alpha}}=\int^{\infty}_{0}\eta^{\alpha}_{1}(\tau)e^{-t^{1/\alpha}\tau L}d\tau
,

\displaystyle t^{\beta}\partial^{\beta}_{t}e^{-tL^{\alpha}}

\displaystyle\simeq

\displaystyle t^{\beta}\int^{\infty}_{0}\partial_{r}\Big(\int^{\infty}_{0}\eta^{\alpha}_{1}(\tau)e^{-(t+r)^{1/\alpha}\tau L}d\tau\Big)\frac{dr}{r^{\beta}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle t^{\beta}\int^{\infty}_{0}\Big(\int^{\infty}_{0}\eta^{\alpha}_{1}(\tau)(t+r)^{1/\alpha-1}\tau Le^{-(t+r)^{1/\alpha}\tau L}d\tau\Big)\frac{dr}{r^{\beta}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle C_{\beta}t^{\beta}\int^{\infty}_{0}\Big(\int^{\infty}_{0}Q^{L}_{\sqrt{(t+r)^{1/\alpha}\tau},1}\frac{dr}{(t+r)r^{\beta}}\Big)\eta^{\alpha}_{1}(\tau)d\tau.

By Proposition
2.10
, we can get

\displaystyle\Big|D_{\alpha,t}^{L,\beta}(x,y)\Big|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{N}t^{\beta}\int^{\infty}_{0}\eta^{\alpha}_{1}(\tau)\Bigg\{\int^{\infty}_{0}((t+r)^{1/\alpha}\tau)^{-n/2}e^{-c|x-y|^{2}/(t+r)^{1/\alpha}\tau}

\displaystyle\quad\Big(1+\frac{(t+r)^{1/2\alpha}\sqrt{\tau}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(1+\frac{(t+r)^{1/2\alpha}\sqrt{\tau}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}\frac{dr}{r^{\beta}(t+r)}\Bigg\}d\tau

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{N}\frac{t^{\beta}\rho(x)^{N}\rho(y)^{N}}{|x-y|^{n+2\alpha\beta}}\int^{\infty}_{0}\eta^{\alpha}_{1}(\tau)\tau^{\alpha\beta-N}\Big(\int^{\infty}_{0}(t+r)^{\beta-N/\alpha-1}r^{-\beta}dr\Big)d\tau

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{C_{N}t^{\beta}}{|x-y|^{n+2\alpha\beta}}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}.

By the arbitrariness of
N
, we obtain

\displaystyle|D_{\alpha,t}^{L,\beta}(x,y)|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{N}\min\Big\{\frac{1}{t^{n/2\alpha}},\ \frac{t^{\beta}}{|x-y|^{n+2\alpha\beta}}\Big\}\Big(1+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(1+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{C_{N}t^{\beta}}{(t^{1/2\alpha}+|x-y|)^{n+2\beta\alpha}}\Big(1+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}.

Case 2:
\beta\geq 1
.
Let
m=[\beta]+1
. We can get

\displaystyle t^{\beta}\partial_{t}^{\beta}e^{-tL^{\alpha}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle c_{\beta}t^{\beta}\int^{\infty}_{0}\partial^{m}_{t}e^{-(t+s)L^{\alpha}}\frac{ds}{s^{\beta+1-m}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle c_{\beta}t^{\beta}\int^{\infty}_{0}(-L)^{m\alpha}e^{-(t+s)L^{\alpha}}\frac{ds}{s^{1+\beta-m}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle c_{\beta}t^{\beta}\int^{\infty}_{0}(t+s)^{m}(-L)^{m\alpha}e^{-(t+s)L^{\alpha}}\frac{ds}{(t+s)^{m}s^{1+\beta-m}}.

It follows from Proposition
3.3
that

\displaystyle\Big|D_{\alpha,t}^{L,\beta}(x,y)\Big|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{N}t^{\beta}\int^{\infty}_{0}\frac{(t+s)^{m}}{(t+s)^{n/2\alpha+m}}\Big(\frac{(t+s)^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{(t+s)^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}\frac{ds}{(t+s)^{m}s^{1+\beta-m}}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{N}t^{\beta}\rho(x)^{N}\rho(y)^{N}\int^{\infty}_{0}(t+s)^{-n/2\alpha-N/2\alpha-m}\frac{ds}{s^{1+\beta-m}}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{C_{N}}{t^{n/2\alpha}}\Big(1+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(1+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}.

On the other hand, we obtain

\displaystyle\Big|D_{\alpha,t}^{L,\beta}(x,y)\Big|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle t^{\beta}\int^{\infty}_{0}\Bigg\{\int^{\infty}_{0}\Big|\partial^{m}_{r}K^{L}_{(t+r)^{1/\alpha}\tau}(x,y)\Big|\frac{dr}{r^{\beta+1-m}}\Bigg\}\eta^{\alpha}_{1}(\tau)d\tau

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{N}t^{\beta}\int^{\infty}_{0}\Bigg\{\int^{\infty}_{0}(t+r)^{-m}((t+r)^{1/\alpha}\tau)^{-n/2}e^{-c|x-y|^{2}/(t+r)^{1/\alpha}\tau}

\displaystyle\quad\Big(\frac{\sqrt{\tau}(t+r)^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{\sqrt{\tau}(t+r)^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}\frac{dr}{r^{\beta+1-m}}\Bigg\}\eta^{\alpha}_{1}(\tau)d\tau

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{N}\frac{t^{\beta}\rho(x)^{N}\rho(y)^{N}}{|x-y|^{n+2\alpha\beta}}t^{-N/\alpha}\int^{\infty}_{0}\Big(\int^{\infty}_{0}(1+u)^{-m+\beta-N/\beta}u^{m-\beta-1}du\Big)\eta^{\alpha}_{1}(\tau)\tau^{\alpha\beta-N}d\tau

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{C_{N}t^{\beta}}{|x-y|^{n+2\alpha\beta}}\Big(1+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(1+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N},

which indicates (
3.14
) holds.
∎

Proposition 3.12
.

Let
\alpha\in(0,1)
and
\beta>0
. Let
0<\delta^{\prime}\leq\delta=\min\{2\alpha,\delta_{0}\}

|D_{\alpha,t}^{L,\beta}(x+h,y)-D_{\alpha,t}^{L,\beta}(x,y)|\leq C_{N}\Big(\frac{|h|}{t^{1/2\alpha}}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}\frac{t^{\beta}}{(t^{1/2\alpha}+|x-y|)^{n+2\alpha\beta}}\Big(1+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}.

Proof.

It is equivalent to verify

(3.15)

\displaystyle|D_{\alpha,t}^{L,\beta}(x+h,y)-D_{\alpha,t}^{L,\beta}(x,y)|\leq C_{N}\Big(\frac{|h|}{t^{1/2\alpha}}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}\min\Big\{\frac{1}{t^{n/2\alpha}},\ \frac{t^{\beta}}{|x-y|^{n+2\alpha\beta}}\Big\}\Big(1+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}.

Without loss of generality, for
m=[\beta]+1
, it holds

\displaystyle t^{\beta}\partial^{\beta}_{t}e^{-tL^{\alpha}}(x,y)=c_{\beta}t^{\beta}\int^{\infty}_{0}(t+s)^{m}(-L)^{m\alpha}e^{-(t+s)L^{\alpha}}\frac{ds}{(t+s)^{m}s^{1+\beta-m}}.

By Proposition
3.3
, we can get

\displaystyle\Big|D_{\alpha,t}^{L,\beta}(x+h,y)-D_{\alpha,t}^{L,\beta}(x,y)\Big|

\displaystyle\leq C_{N}t^{\beta}\int^{\infty}_{0}\frac{(t+s)^{m}({|h|}/{(t+s)^{1/2\alpha}})^{\delta^{\prime}}}{((t+s)^{1/2\alpha}+|x-y|)^{n+2\alpha m}}\Big(1+\frac{(t+s)^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(1+\frac{(t+s)^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}\frac{ds}{(t+s)^{m}s^{1+\beta-m}}

\displaystyle\leq C_{N}t^{\beta}|h|^{\delta^{\prime}}\rho(x)^{N}\rho(y)^{N}\int^{\infty}_{0}(t+s)^{-(n+\delta^{\prime})/2\alpha-N/2\alpha-m}\frac{ds}{s^{1+\beta-m}}

\displaystyle\leq C_{N}t^{-n/2\alpha}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{|h|}{t^{1/2\alpha}}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}.

On the other hand, we obtain

\displaystyle\Big|D_{\alpha,t}^{L,\beta}(x+h,y)-D_{\alpha,t}^{L,\beta}(x,y)\Big|

\displaystyle\leq C_{N}t^{\beta}\int^{\infty}_{0}\Bigg\{\int^{\infty}_{0}(t+r)^{-m-n/2\alpha}\tau^{-n/2}\Big(\frac{|h|}{\sqrt{(t+r)^{1/\alpha}\tau}}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}

\displaystyle\quad\times e^{-c|x-y|^{2}/(t+r)^{1/\alpha}\tau}\Big(\frac{\sqrt{\tau}(t+r)^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{\sqrt{\tau}(t+r)^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}\frac{dr}{r^{\beta+1-m}}\Bigg\}\eta^{\alpha}_{1}(\tau)d\tau

\displaystyle\leq C_{N}\frac{t^{\beta}|h|^{\delta^{\prime}}\rho(x)^{N}\rho(y)^{N}}{|x-y|^{n+2\alpha\beta}}\int^{\infty}_{0}\Bigg\{\int^{\infty}_{0}(t+r)^{-m+\beta-N/\alpha-\delta^{\prime}/2\alpha}r^{m-\beta-1}dr\Bigg\}\eta^{\alpha}_{1}(\tau)\tau^{\alpha\beta-N-\delta^{\prime}/2}d\tau

\displaystyle\leq\frac{C_{N}t^{\beta}}{|x-y|^{n+2\alpha\beta}}\Big(\frac{|h|}{t^{1/2\alpha}}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}\Big(1+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(1+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N},

which implies (
3.15
).

∎

Proposition 3.13
.

Let
\alpha\in(0,1)
,
\beta>0
and
0<\delta^{\prime}\leq\min\{2\alpha,\delta_{0}\}
. For every
N>0
, there exist constants
C_{N}>0
such that

\Big|\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}D_{\alpha,t}^{L,\beta}(x,y)dy\Big|\leq C_{N}\frac{(t^{1/2\alpha}/\rho(x))^{\delta^{\prime}}}{(1+t^{1/2\alpha}/\rho(x))^{N}}.

Proof.

Let
m=[\beta]+1
. By (iii) of Proposition
3.3
, we change the order of integrations to obtain

\displaystyle\Big|\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}D^{L,\beta}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)dy\Big|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\Big|\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\Big\{t^{\beta}\int^{\infty}_{0}\partial^{m}_{t}D^{L,\beta}_{\alpha,t+s}(x,y)\frac{ds}{s^{1+\beta-m}}\Big\}dy\Big|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle t^{\beta}\int^{\infty}_{0}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\Big|\partial^{m}_{t}D^{L,\beta}_{\alpha,t+s}(x,y)\Big|\frac{dyds}{s^{1+\beta-m}}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{N}t^{\beta}\int^{\infty}_{0}\frac{((t+s)^{1/2\alpha}/\rho(x))^{\delta^{\prime}}}{(1+(t+s)^{1/2\alpha}/\rho(x))^{N}}\frac{ds}{s^{1+\beta-m}(t+s)^{m}}.

If
t^{1/2\alpha}>\rho(x)
, then

\displaystyle\Big|\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}D^{L,\beta}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)dy\Big|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{N}t^{\beta}\rho(x)^{N-\delta^{\prime}}\int^{\infty}_{0}(t+s)^{\delta^{\prime}/2\alpha-N/2\alpha-m}s^{m-\beta-1}ds

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{N}\frac{(t^{1/2\alpha}/\rho(x))^{\delta^{\prime}}}{(1+t^{1/2\alpha}/\rho(x))^{N}}.

If
t^{1/2\alpha}\leq\rho(x)
, then

\displaystyle\Big|\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}D^{L,\beta}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)dy\Big|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{N}t^{\beta}\int^{\infty}_{0}\Big((t+s)^{1/2\alpha}/\rho(x)\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}\frac{ds}{s^{1+\beta-m}(t+s)^{m}}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{N}t^{\beta}\rho(x)^{\delta^{\prime}}\int^{\infty}_{0}(t+s)^{\delta^{\prime}/2\alpha-m}s^{m-1-\beta}ds

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{N}\Big(t^{1/2\alpha}/\rho(x)\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}\leq C_{N}\frac{(t^{1/2\alpha}/\rho(x))^{\delta^{\prime}}}{(1+t^{1/2\alpha}/\rho(x))^{N}},

which completes the proof of Proposition
3.13
.
∎

4.
Characterization of Campanato-Morrey spaces associated with
L

Firstly, we deduce a reproducing formula.

Lemma 4.1
.

Let
\alpha\in(0,1)
and
\beta>0
.
The operator
t^{\beta}\partial_{t}^{\beta}e^{-tL^{\alpha}}
defines an isometry from
L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
into
L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{n+1}_{+},dxdt/t)
. Moreover, in the sense of
L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
, it holds

f(x)=c_{\alpha,\beta}\lim_{N\rightarrow\infty}\lim_{\epsilon\rightarrow 0}\int^{N}_{\epsilon}(t^{\beta}\partial_{t}^{\beta}e^{-tL^{\alpha}})^{2}(f)(x)\frac{dt}{t}.

Proof.

Note that for
dE(\lambda)
the spectral resolution of the operator
L
, it follows from

e^{-tL^{\alpha}}=\int^{\infty}_{0}e^{-t\lambda^{\alpha}}dE(\lambda)

that

\displaystyle t^{\beta}\partial^{\beta}_{t}e^{-tL^{\alpha}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle t^{\beta}\int^{\infty}_{0}\partial^{m}_{t}\Big(\int^{\infty}_{0}e^{-(t+s)\lambda^{\alpha}}dE(\lambda)\Big)\frac{ds}{s^{1+\beta-m}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle t^{\beta}\int^{\infty}_{0}\Big(\int^{\infty}_{0}(-1)^{m}\lambda^{\alpha m}e^{-(t+s)\lambda^{\alpha}}dE(\lambda)\Big)\frac{ds}{s^{1+\beta-m}}

\displaystyle\approx

\displaystyle t^{\beta}\int^{\infty}_{0}(-1)^{m}\lambda^{m\alpha}e^{-t\lambda^{\alpha}}\lambda^{\alpha(\beta-m)}dE(\lambda)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int^{\infty}_{0}(t\lambda^{\alpha})^{\beta}e^{-t\lambda^{\alpha}}dE(\lambda).

Then for
f\in L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
, we have

\displaystyle\|t^{\beta}\partial_{t}^{\beta}e^{-tL^{\alpha}}(f)(x)\|^{2}_{L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{n+1}_{+},dxdt/t)}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int^{\infty}_{0}\Big(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}|t^{\beta}\partial_{t}^{\beta}e^{-tL^{\alpha}}(f)(x)|^{2}dx\Big)\frac{dt}{t}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int^{\infty}_{0}\Big\langle t^{\beta}\partial_{t}^{\beta}e^{-tL^{\alpha}}(f),\ t^{\beta}\partial_{t}^{\beta}e^{-tL^{\alpha}}(f)\Big\rangle\frac{dt}{t}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int^{\infty}_{0}\Big\langle(t^{\beta}\partial_{t}^{\beta}e^{-tL^{\alpha}})^{2}(f),\ f\Big\rangle\frac{dt}{t}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int^{\infty}_{0}\int_{0}^{\infty}t^{2\beta}\lambda^{2\alpha\beta}e^{-2t\lambda^{\alpha}}\frac{dt}{t}dE_{f,f}(\lambda)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle C_{\alpha,\beta}\|f\|^{2}_{2}.

Below we only prove that for every pair of sequences
n_{k}\uparrow\infty
and
\epsilon_{k}\downarrow 0
as
k\rightarrow\infty
,

(4.1)

\lim_{k\rightarrow\infty}\int^{n_{k+m}}_{n_{k}}(t^{\beta}\partial^{\beta}_{t}e^{-tL^{\alpha}})^{2}f(x)\frac{dt}{t}=\lim_{k\rightarrow\infty}\int^{\epsilon_{k+m}}_{\epsilon_{k}}(t^{\beta}\partial^{\beta}_{t}e^{-tL^{\alpha}})^{2}f(x)\frac{dt}{t}=0.

If (
4.1
) holds, there exists a function
h\in L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
such that

\lim_{k\rightarrow\infty}\int^{n_{k}}_{\epsilon_{k}}(t^{\beta}\partial^{\beta}_{t}e^{-tL^{\alpha}})^{2}f(x)\frac{dt}{t}=h(x),

which implies that for all
g\in L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
,

\displaystyle\langle h,\ g\rangle

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\Big\langle\lim_{k\rightarrow\infty}\int^{n_{k}}_{\epsilon_{k}}(t^{\beta}\partial^{\beta}_{t}e^{-tL^{\alpha}})^{2}f\frac{dt}{t},\ g\Big\rangle

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\lim_{k\rightarrow\infty}\int^{n_{k}}_{\epsilon_{k}}\Big\langle(t^{\beta}\partial^{\beta}_{t}e^{-tL^{\alpha}})^{2}f,\ g\Big\rangle\frac{dt}{t}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\lim_{k\rightarrow\infty}\int^{n_{k}}_{\epsilon_{k}}\Big\langle t^{\beta}\partial^{\beta}_{t}e^{-tL^{\alpha}}f,\ t^{\beta}\partial^{\beta}_{t}e^{-tL^{\alpha}}g\Big\rangle\frac{dt}{t}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle C_{\alpha,\beta}\langle f,\ g\rangle.

This means
h=C_{\alpha,\beta}f
. Now we verify (
4.1
). As
k\rightarrow\infty
,

\displaystyle\Big\|\int^{n_{k+m}}_{n_{k}}(t^{\beta}\partial^{\beta}_{t}e^{-tL^{\alpha}})^{2}f(x)\frac{dt}{t}\Big\|^{2}_{L^{2}}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\int^{n_{k+m}}_{n_{k}}\Big\|(t^{\beta}\partial^{\beta}_{t}e^{-tL^{\alpha}})^{2}f(x)\Big\|^{2}_{L^{2}}\frac{dt}{t}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int^{\infty}_{0}\int^{n_{k+m}}_{n_{k}}t^{2\beta}\lambda^{2\alpha\beta}e^{-2t\lambda^{\alpha}}\frac{dt}{t}dE_{f,f}(\lambda)\rightarrow 0

since

\lim_{k\rightarrow\infty}\Big|\int^{n_{k+m}}_{n_{k}}t^{2\beta}\lambda^{2\alpha\beta}e^{-2t\lambda^{\alpha}}\frac{dt}{t}\Big|=0.

The integral

\lim_{k\rightarrow\infty}\int^{\epsilon_{k+m}}_{\epsilon_{k}}(t^{\beta}\partial^{\beta}_{t}e^{-tL^{\alpha}})^{2}f(x)\frac{dt}{t}

can be dealt with similarly.

∎

The following inequality was established by Harboure-Salinas-Viviani

]
.

Lemma 4.2
.

(
[
13
, (5.3)]
)

Let
0<\gamma\leq 1
.
For any pair of measurable functions
F
and
G
on
\mathbb{R}^{n+1}_{+}
, we have

\displaystyle\iint_{\mathbb{R}^{n+1}_{+}}|F(x,t)|\cdot|G(x,t)|\frac{dxdt}{t}

\displaystyle\leq C\sup_{B}\Bigg\{\frac{1}{|B|^{1+2\gamma/n}}\iint_{\widehat{B}}|F(x,t)|^{2}\frac{dxdt}{t}\Bigg\}^{1/2}\Bigg\{\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\Big(\int^{\infty}_{0}\int_{|x-y|<t}|G(y,t)|^{2}\frac{dydt}{t^{n+1}}\Big)^{n/2(n+\gamma)}dx\Bigg\}^{1+\gamma/n}.

In Lemma
4.2
, letting

\left\{\begin{aligned} F(x,t):=t^{2\alpha\beta}\partial^{\beta}_{s}e^{-sL^{\alpha}}\mid_{s=t^{2\alpha}}(f)(x)=Q^{L,\beta}_{\alpha,t}(f)(x);\\
G(x,t):=t^{2\alpha\beta}\partial^{\beta}_{s}e^{-sL^{\alpha}}\mid_{s=t^{2\alpha}}(g)(x)=Q^{L,\beta}_{\alpha,t}(g)(x),\end{aligned}\right.

we have

(4.2)

\displaystyle\iint_{\mathbb{R}^{n+1}_{+}}|Q^{L,\beta}_{\alpha,t}(f)(x)|\cdot|Q^{L,\beta}_{\alpha,t}(g)(x)|\frac{dxdt}{t}

\displaystyle\quad\leq C\sup_{B}\Big(\frac{1}{|B|^{1+2\gamma/n}}\iint_{\widehat{B}}|Q^{L,\beta}_{\alpha,t}(f)(x)|^{2}\frac{dxdt}{t}\Big)^{1/2}

\displaystyle\ \quad\times\Bigg\{\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\Big(\int^{\infty}_{0}\int_{|x-y|<t}|Q^{L,\beta}_{\alpha,t}(g)(x)|^{2}\frac{dydt}{t^{n+1}}\Big)^{n/2(n+\gamma)}dx\Bigg\}^{1+\gamma/n}.

On the left-hand side of (
4.2
), since

\left\{\begin{aligned} Q^{L,\beta}_{\alpha,t}(f)(x)=t^{2\alpha\beta}L^{\alpha\beta}e^{-t^{2\alpha}L^{\alpha}}(f);\\
Q^{L,\beta}_{\alpha,t}(g)(x)=t^{2\alpha\beta}L^{\alpha\beta}e^{-t^{2\alpha}L^{\alpha}}(g),\end{aligned}\right.

we can get, via the change of variables,

\displaystyle\iint_{\mathbb{R}^{n+1}_{+}}|Q^{L,\beta}_{\alpha,t}(f)(x)|\cdot|Q^{L,\beta}_{\alpha,t}(g)(x)|\frac{dxdt}{t}

\displaystyle\quad=\iint_{\mathbb{R}^{n+1}_{+}}|t^{2\alpha\beta}L^{\alpha\beta}e^{-t^{2\alpha}L^{\alpha}}(f)(x)|\cdot|t^{2\alpha\beta}L^{\alpha\beta}e^{-t^{2\alpha}L^{\alpha}}(g)(x)|\frac{dxdt}{t}

\displaystyle\quad=\iint_{\mathbb{R}^{n+1}_{+}}|s^{\beta}L^{\alpha\beta}e^{-sL^{\alpha}}(f)(x)|\cdot|s^{\beta}L^{\alpha\beta}e^{-sL^{\alpha}}(g)(x)|\frac{dxds}{s}

\displaystyle\quad=\iint_{\mathbb{R}^{n+1}_{+}}|s^{\beta}\partial^{\beta}_{s}e^{-sL^{\alpha}}(f)(x)|\cdot|s^{\beta}\partial^{\beta}_{s}e^{-sL^{\alpha}}(g)(x)|\frac{dxds}{s}.

On the right-hand side of (
4.2
), using change of variables again, we obtain

\displaystyle\sup_{B}\Big(\frac{1}{|B|^{1+2\gamma/n}}\int^{r_{B}}_{0}\int_{B}|t^{2\alpha\beta}L^{\alpha\beta}e^{-t^{2\alpha}L^{\alpha}}(f)(x)|^{2}\frac{dxdt}{t}\Big)^{1/2}

\displaystyle\quad\lesssim\sup_{B}\Big(\frac{1}{|B|^{1+2\gamma/n}}\int^{r_{B}^{2\alpha}}_{0}\int_{B}|s^{\beta}L^{\alpha\beta}e^{-sL^{\alpha}}(f)(x)|^{2}\frac{dxds}{s}\Big)^{1/2}

\displaystyle\quad=\sup_{B}\Big(\frac{1}{|B|^{1+2\gamma/n}}\int^{r_{B}^{2\alpha}}_{0}\int_{B}|s^{\beta}\partial_{s}^{\beta}e^{-sL^{\alpha}}(f)(x)|^{2}\frac{dxds}{s}\Big)^{1/2},

meanwhile,

\displaystyle\Bigg\{\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\Big(\int^{\infty}_{0}\int_{|x-y|<t}|t^{2\alpha\beta}L^{\alpha\beta}e^{-t^{2\alpha}L^{\alpha}}(g)(y)|^{2}\frac{dydt}{t^{n+1}}\Big)^{n/2(n+\gamma)}dx\Bigg\}^{1+\gamma/n}

\displaystyle\quad\lesssim\Bigg\{\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\Big(\int^{\infty}_{0}\int_{|x-y|<s^{1/2\alpha}}|s^{\beta}L^{\alpha\beta}e^{-sL^{\alpha}}(f)(y)|^{2}\frac{s^{1/2\alpha-1}dyds}{s^{(n+1)/2\alpha}}\Big)^{n/2(n+\gamma)}dx\Bigg\}^{1+\gamma/n}

\displaystyle\quad\lesssim\Bigg\{\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\Big(\int^{\infty}_{0}\int_{|x-y|<s^{1/2\alpha}}|s^{\beta}L^{\alpha\beta}e^{-sL^{\alpha}}(g)(y)|^{2}\frac{s^{1/2\alpha-1}dyds}{s^{n/2\alpha+1}}\Big)^{n/2(n+\gamma)}dx\Bigg\}^{1+\gamma/n}

\displaystyle\quad\lesssim\Bigg\{\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\Big(\int^{\infty}_{0}\int_{|x-y|<s^{1/2\alpha}}|s^{\beta}\partial^{\beta}_{s}e^{-sL^{\alpha}}(g)(y)|^{2}\frac{s^{1/2\alpha-1}dyds}{s^{n/2\alpha+1}}\Big)^{n/2(n+\gamma)}dx\Bigg\}^{1+\gamma/n}.

Finally, we have

(4.3)

\displaystyle\iint_{\mathbb{R}^{n+1}_{+}}|s^{\beta}\partial^{\beta}e^{-sL^{\alpha}}(f)(x)|\cdot|s^{\beta}\partial^{\beta}_{s}e^{-sL^{\alpha}}(g)(x)|\frac{dxds}{s}

\displaystyle\quad\lesssim\sup_{B}\Big(\frac{1}{|B|^{1+2\gamma/n}}\int^{r_{B}^{2\alpha}}_{0}\int_{B}|s^{\beta}\partial_{s}^{\beta}e^{-sL^{\alpha}}(f)(x)|^{2}\frac{dxds}{s}\Big)^{1/2}

\displaystyle\ \quad\times\Bigg\{\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\Big(\iint_{|x-y|<s^{1/2\alpha}}|s^{\beta}\partial^{\beta}_{s}e^{-sL^{\alpha}}(g)(y)|^{2}\frac{s^{1/2\alpha-1}dyds}{s^{n/2\alpha+1}}\Big)^{n/2(n+\gamma)}dx\Bigg\}^{1+\gamma/n}.

For
\alpha\in(0,1)
and
\beta>0
, define an area function
S^{L}_{\alpha,\beta}
as follows:

S^{L}_{\alpha,\beta}(h)(x):=\Big(\iint_{\Gamma_{\alpha}(x)}|t^{\beta}\partial_{t}^{\beta}e^{-tL^{\alpha}}(h)(y)|^{2}\frac{dydt}{t^{n/2\alpha+1}}\Big)^{1/2},

where
\Gamma_{\alpha}(x)
denotes the cone
\{(y,t):\ |x-y|<t^{1/2\alpha}\}
.

Lemma 4.3
.

Let
\alpha\in(0,1)
and
\beta>0
.
The area function
S^{L}_{\alpha,\beta}
is bounded on
L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
.

Proof.

Let

g^{L}_{\alpha,\beta}(h)(x):=\Big(\int^{\infty}_{0}|t^{\beta}\partial^{\beta}_{t}e^{-tL^{\alpha}}h(x)|^{2}\frac{dt}{t}\Big)^{1/2}.

We can get

(4.4)

\displaystyle\|g^{L}_{\alpha,\beta}(h)\|^{2}_{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\Big(\int^{\infty}_{0}|t^{\beta}\partial_{t}^{\beta}e^{-tL^{\alpha}}h(x)|^{2}\frac{dt}{t}\Big)dx

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int^{\infty}_{0}\Big(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}|t^{\beta}\partial_{t}^{\beta}e^{-tL^{\alpha}}h(x)|^{2}dx\Big)\frac{dt}{t}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int^{\infty}_{0}\Big\langle t^{\beta}\partial_{t}^{\beta}e^{-tL^{\alpha}}h,\ t^{\beta}\partial_{t}^{\beta}e^{-tL^{\alpha}}h\Big\rangle\frac{dt}{t}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int^{\infty}_{0}\Big\langle(t^{\beta}\partial_{t}^{\beta}e^{-tL^{\alpha}})^{2}h,\ h\Big\rangle\frac{dt}{t}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int^{\infty}_{0}\int^{\infty}_{0}t^{2\beta}\lambda^{2\alpha\beta}e^{-t\lambda^{\alpha}}dE_{h,h}(\lambda)\frac{dt}{t}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\|h\|_{2}^{2}.

Hence, it follows from (
4.4
) that

\displaystyle\|S^{L}_{\alpha,\beta}h\|^{2}_{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\Big(\iint_{\Gamma_{\alpha}(x)}|t^{\beta}\partial_{t}^{\beta}e^{-tL^{\alpha}}(h)(y)|^{2}\frac{dydt}{t^{\frac{n}{2\alpha}+1}}\Big)dx

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\Big(\int^{\infty}_{0}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}|t^{\beta}\partial_{t}^{\beta}e^{-tL^{\alpha}}(h)(y)|^{2}\chi_{\Gamma_{\alpha}(x)}(y)\frac{dydt}{t^{\frac{n}{2\alpha}+1}}\Big)dx

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\int^{\infty}_{0}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}|t^{\beta}\partial_{t}^{\beta}e^{-tL^{\alpha}}(h)(y)|^{2}\Big(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\chi_{\Gamma_{\alpha}(y)}(x)dx\Big)\frac{dydt}{t^{\frac{n}{2\alpha}+1}}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\int^{\infty}_{0}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}|t^{\beta}\partial_{t}^{\beta}e^{-tL^{\alpha}}(h)(y)|^{2}\frac{dydt}{t}\lesssim\|h\|_{2}^{2}.

∎

Theorem 4.4
.

Assume that
\alpha\in(0,1)
,
\beta>0
and
0<\gamma\leq\min\{2\alpha,2\alpha\beta\}
. Let
f
be a linear combination of
H^{n/(n+\gamma)}_{L}
-atoms. There exists a constant
C
such that

\|S^{L}_{\alpha,\beta}(f)\|_{L^{n/(n+\gamma)}}\leq C\|f\|_{H^{n/(n+\gamma)}_{L}}.

Proof.

Let
a
be an
H^{n/(n+\gamma)}_{L}
-atom associated with a ball
B=B(x_{0},r)
. Then
we write

\|S^{L}_{\alpha,\beta}(a)\|^{n/(n+\gamma)}_{L^{n/(n+\gamma)}}\leq I+II,

\left\{\begin{aligned} &I:=\int_{8B}|S^{L}_{\alpha,\beta}(a)(x)|^{n/(n+\gamma)}dx;\\
&II:=\int_{(8B)^{c}}|S^{L}_{\alpha,\beta}(a)(x)|^{n/(n+\gamma)}dx.\end{aligned}\right.

We use Lemma
4.3
and Hölder’s inequality to obtain

\displaystyle I\lesssim\Big(\int_{8B}|S^{L}_{\alpha,\beta}a(x)|^{2}dx\Big)^{n/2(n+\gamma)}|B|^{(n+2\gamma)/2(n+\gamma)}\lesssim\|a\|^{n/(n+\gamma)}_{2}|B|^{(n+2\gamma)/2(n+\gamma)}\lesssim 1.

in the following two cases.

Case 1:
r<\rho(x_{0})/4
.
For this case,
\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}a(x)dx=0
.
We write
(S^{L}_{\alpha,\beta}(a)(x))^{2}\lesssim I_{1}(x)+I_{2}(x),

where

\left\{\begin{aligned} I_{1}(x)&:=\int^{(|x-x_{0}|/2)^{2\alpha}}_{0}\int_{|x-y|<t^{1/2\alpha}}\Big\{\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}(t^{\beta}\partial_{t}^{\beta}e^{-tL^{\alpha}}(y,x^{\prime})-t^{\beta}\partial_{t}^{\beta}e^{-tL^{\alpha}}(y,x_{0}))a(x^{\prime})dx^{\prime}\Big\}^{2}\frac{dydt}{t^{n/2\alpha+1}};\\

|y-x^{\prime}|\sim|y-x_{0}|
for

and
x\notin B(x_{0},8r)

3.11
and
3.12
to deduce there exists

such that

\displaystyle|I_{1}(x)|

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\int^{(|x-x_{0}|/2)^{2\alpha}}_{0}\int_{|x-y|<t^{1/2\alpha}}\Big\{\int_{B}\Big(\frac{|x^{\prime}-x_{0}|}{t^{1/2\alpha}}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}\frac{t^{\beta}}{(t^{1/2\alpha}+|y-x_{0}|)^{n+2\alpha\beta}}\frac{dx^{\prime}}{|B|^{1+\gamma/n}}\Big\}^{2}\frac{dydt}{t^{n/2\alpha+1}}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\int^{(|x-x_{0}|/2)^{2\alpha}}_{0}\int_{|x-y|<t^{1/2\alpha}}\Big\{\int_{B}\Big(\frac{r}{t^{1/2\alpha}}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}\frac{t^{\beta}}{t^{\beta+n/2\alpha}(1+|y-x_{0}|/t^{1/2\alpha})^{n+2\alpha\beta}}\frac{dx^{\prime}}{|B|^{1+\gamma/n}}\Big\}^{2}\frac{dydt}{t^{n/2\alpha+1}}

\displaystyle\int^{(|x-x_{0}|/2)^{2\alpha}}_{0}\int_{|x-y|<t^{1/2\alpha}}\Big(\frac{r}{t^{1/2\alpha}}\Big)^{2\delta^{\prime}}\frac{1}{t^{n/\alpha}(1+|y-x_{0}|/t^{1/2\alpha})^{2n+4\alpha\beta}}\frac{1}{|B|^{2\gamma/n}}\frac{dydt}{t^{n/2\alpha+1}}.

and
|x-y|<t^{1/2\alpha}

|x-y|<|x-x_{0}|/2

|y-x_{0}|\gtrsim|x-x_{0}|/2
. We have

\displaystyle|I_{1}(x)|

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\int^{(|x-x_{0}|/2)^{2\alpha}}_{0}\int_{|x-y|<t^{1/2\alpha}}\Big(\frac{r}{t^{1/2\alpha}}\Big)^{2\delta^{\prime}}\frac{1}{t^{n/\alpha}(1+|x-x_{0}|/t^{1/2\alpha})^{2n+4\alpha\beta}}\frac{1}{|B|^{2\gamma/n}}\frac{dydt}{t^{n/2\alpha+1}}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\int^{(|x-x_{0}|/2)^{2\alpha}}_{0}\Big(\frac{r}{t^{1/2\alpha}}\Big)^{2\delta^{\prime}}\frac{1}{t^{n/\alpha}(1+|x-x_{0}|/t^{1/2\alpha})^{2n+4\alpha\beta}}\frac{1}{|B|^{2\gamma/n}}\frac{dt}{t}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\frac{r^{2(\delta^{\prime}-\gamma)}}{|x-x_{0}|^{2(n+\delta^{\prime})}},

which, via a direct computation gives

Let us continue with
I_{2}
. Similarly, it follows from Proposition
3.12
that

\displaystyle|I_{2}(x)|

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\int^{\infty}_{|x-x_{0}|^{2\alpha}/2^{2\alpha}}\int_{|x-y|<t^{1/2\alpha}}\Big\{\int_{B}\Big(\frac{|x^{\prime}-x_{0}|}{t^{1/2\alpha}}\Big)^{\delta^{\prime}}\frac{1}{t^{n/2\alpha}}\frac{dx^{\prime}}{|B|^{1+\gamma/n}}\Big\}^{2}\frac{dydt}{t^{n/2\alpha+1}}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\int^{\infty}_{|x-x_{0}|^{2\alpha}/2^{2\alpha}}\int_{|x-y|<t^{1/2\alpha}}\Big(\frac{r}{t^{1/2\alpha}}\Big)^{2\delta^{\prime}}\frac{1}{t^{n/\alpha}}\frac{1}{|B|^{2\gamma/n}}\frac{dydt}{t^{n/2\alpha+1}}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\frac{r^{2(\delta^{\prime}-\gamma)}}{|x-x_{0}|^{2n+2\delta^{\prime}}}.

Hence we still have

\int_{(8B)^{c}}|I_{2}(x)|^{n/2(n+\gamma)}dx\lesssim 1.

Case 2:
\rho(x_{0})/4<r<\rho(x_{0})
.
For this case, the atom

has no canceling condition. We have

\displaystyle|I_{4}(x)|

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\int_{r^{2\alpha}/4^{\alpha}}^{|x-x_{0}|^{2\alpha}/4^{2\alpha}}\int_{|x-y|<t^{1/2\alpha}}\Big\{\int_{B}\frac{t^{\beta}}{(t^{1/2\alpha}+|y-x^{\prime}|)^{n+2\alpha\beta}}\frac{1}{|B|^{1+\gamma/n}}\Big(\frac{\rho(x^{\prime})}{t^{1/2\alpha}}\Big)^{N}dx^{\prime}\Big\}^{2}\frac{dydt}{t^{n/2\alpha+1}}

Lemma 4.5
.

Let
\alpha\in(0,1)
,
q_{t}(\cdot,\cdot)
be a function of
x,y\in\mathbb{R}^{n}
and
t>0
. Assume that for each
N>0
, there exists a constant
C_{N}

such that for
\theta>\gamma
,

|q_{t}(x,y)|\leq C_{N}\Big(1+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}t^{-n/2\alpha}\Big(1+\frac{|x-y|}{t^{1/2\alpha}}\Big)^{-(n+\theta)}.

Then for any
H_{L}^{n/(n+\gamma)}
-atom
a
supported on
B(x_{0},r)
, there exists a constant
C_{x_{0},r}

such that

\sup_{t>0}\Big|\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}q_{t}(x,y)a(y)dy\Big|\leq C_{N,x_{0},r}(1+|x|)^{-n-\theta},x\in\mathbb{R}^{n}.

Proof.

If
x\in B(x_{0},2r)
, then
1+|x|\leq 1+|x-x_{0}|+|x_{0}|\leq 1+2r+|x_{0}|
. It follows from the condition
\|a\|_{\infty}\leq|B(x_{0},r)|^{-1-\gamma/n}
that

\displaystyle\Big|\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}q_{t}(x,y)a(y)dy\Big|

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\int_{B(x_{0},r)}|q_{t}(x,y)||a(y)|dy

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\int_{B(x_{0},r)}t^{-n/2\alpha}\Big(1+\frac{|x-y|}{t^{1/2\alpha}}\Big)^{-n-\theta}r^{-n-\gamma}dy

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle r^{-n-\gamma}\frac{(1+2r+|x_{0}|)^{n+\theta}}{(1+2r+|x_{0}|)^{n+\theta}}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle C_{N,x_{0},r}(1+|x|)^{-n-\theta}.

If
x\notin B(x_{0},2r)
, then for any
y\in B(x_{0},r)
,
|x-y|\sim|x-x_{0}|
. On the other hand,
\rho(y)\sim\rho(x_{0})
since
r<\rho(x_{0})
and
|y-x_{0}|<r
. By Proposition
3.11
, we have

\displaystyle|q_{t}(x,y)|

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle t^{-n/2\alpha}\Big(1+\frac{|x-y|}{t^{1/2\alpha}}\Big)^{-(n+\theta)}\Big(1+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x_{0})}\Big)^{-N}

\displaystyle\Big|\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}q_{t}(x,y)a(y)dy\Big|

\displaystyle\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x_{0})}\Big)^{-\theta}t^{-n/2\alpha}\Big(\frac{|x-x_{0}|}{t^{1/2\alpha}}\Big)^{-(n+\theta)}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}|a(y)|dy

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle(\rho(x_{0}))^{\theta}r^{-\gamma}|x-x_{0}|^{-(n+\theta)}:=C_{\gamma,x_{0},r}|x-x_{0}|^{-(n+2\alpha\beta)}.

Because
x\notin B(x_{0},2r)
, set

, where

. Then

and

\frac{1+|x_{0}|+2r}{2r}|x-x_{0}|=(1+|x_{0}|+2r)|z|\geq 1+|x_{0}|+2r|z|,

which implies that
|x_{0}-x|\geq(1+|x|)/{C_{x_{0},r}}
. This completes the proof of Lemma
4.5
.
∎

Lemma 4.6

\displaystyle|D^{L,\beta}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)|

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\frac{t^{\beta}}{(t^{1/2\alpha}+|x-y|)^{n+2\alpha\beta}}\Big(1+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle t^{-n/2\alpha}\Big(1+\frac{|x-y|}{t^{1/2\alpha}}\Big)^{-(n+2\alpha\beta)}\Big(1+\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x_{0})}\Big)^{-N}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle t^{-n/2\alpha}\Big(1+\frac{|x-y|}{t^{1/2\alpha}}\Big)^{-(n+2\alpha\beta)}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x_{0})}\Big)^{-2\alpha\beta},

which implies that

\displaystyle\Big|\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}D^{L,\beta}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)a(y)dy\Big|

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x_{0})}\Big)^{-2\alpha\beta}t^{-n/2\alpha}\Big(\frac{|x-x_{0}|}{t^{1/2\alpha}}\Big)^{-(n+2\alpha\beta)}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}|a(y)|dy

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle(\rho(x_{0}))^{2\alpha\beta}r^{-\gamma}|x-x_{0}|^{-(n+2\alpha\beta)}:=C_{\gamma,x_{0},r}|x-x_{0}|^{-(n+2\alpha\beta)}.

Because
x\notin B(x_{0},2r)
, set
x=x_{0}+2rz
, where
|z|\geq 1
. Then
1+|x|\leq 1+|x_{0}|+2r|z|
and

\frac{1+|x_{0}|+2r}{2r}|x-x_{0}|=(1+|x_{0}|+2r)|z|\geq 1+|x_{0}|+2r|z|,

which implies that
|x_{0}-x|\geq(1+|x|)/{C_{x_{0},r}}
. The above estimate indicate that
D^{L.\beta}_{\alpha,t}(\cdot,\cdot)
satisfies (
4.5
) with
\theta=2\alpha\beta
. On the other hand,

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}|D^{L,\beta}_{\alpha,t}(f)(x)||D^{L,\beta}_{\alpha,t}(a)(x)|dx

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\Big(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\frac{|f(y)|t^{\beta}}{(|x-y|+t^{1/2\alpha})^{n+2\alpha\beta}}dy\Big)\frac{dx}{(1+|x|)^{n+2\alpha\beta}}\lesssim I_{1}+I_{2},

where

\left\{\begin{aligned} I_{1}:=\iint_{|x-y|>|y|/2}\frac{|f(y)|t^{\beta}}{(|x-y|+t^{1/2\alpha})^{n+2\alpha\beta}}\frac{dxdy}{(1+|x|)^{n+2\alpha\beta}};\\
I_{2}:=\iint_{|x-y|\leq|y|/2}\frac{|f(y)|t^{\beta}}{(|x-y|+t^{1/2\alpha})^{n+2\alpha\beta}}\frac{dxdy}{(1+|x|)^{n+2\alpha\beta}}.\end{aligned}\right.

If
|x-y|<|y|/2
, then
|y|\leq|x-y|+|x|\leq|y|/2+|x|
, i.e.,
|y|\leq 2|x|
.

\displaystyle I_{2}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\Big(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\frac{t^{\beta}}{(|x-y|+t^{1/2\alpha})^{n+2\alpha\beta}}dx\Big)\frac{|f(y)|}{(1+|y|)^{n+2\alpha\beta}}dy<\infty.

For
I_{1}
, we have
|x|\geq|y|/2
since
|x-y|\leq|y|/2
.

\displaystyle I_{1}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\iint_{\mathbb{R}^{n}\times\mathbb{R}^{n}}\frac{|f(y)|t^{\beta}}{(|x-y|+t^{1/2\alpha})^{n+2\alpha\beta}}\frac{dxdy}{(1+|y|)^{n+2\alpha\beta}}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle C_{t}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\Big(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\frac{|f(y)|}{(|y|+1)^{n+2\alpha\beta}}dy\Big)\frac{dx}{(1+|x|)^{n+2\alpha\beta}}<\infty.

Notice that

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}D^{L,\beta}_{\alpha,t}(f)(x)\overline{D^{L,\beta}_{\alpha,t}(a)(x)}dx

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}f(x)\overline{(D^{L,\beta}_{\alpha,t})^{2}(a)(x)}dx

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}f(x)\overline{t^{2\beta}\partial_{t}^{2\beta}e^{-2tL^{\alpha}}(a)(x)}dx,

which, together with the Fubini theorem, indicates that

(4.5)

\displaystyle I

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\lim_{\epsilon\rightarrow 0}\int^{1/\epsilon}_{\epsilon}\Big\{\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}f(y)\overline{t^{2\beta}\partial_{t}^{2\beta}e^{-2tL^{\alpha}}(a)(y)}dy\Big\}\frac{dt}{t}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\lim_{\epsilon\rightarrow 0}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}f(y)\Big\{\int^{1/\epsilon}_{\epsilon}\overline{t^{2\beta}\partial_{t}^{2\beta}e^{-2tL^{\alpha}}(a)(y)}\frac{dt}{t}\Big\}dy.

For the term

\int^{1/\epsilon}_{\epsilon}\overline{t^{2\beta}\partial_{t}^{2\beta}e^{-2tL^{\alpha}}(a)(y)}\frac{dt}{t},

we can see that

\displaystyle\Big|\int^{1/\epsilon}_{\epsilon}{t^{2\beta}\partial_{t}^{2\beta}e^{-2tL^{\alpha}}(a)(y)}\frac{dt}{t}\Big|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\Big|\int^{\infty}_{\epsilon}{t^{2\beta}\partial_{t}^{2\beta}e^{-2tL^{\alpha}}(a)(y)}\frac{dt}{t}\Big|+\Big|\int_{1/\epsilon}^{\infty}{t^{2\beta}\partial_{t}^{2\beta}e^{-2tL^{\alpha}}(a)(y)}\frac{dt}{t}\Big|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\Big|\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\int^{\infty}_{\epsilon}t^{2\beta}\partial^{2\beta}_{t}e^{-2tL^{\alpha}}(x,y)\frac{dt}{t}a(x)dx\Big|

\displaystyle+\Big|\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\int^{\infty}_{1/\epsilon}t^{2\beta}\partial^{2\beta}_{t}e^{-2tL^{\alpha}}(x,y)\frac{dt}{t}a(x)dx\Big|.

By the change of variables, we obtain

\displaystyle\Big|\int^{\infty}_{\epsilon}t^{2\beta}\partial^{2\beta}_{t}e^{-2tL^{\alpha}}(x,y)\frac{dt}{t}\Big|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\Big|C_{\beta}\int^{\infty}_{\epsilon}t^{2\beta}\int^{\infty}_{0}\partial_{t}^{m}e^{-(2t+s)L^{\alpha}}(x,y)\frac{ds}{s^{1+2\beta-m}}\frac{dt}{t}\Big|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\Big|C_{\beta}\int^{\infty}_{\epsilon}t^{2\beta}\int^{\infty}_{0}L^{\alpha m}e^{-(2t+s)L^{\alpha}}(x,y)\frac{ds}{s^{1+2\beta-m}}\frac{dt}{t}\Big|

\displaystyle\simeq

\displaystyle\Big|C_{\beta}\int^{\infty}_{2\epsilon}t^{2\beta}\int^{\infty}_{0}\partial_{t}^{m}e^{-(t+s)L^{\alpha}}(x,y)\frac{ds}{s^{1+2\beta-m}}\frac{dt}{t}\Big|

\displaystyle\simeq

\displaystyle\Big|\int^{\infty}_{2\epsilon}t^{2\beta}\partial^{2\beta}_{t}e^{-tL^{\alpha}}(x,y)\frac{dt}{t}\Big|.

The rest of the proof is divided into three cases.

Case 1:
2\beta<1
.
For this case,
[2\beta]+1=1
. Then a change of variable reaches

\displaystyle\int^{\infty}_{2\epsilon}t^{2\beta}\partial^{2\beta}_{t}e^{-tL^{\alpha}}(x,y)\frac{dt}{t}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int^{\infty}_{2\epsilon}t^{2\beta}\int^{\infty}_{0}\partial_{t}e^{-(t+s)L^{\alpha}}(x,y)\frac{ds}{s^{2\beta}}\frac{dt}{t}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int^{\infty}_{2\epsilon}t^{2\beta}\int^{\infty}_{t}\partial_{u}e^{-uL^{\alpha}}(x,y)\frac{du}{(u-t)^{2\beta}}\frac{dt}{t}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int^{\infty}_{2\epsilon}\partial_{u}e^{-uL^{\alpha}}(x,y)\Big(\int^{u}_{2\epsilon}\Big(\frac{t}{u-t}\Big)^{2\beta}\frac{dt}{t}\Big)du

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int^{\infty}_{2\epsilon}\partial_{u}e^{-uL^{\alpha}}(x,y)\Big(\int^{1}_{2\epsilon/u}\Big(\frac{w}{1-w}\Big)^{2\beta}\frac{dw}{w}\Big)du.

Notice that

e^{-L^{\alpha}}(x,y)\int^{1}_{2\epsilon/2\epsilon}\Big(\frac{w}{1-w}\Big)^{2\beta}\frac{dw}{w}=0,

and as
u\rightarrow\infty
,

|e^{-uL^{\alpha}}(x,y)|\leq\frac{u}{(u^{1/2\alpha}+|x-y|)^{n+2\alpha}}\leq\frac{1}{u^{n/2\alpha}}\rightarrow 0.

An application of integration by parts gives

\displaystyle\int^{\infty}_{2\epsilon}t^{2\beta}\partial^{2\beta}_{t}e^{-tL^{\alpha}}(x,y)\frac{dt}{t}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\int^{\infty}_{2\epsilon}e^{-uL^{\alpha}}(x,y)\frac{\partial}{\partial u}\Big(\int^{1}_{2\epsilon/u}\Big(\frac{w}{1-w}\Big)^{2\beta}\frac{dw}{w}\Big)du

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int^{\infty}_{2\epsilon}e^{-uL^{\alpha}}(x,y)\Big(\frac{2\epsilon}{u-2\epsilon}\Big)^{2\beta}\frac{du}{u}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle I+II,

where

\left\{\begin{aligned} I&:=\int^{\infty}_{2\epsilon}e^{-uL^{\alpha}}(x,y)\Big(\frac{2\epsilon}{u-2\epsilon}\Big)^{2\beta}\chi_{A}(u)\frac{du}{u};\\
II&:=\int^{\infty}_{2\epsilon}e^{-uL^{\alpha}}(x,y)\Big(\frac{2\epsilon}{u-2\epsilon}\Big)^{2\beta}\chi_{A^{c}}(u)\frac{du}{u},\end{aligned}\right.

where
A:=\{u:\ u-2\epsilon\leq\epsilon+|x-y|^{2\alpha}\}
. By Proposition
3.1
,

\displaystyle|I|

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\int^{\infty}_{2\epsilon}\frac{u}{(u^{1/2\alpha}+|x-y|)^{n+2\alpha}}\Big(1+\frac{u^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{u^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{2\epsilon}{u-2\epsilon}\Big)^{2\beta}\chi_{A}(u)\frac{du}{u}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\frac{\epsilon^{2\beta}}{(\epsilon^{1/2\alpha}+|x-y|)^{n+2\alpha}}\Big(1+\frac{\epsilon^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{\epsilon^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}\int^{3\epsilon+|x-y|^{2\alpha}}_{2\epsilon}(u-2\epsilon)^{-2\beta}du

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\epsilon^{n/2\alpha}}\frac{1}{(1+|x-y|/\epsilon^{1/2\alpha})^{n+4\alpha\beta}}\Big(1+\frac{\epsilon^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{\epsilon^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}.

For
II
,

\displaystyle|II|

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\int^{\infty}_{2\epsilon}\frac{u}{(u^{1/2\alpha}+|x-y|)^{n+2\alpha}}\Big(1+\frac{\epsilon^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{\epsilon^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{2\epsilon}{u-2\epsilon}\Big)^{2\beta}\chi_{A^{c}}(u)\frac{du}{u}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\int^{\infty}_{3\epsilon+|x-y|^{2\alpha}}\frac{u}{(u^{1/2\alpha}+|x-y|)^{n+2\alpha}}\Big(1+\frac{\epsilon^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{\epsilon^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}\Big(\frac{2\epsilon}{\epsilon+|x-y|^{2\alpha}}\Big)^{2\beta}\frac{du}{u}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\Big(\frac{2\epsilon}{\epsilon+|x-y|^{2\alpha}}\Big)^{2\beta}\Big(1+\frac{\epsilon^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{\epsilon^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}\int^{\infty}_{3\epsilon+|x-y|^{2\alpha}}(u^{1/2\alpha}+|x-y|)^{-n-2\alpha}du

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\epsilon^{n/2\alpha}}\frac{1}{(1+|x-y|/\epsilon^{1/2\alpha})^{n+4\alpha\beta}}\Big(1+\frac{\epsilon^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{\epsilon^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}.

Case 2:
2\beta=1
.
A direct computation gives

\displaystyle\Big|\int^{\infty}_{2\epsilon}t\partial_{t}e^{-tL^{\alpha}}(x,y)\frac{dt}{t}\Big|

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle|e^{-\epsilon L^{\alpha}}(x,y)|

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\epsilon^{n/2\alpha}}\frac{1}{(1+{|x-y|}/{\epsilon^{1/2\alpha}})^{n+2\alpha}}\Big(1+\frac{\epsilon^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{\epsilon^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}.

Case 3:
2\beta>1
.
Let
k\in\mathbb{Z}_{+}
such that
k-1<2\beta\leq k
,
k\geq 2
.
We obtain

\displaystyle M

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int^{\infty}_{2\epsilon}t^{2\beta}\partial^{2\beta}_{t}e^{-tL^{\alpha}}(x,y)\frac{dt}{t}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int^{\infty}_{2\epsilon}t^{2\beta}\Big(\int^{\infty}_{0}\partial^{k}_{t}e^{-(t+s)L^{\alpha}}(x,y)\frac{ds}{s^{1+2\beta-k}}\Big)\frac{dt}{t}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int^{\infty}_{2\epsilon}t^{2\beta}L^{\alpha k}\Big(\int^{\infty}_{0}e^{-(t+s)L^{\alpha}}(x,y)\frac{ds}{s^{1+2\beta-k}}\Big)\frac{dt}{t}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int^{\infty}_{2\epsilon}t^{2\beta}\Big(\int^{\infty}_{t}L^{\alpha k}e^{-uL^{\alpha}}(x,y)\frac{du}{(u-t)^{1+2\beta-k}}\Big)\frac{dt}{t}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int^{\infty}_{2\epsilon}t^{2\beta}\Big(\int^{\infty}_{t}\partial_{u}^{k}e^{-uL^{\alpha}}(x,y)\frac{du}{(u-t)^{1+2\beta-k}}\Big)\frac{dt}{t}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int^{\infty}_{2\epsilon}\partial_{u}^{k}e^{-uL^{\alpha}}(x,y)\Big(\int^{u}_{2\epsilon}t^{2\beta}(u-t)^{k-2\beta-1}\frac{dt}{t}\Big)du

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int^{\infty}_{2\epsilon}u^{k-1}\partial_{u}^{k}e^{-uL^{\alpha}}(x,y)\Big(\int^{1}_{2\epsilon/u}w^{2\beta}(1-w)^{k-2\beta-1}\frac{dw}{w}\Big)du,

where in the last step we have used the change of variables:
w=t/u
. Notice that

\displaystyle u^{k-1}\partial_{u}^{k}e^{-uL^{\alpha}}(x,y)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\partial_{u}(u^{k-1}\partial_{u}^{k-1}e^{-uL^{\alpha}}(x,y))-(k-1)\partial_{u}(u^{k-2}\partial_{u}^{k-2}e^{-uL^{\alpha}}(x,y))

\displaystyle+\cdots+(-1)^{k-1}(k-1)!e^{-uL^{\alpha}}(x,y).

Then the integration by parts yields
M=\sum^{k-1}_{m=1}C_{m}I_{m}
, where
C_{m}=(-1)^{m-1}(k-1)!/(k-m+1)!
and

I_{m}:=\int^{\infty}_{2\epsilon}u^{m}\partial_{u}^{m}e^{-uL^{\alpha}}(x,y)\frac{(2\epsilon)^{2\beta}u^{1-k}}{(u-2\epsilon)^{1+2\beta-k}}\frac{du}{u}.

We obtain

\displaystyle|I_{m}|\lesssim\int^{\infty}_{2\epsilon}\frac{u^{m}}{(u^{1/2\alpha}+|x-y|)^{n+2\alpha m}}\Big(1+\frac{u^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{u^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}\frac{(2\epsilon)^{2\beta}u^{1-k}}{(u-2\epsilon)^{1+2\beta-k}}\frac{du}{u}\lesssim I_{m}^{(1)}+I^{(2)}_{m},

where

\left\{\begin{aligned} I_{m}^{(1)}&:=&\frac{\epsilon^{2\beta}}{(\epsilon^{1/2\alpha}+|x-y|)^{n+2\alpha m}}\Big(1+\frac{\epsilon^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{\epsilon^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}\int^{3\epsilon}_{2\epsilon}\frac{1}{(u-2\epsilon)^{1+2\beta-k}}\frac{du}{u^{k-m}};\\
I_{m}^{(2)}&:=&\frac{\epsilon^{2\beta}}{(\epsilon^{1/2\alpha}+|x-y|)^{n+2\alpha m}}\Big(1+\frac{\epsilon^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{\epsilon^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}\int^{\infty}_{3\epsilon}\frac{1}{(u-2\epsilon)^{1+2\beta-k}}\frac{du}{u^{k-m}}.\end{aligned}\right.

For
I^{(1)}_{m}
, since
2\epsilon<u<3\epsilon
, we get

\displaystyle I_{m}^{(1)}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\frac{\epsilon^{2\beta}}{(\epsilon^{1/2\alpha}+|x-y|)^{n+2\alpha m}}\Big(1+\frac{\epsilon^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{\epsilon^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}\frac{1}{\epsilon^{k-m}}\int^{3\epsilon}_{2\epsilon}\frac{du}{(u-2\epsilon)^{1+2\beta-k}}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\frac{\epsilon^{2\beta}}{(\epsilon^{1/2\alpha}+|x-y|)^{n+2\alpha m}}\Big(1+\frac{\epsilon^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{\epsilon^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}\frac{1}{\epsilon^{k-m}}\epsilon^{k-2\beta}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\epsilon^{n/2\alpha}}\frac{1}{(1+|x-y|/\epsilon^{1/2\alpha})^{n+2\alpha m}}\Big(1+\frac{\epsilon^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{\epsilon^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}.

Similarly, for
I^{(2)}_{m}
, because
u\in(3\epsilon,\infty)
, then
1/u\lesssim 1/(u-2\epsilon)
. Noticing that
m<2\beta
, we obtain

\displaystyle I^{(2)}_{m}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\frac{\epsilon^{2\beta}}{(\epsilon^{1/2\alpha}+|x-y|)^{n+2\alpha m}}\Big(1+\frac{\epsilon^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{\epsilon^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}\int_{3\epsilon}^{\infty}\frac{du}{(u-2\epsilon)^{1+2\beta-m}}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\frac{\epsilon^{2\beta}}{(\epsilon^{1/2\alpha}+|x-y|)^{n+2\alpha m}}\Big(1+\frac{\epsilon^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{\epsilon^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}\epsilon^{m-2\beta}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\epsilon^{n/2\alpha}}\frac{1}{(1+|x-y|/\epsilon^{1/2\alpha})^{n+2\alpha m}}\Big(1+\frac{\epsilon^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}+\frac{\epsilon^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(y)}\Big)^{-N}.

By Lemma
4.5
, the above estimates in Cases 1-3 indciate that

\sup_{\epsilon>0}\Big|\int^{1/\epsilon}_{\epsilon}{t^{2\beta}\partial_{t}^{2\beta}e^{-2tL^{\alpha}}(a)(y)}\frac{dt}{t}\Big|\lesssim(1+|y|)^{-(n+\gamma+\epsilon)},

Therefore we can use Lemma
4.1
completes the proof.
∎

Finally, we can obtain the following characterization of
BMO^{\gamma}_{L}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
corresponding to the time-fractional derivative.

Theorem 4.7
.

Let
V\in B_{q},q>n
. Assume that
\alpha\in(0,1)
,
\beta>0
,
0<\gamma\leq 1
with

0<\gamma<\min\{2\alpha,2\beta\alpha\}.

Let
f
be a function such that

(4.6)

\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\frac{|f(x)|}{(1+|x|)^{n+\gamma+\epsilon}}dx<\infty

for some
\epsilon>0
. The following statements are equivalent:

(i)

f\in BMO^{\gamma}_{L}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
;

(ii)
There exists
C_{\alpha,\beta}
such that
\|D^{L,\beta}_{\alpha,t}(f)\|_{\infty}\leq C_{\alpha,\beta}t^{\gamma/2\alpha}
;

(iii)
For all
B=B(x_{B},r_{B})\subset\mathbb{R}^{n}
,

(4.7)

\Big(\frac{1}{|B|}\int^{r_{B}^{2\alpha}}_{0}\int_{B}|D^{L,\beta}_{\alpha,t}(f)(x)|^{2}\frac{dxdt}{t}\Big)^{1/2}\lesssim|B|^{\gamma/n}.

Proof.

(i)
\Longrightarrow
(ii). If
f\in BMO^{\gamma}_{L}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
, then
|t^{\beta}\partial^{\beta}_{t}e^{-tL^{\alpha}}f(x)|\lesssim I+II
, where

\left\{\begin{aligned} &I:=\Big|\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}D^{L,\beta}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)(f(y)-f(x))dy\Big|;\\
&II:=\Big|f(x)\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}D^{L,\beta}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)dy\Big|.\end{aligned}\right.

For
I
, we have

\displaystyle I

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\|f\|_{BMO^{\gamma}_{L}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\frac{t^{\beta}|x-y|^{\gamma}}{(t^{1/2\alpha}+|x-y|)^{n+2\alpha\beta}}dy\lesssim t^{\gamma/2\alpha}\|f\|_{BMO^{\gamma}_{L}}.

We further divide the estimation of
II
into the following two cases.

Case 1:
\rho(x)\leq t^{1/2\alpha}
.
By Proposition
3.11
,

\displaystyle II

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\|f\|_{BMO^{\gamma}_{L}}\rho(x)^{\gamma}\Big|\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}D^{L,\beta}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)dy\Big|

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\|f\|_{BMO^{\gamma}_{L}}t^{\gamma/2\alpha}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\frac{t^{\beta}}{(t^{1/2\alpha}+|x-y|)^{n+2\alpha\beta}}dy

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\|f\|_{BMO^{\gamma}_{L}}t^{\gamma/2\alpha}.

Case 2:
\rho(x)>t^{1/2\alpha}
.
We use Proposition
3.13
to obtain that, there exists
\delta^{\prime}>\gamma
such that

\displaystyle II

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\|f\|_{BMO^{\gamma}_{L}}\rho(x)^{\gamma}\Big|\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}D^{L,\beta}_{\alpha,t}(x,y)dy\Big|

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\|f\|_{BMO^{\gamma}_{L}}\rho(x)^{\gamma}\frac{(t^{1/2\alpha}/\rho(x))^{\delta^{\prime}}}{(1+t^{1/2\alpha}/\rho(x))^{N}}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\|f\|_{BMO^{\gamma}_{L}}t^{\gamma/2\alpha}\frac{(t^{1/2\alpha}/\rho(x))^{\delta^{\prime}-\gamma}}{(1+t^{1/2\alpha}/\rho(x))^{N}}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\|f\|_{BMO^{\gamma}_{L}}t^{\gamma/2\alpha}.

(ii)
\Longrightarrow
(iii). Assume that (ii) holds.
Then

\displaystyle\Big(\frac{1}{|B|}\int^{r_{B}^{2\alpha}}_{0}\int_{B}|D^{L,\beta}_{\alpha,t}(f)(x)|^{2}\frac{dxdt}{t}\Big)^{1/2}\lesssim\|f\|_{BMO^{\gamma}_{L}}\Big(\frac{1}{|B|}\int^{r_{B}^{2\alpha}}_{0}\int_{B}t^{\gamma/\alpha}\frac{dxdt}{t}\Big)^{1/2}\lesssim\|f\|_{BMO^{\gamma}_{L}}|B|^{\gamma/n}.

(iii)
\Longrightarrow
(i). Assume that (
4.7
) holds. Let
a
be an
H^{n/(n+\gamma)}_{L}
-atom associated with
B=B(x_{B},r_{B})
. Then by Lemma
4.6
,

\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}f(x)\overline{a(x)}dx\simeq\iint_{\mathbb{R}^{n+1}_{+}}t^{\beta}\partial^{\beta}_{t}e^{-tL^{\alpha}}(f)(x)\overline{t^{\beta}\partial^{\beta}_{t}e^{-tL^{\alpha}}(a)(x)}\frac{dt}{t},

which, together with (
4.3
) and Theorem
4.4
, gives

\displaystyle\Big|\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}f(x)\overline{a(x)}dx\Big|

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\sup_{B}\Big(\frac{1}{|B|^{1+2\gamma/n}}\int^{r_{B}^{2\alpha}}_{0}\int_{B}|t^{\beta}\partial_{t}^{\beta}e^{-tL^{\alpha}}(f)(x)|^{2}\frac{dxdt}{t}\Big)^{1/2}

\displaystyle\quad\times\Bigg\{\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\Big(\iint_{|x-y|<s^{1/2\alpha}}|t^{\beta}\partial^{\beta}e^{-tL^{\alpha}}(a)(y)|^{2}\frac{t^{1/2\alpha-1}dyds}{t^{n/2\alpha+1}}\Big)^{n/2(n+\gamma)}dx\Bigg\}^{1+\gamma/n}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\|S^{L}_{\alpha,\beta}(a)\|_{L^{n/(n+\gamma)}}\sup_{B}\Big(\frac{1}{|B|^{1+2\gamma/n}}\int^{r_{B}^{2\alpha}}_{0}\int_{B}|t^{\beta}\partial_{t}^{\beta}e^{-tL^{\alpha}}(f)(x)|^{2}\frac{dxdt}{t}\Big)^{1/2}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\|a\|_{H^{n/(n+\gamma)}_{L}}.

Hence

T(g):=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}f(x)\overline{g(x)}dx,\ g\in H^{n/(n+\gamma)}_{L}(\mathbb{R}^{n}),

is a bounded linear functional on
H^{n/(n+\gamma)}_{L}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
, equivalently,
f\in\big(H_{L}^{n/(n+\gamma)}(\mathbb{R}^{n})\big)^{*}=BMO^{\gamma}_{L}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
.

∎

Below we consider the characterization of
BMO^{\gamma}_{L}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
via the the spatial gradient. Define a general gradient as
\nabla_{\alpha}:=(\nabla_{x},\partial_{t}^{1/2\alpha})
.

Theorem 4.8
.

Let
V\in B_{q},q>n
. Assume that
\alpha\in(0,1/2-n/2q)
,
\beta>0
and
0<\gamma\leq 1
with

0<\gamma<\min\{2\alpha,2\alpha\beta\}.

Let
f
be a function satisfying (
4.6
). The following statements are equivalent:

(i)

f\in BMO^{\gamma}_{L}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
;

(ii)
There exists a constant
C>0
such that

\|t^{1/2\alpha}\nabla_{\alpha}e^{-tL^{\alpha}}f\|_{\infty}\leq Ct^{\gamma/2\alpha};

(iii)

u(x,t)=e^{-tL^{\alpha}}f(x)
satisfies that, for any balls
B=B(x_{B},r_{B}),

(4.8)

{1\over{|B|}^{1+2\gamma/n}}\int^{r^{2\alpha}_{B}}_{0}\int_{B}|t^{1/2\alpha}\nabla_{\alpha}e^{-tL^{\alpha}}(f)(x)|^{2}\frac{dxdt}{t}\leq C.

Proof.

(i)
\Longrightarrow
(ii). Let
f\in BMO^{\gamma}_{L}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
. By Theorem
4.7
,

\|t^{1/2\alpha}\partial^{1/2\alpha}_{t}e^{-tL^{\alpha}}(f)\|_{\infty}\leq C_{\alpha,\beta}t^{\gamma/2\alpha}.

One writes

\displaystyle t^{1/2\alpha}\nabla_{x}e^{-tL^{\alpha}}f(x)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}t^{1/2\alpha}\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{\alpha,t}\Big(f(z)-f(x)\Big)dz+f(x)t^{1/2\alpha}\nabla_{x}e^{-tL^{\alpha}}(1)(x):=I(x)+II(x).

We first estimate the term
I
. Because
f\in BMO^{\gamma}_{L}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
, then

|f(x)-f(z)|\leq\|f\|_{BMO^{\gamma}_{L}}|x-z|^{\gamma}.
Since

|t^{1/2\alpha}\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x,z)|\lesssim\frac{t}{(t^{1/2\alpha}+|x-z|)^{n+2\alpha}},

a direct computation gives

\displaystyle|I(x)|

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\|f\|_{BMO^{\gamma}_{L}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}|t^{1/2\alpha}\nabla_{x}K^{L}_{\alpha,t}(x,z)|\cdot|x-z|^{\gamma}dz

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\|f\|_{BMO^{\gamma}_{L}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\frac{t|x-z|^{\gamma}}{(t^{1/2\alpha}+|x-z|)^{n+2\alpha}}dz

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle t^{\gamma/2\alpha}\|f\|_{BMO^{\gamma}_{L}}.

By Proposition
3.10
, we have

|t^{1/2\alpha}\nabla_{x}e^{-tL^{\alpha}}(1)|\lesssim\min\Big\{(t^{1/2\alpha}/\rho(x))^{1+2\alpha},\ (t^{1/2\alpha}/\rho(x))^{-N}\Big\}.

The estimate of
II
is divided into two cases.

Case 1:
\rho(x)\leq t^{1/2\alpha}
.

f\in BMO^{\gamma}_{L}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
implies that

|f(x)|\lesssim\rho^{\gamma}(x)\|f\|_{BMO^{\gamma}_{L}}
. Then

\displaystyle II(x)

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\|f\|_{BMO^{\gamma}_{L}}\rho^{\gamma}(x)|t^{1/2\alpha}\nabla_{x}e^{-tL^{\alpha}}(1)(x)|

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\|f\|_{BMO^{\gamma}_{L}}\rho^{\gamma}(x)\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\|f\|_{BMO^{\gamma}_{L}}t^{\gamma/2\alpha}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{-N-\gamma}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\|f\|_{BMO^{\gamma}_{L}}t^{\gamma/2\alpha}\|f\|_{BMO^{\gamma}_{L}}.

Case 2:
\rho(x)>t
.
We can get

\displaystyle II(x)

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\rho^{\gamma}(x)\|f\|_{BMO^{\gamma}_{L}}|t^{1/2\alpha}\nabla_{x}e^{-tL^{\alpha}}(1)(x)|

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\rho^{\gamma}(x)\|f\|_{BMO^{\gamma}_{L}}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{1+2\alpha}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle t^{\gamma/2\alpha}\|f\|_{BMO^{\gamma}_{L}}\Big(\frac{t^{1/2\alpha}}{\rho(x)}\Big)^{1+2\alpha-\gamma}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle t^{\gamma/2\alpha}\|f\|_{BMO^{\gamma}_{L}}.

(ii)
\Longrightarrow
(iii). For every ball
B=B(x_{B},r_{B})
,

\displaystyle\int^{r^{2\alpha}_{B}}_{0}\int_{B}\Big|t^{1/2\alpha}\nabla_{\alpha}e^{-tL^{\alpha}}f(x)\Big|^{2}\frac{dxdt}{t}

) holds.

A positive measure
\nu
on
\mathbb{R}^{n+1}_{+}
is called a
\kappa
-Carleson measure if

\|\nu\|_{\mathcal{C}}:=\sup_{x\in\mathbb{R}^{n},r>0}\frac{\nu(B(x,r)\times(0,r))}{|B(x,r)|^{\kappa}}<\infty.

The following result can be deduced from Theorem
4.8
immediately.

Theorem 4.9
.

Let
V\in B_{q},q>n
. Assume that
\alpha\in(0,1/2-n/2q)
,
\beta>0
and
0<\gamma\leq 1
with

0<\gamma<\min\{2\alpha,2\alpha\beta\}.

Let
d\nu_{\alpha}
be a measure defined by

d\nu_{\alpha}(x,t):=|t\nabla e^{-t^{2\alpha}L^{\alpha}}(f)(x)|^{2}dxdt/t,\ (x,t)\in\mathbb{R}^{n+1}_{+}.

(i)
If
f\in BMO^{\gamma}_{L}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
, then
d\nu_{\alpha}
is a
(1+2\gamma/n)
-Carleson measure;

(ii)
Conversely, if
f\in L^{1}((1+|x|)^{-n-1}dx)
and
d\nu_{\alpha}
is a
(1+2\gamma/n)
-Carleson measure, then
f\in BMO^{\gamma}_{L}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
.

Moreover, in any case, there exists a constant
C>0
such that

C^{-1}\|f\|^{2}_{BMO^{\gamma}_{L}}\leq\|d\nu_{\alpha}\|_{\mathcal{C}}\leq C\|f\|^{2}_{BMO^{\gamma}_{L}}.

Proof.

(i). In Theorem
4.7
, letting
\beta=1
, we obtain for
f\in BMO^{\gamma}_{L}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
,

\frac{1}{|B|^{1+2\gamma/n}}\int^{r^{2\alpha}}_{0}\int_{B}|t\partial_{t}e^{-tL^{\alpha}}(f)(x)|^{2}\frac{dxdt}{t}\lesssim\|f\|^{2}_{BMO^{\gamma}_{L}},

which, together with a change of variable, gives

\frac{1}{|B|^{1+2\gamma/n}}\int^{r}_{0}\int_{B}|t\partial_{t}e^{-t^{2\alpha}L^{\alpha}}(f)(x)|^{2}\frac{dxdt}{t}=\frac{1}{|B|^{1+2\gamma/n}}\int^{r}_{0}\int_{B}|t^{2\alpha}L^{\alpha}e^{-t^{2\alpha}L^{\alpha}}(f)(x)|^{2}\frac{dxdt}{t}\\
=\frac{1}{|B|^{1+2\gamma/n}}\int^{r^{2\alpha}}_{0}\int_{B}|t\partial_{t}e^{-tL^{\alpha}}(f)(x)|^{2}\frac{dxdt}{t}\lesssim\|f\|^{2}_{BMO^{\gamma}_{L}}.

The estimation

\frac{1}{|B|^{1+2\gamma/n}}\int^{r_{B}}_{0}\int_{B}|t\nabla_{x}e^{-t^{2\alpha}L^{\alpha}}f(x)|^{2}\frac{dxdt}{t}\lesssim\|f\|^{2}_{BMO^{\gamma}_{L}}

can be obtained in the manner of Theorem
4.7
.

(ii). Assume that
d\nu_{\alpha}
is a
(1+2\gamma/n)
-Carleson measure, i.e., for any ball
B(x,r)
,

\sup_{B}\frac{1}{|B|^{1+2\gamma/n}}\int^{r_{B}}_{0}\int_{B}|t\nabla e^{-t^{2\alpha}L^{\alpha}}(f)(x)|^{2}\frac{dxdt}{t}<\infty.

Subsequently,

\sup_{B}\frac{1}{|B|^{1+2\gamma/n}}\int^{r_{B}}_{0}\int_{B}|t\partial_{t}e^{-t^{2\alpha}L^{\alpha}}(f)(x)|^{2}\frac{dxdt}{t}<\infty.

It can be deduced from Theorem
4.8
that
f\in BMO^{\gamma}_{L}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
.
∎
