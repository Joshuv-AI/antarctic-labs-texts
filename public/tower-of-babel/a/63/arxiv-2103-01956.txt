A zero density estimate and fractional imaginary parts of zeros for GL 2 \operatorname{GL}_{2} L L -functions

Abstract
. We prove an analogue of Selberg’s zero density estimate for ζ ⁡ ( s ) \zeta(s) that holds for any GL 2 \operatorname{GL}_{2} L L -function. 1.1.
Main results

Let

N(T):=N(0,T)\sim(1/\pi)T\log T

N(\sigma,T)\ll T^{1-\frac{1}{4}(\sigma-\frac{1}{2})}\log T,\qquad\sigma\geq\tfrac{1}{2}.

\frac{1}{N(T)}\#\Big\{\rho=\beta+i\gamma\colon\zeta(\rho)=0,~|\gamma|\leq T,~\beta\in\Big[\frac{1}{2}-\frac{4\log\log T}{\log T},\frac{1}{2}+\frac{4\log\log T}{\log T}\Big]\Big\}\ll\frac{1}{\log T}.

As an application of (
1.2

\lim_{T\to\infty}\frac{1}{T}\textup{meas}\Big\{T\leq t\leq 2T\colon\log|\zeta(\tfrac{1}{2}+it)|\geq V\sqrt{\tfrac{1}{2}\log\log T}\Big\}\sim\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}\int_{V}^{\infty}e^{-u^{2}/2}du.

1.2.
Application to “zero races”

In a letter to Fuss, Chebyshev observed that the generalized Riemann hypothesis implies that primes
p\equiv 3\pmod{4}
tend to be more numerous than primes
p\equiv 1\pmod{4}
. Using the generalized Riemann hypothesis and other hypotheses, Rubinstein and Sarnak
[
RS94
]
began a systematic study of “prime number races” in which they determine how often
\pi(x;4,3)>\pi(x;4,1)
, where
\pi(x;q,a)
equals
\#\{p\leq x\colon p\equiv a\pmod{q}\}
. They proved that

\lim_{X\to\infty}\frac{1}{\log X}\int_{\begin{subarray}{c}2\leq t\leq X\\
\pi(t;4,3)>\pi(t;4,1)\end{subarray}}\frac{dt}{t}=0.9959\ldots

Thus the “bias” toward primes of the form
4n+3
is quite strong. The literature on prime number races which study such inequities is quite vast. See, for instance, the work of Fiorilli, Ford, Harper, Konyagin, Lamzouri, and Martin
[
Fiorilli_Martin
,
FK02
,
FLK13
,
FHL19
]
.

Proof of Theorem

.

to interpolate the bounds in Corollary

, we have

\displaystyle\int_{T/2}^{T}|L(\sigma+it,\pi)M_{\pi}(\sigma+it,T^{\varpi})-1|^{2}dt

\displaystyle\ll T^{\frac{1+A-\sigma}{1+A-(\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{\log T})}}(T^{1+(\theta-A-\frac{1}{2})\varpi}(\log T)^{15})^{1-\frac{1+A-\sigma}{1+A-(\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{\log T})}}\ll T^{1-c(\sigma-\frac{1}{2})}.

. By construction, if

For any

be the rectangular contour with corners

,
\sigma+iT
,

, and

[

]

, we have

\displaystyle\int_{\sigma}^{1}(N_{\pi}(\sigma^{\prime},T)-N_{\pi}(\sigma^{\prime},\tfrac{T}{2}))d\sigma^{\prime}\leq\lim_{M\to\infty}\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{C_{M}}\log\Phi(s)ds

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{T/2}^{T}\log|\Phi(\sigma+it)|dt+\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{\sigma}^{\infty}\arg(\Phi(x+iT))dx-\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{\sigma}^{\infty}\arg(\Phi(x+\tfrac{iT}{2}))dx.

In view of the bound
\log|1+z|\leq|z|

For the second and third integrals, we consider the integrals over

and over

separately.

By the preceding work, it follows by dyadic decomposition that
