Hydrogen and hydrogen-like-ion bound states and hyperfine splittings: finite nuclear size effects
Igor Kuzmenko 1,2 , Tetyana Kuzmenko 1 , Y. Avishai 1,3 , Y. B. Band 1,2,4

Abstract
Using the Dirac equation, we study corrections to electron binding energies and hyperfine splittings of atomic hydrogen and hydrogen-like ions due to finite nuclear size (FNS) effects, relativistic QED radiative corrections and nuclear recoil corrections. Three models for the charge distribution and the magnetic moment distribution within the nucleus are considered. Calculations are carried for light atoms (H, He and K) and heavy atoms (Rb, Cs, Pb, Bi, U). The FNS corrections to the ground-state energy are shown to be smaller than the electron-nucleus reduced mass corrections, and comparable to the relativistic QED radiative corrections for the light nuclei, but much larger than both these corrections for heavy nuclei. Comparison is made with an experiment on the 1 ​ s 1s - 2 ​ s 2s transition frequency for hydrogen. FNS corrections to the ground state hyperfine splitting are comparable in size to the relativistic QED radiative corrections for light nuclei, but are larger for heavy nuclei.

Equation (

the electron mass

defined in Eq. (

This naive expression can be used as a starting

An explicit expression for the

Eides-hydrogen-atom

\displaystyle\varepsilon_{n}^{\mathrm{(Dirac)}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle m_{e}c^{2}+Z^{2}hcR_{\infty}\times

(53)

\displaystyle\Big[\frac{2\mathcal{G}(n)}{1+\mu_{ep}}-\frac{\mu_{ep}\,Z^{2}\alpha^{2}\,\mathcal{G}^{2}(n)}{\big(1+\mu_{ep}\big)^{3}}\Big].

The dimensionless function

is given in Eq. (

The first two terms on the right hand side of Eq. (

in

\displaystyle\tilde{\varepsilon}_{n}^{\mathrm{(Dirac)}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle m_{e}c^{2}-\frac{hcR_{\infty}}{1+\mu_{ep}}\,\bigg\{\frac{Z^{2}}{n^{2}}+\frac{Z^{4}\alpha^{2}}{n^{3}}\,\Big(1-\frac{3}{4n}

(54)

\displaystyle+\frac{\mu_{ep}}{(1+\mu_{ep})^{2}}\,\frac{1}{4n}\Big)\bigg\}.

Here we find the wave functions and binding energies

We rewrite Eq. (

\displaystyle\frac{\hbar c\,r^{2}g^{\prime}_{-}(r)}{mc^{2}-V_{b}(r)+\varepsilon}=r^{2}f_{b,-}(r),

\displaystyle\frac{1}{r^{2}}\,\frac{d}{dr}\bigg(\frac{r^{2}}{mc^{2}-V_{b}(r)+\varepsilon}\,\frac{dg_{b,-}(r)}{dr}\bigg)

f_{b,-}(r)=\frac{\hbar c\,g^{\prime}_{b,-}(r)}{mc^{2}-V_{b}(r)+\varepsilon}.

(57)

(58)

The radial wave functions

IV
Perturbation Theory Corrections due to FNS

In this section we apply perturbation theory for models (a) and (b)
and find the corrections to the ground-state energies of H&HLI (assuming the
potentials
W_{\nu}(r)
and
W_{ba}(r)
are small).

To be specific, we will concentrate on the ground electronic state,

Since an analytic solution to the Dirac equation for a harmonic potential is not known,

The first order correction
\varepsilon_{ba}^{(1)}

\displaystyle\varepsilon_{ba}^{(1)}

\displaystyle=

where
g_{\pm}(r)
and
f_{\pm}(r)

for the model (a). The functions

g_{+}(r)
and
f_{+}(r)
are given in Eq. (
38
), and

g_{-}(r)

A_{\pm}

\displaystyle\mathcal{N}_{a}

\displaystyle 4\pi A_{-}^{2}\int\limits_{0}^{r_{a}}\Big[g_{-}^{2}(r)+f_{-}^{2}(r)\Big]r^{2}dr+

In order to calculate
\varepsilon_{ba}^{(1)}
,

73
)

\varrho_{a}(r)

\varrho_{a}(r)=\left\{\begin{array}[]{cc}\displaystyle\frac{4\pi A_{-}^{2}}{\mathcal{N}}\,\Big[g_{-}^{2}(r)+f_{-}^{2}(r)\Big],&r<r_{a},\\
\displaystyle\frac{4\pi A_{+}^{2}}{\mathcal{N}}\,\Big[g_{+}^{2}(r)+f_{+}^{2}(r)\Big],&r>r_{a},\end{array}\right.

\varepsilon_{ba}^{(1)}=\int\limits_{0}^{r_{b}}W_{ba}(r)\varrho_{a}(r)r^{2}dr.

14

75

Moreover,
\varrho_{a}(r)
is smooth within the interval
0<r<r_{b}

i.e., for any
r\in(0,r_{b})

\big|\varrho(r)-\varrho(r_{a})\big|\ll\varrho(r_{a})
.
Note that, although one may expect that Eq. (

\varepsilon_{ba}^{(1)}\approx\varrho(r_{a})\int\limits_{0}^{r_{b}}W_{ba}(r)r^{2}dr,

(77)

hence the approximation in Eq. (

)
