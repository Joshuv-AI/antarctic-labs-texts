Entanglement polygon inequality in qudit systems
Xue Yang, Yan-Han Yang, Ming-Xing Luo

Abstract
Entanglement is one of important resources for quantum communication tasks. Most of results are focused on qubit entanglement. Our goal in this work is to characterize the multipartite high-dimensional entanglement. We firstly derive an entanglement polygon inequality for the q q -concurrence, which manifests the relationship among all the “one-to-group” marginal entanglements in any multipartite qudit system. This implies lower and upper bounds for the marginal entanglement of any three-qudit system. We further extend to general entanglement distribution inequalities for high-dimensional entanglement in terms of the unified- ( r , s ) (r,s) entropy entanglement including Tsallis entropy, Rényi entropy, and von Neumann entropy entanglement as special cases. These results provide new insights into characterizing bipartite high-dimensional entanglement in quantum information processing.

The

For any bipartite pure state

\displaystyle{\cal C}_{q}(|\phi\rangle_{AB})=F_{q}(\rho_{A}),

-concurrence for a large

Proposition 1
. The

-concurrence is an entanglement measure.

It has been proved that the

-concurrence is an entanglement monotone

II.2
Entanglement polygon inequalities with one-to-group entanglement

Note that an
n
-qudit pure state on Hilbert space

can be generally represented by

\displaystyle|\psi\rangle_{1\cdots{}n}=\sum^{n}_{j=0}\sum^{d_{j}-1}_{s_{j}=0}\alpha_{s_{1}\cdots s_{n}}|s_{1}\cdots s_{n}\rangle,

(7)

\alpha_{s_{1}\cdots s_{n}}
are the coefficients satisfying the normalization condition of

Consider a one-to-group entanglement

of the pure state (
7

refers to the system of

denotes all the systems except for the system

-concurrence

-qudit system as the following theorem.

Theorem 1

For any
n
-qudit pure entangled state
|\psi\rangle
on Hilbert space
\otimes_{i=1}^{n}{\cal H}_{i}
, the following inequality holds

\displaystyle{\cal C}^{j|\overline{j}}_{q}(|\psi\rangle)\leq\sum_{k\neq j,\forall k}{\cal C}^{k|\overline{k}}_{q}(|\psi\rangle)

(8)

for any
q\geq 2
.

Proof.
From the definition (
4
) we obtain

\displaystyle{\cal C}^{j|\overline{j}}_{q}(|\psi\rangle)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle F_{q}(\rho_{j})

(9)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle F_{q}(\rho_{\overline{j}})

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\sum_{k\neq j,\forall k}F_{q}(\rho_{k})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{k\neq j,\forall k}{\cal C}^{k|\overline{k}}_{q}(|\psi\rangle).

(10)

Here, the inequality (
9
) is followed by iteratively using the subadditivity condition in Eq.(
6
), and
F_{q}(\rho_{j})
is the entropy of the reduced density matrix

of the subsystem

10
) is obtained from the definition (
4
). This has proved the result.
\Box

For an arbitrary three-qudit system on Hilbert space

, we have three one-to-other marginal entanglements, namely,
{\cal C}_{q}^{1|23}
,
{\cal C}_{q}^{2|13}
,

. Theorem 1 implies special relationships among these quantities as follows.

Corollary 1

For an arbitrary three-qudit pure entangled state
|\psi\rangle
on Hilbert space

, the

-concurrence

satisfy the following triangle inequalities as

\displaystyle|{\cal C}_{q}^{j|ik}(|\psi\rangle)-{\cal C}_{q}^{k|ij}(|\psi\rangle)|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle{\cal C}_{q}^{i|jk}(|\psi\rangle)

(11)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle{\cal C}_{q}^{j|ik}(|\psi\rangle)+{\cal C}_{q}^{k|ij}(|\psi\rangle).

The right hand side of the inequality (

\displaystyle{\cal C}^{i|jk}_{q}(|\psi\rangle)

\displaystyle F_{q}(\rho_{jk})

\displaystyle|F_{q}(\rho_{j})-F_{q}(\rho_{k})|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle|{\cal C}^{j|ik}_{q}(|\psi\rangle)-{\cal C}^{k|ij}_{q}(|\psi\rangle)|

Obviously, the inequality (

II.3
Entanglement polygon inequalities with general bipartite entanglement

In this subsection, we consider the relationship among the
q
-concurrence of any bipartition of multipartite entanglement. Consider an

-partite system in the state

For any entangled pure entangled state

-concurrence satisfies the following inequality

\displaystyle{\cal C}_{q}^{\bf{A|B}}(|\psi\rangle)\leq\sum^{m}_{i=1}{\cal C}_{q}^{A_{i}|\overline{A}_{i}}(|\psi\rangle),

-concurrence under the bipartition

and

denotes all the particles except

Proof.
According to the

-concurrence (

), we get

\displaystyle{\cal C}_{q}^{\bf{A|B}}(|\psi\rangle)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle F_{q}(\rho_{\bf{A}})

(16)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\sum^{m}_{i=1}F_{q}(\rho_{A_{i}})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum^{m}_{i=1}{\cal C}_{q}^{A_{i}|\overline{A}_{i}}(|\psi\rangle).

Similar to the inequalities (

) and (

-qudit pure state

on Hilbert space

\square

Inspired by ref.

\displaystyle{\cal C}^{j|\overline{j}}_{q}(|\psi\rangle)\leq\frac{{\cal C}^{T}_{q}}{2}(|\psi\rangle).

By regarding

, the present inequality (
20

, its information (quantified by

1

.

Note Theorem 2 is not a special case of Theorem 1, they are not inequivalent. Take a 4-partite system as an example. In Theorem 2, we get new upper bounds of the marginal entanglement of

-concurrence on

In Theorem 1, we provide the restrictions among all “one-to-group” entanglements between a single qudit

Obviously, Theorem 2 describes a global entangled distribution between the partial set

In Sec.II, we have proved the polygon inequality for pure multipartite high-dimensional entanglement by using the entanglement measure of

-concurrence. In this section, we extend the result with the unified-

For a given state

\displaystyle S_{r,s}(\rho)=\frac{1}{(1-r)s}[({\rm{Tr}}\rho^{r})^{s}-1],

where

and

,

;

;

\displaystyle\lim_{s\to 0}S_{r,s}(\rho)=\frac{1}{1-r}\log_{2}{\rm{Tr}}(\rho^{r}):=R_{r}(\rho).

The Rényi entropy is not additive

. Especially, the Rényi entropy satisfies the following inequality

\displaystyle R_{r}(\rho_{A})-R_{0}(\rho_{B})\leq R_{r}(\rho_{AB})\leq R_{r}(\rho_{A})+R_{0}(\rho_{B})

for any

and
\rho_{B}
are reduced density matrices of the subsystem
A
and
B
, respectively. Moreover, the unified
(r,s)
-entropy tends to the Tsallis entropy

;

;

as

\displaystyle\lim_{s\to 1}S_{r,s}(\rho)=\frac{1}{1-r}({\rm{Tr}}(\rho^{r})-1):=T_{r}(\rho).

Here, the Tsallis entropy satisfies the subadditivity and triangle inequality as

:

\displaystyle|T_{r}(\rho_{A})-T_{r}(\rho_{B})|\leq T_{r}(\rho_{AB})\leq T_{r}(\rho_{A})+T_{r}(\rho_{B})

for any

.

For the case of

, the unified
(r,s)
-entropy converges to the von Neumann entropy

;

;
Rastegin2011

\displaystyle\lim_{r\to 1}S_{r,s}(\rho)={\rm{Tr}}\rho\log_{2}\rho:=S(\rho).

Note that the von Neumann entropy satisfies the following Araki-Lieb inequality

\displaystyle|S(\rho_{A})-S(\rho_{B})|\leq S(\rho_{AB})\leq S(\rho_{A})+S(\rho_{B}),

For any pure

From the definition (

\displaystyle S_{r,s}(\rho_{\overline{j}})

\displaystyle\sum_{k\neq j,\forall k}S_{r,s}(\rho_{k})

V
Conclusion

The distribution of multipartite entanglement is an important problem in entanglement theory and quantum information processing. Entanglement polygon inequality provides a distribution relationship of all the one-to-group marginal entanglements for arbitrary pure multi-partite entanglement.
