On approximate quasi Pareto solutions in nonsmooth semi-infinite interval-valued vector optimization problems
Nguyen Huy Hung a , Hoang Ngoc Tuan a , and Nguyen Van Tuyen a

Abstract
This paper deals with approximate solutions of a nonsmooth semi-infinite programming with multiple interval-valued objective functions. We first introduce four types of approximate quasi Pareto solutions of the considered problem by considering the lower-upper interval order relation and then apply some advanced tools of variational analysis and generalized differentiation to establish necessary optimality conditions for these approximate solutions. Sufficient conditions for approximate quasi Pareto solutions of such a problem are also provided by means of introducing the concepts of approximate (strictly) pseudo-quasi generalized convex functions defined in terms of the limiting subdifferential of locally Lipschitz functions. Finally, a Mond–Weir type dual model in approximate form is formulated, and weak, strong and converse-like duality relations are proposed.

Theorem 3.4
.

Let
\bar{x}\in\mathcal{F}

satisfies the (

(1)

Proof.

Since
\bar{x}\in\mathcal{E}
-
\mathcal{S}_{2}^{qw}\eqref{problem}
, there is no

For each

for all

We first justify (i). Assume to the contrary that

f(x)\prec^{s}_{LU}\mathcal{L}(y,\lambda^{L},\lambda^{U},\mu)-\mathcal{E}\|x-y\|.

This means that

f_{i}(x)\prec^{s}_{LU}\mathcal{L}_{i}(y,\lambda^{L},\lambda^{U},\mu)-\mathcal{E}_{i}\|x-y\|,\ \ \forall i\in I,

or, equivalently,

\displaystyle\begin{cases}f_{i}^{L}(x)<f_{i}^{L}(y)-\epsilon_{i}^{U}\|x-y\|,\\
f_{i}^{U}(x)<f_{i}^{U}(y)-\epsilon_{i}^{L}\|x-y\|,\end{cases}

for all

. By (

\mu_{t}\geq 0

, we have

g_{t}(x)\leq 0\leq g_{t}(y)

for all

. Since
(f,g_{T})
is
\mathcal{E}
-pseudo-quasi generalized convex on
\Omega
at
y
, there exists
\nu\in[N(y,\Omega)]^{\circ}
such that

\displaystyle\langle z_{i}^{*L},\nu\rangle+\epsilon_{i}^{U}\|x-y\|<0,\ \ \forall i\in I,

\displaystyle\langle z_{i}^{*U},\nu\rangle+\epsilon_{i}^{L}\|x-y\|<0,\ \ \forall i\in I,

\displaystyle\langle x^{*}_{t},\nu\rangle\leq 0,\ \ \forall t\in T(\mu),

\displaystyle\text{and}\ \ \langle b^{*},\nu\rangle\leq\|x-y\|,\ \ \forall b^{*}\in\mathbb{B}_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}.

Since
\sum_{i\in I}(\lambda_{i}^{L}+\lambda_{i}^{U})=1
, one has

\displaystyle\sum_{i\in I}\big[\langle\lambda_{i}^{L}z_{i}^{*L}

\displaystyle+\lambda_{i}^{U}z_{i}^{*U},\nu\rangle\big]+\sum_{t\in T}\langle x^{*}_{t},\nu\rangle+\sum_{i\in I}(\lambda_{i}^{L}\epsilon_{i}^{U}+\lambda_{i}^{U}\epsilon_{i}^{L})\langle b^{*},\nu\rangle\leq

\displaystyle\sum_{i\in I}\big[\langle\lambda_{i}^{L}z_{i}^{*L}+\lambda_{i}^{U}z_{i}^{*U},\nu\rangle\big]+\sum_{t\in T}\langle x^{*}_{t},\nu\rangle+\sum_{i\in I}(\lambda_{i}^{L}\epsilon_{i}^{U}+\lambda_{i}^{U}\epsilon_{i}^{L})\|x-y\|<0.

On the other hand, since

We now prove (ii). Suppose on the contrary that

f(x)\preceq_{LU}\mathcal{L}(y,\lambda^{L},\lambda^{U},\mu)-\mathcal{E}\|x-y\|.

\begin{cases}f_{i}(x)\leq_{LU}f_{i}(y)-\mathcal{E}_{i}\|x-y\|,\ \ \forall i\in I,\\
f_{k}(x)<_{LU}f_{k}(y)-\mathcal{E}_{k}\|x-y\|,\ \ \text{for at least one}\ \ k\in I.\end{cases}

Hence,
x\neq y

\mathcal{E}
-pseudo-quasi generalized convexity of
(f,g_{T})
at
y
, (
11
), and by the same argument as in the proof of part (i), we can get the contradiction. The proof is completed.

5
Conclusions

In this paper, we focus for the first time on studying approximate solutions of semi-infinite interval-valued vector optimization problems. By employing the Mordukhovich/limiting subdifferential and the Mordukhovich/limiting normal cone and introducing some types of approximate pseudo-quasi generalized convexity of a family of locally Lipschitz functions, we establish optimality conditions of KKT-type and duality relations for proposed approximate solutions of the considered problem. Observe that the class of approximate pseudo-quasi generalized convex functions is properly wider than the class of convex functions in the sense of
[
37
,
27
,
41
]
, our results generalize and improve some existing results. Furthermore, the model of problems of the form (
SIVP
) covers the one of cone-constrained convex vector optimization problems and semidefinite vector optimization problems, and so our approach can be developed to study these problems. We aim to investigate this problem in future work.
