CKM and PMNS mixing matrix from S ​ O ​ ( 2 ) SO(2) flavor symmetry
Guojun Xu a , Ying Zhang a,b

Abstract
The relation between quark masses and CKM mixing is studied based on an approximate chiral S ​ O ​ ( 2 ) L × S ​ O ​ ( 2 ) R SO(2)_{L}\times SO(2)_{R} flavor symmetry of quark mass matrix. In mass hierarchy limit, the mass ratio effect to CKM mixing is suppressed, which separates mass hierarchy and quark flavor mixing into two independent problems. We show that CKM mixing is dominated by two left-handed S ​ O ​ ( 2 ) L u , d SO(2)_{L}^{u,d} symmetry while mass hierarchy only provides slight corrections. The same mixing structure is generalized to lepton sector with extended Dirac neutrinos. The common flavor mixing provides a novel comprehension on the relation between quark CKM mixing and lepton PMNS mixing.

After Higgs particle was found in 2012, all predictions of the Standard Model (the SM) has been realized

In the SM, quark mass matrix

By performing a bi-unitary transformation

V_{CKM}=U^{u}_{L}(U^{d}_{L})^{\dagger}
.
However, unencrypted Yukawa couplings leave mass hierarchy and flavor mixing unknown flavor puzzles.
The same case exists in the lepton sector with massive Dirac neutrinos.
At present it is not clear whether these two flavor problems are controlled by independent mechanism, or one dominates another.
Therefore, decoding mass pattern is a key to recover flavor nature.
