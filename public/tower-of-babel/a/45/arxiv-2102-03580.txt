Study of C C parity violating and strangeness changing J / ψ J/{\psi} → {\to} P ​ P PP weak decays
Yueling Yang, Junliang Lu, Mingfei Duan, Jinshu Huang, Junfeng Sun

Abstract
Although J / ψ J/{\psi} weak decays are rare, they are possible within the standard model of elementary particles. Inspired by the potential prospects of the future intensity frontier, the C C parity violating J / ψ J/{\psi} → {\to} π η ( ′ ) {\pi}{\eta}^{({\prime})} , η ​ η ′ {\eta}{\eta}^{\prime} decays and the strangeness changing J / ψ J/{\psi} → {\to} π ​ K {\pi}K , K η ( ′ ) K{\eta}^{({\prime})} decays are studied with the perturbative QCD approach. It is found that the J / ψ J/{\psi} → {\to} η ​ η ′ {\eta}{\eta}^{\prime} decays have relatively large branching ratios, about the order of 10 − 11 10^{-11} , which might be within the measurement capability and sensitivity of the future STCF experiment.

VIII

Within the SM, the

{\to}

{\pi}^{0}{\eta}^{(\prime)}
and

and the strangeness changing
J/{\psi}

{\to}

In this paper, based on the latest progress and future
