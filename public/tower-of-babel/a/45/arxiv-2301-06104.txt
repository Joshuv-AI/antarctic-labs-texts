Generation of isospin sum rules in heavy hadron weak decays
Di Wang 1

Abstract
Isospin symmetry is the most precise flavor symmetry. In this work, we propose an approach to generate isospin sum rules for heavy hadron decays without the Wigner-Eckhart invariants. The effective Hamiltonian of heavy quark weak decay is fully invariant under a series of isospin lowering operators I − n I_{-}^{n} and then the isospin sum rules can be generated through several master formulas. It provides a systematic way to study the isospin symmetry of c c - and b b -hadron weak decays. The theoretical framework of this approach is presented in detail with the nonleptonic decays of D D and B B mesons as examples. In addition, the V V -/ U U -spin sum rules are derived in a similar algorithm by replacing I − n I_{-}^{n} with V − n V_{-}^{n} / U − n U_{-}^{n} .

There exists a basis in which isospin sum rules involve only CF, SCS or DCS decays respectively.

In general, the flavor sum rules are derived by writing decay amplitudes and combining several modes to form a polygon in the complex plane.
However, this method is laborious and unsystematic.
The authors of Ref.
Grossman:2012ry

The idea is that if there is an operator

,

\alpha
,
\beta

I_{-}
is the isospin lowering operator.
I_{-}

I_{-}

\displaystyle=4\,\big[\mathcal{A}(D^{0}\to\pi^{0}\pi^{0})-\mathcal{A}(D^{0}\to\pi^{+}\pi^{-})-\sqrt{2}\,\mathcal{A}(D^{+}\to\pi^{+}\pi^{0})\big]=0.

\displaystyle={SumI_{-}}\,[D^{0},\pi^{+},K^{0}]-\sqrt{2}\,{SumI_{-}}\,[D^{0},\pi^{0},K^{+}]+{SumI_{-}}\,[D^{+},\pi^{+},K^{+}]

\displaystyle~~=-2\,\big[\sqrt{2}\,\mathcal{A}(D^{0}\to\pi^{0}K^{0})+\mathcal{A}(D^{0}\to\pi^{-}K^{+})

\displaystyle~~~~~~~~~+\sqrt{2}\,\mathcal{A}(D^{+}\to\pi^{0}K^{+})-\mathcal{A}(D^{+}\to\pi^{+}K^{0})\big]=0.

.

and

I_{-}

IV
Summary

Flavor symmetry is a model-independent tool to analyze heavy meson and baryon decays.
The flavor invariants are independent of the detailed dynamics and determined by fitting experimental data.
In this work, we propose a simple algorithm to generate the isospin,
V
-spin and
U
-spin sum rules of heavy hadron decays.
We found that the effective Hamiltonian of heavy quark decay is fully invariant under a series of lowering operators
I_{-}^{n}
,
V_{-}^{n}
and
U_{-}^{n}
.
The isospin,
V
-spin and
U
-spin sum rules can be generated from several master formulas without the Wigner-Eckhart invariants.
Taking the two-body decays of
D
and
B
mesons as examples, our approach is presented in detail.
