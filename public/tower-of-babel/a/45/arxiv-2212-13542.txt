1.
Introduction

The theory of complex cobordism
{\mathbf{M}\mathbf{U}}^{*}(-)
and special unitary cobordism
{\mathbf{M}\mathbf{S}\mathbf{U}}^{*}(-)

[

In particular the coefficient ring

{\mathbf{M}\mathbf{S}\mathbf{U}}_{*}[1/2]=\mathbb{Z}[1/2][x_{2},x_{3},\cdots],\,\,\,\,|x_{i}|=2i

and
SU
-structure forgetful homomorphism is the inclusion

{\mathbf{M}\mathbf{S}\mathbf{U}}_{*}[1/2]\subset{\mathbf{M}\mathbf{U}}_{*}[1/2]=\mathbb{Z}[1/2][a_{1},a_{2},a_{3},\cdots].

With this in mind the generators

in

{\mathbf{M}\mathbf{U}}_{*}[1/2]

having factor

Then we have to check the main Chern number

[
14
]
for the membership of the set of polynomial generators in

{\mathbf{M}\mathbf{S}\mathbf{U}}^{*}[1/2]

In Section 3 we prove

Lemma 1.1
.

Let
\Gamma=\{x_{n},\,n\geq 2\}
be a sequence of any polynomial generators in
{\mathbf{M}\mathbf{S}\mathbf{U}}_{*}[1/2]
viewed as elements in
{\mathbf{M}\mathbf{U}}_{*}[1/2]

i)
\,\Gamma

ii) any subsequence
\Sigma
of

By using Baas-Sullivan theory of cobordism with singularities we deduce

For any subset

{\mathbf{M}\mathbf{S}\mathbf{U}}_{*}[1/2]

\emph{h}_{\Sigma}^{*}(-):={\mathbf{M}\mathbf{U}}^{*}_{\Sigma}[1/2](-)

Proofs

To prove Lemma
1.1
i) let as above
\Sigma
be the ideal generated by any sequence
\Sigma=\{x_{n},\,n\geq 5\}
of polynomial generators in
{\mathbf{M}\mathbf{S}\mathbf{U}}_{*}

(Y)
be the ideal generated by the regular sequence

defined above. It is clear that the latter remains regular after localized away from 2.

One has
(\Sigma)\subset(Y)
because the abelian formal group law is a specialization of the Buchstaber formal group law, which has a greater kernel ideal. By (
2.1
) we have
(\Gamma)\subset(x_{2},Y)
.

It is clear that
x_{2}
is a polynomial generator in
{\mathbf{M}\mathbf{U}}_{*}[1/2]
therefore the ideal
(x_{2},Y)
is also regular.

It suffices to prove the reverse inclusion

((x_{2},Y))\subset\Gamma)
. It can proved by induction to follow the proof of Proposition 5.3 in
[
5
]
and taking into account the structure of the quotient ring
{\mathbf{M}\mathbf{U}}_{*}/(Y)

[
8
]
. We leave the detailed proof to the reader.

ii) For the regularity of any subsequence
\{x_{i_{1}},\cdots x_{i_{n}}\}
of
\Gamma
we need three facts: if
R
is a graded ring and the members of a regular sequence are homogeneous of positive degree, then any permutation of a regular sequence is regular
[
9
]
. Then it is clear by definition that any first
n
members of a regular sequence form a regular sequence and vice versa (including infinite case).

∎

The proof of Theorem
1.2
uses
the Sullivan-Baas construction
[
1
]
of cobordism with singularities. The regular sequence

\Sigma
gives a cohomology theory
{\mathbf{M}\mathbf{U}}^{*}_{\Sigma}(-)
which by regularity
of the ideal
(\Sigma)
has a scalar ring

{\mathbf{M}\mathbf{U}}^{*}_{\Sigma}(pt)={\mathbf{M}\mathbf{U}}_{*}/(\Sigma).

By Mironov

It is clear that

4.
Related genera on
{\mathbf{M}\mathbf{S}\mathbf{U}}_{*}[1/2]
and
{\mathbf{M}\mathbf{S}\mathbf{p}}_{*}[1/2]

Numerous examples of cobordism theory as a classification tool are considered in the literature. Many examples, such as Spin, Sp, SC and SU cobordism, have a complicated structure, and some have not even been fully calculated yet. However, after localized away from 2, all the mentioned rings are subrings in
{\mathbf{M}\mathbf{U}}_{*}[1/2]
and polynomial.

Therefore the restriction of a genus on
{\mathbf{M}\mathbf{U}}_{*}
classifying a cohomology
h_{\Sigma}

defined above gives the corresponding genus on
{\mathbf{M}\mathbf{S}\mathbf{U}}[1/2]

[
16
]
and
{\mathbf{M}\mathbf{S}\mathbf{p}}_{*}[1/2]
.

In particular by Buchstaber it is known that

{\mathbf{M}\mathbf{S}\mathbf{p}}^{*}[1/2](-)
can be viewed as a cohomology theory
\Lambda_{\varkappa^{*}}
defined by a projector
\varkappa^{*}

on
{\mathbf{M}\mathbf{U}}_{*}[1/2]

[
BU
]
. Moreover
{\mathbf{M}\mathbf{S}\mathbf{p}}_{*}[1/2]
forgetful homomorphisms to
{\mathbf{M}\mathbf{U}}_{*}[1/2]

is an isomorphism on the subring in
{\mathbf{M}\mathbf{U}}_{*}[1/2]
generated by the coefficients of the series

p_{i}(\zeta_{1}\otimes\zeta_{2}),,\,\,\,\zeta_{i}\to HP^{\infty},\,\,\,i=1,2.

Let
\Lambda^{\perp}
be the subring in
{\mathbf{M}\mathbf{U}}_{*}
generated by the coefficients of the series

G(x)=c_{1}(\xi+\bar{\xi})=\sum a_{i}c_{2}^{i}(\xi+\bar{\xi}),\,\,\,\xi\to CP^{\infty}.

In
[
BU-NO
]
it is proved that

\Lambda\otimes\Lambda^{\perp}[1/2]={\mathbf{M}\mathbf{U}}_{*}[1/2].

Here by using the splitting principle everything can be easily calculated in terms of the universal formal group law. Therefore it is reasonable to give explicit calculation of all above mentioned genera.

Note that in
[
2
]
A. Baker and J. Morava constructed another but related projector
on
{\mathbf{M}\mathbf{U}}[1/2]
also defining
{\mathbf{M}\mathbf{S}\mathbf{p}}[1/2]
, but not fixing its forgetful image in
{\mathbf{M}\mathbf{U}}[1/2]
.

There are other projectors on
{\mathbf{M}\mathbf{U}}[1/2]
which are not fix the forgetful image maps but still it is possible to work with in the above sense. For example in
[
6
]
Buchstaber constructed the projectors in unitary cobordisms that are related to SU-theory. In
[
5
]
and
[
11
]
this result is used for explicit calculations.
