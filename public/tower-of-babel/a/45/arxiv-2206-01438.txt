Bounds on the mass and mixing of Z ′ Z^{\prime} and W ′ W^{\prime} bosons decaying into different pairings of W W , Z Z , or Higgs bosons using CMS data at the LHC
P. Osland, A. A. Pankov, I. A. Serenkova

Abstract
The full CMS Run 2 datasets with time-integrated luminosity of 137 fb -1 in the diboson channels are used to probe benchmark models with extended gauge sectors such as E 6 E_{6} , left-right symmetric (LR) and the sequential standard model (extended gauge model, EGM), that predict the existence of neutral Z ′ Z^{\prime} - and charged W ′ W^{\prime} -bosons decaying to a pair of bosons W ​ W WW , Z ​ H ZH , W ​ Z WZ and W ​ H WH in the semileptonic final state. These benchmark models are used to interpret the results. Exclusion limits at the 95% C.L. on the Z ′ Z^{\prime} and W ′ W^{\prime} resonance production cross section times branching ratio to electroweak gauge boson pairs in the resonance mass range between 1.0 and 4.5 TeV are here converted to constraints on Z Z - Z ′ Z^{\prime} and W W - W ′ W^{\prime} mixing parameters and masses. We present exclusion regions on the parameter spaces of the Z ′ Z^{\prime} and W ′ W^{\prime} and show that the obtained exclusion regions are significantly extended compared to those derived from the previous analysis performed with Tevatron data as well as with the CMS data collected at 7 and 8 TeV in Run 1. The reported limits are the most restrictive to date.

\displaystyle\sigma(pp\to Z^{\prime}X\to W^{+}W^{-}\,X)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sigma(pp\to Z^{\prime}\,X)\times\text{BR}(Z^{\prime}\to W^{+}W^{-})\;,

(14a)

\displaystyle\sigma(pp\to Z^{\prime}X\to ZH\,X)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sigma(pp\to Z^{\prime}\,X)\times\text{BR}(Z^{\prime}\to ZH)\;.

(14b)

\sigma(pp\to Z^{\prime}X)

\text{BR}(Z^{\prime}\to W^{+}W^{-})=\Gamma_{Z^{\prime}}^{WW}/\Gamma_{Z^{\prime}}

IV.1
The
Z^{\prime}
width

In the computation of the total width
\Gamma_{Z^{\prime}}
we take into account the
following channels:
Z^{\prime}\to f\bar{f}
,
W^{+}W^{-}
, and
ZH

Salvioni et al. 2009
;
Gulov et al. 2018
;
Pankov et al. 2020
;
Osland et al. 2021
, where
H
is the SM Higgs boson and
f
refers to the SM fermions (
f=l,\nu,q
). Throughout the paper we shall
ignore the couplings of the
Z^{\prime}
to any beyond-SM particles such as
right-handed neutrinos, as well as to
SUSY partners and any other exotic fermions.
Any additional states may increase the width of the
Z^{\prime}
.

The total width
\Gamma_{Z^{\prime}}
of the
Z^{\prime}
boson can then be written as
follows:

\Gamma_{Z^{\prime}}=\sum_{f}\Gamma_{Z^{\prime}}^{ff}+\Gamma_{Z^{\prime}}^{WW}+\Gamma_{Z^{\prime}}^{ZH}.

(15)

The two last terms are due to
Z
-
Z^{\prime}

mixing. For the range of
M_{Z^{\prime}}

values below
\sim 3
TeV, the dependence of

\Gamma_{Z^{\prime}}
on the values of
\xi_{Z\text{-}Z^{\prime}}
(within its allowed range) is unimportant.
Therefore, in this mass range, one can approximate the total width as
\Gamma_{Z^{\prime}}\approx\sum_{f}\Gamma_{Z^{\prime}}^{ff}
, where the sum runs over SM fermions only.
Within the approximation above, one can quantify the ratio of
\Gamma_{Z^{\prime}}/M_{Z^{\prime}}
for the benchmark EGM as
3\%
, whereas for the
\psi,\,\eta,\,\chi
and

{\rm LR}
models it varies from
0.5\%
to
2.0\%
.

However, for larger
Z^{\prime}
masses,
M_{Z^{\prime}}>4
TeV, there is an
enhancement in the coupling that cancels the suppression due to the tiny
Z
-
Z^{\prime}

mixing parameter
\xi_{Z\text{-}Z^{\prime}}

Salvioni et al. 2009
. We note that the
“Equivalence theorem”
Chanowitz and Gaillard 1985
suggests a
value for
\text{BR}(Z^{\prime}\to ZH)
comparable to
\text{BR}(Z^{\prime}\to W^{+}W^{-})
,
up to electroweak symmetry breaking effects and phase-space factors.
Throughout this paper, for definiteness, we adopt a scenario where
both partial widths are comparable,
\Gamma_{Z^{\prime}}^{ZH}\simeq\Gamma_{Z^{\prime}}^{WW}
for heavy
M_{Z^{\prime}}

Barger and Whisnant 1987
;
Barger et al. 2009
;
Dib and Gilman 1987
.

For all
M_{Z^{\prime}}
values of interest for our analysis the width
of the
Z^{\prime}
boson is considerably smaller than the experimental
mass resolution
\Delta M
. We adopt the approximation
\Delta M/M\approx 5\%
,
as reported, e.g., in

Sirunyan et al. 2017
for reconstructing the diboson invariant mass of the
W^{+}W^{-}
and
ZH

systems.

The partial width of the
Z^{\prime}\to W^{+}W^{-}
decay
channel can be written as
Altarelli et al. 1989

\Gamma_{Z^{\prime}}^{WW}=\frac{\alpha_{\rm em}}{48}\cot^{2}\theta_{W}\,M_{Z^{\prime}}\left(\frac{M_{Z^{\prime}}}{M_{W}}\right)^{4}\left(1-4\,\frac{M_{W}^{2}}{M_{Z^{\prime}}^{2}}\right)^{3/2}\left[1+20\left(\frac{M_{W}}{M_{Z^{\prime}}}\right)^{2}+12\left(\frac{M_{W}}{M_{Z^{\prime}}}\right)^{4}\right]\cdot\xi_{Z\text{-}Z^{\prime}}^{2}.

(16)

For a fixed mixing factor
\xi_{Z\text{-}Z^{\prime}}
and at large
M_{Z^{\prime}}
where

\Gamma_{Z^{\prime}}^{WW}
dominates over
\sum_{f}\Gamma_{Z^{\prime}}^{ff}
the total width increases
rapidly with the mass

because of the quintic
dependence of the
W^{+}W^{-}

Z^{\prime}
mass. In this case, the

mode (together with
Z^{\prime}\to ZH

\text{BR}(Z^{\prime}\to W^{+}W^{-})\to 0.5

\Gamma_{Z^{\prime}}^{ZH}=\Gamma_{Z^{\prime}}^{WW}
