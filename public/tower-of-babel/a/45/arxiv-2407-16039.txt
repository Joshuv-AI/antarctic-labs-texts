Exact Expressions For Infinitely Many Weil-Petersson Volumes
Ashton Lowenstein

Abstract
Weil-Petersson volumes are the volumes of the moduli spaces of bordered Riemann surfaces and have played an important role in the relationship between two-dimensional quantum gravity and algebraic geometry. In the last couple years progress has been made to understand their role in the context of matrix models, where it is possible to define a generalization of the volumes in terms of an infinite set of coupling constants t k t_{k} . Using a recent open string matrix model construction we calculate the generalized Weil-Petersson volumes for fixed genus g = 0 , 1 g=0,1 and an arbitrary number of boundaries n n . Both results are expressed in terms of the perturbative expansion of the solution to the string equation of the matrix model in the closed string sector. The formalism has the added benefit of applying to type 0A superstring matrix models with nonzero Ramond-Ramond flux.

3
Discussion

The generalized Weil-Petersson volumes are a generalization of the volumes of both non-supersymmetric and supersymmetric bordered Riemann surfaces. By building on results obtained in
[
24
]
we have presented a method by which the generalized Weil-Petersson volumes may be directly computed using the KdV organization of double scaled hermitian matrix models. This allowed us to obtain exact expressions for infinitely many of the volumes, each determined by the function
u
and its perturbative expansion as a solution to one of the string equations (
1.2
) or (
1.3
).

A distinct benefit of this approach is that it circumvents the different recursion relations used for the resolvent functions in Wigner-Dyson and Altland-Zirnbauer models. The general result in (
2.6
) is therefore valid for cases with and without supersymmetry. It should be noted that the differences between the classes of models are shifted to the respective string equations. Another benefit is that it directly generalizes previous work by Zograf (see
e.g.

[
29
]
) on the computation of Weil-Petersson volumes using the string equation formalism.
