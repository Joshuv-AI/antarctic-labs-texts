Abstract
We introduce a notion of quasi-antisymmetric Higgs G G -bundles over curves with marked points. They are endowed with additional structures, which replace the parabolic structures at marked points in the parabolic Higgs bundles. The latter means that the coadjoint orbits are attached to the marked points. The moduli spaces of parabolic Higgs bundles are the phase spaces of complex completely integrable systems. In our case the coadjoint orbits are replaced by the cotangent bundles over some special symmetric spaces in such a way that the moduli space of the modified Higgs bundles are still phase spaces of complex completely integrable systems. We show that the moduli space of the parabolic Higgs bundles is the symplectic quotient of the moduli space of the quasi-antisymmetric Higgs bundle with respect to the action of product of Cartan subgroups. Also, by changing the symmetric spaces we introduce quasi-compact and quasi-normal Higgs bundles. Then the fixed point sets of real involutions acting on their moduli spaces are the phase spaces of real completely integrable systems. Several examples are given including integrable extensions of the SL ⁡ ( 2 ) {\rm SL}(2) Euler-Arnold top, two-body elliptic Calogero-Moser system and the rational SL ⁡ ( 2 ) {\rm SL}(2) Gaudin system together with its real reductions.

where the leave of the of the projection

Type 0.

Consider two isomorphic representations

of

As above we have the projection

\pi\,:\,Bun_{0}(\Sigma_{g,n},G^{\mathbb{C}})\to Bun(\Sigma_{g},G^{\mathbb{C}})

Bun(\Sigma_{g},G^{\mathbb{C}})=G_{0}\setminus Bun_{0}(\Sigma_{g,n},G^{\mathbb{C}})\,,~~G_{0}=\prod_{a}G^{\mathbb{C}}\,.

(2.14)

Type I.

Let
C
be the maximal compact subgroup of

, defined by the involutive automorphism

In the

The group

acts transitively on the symmetric space

. Thereby, similarly to (

(2.19)

where

(2.31)

There is the

\textstyle{\{d_{\bar{A}}\}\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces}

\scriptstyle{\mathcal{G}_{0}}
\textstyle{\quad Bun_{0}(\Sigma_{g,n},G^{\mathbb{C}})\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces}

(2.32)

The action of

We have a natural ”forgetting” projection

2.2
Higgs bundles

Let
\kappa
be a canonical class on the curve
\Sigma_{g}
. The Higgs field
\Phi
is
a
{\rm End}(E(G^{\mathbb{C}}))\otimes\kappa(\Sigma_{g})
-valued section of the bundle of distributions on
\Sigma_{g}
.
The pair
(\Phi\,,d_{\bar{A}})
forms the Higgs bundle over the smooth curve
\Sigma_{g}

[
16
]
. The Higgs bundle is a symplectic space with the form

\Omega=\int_{\Sigma_{g}}(D\Phi,D\bar{A})\,.

(2.34)

It is invariant under the transformations (
2.3
) and

\Phi\to f\Phi f^{-1}\,,~~f\in\mathcal{G}(\Sigma_{g},G^{\mathbb{C}})\,.

(2.35)

The moduli spaces of the Higgs bundles are symplectic quotients with respect to this action.

Now consider the Higgs bundles over punctured curves.
As for the moduli spaces of holomorphic bundles we have
\alpha
and
\beta
realizations of these objects.

2.2.1
\alpha
-model

Consider first the parabolic Higgs bundles
[
22
,
32
]
.
To this end extend the upstairs symplectic space. The
\alpha
-model for the
parabolic Higgs bundles is defined by the data

(\Phi\,,d_{\bar{A}},\cup\mathcal{O}_{a})
, where
\mathcal{O}_{a}=\{{\bf S}(\nu_{a},g_{a})\}
are coadjoint
G^{\mathbb{C}}
-orbits (
C.17
) attached to the marked points.
The symplectic form on this data is

\Omega_{par}=\Omega+\sum_{a}\omega^{KK}_{a}\delta(z-x_{a},\bar{z}-\bar{x}_{a})\,,

(2.36)

where
\omega^{KK}_{a}=\omega^{KK}(\nu_{a})
is the Kirillov-Kostant form (
C.18
).
The corresponding Poisson brackets are
the canonical brackets on the affine space
(D_{\bar{A}},\Phi)
and
the Lie-Poisson brackets for the orbit variables.

The gauge group
\mathcal{G}(\Sigma_{g},G^{\mathbb{C}})
(
2.2
) is generated by symplectomorphisms of the parabolic Higgs bundles.
It acts on the variables as
(
2.3
), (
2.35
) and
g_{a}\to g_{a}f^{-1}(x_{a})
in the representation (
C.18
) of the

d_{\bar{A}}\Phi+\sum_{a=1}^{n}\delta(z-x_{a},\bar{z}-\bar{x}_{a}){\bf S}(\nu_{a},g_{a})=0\,.

(2.37)

It means that in the neighborhood of the marked point

Imposing the moment map constraints (

to the action (

(2.39)

Similarly, the Higgs bundles of types O , I , II and V are
defined by the data

\begin{array}[]{c}(\Phi\,,d_{\bar{A}});\,\prod_{a=1}^{n}T^{*}G^{\mathbb{C}}_{a}\,,~~J=0\,,\\
(\Phi\,,d_{\bar{A}});\,\prod_{a=1}^{n}T^{*}\mathcal{X}_{J,a}\,,~~J=I\,,\,II\,,\,V\,,\end{array}

where

\mathcal{X}_{J,a}
.
The sections of the cotangent bundles

{\bf X}
(

These Higgs bundles are equipped with the symplectic forms

The symplectomorphisms corresponding to

,

Using (

The latter equation

Imposing the moment constraints (
