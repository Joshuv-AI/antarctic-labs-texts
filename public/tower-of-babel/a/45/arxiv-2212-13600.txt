On Ternary F F -manifold Algebras and their Representations
A. Ben Hassine, T. Chtioui, M. Elhamdadi, S. Mabrouk

Abstract
. We introduce a notion of ternary F F -manifold algebras which is a generalization of F F -manifold algebras. We study representation theory of ternary F F -manifold algebras. In particular, we introduce a notion of dual representation which requires additional conditions similar to the binary case. Then, we establish a notion of a coherence ternary F F -manifold algebra. Moreover, we investigate the construction of ternary F F -manifold algebras using F F -manifold algebras. Furthermore, we introduce and investigate a notion of a relative Rota-Baxter operator with respect to a representation and use it to construct ternary pre- F F -manifold algebras.



Keywords:
Ternary
F
-manifold algebra, representation, relative Rota-Baxter operators, ternary pre-
F
-manifold algebras.

2.
Preliminaries

An associative algebra is a pair
(A,\cdot)
, where
A
is a vector space and
\cdot:A\otimes A\longrightarrow A
is a linear map satisfying that for all
x,y,z\in A
, the associator

\mathfrak{as}(x,y,z)=(x\cdot y)\cdot z-x\cdot(y\cdot z)=0
,
i.e.

(x\cdot y)\cdot z=x\cdot(y\cdot z).

Furthermore, if
x\cdot y=y\cdot x
for all
x,y\in A
, then
(A,\cdot)
is called a commutative associative algebra.

A representation of commutative associative algebra
(A,\cdot)
on
a vector space
V
is a linear map

\mu:A\longrightarrow\mathfrak{gl}(V)
, such that for any

x,y\in A
, the following equalities are satisfied:

(2.1)

\displaystyle\mu(x\cdot y)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\mu(x)\mu(y),\forall x,y\in A.

If there exists an element
1_{A}\in A
satisfying
x\cdot 1_{A}=1_{A}\cdot x=x
, then
A
is called unital with unit
1_{A}
.

Let
(V;\mu)
be a representation of a commutative associative algebra
(A,\cdot)
.
In the sequel, define
\mu^{*}:A\longrightarrow\mathfrak{gl}(V^{*})
by

\langle\mu^{*}(x)\xi,v\rangle=-\langle\xi,\mu(x)v\rangle,\quad\forall~x\in A,\xi\in V^{*},v\in V.

Lemma 2.1
.

Under the above notation,
(V^{*},\mu^{*})
is a representation
of
(A,\cdot)
which is called the dual representation of
(V;\mu)
.

A Zinbiel algebra is a pair
(A,\diamond)
, where

A
is a vector space,
\diamond:A\otimes A\longrightarrow A
is
a binary multiplication such that for all
x,y,z\in A
,

(2.2)

x\diamond(y\diamond z)=(y\diamond x)\diamond z+(x\diamond y)\diamond z.

Lemma 2.2
.

Let
(A,\diamond)
be a Zinbiel algebra. Then
(A,\cdot)
is a commutative associative algebra, where
x\cdot y=x\diamond y+y\diamond x
. Moreover, for
x\in A
, define
L_{\diamond}(x):A\longrightarrow\mathfrak{gl}(A)
by

(2.3)

L_{\diamond}(x)(y)=x\diamond y,\quad\forall~y\in A.

Then
(A;L_{\diamond})
is a representation of the commutative associative algebra
(A,\cdot)
.

Definition 2.3
.

A
3
-Lie algebra consists of a vector space
A
equipped with a skew-symmetric linear map called
3
-Lie bracket
[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]:\otimes^{3}A\rightarrow A
such that the following Fundamental Identity holds (for
x_{i}\in A,1\leq i\leq 5
)

(2.4)

[x_{1},x_{2},[x_{3},x_{4},x_{5}]]=[[x_{1},x_{2},x_{3}],x_{4},x_{5}]+[x_{3},[x_{1},x_{2},x_{4}],x_{5}]+[x_{3},x_{4},[x_{1},x_{2},x_{5}]]

In other words, for
x_{1},x_{2}\in A
, the operator

(2.5)

\displaystyle ad_{x_{1},x_{2}}:A\to A,\quad ad_{x_{1},x_{2}}x:=[x_{1},x_{2},x],\quad\forall x\in A,

is a derivation in the sense that

ad_{x_{1},x_{2}}[x_{3},x_{4},x_{5}]=[ad_{x_{1},x_{2}}x_{3},x_{4},x_{5}]+[x_{3},ad_{x_{1},x_{2}}x_{4},x_{5}]+[x_{3},x_{4},ad_{x_{1},x_{2}}x_{5}],\forall x_{3},x_{4},x_{5}\in A.

A morphism between 3-Lie algebras is a linear map that preserves the 3-Lie brackets.

.

Definition 2.5
.

A representation of a 3-Lie algebra
A
on a vector space
V
is a skew-symmetric linear map
\rho:\otimes^{2}A\rightarrow gl(V)
satisfying

\rho(x_{1},x_{2})\rho(x_{3},x_{4})-\rho(x_{3},x_{4})\rho(x_{1},x_{2})=\rho([x_{1},x_{2},x_{3}],x_{4})-\rho([x_{1},x_{2},x_{4}],x_{3})

.

.

.

A

be a

\displaystyle\mathcal{L}_{1}(x,y,u)

(2.15)

\displaystyle\mathcal{L}_{2}(x,y,u)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\mu(x)\rho(y)(u)+\mu(y)\rho(x)(u)-\rho(x\cdot y)(u),

.

Definition 3.1
.

A tuple
(A,\cdot,[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot])
is called a ternary

-manifold algebra if
(A,\cdot)
is a commutative associative algebra and
(A,[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot])
is a

-Lie algebra, such that for all

, holds

(3.18)

\mathcal{L}(x_{1}\cdot x_{2},x_{3},x_{4},x_{5})=x_{1}\cdot\mathcal{L}(x_{2},x_{3},x_{4},x_{5})+x_{2}\cdot\mathcal{L}(x_{1},x_{3},x_{4},x_{5}),

where
\mathcal{L}(x_{1},x_{2},x_{3},x_{4})
is the

-Leibnizator define by

(3.19)

\mathcal{L}(x_{1},x_{2},x_{3},x_{4})=[x_{1},x_{2},x_{3}\cdot x_{4}]-x_{3}\cdot[x_{1},x_{2},x_{4}]-[x_{1},x_{2},x_{3}]\cdot x_{4}.

Example 3.2
.

Every ternary Nambu-Poisson algebras is a ternary
F
-manifold.

Definition 3.3
.

Let
(A,\cdot_{A},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{A})
and
(B,\cdot_{B},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{B})
be two ternary

-manifold algebras. A homomorphism between

(3.20)

\displaystyle f(x_{1}\cdot_{A}x_{2})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle f(x_{1})\cdot_{B}f(x_{2}),

\displaystyle f[x_{1},x_{2},x_{3}]_{A}

Proposition 3.4
.

Let
(A,\cdot_{A},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{A})
and
(B,\cdot_{B},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{B})

is an ternary
F
-manifold algebra, where the product
\cdot_{A\oplus B}
and bracket
[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{A\oplus B}
are given by

\displaystyle(x_{1}+y_{1})\cdot_{A\oplus B}(x_{2}+y_{2})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle x_{1}\cdot_{A}x_{2}+y_{1}\cdot_{B}y_{2},

\displaystyle{[(x_{1}+y_{1}),(x_{2}+y_{2}),(x_{3}+y_{3})]_{A\oplus B}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle[x_{1},x_{2},x_{3}]_{A}+[y_{1},y_{2},y_{3}]_{B}

for all

It easy to check that
(A\oplus B,\cdot_{A\oplus B})
is a commutative associative algebra and
(A\oplus B,[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{A\oplus B})
is a
3
-Lie algebra and for all
x_{i}\in A,y_{i}\in B,~~i=1,\cdots,4

\displaystyle\mathcal{L}_{A\oplus B}(x_{1}+y_{1},x_{2}+y_{2},x_{3}+y_{3},x_{4}+y_{4})=

\displaystyle\mathcal{L}_{A}(x_{1},x_{2},x_{3},x_{4})+\mathcal{L}_{B}(y_{1},y_{2},y_{3},x_{4}).

Using the above identity, we have

\displaystyle\mathcal{L}_{A\oplus B}((x_{1}+y_{1})\cdot_{A\oplus B}(x_{2}+y_{2}),x_{3}+y_{3},x_{4}+y_{4},x_{5}+y_{5})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\mathcal{L}_{A\oplus B}(x_{1}\cdot_{A}x_{2}+y_{1}\cdot_{B}y_{2},x_{3}+y_{3},x_{4}+y_{4},x_{5}+y_{5})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\mathcal{L}_{A}(x_{1}\cdot_{A}x_{2},x_{3},x_{4},x_{5})+\mathcal{L}_{B}(y_{1}\cdot_{B}y_{2},y_{3},y_{4},y_{5})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle x_{1}\cdot_{A}\mathcal{L}_{A}(x_{2},x_{3},x_{4},x_{5})+x_{2}\cdot_{A}\mathcal{L}_{A}(x_{1},x_{3},x_{4},x_{5})

\displaystyle+y_{1}\cdot_{B}\mathcal{L}_{B}(y_{2},y_{3},y_{4},y_{5})+y_{2}\cdot_{B}\mathcal{L}_{B}(y_{1},y_{3},y_{4},y_{5})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(x_{1}+y_{1})\cdot_{A\oplus B}\mathcal{L}_{A\oplus B}(x_{2}+y_{2},x_{3}+y_{3},x_{4}+y_{4},x_{5}+y_{5})

\displaystyle+(x_{2}+y_{2})\cdot_{A\oplus B}\mathcal{L}_{A\oplus B}(x_{1}+y_{1},x_{3}+y_{3},x_{4}+y_{4},x_{5}+y_{5})

Then, Eq. (

Proposition 3.5
.

Let
(A,\cdot_{A},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{A})
be a ternary

be commutative associative algebra. Then

\displaystyle(x_{1}\otimes y_{1})\cdot_{A\otimes B}(x_{2}\otimes y_{2})=(x_{1}\cdot_{A}y_{1})\otimes(x_{2}\cdot_{B}y_{2}),

\displaystyle[x_{1}\otimes y_{1},x_{2}\otimes y_{2},x_{3}\otimes y_{3}]_{A\otimes B}=[x_{1},x_{2},x_{3}]_{A}\otimes(y_{1}\cdot_{B}y_{2}\cdot y_{3}),

for all

Proof.

It is obvious to check that
(A\otimes B,\cdot_{A\otimes B})
is a commutative associative algebra and
(A\otimes B,[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{A\otimes B})
is a
3
-Lie algebra. For any

\displaystyle\mathcal{L}_{A\otimes B}(x_{1}\otimes y_{1},x_{2}\otimes y_{2},x_{3}\otimes y_{3},x_{4}\otimes y_{4})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle[x_{1}\otimes y_{1},x_{2}\otimes y_{2},(x_{3}\otimes y_{3})\cdot_{A\otimes B}(x_{4}\otimes y_{4})]_{A\otimes B}-(x_{3}\otimes y_{3})\cdot_{A\otimes B}[x_{1}\otimes y_{1},x_{2}\otimes y_{2},x_{4}\otimes y_{4}]

\displaystyle-[x_{1}\otimes y_{1},x_{2}\otimes y_{2},x_{3}\otimes y_{3}]\cdot_{A\otimes B}(x_{4}\otimes y_{4})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle[x_{1}\otimes y_{1},x_{2}\otimes y_{2},(x_{3}\cdot_{A}x_{4})\otimes(y_{3}\otimes_{B}y_{4})]_{A\otimes B}-(x_{3}\otimes y_{3})\cdot_{A\otimes B}([x_{1},x_{2},x_{4}]_{A}\otimes(y_{1}\cdot_{B}y_{2}\cdot_{B}y_{4}))

\displaystyle-([x_{1},x_{2},x_{3}]_{A}\otimes(y_{1}\cdot_{B}y_{2}\cdot_{B}y_{3}))\cdot_{A\otimes B}(x_{4}\otimes y_{4})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle[x_{1},x_{2},(x_{3}\cdot_{A}x_{4})]\otimes(y_{1}\cdot_{B}y_{2}\cdot_{B}y_{3}\cdot_{B}y_{4}))-(x_{3}\cdot_{A}[x_{1},x_{2},x_{4}]_{A})\otimes(y_{1}\cdot_{B}y_{2}\cdot_{B}y_{3}\cdot y_{4})

\displaystyle Nx\cdot Ny=N(Nx\cdot y+x\cdot Ny-N(x\cdot y)),\ \forall x,y,\in A.
