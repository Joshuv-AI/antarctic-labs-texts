Electrical resistivity in 2d Kondo lattice systems
Komal Kumari

Abstract
I extend the calculations represented in [ 4 ] regarding the resistivity in Kondo lattice materials from 3 ​ d 3d syatem to 2 ​ d 2d systems. In the present work I consider a 2d system, and memory function is computed. However, results found in 2d case are different from 3d system . I find that in 2 ​ d 2d in low temperature regime( k B ​ T ≪ μ d k_{B}T\ll\mu_{d} ) resistivity shows power law( 1 T \frac{1}{T} ) behaviour and in the high temeprature regime( k B ​ T ≫ μ d k_{B}T\gg\mu_{d} ) resistivity varies linearly with temperature. In 3 ​ d 3d these behaviours are as 1 T \frac{1}{T} and as T 3 2 T^{\frac{3}{2}} respectively.
