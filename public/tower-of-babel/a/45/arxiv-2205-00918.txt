Decay estimate of bivariate Chebyshev coefficients for functions with limited smoothness
Akansha

Abstract
We obtain the decay bounds for Chebyshev series coefficients of functions with finite Vitali variation on the unit square. A generalization of the well known identity, which relates exact and approximated coefficients, obtained using the quadrature formula, is derived. Finally, an asymptotic L 1 L^{1} -approximation error of finite partial sum for functions of bounded variation in sense of Vitali as well as Hardy-Krause, on the unit square is deduced.



Key words.
Functions of Vitali variation, bivariate Chebeshev approximation, Chebyshev decay bounds

\sum_{i=0}^{d_{x}}{\vphantom{\sum}}{}^{\prime}\sum_{j=0}^{d_{y}}{\vphantom{\sum}}{}^{\prime}\left|c_{i,j}-\tilde{c}_{i,j}\right|\leq\sum_{j=0}^{d_{y}}{\vphantom{\sum}}{}^{\prime}\sum_{k_{x}=1}^{\infty}\sum_{i=2k_{x}n_{x}-d_{x}}^{2k_{x}n_{x}+d_{x}}|c_{i,j}|+\sum_{i=0}^{d_{x}}{\vphantom{\sum}}{}^{\prime}\sum_{k_{y}=1}^{\infty}\sum_{j=2k_{y}n_{y}-d_{y}}^{2k_{y}n_{y}+d_{y}}|c_{i,j}|+\sum_{k_{x}=1}^{\infty}\sum_{k_{y}=1}^{\infty}\sum_{i=2k_{x}n_{x}-d_{x}}^{2k_{x}n_{x}+d_{x}}\sum_{j=2k_{y}n_{y}-d_{y}}^{2k_{y}n_{y}+d_{y}}|c_{i,j}|.

For
d_{x}=n_{x}-l_{x},l_{x}=1,2,\ldots,n_{x}-k
and
d_{y}=n_{y}-l_{y},l_{y}=1,2,\ldots,n_{y}-l
, we can write

\displaystyle\|f-\tilde{\mathsf{C}}_{d_{x},d_{y}}[f]\|_{1}\leq

\displaystyle 4\left(\sum_{i=n_{x}-l_{x}+1}^{\infty}\sum_{j=n_{y}-l_{y}+1}^{\infty}\left|c_{i,j}\right|+\sum_{k_{x}=1}^{\infty}\sum_{k_{y}=1}^{\infty}\sum_{i=(2k_{x}-1)n_{x}+l_{x}}^{(2k_{x}+1)n_{x}-l_{x}}\sum_{j=(2k_{y}-1)n_{y}+l_{y}}^{(2k_{y}+1)n_{y}-l_{y}}|c_{i,j}|\right.

\displaystyle\left.+\sum_{j=0}^{d_{y}}{\vphantom{\sum}}{}^{\prime}\sum_{k_{x}=1}^{\infty}\sum_{i=(2k_{x}-1)n_{x}+l_{x}}^{(2k_{x}+1)n_{x}-l_{x}}|c_{i,j}|+\sum_{i=0}^{d_{x}}{\vphantom{\sum}}{}^{\prime}\sum_{k_{y}=1}^{\infty}\sum_{j=(2k_{y}-1)n_{y}+l_{y}}^{(2k_{y}+1)n_{y}-l_{y}}|c_{i,j}|\right)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle 4\left(2\sum_{i=n_{x}-l_{x}+1}^{\infty}\sum_{j=n_{y}-l_{y}+1}^{\infty}\left|c_{i,j}\right|+\sum_{j=0}^{n_{y}-l_{y}}{\vphantom{\sum}}{}^{\prime}\sum_{i=n_{x}-l_{x}+1}^{\infty}\left|c_{i,j}\right|+\sum_{i=0}^{n_{x}-l_{x}}{\vphantom{\sum}}{}^{\prime}\sum_{j=n_{y}-l_{y}+1}^{\infty}\left|c_{i,j}\right|\right).

(3.17)

From the hypothesis, we have
d_{x}(=n_{x}-l_{x})\geq k
and
d_{y}(=n_{y}-l_{y})\geq l
hence, we can use the decay bounds from Theorem
3.1
and
3.2
in the above expression. Using the decay bounds and the telescopic property of the resulting series, we get

\sum_{i=n_{x}-l_{x}+1}^{\infty}\sum_{j=n_{y}-l_{y}+1}^{\infty}\left|c_{i,j}\right|\leq\dfrac{4V_{k,l}}{4kl\pi^{2}}\begin{cases}\Pi_{1}[s](n_{x}-l_{x})\Pi_{1}[r](n_{y}-l_{y}),\\
~~~~~~~~~\mbox{if }k=2s,l=2r,\\
\Pi_{1}[s](n_{x}-l_{x})\Pi_{0}[r](n_{y}-l_{y}),\\
~~~~~~~~~\mbox{if }k=2s,l=2r+1,\\
\Pi_{0}[s](n_{x}-l_{x})\Pi_{1}[r](n_{y}-l_{y}),\\
~~~~~~~~~\mbox{if }k=2s+1,l=2r,\\
\Pi_{0}[s](n_{x}-l_{x})\Pi_{0}[r](n_{y}-l_{y}),\\
~~~~~~~~~\mbox{if }k=2s+1,l=2r+1,\end{cases}

(3.18)

\sum_{j=0}^{n_{y}-l_{y}}{\vphantom{\sum}}{}^{\prime}\sum_{i=n_{x}-l_{x}+1}^{\infty}\left|c_{i,j}\right|\leq(n_{y}-l_{y}+1)\dfrac{4V_{k}}{2k\pi^{2}}\begin{cases}\Pi_{1}[s](n_{x}-l_{x}),~~~~~~~~~\mbox{if }k=2s,\\
\Pi_{0}[s](n_{x}-l_{x}),~~~~~~~~~\mbox{if }k=2s+1,\end{cases}

(3.19)

and

\sum_{i=0}^{n_{x}-l_{x}}{\vphantom{\sum}}{}^{\prime}\sum_{j=n_{y}-l_{y}+1}^{\infty}\left|c_{i,j}\right|\leq(n_{x}-l_{x}+1)\dfrac{4V_{l}}{2l\pi^{2}}\begin{cases}\Pi_{1}[r](n_{y}-l_{y}),~~~~~~~~~\mbox{if }l=2r,\\
\Pi_{0}[r](n_{y}-l_{y}),~~~~~~~~~\mbox{if }l=2r+1,\end{cases}

(3.20)

where

\Pi_{\alpha}[p](n^{*})=\dfrac{1}{\displaystyle\prod_{m=-p}^{p-\alpha}(n^{*}+2m+\alpha)}+\dfrac{1}{\displaystyle\prod_{m=-p}^{p-\alpha}(n^{*}+2m+\alpha+1)}\leq\dfrac{2}{(n^{*}-2p+\alpha)^{2p-\alpha+1}}.

Conclusion

We obtained decay estimates and
L^{1}
-convergence results for bivariate Chebyshev approximations for functions of limited regularity. In general, low-rank approximations of the coefficient tensor are used to approximate 2D functions (see
[
14
]
). However, we are inclined towards the nonlinear approximation methods to approximate functions with discontinuities and using these decay bounds, one can obtain convergence results for Chebyshev-based nonlinear approximation methods, for instance, the bivariate Padé-Chebyshev method.
