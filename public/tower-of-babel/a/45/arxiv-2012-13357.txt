Genera of numerical semigroups and polynomial identities for degrees of syzygies
Leonid G. Fel

Abstract
We derive polynomial identities of arbitrary degree n n for syzygies degrees of numerical semigroups S m = ⟨ d 1 , … , d m ⟩ S_{m}\!=\!\langle d_{1},\ldots,d_{m}\rangle and show that for n ≥ m n\geq m they contain higher genera G r = ∑ s ∈ ℤ > ∖ S m s r G_{r}\!=\!\sum_{s\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{>}\!\!\setminus S_{m}}s^{r} of S m S_{m} . We find a number g m = B m − m + 1 g_{m}\!=\!B_{m}-m+1 of algebraically independent genera G r G_{r} and equations, related any of g m + 1 g_{m}+1 genera, where B m = ∑ k = 1 m − 1 β k B_{m}\!=\!\sum_{k=1}^{m-1}\beta_{k} and β k \beta_{k} denote the total and partial Betti numbers of non-symmetric semigroups. The number g m g_{m} is strongly dependent on symmetry of S m S_{m} and decreases for symmetric semigroups and complete intersections. Keywords: numerical semigroups, degrees of syzygies, the Frobenius number and genus 2010 Mathematics Subject Classification: Primary – 20M14, Secondary – 11P81.
