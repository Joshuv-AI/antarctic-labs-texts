Null Dimensional Reduction of M2-Brane
J. Klusoň

Abstract
We perform a general reduction of an M2-brane on a space-time that admits a null Killing vector leading to fundamental string and D2-brane action in Newton-Cartan background.

Non-Relativistic String

In this section we proceed to the analysis of null dimensional reduction of M-theory and corresponding extended probe which is M2-brane. To do this we presume that the background
metric has light-like isometry

[
31
,
32
]
, for recent extended analysis, see
[
14
]

\displaystyle ds^{2}=g_{MN}dx^{M}dx^{N}=2T_{\mu}dx^{\mu}(du-M_{\nu}dx^{\nu})+h_{\mu\nu}dx^{\mu}dx^{\nu}\ ,

\displaystyle g_{\mu u}=T_{\mu}\ ,\quad g_{\mu\nu}\equiv\hat{H}_{\mu\nu}=H_{\mu\nu}-M_{\mu}T_{\nu}-T_{\mu}M_{\nu}\ ,

where
\mu,\nu=0,1,\dots,9
and where
u-
is light-like coordinate.

In this section we consider M2-brane extended along light-like direction
u
. To do this we
use Polyakov form of M2-brane action (
3
) where we now presume that three dimensional world-volume metric has light-like isometry so that it can be written in the form

\gamma_{\alpha u}=\tau_{\alpha}\ ,\quad\gamma_{\alpha\beta}=h_{\alpha\beta}-m_{\alpha}\tau_{\beta}-\tau_{\alpha}m_{\beta}\ .

(21)

Then we have

\det\gamma=\left|\begin{array}[]{cc}0&\tau_{\beta}\\
\tau_{\alpha}&h_{\alpha\beta}-m_{\alpha}\tau_{\beta}-m_{\beta}\tau_{\alpha}\\
\end{array}\right|=\left|\begin{array}[]{cc}0&\tau_{\beta}\\
\tau_{\alpha}&h_{\alpha\beta}\\
\end{array}\right|=\det(-\tau_{\alpha}\tau_{\beta}+h_{\alpha\beta})\ .

(22)

We further have an inverse metric

\gamma^{uu}=2\Phi\ ,\quad\gamma^{u\alpha}=-\hat{v}^{\alpha}\ ,\quad\gamma^{\alpha\beta}=h^{\alpha\beta}\ ,

(23)

where these fields obey the relation

h^{\alpha\beta}h_{\beta\gamma}-\tau_{\gamma}v^{\alpha}=\delta^{\alpha}_{\gamma}\ ,\quad\tau_{\alpha}v^{\alpha}=-1\ ,\quad h_{\alpha\beta}v^{\beta}=h^{\alpha\beta}\tau_{\beta}=0\ ,

(24)

and where

\hat{v}^{\alpha}=v^{\alpha}-h^{\alpha\beta}m_{\beta}\ ,\quad\Phi=-v^{\alpha}m_{\alpha}+\frac{1}{2}h^{\alpha\beta}m_{\alpha}m_{\beta}\ .

(25)

Finally using the fact that the induced metric has the form

g_{uu}=0\ ,\quad g_{u\alpha}=g_{u\mu}\partial_{\alpha}x^{\mu}=T_{\alpha}\ ,\quad g_{\alpha\beta}=\hat{H}_{\mu\nu}\partial_{\alpha}x^{\mu}\partial_{\beta}x^{\nu}

(26)

we obtain following action

\displaystyle S=-\frac{T_{M2}}{2}\int du\int d^{2}\xi\sqrt{-\det(-\tau_{\alpha}\tau_{\beta}+h_{\alpha\beta})}\times

\displaystyle\times(-2\hat{v}^{\alpha}T_{\alpha}+h^{\alpha\beta}\hat{H}_{\beta\alpha}-1)\ .

Generally
\int du\rightarrow\infty
since
u
is non-compact coordinate. Then we can rescale M2-brane tension as
T_{M2}=\frac{T_{FS}}{\int du}
so that

T_{FS}=T_{M2}\int du\ .

(28)

In summary, we have an action for non-relativistic string in the form

S=-\frac{T_{FS}}{2}\int d^{2}\xi\sqrt{-\det(-\tau_{\alpha}\tau_{\beta}+h_{\alpha\beta})}(-2\hat{v}^{\alpha}T_{\alpha}+h^{\alpha\beta}\hat{H}_{\beta\alpha}-1)\ .

(29)

This is final form of non-relativistic string that is defined by double dimensional reduction of M2-brane along null direction.

The structure of the action
(
29
) suggests that it has non-relativistic form on the world-sheet
due to the presence of two dimensional Newton-Cartan metric. However in order
to gain more insight into its structure let us solve equations of motion for
h^{\alpha\beta},v^{\alpha}
and
m_{\alpha}
.
To do this we use the fact that the matrix
\mathbf{A}_{\alpha\beta}=-\tau_{\alpha}\tau_{\beta}+h_{\alpha\beta}
is non-singular and hence
it has an inverse matrix in the form

\mathbf{A}^{\alpha\beta}=-v^{\alpha}v^{\beta}+h^{\alpha\beta}
so that

\det\mathbf{A}_{\alpha\beta}=\frac{1}{\det\mathbf{A}^{\alpha\beta}}\ .

(30)

Then we can replace determinant
\det\mathbf{A}_{\alpha\beta}
with

\frac{1}{\det\mathbf{A}^{\alpha\beta}}
and hence (
29
)
can be written in the form

S=-\frac{T_{FS}}{2}\int d^{2}\xi\frac{1}{\sqrt{-\det\mathbf{A}^{\alpha\beta}}}(-2\hat{v}^{\alpha}T_{\alpha}+h^{\alpha\beta}\hat{H}_{\beta\alpha}-1)\ .

(31)

Now we are ready to perform variation of this action with respect to

v^{\alpha},h^{\alpha\beta}
and
m_{\alpha}
treating them as independent variables.
Firstly we get equation of motion for
v^{\alpha}

\displaystyle\tau_{\alpha}(-2\hat{v}^{\beta}T_{\beta}+h^{\gamma\delta}\hat{H}_{\delta\gamma}-1)-2T_{\alpha}=0\ .

Further, equations of motion for
h^{\alpha\beta}
have the form

\displaystyle(-\tau_{\alpha}\tau_{\beta}+h_{\alpha\beta})(-2\hat{v}^{\gamma}T_{\gamma}+h^{\gamma\delta}\hat{H}_{\delta\gamma}-1)-2(H_{\alpha\beta}+(m_{\alpha}-M_{\alpha})T_{\beta}+T_{\alpha}(m_{\alpha}-M_{\beta}))=0

and finally equations of motion for
m_{\alpha}
has the form

h^{\alpha\beta}T_{\beta}=0\ .

(34)

This is very important equation that has natural solution when we identify

\tau_{\alpha}=T_{\alpha}\ .

(35)

Then the equation (
3

h^{\alpha\beta}H_{\beta\alpha}-1=0

(36)

This equation has clearly solution in the form

(37)

where
k_{\alpha}=K_{\mu}\partial_{\alpha}x^{\mu}
is arbitrary two dimensional vector.
To see this let us consider the defining equation

h^{\alpha\beta}h_{\beta\gamma}-v^{\alpha}\tau_{\gamma}=\delta^{\alpha}_{\gamma}\ .

(38)

Taking its trace we get

h^{\alpha\beta}h_{\beta\alpha}-v^{\alpha}\tau_{\alpha}=2

(39)

that implies

h^{\alpha\beta}h_{\beta\alpha}=1

(40)

using the fact that
v^{\alpha}\tau_{\alpha}=1

(

-2\tau_{\alpha}\tau_{\beta}-2(m_{\alpha}-M_{\alpha}+k_{\alpha})T_{\beta}-2T_{\alpha}(m_{\beta}-M_{\beta}+k_{\beta})=0

m_{\alpha}=M_{\alpha}+\frac{T_{\alpha}}{2}-k_{\alpha}\ .

Inserting (

S=-T_{FS}\int d^{2}\xi\sqrt{-\det\mathbf{A}_{\alpha\beta}}=T_{FS}\int d^{2}\xi\sqrt{-\det(-T_{\alpha}T_{\beta}+H_{\alpha\beta}-k_{\alpha}T_{\beta}-T_{\alpha}k_{\beta})}\ .

(43)

This is the final form of the action for fundamental string in theory that arises through
null reduction of M-theory. We see that it has Nambu-Goto form despite
of the fact that its Polyakov like form contains world-sheet metric that is non-relativistic. Further, we see that there is family of Newton-Cartan metrics
corresponding to given null background due to the presence of the arbitrary vector
k_{\alpha}
. This is characteristic property of the Newton-Cartan background that is
defined with the help of null reduction of higher dimensional background.

Hamiltonian Formalism

In this section we find Hamiltonian formulation of the non-relativistic string
with the action given in (
43
).
From (
43
) we obtain following
conjugate momenta

p_{\mu}=-T_{FS}\mathbf{A}_{\mu\nu}\partial_{\beta}x^{\nu}\mathbf{A}^{\beta 0}\sqrt{-\det\mathbf{A}}\ ,

(44)

where
\mathbf{A}_{\mu\nu}=-T_{\mu}T_{\nu}+H_{\mu\nu}+k_{\mu}T_{\nu}+T_{\mu}k_{\nu}
. It is important to stress
that the matrix

is non-singular and hence it has an inverse matrix equal to

\mathbf{A}^{\mu\nu}=H^{\mu\nu}-\frac{1}{1+2\Phi}\hat{V}^{\mu}\hat{V}^{\nu}\ ,

(45)

where

\hat{V}^{\mu}=V^{\mu}-H^{\mu\nu}k_{\nu}\ ,\quad\Phi=-k_{\mu}V^{\mu}+\frac{1}{2}k_{\mu}H^{\mu\nu}k_{\nu}\ .

(46)

Then the bare Hamiltonian is equal to

\mathcal{H}_{B}=p_{\mu}\partial_{0}x^{\mu}-\mathcal{L}=-T_{FS}\mathbf{A}_{0\beta}\mathbf{A}^{\beta 0}+\sqrt{-\det\mathbf{A}}=0

(47)

as we should expect for theory that is invariant under two dimensional diffeomorphism. On the other hand we have two primary constraints that follow
from definition of momenta
p_{\mu}
(
44
)

\mathcal{H}_{S}=p_{\mu}\partial_{1}x^{\mu}\approx 0\

(48)

and also

\mathcal{H}_{T}=p_{\mu}\mathbf{A}^{\mu\nu}p_{\nu}+T_{FS}^{2}\mathbf{A}_{\mu\nu}\partial_{1}x^{\mu}\partial_{1}x^{\nu}\approx 0\ .

(49)

Then the total Hamiltonian is the sum of two primary constraints

H=\int d\xi^{1}(N^{S}\mathcal{H}_{S}+N^{T}\mathcal{H}_{T})\ .

(50)

It would be simple exercise to show that
\mathcal{H}_{S},\mathcal{H}_{T}
are first class constraints
which again reflect invariance of the action under two dimensional diffeomorphism.

). In summary, the extended form of M2-brane action has the form

\displaystyle S=-\frac{T_{M2}}{2}\int d^{3}\xi\sqrt{-\gamma}(\gamma^{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}(\hat{H}_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}+Y_{\bar{\alpha}}T_{\bar{\beta}}+T_{\bar{\alpha}}Y_{\bar{\beta}})+

\displaystyle+\gamma^{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}Y_{\bar{\alpha}}Y_{\bar{\beta}}+A_{\bar{\alpha}}\gamma^{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}Y_{\bar{\beta}}+B_{\bar{\alpha}}\gamma^{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}Y_{\bar{\beta}}+A_{\bar{\alpha}}\gamma^{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}B_{\bar{\beta}}-1)+\frac{T_{M2}}{2}\int d^{3}\xi\epsilon^{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}\bar{\gamma}}F_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}Y_{\bar{\gamma}}\ ,

where
F_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}=\partial_{\bar{\alpha}}A_{\bar{\beta}}-\partial_{\bar{\beta}}A_{\bar{\alpha}}
.
Finally we eliminate
Y_{\bar{\alpha}}
by solving their equations of motion that follow from the action above and we get

\displaystyle-\sqrt{-\gamma}\gamma^{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}(Y_{\bar{\beta}}+T_{\bar{\beta}}+\frac{1}{2}A_{\bar{\beta}}+\frac{1}{2}B_{\bar{\beta}})+\frac{1}{2}\epsilon^{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}\bar{\gamma}}F_{\bar{\beta}\bar{\gamma}}=0\ .

From this equation we can express
Y_{\bar{\alpha}}
as

Y_{\bar{\alpha}}=\frac{1}{2\sqrt{-\gamma}}\gamma_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\delta}}\epsilon^{\bar{\delta}\bar{\beta}\bar{\gamma}}F_{\bar{\beta}\bar{\gamma}}-\frac{1}{2}(A_{\bar{\alpha}}+B_{\bar{\alpha}}+2T_{\bar{\alpha}})\ .

(59)

Then inserting this result into the action (
5
)
we obtain

\displaystyle S=-\frac{T_{M2}}{2}\int d^{3}\xi\sqrt{-\gamma}(\gamma^{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}\hat{H}_{\bar{\beta}\bar{\alpha}}-\frac{1}{2}F_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}\gamma^{\bar{\beta}\bar{\beta}^{\prime}}F_{\bar{\beta}^{\prime}\bar{\alpha}^{\prime}}\gamma^{\bar{\alpha}^{\prime}\bar{\alpha}}-

\displaystyle-\frac{1}{4}(A-B)_{\bar{\alpha}}\gamma^{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}(A-B)_{\bar{\beta}}-(A+B)_{\bar{\alpha}}\gamma^{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}T_{\bar{\beta}}-T_{\bar{\alpha}}\gamma^{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}T_{\bar{\beta}}-1)

\displaystyle-\frac{T_{M2}}{4}\int d^{3}\xi\epsilon^{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}\bar{\gamma}}F_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}(A_{\bar{\gamma}}+B_{\bar{\gamma}}+2T_{\bar{\gamma}})\

using the fact that

\displaystyle\frac{1}{(\sqrt{-\gamma})^{2}}F_{\bar{\gamma}\bar{\beta}}\epsilon^{\bar{\gamma}\bar{\beta}\bar{\delta}}\gamma_{\bar{\delta}\bar{\alpha}}\epsilon^{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\omega}\bar{\sigma}}F_{\bar{\omega}\bar{\sigma}}=-2F_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}\gamma^{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\omega}}\gamma^{\bar{\beta}\bar{\delta}}F_{\bar{\omega}\bar{\delta}}\ .

As the next step we introduce two fields
X_{\bar{\alpha}}=A_{\bar{\alpha}}-B_{\bar{\alpha}}
and
2Z_{\bar{\alpha}}=A_{\bar{\alpha}}+B_{\bar{\alpha}}
. We see that the equations of motion for
X_{\bar{\alpha}}
implies
that
X_{\bar{\alpha}}=0
. Then the action has the form

\displaystyle S=-\frac{T_{M2}}{2}\int d^{3}\xi\sqrt{-\gamma}(\gamma^{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}(-T_{\bar{\alpha}}T_{\bar{\beta}}+H_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}-(M_{\bar{\alpha}}+Z_{\bar{\alpha}})T_{\bar{\beta}}+T_{\bar{\alpha}}(M_{\bar{\beta}}+Z_{\bar{\beta}}))-

\displaystyle-\frac{1}{2}F_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}\gamma^{\bar{\beta}\bar{\beta}^{\prime}}F_{\bar{\beta}^{\prime}\bar{\alpha}^{\prime}}\gamma^{\bar{\alpha}^{\prime}\bar{\alpha}}-1)-\frac{T_{M2}}{2}\int d^{3}\xi\epsilon^{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}\bar{\gamma}}F_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}(Z_{\bar{\gamma}}+T_{\bar{\gamma}})\ .

Note that this is similar form of the action as was derived in

[
36
]
in case of standard dimensional reduction of M-theory.

Finally we should solve equations of motion for
\gamma^{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}
that follow from the action above

\displaystyle-\frac{1}{2}\gamma_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}(\gamma^{\bar{\gamma}\bar{\delta}}\mathbf{H}_{\bar{\delta}\bar{\gamma}}-\frac{1}{2}F_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}\gamma^{\bar{\beta}\bar{\beta}^{\prime}}F_{\bar{\beta}^{\prime}\bar{\alpha}^{\prime}}\gamma^{\bar{\alpha}^{\prime}\bar{\alpha}}-1)+\mathbf{H}_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}-F_{\bar{\gamma}\bar{\alpha}}F_{\bar{\beta}\bar{\delta}}\gamma^{\bar{\delta}\bar{\gamma}}=0\ ,

where we defined

\mathbf{H}_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}=-T_{\bar{\alpha}}T_{\bar{\beta}}+H_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}-(M_{\bar{\alpha}}+Z_{\bar{\alpha}})T_{\bar{\beta}}-(M_{\bar{\beta}}+Z_{\bar{\beta}})T_{\bar{\alpha}}\ .

(64)

In the leading order approximation it has solution
[
36
]

\gamma_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}=\mathbf{H}_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}
so that in the leading order approximation the action has the form

\displaystyle S=-T_{M2}\int d^{3}\xi\sqrt{-\det\mathbf{H}_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}}(1-\frac{1}{4}F_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\alpha}^{\prime}}\mathbf{H}^{\bar{\alpha}^{\prime}\bar{\delta}}F_{\bar{\delta}\bar{\gamma}}\mathbf{H}^{\bar{\gamma}\bar{\alpha}})=

\displaystyle\approx-T_{M2}\int d^{3}\xi\sqrt{-\det\mathbf{H}_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}(1-\frac{1}{2}F_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\alpha}^{\prime}}\mathbf{H}^{\bar{\alpha}^{\prime}\bar{\delta}}F_{\bar{\delta}\bar{\gamma}}\mathbf{H}^{\bar{\gamma}\bar{\alpha}})}=

\displaystyle=-T_{M2}\int d^{3}\xi\sqrt{-\det(\mathbf{H}_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}+F_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}})\ ,}

where in the last step we used relations that hold in three dimensions

\det(\mathbf{H}_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}+F_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}})=\det\mathbf{H}_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}\det(\delta_{\bar{\alpha}}^{\bar{\beta}}+F_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\gamma}}\mathbf{H}^{\bar{\gamma}\bar{\beta}})=\det\mathbf{H}_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}\left(1-\frac{1}{2}F_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\alpha}^{\prime}}\mathbf{H}^{\bar{\alpha}^{\prime}\bar{\delta}}F_{\bar{\delta}\bar{\gamma}}\mathbf{H}^{\bar{\gamma}\bar{\alpha}}\right)\ .

(66)

The action (
5
) is final form of D2-brane action in non-relativistic string
theory that is defined by null dimensional reduction of M-theory. We again observe an interesting fact that given M-theory background with null isometry is mapped into
family of Newton-Cartan backgrounds that differ by redefinition of
M_{\mu}
. In particular, choosing
Z_{\mu}=-M_{\mu}
we get the action

S=-T_{M2}\int d^{3}\xi\sqrt{-\det(-T_{\bar{\alpha}}T_{\bar{\beta}}+H_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}+F_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}})}-\frac{T_{M2}}{2}\int d^{3}\xi\epsilon^{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}\bar{\gamma}}F_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}(T_{\bar{\gamma}}-M_{\bar{\gamma}})\ .

(67)

In summary, we found D2-brane action in the theory that is defined by null dimensional reduction of M-theory. It would be certainly interesting to extend this analysis to the case of M5-brane and its null dimensional reduction and study how
the procedure suggested in this paper could be related to the recent discussion of null dimensional reduction of M5-branes presented in

[
33
]
.
