A Direct Construction of Cross Z-Complementary Sets with Flexible Lengths and Large Zero Correlation Zone
Praveen Kumar, Sudhan Majhi, Subhabrata Paul

Abstract
This letter proposes a direct construction for cross Z-complementary sets (CZCSs) with flexible lengths and a large zero correlation zone (ZCZ). CZCS is an extension of the cross Z-complementary pair (CZCP). The maximum possible ZCZ width of a CZCP is half of its sequence length. In this letter, for the first time, a generalized Boolean function based construction of CZCSs with a large number of constituent sequences and a ZCZ ratio of 2 / 3 2/3 is presented. For integers m m and δ \delta , the proposed construction produces CZCS with length expressed as 2 m − 1 + 2 δ 2^{m-1}+2^{\delta} ( 0 ≤ δ < m − 1 , m ≥ 4 0\leq\delta<m-1,m\geq 4 ), where both odd and even lengths CZCS can be obtained. Additionally, the constructed CZCS also feature a complementary set of the same length. Finally, the proposed construction is compared with the existing works.

Preliminaries

This section explains the fundamental definitions, notations, and previously known results required for the proposed construction.

Definition
1

Let
\mathbf{u}

=(u_{0},u_{1},\ldots,u_{N-1})

\sum_{i=0}^{N-1+\tau}\omega^{u_{i-\tau}-v_{i}},&-N+1\leq\tau\leq-1,\\
0,&|\tau|\geq N,\end{cases}

.

Definition
2

A pair of sequences

and
\mathbf{v}

Z

\mathcal{A}({\mathbf{u}})(\tau)+\mathcal{A}({\mathbf{v}})(\tau)=0,\text{ for all }0<\tau<Z.

(2)

The pair
(\mathbf{u},\mathbf{v})
is referred to as a GCP when
Z=N
.

Definition
3

A set

M

(M,N,Z)
-CZCS if it satisfies the following two conditions

\displaystyle{\displaystyle C1}:\sum_{i=0}^{M-1}\mathcal{A}({\mathbf{a}_{i}})(\tau)=0\text{, for all }|\tau|\in\mathcal{T}_{1}\cup\mathcal{T}_{2};

(3)

\displaystyle{\displaystyle C2}:\sum_{i=0}^{M-1}\mathcal{C}({\mathbf{a}_{i},\mathbf{a}_{i+1}})(\tau)=0,\text{ for all }|\tau|\in\mathcal{T}_{2},

where
\mathbf{a}_{M}=\mathbf{a}_{0}
and
\mathcal{T}_{1}=\{1,2,\ldots,Z\}

. For an
(M,N,Z)
-CZCS,

Corresponding to a GBF

IV
Conclusion

We proposed a direct construction of CZCS with variable lengths and large ZCZ width. The proposed GBF based construction generates CZCSs with a ZCZ ratio upto
2/3
. The proposed CZCSs contain a large number of constituent sequences and generate both even and odd length sequences. Compared to the existing construction, the proposed construction is direct, generates CZCSs with larger ZCZ width and flexible lengths, and generalizes many existing works.
