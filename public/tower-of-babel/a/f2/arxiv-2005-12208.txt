But the SP equation describes the evolution of a short pulse in nonlinear media if the pulse center is far from the nearest resonance frequency of the material’s susceptibility. In some sense it represents the opposite extreme from the NLS approximation since that results from expanding the susceptibility in the frequency while equation results from expanding the susceptibility in the wavelength, which means the SP equation provides an increasingly better approximation to the corresponding solution of the Maxwell equations as the pulse duration shortens
