Nonlinear Landau damping for the 2d Vlasov-Poisson system with massless electrons around Penrose-stable equilibria
Lingjia Huang, Quoc-Hung Nguyen and Yiran Xu

Abstract
In this paper, we prove the nonlinear asymptotic stability of the Penrose-stable equilibria among solutions of the 2 ​ d 2d Vlasov-Poisson system with massless electrons.

Introduction

This paper is devoted to study the Vlasov-Poisson system of the form:

\begin{cases}\partial_{t}\mathbf{f}+v\cdot\nabla_{x}\mathbf{f}+E\cdot\nabla_{v}\mathbf{f}=0,\\
E=-\nabla_{x}U,-\Delta U+U=\varrho-1+A(U),~~\varrho(t,x)=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\mathbf{f}(t,x,v)dv,\\
\end{cases}

(1.1)

on the whole space
x\in\mathbb{R}^{2}
,
v\in\mathbb{R}^{2}
, where
\mathbf{f}=\mathbf{f}(t,x,v)\geq 0
is the probability distribution of charged particles in plasma,
\varrho(t,x)
is the electric charge density, and
E=E(t,x)
is electric field and
A:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}
is smooth and satisfies
A(r)=\mathcal{O}(r^{2})
as
r\to 0
. In particular, the system is for massless electrons or ions when
A(r)=r+1-e^{r}
.
This system was extensively studied (
[

,

,

,

,

,

,

,

,

17

,
12
]
), focusing on the global existence, regularity results and longtime behavior of solutions. We are interested in the asymptotic stability of solutions
f_{i}
to (
1.1
) in the form

\mathbf{f}(t,x,v)=\mu(v)+f(t,x,v)

where
\mu(v)
is a stable equilibrium with
\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\mu(v)dv=1
and
\mathbf{f}(t=0,x,v)
closes to
\mu(v)
. So,
f
solves the following perturbed system

\begin{cases}\partial_{t}f+v\cdot\nabla_{x}f+E\cdot\nabla_{v}\mu=-E\cdot\nabla_{v}f,\\
E=-\nabla_{x}U,-\Delta U+U=\rho+A(U),~~\rho(t,x)=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}f(t,x,v)dv,\\
f|_{t=0}=f_{0}.\end{cases}

(1.2)

The following are assumptions on
\mu
and
A
in this paper.

•

Assumption 1:

\mu
satisfies the
Penrose stability condition
:

\inf_{\tau\in\mathbb{R},\xi\in\mathbb{R}^{2}}\left|1-\int_{0}^{+\infty}e^{-i\tau s}\frac{1}{1+|\xi|^{2}}i\xi\cdot\widehat{\nabla_{v}\mu}(s\xi)ds\right|\geq\text{\r{c}},

(1.3)

for some constant
\text{\r{c}}>0
,
\widehat{\nabla_{v}\mu}
is the Fourier transform of
\nabla_{v}\mu
in
\mathbb{R}^{2}
.

•

Assumption 2:

\mu\in L^{1}
satisfies

\|\langle\cdot\rangle^{3}\nabla_{v}\mu(\cdot)\|_{W^{2,\infty}}+\|\langle\cdot\rangle^{7}\nabla\mu(\cdot)\|_{W^{11,1}}\leq M^{*},

for some
M^{*}<\infty
.

•

Assumption 3:

A:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}
is
C^{3}
and satisfies

\displaystyle\sup_{|r|\leq 1}\left(\left|\frac{A(r)}{r^{2}}\right|+\left|\frac{A^{\prime}(r)}{r}\right|+\left|{A^{\prime\prime}(r)}\right|+\left|{A^{(3)}(r)}\right|\right)\leq C_{A},

for some constant
C_{A}>0
.

Note that a particular example for the Assumption 3 is
A(r)=r+1-e^{r}
corresponding to the Vlasov–Poisson system with electrons mass, see
[
10
]
.

Under the Penrose condition (

[

by Mouhot and Villani in the case

with Gevrey or analytic data (see also
[

In the whole space
\mathbb{R}^{d}\times\mathbb{R}^{d}
with

A(U)=0
) in
[

by Bedrossian, Masmoudi, and Mouhot with Sobolev data. Their proof relies on the dispersive mechanism in Fourier space to control the plasma echo resonance. However, a decay in time of their result is far from optimal. In
[

\sum_{j=0,1}\left[(1+t)^{j}\|\nabla^{i}\rho(t)\|_{L^{1}}+(1+t)^{d+j}\|\nabla^{i}\rho(t)\|_{L^{\infty}}\right]\lesssim\varepsilon_{0}\log(t+2),~~\forall~t>0,

(1.4)

[
34
]
. Note that the problem (
1.2
) in dimension

is
critical and open
. Recently, in
[
28

Ionescu, Pausader, Wang, and Widmayer proved the first asymptotic stability result for the unscreened Vlasov-Poisson system (i.e.,

) in

around the Poisson equilibrium, see also in
[
35
]
for the case of a repulsive point charge. The unscreened case is open for the general equilibria. However, in
[
21

7

they studied the linearized unscreened Vlasov-Poisson equation around suitably stable analytic homogeneous equilibria in
\mathbb{R}^{d}_{x}\times\mathbb{R}^{d}_{v}

Very recently, authors in
[
24

established new estimates and cancellations of the kernel to the linearized problem ( see Proposition

\displaystyle\sum_{j=0,1}\sum_{p=1,\infty}\left((1+t)^{j+\frac{d(p-1)}{p}}\|\nabla^{j}\rho(t)\|_{L^{p}}+(1+t)^{j+a+\frac{d(p-1)}{p}}\|\nabla^{j}\rho(t)\|_{\dot{B}^{a}_{p,\infty}}\right)\lesssim\varepsilon_{0},~~\forall t>0,

with

Our goal in this paper is to extend our work in

For

Moreover,

For

\displaystyle\dot{\mathcal{D}}^{a}_{1}h_{1}(x,v)=\sup_{z\in\mathbb{R}^{2}}\frac{|h_{1}(x,v)-h_{1}(x-z,v)|}{|z|^{a}},~~\dot{\mathcal{D}}^{a}_{2}h_{1}(x,v)=\sup_{z\in\mathbb{R}^{2}}\frac{|h_{1}(x,v)-h_{1}(x,v-z)|}{|z|^{a}},

\displaystyle\mathcal{D}^{a}h_{1}=|h_{1}|+\dot{\mathcal{D}}^{a}_{1}h_{1}+\dot{\mathcal{D}}^{a}_{2}h_{1}.

For
h_{2}:\mathbb{R}^{2}_{x}\times\mathbb{R}^{2}_{v}\to\mathbb{R}
and

we define

|||h_{2}|||_{1+a}:=\sum_{p=1,\infty}\sum_{i=0,1}\left\|\mathcal{D}^{a}(\nabla_{x,v}^{i}h_{2})\right\|_{L^{1}_{x}L^{p}_{v}\cap L^{1}_{v}L^{p}_{x}}.

(1.5)

Our main result is as follows.

Theorem 1.1

Let
a\in(0,1)
. There exist
C_{0}>0
,
\varepsilon\in(0,1)
such that if
|||f_{0}|||_{1+a}\leq\varepsilon,

and

\liminf_{\kappa\to 0}|||f_{0}-f_{0}^{\kappa}|||_{1+a}=0

for some sequence

. Then the problem (
1.2
) has a unique global solution
f
with

\|\rho\|_{1+a}+\|U\|_{1+a}\leq C_{0}|||f_{0}|||_{1+a}.

T>0

\mathfrak{g}:[0,T]\times\mathbb{R}^{2}\to\mathbb{R}

\mathbf{E}(t,x)=-\nabla(-\Delta+1)^{-1}(\mathfrak{g})(t,x),~~||\mathfrak{g}||_{1+a,T}<\infty.

(1.6)

for any

(1.9)

Proposition 1.5

Let

(1.10)

2
Dynamics of the density and bootstrap argument

In this section, we recast the system (
1.2
) as a problem for the density
\rho
. Let
\left(X_{s,t}(x,v),V_{s,t}(x,v)\right)
be the flow associated to the vector field
(v,E(t,x))
.
Then, the solution
f
of the equation (
1.2
) is given by

f(t,x,v)=f_{0}(X_{0,t}(x,v),V_{0,t}(x,v))-\int_{0}^{t}E(s,X_{s,t}(x,v))\cdot\nabla_{v}\mu(V_{s,t}(x,v))ds,

(2.1)

(see (1.3) in
[
33
]
with
\mathbf{B}(t,x,v)=(v,E(t,x))
). Set
\mathfrak{g}=\rho+A(U)
, so
E=-\nabla_{x}(-\Delta+1)^{-1}\mathfrak{g}.

We denote

\displaystyle\begin{split}&\mathcal{I}_{f_{0}}(\mathfrak{g})(t,x)=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}f_{0}(X_{0,t}(x,v),V_{0,t}(x,v))dv,\\
&\mathcal{R}(\mathfrak{g})(t,x)=\int_{0}^{t}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\left(E(s,x-(t-s)v)\cdot\nabla_{v}\mu(v)-E(s,X_{s,t}(x,v))\cdot\nabla_{v}\mu(V_{s,t}(x,v))\right)dvds.\end{split}

(2.2)

As
[
24
, (2.5)]
, we obtain that
\rho(t,x)=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}f(t,x,v)dv
is the solution of the following equation:

\rho=G*_{(t,x)}(\mathcal{I}(\mathfrak{g})+\mathcal{R}(\mathfrak{g})+A(U))+\mathcal{I}(\mathfrak{g})+\mathcal{R}(\mathfrak{g}),

(2.3)

where
G
is the kernel satisfying:

\widetilde{G}=\frac{\widetilde{K}}{1-\widetilde{K}},~~\widetilde{K}(\tau,\xi)=\int_{0}^{+\infty}e^{-i\tau t}\frac{1}{1+|\xi|^{2}}i\xi\cdot\widehat{\nabla_{v}\mu}(t\xi)dt,

(2.4)

and
\widetilde{\cdot}
is the ”space-time” Fourier transform on
\mathbb{R}^{2}\times\mathbb{R}
.
Thanks to the Penrose condition, one has

|1-\widetilde{K}|\geq\text{\r{c}}>0,
which implies that

\widetilde{G}
is well defined.

Define for
T>0

\mathcal{V}_{\varepsilon}:=\{h\in L^{1}\cap L^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}^{2}):||h||_{L^{1}\cap L^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}^{2})}\leq\varepsilon\}.

By the standard argument, one has

Lemma 2.1

Let
a\in(0,1)
. There exist
C_{0}\geq 1
and
\tilde{\varepsilon}_{0}\in(0,1)
such that if
\varrho\in\mathcal{V}_{\tilde{\varepsilon}_{0}}
, the problem

-\Delta u+u=\varrho+A(u)

(2.5)

has a unique soluion
u
in
\mathcal{V}_{c\tilde{\varepsilon}_{0}}
satisfying

\sum_{j=0}^{2}\sum_{p=1,\infty}\left(||(u,A(u))||_{L^{p}}+||\nabla^{j}(u,A(u))||_{\dot{B}^{\frac{a}{2}}_{p,\infty}}\right)\leq C_{0}\sum_{p=1,\infty}\left(||\varrho||_{L^{p}}+||\varrho||_{\dot{B}^{a}_{p,\infty}}\right).

(2.6)

In addition, we can define a map
\mathcal{N}:\mathcal{V}_{\tilde{\varepsilon}_{0}}\to\mathcal{V}_{c\tilde{\varepsilon}_{0}}
:
u=\mathcal{N}(\varrho)
for any
\rho\in\mathcal{V}_{\tilde{\varepsilon}_{0}}
. The map
\mathcal{N}
satisfies

\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{2}\sum_{p=1,\infty}\left(||\mathcal{N}(\varrho_{1})-\mathcal{N}(\varrho_{2})||_{L^{p}}+||\nabla^{j}(\mathcal{N}(\varrho_{1})-\mathcal{N}(\varrho_{2}))||_{\dot{B}^{\frac{a}{2}}_{p,\infty}}\right)

\displaystyle\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\leq C_{0}\sum_{p=1,\infty}\left(||\varrho_{1}-\varrho_{2}||_{L^{p}}+||\varrho_{1}-\varrho_{2}||_{\dot{B}^{a}_{p,\infty}}\right).

Thanks to Lemma
2.1
,
\mathfrak{g}
and
U
in (
2.3
) can be performed via
\rho
:

\mathfrak{g}(t)=\rho(t)+A(\mathcal{N}(\rho)(t)),U(t)=\mathcal{N}(\rho(t)),

(2.7)

provided that
\rho(t)\in\mathcal{V}_{\tilde{\varepsilon}_{0}}
.
In particular, we can write (
2.3
) as an equation for the density
\rho
in
[0,T]
when
\rho(t)\in\mathcal{V}_{\tilde{\varepsilon}_{0}}
for any
t\in[0,T]
.

The following is the boundedness of the operator
\mathcal{G}:=G*_{(t,x)}
in
[
24
, Theorem 3.9]
.

Proposition 2.2

There holds

\|G*_{(t,x)}g\|_{1+a,T}\leq C||g\|_{1+a,T},

(2.8)

where
C=C(\|\langle\cdot\rangle^{7}\nabla\mu(\cdot)\|_{W^{11,1}},a,\text{\r{c}}).

The following is our main bootstrap proposition.

Proposition 2.3

Let
a\in(0,1)
. There exist
C_{1}\geq 1
,
\varepsilon_{1}\in(0,1)
such that if

|||f_{0}|||_{1+a}<\infty
and the problem (
1.2
) has a unique solution
(f,U)
in
[0,T]
for some
T<\infty
with

\|\rho\|_{1+a,T}+\|U\|_{1+a,T}\leq\varepsilon_{1}.

(2.9)

Then, we have

\|\rho\|_{1+a,T}+\|U\|_{1+a,T}\leq C_{1}|||f_{0}|||_{1+a}.

Here is the local well-posedness result.

Proposition 2.4

Let
a\in(0,1)
. There exists
\varepsilon_{2}>0
such that if
\rho(0,x)=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}f_{0}(x,v)dv
satisfies

\displaystyle\sum_{p=1,\infty}||\rho(0)||_{L^{p}}+||\nabla\rho(0)||_{\dot{B}^{a}_{p,\infty}}\leq\varepsilon_{2},

(2.10)

\displaystyle|||f_{0}|||_{1+a}<\infty,\quad\lim_{\kappa\to 0}|||f_{0}-f_{0}^{\kappa}|||_{1+a}=0,

(2.11)

for some sequence
f_{0}^{\kappa}\in C^{\infty}_{c}(\mathbb{R}^{2}_{x}\times\mathbb{R}_{v}^{2})
. Then, the problem (
1.2
) has a unique local solution
f
in
[0,T]
with

\displaystyle\|\rho\|_{1+a,T}+\|U\|_{1+a,T}\leq C_{2}\left(\sum_{p=1,\infty}||\rho(0)||_{L^{p}}+||\nabla\rho(0)||_{\dot{B}^{a}_{p,\infty}}\right),

(2.12)

\displaystyle\lim_{t\to 0^{+}}\left(\|\rho-\rho(0)\|_{1+a,t}+\|U-U(0)\|_{1+a,t}\right)=0,

(2.13)

for some
T\in(0,1)
where
C_{2}\geq 1
only depends on
a
and
C_{A}
.

With Proposition
2.3
and Proposition
2.4
in hand, we can obtain Theorem
1.1
.

Proof of Theorem
1.1
.
By (
1.5
), one has

\sum_{p=1,\infty}||\rho(0)||_{L^{p}}+||\nabla\rho(0)||_{\dot{B}^{a}_{p,\infty}}\leq C_{3}|||f_{0}|||_{1+a},

(2.14)

for some
C_{3}\geq 1
. Let
C_{1}\geq 1
,
\varepsilon_{1}\in(0,1)
be in Proposition
2.3
and
C_{2}\geq 1
,
\varepsilon_{2}\in(0,1)
be in Proposition
2.4

T^{*}=\sup\left\{T:\text{the solution $f$ of \eqref{eq2} exists on}~~[0,T],~~\text{s.t}~\|\rho\|_{1+a,T}+\|U\|_{1+a,T}\leq M|||f_{0}|||_{1+a}\right\},

with
M=100C_{1}C_{2}C_{3}
.
Assume

|||f_{0}|||_{1+a}\leq\frac{\min\{\varepsilon_{1},\varepsilon_{2}\}}{100C_{1}C_{2}C_{3}}.

(2.15)

So, thanks to Proposition
2.4
, the problem (
1.2
) has a unique local solution
f
in
[0,T]
satisfying

\|\rho\|_{1+a,T}+\|U\|_{1+a,T}\leq C_{2}\left(\sum_{p=1,\infty}||\rho(0)||_{L^{p}}+||\nabla\rho(0)||_{\dot{B}^{a}_{p,\infty}}\right)\overset{\eqref{zzz17}}{\leq}M|||f_{0}|||_{1+a}.

This gives

Now we suppose that
T^{*}<\infty
. By (

) one has
\|\rho\|_{1+a,T^{\star}}+\|U\|_{1+a,T^{\star}}\leq\varepsilon_{1}.
So, we can apply Proposition
2.3
to get that

\|\rho\|_{1+a,T^{\star}}+\|U\|_{1+a,T^{\star}}\leq C_{1}|||f_{0}|||_{1+a}.

(2.16)

In particular,

\sum_{p=1,\infty}||\rho(T^{\star})||_{L^{p}}+||\nabla\rho(T^{\star})||_{\dot{B}^{a}_{p,\infty}}\leq C_{1}|||f_{0}|||_{1+a}\leq\varepsilon_{2}.

Moreover, by Remark
5.1
one has
|||f(T^{\star})|||_{1+a}<\infty
and
\lim_{\kappa\to 0}|||f(T^{\star})-f_{T^{\star}}^{\kappa}|||_{1+a}=0

for some sequence

.
Hence, by Proposition
2.4
, the solution

can be extended from

to
[0,T^{\star}+\delta]
for some

satisfying

\displaystyle\|\rho\|_{1+a,T^{*}+\delta}+\|U\|_{1+a,T^{*}+\delta}\leq 2(\|\rho\|_{1+a,T^{*}}+\|U\|_{1+a,T^{*}}),

since (
2.13
).
Combining this with (

) to get that

\|\rho\|_{1+a,T^{\star}+\delta}+\|U\|_{1+a,T^{\star}+\delta}\leq 2C_{1}|||f_{0}|||_{1+a}<M|||f_{0}|||_{1+a},

(2.17)

this contradicts to

.
Therefore,

and the proof is complete.

Proposition 3.1

Let
(Y_{s,t},W_{s,t})

As a consequence of Proposition

, we have

Lemma 4.1

Let

(4.2)

The proof of Lemma

We next turn to the reaction term. For any given
F:[0,T]\times\mathbb{R}^{2}\to\mathbb{R}
and
\eta:\mathbb{R}^{2}\to\mathbb{R}

\displaystyle\mathcal{T}[F,\eta](t,x)=-\mathcal{T}_{NL}[F,\eta](t,x)+\mathcal{T}_{L}[F,\eta](t,x),

with

\displaystyle\mathcal{T}_{L}[F,\eta](t,x)=\int_{0}^{t}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}F(s,x-(t-s)v)\eta(v)dvds,

\displaystyle\mathcal{T}_{NL}[F,\eta](t,x)=\int_{0}^{t}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}F(s,X_{s,t}(x,v))\eta(V_{s,t}(x,v))dvds,

where
(X_{s,t},V_{s,t})
are in Proposition
3.1
with
\|\mathfrak{g}\|_{1+a,T}\leq\varepsilon_{0}
.
By changing variable, we reformulate it as follows:

\displaystyle\mathcal{T}_{L}[F,\eta](t,x)=\int_{0}^{t}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}F(s,w+\frac{s}{t}(x-w))\eta(\frac{x-w}{t})\frac{dwds}{t^{2}},

\displaystyle\mathcal{T}_{NL}[F,\eta](t,x)=\int_{0}^{t}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}F\left(s,Y_{s,t}(w,\frac{x-w}{t})+w+\frac{s(x-w)}{t}\right)\eta\left(W_{s,t}(w,\frac{x-w}{t})+\frac{x-w}{t}\right)\frac{dwds}{t^{2}}.

We have the following Lemma.

Lemma 4.2

Let
a\in(0,1)
. Let
\eta
be such that

\sum_{j=0}^{3}\langle v\rangle^{3}|\nabla^{j}\eta(v)|\leq 1.

(4.3)

Then,

(1)

\displaystyle\left|\mathcal{T}[F,\eta](t,x)\right|\lesssim_{a}\|\mathfrak{g}\|_{1+a,T}\int_{0}^{t}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}|F(s,x-(t-s)v)|\frac{1}{\langle s\rangle^{1+a}}\frac{dvds}{\langle v\rangle^{3}}.

(4.4)

In particular, for
p=1,\infty
,

\displaystyle\langle t\rangle^{\frac{2(p-1)}{p}}\|\mathcal{T}[F,\eta](t)\|_{L^{p}}\lesssim_{a}\|\mathfrak{g}\|_{1+a,T}\left(\sup_{s\in[0,t]}\langle s\rangle^{\frac{2(p-1)}{p}}\|F(s)\|_{L^{p}}+\sup_{s\in[0,t]}\|F(s)\|_{L^{1}}\right).

(4.5)

(2)

For any
0\leq t\leq T
,

\displaystyle\sum_{p=1,\infty}\langle t\rangle^{\frac{2(p-1)}{p}+a}\|\mathcal{T}[F,\eta](t)\|_{\dot{B}^{a}_{p,\infty}}\lesssim_{a}\|\mathfrak{g}\|_{1+a,T}^{a}\sum_{p=1,\infty}\sup_{s\in[0,t]}\langle s\rangle^{1+\frac{2(p-1)}{p}}\left(\|F(s)\|_{L^{p}}+\|F(s)\|_{\dot{F}^{a}_{p,\infty}}\right).

(4.6)

(3)

For any
0\leq t\leq T
,

\displaystyle\sum_{p=1,\infty}\langle t\rangle^{\frac{2(p-1)}{p}+1+a}\|\nabla\mathcal{T}[F,\eta](t)\|_{\dot{B}^{a}_{p,\infty}}

\displaystyle\quad\quad\lesssim_{a}||\mathfrak{g}||_{1+a,T}^{a}\sum_{j=0,1}\sum_{p=1,\infty}\sup_{s\in[0,t]}\langle s\rangle^{1+\frac{2(p-1)}{p}}\left(\langle s\rangle^{\frac{a}{2}}\|\nabla^{j}F(s)\|_{\dot{F}^{a}_{p,\infty}}+\|F(s)\|_{L^{p}}\right),

(4.7)

where

Proof.

Step 1)

\displaystyle\quad\left.+\int_{0}^{t}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}F(s,x-(t-s)v)\eta(v)dvds\right|

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{t}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}|F(s,x-(t-s)v)|\left|\eta(V_{s,t}(x,\Psi_{s,t}(t,x)))-\eta(v)\right|dvds

\displaystyle+\int_{0}^{t}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}|F(s,x-(t-s)v)|\Big||\det(\nabla_{v}\Psi_{s,t}(x,v))|-1\Big|\left|\eta(V_{s,t}(x,\Psi_{s,t}(t,x)))\right|dvds.

,

\displaystyle\left|\mathcal{T}[F,\eta](t,x)\right|\overset{\eqref{z3}}{\lesssim_{a}}\|\mathfrak{g}\|_{1+a,T}\int_{0}^{t}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}|F(s,x-(t-s)v)|\frac{1}{\langle s\rangle^{1+a}}\frac{dvds}{\langle v\rangle^{3}}.

\displaystyle\|\mathcal{T}[F,\eta](t)\|_{L^{p}}\lesssim_{a}\|\mathfrak{g}\|_{1+a,T}\int_{0}^{t}\left\|\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}|F(s,x-(t-s)v)|\frac{dv}{\langle v\rangle^{3}}\right\|_{L^{p}_{x}}\frac{ds}{\langle s\rangle^{1+a}}.

\varepsilon_{1}\in(0,\varepsilon_{0}/4)
small. So, we proved Proposition
2.3
.

Remark 5.1

It follows from Proposition
3.1
that
||f(T)|||_{1+a}<\infty.

However, it is easy to check that if there exists
(f_{0}^{\kappa})_{\kappa}\in C^{\infty}_{c}(\mathbb{R}^{2}_{x}\times\mathbb{R}_{v}^{2})
satisfying

\lim_{\kappa\to 0}|||f_{0}-f^{\kappa}_{0}|||_{1+a}=0
, then we can construct
(f_{T}^{\kappa})_{\kappa}\in C^{\infty}_{c}(\mathbb{R}^{2}_{x}\times\mathbb{R}_{v}^{2})
such that
\lim_{\kappa\to 0}|||f(T)-f_{T}^{\kappa}|||_{1+a}=0
.

Next, we prove
Proposition
2.4
. Set

S_{\varepsilon,T_{0}}:=\{\vartheta\in(L^{1}\cap L^{\infty}([0,T])\times\mathbb{R}^{2}):||\vartheta||_{a,T_{0}}=||\vartheta\mathbf{1}_{[0,T_{0}]}(s)||_{a}\leq\varepsilon\},

(5.2)

for
T_{0}\in[0,1]
. Let
\tilde{\varepsilon}_{0}
be in Lemma
2.1
and
\varepsilon_{0}>0
be in Proposition
3.1
.

Thanks to (
2.7
), we can define a map

\mathcal{J}(\rho):=G*_{(t,x)}(\mathcal{I}_{f_{0}}(\mathfrak{g})+\mathcal{R}(\mathfrak{g})+A(U))+\mathcal{I}_{f_{0}}(\mathfrak{g})+\mathcal{R}(\mathfrak{g}),\mathfrak{g}=\rho+A(U),U=\mathcal{N}(\rho),

for
\rho\in S_{\tilde{\varepsilon}_{0},T_{0}}
.

We will prove that
\mathcal{J}(\rho)
has a unique fixed point in
S_{\tilde{\varepsilon}_{1},T_{0}}
for some
\tilde{\varepsilon}_{1}\leq\tilde{\varepsilon}_{0}
and
T_{0}\in(0,1)
.

By (
2.6
) in Lemma
2.1
, for any
\rho\in S_{\tilde{\varepsilon}_{1},T_{0}}
,

||\mathfrak{g}||_{a,T_{0}}\leq C_{0}^{\prime}||\rho||_{a,T_{0}}\leq C_{0}^{\prime}\tilde{\varepsilon}_{1}\leq\varepsilon_{0},

(5.3)

with
\tilde{\varepsilon}_{1}\in(0,\tilde{\varepsilon}_{0})
small.
Let
(Y_{s,t}^{\rho},W_{s,t}^{\rho})
be the characteristics in Proposition
3.1
with
\mathfrak{g}=\rho+A(\mathcal{N}(\rho))
. Using the same argument as
[
24
, Proof of Proposition 4.1]
, we can obtain for any
0\leq s\leq t\leq T_{0}
,

\displaystyle\sum_{i=0,1}\|\nabla_{x}^{i}(Y_{s,t}^{\rho},W_{s,t}^{\rho})\|_{L^{\infty}_{x,v}}+\sup_{\alpha}\frac{\|\delta_{\alpha}^{x}\nabla_{x}(Y_{s,t}^{\rho},W_{s,t}^{\rho})\|_{L^{\infty}_{x,v}}}{|\alpha|^{a}}\lesssim_{a}T_{0}\|\rho\|_{a,T_{0}},

(5.4)

\displaystyle\sum_{i=0,1}\|\nabla_{x}^{i}(Y_{s,t}^{\rho_{1}}-Y_{s,t}^{\rho_{2}},W_{s,t}^{\rho_{1}}-W_{s,t}^{\rho_{2}})\|_{L^{\infty}_{x,v}}

\displaystyle\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad+\sup_{\alpha}\frac{\|\delta_{\alpha}^{x}\nabla_{x}(Y_{s,t}^{\rho_{1}}-Y_{s,t}^{\rho_{2}},W_{s,t}^{\rho_{1}}-W_{s,t}^{\rho_{2}})\|_{L^{\infty}_{x,v}}}{|\alpha|^{a}}\lesssim_{a}T_{0}\|\rho_{1}-\rho_{2}\|_{a,T_{0}},

(5.5)

for any
\rho,\rho_{1},\rho_{2}\in S_{\tilde{\varepsilon}_{1},T_{0}}
. It is easy to obtain from
(
5.4
)
that for any
\rho\in S_{\tilde{\varepsilon}_{1},T_{0}},

\displaystyle||\mathcal{R}(\rho+A(\mathcal{N}(\rho)))||_{1+a,T_{0}}\lesssim_{a}T_{0},~~

\displaystyle||\mathcal{I}_{f_{0}}(\rho+A(\mathcal{N}(\rho)))||_{1+a,T_{0}}\lesssim_{a}|||f_{0}|||_{1+a}.

(5.6)

Hence, since
\mathcal{J}(\rho)-\mathcal{I}_{f_{0}}(\mathfrak{g})=G*_{(t,x)}(\mathcal{I}_{f_{0}}(\mathfrak{g})+\mathcal{R}(\mathfrak{g})+A(U))+\mathcal{R}(\mathfrak{g})
,

||\mathcal{J}(\rho)-\mathcal{I}_{f_{0}}(\mathfrak{g})||_{1+a,T_{0}}\lesssim T_{0}+(1+|||f_{0}|||_{1+a})\int_{0}^{T_{0}}||G(t)||_{L^{1}}dt\lesssim T_{0}(1+|||f_{0}|||_{1+a}),

(5.7)

where we use
[
24
, Theorem 3.6, (3.18)]
in the last inequality. Hence

\displaystyle||\mathcal{J}(\rho)-\rho(0)||_{1+a,T_{0}}\leq||\mathcal{J}(\rho)-\mathcal{I}_{f_{0}}(\mathfrak{g})||_{1+a,T_{0}}+||(\mathcal{I}_{f_{0}}-\mathcal{I}_{f_{0}^{\kappa}})(\mathfrak{g})||_{1+a,T_{0}}

\displaystyle+||\mathcal{I}_{f_{0}^{\kappa}}(\mathfrak{g})-\mathcal{I}_{f_{0}^{\kappa}}(0)||_{1+a,T_{0}}+||\mathcal{I}_{f_{0}^{\kappa}}(0)-\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}f_{0}^{\kappa}(x,v)dv||_{1+a,T_{0}}+||\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}(f_{0}^{\kappa}-f_{0})(x,v)dv||_{1+a,T_{0}}

\displaystyle\overset{\eqref{zzz3}}{\lesssim}T_{0}(C(f_{0}^{\kappa})+|||f_{0}|||_{1+a})+|||f_{0}-f_{0}^{\kappa}|||_{1+a}.

(5.8)

In particular,

\displaystyle\sup_{\rho\in S_{\tilde{\varepsilon}_{0},T_{0}}}||\mathcal{I}_{f_{0}}(\rho+A(\mathcal{N}(\rho)))||_{a,T_{0}}\lesssim T_{0}(C(f_{0}^{\kappa})+|||f_{0}|||_{1+a})+|||f_{0}-f_{0}^{\kappa}|||_{1+a}+||\rho(0)||_{1+a,T_{0}}

\displaystyle\quad\quad\lesssim T_{0}(C(f_{0}^{\kappa})+|||f_{0}|||_{1+a})+|||f_{0}-f_{0}^{\kappa}|||_{1+a}+\sum_{p=1,\infty}(||\rho(0)||_{\dot{B}^{a}_{p,\infty}}+||\rho(0)||_{L^{p}}).

(5.9)

Moreover, as
[
24
, Proof of Theorem 2.2]
, we get from (
5.4
), (
5.5
) and Lemma
2.1
that

||\mathcal{J}(\rho_{1})-\mathcal{J}(\rho_{2})||_{a,T_{0}}\lesssim T_{0}(1+|||f_{0}|||_{1+a})||\rho_{1}-\rho_{2}||_{a,T_{0}},

(5.10)

for any
\rho_{1},\rho_{2}\in S_{\tilde{\varepsilon}_{1},T_{0}}
.
Thanks to (

), (

), (

) and the fixed-point theorem, we obtain that

has a unique fixed point
\rho
in

2.12

\tilde{\varepsilon}_{1}\in(0,\tilde{\varepsilon}_{0})

).
The proof of the Proposition (
2.4
