Adjoint functor theorems for lax-idempotent pseudomonads
Nathanael Arkor, Ivan Di Liberti, Fosco Loregian

Abstract
. For each pair of lax-idempotent pseudomonads R R and I I , for which I I is locally fully faithful and R R distributes over I I , we establish an adjoint functor theorem, relating R R -cocontinuity to adjointness relative to I I . This provides a new perspective on the nature of adjoint functor theorems, which may be seen as methods to decompose adjointness into cocontinuity and relative adjointness. As special cases, we recover variants of the adjoint functor theorem of Freyd, the multiadjoint functor theorem of Diers, and the pluriadjoint functor theorem of Solian–Viswanathan, as well as the adjoint functor theorems for locally presentable categories. More generally, we recover enriched Φ \Phi -adjoint functor theorems for weakly sound classes of weight Φ \Phi .

Introduction

A fundamental result in category theory asserts that left-adjoint functors are cocontinuous, i.e. that they preserve colimits. The role of an
adjoint functor theorem
is to provide sufficient conditions for the converse to hold: that is, given a cocontinuous functor
f
, to provide sufficient conditions for
f
to be left-adjoint. This is fundamentally a question of size: right adjoint functors may be computed as left extensions, hence as certain – potentially large – colimits. Therefore, adjoint functor theorems typically take the form of imposing a size constraint on
f
, limiting the size of this colimit, and hence ensuring that it exists.

In this paper, we are concerned with understanding the precise nature of the interplay between adjointness, cocontinuity, and size. It is our thesis that adjoint functor theorems – and, more generally, relative adjointness theorems – may be seen as an inherently 2-categorical phenomenon, arising from the interaction between a pair of lax-idempotent pseudomonads on a 2-category. To justify our claim, we shall prove a general adjoint 1-cell theorem in a 2-category (
Theorem

2.16
), and show that it captures many instances of adjoint functor theorem present in the literature (
Examples

2.20
,
2.24
,
2.25
and
2.32
).

There are two distinct aspects to our work.

(1)

From a purely formal, 2-categorical perspective, our result is an extension of the work of
[

]
on the interaction between

From the perspective of ordinary and enriched category theory, our result ties together various notions of adjoint functor theorem, providing a conceptual understanding that sheds new light on a classical result. There is one further evident generalisation of the adjoint functor theorem. Namely, rather than simply considering adjoint functor theorems in the context of ordinary category theory, we may be interested in adjoint functor theorems in the context of enriched category theory, internal category theory, fibred and indexed category theory, and so on. 1.2.
Adjointness and lax-idempotent pseudomonads

Appendix A

Our purpose in this section is to give a proof of the observation in

In the following, we denote by

\mathbf{Psmd}_{\mathcal{K}}
the 2-category of pseudomonads and pseudomorphisms on
{\mathcal{K}}
, and by
\mathbf{Lift}_{\mathcal{K}}
the 2-category of pseudomonads and liftings on
{\mathcal{K}}
. Explicitly,
\mathbf{Psmd}_{\mathcal{K}}{}^{\mathrm{op}}
is given by the sub-2-category of the Gray-category

[
GL21

{\mathcal{K}}

\mathbf{Lift}_{\mathcal{K}}{}^{\mathrm{op}}
is given by the sub-2-category of the Gray-category
\mathbf{Lift}(\mathbf{GRAY})
of pseudomonads and liftings in
\mathbf{GRAY}

[
GL21
, §3]
that is spanned by pseudomonads on
{\mathcal{K}}
, liftings whose 1-cells are trivial, and lifting transformations whose 2-cells are trivial.

Theorem A.1
.

Let

I

R+I

Proof.

Specialising
[

R+I

\mathbf{Psmd}_{\mathcal{K}}
