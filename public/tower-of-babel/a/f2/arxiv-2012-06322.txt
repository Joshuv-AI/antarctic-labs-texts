Abstract
In this paper, we proved a theorem that every large enough odd number can be represented as the sum of three almost equal Piatetski-Shapiro primes.

Introduction

In 1937, it was proved by I.M. Vinogradov that every large enough odd number could be represented as the sum of three primes. Today we usually called this theorem the ternary Goldbach-Vinogradov theorem. Later many scholars studied the problem that large enough odd number can be represented as the sum of three primes. In
[
9
,
10
]
, C. D. Pan and C. B. Pan showed that every large enough odd number
n
the Diophantine equation

\displaystyle n=p_{1}+p_{2}+p_{3},\ \ \ |p_{i}-\frac{n}{3}|\leq y,\ \ \ 1\leq i\leq 3,

(1)

with prime variable is solvable for
y=n^{2/3}(\log n)^{c}
, where
c
is a positive constant.

The set of Piatetski-Shapiro primes is of the form

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}_{\gamma}=\{prime\ p|p=[n^{1/\gamma}],\ n\geq 1\}.

For more information about Piatetski-Shapiro primes, we refer to
[
4
,
5
,
8
,
12
,
13
,
14
]
.

In 1992, A. Balog and J. Friedlander
[
1
]
studied the ternary Goldbach-Vinogradov theorem restricted to the set of Piatetski-Shapiro primes. They proved the following result:

\displaystyle\mathop{\mathop{\sum\limits_{p_{1}+p_{2}+p_{3}=n}}}_{p_{i}\in\mathcal{A}_{\gamma}}\frac{1}{\gamma}p_{1}^{1-\gamma}\frac{1}{\gamma}p_{2}^{1-\gamma}\frac{1}{\gamma}p_{3}^{1-\gamma}=\mathfrak{S}_{3}(n)\frac{3n^{2}}{\log^{3}n}+O\Bigg(\frac{n^{2}}{\log^{4}n}\Bigg),

(2)

where
\mathfrak{S}_{3}(n)=\prod\limits_{p|n}(1-\frac{1}{(p-1)^{2}})\prod\limits_{p\nmid n}(1+\frac{1}{(p-1)^{3}})
and
20/21<\gamma<1
.
Later C.H. Jia
[
6
]
extended the range of
\gamma
to
15/16<\gamma<1
,
and A.Kumchev
[
7
]
improved the result to
50/53<\gamma<1
.

In this paper, we combined these two ideas to study whether (1.1) can hold restricted to the set of Piatetski-Shapiro primes.
Specifically, we proved the following theorem that gives an affirmative answer.

Theorem
1. For every large enough odd number
n
the following equation

\displaystyle n=p_{1}+p_{2}+p_{3},\ \ \ |p_{i}-\frac{n}{3}|\leq y,\ \ \ p_{i}\in\mathcal{A}_{\gamma}\ \ \ 1\leq i\leq 3,

(3)

with prime variable is solvable for
y>n^{(52-45\gamma)/8}
and
44/45<\gamma<1
.
