A Stochastic Variance Reduction Algorithm with Bregman Distances for Structured Composite Problems
Nguyen Van Dung, B ă ` \grave{\text{\u{a}}} ng Công Vũ[5mm]

Abstract
We develop a novel stochastic primal dual splitting method with Bregman distances for solving a structured composite problems involving infimal convolutions in non-Euclidean spaces. The sublinear convergence in expectation of the primal-dual gap is proved under mild conditions on stepsize for the general case. The linear convergence rate is obtained under additional condition like the strong convexity relative to Bregman functions.

Introduction

The stochastic gradient method is one of the most popular algorithm in machine learning. This method was introduced over 60 years ago. The main advantage of this type of method is the low computational cost per iteration. However, stochastic gradient algorithm converges slowly and achieves only the sublinear convergence rates even when the underlying problem is strongly convex. To accelerate the convergence of the stochastic gradient algorithms, one of the popular approach is to use the variance reduction techniques. As a result,

Main results

We propose the following primal dual splitting method
for solving Problem
1.1
which incorporates the variance reduction technique as the Bregman proximity operators at each iteration.

Algorithm 3.1

Let
(\bar{x}_{0},\bar{v}_{0})\in\operatorname{int}(\operatorname{dom}(\varphi))\times\operatorname{int}(\operatorname{dom}(\psi))
, let
\gamma>0,\ \theta\in\{0;1\}
and
m
is a strictly positive integer. Let
(\omega_{k})_{k\in\mathbb{N}}
be a strictly positive sequence in
\mathbb{R}
. Let
Q=\{q_{1},\ldots,q_{n}\}
and
Q^{\prime}=\{q^{\prime}_{1},\ldots,q^{\prime}_{n^{\prime}}\}
be the probabilites on
\{1,\ldots,n\}
and
\{1,\ldots,n^{\prime}\}
, respectively.

Set
x^{0}_{0}=x^{0}_{-1}=\bar{x}_{0},\ v^{0}_{0}=v^{0}_{-1}=\bar{v}_{0}.

Iterate:
for
s=0,1,2,\ldots

\displaystyle\bar{x}=\bar{x}_{s},\bar{v}=\bar{v}_{s}

\displaystyle x_{0}=x_{0}^{s},\ x_{-1}=x_{-1}^{s}

\displaystyle v_{0}=v_{0}^{s},\ v_{-1}=v_{-1}^{s}

\displaystyle{\bf iterate:}\ \text{for}\ k=0,1,\ldots,m-1

\displaystyle\hskip 28.45274pt\text{pick}\ i_{k}\in\{1,\ldots,n\}\ \text{and}\ \ j_{k}\in\{1,\ldots,n^{\prime}\}\ \text{randomly according to $Q$ and $Q^{\prime}$, respectively}

\displaystyle\hskip 28.45274pt\begin{cases}y_{k}&=x_{k}+\theta(x_{k}-x_{k-1})\\
z_{k}&=\dfrac{\nabla h_{i_{k}}(y_{k})-\nabla h_{i_{k}}(\bar{x})}{q_{i_{k}}n}+\nabla h(\bar{x})\\
u_{k}&=v_{k}+\theta(v_{k}-v_{k-1})\\
t_{k}&=\dfrac{\nabla\ell_{j_{k}}(u_{k})-\nabla\ell_{j_{k}}(\bar{v})}{q^{\prime}_{j_{k}}n^{\prime}}+\nabla\ell(\bar{v})\\
x_{k+1}&=(\nabla\varphi+\gamma\partial f)^{-1}(\nabla\varphi(x_{k})-\gamma z_{k}-\gamma K^{*}u_{k})\\
v_{k+1}&=(\nabla\psi+\gamma\partial g^{*})^{-1}(\nabla\psi(v_{k})-\gamma t_{k}+\gamma Ky_{k}),\end{cases}

end

\displaystyle\text{set}\ \bar{x}_{s+1}=\sum_{k=1}^{m}\omega_{k}x_{k}\;\text{and}\;\bar{v}_{s+1}=\sum_{k=1}^{m}\omega_{k}v_{k}.

\displaystyle x^{s+1}_{0}=x_{m},\ x^{s+1}_{-1}=x_{m-1},\ v^{s+1}_{0}=v_{m},\ v^{s+1}_{-1}=v_{m-1}.

end

\displaystyle\ \ +4mL_{1}\gamma^{2}\big[G(\bar{x}_{s},v^{\star})-G(x^{\star},\bar{v}_{s})\big].

(3.40)

Note that by the choices of
(x_{0}^{s})_{s\in\mathbb{N}}
and
(x_{-1}^{s})_{s\in\mathbb{N}}
, we have

\mathsf{E}e_{m}+8L_{1}\gamma^{2}\mathsf{E}[T_{m}]=\mathsf{E}e^{s+1}_{0}+8L_{1}\gamma^{2}\mathsf{E}T^{s+1}_{0}.

Summing (
3
) from
s=0
to
s=N-1
, we derive

\displaystyle m\gamma(1-8L_{1}\gamma)\sum\limits_{s=1}^{N}\mathsf{E}[G(\bar{x}_{s},v^{\star})-G(x^{\star},\bar{v}_{s})]

\displaystyle\leq(e^{0}_{0}+8L_{1}\gamma^{2}T^{0}_{0})-(\mathsf{E}e^{N-1}_{m}+8L_{1}\gamma^{2}\mathsf{E}[T^{N-1}_{m}])

\displaystyle+4mL_{1}\gamma^{2}\sum\limits_{s=0}^{N-1}\mathsf{E}[G(\bar{x}_{s},v^{\star})-G(x^{\star},\bar{v}_{s})],

(3.41)

which implies that

\displaystyle m\gamma(1-12L_{1}\gamma)\sum\limits_{s=1}^{N}\mathsf{E}[G(\bar{x}_{s},v^{\star})-G(x^{\star},\bar{v}_{s})]

\displaystyle\leq(e^{0}_{0}+8L_{1}\gamma^{2}T^{0}_{0})-(\mathsf{E}e^{N-1}_{m}+8L_{1}\gamma^{2}\mathsf{E}[T^{N-1}_{m}])

\displaystyle\ \ +4mL_{1}\gamma^{2}[G(\bar{x}_{0},v^{\star})-G(x^{\star},\bar{v}_{0})]

\displaystyle\leq D(\mathsf{x}^{\star},\bar{}\mathsf{x}_{0})+4L_{1}\gamma^{2}(m+2)\big[G(\bar{x}_{0},v^{\star})-G(x^{\star},\bar{v}_{0})\big].

\Rightarrow\mathsf{E}[G(\hat{x}_{N},v^{\star})-G(x^{\star},\hat{v}_{N})]\leq\dfrac{D(\mathsf{x}^{\star},\bar{}\mathsf{x}_{0})+4L_{1}\gamma^{2}(m+2)\big[G(\bar{x}_{0},v^{\star})-G(x^{\star},\bar{v}_{0})\big]}{m\gamma(1-12L_{1}\gamma)N}.

The proof is completed.

Theorem 3.8

Suppose that Assumptions
2.3
,
2.4
and
2.5
are satisfied. Choose
M^{\prime}>\dfrac{2\|K\|}{\alpha}
, denote
\alpha^{\prime}=\alpha-\frac{2\|K\|}{M^{\prime}}
,
\tau=1+\gamma\alpha^{\prime}
,
\eta=4\gamma^{2}L_{1}
,
\sum\limits_{k=1}^{m}\tau^{k-1}=\delta
.
Assume that

\displaystyle 0<\gamma<\min\big\{\dfrac{1}{2\|K\|M^{\prime}+\mu_{0}},\ \dfrac{-1+\sqrt{1+\frac{\alpha^{\prime}}{4L_{1}}}}{\alpha^{\prime}}\big\}

(3.42)

Let
m
is a positive integer such that

\displaystyle m>\dfrac{\ln\lambda}{\ln\tau}\ \ \text{where}\ \lambda=\dfrac{\gamma-\eta\tau}{\eta}.

(3.43)

Let
(\bar{x}_{s})_{s\in\mathbb{N}},\ (\bar{v}_{s})_{s\in\mathbb{N}}
be sequences generated by Algorithm
3.1
with
\theta=0
,
\omega_{k}=\tau^{k-1}/\delta
.
Then, we have

\displaystyle\mathsf{E}[G(\bar{x}_{s},v^{\star})-G(x^{\star},\bar{v}_{s}]\leq\dfrac{\lambda^{-s}}{\eta\delta}[D(\mathsf{x}^{\star},\bar{}\mathsf{x}_{0})+\eta(1+\delta)(G(\bar{x}_{0},v^{\star})-G(x^{\star},\bar{v}_{0}))].

(3.44)

Proof
. By our assumption,
\alpha^{\prime}=\alpha-2\|K\|/M^{\prime}>0
. Hence, we can rewrite (
3.7
) with

M=M^{\prime}
as

\displaystyle\gamma[G(x_{k+1},v^{\star})

\displaystyle-G(x^{\star},v_{k+1})]

\displaystyle\leq D(\mathsf{x}^{\star},\mathsf{x}_{k})-(1+{\gamma\alpha^{\prime}})D(\mathsf{x}^{\star},\mathsf{x}_{k+1})-(1-2\gamma\|K\|M^{\prime}-\gamma\mu_{0})D(\mathsf{x}_{k+1},\mathsf{x}_{k})

\displaystyle+\gamma^{2}\|r_{k}-\mathsf{R}_{k}\|^{2}+\gamma{\langle{{\hat{}\mathsf{x}_{k+1}-\mathsf{x}},{\mathsf{R}_{k}-r_{k}}}\rangle}.

(3.45)

Taking the conditional expectation with respect to
\xi_{[k-1]}
both sides of (
3.45
), using the condition (
3.42
) and Corollary
3.4
, we get:

\displaystyle\gamma\mathsf{E}_{\xi_{k}}[G(x_{k+1},v^{\star})

\displaystyle-G(x^{\star},v_{k+1})]\leq D(\mathsf{x}^{\star},\mathsf{x}_{k})-(1+\gamma\alpha^{\prime})\mathsf{E}_{\xi_{k}}D(\mathsf{x}^{\star},\mathsf{x}_{k+1})

\displaystyle\ \ +4\gamma^{2}L_{1}\bigg([G(x_{k},v^{\star})-G(x^{\star},v_{k})]+[G(\bar{x}_{s},v^{\star})-G(x^{\star},\bar{v}_{s})]\bigg).

(3.46)

Denote
T^{s}_{k}:=T_{k}=G(x_{k},v^{\star})-G(x^{\star},v_{k}),\ \bar{T}_{s}=G(\bar{x}_{s},v^{\star})-G(x^{\star},\bar{v}_{s})
,

d^{s}_{k}:=d_{k}:=D(\mathsf{x}^{\star},\mathsf{x}_{k})
.

We have:

\displaystyle(1+\gamma\alpha^{\prime})\mathsf{E}_{\xi_{k}}d_{k+1}\leq d_{k}-\gamma\mathsf{E}_{\xi_{k}}T_{k+1}+4\gamma^{2}L_{1}(T_{k}+\bar{T}_{s}).

(3.47)

Take expectation on both sides of (
3.47
), we obtain

\displaystyle\tau\mathsf{E}d_{k+1}\leq\mathsf{E}d_{k}-\gamma\mathsf{E}T_{k+1}+\eta\mathsf{E}T_{k}+\eta\bar{T}_{s}.

(3.48)

Multiplying both sides by
\tau^{k}

and telescoping from
k=0
to
m-1
, we derive

\displaystyle\tau^{m}\mathsf{E}d_{m}\leq d_{0}+\eta T_{0}+\eta\sum_{k=1}^{m-1}\tau^{k}\mathsf{E}T_{k}+\eta\bar{T}_{s}\sum_{k=0}^{m-1}\tau^{k}-\gamma\sum_{k=1}^{m}\tau^{k-1}\mathsf{E}T_{k},

(3.49)

which implies

\displaystyle\tau^{m}\mathsf{E}(d_{m}+\eta T_{m})+(\gamma-\eta\tau)\mathsf{E}\sum_{k=1}^{m}\tau^{k-1}T_{k}\leq d_{0}+\eta T_{0}+\eta\sum_{k=1}^{m}\tau^{k-1}\bar{T}_{s}.

(3.50)

From the conditions of
m
and
\gamma
, we have
\tau^{m}>\dfrac{\gamma-\eta\tau}{\eta}
, so we obtain:

\displaystyle\dfrac{\gamma-\eta\tau}{\eta}\mathsf{E}(d_{m}+\eta T_{m})+(\gamma-\eta\tau)\mathsf{E}\sum_{k=1}^{m}\tau^{k-1}T_{k}\leq d_{0}+\eta T_{0}+\eta\sum_{k=1}^{m}\tau^{k-1}\bar{T}_{s}.

(3.51)

From
G
is convex-concave, (
3.51
) implies

\displaystyle\mathsf{E}[d^{s+1}_{0}+\eta T^{s+1}_{0}+\eta\delta\bar{T}_{s+1}]\leq\dfrac{\eta}{\gamma-\eta\tau}[d^{s}_{0}+\eta T^{s}_{0}+\eta\delta\bar{T}_{s}].

(3.52)

Therefore

\displaystyle\mathsf{E}[D(\mathsf{x}^{\star},\mathsf{x}^{s+1}_{0}]+\eta(G(x^{s+1}_{0},v^{\star})

\displaystyle-G(x^{\star},v^{s+1}_{0}))+\eta\delta\bar{T}_{s+1}]

\displaystyle\leq\dfrac{\eta}{\gamma-\eta\tau}[D(\mathsf{x}^{\star},\mathsf{x}^{s}_{0})+\eta(G(x^{s}_{0},v^{\star})-G(x^{\star},v^{s}_{0}))+\eta\delta\bar{T}_{s}].

(3.53)

Hence,

\displaystyle\eta\delta\mathsf{E}[G(\bar{x}_{s},v^{\star})-G(x^{\star},\bar{v}_{s}]\leq\lambda^{-s}[D(\mathsf{x}^{\star},\bar{}\mathsf{x}_{0})+\eta(1+\delta)(G(\bar{x}_{0},v^{\star})-G(x^{\star},\bar{v}_{0}))].

(3.54)

The proof is completed.
