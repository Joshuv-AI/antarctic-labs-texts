Support τ 2 \tau_{2} -tilting and 2-torsion pairs
Jordan McMahon

Abstract
. The theory of τ \tau -tilting was introduced by Adachi–Iyama–Reiten; one of the main results is a bijection between support τ \tau -tilting modules and torsion classes. We are able to generalise this result in the context of the higher Auslander–Reiten theory of Iyama. For a finite-dimensional algebra A A with 2-cluster-tilting subcategory 𝒞 ⊆ mod ​ A \mathcal{C}\subseteq\mathrm{mod}A , we are able to find a correspondence between support τ 2 \tau_{2} -tilting A A -modules and torsion pairs in 𝒞 \mathcal{C} satisfying an additional functorial finiteness condition.
