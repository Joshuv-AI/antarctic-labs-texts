Common end points of multivalued mappings in ordered metric spaces
Talat Nazir, Sergei Silvestrov

Abstract
Using the setting of ordered metric spaces, we obtain common end point of two multivalued mappings satisfying a generalized ( ψ , φ ) (\psi,\varphi) -weak contractive condition. Under comparative condition on the set of end points of multivalued mappings, our results assure the uniqueness of the end point. These results generalize and improve several recent results on single-valued as well as multivalued mappings. MSC 2020 Classification: 54H25, 47H10, 54E50

u
is also an end point for
T
.
It is easy to see that
M(u,u)=\delta(u,Tu)
. Using that

for

, have

\displaystyle\psi(\delta(u,Tu))=\psi(\delta(Su,Tu))\leq\psi(M(u,u))-\varphi(M(u,u))

\displaystyle=\psi(d(u,Tu))-\varphi(d(u,Tu)),

and by an argument similar to the above, we conclude that
\delta(u,Tu)=0

or
\{u\}=Tu
.

Now assume that the end point of

and

that

is a unique common end point for

and

in

, then

\displaystyle\psi(d(u,v))=\psi(\delta(Su,Tv))\leq\psi(M(u,v))-\varphi(M(u,v))

\displaystyle=\psi(d(u,v))-\varphi(d(u,v)),
