K-theoretic Hall algebras of quivers with potential as Hopf algebras
Tudor Pădurariu

Abstract
. Preprojective K-theoretic Hall algebras (KHAs), particular cases of KHAs of quivers with potential, are conjecturally positive halves of the Okounkov–Smirnov affine quantum algebras. It is thus natural to ask whether KHAs of quivers with potential are halves of a quantum group. For a symmetric quiver with potential satisfying a Kunneth-type condition, we construct (positive and negative) extensions of its KHA which are bialgebras. In particular, there are bialgebra extensions of preprojective KHAs and one can construct their Drinfeld double algebra.



1.
Introduction

1.1.
Hall algebras for quivers with potential

Let
Q=(I,E)
be a symmetric quiver, let
W
be a potential of
Q
, and let
d\in\mathbb{N}^{I}
be a dimension vector of
Q
.
Denote by
\mathcal{X}(d)
the stack of representations of
Q
of dimension
d
over
\mathbb{C}
and by
\mathcal{X}(d)_{0}
the (derived) zero locus of the regular function

\text{Tr}\,W:\mathcal{X}(d)\to\mathbb{A}^{1}_{\mathbb{C}}.

Let
D_{\text{sg}}\left(\mathcal{X}(d)_{0}\right)

be the category of singularities. It is equivalent to the category of (coherent) matrix factorizations
\text{MF}(\mathcal{X}(d),W)
for the regular function
\text{Tr}\,W
.
The categorical Hall algebra is the category
\bigoplus_{d\in\mathbb{N}^{I}}D_{\text{sg}}\left(\mathcal{X}(d)_{0}\right)
with multiplication

(1)

m_{d,e}:=p_{d,e*}q_{d,e}^{*}:D_{\text{sg}}\left(\mathcal{X}(d)_{0}\right)\otimes D_{\text{sg}}\left(\mathcal{X}(e)_{0}\right)\to D_{\text{sg}}\left(\mathcal{X}(d+e)_{0}\right),

where
q_{d,e}:\mathcal{X}(d,e)\to\mathcal{X}(d)\times\mathcal{X}(e)
and
p_{d,e}:\mathcal{X}(d,e)\to\mathcal{X}(d+e)
are the natural maps from the stack of extensions
\mathcal{X}(d,e)
. The K-theoretic Hall algebra (KHA) of
(Q,W)
is
K_{0}
of the categorical Hall algebra.

The above construction is suggested by Kontsevich–Soibelman in
[
9
]
where they studied its cohomological version, see
[
16
]
for more details about the categorical and K-theoretic constructions.

1.2.
Preprojective Hall algebras

For
Q
a quiver, let
\mathfrak{P}(d)

be the (derived) stack of representations of the preprojective algebra of

[

[

]
, Varagnolo–Vasserot
[

]

[

[
15
]
. It is thus natural to ask whether one can construct a full quantum group from a KHA.

For
Q
a quiver,
there is a tripled quiver with potential

whose Hall algebra is equivalent, via the Köszul equivalence/ dimensional reduction for categories of singularities

\varepsilon_{i}\in\mathbb{N}^{I}

\varepsilon_{i}
for

is a vector with

We check that

for

products of elements from such dimensions

\deg(y)=a
,
\deg(y^{\prime})=b
,
\deg(x)=a+b=d
.

For elements

, the statement follows from the argument in
[
11
, Exercise IV.2]
, which we now explain. Let
A_{i}=\{1,\cdots,a_{i}\}
and
B_{i}=\{a_{i}+1,\cdots,d_{i}\}
.
Let
E

\textbf{E}=\{(i,i^{\prime},j,j^{\prime})|\,i,i^{\prime}\in I,(j,j^{\prime})\in(A_{i},A_{i^{\prime}}),(B_{i},B_{i^{\prime}}),(B_{i},A_{i^{\prime}})\}.

We will use the shorthand notations
z_{A}
for
z_{ij}
with

and
j\in A_{i}
and
z_{B}
for

and

Using Proposition

, we obtain that:

(x,y*y^{\prime})=\frac{1}{a!b!}\int^{|q|<1<|p|}_{|z_{A}|=|z_{B}|=1}\frac{\iota^{*}_{d}(x)\iota_{a}^{*}(y)\iota_{b}^{*}(y^{\prime})}{\prod_{\textbf{E}}\widetilde{\zeta}_{ii^{\prime}}\left(\frac{z_{ij}}{z_{i^{\prime}j^{\prime}}}\right)\prod_{\begin{subarray}{c}i\in I\\
j,k\end{subarray}}\mu_{i}\left(\frac{z_{ij}}{z_{ik}}\right)}\prod_{\begin{subarray}{c}i\in I\\
j\in A_{i}\end{subarray}}Dz_{ij}\prod_{\begin{subarray}{c}i\in I\\
j\in B_{i}\end{subarray}}Dz_{ij}.

Call the integrand in the above formula

. Recall from the Subsection
4.2.2
that the pairing between an element in
\text{KHA}_{T}\left(\widetilde{Q},\widetilde{W}\right)_{\mathbb{F}}
and a

is zero.
By the formula in (

\displaystyle(\Delta^{\text{op}}(x),y\otimes y^{\prime})

\displaystyle=(\Delta(x),y^{\prime}\otimes y)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{a!b!}\int^{|q|<1<|p|}_{|z_{B}|\ll|z_{A}|}\frac{\iota^{*}_{d}(x)\iota_{b}^{*}(y^{\prime})\iota_{a}^{*}(y)}{\prod_{\textbf{E}}\widetilde{\zeta}_{ii^{\prime}}\left(\frac{z_{ij}}{z_{i^{\prime}j^{\prime}}}\right)\prod_{\begin{subarray}{c}i\in I\\

j\in A_{i}\end{subarray}}Dz_{ij}\prod_{\begin{subarray}{c}i\in I\\

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{a!b!}\int^{|q|<1<|p|}_{|z_{A}|\gg|z_{B}|}\frac{\iota^{*}_{d}(x)\iota_{a}^{*}(y)\iota_{b}^{*}(y^{\prime})}{\prod_{\textbf{E}}\widetilde{\zeta}_{ii^{\prime}}\left(\frac{z_{ij}}{z_{i^{\prime}j^{\prime}}}\right)\prod_{\begin{subarray}{c}i\in I\\
j,k\end{subarray}}\mu_{i}\left(\frac{z_{ij}}{z_{ik}}\right)}\prod_{\begin{subarray}{c}i\in I\\

The last equality holds because

is

-symmetric.

\int^{|q|<1<|p|}_{|z_{A}|=|z_{B}|=1}u\prod_{\begin{subarray}{c}i\in I\\

j\in A_{i}\end{subarray}}Dz_{ij}\prod_{\begin{subarray}{c}i\in I\\
j\in B_{i}\end{subarray}}Dz_{ij}\bigg|_{p\to q}.

The integrand

has poles involving

and
z_{B}
in the left hand side region at

in an edge of

,

(\,,\,)

\displaystyle\prod_{\begin{subarray}{c}i^{\prime}\in I\\
