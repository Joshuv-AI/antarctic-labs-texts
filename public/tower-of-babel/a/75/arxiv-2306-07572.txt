Clairaut anti-invariant Riemannian maps to trans-Sasakian manifolds
Adeeba Zaidi, Gauree Shanker

Abstract
In this article, we introduce Clairaut anti-invariant Riemannian maps from Riemannian manifolds to trans-Sasakian manifolds. We derive necessary and sufficient condition for an anti-invariant map to be Clairaut when base manifold is trans-Sasakian manifold. We discuss the integrability of r ​ a ​ n ​ g ​ e ​ π ∗ range\pi_{*} and ( r ​ a ​ n ​ g ​ e ​ π ∗ ) ⟂ (range\pi_{*})^{\perp} . Further, we establish harmonicity of these map. Finally, we construct a nontrivial example of such maps for justification.

Preliminaries

In this section, we recall the definitions of contact manifolds, trans-Sasakian manifolds and some important properties related to Riemannian maps.

Let

(2n+1)-

(1,1)

1-

\psi^{2}=-I+\eta\otimes\xi,~~~\psi\xi=0,~~\eta\circ\psi=0,~~\eta(\xi)=1,

(2.1)

where

is the identity mapping. A Riemannian metric

on an almost-contact manifold
B
is said to be compatible with the almost-contact structure
(\psi,\xi,\eta)
, if for any vector fields
W,Z\in\varGamma(TB)
,

satisfies (
[
6
]
)

\displaystyle g(\psi W,\psi Z)\

\displaystyle=\ g(W,Z)-\eta(W)\eta(Z),

(2.2)

\displaystyle g(\psi W,Z)

\displaystyle=g(W,\psi Z),\ \eta(W)\ =\ g(W,\xi),

(2.3)

the structure
(\psi,\xi,\eta,g)
is called an almost contact metric structure.

The almost contact structure
(\psi,\xi,\eta)
is said to be normal if
N+d\eta\otimes\xi=0,
where
N
is the
Nijenhuis tensor of
\psi
. If
d\eta=\Phi
, where
\Phi(W,Z)=g(\psi W,Z)
is a tensor field of type
(0,2)
, then an almost contact metric
structure is said to be normal contact metric structure. An almost contact metric manifold

is called a trans-Sasakian manifold of type
(\alpha,\beta)
(
[
6
]
), if it satisfies

\displaystyle(\nabla_{W}\psi)Z\

\displaystyle=\ \alpha(g(W,Z)\xi-\eta(Z)W)+\beta[g(\psi W,Z)\xi-\eta(Z)\psi W],

(2.4)

\displaystyle(\nabla_{W}\eta)Z\

\displaystyle=\ -\alpha g(\psi W,Z)\xi+\beta g(\psi W,\psi Z),

(2.5)

\displaystyle\nabla_{W}\xi

\displaystyle=-\alpha\psi W+\beta(W-\eta(W)\xi),

(2.6)

where
\alpha,\ \beta
are smooth functions and
\nabla
is Levi-Civita connection of
g
on
B

(\alpha,0)
is a
\alpha-
Sasakian manifold and a trans-Sasakian manifold of type
(0,\beta)
is a
\beta-
Kenmotsu manifold. A trans-Sasakian manifold of type (0, 0) is called a cosymplectic manifold. In particular, for
\alpha=1,\beta=0

\alpha=0,\beta=1
