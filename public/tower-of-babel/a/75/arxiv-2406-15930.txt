Irrationality of the Deformed Euler Numbers e s , t , u \mathrm{e}_{s,t,u}
Ronald Orozco López

Abstract
In this paper, we define the deformed Euler ( s , t ) (s,t) -numbers e s , t , u \mathrm{e}_{s,t,u} Furthermore, we prove that e a ​ s , a 2 ​ t , u − 1 \mathrm{e}_{as,a^{2}t,u^{-1}} and e a ​ s , a 2 ​ t , u − 1 − 1 \mathrm{e}_{as,a^{2}t,u^{-1}}^{-1} are irrational numbers when a , u ∈ ℚ a,u\in\mathbb{Q} and | a ​ u | > 1 |au|>1 , thus providing a countable infinite family of irrational numbers. This is the first step in a program to study the irrationality of ( s , t ) (s,t) -analog of known numbers.

Proposition 2
.

For all

it holds that
\mathopen{{\mathchoice{\hbox{$\displaystyle\left\{\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\textstyle\left\{\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\scriptstyle\left\{\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\scriptscriptstyle\left\{\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}}}{n}\mathclose{{\mathchoice{\hbox{$\displaystyle\left\}\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\textstyle\left\}\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\scriptstyle\left\}\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\scriptscriptstyle\left\}\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}}}_{as,a^{2}t}=a^{n-1}\mathopen{{\mathchoice{\hbox{$\displaystyle\left\{\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\textstyle\left\{\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\scriptstyle\left\{\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\scriptscriptstyle\left\{\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}}}{n}\mathclose{{\mathchoice{\hbox{$\displaystyle\left\}\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\textstyle\left\}\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\scriptstyle\left\}\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\scriptscriptstyle\left\}\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}}}_{s,t}

\mathopen{{\mathchoice{\hbox{$\displaystyle\left\{\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\textstyle\left\{\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\scriptstyle\left\{\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\scriptscriptstyle\left\{\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}}}{n}\mathclose{{\mathchoice{\hbox{$\displaystyle\left\}\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\textstyle\left\}\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\scriptstyle\left\}\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\scriptscriptstyle\left\}\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}}}_{as,a^{2}t}!=a^{\binom{n}{2}}\mathopen{{\mathchoice{\hbox{$\displaystyle\left\{\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\textstyle\left\{\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\scriptstyle\left\{\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\scriptscriptstyle\left\{\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}}}{n}\mathclose{{\mathchoice{\hbox{$\displaystyle\left\}\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\textstyle\left\}\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\scriptstyle\left\}\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\scriptscriptstyle\left\}\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}}}_{s,t}!
.

, then

\displaystyle\mathopen{{\mathchoice{\hbox{$\displaystyle\left\{\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\textstyle\left\{\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\scriptstyle\left\{\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\scriptscriptstyle\left\{\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}}}{n}\mathclose{{\mathchoice{\hbox{$\displaystyle\left\}\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\textstyle\left\}\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\scriptstyle\left\}\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\scriptscriptstyle\left\}\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}}}_{as,a^{2}t}

\mathopen{{\mathchoice{\hbox{$\displaystyle\left\{\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\textstyle\left\{\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\scriptstyle\left\{\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\scriptscriptstyle\left\{\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}}}{n}\mathclose{{\mathchoice{\hbox{$\displaystyle\left\}\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\textstyle\left\}\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\scriptstyle\left\}\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\scriptscriptstyle\left\}\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}}}_{as,a^{2}t}=a^{n-1}\mathopen{{\mathchoice{\hbox{$\displaystyle\left\{\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\textstyle\left\{\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\scriptstyle\left\{\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\scriptscriptstyle\left\{\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}}}{n}\mathclose{{\mathchoice{\hbox{$\displaystyle\left\}\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\textstyle\left\}\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\scriptstyle\left\}\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\scriptscriptstyle\left\}\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}}}_{a,t}
