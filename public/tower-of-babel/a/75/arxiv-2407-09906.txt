A refined version of the geometrically m m -step solvable Grothendieck conjecture for genus 0 0 curves over finitely generated fields
Naganori Yamaguchi

Abstract
In the present paper, we show a new result on the geometrically 2 2 -step solvable Grothendieck conjecture for genus 0 0 curves over finitely generated fields. More precisely, we show that two genus 0 0 hyperbolic curves over a finitely generated field k k are isomorphic as k k -schemes (up to Frobenius twists) if and only if the geometrically maximal 2 2 -step solvable quotients of their étale fundamental groups are isomorphic as topological groups over the absolute Galois group of k k . Keywords : anabelian geometry, étale fundamental group, Grothendieck conjecture 2020 Mathematics Subject Classification. Primary : 14H30, Secondary : 14E20.
