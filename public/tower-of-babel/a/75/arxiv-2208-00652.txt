Non-degenerate Hypergraphs with Exponentially Many Extremal Constructions
József Balogh, Felix Christian Clemen, Haoran Luo

Abstract
For every integer t ⩾ 0 t\geqslant 0 , denote by F 5 t F_{5}^{t} the hypergraph on vertex set { 1 , 2 , … , 5 + t } \{1,2,\ldots,5+t\} with hyperedges { 123,124 } ∪ { 34 ​ k : 5 ⩽ k ⩽ 5 + t } \{123,124\}\cup\{34k:5\leqslant k\leqslant 5+t\} . In particular, if n n satisfies certain divisibility conditions, then the extremal F 5 t F_{5}^{t} -free hypergraphs are exactly the balanced complete tripartite hypergraphs with additional hyperedges inside each of the three parts ( V 1 , V 2 , V 3 ) (V_{1},V_{2},V_{3}) in the partition; each part V i V_{i} spans a ( | V i | , 3 , 2 , t ) (|V_{i}|,3,2,t) -design. This generalizes earlier work of Frankl and Füredi on the Turán number of F 5 := F 5 0 F_{5}:=F_{5}^{0} . Our results extend a theory of Erdős and Simonovits about the extremal constructions for certain fixed graphs. Determining Turán numbers and the corresponding extremal constructions is one of the central problems in Extremal Combinatorics and has received extensive attention.

This problem is reasonably well-understood for graphs (the case where

The

It is a famous problem of Turán

In this paper, we give the first examples of hypergraphs which share this (conjectured) property with the tetrahedron in the following sense: each of them has exponentially many non-isomorphic extremal constructions and positive Turán density.
We note that our extremal constructions are close (i.e.

It remains open to find a single hypergraph with

One of the roadblocks in our proof is that there exists another family of almost extremal

-free

The value of

is closely related to designs. An

[

Theorem 1.2

(Dehon
[
3
]
)
.

Let

be integers such that

. Then, there exists an
(n,3,2,t)

If

t!^{-\binom{n}{2}}\left(\left(\frac{t}{e}\right)^{2}(n-2)+o(n-2)\right)^{\frac{t}{3}\binom{n}{2}}=n^{\Omega(n^{2})}.

Thus, by Theorem
1.1

.

Note that if

-design.
Thus, we have the following corollary of Theorem

Corollary 1.3

For every

, if

is sufficiently large and

\mathrm{ex}(n,F_{5}^{t})=\frac{1}{27}n^{3}+\frac{1}{18}tn^{2}-\frac{1}{6}tn.

There does not exist an

-design for every

We cannot give an explicit formula for
\mathrm{ex}(n,F_{2}^{t})
. Similar phenomena often occur in Extremal Combinatorics. An interesting classical example is by Erdős and Simonovits
[

Proof.

\displaystyle s_{t,\pi}^{\circ}(n)

\displaystyle=a_{2}\left(\frac{n-a_{2}}{2}\right)^{2}+t\binom{n}{2}\leqslant\left(\frac{1}{3}-0.01\right)n\left(\frac{n-\left(\frac{1}{3}-0.01\right)n}{2}\right)^{2}+t\binom{n}{2}

\displaystyle\mathrm{ex}(a_{1}+1,F_{2}^{t})-\mathrm{ex}(a_{1},F_{2}^{t})\geqslant\frac{t}{3}\binom{a_{1}+1}{2}-\frac{a_{1}+1}{3}-\frac{t^{2}}{6}-3t-\frac{t}{3}\binom{a_{1}}{2}\geqslant\frac{t}{3}a_{1}-\frac{a_{1}+1}{3}-4t^{2}

\displaystyle|H|=\frac{1}{3}\binom{n-1}{2}+\frac{n}{2}=\frac{1}{3}\binom{n}{2}+\frac{n}{6}+\frac{1}{3}\leqslant\frac{1}{3}\binom{n}{2}+\frac{n}{3}.

Now, suppose there exists an
(n+1,3,2,1)
-design on
[n+1]
. Let
\{v_{i}u_{i}(n+1)\}
be the hyperedges containing vertex
n+1
.
Remove vertex
n+1
, and then add hyperedge
v_{i}u_{i}z_{i}
, where
z_{i}
is an arbitrary vertex, for every
i
.
The resulting hypergraph
H
has the desired properties:
d(x,y)\geqslant 1
for every
x,y\in[n]
and

|H|\leqslant\frac{1}{3}\binom{n+1}{2}=\frac{1}{3}\binom{n}{2}+\frac{n}{3}.\qed

Claim 5.5
.

For every odd
t\geqslant 1
, there exists a
(t+3)
-vertex
3
-graph
H
with
|H|\geqslant\frac{t}{3}\binom{t+3}{2}-\frac{t+3}{3}
such that each pair of vertices has codegree at most
t
.

Proof.

Take a copy of the complete
3
-graph on
t+3
vertices and remove the hyperedge set of a
(t+3)
-vertex
3
-graph
H^{\prime}
, where
|H^{\prime}|\leqslant\frac{1}{3}\binom{t+3}{2}+\frac{t+3}{3}
and
d(x,y)\geqslant 1
for every pair of vertices
x,y
in
H^{\prime}
. Such a hypergraph exists by Claim
5.4
. The resulting hypergraph
H
has the desired properties: every pair of vertices has codegree at most
t
and

|H|\geqslant\binom{t+3}{3}-\left(\frac{1}{3}\binom{t+3}{2}+\frac{t+3}{3}\right)=\frac{t}{3}\binom{t+3}{2}-\frac{t+3}{3}.\qed

Lemma 5.6
.

For odd
t\geqslant 1
and sufficiently large even
n
, we have

\mathrm{ex}(n,F_{2}^{t})\geqslant\frac{t}{3}\binom{n}{2}-\frac{n}{3}-\frac{t^{2}}{6}-3t.

Proof.

Let
s=s(n):=\left\lfloor\frac{n}{t+3}\right\rfloor
. Partition
[n]
into disjoint sets
B_{1}\cup\ldots\cup B_{s}\cup J
, where
|B_{k}|=t+3
for each
k\in[s]
and
0\leqslant|J|<t+3
. Note that
|B_{k}|
and
|J|
are both even. Assume
J=\{w_{1},\ldots,w_{|J|}\}
. Define
G_{1}
to be the
n
-vertex graph where
uv\not\in E(G_{1})
iff
u,v\in B_{k}
for some
k\in[s]
or
\{u,v\}=\{w_{2j-1},w_{2j}\}
for some
j\in[|J|/2]
. Let
G_{2}
be the graph obtained from
G_{1}
by removing
4
edges that form a copy of
C_{4}
, every vertex of which comes from a different set
B_{k}
. Let
G_{3}
be the graph obtained from
G_{1}
by removing
8
edges that form two vertex-disjoint copies of
C_{4}
, where every vertex in these two
C_{4}
’s comes from a different set
B_{k}
. Note that
G_{1},G_{2},G_{3}
are all
(c,2812,1)
-typical for every
c\leqslant 0.9(1/2)^{2812}/(36^{2}\cdot 4^{36})
and every vertex in the graphs
G_{1},G_{2}
and
G_{3}
has an even degree. Further, we have that

|E(G_{1})|=|E(G_{2})|+4=|E(G_{3})|+8,

and hence there exists
i\in\{1,2,3\}
such that 3 divides
|E(G_{i})|
. Therefore,
G_{i}
is
(K_{3},t)
-divisible. By Theorem
5.1
, there exists a
(K_{3},t)
-design of
G_{i}
. Let
H
be the corresponding
3
-graph. Note that if
u,v\in B_{k}
for some
k\in[s]
, then
uv\notin E(G_{i})
, and hence
d(u,v)=0
in
H
. We have

|H|\geqslant\frac{t}{3}\left(\binom{n}{2}-s\binom{t+3}{2}-\frac{|J|}{2}-8\right).

Now, we construct an
n
-vertex hypergraph
H^{\prime}
from
H
by adding to every set
B_{k}
a copy of a
(t+3)
-vertex
3
-graph with at least
\frac{t}{3}\binom{t+3}{2}-\frac{t+3}{3}
hyperedges, each pair of vertices in which has codegree at most
t
. Such a hypergraph exists by Claim
5.5
. Then,
H^{\prime}
satisfies that every pair of vertices has codegree at most
t
and

\displaystyle|H^{\prime}|

\displaystyle\geqslant\frac{t}{3}\left(\binom{n}{2}-s\binom{t+3}{2}-\frac{|J|}{2}-8\right)+s\left(\frac{t}{3}\binom{t+3}{2}-\frac{t+3}{3}\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{t}{3}\binom{n}{2}-\frac{t|J|}{6}-\frac{8t}{3}-\frac{s(t+3)}{3}\geqslant\frac{t}{3}\binom{n}{2}-\frac{t^{2}}{6}-3t-\frac{n}{3}.\qed
