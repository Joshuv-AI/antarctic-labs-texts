Sagar Tirtha Goswami, Subhankar Roy

Abstract
A unique neutrino mass matrix is proposed in the light of normal ordering of neutrino masses, which partially concurs with Tribimaximal (TBM) mixing. The texture accommodates only three real independent parameters, which predict six observational parameters. It rules out any possibility of a vanishing θ 13 \theta_{13} . The texture, in its precise form, is generated in the framework of S ​ U ​ ( 2 ) L ⊗ A 4 SU(2)_{L}\otimes A_{4} symmetry through a combination of type-I and type-II seesaw mechanisms.

In summary, a new model independent texture for neutrino mass matrix is proposed. The texture is consistent with the normal ordering of neutrino masses along with a partial agreement to TBM mixing, i.e.,
\sin\,\theta_{12}=1/\sqrt{3}
and
\sin\,\theta_{23}=1/\sqrt{2}
. The texture rules out any possibility of
\theta_{13}
being zero. The texture is unique because it is not reducible to a
\mu-\tau
symmetric matrix, as might appear at a first glance. We have developed the texture in the flavour basis without using any approximations. The texture emphasises on the equality of the two Majorana phases. Starting from the first principle based on an extended framework of
SU(2)_{L}\otimes A_{4}
, we can obtain the proposed texture in its exact form in a hybrid framework of type-I and type-II seesaw mechanisms. We have shown in detail how the model parameters can be related to texture parameters in exact forms and a numerical estimation of the model parameters is also put forward. The flavon fields associated with the two seesaw mechanisms, after the symmetry breakings, acquire three VEVs each. In our work, we have assumed all the VEVs to be non-zero for both the flavon fields.

We underline that the proposed model independent texture with three free parameters only, predicts six observational parameters and the texture is derivable in its exact form from a model based on a blend of two seesaw mechanisms augmented by proper symmetry realisation.
