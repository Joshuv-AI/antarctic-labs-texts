Abstract
We introduce analogs of creation and annihilation operators, related to involutive and Hecke symmetries R R , and perform bosonic and fermionic realization of the modified Reflection Equation algebras in terms of the so-called Quantum Doubles of Fock type. Also, we introduce Quantum Doubles of Fock type, associated with Birman-Murakami-Wenzl symmetries coming from orthogonal or simplectic Quantum Groups and exhibit the algebras obtained by means of the corresponding bosonization (fermionization). Besides, we apply this scheme to current braidings arising from Hecke symmetries R R via the Baxterization procedure.

By an associative double we mean a couple

By a quantum double (QD) we mean an associative double where the map

with the same braiding

By a bosonic QD of Fock type we mean a double composed of the algebras
\mathrm{Sym}_{R}(V)
and
\mathrm{Sym}_{R}(V^{*})

and

In the classical case, i.e. when

In this section we deal with algebras associated with current braidings.
By a current braiding we mean an operator

R_{12}(u,v)\,R_{23}(u,w)\,R_{12}(v,w)=R_{23}(v,w)\,R_{12}(u,w)\,R_{23}(u,v).

[

for details.

Let us pass to the normalized braidings

g(u,v)=1-\frac{1}{u-v},\quad\mathrm{resp.}\quad g(u,v)=q-\frac{(q-q^{-1})u}{u-v}

It should be emphasized that these operators are still subject to the braid relation (
