Abstract
We report the first simultaneous and independent measurements of the K - p → Σ 0 ​ π 0 \rightarrow\Sigma^{0}\,\pi^{0} and K - p → Λ ​ π 0 \rightarrow\Lambda\,\pi^{0} cross sections around 100 MeV/c kaon momentum. The kaon beam delivered by the DA Φ \Phi NE collider was exploited to detect K - absorptions on Hydrogen atoms, populating the gas mixture of the KLOE drift chamber. The precision of the measurements ( σ K − ​ p → Σ 0 ​ π 0 = 42.8 ± 1.5 ( s t a t . ) − 2.0 + 2.4 ( s y s t . ) mb \sigma_{K^{-}p\rightarrow\Sigma^{0}\pi^{0}}=42.8\pm 1.5(stat.)^{+2.4}_{-2.0}(syst.)\ \mathrm{mb} and σ K − ​ p → Λ ​ π 0 = 31.0 ± 0.5 ( s t a t . ) − 1.2 + 1.2 ( s y s t . ) mb \sigma_{K^{-}p\rightarrow\Lambda\pi^{0}}=31.0\pm 0.5(stat.)^{+1.2}_{-1.2}(syst.)\ \mathrm{mb}\, ) is the highest yet obtained in the low kaon momentum regime.
