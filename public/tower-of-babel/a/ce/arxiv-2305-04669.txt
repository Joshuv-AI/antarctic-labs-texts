Symphonic map from ellipsoid to ellipsoid
Xiang-Zhi Cao

Abstract
In this paper, we proved the existence of Symphonic map from ellipsoid to ellipsoid. We also geive give Hopf construction of Symphonic map from ellipsoid to ellipsoid.

Introductory

It is well known that a map
f:(M,g)\to S^{n}(1)
is harmonic map if and only if

\displaystyle\Delta_{g}f+|\nabla f|^{2}f=0.

When M is
S^{m}
, we can construct harmonic map
f:(S^{m}(1),g)\to S^{n}(1)
by restricting the map between Euclidean space, whose component is harmonic homogeneos polynomials.
