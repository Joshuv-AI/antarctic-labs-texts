is a small parameter,

Proposition 1.1
.

[
28
]
Let
\theta,r>1
and
0<\mu<N
with
\frac{1}{\theta}+\frac{1}{r}=2-\frac{\mu}{N}
. Let
f\in L^{\theta}(\mathbb{R}^{N})

and
g\in L^{r}(\mathbb{R}^{N})
, there exists a sharp constant
C(\theta,r,\mu,N)
independent of
f,g
, such that

(1.9)

\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}{\frac{f(x)g(\xi)}{|x-\xi|^{\mu}}}dxd\xi\leq C(\theta,r,\mu,N)\|f\|_{\theta}\|g\|_{r}.

If
\theta=r=\frac{2N}{2N-\mu}
, then

C(\theta,r,\mu,N)=C_{N,\mu}=\pi^{\frac{\mu}{2}}\frac{\Gamma(\frac{N-\mu}{2})}{\Gamma(N-\frac{\mu}{2})}\Big\{\frac{\Gamma(N)}{\Gamma(\frac{N}{2})}\Big\}^{\frac{N-\mu}{N}}.

There is equality in (
1.9
)
if and only if
f\equiv(const.)g
and

g(x)=\bar{A}(\bar{\gamma}^{2}+|x-\bar{a}|^{2})^{-\frac{N+\mu}{2}}

for some
\bar{A}\in\mathbb{C}
,
0\neq\bar{\gamma}\in\mathbb{R}
and
\bar{a}\in\mathbb{R}^{N}
.

According to Proposition
1.1
, the functional

\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}\frac{|u(x)|^{p}|v(y)|^{p}}{|x-y|^{\mu}}dxdy

is well defined in

if

\frac{2N-\mu}{N}\leq p\leq\frac{2N-\mu}{N-2}
.
Here, it is quite natural to call
\frac{2N-\mu}{N}
the lower Hardy-Littlewood-Sobolev critical exponent and
2_{\mu}^{\ast}:=\frac{2N-\mu}{N-2}
the upper Hardy-Littlewood-Sobolev critical exponent. In the following, we use
S_{H,L}
to denote best constant defined by

(1.10)

S_{H,L}:=\inf_{u\in D^{1,2}(\mathbb{R}^{N})\setminus\{0\}}\frac{\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}|\nabla u|^{2}dx}{\big(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}\frac{|u(x)|^{2_{\mu}^{\ast}}|u(\xi)|^{2_{\mu}^{\ast}}}{|x-\xi|^{\mu}}dxd\xi\big)^{\frac{N-2}{2N-\mu}}}.

In this way, we know that (
1.13
) is closely related to the nonlocal Euler-Lagrange equation

(1.11)

\displaystyle-\Delta u=\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}\frac{u^{2_{\mu}^{\ast}}(\xi)}{|x-\xi|^{\mu}}d\xi\right)u^{2_{\mu}^{\ast}-1},\penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \text{in}\penalty\ \penalty\ \mathbb{R}^{N}.
