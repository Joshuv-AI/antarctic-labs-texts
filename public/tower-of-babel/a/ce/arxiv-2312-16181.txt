On generalized Li-Yau inequalities
Li-Chang Hung

Abstract
. We generalize the Li-Yau inequality for second derivatives and we also establish Li-Yau type inequality for fourth derivatives. Our derivation relies on the representation formula for the heat equation.

Introduction

The Li-Yau inequality asserts that if
u=u(x,t)
is a positive solution to the heat equation, then the logarithm of
u(x,t)
forms a supersolution to Laplace’s equation, i.e.

(1.1)

\Delta\log u\geq-\frac{n}{2t},

where

[
2

Our goal here is to give a direct proof of a generalization of inequality (
1.1

u_{t}=\Delta u

1

u(x,t)=\displaystyle\frac{1}{(4\pi t)^{n/2}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}g(y)e^{-\frac{|x-y|^{2}}{4t}}\,dy\quad(x\in\mathbb{R}^{n},\;t>0),

where the initial condition
