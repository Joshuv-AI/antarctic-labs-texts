General Formulas of the Structure Constants in the 𝔰 ​ 𝔲 ​ ( N ) \mathfrak{su}(N) Lie Algebra
Duncan Bossion, Pengfei Huo

Abstract
We provide the analytic expressions of the totally symmetric and anti-symmetric structure constants in the 𝔰 ​ 𝔲 ​ ( N ) \mathfrak{su}(N) Lie algebra. The derivation is based on a relation linking the index of a generator to the indexes of its non-null elements. The closed formulas obtained to compute the values of the structure constants are simple expressions involving those indexes and can be analytically evaluated without any need of the expression of the generators. We hope that these expressions can be widely used for analytical and computational interest in Physics.

\mathfrak{su}(N)

The generators of the
\mathfrak{su}(N)
Lie algebra can be expressed as follows

;

\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{\mathrm{S}_{nm}}=\frac{\hbar}{2}\big(|m\rangle\langle n|+|n\rangle\langle m|\big),

(3)

\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{\mathrm{A}_{nm}}=-i\frac{\hbar}{2}\big(|m\rangle\langle n|-|n\rangle\langle m|\big),

(4)

\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{\mathrm{D}_{n}}=\frac{\hbar}{\sqrt{2n(n-1)}}\Big(\sum_{k=1}^{n-1}|k\rangle\langle k|+(1-n)|n\rangle\langle n|\Big),

(5)

\mathrm{S}_{nm}

\mathrm{A}_{nm}

\mathrm{Tr}[\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{i}]=0

\mathrm{Tr}[\hat{\mathcal{S}_{i}}\hat{\mathcal{S}_{j}}]=\frac{\hbar^{2}}{2}\delta_{ij}

These higher dimensional

\mathfrak{su}(6)

Kokkedee 1969
; in the search of a Grand Unification Theory (GUT),

Runeson and Richardson 2020

\mathfrak{su}(N)

;

d

The crucial quantities to define an algebra are the generators and what govern their commutation and anti-commutation relations. These relations give rise to the structure constants, where the totally anti-symmetric structure constant,
f_{ijk}
, is defined through the commutation relation

[\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{i},\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{j}]=i\hbar\sum_{k=1}^{N^{2}-1}f_{ijk}\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{k},

(6)

which is anti-symmetric under the exchange of any two indexes. The totally symmetric structure constant,
d_{ijk}
, is determined through the anti-commutation relation

\{\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{i},\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{j}\}=\frac{\hbar^{2}}{N}\delta_{ij}\hat{\mathcal{I}}+\hbar\sum_{k=1}^{N^{2}-1}d_{ijk}\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{k},

(7)

which is symmetric under the exchange of any two indexes. Using the properties in Eq.
6
and Eq.
7
, as well as the fact that the generators are traceless and orthogonal to each other, one can derive the well-known results
Haber 2021

f_{ijk}=-i\frac{2}{\hbar^{3}}\mathrm{Tr}\big[[\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{i},\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{j}]\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{k}\big];~d_{ijk}=\frac{2}{\hbar^{3}}\mathrm{Tr}\big[\{\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{i},\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{j}\}\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{k}\big].

(8)

5

The Totally Anti-symmetric Structure Constants
f_{ijk}
.

The commutation relation between two symmetric generators is

\displaystyle[\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{\mathrm{S}_{nm}},\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{\mathrm{S}_{n^{\prime}m^{\prime}}}]=i\hbar\sum_{k=1}^{N^{2}-1}f_{\mathrm{S}_{nm}\mathrm{S}_{n^{\prime}m^{\prime}}k}\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{k}

(10)

\displaystyle=\frac{\hbar^{2}}{4}\Big[\delta_{nm^{\prime}}(|m\rangle\langle n^{\prime}|-|n^{\prime}\rangle\langle m|)+\delta_{nn^{\prime}}(|m\rangle\langle m^{\prime}|-|m^{\prime}\rangle\langle m|)

\displaystyle~~~+\delta_{mm^{\prime}}(|n\rangle\langle n^{\prime}|-|n^{\prime}\rangle\langle n|)+\delta_{mn^{\prime}}(|n\rangle\langle m^{\prime}|-|m^{\prime}\rangle\langle n|)\Big]

\displaystyle=i\frac{\hbar}{2}\Big[\delta_{nm^{\prime}}\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{\mathrm{A}_{n^{\prime}m}}+\delta_{nn^{\prime}}(\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{\mathrm{A}_{m^{\prime}m}}-\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{\mathrm{A}_{mm^{\prime}}})

\displaystyle~~~+\delta_{mm^{\prime}}(\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{\mathrm{A}_{n^{\prime}n}}-\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{\mathrm{A}_{nn^{\prime}}})-\delta_{mn^{\prime}}\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{\mathrm{A}_{nm^{\prime}}}\Big].

With constraints from
\delta_{ij}
, we can identify the index of each generator, and hence obtain the non-zero analytic expression of
f_{\mathrm{S}_{nm}\mathrm{S}_{n^{\prime}m^{\prime}}k}
, which are summarized in the first line of Eq.
14
.

For a symmetric and an anti-symmetric generator, the commutation relation is

\displaystyle[\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{\mathrm{S}_{nm}},\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{\mathrm{A}_{n^{\prime}m^{\prime}}}]=i\hbar\sum_{k=1}^{N^{2}-1}f_{\mathrm{S}_{nm}\mathrm{A}_{n^{\prime}m^{\prime}}k}\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{k}

(11)

\displaystyle=i\frac{\hbar^{2}}{4}\Big[\delta_{nn^{\prime}}(|m\rangle\langle m^{\prime}|+|m^{\prime}\rangle\langle m|)-\delta_{nm^{\prime}}(|m\rangle\langle n^{\prime}|+|n^{\prime}\rangle\langle m|)

\displaystyle~~~+\delta_{mn^{\prime}}(|n\rangle\langle m^{\prime}|+|m^{\prime}\rangle\langle n|)-\delta_{mm^{\prime}}(|n\rangle\langle n^{\prime}|+|n^{\prime}\rangle\langle n|)\Big]

\displaystyle=i\frac{\hbar}{2}\Big[\delta_{nn^{\prime}}(\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{\mathrm{S}_{m^{\prime}m}}+\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{\mathrm{S}_{mm^{\prime}}})-\delta_{nm^{\prime}}\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{\mathrm{S}_{n^{\prime}m}}

\displaystyle~~~+\delta_{mn^{\prime}}\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{\mathrm{S}_{nm^{\prime}}}-\delta_{mm^{\prime}}(\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{\mathrm{S}_{n^{\prime}n}}+\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{\mathrm{S}_{nn^{\prime}}})

\displaystyle~~~+\frac{\hbar}{2}\delta_{mm^{\prime}}\delta_{nn^{\prime}}(2|m\rangle\langle m|-2|n\rangle\langle n|)\Big].

The first two lines of the last equality in Eq.
11
give us directly several structure constants.
The last line of Eq.
11
contains diagonal elements, hence we know it will be a combination of diagonal generators. In fact, we can prove (see Supplemental Material
SI
, Sec I) that

\displaystyle i\frac{\hbar^{2}}{2}(|m\rangle\langle m|-|n\rangle\langle n|)

(12)

\displaystyle=i\hbar\Big(\sqrt{\frac{n}{2(n-1)}}\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{\mathrm{D}_{n}}+\sum_{k>m}^{n-1}\frac{\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{\mathrm{D}_{k}}}{\sqrt{2k(k-1)}}-\sqrt{\frac{m-1}{2m}}\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{\mathrm{D}_{m}}\Big).

This helps to determine the rest of the structure constant
f_{\mathrm{S}_{nm}\mathrm{A}_{n^{\prime}m^{\prime}}k}
with the expressions documented in Eq.
14
.
The commutation relations between symmetric and diagonal generators are not required as we already obtained all the non-zero structure constants involving diagonal and symmetric generators (as we know that we cannot obtain a diagonal matrix through the commutator of a symmetric and a diagonal generator).

Between two anti-symmetric generators, the commutation relation is

\displaystyle[\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{\mathrm{A}_{nm}},\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{\mathrm{A}_{n^{\prime}m^{\prime}}}]=i\hbar\sum_{k=1}^{N^{2}-1}f_{\mathrm{A}_{nm}\mathrm{A}_{n^{\prime}m^{\prime}}k}\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{k}

(13)

\displaystyle=\frac{\hbar^{2}}{4}\Big[\delta_{nm^{\prime}}(|n^{\prime}\rangle\langle m|-|m\rangle\langle n^{\prime}|)+\delta_{nn^{\prime}}(|m\rangle\langle m^{\prime}|-|m^{\prime}\rangle\langle m|)

\displaystyle~~~+\delta_{mm^{\prime}}(|n\rangle\langle n^{\prime}|-|n^{\prime}\rangle\langle n|)+\delta_{mn^{\prime}}(|m^{\prime}\rangle\langle n|-|n\rangle\langle m^{\prime}|)\Big]

\displaystyle=i\frac{\hbar}{2}\Big[-\delta_{nm^{\prime}}\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{\mathrm{A}_{n^{\prime}m}}+\delta_{nn^{\prime}}(\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{\mathrm{A}_{m^{\prime}m}}-\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{\mathrm{A}_{mm^{\prime}}})

\displaystyle~~~+\delta_{mm^{\prime}}(\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{\mathrm{A}_{n^{\prime}n}}-\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{\mathrm{A}_{nn^{\prime}}})+\delta_{mn^{\prime}}\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{\mathrm{A}_{nm^{\prime}}}\Big],

which helps to determine the structure constants involving all anti-symmetric generators (second line of Eq.
14
). The remaining totally anti-symmetric structure constants are computed through the commutator between two diagonal generators, which is
[\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{\mathrm{D}_{n}},\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{\mathrm{D}_{n^{\prime}}}]=i\hbar\sum_{k=1}^{N^{2}-1}f_{\mathrm{D}_{n}\mathrm{D}_{n^{\prime}}k}\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{k}=0
(see proof in Supplemental Material, Sec II), indicating a zero value for all
f_{\mathrm{D}_{n}\mathrm{D}_{n^{\prime}}k}
. This was a known fact, as the diagonal matrices are generators of the Cartan subalgebra of
\mathfrak{su}(N)
, and they commute by definition
Georgi 2000
.

To summarize, all of the non-zero totally anti-symmetric structure constants are expressed as follows

\displaystyle f_{\mathrm{S}_{nm}\mathrm{S}_{kn}\mathrm{A}_{km}}=f_{\mathrm{S}_{nm}\mathrm{S}_{nk}\mathrm{A}_{km}}=f_{\mathrm{S}_{nm}\mathrm{S}_{km}\mathrm{A}_{kn}}=\frac{1}{2},

(14)

\displaystyle f_{\mathrm{A}_{nm}\mathrm{A}_{km}\mathrm{A}_{kn}}=\frac{1}{2},

\displaystyle f_{\mathrm{S}_{nm}\mathrm{A}_{nm}\mathrm{D}_{m}}=-\sqrt{\frac{m-1}{2m}},~~~f_{\mathrm{S}_{nm}\mathrm{A}_{nm}\mathrm{D}_{n}}=\sqrt{\frac{n}{2(n-1)}},

\displaystyle~~~~~~~~~f_{\mathrm{S}_{nm}\mathrm{A}_{nm}\mathrm{D}_{k}}=\sqrt{\frac{1}{2k(k-1)}},~m<k<n.

One of the interesting usages of these expressions is the construction of an adjoint representation of the
\mathfrak{su}(N)
Lie algebra, which is a defining representation of SO(
N^{2}-1

\hat{\mathcal{T}}_{i}

\hat{\mathcal{T}}_{i}

The anti-commutation relation between two symmetric generators is

\displaystyle\{\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{\mathrm{S}_{nm}},\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{\mathrm{S}_{n^{\prime}m^{\prime}}}\}=\frac{\hbar^{2}}{N}\delta_{\mathrm{S}_{nm}\mathrm{S}_{n^{\prime}m^{\prime}}}\hat{\mathcal{I}}+\hbar\sum_{k=1}^{N^{2}-1}d_{\mathrm{S}_{nm}\mathrm{S}_{n^{\prime}m^{\prime}}k}\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{k}

\displaystyle=\frac{\hbar^{2}}{4}\Big[\delta_{nm^{\prime}}(|m\rangle\langle n^{\prime}|+|n^{\prime}\rangle\langle m|)+\delta_{nn^{\prime}}(|m\rangle\langle m^{\prime}|+|m^{\prime}\rangle\langle m|)

\displaystyle~~~+\delta_{mm^{\prime}}(|n\rangle\langle n^{\prime}|+|n^{\prime}\rangle\langle n|)+\delta_{mn^{\prime}}(|n\rangle\langle m^{\prime}|+|m^{\prime}\rangle\langle n|)\Big]

\displaystyle=\frac{\hbar}{2}\Big[\delta_{nm^{\prime}}\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{\mathrm{S}_{n^{\prime}m}}+\delta_{nn^{\prime}}(\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{\mathrm{S}_{m^{\prime}m}}+\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{\mathrm{S}_{mm^{\prime}}})

\displaystyle~~~+\delta_{mm^{\prime}}(\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{\mathrm{S}_{n^{\prime}n}}+\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{\mathrm{S}_{nn^{\prime}}})+\delta_{mn^{\prime}}\hat{\mathcal{S}}_{\mathrm{S}_{nm^{\prime}}}

\displaystyle~~~+\delta_{nn^{\prime}}\delta_{mm^{\prime}}\frac{\hbar}{2}(2|m\rangle\langle m|+2|n\rangle\langle n|)\Big].

Conclusion.

In this letter, we provide the analytic expressions of the
totally symmetric and totally anti-symmetric structure constants for the
\mathfrak{su}(N)
Lie algebra. We hope that these expressions can be widely used for analytical and computational interest in Physics, as they are valid for any dimension
N
of
\mathfrak{su}(N)
Lie algebra without the need to explicitly compute the commutation and anti-commutation relations or use any generator. The structure constants bear important information on the algebra they belong to, and the possibility to obtain those constants with simple relations can bring insight into the high dimensional
\mathfrak{su}(N)
Lie algebra, which might be challenging otherwise.
