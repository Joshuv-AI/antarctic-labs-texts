Constraints on intersection forms of spin 4-manifolds bounded by Seifert rational homology 3-spheres in terms of μ ¯ \overline{\mu} and κ \kappa invariants.
Masaaki Ue

Abstract
. We give some constraints on intersection forms of spin 4-manifolds bounded by Seifert rational homology 3-spheres in terms of μ ¯ \overline{\mu} -invariant, and compare them with those in terms of κ \kappa -invariant given by Manolescu. Furthermore in case of a Seifert rational homology 3-sphere, we show that the difference between κ \kappa and − μ ¯ -\overline{\mu} is at most 2 (and there is an example where the difference is 2), and they coincide with each other under some extra conditions, including Floer K G K_{G} split cases.
