Rational homology disk smoothings and Lefschetz fibrations
Hakho Choi

Abstract
. In this article, we generalize the results discussed in [ EMV11 ] by introducing a genus to generic fibers of Lefschetz fibrations. That is, we give families of relations in the mapping class groups of genus-1 surfaces with boundaries that represent rational homology disk smoothings of weighted homogeneous surface singularities whose resolution graphs are 3 3 -legged with a bad central vertex.

Theorem 1.1
.

For each resolution graph
\Gamma
in Figure
1
with bad central vertex, there is a relation
W_{\Gamma}=W_{\Gamma}^{\prime}
between words of right-handed Dehn twists in mapping class group of a genus-

with monodromy

is diffeomorphic to the minimal resolution of corresponding singularity

and Lefschetz fibration

with monodromy

To prove Theorem

\Gamma

PALF

on the minimal resolution of the singularity

Then, starting from the global monodromy

Remark 1.1
.

From the Lefschetz fibration

we constructed, we obtain a rational homology ball filling of the link of

. Hence, one may ask whether the total space of
