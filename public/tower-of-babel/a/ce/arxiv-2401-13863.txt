Abstract
This note is to give the analytic necessary and sufficient conditions for the boundedness-from-below conditions of general scalar potentials of two real scalar fields ϕ 1 \phi_{1} and ϕ 2 \phi_{2} and the Higgs bonson 𝐇 \mathbf{H} . Keyword: Boundedness-from-below; Positive definiteness; Scalar potentials; Analytical expression.



1
Introduction

Kannike
[
1
]
presented the boundedness-from-below conditions of general scalar potentials of two real scalar fields
\phi_{1}
and
\phi_{2}
and the Higgs bonson
\mathbf{H}
,

\displaystyle V(\phi_{1},\phi_{2},|H|)=

\displaystyle\lambda_{H}|H|^{4}+\lambda_{H20}|H|^{2}\phi_{1}^{2}+\lambda_{H11}|H|^{2}\phi_{1}\phi_{2}+\lambda_{H02}|H|^{2}\phi_{2}^{2}

(1)

\displaystyle+\lambda_{40}\phi_{1}^{4}+\lambda_{31}\phi_{1}^{3}\phi_{2}+\lambda_{22}\phi_{1}^{2}\phi_{2}^{2}+\lambda_{13}\phi_{1}\phi_{2}^{3}+\lambda_{04}\phi_{2}^{4}.

This is equivalent to an analytic necessary and sufficient conditions of

V(\phi_{1},\phi_{2},|H|)>0\mbox{ for all }\phi_{1},\phi_{2},\mathbf{H}.

For two real scalar fields
\phi_{1}
and
\phi_{2}
and the Higgs bonson
\mathbf{H}
, a general scalar potentials
V(\phi_{1},\phi_{2},|H|)
is rewritten as follows

V(\phi_{1},\phi_{2},|H|)=\lambda_{H}|H|^{4}+M^{2}(\phi_{1},\phi_{2})|H|^{2}+V(\phi_{1},\phi_{2}),

where

M^{2}(\phi_{1},\phi_{2})=\lambda_{H20}\phi_{1}^{2}+\lambda_{H11}\phi_{1}\phi_{2}+\lambda_{H02}\phi_{2}^{2},

V(\phi_{1},\phi_{2})=\lambda_{40}\phi_{1}^{4}+\lambda_{31}\phi_{1}^{3}\phi_{2}+\lambda_{22}\phi_{1}^{2}\phi_{2}^{2}\\
+\lambda_{13}\phi_{1}\phi_{2}^{3}+\lambda_{04}\phi_{2}^{4}.

(2)

So applying the well-known positivity conditions of quadratic polynomial

p(t)=at^{2}+bt+c

for all
t=|H|^{2}\geq 0
(which is showed hundreds of years ago),
V(\phi_{1},\phi_{2},|H|)>0
for all
\phi_{1},\phi_{2},\mathbf{H}
(
a=\lambda_{H}>0
) if and only if for all
\phi_{1},\phi_{2},

\begin{cases}c=V(\phi_{1},\phi_{2})>0,\mbox{ and either }b=M^{2}(\phi_{1},\phi_{2})\geq 0;\mbox{ or }\\
4ac-b^{2}=4\lambda_{H}V(\phi_{1},\phi_{2})-(M^{2}(\phi_{1},\phi_{2}))^{2}>0.\end{cases}

(3)

It is obvious that
M^{2}(\phi_{1},\phi_{2})=\lambda_{H20}\phi_{1}^{2}+\lambda_{H11}\phi_{1}\phi_{2}+\lambda_{H02}\phi_{2}^{2}
is a quadric form with respect to two variables
\phi_{1},\phi_{2}
, and hence, the inequality
M^{2}(\phi_{1},\phi_{2})\geq 0
is equivalent to positive semi-definiteness of its coefficient matrix
M^{2}
. Then by Sylvester’s criterion,
M^{2}(\phi_{1},\phi_{2})\geq 0
if and only if

\lambda_{H20}\geq 0,\ \lambda_{H02}\geq 0,\ \lambda_{H20}\lambda_{H02}-\frac{1}{4}\lambda_{H11}^{2}\geq 0.

(4)

So, Eqs.(
4
) is differ from Eqs.(54) and (55) of Kannicke
[
1
]
.

2
Boundedness-from-below conditions

Now we correct this mistake and present the analytic necessary and sufficient conditions are showed for the boundedness from below of scalar potential of two real scalar fields
\phi_{1}
and
\phi_{2}
and the Higgs doublet
\mathbf{H}
. It follows from the conclusion (
3

the analytic necessary and sufficient conditions of
V(\phi_{1},\phi_{2})>0
,

V(\phi_{1},\phi_{2})=\lambda_{40}\phi_{1}^{4}+\lambda_{31}\phi_{1}^{3}\phi_{2}+\lambda_{22}\phi_{1}^{2}\phi_{2}^{2}\\
+\lambda_{13}\phi_{1}\phi_{2}^{3}+\lambda_{04}\phi_{2}^{4}.

(5)

It is obvious that the discriminant
D\geq 0
is a necessary condition of
V(\phi_{1},\phi_{2})>0
.
Such a positivity condition may trace back to ones of Refs. Rees
[
2
]
, Lazard
[
3

Gadem-Li
[
4

[
5

and Jury-Mansour
[
6
]

[
7

improved their proof, and perfectly gave analytic necessary and sufficient conditions. For more detail about applications of these results, see Song-Qi
[
8
]
also. That is, for all
\phi_{1},\phi_{2}
with
(\phi_{1},\phi_{2})\neq(0,0)
, the binary quartic homogeneous polynomial (
5
),

V(\phi_{1},\phi_{2})>0
if and only if

\begin{cases}\lambda_{40}>0,\lambda_{04}>0,\\
D=0,\ G=0,\ R=0\mbox{ and }Q>0;\\
D>0\mbox{ and }Q\geq 0,\mbox{ or }R>0\end{cases}

(6)

where

\displaystyle G=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{4}\lambda_{40}^{2}\lambda_{13}-\frac{1}{8}\lambda_{40}\lambda_{31}\lambda_{22}+\frac{1}{32}\lambda_{31}^{3}

\displaystyle Q=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{6}\lambda_{40}\lambda_{22}-\frac{1}{16}\lambda_{31}^{2}=\frac{1}{48}(8\lambda_{40}\lambda_{22}-3\lambda_{31}^{2})

\displaystyle I=

\displaystyle\lambda_{40}\lambda_{04}-\frac{1}{4}\lambda_{31}\lambda_{13}+\frac{1}{12}\lambda_{22}^{2}

\displaystyle J=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{6}\lambda_{40}\lambda_{22}\lambda_{04}+\frac{1}{48}\lambda_{31}\lambda_{22}\lambda_{13}-\frac{1}{216}\lambda_{22}^{3}

\displaystyle-\frac{1}{16}\lambda_{40}\lambda_{13}^{2}-\frac{1}{16}\lambda_{31}^{2}\lambda_{04}

\displaystyle D=

\displaystyle I^{3}-27J^{2},R=\lambda_{40}^{2}I-12Q^{2}.

Recently, Qi-Song-Zhang
