Paired ( n − 1 ) (n-1) -to- ( n − 1 ) (n-1) Disjoint Path Covers in Bipartite Transposition-Like Graphs
Anna Coleman, Gabrielle Fischberg, Charles Gong, Joshua Harrington, Tony W. H. Wong

Abstract
A paired k k -to- k k disjoint path cover of a graph G G is a collection of pairwise disjoint path subgraphs P 1 , P 2 , … , P k P_{1},P_{2},\dotsc,P_{k} such that each P i P_{i} has prescribed vertices s i s_{i} and t i t_{i} as endpoints and the union of P 1 , P 2 , … , P k P_{1},P_{2},\dotsc,P_{k} contains all vertices of G G . In this paper, we introduce bipartite transposition-like graphs, which are inductively constructed from lower ranked bipartite transposition-like graphs. We show that every rank n n bipartite transposition-like graph G G admit a paired ( n − 1 ) (n-1) -to- ( n − 1 ) (n-1) disjoint path cover for all choices of S = { s 1 , s 2 , … , s n − 1 } S=\{s_{1},s_{2},\dotsc,s_{n-1}\} and T = { t 1 , t 2 , … , t n − 1 } T=\{t_{1},t_{2},\dotsc,t_{n-1}\} , provided that S S is in one partite set of G G and T T is in the other. MSC: (Primary) 05C45; (Secondary) 05C70, 05C75. Keywords: Disjoint path covers, transposition graphs, Hamiltonian laceable.

Lemma 1.4

Let

Proof.

If a graph is bipartite, then all its vertices can be properly colored with black and white, implying that every subgraph is bipartite. Hence, any rank
1
transposition-like subgraph

is Hamiltonian-laceable by Definition

is equitable. If

has an odd number of vertices, then we claim that the rank

is a weld of

than white. Since there is a perfect matching between

and

inside

, in order to maintain the bipartite property of

than black. This makes it impossible to have a perfect matching between

and

is a weld of

and

has one more black vertex than white and

has one more white vertex than black. As a result,

is equitable. Finally, our result follows from the simple observation that if

are equitable bipartite graphs and their weld

is equitable.
