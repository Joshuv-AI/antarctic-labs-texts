On the quaternionic Serre Weights
Yang CHEN and Haoran WANG

Abstract
. We give a representation theoretic description of the set of quaternionic Serre weights for generic two-dimensional mod p p representations of Gal ⁡ ( ℚ ¯ p / K ) \operatorname{Gal}(\overline{{\mathbb{Q}}}_{p}/K) , where K K is a finite unramified extension of ℚ p {\mathbb{Q}}_{p} .

Let
