Permutation Polynomial Interleaved Zadoff-Chu Sequences
Fredrik Berggren, Branislav M. Popović

Abstract
Constant amplitude zero autocorrelation (CAZAC) sequences have modulus one and ideal periodic autocorrelation function. Such sequences have been used in communications systems, e.g., for reference signals, synchronization signals and random access preambles. We propose a new family CAZAC sequences, which is constructed by interleaving a Zadoff-Chu sequence by a quadratic permutation polynomial (QPP), or by a permutation polynomial whose inverse is a QPP. It is demonstrated that a set of orthogonal interleaved Zadoff-Chu sequences can be constructed by proper choice of QPPs.

Introduction

Constant amplitude zero-autocorrelation (CAZAC) sequences are frequently used in waveforms for radar and for communications systems
[
1
]
, as they provide constant envelope and have ideal periodic autocorrelation function. CAZAC sequence constructions include, e.g., Zadoff-Chu sequences
[
2
]
, Frank sequences
[
3
]
, generalized chirp-like (GCL) sequences
[
4
]

[

]
, cubic polynomial phase sequences
[

, biphase sequences
[

and modulatable CAZAC sequences
[

]

[
9
]
and if the sequence length
N
is non-prime and divisible by a perfect square, then there are infinitely many CAZAC sequences. If
N
is non-prime and not divisible by a perfect square, it is unknown how many CAZAC sequences there are.

It has been shown that the CAZAC property is invariant under certain basic mathematical operations
[
10
]
. Herein, we are considering permutations and we are particularly interested in the properties of a CAZAC sequence being interleaved by a permutation polynomial. However, it appears that prior work has not provided insight on how to determine when/whether the CAZAC property is preserved for an arbitrary permutation, or for which CAZAC sequences it could be preserved. Zadoff-Chu sequences are frequently used in practice, e.g., as random access preambles, synchronization signals and reference signals in 4G and 5G
[
12
]
, and generating large sets of sequences with desirable correlation properties is a well-known problem. Therefore, in this work, we will address permutation polynomial interleaved Zadoff-Chu sequences, as a means to produce more CAZAC sequences.
The necessary definitions are given in Sec. II and the main results are stated in Sec. III. Examples are given in Sec. IV while Sec. V concludes the paper.

V
Conclusions

A new family of CAZAC sequences is proposed which are constructed from permutation polynomial interleaved Zadoff-Chu sequences. This enriches the set of methods that can be used to generate CAZAC sequences. QPP interleaving always preserves the CAZAC property and permutation polynomials of order larger than two could be used, albeit the CAZAC property is not maintained generally. A set of orthogonal sequences can, in many cases, be obtained from using different QPPs. Finally, we have observed from evaluations that, e.g., Frank sequences, also appear to preserve the CAZAC property under QPP interleaving We have also observed counter-examples where the CAZAC property is not preserved, e.g., QPP interleaving for biphase sequences according to
[
7
]
.
