On Some Series Involving the Binomial Coefficients ( 3 ​ n n ) \binom{3n}{n}


Abstract
Using a simple transformation, we obtain much simpler forms for some series involving binomial coefficients ( 3 ​ n n ) \binom{3n}{n} derived by Necdet Batir. New evaluations are given; and connections with Fibonacci numbers and the golden ratio are established. Finally, we derive some Fibonacci and Lucas series involving the reciprocals of ( 3 ​ n n ) \binom{3n}{n} . 2020 Mathematics Subject Classification : 40A05, 11B65, 11B39. Keywords : series; binomial coefficient; binomial sum; Fibonacci number; Lucas number.

In this section we will evaluate identities (

Let

and
L_{n}
denote the
n

X_{n}=X_{n-1}+X_{n-2},\quad n\geq 2,

but with the initial conditions
F_{0}=0
,
F_{1}=1
and
L_{0}=2
,
L_{1}=1

Extending these numbers to negative subscripts gives

F_{-j}=(-1)^{j-1}F_{j},\quad L_{-j}=(-1)^{j}L_{j}.

Throughout the paper, we denote the golden ratio
\alpha=(1+\sqrt{5})/2
and write
\beta=(1-\sqrt{5})/2
, so that
\alpha\beta=-1
and
\alpha+\beta=1
. For any integer
j

F_{j}=\frac{{\alpha^{j}-\beta^{j}}}{{\alpha-\beta}},\qquad L_{j}=\alpha^{j}+\beta^{j}.

We will often require the following identities, valid for any integer

\displaystyle\alpha^{2r}+(-1)^{r+1}

\displaystyle=\alpha^{r}F_{r}\sqrt{5},

(2.2)

\displaystyle\alpha^{2r}+(-1)^{r}

\displaystyle=\alpha^{r}L_{r},

(2.3)

\displaystyle\beta^{2r}+(-1)^{r+1}

\displaystyle=-\beta^{r}F_{r}\sqrt{5},

(2.4)

\displaystyle\beta^{2r}+(-1)^{r}

(2.5)

4
Concluding comments

In this paper we presented new closed forms for some types of infinite series involving binomial coefficients
\binom{3n}{n}
. To prove our results, we applied some routine arguments, combining Batir’s formula (
1.1
) with Binet’s formulas for Fibonacci and Lucas numbers. Using similar techniques, we can establish series evaluations involving binomial coefficients
\binom{3n}{n}
with Fibonacci and Lucas polynomials and other known number and polynomial sequences.

Let us give, for example, a generalization of Theorems
1
and
4
to the case of the Horadam sequence defined by the recurrence

W_{n}=pW_{n-1}-qW_{n-2},\quad n\geq 2,

with initial values
W_{0}=a
and
W_{1}=b
.

Let

\Delta=\sqrt{p^{2}+4q},\,\,\,\alpha_{*}=(p+\Delta)/2,\,\,\,\beta_{*}=(p-\Delta)/2,\,\,\,A=b-a\beta_{*},\,\,\,B=b-a\alpha_{*}.

The following identities hold for positive integer
r
:

\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^{k(r-1)}}{k^{2}\binom{3k}{k}}

\displaystyle\left(\frac{27ABq^{r}}{\Delta^{2}}\right)^{k}W^{-2k}_{r}

\displaystyle=6\arctan^{2}\!\bigg(\frac{\sqrt{3}\sqrt[3]{Bq^{r}}}{2\sqrt[3]{A\alpha_{*}^{2r}}+\sqrt[3]{B(-q)^{r}}}\bigg)-\frac{1}{2}\log^{2}\!\bigg(\frac{\alpha_{*}^{r}\Delta W_{r}}{\big(\sqrt[3]{A\alpha_{*}^{2r}}-\sqrt[3]{B(-q)^{r}}\big)^{3}}\bigg)

and

\displaystyle\frac{A\alpha_{*}^{2r}+B(-q)^{r}}{\sqrt[3]{AB\alpha_{*}^{2r}q^{r}}}

\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^{k(r-1)}}{k\binom{3k}{k}}\left(\frac{27ABq^{r}}{\Delta^{2}}\right)^{k}W^{-2k}_{r}

\displaystyle=2\sqrt{3}\big(\sqrt[3]{A\alpha_{*}^{2r}}-\sqrt[3]{B(-q)^{r}}\big)\arctan\!\bigg(\frac{\sqrt{3}\sqrt[3]{Bq^{r}}}{2\sqrt[3]{A\alpha_{*}^{2r}}+\sqrt[3]{B(-q)^{r}}}\bigg)

\displaystyle\quad-(-1)^{r}\big(\sqrt[3]{A\alpha_{*}^{2r}}+\sqrt[3]{B(-q)^{r}}\big)\log\!\bigg(\frac{\alpha_{*}^{r}\Delta W_{r}}{\big(\sqrt[3]{A\alpha_{*}^{2r}}-\sqrt[3]{B(-q)^{r}}\big)^{3}}\bigg).
