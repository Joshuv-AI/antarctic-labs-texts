Unveiling the a 0 ​ ( 1710 ) a_{0}(1710) nature in the process J / ψ → K ¯ 0 ​ K + ​ ρ − J/\psi\to{\bar{K}}^{0}K^{+}\rho^{-}
Yan Ding, En Wang, De-Min Li, Li-Sheng Geng, Ju-Jun Xie

Abstract
We have investigated the process J / ψ → K ¯ 0 ​ K + ​ ρ − J/\psi\to{\bar{K}}^{0}K^{+}\rho^{-} by taking into account the S S -wave K ∗ ​ K ¯ ∗ {K^{*}\bar{K}^{*}} , ρ ​ ω \rho\omega , and ρ ​ ϕ \rho\phi final-state interactions, where the scalar meson a 0 ​ ( 1710 ) a_{0}(1710) is generated. In addition, we also take into account the contributions from the scalar a 0 ( 980 ) ( → K ¯ 0 K + ) a_{0}(980)(\to\bar{K}^{0}K^{+}) and the intermediate resonances K 1 ( 1270 ) − ( → K ¯ 0 ρ − ) K_{1}(1270)^{-}(\to{\bar{K}}^{0}\rho^{-}) and K 1 ( 1270 ) 0 ( → K + ρ − ) K_{1}(1270)^{0}(\to K^{+}\rho^{-}) . Our results show that, in the K ¯ 0 ​ K + {{\bar{K}}^{0}K^{+}} invariant mass distribution, a clear peak structure around 1.8 GeV appears, which could be associated with the scalar a 0 ​ ( 1710 ) a_{0}(1710) , however, no significant structure of the a 0 ​ ( 980 ) a_{0}(980) is observed. On the other hand, one can find clear peaks of the K 1 ​ ( 1270 ) K_{1}(1270) in the K ¯ 0 ​ ρ − {\bar{K}}^{0}\rho^{-} and K + ​ ρ − K^{+}\rho^{-} invariant mass distributions. The future precise measurement of this process by the BESIII and Belle II Collaborations and the planned Super Tau-Charm Facility (STCF) in the future could shed light on the nature of a 0 ​ ( 1710 ) a_{0}(1710) .

IV
Summary

Assuming the
a_{0}(1710)
as a
K^{*}\bar{K}^{*}
molecular state, we have investigated the process
J/\psi\rightarrow\bar{K}^{0}K^{+}\rho^{-}
by taking into account the contribution from the
S
-wave
\omega\rho
,
K^{*}\bar{K}^{*}
, and
\phi\rho
interactions, as well as the contribution from the intermediate resonances
a_{0}(980)
and
K_{1}(1270)
.

We have predicted one peak structure around 1.8 GeV in the
\bar{K}^{0}K^{+}
invariant mass distribution, which could be associated with the scalar meson
a_{0}(1710)
. However, there is no significant near-threshold enhancement structure of the
a_{0}(980)
in the
K\bar{K}
invariant mass distribution. Furthermore, we have also predicted the
K^{+}\rho^{-}
and
\bar{K}^{0}\rho^{-}
invariant mass distributions of the process
J/\psi\rightarrow\bar{K}^{0}K^{+}\rho^{-}
, and find clear peaks of the resonance
K_{1}(1270)^{0,-}
. Considering the two-pole structure of the
K_{1}(1270)
, we have also calculated the results adopting parameters of the lower pole and find an enhancement structure near 1.3 GeV in the
K^{+}\rho^{-}
and
\bar{K}^{0}\rho^{-}
invariant mass distributions, which implies that future measurements of the
K^{+}\rho^{-}
and
\bar{K}^{0}\rho^{-}
invariant mass distributions could shed light on the two-pole structure of the
K_{1}(1270)

Finally, considering different weights ratio
