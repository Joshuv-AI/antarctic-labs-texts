Beauty 2019 — Conference summary


Abstract
Some highlights from the 18 th international conference on B B physics at frontier machines are presented, including first results from the full LHC Run 2 data and from early Belle II data.

1
Introduction

Beauty 2019 was a lively conference, where we saw first results from the full
LHC Run 2 data and from early Belle 2 data.
There was excitement due to new announcements, mostly in spectroscopy, and
in anticipation of updates of the now long-standing flavour anomalies.
The observation of
C\!P
violation in charm — certainly the HEP highlight of 2019 —
also triggered many discussions.
But the main initial shock was the announcement of the bankruptcy of the
local carrier Adria airways,
which forced most of the attendees to rearrange their travel plans at the last minute.

The scene was nicely set by Chris Quigg, who asked 50 questions to be answered by
experiment and theory
[
1
]
.

Spectroscopy

Who would have guessed that doubly charmed baryons would feature prominently at a Beauty physics conference?
Fifteen years after the putative observation of the
{\mathchar 28932\relax}^{+}_{{c}{c}}

[
4

It was seen in the decay modes
{{\mathchar 28932\relax}^{++}_{{c}{c}}}\!\rightarrow{{\mathchar 28931\relax}^{+}_{c}}{{K}^{-}}{{\pi}^{+}}{{\pi}^{+}}

{{\mathchar 28932\relax}^{++}_{{c}{c}}}\!\rightarrow{\mathchar 28932\relax}_{c}^{+}{{\pi}^{+}}

[

{{\mathchar 28932\relax}^{++}_{{c}{c}}}\!\rightarrow{{D}^{+}}p{{K}^{-}}{{\pi}^{+}}

[

In the meantime we know its mass (
3621.24\pm 0.65\pm 0.31\text{\,Me\kern-1.00006ptV}

However, the mass measurement poses a problem: the
{\mathchar 28932\relax}^{+}_{{c}{c}}
and
{\mathchar 28932\relax}^{++}_{{c}{c}}

cannot be isospin partners as their masses differ by 100

LHCb thus started looking for the singly charged state and did not find any excess at

However, there is a

*Padmanath:2019wid

SELEX state is a statistical fluctuation,
if there is an unusual ispospin splitting, or if the two states

Another conundrum is the
{\mathchar 28938\relax}^{0}_{c}

the FOCUS, WA89 and E687 experiments give a combined lifetime of

C\!P
violation

The most exciting
C\!P
-violation result of the year is the observation of
C\!P
violation in the charm sector.
Using the data collected so far, LHCb measures a significant difference
\Delta A_{C\!P}
between the
C\!P
asymmetries in
{{D}^{0}}\!\rightarrow{{K}^{+}}{{K}^{-}}
and
{{D}^{0}}\!\rightarrow{{\pi}^{+}}{{\pi}^{-}}

[
41
,
*B:Schubiger
]
.
This comes 35, 17 and 6 years after the first observation of
C\!P
violation in the kaon,
{B}^{0}
and
{B}^{0}_{s}

[
43
,
*Aubert:2001nu
,
*Abe:2001xe
,
*LHCb-PAPER-2013-018
]
systems, respectively.
However it is hard to tell if the measured
C\!P
asymmetry is consistent with the SM or not
[
47
]
.
The understanding of the SM contributions to this asymmetry has improved recently — also thanks to wildly varying values of
\Delta A_{C\!P}
reported in previous measurements — but the jury is still out on whether the measured value is consistent with expectations.
