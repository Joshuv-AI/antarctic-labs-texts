Abstract
. In 2015, Swisher generalized the (G.2) supercongruence of Van Hamme to the modulus p 4 p^{4} . In this paper, we first propose two q q -analogues of Swisher’s supercongruence and then a new q q -congruence with parameters is present. Furthermore, we prove a q q -congruence modulo the fourth power of a cyclotomic polynomial, which was conjectured by the authors early.

Introduction

In 1997, Van Hamme
[
23

p

\pi

\displaystyle(\text{G.2})\quad\quad

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{(p-1)/4}(8k+1)\frac{(\frac{1}{4})_{k}^{4}}{k!^{4}}\equiv p\frac{\Gamma_{p}(\frac{1}{2})\Gamma_{p}(\frac{1}{4})}{\Gamma_{p}(\frac{3}{4})}\pmod{p^{3}},\quad\quad\text{if $p\equiv 1\pmod{4}$}.

(1.1)

Here and throughout this paper,
p

\Gamma_{p}(x)
is the
p
-adic Gamma

[
17
]
.
Then the research of the Ramanujan-type congruence and supercongruence has caught a lot of authors’ attention. Later, Swisher
[
22

1.1

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{(p-1)/4}(8k+1)\frac{(\frac{1}{4})_{k}^{4}}{k!^{4}}\equiv-(-1)^{\frac{p-1}{4}}p\Gamma_{p}(\tfrac{1}{2})\Gamma_{p}(\tfrac{1}{4})^{2}\pmod{p^{4}},\quad\quad\text{if $p\equiv 1\pmod{4}$}.

(1.2)

Since
\Gamma_{p}(\frac{1}{4})\Gamma_{p}(\frac{3}{4})=-(-1)^{\frac{p-1}{4}}

Recently, the authors

(1.3)

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{M}[8k+1]_{q^{2}}[8k+1]^{2}\frac{\left(q^{2};q^{8}\right)_{k}^{4}}{\left(q^{8};q^{8}\right)_{k}^{4}}q^{-4k}\equiv-\frac{2[n]_{q^{2}}(q^{4};q^{8})_{(n-1)/4}}{(1+q^{2})(q^{8};q^{8})_{(n-1)/4}}q^{(3-n)/2}\pmod{[n]_{q^{2}}\Phi_{n}(q^{2})^{2}},

here and in what follows

or
