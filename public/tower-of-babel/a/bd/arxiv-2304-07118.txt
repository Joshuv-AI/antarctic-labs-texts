Bosonization, effective action, and R-operation in a generalized Nambu-Jona-Lasinio model
Sergii Kutnii

Abstract
Bosonization in a theory with four-fermion interaction of Nambu-Jona-Lasinio type with additional U(N) symmetry is studied. It is demonstrated that bosonization is not uniquely determined by the interaction terms due to Fierz identities. Effective action including both fermions and composite fields is constructed. R-operation renormalization scheme is developed for the effective action. Relation of bosonization to quantum BBGKY hierarchy is demonstrated and a generalization involving higher-order combinations of fields is proposed. Nambu-Jona-Lasinio model with three-fermion composite fields is studied as an example. Fierz identities for sixth-order combinations of fermions are derived.
