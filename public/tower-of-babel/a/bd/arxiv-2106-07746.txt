Cosimplicial meromorphic functions cohomology on complex manifolds
A. Zuevsky

Abstract
. Developing ideas of [ 13 ] , we introduce canonical cosimplicial cohomology of meromorphic functions for infinite-dimensional Lie algebra formal series with prescribed analytic behavior on domains of a complex manifold M M . Graded differential cohomology of a sheaf of Lie algebras 𝒢 \mathcal{G} via the cosimplicial cohomology of 𝒢 \mathcal{G} -formal series for any covering by Stein spaces on M M is computed. A relation between cosimplicial cohomology (on a special set of open domains of M M ) of formal series of an infinite-dimensional Lie algebra 𝒢 \mathcal{G} and singular cohomology of auxiliary manifold associated to a 𝒢 \mathcal{G} -module is found. Finally, multiple applications in conformal field theory, deformation theory, and in the theory of foliations are proposed. AMS Classification: 53C12, 57R20, 17B69

The main purposes of this paper are:

We also propose applications in conformal field theory, deformation theory, cohomology and characteristic

As it was demonstrated in

Inspite results in previous approaches, it is desirable to

The idea of meromorphic function cosimplician cohomology for complex manifolds was outlined in
