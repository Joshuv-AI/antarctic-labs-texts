A Shimorin-type analytic model on an annulus for left-invertible operators and applications
Paweł Pietrzycki

Abstract
. A new analytic model for left-invertible operators, which extends both Shimorin’s analytic model for left-invertible and analytic operators and Gellar’s model for bilateral weighted shift is introduced and investigated. We show that a left-invertible operator T T , which satisfies certain conditions can be modelled as a multiplication operator ℳ z \mathscr{M}_{z} on a reproducing kernel Hilbert space of vector-valued analytic functions on an annulus or a disc. A similar result for composition operators in ℓ 2 \ell^{2} -spaces is established.

The classical Wold decomposition theorem (see
[

U
is isometry on Hilbert space
\mathcal{H}

\mathcal{H}
is the direct sum of two
subspaces reducing

\mathcal{H}=\mathcal{H}_{u}\oplus\mathcal{H}_{p}

This decomposition is unique and the canonical subspaces are defined by

\displaystyle\mathcal{H}_{u}

\displaystyle=\bigcap_{n=1}^{\infty}U^{n}\mathcal{H}\quad\textrm{and}\quad\mathcal{H}_{p}=\bigoplus_{n=1}^{\infty}U^{n}E,

where
E=\mathcal{N}(U^{*})=\mathcal{H}\ominus U\mathcal{H}
. The Wold decomposition theorem and results analogous to this theorem plays an important role in many areas of operator theory, including the invariant subspace problem for Hilbert spaces of holomorphic functions.

The point is that this multiplication operator is much easier to analyse than is the case in the original setting because of the richer structure of a space of holomorphic functions. This is its great advantage and one of the reasons why it attract attention of researchers.

In

Let

3.
Analytic model

This section provides an analytic model for left-invertible operators. We show that a
left-invertible operator, which satisfies certain conditions can be modelled as a multiplication
operator
on a reproducing kernel Hilbert space of vector-valued analytic functions on an
annulus or a disc.

Let
T\in\boldsymbol{B}(\mathcal{H})
be a left-invertible operator and
E
be a subspace of
\mathcal{H}
denote by
[E]_{T^{*},T^{\prime}}
the following subspace of
\mathcal{H}

[E]_{T^{*},T^{\prime}}:=\bigvee\big(\{T^{*n}x\colon x\in E,n\in\mathbb{N}\}\cup\{{T^{\prime}}^{n}x\colon x\in E,n\in\mathbb{N}\}\big),

where
T^{\prime}
is the Cauchy dual of
T
.

To avoid the repetition, we state the following assumption which will be used
frequently in this section.

(
\clubsuit
)

The operator
T\in\boldsymbol{B}(\mathcal{H})
is left-invertible and
E
is a closed subspace of
\mathcal{H}
such that
[E]_{T^{*},T^{\prime}}=\mathcal{H}
.

Suppose (

♣

‣
3
) holds. In this case, we may construct a Hilbert
\mathscr{H}

associated with
T
, of formal Laurent series with vector coefficients. We proceed as follows. For
each
x\in\mathcal{H}
, define a formal Laurent series
U_{x}
with vector coefficients as

(3.1)

U_{x}(z)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(P_{E}T^{*n}x)\frac{1}{z^{n}}+\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(P_{E}{T^{\prime*n}}x)z^{n}.

Let
\mathscr{H}
denote the vector space of formal Laurent series with vector coefficients of the form

,
x\in\mathcal{H}
.
Consider the map
U:\mathcal{H}\rightarrow\mathscr{H}
defined by

.
As shown in Lemma
3.1

is injective. In particular, we may equip the space

\mathscr{H}
with the inner product induced from

Observe that every

where

P_{E}T^{-n}U^{*}f&\textrm{if $n\in\mathbb{Z}\setminus\mathbb{N}$.}\end{array}\right.

Lemma 3.1
.

Suppose (

♣

‣
3

,

\mathcal{N}({U)}=\{0\}

Proof.

Suppouse that

This implies

We see that the above condition is equivalent to the following one

x\>\bot\>[E]_{T^{*},T^{\prime}}(=\mathcal{H}).

This completes the proof.
