Hillclimbing Higgs inflation Preprint: CTPU-17-18 Preprint: OU-HET/935
Ryusuke Jinno, Kunio Kaneta, Kin-ya Oda

Abstract
We propose a realization of cosmic inflation with the Higgs field when the Higgs potential has degenerate vacua by employing the recently proposed idea of hillclimbing inflation. The resultant inflationary predictions exhibit a sizable deviation from those of the ordinary Higgs inflation.



I
Introduction

Inflation plays an essential role in modern cosmology
Starobinsky 1980
;
Sato 1981
;
Guth 1981
,
not only by addressing the horizon and flatness problems
Guth 1981
,
but also by giving primordial seeds for late-time structures
Mukhanov and Chibisov 1981
.
The properties of primordial perturbations have been strongly constrained by precision cosmology,
especially by the cosmic microwave background (CMB) observations
Ade et al. 2016
,
and such observations are expected to explore the inflationary physics much further in the forthcoming decade.
Nevertheless, the identity of the inflaton, the scalar field causing inflation, is still veiled in mystery.

The Higgs particle—the quantum fluctuation of the Higgs field around its potential minimum—had
long been the last missing element of the Standard Model (SM), and was finally
discovered in 2012
Aad et al. 2012
;
Chatrchyan et al. 2012
.
Ever since, the Higgs field has been the only (possibly) elementary scalar field observed by human beings.
The possibility of realizing inflation with this Higgs field has been studied extensively,
and it has turned out that the Higgs field can indeed be identified as the inflaton
with the help of a large non-minimal coupling
\xi\sim 10^{5}
to the Ricci scalar
Bezrukov and Shaposhnikov 2008
.
a
a

a

In earlier Ref.
Salopek et al. 1989
, the Higgs inflation with essentially the same parameters
\xi\sim 10^{4}

and
\lambda\sim\left(\xi/10^{5}\right)^{2}\sim 10^{-2}
has also been sketched; see also Refs.
Lucchin et al. 1986
;
Futamase and Maeda 1989
;
van der Bij 1994
;
van der Bij 1995
;
Cervantes-Cota and Dehnen 1995
.
It is noted that we may also cope with a smaller
\xi\sim 10
–
10^{2}
under the SM criticality
Hamada et al. 2014a
;
Bezrukov and Shaposhnikov 2014
;
Hamada et al. 2015a
.
See also Ref.
Ema et al. 2017
for the explosive production of longitudinal gauge bosons
and possible strong coupling issues under the presence of the large non-minimal coupling.

This scenario, now called the Higgs inflation,
has been found to fit in the most favored region by CMB observations
Ade et al. 2016
.

Regarding the mass of the Higgs particle
m_{H}=125.09\pm 0.24\,\text{GeV}

Aad et al. 2015
,
there was an interesting prediction based on the Multiple Point Principle (MPP)
Froggatt and Nielsen 1996
.
b
b

b

See Appendix D in Ref.
Hamada et al. 2015b
for a review, and Ref.
Nielsen 2012
for possible generalizations.

The MPP requires that there exist another vacuum in the Higgs potential
around the Planck scale, in addition to the electroweak one.
This means that the Higgs quartic coupling and its beta function both vanish there,

\lambda\sim\beta_{\lambda}\sim 0
.
c
c

c

See e.g. Refs.
Hamada et al. 2013
;
Buttazzo et al. 2013
for more recent analyses.
Especially, it is intriguing that the bare Higgs mass can also vanish around the Planck scale,
and hence there can be a triple coincidence
Hamada et al. 2013
.

The observed Higgs mass has turned out to be almost within 1
\sigma

from the value
m_{H}=135\pm 9\,\text{GeV}
predicted in this way
Froggatt and Nielsen 1996
.

Even though these two scenarios, the Higgs inflation and the MPP, seem attractive,
difficulties arise when it comes to combining them.
The MPP requires a degenerate vacuum around the Planck scale,
which spoils the monotonicity of the Higgs potential
which is necessary for a successful inflation
Hamada et al. 2014b
.
On this regard, an interesting proposal has recently been made by two of the present authors:
the hillclimbing inflation
Jinno and Kaneta 2017
.
This is a general framework which enables a successful inflation with an inflaton potential
with multiple vacua.
This idea opens up a new possibility of
identifying the Higgs field as the inflaton
while having degenerate vacua in the Higgs potential.
The aim of this paper is to pursue this possibility.
d
d

d

The gauge-Higgs unification models fit in the periodic potential case in the general consideration of the hillclimbing inflation
Jinno and Kaneta 2017
. Such a possibility will be pursued in a separate publication.

Then in Sec.

II
Hillclimbing inflation and its predictions

In this section, we briefly summarize
the inflaton behavior and inflationary predictions in the general hillclimbing inflation.
We start from the Jordan-frame action that has a non-minimal coupling between the inflaton and gravity:

\displaystyle S

\displaystyle=\int\text{d}^{4}x\sqrt{-g_{\text{J}}}\left[\frac{1}{2}\Omega R_{\text{J}}-\frac{1}{2}g_{\text{J}}^{\mu\nu}\partial_{\mu}\phi_{\text{J}}\partial_{\nu}\phi_{\text{J}}-V_{\text{J}}\right],

(1)

where (and throughout the paper) we work in the Planck units
M_{\text{P}}=1/\sqrt{8\pi G}=1
unless otherwise stated;
the subscript J indicates that the quantity is given in the Jordan frame;

\phi_{\text{J}}
,
R_{\text{J}}
and
V_{\text{J}}(\phi_{\text{J}})
are
the inflaton, the Ricci scalar and the inflaton potential, respectively;
and
we assume that the conformal factor
\Omega(\phi_{\text{J}})
is positive for the inflaton field values we consider.
Under the Weyl rescaling
g_{\mu\nu}=\Omega g_{\text{J}\mu\nu}
, the Ricci scalar transforms as

\displaystyle R_{\text{J}}

\displaystyle=\Omega\left[R+3\Box\ln\Omega-\frac{3}{2}\left(\partial\ln\Omega\right)^{2}\right],

(2)

and we obtain the Einstein-frame action that has a canonically normalized Ricci scalar:

\displaystyle S

\displaystyle=\int\text{d}^{4}x\sqrt{-g}\left[\frac{1}{2}R-\frac{K}{2}\left(\partial\phi_{\text{J}}\right)^{2}-V\right],

(3)

where

\displaystyle K

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\Omega}+\frac{3}{2}\left(\frac{\text{d}\ln\Omega}{\text{d}\phi_{\text{J}}}\right)^{2},

(4)

\left(\partial\phi_{\text{J}}\right)^{2}=g^{\mu\nu}\partial_{\mu}\phi_{\text{J}}\partial_{\nu}\phi_{\text{J}}
,
and the Einstein-frame potential reads
V=V_{\text{J}}/\Omega^{2}
.
If the second term dominates in Eq. (
4
),
the kinetic term in the action (
3
) reduces to
Kallosh et al. 2014a

\displaystyle-\frac{K}{2}(\partial\phi_{\text{J}})^{2}\simeq-\frac{3}{4}(\partial\ln\Omega)^{2}.

(5)

This means that
\phi\simeq\sqrt{3/2}\ln\Omega
works as a canonically normalized inflaton.
If the potential can be expanded as a series of
\Omega
,
it will become exponentially flat in terms of
\ln\Omega
as we will see below.

In Ref.
Jinno and Kaneta 2017
it has been proposed to use
\Omega\ll 1
for the inflationary dynamics,
instead of
\Omega\gg 1
.
In this class of models, called the hillclimbing inflation,
it is assumed that
V_{\text{J}}
and
\Omega
vanish at the same point
\phi_{\text{J}*}
in the field space,
and the Taylor expansion of the potential starts from

\Omega^{n}

(n\geq 2)
around
\phi_{\text{J}}=\phi_{\text{J}*}
:

\displaystyle V_{\text{J}}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=n}^{\infty}\mathcal{V}_{\text{J},k}\,\Omega^{k},

(6)

with
\mathcal{V}_{\text{J},k}
being constants.
One of the examples is the case where the potential has a quadratic (or higher) minimum
with vanishing cosmological constant at
\phi_{\text{J}}=\phi_{\text{J}*}
,
and
\Omega
vanishes linearly in
\phi_{\text{J}}-\phi_{\text{J}*}
there.
e
e

e

In fact, we have
\text{d}V_{\text{J}}/\text{d}\Omega=(\text{d}V_{\text{J}}/\text{d}\phi_{J})/(\text{d}\Omega/\text{d}\phi_{J})=0
at the minimum in this case.
However, it should be noted that this is just one of the possible realizations
and that
the expansion (
6
) is possible
even if
\text{d}\Omega/\text{d}\phi_{\text{J}}=0
at the vanishing point
\phi_{\text{J}}=\phi_{\text{J}*}
.
Also, if we allow
\Omega
to be proportional to a fractional power of
\phi_{\text{J}}-\phi_{\text{J}*}

around the vanishing point,
it is even possible to remove the assumption that
V_{\text{J}}
at
\phi_{\text{J}}=\phi_{\text{J}*}
is a local minimum.

In Sec.
III

we seek for a realization of this inflationary setup with the Higgs field.
f
f

f

In the context of MPP, the existence of another local minimum of
V_{\text{J}}
is an essential requirement,
as explained in Sec.
I
.
The vanishing
\Omega
at the coincident point can be regarded as a realization of the generalized MPP discussed in Ref.
Nielsen 2012
.

With the Jordan-frame potential (
6
),
the corresponding Einstein-frame potential
V=V_{\text{J}}/\Omega^{2}
reads

\displaystyle V

\displaystyle=V_{0}\left(1-\sum_{k=n}^{\infty}\eta_{k}\Omega^{k}\right)=V_{0}\left(1-\sum_{k=n}^{\infty}\eta_{k}e^{-k\left|\ln\Omega\right|}\right)

(7)

at
\Omega<1
, where we have written
V_{0}:=\mathcal{V}_{\text{J},2}
and
\eta_{k}:=-\mathcal{V}_{\text{J},k+2}/\mathcal{V}_{\text{J},2}
and the leading exponent
n\geq 1
dominantly determines the inflationary predictions.
g
g

g

Having
n\geq 2
means that we assume
\mathcal{V}_{\text{J},k}=0
for
k=3,\dots,n+1
.

The last expression in Eq. (
7
) tells that the potential is exponentially flat
for the canonical inflaton field.
In Sec.
III
we will see that the leading power depends on
the explicit form of the conformal factor we take.

It is remarkable that the Einstein-frame potential
V=V_{\text{J}}/\Omega^{2}
has been lifted up by the small
\Omega
and made monotonic, even around a local minimum of
V_{\text{J}}
.
As is pointed out in Ref.
Jinno and Kaneta 2017
, inflation at
\Omega\ll 1
means that
the Jordan-frame potential
V_{\text{J}}=\Omega^{2}V
increases in time,
that is, the inflaton climbs up the Jordan-frame potential hill.
This observation is crucial in making successful inflation with
inflaton potentials having multiple vacua, as stressed in that paper.
In Sec.
III
we propose taking the SM Higgs field as the inflaton.

For the inflationary predictions, this class of models show attractor behavior called
\eta
-attractor.
h
h

h

It can be shown that these models share the inflationary predictions with some branch of

\alpha
-attractor
Kallosh et al. 2013
;
Kallosh et al. 2014b

at the leading order in the
e
-folding
Jinno and Kaneta 2017
.
However, there are several reasons to distinguish
\xi
- and
\eta
-attractors from
\alpha
-attractor:
First of all, their actions generically differ even after the Weyl transformation.
Second, the reheating and preheating processes depend on
the preferred frame in which the canonically normalized matter fields are introduced;
see Ref.
Ema et al. 2017
for example.
Finally, such a distinction is important in constructing inflation models
with particle-physics motivated potentials, as stressed in Ref.
Jinno and Kaneta 2017
.

Following the standard procedure, the slow-roll parameters with the potential (
7
)
are obtained as

\displaystyle\epsilon_{V}

\displaystyle\equiv\frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{V^{\prime}}{V}\right)^{2}\simeq\frac{3}{4}\frac{1}{n^{2}N^{2}},\;\;\;\;\eta_{V}\equiv\frac{V^{\prime\prime}}{V}\simeq-\frac{1}{N},

(8)

where we used the following expression for the
e
-folding number
N
:

\displaystyle N

\displaystyle\simeq\frac{3}{2}\frac{1}{n^{2}\eta_{n}}\frac{1}{\Omega^{n}}.

(9)

The inflationary predictions at the leading order in
N
become

\displaystyle n_{s}

\displaystyle\simeq 1-\frac{2}{N},\;\;\;\;r\simeq\frac{12}{n^{2}N^{2}},

(10)

where
n_{s}
and
r
are the spectral index and the tensor-to-scalar ratio, respectively.

III
Hillclimbing Higgs inflation

Now let us take the Higgs field as the inflaton. We write its effective potential as

\displaystyle V_{\text{J}}(\phi_{\text{J}})

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{4}\lambda_{\text{eff}}\!\left(\phi_{\text{J}}\right)\phi_{\text{J}}^{4}.

(11)

Around
\phi_{\text{J}}=M\sim 10^{17\text{--}18}\,\text{GeV}
,
the effective coupling can be approximated by
Hamada et al. 2015a

\displaystyle\lambda_{\text{eff}}(\phi_{\text{J}})

\displaystyle=\lambda_{\text{min}}+\beta_{2}\left(\ln{\phi_{\text{J}}\over M}\right)^{2}+\beta_{3}\left(\ln{\phi_{\text{J}}\over M}\right)^{3}+\cdots,

(12)

where
\beta_{2}\simeq 2\times 10^{-5}=:\beta_{2}^{\text{SM}}
in the SM
Hamada et al. 2014b
.
The cubic and higher order terms are loop-suppressed,
\beta_{3},\dots\ll\beta_{2}
, and will be neglected hereafter.

In the following we set
\lambda_{\text{min}}=0
so that the potential becomes zero at
\phi_{\text{J}}=M
by assuming the MPP. In the SM, this is realized with the top quark mass
m_{t}\simeq 171.4\,\text{GeV}
for the strong coupling
\alpha_{s}\simeq 0.1185
, leading to
M\simeq 4\times 10^{18}\,\text{GeV}

Hamada et al. 2015a
.
i
i

i

The precisely-measured top mass
m_{t}^{\text{MC}}=173.1\pm 0.6
GeV
Patrignani et al. 2016

is the Monte-Carlo mass, which is just a parameter in the Monte-Carlo code.
The mass relevant to our discussion is the pole mass.
A theorists’ naive average gives a combined result
m_{t}^{\text{pole}}=173.5\pm 1.1
GeV
Patrignani et al. 2016
,
and the MPP value of the top pole mass
171.4\,\text{GeV}
is within
1.9\,\sigma
of this bound.

However, the precise values of the
\beta_{2}
and
M
that realize
\lambda_{\text{min}}=0
are altered by extra particles such as the heavy right-handed neutrinos and the Higgs-portal dark matter; see e.g. Refs.
Davoudiasl et al. 2005
;
Iso et al. 2009
;
Haba et al. 2014a
;
Hamada et al. 2014c
;
Haba et al. 2014b
;
Hamada et al. 2017
.
Therefore we take them as free parameters hereafter.

IV
Conclusion

In this paper we have proposed a realization of cosmic inflation
using the Higgs field with degenerate vacua.
This realization utilizes the recently proposed idea of hillclimbing inflation
Jinno and Kaneta 2017
,
which is a general framework to enable a successful inflation using an inflaton potential
with multiple vacua.
It has been shown that a successful inflation occurs while the inflaton is climbing up the potential hill
from the high-scale vacuum around the Planck scale to the electroweak vacuum,
and that the resulting inflationary predictions come well within the region
favored by the CMB observations, while showing a sizable deviation from those of the ordinary Higgs inflation.

Though in this paper we have considered only the case where the Higgs field has degenerate vacua,
the original proposal in Ref.
Jinno and Kaneta 2017
can work also when the Higgs potential becomes
negative at some scale. Such a study will be presented in a separate publication.
