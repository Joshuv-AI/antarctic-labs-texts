Solution estimates and stability tests for linear neutral differential equations Journal: Applied Mathematics Letters
Leonid Berezansky, Elena Braverman

Abstract
Explicit exponential stability tests are obtained for the scalar neutral differential equation x ˙ ( t ) − a ( t ) x ˙ ( g ( t ) ) = − ∑ k = 1 m b k ( t ) x ( h k ( t ) ) , \dot{x}(t)-a(t)\dot{x}(g(t))=-\sum_{k=1}^{m}b_{k}(t)x(h_{k}(t)), together with exponential estimates for its solutions. Estimates for solutions of a non-homogeneous neutral equation are also obtained, they are valid on every finite segment, thus describing both asymptotic and transient behavior. For neutral differential equations, exponential estimates are obtained here for the first time. Both the coefficients and the delays are assumed to be measurable, not necessarily continuous functions.

In recent papers
[

[

Assume that

In the present paper, we consider a generalization of (
1.1
) to the case of several delays in the non-neutral part

\dot{x}(t)-a(t)\dot{x}(g(t))=-\sum_{k=1}^{m}b_{k}(t)x(h_{k}(t)).

(1.7)

We refer the reader to the review of known stability results for neutral equations in
[
3
]

For a particular case of constant coefficients and delays sharp results are obtained in

]

The recent paper

[

The purpose of the present paper is two-fold.

1.

Investigate stability of (
1.7

Among other results, we obtain a new exponential stability test which has a very simple form and can be applied to a wide
class of neutral equations.

2.

Develop explicit exponential estimates for solutions of (
1.7

For neutral differential equations, exponential estimates are obtained here for the first time.

Note that our assumptions refer to a half-line
[t_{0},\infty)

[t_{0},t_{1}]

Only few asymptotic formulas for solutions of neutral differential equations are known, see
[

,
12

13

The paper is organized as follows. Section 2 includes some definitions and auxiliary results, among them an estimate for the fundamental function of an equation with

Section 4 presents illustrating examples and discussion.

Main Results

We start with an exponential estimate which later will be used to analyze exponential stability.

Theorem 1

Assume that there exist constants

(3.2)

\begin{array}[]{ll}|x(t)|\leq&\displaystyle M_{0}e^{-\lambda(t-t_{0})}\left[|x(t_{0})|+\frac{e^{\lambda\sigma}-1}{\lambda(1-\|a\|_{[t_{0},\infty)})}\|a\|_{[t_{0},\infty)}\|\psi\|_{[t_{0}-\sigma,t_{0}]}\right.\\

Obtain explicit estimates of solutions for nonlinear neutral differential equations.
