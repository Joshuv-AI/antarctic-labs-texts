Abstract
. In this paper we give an easy proof of the General Neron Desingularization in the frame of regular morphisms between Artinian local rings and Noetherian local rings of dimension one. Key words : Flatness, Completion, Smooth morphisms, regular morphisms. 2010 Mathematics Subject Classification: Primary 13B40, Secondary 14B25,13H05,13J15.



Introduction

A ring morphism
u:A\rightarrow A^{\prime}
has
geometrically regular fibers
if
for all prime ideals
P\in Spec\ A
and all finite field extensions
K
of the fraction field of
A/P
the ring
K\otimes_{A/P}A^{\prime}/PA^{\prime}
is regular. A
flat morphism
u
is
regular
if its fibers are geometrically regular. A regular morphism of finite type is
smooth
and a localization of a smooth algebra is
essentially smooth
.

Let
(A,\mathfrak{m})
be a Noetherian local ring of dimension
\leq 1
,
A^{\prime}
a Noetherian local ring of dimension one and
u:A\rightarrow A^{\prime}
a regular morphism. Let
B=A[Y]/I
,
Y=(Y_{1},\ldots,Y_{n})
. Then any morphism
v:B\rightarrow A^{\prime}
factors through a smooth
A
-algebra
C
, that is
v
is a composite
A
-morphism
B\rightarrow C\rightarrow A^{\prime}.
This is a particular case of the General Neron Desingularization given in
[
4
]
,
[
8
]
,
[
5
]
, with a very difficult proof. An algorithmic proof is given in
[
3
]
for the case when
A,A^{\prime}
are domains, and it is extended in
[
7
]
for arbitrary Noetherian local rings of dimension one.

Let
(A,\mathfrak{m})
be a local Artinian ring,
(A^{\prime},\mathfrak{m}^{\prime})
a Noetherian complete local ring of dimension one such that
k=A/\mathfrak{m}\cong A^{\prime}/\mathfrak{m}^{\prime}
, and
u:A\rightarrow A^{\prime}
be a regular morphism. Suppose that
k\subset A
. Then
\bar{A}^{\prime}=A^{\prime}/\mathfrak{m}A^{\prime}
is a discrete valuation ring (shortly a DVR). Choose
x\in A^{\prime}
such that its class modulo
\mathfrak{m}A^{\prime}
is a local parameter of
\bar{A}^{\prime}
, that is, it generates
\mathfrak{m}^{\prime}\bar{A}^{\prime}
.
Let
B=A[Y]/I
,
Y=(Y_{1},\ldots,Y_{n})
.

The purpose of this paper is to give an easy proof to the following theorem.

Theorem 1
.

Then any morphism
v:B\rightarrow A^{\prime}
factors through a smooth
A
-algebra
C

v
is a composite
A
-morphism
