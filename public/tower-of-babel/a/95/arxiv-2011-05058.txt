|K_{i}|=4|K_{i+1}|
and
K_{1}=K
.
Since
G
is finite there exists a positive integer
s
such that
K_{s}=Z(G)
and
|G|=4^{s}|Z(G)|=2^{2s}|Z(G)|
.
If
s=1
then
|G|=4|Z(G)|
and by Lemma
17
,
G
is a
{\rm CA}
-group, which is not the case. Hence
s>1
.
∎
