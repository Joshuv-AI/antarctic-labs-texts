High-velocity tails of the inelastic and the multi-species mixture Boltzmann equations
Gayoung An and Donghyun Lee

Abstract
. We study high-velocity tails of some homogeneous Boltzmann equations on v ∈ ℝ v d v\in\mathbb{R}_{v}^{d} . First, we consider spatially homogeneous Inelastic Boltzmann equation with noncutoff collision kernel, in the case of moderately soft potentials. We also study spatially homogeneous mixture Boltzmann equations : for both noncutoff collision kernel with moderately soft potentials and cutoff collision kernel with hard potentials. 1.
Introduction

The classical Boltzmann equation describes the statistical behavior of a rarefied collisional gas, consisting of a very large number of identical
particles and perfect elastic binary collisions. The function
f(t,x,v)
is a density of particles at time
t
, at point
x
, having speed
v
. The equation is a partial differential equation of
f(t,x,v)
with nonlinear quadratic term
Q(f,f)(t,x,v).
In this paper, we study two different types of the Boltzmann equation : Inelastic Boltzmann equation (no energy conservation) and the multi-species Mixture Boltzmann.

(
\mathbf{I}
)
The Inelastic(mono-species) Boltzmann equation model
:
Let
v
and
v_{*}
be the velocities of two particles before the collision, and
v^{\prime}
and
v^{\prime}_{*}
be the velocities after the collision. The particles hit each other with the unit vector
{n}\in\mathbb{S}^{d-1}_{+}
and assume that

\displaystyle\langle v^{\prime}-v_{*}^{\prime},n\rangle

\displaystyle=-\alpha\langle v-v_{*},n\rangle,

\displaystyle(v^{\prime}-v^{\prime}_{*})-\langle v^{\prime}-v_{*}^{\prime},n\rangle{n}

\displaystyle=(v-v_{*})-\langle v-v_{*},n\rangle{n},

(1.1)

where
\alpha
is the coefficient of normal restitution,
0<\alpha<1
. In particular, we denote

\beta=\frac{1+\alpha}{2},\quad\frac{1}{2}<\beta<1.

(1.2)

Postcollisional velocities
v^{\prime},v^{\prime}_{*}
can be expressed as

\begin{split}v^{\prime}&=v-\beta\langle v-v_{*},n\rangle{n}=\frac{v+v_{*}}{2}+\frac{1-\beta}{2}(v-v_{*})+\frac{\beta}{2}\;|v-v_{*}|{\sigma},\\
v_{*}^{\prime}&=v_{*}+\beta\langle v-v_{*},n\rangle{n}=\frac{v+v_{*}}{2}-\frac{1-\beta}{2}(v-v_{*})-\frac{\beta}{2}\;|v-v_{*}|{\sigma}\end{split}

(1.3)

for the angular parameter
{n}\in\mathbb{S}^{d-1}_{+},\;{\sigma}\in\mathbb{S}^{d-1}.

It is important to note that unlike to elastic collision, there is no symmetry between
0<\frac{v-v_{*}}{|v-v_{*}|}\cdot\sigma<\frac{\pi}{2}
and
\frac{\pi}{2}<\frac{v-v_{*}}{|v-v_{*}|}\cdot\sigma<\pi
. i.e., taking minus on
{\sigma}
does not reverse
v^{\prime}
and
v_{*}^{\prime}
(
v^{\prime}\nleftrightarrow v_{*}^{\prime}
when
{\sigma}\leftrightarrow-{\sigma}
). Also, the collision is not time-reversible and
|v^{\prime}-v^{\prime}_{*}|\neq|v-v_{*}|
.

The spatially homogeneous Inelastic Boltzmann equation is described by

\displaystyle\partial_{t}f(t,v)=Q(f,f)(t,v),

(1.4)

where
v\in\mathbb{R}^{d},\;t\in\mathbb{R}_{+}.
The function
f(t,v)
is a density of particles of velocity
v
and time
t
. The Inelastic collision operator
Q(f,f)(t,v)
in (
1.4
) satisfies mass and momentum conservation, but not for energy.
We write

\displaystyle\int f(t,v)dv=\int f_{0}(v)dv\doteq M_{0},\quad\int f(t,v)|v|^{2}dv\leq\int f_{0}(v)|v|^{2}dv\doteq E_{0}

(1.5)

for some finite initial mass and energy
M_{0},E_{0}
.

Next, we introduce the weak form of the Inelastic Boltzmann collision operator
Q(f,f)(t,v)
,

\displaystyle\int Q(f,g)(v)\phi(v)\;dv

\displaystyle=\int B(|v-v_{*}|,\cos\theta)f(v_{*})g(v)(\phi(v^{\prime})-\phi(v))\>d\sigma dv_{*}dv,

\displaystyle\text{where }\quad\cos\theta=\langle\frac{v-v_{*}}{|v-v_{*}|},\sigma\rangle,\quad\theta\in[0,\pi].

(1.6)

Here, we take a suitable regular test function
\phi(v)
(which has proper decaying property at infinity) and
v^{\prime}
is given by (
1.3
). We define
B(|v-v_{*}|,\cos\theta)
as

B(|v-v_{*}|,\cos\theta)=|v-v_{*}|^{\gamma}b(\cos\theta),\quad b(\cos\theta)\approx_{\theta\sim 0}|\theta|^{-(d-1)-2s}\tilde{b}(\cos\theta),\quad\int\tilde{b}(\cos\theta)\;d\sigma<+\infty

(1.7)

with
\gamma>-d,

s\in(0,1).
The
\tilde{b}(\cos\theta)
is smooth on
[0,\pi]
and positive on
[0,\pi).

When
d\geq 2
, the following condition

\displaystyle\gamma<0,\quad\gamma+2s\in[0,2]\quad\text{for}\quad d\geq 2

(1.8)

is called the moderately soft potentials in
[
9
,
16
]
.

(
\mathbf{M}
)
The (elastic) multi-species Mixture Boltzmann equation model
: There are
N
different types particles in the system and each particle has mass
m_{j}
for
j=1,2\dots N.
Suppose that a particle with mass
m_{i}
and velocity
v
collides with another particle with mass
m_{j}
and velocity
v_{*}
. We use
v^{\prime}
and
v_{*}^{\prime}
to denote postcollisinoal velocities. The laws of conservation of momentum and energy can be stated as follows :

\displaystyle\begin{split}m_{i}v+m_{j}v_{*}&=m_{i}v^{\prime}+m_{j}v_{*}^{\prime}\\
m_{i}|v|^{2}+m_{j}|v_{*}|^{2}&=m_{i}|v^{\prime}|^{2}+m_{j}|v_{*}^{\prime}|^{2}.\end{split}

(1.9)

Let
{n}
denote collision angle. The postcollisional velocities
v^{\prime},v^{\prime}_{*}
can be written by

\displaystyle\begin{split}v^{\prime}&=v-\frac{2m_{j}}{m_{i}+m_{j}}\langle v-v_{*},n\rangle{n}=\frac{m_{i}}{m_{i}+m_{j}}v+\frac{m_{j}}{m_{i}+m_{j}}v_{*}+\frac{m_{j}}{m_{i}+m_{j}}|v-v_{*}|\sigma,\\
v_{*}^{\prime}&=v_{*}+\frac{2m_{i}}{m_{i}+m_{j}}\langle v-v_{*},n\rangle{n}=\frac{m_{i}}{m_{i}+m_{j}}v+\frac{m_{j}}{m_{i}+m_{j}}v_{*}-\frac{m_{i}}{m_{i}+m_{j}}|v-v_{*}|\sigma\end{split}

(1.10)

for angular parameter
{n}\in\mathbb{S}^{d-1}_{+},\;{\sigma}\in\mathbb{S}^{d-1}.
The collision is time-reversible and
|v^{\prime}-v^{\prime}_{*}|=|v-v_{*}|
. However, the collision is not symmetric when
0<\frac{v-v_{*}}{|v-v_{*}|}\cdot\sigma<\frac{\pi}{2}
and
\frac{\pi}{2}<\frac{v-v_{*}}{|v-v_{*}|}\cdot\sigma<\pi
(
v^{\prime}\nleftrightarrow v_{*}^{\prime}
when
{\sigma}\leftrightarrow-{\sigma}
) unlike the elastic collision between two identical particles.

The spatially homogeneous (elastic) Mixture Boltzmann equation is described by

\displaystyle\partial_{t}f_{i}(t,v)=\sum_{j=1}^{N}Q_{ji}(f_{j},f_{i})(t,v),

v

If

is standard Boltzmann collision operator. The Mixture collision operator in (
1.11
) satisfies mass, momentum, and energy conservations. Summing initial values of mass, energy, and entropy of density functions for
1\leq i\leq N
, we define

\displaystyle\sum_{j=1}^{N}\int f_{j}(0,v)dv\doteq M_{0},\quad\sum_{j=1}^{N}\int f_{j}(0,v)|v|^{2}dv\doteq E_{0}\quad\sum_{j=1}^{N}\int f_{j}(0,v)\log f_{j}(0,v)dv\doteq H_{0}

(1.12)

for some finite values
M_{0}
,
E_{0}
, and
H_{0}
.

(i)
With noncutoff collision kernel, the Mixture collision operator

is written by

\displaystyle Q_{ji}(f_{j},f_{i})(t,v)

\displaystyle=\int B_{ji}(|v-v_{*}|,\cos\theta)(f_{j}(v_{*}^{\prime})f_{i}(v^{\prime})-f_{j}(v_{*})f_{i}(v))\;d\sigma dv_{*},

\displaystyle\text{where}\quad\cos\theta=\langle\frac{v-v_{*}}{|v-v_{*}|},\sigma\rangle,\quad\theta\in[0,\pi].

(1.13)

Here,
v^{\prime}
and
v_{*}^{\prime}
are given by (

).
We define
B_{ji}(|v-v_{*}|,\cos\theta)
as

B_{ji}(|v-v_{*}|,\cos\theta)=|v-v_{*}|^{\gamma}b_{ji}(\cos\theta),\quad b_{ji}(\cos\theta)\approx_{\theta\sim 0}|\theta|^{-(d-1)-2s}\tilde{b}_{ji}(\cos\theta),\quad\int\tilde{b}_{ji}(\cos\theta)\;d\sigma<+\infty

(1.14)

with
\gamma>-d,\;s\in(0,1).
The

is smooth on

The values of

(ii)
With cutoff collision kernel, for

\displaystyle Q_{ji}(f_{j},f_{i})(t,v)

\displaystyle=\int B_{ji}(|v-v_{*}|,\theta)(f_{j}(v_{*}^{\prime})f_{i}(v^{\prime})-f_{j}(v_{*})f_{i}(v))\;dndv_{*},

\displaystyle\text{where}\quad\cos\theta=\langle\frac{v-v_{*}}{|v-v_{*}|},n\rangle,\quad\theta\in[0,\frac{\pi}{2}].

Here,

The Mixture collision operator

\displaystyle\begin{split}Q_{ji}(f_{j},f_{i})(t,v)&=\int B_{ji}(|v-v_{*}|,\cos\theta)(f_{j}(v_{*}^{\prime})f_{i}(v^{\prime})-f_{j}(v_{*})f_{i}(v))\;d\sigma dv_{*},\\

1.10
