Homological propeties of Bimeasure algebras and their BSE properties
Ali Rejali, Maryam Aghakoochaki

Abstract
Let G G and H H be locally compact groups. B ​ M ​ ( G , H ) BM(G,H) denoted the Banach algebra of bounded bilinear forms on C 0 ​ ( G ) × C 0 ​ ( H ) C_{0}(G)\times C_{0}(H) .In this paper, the homological properties of Bimeasure algebras are investigated. It is found and approved that the Bimeasure algebras B ​ M ​ ( G , H ) BM(G,H) is amenable if and only if G G and H H are discrete. The correlation between the weak amenability of B ​ M ​ ( G , H ) BM(G,H) and M ⁡ ( G × H ) M(G\times H) is assessed. It is found and approved that the biprojectivity of the bimeasure algebra B ​ M ​ ( G , H ) BM(G,H) is equivalent to the finiteness of G G and H H . Furthermore, we show that the bimeasure group algebra B ​ M a ​ ( G , H ) BM_{a}(G,H) is a BSE algebra. It will be concluded that B ​ M ​ ( G , H ) BM(G,H) is a BSE- algebra if and only if G G and H H are discrete groups. Keywords: Amenability, Banach algebra, BSE algebra, Bimeasures algebra, weak amenability
