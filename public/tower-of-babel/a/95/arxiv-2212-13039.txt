Elliptic flow of strange and multi-strange hadrons in isobar collisions at s NN \sqrt{s_{\mathrm{NN}}} = 200 GeV at RHIC
\firstname Priyanshi \lastname Sinha \fnsep (for the STAR Collaboration)

Abstract
We report the elliptic flow ( v 2 v_{2} ) of K s 0 K_{s}^{0} , Λ \Lambda , Λ ¯ \bar{\Lambda} , ϕ \phi , Ξ − \Xi^{-} , and Ξ ¯ + \overline{\Xi}^{+} at mid-rapidity for Ru+Ru and Zr+Zr collisions at s NN \sqrt{s_{\mathrm{NN}}} = 200 GeV. The transverse momentum ( p T p_{T} ) and centrality dependence of v 2 v_{2} have been studied. The number of constituent quark (NCQ) scaling for all the (multi-)strange hadrons has also been tested. The dependence in the ratio of average v 2 v_{2} ( ⟨ \langle v 2 v_{2} ⟩ \rangle ) on centrality shows a deviation from unity indicating a difference in nuclear structure and deformation between the isobars. The results show a systematic size dependence when compared to Cu+Cu, Au+Au, and U+U collisions at similar beam energies.

,
\Lambda
,

,

,
\Xi^{-}
, and
