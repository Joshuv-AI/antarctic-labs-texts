1.
Introduction

Let
X
be a smooth projective variety of Kodaira dimension zero and
f:X\to Y
its Albanese map. A fundamental result about
f
due to Kawamata
[
Kaw81
, Theorem 1]
is that
f
is an algebraic fiber space. We denote by
F
the general fiber of
f
. A crucial fact about
F
, proved by Cao-Păun
[
CP17
]
, is that
F
has Kodaira dimension zero. Let
b:=\min\{k\in\mathbb{N}\mid|kK_{F}|\neq\emptyset\}
, and
\tilde{F}
a resolution of the cover branched over the unique divisor in
|bK_{F}|
. We set
\beta_{\tilde{F}}:=\dim H^{\dim{\tilde{F}}}_{prim}({\tilde{F}},\mathbb{C})
as the primitive middle Betti number of
\tilde{F}
, and
N(\beta_{\tilde{F}}):=lcm\{k\in\mathbb{N}\mid\varphi(k)\leq\beta_{\tilde{F}}\}
, where
\varphi
is Euler’s totient function. The following is our main result.

Theorem 1.1
.

The sheaf
f_{*}\omega^{N(\beta_{\tilde{F}})b}_{X}\cong O_{Y}
, and the linear system
|N(\beta_{\tilde{F}})bK_{X}|
is nonempty.

Proof.

It follows from Theorem
1.2
, Lemma
2.1
, and Theorem
2.2
.
∎

The following result due to Hacon-Popa-Schnell is crucial to our proof.

Theorem 1.2
.

[
HPS18
, Corollary 4.3]

The sheaf
f_{*}\omega^{mb}_{X}
is a numerically trivial line bundle for all
m\geq 0
.

Example 1.3
.

Let
X
be a smooth projective variety of Kodaira dimension zero. Set
q(X):=h^{1}(O_{X})
. If
q(X)=\dim X-1
, then
|12K_{X}|\neq\emptyset
. If
q(X)=\dim X-2
, then
|NK_{X}|\neq\emptyset
, where
N=lcm\{k\in\mathbb{N}\mid\varphi(k)\leq 21\}
.

Remark 1.4
.

Note that the constant
m
appearing in work on the effectivity of Iitaka fiber space by Birkar-Zhang
[
BZ16
]
also depends on
\beta_{\tilde{F}}
and
b
, where
F
is the general fiber of the Iitaka fiber space, but in our setting,
F
is the general fiber of the Albanese map.

2.
Parabolic fiber space

We say a fiber space
f:X\to Y
is parabolic if its general fiber has Kodaira dimension zero. Given a parabolic fiber space, we shall denote by
F
its general fiber, by
b
,
\tilde{F}
and
N(\beta_{\tilde{F}})
similarly as in the Introduction. We follow the notations of work on canonical bundle formula by Fujino and Mori
[
Mor87
, Section 5]
,
[
FM00
]
.

Lemma 2.1
.

Let
f:X\to Y
be a parabolic fiber space. If the line bundle
(f_{*}\omega^{mb}_{X/Y})^{\vee}
(where
∨
denotes the reflexive hull) is numerically trivial for all
m\geq 0
, then the sheaf
f_{*}\omega^{b}_{X/Y}
is a line bundle, the
\mathbb{Q}
-divisors
L_{X/Y}
,
L^{ss}_{X/Y}
are integral and
O_{Y}(L_{X/Y})\cong O_{Y}(L^{ss}_{X/Y})\cong f_{*}\omega^{b}_{X/Y}
.

Proof.

For all
m\geq 0
, since
c_{1}(f_{*}\omega^{mb}_{X/Y})=0
, the canonical singular Hermitian metric on
f_{*}\omega^{mb}_{X/Y}
is smooth and flat, and
f_{*}\omega^{mb}_{X/Y}
is a line bundle
[
HPS18
, 4.6]
. By considering the nonzero map
(f_{*}\omega^{b}_{X/Y})^{m}\to f_{*}\omega^{mb}_{X/Y}
, one has
(f_{*}\omega^{b}_{X/Y})^{m}\otimes O_{Y}(E)=f_{*}\omega^{mb}_{X/Y}
for some effective divisor
E
. Since
(f_{*}\omega^{b}_{X/Y})^{m}
and
f_{*}\omega^{mb}_{X/Y}
are numerically trivial,
E
must be zero. Hence we have

f_{*}\omega^{mb}_{X/Y}=(f_{*}\omega^{b}_{X/Y})^{m}

for all
m\geq 0
. Therefore, we have
O_{Y}(L_{X/Y})=f_{*}\omega^{b}_{X/Y}

[
FM00
, Proposition 2.2]
. The canonical bundle formula of
f
has the form

bK_{X/Y}=f^{*}L_{X/Y}+B,

where
B
is an effective divisor satisfying
f_{*}O_{X}(mB)=O_{Y}
for all
m\geq 0
. Define
t_{P}:=\max\{t\in\mathbb{Q}\mid(X,-\frac{1}{b}B+tf^{*}P)\text{ is sub log canonical near the generic point of P}\}
, where
P
is a prime divisor on
Y
, and
L^{ss}_{X/Y}:=L_{X/Y}-b\sum_{P\subset Y}(1-t_{P})P

[
FM00
, Definition 4.3]
. Since
L^{ss}_{X/Y}
is pseudo effective, one has
t_{P}=1
for all
P
by
L_{X/Y}\equiv 0
and
[
BDPP13
]
.
∎

Theorem 2.2
.

Let
f:X\to Y
be a parabolic fiber space. If the
\mathbb{Q}
-divisor
L^{ss}_{X/Y}
is numerically trivial, then the integral divisor
N(\beta_{\tilde{F}})L^{ss}_{X/Y}
is linearly equivalent to zero.

Example 2.3
.

Let
f:X\to Y
be a minimal elliptic surface, then
O_{Y}(12L^{ss}_{X/Y})\cong j^{*}O_{\mathbb{P}}(1)
, where

is the

L^{ss}_{X/Y}\equiv 0

is constant, hence
O_{Y}(12L^{ss}_{X/Y})\cong O_{Y}
.
