Deformed Newton’s ( s , t ) (s,t) -Binomial Series and Generating Functions of Generalized Central Binomial Coefficients and Generalized Catalan Numbers
Ronald Orozco López

Abstract
We define the deformed ( s , t ) (s,t) -binomial formula and the deformed Newton ( s , t ) (s,t) -binomial series, and we will use it to establish the generating functions of the generalized central binomial coefficients and the generalized Catalan numbers.

1
Introduction

The Catalan numbers are defined by

C_{n}=\frac{1}{n+1}\binom{2n}{n}

for
n\geq 0
. It is a known fact that the generating function of Catalan’s numbers is

C(x)=\frac{1-\sqrt{1-4x}}{2x}.

(1)

The generalized Catalan numbers
[
7
,
14
,
2
]
are defined by

C_{\mathopen{{\mathchoice{\hbox{$\displaystyle\left\{\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\textstyle\left\{\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\scriptstyle\left\{\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\scriptscriptstyle\left\{\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}}}{n}\mathclose{{\mathchoice{\hbox{$\displaystyle\left\}\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\textstyle\left\}\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\scriptstyle\left\}\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\scriptscriptstyle\left\}\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}}}}=\frac{1}{\mathopen{{\mathchoice{\hbox{$\displaystyle\left\{\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\textstyle\left\{\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\scriptstyle\left\{\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\scriptscriptstyle\left\{\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}}}{n+1}\mathclose{{\mathchoice{\hbox{$\displaystyle\left\}\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\textstyle\left\}\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\scriptstyle\left\}\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}{\hbox{$\scriptscriptstyle\left\}\vbox to0.0pt{}\right.$}}}}_{s,t}}\genfrac{}{}{0.0pt}{}{2n}{n}_{s,t}.

(2)

The generalized Catalan numbers are polynomials in
s,t
with nonnegative integral coefficients
[
7
]
, and its combinatorial interpretation is given in
[
2
]
. However, a generating function, analogous to Eq.(
1
), for the polynomials
C_{\{n\}}
is not known. This is the main goal of this paper. Therefore all the results are original and useful.

In this paper, we introduce the deformed
(s,t)
-analog of the binomial theorem

(x+y)^{n}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}x^{n-k}y^{k}

as the equation given by

(x\oplus_{u,v}y)^{(n)}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\genfrac{}{}{0.0pt}{}{n}{k}_{s,t}u^{\binom{n-k}{2}}v^{\binom{k}{2}}x^{n-k}y^{k}.

(3)

When make
s=u=q
,
t=v=1
,
a=t
and
x=1
,
y=t
in Eq.(
3
), we obtain the
q
-theorem binomial

\prod_{k=0}^{n-1}(1+q^{k}t)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}q^{k(k-1)/2}\binom{n}{k}_{q}t^{k}

where
\binom{n}{k}_{q}
are the Gaussian binomial coefficients
[
4
,
5
]
.

On the other hand, if in Eq.(
3
) we extend the defining range of
n
to complex numbers, then we obtain the
(u,v)
-deformed Newton’s
(s,t)
-binomial series

(x\oplus_{u,v}y)^{(\alpha)}=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\genfrac{}{}{0.0pt}{}{\alpha}{k}_{s,t}u^{\binom{\alpha-k}{2}}v^{\binom{k}{2}}x^{\alpha-k}y^{k}.

(4)

This article is divided as follows. Section
2
addresses basic and necessary results for developing this article. Section 3 introduces the deformed Newton
(s,t)
-binomial series and establishes its algebraic properties. In Section 4, we express the fibonomial coefficients
\genfrac{}{}{0.0pt}{}{1/2}{n}
and
\genfrac{}{}{0.0pt}{}{-1/2}{n}
in term of central
(s,t)
-binomial coefficients. Finally, we conclude our paper by focusing on finding the generating functions of the generalized binomial coefficients introduced above.

Generalized Fibonacci polynomials
