Coincidence of two Swan conductors of abelian characters
Kazuya Kato, Takeshi Saito

Abstract
There are two ways to define the Swan conductor of an abelian character of the absolute Galois group of a complete discrete valuation field. We prove that these two Swan conductors coincide.



Keywords.
Local field, ramification groups, Swan conductor

2010 Mathematics Subject Classification.
11S15; 14B25

[Français]

Titre. Coïncidence de deux conducteurs de Swan des caractères abéliens

Résumé.
Il y a deux façons de définir le conducteur de Swan d’un caractère abélien du groupe de Galois absolu d’un corps de valuation discrète complet. Nous montrons que ces deux conducteurs de Swan coïncident.

1.0.

1.1.

We briefly review the two Swan conductors, which we denote in this paper by
{\rm Sw}(\chi)
and
{\rm Sw}^{{\rm ab}}(\chi)

{\rm Sw}(\chi)
is defined as follows. There is a decreasing filtration

\chi({\rm Gal}(\bar{K}/K)_{\log}^{s})=\{1\}
for all

[

and Section

{\rm Sw}^{{\rm ab}}(\chi)

is the Brauer group of

[
13

{\rm Sw}^{{\rm ab}}(\chi)
is defined to be the smallest integer

such that
\{\chi,1+{\mathfrak m}_{K}^{n}{\mathfrak m}_{K^{\prime}}\}=0
in
{\rm Br}(K^{\prime})
for any extension
K\to K^{\prime}
of complete discrete valuation fields (such

exists; here
{\mathfrak m}_{*}
denote the maximal ideals). See
[
7
]

{\rm Sw}(\chi)={\rm Sw}^{{\rm ab}}(\chi)

We may assume that
L

We may further assume that
the residue field
F

K

by a standard limit argument.

By Theorem
3
