TIT/HEP-700 April 2024 Brane expansions for anti-symmetric line operator index
Yosuke Imamura, Masato Inoue

Abstract
Based on the D5-brane realization of Wilson line operators in anti-symmetric representations, we propose brane expansion formulas for I N , k I_{N,k} , the Schur index of 𝒩 = 4 {\cal N}=4 U ⁡ ( N ) U(N) SYM decorated by line operators in the anti-symmetric representation of rank k k . For the large N N index I ∞ , k I_{\infty,k} we propose a double-sum expansion, and for finite N N index I N , k I_{N,k} we propose a quadruple-sum expansion. Objects causing finite k k and finite N N corrections are disk D3-branes ending on the D5-brane.

Introduction

Wilson lines are the most basic
and important observables in gauge theories.
They serve as order parameters for detecting phase transitions.
They are also useful in gaining deeper insights into
phenomena such as dualities.
In this work, we study
Wilson line operators of
{\cal N}=4

U(N)
SYM
within the framework of the AdS/CFT correspondence

[
1
,
2
,
3
]
.

Wilson line operators are labeled by representations of the gauge group.
We consider
U(N)
gauge theories,
and irreducible representations are specified by
partitions
\mu
(
\ell(\mu)\leq N
), which are depicted as
Young diagrams.
The number of boxes
k=|\mu|
in
\mu
is referred to as the rank of the representation.

Different holographic realizations exist for Wilson lines.
Wilson line operators in the fundamental representation (
\mu=\{1\}
)
are realized by fundamental strings in the AdS space

[
4
,
5
]
.
If the rank of the representation is of order
N
,
the coincident strings are more naturally described by D-branes.
A Wilson line in the symmetric representation with
\mu=\{k\}

is related to a tubular D3-brane with a cross section
S^{2}\subset AdS_{5}

[
6
]
(See also a discussion in Section
4
).
For the anti-symmetric representation with
\mu=\{1^{k}\}

the corresponding object is a tubular D5-brane with a cross section
S^{4}\subset S^{5}

[
7
]

In both cases, the charge of
k
fundamental strings is carried by the
electric flux on the D-brane.
If the rank is of order
N^{2}

the strings or D-branes deform
the background spacetime significantly, and
the line operators are naturally described by
bubbling geometries
[
8
]
.

Among these realizations of line operators,

\displaystyle\frac{I_{N,k}}{I_{\rm sugra}I_{\rm D5}}=\sum_{m_{x},m_{y},m^{\prime}_{x},m^{\prime}_{y}=0}^{\infty}x^{km_{x}+k^{\prime}m^{\prime}_{x}}y^{km_{y}+k^{\prime}m^{\prime}_{y}}{\cal F}_{m_{x},m_{y},m^{\prime}_{x},m^{\prime}_{y}},

(29)

where
k^{\prime}=N-k

Functions

are not all independent but satisfy

\displaystyle{\cal F}_{m_{x},m_{y},m^{\prime}_{x},m^{\prime}_{y}}={\cal F}_{m^{\prime}_{x},m^{\prime}_{y},m_{x},m_{y}}={\cal F}_{m_{y},m_{x},m^{\prime}_{y},m^{\prime}_{x}}|_{x\leftrightarrow y}

(30)

and by definition

\displaystyle{\cal F}_{m_{x},m_{y},0,0}={\cal F}_{m_{x},m_{y}}.

(31)

We have not yet fully understood how to calculate the
functions
{\cal F}_{m_{x},m_{y}.m^{\prime}_{x},m^{\prime}_{y}}

for general wrapping numbers.
In this subsection, we will calculate the
t
-series expansion of

I_{N,k}
on the gauge theory side for different values of
N
and
k
, and
we extract functions
{\cal F}_{m_{x},m_{y},m^{\prime}_{x},m^{\prime}_{y}}
for small wrapping numbers
by assuming the expansion (
29
).

Let

be the total wrapping number;
m_{\rm tot}=m_{x}+m_{y}+m^{\prime}_{x}+m^{\prime}_{y}
.
There are four functions for

For

The first two are the same as the functions given in the previous section

\displaystyle{\cal F}_{2,0,0,0}

\displaystyle={\cal F}_{2,0}=\sigma_{x}\Pi_{2},

\displaystyle{\cal F}_{1,1,0,0}

\displaystyle={\cal F}_{1,1}=0,

(33)

and the other two extracted from the comparison to

are

\displaystyle{\cal F}_{1,0,1,0}

\displaystyle=\frac{x^{2}(1+\frac{y}{x})}{(1-\frac{y}{x})^{2}}+\frac{x^{3}(2-2\frac{y}{x}+2\frac{y^{2}}{x^{2}}+2\frac{y^{3}}{x^{3}})}{(1-\frac{y}{x})^{2}}

\displaystyle\quad+\frac{x^{4}(3-4\frac{y}{x}+4\frac{y^{2}}{x^{2}}-6\frac{y^{3}}{x^{3}}+6\frac{y^{4}}{x^{4}}+3\frac{y^{5}}{x^{5}})}{(1-\frac{y}{x})^{2}}+{\cal O}(t^{5}),

\displaystyle\quad+\frac{2(\frac{x^{5}}{y}+x^{4}-x^{3}y+x^{2}y^{2}-xy^{3}+y^{4}+\frac{y^{5}}{x})}{(1-\frac{y}{x})(1-\frac{x}{y})}+{\cal O}(t^{5}).

{\cal D}_{x}
and
{\cal D}^{\prime}_{x}

Indeed, we can confirm that

\displaystyle I_{\rm 3d}=\int\frac{dz}{2\pi iz}\mathop{\rm Pexp}\nolimits\left[i_{\rm 3d}\left(z+\frac{1}{z}\right)\right],\quad i_{\rm 3d}=\frac{y^{\frac{1}{2}}(1-x)}{1-q}.

is the index of the theory realized on

Two disks
{\cal D}_{x}

{\cal D}^{\prime}_{y}

3.2
Reduced expansion

We can reduce the quadruple-sum expansion (
29
) to
the double-sum expansion

\displaystyle\frac{I_{N,k}}{I_{\rm sugra}I_{\rm D5}}=\sum_{m,m^{\prime}=0}^{\infty}x^{km+k^{\prime}m^{\prime}}{\cal F}_{m,0,m^{\prime},0},

(41)

by treating the index as a
y
-series.
In this expansion only the
R_{x}
-fixed locus
{\cal C}_{x}

contributes to the index,
and the summation is taken over the two wrapping numbers

m
and
m^{\prime}
asscoiated with
{\cal D}_{x}
and
{\cal D}^{\prime}_{x}
,
respectively.

{\cal F}_{m,0,m^{\prime},0}
is the index of the theory on
{\cal C}_{x}

divided by the D5-brane to
{\cal D}_{x}
and
{\cal D}^{\prime}_{x}
.
We take the S-duality frame in which the tubuler D5-brane
becomes an NS5-brane.
Then, the gauge theory on
{\cal C}_{x}

is the quiver gauge theory with gauge group
U(m)\times U(m^{\prime})
.
The vector multiplets live in two disks
{\cal D}_{x}
and
{\cal D}^{\prime}_{x}
,
and
the bi-fundamental hypermultiplet
lives on the wall dividing
{\cal C}_{x}
into the two parts.
The functions
{\cal F}_{m,0,m^{\prime},0}
are given by the localization formula

\displaystyle{\cal F}_{m,0,m^{\prime},0}

\displaystyle=\sigma_{x}\Bigg[\int_{U(m)}\hskip-13.00005ptdU\int_{U(m^{\prime})}\hskip-13.00005ptdU^{\prime}\mathop{\rm Pexp}\nolimits\bigg(i_{\rm half}\chi_{\rm adj}^{U(m)}(U)

\displaystyle\quad\quad+i_{\rm half}\chi_{\rm adj}^{U(m^{\prime})}(U^{\prime})+i_{\rm 3d}\chi^{U(m)\times U(m^{\prime})}_{\rm bifund}(U,U^{\prime})\bigg)\Bigg],

(42)

where the bi-fundamental character is defined by

\displaystyle\chi^{U(m)\times U(m^{\prime})}_{\rm bifund}(U,U^{\prime})=\mathop{\rm tr}\nolimits(U)\mathop{\rm tr}\nolimits(U^{\prime-1})+\mathop{\rm tr}\nolimits(U^{-1})\mathop{\rm tr}\nolimits(U^{\prime}).

(43)

The first few terms in the
y
-series expansions of the functions for small
m_{\rm tot}
are

\displaystyle{\cal F}_{1,0,1,0}

\displaystyle=\frac{x^{2}}{{(1-x)}^{2}}+\frac{x(3-x)}{1-x}y+\frac{5-x+x^{2}-x^{3}}{1-x}y^{2}+\mathcal{O}(y^{3}),

\displaystyle{\cal F}_{2,0,1,0}

\displaystyle=\frac{-x^{4}}{(1-x)^{2}(1-x^{2})}+\frac{x^{2}(-2-2x+x^{2})}{(1-x)(1-x^{2})}y

\displaystyle\quad+\frac{-2-2x+2x^{2}-2x^{3}+x^{4}}{(1-x)^{2}}y^{2}+\mathcal{O}(y^{3}),

\displaystyle{\cal F}_{3,0,1,0}

\displaystyle=\frac{x^{7}}{(1-x)^{2}(1-x^{2})(1-x^{3})}+\frac{x^{4}(2+x+2x^{2}-x^{3})}{(1-x)(1-x^{2})(1-x^{3})}y

\displaystyle\quad+\frac{x(2+2x+3x^{2}-x^{3}+2x^{4}+x^{6}-x^{7})}{(1-x)(1-x^{2})(1-x^{3})}y^{2}+{\cal O}(y^{3}),

\displaystyle{\cal F}_{2,0,2,0}

\displaystyle=\frac{x^{6}}{(1-x)^{2}(1-x^{2})^{2}}+\frac{x^{3}(1+2x-x^{2})}{(1-x)^{2}(1-x^{2})}y

\displaystyle\quad+\frac{2x(1+3x-3x^{2}+2x^{4}-x^{5})}{(1-x)^{2}(1-x^{2})}y^{2}+{\cal O}(y^{3}).

(44)

See (
27
) for
{\cal F}_{m,0,0,0}={\cal F}_{m,0}=\sigma_{x}\Pi_{m}
.

We numerically confirmed
the expansion (
41
) works well.
For example, for
N=2
and
k=1
, if we sum up the contributions from
m_{\rm tot}\leq 4
,
the discrepancy is

\displaystyle\frac{I_{2,1}}{I_{\rm sugra}I_{\rm D5}}-\sum_{m+m^{\prime}\leq 4}(\cdots)={\cal O}({x}^{14})y^{0}+{\cal O}({x}^{10})y^{1}+{\cal O}(x^{7})y^{2}+{\cal O}(x^{4})y^{3}+{\cal O}(y^{4}),

(45)

and a lot of terms are correctly reproduced.

3.3
Half BPS limit

The half-BPS limit
y\rightarrow 0
of
I_{N,k}
for general values of

N
and
k
is given by
[
20
]

\displaystyle I_{N,k}=\prod_{m=1}^{k}\frac{1}{1-x^{m}}\prod_{m=1}^{N-k}\frac{1}{1-x^{m}}.

(46)

The expansion (
41
) reproduces this factorized form.
In the half-BPS limit the letter index
\sigma_{x}i_{\rm 3d}
of the hypermultiplet on the wall vanishes,
and the wall contribution becomes trivial.
As the result, the functions
{\cal F}_{m,0,m^{\prime},0}
are
factorized to the product of
{\cal F}_{m,0}
and
{\cal F}_{m^{\prime},0}
;

\displaystyle{\cal F}_{m,0,m^{\prime},0}={\cal F}_{m,0}{\cal F}_{m^{\prime},0}=\sigma_{x}(\Pi_{m}\Pi_{m^{\prime}}).

(47)

Then, the double-sum expansion
(
41
) factorizes into two copies of the
simple-sum expansion (
26
) for the large
N
index

\displaystyle\frac{I_{N,k}}{I_{\rm sugra}I_{\rm D5}}

\displaystyle=\left(\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}x^{km}\sigma_{x}\Pi_{m}\right)\left(\sum_{m^{\prime}=0}^{\infty}x^{k^{\prime}m^{\prime}}\sigma_{x}\Pi_{m^{\prime}}\right)=\frac{I_{\infty,k}}{I_{\rm sugra}I_{\rm D5}}\frac{I_{\infty,k^{\prime}}}{I_{\rm sugra}I_{\rm D5}}.

(48)

This correctly reproduces the factorized form
(
46
).

4
Conclusions and discussion

In this paper we investigated the brane expansions for the index

I_{N,k}
of the
{\cal N}=4

U(N)
SYM with
the line operators of the rank
k
anti-symmetric representations.
Our analysis was based on the D5-brane realization of the line-operators.

The large
N
index
I_{\infty,k}
with finite
k

is given by the double-sum brane expansion (
20
),
and the finite
N
index
I_{N,k}
is given by the
quadruple-sum brane expansion (
29
).
By taking an appropriate expansion scheme they reduce to
the simple-sum expansion (
26
) and
the double-sum expansion (
41
), respectively.
The objects causing
the finite
k
and finite
N
corrections
are disk D3-branes ending on the D5-brane.

For the quadruple-sum expansion (
29
) for finite
N
,
the brane indices
{\cal F}_{m_{x},m_{y},m^{\prime}_{x},m^{\prime}_{y}}
are labeled by
the four wrapping numbers,
and the corresponding brane systems consist of
branes extended on the four disks
{\cal D}_{x}
,
{\cal D}_{y}
,
{\cal D}^{\prime}_{x}
, and
{\cal D}^{\prime}_{y}
.
Due to the complication of the systems,
we did not give a general formula
to calculate such contributions.
One interesting fact we found
is the contributions
{\cal F}_{m_{x},m_{y}}={\cal F}_{m_{x},m_{y},0,0}

for
m_{x},m_{y}\geq 1

are vanishing.
This indicates the supersymmetry is
spontaneously broken in the theory realized on

{\cal D}_{x}\cup{\cal D}_{y}
.
In connection with this,
another interesting fact we found is

{\cal F}_{1,1,1,0}\neq 0
.
This means that
the corresponding brane system

{\cal D}_{x}\cup{\cal D}_{y}\cup{\cal D}^{\prime}_{x}

preserves supersymmetry even though
it includes the supersymmetry breaking configuration

{\cal D}_{x}\cup{\cal D}_{y}
as a subsystem.
This indicates that whether the supersymmetry is broken
depends on the global structure of the brane system.
This situation reminds us of the
s
-rules
in Hanany-Witten type brane systems
[
40
]
.
It would be interesting to investigate the
supersymmetry breaking/preserving mechanism on the
brane systems with general wrapping numbers.

There are different directions of generalization
of the results in this work.

One is generalization to multiple tubular D5-branes.
In this work we focused on the anti-symmetric Wilson lines
with
\mu=\{1^{k}\}
.
We can consider a more general
line labeled by
\mu=\{n^{k_{1}-k_{2}},(n-1)^{k_{2}-k_{3}},\ldots,1^{k_{n}}\}
.
The Young diagram has
n
columns, and the line corresponds to
concentric
n
D5-branes.
The
n
D5-branes divide each of
{\cal C}_{x}
and
{\cal C}_{y}

into
n+1
zones.
If we treat the index as
y
-series,
only branes on
{\cal C}_{x}
will contribute to the
index, and we will have a gauge theory depicted as
a linear quiver with
n+1
nodes.
The full expansion will be more complicated and
the theory on a brane system is in general
a quiver gauge theory
with
2(n+1)
nodes.

It is also important to find precise line operators
corresponding to tubular D3-branes, and investigate the finite
k
and finite
N

corrections to the corresponding line operator index.
Although it is often claimed in the literature that
the line operator in a symmetric representation
is holographically dual to a tubular D3-brane configuration found in [6],
fluctuation modes on the D3-brane analyzed in
[
22
]

do not reproduce
the index of the symmetric line operators,
which is identical in the large
N
limit to the index (11)
of the anti-symmetric line operator with the same rank
[
18
]
.
(See Appendix
B
.)
This means some modification is necessary
for line operators dual to the tubular D3-branes.
Regardless of whatever correspond to tubular D3-branes,
it would be interesting to analyze
the line-operator index for the tubular D3-brane insertion
by using brane expansions.
We point out that D-strings are strong candidates for
causing finite
k
corrections.
Let us introduce a coordinate
u
in
AdS_{5}

so that the metric of
AdS_{5}
is given by

\displaystyle ds_{AdS_{5}}^{2}=L_{\rm AdS}^{2}(\cosh^{2}uds_{AdS_{2}}^{2}+du^{2}+\sinh^{2}uds_{S^{2}}^{2}).

(49)

For a tubular D3-brane with string charge
k
, its worldvolume
is given by
u=u_{\rm D3}

[
6
]
where

u_{\rm D3}
is given by

\displaystyle\sinh u_{\rm D3}=\frac{k\sqrt{4\pi g_{s}N}}{4N}.

(50)

The mass of a D-string stretched between the two poles of
S^{2}
is

\displaystyle T_{\rm D1}\int_{-u_{\rm D3}}^{u_{\rm D3}}L_{\rm AdS}\cosh udu=2T_{\rm D1}L_{\rm AdS}\sinh u_{\rm D3}=\frac{k}{L_{\rm AdS}},

(51)

and the dimension of the corresponding operator is
k
.
This fact strongly suggests that D-strings
cause finite
k
corrections to the line operator index.
In addition, D3 giants will give finite
N
corrections.
It would be interesting
to study brane expansions including the contribution from
D-strings and D3 giants.

It is expected that when the numbers of columns and rows
of Young diagrams become of order
N

the holographic correspondent should be
bubbling geometry
[
8
]
.
Recently, relation between the Schur indices of line operators
with such large representations and bubbling geometries
were discussed in
[
21
]
.
It is interesting to investigate the finite
N
corrections
in such background geometries
by using brane expansions.

We hope to revisit these issues in future works.

Appendix A
Character expansion method

In this appendix we derive some useful formulas
using character expansion method

[
41
,
42
]
.
Let us introduce the power sum symmetric polynomials

\displaystyle p_{\lambda}(U)=\prod_{i=1}^{\ell(\lambda)}\mathop{\rm tr}\nolimits U^{\lambda_{i}}=[\mathop{\rm tr}\nolimits(U)]_{\lambda}

(52)

labeled by partitions
\lambda
,
and define the index with insertion of line operators associated with these polynomials.
We start from the localization formula
(
2
) with
s_{\mu}
and
s_{\nu}
replaced by
p_{\lambda}
and
p_{\lambda^{\prime}}
:

\displaystyle I_{N,\lambda,\lambda^{\prime}}

\displaystyle=\langle p_{\lambda}(U)p_{\lambda^{\prime}}(U^{-1})\rangle^{U(N)}

\displaystyle=\int_{U(N)}\hskip-13.00005ptdU\mathop{\rm Pexp}\nolimits(i_{\rm vec}\mathop{\rm tr}\nolimits(U)\mathop{\rm tr}\nolimits(U^{-1}))p_{\lambda}(U)p_{\lambda^{\prime}}(U^{-1}).

(53)

By using the definition of the plethystic exponential we can rewrite this
as

\displaystyle I_{N,\lambda,\lambda^{\prime}}=\int_{U(N)}\hskip-13.00005ptdU\sum_{\lambda^{\prime\prime}}\frac{1}{z_{\lambda^{\prime\prime}}}[i_{\rm vec}]_{\lambda^{\prime\prime}}p_{\lambda+\lambda^{\prime\prime}}(U)p_{\lambda^{\prime}+\lambda^{\prime\prime}}(U^{-1}),

(54)

where
\lambda+\lambda^{\prime}
is the union of two partitions
\lambda
and
\lambda^{\prime}
,
which is given by arranging
\{\lambda_{1},\lambda_{2},\ldots,\lambda_{\ell(\lambda)},\lambda^{\prime}_{1},\lambda^{\prime}_{2},\ldots,\lambda^{\prime}_{\ell(\lambda^{\prime})}\}

in descending order.
The polynomials
p_{\lambda}(U)
are expressed as linear combinations of the Schur polynomials
s_{\mu}(U)

by the formula

\displaystyle p_{\lambda}(U)=\sum_{\mu,\ell(\mu)\leq N}\chi_{\mu}(\lambda)s_{\mu}(U)

(55)

given by Frobenius.

\chi_{\mu}(\lambda)
is the character of
an irreducible representation of the symmetric group
S_{n}
(
n=|\lambda|
) corresponding to the
partition
\mu
evaluated at a permutation of cycle type
\lambda
.
With this formula and the orthonormal relation of
the Schur polynomials

\displaystyle\int_{U(N)}\hskip-13.00005ptdUs_{\mu}(U)s_{\nu}(U^{-1})=\delta_{\mu,\nu},

(56)

we obtain the expression

\displaystyle I_{N,\lambda,\lambda^{\prime}}=\sum_{\lambda^{\prime\prime}}\frac{1}{z_{\lambda^{\prime\prime}}}[i_{\rm vec}]_{\lambda^{\prime\prime}}\sum_{\mu,\ell(\mu)\leq N}\chi_{\mu}(\lambda+\lambda^{\prime\prime})\chi_{\mu}(\lambda^{\prime}+\lambda^{\prime\prime}).

(57)

In the large
N
limit,
the bound
\ell(\mu)\leq N
in the
\mu
-sum
in (
57
) dissapears, and
we can use the orthogonal relation of the characters
\chi_{\mu}(\lambda)
:

\displaystyle\sum_{\mu}\chi_{\mu}(\lambda)\chi_{\mu}(\lambda^{\prime})=z_{\lambda}\delta_{\lambda,\lambda^{\prime}}.

(58)

Then, we obtain

\displaystyle I_{\infty,\lambda,\lambda^{\prime}}

\displaystyle=\delta_{\lambda,\lambda^{\prime}}\sum_{\lambda^{\prime\prime}}\frac{z_{\lambda+\lambda^{\prime\prime}}}{z_{\lambda^{\prime\prime}}}[i_{\rm vec}]_{\lambda^{\prime\prime}}=z_{\lambda}\delta_{\lambda,\lambda^{\prime}}[I_{\rm F1}]_{\lambda}I_{\rm sugra}.

(59)

For
\lambda=\lambda^{\prime}=\{1\}
this gives (
7
).

By using the inverse relation to
(
55
)

\displaystyle s_{\mu}(U)=\sum_{\lambda\vdash|\mu|}\frac{1}{z_{\lambda}}\chi_{\mu}(\lambda)p_{\lambda}(U),

(60)

We can express
\langle s_{\mu}(U)s_{\nu}(U^{-1})\rangle^{U(N)}
as a
linear combination of
I_{N,\lambda,\lambda^{\prime}}
.
For
N=\infty
,
combining
(
59
) and
(
60
)
we obtain

\displaystyle\langle s_{\mu}(U)s_{\nu}(U^{-1})\rangle^{U(\infty)}

\displaystyle=I_{\rm sugra}\sum_{\lambda\vdash|\mu|}\frac{1}{z_{\lambda}}[I_{\rm F1}]_{\lambda}\chi_{\mu}(\lambda)\chi_{\nu}(\lambda).

(61)

This is non-vanishing only when
|\mu|=|\nu|
.

The line operator indices for the symmetric and anti-symmetric representations
can be obtained by substituting

\sigma_{\{1^{k}\}}(\lambda)=\pm 1

and
\sigma_{\{k\}}(\lambda)=1
for
\lambda\vdash k

into (
61
).
The common result is shown in (

).
