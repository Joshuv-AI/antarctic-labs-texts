δ \delta - r r -hyperideals and ϕ \phi - δ \delta - r r -hyperideals of commutative Krasner hyperrings
Peng Xu, Melis Bolat, Elif Kaya, Serkan Onar, Bayram Ali Ersoy, Kostaq Hila

Abstract
. This paper deals with an important class of multialgebras, called Krasner hyperrings. Our purpose is to define the expansion of r r -hyperideals and to extend this concept to ϕ \phi - δ \delta - r r -hyperideal in commutative Krasner hyperrings with nonzero identity. δ \delta - r r -hyperideals of commutative Krasner hyperrings are studied. Some properties of ϕ \phi - δ \delta - r r -hyperideals are investigated and several examples are provided.
