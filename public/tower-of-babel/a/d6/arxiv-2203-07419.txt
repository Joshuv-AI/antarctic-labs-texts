Torsion subgroups, solvability and the Engel condition in associative rings
Orest Artemovych, Victor Bovdi

Abstract
. The connections between the properties of associative rings that are Lie-solvable (Engel, n-Engel, locally finite, respectively) and the properties of their adjoin subgroups are investigated.

Let

We study properties of associative rings and their adjoint groups which are connected with solvability, Engel conditions and periodicity.
