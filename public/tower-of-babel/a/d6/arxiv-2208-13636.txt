Reply to “Comment on ‘Energy Conditions in f ⁡ ( Q ) f(Q) gravity”
Sanjay Mandal, P.K. Sahoo, J.R.L. Santos

Abstract
Recently, Avik De and L.T. How avik claim that we have not followed an effective theory approach for f ⁡ ( Q ) f(Q) gravity, missing relevant terms to compute the energy conditions sanjay . They also state that we did not present how the pressure and energy density follow a set of energy condition criteria. In this reply we show how the effective density and effective pressure found in avik yield to the same Fridmann Equations presented in our article. Therefore, we claim that the comment avik only introduces an equivalent path to derive the Friedmann Equations and that the extra terms found by Avik De and L.T. How are consequence of their specific definition for their effective density and effective pressure. However, such terms do not change the equations for ρ \rho and p p . Consequently, these extra terms are not going to change the energy constraints presented by us in sanjay .
