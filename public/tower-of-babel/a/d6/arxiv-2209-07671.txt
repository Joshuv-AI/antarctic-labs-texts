Observation of anisotropic Dirac cones in the topological material Ti 2 Te 2 P
Gyanendra Dhakal, Firoza Kabir, Ashis K. Nandy, Alex Aperis, Anup Pradhan Sakhya, Subhadip Pradhan, Klauss Dimitri, Christopher Sims, Sabin Regmi, M. Mofazzel Hosen, Yangyang Liu, Luis Persaud, Dariusz Kaczorowski, Peter M. Oppeneer, Madhab Neupane ∗

Abstract
Anisotropic bulk Dirac (or Weyl) cones in three dimensional systems have recently gained intense research interest as they are examples of materials with tilted Dirac (or Weyl) cones indicating the violation of Lorentz invariance. In contrast, the studies on anisotropic surface Dirac cones in topological materials which contribute to anisotropic carrier mobility have been limited. By employing angle-resolved photoemission spectroscopy and first-principles calculations, we reveal the anisotropic surface Dirac dispersion in a tetradymite material Ti 2 Te 2 P on the (001) plane of the Brillioun zone. We observe the quasi-elliptical Fermi pockets at the M ¯ \bar{M} -point of the Brillouin zone forming the anisotropic surface Dirac cones. Our calculations of the ℤ 2 \mathbb{Z}_{2} indices confirm that the system is topologically non-trivial with multiple topological phases in the same material. In addition, the observed nodal-line like feature formed by bulk bands makes this system topologically rich.

The anisotropic surface Dirac cone in a 3D system has been overlooked despite substantial works done on the tilted bulk Dirac cones in semimetallic systems.
The anisotropic Dirac cone at the

Supplementary Note 2: First-principles calculations of Ti

Te
2
P

Structural optimization was carried out within the density-functional formalism as implemented in the Vienna ab initio simulation package (VASP)
vasp
;
vasp1
