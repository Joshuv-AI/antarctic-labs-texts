Explicit Reciprocity Laws for Formal Drinfeld Modules
Marwa Ala Eddine Université de Bourgogne Franche-Comté, Laboratoire de Mathématique de Besançon, 25030 Besançon, France. E-mail: marwa.ala_eddine@univ-fcomte.fr , ORCID: 0000-0001-6911-0580

Abstract
. In this paper, we prove explicit reciprocity laws for a class of formal Drinfeld modules having stable reduction of height one, in the spirit of those existing in characteristic zero (cf. the work of Wiles [ 7 ] ). We begin by defining the Kummer pairing in the language of formal Drinfeld modules defined over local fields of positive characteristic. We then prove explicit formulas for this pairing in terms of the logarithm of the formal Drinfeld module, a certain Coleman power series, torsion points and the trace. Our results extend the explicit formulas already proved by Anglès [ 11 ] for Carlitz modules, and by Bars and Longhi [ 1 ] for sign-normalized rank one Drinfeld modules. The approach followed is similar to the ones followed in the previously mentioned papers [ 7 , 11 , 1 ] , taking into account the subtleties derived from the fact that the formal Drinfeld modules considered are formal power series, and are no longer polynomials. Keywords: formal Drinfeld modules, explicit reciprocity laws, local fields, class field theory 2020 Mathematics Subject Classification: 11F85, 11S31

Introduction

Explicit reciprocity laws have a long history. In 1928, Artin and Hasse
[
10

proved explicit formulas in characteristic zero for the multiplicative group. These formulas were completed by Iwasawa
[

in 1968. In 1978, Wiles
[

proved an important generalization to the case of general Lubin-Tate formal groups. Soon after, Kolyvagin
[

extended all these results to formal groups of finite height. In the present paper, we place ourselves in positive characteristic, and we consider formal Drinfeld modules as defined by Rosen in
[
3

[

[

proved similar formulas for formal Drinfeld modules obtained from sign-normalized rank 1 Drinfeld modules.
