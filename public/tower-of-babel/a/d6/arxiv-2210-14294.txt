1.
Introduction and preliminaries

In 1948, Rabotnov
[
21
]
introduced a special function applied in
viscoelasticity. This function, known today as the Rabotnov fractional
exponential function or briefly Rabotnov function, is defined as
follows

R_{\alpha,\beta}(z)=z^{\alpha}\sum\limits_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{(\beta)^{n}z^{n\left(1+\alpha\right)}}{\Gamma(\left(n+1\right)\left(1+\alpha\right))},\quad(\alpha,\beta,z\in\mathbb{C}\;).

(1)

Rabotnov function is the particular case of the familiar Mittag-Leffler
function
[
18
]
widely used in the solution of fractional order integral
equations or fractional order differential equations. The relation between the
Rabotnov function and Mittag-Leffler function can be written as follows

R_{\alpha,\beta}(z)=z^{\alpha}E_{1+\alpha,1+\alpha}(\beta z^{1+\alpha}),

where
E
is Mittag-Leffler function and
\alpha,\beta,z\in\mathbb{C}
.
Several properties of Mittag-Leffler function and generalized Mittag-Leffler
function can be found in
[
1
,
5
,
6
,
12
,
13
]
.

A generalization of the Rabotnov function
R_{\alpha,\beta}
is given by

R_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}(z)=z^{\alpha}\sum\limits_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{(\beta)^{n}z^{n\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)}}{\Gamma(\left(n+1\right)\left(\gamma+\alpha\right))},\quad(\alpha,\beta,\gamma,z\in\mathbb{C}\;).

(2)

Let
\mathcal{A}
denote the class of functions of the form

f(z)=z+\sum\limits_{n=2}^{\infty}a_{n}z^{n}

(3)

which are analytic in the open unit disc
\mathfrak{U}=\left\{z:\left|z\right|<1\right\}
and hold the normalization condition

f(0)=f^{\prime}(0)-1=0.
Further, by
\mathcal{S}
we shall denote the class
of all functions in
\mathcal{A}
which are univalent in
\mathfrak{U}
.

The Alexander transform
\mathbb{I}[f]:\mathfrak{U}\rightarrow\mathbb{C}
of

f
is defined by
[
4
]

\mathbb{I}[f]={\displaystyle\int\limits_{0}^{z}}\frac{f(t)}{t}dt=z+\sum\limits_{n=2}^{\infty}\frac{a_{n}}{n}z^{n}.

It is clear that the Rabotnov function
R_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}(z)
does not
belong to the family
\mathcal{A}
. Thus, it is natural to consider the
following normalization of Rabotnov function

\displaystyle\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}(z)

\displaystyle=z^{\frac{\gamma}{\gamma+\alpha}}\Gamma\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)R_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}(z^{\frac{1}{\gamma+\alpha}})

\displaystyle=z+\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{\beta^{n}\Gamma\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)}{\Gamma\left(\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)(n+1)\right)}z^{n+1},\;z\in\mathfrak{U}.

(4)

Geometric properties including starlikeness, convexity and close-to-convexity
for the normalized Rabotnov function
\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,1}(z)
were
recently investigated by Eker and Ece in
[
9
]
. A new class of normalized
analytic functions and bi-univalent functions associated with the normalized
Rabotnov function
\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,1}(z)
was also introduced and
studied by Amourah et al.
[
2
]
.

Remark 1.1
.

The function
\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}(z)
contains many
well known functions as its special case, for example:

\left\{\begin{array}[c]{c}\mathbb{R}_{0,-\frac{1}{3},1}(z)=ze^{-\frac{z}{3}},\\
\mathbb{R}_{1,\frac{1}{2},1}(z)=\sqrt{2z}\sinh\sqrt{\frac{z}{2}},\\
\mathbb{R}_{1,-\frac{1}{4},1}(z)=2\sqrt{z}\sin\frac{\sqrt{z}}{2},\\
\mathbb{R}_{1,1,1}(z)=\sqrt{z}\sinh\sqrt{z},\\
\mathbb{R}_{1,2,1}(z)=\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{2z}\sinh\sqrt{2z}.\end{array}\right.

The concept of finding the lower bound of the real part of the ratio
of the partial sum of analytic functions to its infinite series sum was
introduced firstly by Silvia
[
25
]
. Silverman in
[
24
]
found the
partial sums of convex and starlike functions by developed more useful
techniques. After that, several researchers investigated such partial sums for
different subclasses of analytic functions. For more work on partial sums, the
interested readers are referred to
[
7
,
10
,
11
,
12
,
17
,
19
,
20
,
23
]
.

Recently, some researchers have studied on partial sums of special functions.
For example, Orhan and Yagmur in
[
26
]
determined lower bounds for the
normalized Struve functions to its sequence of partial sums. Some lower bounds
for the quotients of normalized Dini functions and their partial sum, as well
as for the quotients of the derivative of normalized Dini functions and their
partial sums were obtained by Aktaş and Orhan in
[
3
]
. Din et al.

[
8
]
found the partial sums of two kinds normalized Wright functions and
the partial sums of Alexander transform of these normalized Wright functions.

Kazımoğlu in
[
15
]
studied the partial sums of the
normalized Miller-Ross function. Kazımoğlu and Deniz
[
16
]

determined lower bounds for the normalized Rabotnov function
\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,1}(z)
to its sequence of partial sums.

Let
(\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}(z))_{m}(z)=z+\sum_{n=1}^{m}\frac{\beta^{n}\Gamma\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)}{\Gamma\left(\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)(n+1)\right)}z^{n+1},m\in\mathbb{N=\{}1,2,3,...\},
be
the sequence of partial sums of normalized Rabotnov function
\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}(z)
given by (
4
) and for
m=0
, we have

(\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}(z))_{0}(z)=z
.

The aim of the present paper is to determine the lower bounds of

\displaystyle\mathfrak{R}\left\{\frac{\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}(z)}{(\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma})_{m}(z)}\right\},\mathfrak{R}\left\{\frac{(\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma})_{m}(z)}{\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}(z)}\right\},\mathfrak{R}\left\{\frac{\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}^{\prime}(z)}{(\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma})_{m}^{\prime}(z)}\right\},\mathfrak{R}\left\{\frac{(\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma})_{m}^{\prime}(z)}{\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}^{\prime}(z)}\right\},

\displaystyle\mathfrak{R}\left\{\frac{\mathbb{I}\left[\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}\right](z)}{(\mathbb{I}\left[\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}\right])_{m}(z)}\right\},\mathfrak{R}\left\{\frac{(\mathbb{I}\left[\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}\right])_{m}(z)}{\mathbb{I}\left[\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}\right](z)}\right\},

where
\mathbb{I}\left[\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}\right]
is the
Alexander transform of
\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}
.

Throughout this paper, we shall restrict our attention to the case of
real-valued
\alpha\geq 0,\gamma\geq 1,

\beta\in\mathbb{C}
and
z\in\mathfrak{U}
.

In order to obtain our results we need the following lemmas.

Lemma 1.2
.

If
n\in\mathbb{N}
and
\alpha\geq 0,\gamma\geq 1,
then

(\gamma+\alpha)^{n-1}(n-1)!\Gamma\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)\leq\Gamma\left((\gamma+\alpha\right)n).

(5)

Proof.

Using the inductive method, we can easily prove the inequality (
5
).
∎

Lemma 1.3
.

Let
\alpha\geq 0,\gamma\geq 1
and
\beta\in\mathbb{C}
. Then the
function
\mathcal{\ }\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}:\mathfrak{U\rightarrow}\mathbb{C}
defined by (
4
) satisfies the following inequalities:

(i)
If
2\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)>\left|\beta\right|
, then

\left|\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}(z)\right|\leq\frac{2\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)+\left|\beta\right|}{2\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)-\left|\beta\right|}\ \ \ \ \ \left(z\in\mathfrak{U}\right).

(ii)
If
\gamma+\alpha>\left|\beta\right|,
then

\left|\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}^{\prime}(z)\right|\leq\frac{\gamma+\alpha+\left|\beta\right|}{\gamma+\alpha-\left|\beta\right|}\ \ \ \ \ \left(z\in\mathfrak{U}\right).

(iii)
If
2\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)>\left|\beta\right|,

then

\left|\mathbb{I}[\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}(z)]]\right|\leq\frac{2(\gamma+\alpha)}{2(\gamma+\alpha)-\left|\beta\right|}\ \ \ \ \ \left(z\in\mathfrak{U}\right).

Proof.

(i) By using the inequality (
5
) of Lemma
1.3
and the inequality

n!\geq 2^{n-1}\ \left(n\in\mathbb{N}\right),

we have

\displaystyle\left|\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}(z)\right|

\displaystyle=\left|z+\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{\beta^{n}\Gamma\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)}{\Gamma\left(\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)(n+1)\right)}z^{n+1}\right|

\displaystyle\leq 1+\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{\left|\beta\right|^{n}\Gamma\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)}{\Gamma\left(\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)(n+1)\right)}

\displaystyle\leq 1+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{\left|\beta\right|^{n}}{\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)^{n}n!}

\displaystyle\leq 1+\frac{\left|\beta\right|}{\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left(\frac{\left|\beta\right|}{2\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)}\right)^{n-1}

\displaystyle=\frac{2\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)+\left|\beta\right|}{2\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)-\left|\beta\right|},\ \left(2\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)>\left|\beta\right|\right).\

(ii) To prove (ii), using the inequality (
5
) of Lemma
1.2
, and
the inequality

2n!\geq n+1\ \ \ \left(n\in\mathbb{N}\right),

we have

\displaystyle\left|\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}^{\prime}(z)\right|

\displaystyle=\left|1+\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{(n+1)\beta^{n}\Gamma\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)}{\Gamma\left(\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)(n+1)\right)}z^{n}\right|

\displaystyle\leq 1+\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{(n+1)\left|\beta\right|^{n}\Gamma\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)}{\Gamma\left(\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)(n+1)\right)}

\displaystyle\leq 1+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{(n+1)\left|\beta\right|^{n}}{\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)^{n}n!}

\displaystyle\leq 1+\frac{2\left|\beta\right|}{\gamma+\alpha}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left(\frac{\left|\beta\right|}{\gamma+\alpha}\right)^{n-1}

\displaystyle=\frac{\gamma+\alpha+\left|\beta\right|}{\gamma+\alpha-\left|\beta\right|},\ \left(\gamma+\alpha>\left|\beta\right|\right).

(iii) Making the use of the inequality (
5
) of Lemma
1.3
and the
inequality

(n+1)!\geq 2^{n}\ \ \ \ \left(n\in\mathbb{N}\right),

we thus find

\displaystyle\left|\mathbb{I}[\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}](z)]\right|

\displaystyle=\left|z+\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{\beta^{n}\Gamma\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)}{(n+1)\Gamma\left(\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)(n+1)\right)}z^{n+1}\right|

\displaystyle\leq 1+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{\left|\beta\right|^{n}}{\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)^{n}(n+1)!}

\displaystyle\leq 1+\frac{\left|\beta\right|}{2(\gamma+\alpha)}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left(\frac{\left|\beta\right|}{2(\gamma+\alpha)}\right)^{n-1}

\displaystyle=\frac{2(\gamma+\alpha)}{2(\gamma+\alpha)-\left|\beta\right|},\ \left(2(\gamma+\alpha)>\left|\beta\right|\right).

∎

Let
w(z)
be an analytic function in
\mathfrak{U}
: In the sequel, we will
use the following well-known result:

\mathfrak{R}\left\{\frac{1+w(z)}{1-w(z)}\right\}>0,\text{\ }z\in\mathfrak{U}\text{ if and only if }|w(z)|<1,\text{\ }z\in\mathfrak{U}\text{.}

Theorem 1.4
.

Let
\alpha\geq 0,\gamma\geq 1
and
2\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)\geq 3\left|\beta\right|
. Then

\mathfrak{R}\left\{\frac{\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}(z)}{(\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma})_{m}(z)}\right\}\geq\frac{2\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)-3\left|\beta\right|}{2\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)-\left|\beta\right|},\text{ \ \ \ \ \ \ }z\in\mathfrak{U},

(6)

and

\mathfrak{R}\left\{\frac{(\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma})_{m}(z))}{\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}(z)}\right\}\geq\frac{2\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)-\left|\beta\right|}{2\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)+\left|\beta\right|},\text{ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ }z\in\mathfrak{U}.

(7)

Proof.

From inequality (i) of Lemma
1.3
, we get

1+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left|A_{n}\right|\leq\frac{2\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)+\left|\beta\right|}{2\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)-\left|\beta\right|},

or equivalently

\left(\frac{2\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)-\left|\beta\right|}{2\left|\beta\right|}\right)\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left|A_{n}\right|\leq 1,

where
A_{n}=\frac{\beta^{n}\Gamma\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)}{\Gamma\left(\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)(n+1)\right)}.

In order to prove the inequality (
6
), we consider the function
w(z)

defined by

\displaystyle\frac{1+w(z)}{1-w(z)}

\displaystyle=\left(\frac{2\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)-\left|\beta\right|}{2\left|\beta\right|}\right)\left[\frac{\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}(z)}{(\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma})_{m}(z)}-\frac{2\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)-3\left|\beta\right|}{2\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)-\left|\beta\right|}\right]

\displaystyle=\frac{1+\sum_{n=1}^{m}A_{n}z^{n}+\left(\frac{2\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)-\left|\beta\right|}{2\left|\beta\right|}\right)\sum_{n=m+1}^{\infty}A_{n}z^{n}}{1+\sum_{n=1}^{m}A_{n}z^{n}}.

(8)

Now, from (
8
) we can write

w(z)=\frac{\left(\frac{2\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)-\left|\beta\right|}{2\left|\beta\right|}\right)\sum_{n=m+1}^{\infty}A_{n}z^{n}}{2+2\sum_{n=1}^{m}A_{n}z^{n}+\left(\frac{2\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)-\left|\beta\right|}{2\left|\beta\right|}\right)\sum_{n=m+1}^{\infty}A_{n}z^{n}}

and

\left|w(z)\right|\leq\frac{\left(\frac{2\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)-\left|\beta\right|}{2\left|\beta\right|}\right)\sum_{n=m+1}^{\infty}\left|A_{n}\right|}{2-2\sum_{n=1}^{m}\left|A_{n}\right|-\left(\frac{2\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)-\left|\beta\right|}{2\left|\beta\right|}\right)\sum_{n=m+1}^{\infty}\left|A_{n}\right|}.

This implies that
|w(z)|\leq 1
if and only if

2\left(\frac{2\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)-\left|\beta\right|}{2\left|\beta\right|}\right)\sum_{n=m+1}^{\infty}\left|A_{n}\right|\leq 2-2\sum_{n=1}^{m}\left|A_{n}\right|.

Which further implies that

\sum_{n=1}^{m}\left|A_{n}\right|+\left(\frac{2\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)-\left|\beta\right|}{2\left|\beta\right|}\right)\sum_{n=m+1}^{\infty}\left|A_{n}\right|\leq 1.

(9)

It suffices to show that the left hand side of (
9
) is bounded above by

\left(\frac{2\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)-\left|\beta\right|}{2\left|\beta\right|}\right)\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left|A_{n}\right|,
which is equivalent to

\left(\frac{2\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)-3\left|\beta\right|}{2\left|\beta\right|}\right)\sum_{n=1}^{m}\left|A_{n}\right|\geq 0.

To prove (
7
), we write

\displaystyle\frac{1+w(z)}{1-w(z)}

\displaystyle=\left(\frac{2\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)+\left|\beta\right|}{2\left|\beta\right|}\right)\left[\frac{(\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma})_{m}(z)}{\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}(z)}-\frac{2\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)-\left|\beta\right|}{2\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)+\left|\beta\right|}\right]

\displaystyle=\frac{1+\sum_{n=1}^{m}A_{n}z^{n}-\left(\frac{2\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)+\left|\beta\right|}{2\left|\beta\right|}\right)\sum_{n=m+1}^{\infty}A_{n}z^{n}}{1+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}A_{n}z^{n}}.

Therefore

\left|w(z)\right|\leq\frac{\left(\frac{2\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)+\left|\beta\right|}{2\left|\beta\right|}\right)\sum_{n=m+1}^{\infty}\left|A_{n}\right|}{2-2\sum_{n=1}^{m}\left|A_{n}\right|-\left(\frac{2\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)-3\left|\beta\right|}{2\left|\beta\right|}\right)\sum_{n=m+1}^{\infty}\left|A_{n}\right|}\leq 1.

The last inequality is equivalent to

\sum_{n=1}^{m}\left|A_{n}\right|+\left(\frac{2\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)-\left|\beta\right|}{2\left|\beta\right|}\right)\sum_{n=m+1}^{\infty}\left|A_{n}\right|\leq 1.

(10)

Since the left hand side of (
10
) is bounded above by
\left(\frac{2\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)-\left|\beta\right|}{2\left|\beta\right|}\right)\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left|A_{n}\right|
, this completes the proof.
∎

Theorem 1.5
.

Let
\alpha\geq 0,\gamma\geq 1
and
\gamma+\alpha\geq 3\left|\beta\right|
. Then

\mathfrak{R}\left\{\frac{\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}^{\prime}(z)}{(\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma})_{m}^{\prime}(z)}\right\}\geq\frac{\gamma+\alpha-3\left|\beta\right|}{\gamma+\alpha-\left|\beta\right|},\text{ \ \ \ }z\in\mathfrak{U},

(11)

and

\mathfrak{R}\left\{\frac{(\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma})_{m}^{\prime}(z)}{\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}^{\prime}(z)}\right\}\geq\frac{\gamma+\alpha-\left|\beta\right|}{\gamma+\alpha+\left|\beta\right|},\text{ \ \ \ \ }z\in\mathfrak{U}.

(12)

Proof.

From part (ii) of Lemma
1.3
, we observe that

1+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(n+1)\left|A_{n}\right|\leq\frac{\gamma+\alpha+\left|\beta\right|}{\gamma+\alpha-\left|\beta\right|},

where
A_{n}=\frac{\beta^{n}\Gamma\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)}{\Gamma\left(\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)(n+1)\right)}
. This implies
that

\left(\frac{\gamma+\alpha-\left|\beta\right|}{2\left|\beta\right|}\right)\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(n+1)\left|A_{n}\right|\leq 1.

Consider

\displaystyle\frac{1+w(z)}{1-w(z)}

\displaystyle=\left(\frac{\gamma+\alpha-\left|\beta\right|}{2\left|\beta\right|}\right)\left[\frac{\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}^{\prime}(z)}{(\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma})_{m}^{\prime}(z)}-\frac{\gamma+\alpha-3\left|\beta\right|}{\gamma+\alpha-\left|\beta\right|}\right]

\displaystyle=\frac{1+\sum_{n=1}^{m}(n+1)A_{n}z^{n}+\left(\frac{\gamma+\alpha-\left|\beta\right|}{2\left|\beta\right|}\right)\sum_{n=m+1}^{\infty}(n+1)A_{n}z^{n}}{1+\sum_{n=1}^{m}(n+1)A_{n}z^{n}}.

Therefore

|w(z)|\leq\frac{\left(\frac{\gamma+\alpha-\left|\beta\right|}{2\left|\beta\right|}\right)\sum_{n=m+1}^{\infty}(n+1)\left|A_{n}\right|}{2-2\sum_{n=1}^{m}(n+1)\left|A_{n}\right|-\left(\frac{\gamma+\alpha-\left|\beta\right|}{2\left|\beta\right|}\right)\sum_{n=m+1}^{\infty}(n+1)\left|A_{n}\right|}\leq 1.

The last inequality is equivalent to

\sum_{n=1}^{m}(n+1)\left|A_{n}\right|+\left(\frac{\gamma+\alpha-\left|\beta\right|}{2\left|\beta\right|}\right)\sum_{n=m+1}^{\infty}(n+1)\left|A_{n}\right|\leq 1.

(13)

It suffices to show that the left hand side of (
13
) is bounded above by

\left(\frac{\gamma+\alpha-\left|\beta\right|}{2\left|\beta\right|}\right)\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(n+1)\left|A_{n}\right|

which is equivalent to

\left(\frac{\gamma+\alpha-3\left|\beta\right|}{2\left|\beta\right|}\right)\sum_{n=1}^{m}(n+1)\left|A_{n}\right|\geq 0.

To prove the result (
12
), we write

\displaystyle\frac{1+w(z)}{1-w(z)}

\displaystyle=\left(\frac{\gamma+\alpha+\left|\beta\right|}{2\left|\beta\right|}\right)\left[\frac{(\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma})_{m}^{\prime}(z)}{\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}^{\prime}(z)}-\frac{\gamma+\alpha-\left|\beta\right|}{\gamma+\alpha+\left|\beta\right|}\right]

where

\left|w(z)\right|\leq\frac{\left(\frac{\gamma+\alpha+\left|\beta\right|}{2\left|\beta\right|}\right)\sum_{n=m+1}^{\infty}(n+1)\left|A_{n}\right|}{2-2\sum_{n=1}^{m}(n+1)\left|A_{n}\right|-\frac{\gamma+\alpha-3\left|\beta\right|}{2\left|\beta\right|}\sum_{n=m+1}^{\infty}(n+1)\left|A_{n}\right|}\leq 1.

The last inequality is equivalent to

\sum_{n=1}^{m}(n+1)\left|A_{n}\right|+\left(\frac{\gamma+\alpha-\left|\beta\right|}{2\left|\beta\right|}\right)\sum_{n=m+1}^{\infty}(n+1)\left|A_{n}\right|\leq 1.

(14)

It suffices to show that the left hand side of (
14
) is bounded above by

\left(\frac{\gamma+\alpha-\left|\beta\right|}{2\left|\beta\right|}\right)\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(n+1)\left|A_{n}\right|.

∎

Theorem 1.6
.

Let
\alpha\geq 0,\gamma\geq 1
and
\gamma+\alpha\geq\left|\beta\right|
. Then

\mathfrak{R}\left\{\frac{\mathbb{I}[\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}](z)}{(\mathbb{I}[\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}])_{m}(z)}\right\}\geq\frac{2(\gamma+\alpha)-2\left|\beta\right|}{2(\gamma+\alpha)-\left|\beta\right|},\text{ \ \ \ \ \ \ }z\in\mathfrak{U},

(15)

and

\mathfrak{R}\left\{\frac{(\mathbb{I}[\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}])_{m}(z)}{\mathbb{I}[\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}](z)}\right\}\geq\frac{2(\gamma+\alpha)-\left|\beta\right|}{2(\gamma+\alpha)},\text{
\ \ \ \ \ \ \ }z\in\mathfrak{U}.

(16)

where
\mathbb{I}[\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}]
is the Alexander

\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}.

Proof.

To prove (
15
) , we consider from part (iii) of Lemma
1.3
so that

1+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{\left|A_{n}\right|}{n+1}\leq\frac{2(\gamma+\alpha)}{2(\gamma+\alpha)-\left|\beta\right|},

or equivalently

\left(\frac{2(\gamma+\alpha)-\left|\beta\right|}{\left|\beta\right|}\right)\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{\left|A_{n}\right|}{n+1}\leq 1

where
A_{n}=\frac{\beta^{n}\Gamma\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)}{\Gamma\left(\left(\gamma+\alpha\right)(n+1)\right)}.
Now, we write

\displaystyle\frac{1+w(z)}{1-w(z)}

\displaystyle=\left(\frac{2(\gamma+\alpha)-\left|\beta\right|}{\left|\beta\right|}\right)\left[\frac{\mathbb{I}[\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}](z)}{(\mathbb{I}[\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}])_{m}(z)}-\frac{2(\gamma+\alpha)-2\left|\beta\right|}{2(\gamma+\alpha)-\left|\beta\right|}\right]

\displaystyle=\frac{1+\sum_{n=1}^{m}\frac{\left|A_{n}\right|}{n+1}z^{n}+\left(\frac{2(\gamma+\alpha)-\left|\beta\right|}{\left|\beta\right|}\right)\sum_{n=m+1}^{\infty}\frac{\left|A_{n}\right|}{n+1}z^{n}}{1+\sum_{n=1}^{m}\frac{\left|A_{n}\right|}{n+1}z^{n}}.

(17)

Now, from (
17
) we can write

w(z)=\frac{\left(\frac{2(\gamma+\alpha)-\left|\beta\right|}{\left|\beta\right|}\right)\sum_{n=m+1}^{\infty}\frac{A_{n}}{n+1}z^{n}}{2+2\sum_{n=1}^{m}\frac{A_{n}}{n+1}z^{n}+\left(\frac{2(\gamma+\alpha)-\left|\beta\right|}{\left|\beta\right|}\right)\sum_{n=m+1}^{\infty}\frac{A_{n}}{n+1}z^{n}}.

Using the fact that
|w(z)|\leq 1
, we get

\left|\frac{\left(\frac{2(\gamma+\alpha)-\left|\beta\right|}{\left|\beta\right|}\right)\sum_{n=m+1}^{\infty}\frac{\left|A_{n}\right|}{n+1}}{2-2\sum_{n=1}^{m}\frac{\left|A_{n}\right|}{n+1}-\left(\frac{2(\gamma+\alpha)-\left|\beta\right|}{\left|\beta\right|}\right)\sum_{n=m+1}^{\infty}\frac{\left|A_{n}\right|}{n+1}}\right|\leq 1.

The last inequality is equivalent to

\sum_{n=1}^{m}\frac{\left|A_{n}\right|}{n+1}+\left(\frac{2(\gamma+\alpha)-\left|\beta\right|}{\left|\beta\right|}\right)\sum_{n=m+1}^{\infty}\frac{\left|A_{n}\right|}{n+1}\leq 1.

It suffices to show that the left hand side of (

\left(\frac{2(\gamma+\alpha)-\left|\beta\right|}{\left|\beta\right|}\right)\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{\left|A_{n}\right|}{n+1},
which is equivalent to

\left(\frac{2(\gamma+\alpha)-2\left|\beta\right|}{\left|\beta\right|}\right)\sum_{n=1}^{m}\frac{\left|A_{n}\right|}{n+1}\geq 0.

The proof of (

1.4
.

3.
Conclusions

Recently, some researchers have studied on partial sums of special functions
like, the normalized Struve functions, the normalized Dini functions, the
normalized Wright functions and the normalized Miller-Ross function. In this
present paper, we have introduced a new generalization of the Rabotnov
function
R_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}
given by (
2
). Furthermore, we
determined lower bounds for the normalized Rabotnov function
\mathbb{R}_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}
to its sequence of partial sums. Several interesting
examples of the main results are also considered.
