On the triple tensor product of generalized Heisenberg Lie superalgebra of rank ≤ 2 \leq 2
IBRAHEM YAKZAN HASAN, Rudra Narayan Padhan

Abstract
. In this article, we compute the Schur multiplier of all generalized Heisenberg Lie superalgebras of rank 2 2 . We discuss the structure of ⊗ 3 H \otimes^{3}H and ∧ 3 H \wedge^{3}H where H H is a generalized Heisenberg Lie superalgebra of rank ≤ 2 \leq 2 . Moreover, we prove that if L L is an ( m ∣ n ) (m\mid n) -dimensional non-abelian nilpotent Lie superalgebra with derived subalgebra of dimension ( r ∣ s ) (r\mid s) , then dim ⊗ 3 L ≤ ( m + n ) ( m + n − ( r + s ) ) 2 \dim\otimes^{3}L\leq(m+n)(m+n-(r+s))^{2} . In particular, for r = 1 , s = 0 r=1,s=0 the equality holds if and only if L ≅ H ⁡ ( 1 ∣ 0 ) L\cong H(1\mid 0) .

Consider

Let

P\times M\longrightarrow M,~~~~~~~(p,m)\mapsto{}^{p}m,

(1)

{}^{[p,p^{\prime}]}m={}^{p}({}^{p^{\prime}}m)-(-1)^{|p||p^{\prime}|}~{}^{p^{\prime}}({}^{p}m),

(2)

{}^{p}{[m,m^{\prime}]}=[{}^{p}m,m^{\prime}]+(-1)^{|p||m|}[m,{}^{p}m^{\prime}],

for all homogeneous
p,p^{\prime}\in P
and
m,m^{\prime}\in M
. For any Lie superalgebra
M

{}^{m}m^{\prime}=[m,m^{\prime}]
. The action of
P

M

{}^{p}m=0

Given two Lie superalgebras

and
P

with underlying supermodule

[(m,p),(m^{\prime},p^{\prime})]=([m,m^{\prime}]+{}^{p}m^{\prime}-(-1)^{|m||p^{\prime}|}({}^{p^{\prime}}m),[p,p^{\prime}])

\partial:M\longrightarrow P
with an action of
P

(1)

\partial({}^{p}{m})=[p,\partial(m)],

(2)

{}^{\partial(m)}{m^{\prime}}=[m,m^{\prime}],

m,m^{\prime}\in M
.

A bilinear function

(1)

f([m,m^{\prime}],n)=f(m,{}^{m^{\prime}}{n})-(-1)^{|m||m^{\prime}|}f(m^{\prime},{}^{m}{n})

\wedge^{2}H=\begin{cases}A(\frac{1}{2}((m-2)(m+1)+(n-2)(n-1)+2)\mid m(n-2)+2)\quad\mbox{if}\;\dim H^{2}=(1\mid 1)\\
A(\frac{1}{2}((m-4)(m-1)+n(n+1)+6)\mid(m-2)n)\quad\mbox{if}\;\dim H^{2}=(2\mid 0).\\
\end{cases}

\otimes^{2}H=\begin{cases}A(l+\frac{1}{2}m(m-1)\mid k+(m-1)(n-1))\quad\mbox{if}\;\dim H^{2}=(1\mid 1)\\
A((m-3)(m-1)+n^{2}+3\mid 2(m-2)n)\quad\mbox{if}\;\dim H^{2}=(2\mid 0),\\
\end{cases}

where
l=\frac{1}{2}((m-2)(m+1)+2(n-2)(n-1)+2),
and
k=m(n-2)+2.

(3)

If
Z^{\wedge}(H)=K\subseteq H^{2}
and
\dim K=(0\mid 1)
, then

\wedge^{2}H=\begin{cases}A(\frac{1}{2}((m-4)(m-1)+n(n+1)+6)\mid(m-2)n)\quad\mbox{if}\;\dim H^{2}=(1\mid 1)\\
A(\frac{1}{2}((m+2)(m-1)+(n-3)(n-2)+2)\mid(m+1)(n-3)+2)\quad\mbox{if}\;\dim H^{2}=(0\mid 2).\\
\end{cases}

\otimes^{2}H=\begin{cases}A((m-2)(m-1)+n(n-1)+4\mid(m-1)(n-1)+(m-2)n)\quad\mbox{if}\;\dim H^{2}=(1\mid 1)\\
A(r+\frac{1}{2}m(m+1)\mid s+m(n-2))\quad\mbox{if}\;\dim H^{2}=(0\mid 2).\\
\end{cases}

where
r=\frac{1}{2}((m+2)(m-1)+2(n-3)(n-2)+2)
and
s=(m+1)(n-3)+2.

Proof.

From Lemma
3.7
, one can see that
\wedge^{2}H
is abelian and
\wedge^{2}H\cong\mathcal{M}(H)\oplus H^{2}
. Hence the results for exterior product of
H
follows from Lemma
4.1
. Now by using Lemma
3.12
, we have
(H/H^{2})\square(H/H^{2})\cong H\square H\cong\Gamma(H/H^{2})
, then

H\square H=\begin{cases}A(\frac{1}{2}m(m+1)+\frac{(n-2)(n-3)}{2}\mid m(n-2))\quad\mbox{if}\;\dim H^{2}=(0\mid 2)\\
A(\frac{1}{2}(m-1)(m-2)+\frac{n(n-1)}{2}\mid(m-2)n)\quad\mbox{if}\;\dim H^{2}=(2\mid 0)\\
A(\frac{1}{2}m(m-1)+\frac{(n-1)(n-2)}{2}\mid(m-1)(n-1))\quad\mbox{if}\;\dim H^{2}=(1\mid 1).\\
\end{cases}

As
\otimes^{2}H\cong H\wedge H\oplus H\square H
, thus the result follows.
∎

Now we determine the dimension and the structure of triple tensor product and exterior product of non-capable generalized Heisenberg Lie superalgebra of rank 2.

Theorem 4.8
.

H
be an

-dimensional non-capable generalized Heisenberg Lie superalgebra

Theorem 4.9
.

Let
L
be an
(m\mid n)
-dimensional non-abelian nilpotent Lie superalgebra with
derived subalgebra of dimension
(r\mid s)
. Then

\dim\otimes^{3}L\leq(m+n)(m+n-(r+s))^{2}.

In particular, for
r=1,s=0
the equality holds if and only if
L\cong H(1\mid 0)
.

Proof.

Suppose
L
is an
(m\mid n)
-dimesional non-abelian nilpotent Lie superalgebra of nilpotency class
k
. We prove by induction on
m+n
. Since
L
is non-abelian,
m+n\geq 2
, from
[
41
]
there is only one non-abelian nilpotent Lie superalgebra when
m+n=2
, i.e.,
L\cong H(0\mid 1)
. Now by Theorem
4.5
, we have
\otimes^{3}L\cong A(0\mid 1)
. Therefore the result holds. As
\gamma_{k}(L)
is central,
\gamma_{k}(L)
and
L
act trivially on each other and so by using Lemma
3.2
,
\gamma_{k}(L)\otimes L\cong\gamma_{k}(L)\otimes L^{ab}
and
(\varphi(L^{2})\otimes L)\otimes L\cong(\varphi(L^{2})\otimes L^{ab})\otimes L^{ab}
. Consider the case when
L/\gamma_{k}(L)
is abelian. Then
L
is a nilpotant Lie superalgebra of class two and
\gamma_{k}(L)=L^{2}
. Thus
\dim\otimes^{3}L/\gamma_{k}(L)=\dim\otimes^{3}L/L^{2}=(m-r\mid n-s)^{3}
. By using Lemma
4.4
, we have

\displaystyle\dim\otimes^{3}L

\displaystyle\leq\dim\otimes^{3}(L/\gamma_{k}(L))+\dim((\gamma_{k}(L)\otimes L)\otimes L)

\displaystyle=(m-r\mid n-s)^{3}+(r\mid s)\times(m-r\mid n-s)^{2}

\displaystyle=(m\mid n)\times(m-r\mid n-s)^{2}

\displaystyle=(m+n)(m+n-(r+s))^{2}.

Now if
L/\gamma_{k}(L)
is non-abelian, then
\dim L/\gamma_{k}(L)=m+n-\dim\gamma_{k}(L)
and
\dim(L/\gamma_{k}(L))^{2}=r+s-\dim\gamma_{k}(L)
, thus by the induction hypothesis

(4.3)

\displaystyle\dim\otimes^{3}(L/\gamma_{k}(L))\leq(m+n-\dim\gamma_{k}(L))(m+n-(r+s))^{2}.

Lemma
4.4
and
4.3
imply that

\displaystyle\dim\otimes^{3}L

\displaystyle\leq\dim\otimes^{3}(L/\gamma_{k}(L))+\dim((\gamma_{k}(L)\otimes L)\otimes L)

\displaystyle\leq(m+n-\dim\gamma_{k}(L)(m+n-(r+s))^{2}+\dim\gamma_{k}(L)(m+n-(r+s))^{2}

\displaystyle=(m+n)(m+n-(r+s))^{2}.

Let
r=1,s=0
. Then from Lemma
3.8
, we have
L\cong H(k\mid l)\oplus A(m-2k-1\mid n-l)
for all
k+l\geq 1
. By using Theorem
4.5
, the equality holds if and only if
L\cong H(1\mid 0)
.
∎
