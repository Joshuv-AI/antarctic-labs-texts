Quantum f(R) gravity and AdS/CFT
M. Bousder

Abstract
We propose to study the entanglement entropy in braneworld modified gravity. We show that the d + 1 d+1 -dimensional action I d + 1 I_{d+1} is the dual of d − 1 d-1 -dimensional entanglement entropy S d − 1 E ​ E S_{d-1}^{EE} . Moreover, we remark that the generalization of action-entropy shows us a new form of f ⁡ ( R ) f(R) gravity which is in good agreement with the most choices of f ⁡ ( R ) f(R) gravity in the literature. We show also that there are two copies of the AdS spaces in holographic entanglement f ⁡ ( R ) f(R) gravity. We have proposed that the time is a holographic projection of the hidden d d -brane on the visible d d -brane. To determine the geometry of this holographic projection, we have used the A ​ d ​ S 2 AdS_{2} geometry of the black hole. Namely, the past of events is memorized over an 𝕊 1 \mathbb{S}^{1} . We show that the time projection is a stereographic projection from a time sphere 𝕊 d − 2 \mathbb{S}^{d-2} on the hidden d d -brane to the visible d d -brane. We suggest that there is a difference between the time in classical gravity and the time in quantum gravity.
