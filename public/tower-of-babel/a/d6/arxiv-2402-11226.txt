the n + 3 n+3 , n + 4 n+4 dimensional homotopy groups of 𝐀 n 2 \mathbf{A}_{n}^{2} -complexes
Tian Jin, Zhongjian Zhu

Abstract
. In this paper, we calculate the n + 3 n+3 , n + 4 n+4 dimensional homotopy groups of indecomposable 𝐀 n 2 \mathbf{A}_{n}^{2} -complexes after localization at 2. This job is seen as a sequel to P.J. Hilton’s work on the n + 1 , n + 2 n+1,n+2 dimensional homotopy groups of 𝐀 n 2 \mathbf{A}_{n}^{2} -complexes (1950-1951). The main technique used is analysing the homotopy property of J ⁡ ( X , A ) J(X,A) , defined by B. Gray for a CW-pair ( X , A ) (X,A) , which is homotopy equivalent to the homotopy fibre of the pinch map X ∪ C ​ A → Σ ​ A X\cup CA\rightarrow\Sigma A . By the way, the results of these homotopy groups have been used to make progress on recent popular topic about the homotopy decomposition of the (multiple) suspension of oriented closed manifolds.
