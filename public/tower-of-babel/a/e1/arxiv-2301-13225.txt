An infinite family of w 1 + ∞ w_{1+\infty} invariant theories on the celestial sphere
Shamik Banerjee, Harshal Kulkarni, Partha Paul

Abstract
In this note we determine the graviton-graviton OPE and the null states in any w 1 + ∞ w_{1+\infty} symmetric theory on the celestial sphere. Our analysis shows that there exists a discrete infinite family of such theories. The MHV-sector and the quantum self dual gravity are two members of this infinite family. Although the Bulk Lagrangian description of this family of theories is not currently known to us, the graviton scattering amplitudes in these theories are heavily constrained due to the existence of null states. Presumably they are exactly solvable in the same way as the minimal models of 2 2 -D CFT.

VIII
Knizhnik-Zamolodchikov type null states

KZ type null states occur at
\mathcal{O}(z^{0}\bar{z}^{1})
of the OPE. The simplest way to derive it is to demand the commutativity of the OPE, i.e,

If we use the consistency condition (

\boxed{\Xi_{n}(\Delta)=\xi(\Delta)+\sum_{k=1}^{n}\Pi_{k}(\Delta+1)=0}

\xi(\Delta)=\boxed{L_{-1}}G^{+}_{\Delta}+H^{0}_{0,-1}H^{1}_{-\frac{3}{2},\frac{1}{2}}G^{+}_{\Delta-1}+H^{0}_{-1,0}G^{+}_{\Delta}+(\Delta-1)\,H^{1}_{-\frac{3}{2},-\frac{1}{2}}G^{+}_{\Delta-1}

As consistency checks we can compute the actions of
