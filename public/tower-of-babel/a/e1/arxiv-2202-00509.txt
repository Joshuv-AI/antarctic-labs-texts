Abstract
This paper studies decentralized convex-concave minimax optimization problems of the form min x ⁡ max y ⁡ f ⁡ ( x , y ) ≜ 1 m ​ ∑ i = 1 m f i ​ ( x , y ) \min_{x}\max_{y}f(x,y)\triangleq\frac{1}{m}\sum_{i=1}^{m}f_{i}(x,y) , where m m is the number of agents and each local function can be written as f i ​ ( x , y ) = 1 n ​ ∑ j = 1 n f i , j ​ ( x , y ) f_{i}(x,y)=\frac{1}{n}\sum_{j=1}^{n}f_{i,j}(x,y) . We study the following decentralized minimax optimization problem of the form

\displaystyle\min_{x\in{\mathcal{X}}}\max_{y\in{\mathcal{Y}}}f(x,y)\triangleq\frac{1}{m}\sum_{i=1}^{m}f_{i}(x,y),

\displaystyle f_{i}(x,y)=\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}f_{i,j}(x,y).

Our task is finding the saddle point of

\displaystyle f(x^{*},y)\leq f(x^{*},y^{*})\leq f(x,y^{*})

We consider the following assumptions for problem (

\displaystyle\left\|\nabla f_{i,j}(x,y)-\nabla f_{i,j}(x^{\prime},y^{\prime})\right\|^{2}\leq L^{2}\big(\left\|x-x^{\prime}\right\|^{2}+\left\|y-y^{\prime}\right\|^{2}\big).

The extragradient (EG) method

\displaystyle\begin{cases}z_{t+1/2}={\mathcal{P}}_{\mathcal{Z}}(z_{t}-\eta_{1}g(z_{t})),\\

Mukherjee and Chakraborty 2020
combined the idea of EG gradient with gradient tracking and proposed GT-Extragradient (GT-EG) algorithm for unconstrained decentralized minimax optimization problem (
1
). The update rule of GE-EG has the following compact form

\displaystyle\begin{cases}{\bf{z}}_{t+1/2}={\bf{z}}_{t}-\eta_{2}{\bf{s}}_{t},\\
{\bf{s}}_{t+1/2}={\bf{s}}_{t}+{\bf{g}}({\bf{z}}_{t+1/2})-{\bf{g}}({\bf{z}}_{t})\\
{\bf{z}}_{t+1}=W{\bf{z}}_{t}-\eta_{2}{\bf{s}}_{t+1/2},\\
{\bf{s}}_{t+1}=W{\bf{s}}_{t}+{\bf{g}}(z_{t+1})-{\bf{g}}({\bf{z}}_{t}),\end{cases}

where
\eta_{2}=\Theta\big(\big(\frac{1}{\kappa\chi^{2}L}\vee\frac{1}{\kappa^{1/3}\chi^{4/3}L}\big)\wedge\frac{1}{\chi^{2}L}\big)
.

6.1

AUC maximization
[

,
49

is targeted to find the binary classifier
\theta\in{\mathbb{R}}^{d}

\{(a_{i,j},b_{i,j})\}_{i,j}
with

a_{i,j}\in{\mathbb{R}}^{d}
,
b_{i,j}\in\{+1,-1\}
,
i=1,\dots,m
and
j=1,\dots,n

We denote
N^{+}
be the numbers of positive and negative instances and let
q=N^{+}/(mn)
. The unconstrained minimax formulation for this model is

\displaystyle\min_{x\in{\mathbb{R}}^{d+2}}\max_{y\in{\mathbb{R}}}f(x,y)\triangleq\frac{1}{mn}\sum_{i=1}^{m}\sum_{j=1}^{n}f_{i,j}(x,y;a_{i,j},b_{i,j},\lambda),

where

,
\lambda
is the regularization parameter and each component function
f_{i,j}(x,y;a_{i,j},b_{i,j},\lambda)
is defined as

\displaystyle f_{i,j}(x,y;a_{i,j},b_{i,j},\lambda)\triangleq

\displaystyle\frac{\lambda}{2}\left\|x\right\|_{2}^{2}-q(1-q)y^{2}+(1-q)\left((\theta^{\top}a_{i,j}-u)^{2}-2(1+y)\theta^{\top}a_{i,j}\right){\mathbb{I}}_{\{b_{i,j}=1\}}

\displaystyle+q\left((\theta^{\top}a_{i,j}-v)^{2}+2(1+y)\theta^{\top}a_{i,j}\right){\mathbb{I}}_{\{b_{i,j}=-1\}}.

We set
\lambda=0.01
for our experiment.

\left\{(a_{i,j},b_{i,j})\right\}_{i,j}
with

The constrained minimax formulation for this model is

\displaystyle\min_{x\in{\mathcal{X}}}\max_{y\in{{\mathcal{Y}}}}f(x,y)\triangleq\frac{1}{mn}\sum_{i=1}^{m}\sum_{j=1}^{n}f_{i,j}(x,y),

6.3
Experimental Results and Discussion

We report our experimental results in Figure
1
and
2
.
We observe that MC-EG outperforms GTEG since GTEG lacks the multi-consensus step and each agent only communicates with its neighbors only once for per update of the variable.
At the same time, the results show that MC-EG is both more communication efficient and more computation efficient than DESM.
This is because of DESM does not include the gradient-tracking step, leading to the algorithm requires more communication rounds when it tries to obtain a high precision solution.
On the other hand, the comparisons show that MC-SVRE achieves the lowest computation cost among all algorithms, but it commonly requires more communication cost than MC-EG, which implies MC-SVRE is suitable for computation sensitive cases. All of these empirical results validates our theoretical analysis.

7
Conclusion

In this paper, we have proposed a variance reduced extragradient method for decentralized convex-concave minimax optimization.
We prove the algorithm achieves best known SFO complexity in theoretical.
We also provide a deterministic variant for this method, which is more communication efficient.
The numerical experiments on machine learning applications of AUC maximization and distributionally robust optimization validate the effectiveness of proposed algorithms.
It would be interesting to extend our algorithms to more general case, such as nonconvex-concave or nonconvex-nonconcave minimax optimization problems.

Appendix A
Some Useful Lemmas

We first provide some useful lemmas will be used in the analysis of MC-SVRE and MC-EG.

Lemma A.1
.

For any
a_{1},\dots,a_{m}\in{\mathbb{R}}^{d}
, we have

\displaystyle\left\|\frac{1}{m}\sum_{i=1}^{m}a_{i}\right\|^{2}\leq\frac{1}{m}\sum_{i=1}^{m}\left\|a_{i}\right\|^{2}.

Proof.

We have

\displaystyle\frac{1}{m}\sum_{i=1}^{m}\left\|a_{i}\right\|^{2}-\left\|\frac{1}{m}\sum_{i=1}^{m}a_{i}\right\|^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{m^{2}}\left(m\sum_{i=1}^{m}\left\|a_{i}\right\|^{2}-\sum_{i=1}^{m}\sum_{j=1}^{m}\langle a_{i},a_{j}\rangle\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{m^{2}}\left((m-1)\sum_{i=1}^{m}\left\|a_{i}\right\|^{2}-2\sum_{i\neq j}\langle a_{i},a_{j}\rangle\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{m^{2}}\left((m-1)\sum_{i=1}^{m}\left\|a_{i}\right\|^{2}-2\sum_{i\neq j}\langle a_{i},a_{j}\rangle\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{m^{2}}\sum_{i\neq j}\left\|a_{i}-a_{j}\right\|_{2}^{2}\geq 0.

∎

Lemma A.2

(
Ye et al. 2020a
)
.

For any matrix

\left\|{\bf{z}}-{\bf{1}}{{\bar{z}}}\right\|\leq\left\|{\bf{z}}\right\|
where

.

Lemma A.3
.

\left\|{\bf{g}}({\bf{z}})-{\bf{g}}({\bf{z}}^{\prime})\right\|\leq L\left\|{\bf{z}}-{\bf{z}}^{\prime}\right\|

Proof.

means

&\geq 2\eta{\mathbb{E}}_{t}\left[\langle{{\bar{g}}}({{\bar{z}}}_{t+1/2}),{{\bar{z}}}_{t+1/2}-{{\bar{z}}}^{*}\rangle\right],\end{split}

(20)

Using the update rule of
{\bf{w}}_{t}
, we have

\displaystyle{\mathbb{E}}_{t}\left\|{{\bar{w}}}_{t+1}-{{\bar{z}}}^{*}\right\|^{2}=(1-p){\mathbb{E}}_{t}\left\|{{\bar{w}}}_{t}-{{\bar{z}}}^{*}\right\|^{2}+p{\mathbb{E}}_{t}\left\|{{\bar{z}}}_{t+1}-{{\bar{z}}}^{*}\right\|^{2}.

(21)

Connecting the inequalities (
20
) and (
21
) implies

\displaystyle 2\eta{\mathbb{E}}_{t}\langle{{\bar{g}}}({{\bar{z}}}_{t+1/2}),{{\bar{z}}}_{t+1/2}-{{\bar{z}}}^{*}\rangle+{\mathbb{E}}_{t}\left\|{{\bar{w}}}_{t+1}-{{\bar{z}}}^{*}\right\|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle(1-p)\left\|{{\bar{z}}}_{t}-{{\bar{z}}}^{*}\right\|^{2}-{\mathbb{E}}_{t}\left\|{{\bar{z}}}_{t+1}-{{\bar{z}}}^{*}\right\|^{2}+p\left\|{{\bar{w}}}_{t}-{{\bar{z}}}^{*}\right\|^{2}+\frac{\eta}{\beta}\left\|{{\bar{z}}}_{t+1/2}-{{\bar{z}}}^{*}\right\|^{2}+\beta\eta\left\|{\mathbb{E}}_{t}[{{\bar{s}}}_{t+1/2}]-g({{\bar{z}}}_{t+1/2})\right\|^{2}

\displaystyle+\frac{6\eta^{2}L^{2}}{m}{\mathbb{E}}_{t}\left\|{\bf{z}}_{t+1/2}-{\bf{1}}{{\bar{z}}}_{t+1/2}\right\|^{2}+\frac{6\eta^{2}L^{2}}{m}{\mathbb{E}}_{t}\left\|{\bf{w}}_{t}-{\bf{1}}{{\bar{w}}}_{t}\right\|^{2}

\displaystyle+{\mathbb{E}}_{t}\left[\langle{{\bar{z}}}_{t+1}-{{\bar{z}}}^{\prime}_{t}+\eta{{\bar{s}}}_{t+1/2}+{{\bar{z}}}_{t+1}-{{\bar{z}}}^{*},\Delta_{t+1/2}\rangle+\langle{{\bar{z}}}_{t+1/2}-{{\bar{z}}}^{\prime}_{t}+\eta{{\bar{s}}}_{t}+{{\bar{z}}}_{t+1/2}-{{\bar{z}}}_{t+1},\Delta_{t}\rangle\right]

\displaystyle+p\left\|{{\bar{z}}}_{t+1}-{{\bar{z}}}^{*}\right\|^{2}+(1-p)\left\|{{\bar{w}}}_{t}-{{\bar{z}}}^{*}\right\|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle(1-p)\left\|{{\bar{z}}}_{t}-{{\bar{z}}}^{*}\right\|^{2}-{\mathbb{E}}_{t}\left\|{{\bar{z}}}_{t+1}-{{\bar{z}}}^{*}\right\|^{2}+p\left\|{{\bar{w}}}_{t}-{{\bar{z}}}^{*}\right\|^{2}+\frac{\eta D^{2}}{\beta}+\frac{\beta L^{2}\eta}{m}\left\|{\bf{z}}_{t+1/2}-{\bf{1}}{{\bar{z}}}_{t+1/2}\right\|^{2}

\displaystyle+\frac{6\eta^{2}L^{2}}{m}{\mathbb{E}}_{t}\left\|{\bf{z}}_{t+1/2}-{\bf{1}}{{\bar{z}}}_{t+1/2}\right\|^{2}+\frac{6\eta^{2}L^{2}}{m}{\mathbb{E}}_{t}\left\|{\bf{w}}_{t}-{\bf{1}}{{\bar{w}}}_{t}\right\|^{2}

\displaystyle+{\mathbb{E}}_{t}\left[\left\|{{\bar{z}}}_{t+1}-{{\bar{z}}}^{\prime}_{t}+\eta{{\bar{s}}}_{t+1/2}+{{\bar{z}}}_{t+1}-{{\bar{z}}}^{*}\right\|\left\|\Delta_{t+1/2}\right\|+\left\|{{\bar{z}}}_{t+1/2}-{{\bar{z}}}^{\prime}_{t}+\eta{{\bar{s}}}_{t}+{{\bar{z}}}_{t+1/2}-{{\bar{z}}}_{t+1}\right\|\left\|\Delta_{t}\right\|\right]

\displaystyle+p\left\|{{\bar{z}}}_{t+1}-{{\bar{z}}}^{*}\right\|^{2}+(1-p)\left\|{{\bar{w}}}_{t}-{{\bar{z}}}^{*}\right\|^{2},

that is

\displaystyle\begin{split}&2\eta{\mathbb{E}}_{t}\langle{{\bar{g}}}({{\bar{z}}}_{t+1/2}),{{\bar{z}}}_{t+1/2}-{{\bar{z}}}^{*}\rangle\\
\leq&(1-p)\left(\left\|{{\bar{z}}}_{t}-{{\bar{z}}}^{*}\right\|^{2}-{\mathbb{E}}_{t}\left\|{{\bar{z}}}_{t+1}-{{\bar{z}}}^{*}\right\|^{2}\right)+\left(\left\|{{\bar{w}}}_{t}-{{\bar{z}}}^{*}\right\|^{2}-{\mathbb{E}}_{t}\left\|{{\bar{w}}}_{t+1}-{{\bar{z}}}^{*}\right\|^{2}\right)+\zeta^{\prime}_{t}\end{split}

(22)

where

\displaystyle\zeta^{\prime}_{t}=

\displaystyle\frac{\eta D^{2}}{\beta}+\frac{6(\eta^{2}+\beta\eta)L^{2}}{m}{\mathbb{E}}_{t}\left\|{\bf{z}}_{t+1/2}-{\bf{1}}{{\bar{z}}}_{t+1/2}\right\|^{2}+\frac{6\eta^{2}L^{2}}{m}{\mathbb{E}}_{t}\left\|{\bf{w}}_{t}-{\bf{1}}{{\bar{w}}}_{t}\right\|^{2}

\displaystyle+{\mathbb{E}}_{t}\left[\left\|{{\bar{z}}}_{t+1}-{{\bar{z}}}^{\prime}_{t}+\eta{{\bar{s}}}_{t+1/2}+{{\bar{z}}}_{t+1}-{{\bar{z}}}^{*}\right\|\left\|\Delta_{t+1/2}\right\|+\left\|{{\bar{z}}}_{t+1/2}-{{\bar{z}}}^{\prime}_{t}+\eta{{\bar{s}}}_{t}+{{\bar{z}}}_{t+1/2}-{{\bar{z}}}_{t+1}\right\|\left\|\Delta_{t}\right\|\right]

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{\eta D^{2}}{\beta}+\frac{6(2\eta^{2}+\beta\eta)L^{2}\delta^{\prime 2}}{m}+\frac{6\left(C_{1}+C_{1/2}\right)\delta^{\prime}}{\sqrt{m}}.

The upper bound of
\zeta^{\prime}_{t}
follows the proof of Lemma
4.6
.

Summing over (
22
) with
t=0,\dots,T-1
, we obtain

\displaystyle\begin{split}&{\mathbb{E}}_{t}\left[f(\hat{x},y^{*})-f(x^{*},\hat{y})\right]\\
\leq&\frac{1}{T}\sum_{t=0}^{T-1}{\mathbb{E}}_{t}\left[f({{\bar{x}}}_{t+1/2},y^{*})-f(x^{*},{{\bar{y}}}_{t+1/2})\right]\\
\leq&\frac{1}{T}\sum_{t=0}^{T-1}{\mathbb{E}}_{t}\langle g({{\bar{z}}}_{t+1/2}),{{\bar{z}}}_{t+1/2}-{{\bar{z}}}^{*}\rangle\\
\leq&\frac{(1-p)\left(\left\|{{\bar{z}}}_{0}-{{\bar{z}}}^{*}\right\|^{2}-{\mathbb{E}}_{T}\left\|{{\bar{z}}}_{T}-{{\bar{z}}}^{*}\right\|^{2}\right)+\left\|{{\bar{w}}}_{0}-{{\bar{z}}}^{*}\right\|^{2}-{\mathbb{E}}_{T}\left\|{{\bar{w}}}_{T}-{{\bar{z}}}^{*}\right\|^{2}+\sum_{t=0}^{T-1}\zeta^{\prime}_{t}}{2\eta T}\\
\leq&\frac{\left\|{{\bar{z}}}_{0}-{{\bar{z}}}^{*}\right\|^{2}}{\eta T}+\frac{1}{2\eta}\left(\frac{\eta D^{2}}{\beta}+\frac{6(2\eta^{2}+\beta\eta)L^{2}\delta^{\prime 2}}{m}+\frac{6\left(C_{1}+C_{1/2}\right)\delta^{\prime}}{\sqrt{m}}\right)\\
\leq&\frac{{\varepsilon}}{2}+\frac{{\varepsilon}}{4}+\frac{{\varepsilon}}{8}+\frac{{\varepsilon}}{8}={\varepsilon}.\end{split}

where the first inequality use Jensen’s inequality; the second inequality use the objective function is convex-concave; the third inequality use the upper bound of
\zeta^{\prime}_{t}
; and the last one is based on the value of parameter settings.

Since each iteration requires
1+np={\mathcal{O}}(1)
SFO calls in expectation and we the algorithm needs to compute the full gradient at first, the total SFO complexity is

\displaystyle{\mathcal{O}}(n+(1+np)T)={\mathcal{O}}\left(n+\frac{\sqrt{n}L}{{\varepsilon}}\right)

in expectation. The number of communication rounds is

\displaystyle KT+K_{0}={\mathcal{O}}\left(\frac{\sqrt{n\chi}L}{{\varepsilon}}\log\left(\frac{nL}{{\varepsilon}}\right)\right).

∎
