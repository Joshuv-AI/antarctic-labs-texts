Spectra of glueballs and oddballs and the equation of state from holographic QCD
Lin Zhang, Chutian Chen, Yidian Chen, and Mei Huang

Abstract
We study the spectra of two-gluon glueballs and three-gluon oddballs and corresponding equation of state in 5 5 -dimensional deformed holographic QCD models in the graviton-dilaton system, where the metric, the dilaton field and dilaton potential are self-consistently solved from each other through the Einstein field equations and the equation of motion of the dilaton field. We compare the models by inputting the dilaton field, inputting the deformed metric and inputting the dilaton potential, and find that with only 2 parameters, the 5 5 -dimensional holographic QCD model predictions on glueballs/oddballs spectra in general are in good agreement with lattice results except two oddballs 0 + ⁣ − 0^{+-} and 2 + ⁣ − 2^{+-} . From the results of glueballs/oddballs spectra at zero temperature and the equation of state at finite temperature, we observe that the model with quadratic dilaton field can simultaneously describe glueballs/oddballs spectra as well as equation of state of pure gluon system. The model with quadratic A E ​ ( z ) A_{E}(z) can describe glueballs/oddballs spectra, but its corresponding equation of state behaves more like N f = 2 + 1 N_{f}=2+1 quark matter. These are consistent with dimension analysis at UV boundary.

\displaystyle j_{\alpha}^{1^{-+},D}(x)=g_{s}^{3}f^{abc}\partial_{\mu}\left[\tilde{G}_{\mu\nu}^{a}(x)\right]\left[\tilde{G}_{v\rho}^{b}(x)\right]\left[G_{\rho\alpha}^{c}(x)\right],

(72)

and

\displaystyle j_{\mu\alpha}^{2^{+-},A}(x)=g_{s}^{3}d^{abc}\left[G_{\mu\nu}^{a}(x)\right]\left[G_{v\rho}^{b}(x)\right]\left[G_{\rho\alpha}^{c}(x)\right],

\displaystyle j_{\mu\alpha}^{2^{+-},B}(x)=g_{s}^{3}d^{abc}\left[G_{\mu\nu}^{a}(x)\right]\left[\tilde{G}_{v\rho}^{b}(x)\right]\left[\tilde{G}_{\rho\alpha}^{c}(x)\right],

\displaystyle j_{\mu\alpha}^{2^{+-},C}(x)=g_{s}^{3}d^{abc}\left[\tilde{G}_{\mu\nu}^{a}(x)\right]\left[G_{v\rho}^{b}(x)\right]\left[\tilde{G}_{\rho\alpha}^{c}(x)\right],

4.2
Equation of motion for scalar, vector and tensor glueballs/oddballs

From the
5
-dimensional action for the glueball/oddball in the string frame Eq. (
65
), we can derive the equation of motion for the glueballs.
The equation of motion for the scalar glueballs
\mathscr{S}
is given as:

\displaystyle-z^{3}{\mathrm{e}}^{-(3A_{s}-p\Phi)}\partial_{z}\left[\frac{1}{z^{3}}{\mathrm{e}}^{3A_{s}-p\Phi}\partial_{z}{\mathscr{S}_{n}}\right]

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{z^{2}}{\mathrm{e}}^{2A_{s}}\mathrm{e}^{-c_{\text{r.m.}}\Phi}M_{\mathscr{S},5}^{2}{\mathscr{S}_{n}}=m_{\mathscr{S},n}^{2}{\mathscr{S}_{n}}.

(74)

Via the substitution

\displaystyle\mathscr{S}_{n}\rightarrow z^{\frac{3}{2}}\mathrm{e}^{-\frac{1}{2}(3A_{s}-p\Phi)}\mathscr{S}_{n},

(75)

the equation can be brought into Schrödinger-like equation

\displaystyle-{\mathscr{S}_{n}}^{{}^{\prime\prime}}+V_{\mathscr{S}}{\mathscr{S}_{n}}=m_{\mathscr{G},n}^{2}{\mathscr{S}_{n}},

(76)

with the
5
-dimensional effective Schrödinger potential

V_{\mathscr{S}}=\frac{3A_{s}^{{}^{\prime\prime}}+\frac{3}{z^{2}}-p\Phi^{{}^{\prime\prime}}}{2}+\frac{\left[3A_{s}^{{}^{\prime}}-\frac{3}{z}-p\Phi^{{}^{\prime}}\right]^{2}}{4}+\frac{1}{z^{2}}e^{2A_{s}}\mathrm{e}^{-c_{\text{r.m.}}\Phi}M_{\mathscr{S},5}^{2}.

(77)

The equation of motion for the vector glueballs
\mathscr{V}_{M}
is given as:

\displaystyle-z\mathrm{e}^{-(A_{s}-p\Phi)}\partial_{z}\left[\frac{1}{z}\mathrm{e}^{A_{s}-p\Phi}\partial_{z}\mathscr{V}_{n}\right]+\frac{1}{z^{2}}\mathrm{e}^{2A_{s}}\mathrm{e}^{-c_{\text{r.m.}}\Phi}M_{\mathscr{V},5}^{2}\mathscr{V}_{n}=m_{\mathscr{V},n}^{2}\mathscr{V}_{n}.

(78)

Via the substitution

\displaystyle\mathscr{V}_{n}\rightarrow z^{\frac{1}{2}}e^{-\frac{1}{2}(A_{s}-p\Phi)}\mathscr{V}_{n},

(79)

the equation can be brought into Schrödinger-like equation

\displaystyle-\mathscr{V}_{n}^{{}^{\prime\prime}}+V_{\mathscr{V}}\mathscr{V}_{n}=m_{\mathscr{V},n}^{2}\mathscr{V}_{n},

(80)

with the
5
-dimensional effective Schrödinger potential

V_{\mathscr{V}}=\frac{A_{s}^{{}^{\prime\prime}}+\frac{1}{z^{2}}-p\Phi^{{}^{\prime\prime}}}{2}+\frac{\left[A_{s}^{{}^{\prime}}-\frac{1}{z}-p\Phi^{{}^{\prime}}\right]^{2}}{4}+\frac{1}{z^{2}}\mathrm{e}^{2A_{s}}\mathrm{e}^{-c_{\text{r.m.}}\Phi}M_{\mathscr{V},5}^{2}.

(81)

The equation of motion for the spin-2 glueballs
\mathscr{T}_{MN}
is given as

\displaystyle-z^{3}\mathrm{e}^{-(3A_{s}-p\Phi)}\partial_{z}\left[\frac{1}{z^{3}}\mathrm{e}^{3A_{s}-p\Phi}\partial_{z}\mathscr{T}_{n}\right]+\frac{1}{z^{2}}\mathrm{e}^{2A_{s}}\mathrm{e}^{-c_{\text{r.m.}}\Phi}M_{\mathscr{T},5}^{2}\mathscr{T}_{n}=m_{\mathscr{T},n}^{2}\mathscr{T}_{n}.

(82)

Via the substitution

\displaystyle\mathscr{T}_{n}\rightarrow z^{\frac{3}{2}}\mathrm{e}^{-\frac{1}{2}(3A_{s}-p\Phi)}\mathscr{T}_{n},

(83)

the equation can be brought into Schrödinger-like equation

\displaystyle-\mathscr{T}_{n}^{{}^{\prime\prime}}+V_{\mathscr{T}}\mathscr{T}_{n}=m_{\mathscr{T},n}^{2}\mathscr{T}_{n},

(84)

with the
5
-dimensional effective Schrödinger potential

V_{\mathscr{T}}=\frac{3A_{s}^{{}^{\prime\prime}}+\frac{3}{z^{2}}-p\Phi^{{}^{\prime\prime}}}{2}+\frac{\left[3A_{s}^{{}^{\prime}}-\frac{3}{z}-p\Phi^{{}^{\prime}}\right]^{2}}{4}+\frac{1}{z^{2}}\mathrm{e}^{2A_{s}}\mathrm{e}^{-c_{\text{r.m.}}\Phi}M_{\mathscr{T},5}^{2}.

(85)

According to Ref.
Karch:2006pv
, the equation of motion for the high spin glueballs
\mathscr{H}_{M_{1}M_{2}\cdots M_{S}}
, the spin
S
of which are larger than
2
, is given as

\displaystyle-z^{2S-1}\mathrm{e}^{-\left[\left(2S-1\right)A_{s}-p\Phi\right]}\partial_{z}\left[\frac{1}{z^{2S-1}}\mathrm{e}^{\left(2S-1\right)A_{s}-p\Phi}\partial_{z}\mathscr{H}_{n}\right]

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{z^{2}}\mathrm{e}^{2A_{s}}\mathrm{e}^{-c_{\text{r.m.}}\Phi}M_{\mathscr{H},5}^{2}\mathscr{H}_{n}=m_{\mathscr{H},n}^{2}\mathscr{H}_{n},

(86)

where
S\geqslant 3
.
Via the substitution

\displaystyle\mathscr{H}_{n}\rightarrow z^{\frac{2S-1}{2}}\mathrm{e}^{-\frac{1}{2}\left[\left(2S-1\right)A_{s}-p\Phi\right]}\mathscr{H}_{n},

(87)

the equation can be brought into Schrödinger-like equation

\displaystyle-\mathscr{H}_{n}^{{}^{\prime\prime}}+V_{\mathscr{H}}\mathscr{H}_{n}=m_{\mathscr{H},n}^{2}\mathscr{H}_{n},

(88)

with the
5
-dimensional effective Schrödinger potential

V_{\mathscr{H}}=\frac{\left(2S-1\right)A_{s}^{{}^{\prime\prime}}+\frac{2S-1}{z^{2}}-p\Phi^{{}^{\prime\prime}}}{2}+\frac{\left[\left(2S-1\right)A_{s}^{{}^{\prime}}-\frac{2S-1}{z}-p\Phi^{{}^{\prime}}\right]^{2}}{4}+\frac{1}{z^{2}}\mathrm{e}^{2A_{s}}\mathrm{e}^{-c_{\text{r.m.}}\Phi}M_{\mathscr{H},5}^{2}.

(89)

6
Conclusion and discussion

In this work, we study scalar, vector and tensor glueballs/oddballs spectra in the framework of
5
-dimensional dynamical holographic QCD model, where the metric structure is deformed self-consistently by the dilaton field. In the framework of holography, the states
J^{PC}
with the same angular momentum
J
and the same C-parity share the same operator thus have the same dimension and
5
-dimensional mass, and the mass splitting for different P-parity states is realized by
\mathrm{e}^{-p\Phi}
in Eq. (
65
). The states
J^{PC}
with the same angular momentum
J
and the same P-parity but different C-parity have different operators. Thus, they have different
5
-dimensional masses, which naturally induces the mass splitting for different C-parity states.

From the results in Table
3
, Fig.
2
, and Fig.
3
, we can see that with only 2 parameters, the model predictions on glueballs/oddballs spectra in general are in good agreement with lattice results except two oddballs
0^{+-}
and
2^{+-}
. Here we also would like to mention that the data predicted by the SP and DP Regge model
Szanyi:2019kkn
to fit the high energy
pp
scattering: using the SP Regge model, the predicted mass for
2^{++}
glueball is
1.747\mathrm{GeV}
; using the DP Regge model, the predicted masses for
2^{++}
glueball and
3^{--}
oddall are
1.758\mathrm{GeV}
and
3.001\mathrm{GeV}
respectively. These predicted values are a little bit lower than the results predicted from holography but still in reasonable regions. It might indicate that the mass
1.747\mathrm{GeV}
/
1.758\mathrm{GeV}

2^{++}
glueball and mass
3.001\mathrm{GeV}

3^{--}
oddball are hybrid glueball/oddball states mixing with quark states.

From the results of glueballs/oddballs spectra at zero temperature and zero density and the equation of state at finite temperature, we obtain the following conclusions. 1) For the same set of vacuum solutions for the Einstein field equations and the equation of motion of the dilaton field
\phi(z)
, inputting the function
A_{E}(z)
and inputting the dilaton potential
V_{\phi}(\phi)
give different equation of state indeed. The difference between Model III and IV are much less than that between Model I and II. 2) The model with quadratic dilaton field
\phi(z)
can simultaneously describe glueballs/oddballs spectra as well as equation of state of pure gluon system. The model with quadratic
A_{E}(z)
can describe glueballs/oddballs spectra, but its corresponding equation of state behaves more like
N_{f}=2+1
quark matter. These are consistent with dimension analysis at UV boundary.
