Complete ADHM Sigma Model
Abbas Ali, P.P. Abdul Salih

Abstract
The field theoretic ADHM instanton has a stringy generalization as a linear sigma model constructed by Witten in 1995. Recently we constructed a complementary version related to it by a duality. In this note we generalize these to a complete ADHM instanton linear sigma model in which above duality is promoted to a Z 2 Z_{2} symmetry.

3
The (1,1) Instanton Case

To analyze the structure of the the moduli space in case of the original and the complementary models it is convenient to go to
(1,1)
instanton cases.
Here the single instantons from both original and complementary branches are present.

When we put
k=1
in the former case and
k^{\prime}=1
in the latter case to get
R^{4}
spacetime from both cases we realize that the minimal non-trivial construction lives on
R^{8}
. The resulting models have
F^{\prime}\times K\times SU(2)_{L}\times SU(2)_{R}\cong SU(2)^{4}
invariance in the original model sector and
F\times K^{\prime}\times SU(2)^{\prime}_{L}\times SU(2)^{\prime}_{R}\cong SU(2)^{4}
in the complementary model sector.

The resulting left moving massless fermions in the original model are
\lambda_{+}^{AY^{\prime}}
and
\lambda_{+}^{YY^{\prime}}
with the reality conditions

\displaystyle\lambda^{AY^{\prime}}_{+}=\epsilon^{AB}\epsilon^{Y^{\prime}Z^{\prime}}\overline{\lambda}_{+BZ^{\prime}},~

\displaystyle\lambda^{YY^{\prime}}_{+}=\epsilon^{YZ}\epsilon^{Y^{\prime}Z^{\prime}}\overline{\lambda}_{+ZZ^{\prime}}

(39)

while in the complementary case these are
\hat{\lambda}_{+}^{A^{\prime}Y}
and
\hat{\lambda}_{+}^{YY^{\prime}}
with reality conditions

\displaystyle\hat{\lambda}^{A^{\prime}Y}_{+}=\epsilon^{A^{\prime}B^{\prime}}\epsilon^{YZ}\overline{\hat{\lambda}}_{+B^{\prime}Z},~

\displaystyle\hat{\lambda}^{YY^{\prime}}_{+}=\epsilon^{YZ}\epsilon^{Y^{\prime}Z^{\prime}}\overline{\hat{\lambda}}_{+ZZ^{\prime}}.

(40)

The Yukawa couplings of the original model are

\displaystyle{C}^{YY^{\prime}}_{~~~~BB^{\prime}}=X_{B}^{~Y}\phi^{~Y^{\prime}}_{B^{\prime}},~

\displaystyle{C}^{AY^{\prime}}_{~~~~BB^{\prime}}=\frac{\rho}{\sqrt{2}}\delta^{A}_{~B}\phi^{~Y^{\prime}}_{B^{\prime}}

(41)

and of the complementary model are as follows

\displaystyle\hat{C}^{YY^{\prime}}_{~~~~BB^{\prime}}=X_{B}^{~Y}\phi^{~Y^{\prime}}_{B^{\prime}},~

\displaystyle\hat{C}^{A^{\prime}Y}_{~~~~BB^{\prime}}=\frac{\omega}{\sqrt{2}}\delta^{A^{\prime}}_{~B^{\prime}}X^{~Y}_{B}.

(42)

The resulting potentials for Witten’s original and our complementary model are

V_{W}=\frac{1}{8}(X^{2}+\rho^{2})\phi^{2},~~~V_{C}=\frac{1}{8}(\phi^{2}+\omega^{2})X^{2}

(43)

respectively. Using the fermionic field ansätz

\displaystyle\lambda^{YY^{\prime}}=\frac{\rho\zeta^{~YY^{\prime}}_{+}}{\sqrt{\rho^{2}+X^{2}}},~

\displaystyle\lambda^{AY^{\prime}}=-\frac{\sqrt{2}X^{A}_{~~Y}\zeta_{+}^{~YY^{\prime}}}{\sqrt{\rho^{2}+\phi^{2}}}.

(44)

for the original case and

\displaystyle\hat{\lambda}^{YY^{\prime}}=\frac{\omega\zeta^{~YY^{\prime}}_{+}}{\sqrt{\omega^{2}+\phi^{2}}},~

\displaystyle\hat{\lambda}^{A^{\prime}Y}=-\frac{\sqrt{2}\phi^{A^{\prime}}_{~~Y^{\prime}}\zeta_{+}^{~YY^{\prime}}}{\sqrt{\omega^{2}+\phi^{2}}}

(45)

for the complementary case, the instanton expressions take the forms

\displaystyle\lambda_{+YY^{\prime}}\partial_{-}\lambda^{YY^{\prime}}_{+}+\lambda_{+AY^{\prime}}\partial_{-}\lambda^{AY^{\prime}}_{+}=\zeta_{+YY^{\prime}}\partial_{-}\zeta^{~YY^{\prime}}_{+}-\zeta_{+YY^{\prime}}

\displaystyle\frac{\frac{1}{2}\epsilon_{AB}(X^{AY}\partial_{-}X^{BZ}+X^{AZ}\partial_{-}X^{BY})}{X^{2}+\rho^{2}}\zeta^{~~~Y^{\prime}}_{+Z}

(46)

and

\displaystyle\hat{\lambda}_{+YY^{\prime}}\partial_{-}\hat{\lambda}^{YY^{\prime}}_{+}+\hat{\lambda}_{+A^{\prime}Y}\partial_{-}\hat{\lambda}^{A^{\prime}Y}_{+}=\hat{\zeta}_{+YY^{\prime}}\partial_{-}\hat{\zeta}^{~YY^{\prime}}_{+}-\hat{\zeta}_{+YY^{\prime}}

\displaystyle\frac{\frac{1}{2}\epsilon_{A^{\prime}B^{\prime}}(\phi^{A^{\prime}Y^{\prime}}\partial_{-}\phi^{B^{\prime}Z^{\prime}}+\phi^{A^{\prime}Z^{\prime}}\partial_{-}\phi^{B^{\prime}Y^{\prime}})}{\phi^{2}+\omega^{2}}\hat{\zeta}^{Y}_{+Z^{\prime}}

(47)

for original and complementary branches respectively.

The total potential in case of double instanton is

V=V_{W}+V_{C}={1\over 8}\left(2X^{2}\phi^{2}+\rho^{2}\phi^{2}+\omega^{2}X^{2}\right).

(48)

The combined moduli space is obtained by minimizing above quadratic expression. The structure of the resulting moduli space can be discussed in conjuction with the two moduli spaces of the original and complementary models.

The structures of the two moduli spaces are the following. In the original model
\phi=0
gives the moduli space for all values of
X
. For vanishing instanton a new branch opens up because
V=\frac{1}{8}X^{2}\phi^{2}
is again zero for all values of
\phi
for
X=0
. This was called
\phi
branch in
[
9
]
and it was a mystery at the time and it continued to Ref.
[
18
]
.

The mystery is resolved if we analyze the moduli space for complementary branch. Expectedly the discussion of this branch of the moduli space is complementary.

In the complementary model
X=0
gives the moduli space for all values of
\phi
. In the small instanton case we again have
V=\frac{1}{8}X^{2}\phi^{2}
and we get another branch for
\phi=0
with all values of
X
. This would be called the
X
branch.

From this discussion it is clear that the extra branches are nothing but the moduli spaces of the other model because the two models become degenerate in the small instanton limit.

With the explanation of the origin of the additional branches the mystery is resloved.

The structure of the moduli space of the complete model in the double instanton case is the following. For the vanishing of one instanton, say
\rho\rightarrow 0
, we get the
\phi
branch for
X=0
and any
\phi
. Then there is another branch that appears for
\omega\rightarrow 0
,
\phi=0
and any
X
. The two branches are related by
X\leftrightarrow\phi
,
\rho\leftrightarrow\omega
. As a result the dual structure of the individual models appears as a
Z_{2}
symmetry. This
Z_{2}
symmetry is there for vanishing of one or the other instanton. It persists for vanishing of both instantons.

4
Discussion

The duality between the original and complementary constructions becomes a
Z_{2}
symmetry in the complete model. This is in consonance with what we expect from the infrared limit of the model.

In the infrared limit, or rather the conformal limit, both original and complementary models will have small N=4 superconformal symmetries
[
21
,
22
]
. The complete model will have large N=4 superconformal symmetry
[
Sevrin:188ab
,
24
,
25
,
26
,
27
]
in the conformal limit. We need better understanding of both of these limits.

The small N=4 superconformal symmetry has an
SU(2)
Kac-Moody subalgebra. This should be visible as an
SU(2)
R symmetry before the conformal limit. The original as well as the complementary models have a galore of
SU(2)
symmetries before the conformal limit. The supersymmetry structure in the present formulation is not entirely transparent. Construction of the complementary and complete ’t Hooft instantons, taking Callan-Harvey-Strominger as the starting original construction, should help in clarifying these issues because the structure of supersymmetry is much more clear in that case. These constructions should also help in clarifying the semi-infinite throat there and its counter part here. It would also be interesting to analyze in what limit the ADHM construction and its generalizations to higher groups reduce to ’t Hooft construction and corresponding generalizations.

The complete model should have two
SU(2)
R symmetries and these would be easy to identify once we identify the
SU(2)
R symmetries in the original and complementary models. The large N=4 superconformal algebra has an
SU(2)\times SU(2)\times U(1)
Kac-Moody subalgebra and hence at least in the superconformal limit we should be able to identify a
U(1)
symmetry in the complete model discussed in this note. Our speculation in this regard is the following. In the pair of scalars
(X,\phi)
we have two linear combinations and one of them is
U(1)
field while another one is a scalar field with background charge. Clearly these aspects need further clarifications. It will also be interesting to do Inönü-Wigner contraction of the complete model and reach the middle N=4 superconformal symmetry and corresponding ADHM linear sigma model
[
28
,
25
,
29
]
.

From the resulting structure it is apparent that the quantization of the original model discussed in Ref.
[
10
]
and the quantization of the complementary model discussed in Ref.
[
19
]
cover all that is needed for quantization of this model.
