Invariance of Schur multiplier, Bogomolov multiplier and the minimal number of generators under a variant of isoclinism
A. E. Antony, Sathasivam K, V.Z. Thomas

Abstract
We introduce the q q -Bogomolov multiplier as a generalization of the Bogomolov multiplier, and we prove that it is invariant under q q -isoclinism. We prove that the q q -Schur Multiplier is invariant under q q - exterior isoclinism, and as an easy consequence we prove that the Schur multiplier is invariant under exterior isoclinism. We also prove that if G G , H H are p p -groups and G / Z ∧ ​ ( G ) ≅ H / Z ∧ ​ ( H ) G/Z^{\wedge}(G)\cong H/Z^{\wedge}(H) , then the cardinality of the minimal number of generators of G G and H H are the same. Moreover we prove some structural results about q q -nonabelian tensor square of groups.
