Constructive Néron Desingularization of algebras with big smooth locus.
Zunaira Kosar, Gerhard Pfister and Dorin Popescu

Abstract
. An algorithmic proof of the General Néron Desingularization theorem and its uniform version is given for morphisms with big smooth locus. This generalizes the results for the one-dimensional case (cf. [ 10 ] , [ 7 ] ). Key words : Smooth morphisms, regular morphisms 2010 Mathematics Subject Classification: Primary 13B40, Secondary 14B25,13H05,13J15.
