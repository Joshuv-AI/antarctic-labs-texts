Periodic solution and asymptotic stability for the magnetohydrodynamic equations with inhomogeneous boundary condition
I. Kondrashuk, E.A. Notte-Cuello, M. Poblete-Cantellano, M. A. Rojas-Medar

Abstract
We show, using the spectral Galerkin method together with compactness arguments, existence and uniqueness of the periodic strong solutions for the magnetohydrodynamics’s type equations with inhomogeneous boundary conditions. Also, we study the asymptotic stability for time periodic solution for this system. In particular, when the magnetic field 𝒉 ⁡ ( x , t ) {\boldsymbol{h}}(x,t) is zero, we obtain existence, uniqueness and asymptotic behavior of the strong solutions to the Navier-Stokes equations with inhomogeneous boundary conditions.



2010 Mathematics Subject Classification: 35Q30, 35B10, 76W05

Keywords: Magnetohydrodynamic equations; periodic solutions.

In presence of a free motion of heavy ions (see Schlüter

{\rm div}\,{\boldsymbol{u}}={\rm div}\,{\boldsymbol{h}}=0&&\end{array}

g(t_{0}+\delta)\leq e^{\int_{t_{0}}^{t_{0}+\delta}\Pi P\left(s\right)ds}g(t_{0})\leq\left(L\beta\right)^{-1}e^{-\beta Lt_{1}}\left(\alpha\left|{\boldsymbol{w}}(0)\right|^{2}+\left|{\boldsymbol{z}}(0)\right|^{2}\right)e^{\int_{t_{0}}^{t_{0}+1}\Pi P(s)ds}.

(47)

Note that

\begin{array}[]{l}\displaystyle\int_{t_{0}}^{t_{0}+1}\Pi P\left(s\right)ds=\Pi\int_{t_{0}}^{t_{0}+1}P\left(s\right)ds\\
=\Pi\displaystyle\int_{t_{0}}^{t_{0}+1}\left[\displaystyle{\frac{1}{\alpha}}\left(1+\left|{\boldsymbol{w}}\right|^{2}+\left|{\boldsymbol{w}}\right|^{4}\right)\left|\nabla{\boldsymbol{w}}\right|^{2}+\left(\left|{\boldsymbol{z}}\right|^{2}+\left|{\boldsymbol{z}}\right|^{4}\right)\left|\nabla{\boldsymbol{z}}\right|^{2}\right]ds\\
\displaystyle+\Pi\int_{t_{0}}^{t_{0}+1}\left[\left(\displaystyle{\frac{2}{\alpha}}+3\right)\left(C\left(\gamma_{1},\gamma_{2}\right)+1\right)\right]ds,\end{array}

(48)

then by (
43
) and (
45
) each term of the above integral is bound
and not depend on the choice of
t_{1},t_{0}
and
\delta.
Hence, we infer
from (
47
) that there exist a constant
c_{1}
independent of
t_{1}

and
t_{0}
such that

g\left(t_{1}+1\right)=g\left(t_{0}+\left(t_{1}+1-t_{0}\right)\right)\leq c_{1}e^{-\beta Lt_{1}},

which implies that

\alpha\left|\nabla{\boldsymbol{w}}(t)\right|^{2}+\left|\nabla{\boldsymbol{z}}(t)\right|^{2}\leq c_{1}e^{-\beta L(t-1)},

for any
t>1.
Thus, the proof of theorem is complete.

Remark:
In this proof in order to estimate some terms, for example the term
\left|-\alpha\left(\mathbf{w}\cdot\nabla\mathbf{w},A\mathbf{w}\right)\right|,
we use the following Sobolev and Ladyzhenskaya’s
inequality to
\varphi\in H^{1},

\left|\varphi\right|_{L^{4}}\leq C\left|\varphi\right|_{L^{2}}^{1/2}\left|\nabla\varphi\right|_{L^{2}}^{1/2},

where
C
is a constant depending on the size of the domain, which is valid
for the two-dimensional case. The three-dimensional case is similar, but we
would have to use the inequality

\left|\varphi\right|_{L^{4}}\leq C\left|\varphi\right|_{L^{2}}^{1/4}\left|\nabla\varphi\right|_{L^{2}}^{3/4},

however, this three-dimensional case will not be done in this work.

Note that the Navier-Stokes equations

\begin{array}[]{lll}\displaystyle\frac{\partial{\boldsymbol{u}}}{\partial t}-\frac{\eta}{{\rho}}\Delta{\boldsymbol{u}}+{\boldsymbol{u}}\cdot\nabla{\boldsymbol{u}}&=&{\boldsymbol{f}}-\displaystyle\frac{1}{\rho}\nabla p^{\ast},\\

are a particular case of the MHD equations when the magnetic field

NS equations. On the other hand, when the magnetic field
{\boldsymbol{h}}
is
identically zero, we can reproduce the results on the asymptotic stability,
obtained by Hsia et al for the Navier-Stokes equations in
[
16
]
.
