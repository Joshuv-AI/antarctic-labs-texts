Unbounded p τ p_{\tau} -Convergence in Vector Lattices Normed by Locally Solid Lattices


Abstract
Let ( x α ) (x_{\alpha}) be a net in a vector lattice normed by locally solid lattice ( X , p , E τ ) (X,p,E_{\tau}) . We say that ( x α ) (x_{\alpha}) is unbounded p τ p_{\tau} -convergent to x ∈ X x\in X if p ⁡ ( | x α − x | ∧ u ) → 𝜏 0 p(\lvert x_{\alpha}-x\rvert\wedge u)\xrightarrow{\tau}0 for every u ∈ X + u\in X_{+} . This convergence has been studied recently for lattice-normed vector lattices as the u ​ p up -convergence in [ 5 , 6 , 7 ] , the u ​ o uo -convergence in [ 14 ] , and, as the u ​ n un -convergence in [ 10 , 13 , 14 , 16 , 18 ] . In this paper, we study the general properties of the unbounded p τ p_{\tau} -convergence.

The
p_{\tau}
-Convergence

Most of the notions and results in this section are direct analogs of well-known facts of the theory of normed lattices and locally solid vector lattices.

Definition 2.1
.

Let
(X,p,E_{\tau})
be an
LSNVL
. A net
(x_{\alpha})
in
X
is called
p_{\tau}
-convergent
to
x\in X
if
p(x_{\alpha}-x)\tc 0
in
E
. We write
x_{\alpha}\xrightarrow{p_{\tau}}x
, and say that
(x_{\alpha})

p_{\tau}
-converges to
x
.

In the following lemma, we give some basic properties of the
p_{\tau}
-convergence.

Lemma 2.1
.

Let
(x_{\alpha})
and
(y_{\beta})
be two nets in an
LSNVL

(X,p,E_{\tau})
, then;

(i)

x_{\alpha}\ptc x
iff
(x_{\alpha}-x)\ptc 0
;

(ii)

if
x_{\alpha}\ptc x
then
y_{\beta}\ptc x
for each subnet
(y_{\beta})
of
(x_{\alpha})
;

(iii)

suppose
x_{\alpha}\ptc x
and
y_{\beta}\ptc y
, then
ax_{\alpha}+by_{\beta}\ptc ax+by
for any
a,b\in\mathbb{R}
;

(iv)

if
x_{\alpha}\ptc x
and
x_{\alpha}\ptc y
then
x=y
;

(v)

if
x_{\alpha}\ptc x
then
\lvert x_{\alpha}\rvert\ptc\lvert x\rvert
.

Proof.

(i)
,
(ii)
and
(iii)
are obvious. Observe the inequality
p(x-y)\leq p(x-x_{\alpha})+p(x_{\alpha}-y)
, and so
(iv)
holds. For
(v)
, by using the inequality
\big\lvert\rvert x_{\alpha}\rvert-\lvert x\rvert\big\rvert\leq\lvert x_{\alpha}-x\rvert
, we have
p(\rvert x_{\alpha}\rvert-\lvert x\rvert)\leq p(x_{\alpha}-x)
. Therefore, we get the result.
∎

Proposition 2.3

Let

\displaystyle p(x_{\alpha}\vee y_{\beta}-x\vee y)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle p(\lvert x_{\alpha}\vee y_{\beta}-x_{\alpha}\vee y\rvert)+p(\lvert x_{\alpha}\vee y-x\vee y\rvert)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle p(\lvert y_{\beta}-y\rvert)+p(\lvert x_{\alpha}-x\rvert).

\rho_{j}\big(p(x_{\alpha}\vee y_{\beta}-x\vee y)\big)\leq\rho_{j}\big(p(y_{\beta}-y)\big)+\rho_{j}\big(p(x_{\alpha}-x)\big)

Theorem 2.4

Any monotone

-convergent net in an

(X,p,E_{\tau})
order converges to its

Proof.

Definition 3.3
.

Let

be an

-unit

and so

. In the following result, we give a generalization of

Theorem 3.5
.

Let
(X,p_{\tau},E)
be an
LSNVL
and

-unit. Then,
x_{\alpha}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle up_{\tau}}}{{\to}}0
iff
p(|x_{\alpha}|\wedge e)\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\tau}}{{\to}}0
.

The forward implication is immediate. For the reverse implication, let

\left|x_{\alpha}\right|\wedge u\leq\left|x_{\alpha}\right|\wedge(u-u\wedge ne)+\left|x_{\alpha}\right|\wedge(u\wedge ne)\leq(u-u\wedge ne)+n(\left|x_{\alpha}\right|\wedge e).

Hence, we get

p(\left|x_{\alpha}\right|\wedge u)\leq p(u-u\wedge ne)+np(\left|x_{\alpha}\right|\wedge e)

for all

and all

. Consider the family of Riesz pseudonorms

which generates the topology

\rho_{j}\big(p(\left|x_{\alpha}\right|\wedge u)\big)\leq\rho_{j}\big(p(u-u\wedge ne)\big)+n\rho_{j}\big(p(\left|x_{\alpha}\right|\wedge e)\big)

.

Theorem 3.6
.

Let
(X,p,E_{\tau})
be an

,

be the order ideal generated by

in

. If

is

, then

-unit.

Proof.

Let

, then there is

4
Convergences in Sublattices

The
up_{\tau}
-convergence passes obviously to sublattices of vector lattices as it was remarked in
[
10
, p.3]
. In opposite to the
uo
-convergence
[
14
, Thm.3.2]
, the
un
-convergence does not pass even from regular sublattice. Let
Y
be a sublattice of an
LSNVL

X=(X,p,E_{\tau})
and
(y_{\alpha})_{\alpha\in A}
be a net in
Y
. Then we can define the
up_{\tau}
-convergence in
Y
as following:
y_{\alpha}\uptc 0
iff
p(\lvert y_{\alpha}\rvert\wedge u)\tc 0
for all
u\in Y_{+}
. It is clear that if a net in a sublattice
Y
of an
LSNVL

(X,p,E_{\tau})

up_{\tau}
-converges to zero in
X
then it also
up_{\tau}
-converges to zero in
Y
. For the converse, we give the following theorem.

Theorem 4.1
.

Let
Y
be a sublattice of an
LSNVL

(X,p,E_{\tau})
and
(y_{\alpha})
be a net in
Y
such that
y_{\alpha}\uptc 0
in
Y
. Thus,
y_{\alpha}\uptc 0
in
X
for each of the following cases:

(i)

Y
is majorizing in
X
;

(ii)

Y
is p-dense in
X
;

(iii)

Y
is a projection band in
X
.

Proof.

Assume
(y_{\alpha})\subseteq Y
is a net such that
y_{\alpha}\uptc 0
in
Y
. Take any vector
0\neq x\in X_{+}
.

(i)
Since
Y
is majorizing in
X
, there exists
y\in Y
such that
x\leq y
. It follows from
0\leq\lvert y_{\alpha}\rvert\wedge x\leq\lvert y_{\alpha}\rvert\wedge y\ptc 0
, we have
y_{\alpha}\uptc 0
in
X
.

(ii)
Choose an arbitrary
0\neq u\in p(X)
. Then there exists
y\in Y
such that
p(x-y)\leq u
. Since
\lvert y_{\alpha}\rvert\wedge x\leq\lvert y_{\alpha}\rvert\wedge\lvert x-y\rvert+\lvert y_{\alpha}\rvert\wedge\rvert y\rvert
, we have

\displaystyle p(\lvert y_{\alpha}\rvert\wedge x)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle p(\lvert y_{\alpha}\rvert\wedge\lvert x-y\rvert)+p(\lvert y_{\alpha}\rvert\wedge\lvert y\rvert)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle p(\lvert x-y\rvert)+p(\lvert y_{\alpha}\rvert\wedge\lvert y\rvert)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle u+p(\lvert y_{\alpha}\rvert\wedge\lvert y\rvert).

Also, since
0\neq u\in p(X)
is arbitrary and
p(\lvert y_{\alpha}\rvert\wedge\lvert y\rvert)\tc 0
, we get
y_{\alpha}\uptc 0
in
X
.

(iii)
Suppose that
Y
is a projection band in
X
. Thus,
X=Y\oplus Y^{\bot}
. Hence
x=x_{1}+x_{2}
with
x_{1}\in Y
and
x_{2}\in Y^{\bot}
. Since
y_{\alpha}\wedge x_{2}=0
, we have

p(y_{\alpha}\wedge x)=p(y_{\alpha}\wedge(x_{1}+x_{2}))\leq p(y_{\alpha}\wedge x_{1})+p(y_{\alpha}\wedge x_{2})=p(y_{\alpha}\wedge x_{1})\tc 0.

So, we get
y_{\alpha}\uptc 0
in
X
.
∎

Remark 4.1
.

If
\rho
is a Riesz pseudonorm on a vector lattice

then
\rho(\frac{1}{n}x)\leq\frac{1}{n}\rho(x)
for all

, p.2]

of an LNVL
(X,p,E)

-almost order bounded
if, for any

, there is

such that
p((\lvert x\rvert-x_{w})^{+})=p(\lvert x\rvert-x_{w}\wedge\lvert x\rvert)\leq w
for any

. In the following theorem, we prove that the

-convergence, which is a

[

, and it is also similar to

[

Theorem 4.4
.

Let
(X,p,E_{\tau})
be an LSLNVL. If
(x_{\alpha})

-almost order bounded and

then
x_{\alpha}\ptc x
.

Suppose that
(x_{\alpha})

is also

p(\lvert x_{\alpha}-x\rvert\wedge x_{w})\tc 0
in

\rho_{j}\big(p(\lvert x_{\alpha}-x\rvert\wedge x_{w})\to 0

\displaystyle\rho_{j}\big(\frac{1}{n}w+p(\lvert x_{\alpha}-x\rvert\wedge x_{w})\big)\ \ \ (\forall j)
