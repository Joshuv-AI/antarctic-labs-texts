Global well-posedness of one-dimensional compressible Navier-Stokes-Vlasov system
Hai-Liang Li, Ling-Yun Shou

Abstract
A fluid-particle model is investigated in the present paper, which consists of the compressible Navier-Stokes equations coupled with the Vlasov equation though a nonlinear drag force. We consider the initial value problem for the one-dimensional compressible Navier-Stokes-Vlasov system and establish the global existence and uniqueness of the weak solution for general initial data in either spatial periodic domain or spatial real line, which is shown to be a strong solution for regular initial data. Moreover, it is showed that for spatial periodic domain, both the fluid velocity and the macroscopic velocity of the particles converge to the same speed exponentially in time, and the compact support of distribution function associated with velocity variable converges to a point set exponentially in time.



Key words:
Fluid-particle model; Nonlinear drag force; Global well-posedness, Large time behavior

\begin{split}\frac{d}{dt}\theta(\rho)+u\theta(\rho)_{x}=\mu(\rho)\frac{\rho_{t}+u\rho_{x}}{\rho}=-\mu(\rho)u_{x},\end{split}

(2.13)

where the function
\theta(\rho)
is given by

\displaystyle\theta(\rho)=:\int_{1}^{\rho}\frac{\mu(s)}{s}ds=\begin{cases}2\log{\rho},&\mbox{if $\beta=0,$ }\\
\log{\rho}+\frac{\rho^{\beta}-1}{\beta},&\mbox{if $\beta>0.$}\end{cases}

(2.14)

Substituting (
2.13
) into (
2.12
), and re-writing the resulted equation along the particle path
\mathcal{X}^{x,t}(s)
for any
(x,t)\in\mathbb{T}\times[0,T]
defined as

\left\{\begin{split}&\frac{d}{ds}\mathcal{X}^{x,t}(s)=u(\mathcal{X}^{x,t}(s),x),\quad s\in[0,t],\\
&\quad\mathcal{X}^{x,t}(t)=x,\end{split}\right.

(2.15)

we have

\begin{split}&\frac{d}{ds}\Big(\theta(\rho)+\mathcal{I}\big(\rho u+\rho\mathcal{I}(n)\big)\Big)(\mathcal{X}^{x,t}(s),s)+\rho^{\gamma}(\mathcal{X}^{x,t}(s),s)\\
&=F(\mathcal{X}^{x,t}(s),s)-\int_{0}^{1}(\mu(\rho)u_{y})(y,s)dy,\end{split}

(2.16)

where

is given by

F(x,t)=:\mathcal{I}(\rho)(x,t)\int_{0}^{1}nw(y,t)dy+\int_{0}^{1}\big[\rho|u|^{2}+\rho u\mathcal{I}(n)+\rho^{\gamma}\big](y,t)dy.

It follows from (
1.5
), (
2.1
) and (
2.7
) for any

that

\left\{\begin{split}&|\mathcal{I}\big(\rho u+\rho\mathcal{I}(n)\big)(x,t)|\leq\|\rho(t)\|_{L^{1}}^{\frac{1}{2}}\|(\sqrt{\rho}u)(t)\|_{L^{2}}+\|\rho(t)\|_{L^{1}}\|n(t)\|_{L^{1}}\leq C_{2},\\
&|F(x,t)|\leq\|\rho(t)\|_{L^{1}}\|f(t)\|_{\mathcal{L}^{1}}^{\frac{1}{2}}\||v|^{2}f(t)\|_{\mathcal{L}^{1}}^{\frac{1}{2}}+\|(\sqrt{\rho}u)(t)\|_{L^{2}}^{2}\\
&\quad\quad\quad~\quad+\|n(t)\|_{L^{1}}\|\rho(t)\|_{L^{1}}^{\frac{1}{2}}\|(\sqrt{\rho}u)(t)\|_{L^{2}}+\|\rho^{\gamma}(t)\|_{L^{1}}\leq C_{3},\end{split}\right.

(2.17)

where
C_{2}>0
and
C_{3}>0
are two constants given by

\left\{\begin{split}&C_{2}=:\|\rho_{0}\|_{L^{1}}^{\frac{1}{2}}(2E_{0})^{\frac{1}{2}}+\|\rho_{0}\|_{L^{1}}\|f_{0}\|_{\mathcal{L}^{1}},\\
&C_{3}=:\big(\|\rho_{0}\|_{L^{1}}\|f_{0}\|_{\mathcal{L}^{1}}^{\frac{1}{2}}+\|\rho_{0}\|_{L^{1}}^{\frac{1}{2}}\|f_{0}\|_{\mathcal{L}^{1}}\big)(2E_{0})^{\frac{1}{2}}+(\gamma+1)E_{0}.\end{split}\right.

Since it follows

\left\{\begin{split}&\mu(\rho)\leq 2,\quad\text{if}\quad 0\leq\rho\leq 1,\\
&\mu(\rho)\leq\beta\theta(\rho)+2,\quad\text{if}\quad\rho\geq 1,\end{split}\right.

(2.18)

we get

\displaystyle\Big|\int_{0}^{1}(\mu(\rho)u_{y})(y,t)dy\Big|

\displaystyle\quad\leq\Big(\int_{0}^{1}\mu(\rho)(y,t)dy\Big)^{\frac{1}{2}}\Big(\int_{0}^{1}(\mu(\rho)|u_{y}|^{2})(y,t)dy\Big)^{\frac{1}{2}}

\displaystyle\quad=1+\frac{1}{4}\Big(\int_{\{y\in(0,1)|\rho(y,t)\leq 1\}}+\int_{\{y\in(0,1)|\rho(y,t)\geq 1\}}\Big)\mu(\rho)(y,t)dy\int_{0}^{1}(\mu(\rho)|u_{y}|^{2})(y,t)dy

\displaystyle\quad\leq 1+\int_{0}^{1}(\mu(\rho)|u_{y}|^{2})(y,t)dy+\frac{\beta}{4}\sup_{x\in\{y\in(0,1)|\rho(y,t)\geq 1\}}\theta(\rho)(x,t)\int_{0}^{1}(\mu(\rho)|u_{y}|^{2})(y,t)dy.

(2.19)

Then, the upper bound of
\rho
uniformly in time can be derived by the arguments due to Zlotnik
[
49
]
. For any
(x,t)\in\mathbb{T}\times[0,T]
, if there is a time

such that it holds

then by the continuity of
\rho(\mathcal{X}^{x,t}(s),s)
with respect to

, we can find a time

to have

\left\{\begin{split}&\rho(\mathcal{X}^{x,t}(s_{*}),s_{*})=C_{4},\\
&\rho(\mathcal{X}^{x,t}(\tau),\tau)>C_{4}\geq(C_{3}+1)^{\frac{1}{\gamma}},\quad\forall\tau\in(s_{*},s].\end{split}\right.

(2.20)

Integrating (
2.16
) over
[s_{*},s]
, we obtain by (
2.17
) and (
2.19
)-(
2.20
) that

\begin{split}&\theta(\rho)(\mathcal{X}^{x,t}(s),s)=\theta(\rho)(\mathcal{X}^{x,t}(s_{*}),s_{*})-\mathcal{I}\big(\rho u+\rho\mathcal{I}(n)\big)(\mathcal{X}^{x,t}(\tau),\tau)|^{\tau=s}_{\tau=s_{*}}\\
&\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad~~-\int_{s_{*}}^{s}\rho^{\gamma}(\mathcal{X}^{x,t}(\tau),\tau)d\tau+\int_{s_{*}}^{s}F(\mathcal{X}^{x,t}(\tau),\tau)d\tau-\int_{s_{*}}^{s}\int_{0}^{1}(\mu(\rho)u_{y})(y,\tau)dyd\tau\\
&\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad~~\leq\theta(C_{4})+2C_{2}+\int_{s_{*}}^{s}\int_{0}^{1}(\mu(\rho)|u_{y}|^{2})(y,\tau)dyd\tau\\
&\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad~~+\frac{\beta}{4}\int_{s_{*}}^{s}\sup_{x\in\{y\in(0,1)|\rho(y,t)\geq 1\}}\theta(\rho)(x,\tau)\int_{0}^{1}(\mu(\rho)|u_{y}|^{2})(y,\tau)dyd\tau,\end{split}

which together with

\begin{split}&\sup_{x\in\{y\in(0,1)|\rho(y,t)\geq 1\}}\theta(\rho)(x,t)\\

&\quad\quad\quad+\frac{\beta}{4}\int_{0}^{T}\sup_{x\in\{y\in(0,1)|\rho(y,t)\geq 1\}}\theta(\rho)(x,\tau)\int_{0}^{1}(\mu(\rho)|u_{y}|^{2})(y,\tau)dyd\tau,\quad t\in[0,T].\end{split}

(2.21)

By virtue of

, (

nwall’s inequality, we have

\begin{split}\sup_{x\in\{y\in(0,1)|\rho(y,t)\geq 1\}}\theta(\rho)(x,t)\leq e^{\frac{\beta E_{0}}{4}}\big(\theta(C_{4})+2C_{2}+E_{0}\big).\end{split}

(2.22)

Thus, we show the upper bound of

uniformly in time.

Finally, integrating (
2.16
) over
[0,t]
, one deduces by
(\ref {basiccnsv})_{4}
, the upper bound in (

), (
2.15
) and (

) for any

that

\begin{split}&\theta(\rho)(x,t)\geq\inf_{x\in\mathbb{T}}\theta(\rho_{0})(x)-\big|\mathcal{I}\big(\rho u+\rho\mathcal{I}(n)\big)(\mathcal{X}^{x,t}(\tau),\tau)|^{\tau=t}_{\tau=0}\big|-T\rho_{+}^{\gamma}\\
&\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad-T\sup_{(x,t)\in\mathbb{T}\times[0,T]}|F(x,t)|-\int_{0}^{T}\Big|\int_{0}^{1}(\mu(\rho)u_{y})(y,\tau)dy\Big|d\tau\\
&\quad\quad\quad\quad\geq\inf_{x\in\mathbb{T}}\theta(\rho_{0})(x)-2C_{2}-(\rho_{+}^{\gamma}+C_{3})T-(\mu(\rho_{+})T)^{\frac{1}{2}}\|\sqrt{\mu(\rho)}u_{x}\|_{L^{2}(0,T;L^{2})}\\
&\quad\quad\quad\quad\geq\inf_{x\in\mathbb{T}}\theta(\rho_{0})(x)-2C_{2}-\frac{\mu(\rho_{+})E_{0}}{4}-(\rho_{+}^{\gamma}+C_{3}+1)T,\end{split}

(2.23)

which together with the fact

\left\{\begin{split}&\theta(\rho)=2\log{\rho},\quad\text{if}~\beta=0,\\
&\theta(\rho)\leq\log{\rho}+\frac{\rho_{+}^{\beta}}{\beta},\quad\text{if}~\beta>0,\end{split}\right.

(2.24)

yields the time-dependent lower bound of
\rho
. The proof of Lemma
2.2
is completed.

Lemma 2.3

Let

Proof.

(2.28)

(5.8)

Adding (

Proof of Theorem
1.2
on exponential time-decay rate:

Let

Making use of (
5.9

\begin{split}&(1-c_{1}\alpha)\mathcal{E}(t)\leq\mathcal{E}^{\alpha}(t)\leq(1+c_{1}\alpha)\mathcal{E}(t).\end{split}

(5.20)

We also deduce from (
5.6

\begin{split}&\int_{\mathbb{T}\times\mathbb{R}}\rho(x,t)|u(x,t)-v|^{2}f(x,v,t)dvdx\\
&\quad\geq\rho_{-}\int_{\mathbb{T}\times\mathbb{R}}|u(x,t)-m_{1}(t)+m_{1}(t)-m_{2}(t)+m_{2}(t)-v|^{2}f(x,v,t)dvdx\\
&\quad=\rho_{-}\int_{\mathbb{T}\times\mathbb{R}}|u(x,t)-m_{1}(t)|^{2}f(x,v,t)dvdx+\rho_{-}|(m_{1}-m_{2})(t)|^{2}\int_{\mathbb{T}\times\mathbb{R}}f(x,v,t)dvdx\\
&\quad\quad+\rho_{-}\int_{\mathbb{T}\times\mathbb{R}}|v-m_{2}(t)|^{2}f(x,v,t)dvdx+2\rho_{-}(m_{1}-m_{2})(t)\int_{\mathbb{T}\times\mathbb{R}}(u(x,t)-m_{1}(t))f(x,v,t)dvdx\\

which together with

) and the Young’s inequality leads to

\begin{split}&\|\big(\sqrt{\rho f}(u-v)\big)(t)\|_{\mathcal{L}^{2}}^{2}\\
&\quad\geq\rho_{-}\overline{n_{0}}\|(u-m_{1})(t)\|_{L^{\infty}}^{2}+\rho_{-}\overline{n_{0}}|(m_{1}-m_{2})(t)|^{2}+\rho_{-}\|\big((v-m_{2})f\big)(t)\|_{\mathcal{L}^{2}}^{2}\\

Applying the Gr

Then according to the Definition

\begin{split}&{\rm{W_{1}}}\big(f(t),(n\delta(v-u_{s}))(t)\big)\\

\begin{split}u_{s}=e^{-\int_{0}^{t}\rho(X^{x,v,t}(\omega),\omega)d\omega}u_{s}+\int_{0}^{t}e^{-\int_{\tau}^{t}\rho(X^{x,v,t}(\omega),\omega)d\omega}\rho(X^{x,v,t}(\tau),\tau)u_{s}d\tau,\end{split}
