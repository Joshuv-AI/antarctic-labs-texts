Hom-Lie-Yamaguti Superalgebras
Donatien Gaparayi 1 and Sylvain Attan 2 1 Ecole Normale Supérieure (E.N.S), BP 6983 Bujumbura, Burundi 2 Département de Mathématiques Université d’Abomey-calavi 01 BP 4521, Cotonou 01, Bénin 1 gapadona@yahoo.fr, 2 syltane2010@yahoo.fr

Abstract
. (Multiplicative) Hom-Lie-Yamaguti superalgebras which generalize Hom-Lie supertriple systems (and subsequently ternary multiplicative Hom-Nambu superalgebras) and Hom-Lie superalgebras in the same way as Lie-Yamaguti superalgebras [ 12 ] generalize Lie supertriple systems and Lie superalgebras are defined. We show that the category of (multiplicative) Hom-Lie-Yamaguti superalgebras is closed under twisting by self-morphisms. Construction of some examples of Hom-Lie-Yamaguti superalgebras is given. The notion of an n t ​ h − n^{th}- derived (binary) Hom-superalgebras is extended to the one of an n t ​ h − n^{th}- derived binary-ternary Hom-superalgebras and it is shown that the category of Hom-Lie-Yamaguti superalgebras is closed under the process of taking n t ​ h − n^{th}- derived Hom-superalgebras.

\displaystyle=\circlearrowleft_{(x,y,z)}((-1)^{|x||z|}[\alpha^{2^{n}-1}(\alpha^{2^{n}-1}([x,y])),(\alpha^{2^{n}-1}(\alpha(z)))])+\circlearrowleft_{(x,y,z)}(\alpha^{2^{{n}+1}-2}((-1)^{|x||z|}\{x,y,z\}))

\displaystyle=\alpha^{2^{{n}+1}-2}(\circlearrowleft_{(x,y,z)}((-1)^{|x||z|}[[x,y],\alpha(z)])+\circlearrowleft_{(x,y,z)}((-1)^{|x||z|}\{y,z,x\}))

\displaystyle=\alpha^{2^{{n}+1}-2}(\circlearrowleft_{(x,y,z)}((-1)^{|x||z|}[[x,y],\alpha(z)]+(-1)^{|x||z|}\{x,y,z\}))

\displaystyle=\alpha^{2^{{n}+1}-2}(0)\,\,\mbox{(by $(SHLY5)$ for $A_{\alpha}$)}

\displaystyle=0

and thus we get
(SHLY5)
for
A^{(n)}
.

Next,

\displaystyle\{[x,y]^{(n)},(\alpha^{2^{n}})(z),(\alpha^{2^{n}})(u)\}^{(n)}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\{\alpha^{2^{n}-1}([x,y]),(\alpha^{2^{n}})(z),(\alpha^{2^{n}})(u)\}^{(n)}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\alpha^{2^{n+1}-2}(\{\alpha^{2^{n}-1}([x,y]),(\alpha^{2^{n}})(z),(\alpha^{2^{n}})(u)\})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\alpha^{2^{n+1}-2}\alpha^{2^{n}-1}(\{[x,y],\alpha(z),\alpha(u)\})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\alpha^{3(2^{n}-1)}(\{[x,y],\alpha(z),\alpha(u)\}).

Therefore

\displaystyle\circlearrowleft_{(x,y,z)}((-1)^{|x||z|}\{[x,y]^{(n)},(\alpha^{2^{n}})(z),(\alpha^{2^{n}})(u)\}^{(n)})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\,\,\circlearrowleft_{(x,y,z)}[\alpha^{3(2^{n}-1)}((-1)^{|x||z|}\{[x,y],\alpha(z),\alpha(u)\})]

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\alpha^{3(2^{n}-1)}(\circlearrowleft_{(x,y,z)}((-1)^{|x||z|}\{[x,y],\alpha(z),\alpha(u)\}))

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\alpha^{3(2^{n}-1)}(0)\,\,\mbox{(by $(SHLY6)$ for $A_{\alpha}$)}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 0

So that we get
(SHLY6)
for
A^{(n)}.
Further, using
(SHLY7)
for
A_{\alpha}
and condition of multiplicativity linearity of
\alpha

\displaystyle\{(\alpha^{2^{n}})(x),(\alpha^{2^{n}})(y),[u,v]^{(n)}\}^{(n)}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\alpha^{2^{n+1}-2}\alpha^{2^{n}-1}(\{\alpha(x),\alpha(y),[u,v]\})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\alpha^{2^{n+1}-2}\alpha^{2^{n}-1}([\{x,y,u\},\alpha^{2}(v)]+(-1)^{|u|(|x|+|y|)}[\alpha^{2}(u),\{x,y,v\}])

\displaystyle\alpha^{2^{n}-1}([\{x,y,u\}^{(n)},(\alpha^{2^{n}})^{2}(v)]+(-1)^{|u|(|x|+|y|)}[(\alpha^{2^{n}})^{2}(u),\{x,y,v\}^{(n)}])

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle[\{x,y,u\}^{(n)},(\alpha^{2^{n}})^{2}(v)]^{(n)}+(-1)^{|u|(|x|+|y|)}[(\alpha^{2^{n}})^{2}(u),\{x,y,v\}^{(n)}]^{(n)}.

Thus
(SHLY7)
holds for
A^{(n)}.
Using repeatedly the condition of multiplicativity and the identity
(SHLY8)

for

for

is as follows.

\displaystyle\{(\alpha^{2^{n}})^{2}(x),(\alpha^{2^{n}})^{2}(y),\{u,v,w\}^{(n)}\}^{(n)}

\displaystyle\alpha^{2^{n+1}-2}(\{(\alpha^{2^{n}})^{2})(x),(\alpha^{2^{n}})^{2})(y),\alpha^{2^{n+1}-2}(\{u,v,w\})\})

.
