On uniqueness of two meromorphic functions sharing a small function
Molla Basir Ahamed

Abstract
. In this paper, we have investigated the uniqueness problems of entire and meromorphic functions concerning differential polynomials sharing a small function. Our results radically extended and improved the results of Bhoosnurmath-Pujari [ 9 ] and Harina - Anand [ 16 ] not only by sharing small function instead of fixed point but also reducing the lower bound of n n . The authors Harina-Anand [ 16 ] made plenty of mistakes in their paper. We have corrected all of them in a more convenient way. At last some open questions have been posed for further study in this direction.

The Nevanlinna theory mainly describes the asymptotic distribution of solutions of the equation

When

It is well known that if two moromorphic functions

also. Next we see from (
3.23
), we have

\displaystyle\overline{N}\left(r,f_{*}^{p}(f_{*}-1)^{q}f_{*}^{\prime}\right)=\overline{N}\left(r,\frac{\alpha^{2}}{g_{*}^{p}(g_{*}-1)^{q}g_{*}^{\prime}}\right).

i.e.,

\displaystyle\overline{N}(r,f_{*})

\displaystyle\leqslant

\displaystyle\overline{N}\left(r,\frac{1}{g_{*}}\right)+\overline{N}\left(r,\frac{1}{g_{*}-1}\right)+\overline{N}\left(r,\frac{1}{g_{*}^{\prime}}\right)

\displaystyle\leqslant

\displaystyle\left(\frac{q}{p+q+1}\right)N\left(r,\frac{1}{g_{*}}\right)+\left(\frac{2}{p+q+3}\right)N\left(r,\frac{1}{g_{*}-1}\right)+\left(\frac{1}{p+q+2}\right)N\left(r,\frac{1}{g_{*}^{\prime}}\right)

\displaystyle\leqslant

\displaystyle\left(\frac{q}{p+q+1}+\frac{2}{p+q+3}+\frac{2}{p+q+2}\right)T(r,g_{*})+S(r,g_{*}).

By applying Second Fundamental Theorem, we get

(3.24)

\displaystyle T(r,f_{*})

\displaystyle\leqslant

\displaystyle\overline{N}\left(r,\frac{1}{f_{*}}\right)+\overline{N}\left(r,\frac{1}{f_{*}-1}\right)+\overline{N}(r,f_{*})+S(r,f_{*})

\displaystyle\leqslant

\displaystyle\left(\frac{q}{p+q+1}+\frac{2}{p+q+3}\right)T(r,f_{*})+\left(\frac{q}{p+q+1}+\frac{2}{p+q+3}+\frac{2}{p+q+2}\right)T(r,g_{*})

\displaystyle+S(r,f_{*})+S(r,g_{*}).

Similarly, we get

(3.25)

\displaystyle T(r,g_{*})

\displaystyle\leqslant

\displaystyle\left(\frac{q}{p+q+1}+\frac{2}{p+q+3}\right)T(r,g_{*})+\left(\frac{q}{p+q+1}+\frac{2}{p+q+3}+\frac{2}{p+q+2}\right)T(r,f_{*})

\displaystyle+S(r,f_{*})+S(r,g_{*}).

From (
3.24
) and (
3.25
), we get

\displaystyle T_{*}(r)\leqslant\left(\frac{2q}{p+q+1}+\frac{4}{p+q+3}+\frac{2}{p+q+2}\right)T_{*}(r)+S_{*}(r).

i.e.,

\displaystyle\left(1-\frac{2q}{p+q+1}-\frac{4}{p+q+3}-\frac{2}{p+q+2}\right)T_{*}(r)\leqslant S_{*}(r),

which contradicts
p\geqslant q+9
.
∎

1.2
.
