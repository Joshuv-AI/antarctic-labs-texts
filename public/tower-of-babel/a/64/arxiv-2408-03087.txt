Tiered tree, Parking function and Postnikov-Shapiro algebra
Biswadeep Bagchi, Srinibas Swain

Abstract
Tiered trees were introduced as a combinatorial object for counting absolutely indecomposable representation of certain quivers and torus orbit of certain homogeneous variety. In this paper, we define a bijection between the set of parallelogram polyominoes and graphical parking functions. Moreover, we defined the space 𝒮 G \mathcal{S}_{G} for complete tiered graphs and described tiered graphs in terms of Whitney’s operations.

Tiered trees

with an integer valued tiering function

•

Counting absolutely irreducible representation of the supernova quivers (quivers with long legs attached to vertices), and

•

Counting certain torus orbit of partial flag variety of type A over finite field with trivial stabilizer.

Tiered trees can be described in terms of symmetric function and theta operators
[
1
]
. These operators enumerates tiered trees in terms of inversion numbers.

Parking functions are sequence in
\mathbb{N}^{n}
which arose in the study of hashing function
[
7
]
. The name of such sequence came in terms of parking for cars. Assume that

parking spots labeled 0 through

. Car

prefers spot
b_{k}

, which means that driver will drive until they reaches
that spot and park there if it is not occupied. If preferred spot is occupied the the driver will continue driving until they come to an unoccupied spot and park the car in this spot. If preferred spot and all the
spots after it are occupied, then the driver cannot park. A parking function of length

is sequence
(b_{1},\dots,b_{n})\in\mathbb{N}^{n}
of parking preferences so that all the cars can park. For an equivalent definition see Definition
3.8
(Section
3
).

Parking functions have appeared in various area of mathematics, more specifically in combinatorics such as hyperplane arrangements
[
14
]
and diagonal harmonics
[
6
]
. Parking functions are related to labelled trees. The number of parking functions of length

is
(n+1)^{n-1}
. By Cayley’s formula this is exactly the number of labelled trees on
n+1
vertices. Moreover, there is an explicit bijection between parking functions and labelled trees. Let

be a parking function, then the

of

is defined by

. The
reversed sum
of
b
is defined by
rs(b)={n\choose 2}-s(b)
. The
reversed sum enumerator
is given by the expression
\sum_{b}q^{rs(b)}
, where
b
ranges over all parking functions of length
n
. Kreweras
[
8
]
proved that the inversion number of labelled trees on
(n+1)
vertices and reversed sum of parking function of length
n
are equal.
Graphical parking functions
were first introduced by Postnikov and Shapiro
[
13
]
. These parking functions are connected to abelian sandpile models of graphs where it is called

(see
[
13

). Graphical parking function or

and reversed sum

the authors extended the definition of

-parking function on any simple graphs and provided an bijection between

-parking function and a spannig trees of graphs by

\sum_{b}q^{rs(b)}=\sum_{T}q^{\kappa(G,T)},

where

ranges over all
G
-parking functions and

is a generalisation of the inversion number of spannig trees. When

vertices the number of

In the same paper
[
13
]
, Postnikov and Shapiro defined some algebras related to graphs. The algebras

and

which are called power algebra and counting spannig tree algebra of a graph

In this paper, we define the graphical parking configuration on tiered trees and provided a bijection between set of parking configurations and labelled parallelogram polyominoes. The proof of the bijection is based on the algorithm defined over the unstable vertices (see Section

This paper is organised in the following way: In Section
