In this section, we give the definition of a

Banach left

For each

{}_{0}B(A^{\sharp},E)=\{T\in B(A^{\sharp},E):T(ab)=a\cdot T(b)\text{ for all }a,b\in A\}.

Clearly,

is in

(f\cdot a)(x)=f(a\cdot x)\hskip 14.22636pt(a\in A,x\in E,f\in E^{*}).

Definition 3.1

Let
A
be a Banach algebra and

A
-module. We say that

is 0-flat if

is (right) 0-injective.

Clearly, each injective module is 0-injective.

We use of the following characterization of 0-injectivity in the sequel without giving the reference.

Let

\rho\in_{A}\hskip-2.84544ptB(K,E)=_{A}\hskip-2.84544ptB(_{0}B(A^{\sharp},E),E)
