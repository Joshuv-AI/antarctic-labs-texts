Abstract
In this paper, the structure of the parafermion vertex operator algebra associated to an integrable highest weight module for simple affine Lie superalgebra o ​ s ​ p ​ ( 1 | 2 ​ n ) osp(1|2n) is studied. Particularly, we determine the generators for this algebra.

Remark 3.2
.

We want to point out that generators

are Virasoro elements, but

\alpha\in\Delta_{0(+)}^{L}

are all in even part of the affine vertex operator superalgebra
