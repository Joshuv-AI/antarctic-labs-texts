An accelerated universe with negative equation of state parameter in Inhomogeneous Cosmology with k k -essence scalar field Volume: 00
Somnath Mukherjee, Debashis Gangopadhyay

Abstract
We obtain a scaling relation for spherically symmetric k-essence scalar fields ϕ ⁡ ( r , t ) \phi(r,t) for an inhomogeneous cosmology with the Lemaitre-Tolman- Bondi (LTB) metric. We show that this scaling relation reduces to the known relation for a homogeneous cosmology when the LTB metric reduces to the Friedmann-Lemaitre-Robertson-Walker (FLRW) metric under certain identifications of the metric functions. A k-essence lagrangian is set up and the Euler-Lagrangian equations solved assuming ϕ ⁡ ( r , t ) = ϕ 1 ​ ( r ) + ϕ 2 ​ ( t ) \phi(r,t)=\phi_{1}(r)+\phi_{2}(t) . The solutions enable the LBT metric functions to be related to the fields. The LTB inhomogeneous universe exhibits accelerated expansion i.e.cosmic acceleration driven by negative pressure.
