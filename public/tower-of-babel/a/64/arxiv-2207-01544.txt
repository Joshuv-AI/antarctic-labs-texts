A note on improved differentiability for the Banach-space valued Finsler γ \gamma -Laplacian
Max Goering and Lukas Koch

Abstract
We obtain improved fractional differentiability of solutions to the Banach-space valued Finsler γ \gamma -Laplacian defined on a σ \sigma -convex, τ \tau -smooth Banach space. The operators we consider are non-linear and very degenerately elliptic. Our results are new already in the ℝ \mathbb{R} -valued setting.

2.1
Banach space geometry

Let

be a real Banach space and

For

In light of Proposition

\left(\rho_{\ast}(x^{\ast})+\rho_{\ast}(y^{\ast})\right)^{\gamma^{\prime}-\tau^{\prime}}\rho_{\ast}\left(x^{\ast}-y^{\ast}\right)^{\tau^{\prime}}\lesssim_{\gamma}\left\langle j_{\rho_{\ast}}^{\gamma^{\prime}}(x^{\ast})-j_{\rho_{\ast}}^{\gamma^{\prime}}(y^{\ast}),x^{\ast}-y^{\ast}\right\rangle\qquad\forall x^{\ast},y^{\ast}\in\mathbb{X}^{\ast},

(15)

We now demonstrate a straightforward relationship between

Lemma 6

Suppose

is a norm on
\mathbb{R}^{m}\otimes\mathbb{R}^{n}
. Then
(\mathbb{R}^{m}\otimes\mathbb{R}^{n},F)
is
2
-smooth. On the other hand, if
\rho\in C^{1}(\mathbb{R}^{m}\otimes\mathbb{R}^{n}\setminus\{0\},\mathbb{R})
is a norm and

is uniformly elliptic in the sense that

\langle\nabla F(x)-\nabla F(y),x-y\rangle\gtrsim\lvert x-y\rvert^{2},

is
2

Proof.

First suppose
F\in C^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{m}\otimes\mathbb{R}^{n}\setminus\{0\},\mathbb{R})
is a norm. Let
x\in\{F=1\}
and
F(y)<1/2
. Then
F(x-y),F(x+y)\in[1/2,3/2]
. From the regularity of

and the fundamental theorem of calculus,

\displaystyle F(x+y)+F(x-y)-2=

\displaystyle F(x+y)-F(x)+F(x-y)-F(x)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{1}\langle\mathrm{D}F(x+ty)-\mathrm{D}F(x-ty),y\rangle\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 2\int_{0}^{1}\int_{0}^{1}\langle\mathrm{D}^{2}F(x-ty+2sy)y,y\rangle s\mathrm{d}s\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\|D^{2}F\|_{L^{\infty}(B_{3/2}\setminus B_{1/2})}F(y)^{2}.

The last inequality holds due to equivalence of norms on
\mathbb{R}^{m}\otimes\mathbb{R}^{n}
.

On the other hand, when

F(x+y)+F(x-y)-2\leq F(x)+F(y)+F(x)+F(y)-2=2F(y)\leq 4F(y)^{2}.

In particular, for all

\tau_{F}(\epsilon)\lesssim(1+\|D^{2}F\|_{L^{\infty}(B_{3/2}\setminus B_{1/2})})\epsilon^{2}

Now suppose

3  A
V
-functional estimate in Banach spaces

In this section, we prove Lemma
2
. In the case when
\mathbb{X}=\mathbb{R}^{m}
and
\rho
is the Euclidean norm this result is well-known. However, even in this set-up our elementary proof is new. For the convenience of the reader, we restate the lemma here. Recall the definition of
V_{p,q}
from (
6
).

Lemma 7
.

Let
(\mathbb{Y},F)
be a Banach space and fix
p,q>1
. Then, for any
\xi,\,\eta\in\mathbb{Y}
,

F\left(V_{p,q}(\xi)-V_{p,q}(\eta)\right)\sim\left(F(\xi)+F(\eta)\right)^{\frac{p-q}{q}}F(\xi-\eta).

(18)

Proof.

Case 1:

p-q\geq 0
.

For
\xi,\eta\in\mathbb{Y}
, convexity of
F
implies the difference quotient

t\mapsto\frac{F(\xi+t\eta)-F(\xi)}{t}

is a non-decreasing function for
t\in[0,\infty)
. Suppose without loss of generality,
F(\xi)\geq F(\eta)
and define
\kappa=\frac{F(\eta)}{F(\xi)}\leq 1
. Since
p-q\geq 0
,
\kappa^{\frac{p-q}{q}}\leq 1
. Using
F(\xi)\kappa=F(\eta)
and convexity of
F
, it follows

\displaystyle F\left(V_{p,q}(\xi)-V_{p,q}(\eta)\right)

\displaystyle=F(\xi)^{\frac{p-q}{q}}\kappa^{\frac{p-q}{q}}\frac{F\left(\xi-\kappa^{\frac{p-q}{q}}\eta\right)-F(\xi)}{\kappa^{\frac{p-q}{q}}}+F(\xi)^{\frac{p}{q}}

\displaystyle\leq F(\eta)^{\frac{p-q}{q}}\left[F(\xi-\eta)-F(\xi)\right]+F(\xi)^{\frac{p}{q}}

\displaystyle=F(\eta)^{\frac{p-q}{q}}F(\xi-\eta)+\left(F(\xi)^{\frac{p-q}{q}}-F(\eta)^{\frac{p-q}{q}}\right)F(\xi)

\displaystyle\lesssim F(\eta)^{\frac{p-q}{q}}F(\xi-\eta)+F(\xi)^{\frac{p-q}{q}}F(\xi-\eta),

where the last line follows by the mean-value theorem applied to
t\mapsto t^{\frac{p-q}{q}}
on
[F(\eta),F(\xi)]
. This verifies

F(V_{p,q}(\xi)-V_{p,q}(\eta))\leq\left(F(\xi)^{\frac{p-q}{q}}+F(\eta)^{\frac{p-q}{q}}\right)F(\xi-\eta)

which suffices since for
a,b>0
and any
\alpha>0
,
a^{\alpha}+b^{\alpha}\leq 2(a+b)^{\alpha}
.
To confirm the reverse inequality, assume again without loss of generality, that
F(\xi)\geq F(\eta)
.
Then, using the reverse triangle inequality,

\displaystyle F\left(V_{p,q}(\xi)-V_{p,q}(\eta)\right)

\displaystyle=F\left(F(\xi)^{\frac{p-q}{q}}(\xi-\eta)-\left(F(\eta)^{\frac{p-q}{q}}-F(\xi)^{\frac{p-q}{q}}\right)\eta\right)

\displaystyle\geq F(\eta)^{\frac{p-q}{q}}F(\xi-\eta)-\left(F(\eta)^{\frac{p-q}{q}}-F(\xi)^{\frac{p-q}{q}}\right)F(\eta)

\displaystyle\geq F(\eta)^{\frac{p-q}{q}}F(\xi-\eta)

\displaystyle\gtrsim(F(\eta)+F(\xi))^{\frac{p-q}{q}}F(\xi-\eta).

This is the desired inequality.

Case 2:

p-q<0
.
Suppose
F(\xi)\geq F(\eta)
and
F(\xi)\kappa=F(\eta)
. Then,
\kappa^{\frac{q-p}{q}}\leq 1
. Akin to the previous case, we estimate,

\displaystyle F\left(V_{p,q}(\xi)-V_{p,q}(\eta)\right)

\displaystyle=F(\eta)^{\frac{p-q}{q}}\kappa^{\frac{q-p}{q}}\frac{F(\kappa^{\frac{q-p}{q}}\xi-\eta)-F(\eta)}{\kappa^{\frac{q-p}{q}}}+F(\eta)^{\frac{p}{q}}

\displaystyle\leq F(\xi)^{\frac{p-q}{q}}\left[F(\xi-\eta)-F(\eta)\right]+F(\eta)^{\frac{p}{q}}

\displaystyle=F(\xi)^{\frac{p-q}{q}}F(\xi-\eta)+F(\eta)^{\frac{p}{q}}-F(\eta)F(\xi)^{\frac{p-q}{q}}.

Since
F(\xi)\geq F(\eta)
,

F(\eta)^{\frac{p}{q}}-F(\eta)F(\xi)^{\frac{p-q}{q}}\leq F(\xi)^{\frac{p-q}{q}}\lvert F(\xi)-F(\eta)\rvert\leq F(\xi)^{\frac{p-q}{q}}F(\xi-\eta).

Recalling
p-q<0
, combining the two previous estimates with the observation that when
\lvert a\rvert\geq\lvert b\rvert
and
\alpha<0
,
\lvert a\rvert^{\alpha}\leq 2^{-\alpha}\lvert a+b\rvert^{\alpha}
finishes the first inequality. We now show the opposite. Assume without loss of generality
F(\xi)>F(\eta)>0
, noting that if
F(\eta)=0
or
F(\xi)=F(\eta)
, there is nothing to prove. Then, using the reverse triangle inequality,

\displaystyle F(V_{p,q}(\eta)-V_{p,q}(\xi))

\displaystyle=F\left(F(\eta)^{\frac{p-q}{q}}(\eta-\xi)-\left(F(\xi)^{\frac{p-q}{q}}-F(\eta)^{\frac{p-q}{q}}\right)\xi\right)

\displaystyle\geq F(\eta)^{\frac{p-q}{q}}F(\eta-\xi)-\left(F(\xi)^{\frac{p-q}{q}}-F(\eta)^{\frac{p-q}{q}}\right)F(\xi)

\displaystyle\geq F(\eta)^{\frac{p-q}{q}}F(\eta-\xi)

\displaystyle\gtrsim\left(F(\eta)+F(\xi)\right)^{\frac{p-q}{q}}F(\eta-\xi).

Since
F(\zeta)=F(-\zeta)
for all
\zeta\in\mathbb{Y}
, this is the desired inequality.
∎
