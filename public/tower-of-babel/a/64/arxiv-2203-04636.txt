Abstract
We present a lattice calculation of the leading corrections to the masses of nucleons and Δ \Delta resonances. These are obtained in QCD+QED at 1 1 st order in the Isospin Breaking parameters α E ​ M \alpha_{EM} , the electromagnetic coupling, and m ^ d − m ^ u Λ Q ​ C ​ D \frac{{\hat{m}}_{d}-{\hat{m}}_{u}}{\Lambda_{QCD}} , coming from the mass difference between u u and d d quarks.

4
Conclusion

In this work we’ve computed on the lattice the LIBEs in the spectrum of mesons and baryons,
getting a prediction for the masses and IB mass splittings of nucleons and the
\Delta(1232)
resonances.
In particular, we note that the full spectrum of the
\Delta(1232)
quadruplet is not completely determined experimentally yet, motivating its investigation.
The values we found are compatible within at most
1.5\sigma
with the experimental predictions.
This is so despite the approximations introduced in the calculation, indicating that at our level of precision the neglected diagrams are physically suppressed as expected.
Their neglection introduce nevertheless systematic effects which can be known only by direct evaluation,
and which we aim to include in a future work.
