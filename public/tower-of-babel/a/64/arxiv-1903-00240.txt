Alternative to Morse-Novikov Theory for a closed 1-form (I)
Dan Burghelea

Abstract
This paper extends the Alternative to Morse-Novikov Theory we have proposed in [ 1 ] from real-and angle-valued map to closed 1-forms. For a topological closed 1-form ω \omega on a compact ANR X , X, a concept generalizing closed differential 1-form on a compact manifold, under the mild hypothesis of tameness, a field κ \kappa and a non-negative integer r r we propose two configurations 𝜹 r ω : ℝ → ℤ ≥ 0 \boldsymbol{\delta}^{\omega}_{r}:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0} and 𝜸 r ω : ℝ > 0 → ℤ ≥ 0 \boldsymbol{\gamma}^{\omega}_{r}:\mathbb{R}_{>0}\to\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0} which recover Novikov-Betti numbers and the Novikov complex associated with a Morse closed 1-form with non degenerated zeros. Precisely, the sum of the multiplicities of the points in the support of 𝜹 r ω \boldsymbol{\delta}^{\omega}_{r} equals the r − r- th Novikov-Betti number and that of the points in the support of 𝜸 r ω \boldsymbol{\gamma}^{\omega}_{r} equals the rank of the boundary map in the Novikov complex. We formulate the basic properties of these configurations, the stability and the Poincare duality when X X is a κ − \kappa- orientable closed topological manifold which in full generality will be proven in the second part of this work.

Topological closed one forms and tameness

2.1
Topological closed 1-form

A topological closed 1-form, abbreviated TC1-form, extends the concept of closed differential 1-form on a smooth manifold
M
to an arbitrary topological space
X.
One way to obtain this is to view it as an equivalence class
of multivalued maps (first definition), an other way is to view it as an equivalence class of equivariant maps on the associated principal
\mathbb{Z}^{k}-
covering (second definition).

First definition

1.

A
multi-valued map
is
a systems
\{U_{\alpha},f_{\alpha}:U_{\alpha}\to\mathbb{R},\alpha\in A\}
s.t.

(a)

U_{\alpha}
are open sets with
X=\cup U_{\alpha},

(b)

f_{\alpha}
are continuous maps s.t
f_{\alpha}-f_{\beta}:U_{\alpha}\cap U_{\beta}\to\mathbb{R}
is locally constant.

2.

Two multi-valued maps are
equivalent
if put together remain a multi-valued map.

Definition 2.1

A
TC1-form

is an equivalence class of multi-valued maps.

A TC1-form
\omega
determines a cohomology class
\xi=\xi(\omega)\in H^{1}(X;\mathbb{R}).

It suffices to show that a representative
\{U_{\alpha},f_{\alpha}:U_{\alpha}\to\mathbb{R}\}

\gamma[a,b]\subset U_{\alpha}
for some

\int_{\gamma}\omega=f_{\alpha}(b)-f_{\alpha}(a);

One denotes by

Clearly
