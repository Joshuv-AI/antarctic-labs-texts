On the weak solutions for the MHD systems with controllable total energy and cross helicity
Changxing Miao and, Weikui Ye

Abstract
In this paper, we prove the non-uniqueness of three-dimensional magneto-hydrodynamic (MHD) system in C ⁡ ( [ 0 , T ] , L 2 ​ ( 𝕋 3 ) ) C([0,T];L^{2}(\mathbb{T}^{3})) for any initial data in H β ¯ ​ ( 𝕋 3 ) H^{\bar{\beta}}(\mathbb{T}^{3}) ( β ¯ > 0 \bar{\beta}>0 ), by exhibiting that the total energy and the cross helicity can be controlled in a given positive time interval. Our results extend the non-uniqueness results of the ideal MHD system to the viscous and resistive MHD system. Different from the ideal MHD system, the dissipative effect in the viscous and resistive MHD system prevents the nonlinear term from balancing the stress error ( R ̊ q , M ̊ q ) (\mathring{R}_{q},\mathring{M}_{q}) as doing in [ 4 ] . We introduce the box flows and construct the perturbation consisting in seven different kinds of flows in convex integral scheme, which ensures that the iteration works and yields the non-uniqueness.



Keywords
: convex integral iteration, the MHD system, weak solutions, cross helicity, non-uniqueness.

Inspired by

(v(0,x),b(0,x))=(v^{\textup{in}},b^{\textup{in}}),~~\text{and}~~\int_{\mathbb{T}^{3}}(|v|^{2}+|b|^{2})\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}x=e(t),~~\text{and}~~~\int_{\mathbb{T}^{3}}v\cdot b\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}x=h(t),~~t\in[1,T],

2.2
The proof sketch of Proposition
2.1

Starting from a solution
(v_{q},b_{q},p_{q},\mathring{R}_{q},\mathring{M}_{q})
satisfying the estimates as in Proposition
2.1
, the broad scheme of the iteration is as follows.

1.

We defined
(v_{\ell_{q}},b_{\ell_{q}},p_{\ell_{q}},\mathring{R}_{\ell_{q}},\mathring{M}_{\ell_{q}})
by mollification, and it is standard in convex integration schemes.

2.

We define a family of exact solutions
(\textsf{{v}}_{l},\textsf{{b}}_{l})_{l\geq 0}
to MHD by exactly solving the MHD system with initial data
(\textsf{{v}}_{l},\textsf{{b}}_{l})|_{t=t_{l}}=(v_{\ell_{q}}(t_{l}),b_{\ell_{q}}(t_{l}))
, where
t_{l}=l\tau_{q}
defines an evenly spaced paritition of
[0,T]
.

3.

These solutions are glued together by a partition of unity, leading to the tuple
(\bar{v}_{q},\bar{b}_{q},\bar{p}_{q},\mathring{\bar{R}}_{q},\mathring{\bar{M}}_{q})
. The stress error terms are zero when
t\in J_{l},~l\geq 0
, see
[
6
,
5
]
.

4.

We define
(v_{q+1},b_{q+1})=(\bar{v}_{q}+w_{q+1},\bar{b}_{q}+d_{q+1})
by constructing a perturbation
(w_{q+1},d_{q+1})
.

5.

Finally, we prove that the inductive estimates (
2.6
)–(
2.11
) hold with
q
replaced by
q+1
.

Step 4 is moderately involved and we breaks it into the following sub-steps :

1.

For times
t\geq 1
, we use the ‘squiggling’ cutoffs
\eta_{l}
from
[
6
,
21
]
that allow energy to be added at such times, even outside the support of
(\mathring{\bar{R}}_{q},\mathring{\bar{M}}_{q})
, while cancelling a large part of
(\mathring{\bar{R}}_{q},\mathring{\bar{M}}_{q})
.

2.

For times
t<1
, we instead employ the straight cutoffs introduced in
[
19
,
5
]
. This ensures that
(\bar{v}_{q},\bar{b}_{q})|_{t=0}=(v^{\textup{in}}*\psi_{\ell_{q-1}}*\psi_{\ell_{q}},b^{\textup{in}}*\psi_{\ell_{q-1}}*\psi_{\ell_{q}})
.

3.

Then we construct the seven parts of the perturbation by using the “box flows”.

\bullet~
Principal flows:
(w^{\textup{(p)}}_{q+1},d^{\textup{(p)}}_{q+1})
plays a role in cancelling the Reynolds and magnetic stresses
(\mathring{\bar{R}}_{q},\mathring{\bar{M}}_{q})
, while this would lead to some extra errors.

\bullet~
Temporal flows:
(w^{\textup{(t)}}_{q+1},d^{\textup{(t)}}_{q+1})
is used to cancel the extra errors which stem from
\div(\phi_{\bar{k}}\bar{k})
, where
\phi_{\bar{k}}
is a traveling-wave.

\bullet~
Inverse traveling wave flows: (
w^{\textup{(v)}}_{q+1},d^{\textup{(v)}}_{q+1}
) is used to cancel the extra errors produced by
\div(\phi_{\bar{\bar{k}}}\bar{\bar{k}})
, where
\phi_{\bar{\bar{k}}}
does not depend on
t
.

\bullet~
Heat conduction flows :
(w^{\Delta}_{q+1},d^{\Delta}_{q+1})
is used to
cancel the extra errors producing by the inverse traveling wave flows (
w^{\textup{(v)}}_{q+1},d^{\textup{(v)}}_{q+1}
).

\bullet~
Corrector flows:
(w^{\textup{(c)}}_{q+1},d^{\textup{(c)}}_{q+1})
is introduced to correct principal perturbation
(w^{\textup{(p)}}_{q+1},d^{\textup{(p)}}_{q+1})
to enforce the incompressibility condition.

\bullet~
Initial flows:
(w^{\textup{(s)}}_{q+1},d^{\textup{(s)}}_{q+1})
can ensure that
(v_{q+1},b_{q+1})|_{t=0}=(v^{\textup{in}}*\psi_{\ell_{q}},b^{\textup{in}}*\psi_{\ell_{q}})
. It should be noted that the above five types of flows are zero when
t=0
.

\bullet~
Helicity flows:
(w^{\textup{(h)}}_{q+1},d^{\textup{(h)}}_{q+1})
makes the cross helicity satisfy (
2.11
) at
q+1
level.

It is noteworthy that the first five flows are enough to produce a weak solution
(v,b)
for (
1.1
), initial flows and helicity flows are used to control the helicity and show the non-uniqueness.

In this section, we construct the perturbation

4.1
Stresses associated with the MHD system

Let
(v_{q+1},b_{q+1})=(\bar{v}_{q}+w_{q+1},\bar{b}_{q}+d_{q+1})
. From (
3.25
), we obtain the following MHD system with new Reynolds and magnetic stresses
(\mathring{R}_{q+1},\mathring{M}_{q+1})
:

\left\{\begin{aligned} \partial_{t}v_{q+1}-\Delta v_{q+1}+\div(v_{q+1}\otimes v_{q+1}-b_{q+1}\otimes b_{q+1})&=\div\mathring{R}_{q+1}-\nabla p_{q+1},\\
\partial_{t}b_{q+1}-\Delta b_{q+1}+\div(v_{q+1}\otimes b_{q+1}-b_{q+1}\otimes v_{q+1})&=\div\mathring{M}_{q+1},\\
(v_{q+1},b_{q+1})|_{t=0}&=(v^{\textup{in}}*\psi_{\ell_{q}},b^{\textup{in}}*\psi_{\ell_{q}}),\end{aligned}\right.

where

\displaystyle p_{q+1}\mathrel{\mathop{:}}=

\displaystyle\bar{p}_{q}(x,t)-\tfrac{1}{3}\tr[w_{q+1}\otimes\bar{v}_{q}+\bar{v}_{q}\otimes w_{q+1}-d_{q+1}\otimes\bar{b}_{q}-\bar{b}_{q}\otimes d_{q+1}]-P_{v},

\displaystyle\mathring{R}_{q+1}=

\displaystyle\mathcal{R}[\partial_{t}w_{q+1}-\Delta w_{q+1}]+[w_{q+1}\mathring{\otimes}\bar{v}_{q}+\bar{v}_{q}\mathring{\otimes}w_{q+1}-d_{q+1}\mathring{\otimes}\bar{b}_{q}-\bar{b}_{q}\mathring{\otimes}d_{q+1}]

\displaystyle+\mathcal{R}[\div(w_{q+1}\otimes w_{q+1}-d_{q+1}\otimes d_{q+1})+\div\mathring{\bar{R}}_{q}-\nabla P_{v}]

\displaystyle:=

\displaystyle R_{q+1}^{\textup{lin}}+R_{q+1}^{\textup{osc}},

\displaystyle\mathring{M}_{q+1}=

\displaystyle\mathcal{R}_{a}[\partial_{t}d_{q+1}-\Delta d_{q+1}]+[w_{q+1}\otimes\bar{b}_{q}+\bar{v}_{q}\otimes d_{q+1}-\bar{b}_{q}\otimes w_{q+1}-d_{q+1}\otimes\bar{v}_{q}]

\displaystyle+\mathcal{R}_{a}\mathbb{P}_{H}[\ \div(w_{q+1}\otimes d_{q+1}-d_{q+1}\otimes w_{q+1})+\div\mathring{\bar{M}}_{q}]

\displaystyle:=

\displaystyle M_{q+1}^{\textup{lin}}+M_{q+1}^{\textup{osc}}.

Here we have used the fact that

for any anti-symmetric matrix

. The definition of

can be seen in (

Before introducing the perturbation

Lemma 4.1

by the fact that

supports in some small 3D boxes. Readers can refer to

\displaystyle\rho_{q,0}(t)

\displaystyle\mathrel{\mathop{:}}=\delta_{q+1}\aleph(t)+\tfrac{\rho_{q}(t)(1-\aleph(t))}{\eta_{-1}(t)+\sum_{l=1}^{N_{q}}\int_{\mathbb{T}^{3}}\eta_{l}^{2}\chi_{v}(x,t)\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}x},

where

\displaystyle\|M_{osc}+\mathcal{R}_{a}[(\partial_{t}d^{\textup{(t)}}_{q+1}+\partial_{t}d^{\textup{(v)}}_{q+1})+(\partial_{t}d^{\Delta}_{q+1}-\Delta d^{\Delta}_{q+1}-\Delta d^{\textup{(v)}}_{q+1})]\|_{L^{1}}

\displaystyle\|M_{q+1}^{\textup{lin}}-\mathcal{R}_{a}[(\partial_{t}d^{\textup{(t)}}_{q+1}+\partial_{t}d^{\textup{(v)}}_{q+1})+(\partial_{t}d^{\Delta}_{q+1}-\Delta d^{\Delta}_{q+1}-\Delta d^{\textup{(v)}}_{q+1})]\|_{L^{1}}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\lambda^{-40\alpha}_{q+1}\delta_{q+2},

and

\displaystyle\|\mathring{R}_{q+1}\|_{L^{1}}\leq

\displaystyle\|R_{q+1}^{\textup{osc}}+\mathcal{R}[(\partial_{t}w^{\textup{(t)}}_{q+1}+\partial_{t}w^{\textup{(v)}}_{q+1})+(\partial_{t}w^{\Delta}_{q+1}-\Delta w^{\Delta}_{q+1}-\Delta w^{\textup{(v)}}_{q+1})]\|_{L^{1}}

\displaystyle+\|R_{q+1}^{\textup{lin}}-\mathcal{R}[(\partial_{t}w^{\textup{(t)}}_{q+1}+\partial_{t}w^{\textup{(v)}}_{q+1})+(\partial_{t}w^{\Delta}_{q+1}-\Delta w^{\Delta}_{q+1}-\Delta w^{\textup{(v)}}_{q+1})]\|_{L^{1}}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\lambda^{-40\alpha}_{q+1}\delta_{q+2}.

This fact shows that (

) holds by replacing

with
q+1

Proposition 6.1

(Energy estimate)
.

For all
t\in[1-\tau_{q},T]
, we have

\bigg|e(t)-\int_{\mathbb{T}^{3}}(|v_{q+1}|^{2}+|b_{q+1}|^{2})\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}x-\tfrac{\delta_{q+2}}{2}\bigg|\leq\frac{\delta_{q+2}}{100}.

Proof.

For
t\in[1-\tau_{q},T]
, the total energy error can be rewritten as follows:

\displaystyle e(t)-\int_{\mathbb{T}^{3}}(|v_{q+1}|^{2}+|b_{q+1}|^{2})\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}x-\frac{\delta_{q+2}}{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle[e(t)-\int_{\mathbb{T}^{3}}(|\bar{v}_{q}|^{2}+|\bar{b}_{q}|^{2})\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}x-\tfrac{\delta_{q+2}}{2}]-\int_{\mathbb{T}^{3}}(|w_{q+1}|^{2}+|d_{q+1}|^{2})\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}x-2\int_{\mathbb{T}^{3}}(w_{q+1}\cdot\bar{v}_{q}+d_{q+1}\cdot\bar{b}_{q})\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}x

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle[e(t)-\int_{\mathbb{T}^{3}}(|\bar{v}_{q}|^{2}+|\bar{b}_{q}|^{2})\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}x-\tfrac{\delta_{q+2}}{2}]+e_{low,0}

\displaystyle-\int_{\mathbb{T}^{3}}(w^{\textup{(p)}}_{q+1}+w^{\textup{(h)}}_{q+1})\cdot(w^{\textup{(p)}}_{q+1}+w^{\textup{(h)}}_{q+1})+(d^{\textup{(p)}}_{q+1}+d^{\textup{(h)}}_{q+1})\cdot(d^{\textup{(p)}}_{q+1}+d^{\textup{(h)}}_{q+1})\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}x,

(6.1)

where

\displaystyle e_{low,0}:=

\displaystyle-\int_{\mathbb{T}^{3}}\big((w^{\textup{(p)}}_{q+1}+w^{\textup{(h)}}_{q+1})\cdot(w^{\textup{(v)}}_{q+1}+w^{\Delta}_{q+1}+w^{\textup{(t)}}_{q+1}+w^{\textup{(c)}}_{q+1}+w^{\textup{(s)}}_{q+1})

(6.2)

\displaystyle+(w^{\textup{(v)}}_{q+1}+w^{\Delta}_{q+1}+w^{\textup{(t)}}_{q+1}+w^{\textup{(c)}}_{q+1}+w^{\textup{(s)}}_{q+1})\cdot w_{q+1}

\displaystyle+(d^{\textup{(p)}}_{q+1}+d^{\textup{(h)}}_{q+1})\cdot(d^{\textup{(v)}}_{q+1}+d^{\Delta}_{q+1}+d^{\textup{(t)}}_{q+1}+d^{\textup{(c)}}_{q+1}+d^{\textup{(s)}}_{q+1})

(6.3)

\displaystyle+(d^{\textup{(v)}}_{q+1}+d^{\Delta}_{q+1}+d^{\textup{(t)}}_{q+1}+d^{\textup{(c)}}_{q+1}+d^{\textup{(s)}}_{q+1})\cdot d_{q+1}\big)\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}x

\displaystyle-2\int_{\mathbb{T}^{3}}w_{q+1}\cdot\bar{v}_{q}+d_{q+1}\cdot\bar{b}_{q}\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}x.

(6.4)

For the last term on the right-hand side of equality (
6
), using
w^{\textup{(pu)}}_{q+1}\cdot w^{\textup{(pb)}}_{q+1}=w^{\textup{(p)}}_{q+1}\cdot w^{\textup{(h)}}_{q+1}=d^{\textup{(p)}}_{q+1}\cdot d^{\textup{(h)}}_{q+1}=0
and
\tr\mathring{\bar{R}}_{q}=0
, we have

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{T}^{3}}(w^{\textup{(p)}}_{q+1}+w^{\textup{(h)}}_{q+1})\cdot(w^{\textup{(p)}}_{q+1}+w^{\textup{(h)}}_{q+1})+(d^{\textup{(p)}}_{q+1}+d^{\textup{(h)}}_{q+1})\cdot(d^{\textup{(p)}}_{q+1}+d^{\textup{(h)}}_{q+1})\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}x

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{T}^{3}}\tr(w^{\textup{(pu)}}_{q+1}\otimes w^{\textup{(pu)}}_{q+1})+(|w^{\textup{(pb)}}_{q+1}|^{2}+|d^{\textup{(p)}}_{q+1}|^{2}+|d^{\textup{(h)}}_{q+1}|^{2}+|w^{\textup{(h)}}_{q+1}|^{2})\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}x

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{T}^{3}}\tr\Big[\sum_{l}\eta^{2}_{l}\rho_{v,q}\sum_{k\in\Lambda_{v}}a^{2}_{k}\psi^{2}_{k}\phi^{2}_{k,\bar{k},\bar{\bar{k}}}\bar{k}\otimes\bar{k}\Big]\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}x

\displaystyle+\int_{\mathbb{T}^{3}}\Big(|w^{\textup{(pb)}}_{q+1}|^{2}+|d^{\textup{(p)}}_{q+1}|^{2}+|d^{\textup{(h)}}_{q+1}|^{2}+|w^{\textup{(h)}}_{q+1}|^{2}\Big)\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}x

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{T}^{3}}\tr\Big[\sum_{l}\eta^{2}_{l}(\rho_{v,q}{\rm Id}-\mathring{\bar{R}}_{q})\Big]\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}x+\int_{\mathbb{T}^{3}}\Big(|w^{\textup{(pb)}}_{q+1}|^{2}+|d^{\textup{(p)}}_{q+1}|^{2}+|d^{\textup{(h)}}_{q+1}|^{2}+|w^{\textup{(h)}}_{q+1}|^{2}\Big)\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}x

\displaystyle+\int_{\mathbb{T}^{3}}\tr\Big[\sum_{l;k\in\Lambda_{v}}\eta^{2}_{l}\rho_{v,q}a^{2}_{k}[\mathbb{P}_{>0}(\psi^{2}_{k})\phi^{2}_{k,\bar{k},\bar{\bar{k}}}+\mathbb{P}_{>0}\phi^{2}_{k,\bar{k},\bar{\bar{k}}}]\bar{k}\otimes\bar{k}\Big]\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}x

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 3\int_{\mathbb{T}^{3}}\sum_{l}\eta^{2}_{l}\rho_{v,q}\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}x+\int_{\mathbb{T}^{3}}\Big(|w^{\textup{(pb)}}_{q+1}|^{2}+|d^{\textup{(p)}}_{q+1}|^{2}+|d^{\textup{(h)}}_{q+1}|^{2}+|w^{\textup{(h)}}_{q+1}|^{2}\Big)\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}x

\displaystyle+\int_{\mathbb{T}^{3}}\tr\Big[\sum_{l;k\in\Lambda_{v}}a^{2}_{v,l,k}[\mathbb{P}_{>0}(\psi^{2}_{k})\phi^{2}_{k,\bar{k},\bar{\bar{k}}}+\mathbb{P}_{>0}\phi^{2}_{k,\bar{k},\bar{\bar{k}}}]\bar{k}\otimes\bar{k}\Big]\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}x.

(6.5)

Since
\eta_{-1}(t)=\aleph(t)=0
for

\displaystyle=\frac{\chi_{v}\rho_{q}(t)}{\sum_{l=1}^{N_{q}}\int_{\mathbb{T}^{3}}\eta^{2}_{l}\chi_{v}(\tilde{x},t)\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}\tilde{x}}.

(6.6)

Recalling the definition of

), we deduce that

\displaystyle 3\int_{\mathbb{T}^{3}}\sum_{l}\eta^{2}_{l}\rho_{v,q}\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}x+\int_{\mathbb{T}^{3}}(|w^{\textup{(pb)}}_{q+1}|^{2}+|d^{\textup{(p)}}_{q+1}|^{2}+|d^{\textup{(h)}}_{q+1}|^{2}+|w^{\textup{(h)}}_{q+1}|^{2})\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}x

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 3\rho_{q}(t)+E(t)=e(t)-\int_{\mathbb{T}^{3}}|\bar{v}_{q}|^{2}+|\bar{b}_{q}|^{2}\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}x-\frac{\delta_{q+2}}{2}.

(

\displaystyle e(t)-\int_{\mathbb{T}^{3}}|\bar{v}_{q}|^{2}+|\bar{b}_{q}|^{2}\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}x-\tfrac{\delta_{q+2}}{2}

\displaystyle+\tr\int_{\mathbb{T}^{3}}\sum_{l;k\in\Lambda_{v}}a^{2}_{v,l,k}[\mathbb{P}_{>0}(\psi^{2}_{k})(\phi^{2}_{k,\bar{k},\bar{\bar{k}}})+\mathbb{P}_{>0}(\phi^{2}_{k,\bar{k},\bar{\bar{k}}})]\bar{k}\otimes\bar{k}\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}x.

(6.8)

Next, using

\Big|\int_{\mathbb{T}^{3}}f\mathbb{P}_{\geq c}gdx\Big|=\Big|\int_{\mathbb{T}^{3}}|\nabla|^{L}f|\nabla|^{-L}\mathbb{P}_{\geq c}gdx\Big|\leq c^{-L}\|g\|_{L^{2}}\|f\|_{H^{L}}

(6.9)

\displaystyle+(d^{\textup{(p)}}_{q+1}+d^{\textup{(h)}}_{q+1})\cdot(d^{\Delta}_{q+1}+d^{\textup{(t)}}_{q+1}+d^{\textup{(c)}}_{q+1}+d^{\textup{(s)}}_{q+1})+(d^{\Delta}_{q+1}+d^{\textup{(t)}}_{q+1}+d^{\textup{(c)}}_{q+1}+d^{\textup{(s)}}_{q+1})\cdot d_{q+1}\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}x\Big|

(6.12)

Finally, putting (

Proposition 6.2

(Helicity estimate)
.

For all
t\in[1-\tau_{q},T]
, we have

\bigg|h(t)-\int_{\mathbb{T}^{3}}v_{q+1}\cdot b_{q+1}\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}x-\frac{\delta_{q+2}}{200}\bigg|\leq\frac{\delta_{q+2}}{1000}.

Proof.

Similar to the proof of Proposition
6.1
, it suffices to control the helicity for
t\in[1-\tau_{q},T]
. Due to

\displaystyle h_{b,q}(t)=\frac{h_{q}(t)}{\sum_{l=0}^{N_{q}}\int_{\mathbb{T}^{3}}\eta^{2}_{l}(x,t)\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}x},~~~t\in[1-\tau_{q},T],

(6.14)

we rewrite that

\displaystyle h(t)-\int_{\mathbb{T}^{3}}v_{q+1}\cdot b_{q+1}\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}x-\tfrac{\delta_{q+2}}{200}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\Big[h(t)-\int_{\mathbb{T}^{3}}\bar{v}_{q}\cdot\bar{b}_{q}\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}x-\tfrac{\delta_{q+2}}{200}\Big]-\int_{\mathbb{T}^{3}}w_{q+1}\cdot d_{q+1}\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}x-\int_{\mathbb{T}^{3}}w_{q+1}\cdot\bar{b}_{q}+\bar{v}_{q}\cdot d_{q+1}\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}x

\displaystyle:=

\displaystyle\Big[h(t)-\int_{\mathbb{T}^{3}}\bar{v}_{q}\cdot\bar{b}_{q}\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}x-\tfrac{\delta_{q+2}}{200}\Big]-\int_{\mathbb{T}^{3}}w^{\textup{(h)}}_{q+1}\cdot d^{\textup{(h)}}_{q+1}\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}x-\int_{\mathbb{T}^{3}}w^{\textup{(p)}}_{q+1}\cdot d^{\textup{(p)}}_{q+1}\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}x+h_{low,0}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\Big[h(t)-\int_{\mathbb{T}^{3}}\bar{v}_{q}\cdot\bar{b}_{q}\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}x-\tfrac{\delta_{q+2}}{200}\Big]-\int_{\mathbb{T}^{3}}w^{\textup{(h)}}_{q+1}\cdot d^{\textup{(h)}}_{q+1}\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}x-0+h_{low,0},

(6.15)

where

\displaystyle h_{low,0}:=

\displaystyle-\int_{\mathbb{T}^{3}}(w_{q+1}-w^{\textup{(h)}}_{q+1}-w^{\textup{(p)}}_{q+1})\cdot(d_{q+1}-d^{\textup{(h)}}_{q+1}-d^{\textup{(p)}}_{q+1})+(w^{\textup{(h)}}_{q+1}+w^{\textup{(p)}}_{q+1})\cdot(d_{q+1}-d^{\textup{(h)}}_{q+1}-d^{\textup{(p)}}_{q+1})

\displaystyle+(w_{q+1}-w^{\textup{(h)}}_{q+1}-w^{\textup{(p)}}_{q+1})\cdot(d^{\textup{(h)}}_{q+1}+d^{\textup{(p)}}_{q+1})\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}x.

By the definitions of

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle h_{b,q}\int_{\mathbb{T}^{3}}\sum_{l;k\in\Lambda_{s}}\eta^{2}_{l}\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}x+\Big\{h_{b,q}\int_{\mathbb{T}^{3}}\sum_{l;k\in\Lambda_{s}}\eta^{2}_{l}\mathbb{P}_{>0}(\psi^{2}_{k})\phi^{2}_{k,\bar{k},\bar{\bar{k}}}\bar{k}\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}x+h_{b,q}\int_{\mathbb{T}^{3}}\sum_{l;k\in\Lambda_{s}}\eta^{2}_{l}\mathbb{P}_{>0}(\phi^{2}_{k,\bar{k},\bar{\bar{k}}})\bar{k}\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}x\Big\}

Using the standard integration by parts and Remark

Since
