Abstract
In this paper we consider an abstract class of time-dependent quasi variational-hemivariational inequalities which involves history-dependent operators and a set of unilateral constraints. First, we establish the existence and uniqueness of solution by using a recent result for elliptic variational-hemivariational inequalities in reflexive Banach spaces combined with a fixed-point principle for history-dependent operators. Then, we apply the abstract result to show the unique weak solvability to a quasistatic viscoelastic frictional contact problem. The contact law involves a unilateral Signorini-type condition for the normal velocity and the nonmonotone normal damped response condition while the friction condition is a version of the Coulomb law of dry friction in which the friction bound depends on the accumulated slip.

\displaystyle\leq|\alpha(t_{n},\zeta_{n})-\alpha(t,\zeta)|(c_{0g}+c_{1g}\|v\|_{X})\|M\|\|v-w_{n}\|+\alpha(t,\zeta)\,g^{0}(Mv;Mv-Mw_{n})

Further, we note that condition

(b) is equivalent
to the condition

\langle\partial g(v_{1})-\partial g(v_{2}),v_{1}-v_{2}\rangle\geq-m_{g}\|v_{1}-v_{2}\|^{2}_{X}\ \ \mbox{for all}\ \ v_{1},v_{2}\in X.

(2.3)

and the references therein. Examples

In the proof of the main result we need the

Lemma 4

Under hypotheses
H(A)
,
H(j)
,
H(M)
and
(H_{0})
,
the multivalued map

A(t,\lambda,\cdot)+M^{*}\partial j(t,\zeta,M\cdot)\colon V\to 2^{V^{*}}\setminus\{\emptyset\}

is monotone for all
(t,\lambda,\zeta)\in[0,T]\times E\times Z
.

Proof.
Let
(t,\lambda,\zeta)\in[0,T]\times E\times Z

be fixed,

v_{1}
,
v_{2}\in V
, and

z_{1}\in\partial j(t,\zeta,Mv_{1})
,

z_{2}\in\partial j(t,\zeta,Mv_{2})

By the definition of the generalized subgradient
and

, we have

\displaystyle\hskip-11.38092pt\langle M^{*}z_{1}-M^{*}z_{2},v_{2}-v_{1}\rangle=\langle z_{1},M(v_{2}-v_{1})\rangle_{X}+\langle z_{2},M(v_{1}-v_{2})\rangle_{X}

\displaystyle\quad\leq j^{0}(t,\zeta,Mv_{1};Mv_{2}-Mv_{1})+j^{0}(t,\zeta,Mv_{2};Mv_{1}-Mv_{2})\leq m_{j}\|M\|^{2}\|v_{1}-v_{2}\|^{2}.

Hence, by

\displaystyle\hskip-8.5359pt\langle A(t,\lambda,v_{1})+M^{*}z_{1}-A(t,\lambda,v_{2})-M^{*}z_{2},v_{1}-v_{2}\rangle=\langle A(t,\lambda,v_{1})-A(t,\lambda,v_{2}),v_{1}-v_{2}\rangle

which completes the proof.
