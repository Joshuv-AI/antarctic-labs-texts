1.
Introduction

The system of barotropic compressible magnetohydrodynamic(MHD)
equations in
\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3}
can be read as follows:

(1.1)

\begin{cases}\operatorname{div}(\rho\mathbf{u})=0,\\
-\nu\Delta\mathbf{u}-(\lambda+\nu)\nabla\operatorname{div}\mathbf{u}+\operatorname{div}(\rho\mathbf{u}\otimes\mathbf{u})-\operatorname{div}(\mathbf{b}\otimes\mathbf{b})+\nabla(P+\frac{|\mathbf{b}|^{2}}{2})=0,\\
-\Delta\mathbf{b}+\operatorname{div}(\rho\mathbf{u}\otimes\mathbf{b})-\operatorname{div}(\mathbf{b}\otimes\mathbf{u})=0,\\
\operatorname{div}\mathbf{b}=0,\end{cases}

where the vector
\mathbf{u}
denotes the flow velocity field and
the scalar function
\rho
represents the density of the fluid.
Without loss of generality, we consider the flows with
\gamma-
law
pressure:

P(\rho):=a\rho^{\gamma},\quad a>0,\quad\gamma>1.

The shear viscosity
\nu
and bulk viscosity
\lambda
are both
constants and satisfy

\nu>0\ ,\ \lambda+\frac{2}{3}\nu>0.

We consider the initial value problem of (
1.1
),
which requires initial conditions

(1.2)

\textbf{u}(x,0)=\textbf{u}_{0}(x)\quad\text{and}\quad\textbf{b}(x,0)=\textbf{b}_{0}(x),\qquad x\in\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3}.

the compressible 3D MHD equations. The local strong solutions to the
compressible MHD with large initial data were obtained, by Vol’pert
and Khudiaev
[
27

[

[
15

Before we look at the main results, we give some definitions for
functional spaces. Given

\|f\|_{L^{p,q}(\mathbb{R}^{3})}:=\begin{cases}\left(\int_{0}^{\infty}t^{q-1}|\{x\in\mathbb{R}^{3}:|f(x)|>t\}|^{\frac{q}{p}}\,dt\right)^{\frac{1}{q}},\quad\mathrm{if}\,\quad q<+\infty,\\
\sup\limits_{t>0}t|\{x\in\mathbb{R}^{3}:|f(x)|>t\}|^{\frac{1}{p}},\quad\mathrm{if}\,\quad q=+\infty.\end{cases}

The space satisfies the continuous embedding (see e.g.
[
21
]
)

(1.3)

L^{p}(\mathbb{R}^{3})=L^{p,p}(\mathbb{R}^{3})\hookrightarrow L^{p,q}(\mathbb{R}^{3})\hookrightarrow L^{p,\infty}(\mathbb{R}^{3})\ ,\ p\leq q<\infty.

.

For

Proof of Theorem
1.1

The following inequalities in Lorentz spaces are useful.

Lemma 2.1

(
[
23
]
)
.

Let
f\in L^{p_{1},q_{1}}(\mathbb{R}^{3})
and
g\in L^{p_{2},q_{2}}(\mathbb{R}^{3})
with
1\leq p_{1},p_{2}\leq\infty
,
1\leq q_{1},q_{2}\leq\infty.
Then
fg\in L^{p,q}(\mathbb{R}^{3})
with

\frac{1}{p}=\frac{1}{p_{1}}+\frac{1}{p_{2}}
,

\frac{1}{q}\leq\frac{1}{q_{1}}+\frac{1}{q_{2}}
and

\begin{split}\|fg\|_{L^{p,q}(\mathbb{R}^{3})}\leq C\|f\|_{L^{p_{1},q_{1}}(\mathbb{R}^{3})}\|g\|_{L^{p_{2},q_{2}}(\mathbb{R}^{3})}\end{split}

for a constant
C>0.

Lemma 2.2

(
[
B1976
]
)
.

Let
\Omega
be a bounded domain in
\mathbb{R}^{n}
,
1<p<\infty
,

1<q\leq\infty
and
f\in L^{p,q}(\Omega)
. Then

\begin{split}\|\nabla^{2}(-\Delta)^{-1}f\|_{L^{p,q}(\Omega)}\leq C\|f\|_{L^{p,q}(\Omega)},\end{split}

where the constant
C>0
is independent of
\Omega
.

To obtain the estimate for the pressure term, we recall the
following lemma from
[
20
]
.

Lemma 2.3
.

Let

p_{1}\in L^{r_{1}}(\mathbb{R}^{3}),

p_{2}\in L^{r_{2}}(\mathbb{R}^{3})
with
1\leq r_{1},r_{2}<\infty.
Suppose that
P-p_{1}-p_{2}
is weakly harmonic, that
is

\Delta(P-p_{1}-p_{2})=0

in the sense of distribution, then there exists a constant

such

\begin{split}P-p_{1}-p_{2}=c\quad\,\mbox{a.e.}\quad\,x\in\mathbb{R}^{3}\end{split}

If furthermore

a.e., then we also have

.

Taking the div operation on both sides of
\eqref{MHD}_{2}
, we have

\Delta(P-p_{1}-p_{2})=0
, where

p_{1}:=(-\Delta)^{-1}\partial_{i}\partial_{j}(\rho u_{i}u_{j})\quad\mbox{and}\quad p_{2}:=(\lambda+2\nu)\operatorname{div}\mathbf{u}.

Using the assumption

and

\dot{H}^{1}(\mathbb{R}^{3})\hookrightarrow L^{6}(\mathbb{R}^{3})
, it follows

p_{1}\in L^{3}(\mathbb{R}^{3})\ ,\ p_{2}\in L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{3}).

Due to Lemma
2.3
, there exists a constant

such
that

a\rho^{\gamma}=P=c+p_{1}+p_{2}.

Considering the function

P_{1}:=\rho^{\gamma-1}-(\frac{c}{a})^{\frac{\gamma-1}{\gamma}}=(\frac{c+p_{1}+p_{2}}{a})^{\frac{\gamma-1}{\gamma}}-(\frac{c}{a})^{\frac{\gamma-1}{\gamma}}
,
we have

\nabla P=\nabla(a\rho^{\gamma})=\frac{a\gamma}{\gamma-1}\rho\nabla(\rho^{\gamma-1})=\frac{a\gamma}{\gamma-1}\rho\nabla P_{1}

and

\begin{split}|P_{1}\rho|\leq(|p_{1}|+|p_{2}|).\end{split}

]

For the convenience of our readers, we give a sketch of the proof.

for some constant

Taking the inner product of
\eqref{MHD}_{2}
with

\mathbf{u}\varphi_{R}
and
\eqref{MHD}_{3}
with
\mathbf{b}\varphi_{R}

and after integrating by parts, it follows that

\begin{split}&\int_{B_{\frac{R}{2}}}\Big(\nu|\nabla\mathbf{u}|^{2}+|\nabla\mathbf{b}|^{2}\Big)\varphi_{R}\,dx+(\lambda+\nu)\int_{\frac{R}{2}}|\operatorname{div}\mathbf{u}|^{2}\,dx\\
&=-\nu\int_{\mathcal{C}(R/2,R)}(|\mathbf{u}|^{2}+|\mathbf{b}|^{2})\Delta\varphi_{R}\,dx-2(\lambda+\nu)\int_{\mathcal{C}(R/2,R)}\operatorname{div}\mathbf{u}\mathbf{u}\cdot\nabla\varphi_{R}\,dx\\
&\quad-\int_{\mathcal{C}(R/2,R)}|\mathbf{u}|^{2}\nabla\varphi_{R}\cdot\rho\mathbf{u}\,dx+\int_{\mathcal{C}(R/2,R)}|\mathbf{b}|^{2}\nabla\varphi_{R}\cdot\mathbf{u}\,dx-\int_{\mathcal{C}(R/2,R)}\nabla P\cdot\mathbf{u}\varphi_{R}\,dx:=\sum_{i=1}^{6}\mathcal{I}_{i},\end{split}

where we use

\begin{split}&-2\nu\int_{\mathbb{R}^{3}}\nabla\mathbf{u}:\mathbf{u}\otimes\nabla\varphi_{R}\,dx=\nu\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}|\mathbf{u}|^{2}\Delta(\varphi_{R})\,dx.\end{split}

,

3\leq q\leq\infty

p=q=3

\begin{split}\int_{\mathcal{C}(R/2,R)}|\mathbf{u}|^{2}|\Delta\varphi_{R}|\,dx\lesssim R^{1-\frac{6}{p}}\|\mathbf{u}\|^{2}_{L^{p,q}(\mathcal{C}(R/2,R))},\end{split}

\end{split}

For

, we have
