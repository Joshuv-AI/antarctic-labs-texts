Radii Constants for Functions with Fixed Second Coefficient
Shalini Rana, Om Ahuja, Naveen Kumar Jain

Abstract
. We introduce three classes of analytic functions with fixed second coefficient which are defined using the class 𝒫 \mathcal{P} of analytic functions with positive real part. The objective is to determine radii such that the three classes are contained in various subclasses of starlike functions. The radii estimated in the present investigation are better than the radii obtained earlier. Furthermore, connections with previous known results are shown.

1.
Introduction

Let
\Delta
denote the open unit disc in
\mathbb{C}
and the class
\mathcal{A}
be defined as collection of all analytic functions in
\Delta
satisfying
f(0)=1
and
f^{\prime}(0)=1
. The class
\mathcal{S}
is defined to be collection of univalent functions in class
\mathcal{A}
.
The well known Bieberbach theorem states that for a univalent function
f(z)=z+a_{2}z^{2}+...
the bound on second coefficient, that is
|a_{2}|\leq 2
, plays an important role in the study of univalent function theory. This bound attracted the interest of many mathematicians which led to the investigation of the class
A_{b}
consisting of the functions of the form
f(z)=z+a_{2}z^{2}+...
,
|a_{2}|=b
for a fixed
b
with
0\leq b\leq 1
. For
n\in\mathbb{N}
and
0\leq b\leq 1
, let
\mathcal{A}_{nb}
be the class of analytic functions of the form
f(z)=z+nbz^{2}+...
for
z\in\Delta
such that
\mathcal{A}_{b}:=\mathcal{A}_{1b}
. The study of class
A_{b}
was initiated as early as 1920 by Gronwall
[
13
]
. He determined the growth and distortion estimates for the class of univalent functions with fixed second coefficients. In 2011, Ali
et al.

[
5
]
obtained various results for the class of functions with fixed second coefficients by applying the theory of second order differential subordination. Later, Lee
et al.

[
19
]
investigated certain applications of differential subordination for such functions. Kumar et al.
[
17
]
determined the best possible estimates on initial coefficients of Ma-Minda type univalent functions. Ali
et al.

[
4
]
obtained sharp radii of starlikeness for certain classes of functions with fixed second coefficients. A survey on functions with fixed initial coefficient can be found in
[
1
]
.

Let
f
and
h
be analytic functions in
\Delta
. Then
f
is subordinate to
h
, that is
f\prec h
if
f(z)=h(w(z))
for some analytic function
w
, with
w(0)=0
and
|w(z)|<1
. In particular, if
h\in\mathcal{S}
, then
f\prec h
provided
f(0)=h(0)
and
f(\Delta)\subset h(\Delta)
. Several well known subclasses of starlike functions were characterized by subordination of
zf^{\prime}/f
to some function in
\mathcal{P}
. Using a function
\psi
, univalent in unit disc
\Delta
with
\RE(\psi(z))>0
, starlike with respect to
\psi(0)=1
, symmetric about the real axis and
\psi^{\prime}(0)>0
, Ma and Minda
[
20
]
gave a unified treatment for several subclasses of starlike functions by studying the class
\mathcal{S}^{*}(\psi)=
{
f\in\mathcal{A}:zf^{\prime}(z)/f(z)\prec\psi(z)
}. Recently, Anand
et al.

[
7
]
also obtained some results for a class of analytic functions defined using the function
\psi
. Several authors considered the class
\mathcal{S}^{*}(\psi)
for various choices of the function
\psi.
For
-1<B<A\leq 1,
let
\psi(z)=(1+Az)/(1+Bz)
be a convex function whose image is symmetric with
respect to the real axis. For this function
\psi
, the class
\mathcal{S}^{*}(\psi)
reduces to the class
\mathcal{S}^{*}[A,B]

[
15
]
(see also
[
38
]
) of Janowski starlike functions. Other well-known choices for
\psi(z)
include
\sqrt{1+z}
,
\exp{z}
,
1+(4/3)z+(2/3)z^{2}
,
1+sin(z)
,
z+\sqrt{1+z^{2}}
,
1+z-z^{3}/3
,
2/(1+e^{-z})
. For a brief survey of these classes, reader may refer to
[
12
,
39
]
.

Consider the class
\mathcal{P}(\alpha)
of analytic functions
p(z)=1+b_{1}z+b_{2}z^{2}+...
satisfying
\RE\{p(z)\}>\alpha
where
0\leq\alpha<1
. It can be noted that the class
\mathcal{P}=\mathcal{P}(0)
. For
p\in\mathcal{P}(\alpha)

[
29
]
, we have
|b_{1}|\leq 2(1-\alpha).

Denote by
\mathcal{P}_{b}(\alpha)
the subclass of
\mathcal{P}(\alpha)
consisting of functions of the form
p(z)=1+2b(1-\alpha)z+...
for
b\in[0,1]
such that
\mathcal{P}_{b}=\mathcal{P}_{b}(0)
.
For any two subclasses
\mathcal{M}
and
\mathcal{N}
of
\mathcal{A}
, the
\mathcal{N}
radius for the class
\mathcal{M}
is the largest number
\rho\in(0,1)
such that
r^{-1}f(rz)\in\mathcal{N},
for all
f\in\mathcal{M}
and for
0<r<\rho.

Among several studies available on radius problems, many results involving ratio between two classes of functions, where one of them belong to some particular subclasses of
\mathcal{A}
has been a center of major focus in geometric function theory and can be seen in
[
31
,
32
,
22
,
23
,
24
]
.
Recently, Lecko and Ravichandran
[
18
]
estimated certain best possible radii for the classes of function
g\in\mathcal{A}
satisfying the conditions
(i)

g/h\in\mathcal{P}
where
h/(zp)\in\mathcal{P}
or
h/(zp)\in\mathcal{P}(1/2)

(ii)

g/(zp)\in\mathcal{P}
.
Motivated by above work, we define three classes of functions by making use of the classes
\mathcal{A}_{6b}
,
\mathcal{A}_{4c}
and
\mathcal{P}
as follows:

H^{1}_{b,c}=\{f\in\mathcal{A}_{6b}:\frac{f}{g}\in\mathcal{P}\ and\ \frac{g}{zp}\in\mathcal{P}\ where\ g\in\mathcal{A}_{4c},\ p\in\mathcal{P}\}

H^{2}_{b,c}=\{f\in\mathcal{A}_{5b}:\frac{f}{g}\in\mathcal{P}\ and\ \frac{g}{zp}\in\mathcal{P}(1/2)\ where\ g\in\mathcal{A}_{3c},\ p\in\mathcal{P}\}

and

H^{3}_{b}=\{f\in\mathcal{A}_{4b}:\frac{f}{zp}\in\mathcal{P}\ where\ p\in\mathcal{P}\}

where
b\in[0,1]
and
c\in[0,1]
.

The results presented in this paper are nice extensions of radii estimates results in
[
18
]
along with some improved radii. It includes radii estimates for functions in the classes
H^{1}_{b,c}
,
H^{2}_{b,c}
and
H^{3}_{b}
to belong to several subclasses of normalized analytic functions
\mathcal{A}
like starlike functions of order
\alpha
, starlike functions associated with lemniscate of Bernoulli, parabolic starlike functions, exponential function, cardioid, sine function, lune, a particular rational function, nephroid and modified sigmoid function.

2.
Analysis and mapping of
zf^{\prime}(z)/f(z)
for
H^{1}_{b,c}
,
H^{2}_{b,c}
and
H^{3}_{b}

In this section, we investigate extremal functions for all three classes
H^{1}_{b,c}
,
H^{2}_{b,c}
and
H^{3}_{b}
which demonstrates the fact that the classes are non empty. Furthermore, we obtain the disc centered at 1, containing the image of the disc
\Delta

under the mapping
zf^{\prime}/f
where
f
belongs to each of these classes.
We begin by stating the following lemmas by McCarty:

Lemma 2.1
.

[
27
]

Let
b\in[0,1]
and
0\leq\alpha<1
. If
p\in\mathcal{P}_{b}(\alpha)
, then for
|z|=r<1
,

\left|\frac{zp^{\prime}(z)}{p(z)}\right|\leq\frac{2(1-\alpha)r}{1-r^{2}}\frac{br^{2}+2r+b}{(1-2\alpha)r^{2}+2b(1-\alpha)r+1}.

Lemma 2.2
.

[
26
]

Let
b\in[0,1]
and
0\leq\alpha<1
. If
p\in\mathcal{P}_{b}(\alpha)
, then for
|z|=r<1
,

\RE\left(\frac{zp^{\prime}(z)}{p(z)}\right)\geq\begin{cases}\frac{-2(1-\alpha)r}{1+2\alpha br+(2\alpha-1)r^{2}}\frac{br^{2}+2r+b}{r^{2}+2br+1}\,\,\text{if}\,\,R_{\alpha}\leq R_{b}\\
\frac{2\sqrt{\alpha C_{1}}-C_{1}-\alpha}{1-\alpha}\quad\quad\quad\quad\,\,\,\,\text{if}\,\,R_{\alpha}\geq R_{b}\end{cases},

where
R_{b}=C_{b}-D_{b}
,
R_{\alpha}=\sqrt{\alpha C_{1}}
, and
r=|z|<1
;

(1)

C_{b}=\frac{(1+br)^{2}-(2\alpha-1)(b+r)^{2}r^{2}}{(1+2br+r^{2})(1-r^{2})}\quad\text{and}\quad D_{b}=\frac{2(1-\alpha)(b+r)(1+br)r}{(1+2br+r^{2})(1-r^{2})}

2.1.
The class
H^{1}_{b,c}

Let the functions
f
and
g
whose Taylor series expansions are given by
f(z)=z+f_{1}z^{2}+...
and
g(z)=z+g_{1}z^{2}+...
be such that
\RE\{f/g\}>0
and
\RE\{g/(zp)\}>0
where
p\in\mathcal{P}
is represented by
p(z)=1+qz+...
Now consider,
{g}/{(zp)}=1+(g_{1}-q)z+...
where
|g_{1}-q|\leq 2
and
|q|\leq 2
which gives
|g_{1}|\leq 4.
Also,
{f}/{g}=1+(f_{1}-g_{1})z+....

where
|f_{1}-g_{1}|\leq 2
and hence
|f_{1}|\leq 6
.
Hence, for
b,c\in[0,1]
, we consider the class involving the function
f
and
g
with fixed second coefficient whose Taylor series expansion is given by
f(z)=z+6bz^{2}+...
and
g(z)=z+4cz^{2}+...
such that
f\in\mathcal{A}_{6b}
and
g\in\mathcal{A}_{4c}
.
If the function
f\in

H^{1}_{b,c}
, then there exists an element
g\in\mathcal{A}_{4c}
and
p\in\mathcal{P}
such that
f/g\in\mathcal{P}
and
g/(zp)\in\mathcal{P}
. Define

h(z)=\frac{f(z)}{g(z)}=1+(6b-4c)z+...\quad\text{and}\quad k(z)=\frac{g(z)}{zp(z)}=1+(4c-q)z+...

where
|6b-4c|\leq 2
and
|4c-q|\leq 2
. Therefore, we observe that
h\in\mathcal{P}_{(6b-4c)/2}
,
k\in\mathcal{P}_{(4c-q)/2}
,
p\in\mathcal{P}_{q/2}
and
f
can be expressed as
f(z)=zp(z)h(z)k(z)
. Then a calculation shows that

(2)

\left|\frac{zf^{\prime}(z)}{f(z)}-1\right|\leq\left|\frac{zh^{\prime}(z)}{h(z)}\right|+\left|\frac{zk^{\prime}(z)}{k(z)}\right|+\left|\frac{zp^{\prime}(z)}{p(z)}\right|.

For
d=|6b-4c|\leq 2
,
s=|4c-q|\leq 2
and
\alpha=0
, using lemma
2.1
, we get

(3)

\left|\frac{zh^{\prime}(z)}{h(z)}\right|\leq\frac{r}{(1-r^{2})}\frac{(dr^{2}+4r+d)}{(r^{2}+dr+1)},\quad\left|\frac{zk^{\prime}(z)}{k(z)}\right|\leq\frac{r}{(1-r^{2})}\frac{(sr^{2}+4r+s)}{(r^{2}+sr+1)},

and

(4)

\left|\frac{zp^{\prime}(z)}{p(z)}\right|\leq\frac{r}{(1-r^{2})}\frac{(qr^{2}+4r+q)}{(r^{2}+qr+1)}.

By (
2
), (
3
) and (
4
), it follows that

(5)

\left|\frac{zf^{\prime}(z)}{f(z)}-1\right|\leq\frac{\begin{multlined}r[(dr^{2}+4r+d)(r^{2}+sr+1)(r^{2}+qr+1)+(sr^{2}+4r+s)\\
\\
(r^{2}+dr+1)(r^{2}+qr+1)+(qr^{2}+4r+q)(r^{2}+dr+1)(r^{2}+sr+1)]\end{multlined}}{(1-r^{2})(r^{2}+dr+1)(r^{2}+sr+1)(r^{2}+qr+1)}.

Define the functions
f_{1}
,
g_{1}
and
p_{1}:\Delta\to\mathbb{C}
by

(6)

f_{1}(z)=\frac{z(1-qz+z^{2})(1-(4c-q)z+z^{2})(1-(6b-4c)z+z^{2})}{(1-z^{2})^{3}},

(7)

g_{1}(z)=\frac{z(1-qz+z^{2})(1-(4c-q)z+z^{2})}{(1-z^{2})^{2}},\quad\text{and}\quad p_{1}(z)=\frac{(1-qz+z^{2})}{(1-z^{2})},

\text{where}\quad|6b-4c|\leq 2,\quad|4c-q|\leq 2\quad\text{and}\quad|q|\leq 2.

By (
6
) and (
7
), we have

\frac{f_{1}(z)}{g_{1}(z)}=\frac{(1-(6b-4c)z+z^{2})}{(1-z^{2})}=\frac{1+w_{1}(z)}{1-w_{1}(z)}

\frac{g_{1}(z)}{zp_{1}(z)}=\frac{(1-(4c-q)z+z^{2})}{(1-z^{2})}=\frac{1+w_{2}(z)}{1-w_{2}(z)}\,\,\text{and}\,\,p_{1}(z)=\frac{(1-qz+z^{2})}{(1-z^{2})}=\frac{1+w_{3}(z)}{1-w_{3}(z)}

where

w_{1}(z)=\frac{z(z-(6b-4c)/2)}{(1-z(6b-4c)/2)},\,w_{2}(z)=\frac{z(z-(4c-q)/2)}{(1-z(4c-q)/2)}\,\,\text{and}\,\,w_{3}(z)=\frac{z(z-q/2)}{(1-zq/2)}

which are analytic functions satisfying the conditions of Schwarz lemma in the unit disc
\Delta
and hence
\RE(f_{1}/g_{1})>0
,
\RE(g_{1}/(zp_{1}))>0
and
\RE(p_{1})>0
. Thus,
f_{1}/g_{1}\in\mathcal{P}_{(6b-4c)/2}
,
g_{1}/(zp_{1})\in\mathcal{P}_{(4c-q)/2}
and
p_{1}\in\mathcal{P}_{(q/2)}
. Thus the function
f_{1}\in H^{1}_{b,c}
and the class
H^{1}_{b,c}
is non-empty.

(8)

F_{1}(z)=\frac{z(1-z^{2})^{3}}{(1-qz+z^{2})(1-(4c-q)z+z^{2})(1-(6b-4c)z+z^{2})}

and
f_{1}(z)
are extreme functions for the class
H^{1}_{b,c}
provided
q\leq 2,\ c\geq q/4
and
b\geq 2c/3.

2.2.
The class
H^{2}_{b,c}

Let
f
and
g
be functions with Taylor series expansions
f(z)=z+f_{1}z^{2}+...

\RE\{f/g\}>0
and
\RE\{g/(zp)\}>1/2
where

is represented by
p(z)=1+qz+...

Now consider,
g/zp=1+(g_{1}-q)z+...

where
|g_{1}-q|\leq 1
(lemma 2, pg 33 et al
[
14
]
) and
|q|\leq 2
which gives
|g_{1}|\leq 3.
Also,
f/g=1+(f_{1}-g_{1})z+....

where
|f_{1}-g_{1}|\leq 2
and hence
|f_{1}|\leq 5
.
Therefore, we consider the class involving the function
f
and
g
with fixed second coefficient whose Taylor series expansion is given by
f(z)=z+5bz^{2}+...
and
g(z)=z+3cz^{2}+...

where
b\in[0,1]
and
c\in[0,1]
such that
f\in\mathcal{A}_{5b}
and
g\in\mathcal{A}_{3c}
. If the function
f\in

H^{2}_{b,c}
then there exists an element
g\in\mathcal{A}_{3c}
and
p\in\mathcal{P}
such that
f/g\in\mathcal{P}
and

Define

and

k(z)=\frac{g(z)}{zp(z)}=1+(3c-q)z+...

It is easy to see that
h\in\mathcal{P}_{(5b-3c)/2}
,
k\in\mathcal{P}_{(3c-q)}
,
p\in P_{q/2}
and
f(z)=zp(z)h(z)k(z)
. Then a calculation shows that

(9)

\frac{zf^{\prime}(z)}{f(z)}=1+\frac{zh^{\prime}(z)}{h(z)}+\frac{zk^{\prime}(z)}{k(z)}+\frac{zp^{\prime}(z)}{p(z)}

so that

(10)

\left|\frac{zf^{\prime}(z)}{f(z)}-1\right|\leq\left|\frac{zh^{\prime}(z)}{h(z)}\right|+\left|\frac{zk^{\prime}(z)}{k(z)}\right|+\left|\frac{zp^{\prime}(z)}{p(z)}\right|.

Let
m=|5b-3c|\leq 2
,
n=|3c-q|\leq 1
and
\alpha=0
. Using Lemma
2.1
for the functions
h,p
and
k
, we have

(11)

\left|\frac{zh^{\prime}(z)}{h(z)}\right|\leq\frac{r}{(1-r^{2})}\frac{(mr^{2}+4r+m)}{(r^{2}+mr+1)},\,\,\left|\frac{zk^{\prime}(z)}{k(z)}\right|\leq\frac{r}{(1-r^{2})}\frac{(nr^{2}+2r+n)}{(nr+1)},

and

(12)

\left|\frac{zp^{\prime}(z)}{p(z)}\right|\leq\frac{r}{(1-r^{2})}\frac{(qr^{2}+4r+q)}{(r^{2}+qr+1)}.

Inequality (
10
) together with (
11
) and (
12
), gives

(13)

\left|\frac{zf^{\prime}(z)}{f(z)}-1\right|\leq\frac{r}{(1-r^{2})}\left(\frac{mr^{2}+4r+m}{r^{2}+mr+1}+\frac{nr^{2}+2r+n}{nr+1}+\frac{qr^{2}+4r+q}{r^{2}+qr+1}\right).

Define the functions
f_{2}
,
g_{2}

:\Delta\to\mathbb{C}
by

(14)

f_{2}(z)=\frac{z(1-qz+z^{2})(1-(3c-q)z)(1-(5b-3c)z+z^{2})}{(1-z^{2})^{3}}

and

(15)

g_{2}(z)=\frac{z(1-qz+z^{2})(1-(3c-q)z)}{(1-z^{2})^{2}},\text{where}\,\,|5b-3c|\leq 2\,\,\text{and}\,\,|3c-q|\leq 1.

It follows from (
7
), (
14
) and (
15
) that

\frac{f_{2}(z)}{g_{2}(z)}=\frac{(1-(5b-3c)z+z^{2})}{(1-z^{2})}=\frac{1+w_{4}(z)}{1-w_{4}(z)}

and

\frac{g_{2}(z)}{zp_{1}(z)}=\frac{(1-(3c-q)z)}{(1-z^{2})}=\frac{1+w_{5}(z)}{1-w_{5}(z)}

where

w_{4}(z)=\frac{z(z-(5b-3c)/2)}{(1-z(5b-3c)/2)}\,\,\text{and}\,\,w_{5}(z)=\frac{z(z-(3c-q))}{(2-(3c-q)z-z^{2})}

which are Schwarz functions in the unit disc
\Delta
and hence
\RE(f_{2}/g_{2})>0
,
\RE(g_{2}/(zp_{1}))>1/2
and
\RE(p_{1})>0
(as shown for class

). Thus,
f_{2}/g_{2}\in\mathcal{P}_{(5b-3c)/2}
,
g_{2}/(zp_{1})\in\mathcal{P}_{(3c-q)}
and
p_{1}\in\mathcal{P}_{(q/2)}
. Thus the function
f_{2}\in H^{2}_{b,c}
and the class
H^{2}_{b,c}
is non-empty.

f_{2}(z)
is an extreme functions for the class
H^{2}_{b,c}
provided
q\leq 2
,
c\geq q/3
and
b\geq 3c/5.

The class

f
and

f(z)=z+f_{1}z^{2}+..

be such that

where

3.
Radius of Starlikeness

In the present section, we determine the radii of the classes
H^{1}_{b,c}
,
H^{2}_{b,c}
