Residue of some Eisenstein series
Shoyu Nagaoka

Abstract
The real analytic Eisenstein series is a special function that has been studied classically. Its generalization to the case of many variables has been studied extensively. Moreover, the analytic properties of certain Eisenstein series on the Siegel modular groups have also been investigated. The purpose of this study is to provide concrete forms of the residue of E 0 ( m ) ​ ( z , s ) E_{0}^{(m)}(z,s) at s = m / 2 s=m/2 .

4.5
Conclusion

We summarize our results in the previous sections.

The following is a main result of this study.

Theorem 4.11
.

\displaystyle\underset{s=m/2}{{\rm Res}}E_{0}^{(m)}(z,s)

\displaystyle=\mathbb{A}^{(m)}(y)+\mathbb{B}^{(m)}(y)\sum_{h\in\Lambda_{m}^{(1)}}\sigma_{0}({\rm cont}(h))\eta_{m}(2y,\pi h;m/2,m/2)\boldsymbol{e}(\sigma(hx)),

where

\displaystyle\mathbb{A}^{(m)}(y)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2}\,\alpha_{m}(y,m/2)\cdot C_{m-1}^{(m)}(y)+\frac{1}{8}\,v(m-1)\text{det}(2y)^{-(m-1)/2}\alpha^{\prime}_{m}(y,m/2)

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{8}\,\beta^{\prime}_{m}(y,m/2),

\displaystyle\mathbb{B}^{(m)}(y)

\displaystyle=2^{m-2}\pi^{m\kappa(m)}\text{det}(y)^{m/2}\Gamma_{m}(m/2)^{-1}\zeta(m)^{-1}

\displaystyle\cdot\prod_{j=1}^{m-2}\zeta(m-j)\,\prod_{j=1}^{m-1}\zeta(2m-2j)^{-1}.

5.1
Low degree cases

In this section, we provide more explicit formulas for
\text{Res}_{s=m/2}E_{0}^{(m)}(z,s)
.

m=2,\,3
. We used the notations in the previous sections as they are.

5.1.1
Case
\boldsymbol{m=2}

In this case, the constant
C_{1}^{(2)}(y)
appearing in the term
\mathbb{A}^{(2)}(y)

can be expressed more explicitly because we can apply the Kronecker limit formula.
For
g\in P_{2}
, we consider the Epstein zeta function

\zeta_{g}(s):=\sum_{\boldsymbol{0}\neq a\in\mathbb{Z}^{(2,1)}/\{\pm 1\}}g[a]^{-s},\qquad\quad\text{Re}(s)>1.

The first Kronecker limit formula asserts that
\zeta_{g}(s)
has the following expression:

\displaystyle\zeta_{g}(s)=\frac{1}{2}(4\text{det}(g))^{-s/2}\left[\frac{2\pi}{s-1}+4\pi\beta(g)+O(s-1)\right]

(5.1)

\displaystyle\beta(g)=\gamma+\frac{1}{2}\log\frac{v^{\prime}}{2\sqrt{\text{det}(g)}}-\log|\eta(W_{g})|^{2}.

(5.2)

Here, for
g=\begin{pmatrix}v^{\prime}&w\\
w&v\end{pmatrix}\in P_{2}
,

W_{g}:=\frac{w+i\sqrt{\text{det}(g)}}{v^{\prime}}\in H_{1},

and
\eta(z)
is the Dedekind eta function:

\eta(z)=\boldsymbol{e}(z/24)\prod_{n=1}^{\infty}(1-\boldsymbol{e}^{n}(z)),\qquad z\in H_{1}.

The relationship between the complete zeta function
\xi_{1}^{(2)}(g,s)
and
\zeta_{g}(s)

is expressed as follows:

\xi_{1}^{(2)}(g,s)=\xi(2s)\zeta_{1}^{(2)}(g,s)=\pi^{-s}\Gamma(s)\zeta_{g}(s).

Therefore, the constant
C_{1}^{(2)}(y)
, which is the constant term of
\xi_{1}^{(2)}(2y,s)
,
can be expressed as

C_{1}^{(2)}(y)=\frac{1}{2}(4\text{det}(y))^{-1/2}\left(\gamma+\log\frac{v^{\prime}}{8\pi}-\log(\text{det}(y))-2\log|\eta(W_{g})|^{2}\right).

Concerning
\alpha_{m}(y,m/2)
,
\alpha^{\prime}_{m}(y,m/2)
,
\cdots
appearing in
\mathbb{A}^{(m)}(y)
,
we can calculate them explicitly as

\displaystyle\alpha_{2}(y,1)

\displaystyle=72\pi^{-2}\text{det}(y)\,\zeta(2)^{-2},

\displaystyle\alpha^{\prime}_{2}(y,1)

\displaystyle=2\pi^{2}\text{det}(y)\,\zeta(2)^{-2}(2\log\pi+\log(\text{det}(y))+2\gamma-6\zeta^{\prime}(2)\,\zeta(2)^{-1}),

\displaystyle\beta_{2}(y,1)

\displaystyle=-\pi^{2}\text{det}(y)^{1/2}\,\zeta(2)^{-2},

\displaystyle\beta^{\prime}_{2}(y,1)

\displaystyle=2\pi^{2}\text{det}^{1/2}\,\zeta(2)^{-2}\left(2\log 2+\tfrac{1}{2}\log(\text{det}(y))-\gamma+2\zeta^{\prime}(0)+3\zeta^{\prime}(2)\zeta(2)^{-1}\right).

From these formula,

\displaystyle\mathbb{A}^{(2)}(y)=\underset{s=1}{{\rm Res}}(F_{0,1,0}^{(2)}(z,s)+F_{0,2,0}^{(2)}(z,s))

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2}\,\alpha_{2}(y,1)\cdot C_{1}^{(2)}(y)+\frac{1}{8}\,v(1)\cdot\text{det}(y)^{-1/2}\alpha^{\prime}_{2}(y,1)+\frac{1}{8}\,\beta^{\prime}_{2}(y,1)

\displaystyle=\frac{18}{\pi^{2}\sqrt{\text{det}(y)}}\left(\frac{1}{2}\gamma+\frac{1}{2}\log\frac{v^{\prime}}{4\pi}-\log|\eta(W_{g})|^{2}\right).

Combining with
\text{Res}_{s=1}F_{0,2,1}^{(3)}(z,s)
, we obtain

Proposition 5.1
.

\displaystyle\underset{s=1}{{\rm Res}}\,E_{0}^{(2)}(z,s)

\displaystyle=\frac{18}{\pi^{2}\sqrt{\text{det}(y)}}\left(\frac{1}{2}\gamma+\frac{1}{2}\log\frac{v^{\prime}}{4\pi}-\log|\eta(W_{g})|^{2}\right)

\displaystyle\quad+\frac{36\text{det}(y)}{\pi^{2}}\sum_{h\in\Lambda_{2}^{(1)}}\sigma_{0}(\text{cont}(h))\eta_{2}(2y,\pi h;1,1)\boldsymbol{e}(\sigma(hx)).

(5.3)
