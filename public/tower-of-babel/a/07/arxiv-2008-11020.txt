Frierson’s 1907 Parameterization of Compound Magic Squares Extended to Orders 3 l 3^{l} , l = 1 , 2 , 3 , . . l=1,2,3,.. , with Information Entropy
P.D.Loly, I.D.Cameron

Abstract
Frierson used a powerful parameterization of the pattern of the order 3 3 associative magic square to construct a family of six related order 3 2 = 9 3^{2}=9 compound (or composite) magic squares, several of them ancient. Stimulated by Bellew’s 1997 extension to order 27 = 3 3 27=3^{3} , we extend these ideas to all orders 3 l 3^{l} , l = 1 , 2 , 3 , . . l=1,2,3,.. , and in addition find simple formulae for the matrix spectra and entropic measures for all those orders. This construction is fractal and we give numerical results to order 243 = 3 6 243=3^{6} which show an information entropy measure converging to a constant value of about 1.168 . 1.168. . for the lowest entropy members. We also briefly consider compounding of an order 4 4 magic square with the lowest entropy, for which we find a similar trend to constant entropy.

With the SVs,

These powerful measures for assessing different magical squares, notably the

(22)

See the Appendix for a sample numerical calculation for
