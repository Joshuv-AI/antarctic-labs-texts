Two Problems on Narayana Numbers And Repeated Digit Numbers


Abstract
This work aims to solve two problems in Diophantine equation of the Narayana sequence ( O ​ E ​ I ​ S ​ A ​ 000930 ) (OEIS{\color[rgb]{1,0,0}A000930}) . In the first part it’s proved that the Narayana number can not be factored as a product of two repdigit numbers for base 2 ≤ b ≤ 50 2\leq b\leq 50 , except in two cases. In The second it has been proved that there is a finite number of solutions up to 290 to express the product of two Narayana numbers as base b − b- repdigits numbers, 2 ≤ b ≤ 50 2\leq b\leq 50 , the proofs of these results use some number-theoretic technique includes Baker’s method of linear forms in logarithms height, and some reduction technique.
