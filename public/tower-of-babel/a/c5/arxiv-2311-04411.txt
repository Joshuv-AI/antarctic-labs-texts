Validation of tight-binding model in system of two semiconductor single-electron lines coupled electrostatically by Schrödinger formalism *
Krzysztof Pomorski 1,2

Abstract
Validation of tight-binding model is given basing on two single-electron lines coupled perturbatively electrostatically in Schrödinger formalism. Scheme for conversion of quantum information from eigenenergy qubits to position based qubits is given. The procedure for determination of system ground is presented. Additional extension schemes of two coupled single-electron lines in case of Rabi oscillations occurring in each position based qubits are given.
