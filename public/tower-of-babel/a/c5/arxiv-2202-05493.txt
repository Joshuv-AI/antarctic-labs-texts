On Grothendieck–Serre conjecture in mixed characteristic for S ​ L 1 , D SL_{1,D}
Ivan Panin

Abstract
Let R be an unramified regular local ring of mixed characteristic, D an Azumaya R-algebra, K the fraction field of R, N ​ r ​ d : D × → R × Nrd:D^{\times}\to R^{\times} the reduced norm homomorphism. Let a ∈ R × a\in R^{\times} be a unit. Suppose the equation N ​ r ​ d = a Nrd=a has a solution over K, then it has a solution over R. Particularly, we prove the following. Let R be as above and a , b , c a,b,c be units in R R . Consider the equation T 1 2 − a ​ T 2 2 − b ​ T 3 2 + a ​ b ​ T 4 2 = c T^{2}_{1}-aT^{2}_{2}-bT^{2}_{3}+abT^{2}_{4}=c . If it has a solution over K K , then it has a solution over R R . Similar results are proved for regular local rings, which are geometrically regular over a discrete valuation ring. These results extend result proven in [ PS ] to the mixed characteristic case.
