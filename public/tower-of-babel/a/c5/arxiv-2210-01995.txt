Introduction

The weak decays of charmed baryons provide an excellent platform for understanding Quantum Chromodynamics with transitions involving the charm quark.
The decay amplitudes consist of factorizable and non-factorizable contributions.
The latter may play a non-trivial or essential role and are approached in various ways, including
the pole model

;

To date, there is no established phenomenological model that consistently describes baryon decays.
Precise measurements of branching fractions of charmed baryon weak decays are useful for studying the dynamics of charmed baryons and testing the predictions of theoretical models.
In addition, the singly Cabibbo-suppressed (SCS) charm decays are essential probes

Detector and data set

This analysis uses the full dataset recorded by the Belle detector
bel

VII
Summary

In summary, based on the entire dataset with integrated luminosity
980~\mbox{\,fb}^{-1}
collected by the Belle detector at the KEKB energy-asymmetric
e^{+}e^{-}
collider,
we present the first observation of the SCS decay
{\mathchar 28931\relax_{c}^{+}\!\rightarrow\!{}pK_{S}^{0}K_{S}^{0}}
with a statistical significance of
{>\!10\sigma}

and measure the branching fractions of
