Lemma 3.15
.

Let
n=2
,
1<s<2
,
0<b<n-s
and
0<\sigma<\infty
. Assume that
f
is of class
C\left(\sigma,s,b\right)
.

(1)
If
f\left(z\right)
is a polynomial in
z
and
\bar{z}
, or if not we assume further that
\sigma\geq 2
, then we have

\left\||x|^{-b}f(u)-|x|^{-b}f(v)\right\|_{S^{\prime}\left(I,\;\dot{H}^{s}\right)}\lesssim\left(T^{\theta_{1}}+T^{\theta_{2}}\right)\left(\left\|u\right\|_{S\left(I,\;H^{s}\right)}^{\sigma}+\left\|v\right\|_{S\left(I,\;H^{s}\right)}^{\sigma}\right)\left\|u-v\right\|_{S\left(I,\;H^{s}\right)},

where

and

(2)
If
f
is not a polynomial and

\displaystyle\begin{split}&\left\||x|^{-b}f(u)-|x|^{-b}f(v)\right\|_{S^{\prime}\left(I,\;\dot{H}^{s}\right)}\lesssim\left(T^{\theta_{1}}+T^{\theta_{2}}\right)\left(\left\|u\right\|_{S\left(I,\;H^{s}\right)}^{\sigma}+\left\|v\right\|_{S\left(I,\;H^{s}\right)}^{\sigma}\right)\left\|u-v\right\|_{S\left(I,\;H^{s}\right)}\\

where

Proof.

We only consider the case (2). The case (1) is treated similarly.
As in the above lemmas, we divide the estimate in
B
and
B^{C}
, indeed,

\left\||x|^{-b}f(u)-|x|^{-b}f(v)\right\|_{S^{\prime}\left(I,\;\dot{H}^{s}\right)}\leq C_{1}+C_{2},

where
C_{1}
and
C_{2}
are given in (3.71) and (3.72) respectively.
First, we estimate
C_{1}
. It is obvious that
\chi_{B^{C}}|x|^{-b}\in L^{\infty}
. We can also see that
\chi_{B^{C}}|x|^{-b}\in\dot{H}^{s}
, since
\frac{n}{2}<s<b+s
.
It follows from Lemma 2.1, Lemma 3.9 and Lemma 3.11 that

\displaystyle\begin{split}&\left\|\chi_{B^{C}}|x|^{-b}(f(u)-f(v))\right\|_{\dot{H}^{s}}\\
&~~~~~\lesssim\left\|\chi_{B^{C}}|x|^{-b}\right\|_{L^{\infty}}\left\|f(u)-f(v)\right\|_{\dot{H}^{s}}+\left\|\chi_{B^{C}}|x|^{-b}\right\|_{\dot{H}^{s}}\left\|f(u)-f(v)\right\|_{\infty}\\
&~~~~~\lesssim\left\|u-v\right\|_{\infty}^{\sigma-1}\left(\left\|u\right\|_{H^{s}}^{2}+\left\|v\right\|_{H^{s}}^{2}\right)+\left(\left\|u\right\|_{H^{s}}^{\sigma}+\left\|v\right\|_{H^{s}}^{\sigma}\right)\left\|u-v\right\|_{H^{s}}.\end{split}

(3.113)

Using (3.113) and Hölder inequality, we have

\displaystyle\begin{split}C_{1}&\leq\left\|\chi_{B^{C}}|x|^{-b}(f(u)-f(v))\right\|_{L^{\gamma\left(2\right)^{{{}^{\prime}}}}\left(I,\;\dot{H}^{s}\right)}\\
&\lesssim T\left(\left\|u\right\|_{L^{\infty}\left(I,\;H^{s}\right)}^{\sigma}+\left\|v\right\|_{L^{\infty}\left(I,\;H^{s}\right)}^{\sigma}\right)\left\|u-v\right\|_{L^{\infty}\left(I,\;H^{s}\right)}\\
&~~~+\left(\left\|u\right\|_{L^{\infty}\left(I,\;H^{s}\right)}^{2}+\left\|v\right\|_{L^{\infty}\left(I,\;H^{s}\right)}^{2}\right)\left\|u-v\right\|_{L^{\beta_{1}}\left(I,\;L^{\infty}\right)}^{\sigma-1},\end{split}

(3.114)

where
\beta_{1}=\sigma-1
.
Next, we estimate
C_{2}
.
Since
b+s<n
, we can take
\hat{r}>2
large enough such that

\frac{b+s}{n}+\frac{1}{\hat{r}}<1.

Putting
\frac{1}{p}=\frac{1}{\hat{r}^{\prime}}-\frac{1}{2}

\frac{1}{p}=\frac{1}{\hat{r}^{\prime}}-\frac{1}{2}>\frac{b+s}{n}-\frac{1}{2}>\frac{b}{n},

\chi_{B}|x|^{-b}\in L^{p}

\frac{1}{\hat{r}^{\prime}}-\frac{b+s}{n}>0

\chi_{B}|x|^{-b}\in\dot{H}_{\hat{r}^{\prime}}^{s}

It follows from Lemma 2.1, Lemma 3.9 and Lemma 3.11 that

\displaystyle\begin{split}&\left\|\chi_{B}|x|^{-b}(f(u)-f(v))\right\|_{\dot{H}_{\hat{r}^{\prime}}^{s}}\\
&~~~~~\lesssim\left\|\chi_{B}|x|^{-b}\right\|_{L^{p}}\left\|f(u)-f(v)\right\|_{\dot{H}^{s}}+\left\|\chi_{B}|x|^{-b}\right\|_{{\dot{H}_{\hat{r}^{\prime}}^{s}}}\left\|f(u)-f(v)\right\|_{\infty}\\
&~~~~~\lesssim\left\|u-v\right\|_{\infty}^{\sigma-1}\left(\left\|u\right\|_{H^{s}}^{2}+\left\|v\right\|_{H^{s}}^{2}\right)+\left(\left\|u\right\|_{H^{s}}^{\sigma}+\left\|v\right\|_{H^{s}}^{\sigma}\right)\left\|u-v\right\|_{H^{s}}.\end{split}

(3.115)

Using (3.115) and Hölder inequality, we have

\displaystyle\begin{split}C_{2}&\leq\left\||x|^{-b}f(u)-|x|^{-b}f(v)\right\|_{L^{\gamma\left(\hat{r}\right)^{{{}^{\prime}}}}\left(I,\;{\dot{H}_{\hat{r}^{\prime}}^{s}\left(B\right)}\right)}\\
