Abstract
In this report we present preliminary results about the tunneling problem for a magnetic Schrödinger operator. As a motivation we consider the 3-D time-dependent Schrödinger operator H ⁡ ( t ) = − h 2 ​ Δ + V + E ⁡ ( t ) ⋅ x H(t)=-h^{2}\Delta+V+E(t)\cdot x where V V is a radial potential and E ⁡ ( t ) E(t) a circularly polarized field with uniform frequency ω \omega . The quantum monodromy operator (QMO) that takes the system through a complete period T = 2 ​ π / ω T=2\pi/\omega , turns out to be unitarily equivalent to e i ​ T ​ P A ​ ( x , h ​ D x ) / h e^{iTP_{A}(x,hD_{x})/h} , where OPEN P A ​ ( x , h ​ D x ) ) P_{A}(x,hD_{x})) identifies with a magnetic Schrödinger operator. When V V is sufficiently confining, OPEN P A ​ ( x , h ​ D x ) ) P_{A}(x,hD_{x})) presents a double magnetic well. Then we construct its semi-classical ground state and examine the splitting between its two first eigenvalues.
