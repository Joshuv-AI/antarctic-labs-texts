Permutation polynomials and complete permutation polynomials over 𝔽 q 3 \mathbb{F}_{q^{3}}
Yanping Wang, Weiguo Zhang, Daniele Bartoli, Qiang Wang

Abstract
Motivated by many recent constructions of permutation polynomials over 𝔽 q 2 \mathbb{F}_{q^{2}} , we study permutation polynomials over 𝔽 q 3 \mathbb{F}_{q^{3}} in terms of their coefficients. Based on the multivariate method and resultant elimination, we construct several new classes of sparse permutation polynomials over 𝔽 q 3 \mathbb{F}_{q^{3}} , q = p k q=p^{k} , p ≥ 3 p\geq 3 . Some of them are complete mappings.



Key Words
Finite field, Permutation polynomial, Complete permutation polynomial, Multivariate method, Resultant.

Preliminaries

In some of our proofs we will need to investigate the solutions of a system of polynomial equations. In these situation an important tool we use is the resultant of two polynomials.
We recall here some basic facts about the resultant.

Definition 2.1
.

(
[
22
]
, p.36) Let

be
two polynomials of formal degree

respectively

with

. Then the resultant
R(f,g)
of the two polynomials is defined by the determinant

\displaystyle R(f,g)=\left|\begin{array}[]{cccccccc}a_{0}&a_{1}&\cdots&a_{n}&0&&\cdots&0\\
0&a_{0}&a_{1}&\cdots&a_{n}&0&\cdots&0\\
\vdots&&&&&&&\vdots\\
0&\cdots&0&a_{0}&a_{1}&&\cdots&a_{n}\\
b_{0}&b_{1}&\cdots&&b_{m}&0&\cdots&0\\
0&b_{0}&b_{1}&\cdots&&b_{m}&\cdots&0\\
\vdots&&&&&&&\vdots\\
0&\cdots&0&b_{0}&b_{1}&&\cdots&b_{m}\\
\end{array}\right|

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{l}\left.\rule{0.0pt}{29.87538pt}\right\}$m$~rows\\
\\
\left.\rule{0.0pt}{29.87538pt}\right\}$n$~rows\end{array}

of order
m+n
.

If

(i.e., if

) and

in
the splitting field of

\mathbb{F}_{q}
, then

is also given by the formula

\displaystyle R(f,g)=a_{0}^{m}\prod_{i=1}^{n}g(\alpha_{i}).

In this case, we obviously have

if and only if

and

have a common root, which is the same as saying that

and

have a common

\mathbb{F}_{q}[x]
of positive degree.

For two polynomials
