An isoperimetric inequality for Hamming balls and local expansion in hypercubes
Zilin Jiang, Amir Yehudayoff

Abstract
We prove a vertex isoperimetric inequality for the n n -dimensional Hamming ball ℬ n ​ ( R ) \mathcal{B}_{n}(R) of radius R R . The isoperimetric inequality is sharp up to a constant factor for sets that are comparable to ℬ n ​ ( R ) \mathcal{B}_{n}(R) in size. A key step in the proof is a local expansion phenomenon in hypercubes.

Introduction

Isoperimetric inequalities allow to control the boundary size or surface area of bodies in terms of their volume. The classical isoperimetric inequality states that in Euclidean spaces, balls have the smallest surface area per given volume. Such inequalities are fundamental in geometry, and are deeply related to many areas of mathematics and physics.

Proposition 3

Suppose

Lemma 6

For every

Lemma 7

For every

R\leq n

, which implies that
L_{1}(x)>Q
for
x\in[0,x_{1}^{+}]
by the concavity of
L_{1}(x)
. Since
x_{2}^{-}<x_{1}^{+}
from
2
, we get the desired inequality
\max(L_{1}(x),L_{2}(x))\geq Q
for all
x\in[0,x^{*}]
for the inductive step.
∎

4
Sharpness

A random variable
H
is said to have the
hypergeometric distribution
with parameters
r,m,n
, written as
H\sim\mathfrak{H}(r;m,n)
, if its probability mass function is given by

\Pr(H=k)=\begin{cases}\binom{m}{k}\binom{n-m}{r-k}/\binom{n}{r}&\text{if }k=0,1,\dots,r,\\
0&\text{otherwise}.\end{cases}

We need the following simple fact about hypergeometric distribution. We shall use the inequality
\binom{2m}{m}\geq 2^{2m}/(2\sqrt{m})
(see, for example,
[
MN09
, Proposition 3.6.2]
for a proof).

Proposition 9
.

If
H\sim\mathfrak{H}(r;\lfloor n/2\rfloor,n)
, then for all
k\in\mathbb{N}
,

\Pr(H=k)\leq O\left(\sqrt{\frac{n}{r(n-r)}}\right).

Proof.

Put
m:=\lfloor n/2\rfloor
. Using
\binom{n}{r}\leq\binom{n}{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}=\Theta(2^{n}/\sqrt{n})
, we compute

\Pr(H=k)=\binom{m}{k}\binom{n-m}{r-k}/\binom{n}{r}=\binom{r}{k}\binom{n-r}{m-k}/\binom{n}{m}\leq O\left(\frac{2^{r}}{\sqrt{r}}\frac{2^{n-r}}{\sqrt{n-r}}\frac{\sqrt{n}}{2^{n}}\right).\qed

Now we are ready to prove
Propositions

2
and
5
.

Appendix A
Proof of
Lemma

Proof of
Lemma

6
.

Observe that for every
k\leq r
, we have

\frac{\binom{n}{k}}{\binom{n}{r}}=\frac{k+1}{r+1}\cdot\frac{n-r}{n-k}\cdot\frac{\binom{n}{k+1}}{\binom{n}{r+1}}\leq\frac{\binom{n}{k+1}}{\binom{n}{r+1}},

which implies that

\frac{\left\lvert\mathcal{B}_{n}(r)\right\rvert}{\left\lvert\mathcal{S}_{n}(r)\right\rvert}=\sum_{k=0}^{r}\frac{\binom{n}{k}}{\binom{n}{r}}\leq\sum_{k=0}^{r}\frac{\binom{n}{k+1}}{\binom{n}{r+1}}\leq\sum_{k=0}^{r+1}\frac{\binom{n}{k}}{\binom{n}{r+1}}=\frac{\left\lvert\mathcal{B}_{n}(r+1)\right\rvert}{\left\lvert\mathcal{S}_{n}(r+1)\right\rvert}.

In particular, when
r\leq n/2
,

\frac{\left\lvert\mathcal{S}_{n}(r)\right\rvert}{\left\lvert\mathcal{B}_{n}(r)\right\rvert}\geq\frac{\left\lvert\mathcal{S}_{n}(\lfloor n/2\rfloor)\right\rvert}{\left\lvert\mathcal{B}_{n}(\lfloor n/2\rfloor)\right\rvert}.

Case:
n
is even.

We know that

\left\lvert\mathcal{S}_{n}(\lfloor n/2\rfloor)\right\rvert=\binom{n}{n/2}\quad\text{and}\quad\left\lvert\mathcal{B}_{n}(\lfloor n/2\rfloor)\right\rvert=2^{n-1}+\frac{1}{2}\binom{n}{n/2}.

Since
\binom{n}{n/2}\geq 2^{n}/(\sqrt{2n})
and
n\geq 3
, we get

\frac{\left\lvert\mathcal{S}_{n}(\lfloor n/2\rfloor)\right\rvert}{\left\lvert\mathcal{B}_{n}(\lfloor n/2\rfloor)\right\rvert}=\frac{1}{2^{n-1}/\binom{n}{n/2}+1/2}\geq\frac{1}{\sqrt{2n}/2+1/2}\geq\frac{1}{\sqrt{n}}.

Case:
n
is odd.

We know that

\left\lvert\mathcal{S}_{n}(\lfloor n/2\rfloor)\right\rvert=\binom{n}{(n-1)/2}\quad\text{and}\quad\left\lvert\mathcal{B}_{n}(\lfloor n/2\rfloor)\right\rvert=2^{n-1}.

Since
\binom{n}{(n-1)/2}=\frac{1}{2}\binom{n+1}{(n+1)/2}\geq 2^{n}/\sqrt{2(n+1)}
and
n\geq 3
, we get

\frac{\left\lvert\mathcal{S}_{n}(\lfloor n/2\rfloor)\right\rvert}{\left\lvert\mathcal{B}_{n}(\lfloor n/2\rfloor)\right\rvert}=\frac{\binom{n}{(n-1)/2}}{2^{n-1}}\geq\frac{2}{\sqrt{2(n+1)}}\geq\frac{1}{\sqrt{n}}.\qed
