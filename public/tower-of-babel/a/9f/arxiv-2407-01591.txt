The α \alpha -induction of Graded Local Conformal Nets
ZIYUN XU

Abstract
The α \alpha -induction of graded local conformal nets is studied. We show that inclusions of graded local conformal nets give rise to braided subfactors so that the α \alpha -induction is still effective for graded local conformal nets. As an application, we give a shorter proof of classification of N = 2 N=2 superconformal nets in the discrete series.

2.3
Graded Local Conformal Nets

The concept of graded local conformal nets is a generalization of the notion of local conformal nets and has been explicitly introduced and studied in
[
9
]
.

Let
S^{1}=\{z\in\mathbb{C}:|z|=1\}
be the unit circle. Let

\operatorname{Diff}(S^{1})
be the infinite dimensional real Lie group of orientation-preserving smooth diffeomorphisms of
S^{1}
and denote by
\operatorname{Diff}(S^{1})^{(n)},n\in\mathbb{N}\cup\{\infty\}
the corresponding
n
-cover. In particular,
\operatorname{Diff}(S^{1})^{(\infty)}
is the universal covering group of
\operatorname{Diff}(S^{1})
. By identifying
\operatorname{PSL}(2,\mathbb{R})
with the group of Möbius transformations on
S^{1}
we can consider it as a three dimensional Lie subgroup of
\operatorname{Diff}(S^{1})
. We denote by
\operatorname{PSL}(2,\mathbb{R})^{(n)}\subset\operatorname{Diff}(S^{1})^{(n)},n\in\mathbb{N}\cup\{\infty\}
the corresponding
n
-cover so that

\operatorname{PSL}(2,\mathbb{R})^{(\infty)}
is the universal covering group of
\operatorname{PSL}(2,\mathbb{R})
. We denote by
\dot{g}\in\operatorname{Diff}(S^{1})
the image of
g\in\operatorname{Diff}(S^{1})^{(\infty)}
under the covering map.

Let
\mathcal{I}
denote the set of all connected non-dense open intervals in
S^{1}
. For
I\in\mathcal{I}
,
I^{\prime}
denotes the interior of
S^{1}\backslash I
. Given
I\in\mathcal{I}
, the subgroup
\operatorname{Diff}(S^{1})_{I}
of diffeomorphisms localized in
I
is defined as the stabilizer of
I
in
\operatorname{Diff}(S^{1})
, namely the subgroup of
\operatorname{Diff}(S^{1})

whose elements are the diffeomorphisms acting trivially on
I^{\prime}
.

Definition 1
.

A graded local conformal net
\mathcal{A}
on
S^{1}
is a map
I\mapsto\mathcal{A}(I)
from the set of intervals(connected, non dense)
\mathcal{I}
to the set of von Neumann algebras acting on a common infinite dimensional separable Hilbert space
\mathcal{H}
which satisfies the following properties:

1.

Isotony.
\mathcal{A}(I)\subset\mathcal{A}(J)
if
I,J\in\mathcal{I}
and
I\subset J
.

2.

Möbius covariance. There is a strongly continuous unitary representation
U
of
\operatorname{PSL}(2,\mathbb{R})^{(\infty)}
such that

U_{g}\mathcal{A}(I)U^{*}_{g}=\mathcal{A}(\dot{g}I),g\in\operatorname{PSL}(2,\mathbb{R})^{(\infty)},I\in\mathcal{I}.

3.

Positive energy. The conformal Hamiltonian
L_{0}
is positive.

4.

Existence and uniqueness of the vacuum. There exists a
U
-invariant vector
\Omega\in\mathcal{H}
which is unique up to a phase and cyclic for
\bigvee_{I\in\mathcal{I}}\mathcal{A}(I)
, the von Neumann algebra generated by the algebras
\mathcal{A}(I),I\in\mathcal{I}
.

5.

Graded locality. There exists a self adjoint unitary(the grading unitary)
\Gamma
on
\mathcal{H}
satisfying
\Gamma\mathcal{A}(I)\Gamma=\mathcal{A}(I)
for all
I\in\mathcal{I}
and
\Gamma\Omega=\Omega
and such that
\mathcal{A}(I^{\prime})\subset Z\mathcal{A}(I)^{\prime}Z^{*},\ I\in\mathcal{I}
, where
Z\coloneqq\frac{1-i\Gamma}{1-i}
.

6.

Diffeomorphism covariance. There is a strongly continuous projective unitary representation of
\operatorname{Diff}(S^{1})^{(\infty)}
, denoted again by
U
, extending the unitary representation of
\operatorname{PSL}(2,\mathbb{R})^{(\infty)}
and such that

U_{g}\mathcal{A}(I)U^{*}_{g}=\mathcal{A}(\dot{g}I),\ g\in\operatorname{Diff}(S^{1})^{(\infty)},\ I\in\mathcal{I},

and such that

U_{g}xU^{*}_{g}=x,x\in\mathcal{A}(I^{\prime}),\ g\in\operatorname{Diff}(S^{1})^{(\infty)}_{I},\ I\in\mathcal{I}.

A local conformal net is a graded local conformal net with trivial gauge unitary
\Gamma=1
. The Bose subnet of a graded local conformal net
\mathcal{A}
is defined as the fixed point subnet
\mathcal{A}^{\mathfrak{F}}
, where
\mathfrak{F}=\operatorname{Ad}\Gamma
is called the Fermi grading. The restriction of
\mathcal{A}^{\mathfrak{F}}
to the Bose subspace of
\mathcal{H}
is a local conformal net with respect to the restriction to this subspace of the projective representation
U
of
\operatorname{Diff}(S^{1})^{(\infty)}
.

Some consequences
[
9
]
,
[
13
]
,
[
15
]
,
[
10
]
of the preceding definition are:

1.

Reeh-Schlieder Property.
\Omega
is cyclic and separating for every
\mathcal{A}(I),I\in\mathcal{I}
.

2.

Bisognano-Wichmann Property. Let
I\in\mathcal{I}
and let
\Delta_{I},J_{I}
be the modular operator and the modular conjugation of
(\mathcal{A}(I),\Omega)
. Then we have

U(\delta_{I}(-2\pi t))=\Delta_{I}^{it},t\in\mathbb{R}.

Moreover, the unitary representation
U:\operatorname{PSL}(2,\mathbb{R})^{(\infty)}\to B(\mathcal{H})
extends to an (anti-)unitary representation of
\operatorname{PSL}(2,\mathbb{R})\rtimes\mathbb{Z}/2
determined by

U(r_{I})=ZJ_{I},

acting covariantly on
\mathcal{A}
. Here
(\delta_{I}(t))_{t\in\mathbb{R}}
is the one-parameter dilation subgroup of
\operatorname{PSL}(2,\mathbb{R})
with respect to
I
and
r_{I}
the reflection of the interval onto the complement
I^{\prime}
.

3.

Graded Haag Duality.
\mathcal{A}(I^{\prime})=Z\mathcal{A}(I)^{\prime}Z^{*}
, for
I\in\mathcal{I}
.

4.

Outer regularity.
\mathcal{A}(I_{0})=\bigcap_{I\in\mathcal{I},I\supset\bar{I_{0}}}\mathcal{A}(I),I_{0}\in\mathcal{I}
.

5.

Additivity. If
I=\bigcup_{\alpha}I_{\alpha}
with
I,I_{\alpha}\in\mathcal{I}
, then
\mathcal{A}(I)=\bigvee_{\alpha}\mathcal{A}(I_{\alpha})
.

6.

Factoriality.
\mathcal{A}(I)
is a type III
1
-factor for
I\in\mathcal{I}
.

7.

Irreducibility.
\bigvee_{I\in\mathcal{I}}\mathcal{A}(I)=B(\mathcal{H})
.

8.

Vacuum Spin-Statistics theorem.
e^{i2\pi L_{0}}=\Gamma
.

9.

Uniqueness of Covariance. For fixed
\Omega
, the strongly continuous projective representation
U
of
\operatorname{Diff}(S^{1})^{(\infty)}
making the net covariant is unique.

Representations of Graded Local Conformal Nets

In this section, we assume that
\mathcal{A}
is a graded local conformal net, although certain notions and results are valid in the Möbius covariance case.

Definition 2
.

A Doplicher-Haag-Roberts (DHR) representation
\lambda

\mathcal{A}
is a map

I\to\lambda_{I}
that associates to an interval
I\in\mathcal{I}
a normal representation
\lambda_{I}
of
\mathcal{A}(I)

such that

\lambda_{\tilde{I}}|_{\mathcal{A}(I)}=\lambda_{I},\ I\subset\tilde{I},

for

We say that a representation

is diffeomorphism covariant if there exists a projective unitary representation

\lambda_{\dot{g}I}(U_{g}xU^{*}_{g})=U_{\lambda}(g)\lambda_{I}(x)U^{*}_{\lambda}(g),\ x\in\mathcal{A}(I),\ g\in\operatorname{Diff}(S^{1})^{\infty}.

Here

denotes the image of

in
\operatorname{Diff}(S^{1})

Definition 3
.

A DHR representation
\lambda
on
\mathcal{H}_{\lambda}
is graded if there exists a self adjoint unitary operator
\Gamma_{\lambda}
on
\mathcal{H}_{\lambda}
such that

\lambda_{I}(\Gamma x\Gamma)=\Gamma_{\lambda}\lambda_{I}(x)\Gamma_{\lambda},\ x\in\mathcal{A}(I),

for
I\in\mathcal{I}
.

Proposition 1

(
[
9
]
, Proposition 12)
.

Let

be an irreducible DHR representation of

. Then

is graded and diffeomorphism covariant.

A localized endomorphism
\lambda
of
\mathcal{A}
is an endomorphism of the universal

(

, Section

is identity for some

. Then we say

.

Definition 4
.

A localized endomorphism of
\lambda
of
\mathcal{A}
is called graded if it commutes with the Fermi grading

, namely,
\Gamma\lambda(x)\Gamma=\lambda(\Gamma x\Gamma)
,

The following proposition generalizes to the graded local conformal net case the well known DHR argument for correspondence between DHR representations and localized endomorphisms.

Proposition 2
.

Let
\pi
be a graded DHR representation of the graded local conformal net
\mathcal{A}
, and suppose

Proof.

By Proposition

Fix an interval
