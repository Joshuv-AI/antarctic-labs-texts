Lemma 2.3
.

There does not exist an

-decomposition of

Proof.

Contrary assume that

-decomposition for some

Then the number of edges of
L_{16}
must divide the number of edges of

Hence

divides
n2^{n-1}.
This shows that

and so,

Since

has
80
edges, there are five copies of the graph
L_{16}
in the
L_{16}
-decomposition of

Every vertex of
Q_{5}
has degree

whereas
L_{16}
has eight vertices of degree 3 and eight of degree 1. Therefore, a degree 3 vertex of any copy of

in the decomposition cannot be a degree 3 vertex of another copy of

This implies that
Q_{5}
has at least 40 vertices, a contradiction.
∎
