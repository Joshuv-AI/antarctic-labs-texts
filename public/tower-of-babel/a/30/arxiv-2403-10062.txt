Product of rectangular Toeplitz (Hankel) Matrices.
Said Bensliman 1 ∗ , Ameur Yagoub 1 , Kamel Toumache 2

Abstract
. In this paper, we study products of asymmetric Toeplitz matrices, we give necessary and sufficient conditions for the product of two asymmetric Toeplitz matrices compatible sizes is asymmetric Toeplitz matrix. We also give some results related to the isometry.

The main purpose of this paper is another type of product, which is the product of two asymmetric Toeplitz matrices

2.
Preliminaries.

Let
M_{n\times m}(\mathbb{C)}
be the set of complex
n\times m
matrices. In this paper, we will number the rows and columns of
n\times m
matrices from

\{e_{1},e_{2},...,e_{n}\}
,

, and
\{\zeta_{1},\zeta_{2},...,\zeta_{l}\}

\mathbb{C}^{n}
,
\mathbb{C}^{m}
, and
\mathbb{C}^{l}
, respectively. Let
I_{n\times m}=(r_{ij})
and
J_{n\times m}=(l_{ij})
be
n\times m
matrices defined as

r_{ij}=\begin{cases}1\quad if\quad i=j\\
0\quad if\quad i\neq j\end{cases}

, and

l_{ij}=\begin{cases}1\quad if\quad i+j=m+1\\
0\quad otherwise\end{cases}
.

If
n=m
, then
I_{n}=I_{n\times n}
and
J_{n}=J_{n\times n}
.

Let
S_{n}
and
P_{n}
be
n\times n
matrices defined as

S_{n}=\left[\begin{array}[]{ccccc}0&0&\cdots&\\
1&0&&\\
0&1&&\\
\vdots&&\ddots&\\
0&\cdots&&1&0\end{array}\right],\textit{ and }P_{n}=\left[\begin{array}[]{cccccc}0&&\cdots&0&1\\
\vdots&&&1&0\\
&&\iddots&&\vdots\\
&&&&\\
1&&&&0\end{array}\right].

Let
\Delta A=A-S_{n}AS_{m}^{\ast}
be the displacement of matrix
A\in M_{n\times m}(\mathbb{C)}
.
Recall that an asymmetric Toeplitz matrix is a matrix of the form

A=\left[\begin{array}[]{ccccccc}a_{0}&\overline{\alpha}_{1}&\cdots&&&\overline{\alpha}_{m-1}\\
a_{1}&a_{0}&&&&\vdots\\
\vdots&\ddots&&&&\\
&&&&&\\
a_{n-1}&&&&&\end{array}\right].

The matrix
H
is called an asymmetric Hankel matrix if it has the form

H=\left[\begin{array}[]{ccccccc}a_{1}&a_{2}&&\cdots&a_{m-1}&a_{0}\\
a_{2}&a_{3}&&&&\overline{\alpha}_{1}\\
\vdots&&&&\iddots&\vdots\\
&&&&&\\
a_{n-1}&&&&&\overline{\alpha}_{n-1}\end{array}\right].

While
H
will be written in the form
H=AP_{m}
, where
A
is a Toeplitz matrix, note that every Hankel matrix can be obtained from an appropriate Toeplitz matrix. If
n=m
, then
A
is a Toeplitz matrix and
H
is a Hankel matrix. From the definition above, it is evident that if
A
is an asymmetric Toeplitz (respectively, Hankel) matrix, then
A^{T}
and
A^{\ast}
are also asymmetric Toeplitz (respectively, Hankel) matrices.

Let
a
and
\alpha
be vectors in
\mathbb{C}^{n}
and
\mathbb{C}^{m}
, respectively, where

a=(0,a_{1},a_{2},...,a_{n-1})^{T}
and
\alpha=(0,\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2},...,\alpha_{m-1})^{T}
. We denote
\hat{a}=(0,\overline{a}_{n-1},\overline{a}_{n-2},...,\overline{a}_{1})^{T}
. And let
A(a,\alpha)
denote an asymmetric Toeplitz matrix, where

A(a,\alpha)=\left[\begin{array}[]{ccccccc}0&\overline{\alpha}_{1}&\cdots&&\overline{\alpha}_{m-1}\\
a_{1}&0&&&\vdots\\
\vdots&\ddots&&&\\
&&&&\\
a_{n-1}&&&&\end{array}\right],

and let
H(a,\alpha)
denote an asymmetric Hankel matrix, where

H(a,\alpha)=\left[\begin{array}[]{ccccccc}a_{1}&a_{2}&&\cdots&a_{m-1}&0\\
a_{2}&a_{3}&&&&\overline{\alpha}_{1}\\
\vdots&&&&\iddots&\vdots\\
&&&&&\\
&&&&&\overline{\alpha}_{n-1}\end{array}\right].

Observe that every asymmetric Toeplitz matrix
A
can be written as

A=A_{0}+a_{0}I_{n\times m},

where
A_{0}=A(a,\alpha).
And every asymmetric Hankel matrix

can be written as

H=H_{0}+a_{0}J_{n\times m},

where
H_{0}=H(a,\alpha)
. We have

In this article, the set of

Lemma 2.1
.

Let
A\in\mathcal{T}(n,m)
. Then

A=\sum\limits_{i=0}^{\min(n,m)-1}S_{n}^{i}(\Delta A)S_{m}^{i\ast}.

Proof.

Since
S_{n}^{n}=0
and
S_{m}^{m\ast}=0
, we have

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{i=0}^{\min(n,m)-1}S_{n}^{i}(\Delta A)S_{m}^{i\ast}

\displaystyle=\sum\limits_{i=0}^{\min(n,m)-1}S_{n}^{i}AS_{m}^{i\ast}-S_{n}^{i+1}AS_{m}^{i+1\ast}

\displaystyle=A-S_{n}^{\min(n,m)}AS_{m}^{\min(n,m)\ast}=A.

∎

Lemma 2.2
.

Let

if and only if

\Delta A=u\otimes\varepsilon_{0}+e_{0}\otimes v,

is
n\times m

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{i=0}^{n-1}(e_{i}\otimes\varepsilon_{i})\sum\limits_{i=0}^{\min{(m,l)}-1}(\varepsilon_{i}\otimes\zeta_{i})=\sum\limits_{i=0}^{n-1}\sum\limits_{k=0}^{\min{(m,l)}-1}(e_{i}\otimes\varepsilon_{i})(\varepsilon_{k}\otimes\zeta_{k})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{i=0}^{n-1}\sum\limits_{k=0}^{\min{(m,l)}-1}\langle\varepsilon_{k},\varepsilon_{i}\rangle(e_{i}\otimes\zeta_{k})=\sum\limits_{i=0}^{\min{(n,l)}-1}e_{i}\otimes\zeta_{i}=I_{n\times l}\in\mathcal{T}(n,l).

And if
l\leq m
, we have

\displaystyle I_{n\times m}I_{m\times l}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{i=0}^{\min{(n,m)}-1}(e_{i}\otimes\varepsilon_{i})\sum\limits_{i=0}^{l-1}(\varepsilon_{i}\otimes\zeta_{i})=\sum\limits_{i=0}^{\min{(n,m)}-1}\sum\limits_{k=0}^{l-1}(e_{i}\otimes\varepsilon_{i})(\varepsilon_{k}\otimes\zeta_{k})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{i=0}^{\min{(n,m)}-1}\sum\limits_{k=0}^{l-1}\langle\varepsilon_{k},\varepsilon_{i}\rangle(e_{i}\otimes\zeta_{k})=\sum\limits_{i=0}^{\min{(n,l)}-1}e_{i}\otimes\zeta_{i}=I_{n\times l}\in\mathcal{T}(n,l).

And if
m<\min(n,l)
, we have
I_{n\times m}I_{m\times l}=\sum\limits_{i=0}^{m-1}e_{i}\otimes\zeta_{i}
, then

\displaystyle\Delta(I_{n\times m}I_{m\times l})

\displaystyle=\sum\limits_{i=0}^{m-1}e_{i}\otimes\zeta_{i}-S_{n}\Big(\sum\limits_{i=0}^{m-1}e_{i}\otimes\zeta_{i}\Big)S_{l}^{\ast}=\sum\limits_{i=0}^{m-1}e_{i}\otimes\zeta_{i}-\sum\limits_{i=0}^{m-1}e_{i+1}\otimes\zeta_{i+1}

\displaystyle=\sum\limits_{i=0}^{m-1}e_{i}\otimes\zeta_{i}-\sum\limits_{i=1}^{m}e_{i}\otimes\zeta_{i}=e_{0}\otimes\zeta_{0}-e_{m}\otimes\zeta_{m}.

A_{0}^{\ast}a+\overline{a}_{0}a_{(n,m)}^{\sharp}+a_{0}\alpha+\dfrac{|a_{0}|^{2}-\|a\|^{2}-1}{2}\varepsilon_{0}=0
and one of the following two cases holds

(1)

A
has a form (
3.2
).

(2)

A=A(a,\lambda\hat{a}_{(m,n)})+a_{0}I_{n\times m}
for some
\lambda\in\mathbb{C}
, such that
|\lambda|=1
.

Proof.

If
m\leq n
.
A
is an isometric matrix if and only if
\Delta(A^{\ast}A-I_{m})=0
.

By Lemma
4.1
, and since
\alpha\otimes\alpha
and
\hat{a}_{(n,m)}\otimes\hat{a}_{(n,m)}
are
m\times m
matrices, where the first row and the first column are zero, then
\Delta(A^{\ast}A-I_{m})=0
if and only if

\alpha\otimes\alpha-\hat{a}_{(n,m)}\otimes\hat{a}_{(n,m)}=0

(4.2)

and

A_{0}^{\ast}a+\overline{a}_{0}a_{(n,m)}^{\sharp}+a_{0}\alpha+\dfrac{|a_{0}|^{2}-\|a\|^{2}-1}{2}\varepsilon_{0}=0.

By Lemma
2.5
, the equation (
4.2
) is satisfied if and only if one of the following two cases holds

(1)

(2)

Remark 4.4
.

Let

. If

, we have

Corollary 4.6
.

Let
