List-decodable Codes for Single-deletion Single-substitution with List-size Two
Wentu Song, Kui Cai, and Tuan Thanh Nguyen

Abstract
In this paper, we present an explicit construction of list-decodable codes for single-deletion and single-substitution with list size two and redundancy 3 ​ log ⁡ n + 4 3\log n+4 , where n n is the block length of the code. Our construction has lower redundancy than the best known explicit construction by Gabrys et al . ( ( arXiv 2021 ) ) , whose redundancy is 4 ​ log ⁡ n + O ⁡ ( 1 ) 4\log n+O(1) .

Introduction

Codes correcting insertion, deletion and substitution errors

[

\log n+2

4

Constructing optimal multiple-edit error correcting codes is much

presented in
[

has asymptotic rate strictly smaller than
1

-

[

The best known
t
-edit correcting codes for

by Sima
et al

. The method in

As a relaxation of the decoding requirement, list-decoding for

III
Main Results

In this section, we present our construction of list-decodable
codes for single-deletion and single-substitution. Our
construction only uses the weight and the first two order VT
syndromes for binary sequences.

We adopt the method of
[

\displaystyle f_{j}(\bm{x})=\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left(\sum_{\ell=1}^{i}\ell^{j-1}\right)x_{i}.

(1)

\displaystyle f_{j}(\bm{x})=\sum_{\ell=1}^{n}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{\ell}i^{j-1}\right)x_{\ell}=\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left(\sum_{\ell=i}^{n}x_{\ell}\right)i^{j-1}.

(2)

The code is given by the following definition, where

and

Definition 1

For any fixed values
