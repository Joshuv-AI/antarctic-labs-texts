SOME CLASSIFICATIONS FOR GAUSS MAP OF TUBULAR HYPERSURFACES IN 𝔼 1 4 \mathbb{E}^{4}_{1} CONCERNING LINEARIZED OPERATORS ℒ k \mathcal{L}_{k}
Ahmet Kazan 1∗ , Mustafa Altın 2 and Nurettin Cenk Turgay 3

Abstract
. In this study, we deal with the Gauss map of tubular hypersurfaces in 4-dimensional Lorentz-Minkowski space concerning the linearized operators ℒ 1 \mathcal{L}_{1} (Cheng-Yau) and ℒ 2 \mathcal{L}_{2} . We obtain the ℒ 1 \mathcal{L}_{1} (Cheng-Yau) operator of the Gauss map of tubular hypersurfaces that are formed as the envelope of a family of pseudo hyperspheres or pseudo hyperbolic hyperspheres whose centers lie on timelike or spacelike curves with non-null Frenet vectors in 𝔼 1 4 \mathbb{E}^{4}_{1} and give some classifications for these hypersurfaces which have generalized ℒ k \mathcal{L}_{k} 1-type Gauss map, first kind ℒ k \mathcal{L}_{k} -pointwise 1-type Gauss map, second kind ℒ k \mathcal{L}_{k} -pointwise 1-type Gauss map and ℒ k \mathcal{L}_{k} -harmonic Gauss map, k ∈ { 1 , 2 } k\in\{1,2\} .

Let

(n+1)

Minkowski space
\mathbb{E}^{n+1}_{1}

A smooth mapping

6

7
]

[
5

(See also
[
8
,
22
]

Let

denote the Gauss map of
M

conclude that

is of
1-type
if and only if it satisfies the equation

\Delta N=\lambda(N+C)

(1.1)

for a constant

Gauss map of some important submanifolds such as catenoid and helicoid of the

10

21

On the other hand, the Gauss map of some hypersurfaces of semi-Euclidean

\Delta N=f_{1}N+f_{2}C

f_{1}
,
f_{2}
and a constant vector

25
]

On the other hand, in

Let

-dimensional

\left\langle\text{ },\right\rangle

If

, so that

or

according to

The operator

acting on the coordinate functions of the Gauss

-dimensional Lorentz-Minkowski space

\mathcal{L}_{k}N=-\varepsilon\mathfrak{C}_{k}\left(\nabla H_{k+1}+\left(nH_{1}H_{k+1}-\left(n-k-1\right)H_{k+2}\right)N\right).

(2.1)

Here,

\left(\begin{array}[c]{c}n\\
k\end{array}\right)H_{k}=\left(-\varepsilon\right)^{k}a_{k},\text{ \ }\left(\left(\begin{array}[c]{c}n\\
k\end{array}\right)=\frac{n!}{k!(n-k)!}\right),

(2.2)

such that

\left.\begin{array}[c]{l}a_{1}=-\sum_{i=1}^{n}\kappa_{i},\\
\\
a_{k}=(-1)^{k}\sum_{i_{1}<i_{2}<...<i_{k}}^{n}\kappa_{i_{1}}\kappa_{i_{2}}...\kappa_{i_{k}},~k=2,3,...,n\end{array}\right\}

(2.3)

and

is called the
k
-th mean curvature of order

.

Also, the constant
\mathfrak{C}_{k}
is given by

\mathfrak{C}_{k}=\left(\begin{array}[c]{c}n\\
k+1\end{array}\right)(-\varepsilon)^{k}.

(2.4)

(For more details about the linearized operator
\mathcal{L}_{k}
, one can see

[
16
]

Definition 1
.

Let
\mathfrak{m}
and
\mathfrak{n}
be non-zero smooth functions
on
M
,
C\in\mathbb{E}_{1}^{n+1}
be a non-zero constant vector and

.

If the Gauss map

\mathbb{E}^{4}_{1}
satisfies

\mathcal{L}_{k}N=\mathfrak{m}N+\mathfrak{n}C,

\mathcal{L}_{k}

\mathcal{L}_{k}N=\mathfrak{m}N,

\mathcal{L}_{k}
-pointwise 1-type Gauss map;

iii:

\mathcal{L}_{k}N=\mathfrak{m}(N+C),
then

has second kind

\mathcal{L}_{k}
-pointwise 1-type Gauss map;

iv:

\mathcal{L}_{k}N=0
, then

is called
\mathcal{L}_{k}
-harmonic.

[

]
