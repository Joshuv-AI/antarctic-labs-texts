Polynomial tau-functions of the symplectic KP, orthogonal KP and BUC hierarchies Thanks: *Corresponding author. Email: yanzw@imu.edu.cn
Denghui Li and Zhaowen Yan ∗

Abstract
. This paper is concerned with the construction of the polynomial tau-functions of the symplectic KP (SKP), orthogonal KP (OKP) hierarchies and universal character hierarchy of B-type (BUC hierarchy), which are proved as zero modes of certain combinations of the generating functions. By applying the strategy of carrying out the action of the quantum fields on vacuum vector, the generating functions for symplectic Schur function, orthogonal Schur function and generalized Q Q -function have been presented. The remarkable feature is that polynomial tau-functions are the coefficients of certain family of generating functions. Furthermore, in terms of the Vandermonde-like identity and properties of Pfaffian, it is showed that the polynomial tau-functions of the SKP, OKP and BUC hierarchies can be written as determinant and Pfaffian forms, respectively. In addition, the soliton solutions of the SKP and OKP hierarchies have been discussed. Keywords : polynomial tau-functions; symplectic/orthogonal Schur function; generalized Q Q -function; generating functions; soliton solutions Mathematics Subject Classifications (2000) : 17B80, 35Q55, 37K10

Polynomial tau-functions and

By means of charged free fermions, we devote to discussing structures and properties of polynomial tau-functions for the SKP hierarchy. Furthermore, the generating functions for polynomial tau-functions of the SKP hierarchy can be obtained by acting the quantum fields of symplectic Schur functions on the bosonic Fock space

3.1.
Quantum fields presentation of symplectic Schur functions and the SKP hierarchy

The symmetric polynomial ring
\Lambda

\Lambda=\mathbb{C}[e_{1},e_{2},\ldots]=\mathbb{C}[h_{1},h_{2},\ldots]=\mathbb{C}[p_{1},p_{2},\ldots]

\displaystyle\mathcal{B}=\bigoplus\limits_{m\in\mathbb{Z}}\mathcal{B}^{m},\ \ \text{where}\ \ \mathcal{B}^{m}=z^{m}\cdot\mathbb{C}[p_{1},p_{2},\ldots]=z^{m}\Lambda.

\displaystyle R(u)\left(z^{m}f\mathbf{(x)}\right)=z^{m+1}u^{m+1}f.

\displaystyle R^{-1}(u)\left(z^{m}f\mathbf{(x)}\right)=z^{m-1}u^{-m}f.

(3.3)

Operators

\displaystyle\psi^{Sp,-}(u)=(1-u^{2})R^{-1}(u)E(-u)H^{\bot}(u)H^{\bot}(\frac{1}{u})=\sum\limits_{k\in\mathbb{Z}+\frac{1}{2}}\psi^{Sp,-}_{k}u^{-k-\frac{1}{2}}.

Definition 3.3

For an unknown function

Let

∎

Lemma 3.5
.

Let
\widehat{X}=\sum\limits_{i>N}C_{i}\psi_{i}^{Sp,+}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{l\in\mathbb{Z}+\frac{1}{2}}\psi_{l}^{Sp,+}\widehat{X}\otimes\psi_{-l}^{Sp,-}\widehat{X}=\sum\limits_{l\in\mathbb{Z}+\frac{1}{2}}(-\widehat{X}\psi_{l}^{Sp,+})\otimes(-\widehat{X}\psi_{-l}^{Sp,-}+C_{l})

\displaystyle(\widehat{X}\otimes\widehat{X})\widehat{\Omega}-\widehat{X}\sum\limits_{l\in\mathbb{Z}+\frac{1}{2}}C_{l}\psi_{l}^{Sp,+}\otimes 1=(\widehat{X}\otimes\widehat{X})\widehat{\Omega}-\widehat{X}^{2}\otimes 1=(\widehat{X}\otimes\widehat{X})\widehat{\Omega}.

∎

Corollary 3.6
.

Let

Multiplying

\displaystyle\widehat{A}_{i}(u)=u^{N_{i}}\widehat{h}_{i}\sum\limits_{k=0}^{\infty}a_{i,k}u^{k},\quad\quad N_{i}\in\mathbb{Z},\widehat{h}_{i},a_{i,k}\in\mathbb{C},a_{i,0}=1,\widehat{h}_{i}\neq 0,i=1,\ldots,l,

(3.25)

\mathbb{C}

\displaystyle\widehat{A}_{i}(u)H(u)=u^{N_{i}}\widehat{h}_{i}\exp\left(\sum\limits_{l=1}^{\infty}\widehat{c}_{i,l}u^{l}\right)\exp\left(\sum\limits_{l=1}^{\infty}\frac{p_{l}}{l}u^{l}\right)

3.3.
N
-soliton solutions of the SKP hierarchy

Now let us consider another extremely important exact solution of SKP hierarchy called the soliton solution.

Let

\displaystyle\Gamma^{Sp}(p,q)=p^{-1}(1-q^{2})R(p)R^{-1}(q)H(p)E(-q)E^{\bot}(-p)H^{\bot}(q)E^{\bot}(-\frac{1}{p})H^{\bot}(\frac{1}{q}).

From Proposition

(3.29)

In particular

Lemma 3.8
.

If
\tau
is a solution of the SKP hierarchy, then

Proof.

Using Eq. (

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{l\in\mathbb{Z}+\frac{1}{2}}(\sum\limits_{m,n\in\mathbb{Z}+\frac{1}{2}}\psi_{l}^{Sp,+}\psi_{m}^{Sp,+}\psi_{n}^{Sp,-}u^{-m-\frac{1}{2}}v^{-n-\frac{1}{2}}\otimes\sum\limits_{m,n\in\mathbb{Z}+\frac{1}{2}}\psi_{-l}^{Sp,-}\psi_{m}^{Sp,+}\psi_{n}^{Sp,-}u^{-m-\frac{1}{2}}v^{-n-\frac{1}{2}})

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{l,m,n\in\mathbb{Z}+\frac{1}{2}}-\psi_{m}^{Sp,+}\delta_{l,-n}u^{-m-\frac{1}{2}}v^{-n-\frac{1}{2}}\otimes\delta_{m,l}\psi_{n}^{Sp,-}u^{-m-\frac{1}{2}}v^{-n-\frac{1}{2}}+\psi_{m}^{Sp,+}\psi_{n}^{Sp,-}\psi_{l}^{Sp,+}u^{-m-\frac{1}{2}}v^{-n-\frac{1}{2}}

\displaystyle\otimes\psi_{m}^{Sp,+}\psi_{n}^{Sp,-}\psi_{-l}^{Sp,-}u^{-m-\frac{1}{2}}v^{-n-\frac{1}{2}}-\psi_{m}^{Sp,+}\delta_{l,-n}u^{-m-\frac{1}{2}}v^{-n-\frac{1}{2}}\otimes\psi_{m}^{Sp,+}\psi_{n}^{Sp,-}\psi_{-l}^{Sp,-}u^{-m-\frac{1}{2}}v^{-n-\frac{1}{2}}

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{l,m,n\in\mathbb{Z}+\frac{1}{2}}\psi_{m}^{Sp,+}\psi_{n}^{Sp,-}\psi_{l}^{Sp,+}u^{-m-\frac{1}{2}}v^{-n-\frac{1}{2}}\otimes\psi_{m}^{Sp,+}\psi_{n}^{Sp,-}\psi_{-l}^{Sp,-}u^{-m-\frac{1}{2}}v^{-n-\frac{1}{2}}

\displaystyle\left(\psi^{Sp,+}(u)\psi^{Sp,-}(v)\otimes\psi^{Sp,+}(u)\psi^{Sp,-}(v)\right)\widehat{\Omega}.

\displaystyle\left(\psi^{Sp,+}(u)\psi^{Sp,-}(v)\otimes\psi^{Sp,+}(u)\psi^{Sp,-}(v)\right)\widehat{\Omega}(\tau\otimes\tau)

(3.32)

4.1.
Quantum fields presentation of orthogonal Schur functions and the OKP hierarchy

Introduce the quantum fields defined by

\displaystyle\psi^{O,+}(u)=u^{-1}(1-u^{2})R(u)H(u)E^{\bot}(-u)E^{\bot}(-\frac{1}{u})=\sum\limits_{k\in\mathbb{Z}+\frac{1}{2}}\psi^{O,+}_{k}u^{-k-\frac{1}{2}},

(4.1)

\displaystyle\psi^{O,-}(u)=R^{-1}(u)E(-u)H^{\bot}(u)H^{\bot}(\frac{1}{u})=\sum\limits_{k\in\mathbb{Z}+\frac{1}{2}}\psi^{O,-}_{k}u^{-k-\frac{1}{2}}.

Proposition 4.1
.

It can be checked that
\psi^{O,+}(u),\psi^{O,-}(u)
satisfy the relations

\displaystyle\psi^{O,\pm}(u)\psi^{O,\pm}(v)+\psi^{O,\pm}(v)\psi^{O,\pm}(u)=0,

(4.2)

\displaystyle\psi^{O,+}(u)\psi^{O,-}(v)+\psi^{O,-}(v)\psi^{O,+}(u)=\delta(u,v).

Equivalently, Eq.(
4.2
) can be expressed as charged free fermions relation

\displaystyle\psi^{O,\pm}_{k}\psi^{O,\pm}_{l}+\psi^{O,\pm}_{l}\psi^{O,\pm}_{k}=0,

\displaystyle\psi^{O,+}_{k}\psi^{O,-}_{l}+\psi^{O,-}_{l}\psi^{O,+}_{k}=\delta_{k,-l}.

(4.3)

Remark 4.2
.

From the formula (
2
) and (
2.7
), we easily get the bosonic form of the fields
\psi^{O,\pm}(u)
:

\displaystyle\psi^{O,+}(u)=u^{-1}(1-u^{2})R(u)\exp\left(\sum\limits_{n\geq 1}\frac{p_{n}}{n}u^{n}\right)\exp\left(-\sum\limits_{n\geq 1}\frac{\partial}{\partial p_{n}}\frac{1}{u^{n}}\right)\exp\left(-\sum\limits_{n\geq 1}\frac{\partial}{\partial p_{n}}u^{n}\right),

\displaystyle\psi^{O,-}(u)=R^{-1}(u)\exp\left(-\sum\limits_{n\geq 1}\frac{p_{n}}{n}u^{n}\right)\exp\left(\sum\limits_{n\geq 1}\frac{\partial}{\partial p_{n}}\frac{1}{u^{n}}\right)\exp\left(\sum\limits_{n\geq 1}\frac{\partial}{\partial p_{n}}u^{n}\right).

(4.4)

Hence we obtain
\psi^{O,+}_{i}(z^{m})=0
if
i>-m-\frac{1}{2}
and
\psi^{O,-}_{i}(z^{m})=0
if
i>m-\frac{1}{2}
.

Definition 4.3
.

For an unknown function
\tau=\tau(x)
, the bilinear equation

(4.5)

(4.6)

Proof.

3.6

4.2.

It is known that orthogonal Schur functions are tau-functions of the OKP hierarchy
[

. Let
\widetilde{G}(u_{1},\ldots,u_{l})
be a generating function of the orthogonal Schur function in

\displaystyle\ \widetilde{G}(u_{1},\ldots,u_{l})=\prod\limits_{1\leq i<j\leq l}\left(u_{i}-u_{j}\right)\prod\limits_{1\leq i\leq j\leq l}\left(1-u_{i}u_{j}\right)\prod\limits_{i=1}^{l}H(u_{i}).

(4.7)

By Proposition
2.1

\displaystyle\psi^{O,+}(u_{1})\cdots\psi^{O,+}(u_{l})(z^{k}f)=z^{k+l}u_{1}^{k}\cdots u_{l}^{k}\widetilde{G}(u_{1},\ldots,u_{l}).

(4.8)

Consider the set of formal Laurent series
\widetilde{A}_{1}(u),\ldots,\widetilde{A}_{l}(u)
, define the formal Laurent series
\widetilde{T}_{i}(u)=\widetilde{A}_{i}(u)H(u)=\sum\limits_{p\in\mathbb{Z}}\widetilde{T}_{i,p}u^{p},\ i=1,\ldots,l

\widetilde{T}(u_{1},\ldots,u_{l})

(u_{1},\ldots,u_{l})

\displaystyle\ \widetilde{T}(u_{1},\ldots,u_{l})=\prod\limits_{1\leq i<j\leq l}\left(u_{i}-u_{j}\right)\prod\limits_{1\leq i\leq j\leq l}\left(1-u_{i}u_{j}\right)\prod\limits_{i=1}^{l}\widetilde{A}_{i}(u_{i})H(u_{i}).

(4.9)

For any vector

\displaystyle\ \widetilde{T}(u_{1},\ldots,u_{l})=\sum\limits_{\boldsymbol{\zeta}\in\mathbb{Z}^{l}}\widetilde{T}_{\boldsymbol{\zeta}}u_{1}^{\zeta_{1}}\cdots u_{l}^{\zeta_{l}}.

(4.10)

Theorem 4.7
.

1)

Formal Laurent series

\displaystyle\widetilde{T}(u_{1},\ldots,u_{l})=\det\left[\left(u_{i}^{l-j}-u_{i}^{l+j}\right)\widetilde{T}_{i}(u_{i})\right]_{1\leq i,j\leq l}.

(4.11)

2)

For any vector

(4.12)

3)

\widetilde{T}_{\boldsymbol{\zeta}}
is a polynomial tau-function of the OKP hierarchy.

Proof.

1)

According to Vandermonde-like identity

\displaystyle\det\left[u_{i}^{k-j}-u_{i}^{k+j}\right]

\displaystyle=

By changing

where

\displaystyle=

Hence

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\det\left[\widetilde{T}_{i,\zeta_{i}-l-j}-\widetilde{T}_{i,\zeta_{i}-l+j}\right]

6.
Conclusions and discussions

In this paper, we have discussed exact solutions of the SKP, OKP and BUC hierarchies including the polynomial-type and soliton-type solutions. It is showed that the generating functions play a vital role in establishing the polynomial tau-functions of the integrable systems. Furthermore, we expressed the polynomial tau-functions of the SKP, OKP and BUC hierarchies as determinant and Pfaffian forms, respectively. The results here are hoped to be helpful for better understanding the essential properties of the SKP, OKP and BUC hierarchies. It is known that symplectic universal character (SUC) and orthogonal universal character (OUC) hierarchies are the extensions of the SKP and OKP hierarchies. However, it should be pointed out that we have not expressed the polynomial tau-functions of the SUC and OUC hierarchies as a perfect determinant form due to the inappropriate quantum fields presentation of SUC and OUC. We will concentrate on studying this interesting question in the near future.
