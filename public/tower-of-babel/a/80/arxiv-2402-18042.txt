Gersten-type conjecture for henselian local rings of normal crossing varieties
Makoto Sakagaito

Abstract
Let n ≥ 0 n\geq 0 be an integer. For a normal crossing variety Y Y over the spectrum of a field k k of positive characteristic p > 0 p>0 , K.Sato defined an étale logarithmic Hodge-Witt sheaf λ Y , r n \lambda^{n}_{Y,r} (cf. [ 24 , p.726, Definition 3.1.1 (1)] ) on the étale site Y e ´ ​ t Y_{\mathrm{\acute{e}t}} which agrees with W r ​ Ω Y , log n W_{r}\Omega^{n}_{Y,\log} (cf. [ 11 ] ) in the case where Y Y is smooth over Spec ⁡ ( k ) \operatorname{Spec}(k) . In this paper, we prove the Gersten-type conjecture for λ r n \lambda^{n}_{r} over the henselization of the local ring 𝒪 Y , y \mathcal{O}_{Y,y} of Y Y at a point y ∈ Y y\in Y . As an application, we prove the relative version of the Gersten-type conjecture for the p p -adic étale Tate twist 𝔗 1 ​ ( n ) \mathfrak{T}_{1}(n) (cf. [ 25 , pp.537–538, Definition 4.2.4] ) over the henselization of the local ring 𝒪 𝔛 , x \mathcal{O}_{\mathfrak{X},x} of a semistable family 𝔛 \mathfrak{X} over the spectrum of a discrete valuation ring B B of mixed characteristic ( 0 , p ) (0,p) at a point x ∈ 𝔛 x\in\mathfrak{X} in the case where B B contains p p -th roots of unity. Moreover, we prove a generalization of Artin’s theorem (cf. [ 10 , p.98, Théorème (3.1)] ) about the Brauer groups.

Let
X
be an integral scheme and
\mathcal{F}

a constant sheaf on

.

\operatorname{H}^{q}_{\mathrm{Nis}}(X,\mathcal{F})=0
