Functional calculus and quantization commutes with reduction for Toeplitz operators on CR manifolds
Andrea Galasso, Chin-Yu Hsiao

Abstract
Given a CR manifold with non-degenerate Levi form, we show that the operators of the functional calculus for Toeplitz operators are complex Fourier integral operators of Szegő type. As an application, we establish semi-classical spectral dimensions for Toeplitz operators. We then consider a CR manifold with a compact Lie group action G G and we establish quantization commutes with reduction for Toeplitz operators. Moreover, we also compute semi-classical spectral dimensions for G G -invariant Toeplitz operators.



Keywords:
CR manifolds, Toeplitz operators, group actions

Introduction

The study of Toeplitz operators plays an important role in modern mathematical research, theoretical physics and it is closely related to various domain of mathematics such as operator theory, spectral theory, analysis, geometry and geometric quantization theory. Boutet de Monvel and Guillemin
[
BG
]
have been set up as an extension of the classical Toeplitz operators on Hardy spaces. They consider
\Pi_{\Sigma}P\Pi_{\Sigma}
, where
\Pi_{\Sigma}
is the Szegő projection associated to a given symplectic conic subset
\Sigma
and
P
is a pseudodifferential operator. The great interest for us is that the Toeplitz operators enjoy similar properties as pseudodifferential operators (symbolic caculus, Weyl law,…). In this work, we will study the functional calculus for Toeplitz operators on a CR manifold with non-degenerate Levi form which is fundamental for our study of Toeplitz operators. We show that the operators of the functional calculus for Toeplitz operators are complex Fourier integral operators of Szegő type. As an application, we establish semi-classical spectral dimensions for Toeplitz operators. We then consider a CR manifold with a compact Lie group action
G
and we establish quantization commutes with reduction for Toeplitz operators. Moreover, we also compute semi-classical spectral dimensions for
G
-invariant Toeplitz operators.

Let us recall some prior literature about micro-local theory of Toeplitz operators, without the pretense of being complete. Toeplitz operators on strictly pseudoconvex domain were introduced in the monograph
[
BG
]
, in the survey
[
P
]
it is reviewed some recent results concerning eigenfunction asymptotics in this context, largely based on the generalization to the almost complex symplectic category by Shiffman and Zelditch. In the presence of symmetries, in
[
MZ
]
some Toeplitz operator type properties in semiclassical analysis was investigated based on the analytic localization techniques developed by Bismut and Lebeau. We refer to
[
MM
]
for more comprehensive bibliographic notes, our approach is based on the Boutet de Monvel–Sjöstrand parametrix for the Szegő kernel.

We now formulate the main results. They are based on
[
Hs
]
,
[
HH
]
and
[
GH
]
where it is studied Toeplitz operators for CR manifolds with non-degenerate Levi-form, in particular in
[
GH
]
we studied the properties of the corresponding star-product inspired by previous results as in
[
BMS
]
and
[

]
. We refer the reader to Section

1.3

), we have

\begin{split}&{\rm Tr\,}(\Pi_{I}(T^{(q)}_{P,m})(x,x))=\sum^{r}_{i=1}a_{i,i}(x,x)=\sum^{r}_{i,j=1}\int b_{i,j}(x,y)a_{j,i}(y,x)\mathrm{dV}_{X}(y)\\
&\leq\sum^{r}_{i,j=1}\sqrt{\int\left|b_{i,j}(x,y)\right|^{2}\mathrm{dV}_{X}(y)\int\left|a_{j,i}(y,x)\right|^{2}\mathrm{dV}_{X}(y)}\\
&\leq\sqrt{\sum^{r}_{i,j=1}\int\left|b_{i,j}(x,y)\right|^{2}\mathrm{dV}_{X}(y)}\sqrt{\int\left|a_{j,i}(y,x)\right|^{2}\mathrm{dV}_{X}(y)}.\end{split}

(79)

Write

\tau(T^{(q)}_{P,m})^{2}(x,y)=\sum^{r}_{i,j=1}d_{i,j}(x,y)f_{i}(x)\otimes f_{k}(y),\ \ d_{i,j}(x,y)\in\mathbb{R},\ \ \forall i,j.

(80)

It is not difficult to see that

\begin{split}&\sum^{r}_{i,j=1}\int\left|b_{i,j}(x,y)\right|^{2}\mathrm{dV}_{X}(y)=\sum^{r}_{i=1}d_{i,i}(x,x)={\rm Tr\,}(\tau(T^{(q)}_{P,m})^{2}(x,x)),\\
&\sum^{r}_{i,j=1}\int\left|a_{i,j}(x,y)\right|^{2}\mathrm{dV}_{X}(y)=\sum^{r}_{i=1}a_{i,i}(x)={\rm Tr\,}(\Pi_{I}(T^{(q)}_{P,m})(x,x)).\end{split}

(81)

From (
79
) and (
81
), we get (
75
).
∎

We can repeat the proof of Lemma
5.1
with minor change and deduce

Lemma 5.2
.

With the notations and assumptions above, let
I\subset\mathbb{R}
be an open bounded interval with
0\notin\overline{I}
. Let
\tau\in\mathcal{C}^{\infty}_{c}(I)

with
0\notin{\rm supp\,}\tau
. We have

{\rm Tr\,}(\tau(T^{(q)}_{P,m})(x,x))\leq\sqrt{{\rm Tr\,}(\Pi_{I}(T^{(q)}_{P,m})(x,x))}\sqrt{{\rm Tr\,}(\tau(T^{(q)}_{P,m})^{2}(x,x))},\ \ \mbox{for every $x\in X$}.

(82)

Hence,

2{\rm Tr\,}(\tau(T^{(q)}_{P,m})(x,x))-{\rm Tr\,}(\tau(T^{(q)}_{P,m})^{2}(x,x))\leq{\rm Tr\,}(\Pi_{I}(T^{(q)}_{P,m})(x,x)),\ \ \mbox{for every $x\in X$}.

(83)

We can now prove the following semi-classical spectral dimensions for Toeplitz operators

Theorem 5.2
.

With the notations and assumptions above, let
I\subset\mathbb{R}
be an open bounded interval with
0\notin\overline{I}
. Suppose that
X
is connected and
X_{{\rm reg\,}}\neq\emptyset
.
We have

\lim_{m\rightarrow+\infty}m^{-n}{\rm dim\,}E_{I}(T^{(q)}_{P,m})=\frac{1}{2}\pi^{-n-1}\int_{\left\{x\in X;\,\sigma^{0}_{P}(x,-\omega_{0}(x))\in I\right\}}\left|{\rm det\,}\mathcal{L}_{x}\right|\mathrm{dV}_{X}(x).

(84)

Proof.

Let
\left\{\tau_{j}\right\}^{+\infty}_{j=1}\subset\mathcal{C}^{\infty}_{c}(\mathbb{R})
with
0\notin{\rm supp\,}\tau_{j}
,
\tau_{j}\equiv 1
on some neighborhood of
I
, for every
j
and

\lim_{j\rightarrow+\infty}\tau_{j}=1_{I}
pointwise, where
1_{I}(x)=1
if
x\in I
,
1_{I}(x)=0
if
x\notin I
. Let
\left\{\hat{\tau}_{j}\right\}^{+\infty}_{j=1}\subset\mathcal{C}^{\infty}_{c}(I)
with
0\notin{\rm supp\,}\hat{\tau}_{j}
, for every
j
and

\lim_{j\rightarrow+\infty}\hat{\tau}_{j}=1_{I}
pointwise. From (
75
) and (
83
), we have for every
j=1,2,\ldots
,

\begin{split}&m^{-n}\int_{X}\Bigr(2{\rm Tr\,}(\hat{\tau}_{j}(T^{(q)}_{P,m})(x,x))-{\rm Tr\,}(\hat{\tau}_{j}(T^{(q)}_{P,m})^{2}(x,x))\Bigr)\mathrm{dV}_{X}(x)\\
&\leq m^{-n}\int_{X}{\rm Tr\,}(\Pi_{I}(T^{(q)}_{P,m})(x,x))\mathrm{dV}_{X}(x)\\
&\leq m^{-n}\int_{X}{\rm Tr\,}(\tau_{j}(T^{(q)}_{P,m})^{2}(x,x))\mathrm{dV}_{X}(x).\end{split}

(85)

Note that

{\rm dim\,}E_{I}(T^{(q)}_{P,m})=\int_{X}{\rm Tr\,}(\Pi_{I}(T^{(q)}_{P,m})(x,x))\mathrm{dV}_{X}(x).

From this observation, (
73
), (
85
) and take
j\rightarrow+\infty
in (
85
), the theorem follows.
∎

5.4
Quantization commutes with reduction on CR manifolds with circle action

Now, we assume that
X
admits a compact Lie group action
G
of dimension
d
. We assume that Assumption
3.1
and Assumption
3.2
hold. We assume further that

Assumption 5.1
.

The Reeb vector field
R
is transversal to the space
\underline{\mathfrak{g}}
at every point
p\in\mu^{-1}(0)
. Furthermore, we assume that the circle action commutes with the one of
G
on
X
:

e^{i\theta}\circ g\circ x=g\circ e^{i\theta}\circ x,\ \ \mbox{for every $x\in X$, $\theta\in[0,2\pi[$, $g\in G$}\,,

(86)

Eventually, we assume

\mbox{$G\times S^{1}$ acts locally free near $\mu^{-1}(0)$}\,.

Fix a
S^{1}\times G
-invariant smooth Hermitian metric
\langle\,\cdot\,|\,\cdot\,\rangle
on
TX\otimes\mathbb{C}
so that
T^{1,0}X
is orthogonal to
T^{0,1}X
,
\underline{\mathfrak{g}}
is orthogonal to
HY\cap JHY
at every point of
Y
,
\langle\,u\,|\,v\,\rangle
is real if
u,v
are real tangent vectors and
\langle\,R\,|\,R\,\rangle=1
. For every
m\in\mathbb{Z}
, we set

\Omega^{0,q}_{m}(X)^{G}:=\left\{u\in\Omega^{0,q}(X)^{G};\,(e^{i\theta})^{*}u=e^{im\theta}u,\ \ \forall e^{i\theta}\in S^{1}\right\}.

Let
L^{2}_{(0,q),m}(X)^{G}
be
the completion of
\Omega^{0,q}_{m}(X)^{G}
with respect to
(\,\cdot\,|\,\cdot\,)
.
Put

({\rm Ker\,}\Box^{q}_{b})^{G}_{m}:=({\rm Ker\,}\Box^{q}_{b})^{G}\cap L^{2}_{(0,q),m}(X).

Let

H^{q}_{b,m}(X)^{G}:=({\rm Ker\,}\Box^{q}_{b})^{G}_{m}.

From Corollary
1.1
, we get

Theorem 5.3
.

With the same notations and assumptions above, if
n_{-}=n_{+}
,
n_{X_{G},-}=n_{X_{G},+}
or
n_{-}\neq n_{+}
,
n_{X_{G},-}\neq n_{X_{G},+}
, then

H^{n_{-}}_{b,m}(X)^{G}\cong H^{n_{X_{G},-}}_{b,m}(X_{G})

for
\left|m\right|\gg 1
.

If
n_{-}=n_{+}
and
n_{X_{G},-}\neq n_{X_{G},+}
, then

H^{n_{-}}_{b,m}(X)^{G}\cong H^{n_{X_{G},-}}_{b,m}(X_{G})\oplus H^{n_{X_{G},+}}_{b,m}(X_{G})

for
\left|m\right|\gg 1
.

If
n_{-}\neq n_{+}
and
n_{X_{G},-}=n_{X_{G},+}
, then

H^{n_{-}}_{b,m}(X)^{G}\oplus H^{n_{+}}_{b,m}(X)^{G}\cong H^{n_{X_{G},-}}_{b,m}(X_{G})

for
\left|m\right|\gg 1
.

5.5
Semi-classical spectral dimensions for invariant Toeplitz operators on CR manifolds with circle action

The
m
-th
G
-invariant Szegő projection is the orthogonal projection

S^{(q)}_{G,m}:L^{2}_{(0,q)}(X)\rightarrow({\rm Ker\,}\Box^{(q)}_{b})^{G}_{m}

with respect to
(\,\cdot\,|\,\cdot\,)
.
Let
P\in L^{0}_{{\rm cl\,}}(X,T^{*0,q}X\boxtimes(T^{*0,q}X)^{*})^{G\times S^{1}}
be a self-adjoint
G\times S^{1}
-invariant pseudodifferential operator on
X
with scalar principal symbol. Put

T^{(q)}_{P,G,m}:=S^{(q)}_{G,m}\circ P\circ S^{(q)}_{G,m}:L^{2}_{(0,q)}(X)\rightarrow L^{2}_{(0,q),m}(X)^{G}.

We can now prove the following semi-classical spectral dimensions for
G
-invariant Toeplitz operators

Theorem 5.4
.

With the notations and assumptions above, let
I\subset\mathbb{R}
be an open bounded interval with
0\notin\overline{I}
. Suppose that
X
is connected and
X_{{\rm reg\,}}\neq\emptyset
..
We have

\lim_{m\rightarrow+\infty}m^{-n+d}{\rm dim\,}E_{I}(T^{(q)}_{P,G,m})=\frac{1}{2}\pi^{-n+d-1}\int_{\left\{x\in X_{G};\,\sigma^{0}_{P_{X_{G}}}(x,-\omega_{0}(x))\in I\right\}}\left|{\rm det\,}\mathcal{L}_{X_{G},x}\right|\mathrm{dV}_{X_{G}}(x).

(87)

Proof.

Let
I_{1}
be an open bounded interval with
0\notin\overline{I_{1}}
,
I\subset I_{1}
,
I\neq I_{1}
. Let
\tau\in\mathcal{C}^{\infty}_{c}(I_{1})
with
0\notin{\rm supp\,}\tau
and
\tau\equiv 1
on some neighborhood of
I
. From
Theorem
4.4
and notice that
{\rm dim\,}E_{\tau}(T^{(n_{+})}_{P,G,m})={\rm dim\,}E_{\tau}(T^{(n_{X_{G},+})}_{P,G,m})=0
if
m\gg 1
, we conclude that if
m\gg 1
, we have

{\rm dim\,}E_{I}(T^{(q)}_{P,G,m})\leq{\rm dim\,}E_{\tau}(T^{(q)}_{P,G,m})\leq{\rm dim\,}E_{I_{1}}(T^{(n_{X_{G},-})}_{P,G,m})\,.

(88)

From (
84
) and (
88
), we get

\begin{split}&\lim_{m\rightarrow+\infty}m^{-n+d}{\rm dim\,}E_{I}(T^{(q)}_{P,G,m})\\
&\leq\lim_{m\rightarrow+\infty}m^{-n+d}{\rm dim\,}E_{I_{1}}(T^{(n_{X_{G},-})}_{P,G,m})=\frac{1}{2}\pi^{-n+d-1}\int_{\widetilde{X_{G}}}\left|{\rm det\,}\mathcal{L}_{X_{G},x}\right|\mathrm{dV}_{X_{G}}(x)\end{split}

(89)

where

\widetilde{X_{G}}:=\left\{x\in X_{G};\,\sigma^{0}_{P_{X_{G}}}(x,-\omega_{0}(x))\in I_{1}\right\}\,.

Let
\hat{I}_{1}
be an open interval with
0\notin\overline{\hat{I}_{1}}
,
\hat{I}_{1}\subset I
,
\hat{I}_{1}\neq I
. Let
\hat{\tau}\in\mathcal{C}^{\infty}_{c}(I)
with
0\notin{\rm supp\,}\hat{\tau}
and
\hat{\tau}\equiv 1
on some neighborhood of
\hat{I}_{1}
. From Theorem
4.4
again, if
m\gg 1
, we have

{\rm dim\,}E_{I}(T^{(q)}_{P,G,m})\geq{\rm dim\,}E_{\hat{\tau}}(T^{(q)}_{P,G,m})\geq{\rm dim\,}E_{\hat{I}_{1}}(T^{(n_{X_{G},-})}_{P,G,m}).

(90)

From (
84
) and (
90
), we get

\begin{split}&\lim_{m\rightarrow+\infty}m^{-n+d}{\rm dim\,}E_{I}(T^{(q)}_{P,G,m})\\
&\geq\lim_{m\rightarrow+\infty}m^{-n+d}{\rm dim\,}E_{\hat{I}_{1}}(T^{(n_{X_{G},-})}_{P,G,m})=\frac{1}{2}\pi^{-n+d-1}\int_{\widehat{X_{G}}}\left|{\rm det\,}\mathcal{L}_{X_{G},x}\right|\mathrm{dV}_{X_{G}}(x)\end{split}

(91)

where

\widehat{X_{G}}:=\left\{x\in X_{G};\,\sigma^{0}_{P_{X_{G}}}(x,-\omega_{0}(x))\in\hat{I}_{1}\right\}

From (
89
) and (
91
), we get (
87
).
∎
