Uniform (d+1)-bundle over the Grassmannian G(d,n)
Rong Du, Yuhang Zhou

Abstract
This paper is dedicated to the classification of uniform vector bundles of rank d + 1 d+1 over the Grassmannian G ⁡ ( d , n ) G(d,n) ( d ≤ n − d d\leq n-d ) over an algebraically closed field in characteristic 0 0 . Specifically, we show that all uniform vector bundles with rank d + 1 d+1 over G ⁡ ( d , n ) G(d,n) are homogeneous.

Introduction

Algebraic vector bundles over a projective variety

\mathbb{P}^{n}
