Toughness and existence of 2 2 -factors
Leyou Xu, Bo Zhou

Abstract
A graph is t t -tough if the deletion of any set of, say, m m vertices from the graph leaves a graph with at most m t \frac{m}{t} components. In 1973, Chvátal suggested the problem of relating toughness to factors in graphs. In 1985, Enomoto et al. showed that each 2 2 -tough graph with at least three vertices has a 2 2 -factor, but for any ϵ > 0 \epsilon>0 , there exists a ( 2 − ϵ ) (2-\epsilon) -tough graph on at least 3 3 vertices having no 2 2 -factor. In recent years, the study of sufficient conditions for graphs with toughness less than 2 2 having a 2 2 -factor has received a paramount interest. In this paper, we give new tight sufficient conditions for a t t -tough graph having a 2 2 -factor when 1 ≤ t < 2 1\leq t<2 by involving independence number, minimum degree, connectivity and forbidden forests, improving and extending some existing results. , no two vertices of

can be in the same component of

. As there is no edges between

and

and

2

\displaystyle\tau(G)

\displaystyle\leq\frac{|A|+\ell^{\prime}+\sum_{t\geq 1}2t|\mathcal{C}_{2t+1}|}{|B|-\ell^{\prime}+\sum_{i=1}^{\ell^{\prime}}h_{G_{i}}(u_{i})}

(3)

\displaystyle\leq\frac{|B|+\sum_{t\geq 1}t|\mathcal{C}_{2t+1}|-1+\ell^{\prime}}{|B|+\sum_{i=1}^{\ell^{\prime}}h_{G_{i}}(u_{i})-\ell^{\prime}}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{(2-\epsilon)|B|+\max_{u\in B}h_{G}(u)-2+\ell^{\prime}}{|B|+\sum_{i=1}^{\ell^{\prime}}h_{G_{i}}(u_{i})-\ell^{\prime}}

\displaystyle\leq 2-\epsilon.

2
) is an equality, so (
