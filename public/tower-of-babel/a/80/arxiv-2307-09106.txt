Theoretical study on Λ c + → Λ ​ K + ​ K ¯ 0 \Lambda_{c}^{+}\to\Lambda K^{+}\bar{K}^{0} decay and Ξ ∗ ​ ( 1690 ) \Xi^{*}(1690) resonance
Si-Wei Liu, Qing-Hua Shen, Ju-Jun Xie

Abstract
We present a theoretical study of Ξ ∗ ​ ( 1690 ) \Xi^{*}(1690) resonance in the Λ c + → Λ ​ K + ​ K ¯ 0 \Lambda_{c}^{+}\to\Lambda K^{+}\bar{K}^{0} decay, where the weak interaction part proceeds through the Cabibbo-favored process c → s + u ​ d ¯ c\to s+u\bar{d} . Next, the intermediate two mesons and one baryon state can be constructed with a pair of q ​ q ¯ q\bar{q} with the vacuum quantum numbers. Finally, the Ξ ∗ ​ ( 1690 ) \Xi^{*}(1690) is mainly produced from the final state interactions of K ¯ ​ Λ \bar{K}\Lambda in coupled channels, and it is shown in the K ¯ ​ Λ \bar{K}\Lambda invariant mass distribution. Besides, the scalar meson a 0 ​ ( 980 ) a_{0}(980) and nucleon excited state N ∗ ​ ( 1535 ) N^{*}(1535) are also taken into account in the decaying channels K + ​ K ¯ 0 K^{+}\bar{K}^{0} and K + ​ Λ K^{+}\Lambda , respectively. Within model parameters, the K + ​ K ¯ 0 K^{+}\bar{K}^{0} , K ¯ 0 ​ Λ \bar{K}^{0}\Lambda and K + ​ Λ K^{+}\Lambda invariant mass distributions are calculated, and it is found that our theoretical results can reproduce well the experimental measurements, especially for the clear peak around 1690 1690 MeV in the K ¯ ​ Λ \bar{K}\Lambda spectrum. The proposed weak decay process Λ c + → Λ ​ K + ​ K ¯ 0 \Lambda_{c}^{+}\to\Lambda K^{+}\bar{K}^{0} and the interaction mechanism can provide valuable information on the nature of the Ξ ∗ ​ ( 1690 ) \Xi^{*}(1690) resonance.

II.2
Contribution of
\Xi^{*}(1690)

On the other hand, the decay process
\Lambda_{c}^{+}\to\Xi^{*}(1690)K^{+}\to\Lambda\bar{K}^{0}K^{+}
can also proceed through

as a Breit-Wigner resonance and decaying into

is also shown in Fig.

\Xi^{*}(1690)

Then, the general decay amplitude for the process
\Lambda_{c}^{+}\to\Xi^{*}(1690)^{0}K^{+}\to\bar{K}^{0}\Lambda K^{+}
can be expressed as:

\mathcal{M}_{e}=\frac{\Gamma_{\Xi^{*}(1690)}}{2}\frac{V_{3}}{M_{\bar{K}^{0}\Lambda}-M_{\Xi^{*}(1690)}+i\frac{\Gamma_{\Xi^{*}(1690)}}{2}},

(24)

where

\bar{K}^{0}\Lambda

MeV and

MeV for the Breit-Wigner mass and width for
\Xi^{*}(1690)
resonance, respectively, which are quoted in the PDG
Zyla et al. 2020

II.3
Invariant mass distributions of the
\Lambda^{+}_{c}\to\Lambda K^{+}\bar{K}^{0}
decay

With the ingredients obtained in the previous sections, we can write the double invariant mass distributions for the
\Lambda_{c}^{+}\to K^{+}\bar{K}^{0}\Lambda
decay as

\frac{d^{2}\Gamma}{dM_{K^{+}\bar{K}^{0}}dM_{\bar{K}^{0}\Lambda}}=\frac{M_{\Lambda}M_{K^{+}\bar{K}^{0}}M_{\bar{K}^{0}\Lambda}}{16\pi^{3}M_{\Lambda_{c}^{+}}^{2}}|\mathcal{M}|^{2},

(25)

where
{\cal M}
is the total decay amplitude. We will take two models for
{\cal M}
:

\displaystyle\mathcal{M}^{\rm I}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\mathcal{M}_{a}+\mathcal{M}_{b}+\mathcal{M}_{d}+\mathcal{M}_{c},

(26)

\displaystyle\mathcal{M}^{\rm II}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\mathcal{M}_{a}+\mathcal{M}_{b}+\mathcal{M}_{d}+e^{i\theta}\mathcal{M}_{e},

(27)

where a relative phase
\theta
is added for Model II, and it is a free parameter. In addition, another relative phase,
\phi
, between
V_{1}
and
V_{2}
is also taken into account. We will replace
V_{2}
with
e^{i\phi}V_{2}
in the following fitting process. Furthermore, it is worth to mention that
V_{1}
,
V_{2}
and
V_{3}
have dimension
{\rm MeV}^{-1}
.

Then the invariant
\bar{K}^{0}\Lambda
and
K^{+}\bar{K}^{0}
mass distributions can be obtained by integrating over the other invariant mass in Eq. (
25
). For a given value of
M_{\bar{K}^{0}\Lambda}
, the invariant mass
M_{K^{+}\bar{K}^{0}}
satisfies the following relation

\displaystyle(M_{K^{+}\bar{K}^{0}}^{\text{min}})^{2}

\displaystyle=(E_{K^{+}}+E_{\bar{K}^{0}})^{2}

(28)

\displaystyle-(\sqrt{E_{K^{+}}^{2}-m_{K^{+}}^{2}}+\sqrt{E_{\bar{K}^{0}}^{2}-m_{\bar{K}^{0}}^{2}})^{2},

\displaystyle(M_{K^{+}\bar{K}^{0}}^{\text{max}})^{2}

\displaystyle=(E_{K^{+}}+E_{\bar{K}^{0}})^{2}

\displaystyle-(\sqrt{E_{K^{+}}^{2}-m_{K^{+}}^{2}}-\sqrt{E_{\bar{K}^{0}}^{2}-m_{\bar{K}^{0}}^{2}})^{2},

where
E_{K^{+}}
and
E_{\bar{K}^{0}}
are the particle energies in the
\bar{K}^{0}\Lambda
rest frame, which can be expressed explicitly

\displaystyle E_{K^{+}}

\displaystyle=\frac{M_{\Lambda_{c}^{+}}^{2}-M_{\bar{K}^{0}\Lambda}^{2}-M_{K^{+}}^{2}}{2M_{\bar{K}^{0}\Lambda}},

(29)

\displaystyle E_{\bar{K}^{0}}

\displaystyle=\frac{M_{\bar{K}^{0}\Lambda}^{2}+M_{\bar{K}^{0}}^{2}-M_{\Lambda}^{2}}{2M_{\bar{K}^{0}\Lambda}}.

Note that the invariant
K^{+}\Lambda
mass distribution can be obtained by substituting
M_{K^{+}\bar{K}^{0}}
with
M_{K^{+}\Lambda}
.

IV
Summary

In this work, we have performed an analysis of the
\Lambda_{c}^{+}\to\Lambda K^{+}\bar{K}^{0}
decay via two cabibbo-favored mechanism. By considering the hadronization process and the final state interaction in the pseudoscalar-baryon and pseudoscalar-pseudoscalar channel with chiral unitary approach, the role of
\Xi^{*}(1690)
,
a_{0}(980)
, and
N^{*}(1535)
resonances are investigated. Taking into account the contributions of these three above resonances, we have calculated the
K^{+}K_{S}^{0}
,
\Lambda K_{S}^{0}
and
\Lambda K^{+}
invariant mass distributions. Up to five free parameters, we have done
\chi^{2}
-fit to the experimental measurements. It was found that the reaction proposed here can describe the experimental data of
BABAR
collaboration
Aubert et al. 2006
. Especially, the clear signal for the
\Xi^{*}(1690)
state can be well reproduced in the
\Lambda K^{0}_{S}
invariant mass spectrum.

Within the fitted model parameters, we have further calculated the ratio of branching fractions
\mathcal{B}(\Lambda_{c}^{+}\to K^{+}\Xi^{*}(1690)\to K^{+}\bar{K}^{0}\Lambda)/\mathcal{B}(\Lambda_{c}^{+}\to\Lambda K^{+}\bar{K}^{0})
to see the contribution of
\Xi^{*}(1690)
resonance in the
\Lambda^{+}_{c}\to\Lambda K^{+}\bar{K}^{0}
decay. The obtained value 0.28 for Model I, where the
\Xi^{*}(1690)
is dynamically generated from the meson-baryon final state interactions, is consistent with the experimental value by Belle collaboration
Abe et al. 2002
. It is expected that the
\Xi^{*}(1690)
state can be analyzed from the precise measurements of the
\Lambda^{+}_{c}\to K^{+}\bar{K}^{0}\Lambda
decay by BESIII, BelleII, and LHCb collaborations in the future
Ablikim et al. 2023
.
