Abstract
. We find new “reasons” for a class of models for not having a universal model in a cardinal λ \lambda . This work, though has consequences in model theory, is really in combinatorial (set theory). We concentrate on a prototypical class which is a simply defined class of models, of combinatorial character - models of T ceq T_{{\rm ceq}} (essentially another representation of T feq T_{{\rm feq}} which was already considered but the proof with T ceq T_{{\rm ceq}} is more transparent). Models of T ceq T_{{\rm ceq}} consist essentially of an equivalence relation on one set and a family of choice functions for it. This class is not simple (in the model theoretic sense) but seems to be very low among the non-simple (first order complete countable) ones. We give sufficient conditions for the non-existence of a universal model for it in λ \lambda . This work may be continued in [ S+ ] .
