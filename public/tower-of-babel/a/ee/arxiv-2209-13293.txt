Iterated Integrals associated with colored rooted trees
Hanamichi Kawamura

Abstract
. In this paper, we introduce iterated integrals associated with colored rooted trees and give proofs for the shuffle relations for 𝒑 \boldsymbol{p} -adic finite and t t -adic symmetric polylogarithms. This method generalizes the theory of the finite multiple zeta values associated with 2 2 -colored rooted trees introduced by Ono.

Definitions and known results

In this paper, a

Definition 2.1

(1)

(4)

•

•

It is clear that a harvestable pair

3.

A

in

is a pair
\tilde{p}\coloneqq(p,v)
consisting of

and its non-zero tangent vector

and tangential base points

and

from
\tilde{p}
to
\tilde{q}
on

such that

\gamma(0)=p,\qquad\gamma(1)=q,\qquad\lim_{t\searrow 0}\frac{\gamma(t)-p}{t}=v,\qquad\lim_{t\nearrow 1}\frac{\gamma(t)-q}{t-1}=-w

and
\gamma((0,1))\subseteq M
. Homotopy classes of such paths admit the composition and the inverse
[
GF
, §3.7]
. Note that, even if we omit the informations of endpoints (and their tangential vectors) as “a path
\gamma
on
M

\gamma(0)
or

can be in

.

Definition 3.1
.
