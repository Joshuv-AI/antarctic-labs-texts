Kolmogorov widths of anisotropic Sobolev classes
A.A. Vasil’eva

Abstract
In this article, order estimates for the Kolmogorov widths of periodic anisotropic Sobolev and Nikol’skii classes are obtained, as well as order estimates for the Kolmogorov widths of anisotropic finite-dimensional balls.



1
Introduction

In this paper, we obtain order estimates for the Kolmogorov widths of anisotropic periodic Sobolev and Nikol’skii classes
W^{\overline{r}}_{\overline{p},\overline{\alpha}}(\mathbb{T}^{d})
and
H^{\overline{r}}_{\overline{p}}(\mathbb{T}^{d})
in the anisotropic space
L_{\overline{q}}(\mathbb{T}^{d})
(see definitions below). Here
\mathbb{T}=[0,\,2\pi]
,
\overline{p}=(p_{1},\,\dots,\,p_{d})
,
\overline{q}=(q_{1},\,\dots,\,q_{d})
,
\overline{r}=(r_{1},\,\dots,\,r_{d})
,
\overline{\alpha}=(\alpha_{1},\,\dots,\,\alpha_{d})
,
r_{j}>0
,
\alpha_{j}\in\mathbb{R}
. The following cases will be considered: 1)
1\leqslant p_{j}\leqslant\infty
,
2\leqslant q_{j}<\infty
,
1\leqslant j\leqslant d
; 2)
1\leqslant p_{j}\leqslant q_{j}\leqslant 2
for
1\leqslant j\leqslant\nu
,
p_{j}\geqslant q_{j}
for
\nu+1\leqslant j\leqslant d
, where
\nu\in\{0,\,1,\,\dots,\,d\}
. Hence we generalize well-known results to anisotropic
\overline{p}
and
\overline{q}
under the conditions on the parameters mentioned above. In addition, we obtain order estimates for the Kolmogorov
n
-widths of finite-dimensional balls
B_{p_{1},\,\dots,\,p_{d}}^{k_{1},\,\dots,\,k_{d}}
in
l_{q_{1},\,\dots,\,q_{d}}^{k_{1},\,\dots,\,k_{d}}
for
1\leqslant p_{j}\leqslant\infty
,
2\leqslant q_{j}<\infty
,
1\leqslant j\leqslant d
,
n\leqslant\frac{k_{1}\dots k_{d}}{2}
. For
d=1
, this result was obtained by Gluskin
[
20
]
, and for
d=2
, it was obtained in
[
45
]
.

We need some notation.

Let
1\leqslant p_{1},\,\dots,\,p_{d}\leqslant\infty
,
\overline{p}=(p_{1},\,\dots,\,p_{d})
,
(X_{1},\,\mu_{1}),\,\dots,\,(X_{d},\,\mu_{d})
be measure spaces. The anisotropic norm
\|\cdot\|_{L_{\overline{p}}(X_{1}\times\dots\times X_{d},\,\mu_{1}\otimes\dots\otimes\mu_{d})}
is defined by induction on
d
: for
d=1
, it is the standard norm in the space
L_{p_{1}}(X_{1},\,\mu_{1})
, and for
d\geqslant 2
, the recurrence relation is as follows:

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{c}\|f\|_{L_{\overline{p}}(X_{1}\times\dots\times X_{d},\,\mu_{1}\otimes\dots\otimes\mu_{d})}=\|g_{f}\|_{L_{p_{d}}(X_{d},\,\mu_{d})},\\
g_{f}(x_{d})=\|f(\cdot,\,x_{d})\|_{L_{(p_{1},\,\dots,\,p_{d-1})}(X_{1}\times\dots\times X_{d-1,}\,\mu_{1}\otimes\dots\otimes\mu_{d-1})};\end{array}

here
f
is a measurable function on
(X_{1}\times\dots\times X_{d},\,\mu_{1}\otimes\dots\otimes\mu_{d})
.
In particular, for finite
p_{1},\,\dots,\,p_{d}
,

\|f\|_{L_{\overline{p}}(X_{1}\times\dots\times X_{d},\,\mu_{1}\otimes\dots\otimes\mu_{d})}=

=\Bigl(\int\limits_{X_{d}}\dots\Bigl(\int\limits_{X_{2}}\Bigl(\int\limits_{X_{1}}|f(x_{1},\,x_{2},\,\dots,\,x_{d})|^{p_{1}}\,d\mu_{1}\Bigr)^{p_{2}/p_{1}}\,d\mu_{2}\Bigr)^{p_{3}/p_{2}}\dots\,d\mu_{d}\Bigr)^{1/p_{d}}.

The space
L_{\overline{p}}(X_{1}\times\dots\times X_{d},\,\mu_{1}\otimes\dots\otimes\mu_{d})
consists of equivalence classes of measurable functions such that
\|f\|_{L_{\overline{p}}(X_{1}\times\dots\times X_{d},\,\mu_{1}\otimes\dots\otimes\mu_{d})}<\infty
.

We denote by
\mathbb{T}
the interval
[0,\,2\pi]
with “glued” endpoints, equipped with the normalized Lebesgue measure (i.e., the measure of
[a,\,b]\subset\mathbb{T}
is
(b-a)/2\pi
).
If we take as
(X_{j},\,\mu_{j})
the set
\mathbb{T}
with normalized Lebesgue measure for each
j=1,\,\dots,\,d
, then the corresponding anisotropic norm will be denoted by
\|\cdot\|_{L_{\overline{p}}(\mathbb{T}^{d})}
.

More information about the spaces with anisotropic norm can be found, e.g., in
[

,
17
]
.

Definition 1

The class

(4)

.

Let
h\in\mathbb{R}
,

. We continue

and set

\Delta_{h}^{1,j}f(x_{1},\,\dots,\,x_{d})=f(x_{1},\,\dots,\,x_{j-1},\,x_{j}+h,\,x_{j+1},\,\dots,\,x_{d})-

For

is defined by the equation

Definition 2

The Nikol’skii class

\displaystyle W^{\overline{r}}_{\overline{p},\overline{\alpha}}(\mathbb{T}^{d})\subset C(\overline{r})H^{\overline{r}}_{\overline{p}}(\mathbb{T}^{d}),

.

Lemma 1
.

Let

,

Proof.

We generalize the arguments in

45
]
. For brevity, we write
\hat{x}=\hat{x}(\overline{s})
. First we prove that there is

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{c}\|g(\hat{x})-x\|^{q_{d}}_{l_{q_{1},\dots,q_{d}}^{k_{1},\dots,k_{d}}}\geqslant\frac{s_{1}^{q_{d}/q_{1}}\dots s_{d-1}^{q_{d}/q_{d-1}}s_{d}}{2}-\\
-q_{d}s_{1}^{\frac{q_{d}}{q_{1}}-1}\dots s_{d-1}^{\frac{q_{d}}{q_{d-1}}-1}\sum\limits_{1\leqslant i_{1}\leqslant k_{1},\dots,1\leqslant i_{d}\leqslant k_{d}}g(\hat{x})_{i_{1},\dots,i_{d}}x_{i_{1},\dots,i_{d}}+c(\overline{q})\|x\|^{q_{d}}_{l_{q_{1},\dots,q_{d}}^{k_{1},\dots,k_{d}}},\end{array}

and the function

is continuous.

Indeed, let
\varphi(x)=\|g(\hat{x})-x\|^{q_{d}}_{l_{q_{1},\dots,q_{d}}^{k_{1},\dots,k_{d}}}
. Then the function
\varphi
is convex,
\varphi(0)=s_{1}^{q_{d}/q_{1}}\dots s_{d-1}^{q_{d}/q_{d-1}}s_{d}

. Hence

-q_{d}s_{1}^{\frac{q_{d}}{q_{1}}-1}\dots s_{d-1}^{\frac{q_{d}}{q_{d-1}}-1}\sum\limits_{1\leqslant i_{1}\leqslant k_{1},\dots,1\leqslant i_{d}\leqslant k_{d}}g(\hat{x})_{i_{1},\dots,i_{d}}x_{i_{1},\dots,i_{d}}.

. Then for all
x
such that

we have

\|g(\hat{x})-x\|^{q_{d}}_{l_{q_{1},\dots,q_{d}}^{k_{1},\dots,k_{d}}}\geqslant\frac{s_{1}^{q_{d}/q_{1}}\dots s_{d-1}^{q_{d}/q_{d-1}}s_{d}}{2}-

-q_{d}s_{1}^{\frac{q_{d}}{q_{1}}-1}\dots s_{d-1}^{\frac{q_{d}}{q_{d-1}}-1}\sum\limits_{1\leqslant i_{1}\leqslant k_{1},\dots,1\leqslant i_{d}\leqslant k_{d}}g(\hat{x})_{i_{1},\dots,i_{d}}x_{i_{1},\dots,i_{d}}+c_{0}(\overline{q},\,t)\|x\|^{q_{d}}_{l_{q_{1},\dots,q_{d}}^{k_{1},\dots,k_{d}}}.

continuously depending on
