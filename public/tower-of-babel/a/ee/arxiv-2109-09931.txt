Saturation Problems in Convex Geometric Hypergraphs
Jason O’Neill, Sam Spiro

Abstract
A convex geometric hypergraph (abbreviated cgh) consists of a collection of subsets of a strictly convex set of points in the plane. Extremal problems for cgh’s have been extensively studied in the literature, and in this paper we consider their corresponding saturation problems. We asymptotically determine the saturation number of two geometrically disjoint r r -tuples. Further, amongst the eight nonisomorphic 3 3 -uniform cgh’s on two edges, we determine the saturation number for seven of these up to order of magnitude and the eighth up to a log factor.

Our main tool for this section is the following characterization of

-saturated cgh’s.

Theorem 3.2
.

Let

and

-cgh

is

-saturated if and only if

for some

with

.

The backwards direction of this statement is relatively easy to prove.

.

Let

-saturated.

Let

-free.

. Let

be any

-set containing

-saturated.

Conjecture 2

H
