Abstract
In general relativity, the contracted Bianchi identity makes the field equation compatible with the energy conservation, likewise in f ⁡ ( R ) f(R) theories of gravity. We show that this classical phenomenon is not guaranteed in the symmetric teleparallel theory, and rather generally f ⁡ ( Q ) f(Q) model specific. We further prove that the energy conservation criterion is equivalent to the affine connection’s field equation of f ⁡ ( Q ) f(Q) theory, and except the f ⁡ ( Q ) = α ​ Q + β f(Q)=\alpha Q+\beta model, the non-linear f ⁡ ( Q ) f(Q) models do not satisfy the energy conservation or, equivalently the second field equation in every spacetime geometry; unless Q Q itself is a constant. So the problem is deep-rooted in the theory, several physically motivated examples are provided in the support.
