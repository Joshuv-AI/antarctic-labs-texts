Teleportation of squeezed states in the absence and presence of dissipation
M Ghasemi

Abstract
In this paper at first we successfully teleport the unknown quantum state which is a superposition of squeezed vacuum state and squeezed one-photon state using the beam splitter in the absence of dissipation. In the continuation, we try to implement the same teleportation protocol, however, in the presence of dissipation effects. To do this task, we use proper entangled channel to reach to perfect teleportation under the influence of decoherence. Finally, we consider another superposition of two squeezed vacuum states with separation in phase by π \pi and teleport it with a different appropriate entangled channel. In fact, we will observe that, one can successfully teleport the considered superposition of squeezed states by choosing proper entangled channels in the presence and absence of dissipation in appropriate chosen conditions.

Teleportation of a superposition of squeezed states

The scheme of quantum teleportation from Alice to Bob by using entangled channel via a beam splitter.

In this section, we want to teleport the unknown quantum state

\displaystyle\ket{\psi}_{a}=\epsilon_{+}\ket{\xi}_{a}+\epsilon_{-}\ket{\xi^{{}^{\prime}}}_{a},

(1)

where
\epsilon_{\pm}
are the undetermined coefficients that satisfy
|{\epsilon_{+}}|^{2}+|{\epsilon_{-}}|^{2}=1
. The state (
1
) includes all Fock states (odd and even basis), since
\ket{\xi}
and
\ket{\xi^{{}^{\prime}}}
are the squeezed vacuum and one-photon states, respectively
[
44
]
,
i.e.
,

\displaystyle\ket{\xi}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle S(\xi)\ket{0}=\Sigma^{\infty}_{n=0}{C_{2n}\ket{2n}},

(2)

\displaystyle\ket{\xi^{{}^{\prime}}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle S(\xi)\ket{1}=\Sigma^{\infty}_{n=0}{D_{2n+1}\ket{2n+1}},

where
\xi=re^{i\varphi}
and
S(\xi)=\exp{[\frac{1}{2}(\xi^{\ast}\hat{a}^{2}-\xi\hat{a}^{\dagger 2})]}
is the squeezing operator by which the expansion coefficients in (
2
) result respectively in
[
45
]

\displaystyle C_{2n}=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{\cosh{r}}}\dfrac{\sqrt{(2n)!}}{2^{n}{n}!}e^{in\varphi}\tanh^{n}{r},

(3)

\displaystyle D_{2n+1}=\dfrac{1}{\cosh^{\frac{3}{2}}{r}}\dfrac{\sqrt{(2n+1)!}}{2^{n}{n}!}e^{in\varphi}\tanh^{n}{r}.

The teleportation protocol is as follows: consider a situation where a field mode b is stored in Alice’s lab, while mode c describes a light beam propagating to Bob (see figure
1
). Initially, Alice and Bob share the maximally entangled channel,

\displaystyle\ket{H_{\xi}}_{b,c}=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\left({\ket{-\xi}_{b}\ket{\xi^{{}^{\prime}}}_{c}+\ket{-\xi^{{}^{\prime}}}_{b}\ket{\xi}_{c}}\right),

(4)

which is made up of squeezed one-photon and vacuum states. So, the whole state of the system reads as:

\displaystyle\ket{\psi}_{a}\otimes\ket{H_{\xi}}_{b,c}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\left[\epsilon_{+}\ket{\xi}_{a}\ket{-\xi}_{b}\ket{\xi^{{}^{\prime}}}_{c}+\epsilon_{+}\ket{\xi}_{a}\ket{-\xi^{{}^{\prime}}}_{b}\ket{\xi}_{c}\right.

(5)

\displaystyle+

\displaystyle\left.\epsilon_{-}\ket{\xi^{{}^{\prime}}}_{a}\ket{-\xi}_{b}\ket{\xi^{{}^{\prime}}}_{c}+\epsilon_{-}\ket{\xi^{{}^{\prime}}}_{a}\ket{-\xi^{{}^{\prime}}}_{b}\ket{\xi}_{c}\right].

Now, to achieve the teleportation purpose, Alice mixes her part of entangled state (field mode b) with the state to be teleported (field mode a) using a beam splitter. Accordingly, the total state after the beam splitter can, in principle, be obtained via the relation,

\ket{\psi}_{\mathrm{out}}=u_{\mathrm{BS}}(\theta)\ket{\psi}_{a}\otimes\ket{H_{\xi}}_{b,c},

(6)

where
u_{\mathrm{BS}}(\theta)=\exp[\theta(\hat{a}\hat{b}^{\dagger}-\hat{a}^{\dagger}\hat{b})]
is the beam splitter operator and
\theta
is the parameter that is related to the reflectivity of beam splitter
[
3
,
46
]
. Therefore, the output state from the beam splitter can readily be obtained as follows:

\displaystyle\ket{\psi}_{\mathrm{out}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\left[\epsilon_{+}\ket{A}_{\mathrm{out}}\ket{\xi^{{}^{\prime}}}_{c}+\epsilon_{+}\ket{B}_{\mathrm{out}}\ket{\xi}_{c}\right.+\left.\epsilon_{-}\ket{C}_{\mathrm{out}}\ket{\xi^{{}^{\prime}}}_{c}+\epsilon_{-}\ket{D}_{\mathrm{out}}\ket{\xi}_{c}\right].

(7)

In Eq. (
7
) we have defined
1
1

1

Notice that
u_{\mathrm{BS}}(\theta)\ket{0,0}=\ket{0,0}
(see Ref.
[
3
]
).
:

\displaystyle\ket{A}_{\mathrm{out}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle u_{\mathrm{BS}}(\theta)\ket{\xi}_{a}\ket{-\xi}_{b}=u_{\mathrm{BS}}(\theta)S_{a}(\xi)S_{b}(-\xi)u_{\mathrm{BS}}^{\dagger}(\theta)\ket{0,0}_{a,b},

(8)

where for
\theta=\frac{\pi}{4}
the state (
8
) results in the well-known two-mode squeezed state as below
[
47
]
:

\displaystyle\ket{A}_{\mathrm{out}}\rightarrow\ket{\xi}_{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle S_{\mathrm{ab}}(\xi)\ket{0,0}_{a,b}=\exp(\xi^{\ast}\hat{a}\hat{b}-\xi\hat{a}^{\dagger}\hat{b}^{\dagger})\ket{0,0}_{a,b}

(9)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(\cosh{r})^{-1}\Sigma^{\infty}_{n=0}{(-1)^{n}e^{in\varphi}(\tanh{r})^{n}\ket{n,n}}.

Other constituents of the state ket in (
7
) for
\theta=\frac{\pi}{4}
are defined as:

\displaystyle\ket{B}_{\mathrm{out}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle u_{\mathrm{BS}}(\frac{\pi}{4})\ket{\xi}_{a}\ket{-\xi^{{}^{\prime}}}_{b}=u_{\mathrm{BS}}(\frac{\pi}{4})S_{a}(\xi)S_{b}(-\xi)\ket{0,1}_{a,b}

(10)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle u_{\mathrm{BS}}(\frac{\pi}{4})S_{a}(\xi)S_{b}(-\xi)u_{\mathrm{BS}}^{\dagger}(\frac{\pi}{4})u_{\mathrm{BS}}(\frac{\pi}{4})\hat{b}^{\dagger}u_{\mathrm{BS}}^{\dagger}(\frac{\pi}{4})\ket{0,0}_{a,b}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\dfrac{S_{ab}(\xi)}{\sqrt{2}}\left(\hat{b}^{\dagger}-\hat{a}^{\dagger}\right)\ket{0,0}_{a,b},

\displaystyle\ket{C}_{\mathrm{out}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle u_{\mathrm{BS}}(\frac{\pi}{4})\ket{\xi^{{}^{\prime}}}_{a}\ket{-\xi}_{b}=u_{\mathrm{BS}}(\frac{\pi}{4})S_{a}(\xi)S_{b}(-\xi)\ket{1,0}_{a,b}

(11)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle u_{\mathrm{BS}}(\frac{\pi}{4})S_{a}(\xi)S_{b}(-\xi)u_{\mathrm{BS}}^{\dagger}(\frac{\pi}{4})u_{\mathrm{BS}}(\frac{\pi}{4})a^{\dagger}u_{\mathrm{BS}}^{\dagger}(\frac{\pi}{4})\ket{0,0}_{a,b}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\dfrac{S_{ab}(\xi)}{\sqrt{2}}\left(\hat{a}^{\dagger}+\hat{b}^{\dagger}\right)\ket{0,0}_{a,b},

\displaystyle\ket{D}_{\mathrm{out}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle u_{\mathrm{BS}}(\frac{\pi}{4})\ket{\xi^{{}^{\prime}}}_{a}\ket{-\xi^{{}^{\prime}}}_{b}=u_{\mathrm{BS}}(\frac{\pi}{4})S_{a}(\xi)S_{b}(-\xi)\ket{1,1}_{a,b}

(12)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle u_{\mathrm{BS}}(\frac{\pi}{4})S_{a}(\xi)S_{b}(-\xi)u_{\mathrm{BS}}^{\dagger}(\frac{\pi}{4})u_{\mathrm{BS}}(\frac{\pi}{4})a^{\dagger}u_{\mathrm{BS}}^{\dagger}(\frac{\pi}{4})u_{\mathrm{BS}}(\frac{\pi}{4})b^{\dagger}u_{\mathrm{BS}}^{\dagger}(\frac{\pi}{4})\ket{0,0}_{a,b}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\dfrac{S_{ab}(\xi)}{2}\left(\hat{a}^{\dagger}+\hat{b}^{\dagger}\right)\left(\hat{b}^{\dagger}-\hat{a}^{\dagger}\right)\ket{0,0}_{a,b}.

Considering all above results, the output state in (
7

\displaystyle\ket{\psi}_{\mathrm{out}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\left[\epsilon_{+}\ket{\xi}_{2}\ket{\xi^{{}^{\prime}}}_{c}+\dfrac{\epsilon_{+}}{\sqrt{2}}S_{ab}(\xi)(\hat{b}^{\dagger}-\hat{a}^{\dagger})\ket{0,0}_{a,b}\ket{\xi}_{c}\right.

(13)

\displaystyle+

\displaystyle\left.\dfrac{\epsilon_{-}}{\sqrt{2}}S_{ab}(\xi)(\hat{b}^{\dagger}+\hat{a}^{\dagger})\ket{0,0}_{a,b}\ket{\xi^{{}^{\prime}}}_{c}+\dfrac{\epsilon_{-}}{2}S_{ab}(\xi)(\hat{b}^{\dagger 2}-\hat{a}^{\dagger 2})\ket{0,0}_{a,b}\ket{\xi}_{c}\right].

Subsequently, Alice performs two photon number measurements on the modes a and b on her side. We denote the probability to find
n
and
m
photons respectively in the modes a and b by
P(n,m)
,
i.e.
,

\displaystyle P(n,m)=\arrowvert_{a}\bra{n}_{b}\bra{m}\psi\rangle_{\mathrm{out}}\arrowvert^{2}.

(14)

In this measurement, if
n
and
m
differ by one (
m=n+1
), the state on Bob’s side (the field mode c) collapses into

\displaystyle\ket{\psi}_{\mathrm{Bob}}=\epsilon_{+}\ket{\xi}_{c}+\epsilon_{-}\ket{\xi^{{}^{\prime}}}_{c}.

(15)

Interestingly, as is clear, provided that the difference between
n
and
m
being one, our teleportation scheme works perfectly. Under the mentioned conditions, Eq. (
14

\displaystyle P(n,n+1)=\dfrac{(n+1)\tanh^{2n}r}{4\cosh^{4}r}.

(16)

The success probability to find the number of photons in either mode such that
m=n+1
reads as
P=\Sigma^{\infty}_{n=0}{P(n,n+1)}
. This summation can be calculated using (
16
), which arrives one at
P=0.25
. This value of success probability is acceptable in the typical schemes for teleportation (see Ref.
[
26
,
48
]
).

3.1
Case 1:

In this subsection we want to teleport the unknown state (
1
) by using the entangled coherent-squeezed channel,

\displaystyle\ket{H_{\xi,\alpha}}^{1}_{b,c}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\left(\ket{\alpha}_{b}\ket{\xi^{{}^{\prime}}}_{c}+\ket{-\alpha}_{b}\ket{\xi}_{c}\right),

are coherent states and
\ket{\xi}

42

\displaystyle\ket{H_{\xi,\alpha}}^{1}_{b,E,c}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\left(\ket{\alpha\sqrt{\eta}}_{b}\ket{\alpha\sqrt{1-\eta}}_{E}\ket{\xi^{{}^{\prime}}}_{c}\right.+\left.\ket{-\alpha\sqrt{\eta}}_{b}\ket{-\alpha\sqrt{1-\eta}}_{E}\ket{\xi}_{c}\right).

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\left(\epsilon_{+}\ket{\xi,\alpha\sqrt{\eta},\xi^{{}^{\prime}}}_{a,b,c}\ket{\alpha\sqrt{1-\eta}}_{E}\right.+\left.\epsilon_{+}\ket{\xi,-\alpha\sqrt{\eta},\xi}_{a,b,c}\ket{-\alpha\sqrt{1-\eta}}_{E}\right.

(19)

\displaystyle\left.\epsilon_{-}\ket{\xi^{{}^{\prime}},\alpha\sqrt{\eta},\xi^{{}^{\prime}}}_{a,b,c}\ket{\alpha\sqrt{1-\eta}}_{E}+\epsilon_{-}\ket{\xi^{{}^{\prime}},-\alpha\sqrt{\eta},\xi}_{a,b,c}\ket{-\alpha\sqrt{1-\eta}}_{E}\right).

By operating a proper measurement of the quasi-Bell state,
i.e.
,
|qB\rangle_{ab}\langle{qB}|

\displaystyle\ket{qB}_{a,b}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\left(\ket{\xi,-\alpha\sqrt{\eta}}+\ket{\xi^{{}^{\prime}},\alpha\sqrt{\eta}}\right),

\displaystyle\ket{\psi}^{1}_{\mathrm{Bob}}=\epsilon_{+}\ket{\xi}_{c}\ket{-\alpha\sqrt{1-\eta}}_{E}+\epsilon_{-}\ket{\xi^{{}^{\prime}}}_{c}\ket{\alpha\sqrt{1-\eta}}_{E}.

4
Summary and conclusions

In this paper we investigated teleportation of two different superpositions of squeezed states in the absence and presence of dissipation. According to our analytical results, in the absence of photonic loss, the unknown state which includes a superposition of squeezed vacuum and squeezed one-photon states was teleported with fidelity (success probability) as
1
(
0.25
). Moreover, under the influence of dissipation, the same unknown state was teleported near perfectly. In addition, we considered the teleportation of a superposition of two squeezed vacuum states separated in phase by
180^{\circ}
. This superposition under proper conditions leads to perfect teleportation (recently, approximate and near to perfect teleportation are reported
[
27
,
48
]
). Also, we found that in the presence of decoherence
(\eta\neq 1)
, the minimum of fidelity is decreased by decreasing
\eta
. Summing up, we could successfully teleport the considered superpositions of squeezed states by properly chosen squeezed entangled state and coherent-squeezed entangled channels with utilizing a beam splitter and applying proper measurements while some suitable conditions are fixed.
