Abstract
. In this paper, we introduce the notion of a relative Rota-Baxter operator of weight λ \lambda on a Lie triple system with respect to an action on another Lie triple system, which can be characterized by the graph of their semidirect product. We also establish a cohomology theory for a relative Rota-Baxter operator of weight λ \lambda on Lie triple systems and use the first cohomology group to classify infinitesimal deformations.

In

2.
Relative Rota-Baxter operators of weight
\lambda
on Lie triple systems

In this section, we first introduce the notion of actions of Lie triple systems, which give rise to the semidirect product Lie triple systems. Then we introduce the notion of relative Rota-Baxter operators of weight
\lambda
on Lie triple system, which can be characterized by the graphs of the semidirect product Lie triple systems. Finally, we establish the relation between relative Rota-Baxter operators of weight
\lambda
on Lie triple systems and Nijenhuis operators on Lie triple systems.

Definition 2.1
.

[
14
]

A Lie triple system is a vector space
\mathfrak{L}
endowed with a trilinear operation
[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{\mathfrak{L}}:\mathfrak{L}\times\mathfrak{L}\times\mathfrak{L}\longrightarrow\mathfrak{L}
satisfying

(2.1)

\displaystyle[a,a,b]_{\mathfrak{L}}=0,

(2.2)

\displaystyle[a,b,c]_{\mathfrak{L}}+[b,c,a]_{\mathfrak{L}}+[c,a,b]_{\mathfrak{L}}=0,

(2.3)

\displaystyle[a,b,[c,d,e]_{\mathfrak{L}}]_{\mathfrak{L}}=[[a,b,c]_{\mathfrak{L}},d,e]_{\mathfrak{L}}+[c,[a,b,d]_{\mathfrak{L}},e]_{\mathfrak{L}}+[c,d,[a,b,e]_{\mathfrak{L}}]_{\mathfrak{L}},

for all
a,b,c,d,e\in\mathfrak{L}
.

Definition 2.2
.

[
26
]

An
\mathfrak{L}
-module is a vector space
V
with a bilinear map

\displaystyle\theta:\mathfrak{L}\times\mathfrak{L}

\displaystyle\longrightarrow{\rm End}(V)

\displaystyle(a,b)

\displaystyle\longrightarrow\theta(a,b),

such that the following conditions hold:

\displaystyle\theta(c,d)\theta(a,b)-\theta(b,d)\theta(a,c)-\theta(a,[b,c,d]_{\mathfrak{L}})+D(b,c)\theta(a,d)=0,

\displaystyle\theta(c,d)D(a,b)-D(a,b)\theta(c,d)+\theta([a,b,c]_{\mathfrak{L}},d)+\theta(c,[a,b,d]_{\mathfrak{L}})=0,

where

\displaystyle D(a,b)=\theta(b,a)-\theta(a,b),

for all
a,b,c,d\in\mathfrak{L}.
Also
\theta
is called a representation of
\mathfrak{L}
on
V.

In particular, if

\displaystyle\theta:\mathfrak{L}\times\mathfrak{L}

\displaystyle\longrightarrow{\rm End}(\mathfrak{L})

\displaystyle(a,b)

\displaystyle\longrightarrow\theta(a,b),

with
\theta(a,b)c=[c,a,b]_{\mathfrak{L}},
and
D(a,b)c=\theta(b,a)c-\theta(a,b)c=[a,b,c]_{\mathfrak{L}}.
Then
\theta
is called an adjoint representation of
\mathfrak{L}
on itself.

Now, let us give the definition of a relative Rota-Baxter operator of weight
\lambda\in\mathbb{F}
from a Lie triple system to another Lie triple system. We recall a derived algebra of a Lie triple system
(\mathfrak{L},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{\mathfrak{L}})
is a subsystem
[\mathfrak{L},\mathfrak{L},\mathfrak{L}]_{\mathfrak{L}}
, and denoted by
\mathfrak{L}^{1}
. The subspace

\displaystyle C(\mathfrak{L})=\{x\in\mathfrak{L}~|~[x,y,z]_{\mathfrak{L}}=0,~\forall~y,z\in\mathfrak{L}\}

is the center of
(\mathfrak{L},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{\mathfrak{L}})
.

Definition 2.3
.

Let
(\mathfrak{L},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{\mathfrak{L}})
and
(\mathfrak{L}^{\prime},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}})
be two Lie triple systems. Let
\theta:\mathfrak{L}\times\mathfrak{L}\rightarrow{\rm End}(\mathfrak{L}^{\prime})
be a representation of the Lie triple system
\mathfrak{L}
on the vector space
\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}
. If for all
x,y\in\mathfrak{L}
,
u,v,w\in\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}
,

\displaystyle\theta(x,y)u\in C(\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}),

\displaystyle\theta(x,y)[u,v,w]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}=0,

then
\theta
is called an action of
(\mathfrak{L},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{\mathfrak{L}})
on
(\mathfrak{L}^{\prime},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}})
. We denote an action by
(\mathfrak{L}^{\prime},\theta)
.

Example 2.4
.

Let
(\mathfrak{L},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{\mathfrak{L}})
be a Lie triple system. If
\mathfrak{L}
satisfies
\mathfrak{L}^{1}\in C(\mathfrak{L})
, then the adjoint representation
\theta:\mathfrak{L}\times\mathfrak{L}\rightarrow{\rm End}(\mathfrak{L})
is an action of
\mathfrak{L}
on itself.

Definition 2.5
.

Let
\theta:\mathfrak{L}\times\mathfrak{L}\rightarrow{\rm End}(\mathfrak{L}^{\prime})
be an action of a Lie triple system
(\mathfrak{L},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{\mathfrak{L}})
on a Lie triple system
(\mathfrak{L}^{\prime},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}})
. A linear map
T:\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}\rightarrow\mathfrak{L}
is called a relative Rota-Baxter operator of weight
\lambda\in\mathbb{F}
from a Lie triple system
(\mathfrak{L}^{\prime},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}})
to a Lie triple system
(\mathfrak{L},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{\mathfrak{L}})
with respect to an action
\theta
if

(2.4)

\displaystyle[Tu,Tv,Tw]_{\mathfrak{L}}=T\Big(D(Tu,Tv)w-\theta(Tu,Tw)v+\theta(Tv,Tw)u+\lambda[u,v,w]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}\Big),

for all
u,v,w\in\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}
.

Definition 2.6
.

Let
T
and
T^{\prime}
be two relative Rota-Baxter operators of weight
\lambda
from a Lie triple system
(\mathfrak{L}^{\prime},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}})
to a Lie triple system
(\mathfrak{L},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{\mathfrak{L}})
with respect to an action
\theta
. A homomorphism from
T
to
T^{\prime}
consists of homomorphisms
\psi_{\mathfrak{L}}:\mathfrak{L}\rightarrow\mathfrak{L}
and
\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}:\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}\rightarrow\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}
such that

(2.5)

\displaystyle\psi_{\mathfrak{L}}\circ T=

\displaystyle T^{\prime}\circ\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}},

(2.6)

\displaystyle\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}(\theta(x,y)u)=

\displaystyle\theta(\psi_{\mathfrak{L}}(x),\psi_{\mathfrak{L}}(y))\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}(u),~~\forall x,y\in\mathfrak{L},~u\in\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}

(2.7)

\displaystyle\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}(D(x,y)u)=

\displaystyle D(\psi_{\mathfrak{L}}(x),\psi_{\mathfrak{L}}(y))\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}(u),~~\forall x,y\in\mathfrak{L},~u\in\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}.

In particular, if both
\psi_{\mathfrak{L}}
and
\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}
are invertible,
(\psi_{\mathfrak{L}},\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}})
is called an isomorphism from
T
to
T^{\prime}
.

Let
\theta:\mathfrak{L}\times\mathfrak{L}\rightarrow{\rm End}(\mathfrak{L}^{\prime})
be an action of a Lie triple system
(\mathfrak{L},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{\mathfrak{L}})
on a Lie triple system
(\mathfrak{L}^{\prime},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}})
. Define
[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{\theta}
on
\mathfrak{L}\oplus\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}
by

\displaystyle[x+u,y+v,z+w]_{\theta}=[x,y,z]_{\mathfrak{L}}+D(x,y)w+\theta(y,z)u-\theta(x,z)v+\lambda[u,v,w]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}},

for all
x,y,z\in\mathfrak{L}
,
u,v,w\in\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}
.

Proposition 2.7
.

Keep notations as above, then
(\mathfrak{L}\oplus\mathfrak{L}^{\prime},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{\theta})
is a Lie triple system, which is called the semidirect product of the Lie triple system
(\mathfrak{L},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{\mathfrak{L}})
on the Lie triple system
(\mathfrak{L}^{\prime},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}})
with respect to the action
\theta
, and denoted by
\mathfrak{L}\ltimes_{\theta}\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}.

Proof.

To prove
(\mathfrak{L}\oplus\mathfrak{L}^{\prime},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{\theta})
is a Lie triple system, we only need to show Eqs. (
2.1
)-(
2.3
) hold. First, we show that
(\mathfrak{L}\oplus\mathfrak{L}^{\prime},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{\theta})
satisfies Eq. (
2.1
), for all
x,y,z\in\mathfrak{L}
,
u,v,w\in\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}
,

\displaystyle[x+u,x+u,z+w]_{\theta}=[x,x,z]_{\mathfrak{L}}+D(x,x)w+\theta(x,z)u-\theta(x,z)u+\lambda[u,u,w]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}=0.

Then, for Eq. (
2.2
), we have

\displaystyle[x+u,y+v,z+w]_{\theta}+[y+v,z+w,x+u]_{\theta}+[z+w,x+u,y+v]_{\theta}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle[x,y,z]_{\mathfrak{L}}+D(x,y)w+\theta(y,z)u-\theta(x,z)v+\lambda[u,v,w]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}

\displaystyle+[y,z,x]_{\mathfrak{L}}+D(y,z)u+\theta(z,x)v-\theta(y,x)w+\lambda[v,w,u]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}

\displaystyle+[z,x,y]_{\mathfrak{L}}+D(z,x)v+\theta(x,y)w-\theta(z,y)u+\lambda[w,u,v]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 0.

At last, we show that Eq. (
2.3
) holds

\displaystyle[a+p,b+q,[x+u,y+v,z+w]_{\theta}]_{\theta}-[[a+p,b+q,x+u]_{\theta},y+v,z+w]_{\theta}

\displaystyle-[x+u,[a+p,b+q,y+v]_{\theta},z+w]_{\theta}-[x+u,y+v,[a+p,b+q,z+w]_{\theta}]_{\theta}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle[a+p,b+q,[x,y,z]_{\mathfrak{L}}+D(x,y)w+\theta(y,z)u-\theta(x,z)v+\lambda[u,v,w]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}]_{\theta}

\displaystyle-[[a,b,x]_{\mathfrak{L}}+D(a,b)u+\theta(b,x)p-\theta(a,x)q+\lambda[p,q,u]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}},y+v,z+w]_{\theta}

\displaystyle-[x+u,[a,b,y]_{\mathfrak{L}}+D(a,b)v+\theta(b,y)p-\theta(a,y)q+\lambda[p,q,v]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}},z+w]_{\theta}

\displaystyle-[x+u,y+v,[a,b,z]_{\mathfrak{L}}+D(a,b)w+\theta(b,z)p-\theta(a,z)q+\lambda[p,q,w]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}]_{\theta}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle[a,b,[x,y,z]_{\mathfrak{L}}]_{\mathfrak{L}}+D(a,b)D(x,y)w+D(a,b)\theta(y,z)u-D(a,b)\theta(x,z)v

\displaystyle+\lambda D(a,b)[u,v,w]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}+\theta(b,[x,y,z]_{\mathfrak{L}})p-\theta(a,[x,y,z]_{\mathfrak{L}})q+\lambda[p,q,D(x,y)w]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}

\displaystyle+\lambda[p,q,\theta(y,z)u]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}-\lambda[p,q,\theta(x,z)v]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}+\lambda[p,q,\lambda[u,v,w]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}

\displaystyle-[[a,b,x]_{\mathfrak{L}},y,z]_{\mathfrak{L}}-D([a,b,x]_{\mathfrak{L}},y)w-\theta(y,z)D(a,b)u-\theta(y,z)\theta(b,x)p

\displaystyle+\theta(y,z)\theta(a,x)q-\lambda\theta(y,z)[p,q,u]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}+\theta([a,b,x]_{\mathfrak{L}},z)v-\lambda[D(a,b)u,v,w]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}

\displaystyle-\lambda[\theta(b,x)p,v,w]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}+\lambda[\theta(a,x)q,v,w]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}-\lambda[\lambda[p,q,u]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}},v,w]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}

\displaystyle-[x,[a,b,y]_{\mathfrak{L}},z]_{\mathfrak{L}}-D(x,[a,b,y]_{\mathfrak{L}})w-\theta([a,b,y]_{\mathfrak{L}},z)u+\theta(x,z)D(a,b)v

\displaystyle+\theta(x,z)\theta(b,y)p-\theta(x,z)\theta(a,y)q+\lambda\theta(x,z)[p,q,v]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}-\lambda[u,\theta(b,y)p,w]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}

\displaystyle-\lambda[u,D(a,b)v,w]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}+\lambda[u,\theta(a,y)q,w]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}+\lambda[u,\lambda[p,q,v]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}},w]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}

\displaystyle-[x,y,[a,b,z]_{\mathfrak{L}}]_{\mathfrak{L}}-D(x,y)\theta(b,z)p-D(x,y)D(a,b)w+D(x,y)\theta(a,z)q

\displaystyle-\theta(y,[a,b,z]_{\mathfrak{L}})u+\theta(x,[a,b,z]_{\mathfrak{L}})v-\lambda D(x,y)[p,q,w]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}-\lambda[u,v,\theta(b,z)p]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}

\displaystyle-\lambda[u,v,D(a,b)w]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}+\lambda[u,v,\theta(a,z)q]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}-\lambda[u,v,\lambda[p,q,w]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 0,

which implies that
(\mathfrak{L}\oplus\mathfrak{L}^{\prime},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{\theta})
is a Lie triple system.
∎

Theorem 2.8
.

Let
\theta:\mathfrak{L}\times\mathfrak{L}\rightarrow{\rm End}(\mathfrak{L}^{\prime})
be an action of a Lie triple system
(\mathfrak{L},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{\mathfrak{L}})
on a Lie triple system
(\mathfrak{L}^{\prime},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}})
. Then a linear map
T:\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}\rightarrow\mathfrak{L}
is a relative Rota-Baxter operator of weight
\lambda
if and only if the graph

\displaystyle Gr(T)=\{Tu+u~|~u\in\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}\}

is a subsystem of the Lie triple system
\mathfrak{L}\ltimes_{\theta}\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}
.

Proof.

Let
T:\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}\rightarrow\mathfrak{L}
be a linear map. For all
u,v,w\in\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}
, we have

\displaystyle[Tu+u,Tv+v,Tw+w]_{\theta}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle[Tu,Tv,Tw]_{\mathfrak{L}}+D(Tu,Tv)w+\theta(Tv,Tw)u-\theta(Tu,Tw)v+\lambda[u,v,w]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}},

which implies that the graph
Gr(T)=\{Tu+u~|~u\in\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}\}
is a subsystem of the Lie triple system
\mathfrak{L}\ltimes_{\theta}\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}
if and only if
T
satisfies

\displaystyle[Tu,Tv,Tw]_{\mathfrak{L}}=T\Big(D(Tu,Tv)w+\theta(Tv,Tw)u-\theta(Tu,Tw)v+\lambda[u,v,w]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}\Big),

which means that
T
is a relative Rota-Baxter operator of weight
\lambda
.
∎

Since the graph
Gr(T)
is isomorphic to
\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}
as vector spaces, then there is an induced Lie triple system structure on
\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}
.

Corollary 2.9
.

Let
T:\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}\rightarrow\mathfrak{L}
be a relative Rota-Baxter operator of weight
\lambda
from a Lie triple system
(\mathfrak{L}^{\prime},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}})
to a Lie triple system
(\mathfrak{L},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{\mathfrak{L}})
with respect to an action
\theta
. Then
(\mathfrak{L}^{\prime},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{T})
is a Lie triple system, called the descendent Lie triple system of
T
, where

\displaystyle[u,v,w]_{T}=D(Tu,Tv)w+\theta(Tv,Tw)u-\theta(Tu,Tw)v+\lambda[u,v,w]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}},~~\forall~u,v,w\in\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}.

Moreover,
T
is a homomorphism from
(\mathfrak{L}^{\prime},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{T})
to
(\mathfrak{L},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{\mathfrak{L}})
.

In the sequel, we give the relationship between relative Rota-Baxter operators of weight
\lambda
and Nijenhuis operators. Recall from
[
6
]
that a Nijenhuis operator on a Lie triple system
(\mathfrak{L},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{\mathfrak{L}})
is a linear map
N:\mathfrak{L}\rightarrow\mathfrak{L}
satisfying

\displaystyle[Nx,Ny,Nz]_{\mathfrak{L}}=

\displaystyle N[Nx,Ny,z]_{\mathfrak{L}}+N[x,Ny,Nz]_{\mathfrak{L}}+N[Nx,y,Nz]_{\mathfrak{L}}-N^{2}[Nx,y,z]_{\mathfrak{L}}

\displaystyle-N^{2}[x,Ny,z]_{\mathfrak{L}}-N^{2}[x,y,Nz]_{\mathfrak{L}}+N^{3}[x,y,z]_{\mathfrak{L}},

for all
x,y,z\in\mathfrak{L}
.

Proposition 2.10
.

Let
\theta:\mathfrak{L}\times\mathfrak{L}\rightarrow{\rm End}(\mathfrak{L}^{\prime})
be an action of a Lie triple system
(\mathfrak{L},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{\mathfrak{L}})
on a Lie triple system
(\mathfrak{L}^{\prime},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}})
. Then a linear map
T:\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}\rightarrow\mathfrak{L}
is a relative Rota-Baxter operator of weight
\lambda
if and only if

\displaystyle\widetilde{T}=\left(\begin{matrix}{\rm id}&T\\
0&0\end{matrix}\right):\mathfrak{L}\oplus\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}\rightarrow\mathfrak{L}\oplus\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}

is a Nijenhuis operator acting on the Lie triple system
\mathfrak{L}\ltimes_{\theta}\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}
.

Proof.

For all
x,y,z\in\mathfrak{L}
,
u,v,w\in\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}
, on the one hand, we have

\displaystyle[\widetilde{T}(x+u),\widetilde{T}(y+v),\widetilde{T}(z+w)]_{\theta}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle[x+Tu,y+Tv,z+Tw]_{\theta}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle[x,y,z]_{\mathfrak{L}}+[Tu,y,z]_{\mathfrak{L}}+[x,Tv,z]_{\mathfrak{L}}+[x,y,Tw]_{\mathfrak{L}}+[x,Tv,Tw]_{\mathfrak{L}}+[Tu,y,Tw]_{\mathfrak{L}}

\displaystyle+[Tu,Tv,z]_{\mathfrak{L}}+[Tu,Tv,Tw]_{\mathfrak{L}}.

On the other hand, since
\widetilde{T}^{2}=\widetilde{T}
, we have

\displaystyle\widetilde{T}\Big([\widetilde{T}(x+u),\widetilde{T}(y+v),z+w]_{\theta}+[\widetilde{T}(x+u),y+v,\widetilde{T}(z+w)]_{\theta}+[x+u,\widetilde{T}(y+v),\widetilde{T}(z+w)]_{\theta}\Big)

\displaystyle-\widetilde{T}^{2}\Big([\widetilde{T}(x+u),y+v,z+w]_{\theta}+[x+u,y+v,\widetilde{T}(z+w)]_{\theta}+[x+u,\widetilde{T}(y+v),z+w]_{\theta}\Big)

\displaystyle+\widetilde{T}^{3}\Big([x+u,y+v,z+w]_{\theta}\Big)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle[x,y,z]_{\mathfrak{L}}+[Tu,y,z]_{\mathfrak{L}}+[x,Tv,z]_{\mathfrak{L}}+[x,y,Tw]_{\mathfrak{L}}+[x,Tv,Tw]_{\mathfrak{L}}+[Tu,y,Tw]_{\mathfrak{L}}

\displaystyle+[Tu,Tv,z]_{\mathfrak{L}}+T\Big(D(Tu,Tv)w+\theta(Tv,Tw)u-\theta(Tu,Tw)v+\lambda[u,v,w]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}\Big),

which implies that
\widetilde{T}
is a Nijenhuis operator on the Lie triple system
\mathfrak{L}\ltimes_{\theta}\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}
if and only if Eq. (
2.4
) holds.
∎

We will end this section with some examples of relative Rota-Baxter operators on Lie triple systems.

Proposition 2.11
.

Let
(\mathfrak{L},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{\mathfrak{L}})
be a Lie triple system such that the adjoint representation
\theta:\mathfrak{L}\times\mathfrak{L}\rightarrow{\rm End}(\mathfrak{L})
is an action of the Lie triple system
(\mathfrak{L},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{\mathfrak{L}})
on itself. Let
\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}
be an abelian subsystem of Lie triple system
\mathfrak{L}
and satisfy
\mathfrak{L}^{1}\cap\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}=0
. Let
\mathfrak{h}
be a compliment of
\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}
such that
\mathfrak{L}=\mathfrak{h}\oplus\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}
as vector spaces. Then the projection
P:\mathfrak{L}\rightarrow\mathfrak{L}
onto the subspace
\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}
, i.e.,
P(k+u)=u
, for all
k\in\mathfrak{h}
,
u\in\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}
, is a relative Rota-Baxter operator of weight
\lambda
from
\mathfrak{L}
to
\mathfrak{L}
with respect to the adjoint action
\theta
.

Proof.

For all
x,y,z\in\mathfrak{L}
, denote by
x^{\prime},y^{\prime},z^{\prime}
their images under the projection
P
. Since
\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}
is abelian and
\mathfrak{L}^{1}\cap\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}=0
, we have

In this section, we define a cohomology of a relative Rota-Baxter operator

Let
(V,\theta)
be a representation of a Lie triple system
(\mathfrak{L},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{\mathfrak{L}})

\displaystyle C^{2n+1}(\mathfrak{L},V):={\rm Hom}(\underbrace{\mathfrak{L}\times...\times\mathfrak{L}}_{(2n+1)},V),~~(n\geq 0),

(2n+1)
-cochains. For any

satisfies

\displaystyle f(x_{1},x_{2},...,x_{2n-2},x,x,y)=0,

\displaystyle f(x_{1},x_{2},...,x_{2n-2},x,y,z)+f(x_{1},x_{2},...,x_{2n-2},y,z,x)+f(x_{1},x_{2},...,x_{2n-2},z,x,y)=0,

for all
x,y,z,x_{i}\in\mathfrak{L},

The coboundary operator
\delta:C^{2n-1}(\mathfrak{L},V)\rightarrow C^{2n+1}(\mathfrak{L},V)
is given by

\displaystyle\begin{aligned} &(\delta f)(x_{1},x_{2},...,x_{2n+1})\\

for any
x_{1},x_{2},...,x_{2n+1}\in\mathfrak{L},

f\in C^{2n-1}(\mathfrak{L},V).

It was proved in
[
26
]
that
\delta\circ\delta=0
. Thus,
(\underset{n=0}{\overset{+\infty}{\oplus}}C^{2n+1}(\mathfrak{L},V),\delta)
is a cochain complex.

Definition 3.1
.

[
26
]

The cohomology of the Lie triple system
\mathfrak{L}
with coefficients in

is the cohomology of the cochain complex

be a relative Rota-Baxter operator of weight
\lambda
from a Lie triple system
(\mathfrak{L}^{\prime},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}})
to a Lie triple system
(\mathfrak{L},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{\mathfrak{L}})
with respect to an action
\theta
. Define the space of
(2n-1)
-cochains by

(3.3)

C^{2n-1}_{T}(\mathfrak{L}^{\prime},\mathfrak{L})=\left\{\begin{array}[]{lr}C^{2n-1}(\mathfrak{L}^{\prime},\mathfrak{L}),&n\geq 1,\\
\mathfrak{L}\wedge\mathfrak{L},&n=0.\end{array}\right.

Define
\partial:C^{2n-1}_{T}(\mathfrak{L}^{\prime},\mathfrak{L})\rightarrow C^{2n+1}_{T}(\mathfrak{L}^{\prime},\mathfrak{L})
by

(3.4)

\partial=\left\{\begin{array}[]{lr}\partial_{T},&n\geq 1,\\

Theorem 3.4
.

(\underset{n=0}{\overset{+\infty}{\oplus}}C^{2n-1}_{T}(\mathfrak{L}^{\prime},\mathfrak{L}),\partial)
is a cochain complex.

Proof.

It follows from Proposition
3.3
and the fact that
\delta_{T}
is the corresponding coboundary operator of the descendent Lie triple system
(\mathfrak{L}^{\prime},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{T})
with coefficient in the representation
(\mathfrak{L},\theta_{T})
directly.
∎

Definition 3.5
.

The coboundary of the cochain complex

is taken to be the cohomology for the relative Rota-Baxter operator

of weight

. Denote the set of
(2n-1)
-cocycles by

, the set of
(2n-1)
-coboundaries by
B_{T}^{2n-1}(\mathfrak{L}^{\prime},\mathfrak{L})
and
(2n-1)
-th cohomology group by

(3.5)

\displaystyle H_{T}^{2n-1}(\mathfrak{L}^{\prime},\mathfrak{L})=Z_{T}^{2n-1}(\mathfrak{L}^{\prime},\mathfrak{L})/B_{T}^{2n-1}(\mathfrak{L}^{\prime},\mathfrak{L}).

3.2.
Infinitesimal deformations of relative Rota-Baxter operators

In this subsection, we use the cohomology theory to characterize infinitesimal deformations of relative Rota-Baxter operators of weight
\lambda
on Lie triple systems.

Let
(\mathfrak{L},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{\mathfrak{L}})
be a Lie triple system over
\mathbb{F}
and
\mathbb{F}[t]
be the polynomial ring in one variable
t
. Then
\mathbb{F}[t]/(t^{2})\otimes_{\mathbb{F}}\mathfrak{L}
is an
\mathbb{F}[t]/(t^{2})
-module. Here we denote
f(t)\otimes_{\mathbb{F}}x\in\mathbb{F}[t]/(t^{2})\otimes_{\mathbb{F}}\mathfrak{L}
by
f(t)x
, where
f(t)\in\mathbb{F}[t]/(t^{2})
and all the vector spaces are finite dimensional vector spaces over
\mathbb{F}
. Moreover,
\mathbb{F}[t]/(t^{2})\otimes_{\mathbb{F}}\mathfrak{L}
is a Lie triple system over
\mathbb{F}[t]/(t^{2})
, where the Lie triple system is defined by

\displaystyle[f_{1}(t)x_{1},f_{2}(t)x_{2},f_{3}(t)x_{3}]=f_{1}(t)f_{2}(t)f_{3}(t)[x_{1},x_{2},x_{3}]_{\mathfrak{L}},

for
f_{i}(t)\in\mathbb{F}[t]/(t^{2})
,
1\leq i\leq 3
,
x_{1},x_{2},x_{3}\in\mathfrak{L}
.

Definition 3.6
.

Let
T:\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}\rightarrow\mathfrak{L}
be a relative Rota-Baxter operator of weight
\lambda
from a Lie triple system
(\mathfrak{L}^{\prime},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}})
to a Lie triple system
(\mathfrak{L},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{\mathfrak{L}})
with respect to an action
\theta
. Let
\mathfrak{T}:\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}\rightarrow\mathfrak{L}
be a linear map. If
T_{t}=T+t\mathfrak{T}
is still a relative Rota-Baxter operator of weight
\lambda
for all
t
, we say that
\mathfrak{T}
generates an infinitesimal deformation of
T
.

Since
T_{t}=T+t\mathfrak{T}
is a relative Rota-Baxter operator of weight
\lambda
, by consider the coefficients of
t^{n}
, for any
u,v,w\in\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}
. We have

(3.6)

\displaystyle\begin{aligned} &[\mathfrak{T}u,Tv,Tw]_{\mathfrak{L}}+[Tu,\mathfrak{T}v,Tw]_{\mathfrak{L}}+[Tu,Tv,\mathfrak{T}w]_{\mathfrak{L}}\\
=&T\Big(\theta(Tv,\mathfrak{T}w)u-\theta(Tu,\mathfrak{T}w)v+D(\mathfrak{T}u,Tv)w\\
&+\theta(\mathfrak{T}v,Tw)u-\theta(\mathfrak{T}u,Tw)v+D(Tu,\mathfrak{T}v)w\Big)\\
&+\mathfrak{T}\Big(D(Tu,Tv)w+\theta(Tv,Tw)u-\theta(Tu,Tw)v+\lambda[u,v,w]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}\Big),\end{aligned}

\displaystyle\begin{aligned} &[\mathfrak{T}u,\mathfrak{T}v,Tw]_{\mathfrak{L}}+[Tu,\mathfrak{T}v,\mathfrak{T}w]_{\mathfrak{L}}+[\mathfrak{T}u,Tv,\mathfrak{T}w]_{\mathfrak{L}}\\
=&\mathfrak{T}\Big(\theta(\mathfrak{T}v,Tw)u-\theta(\mathfrak{T}u,Tw)v+D(Tu,\mathfrak{T}v)w+\theta(Tv,\mathfrak{T}w)u-\theta(Tu,\mathfrak{T}w)v+D(\mathfrak{T}u,Tv)w\Big)\\
&+T\Big(D(\mathfrak{T}u,\mathfrak{T}v)w+\theta(\mathfrak{T}v,\mathfrak{T}w)u-\theta(\mathfrak{T}u,\mathfrak{T}w)v\Big),\end{aligned}

\displaystyle[\mathfrak{T}u,\mathfrak{T}v,\mathfrak{T}w]_{\mathfrak{L}}=\mathfrak{T}\Big(D(\mathfrak{T}u,\mathfrak{T}v)w-\theta(\mathfrak{T}u,\mathfrak{T}w)v+\theta(\mathfrak{T}v,\mathfrak{T}w)u\Big).

Note that Eq. (
3.6
) means that
\mathfrak{T}
is a
1
-cocycle of the relative Rota-Baxter operator
T
. Hence
\mathfrak{T}
defines a cohomology class in
H^{1}_{T}(\mathfrak{L}^{\prime},\mathfrak{L})
.

Definition 3.7
.

Let
T
be a relative Rota-Baxter operator of weight
\lambda
from a Lie triple system
(\mathfrak{L}^{\prime},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}})
to a Lie triple system
(\mathfrak{L},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{\mathfrak{L}})
with respect to an action
\theta
. Two 1-parameter infinitesimal deformations
T_{t}^{1}=T+t\mathfrak{T}_{1}
and
T_{t}^{2}=T+t\mathfrak{T}_{2}
are said to be equivalent if there exist
\mathfrak{X}\in\mathfrak{L}\wedge\mathfrak{L}
such that
({\rm id}_{\mathfrak{L}}+t[\mathfrak{X},-],{\rm id}_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}+tD(\mathfrak{X}))
is a homomorphism from
T_{t}^{1}
to
T_{t}^{2}
. In particular, an infinitesimal deformation
T_{t}=T+t\mathfrak{T}
of a relative Rota-Baxter operator
T
is said to be trivial if there exists
\mathfrak{X}\in\mathfrak{L}\wedge\mathfrak{L}
such that
({\rm id}_{\mathfrak{L}}+t[\mathfrak{X},-],{\rm id}_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}+tD(\mathfrak{X}))
is a homomorphism from
T_{t}
to
T
.

Let
({\rm id}_{\mathfrak{L}}+t[\mathfrak{X},-],{\rm id}_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}+tD(\mathfrak{X}))
is a homomorphism from
T_{t}^{1}
to
T_{t}^{2}
. By Eq. (
2.5
) we get,

\displaystyle({\rm id}_{\mathfrak{L}}+t[\mathfrak{X},-])(T+t\mathfrak{T}_{1})(u)=(T+t\mathfrak{T}_{2})({\rm id}_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}+tD(\mathfrak{X}))(u),

which implies

\displaystyle\left\{\begin{array}[]{lr}\mathfrak{T}_{1}(u)-\mathfrak{T}_{2}(u)=TD(\mathfrak{X})(u)-[\mathfrak{X},Tu]_{\mathfrak{L}},&\\
~~[\mathfrak{X},\mathfrak{T}_{1}(u)]_{\mathfrak{L}}=\mathfrak{T}_{2}D(\mathfrak{X})(u).&\end{array}\right.

Now we are ready to give the main result in this section.

Theorem 3.8
.

Let
T
be a relative Rota-Baxter operator of weight
\lambda
from a Lie triple system
(\mathfrak{L}^{\prime},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}})
to a Lie triple system
(\mathfrak{L},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{\mathfrak{L}})
with respect to an action
\theta
. If two 1-parameter infinitesimal deformations
T_{t}^{1}=T+t\mathfrak{T}_{1}
and
T_{t}^{2}=T+t\mathfrak{T}_{2}
are equivalent, then
\mathfrak{T}_{1}
and
\mathfrak{T}_{2}
are in the same cohomology class in
H^{1}_{T}(\mathfrak{L}^{\prime},\mathfrak{L})
.

Proof.

It is easy to see from the first condition of Eq. (
3.2
) that

\displaystyle\mathfrak{T}_{1}(u)=\mathfrak{T}_{2}(u)+(\partial\mathfrak{X})(u),~~~\forall~u\in\mathfrak{L}^{\prime},

which implies that
\mathfrak{T}_{1}
and
\mathfrak{T}_{2}
are in the same cohomology class.
∎

3.3.
Functorial properties

In this subsection, we show that the cohomology theory for relative Rota-Baxter operators of weight
\lambda
on Lie triple systems enjoys certain functorial properties.

Proposition 3.9
.

Let
T
and
T^{\prime}
be relative Rota-Baxter operators of weight
\lambda
from a Lie triple system
(\mathfrak{L}^{\prime},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}})
to a Lie triple system
(\mathfrak{L},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{\mathfrak{L}})
with respect to an action
\theta
. Let
(\psi_{\mathfrak{L}},\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}})
be a homomorphism from
T
to
T^{\prime}
.

(i)

\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}
is also a homomorphism from the descendent Lie triple system
(\mathfrak{L}^{\prime},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{T})
of
T
to the descendent Lie triple system
(\mathfrak{L}^{\prime},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{T^{\prime}})
of
T^{\prime}
.

(ii)
The induced representation
(\mathfrak{L},\theta_{T})
of the Lie triple system
(\mathfrak{L}^{\prime},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{T})
and the induced representation
(\mathfrak{L},\theta_{T^{\prime}})
of the Lie triple system
(\mathfrak{L}^{\prime},[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]_{T^{\prime}})
satisfies the following relations
:

\displaystyle\psi_{\mathfrak{L}}\theta_{T}(u,v)=\theta_{T^{\prime}}(\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}(u),\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}(v))\psi_{\mathfrak{L}},

and

\displaystyle\psi_{\mathfrak{L}}D_{T}(u,v)=D_{T^{\prime}}(\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}(u),\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}(v))\psi_{\mathfrak{L}},

for all
u,v\in\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}
.

That is, the following diagram commutes
:

\begin{CD}\mathfrak{L}@>{\psi_{\mathfrak{L}}}>{}>\mathfrak{L}\\
@V{\theta_{T}(u,v)}V{}V@V{}V{\theta_{T^{\prime}}(\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}(u),\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}(v))}V\\
\mathfrak{L}\ @>{}>{\psi_{\mathfrak{L}}}>\mathfrak{L}.\end{CD}

Proof.

(i) By Eqs. (
2.5
)-(
2.7
) and Lemma
3.2
, we have

\displaystyle\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}([u,v,w]_{T})=

\displaystyle\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}\Big(D(Tu,Tv)w-\theta(Tu,Tw)v+\theta(Tv,Tw)u+\lambda[u,v,w]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}\Big)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle D\Big(\psi_{\mathfrak{L}}Tu,\psi_{\mathfrak{L}}Tv\Big)\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}(w)-\theta\Big(\psi_{\mathfrak{L}}Tu,\psi_{\mathfrak{L}}Tw\Big)\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}(v)

\displaystyle+\theta\Big(\psi_{\mathfrak{L}}Tv,\psi_{\mathfrak{L}}Tw\Big)\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}(u)+\lambda\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}([u,v,w]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle D\Big(T^{\prime}\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}(u),T^{\prime}\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}(v)\Big)\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}(w)-\theta\Big(T^{\prime}\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}(u),T^{\prime}\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}(w)\Big)\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}(v)

\displaystyle+\theta\Big(T^{\prime}\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}(v),T^{\prime}\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}(w)\Big)\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}(u)+\lambda[\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}(u),\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}(v),\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}(w)]_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle[\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}(u),\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}(v),\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}(w)]_{T^{\prime}}.

(ii) Now, according to Eqs. (
2.5
)-(
2.7
) and Lemma
3.2
, for all
u,v\in\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}
,
x\in\mathfrak{L}
, we have

\displaystyle\psi_{\mathfrak{L}}\Big(\theta_{T}(u,v)x\Big)=

\displaystyle\psi_{\mathfrak{L}}\Big([x,Tu,Tv]_{\mathfrak{L}}-T(D(x,Tu)v-\theta(x,Tv)u)\Big)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle[\psi_{\mathfrak{L}}(x),\psi_{\mathfrak{L}}(Tu),\psi_{\mathfrak{L}}(Tv)]_{\mathfrak{L}}-T^{\prime}\Big(\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}(D(x,Tu)v)-\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}(\theta(x,Tv)u)\Big)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle[\psi_{\mathfrak{L}}(x),T^{\prime}\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}(u),T^{\prime}\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}(v)]_{\mathfrak{L}}-T^{\prime}\Big(D(\psi_{\mathfrak{L}}(x),T^{\prime}\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}(u))\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}(v))

\displaystyle-\theta(\psi_{\mathfrak{L}}(x),T^{\prime}\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}(v))\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}(u))\Big)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\theta_{T}^{\prime}(\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}(u),\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}(v))\psi_{\mathfrak{L}}(x).

Similarly, we have

\displaystyle\psi_{\mathfrak{L}}D_{T}(u,v)=D_{T^{\prime}}(\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}(u),\psi_{\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}}(v))\psi_{\mathfrak{L}},

for all
u,v\in\mathfrak{L}^{\prime}
. We finish the proof.
∎

Let
T
and
T^{\prime}
be relative Rota-Baxter operators of weight

from a Lie triple system

\displaystyle(\partial_{T^{\prime}}p(\omega))(u_{1},u_{2},...,u_{2n-1},u_{2n},u_{2n+1})
