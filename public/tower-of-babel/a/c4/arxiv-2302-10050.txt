Pionic transitions from Z c ​ ( 4020 ) Z_{c}(4020) to D D wave charmonia
Xiao-Yu Qi 1, Qi Wu 2, Dian-Yong Chen 1,3

Abstract
In the present work, we investigate the charmed meson loops contributions to the pionic transitions from Z c ​ ( 4020 ) + Z_{c}(4020)^{+} to the D D wave triplets charmonia by using an effective Lagrangian approach. Our estimations indicate that the predicted branching fraction of Z c ​ ( 4020 ) + → π + ​ ψ ​ ( 1 3 ​ D J ) , J = ( 1 , 2 , 3 ) Z_{c}(4020)^{+}\to\pi^{+}\psi(1^{3}D_{J}),\ J=(1,2,3) are much smaller than the one of Z c ​ ( 4020 ) + → π + ​ h c Z_{c}(4020)^{+}\to\pi^{+}h_{c} . Thus, searching Z c ​ ( 4020 ) ± Z_{c}(4020)^{\pm} in the π ± ​ ψ ​ ( 1 3 ​ D J ) \pi^{\pm}\psi(1^{3}D_{J}) invariant mass distributions is impossible. Thus, the observed peak structures at 4.04 and 4.13 GeV in the π ± ​ ψ ​ ( 3770 ) \pi^{\pm}\psi(3770) invariant mass distributions should not come from the contributions of Z c ​ ( 4020 ) ± Z_{c}(4020)^{\pm} , and further precise experimental measurements of the e + ​ e − → π + ​ π − ​ ψ ​ ( 3770 ) e^{+}e^{-}\to\pi^{+}\pi^{-}\psi(3770) process are needed to decode the nature of these two peak structures.

IV
SUMMARY

About one decade ago, the BESIII Collaboration reported two near thresholds charmonium like states,
Z_{c}(3900)
and
Z_{c}(4020)
. However, their internal structure have not been well decoded until now. Searching for more decay and production process will help us to reveal the nature of these charmonium-like states. With the accumulation of the experimental data, the BESIII Collaboration have observed the cross sections for
e^{+}e^{-}\to\pi^{+}\pi^{-}\psi(3770)
and
e^{+}e^{-}\to\pi^{+}\pi^{-}\psi_{2}(3823)/\pi^{0}\pi^{0}\psi_{2}(3823)
. The hints for the peaks at 4.04 and 4.13 GeV has been observed in the
\pi^{\pm}\psi(3770)
invariant mass distributions. Inspired by the observations of BESIII Collaboration, we investigate charmed meson loop contributions to the decay process of
Z_{c}(4020)^{+}\to\pi^{+}\psi(1^{3}D_{J}),~J=(1,2,3)
by using an effective Lagrangian approach in the present work.

Our estimations indicate that the branching fractions of
Z_{c}(4020)^{+}\to\pi^{+}\psi(1^{3}D_{J}),~J=(1,2,3)
are of the order of
10^{-4}
for
\psi(3770)
and
\psi_{2}(3823)
, and of
10^{-5}
for
\psi_{3}(3843)
, respectively. Thus, we can conclude that searching
Z_{c}(4020)^{\pm}
in the
\pi^{\pm}\psi(1^{3}D_{J}),~J=(1,2,3)
invariant mass distributions of the
e^{+}e^{-}\to\pi^{+}\pi^{-}\psi(1^{3}D_{J}),~J=(1,2,3)
process should be impossible. Hence the peak structures at 4.04 GeV and 4.19 GeV in the
\pi^{\pm}\psi(3770)
invariant mass distributions should not come from the contribution of
Z_{c}(4020)^{\pm}
, and further precise experimental measurements of the
e^{+}e^{-}\to\pi^{+}\pi^{-}\psi(3770)
process are needed to decode the nature of these two peak structures.

Before the end of this work, it is worth mentioning that in the present estimation we connect the final
\pi\psi(1^{3}D_{J}),~J=(1,2,3)
with
Z_{c}(4020)
via charmed meson loops, where
Z_{c}(4020)
has strong coupling with
D^{\ast}\bar{D}^{\ast}
. Such kinds of meson loop mechanism should play the dominant role in the hidden charm decays of
Z_{c}(4020)
in the molecular scenario, while in the tetraquark picture,
Z_{c}(4020)
could directly decay into hidden charm states via the rearrangement of the four constituent quarks, which should be the major contributions to the hidden charm decays of
Z_{c}(4020)
. Thus, the success of meson loop mechanism in the hidden charm decays of
Z_{c}(4020)
could shed light on the molecular nature of
Z_{c}(4020)
in a certain extend.
