Abstract
Inspired by the Schwinger’s representation of angular momentum, we propose a representation of certain operators where we use the algebra of the annihilation and creation operators. In particular, we propose a representation of the Snyder space-time with the help of the annihilation and creation operators, which create and annihilate quantum of space. In addition, we show that by using a matrix representation of the S ​ O ​ ( 3 ) SO(3) or S ​ U ​ ( 2 ) SU(2) Lie algebra it is possible to obtain a representation of the spacial sector of Snyder space-time. Finally, we obtain a quantized expectation value of the area of a sphere. Keywords : Noncommutative space-time; quantum of area; annihilation and creation operators. Lie algebras and their representation with creation and annihilation operators

Suppose that

\displaystyle C_{\mu},\qquad\mu=0,1,2,\cdots d,

(1)

are hermitian matrices of
n\times n,
which satisfy the commutation relations

\displaystyle[C_{\mu},C_{\nu}]=B_{\mu\nu}

where

\displaystyle B_{\mu\nu}

is an hermitian antisymmetric tensor.

In addition suppose that

\displaystyle\hat{a}_{i},\qquad i=,1,2,\cdots n,

(2)

are
n
operators which satisfy the commutation relations

\displaystyle\left[\hat{a}_{k},\hat{a}_{l}\right]

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 0

(3)

\displaystyle\left[\hat{a}_{k},\hat{a}^{\dagger}_{l}\right]

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\delta_{kl}

(4)

\displaystyle\left[\hat{a}^{\dagger}_{k},\hat{a}^{\dagger}_{l}\right]

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 0.

Notice that using the operators (
2
) we can construct the operators

\displaystyle\hat{\cal C}_{\mu}=C_{\mu kl}\hat{a}^{\dagger}_{k}\hat{a}_{l},

\displaystyle\hat{\cal B}_{\mu\nu}=B_{\mu\nu kl}\hat{a}^{\dagger}_{k}\hat{a}_{l}.

In this case we obtain

\displaystyle\left[\hat{\cal C}_{\mu},\hat{\cal C}_{\nu}\right]

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left[C_{\mu kl}\hat{a}^{\dagger}_{k}\hat{a}_{l},C_{\nu rs}\hat{a}^{\dagger}_{r}\hat{a}_{s}\right]=C_{\mu kl}C_{\nu rs}\left[\hat{a}^{\dagger}_{k}\hat{a}_{l},\hat{a}^{\dagger}_{r}\hat{a}_{s}\right]

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle C_{\mu kl}C_{\nu rs}\left(\hat{a}^{\dagger}_{k}\left[\hat{a}_{l},\hat{a}^{\dagger}_{r}\right]\hat{a}_{s}+\hat{a}^{\dagger}_{r}\left[\hat{a}^{\dagger}_{k},\hat{a}_{s}\right]\hat{a}_{l}\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle C_{\mu kl}C_{\nu rs}\left(\hat{a}^{\dagger}_{k}\hat{a}_{s}\delta_{lr}-\hat{a}^{\dagger}_{r}\hat{a}_{l}\delta_{sk}\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle C_{\mu kr}C_{\nu rs}\hat{a}^{\dagger}_{k}\hat{a}_{s}-C_{\mu kl}C_{\nu rk}\hat{a}^{\dagger}_{r}\hat{a}_{l}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle C_{\mu kr}C_{\nu rs}\hat{a}^{\dagger}_{k}\hat{a}_{s}-C_{\mu rs}C_{\nu kr}\hat{a}^{\dagger}_{k}\hat{a}_{s}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left(C_{\mu kr}C_{\nu rs}-C_{\mu rs}C_{\nu kr}\right)\hat{a}^{\dagger}_{k}\hat{a}_{s}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left(C_{\mu kr}C_{\nu rs}-C_{\nu kr}C_{\mu rs}\right)\hat{a}^{\dagger}_{k}\hat{a}_{s}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left(C_{\mu}C_{\nu}-C_{\nu}C_{\mu}\right)_{ks}\hat{a}^{\dagger}_{k}\hat{a}_{s}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left[C_{\mu},C_{\nu}\right]_{ks}\hat{a}^{\dagger}_{k}\hat{a}_{s}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left(B_{\mu\nu}\right)_{ks}\hat{a}^{\dagger}_{k}\hat{a}_{s},

namely

\displaystyle\left[\hat{\cal C}_{\mu},\hat{\cal C}_{\nu}\right]

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\hat{\cal B}_{\mu\nu}.

In the next sections this result will be employed.

The commutation relations of

\displaystyle\left[\hat{x}^{\mu},\hat{x}^{\nu}\right]

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-il^{2}\left(\hat{x}^{\mu}\hat{p}^{\nu}-\hat{x}^{\nu}\hat{p}^{\mu}\right),

(5)

\displaystyle i\left(\eta^{\mu\nu}-l^{2}\hat{p}^{\mu}\hat{p}^{\nu}\right),

(6)

\displaystyle\left[\hat{p}^{\mu},\hat{p}^{\mu}\right]

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 0.

(7)

Summary

In this article, inspired by a seminal work of Schwinger on angular momentum
[
35
]
, we proposed a representation of the spatial sector of Snyder space-time using annihilation and creation operators, which create and annihilate quantum of space. And it allowed us to obtain a quantized expectation value of the area of a sphere. In order to obtain these results we employed the
SO(3)
or
SU(2)
Lie algebra.

It is worth mentioning that with the annihilation and creation operators coherent states can be obtained. Then
in a future work, we plan study coherent states for the Snyder space-time. In particular we shall study the Snyder version of the Fourier transform as in the reference
[
36
]
where it has been done in the standard non-commutative space. Armed with this tool, the Fourier transform for the Snyder space-time, we will address the corrections to some black hole metrics as has been done in
[
37
]
for the standard constant non-commutative space.
