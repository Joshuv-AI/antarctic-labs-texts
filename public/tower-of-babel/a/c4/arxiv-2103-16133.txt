An extension of a theorem of Bers and Finn on the removability of isolated singularities to the Euler-Lagrange equations related to general linear growth problems
Michael Bildhauer & Martin Fuchs

Abstract
1 1 1 AMS subject classification: 49N60, 49Q05, 53A10 A famous theorem of Bers and Finn states that isolated singularities of solutions to the non-parametric minimal surface equation are removable. We show that this result remains valid, if the area functional is replaced by a general functional of linear growth depending on the modulus of the gradient. We emphasize that Serrin ( [ 1 ] ) in fact proved the removability of singularities on sets of ( n − 1 ) (n-1) -dimensional Hausdorff measure zero in an even more general setting. Our main interest is to generalize the comparison principles as outlined, for instance, in Section 10 of [ 2 ] without having the particular geometric structure of minimal surfaces. It turns out that generalized catenoids serve as an appropriate tool for proving our results.

Step 1. Maximum principle.

We observe that in the subsequent considerations we may not assume

[
12

does not apply. We will make use of the following variant:

With Lemma

Corollary 1

The Dirichlet-problem associated to (

Proof of Lemma

.
From (

0=\sum_{i,j=1}^{n}\frac{\partial^{2}G}{\partial p_{i}\partial p_{j}}(\nabla u)\partial_{x_{i}}\partial_{x_{j}}u\quad\mbox{on $D$}\ .

(2.4)

Now we refer to Theorem 10.1, p. 263, of
[
13
]
with coefficients
(

,

,

a_{ij}(x,y,p):=\frac{\partial^{2}G}{\partial p_{i}\partial p_{j}}(p)

which, by (

), are seen to be elliptic. Since we consider the admissible function space

the proof
is complete with the above mentioned reference. ∎

W.l.o.g. we let
