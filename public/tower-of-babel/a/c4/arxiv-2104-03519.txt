Probing new physics for ( g − 2 ) μ (g-2)_{\mu} and gravitational waves
Ruiyu Zhou, Ligong Bian, Jing Shu

Abstract
We study the possibility of probing new physics accounting for ( g − 2 ) μ (g-2)_{\mu} anomaly and gravitational waves with pulsar timing array measurements. The model we consider is either a light gauge boson or neutral scalar interacting with muons. We show that the parameter spaces of dark U ⁡ ( 1 ) U(1) model with kinetic mixing explaining ( g − 2 ) μ (g-2)_{\mu} anomaly can realize a first-order phase transition, and the yield-produced gravitational wave may address the common red noise observed in the NANOGrav 12.5-yr dataset.

V
Conclusions and discussions

In this paper, we study the possibility to explain the
(g-2)_{\mu}
anomaly observed by the FermiLab with dark sectors, including dark
U(1)
and
Z_{3}
complex scalar models. We studied the low mass regions of the parameter spaces that can address the anomaly and found that they are constrained by the
(g-2)_{e}
results if the couplings to electron and muon are the same. For the dark
U(1)
model, we found that the GWB from the FOPT in
U(1)
maybe serves as an interpretation of the common spectrum process observed by the NANOGrav 12.5-yr dataset. Current pulsar timing array experiments cannot probe the
Z_{3}
scalar due to the much lower magnitude of the GWB spectrum.

The Phase Transition models

With the standard methodology, the phase transition can be studied with the thermal one-loop effective potential

\displaystyle V_{eff}(s,T)=V_{\rm 0}(s)+V_{CW}(s)+V^{\rm c.t}_{1}(s)+V_{1}^{T}(s,T)\,.

\displaystyle\partial_{s}(V_{CW}(s)+V^{\rm c.t}_{1}(s))\bigg|_{s=v_{s}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 0\,,

\displaystyle\partial_{s}\partial_{s}(V_{CW}(s)+V^{\rm c.t}_{1}(s))\bigg|_{s=v_{s}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 0\,.

(17)

\displaystyle V_{1}^{T}(s,T)=\frac{T^{4}}{2\pi^{2}}\,\sum_{i}g_{i}J_{B,F}\left(\frac{M_{i}^{2}(s)+\Pi_{i}(T)}{T^{2}}\right),
