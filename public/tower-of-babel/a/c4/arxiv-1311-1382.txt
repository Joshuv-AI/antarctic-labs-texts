New Periodic Solutions for Some Planar N + 3 N+3 -Body Problems with Newtonian Potentials Thanks: Supported partially by NSF of China.
Pengfei Yuan and Shiqing Zhang

Abstract
For some planar Newtonian N + 3 N+3 -body problems, we use variational minimization methods to prove the existence of new periodic solutions satisfying that N N bodies chase each other on a curve, and the other 3 3 bodies chase each other on another curve. From the definition of the group action in equations ( 3.1 ) − ( 3.3 ) (3.1)-(3.3) , we can find that they are new solutions which are also different from all the examples of Ferrario and Terracini (2004) [ 22 ] [22] . Key Words: N + 3 N+3 -body problems, periodic solutions, winding numbers, variational minimizers. 2000 Mathematicals Subject Classification: 34C15, 34C25, 58F
