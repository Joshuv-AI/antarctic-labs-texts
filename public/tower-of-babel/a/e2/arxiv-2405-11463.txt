Lemma 1.1
.

Assume that

For any

\displaystyle{\mathrm{Tr}_{q^{n}/q}(\epsilon)}^{2}-\mathrm{Tr}_{q^{n}/q}(\epsilon^{2})

\displaystyle=\Bigg(\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}\epsilon^{q^{i}}\Bigg)^{2}-\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}\epsilon^{2q^{j}}

\displaystyle=\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}\epsilon^{2q^{i}}+2\cdot\sum_{0\leq i<j\leq n-1}\epsilon^{q^{i}+q^{j}}-\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}\epsilon^{2q^{j}}

\displaystyle=\{({\mathrm{Tr}_{q^{n}/q}(\epsilon)}^{2}-a)-(\mathrm{Tr}_{q^{n}/q}(\epsilon^{2})+a)\}\cdot 2^{-1},
