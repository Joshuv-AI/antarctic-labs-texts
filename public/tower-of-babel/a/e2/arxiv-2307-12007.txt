Meromorphic Continuation Of Global Zeta Function For Number Fields
SUBHAM DE

Abstract
In the paper, we shall establish the existence of a meromorphic continuation of the Global Zeta Function ζ ⁡ ( f , χ ) \zeta(f,\chi) of a Global Number Field K K and also deduce the functional equation for the same, using different properties of the idèle class group 𝒞 K 1 \mathcal{C}_{K}^{1} of a global field K K extensively defined using basic notions of Adèles ( 𝔸 K \mathbb{A}_{K} ) and Idèles ( 𝕀 K \mathbb{I}_{K} ) of K K , and also evaluating Fourier Transforms of functions f f on the space 𝒮 ⁡ ( 𝔸 K ) \mathcal{S}(\mathbb{A}_{K}) of Adèlic Schwartz-Bruhat Functions . A brief overview of most of the concepts required to prove our desired result have been provided to the readers in the earlier sections of the text. 3
Riemann-Roch Theorem

In this section, we shall state and prove one of the most unique and important theorems in the field of
Harmonic Analysis
on
Adèlic Groups
which shall use to prove our main theorem. First, let us mention the
Poisson Summation Formula
in order to prove the

Theorem 3.0.1
.

(Poisson Summation Formula) Consider
f\in\mathcal{S}(\mathbb{A}_{K})
, i.e.,
f
satisfies the following conditions,

(i)

f\in L^{1}(\mathbb{A}_{K})
, and,
f
is continuous.

(ii)

\sum\limits_{\gamma\in K}f(z(y+\gamma))
converges for all idèles
z\in\mathbb{I}_{K}
and for every adèle
y\in\mathbb{A}_{K}
, uniformly for
y
.

(iii)

\sum\limits_{\gamma\in K}|\hat{f}(z\gamma)|
is convergent for every idèle
z\in\mathbb{I}_{K}
.

Then,

\tilde{f}=\tilde{\hat{f}}

Where,
\hat{f}
denotes the Adèlic Fourier Transform of
f
, and,
\tilde{f}(x):=\sum\limits_{\gamma\in K}f(\gamma+x)
,
\forall

x\in\mathbb{A}_{K}
.
In other words,

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{\gamma\in K}f(\gamma+x)=\sum\limits_{\gamma\in K}\hat{f}(\gamma+x).

(3.1)

Proof.

We consider any function
\varphi
on
\mathbb{A}_{K}/K
, induced by some
K
-invariant function
\varphi
on
\mathbb{A}_{K}
. Therefore, by
definition(
1.6.1
)
of
Fourier Transform
, we have,

\hat{\varphi}(z)=\int\limits_{\mathbb{A}_{K}/K}\varphi(t)\psi(tz)\overline{dt}
,
\forall

z\in K

Where,
\psi
is a non-trivial continuous unitary character on
\mathbb{A}_{K}
; and,
\overline{dt}
is the quotient measure on
\mathbb{A}_{K}/K
induced by the measure
dt
on
\mathbb{A}_{K}
; satisfying the relation:

\int\limits_{\mathbb{A}_{K}/K}\hat{f}(t)\overline{dt}=\int\limits_{\mathbb{A}_{K}/K}\sum\limits_{\nu\in K}f(\nu+t)\overline{dt}=\int\limits_{\mathbb{A}_{K}}f(t)dt

for every
f\in\mathcal{S}(\mathbb{A}_{K})
such that,
f
satisfies certain convergence properties.

It is important to mention that, each of the integrals in the above identity is well-defined, since, in the first two integrals, the integration variable

assumes value from the quotient group
\mathbb{A}_{K}/K
.

In order to prove the theorem, we shall need to apply the following two important lemmas:

Lemma 3.0.2

For every continuous function

\hat{f}|_{K}=\hat{\tilde{f}}|_{K}

, (Expanding
\tilde{f}
)
\forall

=\int\limits_{\mathbb{A}_{K}/K}(\sum\limits_{\gamma\in K}f(\gamma+t)\psi((\gamma+t)z))\overline{dt}

[ Since
\psi
being assumed to be unitary on

\psi|_{K}=1

\Rightarrow

\psi(tz)=\psi((\gamma+t)z)
,
\forall

relative to the counting measure on

=\int\limits_{\mathbb{A}_{K}}f(t)\psi(tz)dt

=\hat{f}(z)
.
\forall

∎
