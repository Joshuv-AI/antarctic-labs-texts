On the generating function and growth of the positive singular braid monoid
A. L. Anisimov, G. A. Kameneva, V. V. Vershinin

Abstract
. We prove that the generating function of the positive singular braid monoid is rational and we give the exact formula for such monoid on three strands.

Primary 20F36; Secondary 20M99

1.
Introduction

Braid groups were defined by E. Artin
[
2
]
in 1925 and since then they were studied intensively.
They were also generalized in several directions. E. Brieskorn
[
6
]
defined what are called now
Artin groups of finite type.

The
Baez–Birman monoid

SB_{n}
or
singular braid monoid

[
3
]
,
[
4
]

is defined as the
monoid with generators
\sigma_{i},\sigma_{i}^{-1},x_{i}
,
i=1,\dots,n-1,
and
relations

\displaystyle\sigma_{i}\sigma_{j}=\sigma_{j}\sigma_{i},\ \text{if}\ \ |i-j|>1,

\displaystyle x_{i}x_{j}=x_{j}x_{i},\ \text{if}\ \ |i-j|>1,

\displaystyle x_{i}\sigma_{j}=\sigma_{j}x_{i},\ \text{if}\ \ |i-j|\not=1,

\displaystyle\sigma_{i}\sigma_{i+1}\sigma_{i}=\sigma_{i+1}\sigma_{i}\sigma_{i+1},

\displaystyle\sigma_{i}\sigma_{i+1}x_{i}=x_{i+1}\sigma_{i}\sigma_{i+1},

\displaystyle\sigma_{i+1}\sigma_{i}x_{i+1}=x_{i}\sigma_{i+1}\sigma_{i},

\displaystyle\sigma_{i}\sigma_{i}^{-1}=\sigma_{i}^{-1}\sigma_{i}=1.

In pictures
\sigma_{i}
corresponds to canonical generator of the braid
group and
x_{i}
represents an intersection
of the
i
th and
(i+1)
st strand as in the Figure

.

Let
G
be a finitely presented group or monoid, and let
a_{k}
be the number of its elements of the length k.
One of the aspects of study of groups and monoids is to understand the behaviour of the
sequence
(a_{k})
,
k\in\mathbb{N}
.

Let
B(W)_{n}^{+}
is the positive
Artin monoid of finite type (no inverses of generators),
W
is the corresponding finite Coxeter group.
Let
a_{k}^{[n]}
be the number of elements of the length
k
in such monoid
and

\displaystyle\phi^{[n]}(t)=\sum_{k\geq 0}a^{[n]}_{k}t^{k}

be the corresponding generating function.

P. Deligne proved that
\phi^{[n]}(t)
is a rational function. Independently Xu
proved this for the classical positive braid monoids and gave the following expression of this
function for
B_{3}^{+}

\displaystyle\frac{1}{(1-x)(1-x-x^{2})}.

So
a_{k}=F_{k+3}-1
, where
F_{i}
are the Fibonacci numbers.
Hence the growth rate of the classical braid monoid on three strands, is equal
to
\frac{1+\sqrt{5}}{2}\approx 1.618
.
Concrete formulas for the generating functions for Artin monoids were obtained by
K. Saito etal
[
12
,
11
]
. B. Berceanu and Z. Iqbal proved in
[
5
]
that 4 is a
universal upper bound for the growth of the positive Artin monoids of finite type.

Let
SB_{n}^{+}
the positive singular braid
monoid, i.e. given by the generators
\sigma_{i},x_{i}
,
i=1,\dots,n-1,
and the positive part
of singular braid
relations. Let
b_{k}^{[n]}
be its number of elements of the length
k
in such monoid
and

\displaystyle f^{[n]}(t)=\sum_{k\geq 0}b^{[n]}_{k}t^{k}

be the corresponding generating function.

2.
Statements of the main results

Here we prove the following theorems.

Theorem 2.1
.

The generating function for the positive singular braid monoid
SB_{n}^{+}
is rational.

The monoid
SB_{3}^{+}
is given by four generators
\sigma_{1},\sigma_{2},x_{1},x_{2}
and following relations.

\displaystyle\left\{\begin{array}[]{l}\sigma_{2}\sigma_{1}\sigma_{2}=\sigma_{1}\sigma_{2}\sigma_{1},\\
x_{2}\sigma_{1}\sigma_{2}=\sigma_{1}\sigma_{2}x_{1},\\
x_{1}\sigma_{2}\sigma_{1}=\sigma_{2}\sigma_{1}x_{2},\\
x_{1}\sigma_{1}=\sigma_{1}x_{1},\\
x_{2}\sigma_{2}=\sigma_{2}x_{2}.\end{array}\right.

Theorem 2.2
.

The generating function for the monoid
SB_{3}^{+}
is given by the formula

(2.1)

In particular we have

For

4.
Growth

Let us consider the equation

2t^{3}-t^{2}-3t+1=0.

Its depressed form is as follows

y^{3}-\frac{19}{12}y+\frac{13}{54}=0.

The value of

\Delta=\frac{q^{2}}{4}+\frac{p^{3}}{27}=\frac{1}{4}\left({\frac{13}{54}}\right)^{2}-\frac{1}{27}\left(\frac{19}{12}\right)^{3}

is negative. So, the equation has three real roots.
Let us denote these roots by
r_{1}
,
r_{2}
and
r_{3}
in ascending order. Their approximative values are as follows

\displaystyle r_{1}

\displaystyle\approx-1.161702138,

\displaystyle r_{2}

\displaystyle\approx 0.3210368161,

\displaystyle r_{3}

\displaystyle\approx 1.340665322.

They can be also expressed by the trigonometric formulas. For example for
r_{2}
:

(4.1)

r_{2}=\frac{\sqrt{19}\cos{\left(\frac{\arccos{\left(\frac{26\sqrt{19}}{361}\right)}}{3}+\frac{\pi}{3}\right)}}{3}+\frac{1}{6}\approx 0.3210368161,\\

Let us rewrite the function (
2.1
) in the form

(4.2)

f^{[3]}=\frac{1}{(1-t)(1-3t-t^{2}+2t^{3})}=\\
\frac{1}{2(1-t)(r_{1}-t)(r_{2}-t)(r_{3}-t)}.

and then we express (
4.2
) as a sum with
coefficients
a_{i}
,
i=0,1,2,3
.

f^{[3]}=\frac{a_{0}}{(1-t)}+\frac{a_{1}}{(r_{1}-t)}+\frac{a_{2}}{(r_{2}-t)}+\frac{a_{3}}{(r_{3}-t)}.

After calculations we obtain:

a_{0}=-1,

a_{1}=\frac{1}{2}\frac{2r_{1}^{2}+r_{1}-2}{(r_{1}-r_{2})(r_{1}-r_{3})}\approx-0.06233879045,

a_{2}=\frac{1}{2}\frac{2r_{2}^{2}+r_{2}-2}{(r_{2}-r_{1})(r_{2}-r_{3})}\approx 0.4870988600,

a_{3}=-\frac{1}{2}\frac{2r_{3}^{2}+r_{3}-2}{(r_{3}-r_{1})(r_{3}-r_{2})}\approx 0.5752399310.

We have

\frac{a_{j}}{(r_{j}-t)}=a_{j}\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}r_{j}^{-(i+1)}t^{i},\ \ j=0,1,2,3.

As
|\,r_{j}|>1
in the study of the growth rate the only essential series is

\frac{a_{2}}{(r_{j}-t)}=a_{2}\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}r_{2}^{-(i+1)}t^{i}.

So the growth rate of the
SB_{3}^{+}
is equal to
r_{2}^{-1}
where
r_{2}
is given by the formula (
4.1
). Approximatively it is equal to 3.11, almost two times greater than that of the growth rate of the classical braid monoid on three strands, which is equal to
\frac{1+\sqrt{5}}{2}\approx 1.618
. This looks quite
reasonable because of two new generators.
