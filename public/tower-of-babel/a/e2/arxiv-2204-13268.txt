Quintessential Axions from a New Confining Force
Jihn E. Kim, Younggeun Kim and Soonkeon Nam

Abstract
Almost massless mesons created at the condensation scale of extra nonabelian gauge group can be candidates of dark energy source. This can be another motivation for introducing an additional confining force.
