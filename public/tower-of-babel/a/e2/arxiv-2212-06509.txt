Oblique corrections from triplet quarks Final version published in JHEP 03 , 031 (2023).
Francisco Albergaria, Luís Lavoura, and Jorge C. Romão

Abstract
We present general formulas for the oblique-correction parameters S S , T T , U U , V , V, W W , and X X in an extension of the Standard Model having arbitrary numbers of singlet, doublet, and triplet quarks with electric charges − 4 / 3 -4/3 , − 1 / 3 -1/3 , 2 / 3 2/3 , and 5 / 3 5/3 that mix with the standard quarks of the same charge.
