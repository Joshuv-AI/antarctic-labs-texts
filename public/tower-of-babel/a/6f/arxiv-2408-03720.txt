Abstract
We attempt to apply the Regge trajectory approach to the triply heavy triquarks ( ( Q ​ Q ′ ) ​ Q ¯ ′′ ) ((QQ^{\prime})\bar{Q}^{\prime\prime}) ( Q , Q ′ , Q ′′ = b , c ) (Q,\,Q^{\prime},\,Q^{\prime\prime}=b,\,c) . II.3
Regge trajectory relations for the triply heavy triquarks

A triply heavy triquark consists of one doubly heavy diquark and one heavy antiquark, therefore, it is a heavy-heavy system for the
\lambda
-mode. One of the constituents of the triply heavy triquark, the doubly heavy diquark composed of two heavy quark, is also a heavy-heavy system. Using formulas (
2
), (
10
) and (
11
), we have the Regge trajectory relations for the triply heavy triquarks

\displaystyle M

\displaystyle=m_{R_{\lambda}}+\beta_{x_{\lambda}}(x_{\lambda}+c_{0x_{\lambda}})^{2/3}\;(x_{\lambda}=L,\,N_{r}),

\displaystyle M_{d}

\displaystyle=m_{R_{\rho}}+\beta_{x_{\rho}}(x_{\rho}+c_{0x_{\rho}})^{2/3}\;(x_{\rho}=l,\,n_{r}),

(12)

where

\displaystyle m_{R_{\lambda}}

\displaystyle=M_{d}+m_{q^{\prime\prime}}+C/2,

\displaystyle m_{R_{\rho}}

\displaystyle=m_{q}+m_{q^{\prime}}+C/2,

\displaystyle\beta_{L}

\displaystyle=\frac{3}{2}\left(\frac{\sigma^{2}}{4\mu_{\lambda}}\right)^{1/3}c_{fL},\;\beta_{N_{r}}=\frac{(3\pi)^{2/3}}{2}\left(\frac{\sigma^{2}}{4\mu_{\lambda}}\right)^{1/3}c_{fN_{r}},

\displaystyle\mu_{\lambda}

\displaystyle=\frac{M_{d}m_{q^{\prime\prime}}}{M_{d}+m_{q^{\prime\prime}}},\;\mu_{\rho}=\frac{m_{q}m_{q^{\prime}}}{m_{q}+m_{q^{\prime}}},

\displaystyle\beta_{l}

\displaystyle=\frac{3}{2}\left(\frac{\sigma^{2}}{4\mu_{\rho}}\right)^{1/3}c_{fl},\;\beta_{n_{r}}=\frac{(3\pi)^{2/3}}{2}\left(\frac{\sigma^{2}}{4\mu_{\rho}}\right)^{1/3}c_{fn_{r}}.

(13)

12

) and (

M=M_{d}+m_{q^{\prime\prime}}+C/2+\beta_{x_{\lambda}}(x_{\lambda}+c_{0x_{\lambda}})^{2/3}

IV
Conclusions

In this work, we attempt to apply the Regge trajectory approach to the triply heavy triquarks
((QQ^{\prime})\bar{Q}^{\prime\prime})

(Q,\,Q^{\prime},\,Q^{\prime\prime}=b,\,c)
. We present the triquark Regge trajectory relations, and then employ them to crudely estimate the spectra of the triply heavy triquarks
((cc)\bar{c})
,
((cc)\bar{b})
,
((bc)\bar{c})
,
((bc)\bar{b})
,
((bb)\bar{c})
, and
((bb)\bar{b})
.

The
\lambda
- and
\rho
-trajectories for the triply heavy triquarks are discussed.
The triquark Regge trajectory is a new and very simple approach for estimating the spectra of triquarks. It also provides a simple method to investigate easily the excitations of substructures in pentaquarks and hexaquarks in the triquark picture.

Triquarks are colored states and are not observable. In the diquark-triquark model, the colorless pentaquarks are composed of one triquark and one diquark, and they can be observed. We crudely estimate the spin-averaged masses of the ground states of the pentaquarks
(\bar{c}(cc))(cc)
,
(\bar{b}(cc))(cc)
, and
(\bar{c}(bb))(cc)
by employing the obtained triquark Regge trajectories. The estimated results are consistent with other theoretical predictions.
