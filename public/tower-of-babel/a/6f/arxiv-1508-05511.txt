Abstract
. An algorithmic proof of General Neron Desingularization is given here for one dimensional local domains and it is implemented in Singular . Also a theorem recalling Greenberg’ strong approximation theorem is presented for one dimensional Cohen-Macaulay local rings. Key words : Smooth morphisms, regular morphisms, smoothing ring morphisms. 2010 Mathematics Subject Classification: Primary 13B40, Secondary 14B25,13H05,13J15.



Introduction

A ring morphism
u:A\rightarrow A^{\prime}
has
regular fibers
if for all prime ideals
P\in\operatorname{Spec}A
the ring
A^{\prime}/PA^{\prime}
is a regular ring, i.e. its localizations are regular local rings. It has
geometrically regular fibers
if for all prime ideals
P\in\operatorname{Spec}A
and all finite field extensions
K
of the fraction field of
A/P
the ring
K\otimes_{A/P}A^{\prime}/PA^{\prime}
is regular.
If for all
P\in\operatorname{Spec}A
the fraction field of
A/P
has characteristic
0
then the regular fibers of
u
are geometrically regular fibers. A flat morphism
u
is
regular
if its fibers are geometrically regular.

In Artin approximation theory
[
2
]
an important result is the following theorem generalizing the Neron Desingularization
[
7
]
,
[
2
]
.

(General Neron Desingularization, Popescu

Let
u:A\rightarrow A^{\prime}
be a regular morphism of Noetherian rings and

a finite type

-algebra. Then any
A
-morphism

factors through a smooth

-algebra

, that is

.

The purpose of this paper is to give an algorithmic proof of the above theorem when
A,A^{\prime}
are one dimensional local domains and
A\supset\mathbb{Q}
. This proof is somehow presented by the second author in a lecture given in the frame of a semester of Artin Approximation and Singularity Theory organized in 2015 by CIRM in Luminy (see http://hlombardi.free.fr/Popescu-Luminy2015.pdf). The algorithm was implemented by the authors in the Computer Algebra system
Singular

[

and will be as soon as possible found in a development version as the library

https://github.com/Singular/Source.

We may take the same General Neron Desingularization for

When

1.
The theorem

Let
u:A\rightarrow A^{\prime}
be a flat morphism of Noetherian local domains of dimension
1
. Suppose that
A\supset\mathbb{Q}
and the maximal ideal
m
of
A
generates the maximal ideal of
A^{\prime}
. Then
u
is regular morphism. Moreover, we suppose that there exist canonical inclusions
k=A/m\rightarrow A
,
k^{\prime}=A^{\prime}/mA^{\prime}\rightarrow A^{\prime}
such that
u(k)\subset k^{\prime}
.

Let
B=A[Y]/I
,
Y=(Y_{1},\ldots,Y_{n})
. If
f=(f_{1},\ldots,f_{r})
,
r\leq n
is a system of polynomials from
I
then we can define the ideal
\Delta_{f}
generated by all
r\times r
-minors of the Jacobian matrix
\left(\displaystyle\frac{\partial f_{i}}{\partial Y_{j}}\right)
. After Elkik
[
4
]
let
H_{B/A}
be the radical of the ideal
\sum_{f}\big((f):I\big)\Delta_{f}B
, where the sum is taken over all systems of polynomials
f
from
I
with
r\leq n
.
Then
B_{P}
,
P\in\operatorname{Spec}B
is essentially smooth over
A
if and only if
P\not\supset H_{B/A}
by the Jacobian criterion for smoothness.
Thus
H_{B/A}
measures the non smooth locus of
B
over
A
.

Definition 2
.

B
is
standard smooth
over
A
if there exists
f
in
I
as above such that
1\in\big((f):I\big)\Delta_{f}B
.

The aim of this paper is to give an easy algorithmic proof of the following theorem.

Theorem 3
.

Any
A
-morphism
v:B\rightarrow A^{\prime}
factors through a standard smooth
A
-algebra
B^{\prime}
.

If
A
is essentially of finite type over
\mathbb{Q}
, then the ideal

can be computed in
Singular
by following its definition but it is easier to describe only the ideal
\sum_{f}\big((f):I\big)\Delta_{f}B
defined above. This is the case considered in our algorithmic part, let us say
A\cong k[x]/F
for some variables
x=(x_{1},\ldots x_{t})
, and the completion of
A^{\prime}
is

for some field extension
k\subset K
. When
v
is defined by polynomials

from

then our problem is easy. Let

be the field obtained by adjoining to

is a subring of

containing

which is essentially smooth over

. Then we may take

as a standard smooth

-algebra such that

is a localization of

.
Consequently we suppose usually that

is not polynomial defined and moreover

.

2.
Reduction to the case when
H_{B/A}\cap A\not=0

We may suppose that

-algebras given by

Note that
