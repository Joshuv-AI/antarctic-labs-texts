Quantum Lower Bound for Graph Collision Implies Lower Bound for Triangle Detection
Kaspars Balodis, Jānis Iraids

Abstract
We show that an improvement to the best known quantum lower bound for Graph-Collision problem implies an improvement to the best known lower bound for Triangle problem in the quantum query complexity model. In Graph-Collision we are given free access to a graph ( V , E ) (V,E) and access to a function f : V → { 0 , 1 } f:V\rightarrow\{0,1\} as a black box. We are asked to determine if there exist ( u , v ) ∈ E (u,v)\in E , such that f ⁡ ( u ) = f ⁡ ( v ) = 1 f(u)=f(v)=1 . In Triangle we have a black box access to an adjacency matrix of a graph and we have to determine if the graph contains a triangle. For both of these problems the known lower bounds are trivial ( Ω ⁡ ( n ) \Omega(\sqrt{n}) and Ω ⁡ ( n ) \Omega(n) , respectively) and there is no known matching upper bound.
