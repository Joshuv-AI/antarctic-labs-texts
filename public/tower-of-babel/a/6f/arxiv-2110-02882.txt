Reiterated Homogenization of nonlinear degenerate elliptic operators with nonstandard growth
Joel Fotso Tachago ‡, Hubert Nnang †, Elvira Zappale ⊺

Abstract
. It is shown by means of reiterated two-scale convergence in the Sobolev-Orlicz setting, that the sequence of solutions of a class of highly oscillatory problems involving nonlinear elliptic operators with nonstandard growth, converges to a solution of a suitable homogeneous nonlinear elliptic equation associated to an operator with nonstandard growth.

\displaystyle\theta\left\|\left|Du_{\varepsilon}\right|\right\|_{\Phi,\Omega}^{\sigma}\leq\int_{\Omega}\theta\Phi\left(\left|Du_{\varepsilon}\right|\right)dx\leq 2\left\|f\right\|_{\widetilde{\Phi},\Omega}\left\|u_{\varepsilon}\right\|_{\Phi,\Omega}\leq c\left\|\left|Du_{\varepsilon}\right|\right\|_{\Phi,\Omega},\sigma>1.

We deduce therefore that:

\underset{0<\varepsilon}{\sup}\left\|u_{\varepsilon}\right\|_{W_{0}^{1}L^{\Phi}\left(\Omega\right)}<+\infty.

It is easily observed that if

Corollary 4.2
.

Under the same assumptions of Proposition
4.1
, take
\left(u_{\varepsilon}\right)_{\varepsilon}\subset L^{\Phi}\left(\Omega\right)

Let
\left(u_{n}\right)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\subset\mathcal{C}\left(\overline{\Omega}\right)

(4.1)

\displaystyle I_{1}\left(\varepsilon,n\right):=\int_{\Omega}\left(a_{i}^{\varepsilon}\left(\cdot,\cdot,u_{\varepsilon},\mathbf{f}^{\varepsilon}\right)-a_{i}^{\varepsilon}\left(\cdot,\cdot,u_{0},\mathbf{f}^{\varepsilon}\right)\right)\varphi^{\varepsilon}dx,

4.1

(4.2)

\displaystyle\left|I_{1}\left(\varepsilon,n\right)\right|\leq 2c\left\|\varphi\right\|_{L^{\Phi}\left(\Omega;\mathcal{C}_{per}\left(Y\times Z\right)\right)}\left\|u_{\varepsilon}-u_{0}\right\|_{\Phi,\Omega}^{\alpha}\hbox{ for every }n\in\mathbb{N},

uniformly with respect to
\varepsilon
.

(y,z)\in\left(U_{n}\setminus\mathcal{N}\right)\times V_{n}.
Then
{\widetilde{\psi}_{n}^{\varepsilon=1}}\left(y,z\right)\geq 0
for

,

{\widetilde{\psi}^{\varepsilon=1}}\left(y,z\right)\geq 0

∎

.

Proposition 5.3
.
