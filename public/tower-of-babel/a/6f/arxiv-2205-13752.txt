On Lyndon-Word Representable Graphs
Hossein Teimoori Faal

Abstract
In this short note, we first associate a new simple undirected graph with a given word over an ordered alphabet of n n -letters. We will call it the Lyndon graph of that word. Then, we introduce the concept of the Lyndon-word representable graph as a graph isomorphic to a Lyndon graph of some word. Then, we introduce the generalized Stirling cycle number S ⁡ ( N , n , k ) S(N;n,k) as the number words of length N N with k k distinct Lydon words in their Lyndon factorization over an ordered alphabet of n n -letters . Finally, we conclude the paper with several interesting open questions and conjectures for interested audiences.

For any
