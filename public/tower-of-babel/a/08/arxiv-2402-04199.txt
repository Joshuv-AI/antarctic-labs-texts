Ab initio calculation of the effective Coulomb interactions in M ​ X 2 MX_{2} ( M M =Ti, V, Cr, Mn, Fe, Co, Ni; X X =S, Se, Te): intrinsic magnetic ordering and Mott insulating phase
A. Karbalaee, S. Belbasi, H. Hadipour

Abstract
Correlated phenomena such as magnetism and Mott phase are a very controversial issue in two-dimensional transition metal dichalcogenides (TMDCs). With the aim of finding the value of correlation strength and understanding the origin of ferromagnetic order in TMDCs, we first identify relevant low-energy degrees of freedom on both octahedral T and trigonal prismatic H lattices in M ​ X 2 MX_{2} ( M M =Ti, V, Cr, Mn, Fe, Co, Ni; X X =S, Se, Te) and then determine the strength of the effective Coulomb interactions between localized d d electrons from the first principles using the constrained random-phase approximation. The on-site Coulomb interaction (Hubbard U ) values lie in the range 1.4 − - 3.7 eV (1.1 − - 3.6 eV) and depend on the ground-state electronic structure, d -electron number, and correlated subspace. For most of the TMDCs we obtain 1 < U / W b < 2 1<U/W_{b}<2 (the bandwidth W b W_{b} ), which turn out to be larger than the corresponding values in elementary transition metals. On the basis of the calculated U U and exchange J J interaction, we have checked the condition to be fulfilled for the formation of the ferromagnetic order by Stoner criterion. The results indicate that experimentally observed Mn X 2 X_{2} ( X X =S, Se) and V X 2 X_{2} ( X X =S, Se) have an intrinsic ferromagnetic behavior in pristine form, although V-based materials are close vicinity to the critical point separating ferromagnetic from paramagnetic phase.

The fully screened Coulomb interaction

\tilde{U}(\bm{r},\bm{r}^{\prime},\omega)=\int d\bm{r}^{\prime\prime}\epsilon^{-1}(\bm{r},\bm{r}^{\prime\prime},\omega)V(\bm{r}^{\prime\prime},\bm{r}^{\prime}),

(1)

where

\epsilon(\bm{r},\bm{r}^{\prime},\omega)=\delta(\bm{r}-\bm{r}^{\prime})-\int d\bm{r}^{\prime\prime}V(\bm{r},\bm{r}^{\prime\prime})P(\bm{r}^{\prime\prime},\bm{r}^{\prime},\omega),

(2)

where the RPA polarization function

\begin{gathered}P(\bm{r},\bm{r}^{\prime},\omega)=\\
2\sum_{m}^{\mathrm{occ}}\sum_{m^{\prime}}^{\mathrm{unocc}}\varphi_{m}(\bm{r})\varphi_{m^{\prime}}^{*}(\bm{r})\varphi_{m}^{*}(\bm{r}^{\prime})\varphi_{m^{\prime}}(\bm{r}^{\prime})\\
\times\Bigg[\frac{1}{\omega-\Delta_{mm^{\prime}}+i\eta}-\frac{1}{\omega+\Delta_{mm^{\prime}}-i\eta}\Bigg].\end{gathered}

Here,

\epsilon_{m}
