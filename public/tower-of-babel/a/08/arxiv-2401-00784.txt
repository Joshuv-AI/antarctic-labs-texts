A Short Proof of Bose-Einstein Condensation in the Gross-Pitaevskii Regime and Beyond
Christian Brennecke, Morris Brooks, Cristina Caraci, Jakob Oldenburg

Abstract
We consider dilute Bose gases on the three dimensional unit torus that interact through a pair potential with scattering length of order N κ − 1 N^{\kappa-1} , for some κ > 0 \kappa>0 . For the range κ ∈ [ 0 , 1 43 ) \kappa\in[0,\frac{1}{43}) , [ 1 ] proves complete BEC of low energy states into the zero momentum mode based on a unitary renormalization through operator exponentials that are quartic in creation and annihilation operators. In this paper, we give a new and self-contained proof of BEC of the ground state for κ ∈ [ 0 , 1 20 ) \kappa\in[0,\frac{1}{20}) by combining some of the key ideas of [ 1 ] with the novel diagonalization approach introduced recently in [ 16 ] , which is based on the Schur complement formula. In particular, our proof avoids the use of operator exponentials and is significantly simpler than [ 1 ] .

Introduction and Main Result

We consider
N
interacting bosons in
\Lambda:=\mathbb{T}^{3}=\mathbb{R}^{3}/\mathbb{Z}^{3}
with Hamiltonian

H_{N}=\sum_{i=1}^{N}-\Delta_{x_{i}}+\sum_{1\leq i<j\leq N}N^{2-2\kappa}V(N^{1-\kappa}(x_{i}-x_{j})),

(1)

acting in
L^{2}_{s}(\Lambda^{N})
, the Hilbert space consisting of functions in
L^{2}(\Lambda^{N})
that are invariant with respect to permutations of the
N
particles. We assume the interaction potential
V\in L^{1}(\mathbb{R}^{3})
to have compact support, to be radial and to be pointwise non-negative.

Note that analyzing
H_{N}
is equivalent to analyzing the Hamiltonian of
N
bosons interacting through the unscaled potential
V
in
\mathbb{R}^{3}/L\mathbb{Z}^{3}
for
L=N^{1-\kappa}
. In this sense, we consider regimes of strongly diluted systems of bosons with number of particles density
N^{3\kappa-2}\ll 1
(as long as
\kappa<\frac{2}{3}
). The case
\kappa=0
corresponds to the Gross-Pitaevskii (GP) regime and the case
\kappa=\frac{2}{3}
corresponds to the usual thermodynamic limit (with number of particles density equal to one).

In this paper, we are interested in understanding low energy properties of the Bose gas in regimes that interpolate between the GP and thermodynamic limits. Based on
[
20
,
32
]
, it is well-known that the ground state energy
E_{N}:=\inf\text{spec}(H_{N})
is equal to

E_{N}=4\pi\mathfrak{a}N^{1+\kappa}+o(N^{1+\kappa}),

where
\mathfrak{a}
denotes the scattering length of the potential
V
and where

denotes an error of subleading order, that is
\lim_{N\to\infty}o(N^{1+\kappa})/N^{1+\kappa}=0
. Recall that under our assumptions the scattering length of

is characterized by

\begin{split}8\pi\mathfrak{a}&=\inf\bigg\{\int_{\mathbb{R}^{3}}dx\,\Big(2|\nabla f(x)|^{2}+V(x)|f(x)|^{2}\Big):\lim_{|x|\to\infty}f(x)=1\bigg\}.\end{split}

A question closely related to the computation of the ground state energy is whether the ground state exhibits Bose-Einstein condensation (BEC). If
\psi_{N}
denotes the ground state vector, this means that the largest eigenvalue of the associated reduced one particle density matrix
\gamma_{N}^{(1)}=\text{tr}_{2,\dots,N}|\psi_{N}\rangle\langle\psi_{N}|
remains of size one in the limit
N\to\infty
:

\liminf_{N\to\infty}\|\gamma_{N}^{(1)}\|_{\text{op}}>0.

Theorem 1
.

Let

\lim_{N\to\infty}\langle\varphi_{0},\gamma_{N}^{(1)}\varphi_{0}\rangle=1.

In the following, let us denote by

\text{P}_{\text{L}}:=\big\{p\in\Lambda^{*}:|p|\leq N^{\alpha}\big\}.

(3)

\begin{split}H_{N}\geq\sum_{r\in\Lambda_{+}^{*}}|r|^{2}c^{*}_{r}c_{r}+\frac{N^{\kappa}}{2N}\!\!\!\!\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}p,q,r\in\Lambda^{*}:\\

(4)

where we set

Lemma 3

\begin{split}&\frac{N^{\kappa}}{2N}\!\!\!\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}p,q,r\in\Lambda^{*}:\\
p,q,p+r,q-r\in\text{P}_{\text{L}}\end{subarray}}\!\!\!\!\!\langle\varphi_{p+r}\otimes\varphi_{q-r},V_{\text{ren}}\varphi_{p}\otimes\varphi_{q}\rangle\langle\psi_{N},a^{*}_{p+r}a^{*}_{q-r}a_{p}a_{q}\psi_{N}\rangle\\
&\;\geq 4\pi\mathfrak{a}N^{1+\kappa}-CN^{1+2\kappa-2\alpha}-CN^{\kappa+3\alpha}=4\pi\mathfrak{a}N^{1+\kappa}+o(N)\end{split}

and from Lemma
4
, assuming
\beta=(1+\epsilon)\frac{5}{2}\kappa
for sufficiently small
\epsilon>0
, that

\begin{split}|\langle\psi_{N},R_{N}\psi_{N}\rangle|&\leq CN^{1+5\kappa-2\beta}+CN^{\frac{1}{2}+\frac{9}{2}\kappa+\frac{5}{2}\alpha-2\beta}+CN^{1+\frac{9}{2}\kappa+\frac{1}{2}\beta-\frac{3}{2}\alpha}\\
&\hskip 14.22636pt+CN^{\frac{1}{2}+4\kappa+\alpha+\frac{1}{2}\beta}+CN^{1+4\kappa+\frac{5}{2}\beta-\frac{5}{2}\alpha}\\
&=o(N)+CN^{\frac{1}{2}+\frac{5}{2}\alpha-\frac{1}{2}\kappa+O(\epsilon)}+CN^{\frac{1}{2}+\frac{21}{4}\kappa+\alpha+O(\epsilon)}=o(N).\end{split}

Combining this with the ground state energy upper bound
E_{N}\leq 4\pi\mathfrak{a}N^{1+\kappa}+o(N)
, as pointed out in the second remark after Theorem
1
, we get

\begin{split}4\pi\mathfrak{a}N^{1+\kappa}+o(N)&\geq\langle\psi_{N},H_{N}\psi_{N}\rangle\geq 4\pi\mathfrak{a}N^{1+\kappa}+4\pi^{2}\langle\psi_{N},\mathcal{N}_{+}\psi_{N}\rangle+o(N)\end{split}

and thus conclude that

\lim_{N\to\infty}N^{-1}\langle\psi_{N},\mathcal{N}_{+}\psi_{N}\rangle=\lim_{N\to\infty}\big(1-\langle\varphi_{0},\gamma_{N}^{(1)}\varphi_{0}\rangle\big)=0.\qed

Throughout this appendix, we assume
p,q,s,t\in\Lambda^{*}
and we abbreviate
\langle\mathcal{L}\rangle_{pq,st}:=\langle\varphi_{p}\otimes\varphi_{q},\mathcal{L}\,\varphi_{s}\otimes\varphi_{t}\rangle

for every operator
\mathcal{L}
on
L^{2}(\Lambda^{2})
. Let us begin with a few elementary observations: it is clear that the operator
N^{1-\kappa}V_{N}
preserves the total momentum and that

\langle N^{1-\kappa}V_{N}\rangle_{pq,st}\leq\delta_{p+q,s+t}\int_{\Lambda^{2}}dx_{1}dx_{2}\,N^{3-3\kappa}V(N^{1-\kappa}(x_{1}-x_{2}))\leq\delta_{p+q,s+t}\|V\|_{1}.

Combining this with the fact that
-\Delta_{x_{1}}-\Delta_{x_{2}}+V_{N}
and hence its pseudo-inverse

\mathcal{R}:=\Pi_{\text{H}}\big[\Pi_{\text{H}}(-\Delta_{x_{1}}-\Delta_{x_{2}}+V_{N})\Pi_{\text{H}}\big]^{-1}\Pi_{\text{H}}

from
\Pi_{\text{H}}L^{2}(\Lambda^{2})
to
\Pi_{\text{H}}L^{2}(\Lambda^{2})
also preserve the total momentum, we get that

\begin{split}\langle V_{\text{ren}}\rangle_{pq,st}&\leq C\delta_{pq,st}\sup_{p,q\in\Lambda^{*}}\Big(1+N^{1-\kappa}\big\langle V_{N}^{1/2}\Pi_{\text{H}}V_{N}^{1/2}\mathcal{R}V_{N}^{1/2}\Pi_{\text{H}}V_{N}^{1/2}\big\rangle_{pq,pq}\\
&\hskip 85.35826pt+N^{1-\kappa}\big\langle V_{N}^{1/2}\Pi_{\text{L}}V_{N}^{1/2}\mathcal{R}V_{N}^{1/2}\Pi_{\text{L}}V_{N}^{1/2}\big\rangle_{pq,pq}\Big).\end{split}

To control the right hand side, we make use of the operator inequalities

0\leq\Pi_{\text{H}}V_{N}^{1/2}\mathcal{R}V_{N}^{1/2}\Pi_{\text{H}}\leq 1\;\text{ and }\;\mathcal{R}\leq N^{-2\alpha}\Pi_{\text{H}}\leq N^{-2\alpha}.

This implies on the one hand that

N^{1-\kappa}\big\langle V_{N}^{1/2}\Pi_{\text{H}}V_{N}^{1/2}\mathcal{R}V_{N}^{1/2}\Pi_{\text{H}}V_{N}^{1/2}\big\rangle_{pq,pq}\leq\langle N^{1-\kappa}V_{N}\rangle_{pq,pq}=\|V\|_{1}

and on the other hand that

N^{1-\kappa}\big\langle V_{N}^{1/2}\Pi_{\text{L}}V_{N}^{1/2}\mathcal{R}V_{N}^{1/2}\Pi_{\text{L}}V_{N}^{1/2}\big\rangle_{pq,pq}\leq N^{-2\alpha}\|V\|_{1}\|\Pi_{\text{L}}V_{N}^{1/2}\varphi_{p}\otimes\varphi_{q}\|^{2}_{\infty}.

Looking at the Fourier expansion

\Pi_{\text{L}}V_{N}^{1/2}\varphi_{p}\otimes\varphi_{q}=\sum_{s\in\text{P}_{\text{L}}:p+q-s\in\text{P}_{\text{L}}}N^{2\kappa-2}\widehat{V^{1/2}}((s-p)/N^{1-\kappa})\varphi_{s}\otimes\varphi_{p+q-s},

the assumptions that
V\in L^{1}(\mathbb{R}^{3})
having compact support and that
\alpha\leq 1-\kappa
imply that
\|\Pi_{\text{L}}V_{N}^{1/2}\varphi_{p}\otimes\varphi_{q}\|_{\infty}\leq CN^{\alpha}
so that altogether
|\langle V_{\text{ren}}\rangle_{pq,st}|\leq C\delta_{pq,st}
.

Next, let us switch to the second bound in (
7
). We first show that

|\langle V_{\text{ren}}\rangle_{00,00}-8\pi\mathfrak{a}|\leq CN^{\alpha+\kappa-1}.

(13)

Up to minor modifications, this bound follows as in
[
16
, Appendix A]
, so let us focus on the key steps. Denote by
f
the zero energy scattering solution in
\mathbb{R}^{3}
such that

(-2\Delta+V)f=0

with
\lim_{|x|\to\infty}f(x)=1
. It is well known (see e.g.
[
30
, Appendix C]
) that
0\leq f\leq 1
, that
f
is radial and that for
x\in\mathbb{R}^{3}
outside the support of
V
, we have that
f(x)=1-\mathfrak{a}/|x|.
Moreover, a basic integration by parts shows that

8\pi\mathfrak{a}=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{3}}dx\,V(x)f(x)=\int_{\Lambda}dx\,N^{3-3\kappa}(Vf)(N^{1-\kappa}x).

Let us denote
w:=1-f
which is easily seen to satisfy the bounds

w(x)\leq\frac{C}{|x|},\hskip 14.22636pt|\widehat{w}(p)|\leq\frac{C}{1+|p|^{2}}

for some constant
C>0
(e.g. based on the identity
w=(-2\Delta)^{-1}Vf
). Moreover, pick a smooth bump function
\chi\in C^{\infty}_{c}(B_{1/2}(0))
such that
\chi(x)=1
if
|x|\leq\frac{1}{4}
and define

(x_{1},x_{2})\mapsto\phi_{N}(x_{1}-x_{2}):=\chi(x_{1}-x_{2})w(N^{1-\kappa}(x_{1}-x_{2}))\in L^{2}(\Lambda^{2}).

By slight abuse of notation, we identify
\phi_{N}
with the associated multiplication operator in
L^{2}(\Lambda^{2})
. As explained in
[
16
]
, we then have the identity

\mathcal{R}V_{N}\varphi_{0}\otimes\varphi_{0}=\phi_{N}\varphi_{0}\otimes\varphi_{0}+\mathcal{R}\zeta_{N}\varphi_{0}\otimes\varphi_{0}-(1-\mathcal{R}V_{N})\Pi_{\text{L}}\phi_{N}\varphi_{0}\otimes\varphi_{0},

where
\zeta_{N}(x_{1}-x_{2}):=N^{\kappa-1}\zeta(x_{1}-x_{2})
for

x\mapsto\zeta(x):=2\mathfrak{a}\frac{(\Delta\chi)(x)}{|x|}-4\mathfrak{a}\frac{(\nabla\chi)(x)\cdot(x)}{|x|^{3}}\in C^{\infty}_{0}\big(B_{1/2}(0)\cap\overline{B}^{c}_{1/4}(0)\big).

Using that
8\pi\mathfrak{a}=N^{1-\kappa}\langle V_{N},(1-\phi_{N})\rangle_{00,00}
, this yields

\langle V_{\text{ren}}\rangle_{00,00}=8\pi\mathfrak{a}+N^{1-\kappa}\langle\mathcal{R}V_{N},\zeta_{N}\rangle_{00,00}+N^{1-\kappa}\langle V_{N},(1-\mathcal{R}V_{N})\Pi_{\text{L}}\phi_{N}\rangle_{00,00}.

Now observe that for
|p|>N^{\alpha}
, we have that

\begin{split}\langle\mathcal{R}V_{N}\rangle_{-pp,00}&=\frac{-\langle V_{N}\mathcal{R}V_{N}\rangle_{-pp,00}}{2|p|^{2}}+\frac{\langle V_{N}-\Pi_{\text{L}}(1-V_{N}\mathcal{R})V_{N}\rangle_{-pp,00}}{2|p|^{2}}\\
&=\frac{\langle V_{N}(1-\mathcal{R}V_{N})\rangle_{-pp,00}}{2|p|^{2}}\end{split}

(14)

and otherwise
\langle\mathcal{R}V_{N}\rangle_{-pp,00}=0
(by definition of
\mathcal{R}
) s.t.
N^{1-\kappa}\langle\mathcal{R}V_{N},\zeta_{N}\rangle_{00,00}\leq CN^{\kappa-1}
. Similarly,
|\widehat{\phi}_{N}(p)|\leq CN^{\kappa-1}(1+|p|^{2})^{-1}
and Cauchy-Schwarz imply that

\begin{split}&|N^{1-\kappa}\langle V_{N},(1-\mathcal{R}V_{N})\Pi_{\text{L}}\phi_{N}\rangle_{00,00}|\\
&\;\leq C\|V\|_{1}\big(\|\Pi_{\text{L}}\phi_{N}\|_{\infty}+N^{-\alpha}\|\Pi_{\text{L}}V_{N}^{1/2}\Pi_{\text{L}}\phi_{N}\|_{\infty}\big)\leq CN^{\alpha+\kappa-1}.\end{split}

Combining the previous estimates yields (
13
).
In fact, using
N^{1-\kappa}\langle V_{N}(1-\phi_{N})\rangle_{p+r\,q-r,pq}=\widehat{Vf}(r/N^{1-\kappa})
we can also compute
\langle V_{\text{ren}}\rangle_{00,00}
to higher precision and obtain that

\begin{split}\langle V_{\text{ren}}\rangle_{00,00}=&8\pi\mathfrak{a}+\frac{N^{\kappa-1}}{2}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}0\neq s\in\text{P}_{\text{L}}\end{subarray}}\frac{(8\pi\mathfrak{a})^{2}}{|s|^{2}}+O(N^{\kappa-1})+O(N^{2\kappa+2\alpha-2}).\end{split}

We omit the details as the second term is irrelevant for our range of
\kappa
, it only becomes relevant if one wants to consider the Lee-Huang-Yang order.

To get (
7
), we combine (
13
) with two further steps. On the one hand, we have that

|\langle V_{\text{ren}}\rangle_{pq,st}-\langle V_{\text{ren}}\rangle_{(p+q)0,(s+t)0}|\leq CN^{\kappa-1}\big(|p|+|q|+|s|+|t|\big)

(15)

whenever
p+q=s+t
. This bound follows very similarly as the first bound in (
7
): since
V_{N}
is a multiplication operator, (
15
) clearly holds if we replace
V_{\text{ren}}
by
N^{1-\kappa}V_{N}
. Hence, it is enough to prove (
15
) for
V_{\text{ren}}
replaced by
N^{1-\kappa}V_{N}\mathcal{R}V_{N}
. In this case, we write

\begin{split}&N^{1-\kappa}\langle V_{N}\mathcal{R}V_{N}\rangle_{pq,st}-N^{1-\kappa}\langle V_{N}\mathcal{R}V_{N}\rangle_{(p+q)0,(s+t)0}\\
&\;=N^{1-\kappa}\langle\varphi_{p}\otimes\varphi_{q},V_{N}\mathcal{R}V_{N}(\varphi_{s}\otimes\varphi_{t}-\varphi_{s+t}\otimes\varphi_{0})\rangle\\
&\hskip 11.38092pt+N^{1-\kappa}\langle(\varphi_{p}\otimes\varphi_{q}-\varphi_{p+q}\otimes\varphi_{0}),V_{N}\mathcal{R}V_{N}\varphi_{s+t}\otimes\varphi_{0}\rangle.\end{split}

Now, given any pair
k,l\in\Lambda^{*}
, a direct computation shows that

\begin{split}N^{1-\kappa}\|V_{N}^{1/2}(\varphi_{k}\otimes\varphi_{l}-\varphi_{k+l}\otimes\varphi_{0})\|^{2}&=2\widehat{V}(0)-2\widehat{V}(l/N^{1-\kappa})\leq\frac{C|l|^{2}}{N^{2-2\kappa}}.\end{split}

Note that the last step follows from a second order Taylor expansion and the fact that
(\nabla_{p}\widehat{V}(./N^{1-\kappa})(0)=N^{2-2\kappa}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{3}}dx\,(-ix)V(x)=0
,
V
being radial. Similarly, we get

\begin{split}&N^{-2\alpha}\|\Pi_{\text{L}}V_{N}^{1/2}(\varphi_{k}\otimes\varphi_{l}-\varphi_{k+l}\otimes\varphi_{0})\|^{2}_{\infty}\\
&=N^{-2\alpha}\Big\|\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}s\in\text{P}_{\text{L}}:\\
k+l-s\in\text{P}_{\text{L}}\end{subarray}}\!\!\!\!\!\!N^{2\kappa-2}\big(\widehat{V^{1/2}}((s-k)/N^{1-\kappa})-\widehat{V^{1/2}}((s-k-l)/N^{1-\kappa})\big)\varphi_{s}\otimes\varphi_{k+l-s}\Big\|_{\infty}^{2}\\
&\leq C|l|^{2}/N^{2-2\kappa}.\end{split}

Proceeding now as in the proof of the first bound in (
7
), we obtain (
15
).

Combining (
15
) with (
13
), the second bound in (
7
) thus follows if we prove that

|\langle V_{N}\mathcal{R}V_{N}\rangle_{p0,p0}-\langle V_{N}\mathcal{R}V_{N}\rangle_{00,00}|\leq CN^{2\kappa-\alpha-2}|p|^{2}

(16)

for every
p\in\Lambda^{*}_{+}
. This can be proved similarly as detailed in
[
16
, Appendix A]
: define

-\Delta^{(p)}:=(-i\nabla_{x_{1}}+p)^{2}-\Delta_{x_{2}},\hskip 14.22636pt\mathcal{R}^{(p)}:=\Pi_{\text{H}}^{+}\big[\Pi_{\text{H}}^{+}(-\Delta^{(p)}+V_{N})\Pi_{\text{H}}^{+}\big]^{-1}\Pi_{\text{H}}^{+},

where the orthogonal projection
\Pi_{\text{H}}^{+}
maps onto

\overline{\text{span}\{\varphi_{k}\otimes\varphi_{l}:(k,l)\in(\text{P}_{\text{L}}^{2})^{c}\text{ and }l\neq 0\}}.

Notice that this ensures
\langle\xi,-\Delta^{(p)}\xi\rangle\geq 4\pi^{2}
for every
\xi\in\Pi_{\text{H}}^{+}L^{2}(\Lambda^{2})
, by construction of the projection
\Pi_{\text{H}}^{+}
. In particular,
\mathcal{R}^{(p)}
is well defined. Now, based on the observation

\langle V_{N}\mathcal{R}V_{N}\rangle_{p0,p0}=\langle V_{N}e^{-ipx_{1}}\mathcal{R}e^{ipx_{1}}V_{N}\rangle_{00,00}

and the fact that
-\Delta^{(p)}=e^{-ipx_{1}}(-\Delta_{x_{1}}-\Delta_{x_{2}})e^{ipx_{1}}
for
p\in\Lambda^{*}
, it follows that

\begin{split}&\langle V_{N}e^{-ipx_{1}}\mathcal{R}e^{ipx_{1}}V_{N}\rangle_{00,00}-\langle V_{N}\mathcal{R}^{(p)}V_{N}\rangle_{00,00}\\
&=-\langle V_{N}\mathcal{R}^{(p)}e^{-ipx_{1}}\Pi_{\text{L}}(1-V_{N}\mathcal{R})V_{N}\rangle_{00,p0}+\langle V_{N}(1-\mathcal{R}^{(p)}V_{N})\Pi_{\text{L}}^{+}e^{-ipx_{1}}\mathcal{R}V_{N}\rangle_{00,p0}.\end{split}

Here, we set
\Pi_{\text{L}}^{+}:=1-\Pi_{\text{H}}^{+}
. Since
V_{N}\mathcal{R}^{(p)}
preserves the total momentum and projects onto a subset of
(\text{P}_{\text{L}}^{2})^{c}
, we have that

\begin{split}&\langle V_{N}\mathcal{R}^{(p)}e^{-ipx_{1}}\Pi_{\text{L}}(1-V_{N}\mathcal{R})V_{N}\rangle_{00,p0}\\
&=\sum_{q\in\text{P}_{\text{L}}^{c}\cap\Lambda^{*}_{+};\,s,t\in\text{P}_{\text{L}}}\langle V_{N}\mathcal{R}^{(p)}\rangle_{00,-qq}\langle\varphi_{-q}\otimes\varphi_{q},\varphi_{s-p}\otimes\varphi_{t}\rangle\langle(1-V_{N}\mathcal{R})V_{N}\rangle_{st,p0}=0.\end{split}

On the other hand, using that
\mathcal{R}=\Pi_{\text{H}}\mathcal{R}
so that

\begin{split}\langle\mathcal{R}V_{N}\rangle_{(p-q)q,p0}&=\frac{\langle V_{N}-V_{N}\mathcal{R}V_{N}\rangle_{(p-q)q,p0}}{|p-q|^{2}+|q|^{2}}-\frac{\langle\Pi_{\text{L}}(1-V_{N}\mathcal{R})V_{N}\rangle_{(p-q)q,p0}}{|p-q|^{2}+|q|^{2}}\\
&=\frac{\langle V_{N}-V_{N}\mathcal{R}V_{N}\rangle_{(p-q)q,p0}}{|p-q|^{2}+|q|^{2}}\end{split}

if
(p-q,q)\in(\text{P}_{\text{L}}^{2})^{c}
and
\langle\mathcal{R}V_{N}\rangle_{(p-q)q,p0}=0
otherwise, we obtain that

\begin{split}\big\|\Pi_{\text{L}}^{+}e^{-ipx_{1}}\mathcal{R}V_{N}e^{ipx_{1}}\big\|_{\infty}&\leq\sum_{s\in\text{P}_{\text{L}}}|\langle\mathcal{R}V_{N}\rangle_{(p-s)s,p0}|\leq CN^{\alpha+\kappa-1},\\
\big\|\Pi_{\text{L}}^{+}V_{N}^{1/2}\Pi_{\text{L}}^{+}e^{-ipx_{1}}\mathcal{R}V_{N}e^{ipx_{1}}\big\|_{\infty}&\leq\sum_{s,t\in\text{P}_{\text{L}}}\frac{C}{N^{3-3\kappa}(1+|t|^{2})}\leq CN^{2\alpha+\kappa-1}.\end{split}

Hence, arguing similarly as in the previous steps, we find that

\begin{split}&|\langle V_{N}(1-\mathcal{R}^{(p)}V_{N})\Pi_{\text{L}}^{+}e^{-ipx_{1}}\mathcal{R}V_{N}\rangle_{00,p0}|\\
&\leq\frac{CN^{\kappa}}{N}\big(\big\|\Pi_{\text{L}}^{+}e^{-ipx_{1}}\mathcal{R}V_{N}e^{ipx_{1}}\big\|_{\infty}+N^{-\alpha}\big\|\Pi_{\text{L}}^{+}V_{N}^{1/2}\Pi_{\text{L}}^{+}e^{-ipx_{1}}\mathcal{R}V_{N}e^{ipx_{1}}\big\|_{\infty}\big)\leq CN^{\alpha+2\kappa-2}.\end{split}

Notice here that we used additionally the operator inequalities
-\Delta^{(p)}\geq N^{2\alpha}\Pi_{\text{H}}^{+}
and, as a consequence,
\mathcal{R}^{(p)}\leq N^{-2\alpha}\Pi_{\text{H}}^{+}\leq N^{-2\alpha}
in the image

\Pi_{\text{H}}^{+}\big(\textbf{1}_{\text{P}=0}\,L^{2}(\Lambda^{2})\big)=\Pi_{\text{H}}^{+}\,\overline{\text{span}\{\varphi_{s}\otimes\varphi_{-s}:s\in\Lambda^{*}\}}=\overline{\text{span}\{\varphi_{s}\otimes\varphi_{-s}:|s|>N^{\alpha}\}}

of the space of zero total momentum
\text{P}:=-i\nabla_{x_{1}}-i\nabla_{x_{2}}
under
\Pi_{\text{H}}^{+}
, and that both

V_{N}^{1/2}\Pi_{\text{L}}^{+}V_{N}^{1/2}\Pi_{\text{L}}^{+}e^{-ipx_{1}}\mathcal{R}V_{N}e^{ipx_{1}}\in L^{2}(\Lambda^{2})\;\;\text{ and }\;\;V_{N}^{1/2}\Pi_{\text{L}}^{+}V_{N}^{1/2}\in L^{2}(\Lambda^{2})

have zero total momentum.

Collecting the previous bounds, proving (
16
) reduces to proving that

|\langle V_{N}\mathcal{R}^{(p)}V_{N}\rangle_{00,00}-\langle V_{N}\mathcal{R}V_{N}\rangle_{00,00}|\leq CN^{2\kappa-\alpha-2}|p|^{2}.

To show this, we use that

|\langle\mathcal{R}^{(sp)}V_{N}\rangle_{-qq,00}|=\frac{|\langle V_{N}-V_{N}\mathcal{R}^{(sp)}V_{N}\rangle_{-qq,00}|}{|sp-q|^{2}+|q|^{2}}\leq\frac{CN^{\kappa-1}}{|sp-q|^{2}+|q|^{2}}

for all
s\in[0,1]
and
|q|>N^{\alpha}
(otherwise
\langle\mathcal{R}^{(sp)}V_{N}\rangle_{-qq,00}=0
). Together with

\langle V_{N}\mathcal{R}^{(p)}V_{N}\rangle_{00,00}=\langle V_{N}\mathcal{R}^{(-p)}V_{N}\rangle_{00,00}

and a second order Taylor expansion, we find that

|\langle V_{N}\mathcal{R}^{(p)}V_{N}\rangle_{00,00}-\langle V_{N}\mathcal{R}V_{N}\rangle_{00,00}|\leq CN^{2\kappa-2}|p|^{2}\int_{|q|>N^{\alpha}}dq\,|q|^{-4}\leq N^{2\kappa-\alpha-2}|p|^{2},

which implies (
16
) and thus (
7
).

Finally, (
8
) is a direct consequence of the identity
(-\Delta_{x_{1}}-\Delta_{x_{2}})\eta=\Pi_{\text{H}}V_{\text{ren}}\Pi_{\text{L}}

and the bound (
7
) implying that
(|p|^{2}+|q|^{2})|\langle\eta\rangle_{pq,st}|\leq C\delta_{pq,st}\textbf{1}_{(\text{P}_{\text{L}}^{2})^{c}}((p,q))\textbf{1}_{\text{P}_{\text{L}}^{2}}((s,t))
. ∎
