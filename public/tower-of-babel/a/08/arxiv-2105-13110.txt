Non-singular Morse-Smale flows on n-manifolds with attractor-repeller dynamics
O. V. Pochinka, D. D. Shubin

Abstract
In the present paper the exhaustive classification up to topological equivalence of non-singular Morse-Smale flows on n n -manifolds M n M^{n} with exactly two periodic orbits is presented. Denote by G 2 ​ ( M n ) G_{2}(M^{n}) the set of such flows. Let a flow f t : M n → M n f^{t}:M^{n}\to M^{n} belongs to the set G 2 ​ ( M n ) G_{2}(M^{n}) . Hyperbolicity of periodic orbits of f t f^{t} implies that among them one is an attracting and the other is a repelling orbit. Due to the Poincaré–Hopf theorem, the Euler characteristic of the ambient manifold M n M^{n} is zero. Only torus and Klein bottle can be ambient manifolds for f t f^{t} in case of n = 2 n=2 . The authors established that there are exactly two classes of topological equivalence of flows in G 2 ​ ( M 2 ) G_{2}(M^{2}) if M 2 M^{2} is the torus and three classes if M 2 M^{2} is the Klein bottle. For all odd-dimensional manifolds the Euler characteristic is zero. However, it is known that an orientable 3 3 -manifold admits a flow from G 2 ​ ( M 3 ) G_{2}(M^{3}) if and only if M 3 M^{3} is a lens space L p , q L_{p,q} . In this paper it is proved that every set G 2 ​ ( L p , q ) G_{2}(L_{p,q}) contains exactly two classes of topological equivalence of flows, except the case when L p , q L_{p,q} is homeomorphic to the 3-sphere 𝕊 3 \mathbb{S}^{3} or the projective space ℝ ​ P 3 \mathbb{R}P^{3} , where such a class is unique. Also, it is shown that the only non-orientable n n -manifold (for n > 2 n>2 ), which admits flows from G 2 ​ ( M n ) G_{2}(M^{n}) is the twisted I-bundle over ( n − 1 ) (n-1) -sphere 𝕊 n − 1 ​ × ~ ​ 𝕊 1 \mathbb{S}^{n-1}\tilde{\times}\mathbb{S}^{1} . Moreover, there are exactly two classes of topological equivalence of flows in G 2 ​ ( 𝕊 n − 1 ​ × ~ ​ 𝕊 1 ) G_{2}(\mathbb{S}^{n-1}\tilde{\times}\mathbb{S}^{1}) . Among orientable n n -manifolds only the product of ( n − 1 ) (n-1) -sphere and the circle 𝕊 n − 1 × 𝕊 1 \mathbb{S}^{n-1}{\times}\mathbb{S}^{1} can be ambient manifold for flows from G 2 ​ ( M n ) G_{2}(M^{n}) and G 2 ​ ( 𝕊 n − 1 × 𝕊 1 ) G_{2}(\mathbb{S}^{n-1}{\times}\mathbb{S}^{1}) splits into two topological equivalence classes.
