On Generalized Multi Poly-Euler and Multi Poly-Bernoulli Polynomials
Roberto B. Corcino, Hassan Jolany, Cristina B. Corcino and Takao Komatsu

Abstract
In this paper, we establish more identities of generalized multi poly-Euler polynomials with three parameters and obtain a kind of symmetrized generalization of the polynomials. Moreover, generalized multi poly-Bernoulli polynomials are defined using multiple polylogarithm and derive some properties parallel to those of poly-Bernoulli polynomials. These are generalized further using the concept of Hurwitz-Lerch multiple zeta values. Mathematics Subject Classification (2010). 11B68, 11B73, 05A15. Keywords : multi poly-Euler polynomials, Appell polynomials, multiple polylogarithm, poly-Bernoulli polynomial, Hurwitz-Lerch multiple zeta value, generating function.



1
Introduction

The Euler numbers, denoted by
E_{n}
, are usually introduced as the coefficients of the generating function

\frac{1}{\cosh t}=\frac{2e^{t}}{e^{2t}+1}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}E_{n}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

Eventually, these numbers have been generalized in polynomial form as

\frac{2e^{xt}}{e^{t}+1}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}E_{n}(x)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

where
E_{n}(x)
denote the so-called
Euler polynomials
.

Euler numbers and polynomials have a rich literature in the history of mathematics where bunch of identities and properties have been established including their mathematical and physical applications. These numbers and polynomials have close connections with Bernoulli numbers and polynomials, particularly, in the structures of their properties and generalizations. In fact, in almost every property and generalization of Bernoulli numbers and polynomials there corresponds property and generalization for Euler numbers and polynomials. For instance, Kaneko
[
18
]
introduced the poly-Bernoulli numbers
B^{(k)}_{n}
by means of the following exponential generating function

\frac{{\rm Li}_{k}(1-e^{-x})}{1-e^{-x}}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}B^{(k)}_{n}\frac{x^{n}}{n!}

where

{\rm Li}_{k}(z)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{z^{n}}{n^{k}},

while Ohno and Sasaki
[
21
]
defined poly-Euler numbers as

\frac{{\rm Li}_{k}(1-e^{-4t})}{4t\cosh t}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}E^{(k)}_{n}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

which have been recently extended by H. Jolany et al.
[
17
]
in polynomial form as

\frac{2{\rm Li}_{k}(1-e^{-t})}{1+e^{t}}e^{xt}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}E^{(k)}_{n}(x)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

(1)

It is worth-mentioning that the above generalization of Kaneko has been generalized further by Cenkci and Young
[
7
]
using the concept of Hurwitz-Lerch zeta function
\Phi(z,s,a)
as follows

\Phi(1-e^{-t},k,a)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}B_{n,a}^{(k)}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

(2)

where

\Phi(z,k,a)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{z^{n}}{(n+a)^{k}}.

(3)

The numbers
B_{n,a}^{(k)}
are called the Hurwitz type poly Bernoulli numbers. These numbers have been shown to have explicit formula

B_{n,a}^{(k)}=(-1)^{n}\sum_{m=0}^{n}\frac{(-1)^{m}m!S(n,m)}{(m+a)^{k}}

(4)

where
S(n,m)
denotes the Stirling numbers of the second kind. A parallel version of generalization for Euler numbers is still to be done. However, it is expected that the structure is quite complicated. For instance, one may define it as

\frac{2(1-e^{-t})\Phi(1-e^{-t},k,a)}{1+e^{t}}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}E_{n,a}^{(k)}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

(5)

The numbers
E_{n,a}^{(k)}
may be called the Hurwitz type poly Euler numbers. One can easily derive the explicit formula for
E_{n,a}^{(k)}
as follows

E_{j+1,a}^{(k)}=\sum_{n=j}^{\infty}\sum_{r=j}^{n}\sum_{q=j}^{r}\sum_{m=0}^{n-r}\frac{(-1)^{n-r+m+q+1}\binom{j+1}{q-n+j+1,\;r-q,\;n-r}(n-j)^{r-q}m!S(n-r,m)}{(m+a)^{k}}.

(6)

We notice that the explicit formula of
E_{n,a}^{(k)}
is more complicated than that of
B_{n,a}^{(k)}
.

Another form of generalization of Bernoulli numbers has been defined by Imatomi et al.
[
13
]
in terms of multiple polylogarithm as follows

\frac{{\rm Li}_{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}(1-e^{-t})}{1-e^{-t}}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}{B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

(7)

where

{\rm Li}_{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}(z)=\sum_{0<m_{1}<m_{2}<\ldots<m_{r}}\frac{z^{m_{r}}}{m_{1}^{k_{1}}m_{2}^{k_{2}}\ldots m_{r}^{k_{r}}}.

(8)

These numbers possess respectively the following recurrence relation and explicit formula

\displaystyle{B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{n+1}\left({B}^{(k_{1}-1,k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}-\sum_{m=1}^{n-1}\binom{n}{m-1}{B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{m}\right)

(9)

\displaystyle{B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}

\displaystyle=(-1)^{n}\sum_{n+1\geq m_{1}>m_{2}>\ldots>m_{r}>0}\frac{(-1)^{m_{1}-1}(m_{1}-1)!S(n,m_{1}-1)}{m_{1}^{k_{1}}m_{1}^{k_{2}}\ldots m_{r}^{k_{r}}}.

(10)

Parallel to the above generalization is the generalized multi poly-Euler polynomials which are denoted by
{E}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x;a,b,c)
. These polynomials have been introduced in
[
16
]
by means of the above multiple poly-logarithm, also known as multiple zeta values. More precisely, we have

\frac{2{\rm Li}_{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}(1-(ab)^{-t})}{(a^{-t}+b^{t})^{r}}c^{rxt}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}{E}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x;a,b,c)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

(11)

When
r=1
, (
11
) boils down to the generalized poly-Euler polynomials with three parameters
a,b,c
. Moreover, when
c=e
, (
11
) reduces to the multi poly-Euler polynomials with two parameters
a,b
. These special cases have been discussed intensively in
[
16
,
11
]
.

Some properties of generalized multi poly-Euler polynomials
{E}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x;a,b,c)
are established in
[
16
]
which include the following identities

\displaystyle{E}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x;a,b,c)

\displaystyle=\sum_{i=0}^{n}\binom{n}{i}(r\ln c)^{n-i}{E}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{i}(a,b)x^{n-i}

(12)

\displaystyle{E}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x;a,b,c)

\displaystyle=(\ln a+\ln b)^{n}{E}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}\left(\frac{rx\ln c+\ln a}{\ln a+\ln b}\right)

(13)

\displaystyle\frac{d}{dx}{E}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n+1}(x;a,b,c)

\displaystyle=(n+1)(r\ln c){E}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x;a,b,c)

\displaystyle{E}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x+y;a,b,c)

\displaystyle=\sum_{i=0}^{n}\binom{n}{i}(r\ln c)^{n-i}{E}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{i}(x;a,b,c)y^{n-i}.

Furthermore, an explicit formula for
{E}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x;a,b,c)
is given by

{E}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x;a,b,c)=\sum_{i=0}^{n}\sum_{0<m_{1}<m_{2}<\ldots<m_{r}\atop c_{1}+c_{2}+\ldots=r}\mathcal{J}(m_{1},m_{2},\ldots,m_{r})

(14)

where

\mathcal{J}(m_{1},m_{2},\ldots,m_{r})=\sum_{j=0}^{m_{r}}\frac{2(rx\ln c-j\ln ab)^{n-i}r!(-1)^{j+s}(s\ln ab+r\ln a)^{i}\binom{m_{r}}{j}\binom{n}{i}}{(c_{1}!c_{2}!\ldots)(m_{1}^{k_{1}}m_{2}^{k_{2}}\ldots m_{r}^{k_{r}})}

with
s=c_{1}+2c_{2}+\ldots
.
When
r=1
, these identities reduce to

\displaystyle{E}^{(k)}_{n}(x;a,b,c)

\displaystyle=\sum_{i=0}^{n}\binom{n}{i}(\ln c)^{n-i}{E}^{(k)}_{i}(a,b)x^{n-i}

\displaystyle{E}^{(k)}_{n}(x;a,b,c)

\displaystyle=(\ln a+\ln b)^{n}{E}^{(k)}_{n}\left(\frac{x\ln c+\ln a}{\ln a+\ln b}\right)

\displaystyle\frac{d}{dx}{E}^{(k)}_{n+1}(x;a,b,c)

\displaystyle=(n+1)(\ln c){E}^{(k)}_{n}(x;a,b,c)

\displaystyle{E}^{(k)}_{n}(x+y;a,b,c)

\displaystyle=\sum_{i=0}^{n}\binom{n}{i}(\ln c)^{n-i}{E}^{(k)}_{i}(x;a,b,c)y^{n-i}

which are properties of generalized poly-Euler polynomials (see
[
16
]
).

In this paper, some identities of
{E}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x;a,b,c)
related to Stirling numbers of the second kind are established and certain symmetrized generalization for
{E}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x;a,b,c)
is obtained. On the other hand, generalized multi poly-Bernoulli polynomials are defined and some properties of these polynomials are established parallel to those of the poly-Bernoulli polynomials. Moreover, certain generalization of multi poly-Bernoulli numbers is defined in terms of generalized Hurwitz-Lerch multiple zeta values.

2
Generalized Multi Poly-Euler Polynomials and Stirling Numbers

The generalized poly-Euler polynomials with three parameters
a
,
b
and
c
are defined in
[
16
]
as follows

\frac{2{\rm Li}_{k}(1-(ab)^{-t})}{a^{-t}+b^{t}}c^{xt}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}{E}^{(k)}_{n}(x;a,b,c)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

(15)

Some identities on generalized poly-Euler polynomials are expressed in terms of Stirling numbers of the second kind. Such identities have appeared in Theorem 2.6 of
[
16
]
but with
c=e
. More precisely, we have the following theorem.

Theorem 2.1
.

[
16
]

The generalized poly-Euler polynomials
{E}^{(k)}_{n}(x;a,b)
satisfy the following explicit formulas

\displaystyle{E}^{(k)}_{n}(x;a,b)

\displaystyle=\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\sum_{l=m}^{n}\genfrac{}{}{0.0pt}{0}{l}{m}\binom{n}{l}{E}^{(k)}_{n-l}(-m;a,b)(x)^{(m)}

(16)

\displaystyle{E}^{(k)}_{n}(x;a,b)

\displaystyle=\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\sum_{l=m}^{n}\genfrac{}{}{0.0pt}{0}{l}{m}\binom{n}{l}{E}^{(k)}_{n-l}(0;a,b)(x)_{m}

(17)

\displaystyle{E}^{(k)}_{n}(x;a,b)

\displaystyle=\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\binom{n}{m}\sum_{l=0}^{n-m}\frac{\binom{n-m}{l}}{\binom{l+s}{l}}\genfrac{}{}{0.0pt}{0}{l+s}{s}{E}^{(k)}_{n-m-l}(0;a,b)B^{(s)}_{m}(x)

(18)

\displaystyle{E}^{(k)}_{n}(x;a,b)

\displaystyle=\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\frac{\binom{n}{m}}{(1-\lambda)^{s}}\sum_{j=0}^{s}\binom{s}{j}(-\lambda)^{s-j}{E}^{(k)}_{n-m}(j;a,b)H^{(s)}_{m}(x;\lambda),

(19)

where
(x)^{(n)}=x(x+1)\ldots(x+n-1)
,
(x)_{n}=x(x-1)\ldots(x-n+1)
,

\left(\frac{t}{e^{t}-1}\right)^{s}e^{xt}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}B^{(s)}_{n}(x)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}\mbox{ and }\left(\frac{1-\lambda}{e^{t}-\lambda}\right)^{s}e^{xt}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}H^{(s)}_{n}(x;\lambda)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

Here, we derive some identities for
{E}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x;a,b,c)
which are parallel to those in Theorem
2.1
. The first such identity is given in the following theorem.

Theorem 2.2
.

{E}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x;a,b,c)=\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\sum_{l=m}^{n}(r\log c)^{l}\genfrac{}{}{0.0pt}{0}{l}{m}\binom{n}{l}{E}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n-l}(-m\log c;a,b)(x)^{(m)}.

(20)

Proof.

Note that (
11
) can be written as

\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}{E}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x;a,b,c)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}=\frac{2{\rm Li}_{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}(1-(ab)^{-t})}{(a^{-t}+b^{t})^{r}}(1-(1-e^{-rt\log c}))^{-x}

Using Newton’s Binomial Theorem, we have

\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}{E}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x;a,b)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}=\frac{2{\rm Li}_{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}(1-(ab)^{-t})}{(a^{-t}+b^{t})^{r}}\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\binom{x+m-1}{m}(1-e^{-rt\log c})^{m}

\displaystyle=\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}(x)^{(m)}\frac{(e^{rt\log c}-1)^{m}}{m!}\frac{2{\rm Li}_{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}(1-(ab)^{-t})}{(a^{-t}+b^{t})^{r}}e^{-mrt\log c}

\displaystyle=\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}(x)^{(m)}\left(\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\genfrac{}{}{0.0pt}{0}{n}{m}\frac{(rt\log c)^{n}}{n!}\right)\left(\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}{E}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(-mr\log c;a,b)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}\right)

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left\{\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\sum_{l=m}^{n}(r\log c)^{l}\genfrac{}{}{0.0pt}{0}{l}{m}\binom{n}{l}{E}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n-l}(-mr\log c;a,b)(x)^{(m)}\right\}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

Comparing coefficients completes the proof of (
20
).
∎

In particular, when
c=e
and
r=1
, (
20
) yields (
16
). For the generalization of (
17
), we have the following theorem.

Theorem 2.3
.

{E}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x;a,b,c)=\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\sum_{l=m}^{n}(r\log c)^{l}\genfrac{}{}{0.0pt}{0}{l}{m}\binom{n}{l}{E}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n-l}(0;a,b)(x)^{(m)}.

(21)

Proof.

Note that (
11
) can be written as

\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}{E}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x;a,b,c)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}=\frac{2{\rm Li}_{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}(1-(ab)^{-t})}{(a^{-t}+b^{t})^{r}}((e^{rt\log c}-1)+1)^{x}

Using Newton’s Binomial Theorem, we have

\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}{E}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x;a,b)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}=\frac{2{\rm Li}_{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}(1-(ab)^{-t})}{(a^{-t}+b^{t})^{r}}\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\binom{x}{m}(e^{rt\log c}-1)^{m}

\displaystyle=\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}(x)_{m}\frac{(e^{rt\log c}-1)^{m}}{m!}\frac{2{\rm Li}_{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}(1-(ab)^{-t})}{(a^{-t}+b^{t})^{r}}

\displaystyle=\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}(x)_{m}\left(\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\genfrac{}{}{0.0pt}{0}{n}{m}\frac{(rt\log c)^{n}}{n!}\right)\left(\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}{E}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(0;a,b)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}\right)

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left\{\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\sum_{l=m}^{n}(r\log c)^{l}\genfrac{}{}{0.0pt}{0}{l}{m}\binom{n}{l}{E}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n-l}(0;a,b)(x)_{m}\right\}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

Comparing coefficients completes the proof of (
21
).
∎

Theorem 2.4
.

{E}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x;a,b,c)=\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\binom{n}{m}\sum_{l=0}^{n-m}\frac{\binom{n-m}{l}}{\binom{l+s}{l}}\genfrac{}{}{0.0pt}{0}{l+s}{s}{E}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n-m-l}(0;a,b)B^{(s)}_{m}(xr\log c).

(22)

Proof.

Note that (
11
) can be written as

\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}{E}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x;a,b,c)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}=\left(\frac{(e^{t}-1)^{s}}{s!}\right)\left(\frac{t^{s}e^{xrt\log c}}{(e^{t}-1)^{s}}\right)\left(\frac{2{\rm Li}_{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}(1-(ab)^{-t})}{(a^{-t}+b^{t})^{r}}\right)\frac{s!}{t^{s}}

\displaystyle=\left(\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\genfrac{}{}{0.0pt}{0}{n+s}{s}\frac{t^{n+s}}{(n+s)!}\right)\left(\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}B^{(s)}_{m}(xr\log c)\frac{t^{m}}{m!}\right)\left(\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}{E}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(0;a,b)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}\right)\frac{s!}{t^{s}}

\displaystyle=\left(\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\genfrac{}{}{0.0pt}{0}{n+s}{s}\frac{t^{n+s}}{(n+s)!}\right)\left(\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\sum_{n=m}^{\infty}B^{(s)}_{m}(xr\log c)\frac{t^{m}}{m!}{E}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n-m}(0;a,b)\frac{t^{n-m}}{(n-m)!}\right)\frac{s!}{t^{s}}

\displaystyle=\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\left\{\sum_{n=m}^{\infty}\sum_{l=0}^{n-m}\genfrac{}{}{0.0pt}{0}{l+s}{s}\frac{t^{l+s}}{(l+s)!}B^{(s)}_{m}(xr\log c){E}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n-m-l}(0;a,b)\frac{t^{n-m-l}}{(n-m-l)!}\frac{t^{m}}{m!}\frac{s!}{t^{s}}\right\}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left\{\sum_{m=0}^{n}\binom{n}{m}\sum_{l=0}^{n-m}\frac{\binom{n-m}{l}}{\binom{l+s}{s!}}\genfrac{}{}{0.0pt}{0}{l+s}{s}{E}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n-m-l}(0;a,b)B^{(s)}_{m}(xr\log c)\right\}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

Comparing coefficients completes the proof of (
22
).
∎

Theorem 2.5
.

{E}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x;a,b,c)=\sum_{m=0}^{n}\frac{\binom{n}{m}}{(1-\lambda)^{s}}\sum_{j=0}^{s}\binom{s}{j}(-\lambda)^{s-j}{E}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n-m}(j;a,b)H^{(s)}_{m}(xr\log c;\lambda).

(23)

Proof.

Note that (
11
) can be written as

\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}{E}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x;a,b,c)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}=\left(\frac{(1-\lambda)^{s}}{(e^{t}-\lambda)^{s}}e^{xrt\log c}\right)\left(\frac{(e^{t}-\lambda)^{s}}{(1-\lambda)^{s}}\right)\left(\frac{2{\rm Li}_{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}(1-(ab)^{-t})}{(a^{-t}+b^{t})^{r}}\right)

\displaystyle=\left(\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}H^{(s)}_{n}(xr\log c;\lambda)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}\right)\left(\sum_{j=0}^{s}\binom{s}{j}(-\lambda)^{s-j}\frac{2{\rm Li}_{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}(1-(ab)^{-t})}{(a^{-t}+b^{t})^{r}}e^{jt}\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{(1-\lambda)^{s}}\sum_{j=0}^{s}\binom{s}{j}(-\lambda)^{s-j}\left(\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}H^{(s)}_{n}(xr\log c;\lambda)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}\right)\left(\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}{E}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(j;a,b)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{(1-\lambda)^{s}}\sum_{j=0}^{s}\binom{s}{j}(-\lambda)^{s-j}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\sum_{m=0}^{n}\binom{n}{m}H^{(s)}_{m}(xr\log c;\lambda){E}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n-m}(j;a,b)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left(\sum_{m=0}^{n}\frac{\binom{n}{m}}{(1-\lambda)^{s}}\sum_{j=0}^{s}\binom{s}{j}(-\lambda)^{s-j}{E}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n-m}(j;a,b)H^{(s)}_{m}(xr\log c;\lambda)\right)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

Comparing coefficients completes the proof of (
23
).
∎

The next theorem contains an identity which is obtained by making use of the following differential formula for the generalized poly-logarithm (Hamahata and Masubuchi, Integers)

\frac{d}{dz}{\rm Li}_{k_{1},\dots,k_{r}}(z)=\begin{cases}\frac{1}{z}{\rm Li}_{k_{1},\dots,k_{r-1},k_{r}-1}(z)&\text{if $k_{r}>1$};\\
\frac{1}{1-z}{\rm Li}_{k_{1},\dots,k_{r-1}}(z)&\text{if $k_{r}=1$}.\end{cases}

(24)

Theorem 2.6
.

If
k_{r}>1
, then

\displaystyle E_{n+1}^{(k_{1},\dots,k_{r})}(x)

\displaystyle-r\log(ac^{x})E_{n}^{(k_{1},\dots,k_{r})}(x)

\displaystyle=r\sum_{\nu=0}^{\infty}\binom{n}{\nu}\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\bigl(-j\log(ab)\bigr)^{n-\nu}E_{\nu}^{(k_{1},\dots,k_{r})}(x)

\displaystyle-\log(ab)\sum_{\nu=0}^{\infty}\binom{n}{\nu}\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\bigl(j\log(ab)\bigr)^{n-\nu}E_{\nu}^{(k_{1},\dots,k_{r-1},k_{r}-1)}(x)\,.\qquad\qquad\quad\;\;\;

If
k_{r}=1
, then

\displaystyle E_{n+1}^{(k_{1},\dots,k_{r})}(x)

\displaystyle-r\log(ac^{x})E_{n}^{(k_{1},\dots,k_{r})}(x)

\displaystyle=r\sum_{\nu=0}^{\infty}\binom{n}{\nu}\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\bigl(-j\log(ab)\bigr)^{n-\nu}E_{\nu}^{(k_{1},\dots,k_{r})}(x)+\log(ab)E_{n}^{(k_{1},\dots,k_{r-1})}(x)\,.

Proof.

We differentiate both sides of

2(ac^{x})^{rt}{\rm Li}_{k_{1},\dots,k_{r}}\bigl(1-(ab)^{-t}\bigr)=\bigl(1+(ab)^{t}\bigr)^{r}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}E_{n}^{(k_{1},\dots,k_{r})}(x)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

with respect to
t
using (
24
). If
k_{1}\neq 1
, then

\displaystyle 2r(ac^{x})^{rt}\log(ac^{x})

\displaystyle{\rm Li}_{k_{1},\dots,k_{r}}\bigl(1-(ab)^{-t}\bigr)

\displaystyle+2(ac^{x})^{rt}

\displaystyle\frac{-(ab)^{-t}\log(ab)}{1-(ab)^{-t}}{\rm Li}_{k_{1},\dots,k_{r-1},k_{r}-1}\bigl(1-(ab)^{-t}\bigr)

\displaystyle=r\bigl(1+(ab)^{t}\bigr)^{r-1}(ab)^{t}\log(ab)\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}E_{n}^{(k_{1},\dots,k_{r})}(x)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

\displaystyle\;\;\;\;+\bigl(1+(ab)^{t}\bigr)^{r}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}E_{n}^{(k_{1},\dots,k_{r})}(x)\frac{t^{n-1}}{(n-1)!}\,.

Dividing both sides by
\bigl(1+(ab)^{t}\bigr)^{r}
, we have

\displaystyle r\log(ac^{x})\frac{2(ac^{x})^{rt}}{\bigl(1+(ab)^{t}\bigr)^{r}}

\displaystyle{\rm Li}_{k_{1},\dots,k_{r}}\bigl(1-(ab)^{-t}\bigr)

\displaystyle+\frac{\log(ab)}{1-(ab)^{t}}

\displaystyle\frac{2(ac^{x})^{rt}}{\bigl(1+(ab)^{t}\bigr)^{r}}{\rm Li}_{k_{1},\dots,k_{r-1},k_{r}-1}\bigl(1-(ab)^{-t}\bigr)

\displaystyle=\frac{r(ab)^{t}}{1+(ab)^{t}}\log(ab)\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}E_{n}^{(k_{1},\dots,k_{r})}(x)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}+\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}E_{n+1}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}\,.

Since

\displaystyle\frac{\log(ab)}{1-(ab)^{t}}

\displaystyle=\log(ab)\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}(ab)^{jt}=\log(ab)\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}e^{jt\log(ab)}

\displaystyle=\log(ab)\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\sum_{\nu=0}^{\infty}\frac{\bigl(j\log(ab)\bigr)^{\nu}}{\nu!}t^{\nu}

and

\displaystyle\frac{r(ab)^{t}}{1+(ab)^{t}}

\displaystyle=r\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}(ab)^{-jt}

\displaystyle=r\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\sum_{\nu=0}^{\infty}\frac{\bigl(-j\log(ab)\bigr)^{\nu}}{\nu!}t^{\nu}\,,

we obtain

\displaystyle r\log(ac^{x})\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}

\displaystyle E_{n}^{(k_{1},\dots,k_{r})}(x)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

\displaystyle+\log(ab)

\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\sum_{\nu=0}^{\infty}\bigl(j\log(ab)\bigr)^{\nu}\frac{t^{\nu}}{\nu!}\sum_{\mu=0}^{\infty}E_{\mu}^{(k_{1},\dots,k_{r-1},k_{r}-1)}(x)\frac{t^{\mu}}{\mu!}

\displaystyle=r\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\sum_{\nu=0}^{\infty}\bigl(-j\log(ab)\bigr)^{\nu}\frac{t^{\nu}}{\nu!}\sum_{\mu=0}^{\infty}E_{\mu}\frac{t^{\mu}}{\mu!}+\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}E_{n+1}^{(k_{1},\dots,k_{r})}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}\,.

Hence,

\displaystyle-r\log(ac^{x})\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}

\displaystyle E_{n}^{(k_{1},\dots,k_{r})}(x)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}+\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}E_{n+1}^{(k_{1},\dots,k_{r})}(x)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

\displaystyle=r\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left(\sum_{\nu=0}^{\infty}\binom{n}{\nu}\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\bigl(-j\log(ab)\bigr)^{n-\nu}E_{\nu}^{(k_{1},\dots,k_{r})}(x)\right)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

\displaystyle\;\;\;\;-\log(ab)\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left(\sum_{\nu=0}^{\infty}\binom{n}{\nu}\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\bigl(j\log(ab)\bigr)^{n-\nu}E_{\nu}^{(k_{1},\dots,k_{r-1},k_{r}-1)}(x)\right)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}\,.

Comparing the coefficients on both sides, we have

\displaystyle E_{n+1}^{(k_{1},\dots,k_{r})}(x)

\displaystyle-r\log(ac^{x})E_{n}^{(k_{1},\dots,k_{r})}(x)

\displaystyle=r\sum_{\nu=0}^{\infty}\binom{n}{\nu}\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\bigl(-j\log(ab)\bigr)^{n-\nu}E_{\nu}^{(k_{1},\dots,k_{r})}(x)

\displaystyle\;\;\;\;-\log(ab)\sum_{\nu=0}^{\infty}\binom{n}{\nu}\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\bigl(j\log(ab)\bigr)^{n-\nu}E_{\nu}^{(k_{1},\dots,k_{r-1},k_{r}-1)}(x)\,.

If
k_{r}=1
, then the second term on the left-hand side becomes

2(ac^{x})^{rt}\frac{-(ab)^{-t}\log(ab)}{(ab)^{-t}}{\rm Li}_{k_{1},\dots,k_{r-1}}\,.

After dividing of
\bigl(1+(ab)^{t}\bigr)^{r}
, this second term becomes

\displaystyle-\log(ab)

\displaystyle\frac{2(ac^{x})^{rt}}{\bigl(1+(ab)^{t}\bigr)^{r}}{\rm Li}_{k_{1},\dots,k_{r-1}}

\displaystyle=-\log(ab)\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}E_{n}^{(k_{1},\dots,k_{r-1})}(x)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}\,.

Finally, we get

\displaystyle E_{n+1}^{(k_{1},\dots,k_{r})}(x)

\displaystyle-r\log(ac^{x})E_{n}^{(k_{1},\dots,k_{r})}(x)

\displaystyle=r\sum_{\nu=0}^{\infty}\binom{n}{\nu}\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\bigl(-j\log(ab)\bigr)^{n-\nu}E_{\nu}^{(k_{1},\dots,k_{r})}(x)+\log(ab)E_{n}^{(k_{1},\dots,k_{r-1})}(x)\,.

∎

The generalized multi poly-Bernoulli polynomials satisfy the following identities.

\displaystyle{B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x;a,b,c)

\displaystyle=\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\sum_{l=m}^{n}(r\log c)^{l}\genfrac{}{}{0.0pt}{0}{l}{m}\binom{n}{l}{B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n-l}(-m\log c;a,b)(x)^{(m)}

\displaystyle{B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x;a,b,c)

\displaystyle=\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\sum_{l=m}^{n}(r\log c)^{l}\genfrac{}{}{0.0pt}{0}{l}{m}\binom{n}{l}{B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n-l}(0;a,b)(x)^{(m)}

\displaystyle{B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x;a,b,c)

\displaystyle=\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\binom{n}{m}\sum_{l=0}^{n-m}\frac{\binom{n-m}{l}}{\binom{l+s}{l}}\genfrac{}{}{0.0pt}{0}{l+s}{s}{B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n-m-l}(0;a,b)B^{(s)}_{m}(xr\log c)

\displaystyle{B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x;a,b,c)

\displaystyle=\sum_{m=0}^{n}\frac{\binom{n}{m}}{(1-\lambda)^{s}}\sum_{j=0}^{s}\binom{s}{j}(-\lambda)^{s-j}{B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n-m}(j;a,b)H^{(s)}_{m}(xr\log c;\lambda)

The next theorem contains an explicit formula for
{B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x;a,b,c)
.

Theorem 4.3
.

(
Explicit Formula
)
For
k\in\mathbb{Z}
,
n\geq 0
, we have

{B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x;a,b,c)=\sum_{m_{r}>\ldots>m_{1}>0}\frac{1}{m_{1}^{k_{1}}m_{2}^{k_{2}}\ldots m_{r}^{k_{r}}}\sum_{j=0}^{m_{r}-r}(-1)^{j}\binom{m_{r}-r}{j}(rx-j\ln a-(j+1)\ln b)^{n}.

(30)

Proof.

\displaystyle\frac{{\rm Li}_{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}(1-(ab)^{-t})}{(b^{t}-a^{-t})^{r}}

\displaystyle=b^{-rt}\left(\sum_{m_{r}>\ldots>m_{1}>0}\frac{(1-(ab)^{-t})^{m_{r}-r}}{m_{1}^{k_{1}}m_{2}^{k_{2}}\ldots m_{r}^{k_{r}}}\right)

\displaystyle=b^{-rt}\sum_{m_{r}>\ldots>m_{1}>0}\frac{1}{m_{1}^{k_{1}}m_{2}^{k_{2}}\ldots m_{r}^{k_{r}}}\sum_{j=0}^{m_{r}-r}(-1)^{j}\binom{m_{r}-r}{j}e^{-jt\ln(ab)}

\displaystyle=\sum_{m_{r}>\ldots>m_{1}>0}\frac{1}{m_{1}^{k_{1}}m_{2}^{k_{2}}\ldots m_{r}^{k_{r}}}\sum_{j=0}^{m_{r}-r}(-1)^{j}\binom{m_{r}-r}{j}e^{-t(j\ln a+(j+1)\ln b)}.

So, we get

\frac{{\rm Li}_{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}(1-(ab)^{-t})}{(b^{t}-a^{-t})^{r}}e^{xrt\ln c}\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad

\displaystyle=\sum_{m_{r}>\ldots>m_{1}>0}\frac{1}{m_{1}^{k_{1}}m_{2}^{k_{2}}\ldots m_{r}^{k_{r}}}\sum_{j=0}^{m_{r}-r}(-1)^{j}\binom{m_{r}-r}{j}e^{t(rx-j\ln a-(j+1)\ln b)}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left(\sum_{m_{r}>\ldots>m_{1}>0}\frac{1}{m_{1}^{k_{1}}m_{2}^{k_{2}}\ldots m_{r}^{k_{r}}}\sum_{j=0}^{m_{r}-r}(-1)^{j}\binom{m_{r}-r}{j}(rx-j\ln a-(j+1)\ln b)^{n}\right)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

By comparing the coefficients of
\frac{t^{n}}{n!}
on both sides, the proof is completed.
∎

The next theorem contains an expression of
{B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x;a,b,c)
as polynomial in
x
.

Theorem 4.4
.

The generalized multi poly-Bernoulli polynomials satisfy the following relation

{B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x;a,b,c)=\sum_{i=0}^{n}\binom{n}{i}(\ln c)^{n-i}{B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{i}(a,b)x^{n-i}

(31)

Proof.

Using (
29
), we have

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}{B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x;a,b,c)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{{\rm Li}_{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}(1-(ab)^{-t})}{(b^{t}-a^{-t})^{r}}c^{xt}=e^{xt\ln c}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}{B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(a,b)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\sum_{i=0}^{n}\frac{(xt\ln c)^{n-i}}{(n-i)!}{B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{i}(a,b)\frac{t^{i}}{i!}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left(\sum_{i=0}^{n}\binom{n}{i}(\ln c)^{n-i}{B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{i}(a,b)x^{n-i}\right)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

Comparing the coefficients of
\frac{t^{n}}{n!}
, we obtain the desired result.
∎

Note that, when
a=c=e
and
b=1
, Definition
4.1
reduces to

\frac{{\rm Li}_{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}(1-e^{-t})}{(1-e^{-t})^{r}}e^{rxt}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}{B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

(32)

The following theorem gives a relation between
{B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x;a,b,c)
and
{B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x)
.

Theorem 4.5
.

The generalized multi poly-Bernoulli polynomials satisfy the following relation

{B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x;a,b,c)=(\ln a+\ln b)^{n}{B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}\left(\frac{x\ln c-r\ln b}{\ln a+\ln b}\right)

(33)

Proof.

Using (
29
), we have

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}{B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x;a,b,c)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{{\rm Li}_{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}(1-(ab)^{-t})}{b^{rt}(1-(ab)^{-t})^{r}}e^{xt\ln c}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle e^{\frac{x\ln c-r\ln b}{\ln ab}t\ln ab}\frac{{\rm Li}_{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}(1-e^{-t\ln ab})}{1+e^{-t\ln ab}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(\ln a+\ln b)^{n}{B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}\left(\frac{x\ln c-r\ln b}{\ln a+\ln b}\right)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

Comparing the coefficients of
\frac{t^{n}}{n!}
, we obtain the desired result.
∎

Theorem 4.6
.

The generalized poly-Bernoulli polynomials satisfy the following relation

\frac{d}{dx}{B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n+1}(x;a,b,c)=(n+1)(\ln c){B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x;a,b,c)

(34)

Proof.

Using (
29
), we have

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{d}{dx}{B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x;a,b,c)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{t(\ln c){\rm Li}_{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}(1-(ab)^{-t})}{(b^{t}-a^{-t})^{r}}e^{xrt\ln c}

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{d}{dx}{B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x;a,b,c)\frac{t^{n-1}}{n!}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(r\ln c){B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x;a,b,c)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

Hence,

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{n+1}\frac{d}{dx}{B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n+1}(x;a,b,c)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(\ln c){B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x;a,b,c)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

Comparing the coefficients of
\frac{t^{n}}{n!}
, we obtain the desired result.
∎

The following corollary immediately follows from Theorem
4.6
by taking
c=e
. For brevity, let us denote
{B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x;a,b,e)
by
{B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x;a,b)
.

Corollary 4.7
.

The generalized poly-Bernoulli polynomials are Appell polynomials in the sense that

\frac{d}{dx}{B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n+1}(x;a,b)=(n+1){B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x;a,b)

(35)

Consequently, using the characterization of Appell polynomials
[
20
,
22
,
23
]
, the following addition formula can easily be obtained.

Corollary 4.8
.

The generalized poly-Bernoulli polynomials satisfy the following addition formula

{B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x+y;a,b)=\sum_{i=0}^{n}\binom{n}{i}{B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{i}(x;a,b)y^{n-i}

(36)

However, we can derive the addition formula for
{B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x;a,b,c)
as follows

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}{B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x+y;a,b,c)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{{\rm Li}_{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}(1-(ab)^{-t})}{(b^{t}-a^{-t})^{r}}c^{(x+y)rt}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{{\rm Li}_{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}(1-(ab)^{-t})}{(b^{t}-a^{-t})^{r}}c^{xrt}c^{yrt}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left(\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}{B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x;a,b,c)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}\right)\left(\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(yr\ln c)^{n}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left(\sum_{i=0}^{n}\binom{n}{i}(yr\ln c)^{n-i}{B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{i}(x;a,b,c)\right)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

Comparing the coefficients of
\frac{t^{n}}{n!}
yields the following result.

Theorem 4.9
.

The generalized poly-Bernoulli polynomials satisfy the following addition formula

{B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}(x+y;a,b,c)=\sum_{i=0}^{n}\binom{n}{i}(r\ln c)^{n-i}{B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{i}(x;a,b,c)y^{n-i}.

5
Hurwitz-Lerch Type Multi Poly-Bernoulli Polynomials

Consider the case in which
x=1
,
a=e
and
b=c=1
for the parameters in Definition
4.1
. Then we have

\frac{{\rm Li}_{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}(1-e^{-t})}{(1-e^{-t})^{r}}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}{B}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}_{n}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

(37)

This can be generalized using the following generalization of Hurwitz-Lerch multiple zeta values

{\Phi}_{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}(z,a)=\sum_{0\leq m_{1}\leq m_{2}\leq\ldots\leq m_{r}}\frac{z^{m_{r}}}{(m_{1}+a-r+1)^{k_{1}}(m_{2}+a-r+2)^{k_{2}}\ldots(m_{r}+a)^{k_{r}}}.

(38)

Note that

\displaystyle{\rm Li}_{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}(z)

\displaystyle=\sum_{0<m_{1}<m_{2}<\ldots<m_{r}}\frac{z^{m_{r}}}{m_{1}^{k_{1}}m_{2}^{k_{2}}\ldots m_{r}^{k_{r}}}

\displaystyle=z^{r}\sum_{0<m_{1}<m_{2}<\ldots<m_{r}}\frac{z^{m_{r}-r}}{{m_{1}}^{k_{1}}{m_{2}}^{k_{2}}\ldots{m_{r}}^{k_{r}}}

\displaystyle=z^{r}\sum_{0\leq m_{1}\leq m_{2}\leq\ldots\leq m_{r}}\frac{z^{m_{r}}}{{(m_{1}+1)}^{k_{1}}{(m_{2}+2)}^{k_{2}}\ldots{(m_{r}+r)}^{k_{r}}}

\displaystyle=z^{r}{\Phi}_{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}(z,r)

Thus, we have

\frac{{\rm Li}_{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}(z)}{z^{r}}={\Phi}_{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}(z,r).

(39)

More precisely, one can generalize (
40
) as follows

{\Phi}_{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}(1-e^{-t},a)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}B_{n,a}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

(40)

We call
B_{n,a}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}
as
Hurwitz-Lerch Type Multi Poly-Bernoulli Numbers
. Furthermore, we can define the
Hurwitz-Lerch Type Multi Poly-Bernoulli Polynomials
, denoted by
B_{n,a}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}(x)
, as follows

{\Phi}_{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}(1-e^{-t},a)e^{rxt}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}B_{n,a}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}(x)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

(41)

where
B_{n,a}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}(0)=B_{n,a}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}
.

The next theorem contains an explicit formula for
B_{n,a}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}(x)
expressed in terms of the
(r,\beta)
-Stirling numbers
\genfrac{}{}{0.0pt}{0}{n}{m}_{\beta,r}

[
10
]
, which satisfy the following exponential generating function

\frac{1}{\beta^{m}m!}e^{rt}(e^{\beta t}-1)^{m}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\genfrac{}{}{0.0pt}{0}{n}{m}_{\beta,r}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

(42)

Theorem 5.1
.

The Hurwitz-Lerch type multi poly-Bernoulli polynomials have the following explicit formula

B_{n,a}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}(x)=\sum_{0\leq m_{1}\leq m_{2}\leq\ldots\leq m_{r}\leq n}\frac{m_{r}!\genfrac{}{}{0.0pt}{0}{n}{m_r}_{-1,xr}}{(m_{1}+a-r+1)^{k_{1}}(m_{2}+a-r+2)^{k_{2}}\ldots(m_{r}+a)^{k_{r}}}.

(43)

Proof.

Using (
38
) and (
42
), we have

\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}B_{n,a}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}(x)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}={\Phi}_{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}(1-e^{-t},a)e^{xrt}\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad

\displaystyle=\sum_{0\leq m_{1}\leq m_{2}\leq\ldots\leq m_{r}}\frac{m_{r}!}{(m_{1}+a-r+1)^{k_{1}}(m_{2}+a-r+2)^{k_{2}}\ldots(m_{r}+a)^{k_{r}}}\frac{e^{xrt}(e^{-t}-1)^{m_{r}}}{(-1)^{m_{r}}m_{r}!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{0\leq m_{1}\leq m_{2}\leq\ldots\leq m_{r}}\frac{m_{r}!}{(m_{1}+a-r+1)^{k_{1}}(m_{2}+a-r+2)^{k_{2}}\ldots(m_{r}+a)^{k_{r}}}\sum_{n=m_{r}}^{\infty}\genfrac{}{}{0.0pt}{0}{n}{m_r}_{-1,xr}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\sum_{0\leq m_{1}\leq m_{2}\leq\ldots\leq m_{r}\leq n}\frac{m_{r}!\genfrac{}{}{0.0pt}{0}{n}{m_r}_{-1,xr}}{(m_{1}+a-r+1)^{k_{1}}(m_{2}+a-r+2)^{k_{2}}\ldots(m_{r}+a)^{k_{r}}}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

Comparing the coefficients of
\frac{t^{n}}{n!}
completes the proof of the theorem.
∎

Note that (
42
) implies

\genfrac{}{}{0.0pt}{0}{n}{m_r}_{-1,0}=(-1)^{n+m_{r}}\genfrac{}{}{0.0pt}{0}{n}{m_r}.

Hence, as a direct consequence of Theorem
5.1
, we have the following corollary.

Corollary 5.2
.

The Hurwitz-Lerch type multi poly-Bernoulli numbers equal

B_{n,a}^{(k_{1},k_{2},\ldots,k_{r})}=\sum_{0\leq m_{1}\leq m_{2}\leq\ldots\leq m_{r}\leq n}\frac{(-1)^{n+m_{r}}m_{r}!\genfrac{}{}{0.0pt}{0}{n}{m_r}}{(m_{1}+a-r+1)^{k_{1}}(m_{2}+a-r+2)^{k_{2}}\ldots(m_{r}+a)^{k_{r}}}.

(44)

When
r=1
, equation (
44
) gives

B_{n,a}^{(k_{1})}=\sum_{0\leq m_{1}\leq n}\frac{(-1)^{n+m_{1}}m_{1}!\genfrac{}{}{0.0pt}{0}{n}{m_1}}{(m_{1}+a)^{k_{1}}}

which is exactly the explicit formula for Hurwitz type poly-Bernoulli numbers in Theorem 2.1 of
[
7
]
.
