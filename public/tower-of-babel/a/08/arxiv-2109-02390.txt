Magnetic dipole moments of the T c ​ c + T_{cc}^{+} and Z V + ⁣ + Z_{V}^{++} tetraquark states
K. Azizi, U. Özdem

Abstract
Inspired by the observation of the doubly charmed state T c ​ c T_{cc} and following theoretical studies on its spectroscopic parameters, we investigate its magnetic dipole moment assigning it the quantum numbers J P = 1 + J^{P}=1^{+} and both the compact diquark-antidiquark and molecular structures in the framework of the light-cone QCD. We also calculate the magnetic dipole moment of the theoretically predicted singly charmed state, Z V + ⁣ + Z_{V}^{++} , with two units of electric charge and the quantum numbers J P = 1 − J^{P}=1^{-} in diquark-antidiquark picture. The numerical results are obtained as μ T c ​ c + − D ​ i = 0.66 − 0.23 + 0.34 ​ μ N \mu_{T_{cc}^{+}-Di}=0.66^{+0.34}_{-0.23}~\mu_{N} , μ T c ​ c + − M ​ o ​ l = 0.43 − 0.22 + 0.23 ​ μ N \mu_{T_{cc}^{+}-Mol}=0.43^{+0.23}_{-0.22}~\mu_{N} and μ Z V + ⁣ + = 3.35 − 0.73 + 0.89 ​ μ N \mu_{Z_{V}^{++}}=3.35^{+0.89}_{-0.73}~\mu_{N} . These results may be checked via other phenomenological approaches. The obtained results may be useful in exact determinations of the natures of these states.

The electromagnetic multipole moments of hadrons, besides their spectroscopic parameters, can help us determine their exact nature, substructure, and quantum numbers. These parameters give useful information about the charge and magnetization distributions inside the hadrons that help us gain information on their geometric shapes.
Recall that the magnetic and higher order moments of particles that contain information about the spatial distributions of charge and magnetization within the particles, are directly related to the spatial distributions of quarks and gluons in hadrons.

II
Formalism

In the light-cone QCD sum rule method we calculate a correlation function, as building block of the method, once in terms of hadronic parameters such as electromagnetic form factors, and second, in terms the QCD parameters and DAs of the on-shell photon. By matching the coefficients of suitable Lorentz structures from both sides and using the assumption of quark-hadron duality, we will be able to evaluate the related hadronic observables in terms of QCD degrees of freedom as well as auxiliary parameters entering the calculations at different steps.

II.1
MDM of the
T_{cc}^{+}
state

To evaluate the MDM of the
T_{cc}^{+}
state
within the LCSR, we start with the correlation function

\Pi_{\mu\nu}^{T_{cc}^{+}}(p,q)=i\int d^{4}xe^{ip\cdot x}\langle 0|\mathcal{T}\{J_{\mu}^{T_{cc}^{+}}(x)J_{\nu}^{T_{cc}^{+}\dagger}(0)\}|0\rangle_{\gamma},

(3)

where the sub-index
\gamma
represents the background electromagnetic field and

is the interpolating current of the
T_{cc}^{+}
state
with the quantum numbers
J^{P}=1^{+}

cc\bar{u}\bar{d}

T_{cc}^{+}

\displaystyle J_{\mu}^{T_{cc}^{+}-Di}(x)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle c_{a}^{T}(x)\gamma_{\mu}Cc_{b}(x)\bar{u}_{a}(x)C\gamma_{5}\bar{d}_{b}^{T}(x),

(4)

\displaystyle J_{\mu}^{T_{cc}^{+}-Mol}(x)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\{[\bar{u}_{a}(x)i\gamma_{5}c_{a}(x)][\bar{d}_{b}(x)\gamma_{\mu}c_{b}(x)]+[\bar{u}_{a}(x)\gamma_{\mu}c_{a}(x)][\bar{d}_{b}(x)i\gamma_{5}c_{b}(x)]\}.

In Eq. (
4

u(x)
,
c(x)
and
d(x)
are the quark fields, and

T_{cc}^{+}

In hadronic side, the correlation function is evaluated by its saturation with the intermediate hadronic states. By performing the four-integral over
x

\displaystyle\Pi_{\mu\nu}^{Had-T_{cc}^{+}}(p,q)={\frac{\langle 0\mid J_{\mu}^{T_{cc}^{+}}\mid T_{cc}^{+}(p)\rangle}{p^{2}-m_{T_{cc}^{+}}^{2}}}\langle T_{cc}^{+}(p)\mid T_{cc}^{+}(p+q)\rangle_{\gamma}\frac{\langle T_{cc}^{+}(p+q)\mid{J^{\dagger}}_{\nu}^{T_{cc}^{+}}\mid 0\rangle}{(p+q)^{2}-m_{T_{cc}^{+}}^{2}}+\cdots,

(5)

where q is the photon’s momentum and dots stand for the contributions coming from the higher states and
continuum. The matrix element

\langle 0\mid J_{\mu}^{T_{cc}^{+}}\mid T_{cc}^{+}\rangle

\displaystyle\langle 0\mid J_{\mu}^{T_{cc}^{+}}\mid T_{cc}^{+}\rangle=\lambda_{T_{cc}^{+}}\varepsilon_{\mu}^{\theta}\,,

(6)

with
\lambda_{T_{cc}^{+}}
being the residue of the
T_{cc}^{+}
state and
\varepsilon_{\mu}^{\theta}\

In the existence of the external electromagnetic background field, the matrix element

can be expressed in terms of the Lorentz invariant form factors as follows

\displaystyle\langle T_{cc}^{+}(p,\varepsilon^{\theta})\mid T_{cc}^{+}(p+q,\varepsilon^{\delta})\rangle_{\gamma}

\displaystyle=-\varepsilon^{\tau}(\varepsilon^{\theta})^{\alpha}(\varepsilon^{\delta})^{\beta}\Bigg[G_{1}(Q^{2})~(2p+q)_{\tau}~g_{\alpha\beta}+G_{2}(Q^{2})~(g_{\tau\beta}~q_{\alpha}-g_{\tau\alpha}~q_{\beta})

\displaystyle-\frac{1}{2m_{T_{cc}^{+}}^{2}}G_{3}(Q^{2})~(2p+q)_{\tau}~q_{\alpha}q_{\beta}\Bigg],

(7)

where
\varepsilon^{\delta}

\varepsilon^{\theta}

T_{cc}^{+}

Using Eqs. (
5
)-(

\displaystyle\Pi_{\mu\nu}^{Had-T_{cc}^{+}}(p,q)

\displaystyle=\frac{\varepsilon_{\rho}\lambda_{T_{cc}^{+}}^{2}}{[m_{T_{cc}^{+}}^{2}-(p+q)^{2}][m_{T_{cc}^{+}}^{2}-p^{2}]}\Bigg[G_{2}(Q^{2})\Bigg(q_{\mu}g_{\rho\nu}-q_{\nu}g_{\rho\mu}-\frac{p_{\nu}}{m_{T_{cc}^{+}}^{2}}\big(q_{\mu}p_{\rho}-\frac{1}{2}Q^{2}g_{\mu\rho}\big)+

\displaystyle+\frac{(p+q)_{\mu}}{m_{T_{cc}^{+}}^{2}}\big(q_{\nu}(p+q)_{\rho}+\frac{1}{2}Q^{2}g_{\nu\rho}\big)-\frac{(p+q)_{\mu}p_{\nu}p_{\rho}}{m_{T_{cc}^{+}}^{4}}\,Q^{2}\Bigg)

\displaystyle+\mbox{other independent structures}\Bigg]\,+\cdots.

To determine the MDM, the value of the form factor

\displaystyle F_{M}(Q^{2})=G_{2}(Q^{2})\,,

\displaystyle\mu_{T_{cc}^{+}}=\frac{e}{2\,m_{T_{cc}^{+}}}\,F_{M}(0).

In QCD side, the correlation function in Eq. (
3

To this end, we substitute the explicit forms of the interpolating currents in the correlation function and contract the corresponding quark fields with the help of the Wick’s theorem. \displaystyle\Pi_{\mu\nu}^{\mathrm{QCD-T_{cc}^{+}}}(p,q)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle S_{q}(x)

Any future measurements of its electromagnetic properties and comparison of the results with theoretical predictions will be very useful in our understanding of tetraquarks and as a result the QCD as theory of strong interaction.

IV
Discussion and concluding remarks

The magnetic dipole moments, together with other electromagnetic observables, of the composite particles encode important information on their nature, substructure and geometric shape. They contain information on the spatial distributions of charge and magnetization inside the particles that directly give information on the spatial distributions of quarks and gluons inside hadrons.

In this study, the MDMs of the recently observed doubly charmed exotic state
T_{cc}^{+}
as well as the recently proposed doubly charged four-quark candidate
Z_{V}^{++}
were investigated within the framework of light-cone QCD. We considered both the compact diquark-antidiquark and molecular pictures for
T_{cc}^{+}
, but only diquark-antidiquark organization for the
Z_{V}^{++}
state. They were treated as states with
J^{P}=1^{+}
and
J^{P}=1^{-}
quantum numbers, respectively.

We extracted the values of the MDMs as
\mu_{T_{cc}^{+}-Di}=0.66^{+0.34}_{-0.23}~\mu_{N}
,
\mu_{T_{cc}^{+}-Mol}=0.43^{+0.23}_{-0.22}~\mu_{N}
and
\mu_{Z_{V}^{++}}=3.35^{+0.89}_{-0.73}~\mu_{N}
. The obtained results for the
\mu_{T_{cc}^{+}}
in two pictures are consistent within the presented uncertainties although their central values differ from each other considerably. This indicates that the magnetic dipole moment is not able to distinguish between the two pictures. The obtained results in this study for the magnetic dipole moment of this state together with the results of other theoretical models on the mass, width and other decay properties of this state and comparison of the obtained results with the existing and future experimental data will shed light on the nature of the newly seen
T_{cc}^{+}
tetraquark. The result for the magnetic dipole moment of
Z_{V}^{++}
state, due to its double charge, is considerably large. The orders of MDMs, especially for
\mu_{Z_{V}^{++}}
, indicate that they are accessible in the experiment. Our result on MDM of
Z_{V}^{++}
together with its spectroscopic parameters calculated in Ref.
Agaev:2021jsz
, may help experimental groups in the search for this state and its properties in the experiment. Our results on MDMs may be checked via alternative phenomenological approaches.
