Bourgain-Brezis-Mironescu formula for W q s , p W^{s,p}_{q} -spaces in arbitrary domains
Kaushik Mohanta

Abstract
. Under certain restrictions on s , p , q s,p,q , the Triebel-Lizorkin spaces can be viewed as generalised fractional Sobolev spaces W q s , p W^{s,p}_{q} . In this article, we show that the Bourgain-Brezis-Mironescu formula holds for W q s , p W^{s,p}_{q} -seminorms in arbitrary domain. This addresses an open question raised by Brazke-Schikorra-Yung in [Bourgain-Brezis-Mironescu convergence via Triebel-Lizorkin spaces; Calc. Var. Partial Differential Equations; 2023].

1.
Introduction

Sobolev spaces arise naturally in the study of partial differential equations. They are defined in terms of weak derivatives. For an open set
\Omega\subseteq\mathbb{R}^{N}
, and
1\leq p<\infty
, the Sobolev space
W^{1,p}(\Omega)
is defined to be
\{f\in L^{p}(\Omega)\ |\ [f]_{W^{1,p}(\Omega)}<\infty\}
,
where

[f]_{W^{1,p}(\Omega)}^{p}:=\int_{\Omega}|\nabla f(x)|^{p}dx.

Some closely related spaces are Triebel-Lizorkin spaces
F^{s}_{p,q}(\Omega)
, which are defined to be
\{f|_{\Omega}\ |\ f\in F^{s}_{p,q}(\mathbb{R}^{N})\}
, and are equipped with the norm
[\cdot]_{F^{s}_{p,q}(\mathbb{R}^{N})}
. We shall not define the norm
[\cdot]_{F^{s}_{p,q}(\mathbb{R}^{N})}
as it will not be necessary for the present article. Instead, we refer the reader, for definition and classical results regarding Triebel-Lizorkin spaces, to
[
59
]
, or more modern references like
[
36
,
56
]
. For
1\leq p,q<\infty
,
\max\{0,\frac{N(q-p)}{pq}\}<s<1
, we have the characterisation (see Theorem 1.2 of
[
55
]
)

F^{s}_{p,q}(\mathbb{R}^{N}):=\left\{f\in L^{\max\{p,q\}}(\mathbb{R}^{N})\ \Big|\ \|f\|_{L^{p}(\mathbb{R}^{N})}+[f]_{W^{s,p}_{q}(\mathbb{R}^{N})}<\infty\right\},

where

(1)

[f]_{W^{s,p}_{q}(\Omega)}:=\left(\int_{\Omega}\left(\int_{\Omega}\frac{|f(x)-f(y)|^{q}}{|x-y|^{N+sq}}dy\right)^{\frac{p}{q}}\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}.

In the special case of
p=q
, these spaces are related to the so-called fractional Sobolev spaces
W^{s,p}(\Omega)
, defined by

W^{s,p}(\Omega):=\left\{f\in L^{p}(\Omega)\ \Big|\ \|f\|_{L^{p}(\Omega)}+[f]_{W^{s,p}(\Omega)}<\infty\right\},

where
[f]_{W^{s,p}(\Omega)}:=[f]_{W^{s,p}_{p}(\Omega)}
. Of course, when
\Omega=\mathbb{R}^{N}
, or when
\Omega
is fractional extension domain (see
[
25
]
), we have
W^{s,p}(\Omega)=F^{s}_{p,p}(\Omega)
.

Bourgain-Brezis-Mironescu
[
8
]
showed that for any smooth and bounded domain
\Omega
,
1\leq p<\infty
, and any
f\in W^{1,p}(\Omega)
,

\lim_{s\to 1-}(1-s)[f]_{W^{s,p}(\Omega)}^{p}=K\|\nabla f\|_{L^{p}(\Omega)}^{p}.

Conversely, for any
f\in L^{p}(\Omega)
, if we have

\lim_{s\to 1-}(1-s)[f]_{W^{s,p}(\Omega)}^{p}<\infty,

then
f\in W^{1,p}(\Omega)
if
p>1
and
f\in BV(\Omega)
if
p=1
. Later Dávila
[
23
]
extended this result and proved that for any
f\in BV(\Omega)
,
\lim_{s\to 1-}(1-s)[f]_{W^{s,1}(\Omega)}^{p}=K|\nabla f|(\Omega)
.
This is commonly known as the Bourgain-Brezis-Mironescu formula (BBM formula for short). The subject was further developed in
[
9
,
10
,
18
,
47
,
48
,
53
]
. The BBM formula has been generalised to Orlicz and generalised Orlicz setup in

]
, to variable exponent setup in
[

,

]
,
to magnetic Sobolev spaces
[

,

,

,

,
57
]
, to anisotropic setup
[
37
,

,

]
, to Riemannian manifolds in
[
42
]
, to metric spaces in
[
24
,
35
]
, to Banach function spaces in
[

,

]
. Similar studies are possible in the context of Besov spaces also
[

Our first interest lies in works regarding the domain. That is, how far the smooth-bounded condition, on the domain, can be relaxed. In this direction, we mention three works. The first is due to Lioni-Spector
[
43
,
44
]
. They showed that for an arbitrary domain
\Omega
, and
f\in L^{p}(\Omega)
,

\lim_{\lambda\to 0+}\lim_{s\to 1-}(1-s)\int_{\Omega_{\lambda}}\int_{\Omega_{\lambda}}\frac{|f(x)-f(y)|^{p}}{|x-y|^{N+sp}}dydx=K[f]_{W^{1,p}(\Omega)}^{p}.

where

(2)

\Omega_{\lambda}:=\{x\in\Omega\ |\ \mbox{dist}(x,\partial\Omega)>\lambda\}\cap B(0,\lambda^{-1}).

The second result is due to the author with Bal-Roy
[
5
]
, where it has been shown that the BBM-formula
[
8
]
, holds if we take
\Omega
to be a
W^{1,p}
-extension domain. The third result is due to Drelichman-Duran
[
27
]
. They showed that for
1<p<\infty
, and an arbitrary bounded domain
\Omega
, and any
\tau\in(0,1)
, we have

\lim_{s\to 1-}(1-s)\int_{\Omega}\int_{B(x,\tau\mbox{dist}(x,\partial\Omega))}\frac{|f(x)-f(y)|^{p}}{|x-y|^{N+sp}}dydx=K[f]_{W^{1,p}(\Omega)}^{p}.

The second direction of work regarding the BBM-formula, that we are interested in, is its extension for Triebel-Lizorkin spaces. The first work in this direction was done by Brazke-Schikorra-Yung
[
12
]
. They explained via examining thoroughly various constants of embeddings that although
F^{s}_{p,p}=W^{s,p}
, when
s\in(0,1)
and
F^{s}_{p,2}=W^{1,p}
it makes sense for the scaled
W^{s,p}
seminorm to converge to
W^{1,p}
-seminorm, even when
p\neq 2
. They posed the open problem (see
[
12
, Question 1.12]
) about the asymptotic constant in the identification of the
\|\cdot\|_{W^{s,p}_{q}}\approx\|\cdot\|_{F^{s}_{p,q}}
.

The current article addresses this question by showing the asymptotic behaviour (as
s\to 1-
) of
W^{s,p}_{q}
-seminorms. Similar studies has been done, when
1<q<p<\infty
, in
[
22
]
for
\mathbb{R}^{N}
, and in
[
62
, Theorem 6.1]
for a special class of bounded extension domains (called
(\varepsilon,\infty)
-domains).

We concentrate our focus on the following seminorm (for some
\tau\in(0,1)
)

(3)

[f]_{\tilde{W}^{s,p}_{q}(\Omega)}:=\left(\int_{\Omega}\left(\int_{B(x,\tau\mbox{dist}(x,\partial\Omega))}\frac{|f(x)-f(y)|^{q}}{|x-y|^{N+sq}}dy\right)^{\frac{p}{q}}\right)^{\frac{1}{p}},

as one can then extend the above question for arbitrary bounded domains, motivated by
[
27
]
. We go one step further and show that the boundedness of the domain is not necessary. Our main results are the following:

Theorem 1
.

Let
\Omega\subset\mathbb{R}^{N}
be any open set
\tau\in(0,1)
. Assume one of the following conditions

(1)

1\leq q\leq p<\infty
,

(2)

1<p<q<\infty
with
p\leq N
and
q<\frac{Np}{N-p}
,

(3)

N<p<q<\infty
.

Then there is a constant
K=K(N,p,q)>0
such that for any
f\in W^{1,p}(\Omega)
, we have,

(4)

\lim_{s\to 1-}(1-s)^{\frac{p}{q}}\int_{\Omega}\left(\int_{B(x,\tau\mbox{dist}(x,\partial\Omega))}\frac{|f(x)-f(y)|^{q}}{|x-y|^{N+sq}}dy\right)^{\frac{p}{q}}dx=K\int_{\Omega}|\nabla f(x)|^{p}dx.

Theorem 2
.

Let
\Omega\subset\mathbb{R}^{N}
be an open set,
\tau\in(0,1)
and
1\leq p,q<\infty
. If
f\in L^{p}(\Omega)\cap L^{q}(\Omega)
is such that

L^{*}_{p,q}(f):=\lim_{s\to 1-}\int_{\Omega}\left((1-s)\int_{B(x,\tau\mbox{dist}(x,\partial\Omega))}\frac{|f(x)-f(y)|^{q}}{|x-y|^{N+sq}}dy\right)^{\frac{p}{q}}dx<\infty,

then
f\in W^{1,p}(\Omega)
when
p>1
, and
f\in BV(\Omega)
when
p=1
.

Remark 3
.

3.
Fractional Sobolev space with restricted internal distance

Fix
R>0
and
\tau\in(0,1)
once and for all. Denote
\delta_{x,R,\tau}=\min\{R,\tau\mbox{dist}(x,\partial\Omega)\}
. We shall often drop the

\tau

to denote
\delta_{x,R,\tau}
.

Remark 10
.

If the function

is bounded in
\Omega

large enough, so that
\delta_{x}=\tau\mbox{dist}(x,\partial\Omega)
. Then the particular case
p=q
of

[

Define, for any open set
\Omega\subseteq\mathbb{R}^{N}
,
1\leq p,q<\infty
,

\hat{W}^{s,p}_{q}(\Omega):=\{f\in L^{p}(\Omega)\ |\ [f]_{\hat{W}^{s,p}_{q}(\Omega)}<\infty\}
where

(5)

[f]_{\hat{W}^{s,p}_{q}(\Omega)}^{p}:=\int_{x\in\Omega}\left(\int_{y\in B(x,\delta_{x})}\frac{|f(x)-f(y)|^{q}}{|x-y|^{N+sq}}dy\right)^{\frac{p}{q}}dx.

We shall need some embedding results for these new fractional Sobolev spaces for our purpose. As expected, the case
q\leq p
and

are treated separately.

Lemma 11
.

Let
\Omega\subseteq\mathbb{R}^{N}
be open and
1\leq q\leq p<\infty
. Assume either
D=\tilde{D}=\Omega
or, for some
\frac{1}{2R}>\alpha>0
,
D=\{x\in\Omega\ |\ \mbox{dist}(x,\partial\Omega)<\alpha\}
with
\tilde{D}=\{x\in\Omega\ |\ \mbox{dist}(x,\partial\Omega)<2\alpha\mbox{ or }|x|>\frac{1}{2\alpha}\}
. Then there is a constant
C=C(p,q,R,\Omega,N)
such that for any
f
in
W^{1,p}(\Omega)
,

(6)

(1-s)^{\frac{p}{q}}\int_{x\in D}\left(\int_{y\in B(x,\delta_{x})}\frac{|f(x)-f(y)|^{q}}{|x-y|^{N+sq}}dy\right)^{\frac{p}{q}}dx\leq C[f]_{W^{1,p}(\tilde{D})}^{p}.

Proof.

We have

\displaystyle(1-s)^{\frac{p}{q}}\int_{x\in D}

\displaystyle\left(\int_{y\in B(x,\delta_{x})}\frac{|f(x)-f(y)|^{q}}{|x-y|^{N+sq}}dy\right)^{\frac{p}{q}}dx

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(1-s)^{\frac{p}{q}}\int_{x\in D}\left(\int_{h\in B(0,\delta_{x})}\frac{|f(x+h)-f(x)|^{q}}{|h|^{N+sq}}dh\right)^{\frac{p}{q}}dx

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(1-s)^{\frac{p}{q}}\int_{x\in D}\left(\int_{h\in B(0,\delta_{x})}\frac{|f(x+h)-f(x)|^{q}}{|h|^{p}}\frac{dh}{|h|^{N+sq-q}}\right)^{\frac{p}{q}}dx

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle(1-s)^{\frac{p}{q}}\int_{x\in D}\left(\int_{h\in B(0,\delta_{x})}\int_{0}^{1}|\nabla f(x+th)|^{q}dt\frac{dh}{|h|^{N+sq-q}}\right)^{\frac{p}{q}}dx.

The last inequality follows from the absolute continuity on lines of the
W^{1,p}
-functions. We now have, after a change of variable
y=x+th
, (using that
B(x,t\delta_{x})\subset B(x,\delta_{x})
)

\displaystyle(1-s)^{\frac{p}{q}}\int_{x\in D}

\displaystyle\left(\int_{y\in B(x,\delta_{x})}\frac{|f(x)-f(y)|^{q}}{|x-y|^{N+sq}}dy\right)^{\frac{p}{q}}dx

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle(1-s)^{\frac{p}{q}}\int_{x\in D}\left(\int_{y\in B(x,\delta_{x})}\int_{0}^{1}|\nabla f(y)|^{q}t^{sq-q}dt\frac{dy}{|x-y|^{N+sq-q}}\right)^{\frac{p}{q}}dx

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{(1-s)^{\frac{p}{q}}}{(sq-q+1)^{\frac{p}{q}}}\int_{x\in D}\left(\int_{y\in B(x,\delta_{x})}|\nabla f(y)|^{q}\frac{dy}{|x-y|^{N+sq-q}}\right)^{\frac{p}{q}}dx.

Note that in the above inequality,
\nabla f
is required to be defined only inside
\tilde{D}
. So, we shall take a
0
-extension of
\nabla f
outside
\tilde{D}
. Since we have
\frac{p}{q}\geq 1
, we can use Young’s convolution inequality to get

\displaystyle(1-s)^{\frac{p}{q}}\int_{x\in D}

\displaystyle\left(\int_{y\in B(x,\delta_{x})}\frac{|f(x)-f(y)|^{q}}{|x-y|^{N+sq}}dy\right)^{\frac{p}{q}}dx

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{(1-s)^{\frac{p}{q}}}{(sq-q+1)^{\frac{p}{q}}}\int_{x\in D}\left(\int_{y\in B(x,R)}|\nabla f(y)|^{q}\frac{dy}{|x-y|^{N+sq-q}}\right)^{\frac{p}{q}}dx

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{(1-s)^{\frac{p}{q}}}{(sq-q+1)^{\frac{p}{q}}}\int_{x\in\tilde{D}}|\nabla f(x)|^{p}dx\left(\int_{x\in B(0,R)}\frac{dx}{|x|^{N+sq-q}}\right)^{\frac{p}{q}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{R^{p-sp}}{q^{\frac{p}{q}}}\int_{x\in\tilde{D}}|\nabla f(x)|^{p}dx.

\displaystyle\int_{x\in D}\left((N+sq)(1-s)\int_{t=0}^{\delta_{x}}\frac{1}{t^{sq+1-q}}\left(\int_{r=0}^{1}\frac{1}{t^{N}}\int_{h\in B(0,t)}|\nabla f(x+rh)|^{\beta p}dhdr\right)^{\frac{q}{\beta p}}dt\right)^{\frac{p}{q}}dx

\displaystyle\int_{x\in\mathbb{R}^{N}}(M|\nabla f(x)|^{\beta p})^{\frac{1}{\beta}}\left((N+sq)(1-s)\int_{t=0}^{R}\frac{1}{t^{sq+1-q}}\left(\int_{r=0}^{1}dr\right)^{\frac{q}{\beta p}}dt\right)^{\frac{p}{q}}dx

\displaystyle C(p,q,N,R)\int_{x\in\tilde{D}}|\nabla f(x)|^{p}dx.

Combining

Again, we can estimate

(12)

\displaystyle I_{2}=

\displaystyle\int_{x\in D}\left((1-s)\int_{h\in B(0,\delta_{x})}\frac{|f(x+h)-f(x)|^{q}}{\delta_{x}^{N+sq}}dh\right)^{\frac{p}{q}}dx

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\int_{x\in D}\frac{(1-s)^{\frac{p}{q}}}{\delta_{x}^{sp-p}}\left(\left(\delta_{x}^{-N}\int_{h\in B(0,\delta_{x})}|\nabla f(x+h)|^{p}dh+\delta_{x}^{-N}\int_{h\in B(0,\delta_{x})}\frac{|f(x+h)-f(x)|^{p}}{|h|^{p}}dh\right)^{\frac{q}{p}}\right)^{\frac{p}{q}}dx

Combing

Proof of

Step-1:

\displaystyle\left|(1-s)^{\frac{1}{q}}[f]_{s,p,q,\Omega,R}-K^{\frac{1}{p}}[f]_{W^{1,p}(\Omega)}\right|\leq

\displaystyle(1-s)^{\frac{1}{q}}\left|[f]_{s,p,q,\Omega,R}-[g]_{s,p,q,\Omega,R}\right|

\displaystyle+\left|(1-s)^{\frac{1}{q}}[g]_{s,p,\Omega,R}-K^{\frac{1}{q}}[g]_{W^{1,p}(\Omega)}\right|+K^{\frac{1}{q}}\left|[g]_{W^{1,p}(\Omega)}-[f]_{W^{1,p}(\Omega)}\right|

Step-2:

In view of the previous step, it is now enough to assume that

Also note that,

To complete the proof, it is enough to show that

We shall apply

9

For any

We need to verify the the hypotheses of

Thus

for

. Thus, we have using triangle inequality and then mean value inequality,

\displaystyle|F_{n}(x)-F(x)|

\displaystyle=\left|\left((1-s_{n})\int_{B(0,\delta_{x})}\frac{|f(x+h)-f(x)|^{q}}{|h|^{N+s_{n}q}}dh\right)^{\frac{p}{q}}-K|\nabla f(x)|^{p}\right|

\displaystyle\leq\sup_{y\in\Omega_{i^{2}}}|\nabla f(y)|^{p}\left((1-s_{n})\int_{B(0,R)}\frac{dh}{|h|^{N+s_{n}q-q}}\right)^{\frac{p}{q}}+K|\nabla f(x)|^{p}

\displaystyle\leq C(N,p,q,R)\sup_{y\in\Omega_{i^{2}}}|\nabla f(y)|^{p}.

Since

satisfied for sufficiently large

.

Note that, to show that hypothesis (2) of

\lim_{i\to\infty}\lim_{n\to\infty}\int_{\Omega\setminus\Omega_{2i}}F_{n}(x)dx=0.

We start with an arbitrary

From

Theorem 15
.

Let
\Omega\subset\mathbb{R}^{N}
be an open set. If

We divide the proof into two parts. First, we prove it for a particular case with a bit stronger assumptions, and then give the general proof.

Step-1

\Omega

Using

Hence by

Step-2:
We now prove the theorem in full generality.

For
1<p<\infty

\displaystyle I_{2}

\displaystyle\leq\int_{\Omega}\left(\int_{R\leq|h|\leq\tau\mbox{dist}(x,\partial\Omega)}\frac{|f(x+h)-f(x)|^{q}}{|h|^{N+s_{0}q}}dh\right)^{\frac{p}{q}}dx

\displaystyle\leq 2\int_{\Omega\cap B(0,\lambda_{f})}\left(\int_{R\leq|h|\leq\tau\mbox{dist}(x,\partial\Omega)}\frac{|f(x+h)-f(x)|^{q}}{|h|^{N+s_{0}q}}dh\right)^{\frac{p}{q}}dx.

Using Hölder’s inequality, we get

I_{2}\leq 2\lambda_{f}^{N(1-\frac{p}{q})}\left(\int_{\Omega\cap B(0,\lambda_{f})}\int_{R\leq|h|\leq\tau\mbox{dist}(x,\partial\Omega)}\frac{|f(x+h)-f(x)|^{q}}{|h|^{N+s_{0}q}}dhdx\right)^{\frac{p}{q}}.

Now we can proceed as in Case-1 to show that

I_{2}\leq C(N,p,q,R,f)\|f\|_{L^{q}(\Omega)}^{p}.

This completes the proof.
∎
