cabling of the (twisted) Whitehead link, of which one component
pierces the other twice in the opposite direction each other.
Moreover,
R_{i}
and
R_{j}
are a linear combination of
D_{k,k}
with

k\leq i
and
k\leq j
, respectively. According to Lemma

2.2
, this pair vanishes if
i\neq j
. Therefore, we
can calculate
\langle\mathcal{K}_{p,s}(H_{N})\rangle
as follows:

(3.24)

\displaystyle\langle\mathcal{K}_{p,s}(H_{N})\rangle=\sum_{k=0}^{N}\frac{\{N+k-1;a\}}{\{N-k\}!}t_{k,p}\raisebox{-40.0pt}{
\includegraphics[width]{double-twist-2s-twist-RkRk.pdf}},

and since
R_{k}-H_{k}
is a linear combination of
R_{j}
with
j<k
, we
have

\displaystyle=\raisebox{-40.0pt}{
\includegraphics[width]{double-twist-2s-twist-RkHk.pdf}}\ \ \text{(by Lemma
\ref{Coro:vanishi})}

\displaystyle=(a^{k}q^{k(k-1)})^{2s}\raisebox{-40.0pt}{
\includegraphics[width]{double-twist-2s-twist-with-boxes.pdf}}\ \ \text{(By Lemma
\ref{lemma:ymn} and Lemma \ref{Coro:vanishi})}

\displaystyle=(a^{k}q^{k(k-1)})^{2s}C_{k,k}^{(s)}\raisebox{-40.0pt}{
\includegraphics[width]{double-twist-with-boxes.pdf}}\ \ \text{(By formula
(\ref{formula-Cnn}))}

\displaystyle=(a^{k}q^{k(k-1)})^{2s}C_{k,k}^{(s)}\theta_{k,k}\langle D_{k,k}\rangle\ \ \text{(By Lemma \ref{Coro:vanishi} and Lemma
\ref{lemma:Dmn})}

\displaystyle=(a^{k}q^{k(k-1)})^{2s}C_{k,k}^{(s)}\frac{\{2k-1\}_{2k}\{k-2;a\}_{k}}{\{k\}!}

Therefore,

(3.25)

\displaystyle\mathscr{H}_{N}(\mathcal{K}_{p,s})

\displaystyle=\frac{\langle K_{p,s}(H_{N})\rangle}{\langle U(H_{N})\rangle}

\displaystyle=\frac{\{N\}!}{\{N-1;a\}_{N}}\sum_{k=0}^{N}\frac{\{N+k-1;a\}_{N-k}}{\{N-k\}!}t_{k,p}(a^{k}q^{k(k-1)})^{2s}C_{k,k}^{(2s)}\frac{\{2k-1\}_{2k}\{k-2;a\}_{k}}{\{k\}!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{N}t_{k,p}(a^{k}q^{k(k-1)})^{2s}C_{k,k}^{(s)}\left[\begin{array}[]{@{\,}c@{\,}}N\\
k\end{array}\right]\{N+k-1;a\}_{k}\{k-2;a\}_{k}.

By using formula (
2.10
) and Lemma
3.3
,
we obtain

(3.28)

\displaystyle t_{k,p}=(-1)^{k}(a^{k}q^{k(k-1)})^{2p}\frac{C_{k,k}^{(p)}}{\{k\}!^{2}}.

Substituting it back to formula (
3.25
), we obtain
Theorem
1.2
.
