Abstract

Introduction

This is due to their key roles in both understanding of QCD long-distance dynamics, and the determination of the fundamental parameters of the standard model (SM). III
QCDSR for the electromagnetic form factors

To study the electromagnetic form factor of the vector heavy-light meson to the pseudoscalar heavy-light meson, we firstly write down the three-point correlation function,

\displaystyle{\Pi_{\mu\nu}}(p,p^{\prime})

\displaystyle={i^{2}}\int{{d^{4}}x{d^{4}}y{e^{ip^{\prime}x}}{e^{i(p-p^{\prime})y}}}

(5)

\displaystyle\times\left\langle 0\right|T\{{J_{\mathbb{P}}}(x){j_{\mu}}(y)J_{\nu}^{+}(0)\}\left|0\right\rangle

where
T
denotes the time ordered product,
J_{\mathbb{P}}
and
J^{+}_{\nu}
are the interpolating currents of the pseudoscalar and vector heavy-light mesons, and
j_{\mu}
is the electromagnetic current. These currents are taken as the following forms,

\displaystyle{J_{\mathbb{P}}}(x)

\displaystyle=\bar{q}(x)i{\gamma_{5}}Q(x)

\displaystyle{j_{\mu}}(y)

\displaystyle={{e_{Q}}\bar{Q}(y){\gamma_{\mu}}}Q(y)+{{e_{q}}\bar{q}(y){\gamma_{\mu}}}q(y)

\displaystyle{J_{\nu}}(0)

\displaystyle=\bar{q}(0){\gamma_{\nu}}Q(0)

(6)

In the framework of QCDSR, the correlation function will be calculated at two sides which are called the phenomenological side and the QCD side. Then, according to the quark hadron duality, the calculations of these two sides will be coordinated and the sum rules for electromagnetic form factor in the hadronic level can be obtained.

III.1
The Phenomenological side

In the phenomenological side, a complete sets of hadronic states with the same quantum numbers as the interpolating currents
J_{\mathbb{P}}
and
J^{+}_{\nu}
are inserted into the correlation function. By using the dispersion relation, the correlation function can be written as
Colangelo:2000dp
,

\displaystyle{\Pi^{\mathrm{phy}}_{\mu\nu}}(p,p^{\prime})

\displaystyle=\frac{{\left\langle 0\right|{J_{\mathbb{P}}}(0)\left|{\mathbb{P}(p^{\prime})}\right\rangle\left\langle{\mathbb{V}(p)}\right|J_{\nu}^{+}(0)\left|0\right\rangle}}{{(m_{\mathbb{V}}^{2}-{p^{2}})(m_{\mathbb{P}}^{2}-p{{}^{\prime 2}})}}

(7)

\displaystyle\times\left\langle{\mathbb{P}(p^{\prime})}\right|{j_{\mu}}(y)\left|{\mathbb{V}(p)}\right\rangle+h.c.

where
h.c.
represents the contributions coming from higher resonances and continuum states. The hadronic matrix elements are defined by the following parameterized equations,

\displaystyle\left\langle 0\right|{J_{\mathbb{P}}}(0)\left|{\mathbb{P}(p^{\prime})}\right\rangle

\displaystyle=\frac{{{f_{\mathbb{P}}}m_{\mathbb{P}}^{2}}}{{{m_{q}}+{m_{Q}}}}

\displaystyle\left\langle{\mathbb{V}(p)}\right|J_{\nu}^{+}(0)\left|0\right\rangle

\displaystyle={f_{\mathbb{V}}}{m_{\mathbb{V}}}\zeta_{\nu}^{*}

\displaystyle\left\langle{\mathbb{P}(p^{\prime})}\right|{j_{\mu}}(y)\left|{\mathbb{V}(p)}\right\rangle

\displaystyle={\varepsilon_{\mu\sigma\alpha\beta}}{\zeta^{\sigma}}{p^{\alpha}}p{{}^{\prime\beta}}\frac{{V({q^{2}})}}{{{m_{\mathbb{V}}}+{m_{\mathbb{P}}}}}

(8)

with
q=p-p^{\prime}
. In these above equations,
f_{\mathbb{P}}
and
f_{\mathbb{V}}
are the decay constants of the pseudoscalar and vector heavy-light mesons,
\varepsilon_{\mu\sigma\alpha\beta}
is the Levi-Civita tensor, and
V(q^{2})
is the electromagnetic form factor.
\zeta_{\nu}
is the polarization vector of vector heavy-light meson with the following properties,

\displaystyle\zeta_{\nu}^{*}{\zeta_{\sigma}}=-{g_{\nu\sigma}}+\frac{{{p_{\nu}}{p_{\sigma}}}}{{{p^{2}}}}

(9)

With these above equations, the correlation function in phenomenological side can be expressed as the following form,

\displaystyle{\Pi^{\mathrm{phy}}_{\mu\nu}}(p,p^{\prime})

\displaystyle=-\frac{{{f_{\mathbb{P}}}m_{\mathbb{P}}^{2}{f_{\mathbb{V}}}{m_{\mathbb{V}}}}}{{({m_{q}}+{m_{Q}})({m_{\mathbb{V}}}+{m_{\mathbb{P}}})}}

(10)

\displaystyle\times\frac{{V({q^{2}}){\varepsilon_{\mu\nu\alpha\beta}}{p^{\alpha}}p{{}^{\prime\beta}}}}{{(m_{\mathbb{V}}^{2}-{p^{2}})(m_{\mathbb{P}}^{2}-p{{}^{\prime 2}})}}+h.c.

\displaystyle=\Pi^{\mathrm{phy}}(p,p^{\prime})\varepsilon_{\mu\nu\alpha\beta}{p^{\alpha}}p{{}^{\prime\beta}}+h.c.

where
\Pi^{\mathrm{phy}}(p,p^{\prime})
is called scalar invariant amplitude,
\varepsilon_{\mu\nu\alpha\beta}p^{\alpha}p^{\prime\beta}
is the corresponding tensor structure.

V
Conclusions

In this article, the electromagnetic form factors in space-like regions (
Q^{2}>0
) for vector heavy-light meson to pseudoscalar heavy-light meson are firstly analyzed within the framework of three-point QCDSR. Then, the electromagnetic form factors in
Q^{2}=0
are obtained by fitting the results into analytical functions in space-like regions. Based on these results, the radiative decays of the vector heavy-light mesons are systematically analyzed. We hope the results about electromagnetic form factors and radiative decays can help to shed more light on the nature of the hadrons.
