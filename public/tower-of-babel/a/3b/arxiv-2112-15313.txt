1.
Tautological cohomology classes

In (

Without descendants, the classes (

(1.2)

H^{*}(\bar{I}\mathcal{X})^{\otimes n}\to H^{*}(\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{g,n}),\quad\gamma_{1}\otimes...\otimes\gamma_{n}\mapsto p_{*}\left(\prod_{i=1}^{n}ev_{i}^{*}(\gamma_{i})\cap[\mathcal{K}_{g,n}(\mathcal{X},d)]^{vir}\right).

(1)

\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{g-1,n+2}\to\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{g,n}

2.
Global finite group quotients

An important aspect of the Gromov-Witten theory of stacks
