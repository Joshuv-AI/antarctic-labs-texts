2.
On the Boundedness of the operator
\mathcal{T}

In the following section, let
\Upsilon_{\xi}
be a sequence in
\operatorname{End}_{\mathcal{A}}^{*}\left(\mathcal{H}\right)
such that for every
\xi\in\mathfrak{S}
,
\mathcal{R}(\Upsilon_{\xi})

Theorem 2.1
.

Assume for each
\xi\in\mathfrak{S}
,
\Upsilon_{\xi}(\mathfrak{N}_{\xi})=\mathfrak{N}_{\xi}
and
\Upsilon_{\xi}
in
\operatorname{End}_{\mathcal{A}}^{*}\left(\mathcal{H}\right)
is self-adjoint. If
\{(\mathfrak{N}_{\xi},\Upsilon_{\xi})\}_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}}
constitutes a
g-
fusion frame, depicted by a linear operator
\mathcal{T}
acting on
\hbox{span}\{\mathfrak{N}_{\xi}\}_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}}
, then
\mathcal{T}
is a bounded operator and
\mathcal{N}(\mathcal{U})
remains constant under the application of the right-shift operator. Additionally, the following inequality holds:

\displaystyle 1\leq\|\mathcal{T}\|\leq\sqrt{\dfrac{B}{A}},

with
A
and
B
as the frame bounds for
\{\Upsilon_{\xi}\}_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}}
.

Proof.

If
\{g_{\xi}\}_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}}
is a sequence in
\mathcal{N}(\mathcal{U})^{\perp}

such that
g_{\xi}=0
for all a finite number of
\xi
, then

\displaystyle\langle\mathcal{T}\mathcal{U}\bigg(\{g_{\xi}\}_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}}\bigg),\mathcal{T}\mathcal{U}\bigg(\{g_{\xi}\}_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}}\bigg)\rangle

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle B\sum_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}}\langle g_{\xi},g_{\xi}\rangle

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{B}{A}\langle\mathcal{U}(\{g_{\xi}\}_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}}),\mathcal{U}(\{g_{\xi}\}_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}})\rangle.

This implies that

(2.1)

\displaystyle\langle\mathcal{T}\mathcal{U}(\{g_{\xi}\}_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}}+\{h_{\xi}\}_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}}),\mathcal{T}\mathcal{U}(\{g_{\xi}\}_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}}+\{h_{\xi}\}_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}})\rangle\leq\frac{B}{A}\|\mathcal{U}(\{g_{\xi}\}_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}}+\{h_{\xi}\}_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}})\|^{2}.

For all sequence
\{h_{\xi}\}_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}}
in
\mathcal{N}(\mathcal{U})

From (2.1) and
\hbox{span}\{\mathfrak{N}_{\xi}\}_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}}
is a subspace of
\mathcal{R}(\mathcal{U})
, We conclude that for all
f\in\hbox{span}\{\mathfrak{N}_{\xi}\}_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}}
,

\displaystyle\langle\mathcal{T}(f),\mathcal{T}(f)\rangle\leq\frac{B}{A}\langle f,f\rangle,

Therefore,
\mathcal{T}
is bounded and

\|\mathcal{T}\|\leq\sqrt{\dfrac{B}{A}}.

Since
\{(\mathfrak{N}_{\xi},\Upsilon_{\xi})\}_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}}
is a

g-
fusion frame represented via
\mathcal{T}
, there exists
f_{0}\in\mathcal{H}
such that
\sum_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}}\langle\Upsilon_{\xi}(f_{0}),\Upsilon_{\xi}(f_{0})\rangle\not=0
. On the other hand,

\displaystyle\sum_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}}\bigg\langle\Upsilon_{\xi}(f_{0}),\Upsilon_{\xi}(f_{0})\bigg\rangle

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}}\bigg\langle\Upsilon_{\xi+1}(f_{0}),\Upsilon_{\xi+1}(f_{0})\bigg\rangle

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}}\bigg\langle\mathcal{T}\Upsilon_{\xi}(f_{0}),\mathcal{T}\Upsilon_{\xi}(f_{0})\bigg\rangle

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\|\mathcal{T}\|^{2}\sum_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}}\bigg\langle\Upsilon_{\xi}(f_{0}),\Upsilon_{\xi}(f_{0})\bigg\rangle.

This shows that
\|\mathcal{T}\|\geq 1
.

To finish the proof, let
\{f_{\xi}\}_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}}\in\mathcal{N}(\mathcal{U})
. Then
\sum_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}}\Upsilon_{\xi}(f_{\xi})=0
. Since
\mathcal{T}
is bounded and

\mathcal{T}\Upsilon_{\xi}=\Upsilon_{\xi+1}
, we have

\displaystyle\sum_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}}\Upsilon_{\xi}(f_{\xi-1})

\displaystyle=\sum_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}}\Upsilon_{\xi+1}(f_{\xi})

\displaystyle=\sum_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}}\mathcal{T}\Upsilon_{\xi}(f_{\xi})

\displaystyle=0.

Since
\Upsilon_{\xi}
be self-adjoint
for all
\xi\in\mathfrak{S}
.
We conclude that

\mathcal{U}\mathcal{T}(\{f_{\xi}\}_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}})=0.

That is,
\mathcal{T}(\{f_{\xi}\}_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}})\in\mathcal{N}(\mathcal{U})
.
∎

Theorem 2.2
.

Let
\{(\mathfrak{N}_{\xi},\Upsilon_{\xi})\}_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}}

\mathcal{T}
on

So

for all
\xi\in\mathfrak{S}
.
Similarly, replacing

by
\mathcal{T}^{-1}

\displaystyle 1\leq\|\mathcal{T}^{m}\|\leq\sqrt{\dfrac{B}{A}},

3.
Stability and Linear Independence

Lemma 3.1
.

Let
\{\Upsilon_{\xi}\}_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}}
be a sequence in
\operatorname{End}_{\mathcal{A}}^{*}(\mathcal{H})
with
\mathcal{R}(\Upsilon_{\xi})\subset\mathfrak{N}_{\xi}
for all
\xi\in\mathfrak{S}
. If

\{\Upsilon_{\xi}\}_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}}
is represented via a linear operator
\mathcal{T}
on

\hbox{span}\{\mathfrak{N}_{\xi}\}_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}}

and
\hbox{span}\{\Upsilon_{\xi}\}_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}}

is infinite dimensional, then

\{\Upsilon_{\xi}\}_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}}

is linearly independent and infinite.

Proof.

Assume that

\{\Upsilon_{\xi}\}_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}}

is linearly dependent. Hence there exist constants

\delta_{p},\ldots,\delta_{q}
such that

\displaystyle\sum_{\xi=p}^{q}\delta_{\xi}\Upsilon_{\xi}=0

and
\delta_{\xi_{0}}\not=0
, for some
p\leq\xi_{0}\leq q
. Set

\displaystyle\alpha=\min\bigg\{\xi:\,\,\,p\leq\xi\leq q,\delta_{\xi}\not=0\bigg\}\,\,\,\,\hbox{and}\,\,\,\,\,b=\max\bigg\{\xi:\,\,\,p\leq\xi\leq q,\delta_{\xi}\not=0\bigg\}.

Then

\displaystyle\Upsilon_{b}=\sum_{\xi=\alpha}^{b-1}d_{\xi}\Upsilon_{\xi}\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\hbox{and}\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\Upsilon_{\alpha}=\sum_{\xi=\alpha+1}^{b}d_{\xi}^{\prime}\Upsilon_{\xi}

for certain constants

\alpha_{\xi}
and
\alpha_{\xi}^{\prime}
,
\xi=\alpha,\ldots,b
.
Thus for every
i\in\mathbb{N}
, we have

\displaystyle\mathcal{T}^{i}\Upsilon_{b}

\displaystyle=\sum_{\xi=\alpha}^{b-1}d_{\xi}\mathcal{T}^{i}\Upsilon_{\xi}

\displaystyle=\sum_{\xi=\alpha}^{b-1}d_{\xi}\Upsilon_{\xi+i}

\displaystyle=\sum_{\xi=\alpha+i}^{b+i-1}d_{\xi-i}\Upsilon_{\xi}.

Similarly,

\mathcal{T}^{-i}\Upsilon_{b}=\sum_{\xi=\alpha-i}^{b-1-i}d_{\xi-i}^{{}^{\prime}}\Upsilon_{\xi}.

Therefore, in addition to

\Upsilon_{\xi}=\mathcal{T}^{\xi}\Upsilon_{0}
show that

\hbox{span}\big\{\Upsilon_{\xi}\big\}_{\alpha,\ldots,b}
is invariant under
\mathcal{T}
and
\mathcal{T}^{-1}
. Also, we have

\displaystyle\hbox{span}\big\{\Upsilon_{\xi}\big\}_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}}=\hbox{span}\big\{\Upsilon_{\xi}\big\}_{\alpha,\ldots,b}.

Hence,

\hbox{span}\big\{\Upsilon_{\xi}\big\}_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}}

is finite dimensional, a contradiction.
∎

Theorem 3.3
.

Consider the sequence
\{\widehat{\Upsilon}_{\xi}\}_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}}
within
\operatorname{End}_{\mathcal{A}}^{*}(\mathcal{H})
, where for each
\xi\in\mathfrak{S}
,
\widehat{\Upsilon}_{\xi}(\mathcal{H})\subseteq\mathfrak{N}_{\xi}
. Let
\{(\mathfrak{N}_{\xi},\Upsilon_{\xi})\}_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}}
represent a
g-
fusion frame via a linear operator
\mathcal{T}
acting on
\hbox{span}\{\mathfrak{N}_{\xi}\}_{\xi\in{\mathfrak{S}}}
. Assume the existence of constants
\eta,\beta\in[0,1)
, fulfilling the inequality

(3.7)

\displaystyle\bigg\|\sum_{\xi=1}^{n}\alpha_{\xi}\big(\Upsilon_{\xi}-\widehat{\Upsilon}_{\xi}\big)(f)\bigg\|\leq\eta\bigg\|\sum_{\xi=1}^{n}\alpha_{\xi}\Upsilon_{\xi}(f)\bigg\|+\beta\bigg\|\sum_{\xi=1}^{n}\alpha_{\xi}\widehat{\Upsilon}_{\xi}(f)\bigg\|

for any
f\in\mathcal{H}
and all finite complex sequences
\{\alpha_{\xi}\}_{\xi=1}^{n}
. Under these conditions, the subsequent statements are valid:

\emph{(i)}

\displaystyle\bigg(\frac{1-\eta}{1+\beta}\sqrt{A}\bigg)^{2}\langle f,f\rangle\leq\sum_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}}\langle\widehat{\Upsilon}_{\xi}(f),\Upsilon_{\xi}(f)\rangle\leq\bigg(\frac{1+\eta}{1-\beta}\sqrt{B}\bigg)^{2}\langle f,f\rangle,

where
A
and
B
are frame bounds of
\{(\mathfrak{N}_{\xi},\Upsilon_{\xi})\}_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}}
.

\emph{(ii)}

If the span of
\Upsilon_{\xi}
is infinite-dimensional and
\{\widehat{\Upsilon}_{\xi}\}_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}}
forms an infinite set, then
\{\widehat{\Upsilon}_{\xi}\}_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}}
exhibits linear independence.

Proof.

Select
l\in\mathfrak{S}
and define
\alpha_{\xi}=\varepsilon_{l\xi}
in (3.3), where

\varepsilon_{l\xi}={\begin{cases}0&\text{if }l\neq\xi,\\
1&\text{if }l=\xi.\end{cases}}

Consequently, for any
f\in\mathcal{H}

\displaystyle\|\Upsilon_{l}(f)-\widehat{\Upsilon}_{l}(f)\|\leq\eta\|\Upsilon_{l}(f)\|+\beta\|\widehat{\Upsilon}_{l}(f)\|.

Then

\displaystyle(1-\beta)\|\widehat{\Upsilon}_{l}(f)\|\leq(1+\eta)\|\Upsilon_{l}(f)\|.

Thus

\displaystyle\langle\widehat{\Upsilon}_{l}(f),\Upsilon_{l}(f)\rangle\leq\bigg(\frac{1+\eta}{1-\beta}\bigg)^{2}\langle\Upsilon_{l}(f),\Upsilon_{l}(f)\rangle.

This implies

\displaystyle\sum_{i\in\mathfrak{S}}\langle\widehat{\Upsilon}_{l}(f),\Upsilon_{l}(f)\rangle\leq\bigg(\frac{1+\eta}{1-\beta}\bigg)^{2}\sum_{i\in\mathfrak{S}}\langle\Upsilon_{l}(f),\Upsilon_{l}(f)\rangle\leq\bigg(\frac{1+\eta}{1-\beta}\bigg)^{2}B\langle f,f\rangle,

where
B
is the upper frame bound of
\{(\mathfrak{N}_{\xi},\Upsilon_{\xi})\}_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}}
.

Similarly, for all
i\in\mathfrak{S}
and
f\in\mathcal{H}
,

\langle\widehat{\Upsilon}_{l}(f),\Upsilon_{l}(f)\rangle\geq\bigg(\frac{1-\eta}{1+\beta}\bigg)^{2}\langle\Upsilon_{l}f,\Upsilon_{l}f\rangle.

Hence

\sum_{i\in\mathfrak{S}}\langle\widehat{\Upsilon}_{l}(f),\Upsilon_{l}(f)\rangle\geq\bigg(\frac{1-\eta}{1+\beta}\bigg)^{2}\sum_{i\in\mathfrak{S}}\langle\Upsilon_{l}f,\Upsilon_{l}f\rangle.

In conclusion,

\displaystyle\sum_{i\in\mathfrak{S}}\langle\widehat{\Upsilon}_{l}(f),\Upsilon_{l}(f)\rangle\geq\bigg(\frac{1-\eta}{1+\beta}\bigg)^{2}A\langle f,f\rangle.

That is,

\displaystyle\bigg(\frac{1-\eta}{1+\beta}\sqrt{A}\bigg)^{2}\langle f,f\rangle\leq\sum_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}}\langle\widehat{\Upsilon}_{\xi}(f),\Upsilon_{\xi}(f)\rangle\leq\bigg(\frac{1+\eta}{1-\beta}\sqrt{B}\bigg)^{2}\langle f,f\rangle,

Let
\{\alpha_{\xi}\}_{\xi=1}^{n}
be a finite sequence in
\mathbb{C}
with
\sum_{\xi=1}^{n}\alpha_{\xi}\widehat{\Upsilon}_{\xi}=0
. Given
\eta\in[0,1)
, by (3.3)

\displaystyle\sum_{\xi=1}^{n}\alpha_{\xi}\Upsilon_{\xi}=0.

As per Proposition 3.1,
\alpha_{\xi}=0
for all
k=1,\ldots,n
.
Thus,
\{\widehat{\Upsilon}_{\xi}\}_{\xi\in\mathfrak{S}}
is linearly independent.
