Primes of higher degree and Annihilators of Class groups
Nimish Kumar Mahapatra, Prem Prakash Pandey, Mahesh Kumar Ram

Abstract
. Let L / K L/K be a Galois extension of number fields with Galois group G G . We discuss a new method to obtain elements in ℤ ⁡ [ G ] \mathbb{Z}[G] which annihilate the class group of L L . Using this method, we obtain annihilators of class groups of cyclotomic fields. We show that these annihilators are new. Some more consequences are also discussed. Moreover, we mention some results and connections to highlight importance of primes of higher residue degree.

Introduction

Let

Elements

of

[

For the cyclotomic field

Theorem 1.1
.

Every ideal class in the class group of

Prime ideal of higher residue degree are not that well explored. In this article, we aim to highlight that their study leads to important results. In this direction, the second author proposed a new idea to construct annihilators of class groups for certain relative extension of number fields

2.
Preliminaries

We begin with stating the quadratic reciprocity law. For any pair of odd primes
p,q
we use
(\frac{p}{q})
to denote the Legendre symbol. The law of quadratic reciprocity states the following

\left(\frac{p}{q}\right)\left(\frac{q}{p}\right)=(-1)^{\frac{p-1}{2}\cdot\frac{q-1}{2}}.

If both
p
and
q
are congruent to
3
modulo
4
then the congruence
x^{2}\equiv p\pmod{q}
is solvable if and only if the congruence
x^{2}\equiv-q\pmod{p}
is solvable. When at least one of
p
and
q
is congruent to
1
modulo
4
then the congruence
x^{2}\equiv p\pmod{q}
is solvable if and only if the congruence
x^{2}\equiv q\pmod{p}
is solvable.

In
[
4

Proposition 2.1

Let

be a prime different from

p\equiv 3\pmod{4}
and

.

If
q\equiv 7\pmod{8}
is a prime different from

, then using Proposition

and law of quadratic reciprocity we see that there exists a prime

such that

and
-q
is a quadratic residue modulo

.

Examples with non-trivial
\mathbf{R}_{F/\mathbb{Q}}
and concluding remarks

In this section we provide several examples of biquadratic extensions

for which
\mathbf{R}_{F/\mathbb{Q}}
is non-trivial. Let
F
be a number field of degree
n
over
\mathbb{Q}
and discriminant
d_{F}
. Suppose
n=r_{1}+2r_{2}
, where
r_{1}
and
2r_{2}
denote the number of real and complex embeddings of
F
respectively. Then

M_{F}=\frac{n!}{n^{n}}\left(\frac{4}{\pi}\right)^{r_{2}}\sqrt{\left|d_{f}\right|}

is called the Minkowski constant of
F
. It is well known that every ideal class of
F
contains a prime ideal of norm not exceeding
M_{F}
.

Proposition 5.1
.

The prime ideal of
\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{79})
above
3
is non-principal.

Proof.

We note that the Minkowski constant for
F=\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{79})
is
9
. The factorisation of primes above
2,3,5,7
is easily obtained:

2\mathcal{O}_{F}=\mathfrak{p}_{2}^{2},3\mathcal{O}_{F}=\mathfrak{p}_{3}\mathfrak{p}_{3}^{\prime},5\mathcal{O}_{F}=\mathfrak{p}_{5}\mathfrak{p}_{5}^{\prime},7\mathcal{O}_{F}=\mathfrak{p}_{7}\mathfrak{p}_{7}^{\prime}.

As the class number of
\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{79})
is
3
, it follows that
\mathfrak{p}_{2}
is principal. Next we see that

(10)

\langle 4+\sqrt{79}\rangle=\mathfrak{p}_{3}^{2}\mathfrak{p}_{7}

and

(11)

\langle 3+\sqrt{79}\rangle=\mathfrak{p}_{2}\mathfrak{p}_{5}\mathfrak{p}_{7}.

If the ideal
\mathfrak{p}_{3}
is principal then from (
10
) we find
\mathfrak{p}_{7}
is principal. Now from (
11
) we obtain that
\mathfrak{p}_{5}
is principal. Consequently, all the primes of norm less than the Minkowski constant are principal. This contradiction establishes the proposition.
∎

Theorem 5.2
.

Let
u
be a square-free integer such that
u\equiv 2\pmod{3}
. Let

denote the compositum of

\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{79})

\mathbf{R}_{F/\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{79})}

and
\mathfrak{p}

\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{79})

If
