Phase-selective growth of κ \kappa - vs β \beta -Ga 2 O 3 and (In x Ga 1-x ) 2 O 3 by In-mediated metal exchange catalysis in plasma-assisted molecular beam epitaxy Preprint: AIP/123-QED
A. Ardenghi*, O. Bierwagen*, J. Lähnemann, J. Kler, A. Falkenstein, M. Martin, P. Mazzolini*

Abstract
Its piezo- and potentially ferroelectric properties make the metastable kappa polymorph of Ga 2 O 3 an interesting material for multiple applications, while In-incorporation into any polymorphs of Ga 2 O 3 allows to lower their bandgap. In this work, we provide a guideline to achieve single phase κ \kappa -, β \beta -Ga 2 O 3 as well as their (In x Ga 1-x ) 2 O 3 alloys up to x x = 0.14 and x x = 0.17 respectively, using In-mediated metal exchange catalysis in plasma assisted molecular beam epitaxy (MEXCAT-MBE). The polymorph transition from κ \kappa to β \beta is also addressed, highlighting the fundamental role played by the thermal stability of the κ \kappa -Ga 2 O 3 . Additionally, we also demonstrate the possibility to grow ( 2 ¯ \bar{2} 01) β \beta -Ga 2 O 3 on top of α \alpha -Al 2 O 3 (0001) at temperatures at least 100 °C above those achievable with conventional non-catalyzed MBE, opening the road for increased crystal quality in heteroepitaxy. The role of the substrate, as well as strain and structural defects in the growth of κ \kappa -Ga 2 O 3 is also investigated by growing simultaneously on three different materials: (i) α \alpha -Al 2 O 3 (0001), (ii) 20 nm of ( 2 ¯ \bar{2} 01) β \beta -Ga 2 O 3 on α \alpha -Al 2 O 3 (0001) and (iii) ( 2 ¯ \bar{2} 01) β \beta -Ga 2 O 3 single crystal.
