Magic square and three-zero textures for Dirac neutrinos
Ryota Minamizawa, Yuta Hyodo, Teruyuki Kitabayashi

Abstract
We show a matrix decomposition of flavor mass matrix for Dirac neutrinos M M by sum as M = M ′ + M 0 M=M^{\prime}+M^{0} where M ′ M^{\prime} obeys the feature of the magic square and M 0 M^{0} is three-zero texture. The favorable three-zero textures in the context of magic square are explored. It turned out that so-called the normal ordering of neutrino masses is favored over the inverted ordering in the context of magic square.

2.1
Magic square

A magic square of order

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{|c|c|c|}\hline\cr\ 2&\ 7&\ 6\\
\hline\cr 9&5&1\\
\hline\cr 4&3&8\\
\hline\cr\end{array}\begin{array}[]{c}\leftarrow 15\\

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{ccccccc}&\nearrow&~\uparrow&~\uparrow&~\uparrow&\nwarrow&\\
15&&~15&~15&~15&&15\\

It is known that the following Lucas’s matrix is a simple realization of the magic squares

\displaystyle M^{\rm magic}=\left(\begin{matrix}\gamma-\beta&\gamma+\alpha+\beta&\gamma-\alpha\\

2.2
Decomposition and three-zero textures

With Lucas’s matrix, the Dirac flavor neutrino mass matrix

\displaystyle M=\left(\begin{matrix}a&b&c\\
d&e&f\\
g&h&i\end{matrix}\right),

(12)

can be decomposed

\displaystyle M=M^{\rm magic}+M^{\rm 0},

(13)

\displaystyle M^{\rm 0}=\left(\begin{matrix}a_{0}&b_{0}&c_{0}\\
d_{0}&e_{0}&f_{0}\\
g_{0}&h_{0}&i_{0}\end{matrix}\right).

(14)

This decomposition applies to any arbitrary complex matrix, not just the Dirac flavor neutrino mass matrix.

The number of complex elements in the complex matrix

is nine. Since the number of complex elements in Lucas’s form

is three, the number of independent complex elements in
M^{\rm 0}
is six. Three complex elements in

and
M^{\rm 0}

M^{\rm 0}
may be three-zero textures.

anti-trace

Because the number of parameters is reduced from 5 to 3, the magic square condition in Eq.(

3
Favorable three-zero textures

3.1
Criteria

For previous section, we showed that 24 three-zero textures can be compared with observations. Which of 24 three-zero textures is the most favored?

In this paper, it is assumed that the Dirac flavor neutrino mass matrix

can be decomposed by sum containing the Lucas’s matrix
M^{\rm magic}
. This is the most important assumption. Thus, the case of

\displaystyle\epsilon_{i}^{\ell m}=\left|\frac{M_{\ell m}-(M^{\rm magic}_{i})_{\ell m}}{(M^{\rm magic}_{i})_{\ell m}}\right|.

\displaystyle\epsilon_{i}=\frac{1}{6}\sum_{\ell,m=1,2,3}\epsilon_{i}^{\ell m}=0.

4
Summary

The number of zero elements permitted in the texture zeros neutrino flavor mass matrix is an important consideration. In this paper, we decomposed the Dirac flavor neutrino mass matrix as
M=M^{\prime}+M^{0}
, where
M^{\prime}
is a matrix without zero elements and
M^{0}
is a matrix with zero elements. We have shown that if
M^{\prime}
obeys a magic square, the three elements of
M^{0}
can be naturally zero. The 24 three-zero textures that can be compared to the experimental results are numerically estimated, and we chose the preferred matrices in the context of magic square. It turned out that NO case is favored over IO, in the context of magic square.
