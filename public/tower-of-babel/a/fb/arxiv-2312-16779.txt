A new approach to the study of elliptic semilinear equations
Pilar Herreros

Abstract
. In this paper we define a new operator J J for the study of Δ ​ u + f ⁡ ( u ) = 0 , x ∈ ℝ N , N > 2 . \Delta u+f(u)=0,\quad x\in\mathbb{R}^{N},N>2. The main goal of this paper is to introduce a new approach for the study of radial solutions of
