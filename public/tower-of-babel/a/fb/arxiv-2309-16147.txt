Rayleigh waves in isotropic elastic materials with micro-voids
Emilian Bulgariu, and, Ionel-Dumitrel Ghiba, and, Hassam Khan, Patrizio Neff

Abstract
In this paper, we show that a general method introduced by Fu and Mielke allows to give a complete answer on the existence and uniqueness of a subsonic solution describing the propagation of surface waves in an isotropic half space modelled with the linear theory of isotropic elastic materials with micro-voids. Our result is valid for the entire class of materials admitting real wave propagation which include auxetic materials (negative Poisson’s ration) and composite materials with negative-stiffness inclusions (negative Young’s modulus). Moreover, the used method allows to formulate a simple and complete numerical strategy for the computation of the solution. Keywords: relaxed micromorphic model, materials with micro-voids, Riccati equation, Rayleigh waves, secular equation, existence and uniqueness, micro-voids model, Fu and Mielke’s method. Existence and uniqueness of Rayleigh waves

Following the ideea by Fu and Mielke
[
25
]
we are now looking at (
6.8
) as to an initial value problem and we search the solution in the form

\displaystyle y(x_{2})=e^{-k\,x_{2}\,{\mathcal{E}}}y(0),

(7.1)

where

is to be determined as solution (see (

\displaystyle[{\mathcal{T}}{\mathcal{E}}^{2}-{\rm i}\,({\mathcal{R}}+{\mathcal{R}}^{T}){\mathcal{E}}-{\mathcal{Q}}+\ k^{2}v^{2}{{{\mathbbm{1}}}}]\,y(x_{2})=0,\qquad\qquad\qquad(-{\mathcal{T}}{\mathcal{E}}+{\rm i}\,\,{\mathcal{R}}^{T})\,y(0)=0.

(7.2)

In order to have a proper decay, the eigenvalues of
{\mathcal{E}}
have to be such that their real part is positive. The unknowns in (
7.2
) are
{\mathcal{E}}\in\mathbb{C}^{3\times 3}
and
v
.

We are doing a change of variable by introducing the so called
surface impedance matrix

\displaystyle{{\mathcal{M}}}=-(-{\mathcal{T}}{\mathcal{E}}+{\rm i}\,{\mathcal{R}}^{T})\qquad\iff\qquad{\mathcal{E}}={\mathcal{T}}^{-1}({\mathcal{M}}+{\rm i}\,\,{\mathcal{R}}^{T}),

(7.3)

which, by substituting
\eqref{10}_{2}
into
\eqref{09}_{1}
, must be a solution of the
algebraic Riccati equation

\displaystyle({\mathcal{M}}-{\rm i}\,{\mathcal{R}}){\mathcal{T}}^{-1}({\mathcal{M}}+{\rm i}\,\mathcal{R^{T}})-{\mathcal{Q}}+k\,v^{2}\,{{{\mathbbm{1}}}}=0,\qquad\qquad{{\mathcal{M}}}\,y(0)=0.

(7.4)

Since we are interested in a nontrivial solution
y
, we impose
y(0)\neq 0
, so that the matrix
{\mathcal{M}}
has to satisfy

\displaystyle{\rm det}\,{\mathcal{M}}=0.

(7.5)

The equation (
7.5
) is called
secular equation
for the micro-voids model in terms of the
impedance matrix

{\mathcal{M}}
.

The strategy to solve the system of nonlinear equations (
7.4
)
1
and (
7.5
) is the following. We find an admissible domain for
v
such that, for fixed
v
in this domain we may define the mapping
v\mapsto{{\mathcal{M}}}_{v}
, where
{\mathcal{M}}_{v}
is the solution of (
7.4
)
1
. Note that not any
{\mathcal{M}}_{v}
is admissible because
{\mathcal{M}}_{v}
has to be such that
\text{Re(spec}\,{\mathcal{E}}{\rm)}
is positive, where “
\text{\rm Re\,(spec\,}{\mathcal{E}}{\rm)}
” means the “real part of spectra of
{\mathcal{E}}
”​​. In the end, having
{\mathcal{M}}_{v}
we find
v
as solution of (
7.5
). An important aspect is that we have to be sure that the solution
v
belongs to the admissible domain considered for having a suitable
{\mathcal{M}}_{v}
and that the solution is unique, since otherwise the uniqueness of the matrix
{\mathcal{M}}_{v}
is violated.

Since
{\mathcal{T}}
and
{\mathcal{Q}}
are symmetric and positive definite matrices, many aspects from the above discussions are purely mathematical questions, and they are not specific to the elastic materials with micro-voids.
Indeed, since
{\mathcal{T}}
,
{\mathcal{Q}}
and
\widehat{{{\mathbbm{1}}}}
are symmetric real matrices, the results established in
[
54
,
25
]
remains valid in the framework of the elastic materials with micro-voids.

First, in view of Lemma (
6.1
) and the item i) of Theorem
5.2
, if the constitutive coefficients satisfy the conditions
\mu_{\rm e}>0,\,\lambda_{\rm e}+2\mu_{\rm e}>0,\,\alpha>0
and
(\lambda_{\rm e}+2\mu_{\rm e})\,\xi>\beta^{2}

and
0\leq v<\widehat{v}
, the matrix problem

\displaystyle{\mathcal{T}}{\mathcal{E}}^{2}-{\rm i}\,\,({\mathcal{R}}+{\mathcal{R}}^{T}){\mathcal{E}}-{\mathcal{Q}}+k^{2}v^{2}{{{\mathbbm{1}}}}=0,\qquad\text{\rm Re\,(spec\,}{\mathcal{E}}{\rm)}>0,

obtained from

{\mathcal{M}}_{v}
of the Riccati equation (

satisfying the equation (
