New bosonic hard string scattering amplitudes and extended Gross conjecture in superstring theory
Sheng-Hong Lai, Jen-Chi Lee, Yi Yang

Abstract
We discover new hard string scattering amplitudes (HSSA) with polarizations orthogonal to the scattering plane in the NS sector of 10 ​ D 10D open superstring theory. The corresponding HSSA in the bosonic string theory are of subleading order in energy. In addition, all HSSA are found to be proportional to each other and the ratios among these HSSA are calculated to justify Gross conjecture in 1988 on high energy symmetry of string theory. Moreover, together with these new HSSA, the total number of HSSA calculated in the first massive level of NS sector are found to agree with those of hard polarized fermion string scattering amplitudes (PFSSA) in the R sector calculated recently.

\displaystyle\left\langle V^{(-1)}Tensor^{(-1)}\phi^{(0)}\phi^{(0)}\right\rangle=B\left(\frac{-s}{2},\frac{-t}{2}+\frac{1}{2}\right)

\displaystyle\cdot\left(-k_{3}^{T}\right)\left[\begin{array}[c]{c}\left(\epsilon_{1}\cdot\epsilon_{2}\right)\left(k_{3}\cdot k_{4}\right)\\
-\left(k_{3}\cdot\epsilon_{1}\right)\left(k_{4}\cdot\epsilon_{2}\right)\frac{s}{u+1}\\
+\left(k_{3}\cdot\epsilon_{2}\right)\left(k_{4}\cdot\epsilon_{1}\right)\frac{s}{t+1}\end{array}\right].

where
B\left(\frac{-s}{2},\frac{-t}{2}+\frac{1}{2}\right)=\frac{\Gamma\left(\frac{-s}{2}\right)\Gamma\left(\frac{-t}{2}+\frac{1}{2}\right)}{\Gamma\left(\frac{u}{2}+\frac{1}{2}\right)}
is the Beta function.

Finally in the hard scattering limit we have

\displaystyle k_{1}

\displaystyle=\left(E,-E,0\right),k_{2}=\left(E,E,0\right),

\displaystyle k_{3}

\displaystyle=\left(-E,-E\cos\theta,-E\sin\theta\right),

\displaystyle k_{4}

\displaystyle=\left(-E,E\cos\theta,E\sin\theta\right)

(3.42)

and

\displaystyle\epsilon_{1}

\displaystyle=\epsilon_{2}=e^{T}=\left(0,0,1\right),s=4E^{2},

\displaystyle\frac{s}{u}

\displaystyle\rightarrow\frac{-1}{\cos^{2}\frac{\theta}{2}},\frac{s}{t}\rightarrow\frac{-1}{\sin^{2}\frac{\theta}{2}},k_{3}\cdot k_{4}=\frac{-s}{2}-1.

(3.43)

After a bit calculation, one ends up with

\left\langle V^{(-1)}Tensor_{(\alpha_{-1}^{T}b_{\frac{-1}{2}}^{T})}^{(-1)}\phi^{(0)}\phi^{(0)}\right\rangle=-2k_{3}^{T}E^{2}B

(3.44)

Similar calculations can be performed if one replaces the second vertex

\displaystyle=\frac{-2k_{3}^{T}E^{2}B}{M^{2}},

(3.45)

\displaystyle\left\langle V^{(-1)}Tensor_{(b_{-\frac{3}{2}}^{T})}^{(-1)}\phi^{(0)}\phi^{(0)}\right\rangle

\displaystyle=\frac{2k_{3}^{T}E^{2}B}{M}.

(3.46)

These results justify the ratios calculated in Eq.(

). They also agree

susy

III.3
New hard SSA II

So far all HSSA calculated in this section are from the positive-norm
symmetric spin two state. One expects HSSA contributed from the anti-symmetric
spin three state. In this subsection we propose the following HSSA

\left\langle b_{\frac{-1}{2}}^{T_{K}},b_{\frac{-1}{2}}^{L}b_{\frac{-1}{2}}^{T_{I}}b_{\frac{-1}{2}}^{T_{J}},\phi_{3},\phi_{4}\right\rangle,

(3.51)

which was not considered in
susy
. The calculation of this HSSA is
lengthy but straightforward. We begin with the standard calculation of string
scattering amplitude

\displaystyle\left\langle V^{(-1)}Tensor_{bbb}^{(-1)}\phi^{(0)}\phi^{(0)}\right\rangle={\displaystyle\int}dz_{1}dz_{2}dz_{3}dz_{4}\frac{\left|z_{13}z_{14}z_{34}\right|}{dz_{1}dz_{3}dz_{4}}\left\langle\begin{array}[c]{c}\left(\epsilon_{\mu_{1}}^{T_{K}}\psi_{1}^{\mu_{1}}e^{-\phi_{1}}e^{ik_{1}X_{1}}\right)\left(\epsilon_{\mu_{2}}^{L}\epsilon_{\mu 3}^{T_{I}}\epsilon_{\mu 4}^{T_{J}}\psi_{2}^{\mu_{2}}\psi_{2}^{\mu_{3}}\psi_{2}^{\mu_{4}}e^{-\phi_{1}}e^{ik_{2}X_{2}}\right)\\
\times\left(k_{\mu_{5}}^{3}\psi_{3}^{\mu_{5}}e^{ik_{3}X_{3}}\right)\left(k_{\mu_{6}}^{4}\psi_{4}^{\mu_{6}}e^{ik_{4}X_{4}}\right)\end{array}\right\rangle

\displaystyle=\epsilon_{\mu_{1}}^{T_{K}}\epsilon_{\mu_{2}}^{L}\epsilon_{\mu 3}^{T_{I}}\epsilon_{\mu 4}^{T_{J}}k_{\mu_{5}}^{3}k_{\mu_{6}}^{4}{\displaystyle\int}dz_{2}\left|z_{13}z_{14}z_{34}\right|\left\langle\psi_{1}^{\mu_{1}}\psi_{2}^{\mu_{2}}\psi_{2}^{\mu_{3}}\psi_{2}^{\mu_{4}}\psi_{3}^{\mu_{5}}\psi_{4}^{\mu_{6}}\right\rangle\left\langle e^{-\phi_{1}}e^{-\phi_{2}}\right\rangle\left\langle e^{ik_{1}X_{1}}e^{ik_{2}X_{2}}e^{ik_{3}X_{3}}e^{ik_{4}X_{4}}\right\rangle.

(3.54)

The next step is to fix the gauge
z_{1}=0,z_{3}=1,z_{4}\rightarrow\infty

(0<z_{2}<1)
to get

\displaystyle\left\langle V^{(-1)}Tensor_{bbb}^{(-1)}\phi^{(0)}\phi^{(0)}\right\rangle

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\epsilon_{\mu_{1}}^{T_{K}}\epsilon_{\mu_{2}}^{L}\epsilon_{\mu 3}^{T_{I}}\epsilon_{\mu 4}^{T_{J}}k_{\mu_{5}}^{3}k_{\mu_{6}}^{4}{\displaystyle\int}dz_{2}\left(z_{2}\right)^{k_{1}\cdot k_{2}-2}\left(1-z_{2}\right)^{k_{2}\cdot k_{3}-1}

\displaystyle\cdot\left(\begin{array}[c]{c}\eta^{\mu_{1}\mu_{2}}\eta^{\mu_{4}\mu_{5}}\eta^{\mu_{3}\mu_{6}}-\eta^{\mu_{1}\mu_{2}}\eta^{\mu_{3}\mu_{5}}\eta^{\mu_{4}\mu_{6}}\\
\eta^{\mu_{1}\mu_{3}}\eta^{\mu_{2}\mu_{5}}\eta^{\mu_{4}\mu_{6}}-\eta^{\mu_{1}\mu_{3}}\eta^{\mu_{4}\mu_{5}}\eta^{\mu_{2}\mu_{6}}\\
\eta^{\mu_{1}\mu_{4}}\eta^{\mu_{3}\mu_{5}}\eta^{\mu_{2}\mu_{6}}-\eta^{\mu_{1}\mu_{4}}\eta^{\mu_{2}\mu_{5}}\eta^{\mu_{3}\mu_{6}}\end{array}\right).

We can now use
k_{1}\cdot k_{2}=-\frac{s}{2}+1,k_{2}\cdot k_{3}=-\frac{t}{2}+\frac{1}{2}
and perform the integration to get

\displaystyle\left\langle V^{(-1)}Tensor_{bbb}^{(-1)}\phi^{(0)}\phi^{(0)}\right\rangle

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\epsilon_{\mu_{1}}^{T_{K}}\epsilon_{\mu_{2}}^{L}\epsilon_{\mu 3}^{T_{I}}\epsilon_{\mu 4}^{T_{J}}k_{\mu_{5}}^{3}k_{\mu_{6}}^{4}{\displaystyle\int}dz_{2}\left(z_{2}\right)^{-\frac{s}{2}-1}\left(1-z_{2}\right)^{-\frac{t}{2}-\frac{1}{2}}

\displaystyle\cdot\left(\begin{array}[c]{c}\eta^{\mu_{1}\mu_{3}}\eta^{\mu_{2}\mu_{5}}\eta^{\mu_{4}\mu_{6}}-\eta^{\mu_{1}\mu_{3}}\eta^{\mu_{4}\mu_{5}}\eta^{\mu_{2}\mu_{6}}\\
\eta^{\mu_{1}\mu_{4}}\eta^{\mu_{3}\mu_{5}}\eta^{\mu_{2}\mu_{6}}-\eta^{\mu_{1}\mu_{4}}\eta^{\mu_{2}\mu_{5}}\eta^{\mu_{3}\mu_{6}}\end{array}\right).

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\frac{\Gamma\left(\frac{-s}{2}\right)\Gamma\left(\frac{-t}{2}+\frac{1}{2}\right)}{\Gamma\left(\frac{u}{2}+\frac{1}{2}\right)}

\displaystyle\cdot\left(\begin{array}[c]{c}\delta_{IK}\frac{1}{M}\left(\frac{-t}{2}+\frac{1}{2}\right)k_{4}^{T_{J}}-\delta_{IK}\frac{1}{M}\left(\frac{-u}{2}+\frac{1}{2}\right)k_{3}^{T_{J}}\\
\delta_{JK}\frac{1}{M}\left(\frac{-u}{2}+\frac{1}{2}\right)k_{3}^{T_{I}}-\delta_{JK}\frac{1}{M}\left(\frac{-t}{2}+\frac{1}{2}\right)k_{4}^{T_{I}}\end{array}\right).

In the hard scattering limit, the HSSA reduces to

\displaystyle\left\langle b_{\frac{-1}{2}}^{T_{K}},b_{\frac{-1}{2}}^{L}b_{\frac{-1}{2}}^{T_{I}}b_{\frac{-1}{2}}^{T_{J}},\phi_{3},\phi_{4}\right\rangle

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\left[\begin{array}[c]{c}\delta_{IK}\frac{1}{M}\left(\frac{-t}{2}\right)k_{4}^{T_{J}}-\delta_{IK}\frac{1}{M}\left(\frac{-u}{2}\right)k_{3}^{T_{J}}\\
\delta_{JK}\frac{1}{M}\left(\frac{-u}{2}\right)k_{3}^{T_{I}}-\delta_{JK}\frac{1}{M}\left(\frac{-t}{2}\right)k_{4}^{T_{I}}\end{array}\right]B.

There are three cases to further reduce the result:

Case one
: for
\epsilon^{T_{K}}=e^{T},\epsilon^{T_{I}}=e^{T},\epsilon^{T_{J}}=e^{T_{j}},

\displaystyle-\left[\frac{1}{M}\left(\frac{-t}{2}\right)k_{4}^{T_{j}}-\frac{1}{M}\left(\frac{-u}{2}\right)k_{3}^{T_{j}}+0-0\right]

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{M}\left(\frac{t}{2}\right)\times 0+\frac{1}{M}\left(\frac{-u}{2}\right)\times 0=0.

(3.64)

Case two
: for
\epsilon^{T_{K}}=e^{T},\epsilon^{T_{I}}=e^{T_{i}},\epsilon^{T_{J}}=e^{T_{j}},

-\left[\begin{array}[c]{c}0\times\frac{1}{M}\left(\frac{-t}{2}\right)k_{4}^{T_{j}}-0\times\frac{1}{M}\left(\frac{-u}{2}\right)k_{3}^{T_{j}}\\

(3.65)

Case three
: for
\epsilon^{T_{K}}=e^{T_{k}},\epsilon^{T_{I}}=e^{T},\epsilon^{T_{J}}=e^{T_{j}},

\displaystyle-\left[\begin{array}[c]{c}0\times\frac{1}{M}\left(\frac{-t}{2}\right)k_{4}^{T_{j}}-0\times\frac{1}{M}\left(\frac{-u}{2}\right)k_{3}^{T_{j}}\\
+\delta_{jk}\frac{1}{M}\left(\frac{-u}{2}\right)k_{3}^{T}-\delta_{jk}\frac{1}{M}\left(\frac{-t}{2}\right)k_{4}^{T}\end{array}\right]

\displaystyle=\delta_{jk}\frac{1}{2M}\left(t+u\right)k_{3}^{T}=\frac{-2E^{2}\delta_{jk}k_{3}^{T}}{M}.

(3.68)

In summary, we get the following ratios in the NS sector

\displaystyle\left\langle b_{\frac{-1}{2}}^{T},\alpha_{-1}^{T}b_{\frac{-1}{2}}^{T}\right\rangle:\left\langle b_{\frac{-1}{2}}^{T},\alpha_{-1}^{L}b_{\frac{-1}{2}}^{L}\right\rangle:\left\langle b_{\frac{-1}{2}}^{T},b_{\frac{-3}{2}}^{L}\right\rangle:\left\langle b_{\frac{-1}{2}}^{T_{i}},\alpha_{-1}^{T}b_{\frac{-1}{2}}^{T_{j}}\right\rangle:\left\langle b_{\frac{-1}{2}}^{T_{k}},b_{\frac{-1}{2}}^{L}b_{\frac{-1}{2}}^{T}b_{\frac{-1}{2}}^{T_{l}}\right\rangle

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-2k_{3}^{T}E^{2}B:\frac{-2k_{3}^{T}E^{2}B}{M^{2}}:\frac{2k_{3}^{T}E^{2}B}{M}:\delta_{ij}2k_{3}^{T}E^{2}B:\delta_{lk}\frac{-2k_{3}^{T}E^{2}}{M}B=1:\frac{1}{M^{2}}:-\frac{1}{M}:-\delta_{ij}:\frac{\delta_{lk}}{M}.

(3.69)

where we have, for simplicity, omitted the last two tachyon
vertices in the notation of each HSSA in Eq.(
3.69
), which are the
superstring version of the bosonic string ratios in Eq.(
2.24

IV
Discussion

One main result of this work is the derivation of the superstring version of
Eq.(
2.24
), namely, the ratios in Eq.(
3.69
). The important
motivation to do this derivation is of course due to the recent calculation of
the hard PFSSA in
PFSSA
.

Both the effects of ”worldsheet fermion exchange”
susy
and

\mathit{no}
”inter-particle gauge transformation” of the NS sector shown in
this paper are related to the existence of R sector for the case of
superstring. This means that it is important to include R sector
PFSSA

when considering high energy symmetry of superstring theory. Unfortunately,
the construction of the general massive fermion string vertex operators is
difficult at this stage. However, the results of this paper seem to indicate
that spacetime SUSY of the spectrum may help to provide useful information.
