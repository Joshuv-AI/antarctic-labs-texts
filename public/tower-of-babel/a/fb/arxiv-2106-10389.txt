Dirichlet Problem for Complex Monge-Ampère equation near an isolated Klt singularity
Xin Fu

Abstract
. We solve the Dirichlet Problem for complex Monge-Ampère equation near an isolate Klt singularity, which generalizes the result of Eyssidieux-Guedj-Zeriahi [ 12 ] , where the Monge-Ampère equation is solved on singular varieties without boundary. As a corollary, we construct solutions to Monge-Ampère equation with isolated singularity on strongly pseudoconvex domain Ω \Omega contained in ℂ n \mathbb{C}^{n} .



1.
Introduction

Complex Monge-Ampère equations are a fundamental tool to study Kähler geometry and, in particular, canonical Kähler metrics of Einstein type on smooth and singular Kähler varieties. Yau’s solution to the Calabi conjecture establishes the existence of Ricci flat Kähler metrics on Kähler manifolds of vanishing first Chern class by a priori estimates for complex Monge-Ampère equations
[
26
]
.

Theorem 1.1

(Yau
[
26
]
)
.

Let
(X,\omega)
be a Kähler manifold of complex dimension
n
equipped with a Kähler metric
\omega
.
We consider the following complex Monge-Ampère equation

(1.1)

(\omega+\sqrt{-1}\partial\overline{\partial}\varphi)^{n}=e^{-f}\omega^{n},

where
f\in C^{\infty}(X)
satisfies the normalization condition

\int_{X}e^{-f}\omega^{n}=\int_{X}\omega^{n}=[\omega]^{n}.

In the deep work of Kolodziej
[
18
]
, Yau’s
C^{0}
-estimate for solutions of equation (
1.1
) is tremendously improved by applying the pluripotential theory and it has important applications for singular and degenerate geometric complex Monge-Ampère equations.

Theorem 1.2

(Kolodziej
[
18
]
)
.

Suppose the right hand side of equation (
1.1
) satisfies the following
L^{p}
bound

\int_{X}e^{-pf}\omega^{n}\leq K,~\textnormal{for ~some}~p>1,

then there exists
C=C(X,\omega,p,K)>0
such that any solution
\varphi
of equation (
1.1
) satisfies the the following
L^{\infty}
-estimate

\|\varphi-\sup_{X}\varphi\|_{L^{\infty}(X)}\leq C.

By using Kolodziej’s fundamental estimate, Kähler-Einstein metric on canonical polarized variety with Klt singularity is constructed in
[
12
]
. More precisely, the following holds:

Theorem 1.3

(Eyssidieux-Guedj-Zariahi
[
12
]
, Zhang
[
27
]
)
.

Suppose
X
is canonical polarized or Calabi-Yau variety with Klt singularity, then there exists a solution
\varphi\in\textnormal{PSH}(X,\omega)\cap C^{\infty}(X_{reg})\cap L^{\infty}_{loc}(X_{reg})
on the regular locus of
X
to the equation (
1.1
).

Remark 1.1
.

Choosing suitable right hand side of equation (
1.1
), the metric induced by
\omega+\sqrt{-1}\partial\overline{\partial}\varphi
is a
Kähler-Einstein metric with negative scalar curvature or Ricci flat metric on
X
.

On the other hand, Dirichlet problem for complex Monge-Ampère equation is studied in the fundamental work of Bedford-Taylor
[
2
,
3
]
and also Caffarelli- Kohn-Nirenberg-Spruck
[
6
]
. (see also
[
15
,
7
]
). Therefore it is a natural question to study the Dirichlet problem for complex Monge-Ampère equations near an isolated Klt singularity from the PDE point of view.

Another motivation to study the Dirichlet problem for complex Monge-Ampère equations near an isolated Klt singularity is to understand the tangent cone of Kähler-Einstein metrics proposed by Donaldson-Sun in
[
11
]
. For example, in the discussion section of
[
16
, Page 116]
, it is proposed to study the local Ricci flat metric near an isolated Klt singularity and show that the metric tangent cone only depends on the algebraic structure of the singularity. In this short note, we first show the existence of local Ricci flat metrics by solving Dirichlet problem for complex Monge-Ampère equation.

Now we proceed to set up the equation. Let
(X,p)
be an affine variety embedded in
(\mathbb{C}^{n},0)
with
p
an isolated singular point of
X
and also let
\rho
be a nonnegative smooth plurisubharmonic (PSH) function on
\mathbb{C}^{n}
with
\rho(0)=0
and
\sqrt{-1}\partial\overline{\partial}\rho>0
. We let
\mathcal{U}\subset\subset X
be the open domain defined by

(1.2)

\mathcal{U}=\{\rho<a\}\cap X,

for some sufficiently small constant
a>0
.
In this note, we fix

\rho=\log(1+\sum_{j=1}^{n}|z_{j}|^{2}).

By choosing a generic sufficiently small
a>0
, we can assume that
\partial\mathcal{U}
is smooth and strongly pseudoconvex. We will fix such a domain
\mathcal{U}
for
(X,p)
for the rest of the paper. If the canonical divisor
K_{\mathcal{U}}
is Cartier, we will fix a local volume measure
\Omega_{X}
on
\mathcal{U}
by

\Omega_{X}=(\sqrt{-1})^{n}\nu\wedge\overline{\nu},

where
\nu
is a local generator of
K_{\mathcal{U}}
. Similarly, we can define
\Omega_{X}
when
K_{\mathcal{U}}
is only
\mathbb{Q}
-Cartier.

We will consider the following Dirichlet problem for a complex Monge-Ampère equation related to the Kähler-Einstein metric on
\mathcal{U}
.

(1.3)

\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}(\sqrt{-1}\partial\overline{\partial}\varphi)^{n}=\Omega_{X},\\
&\\
\varphi|_{\partial\mathcal{U}}=\psi,\\
\end{array}\right.

where
\psi\in C^{\infty}(\partial\mathcal{U})
. Now we can state the main result of the paper.

Theorem 1.4
.

Let
(\mathcal{U},p)
be a germ of an isolated Klt singularity defined as in definition
1.2
. There exists a solution
\varphi\in\textnormal{PSH}(\mathcal{U})\cap C^{\infty}(\overline{\mathcal{U}}\setminus\{p\})\cap L^{\infty}_{loc}(\mathcal{U}\setminus\{p\})
of equation (
1.3
). Let
g_{KE}
be the Kähler metric defined by
\sqrt{-1}\partial\overline{\partial}\varphi
in
\mathcal{U}
. Then it satisfies the Kähler-Einstein equation

(1.4)

Ric(g_{KE})=0

in
\mathcal{U}
.

As a corollary, we obtain singular solution to complex Monge-Ampeère equation on a strongly pseudoconvex domain.

Corollary 1.1
.

Let
\Omega
be a strongly pseudoconvex domain in
\mathbb{C}^{n}
with smooth boundary and
V:=dz_{1}\wedge dz_{2}\ldots\wedge dz_{n}
be a holomorphic volume form. Suppose origin
\{o\}
is contained in
\Omega
, then for any
s>0
,
f\in C^{\infty}(\Omega)
,
\psi\in C^{\infty}(\partial\Omega),
and
\lambda=0
or
1
, the following Monge-Ampère equation

(1.5)

\left\{\begin{array}[]{rcl}&&(\sqrt{-1}\partial\overline{\partial}\varphi)^{n}=e^{f+\lambda\varphi}V\wedge\bar{V}\\
&&\varphi|_{\partial\Omega}=\psi\end{array}\right.

admits a singular solution
\varphi\in C^{\infty}(\Omega\setminus\{o\})
satisfying

|\varphi-s\log(\sum_{i=1}^{n}|z_{i}|^{2})|=\mathcal{O}(1).

2.
Preliminaries On Algebraic Singularity

In this section, we introduce some standard definitions on singularity theory from algebraic geometry.

Definition 2.1
.

Let
X
be a normal projective variety such that
K_{X}
is a
\mathbb{Q}
-Cartier divisor. Let
\pi:Y\rightarrow X
be a log resolution and
\{E_{i}\}_{i=1}^{p}
the irreducible components of the exceptional locus
Exc(\pi)
of
\pi
. There there exists a unique collection
a_{i}\in\mathbb{Q}
such that

K_{Y}=\pi^{*}K_{X}+\sum_{i=1}^{p}a_{i}E_{i}.

Then
X
is said to have log-canonical (resp. klt) singularities if

a_{i}\geq-1~\text{(resp.\ $a_{i}>-1$) for all $i$}.

Definition 2.2
.

(X,p)
is said to be a homogeneous hypersurface singularity if
X
is an affine variety defined by one homogeneous polynomial
f
.

Example 2.1
.

Let
X:=\{\sum_{i=1}^{i=n}z^{m}_{i}=0\}
with
1<m<n
, then
(X,0)
is a homogeneous hypersurface Klt singularity.

We briefly explain why the above example is a Klt singularity. Fixing a cone resolution
\pi:Y\to X
of
X
, then there is only one exceptional divisor
E
which is isomorphic to the projective variety
\tilde{X}\subset\mathbb{CP}^{n-1}
defined by
\{\sum_{i=1}^{i=n}z^{m}_{i}=0\}
. Now the difference of canonical class can be written as

K_{Y}=\pi^{*}K_{X}+aE,

where
a
is constant to be determined. By adjunction formula, we have

K_{\tilde{X}}=(a+1)E|_{\tilde{X}}.

Note that when
m<n
,
-K_{\tilde{X}}
is an ample divisor and that the exceptional divisor
E
when restricted on
\tilde{X}
is an anti ample divisor, this implies that
a+1>0
, which is the requirement of Klt singularity.

Let us also introduce the Kodaira Lemma which is quite useful.

Lemma 2.1
.

[
17
, Lemma 2.62]
Let
\pi:Y\to X
be a birational morphism. Also let
\omega
be a Kähler form on
X
and
\theta
be a Kähler form on
Y
. Assume that
Y
is quasi projective and
X
is
\mathbb{Q}
factorial.
Then there exists an effective simple normal crossing divisor
D
supporting on the exceptional locus, a hermitian metric
h_{D}
on the line bundle
D
and a constant
s_{0}>0
such that

\pi^{*}\omega+s\sqrt{-1}\partial\overline{\partial}\log h_{D}>0,

for all
0<s\leq s_{0}
. By adjusting the coefficients of
D
we may assume that
s_{0}=1
and that there exists a constant
\beta>0
such that

\pi^{*}\omega+\sqrt{-1}\partial\overline{\partial}\log h_{D}>\beta\theta.

Proof of Main Theorem

We have to prescribe singularities of the solution

, where
\mathcal{U}
is part of an affine variety

1.2
). To do so, we lift all the data to a log resolution
\pi:Y\rightarrow X
.
By adjunction formula:

K_{Y}=\pi^{*}K_{X}+\sum_{i}a_{i}E_{i}-\sum_{j}b_{j}F_{j},~a_{i}\geq 0,~0<b_{j}\leq 1.

Let
\sigma_{E}
be the defining section for

and
\sigma_{F}

and

with smooth hermitian metric

,

on

. Recall that domain
\mathcal{U}
is defined in (

). Let
\Omega_{Y}
be a smooth strictly positive volume form on

,

\Omega_{Y}=(|\sigma_{E}|^{2}_{h_{E}})^{-1}|\sigma_{F}|^{2}_{h_{F}}\Omega_{X}.

Then lifting equation (
1.3
) to
\pi^{-1}(\mathcal{U})\subset Y
, we have

(3.1)

\left\{\begin{array}[]{rcl}&&(\pi^{*}\sqrt{-1}\partial\overline{\partial}\varphi)^{n}=(|\sigma_{F}|^{2}_{h_{F}})^{-1}|\sigma_{E}|^{2}_{h_{E}}\Omega_{Y},\\
&&\pi^{*}\varphi|_{\partial\mathcal{U}}=\psi.\\
\end{array}\right.

Abusing notation, we still denote the domain
\pi^{-1}(\mathcal{U})
by
\mathcal{U}
.
Let
\theta
be a fixed smooth Kähler form on
Y
(Note that
Y
is quasi projective and we may choose
\theta
as a Kähler form on some compactification
\bar{Y}
of
Y
.) and we consider the following perturbed family of complex Monge-Ampère equations on
\pi^{-1}(\mathcal{U})
for

(3.2)

\left\{\begin{array}[]{rcl}&&(s\theta+\sqrt{-1}\partial\overline{\partial}\varphi_{s})^{n}=(|\sigma_{E}|^{2}_{h_{E}}+s)(|\sigma_{F}|^{2}_{h_{F}}+s)^{-1}\Omega_{Y},\\

For each

, we shall first get solution

for equation (
3.2
). When

3.2
) coincides with equation (
1.3
).

Firstly, let us rewrite the equation (
3.2
).
We choose an arbitrary smooth extension

In particular,

Recall that

\omega:=A\sqrt{-1}\partial\overline{\partial}(\rho-a)+\sqrt{-1}\partial\overline{\partial}\psi_{1}.

Now

Let

\phi_{s}:=\varphi_{s}-A(\rho-a)-\psi_{1},M=A(\rho-a)+\psi_{1},

then we can rewrite equations (

(3.4)

\left\{\begin{array}[]{rcl}&&(\omega+s\theta+\sqrt{-1}\partial\overline{\partial}\varphi_{s})^{n}=(|\sigma_{E}|^{2}_{h_{E}}+s)(|\sigma_{F}|^{2}_{h_{F}}+s)^{-1}\Omega_{Y},\\

The above equations hold for

, we abuse notation and still denote the unknown functions by

. Notice all the estimates we get for

is independent of

. Before we proceed, we do a remark about the

-

Remark 3.1
.

Note that the positive 1-1 form

From now on, we will focus on equations (

Lemma 3.1
.

Let
(X,p)
be a homogeneous hypersurface singularity and
\mathcal{U}
be defined as in (

\left\{\begin{array}[]{rcl}&&(\omega+s\theta+\sqrt{-1}\partial\overline{\partial}\Phi)^{n}\geq(|\sigma_{E}|^{2}_{h_{E}}+s)(|\sigma_{F}|^{2}_{h_{F}}+s)^{-1}\Omega_{Y},\\
&&\Phi|_{\partial\mathcal{U}}=0.\end{array}\right.

Proof.

Let us recall the blowing up of a point construction.

(3.6)

BL_{o}\mathbb{C}^{n}:=\{(z_{1},z_{2}\dots z_{n};l_{1},l_{2},\dots l_{n})\in\mathbb{C}^{n}\times\mathbb{CP}^{n-1}|z_{i}l_{j}=z_{j}l_{i}\}

On the affine chart
U_{i}:=\{l_{i}\neq 0\}
, we have coordinates
(z_{i},u_{j}=\frac{l_{j}}{l_{i}})
, with
j=1,2\dots n
and
j\neq i
. On
U_{i}
, the exceptional divisor
E
is defined by
E:=\{z_{i}=0\}
. Without lose of generality, we may assume at origin

,

\omega=\sum_{i=1}^{i=n}\sqrt{-1}dz_{i}\wedge d\bar{z_{i}}
. Now consider the pullback form
\pi^{*}\omega
at point

,

\displaystyle\pi^{*}\omega

\displaystyle=(1+\sum_{i\neq j}|u_{j}|^{2})\sqrt{-1}dz_{i}\wedge d\bar{z}_{i}+\sqrt{-1}\sum_{i\neq j}|z_{i}|^{2}du_{j}\wedge d\bar{u}_{j}

\displaystyle+\sqrt{-1}\sum_{i\neq j}u_{j}\bar{z}_{i}dz_{i}\wedge d\bar{u}_{j}+\sqrt{-1}\sum_{i\neq j}z_{i}\bar{u}_{j}du_{j}\wedge d\bar{z}_{i}

It is clear that

(3.7)

\displaystyle\begin{pmatrix}\frac{1}{2}+\sum_{j\neq i}|u_{j}|^{2}&Y\\
\bar{Y}^{T}&|z_{i}|^{2}Id_{n-1}\end{pmatrix}

is semipositive, where
Y=(u_{1}\bar{z}_{i},u_{2}\bar{z}_{i}\cdots,\widehat{u_{i}\bar{z}_{i}},\cdots,u_{n}\bar{z}_{i})
.

This shows that we have definite amount of positivity of
\pi^{*}\omega
along the normal direction of
E
.
Now since

is compact, for fixed

.

For general homogeneous hypersurface singularity

Now we construct a subsolution to equation (

For general isolated Klt singularity, although it is expected that

Remark 3.4

We do some preparations for the proof of Proposition

. The following comparison principle is well known.

Lemma 3.2
.

Let
\mathcal{U}

Lemma 3.3

We may assume

on

Lemma 3.4

is
L^{p},p>1
integrable when
s>0
.
Now by the same argument as in the proof of Theorem
1.4
, we conclude that equation (

) admits a bounded solution
|\phi|<C
on
\pi^{-1}(\Omega)\setminus E
as the limit of

, which completes the proof.
∎

Remark 3.6
.

The above corollary can be easily generalized to the case when the solution
