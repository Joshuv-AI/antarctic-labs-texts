Higher residue pairing on Crystalline local systems
Mohammad Reza Rahmati

Abstract
. We explain a generalization of the K. Saito higher residue pairing for local system of p p -adic isocrystals.

1.
introduction

In complex Hodge theory the variation of Hodge structure (VHS) associated to an analytic projective fibration
f:X\to S
between quasi-projective varieties with a finite number of critical points, locally admits a universal flat sesqui-linear nondegenrate pairing namely Higher residue pairing of K. Saito. The construction and properties of the Saito bilinear pairing was first studied in
[
S
]
.
The flat non-degenerate pairing of an isolated singularity can also be stated using the duality between the twisted de Rham complex of
f
and the twisted complex of polyvector fields. This view of point has been considered in
[
LLS
]
with an insight toward mirror symmetry.

The crystalline cohomology of varieties defined over a quotient ring of Witt vectors also satisfies a similar Hodge-Tate decomposition. Therefore a family as above also defines a family of filtered isocrystals which are crystalline representations of the absolute Galois group of a p-adic field. The relative de Rham-Witt complex of the fibration
f
defines a variation of filtered crystalline cohomology over
\textbf{C}:=\widehat{\overline{\textbf{Q}}}
where
Q
is the quotient field of the Witt ring
W(k)
of the perfect field
k
of characteristic
p
.

The family of crystalline cohomologies
H_{crys}^{k}(X_{s}/W)\otimes\textbf{C}
, for each
k
, defines a variation of Hodge-Tate (HT)-structure
H^{(k)}=Rf_{*}\textbf{C}_{X}
together with a flat connection defined locally on
S
. The HT-structure defines the slope filtrations on the fibers
H_{\textbf{C}}
. The p-adic Riemann-Hilbert correspondence guarantees that
H=\ker\nabla
as a 1-1 functorial correspondence between p-adic local systems and flat connections over the base, cf.
[
RZ
]
. We screen this construction over the p-ring
C
to state Kyoji Saito form
S_{f}
over this ring.

2.
The set up for Residue Pairing

Let
k
be a perfect field of char
=p
, and
W=W(k)
be the ring of Witt vectors over
k
. As a set
W
can be considered as the infinite product
k^{\infty}
with specific sum and products given in each component by polynomials in char
=p
. Such a ring has characteristic
0
. Let
Q
be the quotient field of
W
and
\textbf{C}=\widehat{\overline{\textbf{Q}}}
the completion of its algebraic closure. We consider family of projective varieties
X_{s}
over
C
defined by a projective map
f:X\to S
, where
X
and
S
are quasi-projective varieties. We assume
\dim X=n,\ \dim S=1
and
S=B(\textbf{C})
to be a small disc. Then the relative algebraic de Rham-Witt complex
W\Omega_{X/S}^{\bullet}
of the fibration defines defines a Gauss-Manin system of
p
-adic isocrystals

(1)

(E=R^{k}f_{*}W\Omega_{X/S}^{\bullet}\ ,\ \nabla)\ ,\qquad\ker(\nabla)=R^{k}f_{*}\textbf{C}_{X}

where
\nabla:{E}\to{E}\times W\Omega_{S}^{1}
is a flat connection, with kernel the local system
\mathcal{V}=R^{k}f_{*}\textbf{C}
. We may pass to the completion of the local rings over
B(\textbf{C})
and consider the twisted complex

(2)

\widehat{\Omega^{\bullet}}:=(DR_{X}[[\pi]],\ \widehat{d}=d+\frac{df}{\pi}\wedge)

where
\pi
is the uniformizer of the local ring of
B
at
0
. Set
\widehat{{H}}_{f}:=H^{*}(\widehat{\Omega^{\bullet}})
. The module
\widehat{{H}}_{f}
has a natural decreasing filtration
F^{k}
induced by multiplication with
\pi^{k}
. The trace map

(3)

Tr:W\widehat{H}_{f}\longrightarrow\mathcal{O}_{S,0}[[\pi]]

induces a
k[[\pi]]
-homomorphism
\widehat{Res}_{f}
that can be written as

(4)

W\widehat{Res}_{f}=\sum_{k}W\widehat{Res}_{f}^{(k)}(.)\pi^{k}

In fact putting
S_{f}(u,u^{\prime}):=Tr(\tilde{u}.\overline{\tilde{u}^{\prime}})
, where
\tilde{u}
is the lift of
u
in the complex
\widehat{\Omega}^{\bullet}
, defines a bilinear pairing. Therefore we can define the higher residue pairing;

Theorem 2.1
.

(Higher residue pairing on crystalline site) Let
f:X\to S
be a family of projective varieties defined over the p-ring
C
. Let
\widehat{{H}}_{f}
be the cohomology of the twisted relative de Rham-Witt complex of the fibration. Then we have a
C
-nondegenerate bilinear form

(5)

WS_{f}(\ ,\ ):W\widehat{{H}}_{f}\times W\widehat{{H}}_{f}\to\textbf{C}[[\pi]]

Let
u_{1},u_{2}
be local sections of
W\widehat{H}_{f}
, then;

•

WS_{f}(u_{1},u_{2})=\overline{WS_{f}(u_{2},u_{1})}
.

•

WS_{f}(g(\pi)u_{1},u_{2})=WS_{f}(u_{1},v(-\pi)u_{2})=g(\pi)WS_{f}(u_{1},u_{2})
,
\ \ g(\pi)\in\textbf{C}[[\pi]]
.

•

\partial_{\pi}WS_{f}(u_{1},u_{2})=WS_{f}(\partial_{\pi}u_{1},u_{2})+WS_{f}(u_{1},\partial_{\pi}u_{2})
.

•

(\pi\partial_{\pi}+n)WS_{f}(u_{1},u_{2})=WS_{f}(\pi\partial_{\pi}u_{2},u_{1})+WS_{f}(u_{1},\pi\partial_{\pi}u_{2})

•

The induced pairing on
W\widehat{{H}}_{f}/\pi.W\widehat{{H}}_{f}\bigotimes W\widehat{{H}}_{f}/\pi.W\widehat{{H}}_{f}\to\textbf{C}
is the classical Grothendieck residue.

The conjugation is formally done by
\overline{g(\pi)\otimes\eta}=g(-\pi).\eta
. The pairing is defined via the trace map on the cohomology of the complex
\widehat{\Omega}^{\bullet}
by

(6)

S_{f}(g_{1}(\pi)u,g_{2}(\pi)u^{\prime}):=g_{1}(\pi)g_{2}(-\pi)Tr(\tilde{u}.\tilde{u}^{\prime})

The flat pairing (5) is the universal pairing on the local system of isocrystals defined by the variation of crystalline cohomology of fibration. The pairing is called higher residue pairing due to the works by K. Saito
[
S
]
.
