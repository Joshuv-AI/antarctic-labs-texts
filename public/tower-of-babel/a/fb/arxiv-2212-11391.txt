Local well-posedness of Kolmogorov’s two-equation model of turbulence in fractional Sobolev Spaces
Przemysław Kosewski

Abstract
We study Kolmogorov’s two-equation model of turbulence on d − d- dimensional torus. First, the local existence of the solution with the initial data from non-homogeneous fractional Sobolev spaces (Bessel potential spaces) H s H^{s} with s > d 2 s>\frac{d}{2} is proven using energy methods. Next, we show that solutions are unique in the class of solutions guaranteed by the local existence theorem.



Keywords: Kolmogorov’s two-equation model of turbulence, local well-posedness, uniqueness, fractional Sobolev space, Bessel potential.

AMS subject classifications (2020): 35Q35, 76F02, 35A01.

Introduction

In 1941 A. N. Kolmogorov proposed a system of equations describing turbulent motion (see:
[
2
]
,
[
8
]

\displaystyle\nabla\cdot v

\displaystyle=0,

(1)

\displaystyle v_{t}+v\nabla v-\operatorname{div}\left(\frac{b}{\omega}Dv\right)

\displaystyle=-\nabla p,

(2)

\displaystyle\omega_{t}+v\nabla\omega-\operatorname{div}\left(\frac{b}{\omega}\nabla\omega\right)

\displaystyle=-\alpha\omega^{2},

(3)

\displaystyle b_{t}+v\nabla b-\operatorname{div}\left(\frac{b}{\omega}\nabla b\right)

\displaystyle=-b\omega+\frac{b}{\omega}|D(v)|^{2},

(4)

where
v
-mean velocity,

-mean turbulent energy,
\omega

\begin{split}v(0,x)=v_{0}(x),\penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \omega(0,x)=\omega_{0}(x),\penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ b(0,x)=b_{0}(x).\end{split}

(5)

Nowadays, the model is not used in engineering practice, however, the ones used share with Kolmogorov’s model underlying ideas (which manifest as similar dissipation/source terms included in the system). More information regarding turbulence models (e.g.

In

In

In

Preliminaries

Let us start with recalling the definitions of spaces set on
\mathbb{T}^{d}=[0,1)^{d}
.

Definition 1

(see Remark 3.1.5 in
[
22
]
or Section 3.2 and 3.5 in
[
27
]
)
.

Let
\{u_{n}\}_{n=1}^{\infty}\subset C^{\infty}(\mathbb{T}^{d})
,
u\in C^{\infty}(\mathbb{T}^{d})
. We say that
u_{n}\rightarrow u
in
C^{\infty}(\mathbb{T}^{d})
if
\partial^{\alpha}u_{n}\rightarrow\partial^{\alpha}u
uniformly for all
\alpha\in\mathbb{N}_{0}
.
By
\mathcal{D}^{\prime}(\mathbb{T}^{d})
we denote continuous linear functionals on
C^{\infty}(\mathbb{T}^{d})
.

Definition 2

(see Definition 3.1.6 in
[
22
]
)
.

Let
S(\mathbb{Z}^{d})
denote the space of rapidly decaying functions
\mathbb{Z}^{d}\rightarrow\mathbb{C}
. That is,
\varphi\in S(\mathbb{Z}^{d})
if for any
k<\infty
there exists a constant
C_{\varphi,k}
such that

\displaystyle\begin{split}|\varphi(\xi)|\leq\frac{C_{\varphi,k}}{\left(1+|\xi|^{2}\right)^{k/2}}.\end{split}

(15)

The topology on
S(\mathbb{Z}^{d})
is given by the seminorms
p_{k}
, where
k\in\mathbb{N}_{0}
and

\displaystyle\begin{split}p_{k}(\varphi)=\sup_{\xi\in\mathbb{Z}^{d}}\left(1+|\xi|^{2}\right)^{\frac{k}{2}}|\varphi(\xi)|.\end{split}

(16)

Then, a sequence
\{\varphi_{n}\}_{n=1}^{\infty}\subset S(\mathbb{Z}^{d})
converges to the function
\varphi\in S(\mathbb{Z}^{d})
iff

\displaystyle\begin{split}p_{k}(\varphi_{n}-\varphi)\overset{n\rightarrow\infty}{\longrightarrow}0\quad\text{for all }k\in\mathbb{N}_{0}.\end{split}

(17)

By
S^{\prime}(\mathbb{Z}^{d})
we denote continuous linear functionals on
S(\mathbb{Z}^{d})
.

Definition 3

(see Definition 3.1.8 in
[
22
]
)
.

Toroidal Fourier transform
\mathcal{F}_{\mathbb{T}^{d}}=(f\mapsto\hat{f}):C^{\infty}(\mathbb{T}^{d})\rightarrow S(\mathbb{Z}^{d})
is defined by

\displaystyle\begin{split}\hat{f}(\xi)=\int_{\mathbb{T}^{d}}f(x)e^{-2\pi ix\cdot\xi}dx.\end{split}

(18)

Inverse toroidal Fourier transform
\mathcal{F}_{\mathbb{T}^{d}}^{-1}=(h\mapsto\widecheck{h}):S(\mathbb{Z}^{d})\rightarrow C^{\infty}(\mathbb{T}^{d})
is given by

\displaystyle\begin{split}\widecheck{h}(x)=\sum_{\xi\in\mathbb{Z}^{d}}h(\xi)e^{i2\pi x\cdot\xi}.\end{split}

(19)

Definition 4

(see Definition 3.1.27 in
[
22
]
)
.

Fourier transform extends to the mapping
\mathcal{F}_{\mathbb{T}^{d}}:\mathcal{D}^{\prime}(\mathbb{T}^{d})\rightarrow S^{\prime}(\mathbb{Z}^{d})
by the formula

\displaystyle\begin{split}\langle\hat{u},\varphi\rangle:=\langle u,\iota\circ\widecheck{\varphi}\rangle,\end{split}

(20)

where
u\in\mathcal{D}^{\prime}(\mathbb{T}^{d})
,
\varphi\in S(\mathbb{Z}^{d})
and
\iota
is defined by
(\iota\circ\psi)(x)=\psi(-x)
.

Definition 5
.

Let
s\in\mathbb{C}
. Bessel potential
J^{s}
on the torus is defined as follows

\displaystyle\begin{split}(J^{s}f)(x)=\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}^{d}}\left(1+4\pi^{2}|k|^{2}\right)^{s/2}e^{2\pi ix\cdot k}\hat{f}(k),\end{split}

(21)

where
\hat{f}
denotes Fourier transform of
f
.

Now, let us recall the definition of fractional inhomogeneous Sobolev spaces on torus
H^{s}_{p}\left(\mathbb{T}^{d}\right)
.

Definition 6

(see chapter 3.5.4 in
[
20
]
)
.

Let
s
be real number,
p\in(1,\infty)
. The inhomogeneous Sobolev Space
H^{s}_{p}\left(\mathbb{T}^{d}\right)
is defined as follows

\displaystyle\begin{split}H^{s}_{p}\left(\mathbb{T}^{d}\right)=\left\{u\in D^{{}^{\prime}}(\mathbb{T}^{d}):\left\|u\right\|_{H^{s}_{p}}=\left\|{J^{s}u}\right\|_{p}<\infty\right\}.\end{split}

(22)

Also, based on orthogonality of
\{e^{2\pi kx}\}_{k\in\mathbb{Z}^{d}}
in

the following characterisation is valid

\displaystyle\begin{split}H^{s}\left(\mathbb{T}^{d}\right)=\left\{u\in D^{{}^{\prime}}(\mathbb{T}^{d}):\left\|{u}\right\|^{2}_{H^{s}_{2}}=\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}^{d}}\left(1+4\pi^{2}|k|^{2}\right)^{s}\left|\hat{u}(k)\right|^{2}<\infty\right\}.\end{split}

(23)

Moreover, we introduce the following notation

\displaystyle\begin{split}H^{s}_{\operatorname{div}}(\mathbb{T}^{d})=\left\{u\in[H^{s}(\mathbb{T}^{d})]^{d}:\operatorname{div}u=0\right\}.\end{split}

(24)

To simplify further expressions we introduce the following notation:

\displaystyle\begin{split}\left(f,g\right)_{H^{s}}=\left(J^{s}f,\overline{J^{s}g}\right)_{L^{2}(\mathbb{T}^{d})}.\end{split}

(25)

Lemma 5
.

Let

\displaystyle\begin{split}\left\|G(u)-G(v)\right\|_{H^{s}}\leq C\left\|G^{\prime\prime}\right\|_{C^{\lceil s\rceil}}\left\|u-v\right\|_{H^{s}}\left(1+\left\|u\right\|_{H^{s}}+\left\|v\right\|_{H^{s}}\right)^{\lceil s\rceil+1}.\end{split}

Proof.

The lemma is a direct consequence of Lemma

By applying

\displaystyle\begin{split}\left\|G(u)-G(v)\right\|_{H^{s}}\leq\left\|u-v\right\|_{H^{s}}\left\|\int_{0}^{1}G^{\prime}(u+\tau(v-u))d\tau\right\|_{H^{s}}.\end{split}

(33)

Next, we may change the order of the norm and integral to get

\displaystyle\begin{split}\left\|G(u)-G(v)\right\|_{H^{s}}\leq\left\|u-v\right\|_{H^{s}}\int_{0}^{1}\left\|G^{\prime}(u+\tau(v-u))\right\|_{H^{s}}d\tau.\end{split}

As

\displaystyle\begin{split}&\left\|G(u)-G(v)\right\|_{H^{s}}\\
&\quad\quad\quad\leq C\left\|G^{\prime\prime}\right\|_{C^{\lceil s\rceil}}\left\|u-v\right\|_{H^{s}}\int_{0}^{1}\left(1+\left\|u+\tau(v-u)\right\|_{\infty}\right)^{\lceil s\rceil}\left\|u+\tau(v-u)\right\|_{H^{s}}d\tau.\end{split}

(35)

We may estimate the right-hand side using triangle inequality. We get

\displaystyle\begin{split}&\left\|G(u)-G(v)\right\|_{H^{s}}\\
&\quad\quad\quad\leq C\left\|G^{\prime\prime}\right\|_{C^{\lceil s\rceil}}\left\|u-v\right\|_{H^{s}}\left(1+\left\|u\right\|_{\infty}+\left\|v\right\|_{\infty}\right)^{\lceil s\rceil}\left(\left\|u\right\|_{H^{s}}+\left\|v\right\|_{H^{s}}\right).\end{split}

(36)

By using Lemma
3
we obtain desired inequality.

Lemma 6

\begin{split}\left\|\nabla f\right\|_{\infty}\leq C\left\|{f}\right\|_{H^{s}(\Omega)}^{\frac{1}{2}\left(s-\frac{d}{2}\right)}\left\|{f}\right\|_{H^{s+1}(\Omega)}^{1-\frac{1}{2}\left(s-\frac{d}{2}\right)}\text{\penalty\ \penalty\ for\penalty\ }s\in\left(\frac{d}{2},\frac{d}{2}+1\right]\end{split}

(46)

and

\displaystyle\begin{split}\left\|\nabla f\right\|_{\infty}\leq C\left\|f\right\|_{H^{s}}\text{\penalty\ \penalty\ for\penalty\ }s\in\left(\frac{d}{2}+1,\infty\right).\end{split}

(47)

Proof.

First we concentrate on the case
s\in\left(\frac{d}{2},\frac{d}{2}+1\right]
. We see that based on Lemma
3
we have

\displaystyle\begin{split}\left\|{\nabla f}\right\|_{L^{\infty}(\Omega)}\leq C\left\|{\nabla f}\right\|_{H^{\frac{1}{2}\left(s+\frac{d}{2}\right)}(\Omega)}.\end{split}

(48)

We see that

\displaystyle\begin{split}\frac{1}{2}\left(s+\frac{d}{2}\right)=\frac{1}{2}\left(s-\frac{d}{2}\right)(s-1)+\left(1-\frac{1}{2}\left(s-\frac{d}{2}\right)\right)s\end{split}

(49)

and thus we may use interpolation inequality to get

\displaystyle\begin{split}\left\|{\nabla f}\right\|_{L^{\infty}(\Omega)}&\leq C\left\|{\nabla f}\right\|_{H^{s-1}(\Omega)}^{\frac{1}{2}\left(s-\frac{d}{2}\right)}\left\|{\nabla f}\right\|_{H^{s}(\Omega)}^{1-\frac{1}{2}\left(s-\frac{d}{2}\right)}.\end{split}

(50)

Thus based on Lemma
6
we get

\displaystyle\begin{split}\left\|{\nabla f}\right\|_{L^{\infty}(\Omega)}\leq C\left\|{f}\right\|_{H^{s}(\Omega)}^{\frac{1}{2}\left(s-\frac{d}{2}\right)}\left\|{f}\right\|_{H^{s+1}(\Omega)}^{1-\frac{1}{2}\left(s-\frac{d}{2}\right)}.\end{split}

(51)

If

\displaystyle\begin{split}\left\|{\nabla f}\right\|_{L^{\infty}(\Omega)}\leq C_{1}\left\|{\nabla f}\right\|_{H^{s-1}(\Omega)}\leq C_{2}\left\|{f}\right\|_{H^{s}(\Omega)},\end{split}

(52)

which follows from Lemma

6

(64)

3.3

Before we start deriving energy estimates we need to establish some relations between

and the derivative. Let

,

\hat{f}(k)=\int_{\mathbb{T}^{d}}f(x)e^{-2\pi ix\cdot k}dx

\displaystyle\begin{split}\frac{\partial J^{s}f}{\partial x_{i}}(x)&=\frac{\partial}{\partial x_{i}}\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}^{d}:|k|<n}\left(1+4\pi^{2}|k|^{2}\right)^{s/2}e^{2\pi ix\cdot k}\hat{f}(k)\\
&=\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}^{d}:|k|<n}\left(1+4\pi^{2}|k|^{2}\right)^{s/2}e^{2\pi ix\cdot k}(2\pi ik_{i})\hat{f}(k).\end{split}

Now using the properties of Fourier transform acting on a derivative we get

\displaystyle\begin{split}&\frac{\partial J^{s}f}{\partial x_{i}}(x)=\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}^{d}:|k|<n}\left(1+4\pi^{2}|k|^{2}\right)^{s/2}e^{2\pi ix\cdot k}\widehat{\left(\frac{\partial f}{\partial x_{i}}\right)}(k)=J^{s}\left(\frac{\partial f}{\partial x_{i}}\right)(x).\end{split}

(327)

We modify the right-hand side in the following way

\displaystyle\begin{split}&\left|\frac{\partial^{m}\left[\langle\xi,\xi+2\eta\rangle^{r-1}(\xi_{k}+2\eta_{k})\right]}{\partial^{\alpha_{1}}_{\xi_{1}}\dots\partial^{\alpha_{d}}_{\xi_{d}}\partial^{\beta_{1}}_{\eta_{1}}\dots\partial^{\beta_{d}}_{\eta_{d}}}\right|\\
&\penalty\ \penalty\ \leq C(m)(1+r)^{m}\sum_{i=1}^{N_{m}}\left(\frac{9}{7}\frac{|\langle\xi,\xi+2\eta\rangle|}{1+|\eta|^{2}}\right)^{r-1-k_{i}}\left(\frac{7}{9}(1+|\eta|^{2})\right)^{r-1-k_{i}}|\xi|^{\sum_{j=1}^{d}\omega_{i,j}}|\eta|^{\sum_{j=d+1}^{2d}\omega_{i,j}}.\end{split}

(328)

Based on the fact that
r-1-k_{i}\geq 0
and on Lemma
12
we have

\displaystyle\begin{split}&\left|\frac{\partial^{m}\left[\langle\xi,\xi+2\eta\rangle^{r-1}(\xi_{k}+2\eta_{k})\right]}{\partial^{\alpha_{1}}_{\xi_{1}}\dots\partial^{\alpha_{d}}_{\xi_{d}}\partial^{\beta_{1}}_{\eta_{1}}\dots\partial^{\beta_{d}}_{\eta_{d}}}\right|\\
&\penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \leq C(m)(1+r)^{m}\left(\frac{7}{9}\right)^{r}\left(1+|\eta|^{2}\right)^{r-\frac{1}{2}}\sum_{i=1}^{N_{m}}\frac{|\xi|^{\sum_{j=1}^{d}\omega_{i,j}}|\eta|^{\sum_{j=d+1}^{2d}\omega_{i,j}}}{\left(1+|\eta|^{2}\right)^{k_{i}+\frac{1}{2}}}.\end{split}

(329)

Using
\frac{1+|\xi|^{2}}{1+|\eta|^{2}}<\frac{1}{9}
we have

\displaystyle\begin{split}&\left|\frac{\partial^{m}\left[\langle\xi,\xi+2\eta\rangle^{r-1}(\xi_{k}+2\eta_{k})\right]}{\partial^{\alpha_{1}}_{\xi_{1}}\dots\partial^{\alpha_{d}}_{\xi_{d}}\partial^{\beta_{1}}_{\eta_{1}}\dots\partial^{\beta_{d}}_{\eta_{d}}}\right|\\
&\qquad\leq C(m)(1+r)^{m}\left(\frac{7}{9}\right)^{r}\left(1+|\eta|^{2}\right)^{r-\frac{1}{2}}\sum_{i=1}^{N_{m}}\frac{\left(1+|\xi|^{2}+|\eta|^{2}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}\sum_{j=1}^{2d}\omega_{i,j}}}{\left(1+|\xi|^{2}+|\eta|^{2}\right)^{k_{i}+\frac{1}{2}}}.\end{split}

(330)

Now using the fact that
2k_{i}-\sum_{j=1}^{2d}\omega_{i,j}=m-1
we get

\displaystyle\begin{split}\left|\frac{\partial^{m}\left[\langle\xi,\xi+2\eta\rangle^{r-1}(\xi_{k}+2\eta_{k})\right]}{\partial^{\alpha_{1}}_{\xi_{1}}\dots\partial^{\alpha_{d}}_{\xi_{d}}\partial^{\beta_{1}}_{\eta_{1}}\dots\partial^{\beta_{d}}_{\eta_{d}}}\right|&\leq C(m)(1+r)^{m}\left(\frac{7}{9}\right)^{r}\frac{\left(1+|\eta|^{2}\right)^{r-\frac{1}{2}}}{\left(1+|\xi|^{2}+|\eta|^{2}\right)^{\frac{m}{2}}}.\end{split}

(331)

∎

Lemma 16
.

Let
m\in\mathbb{N_{+}}
and
\left\{\alpha_{i}\right\}_{i=1}^{d},\left\{\beta_{i}\right\}_{i=1}^{d}\in\mathbb{N}^{d}
such that
\sum_{i=1}^{d}\left(\alpha_{i}+\beta_{i}\right)=m
. Let
\Phi\in C^{\infty}(\mathbb{R})
such that
\supp\frac{\partial\Phi}{\partial x}\subset[a,b]
for some
a,b>0
. Then we have

\begin{split}\left|\frac{\partial^{m}\left[\Phi\left(\frac{1+|\xi|^{2}}{1+|\eta|^{2}}\right)\right]}{\partial^{\alpha_{1}}_{\xi_{1}}\dots\partial^{\alpha_{d}}_{\xi_{d}}\partial^{\beta_{1}}_{\eta_{1}}\dots\partial^{\beta_{d}}_{\eta_{d}}}\right|&\leq C\left(1+|\xi|^{2}+|\eta|^{2}\right)^{-\frac{m}{2}}\end{split}

(332)

Proof of Lemma
16
.

First, we will show that there exists
N\in\mathbb{N}
,
\left\{\omega_{i,j}\right\}_{i,j=1}^{N,2d}\in\mathbb{N}^{N\times 2d}
,
\left\{k_{i}\right\}_{i=1}^{N},\left\{\kappa_{i,\xi}\right\}_{i=1}^{N},\left\{\kappa_{i,\eta}\right\}_{i=1}^{N}\in\mathbb{N}^{N}
,
\left\{C_{i}\right\}_{i=1}^{N}\in\mathbb{R}^{N}
and such that derivatives can be express in the following way:

\begin{split}&\frac{\partial^{m}\left[\Phi\left(\frac{1+|\xi|^{2}}{1+|\eta|^{2}}\right)\right]}{\partial^{\alpha_{1}}_{\xi_{1}}\dots\partial^{\alpha_{d}}_{\xi_{d}}\partial^{\beta_{1}}_{\eta_{1}}\dots\partial^{\beta_{d}}_{\eta_{d}}}\\
&\penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ =\sum_{i=1}^{N_{m}}C_{i}\Phi^{(k_{i})}\left(\frac{1+|\xi|^{2}}{1+|\eta|^{2}}\right)\frac{(1+|\xi|^{2})^{\kappa_{i,\eta}}}{(1+|\eta|^{2})^{\kappa_{i,\xi}+\kappa_{i,\eta}}}\xi_{1}^{\omega_{i,1}}\dots\xi_{d}^{\omega_{i,d}}\eta_{1}^{\omega_{i,d+1}}\dots\eta_{d}^{\omega_{i,2d}},\end{split}

(333)

where
1\leq k_{i}\leq m
,
2\kappa_{i,\xi}-\sum_{j=1}^{2d}\omega_{i,j}=m
.
We see that for
m=1
such representation is valid. Now we assume that formula is valid for some number of differentiations and we will check if the formula is still valid after additional differentiation. We start with differentiation with respect to
\xi_{j}
:

\displaystyle\begin{split}&\frac{\partial^{m+1}\left[\Phi\left(\frac{1+|\xi|^{2}}{1+|\eta|^{2}}\right)\right]}{\partial^{\alpha_{1}}_{\xi_{1}}\dots\partial^{\alpha_{j}+1}_{\xi_{j}}\dots\partial^{\alpha_{d}}_{\xi_{d}}\partial^{\beta_{1}}_{\eta_{1}}\dots\partial^{\beta_{d}}_{\eta_{d}}}\\
&=\frac{\partial}{\partial_{\xi_{j}}}\left[\sum_{i=1}^{N_{m}}C_{i}\Phi^{(k_{i})}\left(\frac{1+|\xi|^{2}}{1+|\eta|^{2}}\right)\frac{(1+|\xi|^{2})^{\kappa_{i,\eta}}}{(1+|\eta|^{2})^{\kappa_{i,\xi}+\kappa_{i,\eta}}}\xi_{1}^{\omega_{i,1}}\dots\xi_{d}^{\omega_{i,d}}\eta_{1}^{\omega_{i,d+1}}\dots\eta_{d}^{\omega_{i,2d}}\right].\end{split}

(334)

After caring out the differentiation we get

\displaystyle\begin{split}&\frac{\partial^{m+1}\left[\Phi\left(\frac{1+|\xi|^{2}}{1+|\eta|^{2}}\right)\right]}{\partial^{\alpha_{1}}_{\xi_{1}}\dots\partial^{\alpha_{j}+1}_{\xi_{j}}\dots\partial^{\alpha_{d}}_{\xi_{d}}\partial^{\beta_{1}}_{\eta_{1}}\dots\partial^{\beta_{d}}_{\eta_{d}}}\\
&=\sum_{i=1}^{N_{m}}2C_{i}\Phi^{(k_{i}+1)}\left(\frac{1+|\xi|^{2}}{1+|\eta|^{2}}\right)\frac{(1+|\xi|^{2})^{\kappa_{i,\eta}}}{(1+|\eta|^{2})^{\kappa_{i,\xi}+\kappa_{i,\eta}+1}}\xi_{j}\prod_{k=1}^{d}\xi_{k}^{\omega_{i,k}}\eta_{k}^{\omega_{i,d+k}}\\
&+\sum_{i=1}^{N_{m}}C_{i}2\kappa_{i,\eta}\Phi^{(k_{i})}\left(\frac{1+|\xi|^{2}}{1+|\eta|^{2}}\right)\frac{(1+|\xi|^{2})^{\kappa_{i,\eta}-1}}{(1+|\eta|^{2})^{\kappa_{i,\xi}+1+\kappa_{i,\eta}-1}}\xi_{j}\prod_{k=1}^{d}\xi_{k}^{\omega_{i,k}}\eta_{k}^{\omega_{i,d+k}}\\
&+\sum_{i=1}^{N_{m}}C_{i}\omega_{i,j}\Phi^{(k_{i})}\left(\frac{1+|\xi|^{2}}{1+|\eta|^{2}}\right)\frac{(1+|\xi|^{2})^{\kappa_{i,\eta}}}{(1+|\eta|^{2})^{\kappa_{i,\xi}+\kappa_{i,\eta}}}\xi_{j}^{-1}\prod_{k=1}^{d}\xi_{k}^{\omega_{i,k}}\eta_{k}^{\omega_{i,d+k}}\end{split}

(335)

Obtained formula matches the structure from equation (
333
). Now let us check the validity after additional differentiation with respect to
\eta_{j}
:

\displaystyle\begin{split}&\frac{\partial^{m+1}\left[\Phi\left(\frac{1+|\xi|^{2}}{1+|\eta|^{2}}\right)\right]}{\partial^{\alpha_{1}}_{\xi_{1}}\dots\partial^{\alpha_{d}}_{\xi_{d}}\partial^{\beta_{1}}_{\eta_{1}}\dots\partial^{\beta_{j}+1}_{\eta_{j}}\dots\partial^{\beta_{d}}_{\eta_{d}}}\\
&=\frac{\partial}{\partial_{\eta_{j}}}\left[\sum_{i=1}^{N_{m}}C_{i}\Phi^{(k_{i})}\left(\frac{1+|\xi|^{2}}{1+|\eta|^{2}}\right)\frac{(1+|\xi|^{2})^{\kappa_{i,\eta}}}{(1+|\eta|^{2})^{\kappa_{i,\xi}+\kappa_{i,\eta}}}\xi_{1}^{\omega_{i,1}}\dots\xi_{d}^{\omega_{i,d}}\eta_{1}^{\omega_{i,d+1}}\dots\eta_{d}^{\omega_{i,2d}}\right].\end{split}

(336)

After caring out the differentiation we get

\displaystyle\begin{split}&\frac{\partial^{m+1}\left[\Phi\left(\frac{1+|\xi|^{2}}{1+|\eta|^{2}}\right)\right]}{\partial^{\alpha_{1}}_{\xi_{1}}\dots\partial^{\alpha_{d}}_{\xi_{d}}\partial^{\beta_{1}}_{\eta_{1}}\dots\partial^{\beta_{j}+1}_{\eta_{j}}\dots\partial^{\beta_{d}}_{\eta_{d}}}\\
&\quad=\sum_{i=1}^{N_{m}}(-2)C_{i}\Phi^{(k_{i}+1)}\left(\frac{1+|\xi|^{2}}{1+|\eta|^{2}}\right)\frac{(1+|\xi|^{2})^{\kappa_{i,\eta}+1}}{(1+|\eta|^{2})^{\kappa_{i,\xi}+1+\kappa_{i,\eta}+1}}\eta_{j}\prod_{k=1}^{d}\xi_{k}^{\omega_{i,k}}\eta_{k}^{\omega_{i,d+k}}\\
&\quad\phantom{=}+\sum_{i=1}^{N_{m}}C_{i}(-2)(\kappa_{i,\eta}+\kappa_{i,\xi})\Phi^{(k_{i})}\left(\frac{1+|\xi|^{2}}{1+|\eta|^{2}}\right)\frac{(1+|\xi|^{2})^{\kappa_{i,\eta}}}{(1+|\eta|^{2})^{\kappa_{i,\xi}+1+\kappa_{i,\eta}}}\eta_{j}\prod_{k=1}^{d}\xi_{k}^{\omega_{i,k}}\eta_{k}^{\omega_{i,d+k}}\\
&\quad\phantom{=}+\sum_{i=1}^{N_{m}}C_{i}\omega_{i,d+j}\Phi^{(k_{i})}\left(\frac{1+|\xi|^{2}}{1+|\eta|^{2}}\right)\frac{(1+|\xi|^{2})^{\kappa_{i,\eta}}}{(1+|\eta|^{2})^{\kappa_{i,\xi}+\kappa_{i,\eta}}}\eta_{j}^{-1}\prod_{k=1}^{d}\xi_{k}^{\omega_{i,k}}\eta_{k}^{\omega_{i,d+k}}.\end{split}

(337)

Again we see that structure of (
333
) is preserved after differentiation. Now, we can finally prove the estimate (
332
). First, let us observe that

\displaystyle\begin{split}\left|\frac{\partial^{m}\left[\Phi\left(\frac{1+|\xi|^{2}}{1+|\eta|^{2}}\right)\right]}{\partial^{\alpha_{1}}_{\xi_{1}}\dots\partial^{\alpha_{d}}_{\xi_{d}}\partial^{\beta_{1}}_{\eta_{1}}\dots\partial^{\beta_{d}}_{\eta_{d}}}\right|=0\penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \text{ for }(\xi,\eta):\frac{1+|\xi|^{2}}{1+|\eta|^{2}}\leq b\text{ or }\frac{1+|\xi|^{2}}{1+|\eta|^{2}}\geq a\end{split}

(338)

and thus we will focus on the case when
a<\frac{1+|\xi|^{2}}{1+|\eta|^{2}}<b
:

\displaystyle\begin{split}\left|\frac{\partial^{m}\left[\Phi\left(\frac{1+|\xi|^{2}}{1+|\eta|^{2}}\right)\right]}{\partial^{\alpha_{1}}_{\xi_{1}}\dots\partial^{\alpha_{d}}_{\xi_{d}}\partial^{\beta_{1}}_{\eta_{1}}\dots\partial^{\beta_{d}}_{\eta_{d}}}\right|&\leq C\sum_{i=1}^{N_{m}}\frac{(1+|\xi|^{2})^{\kappa_{i,\eta}}}{(1+|\eta|^{2})^{\kappa_{i,\xi}+\kappa_{i,\eta}}}|\xi_{1}|^{\omega_{i,1}}\dots\xi_{d}^{\omega_{i,d}}\eta_{1}^{\omega_{i,d+1}}\dots\eta_{d}^{\omega_{i,2d}}\\
&\leq C\sum_{i=1}^{N_{m}}\frac{(1+|\xi|^{2}+|\eta|^{2})^{\frac{1}{2}\sum_{j=1}^{2d}\omega_{i,j}}}{(1+|\eta|^{2})^{\kappa_{i,\xi}}}.\end{split}

(339)

Now using the inequality
1+|\eta|^{2}\geq\frac{1}{2}\left(1+|\eta|^{2}\right)+\frac{1}{2b}\left(1+|\xi|^{2}\right)\geq\min\left\{\frac{1}{2},\frac{1}{2b}\right\}\left(1+|\eta|^{2}+|\xi|^{2}\right)
to obtain

\displaystyle\begin{split}\left|\frac{\partial^{m}\left[\Phi\left(\frac{1+|\xi|^{2}}{1+|\eta|^{2}}\right)\right]}{\partial^{\alpha_{1}}_{\xi_{1}}\dots\partial^{\alpha_{d}}_{\xi_{d}}\partial^{\beta_{1}}_{\eta_{1}}\dots\partial^{\beta_{d}}_{\eta_{d}}}\right|&\leq C\sum_{i=1}^{N_{m}}\left(1+|\xi|^{2}+|\eta|^{2}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}\left(-2\kappa_{1,\xi}+\sum_{j=1}^{2d}\omega_{i,j}\right)}\\
&\leq C\left(1+|\xi|^{2}+|\eta|^{2}\right)^{-\frac{m}{2}}.\end{split}

(340)

∎

Lemma 17
.

Let
s\in\mathbb{C}
such that
\re s\geq 0
,
m\in\mathbb{N_{+}}
and
\left\{\alpha_{i}\right\}_{i=1}^{d},\left\{\beta_{i}\right\}_{i=1}^{d}\in\mathbb{N}^{d}
such that
\sum_{i=1}^{d}(\alpha_{i}+\beta_{i})=m
. Then, there exists
N\in\mathbb{N}
,
\left\{\omega_{i,j}\right\}_{i,j=1}^{N,2d}\in\mathbb{N}^{dN}
,
\left\{k_{i}\right\}_{i=1}^{N}\in\mathbb{N}^{N}
,
\left\{C_{i}\right\}_{i=1}^{N}\in\mathbb{C}^{N}
and such that

\begin{split}\frac{\partial^{m}\left[(1+|\xi+\eta|^{2})^{s}\right]}{\partial^{\alpha_{1}}_{\xi_{1}}\dots\partial^{\alpha_{d}}_{\xi_{d}}\partial^{\beta_{1}}_{\eta_{1}}\dots\partial^{\beta_{d}}_{\eta_{d}}}=\sum_{i=1}^{N}C_{i}(s)(1+|\xi+\eta|^{2})^{s-k_{i}}\eta_{1}^{\omega_{i,1}}\dots\eta_{d}^{\omega_{i,d}}\xi_{1}^{\omega_{i,d+1}}\dots\xi_{d}^{\omega_{i,2d}},\end{split}

(341)

where
\forall i\in\{1,\dots,N\}

0\leq k_{i}\leq m
,
2k_{i}-\sum_{j=1}^{2d}\omega_{i,j}=m
and
|C_{i}(s)|\leq\overline{C}(1+|s|)^{k_{i}}
.
Also, we have

\begin{split}\left|\frac{\partial^{m}\left[(1+|\xi+\eta|^{2})^{s}\right]}{\partial^{\alpha_{1}}_{\xi_{1}}\dots\partial^{\alpha_{d}}_{\xi_{d}}\partial^{\beta_{1}}_{\eta_{1}}\dots\partial^{\beta_{d}}_{\eta_{d}}}\right|\leq C(1+|s|)^{m}(1+|\xi|^{2}+|\eta|^{2})^{\re s-\frac{m}{2}}\penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \forall(\xi,\eta)\in U,\end{split}

(342)

where
U=\left\{(\xi,\eta):\frac{1+|\xi|^{2}}{1+|\eta|^{2}}>9\penalty\ \text{or}\penalty\ \frac{1+|\xi|^{2}}{1+|\eta|^{2}}<\frac{1}{9}\right\}
. If
\re s-m\geq 0
, then inequality (
342
) holds for
(\xi,\eta)\in\mathbb{R}^{2d}
.

Proof of Lemma
17
.

The proof of representation formula (
341
) follows the same as in Lemma
14
, thus we will concentrate only on inequality.
We get

\begin{split}\left|\frac{\partial^{m}\left[(1+|\xi+\eta|^{2})^{s}\right]}{\partial^{\alpha_{1}}_{\xi_{1}}\dots\partial^{\alpha_{d}}_{\xi_{d}}\partial^{\beta_{1}}_{\eta_{1}}\dots\partial^{\beta_{d}}_{\eta_{d}}}\right|&\leq C(1+|s|)^{m}\sum_{i=1}^{N_{m}}\frac{\left(1+|\eta|^{2}+|\xi|^{2}\right)^{\re s+\frac{1}{2}\sum_{j=1}^{2d}\omega_{i,j}}}{(1+|\xi+\eta|^{2})^{k_{i}}}.\end{split}

(343)

Let us observe that from
1+|\xi|^{2}>9\left(1+|\eta|^{2}\right)
we can derive
-\frac{1}{3}\sqrt{|\xi|^{2}-8}<-|\eta|
. Thus we have

\displaystyle\begin{split}1+|\xi+\eta|^{2}&=1+|\xi|^{2}+|\eta|^{2}+2\langle\xi,\eta\rangle\geq 1+|\xi|^{2}+|\eta|^{2}-2|\xi||\eta|\\
&\geq 1+|\xi|^{2}+|\eta|^{2}-\frac{2}{3}|\xi|\sqrt{|\xi|^{2}-8}\geq\frac{1}{3}\left(1+|\xi|^{2}+|\eta|^{2}\right).\end{split}

(344)

Thus we get

\displaystyle\begin{split}\left|\frac{\partial^{m}\left[(1+|\xi+\eta|^{2})^{s}\right]}{\partial^{\alpha_{1}}_{\eta_{1}}\dots\partial^{\alpha_{d}}_{\eta_{d}}\partial^{\beta_{1}}_{\eta_{1}}\dots\partial^{\beta_{d}}_{\eta_{d}}}\right|&\leq C(1+|s|)^{m}\sum_{i=1}^{N_{m}}\left(1+|\eta|^{2}+|\xi|^{2}\right)^{\re s-\frac{1}{2}(2k_{i}-\sum_{j=1}^{2d}\omega_{i,j})}.\end{split}

(345)

Using the fact that
2k_{i}-\sum_{j=1}^{2d}\omega_{i,j}=m
, we get the desired inequality. The other case
1+|\xi|^{2}<\frac{1}{9}\left(1+|\eta|^{2}\right)
is analogous. Now, let us assume that
\re s-m\geq 0
. Thus from (
341
) we have

\displaystyle\begin{split}\hskip-5.69054pt\left|\frac{\partial^{m}\left[(1+|\xi+\eta|^{2})^{s}\right]}{\partial^{\alpha_{1}}_{\xi_{1}}\dots\partial^{\alpha_{d}}_{\xi_{d}}\partial^{\beta_{1}}_{\eta_{1}}\dots\partial^{\beta_{d}}_{\eta_{d}}}\right|\leq C(1+|s|)^{m}\sum_{i=1}^{N}(1+|\xi+\eta|^{2})^{\re s-k_{i}}\left(1+|\eta|^{2}+|\xi|^{2}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}\sum_{j=1}^{2d}\omega_{i,j}}.\end{split}

(346)

As
k_{i}\leq m
we see that
\re s-k_{i}\geq 0
and thus we have

\displaystyle\begin{split}\left|\frac{\partial^{m}\left[(1+|\xi+\eta|^{2})^{s}\right]}{\partial^{\alpha_{1}}_{\xi_{1}}\dots\partial^{\alpha_{d}}_{\xi_{d}}\partial^{\beta_{1}}_{\eta_{1}}\dots\partial^{\beta_{d}}_{\eta_{d}}}\right|\leq C(1+|s|)^{m}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\left(1+|\eta|^{2}+|\xi|^{2}\right)^{\re s-\frac{1}{2}(2k_{i}-\sum_{j=1}^{2d}\omega_{i,j})}.\end{split}

(347)

Again, using the fact that
2k_{i}-\sum_{j=1}^{2d}\omega_{i,j}=m
, we obtain the desired inequality.
∎
