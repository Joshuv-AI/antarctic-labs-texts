Theorem 1
.

Let
X_{1},\,X_{2},\,\ldots,\,X_{N}
,
N\in\mathbb{N}
be discrete, integer-valued, and independent random variables. Say that

\mathbb{P}\left(X_{1}\geqslant m_{1},\,X_{2}\geqslant m_{2},\,\ldots,\,X_{N}\geqslant m_{N}\right)=1
and

\mathbb{P}\left(X_{1}=m_{1},\,X_{2}=m_{2},\,\ldots,\,X_{N}=m_{N}\right)>0
, where
m_{1},\,m_{2},\ldots,\,m_{N}
are integers such that
m_{1}+m_{2}+\ldots+m_{N}=-D<0
.
Moreover, let
X_{k}\mathrel{\mathop{\kern 0.0pt=}\limits^{d}}X_{k+N}
, for all
k\in\mathbb{N}
in the sequence
X_{1},\,X_{2},\,\ldots
, and recall that:

\displaystyle M

\displaystyle=\max\{m_{1},\,m_{1}+m_{2},\,\ldots,\,m_{1}+m_{2}+\ldots+m_{N}\},

\displaystyle S_{N}

\displaystyle=X_{1}+\ldots+X_{N},

\displaystyle f_{N}(k)

\displaystyle=\mathbb{P}(S_{N}=k),\,k\in\mathbb{Z},

\displaystyle G_{N}(s)

\displaystyle=s^{-D}f_{N}(-D)+s^{-D+1}f_{N}(-D+1)+\ldots,\,0<|s|\leqslant 1,\,s\in\mathbb{C},

\displaystyle F_{\infty}(x)

\displaystyle=\mathbb{P}(X_{1}\leqslant x,\,X_{1}+X_{2}\leqslant x,\,\ldots),\,x\in\mathbb{Z}.

Then:

\displaystyle(\boldsymbol{i})\,F_{\infty}(x)\equiv 0,\textit{ if }\mathbb{E}S_{N}>0\textit{ or }\mathbb{E}S_{N}=0\textit{ with }\mathbb{P}(S_{N}=0)<1.

\displaystyle(\boldsymbol{ii})\,F_{\infty}(x)=0\textit{ when }x<M\textit{ and }F_{\infty}(x)=1\textit{ when }x\geqslant M,\textit{ if }\mathbb{E}S_{N}=0\textit{ with }\mathbb{P}(S_{N}=0)=1.

\displaystyle(\boldsymbol{iii})\,F_{\infty}(x)=0\textit{ for all }x<M;F_{\infty}(0),\,F_{\infty}(1),\,\ldots,\,F_{\infty}(D-1)\textit{ are given in }\eqref{sol_0}-\eqref{sol_D-1},

\displaystyle\textit{ and any other value }F_{\infty}(u),\,u\geqslant M\textit{ is computed by }\eqref{eq:recurrence_M<0}\textit{ when }M\leqslant 0,\,\mathbb{E}S_{N}<0\textit{ and }

\displaystyle\alpha_{1},\,\alpha_{2},\,\ldots\alpha_{D-1},\text{ are the simple roots of }G_{N}(s)=1\textit{ in }|s|\leqslant 1,\,s\neq 1.

\displaystyle(\boldsymbol{iv})\,F_{\infty}(x)=0\textit{ for all }x<M;F_{\infty}(M),\,F_{\infty}(M+1),\,\ldots,\,F_{\infty}(M+D-1)\textit{ are computed by }

\displaystyle\eqref{sol_0}-\eqref{sol_D-1}\textit{ when they replace }F_{\infty}(0),\,F_{\infty}(1),\,\ldots,\,F_{\infty}(D-1)\textit{ there respectively},

\displaystyle\textit{ and any other value }F_{\infty}(x),\,x\geqslant M\textit{ is computed by }\eqref{eq:recurrence_M>0}\textit{ when }M>0,\,\mathbb{E}S_{N}<0\textit{ and }

\displaystyle\alpha_{1},\,\alpha_{2},\,\ldots\alpha_{D-1},\text{ are the simple roots of }G_{N}(s)=1\textit{ in }|s|\leqslant 1,\,s\neq 1.

\displaystyle(\boldsymbol{v})\textit{ The generating function }\Xi(s)=F_{\infty}(0)+F_{\infty}(1)s+\ldots\text{ for }s\text{ such that }|s|<1,

\displaystyle G_{N}(s)\neq 1\textit{ is given by }\eqref{eq:P-1}\textit{ when }F_{\infty}(0),\,F_{\infty}(1),\,\ldots,\,F_{\infty}(D-1)\text{ are known, }M\leqslant 0,\textit{ and }

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}S_{N}<0.

\displaystyle(\boldsymbol{vi})\textit{ The generating function }\tilde{\Xi}(s)=F_{\infty}(M)+F_{\infty}(M)s+\ldots\text{ for }s\text{ such that }|s|<1,

\displaystyle G_{N}(s)\neq 1\textit{ is given by }\eqref{eq:gen_relation_v2}\textit{ when }F_{\infty}(M),\,F_{\infty}(M+1),\,\ldots,\,F_{\infty}(M+D-1)\text{ are known, }

\displaystyle M>0,\textit{ and }\mathbb{E}S_{N}<0.

Proof.

(
i
) If
\mathbb{E}S_{N}>0
, then
F_{\infty}(x)\equiv 0
is implied by the strong law of large numbers, see
[
6
, Thm. 2]
,
[

and
\mathbb{P}(S_{N}=0)<1
, then

(

) The provided conditions imply that

,
X_{2}=m_{2}
,
\ldots

with probability one and

\ldots

(
