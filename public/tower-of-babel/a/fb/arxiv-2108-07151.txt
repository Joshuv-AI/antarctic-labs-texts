Learning Friction Model for Tethered Capsule Robot
Yi Wang, Yuchen He, Xutian Deng, Ziwei Lei, Yiting Chen, Miao Li *

Abstract
With the potential applications of capsule robots in medical endoscopy, accurate dynamic control of the capsule robot is becoming more and more important. In the scale of a capsule robot, the friction between capsule and the environment plays an essential role in the dynamic model, which is usually difficult to model beforehand. In the paper, a tethered capsule robot system driven by a robot manipulator is built, where a strong magnetic Halbach array is mounted on the robot’s end-effector to adjust the state of the capsule. To increase the control accuracy, the friction between capsule and the environment is learned with demonstrated trajectories. With the learned friction model, experimental results demonstrate an improvement of 5.6% in terms of tracking error. Keywords — tethered capsule robot, magnetic actuation, friction model

VI
Conclusion

In this study, a learned friction model for tethered capsule robot is proposed. We design an experimental system, and the experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of the model. In addition, we can get the specific relationship between the control speed
{\bf{v}}
and the friction factor
c
in the process of the capsule robot moving in any plane contact medium. In the future, we will further study the space attitude control of the tethered capsule robots and optimize the structural components of the capsule robot to obtain better control accuracy.
