Abstract

Axial algebras of Monster type ( α , β ) (\alpha,\beta) are a class of non-associative algebras that includes, besides associative algebras, other important examples such as the Jordan algebras and the Griess algebra. 2 2 -generated primitive axial algebras of Monster type ( α , β ) (\alpha,\beta) naturally split into three cases: the case when α ∉ { 2 ​ β , 4 ​ β } \alpha\not\in\{2\beta,4\beta\} , the case α = 4 ​ β \alpha=4\beta and α = 2 ​ β \alpha=2\beta . In this paper we give a complete classification all 2 2 -generated primitive axial algebras of Monster type ( 2 ​ β , β ) (2\beta,\beta) .
