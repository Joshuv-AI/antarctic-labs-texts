HUPD-2212 Effective theory for universal seesaw model and FCNC
Takuya Morozumi 1 2, Apriadi Salim Adam 3, Yuta Kawamura 4, Albertus Hariwangsa Panuluh 1 5, Yusuke Shimizu 1 2 and Kei Yamamoto 6

Abstract
We study the quark sector of the universal seesaw model with SU(2) L × \times SU(2) R × \times U ⁡ ( 1 ) U(1) . The model incorporates the seesaw mechanism with the vector-like quarks (VLQs). The purpose of this work is to study the model with the effective theory. After integrating the heavy five VLQs, we derive the effective theory with four up-type quark and three down type quark. In this work, the FCNC of Z boson for top quark and top ′ quark is derived.
