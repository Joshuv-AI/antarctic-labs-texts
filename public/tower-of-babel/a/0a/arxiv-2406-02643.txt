Seymour and Woodall’s conjecture holds for graphs with independence number two
Rong Chen, Zijian Deng

Abstract
Woodall (and Seymour independently) in 2001 proposed a conjecture that every graph G G contains every complete bipartite graph on χ ⁡ ( G ) \chi(G) vertices as a minor, where χ ⁡ ( G ) \chi(G) is the chromatic number of G G . In this paper, we prove that for each positive integer ℓ \ell with 2 ​ ℓ ≤ χ ⁡ ( G ) 2\ell\leq\chi(G) , each graph G G with independence number two contains a K ℓ , χ ⁡ ( G ) − ℓ ℓ K^{\ell}_{\ell,\chi(G)-\ell} -minor, implying that Seymour and Woodall’s conjecture holds for graphs with independence number two, where K ℓ , χ ⁡ ( G ) − ℓ ℓ K^{\ell}_{\ell,\chi(G)-\ell} is the graph obtained from K ℓ , χ ⁡ ( G ) − ℓ K_{\ell,\chi(G)-\ell} by making every pair of vertices on the side of the bipartition of size ℓ \ell adjacent.

Introduction

All graphs considered in this paper are finite and simple. For a graph
G
, we use
\chi(G)
,
\omega(G)
and
\Delta(G)
to denote the chromatic number, clique number and maximum degree of
G
, respectively.
An
independent set
is a subset of
V(G)
that are pairwise nonadjacent. Let
\alpha(G)
be the
independence number
of
G
, that is the maximum size of independent sets. Let
K_{n}
be a clique on
n
vertices,
P_{n}
a path on
n
vertices. If a graph isomorphic to
H
can be obtained from a graph
G
by deleting vertices or edges and contracting edges, then
H
is a
minor
of
G
, written by
G\succeq_{m}H
.

In 1943, Hadwiger
[
6
]
proposed the following famous conjecture.

Conjecture 1.1
.

(Hadwiger’s conjecture)

Every graph
G
contains
K_{\chi(G)}
as a minor.

Hadwiger’s conjecture has been proven to be true only for graphs with chromatic number at most 6, but is likely difficult to prove for larger values, given that proofs for the cases of
\chi(G)=5
and
\chi(G)=6
already depend on the Four Color Theorem. Hadwiger’s conjecture remains unsolved for a specific class of graphs, namely those with an independence number of at most two.

Conjecture 1.2
.

Every graph
G
with
\alpha(G)\leq 2
contains
K_{\chi(G)}
as a minor.

The significance of this class of graphs with independence number two was first pointed out by Duchet and Meyniel
[
5
]
and Mader independently, and later highlighted in Seymour’s survey paper
[
9
]
.
According to Seymour in
[
9
]
, Conjecture
1.2
could be key to the Hadwiger’s conjecture. He suggested that if the conjecture is valid for these graphs, it might be valid more broadly.
Plummer, Stiebitz, and Toft
[
8
]
showed that, Conjecture
1.2
can be restated as follows.

Conjecture 1.3
.

Every
n
-vertex graph
G
with
\alpha(G)\leq 2
contains
K_{\lceil\frac{n}{2}\rceil}
as a minor.

Chudnovsky in
[
3
]
showed that every
n
-vertex graph
G
with an independence number
\alpha(G)\leq 2
contains a
K_{\lceil\frac{n}{3}\rceil}
-minor, which is also a direct consequence of Duchet and Meyniel’s result in
[
5
]
. This result provides strong evidence for Conjecture
1.3
.
Recently, Botler et al.
[
2
]
proposed a conjecture that is slightly weaker than Conjecture
1.3
.

Conjecture 1.4
.

(
[
2
]
)

Let
G
be an
n
-vertex graph with
\alpha(G)\leq 2
. For any positive integer
\ell
with
\ell<\lceil\frac{n}{2}\rceil
, we have
G\succeq_{m}K_{\ell,\lceil\frac{n}{2}\rceil-\ell}
.

In fact, Woodall
[
11
]
in 2001 (and independently Seymour
[
7
]
) proposed a conjecture that is slightly weaker than Hadwiger’s conjecture and stronger than Conjecture
1.4
.

Conjecture 1.5
.

(
[
11
,
7
]
)

For any positive integer
\ell
with
\ell<\chi(G)
, a graph
G
contains a
K_{\ell,\chi(G)-\ell}
-minor.

In this paper, we show that Conjecture
1.4
holds and Conjecture
1.5
and Conjecture
1.4
are equivalent for graphs with independence number two. In fact, we prove a stronger result than Conjecture
1.4
. To state our result, we need one more definition. Let
K_{m,n}^{m}
be the graph obtained from a complete bipartite graph
K_{m,n}
by making every pair of vertices on the side of the bipartition of size
m
adjacent. In other words,
K_{m,n}^{m}
is obtained from the disjoint union of a
K_{m}
and an independent set on
n
vertices by adding all of the possible edges between them.
In this paper, we prove the following result, implying that Conjecture
1.4
holds and Conjecture
1.5
holds for graphs with independence number two.

Theorem 1.6
.

Let
G
be a graph with
\alpha(G)\leq 2
. For any positive integer
\ell
with
2\ell\leq\chi(G)
, we have
G\succeq_{m}K^{\ell}_{\ell,\chi(G)-\ell}

Theorem 2.7

Theorems

are equivalent.

Proof.

Since

. Hence, it suffices to show that when Theorem

. Assume not.

.

is
\chi(G)
-vertex-critical. Assume not. Then

is

-vertex-critical.

When

, which is a contradiction. Hence,
|V(G)|\leq 2\chi(G)-2
as

. Moreover, since

is
\chi(G)
-vertex-critical,

is not anti-connected by Theorem

.
Then there is a partition

of

. Set

and

. Then

.
By the minimality choice of
G
, we have
G_{1}\succeq_{m}K_{\ell_{1},\chi(G_{1})-\ell_{1}}^{\ell_{1}}
and

G_{2}\succeq_{m}K_{\ell_{2},\chi(G_{2})-\ell_{2}}^{\ell_{2}}

for any positive integers
\ell_{1}
,
\ell_{2}
with

and

nor

is a clique. Moreover, when there are positive integers

,
\ell_{2}
