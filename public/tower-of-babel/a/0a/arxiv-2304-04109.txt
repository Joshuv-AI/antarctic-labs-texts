On odd perfect numbers of special forms
Luis H. Gallardo, Olivier Rahavandrainy

Abstract
. We give necessary conditions for perfection of some families of odd numbers with special multiplicative forms. Extending earlier work of Steuerwald, Kanold, McDaniel et al.

Primary 11A25; Secondary 11A07

The object of this paper is to prove the following two results:

Theorem 1.1
.

There is no odd perfect number of the form

n=5^{\alpha}M^{2\beta}

where

In order to present our second result, we need the following definitions. Let
\Sigma=\{p\;\text{prime}\;\colon p\in\{2,4\}\pmod{7}

Observe that
