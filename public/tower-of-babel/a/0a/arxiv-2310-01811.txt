The Laplacian spectral moments of power hypergraphs
Jueru Liu, Lixiang Chen, Changjiang Bu

Abstract
The d d -th order Laplacian spectral moment of a k k -uniform hypergraph is the sum of the d d -th powers of all eigenvalues of its Laplacian tensor. In this paper, we obtain some expressions of the Laplacian spectral moments for k k -uniform power hypergraphs, and these expressions can be represented by some parameters of graphs. And we show that some graphs can be determined by their high-order Laplacian spectrum by using the Laplacian spectral moments of power hypergraphs.

3
Main results

In this section, we give an expression of the

-th order Laplacian spectral moments for

-uniform hypergraphs. And the explicit expressions of the first

Given two hypergraphs

of
\mathcal{H}

) multi-hypergraph
[
4
]
. For a multi-hypergraph

be the simple

-uniform hypergraph formed by removing duplicate edges of

. And

is called a

of
\mathcal{H}
if
\underline{H}
is a subhypergraph of
\mathcal{H}
. Let
\mathcal{V}_{d}(\mathcal{H})
denote the set of connected Veblen multi-subgraph of
\mathcal{H}
with

For
f=(i_{1}\alpha_{1},\ldots,i_{d}\alpha_{d})\in\mathcal{F}_{d}
and a
k
-uniform hypergraph
\mathcal{H}
(where

for

, and
\underline{H}(f)
is a subhypergraph of
\mathcal{H}
.

A
walk

Since

is the set of all closed walks with the arc multi-set

, we know that

is equal to the number of Eulerian closed walks in the multi-digraph

. For

Lemma 3.4

Let
\mathcal{H}

Proof.

For
