Abstract
With the potential prospects of the Υ ⁡ ( 1 ​ S ) {\Upsilon}(1S) data samples at the running LHC and upcoming SuperKEKB, the Υ ⁡ ( 1 ​ S ) {\Upsilon}(1S) → {\to} B c ​ ρ B_{c}{\rho} weak decay is studied with the pQCD approach.
