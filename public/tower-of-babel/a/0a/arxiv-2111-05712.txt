Abstract
We determine the maximum number of edges in a K 4 K_{4} -minor-free n n -vertex graph of girth g g , when g = 5 g=5 or g g is even. We argue that there are many different n n -vertex extremal graphs, if n n is even and g g is odd.

Proposition 1

Let

Let

A

The graph

Next, we construct a

The graph

We show that actually these are all the extremal examples, when

Notice that the application of Euler’s formula shows us that in any drawing of an extremal graph

Let

Consider all bridges of

Now we know that there are two vertices
