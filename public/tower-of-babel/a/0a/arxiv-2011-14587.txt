Discretization of a distributed optimal control problem with a stochastic parabolic equation driven by multiplicative noise Thanks: Binjie Li was supported in part by the National Natural Science Foundation of China (11901410).
Binjie Li, Qin Zhou

Abstract
A discretization of an optimal control problem of a stochastic parabolic equation driven by multiplicative noise is analyzed. The state equation is discretized by the continuous piecewise linear element method in space and by the backward Euler scheme in time. The convergence rate O ⁡ ( τ 1 / 2 + h 2 ) O(\tau^{1/2}+h^{2}) is rigorously derived.



Keywords:
optimal control, stochastic parabolic equation,
Brownian motion, discretization, convergence

Introduction

Let
\{W(t)\mid\,t\geqslant 0\}
be a one-dimensional Brownian motion
defined on a complete probability space
(\Omega,\mathcal{F},\mathbb{P})
, and
let
\mathbb{F}=\{\mathcal{F}_{t}\}_{t\geqslant 0}
be the natural filtration
of
\{W(t)\mid\,t\geqslant 0\}
. We use
\mathbb{E}X
to denote the
expectation of a scalar/vector-valued random variable
X
defined on
(\Omega,\mathcal{F},\mathbb{P})
. Let
\mathcal{O}\subset\mathbb{R}^{d}
(
d=1,2,3
) be a convex polytope, and let
\Delta
be the realization of the Laplace
operator with homogeneous Dirichlet boundary condition in
L^{2}(\mathcal{O})
.
Assume that
0<\nu,T<\infty
. Let
L_{\mathbb{F}}^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))
be the set of all
L^{2}(\mathcal{O})
valued
\mathbb{F}
-progressively measurable processes belonging to
L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))
.
We consider the following stochastic optimal control problem:

\min_{\begin{subarray}{c}u\in U_{\text{ad}}\\
y\in L_{\mathbb{F}}^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))\end{subarray}}J(u,y):=\frac{1}{2}\mathbb{E}\Big(\lVert{y-y_{d}}\rVert_{L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}^{2}+\nu\lVert{u}\rVert_{L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}^{2}\Big),

(1)

subject to the state equation

\begin{cases}\mathrm{d}y(t)=(\Delta y+u)(t)\,\mathrm{d}t+y(t)\mathrm{d}W(t),\quad 0\leqslant t\leqslant T,\\
y(0)=0,\end{cases}

(2)

where
y_{d}\in L_{\mathbb{F}}^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))
is given. The
above
admissible space
U_{\text{ad}}
is defined by

\displaystyle U_{\text{ad}}

\displaystyle:=\big\{v\in L_{\mathbb{F}}^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))\mid u_{*}\leqslant v\leqslant u^{*}\,\,\mathbb{P}\otimes\,\mathrm{d}t\otimes\,\mathrm{d}x\,\text{a.e.}\big\},

where
-\infty<u_{*}<u^{*}<\infty
and
\mathrm{d}t
and
\mathrm{d}x

are respectively the Lebesgue measures in
(0,T)
and
\mathcal{O}
.

There has been a vast amount of literature on the stochastic optimal control
theory, and by now it is still a very active research area. In this area, the
optimal control of the stochastic parabolic equations has been extensively
studied; see
[
4
,
5
,
9
,
12
,
16
,
20
,
35
]
and the references therein. However, the numerical analysis of
these problems is quite rare. So far, in the literature we are aware of only one
paper addressing this issue. Dunst and Prohl
[
11
]
analyzed a spatial
semi-discretization of an optimal control problem governed by a stochastic heat
equation with multiplicative noise. To our best knowledge, no convergence rate
is available for a full discretization of problem
Eq.

1
.

The numerical analysis of problem
Eq.

1
consists of two key
ingredients: the numerical analysis of a forward stochastic parabolic equation,
namely the state equation
Eq.

2
; the numerical analysis of a backward
stochastic parabolic equation

\begin{cases}\mathrm{d}p(t)=-(\Delta p+y-y_{d}+z)(t)\,\mathrm{d}t+z(t)\,\mathrm{d}W(t),\quad 0\leqslant t\leqslant T,\\
p(T)=0,\end{cases}

(3)

namely the adjoint equation of problem
Eq.

1
. There has already been a
considerable number of papers on the numerical analysis of the forward
stochastic parabolic equations; see
[
2
,
3
,
8
,
17
,
18
,
30
]
and the references therein. There has also been
many papers on the numerical analysis of the backward stochastic differential
equations; see
[
6
,
7
,
15
,
25
,
27
,
31
,
32
,
33
,
34
]
and the references therein. However, the numerical analysis of the
backward stochastic parabolic equations is rather limited.
Wang
[
28
,
29
]
analyzed a time-discretized Galerkin
approximation of a semilinear backward stochastic parabolic equation and a
time-discretization Galerkin approximation of a linear backward stochastic
parabolic equation. Recently, Li and Tang
[
19
]
developed a
splitting-up method for backward stochastic parabolic equations, where no
convergence rate was derived for the general nonlinear case.

In this paper, we consider the convergence of a discrete stochastic optimal
control problem. The state equation is discretized by the continuous piecewise
linear element method in space and the backward Euler scheme in time. The
stability and convergence of the discrete state equation can be easily derived
by the standard techniques. The main challenge in the numerical analysis is that
the process
z
in
Eq.

3
is of low temporal regularity. Although
it appears that the numerical analysis in
[
29
]
may be applied to
equation
Eq.

3
under the condition that
y_{d}\in L^{2}(0,T;H_{0}^{1}(\mathcal{O}))
and

\lVert{y_{d}(t)-y_{d}(s)}\rVert_{L^{2}(\mathcal{O})}\leqslant C\lvert{t-s}\rvert^{1/2}\quad\text{for all }0\leqslant s\leqslant t\leqslant T,

throughout this paper we require only that
y_{d}\in L_{\mathbb{F}}^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))
. To tackle the low temporal regularity of
the process
z
, in the numerical analysis we propose a special discretization
of
Eq.

3
. In this discretization the process
z
is only
discretized in space so that the convergence rate
O(\tau^{1/2}+h^{2})
and
the stability of this discretization are derived. Finally, by the theoretical
results of this discretization, we are able to derive the following error
estimate:

\lVert{y-Y}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))}+\lVert{u-U}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))}\leqslant C(\tau^{1/2}+h^{2}),

where
U
and
Y
are the numerical control and state, respectively.

The rest of this paper is organized as follows.
Section

introduces some

Eq.

1
.

Section

3
introduces a discrete stochastic optimal control problem
and presents its error estimate. Finally,
Section

4
proves the error
estimate in the previous section.

2.2
First order optimality condition

For any
g\in L_{\mathbb{F}}^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))
, the following
forward stochastic parabolic equation

\begin{cases}\mathrm{d}y(t)=(\Delta y+g)(t)\,\mathrm{d}t+y(t)\,\mathrm{d}W(t)\quad\forall t\in[0,T]\\
y(0)=0\end{cases}

(4)

admits a unique strong solution

y\in L_{\mathbb{F}}^{2}\big(\Omega;C([0,T];\dot{H}^{1}(\mathcal{O}))\cap L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}^{2}(\mathcal{O}))\big).

We summarize some standard results of
y
as follows: for any
0\leqslant s\leqslant t\leqslant T
,

\displaystyle y(t)-e^{(t-s)\Delta}y(s)

(5)

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle\int_{s}^{t}e^{(t-r)\Delta}g(r)\,\mathrm{d}r+\int_{s}^{t}e^{(t-r)\Delta}y(r)\,\mathrm{d}W(r),

and, in particular,

y(t)=\int_{0}^{t}e^{(t-r)\Delta}g(r)\,\mathrm{d}r+\int_{0}^{t}e^{(t-r)\Delta}y(r)\,\mathrm{d}W(r);

(6)

for any
g\in L_{\mathbb{F}}^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}^{\beta}(\mathcal{O})))
with
\beta\geqslant 0
,

\displaystyle\lVert{y}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;C([0,T];\dot{H}^{\beta+1}(\mathcal{O})))}+\lVert{y}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}^{\beta+2}(\mathcal{O})))}

(7)

\displaystyle\leqslant{}

\displaystyle C\lVert{g}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}^{\beta}(\mathcal{O})))},

where
C
is a positive constant depending only on
T
. In the sequel, we
will use
S_{0}g
to denote the above
y
.

For any
g\in L_{\mathbb{F}}^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))
, the backward
stochastic parabolic equation

\begin{cases}\mathrm{d}p(t)=-(\Delta p+g+z)(t)\,\mathrm{d}t+z(t)\,\mathrm{d}W(t)\quad\forall t\in[0,T]\\
p(T)=0\end{cases}

(8)

admits a unique strong solution

\displaystyle(p,z)

\displaystyle\in L_{\mathbb{F}}^{2}\big(\Omega;C([0,T];\dot{H}^{1}(\mathcal{O}))\cap L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}^{2}(\mathcal{O}))\big)

\displaystyle\qquad{}\times L_{\mathbb{F}}^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}^{1}(\mathcal{O}))).

We summarize some standard results of
(p,z)
as follows: for any
0\leqslant s\leqslant t\leqslant T
,

\displaystyle p(s)-e^{(t-s)\Delta}p(t)

(9)

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle\int_{s}^{t}e^{(r-s)\Delta}(g+z)(r)\,\mathrm{d}r-\int_{s}^{t}e^{(r-s)\Delta}z(r)\,\mathrm{d}W(r);

for any
g\in L_{\mathbb{F}}^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}^{\beta}(\mathcal{O})))
with
0\leqslant\beta<\infty
,

\displaystyle\lVert{p}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;C([0,T];\dot{H}^{\beta+1}(\mathcal{O})))}+\lVert{p}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}^{\beta+2}(\mathcal{O})))}

(10)

\displaystyle{}+\lVert{z}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}^{\beta+1}(\mathcal{O})))}\leqslant C\lVert{g}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}^{\beta}(\mathcal{O})))},

where
C
is a positive constant depending only on
T
. In the sequel, we
will use
S_{1}g
and
S_{2}g
to denote the above
p
and
z
,
respectively.

(11a)

\displaystyle p=S_{1}(y-y_{d}),

Let

\tau:=T/J

\displaystyle\mathcal{V}_{h}

\displaystyle:=\big\{v_{h}\in\dot{H}^{1}(\mathcal{O})\mid\,v_{h}\text{ is piecewise linear on each }K\in\mathcal{K}_{h}\big\},

\displaystyle\mathcal{X}_{h,\tau}

\displaystyle:=\big\{V\in L_{\mathbb{F}}^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\mathcal{V}_{h}))\mid\,\text{$V$ is constant on }[t_{j},t_{j+1})\,\,\forall 0\leqslant j<J\big\}.

be the

, and define the discrete Laplace operator

\int_{\mathcal{O}}(\Delta_{h}v_{h})w_{h}=-\int_{\mathcal{O}}\nabla v_{h}\cdot\nabla w_{h}

For any

, define

\begin{cases}(\mathcal{S}_{0}g)_{j\!+\!1}-(\mathcal{S}_{0}g)_{j}=\tau\Delta_{h}(\mathcal{S}_{0}g)_{j\!+\!1}+\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j\!+\!1}}\!Q_{h}g(t)\mathrm{d}t\!+\!(\mathcal{S}_{0}g)_{j}\delta W_{j},\,0\leqslant j<J,\\
(\mathcal{S}_{0}g)_{0}=0.\end{cases}

(12)

Here and in what follows, for any

to abbreviate

.

The discretization of problem

is

\min_{U\in U_{\text{ad}}^{h,\tau}}J_{h,\tau}(U):=\frac{1}{2}\mathbb{E}\big(\lVert{\mathcal{S}_{0}U-y_{d}}\rVert_{L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}^{2}+\nu\lVert{U}\rVert_{L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}^{2}\big),

(13)

U_{\text{ad}}^{h,\tau}:=\left\{V\in U_{\text{ad}}\mid\text{$V$ is constant on $[t_{j},t_{j+1})$ for each $0\leqslant j<J$}\right\}.

4.2
Stability and convergence of

Let us first analyze the stability of
\mathcal{S}_{0}
.

Lemma 4.8
.

For any
g\in L_{\mathbb{F}}^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}^{-1}(\mathcal{O})))
, we
have

\displaystyle\max_{0\leqslant j\leqslant J}\lVert{(\mathcal{S}_{0}g)_{j}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}+\lVert{\mathcal{S}_{0}g}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}^{1}(\mathcal{O})))}

(31)

\displaystyle\lesssim{}

\displaystyle\lVert{g}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}^{-1}(\mathcal{O})))}.

Proof.

Let
Y:=\mathcal{S}_{0}g
. Fix
0\leqslant j<J
. By
Eq.

12
we
have

\displaystyle[Y_{j+1}-Y_{j},\,Y_{j+1}]=\Big[\tau\Delta_{h}Y_{j+1}+\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}g(t)\,\mathrm{d}t+Y_{j}\delta W_{j},\,Y_{j+1}\Big],

and so

\displaystyle\lVert{Y_{j+1}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}^{2}+\tau\lVert{Y_{j+1}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;\dot{H}^{1}(\mathcal{O}))}

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle[(1+\delta W_{j})Y_{j},\,Y_{j+1}]+\Big[\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}g(t)\,\mathrm{d}t,\,Y_{j+1}\Big]

\displaystyle\leqslant{}

\displaystyle\sqrt{1+\tau}\lVert{Y_{j}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}\lVert{Y_{j+1}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}+{}

\displaystyle\quad\sqrt{\tau}\lVert{g}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(t_{j},t_{j+1};\dot{H}^{-1}(\mathcal{O})))}\lVert{Y_{j+1}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;\dot{H}^{1}(\mathcal{O}))}

\displaystyle\leqslant{}

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\lVert{Y_{j}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}^{2}+\frac{1+\tau}{2}\lVert{Y_{j+1}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}^{2}+{}

\displaystyle\quad\lVert{g}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(t_{j},t_{j+1};\dot{H}^{-1}(\mathcal{O})))}^{2}+\frac{\tau}{4}\lVert{Y_{j+1}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;\dot{H}^{1}(\mathcal{O}))}^{2}.

It follows that

\displaystyle\lVert{Y_{j+1}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}^{2}+\frac{3\tau}{2(1-\tau)}\lVert{Y_{j+1}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;\dot{H}^{1}(\mathcal{O}))}^{2}

(32)

\displaystyle\leqslant{}

\displaystyle\frac{1}{1-\tau}\lVert{Y_{j}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}^{2}+\frac{2}{1-\tau}\lVert{g}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(t_{j},t_{j+1};\dot{H}^{-1}(\mathcal{O})))}^{2}.

By the fact
Y_{0}=0
, we then use the discrete Gronwall’s inequality to
derive

\max_{0\leqslant j\leqslant J}\lVert{Y_{j}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}\lesssim\lVert{g}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}^{-1}(\mathcal{O})))}.

(33)

Moreover, summing both sides of
Eq.

32
over
j
from
0
to
J-2
gives

\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{J-2}\lVert{Y_{j+1}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}^{2}+\frac{3}{2(1-\tau)}\lVert{Y}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}^{1}(\mathcal{O})))}^{2}

\displaystyle\leqslant{}

\displaystyle\frac{1}{1-\tau}\sum_{j=0}^{J-2}\lVert{Y_{j}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}^{2}+\frac{2}{1-\tau}\lVert{g}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}^{-1}(\mathcal{O})))}^{2},

so that

\displaystyle\frac{3}{2(1-\tau)}\lVert{Y}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}^{1}(\mathcal{O})))}^{2}

\displaystyle\leqslant{}

\displaystyle\Big(\frac{1}{1-\tau}-1\Big)\sum_{j=0}^{J-2}\lVert{Y_{j}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}^{2}+\frac{2}{1-\tau}\lVert{g}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}^{-1}(\mathcal{O})))}^{2}

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle\frac{\tau}{1-\tau}\sum_{j=0}^{J-2}\lVert{Y_{j}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}^{2}+\frac{2}{1-\tau}\lVert{g}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}^{-1}(\mathcal{O})))}^{2}

\displaystyle\lesssim{}

\displaystyle\lVert{g}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}^{-1}(\mathcal{O})))}^{2}\quad\text{(by \lx@cref{creftype\lx@tilde refnum}{eq:S0-stab-inf}).}

It follows that

\lVert{Y}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}^{1}(\mathcal{O})))}\lesssim\lVert{g}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}^{-1}(\mathcal{O})))}.

Finally, combining
Eq.

33
and the above estimate proves

Eq.

31
and hence this lemma.
∎

Lemma 4.11
.

For any

\sum_{j=0}^{J-1}\lVert{y-y(t_{j})}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(t_{j},t_{j+1};L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))}^{2}\lesssim\tau\lVert{g}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))}^{2},

\displaystyle\lVert{y(t)-e^{(t-t_{j})\Delta}y(t_{j})}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}^{2}

\displaystyle\leqslant{}

\displaystyle 2\Big\|{\int_{t_{j}}^{t}e^{(s-t_{j})\Delta}g(s)\,\mathrm{d}s}\Big\|_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}^{2}+2\Big\|{\int_{t_{j}}^{t}e^{(s-t_{j})\Delta}y(s)\,\mathrm{d}W(s)}\Big\|_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}^{2}

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle 2\Big\|{\int_{t_{j}}^{t}e^{(s-t_{j})\Delta}g(s)\,\mathrm{d}s}\Big\|_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}^{2}+2\int_{t_{j}}^{t}\lVert{e^{(s-t_{j})\Delta}y(s)}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}^{2}\,\mathrm{d}s

\displaystyle 2\tau^{2}\lVert{g}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(t_{j},t_{j+1};L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))}^{2}+2\tau\lVert{y}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(t_{j},t_{j+1};L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))}^{2}.

Lemma 4.12
.

Assume that
g\in L_{\mathbb{F}}^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))
. Define
\widetilde{Y}\in\mathcal{X}_{h,\tau}
by

\begin{cases}\widetilde{Y}_{j+1}=\widetilde{Y}_{j}+\tau\Delta_{h}\widetilde{Y}_{j+1}+Q_{h}\Big(\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}g(t)\,\mathrm{d}t+\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}y(t)\,\mathrm{d}W(t)\Big),\,\,0\leqslant j<J,\\
\widetilde{Y}_{0}=0,\end{cases}

(39)

where

. Then

\lVert{y-\widetilde{Y}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))}\lesssim(\tau^{1/2}+h^{2})\lVert{g}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))}.

(40)

Proof.

Since

\displaystyle\lVert{y-\widetilde{Y}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))}^{2}

\displaystyle\leqslant{}

\displaystyle 2\sum_{j=0}^{J-1}\lVert{y-y(t_{j})}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(t_{j},t_{j+1};L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))}^{2}+2\tau\sum_{j=0}^{J-1}\lVert{y(t_{j})-\widetilde{Y}_{j}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}^{2}

\displaystyle\lesssim{}

\displaystyle\tau\lVert{g}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))}^{2}+\tau\sum_{j=0}^{J-1}\lVert{y(t_{j})-\widetilde{Y}_{j}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}^{2}\quad\text{(by \lx@cref{creftype\lx@tilde refnum}{lem:y-yj})},

\tau\sum_{j=0}^{J-1}\lVert{y(t_{j})-\widetilde{Y}_{j}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}^{2}\lesssim(\tau+h^{2})^{2}\lVert{g}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))}^{2}.

(41)

To this end, we proceed as follows. An inductive argument gives, for any
0\leqslant j<J
,

\widetilde{Y}_{j+1}=\sum_{k=0}^{j}(I-\tau\Delta_{h})^{-(j-k+1)}Q_{h}\Big(\int_{t_{k}}^{t_{k+1}}g(t)\,\mathrm{d}t+\int_{t_{k}}^{t_{k+1}}y(t)\,\mathrm{d}W(t)\Big),

(42)

and so from
Eq.

6
we conclude that

\displaystyle y(t_{j+1})-\widetilde{Y}_{j+1}

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{j}\int_{t_{k}}^{t_{k+1}}\big(e^{(t_{j+1}-t)\Delta}-(I-\tau\Delta_{h})^{-(j-k+1)}Q_{h}\big)g(t)\,\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle\quad{}+\sum_{k=0}^{j}\int_{t_{k}}^{t_{k+1}}\big(e^{(t_{j+1}-t)\Delta}-(I-\tau\Delta_{h})^{-(j-k+1)}\big)y(t)\,\mathrm{d}W(t)

\displaystyle=:{}

\displaystyle I_{j}+II_{j}

\displaystyle\Big\|{\int_{t_{k}}^{t_{k+1}}\big(e^{(t_{j+1}-t)\Delta}-(I-\tau\Delta_{h})^{-(j-k+1)}Q_{h}\big)y(t)\,\mathrm{d}W(t)}\Big\|_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}^{2}

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{j}\Big\|{\int_{t_{k}}^{t_{k+1}}(I-\tau\Delta_{h})^{-(j-k+1)}(\widetilde{Y}-Q_{h}y)\,\mathrm{d}W(t)}\Big\|_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}^{2}

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{j}\lVert{(I-\tau\Delta_{h})^{-(j-k+1)}(\widetilde{Y}-Q_{h}y)}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(t_{k},t_{k+1};L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))}^{2}

\displaystyle\leqslant{}

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{j}\lVert{\widetilde{Y}-Q_{h}y}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(t_{k},t_{k+1};L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))}^{2}

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle\lVert{\widetilde{Y}-Q_{h}y}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,t_{j+1};L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))}^{2}

\displaystyle\leqslant{}

\displaystyle\lVert{y-\widetilde{Y}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,t_{j+1};L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))}^{2}.

Hence, applying the discrete Gronwall’s inequality yields, by the fact
E_{0}=0
, that

\max_{0\leqslant j\leqslant J}\lVert{E_{j}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}\lesssim\lVert{y-\widetilde{Y}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))},

which implies

\lVert{E}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))}\lesssim\lVert{y-\widetilde{Y}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))}.

Therefore,

\displaystyle\lVert{y-Y}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))}

\displaystyle\leqslant\lVert{y-\widetilde{Y}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))}+\lVert{E}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))}

\displaystyle\lesssim\lVert{y-\widetilde{Y}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))}

\displaystyle\lesssim(\tau^{1/2}+h^{2})\lVert{g}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))}\quad\text{(by \lx@cref{creftype\lx@tilde refnum}{eq:y-wtY}).}

This proves
Eq.

34
and thus concludes the proof.

\blacksquare

4.3
Discretization of a backward stochastic parabolic equation

This subsection considers a discretization of the following backward stochastic
parabolic equation:

\begin{cases}\mathrm{d}p(t)=-(\Delta p+g)(t)\mathrm{d}t+z(t)\mathrm{d}W(t),\quad 0\leqslant t\leqslant T,\\
p(T)=0,\end{cases}

(47)

where
g\in L_{\mathbb{F}}^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))
is given. We
summarize some standard results of the above equation as follows:

\displaystyle p(t)=\int_{t}^{T}e^{(s-t)\Delta}g(s)\,\mathrm{d}s-\int_{t}^{T}e^{(s-t)\Delta}z(s)\,\mathrm{d}W(s),\quad 0\leqslant t\leqslant T,

(48)

\displaystyle p(t)=\int_{t}^{T}(\Delta p+g)(t)\,\mathrm{d}t-\int_{t}^{T}z(t)\,\mathrm{d}W(t),\quad 0\leqslant t\leqslant T,

(49)

\displaystyle\lVert{p}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;C([0,T];\dot{H}^{1}(\mathcal{O})))}+\lVert{p}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}^{2}(\mathcal{O})))}+{}

\displaystyle\qquad\lVert{z}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}^{1}(\mathcal{O})))}\leqslant C\lVert{g}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))},

(50)

where
C
is a positive constant depending only on
T
. The discretization
seeks
P\in\mathcal{X}_{h,\tau}
and
Z\in L_{\mathbb{F}}^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\mathcal{V}_{h}))
such that

\begin{cases}P_{j}\!-\!P_{j+1}=\tau\Delta_{h}P_{j}+\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}Q_{h}g(t)\,\mathrm{d}t-\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}Z(t)\,\mathrm{d}W(t),\,0\leqslant j<J,\\
P_{J}=0.\end{cases}

(51)

Remark 4.3
.

The inspiration for discretization
Eq.

51
is as follows. Since
z
in
Eq.

47
is of low temporal regularity, for the moment we are
unable to derive any appropriate convergence rate of
Z
if
z
is
discretized in time; however, the convergence rate of
Z
is essential for
our numerical analysis of the adjoint equation of problem
Eq.

1
.
Hence, in discretization
Eq.

51
,
z
is only discretized in
space.

To analyze the convergence of discretization
Eq.

51
, we first analyze
the convergence of the following spatial semidiscretization of equation

Eq.

47
:

\begin{cases}\mathrm{d}p_{h}(t)=-(\Delta_{h}p_{h}+Q_{h}g)(t)\mathrm{d}t+z_{h}(t)\mathrm{d}W(t),\quad 0\leqslant t\leqslant T,\\
p_{h}(T)=0.\end{cases}

(52)

We summarize some standard properties of the above equation as follows. For any

\beta\in\mathbb{R}
, we have

\displaystyle\lVert{p_{h}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;C([0,T];\dot{H}_{h}^{\beta+1}(\mathcal{O})))}+\lVert{p_{h}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}_{h}^{\beta+2}(\mathcal{O})))}

(53)

\displaystyle}{\displaystyle+\lVert{z_{h}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}_{h}^{\beta+1}(\mathcal{O})))}\leqslant C\lVert{Q_{h}g}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}_{h}^{\beta}(\mathcal{O})))},

where
C
is a positive constant depending only on
T
.
For any
0\leqslant s\leqslant t\leqslant T
,

p_{h}(s)-e^{(t-s)\Delta_{h}}p_{h}(t)=\int_{s}^{t}e^{(r-s)\Delta_{h}}Q_{h}g(r)\,\mathrm{d}r-\int_{s}^{t}e^{(r-s)\Delta_{h}}z_{h}(r)\,\mathrm{d}W(r),

(54)

and, in particular,

p_{h}(t)=\int_{t}^{T}e^{(r-t)\Delta_{h}}Q_{h}g(r)\,\mathrm{d}r-\int_{t}^{T}e^{(r-t)\Delta_{h}}z_{h}(r)\,\mathrm{d}W(r).

(55)

Remark 4.4
.

The proofs of
Eqs.

48
,
50
,
53
and
54

are similar to that of
Eqs.

9
and
10
.

Lemma 4.13
.

Assume that
g\in L_{\mathbb{F}}^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))
. Let
(p,z)
be the solution of equation
Eq.

47
, and let
(p_{h},z_{h})
be
the solution of equation
Eq.

52
. Then

\displaystyle\lVert{p-p_{h}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;C([0,T];\dot{H}^{\beta+1}(\mathcal{O})))}+\lVert{p-p_{h}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}^{\beta+2}(\mathcal{O})))}

(56)

\displaystyle}{\displaystyle+\lVert{z-z_{h}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}^{\beta+1}(\mathcal{O})))}\lesssim h^{-\beta}\lVert{g}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))}

for all
-2\leqslant\beta\leqslant-1
.

Proof.

Let

e_{h}^{p}:=p_{h}-Q_{h}p,\quad e_{h}^{z}:=z_{h}-Q_{h}z.

By
Eq.

49
we have

Q_{h}p(t)=\int_{t}^{T}Q_{h}(\Delta p+g)(s)\,\mathrm{d}s-\int_{t}^{T}Q_{h}z(s)\,\mathrm{d}W(s),\quad 0\leqslant t\leqslant T,

so that

\mathrm{d}(Q_{h}p)(t)=-Q_{h}(\Delta p+g)(t)\mathrm{d}t+Q_{h}z(t)\,\mathrm{d}W(t),\quad 0\leqslant t\leqslant T.

Hence, by
Eq.

52
we have

\mathrm{d}e_{h}^{p}(t)=-\big(\Delta_{h}e_{h}^{p}+\Delta_{h}(Q_{h}p)-Q_{h}\Delta p\big)(t)\,\mathrm{d}t+e_{h}^{z}(t)\,\mathrm{d}W(t),\quad 0\leqslant t\leqslant T.

By the fact that
e_{h}^{p}(T)=0
, we then deduce that
(e_{h}^{p},e_{h}^{z})
is
the solution of equation
Eq.

52
with
g
replaced by
\Delta_{h}(Q_{h}p)-Q_{h}\Delta p
. It follows that

\displaystyle\lVert{e_{h}^{p}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;C([0,T];\dot{H}_{h}^{\beta+1}(\mathcal{O})))}+\lVert{e_{h}^{p}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}_{h}^{\beta+2}(\mathcal{O})))}+\lVert{e_{h}^{z}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}_{h}^{\beta+1}(\mathcal{O})))}

\displaystyle\lesssim{}

\displaystyle\lVert{\Delta_{h}(Q_{h}p)-Q_{h}\Delta p}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}_{h}^{\beta}(\mathcal{O})))}\hskip 9.24994pt\text{(by \lx@cref{creftype\lx@tilde refnum}{eq:ph-regu})}

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle\lVert{Q_{h}p-(\Delta_{h})^{-1}Q_{h}\Delta p}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}_{h}^{2+\beta}(\mathcal{O})))}

\displaystyle\lesssim{}

\displaystyle h^{-\beta}\lVert{p}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}^{2}(\mathcal{O})))},

by the standard estimate

\lVert{Q_{h}v-(\Delta_{h})^{-1}Q_{h}\Delta v}\rVert_{\dot{H}_{h}^{2+\beta}(\mathcal{O})}\lesssim h^{-\beta}\lVert{v}\rVert_{\dot{H}^{2}(\mathcal{O})}\quad\forall v\in\dot{H}^{2}(\mathcal{O}).

Hence, by
Eq.

15
we get

\displaystyle\lVert{e_{h}^{p}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;C([0,T];\dot{H}^{\beta+1}(\mathcal{O})))}+\lVert{e_{h}^{p}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}^{\beta+2}(\mathcal{O})))}+\lVert{e_{h}^{z}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}^{\beta+1}(\mathcal{O})))}

\displaystyle\lesssim{}

\displaystyle h^{-\beta}\lVert{p}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}^{2}(\mathcal{O})))}.

On the other hand, we have the following standard estimate:

\displaystyle\lVert{p-Q_{h}p}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;C([0,T];\dot{H}^{\beta+1}(\mathcal{O})))}+\lVert{p-Q_{h}p}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}^{\beta+2}(\mathcal{O})))}

\displaystyle\qquad{}+\lVert{z-Q_{h}z}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}^{\beta+1}(\mathcal{O})))}

\displaystyle\lesssim{}

\displaystyle h^{-\beta}\lVert{p}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;C([0,T];\dot{H}^{1}(\mathcal{O})))}+h^{-\beta}\lVert{p}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}^{2}(\mathcal{O})))}

\displaystyle\qquad{}+h^{-\beta}\lVert{z}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}^{1}(\mathcal{O})))}.

Finally, combining the above two estimates yields

\displaystyle\lVert{p-p_{h}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;C([0,T];\dot{H}^{\beta+1}(\mathcal{O})))}+\lVert{p-p_{h}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}^{\beta+2}(\mathcal{O})))}

\displaystyle\qquad{}+\lVert{z-z_{h}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}^{\beta+1}(\mathcal{O})))}

\displaystyle\lesssim{}

\displaystyle h^{-\beta}\lVert{p}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;C([0,T];\dot{H}^{1}(\mathcal{O})))}+h^{-\beta}\lVert{p}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}^{2}(\mathcal{O})))}

\displaystyle\qquad{}+h^{-\beta}\lVert{z}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}^{1}(\mathcal{O})))}

\displaystyle\lesssim{}

\displaystyle h^{-\beta}\lVert{g}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))}\quad\text{(by \lx@cref{creftype\lx@tilde refnum}{eq:p-h2}).}

This proves
Eq.

56
and thus concludes the proof.
∎

Lemma 4.14
.

Assume that
g\in L_{\mathbb{F}}^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))
. Let
(p_{h},z_{h})
be the solution of equation
Eq.

52
, and let
(P,Z)

be the solution of
Eq.

51
. Then

\displaystyle\max_{0\leqslant j\leqslant J}\lVert{p_{h}(t_{j})-P_{j}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}_{h}^{-1}(\mathcal{O})))}\lesssim\tau\lVert{g}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))},

(57)

\displaystyle\lVert{p_{h}-P}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))}\lesssim\tau^{1/2}\lVert{g}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))},

(58)

\displaystyle\lVert{z_{h}-Z}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}_{h}^{-1}(\mathcal{O})))}\lesssim\tau^{1/2}\lVert{g}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))}.

(59)

Proof.

Let us first prove
Eq.

57
. By
Eq.

55
we have that

p_{h}(t)=\mathbb{E}_{t}\eta(t),\quad 0\leqslant t\leqslant T,

(60)

where

\eta(t):=\int_{t}^{T}e^{(s-t)\Delta_{h}}Q_{h}g(s)\,\mathrm{d}s,\quad 0\leqslant t\leqslant T.

Define
\widetilde{P}\in\mathcal{X}_{h,\tau}
by

\begin{cases}\widetilde{P}_{j}-\widetilde{P}_{j+1}=\tau\Delta_{h}\widetilde{P}_{j}+Q_{h}\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}g(t)\,\mathrm{d}t,\quad 0\leqslant j<J,\\
\widetilde{P}_{J}=0.\end{cases}

(61)

By definition, it is evident that

P_{j}=\mathbb{E}_{t_{j}}\widetilde{P}_{j}\quad\text{for all $0\leqslant j\leqslant J$}.

(62)

By
Eqs.

60
and
62
we obtain

p_{h}(t_{j})-P_{j}=\mathbb{E}_{t_{j}}(\eta(t_{j})-\widetilde{P}_{j})\quad\text{for all $0\leqslant j\leqslant J$}.

so that

\displaystyle\max_{0\leqslant j\leqslant J}\lVert{p_{h}(t_{j})-P_{j}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;\dot{H}_{h}^{-1}(\mathcal{O}))}

\displaystyle\leqslant\max_{0\leqslant j\leqslant J}\lVert{\eta(t_{j})-\widetilde{P}_{j}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;\dot{H}_{h}^{-1}(\mathcal{O}))}.

Hence, the desired estimate
Eq.

57
follows from
Eq.

26
.

Then let us prove
Eq.

58
. Let
0\leqslant j<J
be arbitrary
but fixed. For any
t_{j}<t\leqslant t_{j+1}
, since
Eq.

54

implies

p_{h}(t_{j})-e^{(t-t_{j})\Delta_{h}}p_{h}(t)=\int_{t_{j}}^{t}e^{(s-t_{j})\Delta_{h}}Q_{h}g(s)\,\mathrm{d}s-\int_{t_{j}}^{t}e^{(s-t_{j})\Delta_{h}}z_{h}(s)\,\mathrm{d}W(s),

we have

\displaystyle\lVert{p_{h}(t_{j})-e^{(t-t_{j})\Delta_{h}}p_{h}(t)}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}

\displaystyle\leqslant{}

\displaystyle\Big\|\int_{t_{j}}^{t}e^{(s-t_{j})\Delta_{h}}Q_{h}g(s)\,\mathrm{d}s\Big\|_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}+\Big\|\int_{t_{j}}^{t}e^{(s-t_{j})\Delta_{h}}z_{h}(s)\,\mathrm{d}W(s)\Big\|_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}

\displaystyle\leqslant{}

\displaystyle\int_{t_{j}}^{t}\lVert{e^{(s-t_{j})\Delta_{h}}Q_{h}g(s)}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}\,\mathrm{d}s+\Big(\int_{t_{j}}^{t}\lVert{e^{(s-t_{j})\Delta_{h}}z_{h}(s)}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}^{2}\,\mathrm{d}s\Big)^{1/2}

\displaystyle\leqslant{}

\displaystyle\int_{t_{j}}^{t}\lVert{Q_{h}g(s)}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}\,\mathrm{d}s+\Big(\int_{t_{j}}^{t}\lVert{z_{h}(s)}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}^{2}\,\mathrm{d}s\Big)^{1/2}\hskip 9.24994pt\text{(by \lx@cref{creftype\lx@tilde refnum}{eq:e^tDeltah})}

\displaystyle\leqslant{}

\displaystyle\int_{t_{j}}^{t}\lVert{g(s)}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}\,\mathrm{d}s+\Big(\int_{t_{j}}^{t}\lVert{z_{h}(s)}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}^{2}\,\mathrm{d}s\Big)^{1/2}

\displaystyle\leqslant{}

\displaystyle\sqrt{t-t_{j}}\Big(\int_{t_{j}}^{t}\lVert{g(s)}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}^{2}\,\mathrm{d}s\Big)^{1/2}+\Big(\int_{t_{j}}^{t}\lVert{z_{h}(s)}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}^{2}\,\mathrm{d}s\Big)^{1/2}.

It follows that

\displaystyle\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}\lVert{p_{h}(t_{j})-e^{(t-t_{j})\Delta_{h}}p_{h}(t)}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}^{2}\,\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle\leqslant{}

\displaystyle 2\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}\Big((t-t_{j})\int_{t_{j}}^{t}\lVert{g(s)}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}^{2}\,\mathrm{d}s+\int_{t_{j}}^{t}\lVert{z_{h}(s)}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}^{2}\,\mathrm{d}s\,\Big)\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle\leqslant{}

\displaystyle\tau^{2}\lVert{g}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(t_{j},t_{j+1};L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))}^{2}+2\tau\lVert{z_{h}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(t_{j},t_{j+1};L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))}^{2}.

In addition, by
Eq.

21
we have

\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}\lVert{(I-e^{(t-t_{j})\Delta_{h}})p_{h}(t)}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}^{2}\,\mathrm{d}t\leqslant\tau^{2}\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}\lVert{p_{h}(t)}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;\dot{H}_{h}^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}^{2}\,\mathrm{d}t.

Combining the above two estimates gives

\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{J-1}\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}\lVert{p_{h}(t_{j})-p_{h}(t)}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}^{2}\,\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle\lesssim{}

\displaystyle\tau^{2}\lVert{g}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))}^{2}+\tau\lVert{z_{h}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(t_{j},t_{j+1};L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))}^{2}

\displaystyle\quad{}+\tau^{2}\lVert{p_{h}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}_{h}^{2}(\mathcal{O})))}^{2}

\displaystyle\lesssim{}

\displaystyle\tau\lVert{g}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))}^{2}\quad\text{(by \lx@cref{creftype\lx@tilde refnum}{eq:ph-regu}).}

It follows that

\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{J-1}\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}\lVert{(I-\mathbb{E}_{t_{j}})p_{h}(t)}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}^{2}\,\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle\leqslant{}

\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{J-1}\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}\lVert{p_{h}(t)-p_{h}(t_{j})}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}^{2}\,\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle\lesssim{}

\displaystyle\tau\lVert{g}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))}^{2}.

(63)

We also have

\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{J-1}\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}\lVert{\mathbb{E}_{t_{j}}(p_{h}(t)-\widetilde{P}_{j})}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}^{2}

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{J-1}\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}\lVert{\mathbb{E}_{t_{j}}(\eta(t)-\widetilde{P}_{j})}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}^{2}\quad\text{(by \lx@cref{creftype\lx@tilde refnum}{eq:p-eta})}

\displaystyle\leqslant{}

\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{J-1}\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}\lVert{\eta(t)-\widetilde{P}_{j}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}^{2}

\displaystyle\lesssim{}

\displaystyle\tau^{2}\lVert{g}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))}^{2}\quad\text{(by \lx@cref{creftype\lx@tilde refnum}{eq:xx-2}).}

(64)

A straightforward computation gives

\displaystyle\lVert{p_{h}-P}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))}^{2}

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{J-1}\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}\lVert{p_{h}(t)-P_{j}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}^{2}\,\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{J-1}\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}\lVert{p_{h}(t)-\mathbb{E}_{t_{j}}\widetilde{P}_{j}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}^{2}\,\mathrm{d}t\quad\text{(by \lx@cref{creftype\lx@tilde refnum}{eq:P-wtP})}

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{J-1}\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}\lVert{(I-\mathbb{E}_{t_{j}})p_{h}(t)+\mathbb{E}_{t_{j}}(p_{h}(t)-\widetilde{P}_{j})}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}^{2}\,\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle\leqslant{}

\displaystyle 2\sum_{j=0}^{J-1}\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}\lVert{(I-\mathbb{E}_{t_{j}})p_{h}(t)}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}^{2}+\lVert{\mathbb{E}_{t_{j}}(p_{h}(t)-\widetilde{P}_{j})}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}^{2}\,\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle\lesssim{}

\displaystyle\tau\lVert{g}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))}^{2}\quad\text{(by \lx@cref{creftypeplural\lx@tilde refnum}{eq:jm-1} and\lx@nobreakspace\lx@cref{refnum}{eq:jm-2}).}

This proves estimate
Eq.

58
.

Finally, we prove estimate
Eq.

59
. Let
0\leqslant j<J
be
arbitrary but fixed. By
Eq.

54
we have

p_{h}(t_{j})-e^{\tau\Delta_{h}}p_{h}(t_{j+1})=\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}e^{(t-t_{j})\Delta_{h}}Q_{h}g(t)\,\mathrm{d}t-\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}e^{(t-t_{j})\Delta_{h}}z_{h}(t)\,\mathrm{d}W(t),

and so

\displaystyle\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}e^{(t-t_{j})\Delta_{h}}z_{h}(t)\mathrm{d}W(t)=(I-\mathbb{E}_{t_{j}})\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}e^{(t-t_{j})\Delta_{h}}z_{h}(t)\mathrm{d}W(t)

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle(I-\mathbb{E}_{t_{j}})\Big(e^{\tau\Delta_{h}}p_{h}(t_{j+1})-p_{h}(t_{j})+\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}e^{(t-t_{j})\Delta_{h}}Q_{h}g(t)\mathrm{d}t\Big)

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle(I-\mathbb{E}_{t_{j}})\Big(e^{\tau\Delta_{h}}p_{h}(t_{j+1})+\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}e^{(t-t_{j})\Delta_{h}}Q_{h}g(t)\mathrm{d}t\Big),

by the fact that
p_{h}(t_{j})
is
\mathcal{F}_{t_{j}}
-measurable.
It follows that

\displaystyle\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}z_{h}(t)\,\mathrm{d}W(t)

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}(I-e^{(t-t_{j})\Delta_{h}})z_{h}(t)\,\mathrm{d}W(t)+\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}e^{(t-t_{j})\Delta_{h}}z_{h}(t)\,\mathrm{d}W(t)

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}(I-e^{(t-t_{j})\Delta_{h}})z_{h}(t)\,\mathrm{d}W(t)+{}

\displaystyle\quad(I-\mathbb{E}_{t_{j}})\Big(e^{\tau\Delta_{h}}p_{h}(t_{j+1})+\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}e^{(t-t_{j})\Delta_{h}}Q_{h}g(t)\,\mathrm{d}t\Big).

Therefore, since
Eq.

51
implies

\displaystyle\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}Z(t)\,\mathrm{d}W(t)=(I-\mathbb{E}_{t_{j}})\Big(P_{j+1}+\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}Q_{h}g(t)\,\mathrm{d}t\Big),

we obtain

\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}(z_{h}-Z)(t)\,\mathrm{d}W(t)=\mathbb{I}_{1}+\mathbb{I}_{2}+\mathbb{I}_{3},

(65)

where

\displaystyle\mathbb{I}_{1}

\displaystyle:=\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}\big(I-e^{(t-t_{j})\Delta_{h}}\big)z_{h}(t)\,\mathrm{d}W(t),

\displaystyle\mathbb{I}_{2}

\displaystyle:=(I-\mathbb{E}_{t_{j}})\big(e^{\tau\Delta_{h}}p_{h}(t_{j+1})-P_{j+1}\big),

\displaystyle\mathbb{I}_{3}

\displaystyle:=(I-\mathbb{E}_{t_{j}})\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}\big(e^{(t-t_{j})\Delta_{h}}-I\big)Q_{h}g(t)\,\mathrm{d}t.

For
\mathbb{I}_{1}
we have

\displaystyle\lVert{\mathbb{I}_{1}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;\dot{H}_{h}^{-1}(\mathcal{O}))}

\displaystyle=\Big(\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}\Big\|{(I-e^{(t-t_{j})\Delta_{h}})z_{h}(t)}\Big\|_{L^{2}(\Omega;\dot{H}_{h}^{-1}(\mathcal{O}))}^{2}\,\mathrm{d}t\Big)^{1/2}

\displaystyle\leqslant\tau\lVert{z_{h}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(t_{j},t_{j+1};\dot{H}_{h}^{1}(\mathcal{O})))}\quad\text{(by \lx@cref{creftype\lx@tilde refnum}{eq:I-e^Deltah-h1}).}

For
\mathbb{I}_{2}
we have

\displaystyle\lVert{\mathbb{I}_{2}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;\dot{H}_{h}^{-1}(\mathcal{O}))}\leqslant\lVert{e^{\tau\Delta_{h}}p_{h}(t_{j+1})-P_{j+1}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;\dot{H}_{h}^{-1}(\mathcal{O}))}

\displaystyle\leqslant{}

\displaystyle\lVert{(I-e^{\tau\Delta_{h}})p_{h}(t_{j+1})}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;\dot{H}_{h}^{-1}(\mathcal{O}))}+\lVert{p_{h}(t_{j+1})-P_{j+1}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;\dot{H}_{h}^{-1}(\mathcal{O}))}

\displaystyle\lesssim{}

\displaystyle\tau\lVert{p_{h}(t_{j+1})}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;\dot{H}_{h}^{1}(\mathcal{O}))}+\tau\lVert{g}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))}\quad\text{(by \lx@cref{creftypeplural\lx@tilde refnum}{eq:I-e^Deltah-h1} and\lx@nobreakspace\lx@cref{refnum}{eq:ph-P-inf})}

\displaystyle\lesssim{}

\displaystyle\tau\lVert{g}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))}\quad\text{(by \lx@cref{creftype\lx@tilde refnum}{eq:ph-regu}).}

For
\mathbb{I}_{3}
we have

\displaystyle\lVert{\mathbb{I}_{3}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;\dot{H}_{h}^{-1}\mathcal{O}))}

\displaystyle\leqslant\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}\Big\|{(e^{(t-t_{j})\Delta_{h}}-I)Q_{h}g(t)}\Big\|_{L^{2}(\Omega;\dot{H}_{h}^{-1}(\mathcal{O}))}\,\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle\lesssim\tau^{1/2}\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}\lVert{Q_{h}g(t)}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(\mathcal{O}))}\,\mathrm{d}t\quad\text{(by \lx@cref{creftype\lx@tilde refnum}{eq:I-e^Deltah-l2})}

\displaystyle\lesssim\tau\lVert{Q_{h}g}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(t_{j},t_{j+1};L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))}

\displaystyle\leqslant\tau\lVert{g}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(t_{j},t_{j+1};L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))}.

Combining
Eq.

65
and the above estimates of
\mathbb{I}_{1}
,

\mathbb{I}_{2}
and
\mathbb{I}_{3}
yields

\displaystyle\lVert{z_{h}-Z}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(t_{j},t_{j+1};\dot{H}_{h}^{-1}(\mathcal{O})))}

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle\Big\|{\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}(z_{h}-Z)(t)\,\mathrm{d}W(t)}\Big\|_{L^{2}(\Omega;\dot{H}_{h}^{-1}(\mathcal{O}))}

\displaystyle\lesssim{}

\displaystyle\tau\lVert{z_{h}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(t_{j},t_{j+1};\dot{H}_{h}^{1}(\mathcal{O})))}+\tau\lVert{g}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))},

and hence

\displaystyle\lVert{z_{h}-Z}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}_{h}^{-1}(\mathcal{O})))}

\displaystyle\lesssim{}

\displaystyle\tau\lVert{z_{h}}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;\dot{H}_{h}^{1}(\mathcal{O})))}+\tau^{1/2}\lVert{g}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))}

\displaystyle\lesssim{}

\displaystyle\tau^{1/2}\lVert{g}\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))}\quad\text{(by \lx@cref{creftype\lx@tilde refnum}{eq:ph-regu}).}

This proves
Eq.

59
and thus completes the proof.

\displaystyle[P_{j},P_{j+1}]+\Big[\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}(Z+g)(t)\,\mathrm{d}t,P_{j}\Big]

\displaystyle[P_{j},P_{j+1}]+\Big[\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}Z(t)\,\mathrm{d}t,P_{j}\Big]+\Big[\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}g(t)\,\mathrm{d}t,P_{j}\Big].

\mathbb{I}_{2}
and
\mathbb{I}_{3}

Lemma 4.19
.

For any
f,g\in L_{\mathbb{F}}^{2}(\Omega;L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\mathcal{O})))
, we have

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{T}[\mathcal{S}_{0}f(t),g(t)]\,\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle=\sum_{j=0}^{J-1}\bigg(\Big[\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}f(t)\,\mathrm{d}t,\,(\mathcal{S}_{1}g)_{j+1}\Big]-{}

(89)

\displaystyle\Big[(\mathcal{S}_{0}f)_{j}\delta W_{j},\,\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}(\mathcal{S}_{2}g+g)(t)\,\mathrm{d}t\Big]\bigg).

Proof.

By
Eqs.

12
and
67
, a straightforward computation yields

\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{J-1}\big[(\mathcal{S}_{0}f)_{j+1}-(\mathcal{S}_{0}f)_{j},(\mathcal{S}_{1}g)_{j+1}\big]

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{J-1}\Big[\tau\Delta_{h}(\mathcal{S}_{0}f)_{j+1}+\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}f(t)\,\mathrm{d}t+(\mathcal{S}_{0}f)_{j}\delta W_{j},\,(\mathcal{S}_{1}g)_{j+1}\Big]

\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{J-1}\big[(\mathcal{S}_{1}g)_{j}-(\mathcal{S}_{1}g)_{j+1},\,(\mathcal{S}_{0}f)_{j}\big]

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{J-1}\Big[\tau\Delta_{h}(\mathcal{S}_{1}g)_{j}+\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}(\mathcal{S}_{2}g+g)(t)\,\mathrm{d}t-\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}\mathcal{S}_{2}g(t)\,\mathrm{d}W(t),\,(\mathcal{S}_{0}f)_{j}\Big]

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{J-1}\Big[\tau\Delta_{h}(\mathcal{S}_{1}g)_{j}+\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}(\mathcal{S}_{2}g+g)(t)\,\mathrm{d}t,\,(\mathcal{S}_{0}f)_{j}\Big].

Since a simple calculation gives, by the facts
(\mathcal{S}_{0}f)_{0}=0
and

(\mathcal{S}_{1}g)_{J}=0
, that

\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{J-1}\big[(\mathcal{S}_{0}f)_{j+1}-(\mathcal{S}_{0}f)_{j},(\mathcal{S}_{1}g)_{j+1}\big]=\sum_{j=0}^{J-1}\big[(\mathcal{S}_{0}f)_{j},\,(\mathcal{S}_{1}g)_{j}-(\mathcal{S}_{1}g)_{j+1}\big],

we then obtain

\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{J-1}\Big[\tau\Delta_{h}(\mathcal{S}_{0}f)_{j+1}+\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}f(t)\,\mathrm{d}t+(\mathcal{S}_{0}f)_{j}\delta W_{j},(\mathcal{S}_{1}g)_{j+1}\Big]

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{J-1}\Big[\tau\Delta_{h}(\mathcal{S}_{1}g)_{j}+\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}(\mathcal{S}_{2}g+g)(t)\,\mathrm{d}t,\,(\mathcal{S}_{0}f)_{j}\Big].

(\mathcal{S}_{0}f)_{0}=(\mathcal{S}_{1}g)_{J}=0
, it follows that

\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{J-1}\Big[\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}f(t)\,\mathrm{d}t+(\mathcal{S}_{0}f)_{j}\delta W_{j},(\mathcal{S}_{1}g)_{j+1}\Big]

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{J-1}\Big[\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}(\mathcal{S}_{2}g+g)(t)\,\mathrm{d}t,\,(\mathcal{S}_{0}f)_{j}\Big].

\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{J-1}\Big[\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}g(t)\,\mathrm{d}t,\,(\mathcal{S}_{0}f)_{j}\Big]

\displaystyle=\sum_{j=0}^{J-1}\Big(\Big[\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}f(t)\,\mathrm{d}t,\,(\mathcal{S}_{1}g)_{j+1}\Big]+{}

(90)

\displaystyle\big[(\mathcal{S}_{1}g)_{j+1}\delta W_{j}-\int_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}\mathcal{S}_{2}g(t)\,\mathrm{d}t,\,(\mathcal{S}_{0}f)_{j}\big]\Big).

Eq.

67
we get

\displaystyle-\big[(\mathcal{S}_{1}g)_{j+1},(\mathcal{S}_{0}f)_{j}\delta W_{j}\big]

\displaystyle={}
