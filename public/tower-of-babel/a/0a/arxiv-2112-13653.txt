On quasiconformal extensions of harmonic mappings associated with pre-Schwarzian derivative Thanks: ∗ Corresponding author.
Xiao-Yuan Wang, Jin-Hua Fan, Zhen-Yong Hu and Zhi-Gang Wang ∗

Abstract
. In this paper, we extend Ahlfors’s univalent criteria and Ahlfors’s quasiconformal extension for analytic functions to harmonic mappings defined in the unit disk. Moreover, we give a general quasiconformal extension of harmonic Teichmüller mappings, whose maximal dilatation estimate is asymptotically sharp.



2010 Mathematics Subject Classification
Primary 30C62, 30C55; Secondary 31A05.

1.
Introduction and statements of the main results

Let
\triangle:=\{z:|z|<1\}
be the unit disk in the complex plane
\mathbb{C}
. For a locally univalent analytic function
\phi
in
\triangle
, we denote by
P_{\phi}
the pre-Schwarzian derivative and
S_{\phi}
the Schwarzian derivative of
\phi
as follows:

\displaystyle P_{\phi}=\frac{\phi^{\prime\prime}}{\phi^{\prime}}\ \ {\rm and}\ \ S_{\phi}=\left(P_{\phi}\right)^{\prime}-\frac{1}{2}\left(P_{\phi}\right)^{2}.

For
P_{\phi}
and
S_{\phi}
, we have the following two norms:

\|P_{\phi}\|=\sup_{z\in\triangle}|P_{\phi}|(1-|z|^{2}),

and

\left\|S_{\phi}\right\|=\sup_{z\in\triangle}\left|S_{\phi}\right|(1-|z|^{2})^{2}.

In 1972, Becker
[
3
]
stated that if

(1.1)

\|P_{\phi}\|\leq 1,

then
\phi
is univalent in
\triangle
. Moreover, the constant 1 is sharp (see
[
4
]
). Becker
[
3
]
also proved that if

(1.2)

\|P_{\phi}\|\leq k<1,

then not only
\phi
is univalent, but also it exists a continuous extension
\widetilde{\phi}
to
\overline{\triangle}
and
\widetilde{\phi}(\partial\triangle)
is a quasicircle.

Indeed, Becker
[
3
]
proved that
\phi
has a
K
-quasiconformal extension to
\mathbb{C}
if (
1.2
) holds. We call (
1.1
) and (
1.2
) are Becker’s univalence criterion and Becker’s extended univalence criterion, respectively.
In 1974, Ahlfors gave a generalized result expressed as follows.

Theorem A
.

Let
\phi
be a locally univalent analytic function in
\triangle
.
\sigma
is a continuous function in
\triangle
, which satisfies the following conditions: (i)
\sigma_{z}
and
\sigma_{\overline{z}}
exist in
\triangle
a.e.; (ii)
\frac{1}{\sigma}=0
on
\partial\triangle
; (iii)
\frac{\sigma_{\overline{z}}}{\sigma^{2}}\neq 0
in
\overline{\triangle}
. Then the inequality

(1.3)

\left|\sigma P_{\phi}+\sigma^{2}-\sigma_{z}\right|\leq\left|\sigma_{\overline{z}}\right|\ \ (z\in\triangle)

is sufficient to imply that
\phi
is univalent in
\triangle
.

Moreover, Ahlfors
[
1
]
gave a generalized quasiconformal extension formulated as below.

Theorem B
.

Let
\phi
be a locally univalent analytic function in
\triangle
.
\sigma
is a continuous function in
\triangle
, which satisfies conditions given by Theorem
A
. Then the inequality

(1.4)

\left|\sigma P_{\phi}+\sigma^{2}-\sigma_{z}\right|\leq k\left|\sigma_{\overline{z}}\right|\ \ (0\leq k<1)

is sufficient to imply that
\phi
has an explicit homeomorphic extension

\Phi(z)=\begin{cases}\widetilde{\phi}(z)&(|z|\leq 1),\\
\phi\left(\frac{1}{\overline{z}}\right)+u\left(\frac{1}{\overline{z}}\right)&(|z|>1),\end{cases}

where
u(z)=\phi^{\prime}(z)/{\sigma(z)}
for
z\in\triangle\backslash\{0\}
. Also, the mapping
\Phi
is
K
-quasiconformal in
\mathbb{C}
, where
K=\frac{1+k}{1-k}
.

A special case of interest, suggested by Ahlfors
[
1
]
, arises from taking

(1.5)

\sigma(z)=\frac{(c+1)\overline{z}}{1-|z|^{2}}

in (
1.4
). Then the principal condition for quasiconformal extension displayed as follows:

Theorem C
.

If

(1.6)

\left|c|z|^{2}+\left(1-|z|^{2}\right)zP_{\phi}\right|\leq k<1\quad(|c|\leq k;\,z\in\triangle),

then
\phi
has an explicit homeomorphic extension

(1.7)

\Phi(z)=\begin{cases}\widetilde{\phi}(z)&(|z|\leq 1),\\
\phi\left(\frac{1}{\overline{z}}\right)+u\left(\frac{1}{\overline{z}}\right)&(|z|>1),\end{cases}

where

u(z)=\frac{\phi^{\prime}(z)\left(1-|z|^{2}\right)}{\overline{z}}\quad(z\in\triangle\backslash\{0\}).

Also, the mapping
\Phi
is a
K
-quasiconformal extension in
\mathbb{C}
, where
K=\frac{1+k}{1-k}
.

We remark that even though (
1.4
) appears to depend on
\phi
only through

P_{\phi}
, one may, by choosing
\sigma
to depend on
\phi
, obtain conditions such as the classical
Becker criterion which involve
\phi
through quantities other than
P_{\phi}
.
Instead of
P_{\phi}
in Theorem
B
, Nehari
[
18
]
used non-oscillating solutions of differential equations, established the following criterion with Schwarzian derivative.

Theorem D
.

For a locally univalent analytic function
\phi
in
\triangle
, the condition

(1.8)

\left\|S_{\phi}(z)\right\|\leq 2

implies that
\phi
is univalent.

We call Theorem
D
is the classical Nehari’s univalence criterion.
At the same time, as a supplement note, Hille showed that the bound 2 is the best possible.
Later, a remarkable feature of the explicit extension was provided by Ahlfors and Weill
[
2
]
.

Theorem E
.

Let
\phi
be a locally univalent analytic function in
\triangle
. For a constant
t\;(0\leq t<1)
, if
\|S_{\phi}\|\leq 2t
, then the mapping

(1.9)

F(z)\,=\,\left\{\begin{array}[]{rl}\phi(z)&\quad(|z|\leq 1),\\
E_{\phi}(1/\overline{z})&\quad(|z|>1),\end{array}\right.

is a quasiconformal extension of
\phi
to
\mathbb{C}
,
where

E_{\phi}(\xi)=\phi(\xi)+\frac{(1-|\xi|^{2})\phi^{\prime}(\xi)}{\overline{\xi}-\tfrac{1}{2}(1-|\xi|^{2})P_{\phi}(\xi)}\quad(\xi\in\triangle).

Remark 1.1
.

One can see that Theorem
E
can be obtained by taking

\sigma(z)=\frac{\overline{z}}{1-|z|^{2}}-\frac{1}{2}P_{\phi}

in Theorem
B
. Indeed, Ahlfors and Weill
[
2
]
applied a quasiconformal extension to obtain the Nehari condition (
1.8
) for the half plane.

Ahlfors
[
1
]
also showed general formulas on the problem of univalence and quasiconformal extensibility through the following two results.

Theorem F
.

Let
\phi
be a locally univalent analytic function in
\triangle
. Then the inequality

(1.10)

\left|\frac{1}{2}S_{\phi}+v^{2}-v_{z}\right|\leq k\left|v_{\overline{z}}\right|\ \ (0<k<1)

is sufficient to imply that there exists a
\frac{1+k}{1-k}
-quasiconformal extension of
\phi
to
\mathbb{C}
, where the continue function
v
satisfies
\frac{v_{\overline{z}}}{v^{2}}\neq 0
and
v\rightarrow\infty
for
|z|\rightarrow 1
.

Theorem G
.

Let
\phi
be a locally univalent analytic function in
\triangle
. Then the inequality

(1.11)

\displaystyle\left|\frac{1}{2}S_{\phi}(1-|z|^{2})^{2}-c(1-c)\overline{z}\right|\leq k|c|\quad(0<k<1;\,|c-1|\leq k)

is sufficient to imply that there exists a
\frac{1+k}{1-k}
-quasiconformal extension of
\phi
to
\mathbb{C}
.

We call (
1.3
) is the Ahlfors’s univalent criterion and (
1.4
), (
1.6
), (
1.10
), (
1.11
) are the Ahlfors’s quasiconformal extension criteria. Note that the Becker’s extension criterion arises from taking

(1.12)

\sigma(z)=\frac{\overline{z}}{1-|z|^{2}}

in (
1.4
) or
c=0
in (
1.6
).

Remark 1.2
.

By setting
\sigma(z)=v-1/2P_{\phi}
and
\sigma(z)=c\overline{z}/(1-|z|^{2})-1/2P_{\phi}
in (
1.4
), one can get Theorem
F
and Theorem
G
, respectively.

Remark 1.3
.

By putting
v(z)=c\overline{z}/(1-|z|^{2})
in (
1.10
), one can get Theorem
G
.

Remark 1.4
.

By taking
v(z)=\overline{z}/(1-|z|^{2})
in (
1.10
) or
c=1
in (
1.11
), one can obtain Theorem
E
.

A complex-valued function
f
in
\triangle
is harmonic if
\Delta f=4f_{z\overline{z}}=0
. It is well-known that
f
has a canonical representation
f=h+\overline{g}
, where
h
and
g
are analytic in
\triangle
. Lewy
[
17
]
proved that a harmonic mapping
f
is locally univalent if and only if its Jacobian
J_{f}=\left|h^{\prime}\right|^{2}-\left|g^{\prime}\right|^{2}\neq 0
. If
J_{f}>0\,\left(J_{f}<0\right)
, then
f
is sense-preserving (sense-reserving). Denote by
\omega={g^{\prime}}/{h^{\prime}}
the second complex dilatation of
f=h+\overline{g}
.
If a harmonic mapping has the representation
f=h+\alpha\overline{h}
, where
h
is a conformal mapping and
\alpha
is a constant such that
0<|\alpha|<1
, then it is called a harmonic Teichmüller mapping (see
[
6
]
).

As natural generalizations of conformal mappings, quasiconformal extensions for harmonic mappings have been investigated by many researchers (see
[
9
,
10
]
and the references therein).

In 2003, Chuaqui
et\ al.

[
8
]
generalized the idea of Schwarzian derivatives of
f=h+\overline{g}
into the case of locally univalent harmonic mappings with
\omega=q^{2}
, where
q
is an analytic function. The pre-Schwarzian derivative
P_{f}
and Schwarzian derivative
S_{f}
for sense-preserving harmonic mappings are defined as follows:

(1.13)

\displaystyle P_{f}=\frac{\partial}{\partial z}\log\left|J_{f}\right|=P_{h}-\frac{\overline{\omega}\omega^{\prime}}{1-|\omega|^{2}},

and

(1.14)

\displaystyle S_{f}=S_{h}+\frac{\overline{\omega}}{1-|\omega|^{2}}\left(\frac{h^{\prime\prime}}{h^{\prime}}\omega^{\prime}-\omega^{\prime\prime}\right)-\frac{3}{2}\left(\frac{\overline{\omega}\omega^{\prime}}{1-|\omega|^{2}}\right)^{2},

which were introduced and investigated by Hernández and Martín
[
13
]
. In the same paper, Hernández and Martín proved following result.

Theorem H
.

Let
f=h+\overline{g}
be a sense-preserving harmonic mapping in
\triangle
with the second dilatation
\omega
. If
f
satisfies

(1.15)

\left(1-|z|^{2}\right)|P_{f}|+\frac{\left|\omega^{\prime}(z)\left(1-|z|^{2}\right)\right|}{1-|\omega(z)|^{2}}\leq 1\ \ (z\in\triangle),

then
f
is univalent in
\triangle
. The constant 1 is sharp.

Moreover, Hernández and Martín
[
11
]
obtained a quasiconformal extension as follows.

Theorem I
.

Let
f=h+\overline{g}
be a sense-preserving harmonic mapping in
\triangle
with
\|\omega\|_{\infty}<1
. If
f
satisfies the condition

(1.16)

\left(1-|z|^{2}\right)|P_{f}|+\frac{\left|\omega^{\prime}(z)\left(1-|z|^{2}\right)\right|}{1-|\omega(z)|^{2}}\leq k<1\quad(z\in\triangle),

then
f
has a continuous and injective extension
\widetilde{f}
to
\overline{\triangle}
, and the mapping

(1.17)

F(z)=\begin{cases}\widetilde{f}(z)&(|z|\leq 1),\\
f\left(\frac{1}{\overline{z}}\right)+U\left(\frac{1}{\overline{z}}\right)&(|z|>1),\end{cases}

is a homeomorphism of
\mathbb{C}
onto itself, where

U(z)=\frac{h^{\prime}(z)}{\overline{z}}\left(1-|z|^{2}\right)+\frac{\overline{g^{\prime}(z)}}{z}\left(1-|z|^{2}\right)\quad(z\in\triangle\backslash\{0\}).

Moreover, if
f
satisfies the condition (
1.16
), then
\widetilde{f}(\partial\triangle)
is a quasicircle and
f
can be extended to a quasiconformal mapping in
\mathbb{C}
. Under the additional condition

(1.18)

k<\frac{1-\|\omega\|_{\infty}}{1+\|\omega\|_{\infty}},

the mapping
F
defined by (
1.17
) is an explicit
K
-quasiconformal mapping of
\mathbb{C}
onto itself, where

K=\frac{1+k+\|\omega\|_{\infty}(1-k)}{1-k-\|\omega\|_{\infty}(1+k)}.

The following result was derived by Bravo
et\ al.

[
5
]
, which generalized Theorem
H
. It also can be regarded as a generalization of harmonic analogue of Theorem
A
.

Theorem J
.

Let
f=h+\overline{g}
be a sense-preserving harmonic mapping in
\triangle
with the second dilatation
\omega
. If
f
satisfies

(1.19)

\left|c|z|^{2}+(1-|z|^{2})z{P_{f}}\right|+\frac{|z\omega^{\prime}(z)(1-|z|^{2})|}{1-|\omega(z)|^{2}}\leq 1\ \ (z\in\triangle),

then
f
is univalent in
\triangle
.

Recently, Chen and Que
[
7
]
generalized Theorem
I
as follows.

Theorem K
.

Let
f=h+\overline{g}
be a sense-preserving harmonic mapping in
\triangle
with
\|\omega\|_{\infty}<1
. If
f
satisfies (
1.16
), then for all
|\lambda|\leq 1,f_{\lambda}=h+\lambda\overline{g}
has a continuous and injective extension
\widetilde{f_{\lambda}}
to
\overline{\triangle}
and the mapping

(1.20)

F_{\lambda}(z)=\begin{cases}\widetilde{f}_{\lambda}(z)&(|z|\leq 1),\\
f_{\lambda}\left(\frac{1}{\bar{z}}\right)+U_{\lambda}\left(\frac{1}{\overline{z}}\right)&(|z|>1),\end{cases}

is a homeomorphism of
\mathbb{C}
onto itself, where

U_{\lambda}(z)=\frac{h^{\prime}(z)}{\overline{z}}\left(1-|z|^{2}\right)+\lambda\frac{\overline{g^{\prime}(z)}}{z}\left(1-|z|^{2}\right)\quad(z\in\triangle\backslash\{0\}).

Moreover, if
k
satisfies (
1.18
), then the family of mappings
F_{\lambda}(z)
are
K
-quasiconformal mappings in
\mathbb{C}
, where

(1.21)

K=\frac{1+k+|\lambda|\|\omega\|_{\infty}(1-k)}{1-k-|\lambda|\|\omega\|_{\infty}(1+k)}.

We observe that the following result obtained by Chen and Que
[
7
]
is a quasiconformal extension of harmonic
Teichmüller mappings, whose maximal dilatation estimate is asymptotically sharp.

Theorem L
.

Let
f
be a sense-preserving harmonic mapping in
\triangle
with a representation
f=h+\alpha\overline{h}
, where
h
is a locally univalent analytic function in
\triangle
and
\alpha
is a constant with
|\alpha|<1
. Assume that

(1.22)

\left|P_{h}(z)\left(1-|z|^{2}\right)\right|\leq k<1\quad(z\in\triangle).

Then
f
is a harmonic Teichmüller mappings of
\triangle
and has a continuous and homeomorphic extension
\widetilde{f}
to
\overline{\triangle}
. Moreover, the mapping

(1.23)

F(z)=\begin{cases}\widetilde{f}(z)&\quad(|z|\leq 1),\\
f\left(\frac{1}{\overline{z}}\right)+U_{\alpha}\left(\frac{1}{\overline{z}}\right)&\quad(|z|>1),\end{cases}

is a
K
-quasiconformal mapping of
\mathbb{C}
with

(1.24)

K=\frac{(1+k)(1+|\alpha|)}{(1-k)(1-|\alpha|)},

where

U_{\alpha}(z)=\frac{h^{\prime}(z)\left(1-|z|^{2}\right)}{\bar{z}}+\frac{\alpha\overline{h^{\prime}(z)}\left(1-|z|^{2}\right)}{z}\quad(z\in\triangle\backslash\{0\}).

The maximal dilatation estimate of the quasiconformal extension
F
is asymptotically sharp in
k
, and extremal mappings are of the form
f(z)=az+b\overline{z}
, where
a
and
b
are two nonvanishing constants.

In a recent paper, Hu and Fan
[
14
]
generalized Theorem
K
by posing one of harmonic version of the classical Becker’s criteria. We present it here in a slightly modified form.

Theorem M
.

Let
f=h+\overline{g}
be a sense-preserving harmonic mapping in
\triangle
. Assume that

(1.25)

\left|c|z|^{2}+(1-|z|^{2})z{P_{f}}\right|+\frac{|z\omega^{\prime}(z)(1-|z|^{2})|}{1-|\omega(z)|^{2}}\leq k<1\quad(|c|\leq k;\,z\in\triangle).

Then
f_{\lambda}=h+\lambda\overline{g}\;(|\lambda|\leq 1)
has a continuous and injective extension
\widetilde{f_{\lambda}}
to
\overline{\triangle}
and the family of mappings
F_{\lambda}(z)
defined by (
1.20
) are
K
-quasiconformal mappings of
\mathbb{C}
onto themselves.
Moreover, if
k
satisfies the conditions (
1.18
), then the family of mappings
F_{\lambda}(z)
are
K
-quasiconformal mappings of
\mathbb{C}
, where
K
is given by (
1.21
).

Remark 1.5
.

In Theorem
K
, the condition (
1.16
) for
P_{f}
and (
1.18
) for
k
, imply that
\widetilde{f}_{\lambda}(\partial\triangle)
is a quasicircle.
In Theorem
M
, if inequality (
1.25
) holds for
P_{f}
, it shows that
\widetilde{f}_{\lambda}(\partial\triangle)
is a quasicircle.
We would like to draw
reader’s attention to Theorem
1.3
, it is shown that
\widetilde{f}_{\lambda}(\partial\triangle)
is a quasicircle, if inequality (
1.27
) holds for
P_{f}
.

Motivated essentially by the above discussions, we aim at deriving general harmonic analogues of Theorems
A
and
B
by using the pre-Schwarzian derivative of harmonic mappings.

Theorem 1.1
.

Let
f=h+\overline{g}
be a sense-preserving harmonic mapping in
\triangle
and let
\omega
be its second complex dilatation.
Let
\sigma
be a continuous function in
\triangle
, which satisfies the following conditions: (i)
\sigma_{z}
and
\sigma_{\overline{z}}
exist in
\triangle
a.e.; (ii)
\frac{1}{\sigma}=0
on
\partial\triangle
; (iii)
\frac{\sigma_{\overline{z}}}{\sigma^{2}}\neq 0
in
\overline{\triangle}
. If
f
satisfies the condition

(1.26)

\left|\sigma P_{f}+\sigma^{2}-\sigma_{z}\right|+\frac{|\sigma\omega^{\prime}|}{1-|\omega|^{2}}\leq\left|\sigma_{\overline{z}}\right|\ \ (z\in\triangle),

then
f
is univalent in
\triangle
.

Remark 1.6
.

By setting
\sigma(z)=\frac{(c+1)\overline{z}}{1-|z|^{2}}
in (
1.26
) of Theorem
1.1
, we get Theorem
J
due to Bravo
et\ al.

[
5
]
;
By putting
\sigma(z)=\frac{\overline{z}}{1-|z|^{2}}
in (
1.26
) of Theorem
1.1
, we obtain Theorem
H
due to Hernández and Martín
[
13
]
.

Theorem 1.2
.

Let
f=h+\overline{g}
be a sense-preserving harmonic mapping in
\triangle
and let
\omega
be its second complex dilatation with
\|\omega\|_{\infty}<1
.
Let
\sigma
be a continuous function in
\triangle
, which satisfies conditions given in Theorem
1.1
. Assume that

(1.27)

\left|\sigma P_{f}+\sigma^{2}-\sigma_{z}\right|+\frac{|\sigma\omega^{\prime}|}{1-|\omega|^{2}}\leq k\left|\sigma_{\overline{z}}\right|\ \ (0\leq k<1;\,z\in\triangle).

Then the harmonic mapping
f_{\lambda}=h+\lambda\overline{g}\;(|\lambda|\leq 1)
has a continuous and injective extension
\widetilde{f}
to
\overline{\triangle}
. Moreover, the function

(1.28)

F_{\lambda}(z)=\begin{cases}\widetilde{f}_{\lambda}(z)&(|z|\leq 1),\\
f_{\lambda}\left(\frac{1}{\overline{z}}\right)+U_{\lambda}\left(\frac{1}{\overline{z}}\right)&(|z|>1),\end{cases}

is a homeomorphic extension of
f
to
\mathbb{C}
onto itself. The function
U_{\lambda}
that appears in (
1.28
) is defined by

U_{\lambda}(z)=\frac{h^{\prime}(z)}{\sigma(z)}+\lambda\frac{\overline{g^{\prime}(z)}}{\overline{\sigma(z)}}\quad(z\in\triangle\backslash\{0\}).

Furthermore, if
k
satisfies the condition (
1.18
), then the family of mappings
F_{\lambda}(z)
are
K
-quasiconformal mappings of
\mathbb{C}
, where
K
is given by (
1.21
).

Remark 1.7
.

By setting
\lambda=0
in Theorem
1.2
, we get Theorem
B
exactly given by Ahlfors
[
1
]
;
By putting
\sigma(z)=\frac{\overline{z}}{1-|z|^{2}}
in Theorem
1.2
, we obtain the
result in Theorem
K
due to Chen and Que
[
7
]
and Theorem

due to Hu and Fan
[

]
.
By setting
\sigma(z)=\frac{\overline{z}}{1-|z|^{2}}
and

[

]
.

Theorem 1.3
.

Let
f=h+\overline{g}
be a sense-preserving harmonic mapping in
\triangle
with
\|\omega\|_{\infty}<1
. If
f
satisfies the condition (
1.27
),
then
\widetilde{f}(\partial{\triangle})
is a quasicircle and
f
can be extended to a quasiconformal mapping in
\mathbb{C}
.

By means of the pre-Schwazian derivative and Schwazian derivative of harmonic
mappings, by setting

\sigma=v-\frac{1}{2}P_{f},

and

\sigma=\frac{c\overline{z}}{1-|z|^{2}}-\frac{1}{2}P_{f}

in Theorem
1.2
, we get following two corollaries, respectively.

Corollary 1.1
.

For a sense-preserving harmonic mapping
f
in
\triangle
, the inequality

\left|\frac{1}{2}S_{f}+v^{2}-v_{z}\right|+\left|(v-P_{f})\frac{\omega^{\prime}}{1-|\omega|^{2}}\right|\leq k\left|v_{\overline{z}}-\overline{P_{f}}\right|\ \ (0\leq k<1)

together with
v\rightarrow\infty
for
|z|\rightarrow 1
and
\frac{v_{\overline{z}}}{v^{2}}\neq 0
is sufficient to imply the existence of a
\frac{1+k}{1-k}
-quasiconformal extension of
f
.

Corollary 1.2
.

For a sense-preserving harmonic mapping
f
in
\triangle
, the inequality

(1.29)

\displaystyle\left|\frac{1}{2}S_{f}(1-|z|^{2})^{2}-c(1-c)\overline{z}\right|

\displaystyle+\left|\left(c\overline{z}(1-|z|^{2})-\frac{1}{2}P_{f}(1-|z|^{2})^{2}\right)\frac{\omega^{\prime}}{1-|\omega|^{2}}\right|

\displaystyle\leq k\left|c-\overline{P_{f}}(1-|z|^{2})^{2}\right|\ \ (0\leq k<1;\,|c-1|\leq k)

is sufficient to imply the existence of a
\frac{1+k}{1-k}
-quasiconformal extension of
f
.

Remark 1.8
.

Corollary
1.1
and Corollary
1.2
extended Theorem
F
and Theorem
G
to harmonic cases, respectively.

Theorem 1.4
.

Let
f
be a sense-preserving harmonic mapping in
\triangle
with a representation
f=h+\alpha\overline{h}
, where
h
is a locally univalent analytic function in
\triangle
. Let
\sigma
satisfy the conditions in Theorem
1.1
and
\alpha
be a constant with
|\alpha|<1
. Assume that
h
satisfies the condition

(1.30)

\left|\sigma P_{h}+\sigma^{2}-\sigma_{z}\right|\leq k\left|\sigma_{\overline{z}}\right|\ \ (0\leq k<1).

Then
f
is a harmonic Teichmüller mappings of
\triangle
and has a continuous and homeomorphic extension
\tilde{f}
to
\overline{\triangle}
. Moreover, the mapping

(1.31)

F_{\alpha}(z)=\begin{cases}\widetilde{f}(z)&(|z|\leq 1),\\
f\left(\frac{1}{\overline{z}}\right)+U_{\alpha}\left(\frac{1}{\overline{z}}\right)&(|z|>1),\end{cases}
