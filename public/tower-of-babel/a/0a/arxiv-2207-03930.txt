The possibility of X ⁡ ( 4014 ) X(4014) as a D ∗ ​ D ¯ ∗ D^{\ast}\bar{D}^{\ast} molecular state
Man-Yu Duan 1, Dian-Yong Chen 1,3, En Wang 2

Abstract
Within the framework of the local hidden gauge approach, we have studied the near-threshold interaction of the D ∗ ​ D ¯ ∗ D^{*}\bar{D}^{*} channel with quantum numbers I ⁡ ( J P ​ C ) = 0 ​ ( 0 + ⁣ + ) I(J^{PC})=0(0^{++}) , 0 ​ ( 2 + ⁣ + ) 0(2^{++}) , 1 ​ ( 0 + ⁣ + ) 1(0^{++}) , and 1 ​ ( 2 + ⁣ + ) 1(2^{++}) , respectively. The contact interaction is taken into account alone, since it is expected to give the dominant contribution near the threshold. One pole, which is found in the case of the quantum numbers I ⁡ ( J P ​ C ) = 0 ​ ( 0 + ⁣ + ) I(J^{PC})=0(0^{++}) , could be associated to the X ⁡ ( 4014 ) X(4014) recently observed by the Belle Collaboration. Thus, we suggest that the X ⁡ ( 4014 ) X(4014) may be an D ∗ ​ D ¯ ∗ D^{*}\bar{D}^{*} molecular state with I ⁡ ( J P ​ C ) = 0 ​ ( 0 + ⁣ + ) I(J^{PC})=0(0^{++}) , and more precise information about X ⁡ ( 4014 ) X(4014) , such as resonance parameters and decay properties, could be useful to shed light on its structure.

II
FORMALISM

In this section, we investigate the
D^{*}\bar{D}^{*}
interaction following the approach of Ref.
Molina:2009ct
. The Lagrangian is taken from the local hidden gauge formalism describing the interaction of vector mesons
Bando:1987br
;
Nagahiro:2008cv
,

\mathcal{L}=-\frac{1}{4}\langle V_{\mu\nu}V^{\mu\nu}\rangle\ ,

(3)

where the symbol
\langle~\rangle
stands for the trace of SU(4), and the tensor
V_{\mu\nu}
is defined as

V_{\mu\nu}=\partial_{\mu}V_{\nu}-\partial_{\nu}V_{\mu}-ig[V_{\mu},V_{\nu}]\

(4)

with
V_{\mu}
to be

V_{\mu}=\left(\begin{array}[]{cccc}\frac{\omega}{\sqrt{2}}+\frac{\rho^{0}}{\sqrt{2}}&\rho^{+}&K^{*+}&\bar{D}^{*0}\\
\rho^{-}&\frac{\omega}{\sqrt{2}}-\frac{\rho^{0}}{\sqrt{2}}&K^{*0}&D^{*-}\\
K^{*-}&\bar{K}^{*0}&\phi&D^{*-}_{s}\\
D^{*0}&D^{*+}&D^{*+}_{s}&J/\psi\end{array}\right)_{\mu}\ .

(5)

By expanding the effective Lagrangian in Eq. (
3
), one can obtain two types of
D^{\ast}\bar{D}^{\ast}
interactions, which are vector-meson exchange interactions [Fig.
1
-(a)] and contact interaction [Fig.
1
-(b)], respectively. As for the vector meson exchange interaction, the general form of the amplitude corresponding to diagram Fig.
1
-(a) reads,

\displaystyle i\mathcal{M}=ig_{1}g_{2}\epsilon_{1\mu}\epsilon_{2\nu}\Gamma^{\mu\alpha}\frac{-i(-g^{\nu\beta}+q^{\nu}q^{\beta}/m_{V}^{2})}{q^{2}-m_{V}^{2}+i\epsilon}\epsilon_{3\alpha}^{\ast}\epsilon_{4\beta}^{\ast}\ .

(6)

where
q
is the momentum of the exchanged vector meson, and
\Gamma^{\mu\nu}
is a tensor function of
g^{\mu\nu}
and
p_{i}^{\mu}p_{j}^{\nu}
. As shown in Eq. (
2
), the mass of
X(4014)
is very close to the threshold of
D^{\ast}\bar{D}^{\ast}
and the width is very small. Thus, when we discuss the possibility of
X(4014)
as a
D^{\ast}\bar{D}^{\ast}
molecular state, we should merely consider the energy range around the threshold. In this case, the momenta of the involved mesons are very limited, i.e.,
q^{2}\sim 0
. Then the propagator of the exchanged meson can be approximated by,

\displaystyle\frac{(-g^{\nu\beta}+q^{\nu}q^{\beta}/m_{V}^{2})}{q^{2}-m_{V}^{2}+i\epsilon}\simeq\frac{g^{\nu\beta}}{m_{V}^{2}}

(7)

where the terms suppressed by
\mathcal{O}(q^{2}/m_{V}^{2})
have been neglected. With the above approximation, the amplitudes of the meson exchanged diagrams are in the same form as the one of contact diagram. Thus, in the following, we can only consider the contact interaction in the present work.

Moreover, the decay width of the molecular states are related to the contributions of the coupled channels in the present theoretical models
Zyla:2020zbs
. The uncertainty of the widths reported by Belle Collaborations is very large, while the mass is relatively accurate. Thus as the first step, we can mainly focus on the mass of
X(4014)
, which justifies including the
D^{*}\bar{D}^{*}
single channel in the present work. Our understanding of molecular state will greatly benefit from a systematic spectrum of hadronic molecules based on the one single channel
Dong:2021juy
.

To depict the contact diagram, one can expand the Lagrangian in Eq. (
3
) and the effective Lagrangian for the contact term reads,

\mathcal{L}^{(c)}=\frac{g^{2}}{2}\langle V_{\mu}V_{\nu}V^{\mu}V^{\nu}-V_{\nu}V_{\mu}V^{\mu}V^{\nu}\rangle\ .

(8)

With the above effective Lagrangian, one can obtain the amplitude corresponding to Fig.
1
-(b), which is,

\displaystyle t^{(c)}_{D^{*+}D^{*-}\to D^{*+}D^{*-}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 2g^{2}(\epsilon^{(1)}_{\mu}\epsilon^{(2)\,\mu}\epsilon^{\ast(3)}_{\nu}\epsilon^{\ast(4)\,\nu}+\epsilon^{(1)}_{\mu}\epsilon^{(2)}_{\nu}\epsilon^{\ast(3)\,\mu}\epsilon^{\ast(4)\,\nu}

\displaystyle-2\epsilon^{(1)}_{\mu}\epsilon^{(2)}_{\nu}\epsilon^{\ast(3)\,\nu}\epsilon^{\ast(4)\,\mu})\ ,

\displaystyle t^{(c)}_{D^{*+}{D}^{*-}\to D^{*0}\bar{D}^{*0}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle g^{2}(\epsilon^{(1)}_{\mu}\epsilon^{(2)\,\mu}\epsilon^{\ast(3)}_{\nu}\epsilon^{\ast(4)\,\nu}+\epsilon^{(1)}_{\mu}\epsilon^{(2)}_{\nu}\epsilon^{\ast(3)\,\mu}\epsilon^{\ast(4)\,\nu}

(9)

\displaystyle-2\epsilon^{(1)}_{\mu}\epsilon^{(2)}_{\nu}\epsilon^{\ast(3)\,\nu}\epsilon^{\ast(4)\,\mu})\ ,

where the indices 1, 2, 3, and 4 correspond to the particles with the momenta
p_{1}
,
p_{2}
,
p_{3}
, and
p_{4}
in Fig.
1
-(b). By considering the spin projection operators and the isospin doublets charmed and anticharmed mesons as Refs.
Molina:2009ct
;
Molina:2008jw
, we obtain the kernels of
D^{*}\bar{D}^{*}
interactions for different isospin and total angular momentum, which are,

\displaystyle V^{I=0,J=0}_{D^{*}\bar{D}^{*}\to D^{*}\bar{D}^{*}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 6g_{D}^{2},\qquad V^{I=0,J=2}_{D^{*}\bar{D}^{*}\to D^{*}\bar{D}^{*}}=-3g_{D}^{2},

\displaystyle V^{I=1,J=0}_{D^{*}\bar{D}^{*}\to D^{*}\bar{D}^{*}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 2g_{D}^{2},\qquad V^{I=1,J=2}_{D^{*}\bar{D}^{*}\to D^{*}\bar{D}^{*}}=-g_{D}^{2},

(10)

With the above potentials, one can search the possible poles by solving the Bethe-Salpeter equation, which is

T=[1-VG]^{-1}V\ ,

(11)

where the
G
is the two-meson loop function given by

G=i\int\frac{d^{4}q}{(2\pi)^{4}}\frac{1}{q^{2}-m_{1}^{2}+i\epsilon}\frac{1}{(q-P)^{2}-m_{2}^{2}+i\epsilon}\ ,

(12)

with
m_{1}
and
m_{2}
the masses of the two mesons.
q
is the four-momentum of the meson in the centre of mass frame, and
P
is the total four-momentum of the meson-meson system.

In the present work, we use the dimensional regularization method as indicated in Refs.
Molina:2009ct
;
Duan:2020vye
, and in this scheme, the two-meson loop function
G
can be expressed as,

\displaystyle G

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{16\pi^{2}}\left[\alpha+\log\frac{m_{1}^{2}}{\mu^{2}}+\frac{m_{2}^{2}-m_{1}^{2}+s}{2s}\log\frac{m_{2}^{2}}{m_{1}^{2}}\right.

(13)

\displaystyle+\frac{|\vec{q}\,|}{\sqrt{s}}\left(\log\frac{s-m_{2}^{2}+m_{1}^{2}+2|\vec{q}\,|\sqrt{s}}{-s+m_{2}^{2}-m_{1}^{2}+2|\vec{q}\,|\sqrt{s}}\right.

\displaystyle+\left.\left.\log\frac{s+m_{2}^{2}-m_{1}^{2}+2|\vec{q}\,|\sqrt{s}}{-s-m_{2}^{2}+m_{1}^{2}+2|\vec{q}\,|\sqrt{s}}\right)\right],

where
\mu
and
\alpha
are model parameter, while
\vec{q}\,
is the momentum of the meson in the centre of mass frame, which reads,

|\vec{q}\,|=\frac{\sqrt{\left[s-(m_{1}+m_{2})^{2}\right]\left[s-(m_{1}-m_{2})^{2}\right]}}{2\sqrt{s}}.

(14)

In the complex plane for a general
\sqrt{s}
, the loop function in the second Riemann sheet can be written as

G^{II}(\sqrt{s})=G^{I}(\sqrt{s})+i\frac{|\vec{q}\,|}{4\pi\sqrt{s}},\qquad{\rm Im}(|\vec{q}\,|)>0,

(15)

where
G^{II}
refers to the loop function in the second Riemann sheet, and
G^{I}
is the one in the first Riemann sheet as given by Eq. (
13
) for the
D^{*}\bar{D}^{*}
channel. When searching for the poles, we use
G^{I}
for Re
(\sqrt{s})<m_{1}+m_{2}

G^{II}

(\sqrt{s})>m_{1}+m_{2}

Before we discuss the numerical results, the values of the relevant parameters should be clarified, which include the coupling constants
g_{D}
, the parameter
\mu

\alpha

\alpha

Molina:2009ct

g_{D}=m_{D^{\ast}}/(2f_{D})=6.9

Molina:2009ct

will be changed in the present estimation. Here we vary
g_{D}
from 5 to 8 to check the parameter dependence of the results. It should be clarified that the value of the subtraction constant

;
Duan:2021pll

Molina:2009ct

X(3915)

X(4014)

\gamma\gamma\to\gamma\psi(2S)
are very inaccurate around
4014

\alpha
in a large range to check whether one can reproduce the mass of
X(4014)
in a reasonable parameter range.

IV
Summary

Inspired by recent observation of
X(4014)
in the process
\gamma\gamma\to\gamma\psi(2S)
, we have studied the hidden-charm
D^{*}\bar{D}^{*}
interaction within the framework of the local hidden gauge approach, where the possible
I(J^{PC})
quantum numbers could be
0(0^{++}),0(2^{++}),1(0^{++})
, and
1(2^{++})
respectively. For the case of
I(J^{PC})=0(0^{++})
, our estimations indicate in a large coupling constant range, i.e.,
5.0<g_{D}<8.0
, one can reproduce the measured mass of
X(4014)
by adjusting the subtraction parameter
\alpha
. As for the case of
I(J^{PC})=1(0^{++})
, one can also reproduce the measured mass but only with a large coupling constants
g_{D}
. As for the cases of
I(J^{PC})=0(2^{++})
and
I(J^{PC})=1(2^{++})
, our calculations show that there is no pole in the modulus square of the amplitudes in the considered parameter space.

To summarize, in the
D^{\ast}\bar{D}^{\ast}
molecular scenario, our estimations weakly favor the
I(J^{PC})=0(0^{++})
assignment for
X(4014)
comparing to
I(J^{PC})=1(0^{++})
, and disfavor the
J=2
assignments. However, it should be clarified that the local significance of
X(4014)
is only 2.8
\sigma
, more experimental information about this state is crucial to shed light on its nature.

Before the end of this work, we should mention that there already exist some hints of
X(4140)
in the experimental data. For instance, the Belle Collaboration has measured the inclusive process
e^{+}e^{-}\to J/\psi X
, and one can observe a peak around 4014 MeV in the
M_{\rm rec}(J/\psi)
distribution, as shown in Fig. 1 of Ref.
Belle:2005lik
. Later, the Belle Collaboration reported the study of the processes
e^{+}e^{-}\to J/\psi D^{(*)}\bar{D}^{(*)}
, and there is more events in the energy region
4000<M_{D\bar{D}^{*}}<4025
MeV of the
D\bar{D}^{*}
invariant mass distribution, as depicted in Fig. 2(b) of Ref.
Belle:2007woe
. In addition, in the cross sections of the process
\gamma\gamma\to J/\psi\omega
measured by the Belle Collaboration, one can find the events concentrate in the region
4010\sim 4020
MeV
Belle:2009and
. All the above hints imply that one state with a mass close to the
D^{*}\bar{D}^{*}
threshold and a narrow width exists, which coincides with the
X(4140)
observed by the Belle Collaboration
Belle:2021nuv
.
