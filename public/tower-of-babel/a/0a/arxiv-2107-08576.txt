3.
Moment correspondences and quilted Floer theory

3.1.
Moment correspondences

Let
G
be a compact connected Lie group and
(X,\omega_{X})
a compact monotone Hamiltonian
G
-manifold with moment map
\mu_{X}:X\rightarrow\mathfrak{g}^{\vee}
. Weinstein
[
32
]
defined a Lagrangian correspondence
T^{*}G\xrightarrow{C}X^{-}\times X
, called the
moment correspondence
, defined by

C:=\{(g,\mu_{X}(x),x,g\cdot x)|~g\in G,x\in X\}

where
T^{*}G
is identified with
G\times\mathfrak{g}^{\vee}
by left multiplication. For any
g\in G
, define
L_{g}:=T^{*}_{g}G
and

\Delta_{g}:=\{(x,g\cdot x)|~x\in X\}.

For
g
equal to the identity element
e
of
G
, we also put
L:=L_{e}
and
\Delta:=\Delta_{e}
. A key observation is that for any
g\in G
, the
geometric composition

[
30
]

L_{g}\circ C
is embedded and is equal to
\Delta_{g}
.

In order to apply the quilted Floer theory of Ma’u-Wehrheim-Woodward
[
18
]
to the moment correspondence
C
, it is necessary to establish a suitable monotonicity condition. The following lemma will do the job.

Lemma 3.1
.

For any loop
\varphi:\partial D\rightarrow G
and disks
u_{\pm}:D\rightarrow X
satisfying

(3.1)

u_{+}(e^{i\theta})=\varphi(e^{i\theta})\cdot u_{-}(e^{i\theta})\quad\text{for any }\theta,

define
