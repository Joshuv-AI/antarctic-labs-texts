On the widths of η ⁡ ( 1295 ) \eta(1295) and η ⁡ ( 1405 / 1475 ) \eta(1405/1475)
Yin Cheng 1,2 , Lin Qiu 1,2 , Qiang Zhao 1,2

Abstract
Based on the assignment of the first radial excitation states of the isoscalar pseudoscalars for η ⁡ ( 1295 ) \eta(1295) and η ⁡ ( 1405 / 1475 ) \eta(1405/1475) , we investigate their three-body and four-body decay contributions to the total widths. In agreement with our previous studies we find that the triangle singularity (TS) mechanism arising from the intermediate K ∗ ​ K ¯ K^{*}\bar{K} rescatterings by exchanging a kaon or pion plays a crucial role in both K ​ K ¯ ​ π K\bar{K}\pi and η ​ π ​ π \eta\pi\pi channels. For the η X \eta_{X} ( η X \eta_{X} stands for η ⁡ ( 1295 ) \eta(1295) and η ⁡ ( 1405 / 1475 ) \eta(1405/1475) ) decays into K ​ K ¯ ​ π K\bar{K}\pi , we find that although the transition η X → K ∗ ​ K ¯ + c . c . → K ​ K ¯ ​ π \eta_{X}\to K^{*}\bar{K}+c.c.\to K\bar{K}\pi is the dominant tree-level process, the productions of the intermediate K ​ κ ¯ + c . c . K\bar{\kappa}+c.c. and a 0 ​ ( 980 ) ​ π a_{0}(980)\pi are strongly enhanced by the TS mechanism. For the η X \eta_{X} decays into η ​ π ​ π \eta\pi\pi , we find that the production of the intermediate a 0 ​ ( 980 ) ​ π a_{0}(980)\pi via the triangle transition is the dominant one for η ⁡ ( 1295 ) \eta(1295) partly because of the large η ⁡ ( 1295 ) ​ K ∗ ​ K ¯ \eta(1295)K^{*}\bar{K} coupling. In contrast, the tree-level and triangle loop contributions are compatible and dominant in the η ⁡ ( 1405 / 1475 ) \eta(1405/1475) decays into η ​ π ​ π \eta\pi\pi . It shows that a combined analysis is useful for disentangling the underlying dynamics for these two states.

IV
Summary

In summary, we have made a systematic study of the total widths of
\eta(1295)
and
\eta(1405)
as the first radial excitation states of
\eta
and
\eta^{\prime}
. By fitting the available B.R. fraction data for the
\eta(1405/1475)
decays, we can determine the parameters and make a reasonable description of the invariant mass spectra in both the
\eta(1295)
and
\eta(1405)
decays into
K\bar{K}\pi
and
\eta\pi\pi
. Our study suggests that these two channels are the dominant ones which involve several intermediate two-body transitions, such as
\kappa\bar{K}
,
a_{0}(980)\pi
,
\sigma\eta
and
f_{0}(980)\eta
. In particular, our study shows that the TS mechanism plays a crucial role in the productions of
\kappa\bar{K}
and
a_{0}(980)\pi
in the
K\bar{K}\pi
channel, and in the production of
a_{0}(980)\pi
in the
\eta\pi\pi
channel. This is in agreement with our previous findings.

We have also studied the four-body decays into
K\bar{K}\pi\pi
and
4\pi
for
\eta(1295)
and
\eta(1405)
and find they are rather small. It confirms that the three-body decays into
K\bar{K}\pi
and
\eta\pi\pi
have nearly saturated the total widths of these two states. Note that the interferences due to different intermediate two-body transitions are important. It suggests that a combined partial wave analysis from threshold up to 1.5 GeV is necessary. The relevant analysis will be reported in a forthcoming work.
