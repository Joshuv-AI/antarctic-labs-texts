Half-filled stripe to N e ´ \acute{e} el antiferromagnetism transition in the t ′ t^{\prime} -Hubbard model on honeycomb lattice
Yang Shen, Mingpu Qin

Abstract
We study the ground state of the doped Hubbard model on honeycomb lattice with both nearest ( t t ) and next-nearest neighboring hoppings ( t ′ t^{\prime} ) in the small doping and strongly interacting region. Previous study on the model without t ′ t^{\prime} showed the ground state is a half-filled stripe. We employ density matrix renormalization group and extrapolate the results with truncation errors in the converged region. In the t ′ < 0 t^{\prime}<0 side, we find the half-filled stripe phase at t ′ = 0 t^{\prime}=0 is stable against the frustration of t ′ t^{\prime} until a critical point − 0.4 < t c ′ < − 0.3 -0.4<t^{\prime}_{c}<-0.3 , beyond which the ground state switches to anti-ferromagnetic N e ´ \acute{e} el phase with charge modulation. With further increase of t ′ t^{\prime} to − 0.7 -0.7 , the ground state becomes paramagnetic. In the t ′ > 0 t^{\prime}>0 side, the half-filled stripe stretches to t ′ ≈ 0.7 t^{\prime}\approx 0.7 . We don’t find obvious enhancement of pairing for the range of t ′ t^{\prime} studied. We study width-4 cylinders in this work but the results for spin, charge, and pairing correlation agree qualitatively for periodic and anti-periodic boundary conditions in the half-filled stripe and anti-ferromagnetic N e ´ \acute{e} el phases, suggesting the results are likely to be representative for true two-dimensional systems. The half-filled stripe to anti-ferromagnetic N e ´ \acute{e} el phase transition can be realized on real materials or ultra-cold atom platform.



Understanding the physics of doped Mott insulators is crucial to reveal the microscopic mechanism of high-Tc superconductivity
Lee et al. 2006
.
Hubbard model and its descendant, i.e.,
t-J
model, are the prototype models to study Mott-related physics
Hubbard 1963
;
Scalapino 2012
;
Qin et al. 2022
;
Arovas et al. 2022
.
With a simple form, Hubbard model can host many exotic quantum states
Hubbard 1963
;
Scalapino 2012
;
Qin et al. 2022
;
Arovas et al. 2022
.

Recently, progresses have been made in the study of the doped Hubbard model on the square lattice
LeBlanc et al. 2015
. Collaboration of state-of-the-art numerical methods
Zheng et al. 2017
established the filled-stripe phase in the doped Hubbard model. It was also found that the “pure” Hubbard model (with only nearest neighboring hoppings) on square lattice doesn’t host superconductivity in its ground state
Qin et al. 2020
.

The Hubbard model on honeycomb lattice
Castro Neto et al. 2009
has also been extensively studied, partly due to the interests in the correlation-driven metal-insulator transition and the connection to graphene
Otsuka et al. 2016
;
Assaad and Herbut 2013
.
Similar to the square lattice, honeycomb lattice is bipartite, and the half-filled Hubbard model on honeycomb lattice is also a Mott insulator with antiferromagnetic (AF) N
\acute{e}
el order at strong interactions
Assaad and Herbut 2013
;
Sorella and Tosatti 1992
.
These similarities make honeycomb lattice another playground to study Mott-related physics and to explore possible mechanisms of high-Tc superconductivity.
Recent calculation showed the ground state of the slightly doped Hubbard model on honeycomb lattice also has stripe order similar as the square lattice case
Yang et al. 2021
;
Qin 2022
. The difference between them is that the filling of stripe on honeycomb lattice is
1/2

Yang et al. 2021
;
Qin 2022
. This finding was also confirmed by followed calculations
Xu et al. 2022
;
Peng et al. 2023
.

Given that superconductivity is absent in the pure Hubbard model on the square lattice
Qin et al. 2020
, other terms need to be included in the Hamiltonian to search for the possible superconducting ground state. The simplest term is the next-nearest neighboring hoppings
t^{\prime}
. To account for the particle-hole asymmetry of the phase diagram of cuprates
Scalapino 2012
, a nonzero
t^{\prime}
term is needed in the framework of single band Hubbard model.
Recent calculation for the
t^{\prime}
-Hubbard model on square lattice showed superconductivity indeed emerges with the inclusion of
t^{\prime}

Xu et al. 2023
. It is argued that
t^{\prime}
frustrates the stripe phase and helps the pairs across the stripes to build coherence, so the long-range superconducting order emerges
Xu et al. 2023
.

Model and Method –

The Hamiltonian of the Hubbard model is

\hat{H}=-\sum_{(i,j),\sigma}t_{ij}\left(\hat{c}_{i\sigma}^{\dagger}\hat{c}_{j\sigma}+h.c.\right)+U\sum_{i}\hat{n}_{i\uparrow}\hat{n}_{i\downarrow}

(1)

where
\hat{c}_{i\sigma}^{\dagger}(\hat{c}_{i\sigma})
creates (annihilates) an electron on site
i=(x_{i},y_{i})
with spin
\sigma
, and
U
represents the on-site Coulomb interaction. We consider electron hopping terms up to next-nearest neighbors (
t^{\prime}
) and set the nearest neighboring hopping
t
as energy unit. We focus on
U=8
in this work. We scan a range of
t^{\prime}
from
-0.7
to
0.7
. We only study the hole-doped case. The electron doped case can be transformed to the hole doped case by reversing the sign of
t^{\prime}
through a particle-hole transformation.
The averaged hole concentration away from half-filling is defined as
\delta=N_{h}/N
with
N_{h}=\sum_{i}(1-n_{i})
. An illustration of the honeycomb lattice is sketched in Fig.
1
(a). We rearrange it into a square lattice as shown in Fig.
1
(b) to index the sites more conveniently. After the rearrangement, we study the square system with cylinder geometry, i.e., with periodic/anti-periodic (open) boundary conditions along
y
(
x
) direction. There are totally
N=L_{x}\times L_{y}
lattice sites. We compare the results with PBC and APBC in our calculations to test the finite size effect.

We employ DMRG
White 1992
;
White 1993
in this work, which can provide accurate results of narrow cylinders
Schollwöck 2005
;
Stoudenmire and White 2012
. We focus on 1/16 hole doping regime in which half-filled stripe was found in the model without
t^{\prime}
before
Qin 2022
;
Yang et al. 2021
. We calculate systems with width
L_{y}=4
and length
L_{x}=32
. We apply AF pinning fields with strength
h_{m}=0.5
on the open edges of the width-4 cylinder, which allows us to detect spin order by measuring the local magnetization
\langle\hat{S}_{i}^{z}\rangle
instead of the more demanding spin-spin correlation functions and makes the DMRG calculations easier to converge
White and Chernyshev 2007
. To characterize the charge and spin order, we define rung charge density as
n(x)=\sum_{y=1}^{L_{y}}\left\langle\hat{n}_{i}(x,y)\right\rangle/L_{y}
along the width direction of the cylinders and staggered spin density as
(-1)^{i}\langle\hat{S}_{i}^{z}\rangle
. We also measure the singlet pair-pair correlation function defined as
D(r)=\langle\hat{\Delta}_{i}^{\dagger}\hat{\Delta}_{i+r}\rangle
with
\hat{\Delta}_{i}^{\dagger}=\hat{c}_{(i,1),\uparrow}^{\dagger}\hat{c}_{(i,2),\downarrow}^{\dagger}-\hat{c}_{(i,1),\downarrow}^{\dagger}\hat{c}_{(i,2),\uparrow}^{\dagger}
. The state kept in the DMRG calculation is as large as
m=11000
with a typical truncation error
\epsilon\approx 1.5\times 10^{-5}
and careful extrapolations of DMRG results with truncation errors are performed in the converged region.

Summary and perspectives –
We systematically study the evolution of the half-filled stripe order with next-nearest neighboring hopping
t^{\prime}
on the slightly doped Hubbard model on honeycomb lattice in the strongly interacting region. We employ DMRG and study width-4 cylinders. In the negative
t^{\prime}
side, the half-filled stripe phase is stable against the frustration of
t^{\prime}
until a critical point
-0.4<t^{\prime}_{c}<-0.3
, at which a phase transition from half-filled stripe to AF N
\acute{e}
el phase with charge modulation occurs. The charge-modulated AF N
\acute{e}
el phase terminates at about
t^{\prime}=-0.7
. In the positive
t^{\prime}
side, the half-filled stripe state stretches to about
t^{\prime}=0.7
. Different from the square lattice case
Xu et al. 2023
, we don’t find obvious enhancement of pairing for the range of
t^{\prime}
studied. The results with PBC and APBC agree qualitatively in the half-filled stripe and AF N
\acute{e}
el phases, indicating the results in this work are likely to be representative for real two-dimensional system.
It will be interesting to try to realize the phase transition from half-filled stripe to AF N
\acute{e}
el phase in real materials or in ultra-cold atom platforms.
