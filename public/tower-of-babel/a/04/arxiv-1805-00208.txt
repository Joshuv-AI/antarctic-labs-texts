Some properties of Controlled Fusion Frames
H. Shakoory, R. Ahmadi, N. Behzadi, S. Nami

Abstract
. Controlled frames in Hilbert spaces have been introduced by Balazs, Antoine and Grybos to improve the numerical output of in relation to algorithms for inverting the frame operator. In this paper we have introduced and displayed some new concepts and results on controlled fusion frames for Hilbert spaces. It is shown that controlled fusion frames as a generalization of fusion frames give a generalized way to obtain numerical advantage in the sense of reconditioning to check the fusion frame condition. For this end, we introduce the notion of Q-duality for Controlled fusion frames. Also, we survey the robustness of Controlled fusion frames under some perturbations.

2.
Preliminaries

In this section, some necessary definitions and lemmas are introduced.

Definition 1
.

A sequence
\{f_{i}\}_{i\in I}
in
H
is a frame if there exist constants
0<A\leqslant B<\infty
such that for all
f\in H

\displaystyle A\|f\|^{2}\leqslant\sum_{i\in I}|\langle f,f_{i}\rangle|^{2}\leqslant B\|f\|^{2}.

The constants
A,B
are called frame bounds;
A
is the lower bound and
B
is the upper bound. The frame is thight if
A=B
, it is called a Parseval frame if
A=B=1
. If we only have the upper bound, We call
\{f_{i}\}_{i\in I}
a Bessel sequence. If
\{f_{i}\}_{i\in I}
is a Bessel sequence then the following operators are bounded:

\displaystyle T

\displaystyle:\ell^{2}(I)\longmapsto H

\displaystyle T(c_{i})=\sum_{i\in I}c_{i}f_{i}

\displaystyle T^{*}

\displaystyle:H\longmapsto\ell^{2}(I)

\displaystyle T^{*}f=\{\langle f,f_{i}\rangle\}_{i\in I}

\displaystyle S

\displaystyle:H\longmapsto H

\displaystyle Sf

\displaystyle=TT^{*}f=\sum_{i\in I}\langle f,f_{i}\rangle f_{i}.

These operators are called synthesis operator; analysis operator and frame operator, respectively. The representation space employed in this setting is:

\displaystyle\ell^{2}(I)=\Big\{\{c_{i}\}_{i\in I}:\ c_{i}\in\mathbb{C},\ \sum_{i\in I}|c_{i}|^{2}<\infty\Big\}

.

.
Let
W:=\{W_{i}\}_{i\in I}
be a family of closed subspaces of
H
and

be a family of weights (i.e.
v_{i}>0
for any
i\in I
). We say that
W
is a fusion frame with respect to

if there exists

such that for each

\displaystyle A\|h\|^{2}\leq\sum_{i\in I}v^{2}_{i}\|\pi_{W_{i}}(h)\|^{2}\leq B\|h\|^{2}.

Throughout this paper,

is called a tight controlled fusion frame, if the constants

We define the controlled analysis operator by

\displaystyle T_{W}:H\rightarrow\mathcal{K}_{2,W}

\displaystyle T_{W}(f)=(v_{i}(C^{*}\pi_{W_{i}}C^{\prime})^{\frac{1}{2}}f).

\displaystyle\mathcal{K}_{2,W}:=\{v_{i}(C^{*}\pi_{W_{i}}C^{\prime})^{\frac{1}{2}}f\ :\ f\in H\}\subset(\bigoplus_{i\in I}H)_{l^{2}}.

\displaystyle T^{*}_{W}(v_{i}(C^{*}\pi_{W_{i}}C^{\prime})^{\frac{1}{2}}f)=\sum_{i\in I}v_{i}^{2}C^{*}\pi_{W_{i}}C^{\prime}f.

\displaystyle S_{W}f=T^{*}_{W}T_{W}(f)=\sum_{i\in I}v_{i}^{2}C^{*}\pi_{W_{i}}C^{\prime}f.

Theorem 3.1
.

W
be a
CC^{\prime}
-controlled fusion Bessel sequence for

with bound

\displaystyle T^{*}_{W}:\mathcal{K}_{2,W}\rightarrow H

\displaystyle T^{*}_{W}(v_{i}(C^{*}\pi_{W_{i}}C^{\prime})^{\frac{1}{2}}f)=\sum_{i\in I}v_{i}^{2}C^{*}\pi_{W_{i}}C^{\prime}f.

is well -defined and bounded operator with

Proof.

The necessary condition follows from the definition of
CC^{\prime}
-controlled fusion Bessel sequence. We only need to prove that the sufficient condition holds. Let
T^{*}_{W}
be well-defined and bounded operator with
\|T^{*}_{W}\|\leq\sqrt{B}

\displaystyle(\sum_{i\in I}v_{i}^{2}\langle\pi_{W_{i}}C^{\prime}f,\pi_{W_{i}}Cf\rangle)^{2}

\displaystyle=(\sum_{i\in I}v_{i}^{2}\langle C^{*}\pi_{W_{i}}C^{\prime}f,f\rangle)^{2}

\displaystyle=(\langle T^{*}_{W}\big(v_{i}(C^{*}\pi_{W_{i}}C^{\prime})^{\frac{1}{2}}f\big),f\rangle)^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\|T^{*}_{W}\|^{2}\|(v_{i}(C^{*}\pi_{W_{i}}C^{\prime})^{\frac{1}{2}}f\|^{2}\|f\|^{2}.

But

\displaystyle\|(v_{i}(C^{*}\pi_{W_{i}}C^{\prime})^{\frac{1}{2}}f\|_{2}^{2}=\sum_{i\in I}v_{i}^{2}\langle\pi_{W_{i}}C^{\prime}f,\pi_{W_{i}}Cf\rangle.

It follows that

\displaystyle\sum_{i\in I}v_{i}^{2}\langle\pi_{W_{i}}C^{\prime}f,\pi_{W_{i}}Cf\rangle\leq B\|f\|^{2}.

and this means that

-controlled fusion Bessel sequence for H.

.

Theorem 3.3
.

Let

-controlled fusion frame with frame bounds

\displaystyle\|\pi_{W_{i}}Cu^{*}f\|=\|\pi_{W_{i}}u^{*}Cf\|=\|\pi_{W_{i}}u^{*}\pi_{uW_{i}}C^{*}f\|\leq\|u\|\|\pi_{uW_{i}}C^{*}f\|.

\displaystyle\|f\|^{2}\leq\|(u^{-1})^{*}u^{*}f\|^{2}\leq\|u^{-1}\|^{2}\|u^{*}f\|^{2}.

\displaystyle A\|u^{-1}\|^{-2}\|u\|^{-2}\|f\|^{2}\leq\sum_{i\in I}\|\pi_{uW_{i}}C^{*}f\|.

.

4.
Q-dual and perturbation on Controlled fusion frame

Definition 4
.

Assume that
W
be a
CC^{\prime}
-Controlled fusion frame for H. We call a
CC^{\prime}
-controlled fusion Bessel sequence as
\tilde{W}:=\{(\tilde{W}_{i},z_{i})\}_{i\in I}
the Q-dual
CC^{\prime}
-Controlled fusion frame of
W

Q:\mathcal{K}_{2,W}\longrightarrow\mathcal{K}_{2,\tilde{W}}
such that

\displaystyle T^{*}_{W}QT_{\tilde{W}}=I_{H}.

Theorem 4.1
.

Let
\tilde{W}
be Q-dual
CC^{\prime}
-Controlled fusion frame for
W
and
Q:\mathcal{K}_{2,W}\longrightarrow\mathcal{K}_{2,\tilde{W}}
. Then, the following conditions are equivalent.

(1)

T^{*}_{\tilde{W}}Q^{*}T_{W}=I_{H}
;

(2)

T^{*}_{W}QT_{\tilde{W}}=I_{H}
;

(3)

\langle f,g\rangle=\langle Q^{*}T_{W}f,T_{\tilde{W}}g\rangle=\langle QT_{\tilde{W}}f,T_{W}g\rangle
.

Proof.

Straightforward.
∎

Theorem 4.2
.

If
\tilde{W}
is a Q-dual for
W
, then
\tilde{W}
is a
CC^{\prime}
-controlled fusion frame for H.

Proof.

Let
f\in H
and
B
an upper bound for
W
. Therefore

\displaystyle\|f\|^{4}

\displaystyle=|\langle f,f\rangle|^{2}

\displaystyle=|\langle Q^{*}T_{W_{i}}f,T_{\tilde{W}_{i}}f\rangle|^{2}

\displaystyle=|\langle QT_{\tilde{W}_{i}}f,T_{W_{i}}f\rangle|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\|T_{\tilde{W}_{i}}f\|^{2}\|Q\|^{2}\|T_{W_{i}}f\|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\|T_{\tilde{W}_{i}}f\|^{2}\|Q\|^{2}B\|f\|^{2}

\displaystyle=\|Q\|^{2}B\|f\|^{2}\sum_{i\in I}z_{i}^{2}\langle\pi_{\tilde{W}_{i}}C^{\prime}f,\pi_{\tilde{W}_{i}}Cf\rangle^{2}.

Hence,

\displaystyle B^{-1}\|Q\|^{-2}\|f\|^{2}\leq\sum_{i\in I}z_{i}^{2}\langle\pi_{\tilde{W}_{i}}C^{\prime}f,\pi_{\tilde{W}_{i}}Cf\rangle^{2}

and this completes the proof.
∎

\displaystyle\leq\|v_{i}(C^{*}\pi_{{Z}_{i}}C^{\prime}-C^{*}\pi_{W_{i}}C^{\prime})^{\frac{1}{2}}f\|_{2}+\|v_{i}(C^{*}\pi_{W_{i}}C^{\prime})^{\frac{1}{2}}f\|_{2}

\displaystyle\leq\lambda_{1}\|v_{i}(C^{*}\pi_{W_{i}}C^{\prime})^{\frac{1}{2}}f\|_{2}+\lambda_{2}\|v_{i}(C^{*}\pi_{Z_{i}}C^{\prime})^{\frac{1}{2}}f\|_{2}+

\displaystyle\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ +\|\beta\|_{2}\|f\|+\|v_{i}(C^{*}\pi_{W_{i}}C^{\prime})^{\frac{1}{2}}f\|_{2}.

Hence,

\displaystyle(1-\lambda_{2})\|(v_{i}(C^{*}\pi_{{Z}_{i}}C^{\prime})^{\frac{1}{2}}f\|_{2}\leq(1+\lambda_{1})\|v_{i}(C^{*}\pi_{W_{i}}C^{\prime})^{\frac{1}{2}}f\|_{2}+\|\beta\|_{2}\|f\|.

Since
W
is a
CC^{\prime}
-controlled fusion frame with bounds
A

, then

\displaystyle\|v_{i}(C^{*}\pi_{W_{i}}C^{\prime})^{\frac{1}{2}}f\|^{2}=\sum_{i\in I}v_{i}^{2}\langle\pi_{W_{i}}C^{\prime}f,\pi_{W_{i}}Cf\rangle\leq B\|f\|^{2}.

So,

\displaystyle\|v_{i}(C^{*}\pi_{{Z}_{i}}C^{\prime})^{\frac{1}{2}}f\|_{2}

\displaystyle\leq\dfrac{(1+\lambda_{1})\|v_{i}(C^{*}\pi_{W_{i}}C^{\prime})^{\frac{1}{2}}f\|_{2}+\|\beta\|_{2}\|f\|}{1-\lambda_{2}}

\displaystyle\leq(\dfrac{(1+\lambda_{1})\sqrt{B}+\|\beta\|_{2}}{1-\lambda_{2}}\|f\|).

Thus

\displaystyle\sum_{i\in I}v_{i}^{2}\langle\pi_{Z_{i}}C^{\prime}f,\pi_{Z_{i}}Cf\rangle=\|v_{i}(C^{*}\pi_{{Z}_{i}}C^{\prime})^{\frac{1}{2}}f\|^{2}_{2}

\displaystyle\leq(\dfrac{(1+\lambda_{1})\sqrt{B}+\|\beta\|_{2}}{1-\lambda_{2}}\|f\|)^{2}.

Now, for the lower bound, we have

\displaystyle\|v_{i}(C^{*}\pi_{Z_{i}}C^{\prime})^{\frac{1}{2}}f\|_{2}

\displaystyle=\|v_{i}(C^{*}\pi_{W_{i}}C^{\prime})^{\frac{1}{2}}f-v_{i}(C^{*}\pi_{W_{i}}C^{\prime}-C^{*}\pi_{{Z}_{i}}C^{\prime})^{\frac{1}{2}}f\|_{2}

\displaystyle\ \ \ \ \ -\lambda_{2}\|v_{i}(C^{*}\pi_{{Z}_{i}}C^{\prime})^{\frac{1}{2}}f\|_{2}-\|\beta\|_{2}\|f\|.

\displaystyle(1+\lambda_{2})\|v_{i}(C^{*}\pi_{{Z}_{i}}C^{\prime})^{\frac{1}{2}}f\|_{2}\geq(1-\lambda_{1})\|v_{i}(C^{*}\pi_{W_{i}}C^{\prime})^{\frac{1}{2}}f\|_{2}-\|\beta\|_{2}\|f\|,

\|v_{i}(C^{*}\pi_{{Z}_{i}}C^{\prime})^{\frac{1}{2}}f\|_{2}\geq\dfrac{(1-\lambda_{1})\|v_{i}(C^{*}\pi_{W_{i}}C^{\prime})f\|_{2}-\|\beta\|_{2}\|f\|}{1+\lambda_{2}}.

\displaystyle\|v_{i}(C^{*}\pi_{W_{i}}C^{\prime})^{\frac{1}{2}}f\|^{2}=\sum_{i\in I}v_{i}^{2}\langle\pi_{W_{i}}C^{\prime}f,\pi_{W_{i}}Cf\rangle\geq A\|f\|^{2}.

\displaystyle\|v_{i}(C^{*}\pi_{{Z}_{i}}C^{\prime})^{\frac{1}{2}}f\|_{2}\geq(\dfrac{(1-\lambda_{1})\sqrt{A}-\|\beta\|_{2}}{1+\lambda_{2}}\|f\|).

Thus,

\displaystyle=\|v_{i}(C^{*}\pi_{{Z}_{i}}C^{\prime})^{\frac{1}{2}}f\|^{2}_{2}

\displaystyle\geq(\dfrac{(1-\lambda_{1})\sqrt{A}-\|\beta\|_{2}}{1+\lambda_{2}}\|f\|)^{2}

and the proof is completed.
