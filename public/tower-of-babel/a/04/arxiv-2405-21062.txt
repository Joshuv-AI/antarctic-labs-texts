Algebra of global sections of ψ \psi -bundles on M ¯ 0 , n \overline{M}_{0,n}
Alexander Polishchuk, Eric Rains

Abstract
. We consider the ℤ n \Z^{n} -graded algebra of global sections of line bundles generated by the standard line bundles L 1 , … , L n L_{1},\ldots,L_{n} on M ¯ 0 , n \overline{M}_{0,n} . We find a simple presentation of this algebra by generators and quadratic relations. As an application we prove that the moduli space M ¯ 0 , n ​ [ ψ ] \overline{M}_{0,n}[\psi] of ψ \psi -stable curves of genus 0 0 is Cohen-Macaulay and normal, and the natural map M ¯ 0 , n → M ¯ 0 , n ​ [ ψ ] \overline{M}_{0,n}\to\overline{M}_{0,n}[\psi] is a rational resolution.
