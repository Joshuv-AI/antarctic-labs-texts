A Collatz Conjecture Proof


Abstract
We represent the generalized Collatz function with the recursive ruler function r ⁡ ( 2 ​ n ) = r ⁡ ( n ) + 1 r(2n)=r(n)+1 and r ⁡ ( 2 ​ n + 1 ) = 1 r(2n+1)=1 . We generate even-only and odd-only Collatz subsequences that contain significantly fewer elements term by term, to 2 2 and 1 1 , respectively, than are present in the original 3 ​ n + 1 3n+1 and the Terras-modified Collatz sequences. We show that a nonlinear, coupled system of difference equations yields a complete acyclic (except for the trivial cycle) Collatz tree in odds not divisible by 3 3 with root vertex 1 1 . We construct a complete Collatz tree with the axiom of choice and prove the Collatz conjecture.
