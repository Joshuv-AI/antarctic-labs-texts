A Conjecture of Flach and Morin
Bruno Chiarellotto, Nicola Mazzari, Yukihide Nakada

Abstract
A conjecture recently stated by Flach and Morin relates the action of the monodromy on the Galois invariant part of the p p -adic Beilinson–Hyodo–Kato cohomology of the generic fiber of a scheme defined over a DVR of mixed characteristic to (the cohomology of) its special fiber. We prove the conjecture in the case that the special fiber of the given arithmetic scheme is also a fiber of a geometric family over a curve in positive characteristic.

Remark 9
.

Of course a scheme
Y
in characteristic
p
can be embedded as a closed subscheme in other ways and these other embeddings suggest further directions for research.
For example,
Y
may be the fiber of a 1-dimensional
arithmetic
family, such as a discrete valuation ring of mixed or equal characteristic.

It may be interesting to consider
Y
as as the special fiber of a scheme
X
over a complete discrete valuation ring with residue field
k
and to give meaning to the cohomology

R\Gamma_{Y}(X)

with support in the special fiber.
It may be possible to extend the proof to this situation if such a cohomology is defined.
One could also study a family over a discrete valuation ring of equicharacteristic
p
, for example when
Y
is the special fiber of a scheme
X
over
k\llbracket t\rrbracket
.
Rigid cohomology over such a Laurent series has a more analytic flavor.
It is defined to be a functor

X\mapsto H^{*}_{\operatorname{rig}}(X/\mathscr{E}_{K})

taking values in graded vector spaces over the Amice ring

\mathscr{E}_{K}:=\left\{\sum_{i\in\mathbb{Z}}a_{i}t^{i}\in K\llbracket t,t^{-1}\rrbracket\,:\,\operatorname{sup}_{i}|a_{i}|<\infty,\lim_{i\to-\infty}a_{i}=0\right\}.

To study these objects one can study instead rigid cohomology over the bounded Robba ring
X\mapsto H^{*}_{\operatorname{rig}}(X/\mathscr{E}^{\dagger}_{K})
, where

\mathscr{E}^{\dagger}_{K}=\left\{\sum_{i\in\mathbb{Z}}a_{i}t^{i}\in K\llbracket t,t^{-1}\rrbracket:\operatorname{sup}_{i}|a_{i}|<\infty,\exists\eta<1\operatorname{s.t.}\lim_{i\to-\infty}|a_{i}|\eta^{i}=0\right\}

The bounded Robba ring has the additional virtue that it is a Henselian discretely valued field with residue field
k((t))
.
This cohomology theory is constructed so that when we base change to
\mathscr{E}_{K}
one recovers
\mathscr{E}_{K}
-valued rigid cohomology (see
[
16
, §2.2]
for details).
This is a direction for further research.
