Genus, Fiberedness, τ \tau and ϵ \epsilon of Satellite Knots with n n -Twisted Generalized Mazur patterns
Holt Bodish

Abstract
We study a family of ( 1 , 1 ) (1,1) -pattern knots that generalize the Mazur pattern, and compute the concordance invariants τ \tau and ϵ \epsilon of n n -twisted satellites formed from these patterns. We show that none of the n n -twisted patterns from this family act surjectively on the smooth or rational concordance group. We also determine when the n n -twisted generalized Mazur patterns are fibered in the solid torus, compute their genus in S 1 × D 2 S^{1}\times D^{2} , and show that n n -twisted satellites with generalized Mazur patterns and non-trivial companions are not Floer thin.
