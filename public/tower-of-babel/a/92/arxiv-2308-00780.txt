Higher genus Gromov-Witten theory of [ ℂ n / ℤ n ] [\mathbb{C}^{n}/\mathbb{Z}_{n}] II: crepant resolution correspondence
Deniz Genlik, Hsian-Hua Tseng

Abstract
. We study the structure of the higher genus Gromov-Witten theory of the total space K ​ ℙ n − 1 K\mathbb{P}^{n-1} of the canonical bundle of the projective space ℙ n − 1 \mathbb{P}^{n-1} . We prove the finite generation property for the Gromov-Witten potential of K ​ ℙ n − 1 K\mathbb{P}^{n-1} by working out the details of its cohomological field theory (CohFT). More precisely, we prove that the Gromov-Witten potential of K ​ ℙ n − 1 K\mathbb{P}^{n-1} lies in an explicit polynomial ring using the Givental-Teleman classification of the semisimple CohFTs. In [ 11 ] , we carried out a parallel study for [ ℂ n / ℤ n ] [\mathbb{C}^{n}/\mathbb{Z}_{n}] and proved that the Gromov-Witten potential of [ ℂ n / ℤ n ] [\mathbb{C}^{n}/\mathbb{Z}_{n}] lies in a similar polynomial ring. The main result of this paper is a crepant resolution correspondence for higher genus Gromov-Witten theories of K ​ ℙ n − 1 K\mathbb{P}^{n-1} and [ ℂ n / ℤ n ] [\mathbb{C}^{n}/\mathbb{Z}_{n}] , which is proved by establishing an isomorphism between the polynomial rings associated to K ​ ℙ n − 1 K\mathbb{P}^{n-1} and [ ℂ n / ℤ n ] [\mathbb{C}^{n}/\mathbb{Z}_{n}] . This paper generalizes the works of Lho-Pandharipande [ 19 ] for the case of [ ℂ 3 / ℤ 3 ] [\mathbb{C}^{3}/\mathbb{Z}_{3}] and Lho [ 17 ] for the case [ ℂ 5 / ℤ 5 ] [\mathbb{C}^{5}/\mathbb{Z}_{5}] to arbitrary n ≥ 3 n\geq 3 .

0&\text{ otherwise}.\end{cases}

When

So (
2.9

(2.13)

\exp\left(n\sum_{l>0}(-1)^{(n+1)l}\frac{B_{nl+1}\left(\frac{i}{n}\right)}{nl+1}\frac{z^{nl}}{nl}\right).

If
n

is even, then

\exp\left(n\sum_{l>0}(-1)^{l}\frac{B_{nl+1}\left(\frac{i}{n}\right)}{nl+1}\frac{z^{nl}}{nl}\right).

Consequently, the restriction

to

is the multiplication by (

).
