Abstract
The aim of the paper is to study the ring of differential operators 𝒟 ⁡ ( A ⁡ ( m ) ) {\cal D}(A(m)) on the generalized multi-cusp algebra A ⁡ ( m ) A(m) where m ∈ ℕ n m\in\mathbb{N}^{n} (of Krull dimension n n ).
