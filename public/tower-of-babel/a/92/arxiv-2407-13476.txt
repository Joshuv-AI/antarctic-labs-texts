Four-Charm-Quark Matter from the CMS Collaboration as a Witness of the Development of High-Precision Hadron Spectroscopy
Xiang Liu

Abstract
Recent CMS observations in the di- J / ψ J/\psi invariant mass spectrum reveal new structures at 6638 MeV ( X ⁡ ( 6600 ) X(6600) )) and 7134 MeV, and confirm the X ⁡ ( 6900 ) X(6900) at 6847 MeV. These findings provide crucial insights into four-charm-quark matter. The CMS Collaboration’s result serves as a testament to the progress in exploring exotic hadronic matter.

More importantly, these joint experimental and theoretical efforts will further advance our understanding of the non-perturbative behavior of the strong interaction by these joint. In fact, representing the precision frontier of particle physics, hadron spectroscopy is entering the high-precision era.

These discoveries provide crucial insights into the formation of four-charm-quark matter, a unique form of matter in nature.

Further efforts are needed to decipher these fully charm structures in the di-
J/\psi
invariant mass spectrum. There is challenge for experimental analysis when faced with high precision experimental data. As pointed out in the CMS article

In the following, a precise measurement of the spin and parity would provide deeper insights into the nature of these structures. This high-precision experimental measurement by CMS requires a high-precision theoretical study.

We also expect further experimental results on fully heavy systems from CMS in the near future.
