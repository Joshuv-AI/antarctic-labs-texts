On the high-energy instability of quarkonium production ∗
M.A. Nefedov

Abstract
The perturbative instability of NLO collinear factorisation (CF) computations of p T p_{T} -integrated cross sections of heavy quarkonium production at high hadronic or photon-hadron collision energy is discussed. We resolve this problem via the matching of NLO CF computation with the resummation of higher-order corrections ∝ α s n ​ ln n − 1 ⁡ ( s ^ / M 2 ) \propto\alpha_{s}^{n}\ln^{n-1}(\hat{s}/M^{2}) at high partonic center of mass energies s ^ ≫ M 2 \hat{s}\gg M^{2} . The resummation is performed in the Doubly-Logarithmic Approximation(DLA) of High-Energy Factorisation(HEF) formalism. We also report the results of the first computation of one-loop corrections to impact-factors involving heavy quark-antiquark pair in the intermediate states considered in the Non-Relativistic QCD (NRQCD) factorisation formalism for quarkonium production: Q ​ Q ¯ ​ [ S [ 8 ] 0 1 ] Q\bar{Q}\left[{}^{1}S_{0}^{[8]}\right] and Q ​ Q ¯ ​ [ S [ 1 ] 0 1 ] Q\bar{Q}\left[{}^{1}S_{0}^{[1]}\right] . These results are necessary for the extension of our resummation formalism beyond DLA.

The detailed analysis of NLO CF computation (see Refs. and references therein) shows that this instability comes from the high partonic center-of-mass energy (
\sqrt{\hat{s}}
) region of integration in the collinear factorisation formula, which for both considered processes can be written in a form:

\sigma(\sqrt{S})=\int\limits_{X_{\min}}^{1}\frac{dX}{X}{\cal L}_{ij}(X,\mu_{F})\hat{\sigma}_{ij}(X,\mu_{R},\mu_{F}),

(1)

where

In the photoproduction case, with
\sqrt{S}=\sqrt{s_{\gamma p}}

{\cal L}_{i\gamma}(X,\mu_{F})=Xf_{i}(X,\mu_{F})

\alpha_{s}

\hat{s}=(q_{1}+q_{2})^{2}

J/\psi

g(q_{1})+\gamma(q)\to c\bar{c}\left[{}^{3}S_{1}^{[1]}\right](p)+g,

(3)

The perturbative instability illustrated in Figs.

Such resummation is provided by the High-Energy Facotirsation (HEF) formalism of Refs.
Catani:1990xk
,
Catani:1990eg
,
Collins:1991ty
,
Catani:1994sq
, which resums the series of higher-order corrections to
\hat{\sigma}_{ij}
at leading power in

\alpha_{s}^{n}\ln^{n-1}(1/X)

\ln(1/X)

Lansberg:2023kzf
:

\displaystyle\hskip-34.1433pt\frac{d\hat{\sigma}_{i\gamma}^{\text{(HEF)}}}{dz}(X,\mu_{F},\mu_{R})=\frac{1}{2M^{2}}\int\limits_{0}^{\infty}d{\bf k}_{T}^{2}{\cal C}_{gi}(X,{\bf k}_{T}^{2},\mu_{F},\mu_{R})

\displaystyle\times\int\limits_{1/z}^{\infty}\frac{dy}{y}\frac{d{\cal H}}{dz}({\bf k}_{T}^{2},y,z),

(4)

where we take into account the possibility of experimental cuts on the elasticity variable
z=(Pp)/(Pq)
, with
P
being the proton momentum, and the resummation factor
{\cal C}_{gi}(X,{\bf k}_{T}^{2},\mu_{F},\mu_{R})
in the Doubly-Logarithmic Approximation (DLA) which resums terms
\propto\left[\alpha_{s}(\mu_{R})\ln(1/X)\ln({\bf k}_{T}^{2}/\mu_{F}^{2})\right]^{n}
, to stay consistent with
\mu_{F}
-evolution of PDFs, see the Sec. 2.3 of Ref.
Lansberg:2021vie
and references for more detailed discussion. The coefficient function

is derived in the Appendix A of Ref.
Lansberg:2023kzf
and is related to the following “off-shell” analog of the partonic subprocess (
3

R_{+}(k)+\gamma(q)\to c\bar{c}\left[{}^{3}S_{1}^{[1]}\right](p)+g,

(5)

with

so that
k^{2}=-{\bf k}_{T}^{2}
and
R_{+}
denotes the
Reggeized gluon
which can be defined e.g. using the gauge-invariant EFT for Milti-Regge processes in QCD
Lipatov95
. Coefficient functions of subprocesses with one Reggeized gluon in the initial state, such as (
5

impact-factors

The resummation formula for the
\eta_{c}
hadroproduction case involves two resummation factors
{\cal C}_{gi}

Lansberg:2021vie

2

R_{+}(k_{1})+R_{-}(k_{2})\to c\bar{c}\left[{}^{1}S_{0}^{[1]}\right](p),

(6)

with
k_{1,2}=q_{1,2}+k_{1,2T}

k_{1,2}^{2}=-{\bf k}_{1,2T}^{2}

The HEF resummation outlined above is valid only for

1

\displaystyle\hskip-28.45274pt\hat{\sigma}_{ij}(X)=w_{ij}(X)\hat{\sigma}_{ij}^{\text{(NLO CF)}}(X)

\displaystyle+(1-w_{ij}(X))\hat{\sigma}_{ij}^{\text{(HEF)}}(X),

(7)

which are computed according to the Inverse Error Weighting (InEW) prescription of Ref.
Echevarria:2018qyi

,
Lansberg:2023kzf

\displaystyle\to

\displaystyle Q\bar{Q}\left[{}^{1}S_{0}^{[8]}\right](p),

(8)

\displaystyle R_{+}(k)+g(q)

\displaystyle\to

\displaystyle Q\bar{Q}\left[{}^{1}S_{0}^{[1]}\right](p),

(9)

where
q^{2}=0
,
k^{2}=-{\bf k}_{T}^{2}
,
q^{+}=k^{-}=0
,
q^{-}>0

p^{2}=M^{2}=4m_{c}^{2}
and light-cone components are defined as
k^{\pm}=k^{0}\pm k^{3}

6

To regularise rapidity divergences in scalar one-loop integrals we tilt the direction-vectors of Wilson lines in the effective action from the light-cone:

n_{\pm}\to\tilde{n}_{\pm}=n_{\pm}+rn_{\mp},

(10)

with
0<r\ll 1
as was first proposed in Refs.
Hentschinski:2011tz
,
Chachamis:2012cc
, the same regularisation is also used in our Ref.
Nefedov:2019mrg
. Besides rapidity-divergent scalar one loop integrals which are listed in the latter paper the present computation also contains integrals mixing massive quadratic propagators with linear propagators, which also acquire nontrivial
r
-dependence. Fortunately, for such integrals massive propagators can be traded for massless ones, using the following algebraic identity:

\displaystyle\hskip-22.76219pt\frac{1}{((\tilde{n}_{+}l)+k_{+})(l^{2}-m^{2})}=\frac{1}{((\tilde{n}_{+}l)+k_{+})(l+\kappa\tilde{n}_{+})^{2}}

\displaystyle\hskip-22.76219pt+\frac{2\kappa{\left[(\tilde{n}_{+}l)+\frac{m^{2}+\tilde{n}_{+}^{2}\kappa^{2}}{2\kappa}\right]}}{{((\tilde{n}_{+}l)+k_{+})}(l+\kappa\tilde{n}_{+})^{2}(l^{2}-m^{2})},

(11)

where we choose the parameter
\kappa
in such a way that the linear denominator in the last term in the r.h.s. gets canceled, while in the first term we are left only with a linear and massless quadratic denominators. This identity can be applied recursively to remove all massive quadratic denominators from the integral containing the linear denominator. All additional terms generated by this procedure will be just usual one-loop integrals with quadratic massive or massless propagators. The known results from literature can be used for the latter integrals and we exploit the implementation of
PackageX

Patel:2015tea
into
FeynHelpers

Shtabovenko:2016whf
for this purpose. The new rapidity divergent scalar integrals which we have encountered during this computation are:

\displaystyle\hskip-39.83385ptB_{[-]}(-K,K-q)=\int\frac{d^{D}l}{[\tilde{l}^{-}](l-K)^{2}(l+K-q)^{2}},

(12)

\displaystyle\hskip-39.83385ptC_{[-]}(0,-K,K-q)=\int\frac{d^{D}l}{[\tilde{l}^{-}]l^{2}(l-K)^{2}(l+K-q)^{2}},

(13)

\displaystyle\hskip-39.83385ptB_{[-]}(p,K)=\int\frac{d^{D}l}{[\tilde{l}^{-}](l+p)^{2}(l+K)^{2}},

(14)

\displaystyle\hskip-39.83385ptC_{[-]}(p,K,k)=\int\frac{d^{D}l}{[\tilde{l}^{-}](l+p)^{2}(l+K)^{2}(l+k)^{2}},

(15)

where
K=[p-M^{2}n_{-}/(2q^{-})]/2
with
n_{-}^{\mu}=(1,0,0,1)^{\mu}
. These integrals have the same complexity as the integral
C_{[-]}
with two scales, computed in the Ref.
Nefedov:2019mrg
. We will cover the computation of these integrals in more detail in a longer version of this paper.

3
Conclusions and Outlook

In this contribution we have discussed the perturbative instability of
p_{T}
-integrated cross sections of production of heavy quarkonia at NLO of CF and its resolution through the matching with DLA resummation in the HEF formalism. We also describe our progress towards going beyond DLA, namely the first computation of one-loop corrections to impact factors involving NRQCD states of the
Q\bar{Q}
pair:
Q\bar{Q}\left[{}^{1}S_{0}^{[8]}\right]
and
Q\bar{Q}\left[{}^{1}S_{0}^{[1]}\right]
. The expected structure of rapidity, ultraviolet and infrared divergences had been found, which is a strong cross-check of the computation. In future the real-emission contribution will be also computed to obtain the infrared finite NLO correction to the impact-factors.
