Residue distributions, iterated residues, and the spherical automorphic spectrum
Marcelo De Martino, Volker Heiermann, Eric Opdam

Abstract
. Let G G be a split reductive group over a number field F F . We consider the computation of the inner product of two K K -spherical pseudo Eisenstein series of G G supported in [ T , 𝒪 ⁡ ( 1 ) ] [T,\mathcal{O}(1)] by means of residues, following a classical approach initiated by Langlands.
