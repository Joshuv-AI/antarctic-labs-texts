Representations and cohomolgies of Rota-Baxter 3-Lie algebras


Abstract
The goal of the present paper is to investigate representations and cohomologies of Rota-Baxter 3-Lie algebras with any weight. We introduce representations, matched pairs and Manin triples of Rota-Baxter 3-Lie algebras. Furthermore, we discuss cohomology theory of Rota-Baxter 3-Lie algebras. The deformations and central extensions of Rota-Baxter 3-Lie algebras are also studied.

Due to the importance of Rota-Baxter algebras, it is inevitable to

Representations and matched pairs of Rota-Baxter 3-Lie algebras

A 3-Lie algebra
[
17
]
is a vector space
A
together with a
skew-symmetric linear map
[\cdot,\cdot,\cdot]:\wedge^{3}A\longrightarrow A

satisfying

[x_{1},x_{2},[y_{1},y_{2},y_{3}]]=[[x_{1},x_{2},y_{1}],y_{2},y_{3}]+[y_{1},[x_{1},x_{2},y_{2}],y_{3}]+[y_{1},y_{2},[x_{1},x_{2},y_{3}]]

for all
x_{i},y_{i}\in A
.

Let
A
be a 3-Lie algebra over field
k

and
\lambda\in k

R:A\longrightarrow A
is
said to be a Rota-Baxter operator of weight
\lambda
if it
satisfies

\displaystyle[R(x),R(y),R(z)]

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle R([R(x),R(y),z]+[R(x),y,R(z)]+[x,R(y),R(z)]

\displaystyle+\lambda([R(x),y,z]+[x,R(y),z]+[x,y,R(z)])+\lambda^{2}[x,y,z]),~~~~~~~~~~(2.1)

for any
x,y,z\in A
. We call
(A,R)
a Rota-Baxter 3-Lie algebra of weight
\lambda
.

In view of
[
8
]
, we know that if
(A,R)
is a Rota-Baxter 3-Lie
algebra of weight
\lambda
, then
(A,[\ ,\ ,\ ]_{R},R)
is
also a Rota-Baxter 3-Lie algebra of weight
\lambda
, where
[\ ,\ ,\ ]_{R}
is given by

\displaystyle[x,y,z]_{R}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle[R(x),R(y),z]+[R(x),y,R(z)]+[x,R(y),R(z)]

\displaystyle+\lambda([R(x),y,z]+[x,R(y),z]+[x,y,R(z)])+\lambda^{2}[x,y,z],~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~(2.2)
