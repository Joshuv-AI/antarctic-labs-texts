Analysis of the hidden-charm pentaquark molecular states with strangeness and without strangeness via the QCD sum rules


Abstract
In this article, we investigate the D ¯ ​ Σ c \bar{D}\Sigma_{c} , D ¯ ​ Ξ c ′ \bar{D}\Xi^{\prime}_{c} , D ¯ ​ Σ c ∗ \bar{D}\Sigma_{c}^{*} , D ¯ ​ Ξ c ∗ \bar{D}\Xi_{c}^{*} , D ¯ ∗ ​ Σ c \bar{D}^{*}\Sigma_{c} , D ¯ ∗ ​ Ξ c ′ \bar{D}^{*}\Xi^{\prime}_{c} , D ¯ ∗ ​ Σ c ∗ \bar{D}^{*}\Sigma_{c}^{*} and D ¯ ∗ ​ Ξ c ∗ \bar{D}^{*}\Xi_{c}^{*} pentaquark molecular states with strangeness and without strangeness via the QCD sum rules at length, pay much attention to the light flavor S ​ U ​ ( 3 ) SU(3) breaking effects, and make predictions for new pentaquark molecular states besides assigning the P c ​ ( 4312 ) P_{c}(4312) , P c ​ ( 4380 ) P_{c}(4380) , P c ​ ( 4440 ) P_{c}(4440) , P c ​ ( 4457 ) P_{c}(4457) and P c ​ s ​ ( 4459 ) P_{cs}(4459) self-consistently.
