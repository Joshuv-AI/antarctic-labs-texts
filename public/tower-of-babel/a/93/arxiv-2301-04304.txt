3D Bosons and W 1 + ∞ W_{1+\infty} algebra
Wang Na†, Wu Ke‡

Abstract
In this paper, we consider 3D Young diagrams with at most N N layers in z z -axis direction, which can be constructed by N N 2D Young diagrams on slice z = j z=j , j = 1 , 2 , ⋯ , N j=1,2,\cdots,N from the Yang-Baxter equation. Use 2D Bosons { a j , m , m ∈ ℤ } \{a_{j,m},\ m\in\mathbb{Z}\} associated to 2D Young diagrams on the slice z = j z=j , we constructed 3D Bosons. Then we show the 3D Boson representation of W 1 + ∞ W_{1+\infty} algebra, and the Littlewood-Richardson rule for 3-Jack polynomials from the actions of 3D Bosons on 3D Young diagrams.



Keywords:
Affine Yangian, 3D Young diagrams, 3D Bosons, 3-Jack polynomials, Littlewood-Richardson rule.

The Schur functions defined on 2D Young diagrams are an attractive research object, which were used to determine irreducible characters of highest weight representations of the classical groups, and the Littlewood-Richarson rule for Schur functions show the relations between the representation spaces
[

3D Young diagrams (plane partitions) are a generalization of 2D Young diagrams, which arose naturally in crystal melting model

In this paper, we consider 3D Bosons and the Littlewood-Richardson rule for 3-Jack polynomials on 3D Young diagrams which parallel to 2D Bosons and the Littlewood-Richardson rule for Schur functions or Jack polynomials on 2D Young diagrams.

The Littlewood-Richardson rule for Schur functions are well known, for example,

They show the relations between the irreducible representation spaces of the general linear groups or permutation groups.
