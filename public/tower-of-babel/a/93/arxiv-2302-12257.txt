Preliminaries

We recall some basic facts and definition on modular forms. For more details, we refer to
[
8
,
12
]
. We start with some matrix groups. We define

\displaystyle\Gamma:=\mathrm{SL_{2}}(\mathbb{Z})=

\displaystyle\left\{\begin{bmatrix}a&&b\\
c&&d\end{bmatrix}:a,b,c,d\in\mathbb{Z},ad-bc=1\right\},

\displaystyle\Gamma_{\infty}:=

\displaystyle\left\{\begin{bmatrix}1&n\\
0&1\end{bmatrix}:n\in\mathbb{Z}\right\}.

For a positive integer

, we define

\displaystyle\Gamma_{0}(N):=

\displaystyle\left\{\begin{bmatrix}a&&b\\
c&&d\end{bmatrix}\in\mathrm{SL_{2}}(\mathbb{Z}):c\equiv 0\pmod{N}\right\},

\displaystyle\Gamma_{1}(N):=

\displaystyle\left\{\begin{bmatrix}a&&b\\
c&&d\end{bmatrix}\in\Gamma_{0}(N):a\equiv d\equiv 1\pmod{N}\right\}

and

\displaystyle\Gamma(N):=\left\{\begin{bmatrix}a&&b\\
c&&d\end{bmatrix}\in\mathrm{SL_{2}}(\mathbb{Z}):a\equiv d\equiv 1\pmod{N},\ b\equiv c\equiv 0\pmod{N}\right\}.

A subgroup
\Gamma
of
\mathrm{SL_{2}}(\mathbb{Z})
is called a congruence subgroup if it contains
\Gamma(N)
for some positive integer
N
and the smallest
N
with this property is called its level. Note that
\Gamma_{0}(N)
and
\Gamma_{1}(N)
are congruence subgroups of level

whereas
\mathrm{SL_{2}}(\mathbb{Z})
and
\Gamma_{\infty}
are congruence subgroups of level

The index of
\Gamma_{0}(N)
in
\Gamma
is

\displaystyle[\Gamma:\Gamma_{0}(N)]=N\prod\limits_{p|N}\left(1+\frac{1}{p}\right)

where
p
runs over prime divisors of
N
.

Let
\mathbb{H}
denote the upper half of the complex plane. The group

\displaystyle\mathrm{GL_{2}^{+}}(\mathbb{R}):=\left\{\begin{bmatrix}a&&b\\
c&&d\end{bmatrix}:a,b,c,d\in\mathbb{R},ad-bc>0\right\}

acts on
\mathbb{H}
by
\begin{bmatrix}a&&b\\
c&&d\end{bmatrix}z=\frac{az+b}{cz+d}.
We identify
\infty
with
\frac{1}{0}
and define
\begin{bmatrix}a&&b\\
c&&d\end{bmatrix}\frac{r}{s}=\frac{ar+bs}{cr+ds},
where
\frac{r}{s}\in\mathbb{Q}\cup\{\infty\}
. This gives an action of
\mathrm{GL_{2}^{+}}(\mathbb{R})
on the extended half plane
\mathbb{H}^{*}=\mathbb{H}\cup\mathbb{Q}\cup\{\infty\}
. Suppose that
\Gamma
is a congruence subgroup of
\mathrm{SL_{2}}(\mathbb{Z})
. A cusp of

is an equivalence class in
\mathbb{P}^{1}=\mathbb{Q}\cup\{\infty\}
under the action of
\Gamma
.

The group
\mathrm{GL_{2}^{+}}(\mathbb{R})
also acts on functions

. In particular, suppose that
\gamma=\begin{bmatrix}a&&b\\
c&&d\end{bmatrix}\in\mathrm{GL_{2}^{+}}(\mathbb{R})
. If
f(z)
is a meromorphic function on
\mathbb{H}
and
k
is an integer, then define the slash operator
|_{k}
by

\displaystyle(f|_{k}\gamma)(z):=(\det\gamma)^{k/2}(cz+d)^{-k}f(\gamma z).

Definition 2.1
.

Let
\Gamma
be a congruence subgroup of level
N
. A holomorphic function

is called a modular form of integer weight

on

if the following hold:

(1)

For all

and
\begin{bmatrix}a&&b\\
c&&d\end{bmatrix}\in\Gamma
,

\displaystyle f\left(\frac{az+b}{cz+d}\right)=(cz+d)^{k}f(z).

(2)

If
\gamma\in SL_{2}(\mathbb{Z})
, then
(f|_{k}\gamma)(z)
has a Fourier expnasion of the form

\displaystyle(f|_{k}\gamma)(z):=\sum\limits_{n\geq 0}a_{\gamma}(n)q_{N}^{n}

where
q_{N}:=e^{2\pi iz/N}
.

For a positive integer
k
, the complex vector space of modular forms of weight
k
with respect to a congruence subgroup
\Gamma
is denoted by

.

Definition 2.2
.

[
12
, Definition 1.15]

Let
\chi
be a Dirichlet character modulo

. We say that a modular form

has Nobentypus character

\displaystyle f\left(\frac{az+b}{cz+d}\right)=\chi(d)(cz+d)^{k}f(z)

for all

and
\begin{bmatrix}a&&b\\
c&&d\end{bmatrix}\in\Gamma_{0}(N)
. The space of such modular forms is denoted by

.

The relevant modular forms for the results obtained in this article arise from eta-quotients. We recall the Dedekind eta-function

\displaystyle\eta(z):=q^{1/24}(q;q)_{\infty}=q^{1/24}\prod\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}(1-q^{n})

\displaystyle f(z):=\prod\limits_{\delta|N}\eta(\delta z)^{r_{\delta}}

where

.

.

[

If

[
