CNN based IoT Device Identification
Kahraman Kostas

Abstract
While the use of the Internet of Things is becoming more and more popular, many security vulnerabilities are emerging with the large number of devices being introduced to the market. In this environment, IoT device identification methods provide a preventive security measure as an important factor in identifying these devices and detecting the vulnerabilities they suffer from. In this study, we present a method that identifies devices in the Aalto dataset using the convolutional neural network (CNN).

The CNN is used in network security or classification of network data, while the network packet information is converted to pseudo-images to present to the algorithm. For this operation, the length of the series of numbers to create the image is rounded to the nearest image size (36,64,256 or 1024). For this, the interrupt or 0 padding method is applied. For example, the algorithm adds 26 zeros to a series of 10 numbers to reach the closest length option (36). On the other hand, in a series with a length of 500 numbers, it is ignored by cutting after 256.
