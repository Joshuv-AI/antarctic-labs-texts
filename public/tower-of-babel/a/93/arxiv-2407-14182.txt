Phenomenological study of the charged particles production in pPb collisions at s NN \sqrt{s_{\rm{NN}}} = 5.02 TeV
Kapil Saraswat, Deependra Singh Rawat, Akash Pandey, Venktesh Singh, H. C. Chandola

Abstract
We have studied transverse momentum ( p T p_{\rm{T}} ) spectra of charged hadrons in various pseudo-rapidity ranges for p-Pb collisions at s NN \sqrt{s_{\rm{NN}}} = 5.02 TeV. The medium effects such as collective flow and energy loss resulting from heavy-ion collisions have also been investigated using modified Tsallis distribution function over a wide range of p T p_{\rm{T}} that indicates the transverse collective flow at low and intermediate p T p_{\rm{T}} range and in-medium energy loss in high p T p_{\rm{T}} range.

Tsallis/Hagedorn distribution function and its modification

In heavy-ion collisions, the transverse mass
(
m_{\rm{T}}=\sqrt{p^{2}_{\rm{T}}+m^{2}}
) distribution of the produced particles
can be described by the Hagedron function which is a QCD-inspired summed power
law
[
26
]
given as :

[

]
given as

\displaystyle E~\frac{d^{3}N}{dp^{3}}=C~\Bigg(1+\frac{m_{\rm{T}}}{p_{0}}\Bigg)^{-n}~.

(1)

Eq.(
1

m_{\rm{T}}
region and
the particles produced in QCD hard scatterings reflected in the high
p_{\rm{T}}

region.
We compare this function (Eq.
1

[
23

24

of thermodynamic origin which is given by

\displaystyle E\frac{d^{3}N}{dp^{3}}=C_{n}~m_{\rm{T}}~\Bigg(1+(q-1)\frac{m_{\rm{T}}}{T}\Bigg)^{-1/(q-1)}~.

(2)

The Tsallis distribution [Eq.(
2
)] describes near-thermal systems in
terms of Tsallis parameter
T
and the n0n-extensive parameter
q
which measures
degree of non-thermalization
[
28
]
.
The functions in Eq. (
1
) and in Eq. (
2

mathematical forms with
n=1/(q-1)
and
p_{0}=n~T

Larger values of
n

Both

Many theoretical and phenomenological study predict that

[
32

33
]
for quark-quark point scattering.

n
will be larger if multiple scattering centers are involved.
The study in Ref.
[
34
]
suggests that both the forms given in
Eq. (
1
) and in Eq. (
2
) excellently describe the measured
data hadron spectra in pp collisions.
We use Eq. (
2
) in case of pp collisions. Tsallis/Hagedorn function
is able to describe
p_{\rm{T}}
spectra in pp collisions at all RHIC and LHC
energies.

There have been many attempts to use the Tsallis distribution in heavy ion

The functional form of modified Tsallis distribution

\displaystyle E\frac{d^{3}N}{dp^{3}}

\displaystyle=A_{1}\Bigg[\exp\left(-\frac{\beta p_{\rm{T}}}{p_{1}}\right)+\frac{m_{\rm{T}}}{p_{1}}\Bigg]^{-n_{1}}~:~p_{\rm{T}}<p_{\rm{T_{th}}}~.

(3a)

\displaystyle E\frac{d^{3}N}{dp^{3}}

\displaystyle=A_{2}~\Bigg[\frac{B}{p_{2}}~\Bigg(\frac{p_{\rm{T}}}{q_{0}}\Bigg)^{\alpha}+\frac{m_{\rm{T}}}{p_{2}}\Bigg]^{-n_{2}}~:~p_{\rm{T}}>p_{\rm{T_{th}}}~.

(3b)

Eq. (
3a
) shows the thermal and
collective behaviour of the hadton spectra with the parameters : the temperature
(
T=p_{1}/n_{1}
) and the average transverse flow velocity
(
\beta
)

If we shift the transverse mass distribution in Eq. (

E\frac{d^{3}N}{dp^{3}}=A_{2}~\Bigg[1+\frac{m_{\rm{T}}+\Delta m_{\rm{T}}}{p_{2}}\Bigg]^{-n_{2}}~.

(4)

The energy loss
\Delta m_{\rm{T}}

p_{\rm{T}}
at low
p_{\rm{T}}

The functional form of
\Delta m_{\rm{T}}
has been taken from the

which is given as

\Delta m_{\rm{T}}=B~\Big(\frac{p_{\rm{T}}}{q_{0}}\Big)^{\alpha}~.

(5)

Here, the parameter

The parameter

Using Eq. (

4

gnoring 1 we get Eq. (
3b

applicable for high

4
Conclusion

In the article, we analyze the CMS measured data of the transverse momentum
(
p_{\rm{T}}
) spectra of the charged particles in pp collision at
\sqrt{s}
=
5.02 TeV using Tsallis distribution function. Tsallis distribution function
describes the
p_{\rm{T}}
spectra of the charged particles.
We also analyze the
p_{\rm{T}}
spectra of the charged particles for differnet
pseudo-rapidity range in pPb collisions at
\sqrt{s_{\rm{NN}}}
= 5.02 TeV.
After analyzing the measured data, we found that
p_{\rm{T}}
spectra in pPb
collisions show deviations from the Tsallis distribution function.
To solve this problem, we used the modified Tsallis distribution function which
incorporates the transverse flow in the low
p_{\rm{T}}
region and in-medium
energy loss in the high
p_{\rm{T}}
region.
The modified Tsallis distribution function excellently describe the
p_{\rm{T}}

spectra in pp and pPb colliisons with its parameters which are quantify the
in-medium effects in both above systems.
In the low
p_{\rm{T}}
region, the parameters
n_{1},p_{1}
and
\beta
are
remain same for different pseudo-rapidity ranges.
We found that in the high
p_{\rm{T}}
region, the parameter
\alpha
which
describes the energy loss of the partons as a function of their remains within
the range 0.60 to 0.65.
