Local equivalence of some maximally symmetric ( 2 , 3 , 5 ) (2,3,5) -distributions
Matthew Randall

Abstract
. Using a complex parametrisation of s ​ u ​ ( 2 ) su(2) , we show a change of coordinates that maps the maximally symmetric rolling ( 2 , 3 , 5 ) (2,3,5) -distribution to the flat Cartan distribution. This establishes the local equivalence between the maximally symmetric rolling model and the flat Cartan or Hilbert-Cartan distribution. For the maximally symmetric rolling distribution, we write down the vector fields that bracket-generate to give the split real form of the Lie algebra of 𝔤 2 \mathfrak{g}_{2} , with two of the vector fields in the bracket-generating set given by the span of the rolling distribution.

1.
Introduction

Let
\mathcal{D}
be a maximally non-integrable rank 2 distribution on a 5-manifold
M
. The maximally non-integrable condition of
\mathcal{D}
determines a filtration of the tangent bundle
TM
given by

\mathcal{D}\subset[\mathcal{D},\mathcal{D}]\subset[\mathcal{D},[\mathcal{D},\mathcal{D}]]\cong TM.

The distribution
[\mathcal{D},\mathcal{D}]
has rank 3 while the full tangent space
TM
has rank 5, hence such a geometry is also known as a
(2,3,5)
-distribution. Let
M_{xyzpq}
denote the 5-dimensional mixed order jet space
J^{2,0}(\mathbb{R},\mathbb{R}^{2})\cong J^{2}(\mathbb{R},\mathbb{R})\times\mathbb{R}
with local coordinates given by
(x,y,z,p,q)=(x,y,z,y^{\prime},y^{\prime\prime})
(see also
[
15
]
,
[
16
]
). Let
\mathcal{D}_{F(x,y,z,y^{\prime},y^{\prime\prime})}
denote the maximally non-integrable rank 2 distribution on
M_{xyzpq}
associated to the underdetermined differential equation
z^{\prime}=F(x,y,z,y^{\prime},y^{\prime\prime})
. This means that the distribution is annihilated by the following three 1-forms

\displaystyle\omega_{1}={\rm d}y-p{\rm d}x,\qquad\omega_{2}={\rm d}p-q{\rm d}x,\qquad\omega_{3}={\rm d}z-F(x,y,z,p,q){\rm d}x.

Such a distribution
\mathcal{D}_{F(x,y,z,y^{\prime},y^{\prime\prime})}
is said to be in Monge normal form (see page 90 of
[
15
]
).
When
F(x,y,z,p,q)=q^{2}
, we obtain the 1-forms of the Hilbert-Cartan distribution. In Section 5 of
[
9
]
, it is shown how to associate canonically to such a
(2,3,5)
-distribution a conformal class of metrics of split signature
(2,3)
(henceforth known as Nurowski’s conformal structure or Nurowski’s conformal metrics) such that the rank 2 distribution is isotropic with respect to any metric in the conformal class. The method of equivalence
[

\displaystyle{\rm d}\theta_{1}

\displaystyle=\theta_{1}\wedge(2\Omega_{1}+\Omega_{4})+\theta_{2}\wedge\Omega_{2}+\theta_{3}\wedge\theta_{4},

\displaystyle{\rm d}\theta_{2}

\displaystyle=\theta_{1}\wedge\Omega_{3}+\theta_{2}\wedge(\Omega_{1}+2\Omega_{4})+\theta_{3}\wedge\theta_{5},

(1.1)

\displaystyle{\rm d}\theta_{3}

\displaystyle=\theta_{1}\wedge\Omega_{5}+\theta_{2}\wedge\Omega_{6}+\theta_{3}\wedge(\Omega_{1}+\Omega_{4})+\theta_{4}\wedge\theta_{5},

\displaystyle{\rm d}\theta_{4}

\displaystyle=\theta_{1}\wedge\Omega_{7}+\frac{4}{3}\theta_{3}\wedge\Omega_{6}+\theta_{4}\wedge\Omega_{1}+\theta_{5}\wedge\Omega_{2},

\displaystyle{\rm d}\theta_{5}

where

\displaystyle g=2\theta_{1}\theta_{5}-2\theta_{2}\theta_{4}+\frac{4}{3}\theta_{3}\theta_{3}.

When
