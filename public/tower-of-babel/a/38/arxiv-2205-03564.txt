Cosmology in f ⁡ ( R , L m ) f(R,L_{m}) gravity
Lakhan V. Jaybhaye, Raja Solanki, Sanjay Mandal, P.K. Sahoo

Abstract
In this letter, we investigate the cosmic expansion scenario of the universe in the framework of f ⁡ ( R , L m ) f(R,L_{m}) gravity theory. We consider a non-linear f ⁡ ( R , L m ) f(R,L_{m}) model, specifically, f ⁡ ( R , L m ) = R 2 + L m n + β f(R,L_{m})=\frac{R}{2}+L_{m}^{n}+\beta , where n n and β \beta are free model parameters. Then we derive the motion equations for flat FLRW universe and obtain the exact solution of corresponding field equations. Then we estimate the best fit ranges of model parameters by using updated H ⁡ ( z ) H(z) datasets consisting of 57 points and the Pantheon datasets consisting of 1048 points. Further we investigate the physical behavior of density and the deceleration parameter. The evolution of deceleration parameter depicts a transition from deceleration to acceleration phases of the universe. Moreover, we analyze the stability of the solution of our cosmological model under the observational constraint by considering a linear perturbation. Lastlty, we investigate the behavior of Om diagnostic parameter and we observe that our model shows quintessence type behavior. We conclude that our f ⁡ ( R , L m ) f(R,L_{m}) cosmological model agrees with the recent observational studies and can efficiently describe the late time cosmic acceleration.

The following action governs the gravitational interactions in

S=\int{f(R,L_{m})\sqrt{-g}d^{4}x}

where

The Ricci scalar

R=g^{\mu\nu}R_{\mu\nu}

where the Ricci tensor is defined by

R_{\mu\nu}=\partial_{\lambda}\Gamma^{\lambda}_{\mu\nu}-\partial_{\mu}\Gamma^{\lambda}_{\lambda\nu}+\Gamma^{\lambda}_{\mu\nu}\Gamma^{\sigma}_{\sigma\lambda}-\Gamma^{\lambda}_{\nu\sigma}\Gamma^{\sigma}_{\mu\lambda}

(3)

Here
\Gamma^{\alpha}_{\beta\gamma}
represents the components of the well-known Levi-Civita connection defined by

\Gamma^{\alpha}_{\beta\gamma}=\frac{1}{2}g^{\alpha\lambda}\left(\frac{\partial g_{\gamma\lambda}}{\partial x^{\beta}}+\frac{\partial g_{\lambda\beta}}{\partial x^{\gamma}}-\frac{\partial g_{\beta\gamma}}{\partial x^{\lambda}}\right)

(4)

Now one can acquired the following field equation by varying the action (
1

f_{R}R_{\mu\nu}+(g_{\mu\nu}\square-\nabla_{\mu}\nabla_{\nu})f_{R}-\frac{1}{2}(f-f_{L_{m}}L_{m})g_{\mu\nu}=\frac{1}{2}f_{L_{m}}T_{\mu\nu}

(5)

Here
f_{R}\equiv\frac{\partial f}{\partial R}
,
f_{L_{m}}\equiv\frac{\partial f}{\partial L_{m}}
, and

represents the energy-momentum tensor for the perfect type fluid, defined by

T_{\mu\nu}=\frac{-2}{\sqrt{-g}}\frac{\delta(\sqrt{-g}L_{m})}{\delta g^{\mu\nu}}

(6)

The relation between the trace of energy-momentum tensor

, Ricci scalar

obtained by contracting the field equation (

Rf_{R}+3\square f_{R}-2(f-f_{L_{m}}L_{m})=\frac{1}{2}f_{L_{m}}T

(7)

Here
\square F=\frac{1}{\sqrt{-g}}\partial_{\alpha}(\sqrt{-g}g^{\alpha\beta}\partial_{\beta}F)
for any scalar function

Moreover, one can acquired the following result by taking covariant derivative in equation (

\nabla^{\mu}T_{\mu\nu}=2\nabla^{\mu}ln(f_{L_{m}})\frac{\partial L_{m}}{\partial g^{\mu\nu}}

ds^{2}=-dt^{2}+a^{2}(t)[dx^{2}+dy^{2}+dz^{2}]

Using equation (
