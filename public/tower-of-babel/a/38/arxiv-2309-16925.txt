Lexicographical ordering of hypergraphs via spectral moments
Hong Zhou, Changjiang Bu

Abstract
The lexicographical ordering of hypergraphs via spectral moments is called the S S -order of hypergraphs. In this paper, the S S -order of hypergraphs is investigated. We characterize the first and last hypergraphs in an S S -order of all uniform hypertrees and all linear unicyclic uniform hypergraphs with given girth, respectively. And we give the last hypergraph in an S S -order of all linear unicyclic uniform hypergraphs.

Transformation 1
: Let

Lemma 2.2
.

Let
\mathcal{H}^{\prime}
be obtained from
\mathcal{H}
by transformation 1. Then

Proof.

By the definition of the Zagreb index, we have

\displaystyle M(\mathcal{H}^{\prime})-M(\mathcal{H})

\displaystyle=d^{2}_{\mathcal{H}^{\prime}}(v)-d^{2}_{\mathcal{H}}(v)+d^{2}_{\mathcal{H}^{\prime}}(u)-d^{2}_{\mathcal{H}}(u)

\displaystyle=(d_{\mathcal{H}}(v)+t)^{2}-d_{\mathcal{H}}^{2}(v)+1-(t+1)^{2}

\displaystyle=2t(d_{\mathcal{H}}(v)-1)>0.

Transformation 2
