LTH 1089, DESY 16-110, Nikhef 2016-030 June 2016 Non-singlet coefficient functions for charged-current deep-inelastic scattering to the third order in QCD
A. Vogt

Abstract
We have calculated the coefficient functions for the structure functions F 2 {F_{\,2}} , F L F_{L} and F 3 {F_{\,3}} in ν − ν ¯ \nu\!-\!{\bar{\nu}} charged-current deep-inelastic scattering (DIS) at the third order in the strong coupling α s \alpha_{\rm s}\>\! , thus completing the description of unpolarized inclusive W ± W^{\pm} -exchange DIS to this order of massless perturbative QCD. In this brief note, our new results are presented in terms of compact approximate expressions that are sufficiently accurate for phenomenological analyses. For the benefit of such analyses we also collect, in a unified notation, the corresponding lower-order contributions and the flavour non-singlet coefficient functions for ν + ν ¯ \nu\!+\!\bar{\nu} charged-current DIS. The behaviour of all six third-order coefficient functions at small Bjorken- x x is briefly discussed.

With the exception of the

at

are very small. Towards smaller

are still accurate to about 1% and 3% at
x=10^{\,-8}
and

respectively.
