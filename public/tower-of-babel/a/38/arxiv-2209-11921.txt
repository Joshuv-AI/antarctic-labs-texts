The extended quasi-Einstein manifolds with generalised Ricci solitons
Zhiming Huang, Weijun Lu, Fuhong Su

Abstract
. As a generalization of Einstein manifolds, the nearly quasi-Einstein manifolds and pseudo quasi-Einstein manifolds are both interesting and useful in studying the general relativity. In this paper, we study the extended quasi-Einstein manifolds which derive from pseudo quasi-Einstein manifolds. After showing the existence theorem of extended quasi-Einstein manifold, we give some special geometric properties of such manifolds. At the same time, we also discuss the extended quasi-Einstein manifolds with certain soliton like generalised Ricci soliton or Riemann soliton. Furthermore, we construct some nontrivial example to illustrate these extended quasi-Einstein manifolds.

2.1.
Existence theorem

We state and prove the existence theorem of the extended quasi-Einstein manifold.

Theorem 2.2
.

If the Ricci tensor of a non-flat Riemannian manifold is non-vanishing and satisfies the relation

\displaystyle\text{Ric}(X,W)g(Y,Z)

\displaystyle+\text{Ric}(Y,Z)g(X,W)={{a}_{1}}[g(X,W)g(Y,Z)+g(Y,W)g(X,Z)]

(2.4)

\displaystyle+{{a}_{2}}[\text{Ric}(X,Z)\text{Ric}^{2}(Y,W)+\text{Ric}(Y,W)\text{Ric}^{2}(X,Z)],

Proof.

Let

in (

\displaystyle\text{Ric}(Y,Z)g(U,U)

\displaystyle+\text{Ric}(U,U)g(Y,Z)={{a}_{1}}[g(U,U)g(Y,Z)+g(Y,U)g(Z,U)]

(2.5)

\displaystyle+{{a}_{2}}[\text{Ric}(U,Z)\text{Ric}^{2}(Y,U)+\text{Ric}(Y,U)\text{Ric}^{2}(X,U)].

From above equation (
2.5
),

\displaystyle g(U,U)\text{Ric}(Y,Z)

\displaystyle=[{{a}_{1}}g(U,U)-\text{Ric}(U,U)]g(Y,Z)+{{a}_{1}}\omega(Y)\omega(Z)

(2.6)

\displaystyle+{{a}_{2}}[\omega(QZ)\omega({Q}^{2}Y)+\omega(QY)\omega({Q}^{2}Z)],

where

,
\omega(QY)=\text{Ric}(Y,U)
and
\omega({Q}^{2}Y)=\text{Ric}^{2}(Y,U)
.

Since

\displaystyle\text{Ric}(Y,Z)

\displaystyle=[{{a}_{1}}-\frac{\text{Ric}(U,U)}{g(U,U)}]g(Y,Z)+\frac{{a}_{1}}{g(U,U)}\omega(Y)\omega(Z)

(2.7)

\displaystyle+\frac{{a}_{2}}{g(U,U)}[\omega(QZ)\omega({Q}^{2}Y)+\omega(QY)\omega({Q}^{2}Z)].

Taking

\displaystyle=ag(Y,Z)+b\omega(Y)\omega(Z)+c[{{\omega}_{1}}(Y){{\omega}_{2}}(Z)+{{\omega}_{2}}(Y){{\omega}_{1}}(Z)],

∎

3.
Two solitons structures

In 2014, Nurowski and Randall introduced the concept of the generalised Ricci soliton equations
[
14
]
. These equations depend on three real parameters. Siddiqi MD study generalised Ricci solitons on trans sasakian manifolds
[
13
]
. As a generalied of Ricci soliton, Hiricu and Udriste introduced and studied Riemann soliton
[
16
]
. Riemann solitons are generalized fixed points of the Riemann flow. In 2020, Venkatesha study Riemann solitons and almost Rieman solitons on almost Kenmotsu manifolds
[
15
]
.

A generalised Ricci soliton is a (pseudo)-Riemannian manifold
({{M}^{n}},g)
admitting a smooth vector field
X
, such that

{{\mathcal{L}}_{X}}g+2{{c}_{1}}{{X}^{b}}\odot{{X}^{b}}=2{{c}_{2}}\text{Ric}+2\lambda g,

(3.1)

for arbitrary real constant
{c}_{1}
,
{c}_{2}
and
\lambda
. Here
{{\mathcal{L}}_{X}}g
is the Lie derivative of the metric
g
with respect to
X
,
{{X}^{b}}
is a non-zero
1
-form such that
{{X}^{b}}(Y)=g(X,Y)
,
Ric
is the Ricci tensor of
g
. We call (
3.1
) the generalised Ricci soliton equations. A pair
(g,X)
is called a generalised Ricci soliton if (
3.1
) is satisfied.

A Riemann soliton are defined by a smooth vector field
V
and a real constant
\lambda
which satisfy the following equation

R+\frac{1}{2}{{\mathcal{L}}_{V}}g\wedge g=\frac{\lambda}{2}g\wedge g,

(3.2)

where
{{\mathcal{L}}_{V}}g
denotes the Lie derivative of
g
and
\wedge
is the Kulkarni-Nomizu product.

A vector field
\varphi
on a Riemannian manifold
({{M}^{n}},g)
is said to be a
\varphi(\text{Ric})
-vector field
[
19
]
if it satisfies

{{\nabla}_{X}}\varphi=\mu QX,

(3.3)

where
\mu
is a constant and
Q
is the ricci operator defined by
\text{Ric}(X,Y)=g(QX,Y)
. If
\mu\neq 0
, then the vector field
\varphi
is called a proper
\varphi(\text{Ric})
-vector field.

Next, we consider an extended quasi-Einstein manifold with a generalised Ricci soliton and Riemannian soliton, respectively.
