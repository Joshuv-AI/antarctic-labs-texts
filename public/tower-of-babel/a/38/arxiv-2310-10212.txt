\mathbb{P}^{n}
the
n

K

R_{n}=K[X_{0},\ldots,X_{n}]

X_{0},\ldots,X_{n}

\mathbb{P}^{n}

be positive integers. Denote by
\wp_{i}

. We call the zero-scheme defined by the ideal
\wp^{m_{1}}_{1}\cap\cdots\cap\wp^{m_{s}}_{s}
to be a set of fat points in
\mathbb{P}^{n}
(we also call it to be fat points in
\mathbb{P}^{n}
), and denote it by

Z=m_{1}P_{1}+\cdots+m_{s}P_{s}.

Put
I_{Z}=\wp^{m_{1}}_{1}\cap\cdots\cap\wp^{m_{s}}_{s}
, is the defining ideal of
Z
. The ring
R_{n}/I_{Z}
is the homogeneous coordinate ring of
Z
. It is graded,
R_{n}/I_{Z}=\underset{t\geq 0}{\oplus}(R_{n}/I_{Z})_{t}
, and it has the multiplicity to be

e(R_{n}/I_{Z})=\underset{i=1}{\overset{s}{\sum}}\binom{m_{i}+n-1}{n}

H_{R_{n}/I_{Z}}(t)=\dim_{K}(R_{n}/I_{Z})_{t}

is called the Hilbert function of

. This function strictly increases until it reaches the multiplicity
e(R_{n}/I_{Z})
, at which it stabilizes. The number

\operatorname{reg}(R_{n}/I_{Z})=\min\{t\in\mathbb{N}\ |\ H_{R_{n}/I_{Z}}(t)=e(R_{n}/I_{Z})\}

is called the regularity index of the coordinate ring
R_{n}/I_{Z}
(we call also it to be the regularity index of
Z
, and denote it by
\operatorname{reg}(Z)
).

Let
m
be an integer,
m>n
, and let

\displaystyle\phi_{n,m}:\mathbb{P}^{n}

\displaystyle\to\mathbb{P}^{m}

\displaystyle P=(a_{1},\ldots,a_{n})

\displaystyle\mapsto\phi_{n,m}(P)=(a_{1},\ldots,a_{n},0,\ldots,0)

\phi_{n,m}(P_{i})

. Put

to be the fat points defined by
\mathfrak{q}_{1}^{m_{1}}\cap\cdots\cap\mathfrak{q}_{s}^{m_{s}}
.
The coordinate ring of
\phi_{n,m}(Z)

R_{m}/I_{\phi_{n,m}(Z)}

R_{m}/I_{\phi_{n,m}(Z)}=\underset{t\geq 0}{\oplus}(R_{m}/I_{\phi_{n,m}(Z)})_{t}

R_{m}/I_{\phi_{n,m}(Z)}

\phi_{n,m}(Z)

This function strictly increases until it reaches the multiplicity

Lemma 3.1
.

Let
\phi_{n,n+1}:\mathbb{P}^{n}\to\mathbb{P}^{n+1}
be the embedding,
P\in\mathbb{P}^{n}
and
r
be a positive integer. We have

i) If

, then

.

\wp^{r}=\mathfrak{q}^{r}\cap R_{n}.

Proof.

Let

. Then there exist linear forms
l_{0},\ldots,l_{n}\in R_{n}=K[X_{0},\ldots,X_{n}]
such that

.
Since
\phi_{n,n+1}(P)=(a_{0},\ldots,a_{n},0)\in\mathbb{P}^{n+1}
, we have
\mathfrak{q}=(l_{0},\ldots,l_{n},X_{n+1})
.

i) We argue by induction on
r
. If
f\in\mathfrak{q}=(l_{0},\ldots,l_{n},X_{n+1})
, then
f
can be written

f=\sum_{i=0}^{n}l_{i}f_{i}+X_{n+1}f_{n+1}

,

. We have

\displaystyle f(X_{0},\ldots,X_{n},0)

\displaystyle=\sum_{i=0}^{n}l_{i}(X_{0},\ldots,X_{n})f_{i}(x_{0},\ldots,X_{n},0)+0f_{n+1}(X_{0},\ldots,X_{n},0)

\displaystyle\in(l_{0},\ldots,l_{n})=\wp.

So, i) is true for

. Now we assume that i) is true for

. If

, then

with some

and

,

. We have

by the above result, and

by inductive assumption. Therefore,

\wp=(l_{0},\ldots,l_{n})
and

with linear forms

\wp^{r}\subset\mathfrak{q}^{r}\cap R_{n}.

For any

\displaystyle g

\displaystyle=g(X_{0},\ldots,X_{n},0)

\displaystyle=\sum_{i=1}^{d}h_{i}(X_{0},\ldots,X_{n},0)k_{i}(X_{0},\ldots,X_{n},0)\in\wp\wp^{r-1}=\wp^{r}.

.

∎

Proposition 3.5
.

Let
m>n
be positive integers and
Z=m_{1}P_{1}+\cdots+m_{s}P_{s}
be fat points in
\mathbb{P}^{n}
. Let
\phi_{n,n+1}:\mathbb{P}^{n}\to\mathbb{P}^{n+1}
be the embedding. Put

. Then

\operatorname{reg}(R_{n}/(I_{U}+\wp_{s}^{m_{s}}))=\operatorname{reg}(R_{n+1}/(I_{\phi_{n,n+1}(U)}+\mathfrak{q}_{s}^{m_{s}})).

Proof.

We have
I_{U}=\wp_{1}^{m_{1}}\cap\cdots\cap\wp_{s-1}^{m_{s-1}}

t
such that

\operatorname{reg}(R_{n+1}/(I_{\phi_{n,n+1}(U)}+\mathfrak{q}_{s}^{m_{s}}))>t,

then

\operatorname{reg}(R_{n}/(I_{U}+\wp_{s}^{m_{s}}))>t.

If

\operatorname{reg}(R_{n+1}/(I_{\phi_{n,n+1}(U)}+\mathfrak{q}_{s}^{m_{s}}))>t,

then by Lemma
2.2
there exists a monomial
M=X_{1}^{c_{1}}\cdots X_{n+1}^{c_{n+1}}
,

,

, such that

X_{0}^{t-i}M\notin I_{\phi_{n,n+1}(U)}+\mathfrak{q}_{s}^{i+1}.\qquad(1)

By Lemma
3.1
, ii) we get
\wp_{s}^{i+1}=\mathfrak{q}_{s}^{i+1}\cap R_{n}
. By Corollary
3.2
, ii) we get

I_{U}=I_{\phi_{n,n+1}(U)}\cap R_{n}
. Therefore,

Consider monomial

. If

, then

X_{n+1}^{c_{n+1}}X_{0}^{t-i}N\in I_{U}+\wp_{s}^{i+1}.\qquad\qquad(3)

From
(2)
and

we get

X_{0}^{t-i}M=X_{n+1}^{c_{n+1}}X_{0}^{t-i}N\in I_{\phi_{n,n+1}(U)}+\mathfrak{q}_{s}^{i+1},

which is contradiction with
(1)
. So,

X_{0}^{t-i}N\notin I_{U}+\wp_{s}^{i+1}.

Then by Lemma
2.2
we get

\operatorname{reg}(R/(J_{\alpha}+\wp_{s\alpha}^{m_{s}}))>t.

2. Prove
\operatorname{reg}(R_{n}/(I_{U}+\wp_{s}^{m_{s}}))\leq\operatorname{reg}(R_{n+1}/(I_{\phi_{n,n+1}(U)}+\mathfrak{q}_{s}^{m_{s}}))
: We prove that if there is a non-negative integer
t
such that

\operatorname{reg}(R_{n}/(I_{U}+\wp_{s}^{m_{s}}))>t,

then

\operatorname{reg}(R_{n+1}/(I_{\phi_{n,n+1}(U)}+\mathfrak{q}_{s}^{m_{s}}))>t.

If
\operatorname{reg}(R_{n}/(I_{U}+\wp_{s}^{m_{s}}))>t
, then by Lemma
2.2
there exists a monomial
M=X_{1}^{c_{1}}\cdots X_{n}^{c_{n}}
,

,
i\in\{0,\ldots,m_{s}-1\}
, such that

X_{0}^{t-i}M\notin I_{U}+\wp_{s}^{i+1}.\qquad(4)

If

3.2
, i). Since

\phi_{n,n+1}:\mathbb{P}^{n}\to\mathbb{P}^{n+1}

\operatorname{reg}(R_{n}/I_{Z})=\operatorname{reg}(R_{n+1}/I_{\phi_{n,n+1}(Z)}).

Let

I_{\phi_{n,n+1}(U)}=\mathfrak{q}_{1}^{m_{1}}\cap\cdots\cap\mathfrak{q}_{s-1}^{m_{s-1}}
and
I_{\phi_{n,n+1}(Z)}=I_{\phi_{n,n+1}(U)}\cap\mathfrak{q}_{s}^{m_{s}}
. By Lemma
2.1
we get

\operatorname{reg}(R_{n}/I_{Z})=\max\left\{m_{s}-1,\operatorname{reg}(R_{n}/I_{U}),\operatorname{reg}(R_{n}/(I_{U}+\wp_{s}^{m_{s}}))\right\}

and

\operatorname{reg}(R_{n+1}/I_{\phi_{n,n+1}(Z)})=\max\left\{m_{s}-1,\operatorname{reg}(R_{n+1}/I_{\phi_{n,n+1}(U)}),\operatorname{reg}(R_{n+1}/(I_{\phi_{n,n+1}(U)}+\mathfrak{q}_{s}^{m_{s}}))\right\}.

By Lemma
2.3
we have
\operatorname{reg}(R_{n}/I_{Z})\geq m_{1}+m_{2}-1
and
\operatorname{reg}(R_{n+1}/I_{\phi_{n,n+1}(Z)})\geq m_{1}+m_{2}-1
. So,

\operatorname{reg}(R_{n}/I_{Z})=\max\left\{\operatorname{reg}(R_{n}/I_{U}),\operatorname{reg}(R_{n}/(I_{U}+\wp_{s}^{m_{s}}))\right\}

and

\operatorname{reg}(R_{n+1}/I_{\phi_{n,n+1}(Z)})=\max\left\{\operatorname{reg}(R_{n+1}/I_{\phi_{n,n+1}(U)}),\operatorname{reg}(R_{n+1}/(I_{\phi_{n,n+1}(U)}+\mathfrak{q}_{s}^{m_{s}}))\right\}.

By inductive assumption we have

\operatorname{reg}(R_{n}/I_{U})=\operatorname{reg}(R_{n+1}/I_{\phi_{n,n+1}(U)}).

By Proposition
3.5
we have

\operatorname{reg}(R_{n}/(I_{U}+\wp_{s}^{m_{s}}))=\operatorname{reg}(R_{n+1}/(I_{\phi_{n,n+1}(U)}+\mathfrak{q}_{s}^{m_{s}})).

Therefore,

\operatorname{reg}(R_{n}/I_{Z})=\operatorname{reg}(R_{n+1}/I_{\phi_{n,n+1}(Z)}).

∎

From the result of Benedetti et al. in
[
6
, Lemma 4.4]
we can deduce

\operatorname{reg}(R_{n}/I_{Z})\geq\operatorname{reg}(R_{n+1}/I_{\phi_{n,n+1}(Z)}),

but it is difficult by using the method in
[
6
]
to show the equality.

Theorem 3.7
.

Let
m>n
be positive integers,
Z=m_{1}P_{1}+\cdots+m_{s}P_{s}
be fat points in
\mathbb{P}^{n}
and
\phi_{n,m}:\mathbb{P}^{n}\to\mathbb{P}^{m}
be the embedding. Then

\operatorname{reg}(R_{n}/I_{Z})=\operatorname{reg}(R_{m}/I_{\phi_{n,m}(Z)}).

Proof.

Consider
m-n
the embedding maps

\phi_{i,i+1}:\mathbb{P}^{i}\to\mathbb{P}^{i+1},

i=n,\ldots,m-1
. By Proposition
3.6
we have

\displaystyle\operatorname{reg}(R_{n}/I_{Z})

\displaystyle=\operatorname{reg}(R_{n+1}/I_{\phi_{n,n+1}(Z)}),

\displaystyle\operatorname{reg}(R_{n+1}/I_{\phi_{n,n+1}(Z)})

\displaystyle=\operatorname{reg}(R_{n+2}/I_{\phi_{n+1,n+2}\circ\phi_{n,n+1}(Z)}),

\displaystyle\cdots

\displaystyle\cdots\cdots

\displaystyle\operatorname{reg}(R_{i+1}/I_{\phi_{i,i+1}\circ\cdots\circ\phi_{n,n+1}(Z)})

\displaystyle=\operatorname{reg}(R_{i+2}/I_{\phi_{i+1,i+2}\circ\cdots\circ\phi_{n,n+1}(Z)}),

\displaystyle\cdots

\displaystyle\cdots\cdots

\displaystyle\operatorname{reg}(R_{m-1}/I_{\phi_{m-2,m-1}\circ\cdots\circ\phi_{n,n+1}(Z)})

\displaystyle=\operatorname{reg}(R_{m}/I_{\phi_{m-1,m}\circ\cdots\circ\phi_{n,n+1}(Z)}).

These follow that

\operatorname{reg}(R_{n}/I_{Z})=\operatorname{reg}(R_{m}/I_{\phi_{m-1,m}\circ\cdots\circ\phi_{n,n+1}(Z)}).

But
\phi_{m-1,m}\circ\cdots\circ\phi_{n,n+1}=\phi_{n,m}
. Thus, we get

\operatorname{reg}(R_{n}/I_{Z})=\operatorname{reg}(R_{m}/I_{\phi_{n,m}(Z)}).

∎

The configuration of fat points

\mathbb{P}^{m}

. Use the above theorem we can know the regularity index of the coordinate ring of

when we know the regularity index of the coordinate ring of

.

4
Is the Hilbert function invariant under embedding?

Let
m>n
be positive integers,
Z=m_{1}P_{1}+\cdots+m_{s}P_{s}
be fat points in
\mathbb{P}^{n}

and
\phi_{n,m}:\mathbb{P}^{n}\to\mathbb{P}^{m}
be the embedding. What is the relation between
H_{R_{m}/I_{\phi_{n,m}(Z)}}(t)
and
H_{R_{n}/I_{Z}}(t)
?
So far, we know only that

H_{R_{n}/I_{Z}}(t)=\binom{t+n}{n}-\dim_{K}(I_{Z})_{t}

and it strictly increases until it reaches the multiplicity
e(R_{n}/I_{Z})=\underset{i=1}{\overset{s}{\sum}}\binom{m_{i}+n-1}{n}
, at which it stabilizes. Similarly,

H_{R_{m}/I_{\phi_{n,m}(Z)}}(t)=\binom{t+m}{m}-\dim_{K}(I_{\phi_{n,m}(Z)})_{t}

and it strictly increases until it reaches the multiplicity
e(R_{m}/I_{\phi_{n,m}(Z)})=\underset{i=1}{\overset{s}{\sum}}\binom{m_{i}+m-1}{m}
, at which it stabilizes. Since
m>n
, we have

\underset{i=1}{\overset{s}{\sum}}\binom{m_{i}+m-1}{m}\geq\underset{i=1}{\overset{s}{\sum}}\binom{m_{i}+n-1}{n},

the equality occurs if and only if
m_{1}=\cdots=m_{s}=1
.
By Theorem
3.7
we have

\operatorname{reg}(R_{n}/I_{Z})=\operatorname{reg}(R_{m}/I_{\phi_{n,m}(Z)})
. So if
t\geq\operatorname{reg}(R_{n}/I_{Z})
, then

H_{R_{m}/I_{\phi_{n,m}(Z)}}(t)=\underset{i=1}{\overset{s}{\sum}}\binom{m_{i}+m-1}{m}

and

H_{R_{n}/I_{Z}}(t)=\underset{i=1}{\overset{s}{\sum}}\binom{m_{i}+n-1}{n}.

Thus, we get the following result.

Proposition 4.1
.

Let
m>n
be positive integers,
Z=m_{1}P_{1}+\cdots+m_{s}P_{s}
be fat points in
\mathbb{P}^{n}

and
\phi_{n,m}:\mathbb{P}^{n}\to\mathbb{P}^{m}
be the embedding. If

, then

H_{R_{m}/I_{\phi_{n,m}(Z)}}(t)=\underset{i=1}{\overset{s}{\sum}}\binom{m_{i}+m-1}{m}\geq H_{R_{n}/I_{Z}}(t)=\underset{i=1}{\overset{s}{\sum}}\binom{m_{i}+n-1}{n}.

The equality occurs if and only if
m_{1}=\cdots=m_{s}=1
.

What is about the relation between
H_{R_{m}/I_{\phi_{n,m}(Z)}}(t)
and
H_{R_{n}/I_{Z}}(t)
when
t<\operatorname{reg}(R_{n}/I_{Z})
?

From now on let
\wp^{r}=R_{n}
if

is a non-positive integer and
\wp
is the defining ideal of a point
P\in\mathbb{P}^{n}
.

Lemma 4.2
.

Let
Z=m_{1}P_{1}+\cdots+m_{s}P_{s}
be a set of fat points in
\mathbb{P}^{n}
and
\phi_{n,m}:\mathbb{P}^{n}\to\mathbb{P}^{m}
be the embedding. If
0\leq t<\operatorname{reg}(Z)
and
f\in[I_{\phi_{n,m}(Z)}]_{t}
, then
f
can be written in the form

f=_{t}f+_{t-1}fh_{1}+\cdots+_{1}fh_{t-1}+h_{t},

where
{}_{i}f\in[\wp_{1}^{m_{1}+i-t}\cap\cdots\cap\wp_{s}^{m_{s}+i-t}]_{i}
,
h_{i}\in[K[X_{n+1},\ldots,X_{m}]]_{i}
,
i=1,\ldots,t
.

Proof.

If
f\in[I_{\phi_{n,n+1}(Z)}]_{t}
, then
f\in(K[X_{0},\ldots,X_{n}])[X_{n+1},\ldots,X_{m}]
. So
f
can be written in the form

f=_{t}f+_{t-1}fh_{1}+\cdots+_{i}fh_{t-i}+_{i-1}fh_{t-i-1}+\cdots+_{1}fh_{t-1}+h_{t},\qquad(5)

where
{}_{i}f\in[R_{n}]_{i}=[K[X_{0},\ldots,X_{n}]]_{i}
,
h_{i}\in[K[X_{n+1},\ldots,X_{m}]]_{i}
,
i=1,\ldots,t
. We prove that
{}_{i}f\in[\wp_{1}^{m_{1}-t+i}\cap\cdots\cap\wp_{s}^{m_{s}-t+i}]_{i}
,
i=1,\ldots,t
.

For
i=1,\ldots,t
, since
h_{i}\in[K[X_{n+1},\ldots,X_{m}]]_{i}
, we get
h_{i}(0,\ldots,0)=0
. Thus,

\displaystyle f(X_{0},\ldots,X_{n},0,\ldots,0)

\displaystyle=_{t}f(X_{0},\ldots,X_{n})+_{t-1}f(X_{0},\ldots,X_{n})h_{1}(0,\ldots,0)+\cdots+h_{t}(0,\ldots,0)

\displaystyle=_{t}f(X_{0},\ldots,X_{n})+_{t-1}f(X_{0},\ldots,X_{n})0+\cdots+0

\displaystyle=_{t}f(X_{0},\ldots,X_{n}).

Since
f\in I_{\phi_{n,m}(Z)}
, by Corollary
3.4
we get
f(X_{0},\ldots,X_{n},0,\ldots,0)\in I_{Z}
. Thus,

{}_{t}f=_{t}f(X_{0},\ldots,X_{n})=f(X_{0},\ldots,X_{n},0,\ldots,0)\in I_{Z}.

Note that
\deg(_{t}f)=t
, so we get
{}_{t}f\in[I_{Z}]_{t}
.

For
i=t-1,\ldots,1
, if
{}_{i}fh_{t-i}\neq 0
then
h_{t-i}
has a non-zero term, say
aX_{n+1}^{c_{1}}\cdots X_{m}^{c_{m}}
. Take the derivative to order
t-i
with respect to the variables
X_{n+1},\ldots,X_{m}
of
{}_{t}f
and
{}_{j}fh_{t-j}
,
j=t-1,\ldots,i+1
, we get:

\frac{\partial^{t-i}(_{t}f)}{\partial^{{c_{1}}}X_{n+1}\cdots\partial^{{c_{m}}}X_{m}}=0,\frac{\partial^{t-i}(_{j}fh_{t-j})}{\partial^{{c_{1}}}X_{n+1}\cdots\partial^{{c_{m}}}X_{m}}=0.

Thus, take the derivative to order
t-i
with respect to the variables
X_{n+1},\ldots,X_{m}
on both sides of (5) we get:

\frac{\partial^{t-i}f}{\partial^{{c_{1}}}X_{n+1}\cdots\partial^{{c_{m}}}X_{m}}=(_{i}f)c_{1}!\cdots c_{m}!+(_{i-1}f)\left(\frac{\partial^{t-i}h_{t-i+1}}{\partial^{{c_{1}}}X_{n+1}\cdots\partial^{{c_{m}}}X_{m}}\right)+\cdots+\frac{\partial^{t-i}h_{t}}{\partial^{{c_{1}}}X_{n+1}\cdots\partial^{{c_{m}}}X_{m}}.

For
j=t-i+1,\ldots,t
; if
\frac{\partial^{t-i}h_{j}}{\partial^{{c_{1}}}X_{n+1}\cdots\partial^{{c_{m}}}X_{m}}\neq 0
, then
\frac{\partial^{t-i}h_{j}}{\partial^{{c_{1}}}X_{n+1}\cdots\partial^{{c_{m}}}X_{m}}\in[K[X_{n+1},\ldots,X_{m}]]_{j-t+i}
with
j-t+i\geq 1
. Therefore,

\frac{\partial^{t-i}f}{\partial^{{c_{1}}}X_{n+1}\cdots\partial^{{c_{m}}}X_{m}}(X_{0},\ldots,X_{n},0,\ldots,0)=c_{1}!\cdots c_{m}!_{i}f(X_{0},\ldots,X_{n})=c_{1}!\cdots c_{m}!_{i}f.\qquad(6)

We now prove that
\frac{\partial^{t-i}f}{\partial^{{c_{1}}}X_{n+1}\cdots\partial^{{c_{m}}}X_{m}}(X_{0},\ldots,X_{n},0,\ldots,0)\in\wp_{1}^{m_{1}-t+i}\cap\cdots\cap\wp_{s}^{m_{s}-t+i}.

It is well known that the set of all homogeneous polynomials in
R_{m}
that vanish at
\phi_{n,m}(P_{i})
to order
m_{i}
; for
i=1,\ldots,s
; is the ideal
I_{\phi_{n,m}(Z)}
.
Since
f\in I_{\phi_{n,m}(Z)}
, we have all derivatives of
f
of order
\leq m_{j}-1
vanish at
\phi_{n,m}(P_{j})
,
j=1,\ldots,s
. This implies that all derivatives of
\frac{\partial^{t-i}f}{\partial^{{c_{1}}}X_{n+1}\cdots\partial^{{c_{m}}}X_{m}}
of order
\leq m_{j}-1-t+i
vanish at
\phi_{n,m}(P_{j})
,
j=1,\ldots,s
. So

\frac{\partial^{t-i}f}{\partial^{{c_{1}}}X_{n+1}\cdots\partial^{{c_{m}}}X_{m}}\in\mathfrak{q}_{1}^{m_{1}-t+i}\cap\cdots\cap\mathfrak{q}_{s}^{m_{s}-t+i}.

By Corollary
3.4
we get

\frac{\partial^{t-i}f}{\partial^{{c_{1}}}X_{n+1}\cdots\partial^{{c_{m}}}X_{m}}(X_{0},\ldots,X_{n},0,\ldots,0)\in\wp_{1}^{m_{1}-t+i}\cap\cdots\cap\wp_{s}^{m_{s}-t+i}.\qquad(7)

From
(6)
and (7) we get

{}_{i}f\in\wp_{1}^{m_{1}-t+i}\cap\cdots\cap\wp_{s}^{m_{s}-t+i}.

Note that
\deg(_{i}f)=i
, so
{}_{i}f\in[\wp_{1}^{m_{1}-t+i}\cap\cdots\cap\wp_{s}^{m_{s}-t+i}]_{i}
.
∎

Lemma 4.3
.

Let
Z=m_{1}P_{1}+\cdots+m_{s}P_{s}
be fat points in
\mathbb{P}^{n}
and
\phi_{n,m}:\mathbb{P}^{n}\to\mathbb{P}^{m}
be the embedding. If
0\leq i\leq t<\operatorname{reg}(Z)
,
g\in[\wp_{1}^{m_{1}+i-t}\cap\cdots\cap\wp_{s}^{m_{s}+i-t}]_{i}
and
h_{t-i}\in[R[X_{n+1},\ldots,X_{m}]]_{t-i}
, then

gh_{t-i}\in[I_{\phi_{n,m}(Z)}]_{t}.

Proof.

First, we prove that for
j=1,\ldots,s
, if
f\in[\wp_{j}^{m_{j}+i-t}]_{i}
then

fh_{t-i}\in[\mathfrak{q}_{j}^{m_{j}}]_{t}.

In fact, if
t-i\geq m_{j}
, then
h_{t-i}\in[R[X_{n+1},\ldots,X_{m}]]_{t-i}\subset\mathfrak{q}_{j}^{m_{j}}
. It implies
fh_{t-i}\in\mathfrak{q}_{j}^{m_{j}}
. Note that
\deg(f)=i
and
\deg(h_{t-i})=t-i
, therefore
fh_{t-i}\in[\mathfrak{q}_{j}^{m_{j}}]_{t}
. If
t-i<m_{j}
, then
f\in[\wp_{j}^{m_{j}+i-t}]_{i}\subset\mathfrak{q}_{j}^{m_{j}+i-t}
and
h_{t-i}\in[R[X_{n+1},\ldots,X_{m}]]_{t-i}\subset\mathfrak{q}_{j}^{t-i}
. So,
fh_{t-i}\in[\mathfrak{q}^{m_{j}}]_{t}
.

Now if
g\in[\wp_{1}^{m_{1}+i-t}\cap\cdots\cap\wp_{s}^{m_{s}+i-t}]_{i}
, then
g\in[\wp_{j}^{m_{j}+i-t}]_{i}
for
j=1,\ldots,s
. By using the above result we get
gh_{t-i}\in[\mathfrak{q}_{j}^{m_{j}}]_{t}
for
j=1,\ldots,s
. Therefore,

gh_{t-i}\in[\mathfrak{q}_{1}^{m_{1}}]_{t}\cap\cdots\cap[\mathfrak{q}_{s}^{m_{s}}]_{t}=[I_{\phi_{n,m}(Z)}]_{t}.

∎

Proposition 4.4
.

Let
Z=m_{1}P_{1}+\cdots+m_{s}P_{s}
be fat points in
\mathbb{P}^{n}
and
\phi_{n,m}:\mathbb{P}^{n}\to\mathbb{P}^{m}
be the embedding. If
0\leq t<\operatorname{reg}(R_{n}/I_{Z})
, then

\dim_{K}[I_{\phi_{n,m}(Z)}]_{t}=\dim_{K}[I_{Z}]_{t}+\underset{i=0}{\overset{t-1}{\sum}}\binom{m-n+t-i}{t-i}\dim_{K}[\wp_{1}^{m_{1}-t+i}\cap\cdots\cap\wp_{s}^{m_{s}-t+i}]_{i}.

Proof.

For
i=0,1,\ldots,t
; let
r_{i}=\dim_{K}[\wp_{1}^{m_{1}-t+i}\cap\cdots\cap\wp_{s}^{m_{s}-t+i}]_{i}
and
\{_{i}g_{1},\ldots,\ _{i}g_{r_{i}}\}
be a basis of
K
-vector space
[\wp_{1}^{m_{1}-t+i}\cap\cdots\cap\wp_{s}^{m_{s}-t+i}]_{i}
, put
d_{t-i}=\dim_{K}K[X_{n+1},\ldots,X_{m}]_{t-i}
and let
\{_{t-i}k_{1},\ldots,_{t-i}k_{d_{t-i}}\}
be a basis of
K
-vector space
[K[X_{n+1},\ldots,X_{m}]]_{t-i}
.Then

\{(_{i}g_{j})(_{t-i}k_{l})|i=0,\ldots,t;j=1,\ldots,r_{i};l=1,\ldots,d_{t-i}\}\text{ is linear independent in }[R_{m}]_{t}\qquad(8).

By Lemma
4.2
, each polynomial
f\in[I_{\phi_{n,m}(Z)}]_{t}
can be written in the form

f=_{t}f+_{t-1}fh_{1}+\cdots+_{1}fh_{t-1}+h_{t}=\sum_{i=0}^{t}(_{i}f)(h_{t-i}),

where
{}_{i}f\in[\wp_{1}^{m_{1}+i-t}\cap\cdots\cap\wp_{s}^{m_{s}+i-t}]_{i}
,
h_{i}\in[K[X_{n+1},\ldots,X_{m}]]_{i}
;
i=1,\ldots,t
;
{}_{0}f=h_{0}=1
.
Since
\{_{i}g_{1},\ldots,\ _{i}g_{r_{i}}\}
is a basis of
K
-vector space
[\wp_{1}^{m_{1}-t+i}\cap\cdots\cap\wp_{s}^{m_{s}-t+i}]_{i}
, each
{}_{i}f
can be written in the form

{}_{i}f=\sum_{l=1}^{r_{i}}(c_{i,l})(_{i}g_{l}),

where
c_{i,l}\in K
,
l=1,\ldots,r_{i}
. Since
\{_{t-i}k_{1},\ldots,_{t-i}k_{d_{t-i}}\}
be a basis of
K
-vector space

[K[X_{n+1},\ldots,X_{m}]]_{t-i}
, each
h_{i}
can be written in the form

h_{i}=\sum_{u=1}^{d_{i}}(b_{i,u})(_{i}k_{u}),

where
b_{i,u}\in K
,
u=1,\ldots,d_{i}
.
Therefore,
f
can be written in the form

f=\sum_{i=0}^{t}\left(\sum_{l=1}^{r_{i}}(c_{i,l})(_{i}g_{l})\right)\left(\sum_{u=1}^{d_{t-i}}(b_{{t-i},u})(_{{t-i}}k_{u})\right),

where all
c_{i,l},b_{b-i,u}\in K
. By using Lemma

(_{i}g_{l})(_{t-i}k_{u})\in[I_{\phi_{n,m}(Z)}]_{t}

\left\{(_{i}g_{l})(_{t-i}k_{u})|i=0,\ldots,t;l=1,\ldots,r_{i};u=1,\ldots,d_{t-i}\right\}\text{ is a generated set of }[I_{\phi_{n,n+1}(Z)}]_{t}\qquad(9).

From
(8)
and
(9)
we have
\left\{(_{i}g_{j})(_{t-i}k_{l})|i=0,\ldots,t;j=1,\ldots,r_{i};l=1,\ldots,d_{t-i}\right\}

K
-vector space
[I_{\phi_{n,m}(Z)}]_{t}
and

\displaystyle\dim_{K}[I_{\phi_{n,m}(Z)}]_{t}

\displaystyle=\underset{i=0}{\overset{t}{\sum}}r_{i}d_{t-i}=r_{t}+\underset{i=0}{\overset{t-1}{\sum}}d_{t-i}r_{i}

\displaystyle=\dim_{K}[I_{Z}]_{t}+\underset{i=0}{\overset{t-1}{\sum}}\binom{m-n-1+t-i}{t-i}\dim_{K}\left[\wp_{1}^{m_{1}-t+i}\cap\cdots\cap\wp_{s}^{m_{s}-t+i}\right]_{i}.

∎

Theorem 4.5
.

Let
Z=m_{1}P_{1}+\cdots+m_{s}P_{s}

\phi_{n,m}:\mathbb{P}^{n}\to\mathbb{P}^{m}
be the embedding. If

\displaystyle H_{R_{m}/I_{\phi_{n,m}(Z)}}(t)

\displaystyle=H_{R_{n}/I_{Z}}(t)+\binom{t+m}{m}-\binom{t+n}{n}

\displaystyle-\underset{i=0}{\overset{t-1}{\sum}}\binom{m-n-1+t-i}{t-i}\left(\binom{i+n}{n}-H_{R_{n}/(\wp_{1}^{m_{1}-t+i}\cap\cdots\cap\wp_{s}^{m_{s}-t+i})}(i)\right).

Proof.

We have

H_{R_{m}/I_{\phi_{n,m}(Z)}}(t)=\binom{t+m}{m}-\dim_{K}[I_{\phi_{n,m}(Z)}]_{t}

and

H_{R_{n}/(\wp_{1}^{m_{1}-t+i}\cap\cdots\cap\wp_{s}^{m_{s}-t+i})}(i)=\binom{i+n}{n}-\dim_{K}[\wp_{1}^{m_{1}-t+i}\cap\cdots\cap\wp_{s}^{m_{s}-t+i}]_{i},

4.4

\displaystyle\binom{t+m}{m}-H_{R_{m}/I_{\phi_{n,m}(Z)}}(t)

\displaystyle=\binom{t+n}{n}-H_{R_{n}/(\wp_{1}^{m_{1}}\cap\cdots\cap\wp_{s}^{m_{s}})}(t)

\displaystyle+\underset{i=0}{\overset{t-1}{\sum}}\binom{m-n-1+t-i}{t-i}\left(\binom{i+n}{n}-H_{R_{n}/(\wp_{1}^{m_{1}-t+i}\cap\cdots\cap\wp_{s}^{m_{s}-t+i})}(i)\right).

It follows that

\displaystyle H_{R_{m}/I_{\phi_{n,m}(Z)}}(t)

\displaystyle=H_{R_{n}/I_{Z}}(t)+\binom{t+m}{m}-\binom{t+n}{n}

\displaystyle-\underset{i=0}{\overset{t-1}{\sum}}\binom{m-n-1+t-i}{t-i}\left(\binom{i+n}{n}-H_{R_{n}/(\wp_{1}^{m_{1}-t+i}\cap\cdots\cap\wp_{s}^{m_{s}-t+i})}(i)\right).

∎

Corollary 4.6
.

Let
Z=m_{1}P_{1}+\cdots+m_{s}P_{s}
be fat points in
\mathbb{P}^{n}
and
\phi_{n,m}:\mathbb{P}^{n}\to\mathbb{P}^{m}
be the embedding.

a) If
m=n+1
and
0\leq t<\operatorname{reg}(R_{n}/I_{Z})
, then

H_{R_{n+1}/I_{\phi_{n,n+1}(Z)}}(t)=H_{R_{n}/I_{Z}}(t)+\underset{i=0}{\overset{t-1}{\sum}}H_{R_{n}/(\wp_{1}^{m_{1}-t+i}\cap\cdots\cap\wp_{s}^{m_{s}-t+i})}(i).

b)
H_{R_{m}/I_{\phi_{n,m}(Z)}}(t)\geq H_{R_{n}/I_{Z}}(t).

c) If there exists
m_{j}\geq 2
, then

H_{R_{m}/I_{\phi_{n,m}(Z)}}(t)>H_{R_{n}/I_{Z}}(t).

Proof.

a) If
m=n+1
and
0\leq t<\operatorname{reg}(R_{n}/I_{Z})
, then by using Theorem
4.5
we get

\displaystyle H_{R_{n+1}/I_{\phi_{n,n+1}(Z)}}(t)

\displaystyle=H_{R_{n}/I_{Z}}(t)+\binom{t+n+1}{n+1}-\binom{t+n}{n}

\displaystyle-\underset{i=0}{\overset{t-1}{\sum}}\binom{t-i}{t-i}\left(\binom{i+n}{n}-H_{R_{n}/(\wp_{1}^{m_{1}-t+i}\cap\cdots\cap\wp_{s}^{m_{s}-t+i})}(i)\right)

\displaystyle=H_{R_{n}/I_{Z}}(t)+\binom{t+n+1}{n+1}-\binom{t+n}{n}-\underset{i=0}{\overset{t-1}{\sum}}\binom{i+n}{n}

\displaystyle+\underset{i=0}{\overset{t-1}{\sum}}H_{R_{n}/(\wp_{1}^{m_{1}-t+i}\cap\cdots\cap\wp_{s}^{m_{s}-t+i})}(i)

\displaystyle=H_{R_{n}/I_{Z}}(t)+\underset{i=0}{\overset{t-1}{\sum}}H_{R_{n}/(\wp_{1}^{m_{1}-t+i}\cap\cdots\cap\wp_{s}^{m_{s}-t+i})}(i).

b) If
0\leq t<\operatorname{reg}(R_{n}/I_{Z})
, then by a) we get

\displaystyle H_{R_{n+1}/I_{\phi_{n,n+1}(Z)}}(t)

\displaystyle\geq H_{R_{n}/I_{Z}}(t),

\displaystyle H_{R_{n+2}/I_{\phi_{n+1,n+2}\circ\phi_{n,n+1}(Z)}}(t)

\displaystyle\geq H_{R_{n+1}/I_{\phi_{n,n+1}(Z)}}(t),

\displaystyle\cdots\cdots\cdots\cdots\cdots\cdots\cdots\cdots\cdots\cdots

\displaystyle\cdots\cdot\cdots\cdots\cdots\cdots\cdots\cdots\cdots

\displaystyle H_{R_{m}/I_{\phi_{m-1,m}\circ\phi_{m-2,m-1}\circ\cdots\circ\phi_{n+1,n+2}\circ\phi_{n,n+1}(Z)}}(t)

\displaystyle\geq H_{R_{m-1}/I_{\phi_{m-1,m-1}\circ\cdots\circ\phi_{n+1,n+2}\circ\phi_{n,n+1}(Z)}}(t).

These imply that

H_{R_{m}/I_{\phi_{m-1,m}\circ\phi_{m-2,m-1}\circ\cdots\circ\phi_{n+1,n+2}\circ\phi_{n,n+1}(Z)}}(t)\geq H_{R_{n}/I_{Z}}(t).

But

\phi_{n,m}(Z)=\phi_{m-1,m}\circ\phi_{m-1,m-1}\circ\cdots\circ\phi_{n+1,n+2}\circ\phi_{n,n+1}(Z).

Therefore,

H_{R_{m}/I_{\phi_{n,m}(Z)}}(t)\geq H_{R_{n}/I_{Z}}(t).

If
t\geq\operatorname{reg}(R_{n}/I_{Z})
, then by Proposition
4.1
we get

H_{R_{n+1}/I_{\phi_{n,n+1}(Z)}}(t)\geq H_{R_{n}/I_{Z}}(t).

c) If there exists
m_{j}\geq 2
and
0\leq t<\operatorname{reg}(R_{n}/I_{Z})
, we have
\wp_{j}^{m_{j}-1}\neq R_{n}
. So,

H_{R_{n}/(\wp_{1}^{m_{1}-1}\cap\cdots\cap\wp_{s}^{m_{s}-1})}(t-1)>0.
By using a) we get

H_{R_{n+1}/I_{\phi_{n,n+1}(Z)}}(t)>H_{R_{n}/I_{Z}}(t).

By using b) we have

H_{R_{m}/I_{\phi_{n+1,m}\circ\phi_{n,n+1}(Z)}}(t)\geq H_{R_{n+1}/I_{\phi_{n,n+1}(Z)}}(t).

But
\phi_{n,m}(Z)=\phi_{n+1,m}\circ\phi_{n,n+1}(Z)
. Therefore,

H_{R_{m}/I_{\phi_{n,m}(Z)}}(t)>H_{R_{n}/I_{Z}}(t).

If there exists
m_{j}\geq 2
and
t\geq\operatorname{reg}(R_{n}/I_{Z})
, then by Proposition
4.1
we get

H_{R_{m}/I_{\phi_{n,m}(Z)}}(t)>H_{R_{n}/I_{Z}}(t).

∎
