Split extensions of BiHom-Lie algebras
Nejib Saadaoui

Abstract
In this paper, we introduce the notion of split extension of BiHom-Lie algebra and construct the corresponding cohomology. Also, we establish a one-to-one correspondence between the equivalence classes of extensions of a BiHom-Lie algebra L L by an abelian BiHom-Lie algebra V V and it’s second cohomology group.
