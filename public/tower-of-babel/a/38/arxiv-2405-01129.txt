Generic Torelli with denominators for elliptic surfaces
N. I. Shepherd-Barron

Abstract
We show that a very general Jacobian elliptic surface is determined by its polarized ℚ {\mathbb{Q}} -Hodge structure, subject to various constraints on the irregularity and the geometric genus.
