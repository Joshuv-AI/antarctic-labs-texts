Jacob’s ladders, crossbreeding and infinite sets of meta-functional equations as new species generated by the mother formula
Jan Moser

Abstract
. In this paper we obtain a set of meta-functional equations as new species of formulas in classical mathematical analysis. Mentioned species are generated by crossbreeding complete hybrid formula as a mother formula. Namely, they are generated by an infinite set of crossbreedings on some subsidiary infinite set of meta-functional equations with one neutral factor. DEDICATED TO THE 160th ANNIVERSARY OF DARWIN’S ORIGIN OF SPECIES
