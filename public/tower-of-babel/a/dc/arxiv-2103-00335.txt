Expectation of the Site Frequency Spectrum
Alan R. Rogers, Stephen P. Wooding

Abstract
The site frequency spectrum describes variation among a set of n n DNA sequences. Its i i ’th entry ( i = 1 , 2 , … , n − 1 i=1,2,\ldots,n-1 ) is the number of nucleotide sites at which the mutant allele is present in i i copies. Under selective neutrality, random mating, and constant population size, the expected value of the spectrum is well known but somewhat puzzling. Each additional sequence added to a sample adds an entry to the end of the expected spectrum but does not affect existing entries. This note reviews the reasons for this behavior.
