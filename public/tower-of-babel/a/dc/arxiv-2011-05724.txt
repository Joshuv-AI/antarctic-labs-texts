Extensions of valuation rings containing 𝐐 \bf Q as limits of smooth algebras
Dorin Popescu

Abstract
. We give a necessary and sufficient condition for an extension of valuation rings containing 𝐐 \bf Q to be a filtered direct limit of smooth algebras. Key words : Valuation rings, immediate extensions, pseudo convergent sequences, pseudo limits, Andreé-Quillen homology, smooth morphisms. 2020 Mathematics Subject Classification: Primary 13F30, Secondary 13D03, 13A18, 13B40.



Introduction

Any integral algebraic variety equipped with a dominant morphism from a valuation ring
V
can be desingularized along
V
in characteristic zero as it is shown by the Zariski Uniformization Theorem
[
11
]
. This gives that any valuation ring
V
containing a field
K
of characteristic zero is a filtered union of regular
K
-subalgebras of
V
of finite type, or equivalently of smooth
K
-subalgebras of
V
. In particular, any finite system of polynomial equations over
K
with a solution in
V
can be "embedded" in a finite system of polynomial equations over
K
with a solution in
V
, but for which one can apply the Implicit Function Theorem.

A ring map
A\rightarrow A^{\prime}
is
ind-smooth
if
A^{\prime}
is a filtered direct limit of smooth
A
-algebras. A filtered direct limit (in other words a filtered colimit) is a limit indexed by a small category that is filtered (see
[
9
, 002V]
or
[
9
, 04AX]
). A filtered union is a filtered direct limit in which all objects are subobjects of the final colimit, so that in particular all the transition arrows are monomorphisms. The Zariski Uniformization Theorem says, in particular, that
K\rightarrow V
is ind-smooth, if
K\supset{\bf Q}
. This weaker form has a different proof in
[
8
]
. It was mainly a consequence of
the following theorem
(see
[
8
, Theorem 21]
).

Theorem 1
.

Let
V\subset V^{\prime}
be an immediate extension of valuation rings containing
\bf Q
.
Then
V^{\prime}
is ind-smooth over
V
.

An immediate extension of valuation rings is an extension inducing trivial extensions on residue field and group value extensions.

The goal of this paper is to show the following theorems.

Theorem 2
.

Let
V\subset V^{\prime}
be an extension of valuation rings containing
\bf Q
,
K\subset K^{\prime}
its fraction field extension. Then
V^{\prime}
is ind-smooth over

if and only if the following statements hold

(1)

for each

the ideal
qV^{\prime}
is also prime,

(2)

For any prime ideals

such that

and

the extension

In the next theorem the necessary and sufficient conditions are given in the frame of the value group extension of
