On two-body and three-body spin correlations in leptonic t ​ t ¯ ​ Z t\bar{t}Z production and anomalous couplings at the LHC Preprint: HRI-RECAPP-2022-006
Rafiqul Rahaman

Abstract
We study the anomalous t ​ t ¯ ​ Z t\bar{t}Z couplings in the t ​ t ¯ ​ Z t\bar{t}Z production in leptonic final state at the 13 13 TeV LHC. We use the polarizations of top quarks and Z Z boson, two-body and three-body spin correlations among the top quarks and Z Z boson, and the cross section to probe the anomalous couplings. We estimate one parameter and simultaneous limits on the couplings of the effective vertex as well as the effective operators for a set of luminosities 150 150 fb -1 , 300 300 fb -1 , 1000 1000 fb -1 , and 3000 3000 fb -1 . The polarizations and the spin correlations are found to be helpful on top of the cross section to better constrain the anomalous couplings.

We reconstruct the two neutrinos with detector level events for the polarizations and spin correlations in the

The four momenta of the two neutrinos are then solved using the constraints,

\displaystyle\vec{\cancel{p}}_{T}=\vec{p}_{T}(\nu_{\mu})+\vec{p}_{T}(\bar{\nu}_{\mu}),

\displaystyle m_{\mu^{+}\nu_{\mu}}^{2}=m_{W}^{2}=m_{\mu^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\mu}}^{2},

\displaystyle m_{b\mu^{+}\nu_{\mu}}^{2}=m_{t}^{2}=m_{\bar{b}\mu^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\mu}}^{2}.

(12)

These constraints provide multiple sets of solutions for the components of neutrinos’ momenta consisting of real as well as imaginary values due to the quadratic nature of the constraint equations.
In case all solutions are imaginary, the values of

and

are varied

and

1000
, which is only about
0.2\%
. In an event consisting of several real sets of solutions for neutrinos momenta, the set is chosen with minimum

4
Probe of anomalous
t\bar{t}Z
interaction

In this work, we are interested in studying the anomalous interactions, i.e., a contribution that can be received from beyond the SM, of
t
,
\bar{t}
, and
Z
in the
t\bar{t}Z
production process. We neglect the effect of the four-point contact interaction of four quarks that can enter into the
t\bar{t}Z
production at the LHC, as these are better constrained in
t\bar{t}
production process.
The
t\bar{t}Z
interaction Lagrangian, including new physics, is generally parameterized in a model independent way as
Aguilar-Saavedra:2008nuh
;
AguilarSaavedra:2012vh
;
Ravina:2021kpr
,

{\cal L}_{t\bar{t}Z}=e\bar{t}\left[\gamma^{\mu}\left(C_{1}^{V}+\gamma_{5}C_{1}^{A}\right)+\frac{i\sigma^{\mu\nu}q_{\nu}}{m_{Z}}\left(C_{2}^{V}+i\gamma_{5}C_{2}^{A}\right)\right]tZ_{\mu},

(13)

with
\sigma^{\mu\nu}=\frac{1}{2}\left[\gamma^{\mu},\gamma^{\nu}\right]
and
q_{\nu}=\left(p_{t}-p_{\bar{t}}\right)_{\nu}
, i.e., four momentum transfer of
Z
, with
p_{t}
(
p_{\bar{t}}
) as the four momentum of top (anti-top) quark. In the SM,
C_{2}^{V/A}=0
at tree level and
C_{1}^{V/A}
attain their SM values, which are
C_{1,\text{SM}}^{V}\simeq 0.24
and
C_{1,\text{SM}}^{A}\simeq-0.60
. The couplings
C_{2}^{V}
and
C_{2}^{A}
(
CP
-odd) are the source of weak magnetic and electric
dipole moments of the top quark, respectively and are highly suppressed in the SM
Rontsch:2014cca
;
Schulze:2016qas
;
Aguilar-Saavedra:2008nuh
.

In the effective field theory (EFT) approach with higher dimension operators (
{\cal O}
), the interaction Lagrangian is expressed as,

{\cal L}_{t\bar{t}Z}={\cal L}_{t\bar{t}Z}(\text{SM})+\sum_{n}\sum_{i}\dfrac{C_{i}^{n}}{\Lambda^{(n-4)}}{\cal O}_{i}^{n}

(14)

with index
n
summing over higher dimensions (
>4
) and index
i
summing over all the operators in a givendimension;
C_{i}
are the Wilson coefficients (WCs) corresponding to operator
{\cal O}_{i}
. The
\Lambda
is the cut-off energy scale up to which the EFT is valid. The gauge invariant operators, made of the SM fields, are low energy remnant of some new physics theory at a higher energy scale (
>\Lambda
) with heavy fields, i.e., the heavy degrees of freedom are integrated out to the WCs at low energy.
The anomalous
t\bar{t}Z
interactions in Eq. (
13
) receive contribution from the following dimension-
6
operators
Aguilar-Saavedra:2008nuh
;
BessidskaiaBylund:2016jvp
;
Ravina:2021kpr
,

\displaystyle{\cal O}_{uB}^{ij}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left(\bar{Q}_{i}\sigma^{\mu\nu}u_{j}\right)\tilde{\varphi}B_{\mu\nu},

\displaystyle{\cal O}_{uW}^{ij}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left(\bar{Q}_{i}\sigma^{\mu\nu}\tau^{I}u_{j}\right)\tilde{\varphi}W_{\mu\nu}^{I},

\displaystyle{\cal O}_{\varphi u}^{ij}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left(\varphi^{\dagger}\overleftrightarrow{D}_{\mu}\varphi\right)\left(\bar{u_{i}}\gamma^{\mu}u_{j}\right),

\displaystyle{\cal O}_{\varphi Q}^{1(ij)}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left(\varphi^{\dagger}\overleftrightarrow{D}_{\mu}\varphi\right)\left(\bar{Q_{i}}\gamma^{\mu}Q_{j}\right),

\displaystyle{\cal O}_{\varphi Q}^{3(ij)}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left(\varphi^{\dagger}\overleftrightarrow{D}^{I}_{\mu}\varphi\right)\left(\bar{Q_{i}}\gamma^{\mu}\tau^{I}Q_{j}\right).

(15)

Here,
i,j
are the flavor indices;
Q
is the left-handed quark doublet and
u
are the right-handed singlet quark;
\tau^{I}
are the Pauli matrices,
\varphi
is the Higgs doublet with its dual
\widetilde{\varphi}=i\tau^{2}\varphi^{\star}
;
B_{\mu\nu}^{I}
and
W_{\mu\nu}^{I}
are the
U(1)_{Y}
and
SU(2)_{L}
filed strength tensor, respectively;
D_{\mu}
(
\overleftarrow{D}_{\mu}
) is the
covariant derivative acting on the right (left) and
\overleftrightarrow{D}_{\mu}=D_{\mu}-\overleftarrow{D}_{\mu}
.
The couplings in Eq. (
13
) are related to the WCs of the operators listed in Eq. (
4
) as
Aguilar-Saavedra:2008nuh
;
Aguilar-Saavedra:2018ksv
;
Ravina:2021kpr
,

\displaystyle C_{1}^{V}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle C_{1,\text{SM}}^{V}+\frac{v^{2}}{2\Lambda^{2}\sin\theta_{W}\cos\theta_{W}}\text{Re}\left[-c_{\varphi t}-c_{\varphi Q}^{-}\right],

\displaystyle C_{1}^{A}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle C_{1,\text{SM}}^{A}+\frac{v^{2}}{2\Lambda^{2}\sin\theta_{W}\cos\theta_{W}}\text{Re}\left[-c_{\varphi t}+c_{\varphi Q}^{-}\right],

\displaystyle C_{2}^{V}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{\sqrt{2}v^{2}}{2\Lambda^{2}\sin\theta_{W}\cos\theta_{W}}c_{tZ},

\displaystyle C_{2}^{A}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{\sqrt{2}v^{2}}{2\Lambda^{2}\sin\theta_{W}\cos\theta_{W}}c_{tZ}^{I}

(16)

with

\displaystyle c_{tZ}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\text{Re}[-\sin\theta_{W}C_{uB}^{33}+\cos\theta_{W}C_{uW}^{33}],

\displaystyle c_{tZ}^{I}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\text{Im}[-\sin\theta_{W}C_{uB}^{33}+\cos\theta_{W}C_{uW}^{33}],

\displaystyle c_{\varphi t}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle C_{\varphi u}^{33},

\displaystyle c_{\varphi Q}^{-}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle C_{\varphi Q}^{1(33)}-C_{\varphi Q}^{3(33)}.

(17)

Here,
\theta_{W}
is the Weinberg mixing angle, and
v\simeq 246
GeV is the vacuum expectation value (VEV) of the Higgs.
We define
\Delta C_{1}^{V/A}=C_{1}^{V/A}-C_{1,\text{SM}}^{V/A}
and treat them as anomalous couplings in the rest of the article.

5
Summary

In summary, we studied the anomalous
t\bar{t}Z
interaction in leptonic channel of the
t\bar{t}Z
production process at the
13
TeV LHC with the help of polarizations, two-body and three-body spin correlations of
t
,
\bar{t}
and
Z
on top of the cross sections binned in
p_{T}(Z)
.
We showed how the reconstruction of two neutrinos at the detector level affects the angular distributions corresponding to polarizations and spin correlations compared to the parton level distributions. We identified a few polarization and spin correlation parameters sensitive to only one kind of anomalous couplings, helping us disentangle the effect of the four anomalous couplings that we have considered. The sensitivity of the couplings to polarizations and spin correlations are studied in the form of
\chi^{2}
for a luminosity of
{\cal L}=3000
fb
-1
. The improvements of limits on the couplings are studied over the binned cross sections by successively including the polarization and spin correlation asymmetries. We estimated the one parameter and simultaneous limits at
95\%
C.L. on the anomalous couplings as well as the effective operators for a set of luminosities of
150
fb
-1
,
300
fb
-1
,
1000
fb
-1
, and
3000
fb
-1
.
Our limits on the couplings (except
\Delta C_{1}^{A}
/
C_{\varphi t}
) are better compared to the existing limits obtained by CSM
CMS:2019too
;
CMS:2022hjj
.
The polarizations and spin correlations help in tightening the region of parameters space, especially for the vector and axial-vector couplings (
\Delta C_{1}^{V}
-
\Delta C_{1}^{A}
) in comparison to the cross section by a considerable amount. The parameter space becomes even tinier in the
2l+3l+4l
channel neighboring only the SM point. Our strategy in this analysis can serve as an extra handle in interpreting anomalous interactions on the data at the high energy and high luminosity LHC.
