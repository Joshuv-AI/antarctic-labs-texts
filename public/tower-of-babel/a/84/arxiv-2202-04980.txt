The Magidor Iteration and Restrictions of Ultrapowers to the Ground Model
Eyal Kaplan

Abstract
We study the Magidor iteration of Prikry forcings below a measurable limit of measurables κ \kappa . We first characterize all the normal measures κ \kappa carries in the generic extension, building on and extending the main result of [ 1 ] . Then, for every such normal measure, we prove that the restriction of its ultrapower, from the generic extension to the ground model, is an iterated ultrapower of V V by normal measures. This is done without core model theoretic assumptions; GCH ≤ κ \mbox{GCH}_{\leq\kappa} in the ground model suffices.



Introduction

In this paper we revisit the Magidor iteration of Prikry forcings, which was first introduced in
[
5
]
. Let
\kappa
be a measurable limit of measurables. The Magidor iteration can be used to destroy the measurability of every measurable cardinal
\alpha<\kappa
, while preserving cardinals and the measurability of
\kappa
itself. Given such an iteration
P
and a generic set
G\subseteq P
over the ground model
V
, we consider the following questions:

1.

What are the normal measures on
\kappa
in
V\left[G\right]
?

2.

Given a normal measure
W\in V\left[G\right]
on
\kappa
, let
j_{W}\colon V\left[G\right]\to M\left[H\right]
be its ultrapower embedding. Is
j_{W}\restriction_{V}
an iteration of
V
(by its measures or extenders)?

3.

Given a normal measure
W\in V\left[G\right]
, is
j_{W}\restriction_{V}
a definable class of
V
?

The first question was extensively studied by O. Ben-Neria in
[
1
]
. For every normal measure
U\in V
on
\kappa
, he assigned a corresponding measure
U^{\times}\in V\left[G\right]
on
\kappa
, and showed that the mapping
U\mapsto U^{\times}
is a bijection between the set of normal measures on
\kappa
in
V
, and the set of normal measures on
\kappa
in
V\left[G\right]
. This was done under the assumption that the ground model
V
is the core model and
0^{\lx@paragraphsign}
does not hold. In this paper, we extend this result, weakening the assumption on the ground model
V
:

Theorem 0.1
.

Assume
\mbox{GCH}_{\leq\kappa}
holds in
V
. Let
W\in V\left[G\right]
be a normal measure on
\kappa
. Then
W=U^{\times}
for some normal measure
U\in V
on
\kappa
. Moreover, the measures
\langle U^{\times}\colon U\in V\mbox{ is a normal measure on }\kappa\rangle
are pairwise distinct.

The proof relies on the same methods of Ben-Neria in
[
1
]
; the core-model theoretic aspects of the argument are replaced by the tools developed in
[
4
]
.

The second question is answered affirmatively - for every forcing notion
P
, not only Magidor iterations - under the assumption that there is no inner model with a Woodin cardinal, and the ground model is the core model (see
[
6
]
). We prove that this remains true for the Magidor iteration in much more general settings:

Theorem 0.2
.

Assume
\mbox{GCH}_{\leq\kappa}
holds in
V
. Let
W\in V\left[G\right]
be a normal measure on
\kappa
. Then
j_{W}\restriction_{V}
is an iterated ultrapower of
V
by normal measures.

Moreover, a concrete description of
j_{W}\restriction_{V}
as an iterated ultrapower is given. This uses and extends ideas appearing in
[
3
]
, where iterations of Prikry forcings were considered under the simpler nonstationary support.

The answer to the third question depends on the choice of the normal measures used along the iteration to singularize the measurables of
V
below
\kappa
. In general,
j_{W}\restriction_{V}
may not be definable in
V
(see, e.g., section 5.2 in
[
4
]
). In lemma
3.29
we provide a sufficient condition for definability of
j_{W}\restriction_{V}
as a class of
V
.

This paper is organized as follows: In the first section we present the forcing and its basic properties. In section
2
we prove theorem
0.1
. In section
3
we prove theorem
0.2
, provide a sufficient condition for definability of
j_{W}\restriction_{V}
as a class of
V
, and completely describe the Prikry sequences added to measurables of
M
above
\kappa
in
H
.
