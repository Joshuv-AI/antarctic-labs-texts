Mixing Angle of Ξ Q − Ξ Q ′ \varXi_{Q}-\varXi_{Q}^{\prime} in Heavy Quark Effective Therory
Yoshimitsu Matsui

Abstract
The Heavy Quark Effective Theory provides a systematic method to estimate a mixing angle of hadron states in a heavy quark, such as the charm quark (c) and the bottom quark (b). By using this method, the mixing angle of the baryons Ξ Q − Ξ Q ′ \varXi_{Q}-\varXi_{Q}^{\prime} can be estimated. It is found that the mixing angle between Ξ Q \varXi_{Q} and Ξ Q ′ \varXi_{Q}^{\prime} is given by θ b = 4.51 ∘ ± 0.79 ∘ \theta_{b}=4.51^{\circ}\pm 0.79^{\circ} for Q = b Q=b case and θ c = 8.12 ∘ ± 0.80 ∘ \theta_{c}=8.12^{\circ}\pm 0.80^{\circ} for Q = c Q=c case.

The HQET Lagrangian with
O\left(\Lambda_{QCD}/m_{Q}\right)
corrections is

{\cal L}={\bar{h}}_{v}(iv\cdot D)h_{v}+\frac{1}{2m_{Q}}{\bar{h}}_{v}(iD)^{2}h_{v}+Z_{Q}\frac{1}{2m_{Q}}{\bar{h}}_{v}\sigma^{\mu\nu}G_{\mu\nu}h_{v},

(7)

where
v
is the four velocity of the heavy quark
h
,

Z_{Q}
is a renormalization factor and
G_{\mu\nu}

is the gluon field strength
[
17
]
.
Neglecting the higher order corrections
O\left(\Lambda^{2}_{QCD}/m^{2}_{Q}\right)
,
we have a heavy hadron mass formula

M\left(H_{Q}\right)=m_{Q}+{\bar{\varLambda}}-\frac{\lambda_{1}}{2m_{Q}}-d_{H}\frac{\lambda_{2}}{2m_{Q}},

(8)

where
{\bar{\varLambda}}
is the energy of the light degrees of freedom
in the hadron.
Two
O\left(\Lambda_{QCD}/m_{Q}\right)
correction coefficients are given by the matrix elements

\lambda_{1}=\langle H_{Q}(v)|{\bar{h}}_{v}(iD)^{2}h_{v}|H_{Q}(v)\rangle

(9)

and

d_{H}\lambda_{2}=\frac{1}{2}Z_{Q}\langle H_{Q}(v)|{\bar{h}}_{v}G_{\mu\nu}\sigma^{\mu\nu}h_{v}|H_{Q}(v)\rangle,

(10)

where
d_{H}
is the Clebsch-Gordan factor which can be written by,

d_{H}=-4\langle\vec{S}_{Q}\cdot\vec{s}_{\ell}\rangle.

\vec{S}_{Q}
and
\vec{s}_{Q}
are the spins of the heavy quark and the angular momentum of
the light degrees of freedom respectively.
The
\langle\vec{S}_{Q}\cdot\vec{s}_{\ell}\rangle
can be obtained by

2\langle\vec{S}_{Q}\cdot\vec{S}_{\ell}\rangle=J(J+1)-S_{Q}(S_{Q}+1)-S_{\ell}(S_{\ell}+1),

where
J
,
S_{Q}
,
s_{\ell}
are the magnitude of the total angular momentum and
the spin of the heavy quark and the angular momentum of
the light degrees of freedom respectively.
