Algebraic geometry of bubbling Kähler metrics
Yuji Odaka

Abstract
. We give an algebro-geometric or non-archimedean framework to study bubbling phenomena of Kähler metrics with Euclidean volume growth, after [ DS17 , Sun23 , dBS24 ] . In particular, for any degenerating family to log terminal singularity, we give an algorithm to algebraically construct a finite sequence of birational modifications of the family with milder degenerations, and partially relate with analytic bubbling constructions in [ Sun23 , dBS24 ] . We also provide equivalent approaches in terms of coordinates and valuations.
