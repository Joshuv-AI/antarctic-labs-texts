Finiteness properties and Relatively Hyperbolic Groups
Harsh Patil

Abstract
We show that properties F n F_{n} and F ​ P n FP_{n} hold for a relatively hyperbolic group if and only if they hold for all the peripheral subgroups. As an application we show that there are at least countably many distinct quasi-isometry classes of one-ended groups that are type F n F_{n} but not F n + 1 F_{n+1} and similarly of type F ​ P n FP_{n} and not F ​ P n + 1 FP_{n+1} for all positive integers n n .

Relatively Hyperbolic groups were introduced by Gromov in

Many geometric and algorithmic properties about the group

Two of the most important properties a group can have are finite generation and finite presentability. The property of being finitely presented can be reformulated in terms of a free group action on a locally finite 1-dimensional CW-complex. In
[

, Wall introduced a generalization of these properties. A group is said to be of type

-connected CW-complex.
A group that is of type

F_{\infty}
.

A group is said to be of type

if the

-module

admits a projective resolution which is finitely generated in all dimensions upto
n
. For a group of type

such a projective resolution can be arrived at by taking the chain complex of the universal cover of some classifying space with finite
n
-skeleton. In
[
2

Bestvina-Brady have exhibited groups that are of type

but not of type

(and similarly

but not
F_{n+1}

. In
[

but not

using tools from complex hyperbolic geometry. The long-standing open question of whether every subgroup of a hyperbolic group that is of type

[
13

.

In
[
15

, Osin proved that a relatively hyperbolic group admits a finite relative presentation. It follows that if all the peripheral subgroups are finitely presented then the relatively hyperbolic groups is also finitely presented.
In
[
7

, Dahmani proves that property

for peripherals implies property

and

Let
