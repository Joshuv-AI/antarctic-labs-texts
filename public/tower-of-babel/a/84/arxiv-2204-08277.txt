A Note on Sun’s Conjectures on Apéry-like Sums Involving Lucas Sequences and Harmonic Numbers
Ce Xu and Jianqiang Zhao[1mm] a. School of Mathematics and Statistics, Anhui Normal University, Wuhu 241002, P.R. Chinab. Department of Mathematics, The Bishop’s School, La Jolla, CA 92037, USA

Abstract




Abstract.
In this paper, we will prove Zhi-Wei Sun’s four conjectural identities
on Apéry-like sums involving Lucas sequences and harmonic numbers by using a few results of Davydychev–Kalmykov
[
1
]
.

Keywords
: Apéry-like sums, harmonic numbers, Lucas numbers, Lucas sequences, Clausen function.

AMS Subject Classifications (2020):
11M06, 11B39, 40A05

1
Introduction

The classical Lucas numbers
\{L_{n}\}_{n\geq 0}
are defined by

\displaystyle L_{0}=2,\ L_{1}=1\quad\text{and}\quad L_{n+1}=L_{n}+L_{n-1}\ \forall n\geq 1.

Obviously, for any
n\geq 0
,

L_{n}=\Bigl(\displaystyle\frac{1+\sqrt{5}}{2}\Bigr)^{n}+\Bigl(\displaystyle\frac{1-\sqrt{5}}{2}\Bigr)^{n}.

Let
A
and
B
be integers. Similarly to Sun
[
3
]
, we define the more general Lucas sequence
v_{n}=v_{n}(A,B)
as follows:

\displaystyle v_{0}(A,B):=2,\quad v_{1}(A,B):=A\quad\text{and}\quad v_{n+1}(A,B)=Av_{n}(A,B)-Bv_{n-1}(A,B)\ \forall n\geq 1.

The characteristic equation
x^{2}-Ax+B=0
of the sequence
\{v_{n}\}_{n\geq 0}
has two roots

\alpha=\displaystyle\frac{A+\sqrt{A^{2}-4B}}{2}\quad\text{and}\quad\beta=\displaystyle\frac{A-\sqrt{A^{2}-4B}}{2}.

It is well known that for any integer
n\geq 0
we have

\displaystyle v_{n}(A,B)=\alpha^{n}+\beta^{n}.

For examples, setting
\phi=\displaystyle\frac{\sqrt{5}+1}{2}
we have

\displaystyle v_{n}(3,1)=L_{2n}=\phi^{2n}+\phi^{-2n},

\displaystyle v_{n}(4,1)=(2-\sqrt{3})^{n}+(2+\sqrt{3})^{n},

\displaystyle v_{n}(4,2)=(2-\sqrt{2})^{n}+(2+\sqrt{2})^{n},

\displaystyle v_{n}(5,5)=(\sqrt{5}\phi)^{n}+(\sqrt{5}\phi^{-1})^{n}.

Define
H_{0}:=0
and the classical harmonic numbers

H_{n}:=\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{n}\displaystyle\frac{1}{k}\quad\forall n\geq 1.

In this short note, we will prove the following results.

Theorem 1.1
.

We have

\displaystyle\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\displaystyle\frac{L_{2n}}{n^{2}\binom{2n}{n}}\left(H_{2n}-H_{n-1}\right)=\displaystyle\frac{41\zeta(3)+4\pi^{2}\log(\phi)}{25},

(1.1)

\displaystyle\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\displaystyle\frac{v_{n}(5,5)}{n^{2}\binom{2n}{n}}\left(H_{2n}-H_{n-1}\right)=\displaystyle\frac{124\zeta(3)+\pi^{2}\log\left(5^{5}\phi^{6}\right)}{50},

(1.2)

\displaystyle\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\displaystyle\frac{v_{n}(4,1)}{n^{2}\binom{2n}{n}}\left(H_{2n}-H_{n-1}\right)=\displaystyle\frac{23\zeta(3)+2\pi^{2}\log(2+\sqrt{3})}{12},

(1.3)

\displaystyle\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\displaystyle\frac{v_{n}(4,2)}{n^{2}\binom{2n}{n}}\left(H_{2n}-H_{n-1}\right)=\displaystyle\frac{259\zeta(3)+2\pi^{2}(4\log(2)+8\log(1+\sqrt{2}))}{128}.

(1.4)

Motivated by his study of the congruences in
[
3
]
, Sun
[
4
,
5
,
6
]
proposes
identities (
1.1
) and (
1.2
) as
[
5
, Conjectures 10.62 and 10.63]

(see also
[
4
, Eqs. (3.11) and (3.12)]
). He further conjectures (
1.3
)
and (
1.4
) in
[
6
]
.

2
Proof of Theorem
1.1

Recall that for any positive integer
m
the Clausen function
{\rm Cl}_{m}(\theta)
is defined by

\displaystyle{{\rm Cl}}_{2m-1}(\theta):=\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\displaystyle\frac{\cos(n\theta)}{n^{2m-1}}\quad\text{and}\quad{{\rm Cl}}_{2m}(\theta):=\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\displaystyle\frac{\sin(n\theta)}{n^{2m}}\qquad\forall\theta\in[0,\pi].

Many special values of the Clausen function are closely related to the Riemann zeta values. For example,

{\rm Cl}_{2m-1}(0)=\zeta(2m-1),\quad{\rm Cl}_{2m-1}(\pi)=(2^{2-2m}-1)\zeta(2m-1),\quad{\rm Cl}_{2m-1}\Big(\displaystyle\frac{\pi}{2}\Big)=\displaystyle\frac{{\rm Cl}_{2m-1}(\pi)}{2^{2m-1}}.

(2.5)

The following relations among special values of the Clausen function are well known (see, e.g.,
[
2
, Eq. (6.51)]
):
for any positive integer
r
we have

\displaystyle{\rm Cl}_{3}\big(\tfrac{\pi}{r}\big)+{\rm Cl}_{3}\big(\tfrac{3\pi}{r}\big)+\cdots+{\rm Cl}_{3}\big(\tfrac{(2r-1)\pi}{r}\big)=-\tfrac{3}{4r^{2}}\zeta(3),

(2.6)

\displaystyle{\rm Cl}_{3}\big(\tfrac{2\pi}{r}\big)+{\rm Cl}_{3}\big(\tfrac{4\pi}{r}\big)+\cdots+{\rm Cl}_{3}\big(\tfrac{(2r-2)\pi}{r}\big)=-\big(1-\tfrac{1}{r^{2}}\big)\zeta(3).

(2.7)

Setting
r=4,5,6
in (
2.6
) and
r=5
in (
2.7
) one obtains

\displaystyle{\rm Cl}_{3}\big(\tfrac{\pi}{4}\big)+{\rm Cl}_{3}\big(\tfrac{3\pi}{4}\big)=-\tfrac{3}{128}\zeta(3),\qquad{\rm Cl}_{3}\big(\tfrac{\pi}{5}\big)+{\rm Cl}_{3}\big(\tfrac{3\pi}{5}\big)=\tfrac{9}{25}\zeta(3),

(2.8)

\displaystyle{\rm Cl}_{3}\big(\tfrac{\pi}{6}\big)+{\rm Cl}_{3}\big(\tfrac{5\pi}{6}\big)=\tfrac{1}{12}\zeta(3),\qquad{\rm Cl}_{3}\big(\tfrac{2\pi}{5}\big)+{\rm Cl}_{3}\big(\tfrac{4\pi}{5}\big)=-\tfrac{12}{25}\zeta(3),

(2.9)

since
{\rm Cl}_{3}(\theta)={\rm Cl}_{3}(2\pi\pm\theta)
,
{\rm Cl}_{3}(\pi)=-\tfrac{3}{4}\zeta(3)
and
{\rm Cl}_{3}\big(\tfrac{\pi}{2}\big)=-\tfrac{3}{32}\zeta(3)
by (
2.5
).

On the other hand, Davydychev and Kalmykov have shown that
(see
[
1
, (2.36), (2.37) and (2.67)]
)

\displaystyle\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\displaystyle\frac{1}{n^{3}\binom{2n}{n}}u^{n}=2{\rm Cl}_{3}(\theta)+2\theta{\rm Cl}_{2}(\theta)-2\zeta(3)+\theta^{2}\log\Big(2\sin\displaystyle\frac{\theta}{2}\Big),

\displaystyle\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\displaystyle\frac{H_{n-1}}{n^{2}\binom{2n}{n}}u^{n}=4{\rm Cl}_{3}(\pi-\theta)-2\theta{\rm Cl}_{2}(\pi-\theta)+3\zeta(3),

\displaystyle\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\displaystyle\frac{H_{2n-1}}{n^{2}\binom{2n}{n}}u^{n}=-2{\rm Cl}_{3}(\theta)+4{\rm Cl}_{3}(\pi-\theta)-2\theta{\rm Cl}_{2}(\pi-\theta)-\theta{\rm Cl}_{2}(\theta)+5\zeta(3),

where
u=4\sin^{2}\displaystyle\frac{\theta}{2}
for any
\theta\in[0,\pi]
.
Combining the above three equations we get

\displaystyle\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\displaystyle\frac{u^{n}}{n^{2}\binom{2n}{n}}\left(H_{2n}-H_{n-1}\right)

\displaystyle=\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\displaystyle\frac{u^{n}}{n^{2}\binom{2n}{n}}\left(H_{2n-1}-H_{n-1}+\displaystyle\frac{1}{2n}\right)

\displaystyle=\zeta(3)-{\rm Cl}_{3}(\theta)+\displaystyle\frac{\theta^{2}}{2}\log\Big(2\sin\displaystyle\frac{\theta}{2}\Big).

(2.10)

Taking special values of
u
we obtain

\displaystyle u=\Big(\displaystyle\frac{\sqrt{5}\pm 1}{2}\Big)^{2}=4\sin^{2}\displaystyle\frac{\theta}{2}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad

\displaystyle\sin\displaystyle\frac{\theta}{2}=\displaystyle\frac{\sqrt{5}\pm 1}{4}\quad\text{and}\quad\theta=\displaystyle\frac{2\pi}{5}\pm\displaystyle\frac{\pi}{5},

\displaystyle u=\displaystyle\frac{5\pm\sqrt{5}}{2}=4\sin^{2}\displaystyle\frac{\theta}{2}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad

\displaystyle\sin\displaystyle\frac{\theta}{2}=\sqrt{\displaystyle\frac{5\pm\sqrt{5}}{8}}\quad\text{and}\quad\theta=\displaystyle\frac{3\pi}{5}\pm\displaystyle\frac{\pi}{5},

\displaystyle u=2\pm\sqrt{3}=4\sin^{2}\displaystyle\frac{\theta}{2}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad

\displaystyle\sin\displaystyle\frac{\theta}{2}=\displaystyle\frac{\sqrt{2\pm\sqrt{3}}}{2}\quad\text{and}\quad\theta=\displaystyle\frac{\pi}{2}\pm\displaystyle\frac{\pi}{3},

\displaystyle u=2\pm\sqrt{2}=4\sin^{2}\displaystyle\frac{\theta}{2}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad

\displaystyle\sin\displaystyle\frac{\theta}{2}=\displaystyle\frac{\sqrt{2\pm\sqrt{2}}}{2}\quad\text{and}\quad\theta=\displaystyle\frac{\pi}{2}\pm\displaystyle\frac{\pi}{4}.

Therefore, (
2
) and (
2.8
)–(
2.9
) yield

\displaystyle\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\displaystyle\frac{L_{2n}}{n^{2}\binom{2n}{n}}\left(H_{2n}-H_{n-1}\right)

\displaystyle=2\zeta(3)-{\rm Cl}_{3}\big(\tfrac{3\pi}{5}\big)-{\rm Cl}_{3}\big(\tfrac{\pi}{5}\big)+\displaystyle\frac{4\pi^{2}}{25}\log(\phi)

\displaystyle=\displaystyle\frac{41\zeta(3)+4\pi^{2}\log(\phi)}{25},

\displaystyle\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\displaystyle\frac{v_{n}(5,5)}{n^{2}\binom{2n}{n}}\left(H_{2n}-H_{n-1}\right)

\displaystyle=2\zeta(3)-{\rm Cl}_{3}\big(\tfrac{2\pi}{5}\big)-{\rm Cl}_{3}\big(\tfrac{4\pi}{5}\big)+\displaystyle\frac{\pi^{2}\log\left(5^{5}\phi^{6}\right)}{50}

\displaystyle=\displaystyle\frac{124\zeta(3)+\pi^{2}\log\left(5^{5}\phi^{6}\right)}{50},

\displaystyle\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\displaystyle\frac{v_{n}(4,1)}{n^{2}\binom{2n}{n}}\left(H_{2n}-H_{n-1}\right)

\displaystyle=2\zeta(3)-{\rm Cl}_{3}\big(\tfrac{\pi}{6}\big)-{\rm Cl}_{3}\big(\tfrac{5\pi}{6}\big)+\displaystyle\frac{\pi^{2}\log(2+\sqrt{3})}{6}

\displaystyle=\displaystyle\frac{23\zeta(3)+2\pi^{2}\log(2+\sqrt{3})}{12},

\displaystyle\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\displaystyle\frac{v_{n}(4,2)}{n^{2}\binom{2n}{n}}\left(H_{2n}-H_{n-1}\right)

\displaystyle=2\zeta(3)-{\rm Cl}_{3}\big(\tfrac{\pi}{4}\big)-{\rm Cl}_{3}\big(\tfrac{3\pi}{4}\big)+\displaystyle\frac{\pi^{2}(4\log(2)+8\log(1+\sqrt{2}))}{64}

\displaystyle=\displaystyle\frac{259\zeta(3)+2\pi^{2}(4\log(2)+8\log(1+\sqrt{2}))}{128}.

This concludes the proof of Theorem
1.1
and this note.
