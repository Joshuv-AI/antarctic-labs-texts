Sun’s Three Conjectures on Apéry-like Sums Involving Harmonic Numbers
Ce Xu and Jianqiang Zhao[1mm] a. School of Mathematics and Statistics, Anhui Normal University, Wuhu 241002, PRCb. Department of Mathematics, The Bishop’s School, La Jolla, CA 92037, USA

Abstract




Abstract.
In this paper, we will give another proof of Zhi-Wei Sun’s three conjectures
on Apéry-like sums involving harmonic numbers by proving some identities among special
values of multiple polylogarithms.

Keywords
: Apéry-like sums, harmonic numbers, multiple polylogarithm function, iterated integrals.

AMS Subject Classifications (2020):
11M32, 11B65.

\displaystyle\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\displaystyle\frac{(-1)^{n-1}}{n^{3}\binom{2n}{n}}\left(10H_{n}-\displaystyle\frac{3}{n}\right)=\displaystyle\frac{\pi^{4}}{30},

\displaystyle\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\displaystyle\frac{1}{n^{2}\binom{2n}{n}}\left(3H_{n-1}^{2}+\displaystyle\frac{4}{n}H_{n-1}\right)=\displaystyle\frac{\pi^{4}}{360},

H_{0}:=0\quad\text{and}\quad H_{n}:=\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{n}\displaystyle\frac{1}{k}\quad\forall n\geq 1.

2
Proof of conjectures

The Riemann zeta values
\zeta(k)\ (2\leq k\in\mathbb{N})
are defined by

\displaystyle\zeta(k):=\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\displaystyle\frac{1}{n^{k}}.

(2.1)

In particular, Euler determined the explicit values of zeta values function at even integers in 1775:

\displaystyle\zeta(2k)=-\displaystyle\frac{B_{2k}}{2(2k)!}(2\pi\sqrt{-1})^{2k},

where
B_{n}
are Bernoulli numbers defined by the generating function

\displaystyle\frac{t}{e^{t}-1}=\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}B_{n}\displaystyle\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

These can be regarded as special values of the polylogarithm function
\Li_{k}(x)
. More generally,
for any
k_{1},\dotsc,k_{r}\in\mathbb{N}
, the
classical multiple polylogarithm function
is defined by

\displaystyle\Li_{k_{1},\dotsc,k_{r}}(x_{1},\dotsc,x_{r}):=\displaystyle\sum_{n_{1}>n_{2}>\cdots>n_{r}>0}\displaystyle\frac{x_{1}^{n_{1}}\dotsm x_{r}^{n_{r}}}{n_{1}^{k_{1}}\dotsm n_{r}^{k_{r}}}

which converges if
|x_{1}\cdots x_{j}|<1
for all
1\leq j\leq r
. In particular, for
|x|\leq 1
and
(x,k_{1})\neq(1,1)

\Li_{k_{1},\ldots,k_{r}}(x):=\Li_{k_{1},\ldots,k_{r}}(x,\underbrace{1,\ldots,1}_{r-1})=\displaystyle\sum_{n_{1}>n_{2}>\cdots>n_{r}>0}\displaystyle\frac{x^{n_{1}}}{n_{1}^{k_{1}}\dotsm n_{r}^{k_{r}}}

is the classical
single-variable
multiple polylogarithm function. In particular, if
x=1
then
\Li_{k_{1},\ldots,k_{r}}(1)
become the multiple zeta values
\zeta(k_{1},\ldots,k_{r})
, namely,
\zeta(k_{1},\ldots,k_{r}):=\Li_{k_{1},\ldots,k_{r}}(1)
.

Remark 2.5

In
