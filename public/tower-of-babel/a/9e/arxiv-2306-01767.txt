For each non-negative integer

\leq j

Theorem 1.1

Let

Theorem 1.2

Let

2.4
, there is a prime factor

that divides
c_{2n-\ell-1}
and
c_{2n-\ell-2}
unless either (i)
k=2
and

with some integer

n=13
. For the moment, suppose we are not in either of the situations described by (i) and (ii), and fix

dividing
c_{2n-\ell-1}
and

. Then
p\nmid c_{2n}

p|c_{i}

. Next, we show that the slope of the right-most edge of the

Proof of Theorem

.

1.3

1.4
