Abstract
. We consider compact locally symmetric spaces Γ \ G / H \Gamma\backslash G/H where G / H G/H is a non-compact semisimple symmetric space and Γ \Gamma is a discrete subgroup of G G . We discuss some features of the joint spectrum of the (commutative) algebra D ⁡ ( G / H ) D(G/H) of invariant differential operators acting, as unbounded operators, on the Hilbert space L 2 ​ ( Γ \ G / H ) L^{2}(\Gamma\backslash G/H) of square integrable complex functions on Γ \ G / H \Gamma\backslash G/H . In the case of the Lorentzian symmetric space S ​ O 0 ​ ( 2 , 2 ​ n ) / S ​ O 0 ​ ( 1 , 2 ​ n ) SO_{0}(2,2n)/SO_{0}(1,2n) , the representation theoretic spectrum is described explicitly. The strategy is to consider connected reductive Lie groups L L acting transitively and co-compactly on G / H G/H , a cocompact lattice Γ ⊂ L \Gamma\subset L , and study the spectrum of the algebra D ⁡ ( L / L ∩ H ) D(L/L\cap H) on L 2 ​ ( Γ \ L / L ∩ H ) L^{2}(\Gamma\backslash L/L\cap H) . Though the group G G does not act on L 2 ​ ( Γ \ G / H ) L^{2}(\Gamma\backslash G/H) , we explain how (not necessarily unitary) G G -representations enter into the spectral decomposition of D ⁡ ( G / H ) D(G/H) on L 2 ​ ( Γ \ G / H ) L^{2}(\Gamma\backslash G/H) and why one should expect a continuous contribution to the spectrum in some cases. As a byproduct, we obtain a result on the L L -admissibility of G G -representations. These notes contain the statements of the main results, the proofs and the details will appear elsewhere.

2.
Existence of compact Clifford-Klein forms and spherical triples.

When

\left.\begin{array}[]{lll}\text{existence of a closed connected

(ii)\text{ $L$ acts transitively on $G/H$}\\

\text{forms for $G/H$}\\
