5.2.
Homotopy fibers and pullbacks

For a map of pointed topological spaces

\mathrm{hofiber}(X\to Y):=\{(x,\alpha)\,|\,x\in X,\,\alpha\colon[0,1]\to Y,\text{ such that }\alpha(0)=f(x),\,\alpha(1)=*\}.

X\times_{Y}^{h}Z:=\{(x,\alpha,z)\,|\,x\in X,\,z\in Z,\,\alpha\colon[0,1]\to Y,\text{ such that }\alpha(0)=f(x),\,\alpha(1)=g(z)\}.

5.3.
\mathrm{O}_{m}\times\mathrm{O}_{n-m}
-action on loop spaces

In Subsection

we explained how

\mathrm{Emb}_{\partial}(D^{m},D^{n})
,
\mathrm{Emb}_{\partial}^{fr}(D^{m},D^{n})
,
\mathrm{tEmb}_{\partial}(D^{m},D^{n})
,

\mathrm{Emb}_{\partial D^{m}\times D^{n-m}}(D^{m}\times D^{n-m})

- or
R_{m+1}^{\mathrm{O}_{m}\times\mathrm{O}_{n-m}}
-structure.
We now explain how the corresponding structure appears in the loop spaces that we consider.

The May-Boardman-Vogt theorem says that an
E_{m}
-space

is
E_{m}
-equivalent to an
m
-loop space if and only if
\pi_{0}X
is a group
[
5
,
64
]
.
One has a similar result for
E_{m}^{\mathrm{O}_{m}}
-spaces
[
79
]
. Given a pointed space
Y
with an
\mathrm{O}_{m}
-action preserving the basepoint, the
loop space
\Omega^{m}Y
is naturally an
E_{m}^{\mathrm{O}_{m}}
-algebra, for which the
\mathrm{O}_{m}
-action is defined as follows

g\star f:=g\cdot f\circ g^{-1}:D^{m}\xrightarrow{g^{-1}}D^{m}\xrightarrow{f}Y\xrightarrow{g\cdot(-)}Y,

f\in\Omega^{m}Y
,
g\in\mathrm{O}_{m}
. The first named author and Wahl proved that an
E_{m}^{\mathrm{O}_{m}}
-algebra
X
is
E_{m}^{\mathrm{O}_{m}}
-equivalent to
\Omega^{m}Y

for some pointed
\mathrm{O}_{m}
-space
Y
if and only if
\pi_{0}X
is a group
[
79
]
.

In case
Y
is a pointed
\mathrm{O}_{m}\times\mathrm{O}_{n-m}
-space (the action is required to preserve the basepoint), then
\Omega^{m}Y
is an

E_{m}^{\mathrm{O}_{m}\times\mathrm{O}_{n-m}}
-algebra. The action of
\mathrm{O}_{n-m}
on
\Omega^{m}Y
is defined pointwise:

g\star f:=g\cdot f\colon D^{m}\xrightarrow{f}Y\xrightarrow{g\cdot(-)}Y,

f\in\Omega^{m}Y
,
g\in\mathrm{O}_{n-m}
.

\bullet
For example,
\Omega^{m}\mathrm{hofiber}(\mathrm{V}_{n,m}\to\mathrm{V}_{n,m}^{t})
is an
E_{m}^{\mathrm{O}_{m}\times\mathrm{O}_{n-m}}
-algebra, where
\mathrm{O}_{m}\times\mathrm{O}_{n-m}

acts on
\mathrm{V}_{n,m}=\mathrm{O}_{n}/\mathrm{O}_{n-m}
and on
\mathrm{V}_{n,m}^{t}=\mathrm{TOP}_{n}/\mathrm{TOP}_{n,m}
by conjugation, which is well-defined since

\mathrm{O}_{m}\times\mathrm{O}_{n-m}
lies in the normalizer

\mathrm{O}_{n-m}\subset\mathrm{TOP}_{n,m}

\mathrm{O}_{m}
-action by conjugation, and the
\mathrm{O}_{n-m}
-action by multiplication from the left. Note that this action preserves the basepoint – the constant inclusion

{\mathbb{R}}^{m}\subset{\mathbb{R}}^{n}
.

\bullet
In case

is a group or an associative unital monoid with
\mathrm{O}_{m}\times\mathrm{O}_{n-m}
acting on it by endomorphisms, the loop space
\Omega^{m}Y
becomes an

R_{m+1}^{\mathrm{O}_{m}\times\mathrm{O}_{n-m}}
-algebra. For example, that is the case of
\Omega^{m}\left(\mathrm{O}_{n}\times_{\mathrm{TOP}_{n}}^{h}\mathrm{TOP}_{n,m}\right)

,
\mathrm{TOP}_{n}

\mathrm{TOP}_{n,m}

5.4.
Embeddings modulo immersions spaces

In this section we explain precisely what simplicial sets are meant by

\overline{\mathrm{Diff}}_{\partial}(D^{n}):=\mathrm{hofiber}\left(\mathrm{Diff}_{\partial}(D^{n})\xrightarrow{D}\Omega^{n}O_{n}\right),

{\overline{\mathrm{Emb}}}_{\partial}(D^{m},D^{n}):=\mathrm{hofiber}\left(\mathrm{Emb}_{\partial}(D^{m},D^{n})\xrightarrow{D}\Omega^{m}V_{n,m}\right),

and

The

Similarly, the
