Weighted norm inequalities in Lebesgue spaces with Muckenhoupt
weights and some applications to operators

Abstract




Abstract
In the present work we give a simple method to obtain
weighted norm inequalities in Lebesgue spaces
L_{p,\gamma}
with
Muckenhoupt weights
\gamma
. This method is different from celebrated
Extrapolation or Interpolation Theory. In this method starting point is
uniform norm estimates of special form. Then a procedure give desired
weighted norm inequalities in
L_{p,\gamma}.
We apply this method to
obtain several convolution type inequalities. As an application we consider
a difference operator of type
\Delta_{v}^{r}:=\left(\mathbb{I}-\mathfrak{T}_{v}\right)^{r}
where
\mathbb{I}
is the identity operator,
r\in\mathbb{N}
and

\mathfrak{T}_{v}f\left(x\right):=\frac{1}{v}\int\nolimits_{x}^{x+v}f\left(t\right)dt,\quad x\in\left[-\pi,\pi\right],\text{\quad}v>0,\quad\mathfrak{T}_{0}:=\mathbb{I}.

We obtain main properties of
\Delta_{v}^{r}f
for functions
f
given in
L_{p,\gamma}
,
1\leq p<\infty
, with weights
\gamma
satisfying the
Muckenhoupt’s
A_{p}
condition. Also we consider some applications of
difference operator
\Delta_{v}^{r}
in these spaces. In particular, we
obtain that difference
\left\|\Delta_{v}^{r}f\right\|_{p,\gamma}

is a useful tool for computing the smoothness properties of functions
these spaces. It is obtained that
\left\|\Delta_{v}^{r}f\right\|_{p,\gamma}
is equivalent to Peetre’s
K
-functional.

1
Introduction

Let
\mathbb{T}:=\left[-\pi,\pi\right]
. A function
\gamma:\mathbb{T\rightarrow}\left[0,\infty\right]
will be called weight if
\gamma

is measurable and positive a.e. on
\mathbb{T}
. We denote
\gamma\left(A\right):=\int\nolimits_{A}\gamma\left(u\right)du
for a measurable set
J\subseteqq\mathbb{T}
. An integrable, 2
\pi
-periodic weight function
\gamma,
defined on
\mathbb{T},
satisfies Muckenhoupt’s
A_{1}
condition
(briefly
\gamma\in A_{1}
) if

\left[\gamma\right]_{1}:=\sup\limits_{J\subseteqq\mathbb{T}}\frac{\gamma\left(J\right)}{mes(J)}\underset{x\in J}{ess}\sup\left(\gamma\left(x\right)\right)^{-1}<\infty;

satisfy the Muckenhoupt’s
A_{p}
,
1<p<\infty
, condition (briefly
\gamma\in A_{p}
) if

\left[\gamma\right]_{p}:=\sup\limits_{J\subseteqq\mathbb{T}}\frac{\gamma\left(J\right)}{mes(J)}\left(\frac{1}{mes(J)}\int\nolimits_{J}\left[\gamma\left(u\right)\right]^{\frac{-1}{p-1}}du\right)^{p-1}<\infty\text{,\quad}\left(1<p<\infty\right)

holds with some (Muckenhoupt) constant
\left[\gamma\right]_{p}

independent of
J
.

For example, (i)
\left|x\right|^{\alpha}\in A_{p}
iff
-1<\alpha<p-1
; (ii)
\left|\sin\theta\right|^{\alpha}\in A_{p}
iff
-1<\alpha<p-1
;  (iii) Densities of harmonic measures in
relation to the Lebesgue surface measure on the boundaries of sufficiently
regular domains satisfy the condition
A_{p}.
Some other examples are given
e.g. in the article
[
13
, p.2097]
.

For a weight
\gamma
on
\mathbb{T}
, we denote by
L_{p,\gamma}
,
1\leq p<\infty
the class of real valued measurable functions, defined on
\mathbb{T},
such that

\int\nolimits_{\mathbb{T}}\left|f\left(x\right)\right|^{p}\gamma\left(x\right)dx<\infty\text{ for }1\leq p<\infty.

For
f\in L_{p,\gamma}
,
1\leq p<\infty
we set

\left\|f\right\|_{p,\gamma}^{p}:=\int\nolimits_{\mathbb{T}}\left|f\left(x\right)\right|^{p}\gamma\left(x\right)dx.

When
\gamma\equiv 1
we will set
L_{p}:=L_{p,1}
. Let
C\left(\mathbb{T}\right)
be the collection of continuous functions
f:\mathbb{T}\rightarrow\mathbb{R}
with
\|f\|_{C\left(\mathbb{T}\right)}:=\max\left\{\left|f\left(x\right)\right|:x\in\mathbb{T}\right\}<\infty.

If
p\in[1,\infty)
and
\gamma\in A_{p}
, then embeddings

C\left(\mathbb{T}\right)\hookrightarrow L_{p,\gamma}\hookrightarrow L_{1},

hold, because

\displaystyle\left\|\cdot\right\|_{1}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\left[\gamma\right]_{p}^{\frac{1}{p}}\left\|\gamma\right\|_{1}^{-\frac{1}{p}}\left\|\cdot\right\|_{p,\gamma}\text{ for }f\in L_{p,\gamma}\text{, and}

(1)

\displaystyle\left\|\cdot\right\|_{p,\gamma}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\left\|\gamma\right\|_{1}^{\frac{1}{p}}\left\|\cdot\right\|_{\infty}\text{,
for }f\in C\left(\mathbb{T}\right).

(2)

Rest of the paper is organized as follows. The next subsection contain a

To obtain weighted norm inequality

2.1
Weighted Convolution Inequalities

Let
\lambda\geq 1
,
k_{\lambda}=k_{\lambda}(x)
be 2
\pi
-periodic
function defined on
\mathbb{T}
, such that

\int_{\mathbb{T}}k_{\lambda}(x)dx\leq C_{1}<\infty.

(10)

We define the class of operators
K_{\lambda}f\left(x\right):=\int\limits_{\mathbb{T}}f(x-t)k_{\lambda}(t)dt
for
\lambda\geq 1
.
Then class of operators
\{K_{\lambda}f\}_{1\leq\lambda<\infty}
is
uniformly bounded (in
\lambda
) in
L_{p,\gamma}
for
1\leq p<\infty

and
\gamma\in A_{p}.

Theorem 12

Let
\lambda>0
,
k_{\lambda}=k_{\lambda}(x)
be 2
\pi
-periodic function defined on
\mathbb{T}
, such that (
10
) to hold.
We suppose that
\gamma
is a 2
\pi
-periodic weight on
\mathbb{T}
so
that
\gamma
is belong to the class
A_{p}
,
1\leq p<\infty
. Then,

(i)
F_{K_{\lambda}f}\left(\cdot\right)=K_{\lambda}F_{f}\left(\cdot\right)
and

(ii) the class of operators
\{K_{\lambda}f\}_{1\leq\lambda<\infty}
is
uniformly bounded (in
\lambda
) in
L_{p,\gamma}
, namely,

\left\|K_{\lambda}f\right\|_{p,\gamma}\leq 24\mathbf{c}_{0}\mathbf{c}_{0}^{\prime\prime}\mathbf{c}_{0}^{\prime\prime\prime}C_{1}\left\|f\right\|_{p,\gamma}.

Specific examples of kernels satisfying the conditions (
10
) are among
others, Steklov , Poisson , Cesàro , Jackson and Fejér kernels.

Proof of Theorem
12
.

(i) For any
u\in\mathbb{T},

\displaystyle F_{K_{\lambda}f}\left(u\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int\limits_{\mathbb{T}}K_{\lambda}f\left(x+u\right)G\left(x\right)dx

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int\limits_{\mathbb{T}}\int\limits_{\mathbb{T}}f(x+u-t)k_{\lambda}(t)dtG\left(x\right)dx

=\int\limits_{\mathbb{T}}\int\limits_{\mathbb{T}}f(x+u-t)G\left(x\right)dxk_{\lambda}(t)dt

=\int\limits_{\mathbb{T}}F_{f}\left(u-t\right)k_{\lambda}(t)dt=K_{\lambda}F_{f}\left(u\right).

(ii) Clearly

\left\|K_{\lambda}g\right\|_{C\left(\mathbb{T}\right)}=\left\|\int\limits_{\mathbb{T}}g(x-t)k_{\lambda}(t)dt\right\|_{C\left(\mathbb{T}\right)}\leq C_{1}\left\|g\right\|_{C\left(\mathbb{T}\right)}

for any
g\in C\left(\mathbb{T}\right)
.

Using (i) we obtain

\left\|F_{K_{\lambda}f}\right\|_{C\left(\mathbb{T}\right)}=\left\|K_{\lambda}F_{f}\right\|_{C\left(\mathbb{T}\right)}\leq C_{1}\left\|F_{f}\right\|_{C\left(\mathbb{T}\right)}.

Now Theorem
10
give

\left\|K_{\lambda}f\right\|_{p,\gamma}\leq 24\mathbf{c}_{0}\mathbf{c}_{0}^{\prime\prime}\mathbf{c}_{0}^{\prime\prime\prime}C_{1}\left\|f\right\|_{p,\gamma}

as required.

=C_{5}v^{r-2}v\left\|\frac{d^{r-1}}{dx^{r-1}}R_{v}^{r-1}\left(\mathbb{I}-\mathfrak{T}_{v}\right)f\right\|_{p,\gamma}

=C_{5}v^{r-2}v\left\|\frac{d}{dx}R_{v}\frac{d^{r-2}}{dx^{r-2}}R_{v}^{r-2}\left(\mathbb{I}-\mathfrak{T}_{v}\right)f\right\|_{p,\gamma}

\leq C_{5}^{2}v^{r-2}\left\|\left(\mathbb{I}-\mathfrak{T}_{v}\right)\frac{d^{r-2}}{dx^{r-2}}R_{v}^{r-2}\left(\mathbb{I}-\mathfrak{T}_{v}\right)f\right\|_{p,\gamma}

=C_{5}^{2}v^{r-2}\left\|\frac{d^{r-2}}{dx^{r-2}}R_{v}^{r-2}\left(\mathbb{I}-\mathfrak{T}_{v}\right)^{2}f\right\|_{p,\gamma}

\leq\cdots\leq C_{5}^{r-1}v\left\|\frac{d}{dx}R_{v}\left(\mathbb{I}-\mathfrak{T}_{v}\right)^{r-1}f\right\|_{p,\gamma}\leq C_{5}^{r}\left\|\left(\mathbb{I}-\mathfrak{T}_{v}\right)^{r}f\right\|_{p,\gamma}.

Thus

K_{r}\left(f,v,p,\gamma\right)_{p,\gamma}\leq\left\|f-A_{v}^{r}f\right\|_{p,\gamma}+v^{r}\left\|\frac{d^{r}}{dx^{r}}A_{v}^{r}f\left(x\right)\right\|_{p,\gamma}

\leq 2\max\left\{C_{4}^{r},C_{5}^{r}\right\}\left\|\left(\mathbb{I}-\mathfrak{T}_{v}\right)^{r}f\right\|_{p,\gamma}.
