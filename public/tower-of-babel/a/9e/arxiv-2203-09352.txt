Appendix to Discrete Localities II: p p -local compact groups as localities
Andrew Chermak and Alex Gonzalez

Abstract




Our aim in this appendix is to show that the
p
-local compact groups, introduced in [BLO2] and
further developed in [BLO3], [JLL] and elsewhere, may be viewed as proper localities of a certain kind
- to be called compact localities. As a corollary to a result of Ran Levi and Assaf Libman [LL] (and
with an improvement due to Remi Molinier [M]), we will show that if
F
is the fusion system of a
compact locality
(L,\Delta,S)
then, up to an isomorphism of partial groups which restricts to an
automorphism of
S
,
L
is the unique compact locality on
F
having
\Delta
as its set of objects.
This result, and the formalism of compact localities, provide a bridge between the theory being developed
in this series and a more well-established theory having a much more homotopy-theoretic flavor.

We shall be closely following the arguments in the appendix
to [Ch1], where an equivalence was established between the “
p
-local finite groups”
introduced in [BLO1], and proper, finite, centric localities.

Theorem A.25

Let
(L,\Delta,S)
and

be compact localities on

the same set of objects. Then there exists an isomorphism

of partial groups,

\Delta
is equal to the set

of
F
-centric subgroups of
S

(T_{\Delta}(S)@>\epsilon>>T@>\rho>>F)

p

Following [BLO2] (but with notation which reflects our preference for right-hand composition of morphisms),
