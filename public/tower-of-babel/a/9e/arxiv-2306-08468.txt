Liouville theorem for ϕ \phi - F F -symphonic map , ϕ \phi - F F -harmonic map and and ϕ \phi - Φ S , p , ε \Phi_{S,p,\varepsilon} harmonic map with free boundary

Abstract
In this paper, we obtained Liouville theorem for ϕ \phi - F F -symphonic map , ϕ \phi - F F -harmonic map and ϕ \phi - Φ S , p , ε \Phi_{S,p,\varepsilon} harmonic map with free boundary on metric measure space.
