Dependent measures in independent theories
Karim Khanaki

Abstract
We introduce the notion of dependence , as a property of a Keisler measure, and generalize several results of [ HPS13 ] on generically stable measures (in N ​ I ​ P NIP theories) to arbitrary theories. Among other things, we show that this notion is very natural and fundamental for several reasons: (i) all measures in N ​ I ​ P NIP theories are dependent, (ii) all types and all f ​ i ​ m fim measures in any theory are dependent, and (iii) as a crucial result in measure theory, the Glivenko-Cantelli class of functions (formulas) is characterized by dependent measures.

(2): (Right notion/presentation) We note that two properties have made the results known of model theory in this article generalizable: If
