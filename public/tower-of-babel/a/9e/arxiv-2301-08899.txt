We say that a

-condition, and we denote by
\Phi\in(\Delta_{2})

\Phi(2t)\leq K\Phi(t),\penalty\ \penalty\ \forall t\geq t_{0}.

In the case of
|\Omega|=+\infty

If
\Omega

N
can be a natural number such that
N\geq 1

\Phi
a
N
-function then define the Orlicz space associated with

L^{\Phi}(\Omega)=\left\{u\in L^{1}_{\text{loc}}(\Omega):\penalty\ \int_{\Omega}\Phi\left(\frac{|u|}{\lambda}\right)dx<+\infty\penalty\ \text{for some}\penalty\ \lambda>0\right\}.

The space

is a Banach space endowed with the Luxemburg norm given by

\|u\|_{L^{\Phi}(\Omega)}=\inf\left\{\lambda>0:\int_{\Omega}\Phi\left(\frac{|u|}{\lambda}\right)dx\leq 1\right\}.

In the case that

L^{\Phi}(\Omega)=\left\{u\in L^{1}_{\text{loc}}(\Omega):\penalty\ \int_{\Omega}\Phi(|u|)dx<+\infty\right\}.

The complementary function

\tilde{\Phi}(s)=\max_{t\geq 0}\{st-\Phi(t)\},\penalty\ \penalty\ \forall\;t\geq 0.

The functions
\Phi

are complementary to each other and satisfy the inequality below

\tilde{\Phi}(\Phi^{\prime}(t))\leq\Phi(2t),\;\;\forall\;t>0.

Moreover, we also have a Young type inequality given by

st\leq\Phi(t)+\tilde{\Phi}(s),\penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \forall s,t\geq 0.

Using the above inequality, it is possible to establish the following Holder type inequality:

\left|\int_{\Omega}uvdx\right|\leq 2\|u\|_{L^{\Phi}(\Omega)}\|v\|_{{L^{\tilde{\Phi}}(\Omega)}},\penalty\ \penalty\ \text{for all}\penalty\ \penalty\ u\in L^{\Phi}(\Omega)\penalty\ \penalty\ \text{and}\penalty\ \penalty\ v\in L^{\tilde{\Phi}}(\Omega).
