Exponential stability for a nonlinear porous-elastic system with delay
M.J. Dos Santos, C.A. Raposo, L.G.R. Miranda and B. Feng

Abstract
In this work, we consider the existence of a global solution and the exponential decay of a nonlinear porous elastic system with time delay. The nonlinear term, as well as the delay acting in the equation of the volume fraction. In order to obtain the existence and uniqueness of a global solution, we will use the semigroup theory of linear operators and under a certain relation involving the coefficients of the system together with a Lyapunov functional, we will establish the exponential decay of the energy associated to the system.

\mathcal{L}(t):=ME(t)+\frac{\sqrt{\xi}}{8}\beta I_{1}(t)+NI_{2}(t)+I_{3}(t)+\frac{J\delta}{4\varepsilon}\int_{0}^{1}q\phi_{t}\phi_{x}dx+\frac{\rho\varepsilon}{\mu}\int_{0}^{1}qu_{t}u_{x}dx+I_{4}(t)

Proof 4.13
.

We have

\displaystyle|\mathcal{L}(t)-ME(t)|\leq{}

(103)

\displaystyle{}+N\bigg|\int_{0}^{1}(J\phi_{t}\phi+\rho u_{t}w)dx+\frac{\mu_{1}}{2}\int_{0}^{1}\phi^{2}dx\bigg|

\displaystyle{}+\bigg|J\int_{0}^{1}\bigg(\frac{b}{\sqrt{\xi}}u_{x}+\sqrt{\xi}\phi\bigg)\phi_{t}dx+\frac{bJ}{\sqrt{\xi}}\int_{0}^{1}\phi_{x}u_{t}dx\bigg|

By using Young’s and Poincaré’s inequalities, (

\displaystyle\frac{\sqrt{\xi}}{8}\beta\bigg|-\int_{0}^{1}(\rho uu_{t}+J\phi\phi_{t})dx-\frac{\mu_{1}}{2}\int_{0}^{1}\phi^{2}dx\bigg|\leq{}

\displaystyle{}\frac{\sqrt{\xi}\beta\rho}{16}\int_{0}^{1}u_{t}^{2}dx+\frac{\sqrt{\xi}\beta J}{16}\int_{0}^{1}\phi_{t}^{2}dx

\displaystyle+\bigg[\frac{\sqrt{\xi}\beta c_{p}}{16}(J+\mu)+\frac{\xi^{5/2}\beta\rho c_{p}^{2}}{8b^{2}}\bigg]\int_{0}^{1}\phi_{x}^{2}dx

\displaystyle+\frac{3\xi^{3/2}\beta\rho c_{p}}{16b^{2}}\int_{0}^{1}\bigg(\frac{b}{\sqrt{\xi}}u_{x}+\sqrt{\xi}\phi\bigg)^{2}dx,

\displaystyle N\bigg|\int_{0}^{1}(J\phi_{t}\phi+\rho u_{t}w)dx+\frac{\mu_{1}}{2}\int_{0}^{1}\phi^{2}dx\bigg|\leq{}

\displaystyle{}\frac{N\rho}{2}\int_{0}^{1}u_{t}^{2}dx+\frac{NJ}{2}\int_{0}^{1}\phi_{t}^{2}dx

(105)

\displaystyle+\frac{Nc_{p}}{2}\bigg(J+\mu_{1}+\frac{\rho\xi c_{p}}{\mu}\bigg)\int_{0}^{1}\phi_{x}^{2}dx,

\displaystyle\bigg|J\int_{0}^{1}\bigg(\frac{b}{\sqrt{\xi}}u_{x}+\sqrt{\xi}\phi\bigg)\phi_{t}dx+\frac{bJ}{\sqrt{\xi}}\int_{0}^{1}\phi_{x}u_{t}dx\bigg|\leq{}

\displaystyle{}\frac{Jb^{2}}{2}\int_{0}^{1}u_{t}^{2}dx+\frac{J}{2}\int_{0}^{1}\phi_{t}^{2}dx

(106)

\displaystyle+\frac{J}{2}\int_{0}^{1}\bigg(\frac{b}{\sqrt{\xi}}u_{x}+\sqrt{\xi}\phi\bigg)^{2}dx

\displaystyle+\frac{J}{2\xi}\int_{0}^{1}\phi_{x}^{2}dx,

\displaystyle\frac{J\delta}{4\varepsilon}\int_{0}^{1}|q\phi_{t}\phi_{x}|dx\leq{}

\displaystyle{}\frac{J\delta}{4\varepsilon}\int_{0}^{1}\phi_{t}^{2}dx+\frac{J\delta}{4\varepsilon}\int_{0}^{1}\phi_{x}^{2}dx,

(107)

\displaystyle{}\frac{\rho\varepsilon}{\mu}\int_{0}^{1}u_{t}^{2}dx+\frac{2\rho\xi^{2}c_{p}\varepsilon}{\mu b^{2}}\int_{0}^{1}\phi_{x}^{2}dx

(108)

\displaystyle+\frac{3\rho\xi\varepsilon}{\mu b^{2}}\int_{0}^{1}\bigg(\frac{b}{\sqrt{\xi}}u_{x}+\sqrt{\xi}\phi\bigg)^{2}dx.

103
)-(
108
), we have

|\mathcal{L}(t)-ME(t)|\leq\alpha_{1}\frac{\rho}{2}\int_{0}^{1}u_{t}^{2}dx+\alpha_{2}\frac{J}{2}\int_{0}^{1}\phi_{t}^{2}dx+\alpha_{3}\frac{\delta}{2}\int_{0}^{1}\phi_{x}^{2}dx\\
+\alpha_{4}\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{1}\bigg(\frac{b}{\sqrt{\xi}}u_{x}+\sqrt{\xi}\phi\bigg)^{2}dx+\alpha_{5}\frac{\eta}{2}\int_{0}^{1}\int_{0}^{1}z^{2}(x,y,t)dydx

where

\displaystyle=\frac{\sqrt{\xi}\beta}{8}+N+\frac{Jb^{2}}{\rho}+\frac{2\varepsilon}{\mu},

\displaystyle\alpha_{2}

\displaystyle\alpha_{3}
