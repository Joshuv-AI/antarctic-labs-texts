Monomial realizations and LS paths of fundamental representations for rank 2 2 Kac-Moody algebras
YUKI KANAKUBO

Abstract
For a Kac-Moody algebra 𝔤 \mathfrak{g} of rank 2 2 and a fundamental weight λ \lambda , we explicitly give an isomorphism between the set of Lakshmibai-Seshadri paths 𝔹 ⁡ ( λ ) \mathbb{B}(\lambda) and monomial realization ℳ ⁡ ( λ ) \mathcal{M}(\lambda) . As an application, we also give an explicit form of monomial realization ℳ ⁡ ( λ ) \mathcal{M}(\lambda) in terms of Weyl groups.

Introduction

For a symmetrizable Kac-Moody algebra
\mathfrak{g}
, the crystal bases powerful combinatorial tools to study
representations of quantum groups
U_{q}(\mathfrak{g})

[
Kas90
]
. A bunch of
combinatorial descriptions of crystal bases have been developed.

In

In particular, monomial realizations for rank

]

In

Lit95
]
, a piece-wise linear path
\pi:[0,1]\rightarrow P\otimes\mathbb{R}
called

is a weight lattice.
The endpoint

.
One can define

and

on LS paths for

\lambda
denoted by

has a crystal structure by wt,

,

and some

\varepsilon
and

It is shown in
[

,
Jos
]
that

B(\lambda)
.

In this paper, we will explicitly give an isomorphism between the set of LS paths
\mathbb{B}(\lambda)

and monomial realization
\mathcal{M}(\lambda)

\mathfrak{g}

is of rank

and
\lambda

The paper is organized as follows.
In Sect.2, we review crystals. In Sect.3 and Sect.4, we review LS path models and monomial realizations.
We give our main results in Sect.5 and prove it in Sect.6.

\in I

2.2
Crystals

Definition 2.1
.

[

]

A set

•

.

Let

(1)

A map

3.1
Definition of LS paths

Following
[

]

For

Let

\lambda

The path
\pi

\pi:[0,1]\rightarrow P_{\mathbb{R}}

\pi(t):=\sum_{i=1}^{j-1}(a_{i}-a_{i-1})\tau_{i}(\lambda)+(t-a_{j-1})\tau_{j}(\lambda)\quad\text{for }a_{j-1}\leq t\leq a_{j},

where
P_{\mathbb{R}}:=P\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}}\mathbb{R}
. Let
h_{k}:[0,1]\rightarrow\mathbb{R}
be a function
defined as
t\mapsto\langle h_{k},\pi(t)\rangle

k\in I
.

For
\tau,\sigma\in W/W_{\lambda}
such that
\tau>\sigma
and

such that

,
one defines an
a
-chain for the pair

\kappa_{0}:=\tau>\kappa_{1}:=s_{\beta_{1}}\tau>\kappa_{2}:=s_{\beta_{2}}s_{\beta_{1}}\tau>\cdots>\kappa_{s}:=s_{\beta_{s}}\cdots s_{\beta_{1}}\tau=\sigma,

where

\beta_{1},\cdots,\beta_{s}
are positive real roots satisfying for each
i=1,\cdots,s
,

l(\kappa_{i})=l(\kappa_{i-1})-1\quad\text{and }a\langle\beta_{i}^{\vee},\kappa_{i}(\lambda)\rangle\in\mathbb{Z}.

.

4
Monomial Realizations

Following
[
Kas03
]
, we recall the notion of monomial realizations for crystal bases of highest weight representations.
One defines a crystal structure on the set of Laurent monomials

\displaystyle{\mathcal{Y}}:=\left\{X=\prod\limits_{s\in\mathbb{Z},\ i\in I}X_{s,i}^{\zeta_{s,i}}\,\Bigg|\,\zeta_{s,i}\in\mathbb{Z},\ \zeta_{s,i}=0\text{ except for finitely many }(s,i)\right\}.

For
X=\prod\limits_{s\in\mathbb{Z},\;i\in I}X_{s,i}^{\zeta_{s,i}}\in{\mathcal{Y}}
, we define

\text{wt}(X):=\sum\limits_{i,s}\zeta_{s,i}\Lambda_{i}\in P_{\rm cl}
and

\displaystyle\varphi_{i}(X):=\max\left\{\!\sum\limits_{k\leq s}\zeta_{k,i}\,|\,s\in\mathbb{Z}\!\right\},\!\quad\varepsilon_{i}(X):=\varphi_{i}(X)-\text{wt}(X)(h_{i}).

Next, we put

A_{s,k}:=X_{s,k}X_{s+1,k}\prod\limits_{j\neq k,\ a_{j,k}<0}X_{s+p_{j,k},j}^{a_{j,k}},\ \ \ (s\in\mathbb{Z},\ k\in I)

where
(a_{j,k})
is a generalized Cartan matrix of
\mathfrak{g}
and the integers
\{p_{j,k}\}_{j,k\in I;a_{j,k<0}}
are defined as

p_{j,k}=\begin{cases}1&\text{if }k>j,\\
0&\text{if }j>k.\end{cases}
