A 𝟑 × 𝟑 \boldsymbol{3\times 3} Lax Form for the 𝒒 \boldsymbol{q} -Painlevé Equation of Type 𝑬 𝟔 \boldsymbol{E_{6}}
Kanam PARK

Abstract
For the q q -Painlevé equation with affine Weyl group symmetry of type E 6 ( 1 ) E_{6}^{(1)} , a 2 × 2 2\times 2 matrix Lax form and a second order scalar lax form were known. We give a new 3 × 3 3\times 3 matrix Lax form and a third order scalar equation related to it. Continuous limit is also discussed.

Introduction

The

-Painlevé equation with affine Weyl group symmetry of type

\displaystyle\frac{\big(\overline{f}g-1\big)(fg-1)}{f\overline{f}}=\frac{(g-1/a_{5})(g-1/a_{6})(g-1/a_{7})(g-1/a_{8})}{(g-a_{3})(g-a_{4})},
