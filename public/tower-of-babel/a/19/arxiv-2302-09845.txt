Optical potential for incident and emitted low-energy α \alpha particles. III. Non-statistical processes induced by neutrons on Zr, Nb, and Mo nuclei
M. Avrigeanu, V. Avrigeanu

Abstract
Background: The reliability of a previous α \alpha -particle optical-model potential (OMP) on nuclei with mass number 45 ≤ \leq A A ≤ \leq 209 was proved for emitted α \alpha particles as well, for proton–induced reactions on Zn isotopes [Phys. Rev. C 91 , 064611 (2015), Paper I]. However, the same was not the case of neutrons on Zr stable isotopes [Phys. Rev. C 96 , 044610 (2017), Paper II]. Purpose: A recent assessment of this potential also for nucleon–induced α \alpha -emission on A A ∼ \sim 60 nuclei, including pickup direct reaction and eventual Giant Quadrupole Resonance (GQR) α \alpha -emission, is completed for neutrons incident on Zr, Nb, and Mo stable isotopes. Methods: Consistent sets of input parameters, determined through analysis of independent data, is involved while no further empirical rescaling factors of the γ \gamma and nucleon widths have been involved. Results: A suitable account of all competitive reaction channels is confirmed by careful uncertainty analysis, to avoid parameter ambiguities and/or error compensation. Additional validation of this potential is also supported by recently measured ( α , γ ) (\alpha,\gamma) and ( α , n ) (\alpha,n) cross–sections of Zr and Mo nuclei. Conclusions : An increase of the α \alpha -emission beyond the statistical predictions, through consideration of additional reaction channels of the pickup direct interaction and like –GQR decay, makes possible the description of both absorption and emission of α \alpha particles by the same optical potential.

Introduction

The reliability of a previous
\alpha
-particle optical-model potential (OMP) for target nuclei with mass number 45
\leq
A
\leq
209
Avrigeanu et al. 2014
was also proved for
\alpha
-emission in proton–induced reactions on Zn isotopes
Avrigeanu and Avrigeanu 2015
(Paper I).
However, its use led to underestimated predictions of the statistical Hauser-Feshbach (HF)
Hauser and Feshbach 1952
and pre-equilibrium emission (PE)
Gadioli and Hodgson 1992
models for neutrons incident on Zr stable isotopes
Avrigeanu and Avrigeanu 2017
(Paper II).
On the other hand, it has recently been shown that this potential can also describe
\alpha
-emission from excited nuclei in nucleon-induced reactions within the
A
\sim
60 range
Avrigeanu and Avrigeanu 2021
;
Avrigeanu and Avrigeanu 2022
.
This was possible through additional consideration of the pickup direct reaction (DR) and eventual Giant Quadrupole Resonance (GQR)
\alpha
-emission.
The so-called
\alpha
-potential mystery
Rauscher 2013
related to the account of both
\alpha
-emission and absorption by an OMP, of equal interest for astrophysics and fusion technology, thus received an alternate solution.
Nevertheless, its support by analysis of more data is imperative, while the case of neutrons incident on
A
\sim
90 nuclei
Avrigeanu and Avrigeanu 2017

Thus, the same analysis for the stable isotopes of neighboring elements Zr, Nb, and Mo becomes challenging, with Zr nuclei within both incident and emergent reaction channels.
The
\alpha
-emission in neutron–induced reactions on stable Mo isotopes was targeted in an earlier systematic investigation up to 20 MeV
Reimer et al. 2005
.
However, an
\alpha
-particle OMP
Avrigeanu et al. 1994

A
\sim
60 compound–nuclei (CN)
\alpha
-particle decay led to a significant underestimation at incident energies around
\leq
10 MeV for
92,98
Mo.
This OMP makes predictions that differ significantly from potentials for incident
\alpha

Avrigeanu et al. 2014
;
Avrigeanu et al. 2003
;
Avrigeanu et al. 2006
;
Avrigeanu et al. 2009
;
Avrigeanu and Avrigeanu 2010
.
The discrepancy in results corresponding to OMPs
Avrigeanu et al. 1994
;
Avrigeanu et al. 2003
led even to the assumption of a nuclear–density distribution dependent on nuclear temperature
Avrigeanu et al. 2006
.

The removal of compensation effects of less accurate model parameters is essential to establishing an appropriate
\alpha
-particle OMP
Avrigeanu et al. 2014
;
Avrigeanu et al. 2003
;
Avrigeanu et al. 2006
;
Avrigeanu et al. 2009
;
Avrigeanu and Avrigeanu 2010
or to prove it
Avrigeanu and Avrigeanu 2016
;
Avrigeanu and Avrigeanu 2019
.
This aim has been achieved using ”consistent sets of input parameters, determined through analysis of independent data available in this mass region”

Moreover, a proper account of all competitive reaction channels, beyond the
\alpha

So, there have been avoided empirical rescaling factors of the
\gamma
