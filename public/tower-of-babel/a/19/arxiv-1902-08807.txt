Explicit expressions for real roots of a quartic equation
Nino Krvavica

Abstract




This short article presents explicit expressions for roots of a quartic equation that has all four real roots. Although a general expression for quartic roots is available on Wikipedia
[
4
]
, an optimized and slightly shorter expression for only real roots is presented here. A derivation of closed-form solutions for real roots of a quartic presented in the first part of this paper is taken from
[
2
]
. It is repeated here for completeness and clarity.

Let us consider a general normalized 4th order polynomial equation (quartic)

x^{4}+ax^{3}+bx^{2}+cx+d=0.

(1)

To find the analytical solution to roots of Eq. (
1
), first the cubic term
x^{3}
is eliminated and the general polynomial is converted into a so-called
depressed quartic
by a change of variables. Following Ferrari’s method
[
1
]
, a substitution
x=y-a/4
is introduced, which gives a depressed polynomial

y^{4}+py^{2}+qy+r=0,

(2)

where

p=b-6\left(\frac{a}{4}\right)^{2},

(3)

q=c-2b\left(\frac{a}{4}\right)+8\left(\frac{a}{4}\right)^{3},

(4)

r=d-c\left(\frac{a}{4}\right)+b\left(\frac{a}{4}\right)^{2}-3\left(\frac{a}{4}\right)^{4}.

(5)

The depressed polynomial can be rewritten as

\left(y^{2}+\frac{p}{2}\right)^{2}=-qy+\frac{p^{2}}{4}-r.

(6)

Next, expression
2zy^{2}+zp+z^{2}
is added to both sides of Eq. (
6
), which after some regrouping gives

\left(y^{2}+\frac{p}{2}+z\right)^{2}=2zy^{2}-qy+z^{2}+zp+\frac{p^{2}}{4}-r.

(7)

When
z
is chosen to be any non-zero root
z_{0}
of the so-called
resolvent cubic
equation

8z^{3}+8pz^{2}+(2p^{2}-8r)z-q^{2}=0,

(8)

the right-hand side of Eq. (
7
) can be written as a perfect square; therefore, Eq. (
7
) becomes

\left(y^{2}+\frac{p}{2}+z_{0}\right)^{2}=\left(y\sqrt{2z_{0}}-\frac{q}{2\sqrt{2z_{0}}}\right)^{2}.

(9)

And finally, Eq. (
9
) can be written as a factorized quadratic equation

\left(y^{2}+\sqrt{2z_{0}}y+\frac{p}{2}+z_{0}-\frac{q}{2\sqrt{2z_{0}}}\right)\left(y^{2}-\sqrt{2z_{0}}y+\frac{p}{2}+z_{0}+\frac{q}{2\sqrt{2z_{0}}}\right)=0,

(10)

which is easily solved by a quadratic formula.

Therefore, the solutions to the roots of the general quartic Eq. (
1
) are given by

x_{1,2}=-\frac{a}{4}-\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{2z_{0}}\pm\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{-\left(2p+2z_{0}-\frac{2q}{\sqrt{2z_{0}}}\right)},

(11)

x_{3,4}=-\frac{a}{4}+\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{2z_{0}}\pm\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{-\left(2p+2z_{0}+\frac{2q}{\sqrt{2z_{0}}}\right)}.

(12)

For a general normalized 3rd order polynomial equation (cubic)

x^{3}+\alpha x^{2}+\beta x+\gamma=0,

(13)

a real solution is given by Cardano’s formula
[
1
]

x_{0}=s_{1}+s_{2}-\frac{\alpha}{3},

(14)

with

s_{1}=\sqrt[3]{R+\sqrt{R^{2}+Q^{3}}},

(15)

s_{2}=\sqrt[3]{R-\sqrt{R^{2}+Q^{3}}},

(16)

where

Q=\frac{3\beta-\alpha^{2}}{9},

(17)

R=\frac{9\alpha\beta-27\gamma-2\alpha^{3}}{54}.

(18)

To eliminate redundant divisions and optimize computation of Eq. (
11
) and (
12
), the root of the resolvent cubic equation is expressed via

2z_{0}=\frac{1}{3}\left(S+\frac{\Delta_{0}}{S}-2p\right),

(19)

where

S=6s=\sqrt[3]{\frac{\Delta_{1}+\textrm{sign}(\Delta_{1})\sqrt{\Delta_{1}^{2}-4\Delta_{0}^{3}}}{2}},

(20)

\Delta_{0}=-36Q=b^{2}+12d-3ac,

(21)

\Delta_{1}=432R=27a^{2}d-9abc+2b^{3}-72bd+27c^{2}.

(22)

Note that
\Delta_{1}^{2}-4\Delta_{0}^{3}
, which is a much simpler expression for the discriminant of the resolvent cubic equation
\mathcal{D}_{cubic}
and especially the discriminant of the quartic equation
\mathcal{D}_{quartic}
. Therefore, if
\Delta_{1}^{2}-4\Delta_{0}^{3}<0
, three resolvent cubic roots are all real and the quartic roots are either all complex or all real.
In this case, Eq. (
19
) can be solved trigonometrically
[
3
]
, which is computationally faster than computing the cube root required in Eq. (
20
):

z_{0}=\frac{1}{3}\left(\sqrt{\Delta_{0}}\cos\frac{\phi}{3}-p\right),

(23)

where

\phi=\arccos\left(\frac{\Delta_{1}}{2\sqrt{\Delta_{0}^{3}}}\right).

(24)

To summarize, a closed-form real solutions to the quartic equation can be simplified as follows:

\lambda_{1,4}=-\frac{a}{4}\mp\frac{\sqrt{Z}+\sqrt{-A-Z\pm\frac{B}{\sqrt{Z}}}}{2},

(25)

\lambda_{2,3}=-\frac{a}{4}\mp\frac{\sqrt{Z}-\sqrt{-A-Z\pm\frac{B}{\sqrt{Z}}}}{2}.

(26)

where

Z=2z_{0}=\frac{1}{3}\left(2\sqrt{\Delta_{0}}\cos\frac{\phi}{3}-A\right),

(27)

\phi=\arccos\left(\frac{\Delta_{1}}{2\Delta_{0}\sqrt{\Delta_{0}}}\right),

(28)

with

A=2p=2b-\frac{3a^{2}}{4},

(29)

B=2q=2c-ab+\frac{a^{3}}{4}.

(30)

Finally, by including Eqs. (
27
)-(
30
) and Eqs (
21
) and (
22
) into Eqs. (
25
) and (
26
), the fully explicit form of quartic roots is obtained:

\displaystyle\lambda_{1,4}=

\displaystyle-\frac{a}{4}\mp\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{\frac{a^{2}}{4}-\frac{2b}{3}+\frac{2}{3}\sqrt{-3ac+b^{2}+12d}\cos{\left(\frac{1}{3}\operatorname{acos}{\left(\frac{27a^{2}d-9abc+2b^{3}-72bd+27c^{2}}{\left(-6ac+2b^{2}+24d\right)\sqrt{-3ac+b^{2}+12d}}\right)}\right)}}

(31)

\displaystyle\mp\frac{1}{2}\left[\frac{a^{2}}{2}-\frac{4b}{3}-\frac{2}{3}\sqrt{-3ac+b^{2}+12d}\cos{\left(\frac{1}{3}\operatorname{acos}{\left(\frac{27a^{2}d-9abc+2b^{3}-72bd+27c^{2}}{\left(-6ac+2b^{2}+24d\right)\sqrt{-3ac+b^{2}+12d}}\right)}\right)}\right.

\displaystyle\pm\left.\frac{\frac{a^{3}}{4}-ab+2c}{\sqrt{\frac{a^{2}}{4}-\frac{2b}{3}+\frac{2}{3}\sqrt{-3ac+b^{2}+12d}\cos{\left(\frac{1}{3}\operatorname{acos}{\left(\frac{27a^{2}d-9abc+2b^{3}-72bd+27c^{2}}{\left(-6ac+2b^{2}+24d\right)\sqrt{-3ac+b^{2}+12d}}\right)}\right)}}}\right]^{0.5}

\displaystyle\lambda_{2,3}=

\displaystyle-\frac{a}{4}\mp\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{\frac{a^{2}}{4}-\frac{2b}{3}+\frac{2}{3}\sqrt{-3ac+b^{2}+12d}\cos{\left(\frac{1}{3}\operatorname{acos}{\left(\frac{27a^{2}d-9abc+2b^{3}-72bd+27c^{2}}{\left(-6ac+2b^{2}+24d\right)\sqrt{-3ac+b^{2}+12d}}\right)}\right)}}

(32)

\displaystyle\pm\frac{1}{2}\left[\frac{a^{2}}{2}-\frac{4b}{3}-\frac{2}{3}\sqrt{-3ac+b^{2}+12d}\cos{\left(\frac{1}{3}\operatorname{acos}{\left(\frac{27a^{2}d-9abc+2b^{3}-72bd+27c^{2}}{\left(-6ac+2b^{2}+24d\right)\sqrt{-3ac+b^{2}+12d}}\right)}\right)}\right.

\displaystyle\pm\left.\frac{\frac{a^{3}}{4}-ab+2c}{\sqrt{\frac{a^{2}}{4}-\frac{2b}{3}+\frac{2}{3}\sqrt{-3ac+b^{2}+12d}\cos{\left(\frac{1}{3}\operatorname{acos}{\left(\frac{27a^{2}d-9abc+2b^{3}-72bd+27c^{2}}{\left(-6ac+2b^{2}+24d\right)\sqrt{-3ac+b^{2}+12d}}\right)}\right)}}}\right]^{0.5}
