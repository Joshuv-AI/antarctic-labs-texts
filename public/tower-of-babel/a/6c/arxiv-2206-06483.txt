ℛ ⁡ ( p , q ) − \mathcal{R}(p,q)- deformed super Virasoro n − n- algebra


Abstract
In this paper, we construct the super Witt algebra and super Virasoro algebra in the framework of the ℛ ⁡ ( p , q ) − \mathcal{R}(p,q)- deformed quantum algebras. Moreover, we perform the super ℛ ⁡ ( p , q ) − \mathcal{R}(p,q)- deformed Witt n − n- algebra, the ℛ ⁡ ( p , q ) − \mathcal{R}(p,q)- deformed Virasoro n − n- algebra and discuss the super ℛ ⁡ ( p , q ) − \mathcal{R}(p,q)- Virasoro n − n- algebra ( n n even ). Besides, we define and construct another super ℛ ⁡ ( p , q ) − \mathcal{R}(p,q)- deformed Witt n − n- algebra and study a toy model for the super ℛ ⁡ ( p , q ) − \mathcal{R}(p,q)- Virasoro constraints. Relevant particular cases induced from the quantum algebras known in the literature are deduced from the formalism developped.
