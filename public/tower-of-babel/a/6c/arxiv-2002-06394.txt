A short proof of Spectral Sequences of Filtered Complexes
XIONG Rui

Abstract
The purpose of this article is to present my new proof of the the construction and the convergence theorem of spectral sequences of filtered complexes, which is much shorter and cleaner than the “standard” proof. For the historical remarks, see the last section.



Thanks to Yuri V. Volkov for finding a mistake in the original proof.

1
Some fundamental Lemmate

Here I present several more or less trivial lemmate without proofs which I will use in the proof.
This are all common knowledge in algebra, see for example
[
1
]
.

Lemma 1
(Modular property)

Let
A,B,C
be three subgroups of some bigger abelian group, if
A\subseteq C
, then

(A+B)\cap C=A+(B\cap C).

As a result, it makes no doubt to write
A+B\cap C
.

Lemma 2
(Exchange Limit)

Let
C_{\bullet}
be a directed family of submodules of some bigger abelian group, that is,
each pair of
C_{i},C_{j}
are submodules of some
C_{k}
. Assume
A\subseteq B
, then we have

\begin{array}[]{c}\bigcup\big(A+C_{\bullet}\cap B\big)=A+\big(\bigcup C_{\bullet}\big)\cap B.\end{array}

If furthermore,
C_{\bullet}
is bounded below, that is, some
C_{i}
equals to
\bigcap C_{\bullet}
, then,

\begin{array}[]{c}\bigcap\big(A+C_{\bullet}\cap B\big)=A+\big(\bigcap C_{\bullet}\big)\cap B.\end{array}

Lemma 3
(Zassenhaus’ Butterfly Lemma)

For four subgroups
A,B,C,D
of some bigger abelian group, if
A\subseteq B
and
C\subseteq D
, then

\frac{A+D\cap B}{A+C\cap B}\cong\frac{C+B\cap D}{C+A\cap D}.

2
Functorial Zassenhaus’ Butterfly Lemma

Let
X\stackrel{{\scriptstyle f}}{{\to}}X^{\prime}
be a homomorphism between abelian groups, let

•

A,B,C,D
be four subgroups of
X
with
A\subseteq B
and
C\subseteq D
,

•

A^{\prime},B^{\prime},C^{\prime},D^{\prime}
be four subgroups of
X^{\prime}
with
A^{\prime}\subseteq B^{\prime}
and
C^{\prime}\subseteq D^{\prime}
, and

•

f(A)\subseteq A^{\prime},f(B)\subseteq B^{\prime},f(C)\subseteq C^{\prime},f(D)\subseteq D^{\prime}
.

Theorem 4
(Functorial Butterfly)

Then
f
induces

\hat{f}:\frac{A+D\cap B}{A+C\cap B}\to\frac{A^{\prime}+D^{\prime}\cap B^{\prime}}{A^{\prime}+C^{\prime}\cap B^{\prime}}.

Lemma 5

If
f(B\cap D)=B^{\prime}\cap D^{\prime}
, then
\hat{f}
is surjective.

Since any element on the right is presented by an element
x
of
D^{\prime}\cap B^{\prime}
(since
A^{\prime}
is in the denominator).
By the assumption,
f(y)=x
for some
y\in D
, now
y\in f^{-1}(B^{\prime})=B
, so
x
is mapped by
y
.

Lemma 6

If
A+C=f^{-1}(A^{\prime}+C^{\prime})
, then
\hat{f}
is injective.

Pick any element on the left, assume it is presented by
x\in D\cap B
,
assume
f(x)\in(A^{\prime}+C^{\prime})\cap B^{\prime}
, now
x\in A+C
by assumption,
so is in the denominator
(A+C)\cap B
.

The above two lemmate also follow immediately from theorem
9
as a remark.

Theorem 7
(Adjointness)

If
A=f^{-1}(A^{\prime})
,
B=f^{-1}(B^{\prime})
and
f(C)=C^{\prime}
,
f(D)=D^{\prime}
, then
\hat{f}
is an isomorphism.

Firstly,
f(B\cap D)=B^{\prime}\cap D^{\prime}
.
x\in B^{\prime}\cap D^{\prime}\subseteq D^{\prime}=f(D)
,
there exists
y\in D
such that
f(y)=x\in B^{\prime}
, so
y\in B
also as desired.
Secondly,
A+C=f^{-1}(A^{\prime}+C^{\prime})
. If
f(x)=y+f(z)
with
y\in A^{\prime}
and
z\in C
then
f(x-z)=y\in A^{\prime}
, i.e.
x-z\in A
.
Therefore,
x\in A+C
as desired again.

3
The Construction

Let
(C_{\bullet},\textsf{d})
be a complex with filtration
\mathcal{F}
which is exhaustive (i.e.
C_{n}=\bigcup_{p\in\mathbb{Z}}\mathcal{F}_{p}C_{n}
) and
bounded below (i.e. for each
n
,
p\ll 0\Rightarrow\mathcal{F}_{p}C_{n}=0
). Thus the filtration automatically satisfy the condition in lemma
2
.
For convenience, we also denote

\mathcal{F}_{-\infty}C_{n}=\bigcap_{p}F_{p}C_{n}=0,\qquad\mathcal{F}_{\infty}C_{n}=\bigcup_{p}F_{p}C_{n}=C_{n}.

To invest its homology, define

\mathcal{F}_{p}K_{n}=\textsf{d}^{-1}(\mathcal{F}_{p}C_{n-1})\quad\textrm{ and }\quad\mathcal{F}_{p}I_{n}=\textsf{d}(\mathcal{F}_{p}C_{n+1})

for each
p\in\mathbb{Z}\sqcup\{\infty\}
and
n
.
We now use
\{\mathcal{F}_{p}K_{n}\}_{p}
and
\{\mathcal{F}_{p}I_{n}\}_{p}
to refine the filtration
\{\mathcal{F}_{p}C_{n}\}_{p}
. Define

\begin{cases}\mathsf{Z}_{pq}^{r}=\mathcal{F}_{p-1}C_{p+q}+\mathcal{F}_{p-r}K_{p+q}\cap\mathcal{F}_{p}C_{p+q},\\
\mathsf{B}_{pq}^{r}=\mathcal{F}_{p-1}C_{p+q}+\mathcal{F}_{p+r-1}I_{p+q}\cap\mathcal{F}_{p}C_{p+q},\end{cases}\textrm{ with }r=0,1,\ldots,+\infty.

See lemma
1
for the meaning for notations.
Now, define

\mathsf{E}_{pq}^{r}=\mathsf{Z}_{pq}^{r}/\mathsf{B}_{pq}^{r}.

We have

\begin{array}[]{rll}\dfrac{\mathsf{Z}_{pq}^{r}}{\mathsf{Z}_{pq}^{r+1}}&=\dfrac{\mathcal{F}_{p-1}C_{p+q}+F_{p-r}K_{p+q}\cap\mathcal{F}_{p}C_{p+q}}{\mathcal{F}_{p-1}C_{p+q}+\mathcal{F}_{p-r-1}K_{p+q}\cap\mathcal{F}_{p}C_{p+q}}&\because\textrm{definition}\\[8.61108pt]
&=\displaystyle\dfrac{\mathcal{F}_{p-r-1}K_{p+q}+\mathcal{F}_{p}C_{p+q}\cap\mathcal{F}_{p-r}K_{p+q}}{\mathcal{F}_{p-r-1}K_{p+q}+\mathcal{F}_{p-1}C_{p+q}\cap\mathcal{F}_{p-r}K_{p+q}}&\because\textrm{butterfly lemma }\ref{Btf}\\[8.61108pt]
&\displaystyle\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\textsf{d}}}{{\to}}\dfrac{\mathcal{F}_{p-r-1}C_{p+q-1}+\mathcal{F}_{p}I_{p+q-1}\cap\mathcal{F}_{p-r}C_{p+q-1}}{\mathcal{F}_{p-r-1}C_{p+q-1}+\mathcal{F}_{p-1}I_{p+q-1}\cap\mathcal{F}_{p-r}C_{p+q-1}}&\because\textrm{theorem }\ref{adjointofButterfly}\\[8.61108pt]
&=\dfrac{\mathsf{B}_{p-r,q+r-1}^{r+1}}{\mathsf{B}_{p-r,q+r-1}^{r}}&\because\textrm{definition}.\end{array}

Then we can define

\textsf{d}^{r}_{pq}=\left[\mathsf{E}_{pq}^{r}=\frac{\mathsf{Z}_{pq}^{r}}{\mathsf{B}_{pq}^{r}}\mathop{\twoheadrightarrow}\limits\frac{\mathsf{Z}_{pq}^{r}}{\mathsf{Z}_{pq}^{r+1}}\cong\frac{\mathsf{B}_{p-r,q+r-1}^{r+1}}{\mathsf{B}_{p-r,q+r-1}^{r}}\mathop{\hookrightarrow}\limits\frac{\mathsf{Z}_{p-r,q+r-1}^{r}}{\mathsf{B}_{p-r,q+r-1}^{r}}=\mathsf{E}_{p-r,q+r-1}^{r}\right]

clearly,
\textsf{d}^{r}\circ\textsf{d}^{r}=0
, and at the position
(p,q)
of stage
r
,

\ker\textsf{d}^{r}=\frac{\mathsf{Z}_{pq}^{r+1}}{\mathsf{B}_{pq}^{r}},\quad\operatorname{im}\textsf{d}^{r}=\frac{\mathsf{B}_{pq}^{r+1}}{\mathsf{B}_{pq}^{r}},\quad\textrm{and}\quad\frac{\ker\textsf{d}^{r}}{\operatorname{im}\textsf{d}^{r}}=\mathsf{E}_{pq}^{r+1}.

Thus the above construction of
\{E_{pq}^{r}\}
give rise to a spectral sequence.

4
Convergence Theorem

Theorem 8
(Convergence Theorem)

Given a complex
C_{\bullet}
with filtration
\mathcal{F}
which is exhaustive and bounded below,
then the
\{\mathsf{E}_{pq}^{r}\}
constructed above is convergent to
\mathsf{H}_{n}(C_{\bullet})
.

Proof.

Using butterfly lemma again,

\begin{array}[]{rll}\mathsf{E}_{pq}^{\infty}=\dfrac{\bigcap_{r}\mathsf{Z}_{pq}^{r}}{\bigcup_{r}\mathsf{B}_{pq}^{r}}&=\dfrac{\bigcap_{r}(\mathcal{F}_{p-1}C_{p+q}+\mathcal{F}_{p-r}K_{p+q}\cap\mathcal{F}_{p}C_{p+q})}{\bigcup_{r}(\mathcal{F}_{p-1}C_{p+q}+\mathcal{F}_{p+r-1}I_{p+q}\cap\mathcal{F}_{p}C_{p+q})}&\because\textrm{definition}\\[8.61108pt]
&=\dfrac{\mathcal{F}_{p-1}C_{p+q}+\mathcal{F}_{-\infty}K_{p+q}\cap\mathcal{F}_{p}C_{p+q}}{\mathcal{F}_{p-1}C_{p+q}+\mathcal{F}_{+\infty}I_{p+q}\cap\mathcal{F}_{p}C_{p+q}}&\because\textrm{lemma }\ref{ExLim}\\[8.61108pt]
&=\dfrac{\mathcal{F}_{p-1}C_{p+q}+\ker\textsf{d}\cap\mathcal{F}_{p}C_{p+q}}{\mathcal{F}_{p-1}C_{p+q}+\operatorname{im}\textsf{d}\cap\mathcal{F}_{p}C_{p+q}}&\because\textrm{definition}\\[8.61108pt]
&=\dfrac{\operatorname{im}\textsf{d}+\mathcal{F}_{p}C_{p+q}\cap\ker\textsf{d}}{\operatorname{im}\textsf{d}+\mathcal{F}_{p-1}C_{p+q}\cap\ker\textsf{d}}&\because\textrm{butterfly lemma }\ref{Btf}.\end{array}

Denote
\mathcal{F}_{p}\mathsf{H}_{p+q}=\operatorname{im}\textsf{d}+\mathcal{F}_{p}C_{p+q}\cap\ker\textsf{d}
, then

\begin{array}[]{l}\bigcup_{p}\mathcal{F}_{p}\mathsf{H}_{n}=\operatorname{im}\textsf{d}+\big(\bigcup_{p}\mathcal{F}_{p}C_{n}\big)\cap\ker\textsf{d}=\operatorname{im}\textsf{d}+C_{n}\cap\ker\textsf{d}=\ker\textsf{d},\\
\bigcap_{p}\mathcal{F}_{p}\mathsf{H}_{n}=\operatorname{im}\textsf{d}+\big(\bigcap_{p}\mathcal{F}_{p}C_{n}\big)\cap\ker\textsf{d}=\operatorname{im}\textsf{d}+0\cap\ker\textsf{d}=\operatorname{im}\textsf{d}.\end{array}

from lemma
2
and the assumptions.
So, we define a filtration over
\mathsf{H}_{n}(C_{\bullet})=\dfrac{\ker\textsf{d}}{\operatorname{im}\textsf{d}}
whose subquotient coincide with
\mathsf{E}_{pq}^{\infty}
.
The proof is complete.

5
Computation of
E_{pq}^{0}

Firstly,

\begin{array}[]{rll}\mathsf{Z}_{pq}^{0}&=\mathcal{F}_{p-1}C_{p+q}+\mathcal{F}_{p}K_{p+q}\cap\mathcal{F}_{p}C_{p+q}\\
&=\mathcal{F}_{p-1}C_{p+q}+\textsf{d}^{-1}(\mathcal{F}_{p}C_{p+q-1})\cap\mathcal{F}_{p}C_{p+q}\\
&=\mathcal{F}_{p-1}C_{p+q},\end{array}

since
\mathcal{F}_{p}C_{p+q}\subseteq\textsf{d}^{-1}(\mathcal{F}_{p}C_{p+q-1})
; and

\begin{array}[]{rll}\mathsf{B}_{pq}^{0}&=\mathcal{F}_{p-1}C_{p+q}+\mathcal{F}_{p-1}I_{p+q}\cap\mathcal{F}_{p}C_{p+q}\\
&=\mathcal{F}_{p-1}C_{p+q}+\textsf{d}(\mathcal{F}_{p-1}C_{p+q+1})\cap\mathcal{F}_{p}C_{p+q}\\
&=\mathcal{F}_{p}C_{p+q},\end{array}

since
\textsf{d}(\mathcal{F}_{p-1}C_{p+q+1})\subseteq\mathcal{F}_{p-1}C_{p+q}
.
Hence
E_{pq}^{0}=\dfrac{\mathsf{Z}_{pq}^{0}}{\mathsf{B}_{pq}^{0}}=\dfrac{\mathcal{F}_{p}C_{p+q}}{\mathcal{F}_{p-1}C_{p+q}}
.
Note that the isomorphism
\dfrac{\mathsf{Z}_{pq}^{0}}{\mathsf{Z}_{pq}^{1}}\to\dfrac{\mathsf{B}_{p,q-1}^{1}}{\mathsf{B}_{p,q-1}^{0}}
is induced by
d
,
and by definition
\textsf{d}^{0}
factors through the isomorphism, so it is induced by
d
.

(Extended Zassenhaus’ lemma)
