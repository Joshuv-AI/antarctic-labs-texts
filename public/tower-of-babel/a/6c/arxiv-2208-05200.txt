A frequency-independent bound on trigonometric polynomials of Gaussians and applications
Fanhao Kong 1 and Wenhao Zhao 2

Abstract
We prove a frequency-independent bound on trigonometric functions of a class of singular Gaussian random fields, which arise naturally from weak universality problems for singular stochastic PDEs. This enables us to reduce the regularity assumption on the nonlinearity of the microscopic models (for pathwise convergence) in KPZ and dynamical Φ 3 4 \Phi^{4}_{3} in the previous works of Hairer-Xu and Furlan-Gubinelli to heuristically optimal thresholds required by PDE structures.
