Mohammad El-Hindi and Therrar Kadri

Abstract
. The generalized group of units of the ring modulo n n was first introduced by El-Kassar and Chehade, written as U k ​ ( Z n ) U^{k}(Z_{n}) . In number theory, the Euler totient function
\varphi(n)

Euler’s phi function, is defined for a given

integers are less than or equal to

[
6
]

equation are prime integers. Since then, no one was able to provide a proof.
This problem is known to be Lehmer’s totient problem and has received a
great attention.

The Euler phi function has been generalized through different approaches and
was studied in many domains. In 1983, J.T. Cross, in
[

\beta

In this chapter, we introduce the generalization of the Euler phi function

3.1.
Definition and properties

In this section, we define the generalized Euler phi function, and we determine
its explicit formula.

Definition 4
.

We define the order of the generalized group of units of the
ring
\mathbb{Z}_{n}
to be the generalized Euler phi function denoted by
\varphi^{k}(n)
i.e.

\varphi^{k}(n)=|U^{k}(\mathbb{Z}_{n})|.

Using Example

\displaystyle\left|{{U}^{3}}\left({{\mathbb{Z}}_{1080000}}\right)\right|

Theorem 6
.

\varphi^{k}(n)
is a multiplicative function.

Proof.

Let
R=Z_{n}
and
U^{k}(Z_{n})
be its
k
-th group of units where the prime decomposition of
n
is of the following form,

n=p_{1}^{\alpha_{1}}p_{2}^{\alpha_{2}}\ldots p_{r}^{\alpha_{r}}

then,

U^{k}(Z_{n})\approx U^{k}(\mathbb{Z}_{p_{1}^{\alpha_{1}}})\oplus U^{k}(\mathbb{Z}_{p_{2}^{\alpha_{2}}})\oplus\ldots\oplus U^{k}(\mathbb{Z}_{p_{r}^{\alpha_{r}}})

which leads to,
|U^{k}(\mathbb{Z}_{n})|

=|U^{k}(\mathbb{Z}_{p_{1}^{\alpha_{1}}})\oplus U^{k}(\mathbb{Z}_{p_{2}^{\alpha_{2}}})\oplus\ldots\oplus U^{k}(\mathbb{Z}_{p_{r}^{\alpha_{r}}})|

=|U^{k}(\mathbb{Z}_{p_{1}^{\alpha_{1}}})|\times|U^{k}(\mathbb{Z}_{p_{2}^{\alpha_{2}}})|\times\ldots\times|U^{k}(\mathbb{Z}_{p_{r}^{\alpha_{r}}})|.
By Definition
4
we get,

\varphi^{k}(n)=\prod\limits_{i=1}^{k}\varphi^{k}(p_{i}^{\alpha_{i}})

thus,
\varphi^{k}(n)
is a multiplicative function.
∎

Proposition 7

If

\varphi^{k}(p)=\varphi^{k-1}(p-1)

Proof.

Let
\mathbb{Z}_{n}
the set of integers
\mod n

be its group of units, then by Theorem

U(\mathbb{Z}_{p})\approx\mathbb{Z}_{p-1}

|U^{k}(\mathbb{Z}_{p})|
