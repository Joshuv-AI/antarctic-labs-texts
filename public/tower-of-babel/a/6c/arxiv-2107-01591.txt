Lefschetz Theorem Using Real Morse Theory
Nima Rose Manjila, A J Parameswaran

Abstract
. Taking [ 2 ] as the primary reference, we prove some of the important theorems solely using Real Morse theory and exact sequences. The Proof of Lefschetz hyperplane section theorem is a deviation from the conventional one which uses vanishing cycles, thimbles and monodromy. Instead, we prove using simpler machinery. By making the observation that we can compose the Lefschetz Pencil with a Real Morse function to get a map from the variety to ℝ \mathbb{R} which is ”close” to being a Real Morse function, we prove the genus formula for plane curves and then we prove the Riemann Hurwitz formula for ramified maps between curves by employing techniques from deformation theory. Lastly, we prove the Lefschetz Hyperplane Section Theorem.
