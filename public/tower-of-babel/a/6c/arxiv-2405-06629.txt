A General Hierarchy of Charges at Null Infinity via the Todd Polynomials
Silvia Nagy a, Javier Peraza b, Giorgio Pizzolo a

Abstract
We give a general procedure for constructing an extended phase space for Yang-Mills theory at null infinity, capable of handling the asymptotic symmetries and construction of charges responsible for sub n -leading soft theorems at all orders. The procedure is coordinate and gauge-choice independent, and can be fed into the calculation of both tree and loop-level soft limits. We find a hierarchy in the extended phase space controlled by the Bernoulli numbers arising in Todd genus computations. We give an explicit example of a calculation at tree level, in radial gauge, where we also uncover recursion relations at all orders for the equations of motion and charges.

Charge construction and Algebra

IV
Conclusions

We have shown how the Stueckelberg procedure can be imported from the study of massive gauge theories and cosmology
Stueckelberg 1938
;
Henneaux and Teitelboim 1989
;
Nagy et al. 2020
;
Bansal et al. 2021
, and adapted to the construction of an enlarged phase space at null infinity, capable of producing the charges which will be needed for understanding the symmetry origin of sub
n
- leading effects, both at tree- and loop-level. We have demonstrated the procedure by taking the radial gauge in Bondi coordinates as an example, further giving general
n
-th order recursion relations in this context, which will facilitate the construction of charges to all orders. The recursion relations for the charges within the hierarchy are controlled by the Bernoulli numbers in the expansion of the generating power series for the Todd polynomials. Since
B_{2k+1}
are vanishing for
k>0
, it seems that the higher levels are controlled by the even sub
n
-leading charges. We will explore this further in future work.

A natural question is whether this extends to gravity, where work at the first few subleading orders already exists in several contexts (e.g.,
Cachazo and Strominger 2014
;
Campiglia and Peraza 2021
;
Freidel et al. 2022b
;
Fuentealba et al. 2023
). An encouraging suggestion comes from the toy model calculation in
Nagy and Peraza 2023
, where the self-dual sector of gravity was considered, working in the light-cone gauge. A straightforward double copy relation was established there between the extended phase spaces, as well as the residual symmetry transformations to all orders. An exciting prospect opened up by our results here is whether this can be extended to the full YM and gravitational theories
