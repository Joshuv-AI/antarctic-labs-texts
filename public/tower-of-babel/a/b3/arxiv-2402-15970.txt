A Markovian regime-switching stochastic SEQIR epidemic model with governmental policy
†

Abstract




Abstract:
In this paper, a stochastic SEQIR epidemic model with Markovian regime-switching is proposed and investigated.
The governmental policy and implement efficiency are concerned by a generalized incidence function of the susceptible class.
We have the existence and uniqueness of the globally positive solution to the stochastic model by using the Lyapunov method.
In addition, we study the dynamical behaviors of the disease, and the sufficient conditions for the extinction and persistence in mean are obtained.
Finally, numerical simulations are introduced to demonstrate the theoretical results.

Keywords:
Extinction; Persistence in mean; Governmental policy; Telegraph noise

37N25; 60H10; 60J10; 92B05

Let

\mathbb{S}={1,...,N}

\Gamma=(q_{ij})\in\mathbb{R}^{N\times N}

\displaystyle P(r(t+\Delta)=j|r(t)=i)=\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}q_{ij}\Delta+o(\Delta),&if\ i\neq j,\\

.
That implies

is irreducible and has a unique stationary distribution

, which can be determined by
\pi\Gamma=0
, subjected to
\sum_{k=1}^{N}\pi_{k}=1
,
\pi_{k}>0
, for any

. And assume that the right continuous Markov chain

is independent of the Brownian motion
B(t)
for

.

In general, considering the

-dimensional stochastic differential equation

\displaystyle dx(t)=F(x(t),r(t))dt+g(x(t),r(t))dB(t),\ \text{for}\ t\geq 0,

where

Theorem 3.1.
For

(S(r_{0}),E(r_{0}),Q(r_{0}),I(r_{0}),R(r_{0}))\in\mathbb{R}_{+}^{5}

Proof.

Since the coefficients of model (

,

and initial value
(S(r_{0}),E(r_{0}),Q(r_{0}),I(r_{0}),R(r_{0}))\in\mathbb{R}_{+}^{5}

to model (

) on
[0,\tau_{e}]
.
We need to prove

Let
k_{0}\geq 1
be sufficiently large such

S,E,Q,I,R
are lie in
[k_{0},\frac{1}{k_{0}}]
.
Let

, define the stopping time

\displaystyle\tau_{k}=\inf\left\{t\in\left[0,\tau_{e}\right):S(t)\notin\left(\frac{1}{k},k\right)\text{ or }E(t)\notin\left(\frac{1}{k},k\right)\text{ or }Q(t)\notin\left(\frac{1}{k},k\right)\text{ or }I(t)\notin\left(\frac{1}{k},k\right)\text{ or }R(t)\notin\left(\frac{1}{k},k\right)\right\}.

Let

.

Obviously, we only need to show

.
If the assertion is false, then there exist constants

and
\epsilon\in(0,1)
such that

\mathbb{P}(\tau_{+\infty}\leq T)\geq\epsilon,
which yields that

such that

\mathbb{P}(\tau_{k}\leq T)\geq\epsilon,\ \text{for\ }k\geq k_{1}.

Define a Lyapunov function

\displaystyle V(S,E,Q,I,R)=S-1-\ln S+E-1-\ln E+Q-1-\ln Q+I-1-\ln I+R-1-\ln R.

By Itô’s formula

\displaystyle LV\leq

\displaystyle A(r(t))+\beta(r(t))(1-\rho_{1}(r(t)))(1-\rho_{2}(r(t)))E+5\xi(r(t))+b_{1}(r(t))+b_{2}(r(t))+c(r(t))+\alpha(r(t))

\displaystyle+\sigma(r(t))+\eta(r(t))+\delta(r(t))+\frac{1}{2}\sigma_{0}^{2}(r(t))(1-\rho_{1}(r(t)))^{2}(1-\rho_{2}(r(t)))^{2}(E^{2}+S^{2})+p(r(t))M(r(t))\frac{h(S)}{S}

\displaystyle\hat{A}+\frac{\hat{A}\hat{\beta}(1-\check{\rho}_{1})(1-\check{\rho}_{2})}{\check{\xi}}+4\hat{\xi}+\hat{b_{1}}+\hat{b_{2}}+\hat{\alpha}+\hat{\sigma}+\hat{\xi}+\hat{c}+\hat{\eta}+\hat{\delta}+\hat{p}\hat{M}h^{\prime}(0)+\hat{\sigma}_{0}^{2}(1-\check{\rho}_{1})^{2}(1-\check{\rho}_{2})^{2}\frac{\hat{A}}{\check{\xi}}^{2}.

The remainder of the proof follows that in
[
20

Extinction

In this section, we obtain the condition for the extinction of the disease.
We define

\displaystyle R_{s}^{*}=\frac{\sum_{k=1}^{N}\pi(k)\beta(k)w_{1}(k)\frac{\check{A}}{\hat{\xi}}}{\sum_{k=1}^{N}\pi(k)\left[w_{2}(k)+\frac{\sigma_{0}^{2}(k)}{2}w_{1}^{2}(k)\left(\frac{\check{A}}{\hat{\xi}}\right)^{2}\right]},

where
w_{1}(k)=(1-\rho_{1}(k))(1-\rho_{2}(k))
, and
w_{2}(k)=b_{2}(k)+\alpha(k)+\sigma(k)+\xi(k)
for
k\in\mathbb{S}

Theorem 4.1.

If

\displaystyle\limsup_{t\rightarrow+\infty}E(t)=\limsup_{t\rightarrow+\infty}Q(t)=\limsup_{t\rightarrow+\infty}I(t)=0\ a.s..

Proof.

By applying Itô’s formula to the second equation of model (
1.2
), we have

\displaystyle d\ln E(t)=

\displaystyle\left[\beta(r(t))(1-\rho_{1}(r(t)))(1-\rho_{2}(r(t)))S(t)-(b_{2}(r(t))+\alpha(r(t))+\sigma(r(t))+\xi(r(t)))\right.

(4.1)

\displaystyle\left.-\frac{1}{2}\sigma_{0}^{2}(r(t))(1-\rho_{1}(r(t)))^{2}(1-\rho_{2}(r(t)))^{2}S^{2}(t)\right]dt

\displaystyle+\sigma_{0}(r(t))(1-\rho_{1}(k))(1-\rho_{2}(k))S(t)dB(t).

Integrating both sides of the above equation form
0
to
t

\displaystyle\ln E(t)-\ln e_{0}=

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{t}\left[\beta(r(u))w_{1}(r(u))S(u)-\frac{1}{2}\sigma_{0}^{2}(r(u))w_{1}^{2}(r(u))S^{2}(u)-w_{2}(r(u))\right]du+\Upsilon(t),

where

\Upsilon(t)=\int_{0}^{t}\sigma_{0}(r(u))w_{1}(r(u))S(u)dB(u).

By applying of the large number theorem for local continuous martingales, we can get

\displaystyle\frac{\ln E(t)-\ln e_{0}}{t}=\frac{1}{t}\int_{0}^{t}\left[\beta(r(u))w_{1}(r(u))S(u)-\frac{1}{2}\sigma_{0}^{2}(r(u))w_{1}^{2}(r(u))S^{2}(u)-w_{2}(k)\right]du.

Further, for any
k\in

\mathbb{S}
, we define the function

By the positively invariant set
\Omega
and the assumption

,
we know

is increasing on
\left[0,\beta(k)/\sigma_{0}^{2}(k)w_{1}(k)\right]
, then

\displaystyle\frac{\ln E(t)-\ln e_{0}}{t}\leq\frac{1}{t}\int_{0}^{t}\left[\beta(r(u))w_{1}(r(u))\frac{\check{A}}{\hat{\xi}}-\frac{1}{2}\sigma_{0}^{2}(r(u))w_{1}^{2}(r(u))\frac{\check{A}^{2}}{\hat{\xi}^{2}}-w_{2}(r(u))\right]du.

Taking the superior limit on both sides of the above equation, we can obtain

\displaystyle\limsup_{t\rightarrow\infty}\frac{\ln E(t)-\ln e_{0}}{t}

\displaystyle\leq\limsup_{t\rightarrow\infty}\frac{1}{t}\int_{0}^{t}\left[\beta(r(u))w_{1}(r(u))\frac{\check{A}}{\hat{\xi}}-\frac{1}{2}\sigma_{0}^{2}(r(u))w_{1}^{2}(r(u))\frac{\check{A}^{2}}{\hat{\xi}^{2}}-w_{2}(r(u))\right]du

\displaystyle\limsup_{t\rightarrow\infty}\frac{\ln E(t)-\ln e_{0}}{t}

\displaystyle\leq\sum_{k=1}^{N}\pi(k)\left[\beta(k)w_{1}(k)\frac{\check{A}}{\hat{\xi}}-\frac{1}{2}\sigma_{0}^{2}(k)w_{1}^{2}(k)\frac{\check{A}^{2}}{\hat{\xi}^{2}}-w_{2}(k)\right]

\displaystyle\leq\sum_{k=1}^{N}\pi(k)\left(w_{2}(k)+\frac{1}{2}\sigma_{0}^{2}(k)w_{1}^{2}(k)\frac{\check{A}^{2}}{\hat{\xi}^{2}}\right)\left(R_{s}^{*}-1\right)<0\ a.s.,

Thus for any constant

\displaystyle\frac{dQ(t)}{dt}\leq\check{b}_{2}E(t)-(\hat{b}_{1}+\hat{c}+\hat{\xi})Q(t)\leq\check{b}_{2}\epsilon-(\hat{b}_{1}+\hat{c}+\hat{\xi})Q(t).

\displaystyle\limsup_{t\rightarrow+\infty}Q(t)\leq\frac{\check{b}_{2}\epsilon}{\hat{b}_{1}+\hat{c}+\hat{\xi}}\text{\ a.s..}

\limsup_{t\rightarrow+\infty}Q(t)=0

7
Conclusion and discussion

In this paper, we propose a SEQIR epidemic model with both white and telegraph noises,
and investigate the dynamic behaviors of the diseases.
The governmental policy and the efficiency of policy implemented are both considered in the stochastic model by constructing the generalized function.
Firstly, we get the positively invariant set of the classes of the stochastic model,
and have the existence and uniqueness of the globally positive solution of model (
1.2
).
Then the sufficient condition for the distinction of the disease is obtained.
Furthermore, we have obtained the sufficient condition for persistence in mean by selecting suitable Lyapunov function with regime switching.

Moreover, some interesting topics are deserved for further consideration.
It is found that
\widetilde{R}_{s}^{*}\leq{R}_{s}^{*}
in this paper, and we will continue to investigate what happens to the diseases under the condition
\widetilde{R}_{s}^{*}<1<{R}_{s}^{*}
.
In addition, we can use the similar methods to study more complex epidemic models, such as SEQIR model with media effects.
