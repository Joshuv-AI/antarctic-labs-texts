Stable unital basis, hyperfocal subalgebras and basic Morita equivalences
Tiberiu Coconeţ, Constantin-Cosmin Todea

Abstract
We investigate Conjecture 1.6 introduced by Barker and Gelvin in [ 3 ] , which says that any source algebra of a p p -block ( p p is a prime) of finite group has the unit group containing a basis stabilized by left and right action of the defect group. We will reduce this conjecture to a similar statement about basis of the hyperfocal subalgebras in the source algebra. We will also show that such unital basis of source algebras of two p p -blocks, stabilized by left and right action of the defect group, are transported trough basic Morita equivalences.
