Controlled continuous
g

Abstract




Abstract.

In this paper, we characterize and study the concept of controlled continuous
g
-frame which is an extension of continuous
g
-frame in Hilbert spaces. We introduce the concept of controlled continuous dual
g
-frame and observe some interesting properties of it. Finally, we characterize all controlled continuous dual
g
-frames of a controlled continuous
g
-frame.

Keywords:
continuous
g
-frame; controlled continuous
g
-frame.

2010 Mathematics subject classification:
41A58; 42C15.

1.
introduction

Gabor
[
11
]
introduced a method using a family of elementary functions for reconstructing functions (signals) in
1946
.
The idea of frames originated in the
1952
paper by Duffin and Schaeffer
[
9
]
to address some deep questions in non-harmonic Fourier series. After some decades, Daubechies, Grossmann and Meyer
[
8
]
announced formally the definition of frame in the abstract Hilbert spaces in
1986
. After their work, frame theory began to be widely used, particularly in the more specialized context of wavelet frames and Gabor frames.

Continuous frame is a concept of generalization of frames proposed by G. Kaiser
[
12
]
and independently by Ali, Antoine and Gazeau
[
4
]
to a family indexed by some locally compact space endowed with a Radon measure.
These frames are called as frames associated with measurable spaces by Gabrado and Han in
[
10
]
. Askari-Hemmat, Dehghan and Radjabalipour in
[
5
]
called them as generalized frames and in Mathematical Physics they are referred to as coherent states
[
3
]
.

A continuous
g
-frame is an extension of
g
-frames and continuous frames which was firstly introduced by Abdollahpour and Faroughi in
[
1
]
. In recent years there has been shown considerable interest by functional analysts in the
study of continuous
g
-frames in Hilbert spaces.
For more details, see
[
7
,
13
]
and the references therein.

We begin with a few preliminaries which are needed in the sequel. Let
H,L
be separable Hilbert spaces and
(\Omega,\mu)
be a positive measure space. Let
\{H_{w}\}_{w\in\Omega}
be a family of closed subspaces of
L
. Throughout we consider the index set as
\Omega
and we denote simply
\{H_{w}\}_{w}
for
\{H_{w}\}_{w\in\Omega}
. We denote the set of all bounded linear operators from
H
into
H_{w}
by
L(H,H_{w})
and the set of all bounded linear operators on
H
with bounded inverse by
GL(H)
. The set of all positive operators in
GL(H)
is denoted by
GL^{+}(H)
and
I_{H}
represents the identity operator on
H
. We note that if
P,Q\in GL(H)
, then
P^{\ast},P^{-1}
and
PQ
are also in
GL(H)
.

We consider the space

\ell^{2}(\{H_{w}\}_{w})=\Big\{\{f_{w}\}_{w}:f_{w}\in H_{w},w\in\Omega,\int_{\Omega}\|f_{w}\|^{2}\ d\mu(w)<\infty\Big\}

with the inner product given by

\langle\{f_{w}\}_{w},\{g_{w}\}_{w}\rangle=\int_{\Omega}\langle f_{w},g_{w}\rangle\ d\mu(w).

It is clear that
\ell^{2}(\{H_{w}\}_{w})
is a Hilbert space.

Lemma 1.1
.

[
7
]

Let
T:H\rightarrow H
be a linear operator. Then the following are equivalent:

(1)

There exist constants
0<A\leq B<\infty
, such that
AI_{H}\leq T\leq BI_{H}
.

(2)

T
is positive and there exist constants
0<A\leq B<\infty
such that

A\|f\|^{2}\leq\|T^{\frac{1}{2}}f\|^{2}\leq B\|f\|^{2},\quad\forall f\in H.

(3)

T\in GL^{+}(H)
.

Definition 1.2
.

[
6
]

Let
(\Omega,\mu)
be a measure space with a positive measure
\mu
and
P\in GL(H)
. A
P
-controlled continuous frame is a map
F:\Omega\rightarrow H
such that there exist constants
0<A\leq B<\infty
such that

A\|f\|^{2}\leq\int_{\Omega}\langle f,F(w)\rangle\langle PF(w),f\rangle\ d\mu(w)\leq B\|f\|^{2},\quad\forall f\in H.

Definition 1.3
.

[
1
]
For each
w\in\Omega
, let
\Lambda_{w}\in L(H,H_{w})
.
We say that
\Lambda=\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
is a continuous
g
-frame for
H
with respect to
\{H_{w}\}_{w}
if

(1)

for each
f\in H
,
\{\Lambda_{w}f\}_{w}
is strongly measurable.

(2)

there exist constants
0<A\leq B<\infty
such that

A\|f\|^{2}\leq\int_{\Omega}\|\Lambda_{w}f\|^{2}\ d\mu(w)\leq B\|f\|^{2},\quad\forall f\in H.

(1.1)

The numbers
A,B
are called lower and upper frame bounds for the continuous
g
-frame, respectively. If only the right-hand inequality of (
1.1
) is satisfied, we call
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}

is a continuous
g
-Bessel family for
H
with respect to
\{H_{w}\}_{w}
with bound
B
. If

A=B=\lambda
, we call
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
the
\lambda
-tight continuous
g
-frame. Moreover, if
\lambda=1
,
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
is called the Parseval continuous
g
-frame.

For a given continuous
g
-frame
\Lambda=\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
for
H
with respect to
\{H_{w}\}_{w}
, there exists a unique positive and invertible operator (called the frame operator)
S_{\Lambda}:H\to H
such that for each
f,g\in H
:

\langle S_{\Lambda}f,g\rangle=\int_{\Omega}\langle f,\Lambda_{w}^{*}\Lambda_{w}g\rangle\ d\mu(w)

and
AI_{H}\leq S_{\Lambda}\leq BI_{H}
.

Definition 1.4
.

[
2
]

Let
P,Q\in GL^{+}(H)
and
\Lambda_{w}\in L(H,H_{w})
. We say that
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
is a
(P,Q)
-controlled continuous
g
-frame for
H
with respect to
\{H_{w}\}_{w}
if
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
is a continuous
g
-Bessel family and there exist constants
0<A\leq B<\infty
such that

A\|f\|^{2}\leq\int_{\Omega}\langle\Lambda_{w}Pf,\Lambda_{w}Qf\rangle\ d\mu(w)\leq B\|f\|^{2},\quad\forall f\in H.

(1.2)

The numbers
A
and
B
are called controlled continuous
g
-frame bounds. If the right hand inequality of (
1.2
) holds for all
f\in H
, then
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
is a called a
(P,Q)
-controlled continuous
g
-Bessel family with bound
B
.

If
Q=I_{H}
, we call
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
is a
P
-controlled continuous
g
-frame for
H
with respect to
\{H_{w}\}_{w}
. If
Q=P
, we call
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
is a
(P,P)
-controlled continuous
g
-frame for
H
with respect to
\{H_{w}\}
.

For a
(P,Q)
-controlled continuous
g
-Bessel family
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
with bound
B
, the operator
T_{P\Lambda Q}:\ell^{2}(\{H_{w}\}_{w})\rightarrow H
given by

T_{P\Lambda Q}\{f_{w}\}_{w}=\int_{\Omega}(PQ)^{\frac{1}{2}}\Lambda_{w}f_{w}\ d\mu(w),\quad\forall\{f_{w}\}_{w}\in\ell^{2}(\{H_{w}\}_{w})

is well-defined and its adjoint is given by

T_{P\Lambda Q}^{\ast}:H\rightarrow\ell^{2}(\{H_{w}\}_{w}),\quad T_{P\Lambda Q}^{\ast}f=\{\Lambda_{w}(QP)^{\frac{1}{2}}f\}_{w},\quad\forall f\in H.

T_{P\Lambda Q}
is called the synthesis operator and
T_{P\Lambda Q}^{\ast}
is called the analysis operator of
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
. For a
(P,Q)
-controlled continuous
g
-frame
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
with bounds
A
and
B
, the operator

S_{P\Lambda Q}:H\rightarrow H,\quad S_{P\Lambda Q}f=\int_{\Omega}Q\Lambda_{w}^{\ast}\Lambda_{w}Pf\ d\mu(w),\quad\forall f\in H.

is called the frame operator of
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
. It is a positive invertible operator and
S_{P\Lambda Q}=QS_{\Lambda}P
.

Example 1.5
.

Let
P
and
Q
be any positive definite matrices of order
2
. For each
w\in[0,2\pi]
, we define
\Lambda_{w}=\begin{pmatrix}\cos w\\
\sin w\end{pmatrix}
. Then
\{P\Lambda_{w}Q\}
is a
(P,Q)
-controlled continuous
g
-frame for
\mathbb{R}^{2}
.

2.
controlled continuous
g
-frames in Hilbert spaces

In this section, we present certain conditions under which continuous
g
-frames become
(P,Q)
-controlled continuous
g
-frames. Given any operators
P,Q\in GL^{+}(H)
, if we have a
(P,Q)
-controlled continuous
g
-frame
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
for
H
with respect to
\{H_{w}\}_{w}
, we shall get frame bounds of the continuous
g
-frame
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
in terms of controlled continuous
g
-frame bounds and operator norms of
(PQ)^{\frac{1}{2}}
and
(PQ)^{-\frac{1}{2}}
. It is very useful in estimating bounds of some continuous
g
-frame provided it is a
(P,Q)
-controlled continuous
g
-frame for some operators
P,Q\in GL^{+}(H)
.

Theorem 2.1
.

Let
P,Q\in GL^{+}(H)
. Then
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w\in\Omega}
is a
(P,Q)
-controlled continuous
g
-frame for
H
with respect to
\{H_{w}\}_{w}
if and only if
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
is a continuous
g
-frame for
H
with respect to
\{H_{w}\}_{w}
.

Proof.

Assume that
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
is a
(P,Q)
-controlled continuous
g
-frame for
H
with respect to
\{H_{w}\}_{w}
with bounds
A
and
B
. Then for each
f\in H
, we have

\displaystyle A\|f\|^{2}

\displaystyle=A\|(PQ)^{\frac{1}{2}}(PQ)^{-\frac{1}{2}}f\|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq A\|(PQ)^{\frac{1}{2}}\|^{2}\|(PQ)^{-\frac{1}{2}}f\|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\|(PQ)^{\frac{1}{2}}\|^{2}\int_{\Omega}\langle\Lambda_{w}P(PQ)^{-\frac{1}{2}}f,\Lambda_{w}Q(PQ)^{-\frac{1}{2}}f\rangle\ d\mu(w)

\displaystyle=\|(PQ)^{\frac{1}{2}}\|^{2}\Big\langle\int_{\Omega}Q\Lambda_{w}^{\ast}\Lambda_{w}P(PQ)^{-\frac{1}{2}}f\ d\mu(w),(PQ)^{-\frac{1}{2}}f\Big\rangle

\displaystyle=\|(PQ)^{\frac{1}{2}}\|^{2}\Big\langle QS_{\Lambda}P(PQ)^{-\frac{1}{2}}f,(PQ)^{-\frac{1}{2}}f\Big\rangle

\displaystyle=\|(PQ)^{\frac{1}{2}}\|^{2}\langle S_{\Lambda}P^{\frac{1}{2}}Q^{-\frac{1}{2}}f,Q^{\frac{1}{2}}P^{-\frac{1}{2}}f\rangle

\displaystyle=\|(PQ)^{\frac{1}{2}}\|^{2}\langle Q^{\frac{1}{2}}P^{-\frac{1}{2}}S_{\Lambda}P^{\frac{1}{2}}Q^{-\frac{1}{2}}f,f\rangle

\displaystyle=\|(PQ)^{\frac{1}{2}}\|^{2}\langle S_{\Lambda}f,f\rangle,

hence,

\frac{A}{\|(PQ)^{\frac{1}{2}}\|^{2}}\|f\|^{2}\leq\int_{\Omega}\|\Lambda_{w}f\|^{2}\ d\mu(w).

On the other hand for each
f\in H
, we have

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}\|\Lambda_{w}f\|^{2}\ d\mu(w)

\displaystyle=\langle S_{\Lambda}f,f\rangle

\displaystyle=\langle(PQ)^{-\frac{1}{2}}(PQ)^{\frac{1}{2}}S_{\Lambda}f,f\rangle

\displaystyle=\langle(PQ)^{\frac{1}{2}}S_{\Lambda}f,(PQ)^{-\frac{1}{2}}f\rangle

\displaystyle=\langle S_{\Lambda}(PQ)(PQ)^{-\frac{1}{2}}f,(PQ)^{-\frac{1}{2}}f\rangle

\displaystyle=\langle QS_{\Lambda}P(PQ)^{-\frac{1}{2}}f,(PQ)^{-\frac{1}{2}}f\rangle

\displaystyle=\langle S_{P\Lambda Q}(PQ)^{-\frac{1}{2}}f,(PQ)^{-\frac{1}{2}}f\rangle

\displaystyle\leq B\|(PQ)^{-\frac{1}{2}}\|^{2}\|f\|^{2}.

Finally, we conclude that
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
is a continuous
g
-frame for
H
with respect to
\{H_{w}\}_{w}
with bounds
\frac{A}{\|(PQ)^{\frac{1}{2}}\|^{2}}
and
B\|(PQ)^{-\frac{1}{2}}\|^{2}
.

Conversely, suppose that
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
is a continuous
g
-frame for
H
with respect to
\{H_{w}\}_{w}
with bounds
A
and
B
. Then,

A\langle f,f\rangle\leq\langle S_{\Lambda}f,f\rangle\leq B\langle f,f\rangle,\quad\forall f\in H.

Since
P,Q\in GL^{+}(H)
, by Lemma
1.1
, there exist constants
\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2},\beta_{1},\beta_{2}>0
such that

\alpha_{1}I_{H}\leq P\leq\beta_{1}I_{H},\quad\alpha_{2}I_{H}\leq Q\leq\beta_{2}I_{H}.

So,

\alpha_{1}\alpha_{2}A\|f\|^{2}\leq\int_{\Omega}\langle\Lambda_{w}Pf,\Lambda_{w}Qf\rangle\ d\mu(w)\leq\beta_{1}\beta_{2}B\|f\|^{2},\quad\forall f\in H.

Therefore
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
is a
(P,Q)
-controlled continuous
g
-frame for
H
with respect to
\{H_{w}\}_{w}
.
∎

Theorem 2.2
.

Let
P,Q\in GL^{+}(H)
. Then
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
is a
(P,Q)
-controlled continuous
g
-frame for
H
with respect to
\{H_{w}\}_{w}
if and only if
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
is a
((PQ)^{\frac{1}{2}},(PQ)^{\frac{1}{2}})
-controlled continuous
g
-frame for
H
with respect to
\{H_{w}\}_{w}
.

Proof.

The proof follows from the following facts :

For any
f\in H
, we have

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}\langle\Lambda_{w}Pf,\Lambda_{w}Qf\rangle\ d\mu(w)

\displaystyle=\Big\langle\int_{\Omega}Q\Lambda_{w}^{\ast}\Lambda_{w}Pf\ d\mu(w),f\Big\rangle

\displaystyle=\langle QS_{\Lambda}Pf,f\rangle

\displaystyle=\langle QPS_{\Lambda}f,f\rangle

\displaystyle=\langle(PQ)^{\frac{1}{2}}S_{\Lambda}(PQ)^{\frac{1}{2}}f,f\rangle

\displaystyle=\Big\langle\int_{\Omega}(PQ)^{\frac{1}{2}}\Lambda_{w}^{\ast}\Lambda_{w}(PQ)^{\frac{1}{2}}f\ d\mu(w),f\Big\rangle

\displaystyle=\int_{\Omega}\langle\Lambda_{w}(PQ)^{\frac{1}{2}}f,\Lambda_{w}(PQ)^{\frac{1}{2}}f\rangle\ d\mu(w).

Suppose that
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
is a
(P,Q)
-controlled continuous
g
-frame for
H
with respect to
\{H_{w}\}_{w}
. Hence

A\|f\|^{2}\leq\int_{\Omega}\langle\Lambda_{w}(PQ)^{\frac{1}{2}}f,\Lambda_{w}(PQ)^{\frac{1}{2}}f\rangle\ d\mu(w)\leq B\|f\|^{2},\quad\forall f\in H

where
A
and
B
are continuous frame bounds of
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
. Thus
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
is a
((PQ)^{\frac{1}{2}},(PQ)^{\frac{1}{2}})
-controlled continuous
g
-frame for
H
with respect to.
\{H_{w}\}_{w}
. In a similar way, the other implication can be proved.
∎

Theorem 2.3
.

Let
P,Q\in GL^{+}(H)
. Then
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
is a
(P,Q)
-controlled continuous
g
-frame for
H
with respect to
\{H_{w}\}_{w}
if and only if
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
is a
PQ
-controlled continuous
g
-frame for
H
with respect to
\{H_{w}\}_{w}
.

Proof.

The proof is similar to that of Theorem
2.2
.
∎

Definition 2.4
.

Let
(\Omega,\mu)
be a positive measure space. For each
w\in\Omega
, let
\Omega_{w}
be a measurable subset of
\Omega
. We say that the collection
\{e_{w,v}\}_{v\in\Omega_{w}}
is a continuous orthonormal basis of
H_{w}
if

(1)

f=\int_{\Omega_{w}}\langle f,e_{w,v}\rangle e_{w,v}\ d\mu(w)
, for all
f\in H_{w}
;

(2)

\langle e_{w,v},e_{w,v}\rangle=1
,
\forall v\in\Omega_{w}
;

(3)

\langle e_{w,v_{1}},e_{w,v_{2}}\rangle=0
,
\forall v_{1},v_{2}\in\Omega_{w}
and
v_{1}\neq v_{2}
.

Theorem 2.5
.

Let
P,Q\in GL^{+}(H)
. Then
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
is a
(P,Q)
-controlled continuous
g
-frame for
H
with respect to
\{H_{w}\}_{w}
if and only if
\{u_{w,v}\}_{w\in\Omega,v\in\Omega_{w}}
is a
(P,Q)
-controlled continuous
g
-frame for
H
, where
\{u_{w,v}\}_{w\in\Omega,v\in\Omega_{w}}
is the sequence induced by
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
with respect to
\{e_{w,v}\}_{w\in\Omega,v\in\Omega_{w}}
(i.e.,
u_{w,v}=\Lambda_{w}^{\ast}e_{w,v}
).

Proof.

For each
w\in\Omega
, let
\{e_{w,v}\}_{v\in\Omega_{w}}
be an continuous orthonormal basis for
H_{w}
. Then we have

\Lambda_{w}Pf=\int_{\Omega_{w}}\langle\Lambda_{w}Pf,e_{w,v}\rangle e_{w,v}\ d\mu(v)=\int_{\Omega_{w}}\langle f,P\Lambda_{w}^{\ast}e_{w,v}\rangle e_{w,v}\ d\mu(v)

and

\Lambda_{w}Qf=\int_{\Omega_{w}}\langle\Lambda_{w}Qf,e_{w,v}\rangle e_{w,v}d\mu(v)=\int_{\Omega_{w}}\langle f,Q\Lambda_{w}^{\ast}e_{w,v}\rangle e_{w,v}\ d\mu(v).

It is easy to check that

\displaystyle\langle\Lambda_{w}Pf,\Lambda_{w}Qf\rangle

\displaystyle=\int_{\Omega_{w}}\langle f,Pu_{w,v}\rangle\langle Qu_{w,v},f\rangle\ d\mu(v)

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}\langle\Lambda_{w}Pf,\Lambda_{w}Qf\rangle\ d\mu(w)

\displaystyle=\int_{\Omega}\int_{\Omega_{w}}\langle f,Pu_{w,v}\rangle\langle Qu_{w,v},f\rangle\ d\mu(v)\ d\mu(w).

Hence

A\|f\|^{2}\leq\int_{\Omega}\langle\Lambda_{w}Pf,\Lambda_{w}Qf\rangle\ d\mu(w)\leq B\|f\|^{2},\quad\forall f\in H.

Thus

A\|f\|^{2}\leq\int_{\Omega}\int_{\Omega_{w}}\langle f,Pu_{w,v}\rangle\langle Qu_{w,v},f\rangle\ d\mu(v)\ d\mu(w)\leq B\|f\|^{2},\quad\forall f\in H.

The proof is completed.
∎

Theorem 2.6
.

Let
P,Q\in GL^{+}(H)
. Then
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
is a
(P,Q)
-controlled continuous
g
-frame for
H
with respect to
\{H_{w}\}_{w}
if and only if
\{Pu_{w,v}\}_{w\in\Omega,v\in\Omega_{w}}
is a
QP^{-1}
-controlled continuous
g
-frame for
H
, where
\{u_{w,v}\}_{w\in\Omega,v\in\Omega_{w}}
is the sequence induced by
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
with respect to
\{e_{w,v}\}_{w\in\Omega,v\in\Omega_{w}}
(i.e.,
u_{w,v}=\Lambda_{w}^{\ast}e_{w,v}
).

Proof.

From the proof of Theorem
2.5
, we have

\int_{\Omega}\langle\Lambda_{w}Pf,\Lambda_{w}Qf\rangle\ d\mu(w)=\int_{\Omega}\int_{\Omega_{w}}\langle f,Pu_{w,v}\rangle\langle Qu_{w,v},f\rangle\ d\mu(v)\ d\mu(w).

Then

A\|f\|^{2}\leq\int_{\Omega}\langle\Lambda_{w}Pf,\Lambda_{w}Qf\rangle\ d\mu(w)\leq B\|f\|^{2},\quad\forall f\in H,

is equivalent to

A\|f\|^{2}\leq\int_{\Omega}\int_{\Omega_{w}}\langle f,Pu_{w,v}\rangle\langle QP^{-1}Pu_{w,v},f\rangle d\mu(v)\ d\mu(w)\leq B\|f\|^{2},\quad\forall f\in H.

Hence the proof is completed.
∎

Combining all results presented in the section, we get the following result which gives several interesting characterizations of
(P,Q)
-controlled continuous
g
-frames for
H
with respect to
\{H_{w}\}_{w}
.

Theorem 2.7
.

Let
P,Q\in GL^{+}(H)
. Then the following are equivalent:

(1)

\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
is a
(P,Q)
-controlled continuous
g
-frame for
H
with respect to
\{H_{w}\}_{w}
.

(2)

\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
is a
((PQ)^{\frac{1}{2}},(PQ)^{\frac{1}{2}})
-controlled continuous
g
-frame for
H
with respect to
\{H_{w}\}_{w}
.

(3)

\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
is a
QP
-controlled continuous
g
-frame for
H
with respect to
\{H_{w}\}_{w}
.

(4)

\{u_{w,v}\}_{w\in\Omega,v\in\Omega_{w}}
is a
(P,Q)
-controlled continuous
g
-frame for
H
, where
\{u_{w,v}\}_{w\in\Omega,v\in\Omega_{w}}
is the sequence induced by
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
with respect to
\{e_{w,v}\}_{w\in\Omega,v\in\Omega_{w}}
.

(5)

\{Pu_{w,v}\}_{w\in\Omega,v\in\Omega_{w}}
is a
QP^{-1}
-controlled continuous
g
-frame for
H
, where
\{u_{w,v}\}_{w\in\Omega,v\in\Omega_{w}}
is the sequence induced by
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
with respect to
\{e_{w,v}\}_{w\in\Omega,v\in\Omega_{w}}
.

(6)

\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
is a continuous
g
-frame for
H
with respect to
\{H_{w}\}_{w}
.

Moreover,
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
is a continuous
g
-frame for
H
with respect to
\{H_{w}\}_{w}
with bounds
\frac{A}{\|(PQ)^{\frac{1}{2}}\|^{2}}
and
B\|(PQ)^{-\frac{1}{2}}\|^{2}
.

3.
Controlled continuous dual
g
-frames in Hilbert spaces

In this section, we define controlled continuous dual
g
-frames and characterize them by operator theory.

Definition 3.1
.

Let
P,Q\in GL^{+}(H)
and let
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w},\{\Gamma_{w}\}_{w}
be
(P,P)
-controlled continuous and
(Q,Q)
-controlled continuous
g
-Bessel families for
H
with respect to
\{H_{w}\}_{w}
, respectively.
We say that
\{\Gamma_{w}\}_{w}
is a
(P,Q)
-controlled continuous dual
g
-frame of
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
if

f=\int_{\Omega}P\Lambda_{w}^{\ast}\Gamma_{w}Qf\ d\mu(w),\quad\forall f\in H.

In particular, if
Q=I_{H}
,
\{\Gamma_{w}\}_{w}
is called a
P
-controlled continuous dual
g
-frame of
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
.

Definition 3.2
.

Let
P,Q\in GL^{+}(H)
and let
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w},\{\Gamma_{w}\}_{w}
be
(P,P)
-controlled continuous and
(Q,Q)
-controlled continuous
g
-Bessel families for
H
with respect to
\{H_{w}\}_{w}
respectively.
For the pair
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
and
\{\Gamma_{w}\}_{w}
, we define a
(P,Q)
-controlled continuous dual
g
-frame operator
S_{P\Lambda\Gamma Q}
by

S_{P\Lambda\Gamma Q}f=\int_{\Omega}P\Lambda_{w}^{\ast}\Gamma_{w}Qf\ d\mu(w),\quad\forall f\in H.

It is easy to check that
S_{P\Lambda\Gamma Q}
is a well-defined bounded operator, and

S_{P\Lambda\Gamma Q}=T_{P\Lambda P}T_{Q\Gamma Q}^{\ast}=PT_{\Lambda}T_{\Gamma}^{\ast}Q=PS_{\Lambda\Gamma}Q,

where
S_{\Lambda\Gamma}f=\int_{\Omega}\Lambda_{w}^{\ast}\Gamma_{w}f\ d\mu(w)
. Note that
\{\Gamma_{w}\}_{w}
is a
(P,Q)
-controlled continuous dual
g
-frame of
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
if and only if
S_{P\Lambda\Gamma Q}=I_{H}
.

Theorem 3.3
.

Let
P,Q\in GL^{+}(H)
and let
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w},\{\Gamma_{w}\}_{w}
be
(P,P)
-controlled continuous and
(Q,Q)
-controlled continuous
g
-Bessel families with bounds
B_{\Lambda}
and
B_{\Gamma}
respectively. If
S_{P\Lambda\Gamma Q}
is bounded below, then
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
and
\{\Gamma_{w}\}_{w}
are
(P,P)
-controlled continuous and
(Q,Q)
-controlled continuous
g
-frames respectively.

Proof.

Suppose that there exists a constant
\lambda>0
such that

\|S_{P\Lambda\Gamma Q}f\|\geq\lambda\|f\|,\quad\forall f\in H.

Hence

\displaystyle\lambda\|f\|

\displaystyle\leq\|S_{P\Lambda\Gamma Q}f\|

\displaystyle=\sup_{\|g\|=1}\Big|\langle\int_{\Omega}P\Lambda_{w}^{\ast}\Gamma_{w}Qf\ d\mu(w),g\rangle\Big|

\displaystyle=\sup_{\|g\|=1}\Big|\int_{\Omega}\langle\Gamma_{w}Qf,\Lambda_{w}Pg\rangle\ d\mu(w)\Big|

\displaystyle\leq\sup_{\|g\|=1}\bigg(\int_{\Omega}\|\Gamma_{w}Qf\|^{2}\ d\mu(w))\bigg)^{\frac{1}{2}}\bigg(\int_{\Omega}\|\Lambda_{w}Pg\|^{2}\ d\mu(w)\bigg)^{\frac{1}{2}}

\displaystyle\leq\sqrt{B_{\Lambda}}\bigg(\int_{\Omega}\|\Gamma_{w}Qf\|^{2}\ d\mu(w)\bigg)^{\frac{1}{2}}.

Thus

\frac{\lambda^{2}}{B_{\Lambda}}\|f\|^{2}\leq\int_{\Omega}\|\Gamma_{w}Qf\|^{2}\ d\mu(w),\quad\forall f\in H.

On the other hand, Since

S_{P\Lambda\Gamma Q}^{\ast}=(PS_{\Lambda\Gamma}Q)^{\ast}=QS_{\Gamma\Lambda}P=S_{Q\Gamma\Lambda P},

then
S_{Q\Gamma\Lambda P}
is also bounded below. Similarly, we can prove that
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
is a
(P,P)
-controlled continuous
g
-frame. Hence the proof is completed.
∎

Theorem 3.4
.

Let
P,Q\in GL^{+}(H)
,
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
and let
\{\Gamma_{w}\}_{w}
be
(P,P)
-controlled continuous and
(Q,Q)
-controlled continuous
g
-Bessel families for
H
with respect to
\{H_{w}\}_{w}
respectively. Then the following conditions are equivalent:

(1)

f=\int_{\Omega}P\Lambda_{w}^{\ast}\Gamma_{w}Qf\ d\mu(w)
,
\ \forall f\in H
.

(2)

f=\int_{\Omega}Q\Gamma_{w}^{\ast}\Lambda_{w}Pf\ d\mu(w)
,
\ \forall f\in H
.

(3)

\langle f,g\rangle=\int_{\Omega}\langle\Lambda_{w}Pf,\Gamma_{w}Qg\rangle\ d\mu(w)=\int_{\Omega}\langle\Gamma_{w}Qf,\Lambda_{w}Pg\rangle\ d\mu(w),\ \forall f,g\in H
.

(4)

\|f\|^{2}=\int_{\Omega}\langle\Lambda_{w}Pf,\Gamma_{w}Qf\rangle\ d\mu(w)=\int_{\Omega}\langle\Gamma_{w}Qf,\Lambda_{w}Pf\rangle\ d\mu(w),\ \forall f\in H
.

In case the equivalent conditions are satisfied,
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
and
\{\Gamma_{w}\}_{w}
are
(P,P)
-controlled continuous and
(Q,Q)
-controlled continuous
g
-frames respectively.

Proof.

(1)\Longleftrightarrow(2)
. Let
T_{P\Lambda P}
be the synthesis operator of the
(P,P)
-controlled continuous
g
-Bessel family
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
and
T_{Q\Gamma Q}
be the synthesis operator of the
(P,P)
-controlled continuous
g
-Bessel family
\{\Gamma_{w}\}_{w}
. Condition
(1)
means that
T_{P\Lambda P}T_{Q\Gamma Q}^{\ast}=I_{H}
, and it is equivalent to
T_{Q\Gamma Q}T_{P\Lambda P}^{\ast}
, which is identical to the statement
(2)
. Conversely,
(2)\implies(1)
similarly.

(2)\Longleftrightarrow(3)
. It is clear that
(2)\implies(3)
. For any
f,g\in H
,
\langle f,g\rangle=\int_{\Omega}\langle\Lambda_{w}Pf,\Gamma_{w}Qg\rangle\ d\mu(w)
shows that

\Big\langle f-\int_{\Omega}Q\Gamma_{w}^{\ast}\Lambda_{w}Pf\ d\mu(w),g\Big\rangle=0,\quad\forall g\in H.

Hence
(3)\Longrightarrow(2)
is proved.

(3)\Longleftrightarrow(4)
.
(3)\implies(4)
is obvious. To prove
(4)\implies(3)
, we apply condition
(4)
and get

\displaystyle\|f+g\|^{2}

\displaystyle=\int_{\Omega}\langle\Lambda_{w}P(f+g),\Gamma_{w}Q(f+g)\rangle\ d\mu(w)

\displaystyle=\int_{\Omega}\langle\Lambda_{w}Pf,\Gamma_{w}Qf\rangle\ d\mu(w)+\int_{\Omega}\langle\Lambda_{w}Pf,\Gamma_{w}Qg\rangle\ d\mu(w)

\displaystyle\quad+\int_{\Omega}\langle\Lambda_{w}Pg,\Gamma_{w}Qf\rangle\ d\mu(w)+\int_{\Omega}\langle\Lambda_{w}Pg,\Gamma_{w}Qg\rangle\ d\mu(w).

Similarly,

\displaystyle\|f-g\|^{2}

\displaystyle=\int_{\Omega}\langle\Lambda_{w}Pf,\Gamma_{w}Qf\rangle\ d\mu(w)-\int_{\Omega}\langle\Lambda_{w}Pf,\Gamma_{w}Qg\rangle\ d\mu(w)

\displaystyle\quad-\int_{\Omega}\langle\Lambda_{w}Pg,\Gamma_{w}Qf\rangle\ d\mu(w)+\int_{\Omega}\langle\Lambda_{w}Pg,\Gamma_{w}Qg\rangle\ d\mu(w).

\displaystyle\|f+ig\|^{2}

\displaystyle=\int_{\Omega}\langle\Lambda_{w}Pf,\Gamma_{w}Qf\rangle\ d\mu(w)-i\int_{\Omega}\langle\Lambda_{w}Pf,\Gamma_{w}Qg\rangle\ d\mu(w)

\displaystyle\quad+i\int_{\Omega}\langle\Lambda_{w}Pg,\Gamma_{w}Qf\rangle\ d\mu(w)+\int_{\Omega}\langle\Lambda_{w}Pg,\Gamma_{w}Qg\rangle\ d\mu(w).

\displaystyle\|f-ig\|^{2}

\displaystyle=\int_{\Omega}\langle\Lambda_{w}Pf,\Gamma_{w}Qf\rangle\ d\mu(w)+i\int_{\Omega}\langle\Lambda_{w}Pf,\Gamma_{w}Qg\rangle\ d\mu(w)

\displaystyle\quad-i\int_{\Omega}\langle\Lambda_{w}Pg,\Gamma_{w}Qf\rangle\ d\mu(w)+\int_{\Omega}\langle\Lambda_{w}Pg,\Gamma_{w}Qg\rangle\ d\mu(w).

By polarization identity,

\displaystyle\langle f,g\rangle

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{4}\bigg(\|f+g\|^{2}-\|f-g\|^{2}+i\|f+ig\|^{2}-i\|f-ig\|^{2}\bigg)

\displaystyle=\int_{\Omega}\langle\Lambda_{w}Pf,\Gamma_{w}Qg\rangle\ d\mu(w).

In case the equivalent conditions are satisfied,
S_{Q\Gamma\Lambda P}=I_{H}
implies
\|S_{Q\Gamma\Lambda P}\|=1
, hence by Theorem
3.3
,
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
and
\{\Gamma_{w}\}_{w}
are
(P,P)
-controlled continuous and
(Q,Q)
-controlled continuous
g
-frames respectively.
∎

Theorem 3.5
.

Let
P,Q\in GL^{+}(H)
. A sequence
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
is a
(P,Q)
-controlled continuous
g
-Bessel family for
H
with respect to
\{H_{w}\}_{w}
with bound
B
if and only if the operator
T_{P\Lambda Q}:\ell^{2}(\{H_{w}\}_{w})\rightarrow H
given by

T_{P\Lambda Q}(\{f_{w}\}_{w})=\int_{\Omega}(PQ)^{\frac{1}{2}}\Lambda_{w}^{\ast}f_{w}\ d\mu(w)

is well-defined and bounded with
\|T_{P\Lambda Q}\|\leq\sqrt{B}
.

Proof.

The necessary condition follows from the definition of
(P,Q)
-controlled continuous
g
-Bessel sequence. We only need to prove the sufficient condition. Suppose that
T_{P\Lambda Q}
is well-defined and bound operator with
\|T_{P\Lambda Q}\|\leq\sqrt{B}
. For any
f\in H
, we have

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}\langle\Lambda_{w}Pf,\Lambda_{w}Qf\rangle\ d\mu(w)

\displaystyle=\int_{\Omega}\langle Q\Lambda_{w}^{\ast}\Lambda_{w}Pf,f\rangle\ d\mu(w)

\displaystyle=\langle QS_{\Lambda}Pf,f\rangle

\displaystyle=\langle(QP)^{\frac{1}{2}}S_{\Lambda}(QP)^{\frac{1}{2}}f,f\rangle

\displaystyle=\Big\langle\int_{\Omega}(QP)^{\frac{1}{2}}\Lambda_{w}^{\ast}\Lambda_{w}(QP)^{\frac{1}{2}}f\ d\mu(w),f\Big\rangle

\displaystyle\leq\|T_{P\Lambda Q}\|\bigg(\int_{\Omega}\|\Lambda_{w}(QP)^{\frac{1}{2}}f\|^{2}\ d\mu(w)\bigg)^{\frac{1}{2}}\|f\|

\displaystyle=\|T_{P\Lambda Q}\|\bigg(\int_{\Omega}\langle\Lambda_{w}Pf,\Lambda_{w}Qf\rangle\ d\mu(w)\bigg)^{\frac{1}{2}}\|f\|.

Hence we get

\int_{\Omega}\langle\Lambda_{w}Pf,\Lambda_{w}Qf\rangle\ d\mu(w)\leq\|T_{P\Lambda Q}\|^{2}\|f\|^{2}\leq B\|f\|^{2}.

This shows that
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
is a
(P,Q)
-controlled continuous
g
-Bessel family for
H
with respect to
\{H_{w}\}_{w}
with bound
B
.
∎

Theorem 3.6
.

Let
P,Q\in GL^{+}(H)
and
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
be a
(P,P)
-controlled
g
-frame for
H
with respect to
\{H_{w}\}_{w}
with the synthesis operator
T_{P\Lambda P}
. Then a
(Q,Q)
-controlled continuous
g
-frame
\{\Gamma_{w}\}_{w}
is a
(P,Q)
-controlled continuous dual
g
-frame of
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
if and only if

Q\Gamma_{w}^{\ast}e_{w,v}=U(e_{w,v}\delta_{w}),\quad w\in\Omega,v\in\Omega_{w}

where
U:\ell^{2}(\{H_{w}\}_{w})\rightarrow H
is a bounded left-inverse of
T_{P\Lambda P}^{\ast}
.

Proof.

If
\{g_{w}\}_{w}\in\ell^{2}(\{H_{w}\}_{w})
, then

\{g_{w}\}_{w}=\int_{\Omega}g_{w}\delta_{w}\ d\mu(w)=\int_{\Omega}\int_{\Omega_{w}}\langle g_{w},e_{w,v}\rangle e_{w,v}\delta_{w}\ \ d\mu(v)\ d\mu(w).

Roughly speaking
\{e_{w,v}\delta_{w}\}_{w\in\Omega,v\in\Omega_{w}}
is a continuous orthonormal basis of
\ell^{2}(\{H_{w}\}_{w})
.

If there exists
U:\ell^{2}(\{H_{w}\}_{w})\rightarrow H
is a bounded left-inverse of
T_{P\Lambda P}^{\ast}
such that

Q\Gamma_{w}^{\ast}e_{w,v}=U(e_{w,v}\delta_{w}),\quad w\in\Omega,v\in\Omega_{w}.

For any
f\in H
, we have

\displaystyle f

\displaystyle=UT_{P\Lambda P}^{\ast}f

\displaystyle=U\bigg(\int_{\Omega}\int_{\Omega_{w}}\langle\Lambda_{w}Pf,e_{w,v}\rangle e_{w,v}\delta_{w}\ d\mu(v)\ d\mu(w)\bigg)

\displaystyle=\int_{\Omega}\int_{\Omega_{w}}\langle f,P\Lambda_{w}^{\ast}e_{w,v}\rangle U(e_{w,v}\delta_{w})\ d\mu(v)\ d\mu(w)

\displaystyle=\int_{\Omega}\int_{\Omega_{w}}\langle f,Pu_{w,v}\rangle Q\Gamma_{w}^{\ast}e_{w,v}\ d\mu(v)\ d\mu(w)

\displaystyle=\int_{\Omega}Q\Gamma_{w}^{\ast}\bigg(\int_{\Omega_{w}}\langle Pf,u_{w,v}\rangle e_{w,v}\ d\mu(v)\bigg)\ d\mu(w)

\displaystyle=\int_{\Omega}Q\Gamma_{w}^{\ast}\Lambda_{w}Pf\ d\mu(w),

where
u_{w,v}=\Lambda_{w}^{\ast}e_{w,v}
. By the definition of controlled continuous dual
g
-frame,
\{\Gamma_{w}\}_{w}
is a
(P,Q)
-controlled continuous dual
g
-frame of
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
.

On the other hand, suppose that a
(Q,Q)
-controlled continuous
g
-frame
\{\Gamma_{w}\}_{w}
is a
(P,Q)
-controlled continuous dual
g
-frame of
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
. For any
f\in H
, we have

f=\int_{\Omega}P\Lambda_{w}^{\ast}\Gamma_{w}^{\ast}Qf\ d\mu(w)=\int_{\Omega}Q\Gamma_{w}^{\ast}\Lambda_{w}Pf\ d\mu(w),

that is,
T_{Q\Lambda Q}T_{P\Lambda P}^{\ast}=I_{H}
. Let
U=T_{Q\Gamma Q}
. Then
U:\ell^{2}(\{H_{w}\}_{w})\rightarrow H
is a bounded left-inverse of
T_{P\Lambda P}^{\ast}
. A calculation as above shows that

\int_{\Omega}\int_{\Omega_{w}}\langle f,Pu_{w,v}\rangle Q\Gamma_{w}^{\ast}e_{w,v}\ d\mu(v)\ d\mu(w)=f=\int_{\Omega}\int_{\Omega_{w}}\langle f,Pu_{w,v}\rangle U(e_{w,v}\delta_{w})\ d\mu(v)\ d\mu(w),\forall f\in H.

Combining this with the fact
\{e_{w,v}\}_{v\in\Omega_{w}}
is a continuous orthonormal basis of
H_{w}
, we have

Q\Gamma_{w}^{\ast}e_{w,v}=U(e_{w,v}\delta_{w}),\quad w\in\Omega,v\in\Omega_{w}.

∎

Theorem 3.7
.

Let
P\in GL^{+}(H)
and
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
be a
(P,P)
-controlled continuous
g
-frame for
H
with respect to
\{H_{w}\}_{w}
with the synthesis operator and frame operator
T_{P\Lambda P}
and
S_{P\Lambda P}
, respectively. Then
\{\Gamma_{w}\}_{w}
is a
P
-controlled continuous dual
g
-frame of
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w}
if and only if

\Gamma_{w}f=(Tf)_{w}+\Lambda_{w}S_{P\Lambda P}^{-1}Pf,w\in\Omega,v\in H,

where
T:H\rightarrow\ell^{2}(\{H_{w}\}_{w})
is a bounded linear operator satisfying
T_{P\Lambda P}T=0
.

Proof.

If
T:H\rightarrow\ell^{2}(\{H_{w}\}_{w})
is a bounded linear operator satisfying
T_{P\Lambda P}T=0
, then
\{\Gamma_{w}\}_{w}
is a
g
-Bessel family for
H
with respect to
\{H_{w}\}_{w}
. In fact, any
f\in H
we have

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}\|\Gamma_{w}f\|^{2}\ d\mu(w)

\displaystyle=\int_{\Omega}\|(Tf)_{w}+\Lambda_{w}S_{P\Lambda P}^{-1}Pf\|^{2}\ d\mu(w)

\displaystyle\leq 2\bigg(\int_{\Omega}\|\Lambda_{w}S_{P\Lambda P}^{-1}Pf\|^{2}\ d\mu(w)+\|Tf\|^{2}\bigg)

\displaystyle\leq 2(B\|S_{P\Lambda P}^{-1}P\|^{2}+\|T\|^{2})\|f\|^{2},

where
B
is the upper bound of
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w\in\Omega}
. Furthermore,

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}P\Lambda_{w}^{\ast}\Gamma_{w}f\ d\mu(w)

\displaystyle=\int_{\Omega}P\Lambda_{w}^{\ast}((Tf)_{w}+\Lambda_{w}S_{P\Lambda P}^{-1}Pf)\ d\mu(w)

\displaystyle=T_{P\Lambda P}Tf+\int_{\Omega}P\Lambda_{w}^{\ast}\Lambda_{w}S_{P\Lambda P}^{-1}Pf\ d\mu(w)=f.

Thus
\{\Gamma_{w}\}_{w\in\Omega}
is a
P
-controlled continuous dual
g
-frame of
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w\in\Omega}
.

Now we prove the converse. Assume that
\{\Gamma_{w}\}_{w\in\Omega}
is a
P
-controlled continuous dual
g
-frame of
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w\in\Omega}
. Define the operator
T
as follows:

T:H\rightarrow\ell^{2}(\{H_{w}\}_{w\in\Omega}),\quad f\rightarrow(Tf)_{w},\quad f\in H

satisfying

\Gamma_{w}f=(Tf)_{w}+\Lambda_{w}S_{P\Lambda P}^{-1}Pf,w\in\Omega.

For any
f\in H
, we have

\displaystyle\|Tf\|^{2}

\displaystyle=\int_{\Omega}\|\Gamma_{w}f-\Lambda_{w}S^{-1}_{P\Lambda P}Pf\|^{2}\ d\mu(w)

\displaystyle\leq\int_{\Omega}\|\Gamma_{w}f\|^{2}\ d\mu(w)+\int_{\Omega}\|\Lambda_{w}S_{P\Lambda P}^{-1}Pf\|^{2}\ d\mu(w)

\displaystyle\quad+2\bigg(\int_{\Omega}\|\Gamma_{w}f\|^{2}\ d\mu(w)\bigg)^{\frac{1}{2}}\bigg(\int_{\Omega}\|\Lambda_{w}S_{P\Lambda P}^{-1}Pf\|^{2}\ d\mu(w)\bigg)^{\frac{1}{2}}

\displaystyle\leq(B_{1}+A^{-1}+2\sqrt{B_{1}A^{-1}})\|f\|^{2},

where
B_{1}
is the frame upper bound of
\{\Gamma_{w}\}_{w\in\Omega}
and
A
is the frame lower bound of
\{\Lambda_{w}\}_{w\in\Omega}
. Thus
T
is a linear bounded operator. Moreover, for any
f,g\in H
, we have

\displaystyle\langle T_{P\Lambda P}Tf,g\rangle

\displaystyle=\int_{\Omega}\langle P\Lambda_{w}^{\ast}Tf,g\rangle\ d\mu(w)

\displaystyle=\int_{\Omega}\langle P\Lambda_{w}^{\ast}(\Gamma_{w}f-\Lambda_{w}S_{P\Lambda P}^{-1}Pf),g\rangle\ d\mu(w)

\displaystyle=\int_{\Omega}\langle P\Lambda_{w}^{\ast}\Gamma_{w}f,g\rangle\ d\mu(w)-\int_{\Omega}\langle P\Lambda_{w}^{\ast}\Lambda_{w}S_{P\Lambda P}^{-1}Pf,g\rangle\ d\mu(w)

\displaystyle=\langle f,g\rangle-\langle f,g\rangle=0.

That is
T_{P\Lambda P}T=0
. The proof is completed.
∎
