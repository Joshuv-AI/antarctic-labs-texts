Constructive General Neron Desingularization for one dimensional local rings
Gerhard Pfister, Dorin Popescu

Abstract
An algorithmic proof of General Neron Desingularization is given here for one dimensional local rings and it is implemented in Singular . Also a theorem recalling Greenberg’ strong approximation theorem is presented for one dimensional local rings.

An extension of Greenberg’s theorem on strong approximation

Let
(A,{\mathfrak{m}})
be a Noetherian local ring of dimension one,

the completion of
A
,

,

an
A
-algebra of finite type and

[

A
-morphism

there exists an
A
-morphism

such that

modulo

. More general, if

there exists a linear map
\nu:{\bf N}\rightarrow{\bf N}
such that for each
A
-morphism

there exists an
A
-morphism
v^{\prime}:B\rightarrow A^{\prime}
such that

modulo

.

The aim of this section is to give a result of Greenberg’s type for one dimensional rings when the Jacobian locus is not
too small
. Let
e
be as in the beginning of Section 3 and
\rho\in{\bf N}
. We will show that the map
\nu
given by

works in the special case below.
Suppose that there exists an
A
-morphism

such that
(v(((f):I)\Delta_{f}))\supset{\mathfrak{m}}^{\rho}/{\mathfrak{m}}^{(e+1)(\rho+1)+c}
for some

,
r\leq n
in

.

Theorem 14

Then there exists an
A
-morphism

.

Proof..

We note that the proof of Theorem
2
can work somehow in this case. Let

be an element inducing

. Then
{\mathfrak{m}}^{\rho}\subset(((f):I)\Delta_{f})(y^{\prime}))+{\mathfrak{m}}^{(e+1)(\rho+1)+c}\subset((((f):I)\Delta_{f})(y^{\prime}))+{\mathfrak{m}}^{(e+1)(\rho+2)+2c}\subset\ldots
by hypothesis. It follows that
{\mathfrak{m}}^{\rho}\subset((((f):I)\Delta_{f})(y^{\prime}))
.

As in Step 4 of Section 1 we may complete the Jacobian matrix
(\partial f/\partial Y)
with
n-r
rows from
k^{n}
obtaining a square

matrix

with
v(\det H)\not\in\cup_{p\in\operatorname{Min}A^{\prime}}p

Set

.
Next we follow the proof of Theorem
2
with

,

,

and

Let

where

are new variables. We have

modulo

-morphism

and

As in the proof of Theorem
2
we see that
