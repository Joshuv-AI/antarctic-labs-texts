The congruent number descent of Komotu, Watanabe and Wada.
Allan J. MacLeod

Abstract
We present an elementary exposition of the descent method used by Komoto, Watanabe and Wada to show N = 42553 N=42553 is a congruent number. We generalize to N = 7 ​ p N=7p with p p prime, and try to present the method such that an amateur could understand and perform their own calculations.

Introduction

Whilst surfing the Internet, I came across
[
1
]
. The number
N=42553=7*6079
comes from the paper by Nemenzo
[
2
]
, where it
is stated to be the smallest unproven congruent/non-congruent number (at that time). Komato, Watanabe and Wada prove it is congruent by
explicitly computing a point on the related elliptic curve. I was fascinated by the simplicity of the method, but some details were not given.
I now describe the method, for
N=7*\mbox{prime}
, in full detail.

The elliptic curve is

Y^{2}=X^{3}-N^{2}X

and with
X=du^{2}/v^{2}
,
Y=duw/v^{3}
where
d,u,v,w,\in\mathbb{Z}
, we have

d^{2}w^{2}=d^{3}u^{4}-N^{2}dv^{4}

with
d
squarefree and
d|N
,
\gcd(u,v)=1
,
\gcd(w,v)=1
.

Suppose
N=7p
where
p
is prime. Pick
d=-p
so

w^{2}=-p(u^{4}-49v^{4})

(1.1)

and, since
p|w
we can set
w=pz
, giving

pz^{2}=(49v^{4}-u^{4})=(7v^{2}+u^{2})(7v^{2}-u^{2})

(1.2)

Assume
u,v
both odd so
7v^{2}+u^{2}
is a multiple of
8
. Thus set

7v^{2}+u^{2}=8b^{2}\hskip 56.9055pt7v^{2}-u^{2}=2pa^{2}

giving
z=ab
, and

7v^{2}=4b^{2}+pa^{2}\hskip 56.9055ptu^{2}=4b^{2}-pa^{2}

The latter identity can be written

pa^{2}=4b^{2}-u^{2}=(2b+u)(2b-u)

so set

2b+u=pc^{2}\hskip 56.9055pt2b-u=d^{2}

and
a=c\,d
.

Thus,

4b=pc^{2}+d^{2}\hskip 56.9055pt2u=pc^{2}-d^{2}

From
7v^{2}=4b^{2}+pa^{2}
, we get the identity,

p^{2}c^{4}+6pc^{2}d^{2}+d^{4}=28v^{2}

(1.3)

which is what the method attempts to solve. Let
e=c^{2},f=d^{2}
so we have the quadratic

p^{2}e^{2}+6pef+f^{2}=28v^{2}

which we can write

(e,f,v)\left(\begin{array}[]{lrr}p^{2}&3p&0\\
3p&1&0\\
0&0&-28\end{array}\right)\left(\begin{array}[]{r}e\\
f\\
v\end{array}\right)=0

(1.4)

Let
(1,f_{0},v_{0})
be a solution.
e=1
makes things a lot easier later on, though we cannot always find such a solution. If we can,
f_{0}

must be odd.

Define

\left(\begin{array}[]{l}e\\
f\\
v\end{array}\right)=\left(\begin{array}[]{lrr}1&0&0\\
f_{0}&1&0\\
v_{0}&0&1\end{array}\right)\left(\begin{array}[]{l}g\\
h\\
i\end{array}\right)

(1.5)

so

2f_{0}gh+2g(3hp-28iv_{0})+h^{2}-28i^{2}=0

Thus
h
must be even and we set
h=2j
. This gives the equation

(g,j,i)\left(\begin{array}[]{lrr}0&(f_{0}+3p)/2&-7v_{0}\\
(f_{0}+3p)/2&1&0\\
-7v_{0}&0&-7\end{array}\right)\left(\begin{array}[]{r}g\\
j\\
i\end{array}\right)=0

(1.6)

What we do next is to transform the matrix so that the
(1,3)
element becomes zero and a non-zero element is in the
(2,3)
place.

Let
A=(f_{0}+3p)/2
and
B=-7v_{0}
, and assume
\gcd(A,B)=1
. Then there exists integers
\alpha,\beta
such that
\alpha\,A+\beta\,B=1
.
Then, set

\left(\begin{array}[]{l}g\\
j\\
i\end{array}\right)=\left(\begin{array}[]{lrr}1&0&0\\
0&\alpha&-B\\
0&\beta&A\end{array}\right)\left(\begin{array}[]{l}g\\
k\\
l\end{array}\right)

(1.7)

This gives

(g,k,l)\left(\begin{array}[]{lrr}0&1&0\\
1&\alpha^{2}-7\beta^{2}&-\alpha B-7\beta A\\
0&-\alpha B-7\beta A&-14p^{2}\end{array}\right)\left(\begin{array}[]{r}g\\
k\\
l\end{array}\right)=0

(1.8)

and we can simplify by setting
k=2m,l=2n
to have

m(g+(\alpha^{2}-7\beta^{2})m+7(2\alpha v_{0}-\beta(f_{0}+3p))n)=14p^{2}n^{2}

Define

r=g+(\alpha^{2}-7\beta^{2})m+7(2\alpha v_{0}-\beta(f_{0}+3p))n

(1.9)

so that our equation to solve reduces to

m\,r=14p^{2}\,n^{2}

(1.10)

Set
m=14p^{2}s^{2}
,
r=t^{2}
and
n=st
. Then

c^{2}=e=g=t^{2}+7(\beta(f_{0}+3p)-2\alpha v_{0})ts+14p^{2}(7\beta^{2}-\alpha^{2})s^{2}

so

where
K
is a constant to be determined.

With the help of a symbolic algebra package we find
K=-7
.

Defining

t+\frac{7(\beta(f_{0}+3p)-2\alpha v_{0})s}{2}=W

gives

7s^{2}=W^{2}-c^{2}=(W+c)(W-c)

Then, set
W+c=56y^{2}
,
W-c=2x^{2}
so that
s=4xy
,

and

.

We also have
d^{2}=f=f_{0}g+h=f_{0}g+4(\alpha m-Bn)
.

Using (

56(8\alpha(2p^{2}+7v_{0}^{2})-28\beta v_{0}(f_{0}+3p)-f_{0})x^{2}y^{2}+3136v_{0}xy^{3}+784f_{0}y^{4}

which must be searched for a solution.
