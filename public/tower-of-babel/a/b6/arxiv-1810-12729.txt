Abstract
. The logic 𝕃 θ 1 {\mathbb{L}}^{1}_{\theta} was introduced in [ She12 ] ; it is the maximal logic below 𝕃 θ , θ {\mathbb{L}}_{\theta,\theta} in which a well ordering is not definable. We investigate it for θ \theta a compact cardinal. We prove that it satisfies several parallel of classical theorems on first order logic, strengthening the thesis that it is a natural logic. In particular, two models are 𝕃 θ 1 {\mathbb{L}}^{1}_{\theta} -equivalent iff for some ω \omega -sequence of θ \theta -complete ultrafilters, the iterated ultra-powers by it of those two models are isomorphic. Also for strong limit λ > θ \lambda>\theta of cofinality ℵ 0 \aleph_{0} , every complete 𝕃 θ 1 {\mathbb{L}}^{1}_{\theta} -theory has a so called a special model of cardinality λ \lambda , a parallel of saturated. For first order theory T T and singular strong limit cardinal λ , T \lambda,T has a so called special model of cardinality λ \lambda . Using “special” in our context is justified by: it is unique (fixing T T and λ \lambda ), all reducts of a special model are special too, so we have another proof of interpolation in this case.
