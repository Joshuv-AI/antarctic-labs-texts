Blow-up phenomena and the local well-posedness of a Two-Component b-Family Equations in Besov spaces
Lingli Hu and, Zhaoyang Yin

Abstract
In this paper, we first establish the local well-posednesss of a Two-Component b-Family equations in nonhomogeneous Besov spaces B p , 1 1 + 1 p B^{1+\frac{1}{p}}_{p,1} with 1 ≤ p < + ∞ . 1\leq p<+\infty. Then we present a new blow-up result for the Two-Component b-Family equations.



Keywords
: A Two-Component b-Family equations, Local well-posedness, Blow-up.
