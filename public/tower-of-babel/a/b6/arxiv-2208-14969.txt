Effect of parallel magnetic field on Klein tunneling in p ​ n pn and p ​ n ​ p pnp graphene trilayer junctions
Abderrahim El Mouhafid, Ahmed Jellal

Abstract
The effect of a parallel magnetic field B B and a potential substrate on the transmission, conductivity, and Fano factor of a biased ABC-stacked p ​ n pn and p ​ n ​ p pnp trilayer graphene junction (ABC-TLG) is investigated theoretically at low energy. We discovered that, in the presence of a high magnetic field B = 1400 B=1400 T, the ABC-TLG can exhibit new Klein tunneling at two new incidence k y k_{y} values, in addition to the usual value of k y = 0 k_{y}=0 and whatever the energy value is. Indeed, the transmission of ABC-TLG via p ​ n pn and p ​ n ​ p pnp junctions is transformed into a three-separate transmission of single-like graphene (SLG), with Klein tunneling appearing at k y = 0 k_{y}=0 and k y = ± κ k_{y}=\pm\kappa ( κ \kappa is highly dependent on B B ). Furthermore, the conductivity σ \sigma and Fano factor F F behave similarly to SLG, with ABC-TLG having the lowest σ = 3 ​ g 0 / π \sigma=3g_{0}/\pi and highest F = 1 / 3 F=1/3 at B = 1400 B=1400 T and σ TLG = 3 ​ σ SLG \sigma_{\text{TLG}}=3\sigma_{\text{SLG}} .
