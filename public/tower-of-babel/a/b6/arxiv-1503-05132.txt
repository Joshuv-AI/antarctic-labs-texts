Abstract
Let p 1 p_{1} and p 2 p_{2} be different primes such that p 1 ≡ p 2 ≡ 1 ( mod 4 ) p_{1}\equiv p_{2}\equiv 1\pmod{4} and at least two of the three elements { ( 2 p 1 ) , ( 2 p 2 ) , ( p 1 p 2 ) } \{(\frac{2}{p_{1}}),(\frac{2}{p_{2}}),(\frac{p_{1}}{p_{2}})\} are equal to -1. Let k 2 ( 1 ) k_{2}^{(1)} be the Hilbert 2-class field of k k and k ( ∗ ) = Q ⁡ ( p 1 , p 2 , 2 , i ) k^{(*)}=Q(\sqrt{p_{1}},\sqrt{p_{2}},\sqrt{2},i) be its genus field. Let C k , 2 C_{k,2} denote The 2-part of the class group of k k . The unramified abelian extensions of k k are K 1 = k ⁡ ( p 1 ) K_{1}=k(\sqrt{p_{1}}) , K 2 = k ⁡ ( p 2 ) K_{2}=k(\sqrt{p_{2}}) , K 3 = k ⁡ ( 2 ) K_{3}=k(\sqrt{2}) and k ( ∗ ) k^{(*)} .
