Arrangement for K 2 ∗ K_{2}^{*} meson family
Ting-Yan Li 1,2, Ya-rong Wang 1,2, Cheng-qun Pang 1,2,3

Abstract
Two observed structures with M = 1868 ± 8 − 57 + 40 M=1868\pm 8^{+40}_{-57} MeV and M = 2073 ± 94 − 240 + 245 M=2073\pm 94^{+245}_{-240} MeV are the same states ( K 2 ∗ ​ ( 1980 ) K_{2}^{*}(1980) ) in PDG.The analysis of the mass spectrum and the calculation of the strong decay of K 2 ∗ K_{2}^{*} mesons support the low mass state of K 2 ∗ ​ ( 1980 ) K_{2}^{*}(1980) as 2 3 ​ P 2 2^{3}P_{2} and the high mass state of K 2 ∗ ​ ( 1980 ) K_{2}^{*}(1980) as 1 3 ​ F 2 1^{3}F_{2} in this letter. This analysis brings us very important criterion for the assignment of the observed K 2 ∗ ​ ( 1980 ) K_{2}^{*}(1980) and experimental findings for this assignment is suggested. Additionally, prediction of some partial decay widths are made on the high excitations of K 2 ∗ K_{2}^{*} family. This study is crucial to establishing and searching for their higher excitations in the future.

II.1
The brief review of MGI and QPC models

II.1.1
The MGI model

Godfrey and Isgur raised the GI model for describing relativistic meson spectra with great success, exactly in low-lying mesons
Godfrey and Isgur 1985
.
As for the excited states, the screening potential must be taken into account for coupled-channel effect
Eef et al. 2003
;
Liu et al. 2009
;
Dai et al. 2008
.

The interaction between quark and antiquark is depicted by the Hamiltonian of potential model including kinetic energy pieces and effective potential piece,

\tilde{H}=\sqrt{m_{1}^{2}+\mathbf{p}^{2}}+\sqrt{m_{2}^{2}+\mathbf{p}^{2}}+\tilde{V}_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathbf{p,r}),

(1)

where
m_{1}
and
m_{2}
denote the mass of quark and antiquark respectively, and effective potential
\tilde{V}_{\mathrm{eff}}
contains two ingredients, a short-range
\gamma^{\mu}\otimes\gamma_{\mu}
one-gluon-exchange interaction and a
1\otimes 1
linear confinement interaction. The meaning of tilde will be explained later.

In the nonrelativistic limit, effective potential has familiar format
Godfrey and Isgur 1985
;
Lucha et al. 1991

\displaystyle V_{\mathrm{eff}}(r)=H^{\mathrm{conf}}+H^{\mathrm{hyp}}+H^{\mathrm{so}},

(2)

with

\displaystyle H^{\mathrm{conf}}

\displaystyle=\Big[-\frac{3}{4}(c+br)+\frac{\alpha_{s}(r)}{r}\Big](\bm{F}_{1}\cdot\bm{F}_{2})

\displaystyle=S(r)+G(r)

(3)

\displaystyle H^{\mathrm{hyp}}

\displaystyle=-\frac{\alpha_{s}(r)}{m_{1}m_{2}}\Bigg[\frac{8\pi}{3}\bm{S}_{1}\cdot\bm{S}_{2}\delta^{3}(\bm{r})+\frac{1}{r^{3}}\Big(\frac{3\bm{S}_{1}\cdot\bm{r}\bm{S}_{2}\cdot\bm{r}}{r^{2}}

\displaystyle\quad-\bm{S}_{1}\cdot\bm{S}_{2}\Big)\Bigg](\bm{F}_{1}\cdot\bm{F}_{2}),

(4)

\displaystyle H^{\mathrm{so}}=

\displaystyle H^{\mathrm{so(cm)}}+H^{\mathrm{so(tp)}},

(5)

where
H^{\mathrm{conf}}
includes the spin-independent linear confinement piece
S(r)
and Coulomb-like potential from one-gluon-exchange
G(r)
.
H^{\mathrm{hyp}}
denotes the color-hyperfine interaction consisting tensor and contact terms.

H^{\mathrm{SO}}
is the spin-orbit interaction with

\displaystyle H^{\mathrm{so(cm)}}=\frac{-\alpha_{s}(r)}{r^{3}}\left(\frac{1}{m_{1}}+\frac{1}{m_{2}}\right)\left(\frac{\bm{S}_{1}}{m_{1}}+\frac{\bm{S}_{2}}{m_{2}}\right)\cdot\bm{L}(\bm{F}_{1}\cdot\bm{F}_{2}),

(6)

which is caused by one-gluon-exchange and

\displaystyle H^{\mathrm{so(tp)}}=-\frac{1}{2r}\frac{\partial H^{\mathrm{conf}}}{\partial r}\Bigg(\frac{\bm{S}_{1}}{m^{2}_{1}}+\frac{\bm{S}_{2}}{m^{2}_{2}}\Bigg)\cdot\bm{L},

(7)

which is the Thomas precession term.

For above formulas,
\bm{S}_{1}/\bm{S}_{2}
indicates the spin of quark/antiquark and
\bm{L}

is
the orbital momentum between them.
\bm{F}
is relevant to the Gell-Mann matrix, i.e.,
\bm{F}_{1}=\bm{\lambda}_{1}/2
and
\bm{F}_{2}=-\bm{\lambda}^{*}_{2}/2
, and for a meson,
\langle\bm{F}_{1}\cdot\bm{F}_{2}\rangle=-4/3
.

Now relativistic effects of distinguish influence must be considered especially in meson system, which is embedded in two ways. Firstly, based on the nonlocal interactions and new
\mathbf{r}
dependence, a smearing function is introduced for a meson
q\bar{q}

\rho\left(\mathbf{r}-\mathbf{r^{\prime}}\right)=\frac{\sigma^{3}}{\pi^{3/2}}e^{-\sigma^{2}\left(\mathbf{r}-\mathbf{r^{\prime}}\right)^{2}},

(8)

which is applied to
S(r)
and
G(r)
to obtain smeared potentials
\tilde{S}(r)
and
\tilde{G}(r)
by

\tilde{f}(r)=\int d^{3}r^{\prime}\rho(\mathbf{r}-\mathbf{r^{\prime}})f(r^{\prime}),

(9)

with

\displaystyle\sigma_{12}^{2}=\sigma_{0}^{2}\Bigg[\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{4m_{1}m_{2}}{(m_{1}+m_{2})^{2}}\right)^{4}\Bigg]+s^{2}\left(\frac{2m_{1}m_{2}}{m_{1}+m_{2}}\right)^{2},

(10)

Secondly, owning to relativistic effects, a general potential should rely on the mass-of-center of interacting quarks. Momentum-dependent factors which will be unity in the nonrelativistic limit are applied as

\tilde{G}(r)\to\left(1+\frac{p^{2}}{E_{1}E_{2}}\right)^{1/2}\tilde{G}(r)\left(1+\frac{p^{2}}{E_{1}E_{2}}\right)^{1/2},

(11)

and

\frac{\tilde{V}_{i}(r)}{m_{1}m_{2}}\to\left(\frac{m_{1}m_{2}}{E_{1}E_{2}}\right)^{1/2+\epsilon_{i}}\frac{\tilde{V}_{i}(r)}{m_{1}m_{2}}\left(\frac{m_{1}m_{2}}{E_{1}E_{2}}\right)^{1/2+\epsilon_{i}},

(12)

where
\tilde{V}_{i}(r)
delegates the contact, tensor, vector spin-orbit and scalar spin-orbit terms, and
\epsilon_{i}
is the relevant modification parameters.

The screen effect is considered to be very important for the higher excitation states by the authors of Ref.
Song et al. 2015a
. It could be introduced by the transformation

br+c\rightarrow\frac{b(1-e^{-\mu r})}{\mu}+c
,
where
\mu
is
screened parameter whose particular value is given by Ref.
Pang et al. 2019
. Modified confinement potential also requires similar relativistic correction, which has been mentioned in the GI model.
Then, we further write

\displaystyle\tilde{V}^{\mathrm{scr}}(r)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int d^{3}\bm{r}^{\prime}\rho(\bm{r-r^{\prime}})\frac{b(1-e^{-\mu r^{\prime}})}{\mu}

(13)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{b}{\mu r}\Bigg[r+e^{\frac{\mu^{2}}{4\sigma^{2}}+\mu r}\frac{\mu+2r\sigma^{2}}{2\sigma^{2}}\Bigg(\frac{1}{\sqrt{\pi}}\int_{0}^{\frac{\mu+2r\sigma^{2}}{2\sigma}}e^{-x^{2}}dx-\frac{1}{2}\Bigg)

\displaystyle-e^{\frac{\mu^{2}}{4\sigma^{2}}-\mu r}\frac{\mu-2r\sigma^{2}}{2\sigma^{2}}\Bigg(\frac{1}{\sqrt{\pi}}\int_{0}^{\frac{\mu-2r\sigma^{2}}{2\sigma}}e^{-x^{2}}dx-\frac{1}{2}\Bigg)\Bigg].

The mass spectrum and the wave function of
K_{2}^{*}
mesons can be obtained by solving eigen value and eigen vector of the
\tilde{H}
in Eq. (
1
) with the Simple Harmonic Oscillator(SHO) base expanding method. In configuration and momentum space, SHO wave functions have explicit form respectively

\displaystyle\Psi_{nLM_{L}}(\mathbf{r})=R_{nL}(r,\beta)Y_{LM_{L}}(\Omega_{r}),

\displaystyle\Psi_{nLM_{L}}(\mathbf{p})=R_{nL}(p,\beta)Y_{LM_{L}}(\Omega_{p}),

(14)

with

\displaystyle R_{nL}(r,\beta)=\beta^{3/2}\sqrt{\frac{2n!}{\Gamma(n+L+3/2)}}(\beta r)^{L}e^{\frac{-r^{2}\beta^{2}}{2}}

\displaystyle\times L_{n}^{L+1/2}(\beta^{2}r^{2}),

(15)

\displaystyle R_{nL}(p,\beta)=\frac{(-1)^{n}(-i)^{L}}{\beta^{3/2}}e^{-\frac{p^{2}}{2\beta^{2}}}\sqrt{\frac{2n!}{\Gamma(n+L+3/2)}}{(\frac{p}{\beta})}^{L}

\displaystyle\times L_{n}^{L+1/2}(\frac{p^{2}}{\beta^{2}}),

(16)

where
Y_{LM_{L}}(\mathrm{\Omega})
is spherical harmonic function, and
L_{n-1}^{L+1/2}(x)
is the associated Laguerre polynomial.

A decay process
A\to B+C
can be expressed as

\displaystyle\langle BC|\mathcal{T}|A\rangle=\delta^{3}(\mathbf{P}_{B}+\mathbf{P}_{C})\mathcal{M}^{{M}_{J_{A}}M_{J_{B}}M_{J_{C}}},

(17)

where
\mathbf{P}_{B(C)}
is a three-momentum of a meson
B(C)
in the rest frame of a meson
A
. A superscript
M_{J_{i}}\,(i=A,B,C)
denotes an orbital
magnetic momentum. The transition operator
\mathcal{T}
is introduced to describe a quark-antiquark pair creation from vacuum, which has the quantum number

J^{PC}=0^{++}
, i.e.,
\mathcal{T}
can be written as

\displaystyle\mathcal{T}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-3\gamma\sum_{m}\langle 1m;1~-m|00\rangle\int d\mathbf{p}_{3}d\mathbf{p}_{4}\delta^{3}(\mathbf{p}_{3}+\mathbf{p}_{4})

(18)

\displaystyle\times\mathcal{Y}_{1m}\left(\frac{\textbf{p}_{3}-\mathbf{p}_{4}}{2}\right)\chi_{1,-m}^{34}\phi_{0}^{34}\left(\omega_{0}^{34}\right)_{ij}b_{3i}^{{\dagger}}(\mathbf{p}_{3})d_{4j}^{{\dagger}}(\mathbf{p}_{4}).

It is completely constructed in the form of a visual representation to reflect the creation of a quark-antiquark pair from vacuum, where the quark and antiquark are denoted by indices
3
and
4
, respectively.
The parameter
\gamma
depicts the strength of the creation of
q\bar{q}
from vacuum.
\mathcal{Y}_{\ell m}(\mathbf{p})={|\mathbf{p}|^{\ell}}Y_{\ell m}(\mathbf{p})
are the solid harmonics.
\chi
,
\phi
, and
\omega
denote the spin, flavor, and color wave functions respectively, which can be treated separately.
Subindices
i
and
j
denote the color of a
q\bar{q}
pair.

The decay amplitude can be expressed as another form by the Jacob-Wick formula
Jacob and Wick 1959

\displaystyle\mathcal{M}^{JL}(\mathbf{P})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{\sqrt{4\pi(2L+1)}}{2J_{A}+1}\sum_{M_{J_{B}}M_{J_{C}}}\langle L0;JM_{J_{A}}|J_{A}M_{J_{A}}\rangle

(19)

\displaystyle\times\langle J_{B}M_{J_{B}};J_{C}M_{J_{C}}|{J_{A}}M_{J_{A}}\rangle\mathcal{M}^{M_{J_{A}}M_{J_{B}}M_{J_{C}}},

then the general decay width will be

\displaystyle\Gamma

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{\pi}{4}\frac{|\mathbf{P}|}{m_{A}^{2}}\sum_{J,L}|\mathcal{M}^{JL}(\mathbf{P})|^{2},

(20)

where
m_{A}
is the mass of an initial state
A
.
In our calculation, the spatial wave functions of the mesons are given in Ref.
Pang et al. 2019
. The value of
\gamma
is 11.6.

IV
Conclusion

The observed
K_{2}^{*}(1870)
and
K_{2}^{*}(2070)
are firstly explained as
2^{3}P_{2}
and
1^{3}F_{2}
state respectively. By analyzing the mass spectra of the P-wave and the F-wave
K_{2}^{*}
meson family and calculating the two body strong decay of this two states, we find that our predicted results of
K_{2}^{*}(1870)
are consistent well with the existed experimental findings. Our results about
K_{2}^{*}(2070)
have a large overlap with existed experimental findings. Our theoretical results show that,
K_{2}^{*}(1870)
can be regard as a
2^{3}P_{2}
state through the comparison with the experimental data.
K_{2}^{*}(2070)
is likely to be a
1^{3}F_{2}
state.
1^{3}F_{2}
state may has a relatively large width of
855\pm 225
MeV, and the ratio of
\frac{\Gamma_{\text{K$\rho$}}}{\Gamma_{K^{*}\pi}}
is
1.05-1.13
. Just because of our explanations to
K_{2}^{*}(1870)
and
K_{2}^{*}(2070)
, the spectroscopy of P-wave and the F-wave
K_{2}^{*}
mesons become abundant. Additionally, we predict the decay behaviors of the
K_{2}^{*}(2P)
and
K_{2}^{*}(1F)
state and the decay width of channels like
K\rho
,
K^{*}\pi
and
Kf_{2}(1270)
are calculated. These findings are expected to be revealed in future experiment. Besides the
2^{3}P_{2}
and
1^{3}F_{2}
strange mesons, the decay behaviors of the other higher excited
K_{2}^{*}
mesons have been also predicted in the present work. The masses and widths of these predicted states provide
some basic information that may help for searching for these
strange mesons in future experiments.

Note added: We hope that the resonance parameter (total width) of
K_{2}^{*}(2070)
could be fitted again by experimental group considering the quantum numbers
n^{2S+1}L_{J}=1^{3}F_{2}
of
K_{2}^{*}
, which will provide the powerful criterion to test the information for confirming
K_{2}^{*}(2070)
state further.
