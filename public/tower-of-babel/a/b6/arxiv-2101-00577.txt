Cauchy problem for operators with triple effectively hyperbolic characteristics – Ivrii’s conjecture –
Tatsuo Nishitani

Abstract
Ivrii’s conjecture asserts that the Cauchy problem is C ∞ C^{\infty} well-posed for any lower order term if every singular point of the characteristic variety is effectively hyperbolic. Effectively hyperbolic singular point is at most triple characteristic. If every characteristic is at most double this conjecture has been proved in 1980’. In this paper we prove the conjecture for the remaining cases, that is for operators with triple effectively hyperbolic characteristics.



Keywords: Ivrii’s conjecture, Bézout matrix, triple characteristic, effectively hyperbolic, Cauchy problem, Weyl calculus, Tricomi operator.

Mathematics Subject Classification 2010: Primary 35L30, Secondary 35G10

Lemma 3.1

∎

Lemma 3.2
.

Since

\displaystyle 8\alpha^{3/2}\geq\big|{\hat{b}}_{0}(X)+3{\hat{b}}_{1}(X)\alpha\big|-C\alpha^{2}\geq 3|{\hat{b}}_{1}(X)|\alpha-C\alpha^{2}-\alpha^{3/2}

∎

Lemma 3.3

In a neighborhood of

∎

With
