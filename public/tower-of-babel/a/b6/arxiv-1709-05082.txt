IV
Summary

With anticipation of the potential prospects of
{\Upsilon}(nS)

physics at high-luminosity heavy-flavor factories,
search for
{\Upsilon}(nS)
weak decay seems to be experimentally
feasible. A theoretical study of
{\Upsilon}(nS)
weak decay is
seasonable and necessary.
In this paper, we investigated the bottom- and charm-changing

{\Upsilon}(nS)

{\to}

B_{c}^{\ast}D_{s,d}
decays with
phenomenological pQCD approach.
It is expected that branching ratio for
{\Upsilon}(nS)

{\to}

B_{c}^{\ast}D_{s}
decay could be up to
{\cal O}(10^{-10})
,
which might be measurable at the future LHC and SuperKEKB
experiments.
