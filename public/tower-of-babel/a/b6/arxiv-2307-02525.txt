Emergent Global Symmetry from IR N-ality
Anindya Dey

Abstract
We present a new family of IR dualities in three space-time dimensions with eight supercharges. In contrast to 3d mirror symmetry, these dualities map Coulomb branches to Coulomb branches and Higgs branches to Higgs branches in the deep IR. For a large class of quiver gauge theories with an emergent Coulomb branch global symmetry, one can construct a sequence of such dualities by step-wise implementing a set of quiver mutations. The duality sequence gives a set of quiver gauge theories which flow to the same IR fixed point – a phenomenon we refer to as IR N-ality. We show that this set of N-al quivers always contains a theory for which the rank of the IR Coulomb branch symmetry is manifest in the UV. For a special subclass of theories, the emergent symmetry algebra itself can be read off from the quiver description of the aforementioned theory.



Introduction.
Some of the most interesting non-perturbative phenomena in QFTs
in three and four space-time dimensions
Seiberg 1995
;
Aharony 1997
;
Giveon and Kutasov 2009
;
Intriligator and Seiberg 1996

arise in the IR limit, where the theories may become
strongly-interacting at special points of the vacuum moduli space. Broadly speaking, the properties of a QFT that arise in the neighborhood
of such special points but are not manifest in the UV description, are collectively referred to as
emergent

properties. A particularly important example involves the global symmetry of the QFT at these special points.

3d
{\cal N}=4
theories provide a rich laboratory for studying non-perturbative phenomena in
QFTs. The theories are super-renormalizable in the UV and generically flow to strongly-coupled SCFTs in the IR.
The vacuum moduli space has two distinguished branches : the Higgs branch (HB), which is protected from quantum corrections
by a non-renormalization theorem, and the Coulomb branch (CB), which receives 1-loop as well as non-perturbative corrections.
We will focus on theories which are
good
in the Gaiotto-Witten sense
Gaiotto and Witten 2009
– the two branches in this case
intersect at a single point where the IR SCFT lives. 3d
{\cal N}=4
theories also present interesting examples of IR duality – a pair of distinct theories in
the UV flowing to the same IR SCFT. A particularly important example of such a duality is 3D Mirror Symmetry
Intriligator and Seiberg 1996
;
de Boer et al. 1997

which acts by mapping the CB of one theory to the HB of the other and vice-versa, in the deep IR.

The HB 0-form symmetry, including its global form, is classically manifest. For the CB, however, the IR symmetry algebra
\mathfrak{g}^{\rm IR}_{\rm C}
may be larger
compared to the UV-manifest symmetry
\mathfrak{g}^{\rm UV}_{\rm C}
. If the rank of the IR symmetry is greater than the UV-manifest rank, we will refer to the IR symmetry as

emergent
, otherwise we will simply refer to it as
enhanced
.

{\cal N}=4

More generally, however, one may have
\text{rk}(\mathfrak{g}^{\rm IR}_{\rm C})>\text{rk}(\mathfrak{g}^{\rm UV}_{\rm C})

are simply not visible in the UV Lagrangian. These are often referred to as theories with “hidden FI parameters”
Hanany and Zaffaroni 1998
;
Feng and Hanany 2000
;
Gaiotto and Witten 2009
.

Outline of the paper.
For certain ranges of

A unitary-special unitary quiver gauge theory

The theory
{\cal T}
has an emergent CB symmetry characterized by the presence of hidden FI parameters which implies that
the rank of the IR symmetry is greater than UV-manifest rank. The sequence of dualities provides a neat way to study the
emergent CB symmetry of
{\cal T}
. We have shown that duality sequence produces at least one theory
{\cal T}_{\rm maximal}
for which
the correct rank of the IR symmetry becomes manifest from the quiver description. For a subclass of theories, one may even be
able to read off the correct symmetry algebra. Using a simple example, we demonstrated that this is indeed the case when
{\cal T}
is a
linear quiver.

Our formalism gives the first systematic way to study the emergent CB symmetry (and therefore hidden FI parameters) in 3d
{\cal N}=4

theories which do not have a realization in String Theory (like the Hanany-Witten
Hanany and Witten 1997
description or a description in terms of
magnetic quivers
Bourget et al. 2022
). It also leads to an extremely efficient algorithm for generating the 3d mirrors of unitary-special unitary quivers
with generic shape, which will be presented in a paper to appear soon. Analogous to 3d mirror symmetry, various aspects of these IR dualities
– for example, the duality maps for BPS local operators and line defects – should furnish interesting physics and deserve detailed investigation.
Finally, one expects to find novel non-supersymmetric dualities as one subjects these N-al theories to soft supersymmetry-breaking, in a
fashion similar to
Kachru et al. 2016
. Some of these topics will be addressed in future work.
