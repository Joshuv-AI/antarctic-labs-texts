Weighted Super Poincaré Inequalities for Infinite-Dimensional Extension of the Dirichlet Distribution corresponding author email address: 201321130173@mail.bnu.edu.cn
Weiwei Zhang

Abstract
For the infinite-dimensional extension of the Dirichlet distribution, the super Poincaré inequality does not hold based on the result in [ 14 ] , so we establish the weighted super Poincaré inequalities for this measure with respect to two different Dirichlet forms respectively.



AMS subject Classification: 60J60, 60H10.

Keywords: infinite-dimensional Dirichlet distribution, Poincaré inequality, super Poincaré inequality, weighted super Poincaré inequality.

1
Introduction

Let
n\geq 1
be a natural number, and let
\alpha=(\alpha_{1},\cdots,\alpha_{n+1})\in(0,\infty)^{n+1}.
The Dirichlet distribution
\mu^{(n)}_{\alpha}
with parameter
\alpha
is a probability measure on the set

\Delta^{(n)}:=\bigg\{x=(x_{i})_{1\leq i\leq n}\in[0,1]^{n}:\ |x|_{1}:=\sum_{i=1}^{n}x_{i}\leq 1\bigg\}

with density function

(1.1)

\rho(x):=\frac{\Gamma(|\alpha|_{1})}{\prod_{1\leq i\leq n+1}\Gamma(\alpha_{i})}(1-|x|_{1})^{\alpha_{n+1}-1}\prod_{1\leq i\leq n}x_{i}^{\alpha_{i}-1},\ \ x=(x_{i})_{1\leq i\leq n}\in\Delta^{(n)},

where
|\alpha|_{1}:=\sum_{i=1}^{n+1}\alpha_{i}
, denoted by
D(\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2},\cdots,\alpha_{n},\alpha_{n+1}).

This distribution arises naturally in Bayesian inference as conjugate prior for categorical distribution, and it describes the distribution of allelic frequencies in population genetics, see for instance
[
7
]
.
We introduce two generators of diffusion processes whose stationary distributions both are the Dirichlet distribution.

(1)

The first type operator and Dirichlet form:

(1.2)

\begin{split}&L_{\alpha,1}^{(n)}f(x)=\sum_{i=1}^{n}x_{i}(1-|x|_{1})(\partial_{i}^{2}f)(x)+\{\alpha_{i}(1-|x|_{1})-\alpha_{\infty}x_{i}\}\partial_{i}f(x),\quad f\in C^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{n}).\\
&\mathscr{E}_{\alpha,1}^{(n)}(f,g):=\mu_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}^{(n)}\bigg((1-|x|_{1})\sum_{i=1}^{n}x_{i}\partial_{i}f\partial_{n}g\bigg),\quad f,g\in C^{1}(\mathbb{R}^{n}).\end{split}

(2)

The second type operator and Dirichlet form:

(1.3)

\begin{split}&L_{\alpha,2}^{(n)}f(x)=\sum_{i,j=1}^{n}x_{i}(\delta_{ij}-x_{j})(\partial_{ij}f)(x)+\sum_{i=1}^{n}(\alpha_{i}-|\alpha|_{1}x_{i})\partial_{i}f(x),\quad f\in C^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{n}).\\
&\mathscr{E}_{\alpha,2}^{(n)}(f,g):=\mu_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}^{(n)}\bigg(\sum_{i,j=1}^{n}x_{i}(\delta_{ij}-x_{j})\partial_{i}f\partial_{j}g\bigg),\quad f,g\in C^{1}(\mathbb{R}^{n}).\end{split}

[
3
]
extend the measure
\mu^{(n)}_{\alpha}
and the operator (
1.2
) to the infinite-dimensional case.
Consider the infinite-dimensional simplex

\Delta^{(\infty)}:=\bigg\{x\in[0,1]^{\mathbb{N}}:\ |x|_{1}:=\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}x_{i}\leq 1\bigg\},

which is equipped with the
L^{1}
-metric
|x-y|_{1}.

Let

\alpha^{(n)}:=(\alpha_{1},\cdots,\alpha_{n-1},\sum_{i\geq n}\alpha_{i},\alpha_{\infty}).

We define a probability measure on
\Delta^{(n)},

\mu^{(n)}_{\alpha^{(n)}}:=D(\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2},\cdots,\alpha_{n-1},\Sigma_{i\geq n}\alpha_{i},\alpha_{\infty});

a probability measure on
\Delta^{(\infty)},

\mu^{(m)}_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}(dx):=\mu^{(n)}_{\alpha^{(n)}}(dx_{1},\cdots,dx_{n})\prod_{i>n}\delta_{0}(dx_{i}).

According to
[
3
]
, we know
\{\mu^{(m)}_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}\}_{m\geq 1}
weakly converges and we denote the limit as
\mu_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}^{(\infty)}.

\mathcal{F}C^{p}
is the
C^{p}
-cylindrical functions for
p\geq 1,

\mathcal{F}C^{p}:=\{\Delta^{(\infty)}\ni x:=(x_{i})_{i\geq 1}\mapsto f(x_{1},\cdots,x_{m}):m\geq 1,f\in C^{p}(\Delta^{(m)})\}.

For the Dirichlet distribution and the first type Dirichlet form, S. Feng, L. Miclo and F.-Y. Wang have established the Poincaré inequalities for the finite-dimensional case and the infinite-dimensional case in
[
3
]
. Then the super Poincaré inequality for the finite-dimensional case is established in
[
14
]
by F.-Y. Wang and the author of this paper. What’s more,
[
3
]
also proved that the form

\displaystyle\mathscr{E}_{\alpha,1}^{(\infty)}(f,f):=\mu_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}^{(\infty)}\bigg((1-|x|_{1})\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}x_{n}\partial_{n}f\partial_{n}g\bigg)\quad f,g\in\mathcal{F}C^{1}

is closable in
L^{2}(\mu_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}^{(\infty)})
and the closure is a symmetric Dirichlet form. The generator

(L_{\alpha,1}^{(\infty)},\mathscr{D}(L_{\alpha,1}^{(\infty)}))
of the Dirichlet form
\mathscr{E}_{\alpha,1}^{(\infty)}
satisfies
\mathcal{F}C^{2}\subset\mathscr{D}(L_{\alpha,1}^{(\infty)}),

\displaystyle L_{\alpha,1}^{(\infty)}f(x)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\bigg(x_{n}(1-|x|_{1})\partial_{n}^{2}f(x)+\{\alpha_{n}(1-|x|_{1})-\alpha_{\infty}x_{n}\}\partial_{n}f(x)\bigg),\quad f\in\mathcal{F}C^{2}.

In
[
10
]
, Stannat have established the Poincaré inequality for the Dirichlet distribution with respect to the second type Dirichlet form and also established the Poincaré inequality for the Fleming-Viot process. In
[
14
]
, F.-Y. Wang and the author of this paper have established the super Poincaré inequality for the second type Dirichlet form about finite-dimensional case. Following the idea of
[
3
]
, we can also prove that the form

\displaystyle\mathscr{E}_{\alpha,2}^{(\infty)}:=\mu_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}^{(\infty)}\bigg(\sum_{i,j=1}^{\infty}x_{i}(\delta_{ij}-x_{j})\partial_{i}f\partial_{j}g\bigg)\quad f,g\in\mathcal{F}C^{1}

is closable in
L^{2}(\mu_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}^{(\infty)})
and the closure is a symmetric Dirichlet form. The generator

(L_{\alpha,2}^{(\infty)},\mathscr{D}(L_{\alpha,2}^{(\infty)}))
of the Dirichlet form
\mathscr{E}_{\alpha,2}^{(\infty)}
satisfies
\mathcal{F}C^{2}\subset\mathscr{D}(L_{\alpha,2}^{(\infty)}),

\displaystyle L_{\alpha,2}^{(\infty)}f(x)=\sum_{i,j=1}^{\infty}x_{i}(\delta_{ij}-x_{j})(\partial_{ij}f)(x)+\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}(\alpha_{i}-|\alpha|_{1}x_{i})\partial_{i}f(x),\quad f\in\mathcal{F}C^{2}.

In
[
10
]
, Stannat proved the invalidity of the log-Sobolev inequality when the state space contains infinite states. In fact, by the same method, we can prove the F-Sobolev inequality doesn’t hold, then neither does the super Poincaré inequality hold.

In this paper, we firstly introduce some properties of the Dirichlet distribution in section one, then we establish the weighted super Poincaré inequality for the Dirichlet form
\mathscr{E}_{\alpha,1}^{(\infty)}
and the measure
\mu_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}^{(\infty)}
in section two.
At last, we obtain the weighted super Poincaré inequality for the Dirichlet form
\mathscr{E}_{\alpha,2}^{(\infty)}
and the measure
\mu_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}^{(\infty)}
in section three.

2
Property of the Dirichlet distribution

The Dirichlet distribution possesses many nice properties. One of the properties is the partition property of
\mu_{\alpha}^{(n)}
for
\alpha\in(0,\infty)^{n}.

Let
(X_{1},\cdots,X_{n})
have law
\mu_{\alpha}^{(n)},
let
\{A_{1},\cdots,A_{k}\}
be a partition of the set
\{1,\cdots,n\},
and set

Y_{j}=\sum_{r\in A_{j}}X_{r},\quad\beta_{j}=\sum_{r\in A_{j}}\alpha_{r},\quad j=1,\cdots,k.

Then
(Y_{1},\cdots,Y_{k})
has law
\mu_{\beta}^{(k)}
with parameter
\beta:=(\beta_{1},\cdots,\beta_{k})\in(0,\infty)^{k}.

We give another property of the Dirichlet distribution
\mu_{\alpha}^{(n)}
which we will use later.
For any
m>n,
we define the map

T_{m}:\Delta^{(n)}\times\Delta^{(m-n)}\rightarrow\Delta^{(m)},

T_{m}:(x_{1},x_{2},\cdots,x_{m})\longmapsto(x_{1}(1-\Sigma_{n<i\leq m}x_{i}),\cdots,x_{n}(1-\Sigma_{n<i\leq m}x_{i}),x_{n+1},\cdots,x_{m}).

T:\Delta^{(n)}\times\Delta^{(\infty)}\rightarrow\Delta^{(\infty)},

T:(x_{1},x_{2},\cdots)\longmapsto(x_{1}(1-\Sigma_{i>n}x_{i}),\cdots,x_{n}(1-\Sigma_{i>n}x_{i}),x_{n+1},\cdots).

Fixed
n\geq 1,

\forall f\in\mathcal{F}C^{1}(\Delta^{(\infty)}),
we denote the number of the variables of
f
as
m.

Let

\mu_{1}:=D(\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2},\cdots,\alpha_{n},\alpha_{\infty})

is a probability measure on
\Delta^{(n)}.

\mu_{2}^{(m)}:=D(\alpha_{n+1},\alpha_{n+2},\cdots,\alpha_{m-1},\Sigma_{i\geq m}\alpha_{i},\Sigma_{i=1}^{n}\alpha_{i}+\alpha_{\infty})

is a probability measure on
\Delta^{(m-n)}.

\mu_{2,\infty}^{(m)}(dx):=\mu_{2}^{(m)}(dx_{1},\cdots,dx_{m})\prod_{i>m}\delta_{0}(dx_{i})

is a probability measure on
\Delta^{(\infty)}.

By the same proof of the weak convergence of
\{\mu^{(m)}_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}\}_{m\geq 1}
in
[
3
]
, we know
\{\mu_{2}^{(m)}\}_{m\geq 1}
weak converges and we define the limit as
\mu_{2}^{(\infty)}.

Proposition 2.1
.

\mu_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}^{(\infty)}(f)=\mu_{1}(\mu_{2}^{(\infty)}(f\circ T)):=\int_{\Delta^{(n)}}\int_{\Delta^{(\infty)}}f\circ T(x,y)\mu_{2}^{(\infty)}(dy)\mu_{1}(dx).

Proof.

As
f
has
m
variables,
so

\displaystyle\mu_{1}(\mu_{2,\infty}^{(m)}(f\circ T_{m}))=\mu_{1}(\mu_{2}^{(m)}(f\circ T_{m}))

\displaystyle=\frac{\Gamma(\sum_{i=1}^{n}\alpha_{i}+\alpha_{\infty})}{\prod_{i=1}^{n}\Gamma(\alpha_{i})\Gamma(\alpha_{\infty})}\frac{\Gamma(\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}\alpha_{i})}{\prod_{i=n+1}^{m-1}\Gamma(\alpha_{i})\Gamma(\sum_{i\geq m}\alpha_{i})\Gamma(\sum_{i=1}^{n}\alpha_{i}+\alpha_{\infty})}

\displaystyle\qquad\cdot\int_{\Delta^{(m)}}f(T_{m}(x))x_{1}^{\alpha_{1}-1}\cdots x_{n}^{\alpha_{n}-1}\cdot\bigg(1-\sum_{i=1}^{n}x_{i}\bigg)^{\alpha_{\infty}-1}x_{n+1}^{\alpha_{n+1}-1}\cdots x_{m-1}^{\alpha_{m}-1}x_{m}^{\Sigma_{i\geq m}\alpha_{i}-1}

\displaystyle\qquad\cdot\bigg(1-\sum_{i=n+1}^{m}x_{i}\bigg)^{\sum_{i=1}^{n}\alpha_{i}+\alpha_{\infty}-1}dx_{1}\cdots dx_{m}

\displaystyle=\frac{\Gamma(\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}\alpha_{i})}{\prod_{i=1}^{m-1}\Gamma(\alpha_{i})\Gamma(\sum_{i\geq m}\alpha_{i})\Gamma(\alpha_{\infty})}\cdot\int_{\Delta^{(m)}}f(T_{m}(x))x_{1}^{\alpha_{1}-1}\cdots x_{n}^{\alpha_{n}-1}\bigg(1-\sum_{i=n+1}^{m}x_{i}\bigg)^{\sum_{i=1}^{n}\alpha_{i}-n}

\displaystyle\qquad\cdot x_{n+1}^{\alpha_{n+1}-1}\cdots x_{m-1}^{\alpha_{m}-1}\cdot x_{m}^{\sum_{i\geq m}\alpha_{i}-1}\cdot\bigg[\bigg(1-\sum_{i=n+1}^{m}x_{i}\bigg)\bigg(1-\sum_{i=1}^{n}x_{i}\bigg)\bigg]^{\alpha_{\infty}-1}

\displaystyle\qquad\cdot\bigg(1-\sum_{i=n+1}^{m}x_{i}\bigg)^{n}dx_{1}\cdots dx_{m}

\displaystyle=\frac{\Gamma(\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}\alpha_{i})}{\prod_{i=1}^{m-1}\Gamma(\alpha_{i})\Gamma(\sum_{i\geq m}\alpha_{i})\Gamma(\alpha_{\infty})}

\displaystyle\qquad\cdot\int_{\Delta^{(m)}}f(y)y_{1}^{\alpha_{1}-1}\cdots y_{m}^{\sum_{i\geq m}\alpha_{i}-1}\bigg(1-\sum_{i=1}^{m}y_{i}\bigg)^{\alpha_{\infty}-1}\bigg(1-\sum_{i=n+1}^{m}x_{i}\bigg)^{n}

\displaystyle\qquad\cdot\det(\nabla T^{-1}_{m}(y))dy_{1}\cdots dy_{m}

\displaystyle=\frac{\Gamma(\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}\alpha_{i})}{\prod_{i=1}^{m-1}\Gamma(\alpha_{i})\Gamma(\sum_{i\geq m}\alpha_{i})\Gamma(\alpha_{\infty})}\cdot\int_{\Delta^{(m)}}f(y)y_{1}^{\alpha_{1}-1}\cdots y_{m}^{\sum_{i\geq m}\alpha_{i}-1}

\displaystyle\qquad\cdot\bigg(1-\sum_{i=1}^{m}y_{i}\bigg)^{\alpha_{\infty}-1}dy_{1}\cdots dy_{m}

\displaystyle=\mu^{(m)}_{\alpha^{(m)}}(f)=\mu^{(m)}_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}(f).

We have used

\det(\nabla T^{-1}_{m}(y))=\bigg(1-\sum_{i=n+1}^{m}x_{i}\bigg)^{-n},

\displaystyle\bigg[\bigg(1-\sum_{i=n+1}^{m}x_{i}\bigg)\bigg(1-\sum_{i=1}^{n}x_{i}\bigg)\bigg]=\bigg(1-\sum_{i=n+1}^{m}x_{i}\bigg)-\bigg(1-\sum_{i=n+1}^{m}x_{i}\bigg)\sum_{i=1}^{n}x_{i}=1-\sum_{i=1}^{m}y_{i}.

So we get

(2.1)

\mu_{1}(\mu_{2,\infty}^{(m)}(f\circ T_{m}))=\mu^{(m)}_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}(f).

Let
m\rightarrow\infty
at the both side of (
2.1
),
we get

\mu_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}^{(\infty)}(f)=\mu_{1}(\mu_{2}^{(\infty)}(f\circ T)):=\int_{\Delta^{(n)}}\int_{\Delta^{(\infty)}}f\circ T(x,y)\mu_{2}^{(\infty)}(dy)\mu_{1}(dx).

∎

3
The weighted super Poincaré inequality for the first type Dirichlet form

According to Theorem 1.1 in
[
14
]
, from the Nash inequality, we can get the super Poincaré inequality for the Dirichlet distribution
\mu^{(n)}_{\alpha}
and Dirichlet form
\mathscr{E}_{\alpha,1}^{(n)}.
Combining the super Poincaré inequality with the Poincaré inequality, we can obtain the weighted super Poincaré inequality for infinite-dimensional Dirichlet distribution and Dirichlet form
\widetilde{\mathscr{E}}_{\alpha,1}^{(\infty)}.

Theorem 3.1
.

Let
\gamma_{i}\geq 1,~i\geq 1.
Denote

\widetilde{\mathscr{E}}_{\alpha,1}^{(\infty)}(f,f):=\mu_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}^{(\infty)}\bigg(\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}\gamma_{i}y_{i}(1-|y|_{1})(\partial_{i}f)^{2}\bigg),\quad f\in\mathscr{D}(\widetilde{\mathscr{E}}_{\alpha,1}^{(\infty)}).

Then the weighted super Poincaré inequality

\mu_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}^{(\infty)}(f^{2})\leq r\widetilde{\mathscr{E}}_{\alpha,1}^{(\infty)}(f,f)+\beta^{(1)}(r)\mu_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}^{(\infty)}(\mid f\mid)^{2},\quad f\in\mathcal{F}C^{1}(\Delta^{(\infty)})

holds,
there is a positive constat
c_{n}
such that

\beta^{(1)}(r)\leq c_{n}\bigg(\frac{r}{3}\bigg)^{-[\sum_{i=1}^{n}1\lor(2\alpha_{i})+(\alpha_{\infty}-1)^{+}]},

where
n
comes from the smallest value which satisfies

\frac{1}{(\sum_{i=1}^{n}\alpha_{i}+\alpha_{\infty})\inf_{i>n}\gamma_{i}}\leq r.

Proof.

\forall r>0,

n
is the smallest value which satisfies

\frac{1}{(\sum_{i=1}^{n}\alpha_{i}+\alpha_{\infty})\inf_{i>n}\gamma_{i}}\leq r.

\forall f\in\mathcal{F}C^{1}(\Delta^{(\infty)}),
and the number of the variables of
f
is denoted as
m.

If
m\leq n,
by the result of
[
14
]
, we have the super Poincaré inequality

\displaystyle\mu_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}^{(\infty)}(f^{2})

\displaystyle\leq r\mu_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}^{(\infty)}\bigg(\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}x_{i}(1-|x|_{1})(\partial_{i}f)^{2}\bigg)+\beta_{n}(r)\mu_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}^{(\infty)}(\mid f\mid)^{2}

\displaystyle\leq r\mu_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}^{(\infty)}\bigg(\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}x_{i}\gamma_{i}(1-|x|_{1})(\partial_{i}f)^{2}\bigg)+\beta_{n}(r)\mu_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}^{(\infty)}(\mid f\mid)^{2}

\displaystyle=r\widetilde{\mathscr{E}}_{\alpha,1}^{(\infty)}(f,f)+\beta^{(1)}(r)\mu_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}^{(\infty)}(\mid f\mid)^{2},

there is a positive constant
c_{n}
such that

\beta^{(1)}(r)\leq c_{n}\bigg(\frac{r}{3}\bigg)^{-[\sum_{i=1}^{n}1\lor(2\alpha_{i})+(\alpha_{\infty}-1)^{+}]}.

If
m>n,
according to
[
3
, Theorem 1.2]
, we have the Poincaré inequality for
\mu_{2}^{(\infty)},

\mu_{2}^{(\infty)}(f^{2})\leq\frac{1}{\sum_{i=1}^{n}\alpha_{i}+\alpha_{\infty}}\mu_{2}^{(\infty)}\bigg(\sum_{i=n+1}^{\infty}x_{i}(1-|x|_{1})(\partial_{i}f)^{2}\bigg)+\mu_{2}^{(\infty)}(\mid f\mid)^{2}.

According to
[
14
, Theorem 1.1]
, we have the super Poincaré inequality for
\mu_{1},

\mu_{1}(f^{2})\leq r\mu_{1}\bigg(\sum_{i=1}^{n}x_{i}(1-|x|_{1})(\partial_{i}f)^{2}\bigg)+\beta_{n}(r)\mu_{1}(\mid f\mid)^{2},

there is a positive constant
c_{n}
such that

\beta_{n}(r)\leq c_{n}r^{-[\sum_{i=1}^{n}1\lor(2\alpha_{i})+(\alpha_{\infty}-1)^{+}]}.

Combining them together we get

\displaystyle\mu_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}^{(\infty)}(f^{2})=\mu_{1}(\mu_{2}^{(\infty)}(f^{2}\circ T))

\displaystyle\leq\mu_{1}\bigg(\frac{1}{\sum_{i=1}^{n}\alpha_{i}+\alpha_{\infty}}\mu_{2}^{(\infty)}\bigg(\sum_{i=n+1}^{\infty}x_{i}(1-|x|_{1})(\partial_{i}(f\circ T))^{2}\bigg)\bigg)+\mu_{1}(\mu_{2}^{(\infty)}(\mid f\circ T\mid)^{2})

\displaystyle=\mu_{1}\bigg(\frac{1}{\sum_{i=1}^{n}\alpha_{i}+\alpha_{\infty}}\mu_{2}^{(\infty)}\bigg(\sum_{i=n+1}^{\infty}x_{i}(1-|x|_{1})\bigg((\partial_{i}f)\circ T-\sum_{j=1}^{n}x_{j}(\partial_{j}f)\circ T\bigg)^{2}\bigg)\bigg)

\displaystyle{\quad}+r\mu_{1}\bigg(\mu_{2}^{(\infty)}\bigg(\sum_{i=1}^{n}x_{i}(1-|x|_{1})(\partial_{i}(f\circ T))^{2}\bigg)\bigg)+\beta_{n}(r)\mu_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}^{(\infty)}(\mid f\mid)^{2}

\displaystyle\leq 2\mu_{1}\bigg(\frac{1}{\sum_{i=1}^{n}\alpha_{i}+\alpha_{\infty}}\mu_{2}^{(\infty)}\bigg(\sum_{i=n+1}^{\infty}x_{i}(1-|x|_{1})(\partial_{i}f)^{2}\circ T\bigg)\bigg)

\displaystyle{\quad}+2\mu_{1}\bigg(\frac{1}{\sum_{i=1}^{n}\alpha_{i}+\alpha_{\infty}}\mu_{2}^{(\infty)}\bigg(\sum_{i=n+1}^{\infty}x_{i}(1-|x|_{1})\bigg(\sum_{j=1}^{n}x_{j}^{2}(\partial_{j}f)^{2}\circ T\bigg)\bigg)\bigg)

\displaystyle{\quad}+r\mu_{1}\bigg(\mu_{2}^{(\infty)}\bigg(\sum_{i=1}^{n}x_{i}(1-|x|_{1})\bigg(\partial_{i}f\circ T\cdot\bigg(1-\sum_{i=n+1}^{\infty}x_{i}\bigg)\bigg)^{2}\bigg)\bigg)

\displaystyle{\quad}+\beta_{n}(r)\mu_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}^{(\infty)}(\mid f\mid)^{2}

\displaystyle\leq 2\mu_{1}\bigg(\frac{1}{\sum_{i=1}^{n}\alpha_{i}+\alpha_{\infty}}\mu_{2}^{(\infty)}\bigg(\bigg[\sum_{i=n+1}^{\infty}x_{i}(1-|x|_{1})(\partial_{i}f)^{2}\bigg]\circ T\bigg)\bigg)

\displaystyle{\quad}+2\mu_{1}\bigg(\frac{1}{\sum_{i=1}^{n}\alpha_{i}+\alpha_{\infty}}\mu_{2}^{(\infty)}\bigg(\bigg[\sum_{i=1}^{n}x_{i}(1-|x|_{1})(\partial_{i}f)^{2}\bigg]\circ T\bigg)\bigg)

\displaystyle{\quad}+r\mu_{1}\bigg(\mu_{2}^{(\infty)}\bigg(\bigg[\sum_{i=1}^{n}x_{i}(1-|x|_{1})(\partial_{i}f)^{2}\bigg]\circ T\bigg)\bigg)

\displaystyle{\quad}+\beta_{n}(r)\mu_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}^{(\infty)}(\mid f\mid)^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{2}{\sum_{i=1}^{n}\alpha_{i}+\alpha_{\infty}}\mu_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}^{(\infty)}\bigg(\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}x_{i}(1-|x|_{1})(\partial_{i}f)^{2}\bigg)+r\mu_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}^{(\infty)}\bigg(\sum_{i=1}^{n}x_{i}(1-|x|_{1})(\partial_{i}f)^{2}\bigg)

\displaystyle{\quad}+\beta_{n}(r)\mu_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}^{(\infty)}(\mid f\mid)^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{2}{(\sum_{i=1}^{n}\alpha_{i}+\alpha_{\infty})\inf_{i\geq 1}\gamma_{i}}\mu_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}^{(\infty)}\bigg(\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}x_{i}\gamma_{i}(1-|x|_{1})(\partial_{i}f)^{2}\bigg)

\displaystyle{\quad}+r\mu_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}^{(\infty)}\bigg(\sum_{i=1}^{n}x_{i}(1-|x|_{1})(\partial_{i}f)^{2}\bigg)+\beta_{n}(r)\mu_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}^{(\infty)}(\mid f\mid)^{2}

\displaystyle\leq 3r\mu_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}^{(\infty)}\bigg(\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}x_{i}\gamma_{i}(1-|x|_{1})(\partial_{i}f)^{2}\bigg)+\beta_{n}(r)\mu_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}^{(\infty)}(\mid f\mid)^{2}.

We have used

\gamma_{i}\geq 1,i\geq 1

and let

\frac{1}{\inf_{i>n}\bigg(\sum_{i=1}^{n}\alpha_{i}+\alpha_{\infty}\bigg)\gamma_{i}}\leq r

to get these inequalities.
So we get

\mu_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}^{(\infty)}(f^{2})\leq r\widetilde{\mathscr{E}}_{\alpha,1}^{(\infty)}(f,f)+\beta_{n}\bigg(\frac{r}{3}\bigg)\mu_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}^{(\infty)}(\mid f\mid)^{2},

where
n
is the smallest value which satisfies

\frac{1}{\inf_{i>n}(\sum_{i=1}^{n}\alpha_{i}+\alpha_{\infty})\gamma_{i}}\leq r.

∎

The weighted super Poincaré inequality for the second type Dirichlet form

\beta_{n}(r)\leq c_{n}r^{-[\sum_{i=1}^{n}1\lor(2\alpha_{i})+(\alpha_{\infty}-1)^{+}]}.

Combining them together we get

\displaystyle\mu_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}^{(\infty)}(f^{2})=\mu_{1}(\mu_{2}^{(\infty)}(f^{2}\circ T))

\displaystyle\leq\mu_{1}\bigg(\frac{1}{\sum_{i=n+1}^{\infty}\alpha_{i}+\alpha_{\infty}}\mu_{2}^{(\infty)}\bigg(\sum_{i,j=n+1}^{\infty}x_{i}(\delta_{ij}-x_{j})\partial_{i}(f\circ T)\partial_{j}(f\circ T)\bigg)\bigg)

\displaystyle{\quad}+\mu_{1}(\mu_{2}^{(\infty)}(\mid f\circ T\mid)^{2})

\displaystyle=\mu_{1}\bigg(\frac{1}{\sum_{i=n+1}^{\infty}\alpha_{i}+\alpha_{\infty}}\mu_{2}^{(\infty)}\bigg(\sum_{i,j=n+1}^{\infty}x_{i}(\delta_{ij}-x_{j})\bigg([\partial_{i}f\circ T-\sum_{k=1}^{n}x_{k}\partial_{k}f\circ T]

\displaystyle{\quad}\cdot[\partial_{j}f\circ T-\sum_{k=1}^{n}x_{k}\partial_{k}f\circ T]\bigg)\bigg)\bigg)+r\mu_{1}\bigg(\mu_{2}^{(\infty)}\bigg(\sum_{i,j=1}^{n}x_{i}(\delta_{ij}-x_{j})\partial_{i}(f\circ T)\partial_{j}(f\circ T)\bigg)\bigg)

\displaystyle{\quad}+\beta_{n}(r)\mu_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}^{(\infty)}(\mid f\mid)^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\mu_{1}\bigg(\frac{1}{\sum_{i=1}^{n}\alpha_{i}+\alpha_{\infty}}\mu_{2}^{(\infty)}\bigg(\bigg[\sum_{i,j=n+1}^{\infty}y_{i}(\delta_{ij}-y_{j})\partial_{i}f\partial_{j}f\bigg]\circ T\bigg)\bigg)

\displaystyle{\quad}-2\mu_{1}\bigg(\frac{1}{\sum_{i=1}^{n}\alpha_{i}+\alpha_{\infty}}\mu_{2}^{(\infty)}\bigg(\sum_{i=n+1}^{\infty}\sum_{k=1}^{n}y_{i}y_{k}\partial_{i}f\partial_{k}f\bigg)\circ T\bigg)\bigg)

\displaystyle{\quad}+\mu_{1}\bigg(\frac{1}{\sum_{i=1}^{n}\alpha_{i}+\alpha_{\infty}}\mu_{2}^{(\infty)}\bigg(\sum_{j=n+1}^{\infty}y_{j}\bigg(1-\sum_{j=n+1}^{\infty}y_{j}\bigg)\bigg(\sum_{k=1}^{n}x_{k}\partial_{k}f\circ T\bigg)^{2}\bigg)\bigg)

\displaystyle{\quad}+r\mu_{1}\bigg(\mu_{2}^{(\infty)}\bigg(\sum_{i,j=1}^{n}x_{i}\bigg(1-\sum_{j=n+1}^{\infty}x_{j}\bigg)^{2}(\delta_{ij}-x_{j})(\partial_{i}f\partial_{j}f)\circ T\bigg)\bigg)

\displaystyle{\quad}+\beta_{n}(r)\mu_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}^{(\infty)}(\mid f\mid)^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{\bigg(\sum_{i=1}^{n}\alpha_{i}+\alpha_{\infty}\bigg)}\mu_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}^{(\infty)}\bigg(\sum_{i,j=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{1-\sum_{i>n}x_{i}}x_{i}(\delta_{ij}-x_{j})\partial_{i}f\partial_{j}f\bigg)

\displaystyle{\quad}+r\mu_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}^{(\infty)}\bigg(\sum_{i,j=1}^{n}x_{i}(\delta_{ij}-x_{j})\partial_{i}f\partial_{j}f\bigg)+\beta_{n}(r)\mu_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}^{(\infty)}(\mid f\mid)^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{\bigg(\sum_{i=1}^{n}\alpha_{i}+\alpha_{\infty}\bigg)\inf_{i>n}\gamma_{i}}\mu_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}^{(\infty)}\bigg(\sum_{i,j=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{1-\sum_{i>n}x_{i}}\gamma_{i}\gamma_{j}x_{i}(\delta_{ij}-x_{j})\partial_{i}f\partial_{j}f\bigg)

\displaystyle{\quad}+r\mu_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}^{(\infty)}\bigg(\sum_{i,j=1}^{n}x_{i}(\delta_{ij}-x_{j})\partial_{i}f\partial_{j}f\bigg)+\beta_{n}(r)\mu_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}^{(\infty)}(\mid f\mid)^{2}

\displaystyle\leq 2r\mu_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}^{(\infty)}\bigg(\sum_{i,j=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{1-\sum_{i>n}x_{i}}\gamma_{i}\gamma_{j}y_{i}(\delta_{ij}-y_{j})\partial_{i}f\partial_{j}f\bigg)+\beta_{n}(r)\mu_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}^{(\infty)}(\mid f\mid)^{2}.

We have used
\gamma_{i}\geq 1,~i\geq 1
and

\frac{1}{\bigg(\sum_{i=1}^{n}\alpha_{i}+\alpha_{\infty}\bigg)\inf_{i>n}\gamma_{i}}\leq r

to get these inequalities.
So we get

\mu_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}^{(\infty)}(f^{2})\leq r\widetilde{\mathscr{E}}_{\alpha,2}^{(\infty)}(f,f)+\beta_{n}(r)\mu_{\alpha,\alpha_{\infty}}^{(\infty)}(\mid f\mid)^{2},

there is a positive constant
c_{n}
such that

\beta_{n}(r)\leq c_{n}\bigg(\frac{r}{2}\bigg)^{-[\sum_{i=1}^{n}1\lor(2\alpha_{i})+(\alpha_{\infty}-1)^{+}]},

where
n
is the smallest value which satisfies

\frac{1}{\inf_{i>n}(\sum_{i=1}^{n}\alpha_{i}+\alpha_{\infty})\gamma_{i}}\leq r.

∎
