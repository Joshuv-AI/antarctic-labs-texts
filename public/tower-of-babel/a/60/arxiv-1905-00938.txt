One-dimensional Actions of Higman’s Group
Michele Triestino

Abstract
We build a faithful action of Higman’s group on the line by homeomorphisms, answering a question of Yves de Cornulier. As a by-product we obtain many quasimorphisms from Higman’s group into the reals. We also show that every action by C 1 \mathchar 28995^{\mathchar 28721} -diffeomorphisms of Higman’s group on the line or the circle is trivial.
