1.
Introduction

A topological Azumaya algebra of degree
n
is a bundle of
\mathbb{C}
-algebras
\mathcal{A}
over a topological space, where each fiber is isomorphic to
\M(n,\mathbb{C})
. This concept is a topological version of an Azumaya algebra of degree
n
over a scheme
X
, which may be defined as a locally free
\mathcal{O}_{X}
-algebra
\mathcal{A}
such that the stalk
\mathcal{A}_{x}
at every geometric point
x:\Spec F\to X
is
\M(n,F)
. This generalizes the classical notion of central simple algebras (CSAs)
[
10
,
9
,
12
]
. Involutions on CSAs are ring-antiautomorphisms of period
2
. Knus–Parimala–Srinivas introduced the concept an involution on an Azumaya algebra over a scheme
[
15
]
. An
involution (of the first kind) on
\mathcal{A}
is a fiber bundle automorphism
\tau:\mathcal{A}\rightarrow\mathcal{A}
such that
\tau\circ\tau=\id_{\mathcal{A}}
, and when restricted to fibers it is an anti-automorphism of complex algebras, i.e.
\tau|_{\mathcal{A}^{-1}(x)}
is linear and
\tau(ab)=\tau(b)\tau(a)
for all
a,b\in\mathcal{A}^{-1}(x)
and for all
x\in X
. In this case,
(\mathcal{A},\tau)
is called a
topological Azumaya algebra with involution
. The involution
\tau
is said to be
symplectic
(
orthogonal
) if the restriction
\tau|_{\mathcal{A}^{-1}(x)}:\mathcal{A}^{-1}(x)\to\mathcal{A}^{-1}(x)
is a symplectic (an orthogonal) involution of complex algebras for all
x\in X
.

Central simple algebras possess numerous structural properties, and extensive research is devoted to exploring the extent to which these properties can be extended to Azumaya algebras. See
[
2
,
3
,
4
,
5
,
8
,
7
,
6
]
. One relevant example for the work discussed in this paper is as follows: Let
m
and
n
be coprime integers. It is well-known that every CSA of degree
mn
factors as the tensor product of a degree-
m
CSA and a degree-
n
CSA. See
[
21
, Theorem 5.7]
and
[
11
, Proposition 4.5.16]
. By contrast to CSAs without involution, if
m
and
n
are coprime, then it can happen that a CSA with an involution of the first kind
(A,\tau)
does not decompose as a tensor product of CSAs with involution of the first kind
(A_{1},\tau_{1})\otimes_{\mathbb{C}}(A_{2},\tau_{2})
with
\deg A_{1}=m
and
\deg A_{2}=n
. See
[
1
,
16
,
18
,
6
]
. Antieau and Williams showed that prime tensor product decomposition fails for Azumaya algebras without involution
[
3
, Corollary 1.3]
. On the other hand, Arcila-Maya demonstrated that such a decomposition exists for topological Azumaya algebras without involution, and for those with an orthogonal involution if the base
X
is a CW complex of dimension at most
2\min\{m,n\}+1
and
\min\{m,n\}
, respectively. See
[
7
, Theorem 1.3, Remark 3.7]
and
[
6
, Theorem 1.2, Theorem 1.5]
.

We prove in Proposition
3.6
that topological Azumaya algebras with symplectic involutions may not decompose as the tensor product of topological Azumaya algebras with involutions of the first kind. Additionally, we provide conditions on a positive integer
n
and a topological space
X
such that a topological Azumaya algebra of degree
2n
over
X
with a symplectic involution decomposes as the tensor product of topological Azumaya algebras with involution. We prove the following theorem:

Theorem 1.1
.

Let
X
be a CW complex such that
\dim(X)\leq 7
. Let
m
and
n
be positive integers such that
m>1
,
n>7
, and
n
is odd.

If
\mathcal{A}
is a topological Azumaya algebra of degree
2mn
over
X
with a symplectic involution, then there exist topological Azumaya algebras
\mathcal{A}_{2m}
and
\mathcal{A}_{n}
of degrees
2m
and
n
, respectively, such that
\mathcal{A}_{2m}
has a symplectic involution,
\mathcal{A}_{n}
has an orthogonal involution and is Brauer-trivial, and
\mathcal{A}\cong\mathcal{A}_{2m}\otimes\mathcal{A}_{n}
.

For the particular case of topological Azumaya algebras of the form
\End(\mathcal{V})
for
\mathcal{V}
a symplectic vector bundle of rank
2n
, we obtain the decomposition result for the dimension of the underlying space in a larger range:

Theorem 1.2
.

Let
m
and
n
be positive integers such that
n
is odd. Let
X
be a CW complex such that
\dim(X)\leq n
. If
\mathcal{V}
is a symplectic bundle of rank
2mn
over
X
, then there exist a symplectic bundle
\mathcal{V}_{2m}
of rank
2m
, and an orthogonal bundle
\mathcal{V}_{n}
of rank
n
such that
\mathcal{V}\cong\mathcal{V}_{2m}\otimes\mathcal{V}_{n}
.

We prove in Theorem
1.1
that a map
X\rightarrow\B\PSp(mn,\mathbb{C})
can be lifted to
\B\PSp(m,\mathbb{C})\times\B\SO(n,\mathbb{C})
when the dimension of
X
is less than
7
. The proof of Theorem
1.1
relies in the description of the homomorphisms induced on homotopy groups by the
r
-fold direct sum of symplectic matrices
\boxplus^{r}:\Sp(m,\mathbb{C})\to\Sp(rm,\mathbb{C})
, and by the tensor product operations
\otimes:\Sp(m,\mathbb{C})\times\Or(n,\mathbb{C})\to\Sp(mn,\mathbb{C})
and
\boxtimes:\Sp(m,\mathbb{C})\times\Sp(n,\mathbb{C})\to\Or(4mn,\mathbb{C})
, defined in
[
6
, Section 2.2]
, in the range
\{0,1,\dots,4m+2\}
. We call this set “
the stable range for
\Sp(n,\mathbb{C})
”. More concretely we prove the following result

Proposition 1.3
.

Let
i<4m+2
and
i<n-1
, and let
\otimes:\Sp(m,\mathbb{C})\times\Or(n,\mathbb{C})\to\Sp(mn,\mathbb{C})
be the tensor product. The induced homomorphism
\otimes_{*}:\pi_{i}(\Sp(m))\times\pi_{i}(\Or(n))\to\pi_{i}(\Sp(mn))

is given by

\otimes_{*}(x,y)=nx+2my\quad\text{for}\quad x\in\pi_{i}(\Sp(m))\quad\text{and}\quad y\in\pi_{i}(\Or(n)).

This paper is a sequel to
[
6
]
, where we determined conditions for the decomposition of topological Azumaya algebras with orthogonal involutions over finite CW complexes.
