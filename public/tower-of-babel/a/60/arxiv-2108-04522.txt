Several weighted Hardy, Hardy-Sobolev-Maz’ya and related inequalities Thanks: AMS Subject Classifications: 26D10, 35A23
Ismail Kömbe, Sümeyye Bakim and Reyhan Tellioğlu Balekoğlu

Abstract
. In this paper we establish several Hardy and Hardy-Sobolev type inequalities with homogeneous weights on the first orthant ℝ ∗ n := { ( x 1 , … , x n ) : x 1 > 0 , … , x n > 0 } \displaystyle\mathbb{R}_{*}^{n}:=\{(x_{1},\ldots,x_{n}):x_{1}>0,\ldots,x_{n}>0\} . We then use some of them to produce Hardy type inequalities with remainder terms. Furthermore, we obtain some interpolation inequalities and Maz’ya type inequalities with remainder terms with the help of Maz’ya inequality and Sobolev inequality of Cabré and Ros-Orton on the upper half space ℝ + n := { x = ( x 1 , … , x n ) | x n > 0 } \mathbb{R}_{+}^{n}:=\{x=(x_{1},\ldots,x_{n})|\,x_{n}>0\} .



1.
Introduction

The classical Hardy inequality states that

(1.1)

\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\geq\left|\frac{n-p}{p}\right|^{p}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\frac{{|\phi|}^{p}}{|x|^{p}}dx,

and holds for all
\phi\in C_{0}^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
if
1<p<n
, and for all
\phi\in C_{0}^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}^{n}\setminus\{0\})
if
p>n
. The constant
|\frac{n-p}{p}|^{p}
in the above inequality is sharp and is never achieved by nontrivial functions.
In the critical case
p=n
, the inequality
(\ref{1.1})
fails for any constant. However, there is another version of the Hardy inequality slightly different from
(\ref{1.1})
,

(1.2)

\int_{B_{1}}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\geq\left(\frac{p-1}{p}\right)^{p}\int_{B_{1}}\frac{|\phi|^{p}}{|x|^{p}\left(\ln(\frac{1}{|x|})\right)^{p}}dx,

which is essentially proved by Leray
[
15
]
in
\mathbb{R}^{2}
and later by Adimurthi and Sandeep
[
1
]
in
\mathbb{R}^{n}
,
n\geq 3
. Here
B_{1}
is the unit ball in
\mathbb{R}^{n}
centered at the origin and
\phi\in C_{0}^{\infty}(B_{1}\setminus\{0\})
, and the constant
(\frac{p-1}{p})^{p}
is sharp.

Another inequality involving non-radial weight is the Maz’ya inequality
[
18
]
,

(1.3)

\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}|x_{n}|^{p-1}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\geq\frac{1}{(2p)^{p}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\frac{|\phi|^{p}}{(x_{n-1}^{2}+x_{n}^{2})^{\frac{1}{2}}}dx,

where
\phi\in C_{0}^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
and
1<p<\infty
.

These inequalities play important roles in many areas such as analysis, geometry and mathematical physics. By virtue of aforementioned reasons, there is a vast amount of literature on these inequalities together with their generalizations, extensions, and refinements. We refer the interested reader to the monographs
[
12
]
,
[
5
]
,
[
9
]
,
[
2
]
,
[
20
]
, the papers
[

]
,

]
,
[

]
,
[

]
,
[

]
,
[
11
]
, and the references cited therein.

Before we state our results, let us first briefly recall the related results in the literature. For instance, Davies
[
5
]
proved the following extension of Hardy’s inequality for convex
domains
\Omega\subset\mathbb{R}^{n}
,
n\geq 2
,

(1.4)

\int_{\Omega}|\nabla\phi|^{2}dx\geq\frac{1}{4}\int_{\Omega}\frac{|\phi|^{2}}{\delta^{2}}dx,

where
\phi\in C_{0}^{\infty}\left(\Omega\right)
. Here

\delta\left(x\right)=\mathrm{dist}\,\left(x,\partial\Omega\right)=\min_{y\in\partial\Omega}\left|x-y\right|

is the distance function to the boundary
\partial\Omega
. Moreover the constant
\frac{1}{4}
is sharp (see
[
17
]
). When
\Omega
is the upper half-space
\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n}
, the inequality (
1.4
) takes the form

(1.5)

\int_{\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n}}|\nabla\phi|^{2}dx\geq\frac{1}{4}\int_{\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n}}\frac{|\phi|^{2}}{x_{n}^{2}}dx.

An improved version of the inequality (
1.5
), which has been obtained by Maz’ya much earlier in his book
[
18
]
,

(1.6)

\int_{\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n}}|\nabla\phi|^{2}dx\geq\frac{1}{4}\int_{\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n}}\frac{|\phi|^{2}}{x_{n}^{2}}dx+C_{n}\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n}}x_{n}^{\gamma}|\phi|^{p}dx\right)^{\frac{2}{p}},

where
\phi\in C_{0}^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n})
,
n\geq 3
,
2<p\leq\frac{2n}{n-2}
,
\gamma=\frac{(n-2)p}{2}-n
and
C_{n}
is a positive constant. If
p=\frac{2n}{n-2}
then we have the following celebrated Hardy-Sobolev-Maz’ya inequality

(1.7)

\int_{\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n}}|\nabla\phi|^{2}dx\geq\frac{1}{4}\int_{\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n}}\frac{|\phi|^{2}}{x_{n}^{2}}dx+C_{n}\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n}}|\phi|^{\frac{2n}{n-2}}dx\right)^{\frac{n-2}{n}}.

On the other hand, substituting
\phi(x)=x_{n}^{-1/2}\varphi(x)
into (
1.3
) gives another improved version of (
1.5
)

(1.8)

\int_{\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n}}|\nabla\varphi|^{2}dx\geq\frac{1}{4}\int_{\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n}}\frac{|\varphi|^{2}}{x_{n}^{2}}dx+\frac{1}{16}\int_{\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n}}\frac{|\varphi|^{2}}{(x_{n-1}^{2}+x_{n}^{2})^{\frac{1}{2}}x_{n}}dx,

where
\varphi\in C_{0}^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n})
.

Regarding the
L^{2}
improved versions of (
1.1
) for a bounded domain
\Omega
, we have the following sharp Hardy-Poincaré and Hardy-Sobolev inequalities, which have been proved by Brezis and Vázquez
[
3
]

(1.9)

\int_{\Omega}|\nabla\phi|^{2}dx\geq\left(\frac{n-2}{2}\right)^{2}\int_{\Omega}\frac{|\phi|^{2}}{|x|^{2}}dx+\mu\left(\frac{\omega_{n}}{|\Omega|}\right)^{2/n}\int_{\Omega}|\phi|^{2}dx,

and

(1.10)

\int_{\Omega}|\nabla\phi|^{2}dx\geq\left(\frac{n-2}{2}\right)^{2}\int_{\Omega}\frac{|\phi|^{2}}{|x|^{2}}dx+C\left(\int_{\Omega}|\phi|^{q}dx\right)^{\frac{2}{q}},

where
\phi\in C_{0}^{1}(\Omega)
,
C=C(\Omega,n)>0
,
\omega_{n}

and
|\Omega|
denote the
n
-dimensional Lebesgue measure of the
unit ball
B\subset\mathbb{R}^{n}
and the domain
\Omega
,
respectively. Here
\mu
is the first eigenvalue of the Laplace
operator in the two dimensional unit disk, and it is optimal when

\Omega
is a ball centered at the origin,

2\leq q<\frac{2n}{n-2}
and
q
cannot be replaced by

\frac{2n}{n-2}
.

In a series of papers
[
21
]
-
[
22
]
Tidblom studied various Hardy type inequalities with remainder terms and proved the following inequality in
[
21
]
,

(1.11)

\int_{\mathbb{R}_{*}^{n}}|\nabla\phi|^{2}dx\geq\frac{1}{4}\int_{\mathbb{R}_{*}^{n}}\frac{|\phi|^{2}}{|x|^{2}}+\left(\frac{2n-1}{4n^{2}}\right)\int_{\mathbb{R}_{*}^{n}}\left(\frac{1}{x_{1}^{2}}+\frac{1}{x_{2}^{2}}+...+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{2}}\right)|\phi|^{2}dx,

where
\phi\in C_{0}^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}_{*}^{n})
. Moreover, Tidblom asked whether the inequality

(1.12)

\int_{\Omega}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\geq C\int_{\Omega}\left(\frac{1}{x_{1}^{p}}+\frac{1}{x_{2}^{p}}\right)|\phi|^{p}dx,

holds for some positive constant
C
. In
[
22
]
, Tidblom obtained the following inequality, which is the
L^{p}
analogue of Maz’ya’s inequality (
1.8
),

(1.13)

\int_{\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n}}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\geq\left(\frac{p-1}{p}\right)^{p}\int_{\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n}}\frac{|\phi|^{p}}{x_{n}^{p}}dx+\alpha(p,\tau)\int_{\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n}}\frac{|\phi|^{p}}{x_{n}^{p-\tau}(x_{n-1}^{2}+x_{n}^{2})^{\frac{\tau}{2}}}dx,

where
p>1
,
\tau>0
,
\alpha(p,\tau)
is a positive constant and
\phi\in C_{0}^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n})
.

On the other hand, Filippas, Tertikas and Tidblom
[
7
]
proved the following inequality which has a critical Sobolev exponent,

(1.14)

\int_{\mathbb{R}_{k_{+}}^{n}}|\nabla\phi|^{2}dx\geq\frac{1}{4}\int_{\mathbb{R}_{k_{+}}^{n}}\left(\frac{1}{x_{1}^{2}}+\dots+\frac{1}{x_{k}^{2}}\right)|\phi|^{2}dx+C\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}_{k_{+}}^{n}}|\phi|^{\frac{2n}{n-2}}dx\right)^{\frac{n-2}{n}},

where
\mathbb{R}_{k_{+}}^{n}=\{x=(x_{1},\ldots,x_{n})|\,x_{1}>0,\ldots,x_{k}>0\}\subset\mathbb{R}^{n}
,
\phi\in C_{0}^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}_{k_{+}}^{n})
and
C
is a positive constant.

Recently, Cabré and Ros-Orton
[
4
]
obtained a sharp form of the Sobolev inequality with monomial weight
x^{A}
, where
A=(A_{1},\dots,A_{n})
and

x^{A}:=|x_{1}|^{A_{1}}\cdots|x_{n}|^{A_{n}},\quad A_{1}\geq 0,\dots,A_{n}\geq 0.

More precisely, the following inequality holds for all
\phi\in C_{0}^{1}(\mathbb{R}_{*}^{n})

(1.15)

\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}_{*}^{n}}x^{A}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}\geq S_{p}(D)\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}_{*}^{n}}x^{A}|\phi|^{p^{*}}dx\right)^{\frac{1}{p^{*}}},

where
\mathbb{R}_{*}^{n}=\{x=(x_{1},\dots,x_{n})\in\mathbb{R}^{n}:x_{i}>0\quad\text{when}\quad A_{i}\geq 0\}
,
D=n+A_{1}+\dots+A_{n}
,
1<p<D
,
p^{*}=\frac{Dp}{D-p}
, the constant
S_{p}(D)>0
is sharp (its explicit value can be found in
[
4
]
) and the equality
holds if and only if
\phi_{a,b}(x)=\left(a+b|x|^{\frac{p}{p-1}}\right)^{1-\frac{D}{p}}
, where
a
and
b
are any positive constants.

Motivated by the above results, we have three main purposes in this paper. The first one is to study weighted Hardy type inequalities on the first orthant

\mathbb{R}_{*}^{n}:=\{x=(x_{1},\ldots,x_{n})|\,x_{1}>0,\ldots,x_{n}>0\}.

The second purpose is to obtain various explicit remainder terms for Hardy type inequalities on some subdomains of
\mathbb{R}_{*}^{n}
. The last purpose is to study Maz’ya-Sobolev inequality and Maz’ya type inequalities with remainder terms in the upper half-space

\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n}:=\{x=(x_{1},\ldots,x_{n})|\,x_{n}>0\}.

2.
Weighted Hardy and Poincaré Type Inequalities

Throughout this section and the next section, we assume that
\Omega
is a bounded domain with smooth boundary
\partial\Omega
in
\mathbb{R}_{*}^{n}:=\{x=(x_{1},\ldots,x_{n})|\,x_{1}>0,\ldots,x_{n}>0\}
,
n\geq 2
. The generic point is
x=\left(x_{1},\ldots,x_{n}\right)\in\mathbb{R}^{n}
and
r=\left|x\right|=\sqrt{x_{1}^{2}+\cdots+x_{n}^{2}}.
We denote
B_{R}=\left\{x\in\mathbb{R}^{n}:\left|x\right|<R\right\}
is the open ball at the origin with radius
R
in
\mathbb{R}^{n}
.

We begin this section by proving a Hardy type inequality with the homogeneous weight
w(x_{1},\dots,x_{n})=x_{1}^{\alpha}\cdots x_{n}^{\alpha}
where
\alpha\in\mathbb{R}
. In the special case where
\alpha=0
, our result gives an affirmative answer to Tidblom’s question (
1.12
).

Theorem 2.1
.

Let
\phi\in C_{0}^{\infty}(\Omega)
,
p>2
,
\alpha\in\mathbb{R}
and
p-\alpha-1>0
. Then we have,

(2.1)

\int_{\Omega}(x_{1}\dots x_{n})^{\alpha}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\geq\left(\frac{p-\alpha-1}{p}\right)^{p}\int_{\Omega}(x_{1}\dots x_{n})^{\alpha}\left(\frac{1}{x_{1}^{p}}+\dots+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{p}}\right)|\phi|^{p}dx.

Proof.

Consider the vector field

\textbf{F}=(x_{1}^{1-p},x_{2}^{1-p},\dots,x_{n}^{1-p}).

Direct computation shows that

(2.2)

\text{div}\,\textbf{F}=(1-p)\left(\frac{1}{x_{1}^{p}}+\frac{1}{x_{2}^{p}}+\dots+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{p}}\right).

Multiplying both sides of (
2.2
) by
(x_{1}\dots x_{n})^{\alpha}|\phi|^{p}
, and integrating by parts and rearranging terms in a standard way we get

(2.3)

\frac{p-1-\alpha}{p}\int_{\Omega}\left(\frac{1}{x_{1}^{p}}+\dots+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{p}}\right)(x_{1}\dots x_{n})^{\alpha}|\phi|^{p}dx=\int_{\Omega}(x_{1}\dots x_{n})^{\alpha}|\phi|^{p-1}\nabla\phi\cdot\textbf{F}dx.

Denote by
L
and
R
the left-hand side and the right-hand side of
(
2.3
)
, respectively. An application of Hölder’s and Young’s inequality yields,

(2.4)

L=R\leq\epsilon\int_{\Omega}(x_{1}\dots x_{n})^{\alpha}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx+(\frac{p-1}{p})(p\epsilon)^{\frac{1}{1-p}}\int_{\Omega}(x_{1}\dots x_{n})^{\alpha}|\textbf{F}|^{\frac{p}{p-1}}|\phi|^{p}dx

where
\epsilon>0
and will be chosen later. On the other hand, one can show that

(2.5)

|\textbf{F}|^{\frac{p}{p-1}}\leq\frac{1}{x_{1}^{p}}+\frac{1}{x_{2}^{p}}+\dots+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{p}}.

Substituting (
2.5
) into (
2.4
) and rearranging the terms gives

H_{p,\alpha}(\epsilon)\int_{\Omega}(x_{1}\dots x_{n})^{\alpha}\left(\frac{1}{x_{1}^{p}}+\frac{1}{x_{2}^{p}}+\dots+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{p}}\right)|\phi|^{p}dx\leq\int_{\Omega}(x_{1}\dots x_{n})^{\alpha}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx,

where
H_{p,\alpha}(\epsilon)=\frac{p-1}{p}\left(\frac{1-(p\epsilon)^{\frac{1}{1-p}}}{\epsilon}\right)-\frac{\alpha}{p\epsilon}.

Note that the function
H_{p,\alpha}(\epsilon)
attains the maximum for
\epsilon=\frac{1}{p}\left(\frac{p-\alpha-1}{p}\right)^{1-p}
, and this maximum is equal to
(\frac{p-\alpha-1}{p})^{p}
. Now we have the desired
inequality (
2.1
),

\int_{\Omega}(x_{1}\dots x_{n})^{\alpha}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\geq\left(\frac{p-\alpha-1}{p}\right)^{p}\int_{\Omega}(x_{1}\dots x_{n})^{\alpha}\left(\frac{1}{x_{1}^{p}}+\dots+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{p}}\right)|\phi|^{p}dx.

∎

We now present the following weighted Hardy-Sobolev inequality that can be obtained by Hölder interpolation between the Hardy type inequality (
2.1
) and the Sobolev inequality (
1.15
) with
A_{1}=\dots=A_{N}=\alpha
.

Corollary 2.1
.

Let
2<p<n(1+\alpha)
,
0\leq\alpha<p-1
,
0\leq s\leq p
and
p^{*}(s)=p\left(\frac{D-s}{D-p}\right)
. Then the following inequality holds,

(2.6)

\left(\int_{\Omega}(x_{1}\dots x_{n})^{\alpha}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}\geq C\left(\int_{\Omega}(x_{1}\dots x_{n})^{\alpha}\left(\frac{1}{x_{1}^{p}}+\dots+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{p}}\right)^{\frac{s}{p}}|\phi|^{p^{*}(s)}dx\right)^{\frac{1}{p^{*}(s)}},

for all
\phi\in C_{0}^{\infty}(\Omega)
. Here
C=\left(\frac{p-\alpha-1}{p}\right)^{\frac{s(D-p)}{p(D-s)}}\left(S_{p}(D)\right)^{\frac{D(p-s)}{p(D-s)}}
and
D=n(1+\alpha)
.

When
s=0
, then (
2.6
) is the Sobolev inequality (
1.15
) with
A_{1}=\dots=A_{N}=\alpha
, whereas for
s=p
, we get the Hardy type inequality (
2.1
).

Using the inequality (
2.1
) and the arithmetic-geometric mean inequality, we obtain the following weighted Hardy type inequality.

Theorem 2.2
.

Let
\phi\in C_{0}^{\infty}(\Omega)
,
p>2
,
\alpha\in\mathbb{R}
and
p-\alpha-1>0
. Then we have,

(2.7)

\int_{\Omega}\left(x_{1}\dots x_{n}\right)^{\alpha}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\geq n\left(\frac{p-\alpha-1}{p}\right)^{p}\int_{\Omega}\left(x_{1}\dots x_{n}\right)^{\alpha-\frac{p}{n}}|\phi|^{p}dx.

Proof.

The inequality between the arithmetic and geometric means of
n
positive numbers
x_{i}^{-p}
,
i=1,2,\dots,n
may be written as

(2.8)

\frac{n}{(x_{1}x_{2}\dots x_{n})^{p/n}}\leq\frac{1}{x_{1}^{p}}+\frac{1}{x_{2}^{p}}+\dots+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{p}}.

Multiplying both sides of (
2.8
) by
\left(x_{1}\dots x_{n}\right)^{\alpha}|\phi|^{p}
and integrating, we obtain

(2.9)

n\int_{\Omega} \frac{|\phi|^{p}}{(x_{1}x_{2}\dots x_{n})^{\frac{p}{n}-\alpha}}dx\leq\int_{\Omega}\left(x_{1}\dots x_{n}\right)^{\alpha}\left(\frac{1}{x_{1}^{p}}+\frac{1}{x_{2}^{p}}+\dots+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{p}}\right)|\phi|^{p}dx.

By applying the inequality (
2.1
) in Theorem
2.1
to the right hand side of (
2.9
), it follows that

(2.10)

\int_{\Omega}\left(x_{1}\dots x_{n}\right)^{\alpha}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\geq n\left(\frac{p-\alpha-1}{p}\right)^{p}\int_{\Omega}\left(x_{1}\dots x_{n}\right)^{\alpha-\frac{p}{n}}|\phi|^{p}dx.

The proof of Theorem
2.2
is now complete.
∎

We continue to find other weighted inequalities, and we will achieve this by selecting some special vector fields. Our results are as follows.

Theorem 2.3
.

Let
p>1
. Then for all
\phi\in C_{0}^{\infty}(\Omega)
, we have

(2.11)

\int_{\Omega}|x|^{p}(x_{1}x_{2}\dots x_{n})|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\geq\left(\frac{2n}{p}\right)^{p}\int_{\Omega}(x_{1}x_{2}\dots x_{n})|\phi|^{p}dx.

Moreover, for every
\phi\in C_{0}^{\infty}(B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{*}^{n})

(2.12)

\int_{B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{*}^{n}}(x_{1}x_{2}\dots x_{n})|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\geq\left(\frac{2n}{pR}\right)^{p}\int_{B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{*}^{n}}(x_{1}x_{2}\dots x_{n})|\phi|^{p}dx.

Proof.

The method of proof is essentially the same as that used in proving Theorem
2.1
, but using the vector field

\textbf{F}=(x_{1}^{2}x_{2}\dots x_{n},x_{1}x_{2}^{2}\dots x_{n},\dots,x_{1}x_{2}\dots x_{n}^{2}).

We omit the details.

∎

Next, we have the following theorem.

Theorem 2.4
.

Let
p>1
. Then for all
\phi\in C_{0}^{\infty}(\Omega)
, we have

(2.13)

\int_{\Omega}\frac{|x|^{p}}{x_{1}^{p}x_{2}^{p}\dots x_{n}^{p}}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\geq\left(\frac{n(p-1)}{p}\right)^{p}\int_{\Omega}\frac{|\phi|^{p}}{x_{1}^{p}x_{2}^{p}\dots x_{n}^{p}}dx.

Proof.

The proof follows using the same argument as in the proof of Theorem
2.1
but using the vector field

\textbf{F}=(-x_{1}^{1-p}x_{2}^{-p}\dots x_{n}^{-p},-x_{1}^{-p}x_{2}^{1-p}\dots x_{n}^{-p},\dots,-x_{1}^{-p}x_{2}^{-p}\dots x_{n}^{1-p}).

We omit the details.
∎

As a final result of this section, we are going to use Theorem
2.1
to prove the following interpolation inequality.

Theorem 2.5
.

Let
p>2
. Then for all
\phi\in C_{0}^{\infty}(\Omega)
, we have

(2.14)

\begin{aligned} \left(\int_{\Omega}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\right)\left(\int_{\Omega}\left(\frac{1}{x_{1}^{p}}+\dots+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{p}}\right)|\phi|^{p}dx\right)^{p-1}\geq K\left(\int_{\Omega}\frac{|\phi|^{p}}{x_{1}^{p}+\dots+x_{n}^{p}}dx\right)^{p}\end{aligned},

where
K=(\frac{n^{2}(p-1)}{p})^{p}
.

Proof.

Let
u=(x_{1}^{p/2},x_{2}^{p/2},\dots,x_{n}^{p/2})
and
v=(\frac{1}{x_{1}^{p/2}},\frac{1}{x_{2}^{p/2}},\dots,\frac{1}{x_{n}^{p/2}})
be vectors in
\Omega
. Application of the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality yields

(2.15)

\frac{1}{x_{1}^{p}}+\frac{1}{x_{2}^{p}}+\dots+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{p}}\geq\frac{n^{2}}{x_{1}^{p}+x_{2}^{p}+\dots+x_{n}^{p}}.

Multiplying both sides of (
2.15
) by
|\phi|^{p}
and integrating over
\Omega
, we obtain

(2.16)

\displaystyle n^{2}\int_{\Omega}\frac{|\phi|^{p}}{(x_{1}^{p}+x_{2}^{p}+\dots+x_{n}^{p})}dx\leq\int_{\Omega}\left(\frac{1}{x_{1}^{p}}+\dots+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{p}}\right)|\phi|^{p}dx.

Let us denote the right hand side of (
2.16
) by
R
.
It is clear that

(2.17)

R=\left(\int_{\Omega}\left(\frac{1}{x_{1}^{p}}+\dots+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{p}}\right)|\phi|^{p}dx\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}\left(\int_{\Omega}\left(\frac{1}{x_{1}^{p}}+\dots+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{p}}\right)|\phi|^{p}dx\right)^{1-\frac{1}{p}}.

Applying the inequality (
2.1
) with
\alpha=0
to the first integral term in (
2.17
) we find

(2.18)

R\leq\frac{p}{p-1}\left(\int_{\Omega}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\right)^{1/p}\left(\int_{\Omega}\left(\frac{1}{x_{1}^{p}}+\dots+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{p}}\right)|\phi|^{p}dx\right)^{\frac{p-1}{p}}.

Combining (
2.16
) and (
2.18
) gives the desired inequality,

\displaystyle\left(\int_{\Omega}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\right)\left(\int_{\Omega}\left(\frac{1}{x_{1}^{p}}+\dots+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{p}}\right)|\phi|^{p}dx\right)^{p-1}\geq K\left(\int_{\Omega}\frac{|\phi|^{p}}{x_{1}^{p}+\dots+x_{n}^{p}}dx\right)^{p},

where
K=\left(\frac{n^{2}(p-1)}{p}\right)^{p}
.
∎

3.
Hardy type inequalities with remainders

The purpose of this section is to obtain various remainder terms for the following Hardy type inequality,

(3.1)

\int_{\Omega}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\geq H_{p}\int_{\Omega}(\frac{1}{x_{1}^{p}}+\frac{1}{x_{2}^{p}}+\dots+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{p}})|\phi|^{p}dx,

where
H_{p}
is a positive constant and will be determined later. Our methods are constructive and give explicit remainder terms.

Our first result is the following Hardy-Sobolev-Maz’ya type inequality.

Theorem 3.1
.

Let
p>2
,
D=2n
and
\phi\in C_{0}^{\infty}(\Omega)
. Then we have,

(3.2)

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\geq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{p^{p}}\int_{\Omega}\left(\frac{1}{x_{1}^{p}}+\frac{1}{x_{2}^{p}}+\dots+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{p}}\right)|\phi|^{p}dx

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{2^{p-1}-1}\left(S_{p}(D)\right)^{p}\left(\int_{\Omega}(x_{1}x_{2}\dots x_{n})^{\frac{-p}{D-p}}|\phi|^{\frac{Dp}{D-p}}dx\right)^{\frac{D-p}{D}}.

Moreover, for every
\phi\in C_{0}^{\infty}(B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{*}^{n})
,

(3.3)

\displaystyle\int_{B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{*}^{n}}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\geq\frac{1}{p^{p}}

\displaystyle\int_{B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{*}^{n}}\left(\frac{1}{x_{1}^{p}}+\frac{1}{x_{2}^{p}}+\dots+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{p}}\right)|\phi|^{p}dx

\displaystyle+C\left(\int_{B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{*}^{n}}|\phi|^{\frac{Dp}{D-p}}dx\right)^{\frac{D-p}{D}}

where
C=\frac{\left(S_{p}(D)\right)^{p}}{2^{p-1}-1}(\frac{\sqrt{n}}{R})^{\frac{p}{2}}
.

Proof.

Let
\phi\in C_{0}^{\infty}(\Omega)
and define
\phi=(x_{1}x_{2}\dots x_{n})^{\alpha}\psi
where
\alpha>0
. Then we have

|\nabla\phi|^{p}=|\alpha(x_{1}x_{2}\dots x_{n})^{\alpha}(\frac{1}{x_{1}},\frac{1}{x_{2}},\dots,\frac{1}{x_{n}})\psi+(x_{1}x_{2}\dots x_{n})^{\alpha}\nabla\psi|^{p}.

We now use the following convexity inequality which is valid for any
a,b\in\mathbb{R}^{n}
and
p\geq 2
,

(3.4)

|a+b|^{p}-|a|^{p}\geq p|a|^{p-2}a\cdot b+c(p)|b|^{p}

where
c(p)=\frac{1}{2^{p-1}-1}
(see
[
16
]
). This yields

(3.5)

\displaystyle|\nabla\phi|^{p}\geq

\displaystyle\alpha^{p}(x_{1}x_{2}\dots x_{n})^{p\alpha}(\frac{1}{x_{1}^{2}}+\frac{1}{x_{2}^{2}}+\dots+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{2}})^{\frac{p}{2}}|\psi|^{p}

\displaystyle+\alpha^{p-1}(x_{1}x_{2}\dots x_{n})^{p\alpha}(\frac{1}{x_{1}^{2}}+\frac{1}{x_{2}^{2}}+\dots+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{2}})^{\frac{p-2}{2}}(\frac{1}{x_{1}},\frac{1}{x_{2}},\dots,\frac{1}{x_{n}})\cdot\nabla(|\psi|^{p})

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{2^{p-1}-1}(x_{1}x_{2}\dots x_{n})^{p\alpha}|\nabla\psi|^{p}.

Applying integration by parts over
\Omega
gives

(3.6)

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\geq

\displaystyle\alpha^{p}\int_{\Omega}(x_{1}x_{2}\dots x_{n})^{p\alpha}(\frac{1}{x_{1}^{2}}+\frac{1}{x_{2}^{2}}+\dots+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{2}})^{\frac{p}{2}}|\psi|^{p}dx

\displaystyle-\alpha^{p-1}\int_{\Omega}\text{div}\left((x_{1}\dots x_{n})^{p\alpha}(\frac{1}{x_{1}^{2}}+\dots+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{2}})^{\frac{p-2}{2}}(\frac{1}{x_{1}},\dots,\frac{1}{x_{n}})\right)|\psi|^{p}dx

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{2^{p-1}-1}\int_{\Omega}(x_{1}x_{2}\dots x_{n})^{p\alpha}|\nabla\psi|^{p}dx.

Let us denote the divergence term on the right hand side of (
3.6
) by
M
. A straightforward computation shows that

(3.7)

\displaystyle M:=

\displaystyle-\alpha^{p-1}\text{div}\left((x_{1}x_{2}\dots x_{n})^{p\alpha}(\frac{1}{x_{1}^{2}}+\dots+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{2}})^{\frac{p-2}{2}}(\frac{1}{x_{1}},\dots,\frac{1}{x_{n}})\right)

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle-\alpha^{p-1}(\alpha p-1)(x_{1}x_{2}\dots x_{n})^{p\alpha}(\frac{1}{x_{1}^{2}}+\frac{1}{x_{2}^{2}}+\dots+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{2}})^{\frac{p}{2}}.

Substituting (
3.7
) into (
3.6
) gives

(3.8)

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\geq

\displaystyle\left((1-p)\alpha^{p}+\alpha^{p-1}\right)\int_{\Omega}(x_{1}x_{2}\dots x_{n})^{p\alpha}(\frac{1}{x_{1}^{2}}+\frac{1}{x_{2}^{2}}+\dots+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{2}})^{\frac{p}{2}}|\psi|^{p}dx

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{2^{p-1}-1}\int_{\Omega}(x_{1}x_{2}\dots x_{n})^{p\alpha}|\nabla\psi|^{p}dx.

Optimizing the coefficient in front of the first term on the right hand side, one gets
\alpha=\frac{1}{p}
. Substituting this value of
\alpha
into (
3.8
), we get

(3.9)

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\geq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{p^{p}}\int_{\Omega}(x_{1}x_{2}\dots x_{n})(\frac{1}{x_{1}^{2}}+\frac{1}{x_{2}^{2}}+\dots+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{2}})^{\frac{p}{2}}|\psi|^{p}dx

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{2^{p-1}-1}\int_{\Omega}(x_{1}x_{2}\dots x_{n})|\nabla\psi|^{p}dx.

Furthermore, using the inequality

\left(\frac{1}{x_{1}^{2}}+\frac{1}{x_{2}^{2}}+\dots+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{2}}\right)^{\frac{p}{2}}\geq\frac{1}{x_{1}^{p}}+\frac{1}{x_{2}^{p}}+\dots+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{p}}

we conclude that

(3.10)

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\geq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{p^{p}}\int_{\Omega}\left(\frac{1}{x_{1}^{p}}+\frac{1}{x_{2}^{p}}+\dots+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{p}}\right)|\phi|^{p}dx

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{2^{p-1}-1}\int_{\Omega}(x_{1}x_{2}\dots x_{n})|\nabla\psi|^{p}dx.

Applying the weighted Sobolev inequality (
1.15
) to the
second integral term on the right hand side of (
3.10
), we get the desired inequality (
3.2
),

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\geq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{p^{p}}\int_{\Omega}\left(\frac{1}{x_{1}^{p}}+\frac{1}{x_{2}^{p}}+\dots+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{p}}\right)|\phi|^{p}dx

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{2^{p-1}-1}\left(S_{p}(D)\right)^{p}\left(\int_{\Omega}(x_{1}x_{2}\dots x_{n})^{\frac{-p}{2n-p}}|\phi|^{\frac{Dp}{D-p}}dx\right)^{\frac{D-p}{D}}.

Furthermore, with the help of arithmetic-geometric mean inequality, we can easily show that

(x_{1}x_{2}\dots x_{n})^{\frac{-p}{2n-p}}\geq(\frac{\sqrt{n}}{r})^{\frac{np}{2n-p}}>(\frac{\sqrt{n}}{R})^{\frac{np}{2n-p}}.

Hence, for any
\phi\in C_{0}^{\infty}(B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{*}^{n})
, we have the desired inequality (
3.3
),

\displaystyle\int_{B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{*}^{n}}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\geq\frac{1}{p^{p}}

\displaystyle\int_{B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{*}^{n}}\left(\frac{1}{x_{1}^{p}}+\frac{1}{x_{2}^{p}}+\dots+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{p}}\right)|\phi|^{p}dx

\displaystyle+C\left(\int_{B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{*}^{n}}|\phi|^{\frac{Dp}{D-p}}dx\right)^{\frac{D-p}{D}}

where
C=\frac{\left(S_{p}(D)\right)^{p}}{2^{p-1}-1}(\frac{\sqrt{n}}{R})^{\frac{p}{2}}
.
∎

Using Theorem
2.3
and the method described above, we obtain the following Hardy-Poincaré type inequality.

Theorem 3.2
.

Let
\phi\in C_{0}^{\infty}(B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{*}^{n})
and
p>2
. Then we have,

(3.11)

\displaystyle\int_{B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{*}^{n}}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\geq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{p^{p}}\int_{B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{*}^{n}}\left(\frac{1}{x_{1}^{p}}+\frac{1}{x_{2}^{p}}+\dots+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{p}}\right)|\phi|^{p}dx

\displaystyle+(\frac{1}{2^{p-1}-1})(\frac{2n}{pR})^{p}\int_{B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{*}^{n}}|\phi|^{p}dx.

Proof.

Let
\phi\in C_{0}^{\infty}(B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{*}^{n})
and define
\psi=(x_{1}x_{2}\dots x_{n})^{-\frac{1}{p}}\phi
.
Using the same argument as in Theorem
3.1
, we have the following inequality (see (
3.10
))

(3.12)

\displaystyle\int_{B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{*}^{n}}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\geq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{p^{p}}\int_{B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{*}^{n}}\left(\frac{1}{x_{1}^{p}}+\frac{1}{x_{2}^{p}}+\dots+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{p}}\right)|\phi|^{p}dx

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{2^{p-1}-1}\int_{B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{*}^{n}}(x_{1}x_{2}\dots x_{n})|\nabla\psi|^{p}dx.

An application of the inequality (
2.12
) to the second term on the right-hand side of (
3.12
) which gives the desired inequality

\displaystyle\int_{B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{*}^{n}}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\geq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{p^{p}}\int_{B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{*}^{n}}\left(\frac{1}{x_{1}^{p}}+\frac{1}{x_{2}^{p}}+\dots+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{p}}\right)|\phi|^{p}dx

\displaystyle+(\frac{1}{2^{p-1}-1})(\frac{2n}{pR})^{p}\int_{B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{*}^{n}}|\phi|^{p}dx.

∎

We now present another theorem that allows us to obtain different remainder terms for the Hardy type inequality (
3.1
).

Theorem 3.3
.

Let
p>2
. Let
\delta
be nonnegative function and
\delta(x)\in C^{2}(\Omega)
such
that
-\emph{div}\left((x_{1}x_{2}\dots x_{n})\frac{|\nabla\delta|^{p-2}}{\delta^{p-2}}\nabla\delta\right)\geq 0
in the sense of distributions. Then for all
\phi\in C_{0}^{\infty}(\Omega)
, we have

(3.13)

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\geq\frac{1}{p^{p}}\int_{\Omega}\left(\frac{1}{x_{1}^{p}}+\frac{1}{x_{2}^{p}}+\dots+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{p}}\right)|\phi|^{p}dx+(\frac{1}{2^{p-1}-1})\frac{1}{p^{p}}\int_{\Omega}\frac{|\nabla\delta|^{p}}{\delta^{p}}|\phi|^{p}dx.

Proof.

Let
\phi\in C_{0}^{\infty}(\Omega)
and define
\psi=(x_{1}x_{2}\dots x_{n})^{-\frac{1}{p}}\phi
.
Using the same argument as in Theorem
3.1
, we have the following inequality (see (
3.10
))

(3.14)

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\geq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{p^{p}}\int_{\Omega}\left(\frac{1}{x_{1}^{p}}+\frac{1}{x_{2}^{p}}+\dots+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{p}}\right)|\phi|^{p}dx

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{2^{p-1}-1}\int_{\Omega}(x_{1}x_{2}\dots x_{n})|\nabla\psi|^{p}dx.

Now we focus on the second term on the right-hand side of this inequality. Let us define a new variable
\varphi(x):=\delta^{-1/p}\psi(x)
. It follows from the convexity inequality (
3.4
) that

|\nabla\psi|^{p}\geq\frac{|\varphi|^{p}}{p^{p}}\delta^{1-p}|\nabla\delta|^{p}+p^{1-p}|\nabla\delta|^{p-2}\delta^{2-p}\nabla\delta\cdot\nabla(|\varphi|^{p})

and therefore

(3.15)

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}(x_{1}x_{2}\dots x_{n})|\nabla\psi|^{p}dx\geq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{p^{p}}\int_{\Omega}(x_{1}x_{2}\dots x_{n})|\nabla\delta|^{p}\delta^{1-p}|\varphi|^{p}dx

\displaystyle+p^{1-p}\int_{\Omega}(x_{1}x_{2}\dots x_{n})\frac{|\nabla\delta|^{p-2}}{\delta^{p-2}}\nabla\delta\cdot\nabla(|\varphi|^{p})dx.

Applying integration by parts to the second term on the right hand side of (
3.15
) gives

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}(x_{1}x_{2}\dots x_{n})|\nabla\psi|^{p}dx\geq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{p^{p}}\int_{\Omega}(x_{1}x_{2}\dots x_{n})|\nabla\delta|^{p}\delta^{1-p}|\varphi|^{p}dx

\displaystyle-p^{1-p}\int_{\Omega}\text{div}\left((x_{1}x_{2}\dots x_{n})\frac{|\nabla\delta|^{p-2}}{\delta^{p-2}}\nabla\delta\right)|\varphi|^{p}dx.

Since
-\text{div}\left((x_{1}x_{2}\dots x_{n})\frac{|\nabla\delta|^{p-2}}{\delta^{p-2}}\nabla\delta\right)\geq 0
and
\varphi=(x_{1}x_{2}\dots x_{n})^{\frac{-1}{p}}\phi\delta^{-1/p}
then we get

(3.16)

\int_{\Omega}(x_{1}x_{2}\dots x_{n})|\nabla\psi|^{p}dx\geq\frac{1}{p^{p}}\int_{\Omega}\frac{|\nabla\delta|^{p}}{\delta^{p}}|\phi|^{p}dx.

Substituting (
3.16
) into (
3.15
) gives the desired inequality

\int_{\Omega}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\geq\frac{1}{p^{p}}\int_{\Omega}\left(\frac{1}{x_{1}^{p}}+\frac{1}{x_{2}^{p}}+\dots+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{p}}\right)|\phi|^{p}dx+(\frac{1}{2^{p-1}-1})\frac{1}{p^{p}}\int_{\Omega}\frac{|\nabla\delta|^{p}}{\delta^{p}}|\phi|^{p}dx.

∎

3.1.
Applications of Theorem
3.3

By applying Theorem
3.3
to particular
\delta(x)
, we obtain several Hardy type inequalities with remainder terms. First, let us consider the function,

\delta(x):=e^{-(2^{p-1}-1)^{\frac{1}{p}}n|x|}.

Note that
\delta
fulfills the assumptions of Theorem
3.3
when
|x|\leq\frac{2n-1}{n}\left(\frac{1}{2^{p-1}-1}\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}.
Hence, we have the following Hardy type inequality, which has a Poincaré remainder term.

Corollary 3.1
.

Let
2<p<n
and
R=\frac{2n-1}{n}(\frac{1}{2^{p-1}-1})^{\frac{1}{p}}
. Then for all
\phi\in C_{0}^{\infty}(B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{*}^{n})
, we have

(3.17)

\displaystyle\int_{B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{*}^{n}}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\geq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{p^{p}}\int_{B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{*}^{n}}\left(\frac{1}{x_{1}^{p}}+\frac{1}{x_{2}^{p}}+\dots+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{p}}\right)|\phi|^{p}dx

\displaystyle+\left(\frac{n}{p}\right)^{p}\int_{B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{*}^{n}}|\phi|^{p}dx.

On the other hand, by making the choice

\delta(x):=\left(\ln\frac{1}{|x|}\right)^{(2^{p-1}-1)^{\frac{1}{p}}(p-1)},

gives the following Hardy-Leray type inequality.

Corollary 3.2
.

Let
2<p<n
and
R=e^{\frac{(1-p)((2^{p-1}-1)^{1/p}-1)}{2n-p}}
. Then for all
\phi\in C_{0}^{\infty}(B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{*}^{n})
, we have

(3.18)

\displaystyle\int_{B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{*}^{n}}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\geq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{p^{p}}\int_{B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{*}^{n}}\left(\frac{1}{x_{1}^{p}}+\frac{1}{x_{2}^{p}}+\dots+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{p}}\right)|\phi|^{p}dx

\displaystyle+\left(\frac{p-1}{p}\right)^{p}\int_{B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{*}^{n}}\frac{|\phi|^{p}}{|x|^{p}\left(\ln(\frac{1}{|x|})\right)^{p}}dx.

Finally, let us consider the function

\delta(x):=|x|^{(p-n)(2^{p-1}-1)^{\frac{1}{p}}}.

In fact, this choice combines both the Hardy (
1.1
) and Hardy type (
3.1
) inequalities into a single inequality.

Corollary 3.3
.

Let
2<p<n
. Then for all
\phi\in C_{0}^{\infty}(\Omega)
, we have

(3.19)

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\geq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{p^{p}}\int_{\Omega}\left(\frac{1}{x_{1}^{p}}+\frac{1}{x_{2}^{p}}+\dots+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{p}}\right)|\phi|^{p}dx

\displaystyle+\left(\frac{n-p}{p}\right)^{p}\int_{\Omega}\frac{|\phi|^{p}}{|x|^{p}}dx.

4.
Maz’ya type inequalities with remainder terms

In this final section we assume that
\Omega
is a bounded domain with smooth boundary
\partial\Omega
in the upper half space
\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n}
. The purpose of this section is to obtain various Maz’ya type inequalities with remainder terms. Furthermore, we present some interpolation inequalities which will be useful in what follows. To motivate our discussion, let us start with the following family of Maz’ya-Sobolev inequalities.

Lemma 4.1
.

Let
1<p<n+p-1
,
0\leq s\leq p
and
\phi\in C_{0}^{\infty}(\Omega)
. Then we have,

(4.1)

\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n}}x_{n}^{p-1}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}\geq C\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n}}\frac{x_{n}^{(p-1)(p-s)/p}}{(x_{n-1}^{2}+x_{n}^{2})^{s/2p}}|\phi|^{p^{*}(s)}dx\right)^{\frac{1}{p^{*}(s)}},

where
D=n+p-1
,
p^{*}(s)=p\left(\frac{D-s}{D-p}\right)
and

C=\frac{\left(S_{p}(D)\right)^{\frac{D(p-s)}{p(D-s)}}}{(2p)^{\frac{s}{p^{*}(s)}}}
.

Proof.

The proof follows by applying Hölder’s inequality, then the Maz’ya inequality (
1.3
) and the Sobolev inequality of Cabré and Ros-Orton (
1.15
) with
A_{1}=\dots=A_{n-1}=0,A_{n}=p-1
. ∎

Inspired by the Maz’ya inequality (
1.3
), we present the following Maz’ya type inequality (
4.2
) and its improved version (in the sense that nonnegative terms are added on the right hand side of (
4.2
)) over the set
B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n}
. The following is our result.

Theorem 4.1
.

Let
2\leq p<n+p-1
. Then for all
\phi\in C_{0}^{\infty}(\Omega)
, we have

(4.2)

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}x_{n}^{p-1}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\geq\frac{1}{p^{p}}\int_{\Omega}\frac{x_{n}^{p-1}}{(x_{n-1}^{2}+x_{n}^{2})^{p/2}}|\phi|^{p}dx.

Moreover, for every
\phi\in C_{0}^{\infty}(B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n})
,

(4.3)

\displaystyle\int_{B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n}}x_{n}^{p-1}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\geq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{p^{p}}\int_{B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n}}\frac{x_{n}^{p-1}}{(x_{n-1}^{2}+x_{n}^{2})^{p/2}}|\phi|^{p}dx

\displaystyle+C\left(\int_{B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n}}x_{n}^{p-1}(x_{n-1}^{2}+x_{n}^{2})^{\frac{D}{2(D-p)}}|\phi|^{\frac{Dp}{D-p}}dx\right)^{\frac{D-p}{D}}.

where
D=n+p-1
and
C=\frac{\left(S_{p}(D)\right)^{p}}{(2^{p-1}-1)R}
.

Proof.

Let
\phi\in C_{0}^{\infty}(\Omega)
and define
\phi=\rho^{\alpha}\psi
where
\alpha<0
and
\rho=(x_{n-1}^{2}+x_{n}^{2})
. Then we have

|\nabla\phi|^{p}=|\alpha\rho^{\alpha-1}\psi\nabla\rho+\rho^{\alpha}\nabla\psi|^{p}.

We use the convexity inequality (
3.4
) with
a=\alpha\rho^{\alpha-1}\psi\nabla\rho
and
b=\rho^{\alpha}\nabla\psi
to get

(4.4)

\displaystyle|\nabla\phi|^{p}\geq

\displaystyle|\alpha|^{p}\rho^{p\alpha-p}|\nabla\rho|^{p}|\psi|^{p}

\displaystyle+\alpha|\alpha|^{p-2}\rho^{p\alpha-p+1}|\nabla\rho|^{p-2}\nabla\rho\cdot\nabla(|\psi|^{p})

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{2^{p-1}-1}\rho^{p\alpha}|\nabla\psi|^{p}.

Multiplying both sides of
(\ref{4.4})
by
x_{n}^{p-1}
and integrating by parts over the set
\Omega
gives,

(4.5)

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}x_{n}^{p-1}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\geq

\displaystyle|\alpha|^{p}\int_{\Omega}x_{n}^{p-1}\rho^{p\alpha-p}|\nabla\rho|^{p}|\psi|^{p}dx

\displaystyle-\alpha|\alpha|^{p-2}\int_{\Omega}\text{div}(x_{n}^{p-1}\rho^{\alpha p-p+1}|\nabla\rho|^{p-2}\nabla\rho)|\psi|^{p}dx

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{2^{p-1}-1}\int_{\Omega}x_{n}^{p-1}\rho^{p\alpha}|\nabla\psi|^{p}dx.

A direct computation shows that

(4.6)

\displaystyle\text{div}(x_{n}^{p-1}\rho^{\alpha p-p+1}|\nabla\rho|^{p-2}\nabla\rho)=2^{p-1}x_{n}^{p-1}\rho^{\alpha p-\frac{p}{2}}(1+2\alpha p)=0

when
\alpha=-\frac{1}{2p}
.
Substituting
(\ref{4.6})
into
(\ref{4.5})
and writing
\psi=(x_{n-1}^{2}+x_{n}^{2})^{\frac{1}{2p}}\phi
, we get

(4.7)

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}x_{n}^{p-1}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\geq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{p^{p}}\int_{\Omega}\frac{x_{n}^{p-1}}{(x_{n-1}^{2}+x_{n}^{2})^{p/2}}|\phi|^{p}dx

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{2^{p-1}-1}\int_{\Omega}x_{n}^{p-1}\rho^{-1/2}|\nabla\psi|^{p}dx.

By dropping the nonnegative last term in (
4.7
), we obtain the desired inequality

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}x_{n}^{p-1}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\geq\frac{1}{p^{p}}\int_{\Omega}\frac{x_{n}^{p-1}}{(x_{n-1}^{2}+x_{n}^{2})^{p/2}}|\phi|^{p}dx.

Now let us look at the second part of Theorem
4.1
. The inequality (
4.7
) is valid for
\phi\in C_{0}^{\infty}(B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n})
. Since
\rho^{-1/2}=(x_{n-1}^{2}+x_{n}^{2})^{-1/2}\geq(x_{1}^{2}+\dots+x_{n}^{2})^{-1/2}=\frac{1}{R}
, one can write

(4.8)

\displaystyle\int_{B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n}}x_{n}^{p-1}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\geq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{p^{p}}\int_{B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n}}\frac{x_{n}^{p-1}}{(x_{n-1}^{2}+x_{n}^{2})^{p/2}}|\phi|^{p}dx

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{(2^{p-1}-1)R}\int_{B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n}}x_{n}^{p-1}|\nabla\psi|^{p}dx.

We now apply weighted Sobolev inequality (
1.15
) (see also
[
19
]
, Proposition 1.1) to the second integral term on the right hand side of (
4.9
) and obtain

(4.9)

\int_{B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n}}x_{n}^{p-1}|\nabla\psi|^{p}dx\geq\left(S_{p}(D)\right)^{p}\left(\int_{B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n}}x_{n}^{p-1}|\psi|^{\frac{Dp}{D-p}}dx\right)^{\frac{D-p}{D}}.

Substituting (
4.9
) into (
4.8
) and taking back substitution
\psi=(x_{n-1}^{2}+x_{n}^{2})^{\frac{1}{2p}}\phi
, we obtain the desired inequality,

\displaystyle\int_{B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n}}x_{n}^{p-1}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\geq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{p^{p}}\int_{B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n}}\frac{x_{n}^{p-1}}{(x_{n-1}^{2}+x_{n}^{2})^{p/2}}|\phi|^{p}dx

\displaystyle+\frac{\left(S_{p}(D)\right)^{p}}{(2^{p-1}-1)R}\left(\int_{B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n}}x_{n}^{p-1}(x_{n-1}^{2}+x_{n}^{2})^{\frac{D}{2(D-p)}}|\phi|^{\frac{Dp}{D-p}}dx\right)^{\frac{D-p}{D}}.

∎

Furthermore, by applying the Maz’ya-Sobolev inequality (
4.1
) in (
4.8
), we obtain the following Maz’ya type inequality, which has a weighted Sobolev remainder term.

Corollary 4.1
.

Let
2\leq p<n+p-1
. Then for all
\phi\in C_{0}^{\infty}(B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n})
, we have

(4.10)

\displaystyle\int_{B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n}}x_{n}^{p-1}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\geq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{p^{p}}\int_{B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n}}x_{n}^{p-1}\frac{|\phi|^{p}}{(x_{n-1}^{2}+x_{n}^{2})^{p/2}}dx

\displaystyle+\frac{C^{p}}{(2^{p}-1)R}\left(\int_{B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n}}\frac{x_{n}^{(p-1)(p-s)/p}}{(x_{n-1}^{2}+x_{n}^{2})^{\frac{s}{2p}}}|\phi|^{p^{*}(s)}dx\right)^{\frac{p}{p^{*}(s)}},

where
0\leq s\leq p
,
D=n+p-1
,
C=\frac{\left(S_{p}(D)\right)^{\frac{D(p-s)}{p(D-s)}}}{(2p)^{\frac{s}{p^{*}(s)}}}
and
p^{*}(s)=p\left(\frac{D-s}{D-p}\right)
.

On the other hand, by Hölder’s inequality with the conjugate exponents
p/s
,
p/p-s
, Maz’ya type inequality (
4.2
) and the Sobolev inequality (
1.15
), we have the following Maz’ya-Sobolev type inequality.

Corollary 4.2
.

Let
2\leq p<n+p-1
and
\phi\in C_{0}^{\infty}(\Omega)
. Then we have,

(4.11)

\left(\int_{\Omega}x_{n}^{p-1}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}\geq C\left(\int_{\Omega}\frac{x_{n}^{p-1}}{(x_{n-1}^{2}+x_{n}^{2})^{\frac{s}{2}}}|\phi|^{p^{*}(s)}dx\right)^{\frac{1}{p^{*}(s)}},

where
0\leq s\leq p
,
D=n+p-1
,
p^{*}(s)=p\left(\frac{D-s}{D-p}\right)
and

C=\frac{\left(S_{p}(D)\right)^{\frac{D(p-s)}{p(D-s)}}}{p^{\frac{s}{p^{*}(s)}}}
.

Remark 4.1
.

Note that one can obtain further Maz’ya type inequalities with remainder terms by using the method in Theorem
4.1
and the inequality (
4.11
).

We now present the second main theorem of this section, which allows us to construct additional Maz’ya type inequalities with remainder terms.

Theorem 4.2
.

Let
p\geq 2
and
\delta\in C^{2}(\Omega)
be nonnegative function such that

-\emph{div}(x_{n}^{p-1}(x_{n-1}^{2}+x_{n}^{2})^{-1/2}\frac{|\nabla\delta|^{p-2}}{\delta^{p-2}}\nabla\delta)\geq 0,

in the sense of distributions.
Then for all
\phi\in C_{0}^{\infty}(\Omega)
, we have

(4.12)

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}x_{n}^{p-1}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\geq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{p^{p}}\int_{\Omega}\frac{x_{n}^{p-1}}{(x_{n-1}^{2}+x_{n}^{2})^{p/2}}|\phi|^{p}dx

\displaystyle+\left(\frac{1}{2^{p-1}-1}\right)\frac{1}{p^{p}}\int_{\Omega}x_{n}^{p-1}\frac{|\nabla\delta|^{p}}{\delta^{p}}|\phi|^{p}dx.

Proof.

Let
\phi\in C_{0}^{\infty}(\Omega)
and define
\psi=(x_{n-1}^{2}+x_{n}^{2})^{\frac{-1}{2p}}\phi
. Using the same argument as in Theorem
4.1
, we have the following inequality

(4.13)

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}x_{n}^{p-1}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\geq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{p^{p}}\int_{\Omega}\frac{x_{n}^{p-1}}{(x_{n-1}^{2}+x_{n}^{2})^{p/2}}|\phi|^{p}dx

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{2^{p-1}-1}\int_{\Omega}x_{n}^{p-1}\rho^{-1/2}|\nabla\psi|^{p}dx.

Now we focus on the second term on the right-hand side of this inequality. Let us define a new variable
\varphi(x):=\delta(x)^{-1/p}\psi(x)
. By using the convexity inequality (
3.4
), one has

|\nabla\psi|^{p}\geq\frac{|\varphi|^{p}}{p^{p}}\delta^{1-p}|\nabla\delta|^{p}+p^{1-p}|\nabla\delta|^{p-2}\delta^{2-p}\nabla\delta\cdot\nabla(|\varphi|^{p}).

Hence,

(4.14)

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}x_{n}^{p-1}\rho^{-1/2}|\nabla\psi|^{p}dx\geq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{p^{p}}\int_{\Omega}x_{n}^{p-1}\rho^{-1/2}|\nabla\delta|^{p}\delta^{1-p}|\varphi|^{p}dx

\displaystyle+p^{1-p}\int_{\Omega}x_{n}^{p-1}\rho^{-1/2}\frac{|\nabla\delta|^{p-2}}{\delta^{p-2}}\nabla\delta\cdot\nabla(|\varphi|^{p})dx.

Applying integration by parts to the second term on the right hand side of (
4.14
), then using the differential inequality
-\text{div}(x_{n}^{p-1}(x_{n-1}^{2}+x_{n}^{2})^{-1/2}\frac{|\nabla\delta|^{p-2}}{\delta^{p-2}}\nabla\delta)\geq 0
and taking back substitution
\varphi=\delta^{-1/p}\psi
, we get

(4.15)

\int_{\Omega}x_{n}^{p-1}\rho^{-1/2}|\nabla\psi|^{p}dx\geq\frac{1}{p^{p}}\int_{\Omega}x_{n}^{p-1}(x_{n-1}^{2}+x_{n}^{2})^{-1/2}\frac{|\nabla\delta|^{p}}{\delta^{p}}|\psi|^{p}dx.

Substituting (
4.15
) into (
4.13
) gives

(4.16)

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}x_{n}^{p-1}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\geq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{p^{p}}\int_{\Omega}\frac{x_{n}^{p-1}}{(x_{n-1}^{2}+x_{n}^{2})^{p/2}}|\phi|^{p}dx

\displaystyle+\left(\frac{1}{2^{p-1}-1}\right)\frac{1}{p^{p}}\int_{\Omega}x_{n}^{p-1}(x_{n-1}^{2}+x_{n}^{2})^{-1/2}\frac{|\nabla\delta|^{p}}{\delta^{p}}|\psi|^{p}dx.

Now, taking back substitution
\psi=(x_{n-1}^{2}+x_{n}^{2})^{\frac{1}{2p}}\phi
,
we obtain the desired inequality

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}x_{n}^{p-1}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\geq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{p^{p}}\int_{\Omega}\frac{x_{n}^{p-1}}{(x_{n-1}^{2}+x_{n}^{2})^{p/2}}|\phi|^{p}dx

\displaystyle+\left(\frac{1}{2^{p-1}-1}\right)\frac{1}{p^{p}}\int_{\Omega}x_{n}^{p-1}\frac{|\nabla\delta|^{p}}{\delta^{p}}|\phi|^{p}dx.

∎

4.1.
Applications of Theorem
4.2

It is worth noting that one can obtain as many Maz’ya type inequalities with remainder terms as one can find a function
\delta
satisfying the hypothesis in Theorem
4.2
. Before proceeding, we would like to mention the following inequalities were given a second name based on the remainder terms we obtained. Let us begin by considering the function,

\delta:=e^{-(2^{p-1}-1)^{\frac{1}{p}}n|x|}.

After some computations, we have the following Maz’ya-Poincaré type inequality.

Corollary 4.3
.

Let
2<p<n
,
R=\frac{n+p-3}{n(2^{p-1}-1)^{1/p}}
and
\phi\in C_{0}^{\infty}(B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n})
. Then we have,

(4.17)

\displaystyle\int_{B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n}}x_{n}^{p-1}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\geq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{p^{p}}\int_{B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n}}\frac{x_{n}^{p-1}}{(x_{n-1}^{2}+x_{n}^{2})^{p/2}}|\phi|^{p}dx

\displaystyle+\left(\frac{n}{p}\right)^{p}\int_{B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n}}x_{n}^{p-1}|\phi|^{p}dx.

Another application of Theorem
4.2
with the special function

\delta:=\left(\ln\frac{1}{|x|}\right)^{(2^{p-1}-1)^{\frac{1}{p}}(p-1)},

gives the following Maz’ya-Leray type inequality.

Corollary 4.4
.

Let
2<p<n
,
R=e^{\frac{(1-p)((2^{p-1}-1)^{1/p}-1)}{n-2}}
and
\phi\in C_{0}^{\infty}(B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n})
. Then we have,

(4.18)

\displaystyle\int_{B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n}}x_{n}^{p-1}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\geq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{p^{p}}\int_{B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n}}\frac{x_{n}^{p-1}}{(x_{n-1}^{2}+x_{n}^{2})^{p/2}}|\phi|^{p}dx

\displaystyle+\left(\frac{p-1}{p}\right)^{p}\int_{B_{R}\cap\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n}}x_{n}^{p-1}\frac{|\phi|^{p}}{|x|^{p}\left(\ln(\frac{1}{|x|})\right)^{p}}dx.

Finally, let us consider the following function

\delta:=|x|^{(2^{p-1}-1)^{\frac{1}{p}}(p-n)}.

Note that
\delta
satisfies the hypotheses of Theorem
4.2
on
\Omega
when
\frac{n}{2}+1\leq p<n
. Hence we have the following inequality, which includes both
Maz’ya and Hardy type inequalities.

Corollary 4.5
.

Let
\frac{n}{2}+1\leq p<n
and
\phi\in C_{0}^{\infty}(\Omega)
. Then we have,

(4.19)

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}x_{n}^{p-1}|\nabla\phi|^{p}dx\geq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{p^{p}}\int_{\Omega}\frac{x_{n}^{p-1}}{(x_{n-1}^{2}+x_{n}^{2})^{p/2}}|\phi|^{p}dx

\displaystyle+\left(\frac{n-p}{p}\right)^{p}\int_{\Omega}x_{n}^{p-1}\frac{|\phi|^{p}}{|x|^{p}}dx.
