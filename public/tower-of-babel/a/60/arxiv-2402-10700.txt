Resonance of Gravitational Axions-like Particles
Jorge Gamboa, Fernando Mendez

Abstract
The motion of gravitational axion-like particles (ALP) around a Kerr black hole is analyzed, paying attention to resonance and distribution of spectral radiation. We first discuss the computation of g ​ R ~ μ ​ ν ​ ρ ​ ρ ​ σ ​ R μ ​ ν ​ ρ ​ σ \sqrt{g}{\tilde{R}}_{\mu\nu\rho\rho\sigma}R^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma} and its implications with Pontryagin’s theorem and a detailed analysis of Teukolsky’s master equation is done. After carefully analyzing the Teukolsky master equation, we show that this system exhibits resonance when ω ≳ μ \omega\gtrsim\mu where μ \mu is the mass of the ALP. A skew-normal distribution can approximate the energy distribution, and we can calculate the mean lifetime of the resonance for black holes with masses between 100 to 1000 M ​ ⊙ M\textsubscript{$\odot$} . This range corresponds to a duration between 10 − 1 10^{-1} s and 10 41 10^{41} s, the observation range used in LIGO data.

Conclusions

This research paper explores the movement of gravitational axions in a Kerr black hole background
using analytical and numerical methods. One interesting finding is that resonance occurs
when
\omega\gtrsim\mu
, similar to the Detweiler-resonance discussed in
detweiler
.
However, the Detweiler-resonance is related to a massless scalar. This research concludes that
this resonance always occurs if
\ell
is odd and a Pontryaguin-like source is present.

Another important observation is that Figures 6 and 7 show that the spectral maxima shifts to
the right, and the radiated power decreases with
{\bar{\omega}}
. Additionally, the
maximum becomes significantly smaller when
\ell
grows. However, it is interesting to note

\bar{\mu}
.

For
\bar{\mu}\gtrsim 0.3

\mbox{\small{erf}}(x)=\frac{2}{\sqrt{\pi}}\int_{0}^{x}e^{-t^{2}}\,dt.

The function

So, an estimation of the mean width of the curves in Figures

2\sqrt{\mbox{Var}}
