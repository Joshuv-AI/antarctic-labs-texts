On Sharp Beckner’s Inequality for Axially Symmetric Functions on 𝕊 4 \mathbb{S}^{4}
Tuoxin Li, Juncheng Wei, Zikai Ye

Abstract
. We prove that axially symmetric solutions to the Q Q -curvature type problem α ​ P 4 ​ u + 6 ​ ( 1 − e 4 ​ u ∫ 𝕊 4 e 4 ​ u ) = 0 on ​ 𝕊 4 \alpha P_{4}u+6(1-\frac{e^{4u}}{\int_{\mathbb{S}^{4}}e^{4u}})=0\ \ \ \ \ \mbox{on}\ \mathbb{S}^{4} must be constants, provided that 1 2 ≤ α < 1 \frac{1}{2}\leq\alpha<1 . This result is sharp in view of the existence of nonconstant solutions to the equation by Gui,Hu and Xie [ 15 ] for 1 5 < α < 1 2 \frac{1}{5}<\alpha<\frac{1}{2} . As a consequence, we prove a sharp Beckner’s inequality on 𝕊 4 \mathbb{S}^{4} for axially symmetric functions with center of mass at the origin. This answers an open question in [ 15 ] in which the corresponding results were proved for α ≥ 0.517 \alpha\geq 0.517 . To close the gap, we make use of some quantitative properties of Gegenbauer polynomials. One of the key ingredients in our proof is the pointwise estimate of large parameters asymptotic expansions for Gegenbauer polynomials proved in Nemes and Daalhuis [ 23 ] .

We will use the same strategy, as in
