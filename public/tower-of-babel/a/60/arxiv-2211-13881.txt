Lemma 4.3
.

We have

(i)
\Delta\left(v\left(\frac{\lambda^{2}x}{|x|^{2}}\right)\right)=\frac{2\alpha}{|x|^{2}}-\frac{1}{2\omega_{3}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{4}}\frac{u^{p_{2}}(y)e^{q_{2}v(y)}}{\left|x-\frac{\lambda^{2}y}{|y|^{2}}\right|^{2}}dy

for

Proof.

A direct calculation shows that

\displaystyle\Delta\left(v\left(\frac{\lambda^{2}x}{|x|^{2}}\right)\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{\lambda^{4}}{|x|^{4}}\Delta v\left(\frac{\lambda^{2}x}{|x|^{2}}\right)-4\lambda^{2}\frac{x\cdot\nabla v\left(\frac{\lambda^{2}x}{|x|^{2}}\right)}{|x|^{4}}

\displaystyle=-\frac{\lambda^{4}}{2\omega_{3}|x|^{4}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{4}}\frac{u^{p_{2}}(y)e^{q_{2}v(y)}}{\left|\frac{\lambda^{2}x}{|x|^{2}}-y\right|^{2}}dy+\sum^{4}_{i=1}\frac{\lambda^{2}x_{i}}{\omega_{3}|x|^{4}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{4}}\frac{\frac{\lambda^{2}x_{i}}{|x|^{2}}-y_{i}}{\left|\frac{\lambda^{2}x}{|x|^{2}}-y\right|^{2}}u^{p_{2}}(y)e^{q_{2}v(y)}dy

\displaystyle=\frac{\lambda^{4}}{2\omega_{3}|x|^{4}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{4}}\frac{u^{p_{2}}(y)e^{q_{2}v(y)}}{\left|\frac{\lambda^{2}x}{|x|^{2}}-y\right|^{2}}dy-\frac{\lambda^{2}}{\omega_{3}|x|^{4}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{4}}\frac{x\cdot y}{\left|\frac{\lambda^{2}x}{|x|^{2}}-y\right|^{2}}u^{p_{2}}(y)e^{q_{2}v(y)}dy

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2\omega_{3}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{4}}\frac{u^{p_{2}}(y)e^{q_{2}v(y)}}{\left|x-\frac{|x|^{2}}{\lambda^{2}}y\right|^{2}}dy-\frac{1}{\omega_{3}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{4}}\frac{\frac{1}{\lambda^{2}}x\cdot y}{\left|x-\frac{|x|^{2}}{\lambda^{2}}y\right|^{2}}u^{p_{2}}(y)e^{q_{2}v(y)}dy

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2\omega_{3}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{4}}\frac{1-\frac{2x\cdot y}{\lambda^{2}}}{\left|x-\frac{|x|^{2}}{\lambda^{2}}y\right|^{2}}u^{p_{2}}(y)e^{q_{2}v(y)}dy

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2\omega_{3}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{4}}\frac{\frac{1}{\lambda^{4}}\left[|x|^{2}\left|\frac{\lambda^{2}x}{|x|^{2}}-y\right|^{2}-|x|^{2}|y|^{2}\right]}{\left|x-\frac{|x|^{2}}{\lambda^{2}}y\right|^{2}}u^{p_{2}}(y)e^{q_{2}v(y)}dy

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2\omega_{3}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{4}}\left[\frac{1}{|x|^{2}}-\frac{|x|^{2}|y|^{2}}{\lambda^{4}\left|x-\frac{|x|^{2}}{\lambda^{2}}y\right|^{2}}\right]u^{p_{2}}(y)e^{q_{2}v(y)}dy

\displaystyle=\frac{2\alpha}{|x|^{2}}-\frac{1}{2\omega_{3}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{4}}\frac{u^{p_{2}}(y)e^{q_{2}v(y)}}{\left|x-\frac{\lambda^{2}y}{|y|^{2}}\right|^{2}}dy

and

\displaystyle-\Delta\widetilde{S}^{v}_{\lambda}

\displaystyle=-\Delta v(x)+\Delta\left(v\left(\frac{\lambda^{2}x}{|x|^{2}}\right)\right)-\frac{2\mu}{|x|^{2}}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2\omega_{3}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{4}}\frac{u^{p_{2}}(y)e^{q_{2}v(y)}}{|x-y|^{2}}dy-\frac{1}{2\omega_{3}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{4}}\frac{u^{p_{2}}(y)e^{q_{2}v(y)}}{\left|x-\frac{\lambda^{2}y}{|y|^{2}}\right|^{2}}dy+\frac{2(\alpha-\mu)}{|x|^{2}}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2\omega_{3}}\int_{B_{\lambda}}\frac{u^{p_{2}}(y)e^{q_{2}v(y)}}{|x-y|^{2}}dy+\frac{1}{2\omega_{3}}\int_{B_{\lambda}^{c}}\frac{u^{p_{2}}(y)e^{q_{2}v(y)}}{|x-y|^{2}}dy

\displaystyle-\frac{1}{2\omega_{3}}\int_{B_{\lambda}}\frac{u^{p_{2}}(y)e^{q_{2}v(y)}}{\left|x-\frac{\lambda^{2}y}{|y|^{2}}\right|^{2}}dy-\frac{1}{2\omega_{3}}\int_{B^{c}_{\lambda}}\frac{u^{p_{2}}(y)e^{q_{2}v(y)}}{\left|x-\frac{\lambda^{2}y}{|y|^{2}}\right|^{2}}dy+\frac{2(\alpha-\mu)}{|x|^{2}}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2\omega_{3}}\int_{B_{\lambda}}\frac{u^{p_{2}}(y)e^{q_{2}v(y)}}{|x-y|^{2}}dy+\frac{1}{2\omega_{3}}\int_{B_{\lambda}}\frac{u^{p_{2}}\left(\frac{\lambda^{2}z}{|z|^{2}}\right)e^{q_{2}v\left(\frac{\lambda^{2}z}{|z|^{2}}\right)}}{\left|x-\frac{\lambda^{2}z}{|z|^{2}}\right|^{2}}\frac{\lambda^{8}}{|z|^{8}}dz

\displaystyle-\frac{1}{2\omega_{3}}\int_{B_{\lambda}}\frac{u^{p_{2}}(y)e^{q_{2}v(y)}}{\left|x-\frac{\lambda^{2}y}{|y|^{2}}\right|^{2}}dy-\frac{1}{2\omega_{3}}\int_{B_{\lambda}}\frac{u^{p_{2}}\left(\frac{\lambda^{2}z}{|z|^{2}}\right)e^{q_{2}v\left(\frac{\lambda^{2}z}{|z|^{2}}\right)}}{|x-z|^{2}}\frac{\lambda^{8}}{|z|^{8}}dz+\frac{2(\alpha-\mu)}{|x|^{2}}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2\omega_{3}}\int_{B_{\lambda}}\left[\frac{1}{|x-y|^{2}}-\frac{1}{\left|x-\frac{\lambda^{2}y}{|y|^{2}}\right|^{2}}\right][u^{p_{2}}(y)e^{q_{2}v(y)}-u_{\lambda}^{p_{2}}e^{q_{2}v_{\lambda}}]dy+\frac{2(\alpha-\mu)}{|x|^{2}}.

Proof of Theorem
1.1
.
Define
f(x)=e^{v(x)}
, then it follows from Proposition
5.6
that

f(x)=\frac{\lambda_{b}^{\mu}}{|x-b|^{\mu}}f\left(\frac{\lambda_{b}^{2}(x-b)}{|x-b|^{2}}+b\right)

and

u(x)=\frac{\lambda_{b}^{2}}{|x-b|^{2}}u\left(\frac{\lambda_{b}^{2}(x-b)}{|x-b|^{2}}+b\right).

Let

A=\lim_{|x|\rightarrow\infty}|x|^{\mu}f(x)=\lambda_{b}^{\mu}f(b)

and

B=\lim_{|x|\rightarrow\infty}|x|^{2}u(x)=\lambda_{b}^{2}u(b),

where
A
,
B>0
. We first assume that
A=B=1
. Since

u(x)=\frac{\lambda_{0}^{2}}{|x|^{2}}u\left(\frac{\lambda_{0}^{2}x}{|x|^{2}}\right)=\frac{\lambda_{b}^{2}}{|x-b|^{2}}u\left(\frac{\lambda_{b}^{2}(x-b)}{|x-b|^{2}}+b\right),

then one has

u(x)=\frac{\lambda_{0}^{2}}{|x|^{2}}\left[u(0)+\nabla u(0)\frac{\lambda_{0}^{2}x}{|x|^{2}}+o\left(\frac{1}{|x|}\right)\right]

(5.6)

and

u(x)=\frac{\lambda_{b}^{2}}{|x-b|^{2}}\left[u(b)+\nabla u(b)\frac{\lambda_{b}^{2}(x-b)}{|x-b|^{2}}+o\left(\frac{1}{|x-b|}\right)\right]

(5.7)

as
|x|\rightarrow\infty
. It follows from equation (
5.6
) and (
5.7
) that

\frac{\partial u(b)}{\partial x_{i}}u(b)^{-2}=\frac{\partial u(0)}{\partial x_{i}}u(0)^{-2}-2b_{i}

and

(u^{-1})_{i}(b)=2b_{i}+(u^{-1})_{i}(0)=\frac{\partial}{\partial b_{i}}(|b|^{2}+\nabla u^{-1}(0)\cdot b).

Therefore,

u(b)=\frac{1}{|b-d_{0}|^{2}+d}.

Similarly, we conclude that

f(b)=\left(\frac{1}{|b-d_{0}|^{2}+d}\right)^{\frac{\mu}{2}}

and

v(b)=\frac{\mu}{2}\ln\left(\frac{1}{|b-d_{0}|^{2}+d}\right).

Finally, if we don’t assume
A=B=1
, then

u(x)=\frac{C_{1}(\varepsilon)}{|x-x_{0}|^{2}+\varepsilon^{2}}

and

v(x)=\frac{3-p_{1}}{q_{1}}\ln\left(\frac{C_{2}(\varepsilon)}{|x-x_{0}|^{2}+\varepsilon^{2}}\right).

Then, by Proposition
5.1
and direct calculations, one obtains

\left\{\begin{aligned} &C_{1}^{p_{1}}C_{2}^{3-p_{1}}=8C_{1}\varepsilon^{2}\\
&C_{1}^{p_{2}}C_{2}^{4-p_{2}}=48\mu\varepsilon^{4}.\end{aligned}\right.

This completes the proof of Theorem
1.1
.
\Box
