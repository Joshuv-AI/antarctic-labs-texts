Rank growth of Elliptic Curves in S 4 S_{4} and A 4 A_{4} -quartic extensions of the rationals
Daniel Keliher

Abstract
. We investigate the rank growth of elliptic curves from ℚ \mathbb{Q} to S 4 S_{4} and A 4 A_{4} -quartic extensions K / ℚ K/\mathbb{Q} . In particular, we are interested in the quantity rk ⁡ ( E / K ) − rk ⁡ ( E / ℚ ) \mathrm{rk}(E/K)-\mathrm{rk}(E/\mathbb{Q}) for fixed E E and varying K K . When rk ⁡ ( E / ℚ ) ≤ 1 \mathrm{rk}(E/\mathbb{Q})\leq 1 , with E E subject to some other conditions, we prove there are infinitely many S 4 S_{4} -quartic extensions K / ℚ K/\mathbb{Q} over which E E does not gain rank, i.e. such that rk ⁡ ( E / K ) − rk ⁡ ( E / ℚ ) = 0 \mathrm{rk}(E/K)-\mathrm{rk}(E/\mathbb{Q})=0 . To do so, we show how to control the 2-Selmer rank of E E in certain quadratic extensions, which in turn contributes to controlling the rank in families of S 4 S_{4} and A 4 A_{4} -quartic extensions of ℚ \mathbb{Q} .

3.
The 2-Selmer Groups and Quadratic Twists

In the previous section, we established that the rank growth from

3.1.
The 2-Selmer Group

We now recall the definition of the 2-Selmer group for an elliptic curve

0\rightarrow E[2]\rightarrow E(\bar{\mathbb{Q}})\overset{\times 2}{\rightarrow}E(\bar{\mathbb{Q}})\rightarrow 0.

This in turn yields a long exact sequence of Galois cohomology groups, which, after quotienting appropriately, gives rise to the following diagram.

{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces E(L)/2E(L)}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces{H^{1}(L,E[2])}}

Now, define subgroups

of each local cohomology

H^{1}_{f}(L_{v},E[2])\coloneqq\mathrm{Image}\left(E(L_{v})/2E(K_{v})\rightarrow H^{1}(L_{v},E[2])\right).

Definition 3.1
.

The 2-Selmer group of

, denoted
\mathrm{Sel}_{2}(E/L)
, is the

-vector space defined by the exactness of the following sequence:

0\rightarrow\mathrm{Sel}_{2}(E/L)\rightarrow H^{1}(L,E[2])\rightarrow\bigoplus_{v}H^{1}(L_{v},E[2])/H^{1}_{f}(L_{v},E[2]).

We may think of the elements of the 2-Selmer group as being the classes in

which, for every place

, land in the image of

. That is, elements of

which everywhere locally satisfy the local conditions determined by

.

Further, the 2-Selmer group fits into a short exact sequence

0\rightarrow E(L)/2E(L)\rightarrow\mathrm{Sel}_{2}(E/L)\rightarrow\Sha(E/L)[2]\rightarrow 0,

where
\Sha(E/L)[2]
are the elements of the Shafarevich-Tate group of

with order dividing 2. We have

(3.1)

\dim_{\mathbb{F}_{2}}\mathrm{Sel}_{2}(E/L)=\left(\mathrm{rk}(E/L)+\dim_{\mathbb{F}_{2}}E(L)[2]\right)+\dim_{\mathbb{F}_{2}}\Sha(E/L)[2]

and further that
\mathrm{rk}(E/L)\leq\dim_{\mathbb{F}_{2}}\mathrm{Sel}_{2}(E/L).
It is by this relation that we’ll access the ranks of the various elliptic curves and twists discussed later in the paper.
