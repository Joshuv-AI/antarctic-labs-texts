is the neighbourhood retract of the space

.

Proof.
Consider the following set

O_{\delta_{x}}=\left\{\mu=\bigoplus\limits_{i=1}^{n}\lambda_{i}\odot\delta_{x_{i}}\in I_{\omega}(X):\lambda_{i}=0\mbox{ at }x_{i}=x\mbox{ and }\lambda_{i}<-ln2\mbox{ at }x_{i}\neq x\right\}.

We will show that
O_{\delta_{x}}

. Let
\nu=\bigoplus\limits_{i=1}^{k}\lambda_{i}\odot\delta_{x_{i}}\in O_{\delta_{x}}

\lambda_{i}=0
at
x_{i}=x
and
\lambda_{i}<-ln2
at
x_{i}\neq x
. For points

we choose disjoint open neighbourhoods

,

\varepsilon=\min\left\{-\lambda_{i}-ln2:\ i\in\{1,\ ...,\ k\}\setminus\{i_{0}\}\right\}>0

\mu=\bigoplus\limits_{j=1}^{s}\gamma_{j}\odot\delta_{y_{j}}\in\left\langle\nu;U_{1},...,U_{n};\varepsilon\right\rangle
. Then

, and

and
\bigoplus\limits_{y_{j}\in U_{i}}|\lambda_{i}-\gamma_{j}|<\varepsilon
. On construction for every
i
and for all
j
,
y_{j}\in U_{i}
, we have

|\lambda_{i}-\gamma_{j}|\leq\bigoplus\limits_{y_{j}\in U_{i}}|\lambda_{i}-\gamma_{j}|<\varepsilon=\min\left\{-\lambda_{i}-ln2:\ i=1,\ ...,\ k,i\neq i_{0}\right\}\leq-\lambda_{i}-ln2,

i. e.
|\lambda_{i}-\gamma_{j}|<-\lambda_{i}-ln2.
From where we have

\gamma_{j}<-ln2
. Hence,
\left\langle\nu;U_{1},...,U_{n};\varepsilon\right\rangle\subset O_{\delta_{x}}
. Thus, the set
O_{\delta_{x}}
is open.

It is obvious that
I_{f}(X)\subset\bigcup\limits_{x\in X}O_{\delta_{x}}
. If
\nu\in\bigcup\limits_{x\in X}O_{\delta_{x}}

\nu\in O_{\delta_{x}}

r(\nu)=

i\neq i_{0}\end{subarray}}^{n}(-ln(n+1))\odot\delta_{x_{i}},&\text{if $\lambda_{i}\geq-ln(n+1)$ for some $i\in\{1,...,n\}\setminus\{i_{0}\}$},\\

\nu=\bigoplus\limits_{i=1}^{n}\lambda_{i}\odot\delta_{x_{i}}\in O_{\delta_{x}}

Theorem 3.

Let
X
be an

-compact. Then
I_{f}(X)

-compact.

Proof.
Let
X
be neighbourhood retract some compact

,

be open set in
Y
such that
U\supset X
and there is a retraction
r:U\rightarrow X
.

Consider open set
\bigcup\limits_{x\in U}O\delta_{x}
in
I_{f}(Y)

I_{f}(X)\subset\bigcup\limits_{x\in U}O\delta_{x}
, and if
\nu\in\bigcup\limits_{x\in X}O_{\delta_{x}}

x\in X

\nu\in O_{\delta_{x}}

\nu\in\bigcup\limits_{x\in U}O\delta_{x}\subset I_{f}(Y)

\nu=\bigoplus\limits_{i=1}^{n}\lambda_{i}\odot\delta_{y_{i}}
