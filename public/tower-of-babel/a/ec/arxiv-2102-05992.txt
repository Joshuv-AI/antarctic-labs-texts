The classification of Kleinian groups of Hausdorff dimensions at most one and Burnside’s conjecture
Yong Hou

Abstract
In this paper we provide the complete classification of convex cocompact Kleinian group of Hausdorff dimensions less than 1 . 1. In particular, we prove that every convex cocompact Kleinian group of Hausdorff dimension < 1 <1 is a classical Schottky group. This upper bound is sharp. The result implies that the converse of Burside’s conjecture [ 4 ] is true: All non-classical Schottky groups must have Hausdorff dimension ≥ 1 \geq 1 . The upper bounds of Hausdorff dimensions of classical Schottky groups has long been established by Phillips-Sarnak [ 15 ] and Doyle [ 7 ] . The prove of the theorem relies on the result of Hou [ 10 ] .

Proof of Theorem
1.1

Proof.

(Theorem
1.1
)

First note that by Selberg Lemma we can just assume Kleinian group to be torsion-free.

Now note that if a Kleinian group
\Gamma
of
\mathfrak{D}_{\Gamma}<1
then it must be free. To show this, assume otherwise. Since
\mathfrak{D}_{\Gamma}<1
and
\Gamma
is purely loxodromic, it is convex-cocompact of second-kind. There exists an imbedded surface

\mathscr{R}=\Omega_{\Gamma}/\Gamma
in
\mathbb{H}^{3}/\Gamma.
If
\mathscr{R}
is incompressible then, subgroup
\pi_{1}(\mathscr{R})\subset\Gamma
have
\mathfrak{D}_{\pi_{1}(\mathscr{R})}=1

which is contradiction. If
\mathscr{R}
is compressible then, we can cut along compression disks. We either end with incompressible surface as before or after finitely many steps of cutting we obtain topological ball, which implies
\mathbb{H}^{3}/\Gamma
is handle-body, hence free.

Let
\Gamma\in\mathfrak{J}^{1}_{g}
be a non-classical Schottky group with
\mathfrak{D}_{\Gamma}<1.
Let
\{\Gamma_{n}\}\subset\mathfrak{H}
, and
\Gamma_{n}\to\Gamma.
Since
\Gamma
is Schottky group, it follows from Lemma
3.9
, that there exists an open set
\mathscr{O}(\eta_{\Gamma})

such that every element is quasi-circle of
\Gamma
. Since
\Gamma
is a non-classical Schottky group, hence by Lemma
3.11
, we must have every open set about

\eta_{\Gamma}
contains some non-quasi-circle, in-particular we must have
\mathscr{O}(\eta_{\Gamma})
contain a non-quasi-circle. Hence we must have
\Gamma
is a classical Schottky group. This implies
\mathfrak{J}_{g,o}\cap\mathfrak{J}_{g}^{1}
is both open and closed in
\mathfrak{J}_{g}^{1}
i.e. connected component of

\mathfrak{J}_{g}^{1}.
Now it follows from Proposition
2.12
,
\mathfrak{J}_{g,o}\cap\mathfrak{J}_{g}^{1}=\mathfrak{J}_{g}^{1}.

Finally, sharpness comes from the fact that, there exists Kleinian groups which is not free of Hausdorff dimension equal to one. Hence we have our result.
