α \alpha -induction for bi-unitary connections
Yasuyuki Kawahigashi

Abstract
The tensor functor called α \alpha -induction produces a new unitary fusion category from a Frobenius algebra object, or a Q Q -system, in a braided unitary fusion category. In the operator algebraic language, it gives extensions of endomorphism of N N to M M arising from a subfactor N ⊂ M N\subset M of finite index and finite depth which gives a braided fusion category of endomorphisms of N N . It is also understood in terms of Ocneanu’s graphical calculus. We study this α \alpha -induction for bi-unitary connections, which give a characterization of finite-dimensional nondegenerate commuting squares and gives certain 4 4 -tensors appearing in recent studies of 2 2 -dimensional topological order. We show that the resulting α \alpha -induced bi-unitary connections are flat if we start with a commutative Frobenius algebra, or a local Q Q -system. Examples related to chiral conformal field theory and the Dynkin diagrams are presented.
