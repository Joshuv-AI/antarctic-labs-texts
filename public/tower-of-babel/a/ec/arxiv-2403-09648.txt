Stochastic Processes and Mean Square Calculus on Fractal Curves
Alireza Khalili Golmankhaneh, Kerri Welch, Cristina Serpa Ivanka Stamova

Abstract
In this paper, random and stochastic processes are defined on fractal curves. Fractal calculus is used to define cumulative distribution function, probability density function, moments, variance, and correlation function of stochastic process on fractal curves. A new framework which is a generalization of mean square calculus is formulated. The sequence of random variables on the fractal curve, fractal mean square continuity, mean square F α F^{\alpha} -derivative, and fractal mean square integral. The mean square solution of a fractal stochastic equation is derived and plotted in order to show the details.
