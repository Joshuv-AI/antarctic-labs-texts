Low level definability above large cardinals
Farmer Schlutzenberg

Abstract
We study some connections between definability in generalized descriptive set theory and large cardinals, particularly measurable cardinals and limits thereof, working in ZFC. We show that if κ \kappa is a limit of measurable cardinals then there is no Σ 1 ​ ( ℋ κ ∪ OR ) \Sigma_{1}(\mathcal{H}_{\kappa}\cup\mathrm{OR}) wellorder of a subset of 𝒫 ⁡ ( κ ) \mathcal{P}(\kappa) of length ≥ κ + \geq\kappa^{+} ; this answers a question of Lücke and Müller. However, in M 1 M_{1} , the minimal proper class mouse with a Woodin cardinal, for every uncountable cardinal κ \kappa which is not a limit of measurables, there is a Σ 1 ​ ( ℋ κ ∪ { κ } ) \Sigma_{1}(\mathcal{H}_{\kappa}\cup\{\kappa\}) good wellorder of ℋ κ + \mathcal{H}_{\kappa^{+}} . If κ \kappa is a limit of measurables then there is no Σ 1 ​ ( ℋ κ ∪ OR ) \Sigma_{1}(\mathcal{H}_{\kappa}\cup\mathrm{OR}) mad family ℱ ⊆ 𝒫 ⁡ ( κ ) \mathscr{F}\subseteq\mathcal{P}(\kappa) of cardinality > κ >\kappa , and if also cof ⁡ ( κ ) > ω \cof(\kappa)>\omega then there is no Σ 1 ​ ( ℋ κ ∪ OR ) \Sigma_{1}(\mathcal{H}_{\kappa}\cup\mathrm{OR}) almost disjoint family ℱ ⊆ 𝒫 ⁡ ( κ ) \mathscr{F}\subseteq\mathcal{P}(\kappa) of cardinality > κ >\kappa . However, relative to the consistency of large cardinals, Π 1 ​ ( { κ } ) \Pi_{1}(\{\kappa\}) mad families and maximal independent families ℱ ⊆ 𝒫 ⁡ ( κ ) \mathscr{F}\subseteq\mathcal{P}(\kappa) can exist, when κ \kappa is a limit of measurables, and even more. We also examine some of the features of L ⁡ [ U ] L[U] , and answer another question of Lücke and Müller, showing that if κ \kappa is a weakly compact cardinal such that every Σ 1 ​ ( ℋ κ ∪ { κ } ) \Sigma_{1}(\mathcal{H}_{\kappa}\cup\{\kappa\}) subset of 𝒫 ⁡ ( κ ) \mathcal{P}(\kappa) of cardinality > κ >\kappa contains the range of a perfect function, then there is an inner model satisfying “there is a weakly compact limit of measurable cardinals”.

).
If
\kappa
is the least inaccessible limit of measurables,
then the set

of all limits of measurables

-iterable cardinal is inaccessible

9.2 Theorem
.

Let
\kappa
be an uncountable cardinal. Then:

1.

Suppose

and
\mu
is measurable,
\kappa
is
\mu
-steady and

.
Then
\mathrm{Club}_{\kappa}

(

)
.

\mathrm{Club}_{\kappa}

is not
\Pi_{1}(S_{j})
.

Suppose
\kappa
is an inaccessible limit of measurables.
Then
\mathrm{Club}_{\kappa}
is not
\Pi_{1}(V_{\kappa}\cup\mathrm{OR})
.

Proof.

Part
1
:
Suppose otherwise. Then the set of stationary subsets of
\kappa
is
\Sigma_{1}(S_{j})
,
since given

,

S
is stationary iff

we have that for each

is stationary.

Part

In this section we describe a variant of
[

, Theorem 2.19]
.
The adaptation involves standard forcing techniques; see for example
[
2
]
.

10.1 Theorem
.

Assume ZFC +
\kappa
is
\lambda
-supercompact, where
\lambda>\kappa
is inaccessible
and
2^{\lambda}=\lambda^{+}
. Then in a forcing extension
V[G]
,
\kappa
is
\lambda
-supercompact,
(2^{\kappa})^{V[G]}=\kappa^{+V[G]}=\lambda
and
for every
X\subseteq\mathcal{P}(\kappa)^{V[G]}

such that
X
is definable over
V[G]

from elements of
V\cup\mathcal{P}(\kappa)^{V[G]}
and
X
has cardinality
>\kappa
, we have that

X
has a perfect subset,
and
X
is not a wellorder.

Proof.

Let
\mathbb{P}
be length
\kappa
Easton support iteration where at inaccessible stages
\alpha
,
we force with the Levy collapse
\mathrm{Coll}(\alpha,{<\lambda_{\alpha}})
,
where
\lambda_{\alpha}
is the least inaccessible
>\alpha
, and at other stages
\alpha
the empty forcing.
Let
\mathbb{P}^{+}=\mathbb{P}*\dot{\mathrm{Coll}}(\kappa,{<\lambda})
.
Let
G^{+}
be
(V,\mathbb{P}^{+})
-generic
and
G=G^{+}\!\upharpoonright\!\kappa
(so
G
is
(V,\mathbb{P})
-generic).

Let
j:V\to M
be a
\lambda
-supercompactness embedding.
Note that
\mathbb{P}^{+}\in M

and
G^{+}
is
(M,\mathbb{P}^{+})
-generic.
We have
\lambda=\kappa^{+V[G^{+}]}=\kappa^{+M[G^{+}]}
.
Now
V\models
“
{{}^{\lambda}}M\subseteq M
”,
and considering
\mathbb{P}^{+}
-names in
V

for
\lambda
-sequences in
V[G^{+}]
,
a straightforward calculation gives that
V[G^{+}]\models
“
{{}^{\lambda}}(M[G^{+}])\subseteq M[G^{+}]
”.

Let
H^{+}=j``G^{+}
. So
H^{+}\in M[G^{+}]
.
Working in
M[G^{+}]
,
let
\widetilde{q}
be the canonical
j(\mathbb{P})
-name for the union
of those conditions of form
(j(p)_{j(\kappa)})_{G^{+}*\dot{I}}

for
p\in G^{+}
,
where
\dot{I}
is the canonical
name for the generic filter for the tail forcing
j(\mathbb{P})\!\upharpoonright\!(\kappa+1,j(\kappa))
,
and where
j(p)_{\alpha}

is the
\alpha
th component of
j(p)
.
Note that in
M[G^{+}]
,

\widetilde{q}
is forced (by
j(\mathbb{P})\!\upharpoonright\!(\kappa+1,j(\kappa))
)
to be a
\mathrm{Col}(j(\kappa),{<j(\lambda)})
-name, because the collection of conditions is forced to be pairwise compatible, even closed under witnesses for such compatibility,
and because the collection has size
\lambda
, which is smaller than the closure of the forcing
(which is
j(\kappa)
). So in
M[G^{+}]
,
\widetilde{q}
yields a condition
in
j(\mathbb{P})\!\upharpoonright\!(\kappa+1,j(\kappa))*\mathrm{Coll}(j(\kappa),{<j(\lambda)})
.

Now working in
V[G^{+}]
,
we construct an
M[G^{+}]
-generic
filter

for this tail forcing with
\widetilde{q}\in I
. This is possible
because the forcing in question
has size
j(\lambda)
in

(which is inaccessible in

),
and
\mathrm{card}^{V[G^{+}]}(j(\lambda))=\kappa^{+V[G^{+}]}=\lambda
,
so since
V\models
“
2^{\lambda}=\lambda^{+}
”,
there are only
\kappa^{++V[G^{+}]}=\lambda^{+V[G^{+}]}=\lambda^{+V}
-
many dense subsets to meet,
and the forcing is closed under
\lambda
-sequences in both
V[G^{+}]
and
M[G^{+}]
, and
M[G^{+}]
is closed
under
\lambda
-sequences in
V[G^{+}]
.
(In
V[G^{+}]
, enumerate those dense sets in ordertype
\lambda^{+V[G^{+}]}
,
and meet them in turn.)

So
M[G^{+},I]\subseteq V[G^{+}]
.
But now
j:V\to M

extends to
j^{+}:V[G^{+}]\to M[G^{+},I]

with
j^{+}(G^{+})=G^{+}*I
,
and note that
j^{+}
witnesses that
\kappa

\kappa^{+V[G^{+}]}
-supercompact in

.

It remains to verify the regularity properties of sets
X

V[G^{+}]
from some

and some

\lambda=\kappa^{+V[G^{+}]}

in

The fact that
