§1 Introduction

Let

\mathbb{F}_{2}^{d_{2}}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle v_{2}}}{{\leftarrow}}\mathbb{F}_{2}^{d_{1}}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle v_{1}}}{{\leftarrow}}\mathbb{F}_{2}^{d_{0}}

where
{d_{0}}
,
{d_{1}}
,
{d_{2}}
