Mixing and CP Violation in beauty and charm at LHCb
Laís Soares Lavra on behalf of the LHCb collaboration

Abstract
This document presents eight recent and new analyses from the LHCb experiment covering results on the CKM angle γ \gamma and precision measurements of the charm mixing parameters. In addition, the latest CP violation measurements in charm and beauty decays are reported.

CP violation and mixing in charm

Charm meson oscillations are characterised by the mixing parameters,
x=\Delta{M}/{\Gamma}
and

y=\Delta{\Gamma}/{(2\Gamma)}

\Delta{M}

\Delta{\Gamma}

\left\lvert D_{1,2}\right\rangle=p\left\lvert D^{0}\right\rangle\pm q\left\lvert\bar{D}^{0}\right\rangle
, and
\Gamma
is the average decay width.
CP
violation in the mixing can occur if
|q/p|\neq 1
or in the interference between mixing and decay if
\phi_{f}=\arg(q\bar{{A}}_{f}/p{A}_{f})\neq
0 or
\pi

3

D^{0}(\bar{D}^{0})\rightarrow f

CP

\displaystyle x=(3.98^{+0.56}_{-0.54})\times 10^{-3},

3
CP violation in charmless b-meson decays

Charmless
b
-meson decays provide an interesting environment to observe
CP
violation effects since these decays have contributions
from both penguin and tree-level processes with amplitudes of similar size. In addition, in multi-body decays the different intermediate states can interfere with each other, producing large strong-phase differences, which can lead to enhanced
CP
violation in certain regions of phase space.
