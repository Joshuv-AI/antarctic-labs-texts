Investigating the exceptionality of scattered polynomials
Daniele Bartoli, Giovanni Zini, Ferdinando Zullo

Abstract
Scattered polynomials over a finite field 𝔽 q n \mathbb{F}_{q^{n}} have been introduced by Sheekey in 2016, and a central open problem regards the classification of those that are exceptional . So far, only two families of exceptional scattered polynomials are known. Very recently, Longobardi and Zanella weakened the property of being scattered by introducing the notion of L- q t q^{t} -partially scattered and R- q t q^{t} -partially scattered polynomials, for t t a divisor of n n . Indeed, a polynomial is scattered if and only if it is both L- q t q^{t} -partially scattered and R- q t q^{t} -partially scattered. In this paper, by using techniques from algebraic geometry over finite fields and function fields theory, we show that the property which is is the hardest to be preserved is the L- q t q^{t} -partially scattered one. On the one hand, we are able to extend the classification results of exceptional scattered polynomials to exceptional L- q t q^{t} -partially scattered polynomials. On the other hand, the R- q t q^{t} -partially scattered property seems more stable. We present a large family of R- q t q^{t} -partially scattered polynomials, containing examples of exceptional R- q t q^{t} -partially scattered polynomials, which turn out to be connected with linear sets of so-called pseudoregulus type . In order to detect new examples of polynomials which are R- q t q^{t} -partially scattered, we introduce two different notions of equivalence preserving this property and concerning natural actions of the groups Γ ​ L ​ ( 2 , q n ) {\rm\Gamma L}(2,q^{n}) and Γ ​ L ​ ( 2 ​ n / t , q t ) {\rm\Gamma L}(2n/t,q^{t}) . In particular, our family contains many examples of inequivalent polynomials, and geometric arguments are used to determine the equivalence classes under the action of Γ ​ L ​ ( 2 ​ n / t , q t ) {\rm\Gamma L}(2n/t,q^{t}) .



Keywords:
Linearized polynomial, scattered polynomial, exceptionality, equivalence, linear set.

Proof.

A geometric description

A point set
L
of
\Lambda=\mathrm{PG}(V,{\mathbb{F}}_{q^{n}})\allowbreak=\mathrm{PG}(r-1,q^{n})
is said to be an
{\mathbb{F}}_{q}
-linear set
of
\Lambda
of rank
k
if it is defined by the non-zero vectors of a
k
-dimensional
{\mathbb{F}}_{q}
-vector subspace
U
of
V
, i.e.

L=L_{U}:=\{\langle{\bf u}\rangle_{\mathbb{F}_{q^{n}}}:{\bf u}\in U\setminus\{{\bf 0}\}\}.

For any subspace

of
\Lambda
, the
weight
of
S
in
L_{U}
is defined as
w_{L_{U}}(S)=\dim_{\mathbb{F}_{q}}(U\cap Z)
.
We also recall that two linear sets
L_{U}
and
L_{W}
of
\mathrm{PG}(r-1,q^{n})
are said to be
\mathrm{P\Gamma L}
-equivalent
(or simply

) if there is an element
\varphi
in
\mathrm{P\Gamma L}(r,q^{n})
such that

.
An important family of linear set is given by the so called

, that is linear sets defined by scattered subspaces.
Clearly, the rank of a scattered linear set is bounded above by
\frac{rn}{2}
.
For further details on linear sets see
[
22
,
34
]
.

In
[
26
]
, generalizing
[

,
21
,
30
]
, a class of scattered linear sets of maximum rank in
\mathrm{PG}(t^{\prime}-1,q^{t})
was presented.
Let
L_{U}
be a scattered
\mathbb{F}_{q}
-linear set of
\Lambda=\mathrm{PG}(2t^{\prime}-1,q^{t})
of rank

, with

.
We say that

is of

if

•

there exist

pairwise disjoint lines

of

for every

•

there exist exactly two

and

disjoint from

and such that

See

We start by defining a natural equivalence between two linearized polynomials.
Let

{\mathbb{F}}_{q}
-linearized polynomials over
\mathbb{F}_{q^{n}}

\mathbb{F}_{q^{n}}\times\mathbb{F}_{q^{n}}

We say that

and

are

if there exists
\varphi\in\mathrm{\Gamma L}(2,q^{n})
such that

and
\sigma\in\mathrm{Aut}(\mathbb{F}_{q^{n}})

[
5
, Section 1]

[

-partially scattered.

.

and

-linearized polynomials of

-partially scattered, then

-partially scattered.

implies that

one gets
b^{q^{t}}=b
. Together with
a\in\mathbb{F}_{q^{\gcd(t,|k-s|)}}^{*}
,
c=0
, and
d=a^{q^{s}}
, this is enough to satisfy the polynomial identity (

).
∎

Corollary 7.8
.

Let

2s<t

and if

In particular,

).

Proof.

Since

Corollary 7.9
.

Let

be an odd prime power. Let

be such that either
k=1

, or

\gcd(k,2t)=1

Then

\mathcal{G}(x^{q^{t-k}}+x^{q^{2t-k}}+x^{q^{k}}-x^{q^{t+k}})=\left\{\left(\begin{array}[]{cc}a&0\\
0&a^{q}\end{array}\right)\colon a\in{\mathbb{F}}_{q^{2}}^{*}\right\},

if
t
is even,
and

\mathcal{G}(x^{q^{t-k}}+x^{q^{2t-k}}+x^{q^{k}}-x^{q^{t+k}})=\left\{\left(\begin{array}[]{cc}a&b\\
-4b&a\end{array}\right)\colon a\in{\mathbb{F}}_{q},b^{q}+b=0,a^{2}+4b^{2}\neq 0\right\},

if
t
is odd.

Proof.

The polynomial identity (
12
) reads

cx+d(x^{q^{t-k}}+x^{q^{2t-k}}+x^{q^{k}}-x^{q^{t+k}})=a^{q^{t-k}}x^{q^{t-k}}+b^{q^{t-k}}(x^{q^{2t-2k}}+x^{q^{t-2k}}+x^{q^{t}}-x)

+a^{q^{2t-k}}x^{q^{2t-k}}+b^{q^{2t-k}}(x^{q^{t-2k}}+x^{q^{2t-2k}}+x-x^{q^{t}})+a^{q^{k}}x^{q^{k}}+b^{q^{k}}(x^{q^{t}}+x+x^{q^{2k}}-x^{q^{t+2k}})

-a^{q^{t+k}}x^{q^{t+k}}-b^{q^{t+k}}(x+x^{q^{t}}+x^{q^{t+2k}}-x^{q^{2k}}).

By our assumptions on
t
and
k

\mathcal{T}=\{0,t-k,2t-k,k,t+k,2t-2k,t-2k,t,2k,t+2k\}\subseteq\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}

has size

.
So,
a,b,c,d

\left\{\begin{array}[]{lllllllllll}c=-b^{q^{t-k}}+b^{q^{2t-k}}+b^{q^{k}}-b^{q^{t+k}},\\
d=a^{q^{t-k}},\\
d=a^{q^{2t-k}},\\
d=a^{q^{k}},\\
d=a^{q^{t+k}},\\
b^{q^{t-k}}+b^{q^{2t-k}}=0,\\
b^{q^{t-k}}-b^{q^{2t-k}}+b^{q^{k}}-b^{q^{t+k}}=0,\\
\end{array}\right.

(13)

By the last equation we have
b^{q^{t}}+b=0
, which replaced in the seventh equation of (

) implies
b^{q^{t+2k}}+b=0
, that is
b^{q^{2k}}=b
.
Since

If

When

8
A weaker equivalence

In this section we present a weaker definition of equivalence between two linearized polynomials of
{\mathbb{F}}_{q^{n}}[x]
which preserves the property of being R-
q^{t}
-partially scattered.
Let
f(x)
and
g(x)
be two
{\mathbb{F}}_{q}
-linearized polynomials in
{\mathbb{F}}_{q^{n}}[x]
.
We say that
f(x)
and
g(x)
are
weakly equivalent
if there exists
\varphi\in\mathrm{\Gamma L}(2t^{\prime},q^{t})
such that
U_{f}^{\varphi}=U_{g}
.

Clearly
\Gamma\mathrm{L}(2,q^{n})
is a subgroup of
\Gamma\mathrm{L}(2t^{\prime},q^{t})
(as
\Gamma\mathrm{L}(2,q^{n})
is the stabiliser of a Desarsguesian spread of
V(2t^{\prime},q^{t})
in
\Gamma\mathrm{L}(2t^{\prime},q^{t})
), and hence two equivalent
{\mathbb{F}}_{q}
-linearized polynomials are also weakly equivalent.

Proposition 8.1
.

Let
f(x)
and
g(x)
be two weakly equivalent
{\mathbb{F}}_{q}
-linearized polynomials in
{\mathbb{F}}_{q^{n}}[x]
.
If
f(x)
is R-
q^{t}
-partially scattered, then also
g(x)
is R-
q^{t}
-partially scattered.

Proof.

By hypothesis
U_{f}
and
U_{g}
are
\Gamma\mathrm{L}(2t^{\prime},q^{t})
-equivalent and
U_{f}
is a scattered
{\mathbb{F}}_{q}
-subspace of
V(2t^{\prime},q^{t})
, so that also
U_{g}
is a scattered
{\mathbb{F}}_{q}
-subspace of
V(2t^{\prime},q^{t})
and hence
g(x)
is R-
q^{t}
-partially scattered because of Theorem
2.2
.
∎

The two notions of equivalence do not coincide.
To show this, we make use of the following result.

Theorem 8.2
.

Let
n=tt^{\prime}
, for some
t,t^{\prime}\in\mathbb{N}
and let
s,s^{\prime}\in\mathbb{N}
be such that
\gcd(s,t)=\gcd(s^{\prime},t)=1
. Let

f(x)=\sum_{i=0}^{t^{\prime}-1}a_{i}x^{q^{it+s}}
and
g(x)=\sum_{i=0}^{t^{\prime}-1}b_{i}x^{q^{it+s^{\prime}}}
two R-
q^{t}
-partially scattered polynomials.
Then
f(x)
and
g(x)
are weakly equivalent if and only if
s\equiv\pm s^{\prime}\pmod{t}
.

Proof.

If
f(x)
and
g(x)
are weakly equivalent then the
{\mathbb{F}}_{q}
-linear sets
L_{U_{f}}
and
L_{U_{g}}
are
\mathrm{P\Gamma L}(t^{\prime},q^{t})
-equivalent.
By Theorem
6.1
,
L_{U_{f}}
and
L_{U_{g}}
are of pseudoregulus type and
[
26
, Theorem 3.7]
implies that the companion automorphisms
\sigma_{f}
and
\sigma_{g}
of
f(x)
and
g(x)
, respectively, are such that
\sigma_{f}=\sigma_{g}^{\pm 1}
, that is
s\equiv\pm s^{\prime}\pmod{t}
.
Assume now that
s\equiv s^{\prime}\pmod{t}
.
Consider the
{\mathbb{F}}_{q^{t}}
-linear map
F
of
{\mathbb{F}}_{q^{n}}\times{\mathbb{F}}_{q^{n}}
defined by

F(x,y)=(x,g(f^{-1}(y))),

for every
x,y\in\mathbb{F}_{q^{n}}
.
Clearly,
F(U_{f})=U_{g}
.
If
s\equiv-s^{\prime}\pmod{t}
, then consider the
{\mathbb{F}}_{q^{t}}
-semilinear map
F
of
{\mathbb{F}}_{q^{n}}\times{\mathbb{F}}_{q^{n}}
defined by

F(x,y)=(f^{-1}(y),g(x)),

for every
x,y\in\mathbb{F}_{q^{n}}
.
Again,
F(U_{f})=U_{g}
.
∎

9
Open problems

We conclude the papers by pointing out some open problems.

•

In Proposition
3.4
we characterize LP-polynomials which are L-
q^{t}
-partially scattered or R-
q^{t}
-partially scattered when
n
is odd. The techniques developed in
[
39
]
may be useful to extend this characterization when
n
is even.

•

It would be interesting to find non-monomial exceptional L-
q^{t}
-partially scattered polynomials
f(x)
which are not exceptional scattered.
As already noted in Section
4
, this may be done through the investigation of the structure of the Galois group of
f(x)-sx^{q^{\ell}}
.

•

Regarding the family of polynomials introduced in Section
5
, the weak equivalence issue has been completely solved in Section
8
, whereas less is known when considering the equivalence defined in Section
7
. Therefore it would be of interest to determine the equivalence classes of the family in Section
5
under the latter equivalence.

•

Proposition
2.3
provides a practical machinery to construct families of R-
q^{t}
-partially scattered polynomials. Indeed, we use such a proposition to construct the family in Section
5
by means of the R-
q^{t}
-partially scattered polynomials of monomial type. Other examples of R-
q^{t}
-partially scattered polynomials could arise from non-monomial ones.
