On the difference of initial logarithmic coefficients for the class of univalent functions
Milutin Obradović, Nikola Tuneski

Abstract
. In this paper we give estimates of the differences | γ 3 | − | γ 2 | |\gamma_{3}|-|\gamma_{2}| and | γ 4 | − | γ 3 | |\gamma_{4}|-|\gamma_{3}| for the class of functions f f univalent in the unit disc and normalized by f ⁡ ( 0 ) = f ′ ​ ( 0 ) − 1 = 0 f(0)=f^{\prime}(0)-1=0 . Here, γ 2 \gamma_{2} , γ 3 \gamma_{3} and γ 4 \gamma_{4} are the initial logarithmic coefficients of the function f f .

1.
Introduction and preliminaries

As usual, let
\mathcal{A}
be the class of functions
f
that are analytic in the open unit disc
{\mathbb{D}}=\{z:|z|<1\}
of the form

(1)

f(z)=z+a_{2}z^{2}+a_{3}z^{3}+\cdots,

and let
\mathcal{S}
be the subclass of
\mathcal{A}
consisting of functions that are univalent in
{\mathbb{D}}
.

One of the problems in the theory of univalent functions is finding sharp estimates of logarithmic coefficient,
\gamma_{n}
, of a univalent function
f\in\mathcal{S}
, defined by

(2)

\log\frac{f(z)}{z}=2\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\gamma_{n}z^{n}.

From the relations (
1
) and (
2
), after equating the coefficients we receive the next initial logarithmic coefficients:

(3)

\gamma_{1}=\frac{a_{2}}{2},\quad\gamma_{2}=\frac{1}{2}\left(a_{3}-\frac{1}{2}a_{2}^{2}\right),\quad\text{and}\quad\gamma_{3}=\frac{1}{2}\left(a_{4}-a_{2}a_{3}+\frac{1}{3}a_{2}^{3}\right).

Relatively little exact information is known about the logarithmic coefficients. The natural conjecture
|\gamma_{n}|\leq 1/n
, inspired by the Koebe function (whose logarithmic coefficients are
1/n
) is false even in order of magnitude (see Duren
[
1
, Section 8.1]
), and true only for the class of starlike functions (
[
7
]
). For the class
\mathcal{S}
the sharp estimates of single logarithmic coefficients are known only for
\gamma_{1}
and
\gamma_{2}
, namely,

|\gamma_{1}|\leq 1\quad\mbox{and}\quad|\gamma_{2}|\leq\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{e}=0.635\ldots.

In their papers
[
4
]
and
[
5
]
, the authors gave estimates
|\gamma_{3}|\leq 0.5566178\ldots
and

|\gamma_{4}|\leq 0.51059\ldots
for the class
\mathcal{S}.

In this paper we will give the estimates for the differences
|\gamma_{3}|-|\gamma_{2}|
and
|\gamma_{4}|-|\gamma_{3}|
in the class
\mathcal{S}
. The sharp estimates of
|\gamma_{2}|-|\gamma_{1}|
were established in
[
3
]
, with a simple proof given in
[
6
]
using different technique.

In this paper our main tool will be a method based on Grunsky’s coefficients, used previously in
[
4
]
and
[
5
]
. This method is different than the method used in
[
3
]
, or in
[
6
]
.

We will use mainly the notations and results given in the book of N.A. Lebedev (
[
2
]
).

Let
f\in\mathcal{S}
and let

\log\frac{f(t)-f(z)}{t-z}=\sum_{p,q=0}^{\infty}\omega_{p,q}t^{p}z^{q},

where
\omega_{p,q}
are so-called Grunsky’s coefficients with property
\omega_{p,q}=\omega_{q,p}
.
For those coefficients we have the next Grunsky’s inequality (
[
1
,
2
]
):

(4)

\sum_{q=1}^{\infty}q\left|\sum_{p=1}^{\infty}\omega_{p,q}x_{p}\right|^{2}\leq\sum_{p=1}^{\infty}\frac{|x_{p}|^{2}}{p},

where
x_{p}
are arbitrary complex numbers such that last series converges.

Further, it is well-known that if function

(5)

f(z)=z+a_{2}z^{2}+a_{3}z^{3}+\cdots

belongs to
\mathcal{S}
, then also

f_{2}(z)=\sqrt{f(z^{2})}=z+c_{3}z^{3}+c_{5}z^{5}+\cdots

belongs to the class
\mathcal{S}
. For the function
f_{2}
we have the appropriate Grunsky’s
coefficients of the form
\omega_{2p-1,2q-1}
, and the inequality (
4
) has the form

(6)

\sum_{q=1}^{\infty}(2q-1)\left|\sum_{p=1}^{\infty}\omega_{2p-1,2q-1}x_{2p-1}\right|^{2}\leq\sum_{p=1}^{\infty}\frac{|x_{2p-1}|^{2}}{2p-1}.

As it has been shown in
[
2
, p.57]
, if
f
is given by (
1
) then the coefficients
a_{2}
,
a_{3}
,
a_{4}
and
a_{5}

are expressed by Grunsky’s coefficients
\omega_{2p-1,2q-1}
of the function
f_{2}
given by
(
5
) in the following way:

(7)

\begin{split}a_{2}&=2\omega_{11},\\
a_{3}&=2\omega_{13}+3\omega_{11}^{2},\\
a_{4}&=2\omega_{33}+8\omega_{11}\omega_{13}+\frac{10}{3}\omega_{11}^{3},\\
a_{5}&=2\omega_{35}+8\omega_{11}\omega_{33}+5\omega_{13}^{2}+18\omega_{11}^{2}\omega_{13}+\frac{7}{3}\omega_{11}^{4},\\
0&=3\omega_{15}-3\omega_{11}\omega_{13}+\omega_{11}^{3}-3\omega_{33},\\
0&=\omega_{17}-\omega_{35}-\omega_{11}\omega_{33}-\omega_{13}^{2}+\frac{1}{3}\omega_{11}^{4}.\end{split}

We note that in the book of Lebedev
[
2
]
there exists a typing mistake for the coefficient
a_{5}
. Namely, instead of the term
5\omega_{13}^{2}
, there is
5\omega_{15}^{2}
.

2.
Maine results

Theorem 1
.

Let
\gamma_{2}
,
\gamma_{3}
and
\gamma_{4}
be the logarithmic coefficients of function
f\in\mathcal{S}
. Then

|\gamma_{3}|-|\gamma_{2}|\leq\frac{1}{\sqrt{5}}\quad\text{and}\quad|\gamma_{4}|-|\gamma_{3}|\leq\frac{1}{\sqrt{7}}.

Proof.

For our consideration we need the following facts.
From (
6
), choosing
x_{2p-1}=0
when
p=3,4,\ldots
, we have

\begin{split}&|\omega_{11}x_{1}+\omega_{31}x_{3}|^{2}+3|\omega_{13}x_{1}+\omega_{33}x_{3}|^{2}+5|\omega_{15}x_{1}+\omega_{35}x_{3}|^{2}\\
+&7|\omega_{17}x_{1}+\omega_{37}x_{3}|^{2}\leq|x_{1}|^{2}+\frac{|x_{3}|^{2}}{3}.\end{split}

If additionally,
x_{1}=1
and
x_{3}=0
, then

|\omega_{11}|^{2}+3|\omega_{13}|^{2}+5|\omega_{15}|^{2}+7|\omega_{17}|^{2}\leq 1,

and from here also

|\omega_{11}|^{2}+3|\omega_{13}|^{2}+5|\omega_{15}|^{2}\leq 1,

|\omega_{11}|^{2}+3|\omega_{13}|^{2}\leq 1,

and

|\omega_{11}|^{2}\leq 1.

From the previous inequalities we have

(8)

\begin{split}|\omega_{11}|&\leq 1,\\
|\omega_{13}|&\leq\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\sqrt{1-|\omega_{11}|^{2}},\\
|\omega_{15}|&\leq\frac{1}{\sqrt{5}}\sqrt{1-|\omega_{11}|^{2}-3|\omega_{13}|^{2}},\\
|\omega_{17}|&\leq\frac{1}{\sqrt{7}}\sqrt{1-|\omega_{11}|^{2}-3|\omega_{13}|^{2}-5|\omega_{15}|^{2}}.\end{split}

Also, from the fifth relation in (
7
) we obtain

(9)

\omega_{33}=\omega_{15}-\omega_{11}\omega_{13}+\frac{1}{3}\omega_{11}^{3}.

Now, for the first estimate of the theorem, using (
3
) and (
7
), we have

(10)

\begin{split}|\gamma_{3}|-|\gamma_{2}|&=\frac{1}{2}\left|a_{4}-a_{2}a_{3}+\frac{1}{3}a_{2}^{3}\right|-\frac{1}{2}\left|a_{3}-\frac{1}{2}a_{2}^{2}\right|\\
&=|\omega_{33}+2\omega_{11}\omega_{13}|-\left|\omega_{13}+\frac{1}{2}\omega_{11}^{2}\right|,\end{split}

and after applying
|\omega_{11}|\leq 1
, brings

\begin{split}|\gamma_{3}|-|\gamma_{2}|&\leq|\omega_{33}+2\omega_{11}\omega_{13}|-|\omega_{11}|\cdot\left|\omega_{13}+\frac{1}{2}\omega_{11}^{2}\right|\\
&\leq\left|(\omega_{33}+2\omega_{11}\omega_{13})-(\omega_{11})\left(\omega_{13}+\frac{1}{2}\omega_{11}^{2}\right)\right|\\
&=\left|\omega_{33}+\omega_{11}\omega_{13}-\frac{1}{2}\omega_{11}^{3}\right|.\end{split}

From here, having in mind the relation (
9
) and inequalities (
8
), we receive

(11)

\begin{split}|\gamma_{3}|-|\gamma_{2}|&\leq\left|\omega_{15}-\frac{1}{6}\omega_{11}^{3}\right|\leq|\omega_{15}|+\frac{1}{6}|\omega_{11}|^{3}\\
&\leq\frac{1}{\sqrt{5}}\sqrt{1-|\omega_{11}|^{2}-3|\omega_{13}|^{2}}+\frac{1}{6}|\omega_{11}|^{3}\\
&=:\Phi(|\omega_{11}|,|\omega_{13}|),\end{split}

where

\Phi(u,v)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{5}}\sqrt{1-u^{2}-3v^{2}}+\frac{1}{6}u^{3}

with domain

\Omega=\left\{(u,v):0\leq u\leq 1,\,0\leq v\leq\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\sqrt{1-u^{2}}\right\}.

It remains to find the maximal value of
\Phi
on the domain
\Omega
.

Is easy to verify that the function
\Phi(u,v)
has no interior singular points in the domain
\Omega
(
\Phi^{\prime}_{v}(u,v)=0
, if, and only of,
v=0
).
On the edges:

\begin{split}\Phi(u,0)&=\frac{1}{\sqrt{5}}\sqrt{1-u^{2}}+\frac{1}{6}u^{3}\leq\Phi(0,0)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{5}}=0.44721\ldots,\\
\Phi(0,v)&=\frac{1}{\sqrt{5}}\sqrt{1-3v^{2}}\leq\frac{1}{\sqrt{5}}=0.44721\ldots\\
\Phi\left(u,\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\sqrt{1-u^{2}}\right)&=\frac{1}{6}u^{3}\leq\frac{1}{6}=0.1(6).\end{split}

Using all previous facts and (
11
), we finally conclude that

|\gamma_{3}|-|\gamma_{2}|\leq\frac{1}{\sqrt{5}}.

As for second estimate of this theorem, from (
2
) we receive

\gamma_{4}=\frac{1}{2}\left(a_{5}-a_{2}a_{4}-\frac{1}{2}a_{3}^{2}+a_{2}^{2}a_{3}-\frac{1}{4}a_{2}^{4}\right),

and by using the relations (
7
),

(12)

\gamma_{4}=\frac{1}{2}\left(2\omega_{35}+3\omega_{13}^{2}+4\omega_{11}\omega_{33}+4\omega_{11}^{2}\omega_{13}-\frac{5}{6}\omega_{11}^{4}\right).

Next, from the last two relations in (
7
) we can express
\omega_{33}
and
\omega_{35}
and apply them in
(
12
). After some calculations, we receive

\gamma_{4}=\omega_{17}+\omega_{11}\omega_{15}+\omega_{11}^{2}\omega_{13}+\frac{1}{2}\omega_{13}^{2}+\frac{1}{4}\omega_{11}^{4}.

Now, using the above expression for
\gamma_{4}
, the expression for
\gamma_{3}
obtained within (
10
),
\gamma_{3}=\omega_{33}+2\omega_{11}\omega_{13}
, and the expressions for
\omega_{33}
and
\omega_{35}
from the last two lines from (
7
), having in mind that
|\omega_{11}|\leq 1
, we receive:

(13)

\begin{split}|\gamma_{4}|-|\gamma_{3}|&\leq|\gamma_{4}|-|\omega_{11}||\gamma_{3}|\\
&\leq|\gamma_{4}-\omega_{11}\gamma_{3}|\\
&=\left|\omega_{17}+\frac{1}{2}\omega_{13}^{2}-\frac{1}{12}\omega_{11}^{4}\right|\\
&\leq|\omega_{17}|+\frac{1}{2}|\omega_{13}|^{2}+\frac{1}{12}|\omega_{11}|^{4}\\
&\leq\frac{1}{\sqrt{7}}\sqrt{1-|\omega_{11}|^{2}-3|\omega_{13}|^{2}-5|\omega_{15}|^{2}}+\frac{1}{2}|\omega_{13}|^{2}+\frac{1}{12}|\omega_{11}|^{4}\\
&\leq\frac{1}{\sqrt{7}}\sqrt{1-|\omega_{11}|^{2}-3|\omega_{13}|^{2}}+\frac{1}{2}|\omega_{13}|^{2}+\frac{1}{12}|\omega_{11}|^{4}\\
&=:\Psi(|\omega_{11}|^{2},|\omega_{13}|^{2}),\end{split}

where

\Psi(s,t)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{7}}\sqrt{1-s-3t}+\frac{1}{2}t+\frac{1}{12}s^{2},

0\leq s\leq 1
,
0\leq t\leq\frac{1}{3}(1-s)
.
It is easily verified that the function
\Psi(s,t)
attains its maximum
\frac{1}{\sqrt{7}}
for
s=t=0
.
Finally, from (
13
),

|\gamma_{4}|-|\gamma_{3}|\leq\frac{1}{\sqrt{7}}.

∎

Previous theorem leads to the conjecture for the difference of the moduli of two consecutive logarithmic coefficients of univalent functions.

Conjecture 1
.

If
\gamma_{n}
is logarithmic coefficient of function
f\in\mathcal{S}
, then

|\gamma_{n}|-|\gamma_{n-1}|\leq\frac{1}{\sqrt{2n-1}},\quad n=3,4,\ldots.
