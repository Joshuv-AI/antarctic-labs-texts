Prospect study for measurement of H ​ b ​ b ¯ \mathchar 29000\mathchar 29026\bar{\mathchar 29026} coupling at the LHC and FCC-hh


Abstract
This paper employs the H + b + jets \mathchar 29000\mathchar 8235\mathchar 29026\mathchar 8235\text{jets} signature in proton-proton collisions to explore the structure of the H ​ b ​ b ¯ \mathchar 29000\mathchar 29026\bar{\mathchar 29026} couplings.The focus of the analysis lies in the decay of the Higgs boson into a photon pair, taking into account both reducible and irreducible backgrounds and a realistic simulation of the detector effects. To enhance the discrimination between signal and background, a multivariate analysis is employed to analyse the kinematic variables and optimise the signal-to-background ratio. The results indicate that the H + b + jets \mathchar 29000\mathchar 8235\mathchar 29026\mathchar 8235\text{jets} process can significantly contribute to the precise measurement of CP-even and CP-odd couplings between the bottom quark and the Higgs boson at the LHC and FCC-hh. Finally, a novel asymmetry is introduced for the purpose of probing CP violation within the H ​ b ​ b ¯ \mathchar 29000\mathchar 29026\bar{\mathchar 29026} coupling, formulated exclusively based on lab-frame momenta.
