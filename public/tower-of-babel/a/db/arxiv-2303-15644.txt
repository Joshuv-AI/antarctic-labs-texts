Weighted sum formula for variants of half multiple zeta values Thanks: The third-named author is supported by Waseda University Grants for Special Research Projects (Grant No. 2022C-300).
Hanamichi Kawamura, Takumi Maesaka, Masataka Ono

Abstract
. We prove some weighted sum formulas for half multiple zeta values, half finite multiple zeta values, and half symmetric multiple zeta values. The key point of our proof is Dougall’s identity for the generalized hypergeometric function F 4 5 {}_{5}F_{4} . Similar results for interpolated refined symmetric multiple zeta values and half refined symmetric multiple zeta values are also discussed.

1.
Introduction

For positive integers
k_{1},\ldots,k_{r}
with
k_{r}\geq 2
, multiple zeta value (MZV)
\zeta(k_{1},\ldots,k_{r})
is a real number defined by

\zeta(k_{1},\ldots,k_{r})\coloneqq\lim_{N\rightarrow\infty}\zeta_{\leq N}(k_{1},\ldots,k_{r})\coloneqq\lim_{N\rightarrow\infty}\sum_{0<n_{1}<\cdots<n_{r}\leq N}\frac{1}{n^{k_{1}}_{1}\cdots n^{k_{r}}_{r}}.

For any function
F
on the sets of indices and the empty index
\varnothing
, we set
F(\varnothing)
to be a unit element. A lot of
\mathbb{Q}
-linear relations among MZVs are known. The weighted sum formula, which is first proved by Ohno and Zudilin
[
21
]
and there are many variants (for example,
[
5
]
,
[
7
]
and
[
15
]
), is one of the most fundamental
\mathbb{Q}
-relation among MZVs.

Many variants of multiple zeta values are studied. For an index
{\boldsymbol{k}}=(k_{1},\ldots,k_{r})
with
k_{r}\geq 2
and an indeterminate
t
, Yamamoto
[
26
]
defined the interpolated multiple zeta values (
t
-MZV)
\zeta^{t}({\boldsymbol{k}})
by

\zeta^{t}({\boldsymbol{k}})\coloneqq\sum_{{\boldsymbol{l}}=(l_{1},\ldots,l_{s})\preceq{\boldsymbol{k}}}\zeta({\boldsymbol{l}})t^{r-s}\in\mathcal{Z}[t].

Here,
{\boldsymbol{l}}\preceq{\boldsymbol{k}}
means that
{\boldsymbol{l}}
runs over the all indices of the form
{\boldsymbol{l}}=(k_{1}\square\cdots\square k_{r})
in which each
\square
is filled by the comma , or the plus +, and
\mathcal{Z}
is the
\mathbb{Q}
-algebra generated by all MZVs. Note that
\zeta^{0}({\boldsymbol{k}})
coincides with MZV and
\zeta^{1}({\boldsymbol{k}})
coincides with so called the multiple zeta star value
\zeta^{\star}({\boldsymbol{k}})
. Yamamoto proved the sum formula for
t
-MZVs in
[
26
]
, and Li proved a kind of weighted sum formula for
t
-MZVs in
[
17
]
. In this paper, we prove a “
2^{k_{r}}
-type” weighted sum formula for
1/2
-MZVs, that is,
t
-MZVs with
t=1/2
. For positive integers
i\leq r<k
, let
I_{i}(k,r)
be the set of indices
{\boldsymbol{k}}=(k_{1},\ldots,k_{r})
of
{\mathrm{wt}}({\boldsymbol{k}})=k,{\mathrm{dep}}({\boldsymbol{k}})=r
and
k_{i}\geq 2
, where
{\mathrm{wt}}({\boldsymbol{k}})
means the sum of entries and
{\mathrm{dep}}({\boldsymbol{k}})
is the number of entries.

Theorem 1.1
.

For positive integers
r<k
, we have

\sum_{{\boldsymbol{k}}\in I_{r}(k,r)}2^{k_{r}}\zeta^{\frac{1}{2}}({\boldsymbol{k}})=\frac{1}{2^{r-2}}\biggl\{2^{k-1}-1+\sum_{n=0}^{r-1}\binom{k-1}{n}\biggr\}\zeta(k).

Recently, Kaneko and Zagier defined kinds of variants of MZV, that is, the finite multiple zeta values (FMZVs) and the symmetric multiple zeta values (SMZVs).

Definition 1.2
.

We set

\mathcal{A}\coloneqq\left.\biggl(\prod_{\text{$p$ : prime}}\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}\biggr)\right/\biggl(\bigoplus_{\text{$p$ : prime}}\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}\biggr).

Note that since
\mathbb{Q}
can be embedded in
\mathcal{A}
diagonally,
\mathcal{A}
becomes a commutative
\mathbb{Q}
-algebra.

Definition 1.3
.

For an index
{\boldsymbol{k}}=(k_{1},\ldots,k_{r})
, we define a finite multiple zeta value
\zeta_{\mathcal{A}}({\boldsymbol{k}})
by

\zeta_{\mathcal{A}}({\boldsymbol{k}})\coloneqq(\zeta_{\leq p-1}({\boldsymbol{k}})\bmod{p})_{p}\in\mathcal{A}.

Definition 1.4
.

For positive integers
k_{1},\ldots,k_{r}
and
\bullet\in\{\ast,\mathbin{\mathcyr{sh}}\}
, set

\zeta^{\bullet}_{\mathcal{S}}(k_{1},\ldots,k_{r})\coloneqq\sum_{i=0}^{r}(-1)^{k_{i+1}+\cdots+k_{r}}\zeta^{\bullet}(k_{1},\ldots,k_{i};T)\zeta^{\bullet}(k_{r},\ldots,k_{i+1};T).

Here,
\zeta^{\bullet}({\boldsymbol{k}};T)\in\mathcal{Z}[T]
is the
\bullet
-regularized MZVs defined by Ihara, Kaneko and Zagier in
[
13
]
. For the detailed definition, see Section 2. It is known that
\zeta^{\bullet}_{\mathcal{S}}({\boldsymbol{k}})
is independent of the variable
T
and
\zeta^{\ast}_{\mathcal{S}}({\boldsymbol{k}})\equiv\zeta^{\mathbin{\mathcyr{sh}}}_{\mathcal{S}}({\boldsymbol{k}})\bmod{\zeta(2)\mathcal{Z}}
(for example, see
[
14
, Proposition 3.2.4]
or
[
22
, Proposition 2.1]
). We define symmetric multiple zeta value
\zeta_{\mathcal{S}}({\boldsymbol{k}})
as an element in
\overline{\mathcal{Z}}\coloneqq\mathcal{Z}/\zeta(2)\mathcal{Z}
by

\zeta_{\mathcal{S}}({\boldsymbol{k}})\coloneqq\zeta^{\bullet}_{\mathcal{S}}({\boldsymbol{k}})\bmod{\zeta(2)\mathcal{Z}}.

Seki
[
25
]
, Murahara and the third-named author
[
20
]
introduced the interpolated FMZVs and SMZVs. For an index
{\boldsymbol{k}}=(k_{1},\ldots,k_{r})
, an indeterminate

\zeta^{t}_{\mathcal{F}}({\boldsymbol{k}})\coloneqq\sum_{{\boldsymbol{l}}\preceq{\boldsymbol{k}}}\zeta_{\mathcal{F}}({\boldsymbol{l}})t^{r-{\mathrm{dep}}({\boldsymbol{l}})}.

The sum formula for FMZVs and SMZVs was conjectured by Kaneko, and proved by Saito and Wakabayashi for FMZVs

In this paper, we also prove a kind of weighted sum formula for

-FMZVs and
1/2
-SMZVs. Moreover, combining the sum formula for

-MZV and

-type”weighted sum formula of

For a positive integer
k\geq 2
, set

(1.1)

\mathfrak{Z}_{\mathcal{F}}(k)\coloneqq\begin{cases}(\mathfrak{Z}_{p}(k)\bmod{p})_{p}\coloneqq\biggl(\displaystyle\frac{B_{p-k}}{k}\bmod{p}\biggr)_{p}\in\mathcal{A}&\text{if $\mathcal{F}=\mathcal{A}$},\\
\zeta(k)\bmod{\zeta(2)}\in\overline{\mathcal{Z}}&\text{if $\mathcal{F}=\mathcal{S}$}.\end{cases}

Here,

Theorem 1.5
.

For positive integers

(1.2)

\sum_{{\boldsymbol{k}}\in I_{i}(k,r)}(2^{k_{i}-2}-1)\zeta^{\frac{1}{2}}_{\mathcal{F}}({\boldsymbol{k}})=\frac{(-1)^{i}\{(-1)^{r}-(-1)^{k}\}}{2^{r}}\biggl\{2^{k-1}-\sum_{u=0}^{i-1}\binom{k-1}{u}-\sum_{v=0}^{r-i}\binom{k-1}{v}\biggr\}\mathfrak{Z}_{\mathcal{F}}(k)

(1.3)

\sum_{{\boldsymbol{k}}\in I_{r}(k,r)}2^{k_{r}}\zeta^{\frac{1}{2}}_{\mathcal{F}}({\boldsymbol{k}})=\frac{1+(-1)^{r}}{2^{r-2}}\biggl\{2^{k-1}-1+\sum_{u=0}^{r-1}\binom{k-1}{u}\biggr\}\mathfrak{Z}_{\mathcal{F}}(k)

p

4.7
) is considered by taking
\bmod{p}
. Thus, in
(\mathbb{Z}/p^{2}\mathbb{Z})\llbracket a,b,c\rrbracket
, the equation (
4.7
) is equivalent to

\displaystyle p\sum_{n=1}^{p-1}\frac{2n+a}{n(n+a)}\frac{(b)_{n}(c)_{n}}{(1+a-b)_{n}(1+a-c)_{n}}

\displaystyle=1-\frac{a(a-b-c)}{(a-b)(a-c)}\frac{(1+a)_{p-1}(1+a-b-c)_{p-1}}{(1+a-b)_{p-1}(1+a-c)_{p-1}}

\displaystyle\quad-\frac{bc}{(a-b)(a-c)}\frac{(1+b)_{p-1}(1+c)_{p-1}}{(1+a-b)_{p-1}(1+a-c)_{p-1}}

\displaystyle=-\frac{a(a-b-c)}{(a-b)(a-c)}\biggl\{\frac{(1+a)_{p-1}(1+a-b-c)_{p-1}}{(1+a-b)_{p-1}(1+a-c)_{p-1}}-1\bigg\}

\displaystyle\quad-\frac{bc}{(a-b)(a-c)}\biggl\{\frac{(1+b)_{p-1}(1+c)_{p-1}}{(1+a-b)_{p-1}(1+a-c)_{p-1}}-1\biggr\}.

By applying Lemma
4.3
for the right-hand side of (
4.7
), we have

\displaystyle p\sum_{n=1}^{p-1}\frac{2n+a}{n(n+a)}\frac{(b)_{n}(c)_{n}}{(1+a-b)_{n}(1+a-c)_{n}}

\displaystyle=\frac{p}{(a-b)(a-c)}\biggl\{(a-b-c)\sum_{n=2}^{\infty}\mathfrak{Z}_{p}(n)a^{n}+a\sum_{n=2}^{\infty}\mathfrak{Z}_{p}(n)(a-b-c)^{n}

\displaystyle\quad+b\sum_{n=2}^{\infty}\mathfrak{Z}_{p}(n)c^{n}+c\sum_{n=2}^{\infty}\mathfrak{Z}_{p}(n)b^{n}-(c-a)\sum_{n=2}^{\infty}\mathfrak{Z}_{p}(n)(b-a)^{n}-(b-a)\sum_{n=2}^{\infty}\mathfrak{Z}_{p}(n)(c-a)^{n}\biggr\}.

in
(\mathbb{Z}/p^{2}\mathbb{Z})\llbracket a,b,c\rrbracket
. Since the equality in
\mathbb{F}_{p}\llbracket a,b,c\rrbracket
obtained after dividing by
p
holds for any sufficiently large prime
p
, we obtained the desired formula in
\mathcal{A}\llbracket a,b,c\rrbracket
.
∎

Lemma 4.4
.

In
\mathcal{A}\llbracket A,B,W\rrbracket
, we have

\displaystyle\Bigl(G^{\frac{1}{2}}_{{\mathrm{wtd}},\leq p-1}(A,B,W)\bmod{p}\Bigr)_{p}

\displaystyle=\frac{W^{2}}{2(1+\frac{A}{2})(1+\frac{B}{2})}\biggl\{\frac{A+B}{2}\psi_{\mathcal{A}}(2W)+2\psi_{\mathcal{A}}\Bigl(\frac{A+B}{2}W\Bigr)+\Bigl(1-\frac{A}{2}\Bigr)\psi_{\mathcal{A}}\Bigl(\Bigl(1-\frac{B}{2}\Bigr)W\Bigr)

\displaystyle\qquad+\Bigl(1-\frac{B}{2}\Bigr)\psi_{\mathcal{A}}\Bigl(\Bigl(1-\frac{A}{2}\Bigr)W\Bigr)+\Bigl(1+\frac{A}{2}\Bigr)\psi_{\mathcal{A}}\Bigl(\Bigl(1+\frac{B}{2}\Bigr)W\Bigr)+\Bigl(1+\frac{B}{2}\Bigr)\psi_{\mathcal{A}}\Bigl(\Bigl(1+\frac{A}{2}\Bigr)W\Bigr)\biggr\}.

Proof.

By a similar calculation to that of
G^{\frac{1}{2}}_{{\mathrm{wtd}}}(A,B,W)
in the proof of Proposition
3.3
, we have

G^{\frac{1}{2}}_{{\mathrm{wtd}},\leq N}(A,B,W)=\sum^{N}_{n=1}\frac{W^{3}(n-W)}{n(n-2W)}\frac{(1-(1-\frac{A}{2})W)_{n-1}}{(1-(1+\frac{A}{2})W)_{n}}\frac{(1-(1-\frac{B}{2})W)_{n-1}}{(1-(1+\frac{B}{2})W)_{n}}.

Then, by substituting
N=p-1,a=-2W,b=(1+\frac{A}{2})W,c=(1+\frac{B}{2})W
in (
4.6
) and using
\psi_{\mathcal{A}}(-X)=-\psi_{\mathcal{A}}(X)
, we obtained the desired formula.
∎

Proof of Theorem
1.5
for
\mathcal{F}=\mathcal{A}
.

From (
4.5
) and Lemma
4.4
, by using the definition of
\psi_{\mathcal{A}}(X)
and
\mathfrak{Z}_{\mathcal{A}}(n)=0
for any even positive integer
n
, we have

(4.8)

\displaystyle\begin{split}&\Bigl(F^{\frac{1}{2}}_{{\mathrm{wtd}},\leq p-1}(A,-B,W)\bmod{p}\Bigr)_{p}\\
&=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\mathfrak{Z}_{\mathcal{A}}(2n+1)\sum_{l=0}^{n}\sum_{a=0}^{l}\sum_{b=0}^{n-l-1}\binom{2n+1}{2l+1}\biggl\{\Bigl(\frac{B}{2}\Bigr)^{2a+1}\Bigl(\frac{A}{2}\Bigr)^{2b}+\Bigl(\frac{A}{2}\Bigr)^{2a+1}\Bigl(\frac{B}{2}\Bigr)^{2b}\biggr\}W^{2n+1}.\end{split}

Let
1\leq i\leq r<k
. We compare the coefficients of the both-hand side in
(
4.8
)
at
A^{i-1}B^{r-i}W^{k}
. Since only terms with odd exponent of
W
in the right-hand side of
(
4.8
)
appear, Theorem
1.5
for
\mathcal{F}=\mathcal{A}
is proved if
k
is even.

Let consider the case
k
is odd and set
k=2n+1
. Since the parities of exponent of
A
and
B
in the left-hand side of (
4.9
) are different,
r
must be even. Thus we have

(4.9)

\sum_{{\boldsymbol{k}}\in I_{i}(k,r)}(2^{k_{i}-2}-1)\zeta^{\frac{1}{2}}_{\mathcal{A}}({\boldsymbol{k}})=\begin{cases}\dfrac{1}{2^{r-1}}\mathfrak{Z}_{\mathcal{A}}(k)\displaystyle\sum_{l=i/2}^{(k-r+i-3)/2}\binom{k}{2l+1}&\text{if $i$ is even},\\
-\dfrac{1}{2^{r-1}}\mathfrak{Z}_{\mathcal{A}}(k)\displaystyle\sum_{l=(r-i+1)/2}^{(k-i-2)/2}\binom{k}{2l+1}&\text{if $i$ is odd}.\end{cases}

It is easy to see that the rational multiple in the right-hand side of (
4.9
) coincides with

\frac{(-1)^{i}\{(-1)^{r}-(-1)^{k}\}}{2^{r}}\biggl\{2^{k-1}-\sum_{u=0}^{i-1}\binom{k-1}{u}-\sum_{v=0}^{r-i}\binom{k-1}{v}\biggr\},

we obtain the desired formula.
∎

Remark 4.5
.

Set

(4.10)

\displaystyle\begin{split}F^{t}_{\leq N}(A,B,W)&\coloneqq\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}{\boldsymbol{k}},{\boldsymbol{l}}\in\mathcal{I}\\
a\geq 2\end{subarray}}\zeta^{t}({\boldsymbol{k}},a,{\boldsymbol{l}})A^{{\mathrm{dep}}({\boldsymbol{k}})}B^{{\mathrm{dep}}({\boldsymbol{l}})}W^{{\mathrm{wt}}({\boldsymbol{k}})+{\mathrm{wt}}({\boldsymbol{l}})+a}\\
&=\sum_{1\leq i\leq r<k}\biggl\{\sum_{{\boldsymbol{k}}\in I_{i}(k,r)}\zeta^{t}_{\leq p-1}({\boldsymbol{k}})\biggr\}A^{i-1}B^{r-i}W^{k}.\end{split}

By a similar calculation, we have

(4.11)

F^{t}_{\leq p-1}(A,B,W)\equiv\sum_{n=1}^{p-1}\frac{W^{2}(n-W)}{n}\frac{(1-(1-(1-t)A)W)_{n-1}}{(1-(1-(1-t)B)W)_{n}}\frac{(1-(1+tB)W)_{n-1}}{(1-(1+tA)W)_{n}}\pmod{p}.

On the other hand, from Gauss’ hypergeometric theorem, for a positive integer
N
, we have

\sum_{n=1}^{N}\frac{(a)_{n}}{n(1-b)_{n}}\frac{(-N)_{n}}{n!}=\frac{(1-a-b)_{N}}{(1-a)_{N}},

which is equivalent to the Chu–Vandermonde identity. Therefore, by a similar argument, we have

(4.12)

\Biggl(\sum_{n=1}^{p-1}\frac{(a)_{n}}{n(1-b)_{n}}\bmod{p}\Biggr)_{p}=\frac{1}{b}\bigl\{\psi_{\mathcal{A}}(a+b)-\psi_{\mathcal{A}}(a)-\psi_{\mathcal{A}}(b)\bigr\}.

Thus, if we set
a=-W(1-A),b=-W(1-B)
in (
4.12
), and
t=0
in (
4.10
) and (
4.11
), we obtain the
i
-admissible sum formula for FMZV which was first proved by Saito and Wakabayashi
[
24
]
. Moreover, if we set
a=-W+(1-t)AW,b=W+tAW
in (
4.12
), we obtain the
r
-admissible sum formula for
t
-FMZV which was first proved by Seki
[
25
]
.

5.
Some sum formulas for refined symmetric multiple zeta values

In this section, we prove some sum formulas of the interpolated refined symmetric multiple zeta values (
t
-RSMZVs). The refined symmetric multiple zeta value (RSMZV) was introduced first by Jarossay. In
[
14
]
, he was introduced an object called the
\Lambda
-adjoint multiple zeta values by using the theory of associators, and the refined symmetric multiple zeta values as the coefficients of the
\Lambda
-adjoint multiple zeta values. Independently, Hirose
[
8
]
also defined RSMZV using the notion of iterated integrals.

Definition 5.1

(Interpolated refined SMZV)
.

For an index
{\boldsymbol{k}}
, set

\zeta^{t,\ast}({\boldsymbol{k}};T)\coloneqq\sum_{{\boldsymbol{l}}\preceq{\boldsymbol{k}}}\zeta^{\ast}({\boldsymbol{l}};T)t^{{\mathrm{dep}}({\boldsymbol{k}})-{\mathrm{dep}}({\boldsymbol{l}})}\in\mathcal{Z}[t,T].

We define the interpolated refined symmetric multiple zeta value (
t
-RSMZV)
\zeta^{t}_{\mathcal{RS}}({\boldsymbol{k}})\in\mathcal{Z}[2\pi\sqrt{-1}][t]
by

(5.1)

\zeta^{t}_{\mathcal{RS}}({\boldsymbol{k}})\coloneqq\sum_{i=0}^{{\mathrm{dep}}({\boldsymbol{k}})}(-1)^{{\mathrm{wt}}({\boldsymbol{k}}^{[i]})}\zeta^{t,\ast}\biggl({\boldsymbol{k}}_{[i]};-\frac{\pi\sqrt{-1}}{2}\biggr)\zeta^{t,\ast}\biggl(\overleftarrow{{\boldsymbol{k}}^{[i]}};\frac{\pi\sqrt{-1}}{2}\biggr).

Remark 5.2
.

We define
t
-RSMZV by mimicking the definition of the
\xi
-values defined by Bachmann–Takeyama–Tasaka in
[
2
]
. Hirose mentioned in
[
8
, Remark 13]
that the refined symmetric multiple zeta value coincides with their
\xi
-value. We can give an equivalent definition of
t
-RSMZV by using the iterated integral expression.

Remark 5.3
.

It is easy to see that

\zeta^{t}_{\mathcal{RS}}({\boldsymbol{k}})\equiv\zeta^{t}_{\mathcal{S}}({\boldsymbol{k}})\bmod{2\pi\sqrt{-1}\mathcal{Z}[2\pi\sqrt{-1}][t]}

in
\mathcal{Z}[2\pi\sqrt{-1}][t]/2\pi\sqrt{-1}\mathcal{Z}[2\pi\sqrt{-1}][t]\cong(\mathcal{Z}[2\pi\sqrt{-1}]/2\pi\sqrt{-1}\mathcal{Z}[2\pi\sqrt{-1}])[t]\cong\overline{\mathcal{Z}}[t]
.

Proposition 5.4
.

In
\mathcal{Z}[2\pi\sqrt{-1}][A,B]\llbracket W\rrbracket
, we have

\displaystyle\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}{\boldsymbol{k}},{\boldsymbol{l}}\in\mathcal{I}\\
a\geq 2\end{subarray}}(2^{a-2}-1)\zeta^{\frac{1}{2}}_{\mathcal{RS}}({\boldsymbol{k}},a,{\boldsymbol{l}})A^{{\mathrm{dep}}({\boldsymbol{k}})}B^{{\mathrm{dep}}({\boldsymbol{l}})}W^{{\mathrm{wt}}({\boldsymbol{k}})+a+{\mathrm{wt}}({\boldsymbol{l}})}

\displaystyle=F^{\frac{1}{2}}_{{\mathrm{wtd}}}(A,-B,W)\frac{(2+B)\sin(\pi(1-\frac{B}{2})W)}{(2-B)\sin(\pi(1+\frac{B}{2})W)}\exp(-\pi\sqrt{-1}BW)

\displaystyle\quad+F^{\frac{1}{2}}_{{\mathrm{wtd}}}(B,-A,-W)\frac{(2+A)\sin(\pi(1-\frac{A}{2})W)}{(2-A)\sin(\pi(1+\frac{A}{2})W)}\exp(\pi\sqrt{-1}AW).

Proof.

This proposition is proved in the same way as Lemma
3.1
by using Corollary
2.3
.
∎

For non-negative integers
h\leq l
, set

a_{h,l}\coloneqq\sum_{0\leq i\leq j\leq\lfloor l/2\rfloor}\sum_{u\in\mathbb{Z}}(-1)^{l-j+u}\frac{(2-2^{2i})2^{-2j-h}B_{2i}}{(2i)!(2j-2i+1)!(l-2j)!}\\
\times\binom{2j-2i}{u}\binom{2i}{2j-(l-h)-u}(2\pi\sqrt{-1})^{l},

where we understand that the binomial coefficient
\binom{a}{b}
is zero if
a<b
or
b<0
. Therefore the inner sum is finite and
a_{h,l}
is well-defined.

Theorem 5.5
.

For positive integers
i\leq r<k
, we have

(5.2)

\displaystyle\begin{split}&\sum_{{\boldsymbol{k}}=(k_{1},\ldots,k_{r})\in I_{i}(k,r)}(2^{k_{i}-2}-1)\zeta^{\frac{1}{2}}_{\mathcal{RS}}({\boldsymbol{k}})\\
&=\sum_{0\leq h\leq l\leq k-2}\biggl\{(-1)^{r-i-h}\biggl(2^{k-l-1}-\sum_{u=0}^{i-1}\binom{k-l-1}{u}-\sum_{v=0}^{r-i-h}\binom{k-l-1}{v}\biggl)\\
&\quad+(-1)^{k+i-1-h}\biggl(2^{k-l-1}-\sum_{u=0}^{r-i}\binom{k-l-1}{u}-\sum_{v=0}^{i-1-h}\binom{k-l-1}{v}\biggr)\biggr\}a_{h,l}\frac{\zeta(k-l)}{2^{r-h}}.\end{split}

Proof.

Set

Z_{a,b}(k)\coloneqq\Biggl\{2^{k-1}-\sum_{u=0}^{a}\binom{k-1}{u}-\sum_{v=0}^{b}\binom{k-1}{v}\Biggr\}\frac{\zeta(k)}{2^{a+b+1}}.

Then, by the Taylor expansion of
\sin X,\csc X=1/\sin X
and
\exp X
, we have

\displaystyle\frac{(2+B)\sin(\pi(1-\frac{B}{2})W)}{(2-B)\sin(\pi(1+\frac{B}{2})W)}\exp(-\pi\sqrt{-1}BW)=\sum_{0\leq h\leq k}a_{h,k}B^{h}W^{k}.

Therefore, the coefficient of

F^{\frac{1}{2}}_{{\mathrm{wtd}}}(A,-B,W)\frac{(2+B)\sin(\pi(1-\frac{B}{2})W)}{(2-B)\sin(\pi(1+\frac{B}{2})W)}\exp(-\pi\sqrt{-1}BW)

at
A^{a}B^{b}W^{k}
coincides with

\sum_{0\leq h\leq l\leq k-2}(-1)^{b-h}a_{h,l}Z_{a,b-h}(k-l).

Therefore, the coefficient of the right-hand side of (
5.2
) at
A^{i-1}B^{r-i}W^{k}
coincides with

\sum_{0\leq h\leq l\leq k-2}\bigl\{(-1)^{r-i-h}a_{h,l}Z_{i-1,r-i-h}(k-l)+(-1)^{k+i-1-h}a_{h,l}Z_{r-i,i-1-h}(k-l)\bigr\},

which completes the proof of Proposition
5.4
.
∎

Proposition 5.6
.

In
\mathcal{Z}[2\pi\sqrt{-1}][t,A,B]\llbracket W\rrbracket
, we have

(5.3)

\displaystyle\begin{split}&\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}{\boldsymbol{k}},{\boldsymbol{l}}\in\mathcal{I}\\
a\geq 2\end{subarray}}\zeta^{t}_{\mathcal{RS}}({\boldsymbol{k}},a,{\boldsymbol{l}})A^{{\mathrm{dep}}({\boldsymbol{k}})}B^{{\mathrm{dep}}({\boldsymbol{l}})}W^{{\mathrm{wt}}({\boldsymbol{k}})+a+{\mathrm{wt}}({\boldsymbol{l}})}\\
&=F^{t}(A,-B,W)\frac{(1+tB)\sin(\pi(1-(1-t)B)W)}{(1-(1-t)B)\sin(\pi(1+tB)W)}\exp(-\pi\sqrt{-1}BW)\\
&\quad+F^{t}(B,-A,-W)\frac{(1+tA)\sin(\pi(1-(1-t)A)W)}{(1-(1-t)A)\sin(\pi(1+tA)W)}\exp(\pi\sqrt{-1}AW).\end{split}

Proof.

This proposition is also proved in the same way as Lemma
3.1
by using Corollary
2.3
.
∎

For non-negative integers
h\leq l
, we define
a_{h,l}(t)\in\mathbb{Q}[2\pi\sqrt{-1}][t]
by

a_{h,l}(t)\coloneqq\sum_{0\leq i\leq j\leq\lfloor l/2\rfloor}\sum_{u\in\mathbb{Z}}(-1)^{l-j+u}\frac{(2-2^{2i})B_{2i}}{(2i)!(2j-2i+1)!(l-2j)!}\\
\times\binom{2j-2i}{u}\binom{2i}{2j-(l-h)-u}t^{2j-(l-h)-u}(1-t)^{u}(2\pi\sqrt{-1})^{l}.

As mentioned in Remark
5.2
, Bachmann–Takeyama–Tasaka’s
\xi
-value coincides with the RSMZV. Thus, the following theorem can be regarded as an interpolation of their result
[
3
, Corollary 1.3]
.

Theorem 5.7
.

For positive integers
r<k
, we have

\displaystyle\sum_{{\boldsymbol{k}}\in I_{r}(k,r)}\zeta^{t}_{\mathcal{RS}}({\boldsymbol{k}})

\displaystyle=\sum_{j=0}^{r-1}\binom{k-1}{j}t^{j}(1-t)^{r-1-j}\zeta(k)

\displaystyle\quad+\sum_{0\leq h\leq l\leq k-2}\sum_{j=0}^{r-1-h}(-1)^{r-1-h+k}\binom{k-l-1}{j}(1-t)^{j}t^{r-1-h-j}a_{h,l}(t)\zeta(k-l).

Proof.

By Proposition
5.6
, we have

(5.4)

\displaystyle\begin{split}&\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}{\boldsymbol{k}}\in\mathcal{I}\\
a\geq 2\end{subarray}}\zeta^{t}_{\mathcal{RS}}({\boldsymbol{k}},a)A^{{\mathrm{dep}}({\boldsymbol{k}})}W^{{\mathrm{wt}}({\boldsymbol{k}})+a}\\
&=F^{t}(A,0,W)+F^{t}(0,-A,-W)\frac{(1+tA)\sin(\pi(1-(1-t)A)W)}{(1-(1-t)A)\sin(\pi(1+tA)W)}\exp(\pi\sqrt{-1}AW).\end{split}

We have

(5.5)

\displaystyle\begin{split}F^{t}(A,0,W)&=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{W^{2}}{n(n-(1-(1-t)A)W))}\frac{(1-(1-(1-t)A)W)_{n}}{(1-(1+tA)W)_{n}}\\
&=\sum_{k=2}^{\infty}\sum_{r=0}^{k-2}\sum_{j=0}^{r}\binom{k-1}{j}t^{j}(1-t)^{r-j}\zeta(k)A^{r}W^{k}\end{split}

and

(5.6)

F^{t}(0,-A,-W)=F^{1-t}(-A,0,-W)=\sum_{k=2}^{\infty}\sum_{r=0}^{k-2}(-1)^{r+k}\sum_{j=0}^{r}\binom{k-1}{j}(1-t)^{j}t^{r-j}\zeta(k)A^{r}W^{k}.

By the Taylor expansion of
\sin X,\csc X=1/\sin X
and
\exp X
, we have

(5.7)

\frac{(1+tA)\sin(\pi(1-(1-t)A)W)}{(1-(1-t)A)\sin(\pi(1+tA)W)}\exp(\pi\sqrt{-1}AW)=\sum_{h=0}^{k}(-1)^{k}a_{h,k}(t)A^{h}W^{k}.

Then, from (
5.4
), (
5.5
), (
5.6
) and (
5.7
), we see that the coefficient of the right-hand side of (
5.3
) at
A^{r-1}B^{0}W^{k}
coincides with the sum of the coefficient of

\sum_{i=0}^{k-2}\sum_{s=0}^{k-2-i}(-1)^{s+k-i}\sum_{j=0}^{s}\binom{k-i-1}{j}(1-t)^{j}t^{s-j}\zeta(k-i)A^{s}\cdot\sum_{h=0}^{i}(-1)^{i}a_{h,i}A^{h}

at
A^{r-1}
and

\sum_{j=0}^{r-1}\binom{k-1}{j}t^{j}(1-t)^{r-1-j}\zeta(k).

This completes the proof of Theorem
5.7
.
∎

Combining Theorems
5.5
and
5.7
, we obtain the weighted sum formula for 1/2-RSMZV of the same type as Theorem
1.5
. Let
\delta_{a,b}
denote Kronecker’s delta.

Corollary 5.8
.

For positive integers
r<k
, we have

\displaystyle\sum_{(k_{1},\ldots,k_{r})\in I_{r}(k,r)}2^{k_{r}}\zeta^{\frac{1}{2}}_{\mathcal{RS}}(k_{1},\ldots,k_{r})

\displaystyle=\sum_{0\leq h\leq l\leq k-2}\Biggl\{(-1)^{h}\biggl(2^{k-l-1}-\sum_{j=0}^{r-1}\binom{k-l-1}{j}-\delta_{h,0}\biggr)

\displaystyle\quad\qquad\qquad+(-1)^{k+r-1-h}\biggl(2^{k-l-1}+\sum_{j=0}^{r-1-h}\binom{k-l-1}{j}-1\biggr)+2\sum_{j=0}^{r-1-h}\binom{k-1}{j}\Biggr\}a_{h,l}\frac{\zeta(k-l)}{2^{r-h}}.
