Abstract
We have analyzed the thermodynamic properties of magnetized black-hole in the background of non-linear electrodynamics with two parameters β \beta and γ \gamma . We have studied the Bekenstein-Hawking entropy, Hawking temperature, specific heats in two-dimesional surface plots as a function of event horizon ( r + r_{+} ) and γ \gamma . We showed the variation profiles of the above thermodynamic parameters for γ \gamma [ 0 → 1 0\rightarrow 1 ]. We identified regions of parameters for the possible phase-transitions and the stability of the black-holes.

2
Non-linear Electrodynamics (NLED)-model

The Lagrangian density of NLED model is as (following
[
12
]
):

{\cal L}=-\frac{{\cal F}}{1+(\beta{\cal F})^{\gamma}},

(1)

where the parameter
\beta
has the dimensions of 4D-volume (manifests the strength of the coupling), and
\gamma
(manifests the order of interaction) is the dimensionless parameter
[
12
]
. The
{\cal F}=(1/4)F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu}=(\textbf{B}^{2}-\textbf{E}^{2})/2
, where

F_{\mu\nu}=\partial_{\mu}A_{\nu}-\partial_{\nu}A_{\mu}
is the field strength tensor, with
A_{\mu}=(\phi,\vec{A})
the
\phi
and
\vec{A}
are the scalar and vector potential respectively.

By using the Euler-Lagrange (EL) equation one can obtain the field equations

\partial_{\mu}\left({\cal L}_{\cal F}F^{\mu\nu}\right)=0,

(2)

where
{\cal L}_{\cal F}=\partial{\cal L}/\partial{\cal F}
.

The EL equation on Lagrangian density leads to

{\cal L}_{\cal F}=\frac{(\gamma-1)(\beta{\cal F})^{\gamma}-1}{(1+(\beta{\cal F})^{\gamma})^{2}}.

(3)

To hold the causality principle, the group velocity over the background is less than photon
speed to be
{\cal L}_{\cal F}\leq 0

[
19
]
. This confirms the absence of tachyon.
In our analysis we imply that
0\leq\gamma\leq 1
. We considered only magnetized black holes
(
E
=0,
{\cal F}=\textbf{B}^{2}/2
).

The symmetrical energy-momentum tensor (is obtained from Eqn.
1
) is:

T_{\mu\nu}=\frac{(\gamma-1)(\beta{\cal F})^{\gamma}-1}{[1+(\beta{\cal F})^{\gamma}]^{2}}F_{\mu}^{~\alpha}F_{\nu\alpha}-g_{\mu\nu}{\cal L}.

(4)

The trace of the energy-momentum tensor Eqn.
4
leads to

{\cal T}\equiv T^{\mu}_{\mu}=\frac{4\gamma{\cal F}(\beta{\cal F})^{\gamma}}{[1+(\beta{\cal F})^{\gamma}]^{2}}.

(5)

The trace of Eqn.5 is non-zero due to the presence of dimensional parameter
\beta=1
and scale invariance is broken.

The models exhibits Maxwell’s Electrodynamics at the weak field limit,
\beta{\cal F}\ll 1
,
{\cal L}\rightarrow-{\cal F}
, i.e., holds the correspondence principle.

3
Magnetized black holes

We will study magnetically charged black hole (
\textbf{E}=0
). The action of NLED-GTR is

I=\int d^{4}x\sqrt{-g}\left(\frac{1}{2\kappa^{2}}R+{\cal L}\right),

(6)

where
\kappa^{2}=8\pi G\equiv M_{Pl}^{-2}
,
G
is Newton’s constant,
M_{Pl}
is the reduced Planck mass,
and
R
is the Ricci scalar. By varying action Eqn.
6
with respect to the metric and
electric potential one can obtain the Einstein and electromagnetic field equations

R_{\mu\nu}-\frac{1}{2}g_{\mu\nu}R=-\kappa^{2}T_{\mu\nu},

(7)

\partial_{\mu}\left[\sqrt{-g}{\cal L}_{\cal F}F^{\mu\nu}\right]=0.

(8)

The line element having the spherical symmetry is

ds^{2}=-f(r)dt^{2}+\frac{1}{f(r)}dr^{2}+r^{2}(d\vartheta^{2}+\sin^{2}\vartheta d\phi^{2}).

(9)

The metric function is defined by the relation
[
21
]

f(r)=1-\frac{2GM(r)}{r},

(10)

and the mass function is given by

M(r)=\int_{0}^{r}\rho_{M}(r)r^{2}dr,

(11)

where
\rho_{M}
is the magnetic energy density.

The magnetic energy density (
E
=0) is found from Eq.
4

\rho_{M}=T_{0}^{~0}=\frac{{\cal F}}{1+(\beta{\cal F})^{\gamma}}.

(12)

For the magnetized black hole
[
21
]
the field-strength with magnetic charge(
q
) and at distance
r
is
{\cal F}=q^{2}/(2r^{4})
. For simplicity we set the value of
q=1
and
\beta=1
(which turns out
to be some kind of scale factor) in our analysis.

4
Thermodynamic Parameters

In this section we are summarizing few important thermodynamic parameters which are relevant to our analysis.

\bullet
The Bekenstein-Hawking entropy (area law) is

S_{BH}=\frac{\pi r_{+}^{2}}{G}

(13)

where
r_{+}
is the event horizon and G is the Newton’s Gravitational constant. We set G=1 in our analysis.

\bullet
The Hawking temperature of the black hole horizon is

T_{H}=\frac{\kappa_{S}}{2\pi}=\frac{f^{\prime}(r_{+})}{4\pi},

(14)

where
\kappa_{S}
is the surface gravity.

\bullet
The specific heat capacity

C_{q}=T_{H}\left(\frac{\partial S}{\partial T_{H}}\right)_{q}=\frac{T_{H}\partial S/\partial r_{+}}{\partial T_{H}/\partial r_{+}}=\frac{2\pi r_{+}T_{H}}{G\partial T_{H}/\partial r_{+}}.

(15)

We will see that (following Eq.
15
) the heat capacity diverges if the Hawking temperature has extremum.

Summary and Conclusion

We have analyzed the thermodynamic properties of magnetized Black-hole in non-linear electrodynamics model. The model is characterized by two parameters
\beta
and
\gamma
. In our numerical analysis
we set
\beta=1
throughout. We varied
\gamma
