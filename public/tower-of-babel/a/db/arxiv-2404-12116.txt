Ore sets, denominator sets and left or right regular elements of a ring

The aim of this section is to present several results on and constructions of left Ore and denominator sets of a ring. (7\Leftrightarrow 8)

Theorem 5.9
.

1.

(1-\sigma)^{d}(\phi)=d!\lambda_{d}
where

\sum_{\alpha\in\Pi_{d}}{\cal P}_{n}\sigma^{\alpha}(\phi)={\cal P}_{n}
, i.e.
\bigcap_{\alpha\in\Pi_{d}}V(\sigma^{\alpha}(\phi))=\emptyset
.

3.

For every automorphism
\tau\in{\rm Aut}_{K}({\cal P}_{n})
, the automorphisms
\sigma_{1}^{\prime}=\tau\sigma_{1}\tau^{-1},\ldots,\sigma_{n}^{\prime}=\tau\sigma_{n}\tau^{-1}\in{\rm Aut}_{K}({\cal P}_{n})
commute

\sum_{\alpha\in\Pi_{d}}{\cal P}_{n}\sigma^{\prime\alpha}(\tau(\phi))={\cal P}_{n}
, i.e.
\bigcap_{\alpha\in\Pi_{d}}V(\sigma^{\prime\alpha}(\tau(\phi)))=\emptyset
.

Proof.

1. Since
{\cal P}_{n}={\cal P}_{n-1}\otimes K[H_{n}]
,
\phi=\phi_{n-1}H_{n}^{d_{n}}+\psi_{n-1}H_{n}^{d_{n}-1}+\cdots

3. Clearly, the automorphisms
