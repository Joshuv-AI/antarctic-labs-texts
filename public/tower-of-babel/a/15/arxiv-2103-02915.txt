Rank r r DT theory from rank 0 0
S. Feyzbakhsh and R. P. Thomas

Abstract
. Fix a Calabi-Yau 3-fold X X satisfying the Bogomolov-Gieseker conjecture of Bayer-Macrì-Toda, such as the quintic 3-fold. We express Joyce’s generalised DT invariants counting Gieseker semistable sheaves of any rank r ≥ 1 r\geq 1 on X X in terms of those counting sheaves of rank 0 and pure dimension 2. The basic technique is to reduce the ranks of sheaves by replacing them by the cokernels of their Mochizuki/Joyce-Song pairs and then use wall crossing to handle their stability.
