Addendum to “Structure of seeds in generalized cluster algebras”
Tomoki Nakanishi

Abstract
. This is Addendum to “Structure of seeds in generalized cluster algebras”, Pacific J. Math. 277 (2015), 201–218. We extend the class of generalized cluster algebras studied therein to embrace examples in some applications.



2020 Mathematics Subject Classification
13F60

The definition of the class of generalized cluster algebras
in
[
Nak15
]
turned out to be too restrictive
for some applications (e.g.,
[
GM23
]
).
However, it is easily extended as follows.
Let
\mathbb{P}
be the semifield in Section 2A.
In (2.1)
we replace each data
z_{i,s}\in\mathbb{P}

with
z_{i,s}\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}\mathbb{P}
.
For
z_{i,s}\neq 0
,
let
z_{i,s}=\sum_{j}m_{j}p_{j}
(
m_{j}\in\mathbb{Z}_{>0}
,
p_{j}\in\mathbb{P}
),
where the multiplication by
m_{j}
is in
\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}\mathbb{P}
.
Then,
we set
\tilde{z}_{i,s}=\bigoplus_{j}m_{j}p_{j}\in\mathbb{P}
, where the multiplication by
m_{j}
is in the semifield

\mathbb{P}
.
For
z_{i,s}=0
, we formally set
\tilde{z}_{i,s}=0
, though this is not an element in
\mathbb{P}
.
Then, we replace the formulas (2.5) and (2.6) with

\displaystyle{y}^{\prime}_{i}

\displaystyle=\begin{cases}\displaystyle{y}_{k}^{-1}&i=k\\
\displaystyle{y}_{i}\Biggl({y}_{k}^{[\varepsilon{b}_{ki}]_{+}}\Biggr)^{d_{k}}\Biggl(\bigoplus_{s=0}^{d_{k}}\tilde{z}_{k,s}{y}_{k}^{\varepsilon s}\Biggr)^{-{b}_{ki}}&i\neq k,\\
\end{cases}

\displaystyle x^{\prime}_{i}

\displaystyle=\begin{cases}\displaystyle x_{k}^{-1}\Biggl(\prod_{j=1}^{n}x_{j}^{[-\varepsilon{b}_{jk}]_{+}}\Biggr)^{d_{k}}\frac{\displaystyle\sum_{s=0}^{d_{k}}z_{k,s}\hat{y}_{k}^{\varepsilon s}}{\displaystyle\bigoplus_{s=0}^{d_{k}}\tilde{z}_{k,s}{y}_{k}^{\varepsilon s}}&i=k\\
x_{i}&i\neq k,\\
\end{cases}

where, if
\tilde{z}_{k,s}=0
appears in the sum in
\mathbb{P}
, we simply ignore it.

If we set
\mathbb{P}
to be the trivial semifield,
it embraces the examples in
[
GM23
]
.
If there is some
z_{i,s}\not\in\mathbb{P}
,
it is no longer a generalized cluster algebra in the sense of
[
CS14
]
;
accordingly, the correspondence given in Section 2A after Proposition 2.5 does not hold.
However, all the other propositions and theorems remain valid with the obvious modification,
namely, replacing
F_{i}^{t}|_{\mathbb{P}}(\mathbf{y},\mathbf{z})
with

F_{i}^{t}|_{\mathbb{P}}(\mathbf{y},\tilde{\mathbf{z}})
in Theorems 3.22 and 3.23.
