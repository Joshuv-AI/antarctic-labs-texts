denote the spectral idemoptent

If

(2)

and with respect to this decomposition of

A=A_{1}\oplus A_{2},

A_{1}
is invertible operator on
\mathcal{R}(AA^{D})

\mathcal{N}(AA^{D})

A fascinating problem in Drazin inverse theory revolves around examining the Drazin invertibility for the sum and product of two Drazin invertible elements. The investigation of the Drazin inverse’s additive and multiplicative properties is significantly influenced by the commutative relationship between two elements. In the literature, several results pertaining to this aspect can be found in
deng2008drazin
;
deng2010new
;
wei2011note
. Instead of taking commutativity, if we consider the weaker relation
a^{2}b=ba^{2}
for
a,b\in\mathcal{A}^{D}(\text{or }\mathcal{A}^{d})
, then, in general, without any additional condition on
a,b
, it becomes challenging to deduce any conclusions about the Drazin(or g-Drazin) invertibility of
ab
and
a+b
. Chen and Sheibani

provided several equivalent conditions for the g-Drazin invertibility of

, assuming that
\lambda a^{2}b=\lambda^{\prime}ba^{2}=aba
and
\mu ab^{2}=\mu^{\prime}b^{2}a=bab,
where
\lambda,\lambda^{\prime},\mu,\mu^{\prime}
are non zero complex numbers. Recently, Qin and Lu
qin2023drazin
proved these results under weaker conditions
a^{2}b=aba
and
b^{2}a=bab
. In this context, motivated by weakly commutativity relation
pondvelivcek1975weakly

Definition 1.1

Two elements
a,b\in\mathcal{A}
are said to be
a
-weakly commutative if there exists

satisfying
ab=ca\text{ and }ba=ac.

Note that commutativity is a particular case of Definition

Remark 1.2

According to Definition
1.1
, if

are
a
-weakly commutative, then

are also
a
-weakly commutative.

Definition 1.3

For

are
a
-weakly commutative as well as
b
-weakly commutative also then we say

\{a,b\}

If two elements

Since

-weakly commutative and

commutes with each other.

Proof 1.7

Since

If
