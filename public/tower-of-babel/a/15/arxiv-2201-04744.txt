Crossed Burnside rings and cohomological Mackey 2 2 -motives
ODA, Fumihito

Abstract
Balmer and Dell’Ambrogio introduced the pseudo-functor 𝒫 \mathcal{P} from the bicategory of 𝕜 {\mathbbm{k}} -linear Mackey 2 2 -motives to the bicategory of 𝕜 {\mathbbm{k}} -linear cohomological Mackey 2 2 -motives over a commutative ring 𝕜 {\mathbbm{k}} . They showed that 𝒫 \mathcal{P} maps the general Mackey 2 2 -motives to the cohomological Mackey 2 2 -motives by using the ring homomorphism from the crossed Burnside ring of a finite group G G over 𝕜 {\mathbbm{k}} to the center Z ​ 𝕜 ​ G Z{\mathbbm{k}}G of group algebra 𝕜 ​ G {\mathbbm{k}}G ( [ BD21 , Theorem 5.3] ). We study the behavior of motivic decomposition of cohomological Mackey 2 2 -motives as images by 𝒫 \mathcal{P} of motivic decomposition of Mackey 2 2 -motives.
