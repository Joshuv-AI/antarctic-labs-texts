Supplementary Figure 1
. The 1979-2018 trends in springtime (SON) EKE in reanalyses
mean (blue) and CMIP6 mean (gray). The black circles show the trends
from the individual reanalyses/members, where filled (open) circles
show trends that are (not) statistically significant at the
5\%

level based on a Student’s t-test. The black
bar shows the
95\%
confidence interval of the mean reanalyses trend. Unlike during winter, in spring
models seems to capture part of the reanalyses trends.

Supplementary Figure 6
. The 1979-2018 trends in EKE in reanalyses mean
(blue) and CESM mean (gray). The black circles show the trends from
the individual reanalyses/members, where filled (open) circles show
trends that are (not) statistically significant at the
5\%
level
based on a Student’s t-test. Here the EKE is defined as deviations
from monthly mean, which might explain the small overlap between models
and one reanalysis (i.e., including low frequency phenomena).

Supplementary Figure 8
. The 1979-2018 eddy kinetic energy trends
plotted against the trends in
a,
Eady growth rate and

Supplementary Figure 9
. The 1979-2018 trends in eddy heat flux (
v^{\prime}T^{\prime}
,
upper row) and eddy moisture flux (
v^{\prime}q^{\prime}
, bottom row) plotted against
the trends in baroclinic (left column) and barotropic (right column)
growth rates of mid-latitude eddies, calculated from a linear normal-mode
instability analysis. Contours show the two-dimensional probability
density function of the trends in CMIP6 models, estimated by fitting
a kernel distribution, and the correlation appears in each panel.
The black circles show the trends from reanalyses.

respectively.
