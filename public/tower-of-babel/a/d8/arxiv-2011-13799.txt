S. Banik ∗1,2 , R.K. Shikha ∗∗,2 , A.A. Noman ∗∗∗,2 , N.A. Chowdhury †,3 , A. Mannan ‡,4 , T.S. Roy §,5 , and A.A. Mamun §§,2

Abstract
A nonlinear Schrödinger equation (NLSE) has been derived by employing reductive perturbation method for investigating the modulational instability of dust-ion-acoustic waves (DIAWs) in a four-component plasma having stationary negatively charged dust grains, inertial warm ions, and inertialess non-thermal electrons and positrons. It is observed that under consideration, the plasma system supports both modulationally stable and unstable domains, which are determined by the sign of the dispersive and nonlinear coefficients of NLSE, of the DIAWs. It is also found that the nonlinearity as well as the height and width of the first and second-order rogue waves increases with the non-thermality of electron and positron. The relevancy of our present investigation to the observations in space plasmas is pinpointed.

4
Instability analysis

To study the MI of DIAWs, we consider the linear solution of the Eq.
(
19
) in the form
\Phi=\widetilde{\Phi}e^{iQ|\widetilde{\Phi}|^{2}\tau}
+c.c.,
where
\widetilde{\Phi}=\widetilde{\Phi}_{0}+\epsilon\widetilde{\Phi}_{1}

and
\widetilde{\Phi}_{1}=\widetilde{\Phi}_{1,0}e^{i(\widetilde{k}\xi-\widetilde{\omega}{\tau})}+c.c

wave number
\widetilde{k}
and frequency
\widetilde{\omega}
which are different from
k
and

\omega

19

nonlinear dispersion relation
[
18

19
]

\displaystyle\hskip-36.98866pt\widetilde{\omega}^{2}=P^{2}\widetilde{k}^{2}\Big(\widetilde{k}^{2}-\frac{2|\widetilde{\Phi}_{0}|^{2}}{P/Q}\Big).\

(22)

It is observed here that the ratio
P/Q
is negative (i.e.,

P/Q<0
), the DIAWs will be modulationally stable. On the other
hand, if the ratio
P/Q
is positive (i.e.,
P/Q>0
), the DIAWs
will be modulationally unstable. We have graphically examined
the effect of temperature of the ion and electron as well as the charge state of the warm positive ion in recognizing the stable
(i.e.,
k<k_{c}
) and unstable (i.e.,

) domains of DIAWs in Fig.
1
, and it is
clear from this figure that (a) the plasma system under consideration supports the DIAWs

) or unstable (i.e.,

increase in the value of the charge state of the warm positive ion when

and

(c) the

decreases with the increase in the value of the ion temperature while increases with

et al.

[

It is obvious from Eq. (

the nonlinearity of the plasma system increases with the charge state and number density of the
warm ion, but decreases with the charge state and number density of the stationary negatively charged massive dust grains.

Equation (

of DIAWs energy is concentrated into a comparatively small

(ii) The physics of this result is that with increasing the

6
Conclusion

In this study, we have performed a nonlinear analysis of DIAWs in an unmagnetized EPIDPM
having stationary massive nagetively charged dust grains, inertial warm positive ions,
and inertialess non-thermal Cairns’ distributed electrons and positrons. The evolution of
DIAWs is governed by the standard NLSE, and the coefficients
P
and
Q
of NLSE can recognize the modulationally stable and
unstable domains of DIAWs. It is observed that the critical wave number, for which the MI sets in, decreases with ion temperature but
increases with electron temperature. The nonlinearity as well as the height and width of the DIARWs increases with the non-thermality
of electrons and positrons. The limitation of this work is that the gravitational and magnetic fields are not consider.
In future and for better understanding, someone can
investigate the nonlinear propagation in a four-component EPIDPM by considering the gravitational and magnetic
fields. However, these results may be applicable in understanding the conditions of the MI of DIAWs and associated DIARWs in Jupiter’s
magnetosphere
[

]
, Milky Way
[

]
, auroral zone

,

[
2
]
, the hot spots on dust rings in the galactic
centre
[
3
,
4
,
5
,
6
]
, interstellar medium
[
2
]
,
and around pulsars
[
