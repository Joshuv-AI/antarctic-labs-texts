A new infinite family of irregular algebraic surfaces with canonical map of degree 8
NGUYEN BIN

Abstract
In this note we construct an unlimited family of irregular algebraic surfaces of general type with canonical map of degree 8 8 , irregularity 1 1 and arbitrarily large geometric genus such that the image of the canonical map is not a surface of minimal degree.
