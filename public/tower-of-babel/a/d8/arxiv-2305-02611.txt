The study of the cohomological structure of the fixed point set and orbit space has been an interesting problem in transformation groups. Smith

p

n

p

r

He has also proved that if

acts effectively on the real projective space then the fixed point set is either empty or it has two components having mod
2
cohomology real projective space
[
12

. Bredon
[

generalizes this result for

a prime, actions on cohomology projective spaces. Bredon
[
2
]
also proved that if a finitistic space

satisfies poincaré duality with respect to Čech cohomology with
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
-coefficients then each component of the fixed point set also satisfies poincaré duality. Puppe
[
14

is totally nonhomologous to zero in
X_{G}
(Borel space) then the number of generators of the cohomology ring of each component of the fixed point set with
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
-coefficient is at most the number of generators of
H^{*}(X).
The fixed point sets of involutions on the product of projective spaces and Dold manifolds have been determined in
[
4
,
8
]
.

On the other hand, it is well known that the orbit spaces of free actions of

.
