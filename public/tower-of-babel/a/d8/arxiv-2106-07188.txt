On exact estimates of the order of approximation of functions of several variables in the anisotropic Lorentz - Zygmund space
G. Akishev

Abstract




Abstract.

In this paper we consider

m

Lemma 1
.

Let
\alpha,\beta\in\mathbb{R}
,
l\in\mathbb{N}
.

1) If
1-\alpha>0
and
1-\beta>0
, then

\sum\limits_{0\leqslant s<l}(s+1)^{-\alpha}(l-s)^{-\beta}\asymp(l+1)^{1-(\alpha+\beta)}.

2) If
\alpha=1,\beta=1,
then

\sum\limits_{0\leqslant s<l}(s+1)^{-1}(l-s)^{-1}\asymp l^{-1}\ln(1+l).

3) If
\alpha=1,0<\beta<1,
then

\sum\limits_{0<s<l}s^{-1}(l-s)^{-1}\asymp l^{-\beta}\ln(1+l).

Proof.
If
\alpha,\beta\in\mathbb{R}
, then

[formula]

\sum\limits_{0\leqslant s<l}(s+1)^{-\alpha}(l-s)^{-\beta}=\sum\limits_{0\leqslant s<\frac{l}{2}}(s+1)^{-\alpha}(l-s)^{-\beta}+\sum\limits_{\frac{l}{2}\leqslant s<l}(s+1)^{-\alpha}(l-s)^{-\beta}.

If
1-\alpha>0,
then

[formula]

C_{1}(\alpha)(l+1)^{1-\alpha}\leqslant\sum\limits_{0\leqslant s<\frac{l}{2}}(s+1)^{-\alpha}\leqslant C_{2}(\alpha)(l+1)^{1-\alpha}.

If
1-\beta>0,
then

[formula]

C_{1}(\beta)(l+1)^{1-\beta}\leqslant\sum\limits_{\frac{l}{2}\leqslant s<l}(l-s)^{-\beta}\leqslant C_{2}(\beta)(l+1)^{1-\beta}.

If
\beta<0
and
1-\alpha>0,
then

[formula]

\sum\limits_{0\leqslant s<l}s^{-\alpha}(l-s)^{-\beta}\leqslant l^{-\beta}\sum\limits_{0\leqslant s<l}s^{-\alpha}\leqslant C_{2}(\alpha)(l+1)^{1-\alpha-\beta}.

If
\alpha<0
and
1-\beta>0,
then

[formula]

\sum\limits_{0\leqslant s<l}s^{-\alpha}(l-s)^{-\beta}\leqslant l^{-\alpha}\sum\limits_{0\leqslant s<l}(l-s)^{-\beta}\leqslant C(\alpha)(l+1)^{1-\alpha-\beta}.

From inequalities (1)–(5), we obtain

\sum\limits_{0\leqslant s<l}(s+1)^{-\alpha}(l-s)^{-\beta}\asymp(l+1)^{1-(\alpha+\beta)}.

The first point is proven.

2) If
\alpha=\beta=1,
then

[formula]

\sum\limits_{0\leqslant s<\frac{l}{2}}(s+1)^{-1}\asymp\ln(1+l)

and

[formula]

\sum\limits_{\frac{l}{2}\leqslant s<l}(l-s)^{-1}\asymp\ln(1+l).

Therefore, according to inequalities (6), (7), we have

\sum\limits_{0\leqslant s<l}(s+1)^{-1}(l-s)^{-1}\leqslant\left(\frac{l}{2}\right)^{-1}\sum\limits_{0\leqslant s<\frac{l}{2}}(s+1)^{-1}+\left(\frac{l}{2}+1\right)^{-1}\sum\limits_{\frac{l}{2}\leqslant s<l}(l-s)^{-1}\leqslant C_{3}l^{-1}\ln(1+l).

Reverse inequality

\sum\limits_{0\leqslant s<l}(s+1)^{-1}(l-s)^{-1}\geqslant\sum\limits_{0<s<l}(s+1)^{-1}l^{-1}\geqslant Cl^{-1}\ln(1+l).

The second statement is proved.

3) If
\alpha=1,0<\beta<1,
then from (5) we obtain

\sum\limits_{0<s<l}s^{-1}(l-s)^{-\beta}\leqslant\left(\frac{l}{2}\right)^{-\beta}\sum\limits_{0<s<\frac{l}{2}}s^{-1}+\left(\frac{l}{2}\right)^{-1}\sum\limits_{\frac{l}{2}\leqslant s<l}(l-s)^{-\beta}\leqslant

\leqslant Cl^{-\beta}\ln(1+l).

Lemma 1 is proved

Lemma 2
.

Let
\theta,\beta\in(0,\infty)
. Then, for any
\lambda_{1},\lambda_{2}\in\mathbb{R}
, the following relation holds:

[formula]

\sum\limits_{s=0}^{n}2^{-s\beta\theta}(s+1)^{\lambda_{2}\theta}(n-s+1)^{\lambda_{1}\theta}\asymp(n+1)^{\lambda_{1}\theta}

[formula]

\sum\limits_{s=0}^{n}2^{s\beta\theta}(s+1)^{\lambda_{2}\theta}(n-s+1)^{\lambda_{1}\theta}\asymp 2^{n\beta\theta}(n+1)^{\lambda_{2}\theta}

Proof.
Let
n\in\mathbb{N}
,
n\leqslant x<n+1
. Then

F(x)=\int_{0}^{x}2^{-t\beta\theta}(t+1)^{\lambda_{2}\theta}(x-t+1)^{\lambda_{1}\theta}dt\geqslant

[formula]

\sum\limits_{k=0}^{n-1}2^{-k\beta\theta}(k+1)^{\lambda_{2}\theta}(n-k+1)^{\lambda_{1}\theta}.

Therefore, it suffices to estimate the function
F(x)
.

Let
\beta>0
. We will choose a number
\delta\in(0,\beta)
,
then

F(x)=2^{-x\theta\delta}\int_{0}^{x}2^{-t(\beta-\delta)\theta}(t+1)^{\lambda_{2}\theta}2^{(x-t)\theta\delta}(x-t+1)^{\lambda_{1}\theta}dt.

Since

increases by

, then

F(x)\leqslant 2^{-x\theta\delta}2^{x\theta\delta_{1}}(x+1)^{\lambda_{1}\theta}\int_{0}^{x}2^{-t(\beta-\delta)\theta}(t+1)^{\lambda_{2}\theta}dt

[formula]

\leqslant(x+1)^{\lambda_{1}\theta}\int_{0}^{\infty}2^{-t(\beta-\delta)\theta}(t+1)^{\lambda_{2}\theta}dt.

Since
\beta-\delta>0
, the integral

\int_{0}^{\infty}2^{-t(\beta-\delta)\theta}(t+1)^{\lambda_{2}\theta}dt

converges. Hence from (11), we obtain

Therefore,

F(x)\geqslant(x+1)^{\lambda_{1}\theta}\int_{0}^{1/2}2^{-t\delta_{1}\theta}(t+1)^{\lambda_{2}\theta}dt

If
\lambda_{1}>0

F(x)\geqslant\int_{0}^{x/2}2^{-t\beta\theta}(t+1)^{\lambda_{2}\theta}(x-t+1)^{\lambda_{1}\theta}dt\geqslant(\frac{x}{2}+1)^{\lambda_{1}\theta}\int_{0}^{x/2}2^{-t\beta\theta}(t+1)^{\lambda_{2}\theta}dt

Now relation (8) follows from these inequalities and (10), (11).
