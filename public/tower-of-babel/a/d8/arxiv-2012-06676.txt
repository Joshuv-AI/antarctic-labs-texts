Abstract
. In 1944 Dyson defined the rank of a partition as the largest part minus the number of parts, and conjectured that the residue of the rank mod 5 5 divides the partitions of 5 ​ n + 4 5n+4 into five equal classes. This gave a combinatorial explanation of Ramanujan’s famous partition congruence mod 5 5 . He made an analogous conjecture for the rank mod 7 7 and the partitions of 7 ​ n + 5 7n+5 . In 1954 Atkin and Swinnerton-Dyer proved Dyson’s rank conjectures by constructing several Lambert-series identities basically using the theory of elliptic functions. In 2016 the author gave another proof using the theory of weak harmonic Maass forms. In this paper we describe a new and more elementary approach using Hecke-Rogers series.

-m-1\leq j\leq m&\mbox{if $n=2m+1$}.\end{cases}

It follows that

(4.12)

\displaystyle A_{5,0}\left(\underset{(n,j)\in\mathcal{S}_{2}\pmod{5}}{\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\sum_{j=-\lfloor n/2\rfloor}^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}}(-1)^{n+j}q^{\frac{1}{2}(n^{2}-3j^{2})+\frac{1}{2}(n-j)}\right)

\displaystyle={\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\sum_{j=-\lfloor n/2\rfloor}^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}}(-1)^{n+j}\left(q^{n+1}+q^{-n}\right)q^{\frac{5}{2}(n^{2}-3j^{2})+\frac{5}{2}(n-j)}-\sum_{m=-\infty}^{\infty}(-1)^{m}q^{m(5m+1)/2}

\displaystyle=\overset{\text{\lower 3.0pt\hbox{$\sim$}}}{R}(q,q^{5})-J_{5,2},

by (
2.20
), (
4.5
) and Jacobi’s
triple product identity (
2.3
).
Now

(4.13)

\displaystyle\underset{(n,j)\in\mathcal{S}_{1}\pmod{5}}{\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\sum_{j=-\lfloor n/2\rfloor}^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}}(-1)^{n+j}q^{\frac{1}{2}(n^{2}-3j^{2})+\frac{1}{2}(n-j)}

\displaystyle=U_{5,0}^{*}\left({\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\sum_{j=-\lfloor n/2\rfloor}^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}}(-1)^{n+j}q^{\frac{1}{2}(n^{2}-3j^{2})+\frac{1}{2}(n-j)}\right)

\displaystyle=U_{5,0}^{*}\left((q)_{\infty}^{2}\right)\qquad\qquad\mbox{(by (\ref{eq:HRE12}))}

\displaystyle=\frac{J_{25}^{2}\,J_{25,10}^{2}}{J_{25,5}^{2}},

by (
3.4
). Thus from (
4.8
), (
4.12
) and (

we have

(4.14)

U_{5,0}\left(\overset{\text{\lower 3.0pt\hbox{$\sim$}}}{R}(\zeta,q)\right)=(1+\zeta)\,\frac{J_{5}^{2}\,J_{5,2}^{2}}{J_{5,1}^{2}}-(2+2\zeta+\zeta^{3})\,\left(\overset{\text{\lower 3.0pt\hbox{$\sim$}}}{R}(q,q^{5})-J_{5,2}\right),

which is (
4.6
). The proof of (
4.7
) is analogous.
∎

Now by (
4.5
) and the definition of
\phi(q)
we have

(4.15)

\displaystyle\overset{\text{\lower 3.0pt\hbox{$\sim$}}}{R}(q,q^{5})

\displaystyle=(q^{2};q^{5})_{\infty}(q^{3};q^{5})_{\infty}(q^{5};q^{5})_{\infty}\frac{1}{1-q}R(q,q^{5})

\displaystyle=J_{5,2}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{q^{5n^{2}}}{(q;q^{5})_{n+1}(q^{4};q^{5})_{n}}\qquad\qquad\mbox{(by Jacobi's triple product (\ref{eq:jacp}))}

\displaystyle=J_{5,2}\,\left(1+\phi(q)\right).

By replacing

\displaystyle=(1+\zeta)\left(J_{25,10}+q(\zeta+\zeta^{4})J_{25,5}\right)\left(R_{0}(q^{5})+q\frac{J_{25}^{2}}{J_{25,5}}+q^{2}(\zeta+\zeta^{4})\frac{J_{25}^{2}}{J_{25,10}}+q^{3}R_{3}(q^{5})\right)

\displaystyle U_{7,4}\left((\zeta q)_{\infty}(\zeta^{-1}q)_{\infty}(q)_{\infty}\,R(\zeta,q)\right)

6.
Conclusion

In this paper we presented a new approach to proving Dyson’s rank conjectures.
This new approach involved utilising various Hecke-Rogers identities.
We showed how this method gave a new proof for Dyson’s mod 5 rank
conjecture as well as the related identity in Ramanujan’s Lost Notebook.
We end by listing some problems.

(

See

Find simple proofs of the four Hecke-Rogers identities
(
2.18
)–(
2.21
). Combinatorial proofs are also needed.
These results would lead to a truly elementary proof of Dyson’s rank

Apply the methods of this paper to other rank-type functions, including

, and Jenning-Shaffer’s exotic
Bailey-Slater spt-functions
[

]
