On basic velocity estimates for the plane steady-state Navier–Stokes system and its applications
Mikhail Korobkov, Xiao Ren

Abstract
We consider some new estimates for general steady Navier-Stokes solutions in plane domains. According to our main result, if the domain is convex, then the difference between mean values of the velocity over two concentric circles is bounded (up to a constant factor) by the square-root of the Dirichlet integral in the annulus between the circles. The constant factor in this inequality is universal and does not depend on the ratio of the circle radii. Several applications of these formulas are discussed.

Introduction and main results

We study the stationary Navier–Stokes equations

\left\{\begin{aligned} &-\Delta\mathbf{w}+(\mathbf{w}\cdot\nabla)\mathbf{w}+\nabla p=\mathbf{0}\ \ \mathrm{in}\ \Omega,\\
&\nabla\cdot\mathbf{w}=0\ \ \mathrm{in}\ \Omega\\

Still there are a lot of open problems concerning stationary solutions in exterior plane domains, for example, the simplest in formulation but very important from the physical point of view

2.1
Some properties of

For a function

We use standard notations for Sobolev spaces

Below for

\Omega_{\rho_{1},\rho_{2}}=\{z\in\mathbb{R}^{2}:\rho_{1}<|z|<\rho_{2}\}.

Further, for a function

denote by
D_{f}(\rho_{1},\rho_{2})
the corresponding
Dirichlet integral:

D_{f}(\rho_{1},\rho_{2}):=\int\limits_{\Omega_{\rho_{1},\rho_{2}}}|\nabla f|^{2}.

Using this notation, recall three elementary facts concerning

-functions.

Lemma 11
.

For any

we have

|\bar{f}(\rho_{2})-\bar{f}(\rho_{1})|\leq\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}\left(\ln\frac{\rho_{2}}{\rho_{1}}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}\cdot\bigl(D_{f}(\rho_{1},\rho_{2})\bigr)^{\frac{1}{2}},

(2.1)

where
\bar{f}
means the mean value of

over the circle

:

\bar{f}(r):=\frac{1}{2\pi r}\int\limits_{|z|=r}f(z)\,ds.

Lemma 12
.

Fix a number

. Let

Summarize the results of these lemmas, we receive

Lemma 13

Under conditions of Lemma

\sup\limits_{|z|=r}|f(z)-\bar{f}(R)|\leq\tilde{c}_{\beta}\sqrt{D_{f}(\beta R,R)},

r_{*}\in\bigl(\frac{1}{2}r_{0},r_{0}\bigr)
such that

\int\limits_{S_{r_{*}}(z_{0})}|{\mathbf{u}}_{k}|^{2}\,ds\leq C\,r_{0}{\varepsilon}_{k}^{2},

(3.37)

\int\limits_{S_{r_{*}}(z_{0})}|\nabla{\mathbf{w}}|^{2}\,ds\leq\frac{C}{r_{0}}{\varepsilon}_{k}^{2},

(3.38)

consequently,

\int\limits_{S_{r_{*}}(z_{0})}|\nabla{\mathbf{w}}|\,ds\leq C{\varepsilon}_{k}.

(3.39)

From (
3.37
) we conclude, that there exists a point
z_{*}\in S_{r_{*}}(z_{0})
such that

|{\mathbf{u}}_{k}(z_{*})|\leq C{\varepsilon}_{k}.

Then by construction and from (
3.35
) we get

|{\mathbf{w}}(z_{*})-{\mathbf{w}}_{0}|\leq\delta_{k}+C{\varepsilon}_{k}.

From the last estimate and from (
3.39
) we conclude

\sup\limits_{z\in S_{r_{*}}(z_{0})}|{\mathbf{w}}(z)-{\mathbf{w}}_{0}|\leq\delta_{k}+C{\varepsilon}_{k}.

(3.40)

Then from the ”unbelievable” estimate (
1.8
) applying for the disk
B_{r_{*}}(z_{0})
we obtain the required estimate

|{\mathbf{w}}(z_{0})-{\mathbf{w}}_{0}|\leq C^{\prime}(\delta_{k}+{\varepsilon}_{k}).

(3.41)

The remaining two cases (
ii
)–(
iii
) are treated similarly using (
3.36
). Because of arbitrariness of
z_{0}\in\Omega_{k}
, we proved really that

\sup\limits_{z\in\Omega_{k}}|{\mathbf{w}}(z)-{\mathbf{w}}_{0}|\to 0\qquad\mbox{ as }k\to\infty.

(3.42)

By construction, this implies the required uniform convergence (
1.13
). Theorem
7
is proved. ∎

Proof of Corollary
5
. Let assumptions of Corollary
5
be fulfilled. Fix
\delta\in(0,1)
. Take arbitrary
z_{0}\in B_{\delta R}
and fix it also.
Denote
r_{0}=R-|z_{0}|
. Then by construction

r_{0}>(1-\delta)R,

(3.43)

B(z_{0},r_{0})\subset B_{R}.

(3.44)

Denote
{\mathbf{w}}_{0}=\bar{\mathbf{w}}(R)
. Take a ”good” circle
S_{r_{1}}
with radius
r_{1}\in\biggl(\frac{1+2\delta}{3}R,\frac{1+\delta}{2}R\biggr)
such that

\sup\limits_{z\in S_{r_{1}}}|{\mathbf{w}}(z)-{\mathbf{w}}_{0}|\leq C_{\delta}\biggl(\int_{B_{R}}|\nabla\mathbf{w}|^{2}\biggr)^{\frac{1}{2}},

(3.45)

the existence of such
{r_{1}}
follows directly from Lemmas
11
,
13
. By construction,

r_{1}-|z_{0}|>\frac{1+2\delta}{3}R-\delta R=\frac{1-\delta}{3}R,

r_{0}-\bigl(r_{1}-|z_{0}|\,\bigr)=R-|z_{0}|-r_{1}+|z_{0}|=R-r_{1}>\frac{1-\delta}{2}R,

in particular,

\frac{r_{0}}{r_{1}-|z_{0}|}>\frac{2}{1+\delta}.

(3.46)

Now again take a good circle
S_{r_{*}}(z_{0})
centered at
z_{0}
with radius
r_{*}\in\bigl(r_{1}-|z_{0}|,r_{0}\bigr)

satisfying

\sup\limits_{z_{1},z_{2}\in S_{r_{*}}(z_{0})}|{\mathbf{w}}(z_{1})-{\mathbf{w}}(z_{2})|\leq C^{\prime}_{\delta}\biggl(\int_{B_{R}}|\nabla\mathbf{w}|^{2}\biggr)^{\frac{1}{2}},

(3.47)

the existence of such
{r_{*}}
follows directly from (
3.46
) and from Lemma
12
applied to the annulus

\{z\in{\mathbb{R}}^{2}:r_{1}-|z_{0}|<|z-z_{0}|<r_{0}\}.

By construction, since
r_{*}>r_{1}-|z_{0}|
, we have

S_{r_{*}}(z_{0})\cap S_{r_{1}}\neq\emptyset.

Thus, from ”goodness” of these both circles (see (
3.45
) and (
3.47
) ) we obtain

\sup\limits_{z\in S_{r_{*}}(z_{0})}|{\mathbf{w}}(z)-{\mathbf{w}}_{0}|\leq C^{\prime\prime}_{\delta}\biggl(\int_{B_{R}}|\nabla\mathbf{w}|^{2}\biggr)^{\frac{1}{2}},

(3.48)

Then from the ”unbelievable” estimate (
1.8
) applying to the ball
B_{r_{*}}(z_{0})
we obtain the required estimate

|{\mathbf{w}}(z_{0})-{\mathbf{w}}_{0}|=|{\mathbf{w}}(z_{0})-\bar{\mathbf{w}}(R)|\leq C^{\prime\prime\prime}_{\delta}\biggl(\int_{B_{R}}|\nabla\mathbf{w}|^{2}\biggr)^{\frac{1}{2}}.

Because of arbitrariness of
z_{0}\in B_{\delta R}
, Corollary
5
is proved completely. ∎

6

1.3

\bar{\mathbf{w}}(r_{2})
