Incomplete sets in 𝐏 {\mathbf{P}} for logspace reduction
Reiner Czerwinski

Abstract
In this article, we investigate the behaviour of TMs with time limit and tape space limit. This problem is in 𝐏 {\mathbf{P}} when the time limit is unary coded. When both limits go to infinity, it is undecidable which limit is exceeded first. Thus logspace incomplete sets can be constructed in 𝐏 {\mathbf{P}} . This implies 𝐋 ≠ 𝐏 {\mathbf{L}}{}\not={\mathbf{P}} .

Space Hierarchy within
{\mathbf{P}}

In this section we use Turing-reduction instead of many-one-reduction
because for any sets
X\not\leq_{T}^{\log}Y\;\Rightarrow\;X\not\leq_{m}^{\log}Y
.

Theorem 2
.

If
h
and
l
are space-constructible functions with
l\in o(h)

and
l\in\Omega(\log)
then

B(h)\not\leq_{T}^{\log}B(l)

Proof.

Due to the space hierarchy theorem
[
SHI65
]
, there is a set
X\in{\mathbf{DSPACE}}(h)
with

X\not\in{\mathbf{DSPACE}}(l)
.
Thus
X\leq_{T}^{\log}B(h)
and
X\not\leq_{T}^{\log}B(l)
are valid.
This implies
B(h)\not\leq_{T}^{\log}B(l)
.
∎

.
In this case, there is no reduction function

Notes on the Relativization Barrier

To separate

and
{\mathbf{P}}
, the relativization barrier applies as for
{\mathbf{P}}
versus
{\mathbf{NP}}

[

]
