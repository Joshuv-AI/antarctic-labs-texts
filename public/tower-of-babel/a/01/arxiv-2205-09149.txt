Cofinal morphism of polynomial monads and double delooping
Florian De Leger

Abstract
. Using the theory of internal algebras classifiers developed by Batanin and Berger, we construct a morphism of polynomial monads which we prove is homotopically cofinal. We then describe how this result constitutes the main conceptual argument for a categorical direct double delooping proof of the Turchin-Dwyer-Hess theorem concerning the explicit double delooping of spaces of long knots.

If

are contractible, then there is a weak equivalence between simplicial sets

This result is remarkable especially because of an earlier result from Sinha
[
16

which connects the space of
long knots modulo immersions

[
10
]
with the totalization of the Kontsevich operad.
Due to its importance in algebraic geometry, many proofs of the Turchin-Dwyer-Hess theorem, and generalisation to higher dimensions, exist in the literature
[

,
6
,
8
,
9
,
10
,
18
]
. However, we believe that our techniques can provide a new insight and a conceptual explanation of the result which can be generalised to higher dimensions, as we develop hereafter.

\textstyle{E}

which states that there is a homotopically cofinal morphism of polynomial monads from

in any simplicial model category

and show that, if

The last technical step is about the “pointedness” of infinitesimal bimodules. We will prove that mapping spaces in the categories of infinitesimal bimodule and pointed infinitesimal bimodules are weakly equivalent.
