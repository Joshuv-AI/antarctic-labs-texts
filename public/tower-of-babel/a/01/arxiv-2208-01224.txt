Sums of k k -bonacci Numbers
Harold R. Parks, Dean C. Wills

Abstract
. We give a combinatorial proof of a formula giving the partial sums of the k k -bonacci sequence as alternating sums of powers of two multiplied by binomial coefficients. As a corollary we obtain a formula for the k k -bonacci numbers.

Theorem 2.1

For

Proof.

To complete the proof, we will show that for

the recurrence relation of (

For each tiling of the ruler of length

That rightmost tile has a length

The part of the

Corollary 3.1
.

For
k\geq 1
,
n\geq 0
,
and

{\lfloor n/k\rfloor}\geq m\geq{\lfloor n/(k+1)\rfloor}

\sum_{i=0}^{n}f_{i}^{(k)}=\sum_{j=0}^{m}(-1)^{j}\binom{n-jk}{j}2^{n-j(k+1)}\,.

(3.1)

Proof.

The summands that are on the right-hand side of
(
3.1
), but not on the right-hand side of
(
1.2
), are those for which

{\lfloor n/(k+1)\rfloor}<j\leq{\lfloor n/k\rfloor}

holds.
Note that the left-hand inequality is
equivalent to
n-jk<j

is equivalent to

equals

.

Corollary 3.2

For

Proof.

For

\displaystyle f_{n}^{(k)}

\displaystyle=\sum_{j=0}^{q}(-1)^{j}\binom{n-jk}{j}2^{n-j(k+1)}-\sum_{j=0}^{q}(-1)^{j}\binom{(n-1)-jk}{j}2^{(n-1)-j(k+1)}
