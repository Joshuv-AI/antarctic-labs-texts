Formation and construction of a multidimensional shock wave for the first order hyperbolic conservation law with smooth initial data
Yin Huicheng, Zhu Lu 1. School of Mathematical Sciences and Institute of Mathematical Sciences,Nanjing Normal University, Nanjing, 210023, China. 2. College of Science, Hohai University, Nanjing, 210098, China

Abstract
In this paper, the problem on formation and construction of a multidimensional shock wave is studied for the first order conservation law ∂ t u + ∂ x F ⁡ ( u ) + ∂ y G ⁡ ( u ) = 0 \partial_{t}u+\partial_{x}F(u)+\partial_{y}G(u)=0 with smooth initial data u 0 ​ ( x , y ) u_{0}(x,y) . It is well-known that the smooth solution u u will blow up on the time T ∗ = − 1 min ⁡ H ⁡ ( ξ , η ) T^{*}=-\frac{1}{\min{H(\xi,\eta)}} when min ⁡ H ⁡ ( ξ , η ) < 0 \min{H(\xi,\eta})<0 holds for H ⁡ ( ξ , η ) = ∂ ξ ( F ′ ​ ( u 0 ​ ( ξ , η ) ) ) + ∂ η ( G ′ ​ ( u 0 ​ ( ξ , η ) ) ) H(\xi,\eta)=\partial_{\xi}(F^{\prime}(u_{0}(\xi,\eta)))+\partial_{\eta}(G^{\prime}(u_{0}(\xi,\eta))) , more precisely, only the first order derivatives ∇ t , x , y u \nabla_{t,x,y}u blow up on t = T ∗ t=T^{*} meanwhile u u itself is still continuous until t = T ∗ t=T^{*} . Under the generic nondegenerate condition of H ⁡ ( ξ , η ) H(\xi,\eta) , we construct a local weak entropy solution u u for t ≥ T ∗ t\geq T^{*} which is not uniformly Lipschitz continuous on two sides of a shock surface Σ \Sigma . The strength of the constructed shock is zero on the initial blowup curve Γ \Gamma and then gradually increases for t > T ∗ t>T^{*} . Additionally, in the neighbourhood of Γ \Gamma , some detailed and precise descriptions on the singularities of solution u u are given.
