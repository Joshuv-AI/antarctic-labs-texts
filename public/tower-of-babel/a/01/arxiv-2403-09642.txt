Abstract
The prime-counting function π ⁡ ( x ) \pi(x) which returns the number of primes smaller or equal to a given number is a topic of interest in number theory. An algorithm based on a cyclic group isomorphic to Z / n ​ Z Z/nZ , the so-called Z Z -functions, was proposed in view to outperform its pieers. The approach suggests a time complexity 𝒪 ⁡ ( x 1 / 2 ) \mathcal{O}(x^{1/2}) in agreement with optimality of a 2-D squared adaptive-recursive algorithm. The present work is a presentation of various approaches as ascending factorization, the main sequence of odd numbers and partial sequences, T-series, counting function of prime composites, Z Z -modular forms and combinatorial aspects.
