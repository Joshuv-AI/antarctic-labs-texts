Chetan Balwe, Amit Hogadi, Rakesh Pawar

Abstract
. The definition of Milnor-Witt cycle modules in [ 5 ] can easily be adapted over general regular base schemes. However, there are simple examples (see ( 2.9 )) to show that Gersten complex fails to be exact for cycle modules in general if the base is not a field. The goal of this article is to show that, for a restricted class of Milnor-Witt cycle modules over an excellent DVR satisfying an extra axiom, called here as R5, the expected properties of exactness of Gersten complex and 𝔸 1 \mathbb{A}^{1} -invariance hold. Moreover R5 is vacuously satisfied when the base is a field and it is also satisfied by K MW {K}^{\rm MW} over any base. As a corollary, we obtain the strict 𝔸 1 \mathbb{A}^{1} -invariance and the exactness of Gersten complex for K MW {K}^{\rm MW} over an excellent DVR.

1.
Introduction

Rost’s theory of cycle modules was generalized to Milnor-Witt cycle modules (or MW-cycle modules) by N. Feld in
[
5
]
. With minor modifications and verifications, this definition makes sense as it is, over a regular excellent base scheme.

Over a field, an MW-cycle module
M
satisfies two additional properties (see
[
5
, 8.1, 9.1]
) implied by the axioms, viz. local acylicity and homotopy invariance. The definition of a cycle module makes it possible to write a Gersten complex for it over any scheme equipped with a graded line bundle. Local acyclicity is a consequence of exactness of this Gersten complex in positive degrees.
For a MW-cycle module over a positive dimensional base scheme, the Gersten complex may not be exact (see Example
2.9
) in positive degrees. We introduce an additional axiom, called R5, to remedy this situation. The axiom R5 says (see Definition
2.13
) that for a field
F
and finitely many horizontal valuations (see Definition
2.1
)
v_{1},..,v_{n}
, the sum of residue morphisms

M(F,*)\xrightarrow{\sum_{i=1}^{n}\partial_{v_{i}}}\bigoplus_{i=1}^{n}M(\kappa(v_{i}),*)

is surjective.

Given a cycle module
M
and a graded line bundle
\mathcal{L}
on a scheme
X
, one has a naturally defined Nisnevich sheaf,
M(X,\mathcal{L})
(see (
2.6
)). The following is one of the main results of this paper:

Theorem 1.1

(Local acyclicity)
.

Let
S
be the spectrum of an excellent DVR. Let
M
be a MW-cycle module on
S
satisfying the property R5 (see Definition
2.13
).
Let
X
be an essentially smooth semi-local
S
-scheme and
\mathcal{L}
be a graded line bundle on
X
, then the Gersten complex
C^{\bullet}(X,M,\mathcal{L})
is exact in degrees
\geq 1
.
In particular,

H^{i}_{Nis}(X,M(X,\mathcal{L}))=0\ \forall\ i>0.

The requirement for this additional axiom R5 can be justified as follows. Whenever the field
F
appearing in the condition R5 is the function field of a smooth
S
-scheme, R5 is exactly equivalent to the exactness of Gersten complex for a suitable semi-local one dimensional scheme. In particular, R5 is motivated from a property always satisfied by MW-cycle modules over a perfect field (see Proposition
2.11
for an elementary argument). Theorem
1.1
may be loosely paraphrased by saying that exactness of Gersten complex for general semi-local essentially smooth schemes over a DVR follows from local acyclicity in the one-dimensional case. However, this should be taken with the caveat that R5 only refers to horizontal valuations and it is applied to fields
F
which are not necessarily function fields of smooth schemes. The strategy of the proof is motivated by
[
13
, Theorem 3.1]
, which is similar to the original argument in
[
15
, §6]
, and is also used in
[
5
, 8.1]
.

Remark 1.2
.

Note that the vanishing in Theorem
1.1
implies that the Zariski and the Nisnevich cohomology groups of
M(X,\mathcal{L})
agree.

In the last section, we show (see Theorem
1.3
) that strict
\mathbb{A}^{1}
-invariance holds for MW-cycle modules satisfying R5.

Theorem 1.3
.

Let
X
be a smooth
S
-scheme. Then for all
i\geq 0
,

H^{i}_{Nis}(X,M(X,\mathcal{L}))\simeq H^{i}_{Nis}(\mathbb{A}^{1}_{X},M(\mathbb{A}^{1}_{X},\mathcal{L}\otimes(\omega_{\mathbb{A}^{1}_{X}/X}^{\vee},-1))).

The above theorem is deduced from local-acyclicity and
\mathbb{A}^{1}
-invariance in the field case using a spectral sequence argument. The main example of a MW-cycle module is
{K}^{\rm MW}
itself.

Theorem 1.4
.

Let
S
be a regular excellent scheme. Then

{K}^{\rm MW}
is a MW-cycle module over
S
satisfying the property R5.

As a corollary of the above results, we get

Corollary 1.5
.

Over an excellent DVR, the Milnor-Witt sheaves
K_{n}^{MW}
are strictly
\mathbb{A}^{1}
-invariant.

Remark 1.6
.

The entire argument of (
1.5
) and also analogues of Theorems
1.1
and
1.3
also work with MW-cycle modules replaced by cycle modules as defined by Rost using Milnor K-theory. In particular the arguments in this paper also show that the Milnor K-theory sheaves
K^{M}_{n}
, are strictly
\mathbb{A}^{1}
-invariant.
When
S
is the spectrum of a field
k
, (not necessarily perfect), the strict
\mathbb{A}^{1}
-invariance for
K_{n}^{M}
is proved in
[
15
, Proposition 8.6]
. When
k
is a perfect field, strict
\mathbb{A}^{1}
-invariance of
{K}^{\rm MW}_{n}
was shown in
[
14
, Theorem 5.38]
.
