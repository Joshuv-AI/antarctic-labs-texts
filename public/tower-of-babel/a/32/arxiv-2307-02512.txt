Application of the Deffuant model in money exchange
Hsin-Lun Li

Abstract
. A money transfer involves a buyer and a seller. A buyer buys goods or services from a seller. The money the buyer decreases is the same as that the seller increases. At each time step, a pair of socially connected agents are selected and transact in agreed money. We evolve the Deffuant model to a money exchange system and study circumstances under which asymptotic stability holds, or equal wealth can be achieved.

Introduction

The Deffuant model is one of the popular models in opinion dynamics. The original Deffuant model consists of a finite number of agents whose opinion is a number in

\displaystyle\begin{aligned} &x_{i}(t+1)=x_{i}(t)+\mu(x_{j}(t)-x_{i}(t))\mathbbm{1}\{|x_{i}(t)-x_{j}(t)|\leq\epsilon\}\\
&x_{j}(t+1)=x_{j}(t)+\mu(x_{i}(t)-x_{j}(t))\mathbbm{1}\{|x_{i}(t)-x_{j}(t)|\leq\epsilon\}\end{aligned}

Main results

It turns out that equal wealth can be achieved if all agents are willing and possible to transact and the social graph is connected infinitely many times. Namely, all agents eventually have the average money of the total money in the system.

Theorem 3
.

Assume that
\sup_{t\geq 0}|\mu(t)-1/2|<1/2

\bigcup_{a\in S}a\supset\binom{[n]}{2}

Lemma 5

Proof.

Let

Clearly,

\displaystyle(m_{p}-m_{q})^{2}-(m_{p}^{\star}-m_{q}^{\star})^{2}=[1-(1-2\mu)^{2}](m_{p}-m_{q})^{2}

\displaystyle\hskip 56.9055pt=(2-2\mu)2\mu/\mu^{2}(m_{p}-m_{p}^{\star})^{2}=4\frac{(1-\mu)}{\mu}(m_{p}-m_{p}^{\star})^{2},

\displaystyle(m_{p}-m_{i})^{2}-(m_{p}^{\star}-m_{i})^{2}=(m_{p}-m_{p}^{\star})^{2}+2(m_{p}-m_{p}^{\star})(m_{p}^{\star}-m_{i}),

\displaystyle(m_{q}-m_{i})^{2}-(m_{q}^{\star}-m_{i})^{2}=(m_{q}-m_{q}^{\star})^{2}+2(m_{q}-m_{q}^{\star})(m_{q}^{\star}-m_{i}).

Since

\displaystyle(m_{p}-m_{i})^{2}-(m_{p}^{\star}-m_{i})^{2}+(m_{q}-m_{i})^{2}-(m_{q}^{\star}-m_{i})^{2}

\displaystyle\hskip 28.45274pt=2(m_{p}-m_{p}^{\star})^{2}+2(m_{p}-m_{p}^{\star})(m_{p}^{\star}-m_{q}^{\star})

\displaystyle\hskip 28.45274pt=[2+2(1-2\mu)/\mu](m_{p}-m_{p}^{\star})^{2}=2(\frac{1}{\mu}-1)(m_{p}-m_{p}^{\star})^{2}.

In particular,

\displaystyle Z(t)-Z(t+1)=\sum_{i,j\in[n]}[(m_{i}-m_{j})^{2}-(m_{i}^{\star}-m_{j}^{\star})^{2}]

\displaystyle\hskip 14.22636pt=2\bigg\{(m_{p}-m_{q})^{2}-(m_{p}^{\star}-m_{q}^{\star})^{2}

\displaystyle\hskip 14.22636pt=2\big[4(\frac{1}{\mu}-1)+2(n-2)(\frac{1}{\mu}-1)\big](m_{p}-m_{p}^{\star})^{2}=4n(\frac{1}{\mu}-1)(m_{p}-m_{p}^{\star})^{2}.
