Abstract

A ( w , g , m ) (w,g;m) -comb is a tile composed of m m rectangular sub-tiles of dimensions w × 1 w\times 1 separated by gaps of width g g . The tiling of an
n

board divided into
n

concerning powers of the Fibonacci numbers
[

4

In

-fences where

Theorem 1.1

In Section

Although various methods have been devised to find expressions for
permanents of (0,1) Toeplitz matrices (or the equivalent problem of
enumerating the number of strongly restricted permutations)

[
7
,
14
,
15
,

, our results concerning

P_{n}^{\{-2,M-1,m\}}
appear from the literature to be only the second
explicit connection, after that of certain cases of

techniques used previously to obtain bijective proofs of identities
using fences can be modified for comb tilings and applied to obtain
further identities concerning other sequences in a straightforward
manner.
