On the perverse filtration of the moduli spaces of 1-dimensional sheaves on ℙ 2 \mathbb{P}^{2} and P=C conjecture. Thanks: The author is supported by NSFC 21022107.
Yao Yuan

Abstract
. Let M ⁡ ( d , χ ) M(d,\chi) be the moduli space of semistable 1-dimensional sheaves supported at curves of degree d d on ℙ 2 \mathbb{P}^{2} , with Euler characteristic χ \chi . We have the Hilbert-Chow morphism π : M ⁡ ( d , χ ) → | d ​ H | \pi:M(d,\chi)\rightarrow|dH| sending each sheaf to its support. We study the perverse filtration on H ∗ ​ ( M ​ ( d , χ ) , ℚ ) H^{*}(M(d,\chi),\mathbb{Q}) via map π \pi , especially the “P=C” conjecture posed by Kononov-Pi-Shen. We show that “P=C” conjecture holds for H ∗ ≤ 4 ​ ( M ⁡ ( d , χ ) , ℚ ) H^{*\leq 4}(M(d,\chi),\mathbb{Q}) for any d ≥ 4 d\geq 4 , ( d , χ ) = 1 (d,\chi)=1 . The main strategy is to relate M ⁡ ( d , χ ) M(d,\chi) to the Hilbert scheme S [ n ] S^{[n]} of n n -points and transfer the problem to some properties on H ∗ ​ ( S [ n ] , ℚ ) H^{*}(S^{[n]},\mathbb{Q}) . We use induction on n n to achieve the desired properties. Our proof involves some complicated calculations. Keywords: Perverse filtrations, Chern classes, P=C conjecture, Moduli spaces of sheaves, Hilbert schemes of points, intersection theory.

Introduction.

1.1.
Decomposition theorem and Perverse filtration.

We work over the complex numbers
\mathbb{C}
.

Let
f:X\rightarrow Y
be a proper morphism between two nonsingular quasi-projective varieties with
\dim X=n_{X},\dim Y=n_{Y}
. For simplicity, we assume moreover every fiber of
f
has the same dimension
r=n_{X}-n_{Y}
. By the well-known Decomposition theorem over
\mathbb{C}
(Théorèmes 6.25, 6.2.10 in
[
2
]
) we have

Rf_{*}\mathbb{Q}_{X}[n_{X}]\cong\bigoplus_{i}\prescript{p}{}{\mathcal{H}}^{i}(Rf_{*}\mathbb{Q}_{X}[n_{X}])[-i],

where
\prescript{p}{}{\mathcal{H}}^{i}(Rf_{*}\mathbb{Q}_{X}[n_{X}])
are all semisimple perverse sheaves on
Y
. Define

\prescript{p}{}{\tau}_{\leq i}(R\pi_{*}\mathbb{Q}_{X}[n_{X}])):=\bigoplus_{j\leq i}\prescript{p}{}{\mathcal{H}}^{j}(Rf_{*}\mathbb{Q}_{X}[n_{X}])[-i]

For each
m\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}
we have the
perverse filtration
(with respect to
f
)
P_{\bullet}
on the cohomology group
H^{m}(X,\mathbb{Q})
as follows

P_{0}H^{m}(X,\mathbb{Q})\subset P_{1}H^{m}(X,\mathbb{Q})\subset\cdots\subset P_{2r}H^{m}(X,\mathbb{Q})=H^{m}(X,\mathbb{Q}),

where

P_{i}H^{m}(X,\mathbb{Q}):=\operatorname{Im}\left\{H^{m-n_{X}}(Y,\prescript{p}{}{\tau}_{\leq i}(R\pi_{*}\mathbb{Q}_{X}[n_{X}]))\rightarrow H^{m}(X,\mathbb{Q})\right\}.

Hence we naturally have

P_{i}H^{m}(X,\mathbb{Q})\cong\bigoplus_{j\leq i}\mathbb{H}^{m-n_{X}-j}(Y,\prescript{p}{}{\mathcal{H}}^{i}(Rf_{*}\mathbb{Q}_{X}[n_{X}])).

We say that a class
\gamma\in H^{*}(X,\mathbb{Q})
has
perversity

k
if
\gamma\in P_{k}H^{*}(X,\mathbb{Q})\setminus P_{k-1}H^{*}(X,\mathbb{Q})
. Perverse filtration in general is very complicated to describe since we know very few on the perverse truncation functor. However if
Y
is projective, denote by
\eta
the pullback of some ample divisor on
Y
to
X
, then we have the following description due to de Cataldo-Migliorini in
[
6
, Proposition 5.2.4]
.

Proposition 1.1
.

We have

P_{k}H^{m}(X,\mathbb{Q})=\sum_{i\geq 1}\left(\operatorname{Ker}(\eta^{n_{Y}+k+i-m})\cap\operatorname{Im}(\eta^{i-1})\right)\cap H^{m}(X,\mathbb{Q}).

In particular for any
\gamma\in H^{*}(X,\mathbb{Q})
of perversity
k
,
\eta^{n}\gamma\in H^{*+2n}(M(d,\chi),\mathbb{Q})
is either zero or of perversity
k
for any
n\in\mathbb{Z}_{>0}
.

1.2.
Motivation and the main result.

We consider the following two kinds of moduli space
\mathcal{M}^{H}_{S}(L,\chi)
and
\mathcal{M}_{C_{0},D}^{Higgs}(n,\chi)
as follows.

(A)

\mathcal{M}^{H}_{X}(L,\chi)
parametrizes semistable sheaves
\mathcal{F}
with respect to the polarization
H
on a projective surface
S
, which are supported on curves in the linear system
|L|
and of Euler characteristic
\chi
. We have a Hilbert-Chow morphism

(1.1)

\pi_{L,\chi}:\mathcal{M}^{H}_{S}(L,\chi)\rightarrow|L|,~~\mathcal{F}\mapsto\text{supp}(\mathcal{F}).

(B)

\mathcal{M}_{C_{0},D}^{Higgs}(n,\chi)
parametrizes semistable Higgs bundles
(\mathcal{E},\Theta)
with respect to the effective divisor
D
on a smooth curve
C_{0}
with
\mathcal{E}
rank
n
and Euler characteristic
\chi
. We have the Hitchin fibration

(1.2)

\pi_{D,\chi}:\mathcal{M}_{C_{0},D}^{Higgs}(n,\chi)\rightarrow\bigoplus_{i=1}^{n}H^{0}(C_{0},\mathcal{O}_{C_{0}}(iD)),

sending
(\mathcal{E},\Theta)
to the coefficients
(-\text{tr}(\Theta),\cdots,(-1)^{n}\det(\Theta))
of the characteristic polynomials
\text{char}(\Theta)
of the morphism
\Theta:\mathcal{E}\rightarrow\mathcal{E}(D)
.

Both
\pi_{L,\chi}
and
\pi_{D,\chi}
are proper maps. Under suitable assumptions they are also maps between nonsingular quasiprojective varieties with fibers of the same dimensions.

When the fibration in (A) or (B) is Lagragian, the perverse filtration can have a nice coincidence with some weight filtration: we have the so called “P=W” property conjectured in
[
4
]
, proved in
[
5
]
for case (A) with
S
an abelian surface, in
[
10
]
and
[
12
]
independently for case (B) with
D
the canonical divisor of
C_{0}
. In particular, in these cases the perverse filtration is
multiplicative
, i.e. for any
k_{1},k_{2},m_{1},m_{2}

(1.3)

P_{k_{1}}H^{m_{1}}(X,\mathbb{Q})\cdot P_{k_{2}}H^{m_{2}}(X,\mathbb{Q})\subset P_{k_{1}+k_{2}}H^{m_{1}+m_{2}}(X,\mathbb{Q}),

where
\cdot
denotes the cup product on
H^{*}(X,\mathbb{Q})
.

Now in (A) let
S=\mathbb{P}^{2}
, then the Hilbert-Chow morphism is not Lagrangian. It is interesting to ask in this case whether there is any analog of “P=W” phenomenon, and whether the perverse filtration is multiplicative. Let
H
be the hyperplane class in
\mathbb{P}^{2}
. Let
M(d,\chi)
be the moduli space of stable sheaves supported at curves in
|dH|
and with Euler characteristic
\chi
.
M(d,\chi)
is smooth since
\mathbb{P}^{2}
is Fano. We also ask
M(d,\chi)
to be projective
\Leftrightarrow(d,\chi)=1
.

By Pi-Shen’s work in
[
15
]
, a minimal set of generators of
H^{*}(M(d,\chi),\mathbb{Q})
can be chosen as

(1.4)

\Sigma:=\{c_{0}(2),c_{2}(0),c_{k}(0),c_{k-1}(1),c_{k-2}(2),k\in{3,\cdots,d-1}\}.

In
[
11
]
, Kononov-Pi-Shen call the subspace

H^{*\leq 2d-4}(M(d,\chi),\mathbb{Q})\subset H^{*}(M(d,\chi),\mathbb{Q})

the
free part
of the cohomology and they define the
Chern filtration
of the free part

C_{0}H^{*\leq 2d-4}(M(d,\chi),\mathbb{Q})\subset C_{1}H^{*\leq 2d-4}(M(d,\chi),\mathbb{Q})\subset\cdots\subset H^{*\leq 2d-4}(M(d,\chi),\mathbb{Q}),

where the
k
-th piece
C_{k}H^{*\leq 2d-4}
is defined as the span of all monomials

\prod_{i=1}^{s}c_{k_{i}}(j_{i})\in H^{*\leq 2d-4}(M(d,\chi),\mathbb{Q}),\text{ and }\sum_{i=1}^{s}k_{i}\leq k,

where
c_{k_{i}}(j_{i})
are generators given in (
1.4
).

Kononov-Pi-Shen pose the following conjecture which is also called “P=C” conjecture.

Conjecture 1.2

(Conjecture 0.3 in
[
11
]
)
.

For
d\geq 3
, we have

P_{k}H^{*\leq 2d-4}(M(d,\chi),\mathbb{Q})=C_{k}H^{*\leq 2d-4}(M(d,\chi),\mathbb{Q}).

Conjecture
1.2
can be viewed as an analog of “P=W” conjecture for Lagragian fibration cases. Our result is as follows.

Theorem 1.3

(Corollary
2.10
)
.

For any
d\geq 4
and
\chi
co-prime to
d
, we have

P_{k}H^{*\leq 4}(M(d,\chi),\mathbb{Q})=C_{k}H^{*\leq 4}(M(d,\chi),\mathbb{Q}).

Our strategy to prove Theorem

is to relate the moduli space
M(d,\chi)
to the Hilbert scheme
S^{[n]}
of
n
-points on
S
, then we transfer the perversity of those generators
c_{k}(j)
to some intersection properties of some classes on
S^{[n]}
, and finally we do a lot of computations to show those properties.

Remark 1.4
.

In principle, following our strategy one can compute the perversity of more elements in
H^{*\leq 2d-4}(M(d,\chi),\mathbb{Q})
. But it may cost one huge amount of computations. Hence at this moment we just finish the case
H^{*\leq 4}(M(d,\chi),\mathbb{Q})
and leave others to the future study.

The plan of the paper.

We will introduce some notations and conventions in next subsection. In §2 we review in more details on the cohomology theory of

(2.6)

\displaystyle c^{\prime}_{k}(0)
