Two torsion, the endomorphism field, and the finitude of endomorphism algebras for a class of abelian varieties
Pip Goodman

Abstract
A conjecture attributed to Robert Coleman states there should only be finitely many isomorphism classes for endomorphism algebras of abelian varieties of fixed dimension over a fixed number field. In this paper, we establish new cases of this conjecture, given conditions on the 2-torsion field. In fact, for the abelian varieties considered, we provide an explicit list of possible endomorphism algebras. This list is then refined by studying the endomorphism field. In particular, we find conditions which guarantee it is contained in the 2-torsion field.

The case

Theorem 2.1

Suppose

L\nsubseteq K(A[2])

•

\Gal(E/\mathbb{Q})

•

\End(A)
is 2-maximal in
E
, then
2
is wildly ramified in
E/\mathbb{Q}
.

In particular, if
E/\mathbb{Q}
is Galois,
\End(A)
is a 2-maximal order and
2
is not wildly ramified, then

.

Proof.

If
\Gal(L/K)
acts faithfully on
\End(A)\otimes\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}
, then we may recuperate Lemma 2.1 in
[
Sil92
]
with
N=2
,
\Lambda=\End(A)
and
G
the image of
\Gal(L/K)
in
\Aut(\Lambda)
.
Following through the proofs of Propositions 2.2, 2.3 and Theorem 2.4 in
[
Sil92
]
shows

. Hence we may suppose the action of
\Gal(L/K)
on
\End(A)\otimes\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}
has a non-trivial element
\gamma
in its kernel.

Since

acts on

by

-linear automorphisms of
\End(A)
, we may view its image as a subgroup of
\Gal(E/\mathbb{Q})
.
Identify

with its image in
\Gal(E/\mathbb{Q})
.
Let

be the rank of

as a free

-module and view

as a (finite) subgroup of

The reduction map

is injective on finite groups and the kernel of

Assume now

is a 2-maximal order in
E
.
As

acts trivially on the order

, it also acts trivially on
\End(A)
modulo every prime above

Since

is 2-maximal, we find

∎

Let

Suppose

•

[L:K]

\End^{0}(A)
is a subfield of
\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_{p})

•

[L:K]

is isogenous over

\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_{p})
.

In particular there are only finitely many possibilities for
\End^{0}(A)
.

Proof.

Suppose

[
Goo21
]
,

is isogenous over
\bar{\mathbb{Q}}
to the power of an absolutely simple abelian variety with complex multiplication by a proper subfield
F

Hence we may suppose

The criterion of Néron-Ogg-Shafarevich implies that the image of the inertia group

Furthermore, as the trace of

The reduction of

This allows us to apply

As

Following

Owing in part to the fact
