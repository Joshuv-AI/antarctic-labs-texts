1.
Introduction

Let
M
be a smooth compact surface. The group of diffeomorphisms
\mathcal{D}(M)
of
M
acts on the space of smooth functions
C^{\infty}(M)
by the rule

C^{\infty}(M)\times\mathcal{D}(M)\to C^{\infty}(M),\quad(f,h)=f\circ h.

(1.1)

The set

\mathcal{S}(f)=\{h\in\mathcal{D}(M)\;|\;f\circ h=f\}
is called the

of the function

under action (
1.1
). Endow

and
\mathcal{D}(M)
with the corresponding Whitney topologies. The topology on
\mathcal{D}(M)
induces a certain topology on the stabilizer
\mathcal{S}(f).

Let
\mathcal{F}(M)\subset C^{\infty}(M,\mathbb{R})
be the set of smooth functions satisfying the following two conditions:

(B)

the function
f
takes a constant value at each connected component of
\partial M
, and all critical points of
f
belong to the interior of

(P)

for each critical point

of

the germ
(f,x)
of

at

is smoothly equivalent to some homogeneous polynomial
f_{x}:\mathbb{R}^{2}\to\mathbb{R}
without multiple linear factors.

It is well-known that each homogeneous polynomial
f_{x}:\mathbb{R}^{2}\to\mathbb{R}
splits into a product of linear and irreducible over
\mathbb{R}
quadratic factors.
Condition (P) means that

f_{x}=\prod_{i=1}^{n}L_{i}\cdot\prod_{j=1}^{m}Q_{j}^{q_{j}},

(1.2)

where

L_{i}(x,y)=a_{i}x+b_{i}y
is a linear form,
Q_{j}=c_{j}x^{2}+d_{j}xy+e_{j}y^{2}
is an irreducible quadratic form such that
L_{i}/L_{i^{\prime}}\neq\mathrm{const}
for
i\neq i^{\prime}
, and
Q_{j}/Q_{j^{\prime}}\neq\mathrm{const}
for
j\neq j^{\prime}
.
So, if
\deg f_{x}\geq 2
, then
0
is an isolated critical point of
f_{x}.

Recall that if

is a germ of

-function such that

is an isolated critical point of

,
then there is a germ of a homeomorphism

such that

{f}_{x}\circ h(z)=\begin{cases}\pm|z|^{2},&\text{if $0$ is a local extremum, \cite[cite]{[\@@bibref{}{Danser}{}{}]}},\\
\mathrm{Re}(z^{n}),&\text{for some $n\in\mathbb{N}$ otherwise, \cite[cite]{[\@@bibref{}{Prishlyak}{}{}]}}.\end{cases}

If

is not a local extreme, then the number

does not depend of a particular choice of

In this case the point

will be called a

-saddle
, or simply an

-saddle

The number

Let

(C)

Let

If

Let

, and

be a diffeomorphism such that
\phi(h)=g.
Define the map

\tau(g,e)=h(e),\quad e\in\Xi.

We claim that this definition does not depend of a particular choice of such

Let

be diffeomorphisms such that
\phi(h_{1})=\phi(h_{2}).
Then
\phi(h_{1}\circ h_{2}^{-1})=1_{H},
where

be the unit of

By definition of the unit

, we have

for each

-dimensional component

of
\Xi.
Then, by condition (C),

So, the map

is well-defined.
It is easy to see that

where
1_{H}
is the unit of
H,
and

and

Thus

is an

-action on
\Xi.

Suppose

for some

so the
