α \alpha knockout reaction as a new probe for α \alpha formation in α \alpha -decay nuclei
Kazuki Yoshida, Junki Tanaka

Abstract
Background The α \alpha -decay phenomenon has been studied for more than a century. Its mechanism is simply explained by two factors, the α \alpha formation probability and the penetration process through the potential barrier. Purpose As an alternative to the α \alpha -decay lifetime measurement, we propose the proton-induced α \alpha knockout reaction, ( p p , p ​ α p\alpha ), as a new probe for the surface α \alpha formation probability of α \alpha -decay nuclei. Method The 210,212 Po( p p , p ​ α p\alpha ) 206,208 Pb reaction is described by the distorted-wave impulse approximation framework. Results It is shown that the 212 Po/ 210 Po ratio of the α \alpha knockout cross sections agrees with that of the surface α \alpha formation probabilities determined by lifetime measurements. Conclusions It was confirmed that the ( p p , p ​ α p\alpha ) reaction cross sections correspond to the α \alpha formation probability of the nuclear surface. The result implies that the ( p p , p ​ α p\alpha ) cross section is a direct probe for the surface α \alpha formation probability, which is an essential quantity for complete understanding of the α \alpha -decay phenomenon.
