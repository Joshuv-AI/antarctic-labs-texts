Fields of U U -invariants of matrix tuples
A.N. Panov

Abstract
The general linear group GL ⁡ ( n ) {\mathrm{GL}}(n) acts on the direct sum of m m copies of Mat ⁡ ( n ) {\mathrm{Mat}}(n) by the adjoint action. The action of GL ⁡ ( n ) {\mathrm{GL}}(n) induces the action of the unitriangular subgroup U U . We present the system of free generators of the field of U U -invariants.

Introduction

One of the classical problems of the theory of invariants is the problem of description of invariants of matrix tuples. Let
K
be an arbitrary field. Let
{\mathrm{Mat}}(n)
stand for the linear space of
(n\times n)
-matrices with entries in the field
K
and
{\mathrm{GL}}(n)
be the general linear group of order
n
.
Let us consider the linear space

{\mathcal{H}}={\mathrm{Mat}}(n)\oplus\ldots\oplus{\mathrm{Mat}}(n)

of
m
-tuples of
{\mathrm{Mat}}(n)
. The group
{\mathrm{GL}}(n)
acts on
{\mathcal{H}}
by the formula

{\mathrm{Ad}}_{g}(X_{1},\ldots,X_{m})=(gX_{1}g^{-1},\ldots,gX_{m}g^{-1}),

where

g\in{\mathrm{GL}}(n)

and
X_{1},\ldots,X_{m}\in{\mathrm{Mat}}(n)
.
The action of the group
{\mathrm{GL}}(n)
on
{\mathcal{H}}
defines the representation

\rho(g)f(X_{1},\ldots,X_{m})=f(g^{-1}X_{1}g,\ldots,g^{-1}X_{m}g)

of the group
{\mathrm{GL}}(n)
in the space of regular functions
K[{\mathcal{H}}]
. This representation is extended to the action of
{\mathrm{GL}}(n)
on the field of rational functions
K({\mathcal{H}})
.
For a given subgroup
G\subseteq GL(n)
, the problem is to describe the algebra (respectively, the field) of invariants with respect to the action of
G
on
{\mathcal{H}}
.

In the case
G={\mathrm{GL}}(n)
(or
G={\mathrm{SL}}(n)
), this problem is solved in the framework of the classical theory of invariants in tensors (see
[
1
,
2
,
3
]
). The algebra of
{\mathrm{GL}}(n)
-invariants is generated by the system of polynomials
\mathrm{Tr}(A_{i_{1}}\cdots A_{i_{p}})
, where
1\leqslant i_{1},\ldots,i_{p}\leqslant m
.

The group
{\mathrm{GL}}(n)
contains the subgroup of unitriangular matrices
U={\mathrm{UT}}(n)
, which consists of the upper triangular matrices with ones on the diagonal. We consider the algebra of
U
-invariants
K[{\mathcal{H}}]^{U}
and the field of
U
-invariants
K({\mathcal{H}})^{U}
.

Little is known about the structure of the algebra of invariants
K[{\mathcal{H}}]^{U}
. It follows from
[
1
, Theorem 3.13]
that the algebra
K[{\mathcal{H}}]^{U}
is finitely generated. For
K[{\mathcal{H}}]^{U}
, the problem of construction of system of generators with their relations is an unsoled problem even for
m=1
.

The structure of the field
K({\mathcal{H}})^{U}
is much simpler. The group
U
acts on
{\mathcal{H}}
by unipotent transformations. It implies that

1) the field
K({\mathcal{H}})^{U}
is rational
[
4
]
, i.e. it is a pure transcendental extension over the main field
K
,

2) the field
K({\mathcal{H}})^{U}
is a field of fractions of the algebra of invariants
K[{\mathcal{H}}]^{U}

[
1
, Theorem 3.3]
.

The goal of this paper is to construct a system of free generators of the field of
U
-invariants
K({\mathcal{H}})^{U}
.
For the adjoint action of
U
on
{\mathrm{Mat}}(n)
(the case
m=1
), the system generators of the field of
U
-invariants is presented in Theorem
2
.
This system of generators is not unique. The another system of free generators of
K({\mathrm{Mat}}(n))^{U}
was constructed in
[
5
,
6
]
.

For an arbitrary
m
, the system of free generators of
K({\mathcal{H}})^{U}
is presented in Theorem
3
.
We apply invariants
\{P_{ik}(X,Y)\}
from (
1
). The similar invariants were earlier used in the papers
[
7
,
8
]
.
