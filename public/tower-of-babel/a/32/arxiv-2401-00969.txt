Weaving continuous generalized frames for operators
Hafida Massit 1 , Mohamed Rossafi 2 and Choonkil Park 3∗

Abstract
. Recently, Bemrose et al. [ 2 ] developed a theory of weaving frames, which was motivated by a problem regarding distributed signal processing. In this present article, we introduce the atomic g g -system and we generalize some of the known results in continuous L L -frames, weaving continuous and weaving continuous g g -frames, also we study weaving continuous L L - g g -frames in Hilbert spaces. Moreover, we study the behaviour continuous L L - g g -frames under some perturbations, and we show that approximate L L -duals are stable under small perturbation and that it is possible to remove some elements of a woven continuous L L - g g -frame and still have a woven continuous L L - g g -frame.

\displaystyle=S_{\chi}^{\mathcal{J}}+S_{\xi}^{\mathcal{J}^{C}}+S_{\chi^{\prime}}^{\mathcal{J}}+S_{\xi^{\prime}}^{\mathcal{J}^{C}}+S_{\chi,\chi^{\prime}}^{\mathcal{J}}+S_{\chi^{\prime},\chi}^{\mathcal{J}}+S_{\xi,\xi^{\prime}}^{\mathcal{J}^{C}}+S_{\xi^{\prime},\xi}^{\mathcal{J}^{C}}

\displaystyle\geq ALL^{\ast}+A^{\prime}LL^{\ast}

\displaystyle=(A+A^{\prime})LL^{\ast}.

Also,
\{\chi_{\varsigma}+\chi^{\prime}_{\varsigma}\}_{\varsigma\in\mathcal{J}}\cup\{\xi_{\varsigma}+\xi^{\prime}_{\varsigma}\}_{\varsigma\in\mathcal{J}^{C}}
is a
g-
Bessel sequence.
∎

Definition 2.9
.

Let
\chi=\{\chi_{\varsigma}\}_{\varsigma\in\mathfrak{A}}
and
\xi=\{\xi_{\varsigma}\}_{\varsigma\in\mathfrak{A}}
be
g-
Bessel sequences.

(1)

\xi
is called an
L-
dual of
\chi
if for each
f\in\mathcal{H}
, we have
Lf=S_{\chi,\xi}(f)=\int_{\mathfrak{A}}\xi_{\varsigma}^{\ast}\chi_{\varsigma}(f)d\mu(\varsigma)
.

(2)

\xi
is called an approximate
L-
dual of
\chi
if there exists
0<t<1
such that for every
f\in\mathcal{H}

\|L(f)-S_{\chi,\xi}(f)\|\leq t\|L(f)\|.

Proposition 2.10
.

Let
\xi=\{\xi_{\varsigma}\}_{\varsigma\in\mathfrak{A}}
be an approximate
L-
dual of
\chi
.
Then
\chi
has an
L-
dual and every element
L(f)
of
R(L)
can be reconstructed from
\{\xi_{\varsigma}^{\ast}\circ\chi_{\varsigma}(f)\}_{\varsigma\in\mathfrak{A}}
.

Proof.

Since
\xi
is an approximate
L-
dual of
\chi
, there exists
0<t<1
such that

(2.1)

\|L(f)-S_{\chi,\xi}(f)\|\leq t\|L(f)\|,\;\;f\in\mathcal{H}.

Then
S_{\chi,\xi}(f)=0\Leftrightarrow L(f)=0
. Therefore, we get

V:R(L)\Rightarrow R(S_{\xi,\chi}),

which is defined by

V(L(f))=S_{\xi,\chi}(f),\;\;\forall f\in\mathcal{H}.

Hence
V
is an injective bounded linear map. By (
2.1
), we obtain

(2.2)

\|L(f)-V(L(f))\|\leq t\|L(f)\|,\;\;f\in\mathcal{H}

and so, for every
f\in\mathcal{H}
,

(1-t)\|Lf\|\leq\|V(L(f))\|\leq(1+t)\|Lf\|.

Hence
V
has a closed range,
R(V)=R(S_{\xi,\chi})
and we can extend

V^{-1}:R(S_{\xi,\chi})\rightarrow R(L)
to
U:\mathcal{H}\rightarrow\mathcal{H}
by
U=V^{-1}\circ\pi_{R(L)}
, where
\pi_{R(L)}
is the orthogonal projection of
\mathcal{H}
onto
R(V)
. Then

L(f)=U\circ S_{\xi,\chi}(f)=\int_{\mathfrak{A}}(U\circ\xi_{\varsigma}^{\ast})\circ\chi_{\varsigma}(f)d\mu(\varsigma),\;\;f\in\mathcal{H}.

Thus
\{\xi_{\varsigma}\circ U^{\ast}\}_{\varsigma\in\mathfrak{A}}
is an
L-
dual of
\{\chi_{\varsigma}\}_{\varsigma\in\mathfrak{A}}
.
∎

Theorem 2.11
.

Let
\chi=\{\chi_{\varsigma}\in B(\mathcal{H},\mathcal{H_{\varsigma}})\}_{\varsigma\in\mathfrak{A}}
be a continuous
g-
Bessel sequence and
\Phi=\{\phi\in B(\mathcal{H},\mathcal{H_{\varsigma}})\}_{\varsigma\in\mathfrak{A}}
be an approximate
L-
dual (resp.
L-
dual) of
\chi
with
0<t<1
and upper bound
D
. If
\xi=\{\xi_{\varsigma}\}\in B(\mathcal{H},\mathcal{H_{\varsigma}})\}_{\varsigma\in\mathfrak{A}}
is a sequence such that

(\int_{\mathfrak{A}}\|(\chi_{\varsigma}-\xi_{\varsigma})(f)\|^{2}d\mu(\varsigma))^{\frac{1}{2}}\leq G\|Lf\|,\;\;f\in\mathcal{H},

and
\sqrt{DG}<1-t
(resp.
DG<1
), then
\Phi
is an approximate
L-
dual of
\xi
.

Proof.

Let
B
be an upper bound for
\chi
. Then we have

(\int_{\mathfrak{A}}\|\xi_{\varsigma}f\|^{2}d\mu(\varsigma))^{\frac{1}{2}}\leq(\int_{\mathfrak{A}}\|\chi_{\varsigma}f\|^{2}d\mu(\varsigma))^{\frac{1}{2}}+(\int_{\mathfrak{A}}\|\xi_{\varsigma}f-\chi_{\varsigma}f\|^{2}d\mu(\varsigma))^{\frac{1}{2}}\leq(\sqrt{B}+\sqrt{G}\|L\|)\|f\|.

This implies that
\xi
is a
g-
Bessel sequence.

We have

\|S_{\Phi,\chi}f-S_{\Phi,\xi}f\|\leq sup_{\|g\|=1}\{(\int_{\mathfrak{A}}\|(\chi_{\varsigma}-\xi_{\varsigma})(f)\|^{2}d\mu(\varsigma))^{\frac{1}{2}}(\int_{\mathfrak{A}}\|\phi g\|^{2}d\mu(\varsigma))^{\frac{1}{2}}\}\leq\sqrt{DG}\|Lf\|.

Then

\|Lf-S_{\Phi,\xi}f\|\leq\|Lf-S_{\Phi,\chi}f\|+\|S_{\Phi,\chi}f-S_{\Phi,\xi}f\|\leq(t+\sqrt{DG})\|Lf\|.

Since
t+\sqrt{DG}<1
, we obtain the result.
∎

Theorem 2.12
.

Let
\chi=\{\chi_{\varsigma}\in B(\mathcal{H},\mathcal{H_{\varsigma}})\}_{\varsigma\in\mathfrak{A}}
and
\xi=\{\xi_{\varsigma}\in B(\mathcal{H},\mathcal{H_{\varsigma}})\}_{\varsigma\in\mathfrak{A}}
be
(A,\;B)
woven continuous
L-g-
frames and let
K\in B(\mathcal{H})
and
K_{\varsigma},\;K^{\prime}_{\varsigma}\in B(\mathcal{H}_{\varsigma})
. If there exist
0<\alpha<\beta<\infty
such that for each
\varsigma\in\mathfrak{A}
and
F_{\varsigma}\in\mathcal{H_{\varsigma}}
,
\alpha\|F_{\varsigma}\|\leq\|K_{\varsigma}f\|
,
\|K^{\prime}_{\varsigma}F_{\varsigma}\|\leq\beta\|F_{\varsigma}\|
, then
\{\chi^{\prime}_{\varsigma}=K_{\varsigma}\chi_{\varsigma}K\}_{\varsigma\in\mathfrak{A}}
and
\{\xi_{\varsigma}=K^{\prime}_{\varsigma}\xi^{\prime}_{\varsigma}K\}_{\varsigma\in\mathfrak{A}}
are woven continuous
T^{\ast}L-g
frames with universal bounds
\alpha^{2}A
and
\beta^{2}B\|K\|^{2}
.

Moreover, if
KL^{\ast}=L^{\ast}K
,
\alpha\|F\|\leq\|KF\|
, then
\chi^{\prime}=\{\chi^{\prime}_{\varsigma}\in B(\mathcal{H},\mathcal{H_{\varsigma}})\}_{\varsigma\in\mathfrak{A}}
and
\xi^{\prime}=\{\xi^{\prime}_{\varsigma}\in B(\mathcal{H},\mathcal{H_{\varsigma}})\}_{\varsigma\in\mathfrak{A}}
are woven continuous
L-g-
frames with universal bounds
\alpha^{4}A
and
\beta^{2}B\|K\|^{2}
.

Proof.

For every
J\subset\mathfrak{A}
, we have

\displaystyle\int_{J}\|\chi^{\prime}_{\varsigma}F\|^{2}d\mu(\varsigma)+\int_{J^{C}}\|\xi^{\prime}_{\varsigma}F\|^{2}d\mu(\varsigma)

\displaystyle=\int_{J}\|K_{\varsigma}\chi_{\varsigma}KF\|^{2}d\mu(\varsigma)+\int_{J^{C}}\|K^{\prime}\xi^{\prime}_{\varsigma}KF\|^{2}d\mu(\varsigma)

\displaystyle\leq\int_{J}\|K_{\varsigma}\|^{2}\|\chi_{\varsigma}KF\|^{2}d\mu(\varsigma)+\int_{J^{C}}\|K^{\prime}\|^{2}\|\xi^{\prime}_{\varsigma}KF\|^{2}d\mu(\varsigma)

\displaystyle\leq\beta^{2}(\int_{J}\|\chi_{\varsigma}KF\|^{2}d\mu(\varsigma)+\int_{J^{C}}\|\xi^{\prime}_{\varsigma}KF\|^{2}d\mu(\varsigma))

\displaystyle\leq\beta^{2}B\|K\|^{2}\|F\|^{2},\;\;\forall F\in\mathcal{H}.

Similarly, we obtain

\int_{J}\|\chi^{\prime}_{\varsigma}F\|^{2}d\mu(\varsigma)+\int_{J^{C}}\|\xi^{\prime}_{\varsigma}F\|^{2}d\mu(\varsigma)\geq\alpha^{2}A\|(K^{\ast}L)^{\ast}F\|^{2},\;\;\;\forall F\in\mathcal{H}.

This completes the proof.
∎

Corollary 2.13
.

Let
\chi=\{\chi_{\varsigma}\in B(\mathcal{H},\mathcal{H_{\varsigma}})\}_{\varsigma\in\mathfrak{A}}
be an
L-g-
frame for
\mathcal{H}
and
K\in B(\mathcal{H})
be invertible. Then

(1)

\{\chi_{\varsigma}K\}_{\varsigma}
is a continuous
L-g-
frame, when
\xi L^{\ast}=L^{\ast}\xi.

(2)

\{K\chi_{\varsigma}\}_{\varsigma}
is a continuous
L-g-
frame, when
\mathcal{H}_{\varsigma}\subset\mathcal{H}
,
\forall\varsigma\in\mathfrak{A}.

Proof.

Let
\{\chi_{\varsigma}\}_{\varsigma\in\mathfrak{A}}
be a continuous
L-g-
frame with bounds
A
and
B
.

(1)
For every
f\in\mathcal{H}
, we have

\displaystyle\dfrac{A}{\|K^{-1}\|^{2}}\|L^{\ast}f\|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq A\|KL^{\ast}f\|^{2}=A\|L^{\ast}Kf\|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\int_{\mathfrak{A}}\|\chi_{\varsigma}Kf\|^{2}d\mu(\varsigma)

\displaystyle\leq B\|Kf\|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq B\|K\|^{2}\|f\|^{2}

(2)
We have

\displaystyle\dfrac{A}{\|K^{-1}\|^{2}}\|L^{\ast}f\|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\dfrac{1}{\|K^{-1}\|^{2}}\int_{\mathfrak{A}}\|K\chi_{\varsigma}f\|^{2}d\mu(\varsigma)

\displaystyle\leq\|K\|^{2}\int_{\mathfrak{A}}\|\chi_{\varsigma}f\|^{2}d\mu(\varsigma)

\displaystyle\leq B\|K\|^{2}\|f\|^{2},\;\;\forall f\in\mathcal{H}.

This completes the proof.
∎

Proposition 2.14
.

Let
\{\chi_{\varsigma}\}_{\varsigma\in\mathfrak{A}}

\int_{J}\|\chi_{\varsigma}f\|^{2}d\mu(\varsigma)\leq C\|L^{\ast}f\|^{2},\;\;\forall f\in\mathcal{H}

\{\chi_{\varsigma}\}_{\varsigma\in\mathcal{J}^{C}}

.

\displaystyle\int_{E}|\langle f,\chi_{\varsigma}\rangle|^{2}d\mu(\varsigma)+\int_{E^{C}}|\langle f,\xi_{\varsigma}\rangle|^{2}d\mu(\varsigma)

Theorem 2.17
.

Let
\{\chi_{\varsigma}\}_{\varsigma\in\mathfrak{A}}
and
\{\xi_{\varsigma}\}_{\varsigma\in\mathfrak{A}}
be woven continuous
L-g-
frames and
\{\chi^{\prime}_{\varsigma}\}_{\varsigma\in\mathfrak{A}}
,
\{\xi^{\prime}_{\varsigma}\}_{\varsigma\in\mathfrak{A}}
be
(\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2}),\;(\beta_{1},\beta_{{2}})
-perturbations of
\{\chi_{\varsigma}\}_{\varsigma\in\mathfrak{A}}
and
\{\xi_{\varsigma}\}_{\varsigma\in\mathfrak{A}}
, respectively. Then
\{\chi^{\prime}_{\varsigma}\}_{\varsigma\in\mathfrak{A}}
and

\{\xi^{\prime}_{\varsigma}\}_{\varsigma\in\mathfrak{A}}
are woven
L-g-
frames.

Proof.

For
f\in\mathcal{H}
, we have

\|\chi^{\prime}_{\varsigma}f\|-\|\chi_{\varsigma}f\|\leq\|\chi^{\prime}_{\varsigma}f-\chi_{\varsigma}f\|\leq\alpha_{1}\|\chi_{\varsigma}f\|+\alpha_{2}\|\chi^{\prime}_{\varsigma}f\|.

So

\dfrac{1-\alpha_{1}}{1+\alpha_{2}}\|\chi_{\varsigma}f\|\leq\|\chi^{\prime}_{\varsigma}f\|\leq\dfrac{1+\alpha_{1}}{1-\alpha_{2}}\|\chi_{\varsigma}f\|.

Similarly, we have

\dfrac{1-\beta_{1}}{1+\beta_{2}}\|\xi_{\varsigma}f\|\leq\|\xi^{\prime}_{\varsigma}f\|\leq\dfrac{1+\beta_{1}}{1-\beta_{2}}\|\xi_{\varsigma}f\|.

For
\mathcal{J}\subset\mathfrak{A}
and for all
f\in\mathcal{H}
,

\displaystyle min\{(\dfrac{1-\alpha_{1}}{1+\alpha_{2}})^{2},(\dfrac{1-\beta_{1}}{1+\beta_{2}})^{2}\}(\int_{\mathcal{J}}\|\chi_{\varsigma}f\|^{2}d\mu(\varsigma)+\int_{\mathcal{J}^{C}}\|\xi_{\varsigma}f\|^{2}d\mu(\varsigma))

\displaystyle\leq\int_{\mathcal{J}}\|\chi_{\varsigma}f\|^{2}d\mu(\varsigma)+\int_{\mathcal{J}^{C}}\|\xi_{\varsigma}f\|^{2}d\mu(\varsigma)

\displaystyle\leq max\{(\dfrac{1-\alpha_{1}}{1+\alpha_{2}})^{2},(\dfrac{1-\beta_{1}}{1+\beta_{2}})^{2}\}(\int_{\mathcal{J}}\|\chi_{\varsigma}f\|^{2}d\mu(\varsigma)+\int_{\mathcal{J}^{C}}\|\xi_{\varsigma}f\|^{2}d\mu(\varsigma)).

This completes the proof.
∎

Corollary 2.18
.

Let
\{\chi_{\varsigma}\}_{\varsigma\in\mathfrak{A}}
and
\{\xi_{\varsigma}\}_{\varsigma\in\mathfrak{A}}
be woven continuous
L-g-
frames and
\{\chi^{\prime}_{\varsigma}\}_{\varsigma\in\mathfrak{A}}
,
\{\xi^{\prime}_{\varsigma}\}_{\varsigma\in\mathfrak{A}}
be sequences and

such that
for all

and all
\varsigma\in\mathfrak{A},

\displaystyle\|\chi_{\varsigma}f-\chi^{\prime}_{\varsigma}f\|\leq m_{1}\;min\{\|\chi_{\varsigma}f\|,\|\chi^{\prime}_{\varsigma}f\|\},

\displaystyle\|\xi_{\varsigma}f-\xi^{\prime}_{\varsigma}f\|\leq m_{2}\;min\{\|\xi_{\varsigma}f\|,\|\xi^{\prime}_{\varsigma}f\|\}.

Then
\{\chi^{\prime}_{\varsigma}\}_{\varsigma\in\mathfrak{A}}
and
\{\xi^{\prime}_{\varsigma}\}_{\varsigma\in\mathfrak{A}}
are woven continuous
L-g-

Proof.

For all
\varsigma\in\mathfrak{A}

\dfrac{1}{1+m_{1}}\|\chi_{\varsigma}f\|\leq\|\chi^{\prime}_{\varsigma}f\|\leq(m_{1}+1)\|\chi_{\varsigma}f\|,

\dfrac{1}{1+m_{2}}\|\xi_{\varsigma}f\|\leq\|\xi^{\prime}_{\varsigma}f\|\leq(m_{2}+1)\|\xi_{\varsigma}f\|.

By a similar method to the proof of Theorem
2.17
, we have the result.
