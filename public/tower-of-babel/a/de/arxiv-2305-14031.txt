Generating functions of multiple t t -star values of general level
Zhonghua Li and Lu Yan

Abstract
In this paper, we study the explicit expressions of multiple t t -star values with an arbitrary number of blocks of twos of general level. We give an expression of a generating function of such values, which generalizes the results for multiple zeta-star values and multiple t t -star values. This derived generating function can provide expressions of multiple t t -star values of general level in terms of the alternating multiple t t -half values of general level with additional factorial and pochhammer symbol. As applications, some specific evaluations of multiple t t -star values of general level with one-two-three or more general indices are given. These evaluations contribute to a deeper understanding of the properties of multiple t t -star values of general level.

multiple
t

t
-star value of general level, alternating multiple
t
-half value, generating function.

The finite form of multiple
t
-star values of level
N

In this section, we prove a finite version of the generating function of multiple
t
-star values of level
N
. Let
n
be a fixed nonnegative integer. For any
\boldsymbol{s}=(s_{1},\ldots,s_{u})\in\mathbb{N}^{u}
, we define the finite form of multiple
t
-star value of level
N
by

{}_{n}t_{N,a}^{\star}(\boldsymbol{s})=\sum_{n\geq k_{1}\geq\cdots\geq k_{u}\geq 0}\frac{1}{(Nk_{1}+a)^{s_{1}}\cdots(Nk_{u}+a)^{s_{u}}},

where
N
is a fixed positive integer and
a\in\{1,2,\ldots,N\}
. Here we treat
{}_{n}t_{N,a}^{\star}(\emptyset)=1
. For
d\in\mathbb{N}_{0}
,
\boldsymbol{c}=(c_{1},\ldots,c_{d})\in\mathbb{N}^{d}
and
\boldsymbol{z}=(z_{0},z_{1},\ldots,z_{d})\in\mathbb{C}^{d+1}
, we denote the generating function of the finite form of multiple
t
-star values of level
N
with an arbitrary number of blocks of twos as

\displaystyle D_{n}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})=\sum_{b_{0},b_{1},\ldots,b_{d}\geq 0}{}_{n}t_{N,a}^{\star}(\{2\}^{b_{0}},c_{1},\{2\}^{b_{1}},\ldots,c_{d},\{2\}^{b_{d}})z_{0}^{2b_{0}}z_{1}^{2b_{1}}\cdots z_{d}^{2b_{d}}.

Recall that for an integer
c
, the
\{s\}^{c}
denotes a sequence obtained by repeating
s
exactly
c
times if
c>0
; otherwise,
\{s\}^{c}
is an empty string.

We give some lemmas in Subsection
2.1
, and prove an explicit expression of the generating function
D_{n}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})
in Subsection
2.2
.

2.1
Preliminary Lemmas

Lemma 2.1
.

For integers
a,N
with
1\leq a\leq N
and
n,l\in\mathbb{N}_{0}
, we have

\displaystyle\sum_{k=l+1}^{n}\frac{2\left(k+\frac{a}{N}\right)}{(n-k)!\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{n+k+1}}=\frac{n-l}{(n-l)!\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{n+l+1}}

(2.1)

and

\displaystyle\sum_{k=l+1}^{n}\frac{2(-1)^{k}}{(n-k)!\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{n+k+1}}=\frac{(-1)^{l+1}(n-l)}{\left(n+\frac{a}{N}\right)(n-l)!\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{n+l+1}}.

(2.2)

Proof.
We may assume that
n\geq l+1
. To prove (
2.1
), we observe that

\displaystyle\frac{2\left(k+\frac{a}{N}\right)}{(n-k)!\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{n+k+1}}=I(n;k+1)-I(n;k)

(2.3)

for any integer
k
with
l+1\leq k\leq n
, where

\displaystyle I(n;j)=\frac{-1}{(n-k)!\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{n+k}}

for
l+1\leq j\leq n
and
I(n;n+1)=0
. Then summing both sides of (
2.3
) over
k
from
l+1
to
n
, (
2.1
) follows easily.

Similarly, we set

\displaystyle J(n;j)=\frac{(-1)^{j+1}}{\left(n+\frac{a}{N}\right)(n-j)!\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{n+j}}

for
l+1\leq j\leq n
and
J(n;n+1)=0
. It is easy to see that for any integer
k
with
l+1\leq k\leq n
,

\displaystyle\frac{2(-1)^{k}}{(n-k)!\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{n+k+1}}=J(n;k+1)-J(n;k).

Then summing both sides of the above equation over
k
from
l+1
to
n
, we obtain (
2.2
).
∎

Lemma 2.2
.

For integers
a,N
with
1\leq a\leq N
and
n,l,c\in\mathbb{N}_{0}
, we have

\displaystyle\sum_{k=l}^{n}\frac{(-1)^{k}}{(n-k)!\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{n+k+1}}W_{k,l}^{\#}(\{1\}^{c})=\frac{(-1)^{l}(Nl+a)}{(Nn+a)^{c+1}(n-l)!\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{n+l+1}}.

(2.4)

Proof.
We may assume that
n\geq l
. The proof is by induction on
c
. For
c=0
, we need to show

\displaystyle\sum_{k=l}^{n}\frac{(-1)^{k}}{(n-k)!\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{n+k+1}}\cdot 2^{\triangle(k,l)}=\frac{(-1)^{l}(Nl+a)}{(Nn+a)(n-l)!\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{n+l+1}}.

(2.5)

By the definition of
\triangle(k,l)
, the left-hand side of (
2.5
) is

\frac{(-1)^{l}}{(n-l)!\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{n+l+1}}+\sum_{k=l+1}^{n}\frac{2(-1)^{k}}{(n-k)!\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{n+k+1}}.

Applying (
2.2
), we find the left-hand side of (
2.5
) becomes

\frac{(-1)^{l}}{(n-l)!\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{n+l+1}}+\frac{(-1)^{l+1}(n-l)}{\left(n+\frac{a}{N}\right)(n-l)!\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{n+l+1}},

which exactly is the right-hand side of (
2.5
).

If
c\geq 1
, the left-hand side of (
2.4
) is

\displaystyle\sum_{k=l}^{n}\frac{(-1)^{k}}{(n-k)!\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{n+k+1}}\sum_{k\geq l_{1}\geq\cdots\geq l_{c}\geq l}\frac{2^{\triangle(k,l_{1})+\triangle(l_{1},l_{2})+\cdots+\triangle(l_{c},l)}}{(Nl_{1}+a)\cdots(Nl_{c}+a)}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{n\geq l_{1}\geq\cdots\geq l_{c}\geq l}\left\{\sum_{k=l_{1}}^{n}\frac{(-1)^{k}2^{\triangle(k,l_{1})}}{(n-k)!\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{n+k+1}}\right\}\frac{2^{\triangle(l_{1},l_{2})+\cdots+\triangle(l_{c},l)}}{(Nl_{1}+a)(Nl_{2}+a)\cdots(Nl_{c}+a)}.

Using (
2.5
) to deal with the part in the curly brace, we find the left-hand side of (
2.4
) is

\sum_{n\geq l_{1}\geq\cdots\geq l_{c}\geq l}\frac{(-1)^{l_{1}}(Nl_{1}+a)}{(Nn+a)(n-l_{1})!\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{n+l_{1}+1}}\frac{2^{\triangle(l_{1},l_{2})+\cdots+\triangle(l_{c},l)}}{(Nl_{1}+a)(Nl_{2}+a)\cdots(Nl_{c}+a)},

which is

\frac{1}{Nn+a}\sum_{l_{1}=l}^{n}\frac{(-1)^{l_{1}}}{(n-l_{1})!\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{n+l_{1}+1}}W_{l_{1},l}^{\#}(\{1\}^{c-1}).

Then formula (
2.4
) easily follows from the inductive hypothesis for
c-1
.
∎

2.2
Explicit expression of
D_{n}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})

Theorem 2.3
.

Let
a,N
be integers with
1\leq a\leq N
. For any
n,d\in\mathbb{N}_{0}
,
\boldsymbol{c}=(c_{1},\ldots,c_{d})\in(\mathbb{N}\setminus\{2\})^{d}
and
\boldsymbol{z}=(z_{0},z_{1},\ldots,z_{d})\in\mathbb{C}^{d+1}
with
|z_{j}|<1,j=0,1,\ldots,d
, we have

\displaystyle D_{n}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})=\sum_{n\geq k_{0}\geq k_{1}\geq\cdots\geq k_{d}\geq 0}\frac{N\left[\left(\frac{a}{N}\right)_{n+1}\right]^{2}}{(n-k_{0})!\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{n+k_{0}+1}}\cdot\frac{\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{k_{d}}}{k_{d}!(Nk_{d}+a)}

\displaystyle\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\times\prod_{i=0}^{d}\frac{(-1)^{k_{i}\delta_{i}}(Nk_{i}+a)^{\delta_{i}-1}}{(Nk_{i}+a)^{2}-z_{i}^{2}}W_{k_{i-1},k_{i}}^{\#}(\{1\}^{c_{i}-3}),

(2.6)

where
k_{-1}=-1
,
\delta_{i}=\delta(c_{i})+\delta(c_{i+1})
with
c_{0}=1
and
c_{d+1}=0
, the Pochhammer symbol
(a)_{n}
is defined by

(a)_{n}=\frac{\Gamma(a+n)}{\Gamma(a)}=\begin{cases}1&\text{if\;}n=0,\\
a(a+1)\cdots(a+n-1)&\text{if\;}n>0.\end{cases}

Applying power series expansion
\frac{1}{(2k-1)^{2}-z^{2}}=\sum\limits_{b=0}^{\infty}\frac{z^{2b}}{(2k-1)^{2b+2}}
and comparing the coefficients of
z_{0}^{2b_{0}}z_{1}^{2b_{1}}\cdots z_{d}^{2b_{d}}
in (
2.3
), we get the following corollary.

Corollary 2.4
.

Let
a,N
be integers with
1\leq a\leq N
. For any
n,d\in\mathbb{N}_{0}
,
c_{1},\ldots,c_{d}\in\mathbb{N}\setminus\{2\}
and
b_{0},b_{1},\ldots,b_{d}\in\mathbb{N}_{0}
, we have

{}_{n}t^{\star}_{N,a}(\{2\}^{b_{0}},c_{1},\{2\}^{b_{1}},\ldots,c_{d},\{2\}^{b_{d}})=\sum_{n\geq k_{0}\geq k_{1}\geq\cdots\geq k_{d}\geq 0}\frac{N\left[\left(\frac{a}{N}\right)_{n+1}\right]^{2}}{(n-k_{0})!\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{n+k_{0}+1}}\cdot\frac{\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{k_{d}}}{k_{d}!(Nk_{d}+a)}

\displaystyle\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\times\prod_{i=0}^{d}\frac{(-1)^{k_{i}\delta_{i}}}{(Nk_{i}+a)^{2b_{i}+3-\delta_{i}}}W_{k_{i-1},k_{i}}^{\#}(\{1\}^{c_{i}-3}),

where
k_{-1}=-1
,
\delta_{i}=\delta(c_{i})+\delta(c_{i+1})
with
c_{0}=1
and
c_{d+1}=0
, and
(a)_{n}
is the Pochhammer symbol.

Proof of Theorem
2.3
.

We apply induction on
n+d
. For
n=0
, we have

\displaystyle D_{0}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})=\sum_{b_{0},b_{1},\ldots,b_{d}\geq 0}\frac{z_{0}^{2b_{0}}\cdots z_{d}^{2b_{d}}}{a^{2b_{0}+\cdots+2b_{d}+c_{1}+\cdots+c_{d}}}=\frac{1}{a^{c_{1}+\cdots+c_{d}}}\prod_{i=0}^{d}\frac{a^{2}}{a^{2}-z_{i}^{2}},

and the right-hand side of (
2.3
) is

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\prod_{i=0}^{d}\frac{a^{\delta_{i}-1}}{a^{2}-z_{i}^{2}}\cdot W_{-1,0}^{\#}(\emptyset)W_{0,0}^{\#}(\{1\}^{c_{1}-3})\cdots W_{0,0}^{\#}(\{1\}^{c_{d}-3})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\prod_{i=0}^{d}\frac{a^{2}}{a^{2}-z_{i}^{2}}\cdot\frac{a^{2\delta(c_{1})+\cdots+2\delta(c_{d})-3d}}{a^{c_{1}-3+2\delta(c_{1})}\cdots a^{c_{d}-3+2\delta(c_{d})}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{a^{c_{1}+\cdots+c_{d}}}\prod_{i=0}^{d}\frac{a^{2}}{a^{2}-z_{i}^{2}}.

So (
2.3
) is true for
n=0
.

For
d=0
, we observe that for
n>0
,

{}_{n}t_{N,a}^{\star}(\{2\}^{b_{0}})=\sum_{k=0}^{b_{0}}\frac{{}_{n-1}t_{N,a}^{\star}(\{2\}^{k})}{(Nn+a)^{2(b_{0}-k)}}.

Hence we obtain a recursive formula

D_{n}(;z_{0})=\frac{(Nn+a)^{2}}{(Nn+a)^{2}-z_{0}^{2}}D_{n-1}(;z_{0}).

Using the above formula repeatedly, we have

\displaystyle D_{n}(;z_{0})=\prod_{j=1}^{n}\frac{(Nj+a)^{2}}{(Nj+a)^{2}-z_{0}^{2}}D_{0}(;z_{0})=\prod_{j=0}^{n}\frac{(Nj+a)^{2}}{(Nj+a)^{2}-z_{0}^{2}}.

By the method of partial fractional decomposition, we get

\displaystyle D_{n}(;z_{0})=

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{n}\frac{2(-1)^{k}N\left[\left(\frac{a}{N}\right)_{n+1}\right]^{2}\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{k}}{k!(n-k)!\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{n+k+1}(Nk+a)}\cdot\frac{(Nk+a)^{2}}{(Nk+a)^{2}-z_{0}^{2}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{n}\frac{N\left[\left(\frac{a}{N}\right)_{n+1}\right]^{2}}{(n-k)!\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{n+k+1}}\cdot\frac{\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{k}}{k!(Nk+a)}\cdot\frac{2(-1)^{k}(Nk+a)^{2}}{(Nk+a)^{2}-z_{0}^{2}}.

Therefore, (
2.3
) is proved for
d=0
.

Now assume that
n>0
and
d>0
. Since

{}_{n}t^{\star}_{N,a}(\{2\}^{b_{0}},c_{1},\{2\}^{b_{1}},\ldots,c_{d},\{2\}^{b_{d}})=

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{b_{0}}\frac{1}{(Nn+a)^{2b_{0}-2k}}{}_{n-1}t^{\star}_{N,a}(\{2\}^{k},c_{1},\{2\}^{b_{1}},\ldots,c_{d},\{2\}^{b_{d}})

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{(Nn+a)^{2b_{0}+c_{1}}}{}_{n}t^{\star}_{N,a}(\{2\}^{b_{1}},c_{2},\{2\}^{b_{2}},\ldots,c_{d},\{2\}^{b_{d}}),

we deduce that

\displaystyle D_{n}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})=

\displaystyle\sum_{b_{0},\ldots,b_{d}\geq 0}\sum_{k=0}^{b_{0}}\frac{1}{(Nn+a)^{2b_{0}-2k}}{}_{n-1}t^{\star}_{N,a}(\{2\}^{k},c_{1},\{2\}^{b_{1}},\ldots,c_{d},\{2\}^{b_{d}})z_{0}^{2b_{0}}\cdots z_{d}^{2b_{d}},

\displaystyle+\sum_{b_{0}\geq 0}\frac{z_{0}^{2b_{0}}}{(Nn+a)^{2b_{0}+c_{1}}}D_{n}(\boldsymbol{c}^{-};\boldsymbol{z}^{-}),

where
\boldsymbol{c}^{-}=(c_{2},\ldots,c_{d})
and
\boldsymbol{z}^{-}=(z_{1},z_{2},\ldots,z_{d})
. Then it is easy to get the following recursive formula

\displaystyle D_{n}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})=\frac{(Nn+a)^{2}}{(Nn+a)^{2}-z_{0}^{2}}D_{n-1}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})+\frac{(Nn+a)^{2-c_{1}}}{(Nn+a)^{2}-z_{0}^{2}}D_{n}(\boldsymbol{c}^{-};\boldsymbol{z}^{-}).

(2.7)

Let
\widetilde{D}_{n}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})
denote the right-hand side of (
2.3
). According to (
2.7
), we need to prove

\displaystyle\frac{(Nn+a)^{2}}{(Nn+a)^{2}-z_{0}^{2}}D_{n-1}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})-\widetilde{D}_{n}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})=-\frac{(Nn+a)^{2-c_{1}}}{(Nn+a)^{2}-z_{0}^{2}}D_{n}(\boldsymbol{c}^{-};\boldsymbol{z}^{-}).

(2.8)

By the induction hypothesis for
D_{n-1}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})
, the left hand-side of (
2.8
) is

\displaystyle\frac{(Nn+a)^{2}}{(Nn+a)^{2}-z_{0}^{2}}\sum_{n-1\geq k_{0}\geq k_{1}\geq\cdots\geq k_{d}\geq 0}\frac{N\left[\left(\frac{a}{N}\right)_{n}\right]^{2}}{(n-1-k_{0})!\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{n+k_{0}}}\cdot\frac{\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{k_{d}}}{k_{d}!(Nk_{d}+a)}

\displaystyle\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\times\prod_{i=0}^{d}\frac{(-1)^{k_{i}\delta_{i}}(Nk_{i}+a)^{\delta_{i}-1}}{(Nk_{i}+a)^{2}-z_{i}^{2}}W_{k_{i-1},k_{i}}^{\#}(\{1\}^{c_{i}-3})

\displaystyle-\sum_{n\geq k_{0}\geq k_{1}\geq\cdots\geq k_{d}\geq 0}\frac{N\left[\left(\frac{a}{N}\right)_{n+1}\right]^{2}}{(n-k_{0})!\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{n+k_{0}+1}}\cdot\frac{\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{k_{d}}}{k_{d}!(Nk_{d}+a)}

\displaystyle\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\times\prod_{i=0}^{d}\frac{(-1)^{k_{i}\delta_{i}}(Nk_{i}+a)^{\delta_{i}-1}}{(Nk_{i}+a)^{2}-z_{i}^{2}}W_{k_{i-1},k_{i}}^{\#}(\{1\}^{c_{i}-3}),

which is

\displaystyle\sum_{n\geq k_{0}\geq\cdots\geq k_{d}\geq 0}\prod_{i=0}^{d}\frac{(-1)^{k_{i}\delta_{i}}(Nk_{i}+a)^{\delta_{i}-1}}{(Nk_{i}+a)^{2}-z_{i}^{2}}W_{k_{i-1},k_{i}}^{\#}(\{1\}^{c_{i}-3})\cdot\frac{N\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{k_{d}}}{k_{d}!(Nk_{d}+a)}

\displaystyle\quad\quad\quad\times\left\{\frac{(Nn+a)^{2}}{(Nn+a)^{2}-z_{0}^{2}}\cdot\frac{\left[\left(\frac{a}{N}\right)_{n}\right]^{2}}{(n-1-k_{0})!\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{n+k_{0}}}-\frac{\left[\left(\frac{a}{N}\right)_{n+1}\right]^{2}}{(n-k_{0})!\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{n+k_{0}+1}}\right\}.

As the part in braces can be simplified as

the left-hand side of (

\displaystyle\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\times\prod_{i=1}^{d}\frac{(-1)^{k_{i}\delta_{i}}(Nk_{i}+a)^{\delta_{i}-1}}{(Nk_{i}+a)^{2}-z_{i}^{2}}W_{k_{i-1},k_{i}}^{\#}(\{1\}^{c_{i}-3}).

(2.9)

Let
\sum\nolimits_{k_{0}}
denote the inner sum over
k_{0}
in (

\displaystyle\sum\nolimits_{k_{0}}=\sum_{k_{0}=k_{1}}^{n}\frac{(-1)^{k_{0}(1+\delta(c_{1}))}(Nk_{0}+a)^{\delta(c_{1})}}{(n-k_{0})!\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{n+k_{0}+1}}W_{k_{0},k_{1}}^{\#}(\{1\}^{c_{1}-3}).

If

,
\delta(c_{1})=1
,

\displaystyle\sum\nolimits_{k_{0}}=

\displaystyle\sum_{k_{0}=k_{1}}^{n}\frac{Nk_{0}+a}{(n-k_{0})!\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{n+k_{0}+1}}2^{\triangle(k_{0},k_{1})}.

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{Nk_{1}+a}{(n-k_{1})!\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{n+k_{1}+1}}+2\sum_{k_{0}=k_{1}+1}^{n}\frac{Nk_{0}+a}{(n-k_{0})!\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{n+k_{0}+1}}.

\sum\nolimits_{k_{0}}=\frac{Nk_{1}+a}{(n-k_{1})!\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{n+k_{1}+1}}+\frac{Nn-Nk_{1}}{(n-k_{1})!\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{n+k_{1}+1}}=\frac{Nn+a}{(n-k_{1})!\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{n+k_{1}+1}}.

Using the above result together with (
2.2
), we find the left-hand side of (

\displaystyle\frac{-(Nn+a)}{(Nn+a)^{2}-z_{0}^{2}}\sum_{n\geq k_{1}\geq\cdots\geq k_{d}\geq 0}\frac{N\left[\left(\frac{a}{N}\right)_{n+1}\right]^{2}}{(n-k_{1})!\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{n+k_{1}+1}}\cdot\frac{\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{k_{d}}}{k_{d}!(Nk_{d}+a)}

,
\delta(c_{1})=0
, by Lemma

\displaystyle\sum\nolimits_{k_{0}}=\sum_{k_{0}=k_{1}}^{n}\frac{(-1)^{k_{0}}}{(n-k_{0})!\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{n+k_{0}+1}}W_{k_{0},k_{1}}^{\#}(\{1\}^{c_{1}-3})=\frac{(-1)^{k_{1}}(Nn+a)^{2-c_{1}}(Nk_{1}+a)}{(n-k_{1})!\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{n+k_{1}+1}}

Combining this formula with (

\displaystyle\frac{-(Nn+a)^{2-c_{1}}}{(Nn+a)^{2}-z_{0}^{2}}\sum_{n\geq k_{1}\geq\cdots\geq k_{d}\geq 0}\frac{N\left[\left(\frac{a}{N}\right)_{n+1}\right]^{2}}{(n-k_{1})!\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{n+k_{1}+1}}\cdot\frac{\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{k_{d}}}{k_{d}!(Nk_{d}+a)}

\displaystyle\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\times\frac{2(-1)^{k_{1}(1+\delta(c_{2}))}(Nk_{1}+a)^{\delta(c_{2})}}{(Nk_{1}+a)^{2}-z_{1}^{2}}\prod_{i=2}^{d}\frac{(-1)^{k_{i}\delta_{i}}(Nk_{i}+a)^{\delta_{i}-1}}{(Nk_{i}+a)^{2}-z_{i}^{2}}W_{k_{i-1},k_{i}}^{\#}(\{1\}^{c_{i}-3}),

which by the inductive hypothesis for

is

Hence we conclude that

Lemma 3.1
.

Let

\boldsymbol{c}=(c_{1},\ldots,c_{d})\in(\mathbb{N}\setminus\{2\})^{d}

\displaystyle\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}D_{n}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})=D(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z}),

and the convergence is uniform in any closed region
E
:
|z_{0}|\leq u_{0}<1,|z_{1}|\leq u_{1}<1,\ldots,|z_{d}|\leq u_{d}<1

Proof.
For
|z|<1

\displaystyle\prod_{k=m}^{n}\left(1-\frac{z^{2}}{(Nk+a)^{2}}\right)^{-1}=\sum_{l=0}^{\infty}{}_{n,m}t_{N,a}^{\star}(\{2\}^{l})z^{2l}.

(3.2)

We also set

s_{1}>1

n

{}_{\infty,m}t_{N,a}^{\star}(\boldsymbol{s})=\sum_{k_{1}\geq\cdots\geq k_{u}\geq m}\frac{1}{(Nk_{1}+a)^{s_{1}}\cdots(Nk_{u}+a)^{s_{u}}}.

If
d=0

3.2
), we get

\displaystyle D_{n}(;z_{0})=\prod_{k=0}^{n}\left(1-\frac{z_{0}^{2}}{(Nk+a)^{2}}\right)^{-1}\quad\text{and}\quad\quad D(;z_{0})=\prod_{k=0}^{\infty}\left(1-\frac{z_{0}^{2}}{(Nk+a)^{2}}\right)^{-1}.

Hence the lemma is true for
d=0
.

Now assume that
d\geq 1
. Using (
3.2
), we have

\displaystyle D_{n}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{n\geq k_{1}\geq\cdots\geq k_{d}\geq 0}\frac{\prod\limits_{k=k_{1}}^{n}\left(1-\frac{z_{0}^{2}}{(Nk+a)^{2}}\right)^{-1}\prod\limits_{k=k_{2}}^{k_{1}}\left(1-\frac{z_{1}^{2}}{(Nk+a)^{2}}\right)^{-1}\cdots\prod\limits_{k=0}^{k_{d}}\left(1-\frac{z_{d}^{2}}{(Nk+a)^{2}}\right)^{-1}}{(Nk_{1}+a)^{c_{1}}\cdots(Nk_{d}+a)^{c_{d}}}.

\displaystyle D_{n}^{\infty}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{n\geq k_{1}\geq\cdots\geq k_{d}\geq 0}\frac{\prod\limits_{k=k_{1}}^{\infty}\left(1-\frac{z_{0}^{2}}{(Nk+a)^{2}}\right)^{-1}\prod\limits_{k=k_{2}}^{k_{1}}\left(1-\frac{z_{1}^{2}}{(Nk+a)^{2}}\right)^{-1}\cdots\prod\limits_{k=0}^{k_{d}}\left(1-\frac{z_{d}^{2}}{(Nk+a)^{2}}\right)^{-1}}{(Nk_{1}+a)^{c_{1}}\cdots(Nk_{d}+a)^{c_{d}}},

then

\displaystyle|D_{n}^{\infty}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})-D_{n}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\sum_{n\geq k_{1}\geq\cdots\geq k_{d}\geq 0}\frac{\prod\limits_{k=k_{1}}^{n}\left(1-\frac{z_{0}^{2}}{(Nk+a)^{2}}\right)^{-1}\prod\limits_{k=k_{2}}^{k_{1}}\left(1-\frac{z_{1}^{2}}{(Nk+a)^{2}}\right)^{-1}\cdots\prod\limits_{k=0}^{k_{d}}\left(1-\frac{z_{d}^{2}}{(Nk+a)^{2}}\right)^{-1}}{(Nk_{1}+a)^{c_{1}}\cdots(Nk_{d}+a)^{c_{d}}}

(3.3)

Assume that
|z_{0}|\leq u_{0}<1,|z_{1}|\leq u_{1}<1,\ldots,|z_{d}|\leq u_{d}<1
. Using the infinite product formula for the sine function

\frac{\sin\pi z}{\pi z}=\prod\limits_{k=1}^{\infty}\left(1-\frac{z^{2}}{k^{2}}\right)<\prod\limits_{k=0}^{\infty}\left(1-\frac{z^{2}}{(Nk+a)^{2}}\right),

we obtain

\displaystyle\sum_{n\geq k_{1}\geq\cdots\geq k_{d}\geq 0}\frac{\prod\limits_{k=k_{1}}^{n}\left(1-\frac{z_{0}^{2}}{(Nk+a)^{2}}\right)^{-1}\prod\limits_{k=k_{2}}^{k_{1}}\left(1-\frac{z_{1}^{2}}{(Nk+a)^{2}}\right)^{-1}\cdots\prod\limits_{k=0}^{k_{d}}\left(1-\frac{z_{d}^{2}}{(Nk+a)^{2}}\right)^{-1}}{(Nk_{1}+a)^{c_{1}}\cdots(Nk_{d}+a)^{c_{d}}}

\displaystyle<

\displaystyle\prod_{i=0}^{d}\frac{\pi|z_{i}|}{\sin(\pi|z_{i}|)}{}_{n}t_{N,a}^{\star}(\boldsymbol{c})<\prod_{i=0}^{d}\frac{\pi u_{i}}{\sin(\pi u_{i})}t_{N,a}^{\star}(\boldsymbol{c}).

3.2

\displaystyle\left|\prod\limits_{k=n+1}^{\infty}\left(1-\frac{z_{0}^{2}}{(Nk+a)^{2}}\right)^{-1}-1\right|=\left|\sum_{l=1}^{\infty}{}_{\infty,n+1}t_{N,a}^{\star}(\{2\}^{l})z_{0}^{2l}\right|<\sum_{l=1}^{\infty}{}_{\infty,n+1}t_{N,a}^{\star}(\{2\}^{l}).

as

as
n\rightarrow\infty

Lemma 3.2
.

Let
N\in\mathbb{N}
,
a\in\{1,2,\ldots,N\}
,
\varepsilon,p,q,C\in\mathbb{R}
with
\varepsilon>0,p>1,C>0
, and let
M_{k}
be real numbers satisfying

|M_{k}|<\frac{C\log^{q}(Nk+N+a)}{k^{p}+\varepsilon}

for any
k\in\mathbb{N}_{0}
. Then we have

\displaystyle\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}\sum_{k=0}^{n}|M_{k}|\frac{N\left[\left(\frac{a}{N}\right)_{n+1}\right]^{2}}{(n-k)!\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{n+k+1}}=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}|M_{k}|\frac{N}{B\left(\frac{a}{N},\frac{a}{N}\right)},

where
B(a,b)
is the Beta function.

Proof.
Let

U_{k,n}=\frac{N\left[\left(\frac{a}{N}\right)_{n+1}\right]^{2}}{(n-k)!\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{n+k+1}}=\frac{N\Gamma\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)}{\left[\Gamma\left(\frac{a}{N}\right)\right]^{2}}\cdot\frac{\left[\Gamma\left(n+1+\frac{a}{N}\right)\right]^{2}}{\Gamma(n+1-k)\Gamma\left(n+1+k+\frac{2a}{N}\right)},

where
\Gamma(a)
is the Gamma function. For any
k\in\mathbb{N}_{0}
, applying the well-known Stirling’s formula (cf.
[
5
,
7
]
)

\Gamma(x)\sim\sqrt{2\pi}(x-1)^{x-\frac{1}{2}}e^{-(x-1)},\quad x\rightarrow\infty,

and the limit formula

\left(1+\frac{1}{x}\right)^{x}\sim e,\quad x\rightarrow\infty,

we obtain that

\displaystyle\frac{\left[\Gamma\left(n+1+\frac{a}{N}\right)\right]^{2}}{\Gamma(n+1-k)\Gamma\left(n+1+k+\frac{2a}{N}\right)}

\displaystyle\sim

\displaystyle\frac{n+\frac{a}{N}}{\sqrt{(n-k)\left(n+k+\frac{2a}{N}\right)}}\left(1+\frac{k+\frac{a}{N}}{n-k}\right)^{n-k}\left(1+\frac{-k-\frac{a}{N}}{n+k+\frac{2a}{N}}\right)^{n+k+\frac{2a}{N}}

\displaystyle\sim

\displaystyle 1,\quad n\rightarrow\infty.

Therefore, we have

\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}U_{k,n}=\frac{N\Gamma\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)}{\left[\Gamma\left(\frac{a}{N}\right)\right]^{2}}=\frac{N}{B\left(\frac{a}{N},\frac{a}{N}\right)},

which leads to the conclusion that
U_{k,n}
is a bounded sequence of numbers. It is easy to verify that
U_{k,n}
is decreasing with respect to
k
, so we have

0\leq U_{k,n}\leq U_{0,n}.

Note that

\frac{U_{k,n+1}}{U_{k,n}}=\frac{\left(n+1+\frac{a}{N}\right)^{2}}{(n-k+1)\left(n+k+1+\frac{2a}{N}\right)}=\frac{n^{2}+\left(2+\frac{2a}{N}\right)n+\left(1+\frac{a}{N}\right)^{2}}{n^{2}+\left(2+\frac{2a}{N}\right)n+(1-k)\left(k+1+\frac{2a}{N}\right)}>1,

so
U_{k,n}
is increasing with respect to
n
.
Since
|M_{k}|<\frac{C\log^{q}(Nk+N+a)}{k^{p}+\varepsilon}
with
p>1
,

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}|M_{k}|U_{k,n}-\sum_{k=0}^{n}|M_{k}|U_{k,n}=\sum_{k=n+1}^{\infty}|M_{k}|U_{k,n}\rightarrow 0

(3.6)

as
n\rightarrow\infty
. By Monotone Convergence Theorem, we have

\displaystyle\lim\limits_{n\rightarrow\infty}\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}|M_{k}|U_{k,n}=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}|M_{k}|\lim\limits_{n\rightarrow\infty}U_{k,n}=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}|M_{k}|\frac{N}{B\left(\frac{a}{N},\frac{a}{N}\right)}.

(3.7)

Hence, (
3.6
) and (
3.7
) can deduce the desired result.
∎

Lemma 3.3
.

For
N\in\mathbb{N}
,
a\in\{1,2,\ldots,N\}
,
k_{0},d\in\mathbb{N}_{0}

\displaystyle\widetilde{D}_{k_{0}}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})=\sum_{k_{0}\geq k_{1}\geq\cdots\geq k_{d}\geq 0}\frac{\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{k_{d}}}{k_{d}!(Nk_{d}+a)}\prod_{i=0}^{d}\frac{(-1)^{k_{i}\delta_{i}}(Nk_{i}+a)^{\delta_{i}-1}}{(Nk_{i}+a)^{2}-z_{i}^{2}}W_{k_{i-1},k_{i}}^{\#}(\{1\}^{c_{i}-3}),

(3.8)

\displaystyle W_{k_{i-1},k_{i}}^{\#}(\{1\}^{c_{i}-3})<C_{2}{\log}^{c_{i}-1}(Nk_{0}+N+a),

is a positive constant. Notice that
\delta_{0}=1

for

, and

\frac{\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{k_{d}}}{k_{d}!(Nk_{d}+a)}<\frac{k_{d}+1}{Nk_{d}+a}\leq 1.

So we deduce that

\displaystyle\left|\widetilde{D}_{k_{0}}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})\right|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{2}{|(Nk_{0}+a)^{2}-z_{0}^{2}|}\left|\sum_{k_{0}\geq k_{1}\geq\cdots\geq k_{d}\geq 0}\frac{\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{k_{d}}}{k_{d}!(Nk_{d}+a)}\prod_{i=1}^{d}\frac{(-1)^{k_{i}\delta_{i}}(Nk_{i}+a)^{\delta_{i}-1}}{(Nk_{i}+a)^{2}-z_{i}^{2}}W_{k_{i-1},k_{i}}^{\#}(\{1\}^{c_{i}-3})\right|

\displaystyle<

\displaystyle C_{3}\cdot\frac{\log^{c_{1}+\cdots+c_{d}-d}(Nk_{0}+N+a)}{(Nk_{0}+a)^{2}-|z_{0}|^{2}}\sum_{k_{0}\geq k_{1}\geq\cdots\geq k_{d}\geq 0}\prod_{i=1}^{d}\frac{Nk_{i}+a}{(Nk_{i}+a)^{2}-|z_{i}|^{2}},

where
C_{3}
is a positive constant. Let
|z_{\max}|=\max\{|z_{1}|,\ldots,|z_{d}|\}
, then

\displaystyle\left|\widetilde{D}_{k_{0}}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})\right|

\displaystyle<C_{4}\cdot\frac{\log^{c_{1}+\cdots+c_{d}-d}(Nk_{0}+N+a)}{(Nk_{0}+a)^{2}-|z_{0}|^{2}}\left(\sum_{k=1}^{k_{0}}\frac{1}{Nk+a-|z_{\max}|^{2}}\right)^{d}

\displaystyle<C_{5}\cdot\frac{\log^{c_{1}+\cdots+c_{d}}(Nk_{0}+N+a)}{(Nk_{0}+a)^{2}-|z_{0}|^{2}}

\displaystyle<C_{6}\cdot\frac{\log^{c_{1}+\cdots+c_{d}}(Nk_{0}+N+a)}{k_{0}^{2}+a^{2}-|z_{0}|^{2}},

where
C_{4},C_{5}
and
C_{6}
are positive constants. Hence by setting
\varepsilon=a^{2}-|z_{0}|^{2}
,
q=c_{1}+\cdots+c_{d}
,
p=2
and
C=C_{6}
, we conclude the result.
∎

3.2
Main theorems

The following theorem generalizes the results of
[
9
, Theorem 1.4]
and
[
19
, Theorem 3.1]
.

Theorem 3.4
.

Let
a,N
be integers with
1\leq a\leq N
. For any
d\in\mathbb{N}_{0}
,
\boldsymbol{c}=(c_{1},\ldots,c_{d})\in(\mathbb{N}\setminus\{2\})^{d}
with
c_{1}\geq 3
, and
\boldsymbol{z}=(z_{0},z_{1},\ldots,z_{d})\in\mathbb{C}^{d+1}
with
|z_{j}|<1,j=0,1,\ldots,d
, we have

\displaystyle D(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{N}{B\left(\frac{a}{N},\frac{a}{N}\right)}\sum_{k_{0}\geq k_{1}\geq\cdots\geq k_{d}\geq 0}\frac{\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{k_{d}}}{k_{d}!(Nk_{d}+a)}\prod_{i=0}^{d}\frac{(-1)^{k_{i}\delta_{i}}(Nk_{i}+a)^{\delta_{i}-1}}{(Nk_{i}+a)^{2}-z_{i}^{2}}W_{k_{i-1},k_{i}}^{\#}(\{1\}^{c_{i}-3}),

(3.9)

where
k_{-1}=-1
,
\delta_{i}=\delta(c_{i})+\delta(c_{i+1})
with
c_{0}=1
and
c_{d+1}=0
,
(a)_{n}
is the Pochhammer symbol,
B(a,b)
is the Beta function.

The following theorem provides explicit expressions of multiple
t
-star values of level
N
with an arbitrary number of blocks of twos.

Theorem 3.5
.

Let
a,N
be integers with
1\leq a\leq N
. For any
d,b_{0},b_{1},\ldots,b_{d}\in\mathbb{N}_{0}
,
c_{1},\ldots,c_{d}\in\mathbb{N}\setminus\{2\}
with
c_{1}\geq 3
if
b_{0}=0
and
d\geq 1
, we have

\displaystyle t_{N,a}^{\star}(\{2\}^{b_{0}},c_{1},\{2\}^{b_{1}},\ldots,c_{d},\{2\}^{b_{d}})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{N}{B\left(\frac{a}{N},\frac{a}{N}\right)}\sum_{k_{0}\geq k_{1}\geq\cdots\geq k_{d}\geq 0}\frac{\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{k_{d}}}{k_{d}!(Nk_{d}+a)}\prod_{i=0}^{d}\frac{(-1)^{k_{i}\delta_{i}}}{(Nk_{i}+a)^{2b_{i}+3-\delta_{i}}}W_{k_{i-1},k_{i}}^{\#}(\{1\}^{c_{i}-3}),

(3.10)

where
k_{-1}=-1
,
\delta_{i}=\delta(c_{i})+\delta(c_{i+1})
with
c_{0}=1
and
c_{d+1}=0
,
(a)_{n}
is the Pochhammer symbol,
B(a,b)
is the Beta function.

Proof of Theorem
3.4

By Lemma
3.1
and Theorem
2.3
, we obtain that

D(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})=\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}D_{n}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})=\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}\sum_{k_{0}=0}^{n}\frac{N\left[\left(\frac{a}{N}\right)_{n+1}\right]^{2}}{(n-k_{0})!\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{n+k_{0}+1}}\widetilde{D}_{k_{0}}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z}),

where
\widetilde{D}_{k_{0}}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})
is defined in (
3.8
). Applying Lemmas
3.2
and
3.3
, we have

D(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})=\frac{N}{B\left(\frac{a}{N},\frac{a}{N}\right)}\sum_{k_{0}=0}^{\infty}\widetilde{D}_{k_{0}}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z}).

This completes the proof.
∎

Proof of Theorem
3.5

Set
\boldsymbol{b}=(b_{0},b_{1},
…
,b_{d})
and

\displaystyle\widetilde{D}_{k_{0}}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{b})=\sum_{k_{0}\geq k_{1}\geq\cdots\geq k_{d}\geq 0}\frac{\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{k_{d}}}{k_{d}!(Nk_{d}+a)}\prod_{i=0}^{d}\frac{(-1)^{k_{i}\delta_{i}}}{(Nk_{i}+a)^{2b_{i}-\delta_{i}+3}}W_{k_{i-1},k_{i}}^{\#}(\{1\}^{c_{i}-3}).

According to the proof of Lemma
3.3
, it is easy to deduce that there exist
\varepsilon,p,q,C\in\mathbb{R}
with
p>1
and
\varepsilon,C>0
such that for all
k_{0}\in\mathbb{N}_{0}
,

\displaystyle\left|\widetilde{D}_{k_{0}}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{b})\right|<\frac{C\log^{q}(Nk_{0}+N+a)}{k_{0}^{p}+\varepsilon}.

(3.11)

By taking the limit
n\rightarrow\infty
in Corollary
2.4
and using (
3.11
) and Lemma
3.2
, we conclude the desired result.
∎

4
Evaluations for multiple
t
-star values of level
N

In this section, we provide some evaluations of multiple
t
-star values of level
N
. For
\boldsymbol{s}=(s_{1},\ldots,s_{u})\in(\mathbb{Z}\backslash\{0\})^{u}
with
s_{1}\neq 1
, we define the alternating multiple
t
-value of level
N
containing factorial and pochhammer symbols by

\displaystyle\widetilde{t}_{N,a}(\boldsymbol{s})=\widetilde{t}_{N,a}(s_{1},\ldots,s_{u})=\sum_{k_{1}>\cdots>k_{u}\geq 0}\frac{{\operatorname{sign}(s_{1})}^{k_{1}}\cdots{\operatorname{sign}(s_{u})}^{k_{u}}}{{(Nk_{1}+a)}^{|s_{1}|}\cdots{(Nk_{u}+a)}^{|s_{u}|}}\cdot\frac{\left(\frac{2a}{N}\right)_{k_{u}}}{k_{u}!},

where
\operatorname{sign}(s)
is
1
if
s>0
and
-1
if
s<0
. Let
r
be a variable. The interpolated alternating multiple
t
-values of level
N
are defined by

\displaystyle{\widetilde{t}}_{N,a}^{r}(\boldsymbol{s})=\widetilde{t}_{N,a}^{r}(s_{1},\ldots,s_{u})=\sum_{\circ=``+"\text{or}``,"}r^{\#\text{pluses}}\widetilde{t}_{N,a}(s_{1}\circ s_{2}\circ\cdots\circ s_{u}),

where

s+l=\begin{cases}|s|+|l|&\text{if\;}sl>0,\\
-(|s|+|l|)&\text{if\;}sl<0.\end{cases}

As usual, we may use the bar notation. For example, for positive integers
s_{1},s_{2}
, we denote
\widetilde{t}_{N,a}(-s_{1},s_{2})
by
\widetilde{t}_{N,a}(\overline{s_{1}},s_{2})
and denote
\widetilde{t}_{N,a}^{r}(-s_{1},s_{2})
by
\widetilde{t}_{N,a}^{r}(\overline{s_{1}},s_{2})
.

Remark 4.1
.

Setting
N=a=1
, we get the interpolated alternating multiple zeta value,

\displaystyle\zeta^{r}(\boldsymbol{s})=\zeta^{r}(s_{1},\ldots,s_{u})=\sum_{\circ=``+"\text{or}``,"}r^{\#\text{pluses}}\zeta(s_{1}\circ s_{2}\circ\cdots\circ s_{u}),

which is an alternate version of interpolated multiple zeta value as defined by S. Yamamoto
[
27
]
. Setting
N=2,a=1
, we obtain the interpolated alternating multiple
t
-values
[
19
]
,

\displaystyle t^{r}(\boldsymbol{s})=t^{r}(s_{1},\ldots,s_{u})=\sum_{\circ=``+"\text{or}``,"}r^{\#\text{pluses}}t(s_{1}\circ s_{2}\circ\cdots\circ s_{u}).

Therefore, Theorem
3.5
can be rewritten as the following theorem.

Theorem 4.2
.

Let
a,N
be integers with
1\leq a\leq N
. For any
d,b_{0},b_{1},\ldots,b_{d}\in\mathbb{N}_{0}
,
c_{1},\ldots,c_{d}\in\mathbb{N}\setminus\{2\}
with

if
b_{0}=0
and

, we have

\displaystyle t_{N,a}^{\star}(\{2\}^{b_{0}},c_{1},\{2\}^{b_{1}},\ldots,c_{d},\{2\}^{b_{d}})=\frac{N\cdot 2^{|\boldsymbol{c}|+1-2d+2\delta(\boldsymbol{c})}}{B\left(\frac{a}{N},\frac{a}{N}\right)}

\displaystyle\times{\widetilde{t}}_{N,a}^{1/2}((-1)^{\delta(c_{1})-1}(2b_{0}+2-\delta(c_{1})),\{1\}^{c_{1}-3},(-1)^{\delta(c_{1})+\delta(c_{2})}(2b_{1}+3-\delta(c_{1})-\delta(c_{2})),

\displaystyle(-1)^{\delta(c_{d})}(2b_{d}+2-\delta(c_{d}))),

where

is the Beta function,

,

Taking
