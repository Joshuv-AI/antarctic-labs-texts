Wall-crossing formula for framed quiver moduli
Ryo Ohkawa

Abstract
. We investigate the wall-crossing phenomena for moduli of framed quiver representations. These spaces are expected to be highly useful in capturing the representation theoretic essence of special functions in integrable systems. Within this class of moduli spaces, we focus on the type A A flag manifold, type A A affine Laumon spaces, Nakajima quiver variety, and framed moduli of sheaves on the projective plane and the blow-up as main motivating examples. Specifically, we examine the wall-crossing formulas for integrals of Euler classes over these moduli spaces.

Framed quiver representation is a quiver representation with a framing.
Moduli of framed quiver representations are studied by Reineke

These moduli spaces contain interesting examples with applications to various areas such as

As one of such examples, from affine Laumon space,

As an approach to study these functions systematically, we summarize method using wall-crossing

Mochizuki
[
19

studied wall-crossing formula for moduli of parabolic sheaves on surfaces.
He studied integrals over these moduli spaces which depend on stability parameters.
The set of stability parameters are divided into
connected open sets called chambers, and the boundary walls.
The wall-crossing formula describes differences between
integrals over two moduli spaces lying on neighboring chambers along one wall

In particular, it is applied to study Donaldson invariants and Seiberg-Witten invariants of algebraic surfaces,
but his theory is formulated in vast general setting.

In the case of the blow-up

2.1.
Setting

We consider a pair

We consider a finite dimensional

-graded vector space

\mathop{\mathrm{Rep}}\nolimits_{Q}(V)=\prod_{a\in Q_{1}}=\{(B_{h})\in\mathop{\mathrm{Hom}}\nolimits_{\mathbb{C}}(V_{\text{out}(h)},V_{\text{in}(h)})\mid(B_{h})\text{ satisfies relations in }Q_{2}\}

Q
-representations on

defined as follows.
We put

\mathbb{M}_{Q}(V)=\prod_{a\in Q_{1}}\mathop{\mathrm{Hom}}\nolimits_{\mathbb{C}}(V_{\mathop{\mathrm{out}}\nolimits(a)},V_{\mathop{\mathrm{in}}\nolimits(a)}),\quad\mathbb{L}_{Q}(V)=\prod_{l\in Q_{2}}\mathop{\mathrm{Hom}}\nolimits_{\mathbb{C}}(V_{\mathop{\mathrm{out}}\nolimits(l)},V_{\mathop{\mathrm{in}}\nolimits(l)}),

We consider

-action on
\mathop{\mathrm{Rep}}\nolimits_{Q}(V)

\displaystyle\zeta_{\infty}=\begin{cases}-\sum_{i\in I}\zeta_{i}\mathop{\mathrm{dim}}\nolimits V_{i}&\text{ when }\mathop{\mathrm{dim}}\nolimits V_{\infty}=1\\
0&\text{ when }\mathop{\mathrm{dim}}\nolimits V_{\infty}=0.\end{cases}

(1)

For a
Q_{0}
-graded subspace

of

We alway assume

.
This automatically holds when

Definition 2.1
.

We say that a

Here we note that

trivially acts on
\mathop{\mathrm{Rep}}\nolimits_{Q}(V)

When

In the following, we fix

For

3.2.
(\zeta,\eta)
-stability

As stability parameters, we take

\eta=(\eta_{1},\ldots,\eta_{L})\in(\mathbb{Q}_{>0})^{L}
, and put

\mu_{(\zeta,\eta)}(\tilde{S})=\frac{\sum_{v\in Q_{0}}\zeta_{v}\mathop{\mathrm{dim}}\nolimits S_{i}+\sum_{k=1}^{L}\eta_{k}\mathop{\mathrm{dim}}\nolimits\tilde{S}_{(0,k)}}{\mathop{\mathrm{dim}}\nolimits S_{\infty}+\sum_{i\in I}\mathop{\mathrm{dim}}\nolimits S_{i}}

for any
\tilde{Q}_{0}
-graded vector space
\tilde{S}=S\oplus\bigoplus_{k=0}^{L}\tilde{S}_{(0,k)}

where
S=\bigoplus_{v\in Q_{0}}S_{v}

We say that
\tilde{\rho}\in\mathop{\mathrm{Rep}}\nolimits_{\tilde{Q}}(\tilde{V})
is

-semistable if for any non-zero proper sub-representation
\tilde{S}

\displaystyle\mu_{(\zeta,\eta)}(\tilde{S})\leq\mu_{(\zeta,\eta)}(\tilde{V}).

(3)

If inequality is always strict, we say that
\tilde{\rho}
is
(\zeta,\eta)
-stable.

Lemma 3.1
.

If
\tilde{\rho}
is
(\zeta,\eta)
-semistable, then the map
\tilde{V}_{(0,L)}\to V_{0}

is an isomorphism, and

\tilde{V}_{(0,k)}\to\tilde{V}_{(0,k+1)}

are all injective for
k=1,\ldots,L-1

Proof.

For any sub-representation
\tilde{S}
of
\tilde{\rho}
, the inequality (
3
) is equivalent to

\displaystyle\begin{vmatrix}\sum_{i\in Q_{0}}\zeta_{i}\mathop{\mathrm{dim}}\nolimits S_{i}+\sum_{k=1}^{L}\eta_{k}\mathop{\mathrm{dim}}\nolimits\tilde{S}_{(0,k)}&\sum_{k=1}^{L}\eta_{k}\mathop{\mathrm{dim}}\nolimits\tilde{V}_{(0,k)}\\
\sum_{v\in Q_{0}}\mathop{\mathrm{dim}}\nolimits S_{v}&\mathop{\mathrm{dim}}\nolimits V\end{vmatrix}\leq 0.

(4)
