Additional Fibonacci–Bernoulli relations


Abstract
We continue our study on relationships between Fibonacci (Lucas) numbers and Bernoulli numbers and polynomials. The derivations of our results are based on functional equations for the respective generating functions, which in our case are combinations of hyperbolic functions. Special cases and some corollaries will highlight interesting aspects of our findings.

Introduction

Fibonacci and Lucas numbers satisfy the linear second-order recurrence

u_{n}=u_{n-1}+u_{n-2},\quad n\geq 2,

with initial conditions
F_{0}=0
,
F_{1}=1
and
L_{0}=2
,
L_{1}=1
, respectively. Both sequences have a long history and are very popular among mathematicians as they appear in important mathematical branches such as number theory, combinatorics and graph theory. They have entries A000045 and A000032 in the On-Line Encyclopedia of Integer Sequences
[
13
]
. Excellent references on these sequences are the books
[
12
,
14
]
.

As usual, Bernoulli polynomials are defined by the exponential generating function
[
11
, Chapter 1, Section 1.3]

H(x,z):=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}B_{n}(x)\frac{z^{n}}{n!}=\frac{ze^{xz}}{e^{z}-1},\qquad|z|<2\pi.

For
n\geq 0
, they satisfy the following functional relations

[
11
, Chapter 1, Section 1.3]
:

B_{n}(1+x)-B_{n}(x)=nx^{n-1},

(1)

B_{n}(1-x)=(-1)^{n}B_{n}(x),

(2)

B_{n}(-x)=(-1)^{n}\big(B_{n}(x)+nx^{n-1}\big),

(3)

{B_{n}(mx)}={m^{n-1}}\sum_{k=0}^{m-1}{B_{n}\Big({x+\frac{k}{m}}\Bigr)},\qquad m\geq 1.

(4)

Also, Bernoulli polynomials have the property

B_{n}(x+z)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}{\binom{n}{k}B_{n-k}(x)z^{k}},

from which we get

B_{n}(x)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}{\binom{n}{k}B_{n-k}x^{k}},

(5)

where
B_{n}=B_{n}(0)
are the Bernoulli numbers. The Bernoulli numbers are rational numbers starting with

B_{0}=1,\,\,\,\,B_{1}=-\frac{1}{2},\,\,\,\,B_{2}=\frac{1}{6},\,\,\,\,B_{4}=-\frac{1}{30},\,\,\,\,B_{6}=\frac{1}{42},

and
B_{2n+1}=0
for
n\geq 1
.

The following identities connect Fibonacci numbers to Bernoulli polynomials and are proved in
[
7
]
:
for each integers
n\geq 0
,
j\geq 1
, and
x\in\mathbb{C}
,

\sum_{k=0}^{n}{n\choose k}F_{jk}\big(\sqrt{5}F_{j}\big)^{n-k}B_{n-k}(x)=nF_{j}\big((\sqrt{5}x+\beta)F_{j}+F_{j-1}\big)^{n-1},

\sum_{k=0}^{n}{n\choose k}F_{jk}\big(-\sqrt{5}F_{j}\big)^{n-k}B_{n-k}(x)=nF_{j}\big((\alpha-\sqrt{5}x)F_{j}+F_{j-1}\big)^{n-1},

where
\alpha=\frac{1+\sqrt{5}}{2}
is the golden ratio and
\beta=\frac{1-\sqrt{5}}{2}=-\frac{1}{\alpha}
. Other results in this direction are contained in
[
3
,
4
,
5
,
9
,
10
,
15
,
16
,
17
,
18
]
, among others.

In this paper, we state new relations involving Fibonacci and Lucas numbers and Bernoulli numbers and polynomials. We will work with many exponential generating functions. The results stated are complements of the recent discoveries from
[
5
,
7
,
8
]
. Some of our results were announced without proofs in
[
2
]
.

Identities from hidden threefold convolutions

We begin with a known lemma
[
12
, Vol. 1, p. 251]
.

Lemma 1
.

Let
n
and
j
be positive integers. Then

\sum_{k=0}^{n}{n\choose k}F_{jk}F_{j(n-k)}=\frac{2^{n}L_{jn}-2L_{j}^{n}}{5},

(6)

\sum_{k=0}^{n}{n\choose k}L_{jk}L_{j(n-k)}=2^{n}L_{jn}+2L_{j}^{n},

(7)

\sum_{k=0}^{n}{n\choose k}F_{jk}L_{j(n-k)}=2^{n}F_{jn}.

(8)

Our first main result is the following theorem.

Theorem 2
.

Let
n
and
j
be positive integers. Then

\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}k=0\\
n-k\equiv 0\,(\mathrm{mod}\,2)\end{subarray}}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\big(2^{k}L_{jk}-2L_{j}^{k}\big)(\sqrt{5}F_{j})^{n-k}\frac{B_{n-k+2}}{n-k+2}=\frac{2^{n+2}L_{j(n+2)}-2L_{j}^{n+2}}{5(n+1)(n+2)F_{j}^{2}}-L_{j}^{n},

(9)

\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}k=0\\
n-k\equiv 0\,(\mathrm{mod}\,2)\end{subarray}}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\big(2^{k}L_{jk}+2L_{j}^{k}\big)(\sqrt{5}F_{j})^{n-k}\frac{2^{n-k+2}-1}{n-k+2}B_{n-k+2}=L_{j}^{n},

(10)

and

\displaystyle\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}k=0\\
n-k\equiv 0\,(\mathrm{mod}\,2)\end{subarray}}^{n}\!\!\binom{n}{k}2^{k}F_{jk}

\displaystyle(\sqrt{5}F_{j})^{n-k}\frac{B_{n-k+2}}{n-k+2}+\frac{2}{\sqrt{5}}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}k=0\\
n-k\equiv 1\,(\mathrm{mod}\,2)\end{subarray}}^{n-1}\!\!\binom{n}{k}L^{k}_{j}(\sqrt{5}F_{j})^{n-k}\frac{B_{n-k+1}}{n-k+1}

\displaystyle=\frac{2^{n+3}F_{j(n+2)}}{5(n+1)(n+2)F_{j}^{2}}-\frac{2L_{j}^{n+1}}{5(n+1)F_{j}}.

(11)

Proof.

Let
F(z)
and
L(z)
denote the exponential generating functions of sequences
(F_{jn})_{n\geq 0}
and
(L_{jn})_{n\geq 0}

F^{2}(z)=\frac{4}{5}e^{L_{j}z}\sinh^{2}\Big(\frac{\sqrt{5}F_{j}}{2}z\Big),

(12)

L^{2}(z)=4e^{L_{j}z}\cosh^{2}\Big(\frac{\sqrt{5}F_{j}}{2}z\Big).

(13)

From the power series for the cotangent
[
11
, Chapter 1.3]

\coth z=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}2^{2n}B_{2n}\frac{z^{2n-1}}{(2n)!}

we get

-\frac{d}{dz}\coth z=\frac{1}{\sinh^{2}z}=\frac{1}{z^{2}}-\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(2n-1)2^{2n}B_{2n}\frac{z^{2n-2}}{(2n)!}\,.

(14)

Hence, we see that the functional equation (
12

6

S_{2}(z)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\big(2^{n}L_{jn}-2L_{j}^{n}\big)\frac{z^{n}}{n!}\cdot\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(2n-1)5^{n-1}F_{j}^{2n-2}B_{2n}\frac{z^{2n-2}}{(2n)!}\,.

In the series

the first two terms are zero and therefore

S_{1}(z)=\frac{1}{5F_{j}^{2}}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{2^{n+2}L_{j(n+2)}-2L_{j}^{n+2}}{(n+2)(n+1)}\frac{z^{n}}{n!}\,.

The second series
S_{2}(z)
equals

S_{2}(z)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\big(2^{n}L_{jn}-2L_{j}^{n}\big)\frac{z^{n}}{n!}\cdot\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(2n+1)5^{n}F_{j}^{2n}B_{2n+2}\frac{z^{2n}}{(2n+2)!}

and is a simple Cauchy product. Expanding and comparing the coefficients of

proves (
9

The underlying functional equation for identity (

\frac{F(z)L(z)}{\sinh^{2}\!\Big(\frac{\sqrt{5}F_{j}}{2}z\Big)}=\frac{4}{\sqrt{5}}e^{L_{j}z}\coth\!\Big(\frac{\sqrt{5}F_{j}}{2}z\Big).

\displaystyle=\frac{8}{5F_{j}z}+4\left(\frac{1}{5F_{j}^{2}}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}2^{n+2}\frac{F_{j(n+2)}}{(n+2)(n+1)}\frac{z^{n}}{n!}-\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}2^{n}F_{jn}\frac{z^{n}}{n!}\cdot\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{B_{2n+2}}{2n+2}5^{n}F_{j}^{2n}\frac{z^{2n}}{(2n)!}\right),

Now, we can apply the Cauchy multiplication theorem on both sides. When simplifying the RHS, use
\binom{n-1}{k}\frac{n}{n-k}=\binom{n}{k}

Corollary 20
.

Let

\displaystyle=nF_{j}

\displaystyle q^{1-n}\sum_{r=1}^{q-1}\Big((\pm\sqrt{5}F_{j}r+qL_{j})^{n-1}+(\pm\sqrt{5}F_{j}(r-q)+qL_{j})^{n-1}\Big).

Proof.
