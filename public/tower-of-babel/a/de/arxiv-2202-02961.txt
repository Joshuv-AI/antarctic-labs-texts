Efficient ADMM Decoder for Non-binary LDPC Codes with Codeword-Independent Performance
Xiaomeng Guo, Yongchao Wang

Abstract
In this paper, we devote to devise a non-binary low-density parity-check (LDPC) decoder in Galois fields of characteristic two ( 𝔽 2 q \mathbb{F}_{2^{q}} ) via the alternating direction method of multipliers (ADMM) technique. Through the proposed bit embedding technique and the decomposition technique of the three-variables parity-check equation, an efficient ADMM decoding algorithm for non-binary LDPC codes is proposed. The computation complexity in each ADMM iteration is roughly 𝒪 ⁡ ( n ​ q ) \mathcal{O}(nq) , which is significantly lower than the existing LDPC decoders. Moreover, we prove that the proposed decoder satisfies the favorable property of the codeword-independent. Simulation results demonstrate the outstanding performance of the proposed decoder in contrast with state-of-the-art LDPC decoders.

We show through numerical simulations that the proposed ADMM decoder outperforms state-of-the-art decoders in error correction performance and complexity.

III
Non-convex QP formulation and its ADMM solving algorithm

III-A

Non-Convex QP Formulation

A classical way for the binary constraint (
17c

\mathbf{v}\in[0,1]^{nq+\Gamma_{a}}

which leads to the following non-convex QP decoder

\displaystyle\min_{\mathbf{v}}\

\displaystyle\mathbf{q}^{T}\mathbf{v}-\frac{\alpha}{2}\|\mathbf{v}-0.5\|^{2}_{2},

(18a)

\displaystyle{\rm s.t.}\

\displaystyle\mathbf{Av}\preceq\mathbf{b},

(18b)

\displaystyle\mathbf{v}\in[0,1]^{nq+\Gamma_{a}}.

(18c)

Inspired by the intrinsic structure of model (

Meanwhile, the computational complexity in each iteration scales linearly with the non-binary LDPC codes length and the considered length

VI
Conclusion

In this paper, an efficient decoder is proposed for non-binary LDPC codes in
\mathbb{F}_{2^{q}}
through ADMM method based on the proposed bit embedding technique.
We show that the decoding complexity grows linearly as the non-binary LDPC codes length and the considered length
q
, which outperforms state-of-the-art ADMM decoders.
Moreover the performance of the proposed decoding algorithm satisfies the codeword-independent property.
Numerical results reveal the outstanding performance in error-correction and complexity compared with other ADMM decoders.

\mathbf{e}_{1,\tau}^{0,k+1}=\mathcal{M}_{\mathbf{v}_{\tau}}(\mathbf{e}_{1,\tau}^{k+1})
based on (
50
), there are
\mathbf{e}_{1,\tau}^{0,k+1}=[{e}_{1,\tau_{2}}^{k+1},{e}_{1,\tau_{1}}^{k+1},{e}_{1,\tau_{4}}^{k+1},{e}_{1,\tau_{3}}^{k+1}]^{T}
when
\mathbf{v}_{\tau}=[1,1,0]^{T}
. Thus, when
\mathbf{v}_{\tau}=[1,1,0]^{T}
, from (
21d
) and (
47
) we have

\displaystyle\mathbf{y}_{1,\tau}^{0,{k+1}}

\displaystyle=\mathbf{y}_{1,\tau}^{0,{k}}+\mu\left(\mathbf{Tv}_{\tau}^{0,{k+1}}+\mathbf{e}_{1,\tau}^{0,{k+1}}-\mathbf{w}\right)

(57)

\displaystyle=\left[\begin{array}[]{c}y_{1,\tau_{2}}^{k}\\
y_{1,\tau_{1}}^{k}\\
y_{1,\tau_{4}}^{k}\\
y_{1,\tau_{3}}^{k}\end{array}\right]+\mu\left(\mathbf{T}\left[\begin{array}[]{c}1-v_{\tau_{1}}^{k+1}\\
1-v_{\tau_{2}}^{k+1}\\
v_{\tau_{3}}^{k+1}\end{array}\right]+\left[\begin{array}[]{c}e_{1,\tau_{2}}^{k+1}\\
e_{1,\tau_{1}}^{k+1}\\
e_{1,\tau_{4}}^{k+1}\\
e_{1,\tau_{3}}^{k+1}\end{array}\right]-\mathbf{w}\right)

\displaystyle\left.=\left[\begin{array}[]{l}y_{1,\tau_{2}}^{k}+\mu\left(-v_{\tau_{1}}^{k+1}+v_{\tau_{2}}^{k+1}-v_{\tau_{3}}^{k+1}+e_{1,\tau_{2}}^{k+1}\right)\\
y_{1,\tau_{1}}^{k}+\mu\left(v_{\tau_{1}}^{k+1}-v_{\tau_{2}}^{k+1}-v_{\tau_{3}}^{k+1}+e_{1,\tau_{1}}^{k+1}\right)\\

Since
\mathbf{w}=[0,0,0,2]^{T}
,
\mathbf{y}_{1,\tau}^{0,k+1}

\displaystyle\mathbf{y}_{1,\tau}^{0^{k+1}}

\displaystyle=\left[\begin{array}[]{c}y_{1,\tau_{2}}^{k}+\mu\left(\left(-v_{\tau_{1}}^{k+1}+v_{\tau_{2}}^{k+1}-v_{\tau_{3}}^{k+1}\right)+e_{1,\tau_{2}}^{k+1}-w_{2}\right)\\
y_{1,\tau_{1}}^{k}+\mu\left(\left(v_{\tau_{1}}^{k+1}-v_{\tau_{2}}^{k+1}-v_{\tau_{3}}^{k+1}\right)+e_{1,\tau_{1}}^{k+1}-w_{1}\right)\\

y_{1,\tau_{3}}^{k}+\mu\left(\left(-v_{\tau_{1}}^{k+1}-v_{\tau_{2}}^{k+1}+v_{\tau_{3}}^{k+1}\right)+e_{1,\tau_{3}}^{k+1}-w_{3}\right)\end{array}\right]

(58)

\displaystyle=\left[y_{1,\tau_{2}}^{k+1},y_{1,\tau_{1}}^{k+1},y_{1,\tau_{4}}^{k+1},y_{1,\tau_{3}}^{k+1}\right]^{T},

i.e.,

\mathbf{y}_{1,\tau}^{0,k+1}=\mathcal{M}_{\mathbf{v}_{\tau}}(\mathbf{y}_{1,\tau}^{k+1}),

(59)

when
\mathbf{v}_{\tau}=[1,1,0]^{T}
.

With similar derivation, we can see (
59
) also holds when
\mathbf{v}_{\tau}=[1,0,1]^{T},[0,1,1]^{T},[0,0,0]^{T}

\mathbf{y}_{1}^{k+1}

(60)

)

\displaystyle=-(y_{2,i}^{k}+\mu(v_{i}^{k+1}-e_{2,i}^{k+1})),

(62)
