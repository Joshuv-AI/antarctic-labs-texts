PAULSEN AND PROJECTION PROBLEMS FOR BANACH SPACES
K. MAHESH KRISHNA

Abstract




Abstract
: Based on the two decades old celebrated Paulsen problem and its solutions for Hilbert spaces by Kwok, Lau, Lee, Ramachandran, Hamilton, and Moitra, we formulate Paulsen problem for Banach spaces. We also formulate projection problem for Banach spaces.

Keywords
: Paulsen problem, Projection problem, Approximate Schauder Frame.

Mathematics Subject Classification (2020)
: 42C15.

Definition 1.1
.

[
35
,
3
]

A collection
\{\tau_{j}\}_{j=1}^{n}
in a finite dimensional Hilbert space
\mathcal{H}
over
\mathbb{K}
(
\mathbb{R}
or
\mathbb{C}
) is said to be a
frame
for
\mathcal{H}
if there exist
a,b>0
such that

\displaystyle a\|h\|^{2}\leq\sum_{j=1}^{n}|\langle h,\tau_{j}\rangle|^{2}\leq b\|h\|^{2},\quad\forall h\in\mathcal{H}.

A frame
\{\tau_{j}\}_{j=1}^{n}
for
\mathcal{H}
is said to be
Parseval
if

\displaystyle h=\sum_{j=1}^{n}\langle h,\tau_{j}\rangle\tau_{j},\quad\forall h\in\mathcal{H}.

A frame
\{\tau_{j}\}_{j=1}^{n}
for
\mathcal{H}
is said to be
tight
if

\displaystyle a\|h\|^{2}=\sum_{j=1}^{n}|\langle h,\tau_{j}\rangle|^{2},\quad\forall h\in\mathcal{H}.

A frame
\{\tau_{j}\}_{j=1}^{n}
for
\mathcal{H}
is said to be
unit norm
if
\|\tau_{j}\|=1
, for all
1\leq j\leq n
.

A frame
\{\tau_{j}\}_{j=1}^{n}
for
\mathcal{H}
induces three operators defined as follows.

(i)

Analysis operator
\theta_{\tau}:\mathcal{H}\ni h\mapsto\theta_{\tau}h\coloneqq(\langle h,\tau_{j}\rangle)_{j=1}^{n}\in\mathbb{K}^{n}
.

(ii)

Synthesis operator
\theta_{\tau}^{*}:\mathbb{K}^{n}\ni(a_{j})_{j=1}^{n}\mapsto\theta_{\tau}^{*}(a_{j})_{j=1}^{n}\coloneqq\sum_{j=1}^{n}a_{j}\tau_{j}\in\mathcal{H}
.

(iii)

Frame operator
S_{\tau}:\mathcal{H}\ni h\mapsto S_{\tau}h\coloneqq\sum_{j=1}^{n}\langle h,\tau_{j}\rangle\tau_{j}\in\mathcal{H}
.

Frame condition then gives that the analysis operator is injective, synthesis operator is surjective and the frame operator is positive and invertible. Further, the frame operator factors as
S_{\tau}=\theta_{\tau}^{*}\theta_{\tau}
. Since frame operator is positive and invertible, one has the conclusion that
\{S_{\tau}^{-1/2}\tau_{j}\}_{j=1}^{n}
is a Parseval frame for
\mathcal{H}
. In general, given a frame, construction of Parseval frame in this way is difficult. Also, given a collection it is easily to verify that whether it is a frame than to verify that it is a Parseval frame. This leads to the following notions.

Definition 1.2
.

[
6

A Parseval frame
\{\tau_{j}\}_{j=1}^{n}
for a
d
-dimensional Hilbert space
\mathcal{H}
is called an

\displaystyle\|\tau_{j}\|^{2}=\frac{d}{n},\quad\forall 1\leq j\leq n.

.

[
6

d
-dimensional Hilbert space
\mathcal{H}

\displaystyle(1-\varepsilon)I_{\mathcal{H}}\leq S_{\tau}\leq(1+\varepsilon)I_{\mathcal{H}}.

.

[

\mathcal{H}

(

) if

\displaystyle(1-\varepsilon)\frac{d}{n}\leq\|\tau_{j}\|^{2}\leq(1+\varepsilon)\frac{d}{n},\quad\forall 1\leq j\leq n.

[

Theorem 1.7

[

Projection problem is another classical problem in Hilbert space theory which states as follows.

[

(

Let
\mathcal{H}
be a d-dimensional Hilbert space with orthonormal basis

g:(0,1)\times\mathbb{N}\times\mathbb{N}\to[0,\infty)
satisfying the following: If
P:\mathcal{H}\to\mathcal{H}
is an orthogonal projection of rank

\displaystyle(1-\varepsilon)\frac{n}{d}\leq\|Pu_{k}\|^{2}\leq(1+\varepsilon)\frac{n}{d},\quad\forall 1\leq k\leq d,

then there exists an orthogonal projection
Q:\mathcal{H}\to\mathcal{H}
with

\displaystyle\|Qu_{k}\|^{2}=\frac{n}{d},\quad\forall 1\leq k\leq d,

satisfying

\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{d}\|Pu_{k}-Qu_{k}\|^{2}\leq g(\varepsilon,n,d).

Problem 2.14

(
Projection problem for Banach spaces
)

Let
\mathcal{X}

d

(\{\zeta_{k}\}_{k=1}^{d},\{u_{k}\}_{k=1}^{d})

g:(0,1)\times\mathbb{N}\times\mathbb{N}\to[0,\infty)
satisfying the following: If
P:\mathcal{X}\to\mathcal{X}
is a projection (idempotent) of rank
n
satisfying

\displaystyle(1-\varepsilon)\frac{n}{d}\leq\|Pu_{k}\|^{2}=\|\zeta_{k}P\|^{2}=|\zeta_{k}(Pu_{k})|\leq(1+\varepsilon)\frac{n}{d},\quad\forall 1\leq k\leq d,

then there exists a projection (idempotent)
Q:\mathcal{X}\to\mathcal{X}
with

\displaystyle\|Qu_{k}\|^{2}=\|\zeta_{k}Q\|^{2}=|\zeta_{k}(Pu_{k})|=\frac{n}{d},\quad\forall 1\leq k\leq d,

satisfying

\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{d}\frac{1}{2}(\|Pu_{k}-Qu_{k}\|^{2}+\|\zeta_{k}P-\zeta_{k}Q\|^{2})\leq g(\varepsilon,n,d).

We already remarked that Paulsen and projection problems were connected using chordal distance. Even though we are unable to connect Pausen and projection problems for Banach spaces, we introduce the notion of chordal distance for subspaces of Banach spaces. Chordal distance between two subspaces of a finite dimensional Hilbert space is defined using angles between them. Since we do not have inner product we take alternative way using a characterization to introduce the notion for Banach spaces.

Definition 2.15
.

Let
\mathcal{X}
be a
d
-dimensional Banach space and

\mathcal{Y}

\displaystyle\operatorname{dist}_{\operatorname{chordal}}(\mathcal{Y},\mathcal{Z})\coloneqq(m-\operatorname{Trace}(PQ))^{\frac{1}{2}}.
