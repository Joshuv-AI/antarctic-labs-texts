BSE - property of tensor product Banach algebras

Abstract
Let A A and B B be commutative semisimple Banach algebras. In this paper, the BSE and BED - property of tensor Banach algebra A ⊗ γ B A{\otimes}_{\gamma}B with respect to the Banach algebras A A and B B are assesed. In particular, BSE and BED - structure of vector-valued group algebra L 1 ​ ( G , A ) L_{1}(G,A) and C ∗ C^{*} - algebra C 0 ​ ( X , A ) C_{0}(X,A) characterized. Keywords: Banach algebra, BSE - algebra, BED - algebra, tensor product.

.
Assume that
\sigma_{n}=\sum_{k=1}^{l_{n}}\sigma_{k}^{n}\otimes\eta_{k}^{n}
, where
\sigma_{k}^{n}\in C_{\textup{BSE}}^{0}(\Delta(A))
and
\eta_{k}^{n}\in C_{\textup{BSE}}^{0}(\Delta(B))
.
If
S_{n}=\sum_{k=1}^{p_{n}}\mu_{k}^{n}\odot\nu_{k}^{n}=\sigma_{n}|_{\Delta(A)\times\Delta(B)}
, then

\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{l_{n}}\sigma_{k}^{n}\otimes\eta_{k}^{n}(\widehat{\varphi},\widehat{\psi})

\displaystyle:=\sum_{k=1}^{l_{n}}\widehat{\varphi}(\sigma_{k}^{n})\widehat{\psi}(\eta_{k}^{n})

\displaystyle:=\sum_{k=1}^{l_{n}}\sigma_{k}^{n}(\varphi)\eta_{k}^{n}(\psi)

\displaystyle:=\sum_{k=1}^{l_{n}}\mu_{k}^{n}(\varphi)\nu_{k}^{n}(\psi)

\displaystyle=S_{n}(\varphi,\psi)

It is clear that
{\widehat{\varphi}}\in{C_{\textup{BSE}}^{0}(\Delta(A))}^{*}
and

{\widehat{\psi}}\in{C_{\textup{BSE}}^{0}(\Delta(B))}^{*}
. Therefore

S_{n}=\sum_{k=1}^{l_{n}}\mu_{k}^{n}\odot\nu_{k}^{n}\in<C_{\textup{BSE}}^{0}(\Delta(A))\odot C_{\textup{BSE}}^{0}(\Delta(B))>

where
S_{n}(\varphi\otimes\psi)=\sigma_{n}({\widehat{\varphi}},{\widehat{\psi}})
, for all
\varphi\in\Delta(A)
,
\psi\in\Delta(B)
and
n\in\mathbb{N}
. The following is yield:

\displaystyle\sigma:=\underset{n}{lim}\sigma_{n}=\underset{n}{lim}(\sum_{k=1}^{l_{n}}\sigma_{k}^{n}\otimes\eta_{k}^{n})

At a result

\displaystyle\sigma(\varphi\otimes\psi)=\sigma({\widehat{\varphi}},{\widehat{\psi}})

\displaystyle=\underset{n}{lim}\sigma_{n}({\widehat{\varphi}},{\widehat{\psi}})

\displaystyle=\underset{n}{lim}\sum_{k=1}^{l_{n}}\mu_{k}^{n}\odot\nu_{k}^{n}(\varphi\otimes\psi)=\underset{n}{lim}S_{n}(\varphi,\psi)

and

\|S_{n}-\sigma\|_{\textup{BSE}}\leq\|\sigma_{n}-\sigma\|_{\epsilon}\to 0

At a result

Therefore

C_{\textup{BSE}}^{0}(\Delta(A\otimes_{\gamma}B))\subseteq C_{\textup{BSE}}^{0}(\Delta(A))\overset{{\textup{BSE}}}{\odot}C_{\textup{BSE}}^{0}(\Delta(B)).

∎
