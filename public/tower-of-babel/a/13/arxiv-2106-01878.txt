Chu representations of categories related to constructive mathematics
Iosif Petrakis

Abstract
If 𝒞 \mathcal{C} is a closed symmetric monoidal category, the Chu category Chu ​ ( 𝒞 , γ ) \textnormal{{Chu}}(\mathcal{C},\gamma) over 𝒞 \mathcal{C} and an object γ \gamma of it was defined by Chu in [ 5 ] , as a ∗ * -autonomous category generated from 𝒞 \mathcal{C} . In [ 6 ] Bishop introduced the category 𝓟 ⟧ ⟦ ( X ) \bm{\mathcal{P}}^{\bm{\rrbracket}\bm{\llbracket}}(X) of complemented subsets of a set X X , in order to overcome the problems generated by the use of negation in constructive measure theory. In [ 34 ] Shulman mentions that Bishop’s complemented subsets correspond roughly to the Chu construction. In this paper we explain this correspondence by showing that there is a Chu representation (a full embedding) of 𝓟 ⟧ ⟦ ( X ) \bm{\mathcal{P}}^{\bm{\rrbracket}\bm{\llbracket}}(X) into Chu ​ ( 𝐒𝐞𝐭 , X × X ) \textnormal{{Chu}}(\mathrm{\mathbf{Set}},X\times X) . A Chu representation of the category of Bishop spaces into Chu ​ ( 𝐒𝐞𝐭 , ℝ ) \textnormal{{Chu}}(\mathrm{\mathbf{Set}},{\mathbb{R}}) is shown, as the constructive analogue to the standard Chu representation of the category of topological spaces into Chu ​ ( 𝐒𝐞𝐭 , 𝟚 ) \textnormal{{Chu}}(\mathrm{\mathbf{Set}},\mathbb{2}) . In order to represent the category of predicates (with objects pairs ( X , A ) (X,A) , where A A is a subset of X X , and the category of complemented predicates (with objects pairs ( X , A ) (X,A) , where A A is a complemented subset of X X , we generalise the Chu construction by defining the Chu category over a cartesian closed category 𝒞 \mathcal{C} and an endofunctor on 𝒞 \mathcal{C} . Finally, we introduce the antiparallel Grothendieck construction over a product category and a contravariant 𝐒𝐞𝐭 \mathrm{\mathbf{Set}} -valued functor on it of which the Chu construction is a special case, in case 𝒞 \mathcal{C} is a locally small, cartesian closed category. Keywords : category theory, Chu construction, Grothendieck construction, constructive mathematics, complemented subsets, Bishop spaces.

There is a rich representation theory connected to the Chu construction, as many important, and quite different categories are
