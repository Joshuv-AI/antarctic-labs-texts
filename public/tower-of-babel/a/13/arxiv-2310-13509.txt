\displaystyle K

\displaystyle n

\displaystyle\mathbb{Z}_{K}

\displaystyle d_{K}

(1.1)

\Delta(\alpha)=\pm(\mathbb{Z}_{K}:\mathbb{Z}[\alpha])^{2}\cdot d_{K},

\displaystyle 1930

8

\displaystyle n\leq 7

\displaystyle p\mathbb{Z}_{K}

\displaystyle\nu_{p}

\displaystyle p
-adic valuation of
\displaystyle\mathbb{Q}

\displaystyle 22

[
19

\displaystyle\nu_{p}(i(K))

\displaystyle i(K)

[
23

\displaystyle p

\displaystyle\nu_{p}(i(K))

\displaystyle p\mathbb{Z}_{K}

[
20

\displaystyle p
-adic characterization of the index of a number field. In
[
18
]
, Nakahara studied the index of non-cyclic but abelian biquadratic number fields. In
[
10
]
Gaál et al. characterized the field indices of biquadratic number fields having Galois group
\displaystyle V_{4}
. In
[
5
]
, we characterized the index and studied the monogenity of any sextic number field defined by
\displaystyle x^{6}+ax^{5}+b
. In
[
6

, for every rational prime
\displaystyle p

\displaystyle\nu_{p}(i(K))

\displaystyle x^{7}+ax^{3}+b

[
4

\displaystyle x^{5}+ax^{3}+b

[

We have introduced the corresponding concepts in several former papers. Here we only give the theorem of index of Ore which plays a key role for proving our main results.
