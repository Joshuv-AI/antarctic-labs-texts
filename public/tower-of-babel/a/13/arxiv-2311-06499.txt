Anwesh Ray

1.
Introduction

Let
K
be a number field and
p
be a prime number. Denote by
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
, the ring of
p
-adic integers. A
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
-extension
K_{\infty}/K
is an infinite Galois extension of
K
such that
\operatorname{Gal}(K_{\infty}/K)
is isomorphic to
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
as a topological group. Let
K_{n}/K
be the subfield of
K_{\infty}
for which
\operatorname{Gal}(K_{n}/K)\simeq\mathbb{Z}/p^{n}\mathbb{Z}
. Denote by
\operatorname{Cl}_{p}(K_{n})
the
p
-primary part of the class group of
K_{n}
. We write
h_{p}(K_{n})=p^{e_{n}}
to denote the cardinality of
\operatorname{Cl}_{p}(K_{n})
. The fields
\{K_{n}\}
form a
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
-tower of extensions

K=K_{0}\subset K_{1}\subset\dots\subset K_{n}\subset K_{n+1}\subset\dots,

Iwasawa showed that there are constants
\mu,\lambda\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}
and
\nu\in\mathbb{Z}
such that

e_{n}=p^{n}\mu+n\lambda+\nu

for
n\gg 0
(cf.
[
Iwa73
]
or
[
Was97
, Chapter 13]
). The quantities
\mu
and
\lambda
are the
\mu
and
\lambda
-invariants associated to
K_{\infty}/K
respectively. The
cyclotomic
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
-extension

K_{\operatorname{cyc}}/K
is the unique
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
-extension of
K
which is contained in
K(\mu_{p^{\infty}})
. Iwasawa conjectured that for the cyclotomic
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
-extension of any number field
K
, the
\mu
-invariant vanishes. When
K/\mathbb{Q}
is an abelian extension, the conjecture was resolved by Ferrero and Washington
[
FW79
]
.

Mazur (cf.
[
Maz72
]
) introduced a generalization of Iwasawa’s theory in the context of Selmer groups associated to abelian varieties defined over a number field with good ordinary reduction at the primes above
p
. The Selmer groups are defined by local conditions prescribed the images of (local) Kummer maps. A closer analogy holds with the class group, when one considers the
fine Selmer group
. This is the Selmer group for which the local conditions at primes of bad reduction are defined by the strict local condition. In this context, there is an analogue of the
\mu=0
conjecture, that was formulated by Coates and Sujatha (cf.
[
CS05
, Conjecture A]
).

In this article, we study function field analogues of questions posed for elliptic curves over number fields. Drinfeld modules natural objects to consider over function fields that give rise to compatible systems of Galois representations. What is important is that for Drinfeld modules of
generic characteristic
these representations cut out separable extensions and thus it is possible to define an analogue of the fine Selmer group in this context. Let
q
be a prime power and
\mathbb{F}_{q}
be the field with
q
elements. Denote by
A
the polynomial ring
\mathbb{F}_{q}[T]
and
F
its fraction field. Let
\phi
be a Drinfeld module over
F
, and
\mathfrak{p}
be a non-zero prime ideal of
A
. Denote by
\mathcal{O}
the completion of
A
at
\mathfrak{p}
, and set
\mathcal{K}\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=\operatorname{Frac}\mathcal{O}
. Then, its module of
\mathfrak{p}
-primary torsion points
\phi[\mathfrak{p}^{\infty}]
is isomorphic to
(\mathcal{K}/\mathcal{O})^{r}
, where
r
is the rank of
\phi
.

Let
F^{\operatorname{nr}}=\bar{\mathbb{F}}_{q}(T)
be the maximal unramified extension of
F
, and choose isomorphisms

\operatorname{Gal}(F^{\operatorname{nr}}/F)\xrightarrow{\sim}\operatorname{Gal}(\bar{\mathbb{F}}_{q}/\mathbb{F}_{q})\xrightarrow{\sim}\widehat{\mathbb{Z}}.

Denote by
\mathcal{F}
the unique
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
-extension of
F
that is contained in
F^{\operatorname{nr}}
. This
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
-extension is given by
\mathcal{F}=\kappa(T)
, where
\kappa\subset\bar{\mathbb{F}}_{q}
is a
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
-extension of
\mathbb{F}_{q}
. It is for this reason that
\mathcal{F}
is referred to as the
constant
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
-extension
of
F
. It is of significance that any prime of
F
is finitely decomposed in the infinite extension
\mathcal{F}
. Let
\Gamma
denote the Galois group
\Gamma\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=\operatorname{Gal}(\mathcal{F}/F)
and
\Lambda_{\mathcal{O}}\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=\mathcal{O}\llbracket\Gamma\rrbracket
be the
Iwasawa algebra
over
\mathcal{O}
. We define the fine Selmer group
\operatorname{Sel}_{0}(\phi[\mathfrak{p}^{\infty}]/\mathcal{F})
to be the subgroup of
H^{1}(\operatorname{Gal}(F^{\operatorname{sep}}/\mathcal{F}),\phi[\mathfrak{p}^{\infty}])
consisting of cohomology classes that are trivial when restricted to any prime of
\mathcal{F}
. This Selmer group is a module over the Iwasawa algebra
\Lambda_{\mathcal{O}}
and is the precise analogue of the Selmer group considered in
[
CS05
]
. We study the algebraic structure of
\operatorname{Sel}_{0}(\phi[\mathfrak{p}^{\infty}]/\mathcal{F})
and show that it is cofinitely generated and cotorsion over
\Lambda_{\mathcal{O}}
. We define the Iwasawa
\mu
and
\lambda
invariants associated to
\operatorname{Sel}_{0}(\phi[\mathfrak{p}^{\infty}]/\mathcal{F})
, denoted
\mu_{\mathfrak{p}}(\phi)
and
\lambda_{\mathfrak{p}}(\phi)
. We show that
\mu_{\mathfrak{p}}(\phi)=0
and that
\operatorname{Sel}_{0}(\phi[\mathfrak{p}^{\infty}]/\mathcal{F})
is cofinitely generated as a
\mathcal{O}
-module, and its
\mathcal{O}
-corank is equal to
\lambda_{\mathfrak{p}}(\phi)
. Moreover, we obtain an upper bound for the size of
\lambda_{\mathfrak{p}}(\phi)
in terms of the dimension of the
residual fine Selmer group

\operatorname{Sel}_{0}(\phi[\mathfrak{p}]/\mathcal{F})
and certain local invariants associated to the primes at which
\phi
has bad reduction and the primes
\mathfrak{p}
and
\infty
. Let
\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{p}}
denote the residue field of
\mathcal{O}
. The main result is stated below.

Theorem 1.1
.

Let
\phi
be a Drinfeld module over
F
of rank
r
and
\mathfrak{p}
be a non-zero prime of
A
. Denote by
S
the set of primes
\{\mathfrak{p},\infty\}
and the primes at which
\phi
has bad reduction. Let
\mathcal{F}
be the constant
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
-extension of
F
. Then, the following assertions hold

(1)

\operatorname{Sel}_{0}(\phi[\mathfrak{p}^{\infty}]/\mathcal{F})
is a cofinitely generated cotorsion
\Lambda_{\mathcal{O}}
-module with
\mu_{\mathfrak{p}}(\phi)=0
,

(2)

\operatorname{Sel}_{0}(\phi[\mathfrak{p}^{\infty}]/\mathcal{F})
is cofinitely generated as a module over
\mathcal{O}
and has corank equal to
\lambda_{p}(\phi)
.

(3)

Let
\operatorname{Sel}_{0}(\phi[\mathfrak{p}]/\mathcal{F})
be the residual fine Selmer group (cf. Definition
4.2
). Then, we have the following bound for the
\lambda
-invariant

\lambda_{\mathfrak{p}}(\phi)\leq\operatorname{dim}_{\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{p}}}\operatorname{Sel}_{0}(\phi[\mathfrak{p}]/\mathcal{F})+\sum_{w\in S(\mathcal{F})}\dim_{\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{p}}}\left(H^{0}(\mathcal{F}_{w},\phi[\mathfrak{p}^{\infty}])\otimes_{\mathcal{O}}\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{p}}\right).

Thus, the equivalent of the
\mu=0
conjecture of Coates and Sujatha is resolved for Drinfeld modules over
F
. In the bound for the
\lambda
-invariant above, the dimension of
\operatorname{Sel}_{0}(\phi[\mathfrak{p}]/\mathcal{F})
is shown to be finite. The set of primes
S(\mathcal{F})
is finite and the local invariants satisfy

\dim_{\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{p}}}\left(H^{0}(\mathcal{F}_{w},\phi[\mathfrak{p}^{\infty}])\otimes_{\mathcal{O}}\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{p}}\right)\leq r.

Furthermore, if
H^{0}(F_{v},\phi[\mathfrak{p}])=0
, then,
H^{0}(\mathcal{F}_{w},\phi[\mathfrak{p}^{\infty}])=0
for all primes
w|v
(cf. Proposition
4.6
). Our technique leverages a theorem of Rosen, who proves
\mu=0
for the
p
-primary class groups in a constant
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
-extension of a function field (cf.
[
Ros73
, p.293, l.-2]
or
[
Ros02
, Theorem 11.5]
). The referee has pointed out that the methods in this paper should in principle generalize to the setting of Drinfeld modules over a function field, however, this has not been undertaken in the current work.

The article consists of four sections, including the introduction. In section
2
we discuss the theory of Drinfeld modules and set up relevant notation. In section
3
, the fine Selmer group is introduced and we associate the Iwasawa invariants to this module. In the section
4
, the main result is proven. We provide an illustrative example in subsection
4.3
.

Drinfeld modules and associated Galois representations

]

in

For

\operatorname{G}_{S}\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=\operatorname{Gal}(F_{S}/F)

\begin{split}&f(x+y)=f(x)+f(y);\\
&f(\alpha x)=\alpha f(x)\text{ for all }\alpha\in\mathbb{F}_{q}.\end{split}

Given an
\mathbb{F}_{q}
-algebra
K
, denote by

that are

-linear. For

. Let

be the ring of twisted polynomials

f(\tau)=\sum_{i=0}^{d}a_{i}\tau^{i}

. In this ring, addition is given by

\sum_{i}a_{i}\tau^{i}+\sum_{i}b_{i}\tau^{i}=\sum_{i}(a_{i}+b_{i})\tau^{i},

and multiplication is prescribed by the relation

\begin{split}\tau^{i}\alpha=\alpha^{q^{i}}\tau^{i}.\end{split}

Since
\alpha^{q}=\alpha
for all
\alpha\in\mathbb{F}_{q}
, we find that
K\langle x\rangle
is an
\mathbb{F}_{q}
-algebra.

There is a natural isomorphism of
\mathbb{F}_{q}
-algebras

\eta\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}K\{\tau\}\xrightarrow{\sim}K\langle x\rangle,

defined by

\eta\left(\sum_{i=0}^{d}a_{i}\tau^{i}\right)\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=\sum_{i=0}^{d}a_{i}x^{q^{i}}.

For
f(\tau)\in K\{\tau\}
, we shall set
f(x)\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=\eta(f)
.

Definition 2.1
.

Let

, write

f(\tau)=\sum_{i=h}^{d}a_{i}\tau^{i},

where
a_{h}a_{d}\neq 0
. Then, the
height
(resp.

) of

is defined by setting
\operatorname{ht}_{\tau}(f)\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=h
(resp.
\operatorname{deg}_{\tau}(f)\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=d
). The formal derivative of
f(x)
is given by
\partial(f)=a_{0}
(taken to be
0
if
\operatorname{ht}_{\tau}(f)>0
). We find that
f(x)
is separable if an only if
\operatorname{ht}_{\tau}(f)=0
.

Let
K
be an
\mathbb{F}_{q}
-algebra, we say that
K
is an
A
-field if there is a prescribed homomorphism of
\mathbb{F}_{q}
-algebras
\gamma\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}A\rightarrow K
.

Definition 2.2
.

Let
K
be an
A
-field and

. A

of rank

\phi\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}A\rightarrow K\{\tau\},

taking

(1)

\partial(\phi_{a})=\gamma(a)
,

(2)

\operatorname{deg}_{\tau}(\phi_{a})=r\operatorname{deg}_{T}(a)
.

We shall set
r(\phi)
to denote the rank of
\phi
. Since
\phi
is a homorphism of
\mathbb{F}_{q}
-algebras, it completely determined by

\phi_{T}=\gamma(T)+\sum_{i=1}^{r}a_{i}\tau^{i},

where
a_{r}\neq 0
.

Definition 2.3
.

Let
(K,\gamma)
be an
A
-field. We set

if
\gamma
is injective. We say in this case that

is
generic characteristic
. On the other hand, if

, we set
\operatorname{char}_{A}(K)\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=\mathcal{P}
.

We note that if
K
has generic characteristic, then
\partial(\phi_{a})=\gamma(a)\neq 0
for all
a\neq 0
in

. Therefore, in this case,
\phi_{a}(x)
is a separable polynomial for all
a\in A
. When
\operatorname{char}_{A}(K)=\mathcal{P}\neq 0
, we find that
\phi_{a}(x)
is separable polynomial if and only if
\mathcal{P}\nmid a
. Given a non-zero ideal
\mathfrak{a}
of
A
, define
\phi_{\mathfrak{a}}\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=\phi_{a}
, where
a
is the monic polynomial that generates

Since

\gamma

H(\phi)\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=\begin{cases}0&\text{ if }\operatorname{char}_{A}(K)=0;\\
\left(\frac{\operatorname{ht}_{\tau}(\phi_{\mathcal{P}})}{\operatorname{deg}\mathcal{P}}\right)&\text{ if }\operatorname{char}_{A}(K)=\mathcal{P}\neq 0.\\
\end{cases}

Given a Drinfeld modules

This ring is in fact is an

\phi_{a}
. Since

Let
\phi

\operatorname{deg}u+\operatorname{deg}(u^{-1})=\operatorname{deg}_{\tau}(uu^{-1})=\operatorname{deg}_{\tau}(1)=0.

\begin{split}\psi_{T}=&\sum_{i=0}^{r}c^{-1}g_{i}\tau^{i}c\\
=&\sum_{i=0}^{r}c^{q^{i}-1}g_{i}\tau^{i}.\end{split}

Given a non-zero ideal

3.
Fine Selmer groups and their properties

Recall that

Proof.

Assume that

is is a cofinitely generated, cotorsion as a

-module with

is finite.

Lemma 4.3
.

The natural map

\operatorname{Sel}_{0}(\phi[\mathfrak{p}]/\mathcal{F})\xrightarrow{\gamma}\operatorname{Sel}_{0}(\phi[\mathfrak{p}^{\infty}]/\mathcal{F})[\mathfrak{p}]

has finite kernel and cokernel, whose size satisfy the upper bounds

\begin{split}&\#\operatorname{ker}\gamma\leq\#\left(H^{0}(\mathcal{F},\phi[\mathfrak{p}^{\infty}])\otimes_{\mathcal{O}}\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{p}}\right)\leq|\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{p}}|^{r},\\
&\#\operatorname{cok}\gamma\leq\prod_{w\in S(\mathcal{F})}\#\left(H^{0}(\mathcal{F}_{w},\phi[\mathfrak{p}^{\infty}])\otimes_{\mathcal{O}}\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{p}}\right)\leq|\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{p}}|^{\#S(\mathcal{F})r}.\end{split}

Proof.

From the commutative diagram (
4.2
), we obtain the exact sequence

0\rightarrow\operatorname{ker}\gamma\rightarrow\operatorname{ker}\beta\rightarrow\operatorname{ker}h^{\prime}\rightarrow\operatorname{cok}\gamma\rightarrow 0.

Therefore, in particular, we find that

\begin{split}&\#\operatorname{ker}\gamma\leq\#\operatorname{ker}\beta=\#\left(H^{0}(\mathcal{F},\phi[\mathfrak{p}^{\infty}])\otimes_{\mathcal{O}}\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{p}}\right),\\
&\#\operatorname{cok}\gamma\leq\#\operatorname{ker}h^{\prime}\leq\#\operatorname{ker}h\leq\prod_{w\in S(\mathcal{F})}\#\left(H^{0}(\mathcal{F}_{w},\phi[\mathfrak{p}^{\infty}])\otimes_{\mathcal{O}}\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{p}}\right).\end{split}

Since
\phi[\mathfrak{p}^{\infty}]\simeq\left(\mathcal{K}/\mathcal{O}\right)^{r}
, we find that

\#\left(H^{0}(\mathcal{F},\phi[\mathfrak{p}^{\infty}])\otimes_{\mathcal{O}}\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{p}}\right),\#\left(H^{0}(\mathcal{F}_{w},\phi[\mathfrak{p}^{\infty}])\otimes_{\mathcal{O}}\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{p}}\right)\leq|\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{p}}|^{r}.

The result has thus been proven.
∎

Proposition 4.4
.

The following are equivalent

(1)

\operatorname{Sel}_{0}(\phi[\mathfrak{p}^{\infty}]/\mathcal{F})
is a cofinitely generated cotorsion
\Lambda_{\mathcal{O}}
-module with
\mu_{\mathfrak{p}}(\phi)=0
.

(2)

\operatorname{Sel}_{0}(\phi[\mathfrak{p}]/\mathcal{F})
is finite.

Furthermore, if the above equivalent assertions are satisfied, then,

\lambda_{\mathfrak{p}}(\phi)\leq\operatorname{dim}_{\kappa}\operatorname{Sel}_{0}(\phi[\mathfrak{p}]/\mathcal{F})+\sum_{w\in S(\mathcal{F})}\dim_{\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{p}}}\left(H^{0}(\mathcal{F}_{w},\phi[\mathfrak{p}^{\infty}])\otimes_{\mathcal{O}}\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{p}}\right).

Proof.

Let
M
denote the Selmer group
\operatorname{Sel}_{0}(\phi[\mathfrak{p}^{\infty}]/\mathcal{F})
. Lemma
3.5
asserts that
M
is a cofinitely generated, cotorsion as a
\Lambda_{\mathcal{O}}
-module with
\mu_{\mathfrak{p}}(M)=0
if and only if
M[\varpi]
is finite. On the other hand, it follows from Lemma
4.3
that
M[\varpi]
is finite if and only if
\operatorname{Sel}_{0}(\phi[\mathfrak{p}]/\mathcal{F})
is finite. This proves that the conditions (
1
) and (
2
) are equivalent. In order to obtain the upper bound on
\lambda_{\mathfrak{p}}(\phi)
we recall from Lemma
3.5
that
\lambda_{\mathfrak{p}}(\phi)\leq\dim_{\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{p}}}M[\varpi]
. Note that
M[\varpi]=\operatorname{Sel}_{0}(\phi[\mathfrak{p}^{\infty}]/\mathcal{F})[\mathfrak{p}]
; it follows from Lemma
4.3
that

\begin{split}\dim_{\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{p}}}M[\varpi]\leq&\dim_{\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{p}}}\operatorname{Sel}_{0}(\phi[\mathfrak{p}]/\mathcal{F})+\dim_{\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{p}}}\operatorname{cok}\gamma,\\
\leq&\dim_{\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{p}}}\operatorname{Sel}_{0}(\phi[\mathfrak{p}]/\mathcal{F})+\sum_{w\in S(\mathcal{F})}\dim_{\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{p}}}\left(H^{0}(\mathcal{F}_{w},\phi[\mathfrak{p}^{\infty}])\otimes_{\mathcal{O}}\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{p}}\right).\end{split}

This concludes the proof of the result.
∎

We demonstrate conditions under which the terms
\dim_{\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{p}}}\left(H^{0}(\mathcal{F}_{w},\phi[\mathfrak{p}^{\infty}])\otimes_{\mathcal{O}}\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{p}}\right)
vanish.

Lemma 4.5
.

Let
G
and
M
be finite abelian groups of
p
-power order such that
G
acts on
M
. Suppose that
M^{G}=0
, then
M=0
.

Proof.

The result follows from
[
NSW08
, Proposition 1.6.12]
.
∎

Proposition 4.6
.

Assume that
v
is a prime at which

H^{0}(F_{v},\phi[\mathfrak{p}])=0.

Then, for all primes
w\in v(\mathcal{F})
, we have that

H^{0}(\mathcal{F}_{w},\phi[\mathfrak{p}^{\infty}])=0.

Proof.

Let
M\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=H^{0}(\mathcal{F}_{w},\phi[\mathfrak{p}])
and
\Gamma\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=\operatorname{Gal}(\mathcal{F}_{w}/F_{v})
, and identify
M^{\Gamma}
with
H^{0}(\mathcal{F}_{v},\phi[\mathfrak{p}])
. By assumption,
M^{\Gamma}=0
. The action of
\Gamma
is continuous and
M
is finite. Thus, there is a finite index subgroup
\Gamma_{0}
of
\Gamma
such that
M=M^{\Gamma_{0}}
. It follows from Lemma
4.5
that
M=M^{\Gamma/\Gamma_{0}}=0
. In particular, this implies that

H^{0}(\mathcal{F}_{w},\phi[\mathfrak{p}^{\infty}])=0.

∎

Proposition 4.7
.

With respect to above notation, the residual Selmer group
\operatorname{Sel}_{0}(\phi[\mathfrak{p}]/\mathcal{F})
is finite.

Proof.

Set
L\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=F(\phi[\mathfrak{p}])
to be the field extension of
F
in
F^{\operatorname{sep}}
that is fixed by the kernel of
\rho_{\phi,\mathfrak{p}}\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}\operatorname{G}_{F}\rightarrow\operatorname{Aut}(\phi[\mathfrak{p}])
. Denote by
\mathcal{L}
the constant
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
-extension of
L
and identify
\mathcal{L}
with the composite
\mathcal{F}\cdot L
. Let
H_{S}(\mathcal{L})
be the maximal abelian unramified prop-extension of
\mathcal{L}
in which the all primes of
S(\mathcal{L})
are completely split. Set
X_{S}(\mathcal{L})
to denote the Galois group
\operatorname{Gal}(H_{S}(\mathcal{L})/\mathcal{L})
. Theorem
4.1
asserts that the
\mu
-invariant of
X_{S}(\mathcal{L})
is equal to
0
. It thus follows from the structure theory of
\Lambda_{\mathbb{Z}_{p}}
-modules that
X_{S}(\mathcal{L})
is finitely generated
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
-module. Let
\bar{H}_{S}(\mathcal{L})
be the maximal
p
-elementary extension of
\mathcal{L}
that is contained in
H_{S}(\mathcal{L})
. Set
\bar{X}_{S}(\mathcal{L})\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=\operatorname{Gal}(\bar{H}_{S}(\mathcal{L})/\mathcal{L})
, and identify
\bar{X}_{S}(\mathcal{L})
with
X_{S}(\mathcal{L})/pX_{S}(\mathcal{L})
.

Set
G\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=\operatorname{Gal}(\mathcal{L}/\mathcal{F})
, note that the restriction of
\rho_{\phi,\mathfrak{p}}
to
\operatorname{G}_{L}
is trivial, hence,
\phi[\mathfrak{p}]^{\operatorname{G}_{L}}=\phi[\mathfrak{p}]
. Consider the inflation-restriction sequence

0\rightarrow H^{1}(G,\phi[\mathfrak{p}])\xrightarrow{inf}H^{1}(F_{S}/\mathcal{F},\phi[\mathfrak{p}])\xrightarrow{res}H^{1}(F_{S}/\mathcal{L},\phi[\mathfrak{p}]).

The module
\phi[\mathfrak{p}]
is a trivial module over
\operatorname{G}_{\mathcal{L}}
, and hence

H^{1}(F_{S}/\mathcal{L},\phi[\mathfrak{p}])\simeq\operatorname{Hom}(\operatorname{Gal}(F_{S}/\mathcal{L}),\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{p}}^{r}).

For
f\in\operatorname{Sel}_{0}(\phi[\mathfrak{p}]/\mathcal{F})
, the restriction of
f
to
\operatorname{Gal}(F_{S}/\mathcal{L})
is unramified at all primes and split at all primes of
S(\mathcal{L})
. Therefore,
\operatorname{res}(f)
factors as a homomorphism of groups

\operatorname{res}(f)\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}\bar{X}_{S}(\mathcal{L})\rightarrow\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{p}}^{r}.

Since
X_{S}(\mathcal{L})
is finitely generated as a
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
-module,
\bar{X}_{S}(\mathcal{L})
is finite. Thus, we find that
\operatorname{res}\left(\operatorname{Sel}_{0}(\phi[\mathfrak{p}]/\mathcal{F})\right)
is finite. On the other hand, since
G
is finite, the cohomology group
H^{1}(G,\phi[\mathfrak{p}])
is finite. Therefore, we have shown that
\operatorname{Sel}_{0}(\phi[\mathfrak{p}]/\mathcal{F})
is finite.
∎

We now prove Theorem
1.1
.

Proof.

Part (
1
) and (
3
) of the result are a direct consequence of Proposition
4.4
and Proposition
4.7
. On the other hand, part (
2
) is an easy consequence of part (
1
) and the structure theory of
\Lambda_{\mathcal{O}}
-modules. In greater detail, since
Y(\phi[\mathfrak{p}^{\infty}]/\mathcal{F})
is finitely generated and torsion as a module over
\Lambda_{\mathcal{O}}
with
\mu=0
, it follows that it is pseudo-isomorphic to a free
\mathcal{O}
-module of rank
\lambda_{\mathfrak{p}}(\phi)
. Therefore, it is finitely generated as an
\mathcal{O}
-module with
\mathcal{O}
-rank equal to
\lambda_{p}(\phi)
.
∎

4.3.
An illustrative example

Let us illustrate Theorem
1.1
through an example. The Carlitz module over
F
is the rank
1
Drinfeld module defined by taking
\phi_{T}\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=T+\tau
. For simplicity, we assume that
p
is odd and
q=p
. Letting
\mathfrak{p}\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=(T)
, it is easy to see that
\phi
has good reduction at all primes and thus one can take the set
S
in Theorem
1.1
to be
\{\mathfrak{p},\infty\}
. Thus the Theorem asserts that
\mu_{\mathfrak{p}}(\phi)=0
and that

\begin{split}\lambda_{\mathfrak{p}}(\phi)&\leq\operatorname{dim}_{\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{p}}}\operatorname{Sel}_{0}(\phi[\mathfrak{p}]/\mathcal{F})\\
+&\dim_{\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{p}}}\left(H^{0}(\mathcal{F}_{\mathfrak{p}},\phi[\mathfrak{p}^{\infty}])\otimes_{\mathcal{O}}\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{p}}\right)+\dim_{\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{p}}}\left(H^{0}(\mathcal{F}_{\infty},\phi[\mathfrak{p}^{\infty}])\otimes_{\mathcal{O}}\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{p}}\right).\end{split}

We identify
\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{p}}
with the field with
p
elements. Note that in the above formula,

\dim_{\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{p}}}\left(H^{0}(\mathcal{F}_{w},\phi[\mathfrak{p}^{\infty}])\otimes_{\mathcal{O}}\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{p}}\right)\leq\operatorname{dim}_{\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{p}}}\phi[\mathfrak{p}]=1

for
w\in\{\mathfrak{p},\infty\}
. The extension
\mathcal{F}_{\mathfrak{p}}
is an unramified extension of
F_{\mathfrak{p}}
. Since
\phi_{T}(x)/x=T+x^{p-1}
is Eisenstein at
\mathfrak{p}
, it follows that the character associated to
\phi[\mathfrak{p}]
is ramified at
\mathfrak{p}
. As a result,
\phi[\mathfrak{p}]
is nontrivial as a module over the absolute Galois group of
\mathcal{F}_{\mathfrak{p}}
. Therefore, one has that

\dim_{\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{p}}}\left(H^{0}(\mathcal{F}_{\mathfrak{p}},\phi[\mathfrak{p}^{\infty}])\otimes_{\mathcal{O}}\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{p}}\right)=\dim_{\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{p}}}\left(H^{0}(\mathcal{F}_{\mathfrak{p}},\phi[\mathfrak{p}])\right)=0.

On the other hand, it follows from
[
Pap23
, Theorem 7.1.13]
that
\phi[\mathfrak{p}]
is totally ramified at
\infty
. A similar argument therefore yields that

\dim_{\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{p}}}\left(H^{0}(\mathcal{F}_{\infty},\phi[\mathfrak{p}^{\infty}])\otimes_{\mathcal{O}}\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{p}}\right)=0.

Thus, we have shown that

\lambda_{\mathfrak{p}}(\phi)\leq\operatorname{dim}_{\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{p}}}\operatorname{Sel}_{0}(\phi[\mathfrak{p}]/\mathcal{F}).
