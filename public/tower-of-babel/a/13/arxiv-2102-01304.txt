The Boundedness of Fractional Integral Operators in Local and Global Mixed Morrey-type Spaces Thanks: The research was supported by the National Natural Science Fundation of China(12061069).
Houkun Zhang, Jiang Zhou

Abstract




Abstract:
In this paper, we introduced the local and global mixed Morrey-type spaces, and some properties of these spaces are also studied. After that, the necessary conditions of the boundedness of fractional integral operators
I_{\alpha}
are studied respectively in mixed-norm Lebesgue spaces and the local mixed Morrey-type spaces. Last but not least, the boundedness of
I_{\alpha}
is obtained by Hardy operators’ boundedness in weighted Lebesgue spaces. As corollaries, we acquire the boundedness of
I_{\alpha}
in mixed Morrey spaces(
Corollary 5.6
) and mixed Lebesgue spaces(
Corollary 5.7
) respectively. Particularly,
Corollary 5.7
obtains a sufficient and necessary condition.

Keywords:
Local and global mixed Morrey-type spaces; Fractional integral operators; Hardy operators

From

Lemma 2.1
(Hölder’s inequality for
L_{\vec{p}}
). Let

\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}f(x)g(x)\,dx\leq\|f\|_{L_{\vec{p}}}\|g\|_{L_{\vec{p}\,^{\prime}}}

holds.

Remark 2.2.
In particular, if

\frac{1}{\vec{p}}+\frac{1}{\vec{q}}=\frac{1}{\vec{r}}
,

and

, then

\|fg\|_{L_{\vec{r}}}\leq\|f\|_{L_{\vec{p}}}\|g\|_{L_{\vec{q}}}

also holds.

They are easily proved by successive applications of classical Hölder’s inequality.

Lemma 2.3
(Minkowski’s inequality for
L_{\vec{p}}
). Let
1\leq\vec{p}\leq\infty
. If
f,g\in L_{\vec{p}}
, then

\|f+g\|_{L_{\vec{p}}}\leq\|f\|_{L_{\vec{p}}}+\|g\|_{L_{\vec{p}}}.

It is easy to prove it by successive applications of classical Minkowski’s inequality.

Lemma 2.4
(Fatou’s property for
L_{\vec{p}}
). Let
0<\vec{p}\leq\infty
. Let
\{f_{i}\}_{i=1}^{\infty}
be a sequence of non-negative measurable functions on
\mathbb{R}^{n}
. Then

\left\|\liminf_{i\rightarrow\infty}f_{i}\right\|\leq\liminf_{i\rightarrow\infty}\left\|f_{i}\right\|.

Remark 2.5.
By classical Fatou’s property, it also can be proved that

\limsup_{i\rightarrow\infty}\left\|f_{i}\right\|\leq\left\|\limsup_{i\rightarrow\infty}f_{i}\right\|.

We state the Lebesgue differential theorem in the setting of mixed-norm Lebesgue spaces as the following lemma.

Lemma 2.6.
Let

and

, then

\lim_{r\rightarrow 0}\|\chi_{Q(x,r)}\|_{L_{\vec{p}}(\mathbb{R}^{n})}^{-1}\|f\|_{L_{\vec{p}}(Q(x,r))}=|f(x)|\penalty\ \penalty\ a.e.\penalty\ x\in\mathbb{R}^{n}.

Proof:
Let
\vec{p}=(p_{1},p_{2},\cdots,p_{n})
. Without less of generality, we assume

It is easy to know that

\|\chi_{Q(x,r)}\|_{L_{\vec{p}}}=|2r|^{\sum_{i=1}^{2}\frac{1}{p_{i}}},

where

Therefore, by the classical Lebesgue differential theorem, we get that for any
\epsilon>0
, there exist
\delta>0
, such that

and

\displaystyle\|\chi_{Q(x,r)}\|_{L_{\vec{p}}(\mathbb{R}^{n})}^{-1}\|f\|_{L_{\vec{p}}(Q(x,r))}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle|2r|^{-\sum_{i=1}^{2}\frac{1}{p_{i}}}\bigg(\int_{\mathbb{R}}\Big(\int_{\mathbb{R}}|f(y_{1},y_{2})|^{p_{1}}\chi_{Q(x,r)}(y_{1},y_{2})\,dy_{1}\Big)^{\frac{p_{2}}{p_{1}}}\,dy_{2}\bigg)^{\frac{1}{p_{2}}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\bigg(\frac{1}{2r}\int_{I_{2}}\Big(\frac{1}{2r}\int_{I_{1}}|f(y_{1},y_{2})|^{p_{1}}\,dy_{1}\Big)^{\frac{p_{2}}{p_{1}}}\,dy_{2}\bigg)^{\frac{1}{p_{2}}}

\displaystyle|f(x_{1},x_{2})|+\epsilon^{\frac{1}{p_{1}}}+\epsilon^{\frac{1}{p_{2}}}

where

\lim_{r\rightarrow 0}\|\chi_{Q(x,r)}\|_{L_{\vec{p}}(\mathbb{R}^{n})}^{-1}\|f\|_{L_{\vec{p}}(Q(x,r))}=|f(x)|\penalty\ \penalty\ a.e.\penalty\ x\in\mathbb{R}^{n}.
