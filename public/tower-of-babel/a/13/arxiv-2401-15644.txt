Abstract
. We build models using an indiscernible model sub-structures of λ κ ≥ {}^{\kappa\geq}\lambda and related more complicated structures. We use this to build various Boolean algebras.

This seems to be a reasonable strategy, and it works (see a little more below).
Now suppose, moreover, that we are asked to construct a complete Boolean algebra

\mathbf{B}
of cardinality
\lambda

We should assume that
\lambda^{\aleph_{0}}=\lambda

Assume toward a contradiction that

is a non-trivial, one-to-one endomorphism. We
can find

[
