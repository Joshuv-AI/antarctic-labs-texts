On the singular limit problem for a discontinuous nonlocal conservation law
Alexander Keimer, Lukas Pflug

Abstract
In this contribution we study the singular limit problem of a nonlocal conservation law with a discontinuity in space. The specific choice of the nonlocal kernel involving the spatial discontinuity as well enables it to obtain a maximum principle for the nonlocal equation. The corresponding local equation can be transformed diffeomorphically to a classical scalar conservation law where the well-know Kružkov theory can be applied. However, the nonlocal equation does not scale that way which is why the study of convergence is interesting to pursue. For exponential kernels of the nonlocal operator, we establish the converge to the corresponding local equation under mild conditions on the involved discontinuous velocity. We illustrate our results with some numerical examples.



Keywords:

Nonlocal Conservation Law, Discontinuous nonlocal conservation law, Existence, Uniqueness, Singular limit, Entropy solution, Convergence nonlocal to local

Lemma 4.1

(Transport equation for

Given the dynamics in

\displaystyle=-\tfrac{1}{\eta}\!\!\int_{x}^{\infty}\!\!\!\!\mathrm{e}^{\frac{x-y}{\eta}}v(y)\mathcal{W}(t,y)V^{\prime}(\mathcal{W}(t,y))\mathcal{W}_{y}(t,y)\,\mathrm{d}y

\displaystyle-\int_{x}^{\infty}\mathrm{e}^{\frac{x-y}{\eta}}v^{\prime}(y)V(\mathcal{W}(t,y))\mathcal{W}_{y}(t,y)\,\mathrm{d}y,

\displaystyle(t,x)\in{\Omega_{T}},

\displaystyle x\in\mathbb{R}.

Recalling the nonlocal term we have for

The derivative of

\displaystyle\partial_{x}\mathcal{W}_{\eta}(t,x)=\tfrac{1}{\eta}\big(\mathcal{W}_{\eta}(t,x)-v(x)q(t,x)\big)\Rightarrow v(x)q(t,x)=\mathcal{W}_{\eta}(t,x)-\eta\partial_{x}\mathcal{W}_{\eta}(t,x).

\displaystyle\partial_{t}\mathcal{W}_{\eta}(t,x)

\displaystyle=-\tfrac{1}{\eta}\int_{x}^{\infty}v(y)\exp(\tfrac{x-y}{\eta})\partial_{y}\big(v(y)V(\mathcal{W}_{\eta}(t,y))q(t,y)\big)\,\mathrm{d}y

\displaystyle\quad-\tfrac{1}{\eta^{2}}\int_{x}^{\infty}v(y)^{2}\mathrm{e}^{\frac{x-y}{\eta}}V(\mathcal{W}_{\eta}(t,y))q(t,y)\,\mathrm{d}y,

taking advantage of identity

\displaystyle\quad+\tfrac{1}{\eta}\int_{x}^{\infty}\!\!\!\!\!v^{\prime}(y)\exp(\tfrac{x-y}{\eta})V(\mathcal{W}_{\eta}(t,y))\mathcal{W}_{\eta}(t,y)\,\mathrm{d}y

\displaystyle\quad-\int_{x}^{\infty}\!\!\!\!\!v^{\prime}(y)\exp(\tfrac{x-y}{\eta})V(\mathcal{W}_{\eta}(t,y))\partial_{y}\mathcal{W}_{\eta}(t,y)\,\mathrm{d}y

\displaystyle\quad-\tfrac{1}{\eta^{2}}\int_{x}^{\infty}\!\!\!\!v(y)\mathrm{e}^{\frac{x-y}{\eta}}V(\mathcal{W}_{\eta}(t,y))\mathcal{W}_{\eta}(t,y)\,\mathrm{d}y

\displaystyle\quad+\tfrac{1}{\eta}\int_{x}^{\infty}\!\!\!\!v(y)\mathrm{e}^{\frac{x-y}{\eta}}V(\mathcal{W}_{\eta}(t,y))\partial_{y}\mathcal{W}_{\eta}(t,y)\,\mathrm{d}y

and an integration by parts in the latter term

\displaystyle=-v(x)V(\mathcal{W}_{\eta}(t,x))\partial_{x}\mathcal{W}_{\eta}(t,x)-\int_{x}^{\infty}\!\!\!\!\!v^{\prime}(y)\exp(\tfrac{x-y}{\eta})V(\mathcal{W}_{\eta}(t,y))\partial_{y}\mathcal{W}_{\eta}(t,y)\,\mathrm{d}y

Lemma 4.2

(Vanishing

at negative infinity)
.

Let

\lim_{x\rightarrow-\infty}\partial_{x}\mathcal{W}_{\eta}[v\cdot q](x)=0.

Proof.

We have for all

and

\displaystyle\big|\partial_{x}\mathcal{W}_{\eta}[v\cdot q](x)\big|

\displaystyle=\big|\mathcal{W}_{\eta}\big[v^{\prime}\cdot q+v\cdot\partial_{x}q\big](x)\big|

\displaystyle\leq\tfrac{1}{\eta}\int_{-\infty}^{y^{*}}|v^{\prime}(y)q(y)|\,\mathrm{d}y+\tfrac{1}{\eta}\int_{-\infty}^{y^{*}}|v(y)\partial_{y}q(y)|\,\mathrm{d}y

\displaystyle\quad+\tfrac{1}{\eta}\int_{y^{*}}^{\infty}\mathrm{e}^{\frac{x-y}{\eta}}|v^{\prime}(y)q(y)|\,\mathrm{d}y+\tfrac{1}{\eta}\int_{y^{*}}^{\infty}\mathrm{e}^{\frac{x-y}{\eta}}|v(y)\partial_{y}q(y)|\,\mathrm{d}y

\displaystyle\leq\tfrac{1}{\eta}\|q\|_{{\mathsf{L}\!}^{\infty}(\mathbb{R})}|v|_{\mathsf{TV}((-\infty,y^{*}))}+\tfrac{1}{\eta}\|v\|_{{\mathsf{L}\!}^{\infty}(\mathbb{R})}|q|_{\mathsf{TV}((-\infty,y^{*}))}

\displaystyle\quad+\tfrac{1}{\eta}\int_{y^{*}}^{\infty}\mathrm{e}^{\frac{x-y}{\eta}}|v^{\prime}(y)q(y)|\,\mathrm{d}y+\tfrac{1}{\eta}\int_{y^{*}}^{\infty}\mathrm{e}^{\frac{x-y}{\eta}}|v(y)\partial_{y}q(y)|\,\mathrm{d}y.

Now, we chose

negative enough so that
|q|_{\mathsf{TV}(-\infty,y^{*})}
and
|v|_{\mathsf{TV}((-\infty,y^{*}))}
are arbitrary small and letting

in the second two terms using the dominated convergence theorem, we have that both terms vanish. Altogether, we obtain

\lim_{x\rightarrow-\infty}|\partial_{x}\mathcal{W}_{\eta}[v\cdot q](x)|=0.

∎

\displaystyle=\iint_{{\Omega_{T}}}\phi_{y}(t,y)\big(G(\rho_{\eta}^{\varepsilon})-G(\mathcal{W}_{\eta}[\rho_{\eta}^{\varepsilon}])\big)\,\mathrm{d}y

(27)

with

G:\begin{cases}\mathbb{R}&\rightarrow\mathbb{R}\\
x&\mapsto\int_{0}^{x}s^{2}V^{\prime}(s)\,\mathrm{d}s.\end{cases}

Obviously,
G\in\mathsf{W}^{1,\infty}_{\textnormal{loc}}(\mathbb{R})
and again – in analogy to
Eq.

26
– we can let
\varepsilon\rightarrow 0
and later on
\eta\rightarrow 0
to have

\displaystyle|\eqref{eq:424242}|\leq\|\phi_{y}\|_{{\mathsf{L}\!}^{\infty}(({\Omega_{T}}))}\|G^{\prime}\|_{{\mathsf{L}\!}^{\infty}((0,\|vq_{0}\|_{{\mathsf{L}\!}^{\infty}(\mathbb{R})}))}\|\rho_{\eta}-\mathcal{W}_{\eta}[\rho_{\eta}]\|_{\mathsf{C}([0,T];{\mathsf{L}\!}^{1}(\mathbb{R}))}\overset{\eta\rightarrow 0}{\longrightarrow}0.

This results in the fact that we obtain

\lim_{\eta\rightarrow 0}\mathcal{EF}\big[\phi,(\cdot)^{2},q_{\eta}\big]\geq 0

and thus, the nonlocal solution satisfies in the limit
\eta\rightarrow 0
the Entropy condition.
∎

5
Convergence of the solution of the nonlocal discontinuous conservation law to the solution of the corresponding local conservation law

In this short section, we present the main result of this paper with all necessary conditions.

Theorem 5.1

(Convergence – discontinuous nonlocal to local)
.

Let
v\in{\mathsf{L}\!}^{\infty}(\mathbb{R};\mathbb{R}_{\geq\underline{v}}),\ \underline{v}\in\mathbb{R}_{>0}
be given and assume in addition the OSL condition
Eq.

19
, i.e.

\exists L\in\mathbb{R}\ \forall x,y\in\mathbb{R},\ x\geq y:\ v(x)-v(y)\leq L(x-y)

Let furthermore
q_{0}\in{\mathsf{BV}}(\mathbb{R};\mathbb{R}_{\geq 0})
be given and
V
may satisfy
Eq.

25
, i.e.,

x\mapsto xV(x)\text{ strictly concave }\Longleftrightarrow 2V^{\prime}(x)+xV^{\prime\prime}(x)<0,\ \forall x\in\big[0,\|v\cdot q_{0}\|_{{\mathsf{L}\!}^{\infty}(\mathbb{R})}\big].

Then, the solution of the discontinuous nonlocal conservation law
q_{\eta}\in\mathsf{C}\big([0,T];{\mathsf{L}\!}^{1}(\mathbb{R})\big)
in
Definition

1.2
converges in
\mathsf{C}\big([0,T];{\mathsf{L}\!}^{1}_{\textnormal{loc}}(\mathbb{R})\big)
to the Entropy solution of the (local) discontinuous conservation law
q_{*}\in\mathsf{C}\big([0,T];{\mathsf{L}\!}^{1}_{\textnormal{loc}}(\mathbb{R})\big)
in
Definition

1.1
if
\eta\in\mathbb{R}_{>0}
approaches zero, in formulae

\lim_{\eta\rightarrow 0}\big\|q_{\eta}-q_{*}\big\|_{\mathsf{C}([0,T];{\mathsf{L}\!}^{1}_{\textnormal{loc}}(\mathbb{R}))}=0.

Even more, also the nonlocal term converges in the following sense

\lim_{\eta\rightarrow 0}\big\|\mathcal{W}_{\eta}[v\cdot q_{\eta}]-v\cdot q_{*}\big\|_{\mathsf{C}([0,T];{\mathsf{L}\!}^{1}_{\textnormal{loc}}(\mathbb{R}))}=0.

Proof.

For subsequences
(\eta_{k})_{k\in\mathbb{N}},
this is a direct consequence of
Theorem

4.2
and
Theorem

4.1
. Thanks to the fact that the local entropy limit is unique, we can extend it straight forward to any sequence converging to zero.
∎

Remark 5.1

(Convergence and generalizations)
.

Several points are in order to be made:

Convergence for each sequence:

The convergence result in
Theorem

5.1
holds for each sequence
\eta_{k}
converging to zero, and is thus a general approximation result.

Convergence for more general kernels:

As pointed out before, the methods used here are heavily inspired by
[
17
]
, but it is very likely that they can be extended to general kernels as done in
[
19
]
for the case without discontinuities. We do not go into details.

7
Conclusions and future work

We have established the convergence of solutions of a specific discontinuous nonlocal conservation law where the discontinuity is not only located in the velocity but also in the nonlocal term as a type of weight, and the nonlocal kernel approaches a Dirac distribution. On the local side, the emerging discontinuous local conservation law could be diffeomorphically transformed into a classical conservation law without discontinuity where typical Entropy conditions yield existence and uniqueness. However, this transform was not possible for the nonlocal discontinuous conservation law which justifies the outlined analysis furthermore.
Typical
\mathsf{TV}
estimates on the solution itself were not successful and proved in previous contributions as actual impossible
[
18
]
, however, by relying on
\mathsf{TV}
estimates of the nonlocal term we obtained strong convergence in
\mathsf{C}({\mathsf{L}\!}^{1}_{\textnormal{loc}})
.

The application of this result is currently only driven of the mathematical interest to better understand the singular limit problem, but there might be a further interpretation as the discontinuous nonlocal conservation law might prove to be a rather good approximation for traffic flow with different road capacities/velocities (at the discontinuities). However, this requires a more application driven study on the suggested dynamics which is out of scope of this contribution.
