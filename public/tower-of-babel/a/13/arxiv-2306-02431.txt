Sharp coefficients bounds for Starlike functions associated with Gregory coefficients
Erhan Deniz 1 , {}^{1}, Sercan Kazımoğlu 1 and H. M. Srivastava 2,3,4,5,6

Abstract
. In this paper we introduced the class 𝒮 G ∗ \mathcal{S}_{G}^{\ast} of analytic functions which is related with starlike functions and generating function of Gregory coefficients. By using bounds on some coefficient functionals for the family of functions with positive real part, we obtain for functions in the class 𝒮 G ∗ \mathcal{S}_{G}^{\ast} several sharp coefficient bounds on the first six coeffcients and also further sharp bounds on the corresponding Hankel determinants.

to our century), the reason for which in literature we can find them under

second kind, Cauchy numbers, etc.). For the same
