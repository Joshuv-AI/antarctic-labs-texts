SOME IDENTITIES AND INEQUALITIES FOR G-FUSION FRAMES
Ramazan Zarghami Farfar, Vahid Sadri, Reza Ahmadi

Abstract
. G-fusion frames which are obtained from the combination of g-frames and fusion frames were recently introduced for Hilbert spaces. In this paper, we present a new identity for g-frames which was given by Najati in a special case. Also, by using the idea of this identity and their dual frames, some equlities and inequalities will be presented for g-fusion frames.

2.
Preliminaries

Throughout this paper,
H
and
K
are separable Hilbert spaces,
\pi_{V}
is the orthogonal projection from
H
onto a closed subspace
V\subset H
and
\mathcal{B}(H,K)
is the collection of all the bounded linear operators of
H
into
K
. If
K=H
, then
\mathcal{B}(H,H)
will be denoted by
\mathcal{B}(H)
.
Also,
\{H_{j}\}_{j\in\mathbb{J}}
is a sequence of Hilbert spaces and
\Lambda_{j}\in\mathcal{B}(H,H_{j})
for each
j\in\mathbb{J}
where
\mathbb{J}
is a subset of
\mathbb{Z}
.
The following lemmas from Operator Theory are useful.

Lemma 1
.

(
[
13
]
)
Let
V\subseteq H
be a closed subspace, and
T
be a linear bounded operator on
H
. Then

\pi_{V}T^{*}=\pi_{V}T^{*}\pi_{\overline{TV}}.

Lemma 2
.

Let
u\in\mathcal{B}(H)
be adjoint and
v:=au^{2}+bu+c
where
a,b,c\in\mathbb{R}
.

(I)

If
a>0
, then

\inf_{\|f\|=1}\langle vf,f\rangle\geq\frac{4ac-b^{2}}{4a}.

(II)

If
a<0
, then

\sup_{\|f\|=1}\langle vf,f\rangle\leq\frac{4ac-b^{2}}{4a}.

Lemma 3
.

If
u,v
are operators on
H
satisfying
u+v=id_{H}
, then
u-v=u^{2}-v^{2}
.

Proof.

We can write

u-v=2u-id_{H}=u^{2}-(id_{H}-u)^{2}=u^{2}-v^{2}.

∎

We define the space
\mathscr{H}_{2}:=(\sum_{j\in\mathbb{J}}\oplus H_{j})_{\ell_{2}}
by

\displaystyle\mathscr{H}_{2}=\big\{\{f_{j}\}_{j\in\mathbb{J}}\ :\ f_{j}\in H_{j},\ \sum_{j\in\mathbb{J}}\|f_{j}\|^{2}<\infty\big\},

with the inner product defined by

\langle\{f_{j}\},\{g_{j}\}\rangle=\sum_{j\in\mathbb{J}}\langle f_{j},g_{j}\rangle.

It is clear that
\mathscr{H}_{2}
is a Hilbert space with pointwise operations.

Definition 1
.

We call the sequence
\{\Lambda_{j}\}_{j\in\mathbb{J}}
a g-frame for
H
with respect to
\{H_{j}\}_{j\in\mathbb{J}}
if there exist
0<A\leq B<\infty
such that for each
f\in H

(1)

\displaystyle A\|f\|^{2}\leq\sum_{j\in\mathbb{J}}\|\Lambda_{j}f\|^{2}\leq B\|f\|^{2}.

If
A=B=1
, we call
\{\Lambda_{j}\}_{j\in\mathbb{J}}
a Parseval g-frame.
The synthesis and analysis operators in g-frames are defined by

\displaystyle T_{\Lambda}

\displaystyle:\mathscr{H}_{2}\longrightarrow H\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ ,\ \ \ \ \ \ T_{\Lambda}^{*}:H\longrightarrow\mathscr{H}_{2}

\displaystyle T_{\Lambda}(\{f_{j}\}_{j\in\mathbb{J}})

\displaystyle=\sum_{j\in\mathbb{J}}\Lambda_{j}^{*}f_{j}\ \ \ ,\ \ \ \ T_{\Lambda}^{*}(f)=\{\Lambda_{j}f\}_{j\in\mathbb{J}}.

Therefore, the g-frame operator is defined by

Sf=TT^{*}f=\sum_{j\in\mathbb{J}}\Lambda^{*}_{j}\Lambda_{j}f.

The operator
S
is a bounded, positive and invertible.
If
\tilde{\Lambda}_{j}:=\Lambda_{j}S^{-1}
, then
\{\tilde{\Lambda}_{j}\}_{j\in\mathbb{J}}
is called a (canonical) dual g-frame of
\{\Lambda_{j}\}_{j\in\mathbb{J}}
and we can write

(2)

\displaystyle f=\sum_{j\in\mathbb{J}}\tilde{\Lambda}^{\ast}_{j}\Lambda_{j}f=\sum_{j\in\mathbb{J}}\Lambda^{\ast}_{j}\tilde{\Lambda}_{j}f.

If
\{\Lambda_{j}\}_{j\in\mathbb{J}}
is a g-frame for
H
with bounds
A
and
B
, respectively, then
\{\tilde{\Lambda}_{j}\}_{j\in\mathbb{J}}
is a g-frame for
H
too with bounds
B^{-1}
and
A^{-1}
, respectively (for more details, see
[
22
]
).

Definition 2
.

Let
W=\{W_{j}\}_{j\in\mathbb{J}}
be a family of closed subspaces of
H
,
\{v_{j}\}_{j\in\mathbb{J}}
be a family of weights, i.e.
v_{j}>0
. We say
\Lambda:=(W_{j},\Lambda_{j},v_{j})
is a g-fusion frame for
H
if there exists
0<A\leq B<\infty
such that for each
f\in H

(3)

\displaystyle A\|f\|^{2}\leq\sum_{j\in\mathbb{J}}v_{j}^{2}\|\Lambda_{j}\pi_{W_{j}}f\|^{2}\leq B\|f\|^{2}.

We call
\Lambda
a Parseval g-fusion frame if
A=B=1
. When the right hand of (
3
) holds,
\Lambda
is called a g-fusion Bessel sequence for
H
with bound
B
. Throughout this paper,
\Lambda
will be a triple
(W_{j},\Lambda_{j},v_{j})
with
j\in\mathbb{J}
.

The synthesis and analysis operators in the g-fusion frames are defined by (for more details, see
[
21
]
)

\displaystyle T_{\Lambda}

\displaystyle:\mathscr{H}_{2}\longrightarrow H\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ ,\ \ \ \ \ \ T_{\Lambda}^{*}:H\longrightarrow\mathscr{H}_{2}

\displaystyle T_{\Lambda}(\{f_{j}\}_{j\in\mathbb{J}})

\displaystyle=\sum_{j\in\mathbb{J}}v_{j}\pi_{W_{j}}\Lambda_{j}^{*}f_{j}\ \ \ ,\ \ \ \ T_{\Lambda}^{*}(f)=\{v_{j}\Lambda_{j}\pi_{W_{j}}f\}_{j\in\mathbb{J}}.

Thus, the g-fusion frame operator is given by

S_{\Lambda}f=T_{\Lambda}T^{*}_{\Lambda}f=\sum_{j\in\mathbb{J}}v_{j}^{2}\pi_{W_{j}}\Lambda^{*}_{j}\Lambda_{j}\pi_{W_{j}}f.

Therefore

A\ id_{H}\leq S_{\Lambda}\leq B\ id_{H}.

This means that
S_{\Lambda}
is a bounded, positive and invertible operator (with adjoint inverse) and we have

B^{-1}id_{H}\leq S_{\Lambda}^{-1}\leq A^{-1}id_{H}.

So, we have the folloeing reconstruction formula for any
f\in H

(4)

f=\sum_{j\in\mathbb{J}}v_{j}^{2}\pi_{W_{j}}\Lambda^{*}_{j}\Lambda_{j}\pi_{W_{j}}S^{-1}_{\Lambda}f=\sum_{j\in\mathbb{J}}v_{j}^{2}S^{-1}_{\Lambda}\pi_{W_{j}}\Lambda^{*}_{j}\Lambda_{j}\pi_{W_{j}}f.

Let
\tilde{\Lambda}:=(S^{-1}_{\Lambda}W_{j},\Lambda_{j}\pi_{W_{j}}S_{\Lambda}^{-1},v_{j})
. Then,
\tilde{\Lambda}
is called the
(canonical) dual g-fusion frame
of
\Lambda
. Hence, for each
f\in H
, we get (see
[
21
]
)

(5)

\displaystyle f=\sum_{j\in\mathbb{J}}v_{j}^{2}\pi_{W_{j}}\Lambda^{*}_{j}\tilde{\Lambda_{j}}\pi_{\tilde{W_{j}}}f=\sum_{j\in\mathbb{J}}v_{j}^{2}\pi_{\tilde{W_{j}}}\tilde{\Lambda_{j}}^{*}\Lambda_{j}\pi_{W_{j}}f,

where
\tilde{W_{j}}:=S^{-1}_{\Lambda}W_{j}\ ,\ \tilde{\Lambda_{j}}:=\Lambda_{j}\pi_{W_{j}}S_{\Lambda}^{-1}.

Thus, we obtain

(6)

\displaystyle\langle S^{-1}_{\Lambda}f,f\rangle=\sum_{j\in\mathbb{J}}v_{j}^{2}\|\tilde{\Lambda}_{j}\pi_{\tilde{W}_{j}}f\|^{2}.

3.
The Main Results

Let
\{\Lambda_{j}\}_{j\in\mathbb{J}}
be a g-frame for
H
with respect to
\{H_{j}\}_{j\in\mathbb{J}}
with boinds
A,B
and
\{\tilde{\Lambda}_{j}\}_{j\in\mathbb{J}}
be a (canonical) dual g-frame of
\{\Lambda_{j}\}_{j\in\mathbb{J}}
. Suppose that
\mathbb{I}\subseteq\mathbb{J}
and let

\displaystyle S_{\mathbb{I}}

\displaystyle:H\rightarrow H

\displaystyle S_{\mathbb{I}}f

\displaystyle:=\sum_{j\in\mathbb{I}}\Lambda^{\ast}_{j}\tilde{\Lambda}_{j}f.

We have

\displaystyle\|S_{\mathbb{I}}f\|^{2}

\displaystyle=\big(\sup_{\|h\|=1}|\langle S_{\mathbb{I}}f,h\rangle|\big)^{2}

\displaystyle=\sup_{\|h\|=1}\big(\sum_{j}|\langle\tilde{\Lambda}_{j}f,\Lambda_{j}h\rangle|\big)^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\sum_{j}\|\tilde{\Lambda}_{j}f\|^{2}.\sup_{\|h\|=1}\sum_{j}\|\Lambda_{j}h\|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq BA^{-1}\|f\|^{2}.

Thus,
S_{\mathbb{I}}\in\mathcal{B}(H)
and is positive. From (
2
), we obtain that
S_{\mathbb{I}}+S_{\mathbb{I}^{c}}=id_{H}
.

Theorem 3.1
.

For
f\in H
, we have

\displaystyle\sum_{j\in\mathbb{I}}\langle\tilde{\Lambda}_{j}f,\Lambda_{j}f\rangle-\|S_{\mathbb{I}}f\|^{2}=\sum_{j\in\mathbb{I}^{c}}\overline{\langle\tilde{\Lambda}_{j}f,\Lambda_{j}f\rangle}-\|S_{\mathbb{I}^{c}}f\|^{2}

where
\mathbb{I}^{c}
is the complement of
\mathbb{I}
.

Proof.

For each
f\in H
, we have

\displaystyle\sum_{j\in\mathbb{I}}\langle\tilde{\Lambda}_{j}f,\Lambda_{j}f\rangle-\|\sum_{j\in\mathbb{I}}\Lambda^{\ast}_{j}\tilde{\Lambda}_{j}f\|^{2}

\displaystyle=\langle S_{\mathbb{I}}f,f\rangle-\|S_{\mathbb{I}}f\|^{2}

\displaystyle=\langle S_{\mathbb{I}}f,f\rangle-\langle S^{\ast}_{\mathbb{I}}S_{\mathbb{I}}f,f\rangle

\displaystyle=\langle(id_{H}-S_{\mathbb{I}})^{\ast}S_{\mathbb{I}}f,f\rangle

\displaystyle=\langle S^{\ast}_{\mathbb{I}^{c}}(id_{H}-S_{\mathbb{I}^{c}})f,f\rangle

\displaystyle=\langle S^{\ast}_{\mathbb{I}^{c}}f,f\rangle-\langle S^{\ast}_{\mathbb{I}^{c}}S_{\mathbb{I}^{c}}f,f\rangle

\displaystyle=\langle f,S_{\mathbb{I}^{c}}f\rangle-\langle S_{\mathbb{I}^{c}}f,S_{\mathbb{I}^{c}}f\rangle

\displaystyle=\sum_{j\in\mathbb{I}^{c}}\langle\Lambda_{j}f,\tilde{\Lambda}_{j}f\rangle-\|\sum_{j\in\mathbb{I}^{c}}\Lambda^{\ast}_{j}\tilde{\Lambda}_{j}f\|^{2}

\displaystyle=\sum_{j\in\mathbb{I}^{c}}\overline{\langle\tilde{\Lambda}_{j}f,\Lambda_{j}f\rangle}-\|\sum_{j\in\mathbb{I}^{c}}\Lambda^{\ast}_{j}\tilde{\Lambda}_{j}f\|^{2}

and the proof is completed.
∎

Now, if
\{\Lambda_{j}\}_{j\in\mathbb{J}}
is a Parseval g-frame, then
\tilde{\Lambda_{j}}=\Lambda_{j}
and we obtain the following famous formula:

\displaystyle\sum_{j\in\mathbb{I}}\|\Lambda_{j}f\|^{2}-\|S_{\mathbb{I}}f\|^{2}=\sum_{j\in\mathbb{I}^{c}}\|\Lambda_{j}f\|^{2}-\|S_{\mathbb{I}^{c}}f\|^{2},

where
S_{\mathbb{I}}f=\sum_{j\in\mathbb{I}}\Lambda^{\ast}_{j}\Lambda_{j}f
.

The same can be presented for g-fusion frames. Let
\Lambda
be a g-fusion frame for
H
with (canonical) dual g-fusion frame
\tilde{\Lambda}=(\tilde{W_{j}},\tilde{\Lambda_{j}},v_{j})
where
\tilde{W_{j}}=S^{-1}_{\Lambda}W_{j}
and
\tilde{\Lambda_{j}}=\Lambda_{j}\pi_{W_{j}}S_{\Lambda}^{-1}
. For simplicity, we show the following operator with the same symbol
S_{\mathbb{I}}
in which again
\mathbb{I}
is a finite subset of
\mathbb{J}
:

(7)

\displaystyle S_{\mathbb{I}}f=\sum_{j\in\mathbb{I}}v_{j}^{2}\pi_{W_{j}}\Lambda^{*}_{j}\tilde{\Lambda_{j}}\pi_{\tilde{W_{j}}}f,\ \ \ \ \ (\forall f\in H).

It is easy to check that,
S_{\mathbb{I}}\in\mathcal{B}(H)
and positive. Again, we have

S_{\mathbb{I}}+S_{\mathbb{I}^{c}}=id_{H}.

Remark 1
.

Let
\Lambda
be a Parseval g-fusion frame for
H
. Since
\mathcal{B}(H)
is a
C^{*}
-algebra and
S_{\mathbb{I}}
is a positive, so

r(S_{\mathbb{I}})=\|S_{\mathbb{I}}\|
, where
r
is the spectral radius. Thus

\max_{\lambda\in\sigma(S_{\mathbb{I}})}|\lambda|=r(S_{\mathbb{I}})\leq 1

and we conclude that
\sigma(S_{\mathbb{I}})\in[0,1]
.

Theorem 3.2
.

Let
f\in H
, then

\displaystyle\sum_{j\in\mathbb{I}}v_{j}^{2}\langle\tilde{\Lambda}_{j}\pi_{\tilde{W_{j}}}f,\Lambda_{j}\pi_{W_{j}}f\rangle-\|S_{\mathbb{I}}f\|^{2}=\sum_{j\in\mathbb{I}^{c}}v_{j}^{2}\overline{\langle\tilde{\Lambda}_{j}\pi_{\tilde{W_{j}}}f,\Lambda_{j}\pi_{W_{j}}f\rangle}-\|S_{\mathbb{I}^{c}}f\|^{2}.

Proof.

The proof follows by a similar argument to the proof of Theorem
3.1
.
∎

Corollary 1
.

Let
\Lambda
be a Parseval g-fusion frame for
H
. Then

\displaystyle\sum_{j\in\mathbb{I}}v_{j}^{2}\|\Lambda_{j}\pi_{W_{j}}f\|^{2}-\|\sum_{j\in\mathbb{I}}v_{j}^{2}\pi_{W_{j}}\Lambda^{*}_{j}

\displaystyle\Lambda_{j}\pi_{W_{j}}f\|^{2}=

\displaystyle=\sum_{j\in\mathbb{I}^{c}}v_{j}^{2}\|\Lambda_{j}\pi_{W_{j}}f\|^{2}-\|\sum_{j\in\mathbb{I}^{c}}v_{j}^{2}\pi_{W_{j}}\Lambda^{*}_{j}\Lambda_{j}\pi_{W_{j}}f\|^{2}.

Moreover

\displaystyle\sum_{j\in\mathbb{I}}v_{j}^{2}\|\Lambda_{j}\pi_{W_{j}}f\|^{2}+\|\sum_{j\in\mathbb{I}^{c}}v_{j}^{2}\pi_{W_{j}}\Lambda^{*}_{j}\Lambda_{j}\pi_{W_{j}}f\|^{2}\geq\frac{3}{4}\|f\|^{2}.

Proof.

If
f\in H
, we obtain

\displaystyle\sum_{j\in\mathbb{I}}v_{j}^{2}\|\Lambda_{j}\pi_{W_{j}}f\|^{2}+\|S_{\mathbb{I}^{c}}f\|^{2}

\displaystyle=\big\langle(S_{\mathbb{I}}+S^{2}_{\mathbb{I}^{c}})f,f\big\rangle

\displaystyle=\big\langle(S_{\mathbb{I}}+id_{H}-2S_{\mathbb{I}}+S^{2}_{\mathbb{I}})f,f\big\rangle

\displaystyle=\langle(id_{H}-S_{\mathbb{I}}+S_{\mathbb{I}}^{2})f,f\rangle.

Now, by Lemma
2
for
a=1
,
b=-1
and
c=1
the inequality holds.
∎

Corollary 2
.

Let
\Lambda
be a Parseval g-fusion frame for
H
. Then

0\leq S_{\mathbb{I}}-S_{\mathbb{I}}^{2}\leq\frac{1}{4}id_{H}

Proof.

We have
S_{\mathbb{I}}S_{\mathbb{I}^{c}}=S_{\mathbb{I}^{c}}S_{\mathbb{I}}
. Then
0\leq S_{\mathbb{I}}S_{\mathbb{I}^{c}}=S_{\mathbb{I}}-S_{\mathbb{I}}^{2}
. Also, by Lemma
2
, we get

S_{\mathbb{I}}-S_{\mathbb{I}}^{2}\leq\frac{1}{4}id_{H}.

∎

Theorem 3.3
.

Let
\Lambda
be a g-fusion frame with the g-fusion frame operator
S_{\Lambda}
. If
\mathbb{I}\subseteq\mathbb{J}
and
f\in H
, then

\displaystyle\sum_{j\in\mathbb{I}}v_{j}^{2}\|\Lambda_{j}\pi_{W_{j}}f\|^{2}+\|S_{\Lambda}^{-\frac{1}{2}}S_{\mathbb{I}^{c}}f\|^{2}=\sum_{j\in\mathbb{I}^{c}}v_{j}^{2}\|\Lambda_{j}\pi_{W_{j}}f\|^{2}+\|S_{\Lambda}^{-\frac{1}{2}}S_{\mathbb{I}}f\|^{2}.

Proof.

Let
\Theta_{j}:=\Lambda_{j}\pi_{W_{j}}S_{\Lambda}^{-\frac{1}{2}}
and
X_{j}:=S_{\Lambda}^{-\frac{1}{2}}W_{j}
. In
[
21
]
has been showed that
(X_{j},\Theta_{j},v_{j})
is a Parseval g-fusion frame for
H
. Then, by Corollary
1
, we have

\displaystyle\sum_{j\in\mathbb{I}}v_{j}^{2}\|\Theta_{j}\pi_{X_{j}}f\|^{2}-\|\sum_{j\in\mathbb{I}}v_{j}^{2}\pi_{X_{j}}\Theta^{*}_{j}

\displaystyle\Theta_{j}\pi_{X_{j}}f\|^{2}=

\displaystyle=\sum_{j\in\mathbb{I}^{c}}v_{j}^{2}\|\Theta_{j}\pi_{X_{j}}f\|^{2}-\|\sum_{j\in\mathbb{I}^{c}}v_{j}^{2}\pi_{X_{j}}\Theta^{*}_{j}\Theta_{j}\pi_{X_{j}}f\|^{2}.

By replacing
S_{\Lambda}^{\frac{1}{2}}f
instead of
f
and this fact that

\displaystyle\sum_{j\in\mathbb{I}}v_{j}^{2}\pi_{X_{j}}\Theta^{*}_{j}\Theta_{j}\pi_{X_{j}}f

\displaystyle=\sum_{j\in\mathbb{I}}v_{j}^{2}(\Theta^{*}_{j}\pi_{X_{j}})^{*}\Theta_{j}\pi_{X_{j}}f

\displaystyle=\sum_{j\in\mathbb{I}}v_{j}^{2}(\Lambda_{j}\pi_{W_{j}}S_{\Lambda}^{-\frac{1}{2}}\pi_{X_{j}})^{*}\Lambda_{j}\pi_{W_{j}}S_{\Lambda}^{-\frac{1}{2}}\pi_{X_{j}}f

\displaystyle=\sum_{j\in\mathbb{I}}v_{j}^{2}S_{\Lambda}^{-\frac{1}{2}}\pi_{W_{j}}\Lambda_{j}^{*}\Lambda_{j}\pi_{W_{j}}S_{\Lambda}^{-\frac{1}{2}}f

\displaystyle=S_{\Lambda}^{-\frac{1}{2}}S_{\mathbb{I}}S_{\Lambda}^{-\frac{1}{2}}f,

the proof follows.
∎

Corollary 3
.

Let
\Lambda
be a g-fusion frame with the g-fusion frame operator
S_{\Lambda}
. If
\mathbb{I}\subseteq\mathbb{J}
, then

0\leq S_{\mathbb{I}}-S_{\mathbb{I}}S^{-1}_{\Lambda}S_{\mathbb{I}}\leq\frac{1}{4}S_{\Lambda}.

Proof.

In the proof of Theorem
3.3
, we showed that

\sum_{j\in\mathbb{I}}v_{j}^{2}\pi_{X_{j}}\Theta^{*}_{j}\Theta_{j}\pi_{X_{j}}f=S_{\Lambda}^{-\frac{1}{2}}S_{\mathbb{I}}S_{\Lambda}^{-\frac{1}{2}}f.

By Corollary
2
we get

0\leq\sum_{j\in\mathbb{I}}v_{j}^{2}\pi_{X_{j}}\Theta^{*}_{j}\Theta_{j}\pi_{X_{j}}f-\big(\sum_{j\in\mathbb{I}}v_{j}^{2}\pi_{X_{j}}\Theta^{*}_{j}\Theta_{j}\pi_{X_{j}}f\big)^{2}\leq\frac{1}{4}id_{H}.

Therefore,

0\leq S_{\Lambda}^{-\frac{1}{2}}(S_{\mathbb{I}}-S_{\mathbb{I}}S^{-1}_{\Lambda}S_{\mathbb{I}})S_{\Lambda}^{-\frac{1}{2}}\leq\frac{1}{4}id_{H}

and the proof is completed.
∎

Corollary 4
.

Suppose that
\Lambda
is a g-fusion frame with the g-fusion frame operator
S_{\Lambda}
. If
\mathbb{I}\subseteq\mathbb{J}
and
f\in H
, then

\displaystyle\sum_{j\in\mathbb{I}}v_{j}^{2}\|\Lambda_{j}\pi_{W_{j}}f\|^{2}+\|S_{\Lambda}^{-\frac{1}{2}}S_{\mathbb{I}^{c}}f\|^{2}\geq\frac{3}{4}\|S_{\Lambda}^{-1}\|^{-1}\|f\|^{2}.

Proof.

By Theorem
3.3
and Corollary
1
, we can write

\displaystyle\sum_{j\in\mathbb{I}}v_{j}^{2}\|\Lambda_{j}\pi_{W_{j}}f\|^{2}+\|S_{\Lambda}^{-\frac{1}{2}}S_{\mathbb{I}^{c}}f\|^{2}

\displaystyle=\sum_{j\in\mathbb{I}}v_{j}^{2}\|\Theta_{j}\pi_{X_{j}}S^{\frac{1}{2}}_{\Lambda}f\|^{2}+\|\sum_{j\in\mathbb{I}^{c}}v_{j}^{2}\pi_{X_{j}}\Theta^{*}_{j}\Theta_{j}\pi_{X_{j}}S^{\frac{1}{2}}_{\Lambda}f\|^{2}

\displaystyle\geq\frac{3}{4}\|S^{\frac{1}{2}}_{\Lambda}f\|^{2}

\displaystyle=\frac{3}{4}\langle S_{\Lambda}f,f\rangle

\displaystyle\geq\frac{3}{4}\|S_{\Lambda}^{-1}\|^{-1}\|f\|^{2}.

∎

Theorem 3.4
.

Let
\Lambda
be a Parseval g-fusion frame for
H
and
\mathbb{I}\subseteq\mathbb{J}
. Then

(I)

0\leq S_{\mathbb{I}}-S_{\mathbb{I}}^{2}\leq\dfrac{1}{4}id_{H}
.

(II)

\dfrac{1}{2}id_{H}\leq S_{\mathbb{I}}^{2}+S_{\mathbb{I}^{c}}^{2}\leq\dfrac{3}{2}id_{H}
.

Proof.

(I). Since
S_{\mathbb{I}}+S_{\mathbb{I}^{c}}=id_{H}
, then
S_{\mathbb{I}}S_{\mathbb{I}^{c}}+S^{2}_{\mathbb{I}^{c}}=S_{\mathbb{I}^{c}}
. Thus,

S_{\mathbb{I}}S_{\mathbb{I}^{c}}=S_{\mathbb{I}^{c}}-S^{2}_{\mathbb{I}^{c}}=S_{\mathbb{I}^{c}}(id_{H}-S_{\mathbb{I}^{c}})=S_{\mathbb{I}^{c}}S_{\mathbb{I}}.

But,
\Lambda
is a Parseval, so
0\leq S_{\mathbb{I}}S_{\mathbb{I}^{c}}=S_{\mathbb{I}}-S_{\mathbb{I}}^{2}
. On the other hand, by Lemma
3
, we get

S_{\mathbb{I}}-S_{\mathbb{I}}^{2}\leq\frac{1}{4}id_{H}.

(II). We saw that
S_{\mathbb{I}}S_{\mathbb{I}^{c}}=S_{\mathbb{I}^{c}}S_{\mathbb{I}}
, then by Lemma
3
,

S_{\mathbb{I}}^{2}+S_{\mathbb{I}^{c}}^{2}=id_{H}-2S_{\mathbb{I}}S_{\mathbb{I}^{c}}=2S_{\mathbb{I}}^{2}-2S_{\mathbb{I}}+id_{H}\geq\frac{1}{2}id_{H}.

On the other hand, we have again by Lemma
3
and
0\leq S_{\mathbb{I}}-S_{\mathbb{I}}^{2}
,

S_{\mathbb{I}}^{2}+S_{\mathbb{I}^{c}}^{2}\leq id_{H}+2S_{\mathbb{I}}-2S_{\mathbb{I}}^{2}\leq\frac{3}{2}id_{H}.

∎

Corollary 5
.

Let
\Lambda
be a g-fusion frame with the g-fusion frame operator
S_{\Lambda}
. If
\mathbb{I}\subseteq\mathbb{J}
, then

\frac{1}{2}S_{\Lambda}\leq S_{\mathbb{I}}S^{-1}_{\Lambda}S_{\mathbb{I}}-S_{\mathbb{I}^{c}}S^{-1}_{\Lambda}S_{\mathbb{I}^{c}}\leq\frac{3}{2}S_{\Lambda}.

Proof.

The proof is similar to the proof of Corollary
3
.
∎

Theorem 3.5
.

Let
\Lambda
be a g-fusion frame with the g-fusion frame operator
S_{\Lambda}
. If
\mathbb{I}\subseteq\mathbb{J}
, then for any
f\in H

\displaystyle\sum_{j\in\mathbb{I}}v_{j}^{2}\|\Lambda_{j}\pi_{W_{j}}f\|^{2}-\sum_{j\in\mathbb{J}}v_{j}^{2}\|\tilde{\Lambda}_{j}\pi_{\tilde{W}_{j}}M_{\mathbb{I}}f\|^{2}=\sum_{j\in\mathbb{I}^{c}}v_{j}^{2}\|\Lambda_{j}\pi_{W_{j}}f\|^{2}-\sum_{j\in\mathbb{J}}v_{j}^{2}\|\tilde{\Lambda}_{j}\pi_{\tilde{W}_{j}}M_{\mathbb{I}^{c}}f\|^{2}

where

\displaystyle M_{\mathbb{I}}f=\sum_{j\in\mathbb{I}}v_{j}^{2}\pi_{W_{j}}\Lambda^{*}_{j}\Lambda_{j}\pi_{W_{j}}f.

Proof.

Via the definition of
S_{\Lambda}
, it is clear that
M_{\mathbb{I}}+M_{\mathbb{I}^{c}}=S_{\Lambda}
. Therefore,
S^{-1}_{\Lambda}M_{\mathbb{I}}+S^{-1}_{\Lambda}M_{\mathbb{I}^{c}}=id_{H}
. Hence, by Lemma
3

S^{-1}_{\Lambda}M_{\mathbb{I}}-S^{-1}_{\Lambda}M_{\mathbb{I}^{c}}=(S^{-1}_{\Lambda}M_{\mathbb{I}})^{2}-(S^{-1}_{\Lambda}M_{\mathbb{I}^{c}})^{2}.

Thus, for each
f,g\in H
we obtain
