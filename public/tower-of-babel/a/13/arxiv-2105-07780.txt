Jacob’s ladders, class of crossbreeding conserving certain cell of meta-functional equations and existence of cancerous growth of that cell
Jan Moser

Abstract
. In this paper a set of 48 crossbreedings on certain cell of meta-functional equations preserving the cell is obtained. In opposite direction we presente an example of very complicated meta-functional equation not obtained in basic cell though it is generated by sequence of internal crossbreedings. Such a formula is called as cancerous growth on the basic cell. DEDICATED TO GREGOR MENDEL AND IT’S PEA-CROSSBREEDING

3.
Jacob’s ladders

3.1.

Let us remind that the Jacob’s ladder

\varphi_{1}(t)=\frac{1}{2}\varphi(t)

we have introduced in
[
1
]
(see also
[
4
]
), where the function
\varphi(t)
is arbitrary continuous solution of the nonlinear integral equation (also introduced in
[
1
]
)

\int_{0}^{\mu[x(T)]}Z^{2}(t)e^{-\frac{2}{x(T)}t}{\rm d}t=\int_{0}^{T}Z^{2}(t){\rm d}t

where

\begin{split}&Z(t)=e^{i\vartheta(t)}\zeta\left(\frac{1}{2}+it\right),\\
&\vartheta(t)=-\frac{t}{2}\ln\pi+\im\left\{\ln\Gamma\left(\frac{1}{4}+i\frac{t}{2}\right)\right\},\end{split}

where each admissible function
\mu(y)
generates the solution

y=\varphi(T;\mu)=\varphi(T);\ \mu(y)\geq 7y\ln y.

We call the function
\varphi_{1}(T)
the Jacob’s ladder as an analogue of the Jacob’s dream in Chumash, Bereishis, 28:12.

3.2.

Next, let us remind that the classical Hardy-Littlewood integral (1918)

(3.1)

\int_{0}^{T}\left|\zeta\left(\frac{1}{2}+it\right)\right|^{2}{\rm d}t

has the following expression

(3.2)

\int_{0}^{T}\left|\zeta\left(\frac{1}{2}+it\right)\right|^{2}{\rm d}t=T\ln T+(2c-1-\ln 2\pi)T+R(T),

with, for example, the Ingham’s estimate of the error term

(3.3)

R(T)=\mathcal{O}(\sqrt{T}\ln T),\ T\to\infty.

However, by the Good’s
\Omega
-theorem (1977) we have that

(3.4)

R(T)=\Omega(T^{1/4}),\ T\to\infty.

Remark 8
.

It follows from (
3.4
) that

(3.5)

\limsup_{T\to\infty}|R(T)|=+\infty,

that is every expression of the type (
3.2
) of the Hardy-Littlewood integral possesses an unbounded error term at
T\to\infty
.

3.3.

Under the circumstances (
3.2
) and (
3.5
) we have proved that the Hardy-Littlewood integral (
3.1
) has an infinite set of other completely new almost exact representations expressed by the following

Formula1
.

(3.6)

\begin{split}&\int_{0}^{T}\left|\zeta\left(\frac{1}{2}+it\right)\right|^{2}{\rm d}t=\varphi_{1}(T)\ln\{\varphi_{1}(T)\}+\\
&+(c-\ln 2\pi)\varphi_{1}(T)+c_{0}+\mathcal{O}\left(\frac{\ln T}{T}\right),\ T\to\infty\end{split}

with the property (comp. (
3.5
))

(3.7)

\lim_{T\to\infty}R_{1}(T)=\lim_{T\to\infty}\left\{\mathcal{O}\left(\frac{\ln T}{T}\right)\right\}=0,

where
c
is the Euler’s constant and
c_{0}
is the constant from the Titchmarsh-Kober-Atkinson formula.

Comparison between (
