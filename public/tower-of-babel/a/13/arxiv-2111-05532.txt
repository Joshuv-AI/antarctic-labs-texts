Abstract
. We discuss the question of extending homeomorphism between closed subsets of the Cantor discontinuum D τ D^{\tau} . For every set P ⊂ D τ P\subset D^{\tau} let 𝔏 P \mathfrak{L}_{P} be the set of cardinality λ \lambda such that the λ \lambda -interior of P P is not empty. It is established that any homeomorphism f f between two proper closed subsets P P and K K of D τ D^{\tau} can be extended to an autohomeomorphism of D τ D^{\tau} provided the sets 𝔏 P \mathfrak{L}_{P} and 𝔏 K \mathfrak{L}_{K} do not have so many points of discontinuity and f f preserves the λ \lambda -interiors of P P and K K .

1.
Introduction

According to
[
5
]
, in 1951 Ryll-Nardzewski presented his solution of Knaster’s problem on extension of homeomorphisms on closed subsets of the Cantor set
D^{\aleph_{0}}
. Ryll-Nardzewski’s proof was based on Boolean algebras and it was not published.

Here is Ryll-Nardzewski’s theorem, see
[
5
]
:
Let
P,K
be a proper closed subsets of the Cantor set
D^{\aleph_{0}}
and
f
be a homeomorphism between
P
and
K
such that
f(\rm{int}~P)=\rm{int}~K
. Then there exists an autohomeomorphism of
D^{\aleph_{0}}
extending
f
.

A topological proof of Ryll-Nardzewski’s theorem was established by Knaster-Reichbach
[
5
]
. More precisely, Knaster-Reichbach reduced their proof to the case when
\rm{int}~P=\rm{int}~K=\varnothing
, see Theorem 2.6 below. The non-metrizable analogue of Knaster-Reichbach’s theorem was proved in our paper

[
10
, Theorem 1.2]
.

The present article is devoted to the proof of a non-metrizable analogue of the Ryll-Nardzewski theorem. In contrast to the metrizable case, the non-metrizable version of Ryll-Nardzewski’s theorem cannot be directly obtained from
[
10
, Theorem 1.2]
. The key role in our proof plays the concept of
\lambda
-interior.

For a space
X
, a subset
P\subset X
and an infinite cardinal
\lambda
we denote by
P^{(\lambda)}
the
\lambda
-interior
of
P
in
X
, i.e. the set all
x\in P
such that there exists a
G_{\lambda}
-subset
K
of
X
with
x\in K\subset P
. If
\lambda
is finite, then
P^{(\lambda)}
is the ordinary interior of
P
and it is denoted by
P^{(0)}
. If there exists
\tau\geq\aleph_{0}
such that

P^{(\lambda)}
is empty for all
\lambda<\tau
, we say that
P
is
\tau
-negligible in
X
. The sets of all cardinals
\lambda
with
P^{(\lambda)}\neq\varnothing
is denoted by
\mathfrak{L}_{P}
.

Let
\lambda\in\mathfrak{L}_{P}
be a limit cardinal. We say that
\mathfrak{L}_{P}
is
discontinuous at
\lambda
if

\overline{\bigcup_{\gamma<\lambda}P^{(\gamma)}}\subsetneqq P^{(\lambda)}
, otherwise
\mathfrak{L}_{P}
is said to be
continuous at
\lambda
. Denote by
\mathfrak{L}_{P}^{d}
the cardinals of discontinuity for
\mathfrak{L}_{P}
. The set
\mathfrak{L}_{P}^{d}
is said to be discrete in
\mathfrak{L}_{P}
if for every
\lambda\in\mathfrak{L}_{P}^{d}
there is
\gamma<\lambda
such that the interval
(\gamma,\lambda)
is disjoint from
\mathfrak{L}_{P}^{d}
. Obviously,
\mathfrak{L}_{P}^{d}
is discrete in
\mathfrak{L}_{P}
if
\mathfrak{L}_{P}^{d}
is finite, in particular that is true
provided
\mathfrak{L}_{P}
is finite.

Now, we can formulate a non-metrizable analogue of Ryll-Nardzewski’s theorem.

Theorem 1.1
.

Let
f
be a homeomorphism between two proper closed subsets
P
and
K
of
D^{\tau}
such that
\mathfrak{L}_{P}^{d}
is discrete in
\mathfrak{L}_{P}
and
f(P^{(\lambda)})=K^{(\lambda)}
for every
\lambda\in\mathfrak{L}_{P}
. Then
f
can be extended to a homeomorphism of
D^{\tau}
.

Observe that the condition
f(P^{(\lambda)})=K^{(\lambda)}
for any cardinal number
\lambda\geq 0
is necessary for the
existence of a homeomorphic extension of
f
. Let’s also note that the case
P^{(\lambda)}=\varnothing
for all
\lambda<\tau
was settled in
[
10
]
.

Question 1.2
.

Let
f
be a homeomorphism between two proper closed subsets
P
and
K
of
D^{\tau}
. Is it true that
f
can be extended to a homeomorphism of
D^{\tau}
provided
f(P^{(\lambda)})=K^{(\lambda)}
for every
\lambda\in\mathfrak{L}_{P}
?

3.
Homeomorphisms on product spaces

For any space

We use the technique developed in

Proposition 3.1

Let

\ddot{o}

•

Let
