Galois scaffolds for extraspecial p p -extensions in characteristic 0
Kevin Keating, Paul Schwartz

Abstract
Let K K be a local field of characteristic 0 with residue characteristic p p . Let G G be an extraspecial p p -group and let L / K L/K be a totally ramified G G -extension. In this paper we find sufficient conditions for L / K L/K to admit a Galois scaffold. This leads to sufficient conditions for the ring of integers 𝔒 L \mathfrak{O}_{L} to be free of rank 1 over its associated order 𝔄 L / K \mathfrak{A}_{L/K} , and to stricter conditions which imply that 𝔄 L / K \mathfrak{A}_{L/K} is a Hopf order in the group ring K ⁡ [ G ] K[G] .



1
Introduction

Let
L/K
be a finite totally ramified extension of
local fields and set
G=\Gal(L/K)
. The associated
order in
K[G]
of the ring of integers
\mathfrak{O}_{L}
of
L

is defined to be

\mathfrak{A}_{L/K}=\{\lambda\in K[G]:\lambda(\mathfrak{O}_{L})\subset\mathfrak{O}_{L}\}.

Thus
\mathfrak{A}_{L/K}
is an
\mathfrak{O}_{K}
-order in
K[G]
, and

\mathfrak{O}_{L}
is a module over
\mathfrak{A}_{L/K}
. It is a
classical problem to determine when
\mathfrak{O}_{L}
is free
(necessarily of rank 1) over
\mathfrak{A}_{L/K}
. In the case
where
L/K
is tamely ramified we have

\mathfrak{A}_{L/K}=\mathfrak{O}_{K}[G]
, and E. Noether showed that
\mathfrak{O}_{L}

is free over
\mathfrak{A}_{L/K}

[
Noe32
]
. Much less is known
about this problem in the case where
L/K
is wildly
ramified. When
L/K
is a ramified
C_{p}
-extension,
necessary and sufficient conditions for
\mathfrak{O}_{L}
to be
free over
\mathfrak{A}_{L/K}
were given in
[
BF72
,
BBF72
]
for
the case
\ch(K)=0
, and in
[
Aib03
,
Let05
]
for the
case
\ch(K)=p
. Sufficient conditions are known for

\mathfrak{O}_{L}
to be free over
\mathfrak{A}_{L/K}
in the cases where

G
is an elementary abelian
p
-group
[
BE18
]
,

G\cong C_{p^{2}}
and
\ch(K)=p

[
BE13
]
, or

G\cong C_{p^{2}}
and
\ch(K)=0

[
KS22
]
. When

\ch(K)=p
,
[
EK23
]
gives sufficient conditions for

\mathfrak{O}_{L}
to be free over
\mathfrak{A}_{L/K}
for an arbitrary

p
-group
G
.

In this paper we give sufficient conditions for

\mathfrak{O}_{L}
to be free over
\mathfrak{A}_{L/K}
in the cases where

\ch(K)=0
and
G=\Gal(L/K)
is an extraspecial

p
-group. As an application we give sufficient
conditions for
\mathfrak{A}_{L/K}
to be a Hopf order. Our
proofs are based on constructing extensions
L/K
which
possess a Galois scaffold, as defined in
[
BCE18
]
.
We focus on the case
\ch(K)=0
, but all the statements
and proofs presented here are valid, and often simpler,
when
\ch(K)=p
.

Let
K
be a local field of characteristic 0 with
residue characteristic
p
. Then
K
is complete with
respect to a discrete valuation
v_{K}
which is
normalized so that
v_{K}(K^{\times})=\mathbb{Z}
. The ring of
integers of
K
is
\mathfrak{O}_{K}=\{x\in K:v_{K}(x)\geq 0\}
and
the unique maximal ideal of
\mathfrak{O}_{K}
is

\mathfrak{M}_{K}=\{x\in K:v_{K}(x)\geq 1\}
. Let
\pi_{K}
be a
uniformizer for
K
. Thus
\pi_{K}
is any element of

\mathfrak{O}_{K}
that satisfies
v_{K}(\pi_{K})=1
, or equivalently

\mathfrak{M}_{K}=(\pi_{K})
. We assume that the residue field

\mathfrak{O}_{K}/\mathfrak{M}_{K}
of
K
is a perfect field of characteristic

p
. Let
e_{K}=v_{K}(p)
be the absolute
ramification index of
K
and let
K^{sep}
be a
separable closure of
K
. Then
v_{K}
extends uniquely
to a valuation on
K^{sep}
, which we also denote by

v_{K}
. We will often work with towers

K_{0}\subset K_{1}\subset\dots\subset K_{n}
of field
extensions of degree
p
. In this case we usually
denote
v_{K_{i}},\mathfrak{O}_{K_{i}},\mathfrak{M}_{K_{i}},\pi_{K_{i}},e_{K_{i}}
by

v_{i},\mathfrak{O}_{i},\mathfrak{M}_{i},\pi_{i},e_{i}
.

2
Higher ramification theory

In this section we recall some facts about higher
ramification theory of finite separable extensions of
local fields. We do not assume that our extensions are
Galois, but for simplicity we only consider extensions
whose degree is a power of
p
. For more information
about higher ramification theory for extensions which
are separable, but not necessarily Galois, see

[
Hel90
]
,
[
Hel91
]
, the appendix to
[
Del84
]
,
or Chapter 13 of
[
CGK+21
]
.

Let
K
be a local field with residue
characteristic
p
and let
L/K
be a subextension of

K^{sep}/K
of degree
p^{n}
. Let
L_{0}/K
be the maximal
unramified subextension of
L/K
. Let
\Gamma
denote
the set of
K
-embeddings of
L
into
K^{sep}
, and let

\Gamma_{0}
be the subset of
\Gamma
consisting of

\sigma\in\Gamma
such that
\sigma(L_{0})=L_{0}
and

\sigma
induces the identity map on the residue field
of
L_{0}
. For real
x>0
define

\Gamma_{x}=\{\gamma\in\Gamma_{0}:v_{L}(\gamma(\pi_{L})-\pi_{L})\geq x+1\}.

Then
\Gamma_{x}
does not depend on the choice of
uniformizer
\pi_{L}
for
L
. Observe that

\Gamma_{x}\subset\Gamma_{y}
for
0\leq y\leq x
, and

\Gamma_{x}=\{\id_{L}\}
for sufficiently large
x
. We
say that
\Gamma_{x}
is the
x
th subset in the lower
ramification filtration of
\Gamma
.

Lemma 2.1
.

For each
x>0
,
|\Gamma_{x}|
is a power of
p
.

Proof.

Let
M/K
be the Galois closure of
L/K
. Set

G=\Gal(M/K)
and
H=\Gal(M/L)
. Then
\Gamma
can be
identified with the coset space
G/H
. Let
x>0
and
set

G(x)=\{\sigma\in G:v_{L}(\sigma(\pi_{L})-\pi_{L})\geq x+1\}.

Then
H\leq G(x)\leq G
and
\Gamma_{x}
is identified
with
G(x)/H
. The lemma now follows from
Lagrange’s theorem.
∎

Say that
b>0
is a lower ramification number for

L/K
if
\Gamma_{b}\not=\Gamma_{b+\epsilon}
for all

\epsilon>0
. Say that
b>0
is a lower ramification
number with multipicity
m
if

|\Gamma_{b}|/|\Gamma_{b+\epsilon}|=p^{m}
. Let
p^{t}
be
the ramification index of
L/K
. Then
L/K
has
t

positive lower ramification numbers, counted with
multiplicity. The positive lower ramification numbers
of
L/K
can be viewed either as a multiset or as a
nondecreasing sequence
b_{1}\leq b_{2}\leq\dots\leq b_{t}
of
rational numbers. To account for the unramified part of

L/K
we say that
-1
is a lower ramification number of

L/K
with multipicity
n-t
. Thus
L/K
has a total of

n
lower ramification numbers, counted with
multiplicity.

We define the positive upper ramification numbers
of
L/K
by setting
u_{1}=b_{1}
and

u_{i+1}=u_{i}+p^{-i}(b_{i+1}-b_{i})
for
1\leq i\leq t-1
.
In addition, if
t<n
we say that
-1
is an upper
ramification number of
L/K
with multipicity
n-t
.
For
1\leq i\leq t
let
\Gamma^{u_{i}}=\Gamma_{b_{i}}
, and
for real
x\geq 0
set

\Gamma^{x}=\begin{cases}\Gamma^{u_{1}}&\text{if }0\leq x\leq u_{1},\\
\Gamma^{u_{j}}&\text{if $u_{j-1}<x\leq u_{j}$ for some
$2\leq j\leq t$},\\
\{\id_{L}\}&\text{if }x>u_{t}.\end{cases}

Let
M/K
be a subextension of
L/K
, and let
\Delta

be the set of
K
-embeddings of
M
into
K^{sep}
. We
can define ramification subsets
\Delta_{x}
and

\Delta^{x}
of
\Delta
, which give us ramification
numbers for
M/K
. For
x>0
we have

\Delta^{x}=\{\gamma|_{M}:\gamma\in\Gamma^{x}\}
(see for
instance Theorem 13.15 in
[
CGK+21
]
). It follows that
the multiset of upper ramification numbers of
M/K
is
contained in the multiset of upper ramification numbers
of
L/K
.

Let
L/K
be a Galois extension of degree
p^{n}
and
set
G=\Gal(L/K)
. Then
G
may be identified with the
set of
K
-embeddings of
L
into
K^{sep}
, so the
definitions given above are valid with
\Gamma
replaced
by
G
. In this setting we find that
G_{x}
and
G^{x}

are normal subgroups of
G
for all
x\geq 0
. Let

H\trianglelefteq G
and set
M=L^{H}
. Then the upper
ramification filtration of
\Gal(M/K)\cong G/H
is given
by
(G/H)^{x}=G^{x}H/H
for
x\geq 0
.

We will need the following technical facts about
ramification numbers:

Proposition 2.2
.

[
BE18
, Example 1.2]

Let
L/K
be a totally ramified
C_{p}
-extension of
local fields with ramification number
b
, and let

x\in L\smallsetminus\{0\}
. Then

v_{L}((\sigma-1)x)\geq v_{L}(x)+b
, with equality if and
only if
p\nmid v_{L}(x)
.

Proposition 2.3
.

Let
L/K
be a totally ramified Galois extension of
degree
p^{n}
, with upper ramification numbers

u_{1}\leq\dots\leq u_{n-2}\leq u_{n-1}<u_{n}
. Set

G=\Gal(L/K)
, and suppose there is
H\leq Z(G)
such
that
H\cong C_{p}^{2}
. Let
M=L^{H}
be the fixed field of

H
, and assume that
u_{n}
is not an upper ramification
number of
M/K
. Then there is a unique

C_{p}
-subextension
F_{0}/M
of
L/M
such that
F_{0}/K

has ramification numbers
u_{1},\dots,u_{n-2},u_{n-1}
.
For all other
C_{p}
-subextensions
F/M
of
L/M
,
u_{n}

is an upper ramification number of
F/K
.

Proof.

Since
u_{n}
is not an upper ramification number of
M/K

we have
G^{u_{n}}H/H=(G/H)^{u_{n}}=\{1\}
, and hence

G^{u_{n}}\leq H
. Let
A\leq H
be such that
|A|=p
.
Then
(G/A)^{u_{n}+\epsilon}=G^{u_{n}+\epsilon}A/A
is
necessarily trivial, and
(G/A)^{u_{n}}=G^{u_{n}}A/A
is
trivial if and only if
G^{u_{n}}\leq A
. Since

|G^{u_{n}}|=p
it follows that
u_{n}
is an upper
ramification number of
L^{A}/K
if and only if

A\not=G^{u_{n}}
. Set
F_{0}=L^{G^{u_{n}}}
. Then
u_{n}
is
not an upper ramification number of
F_{0}/K
, so the
upper ramification numbers of
F_{0}/K
are

u_{1},\dots,u_{n-2},u_{n-1}
.
∎

Proposition 2.4
.

Let
L/K
be a totally ramified extension of degree

p^{n}
. Let
b_{1}\leq\dots\leq b_{n}
be the lower
ramification numbers of
L/K
and let

u_{1}\leq\dots\leq u_{n}
be the upper ramification numbers.
Then for
1\leq i\leq n-1
and
m\geq i
we have

b_{i+1}-b_{i}\leq p^{m}(u_{i+1}-u_{i})
.

Proof.

In fact
b_{i+1}-b_{i}=p^{i}(u_{i+1}-u_{i})\leq p^{m}(u_{i+1}-u_{i})
.
∎

Corollary 2.5
.

Let
L/K
be a totally ramified extension of degree

p^{n}
. Let
b_{1}\leq\dots\leq b_{n}
be the lower
ramification numbers of
L/K
and let

u_{1}\leq\dots\leq u_{n}
be the upper ramification numbers.
Then

1.

b_{j}-b_{i}\leq p^{j-1}(u_{j}-u_{i})
for

1\leq i\leq j\leq n
.

2.

b_{j}\leq p^{j-1}u_{j}
for
1\leq j\leq n
, with
equality only if
j=1
.

Proof.

(a) The claim is clear when
i=j
. Now assume

1\leq i<j\leq n
. Proposition
2.4
gives

b_{h+1}-b_{h}\leq p^{j-1}(u_{h+1}-u_{h})
for

i\leq h\leq j-1
. Thus

\displaystyle b_{j}-b_{i}

\displaystyle=\sum_{h=i}^{j-1}\,(b_{h+1}-b_{h})

\displaystyle\leq\sum_{h=i}^{j-1}\,p^{j-1}(u_{h+1}-u_{h})

\displaystyle=p^{j-1}(u_{j}-u_{i}).

(b) This follows from (a) by setting
i=1
and noting
that
b_{1}=u_{1}
.
∎

3
Constructing abelian
p
-extensions

Let
K
be a local field of characteristic 0 whose
residue field has characteristic
p
. In this section
we use Artin-Schreier polynomials and Witt addition
polynomials to construct totally ramified abelian

p
-extensions
L/K
. These results will be used in
Sections
4
and
5
. The
corresponding constructions for local fields of
characteristic
p
are well-known.

We define the Artin-Schreier polynomial

\wp(X)\in\mathbb{F}_{p}[X]
by
\wp(X)=X^{p}-X
. Given
a\in K

with
v_{K}(a)>-pe_{K}/(p-1)
, choose
\alpha\in K^{sep}

such that
\wp(\alpha)=a
. Let
\mathcal{G}_{K}=\Gal(K^{sep}/K)

be the absolute Galois group of
K
and let

\sigma\in\mathcal{G}_{K}
. By
[
MW56
, Theorem 5]
there is

m_{0}\in\mathbb{Z}
such that

(\sigma-1)\alpha\equiv m_{0}\pmod{\mathfrak{M}_{K}}
. Define

m_{\sigma}
to be the image of
m_{0}
in

\mathbb{F}_{p}\cong\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}
. Then
m_{\sigma}
does not depend on
the choice of
\alpha
, so we may define

\chi_{a}^{K}:\mathcal{G}_{K}\to\mathbb{F}_{p}
by
\chi_{a}^{K}(\sigma)=m_{\sigma}
.
Then
\chi_{a}^{K}
is a continuous homomorphism.

Proposition 3.1
.

Let
a\in K
satisfy
-pe_{K}/(p-1)<v_{K}(a)<0
and

p\nmid v_{K}(a)
. Choose
\alpha\in K^{sep}
such that

\wp(\alpha)=a
and set
L=K(\alpha)
. Then
L/K
is a
totally ramified
C_{p}
-extension with upper and lower
ramification number
-v_{K}(a)
. Set

H=\ker\chi_{a}^{K}\leq\mathcal{G}_{K}
. Then
L=(K^{sep})^{H}
is the
fixed field of
H
.

Proof.

Set
\wp(K)=\{\wp(b):b\in K\}
. Our hypotheses on
a

imply that
a\not\in\wp(K)
, so
\chi_{a}^{K}
is
nontrivial. Hence by
[
MW56
, Theorem 5]
,
L/K
is a

C_{p}
-extension. Furthermore, there is

\tau\in\Gal(L/K)
such that

(\tau-1)\alpha\equiv 1\pmod{\mathfrak{M}_{L}}
. Since

p
does not divide
v_{K}(a)=pv_{K}(\alpha)
we see that

L/K
is totally ramified. In addition, it follows from
Proposition 2.5 of
[
FV02
, III]
that the ramification
number of
L/K
is
-v_{L}(\alpha)=-v_{K}(a)
. It is clear
from Galois theory that
L=(K^{sep})^{H}
.
∎

The following is proved as Lemma 3.5 in

[
KS22
]
:

Lemma 3.2
.

Let
a\in K\smallsetminus\wp(K)
be such that

u=-v_{K}(a)
satisfies
0<u<pe_{K}/(p-1)
. Let

\alpha
be a root of
f(X)=X^{p}-X-a
and set

L=K(\alpha)
.

1.

If
L/K
is a totally ramified
C_{p}
-extension
then there is
\sigma\in\Gal(L/K)
such that

\sigma(\alpha)=\alpha+1+\epsilon
for some

\epsilon\in L
with

v_{K}(\epsilon)\geq e_{K}-(1-p^{-1})u
.

2.

If
p\nmid u
, then
L/K
is a totally ramified

C_{p}
-extension and

v_{K}(\epsilon)=e_{K}-(1-p^{-1})u
.

Proposition 3.3
.

Let
L
be a local field with residue characteristic
p

and let
\alpha\in L
satisfy
v_{L}(\alpha)=-u
, with

0<u<e_{L}/(p-1)
. Let
\beta\in L
be such that

\wp(\alpha)\equiv\wp(\beta)\pmod{\mathfrak{M}_{L}}
. Then there is

k\in\mathbb{Z}
such that
\beta\equiv\alpha+k\pmod{\mathfrak{M}_{L}}
.

Proof.

Set
\delta=\beta-\alpha
. Then

\wp(\beta)-\wp(\alpha)=-\delta+\sum_{i=1}^{p}\binom{p}{i}\alpha^{p-i}\delta^{i}.

(3.1)

If
v_{L}(\delta)<0
then

v_{L}(\delta^{p})<v_{L}(\binom{p}{i}\alpha^{p-i}\delta^{i})
for

1\leq i\leq p-1
and
v_{L}(\delta^{p})<v_{L}(\delta)
. This
implies
v_{L}(\wp(\beta)-\wp(\alpha))=v_{L}(\delta^{p})<0
, a
contradiction. Hence
v_{L}(\delta)\geq 0
. It now follows
from (
3.1
) and the assumption on
v_{L}(\alpha)

that
0\equiv-\delta+\delta^{p}\pmod{\mathfrak{M}_{L}}
. Thus

\delta\equiv k\pmod{\mathfrak{M}_{L}}
for some
k\in\mathbb{Z}
.
∎

We will make frequent use of the following result:

Proposition 3.4
.

Let
a,b\in K
satisfy
v_{K}(b)\geq v_{K}(a)>-pe_{K}/(p-1)

and
v_{K}(p^{p}a^{p-1}b)>0
. Then

1.

\chi_{a+b}^{K}=\chi_{a}^{K}+\chi_{b}^{K}
.

2.

For each root
\alpha
of
X^{p}-X-a
and each root

\beta
of
X^{p}-X-b
there is a unique root
\gamma
of

X^{p}-X-(a+b)
such that

\gamma\equiv\alpha+\beta\pmod{p\alpha^{p-1}\beta}
.
Furthermore, we have
\gamma\in K(\alpha,\beta)
.

Proof.

(a) This is proved in Proposition 5 of
[
MW56
]
.

(b) By the proof of Proposition 5 in
[
MW56
]
there
is a unique root
\gamma
of
X^{p}-X-(a+b)
such that

\delta:=\alpha+\beta-\gamma
satisfies
v_{K}(\delta)>0
.
As in the proof of Proposition
3.3
we get

\displaystyle-\delta+\sum_{i=1}^{p}\binom{p}{i}\gamma^{p-i}\delta^{i}

\displaystyle=\wp(\alpha+\beta)-\wp(\gamma)

\displaystyle\equiv\wp(\alpha)+\wp(\beta)-(a+b)

\displaystyle\pmod{p\alpha^{p-1}\beta}

\displaystyle\equiv 0

\displaystyle\pmod{p\alpha^{p-1}\beta}.

Hence
\delta\equiv 0\pmod{p\alpha^{p-1}\beta}
. It
follows from (a) that
\gamma\in K(\alpha,\beta)
.
∎

Corollary 3.5
.

Let
K,a,b,\alpha,\beta,\gamma
satisfy the conditions
of Proposition
3.4
. Then for all

\sigma\in\Gal(K(\alpha,\beta)/K)
we have

(\sigma-1)(\gamma)\equiv(\sigma-1)(\alpha)+(\sigma-1)(\beta)\pmod{p\alpha^{p-1}}.

Proof.

By Lemma
3.2
there are
k,\ell,m\in\mathbb{Z}

such that

\displaystyle(\sigma-1)(\alpha)

\displaystyle\equiv k

\displaystyle\pmod{p\alpha^{p-1}}

\displaystyle(\sigma-1)(\beta)

\displaystyle\equiv\ell

\displaystyle\pmod{p\beta^{p-1}}

\displaystyle(\sigma-1)(\gamma)

\displaystyle\equiv m

\displaystyle\pmod{p\gamma^{p-1}}.

Since
\chi_{a+b}^{K}(\sigma)=\chi_{a}^{K}(\sigma)+\chi_{b}^{K}(\sigma)
we have
m\equiv k+\ell\pmod{p}
.
Since
v_{K}(\alpha)\leq v_{K}(\beta)
and

v_{K}(\alpha)\leq v_{K}(\gamma)
, the corollary follows from
this.
∎

Corollary 3.6
.

Let
a,a^{\prime}\in K
satisfy
v_{K}(a)>-pe_{K}/(p-1)
and

a\equiv a^{\prime}\pmod{\mathfrak{M}_{K}}
. Then

1.

\chi_{a}^{K}=\chi_{a^{\prime}}^{K}
.

2.

For each root
\alpha
of
X^{p}-X-a
there is a
root
\alpha^{\prime}
of
X^{p}-X-a^{\prime}
such that

v_{K}(\alpha-\alpha^{\prime})>0
.

Proof.

We have
a^{\prime}=a+b
with
v_{K}(b)>0
. Since
\chi_{b}
is the
trivial character, the corollary follows.
∎

Proposition 3.7
.

Let
u<e_{K}
be a positive integer. Choose

a_{1},\dots,a_{r}\in\mathfrak{M}_{K}^{-u}
such that

\overline{S}=\{a_{i}+\mathfrak{M}_{K}^{-u+1}:1\leq i\leq r\}

is an
\mathbb{F}_{p}
-linearly independent subset of

\mathfrak{M}_{K}^{-u}/\mathfrak{M}_{K}^{-u+1}
. For
1\leq i\leq r
let

\alpha_{i}\in K^{sep}
be a root of
X^{p}-X-a_{i}
. Then

L=K(\alpha_{1},\dots,\alpha_{r})
is an elementary abelian

p
-extension of
K
of rank
r
, with a single upper
and lower ramification number
u
of multiplicity
r
.

Proof.

Clearly
L/K
is an elementary abelian
p
-extension of
degree
p^{s}
for some
s\leq r
. Let

n_{1},\dots,n_{r}\in\mathbb{Z}
and set
b=n_{1}a_{1}+\dots+n_{r}a_{r}
.
Then
\chi_{b}^{K}=n_{1}\chi_{a_{1}}^{K}+\dots+n_{r}\chi_{a_{r}}^{K}
by
Proposition
3.4
(a) and the bound on
u
.
Therefore
\chi_{b}^{K}
induces a character from

\Gal(L/K)
to
\mathbb{F}_{p}
. If
n_{1}^{\prime},\dots,n_{r}^{\prime}
are
integers such that
b^{\prime}=n_{1}^{\prime}a_{1}+\dots+n_{r}^{\prime}a_{r}
satisfies

\chi_{b^{\prime}}^{K}=\chi_{b}^{K}
then
\chi_{b^{\prime}-b}^{K}
is trivial.
Hence
b^{\prime}\equiv b\pmod{\mathfrak{M}_{K}^{-u}}
, so by the
independence of
\overline{S}
we get
n_{i}^{\prime}\equiv n_{i}\pmod{p}

for
1\leq i\leq r

p^{2}

of order

We then show how to construct totally ramified

Definition 4.1

p

To construct Galois scaffolds for

8

A Galois scaffold is a structure that can be attached to
certain totally ramified Galois
p
-extensions. A
Galois scaffold for

]

For

be a local field with residue characteristic

of degree

of

Theorem 10.1
.

Let
G
be equal to either
H(n)
or
