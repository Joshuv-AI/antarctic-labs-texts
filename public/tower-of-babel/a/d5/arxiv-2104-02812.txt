Certain study of Generalized Apostol-Bernoulli poly-Daehee polynomials and its properties
Nabiullah Khan and Saddam Husain

Abstract
. In this paper, we present a new type of generating function of generalized Apostol-Bernoulli poly Daehee polynomial (GABPDP). By using generating function of GABPDP, we discus some special cases and useful identites of generalized Apostol-Bernoulli poly Daehee polynomials. We also drive implicit summation formulae of it.

2.
Generalized Apostol-Bernoulli Poly-Daehee Polynomials

In this section, we define a generalization and unification of generalized Apostol-Bernoulli poly-Daehee polynomials (GABPDP) and define a certain useful properties and implicit formulae of Apostol-Bernoulli poly-Daehee Polynomials. which is defined as follows:

For
\gamma,\eta\in\mathbb{R},n,m\in\mathbb{N}
and
a\in\mathbb{C}
we have define the following generating function for generalized Apostol-Bernoulli poly Daehee polynomials (GABPDP):

(1+x)^{\gamma}\,\,\frac{log(1+x)}{L_{ik}(1-e^{-x})}e^{\eta{x}}\left(\frac{x^{m}}{\lambda e^{x}-\sum\limits_{l=0}^{m-1}\frac{x^{l}}{l!}}\right)^{a}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{x^{n}}{n!}\,{{}_{\mathfrak{B}}{\mathfrak{D}}_{n,a}^{[k,m-1]}(\gamma,\eta;\lambda)}.

(2.1)

If
\gamma=\eta=0
, then
{}_{\mathfrak{B}}{\mathfrak{D}}_{n,a}^{[k,m-1]}(0,0;\lambda):={{}_{\mathfrak{B}}{\mathfrak{D}}_{n,a}^{[k,m-1]}(\lambda)}
are called generalized Apostol-Bernoulli poly-Daehee number (GABPDN).

Theorem 2.1
.

Let
\gamma,\eta\in\mathbb{R}
,
n,m\in\mathbb{N}_{0}
then GABPDP satisfy the following relation:

{{}_{\mathfrak{B}}{\mathfrak{D}}_{n,a}^{[k,m-1]}(\gamma,\eta;\lambda)}=\sum\limits_{j=0}^{n}\binom{n}{j}{{\mathfrak{D}}_{n-j}^{(k)}(\gamma)}{\mathfrak{B}}_{j}^{[m-1,a]}(\eta;\lambda).

(2.2)

Proof.

Using equation (
2.1
), we obtain

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{x^{n}}{n!}\,{{}_{\mathfrak{B}}{\mathfrak{D}}_{n,a}^{(k)}(\gamma,\eta;\lambda)}=

\displaystyle(1+x)^{\gamma}\,\,\frac{log(1+x)}{L_{ik}(1-e^{-x})}e^{\eta{x}}\left(\frac{x^{m}}{\lambda e^{x}-\sum\limits_{l=0}^{m-1}\frac{x^{l}}{l!}}\right)^{a}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left(\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{x^{n}}{n!}\,{\mathfrak{D}}_{n}^{(k)}(\gamma)\right)\left(\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\frac{x^{j}}{j!}\,{\mathfrak{B}}_{j}^{[m-1,a]}(\eta;\lambda)\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\,\,\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\frac{x^{n+j}}{n!j!}{\mathfrak{D}}_{n}^{(k)}(\gamma){\mathfrak{B}}_{j}^{[m-1,a]}(\eta;\lambda).

Replceing n by n-j and and using series arrangement technique, we obtain

\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{x^{n}}{n!}\,{{}_{\mathfrak{B}}{\mathfrak{D}}_{n,a}^{[k,m-1]}(\gamma,\eta;\lambda)}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{x^{n}}{n!}\left(\sum\limits_{j=0}^{n}\binom{n}{j}{\mathfrak{D}}_{n-j}^{(k)}(\gamma){\mathfrak{B}}_{j}^{[m-1,a]}(\eta;\lambda)\right).

By equating both side with same power of
x^{n}
we get desired result.
∎

{{}_{\mathfrak{B}}{\mathfrak{D}}_{n,a}^{[k,m-1]}(\gamma,\eta;\lambda)}=\frac{{{}_{\mathfrak{B}}{\mathfrak{D}}_{n+1,a}^{[k,m-1]}(\gamma+1,\eta;\lambda)}-{{}_{\mathfrak{B}}{\mathfrak{D}}_{n+1,a}^{[k,m-1]}(\gamma,\eta;\lambda)}}{n+1}.

=(1+x)^{\gamma+1}\,\,\frac{log(1+x)}{L_{ik}(1-e^{-x})}e^{\eta{x}}\left(\frac{x^{m}}{\lambda e^{x}-\sum\limits_{l=0}^{m-1}\frac{x^{l}}{l!}}\right)^{a}-(1+x)^{\gamma}\,\,\frac{log(1+x)}{L_{ik}(1-e^{-x})}e^{\eta{x}}\left(\frac{x^{m}}{\lambda e^{x}-\sum\limits_{l=0}^{m-1}\frac{x^{l}}{l!}}\right)^{a}

_{\mathfrak{B}}{\mathfrak{D}}_{n,a,b+c}^{[k,m-1]}(\gamma,\eta;\lambda)=\sum\limits_{n,q=0}^{b,c}\binom{b}{n}\binom{c}{q}(\eta-\omega)^{n+q}~~_{\mathfrak{B}}{\mathfrak{D}}_{n,a;b+c-n-q}^{[k,m-1]}(\gamma,\omega;\lambda)
