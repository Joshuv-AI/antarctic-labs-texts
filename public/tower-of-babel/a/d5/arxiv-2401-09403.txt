Arithmetic Hodge-Iwasawa Moduli Stacks
Xin Tong

Abstract
In this article, we are going to construct arithmetic moduli stacks of G G -bundles after our previous construction on Hodge-Iwasawa theory. These stacks parametrize certain Hodge-Iwasawa structures in a coherent way.
