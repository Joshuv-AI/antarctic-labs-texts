Stability range of parameters at fixed points for a class of complex dynamics
Zhen-Hua Feng, Hai-Bo Sang, B. S. Xie

Abstract
We study the parameters range for the fixed point of a class of complex dynamics with the rational fractional function as R n , a , c ​ ( z ) = z n + a z n + c R_{n,a,c}(z)=z^{n}+\frac{a}{z^{n}}+c , where n = 1 , 2 , 3 , 4 n=1,2,3,4 is specified, a a and c c are two complex parameters. The relationship between two parameters, a a and c c , is obtained at the fixed point. Moreover the explicit expression of the parameter a a and c c in terms of λ \lambda is derived, where λ \lambda is the derivative function at fixed point. The parameter regimes for the stability of the fixed point are presented numerically for some typical different cases.

I
Introduction

Rational fractional complex dynamics is the study of iteration, which is the process of repeatedly applying a rational fractional
R(z)
to itself. For example, we select a value
z_{0}
in the domain of
R(z)
and then use
R(z)
to continuously iterate at the point
z_{0}
to obtain the sequence

\left\{z_{0},z_{1}=R(z_{0}),z_{2}=R(z_{1}),\dots,z_{n}=R(z_{n-1}),\dots\right\}.

(1)

Note that the sequence is the orbit of
z_{0}
under
R(z)
.
z_{0}
is a fixed point if
z_{0}=z_{1}=z_{2}=\dots
. The derivative of
R(z)
at
z_{0}
is the multiplier
\lambda
, i.e.
R^{\prime}(z_{0})=\lambda
. Fixed points can be classified according to
\lambda
as follows
[
1
]
:

•

Superattraction:
\lambda=0

•

Attraction:
0<|\lambda|<1
.

•

Repelling:
|\lambda|>1
.

•

Rationally neutral:
|\lambda|=1
and
|\lambda^{n}|=1
for some integer
n
.

•

Irrationally neutral:
|\lambda|=1
but
|\lambda^{n}|
is never 1.

These superattraction, attraction and repelling fixed points make up the Fatou set, the complement of Fatou set is Julia set
[
2
]
.

For
R(z)=z^{2}+c
, those parameters c that can generate the Julia set at the superattraction point
z=0
form the famous Mandelbrot set
[
1
,
2
,
3
,
4
]
. Furthermore, the rational fractions
R_{n,a,0}=z^{n}+\frac{a}{z^{n}}
and
R_{n,a,c}=z^{n}+\frac{a}{z^{n}}+c
have been studied in detail
[
4
,
5
]
. However, the relationship between parameters is not obtained.

We study the rational fractional
R_{n,a,c}=z^{n}+\frac{a}{z^{n}}+c
and obtain the relationship between the parameters
a
,
c
and
\lambda
, which denoted
F(a,c,\lambda)
. The relationship between the parameters is precise when
n=1,2,3,4
and approximate when
n>4
. When one parameter is fixed,
F(a,c,\lambda)
maps the unit circle in the
\lambda
plane to the stability range of the other parameter.

II
The relationship of the parameters

For
R(z)=z^{n}+\frac{a}{z^{n}}+c
, the fixed point satisfies the following equation

z=z^{n}+\frac{a}{z^{n}}+c,

(2)

and the multiplier
\lambda
satisfies

\frac{\lambda}{n}z=z^{n}-\frac{a}{z^{n}}.

(3)

Substituting Eq.(
2
) into Eq.(
3
), we obtain

\frac{\lambda}{n}\left(z^{n}+\frac{a}{z^{n}}+c\right)=z^{n}-\frac{a}{z^{n}}.

(4)

After a simple calculation, we obtain

\left(1-\frac{\lambda}{n}\right)z^{2n}-\frac{\lambda}{n}cz^{n}-\left(1+\frac{\lambda}{n}\right)a=0.

(5)

The Eq. (
5
) is easily solved for
z^{n}
as that

z^{n}=\frac{c}{2\left(1-\frac{\lambda}{n}\right)}\left[\frac{\lambda}{n}\pm\sqrt{\frac{\lambda^{2}}{n^{2}}+\frac{4a}{c^{2}}\left(1-\frac{\lambda^{2}}{n^{2}}\right)}\right].

(6)

Now we define

\xi=\frac{c}{1-\frac{\lambda^{2}}{n^{2}}}\left[1\pm\sqrt{\frac{\lambda^{2}}{n^{2}}+\frac{4a}{c^{2}}\left(1-\frac{\lambda^{2}}{n^{2}}\right)}\right],

(7)

and combining Eq.(
2
) and Eq.(
6
), it yields

2\xi^{n}-\left(1+\frac{\lambda}{n}\right)\xi+c=0.

(8)

From Eqs.(
7
) and (
8
) one can obtain
c
easily,

c=\left(1-\frac{\lambda}{n}\right)\xi-\frac{2a}{\xi^{n}}.

(9)

Moreover, combining Eqs.(
8
) and (
9
), we obtain

\xi^{2n}-\frac{\lambda}{n}\xi^{n+1}-a=0.

(10)

When Eq.(
8
) can be solved for
\xi
, then substituting
\xi
into Eq.(
10
), therefore, one concludes that
a
can be expressed by
c
and
\lambda
. Similarly, by inserting the
\xi
as roots of Eq.(
10
) into Eq.(
8
), then
c
would be expressed by
a
and
\lambda
.

VI
Conclusion

In this paper, we obtain the exact or/and approximate analytical expressions about the stability parameter range of the fixed point for
R_{n,a,c}=z^{n}+\frac{a}{z^{n}}+c
when the integer
n
is small or/and large, respectively. Using these important analytical formulas, we can determine the stability range of the parameters conveniently via numerical calculations, in particularly, for the cases when
n=1,2,3
and
4
.

In the previous section we obtained the Eq.(
