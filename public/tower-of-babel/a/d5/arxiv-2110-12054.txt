The observation by LHCb of a tetraquark
T_{cc}
implies a rich spectrum of its
strange counterpart with quark content
cc\bar{q}\bar{s}
. If the observed

D^{*+}D^{0}
enhancement is merely due to kinematics, one might expect the
prediction of
M(T_{cc,s})=4106
MeV to fail. A corresponding effect in

M(D^{0}D^{0}K^{+})
around threshold would occur at
M(D_{s}(2714)D^{0})=4588
MeV.
If, on the other hand, one generalizes the approach of Ref.
Karliner and Rosner 2017
to systems with quark content
cc\bar{q}\bar{s}
, one
expects several states decaying to a pair of charmed mesons, one nonstrange and
one strange, including one around 4100 MeV decaying to
D^{*}D_{s}
and
DD_{s}^{*}

and one not far from threshold decaying to
D^{0}D^{0}K^{+}
.
