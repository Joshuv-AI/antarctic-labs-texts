Rota-Baxter operators on Turaev’s Hopf group (co)algebras I: Basic definitions and related algebraic structures Thanks: * Corresponding author
Tianshui Ma *, Jie Li, Liangyun Chen, Shuanhong Wang

Abstract
. We find a natural compatible condition between the Rota-Baxter operator and Turaev’s (Hopf) group-(co)algebras, which leads to the concept of Rota-Baxter Turaev’s (Hopf) group-(co)algebra. Two characterizations of Rota-Baxter Turaev’s group-algebras (abbr. T-algebras) are obtained: one by Atkinson factorization and the other by T-quasi-idempotent elements. The relations among some related Turaev’s group algebraic structures (such as (tri)dendriform T-algebras, Zinbiel T-algebras, pre-Lie T-algebras, Lie T-algebras) are discussed, and some concrete examples from the algebras of dimensions 2,3 and 4 are given. At last we prove that Rota-Baxter Poisson T-algebras can produce pre-Poisson T-algebras and Poisson T-algebras can be obtained from pre-Poisson T-algebras.

2.1.
Definition and properties

Definition 2.1
.

Let
\pi
be a semigroup and
\lambda\in K
. A
T-algebra

[
36
]
is a pair
(\{A_{\varphi}\}_{\varphi\in\pi},\{\mu_{p,q}\}_{p,q\in\pi})
, where
\{A_{\varphi}\}_{\varphi\in\pi}
is a family of vector spaces together with a family of linear maps
\{\mu_{p,q}:A_{p}\otimes A_{q}\longrightarrow A_{pq}\}_{p,q\in\pi}
(write
\mu_{p,q}(a\otimes b)=a\cdot_{p,q}b
) such that

\displaystyle\mu_{pq,t}\circ(\mu_{p,q}\otimes{\rm id}_{A_{t}})=\mu_{p,qt}\circ({\rm id}_{A_{p}}\otimes\mu_{q,t}),

(2.1)

for all
p,q,t\in\pi
.
A
Rota-Baxter T-algebra of weight
\lambda
is a quadruple
(\{A_{\varphi}\}_{\varphi\in\pi},\{\mu_{p,q}\}_{p,q\in\pi},\{R_{\varphi}\}_{\varphi\in\pi}
,
\lambda)
(abbr.
(A,R)
), where
(\{A_{\varphi}\}_{\varphi\in\pi},\{\mu_{p,q}\}_{p,q\in\pi})
is a T-algebra and
\{R_{\varphi}:A_{\varphi}\longrightarrow A_{\varphi}\}_{\varphi\in\pi}
is a family of linear maps such that

\displaystyle\mu_{p,q}\circ(R_{p}\otimes R_{q})=R_{pq}\circ\mu_{p,q}\circ(R_{p}\otimes{\rm id}_{q}+{\rm id}_{p}\otimes R_{q}+\lambda{\rm id}_{p}\otimes{\rm id}_{q}),

(2.2)

for all
p,q\in\pi
.

If furthermore
\pi
is a monoid (i.e., a semigroup with a unit 1) and there is a linear map
\eta:K\longrightarrow A_{1}
such that

\displaystyle\mu_{p,1}\circ({\rm id}_{A_{p}}\otimes\eta)={\rm id}_{A_{p}}=\mu_{1,p}\circ(\eta\otimes{\rm id}_{A_{p}}),

(2.3)

then we call Rota-Baxter T-algebra
(A,R)

unital
.

Lemma 2.3
.

Let
(A,R)
be a Rota-Baxter T-algebra of weight

\displaystyle\tilde{R}:=\{\tilde{R}_{\varphi}=-\lambda id_{A_{\varphi}}-R_{\varphi}\}.

(2.4)

Then

is also a Rota-Baxter T-algebra of weight

Proof.

For any

,

\displaystyle\tilde{R}_{p}(a)\cdot_{p,q}\tilde{R}_{q}(b)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(-\lambda a-R_{p}(a))\cdot_{p,q}(-\lambda b-R_{q}(b))

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\lambda^{2}a\cdot_{p,q}b+\lambda R_{p}(a)\cdot_{p,q}b+\lambda a\cdot_{p,q}R_{q}(b)+R_{p}(a)\cdot_{p,q}R_{q}(b)

\displaystyle(\lambda id_{A_{pq}}+R_{pq})(R_{p}(a)\cdot_{p,q}b+a\cdot_{p,q}R_{q}(b)+\lambda a\cdot_{p,q}b)

\displaystyle\tilde{R}_{pq}(\tilde{R}_{p}(a)\cdot_{p,q}b+a\cdot_{p,q}\tilde{R}_{q}(b)+\lambda a\cdot_{p,q}b).

This is what we need.

for Rota-Baxter T-algebras.

Proposition 2.4
.

Let
\pi
be a semigroup,
A
be a T-algebra and
R=\{R_{\varphi}:A_{\varphi}\longrightarrow A_{\varphi}\}
be a family of linear maps. Assume that
\lambda\in K
have no zero divisor in
\{A_{\varphi}\}_{\varphi\in\pi}
(which means that if for
\lambda\neq 0
and

such that

if and only if, for any

,

and

, there is an element

such that

\displaystyle R_{p}(a)\cdot_{p,q}R_{q}(b)=R_{pq}(c),\quad\tilde{R}_{p}(a)\cdot_{p,q}\tilde{R}_{q}(b)=-\tilde{R}_{pq}(c).

(2.5)

Proof.

(
\Longrightarrow
) Let
(A,R)
be a Rota-Baxter T-algebra of weight
\lambda\neq 0
. Then, for any
a\in A_{p}
,
b\in A_{q}
and

\displaystyle R_{p}(a)\cdot_{p,q}R_{q}(b)=R_{pq}\big(R_{p}(a)\cdot_{p,q}b+a\cdot_{p,q}R_{q}(b)+\lambda a\cdot_{p,q}b\big).

Let

. Then the above equality gives

\displaystyle R_{p}(a)\cdot_{p,q}R_{q}(b)=R_{pq}(c).

Based on the proof of Lemma

\displaystyle\tilde{R}_{p}(a)\cdot_{p,q}\tilde{R}_{q}(b)=-\tilde{R}_{pq}(c).

(
\Longleftarrow
) For any
a\in A_{p}
,
b\in A_{q}
and
p,q\in\pi
. Suppose that there exists an element

such that

. Then we have

\displaystyle-\lambda c

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle R_{pq}(c)+\tilde{R}_{pq}(c)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle R_{p}(a)\cdot_{p,q}R_{q}(b)-\tilde{R}_{p}(a)\cdot_{p,q}\tilde{R}_{q}(b)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle R_{p}(a)\cdot_{p,q}R_{q}(b)-\lambda(R_{p}(a)\cdot_{p,q}b+a\cdot_{p,q}R_{q}(b)+\lambda a\cdot_{p,q}b)-R_{p}(a)\cdot_{p,q}R_{q}(b)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\lambda(R_{p}(a)\cdot_{p,q}b+a\cdot_{p,q}R_{q}(b)+\lambda a\cdot_{p,q}b).

So we get

\displaystyle c

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle R_{p}(a)\cdot_{p,q}b+a\cdot_{p,q}R_{q}(b)+\lambda a\cdot_{p,q}b.

Then

\displaystyle R_{p}(a)\cdot_{p,q}R_{q}(b)=R_{pq}(R_{p}(a)\cdot_{p,q}b+a\cdot_{p,q}R_{q}(b)+\lambda a\cdot_{p,q}b),

as desired.
∎

Now we give the two useful definitions.

Definition 2.5
.

A family of linear maps
R=\{R_{\varphi}:A_{\varphi}\longrightarrow A_{\varphi}\}_{\varphi\in\pi}
is called
T-idempotent
if
R_{\varphi}^{2}=R_{\varphi},~\forall~\varphi\in\pi
. A family of linear maps
R=\{R_{\varphi}:A_{\varphi}\longrightarrow A_{\varphi}\}_{\varphi\in\pi}
is called
T-quasi-idempotent of weight
\lambda
if
R_{\varphi}^{2}=-\lambda R_{\varphi},\forall~\varphi\in\pi
.

Then we can obtain an equivalent description of Rota-Baxter T-algebras by T-quasi-idempotency.

Proposition 2.6
.

Let
\pi
be a monoid,
A
be a unital T-algebra and
R=\{R_{\varphi}:A_{\varphi}\longrightarrow A_{\varphi}\}_{\varphi\in\pi}
be left
A
-linear in the sense that
R_{pq}(a\cdot_{p,q}b)=a\cdot_{p,q}R_{q}(b),\forall~a\in A_{p},b\in A_{q},p,q\in\pi
. Then
(A,R)
is a Rota-Baxter T-algebra of weight
\lambda
if and only if
R
is T-quasi-idempotent of weight
\lambda
.

Proof.

Under the condition of left
A
-linearity of
R
, for
a\in A_{p}
,
b\in A_{q}
and
p,q\in\pi
, we can get

\displaystyle R_{p}(a)\cdot_{p,q}R_{q}(b)=R_{pq}(R_{p}(a)\cdot_{p,q}b+a\cdot_{p,q}R_{q}(b)+\lambda a\cdot_{p,q}b)

\displaystyle\Leftrightarrow a\cdot_{p,q}R_{q}^{2}(b)+\lambda a\cdot_{p,q}R_{q}(b)=0\Leftrightarrow R_{q}^{2}+\lambda R_{q}=0,

finishing the proof.
∎

Proposition 2.7
.

Let
(A,R)
be a Rota-Baxter T-algebra of weight
\lambda
. If

is T-idempotent, then

\displaystyle(1+\lambda)R_{pq}(a\cdot_{p,q}R_{q}(b))=0,\quad(1+\lambda)R_{pq}(R_{p}(a)\cdot_{p,q}b)=0,

(2.6)

\displaystyle(1+\lambda)(R_{p}(a)\cdot_{p,q}R_{q}(b)-\lambda R_{pq}(a\cdot_{p,q}b))=0,

for all
a\in A_{p}
,
b\in A_{q}
and
p,q\in\pi
.

Proof.

For any
a\in A_{p}
,

and

,

\displaystyle R_{p}(a)\cdot_{p,q}R_{q}(b)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle R_{p}(a)\cdot_{p,q}R_{q}^{2}(b)=R_{p}(a)\cdot_{p,q}R_{q}(R_{q}(b))

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle R_{pq}(R_{p}(a)\cdot_{p,q}R_{q}(b)+a\cdot_{p,q}R_{q}^{2}(b)+\lambda a\cdot_{p,q}R_{q}(b))

2.3.

2.3.1.
Dendriform T-algebras from tridendriform T-algebras

Motivated by algebraic K-theory, Loday invented the concept of a dendriform algebra. Loday and Ronco introduced the concept of a tridendriform algebra (previously also called a dendriform trialgebra) in the study of polytopes and Koszul duality
[
23
]
. We now give the T-version.

Definition 2.21
.

Let
\pi
be a semigroup. A
dendriform T-algebra
is a family of vector spaces
\{A_{\varphi}\}_{\varphi\in\pi}
with a family of binary operations
\{\prec_{p,q},\succ_{p,q}:A_{p}\otimes A_{q}\longrightarrow A_{pq}\}_{p,q\in\pi}
such that for
a\in A_{p}
,
b\in A_{q}
,
c\in A_{t}
and
p,q,t\in\pi
,

\displaystyle(a\prec_{p,q}b)\prec_{pq,t}c

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle a\prec_{p,qt}(b\prec_{q,t}c+b\succ_{q,t}c),

(2.10)

\displaystyle(a\succ_{p,q}b)\prec_{pq,t}c

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle a\succ_{p,qt}(b\prec_{q,t}c),

(2.11)

\displaystyle(a\prec_{p,q}b+a\succ_{p,q}b)\succ_{pq,t}c

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle a\succ_{p,qt}(b\succ_{q,t}c).

(2.12)

For simplicity, we denote it by
(\{A_{\varphi}\}_{\varphi\in\pi},\{\prec_{p,q}\}_{p,q\in\pi},\{\succ_{p,q}\}_{p,q\in\pi})
or
(A,\prec,\succ)
.

Definition 2.22
.

Let
\pi
be a semigroup. A
tridendriform T-algebra
is a family of vector spaces
\{A_{\varphi}\}_{\varphi\in\pi}
with a family of binary operations
\{\prec_{p,q},\succ_{p,q},\bullet_{p,q}:A_{p}\otimes A_{q}\longrightarrow A_{pq}\}_{p,q\in\pi}
such that for
a\in A_{p}
,
b\in A_{q}
,
c\in A_{t}
and
p,q,t\in\pi
,

\displaystyle(a\prec_{p,q}b)\prec_{pq,t}c

\displaystyle=

4.1.

Pre-Lie algebras are also called Vinberg algebras, as they appear under the name “left-symmetric algebras” in E. B. Vinberg

Definition 4.2
.

A

is a family of vector spaces
\{A_{\varphi}\}_{\varphi\in\pi}
with a family of binary operations
\{\ast_{p,q}:A_{p}\otimes A_{q}\longrightarrow A_{pq}\}_{p,q\in\pi}

\displaystyle a\ast_{p,qt}(b\ast_{q,t}c)-(a\ast_{p,q}b)\ast_{pq,t}c=b\ast_{q,pt}(a\ast_{p,t}c)-(b\ast_{q,p}a)\ast_{qp,t}c,

a\in A_{p}
,
b\in A_{q}
,
c\in A_{t}
and
p,q,t\in\pi
. We denote it by
(\{A_{\varphi}\}_{\varphi\in\pi},\{\ast_{p,q}\}_{p,q\in\pi})
.

Proposition 4.3
.

Let
(\{A_{\varphi}\}_{\varphi\in\pi},\{\prec_{p,q}\}_{p,q\in\pi},\{\succ_{p,q}\}_{p,q\in\pi})
be a dendriform T-algebra. Then
(\{A_{\varphi}\}_{\varphi\in\pi},

\{\ast_{p,q}\}_{p,q\in\pi})
is a pre-Lie T-algebra, where
\{\ast_{p,q}:A_{p}\otimes A_{q}\longrightarrow A_{pq}\}_{p,q\in\pi}
defined by

\displaystyle a\ast_{p,q}b:=a\succ_{p,q}b-b\prec_{q,p}a,

(4.3)

for
a\in A_{p}
,
b\in A_{q}
and
p,q\in\pi
.

Proof.

For
a\in A_{p}
,
b\in A_{q}
,
c\in A_{t}
and
p,q,t\in\pi
, we have

\displaystyle a\ast_{p,qt}(b\ast_{q,t}c)-(a\ast_{p,q}b)\ast_{pq,t}c

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad\stackrel{{\scriptstyle(\ref{eq:6.2})}}{{=}}a\succ_{p,qt}(b\succ_{q,t}c-c\prec_{t,q}b)-(b\succ_{q,t}c-c\prec_{t,q}b)\prec_{qt,p}a

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad\qquad-(a\succ_{p,q}b-b\prec_{q,p}a)\succ_{pq,t}c+c\prec_{t,pq}(a\succ_{p,q}b-b\prec_{q,p}a)

\displaystyle\qquad\quad\stackrel{{\scriptstyle(\ref{eq:5.4})-(\ref{eq:5.6})}}{{=}}(a\prec_{p,q}b)\succ_{pq,t}c+(a\succ_{p,q}b)\succ_{pq,t}c-a\succ_{p,tq}(c\prec_{t,q}b)-b\succ_{q,tp}(c\prec_{t,p}a)

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad\qquad+c\prec_{t,qp}(b\prec_{q,p}a)+c\prec_{t,qp}(b\succ_{q,p}a)-(a\succ_{p,q}b)\succ_{pq,t}c+(b\prec_{q,p}a)\succ_{qp,t}c

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad\qquad+c\prec_{t,pq}(a\succ_{p,q}b)-c\prec_{t,qp}(b\prec_{q,p}a)

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad\quad=(a\prec_{p,q}b)\succ_{pq,t}c-a\succ_{p,tq}(c\prec_{t,q}b)-b\succ_{q,tp}(c\prec_{t,p}a)+c\prec_{t,qp}(b\succ_{q,p}a)

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad\qquad+(b\prec_{q,p}a)\succ_{qp,t}c+c\prec_{t,pq}(a\succ_{p,q}b).

Similarly, we have

\displaystyle b\ast_{q,pt}(a\ast_{p,t}c)-(b\ast_{q,p}a)\ast_{qp,t}c=(b\prec_{q,p}a)\succ_{qp,t}c-b\succ_{q,tp}(c\prec_{t,p}a)

\displaystyle-a\succ_{p,tq}(c\prec_{t,q}b)+c\prec_{t,pq}(a\succ_{p,q}b)+(a\prec_{p,q}b)\succ_{pq,t}c+c\prec_{t,qp}(b\succ_{q,p}a).

(\{A_{\varphi}\}_{\varphi\in\pi},\{\ast_{p,q}\}_{p,q\in\pi})
is a pre-Lie T-algebra.

\displaystyle[R_{p}(a),R_{q}(b)]_{p,q}=R_{pq}([a,R_{q}(b)]_{p,q})+R_{pq}([R_{p}(a),b]_{p,q})+\lambda R_{pq}([a,b]_{p,q}),

(4.8)

for
a\in A_{p}
,
b\in A_{q}
and
p,q\in\pi

(\{A_{\varphi}\}_{\varphi\in\pi},\{[,]_{p,q}\}_{p,q\in\pi},\{R_{\varphi}\}_{\varphi\in\pi},\lambda)

Proposition 4.13
.

Let
(\{A_{\varphi}\}_{\varphi\in\pi},\{[,]_{p,q}\}_{p,q\in\pi},\{R_{\varphi}\}_{\varphi\in\pi})

(\{A_{\varphi}\}_{\varphi\in\pi},\{\ast_{p,q}\}_{p,q\in\pi})

\displaystyle a\ast_{p,q}b=[R_{p}(a),b]_{p,q}.

(4.9)

Proof.

For all

,

,

, we can check the result as follows.

\displaystyle a\ast_{p,qt}(b\ast_{q,t}c)-(a\ast_{p,q}b)\ast_{pq,t}c

\displaystyle\stackrel{{\scriptstyle(\ref{eq:6.15})}}{{=}}

\displaystyle[R_{p}(a),[R_{q}(b),c]_{q,t}]_{p,qt}-[R_{pq}([R_{p}(a),b]_{p,q}),c]_{pq,t}

4.5.
Dendriform T-algebra and Zinbiel T-algebra

Categorical properties of Leibniz algebras is studied by J.-L. Loday in
[
24
]
and considered in this connection a new object-Zinbiel algebra, which is related to the Koszul dual to the category of Leibniz algebras.

Definition 4.14
.

A
Zinbiel T-algebra
is a family of vector spaces
\{A_{\varphi}\}_{\varphi\in\pi}
together with a family of binary operations
\{\star_{p,q}:A_{p}\otimes A_{q}\longrightarrow A_{pq}\}_{p,q\in\pi}
such that

\displaystyle a\star_{p,qt}(b\star_{q,t}c)=(a\star_{p,q}b)\star_{pq,t}c+(b\star_{q,p}a)\star_{qp,t}c

(4.10)

for
a\in A_{p}
,
b\in A_{q}
,
c\in A_{t}
and
p,q,t\in\pi
. We denote it by
(\{A_{\varphi}\}_{\varphi\in\pi},\{\star_{p,q}\}_{p,q\in\pi})
.

Proposition 4.15
.

Let
(\{A_{\varphi}\}_{\varphi\in\pi},\{\prec_{p,q}\}_{p,q\in\pi},\{\succ_{p,q}\}_{p,q\in\pi})
be a commutative dendriform T-algebra in the sense of

\displaystyle a\succ_{p,q}b=b\prec_{q,p}a,

(4.11)

define

\displaystyle a\star_{p,q}b:=a\succ_{p,q}b=b\prec_{q,p}a,

(4.12)

for
a\in A_{p}
,
b\in A_{q}
and
p,q\in\pi
. Then
(\{A_{\varphi}\}_{\varphi\in\pi},\{\star_{p,q}\}_{p,q\in\pi})
is a Zinbiel T-algebra.

Proof.

Since
(\{A_{\varphi}\}_{\varphi\in\pi},\{\prec_{p,q}\}_{p,q\in\pi},\{\succ_{p,q}\}_{p,q\in\pi})
is a dendriform T-algebra, then we have

\displaystyle a\succ_{p,qt}(b\succ_{q,t}c)=(a\prec_{p,q}b+a\succ_{p,q}b)\succ_{pq,t}c.

Hence

\displaystyle a\star_{p,qt}(b\star_{q,t}c)=(a\star_{p,q}b)\star_{pq,t}c+(b\star_{q,p}a)\star_{qp,t}c,

finishing the proof.
∎

Corollary 4.16
.

(1) Let
(A,R)
be a Rota-Baxter T-algebra. Then
(\{A_{\varphi}\}_{\varphi\in\pi},

\{\ast_{p,q}\}_{p,q\in\pi})
is a pre-Lie T-algebra, where

\displaystyle a\ast_{p,q}b:=R_{p}(a)\cdot_{p,q}b-b\cdot_{q,p}R_{p}(a)-\lambda b\cdot_{q,p}a,

(4.13)

for
a\in A_{p}
,
b\in A_{q}
and
p,q\in\pi
.

(2) Let
(A,R)
be a Rota-Baxter T-algebra such that
R_{p}(a)\cdot_{p,q}b=b\cdot_{q,p}R_{p}(a)+\lambda b\cdot_{q,p}a
. Then
(\{A_{\varphi}\}_{\varphi\in\pi},\{\star_{p,q}\}_{p,q\in\pi})
is a Zinbiel T-algebra, where

\displaystyle a\star_{p,q}b:=R_{p}(a)\cdot_{p,q}b

(4.14)

for
a\in A_{p}
,
b\in A_{q}
and
p,q\in\pi
.

Proof.

(1) It follows from Proposition
4.3
and Part (2) in Proposition
2.25
.

(2) It follows from Proposition
4.15
and Part (2) in Proposition
2.25
.
∎

Lemma 4.17
.

Let
(\{A_{\varphi}\}_{\varphi\in\pi},\{\star_{p,q}\}_{p,q\in\pi})
be a Zinbiel T-algebra. Then

\displaystyle a\star_{p,qt}(b\star_{q,t}c)=b\star_{q,pt}(a\star_{p,t}c)

(4.15)

where
a\in A_{p}
,
b\in A_{q}
,
c\in A_{t}
and
p,q,t\in\pi
.

Proof.

By Eq.(
4.10
), for
a\in A_{p}
,
b\in A_{q}
,

, we have

\displaystyle a\star_{p,qt}(b\star_{q,t}c)=(a\star_{p,q}b)\star_{pq,t}c+(b\star_{q,p}a)\star_{qp,t}c,

and

\displaystyle b\star_{q,pt}(a\star_{p,t}c)=(b\star_{q,p}a)\star_{qp,t}c+(a\star_{p,q}b)\star_{pq,t}c.

Hence, Eq.(
4.15
) holds. This completes the proof.
∎

Proposition 4.18
.

Let
(\{A_{\varphi}\}_{\varphi\in\pi},\{\star_{p,q}\}_{p,q\in\pi})
be a Zinbiel T-algebra. Define

\displaystyle a\prec_{p,q}b=b\star_{q,p}a,\quad a\succ_{p,q}b=a\star_{p,q}b,

(4.16)

for
a\in A_{p}
,
b\in A_{q}
and
p,q\in\pi
. Then
(\{A_{\varphi}\}_{\varphi\in\pi},\{\prec_{p,q}\}_{p,q\in\pi},\{\succ_{p,q}\}_{p,q\in\pi})
is a dendriform T-algebra.

Proof.

By Lemma
4.17
,
(a\succ_{p,q}b)\prec_{pq,t}c=a\succ_{p,qt}(b\prec_{q,t}c)
holds. And for all
a\in A_{p}
,
b\in A_{q}
,
c\in A_{t}
, we have

\displaystyle(a\prec_{p,q}b)\prec_{pq,t}c

\displaystyle\stackrel{{\scriptstyle(\ref{eq:6.7})}}{{=}}

\displaystyle(c\star_{t,q}b)\star_{tq,p}a+(b\star_{q,t}c)\star_{qt,p}a

\displaystyle\stackrel{{\scriptstyle(\ref{eq:6.18})}}{{=}}

\displaystyle a\prec_{p,qt}(b\prec_{q,t}c+b\succ_{q,t}c),

and

\displaystyle(a\prec_{p,q}b+a\succ_{p,q}b)\succ_{pq,t}c

\displaystyle\stackrel{{\scriptstyle(\ref{eq:6.18})}}{{=}}

\displaystyle(b\star_{q,p}a)\star_{qp,t}c+(a\star_{p,q}b)\star_{pq,t}c

\displaystyle\stackrel{{\scriptstyle(\ref{eq:6.7})}}{{=}}

\displaystyle a\star_{p,qt}(b\star_{q,t}c)

\displaystyle\stackrel{{\scriptstyle(\ref{eq:6.18})}}{{=}}

\displaystyle a\succ_{p,qt}(b\succ_{q,t}c).

Thus,
(\{A_{\varphi}\}_{\varphi\in\pi},\{\prec_{p,q}\}_{p,q\in\pi},\{\succ_{p,q}\}_{p,q\in\pi})
is a dendriform T-algebra.
∎

4.6.

Pre-Poisson algebra was proposed by Aguiar in

(\{A_{\varphi}\}_{\varphi\in\pi},

is a commutative T-algebra,
(\{A_{\varphi}\}_{\varphi\in\pi},

\{[,]_{p,q}\}_{p,q\in\pi})

\displaystyle[a,b\cdot_{q,t}c]_{p,qt}=[a,b]_{p,q}\cdot_{pq,t}c+b\cdot_{q,pt}[a,c]_{p,t},

(4.18)

for
a\in A_{p}
,
b\in A_{q}
,
c\in A_{t}
and
p,q,t\in\pi
.

Definition 4.21
.

A
pre-Poisson T-algebra
is a triple
(\{A_{\varphi}\}_{\varphi\in\pi},\{\star_{p,q}\}_{p,q\in\pi},\{\ast_{p,q}\}_{p,q\in\pi})
where
(\{A_{\varphi}\}_{\varphi\in\pi},

\{\star_{p,q}\}_{p,q\in\pi})
is a Zinbiel T-algebra,
(\{A_{\varphi}\}_{\varphi\in\pi},\{\ast_{p,q}\}_{p,q\in\pi})

for
a\in A_{p}
,

,

and
