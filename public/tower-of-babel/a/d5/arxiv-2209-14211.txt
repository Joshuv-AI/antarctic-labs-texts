On Gelfand graded commutative rings
Mohamed Aqalmoun

Abstract
. This paper deals with the graded commutative rings in which every homogeneous prime ideal is contained in a unique homogeneous maximal ideal called Gelfand graded ring. The purpose is to establish some topological and algebraic characterizations of these rings, one of which is the algebraic analogue of the Urysohn’s lemma. Finally we look at a special class of those graded rings called pm + graded rings which can be viewed as graded ring with a Gelfand strong property.

Let
G
be a group with identity

be a commutative ring with unit. Then

of

. The elements of

of degree

is called the

in

Let

be a

-graded commutative ring and

be an ideal of

. Then

is called a graded ideal of

. If

is a

-graded ideal of

-graded ring. Indeed

-graded ring. Indeed

A

-graded ideal

-graded prime spectrum or homogeneous prime spectrum of

-graded prime ideals of

. A

-graded ideal

-graded maximal ideal of

-graded ideal of

, then

-graded maximal ideals of

-graded ideal of

For a commutative ring

Theorem 1.1
.

For a commutative ring

(1)

(2)

\mathrm{Max}(R)
is a Zariski retract of

(3)

\mathrm{Spec}(R)

(4)

(5)

2.
Gelfand graded rings

We start this section by the following result which will be relevant in the sequel.

Proposition 2.1
.

Let
R
be a
G
-graded commutative ring and
I
be a
G
-graded ideal of
R
. Let
(I_{\alpha})_{\alpha\in\Lambda}
be a collection of proper
G
-graded ideals of
R
such that
I\subseteq\cup_{\alpha\in\Lambda}I_{\alpha}
with the property that, if
ab\in I
where
a,b\in h(R)
then
a\in\cup_{\alpha\in\Lambda}I_{\alpha}
or
b\in\cup_{\alpha\in\Lambda}I_{\alpha}
. Then there exists a homogeneous prime ideal
Q
of
R
such that
I\subseteq Q
and
Q\cap h(R)\subseteq\cup_{\alpha\in\Lambda}I_{\alpha}
.

Proof.

Note that by hypothesis if
r_{1},\ldots,r_{n}\in h(R)
such that
r_{1}\ldots r_{n}\in I
, then
r_{i}\in\cup_{\alpha\in\Lambda}I_{\alpha}
for some
i
. Now, consider

S=\{r_{1}\ldots r_{n}\ /\ n\geq 1,\ r_{i}\in h(R)-\cup_{\alpha\in\Lambda}I_{\alpha}\}

Observe that
S
is a multiplicatively closed subset of
R
and
S\subseteq h(R)
. Moreover
I\cap S=\emptyset
. By Zorn’s Lemma (see
[
8
, Lemma
4.7
]
), there exists a homogeneous prime ideal
Q
of
R
such that
I\subseteq Q
and
Q\cap S=\emptyset
. If
r\in Q\cap h(R)
, then
r\not\in S
, that is
r\in\cup_{\alpha\in\Lambda}I_{\alpha}
. Thus
Q\cap h(R)\subseteq\cup_{\alpha\in\Lambda}I_{\alpha}
.
∎

Let

The following Theorem is a topological characterization of Gelfand graded ring it terms of retraction.

Theorem 2.6
.

Let
R
be a
G
-graded commutative ring. The following statements are equivalent.

(1)

R
is a Gelfand
G
-graded ring.

(2)

G\mathrm{Max}(R)
is a Zariski retract of
G\mathrm{Spec}(R)
.

Proof.

(1)\Rightarrow(2)
. Let
\varphi:G\mathrm{Spec}(R)\to G\mathrm{Max}(R)
where
\varphi(P)
is the unique
G
-graded maximal ideal containing
P
. Let
F
be a closed subset of
G\mathrm{Max}(R)
. It is easy to see that
F
is the set of all
G
-graded maximal ideals of
R
containing
J:=\cap_{M\in F}M
. We must show that
\varphi^{-1}(F)
is a closed subset of
G\mathrm{Spec}R
. It is enough to show that
\varphi^{-1}(F)=V_{G}(I)
where
I=\cap_{\varphi(P)\in F}P
.

Let
P\in\varphi^{-1}(F)
, then
\varphi(P)\in F
, thus
I\subseteq P
that is
P\in V_{G}(I)
.

Let
Q\in V_{G}(I)
that is

. In particular
I\subseteq Q\cap(\cup_{M\in F}M)
. Now, let

with

and

. We must show that
x\in Q\cap(\cup_{M\in F}M)
.

First case;
y\not\in Q
. In this case

since
I\subseteq Q
, and
x\in Q
since
Q
is homogeneous prime. But
y\not\in I=\cap_{\varphi(P)\in F}P
implies that

for some

with
\varphi(P_{0})\in F
. Since

and

, we get

. Thus

. Therefore
x\in Q\cap(\cup_{M\in F}M)
.

Second case;

y\not\in\cup_{M\in F}M
, that is
y\not\in M
for all
M\in F
. If
P\in\varphi^{-1}(F)
, then
P\subseteq\varphi(P)\in F
so

. We see that

, hence
xy\in P
for all
P\in\varphi^{-1}(F)
, so
x\in P
for all
P\in\varphi^{-1}(F)
. Therefore
x\in I
, thus
x\in Q\cap(\cup_{M\in F}M)
.

Now,
I\subseteq\cup_{M\in F}(Q\cap M)
, by Lemma
2.1
, there exists homogeneous prime ideal
Q^{\prime}
of
R
such that
I\subseteq Q^{\prime}
and

. On one hand

, implies that
Q^{\prime}\subseteq Q
. On other hand
Q^{\prime}\cap h(R)\subseteq\cup_{M\in F}M
and
F
is the set of all graded maximal ideals of
R
containing
J
, by Lemma
2.3
,
Q^{\prime}\subseteq M
for some
M\in F
. It follows that
\varphi(Q^{\prime})=M
, therefore
\varphi(Q)=\varphi(Q^{\prime})\in F
, that is
Q\in\varphi^{-1}(F)
.

(2)\Rightarrow(1)
. Assume that
G\mathrm{Max}(R)
is a retract of
G\mathrm{Spec}(R)
. Let
\phi:G\mathrm{Spec}(R)\to G\mathrm{Max}(R)
be any retraction. Let
P\in G\mathrm{Spec}(R)
and

containing
P
. Since
\phi(P)
is a
G
-graded maximal ideal of
R
,
V_{G}(\phi(P))=\{\phi(P)\}
is a closed subset of
G\mathrm{Max}(R)
. Thus
\phi^{-1}(\{\phi(P)\})
is a closed subset of
G\mathrm{Spec}(R)
. It follows that
M\in\phi^{-1}(\{\phi(P)\})
since
P\in\phi^{-1}(\{\phi(P)\})
and closed subset are closed under specialization. Therefore

, hence
\phi(P)
is the unique

-graded maximal ideal of

.
∎

Let

(1)

(2)

For a topological space

In the next result, we give an algebraic version of the Urysohn lemma. To do, we fix some notations and conventions. Let
