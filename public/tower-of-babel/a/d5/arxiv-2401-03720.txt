CKM γ \gamma measurements at LHCb
*

Abstract
The tree-level measurement of the CKM γ \gamma angle is a crucial test of C ​ P {C\!P} violation in the Standard Model (SM). Discrepancies between direct measurements (tree-level decays) and indirect measurements (loop decays) could indicate physics beyond the SM. Recent measurements using decays such as B 0 → D ​ K ∗ 0 , B ± → [ D ​ π 0 / γ ] D ∗ ​ h ± B^{0}\rightarrow DK^{*0},B^{\pm}\rightarrow[D\pi^{0}/\gamma]_{D^{*}}h^{\pm} (where D D decays to K S 0 ​ h + ​ h − {{K}^{0}_{\mathrm{S}}}h^{+}h^{-} with h = K , π h=K,\pi ) and B ± → [ h ′ ⁣ + ​ h ′ ⁣ − ​ π + ​ π − ] D ​ h ± B^{\pm}\rightarrow[h^{\prime+}h^{\prime-}\pi^{+}\pi^{-}]_{D}h^{\pm} are presented. Additionally, the combination of previous γ \gamma measurements at LHCb , excluding the aforementioned results, is also presented. The LHCb result, with a precision of ( 63.8 − 3.7 + 3.5 ) ∘ (63.8^{+3.5}_{-3.7})^{\circ} establishes itself as a dominant measurement in this field.

Direct measurements of

Therefore, a discrepancy between direct and indirect measurements would be a clear indication of new physics.

The most powerful method for determining

D

{C\!P}

D

BPGGSZ utilises

{{K}^{0}_{\mathrm{S}}}h^{+}h^{-}

The first measurement of

The analysis is performed across DP bins, which are optimized for sensitivity to local
