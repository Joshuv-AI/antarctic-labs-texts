Necessary conditions for existence of Γ n \Gamma_{n} -contractions and examples of Γ 3 \Gamma_{3} -contractions
Shubhankar Mandal and Avijit Pal

Abstract
. The fundamental result of B. Sz. Nazy states that every contraction has a coisometric extension and a unitary dilation. The isometric dilation of a contraction on a Hilbert space motivated whether this theory can be extended sensibly to families of operators. It is natural to ask whether this idea can be generalized, where the contraction T T is substituted by a commuting n n -tuples of operators ( S 1 , ⋯ , S n ) (S_{1},\cdots,S_{n}) acting on some Hilbert space having Γ n \Gamma_{n} as a spectral set. We derive the necessary conditions for the existence of a Γ n \Gamma_{n} -isometric dilation for Γ n \Gamma_{n} -contractions. Also we discuss an example of a Γ 3 \Gamma_{3} -contraction ( S 1 , S 2 , S 3 ) (S_{1},S_{2},S_{3}) acting on some Hilbert space ℋ , \mathcal{H}, which has a Γ 3 \Gamma_{3} -isometric dilation, but it fails to satisfy the following condition: E 1 ∗ ​ E 1 − E 1 ​ E 1 ∗ = E 2 ∗ ​ E 2 − E 2 ​ E 2 ∗ , E_{1}^{*}E_{1}-E_{1}E_{1}^{*}=E_{2}^{*}E_{2}-E_{2}E_{2}^{*}, where E 1 E_{1} and E 2 E_{2} are the fundamental operators of ( S 1 , S 2 , S 3 ) , (S_{1},S_{2},S_{3}), ( S 1 , S 2 ) (S_{1},S_{2}) is a pair of commuting contractions and S 3 S_{3} is a partial isometry. Thus, the set of sufficient conditions for the existence of a Γ 3 \Gamma_{3} -isometric dilation breaks down, in general, to be necessary, even when the Γ 3 \Gamma_{3} -contraction ( S 1 , S 2 , S 3 ) (S_{1},S_{2},S_{3}) has the special structure as described above.

Therefore by using Theorem (
3.3
) we conclude that the triple

\left(S_{1},S_{2},S_{3}\right)
is
\Gamma_{3}
-contraction and has a
\Gamma_{3}
-isometric dilation. Since
T_{z}
is an isometry, one can easily verify that
S_{3}
is a partial isometry. First we will compute the defect operator for
S_{3}:

D^{2}_{S_{3}}=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}I_{H^{2}}&0\\
0&I_{H^{2}}\end{smallmatrix}\right)-\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&0\\
T_{z}&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)^{*}\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&0\\
T_{z}&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&0\\
0&I_{H^{2}}\end{smallmatrix}\right)=D_{S_{3}}.

Let us consider

(A_{1},A_{2})=(\frac{I_{H^{2}}+T_{z}}{3},\frac{T_{z}}{3}).

Notice that

\displaystyle S_{1}-S_{2}^{*}S_{3}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{3}\left(\begin{smallmatrix}I_{H^{2}}+T_{z}&0\\
I_{H^{2}}&I_{H^{2}}+T_{z}\end{smallmatrix}\right)-\frac{1}{3}\left(\begin{smallmatrix}T_{z}&0\\
I_{H^{2}}+T_{z}&T_{z}\end{smallmatrix}\right)^{*}\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&0\\
T_{z}&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{3}\left(\begin{smallmatrix}I_{H^{2}}+T_{z}&0\\
I_{H^{2}}&I_{H^{2}}+T_{z}\end{smallmatrix}\right)-\frac{1}{3}\left(\begin{smallmatrix}I_{H^{2}}+T_{z}&0\\
I_{H^{2}}&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{3}\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&0\\
0&I_{H^{2}}+T_{z}\end{smallmatrix}\right)=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&0\\
0&I_{H^{2}}\end{smallmatrix}\right)\frac{1}{3}\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&0\\
0&I_{H^{2}}+T_{z}\end{smallmatrix}\right)\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&0\\
0&I_{H^{2}}\end{smallmatrix}\right)=D_{S_{3}}A_{1}D_{S_{3}}

and

\displaystyle S_{2}-S_{1}^{*}S_{3}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{3}\left(\begin{smallmatrix}T_{z}&0\\
I_{H^{2}}+T_{z}&T_{z}\end{smallmatrix}\right)-\frac{1}{3}\left(\begin{smallmatrix}I_{H^{2}}+T_{z}&0\\
I_{H^{2}}&I_{H^{2}}+T_{z}\end{smallmatrix}\right)^{*}\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&0\\
T_{z}&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{3}\left(\begin{smallmatrix}T_{z}&0\\
I_{H^{2}}+T_{z}&T_{z}\end{smallmatrix}\right)-\frac{1}{3}\left(\begin{smallmatrix}T_{z}&0\\
I_{H^{2}}+T_{z}&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{3}\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&0\\
0&T_{z}\end{smallmatrix}\right)=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&0\\
0&I_{H^{2}}\end{smallmatrix}\right)\frac{1}{3}\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&0\\
0&T_{z}\end{smallmatrix}\right)\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&0\\
0&I_{H^{2}}\end{smallmatrix}\right)=D_{S_{3}}A_{2}D_{S_{3}}

which implies that
(A_{1},A_{2})
are same as the fundamental operators
(E_{1},E_{2})
of
\left(S_{1},S_{2},S_{3}\right).
Notice that

A_{1}^{*}A_{1}-A_{1}A_{1}^{*}=\frac{1}{9}(I-T_{z}T_{z}^{*})=A_{2}^{*}A_{2}-A_{2}A_{2}^{*}.

One can easily verify that the conditions
(1)
and
(2)
of Corollary (
3.5
) are satisfied. To verify the condition
(3)
of Corollary (
3.5
), we need to show that
D_{1}^{*}D_{1}-D_{1}D_{1}^{*}=D_{2}^{*}D_{2}-D_{2}D_{2}^{*}.
It is also easy to check that
\ker S_{3}=\{0\}\oplus H^{2}
and

(S_{1},S_{2})_{|_{\ker S_{3}}}=(D_{1},D_{2})=(\frac{I_{H^{2}}+T_{z}}{3},\frac{T_{z}}{3}),
which implies

D_{1}^{*}D_{1}-D_{1}D_{1}^{*}=D_{2}^{*}D_{2}-D_{2}D_{2}^{*}=\frac{1}{9}(I-T_{z}T_{z}^{*}).

Thus, the condition
(3)
of Corollary (
3.5
) is satisfied.

The subsequent example of a
\Gamma_{3}
-contraction has a
\Gamma_{3}
-isometric dilation for which condition
(3)
in Corollary (
3.5
) fails, that is,

E_{1}^{*}E_{1}-E_{1}E_{1}^{*}\neq E_{2}^{*}E_{2}-E_{2}E_{2}^{*}.

In other words, we can conclude that the set of sufficient conditions for existence of a
\Gamma_{3}
-isometric dilation in Theorem (
2.8
) breaks down in general to be necessary, even when the
\Gamma_{3}
-contraction
(S_{1},S_{2},S_{3})
has the following forms:

(1)

(S_{1},S_{2})
is a
2
-tuple of commuting contractions on some Hilbert space
\mathcal{H},
and

(2)

S_{3}
is a partial isometry on
\mathcal{H}.

Example 2
.

Let us consider the following pair of contractions on
H^{2}\oplus H^{2}\oplus H^{2}
:

(T_{1},T_{2})=\left(\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&0&I_{H^{2}}\\
0&0&0\\
I_{H^{2}}&0&0\end{smallmatrix}\right),\left(\begin{smallmatrix}T_{z}&0&0\\
0&0&0\\
0&0&T_{z}\end{smallmatrix}\right)\right),

where
T_{z}
is a multiplication operator on
H^{2}.
Let

S_{1}=\frac{1}{3}(T_{1}+T_{2}+V_{3})=\frac{1}{3}\left(\begin{smallmatrix}I_{H^{2}}+T_{z}&0&I_{H^{2}}\\
0&T_{z}&0\\
I_{H^{2}}&0&I_{H^{2}}+T_{z}\end{smallmatrix}\right),S_{2}=\frac{1}{3}(T_{1}T_{2}+T_{2}V_{3}+T_{1}V_{3})=\frac{1}{3}\left(\begin{smallmatrix}T_{z}&0&I_{H^{2}}+T_{z}\\
0&0&0\\
I_{H^{2}}+T_{z}&0&T_{z}\end{smallmatrix}\right)

and
S_{3}=T_{1}T_{2}V_{3}=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&0&T_{z}\\
0&0&0\\
T_{z}&0&0\end{smallmatrix}\right),
where
V_{3}=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}I_{H^{2}}&0&0\\
0&T_{z}&0\\
0&0&I_{H^{2}}\end{smallmatrix}\right).
Therefore by Theorem (
3.3
) the triple

\left(S_{1},S_{2},S_{3}\right)
is
\Gamma_{3}
-contraction which has a
\Gamma_{3}
-isometric dilation. As
T_{z}
is an isometry, one can easily check that
S_{3}
is a partial isometry. Therefore we can apply the conclusion of the Proposition (
3.4
) for this triple. The first step for computing the fundamental operators
E_{1}
and
E_{2},
is to calculate the defect operator for
S_{3}:

D^{2}_{S_{3}}=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}I_{H^{2}}&0&0\\
0&I_{H^{2}}&0\\
0&0&I_{H^{2}}\end{smallmatrix}\right)-\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&0&T_{z}\\
0&0&0\\
T_{z}&0&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)^{*}\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&0&T_{z}\\
0&0&0\\
T_{z}&0&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&0&0\\
0&I_{H^{2}}&0\\
0&0&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)=D_{S_{3}}.

Set

(A_{1},A_{2})=(\frac{T_{z}}{3},0).

The following computations

\displaystyle S_{1}-S_{2}^{*}S_{3}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{3}\left(\begin{smallmatrix}I_{H^{2}}+T_{z}&0&I_{H^{2}}\\
0&T_{z}&0\\
I_{H^{2}}&0&I_{H^{2}}+T_{z}\end{smallmatrix}\right)-\frac{1}{3}\left(\begin{smallmatrix}T_{z}&0&I_{H^{2}}+T_{z}\\
0&0&0\\
I_{H^{2}}+T_{z}&0&T_{z}\end{smallmatrix}\right)^{*}\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&0&T_{z}\\
0&0&0\\
T_{z}&0&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{3}\left(\begin{smallmatrix}I_{H^{2}}+T_{z}&0&I_{H^{2}}\\
0&T_{z}&0\\
I_{H^{2}}&0&I_{H^{2}}+T_{z}\end{smallmatrix}\right)-\frac{1}{3}\left(\begin{smallmatrix}I_{H^{2}}+T_{z}&0&I_{H^{2}}\\
0&0&0\\
I_{H^{2}}&0&I_{H^{2}}+T_{z}\end{smallmatrix}\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{3}\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&0&0\\
0&T_{z}&0\\
0&0&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)

\displaystyle=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&0&0\\
0&I_{H^{2}}&0\\
0&0&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)\frac{1}{3}\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&0&0\\
0&T_{z}&0\\
0&0&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&0&0\\
0&I_{H^{2}}&0\\
0&0&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)=D_{S_{3}}A_{1}D_{S_{3}}

and

\displaystyle S_{2}-S_{1}^{*}S_{3}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{3}\left(\begin{smallmatrix}T_{z}&0&I_{H^{2}}+T_{z}\\
0&0&0\\
I_{H^{2}}+T_{z}&0&T_{z}\end{smallmatrix}\right)-\frac{1}{3}\left(\begin{smallmatrix}I_{H^{2}}+T_{z}&0&I_{H^{2}}\\
0&T_{z}&0\\
I_{H^{2}}&0&I_{H^{2}}+T_{z}\end{smallmatrix}\right)^{*}\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&0&T_{z}\\
0&0&0\\
T_{z}&0&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{3}\left(\begin{smallmatrix}T_{z}&0&I_{H^{2}}+T_{z}\\
0&0&0\\
I_{H^{2}}+T_{z}&0&T_{z}\end{smallmatrix}\right)-\frac{1}{3}\left(\begin{smallmatrix}T_{z}&0&I_{H^{2}}+T_{z}\\
0&0&0\\
I_{H^{2}}+T_{z}&0&T_{z}\end{smallmatrix}\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{3}\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&0&0\\
0&0&0\\
0&0&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)=D_{S_{3}}A_{2}D_{S_{3}}

implies that
(A_{1},A_{2})
are the same as the fundamental operators
(E_{1},E_{2})
of
\left(S_{1},S_{2},S_{3}\right).
Note that

A_{1}^{*}A_{1}-A_{1}A_{1}^{*}=\frac{1}{9}(I-T_{z}T_{z}^{*})\neq 0~~{\rm{while~~}}A_{2}^{*}A_{2}-A_{2}A_{2}^{*}=0.

One can easily verify that
\ker S_{3}=\{0\}\oplus H^{2}\oplus\{0\}
and
(S_{1},S_{2})_{|_{\ker S_{3}}}=(D_{1},D_{2})=(\frac{T_{z}}{3},0).
Also, we have

D_{1}^{*}D_{1}-D_{1}D_{1}^{*}=\frac{1}{9}(I-T_{z}T_{z}^{*})\neq 0~~{\rm{while~~}}D_{2}^{*}D_{2}-D_{2}D_{2}^{*}=0,

which shows that the condition
(3)
of Corollary (
3.5
) is disobeyed.

Remark 3.6
.

In Example
2,
we notice that if
(S_{1},S_{2})
is a
2
-tuple of commuting contractions on
H^{2},
and
S_{3}
is a partial isometry on
H^{2},
then we have

D_{1}^{*}D_{1}-D_{1}D_{1}^{*}\neq D_{2}^{*}D_{2}-D_{2}D_{2}^{*}.

Therefore, it follows from condition
(3)
of Corollary (
3.5
) that

E_{1}^{*}E_{1}-E_{1}E_{1}^{*}\neq E_{2}^{*}E_{2}-E_{2}E_{2}^{*}

This shows that the
\Gamma_{3}
-contraction
(S_{1},S_{2},S_{3})
does have
\Gamma_{3}
-isometric dilation, but it fails to satisfy the following condition:

E_{1}^{*}E_{1}-E_{1}E_{1}^{*}\neq E_{2}^{*}E_{2}-E_{2}E_{2}^{*}.

However, as has been mentioned, Example
2
above appears that the condition
E_{1}^{*}E_{1}-E_{1}E_{1}^{*}\neq E_{2}^{*}E_{2}-E_{2}E_{2}^{*}
is not necessary for existing of a
\Gamma_{3}
-isometric dilation. Still now we are not able to locate an example of
\Gamma_{n}
-contraction for
n\geq 3,
which fails to satisfy one of the necessary conditions. Thus, the existence of rational dilation for
\Gamma_{n}
-contraction, for
n\geq 3
is still an open question.
