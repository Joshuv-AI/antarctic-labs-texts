Theorem 3.1

3.1

Then,

Proof.

For every

, we have

\displaystyle Sf

\displaystyle=\mathcal{T}f+\xi f

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\alpha_{k}\left\langle f,e_{k}\right\rangle e_{k}+\xi f

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\alpha_{k}\left\langle f,e_{k}\right\rangle e_{k}+\xi\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\left\langle f,e_{k}\right\rangle e_{k}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\left(\alpha_{k}+\xi\right)\left\langle f,e_{k}\right\rangle e_{k}.

Consequently,

\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\left\langle f,f_{k}\right\rangle\left\langle f_{k},f\right\rangle

\displaystyle=\langle Sf,f\rangle

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\left(\alpha_{k}+\xi\right)\left\langle f,e_{k}\right\rangle\left\langle e_{k},f\right\rangle

\displaystyle\geq\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\left(\inf_{k}\alpha_{k}+\xi\right)\left\langle f,e_{k}\right\rangle\left\langle e_{k},f\right\rangle

\displaystyle\geq\alpha\left\langle f,f\right\rangle.

Where
\alpha=\xi+\inf_{k}\alpha_{k}>0.

, we obtain the frame
\left\{f_{k}\right\}_{k\in\sigma^{c}}\cup\left\{\tilde{f}_{k}\right\}_{k\in\sigma}
. According to the definition
3.2
, the frame operators for
\left\{f_{k}\right\}_{k-1}^{\infty}
and
\left\{\tilde{f}_{k}\right\}_{k=1}^{\infty}
can be written as

\displaystyle S_{F}f

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\left\langle f,f_{k}\right\rangle f_{k}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k\in\sigma}\left\langle f,f_{k}\right\rangle f_{k}+\sum_{k\in\sigma^{c}}\left\langle f,f_{k}\right\rangle f_{k}

\displaystyle=\mathcal{K}_{1}f+f

and

\displaystyle S_{G}f

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\left\langle f,\tilde{f}_{k}\right\rangle\tilde{f}_{k}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k\in\sigma^{c}}\left\langle f,\tilde{f}_{k}\right\rangle\tilde{f}_{k}+\sum_{k\in\sigma}\left\langle f,\tilde{f}_{k}\right\rangle\tilde{f}_{k}

\displaystyle=\mathcal{K}_{2}f+f.

Make clear the frame operator of
\left\{f_{k}\right\}_{k\in\sigma^{c}}\cup\left\{\tilde{f}_{k}\right\}_{k\in\sigma}
by
S_{F+G}
. Then for
f\in\mathcal{H}
,

\displaystyle S_{F+G}f

\displaystyle=\sum_{k\in\sigma^{c}}\left\langle f,f_{k}\right\rangle f_{k}+\sum_{k\in\sigma}\left\langle f,\tilde{f}_{k}\right\rangle\tilde{f}_{k}

\displaystyle=\mathcal{K}_{1}f+\mathcal{K}_{2}f+f

\displaystyle=\left(\mathcal{K}_{1}+\mathcal{K}_{2}\right)f+f.

Thus,

S_{F+G}=\mathcal{K}_{1}+\mathcal{K}_{2}.

Since
\mathcal{K}_{1}
and
\mathcal{K}_{2}
are compact operators,
S_{F+G}
is a compact operator, which is absurd.
∎
