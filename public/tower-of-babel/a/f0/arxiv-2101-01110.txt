Quadratic relations of the deformed W W -superalgebra 𝒲 q , t ​ ( A ​ ( M , N ) ) {\cal W}_{q,t}\bigl(A(M,N)\bigr)
Takeo KOJIMA

Abstract
We find the free field construction of the basic W W -current and screening currents for the deformed W W -superalgebra 𝒲 q , t ​ ( A ​ ( M , N ) ) {\cal W}_{q,t}\bigl(A(M,N)\bigr) associated with Lie superalgebra of type A ⁡ ( M , N ) A(M,N) . Using this free field construction, we introduce the higher W W -currents and obtain a closed set of quadratic relations among them. These relations are independent of the choice of Dynkin-diagrams for the Lie superalgebra A ⁡ ( M , N ) A(M,N) , though the screening currents are not. This allows us to define 𝒲 q , t ​ ( A ​ ( M , N ) ) {\cal W}_{q,t}\bigl(A(M,N)\bigr) by generators and relations.
