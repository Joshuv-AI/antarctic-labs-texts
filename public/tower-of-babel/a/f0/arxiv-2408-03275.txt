Topology of 𝔾 m \mathbb{G}_{m} -actions and applications to the moduli of Higgs bundles
Andres Fernandez Herrero, Siqing Zhang

Abstract
We explain some results concerning the topology of varieties and stacks equipped with an action of the multiplicative group 𝔾 m \mathbb{G}_{m} . We apply these techniques to the moduli of Higgs bundles. Our main application is to upgrade the cohomological Nonabelian Hodge Theorem in positive characteristic [ dCGZ23 , HZ23b ] to an isomorphism of cohomology rings compatible with cup product.

Introduction

Let
C
be a smooth projective connected curve of genus
g\geq 2
over an algebraically closed field
k
of positive characteristic
p>0
. Given a connected reductive group
G
over
k
, the Nonabelian Hodge Theorem in
[
Gro16
,
CZ15
]
relates the de Rham moduli stack
\mathcal{M}_{dR,G}(C)
of
G
-bundles with connection on the curve
C
and the Dolbeault moduli stack
\mathcal{M}_{Dol,G}(C^{\prime})
of
G
-Higgs bundles on the Frobenius twist
C^{\prime}
. In
[
dCGZ23
,
HZ23b
]
, this is refined to be compatible with semistability. In particular, for any given fixed degree
d\in\pi_{1}(G)
, there is a version of the Nonabelian Hodge Theorem relating the de Rham moduli space
\mathbb{M}_{dR,G}^{pd}(C)
of semistable
G
-connections of degree
pd
on
C
and the Dolbeault moduli space
\mathbb{M}_{Dol,G}^{d}(C^{\prime})
of semistable
G
-Higgs bundles of degree
d
on
C^{\prime}
.

The Dolbeault (resp. de Rham) moduli space admits a Hitchin (resp. de Rham-Hitchin) morphism to the Hitchin base
A_{G,\omega_{C^{\prime}}}
.
Applying the Decomposition Theorem
[
BBDG18
]
to the (de Rham-)Hitchin morphism, we obtain an isomorphism between the intersection cohomology groups of the Dolbeault and de Rham moduli spaces
[
dCGZ23
,
HZ23b
]
.
However, for the ordinary cohomology rings of these moduli spaces, the Decomposition Theorem argument no longer applies, and
loc.cit.
only have results locally over
A_{G,\omega_{C^{\prime}}}
, relating certain cohomology sheaves.
Our main contribution in this paper is to complete the study of the Nonabelian Hodge Theorem at the level of cohomology rings by establishing the following global result.

Main Theorem

(=
Theorem

3.5
)
.

Let
k
be an algebraically closed field of characteristic
p>0
. Let

be a connected reductive group over

We use the proof of the main theorem as an opportunity to discuss some general results on the topology of stacks and schemes equipped with actions of the multiplicative group
\mathbb{G}_{m}
(see

2
). We make further use of these results in three additional applications:

1.1
Notation and conventions on derived categories of sheaves

In this paper, we work over an algebraically closed ground field
k
. All stacks and schemes are assumed to live over
k
. We fix once and for all a prime number
\ell
distinct from the characteristic of our ground field
k
. For any quasi-separated algebraic stack
\mathcal{X}
locally of finite type over
k
, we use the following:

Notation 1.1

(Derived category of
\ell
-adic sheaves)
.

Let
D^{b}_{c}(\mathcal{X},\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell})
denote the bounded derived category of constructible
\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}
-complexes on
\mathcal{X}
(see
[
LO08
, Rem. 3.21]
). We denote by
\Sh_{c}(\mathcal{X},\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell})
the abelian category of constructible
\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}
-sheaves on
\mathcal{X}
; this is the heart of the standard
t
-structure on
D^{b}_{c}(\mathcal{X},\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell})
.

Given an object in
D^{b}_{c}(\mathcal{X},\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell})
, we denote by
R\Gamma(\mathcal{X},\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell})
the complex of derived global sections, which is an object in the bounded-below derived category
D^{+}(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell})
of
\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}
-vector spaces.

If the ground field
k
is
\mathbb{C}
, then we use the following:

Notation 1.2

(Derived category of mixed Hodge modules)
.

Let
D_{H}^{+}(\mathcal{X})
denote the bounded-below derived category of mixed Hodge modules constructed in
[
Tub24
]
. We denote by
D_{H,c}^{+}(\mathcal{X})
the bounded-below derived category of cohomologically constructible mixed Hodge modules.

Pushing forward under the structure morphism
\mathcal{X}\to\operatorname{Spec}(\mathbb{C})
preserves constructibility of mixed Hodge modules. For any element
E\in D^{+}_{H,c}(\mathcal{X})
, we denote by
R\Gamma(\mathcal{X},E)
the corresponding pushforward in
D^{+}_{H,c}(\operatorname{Spec}(\mathbb{C}))
. There is a notion of weights for objects of
D_{H,c}(\operatorname{Spec}(\mathbb{C}))
(cf.
[
Tub24
, Defn. 3.17]
), and therefore there is a notion of purity for complexes. We say that a complex in
D_{H,c}(\operatorname{Spec}(\mathbb{C}))
is pure if it is pure of weight
0
.

Some general results

2.1
Cohomology of equivariant sheaves

In this subsection, we work in the following context.

Context 2.1
.

Let
i:\mathcal{Z}\hookrightarrow\mathcal{X}
be a closed immersion of quasi-separated algebraic stacks that are locally of finite type over
k
. Suppose that we have a morphism
H:\mathbb{A}^{1}\times\mathcal{X}\to\mathcal{X}
such that
H|_{1\times\mathcal{X}}:\mathcal{X}\to\mathcal{X}
is the identity and both restrictions
H|_{0\times\mathcal{X}}:\mathcal{X}\to\mathcal{X}
and
H|_{\mathbb{A}^{1}\times\mathcal{Z}}:\mathbb{A}^{1}\times\mathcal{Z}\to\mathcal{X}
factor through
\mathcal{Z}\subset\mathcal{X}
.

Proposition 2.2
.

In
Context

2.1
, let
E\in D^{b}_{c}(\mathcal{X},\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell})
be a complex of sheaves satisfying
H^{*}E\cong p_{2}^{*}E
, where
p_{2}:\mathbb{A}^{1}\times\mathcal{X}\to\mathcal{X}
denotes the second projection. Then, the natural morphism of derived global sections
i^{*}:R\Gamma(\mathcal{X},E)\to R\Gamma(\mathcal{Z},i^{*}E)
is an isomorphism in the derived category of
\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}
-vector spaces.

Proof.

This is known in the context of varieties (see
[
dCMM18
, Lem. 4.2]
), and the same proof applies verbatim in the context of algebraic stacks.
∎

Proposition 2.3
.

Let
A
be a separated scheme locally of finite type over
k
equipped with an action of
\mathbb{G}_{m}
. Suppose that the action morphism
a:\mathbb{G}_{m}\times A\to A
extends to a morphism
\widetilde{a}:\mathbb{A}^{1}\times A\to A
. Let
i:A^{\mathbb{G}_{m}}\hookrightarrow A
denote the closed immersion from the subscheme of fixed points. Then, given any
\mathbb{G}_{m}
-equivariant constructible sheaf
F
in
\Sh_{c}(A,\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell})
, we have a canonical identification
R\Gamma(A,F)\cong R\Gamma(A^{\mathbb{G}_{m}},i^{*}F)
. In particular, if
A^{\mathbb{G}_{m}}
has dimension
0
, then
\Hcoh^{j}(A,F)=0
for all
j>0
.

Proof.

The isomorphism
R\Gamma(A,F)\cong R\Gamma(A^{\mathbb{G}_{m}},i^{*}F)
follows from
[
Kha23
, Prop. A.1]
. If
A^{\mathbb{G}_{m}}
has dimension
0
, then for any
j>0
we get
\Hcoh^{j}(A,F)=\Hcoh^{j}(A^{\mathbb{G}_{m}},i^{*}F)=0
, where the last equality follows from the vanishing theorems in
[
APG+73
, Exp. X, §4]
.
∎

Note that
Proposition

2.3
does not follow from
Proposition

2.2
since the
\mathbb{G}_{m}
-equivariant sheaf
F
may not satisfy
\widetilde{a}^{*}F\cong p_{2}^{*}F
.
Indeed, in the example where
\widetilde{a}
is the contracting action of
\mathbb{A}^{1}
on
\mathbb{A}^{n}
, the condition
\widetilde{a}^{*}F\cong p_{2}^{*}F
would force
F
to be constant.

If the ground field
k
is
\mathbb{C}
, then there is a version of
Proposition

2.2
in the context of mixed Hodge modules.

Proposition 2.4
.

In
Context

2.1
, suppose that the ground field is
\mathbb{C}
, and let
E
be an object in
D^{+}_{H,c}(\mathcal{X})
satisfying
H^{*}E\cong p^{*}_{2}E
. Then, the natural morphism
i^{*}:R\Gamma(\mathcal{X},E)\to R\Gamma(\mathcal{Z},i^{*}E)
is an isomorphism in
D_{H,c}^{+}(\operatorname{Spec}(\mathbb{C}))
.

Proof.

There is a forgetful functor
rat:D_{H,c}^{+}(\operatorname{Spec}(\mathbb{C}))\to D(\mathbb{Q})
to the derived category of
\mathbb{Q}
-vector spaces (where we think of the latter as the ind-completion of the bounded constructible derived category on the point). The functor
rat
is conservative, and hence it suffices to check that
i^{*}:R\Gamma(\mathcal{X},E)\to R\Gamma(\mathcal{X},i^{*}E)
is an isomorphism after applying
rat
. This can be proven by the same argument as in
Proposition

2.2
.
∎

2.2
Connectedness and fixed points

Definition 2.5

(
[
HZ23a
, Defn. B1]
)
.

Let
Y
be a separated scheme locally of finite type over
k
. We say that an action of
\mathbb{G}_{m}
on
Y
is contracting if for any discrete valuation ring
R
and morphism
f:Spec(R)\to Y
, there exists a
\mathbb{G}_{m}
-equivariant morphism
\widetilde{f}:\mathbb{A}^{1}_{R}\to Y
such that
\widetilde{f}(1)=f
.

Let
Y
be separated scheme locally of finite type over
k
which is equipped with a
\mathbb{G}_{m}
-action. Following
[
Dri15
]
, we define the functor
Y^{+}
from
k
-schemes to sets that sends
T\to S
to the set of
\mathbb{G}_{m}
-equivariant morphism of
T
-schemes
\mathbb{A}^{1}_{T}\to Y_{T}
. The functor
Y^{+}
is represented by an algebraic space locally of finite type over
k

[
HL22
, Prop. 1.4.1]
. There are natural morphisms
\ev_{0}:Y^{+}\to Y
and
\ev_{1}:Y^{+}\to Y
defined by evaluating at
0\in\mathbb{A}^{1}
and
1\in\mathbb{A}^{1}
respectively. The morphism
\ev_{1}:Y^{+}\to Y
is a monomorphism of finite type, and so it follows that
Y^{+}
is also a separated scheme locally of finite type over
k

[
Sta24
,
Tag 03XX
]
. The morphism
\ev_{0}:Y^{+}\to Y
factors through the closed subscheme
Y^{\mathbb{G}_{m}}\subset Y
of
\mathbb{G}_{m}
-fixed points (denote by
Y^{0}
in
[
HL22
, Prop. 1.4.1]
). In terms of the morphism
\ev_{1}:Y^{+}\to Y
, we have that the
\mathbb{G}_{m}
-action is contracting if and only if
\ev_{1}:Y^{+}\to Y
is a surjective closed immersion. In particular, if
Y
is reduced and the
\mathbb{G}_{m}
-action is contracting, then
Y^{+}=Y
.

Proposition 2.6
.

Let
f:X\to Y
be a proper
\mathbb{G}_{m}
-equivariant morphism of separated schemes that are locally of finite type
k
. Suppose that the
\mathbb{G}_{m}
-action on
Y
is contracting. Then,
X
is connected if and only if
X\times_{Y}Y^{\mathbb{G}_{m}}
is connected.

Proof.

By the discussion above, up to replacing
Y
with its reduced subscheme, we may assume that the action
a:\mathbb{G}_{m}\times Y\to Y
extends to a morphism
H:\mathbb{A}^{1}\times Y\to Y
.

The scheme
X
is connected if and only if we have
\Hcoh^{0}(X,\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell})=\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}
, and similarly
X\times_{Y}Y^{\mathbb{G}_{m}}
is connected if and only if
\Hcoh^{0}(X\times_{Y}Y^{\mathbb{G}_{m}},\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell})=\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}
. Therefore, it suffices to establish an isomorphism
\Hcoh^{0}(X,\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell})\cong\Hcoh^{0}(X\times_{Y}Y^{\mathbb{G}_{m}},\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell})
. Let
i:Y^{\mathbb{G}_{m}}\hookrightarrow Y
denote the closed immersion from the scheme of fixed points. By proper base change applied to the morphism
f
, we have
\Hcoh^{0}(X\times_{Y}Y^{\mathbb{G}_{m}},\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell})=\Hcoh^{0}(Y^{\mathbb{G}_{m}},i^{*}f_{*}\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell})
. Therefore, it suffices to show that the following is an isomorphism:

\Hcoh^{0}(X,\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell})=\Hcoh^{0}(Y,f_{*}\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell})\xrightarrow{i^{*}}\Hcoh^{0}(Y^{\mathbb{G}_{m}},i^{*}f_{*}\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell})=\Hcoh^{0}(X\times_{Y}Y^{\mathbb{G}_{m}},\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}).

This follows from
Proposition

2.3
, where we use
\mathcal{Z}=Y^{\mathbb{G}_{m}}\hookrightarrow Y=\mathcal{X}
and
F=f_{*}\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}
.
∎

The statement of
Proposition

2.6
was an attempt to extract the topological content of the arguments in
[
PPN19
]
. In particular, as a direct consequence of
Proposition

2.6
, we may recover
[
PPN19
, Thm. 1.1]
. We state and prove a more general formulation of
[
PPN19
, Thm. 1.1]
in
Section

4.2
.

3.1
Non Abelian Hodge Theory in positive characteristic

For this subsection, we fix the following setup.

Context 3.1
.

(1)

Suppose that the algebraically closed ground field
k
has characteristic
p>0
.

(2)

Let
G
be a connected reductive group over
k
which satisfies the low height property (
[
HZ23a
, Defn. 2.29]
).

(3)

Let
C
be a smooth projective connected curve of genus
g\geq 2
, and let
C^{\prime}
denote the Frobenius twist of
C
.

(4)

We fix a degree
d\in\pi_{1}(G)
.

The main varieties of interest in this subsection are the following. We refer the reader to
[
HZ23a
, §2]

for detailed treatments of their constructions.

Notation 3.2

(Moduli spaces)
.

We denote by
\mathbb{M}_{Dol,G}^{d}(C^{\prime})
the Dolbeault moduli space of semistable
G
-Higgs bundles of degree
d
on the Frobenius twist
C^{\prime}
.

By a
G
-connection on
C
we mean
a
G
-bundle on
C
together with a connection.
We denote by
\mathbb{M}_{dR,G}^{pd}(C)
the de Rham moduli space of semistable
G
-connections of degree
pd
on
C
.

We will use the corresponding Hitchin fibrations for both moduli spaces. See
[
HZ23a
, §5]
for more details on the construction of the following morphisms.

Notation 3.3

(Hitchin morphisms)
.

Let
A_{G,\omega_{C^{\prime}}}
denote the Hitchin base for the curve
C^{\prime}
. We denote by
h_{Dol}:\mathbb{M}_{Dol,G}^{d}(C^{\prime})\to A_{G,\omega_{C^{\prime}}}
(resp.
h_{dR}:\mathbb{M}_{dR,G}^{pd}(C)\to A_{G,\omega_{C^{\prime}}}
) the Hitchin morphisms (resp. the de Rham-Hitchin morphism).

There is a relative cup product for the morphism
h_{Dol}
that equips
\bigoplus_{i=0}^{\infty}R^{i}(h_{Dol})_{*}\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}
with the structure of a sheaf of graded commutative
\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}
-algebras on
A_{G,\omega_{C^{\prime}}}
. Similarly,
\bigoplus_{i=0}^{\infty}R^{i}(h_{dR})_{*}\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}
is naturally equipped with the structure of a sheaf of graded commutative
\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}
-algebras.

Lemma 3.4
.

With assumptions as in
Context

3.1
, there is a canonical isomorphism of sheaves of graded commutative
\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}
-algebras

can:\bigoplus_{i=0}^{\infty}R^{i}(h_{Dol})_{*}\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}\xrightarrow{\sim}\bigoplus_{i=0}^{\infty}R^{i}(h_{dR})_{*}\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}.

Proof.

We use the canonical isomorphism
can:\bigoplus_{i=0}^{\infty}R^{i}(h_{Dol})_{*}\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}\xrightarrow{\sim}\bigoplus_{i=0}^{\infty}R^{i}(h_{dR})_{*}\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}
of graded
\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}
-sheaves constructed in
[
HZ23b
, Thm. 4.15(4)]
. We need to check that
can
is compatible with the algebra structures. Since the formation of the sheaves of graded commutative algebras is étale local on the base
A_{G,\omega_{C^{\prime}}}
, we may check the compatibility of
can
étale locally on
A_{G,\omega_{C^{\prime}}}
. But, after passing to an étale cover of the base, the morphism
can
is induced, by construction, from an isomorphism of the moduli spaces, and therefore it is compatible with the algebra structures induced by cup products.
∎

Theorem 3.5
.

With assumptions as in
Context

3.1
, there is a canonical isomorphism of cohomology rings:

\Hcoh^{\bullet}(\mathbb{M}_{Dol,G}^{d}(C^{\prime}),\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell})\cong\Hcoh^{\bullet}(\mathbb{M}_{dR,G}^{pd}(C),\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}).

(1)

Proof.

The Hitchin morphism
h_{Dol}:\mathbb{M}_{Dol,G}^{d}(C^{\prime})\to A_{G,\omega_{C^{\prime}}}
is equivariant with respect to certain scaling
\mathbb{G}_{m}
-actions such that the action on
A_{G,\omega_{C^{\prime}}}
is contracting and has a unique fixed point
\mathfrak{o}\in A_{G,\omega_{C^{\prime}}}(k)
. For every
i\geq 0
, the higher direct image
R^{i}(h_{Dol})_{*}\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}
is a constructible
\mathbb{G}_{m}
-equivariant
\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}
-sheaf on
A_{G,\omega_{C^{\prime}}}
. Therefore, by
Proposition

2.3
, we have
\Hcoh^{j}(A_{G,\omega_{C^{\prime}}},R^{i}(h_{Dol})_{*}\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell})=0
for all
j>0
. This implies that the
E_{2}
-page of the Leray spectral sequence for the composition
\mathbb{M}_{Dol,G}^{d}(C^{\prime})\to A_{G,\omega_{C^{\prime}}}\to\operatorname{Spec}(k)
is concentrated in a single column, and hence we get a canonical isomorphism of
\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}
-algebras

\Hcoh^{\bullet}(\mathbb{M}_{Dol,G}^{d}(C^{\prime}),\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell})\cong\bigoplus_{i=0}^{\infty}\Hcoh^{0}(A_{G,\omega_{C^{\prime}}},R^{i}(h_{Dol})_{*}\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}),

(2)

where the algebra structure on the right-hand side is induced from the algebra structure on the sheaf
\bigoplus_{i=0}^{\infty}R^{i}(h_{Dol})_{*}\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}
.

Since
R^{i}(h_{Dol})_{*}\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}\cong R^{i}(h_{dR})_{*}\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}
for all
i\geq 0
, we also have
\Hcoh^{j}(A_{G,\omega_{C^{\prime}}},R^{i}(h_{dR})_{*}\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell})=0
for all
j>0
. Therefore, we have a similar canonical isomorphism of
\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}
-algebras induced by the Leray spectral sequence

\Hcoh^{\bullet}(\mathbb{M}_{dR,G}^{pd}(C),\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell})\cong\bigoplus_{i=0}^{\infty}\Hcoh^{0}(A_{G,\omega_{C^{\prime}}},R^{i}(h_{dR})_{*}\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}).

(3)

Using the canonical isomorphism
can
of sheaves of algebras from
Lemma

3.4
, we obtain our desired chain of canonical identifications of
\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}
-algebras:

\displaystyle\Hcoh^{\bullet}(\mathbb{M}_{Dol,G}^{d}(C^{\prime}),\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell})\cong\bigoplus_{i=0}^{\infty}\Hcoh^{0}\left(A_{G,\omega_{C^{\prime}}},R^{i}(h_{Dol})_{*}\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}\right)\cong\bigoplus_{i=0}^{\infty}\Hcoh^{0}\left(A_{G,\omega_{C^{\prime}}},R^{i}(h_{dR})_{*}\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}\right)\cong\Hcoh^{\bullet}(\mathbb{M}_{dR,G}^{pd}(C),\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}).

∎

3.2
The cohomology of the nilpotent cones

Notation 3.6

(Nilpotent cones)

Let

R^{i}(h_{Dol})_{*}\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}
on
A_{G,\omega_{C^{\prime}}}
, we obtain a canonical isomorphism of
\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}
-algebras

\bigoplus_{i=0}^{\infty}\Hcoh^{0}(A_{G,\omega_{C^{\prime}}},R^{i}(h_{Dol})_{*}\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell})\cong\bigoplus_{i=0}^{\infty}\Hcoh^{0}(\mathfrak{o},i_{\mathfrak{o}}^{*}R^{i}(h_{Dol})_{*}\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}),

(7)

where
i_{\mathfrak{o}}:\mathfrak{o}\hookrightarrow A_{G,\omega_{C^{\prime}}}
is the closed immersion of the origin.
Combining (
2
), (
7
) and proper base change, we see that the left vertical restriction morphism in (
6
) is an isomorphism of cohomology rings.
By
Lemma

3.4
, (
7
), and (
3
), we have that the right vertical restriction morphism in (
6
) is also an isomorphism of cohomology rings.

To show that (
6
) is commutative, it suffices to prove that the isomorphism (
5
) is identified with the isomorphism
\Hcoh^{0}(i_{\mathfrak{o}}^{*}can)
, where the morphism
can
is as in
Lemma

3.4
.
The morphism
can
is obtained in the following way: we choose étale local trivializations of the
\mathbb{P}^{o}
-torsor
\mathbb{H}^{o}
over
A_{G,\omega_{C^{\prime}}}
, which induces étale local isomorphisms of the form
R^{i}(h_{Dol})_{*}\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}\xrightarrow{\sim}R^{i}(h_{dR})_{*}\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}
.
We then use the Homotopy Lemma
[
HZ23b
, Lem. 4.13]
to show that those étale local isomorphisms are independent of the trivializations of
\mathbb{H}^{o}
, and thus glue to a global isomorphism
can
.
Therefore, up to the proper base change isomorphism, the morphism
\Hcoh^{0}(i_{\mathfrak{o}}^{*}can)
is given by an isomorphism of schemes
\mathbb{N}_{Dol,G}^{d}(C^{\prime})\xrightarrow{\sim}\mathbb{N}_{dR,G}^{pd}(C)
induced by (
4
) and a trivialization of
\mathbb{H}^{o}|_{\mathfrak{o}}
.
By the discussion above
Theorem

3.7
, we see that
\Hcoh^{0}(i_{\mathfrak{o}}^{*}can)
coincides with (
5
) up to the proper base change isomorphism.
∎

Remark 3.8
.

In the case when
G=\mathrm{GL}_{n}
and the degree
d\in\pi_{1}(\mathrm{GL}_{n})\cong\mathbb{Z}
is coprime to
n
, the isomorphisms in
Theorem

3.7
recovers
[
dCZ22a
, Thm. 2.4]
, which is one of the main results in that paper. We take this opportunity to remark that there is an gap in the proof
loc. cit
, and our method above supplies the correct proof:
Let us use the notation in
loc. cit.

The main theorem, which is called the Cohomological Simpson Correspondence, is proved twice: first in Theorem 2.1 and then in Theorem 3.6.
The latter is a refinement of the former, with a different proof.
The proof of Theorem 2.1 contains a gap: we cannot deduce (22), which says
h_{dR,*}\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}\cong h_{Hod,0,*}\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}
, from (17), which says that
h_{dR}\times\mathrm{id}_{\mathbb{G}_{m}}=h_{Hod}|_{\mathbb{G}_{m}}
, and (21), which identifies the two terms in (17) with the nearby cycles of
(h_{dR}\times\mathrm{id}_{\mathbb{G}_{m}})_{*}\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}
and
(h_{Hod}|_{\mathbb{G}_{m}})_{*}\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}
.
The problem is that there is an implicit non-identity isomorphism on the common target of the two morphisms in (17), see
[
dCZ22b
, Lem. 4.5]
for the precise statement.
We do not know if (22) is true.
Because of Theorem 3.6, the Cohomological Simpson Correspondence still holds, but the flawed proof of Theorem 2.1 affects the proof Theorem 2.4, where (22) is used.
Our result above shows that Theorem 2.4, and all the results depending on it, are still true.

Let us also remark that the idea of using results similar to
[
dCMM18
, Lem. 4.2]
to show the
E_{2}
-degeneration of the Leray spectral sequence as in the proof of
Theorem

3.5
is suggested by de Cataldo when we were trying to remedy
[
dCZ22a
, Thm. 2.4]
.

4
Further Applications

In this section, we use our study of the topology of
\mathbb{G}_{m}
-actions in
Section

2
to obtain a result on the cohomology of the moduli stack of
G
-Higgs bundles and a result on very stable
G
-bundles.
We believe that these results are of independent interest.

4.1
The cohomology of the stack of
G
-Hitchin pairs

In this subsection, we study the cohomology of the whole moduli stack of
G
-Higgs bundles without imposing any form of semistability (as opposed to the case of the moduli spaces considered in
Section

3
). We place ourselves in the following

Context 4.1
.

Fix a connected reductive group
G
and a smooth connected projective curve
C
over the algebraically closed ground field
k
. We fix a degree
d\in\pi_{1}(G)
and a line bundle
\mathcal{L}
.

Notation 4.2
.

Let
\mathscr{B}un_{G}^{d}(C)
denote the stack that parametrizes
G
-bundles of degree
d
on
C
(see
[
Hof10
, Thm. 5.8]
for the notion of degree of a
G
-bundle).

Definition 4.3

(Stack of
G
-Hitchin pairs)
.

We denote by
\mathscr{H}\!it_{G,\mathcal{L}}^{d}(C)
the algebraic stack that parametrizes pairs
(E,\varphi)
consisting of a
G
-bundle
E
of degree
d
on
C
and an
\mathcal{L}
-twisted Higgs field
\varphi\in\Hcoh^{0}(C,\ad(E)\otimes\mathcal{L})
, where
\ad(E):=E\times^{G}\text{Lie}(G)
is the adjoint vector bundle.

Proposition 4.4
.

Suppose that we have the setup as in
Context

4.1
. Then, we have a natural isomorphism
R\Gamma(\mathscr{H}\!it_{G,\mathcal{L}}^{d}(C),\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell})\to R\Gamma(\mathscr{B}un_{G}^{d}(C),\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell})
. If the ground field is
\mathbb{C}
, there is an isomorphism
R\Gamma(\mathscr{H}\!it_{G,\mathcal{L}}^{d}(C),\mathbb{Q})\to R\Gamma(\mathscr{B}un_{G}^{d}(C),\mathbb{Q})
in
D^{+}_{H,c}(\operatorname{Spec}(\mathbb{C}))
.

Proof.

This follows from applying
Proposition

2.2
and
Proposition

2.4
where we set
\mathcal{X}=\mathscr{H}\!it_{G,\mathcal{L}}^{d}
and
\mathcal{Z}=\mathscr{B}un_{G}^{d}(C)
, and we use the morphisms

i:\mathscr{B}un_{G}(C)^{d}\hookrightarrow\mathscr{H}\!it_{G,\mathcal{L}}^{d}(C),\;\;\;E\mapsto(E,0)

H:\mathbb{A}^{1}\times\mathscr{H}\!it_{G,\mathcal{L}}^{d}(C)\to\mathscr{H}\!it_{G,\mathcal{L}}^{d}(C),\;\;\;(t,(E,\varphi))\mapsto(E,t\cdot\varphi).

∎

Corollary 4.5
.

In
Context

4.1
, suppose that
G
is semisimple and that the ground field is
\mathbb{C}
. Then, the cohomology
\Hcoh^{\bullet}(\mathscr{H}\!it_{G,\mathcal{L}}^{d},\mathbb{Q})
is pure.

Proof.

This is a consequence of
Proposition

4.4
and the fact that the cohomology
\Hcoh^{\bullet}(\mathscr{B}un_{G}^{d}(C),\mathbb{Q})
is pure by
[
AB83
]
, see also
[
AD08
, Cor. 4.5]
.
∎

Remark 4.6
.

If the degree of the line bundle
\mathcal{L}
satisfies
\deg(\mathcal{L})>2g-2
, where
g
is the genus of the curve, then it was recently observed that, more surprisingly, the Borel-Moore homology of
\mathscr{H}\!it_{G,\mathcal{L}}^{d}
is also pure (see
[
Kin24
, Rem. 5.6]
).

Remark 4.7
.

If we use the notion of purity in the sense of Frobenius eigenvalues, then
Corollary

4.5
also applies in the case when
C
is defined over a finite field and
G
is semisimple by the main results in
[
HS10
]
.

4.2
Very stable
G
-bundles

For this subsection, we place ourselves in the following.

Context 4.8
.

Fix a connected reductive group
G
and a smooth connected projective curve
C
over the algebraically closed ground field
k
. We fix a degree
d\in\pi_{1}(G)
and a line bundle
\mathcal{L}
. If the field
k
has positive characteristic, then we impose the assumption that
G
satisfies the low height property as in
[
HZ23a
, Def. 2.29]
.

Notation 4.9
.

Let
\mathbb{M}_{Hit,G,\mathcal{L}}^{d}(C)
denote the moduli space of semistable
\mathcal{L}
-twisted
G
-Hitchin pairs on
C
(cf.
[
HZ23a
, Lem. 5.18]
).

The scheme
\mathbb{M}_{Hit,G,\mathcal{L}}^{d}(C)
is of finite type over
k
equipped with a proper Hitchin morphism
h:\mathbb{M}_{Hit,G,\mathcal{L}}^{d}(C)\to A_{G,\mathcal{L}}
into the corresponding affine Hitchin base
A_{G,\mathcal{L}}

[
AHLH23
, Cor. 6.21+Rmk. 6.22]
.

Definition 4.10
.

A stable
G
-bundle
E
is called very stable with respect to
\mathcal{L}
if and only if its only nilpotent
\mathcal{L}
-twisted Higgs field
\varphi\in\Hcoh^{0}(C,\ad(E)\otimes\mathcal{L})
is the zero section.

Given a stable
G
-bundle
E
, let
V_{G}
denote the vector space
\Hcoh^{0}(C,\ad(E)\otimes\mathcal{L})
thought of as an affine scheme. There is a locally closed immersion

V_{G}\hookrightarrow\mathbb{M}_{Hit,G,\mathcal{L}}^{d}(C),\;\;\;\varphi\mapsto(E,\varphi).

Theorem 4.11

(
[
PPN19
, Thm. 1.1]
)
.

Suppose that we are in the setup of
Context

4.8
. Given a stable
G
-bundle
E
, the following statements are equivalent:

(a)

The inclusion
V_{G}\hookrightarrow\mathbb{M}_{Hit,G,\mathcal{L}}^{d}(C)
is a closed immersion.

(b)

The composition
h_{V_{G}}:V_{G}\to\mathbb{M}_{Hit,G,\mathcal{L}}^{d}(C)\to A_{G,\mathcal{L}}
is proper.

(c)

The morphism
h_{V_{G}}:V_{G}\to A_{G,\mathcal{L}}
is quasifinite.

(d)

E
is very stable with respect to
\mathcal{L}
.

Proof.

(a)
\Leftrightarrow
(b). This is immediate.

(b)
\Rightarrow
(c). This follows because the source
V_{G}
is affine.

(c)
\Rightarrow
(b). We equip the vector space
V_{G}
with the contracting scaling
\mathbb{G}_{m}
-action, and we equip the Hitchin base
A_{G,\mathcal{L}}
with its standard contracting
\mathbb{G}_{m}
-action. The origin
\mathfrak{o}\in A_{G,\mathcal{L}}(k)
is the unique
\mathbb{G}_{m}
-fixed point in
A_{G,\mathcal{L}}
. If
h_{V_{G}}
is quasifinite, then
h^{-1}_{V_{G}}\left(A_{G,\mathcal{L}}^{\mathbb{G}_{m}}\right)=h^{-1}_{V_{G}}(\mathfrak{o})
is finite. Hence
[
HZ23a
, Prop. B5]
applies to show that
h_{V_{G}}
is proper.

(c)
\Rightarrow
(d). By the equivalences proven in the paragraphs above, we know that
h_{V_{G}}
is finite. Equip
V_{G}
and
A_{G,\mathcal{L}}
with the
\mathbb{G}_{m}
-actions as explained in the proof of (c)
\Rightarrow
(b). Since
V_{G}
is connected,
Proposition

2.6
implies that
h_{V_{G}}^{-1}(\mathfrak{o})
is connected. Since
h_{V_{G}}^{-1}(\mathfrak{o})
is finite, it must consist of a single point. By definition, the points in
h_{V_{G}}^{-1}(\mathfrak{o})
correspond to nilpotent Higgs fields on
E
, and therefore we conclude that the only nilpotent Higgs field is
0
. Hence,
E
is very stable.

(d)
\Rightarrow
(c). Equip
V_{G}
and
A_{G,\mathcal{L}}
with the
\mathbb{G}_{m}
-actions as explained in the proof of (c)
\Rightarrow
(b). By upper-semicontinuity of fiber dimension and the existence of zero limits for the
\mathbb{G}_{m}
-action on
V_{G}
, it follows that the dimension of the preimage
h^{-1}_{V_{G}}(\mathfrak{o})
of the unique fixed point
\mathfrak{o}\in A_{G,\mathcal{L}}(k)
is maximal. By assumption, this preimage consists of a single point, and so it has dimension
0
. It follows that all the fibers of
h_{V_{G}}
have dimension
0
, as desired.
∎

2.6
