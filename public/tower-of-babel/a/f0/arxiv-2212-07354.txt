Hypersurfaces with mean curvature prescribed by an ambient function: compactness results
Costante Bellettini

Abstract
We consider, in a first instance, the class of boundaries of sets with locally finite perimeter whose (weakly defined) mean curvature is g ​ ν g\nu , for a given continuous positive ambient function g g , and where ν \nu denotes the inner normal. It is well-known that taking limits in the sense of varifolds within this class is not possible in general, due to the appearence of “hidden boundaries”, that is, portions (of positive measure with even multiplicity) on which the (weakly defined) mean curvature vanishes, so that g g does not prescribe the mean curvature in the limit. As a special instance of a more general result, we prove that locally uniform L q L^{q} -bounds on the (weakly defined) second fundamental form, for q > 1 q>1 , in addition to the customary locally uniform bounds on the perimeters, lead to a compact class of boundaries with mean curvature prescribed by g g . The proof relies on treating the boundaries as oriented integral varifolds, in order to exploit their orientability feature (that is lost when treating them as (unoriented) varifolds). Specifically, it relies on the formulation and analysis of a weak notion of curvature coefficients for oriented integral varifolds (inspired by Hutchinson’s work [ 6 ] ). This framework gives (with no additional effort) a compactness result for oriented integral varifolds with curvature locally bounded in L q L^{q} with q > 1 q>1 and with mean curvature prescribed by any g ∈ C 0 g\in C^{0} (in fact, the function can vary with the varifold for which it prescribes the mean curvature, as long as there is locally uniform convergence of the prescribing functions). Our notions and statements are given in a Riemannian manifold, with the oriented varifolds that need not arise as boundaries (for instance, they could come from two-sided immersions).

Remark 4.1
.

Note that Theorem
1
is indeed a special case of Theorem
2
. This follows from the argument in Section
2.3
, which shows that

is an oriented integral varifold with curvature, with curvature coefficients

\delta_{i}(\nu_{E_{\ell}}\cdot\textbf{e}_{a})=-(\nabla_{\textbf{e}_{i}^{T}}\textbf{e}_{a}^{T})^{N}
, where
T
and
N
stand respectively for tangential and normal components of a vector with respect to
\partial E_{\ell}
. Therefore
\int\sum_{i,j}|W^{\ell}_{ij}|^{q}dV^{\ell}\leq\int_{\partial E_{\ell}}|B^{\ell}|^{q}d\mathcal{H}^{n}

is the (classical) second fundamental form of

Our first observation is that the class of oriented

-varifolds in

with curvature in

is compact (in the oriented varifold topology) under locally uniform bounds on the masses and on the

-norms of the curvature coefficients, for

. This can be deduced as immediate consequence of the (more general) convergence of measure-function pairs in
[
6
]
(using
[
6

). We prove it here directly in terms of convergence of (vector-valued) measures. (Here we work on the sequence

under consideration; the general statement of this compactness property will be given in Proposition
5.2
.)

Consider the sequence of vector-valued measures
\vec{\mu}_{\ell}=\vec{W}^{\ell}V^{\ell}
, where
\vec{W}^{\ell}
is the vector with
(n+1)^{2}
entries
\{W^{\ell}_{ia}\}
for
(a,i)\in\{1,\ldots,n+1\}\times\{1,\ldots,n+1\}
(for a fixed order on this set of indeces). By assumption,
\int_{U\times\mathbb{R}^{n+1}}|W_{ia}^{\ell}|^{q}dV^{\ell}\leq K
for all
\ell
and for all
i,a
, for the given

.
Then the measures
\vec{\mu}_{\ell}
have locally uniformly bounded total variations,
|\vec{\mu}_{\ell}|(U\times\mathbb{R}^{n+1})=\int_{U\times\mathbb{R}^{n+1}}|\vec{W}^{\ell}|dV^{\ell}\leq K^{1/q}C^{1/q^{\prime}}
, where
C
is the upper bound for the perimeters of
E_{\ell}
in
U
(provided by (
1
)) and
q^{\prime}
is the conjugate of
q
. By standard weak* compactness for Radon measures we can extract a (subsequential) limit
\vec{\mu}
. (We will use below the fact that the total variation is lower semi-continuous.) Extracting possibly a further subsequence, we also have the existence of an oriented integral varifold
V
to which

converge, thanks to the compactness theorem of Hutchinson, recalled in Section
2.1
. Let
A\subset U\times\mathbb{R}^{n+1}
be open and bounded; for
t>0
let
A_{t}
be the set of points in
A
at distance
\geq t
from
\partial A
; let

with

and

|\vec{\mu}|(A_{t})\leq\liminf_{\ell\to\infty}|\vec{\mu_{\ell}}|(A_{t})=\liminf_{\ell\to\infty}\int_{A_{t}}|\vec{W}^{\ell}|dV^{\ell}\leq\liminf_{\ell\to\infty}\int_{U\times\mathbb{R}^{n+1}}u|\vec{W}^{\ell}|dV^{\ell}\leq

\liminf_{\ell\to\infty}\left(\int_{U\times\mathbb{R}^{n+1}}|\vec{W}^{\ell}|^{q}dV^{\ell}\right)^{1/q}\left(\int u^{q^{\prime}}dV^{\ell}\right)^{1/q^{\prime}}\leq K^{1/q}\liminf_{\ell\to\infty}\left(\int u^{q^{\prime}}dV^{\ell}\right)^{1/q^{\prime}}

Then (since these are Radon measures) we get

is absolutely continuous with respect to

\vec{W}V

The convergence

The oriented integral varifold

Following

-varifold in
