On 3-generated 6-transposition groups
Vsevolod A. Afanasev, Andrey S. Mamontov

Abstract
We study 6 6 -transposition groups, i.e. groups generated by a normal set of involutions D D , such that the order of the product of any two elements from D D does not exceed 6 6 . We classify most of the groups generated by 3 elements from D D , two of which commute, and prove they are finite.
