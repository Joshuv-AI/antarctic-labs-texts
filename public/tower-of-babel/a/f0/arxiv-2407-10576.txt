Vector spaces over finite commutative rings
Jun Guo, Junli Liu, Qiuli Xu

Abstract
Vector spaces over finite fields and Anzahl formulas of subspaces were studied by Wan (Geometry of Classical Groups over Finite Fields, Science Press, 2002). As a generalization, we study vector spaces and singular linear spaces over commutative rings, and obtain some Anzahl formulas and dimensional formula for subspaces. Moreover, we discuss arcs and caps by using these subspaces. AMS classification : 13C10, 15A33, 20G35, 52E21, 51E22 Key words : Commutative ring, vector space, singular linear space, arc, cap
