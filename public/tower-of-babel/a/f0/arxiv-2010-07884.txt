C_{m}=\max_{\phi\in[l]:h_{\phi}=\cdots=h_{\phi+q}}(2^{q+1}-2)

Response to Reviewer 2

Comments to the Author
This paper introduces a kernel processing algorithm called window processing.
This algorithm calculates the path scores by first transforming the target kernel into an Arikan’s kernel,
and then calculating a collection of the corresponding path scores on the Arikan’s kernel. This approach applies to general kernels with the size being a power of 2.
In section IV and section V, the authors also propose several ways to further simplifies the decoding complexity (like CSE, and the reuse of some sub path scores).
The simulation results show that this window processing algorithm can achieve the good performance of the selected kernels with decent complexity.

It’s a very nice paper that comprehensively describes the proposed window processing algorithm.
I’d like to suggest the acceptance of this paper.

We thank the Reviewer for his efforts on reading and reviewing of the paper.
