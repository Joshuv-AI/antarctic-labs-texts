Introduction

1.1.
Background and motivation

This paper is concerned with the quasilinear Schrödinger equation of the form

(1.1)

-{\mathrm{div}}\left(\varphi^{2}(u)\nabla u\right)+\varphi(u)\varphi^{\prime}(u)|\nabla u|^{2}+\lambda u=f(u),\quad x\in\mathbb{R}^{N},

Quasilinear equations of the form (

[

In the last decades, quasilinear Schrödinger equations have received a considerable attentions from researchers on the existence of nontrivial solutions. In

When

Different to the case

1.3.
Strategy of this paper

First, by virtue of a dual approach in
[
26
]
, we transform equation (
1.1

\lambda>0
, we show the semilinear elliptic equation obtained admits at least one positive and radially symmetric solution

. Similarly to
[
13
]
, by using the blow-up argument and a Liouville theorem, as

or

-norms of
\phi^{-1}\left(v_{\lambda}\right)

To seek normalized solutions of (

If ones take

, then we get

t=\phi\left(s\right)=\int_{0}^{s}\varphi\left(\tau\right)\mathrm{d}\tau

and

\lambda\lim_{t\to+\infty}\frac{\phi^{-1}\left(t\right)}{t\varphi\left(\phi^{-1}\left(t\right)\right)}=\lambda\lim_{s\to+\infty}\frac{s}{\phi\left(s\right)\varphi\left(s\right)},

Noting that
\lim\limits_{s\to+\infty}\varphi\left(s\right)=a^{\ast}
,

\displaystyle\lambda\lim\limits_{s\to+\infty}\frac{s}{\phi\left(s\right)\varphi\left(s\right)}

\displaystyle=\frac{\lambda}{a^{\ast}}\lim\limits_{s\to+\infty}\frac{s}{\int_{0}^{s}\varphi\left(\tau\right)\mathrm{d}\tau}

\displaystyle=\frac{\lambda}{a^{\ast}}\lim\limits_{s\to+\infty}\frac{1}{\varphi\left(s\right)}

\displaystyle=\frac{\lambda}{(a^{\ast})^{2}}.

Meanwhile, it follows from
(F_{2})
that

\displaystyle\lim_{t\to+\infty}\frac{f\left(\phi^{-1}\left(t\right)\right)}{t\varphi\left(\phi^{-1}\left(t\right)\right)}

\displaystyle=\lim_{s\to+\infty}\frac{f\left(s\right)}{\phi\left(s\right)\varphi\left(s\right)}=\frac{1}{a^{\ast}}\lim_{s\to+\infty}\frac{f\left(s\right)}{\phi\left(s\right)}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{(a^{\ast})^{2}}\lim_{s\to+\infty}\frac{f\left(s\right)}{s}=+\infty.

Thus we obtain that

\lim_{t\to+\infty}\frac{\int_{0}^{t}g_{\lambda}\left(\tau\right)\mathrm{d}\tau}{t^{2}}=\infty,

which implies that there exists
T>0
such that
\int_{0}^{T}g_{\lambda}\left(\tau\right)\mathrm{d}\tau>\frac{\lambda}{2}T^{2}
.

(iv)

Set
p=\beta-1
and notice that
\phi(s)\geq s
for any
s>0
, we have

\displaystyle\lim\limits_{s\rightarrow+\infty}\frac{g_{\lambda}(s)}{s^{p}}

\displaystyle=\lim_{s\rightarrow+\infty}\frac{f\left(\phi^{-1}(s)\right.)}{s^{p}\varphi\left(\phi^{-1}(s)\right.)}+\lim_{s\rightarrow+\infty}\frac{\lambda s-\lambda\frac{\phi^{-1}(s)}{\varphi\left(\phi^{-1}\left(s\right)\right)}}{s^{p}}

\displaystyle=\lim\limits_{t\rightarrow+\infty}\frac{f(t)}{(\phi(t))^{p}\varphi(t)}-\lambda\lim_{t\rightarrow+\infty}\frac{t}{(\phi(t))^{p}\varphi(t)}

\displaystyle=\lim\limits_{t\rightarrow+\infty}\frac{f(t)}{(\phi(t))^{p}\varphi(t)}=\lim_{t\rightarrow+\infty}\frac{f(t)}{\left(ta^{*}\right)^{p}a^{*}}

\displaystyle=\lim\limits_{t\rightarrow+\infty}\frac{f(t)}{t^{p}}\cdot\frac{1}{\left(a^{*}\right)^{p+1}}=\frac{\mu_{2}}{\left(a^{*}\right)^{p+1}}.

(v)

It suffices to show that
\varphi(t)g_{\lambda}(\phi(t))\leq\phi(t)\frac{\rm{d}}{\rm{d}t}[g_{\lambda}(\phi(t))]
for
t>0
small. Obviously,

\displaystyle\frac{\rm{d}}{\rm{d}t}[g_{\lambda}(\phi(t))]

\displaystyle=\frac{f^{\prime}(t)\varphi(t)-f(t)\varphi^{\prime}(t)}{\varphi^{2}(t)}+\frac{\lambda\varphi^{3}(t)-\lambda\varphi(t)+\lambda t\varphi^{\prime}(t)}{\varphi^{2}(t)}

By
(\varphi_{0})
, for any
t\geq 0
,
\phi(t)\leq t\varphi(t)
and then

\phi(t)\varphi^{-2}(t)\left[\varphi^{3}(t)-\varphi(t)+t\varphi^{\prime}(t)\right]\geq\phi(t)\varphi(t)-t.

Meanwhile, by
(F_{2})
,

\lim_{t\rightarrow 0^{+}}\frac{\phi(t)\varphi(t)f^{\prime}(t)}{f(t)}=\alpha-1>1,

which implies that for
t>0
small,

\phi(t)\left[f^{\prime}(t)\varphi(t)-f(t)\varphi^{\prime}(t)\right]\geq\varphi^{2}(t)f(t).

Thus, it follows that

\phi(t)\frac{\rm{d}}{\rm{d}t}[g_{\lambda}(\phi(t))]\geq f(t)-\lambda t+\lambda\phi(t)\varphi(t)=\varphi(t)g_{\lambda}(\phi(t)),\,t>0\,\,\mbox{small}.

∎

\displaystyle\frac{f^{\prime}(\phi^{-1}(t))\varphi(\phi^{-1}(t))-f(\phi^{-1}(t))\varphi^{\prime}(\phi^{-1}(t))}{\lambda[\varphi(\phi^{-1}(t))]^{3}}

\displaystyle-\frac{\varphi(\phi^{-1}(t))-\phi^{-1}(t)\varphi^{\prime}(\phi^{-1}(t))}{[\varphi(\phi^{-1}(t))]^{3}}+1.

Thanks to

in

\lim_{\lambda\rightarrow 0^{+}}\lambda^{\frac{1}{\alpha-2}}[\theta(x)v_{\lambda}^{(1)}(x)+(1-\theta(x))v_{\lambda}^{(2)}(x)]=0\,\,\,\mbox{uniformly for}\,\,x\in\mathbb{R}^{N},

which implies that for any

in

and

Similarly to

, we can get one contradiction.

Now, we consider the case of
