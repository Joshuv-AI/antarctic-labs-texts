Kantowski-Sachs spherically symmetric solutions in teleparallel F ⁡ ( T ) F(T) gravity
A. Landry

Abstract
In this paper, we investigate time-dependent Kantowski-Sachs spherically symmetric teleparallel F ⁡ ( T ) F(T) gravity in vacuum and in a perfect isotropic fluid. We begin by finding the field equations and solve for new teleparallel F ⁡ ( T ) F(T) solutions. With a power-law ansatz for the coframe functions, we find new non-trivial teleparallel F ⁡ ( T ) F(T) vacuum solutions. We then proceed to find new non-trivial teleparallel F ⁡ ( T ) F(T) solutions in a perfect isotropic fluid with both linear and non-linear equation of state. We find a great number of new exact and approximated teleparallel F ⁡ ( T ) F(T) solutions. These classes of new solutions are relevant for future cosmological applications.

The teleparallel

The role of symmetry is not as clear as in pseudo-Riemannian geometry, where symmetry is defined in terms of the Killing Vectors (KVs). The Riemannian geometry in GR is defined by the Levi-Civita curvature and full dependent on the metric. But it is very different for the case of teleparallel

The frame based approach development for determining the spacetime symmetries has been explored

7

VI
Discussion and Conclusion

The main aim of this paper was to obtain KS teleparallel
F(T)
solutions for vacuum, linear and non-linear perfect fluids. This was achieved through the use of various ansatzes, for example power-law and exponential (infinite superposition of power-laws). We obtained not only power-law
F(T)
solutions, but also more complex
F(T)
as we can see in sections
IV.1
and
V.1
for

via power-law ansatz solutions. As mentioned before, these teleparallel
F(T)

A_{3}

t

IV.3
and
V.3

However, some special solutions are found in sections

In the case of a non-linear perfect fluids in section
