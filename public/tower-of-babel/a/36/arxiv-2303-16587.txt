Abstract

Introduction

The Hardy-Littlewood maximal operator

[

[

]

[
9
]
, Lebesgue spaces with variable exponent
[
3
]
, Musielak-Orlicz spaces
[
7
]
. Moreover,

is bounded in Hölder spaces
C^{0,s}(X)
(see
[
2
]
), where
(X,d,\mu)
satisfies the so-called
\delta
-annular decay property

Let us observe that since

Is the Hardy–Littlewood maximal operator continuous in

The positive answer to this question is given in

The remainder of the paper is structured as follows. In Section 2, we introduce the notations and recall the definitions. In Section 3 we prove boundedness of the maximal operator in the Musielak-Orlicz-Sobolev spaces. Auxiliary results, which are needed in the proof of the main result, are contained in Section 4. Our principal assertion, concerning the continuity of the maximal operator in the Musielak-Orlicz-Sobolev is formulated and proven in the last section.

3.
Boundedness of the maximal operator

For
f\in L^{1}_{\textnormal{loc}}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
we define the maximal function
Mf\colon\mathbb{R}^{n}\to\mathbb{R}
in a standard way

Mf(x)=\sup_{r>0}\mathop{\mathchoice{\kern 1.28333pt\vbox{\hrule width=3.50002pt}\kern-4.78336pt}{\kern 1.16664pt\vbox{\hrule width=3.50002pt}\kern-4.66666pt}{\kern 1.02428pt\vbox{\hrule width=3.07292pt}\kern-4.09721pt}{\kern 0.96526pt\vbox{\hrule width=2.89586pt}\kern-3.86111pt}}\mathopen{}\int_{B(x,r)}|f(z)|\,dz.

Let us recall the crucial theorem about boudedness of maximal function in the Musielak-Orlicz spaces.

Theorem 3.1

(
[
6

)
.

Let

,
(A1)

Now, we are in position to formulate and prove the main result of this section.

Theorem 3.2

Let

(A2)

\displaystyle|D_{i}Mf(x)|\leq MD_{i}f(x),

4

4

Proof.

For

. By Theorem

\displaystyle|D_{i}g_{k}(x)|\leq\max_{1\leq m\leq k}\left|D_{i}(|f|*h_{r_{m}})(x)\right|\leq M(D_{i}|f|)(x)=M(D_{i}f)(x)
