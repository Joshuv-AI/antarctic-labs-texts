Canonical Witt formal scheme extensions and p p -torsion groups
Alessandra Bertapelle 1, Nicola Mazzari 1, Arnab Saha 2

Abstract
. We study the n n -th arithmetic jet space of the p p -torsion subgroup attached to a smooth commutative formal group scheme. We show that the n n -th jet space above fits in the middle of a canonical short exact sequence between a power of the formal scheme of Witt vectors of length n n and the p p -torsion subgroup we started with. This result generalizes a result of Buium on roots of unity.
