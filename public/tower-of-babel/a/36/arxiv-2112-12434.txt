Moduli Space of Sheaves and Categorified Commutator of Functors
Yu Zhao

Abstract
. Neguţ constructed an action of the quantum toroidal algebra action on the K-theory of the smooth moduli space of stable sheaves over an algebraic surface, which generalized the action studied by Nakajima, Grojnowski and Baranovsky in cohomology. In this paper, we construct a weak categorification of Neguţ’s action. The new ingredient is two intersection-theoretic descriptions of the quadruple moduli space of stable sheaves.
