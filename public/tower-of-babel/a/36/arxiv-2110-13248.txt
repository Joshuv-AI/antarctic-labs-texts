Partially Explicit Time Discretization for Nonlinear Time Fractional Diffusion Equations
Wenyuan Li, Anatoly Alikhanov, Yalchin Efendiev, Wing Tat Leung

Abstract




Abstract

Nonlinear time fractional partial differential equations are widely used in modeling and simulations. In many applications, there are high contrast changes in media properties. For solving these problems, one often uses coarse spatial
grid for spatial resolution. For temporal discretization, implicit methods
are often used.
For implicit methods, though the time step can be relatively large,
the equations are difficult to compute due to the nonlinearity and
the fact that one deals with large-scale systems.
On the other hand, the discrete system in explicit methods are easier to
compute but it requires small time steps.
In this work, we propose the partially explicit scheme following
earlier works on developing partially explicit methods for nonlinear diffusion equations. In this scheme, the diffusion term is treated partially explicitly and the reaction term is treated fully explicitly. With the appropriate construction of spaces and stability analysis, we find that the required time step in our proposed scheme scales as the coarse mesh size, which creates a great saving in computing. The main novelty of this work is the extension
of our earlier works for diffusion equations to time fractional diffusion
equations. For the case of fractional diffusion equations, the constraints on
time steps are more severe and the proposed methods alleviate this
since the time step in partially explicit method scales as the coarse mesh size.
We present stability results. Numerical results are presented where
we compare our proposed partially explicit methods with a fully implicit
approach. We show that our proposed approach provides similar results, while
treating many degrees of freedom in nonlinear terms explicitly.

Introduction

Multiscale features arise in many problems such as the flow in porous media. The multiscale nature appears because of the variations in media properties. For instance, the permeability field can vary at different scales and have high contrast. There are many multiscale methods developed for parabolic partial differential equations. However, there are only a small amount of research on multiscale time fractional diffusion reaction equations while they have many applications.

Our work starts with the nonlinear time fractional partial differential

\cfrac{\partial^{\alpha}u}{\partial t^{\alpha}}+f(u)+g(u)=0,

where

is the diffusion operator and
g(u)

challenging and we leave it for future studies. In general, one can
easily solve the coupled mass matrix and the main difficulty is
in handling high contrast diffusion equation, which we handle via
our partial explicit method.

The main findings of our paper are as follows.

•

We propose a partially explicit scheme for time fractional
nonlinear equations.

•

The time step in our method scales as the coarse mesh size.

•

The stability analysis is performed for time fractional case.

We present numerical results. We consider high contrast linear
and nonlinear diffusion operators
and nonlinear reaction terms. We identify coarse degrees of freedom
and solve them in nonlinear systems implicitly. Other degrees of freedom
are treated explicitly. We consider two types of source terms.
The first source term is a smooth function and the second source term
is a singular functions with locally supported jump. In all cases,
we compare our proposed method with the fully implicit method.
We show that our proposed method gives similar accuracy while
treating additional degrees of freedom in nonlinear terms explicitly.

2
Problem Setting

Let
\Omega
be a bounded domain in
\mathbb{R}^{d}
(
d=1,2,3
) and the boundary
\partial\Omega
is sufficiently smooth.
We want to discuss the
time fractional nonlinear diffusion reaction equation

\displaystyle\frac{\partial^{\alpha}u(x,t)}{\partial t^{\alpha}}+f(u)+g(u)=0.

(2.1)

We let
t_{0}=0
be the initial time and
T
be the final time.
We define
\Delta t=\frac{T}{K}
(
\Delta t<<T
),
t_{j}=j\cdot\Delta t
and
u^{j}=u(t_{j})
,
0\leq j\leq K
.
Let
b_{k}=(k+1)^{1-\alpha}-k^{1-\alpha}
and

\alpha_{0}=\Gamma(2-\alpha)(\Delta t)^{\alpha}
.
Discretize the time fractional derivative as in
[
39
]
, we have

\displaystyle\frac{\partial^{\alpha}u(x,t_{k+1})}{\partial t^{\alpha}}=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1-\alpha)}\int_{0}^{t_{k+1}}\frac{\partial u(x,s)}{\partial s}\frac{1}{(t_{k+1}-s)^{\alpha}}ds

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1-\alpha)}\sum_{j=0}^{k}\int^{t_{j+1}}_{t_{j}}\frac{\partial u(x,s)}{\partial s}\frac{1}{(t_{k+1}-s)^{\alpha}}ds

\displaystyle\approx

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1-\alpha)}\sum_{j=0}^{k}\frac{u(t_{j+1})-u(t_{j})}{\Delta t}\int^{t_{j+1}}_{t_{j}}\frac{1}{(t_{k+1}-s)^{\alpha}}ds

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1-\alpha)\Delta t}\sum_{j=0}^{k}(u(t_{j+1})-u(t_{j}))\Big(\frac{-1}{1-\alpha}(t_{k+1}-s)^{1-\alpha}\Big)\Big|_{t_{j}}^{t_{j+1}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{(1-\alpha)\Gamma(1-\alpha)\Delta t}\sum_{j=0}^{k}(u(t_{j+1})-u(t_{j}))((t_{k+1}-t_{j})^{1-\alpha}-(t_{k+1}-t_{j+1})^{1-\alpha})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(2-\alpha)\Delta t}\sum_{j=0}^{k}(u(t_{j+1})-u(t_{j}))(\Delta t)^{1-\alpha}((k+1-j)^{1-\alpha}-(k-j)^{1-\alpha})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(2-\alpha)(\Delta t)^{\alpha}}\sum_{j=0}^{k}b_{k-j}(u(t_{j+1})-u(t_{j}))

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\alpha_{0}}\sum_{j=0}^{k}b_{k-j}(u^{j+1}-u^{j}).

Now Equation (
2.1
) becomes

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\alpha_{0}}\sum_{j=0}^{k}b_{k-j}(u^{j+1}-u^{j})+f(u)+g(u)=0,

where
f=\cfrac{\delta F}{\delta u}
and
g=\cfrac{\delta G}{\delta u}
.

f
is the contrast dependent term (which introduces stiffness) while
g
does not depend on the contrast.
Let
V
be a Hilbert space.
We assume that
f(u)\in V^{\ast}
and
g(u)\in L^{2}(\Omega)
for all
u\in V
.
For simplicity, we use
(\cdot,\cdot)
to denote
(\cdot,\cdot)_{V^{\ast},V}
.
To prove the stability of our schemes, we need to make some assumptions on
F
and
G
. We use
\|\cdot\|
to denote
\|\cdot\|_{L^{2}}
.

•

The second variational derivative of
F
and
G
satisfy

\displaystyle\delta^{2}F(u)(v,v)\geq c(u)\|v\|_{V}^{2},\quad\forall u,v\in V,

\displaystyle\delta^{2}G(u)(v,v)\geq b(u)\|v\|^{2},\quad\forall u,v\in V,

where
0\leq c(u)<\infty
and
-\underline{b}\leq b(u)<\infty

(
\underline{b}>0
) do not depend on
v
.

•

The second variational derivative of
F
and
G
is bounded

\displaystyle|\delta^{2}F(u)(w,v)|\leq C(u)\|v\|_{V}\|w\|_{V},\quad\forall u,v,w\in V,

\displaystyle|\delta^{2}G(u)(w,v)|\leq B\|v\|\|w\|,\quad\forall u,v,w\in V,

where
0<C(u)<\infty
and
0<B<\infty
do not depend on
v
,
w
.

Example 1
.

For
F=\cfrac{1}{2}\int_{\Omega}\kappa|\nabla u|^{2}
, we have

\left(\cfrac{\delta F}{\delta u},v\right)=\int_{\Omega}\kappa\nabla u\cdot\nabla v

and

\delta^{2}F(u)(w,v)=\int_{\Omega}\kappa\nabla v\cdot\nabla w,\quad\forall u\in V.

In this case, the norm we use is
\|v\|_{V}=\|\kappa^{\frac{1}{2}}\nabla v\|
. We find that

c(u)=C(u)\equiv 1.

We also need the following lemma to prove stability (see
[
32
]
).

Lemma 2.1
.

Let
N\in\mathbb{N}
. We have

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{N}\sum_{j=0}^{k}b_{k-j}(u^{j+1}-u^{j},u^{k+1}-u^{k})\geq\frac{1}{2}\sum_{k=0}^{N}\|u^{k+1}-u^{k}\|^{2}.

Proof.

By definition of
b_{k}
, we have

\displaystyle b_{k}=(k+1)^{1-\alpha}-k^{1-\alpha}=\frac{1}{1-\alpha}\int_{0}^{1}(k+s)^{-\alpha}ds.

Note that
\theta(k)=(k+s)^{-\alpha}
is a complete monotonic function.
By Hausdorff-Bernstein-Widder theorem, we obtain

\displaystyle(k+s)^{-\alpha}=\int_{0}^{\infty}e^{-k\tau}dg_{s}(\tau),

where
g_{s}
is a cumulative distribution function.
Thus, we have

\displaystyle\quad\sum_{k=0}^{N}\sum_{j=0}^{N}b_{|k-j|}(u^{j+1}-u^{j},u^{k+1}-u^{k})

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{1-\alpha}\int_{\Omega}\int_{0}^{1}\int_{0}^{\infty}\sum_{k=0}^{N}\sum_{j=0}^{N}e^{-|k-j|\tau}(u^{j+1}-u^{j})(u^{k+1}-u^{k})dg_{s}(\tau)dsdx.

Let
M
be a matrix with
M_{k,j}=e^{-|k-j|t}
. Then
M
is positive definite for
t>0
. Thus, we get

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{N}\sum_{j=0}^{N}b_{|k-j|}(u^{j+1}-u^{j},u^{k+1}-u^{k})>0.

With this inequality, we find that

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{N}\sum_{j=0}^{k}b_{k-j}(u^{j+1}-u^{j},u^{k+1}-u^{k})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\sum_{k=0}^{N}(u^{k+1}-u^{k},u^{k+1}-u^{k})+\frac{1}{2}\sum_{k=0}^{N}\sum_{j=0}^{N}b_{|k-j|}(u^{j+1}-u^{j},u^{k+1}-u^{k})

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\sum_{k=0}^{N}(u^{k+1}-u^{k},u^{k+1}-u^{k})=\frac{1}{2}\sum_{k=0}^{N}\|u^{k+1}-u^{k}\|^{2}.

∎

3
Three Schemes and Stability

3.1
Implicit Scheme

Implicit scheme is also called backward Euler scheme.
Let
V_{H}\subset H^{1}_{0}(\Omega)
be the finite element space.
In this case, we consider
\{u_{k}\}_{k=0}^{K}\subset V_{H}
such that:

\displaystyle(\frac{1}{\alpha_{0}}\sum_{j=0}^{k}b_{k-j}(u^{j+1}-u^{j}),v)+(f(u^{k+1})+g(u^{k+1}),v)=0,\quad\forall v\in V_{H}.

(3.1)

Now we want to show the stability of this implicit case.

Theorem 3.1
.

If
\alpha_{0}\leq\cfrac{1}{\underline{b}}
,
then the implicit scheme (
3.1
) is stable with

F(u^{N+1})+G(u^{N+1})\leq F(u^{0})+G(u^{0}),

where
0\leq N\leq K-1
and
N\in\mathbb{N}
.

Proof.

Let
v=u^{k+1}-u^{k}
.
As

\displaystyle F(u^{k+1})=F(u^{k})+(f(u^{k+1}),u^{k+1}-u^{k})-\frac{1}{2}\delta^{2}F(\xi_{1}^{k})(u^{k+1}-u^{k},u^{k+1}-u^{k}),

\displaystyle G(u^{k+1})=G(u^{k})+(g(u^{k+1}),u^{k+1}-u^{k})-\frac{1}{2}\delta^{2}G(\xi_{2}^{k})(u^{k+1}-u^{k},u^{k+1}-u^{k}),

where
\xi_{i}^{k}=\lambda_{i}^{k}u^{k}+(1-\lambda_{i}^{k})u^{k+1}
with

\lambda_{i}^{k}\in[0,1]
and
i\in\{1,2\}
. By Lemma
2.1
and assumptions on
F
and
G
, we have

\displaystyle 0

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\alpha_{0}}\sum_{k=0}^{N}\sum_{j=0}^{k}b_{k-j}(u^{j+1}-u^{j},u^{k+1}-u^{k})+\sum_{k=0}^{N}(f(u^{k+1})+g(u^{k+1}),u^{k+1}-u^{k})

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\alpha_{0}}\sum_{k=0}^{N}\sum_{j=0}^{k}b_{k-j}(u^{j+1}-u^{j},u^{k+1}-u^{k})+\sum_{k=0}^{N}[F(u^{k+1})-F(u^{k})+\frac{1}{2}\delta^{2}F(\xi_{1}^{k})(u^{k+1}-u^{k},u^{k+1}-u^{k})

\displaystyle\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad+G(u^{k+1})-G(u^{k})+\frac{1}{2}\delta^{2}G(\xi_{2}^{k})(u^{k+1}-u^{k},u^{k+1}-u^{k})]

\displaystyle\geq\frac{1}{2\alpha_{0}}\sum_{k=0}^{N}\|u^{k+1}-u^{k}\|^{2}+\sum_{k=0}^{N}[F(u^{k+1})-F(u^{k})+\frac{c(\xi_{1}^{k})}{2}\|u^{k+1}-u^{k}\|_{V}^{2}

\displaystyle\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad+G(u^{k+1})-G(u^{k})+\frac{b(\xi_{2}^{k})}{2}\|u^{k+1}-u^{k}\|^{2}]

\displaystyle\geq(\frac{1}{2\alpha_{0}}-\frac{\underline{b}}{2})\sum_{k=0}^{N}\|u^{k+1}-u^{k}\|^{2}+F(u^{N+1})-F(u^{0})+G(u^{N+1})-G(u^{0})+0.

Thus, if
\alpha_{0}\underline{b}\leq 1
, we have

\displaystyle F(u^{N+1})+G(u^{N+1})\leq F(u^{0})+G(u^{0}).

∎

3.2
Explicit Scheme

We consider
\{u^{k}\}_{k=0}^{K}\subset V_{H}
such that

\displaystyle(\frac{1}{\alpha_{0}}\sum_{j=0}^{k}b_{k-j}(u^{j+1}-u^{j}),v)+(f(u^{k})+g(u^{k}),v)=0,\quad\forall v\in V_{H}.

(3.2)

Theorem 3.2
.

If
\alpha_{0}\Big(\cfrac{C(\xi)\|u^{k+1}-u^{k}\|^{2}_{V}}{\|u^{k+1}-u^{k}\|^{2}}+B\Big)\leq 1
for any
\xi=(1-\lambda)u^{k+1}+\lambda u^{k}
with
\lambda\in[0,1]
and
0\leq k\leq N
, then the explicit scheme (
3.2
) is stable with

F(u^{N+1})+G(u^{N+1})\leq F(u^{0})+G(u^{0}),

where
0\leq N\leq K-1
and
N\in\mathbb{N}
.

Proof.

Let
v=u^{k+1}-u^{k}
.
As

\displaystyle F(u^{k+1})=F(u^{k})+(f(u^{k}),u^{k+1}-u^{k})+\frac{1}{2}\delta^{2}F(\xi_{1}^{k})(u^{k+1}-u^{k},u^{k+1}-u^{k}),

\displaystyle G(u^{k+1})=G(u^{k})+(g(u^{k}),u^{k+1}-u^{k})+\frac{1}{2}\delta^{2}G(\xi_{2}^{k})(u^{k+1}-u^{k},u^{k+1}-u^{k}),

where
\xi_{i}^{k}=\lambda_{i}^{k}u^{k}+(1-\lambda_{i}^{k})u^{k+1}
with

\lambda_{i}^{k}\in[0,1]
and
i\in\{1,2\}
.
By Lemma
2.1
and assumptions on
F
and
G
, we have

\displaystyle 0

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\alpha_{0}}\sum_{k=0}^{N}\sum_{j=0}^{k}b_{k-j}(u^{j+1}-u^{j},u^{k+1}-u^{k})+\sum_{k=0}^{N}(f(u^{k})+g(u^{k}),u^{k+1}-u^{k})

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\alpha_{0}}\sum_{k=0}^{N}\sum_{j=0}^{k}b_{k-j}(u^{j+1}-u^{j},u^{k+1}-u^{k})+\sum_{k=0}^{N}\Big[F(u^{k+1})-F(u^{k})-\frac{1}{2}\delta^{2}F(\xi_{1}^{k})(u^{k+1}-u^{k},u^{k+1}-u^{k})

\displaystyle\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad+G(u^{k+1})-G(u^{k})-\frac{1}{2}\delta^{2}G(\xi_{2}^{k})(u^{k+1}-u^{k},u^{k+1}-u^{k})\Big]

\displaystyle\geq\frac{1}{2\alpha_{0}}\sum_{k=0}^{N}\|u^{k+1}-u^{k}\|^{2}+\sum_{k=0}^{N}\Big[F(u^{k+1})-F(u^{k})-\frac{C(\xi_{1}^{k})}{2}\|u^{k+1}-u^{k}\|^{2}_{V}

\displaystyle\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad+G(u^{k+1})-G(u^{k})-\frac{B}{2}\|u^{k+1}-u^{k}\|^{2}\Big]

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{N}\Big[(\frac{1}{2\alpha_{0}}-\frac{B}{2})\|u^{k+1}-u^{k}\|^{2}-\frac{C(\xi^{k}_{1})}{2}\|u^{k+1}-u^{k}\|^{2}_{V}\Big]+F(u^{N+1})+G(u^{N+1})-F(u^{0})+G(u^{0}).

Thus, if
\alpha_{0}\Big(\cfrac{C(\xi)\|u^{k+1}-u^{k}\|^{2}_{V}}{\|u^{k+1}-u^{k}\|^{2}}+B\Big)\leq 1
for any
\xi=(1-\lambda)u^{k+1}+\lambda u^{k}
with
0\leq k\leq N
, we have

F(u^{N+1})+G(u^{N+1})\leq F(u^{0})+G(u^{0}).

∎

We remark that when the contrast is large, the time step in explicit scheme must be extremely small.

3.3
Partially Explicit Scheme

To find a more efficient method, we propose the partially explicit scheme. We split
V_{H}
into a direct sum of two subspace
V_{H,1}
and
V_{H,2}
, i.e.
V_{H}=V_{H,1}\oplus V_{H,2}
. Then the finite element solution is
u=u_{1}+u_{2}
with
u_{1}\in V_{H,1}
and
u_{2}\in V_{H,2}
. We will discuss the construction of these two space in Section
4.1
. Consider
\{u^{k}\}_{k=0}^{K}\subset V_{H}
such that

\displaystyle(\frac{1}{\alpha_{0}}\sum_{j=0}^{k}b_{k-j}(u^{j+1}_{1}-u^{j}_{1}+u^{j+1}_{2}-u^{j}_{2}),v_{1})+(f(u^{k+1}_{1}+u^{k}_{2}),v_{1})+(g(u^{k}_{1}+u^{k}_{2}),v_{1})=0,\quad\forall v_{1}\in V_{H,1},

\displaystyle(\frac{1}{\alpha_{0}}\sum_{j=0}^{k}b_{k-j}(u^{j+1}_{1}-u^{j}_{1}+u^{j+1}_{2}-u^{j}_{2}),v_{2})+(f(u^{k+1}_{1}+u^{k}_{2}),v_{2})+(g(u^{k}_{1}+u^{k}_{2}),v_{2})=0,\quad\forall v_{2}\in V_{H,2}.

As
V_{H,1}\cap V_{H,2}=\emptyset
, by the strengthened Cauchy-Schwarz inequality
[
2
]
, we have

\gamma:=\sup\limits_{v_{1}\in V_{H,1},v_{2}\in V_{H,2}}\cfrac{|(v_{1},v_{2})|}{\|v_{1}\|\|v_{2}\|}<1.

Theorem 3.3
.

Let
\bar{c}=\inf_{u\in V_{H}}c(u)
,
\bar{C}_{2}=\sup_{\xi\in V_{H}}C_{2}(\xi)

and

C_{2}(\xi)=\sup_{v\in V_{H},w\in V_{H,2}}\cfrac{1}{\|v\|_{V}\|w\|_{V}}\delta^{2}F(\xi)(v,w)\leq C(\xi).

If

\displaystyle\cfrac{\bar{C}_{2}}{\bar{c}}\sup\limits_{v_{2}\in V_{H,2}}\cfrac{\|v_{2}\|_{V}^{2}}{\|v_{2}\|^{2}}\leq\cfrac{1-\gamma}{\alpha_{0}}-B(1+\gamma),

(3.3)

we have

\displaystyle F(u^{N+1})+G(u^{N+1})\leq F(u^{0})+G(u^{0}),

where
0\leq N\leq K-1
and
N\in\mathbb{N}
.

Proof.

Let
v_{1}=u_{1}^{k+1}-u_{1}^{k}
and
v_{2}=u_{2}^{k+1}-u_{2}^{k}
. We obtain

\displaystyle(\frac{1}{\alpha_{0}}\sum_{j=0}^{k}b_{k-j}(u^{j+1}_{1}-u^{j}_{1}+u^{j+1}_{2}-u^{j}_{2}),u_{1}^{k+1}-u_{1}^{k})+(f(u^{k+1}_{1}+u^{k}_{2}),u_{1}^{k+1}-u_{1}^{k})+(g(u^{k}_{1}+u^{k}_{2}),u_{1}^{k+1}-u_{1}^{k})=0,

\displaystyle(\frac{1}{\alpha_{0}}\sum_{j=0}^{k}b_{k-j}(u^{j+1}_{1}-u^{j}_{1}+u^{j+1}_{2}-u^{j}_{2}),u_{2}^{k+1}-u_{2}^{k})+(f(u^{k+1}_{1}+u^{k}_{2}),u_{2}^{k+1}-u_{2}^{k})+(g(u^{k}_{1}+u^{k}_{2}),u_{2}^{k+1}-u_{2}^{k})=0.

Summing up these two equations, we get

\displaystyle(\frac{1}{\alpha_{0}}\sum_{j=0}^{k}b_{k-j}(u^{j+1}-u^{j}),u^{k+1}-u^{k})+(f(u^{k+1}_{1}+u^{k}_{2}),u^{k+1}-u^{k})+(g(u^{k}_{1}+u^{k}_{2}),u^{k+1}-u^{k})=0.

By Lemma
2.1
, we have

\displaystyle(\frac{1}{\alpha_{0}}\sum_{k=0}^{N}\sum_{j=0}^{k}b_{k-j}(u^{j+1}-u^{j}),u^{k+1}-u^{k})

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2\alpha_{0}}\sum_{k=0}^{N}\|u^{k+1}-u^{k}\|^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2\alpha_{0}}\sum_{k=0}^{N}\sum_{i=1}^{2}\|u_{i}^{k+1}-u_{i}^{k}\|^{2}+\frac{1}{2\alpha_{0}}\sum_{k=0}^{N}2(u_{1}^{k+1}-u_{1}^{k},u_{2}^{k+1}-u_{2}^{k})

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2\alpha_{0}}\sum_{k=0}^{N}\sum_{i=1}^{2}\|u_{i}^{k+1}-u_{i}^{k}\|^{2}-\frac{1}{2\alpha_{0}}\sum_{k=0}^{N}2\gamma\|u_{1}^{k+1}-u_{1}^{k}\|\|u_{2}^{k+1}-u_{2}^{k}\|

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2\alpha_{0}}\sum_{k=0}^{N}\sum_{i=1}^{2}\|u_{i}^{k+1}-u_{i}^{k}\|^{2}-\frac{\gamma}{2\alpha_{0}}\sum_{k=0}^{N}\sum_{i=1}^{2}\|u_{i}^{k+1}-u_{i}^{k}\|^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1-\gamma}{2\alpha_{0}}\sum_{k=0}^{N}\sum_{i=1}^{2}\|u_{i}^{k+1}-u_{i}^{k}\|^{2}.

There exists
\xi_{i}^{k}=\lambda_{i}^{k}u^{k}+(1-\lambda_{i}^{k})u^{k+1}
,
\lambda_{i}^{k}\in[0,1]
and

\displaystyle(f(u^{k+1}),u^{k+1}-u^{k})=F(u^{k+1})-F(u^{k})+\frac{1}{2}\delta^{2}F(\xi_{1}^{k})(u^{k+1}-u^{k},u^{k+1}-u^{k}),

Then,

\displaystyle 0=

\displaystyle(\frac{1}{\alpha_{0}}\sum_{k=0}^{N}\sum_{j=0}^{k}b_{k-j}(u^{j+1}-u^{j}),u^{k+1}-u^{k})+\sum_{k=0}^{N}(f(u_{1}^{k+1}+u_{2}^{k}),u^{k+1}-u^{k})+\sum_{k=0}^{N}(g(u_{1}^{k}+u_{2}^{k}),u^{k+1}-u^{k})

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1-\gamma}{2\alpha_{0}}\sum_{k=0}^{N}\sum_{i=1}^{2}\|u_{i}^{k+1}-u_{i}^{k}\|^{2}+\sum_{k=0}^{N}(f(u_{1}^{k+1}+u_{2}^{k})-f(u^{k+1}),u^{k+1}-u^{k})

\displaystyle\quad+\sum_{k=0}^{N}(F(u^{k+1})-F(u^{k})+\frac{1}{2}\delta^{2}F(\xi_{1}^{k})(u^{k+1}-u^{k},u^{k+1}-u^{k})

\displaystyle\quad+\sum_{k=0}^{N}(G(u^{k+1})-G(u^{k})-\frac{1}{2}\delta^{2}G(\xi_{2}^{k})(u^{k+1}-u^{k},u^{k+1}-u^{k})

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\frac{1-\gamma}{2\alpha_{0}}\sum_{k=0}^{N}\sum_{i=1}^{2}\|u_{i}^{k+1}-u_{i}^{k}\|^{2}+\sum_{k=0}^{N}(f(u_{1}^{k+1}+u_{2}^{k})-f(u^{k+1}),u^{k+1}-u^{k})

\displaystyle\quad+(F(u^{N+1})+G(u^{N+1}))-(F(u^{0})+G(u^{0}))+\sum_{k=0}^{N}\frac{c(\xi_{1}^{k})}{2}\|u^{k+1}-u^{k}\|_{V}^{2}-\sum_{k=0}^{N}\frac{B}{2}\|u^{k+1}-u^{k}\|^{2}

\displaystyle(\frac{1-\gamma}{2\alpha_{0}}-\frac{B}{2}(1+\gamma))\sum_{k=0}^{N}\sum_{i=1}^{2}\|u_{i}^{k+1}-u_{i}^{k}\|^{2}+\sum_{k=0}^{N}(f(u_{1}^{k+1}+u_{2}^{k})-f(u^{k+1}),u^{k+1}-u^{k})

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{N}(f(u^{k+1})-f(u_{1}^{k+1}+u_{2}^{k}),u^{k+1}-u^{k})\leq(\frac{1-\gamma}{2\alpha_{0}}-\frac{B}{2}(1+\gamma))\sum_{k=0}^{N}\sum_{i=1}^{2}\|u_{i}^{k+1}-u_{i}^{k}\|^{2}+\sum_{k=0}^{N}\frac{\bar{c}}{2}\|u^{k+1}-u^{k}\|_{V}^{2}.

There exists
\tilde{\xi}^{k}=\tilde{\lambda}^{k}(u_{1}^{k+1}+u_{2}^{k})+(1-\tilde{\lambda}^{k+1})u^{k+1}
with

such that

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{N}(f(u^{k+1})-f(u_{1}^{k+1}+u_{2}^{k}),u^{k+1}-u^{k})

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{N}(\delta^{2}F(\tilde{\xi}^{k})(u^{k+1}-u^{k}),u^{k+1}_{2}-u_{2}^{k})

\displaystyle\leq\sum_{k=0}^{N}C_{2}(\tilde{\xi}^{k})\|u^{k+1}-u^{k}\|_{V}\|u^{k+1}_{2}-u_{2}^{k}\|_{V}

\displaystyle\leq\sum_{k=0}^{N}\bar{C_{2}}\|u^{k+1}-u^{k}\|_{V}\|u^{k+1}_{2}-u_{2}^{k}\|_{V}

\displaystyle\leq\sum_{k=0}^{N}(\frac{\bar{c}}{2}\|u^{k+1}-u^{k}\|_{V}^{2}+\frac{\bar{C_{2}}}{2\bar{c}}\|u^{k+1}_{2}-u_{2}^{k}\|_{V}^{2}).

Thus, we need

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{N}(\frac{\bar{c}}{2}\|u^{k+1}-u^{k}\|_{V}^{2}+\frac{\bar{C_{2}}}{2\bar{c}}\|u^{k+1}_{2}-u_{2}^{k}\|_{V}^{2})\leq(\frac{1-\gamma}{2\alpha_{0}}-\frac{B}{2}(1+\gamma))\sum_{k=0}^{N}\sum_{i=1}^{2}\|u_{i}^{k+1}-u_{i}^{k}\|^{2}+\sum_{k=0}^{N}\frac{\bar{c}}{2}\|u^{k+1}-u^{k}\|_{V}^{2},

which is equivalent to

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{N}\frac{\bar{C_{2}}}{2\bar{c}}\|u^{k+1}_{2}-u_{2}^{k}\|_{V}^{2}\leq(\frac{1-\gamma}{2\alpha_{0}}-\frac{B}{2}(1+\gamma))\sum_{k=0}^{N}\|u_{1}^{k+1}-u_{1}^{k}\|^{2}+(\frac{1-\gamma}{2\alpha_{0}}-\frac{B}{2}(1+\gamma))\sum_{k=0}^{N}\|u_{2}^{k+1}-u_{2}^{k}\|^{2}.

(3.4)

Thus, if
\cfrac{\bar{C}_{2}}{\bar{c}}\sup_{v_{2}\in V_{H,2}}\cfrac{\|v_{2}\|_{V}^{2}}{\|v_{2}\|^{2}}\leq\cfrac{1-\gamma}{\alpha_{0}}-B(1+\gamma)
,
then (
3.4
) is satisfied and
we have

\displaystyle F(u^{N+1})+G(u^{N+1})\leq F(u^{0})+G(u^{0}).

∎

4.1.1
CEM method

In this section, we present the CEM finite element space.
The finite element space is constructed by solving
a constrained energy minimization problem. Let
\mathcal{T}_{H}

be a coarse grid partition of
\Omega

K_{i}\in\mathcal{T}_{H}
,
we first have to build a collection of auxiliary basis in

Let

be a partition of unity functions corresponding to an overlapping partition of the domain.
We solve the following eigenvalue problem:

\displaystyle\int_{K_{i}}\kappa\nabla\psi_{j}^{(i)}\cdot\nabla v=\lambda_{j}^{(i)}s_{i}(\psi_{j}^{(i)},v),\quad\forall v\in V(K_{i}),

where

\displaystyle s_{i}(u,v)=\int_{K_{i}}\tilde{\kappa}uv,\quad\tilde{\kappa}=\kappa H^{-2}\;\text{or}\;\tilde{\kappa}=\kappa\sum_{i}\left|\nabla\chi_{i}\right|^{2}.

We then rearrange and gather the
L_{i}
eigenfunctions corresponding to the first
L_{i}
smallest eigenvalues.
We define

V_{aux}^{(i)}:=\text{span}\{\psi_{j}^{(i)}:\;1\leq j\leq L_{i}\}.

We need to construct a projection operator
\Pi:L^{2}(\Omega)\mapsto V_{aux}\subset L^{2}(\Omega)

s(\Pi u,v)=s(u,v),\quad\forall v\in V_{aux}:=\sum_{i=1}^{N_{e}}V_{aux}^{(i)},

with

and
N_{e}
being the number of coarse elements.
We define

to be an oversampling domain of
K_{i}

K_{i}

[

For every auxiliary basis
\psi_{j}^{(i)}

\displaystyle a(\phi_{j}^{(i)},v)+s(\mu_{j}^{(i)},v)

\displaystyle=0,\quad\forall v\in V(K_{i}^{+}),

\displaystyle s(\phi_{j}^{(i)},\nu)

\displaystyle=s(\psi_{j}^{(i)},\nu),\quad\forall\nu\in V_{aux}(K_{i}^{+}),

for some
\mu_{j}^{(i)}\in V_{aux}

\displaystyle:=\text{span}\{\phi_{j}^{(i)}:\;1\leq i\leq N_{e},1\leq j\leq L_{i}\}.

5
Conclusions

In this paper, we propose a partially explicit scheme to solve the time fractional nonlinear diffusion reaction equation. This work continues the earlier works on linear problems. The explicit scheme requires extremely small time step because of high contrast and time fractional power. We formulate a condition on the spaces for the partially explicit scheme to be stable. We introduce a way to split
V_{H}
and construct
V_{H,1}
and
V_{H,2}
which satisfy the stability condition. We find that the time step in the partially explicit scheme scales as the coarse mesh size, which provides computational savings. The main advantage
of our scheme is that it handles additional degrees of freedom
(those beyond few coarse-grid degrees of freedom) explicitly for
nonlinear diffusion and reaction terms.
From the numerical experiments, we conclude that the partially explicit scheme can obtain similar accuracy as the fully implicit CEM scheme with additional basis.
