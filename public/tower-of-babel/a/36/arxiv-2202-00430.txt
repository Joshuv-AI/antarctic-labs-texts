Derivation functors and Lusztig’s induction functors Thanks: This work was supported by National Natural Science Foundation of China (No. 11771445)
Minghui Zhao

Abstract
. Lusztig proved the compatibility of induction functors and restriction functors for Lusztig’s perverse sheaves. Fang-Lan-Xiao established a categorification of Green’s formula and gave a sheaf-level proof of this compatibility for all semisimple complexes. As an application, we study the relation between induction functors and derivation functors, which is a kind of special restriction functors.

1.
Introduction

1.1.

Let
\mathbf{U}
be a quantum group associated to a quiver
Q=(I,H)
and
\mathbf{U}^{-}
be its negative part. The algebra
\mathbf{U}^{-}
is defined by the generators
F_{i}
for
i\in I
and quantum Serre relations.
In
[
9
]
, Ringel introduced the twisted Ringel-Hall algebra
H^{\ast}_{q}(Q)
and proved that the composition subalgebra of
H^{\ast}_{q}(Q)
is isomorphic to a specilization of
\mathbf{U}^{-}
. In
[
4
]
, Green introduced the comultiplication and showed that the comultiplication is an algebra homomorphism by using Green’s formula.

Fix an
I
-graded vector space
{\mathbf{V}}=\bigoplus_{i\in I}{\mathbf{V}}_{i}
with dimension vector
\nu\in\mathbb{N}I
and denote by
E_{{\mathbf{V}}}
the variety consisting of representations of the quiver
Q
.
There is a
G_{\mathbf{V}}=\prod_{i\in I}GL({\mathbf{V}}_{i})
action on
E_{\mathbf{V}}
.
Denote by
{\mathcal{D}}_{G_{\mathbf{V}}}^{b}(E_{\mathbf{V}})
the
G_{\mathbf{V}}
-equivariant bounded derived category of
\overline{{\mathbb{Q}}}_{l}
-constructible mixed complexes on
E_{\mathbf{V}}

and by
{\mathcal{D}}_{G_{\mathbf{V}}}^{b,ss}(E_{\mathbf{V}})
the subcategory of
{\mathcal{D}}_{G_{\mathbf{V}}}^{b}(E_{\mathbf{V}})
consisting of semisimple complexes (see
[
5
,
1
]
).

Inspired by the work of Ringel,
Lusztig (
[
6
,
7
]
) gave a categorification of
\mathbf{U}_{\nu}^{-}
by using an additive subcategory
\mathcal{Q}_{\nu}
of
{\mathcal{D}}_{G_{\mathbf{V}}}^{b,ss}(E_{\mathbf{V}})
.
The multiplication and comultiplication in
\mathbf{U}^{-}
are induced by induction functors
\mathrm{Ind}^{\alpha+\beta}_{\alpha,\beta}

and restriction functors
\mathrm{Res}^{\alpha+\beta}_{\alpha,\beta}
, respectively.

Let
K_{\nu}
be the Grothendieck group of the category
{\mathcal{D}}_{G_{\mathbf{V}}}^{b,ss}(E_{\mathbf{V}})
and
\mathbf{K}=\bigoplus_{\nu\in{\mathbb{N}}I}K_{\nu}.

The functors
A\ast B=\mathrm{Ind}^{\alpha+\beta}_{\alpha,\beta}(A\boxtimes B)
induce a multiplication on
\mathbf{K}
.
The algebra
\mathbf{K}
contains
\mathbf{U}^{-}
as a subalgebra (
[
11
]
).

Fix
\alpha,\beta,\alpha^{\prime},\beta^{\prime}\in\mathbb{N}I
such that
\alpha+\beta=\alpha^{\prime}+\beta^{\prime}
.
Let
\mathcal{N}
be the set of
(\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2},\beta_{1},\beta_{2})\in(\mathbb{N}I)^{4}
such that
\alpha_{1}+\alpha_{2}=\alpha,\beta_{1}+\beta_{2}=\beta,\alpha_{1}+\beta_{1}=\alpha^{\prime},\alpha_{2}+\beta_{2}=\beta^{\prime}
.
For any
A\in\mathcal{Q}_{{\alpha}}
and
B\in\mathcal{Q}_{{\beta}}
, Lusztig (
[
7
]
) proved the compatibility of induction functors and restriction functors:

(1)

\displaystyle\mathrm{Res}^{\alpha^{\prime}+\beta^{\prime}}_{\alpha^{\prime},\beta^{\prime}}\mathrm{Ind}^{\alpha+\beta}_{\alpha,\beta}(A\boxtimes B)

\displaystyle\simeq

\displaystyle\bigoplus_{\lambda\in\mathcal{N}}(\mathrm{Ind}^{\alpha^{\prime}}_{\alpha_{1},\beta_{1}}\times\mathrm{Ind}^{\beta^{\prime}}_{\alpha_{2},\beta_{2}})(\tau_{\lambda})_{!}(\mathrm{Res}^{\alpha}_{\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2}}(A)\times\mathrm{Res}^{\beta}_{\beta_{1},\beta_{2}}(B))[-(\alpha_{2},\beta_{1})](-\frac{(\alpha_{2},\beta_{1})}{2}).

Formula (
1
) implies that the comultiplication on
\mathbf{U}^{-}
is a homomorphism of algebras.

By Green’s formula and the sheaf-function correspondence, Xiao-Xu-Zhao (
[
11
]
) proved that Formula (
1
) holds for all
A\in{\mathcal{D}}_{G_{{\mathbf{V}}_{\alpha}}}^{b,ss}(E_{{\mathbf{V}}_{\alpha}})
and
B\in{\mathcal{D}}_{G_{{\mathbf{V}}_{\beta}}}^{b,ss}(E_{{\mathbf{V}}_{\beta}})
.
In
[
3
]
, Fang-Lan-Xiao established a categorification of Green’s formula and gave a sheaf-level proof of Formula (
1
) for all
A\in{\mathcal{D}}_{G_{{\mathbf{V}}_{\alpha}}}^{b,ss}(E_{{\mathbf{V}}_{\alpha}})
and
B\in{\mathcal{D}}_{G_{{\mathbf{V}}_{\beta}}}^{b,ss}(E_{{\mathbf{V}}_{\beta}})
.

1.2.

For any
i\in I
, Lusztig (
[
8
]
) introduced derivations
{{}_{i}r}:\mathbf{U}^{-}\rightarrow\mathbf{U}^{-}
and
{r_{i}}:\mathbf{U}^{-}\rightarrow\mathbf{U}^{-}
such that

(2)

{{}_{i}r}^{m}(xy)=\sum_{t=0}^{m}v^{(\nu-ti,(m-t)i)+t(m-t)}\frac{[m]_{v}!}{[t]_{v}![m-t]_{v}!}{{}_{i}r}^{t}(x){{}_{i}r}^{m-t}(y)

and

(3)

{r^{m}_{i}}(xy)=\sum_{t=0}^{m}v^{(ti,\nu^{\prime}-(m-t)i)+t(m-t)}\frac{[m]_{v}!}{[t]_{v}![m-t]_{v}!}{r_{i}^{t}}(x){r_{i}^{m-t}}(y)

for

and

. As a generalization, Chen-Xiao (

) introduced derivations

and
{\delta_{i}}:H^{\ast}_{q}(Q)\rightarrow H^{\ast}_{q}(Q)
satisfying similar relations.

Denote by

and
{\mathcal{R}_{mi}}=\mathrm{Res}^{\alpha}_{\alpha-mi,mi}
the derivation functors, which induce derivations
{{}_{i}r}
and
{r_{i}}
on
\mathbf{K}
such that

{{}_{i}r^{m}}([A])=v^{\frac{m(m-1)}{2}}[D({{}_{mi}\mathcal{R}}(DA))]

and

{r_{i}^{m}}([A])=v^{\frac{m(m-1)}{2}}[D({\mathcal{R}_{mi}}(DA))]

for any
[A]\in\mathbf{K}
, where

is the Verdier duality on
{\mathcal{D}}_{G_{{\mathbf{V}}}}^{b}(E_{{\mathbf{V}}})
.

Fix
A\in{\mathcal{D}}_{G_{{\mathbf{V}}_{\alpha}}}^{b,ss}(E_{{\mathbf{V}}_{\alpha}})
and
B\in{\mathcal{D}}_{G_{{\mathbf{V}}_{\beta}}}^{b,ss}(E_{{\mathbf{V}}_{\beta}})

Formula (
1

and

(5)

{\mathcal{R}_{mi}}(A\ast B)\simeq\bigoplus_{t=a}^{b}\mathbf{1}_{pt}^{f_{m,t}(v)}\boxtimes({\mathcal{R}_{ti}}(A)\ast{\mathcal{R}_{(m-t)i}}(B))[-P^{\prime}_{t}](-\frac{P^{\prime}_{t}}{2}),

when
\alpha^{\prime}=mi
and
\beta^{\prime}=mi
, respectively.
Here,
\alpha=\sum_{i\in I}\alpha_{i}i
,
\beta=\sum_{i\in I}\beta_{i}i
,

,

,

and

.

Formulas (

{{}_{i}r}
and

on
