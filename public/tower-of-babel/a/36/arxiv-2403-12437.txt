Irreducibility and Rigidity in Digital Images
Laurence Boxer

Abstract
We study how the properties of irreducibility and rigidity in digital images interact with Cartesian products, wedges, and cold and freezing sets. Key words and phrases: digital topology, digital image, freezing set, reducible, rigid MSC: 54B20, 54C35

2.1
Adjacencies

This section is largely
quoted or paraphrased
from
[

]
.

Let
u,n\in{\mathbb{N}}
,
1\leq u\leq n
.
For
X\subset{\mathbb{Z}}^{n}
,

x=(x_{1},\ldots,x_{n}),~y=(y_{1},\ldots,y_{n})\in X

are

-adjacent if and only if

•

x\neq y
, and

•

for at most
u

\mid x_{i}-y_{i}\mid=1
, and

•

for all indices
j
such that

\mid x_{j}-y_{j}\mid\neq 1
, we have

x_{j}=y_{j}
.

The
c_{u}
adjacencies are the adjacencies most used
in digital topology, especially
c_{1}
and
c_{n}
.

In low dimensions, it is also common to denote a

c_{u}
adjacency by the number of points that can
have this adjacency with a given point in
{\mathbb{Z}}^{n}
. E.g.,

•

in
{\mathbb{Z}}
,
c_{1}
-adjacency is 2-adjacency;

•

in
{\mathbb{Z}}^{2}
,
c_{1}
-adjacency is 4-adjacency and

c_{2}
-adjacency is 8-adjacency;

•

in
{\mathbb{Z}}^{3}
,
c_{1}
-adjacency is 6-adjacency,

c_{2}
-adjacency is 18-adjacency, and

c_{3}
-adjacency is 26-adjacency.

We use the notations
y\leftrightarrow_{\kappa}x
, or, when
the adjacency
\kappa
can be assumed,
y\leftrightarrow x
, to mean

x
and
y
are
\kappa
-adjacent.
The notations
y\leftrightarroweq_{\kappa}x
, or, when

\kappa
can be assumed,
y\leftrightarroweq x
, mean either

y=x
or
y\leftrightarrow_{\kappa}x
.

A sequence
P=\{y_{i}\}_{i=0}^{m}
in a digital image
(X,\kappa)
is
a
\kappa
-path from
a\in X
to
b\in X
if

a=y_{0}
,
b=y_{m}
, and
y_{i}\leftrightarroweq_{\kappa}y_{i+1}

for
0\leq i<m
.

X
is
\kappa
-connected

[
17
]
,
or
connected
when
\kappa

is understood, if for every pair of points

there
exists a
\kappa
-path in

from
a
to
b
.

A
(digital)
\kappa
-closed curve
is a

S=\{s_{i}\}_{i=0}^{m-1}
such that
s_{0}\leftrightarrow_{\kappa}s_{m-1}
,
and

implies
s_{i}\neq s_{j}
. If also

\kappa

Definition 2.1
.

[
3
]
(generalizing a definition of
[

]

Let

A function

\kappa

\lambda

When

\kappa

-continuous

When the adjacency relations are understood, we will simply say that

Theorem 2.2
.

[
17
,
3
]

A function

x\leftrightarrow x^{\prime}
in

f(x)\leftrightarroweq f(x^{\prime})
.

See also
[

A digital

(\kappa,\lambda)
-continuous

-continuous.

A

Let

The

A homotopy between continuous functions may be thought of as
a continuous deformation of one of the functions into the

[

For Cartesian products of digital images

if and only if

•

x_{i}\leftrightarrow_{\kappa_{i}}x_{i}^{\prime}
, and

•

x_{j}=x_{j}^{\prime}
.

.

[
11
]

Let
(X_{i},\kappa_{i})

be a digital image,

Let

(X,NP_{v}(\kappa_{1},\ldots,\kappa_{v}))

then each
(X_{i},\kappa_{i})

is rigid.

•

f(y)=H(y,1)\in S\setminus\{x_{0}\}
