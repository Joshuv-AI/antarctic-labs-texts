Importance of the heavy-quarks longitudinal structure function measurements at future circular collider energies Preprint: APS/123-QED
G.R.Boroun

Abstract
In this article, we consider the ratio of structure functions for heavy quark pair production at low values of x x . The importance of this ratio for charm and beauty pair production is examined according to the Hadron Electron Ring Accelerator (HERA) data. The behavior of these ratios considers due to the hard-pomeron behavior of the gluon distribution function. The results are in good agreement with respect to the HERA data. Expanding this data to the range of new energies underscore the importance of these measurements for heavy quarks. The ratio of charm and beauty structure functions at the proposed Large Hadron electron Collider (LHeC) is considered as a function of invariant center-of-mass energy. For top pair production this ratio is extracted with known kinematics of the LHeC and Future Circular Collider electron-hadron (FCC-eh) colliders. Comparison of the results obtained for the ratio of top structure functions in LHeC and FCC-eh are proportional to the specified inelasticity y y range.

perturbative scales
\mu^{2}

3. Method

Based on the hard-pomeron behavior of the distribution functions,
the ratio of heavy-quark structure functions is formulated based
on the coefficient functions and gluon exponent. After doing the
integration over
z
, the ratio
F_{L}/F_{2}
for heavy quarks can
be rewritten in a convolution form as the ratio

F_{L}^{\mathcal{Q}\overline{\mathcal{Q}}}/F_{2}^{\mathcal{Q}\overline{\mathcal{Q}}}

is defined by

\displaystyle\frac{F_{L}^{\mathcal{Q}\overline{\mathcal{Q}}}}{F_{2}^{\mathcal{Q}\overline{\mathcal{Q}}}}=\frac{C_{L,g}^{Q\overline{Q}}(x,\xi){\odot}x^{\lambda_{g}}}{C_{2,g}^{Q\overline{Q}}(x,\xi){\odot}x^{\lambda_{g}}},

(14)

where
[f{\odot}g](x)=\int_{x}^{1}(dy/y)f(y)g(y)
. In the
analytical form, power-like behavior of the heavy-quark structure
functions is generically written as follows

\displaystyle\frac{\partial}{\partial{\ln}\frac{1}{x}}{\ln}\frac{F_{L}^{\mathcal{Q}\overline{\mathcal{Q}}}(x,Q^{2})}{F_{2}^{\mathcal{Q}\overline{\mathcal{Q}}}(x,Q^{2})}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\lambda_{L}^{\mathcal{Q}\overline{\mathcal{Q}}}-\lambda_{2}^{\mathcal{Q}\overline{\mathcal{Q}}}

\displaystyle=\frac{\partial}{\partial{\ln}\frac{1}{x}}{\ln}\frac{C_{L,g}^{\mathcal{Q}\overline{\mathcal{Q}}}(x,\xi){\odot}x^{\lambda_{g}}}{C_{2,g}^{\mathcal{Q}\overline{\mathcal{Q}}}(x,\xi){\odot}x^{\lambda_{g}}}.

The exponents
\lambda_{L}
and
\lambda_{2}
for heavy-quark
production are defined by the derivatives of the heavy-quark
structure functions by the following forms

\displaystyle\lambda_{L}^{\mathcal{Q}\overline{\mathcal{Q}}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle{\partial\ln F_{L}^{\mathcal{Q}\overline{\mathcal{Q}}}(x,Q^{2})}/{\partial\ln(1/x)},

\displaystyle\lambda_{2}^{\mathcal{Q}\overline{\mathcal{Q}}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle{\partial\ln F_{2}^{\mathcal{Q}\overline{\mathcal{Q}}}(x,Q^{2})}/{\partial\ln(1/x)}.

(16)

The importance of the relationship between

\sigma^{\mathcal{Q}\overline{\mathcal{Q}}}_{\mathrm{red}}
and

F_{2}^{\mathcal{Q}\overline{\mathcal{Q}}}(x,Q^{2})
in Eq.(4)
depends on the functions of

5. Summary and Conclusion

We presented the ratio

F_{L}^{\mathcal{Q}\overline{\mathcal{Q}}}/F_{2}^{\mathcal{Q}\overline{\mathcal{Q}}}

for charm and beauty pair production with respect to HERA data.
The behavior of these ratios are in good agreement in comparison
with experimental data in a wide range of
W^{2}
values. Indeed
a power-law behavior for the ratio of structure functions for
heavy quark pair production is predicted. Results as a function of

Q^{2}
for an invariant constant value are in agreement with
those extracted in literature in the framework of perturbative
QCD. Then we have studied the production of heavy-pair quarks in
new electron proton collisions (i.e., LHeC and FCC-eh). The ratio
of charm and beauty structure functions are studied in center of
mass energy
\sqrt{s}=1.3~\mathrm{TeV}
which proposed at LHeC
collider. For the top quark pair production, which will be one
kind of important production channel at LHeC and FCC-eh, the ratio
of structure functions determined and compared together with
respect to the center-of-mass energies in new colliders. The
results of numerical calculations for heavy quarks in LHeC and
FCC-eh are available with respect to the inelasticity defined in
accordance with the center of mass energies. These results
highlight the importance of measuring the longitudinal structure
function in the production of heavy quarks in the future with
respect to the energies available in the new accelerators.
