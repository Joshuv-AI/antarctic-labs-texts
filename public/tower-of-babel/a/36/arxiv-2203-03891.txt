Abstract
. Let D D be an open set of ℝ d \mathbb{R}^{d} , α ∈ ( 0 , 2 ) \alpha\in(0,2) and let ℒ α D \mathcal{L}_{\alpha}^{D} be the generator of the censored α \alpha -stable process in D D . In this paper, we establish sharp two-sided heat kernel estimates for ℒ α D − κ \mathcal{L}_{\alpha}^{D}-\kappa , with κ \kappa being a non-negative critical potential and D D being a C 1 , β C^{1,\beta} open set, β ∈ ( ( α − 1 ) + , 1 ] \beta\in((\alpha-1)_{+},1] . The potential κ \kappa can exhibit multi-singularities and our regularity assumption on D D is weaker than the regularity assumed in earlier literature on heat kernel estimates of fractional Laplacians.



1.
Introduction

The transition density of a Markov process
\{X_{t}\}_{t\geq 0}
, when it exists, encapsulates all the statistical information about the process. For a few notable examples, such as Brownian motion, the Cauchy process, and the Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process, explicit formulas for the transition density are known. However, for most Markov processes, such explicit expressions are not available. The transition density, often referred to as the heat kernel of the process’s generator, plays a central role in both analysis and probability theory. Consequently, deriving sharp two-sided estimates for heat kernels has become a significant focus of research in these fields.

Assume
\alpha\in(0,2)
. An isotropic
\alpha
-stable process
\{Z_{t}\}_{t\geq 0}
on
\mathbb{R}^{d}
is a Lévy process with

\mathbb{E}\exp(i\xi\cdot(Z_t-Z_0))=\exp(-t|\xi|^{\alpha}),\quad\xi\in\mathbb{R}^{d},t\geq 0.

The generator of
Z
is the fractional Laplacian

-(-\Delta)^{\alpha/2}
which can be written as

-(-\Delta)^{\alpha/2}f(x)=\mathcal{A}(d,-\alpha)\text{ p.v.}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{d}}\frac{f(y)-f(x)}{|y-x|^{d+\alpha}}dy,

where

(1.1)

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}(d,-\alpha)=\frac{\alpha 2^{\alpha-1}\Gamma((\alpha+d)/2)}{\pi^{d/2}\Gamma(1-\alpha/2)}.

It is well known (see
[
3
]
for instance) that the heat kernel
p(t,x,y)
of

Z
has the following estimates:
there exists
\delta\in(0,1]
such that

\delta\big(t^{-d/\alpha}\wedge\frac{t}{|x-y|^{d+\alpha}}\big)\leq p(t,x,y)\leq\delta^{-1}\big(t^{-d/\alpha}\wedge\frac{t}{|x-y|^{d+\alpha}}\big),

for all
(t,x,y)\in(0,\infty)\times\mathbb{R}^{d}\times\mathbb{R}^{d}
.
In this paper, we will use

A\asymp B
to denote that
A/B
is bounded between two positive constants. So the estimates above can be written as
p(t,x,y)\asymp t^{-d/\alpha}\wedge\frac{t}{|x-y|^{d+\alpha}}
.

Suppose
D
is an open subset of
\mathbb{R}^{d}
and

is the killed
\alpha
-stable process in

has a heat kernel

were established only recently in
[
7
]
. The main result of
[
7
]
is as follows.
If

is a

admits the following estimates:

•

For any
T>0
, on
(0,T)\times D\times D

(1.2)

p^{D}(t,x,y)\asymp\left(1\wedge\frac{\delta_{D}(x)}{t^{1/\alpha}}\right)^{\frac{\alpha}{2}}\left(1\wedge\frac{\delta_{D}(y)}{t^{1/\alpha}}\right)^{\frac{\alpha}{2}}p(t,x,y),

where
\delta_{D}(x)
denotes the distance between
x
and
D^{c}
.

•

Suppose in addition that
D
is bounded. Then for any
T>0
, on
[T,\infty)\times D\times D
,

(1.3)

p^{D}(t,x,y)\asymp e^{-\lambda_{1}t}\delta_{D}(x)^{\frac{\alpha}{2}}\delta_{D}(y)^{\frac{\alpha}{2}},

where

is the largest eigenvalue of

In the case when

-fat open set,

In

Remark 2.7

(B.3)

\displaystyle I_{2}\gtrsim-c_{n}\delta_{D_{\kappa}}(z-u)^{p_{j}-\alpha-1}-\delta_{D_{\kappa}}(z-u)^{p_{j}}\gtrsim-\frac{c_{n}}{a_{n}^{-p_{j}+\alpha+1}}-\delta_{D_{\kappa}}(z-u)^{p_{j}}.
