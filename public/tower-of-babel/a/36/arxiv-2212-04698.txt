Pseudo scalar dark matter in a generic U ( 1 ) X (1)_{X} model Preprint: EPHOU-21-017
Shivam Gola

Abstract
We consider a U ​ ( 1 ) X U(1)_{X} extension of the Standard Model (SM), where the spontaneous breaking of U ​ ( 1 ) X U(1)_{X} gauge group results in a pseudo scalar particle which is the proposed candidate for dark matter. In the model, we introduce three right-handed neutrinos (RHNs) N R i N_{R}^{i} and two extra scalars Φ \Phi , χ \chi , which are SM gauge singlets but charged under U ​ ( 1 ) X U(1)_{X} gauge group. Right-handed neutrinos are required to have the model anomaly free and explain the neutrino oscillation data. The heaviest scalar breaks the U ​ ( 1 ) X U(1)_{X} gauge symmetry and the other extra scalar gives a pseudo scalar DM candidate. A pseudo scalar dark matter (DM) is an interesting candidate as it naturally evades the stringent direct detection bounds due to its coupling structure. We study the phenomenology of this pseudo-scalar DM while considering several theoretical and experimental constraints. We find that in our model, there is a feasible parameter space, which satisfies by the DM lifetime bound, relic and direct detection constraints while respecting the colliders and other bounds. Keywords: Right handed neutrinos, pseudo scalar, Dark Matter, U(1) X gauge

3.1
Vacuum Stability condition

The scalar potential

must be bounded from below
[

and it can be determined from the following symmetric matrix which comes from the quadratic part of the potential,

\displaystyle V^{4}_{s}=\begin{pmatrix}\lambda_{H}&\frac{\lambda_{H\Phi}}{2}&\frac{\lambda_{H\chi}}{2}\\
\frac{\lambda_{H\Phi}}{2}&\lambda_{\Phi}&\frac{\lambda_{\Phi\chi}}{2}\\
\frac{\lambda_{H\chi}}{2}&\frac{\lambda_{\Phi\chi}}{2}&\lambda_{\chi}\\
\end{pmatrix}

(44)

Above matrix will be positive-definite if following conditions are satisfied,

\displaystyle\lambda_{H}>0,\hskip 28.45274pt4\lambda_{H}\lambda_{\Phi}-\lambda_{H\Phi}^{2}>0,

\displaystyle(-\lambda_{H}\lambda_{\Phi\chi}^{2}+\lambda_{H\Phi}\lambda_{\Phi\chi}\lambda_{H\chi}-\lambda_{\Phi}\lambda_{H\chi}^{2}+4\lambda_{H}\lambda_{\Phi}\lambda_{\chi}-\lambda_{H\Phi}^{2}\lambda_{\chi})>0.

(45)

45

Conclusion

We have considered a generic
U(1)_{X}
extension of SM which accommodates a pseudo scalar DM candidate and neutrino mass generation mechanism. In the model, we have three RHNs and two complex scalars
\Phi
,
\chi
additionally and all are charged under the
U(1)_{X}
gauge group.
U(1)_{X}
gauge is broken by the vev of
\Phi
.
\chi
field also gets vev

which results in a massive pseudo scalar particle. The pseudo scalar has coupling with Higgs and neutral gauge bosons and its phenomenology is controlled by a few parameters such as
x_{H}
,
\kappa
,
m_{h_{i}}
,
g^{\prime}_{1}
,
M_{Z^{\prime}}
and
m_{A}
. We have found that pseudo scalar have sufficient parameter space which is satisfied by DM lifetime constraint while remaining consistent. We found the allowed parameter space by the relic density constraints, Invisible Higgs width constraint and other bounds. Direct detection check is also done in our model. The tree-level DM-nucleon scattering amplitude vanishes in non-relativistic limit but one loop contribution is finite. We have shown the one-loop contribution to scattering cross-section against the experimental limit from XENON1T, LZ and neutrino floors from five different targets. In our model, the pseudo scalar DM have sufficient parameter space which is consistent with several theoretical and experimental constraints and can be tested at future colliders and direct detection experiments.
