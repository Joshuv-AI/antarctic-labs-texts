2.1.
Quasi-convexity and operator monotone functions

In some problems of approximation

Theorem 1

Let

\displaystyle f(t)=\alpha+\beta t+\int_{0}^{\infty}\left(\frac{\lambda}{\lambda^{2}+1}-\frac{1}{\lambda+t}\right)d\mu(\lambda),
