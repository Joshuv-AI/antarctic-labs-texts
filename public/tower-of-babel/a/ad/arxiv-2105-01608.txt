On the duality between homological quantum codes of a hypermap and its dual hypermap
Zihan Lei

Abstract
From a given topological hypermap H H , we define two related hypermaps H △ H^{\triangle} and H ∇ H^{\nabla} as complements of the ordinary dual hypermap H ∗ H^{*} along with the concepts of their edge hypermap quantum codes 𝒞 △ \mathcal{C}^{\triangle} and 𝒞 ∇ \mathcal{C}^{\nabla} . We then show that, when the sets of special darts are naturally corresponded, the duality between the ordinary hypermap quantum code 𝒞 \mathcal{C} from H H and the one 𝒞 ∗ \mathcal{C}^{*} from H ∗ H^{*} can be greatly simplified to the duality between 𝒞 △ \mathcal{C}^{\triangle} and 𝒞 ∇ \mathcal{C}^{\nabla} .



Keywords
hypermap, quantum code, surface code, orientable surface, bipartite graph
