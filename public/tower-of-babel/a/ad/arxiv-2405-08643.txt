On the positivity of MS ¯ \overline{\rm{MS}} distributions
Felix Hekhorn

Abstract
We discuss the positivity of parton distribution functions using the common MS ¯ \overline{\rm{MS}} factorization scheme. We find that in the perturbative regime MS ¯ \overline{\rm{MS}} PDFs inherit the strict positivity of physical PDFs. We explicitly discuss the scheme transformation by using suitable physical observables and find that MS ¯ \overline{\rm{MS}} PDFs are positive above the scale Q 2 ≳ 5 GeV Q^{2}\gtrsim$5\text{\,}\mathrm{GeV}$ . Finally, we comment on the direct counterpart of longitudinally polarized PDFs, finding agreement with the unpolarized counterpart.

The above discussion holds independently of the spin-alignment of the scattering parton and its parent hadron
