The square of every subcubic planar graph of girth at least 6 is 7-choosable
Seog-Jin Kim, Xiaopan Lian

Abstract
The square of a graph G G , denoted G 2 G^{2} , has the same vertex set as G G and has an edge between two vertices if the distance between them in G G is at most 2 2 . Thomassen [ 15 ] and Hartke, Jahanbekam and Thomas [ 7 ] proved that χ ⁡ ( G 2 ) ≤ 7 \chi(G^{2})\leq 7 if G G is a subcubic planar graph. A natural question is whether χ ℓ ​ ( G 2 ) ≤ 7 \chi_{\ell}(G^{2})\leq 7 or not if G G is a subcubic planar graph. It was showed in [ 5 ] that χ ℓ ​ ( G 2 ) ≤ 7 \chi_{\ell}(G^{2})\leq 7 if G G is a subcubic planar graph of girth at least 7. We prove that χ ℓ ​ ( G 2 ) ≤ 7 \chi_{\ell}(G^{2})\leq 7 if G G is a subcubic planar graph of girth at least 6.

The

[

Bousquet, Deschamps, Meyer and Pierron

11
, the 2-vertices are only possibly contained in the cycles
D_{1},\ldots,D_{k}
. Hence, the number of 2-vertices contained in
C
is at most
\sum^{k}_{i=1}\lfloor\frac{|E(D_{i})|}{4}\rfloor\leq\lfloor\frac{\sum^{k}_{i=1}{|E(D_{i})|}}{4}\rfloor\leq\lfloor\frac{|E(W)|}{4}\rfloor=\lfloor\frac{d(C)}{4}\rfloor
.
Therefore, there are at most
\lfloor\frac{d(C)}{4}\rfloor
2-vertices on the boundary of
C
.
Thus Claim
12
holds.
∎

Now, we consider the case when
d(C)\geq 7
. If
C
is a 7-cycle, then
C
has at most one 2-vertex. So,
\omega^{\prime}(C)\geq d(C)-6-1=0
.
If
C
is a cycle of length at least 8, then
\omega^{\prime}(x)\geq 0
since
d(C)-6-\lfloor\frac{d(C)}{4}\rfloor\geq 0
. Hence
\omega^{\prime}(x)\geq 0
for every
x\in F(G)
.

Hence, by (1) and (2), we have that
\omega^{\prime}(x)\geq 0
for every
x\in V(G)\cup F(G)
. This completes the proof of Theorem
3
.
∎
