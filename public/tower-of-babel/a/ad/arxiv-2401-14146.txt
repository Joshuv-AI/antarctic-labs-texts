Cohomology of toric diagrams
Grigory Solomadin

Abstract
. In this note, integral cohomology of homotopy colimits for toric diagrams and their classifying spaces over regular CW posets are described in terms of sheaf cohomology. Split T T -CW-complexes with CW orbit poset C C have such decomposition (up to a homeomorphism) in terms of a T T -diagram D D over C C . Equivariant formality for hocolim ⁡ D \hoc D is equivalent to H o ​ d ​ d ​ ( hocolim ⁡ D ) = 0 H^{odd}(\hoc D)=0 (over ℚ \mathbb{Q} , or over ℤ \mathbb{Z} for connected stabilizers) provided that C C is an oriented homology manifold. The integral singular cohomology groups and bigraded Betti numbers are computed in this setting. Similar descriptions are provided for skeleta of toric manifolds and compact nonsingular toric varieties. The cohomological orbit spectral sequence collapse over ℤ \mathbb{Z} at the second page is proved for any compact toric variety.

1.
Introduction

Let
X^{2n}
be a compact oriented manifold with an effective action of the compact torus
T=(S^{1})^{k}
, with a non-empty and finite
T
-fixed point set.
Properties of the orbit space
Q=X/T
(a Hausdorff space) and of the Borel equivariant cohomology
H^{*}_{T}(X)
are closely related to the properties of the cohomology ring
H^{*}(X;\ \mathbb{Z})
under certain assumptions.
For instance, consider the case of a
torus manifold
introduced by Hattori and Masuda in
[
ha-ma-03
]
, i.e. when the
complexity

n-k
of the action is zero.
The condition on the isotropy subgroup of
x\in X
to have a fixed dimension cuts a subset in
X
.
By definition, the connected components of this subset project onto
open faces
by the map
X\to Q
.
The closure of an open face in
Q
is called a
face
of the
T
-space
X
.
The
orbit poset
of
X
is given by the partial order opposite to inclusion on the set of faces of
X
.
The
T
-action on
X
is called
locally standard
if there exists an atlas of
T
-invariant charts covering
X
such that each chart of the atlas is
T
-equivariantly homeomorphic to the standard rotational
T^{n}
-action on
\mathbb{C}^{n}
at the origin.
The vanishing
H^{odd}(X;\ \mathbb{Z})=0
for a torus manifold is equivalent to acyclicity of proper (i.e
\neq Q
) faces of
X
, together with local standardness condition by
[
ma-pa-06
]
.
The orbit space of an equivariantly formal torus manifold is acyclic by
[
ma-pa-06
]
.
Local standardness implies that
Q
is a manifold with corners and therefore a homology manifold.
The class of torus manifolds with acyclic proper faces was studied in a series of works
[
ay-17
,
ay-16
]
.
Any
T
-space from this class is equivariantly homeomorphic to the
quotient construction

Q\times T/\sim
, see
[
da-ja-91
,
yo-11
]
.
In
[
ay-17
,
ay-16
]
, the orbit spectral sequences for the quotient construction and
Q\times T
were compared, and cellular sheaves over Alexandrov spaces (see
[
cu-14
]
) were applied.
Betti numbers for those of such spaces that admit a
characteristic function
were computed in
[
ay-17
,
ay-16
]
.
The orbit spectral sequence for a torus manifold has nontrivial differentials of order
\geqslant 2
, in general.
In the particular case of a complete (possibly, singular) toric variety the orbit spectral sequence (over the field of complex numbers) arose in the work of Danilov
[
da-78
]
.
He proved the collapse of this spectral sequence at the second page (i.e. all differentials of order
\geqslant 2
are zero).
The similar result in Borel-Moore homology with rational coefficients was proved in
[
to-14
, §5]
.

The cohomological Eilenberg-Moore spectral sequence, e.g. see
[
mcc-01
]
, (EMSS, for short) of the Borel fibration for a
T
-space
X
starts from the bigraded components of the
\Tor
-module

\Tor_{H^{*}(BT)}(H^{*}_{T}(X),\mathbb{Z})
at the second page and converges to
H^{*}(X)
.
The multiplication of this spectral sequence is known to have a nontrivial extension, in general (e.g. see
[
fr-21
]
).
In the case of a
partial quotient of a moment-angle complex
the rigorous formula identifying the cup-product with the
twisted product
on
\Tor
-groups was recently obtained in
[
fr-21
]
.
The homotopy type of the skeleton for a complex projective space
\mathbb{C}P^{m-1}
(a partial quotient with a
T^{m-1}
-action) was obtained in
[
fu-19
]
.
Together with
[
gr-th-07
]
, this leads to the computation of Betti numbers of such space.

By definition
[
go-ko-mc-98
]
, a
T
-space is
equivariantly formal
if the Serre spectral sequence of the corresponding Borel fibration collapses at the second page.
Equivariant formality is equivalent to vanishing of all higher
\Tor
-groups from the second page of the corresponding Eilenberg-Moore spectral sequence.
The equivariant cohomology ring of any compact equivariantly formal toric orbifold with vanishing odd cohomology was described in
[
fr-10
]
in terms of the ring of piecewise polynomial functions on the corresponding fan.
Furthermore, it was shown in
[
fr-10
]
that
H^{*}(X_{\Sigma};\ \mathbb{Z})
is torsion-free under the same conditions on
X_{\Sigma}
.
Recently, a criterion of equivariant formality (over rational numbers) for partial quotients over toric manifolds (in the sense of
[
da-ja-91
]
) was provided in
[
ku-23
]
.
This result together with
[
ma-pa-06
]
implies that any partial quotient
X\neq M
over any toric manifold
M
is not equivariantly formal.

A
toric diagram

D\in[C,\Top]
over a small category
C
is a functor such that
D(c)
is a compact torus and
D(c\to c^{\prime})
is a group homomorphism,
c\in C_{0}
,
(c\to c^{\prime})\in C_{1}
, see
[
we-zi-zi-99
]
.
A toric diagram is called a
T
-diagram
if there is an object-wise epimorphism
\kappa T\to D
of diagrams, where
\kappa T
denotes the constant diagram.
Consider any
T
-CW-complex
X
such that
X
is
split
, i.e. the projection
X\to X/T
admits an equivariant section, and the orbit poset of
X
is a CW poset.
Then the
T
-space
X
is equivariantly homeomorphic to the homotopy colimit
\hoc D
(in the standard realization) of a
T
-diagram
D
over the orbit poset
C
of
X
.
(Every face of the space
\hoc D
is contractible.)
The converse also holds provided that
D
is
reduced
, i.e. any two
T
-cells
\sigma\subset\sigma^{\prime}
in
X
have different stabilizers (see Theorem
6.12
).
The Borel fibration of
\hoc D
takes form
\hoc BS\to BT
, where the diagram
S
is given by the stabilizers for the
T
-action on
\hoc D
(see Proposition
7.2
).

For any diagram
D\in[C,\Top]
over a CW poset
C
, the skeletal filtration of the nerve of
C
induces the filtration on
\hoc D
.
The cohomological
Bousfield-Kan spectral sequence
of
D
(BKSS, for short), i.e. the spectral sequence of this filtration, converges to
H^{*}(\hoc D;\ \Bbbk)
, where
\Bbbk=\mathbb{Q},\mathbb{Z}

[
bo-ka-72
,
bo-87
]
.
The second page of BKSS is given by sheaf cohomology of the refinement sheaf
sH^{*}(D)
for the cellular cosheaf
H^{*}(D)
, or equivalently by higher derived functors of
\lim sH^{*}(D)
.
Based on the ideas from
[
la-tu-vo-10
]
, below we give the cohomological version of BKSS collapse at the second page for any
formal diagram

D
over a graded finite poset.
(The extension of BKSS is trivial in the category of
\Bbbk
-modules.)
The functorial models of a
simplicial torus
(in particular, a simplicial abelian group) and its classifying space
[
fr-21
]
are used to show formality of any toric diagram
D
over
\mathbb{Z},\mathbb{Q}
, and formality of any quasitoric diagram
BD
over
\Bbbk
(i.e. objects of
D
are quasitori).
Here,
\Bbbk=\mathbb{Z},\mathbb{Q}
, if any object of
D
is connected, and
\Bbbk=\mathbb{Q}
otherwise.

Below we derive three main results of this note from the BKSS collapse.
Let
X_{\Sigma}
be any compact toric variety with the corresponding complete rational polyhedral fan
\Sigma
of dimension
d
consisting of salient cones.
There is the
T
-diagram
D_{\Sigma}
such that
X_{\Sigma}
is equivariantly homotopy equivalent to
\hoc D_{\Sigma}
, as described in
[
we-zi-zi-99
,
li-so-22
]
.

Theorem 1
.

The orbit spectral sequence of
X_{\Sigma}
collapses at the second page with trivial extension.
There is the abelian group isomorphism

H^{i}(X_{\Sigma};\ \mathbb{Z})\cong\bigoplus_{s+t=i}H^{cw}_{d-s}(\Sigma;\ H^{t}(D_{\Sigma})),

with the direct sum of cellular homology groups for the poset
\Sigma
with coefficients in the cosheaf.

Theorem
1
(see Theorem
6.15
) provides a possible explanation of the collapse for the Danilov spectral sequence
[
da-78
]
.
This theorem positively resolves the conjecture (for cohomology with integral coefficients) of M. Franz
[
fr-06
, Conjecture 6.4]
in the case of a compact toric variety.
(In
[
fr-06
]
the collapse result was obtained over any commutative unital ring
R
for any nonsingular toric variety).
A more general result holds for the spectral sequence induced by the coskeletal filtration on
C
for any toric diagram over any oriented homology manifold poset (see Theorem
5.12
).
This filtration coincides with the orbit filtration for any strongly reduced
T
-diagram (see Section
6
).
The proof of Theorem
1
relies on the Poincaré-Lefschetz type duality for sheaf cohomology using the
Zeeman-McCrory spectral sequence
(ZMSS, for short) collapse (e.g. see
[
ay-17
,
ch-23
]
).
It is expected that Theorem
1
will be helpful for explicit computations of cohomology for singular toric varieties (compare with
[
fr-10
]
).
For instance, the orbit spectral sequence collapse rules out one of the two cases considered in
[
Ho-08
]
.

Theorem 2
.

Let
D
be any reduced
T
-diagram over an oriented homology manifold
C
of dimension
d
.
Let
\Bbbk=\mathbb{Q}
, or let
\Bbbk=\mathbb{Z}
if all stabilizers of the
T
-action on
\hoc D
are connected.
Then
\hoc D
is equivariantly formal over
\Bbbk
iff
H^{odd}(\hoc D;\ \Bbbk)=0
.
In such case, the sheaf
sH^{*}(BS;\ \Bbbk)
is acyclic, and the orbit space
\ord C
is
\Bbbk
-acyclic.
Then one has the isomorphisms

(1)

H^{*}_{T}(\hoc D;\ \Bbbk)\cong\lim sH^{*}(BS),\ H^{2i}(\hoc D;\ \Bbbk)\cong\lmm^{i}sH^{i}(D),

of modules over
H^{*}(BT)
and
\Bbbk
, respectively.
Furthermore,
H^{*}(\hoc D;\ \Bbbk)
is torsion-free.

Theorem
2
implies that the closure of any orbit in
\hoc D
is equivariantly formal, has vanishing odd cohomology and is torsion-free (see Corollary
9.10
).
Assuming that
X
is a torus manifold such that its orbit poset is a regular CW poset, the criterion of
H^{odd}(X)=0
from
[
ma-pa-06
]
follows in one direction from Theorem
2
in such a particular case (see Theorem
9.7
).
The proof of Theorem
2
relies in particular on ABFP exact sequence
[
fr-pu-06
]
, BKSS and ZMSS collapse, mentioned above.
Another key tool of the proof is the
t
-graded
comparison spectral sequence
(CSS, for short) of a
T
-diagram
D
introduced in Section
8
.
This spectral sequence compares EMSS and BKSS in the following sense.
The target of the CSS is given by the respective line from the second page of BKSS.
The second page of CSS is concentrated in a line coinciding with the line from the second page of the respective EMSS (up to a certain change of indices), provided that the sheaf
sH^{*}(BS)
is acyclic.
As a byproduct, we obtain the description of
bigraged Betti numbers
of
\hoc D
(i.e. the dimensions of the second page for EMSS) when
sH^{*}(BS)
is acyclic.

We derive the explicit formulas for the Betti numbers for
H^{*}(\hoc D)
below under slightly more general conditions compared to those of Theorem
2
.
This computation follows from the above results by relating the Euler characteristic of the first and second pages from BKSS.
Betti numbers of tropical toric varieties are expressed by a similar formula, see
[
ch-23
]
.
Furthermore, we describe the cohomology groups and bigraded Betti numbers of skeleta for toric manifolds.
This is the third main result of the present note.
(In fact, the computation holds in a more general setting of skeleta for a
T
-characteristic diagram over an oriented homology manifold, see Section
10
).
Notice that skeleta for toric varieties are different from broken toric varieties over skeleta considered in
[
su-23
]
.
In the case of a skeleton for
\mathbb{C}P^{m-1}
we compare these formulas with those implied by
[
fu-19
]
,
[
gr-th-07
]
.
Our computation is based on the dual to the Taylor-type complex introduced in
[
ay-17
]
, combined with the aforementioned methods of BKSS collapse and comparison SS.

of a

2.
BKSS collapse

In this section we recall the definition of the cohomological Bousfield-Kan spectral sequence (BKSS, for short).
Then we prove the collapse result for BKSS of any formal diagram over any finite graded poset
C
(Theorem
2.12
).
We use the ideas (formality of diagrams) from
[
la-tu-vo-10
]
adjusted to computation for cohomology of simplicial spaces.
In what follows, we refer to the standard notions of simplicial homotopy theory from
[
go-ja-09
]
.

Definition 2.1
.

Let
\Delta
,
\Delta_{n}
be the categories of all finite ordered sets, and of all ordered sets of cardinality
\leqslant n
, respectively.
Let
\Top
denote the category of compactly generated weakly Hausdorff topological spaces and continuous maps between them.
For a simplicial space
X_{\bullet}\in[\Delta^{op},\Top]
let

\tr_{i}X_{\bullet}:=\iota^{n}_{*}X_{\bullet}\in[\Delta_{n}^{op},\Top],

where
\iota^{n}\colon\Delta_{n}\to\Delta
is the natural functor.
Let

\mathrm{sk}_{i}\colon[\Delta_{n}^{op},\Top]\to[\Delta^{op},\Top],

be the left adjoint of
\tr_{i}
given by the respective left Kan extension.
The functor

\Sk_{i}:=\mathrm{sk}_{i}\circ\tr_{i}\colon[\Delta^{op},\Top]\to[\Delta^{op},\Top],

is called the
i
-skeleton
of
X_{\bullet}
.

Let
\tilde{X}_{\bullet}\to X_{\bullet}
be a cofibrant replacement of a simplicial space.
The counit of the adjunction
sk_{i}\dashv\tr_{i}
is the map
\Sk_{i}\tilde{X}_{\bullet}\to\tilde{X}_{\bullet}
which is a cofibration.
The simplicial space
X_{\bullet}
has dimension
n
if
X_{\bullet}=\Sk^{n}X_{\bullet}
.
In what follows, we consider only simplicial spaces of finite dimension, whose every object is cofibrant.
Using the Quillen model structure, we often replace a weak equivalence in
\Top
with a homotopy equivalence by Whitehead theorem for diagrams consisting of CW-complexes.
By taking the geometric realization of an
n
-dimensional simplicial space, one obtains the filtration

(2)

\lvert\Sk_{0}\tilde{X}_{\bullet}\rvert\subset\cdots\subset\lvert\Sk_{n}\tilde{X}_{\bullet}\rvert=\lvert\tilde{X}_{\bullet}\rvert,

where
\lvert X_{\bullet}\rvert
denotes the realization of a simplicial space.
(Notice that
\lvert\tilde{X}_{\bullet}\rvert\to\lvert X_{\bullet}\rvert
is a weak equivalence.)

Definition 2.2
.

For a diagram
D\in[C,\Top]
over a small category
C
the
simplicial replacement

\srep_{\bullet}D\in[\Delta^{op},\Top]
of
D
is given by the set of
n
-simplices

\biggl\{\bigsqcup_{\sigma\in N_{n}(C)}D(c_{n})|\ \sigma=(c_{0}\leftarrow\cdots\leftarrow c_{n})\in N_{n}(C)\biggr\},

where
N_{\bullet}(C)
is the nerve category of
C
.
The face and degeneracy maps are given by the usual formulas
[
go-ja-09
]
.

Let
\Bbbk=\mathbb{Z},\mathbb{Q}
.
From now on we assume that
C
is a finite graded poset (or, more generally, a finite Reedy category).

Definition 2.3

(
[
bo-ka-72
,
bo-87
]
)
.

The cohomological
Bousfield-Kan spectral sequence
(BKSS, for short) of a simplicial set
X_{\bullet}
is the first quadrant spectral sequence
((E_{X_{\bullet}})_{r}^{p,q},d_{r})
,
r\geqslant 1
, in cohomology with coefficients in a ring
\Bbbk
, corresponding to the filtration (
2
).
The cohomological Bousfield-Kan spectral sequence
((E_{D})_{r}^{p,q},d_{r})
of a diagram
D\in[C,\Top]
over
C
with values in
\Top
is the BKSS of the simplicial replacement
\srep_{\bullet}D

[
go-ja-09
]
.

Definition 2.4
.

The realization
\lvert\srep_{\bullet}D\rvert
of the simplicial replacement for a
C
-diagram
D
of spaces is called the
homotopy colimit

\hoc D
of
D
.

Remark 2.5
.

The above definition agrees up to a weak equivalence with the colimit
\col\tilde{D}
of a cofibrant replacement
\tilde{D}\to D
, provided that
D
is objectwise cofibrant
[
du
]
.
In particular,
\hoc
is the left derived functor of
\col
.
More explicitly,

\tilde{D}(c)=\lvert\srep_{\bullet}u^{*}_{c}D\rvert,

where
u_{c}\colon C\downarrow c\to C
is the forgetting functor from the respective arrow category.
Furthermore, for every
f\colon c\to c^{\prime}
the corresponding map
\tilde{D}(f)=(u_{f})_{*}
is induced by the functor
u_{f}\colon C\downarrow c\to C\downarrow c^{\prime}
between the comma-categories.
By considering only nondegenerate simplices in the nerve of
C
, one can describe
\hoc D
as follows

(3)

\hoc D\simeq\biggl(\bigsqcup_{\sigma=c_{0}\leftarrow\cdots\leftarrow c_{i}}\Delta_{\sigma}\times D(c_{i})\biggr)/\sim,

\bigl(x,D(c_{i}\to c^{\prime}_{j})y\bigr)\sim(\Inc_{\sigma^{\prime}\to\sigma}x,y),\ x\in\Delta_{\sigma^{\prime}},\ y\in D(c_{i}),

where the coproduct is taken by all strictly increasing chains in
C
and

\Inc_{\sigma^{\prime}\to\sigma}\colon\Delta_{\sigma^{\prime}}\to\Delta_{\sigma},\ \sigma^{\prime}=(c^{\prime}_{0}\leftarrow\cdots\leftarrow c^{\prime}_{j})\to\sigma,

is the natural inclusion of faces.

Remark 2.6
.

The simplicial space
\srep D_{\bullet}
is cofibrant
[
go-ja-09
]
.
For a simplicial space
X_{\bullet}
the spectral sequence
((E_{X_{\bullet}})_{r}^{p,q},d)
is isomorphic (from the second page) to the spectral sequence of the simplicial dga
C^{*}(X_{\bullet})
of normalized cochains with the filtration by simplicial degree (the proof is similar to
[
bo-87

The BKSS second page is well-known to be expressed in terms of higher limits (e.g. see
[
no-ra-05
]

Therefore, the BKSS for a diagram

takes the following form (see Section

{\lim}^{i}sH^{j}(D)\Rightarrow H^{i+j}(\hoc\ D).

The proof of the following proposition is obtained trivially from naturality of BKSS with respect to morphism of diagrams.

Proposition 2.7

For any morphism of diagrams
\alpha\colon D\to X
, where

,

-module structure on the BKSS target

Let

Definition 4.1

Proposition 10.1

Proof.

Remark 10.16
.

The skeletal filtration on

\hoc D
corresponds to the orbit filtration.
