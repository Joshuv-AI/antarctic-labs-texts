Existence Theorem


Abstract
This paper is dedicated to investigating the existence of solutions to the initial value problem (IVP) for a coupled system of Ψ \Psi -Hilfer hybrid fractional differential equations (FDEs) and boundary value problem (BVP) for a coupled system of Ψ \Psi -Hilfer hybrid FDEs. Analysis of the current paper depends on the two fixed point theorems involving three operators characterized on Banach algebra. In the view of an application, we provided concrete examples to exhibit the effectiveness of our achieved results.

which is the condition (

Step 1:

For any

\displaystyle\left\|A(y,x)-A(\bar{y},\bar{x})\right\|_{E}

\displaystyle=\left\|\left(A_{1}(y,x),A_{2}(y,x)\right)-\left(A_{1}(\bar{y},\bar{x}),A_{2}(\bar{y},\bar{x})\right)\right\|_{E}

\displaystyle=\left\|\left(A_{1}(y,x)-A_{1}(\bar{y},\bar{x})\right),\left(A_{2}(y,x)-A_{2}(\bar{y},\bar{x})\right)\right\|_{E}

\displaystyle=\left\|A_{1}(y,x)-A_{1}(\bar{y},\bar{x})\right\|_{C_{1-\xi;\,\Psi}\left(J,\mathbb{R}\right)}+\left\|A_{2}(y,x)-A_{2}(\bar{y},\bar{x})\right\|_{C_{1-\xi;\,\Psi}\left(J,\mathbb{R}\right)}.

Now, using the hypothesis

\displaystyle\left|\left(\Psi\left(t\right)-\Psi\left(0\right)\right)^{1-\xi}\left(A_{1}(y,x)(t)-A_{1}(\bar{y},\bar{x})(t)\right)\right|

\displaystyle\left|\left(\Psi\left(t\right)-\Psi\left(0\right)\right)^{1-\xi}y(t)\right|

\displaystyle=\left|\left(\Psi\left(t\right)-\Psi\left(0\right)\right)^{1-\xi}\left[u_{1}(t,y(t),x(t))\left\{\left(\Psi\left(t\right)-\Psi\left(0\right)\right)^{\xi-1}\,\bar{\Omega}_{1}+\mathcal{I}_{0^{+}}^{\mu\,;\,\Psi}v_{1}\left(t,\bar{y}(t),\bar{x}(t)\right)\right\}\right.\right.

\displaystyle\left.\left.~~+w_{1}(t,y(t),x(t))\right]\right|

\displaystyle\leq M_{1}\left\{\left|\bar{\Omega}_{1}\right|+\frac{\left(\Psi\left(t\right)-\Psi\left(0\right)\right)^{1-\xi}}{\Gamma\left(\mu\right)}\int_{0}^{t}\Psi^{\prime}(s)(\Psi(t)-\Psi(s))^{\mu-1}\left(\Psi\left(s\right)-\Psi\left(0\right)\right)^{1-\xi}g_{1}(s)\,ds\right\}

Conclusion

The existence of solution of IVP and BVP for coupled system of
\Psi
-Hilfer hybrid FDEs is achieved by using fixed point theorem for the three operators. It is observed that the existence result obtained for the BVPs of coupled system of
\Psi
-Hilfer hybrid FDEs includes the study of coupled system for IVP
(a=1,b=0)
, periodic BVP
(a=1,b=-1,y_{0}=0)
and anti-periodic BVP
(a=1,b=1,y_{0}=0)
involving
\Psi
-Hilfer fractional derivative. Further, we have provided an examples to illustrate the validity of our outcomes.

As presented in the body of the paper, we have successfully obtained the main results of this paper. However, some open problems that still need to be answered that involve the theory of fractional hybrid differential equations, namely:

1.

Would it be possible to discuss the existence of mild solutions to Eqs.(
1.1
)-(
1.2
) problems? What are the necessary and sufficient conditions for this to happen?

2.

As a consequence of item 1, we can ask about the uniqueness and stability of mild solutions.

3.

Is it possible to guarantee solutions involving sectorial and almost-sectorial operators?

There are some questions that need to be answered as outlined above, which will enrich the theory. Other questions about fractional hybrid differential equations, are being discussed and future works are being elaborated, which allowed to answer these questions and others that are still open.
