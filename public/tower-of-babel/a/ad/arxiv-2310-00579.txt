Permutation orbifolds of vertex operator superalgebra and associative algebras
Chongying Dong, Feng Xu, Nina Yu

Abstract
Let V V be a vertex operator superalgebra and g = ( 1 2 ⋯ k ) g=\left(1\ 2\ \cdots k\right) be a k k -cycle which is viewed as an automorphism of the tensor product vertex operator superalgebra V ⊗ k V^{\otimes k} . In this paper, we construct an explicit isomorphism from A g ​ ( V ⊗ k ) A_{g}\left(V^{\otimes k}\right) to A ⁡ ( V ) A\left(V\right) if k k is odd and to A σ ​ ( V ) A_{\sigma}\left(V\right) if k k is even where σ \sigma is the canonical automorphism of V V of order 2 determined by the superspace structure of V . V. These recover previous results by Barron and Barron-Werf that there is a one-to-one correspondence between irreducible g g -twisted V ⊗ k V^{\otimes k} -modules and irreducible V V -modules (resp. irreducible σ \sigma -twisted V V -modules) when k k is odd (resp. even). This explicit isomorphism is expected to be useful in our further study on the Zhu algebra of fixed point subalgebra.

The isomorphism between

\left(V\right)

In this subsection, we construct an isomorphism between

and
A\left(V\right)
as associative algebras where

then construct an isomorphism from

Lemma 4.2
.

Let
g=\left(1\ 2\cdots k\right)

with
k
odd. Then
A_{g}\left(V^{\otimes k}\right)
is spanned
by
[\sum_{a=1}^{k}v^{a}]
for
v\in V_{\bar{0}}
.

Proof.

Let
h=g\sigma.
Then
o\left(g\sigma\right)=2k
. By Theorem
3.2
,

A_{g}\left(V^{\otimes k}\right)
is spanned by vectors of form
\sum_{a=1}^{2k}h^{a}x_{v_{1},\cdots,v_{n}}^{a_{1},\cdots,a_{n}}

modulo
O_{g}(V)
for
v_{j}\in V
,
j=1,\cdots,n
and
n=1,\cdots,k
.
Note that
\sigma\left(x_{v_{1},\cdots,v_{n}}^{a_{1},\cdots,a_{n}}\right)=(-1)^{i}x_{v_{1},\cdots,v_{n}}^{a_{1},\cdots,a_{n}}

for
x_{v_{1},\cdots,v_{n}}^{a_{1},\cdots,a_{n}}\in\left(V^{\otimes k}\right)_{\overline{i}}

with
i=0,1.
It is easy to check that

\displaystyle\sum_{a=1}^{2k}h^{a}x_{v_{1},\cdots,v_{n}}^{a_{1},\cdots,a_{n}}

\displaystyle=\begin{cases}2\sum_{a=1}^{k}g^{a}x_{v_{1},\cdots,v_{n}}^{a_{1},\cdots,a_{n}}&\text{for\ }x_{v_{1},\cdots,v_{n}}^{a_{1},\cdots,a_{n}}\in\left(V^{\otimes k}\right)_{\bar{0}}\\
0&\text{for}\ x_{v_{1},\cdots,v_{n}}^{a_{1},\cdots,a_{n}}\in\left(V^{\otimes k}\right)_{\bar{1}}.\end{cases}

Therefore,
A_{g}\left(V^{\otimes k}\right)
is spanned by vectors
of form
\sum_{a=1}^{k}g^{a}x_{v_{1},\cdots,v_{n}}^{a_{1},\cdots,a_{n}}

modulo
O_{g}(V)
with
x_{v_{1},\cdots,v_{n}}^{a_{1},\cdots,a_{n}}\in\left(V^{\otimes k}\right)_{\bar{0}}
.

Let
n=2,\cdots,k
. We will prove that any vector of the form
\sum_{a=1}^{k}g^{a}\left(x_{v_{1},\cdots,v_{n}}^{a_{1},\cdots,a_{n}}\right)

with
x_{v_{1},\cdots,v_{n}}^{a_{1},\cdots,a_{n}}\in\left(V^{\otimes k}\right){}_{\bar{0}}

can be reduced to a
\left(n-1\right)
-tensor vector modulo
O_{g}(V)

and hence by induction
A_{g}\left(V^{\otimes k}\right)
is spanned
by vectors of the form

\sum_{a=1}^{k}v^{a}
,

v\in V_{\bar{0}}
. We only give a proof for
(a_{1},\cdots,a_{n})=(1,\cdots,n)

and the proof for general case is similar. Let
\xi=e^{\frac{\pi i}{k}}
.
Note that the eigenspace of
h
with eigenvalue
\xi^{s}
is given
by

\left(V^{\otimes k}\right)^{s\ast}=\left\{\sum_{a=1}^{2k}\xi^{-\left(a-1\right)s}h^{a}x_{v_{1},\cdots,v_{k}}^{1,\cdots,k}\mid v_{1},\cdots,v_{k}\in V\right\},

where
0\leq s\leq 2k-1
. We will use the fact that for any
\sum_{a=1}^{2k}\xi^{-\left(a-1\right)s}h^{a-1}u^{1}\in\left(V^{\otimes k}\right)^{s\ast}

and
\sum_{b=1}^{2k}\xi^{\left(b-1\right)s}h^{b-1}x_{v_{1},\cdots,v_{n-1}}^{1,\cdots,n-1}\in\left(V^{\otimes k}\right)^{\left(2k-s\right)\ast}

with
s=1,\cdots,2k-1,

0\equiv\left(\sum_{a=1}^{2k}\xi^{-\left(a-1\right)s}h^{a-1}u^{1}\right)\circ_{g}\left(\sum_{b=1}^{2k}\xi^{\left(b-1\right)s}h^{b-1}x_{v_{1},\cdots,v_{n-1}}^{1,\cdots,n-1}\right).

(1) Let
u^{1}\in\left(V^{\otimes k}\right)_{\bar{0}}
and
x_{v_{1},\cdots,v_{n-1}}^{1,\cdots,n-1}\in\left(V^{\otimes k}\right)_{\bar{0}}
.
Then we have
\sigma\left(u^{1}\right)=u^{1}
and
\sigma\left(x_{v_{1},\cdots,v_{n-1}}^{1,\cdots,n-1}\right)=x_{v_{1},\cdots,v_{n-1}}^{1,\cdots,n-1}.

Therefore

\displaystyle\sum_{a=1}^{2k}\xi^{-\left(a-1\right)s}h^{a-1}u^{1}

\displaystyle=\sum_{a=1}^{2k}\xi^{-\left(a-1\right)s}g^{a-1}u^{1}

\displaystyle=\sum_{a=1}^{k}\left(1+\left(-1\right)^{s}\right)\xi^{-\left(a-1\right)s}u^{2-a+k}

\displaystyle=\begin{cases}0&\text{if\ }s\ \text{is\ odd}\\
\sum_{a=1}^{k}2\xi^{-\left(a-1\right)s}u^{2-a+k}&\text{if\ }s\ \text{is\ even,}\end{cases}

(4.3)

and

\displaystyle\sum_{b=1}^{2k}\xi^{\left(b-1\right)s}h^{b-1}x_{v_{1},\cdots,v_{n-1}}^{1,\cdots,n-1}=

\displaystyle\sum_{b=1}^{2k}\xi^{\left(b-1\right)s}g^{b-1}x_{v_{1},\cdots,v_{n-1}}^{1,\cdots,n-1}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{b=1}^{k}\xi^{\left(b-1\right)s}\left(1+\left(-1\right)^{s}\right)g^{b-1}x_{v_{1},\cdots,v_{n-1}}^{1,\cdots,n-1}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\begin{cases}0&\text{if\ }s\ \text{is\ odd}\\
2\sum_{b=1}^{k}\xi^{\left(b-1\right)s}g^{b-1}x_{v_{1},\cdots,v_{n-1}}^{1,\cdots,n-1}&\text{if\ }s\ \text{is\ even}\end{cases}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\begin{cases}0&\text{if\ }s\ \text{is\ odd}\\
2\sum_{b=1}^{k}\xi^{bs}\prod_{i=1}^{b}\tau_{i,n-1}x_{v_{1},v_{2},\cdots,v_{n-1}}^{1-b+k,2-b+k,\cdots,n-1-b+k}&\text{if\ }s\ \text{is\ even.}\end{cases}

Now for
1\leq s\leq 2k-1
with
s
even, we have

\displaystyle 0

\displaystyle\equiv\left(\sum_{a=1}^{2k}\xi^{-\left(a-1\right)s}h^{a-1}u^{1}\right)\circ_{g}\left(\sum_{b=1}^{2k}\xi^{\left(b-1\right)s}h^{b-1}x_{v_{1},\cdots,v_{n-1}}^{1,\cdots,n-1}\right)

\displaystyle\equiv\left(2\sum_{a=1}^{k}\xi^{-\left(a-1\right)s}u^{2-a+k}\right)\circ_{g}\left(2\sum_{b=1}^{k}\xi^{bs}\prod_{i=1}^{b}\tau_{i,n-1}x_{v_{1},v_{2},\cdots,v_{n-1}}^{1-b+k,2-b+k,\cdots,n-1-b+k}\right)

\displaystyle=4\text{Res}_{z}Y\left(\sum_{a=1}^{k}\xi^{-\left(a-1\right)s}u^{2-a+k},z\right)\left(\sum_{b=1}^{k}\xi^{bs}\prod_{i=1}^{b}\tau_{i,n-1}x_{v_{1},v_{2},\cdots,v_{n-1}}^{1-b+k,2-b+k,\cdots,n-1-b+k}\right)\frac{\left(1+z\right)^{\text{wt}u-1+\frac{s}{k}}}{z}

\displaystyle=4\sum_{a=1}^{k}\sum_{b=1}^{k}\prod_{i=1}^{b}\tau_{i,n-1}\xi^{\left(b-a+1\right)s}\text{Res}_{z}Y\left(u^{2-a+k},z\right)\left(x_{v_{1},v_{2},\cdots,v_{n-1}}^{1-b+k,2-b+k,\cdots,n-1-b+k}\right)\frac{\left(1+z\right)^{\text{wt}u-1+\frac{s}{k}}}{z}

\displaystyle=4\sum_{b=1}^{k}\ \sum_{2-a\notin\left\{1-b,2-b,\cdots,n-1-b\right\}}\prod_{i=1}^{b}\tau_{i,n-1}\xi^{\left(b-a+1\right)s}x_{u,v_{1},\cdots,v_{n-1}}^{2-a+k,1-b+k,2-b+k,\cdots,n-1-b+k}

\displaystyle+4\sum_{b=1}^{k}\prod_{i=1}^{b}\tau_{i,n-1}\sum_{j=1}^{n-1}\xi^{\left(j-1\right)s}x_{v_{1},\cdots,v_{j-1},v_{j}^{\prime},v_{j+1},\cdots,v_{n-1}}^{1-b+k,\cdots,j-1-b+k,j-b+k,j+1-b+k,\cdots,n-1-b+k},

where

For

, set

Then

\displaystyle\sum_{b=1}^{k}\sum_{2-a\notin\left\{1-b,2-b,\cdots,n-1-b\right\}}\prod_{i=1}^{b}\tau_{i,n-1}\xi^{\left(b-a+1\right)s}x_{u,v_{1},\cdots,v_{n-1}}^{2-a+k,1-b+k,2-b+k,\cdots,n-1-b+k}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{j=1}^{k-n+1}\xi^{(n+j-2)s}y_{j}\equiv u_{s}.

Since
s

Theorem 4.4
.

Let
g=\left(1\ 2\cdots k\right)

with
k
odd. Define

\displaystyle\phi:

\displaystyle\ A_{g}\left(V^{\otimes k}\right)\to A\left(V\right)

\displaystyle\left[\sum_{a=1}^{k}u^{a}\right]\mapsto\left[k\Delta_{k}\left(1\right)u\right],u\in V_{\bar{0}}.

Then
\phi
gives an isomorphism between
A_{g}\left(V^{\otimes k}\right)

and
A\left(V\right).

Proof.

Let
h=g\sigma
as before. Recall that
A_{g}\left(V^{\otimes k}\right)=V^{\otimes k}/O_{g}\left(V^{\otimes k}\right)

and
A\left(V\right)=V/O\left(V\right)
where
O_{g}\left(V^{\otimes k}\right)

and
O\left(V\right)
are defined in Section
2
. To
show that
\phi
is an isomorphism between the associative algebras

A_{g}\left(V^{\otimes k}\right)
and
A\left(V\right)
, we first
need to show that
\phi
is well-defined.

Since
A_{g}\left(V^{\otimes k}\right)=\left(V^{\otimes k}\right){}^{0\ast}/O_{g}\left(V^{\otimes k}\right)\cap\left(V^{\otimes k}\right){}^{0\ast}

and
O_{g}\left(V^{\otimes k}\right)\cap\left(V^{\otimes k}\right){}^{0\ast}

is spanned by
u\circ_{g}v
for
u\in\left(V^{\otimes k}\right){}^{s\ast}

and
v\in\left(V^{\otimes k}\right){}^{\left(2k-s\right)\ast}
, we
map
u\circ_{g}v
to
0
for
s=1,...,2k-1.
In fact, from the
proof of Lemma
4.2
we see that
\left[u\circ_{g}v\right]=0

just gives an identification between
p
-tensor vectors and
q
-tensor
vectors in
A_{g}\left(V^{\otimes k}\right)
such that either
p>1

or
q>1.
So the main task is to show that for any
u,v\in V^{\otimes k}
,

\phi\left(u\circ_{g}v\right)
\in O\left(V\right).

Let
\overline{u}=\sum_{a=1}^{k}u^{a}
,
\overline{v}=\sum_{b=1}^{k}v^{b}\in\left(V^{\otimes k}\right)^{0\ast}

where
u,v\in V_{\bar{0}}
. Then we have

\overline{u}\circ_{g}\overline{v}=\text{Res}_{z}\sum_{a,b=1}^{k}Y\left(u^{a},z\right)v^{b}\frac{\left(1+z\right)^{\text{wt}u}}{z^{2}}=\sum_{j=1}^{k}\left(u\circ v\right)^{j}+\text{wt}u\sum_{j=1}^{k-1}x_{j}+\sum_{j=1}^{k-1}y_{j},

where

x_{j}=\sum_{m=0}^{k-1}g^{m}x_{u,v}^{1,1+j}=\sum_{m=0}^{k-1}x_{u,v}^{1-m+k,1+j-m+k}\equiv\sum_{t=1}^{k-1}\frac{1}{k}\left(\eta^{-jt}-1\right)u_{t},

y_{j}=\sum_{m=0}^{k-1}g^{m}x_{u_{-2}\textbf{1},v}^{1,j}=\sum_{m=0}^{k-1}x_{u_{-2}\textbf{1},v}^{1-m+k,1+j-m+k}\equiv\sum_{t=1}^{k-1}\frac{1}{k}\left(\eta^{-jt}-1\right)w_{t}

with

u_{t}=-\text{Res}_{z}\sum_{j=1}^{k}\left(Y\left(u,z\right)v\frac{\left(1+z\right)^{\text{wt}u-1+\frac{t}{k}}}{z}\right)^{j},

and

w_{t}=-\text{Res}_{z}\sum_{j=1}^{k}\left(Y\left(u_{-2}\textbf{1},z\right)v\frac{\left(1+z\right)^{\text{wt}u+\frac{t}{k}}}{z}\right)^{j},

where we use Remark
4.3
. By the same arguments given
in
[
DXY
, Theorem 3.7]
, one can prove that
\phi
is an isomorphism
between
A_{g}\left(V^{\otimes k}\right)
and
A\left(V\right).

∎

for
x_{v_{1},\cdots,v_{n}}^{a_{1},\cdots,a_{n}}\in\left(V^{\otimes k}\right)_{\bar{i}}

with

It is easy to check that

\sum_{a=1}^{k}h^{a}x_{v_{1},\cdots,v_{n}}^{a_{1},\cdots,a_{n}}=\begin{cases}\sum_{a=1}^{k}g^{a}x_{v_{1},\cdots,v_{n}}^{a_{1},\cdots,a_{n}}&\text{if\ }x_{v_{1},\cdots,v_{n}}^{a_{1},\cdots,a_{n}}\in\left(V^{\otimes k}\right)_{\bar{0}}\\
\sum_{a=1}^{k}g^{a}(-1)^{a}x_{v_{1},\cdots,v_{n}}^{a_{1},\cdots,a_{n}}&\text{if}\ x_{v_{1},\cdots,v_{n}}^{a_{1},\cdots,a_{n}}\in\left(V^{\otimes k}\right)_{\bar{1}}.\end{cases}

Let
n=2,\cdots,k
. We will first prove that any vector of the form

\sum_{a=1}^{k}g^{a}\left(x_{v_{1},\cdots,v_{n}}^{a_{1},\cdots,a_{n}}\right)

with
x_{v_{1},\cdots,v_{n}}^{a_{1},\cdots,a_{n}}\in\left(V^{\otimes k}\right)_{\bar{0}}

and
\sum_{a=1}^{k}g^{a}(-1)^{a}x_{v_{1},\cdots,v_{n}}^{a_{1},\cdots,a_{n}}

with

can be reduced to a
\left(n-1\right)
-tensor vector modulo
O_{g}\left(V^{\otimes k}\right)

and then by induction we will prove that
A_{g}\left(V^{\otimes k}\right)

is spanned by vectors of the form
\sum_{a=1}^{k}v^{a}
for
v\in V_{\bar{0}}

and
\left[\sum_{a=1}^{k}\left(-1\right)^{a-1}v^{a}\right]
for

We only give a proof for
(a_{1},\cdots,a_{n})=(1,\cdots,n)
and
the proof for general case is similar. Note that the eigenspace of

is given by
\left(V^{\otimes k}\right)^{s\ast}=\left\{\sum_{a=1}^{k}\eta^{-\left(a-1\right)s}h^{a}x_{v_{1},\cdots,v_{k}}^{1,\cdots,k}\mid v_{1},\cdots,v_{k}\in V\right\}
,
where
\eta=e^{\frac{2\pi i}{k}}
and
0\leq s\leq k
. We will use
the fact that for
\sum_{a=1}^{k}\eta^{-\left(a-1\right)s}h^{a-1}u^{1}\in\left(V^{\otimes k}\right)^{s\ast}

and
\sum_{b=1}^{k}\eta^{\left(b-1\right)s}h^{b-1}x_{v_{1},\cdots,v_{n-1}}^{1,\cdots,n-1}\in\left(V^{\otimes k}\right)^{\left(k-s\right)\ast}

with

0\equiv\left(\sum_{a=1}^{k}\eta^{-\left(a-1\right)s}h^{a-1}u^{1}\right)\circ_{g}\left(\sum_{b=1}^{k}\eta^{\left(b-1\right)s}h^{b-1}x_{v_{1},\cdots,v_{n-1}}^{1,\cdots,n-1}\right).

(1) First we prove that any
n
-tensor vector in
\left(V^{\otimes k}\right)_{\bar{0}}^{0\ast}

can be reduced to vectors of the form
\left[\sum_{a=1}^{k}v^{a}\right]

for

(i) Let

and
x_{v_{1},\cdots,v_{n-1}}^{1,\cdots,n-1}\in\left(V^{\otimes k}\right)_{\bar{0}}.

Then

Therefore we have

\sum_{a=1}^{k}\eta^{-\left(a-1\right)s}h^{a-1}u^{1}=\sum_{a=1}^{k}\eta^{-\left(a-1\right)s}g^{a-1}u^{1}=\sum_{a=1}^{k}\eta^{-\left(a-1\right)s}u^{2-a+k}

and

\displaystyle\sum_{b=1}^{k}\eta^{\left(b-1\right)s}h^{b-1}x_{v_{1},\cdots,v_{n-1}}^{1,\cdots,n-1}

\displaystyle=\sum_{b=1}^{k}\eta^{\left(b-1\right)s}g^{b-1}x_{v_{1},\cdots,v_{n-1}}^{1,\cdots,n-1}

\displaystyle=\sum_{b=1}^{k}\eta^{bs}g^{b}x_{v_{1},\cdots,v_{n-1}}^{1,\cdots,n-1}

\displaystyle=\sum_{b=1}^{k}\eta^{bs}\prod_{i=1}^{b}\tau_{i,n-1}x_{v_{1},v_{2},\cdots,v_{n-1}}^{1-b+k,2-b+k,\cdots,n-1-b+k},

m-b

\displaystyle 0

\displaystyle\equiv\left(\sum_{a=1}^{k}\eta^{-\left(a-1\right)s}h^{a-1}u^{1}\right)\circ_{g}\left(\sum_{b=1}^{k}\eta^{\left(b-1\right)s}h^{b-1}x_{v_{1},\cdots,v_{n-1}}^{1,\cdots,n-1}\right)

\displaystyle\widetilde{u}\ast_{g}\overline{v}

\displaystyle=\text{Res}_{z}Y\left(\sum_{a=1}^{k}\left(-1\right)^{a-1}u^{a},z\right)\left(\sum_{b=1}^{k}v^{b}\right)\frac{\left(1+z\right)^{\text{wt}u}}{z}

\displaystyle=\sum_{a\not=b}\left(-1\right)^{a-1}x_{u,v}^{a,b}+\sum_{j=1}^{k}\left(-1\right)^{j-1}\left(u\ast_{\sigma}v\right)^{j}

\displaystyle=\sum_{b-a=j,j=1,\cdots,k-1}\left(-1\right)^{a-1}x_{u,v}^{a,b}+\sum_{j=1}^{k}\left(-1\right)^{j-1}\left(u\ast_{\sigma}v\right)^{j}

\displaystyle=\sum_{a=1}^{k}\sum_{j=1}^{k-1}\left(-1\right)^{a-1-j}x_{u,v}^{a,a+j}+\sum_{j=1}^{k}\left(-1\right)^{j-1}\left(u\ast_{\sigma}v\right)^{j}

\displaystyle=\sum_{j=1}^{k-1}y_{j}+\sum_{j=1}^{k}\left(-1\right)^{j-1}\left(u\ast_{\sigma}v\right)^{j},

where
y_{j}=\sum_{a=1}^{k}\left(-1\right)^{a-1-j}x_{u,v}^{a,a+j}
.
Using (
4.9
) and
[
DXY
, Lemma 3.2]
, we obtain

\phi\left(\widetilde{u}\ast_{g}\overline{v}\right)=k\Delta_{k}\left(1\right)\text{Res}_{z}Y\left(u,z\right)v\left(1+z\right)^{\text{wt}u}\left(\frac{1}{z}+\sum_{t=1}^{k-1}\frac{\left(1+z\right)^{\text{wt}u-1+\frac{t}{k}}}{z}\right).

By the same arguments in
[
DXY
, Theorem 3.7]
, we obtain
\phi\left(\widetilde{u}\ast_{g}\overline{v}\right)=\phi\left(\widetilde{u}\right)\ast_{\sigma}\phi\left(\overline{v}\right)
.

(iv) For
\widetilde{u}=\sum_{a=1}^{k}\left(-1\right)^{a+1}u^{a},\widetilde{v}=\sum_{b=1}^{k}\left(-1\right)^{b+1}v^{b}\in\left(V^{\otimes k}\right)_{\bar{1}}^{0\ast}

with
u,v\in V_{\bar{1}}
, we have

\displaystyle\widetilde{u}\ast_{g}\widetilde{v}

\displaystyle=\text{Res}_{z}Y\left(\sum_{a=1}^{k}\left(-1\right)^{a-1}u^{a},z\right)\left(\sum_{b=1}^{k}\left(-1\right)^{b-1}v^{b}\right)\frac{\left(1+z\right)^{\text{wt}u}}{z}

\displaystyle=\sum_{a\not=b}\left(-1\right)^{a+b}x_{u,v}^{a,b}+\sum_{j=1}^{k}\left(u\ast_{\sigma}v\right)^{j}

\displaystyle=\sum_{b-a=j,j=1,\cdots,k-1}\left(-1\right)^{a+b}x_{u,v}^{a,b}+\sum_{j=1}^{k}\left(u\ast_{\sigma}v\right)^{j}

\displaystyle=\sum_{a=1}^{k}\sum_{j=1}^{k-1}\left(-1\right)^{j}x_{u,v}^{a,a+j}+\sum_{j=1}^{k}\left(u\ast_{\sigma}v\right)^{j}

\displaystyle=\sum_{j=1}^{k-1}y_{j}+\sum_{j=1}^{k}\left(u\ast_{\sigma}v\right)^{j},

where
y_{j}=\sum_{a=1}^{k}\left(-1\right)^{j}x_{u,v}^{a,a+j}
. As
before, we have

\displaystyle\phi\left(\widetilde{u}\ast_{g}\widetilde{v}\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\frac{1}{k}\sum_{t,j=1}^{k-1}\left(\eta^{-jt}-1\right)k\Delta_{k}\left(1\right)\text{Res}_{z}Y\left(u,z\right)v\frac{\left(1+z\right)^{\text{wt}u-1+\frac{t}{k}}}{z}+k\Delta_{k}\left(1\right)\left(u\ast_{\sigma}v\right).

Then by the arguments in
[
DXY
, Theorem 3.7]
, one can prove
\phi\left(\widetilde{u}\ast_{g}\widetilde{v}\right)=\phi\left(\widetilde{u}\right)\ast_{\sigma}\phi\left(\widetilde{v}\right)
.

Combining (i)-(iv), we get that
\phi
is a homomorphism.

Since
\Delta_{k}\left(1\right)
is an invertible operator, we define

\displaystyle\psi:

\displaystyle A_{\sigma}\left(V\right)\to A_{g}\left(V^{\otimes k}\right)

\displaystyle u\mapsto\frac{1}{k}\sum_{a=1}^{k}\left(\Delta_{k}\left(1\right)^{-1}u\right)^{a}.

Thus
\psi
is the inverse of
\phi
and
\phi
defines an isomorphism.
∎

For arbitrary element
g\in S_{k}
, one has
g=g_{1}\cdots g_{s}g_{s+1}\cdots g_{s+t}

which is a product of disjoint cycles with
g_{1},\cdots,g_{s}
odd
cycles and
g_{s+1},\cdots,g_{s+t}
even cycles. If
U,W
are vertex
operator algebras with automorphisms
f,h
respectively, then
f\otimes h

is an automorphism of
U\otimes W
and
A_{f\otimes h}\left(U\otimes W\right)

is isomorphic to
A_{f}(U)\otimes_{\mathbb{C}}A_{h}(W).
Using this
fact and Theorems
4.4
and
4.7
, we conclude
this paper with the following corollary.

Corollary 4.8
.

Let
g=g_{1}\cdots g_{s}g_{s+1}\cdots g_{s+t}\in S_{k}

be a product of disjoint cycles with
g_{1},\cdots,g_{s}
odd cycles
and
g_{s+1},\cdots,g_{s+t}
even cycles. Then
A_{g}\left(V^{\otimes k}\right)

is isomorphic to
\left(A\left(V\right)\right){}^{\otimes s}\otimes\left(A_{\sigma}\left(V\right)\right)^{\otimes t}

where the tensor is over
\mathbb{C}.
