Construction of Optimal Binary Z-Complementary Code Sets with New Lengths
Gobinda Ghosh, Sudhan Majhi, Shubabrata Paul

Abstract
Z-complementary code sets (ZCCSs) are used in multicarrier code-division multiple access (MC-CDMA) systems, for interference-free communication over multiuser and quasi-asynchronous environments. In this letter, we propose three new constructions of optimal binary ( R ​ 2 k + 1 , 2 k + 1 , R ​ γ , γ ) \left(R2^{k+1},2^{k+1},R\gamma,\gamma\right) -ZCCS, ( R ​ 2 k + 1 , 2 k + 1 , R ​ 2 m 2 , 2 m 2 ) \left(R2^{k+1},2^{k+1},R2^{m_{2}},2^{m_{2}}\right) -ZCCS and ( 2 k + 1 , 2 k + 1 , 3 ​ γ , 2 ​ γ ) \left(2^{k+1},2^{k+1},3\gamma,2\gamma\right) -ZCCS based on generalized Boolean functions (GBFs), where γ = 2 m 1 − 1 + 2 m 1 − 3 , m 1 ≥ 5 , k ≥ 1 , m 2 ≥ 1 \gamma=2^{m_{1}-1}+2^{m_{1}-3},m_{1}\geq 5,k\geq 1,m_{2}\geq 1 and R R is any even number. The proposed ZCCSs cover many unreported lengths and large set sizes.

VI
Conclusion

Three families of new optimal binary ZCCSs with novel parameters are proposed in this work. The structures are based on GBFs and we propose three distinct direct methods to generate optimal binary
