Sharp Pointwise Weyl Laws for Schrödinger operators with singular potentials on flat tori
Xiaoqi Huang, Cheng Zhang

Abstract
. The Weyl law of the Laplacian on the flat torus 𝕋 n \mathbb{T}^{n} is concerning the number of eigenvalues ≤ λ 2 \leq\lambda^{2} , which is equivalent to counting the lattice points inside the ball of radius λ \lambda in ℝ n \mathbb{R}^{n} . The leading term in the Weyl law is c n ​ λ n c_{n}\lambda^{n} , while the sharp error term O ⁡ ( λ n − 2 ) O(\lambda^{n-2}) is only known in dimension n ≥ 5 n\geq 5 . Determining the sharp error term in lower dimensions is a famous open problem (e.g. Gauss circle problem). In this paper, we show that under a type of singular perturbations one can obtain the pointwise Weyl law with a sharp error term in any dimensions. This result establishes the sharpness of the general theorems for the Schrödinger operators H V = − Δ g + V H_{V}=-\Delta_{g}+V in the previous work [ 22 ] of the authors, and extends the 3-dimensional results of Frank-Sabin [ 11 ] to any dimensions by using a different approach. Our approach is a combination of Fourier analysis techniques on the flat torus, Li-Yau’s heat kernel estimates, Blair-Sire-Sogge’s eigenfunction estimates, and Duhamel’s principle for the wave equation.

Consider the Schrödinger operators
H_{V}=-\Delta_{g}+V
on

\lim_{\delta\to 0}\sup_{x\in M}\int_{d_{g}(y,x)<\delta}|V(y)|W_{n}(d_{g}(x,y))dy=0,

W_{n}(r)=\begin{cases}r^{2-n},\quad\quad\quad\quad n\geq 3\\
\log(2+r^{-1}),\ \ n=2\end{cases}

and

(M,g)

Note that by Hölder inequality, we have

behavior, while neither one is contained in the other one for

. For instance, singularities of the type

in three dimensions. See e.g. Simon
[
35

for a detailed introduction to the Schrödinger operators with potentials in the Kato class and their physical motivations.

The assumption that

is essentially self-adjoint and bounded from below, and eigenfunctions of

[

]

[

). Assuming, as we may, that

\{\tau_{k}\}_{k=1}^{\infty},

H_{V}e_{\tau_{k}}=\tau^{2}_{k}e_{\tau_{k}},\ \ {\rm and}\ \ \int_{M}|e_{\tau_{k}}(x)|^{2}\,dx=1.

After possibly adding a constant to

is bounded below by one, i.e.,

(0.6)

\|f\|_{2}^{2}\leq\langle\,H_{V}f,\,f\,\rangle,\quad f\in\text{Dom }(H_{V}).

Moreover, we shall let

H^{0}=-\Delta_{g}

H^{0}e^{0}_{j}=\lambda^{2}_{j}e^{0}_{j},\quad\text{and }\,\,\int_{M}|e^{0}_{j}(x)|^{2}\,dx=1.

Let

Theorem 0.2
.

Let
n\geq 2
and
M=\mathbb{T}^{n}=\mathbb{R}^{n}/2\pi\mathbb{Z}^{n}
. Fix
x_{0}\in M
. Let
R(\lambda,x)
be defined in (
0.13
). Let
0<\eta<1
and
V(x)=\rho(d_{g}(x,x_{0}))d_{g}(x,x_{0})^{-2+\eta}
, where
\rho\in C_{0}^{\infty}((-\pi,\pi))
and
\rho(0)\neq 0
. Then we have

(0.14)

|R(\lambda,x_{0})|\gtrsim\lambda^{n-\eta}

and

(0.15)

\sup_{x\in M}|R(\lambda,x)|\approx\lambda^{n-\eta}.

Remark 0.3
.

We remark that (
0.14
) and (
0.15
) can hold for more general non-radial potentials. Indeed, they still hold for

V(x)=\rho(d_{g}(x,x_{0}))d_{g}(x,x_{0})^{-2+\eta}+V_{0}(x),

where
V_{0}(x)
is a real-valued potential with “lower-order singularities”. For instance,
V_{0}
can be any functions in
L^{p}(\mathbb{T}^{n})
with
p>\frac{n}{2-\eta}
. It can be proved by slightly modifying the proof of Theorem
0.2
.

These results are interesting in its own right, since the sharp error terms in the Weyl laws of the Laplacian on flat tori have not been completely understood so far, and they are closely related to many famous open problems including the Gauss circle problem and Waring’s problem. Understanding the behavior of spectral properties when the operators undergo a “small change” is the core issue in perturbation theory. See Kato’s book
[
27
]
and Simon’s survey paper
[
35
]
for more precise and comprehensive discussions.

These results show that Theorem
0.1
is sharp in the following sense. It is straightforward to check that
V(x)
in Theorem
0.2
is in the Kato class, and it belongs to
L^{p}(M)
for all
p<\frac{n}{2-\eta}
. On the one hand, since
\frac{n}{2-\eta}
can be arbitrarily close to
n
as
\eta
goes to 1, the condition
L^{n}(M)
in Theorem
0.1
cannot be replaced by
L^{p}(M),\ \forall p<n
. Thus,
p=n
is the threshold for the validity of the sharp pointwise Weyl law on the
L^{p}
scale. On the other hand, as pointed by Simon
[
35
, Section A3]
, the Kato class is exactly the border for bounded eigenfunctions (i.e. the pointwise Weyl law). Indeed,
H_{V}
may have unbounded eigenfunctions if
V\notin\mathcal{K}(M)
. Specifically, when
n\geq 3
, the potential
V(x)=|x|^{-2}(\log(2+|x|^{-1}))^{-\alpha}
belongs to the Kato class if and only if
\alpha>1
. If
\alpha=1
, it can have unbounded eigenfunctions (see
[
35
, Section A3]
,
[
5
, page 5]
). Since
\eta
in (
0.15
) can be arbitrarily close to 0, the error term

in Theorem
0.1
is sharp in the sense that any power improvement is not possible.

Remark 0.4
.

The possibility of smaller improvements, such as logarithmic improvement, is more subtle. For
x>0
and integer
k\geq 1
, let
{\rm Log}(x)=\log(2+x)
, and let

{\rm Log}^{(k)}(x)=({\rm Log}\circ...\circ{\rm Log})(x)

be the
k
-iterated logarithm. For example,
{\rm Log}^{(2)}(x)=\log(2+\log(2+x))
. When
n\geq 3
,
k\geq 1
and
\alpha>1
, the potentials

V_{1}(x)=|x|^{-2}({\rm Log}(|x|^{-1}))^{-\alpha},

V_{2}(x)=|x|^{-2}({\rm Log}(|x|^{-1}))^{-1}({\rm Log}^{(2)}(|x|^{-1}))^{-\alpha},

…

V_{k}(x)=|x|^{-2}({\rm Log}(|x|^{-1}))^{-1}({\rm Log}^{(2)}(|x|^{-1}))^{-1}\cdot\cdot\cdot({\rm Log}^{(k)}(|x|^{-1}))^{-\alpha}

belong to the Kato class (see
[

, Proposition A.2.5]
). One may naturally expect that the error term for

is
\approx\lambda^{n}({\rm Log}^{(k)}\lambda)^{-\delta}
for some

, which means that the error term

in Theorem

cannot have any iterated-log improvement. It might be verified by refining the proof of Theorem

in
\mathbb{R}^{n}
.

Our results are new in any dimension

proved (

[

]

These two properties play a key role in the proofs of Propositions

Lemma 1.1

(Spectral projection bounds,
[

]
,
[

Let

If
\,V\in\mathcal{K}(M)\cap L^{n/2}(M)

\|{\rm 1\hskip-1.72218pt\rule{0.43057pt}{6.54443pt}\hskip 0.86108pt}_{[\lambda,\lambda+1)}(P_{V})\|_{L^{2}\to L^{p}}\lesssim\lambda^{\sigma(p)},\ 2\leq p\leq\infty.

These

[

, and recently extended to the Schrödinger operators with critically singular potentials by Blair-Sire-Sogge
[
5
]

[

Lemma 1.2

(Heat kernel bounds,
[
32
]
,
[
41
]
)

If

1,\ \ \text{otherwise}.\end{cases}

Here Inj(

The heat kernel bounds were proved by Li-Yau
[

]
for smooth potentials, and extended to the Kato class by Sturm
[
41
]
.
Note that

so we have the following eigenfunction bounds.

If

(1.1)

Our potentials

. The second bound means the number of lattice points in the intersection of the annulus and a ball of radius

.
Thus

(2.10)

\#S_{\ell m}\lesssim\#J_{\ell m}\cdot\max_{j}\#K_{\ell m}(j)\lesssim\lambda^{n-1}2^{(n-1)m}(2^{\ell}+1)^{2}.

Note that
|e_{j}^{0}(x)|\leq 1
for any

and
x\in M
. Thus for any
x\in M
we have

\displaystyle|

\displaystyle\tilde{R}_{1}^{\prime}(\lambda,x)|\lesssim\sum_{|j|<\lambda}\sum_{|k|\geq\lambda}\frac{1}{|k|^{2}-|j|^{2}}(1+|k-j|)^{-n+2-\eta}

\displaystyle\lesssim\sum_{|j|\leq\lambda/2}\sum_{|k|\geq\lambda}|k|^{-n-\eta}+\sum_{|j|<\lambda}\sum_{|k|\geq 2\lambda}|k|^{-n-\eta}

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad+\sum_{\lambda/2<|j|<\lambda}\sum_{\lambda\leq|k|<2\lambda}\frac{1}{|k|^{2}-|j|^{2}}(1+|k-j|)^{-n+2-\eta}

\displaystyle\lesssim\lambda^{n-\eta}+\sum_{\ell}\sum_{m}\sum_{(j,k)\in S_{\ell m}}\lambda^{-1}2^{-\ell}2^{(-n+2-\eta)m}

\displaystyle\lesssim\lambda^{n-\eta}+\sum_{\ell\in\mathbb{Z}:\lambda^{-1}\lesssim 2^{\ell}\leq\lambda}\sum_{m\in\mathbb{N}:2^{\ell}\lesssim 2^{m}\lesssim\lambda}\lambda^{-1}2^{-\ell}2^{(-n+2-\eta)m}\cdot\lambda^{n-1}2^{(n-1)m}(2^{\ell}+1)^{2}

\displaystyle\lesssim\lambda^{n-\eta}.

Proof of (

).
Let
\psi(j)=h(|j|)-{\rm 1\hskip-1.72218pt\rule{0.43057pt}{6.54443pt}\hskip 0.86108pt}_{\lambda}(|j|)
. By (

(2.11)

|\psi(j)|\lesssim(1+\lambda^{-1+\eta+\varepsilon}||j|-\lambda|)^{-N},\ \forall N.

We claim that

(2.12)

|\tilde{R}_{1}-\tilde{R}_{1}^{\prime}|(\lambda,x)\leq\sum_{j}\sum_{k}\Big|\frac{\psi(j)-\psi(k)}{|j|^{2}-|k|^{2}}U_{jk}\Big|\lesssim\lambda^{n-\eta-\varepsilon+\sigma},\ \forall\sigma>0.

Here when
|j|=|k|
, we define

(2.13)

\frac{\psi(j)-\psi(k)}{|j|^{2}-|k|^{2}}=\frac{h^{\prime}(|j|)}{2|j|}.

When
|j|=|k|=0
, it is defined to be 0, since
h^{\prime}(0)=h^{\prime\prime}(0)=0
.

Now we prove the claim (
2.12
). By the symmetry between
j
and
k
, we may split the sum into the following six cases:

(i)

\lambda/2<|j|<2\lambda,\ \lambda/2<|k|<2\lambda

(ii)

\lambda/2<|j|<2\lambda,\ |k|\geq 2\lambda

(iii)

\lambda/2<|j|<2\lambda,\ |k|\leq\lambda/2

(iv)

|j|\leq\lambda/2,\ |k|\leq\lambda/2

(v)

|j|\leq\lambda/2,\ |k|\geq 2\lambda

(vi)

|j|\geq 2\lambda,\ |k|\geq 2\lambda
.

In the following, we show that case (i) contributes to the desired bound

, and other cases satisfy better bounds.

In case (i), by symmetry we just need to show

(2.14)

\sum_{\lambda/2<|j|<2\lambda}\sum_{\lambda/2<|k|<2\lambda,|k|\neq|j|}\Big|\frac{\psi(j)}{|j|^{2}-|k|^{2}}U_{jk}\Big|\lesssim\lambda^{n-\eta-\varepsilon+\sigma},\ \forall\sigma>0,

(2.15)

\sum_{\lambda/2<|j|<2\lambda}\sum_{|k|=|j|}\Big|\frac{h^{\prime}(|j|)}{2|j|}U_{jk}\Big|\lesssim\lambda^{n-1-\eta}.

Fix a Littlewood-Paley bump function

\beta\in C^{\infty}_{0}((1/2,2))
satisfying

\sum_{\ell=-\infty}^{\infty}\beta(2^{-\ell}s)=1,\quad s>0.

Let
\ell_{0}
be the largest integer such that

) and (

) hold:

U_{jk}\approx(1+|j-k|)^{-n+2-\eta}.

Since

,
|j|\approx|k|\approx\lambda
and
|j|\neq|k|
, we have
\lambda^{-1}\lesssim||j|-|k||\lesssim\lambda
and
|j|+|k|\approx\lambda
. Then for

we have

\displaystyle\sum_{\ell}\sum_{\lambda/2<|j|,\,|k|<2\lambda}\Big|\frac{\psi(j)}{|j|^{2}-|k|^{2}}U_{jk}\beta(2^{-\ell}||j|-|k||)\Big|

\displaystyle\lesssim\sum_{\ell}\sum_{\lambda/2<|j|,\,|k|<2\lambda}2^{-\ell}\lambda^{-1}(1+2^{-\ell_{0}}||j|-\lambda|)^{-N}(1+|j-k|)^{-n+2-\eta}\beta(2^{-\ell}||j|-|k||)

\displaystyle\lesssim\sum_{1<2^{\ell}\lesssim\lambda}\sum_{p\geq\ell_{0}}\sum_{m\geq 0}2^{-\ell}\lambda^{-1}(1+2^{-\ell_{0}}2^{p})^{-N}2^{(-n+2-\eta)m}\cdot\lambda^{n-1}2^{p}\cdot 2^{(n-1)m}2^{\ell}

\displaystyle\ \ \ \ \ \ \ +\sum_{\lambda^{-1}\lesssim 2^{\ell}\leq 1}\sum_{p\geq\ell_{0}}\sum_{m\geq 0}2^{-\ell}\lambda^{-1}(1+2^{-\ell_{0}}2^{p})^{-N}2^{(-n+2-\eta)m}\cdot\lambda^{n-1}2^{p}\cdot F_{n}(\lambda,2^{m})2^{\ell}\lambda

Here

is the maximal number of lattice points on a spherical cap of size

of the sphere

. In the calculation above, we count the lattice points in the intersection of a ball of radius

and an annulus of outer radius
\lambda
and width

. We exploit the following precise estimates for

\forall\sigma>0

F_{n}(\lambda,r)\lesssim\begin{cases}\lambda^{\sigma}(r^{n-1}\lambda^{-1}+r^{n-3}),\ \ \ n\geq 5\\
\lambda^{\sigma}(r^{3}\lambda^{-1}+r^{3/2}),\ \ \ \ \ \ \ n=4\\
\lambda^{\sigma}(r+1),\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ n=3\\
\lambda^{\sigma},\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\ \ n=2\end{cases}

. Sometimes the trivial estimate is sufficient for our purpose (e.g. the proof of (

)). To prove (

So we have

\displaystyle\lesssim\lambda^{n-1-\eta}.

Here we only use the trivial estimate (
2.17
).

In case (ii), we need to show

(2.18)

\sum_{\lambda/2<|j|<2\lambda}\sum_{|k|\geq 3\lambda}\Big|\frac{\psi(j)-\psi(k)}{|j|^{2}-|k|^{2}}U_{jk}\Big|\lesssim\lambda^{n-2\eta-\varepsilon},

(2.19)

\sum_{\lambda/2<|j|\leq\frac{3}{2}\lambda}\sum_{2\lambda\leq|k|<3\lambda}\Big|\frac{\psi(j)-\psi(k)}{|j|^{2}-|k|^{2}}U_{jk}\Big|\lesssim\lambda^{n-2\eta-\varepsilon},

Similarly, for (

\displaystyle\sum_{\lambda/2<|j|\leq\frac{3}{2}\lambda}

\displaystyle\sum_{2\lambda\leq|k|<3\lambda}\Big|\frac{\psi(j)-\psi(k)}{|j|^{2}-|k|^{2}}U_{jk}\Big|

\displaystyle\lesssim\sum_{\lambda/2<|j|\leq\frac{3}{2}\lambda}\sum_{2\lambda\leq|k|<3\lambda}((1+2^{-\ell_{0}}||j|-\lambda|)^{-N}+\lambda^{-N})\lambda^{-2}\lambda^{-n+2-\eta}

Moreover, (

\sum_{j}\sum_{k}\Big|\frac{\psi(j)-\psi(k)}{|j|^{2}-|k|^{2}}\Big|(1+|j-k|)^{-n+1-\eta}\lesssim\lambda^{n-\eta-\varepsilon+\sigma},\ \forall\sigma>0.

\sum_{j}\sum_{k}\Big|\frac{{\rm 1\hskip-1.72218pt\rule{0.43057pt}{6.54443pt}\hskip 0.86108pt}_{\lambda}(|j|)-{\rm 1\hskip-1.72218pt\rule{0.43057pt}{6.54443pt}\hskip 0.86108pt}_{\lambda}(|k|)}{|j|^{2}-|k|^{2}}\Big|(1+|j-k|)^{-n+1-\eta}\lesssim\lambda^{n-\eta-1}.

,
[

.

in (

). For simplicity, we just assume
V\in L^{\frac{n}{2-\eta}}(M)
, though actually the original

is in

,

. Applying the same argument to the original

Let
\ell_{0}
be the largest integer such that

For

and

, let

(3.3)

\displaystyle a_{jk\tau_{\ell}}=

\displaystyle\frac{f(|k|)-f(\tau_{\ell})}{|k|^{2}-\tau_{\ell}^{2}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{h(|k|)-h(|j|)}{(|k|^{2}-|j|^{2})(|k|^{2}-\tau_{\ell}^{2})}+\frac{h(\tau_{\ell})-h(|j|)}{(\tau_{\ell}^{2}-|j|^{2})(\tau_{\ell}^{2}-|k|^{2})}

\displaystyle a_{jk\tau_{\ell}}^{0}+a_{jk\tau_{\ell}}^{1}.

Now we rewrite

3.1.
Some useful lemmas

To prove the desired bound for
R_{2}
, we need the following lemma which separate the contribution of the various components in (
3.5
) above. Fix
p_{0}=\frac{2n}{n-2+\eta}
. Then Sogge’s exponent

\sigma(p_{0})=\begin{cases}\frac{(n-1)(2-\eta)}{4n},\ \ \ \eta>\frac{2}{n+1}\\
\frac{1-\eta}{2},\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \eta\leq\frac{2}{n+1}\ \ .\end{cases}

Note that for
\eta\in(0,1]
we always have
\sigma(p_{0})\leq\frac{1}{2}-\frac{1}{4}\eta
.

Lemma 3.2
.

Let
I\subset{\mathbb{R}}_{+}
and for
eigenvalues
\tau_{\ell}\in I
assume that
\delta_{\tau_{\ell}}\in[0,\delta]
. Then if
m\in C^{1}({\mathbb{R}}_{+}\times M)
, and
V\in L^{\frac{n}{2-\eta}}(M)
, we have

(3.7)

\int_{M}\Bigl|\sum_{\tau_{\ell}\in I}m(\delta_{\tau_{\ell}},y)\,a_{\ell}V(y)e_{\tau_{\ell}}(y)\Bigr|\,dy\\
\leq\|V\|_{L^{\frac{n}{2-\eta}}(M)}\cdot\Bigl(\,\|m(0,\,\cdot\,)\|_{L^{p_{0}}(M)}+\int_{0}^{\delta}\bigl\|\tfrac{\partial}{\partial s}m(s,\,\cdot\,)\bigr\|_{L^{p_{0}}(M)}\,ds\,\Bigr)\\
\times\sup_{s\in[0,\delta]}\bigl\|\sum_{\tau_{\ell}\in I}{\rm 1\hskip-1.72218pt\rule{0.43057pt}{6.54443pt}\hskip 0.86108pt}_{[0,\delta_{\tau_{\ell}}]}(s)a_{\ell}e_{\tau_{\ell}}\,\bigr\|_{L^{p_{0}}(M)}.

Proof.

We shall use the fact that

m(\delta_{\tau_{\ell}},y)=m(0,y)+\int_{0}^{\delta}{\rm 1\hskip-1.72218pt\rule{0.43057pt}{6.54443pt}\hskip 0.86108pt}_{[0,\delta_{\tau_{\ell}}]}(s)\,\tfrac{\partial}{\partial s}m(s,y)\,ds,

where
{\rm 1\hskip-1.72218pt\rule{0.43057pt}{6.54443pt}\hskip 0.86108pt}_{[0,\delta_{\tau_{\ell}}]}(s)
is the indicator function of the interval
[0,\delta_{\tau_{\ell}}]\subset[0,\delta]
.
Therefore, by Hölder’s inequality and Minkowski’s inequality,
the left side of (
3.7
) is dominated by
\|V\|_{L^{\frac{n}{2-\eta}}(M)}
times

\displaystyle\big(\int_{M}\bigl|\,m(0,y)\cdot\sum_{\tau_{\ell}\in I}a_{\ell}e_{\tau_{\ell}}(y)\,\bigr|^{\frac{n}{n-2+\eta}}\,dy\big)^{\frac{n-2+\eta}{n}}

\displaystyle+

\displaystyle c^{0}_{jk\tau_{\ell}}+c^{1}_{jk\tau_{\ell}}.

The arguments that we shall use to control terms involving
c^{0}_{jk\tau_{\ell}}
and
c^{1}_{jk\tau_{\ell}}
are similar, so for simplicity we shall only give the details for

c^{0}_{jk\tau_{\ell}}
here. Recall that by definition,

V_{jk}=\int_{M}\overline{e_{j}^{0}(y)}e_{k}^{0}(y)V(y)dy.

Thus, it suffices to estimate

\displaystyle\sum_{j,k,\tau_{\ell}}\beta_{\ell_{1}}(\tau_{\ell}-|k|)\beta_{\ell_{2}}(\tau_{\ell}-|j|)\iint c^{0}_{jk\tau_{\ell}}\,e_{j}^{0}(x)\overline{e_{j}^{0}(y)}e_{k}^{0}(y)V(y)\overline{e_{k}^{0}(z)}V(z)e_{\tau_{\ell}}(z)\overline{e_{\tau_{\ell}}(x)}dzdy

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{\tau_{\ell}\in[1,2\lambda]}\iint K_{2}(\tau_{\ell},x,y)K_{1}(\tau_{\ell},y,z)h(\tau_{\ell})V(y)V(z)e_{\tau_{\ell}}(z)\overline{e_{\tau_{\ell}}(x)}dzdy

where

K_{\nu}(\tau_{\ell},x,y)=\sum_{j}\frac{\beta_{\ell_{\nu}}(\tau_{\ell}-|j|)}{\tau_{\ell}^{2}-|j|^{2}}e_{j}^{0}(x)\overline{e_{j}^{0}(y)},\,\,\,\nu=1,2.

We claim that
K_{\nu}(\tau_{\ell},x,y)
are pseudo-differential operators and their kernels satisfy

(4.18)

|K_{\nu}(\tau_{\ell},x,y)|\lesssim 2^{(n-2)\ell_{\nu}}(1+2^{\ell_{\nu}}|x-y|)^{-N},\ \forall\,N.

To prove the claim (
4.18
), we observe that
K_{\nu}(\tau_{\ell},x,y)
is the kernel of
2^{-2\ell_{\nu}}m(P^{0}/2^{\ell_{\nu}})
where

m(t)=\beta(2^{-\ell_{\nu}}\tau_{\ell}-t)((2^{-\ell_{\nu}}\tau_{\ell})^{2}-t^{2})^{-1}.

Since
2^{-\ell_{\nu}}\tau_{\ell}\lesssim 1
and
\beta\in C_{0}^{\infty}
,
m(t)
is a symbol of order
\mu
for any
\mu<-n
, i.e.

|\partial_{t}^{\alpha}m(t)|\leq C_{\alpha}(1+|t|)^{\mu-\alpha},\ \forall\alpha.

Then the claim follows from Lemma
3.3
.

Moreover, by using the rough eigenfunction bounds (
1.1
),

(4.19)

\sum_{\tau_{\ell}\in[1,2\lambda]}\big|h(\tau_{\ell})e_{\tau_{\ell}}(z)\overline{e_{\tau_{\ell}}(x)}\big|\lesssim\lambda^{n},\,\,\,\forall\,z,\,\,x\in M.

By (
4.19
) and using Hölder’s inequality twice, we have

\displaystyle\sum_{\tau_{\ell}\in[1,2\lambda]}\iint K_{2}(\tau_{\ell},x,y)K_{1}(\tau_{\ell},y,z)h(\tau_{\ell})V(y)V(z)e_{\tau_{\ell}}(z)\overline{e_{\tau_{\ell}}(x)}dzdy

\displaystyle\lesssim\lambda^{n}\,\|V\|^{2}_{L^{\frac{n}{2-\eta}}(M)}\sup_{\tau_{\ell},x}\|K_{2}(\tau_{\ell},x,\cdot)\|_{L^{\frac{n}{n-2+\eta}}(M)}\cdot\sup_{\tau_{\ell},y}\|K_{1}(\tau_{\ell},y,\cdot)\|_{L^{\frac{n}{n-2+\eta}}(M)}

\displaystyle\lesssim\lambda^{n}2^{-\ell_{1}\eta}2^{-\ell_{2}\eta}.

Summing over
\ell_{1},\ell_{2}
, we get the desired bound
\lambda^{n-2\eta}
.

For the other term
c_{jk\tau_{\ell}}^{1}
, we just need to replace
K_{2}
above by

K_{2}(\tau_{\ell},x,y)=\sum_{j}\frac{\beta_{\ell_{2}}(\tau_{\ell}-|j|)}{\tau_{\ell}^{2}-|j|^{2}}h(|j|)e_{j}^{0}(x)\overline{e_{j}^{0}(y)}

and the kernel estimate (
4.18
) still holds by Lemma
3.3
. Moreover, by using the rough eigenfunction bounds (
1.1
), we have

\sum_{\tau_{\ell}\in[1,2\lambda]}\big|e_{\tau_{\ell}}(z)\overline{e_{\tau_{\ell}}(x)}\big|\lesssim\lambda^{n},\,\,\,\forall\,z,x\in M.

Then we can get the same bound
\lambda^{n-2\eta}
by repeating the argument above.

Next, we handle
a^{0}_{jk\tau_{\ell}}
. We consider three subcases separately:
2^{\ell_{2}}\approx 2^{\ell_{1}}
,
2^{\ell_{2}}<2^{\ell_{1}-3}
,
2^{\ell_{2}}>2^{\ell_{1}+3}
. Since
|k|,|j|>2\tau_{\ell}
, we have
|k|\approx|j|
,
|k|\geq 2|j|
,
|j|\geq 2|k|
respectively in these cases.

(i)

2^{\ell_{1}}\approx 2^{\ell_{2}}
, namely
|\ell_{1}-\ell_{2}|\leq 3
.

In this case,
|k|\approx|j|\approx 2^{\ell_{1}}
, so
by the mean value theorem,

\Big|\frac{h(|k|)-h(|j|)}{|k|^{2}-|j|^{2}}\Big|\lesssim 2^{-\ell_{1}}|h^{\prime}(|k|)|\lesssim 2^{-\ell_{1}}2^{-\ell_{0}}2^{-N\ell_{1}},\,\,\,\forall N.

Then

|a_{jk\tau_{\ell}}^{0}|\lesssim 2^{-3\ell_{1}}2^{-\ell_{0}}2^{-N\ell_{1}}\lesssim 2^{-N\ell_{1}}.

By (
1.1
) and Hölder’s inequality,

\displaystyle|\sum_{j,k,\tau_{\ell}}\beta_{\ell_{1}}(\tau_{\ell}-|k|)\beta_{\ell_{2}}(\tau_{\ell}-|j|)\iint a^{0}_{jk\tau_{\ell}}\,e_{j}^{0}(x)\overline{e_{j}^{0}(y)}V(y)e_{k}^{0}(y)\overline{e_{k}^{0}(z)}V(z)e_{\tau_{\ell}}(z)\overline{e_{\tau_{\ell}}(x)}dydz|

\displaystyle\lesssim\|V\|_{L^{1}(M)}^{2}\sum_{|j|\approx 2^{\ell_{1}}}\sum_{|k|\approx 2^{\ell_{1}}}2^{-N\ell_{1}}\cdot\lambda^{n}

\displaystyle\approx 2^{2n\ell_{1}}2^{-N\ell_{1}}\cdot\lambda^{n}.

Summing over
\ell_{1},\ell_{2}
, we get
\lambda^{3n-N}
, which is bounded by a constant for large
N
.

(ii)

2^{\ell_{2}}>2^{\ell_{1}+3}
.

In this case,
2^{\ell_{2}}\approx|j|\geq 2|k|\approx 2^{\ell_{1}}
. We write

\displaystyle a^{0}_{jk\tau_{\ell}}=

\displaystyle\frac{h(|k|)}{(|k|^{2}-|j|^{2})(|k|^{2}-\tau_{\ell}^{2})}-\frac{h(|j|)}{(|k|^{2}-|j|^{2})(|k|^{2}-\tau_{\ell}^{2})}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle d^{0}_{jk\tau_{\ell}}+d^{1}_{jk\tau_{\ell}}

The second term satisfies

|d_{jk\tau_{\ell}}^{1}|\lesssim 2^{-N\ell_{2}},\,\,\,\forall N,

which implies

\displaystyle|\sum_{j,k,\tau_{\ell}}\beta_{\ell_{1}}(\tau_{\ell}-|k|)\beta_{\ell_{2}}(\tau_{\ell}-|j|)\iint d^{1}_{jk\tau_{\ell}}\,e_{j}^{0}(x)\overline{e_{j}^{0}(y)}V(y)e_{k}^{0}(y)\overline{e_{k}^{0}(z)}V(z)e_{\tau_{\ell}}(z)\overline{e_{\tau_{\ell}}(x)}dz|

\displaystyle\lesssim\|V\|_{L^{1}(M)}^{2}\sum_{|j|\approx 2^{\ell_{2}}}\sum_{|k|\approx 2^{\ell_{1}}}2^{-N\ell_{2}}\cdot\lambda^{n}

\displaystyle\approx 2^{n\ell_{2}}2^{n\ell_{1}}2^{-N\ell_{2}}\cdot\lambda^{n}.

Summing over
\ell_{1}
,
\ell_{2}
, we get
\lambda^{3n-N}
, which is bounded by a constant for large
N
.

It remains to deal with
d_{jk\tau_{\ell}}^{0}
. We write

\displaystyle\sum_{j,k,\tau_{\ell}}\beta_{\ell_{1}}

\displaystyle(\tau_{\ell}-|k|)\beta_{\ell_{2}}(\tau_{\ell}-|j|)\iint d^{0}_{jk\tau_{\ell}}\,e_{j}^{0}(x)\overline{e_{j}^{0}(y)}e_{k}^{0}(y)V(y)\overline{e_{k}^{0}(z)}V(z)e_{\tau_{\ell}}(z)\overline{e_{\tau_{\ell}}(x)}dzdy

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{k,\tau_{\ell}}\iint K_{2}(\tau_{\ell},k,x,y)K_{1}(\tau_{\ell},k,y,z)V(y)V(z)e_{\tau_{\ell}}(z)\overline{e_{\tau_{\ell}}(x)}dzdy

where

K_{2}(\tau_{\ell},k,x,y)=\sum_{j}\frac{\beta_{\ell_{2}}(\tau_{\ell}-|j|)}{|k|^{2}-|j|^{2}}e_{j}^{0}(x)\overline{e_{j}^{0}(y)},

and

K_{1}(\tau_{\ell},k,y,z)=\frac{\beta_{\ell_{1}}(\tau_{\ell}-|k|)}{|k|^{2}-\tau_{\ell}^{2}}h(|k|)e_{k}^{0}(y)\overline{e_{k}^{0}(z)}.

By Lemma
3.3
, if we argue as in the proof of (
4.18
), we can see that
K_{2}
is a pseudo-differential operators and its kernel
K_{2}(\tau_{\ell},k,x,y)
satisfies

(4.20)

\sup_{\tau_{\ell},k}|K_{2}(\tau_{\ell},k,x,y)|\lesssim 2^{(n-2)\ell_{2}}(1+2^{\ell_{2}}|x-y|)^{-N}\,\,\,\forall N,

Moreover,

(4.21)

\sup_{\tau_{\ell},k,y,z}|K_{1}(\tau_{\ell},k,y,z)|\lesssim 2^{-N\ell_{1}},\,\,\,\forall N.

By Hölder’s inequality and (
1.1
), we have

\displaystyle\sum_{|k|\approx 2^{\ell_{1}}}\sum_{\tau_{\ell}}\iint K_{2}(\tau_{\ell},k,x,y)K_{1}(\tau_{\ell},k,x,y)V(y)V(z)e_{\tau_{\ell}}(z)\overline{e_{\tau_{\ell}}(x)}dzdy

\displaystyle\lesssim 2^{n\ell_{1}}\lambda^{n}\,\|V\|_{L^{\frac{n}{2-\eta}}(M)}\|V\|_{L^{1}(M)}\sup_{\tau_{\ell},k,x}\|K_{2}(\tau_{\ell},k,x,\cdot)\|_{L^{\frac{n}{n-2+\eta}}(M)}\sup_{\tau_{\ell},k,y}\|K_{1}(\tau_{\ell},k,y,\cdot)\|_{L^{\infty}(M)}

\displaystyle\lesssim 2^{n\ell_{1}}\lambda^{n}2^{-\eta\ell_{2}}2^{-N\ell_{1}}.

Summing over
\ell_{1},\ell_{2}
, we get
\lambda^{2n-\eta-N}
, which is bounded by a constant for large
N
.

(iii)

2^{\ell_{2}}<2^{\ell_{1}-3}
.

The argument is similar to (ii), but we give the details for completeness. In this case,
2^{\ell_{1}}\approx|k|\geq 2|j|\approx 2^{\ell_{2}}
. As before, we write

\displaystyle a^{0}_{jk\tau_{\ell}}=

\displaystyle\frac{h(|k|)}{(|k|^{2}-|j|^{2})(|k|^{2}-\tau_{\ell}^{2})}-\frac{h(|j|)}{(|k|^{2}-|j|^{2})(|k|^{2}-\tau_{\ell}^{2})}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle d^{0}_{jk\tau_{\ell}}+d^{1}_{jk\tau_{\ell}}

The first term satisfies

|d_{jk\tau_{\ell}}^{0}|\lesssim 2^{-N\ell_{1}},\ \forall N.

which implies

\displaystyle|\sum_{j,k,\tau_{\ell}}\beta_{\ell_{1}}(\tau_{\ell}-|k|)\beta_{\ell_{2}}(\tau_{\ell}-|j|)\iint d^{0}_{jk\tau_{\ell}}\,e_{j}^{0}(x)\overline{e_{j}^{0}(y)}V(y)e_{k}^{0}(y)\overline{e_{k}^{0}(z)}V(z)e_{\tau_{\ell}}(z)\overline{e_{\tau_{\ell}}(x)}dz|

\displaystyle\lesssim\|V\|_{L^{1}(M)}^{2}\sum_{|j|\approx 2^{\ell_{2}}}\sum_{|k|\approx 2^{\ell_{1}}}2^{-N\ell_{1}}\cdot\lambda^{n}

\displaystyle\approx 2^{n\ell_{2}}2^{n\ell_{1}}2^{-N\ell_{1}}\cdot\lambda^{n}.

Summing over
\ell_{1}
,
\ell_{2}
we get
\lambda^{3n-N}
, which is bounded by a constant for large
N
.

Now it remains to handle
d_{jk\tau_{\ell}}^{1}
. We write

\displaystyle\sum_{j,k,\tau_{\ell}}\beta_{\ell_{1}}

\displaystyle(\tau_{\ell}-|k|)\beta_{\ell_{2}}(\tau_{\ell}-|j|)\iint d^{1}_{jk\tau_{\ell}}\,e_{j}^{0}(x)\overline{e_{j}^{0}(y)}e_{k}^{0}(y)V(y)\overline{e_{k}^{0}(z)}V(z)e_{\tau_{\ell}}(z)\overline{e_{\tau_{\ell}}(x)}dzdy\big|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{\tau_{\ell},j}\iint K_{2}(\tau_{\ell},j,x,y)K_{1}(\tau_{\ell},j,y,z)V(y)V(z)e_{\tau_{\ell}}(z)\overline{e_{\tau_{\ell}}(x)}dzdy

where

K_{2}(\tau_{\ell},j,x,y)=\beta_{\ell_{2}}(\tau_{\ell}-|j|)h(|j|)e_{j}^{0}(x)\overline{e_{j}^{0}(y)},

and

K_{1}(\tau_{\ell},j,y,z)=\sum_{k}\frac{\beta_{\ell_{1}}(\tau_{\ell}-|k|)}{(|k|^{2}-|j|^{2})(|k|^{2}-|\tau_{\ell}|^{2})}e_{k}^{0}(y)\overline{e_{k}^{0}(z)}.

By Lemma
3.3
, if we argue as in the proof of (
4.18
), we can show that
K_{1}
is a pseudo-differential operator and its kernel
K_{1}(\tau_{\ell},j,y,z)
satisfies

(4.22)

\sup_{\tau_{\ell},j}|K_{1}(\tau_{\ell},j,y,z)|\lesssim 2^{(n-4)\ell_{1}}(1+2^{\ell_{1}}|y-z|)^{-N},\,\,\,\forall N,

Moreover,

\sup_{\tau_{\ell},j,x,y}|K_{2}(\tau_{\ell},j,x,y)|\lesssim 2^{-N\ell_{2}},\,\,\,\forall N.

By Hölder’s inequality and (
1.1
), we have

\displaystyle\sum_{|j|\approx 2^{\ell_{2}}}\sum_{\tau_{\ell}}\iint K_{2}(\tau_{\ell},j,x,y)K_{1}(\tau_{\ell},j,y,z)V(y)V(z)e_{\tau_{\ell}}(z)\overline{e_{\tau_{\ell}}(x)}dzdy

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle 2^{n\ell_{2}}\lambda^{n}\,\|V\|_{L^{\frac{n}{2-\eta}}(M)}\|V\|_{L^{1}(M)}\sup_{\tau_{\ell},j,x}\|K_{2}(\tau_{\ell},j,x,\cdot)\|_{L^{\infty}(M)}\sup_{\tau_{\ell},j,y}\|K_{1}(\tau_{\ell},j,y,\cdot)\|_{L^{\frac{n}{n-2+\eta}}(M)}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle 2^{n\ell_{2}}\lambda^{n}2^{-N\ell_{2}}2^{-\ell_{1}(\eta+2)}.

Summing over
\ell_{1},\ell_{2}
, we get
\lambda^{2n-2-\eta-N}
, which is bounded by a constant for large
N
.

5.
High-frequency estimates

In this section we shall handle the high frequencies
\tau_{\ell}>2\lambda
. Our goal is to show that

(5.1)

\Big|\sum_{j,k}\sum_{\tau_{\ell}>2\lambda}\int a_{jk\tau_{\ell}}\,e_{j}^{0}(x)V_{jk}\overline{e_{k}^{0}(z)}V(z)e_{\tau_{\ell}}(z)\overline{e_{\tau_{\ell}}(x)}dz\Big|\lesssim\lambda^{n-\frac{3}{2}\eta}\log\lambda,

for
a_{jk\tau_{\ell}}
defined as in (
3.4
).

We shall divide our discussion into the following three cases:

\bullet

\tau_{\ell}\approx|k|

\bullet

\tau_{\ell}\lesssim|k|

\bullet

\tau_{\ell}\gtrsim|k|
.

The first two cases can be handled in a way that is similar to the low-frequency case using the decay properties of
h(\tau)
for large
\tau
as in (
1.5
). More explicitly,
the first case is straightforward and can be handled by Lemma
3.2
. The second case consists of two subcases: Case 2.1 (
|j|\lesssim\tau_{\ell}\lesssim|k|
) and Case 2.2 (
|j|,|k|\gtrsim\tau_{\ell}
). We still apply Lemma
3.2
to the first one, and use the kernel estimates of pseudo-differential operators (Lemma
3.3
) for the other. The third case is more involved, and
a_{jk\tau_{\ell}}^{0}
,
a_{jk\tau_{\ell}}^{1}
are treated seperately. We first split the term with
a_{jk\tau_{\ell}}^{0}
into three parts (see (
5.31
)), and handle them in Cases 3.1.1, 3.1.2, 3.1.3. The first one is handled by the heat kernel estimates (Lemma
1.2
), while the other two follow from Lemma
3.2
. Next, to handle the term with
a_{jk\tau_{\ell}}^{1}
, we expand

\frac{1}{\tau_{\ell}^{2}-|j|^{2}}=\tau_{\ell}^{-2}+\tau_{\ell}^{-2}\bigl(|j|/\tau_{\ell}\bigr)^{2}+\cdots+\tau_{\ell}^{-2}\bigl(|j|/\tau_{\ell}\bigr)^{2N-2}\\
+(|j|/\tau_{\ell})^{2N}\frac{1}{\tau_{\ell}^{2}-|j|^{2}}

and expand
\frac{1}{\tau_{\ell}^{2}-|k|^{2}}
similarly. Then we split the product of the two expansions into three parts, and handle them seperately in Cases 3.2.1, 3.2.2, 3.2.3. The first two cases follow from Lemma
3.2
. To handle Case 3.2.3, we split it into three cases (see (
5.57
)): Cases 3.2.3a, 3.2.3b, 3.2.3c. Only Case 3.2.3a is handled by the heat kernel estimates (Lemma
1.2
), while the remaining cases still follow from Lemma
3.2
.

Moreover, Lemma
1.1
(spectral projection bounds) and Corollary
1.3
(rough eigenfunction bounds) are basic tools applied to all the cases.

Case 1

\tau_{\ell}\approx|k|
.

Fix a smooth bump function
\phi\in C_{0}^{\infty}(\mathbb{R})
satisfying
{\rm 1\hskip-1.72218pt\rule{0.43057pt}{6.54443pt}\hskip 0.86108pt}_{[0.9,1.1]}\leq\phi\leq{\rm 1\hskip-1.72218pt\rule{0.43057pt}{6.54443pt}\hskip 0.86108pt}_{[0.8,1.2]}
. Our current task is to show that

(5.2)

|\sum_{j,k}\sum_{\tau_{\ell}>2\lambda}\phi(|k|/\tau_{\ell})\int a_{jk\tau_{\ell}}\,e_{j}^{0}(x)V_{jk}\overline{e_{k}^{0}(z)}V(z)e_{\tau_{\ell}}(z)\overline{e_{\tau_{\ell}}(x)}dz|\lesssim\lambda^{n-\frac{3}{2}\eta}.

Let
\tau_{\ell}\in[2^{s},2^{s+1}]
,
2^{s}>2\lambda
. In this case, we have
0.8\tau_{\ell}\leq|k|\leq 1.2\tau_{\ell}
, and then
|k|\approx 2^{s}
.
If
|j|>1.3\tau_{\ell}
, then
|j|>\frac{13}{12}|k|
and by (
1.5
)

|a_{jk\tau_{\ell}}|\lesssim|j|^{-2}2^{-Ns},\ \forall N.

If
0.7\tau_{\ell}\leq|j|\leq 1.3\tau_{\ell}
, then by the mean value theorem, if we argue as in the proof of (
4.9
), one can show that

|a_{jk\tau_{\ell}}|\lesssim 2^{-Ns},\ \forall N.

If
|j|<0.7\tau_{\ell}
, then similarly by the mean value theorem,

|a_{jk\tau_{\ell}}|\lesssim 2^{-4s}.

Let

b_{k,\tau_{\ell}}=\sum_{j}\phi(|k|/\tau_{\ell})a_{jk\tau_{\ell}}\,e_{j}^{0}(x)V_{jk}.

Combining the estimates on
a_{jk\tau_{\ell}}
with the bound (
3.6
) on
V_{jk}
, we get

|b_{k,\tau_{\ell}}|\lesssim\lambda^{n}2^{(-n-2-\eta)s}

and similarly

|\partial_{\tau_{\ell}}b_{k,\tau_{\ell}}|\lesssim\lambda^{n}2^{(-n-2-\eta)s}2^{-s}.

Thus by Sobolev inequality, we have

(5.3)

\displaystyle\|\sum_{|k|\approx 2^{s}}b_{k,2^{s}}e_{k}^{0}(\cdot)\|_{L^{p_{0}}(M)}

\displaystyle\lesssim 2^{s/2}2^{\sigma(p_{0})s}\cdot 2^{ns/2}\cdot\lambda^{n}2^{(-n-2-\eta)s}

\displaystyle\lesssim\lambda^{n}2^{(-n/2-1-\frac{5}{4}\eta)s}

And similarly for
\tau\in[2^{s},2^{s+1}]

(5.4)

\|\sum_{|k|\approx 2^{s}}\partial_{\tau}b_{k,\tau}e_{k}^{0}(\cdot)\|_{L^{p_{0}}(M)}\lesssim\lambda^{n}2^{(-n/2-1-\frac{5}{4}\eta)s}2^{-s}.

In view of (
5.3
) and (
5.4
), by applying Lemma
3.2
with
\delta=2^{s}
, we have

(5.5)

\displaystyle|\int

\displaystyle\sum_{\tau_{\ell}\in[2^{s},2^{s+1}]}\sum_{k}b_{k,\tau_{\ell}}\overline{e_{k}^{0}(z)}V(z)e_{\tau_{\ell}}(z)\overline{e_{\tau_{\ell}}(x)}dz|

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\|V\|_{L^{\frac{n}{2-\eta}}(M)}\lambda^{n}2^{(-n/2-1-\frac{5}{4}\eta)s}\cdot 2^{s/2}2^{\sigma(p_{0})s}(\sum_{\tau_{\ell}\in[2^{s},2^{s+1}]}|e_{\tau_{\ell}}(x)|^{2})^{1/2}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\|V\|_{L^{\frac{n}{2-\eta}}(M)}\lambda^{n}2^{(-n/2-1-\frac{5}{4}\eta)s}\cdot 2^{s/2}2^{(\frac{1}{2}-\frac{1}{4}\eta)s}2^{ns/2}.

Summing over
s
, we get the desired bound
\lambda^{n-\frac{3}{2}\eta}
.

Case 2

\tau_{\ell}\lesssim|k|
.

Let
\rho_{1}=(1-\phi){\rm 1\hskip-1.72218pt\rule{0.43057pt}{6.54443pt}\hskip 0.86108pt}_{(1,\infty)}\in C^{\infty}(\mathbb{R})
. We have
{\rm 1\hskip-1.72218pt\rule{0.43057pt}{6.54443pt}\hskip 0.86108pt}_{[1.2,\infty)}\leq\rho_{1}\leq{\rm 1\hskip-1.72218pt\rule{0.43057pt}{6.54443pt}\hskip 0.86108pt}_{[1.1,\infty)}
. Our goal in this section is

(5.6)

|\sum_{j,k}\sum_{\tau_{\ell}>2\lambda}\rho_{1}(|k|/\tau_{\ell})\int a_{jk\tau_{\ell}}\,e_{j}^{0}(x)V_{jk}\overline{e_{k}^{0}(z)}V(z)e_{\tau_{\ell}}(z)\overline{e_{\tau_{\ell}}(x)}dz|\lesssim\lambda^{n-\frac{3}{2}\eta}\log\lambda.

Let
\tau_{\ell}\in[2^{s},2^{s+1}]
,
2^{s}>2\lambda
. In this case, we have
|k|\geq 1.1\tau_{\ell}>2.2\lambda
.
We just need to estimate

\sum_{\tau_{\ell}\in[2^{s},2^{s+1}]}\sum_{j,k}\rho_{1}(|k|/\tau_{\ell})\beta_{\ell_{1}}(|k|)\beta_{\ell_{2}}(|j|)\int a_{jk\tau_{\ell}}\,e_{j}^{0}(x)V_{jk}\overline{e_{k}^{0}(z)}V(z)e_{\tau_{\ell}}(z)\overline{e_{\tau_{\ell}}(x)}dz

for
\ell_{1}\geq s,\ell_{2}\geq\ell_{0}
.
We consider two cases separately:
2^{\ell_{2}}\leq 2^{s+2}
,
2^{\ell_{2}}>2^{s+2}
.

Case 2.1

2^{\ell_{2}}\leq 2^{s+2}
.

In this case, we have
|k|\pm\tau_{\ell}\approx|k|+|j|\approx|k|\approx 2^{\ell_{1}}
,
\tau_{\ell}+|j|\approx 2^{s}
. So we get for all
N
,

|a_{jk\tau_{\ell}}^{0}|\lesssim\begin{cases}2^{-4\ell_{1}},\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ |j|\leq 1.2\lambda\\
2^{-4\ell_{1}}2^{-N\ell_{2}},\ \ \ 1.2\lambda<|j|<0.8|k|\\
2^{-N\ell_{1}},\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ |j|\geq 0.8|k|\end{cases}

and

|a_{jk\tau_{\ell}}^{1}|\lesssim\begin{cases}2^{-2\ell_{1}}2^{-2s},\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ |j|<1.2\lambda\\
2^{-2\ell_{1}}2^{-2s}2^{-N\ell_{2}},\ \ \ 1.2\lambda<|j|<0.8\tau_{\ell}\\
2^{-2\ell_{1}}2^{-Ns},\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ |j|\geq 0.8\tau_{\ell}.\end{cases}

So we have

|a_{jk\tau_{\ell}}|\lesssim\begin{cases}2^{-2\ell_{1}}2^{-2s},\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ |j|\leq 1.2\lambda\\
2^{-2\ell_{1}}2^{-2s}2^{-N\ell_{2}},\ \ \ 1.2\lambda<|j|<0.8|k|\\
2^{-2\ell_{1}}2^{-Ns},\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ |j|\geq 0.8|k|.\end{cases}

Let

b_{k,\tau_{\ell}}=\sum_{j}\rho_{1}(|k|/\tau_{\ell})\beta_{\ell_{1}}(|k|)\beta_{\ell_{2}}(|j|)a_{jk\tau_{\ell}}\,e_{j}^{0}(x)V_{jk}.

Combining the estimates on
a_{jk\tau_{\ell}}
with the bound (
3.6
) on
V_{jk}
, we have

|b_{k,\tau_{\ell}}|\lesssim\lambda^{n}2^{(-n-\eta)\ell_{1}}2^{-2s}

and similarly

|\partial_{\tau_{\ell}}b_{k,\tau_{\ell}}|\lesssim\lambda^{n}2^{(-n-\eta)\ell_{1}}2^{-3s}.

By using Sobolev inequality, we have

(5.7)

\displaystyle\|\sum_{|k|\approx 2^{\ell_{1}}}b_{k,2^{s}}e_{k}^{0}(\cdot)\|_{L^{p_{0}}(M)}

\displaystyle\lesssim 2^{\ell_{1}/2}2^{\sigma(p_{0})\ell_{1}}2^{n\ell_{1}/2}\cdot\lambda^{n}2^{(-n-\eta)\ell_{1}}2^{-2s}

\displaystyle\approx\lambda^{n}2^{(-n/2+1-\frac{5}{4}\eta)\ell_{1}}2^{-2s}.

And similarly for
\tau\in[2^{s},2^{s+1}]

(5.8)

Now we handle these three terms in Cases 3.2.3a, 3.2.3b, 3.2.3c respectively.

Case 3.2.3a

We need to show

(5.58)

\displaystyle|

\displaystyle\sum_{j,k}\sum_{\tau_{\ell}>2\lambda}\int h(|j|)|j|^{2\mu_{2}}|k|^{2\mu_{1}}c^{0}_{k,\tau_{\ell}}\,e_{j}^{0}(x)V_{jk}\overline{e_{k}^{0}(z)}V(z)e_{\tau_{\ell}}(z)\overline{e_{\tau_{\ell}}(x)}dz|

\displaystyle\lesssim\lambda^{n-\frac{3}{2}\eta}.

We shall divide our task into proving

(5.59)

\displaystyle|

\displaystyle\sum_{j,k}\sum_{\tau_{\ell}\leq 2\lambda}\int h(|j|)|j|^{2\mu_{2}}|k|^{2\mu_{1}}c^{0}_{k,\tau_{\ell}}\,e_{j}^{0}(x)V_{jk}\overline{e_{k}^{0}(z)}V(z)e_{\tau_{\ell}}(z)\overline{e_{\tau_{\ell}}(x)}dz|

\displaystyle\lesssim\lambda^{n-\frac{3}{2}\eta}

as well as

(5.60)

\displaystyle|

\displaystyle\sum_{j,k}\sum_{\tau_{\ell}}\int h(|j|)|j|^{2\mu_{2}}|k|^{2\mu_{1}}c^{0}_{k,\tau_{\ell}}\,e_{j}^{0}(x)V_{jk}\overline{e_{k}^{0}(z)}V(z)e_{\tau_{\ell}}(z)\overline{e_{\tau_{\ell}}(x)}dz|

\displaystyle\lesssim\lambda^{n-2\eta}.

Let

b_{k}=\sum_{|j|\in I_{\ell_{2}}}h(|j|)|j|^{2\mu_{2}}|k|^{2\mu_{1}}e_{j}^{0}(x)V_{jk}.

Then for
|k|\in I_{\ell_{1}}
, we have

(5.61)

\displaystyle|b_{k}|\lesssim 2^{2\mu_{1}\ell_{1}}2^{(2\mu_{2}+2-\eta)\ell_{2}}\cdot\begin{cases}1,\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ 2^{\ell_{1}}\leq 80\lambda\ {\rm and}\ 2^{\ell_{2}}\approx 10\lambda\\
2^{-N_{1}\ell_{2}},\ \ \ \ \ \ \ 2^{\ell_{1}}\leq 80\lambda\ {\rm and}\ 2^{\ell_{2}}>10\lambda\\
2^{-N_{1}\ell_{2}},\ \ \ \ \ \ \ 2^{\ell_{1}}>80\lambda\ {\rm and}\ 2^{\ell_{2}}>2^{\ell_{1}-2}\\
2^{(n-2+\eta)(\ell_{2}-\ell_{1})},\ \ 2^{\ell_{1}}>80\lambda\ {\rm and}\ 2^{\ell_{2}}\approx 10\lambda\\
2^{(n-2+\eta)(\ell_{2}-\ell_{1})}2^{-N_{1}\ell_{2}},\ \ 2^{\ell_{1}}>80\lambda\ {\rm and}\ 10\lambda<2^{\ell_{2}}\leq 2^{\ell_{1}-2}.\end{cases}

By using Sobolev inequality,

\displaystyle\|

\displaystyle\sum_{|k|\in I_{\ell_{1}}}b_{k}e_{k}^{0}(\cdot)\|_{L^{p_{0}}(M)}\lesssim 2^{\ell_{1}/2}2^{\sigma(p_{0})\ell_{1}}\|\sum_{|k|\in I_{\ell_{1}}}b_{k}e_{k}^{0}(\cdot)\|_{L^{2}(M)}

\displaystyle\lesssim 2^{(n/2+1-\frac{1}{4}\eta)\ell_{1}}\cdot\sup_{k}|b_{k}|.

Then by Hölder’s inequality, we have for fixed
0\leq t\leq 2^{-2\ell_{1}}

\displaystyle|\int

\displaystyle\sum_{|k|\in I_{\ell_{1}}}\sum_{\tau_{\ell}\leq 2\lambda}b_{k}t^{\mu_{1}+\mu_{2}+1}e^{-t\tau_{\ell}^{2}}\overline{e_{k}^{0}(z)}V(z)e_{\tau_{\ell}}(z)\overline{e_{\tau_{\ell}}(x)}dz|

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\|V\|_{L^{\frac{n}{2-\eta}}(M)}\|\sum_{|k|\in I_{\ell_{1}}}b_{k}e_{k}^{0}(\cdot)\|_{L^{p_{0}}(M)}\cdot 2^{-2\ell_{1}(\mu_{1}+\mu_{2}+1)}\lambda^{1/2}\lambda^{\sigma(p_{0})}(\sum_{\tau_{\ell}\in[1,2\lambda]}|e_{\tau_{\ell}}(x)|^{2})^{1/2}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\lambda^{\frac{n}{2}+1-\frac{1}{4}\eta}2^{(\frac{n}{2}-1-\frac{1}{4}\eta-2\mu_{2})\ell_{1}}2^{(2\mu_{2}+2-\eta)\ell_{2}}

\displaystyle\quad\cdot\begin{cases}1,\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ 2^{\ell_{1}}\leq 80\lambda\ {\rm and}\ 2^{\ell_{2}}\approx 10\lambda\\
2^{-N_{1}\ell_{2}},\ \ \ \ \ \ \ 2^{\ell_{1}}\leq 80\lambda\ {\rm and}\ 2^{\ell_{2}}>10\lambda\\
2^{-N_{1}\ell_{2}},\ \ \ \ \ \ \ 2^{\ell_{1}}>80\lambda\ {\rm and}\ 2^{\ell_{2}}>2^{\ell_{1}-2}\\
2^{(n-2+\eta)(\ell_{2}-\ell_{1})},\ \ 2^{\ell_{1}}>80\lambda\ {\rm and}\ 2^{\ell_{2}}\approx 10\lambda\\
2^{(n-2+\eta)(\ell_{2}-\ell_{1})}2^{-N_{1}\ell_{2}},\ \ 2^{\ell_{1}}>80\lambda\ {\rm and}\ 10\lambda<2^{\ell_{2}}\leq 2^{\ell_{1}-2}.\end{cases}

Fix
N_{1}\geq 2N+2n
. Integrating over
0\leq t\leq 2^{-2\ell_{1}}
and summing over
2^{\ell_{1}},2^{\ell_{2}}\gtrsim\lambda
, we obtain (
5.59
).

To prove (
5.60
), by the heat kernel bounds in Lemma
1.2
, we have

\displaystyle|\sum_{|k|\in I_{\ell_{1}}}\sum_{\tau_{\ell}}\int_{0}^{2^{-2\ell_{1}}}

\displaystyle b_{k}\,t^{\mu_{1}+\mu_{2}+1}e^{-t\tau_{\ell}^{2}}\overline{e_{k}^{0}(z)}e_{\tau_{\ell}}(z)\overline{e_{\tau_{\ell}}(x)}dt|

\displaystyle\lesssim\int_{0}^{2^{-2\ell_{1}}}t^{\mu_{1}+\mu_{2}+1}t^{-\frac{n}{2}}e^{-cd_{g}(z,x)^{2}/t}dt\cdot\sum_{|k|\in I_{\ell_{1}}}|b_{k}|

\displaystyle\lesssim K(z,x)\cdot 2^{n\ell_{1}}\cdot\sup_{k}|b_{k}|

where

K(z,x)=\begin{cases}\log(2+(2^{\ell_{1}}d_{g}(z,x))^{-1})(1+2^{\ell_{1}}d_{g}(z,x))^{-N},\,\,\,\text{if}\,\,\,2\mu_{1}+2\mu_{2}+4=n\\
2^{-(2\mu_{1}+2\mu_{2}+4-n)\ell_{1}}(1+2^{\ell_{1}}d_{g}(z,x))^{-N},\,\,\,\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \text{if}\,\,\,2\mu_{1}+2\mu_{2}+4>n\\
d_{g}(z,x)^{2\mu_{1}+2\mu_{2}+4-n}(1+2^{\ell_{1}}d_{g}(z,x))^{-N},\,\,\,\ \ \ \ \ \text{if}\,\,\,2\mu_{1}+2\mu_{2}+4<n.\end{cases}

It is straightforward to check that

\|K(\cdot,x)\|_{L^{\frac{n}{n-2+\eta}}}\lesssim 2^{-(2\mu_{1}+2\mu_{2}+2+\eta)\ell_{1}},

then by Hölder’s inequality and (
5.61
), we have

\displaystyle|\int\sum_{|k|\in I_{\ell_{1}}}\sum_{\tau_{\ell}}

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{2^{-2\ell_{1}}}b_{k}\,t^{\mu_{1}+\mu_{2}+1}e^{-t\tau_{\ell}^{2}}\overline{e_{k}^{0}(z)}V(z)e_{\tau_{\ell}}(z)\overline{e_{\tau_{\ell}}(x)}dtdz|

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\|V\|_{L^{\frac{n}{2-\eta}}(M)}2^{(n-2\mu_{2}-2-\eta)\ell_{1}}2^{(2\mu_{2}+2-\eta)\ell_{2}}

\displaystyle\quad\cdot\begin{cases}1,\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ 2^{\ell_{1}}\leq 80\lambda\ {\rm and}\ 2^{\ell_{2}}\approx 10\lambda\\
2^{-N_{1}\ell_{2}},\ \ \ \ \ \ 2^{\ell_{1}}\leq 80\lambda\ {\rm and}\ 2^{\ell_{2}}>10\lambda\\
2^{-N_{1}\ell_{2}},\ \ \ \ \ \ 2^{\ell_{1}}>80\lambda\ {\rm and}\ 2^{\ell_{2}}>2^{\ell_{1}-2}\\
2^{(n-2+\eta)(\ell_{2}-\ell_{1})},\ \ 2^{\ell_{1}}>80\lambda\ {\rm and}\ 2^{\ell_{2}}\approx 10\lambda\\
2^{(n-2+\eta)(\ell_{2}-\ell_{1})}2^{-N_{1}\ell_{2}},\ \ 2^{\ell_{1}}>80\lambda\ {\rm and}\ 10\lambda<2^{\ell_{2}}\leq 2^{\ell_{1}-2}.\end{cases}

Fix
N_{1}\geq 2N+2n
. Summing over
2^{\ell_{1}},2^{\ell_{2}}\gtrsim\lambda
, we obtain (
5.60
).

Case 3.2.3b

We need to show

(5.62)

|\sum_{j,k}\sum_{\tau_{\ell}>2\lambda}\int h(|j|)|j|^{2\mu_{2}}|k|^{2\mu_{1}}c^{1}_{k,\tau_{\ell}}\,e_{j}^{0}(x)V_{jk}\overline{e_{k}^{0}(z)}V(z)e_{\tau_{\ell}}(z)\overline{e_{\tau_{\ell}}(x)}dz|\lesssim\lambda^{n-\frac{3}{2}\eta}.

In this case, we have
|j|\geq 0.8\tau_{\ell}
or
|k|\geq 0.8\tau_{\ell}
on the support of
1-\rho_{2}(|k|/\tau_{\ell})\rho_{2}(|j|/\tau_{\ell})
. Let
\tau_{\ell}\in[2^{s},2^{s+1}]
,
2^{s}>2\lambda
,
and for
|j|\in I_{\ell_{2}}
, let

(5.63)

b_{k,\tau_{\ell}}=\sum_{|j|\in I_{\ell_{2}}}h(|j|)|j|^{2\mu_{2}}|k|^{2\mu_{1}}e_{j}^{0}(x)V_{jk}(1-\rho_{2}(|k|/\tau_{\ell})\rho_{2}(|j|/\tau_{\ell})).

Then for
|k|\in I_{\ell_{1}}
, we have

(5.64)

\displaystyle|b_{k,\tau_{\ell}}|\lesssim 2^{2\mu_{1}\ell_{1}}2^{(2\mu_{2}+2-\eta)\ell_{2}}\cdot\begin{cases}1,\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ 2^{\ell_{1}}\leq 80\lambda\ {\rm and}\ 2^{\ell_{2}}\approx 10\lambda\\
2^{-N_{1}\ell_{2}},\ \ \ \ \ \ \ 2^{\ell_{1}}\leq 80\lambda\ {\rm and}\ 2^{\ell_{2}}>10\lambda\\
2^{-N_{1}\ell_{2}},\ \ \ \ \ \ \ 2^{\ell_{1}}>80\lambda\ {\rm and}\ 2^{\ell_{2}}>2^{\ell_{1}-2}\\
2^{(n-2+\eta)(\ell_{2}-\ell_{1})},\ \ 2^{\ell_{1}}>80\lambda\ {\rm and}\ 2^{\ell_{2}}\approx 10\lambda\\
2^{(n-2+\eta)(\ell_{2}-\ell_{1})}2^{-N_{1}\ell_{2}},\ \ 2^{\ell_{1}}>80\lambda\ {\rm and}\ 10\lambda<2^{\ell_{2}}\leq 2^{\ell_{1}-2}.\end{cases}

and then by Sobolev inequality

\displaystyle\|\sum_{|k|\in I_{\ell_{1}}}

\displaystyle b_{k,2^{s}}e_{k}^{0}(\cdot)\|_{L^{p_{0}}(M)}\lesssim 2^{\ell_{1}/2}2^{\sigma(p_{0})\ell_{1}}\|\sum_{|k|\in I_{\ell_{1}}}b_{k,2^{s}}e_{k}^{0}(\cdot)\|_{L^{2}(M)}

\displaystyle\lesssim 2^{(n/2+1-\frac{1}{4}\eta)\ell_{1}}\cdot\sup_{k,\tau_{\ell}}|b_{k,\tau_{\ell}}|.

And similarly we may obtain the estimates for
\partial_{\tau}b_{k,\tau}
.
By applying Lemma
3.2
with
\delta=2^{s}
, we have for fixed
0\leq t\leq 2^{-2\ell_{1}}
,

\displaystyle|\int\sum_{\tau_{\ell}\approx 2^{s}}

\displaystyle\sum_{|k|\in I_{\ell_{1}}}b_{k,\tau_{\ell}}t^{\mu_{1}+\mu_{2}+1}e^{-t\tau_{\ell}^{2}}\overline{e_{k}^{0}(z)}V(z)e_{\tau_{\ell}}(z)\overline{e_{\tau_{\ell}}(x)}dz|

\displaystyle\lesssim\|V\|_{L^{\frac{n}{2-\eta}}(M)}2^{ns/2}2^{s/2}2^{(\frac{1}{2}-\frac{1}{4}\eta)s}\cdot 2^{(n/2-1-\frac{1}{4}\eta-2\mu_{2})\ell_{1}}2^{(2\mu_{2}+2-\eta)\ell_{2}}

\displaystyle\quad\cdot\begin{cases}1,\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ 2^{\ell_{1}}\leq 80\lambda\ {\rm and}\ 2^{\ell_{2}}\approx 10\lambda\\

2^{(n-2+\eta)(\ell_{2}-\ell_{1})}2^{-N_{1}\ell_{2}},\ \ 2^{\ell_{1}}>80\lambda\ {\rm and}\ 10\lambda<2^{\ell_{2}}\leq 2^{\ell_{1}-2}.\end{cases}

Fix
N_{1}\geq 2N+2n
. Note that
2^{s}\lesssim 2^{\ell_{1}}
or
2^{s}\lesssim 2^{\ell_{2}}
. Integrating over

and summing over

5.62
).

We need to show

(5.65)

|\sum_{j,k}\sum_{\tau_{\ell}>2\lambda}\int h(|j|)|j|^{2\mu_{1}}|k|^{2\mu_{2}}c^{2}_{k,\tau_{\ell}}\,e_{j}^{0}(x)V_{jk}\overline{e_{k}^{0}(z)}V(z)e_{\tau_{\ell}}(z)\overline{e_{\tau_{\ell}}(x)}dz|\lesssim\lambda^{n-\frac{3}{2}\eta}.

In this case, we have
|j|\leq 0.9\tau_{\ell}
and
|k|\leq 0.9\tau_{\ell}
on the support of
\rho_{2}(|k|/\tau_{\ell})\rho_{2}(|j|/\tau_{\ell})
.
Let
\tau_{\ell}\in[2^{s},2^{s+1}]
,
2^{s}>2\lambda
,
and for
|j|\in I_{\ell_{2}}
, let

(5.66)

b_{k,\tau_{\ell}}=\sum_{|j|\in I_{\ell_{2}}}h(|j|)|j|^{2\mu_{1}}|k|^{2\mu_{2}}e_{j}^{0}(x)V_{jk}\rho_{2}(|k|/\tau_{\ell})\rho_{2}(|j|/\tau_{\ell}).

Then for
|k|\in I_{\ell_{1}}
, we have

(5.67)

\displaystyle|b_{k,\tau_{\ell}}|\lesssim 2^{2\mu_{1}\ell_{1}}2^{(2\mu_{2}+2-\eta)\ell_{2}}\cdot\begin{cases}1,\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ 2^{\ell_{1}}\leq 80\lambda\ {\rm and}\ 2^{\ell_{2}}\approx 10\lambda\\
2^{-N_{1}\ell_{2}},\ \ \ \ \ \ \ 2^{\ell_{1}}\leq 80\lambda\ {\rm and}\ 2^{\ell_{2}}>10\lambda\\

\displaystyle\|\sum_{|k|\in I_{\ell_{1}}}

\displaystyle b_{k,2^{s}}e_{k}^{0}(\cdot)\|_{L^{p_{0}}(M)}\lesssim 2^{\ell_{1}/2}2^{\sigma(p_{0})\ell_{1}}\|\sum_{|k|\in I_{\ell_{1}}}b_{k,2^{s}}e_{k}^{0}(\cdot)\|_{L^{2}(M)}

\displaystyle\lesssim 2^{(n/2+1-\frac{1}{4}\eta)\ell_{1}}\cdot\sup_{k,\tau_{\ell}}|b_{k,\tau_{\ell}}|.

And similarly we may obtain the estimates for
\partial_{\tau}b_{k,\tau}
.
Recall that
|w(x)|\lesssim e^{-x/2}

\tau_{\ell}\approx 2^{s}

3.2
with
\delta=2^{s}

0\leq t\leq 2^{-2\ell_{1}}

\displaystyle|\int

\displaystyle\sum_{\tau_{\ell}\approx 2^{s}}\sum_{|k|\in I_{\ell_{1}}}b_{k,\tau_{\ell}}\frac{w(\tau_{\ell}^{2}2^{-2\ell_{1}})}{2^{2\ell_{1}(\mu_{1}+\mu_{2}+2)}}\overline{e_{k}^{0}(z)}V(z)e_{\tau_{\ell}}(z)\overline{e_{\tau_{\ell}}(x)}dz|

\displaystyle\quad\cdot\begin{cases}1,\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ 2^{\ell_{1}}\leq 80\lambda\ {\rm and}\ 2^{\ell_{2}}\approx 10\lambda\\
2^{-N_{1}\ell_{2}},\ \ \ \ \ \ \ 2^{\ell_{1}}\leq 80\lambda\ {\rm and}\ 2^{\ell_{2}}>10\lambda\\

2^{(n-2+\eta)(\ell_{2}-\ell_{1})},\ \ 2^{\ell_{1}}>80\lambda\ {\rm and}\ 2^{\ell_{2}}\approx 10\lambda\\
2^{(n-2+\eta)(\ell_{2}-\ell_{1})}2^{-N_{1}\ell_{2}},\ \ 2^{\ell_{1}}>80\lambda\ {\rm and}\ 10\lambda<2^{\ell_{2}}\leq 2^{\ell_{1}-2}.\end{cases}

. Note that

. Summing over

5.65
).

This completes the proof of Case 3.2.3.
