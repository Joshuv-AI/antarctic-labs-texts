Geodesics on Regular Constant Distance Surfaces
J. J. P. Veerman

Abstract
Suppose that the surfaces K 0 K_{0} and K r K_{r} are the boundaries of two convex, complete, connected C 2 C^{2} bodies in ℝ 3 \mathbb{R}^{3} . Assume further that the (Euclidean) distance between any point x x in K r K_{r} and K 0 K_{0} is always r r ( r > 0 r>0 ). For x x in K r K_{r} , let Π ⁡ ( x ) \Pi(x) denote the nearest point to x x in K 0 K_{0} . We show that the projection Π \Pi preserves geodesics in these surfaces if and only if both surfaces are concentric spheres or co-axial round cylinders. This is optimal in the sense that the main step to establish this result is false for C 1 , 1 C^{1,1} surfaces. Finally, we give a non-trivial example of a geodesic preserving projection of two C 2 C^{2} non -constant distance surfaces. The question whether for any C 2 C^{2} convex surface S 0 S_{0} , there is a surface S S whose projection to S 0 S_{0} preserves geodesics is open.

Remark.
In view of Proposition

It is clear that if
