Preliminaries and definitions

Recall that a
\rm C^{*}
-algebra

\rm SP

hereditary
\rm C^{*}
-subalgebra of

Let
A
be a
{\rm C^{*}}
-algebra, and let

denote the
{\rm C^{*}}
-algebra of

elements of
A
. Let

denote the algebraic inductive limit of the sequence
({\rm M}_{n}(A),\phi_{n}),

where
\phi_{n}:{\rm M}_{n}(A)\to{\rm M}_{n+1}(A)

Let

(respectively,
{\rm M}_{n}(A)_{+}

the positive elements of
{\rm M}_{\infty}(A)
(respectively,
{\rm M}_{n}(A)
). Given

one says that

is Cuntz subequivalent to

) if there is a sequence
(v_{n})_{n=1}^{\infty}

of elements of
{\rm M}_{\infty}(A)
such that

One says that
a
and

a\sim b

a\precsim b
and
b\precsim a
. We shall write
\langle a\rangle
for the Cuntz equivalence class of

Let
A
be a unital
{\rm C^{*}}
-algebra. Recall
[
1
]
that a positive element

is called purely positive if

is
not Cuntz equivalent to a projection. Let

be a stably finite
{\rm C^{*}}
-algebra (c.f.,
[
40
]

[

Given
a

{\rm M}_{\infty}(A)_{+}
and

we shall denote by

the element of
{\rm C^{*}}(a)
corresponding (via the functional calculus) to the function
f(t)={\max(0,t-\varepsilon)},~~t\in\sigma(a)

((a-\varepsilon_{1})_{+}-\varepsilon_{2})_{+}=(a-(\varepsilon_{1}+\varepsilon_{2}))_{+}.

Theorem 3.5
.

Let
\Omega
be a class of
\rm C^{*}
-algebras which have the property
\rm{SP}

\rm{SP}

Proof.

Let
B
be a non-zero hereditary
\rm C^{*}
-subalgebra of
A
. We must show that
B
contains a non-zero projection.
Choose a positive element
a
of
B
,
\|a\|=1
.

Let
0<\varepsilon<1
be two positive numbers. Define

\displaystyle f_{\varepsilon}(t)=\begin{cases}1,&\text{if}\quad t\geq\varepsilon\\
(2t-\varepsilon)/\varepsilon,&\text{if}\quad\varepsilon/2<t\leq\varepsilon\\
0,&\text{if}\quad 0\leq t\leq\varepsilon/2.\end{cases}

Let
F=\{a,~a^{1/2}\}
, and any
\varepsilon^{\prime}>0
. Since

-subalgebra

, for any

\|gag\|\geq 1-\varepsilon^{\prime}
.

By
(1)
and
(2)
, with sufficiently small
\varepsilon^{\prime}
, there exists an element of norm at most one

a^{\prime}\in D_{+}

\rm{SP}
