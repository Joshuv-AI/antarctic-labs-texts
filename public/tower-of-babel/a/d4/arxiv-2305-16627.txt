On the Gille theorem for the relative projective line
Ivan Panin, Anastasia Stavrova

Abstract
Let X X be a Noetherian separated scheme. Let G G be a reductive X X -group scheme, and let E E be a principal G G -bundle over ℙ X 1 \mathbb{P}^{1}_{X} . We prove that if the restriction of E E to ∞ × X \infty\times X is Zariski locally trivial, then E E is itself Zariski locally trivial.



1
Main results

All the rings in the present text are commutative and Noetherian.
All the schemes in the present text are Noetherian and separated.

Let
R
be a ring. Recall that an
R
-group scheme
\mathbf{G}
is called
reductive
,
if it is affine and smooth as an
R
-scheme and if, moreover,
for each algebraically closed field
\Omega

is

coincides with
[

Assume that

\mathbf{G}
is a reductive

-group scheme.
Recall that a

\mathbf{G}

It is well known that such a bundle is trivial locally in étale topology but in general not in the Zariski topology.
The first main result of our paper is the following theorem.

Theorem 1.1
.

Let
X
be a scheme. Let
\mathbf{G}
be a reductive
X
-group scheme

\mathcal{G}
be a principal
\mathbf{G}
-bundle over
\mathbb{P}^{1}_{X}

is Zarisky locally trivial on
\mathbb{P}^{1}_{X}
.

If

is a reductive

-bundles over

by means of a cocharacter

and any two such bundles are isomorphic if and

In particular, every such principal

]

, Th. 3.4]
).
These results are used in the present text in a substantial way.

Lemma 2.1
.

Let
W
be an affine

Let
\mathbf{H}
be a simply connected semi-simple
W
-group scheme and
\mathbf{H}^{\prime}=\text{GL}_{N,W}

Let

-group scheme.

Let

be a principal
\mathbf{H}
-bundle, and let

be the corresponding principal
H^{\prime}
-bundle.
If
M
is a trivial
\mathbf{H}^{\prime}
-bundle, then there exists a principal

\mathbf{H}
-bundle

over

pr^{*}(F_{0})\cong F
,
where

Proof.

By
[

the
fppf

is representable by an affine
W
-scheme

of finite type over

Consider the long exact sequence of pointed sets

1\to\mathbf{H}(\mathbf{P}^{1}_{W})\xrightarrow{j_{*}}\mathbf{H}^{\prime}(\mathbf{P}^{1}_{W})\to X(\mathbf{P}^{1}_{W})\xrightarrow{\partial}H^{1}_{\text{\'{e}t}}(\mathbf{P}^{1}_{W},\mathbf{H})\xrightarrow{j_{*}}H^{1}_{\text{\'{e}t}}(\mathbf{P}^{1}_{W},\mathbf{H}^{\prime}).

Since

is trivial, there is

\partial(\varphi)=F
.

The

W

Consider another long exact sequence of pointed sets, this time the one corresponding to the scheme

In particular, we get the following commutative square

\textstyle{X(W)\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces}
\scriptstyle{pr^{*}_{W}}
\scriptstyle{\partial}
\textstyle{H^{1}_{\text{\'{e}t}}(W,H)\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces}
\scriptstyle{pr^{*}_{W}}
\textstyle{X(\mathbf{P}^{1}_{W})\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces}
\scriptstyle{\partial}
\textstyle{H^{1}_{\text{\'{e}t}}(\mathbf{P}^{1}_{W},H).}

(1)

We have
\text{\rm pr}^{*}_{W}(s)=\varphi
. Hence

F=\partial(\varphi)=\partial(\text{\rm pr}^{*}_{W}(s))=pr^{*}_{W}(\partial(s)).

Setting

In what follows we use the notion of an isotropic simply connected semisimple group introduced in the Introduction.
The following lemma is in order

Lemma 2.3
.

Let
\tilde{W}
be a connected affine scheme, which is finite étale over a connected affine scheme
W
, and let

\tilde{\mathbf{G}}
is a simple simply connected
\tilde{W}
-group scheme.

(i)
If
\tilde{\mathbf{G}}
is isotropic and
\tilde{\mathbf{P}}\subset\tilde{\mathbf{G}}

(ii)
Assume that
W
is, moreover, semi-local. If
R_{\tilde{W}/W}(\tilde{\mathbf{G}})
has a proper parabolic
W
-subgroup,
then
\tilde{\mathbf{G}}
is isotropic, and, as a consequence,
R_{\tilde{W}/W}(\tilde{\mathbf{G}})
is isotropic and has a strictly proper
parabolic subgroup.

Proof.

(i)
Write
W=\spec R
for a connected commutative ring
R
, and take a ring homomorphism
s:R\to\Omega(s)
from
R
to an algebraically closed field

\Omega(s)
. Then
\tilde{W}\times_{W}\spec\Omega(s)
is isomorphic to the disjoint union
of
[\tilde{W}:W]
copies of
\spec\Omega(s)
, and
R_{\tilde{W}/W}(\tilde{\mathbf{G}})\times_{W}\spec\Omega(s)
is isomorphic to the product
of
[\tilde{W}:W]
copies of the simple simply connected algebraic
\Omega(s)
-group
\tilde{\mathbf{G}}_{\Omega(s)}
.
Consequently, the Dynkin diagram of
R_{\tilde{W}/W}(\tilde{\mathbf{G}})\times_{W}\spec\Omega(s)

is a disjoint union of the same number of copies of the Dynkin diagram of
\tilde{\mathbf{G}}_{\Omega(s)}
.
Similarly,
R_{\tilde{W}/W}(\tilde{\mathbf{P}})\times_{W}\spec\Omega(s)
is isomorphic to the product
of
[\tilde{W}:W]
copies of the parabolic subgroup
\tilde{\mathbf{P}}_{\Omega(s)}
of
\tilde{\mathbf{G}}_{\Omega(s)}
.
Consequently,
R_{\tilde{W}/W}(\tilde{\mathbf{P}})\times_{W}\spec\Omega(s)
is a strictly proper parabolic subgroup of

diagram of this group.
Since

is also a smooth closed
W
-subgroup
of

, by the very definition of a parabolic subgroup

(ii)
Assume that
W
is semi-local. Then
\tilde{W}
is also semi-local.
If
R_{\tilde{W}/W}(\tilde{\mathbf{G}})
has a proper parabolic subgroup,
by
[

contains a closed subgroup of the form

.
By the adjunction between Weil restriction and base change, it follows that

[

has a proper parabolic

-subgroup. Since

is connected, this parabolic subgroup is necessarily strictly proper. Hence

is
isotropic. It remains to apply

.

Lemma 7.6
.

Let
k
be a finite field. Let
c=\sharp(k)
(the cardinality of
k

Let
d\geq 1
be an integer and

k(d)/k
be a unique finite field extension of degree

Let

be a prime which is co-prime as to the characteristic of the field
k

so to the integer
d

Then

(1)
Irr_{k}(q)=(c^{q}-c)/q

(2) the
k

is the field

(3)
Irr_{k}(dq)\geq Irr_{k}(q)

(4)
Irr_{k}(q)=(c^{q}-c)/q\gg 0

Proof.

The assertions (1) and (2) are clear. The assertion (3) is clear for

.
To prove the assertion (3) for

note that

Irr_{k}(dq)=(c^{dq}-c^{d}-c^{q}+c)/dq

It remains to check that

Which in turn is

c^{d}(c^{d(q-1)}-1)\geq(d+1)(c^{q}-c)

So, the assertion (3) is proved.
It yields the assertion (4).
These proves the lemma.
