Generalized Turán number for linear forests
Xiutao ZHU, Yaojun CHEN

Abstract
The generalized Turán number e ​ x ​ ( n , K s , H ) ex(n,K_{s},H) is defined to be the maximum number of copies of a complete graph K s K_{s} in any H H -free graph on n n vertices. Let F F be a linear forest consisting of k k paths of orders ℓ 1 , ℓ 2 , … , ℓ k \ell_{1},\ell_{2},...,\ell_{k} . In this paper, by characterizing the structure of the F F -free graph with large minimum degree, we determine the value of e ​ x ​ ( n , K s , F ) ex(n,K_{s},F) for n = Ω ⁡ ( | F | s ) n=\Omega\left(|F|^{s}\right) and k ≥ 2 k\geq 2 except some ℓ i = 3 \ell_{i}=3 , and the corresponding extremal graphs. The special case when s = 2 s=2 of our result improves some results of Bushaw and Kettle (2011) and Lidický et al. (2013) on the classical Turán number for linear forests. Key words : Generalized Turán number; Minimum degree; Linear forests

The Turán number of

, denoted by

H
-free graph on

ex(n,H)=\left(\frac{\chi-2}{\chi-1}+o(1)\right){n\choose 2}.

In viewing this result, one can see that the asymptotic behavior of

Theorem 1

(Erdős and Gallai
[
10
]

ex(n,P_{\ell})\leq\frac{(\ell-2)n}{2}
, equality holds if and only if

In the same paper, they also considered the graph

and determined

and the extremal graphs.
Note that both a path

and
k
independent edges

are special linear forests. In 2011, Bushaw and Kettle

generalized the above results by determining the Turán number and extremal graph for the linear forest consisting of the paths of the same lengths.

Theorem 2

(Bushaw and Kettle

]

Let

Theorem 4

Let
F
be a linear forest so that
\ell_{i}\not=3
for
1\leq i\leq k
and
k\geq 2
. Suppose
n\geq 5\binom{|F|-1}{s}^{2}/\left({(|F|-1)\binom{\delta_{F}}{s-1}}\right)+\delta_{F}
and
s\leq\delta_{F}+1
.
If
F\neq 2P_{\ell}
with
\ell
odd, then

ex(n,K_{s},F)=max\left\{\mathcal{N}_{s}(G_{F}(n)),ex(n,K_{s},P_{\ell_{1}})\right\},

and if
F=2P_{\ell}
with
\ell
odd, then

ex(n,K_{s},F)=\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\mathcal{N}_{s}(G_{F}(n)),&~for~s\leq\delta_{F},\\
\lfloor\frac{n-1}{\ell-1}\rfloor\ell+\mu,&~for~s=\delta_{F}+1,\end{array}\right.

where
\mu=1
if
n-1\equiv\ell-2
(mod
\ell-1
), and
\mu=0
otherwise.

It is worth noting that in Theorem
4
,
\mathcal{N}_{s}(G_{F}(n))\geq ex(n,K_{s},P_{\ell_{1}})
is not always true. For example, if
s\geq 3
and
\ell_{1}\gg\ell_{2}+\cdots+\ell_{k}
, then
\mathcal{N}_{s}(G_{F}(n))\leq ex(n,K_{s},P_{\ell_{1}})
. Thus it is reasonable to use
max\{\mathcal{N}_{s}(G_{F}(n)),ex(n,K_{s},P_{\ell_{1}})\}
rather than
\mathcal{N}_{s}(G_{F}(n))
.
However, it is always true that
\mathcal{N}_{s}(G_{F}(n))\geq ex(n,K_{s},P_{\ell_{1}})
if
s=2
. Thus, we have

Corollary 1

Let
F
be a linear forest so that
\ell_{i}\not=3
for
1\leq i\leq k
and
k\geq 2
. If
n=\Omega\left(|F|^{2}\right)
, then

ex(n,F)=e(G_{F}(n))
.

This implies Conjecture

is indeed true for all

, which reduces the lower bound for

from exponential to polynomial in

.

The main idea to prove Theorem

-free graph until the remaining graph is empty or has large minimum degree. And then we count the number of copies of

be a hamiltonian path starting at
v
in
B_{i}
for

. Because
|Q_{i}|\geq\delta_{F}+1
,
|Q_{1}-v|\geq\delta_{F}
and
Q_{2}\cup Q_{3}
is a path of order at least
2\delta_{F}+1
. Since

is
F
-free, by Lemma
7
, we have
|Q_{1}-v|\leq\delta_{F}+1
.

If
|Q_{1}-v|=\delta_{F}
, then by Lemma
6
,
F\in\{5P_{5},2P_{\ell},P_{\ell+1}\cup P_{\ell}\}
, or

with
\ell
odd. In this case, noting that
\delta(G)\geq\delta_{F}
, we can see that
G=K_{1}+tK_{\delta_{F}}
with
t\geq 4
(because
n\geq 2|F|
). Note that
G
contains
F
if
F=5P_{5}
, hence
F\in\{2P_{\ell},P_{\ell+1}\cup P_{\ell}\}
, or
F\in\{P_{\ell+2}\cup P_{\ell},2P_{\ell}\cup P_{2}\}
with
\ell
odd. If
|Q_{1}-v|=\delta_{F}+1
, then by Lemma
7
and the fact that
P_{2\delta_{F}+1}\cup P_{\delta_{F}+1}
has no

F
, we have
F=2P_{\ell}
and
\ell
is odd. In this case, it is not difficult to deduce that

.

Case 2.

G
contains at least three end blocks and two cut vertices.

By the assumption, we may assume that
B_{1},B_{2},B_{3}
are three end blocks and
b_{i}\in B_{i}
is the only cut vertex of
B_{i}
with
b_{1}\not=b_{2}
. Let
Q_{i}
be the longest path in
B_{i}
starting at
b_{i}
for

,
P
a longest path connecting
b_{1},b_{2}
and
P^{\prime}
a path with endpoints
b_{3}
and
v
such that
V(P)\cap V(P^{\prime})=\{v\}
. Because
\delta(G)\geq\delta_{F}
, we have
|Q_{i}|\geq\delta_{F}+1
. Thus, it is not difficult to see that the subgraph consisting of the paths
Q_{1},Q_{2},Q_{3},P,P^{\prime}
contains

P_{2\delta_{F}+1}\cup P_{\delta_{F}+1}
which is
F
-free. By Lemma
7
,
F=2P_{\ell}
and
\ell
is odd.

Let
S=V(B_{1})\cup V(B_{2})\cup V(P)
. It is clear that
N_{S}(u)\subseteq V(P)
for any
u\in V(G)\backslash S
.

Obviously,
\delta(G)\geq\delta_{F}=\ell-2
. Since
G
is
F
-free, we have
|Q_{1}\cup P\cup Q_{2}|\leq 2\ell-1
, and hence
|P|\leq 3
. Note that
|P|\geq 2
, we have
|P|=2
or 3.

If
|P|=2
, then
P=b_{1}b_{2}
. For any
u\in V(G)\backslash S
, by the maximality of
P
,
d_{P}(u)\leq 1
, which means
N_{S}(u)\subseteq\{b_{1}\}
or
N_{S}(u)\subseteq\{b_{2}\}
. If
u_{1},u_{2}\in V(G)\backslash S
with
u_{1}b_{1},u_{2}b_{2}\in E(G)
, then
G
contains
2P_{\ell}
, and hence we may assume that
N_{S}(u)\subseteq\{b_{1}\}
. In this case,
G-b_{1}
contains at least
3
components and each one is
P_{\ell}
-free for otherwise

has
2P_{\ell}
. Since
\delta(G)\geq\ell-2
, each component contains
\ell-2
or
\ell-1
vertices,
B_{2}=K_{\ell-1}
and
G-V(B_{2})\subseteq K_{1}+(s_{1}K_{\ell-1}\cup s_{2}K_{\ell-2})
. Of course,

.

If
|P|=3
, then
N(u)\cap\{b_{1},b_{2}\}=\emptyset
for otherwise
G
has
2P_{\ell}
. Thus,
P=b_{1}vb_{2}
and
N_{S}(u)\subseteq\{v\}
for any
u\in V(G)\backslash S
. If
|B_{1}|\geq\ell
, then since
\delta(B_{1})\geq\ell-3
, by Lemma
1
we have
p(B_{1})\geq min\{|B_{1}|,2(\ell-3)+1\}\geq\ell
, that is,

contains a
P_{\ell}
. Note that
Q_{2}\cup\{v\}
also contains a
P_{\ell}
, a contradiction. Thus,
|B_{1}|=\ell-1
. By symmetry,
|B_{2}|=\ell-1
. Since
\delta(G)\geq\ell-1
, we have
B_{1}=B_{2}=K_{\ell-1}
. By the assumption,
V(G)\backslash S\not=\emptyset
. Let

, then

d_{G^{\prime}}(v)\geq\delta_{F}-2
and

for any

.

If

, then
Q_{1}\cup\{b_{1}b_{2}\}\cup Q_{2}
contains a
P_{\ell}
. If
G’-v
contains only one component, then

contains a path of order at least

Because

-free, we have

. In this case,

. If

If

, and each one is

-free, for otherwise

, each component of

\ell-1

, and so

.

The proof of Theorem

4
Generalized Turán number

In this section, we determine the generalized Turán number
ex(n,K_{s},F)
. We will use a strategy to reduce the problem to graphs with large minimum degree. For a fixed
F
, we define a process of
G
, called
\left(\delta_{F}-1\right)
-
disintegration
as follows: for
j=n
, let
G^{n}=G
, and for
j<n
, let
G^{j}
be obtained from
G^{j+1}
by deleting a vertex of degree less than
\delta_{F}
in
G^{j+1}
if such a vertex exists. The process terminates at
G^{m}
when
\delta(G^{m})\geq\delta_{F}
or
m=0
. We call
G^{m}
the
\delta_{F}
-
core
of
G
.

Before starting to prove Theorem
4
, as a preparation, we first discuss the properties of the
\delta_{F}
-core of
F
-free graphs for some special
F
.

Claim 1

Let
F
be a linear forest and
G
an
F
-free graph with
\delta(G)\geq\delta_{F}
and components
G_{1},G_{2},\ldots,G_{c}
, where
|G_{1}|\geq\cdots\geq|G_{c}|
. Then
G=G_{1}\cup(c-1)K_{\ell-1}
if
F=2P_{\ell}
and
\ell
is odd, and
|G_{i}|\leq|F|-1
for all
1\leq i\leq c
otherwise.

Proof
. If
F=2P_{\ell}
and
\ell
is odd, then
\delta_{F}=\ell-2
. Since
p(G_{i})\geq min\{|G_{i}|,2\ell-3\}
by Lemma
1
, we have
|G_{i}|\leq\ell-1
which implies
G_{i}=K_{\ell-1}
for
2\leq i\leq c
, and hence the result follows.

If
F\not=2P_{\ell}
with
\ell
odd, we suppose
|G_{1}|\geq|F|
. Since
G_{1}
contains no hamiltonian path, then
\alpha(G)\geq 3
by Lemma
2
.
Obviously,

\sigma_{3}(G_{1})\geq 3\delta_{F}\geq 3(\delta_{F^{\prime}}+1),

where
F^{\prime}=F-P_{\ell_{k}}
. By Lemma
3
, we have
F^{\prime}\subseteq G_{1}
. Since
G_{2}
contains
P_{\ell_{k}}
, we can see that
G
contains
F
, a contradiction.

\hfill\blacksquare

Claim 2

Let
F\in\{3P_{5},2P_{\ell},P_{\ell}\cup P_{\ell+1},P_{\ell}\cup P_{\ell+2},2P_{\ell}\cup P_{2}\}
and
s\leq\delta_{F}+1
.

If
F\not=2P_{\ell}
with
\ell
odd or
F\not=3P_{5}
, then

\mathcal{N}_{s}(G_{F}(t(\ell-1)+1))\geq\mathcal{N}_{s}(K_{1}+tK_{\ell-1}).

If
F=3P_{5}
, then

\mathcal{N}_{s}(G_{F}(4t+2))>\mathcal{N}_{s}(K_{2}+tK_{4}).

If
F=2P_{\ell}
with
\ell
odd,
2\leq s\leq\ell-2
and
m\geq 5{\binom{|F|-1}{s}}/{\binom{\delta_{F}}{s-1}}
, then

\mathcal{N}_{s}(G_{F}(m))=max\{\mathcal{N}_{s}(G)~|~G~is~F\text{-}free,~|G|=m~and~\delta(G)\geq\delta_{F}\}.

Proof
. If
F\not=2P_{\ell}
with
\ell
odd or
F\not=3P_{5}
, then
\delta_{F}=\ell-1
. Thus we have

\begin{split}\mathcal{N}_{s}(G_{F}(t(\ell-1)+1))-\mathcal{N}_{s}(K_{1}+tK_{\ell-1})&\geq\binom{\ell-1}{s}+(m-\ell+1)\binom{\ell-1}{s-1}-t\binom{\ell}{s}\\
&=(t-1)\left(\ell-1-\frac{\ell}{s}\right)\binom{\ell-1}{s-1}\geq 0,\end{split}

that is,
\mathcal{N}_{s}(G_{F}(t(\ell-1)+1))\geq\mathcal{N}_{s}(K_{1}+tK_{\ell-1})
.

If
F=3P_{5}
, then
\delta_{F}=5
. Thus we have

\begin{split}\mathcal{N}_{s}(G_{F}(4t+2))-\mathcal{N}_{s}(K_{2}+tK_{4})&\geq\binom{5}{s}+(4t-3)\binom{5}{s-1}+\binom{5}{s-2}-t\binom{6}{s}\\
&=\big((2s/3-1)(t-1)-s/2\big)\binom{6}{s}+\binom{5}{s-2}>0.\end{split}

If
F=2P_{\ell}
with
\ell
odd, then
\delta_{F}=\ell-2
.
Since
m\geq 5{\binom{|F|-1}{s}}/{\binom{\delta_{F}}{s-1}}
and
G
is
F
-free, by Theorem
5
and Claim
1
, we have
G\in\{G_{F}(m),K_{1}+(t_{1}K_{\ell-1}\cup t_{2}K_{\ell-2}),G_{1}\cup tK_{\ell-1}\}
, where
G_{1}
is the largest component of
G
with
|G_{1}|=n_{1}
if
G
is disconnected.
We first show
\mathcal{N}_{s}\big(G_{F}(m)\big)\geq\mathcal{N}_{s}\big(K_{1}+(t_{1}K_{\ell-1}\cup t_{2}K_{\ell-2})\big)
when
m=1+t_{1}(\ell-1)+t_{2}(\ell-2)
. Noting that
s\geq 2
and
m\geq 5{\binom{|F|-1}{s}}/{\binom{\delta_{F}}{s-1}}
implying
t_{1}+t_{2}\geq 5
, we have

\begin{split}&\mathcal{N}_{s}\big(G_{F}(m)\big)-\mathcal{N}_{s}\big(K_{1}+(t_{1}K_{\ell-1}\cup t_{2}K_{\ell-2})\big)\\
=&\binom{\ell-2}{s-2}+(m-\ell+2)\binom{\ell-2}{s-1}+\binom{\ell-2}{s}-t_{2}\binom{\ell-1}{s}-t_{1}\binom{\ell}{s}\\
=&\binom{\ell-2}{s-2}+\biggl(t_{1}(\ell-1)+(t_{2}-1)(\ell-2)+1\biggr)\binom{\ell-2}{s-1}+\frac{\ell-s-1}{s}\binom{\ell-2}{s-1}\\
&~~~~~~~~~~~-t_{1}\frac{\ell(\ell-1)}{s(\ell-s)}\binom{\ell-2}{s-1}-t_{2}\frac{\ell-1}{s}\binom{\ell-2}{s-1}\\
=&\binom{\ell-2}{s-2}+\biggl(t_{1}(\ell-1)\left(1-\frac{\ell}{s(\ell-s)}\right)+(t_{2}-1)\left(\ell-2-\frac{\ell-1}{s}\right)\biggr)\binom{\ell-2}{s-1}\\
\geq&\binom{\ell-2}{s-2}+\biggl(t_{1}(\ell-1)\frac{\ell-4}{2(\ell-2)}+(t_{2}-1)\frac{\ell-3}{2}\biggr)\binom{\ell-2}{s-1}>0.\end{split}

Next we show
\mathcal{N}_{s}(G_{F}(m))\geq\mathcal{N}_{s}(G_{1}\cup tK_{\ell-1})
when
m=n_{1}+t(\ell-1)
. If
n_{1}\geq 2|F|
, then
G_{1}\subseteq G_{F}(n_{1})
by Lemma
9
. Thus we have

\begin{split}\mathcal{N}_{s}\left(G_{F}(m)\right)-\mathcal{N}_{s}(G_{1}\cup tK_{\ell-1})\geq&\mathcal{N}_{s}(G_{F}(m))-\mathcal{N}_{s}(G_{F}(n_{1}))-t\binom{\ell-1}{s}\\
=&t(\ell-1)\binom{\ell-2}{s-1}-t\binom{\ell-1}{s}>0.\end{split}

If
\ell-1\leq n_{1}\leq 2|F|-1
, then noting that
G_{1}
is
P_{2\ell}
-free, by Theorem
3
we have

\mathcal{N}_{s}(G_{1})\leq\frac{n_{1}}{2\ell-1}\binom{2\ell-1}{s}.

Thus, note that
m\geq 5{\binom{|F|-1}{s}}/{\binom{\delta_{F}}{s-1}}
, that is,
m\geq 5{\binom{2\ell-1}{s}}/{\binom{\ell-2}{s-1}}
, we have

\begin{split}&\mathcal{N}_{s}(G_{F}(m))-\mathcal{N}_{s}(G_{1}\cup tK_{\ell-1})\\
\geq&(m-\ell+2)\binom{\ell-2}{s-1}+\binom{\ell-2}{s}+\binom{\ell-2}{s-2}-\frac{n_{1}}{2\ell-1}\binom{2\ell-1}{s}-t\binom{\ell-1}{s}\\
=&\biggl(m-\ell+2\biggr)\binom{\ell-2}{s-1}+\frac{\ell-s-1}{s}\binom{\ell-2}{s-1}+\frac{s-1}{\ell-s}\binom{\ell-2}{s-1}\\
&~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~-\frac{n_{1}}{2\ell-1}\binom{2\ell-1}{s}-t\frac{\ell-1}{s}\binom{\ell-2}{s-1}\\
=&\biggl((m-\ell+1)\left(1-\frac{1}{s}\right)+\frac{n_{1}}{s}+\frac{s-1}{\ell-s}\biggr)\binom{\ell-2}{s-1}-\frac{n_{1}}{2\ell-1}\binom{2\ell-1}{s}\\
>&\frac{m}{2}\binom{\ell-2}{s-1}-\frac{n_{1}}{2\ell-1}\binom{2\ell-1}{s}>0.\end{split}

Therefore, the latter part holds.

\hfill\blacksquare

Proof of Theorem
4
.
Let
F=P_{\ell_{1}}\cup\cdots\cup P_{\ell_{k}}
be a linear forest with
k\geq 2,\ell_{i}\not=3
and
G
an
F
-free graph of order
n
with
\mathcal{N}_{s}(G)=ex(n,K_{s},F)
and
s\leq\delta_{F}+1
. Obviously,

\displaystyle\mathcal{N}_{s}(G)\geq max\{\mathcal{N}_{s}(G_{F}(n)),ex(n,K_{s},P_{\ell_{1}})\}.

(4.1)

Applying
\left(\delta_{F}-1\right)
-
disintegration
to
G
and let
G^{m}
be the
\delta_{F}
-
core
of
G
. If
m=0
, then since one vertex is deleted at each step during the process of
(\delta_{F}-1)
-
disintegration
, that destroys at most
\binom{\delta_{F}-1}{s-1}
copies of
K_{s}
in
G
, we have

\begin{split}\mathcal{N}_{s}(G)&\leq\binom{\delta_{F}}{s}+\left(n-\delta_{F}\right)\binom{\delta_{F}-1}{s-1}\\
&<\binom{\delta_{F}}{s}+\left(n-\delta_{F}\right)\binom{\delta_{F}}{s-1}\leq\mathcal{N}_{s}(G_{F}(n)),\end{split}

which contradicts (
4.1
). This implies that
\delta(G^{m})\geq\delta_{F}
and
m\geq\delta_{F}+1
.

By the definitions of
\left(\delta_{F}-1\right)
-
disintegration
, we have

\mathcal{N}_{s}\left(G^{j+1}\right)-\mathcal{N}_{s}\left(G^{j}\right)\leq\binom{\delta_{F}-1}{s-1}.

(4.2)

If
m\leq j\leq n-1
, then

\mathcal{N}_{s}(G_{F}(j+1))-\mathcal{N}_{s}(G_{F}(j))\geq\binom{\delta_{F}}{s-1}.

(4.3)

Recall that
s\leq\delta_{F}+1
, by (
4.2
) and (
4.3
), we have

\mathcal{N}_{s}\left(G^{j}\right)-\mathcal{N}_{s}\left(G_{F}(j)\right)\geq\mathcal{N}_{s}\left(G^{j+1}\right)-\mathcal{N}_{s}(G_{F}(j+1))+1,

and so

\mathcal{N}_{s}\left(G^{m}\right)-\mathcal{N}_{s}\left(G_{F}(m)\right)\geq\mathcal{N}_{s}\left(G^{n}\right)-\mathcal{N}_{s}(G_{F}(n))+(n-m),

which implies that

\mathcal{N}_{s}(G^{m})\geq n-m+\mathcal{N}_{s}(G_{F}(m))\geq n-\delta_{F}.

On the other hand, since
G^{m}
has no path of order
|F|
, by Theorem
3
, we have

\mathcal{N}_{s}(G^{m})\leq\frac{m}{|F|-1}\binom{|F|-1}{s}.

Thus, note that
n\geq 5\binom{|F|-1}{s}^{2}/\left({(|F|-1)\binom{\delta_{F}}{s-1}}\right)+\delta_{F}
, we have

m\geq\left(n-\delta_{F}\right)\left(|F|-1\right)\left/\binom{|F|-1}{s}\right.\geq 5\binom{|F|-1}{s}\left/{\binom{\delta_{F}}{s-1}}\right.,

(4.4)

which means the core
G^{m}
contains indeed at least
5\binom{|F|-1}{s}/{\binom{\delta_{F}}{s-1}}
vertices.

If
G^{m}
is disconnected, we assume that the components of
G^{m}
are
G_{1}^{m},G_{2}^{m},...,G_{c}^{m}
with
|G_{1}^{m}|\geq|G_{2}^{m}|\geq\cdots\geq|G_{c}^{m}|
.

(i)
F\neq 2P_{\ell}
with
\ell
odd. By (
4.4
), we have
m\geq 3|F|
. If
G^{m}
is connected, then by Theorem
5
,
G^{m}\in\{G_{F}(m),K_{1}+tK_{\ell-1},K_{2}+tK_{4}\}
. By Claim
2
, we can see

\begin{split}\mathcal{N}_{s}(G)&\leq\mathcal{N}_{s}(G^{m})+(n-m)\binom{\delta_{F}-1}{s-1}\leq\mathcal{N}_{s}(G_{F}(m))+(n-m)\binom{\delta_{F}-1}{s-1}\\
&\leq\mathcal{N}_{s}(G_{F}(m))+(n-m)\binom{\delta_{F}}{s-1}\leq\mathcal{N}_{s}(G_{F}(n)),\end{split}

with equality holds if and only if
n=m
and
G=G_{F}(n)
.

If
G^{m}
is disconnected, then since
m\geq 3|F|
, we have
c\geq 4
by Claim
1
. If two of the components of
G^{m}
are contained in one component of
G
, then we can choose
G_{i}^{m},G_{j}^{m},G_{k}^{m}
such that
G_{i}^{m},G_{j}^{m}
are contained in one component of
G-G_{k}^{m}
. Because
\delta(G^{m})\geq\delta_{F}
, we can see that
G-G_{k}^{m}
contains
P_{2\delta_{F}+2}
and
G_{k}^{m}
contains
P_{\delta_{F}+1}
, which implies
G
contains
F
by Lemma
7
, a contradiction. Thus, we may assume
G_{1},G_{2},...,G_{s}
are components of
G
and
G^{m}_{i}\subseteq G_{i},1\leq i\leq c\leq s
. In this case, each
G_{i}
contains a
P_{\delta_{F}+1}
.
If
G
contains a
P_{\ell_{1}}
, say
P_{\ell_{1}}\subseteq G_{1}
, then since
c\geq 4
,
G
contains
P_{\ell_{1}}\cup 3P_{\delta_{F}+1}
. Assume that
x,y
are the endpoints of two distinct
P_{\delta_{F}+1}
. Then
P_{\delta_{F}+1}\cup\{xy\}\cup P_{\delta_{F}+1}
is a path of oder
2\delta_{F}+2
, and so
3P_{\delta_{F}+1}\cup\{xy\}
contains
F
by Lemma
7
. If
F
uses
xy
to form some
P_{\ell_{i}}
, then replace this
P_{\ell_{i}}
with a
P_{\ell_{i}}
from
G_{1}
, we find an
F
in
G
, a contradiction. This is to say that
G
is
P_{\ell_{1}}
-free and so

\mathcal{N}_{s}(G)\leq ex(n,K_{s},P_{\ell_{1}})
. Therefore, we can obtain that
\mathcal{N}_{s}(G)\leq max\{\mathcal{N}_{s}(G_{F}(n)),ex(n,K_{s},P_{\ell_{1}})\}
. Combining this with (
4.1
), we get that
\mathcal{N}_{s}(G)=max\{\mathcal{N}_{s}(G_{F}(n)),ex(n,K_{s},P_{\ell_{1}})\}
.

(ii)
F=2P_{\ell}
and
\ell
is odd. By Theorem
4
and Claim
1
,
G^{m}
is
G_{F}(m)
or
K_{1}+(t_{1}K_{\ell-2}\cup t_{2}K_{\ell-1})
or
G^{m}_{1}\cup tK_{\ell-1}
. If
s\leq\ell-2
, then because
m\geq 5\binom{|F|-1}{s}/{\binom{\delta_{F}}{s-1}}
, by Claim
2
, we have

\begin{split}\mathcal{N}_{s}(G)&\leq\mathcal{N}_{s}(G^{m})+(n-m)\binom{\delta_{F}-1}{s-1}\\
&\leq\mathcal{N}_{s}(G_{F}(m))+(n-m)\binom{\delta_{F}}{s-1}\leq\mathcal{N}_{s}(G_{F}(n)).\end{split}

At last, if
s=\delta_{F}+1=\ell-1
, recall that each time we delete a vertex during the process of
(\delta_{F}-1)
-
disintegration
, no copy of

is destroyed. Therefore,
\mathcal{N}_{s}(G)=\mathcal{N}_{s}(G^{m})
. It is easy to show that
K_{1}+(tK_{\ell-1}\cup K_{r})
contains the most copies of
K_{s}
among all kinds of
\delta_{F}
-core
when

is large, where

(mod
\ell-1
), that is
\mathcal{N}_{s}(G)=\lfloor\frac{n-1}{\ell-1}\rfloor\ell+1_{r=\ell-2}
.

Combining (i) and (ii), we complete the proof.
\hfill\blacksquare
