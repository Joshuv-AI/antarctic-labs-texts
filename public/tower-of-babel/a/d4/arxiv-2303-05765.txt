, where the Schur multiplier
\mathcal{M}(G)

given by

The Main Results

In
2015
U. Jezernik and P. Moravec in
[
7
]
, showed that if
G
is a finite group and
H
is a subgroup of
G
of index
n
, then
{{\tilde{B_{0}}}(G)}^{n}
(the nth power of Bogomolov multiplier), is isomorphic to a subgroup of
{\tilde{B_{0}}}(H)
. In this section we want to generalize above result to the center by center by
w
variety of groups, where
w
is any outer commutator word.

Lemma 2.1
.

Let
A
be a marginal VP subgroup of
H
i.e.
A\leq V^{*}(H)
and
A\cap T(H)=1
. If
G\cong{H}/{A}
, then
V(H)\cap A
is a homomorphic image of
\mathcal{V}{\tilde{B_{0}}}(G)
.

Proof.

Let
H\cong{F}/{R}
be a free presentation of
H
. Then for some subgroup
K
of
F
, we have
A\cong{K}/{R}
. Hence
G\cong{F}/{K}
and by using the definition

\mathcal{V}{\tilde{B_{0}}}(G)=\frac{K\cap V(F)}{<T(F)\cap K>}\ \ ;\ \ T(F)=\{v(f_{1},...,f_{s})\ |\ v\in V,f_{i}\in F;1\leq i\leq s\}

and Dedekind’s law, we have

\displaystyle V(H)\cap A

\displaystyle=V(\frac{F}{R})\cap\frac{K}{R}=\frac{V(F)R}{R}\cap\frac{K}{R}=\frac{V(F)R\cap K}{R}

\displaystyle=\frac{(V(F)\cap K)R}{R}\cong\frac{V(F)\cap K}{(V(F)\cap K)\cap R}=\frac{V(F)\cap K}{V(F)\cap R}.

Since
{K}/{R}
is a marginal VP subgroup of
{F}/{R}
, by using
1.5
,
({K}/{R})\cap T({F}/{R})=1
. So
<T(F)\cap K>\subseteq R
. Thus we have

\displaystyle V(H)\cap A=\frac{V(F)\cap K}{V(F)\cap R}\cong\frac{\frac{V(F)\cap K}{<T(F)\cap K>}}{\frac{V(F)\cap R}{<T(F)\cap K>}}=\frac{\mathcal{V}{\tilde{B_{0}}}(G)}{\frac{V(F)\cap R}{<T(F)\cap K>}}.

Therefore the result holds.
∎

Corollary 2.2
.

Let
\mathcal{V}
be a Schur-Baer variety and
G
be a finite group. If
H
be a group with a marginal VP subgroup
A
, such that
{H}/{A}\cong G
. Then
V(H)\cap A
is isomorphic to a subgroup of
\mathcal{V}{\tilde{B_{0}}}(G)
.

Proof.

Using Lemma
2.1
,
V(H)\cap A
is a homomorphic image of
\tilde{B_{0}}
-invariant
\mathcal{V}{\tilde{B_{0}}}(G)
. Also, Remark
1.10
and Definition
3.1
in
[
1
]
show that
\mathcal{V}{\tilde{B_{0}}}(G)
is a finite abelian group. Hence according to the property of finite abelian groups,
V(H)\cap A
is isomorphic to a subgroup of
\mathcal{V}{\tilde{B_{0}}}(G)
.
∎

Theorem 2.4

be the variety of groups is defined by the commutator word
