Abstract
. In this paper we prove and discuss some new ( H p , L p ) \left(H_{p},L_{p}\right) type inequalities for partial Sums and Fejér means with respect to Walsh system. It is also proved that these results are the best possible in a special sense. As applications, both some well-known and new results are pointed out.

The elements of

x:=(x_{0},x_{1},...,x_{j},...)\qquad\left(x_{k}=0,1\right).

It is easy to give a base for the neighbourhood of

for any

\backslash

It is evident that

(17)

\overline{I_{M}}=\left(\overset{M-2}{\underset{k=0}{\bigcup}}\overset{M-1}{\underset{l=k+1}{\bigcup}}I_{l+1}\left(e_{k}+e_{l}\right)\right)\bigcup\left(\underset{k=0}{\bigcup\limits^{M-1}}I_{M}\left(e_{k}\right)\right)=\underset{k=0}{\bigcup\limits^{M-1}}I_{k}\backslash I_{k+1}.

Remark 1

\sigma_{n}

\displaystyle\qquad\sigma_{n}f(x)

\displaystyle:

\displaystyle K_{n}(x)

\displaystyle:

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{n}\overset{n}{\underset{k=1}{\sum}}D_{k}(x)\text{ \qquad\thinspace}\left(\text{ }n\in\mathbb{N}_{+}\text{ }\right)
