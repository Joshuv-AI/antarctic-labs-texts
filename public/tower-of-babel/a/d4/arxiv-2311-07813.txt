Rigidity of Travelling Times for Strictly Convex Obstacles in Riemannian Manifolds
Tal Gurfinkel, Lyle Noakes, Luchezar Stoyanov

Abstract
Let K K and L L be two disjoint unions of strictly convex obstacles contained within a Riemannian manifold with boundary S S of dimension m ≥ 2 m\geq 2 . The sets of travelling times 𝒯 K \mathcal{T}_{K} and 𝒯 L \mathcal{T}_{L} of K K and L L , respectively, are composed of triples ( x , y , t ) ∈ ∂ S × ∂ S × ℝ + (x,y,t)\in\partial S\times\partial S\times\mathbb{R}^{+} where t t is the length of a billiard trajectory with endpoints x x and y y that reflects elastically on K K (or L L for ( x , y , t ) ∈ 𝒯 L (x,y,t)\in\mathcal{T}_{L} ). In [ GNS23 ] it was shown that (under some natural curvature bounds on S S ) if 𝒯 K = 𝒯 L \mathcal{T}_{K}=\mathcal{T}_{L} and K K and L L were equivalent up to tangency then K = L K=L . In this paper we remove this requirement for K K and L L , and show that if 𝒯 K = 𝒯 L \mathcal{T}_{K}=\mathcal{T}_{L} then K = L K=L whenever m ≥ 3 m\geq 3 .
