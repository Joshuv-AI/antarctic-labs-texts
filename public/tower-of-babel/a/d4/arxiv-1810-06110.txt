Scrambling time from local perturbations of the rotating BTZ black hole
Andrius Štikonas

Abstract
In this paper, we investigate the entanglement entropy of the rotating BTZ black hole perturbed by a massive back-reacting free falling particle. Then, mutual information between two finite intervals in two asymptotic regions of rotating BTZ is derived. It allows us to find the scrambling time, the time scale in which mutual information vanishes. We give a dual large c c CFT description in terms of a thermofield double state with different temperatures for left and right moving modes that is perturbed by a local operator. Exact matching between gravity and CFT results is obtained.

2.3.1
Left boundary

We will calculate correlators using the same method as in

\displaystyle w(x)

\displaystyle\bar{w}(\bar{x})

\braket{\psi(x_{1},\bar{x}_{1})\psi(x_{4},\bar{x}_{4})}_{C_{1}}=\left|\frac{\beta_{+}\beta_{-}}{\pi^{2}}\sinh\left(\frac{\pi x_{14}}{\beta_{+}}\right)\sinh\left(\frac{\pi\bar{x}_{14}}{\beta_{-}}\right)\right|^{-2h_{\psi}}.

To calculate 4-point function we combine exponential maps with a Möbius map

\tr\rho^{n}_{A}(t)=\left|\frac{\beta_{+}\beta_{-}}{\pi^{2}\epsilon_{\mathrm{UV}}^{2}}\sinh\left(\frac{\pi x_{23}}{\beta_{+}}\right)\sinh\left(\frac{\pi\bar{x}_{23}}{\beta_{-}}\right)\right|^{-2H_{\sigma}}\left|1-z\right|^{4H_{\sigma}}G(z,\bar{z}),

\displaystyle G(z,\bar{z})

\displaystyle\simeq 1+\frac{2\pi i\epsilon}{\beta_{+}}\frac{\sinh\frac{\pi\,L}{\beta_{+}}}{\sinh\frac{\pi(y+L-t_{-}-t_{\omega})}{\beta_{+}}\sinh\frac{\pi(y-t_{-}-t_{\omega})}{\beta_{+}}}+{\cal O}(\epsilon^{2}),

Since four point function above is approximated with a multi valued function, so we have to be careful when we use these cross-rations in equation (

•

Early time:

•

For antiholomorphic coordinates in all cases we have that

\Delta S_{A}=\begin{cases}0\,,\quad\text{ when }t_{-}+t_{\omega}<y\,\,\text{or}\,\,t_{-}+t_{\omega}>y+L,\\

A

. On the other hand, antiholomorphic mode moves in the opposite direction and never passes through region

. So it is expected that
\Delta S_{A}
does not depend on
\beta_{-}
. Note that if we consider negative

does not depend on
\beta_{+}
.

For S-channel, we can write an operator product expansion (OPE) of twist operators

\sigma_{n}(z,\bar{z})\tilde{\sigma}_{n}(1,1)\sim\mathbb{I}+{\mathcal{O}}\left((z-1)^{r}\right)\quad\quad r\in\mathbb{N}.

The dominant contribution in the OPE expansion comes from identity operator. We will ignore all higher order terms. Then summation over

\displaystyle\braket{\psi|\sigma_{n}(z_{5},\bar{z}_{5})\tilde{\sigma}_{n}(1,1)|\psi}

(3.51)

\displaystyle|\Delta\varphi|

\displaystyle\simeq\pi-\frac{1}{|D_{1}|D_{2}}\big[|D_{1}|(\sinh\tilde{\phi}_{2}-\tanh\lambda_{3}\sinh\tilde{t}_{2})+D_{2}(\sinh\tilde{\phi}_{1}-\tanh\lambda_{3}\sinh\tilde{t}_{1})\big].

(3.52)

We can simplify hyperbolic functions to

\displaystyle|D_{1}|D_{2}(|\Delta\varphi|-|\Delta\tau|)

\displaystyle=\frac{\cosh\lambda_{3}}{\cosh\lambda_{2}}(1+\tanh\lambda_{3})\left[-\sinh(\tilde{t}_{2}-\tilde{t}_{1})+\sinh(\tilde{\phi}_{2}-\tilde{t}_{1})-\sinh(\tilde{\phi}_{2}-\tilde{\phi}_{1})+\sinh(\tilde{t}_{2}-\tilde{\phi}_{1})\right]

\displaystyle=4\frac{\cosh\lambda_{3}}{\cosh\lambda_{2}}(1+\tanh\lambda_{3})\sinh\frac{\tilde{t}_{1}-\tilde{\phi}_{1}}{2}\sinh\frac{\tilde{t}_{2}-\tilde{\phi}_{2}}{2}\sinh\frac{\tilde{t}_{1}+\tilde{\phi}_{1}-\tilde{t}_{2}-\tilde{\phi}_{2}}{2}.

(3.53)

Let us substitute the values of co-rotating coordinates

\displaystyle|D_{1}|D_{2}(|\Delta\varphi|-|\Delta\tau|)

\displaystyle=4\frac{\cosh\lambda_{3}}{\cosh\lambda_{2}}(1+\tanh\lambda_{3})\sinh\frac{\pi(t_{\omega}+t_{-}-L_{1})}{\beta_{+}}\sinh\frac{\pi(t_{\omega}+t_{-}-L_{2})}{\beta_{+}}\sinh\frac{\pi(L_{1}-L_{2})}{\beta_{-}}

\displaystyle=4\frac{\cosh\lambda_{3}}{\cosh\lambda_{2}}(1+\tanh\lambda_{3})\sinh\frac{\pi(t_{\omega}+t_{-}-L_{1})}{\beta_{+}}\sinh\frac{\pi(L_{2}-t_{\omega}-t_{-})}{\beta_{+}}\sinh\frac{\pi L}{\beta_{-}}.

(3.54)

We can rewrite this as

\displaystyle\delta:

\displaystyle=|\Delta\varphi|-|\Delta\tau|

\displaystyle=\frac{4}{|D_{1}|D_{2}}\frac{\cosh\lambda_{3}}{\cosh\lambda_{2}}(1+\tanh\lambda_{3})\sinh\frac{\pi L}{\beta_{-}}\sinh\frac{\pi L}{\beta_{+}}\cdot\frac{\sinh\frac{\pi(t_{\omega}+t_{-}-L_{1})}{\beta_{+}}\sinh\frac{\pi(L_{2}-t_{\omega}-t_{-})}{\beta_{+}}}{\sinh\frac{\pi L}{\beta_{+}}}.

(3.55)

Now we can calculate the length of the geodesic connecting the two points

\displaystyle L_{\gamma}

\displaystyle\simeq\log\left[\frac{2\rho^{(1)}\rho^{(2)}}{R^{2}}\frac{\cos\left(a|\Delta\tau|\right)-\cos\left(a|\Delta\varphi|\right)}{a^{2}}\right]

(3.56)

\displaystyle\simeq\log\left[\frac{2\rho^{(1)}\rho^{(2)}}{R^{2}}\frac{\sin\pi a}{a}\delta\right]

(3.57)

\displaystyle\simeq\log\left[\frac{\beta_{-}\beta_{+}}{\pi^{2}z_{\infty}^{2}}\sinh\frac{\pi L}{\beta_{-}}\sinh\frac{\pi L}{\beta_{+}}\right]

\displaystyle+\log\left[2\cosh\lambda_{2}\cosh\lambda_{3}(1+\tanh\lambda_{3})\frac{\sin\pi a}{a}\frac{\sinh\frac{\pi(t_{\omega}+t_{-}-L_{1})}{\beta_{+}}\sinh\frac{\pi(L_{2}-t_{\omega}-t_{-})}{\beta_{+}}}{\sinh\frac{\pi L}{\beta_{+}}}\right].

(3.58)

Note that

2\cosh\lambda_{2}\cosh\lambda_{3}(1+\tanh\lambda_{3})=2\frac{R}{\epsilon\sqrt{r_{+}^{2}-r_{-}^{2}}}\frac{r_{+}}{\sqrt{r_{+}^{2}-r_{-}^{2}}}\frac{r_{+}+r_{-}}{r_{+}}=\frac{\beta_{+}}{\pi\epsilon}.

(3.59)

Thus entanglement entropy is

S_{A}=S_{\text{thermal}}+\frac{c}{6}\log\left[\dfrac{\beta_{+}}{\pi\epsilon}\dfrac{\sin\pi a}{a}\frac{\sinh\dfrac{\pi(t_{\omega}+t_{-}-L_{1})}{\beta_{+}}\sinh\dfrac{\pi(L_{2}-t_{\omega}-t_{-})}{\beta_{+}}}{\sinh\dfrac{\pi L}{\beta_{+}}}\right].

(3.60)

So holographic entanglement entropy precisely matches the CFT result (
2.21
).

3.3.2
Geodesic on the right boundary

The two endpoints of the entanglement region
B
in the right boundary are
(t_{+},r_{\infty},L_{1})
and
(t_{+},r_{\infty},L_{2})
. Their radial coordinates satisfy

\rho^{(1)}\rho^{(2)}\simeq R^{2}\frac{\beta_{-}\beta_{+}}{4z_{\infty}^{2}}\cosh^{2}\lambda_{2}|D_{1}D_{2}|,

(3.61)

where

D_{i}=\cosh\tilde{\phi}_{i}+\cosh\tilde{t}_{i}\equiv\cosh\left(\frac{r_{+}L_{i}}{R}+\frac{r_{-}(t_{+}+t_{\omega})}{R}\right)+\cosh\left(\frac{r_{+}(t_{+}+t_{\omega})}{R}+\frac{r_{-}L_{i}}{R}\right).

(3.62)

The other coordinates satisfy

\displaystyle\tan\tau^{(i)}\simeq-\frac{\cosh\lambda_{3}}{\cosh\lambda_{2}}\frac{\sinh\tilde{t}_{i}+\tanh\lambda_{3}\sinh\tilde{\phi}_{i}}{D_{i}},

(3.63)

\displaystyle\tan\varphi^{(i)}\simeq-\frac{\cosh\lambda_{3}}{\cosh\lambda_{2}}\frac{\sinh\tilde{\phi}_{i}+\tanh\lambda_{3}\sinh\tilde{t}_{i}}{D_{i}}.

(3.64)

On the right boundary
D_{i}>0
, so we always have

\displaystyle\tau^{(i)}\simeq\pi-\frac{\cosh\lambda_{3}}{\cosh\lambda_{2}}\frac{\sinh\tilde{t}_{i}+\tanh\lambda_{3}\sinh\tilde{\phi}_{i}}{D_{i}},

(3.65)

\displaystyle\varphi^{(i)}\simeq\pi-\frac{\cosh\lambda_{3}}{\cosh\lambda_{2}}\frac{\sinh\tilde{\phi}_{i}+\tanh\lambda_{3}\sinh\tilde{t}_{i}}{D_{i}}.

(3.66)

Calculation proceeds as in early time on the left boundary with
\lambda_{3}\mapsto-\lambda_{3}
. But early time case did not depend on
\lambda_{3}
anyway.

Geodesic length is

\displaystyle L_{\gamma}

\displaystyle\simeq\log\left[\frac{2\rho^{(1)}\rho^{(2)}}{R^{2}}\frac{\cos\left(a|\Delta\tau|\right)-\cos\left(a|\Delta\varphi|\right)}{a^{2}}\right]\simeq\log\left[\frac{\rho^{(1)}\rho^{(2)}}{R^{2}}(|\Delta\varphi|^{2}-|\Delta\tau|^{2})\right]

(3.67)

\displaystyle\simeq\log\left[\frac{\beta_{-}\beta_{+}}{\pi^{2}z_{\infty}^{2}}\sinh\frac{\pi L}{\beta_{-}}\sinh\frac{\pi L}{\beta_{+}}\right].

(3.68)

This again reproduces thermal entanglement entropy answer

S_{B}=\frac{c}{6}\log\left[\frac{\beta_{-}\beta_{+}}{\pi^{2}z_{\infty}^{2}}\sinh\frac{\pi L}{\beta_{-}}\sinh\frac{\pi L}{\beta_{+}}\right].

(3.69)

3.3.3
Geodesics across the horizon

We will repeat the calculation with points on the different boundaries. In this case geodesic connects points
(t_{\mp},r_{\infty},L_{i})
(the first point is on the left boundary, the second is on the right). Possible geodesics are shown in
Figure 3
. In order to find entanglement entropy, we need to compare the lengths of geodesics across the horizon and geodesics on the same boundary. Then entanglement entropy of
S_{A\cup B}
will be given by the set of geodesics with the smaller length. This mirrors
S
and
T
-channel expansion in the CFT and ensures that mutual information is non-negative.

Figure 3:
Spatial cross-section of BTZ. There is a competition between green geodesics across the horizon and blue geodesics connecting end points on the same boundary.

The product of radial coordinates satisfies

\rho^{(1)}\rho^{(2)}\simeq R^{2}\frac{\beta_{-}\beta_{+}}{4z_{\infty}^{2}}\cosh^{2}\lambda_{2}|D_{1}D_{2}|,

(3.70)

as before but now
D_{1}
and
D_{2}
have different expressions. We will add
\pm
superscripts to keep track of which boundary co-rotating coordinates belong to

\displaystyle D_{1}=\cosh\tilde{\phi}_{i}^{-}-\cosh\tilde{t}_{i}^{-},

(3.71)

\displaystyle D_{2}=\cosh\tilde{\phi}^{+}_{i}+\cosh\tilde{t}^{+}_{i}.

(3.72)

Points on the boundary.

At early times
L_{i}>t_{-}+t_{\omega}
points on the left boundary satisfy

\displaystyle\tau^{(1)}

\displaystyle\simeq\hphantom{\pi-}\ \frac{\cosh\lambda_{3}}{\cosh\lambda_{2}}\frac{\sinh\tilde{t}_{i}^{-}-\tanh\lambda_{3}\sinh\tilde{\phi}_{i}^{-}}{D_{1}},

(3.73)

\displaystyle\varphi^{(1)}

\displaystyle\simeq\pi-\frac{\cosh\lambda_{3}}{\cosh\lambda_{2}}\frac{\sinh\tilde{\phi}_{i}^{-}-\tanh\lambda_{3}\sinh\tilde{t}_{i}^{-}}{D_{1}}.

(3.74)

whereas at late times

\displaystyle\tau^{(1)}

\displaystyle\simeq\pi-\frac{\cosh\lambda_{3}}{\cosh\lambda_{2}}\frac{\sinh\tilde{t}_{i}^{-}-\tanh\lambda_{3}\sinh\tilde{\phi}_{i}^{-}}{D_{1}},

(3.75)

\displaystyle\varphi^{(1)}

\displaystyle\simeq\hphantom{\pi-}\ \frac{\cosh\lambda_{3}}{\cosh\lambda_{2}}\frac{\sinh\tilde{\phi}_{i}^{-}-\tanh\lambda_{3}\sinh\tilde{t}_{i}^{-}}{D_{1}}.

(3.76)

Similarly, coordinates boundary points on the right are always

\displaystyle\tau^{(2)}

\displaystyle\simeq\hphantom{\pi}-\frac{\cosh\lambda_{3}}{\cosh\lambda_{2}}\frac{\sinh\tilde{t}_{i}^{+}+\tanh\lambda_{3}\sinh\tilde{\phi}_{i}^{+}}{D_{2}},

(3.77)

\displaystyle\varphi^{(2)}

\displaystyle\simeq\pi-\frac{\cosh\lambda_{3}}{\cosh\lambda_{2}}\frac{\sinh\tilde{\phi}_{i}^{+}+\tanh\lambda_{3}\sinh\tilde{t}_{i}^{+}}{D_{2}}.

(3.78)

Early time.

At early times
t_{-}+t_{\omega}<L_{i}
we have

\displaystyle|\Delta\tau|

\displaystyle=|\tau^{(1)}-\tau^{(2)}|\simeq\frac{1}{D_{1}D_{2}}\frac{\cosh\lambda_{3}}{\cosh\lambda_{2}}\left|D_{2}(\sinh\tilde{t}_{i}^{-}-\tanh\lambda_{3}\sinh\tilde{\phi}_{i}^{-})+D_{1}(\sinh\tilde{t}_{i}^{+}+\tanh\lambda_{3}\sinh\tilde{\phi}_{i}^{+})\right|\,,

(3.79)

\displaystyle|\Delta\varphi|

\displaystyle=|\varphi^{(1)}-\varphi^{(2)}|\simeq\frac{1}{D_{1}D_{2}}\frac{\cosh\lambda_{3}}{\cosh\lambda_{2}}\left|D_{2}(\sinh\tilde{\phi}_{i}^{-}-\tanh\lambda_{3}\sinh\tilde{t}_{i}^{-})-D_{1}(\sinh\tilde{\phi}_{i}^{+}+\tanh\lambda_{3}\sinh\tilde{t}_{i}^{+})\right|.

We now use the following identity

\displaystyle D_{1}D_{2}(|\Delta\varphi|^{2}-|\Delta\tau|^{2})

\displaystyle=2\frac{\cosh^{2}\lambda_{3}}{\cosh^{2}\lambda_{2}}(1-\tanh^{2}\lambda_{3})\left[\cosh(\tilde{\phi}_{i}^{-}-\tilde{\phi}_{i}^{+})+\cosh(\tilde{t}_{i}^{-}-\tilde{t}_{i}^{+})\right]

(3.80)

\displaystyle=\frac{4}{\cosh^{2}\lambda_{2}}\cosh\frac{\tilde{\phi}_{i}^{-}-\tilde{\phi}_{i}^{+}+\tilde{t}_{i}^{-}-\tilde{t}_{i}^{+}}{2}\cosh\frac{\tilde{\phi}_{i}^{-}-\tilde{\phi}_{i}^{+}-\tilde{t}_{i}^{-}+\tilde{t}_{i}^{+}}{2}.

(3.81)

Let
\Delta t=t_{-}-t_{+}
, then

D_{1}D_{2}(|\Delta\varphi|^{2}-|\Delta\tau|^{2})=\frac{4}{\cosh^{2}\lambda_{2}}\sinh\frac{\pi\Delta t}{\beta_{-}}\sinh\frac{\pi\Delta t}{\beta_{+}}.

(3.82)

Plugging this into the geodesic length equation (
3.4
), we obtain that at early times

S_{A\cup B}=\frac{c}{6}\log\left[\frac{\beta_{-}\beta_{+}}{\pi^{2}z_{\infty}^{2}}\cosh\frac{\pi\Delta t}{\beta_{-}}\cosh\frac{\pi\Delta t}{\beta_{+}}\right].

(3.83)

Late time.

In the late time regime
t_{-}+t_{\omega}>L_{i}
we have

\displaystyle|\Delta\tau|

\displaystyle=|\tau^{(1)}-\tau^{(2)}|\simeq\pi-\frac{1}{|D_{1}|D_{2}}\frac{\cosh\lambda_{3}}{\cosh\lambda_{2}}\left|D_{2}(\sinh\tilde{t}_{i}^{-}-\tanh\lambda_{3}\sinh\tilde{\phi}_{i}^{-})-|D_{1}|(\sinh\tilde{t}_{i}^{+}+\tanh\lambda_{3}\sinh\tilde{\phi}_{i}^{+})\right|\,,

(3.84)

\displaystyle|\Delta\varphi|

\displaystyle=|\varphi^{(1)}-\varphi^{(2)}|\simeq\pi-\frac{1}{|D_{1}|D_{2}}\frac{\cosh\lambda_{3}}{\cosh\lambda_{2}}\left|D_{2}(\sinh\tilde{\phi}_{i}^{-}-\tanh\lambda_{3}\sinh\tilde{t}_{i}^{-})+|D_{1}|(\sinh\tilde{\phi}_{i}^{+}+\tanh\lambda_{3}\sinh\tilde{t}_{i}^{+})\right|.

As in the non-rotating case,
|\Delta\tau|
and
|\Delta\varphi|
are close to each other, so we define
\delta=|\Delta\varphi|-|\Delta\tau|
. Then

\displaystyle|D_{1}|D_{2}

\displaystyle(|\Delta\varphi|-|\Delta\tau)

(3.85)

\displaystyle=2\frac{\cosh\lambda_{3}}{\cosh\lambda_{2}}(1+\tanh\lambda_{3})\left[\sinh(\tilde{t}_{i}^{-}-\tilde{t}_{i}^{+})+\sinh(\tilde{t}_{i}^{-}-\tilde{\phi}_{i}^{+})-\sinh(\tilde{\phi}_{i}^{-}-\tilde{\phi}_{i}^{+})-\sinh(\tilde{\phi}_{i}^{-}-\tilde{t}_{i}^{+})\right]

(3.86)

\displaystyle=4\frac{\cosh\lambda_{3}}{\cosh\lambda_{2}}(1+\tanh\lambda_{3})\cosh\frac{\tilde{t}^{-}_{i}+\tilde{\phi}^{-}_{i}-\tilde{t}^{+}_{i}-\tilde{\phi}^{+}_{i}}{2}\cosh\frac{\tilde{t}^{+}_{i}-\tilde{\phi}_{i}^{+}}{2}\sinh\frac{\tilde{t}^{-}_{i}-\tilde{\phi}_{i}^{-}}{2}.

(3.87)

|D_{1}|D_{2}(|\Delta\varphi|-|\Delta\tau)=4\frac{\cosh\lambda_{3}}{\cosh\lambda_{2}}(1+\tanh\lambda_{3})\cosh\frac{\pi\Delta t}{\beta_{-}}\cosh\frac{\pi(t_{+}+t_{\omega}-L_{i})}{\beta_{+}}\sinh\frac{\pi(t_{-}+t_{\omega}-L_{i})}{\beta_{+}}.

(3.88)

Therefore, the lengths of geodesics are

\displaystyle L^{1}_{\gamma}

\displaystyle\simeq\log\left[\frac{\beta_{-}\beta_{+}\cosh\frac{\pi\Delta t}{\beta_{-}}\cosh\frac{\pi\Delta t}{\beta_{+}}}{\pi^{2}z_{\infty}^{2}}\frac{\beta_{+}}{\pi\epsilon}\frac{\sin\pi a}{a}\frac{\sinh\frac{\pi(t_{-}+t_{\omega}-L_{1})}{\beta_{+}}\cosh\frac{\pi(t_{+}+t_{\omega}-L_{1})}{\beta_{+}}}{\cosh\frac{\pi\Delta t}{\beta_{+}}}\right],

(3.89)

\displaystyle L^{2}_{\gamma}

\displaystyle\simeq\log\left[\frac{\beta_{-}\beta_{+}\cosh\frac{\pi\Delta t}{\beta_{-}}\cosh\frac{\pi\Delta t}{\beta_{+}}}{\pi^{2}z_{\infty}^{2}}\frac{\beta_{+}}{\pi\epsilon}\frac{\sin\pi a}{a}\frac{\sinh\frac{\pi(t_{-}+t_{\omega}-L_{2})}{\beta_{+}}\cosh\frac{\pi(t_{+}+t_{\omega}-L_{2})}{\beta_{+}}}{\cosh\frac{\pi\Delta t}{\beta_{+}}}\right].

(3.90)

Finally, the entanglement entropy is given by Ryu-Takayanagi formula

S_{A\cup B}\simeq\min\begin{cases}\frac{c}{6}\left(L^{1}_{\gamma}+L^{2}_{\gamma}\right),\\
S_{A}+S_{B}.\end{cases}

(3.91)

which matches with the CFT result.

Intermediate time.

As in
[
14
]
, the intermediate time case is a combination of early and late times cases. The lengths of each geodesic are,

\displaystyle L^{1}_{\gamma}

\displaystyle\simeq\log\left[\frac{\beta_{-}\beta_{+}}{\pi^{2}z_{\infty}^{2}}\cosh\frac{\pi\Delta t}{\beta_{-}}\cosh\frac{\pi\Delta t}{\beta_{+}}\frac{\beta_{+}}{\pi\epsilon}\frac{\sin\pi a}{a}\frac{\sinh\frac{\pi(t_{-}+t_{\omega}-L_{1})}{\beta_{+}}\cosh\frac{\pi(t_{+}+t_{\omega}-L_{1})}{\beta_{+}}}{\cosh\frac{\pi\Delta t}{\beta_{+}}}\right],

(3.92)

\displaystyle L^{2}_{\gamma}

\displaystyle\simeq\log\left[\frac{\beta_{-}\beta_{+}}{\pi^{2}z_{\infty}^{2}}\cosh\frac{\pi\Delta t}{\beta_{-}}\cosh\frac{\pi\Delta t}{\beta_{+}}\right].

(3.93)

All holographic entanglement entropies in the rotating BTZ case exactly match their CFT equivalents, so bulk mutual information and the scrambling time will also match the CFT result (
2.50
).

4
Summary

In this paper we discussed a massive free-falling particle in the rotating BTZ background. We calculated particle’s back-reaction on the geometry and used it to find entanglement entropy and scrambling time. We compared our results with the CFT calculation and found perfect matching.

One of the possible future directions would be to consider how particle with a spin back-reacts on BTZ geometry.
