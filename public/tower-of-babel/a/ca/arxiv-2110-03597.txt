Introduction

The properties of conics regarding incidence have been extensively researched due to their applicability and are generally easier to tackle using projective geometry. However, metric properties, which are closely tied to the Euclidean plane have no better tools in general than analytic geometry. We provide a relationship between pencils of conics and circles.
