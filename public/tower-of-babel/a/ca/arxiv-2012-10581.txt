Reinvestigating the B B → {\to} P ​ P PP decays by including the contributions from ϕ B ​ 2 {\phi}_{B2}
Yueling Yang, Lan Lang, Xule Zhao, Jinshu Huang, Junfeng Sun

Abstract
Considering the B B mesonic distribution amplitude ϕ B ​ 2 {\phi}_{B2} , we reinvestigated the B B → {\to} P ​ P PP (where P P = = π {\pi} and K K ) decays with the perturbative QCD (pQCD) approach based on the k T k_{T} factorization for three scenarios. It is found that the contributions of ϕ B ​ 2 {\phi}_{B2} to formfactors F 0 B → P ​ ( 0 ) F_{0}^{B{\to}P}(0) and branching ratios are comparable with those from the NLO corrections. The B B → {\to} K ​ π K{\pi} decays could be well explained by considering the ϕ B ​ 2 {\phi}_{B2} . Hence, when the nonleptonic B B decays are studied with the pQCD approach, the ϕ B ​ 2 {\phi}_{B2} should be taken into account seriously. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.103.056006
