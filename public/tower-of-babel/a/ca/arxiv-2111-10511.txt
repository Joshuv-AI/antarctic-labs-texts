Constraint on the equation of state parameter ( ω \omega ) in non-minimally coupled f ⁡ ( Q ) f(Q) gravity
Sanjay Mandal, P.K. Sahoo

Abstract
We study observational constraints on the modified symmetric teleparallel gravity, the non-metricity f ⁡ ( Q ) f(Q) gravity, which reproduces background expansion of the universe. For this purpose, we use Hubble measurements, Baryonic Acoustic Oscillations (BAO), 1048 Pantheon supernovae type Ia data sample which integrate SuperNova Legacy Survey (SNLS), Sloan Digital Sky Survey (SDSS), Hubble Space Telescope (HST) survey, Panoramic Survey Telescope and Rapid Response System (Pan-STARRS1). We confront our cosmological model against observational samples to set constraints on the parameters using Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) methods. We find the equation of state parameter ω = − 0.853 − 0.020 + 0.015 \omega=-0.853^{+0.015}_{-0.020} and ω = − 0.796 − 0.074 + 0.049 \omega=-0.796^{+0.049}_{-0.074} for Hubble and Pantheon samples, respectively. As a result, the f ⁡ ( Q ) f(Q) model shows the quintessence behavior and deviates from Λ \Lambda CDM.

II
Action and Field equations

Here, we consider the action for matter coupling in
f(Q)
gravity is given by
Jimenez/2018

S=\int d^{4}x\sqrt{-g}\left[\frac{1}{2}f_{1}(Q)+f_{2}(Q)L_{M}\right],

(1)

where
g
is the determinant of metric,
f_{1}(Q)\,\&\,f_{2}(Q)
are the arbitrary functions of the non-metricity
Q

L_{M}
is the Lagrangian for the matter fields.

The nonmetricity tensor and its traces are such that

Q_{\gamma\mu\nu}=\nabla_{\gamma}g_{\mu\nu}\,,

(2)

Q_{\gamma}={{Q_{\gamma}}^{\mu}}_{\mu}\,,\qquad\widetilde{Q}_{\gamma}={Q^{\mu}}_{\gamma\mu}\,.

(3)

Moreover, the superpotential as a function of nonmetricity tensor is given by

4{P^{\gamma}}_{\mu\nu}=-{Q^{\gamma}}_{\mu\nu}+2Q_{({\mu^{{}^{\gamma}}}{\nu})}-Q^{\gamma}g_{\mu\nu}-\widetilde{Q}^{\gamma}g_{\mu\nu}-\delta^{\gamma}_{{(\gamma^{{}^{Q}}}\nu)}\,,

(4)

where the trace of nonmetricity tensor
Jimenez/2018
reads

Q=-Q_{\gamma\mu\nu}P^{\gamma\mu\nu}\,.

(5)

To simplify the formulation, let us introduce the following notations

\displaystyle f=f_{1}(Q)\,+2f_{2}(Q)L_{M},

(6)

\displaystyle F=f_{1}^{\prime}(Q)+2f_{2}^{\prime}(Q)L_{M},

(7)

where primes (’) represent the derivatives of functions
f_{1}(Q)\,\&\,f_{2}(Q)
with respect to
Q

The energy-momentum tensor for the fluid description of the spacetime can be written by its definition

T_{\mu\nu}=-\frac{2}{\sqrt{-g}}\frac{\delta(\sqrt{-g}\mathcal{L}_{m})}{\delta g^{\mu\nu}}\,.

(8)

By varying action (
1

\frac{2}{\sqrt{-g}}\nabla_{\gamma}\left(\sqrt{-g}F{P^{\gamma}}_{\mu\nu}\right)+\frac{1}{2}g_{\mu\nu}f_{1}\\
+F\left(P_{\mu\gamma i}{Q_{\nu}}^{\gamma i}-2Q_{\gamma i\mu}{P^{\gamma i}}_{\nu}\right)=-f_{2}T_{\mu\nu}\,.

(9)

Now, one can use (

modified gravity.

III
The
f(Q)
cosmology

To explore several cosmological applications, we presume the homogeneous, isotropic and spatially flat line element given by

ds^{2}=-N^{2}(t)dt^{2}+a^{2}(t)\delta_{ij}dx^{i}dx^{j},

(10)

where

\delta_{ij}
is the Kronecker delta and

H=\frac{\dot{a}}{a},\,\,\,\,\ T=\frac{\dot{N}}{N},

respectively. For this line element the non-metricity read as
Q=6(H/N)^{2}

We shall work on the perfect fluid matter distribution, for which the energy momentum tensor (
8

9

f_{2}\rho=\frac{f_{1}}{2}-6F\frac{H^{2}}{N^{2}},

(12)

-f_{2}p=\frac{f_{1}}{2}-\frac{2}{N^{2}}[(\dot{F}-FT)H+F(\dot{H}+3H^{2})],

(13)

respectively. Here,

(14)

From (
14

\dot{\rho}+3H(\rho+p)=0

(15)

This is compatible with the isotropic and homogeneous background of the universe (see more details about the continuity equation in
