BEST Impact on Sterile Neutrino Hypothesis Preprint: INR-TH/2021-017
Vladislav Barinov, Dmitry Gorbunov

Abstract
Recently the Baksan Experiment on Sterile Transitions (BEST) has presented results Barinov et al. 2021 confirming the gallium anomaly —a lack of electron neutrinos ν e \nu_{e} at calibrations of SAGE Abdurashitov et al. 1999 ; Abdurashitov et al. 2006 and GALLEX Kaether et al. 2010 —at the statistical significance exceeding 5 σ \sigma . This result is consistent with explanation of the gallium anomaly as electron neutrino oscillations into sterile neutrino, ν s \nu_{s} . Within this explanation the BEST experiment itself provides the strongest evidence for the sterile neutrino among all the previous anomalous results in the neutrino sector. We combine the results of gallium experiments with searches for sterile neutrinos in reactor antineutrino experiments (assuming CPT-conservation in the 3 + 1 3+1 neutrino sector). While the “gallium” best-fit point in the model parameter space (sterile neutrino mass squared m ν s 2 ≈ 1.25 m_{\nu_{s}}^{2}\approx 1.25 eV 2 , sterile-electron neutrino mixing sin 2 ⁡ 2 ​ θ ≈ 0.34 \sin^{2}2\theta\approx 0.34 ) is excluded by these searches, a part of the BEST-favored 2 σ \sigma region with m ν s 2 > 5 m^{2}_{\nu_{s}}>5 eV 2 is consistent with all of them. Remarkably, the regions advertised by anomalous results of the NEUTRINO-4 experiment Serebrov et al. 2019 overlap with those of the BEST experiment: the best-fit point of the joint analysis is sin 2 ⁡ 2 ​ θ ≈ 0.38 \sin^{2}2\theta\approx 0.38 , m ν s 2 ≈ 7.3 m_{\nu_{s}}^{2}\approx 7.3 eV 2 , the favored region will be explored by the KATRIN experiment Aker et al. 2021 . The sterile neutrino explanation of the BEST results would suggest not only the extension of the Standard Model of particle physics, but also either serious modifications of the Standard Cosmological Model and Solar Model, or a specific modification of the sterile sector needed to suppress the sterile neutrino production in the early Universe and in the Sun.



I
Introduction

Neutrino oscillations provide us with the only direct evidence for
incompleteness of the Standard Model of particle physics (SM), which
fails to explain them. While the fundamental physics responsible for
the oscillations is still elusive, results of the majority of
neutrino experiments can be phenomenologically described by
introducing neutrino mass matrix. The joint analysis (see
e.g. Ref.

determines three mixing angles between the mass and flavor neutrino
eigenstates and two squared-mass differences.

II
Results of the BEST experiment

Therefore, both particle physics and cosmology would benefit from
settling this issue with neutrino anomalies, and there are various
projects and ongoing experiments dedicated to clarification of
particular anomalous results. One of these experiments is Baksan
Experiment on Sterile Transitions (BEST) aimed at exploring the
gallium anomaly

Laveder 2007

which is the lack of electron neutrino events from

Abdurashitov et al. 2006

experiments. BEST was placed in the Baksan Neutrino Observatory of INR RAS

SAGE calibration its geometry has been specifically designed to achieve the

tuned to explain the gallium anomaly via
\nu_{e}\leftrightarrow\nu_{s}

BEST exploited an artificial chromium source
simultaneously irradiating two volumes filled with gallium, which
allowed one to measure the neutrino flux at two different distances.
The BEST experiment

the gallium anomaly and are consistent with its explanation as electron neutrino
oscillations into the sterile components.
In the BEST experiment the artificial source was very compact, placed
in the center of the inner spherical volume, which was placed in the center of
the outer cylindrical volume.
The neutrino fluxes were

IV
Conclusions

To summarize, we investigate how the recent results of BEST
experiment can affect the hypothesis of ’3+1’ scheme in neutrino
sector. Note in passing that the explanation of the observed lack of neutrino
events as oscillations to the sterile neutrino is not the only
possibility involving new physics. Moreover, an overall error in
neutrino cross section on gallium or some issues in extraction
efficiency cannot be ruled out, though all the aspects of experimental
procedure have been verified and many have been
double-checked
Barinov et al. 2021
.
