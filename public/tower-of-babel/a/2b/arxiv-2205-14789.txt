A dichotomy result for closed characteristics on compact star-shaped hypersurfaces in 𝐑 2 ​ n {\bf R}^{2n}
Huagui Duan, Hui Liu, Wenyan Ren

Abstract
In this paper, we prove that if all closed characteristics on a compact non-degenerate star-shaped hypersurface Σ \Sigma in 𝐑 2 ​ n {\bf R}^{2n} are elliptic, then either there exist exactly n n geometrically distinct closed characteristics, or there exist infinitely many geometrically distinct closed characteristics.



Key words
: Closed characteristic, star-shaped hypersurface, elliptic, Maslov-type index.

2010 Mathematics Subject Classification
: 58E05, 37J45, 34C25.

1
Introduction and main results

Let
\Sigma
be a
C^{3}
compact hypersurface in
{\bf R}^{2n}
strictly star-shaped with respect to the origin, i.e.,
the tangent hyperplane at any
x\in\Sigma
does not intersect the origin. We denote the set of all such
hypersurfaces by
{\cal H}_{st}(2n)
, and denote by
{\cal H}_{con}(2n)
the subset of
{\cal H}_{st}(2n)
which consists of all
strictly convex hypersurfaces. We consider closed characteristics
(\tau,y)
on
\Sigma
, which are solutions
of the following problem

\left\{\matrix{\dot{y}=JN_{\Sigma}(y),\cr y(\tau)=y(0),\cr}\right.

(1.1)

where
J=\left(\matrix{0&-I_{n}\cr I_{n}&0\cr}\right)
,
I_{n}
is the identity matrix in
{\bf R}^{n}
,
\tau>0
,
N_{\Sigma}(y)
is the outward
normal vector of
\Sigma
at
y
normalized by the condition
N_{\Sigma}(y)\cdot y=1
. Here
a\cdot b
denotes
the standard inner product of
a,b\in{\bf R}^{2n}
. A closed characteristic
(\tau,y)
is
prime
, if
\tau

is the minimal period of
y
. Two closed characteristics
(\tau,y)
and
(\sigma,z)
are
geometrically
distinct
, if
y({\bf R})\not=z({\bf R})
. We denote by
{\cal T}(\Sigma)
the set of geometrically distinct
closed characteristics
(\tau,y)
on
\Sigma\in\mathcal{H}_{st}(2n)
. A closed characteristic

(\tau,y)
is
non-degenerate
if
1
is a Floquet multiplier of
y
of precisely algebraic multiplicity

2
;
hyperbolic
if
1
is a double Floquet multiplier of it and all the other Floquet multipliers
are not on
{\bf U}=\{z\in{\bf C}\mid|z|=1\}
, i.e., the unit circle in the complex plane;
elliptic

if all the Floquet multipliers of
y
are on
{\bf U}
;
irrationally elliptic
if
1
is its double Floquet multipliers, and the other
(2n-2)
locating on the unit circle with rotation angles being irrational multiples of
\pi
. We call a
\Sigma\in\mathcal{H}_{st}(2n)

non-degenerate
if
all the closed characteristics on
\Sigma
together with all of their iterations are non-degenerate.

There is a long standing conjecture on the
number of closed characteristics on compact convex hypersurfaces in

{\bf R}^{2n}
:

\,{}^{\#}{\cal T}({\Sigma})\geq n,\qquad\forall\;{\Sigma}\in{\cal H}_{con}(2n).

(1.2)

In 1978, P. Rabinowitz in
[
Rab
]
proved

{}^{\#}{\cal T}({\Sigma})\geq 1
for any
{\Sigma}\in{\cal H}_{st}(2n)
and A. Weinstein in
[
Wei
]

proved
{}^{\#}{\cal T}({\Sigma})\geq 1
for any
{\Sigma}\in{\cal H}_{con}(2n)
independently.
When
n\geq 2
, in 1987-1988, I. Ekeland-L. Lassoued, I. Ekeland-H. Hofer, and A. Szulkin (cf.
[
EkL
]
,

[
EkH
]
,
[
Szu
]
) proved

\,{}^{\#}{\cal T}({\Sigma})\geq 2,\qquad\forall\,{\Sigma}\in{\cal H}_{con}(2n).

In
[
HWZ1
]
of 1998, H. Hofer, K. Wysocki, and E. Zehnder proved
\,{}^{\#}{\cal T}({\Sigma})=2
or
\infty
holds
for every
{\Sigma}\in{\cal H}_{con}(4)
.
In
[
LoZ
]
of 2002, Y. Long and C. Zhu further proved

\;{}^{\#}{\cal T}({\Sigma})\geq\left[\frac{n}{2}\right]+1,\qquad\forall\,{\Sigma}\in{\cal H}_{con}(2n).

In particular, if all the prime closed characteristics on
{\Sigma}
are non-degenerate,
then
\,{}^{\#}{\cal T}({\Sigma})\geq n
, cf. Theorem 1.1 and Corollary 1.1 of
[
LoZ
]
.
In
[
WHL
]
of 2007,
W. Wang, X. Hu and Y. Long proved
\,{}^{\#}{\cal T}({\Sigma})\geq 3
for
every
{\Sigma}\in{\cal H}_{con}(6)
. In
[
Wan1
]
of 2016, W. Wang proved
\,{}^{\#}{\cal T}({\Sigma})\geq\left[\frac{n+1}{2}\right]+1
for every
{\Sigma}\in{\cal H}_{con}(2n)
. In
[
Wan2
]
of 2016, W. Wang proved
\,{}^{\#}{\cal T}({\Sigma})\geq 4
for every
{\Sigma}\in{\cal H}_{con}(8)
.

Note that every contact form supporting the standard contact structure on
M=S^{2n-1}

arises from embeddings of
M
into
{\bf R}^{2n}
as a strictly star-shaped hypersurface enclosing the origin,
it is conjectured that in fact the conjecture (
1.2
) holds for any
{\Sigma}\in{\cal H}_{st}(2n)
. For star-shaped case,
[
Gir
]
of 1984 and
[
BLMR
]
of 1985 show that
\;{}^{\#}{\cal T}({\Sigma})\geq n
for
{\Sigma}\in{\cal H}_{st}(2n)
under some pinching conditions. In
[
HuL
]
of 2002, X. Hu and Y. Long proved that
\;{}^{\#}{\cal T}({\Sigma})\geq 2
for any non-degenerate
{\Sigma}\in{\cal H}_{st}(2n)
. In
[
HWZ2
]
of 2003,
H. Hofer, K. Wysocki, and E. Zehnder proved that
\,{}^{\#}{\cal T}({\Sigma})=2
or
\infty
holds for every non-degenerate

{\Sigma}\in{\cal H}_{st}(4)
provided that all stable and unstable manifolds of the hyperbolic closed orbits
on
{\Sigma}
intersect transversally, and recently this transversal condition was removed in an important paper by D. Cristofaro-Gardiner, M. Hutchings and D. Pomerleano
[
CGHP
]
. In
[
CGH
]
of 2016, D. Cristofaro-Gardiner and M. Hutchings proved that
\;{}^{\#}{\cal T}({\Sigma})\geq 2
for every contact manifold
{\Sigma}
of dimension three. Later various proofs of this result for star-shaped hypersurfaces have been given in
[
GHHM
]
,
[
LLo1
]
and
[
GiG
]
.

Since the appearance of
[
HWZ1
]
and
[
HWZ2
]
, Hofer, among others, has popularized in many talks
the following much more stronger conjecture than (

), cf., Conjeture 1.5 of
[

]

Conjecture 1.1.

For every integer
n\geq 2
, there holds

\displaystyle\{\,^{\#}{\cal T}({\Sigma})\mid{\Sigma}\in{\cal H}_{st}(2n)\}=\{n\}\cup\{+\infty\}.

A typical example is the following weakly non-resonant ellipsoid:

\displaystyle\mathcal{E}_{n}(r)=\left\{z=(x_{1},\ldots,x_{n},y_{1},\ldots,y_{n})\in{\bf R}^{2n}\left|\frac{}{}\right.\frac{1}{2}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\frac{x_{i}^{2}+y_{i}^{2}}{r_{i}^{2}}=1\right\},

where

for
1\leq i\leq n
, we
have
\mathcal{E}_{n}(r)\in\mathcal{H}_{st}(2n)
. In this case, the corresponding Hamiltonian system is linear and all the solutions of (
1.1
) can be computed explicitly. It is easy to verify that
{}^{\#}{\cal T}(\mathcal{E}_{n}(r))=n
, and all the closed characteristics on
\mathcal{E}_{n}(r)
are irrationally elliptic whenever
\frac{r_{i}}{r_{j}}\notin{\bf Q}
for all
i\neq j
, and
{}^{\#}{\cal T}(\mathcal{E}_{n}(r))=\infty
otherwise.

Motivated by the dichotomy results of
[
HWZ1
]
and
[
HWZ2
]
, the detailed information about the “exceptional” case of hypersurfaces with exactly two closed characteristics arouses many interest. In
[
Lon1
]
of 2000, Long proved that
{\Sigma}\in{\cal H}_{con}(4)

and
\,{}^{\#}{\cal T}({\Sigma})=2
imply that both of the closed characteristics must be elliptic.
In
[
WHL
]
of 2007, W. Wang, X. Hu and Y. Long proved further that

{\Sigma}\in{\cal H}_{con}(4)
and
\,{}^{\#}{\cal T}({\Sigma})=2
imply that both of the closed characteristics must be irrationally
elliptic. In
[
LLo2
]
of 2015 and
[
LLo3
]
of 2017, H. Liu and Y. Long proved
that the existence of exactly two closed characteristics on
{\Sigma}\in{\cal H}_{st}(4)
implies that both of them must be irrationally elliptic
provided that
{\Sigma}
is symmetric with respect to the origin. Recently, D. Cristofaro-Gardiner, U.L. Hryniewicz, M. Hutchings and H. Liu
[
CGHHL
]

removed this symmetric condition and proved a more general result that if there exist exactly two closed orbits on a contact manifold of dimension three, then both orbits are irrationally elliptic and this manifold is diffeomorphic to the three-sphere or a lens
space.

In
[
Vit2
]
of 1989, C. Viterbo proved the existence of infinitely many closed characteristics on a compact star-shaped hypersurface
\Sigma
in
{\bf R}^{4n}
, if all closed characteristics on
\Sigma
are hyperbolic. This result has also been obtained by U. L. Hryniewicz and L. Macarini in Corollary 1.8 of
[
HM
]
.

In this paper, motivated by the above results, we prove Conjecture 1.1 provided that all closed characteristics on
\Sigma\in\mathcal{H}_{st}(2n)
are non-degenerate and elliptic.

Theorem 1.2.

If all closed characteristics on a compact non-degenerate star-shaped hypersurface

\Sigma
in
{\bf R}^{2n}
are elliptic, then either there exist exactly
n
geometrically
distinct closed characteristics, or there exist infinitely many geometrically
distinct closed characteristics.

{\bf N}
,
{\bf N}_{0}
,

,

,

,

,
\{a\}=a-[a]
,

and
\varphi(a)=E(a)-[a]
. Denote by

and

the standard
inner product and norm in
{\bf R}^{2n}
. Denote by

the standard

-space

, we denote by

, i.e.,

,

In this paper we use

and

.

Now we fix a

(F)
There exist only finitely many geometrically distinct prime closed characteristics

\qquad\qquad\{(\tau_{j},y_{j})\}_{1\leq j\leq k}

Let

[

together with
\varphi(0)=0=\varphi^{\prime}(0)

\varphi^{\prime\prime}(0)=1=\lim_{t\rightarrow 0^{+}}\frac{\varphi^{\prime}(t)}{t}

define
\varphi_{a}
and the Hamiltonian function
\widetilde{H}_{a}(x)=a{\varphi}_{a}(j(x))
via Lemma 2.2 and Lemma 2.4
in
[
LLW
]
. The precise dependence of
\varphi_{a}
on

[
LLW
]

For technical reasons we further modify the Hamiltonian
\widetilde{H}_{a}
, and define the new Hamiltonian
function
H_{a}
via Proposition 2.5 of
[
LLW
]
and consider the fixed period problem

\dot{x}(t)=JH_{a}^{\prime}(x(t)),\quad x(0)=x(T).

(2.1)

Then
H_{a}\in C^{3}({\bf R}^{2n}\setminus\{0\},{\bf R})\cap C^{1}({\bf R}^{2n},{\bf R})
.
Solutions of (
2.1
) are
x\equiv 0
and

with

\frac{{\varphi}_{a}^{\prime}(\rho)}{\rho}=\frac{\tau}{aT}
, where
(\tau,y)
is a solution of (

non-zero solutions of (
2.1

1.1
) with period

\tau<aT
.

For any
a>\frac{\hat{\tau}}{T}
, we can choose some large constant

such that

H_{a,K}(x)=H_{a}(x)+\frac{1}{2}K|x|^{2}

(2.2)

is a strictly convex function, that is,

(\nabla H_{a,K}(x)-\nabla H_{a,K}(y),x-y)\geq\frac{{\epsilon}}{2}|x-y|^{2},

(2.3)

for all

, and some positive
{\epsilon}
. Let
H_{a,K}^{*}
be the Fenchel dual of
H_{a,K}

defined by

H_{a,K}^{\ast}(y)=\sup\{x\cdot y-H_{a,K}(x)\;|\;x\in{\bf R}^{2n}\}.

The dual action functional on
X=W^{1,2}({\bf R}/{T{\bf Z}},{\bf R}^{2n})
is defined by

F_{a,K}(x)=\int_{0}^{T}{\left[\frac{1}{2}(J\dot{x}-Kx,x)+H_{a,K}^{*}(-J\dot{x}+Kx)\right]dt}.

(2.4)

Then
F_{a,K}\in C^{1,1}(X,{\bf R})

satisfies the

x
is a critical point of
F_{a,K}
if and only if it is a solution of (
2.1
). Moreover,

F_{a,K}(x_{a})<0
and it is independent of
K

F_{a,K}
.

When
KT\notin 2\pi{\bf Z}
, the map
x\mapsto-J\dot{x}+Kx
is a Hilbert space isomorphism between

X=W^{1,2}({\bf R}/({T{\bf Z}});{\bf R}^{2n})
and

by

and the functional

\Psi_{a,K}(u)=\int_{0}^{T}{\left[-\frac{1}{2}(M_{K}u,u)+H_{a,K}^{*}(u)\right]dt},\qquad\forall\,u\in E.

(2.5)

Then

is a critical point of

if and only if

\Psi_{a,K}
.

Suppose

\Psi_{a,K}
.
Then the formal Hessian of

Similarly, we define the index and nullity of
