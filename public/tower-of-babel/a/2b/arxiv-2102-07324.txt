4.1.
Basic settings in Moran Construction

Let
\mathcal{I}=\{I_{n,j}:1\leq j\leq m_{n},n\in\mathbb{N}\}
be a sequence of the intervals
in
[0,1]
such that

(1)

for any

(2)

for any

1\leq i\leq m_{n}

(3)

for each

is contained in one of the elements in
I_{n,i}
for
1\leq j\leq m_{n+1}
.

We denote

and
R_{n}=\max\limits_{1\leq i\leq m_{n}}\textrm{diam}(I_{n,i})
. We assume that
\lim\limits_{n\rightarrow\infty}R_{n}=0
. Let

. Here,

is said to be the

-th fundamental interval in the
n
-th level
of

. We will call

to be Moran set. For any

, there exists a sequence of nested intervals

such that

.

The following observation is crucial in the estimates of the lower bound of the Hausdroff dimension of the Moran set.

Lemma 6
.

Assume that the fundamental intervals satisfy the following conditions

(22)

\begin{split}\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}\frac{\log R_{n}}{\log r_{n}}=1\quad\text{and}\quad\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}\frac{\log r_{n+1}}{\log r_{n}}=1.\end{split}

Let
\eta
be a measure supported on
Y
which satisfies the balanced property

(23)

\lim\limits_{n\rightarrow\infty}\frac{\min\limits_{1\leq i\leq m_{n}}\log\eta(I_{n,i})}{\max\limits_{1\leq i\leq m_{n}}\log\eta(I_{n,i})}=1.

Then we have

\begin{split}\limsup_{r\rightarrow 0}\frac{\log\eta(B(x,r))}{\log r}=\limsup_{n\rightarrow\infty}\frac{\log\eta(I_{n,l_{n}(x)})}{\log r_{n}}\\
\liminf_{r\rightarrow 0}\frac{\log\eta(B(x,r))}{\log r}=\liminf_{n\rightarrow\infty}\frac{\log\eta(I_{n,l_{n}(x)})}{\log r_{n}}\end{split}

for any
x\in Y
.

Proof.

For any
x\in Y
,
r>0
, there exists a nested sequence of intervals
\{I_{k,l_{k}(x)}\}_{k=1}^{\infty}
, such that
x\in I_{k,l_{k}(x)}
for any
k\in\mathbb{N}
. And there always exits a unique integer
n
such that
R_{n+1}<r<R_{n}
.

We assume that there exists
N=N(x,n,r)
intervals in
\{I_{n,j}\}_{j=1}^{m_{n}}
which intersect with
B(x,r)
. It follows that

Nr_{n}\leq\underset{B(x,r)\cap I_{n,j}\neq\emptyset}{\sum}|I_{n,j}|\leq 4R_{n}.

Thus, we get
N\leq\frac{4R_{n}}{r_{n}}
. Combining the fact that
B(x,r)
contains at least one element in
\{I_{n+1,j}:1\leq j\leq m_{n+1}\}
, we have

\frac{\log\eta(B(x,r))}{\log r}\leq\frac{\min_{1\leq j\leq m_{n+1}}\log\eta(I_{n+1,j})}{\log R_{n}}

and

\frac{\log\eta(B(x,r))}{\log r}\geq\frac{\log R_{n}-\log r_{n}}{\log R_{n+1}}+\frac{\max_{1\leq j\leq m_{n}}\log\eta(I_{n,j})}{\log R_{n+1}}.

By (
22
) and (
23
), we proved the desired conclusion.
∎

It seems that the conditions in Lemma
6
are too strong and awkward at the first glance. Indeed they are very useful especially in estimating the lower bound of the Hausdroff dimension in the Moran constructions.

4.2.
Moran construction driven by dynamical system

In many applications, the fundamental intervals in the different levels in Moran construction was obtained by dynamical system. In this section, we try to introduce a framework for Moran construction driven by dynamical system.

For each
n\in\mathbb{N}\cup\{0\}
, let

In other words, we have

We can introduce a family of measures

\mathcal{I}_{n}

\eta_{n}(J_{n})=\prod_{k=0}^{n}\mu_{k}(\hat{I}_{k,t_{k}}).

It is easy to see the family of measures

\eta(J_{n})=\prod_{k=0}^{n}\mu_{k}(\hat{I}_{k,t_{k}}).

For any

, any

\hat{I}_{k,t_{k}}=\hat{I}_{k,t_{k}}(x)

such that

and

.

We can transfer Lemma
6
to the following result, which will be very helpful in the construction of Moran set.

Lemma 7

Let
\eta
satisfies the balanced property defined in Lemma
6

\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}\frac{\log\textrm{diam}J_{n}(x)}{\sum\limits_{i=0}^{n}\log\textrm{diam}I_{i,t_{i}}(x)}=1

\displaystyle\frac{1}{n}\bigg|S_{n_{q}}f_{i}(\pi(\omega))-n_{q}\int f_{i}d\mu\bigg|+\frac{1}{n}\bigg|S_{n-n_{q}}f_{i}(\sigma^{n-n_{q}}\pi(\omega))-(n-n_{q})\int f_{i}d\mu\bigg|

\displaystyle\frac{1}{n}\bigg|S_{n_{q}}f_{i}(\pi(\omega))-n_{q}\int f_{i}d\mu\bigg|+\frac{2(n-n_{q})\|f_{i}\|}{n}

\displaystyle\frac{1}{n_{q}}\bigg|S_{n_{q}}f_{i}(\pi(\omega))-n_{q}\int f_{i}d\mu\bigg|+\frac{2(n_{q+1}-n_{q})\|f_{i}\|}{n_{q}}.

Taking
n
goes to infinity, we complete the proof of Lemma
8
.
