Search for the η \eta -mesic Helium bound state with the WASA-at-COSY facility
Magdalena Skurzok a, Wojciech Krzemień b, Oleksandr Rundel a, Pawel Moskal a for the WASA-at-COSY Collaboration

Abstract
We performed a search for He 4 {}^{4}\hskip-0.85355pt\mbox{He} - η \eta bound state with high statistics and high acceptance with the WASA-at-COSY facility using a ramped beam technique. The signature of η \eta -mesic nuclei is searched for in d ​ d → dd\rightarrow 3 He n π 0 {}^{3}\hskip-0.85355pt\mbox{He}n\pi{}^{0} and d ​ d → dd\rightarrow 3 He p π − {}^{3}\hskip-0.85355pt\mbox{He}p\pi{}^{-} reactions by the measurement of the excitation functions in the vicinity of the η \eta production threshold. This paper presents the experimental method and the preliminary results of the data analysis for d ​ d → dd\rightarrow 3 He n π 0 {}^{3}\hskip-0.85355pt\mbox{He}n\pi{}^{0} process.

Search for

-
\eta

We performed the search for the
{}^{4}\hskip-0.85355pt\mbox{He}
-
\eta
bound state with unique accuracy with the WASA facility, installed at the COSY synchrotron in Forschungszentrum Jülich (Germany). The advantage of this detection system is the possibility of a simultaneous measurement of all ejectiles with high acceptance while continuously changing the beam momentum around the
\eta
production threshold. The signature of the
\eta
-mesic
nuclei is searched for by studying the excitation function for the chosen decay channels of the
{}^{4}\hskip-0.85355pt\mbox{He}
-
\eta
system, formed in deuteron-deuteron collision
[
40
,
5
]
. Till now two experiments were carried out focusing on the bound state decay into
{}^{3}\hskip-0.85355pt\mbox{He}
and a nucleon-pion pair
[

,
5
,
41
,
6
,
42

.

The first experiment was carried out in June 2008, by measuring the excitation function of the
dd\rightarrow

{}^{3}\hskip-0.85355pt\mbox{He}p\pi{}^{-}
reaction near the
\eta
production threshold covering the excess energy range from -51.4 MeV up to 22 MeV. In the excitation function no structure which could be interpreted as a resonance originating from decay of the
\eta
-mesic
{}^{4}\hskip-0.85355pt\mbox{He}
was observed. The upper limit of the total cross-section for the bound state formation and decay in the
dd\rightarrow
(
{}^{4}\hskip-0.85355pt\mbox{He}
-
\eta)_{bound}\rightarrow

{}^{3}\hskip-0.85355pt\mbox{He}p\pi{}^{-}
process was determined and varies from 20 nb to 27 nb at the confidence level 90%
[
41
,
6
]
.

In the second experiment, in November 2010, about 10 times higher statistics were collected with respect to the previous measurement. The search for the
{}^{4}\hskip-0.85355pt\mbox{He}
-
\eta
bound state was performed for two reactions
dd\rightarrow

{}^{3}\hskip-0.85355pt\mbox{He}n\pi{}^{0}
and
dd\rightarrow

{}^{3}\hskip-0.85355pt\mbox{He}p\pi{}^{-}
via the measurement of the excitation function for each of them in the vicinity of the
\eta

\eta

dd\rightarrow

{}^{4}\hskip-0.85355pt\mbox{He}\eta
reaction at 2.336 GeV/c, which corresponds to a range of excess energy
Q
\in
(-70,30) MeV

dd\rightarrow

{}^{3}\hskip-0.85355pt\mbox{He}n\pi{}^{0}

The

\Delta
E-E method

\pi^{0}

Events corresponding to the bound states production were selected by applying cuts in the
{}^{3}\hskip-0.85355pt\mbox{He}
center of mass (CM) momentum, nucleon CM kinetic energy, pion CM kinetic energy and the opening angle between neutron-pion pair in the CM based on Monte Carlo simulations.

The excitation functions for the reaction were determined for a region rich in signal corresponding to momenta of the
{}^{3}\hskip-0.85355pt\mbox{He}
in the CM system in range

The excitation function was obtained by normalizing the events selected in individual excess energy intervals by the corresponding integrated luminosities (the detailed description of the luminosity determination one can find in Ref.
[
42

44

No narrow resonance-like strucutre, which could be interpreted as the indication of the
\eta

3
Conclusion

The performed analysis allows for the determination of the excitation function for
dd\rightarrow(^{4}\hskip-0.85355pt\mbox{He}
-
\eta)_{bound}\rightarrow

{}^{3}\hskip-0.85355pt\mbox{He}n\pi{}^{0}
process and the estimation of the upper limit of the cross section for the
\eta
-mesic
{}^{4}\hskip-0.85355pt\mbox{He}
formation and decay. The obtained excitation function does not show any narrow structure which could be a signature of the bound state with width less than 50 MeV. The upper limit is by a factor of five larger than theoretically predicted value
[
21
]
therefore, the current measurement does not exclude the existence of bound state in this process.
