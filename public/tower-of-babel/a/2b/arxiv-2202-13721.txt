Concentrated solutions for a critical elliptic equation
Lipeng Duan and Shuying Tian

Abstract
. In this paper, we are concerned with the following elliptic equation { − Δ ​ u = Q ⁡ ( x ) ​ u 2 ∗ − 1 + ε ​ u s , in ​ Ω , u > 0 , in ​ Ω , u = 0 , on ​ ∂ Ω , \begin{cases}-\Delta u=Q(x)u^{2^{*}-1}+\varepsilon u^{s},~&{\text{in}~\Omega},\\[2.84526pt] u>0,~&{\text{in}~\Omega},\\[2.84526pt] u=0,&{\text{on}~\partial\Omega},\end{cases} where N ≥ 3 N\geq 3 , s ∈ [ 1 , 2 ∗ − 1 ) s\in[1,2^{*}-1) with 2 ∗ = 2 ​ N N − 2 2^{*}=\frac{2N}{N-2} , ε > 0 \varepsilon>0 , Ω \Omega is a smooth bounded domain in ℝ N \mathbb{R}^{N} . Introduction and main results

In this paper, we are concerned with the following elliptic equations

\begin{cases}-\Delta u=Q(x)u^{2^{*}-1}+\varepsilon u^{s},~&{\text{in}~\Omega},\\[2.84526pt]
u>0,~&{\text{in}~\Omega},\\[2.84526pt]

where

(1.2)

and

In this case, the positive solution will concentrate at the critical point of the Robin function or the Kirchhoff-Routh function. Also we point out that the research on the

Theorem A.
Suppose that
