Analysis of the L3 BEC at Z 0 -pole – Comparison of the conventional formula against the τ \tau -model –
Takuya Mizoguchi, Seiji Matsumoto, Minoru Biyajima

Abstract
The L3 Collaboration reported data on 2-jet and 3-jet Bose–Einstein correlations (BECs) with the results obtained through the τ \tau -model in 2011. In this study, we analyze these correlations using the conventional formula with the Gaussian long-range correlation ( CF I × LRC ( Gauss ) {\rm CF_{I}\times LRC_{(Gauss)}} ). The estimated ranges of interactions for 2-jet and 3-jet, R 2 − jet R_{\rm 2\mathchar 45jet} and R 3 − jet R_{\rm 3\mathchar 45jet} , are almost the same magnitude as those by τ \tau -model: R 2 − jet = 0.83 ± 0.05 R_{\rm 2\mathchar 45jet}=0.83\pm 0.05 (stat) fm and R 3 − jet = 1.09 ± 0.04 R_{\rm 3\mathchar 45jet}=1.09\pm 0.04 (stat) fm. The anticorrelation in BEC (less than 1.0) observed by the L3 Collaboration is related to the partially negative density profile ( ρ τ , BE ​ ( ξ ) \mbox{\Large$\rho$}_{\rm\tau,BE}(\xi) in ξ \xi space) in the τ \tau -model and the LRC ( Gauss ) = 1 / ( 1 + α ​ e − β ​ Q 2 ) {\rm LRC_{(Gauss)}}=1/(1+\alpha e^{-\beta Q^{2}}) in CF I × LRC ( Gauss ) {\rm CF_{I}\times LRC_{(Gauss)}} , respectively. Remarkably, the probability P τ ​ ( ξ ) P_{\tau}(\xi) calculated from the Levy canonical form exhibits partially negative behavior.

Introduction

The L3 Collaboration reported their data on 2-jet and 3-jet Bose–Einstein correlation (BEC) in
e^{+}e^{-}
collisions at Z
0
-pole in 2011
[
1
]
. The BEC is described in terms of the four momentum transfer
Q=\sqrt{-(p_{1}-p_{2})^{2}}
,

\displaystyle R_{2}=\frac{\rho_{2}(p_{1},\,p_{2})}{\rho_{0}(p_{1},\,p_{2})}=\frac{\rho_{2}(Q)}{\rho_{0}(Q)}

(1)

where the numerator is the two-particle (mainly pions) distribution including the BE effect and the denominator is the two-particle distribution without the BE effect which is the mixture procedure. That means the
\rho_{0}(Q)
distribution made from different events.

Furthermore, introducing various corrections, L3 Collaboration adopted the following ratio expressed by Eq. (
1
),

\displaystyle R_{2}^{\rm(L3)}=\frac{R_{\rm 2\,data}\cdot R_{\rm 2\,gen}}{R_{\rm 2\,det}\cdot R_{\rm 2\,gen\mathchar 45noBE}}.

(2)

In Eq. (
2
), the suffixes data, gen, det, and gen-noBE mean the following ensembles, respectively:

1) data:

the data sample.

2) gen:

a generator-level Monte Carlo (MC) sample.

3) det:

the same MC sample passed through detector simulation and subject to the same selection procedure as the data.

and 4) gen-noBE:

a generator-level sample of a MC generated without BEC simulation.

Finally,
R_{2}^{\rm(L3)}
data are analyzed by the following
\tau
-model based on the Levy canonical form to explain the anticorrelation (BEC: less than 1.0) observed in the interval
0.5\ {\rm GeV}\ Q<1.5
GeV:

\displaystyle F_{\rm\tau}=\left[1+\lambda\cos\left((R_{a}Q)^{2\alpha_{\tau}}\right)\exp\left(-(RQ)^{2\alpha_{\tau}}\right)\right]\times{\rm LRC_{(linear)}},

(3)

where
R
denotes the magnitude of the interaction region and
R_{a}
does a constrain expressed by
R_{a}^{2\alpha_{\tau}}=\tan(\alpha_{\tau}\pi/2)R^{2\alpha_{\tau}}
. The long-range correlation (
{\rm LRC_{(linear)}}
) is expressed as
{\rm LRC_{(linear)}}=C(1+\delta Q)
. The parameter
\lambda
denotes the degree of coherence, and
\alpha_{\tau}
represents the characteristic index introduced in the Levy canonical form in the stochastic theory
[
2
,
3
]
.

In contrast, OPAL
[
4
]
, DELPHI
[
5
]

\displaystyle{\rm DR}=\frac{C_{2}^{\rm data}(Q)}{C_{2}^{\rm MC}(Q)}=\frac{N^{(2+:2-)}(Q)/N^{(+-)}(Q)}{N_{\rm MC}^{(2+:2-)}(Q)/N_{\rm MC}^{(+-)}(Q)},

(4)

where

The anticorrelation in the 2-jet and 3-jet events, i.e.,
R_{2}^{\rm(L3)}

0.5\leq Q\leq 1.5

In the conventional formula with the function,
{\rm CF_{I}(Exp)\times LRC_{(Gauss)}}
, the anticorrelation is explained by the Gaussian function with
{\rm LRC_{(Gauss)}-1}=\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}(-\alpha)^{k}\exp(-k\beta Q^{2})

({\rm LRC_{(Gauss)}-1}
):
F_{\rm cross}(Q)=e^{-RQ}\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}(-\alpha)^{k}e^{-k\beta Q^{2}}
. For reference, we present data on

at
Z^{0}
-ploe by DELPHI Collaboration
[
5
]
in Appendix
A
because we have no-information on them (
R_{\rm 2\,data}
,

and
(R_{\rm 2\,gen}/R_{\rm 2\,det})
) by L3 Collaboration at present.

Alternatively, in the
