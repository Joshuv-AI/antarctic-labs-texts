Abstract.

Proof.

Let

(1)

Let

\alpha_{1}

\beta_{1}

(2)

\frac{d-b}{b}=\frac{a-b}{a+b}.

Eliminating
b

1

equation for

d=2a/(1+(1\pm\sqrt{n})^{2})
. But in the minus sign

2b-d=2a(1-4\sqrt{n})/(n^{2}-n+2\sqrt{n}+2)<0
by (
1
).
Hence
d=2a/(1+(1+\sqrt{n})^{2})
. This proves (ii). Let
|BC|=2h

from the right triangle
formed by the line
BC

\alpha_{1}
and

\beta_{1}

\alpha_{1}
to
BC
, we get

(a-h)^{2}+(a-d)^{2}=a^{2}
.
Solving the equation for
h
, we have
h=a-\sqrt{(2a-d)d}=an/(1+(1+\sqrt{n})^{2})
.
This proves (i).
