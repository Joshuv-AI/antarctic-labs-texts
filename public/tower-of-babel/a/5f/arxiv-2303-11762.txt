Abstract
The polarized cross section ratio σ L ​ T ′ / σ 0 \sigma_{LT^{\prime}}/\sigma_{0} from hard exclusive π − ​ Δ + ⁣ + \pi^{-}\Delta^{++} electroproduction off an unpolarized hydrogen target has been extracted based on beam-spin asymmetry measurements using a 10.2 GeV / 10.6 GeV incident electron beam and the CLAS12 spectrometer at Jefferson Lab. The study, which provides the first observation of this channel in the deep-inelastic regime, focuses on very forward-pion kinematics in the valence regime, and photon virtualities ranging from 1.5 GeV 2 up to 7 GeV 2 . The reaction provides a novel access to the d d -quark content of the nucleon and to p → Δ + ⁣ + p\rightarrow\Delta^{++} transition generalized parton distributions. A comparison to existing results for hard exclusive π + ​ n \pi^{+}n and π 0 ​ p \pi^{0}p electroproduction is provided, which shows a clear impact of the excitation mechanism, encoded in transition generalized parton distributions, on the asymmetry.

Hard exclusive meson electroproduction provides a powerful tool to study the structure of the nucleon and the underlying reaction dynamics as the process amplitude depends on Generalized Parton Distributions (GPDs)

For longitudinally polarized virtual photons, the factorization of the process amplitude into a perturbatively calculable hard-scattering part and two soft parts has been proven at large photon virtuality

x_{B}

Within such a framework we can significantly extend our understanding of the strong interaction dynamics underlying the generation of the structure of ground and excited nucleon states as relativistic bound systems of quarks and gluons. For this purpose, another set of GPDs, the so-called transition GPDs, have been introduced.

The electron identification was based on a lead-scintillator electromagnetic sampling calorimeter in combination with a Čherenkov counter. In summary, we have performed a first multidimensional measurement of the structure function ratio
\sigma_{LT^{\prime}}/\sigma_{0}
for
\vec{e}p\to e^{\prime}\pi^{-}\Delta^{++}

p\to\Delta
transition GPDs. The measurement can give us a direct access to the

p\to\Delta
transition GPDs. The observed results in comparison to the

The measurements presented in this work have initiated first theoretical investigations of the hard exclusive
\pi^{-}\Delta^{++}
production based on transition GPDs

N\to N^{*}
DVCS process, as well as other
N\to N^{*}
deeply virtual meson production (DVMP) channels at JLab and at the future EIC with an extension to the strangeness sector.
