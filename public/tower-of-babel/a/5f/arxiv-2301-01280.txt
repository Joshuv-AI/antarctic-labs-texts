Introduction

Let
B_{n}^{[1]}:C[0,1]\to C[0,1]
be the classical Bernstein operator defined as

B_{n}^{[1]}f(x)=\displaystyle\sum_{i=0}^{n}f\left(\dfrac{i}{n}\right)p_{n,i}(x),

where
p_{n,i}(x)={n\choose i}x^{i}(1-x)^{n-i},\,x\in[0,1]
.

For a fixed
j\in{\mathbb{N}}
,
j\geq 2
and for
n\geq j
, Aldaz, Kounchev and Render
[
2
]
introduced a polynomial operator
B_{n,j}^{[1]}:C[0,1]\to C[0,1]
that fixes
e_{0}
and
e_{j}
, investigated its approximation properties and gave applications to CAGD. The operator is explicitly given by

B_{n,j}^{[1]}f(x)=\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{n}f\left(t_{n,k}^{j}\right)p_{n,k}(x),

where

t_{n,k}^{j}=\left(\dfrac{k(k-1)\dots(k-j+1)}{n(n-1)\dots(n-j+1)}\right)^{1/j}.

The Voronovskaja-type formula for the sequence
(B_{n,j}^{[1]})_{n\geq 1}
was conjectured in
[
4
]
and proved in
[
3
]
,
[
5
]
.

For
f\in C([0,1]^{2})
, the tensor product
B_{n}^{[1]}\otimes B_{n}^{[1]}
is given by

B_{n}^{[2]}f(x,y):=(B_{n}^{[1]}\otimes B_{n}^{[1]})f(x,y)=\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{n}\sum_{l=0}^{n}f\left(\dfrac{k}{n},\frac{l}{n}\right)p_{n,k}(x)p_{n,l}(y).

(1.1)

Let
B_{n,j}^{[1]}:C[0,1]\to C[0,1]
be the AKR operator and
(x,y)\in[0,1]^{2}
. Then, for
f\in C([0,1]^{2})
, the tensor product
B_{n,j}^{[1]}\otimes B_{n,j}^{[1]}
is given by

\displaystyle B_{n,j}^{[2]}f(x,y)

\displaystyle:=(B_{n,j}^{[1]}\otimes B_{n,j}^{[1]})f(x,y)

\displaystyle=\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{n}\sum_{l=0}^{n}f\left(t_{n,k}^{j},t_{n,l}^{j}\right)p_{n,k}(x)p_{n,l}(y),\,\,(x,y)\in[0,1]^{2}.

(1.2)

A conjecture concerning the Voronovskaja-type formula for the sequence
(B_{n,j}^{[2]})
was formulated in
[

]

2
Proof of Conjecture

For the sake of conciseness we consider only the case
j=2
, but obviously the proof can be extended to arbitrary
j
.

Let
k
and
n
be integers,
n\geq 2
,
0\leq k\leq n
. Define

R(n,k):=\dfrac{k}{n}-\sqrt{\dfrac{k(k-1)}{n(n-1)}}-\dfrac{1}{2n}+\dfrac{k}{2n^{2}}.

It is elementary to prove that

\displaystyle R(n,0)=-\dfrac{1}{2n},

(2.1)

\displaystyle R(n,k)\geq 0,\,\,k=1,2,\dots,n,

(2.2)

\displaystyle 0\leq\dfrac{k}{n}-\sqrt{\dfrac{k(k-1)}{n(n-1)}}\leq\dfrac{1}{n}.

(2.3)

Lemma 2.1
.

If
0<x\leq 1
, then

\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}n\sum_{k=1}^{n}p_{n,k}(x)R(n,k)=0.

(2.4)

Proof.

Let
x\in(0,1]
and
f\in C^{2}[0,1]
. It is known (see
[
3
]
,
[
5
]
) that

\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}n(B_{n,2}^{[1]}f(x)-f(x))=\dfrac{x(1-x)}{2}f^{\prime\prime}(x)-\dfrac{1-x}{2}f^{\prime}(x).

It is also well known that

\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}n(B_{n}^{[1]}f(x)-f(x))=\dfrac{x(1-x)}{2}f^{\prime\prime}(x).

It follows that

\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}n\left(B_{n,2}^{[1]}f(x)-B_{n}^{[1]}f(x)\right)=-\dfrac{1-x}{2}f^{\prime}(x).

In particular, for the function
f(t)=t
, we get

\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}n\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{n}p_{n,k}(x)\left(\sqrt{\dfrac{k(k-1)}{n(n-1)}}-\dfrac{k}{n}\right)=-\dfrac{1-x}{2}.

This can be written as

\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}n\sum_{k=1}^{n}p_{n,k}(x)\left[\dfrac{1}{2n}\left(1-\dfrac{k}{n}\right)+R(n,k)\right]=\dfrac{1-x}{2},

i.e.,

\dfrac{1}{2}\lim_{n\to\infty}\sum_{k=1}^{n}p_{n,k}(x)\left(1-\dfrac{k}{n}\right)+\lim_{n\to\infty}n\sum_{k=1}^{n}p_{n,k}(x)R(n,k)=\dfrac{1-x}{2}.

(2.5)

Let us remark that

\dfrac{1}{2}\lim_{n\to\infty}\sum_{k=1}^{n}p_{n,k}(x)\left(1-\dfrac{k}{n}\right)=\dfrac{1}{2}\lim_{n\to\infty}\left(B_{n}^{[1]}(1-t;x)-(1-x)^{n}\right)=\dfrac{1}{2}(1-x).

Combined with (
2.5
) this leads to (
2.4
), and the proof is finished.
∎

Theorem 2.1
.

Let
0<x\leq 1
,
0<y\leq 1
,
f\in C^{2}([0,1]^{2})
. Then

\displaystyle\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}n\left(B_{n,2}^{[2]}f(x,y)-f(x,y)\right)

\displaystyle=\dfrac{x(1-x)}{2}f_{x^{2}}^{\prime\prime}(x,y)+\dfrac{y(1-y)}{2}f_{y^{2}}^{\prime\prime}(x,y)-\dfrac{1-x}{2}f_{x}^{\prime}(x,y)-\dfrac{1-y}{2}f_{y}^{\prime}(x,y).

(2.6)

Proof.

First we have

\displaystyle n\left(B_{n,2}^{[2]}f(x,y)-B_{n}^{[2]}f(x,y)\right)

\displaystyle=n\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{n}\sum_{l=0}^{n}p_{n,k}(x)p_{n,l}(y)\left[f\left(\sqrt{\dfrac{k(k-1)}{n(n-1)}},\sqrt{\dfrac{l(l-1)}{n(n-1)}}\right)-f\left(\dfrac{k}{n},\dfrac{l}{n}\right)\right]

\displaystyle=E_{n}f(x,y)+F_{n}f(x,y)+G_{n}f(x,y),

where

\displaystyle E_{n}f(x,y)

\displaystyle:=n\sum_{k=0}^{n}\sum_{l=0}^{n}p_{n,k}(x)p_{n,l}(y)\left(\sqrt{\dfrac{k(k-1)}{n(n-1)}}-\dfrac{k}{n}\right)f^{\prime}_{x}\left(\dfrac{k}{n},\dfrac{l}{n}\right),

\displaystyle F_{n}f(x,y)

\displaystyle:=n\sum_{k=0}^{n}\sum_{l=0}^{n}p_{n,k}(x)p_{n,l}(y)\left(\sqrt{\dfrac{l(l-1)}{n(n-1)}}-\dfrac{l}{n}\right)f^{\prime}_{y}\left(\dfrac{k}{n},\dfrac{l}{n}\right),

\displaystyle G_{n}f(x,y)

\displaystyle:=\dfrac{n}{2}\sum_{k=0}^{n}\sum_{l=0}^{n}p_{n,k}(x)p_{n,l}(y)\left\{\left(\sqrt{\dfrac{k(k-1)}{n(n-1)}}-\dfrac{k}{n}\right)^{2}f^{\prime\prime}_{x^{2}}(\xi,\eta)\right.

\displaystyle+2\left(\sqrt{\dfrac{k(k-1)}{n(n-1)}}-\dfrac{k}{n}\right)\left(\sqrt{\dfrac{l(l-1)}{n(n-1)}}-\dfrac{l}{n}\right)f^{\prime\prime}_{xy}(\xi,\eta)

\displaystyle+\left.\left(\sqrt{\dfrac{l(l-1)}{n(n-1)}}-\dfrac{l}{n}\right)^{2}f^{\prime\prime}_{y^{2}}(\xi,\eta)\right\},

for suitable
(\xi,\eta)
furnished by Taylor’s formula. Using (
2.3
) we see that

\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}G_{n}f(x,y)=0.

(2.7)

Moreover,

\displaystyle\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}E_{n}f(x,y)

\displaystyle=-\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}n\sum_{k=0}^{n}\sum_{l=0}^{n}p_{n,k}(x)p_{n,l}(y)\left[\dfrac{1}{2n}\left(1-\dfrac{k}{n}\right)+R(n,k)\right]f_{x}^{\prime}\left(\dfrac{k}{n},\dfrac{l}{n}\right)

\displaystyle=-\dfrac{1}{2}\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}\sum_{k=0}^{n}\sum_{l=0}^{n}p_{n,k}(x)p_{n,l}(y)\left(1-\dfrac{k}{n}\right)f_{x}^{\prime}\left(\dfrac{k}{n},\dfrac{l}{n}\right)

\displaystyle-\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}n\sum_{k=0}^{n}\sum_{l=0}^{n}p_{n,k}(x)p_{n,l}(y)R(n,k)f_{x}^{\prime}\left(\dfrac{k}{n},\dfrac{l}{n}\right)

\displaystyle=-\dfrac{1}{2}\lim_{n\to\infty}B_{n}^{[2]}\left((1-s)f^{\prime}_{x}(s,t);(x,y)\right)

\displaystyle-\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}n\sum_{k=1}^{n}\sum_{l=0}^{n}p_{n,k}(x)p_{n,l}(y)R(n,k)f_{x}^{\prime}\left(\dfrac{k}{n},\dfrac{l}{n}\right)

\displaystyle+\lim_{n\to\infty}n\sum_{l=0}^{n}(1-x)^{n}p_{n,l}(y)\dfrac{1}{2n}f_{x}^{\prime}\left(0,\dfrac{l}{n}\right).

The first term equals
-\dfrac{1}{2}(1-x)f_{x}^{\prime}(x,y)
.

Moreover, using (
2.2
) we have

\displaystyle\left|\displaystyle n\sum_{k=1}^{n}\sum_{l=0}^{n}p_{n,k}(x)p_{n,l}(y)R(n,k)f_{x}^{\prime}\left(\dfrac{k}{n},\dfrac{l}{n}\right)\right|

\displaystyle\leq\sum_{l=0}^{n}\left(n\sum_{k=1}^{n}p_{n,k}(x)R(n,k)\|f_{x}^{\prime}\|_{\infty}\right)p_{n,l}(y)

\displaystyle=n\sum_{k=1}^{n}p_{n,k}(x)R(n,k)\|f_{x}^{\prime}\|_{\infty},

and (
2.4
) shows that the second term is zero. The third one is also zero, and so

\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}E_{n}f(x,y)=-\dfrac{1-x}{2}f_{x}^{\prime}(x,y).

(2.8)

Similarly,

\lim_{n\to\infty}F_{n}f(x,y)=-\dfrac{1-y}{2}f_{y}^{\prime}(x,y).

(2.9)

Now (
2.7
), (
2.8
), (
2.9
) yield

\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}n\left(B_{n,2}^{[2]}f(x,y)-B_{n}^{[2]}f(x,y)\right)=-\dfrac{1-x}{2}f_{x}^{\prime}(x,y)-\dfrac{1-y}{2}f_{y}^{\prime}(x,y).

(2.10)

On the other hand, it is well known that

\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}n(B_{n}^{[2]}f(x,y)-f(x,y))=\dfrac{x(1-x)}{2}f_{x^{2}}^{\prime\prime}(x,y)+\dfrac{y(1-y)}{2}f_{y^{2}}^{\prime\prime}(x,y).

(2.11)

From (
2.10
) and (
2.11
) we get (
2.6
) and the theorem is proved.
∎
