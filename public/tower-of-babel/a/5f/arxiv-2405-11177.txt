Abstract
We introduce a new elliptic quantum toroidal algebra U q , κ , p ​ ( 𝔤 t ​ o ​ r ) U_{q,\kappa,p}({g}_{tor}) associated with an arbitrary toroidal algebra 𝔤 t ​ o ​ r {g}_{tor} . We show that U q , κ , p ​ ( 𝔤 t ​ o ​ r ) U_{q,\kappa,p}({g}_{tor}) contains two elliptic quantum algebras associated with a corresponding affine Lie algebra 𝔤 ^ \widehat{{g}} as subalgebras. They are analogue of the horizontal and the vertical subalgebras in the quantum toroidal algebra U q , κ ​ ( 𝔤 t ​ o ​ r ) U_{q,\kappa}({g}_{tor}) . A Hopf algebroid structure is introduced as a co-algebra structure of U q , κ , p ​ ( 𝔤 t ​ o ​ r ) U_{q,\kappa,p}({g}_{tor}) using the Drinfeld comultiplication. We also investigate the Z Z -algebra structure of U q , κ , p ​ ( 𝔤 t ​ o ​ r ) U_{q,\kappa,p}({g}_{tor}) and show that the Z Z -algebra governs the irreducibility of the level ( k ( ≠ 0 ) , l ) (k(\not=0),l) -infinite dimensional U q , κ , p ​ ( 𝔤 t ​ o ​ r ) U_{q,\kappa,p}({g}_{tor}) -modules in the same way as in the elliptic quantum group U q , p ​ ( 𝔤 ^ ) U_{q,p}(\widehat{{g}}) . As an example, we construct the level ( 1 , l ) (1,l) irreducible representation of U q , κ , p ​ ( 𝔤 t ​ o ​ r ) U_{q,\kappa,p}({g}_{tor}) for the simply laced 𝔤 t ​ o ​ r {g}_{tor} . We also construct the level ( 0 , 1 ) (0,1) representation of U q , κ , p ​ ( 𝔤 ​ 𝔩 N , t ​ o ​ r ) U_{q,\kappa,p}({{gl}}_{N,tor}) and discuss a conjecture on its geometric interpretation as an action on the torus equivariant elliptic cohomology of the affine A N − 1 A_{N-1} quiver variety.
