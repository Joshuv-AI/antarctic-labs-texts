Abstract
. We consider solutions satisfying the Neumann zero boundary condition and a linearized mean field game system in Ω × ( 0 , T ) \Omega\times(0,T) , where Ω \Omega is a bounded domain in ℝ d \mathbb{R}^{d} and ( 0 , T ) (0,T) is the time interval. We prove two kinds of stability results in determining the solutions. The first is Hölder stability in time interval ( ε , T ) (\varepsilon,T) with arbitrarily fixed ε > 0 \varepsilon>0 by data of solutions in Ω × { T } \Omega\times\{T\} . The second is the Lipschitz stability in Ω × ( ε , T − ε ) \Omega\times(\varepsilon,T-\varepsilon) by data of solutions in arbitrarily given subdomain of Ω \Omega over ( 0 , T ) (0,T) .

[formula]

\left\{\begin{array}[]{rl}&\partial_{t}u+\Delta u=Q_{1}u+Sv+F,\\

P_{1}w_{1}=\partial_{t}w_{1}-s\lambda\varphi w_{1}+\Delta w_{1}=\widetilde{F}e^{s\varphi}

and so

\displaystyle\|P_{1}w_{1}\|^{2}_{L^{2}(Q)}\geq\int_{Q}|\partial_{t}w_{1}|^{2}dxdt+2\int_{Q}(\partial_{t}w_{1})\Delta w_{1}dxdt-\int_{Q}2s\lambda\varphi w_{1}(\partial_{t}w_{1})dxdt

\displaystyle=:

\displaystyle\int_{Q}|\partial_{t}w_{1}|^{2}dxdt+\widetilde{I_{1}}+\widetilde{I_{2}}.

Now we estimate

\displaystyle\widetilde{I_{1}}=-2\int_{Q}\nabla(\partial_{t}w_{1})\cdot\nabla w_{1}dxdt=-\int_{Q}\partial_{t}(|\nabla w_{1}|^{2})dxdt

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}\left[|\nabla w_{1}|^{2}\right]^{0}_{T}dx=\int_{\Omega}(|\nabla w_{1}(x,0)|^{2}-|\nabla w_{1}(x,T)|^{2})dx

and

\displaystyle\widetilde{I_{2}}=-\int_{Q}2w_{1}(\partial_{t}w_{1})s\lambda\varphi dxdt=-\int_{Q}\partial_{t}(|w_{1}|^{2})s\lambda\varphi dxdt

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}\left[s\lambda\varphi|w_{1}|^{2}\right]^{0}_{T}dx+\int_{Q}s\lambda^{2}\varphi|w_{1}|^{2}dxdt.

Consequently,

[formula]

\int_{Q}|\partial_{t}w_{1}|^{2}dxdt+\int_{Q}s\lambda^{2}\varphi|w_{1}|^{2}dxdt+\int_{\Omega}(s\lambda|w_{1}(x,0)|^{2}+|\nabla w_{1}(x,0)|^{2})dx

\leq C\int_{Q}|\widetilde{F}|^{2}e^{2s\varphi}dxdt+C\int_{\Omega}(s\lambda\varphi(T)|w_{1}(x,T)|^{2}+|\nabla w_{1}(x,T)|^{2})dx.

Next we consider

w_{1}P_{1}w_{1}=w_{1}\partial_{t}w_{1}-s\lambda\varphi|w_{1}|^{2}+w_{1}\Delta w_{1}\quad\mbox{in $Q$}.

Integrating over
Q
, we obtain

\int_{Q}\frac{1}{2}\partial_{t}(|w_{1}|^{2})dxdt-\int_{Q}s\lambda\varphi|w_{1}|^{2}dxdt-\int_{Q}|\nabla w_{1}|^{2}dxdt=\int_{Q}w_{1}(P_{1}w_{1})dxdt.

Hence

\displaystyle-\frac{1}{2}\int_{\Omega}\left[|w_{1}|^{2}\right]^{T}_{0}dx+\int_{Q}s\lambda\varphi|w_{1}|^{2}dxdt+\int_{Q}|\nabla w_{1}|^{2}dxdt

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\int_{Q}w_{1}(P_{1}w_{1})dxdt\leq\int_{Q}|w_{1}||P_{1}w_{1}|dxdtdxdt,

that is, the multiplication by
\lambda
yields

\displaystyle\lambda\int_{Q}|\nabla w_{1}|^{2}dxdt+\int_{Q}s\lambda^{2}\varphi|w_{1}|^{2}dxdt+\int_{\Omega}\lambda\left[|w_{1}|^{2}\right]^{0}_{T}dx\leq

\displaystyle C\int_{Q}\lambda^{2}|w_{1}|^{2}dxdt+C\int_{Q}|\widetilde{F}|^{2}e^{2s\varphi}dxdt.

Here we used

\int_{Q}\lambda|w_{1}||P_{1}w_{1}|dxdt\leq\frac{1}{2}\int_{Q}\lambda^{2}|w_{1}|^{2}dxdt+\frac{1}{2}\int_{Q}|P_{1}w_{1}|^{2}dxdt.

Adding (4.5), we see

\displaystyle\int_{Q}|\partial_{t}w_{1}|^{2}dxdt+\int_{Q}\left(2s\lambda^{2}\varphi-C\lambda^{2}\right)|w_{1}|^{2}dxdt+\int_{Q}\lambda|\nabla w_{1}|^{2}dxdt

\displaystyle+

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}\{(s\lambda+\lambda)|w_{1}(x,0)|^{2}+|\nabla w_{1}(x,0)|^{2}\}dx

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C\int_{Q}|\widetilde{F}|^{2}e^{2s\varphi}dxdt+C\int_{\Omega}\{(s\lambda\varphi(T)+\lambda)|w_{1}(x,T)|^{2}+|\nabla w_{1}(x,T)|^{2}\}dx.

Using

\frac{1}{s\varphi}|\partial_{t}v|^{2}e^{2s\varphi}\leq\frac{1}{s\varphi}|\partial_{t}w|^{2}+Cs\lambda^{2}\varphi|w|^{2},

we can complete the proof of (4.4).

\blacksquare

Third Step.

We set
u_{1}:=\varphi^{\frac{1}{2}}u
. Then

\partial_{t}u_{1}=\frac{1}{2}\lambda\varphi^{\frac{1}{2}}u+\varphi^{\frac{1}{2}}\partial_{t}u
,
and so

[formula]

\partial_{t}u_{1}+\Delta u_{1}=\varphi^{\frac{1}{2}}\widetilde{F}+\frac{1}{2}\lambda\varphi^{\frac{1}{2}}u.

Therefore the application of (4.4) to (4.6) yields

\displaystyle\int_{Q}\biggl\{\frac{1}{s\varphi}\varphi\left(\left|\partial_{t}u+\frac{1}{2}\lambda u\right|^{2}+|\Delta u|^{2}\right)+\lambda\varphi|\nabla u|^{2}+s\lambda^{2}\varphi^{2}|u|^{2}\biggr\}e^{2s\varphi}dxdt

\displaystyle+

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}(s\lambda|u_{1}(x,0)|^{2}+|\nabla u_{1}(x,0)|^{2})e^{2s}dx

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int_{Q}\biggl\{\frac{1}{s\varphi}(|\partial_{t}u_{1}|^{2}+|\Delta u_{1}|^{2})+\lambda|\nabla u_{1}|^{2}+s\lambda^{2}\varphi|u_{1}|^{2}\biggr\}e^{2s\varphi}dxdt

\displaystyle+

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}(s\lambda|u_{1}(x,0)|^{2}+|\nabla u_{1}(x,0)|^{2})e^{2s}dx

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C\int_{Q}|\widetilde{F}|^{2}\varphi e^{2s\varphi}dxdt+C\int_{Q}\lambda^{2}\varphi|u|^{2}e^{2s\varphi}dxdt

[formula]

+C\int_{\Omega}(s\lambda\varphi(T)|u_{1}(x,T)|^{2}+|\nabla u_{1}(x,T)|^{2})e^{2s\varphi(T)}dx.

Hence, choosing
s>0
large, we can absorb the second term on the right-hand
side of (4.7) into
the left-hand side, we obtain

[formula]

\int_{Q}(|\partial_{t}u|^{2}-C\left|\frac{1}{2}\lambda u\right|^{2}+|\Delta u|^{2}+s\lambda\varphi|\nabla u|^{2}+s^{2}\lambda^{2}\varphi^{2}|u|^{2})e^{2s\varphi}dxdt

\displaystyle+

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}(s^{2}\lambda|u(x,0)|^{2}+s|\nabla u(x,0)|^{2})e^{2s}dx

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C\int_{Q}s\varphi|\widetilde{F}|^{2}e^{2s\varphi}dxdt+C\int_{\Omega}(s^{2}\lambda\varphi(T)^{2}|u(x,T)|^{2}+s\varphi(T)|\nabla u(x,T)|^{2})e^{2s\varphi(T)}dx

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle C\int_{Q}s\varphi|\widetilde{F}|^{2}e^{2s\varphi}dxdt+CD(u(\cdot,T)).

Here and henceforth, for short descriptions, we set

D(u(\cdot,T)):=\int_{\Omega}(s^{2}\lambda\varphi(T)^{2}|u(x,T)|^{2}+s\varphi(T)|\nabla u(x,T)|^{2})e^{2s\varphi(T)}dx

with fixed large
\lambda,s>0
.

Fourth Step.

We set
\widetilde{F}:=Q_{1}u+Sv
and
\widetilde{G}:=Q_{2}(u,v)+\rho_{0}\Delta u
.
We note that

\displaystyle|\widetilde{F}(x,t)|\leq C(|Sv(x,t)|+|u(x,t)|+|\nabla u(x,t)|),

\displaystyle|\widetilde{G}(x,t)|\leq C(|u(x,t)|+|\nabla u(x,t)|+|v(x,t)|+|\nabla v(x,t)|+|\Delta u(x,t)|)

for
(x,t)\in Q
. Moreover, in the case (1.4), we estimate

\int_{Q}s\varphi|Sv(x,t)|^{2}e^{2s\varphi}dxdt=\int^{T}_{0}s\varphi e^{2s\varphi}\left(\int_{\Omega}|Sv(x,t)|^{2}dx\right)dt=\int^{T}_{0}s\varphi e^{2s\varphi}\|Sv(\cdot,t)\|_{L^{2}(\Omega)}^{2}dt

\leq C\int^{T}_{0}s\varphi e^{2s\varphi}\|v(\cdot,t)\|^{2}_{L^{2}(\Omega)}dt=C\int_{Q}s\varphi|v|^{2}e^{2s\varphi}dxdt.

Therefore,

[formula]

\int_{Q}s\varphi|\widetilde{F}|^{2}e^{2s\varphi}dxdt\leq C\int_{Q}s\varphi(|v|^{2}+|u|^{2}+|\nabla u|^{2})e^{2s\varphi}dxdt.

Moreover (4.8) implies

\int_{Q}|\widetilde{G}|^{2}e^{2s\varphi}dxdt\leq C\int_{Q}(|u|^{2}+|\nabla u|^{2}+|v|^{2}+|\nabla v|^{2}+|\Delta u|^{2})e^{2s\varphi}dxdt

[formula]

\leq C\int_{Q}s\varphi|\widetilde{F}|^{2}e^{2s\varphi}dxdt+C\int_{Q}(|v|^{2}+|\nabla v|^{2})e^{2s\varphi}dxdt+CD(u(\cdot,T)).

Adding (4.2) and (4.8), and applying (4.9) and (4.10), we obtain

\displaystyle\int_{Q}\biggl\{|\partial_{t}u|^{2}+|\Delta u|^{2}+s\lambda\varphi|\nabla u|^{2}+s^{2}\lambda^{2}\varphi^{2}|u|^{2}+\frac{1}{s\varphi}(|\partial_{t}v|^{2}+|\Delta v|^{2})+\lambda|\nabla v|^{2}+s\lambda^{2}\varphi|v|^{2}\biggr\}e^{2s\varphi}dxdt

\displaystyle+

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}(s\lambda|v(x,0)|^{2}e^{2s}+|\nabla v(x,T)|^{2}e^{2s\varphi(T)})dx

\displaystyle+

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}(s^{2}\lambda|u(x,0)|^{2}+s|\nabla u(x,0)|^{2})e^{2s}dx

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C\int_{Q}(s\varphi|\widetilde{F}|^{2}+|\widetilde{G}|^{2})e^{2s\varphi}dxdt+C\int_{\Omega}(s\lambda\varphi(T)|v(x,T)|^{2}e^{2s\varphi(T)}+|\nabla v(x,0)|^{2}e^{2s})dx+CD(u(\cdot,T))

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C\int_{Q}s\varphi(|v|^{2}+|u|^{2}+|\nabla u|^{2})e^{2s\varphi}dxdt+C\int_{Q}(|v|^{2}+|\nabla v|^{2})e^{2s\varphi}dxdt

\displaystyle+

\displaystyle CD(u(\cdot,T))+C\int_{\Omega}(s\lambda\varphi(T)|v(x,T)|^{2}e^{2s\varphi(T)}+|\nabla v(x,0)|^{2}e^{2s})dx.

Choosing
s,\lambda>0
large and noting that the powers of the terms

|v|^{2}
are
s\lambda^{2}\varphi
on the left-hand side and

s\varphi
and
1
on the right-hand side, etc., we can absorb the first and
the second terms on the right-hand
side into the left-hand side,
we complete the proof of Theorem 2.1.

\blacksquare
