Additive cycle complex and coherent duality
Fei Ren

Abstract
. Let k k be a field of positive characteristic p p , and X X be a separated of finite type k k -scheme of dimension d d . We construct a cycle map from the additive cycle complex to the residual complex of Serre-Grothendieck coherent duality theory. This map is compatible with a cubical version of the map constructed in [ Ren23 ] when k k is perfect. As a corollary, we get injectivity statements for (additive) higher Chow groups as well as for motivic cohomology (with modulus) with ℤ / p \mathbb{Z}/p coefficients when k k is algebraically closed.

E^{1}_{s.t}=\bigoplus_{x\in X_{(s)}}{\operatorname{CH}}_{d-r}(k(x),s+t-2d+2r;\mathbb{Z}/p^{n})\Rightarrow{\operatorname{CH}}_{d-r}(X,s+t-2d+2r;\mathbb{Z}/p^{n})=H^{2d-s-t}(\mathbb{Z}(r)_{X}(X)/p^{n})

for all

. Hence the spectral sequence degenerate at

, and
we have canonical isomorphisms

H^{d-s+r}(\mathbb{Z}(r)_{X}(X)/p^{n})\simeq H_{s}(E_{\bullet,d-r}^{1})

for all

, given by the edge morphism of the spectral sequence.
Instead of using the simplicial map of Nesterenko–Suslin
[
NS89
]
, we use the cubical map of Totaro
[
Tot92
, §3]
to identify

{\operatorname{CH}}_{d-r}(k(x),s-d+r;\mathbb{Z}/p^{n})\xrightarrow{\simeq}K^{M}_{s-d+r}(k(x))/p^{n},\quad Z\mapsto\operatorname{Nm}_{k(Z)/k(x)}(\{y_{1},\dots,y_{s-d+r}\}).

Hence we have an isomorphism of complexes

which is precisely our

.
This proves the proposition.

\partial^{\operatorname{res}}_{\widetilde{\overline{Z}},\widetilde{\overline{z}}}(\frac{1}{t}d\log\{y_{1},...,y_{q}\})=0
.

c)

Zero symbols.

When
\widetilde{\overline{z}}\in\rho_{\overline{Z}}^{-1}(\overline{{\mathrm{pr}}}^{-1}(w)\cap\big(\bigcup_{i=1}^{q}\{y_{i}=1\}\setminus\{t=0\text{ or }\infty\}\big))
,

\alpha^{\prime}_{w}\circ\partial^{{\mathrm{cyc}}}(Z)
have no summand supported on this point. So the aim is still to show that the summand of
\partial^{\operatorname{res}}_{W,w}\circ\alpha^{\prime}(Z)
supported on point
\widetilde{\overline{z}}
vanishes. This is true because the symbol
\{y_{1},...,y_{q}\}\in K^{M}_{q}(k(\eta_{\widetilde{\overline{Z}}}))
is zero when one of the
y_{i}
is 1. (It’s actually already zero in the tensor product
k(\eta_{\widetilde{\overline{Z}}})^{*}\otimes\cdots\otimes k(\eta_{\widetilde{\overline{Z}}})^{*}
before modding out the Steinberg relations.)

d)

A priori regular locus.

When
\widetilde{\overline{z}}\in\rho_{\overline{Z}}^{-1}(\overline{{\mathrm{pr}}}^{-1}(w)\cap\{t=\infty\})
,
\alpha^{\prime}_{w}\circ\partial^{{\mathrm{cyc}}}(Z)
still have no summand supported on this point. We claim that

\frac{1}{t}d\log\{y_{1},...,y_{q}\}\in\Omega^{q}_{\widetilde{\overline{Z}},\widetilde{\overline{z}}}
. This is because of the property of log poles: the rational form
d\log\{y_{1},...,y_{q}\}
has at most a pole of order 1 at point
\widetilde{\overline{z}}
. And
\frac{1}{t}
has at least a zero of order 1, thus the whole form
\frac{1}{t}d\log\{y_{1},...,y_{q}\}
is regular at point
\widetilde{\overline{z}}
.

To summarize:

(4.0.6)

\displaystyle-\operatorname{Tr}^{-q+1}_{\overline{\{w\}}^{W}\hookrightarrow W}\circ\alpha^{\prime}_{w}

\displaystyle\circ\partial^{{\mathrm{cyc}}}(Z)

\displaystyle=\sum_{\widetilde{\overline{z}}\in(\overline{{\mathrm{pr}}}\circ\rho_{\overline{Z}})^{-1}(w)\cap\widetilde{Z}}(-1)^{i(\widetilde{\overline{z}})}\mathrm{v}_{\widetilde{\overline{z}}}(y_{i(\widetilde{\overline{z}})})\operatorname{Tr}^{-q+1}_{\overline{\{\widetilde{\overline{z}}\}}^{\widetilde{\overline{Z}}}\xrightarrow{\overline{{\mathrm{pr}}}\circ\rho_{\overline{Z}}}W}(\frac{1}{t}d\log\{y_{1},...,\widehat{y_{i(\widetilde{\overline{z}})}},...,y_{q}\})
