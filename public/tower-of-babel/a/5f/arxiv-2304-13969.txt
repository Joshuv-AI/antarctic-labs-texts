Abstract
In this work, the strong coupling constants of the vertices D ​ D ​ J / ψ DDJ/\psi , D ​ D ∗ ​ J / ψ DD^{*}J/\psi , D ∗ ​ D ∗ ​ J / ψ D^{*}D^{*}J/\psi , D ​ D ∗ ​ η c DD^{*}\eta_{c} and D ∗ ​ D ∗ ​ η c D^{*}D^{*}\eta_{c} are calculated within the framework of the QCD sum rules. For each vertex, we analyze the momentum dependence of the coupling constants by considering all possible off-shell cases. In these analyses, we consider the contributions of the vacuum condensate terms ⟨ q ¯ ​ q ⟩ \langle\overline{q}q\rangle , ⟨ q ¯ ​ g s ​ σ ​ G ​ q ⟩ \langle\overline{q}g_{s}\sigma Gq\rangle , ⟨ g s 2 ​ G 2 ⟩ \langle g_{s}^{2}G^{2}\rangle , ⟨ f 3 ​ G 3 ⟩ \langle f^{3}G^{3}\rangle and ⟨ q ¯ ​ q ⟩ ​ ⟨ g s 2 ​ G 2 ⟩ \langle\overline{q}q\rangle\langle g_{s}^{2}G^{2}\rangle . Then, the momentum dependent coupling constants are fitted into analytical functions g ⁡ ( Q 2 ) g(Q^{2}) and are extrapolated into time-like regions. The values of strong coupling constants are obtained by using on-shell conditions of the intermediate mesons( Q 2 = − m 2 Q^{2}=-m^{2} ). The final results are as follows, g D ​ D ​ J / ψ = 5.01 − 0.16 + 0.58 g_{DDJ/\psi}=5.01^{+0.58}_{-0.16} , g D ​ D ∗ ​ J / ψ = 3.55 − 0.21 + 0.20 g_{DD^{*}J/\psi}=3.55^{+0.20}_{-0.21} GeV -1 , g D ∗ ​ D ∗ ​ J / ψ = 5.10 − 0.43 + 0.59 g_{D^{*}D^{*}J/\psi}=5.10^{+0.59}_{-0.43} , g D ​ D ∗ ​ η c = 3.68 − 0.11 + 0.39 g_{DD^{*}\eta_{c}}=3.68^{+0.39}_{-0.11} and g D ∗ ​ D ∗ ​ η c = 4.87 − 0.40 + 0.42 g_{D^{*}D^{*}\eta_{c}}=4.87^{+0.42}_{-0.40} GeV -1 .
