Resonances for vector-valued Jacobi operators on half-lattice
Evgeny Korotyaev

Abstract
. We study resonances for Jacobi operators on the half lattice with matrix valued coefficient and finitely supported perturbations. We describe a forbidden domain, the geometry of resonances and their asymptotics when the main coefficient of the perturbation (determining its length of support) goes to zero. Moreover, we show that 1) Any sequence of points on the complex plane can be resonances for some Jacobi operators. In particular, the multiplicity of a resonance can be any number. 2) The Jost determinant coincides with the Fredholm determinant up to the constant. 3) The S-matrix on the a.c spectrum determines the perturbation uniquely. 5) The value of the Jost matrix at any finite sequence of points on the a.c spectrum determine the Jacobi matrix uniquely. The length of this sequence is equals to the upper point of the support perturbation.

2.1.
Determinants and Traces

Let
|\cdot|
be the operator norm in the space
{\mathbb{C}}^{d}
.
For a set
Y\subset{\mathbb{Z}}
(below
Y={\mathbb{N}},\;{\mathbb{Z}}_{+}={\mathbb{N}}\cup\{0\}
or

{\mathbb{Z}}
) define the spaces
\ell^{2}(Y)
and
\ell^{\infty}(Y)
as the
spaces of sequences
(h_{x})_{x\in Y}
equipped with the norms given
by
\|h\|_{2}^{2}=\sum_{x\in Y}|h_{x}|^{2}\geqslant 0
and
\|h\|_{\infty}=\sup_{x\in Y}|h_{x}|
respectively.

Let us recall some well-known facts from
[
21
]
. Define the
space
{\mathbb{B}}
of bounded operators acting on the Hilbert space
{\mathcal{K}}

equipped with the norm
\|\cdot\|
. Let
{\mathbb{B}}_{2}({\mathcal{K}})
and

{\mathbb{B}}_{1}({\mathcal{K}})
be the Hilbert-Schmidt class and the trace of compact
operators on the Hilbert space
{\mathcal{K}}

equipped with the norms

\|A\|_{{\mathbb{B}}_{2}}^{2}=\mathop{\mathrm{Tr}}\nolimits(A^{*}A)<\infty
and
\|\cdot\|_{{\mathbb{B}}_{1}}

respectively.

\bullet
Let
A,B\in{\mathbb{B}}
and
AB,BA\in{\mathbb{B}}_{1}
. Then

{\rm Tr}\,AB={\rm Tr}\,BA,

(2.1)

\mathop{\mathrm{det}}\nolimits(I+AB)=\mathop{\mathrm{det}}\nolimits(I+BA).

(2.2)

\bullet
Let
A\in{\mathcal{B}}_{1}
. Then

|\mathop{\mathrm{det}}\nolimits(I+A)|\leqslant e^{\|A\|_{{\mathcal{B}}_{1}}}.

(2.3)

\bullet
Let a function
A(\cdot):{\mathscr{D}}\to{\mathbb{B}}_{1}
be analytic in a
domain
{\mathscr{D}}\subset{{\mathbb{C}}}
, and the operator
(I+A(k))^{-1}
is
bounded for any
k\in{\mathscr{D}}
. Then the function
F(k)=\mathop{\mathrm{det}}\nolimits(I+A(k))

is analytic in
{\mathscr{D}}
and satisfies

F^{\prime}(k)=F(k)\mathop{\mathrm{Tr}}\nolimits(I+A(k))^{-1}A^{\prime}(k)\hskip 20.00003pt\forall\ k\in{\mathscr{D}}.

(2.4)

\bullet
Let
A\in{\mathbb{B}}_{1}
. Then the series
\sum_{j=1}^{\infty}\mathop{\mathrm{Tr}}\nolimits{(-A)^{j}\over j}
converges and

\mathop{\mathrm{det}}\nolimits(I+A)=\exp\biggr[-\sum_{j=1}^{\infty}\mathop{\mathrm{Tr}}\nolimits{(-A)^{j}\over j}\biggr],

(2.5)

and, in particular, if
\|A\|_{{\mathbb{B}}_{1}}
is small enough, then

\ln\mathop{\mathrm{det}}\nolimits(I+A)=\mathop{\mathrm{Tr}}\nolimits\Big[A-{A^{2}\over 2}+O(\|A\|_{{\mathbb{B}}_{2}}^{3})\Big].

(2.6)

\bullet
Let
A^{adj}
be the adjugate matrix of a
d\times d
matrix

A
. If
A
is an invertible matrix, then

A^{-1}={1\over\mathop{\mathrm{det}}\nolimits A}A^{adj}.

(2.7)

\bullet
For a
d\times d
matrix
A
the determinants has a
decomposition

\mathop{\mathrm{det}}\nolimits(A-\tau 1\!\!1_{d})=\sum_{0}^{d}\mathfrak{T}_{j}\tau^{d-j},

(2.8)

where
\mathfrak{T}_{0}=1
and the functions
\mathfrak{T}_{j}
are given by (see p.331-333 in
[
66
]
)

\mathfrak{T}_{1}=-T_{1},\ \ \mathfrak{T}_{2}=-{1\over 2}(T_{2}+T_{1}\mathfrak{T}_{1}),\ \ .....,\mathfrak{T}_{j}=-{1\over m}\sum_{0}^{j-1}T_{j-s}\mathfrak{T}_{s},..,\hskip 10.00002ptT_{j}=\mathop{\mathrm{Tr}}\nolimits A^{j},\hskip 10.00002ptj\in{\mathbb{N}}.

(2.9)
