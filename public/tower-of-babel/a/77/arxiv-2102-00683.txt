Non-SUSY Heterotic String Vacua of Gepner Models with Vanishing Cosmological Constant
Koji Aoyama

Abstract
We study a natural generalization of that given in [ 24 ] to heterotic string. Namely, starting from the generic Gepner models for Calabi-Yau 3-folds, we construct the non-SUSY heterotic string vacua with the vanishing cosmological constant at the one loop. We especially focus on the asymmetric orbifolding based on some discrete subgroup of the chiral U ⁡ ( 1 ) U(1) -action which acts on both of the Gepner model and the S ​ O ​ ( 32 ) SO(32) or E 8 × E 8 E_{8}\times E_{8} -sector. We present a classification of the relevant orbifold models leading to the string vacua with the properties mentioned above. In some cases, the desired vacua can be constructed in the manner quite similar to those given in [ 24 ] for the type II string, in which the orbifold groups contain two generators with the discrete torsions. On the other hand, we also have simpler models that are just realized as the asymmetric orbifolds of cyclic groups with only one generator.

3.2
Criterion for the Desired Models

At this stage
let us clarify the ‘criterion’ to search for the heterotic string vacua with
the desired properties.
To this end, we denote the contributions to the torus partition function
from the each twisted sector as ‘
Z_{(\alpha,\beta)}(\tau)
’, (
\alpha,\beta\in\mathbb{Z}_{4K}
).
That is, we define

\displaystyle Z_{(\alpha,\beta)}(\tau)\equiv\mbox{Tr}_{\widehat{\delta}^{\alpha}\mbox{\scriptsize-twisted}}\,\left[\widehat{\delta}^{\beta}\,q^{L_{0}-\frac{\hat{c}}{8}}\overline{q^{\tilde{L}_{0}-\frac{\hat{c}}{8}}}\right],

(3.17)

for the convenience.
By our definition of the orbifold action
\widehat{\delta}
presented above,
the building blocks
Z_{(\alpha,\beta)}(\tau)
covariantly behave under the modular transformations;

\displaystyle Z_{(\alpha,\beta)}\left(-\frac{1}{\tau}\right)=Z_{(\beta,-\alpha)}(\tau),\hskip 28.45274ptZ_{(\alpha,\beta)}(\tau+1)=Z_{(\alpha,\alpha+\beta)}(\tau).

(3.18)

We require the following conditions;

•

For the ‘even sectors’
{}^{\forall}\alpha,\beta\in 2\mathbb{Z}
, each building block
Z_{(\alpha,\beta)}(\tau)
separately vanishes;

\displaystyle Z_{(\alpha,\beta)}(\tau)\equiv 0,

(3.19)

•

The partition function for the untwisted sector does not vanish;

\displaystyle Z_{0}(\tau)\equiv\frac{1}{4K}\sum_{\beta\in\mathbb{Z}_{4K}}\,Z_{(0,\beta)}\not\equiv 0,

(3.20)

•

For all the twisted sectors of
\widehat{\delta}^{\alpha}
with
\alpha\in 2\mathbb{Z}+1
, we require

\displaystyle\sum_{\beta^{\prime}}\,Z_{(\alpha,2\beta^{\prime})}(\tau)\equiv 0.\hskip 28.45274pt\left({}^{\forall}\alpha\in 2\mathbb{Z}+1\right)

(3.21)

Note that,
(
3.21
) just implies

\displaystyle\sum_{\alpha\in 2\mathbb{Z}+1\,\mbox{\scriptsize or}\,\beta\in 2\mathbb{Z}+1}\,Z_{(\alpha,\beta)}(\tau)\equiv 0,

(3.22)

due to the modular covariance (
3.18
).
Thus, combining it together with the requirement (
3.19
), we can conclude that
the total partition function should vanish.

We also note that, in this situation, the bose-fermi cancellation can only occur
among the
different
twisted sectors because of the condition (
3.20
).
On the other hand, the possible space-time supercharges should be of the form such as

\displaystyle{\cal Q}^{\alpha}=\oint d\bar{z}\,{\cal J}_{R}^{\alpha}(\bar{z})
,
which is consistent with the conservation on the world-sheet.
However, any operators of this form cannot induce the expected bose-fermi cancellation, because
the relevant twisted sectors are associated to the
left-moving
operator
\delta_{L}
.
In this way, we conclude that we do not have any space-time supercharges as the operators
consistently acting on the whole Hilbert space and conserved on the world-sheet.
This is the reason why we claim that the heterotic string vacua
that satisfy the above requirements are non-supersymmetric ones.

Some Comments

In this paper, as an extension of our previous work
[
24
]
, we have studied
the construction of non-SUSY heterotic string vacua with the vanishing cosmological constant at one-loop,
based on the asymmetric orbifolding of the Gepner models.
We would like to add a few comments:

•

In the string vacua we constructed, we could not make up the space-time supercharges that
are conserved
on the world-sheet and consistently realizing the bose-fermi cancellation expected from the one-loop partition functions.
We would like to here emphasize that
Z_{\mbox{\scriptsize one-loop}}(\tau)\equiv 0
just implies the bose-fermi cancellation

under the free string limit.

Therefore, even if they might induce some low-energy effective field theories with unbroken SUSY,
the absence of supercharges in the above sense should imply that
they could not be supersymmetric ones
when turning on the string interactions
described by
general world-sheets with higher genera.
It would be thus capable for them to generate small non-vanishing cosmological constants after incorporating the
(perturbative or non-perturbative) stringy quantum corrections,
although such analyses still look very hard to carry out
due to the complexities of spectra arising from various twisted sectors.

•

When being motivated by the cosmological constant problem, it would be more desirable but much more non-trivial situations
that we have
the vanishing one-loop cosmological constant
without
the bose-fermi cancellation at each mass level
(in other words,

Z(\tau)\not\equiv 0
, but

\displaystyle\Lambda\equiv\int\frac{d^{2}\tau}{\tau_{2}^{2}}\,Z(\tau)=0
).
On the other hand,
a characteristic feature of the string vacua given in the present paper (and those given in
[
24
]
) is
that we have the bose-fermi cancellation among the different twisted sectors of the relevant orbifolding,
as was emphasized several times.
We would like to discuss elsewhere the possibility to realize such ‘desirable situations’,
at least, in some
point particle
theories
with infinite mass spectra (not necessarily, string theories), by implementing this feature

[
32
]
.
