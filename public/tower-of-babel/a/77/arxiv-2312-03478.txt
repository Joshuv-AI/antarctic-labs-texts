On the Generalizations of the Cauchy-Schwarz-Bunyakovsky Inequality with Applications to Elasticity
Dimitra Labropoulou, Thanasis Labropoulos, Panayiotis Vafeas, Dimitris M. Manias

Abstract
In this article we present both the discrete and the integral form of Cauchy-Bunyakovsky-Schwarz ( CBS \rm CBS ) inequality, some important generalizations in the n n -dimensional Euclidean space and in linear subspaces of it, as well as the strengthened CBS \rm CBS . The last CBS \rm CBS inequality plays an important role in elasticity problems. A geometrical interpretation and a collection of the most important proofs of it are, also, presented.

Cauchy’s inequality for non-negative real numbers is expressed as

Proposition 1
.

If

are non-negative real numbers, then

\frac{{\alpha_{1}+\alpha_{2}+...+\alpha_{\nu}}}{\nu}\geqslant\sqrt[\nu]{{\alpha_{1}\cdot\alpha_{2}...\cdot\alpha_{\nu}}}~.

\frac{{\alpha_{1}+\alpha_{2}+...+\alpha_{\nu}}}{\nu}\geqslant\sqrt[\nu]{{\alpha_{1}\cdot\alpha_{2}...\cdot\alpha_{\nu}}}\geqslant\frac{\nu}{{\dfrac{1}{{\alpha_{1}}}+\dfrac{1}{{\alpha_{2}}}+...+\dfrac{1}{{\alpha_{\nu}}}}}~.

(2)

Equality is true only when
