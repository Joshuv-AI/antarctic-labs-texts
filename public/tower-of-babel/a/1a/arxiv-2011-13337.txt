Massive kite diagrams with elliptics


Abstract
We present the results for two-loop massive kite master integrals with elliptics in terms of iterated integrals with algebraic kernels. The key ingredients are new integral representations for sunset subgraphs in d = 4 − 2 ​ ε d=4-2\varepsilon and d = 2 − 2 ​ ε d=2-2\varepsilon dimensions together with differential equations for considered kite master integrals in A + B ​ ε A+B\varepsilon form. The obtained results can be easily generalized to all orders in ε \varepsilon -expansion and show that the class of functions defined as iterated integrals with algebraic kernels may be large enough for writing down results for a large class of massive Feynman diagrams.
