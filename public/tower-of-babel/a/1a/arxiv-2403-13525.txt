Abstract
The f f adjacency matrix is a type of edge-weighted adjacency matrix, whose weight of an edge i ​ j ij is f ⁡ ( d i , d j ) f(d_{i},d_{j}) , where f f is a real symmetric function and d i , d j d_{i},d_{j} are the degrees of vertex i i and vertex j j . The f f -spectral radius of a graph is the spectral radius of its f f -adjacency matrix. In this paper, the effect of subdividing an edge on f f -spectral radius is discussed. Some necessary conditions of the extremal graph with given order and size are derived. As an example, we obtain the bicyclic graph(s) with the smallest f f -spectral radius for fixed order n ≥ 8 n\geq 8 by applying generalized Lu-Man method.



Keywords:
Weighted adjacency matrix, Spectral radius, Bicyclic graph, Generalized Lu-Man method

\displaystyle\sum_{iv\in E(G^{\prime})}f(d_{i},d_{v})x_{i}^{\prime},

\displaystyle f_{1}(\tau/\theta)=1-(4\tanh(2\tau)-3\tanh(\tau))\tanh(\theta)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 1-(3-\sqrt{3})\tanh(\theta)>1-(3-\sqrt{3})\tanh(\tau)=2-\sqrt{3}>0.

If
\theta>\frac{2}{3}\tau

\displaystyle f_{1}(x)\geq f_{1}\left(\frac{3}{2}\right)

\displaystyle=1+3\tanh(\theta)\tanh\left(\frac{3}{2}\theta\right)-4\tanh(\theta)\tanh(3\theta)

\displaystyle=\frac{2\left((t-2)^{2}t^{4}+(2t-1)^{2}\right)t^{2}}{(t^{4}-t^{2}+1)(t^{2}-t+1)(t^{2}+1)^{2}}

\displaystyle>0.\qquad(\text{Since }t>1)

So

when

.
∎
