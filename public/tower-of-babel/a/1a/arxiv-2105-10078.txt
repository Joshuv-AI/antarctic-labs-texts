Peiyu Zhang, Ming Chen, Dajun Liu

Abstract
We give the definition of Gorenstein syzygy objects in extriangulated categories and obtain a characterization. For a recollement R ⁡ ( 𝒜 ′ , 𝒜 , 𝒜 ′′ ) R(\mathcal{A^{\prime}},~\mathcal{A},~\mathcal{A^{\prime\prime}}) of extriangulated categories, we mainly show that Gorenstein syzygy objects in 𝒜 ′ \mathcal{A^{\prime}} and 𝒜 ′′ \mathcal{A^{\prime\prime}} induce Gorenstein syzygy objects in 𝒜 \mathcal{A} under certain conditions, and prove that Gorenstein syzygy objects in 𝒜 \mathcal{A} induce Gorenstein syzygy objects in 𝒜 ′ \mathcal{A^{\prime}} and 𝒜 ′′ \mathcal{A^{\prime\prime}} under certain conditions, 2000 Mathematics Subject Classification: 18A40 16E10 18G25 Keywords : Extriangulated categories, Gorenstein syzygy objects, Recollement.
