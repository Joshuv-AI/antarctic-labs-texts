The Fontaine–Ogus realisation of Laumon 1-motives
Nicola Mazzari

Abstract
. We construct the (filtered) Ogus realisation of Laumon 1 1 -motives over a number field. This realisation extends the functor defined on Deligne 1 1 -motives by Andreatta, Barbieri-Viale and Bertapelle.



2010 Mathematics Subject Classification
14F30, 14L15, 14F40

1.
Introduction

Let
K
be a number field and let
\mathbf{FOg}(K)
be the
\mathbb{Q}
-linear abelian category of filtered Ogus structures over
K
defined in
[
1
, § 1.3]
. The main result of
[
1
]
is the existence a realisation functor
T_{\mathbf{FOg}}:\mathcal{M}_{1,\mathbb{Q}}(K)\to\mathbf{FOg}(K)
from the category of Deligne 1-motives over
K
up to isogeny. Moreover this functor is fully faithful.

In the present article we extend the above realisation
T_{\mathbf{FOg}}
to the category
\mathcal{M}_{1,\mathbb{Q}}^{a}(K)
of Laumon 1-motives over
K
up to isogeny.

First we prove that
T_{\mathbf{FOg}}
factors through a finer category
\mathbf{MFOg}(K)
obtained by enriching
\mathbf{FOg}(K)
with the Hodge filtration. We also require the admissibility condition used by Fontaine defining the category
\mathbf{MF}_{K}^{\rm ad}
of admissible filtered
\phi
-modules
[
9
]
. For this reason we call
\mathbf{MFOg}(K)
the category of Fontaine–Ogus structures over
K
. More precisely
T_{\mathbf{FOg}}
factors through the full subcategory
\mathbf{MFOg}_{1}(K)\subset\mathbf{MFOg}(K)
of Fontaine–Ogus modules of level
\leq 1
(see §
3.2
).

Then we define the category
\mathbf{MFOg}^{a}_{(1)}(K)
, which is analogous to the category considered in
[
4
]
, containing
\mathbf{MFOg}_{(1)}(K)
as a full subcategory. Now we can state the main result of this paper.

Theorem 1.1
.

There exists a is fully faithful realisation functor

T^{a}:\mathcal{M}_{1,\mathbb{Q}}^{a}(K)\to\mathbf{MFOg}^{a}_{(1)}(K)

extending
T_{\mathbf{FOg}}
.

Indeed it is possible to extend
T_{\mathbf{FOg}}
to Laumon 1-motives just adding some extra vector spaces to the definition of
\mathbf{FOg}
. The introduction of
\mathbf{MFOg}(K)
and
\mathbf{MFOg}^{a}(K)
is necessary in order to preserve fully faithfulness.

The definition of
\mathbf{MFOg}^{a}_{(1)}(K)
and the full faithfulness depend on results about the category of Laumon 1-motives in §
2.3
: we express the latter category as an iterated fibre product of categories. There is another important ingredient in the definition of
\mathbf{MFOg}^{a}_{(1)}(K)
, which is necessary to make the functor full: it depends on the
section

\gamma_{M}
described in Remark
4.3
.

Further, in Corollary of §
2.3
we find a shorter proof of the fact that the cohomological dimension of Laumon 1-motives is
1
(cf.
[
10
]
).

2.
1-motives

2.1.
Laumon 1-motives

Let
K
be a (fixed) field of characteristic zero (later it will be a number field).
Let
{\sf Ab}_{K}
be the category of sheaves of abelian groups on the category of affine
K
-schemes endowed with the fppf topology. We will consider both the category of commutative
K
-group schemes and that of formal
K
-group schemes as full subcategories of
{\sf Ab}_{K}
.

A
Laumon 1-motive
over
K
(or an effective free 1-motive over
K
, cf.
[
3
, 1.4.1]
) is the data of

(1)

A (commutative) formal group
F
over
K
, such that
\Lie F
is a finite dimensional
K
-vector space and
F(\bar{K})=\lim_{[K^{\prime}:K]<\infty}F(K^{\prime})
is a finitely generated and torsion-free
\Gal(\bar{K}/K)
-module.

(2)

A connected commutative algebraic group scheme
{G}
over
K
.

(3)

A morphism
u:{F}\to{G}
in the category
\sf Ab_{K}
.

Remark 2.1
.

(1)

It is known that any formal
K
-group
{F}
splits canonically as product
{F}^{\circ}\times{F}_{{\rm\acute{e}t}}
where
{F}^{\circ}
is the identity component of
{F}
and is a connected formal
K
-group, and
{F}_{{\rm\acute{e}t}}={F}/{F}^{\circ}
is étale. Moreover,
{F}_{{\rm\acute{e}t}}
admits a maximal sub-group scheme
{F}_{\rm tor}
, étale and finite, such that the quotient
{F}_{{\rm\acute{e}t}}/{F}_{\rm tor}={F}_{\rm fr}
is étale-locally constant of the type
\mathbb{Z}^{r}
. One says that
{F}
is torsion-free if
{F}_{\rm tor}=0
.

(2)

By a theorem of Chevalley any connected algebraic group scheme
{G}
is the extension of an abelian variety
{A}
by a linear
K
-group scheme
{L}
that is product of its maximal sub-torus
{T}
with a vector
K
-group scheme
{V}
. We denote by
G_{\times}=G/V
the semi-abelian quotient of
G
. (See
[
8
]

for more details on algebraic and formal groups)

2.2.
Morphisms

We can consider a Laumon 1-motive (over
K
)
M=[u:{F}\to{G}]
as a complex of sheaves in
{\sf Ab}_{K}
concentrated in degree
0,1
. A
morphism
of Laumon 1-motives is a commutative square in the category
\Ab_{K}
. We denote by
\mathcal{M}^{a}_{1}=\mathcal{M}^{a}_{1}(K)

K

We define the category

The category of Deligne 1-motives over
K
is the full sub-category
\mathcal{M}_{1}(K)
of
\mathcal{M}_{1}^{a}(K)
whose objects are
M=[u:{F}\to{G}]
such that
{F}^{\circ}=0
and
{G}
is semi-abelian (cf.
[
7

). We can also define the
up to isogeny
version

\mathcal{M}^{a}_{1,\mathbb{Q}}(K)

3.2.
Fontaine–Ogus modules

Let now
K
be a number field and
M
be a
1
-motive over
K
. We know that for some

,
M
can be considered as a lisse 1-motive over

[

. Then, for all finite and unramified places

,
T_{\mathrm{dR}}(M_{K_{v}})
can be consider as an object of
\mathbf{MF}^{{\rm ad}}_{K_{v}}
(by the previous section).

This motivates the following definition

Definition 3.2
.

Let
\mathbf{MFOg}(K)
be the category whose objects are systems
(T,\mathrm{Fil}^{\bullet})
such that

•

T=(T_{\mathrm{dR}},(T_{v})_{v})\in\mathbf{FOg}(K)
. We denote by
\phi_{v}
the Frobenius on
T_{v}

•

\mathrm{Fil}^{\bullet}
is a (decreasing, exhaustive) filtration on
T_{\mathrm{dR}}
(called Hodge filtration).

•

for almost all
v
,
(T_{v},\mathrm{Fil}^{\bullet}_{v}=\mathrm{Fil}^{\bullet}\otimes K_{v},\phi_{v})
is an admissible filtered
\phi
-module over
K_{v}
.

Morphisms of
\mathbf{MFOg}(K)
are morphism of
\mathbf{FOg}(K)
compatible with respect to the “Hodge” filtration.

Proposition 3.3
.

The category
\mathbf{MFOg}(K)
is abelian.

Proof.

It is clear how to define kernels and cokernels. We already know that
\mathbf{FOg}
is abelian, hence morphisms are strict with respect to the weight filtration. We have to prove that morphisms are strictly compatible with respect to the Hodge filtration. This follows form the fact that morphisms are strict in
\mathbf{MF}_{K_{v}}^{{\rm ad}}
.
∎

Proposition 3.4
.

The filtered Ogus realisation
T_{\mathbf{FOg}}
factors through
\mathbf{MFOg}
via

T_{\mathbf{MFOg}}:\mathcal{M}_{1}(K)\rightarrow\mathbf{MFOg}(K)\ ,

induced by

T_{\mathbf{MFOg}}(M)=(T_{\mathbf{FOg}}(M),\mathrm{Fil}^{\bullet}T_{\mathrm{dR}}(M))\ .

Moreover
T_{\mathbf{MFOg}}
is fully faithful.

Proof.

The de Rham realisation respects the Hodge filtration. To get the full faithfulness we just need to note that the forgetful functor

\mathbf{MFOg}(K)\rightarrow\mathbf{FOg}(K)\ ,(T,\mathrm{Fil}^{\bullet})\mapsto T\ ,

is faithful.
∎

Remark 3.5
.

In
[
5
]
it is proven that the filtered Ogus realisation

extends to the category of Voevodsky motives. Also the latter functor

can be extended to Voevodsky motives. In fact it is straightforward to add the Hodge filtration and it is possible to prove by devissage the required admissibility condition.
