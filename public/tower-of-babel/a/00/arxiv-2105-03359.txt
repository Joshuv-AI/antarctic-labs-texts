Superidentities for the algebras U ​ T 2 UT_{2} and U ​ T 3 UT_{3} on a finite field
Ronald Ismael Quispe Urure

Abstract
Let F F be a finite field and consider U ​ T n UT_{n} the algebra of n × n n\times n upper triangular matrices over F F . In [ 1 ] , it was proved that every G G -grading is elementary. In [ 2 ] , the authors classified all nonisomorphic elementary G G -gradings. They also described the set of all G G -graded polynomial identities for U ​ T ​ n UTn when F F is an infinite field. In [ 3 ] , was described the all G G -graded polynomial identities for U ​ T n UT_{n} when F F is a finite field. In this work, we will discuss the case when G = ℤ 2 G=\mathbb{Z}_{2} , n = 2 , 3 n=2,3 and F F is a finite field.



Keywords:
Graded identities, Basis of identities, Upper triangular matrices, Finite fields.

Let

[u_{1},u_{2}]=u_{1}u_{2}-u_{2}u_{1}

for

,

.

We say that
\mathcal{A}
is a

\mathcal{A}^{(0)}\mathcal{A}^{(0)}+\mathcal{A}^{(1)}\mathcal{A}^{(1)}\subseteq\mathcal{A}^{(0)}

Let
\mathcal{B}
a subalgebra of

.

\mathcal{B}
is a

of

induced by grading

.

Let

and

The endomorphisms and isomorphisms of superalgebras are defined in the usual way.
