Tongkeun Chang, Bum Ja Jin

Abstract
. In this paper, we study the decay rate of the Stokes flow in an exterior domain with a slowly decaying initial data 𝐮 0 ​ ( x ) = O ⁡ ( | x | − α ) , 0 < α ≤ n {\bf u}_{0}(x)=O(|x|^{-\alpha}),0<\alpha\leq n . As an application we find the unique strong solution of the Navier-Stokes equations corresponding to a slowly decaying initial data. We also derive the pointwise decay estimate of the Navier-Stokes flow. Our decay rates will be optimal compared with the decay rates of the heat flow. 2000 Mathematics Subject Classification: primary 35Q30 , secondary 76D05. Keywords and phrases: Stokes, Navier-Stokes, Pointwise, Slowly decaying, Exterior domain.



§1.
Introduction

Let
\Omega
be an exterior domain in
{\mathbb{R}}^{n}
,
n\geq 3
.
In this paper we are interested in the asypmtotic behaviour of the Stokes and Navier-Stokes flow of a slowly decaying initial data.

First, we consider an initial-boundary value problem for the Stokes equations in
\Omega\times(0,\infty)
:

\displaystyle\left\{\begin{array}[]{l}\vskip 5.69054pt\partial_{t}{\bf u}-\Delta{\bf u}+\nabla p={\rm div}\,{\mathcal{F}}\,\,\mbox{ in }\,\,\Omega\times(0,\infty),\\
\vskip 5.69054pt\mbox{\rm div}\,{\bf u}=0\,\,\mbox{ in }\Omega\times(0,\infty),\\
\vskip 5.69054pt{\bf u}=0\,\,\mbox{ on }\,\,{\partial\Omega}\times(0,\infty),\\
\vskip 5.69054pt\lim_{|x|\rightarrow\infty}{\bf u}(x,t)=0\,\,\mbox{ for }\,\,t>0,\\
{\bf u}(x,0)={\bf u}_{0}(x)\,\,\mbox{ for }\,\,x\in\Omega.\end{array}\right.

Here,
{\bf u}=(u_{1},\cdots,u_{n})
and
p
denote the unknown vector field
and the unknown scalar function. respectively, while
{\bf u}_{0}
is a prescribed initial data.

For the simplicity we assume that
B_{\frac{1}{2}}\subset\Omega^{c}\subset B_{1},
where
B_{r}
is the ball of radius
r
centered at the origin.
We assume that the initial data satisfies the following two conditions:

(1.6)

\displaystyle\mbox{\rm div}\ {\bf u}_{0}=0\mbox{ in }\Omega\mbox{ in distributional sense,}

(1.7)

{\bf u}_{0}\cdot{\bf n}=0\mbox{ on }\partial\Omega\mbox{ in trace sense}.

Here
{\bf n}
is the unit outer normal vector to
\partial\Omega
.
The above conditions are the compatibility conditions so that the zero extension of
{\bf u}_{0}
becomes divergence free in
{\mathbb{R}}^{n}
.

Let
{\mathbb{P}}_{q}
be the continuous projection operator from
L^{q}(\Omega)
to
J_{q}(\Omega)
(:=the completion of
C^{\infty}_{0,\sigma}(\Omega)
in
L^{q}(\Omega)

A_{q}=-{\mathbb{P}}_{q}\Delta
be the Stokes operator
with dense domain
{\mathcal{D}}(A_{q})=\{{\bf u}\in W^{2}_{q}(\Omega):\ \mbox{\rm div}\ {\bf u}=0,\ {\bf u}|_{\partial\Omega}=0\}

A_{q}
generates a bounded analytic semigroup
e^{-tA_{q}}
.
From now on,
A=A_{q}
without confusion.
H. Iwashita
[
24

showed that

\displaystyle\|e^{-tA}f\|_{L^{q}(\Omega)}

\displaystyle\leq ct^{-\frac{n}{2}(\frac{1}{r}-\frac{1}{q})}\|f\|_{L^{r}(\Omega)},\,\,1<r\leq q<\infty,

\displaystyle\|\nabla e^{-tA}f\|_{L^{q}(\Omega)}

\displaystyle\leq ct^{-\frac{1}{2}-\frac{n}{2}(\frac{1}{r}-\frac{1}{q})}\|f\|_{L^{r}(\Omega)},\,\,1<r\leq q\leq n,

for any
f\in J_{q}(\Omega)
and
n\geq 3

[

\displaystyle\|\partial_{t}^{k}e^{-tA}f\|_{L^{q}(\Omega)}

\displaystyle\leq ct^{-k-\frac{n}{2}(\frac{1}{r}-\frac{1}{q})}\|f\|_{L^{r}(\Omega)},\ 1\leq r<q\leq\infty,\mbox{ or }1<r=q\leq\infty,k=0,1,

\displaystyle\|\nabla e^{-tA}f\|_{L^{q}(\Omega)}

\displaystyle\leq\left\{\begin{array}[]{l}ct^{-\frac{1}{2}-\frac{n}{2}(\frac{1}{r}-\frac{1}{q})}\|f\|_{L^{r}(\Omega)},\,\,1<r\leq q\leq n\\
ct^{-\frac{n}{2r}}\|f\|_{L^{r}(\Omega)},\ t\geq 1,\,\,n<q<\infty\end{array}\right.

for any

In this paper we consider a slowly decaying initial data with

The following is the precise statement of our first result.

Theorem 1.1
.

Let
\Omega\subset{\mathbb{R}}^{n}
,
n\geq 3
be an exterior domain of smooth boundary with
B_{\frac{1}{2}}\subseteq\Omega^{c}\subseteq B_{1}

1.6

\displaystyle\sup_{x\in\Omega}|x|^{\alpha}|{\bf u}_{0}(x)|:=M_{0}<\infty.

Then it holds that

\displaystyle\|e^{-tA}{\bf u}_{0}\|_{L^{q}(\Omega)}\leq c_{\alpha,q}M_{0}\left\{\begin{array}[]{l}\vskip 5.69054pt(1+t)^{-\frac{\alpha}{2}+\frac{n}{2q}},\quad 0<\alpha<n,\\
(1+t)^{-\frac{n}{2}+\frac{n}{2q}}\ln(2+t),\quad\alpha=n.\end{array}\right.

Moreover, it holds that

(1.11)

\displaystyle\begin{split}|e^{-tA}{\bf u}_{0}(x)|&\leq\left\{\begin{array}[]{l}\vskip 5.69054ptc_{\alpha}M_{0}(1+|x|+\sqrt{t})^{-\alpha}\quad\mbox{ if }\quad 0<\alpha\leq n-1\\
c_{\alpha,\delta}M_{0}(1+|x|+\sqrt{t})^{-\alpha+\delta}\,\,\mbox{ for any small }\delta>0,\,\mbox{ if }\,\,n-1<\alpha\leq n.\end{array}\right.\end{split}

Proof.

According to Theorem 6.6.1 in

\displaystyle\|T({\bf\varphi},\pi)\|_{L^{r}(\partial\Omega)}

\displaystyle\leq c\|T({\bf\varphi},\pi)\|_{W^{\frac{1}{r}}_{r,1}(\Omega_{R})}\leq c\|T({\bf\varphi},\pi)\|_{W^{1,r}(\Omega_{R})}

\displaystyle\leq c\big(\|\nabla^{2}{\bf\varphi}\|_{L^{r}(\Omega)}+\|\nabla\pi\|_{L^{r}(\Omega_{R})}+\|\nabla{\bf\varphi}\|_{L^{r}(\Omega_{R})}+\|\pi\|_{L^{r}(\Omega_{R})}\big).

Since
