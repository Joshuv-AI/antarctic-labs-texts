Competition-common enemy graphs of degree-bounded digraphs
Myungho Choi, Hojin Chu, Suh-Ryung Kim

Abstract
The competition-common enemy graph (CCE graph) of a digraph D D is the graph with the vertex set V ⁡ ( D ) V(D) and an edge u ​ v uv if and only if u u and v v have a common predator and a common prey in D D . If each vertex of a digraph D D has indegree at most i i and outdegree at most j j , then D D is called an ⟨ i , j ⟩ \langle i,j\rangle digraph. In this paper, we fully characterize the CCE graphs of ⟨ 2 , 2 ⟩ \langle 2,2\rangle digraphs. Then we investigate the CCE graphs of acyclic ⟨ 2 , 2 ⟩ \langle 2,2\rangle digraphs, and prove that any CCE graph of an acyclic ⟨ 2 , 2 ⟩ \langle 2,2\rangle digraph with at most seven components is interval, and the bound is sharp. While characterizing acyclic ⟨ 2 , 2 ⟩ \langle 2,2\rangle digraphs that have interval graphs as their competition graphs, Hefner et al . (1991) initiated the study of competition graphs of degree-bounded digraphs. Recently, Lee et al . (2017) and Eoh and Kim (2021) studied phylogeny graphs of degree-bounded digraphs to extend their work.

Lemma 4.6
.

Let
G
be the
CCE
graph of a
(2,2)
digraph
D
. Suppose that
G
has a cycle
C
of length
m
for some

.
Then
|N^{+}(C)\cup N^{-}(C)|\geq m+3

and
|N^{+}(C)\cap N^{-}(C)|\leq m-3
.

Proof.

Let
C:=C_{v,m}
.
By Proposition
2.6
(i) and Proposition
4.5
,

with

Suppose, to the contrary, that

|N^{+}(C)\cup N^{-}(C)|\leq m+2
.
Then, as we have shown
|N^{+}(C)|=|N^{-}(C)|=m
,

|N^{+}(C)\cap N^{-}(C)|\geq m-2

and so

|N^{+}(C)-N^{-}(C)|\leq 2.

(7)

.

x_{1}=v^{-}_{j,j+1}
for some

.

u_{i}=v^{+}_{i,i+1}
for each
1\leq i\leq m
(
u_{t+m}=u_{t}
for any positive integer

).

\{u_{j-1},u_{j},u_{j+1}\}\subseteq N^{+}(C)
,
at least one of

).
Let
x_{2}
be one of such vertices.
Then,
since
v_{j}\to u_{j-1}
,
v_{j}\to u_{j}
, and

By similar argument,

Theorem 4.7
.

Let
\mathcal{G}_{\ell}
be the set of graphs having the minimum number of components among

CCE graphs containing a cycle of length

.
