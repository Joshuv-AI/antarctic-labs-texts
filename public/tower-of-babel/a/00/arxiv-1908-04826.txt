A Gevrey class semigroup, exponential decay and Lack of analyticity for a system formed by a Kirchhoff-Love plate equation and the equation of a membrane-like electric network with indirect fractional damping
Fredy Maglorio Sobrado Suárez

Abstract
The emphasis in this paper is on the Coupled System of a Kirchhoff-Love Plate Equation with the Equation of a Membrane-like Electrical Network, where the coupling is of higher order given by the Laplacian of the displacement velocity γ ​ Δ ​ u t \gamma\Delta u_{t} and the Laplacian of the electric potential field γ ​ Δ ​ v t \gamma\Delta v_{t} , here only one of the equations is conservative and the other has dissipative properties. The dissipative mechanism is given by an intermediate damping ( − Δ ) θ ​ v t (-\Delta)^{\theta}v_{t} between the electrical damping potential for θ = 0 \theta=0 and the Laplacian of the electric potential for θ = 1 \theta=1 . We show that S ⁡ ( t ) = e 𝔹 ​ t S(t)=e^{\mathbb{B}t} is not analytic for θ ∈ [ 0 , 1 ) \theta\in[0,1) and analytic for θ = 1 \theta=1 , however S ⁡ ( t ) = e 𝔹 ​ t S(t)=e^{\mathbb{B}t} decays exponentially for 0 ≤ θ ≤ 1 0\leq\theta\leq 1 and S ⁡ ( t ) S(t) is of Gevrey class s > 2 + θ θ s>\frac{2+\theta}{\theta} when the parameter θ \theta lies in the interval ( 0 , 1 ) (0,1) .
