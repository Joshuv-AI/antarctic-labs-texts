Two-Dimensional Z Z -Complementary Array Quads with Low Column Sequence PMEPRs
Shibsankar Das ∗ , Adrish Banerjee ∗ and Udaya Parampalli †

Abstract
In this paper, we first propose a new design strategy of 2D Z Z -complementary array quads (2D-ZCAQs) with feasible array sizes. A 2D-ZCAQ consists of four distinct unimodular arrays satisfying zero 2D auto-correlation sums for non-trivial 2D time-shifts within certain zone. Then, we obtain the upper bounds on the column sequence peak-to-mean envelope power ratio (PMEPR) of the constructed 2D-ZCAQs by using specific auto-correlation properties of some seed sequences. The constructed 2D-ZCAQs with bounded column sequence PMEPR can be used as a potential alternative to 2D Golay complementary array sets for practical applications.

Given two complex-valued arrays

When

, two 2D arrays

\textit{{x}}=(x_{j})_{j=0}^{N_{2}-1}

\textit{{y}}=(y_{j})_{j=0}^{N_{2}-1}

\rho_{\textbf{{x}},\textbf{{y}}}(\tau)=\sum_{j=0}^{N_{2}-1-\tau}x_{j}y_{j+\tau}^{*}.

(2)

The 1D-AACF of

is simply denoted by

.

A pair

\displaystyle\rho_{\textbf{{x}}}(\tau)+\rho_{\textbf{{y}}}(\tau)=\begin{cases}2N,\ \text{if}\ \tau=0\\
0,\ \quad\text{if}\ 0<|\tau|\leq N-1.\end{cases}

(3)

Note that binary GCPs exist only for lengths

with
\alpha,\beta,\gamma\geq 0

\alpha+\gamma\geq 1-\beta
. However, complex GCPs allow more flexible lengths of the form

with

and
(b+d)+(c+e)\leq a+1+2u

[
27
]
.

Definition
2
(

A pair

-complementary pair (ZCP)

if

\displaystyle\rho_{\textbf{{x}}}(\tau)+\rho_{\textbf{{y}}}(\tau)=\begin{cases}2N;\ \tau=0\\
0;\ \quad\ 0<|\tau|\leq Z-1,\end{cases}

(4)

where
Z\leq N
. In this case,
Z

Note that a ZCP becomes conventional GCP if

II-B

Column Sequence PMEPR in MC-CDMA Systems

For a given
q
-PSK modulated array
\textit{{X}}=\left[\xi_{q}^{\hat{x}_{i,j}}\right]_{i=0,j=0}^{N_{1}-1,N_{2}-1}
, let
\textit{{X}}^{T}_{j}=\left[X_{0,j},X_{1,j},\cdots,X_{N_{1}-1,j}\right]^{T}
be the
j
-th column sequence of
X
, where
0\leq j\leq N_{2}-1
. Then, the time-domain complex baseband OFDM signal can be written by

\displaystyle S_{\textbf{{X}}_{j}^{T}}(t)

\displaystyle=\sum_{i=0}^{N_{1}-1}\xi_{q}^{\hat{x}_{i,j}}e^{2\pi\sqrt{-1}(f_{c}+i\Delta f)t},\ 0\leq t\leq\frac{1}{\Delta f},

(6)

where
f_{c}
denotes the carrier frequency,
\Delta f
refers to the subcarrier spacing, and
N_{1}
denotes the number of subcarriers in OFDM system. The instantaneous envelope power ratio (IEPR) of the column sequence
\textbf{{X}}_{j}^{T}
is given by
P_{\textbf{{X}}_{j}^{T}}(t)=\frac{\left|S_{\textbf{{X}}_{j}^{T}}(t)\right|^{2}}{N_{1}}
. Note that
N_{1}
is the mean envelope power for
q
-PSK modulated sequences. Then, the peak-to-mean envelope power ratio (PMEPR)
[
2
]
,
[
3
]
of the column sequence
\textbf{{X}}_{j}^{T}
can be defined by

\displaystyle\text{PMEPR}\left(\textbf{{X}}_{j}^{T}\right)=\text{sup}_{0\leq t\leq\frac{1}{\Delta f}}P_{\textbf{{X}}_{j}^{T}}(t).

(7)

In MC-CDMA system, the column sequence PMEPR value of
\textbf{{X}}_{j}^{T}
should be as low as possible. In this case, the column sequence
\textbf{{X}}_{j}^{T}
is spreaded over the
N_{1}
subcarriers in the
j
-th chip-slot
[
38
]
. That is,
X_{i,j}
is the modulated signal in the
i
-th subcarrier for
i=0,1,\cdots,N_{1}-1
.

V
Conclusion

In this paper, we have investigated a new construction for 2D-ZCAQs with various array sizes
L\times N
, where
N=2^{\alpha}10^{\beta}26^{\gamma}
or
2^{a+u}3^{b}5^{c}11^{d}13^{e}
and
L
is any positive integer
\geq 2
. The admissible lengths of 1D-GCPs and 1D-ZCPs allow us to offer more feasible array sizes of 2D-ZCAQs. Furthermore, we have shown that the column sequence PMEPR of the proposed 2D-ZCAQ is upper bounded by
4
when we use seed 1D-ZCPs from
[
9
]
,
[
14
]
and
[
13
, Th. 1]
. The proposed 2D-ZCAQs with low column sequence PMEPRs can be utilized as alternatives to 2D-GCASs in UWB MC-CDMA systems.
