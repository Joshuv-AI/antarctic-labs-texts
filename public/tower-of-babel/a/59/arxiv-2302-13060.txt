New bounds on the cardinality of n n -Hausdorff and n n -Urysohn spaces
Maddalena Bonanzinga, Nathan Carlson, Davide Giacopello

Abstract
Two new cardinal functions defined in the class of n n -Hausdorff and n n -Urysohn spaces that extend pseudocharacter and closed pseudocharacter respectively are introduced. Through these new functions bounds on the cardinality of n n -Urysohn spaces that represent variations of known results are given. Also properties of n n -Urysohn n n -H-closed spaces are proved.



Keywords:

n
-Hausdorff spaces,
n
-Urysohn spaces,
n
-H-closed space, pseudocharacter.

Proof.
