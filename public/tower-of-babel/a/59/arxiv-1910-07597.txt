Abstract
In this paper we will show that one can summarize the major two particle reaction plane azimuthal correlations for Au + Au mid-central collisions at s N ​ N = \sqrt{s_{NN}}= 200 GeV by defining a 2D azimuthal space which is a summary of the event by event average.
