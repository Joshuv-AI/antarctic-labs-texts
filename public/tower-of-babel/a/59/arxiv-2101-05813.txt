Remarks on nuclear matter: how an ω 0 \omega_{0} condensate can spike the speed of sound, and a model of Z ⁡ ( 3 ) Z(3) baryons
Robert D. Pisarski

Abstract
I make two comments about nuclear matter. First, I consider the effects of a coupling between the O ⁡ ( 4 ) O(4) chiral field, ϕ → \vec{\phi} , and the ω μ \omega_{\mu} meson, ∼ + ω μ 2 ​ ϕ → 2 \sim+\,\omega_{\mu}^{2}\,\vec{\phi}^{\,2} ; for any net baryon density, a condensate for ω 0 \omega_{0} is unavoidably generated. I assume that with increasing density, a decrease of the chiral condensate and the effective ω 0 \omega_{0} mass gives a stiff equation of state (EoS). In order to match that onto a soft EoS for quarkyonic matter, I consider an O ⁡ ( N ) O(N) field at large N N , where at nonzero temperature quantum fluctuations disorder any putative pion “condensate” into a quantum pion liquid (Q π \pi L) Pisarski et al. 2020 . In this paper I show that the Q π \pi L persists at zero temperature. If valid qualitatively at N = 4 N=4 , the ω 0 \omega_{0} mass goes up sharply and suppresses the ω 0 \omega_{0} condensate. This could generate a spike in the speed of sound at high density, which is of relevance to neutron stars. Second, I propose a toy model of a Z ⁡ ( 3 ) Z(3) gauge theory with three flavors of fermions, where Z ⁡ ( 3 ) Z(3) vortices confine fermions into baryons. In 1 + 1 1+1 dimensions this model can be studied numerically with present techniques, using either classical or quantum computers.
