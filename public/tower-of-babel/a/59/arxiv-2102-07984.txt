Hunting for direct CP violation in B ¯ s 0 → π + ​ π − ​ K ∗ 0 \bar{B}_{s}^{0}\to{\pi^{+}}{\pi^{-}}K^{*0}
Sheng-Tao Li 1 , Gang Lü 2

Abstract
In perturbative QCD approach, based on the first order of isospin symmetry breaking, we study the direct C ​ P CP violation in the decay of B ¯ s 0 → ρ ⁡ ( ω ) ​ K ∗ 0 → π + ​ π − ​ K ∗ 0 \bar{B}_{s}^{0}\to\rho(\omega)K^{*0}\to{\pi^{+}}{\pi^{-}}K^{*0} . An interesting mechanism is applied to enlarge the C ​ P CP violating asymmetry involving the charge symmetry breaking between ρ \rho and ω \omega . We find that the C ​ P CP violation is large by the ρ − ω \rho-\omega mixing mechanism when the invariant masses of the π + ​ π − \pi^{+}\pi^{-} pairs is in the vicinity of the ω \omega resonance. For the decay process of B ¯ s 0 → ρ ⁡ ( ω ) ​ K ∗ 0 → π + ​ π − ​ K ∗ 0 \bar{B}^{0}_{s}\to\rho(\omega)K^{*0}\to{\pi^{+}}{\pi^{-}}K^{*0} , the maximum C ​ P CP violation can reach − 59.12 % -59.12\% . Furthermore, taking ρ − ω \rho-\omega mixing into account, we calculate the branching ratio for B ¯ s 0 → ρ ⁡ ( ω ) ​ K ∗ 0 \bar{B}_{s}^{0}\rightarrow\rho(\omega)K^{*0} . We also discuss the possibility of observing the predicted C ​ P CP violation asymmetry at the LHC.

Charge-Parity (

;

Carter:1980hr
;
Carter:1980tk
;

Aaij:2013bla
;
Aaij:2014iva
;

B

\phi

B

;

Bauer:2000yr
;
Bauer:2001cu

Most factorization approaches are based on heavy quark expansion and light-cone expansion in which only the leading power or part of the next to leading power contributions are calculated to compare with the experiments.
