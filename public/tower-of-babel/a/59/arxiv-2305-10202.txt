A characterization result of cofinite local cohomology modules
Moharram Aghapournahr, Leif Melkersson

Abstract
. Let R R be a commutative Noetherian ring, 𝔞 \mathfrak{a} an ideal of R R , M M an arbitrary R R -module and N N a finite R R -module. We prove that [ 23 , Theorem 2.1] and [ 2 , Proposition 3.3 (i) ⇔ \Leftrightarrow (ii)] are true for any Serre subcategory of R R -modules. We also prove a characterization theorem for H 𝔞 i ⁡ ( M ) \lc_{\mathfrak{a}}^{i}(M) and H 𝔞 i ⁡ ( N , M ) \lc_{\mathfrak{a}}^{i}(N,M) to be 𝔞 \mathfrak{a} -cofinite for all i i , whenever one of the following cases holds: (a) ara ⁡ ( 𝔞 ) ≤ 1 \ara(\mathfrak{a})\leq 1 , (b) dim R / 𝔞 ≤ 1 \dim R/\mathfrak{a}\leq 1 or (c) dim R ≤ 2 \dim R\leq 2 . Remark 2.1

Observe that if

This follows by induction on

Let

For the first assertion we use the map

{0\underset{M}{:}\mathfrak{a}^{l+1}}\to{(0\underset{M}{:}\mathfrak{a}^{l})_{1}^{s}}

For the second assertion use the map

({\overline{u}}_{i})_{1}^{s}\mapsto{\sum_{1}^{s}\overline{x_{i}u_{i}}}

Theorem 3.6
.

Let
M
be an
R
-module and suppose one of the following cases holds:

(a)

\ara(\mathfrak{a})\leq 1
;

(b)

\dim R/\mathfrak{a}\leq 1
;

(c)

\dim R\leq 2
.

Then, for any finite
R
-module
N
,
\lc^{i}_{\mathfrak{a}}(N,M)
is
\mathfrak{a}
-cofinite for all
i\geq 0
if and only if
\Ext^{i}_{R}(R/\mathfrak{a},M)
is finite
R
-module for all
i\geq 0
.

Proof.

First suppose for any finite
R
-module
N
,
\lc^{i}_{\mathfrak{a}}(N,M)
is
\mathfrak{a}
-cofinite for all
i\geq 0
. Let
N=R
, then it follows by Theorem
3.4
, that
\Ext^{i}_{R}(R/\mathfrak{a},M)
is finite
R
-module for all
i\geq 0
.

To prove the converse we use induction on
i
. Let
i=0
, then it follows by Lemma
3.5
(a) that

\lc^{0}_{\mathfrak{a}}(N,M)=\Gamma_{\mathfrak{a}}(N,M)\cong\Hom_{R}(N,\Gamma_{\mathfrak{a}}(M)).

Since
\Gamma_{\mathfrak{a}}(M)
is
\mathfrak{a}
-cofinite by Theorem
3.4
,
so the assertion follows by
[
4
, Theorem 4.1]
,
[
22
, Corollary 2.7]
and
[
23
, Theorem 7.4]
, respectively in the cases (a), (b) and (c). Now assume that
i>0
and that the claim holds for
i-1
. Since
\G_{\mathfrak{a}}(M)
is
\mathfrak{a}
-cofinite and
\Ext^{i}_{R}(R/\mathfrak{a},M)
is finite
R
-module for all
i\geq 0
. Using the short exact sequence

0\longrightarrow\G_{\mathfrak{a}}(M)\longrightarrow M\longrightarrow M/\G_{\mathfrak{a}}(M)\longrightarrow 0,

it is easy to see that the
R
-modules
{\Ext}^{i}_{R}(R/{\mathfrak{a}},M/\G_{\mathfrak{a}}(M))
are finite for all
i\geq 0
. Now, by applying the derived functor
\Gamma_{\mathfrak{a}}(N,-)
to the same short exact sequence and using Lemma
3.5
(b), we obtain the long exact sequence

\dots\longrightarrow\Ext_{R}^{i}(N,\G_{\mathfrak{a}}(M))\overset{f_{i}}{\longrightarrow}\lc^{i}_{\mathfrak{a}}(N,M)\overset{g_{i}}{\longrightarrow}\lc^{i}_{\mathfrak{a}}(N,M/\G_{\mathfrak{a}}(M))\overset{h_{i}}{\longrightarrow}\Ext_{R}^{i}(N,\G_{\mathfrak{a}}(M))\overset{f_{i+1}}{\longrightarrow}\lc^{i+1}_{\mathfrak{a}}(N,M)\longrightarrow\dots

which yields short exact sequences

0\longrightarrow\Ker{f_{i}}\longrightarrow\Ext_{R}^{i}(N,\G_{\mathfrak{a}}(M))\longrightarrow\Image{f_{i}}\longrightarrow 0,

0\longrightarrow\Image{f_{i}}\longrightarrow\lc^{i}_{\mathfrak{a}}(N,M)\longrightarrow\Image{g_{i}}\longrightarrow 0

and

0\longrightarrow\Image{g_{i}}\longrightarrow\lc^{i}_{\mathfrak{a}}(N,M/\G_{\mathfrak{a}}(M))\longrightarrow\Ker{f_{i+1}}\longrightarrow 0.

Since
\Ext_{R}^{i}(N,\G_{\mathfrak{a}}(M))
is
\mathfrak{a}
-cofinite
R
-module for all

by
[
4
, Theorem 4.1]
,
[
22

and
[
23
, Theorem 7.4]

\lc^{i}_{\mathfrak{a}}(N,M)

\mathfrak{a}
-cofinite
R
-module if and only if
\lc^{i}_{\mathfrak{a}}(N,M/\G_{\mathfrak{a}}(M))
is
\mathfrak{a}
-cofinite
R
-module for all
i\geq 0
. Therefore it suffices to show that
\lc^{i}_{\mathfrak{a}}(N,M/\G_{\mathfrak{a}}(M))
is
\mathfrak{a}
-cofinite
R
-module for all
i\geq 0
. To this end consider the exact sequence

0\longrightarrow M/\G_{\mathfrak{a}}(M)\longrightarrow E\longrightarrow L\longrightarrow 0,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,(\dagger)

in which
E
is an injective
\mathfrak{a}
-torsion free module. Since

\G_{\mathfrak{a}}(M/\G_{\mathfrak{a}}(M))=0=\G_{\mathfrak{a}}(E)
, thus
\Hom_{R}(R/\mathfrak{a},E)=0
and
\G_{\mathfrak{a}}(N,M/\G_{\mathfrak{a}}(M))=0=\G_{\mathfrak{a}}(N,E)
by Lemma
3.5
(a). Applying the derived functors of
\Hom_{R}(R/\mathfrak{a},-)
and
\Gamma_{\mathfrak{a}}(N,-)
to the short exact sequence
(\dagger)

we obtain, for all
i>0
, the isomorphisms

\lc^{i-1}_{\mathfrak{a}}(N,L)\cong\lc^{i}_{\mathfrak{a}}(N,M/\G_{\mathfrak{a}}(M))
.

and

{\Ext}^{i-1}_{R}(R/{\mathfrak{a}},L)\cong{\Ext}^{i}_{R}(R/{\mathfrak{a}},M/\G_{\mathfrak{a}}(M))

From what has already been proved, we conclude that
{\Ext}^{i-1}_{R}(R/{\mathfrak{a}},L)

\lc^{i-1}_{\mathfrak{a}}(N,L)

\lc^{i}_{\mathfrak{a}}(N,M/\G_{\mathfrak{a}}(M))

4.
Artinianness of local cohomology modules

In general it is not true that when
M
is a finite
R
-module over the Noetherian ring
R
, such that the local cohomology modules
\lc^{i}_{\mathfrak{a}}(M)
have support in
\Max R
for all
i>r
, then they are Artinian
R
-module for all
i>r
. For this see the example by Hartshorne in
[
13
, page 151]
. However we prove that this is true in the case when
\dim R/{\mathfrak{a}}=1
. To do this we need the following lemma.

Lemma 4.1
.

Let
\mathfrak{a}
be an ideals of a Noetherian ring
R
, such that
\dim R/{\mathfrak{a}}=1
. Let
M
be a finite
R
-module and
r
an integer. Then
\dim M_{\mathfrak{p}}\leq r
for each prime ideal
\mathfrak{p}
minimal over
\mathfrak{a}
if and only if
\Supp_{R}(\lc^{i}_{\mathfrak{a}}(M))\subseteq\Max(R)
for all
i>r
.

Proof.

This is the direct consequence of Grothendieck’s vanishing theorem and the isomorphism

\lc^{i}_{\mathfrak{a}}(M)_{\mathfrak{p}}\cong\lc^{i}_{{\mathfrak{a}}R_{\mathfrak{p}}}(M_{\mathfrak{p}})
for each prime ideal
\mathfrak{p}
minimal over
\mathfrak{a}
. ∎

Theorem 4.2
.

Let
\mathfrak{a}
be an ideals of a Noetherian ring
R
, with
\dim R/{\mathfrak{a}}=1
. For an
R
-module
M
and integer an
r
the following conditions are equivalent;

(i)

\Supp_{R}(\lc^{i}_{\mathfrak{a}}(M))\subseteq\Max(R)
for each
i>r
.

(ii)

\lc^{i}_{\mathfrak{a}}(M)
is an Artinian
R
-module for each
i>r
.

(iii)

\lc^{i}_{\mathfrak{a}}(M)
is an Artinian
\mathfrak{a}
-cofinte
R
-module for each
i>r
.

Proof.

The implications (iii)
\Rightarrow
(ii)
\Rightarrow
(i) always hold, so let us assume (i) and prove that (iii) hold. We do induction on
r
. First we note that if
N
is a finite
R
-module, such that
\Supp_{R}(\lc^{i}_{\mathfrak{a}}(N))\subseteq\Max(R)
, then
\lc^{i}_{\mathfrak{a}}(N)
is
\mathfrak{a}
-cofinite Artinian for all
i
. Let
r=0
and put
N=M/\G_{\mathfrak{a}}(M)
. Then
\lc^{0}_{\mathfrak{a}}(N)=0
and
\lc^{i}_{\mathfrak{a}}(N)\cong\lc^{i}_{\mathfrak{a}}(M)
for all
i>0
. Hence by the above remark
\lc^{i}_{\mathfrak{a}}(N)
is
\mathfrak{a}
-cofinite Artinian for all
i
. It follows that
\lc^{i}_{\mathfrak{a}}(M)
is Artinian and
\mathfrak{a}
-cofinite for all
i>0
.

Next let
r>0
and put
N=M/\G_{\mathfrak{a}}(M)
. Then
\lc^{0}_{\mathfrak{a}}(N)=0
and we can take
x\in\mathfrak{a}
such that
x
is
N
-regular.

Then by Lemma
4.1
,
\dim(N/xN)_{\mathfrak{p}}\leq r-1
for each prime ideal
\mathfrak{p}
minimal over
\mathfrak{a}
. Applying the induction hypothesis to
N/xN
, we have that
\lc^{i}_{\mathfrak{a}}(N/xN)
is
\mathfrak{a}
-cofinite Artinian for all
i>r-1
. Next applying local cohomology functor to the exact sequence
0\longrightarrow N\longrightarrow N\longrightarrow N/xN\longrightarrow 0,
we can conclude that
(0:_{\lc^{i}_{\mathfrak{a}}(N)}x)
is
\mathfrak{a}
-cofinite Artinian for all
i>r
. It follows that
\lc^{i}_{\mathfrak{a}}(N)
is Artinian and
\mathfrak{a}
-cofinite for all
i>r
. Therefore also
\lc^{i}_{\mathfrak{a}}(M)
, for all
i>r
.

∎
