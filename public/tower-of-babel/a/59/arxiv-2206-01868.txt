Existence and boundary behaviour of radial solutions for weighted elliptic systems with gradient terms
Daniel Devine Gurpreet Singh

Abstract
We are concerned with the existence and boundary behaviour of positive radial solutions for the system { Δ ​ u = | x | a ​ v p in ​ Ω , Δ ​ v = | x | b ​ v q ​ f ​ ( | ∇ u | ) in ​ Ω , \left\{\begin{aligned} \Delta u&=|x|^{a}v^{p}&&\quad\mbox{ in }\Omega,\\ \Delta v&=|x|^{b}v^{q}f(|\nabla u|)&&\quad\mbox{ in }\Omega,\end{aligned}\right. where Ω ⊂ ℝ N \Omega\subset\mathbb{R}^{N} is either a ball centered at the origin or the whole space ℝ N \mathbb{R}^{N} , a a , b b , p p , q > 0 q>0 , and f ∈ C 1 [ 0 , ∞ ) f\in C^{1}[0,\infty) is an increasing function such that f ⁡ ( t ) > 0 f(t)>0 for all t > 0 t>0 . Lemma 1

Let
(u,v)
be a positive radial solution of (

\lim_{|x|\nearrow R}v(x)=\infty

\int_{1}^{\infty}\frac{ds}{\left(\int_{0}^{s}\sqrt{f(t)}dt\right)^{\frac{2p}{2p-q+1}}}<\infty.

Proof.

First, assume that
\lim_{r\to R}v(r)=\infty
. System (

\left\{\begin{aligned} &(u^{\prime}(r)r^{N-1})^{\prime}=r^{N+a-1}v^{p}(r),\\
&(v^{\prime}(r)r^{N-1})^{\prime}=r^{N+b-1}v^{q}(r)f(|\nabla u|),\\
&u^{\prime}(0)=v^{\prime}(0)=0.\end{aligned}\right.

An integration over
(0,r)
,
0<r<R
, then gives us

\left\{\begin{aligned} u^{\prime}(r)&=r^{1-N}\int_{0}^{t}t^{N+a-1}v^{p}(t)dt,\\
v^{\prime}(r)&=r^{1-N}\int_{0}^{t}t^{N+b-1}v^{q}(t)f(|\nabla u|)dt,\end{aligned}\right.

from which it follows that
u
and
v
are increasing in
(0,R)
. We can thus take the first integral equation above and estimate

as

u^{\prime}(r)\leq r^{1-N}v^{p}(r)\int_{0}^{r}t^{N+a-1}dt=\frac{r^{a+1}v^{p}(r)}{N+a}

so from (
1
) we have that

r^{a}v^{p}(r)\leq u^{\prime\prime}(r)+\frac{N-1}{r}\cdot\frac{r^{a+1}v^{p}(r)}{N+a},

which further implies,

\frac{1+a}{N+a}r^{a}v^{p}(r)\leq u^{\prime\prime}(r).

Similarly we have

v^{\prime}(r)\leq\frac{r^{b}v^{q}(r)f(u^{\prime}(r))}{N+b},

from which it follows

\frac{1+b}{N+b}r^{b}v^{q}(r)f(u^{\prime}(r))\leq v^{\prime\prime}(r).

We thus have the following two estimates for all
0<r<R
, which will be important as we proceed:

\frac{1+a}{N+a}r^{a}v^{p}(r)\leq u^{\prime\prime}\leq r^{a}v^{p}(r),

(17)

\frac{1+b}{N+b}r^{b}v^{q}(r)f(u^{\prime}(r))\leq v^{\prime\prime}\leq r^{b}v^{q}(r)f(u^{\prime}(r)).

(18)

Now, where

\displaystyle\frac{v^{\prime}(r)^{2}}{2}

\displaystyle v^{p+\frac{1-q}{2}}(r)-v^{p+\frac{1-q}{2}}(r_{0})
