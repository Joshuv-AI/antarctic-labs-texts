Similarly, we investigate the abnormal decay process depicted in Fig.
1
(c) through the three-point correlation function

T^{c}_{\mu\nu}(p,k,q)=\int d^{4}xe^{-ikx}\langle 0|{\mathbb{T}}J_{\mu}^{\eta_{1}}(0)J_{\nu}^{\eta^{\prime}\dagger}(x)|\eta(q)\rangle\,.

(28)

In this case the
\eta
meson is produced by the QCD axial anomaly through Eq. (
2
), and the coupling constant
g^{c}_{\eta\eta^{\prime}}
is defined through the Lagrangian

\mathcal{L}^{c}_{\eta\eta^{\prime}}=ig^{c}_{\eta\eta^{\prime}}\eta_{1}^{\mu}(\partial_{\mu}\eta^{\prime})\eta\,.

(29)

We follow the same procedures to obtain

|g^{c}_{\eta\eta^{\prime}}|=0.53\,,

(30)

which is used to further derive

\Gamma(\eta_{1}(1855)\xrightarrow{c}\eta\eta^{\prime})=0.74^{+0.42}_{-0.18}{\rm~MeV}\,.

(31)

This value is at the same level as that given in Eq. (
27
). However, we are not able to exactly derive the phase angle between their amplitudes, so we shall simply sum over these partial decay widths in Sec.
IV
.

The normal decay process depicted in Fig.
1
(a), with one quark-antiquark pair excited from the valence gluon, has been studied in Ref.
Chen:2010ic
for the
\bar{q}qg
hybrid meson. We replace the light
up/down
quarks by the
strange
quarks, and evaluate its partial decay width to be

\Gamma(\eta_{1}(1855)\xrightarrow{a}\eta\eta^{\prime})=0.68^{+2.26}_{-0.36}{\rm~MeV}\,.

(32)

This value is smaller than that given in Eq. (
27
), so the abnormal decay process depicted in Fig.
1
(b) with the
\eta^{\prime}
meson produced by the QCD axial anomaly does enhance the
\eta\eta^{\prime}
decay mode. Note that this value 0.68 MeV is larger than that obtained in Ref.
Chen:2010ic
, because the gluon condensates
\langle\alpha_{s}GG\rangle
and
\langle g_{s}^{3}G^{3}\rangle
used here are larger than those used there.

Besides the Lorentz structure
k_{\mu}k_{\nu}
investigated above, the three-point correlation functions
T^{b}_{\mu\nu}(p,k,q)
given in Eq. (
21
) and Eq. (
23
) also contain the Lorentz structure
\left(g_{\mu\nu}-{k_{\mu}k_{\nu}/k^{2}}\right)
. These components can be used to study the contribution of the QCD axial anomaly to the
f_{1}\eta
decay channel. Assuming the mass of
\eta_{1}
to be around 2100 MeV, its decay into the
f_{1}(1420)\eta
channel is kinematically allowed. However, the partial decay width of this mode is evaluated to be quite small, so we shall not discuss it any more.

In this paper we apply the method of QCD sum rules to study the
\eta_{1}(1855)
recently observed by BESIII as the
\bar{s}sg
hybrid meson of
I^{G}J^{PC}=0^{+}1^{-+}
, and calculate its partial decay widths into the
\eta\eta^{\prime}
,
K^{*}(892)\bar{K}
, and
K_{1}(1270)\bar{K}
channels. We have considered the normal decay process depicted in Fig.
1
(a) with one quark-antiquark pair excited from the valence gluon. We have also considered the abnormal decay processes depicted in Fig.
1
(b,c) with the
\eta^{(\prime)}
mesons produced by the QCD axial anomaly. The results are

\displaystyle\Gamma(\eta_{1}(1855)\xrightarrow{a}\eta\eta^{\prime})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 0.7^{+2.3}_{-0.4}{\rm~MeV}\,,

\displaystyle\Gamma(\eta_{1}(1855)\xrightarrow{b}\eta\eta^{\prime})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 1.8^{+0.7}_{-0.4}{\rm~MeV}\,,

\displaystyle\Gamma(\eta_{1}(1855)\xrightarrow{c}\eta\eta^{\prime})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 0.7^{+0.4}_{-0.2}{\rm~MeV}\,,

\displaystyle\Gamma(\eta_{1}(1855)\to K^{*}(892)\bar{K}+c.c.)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 98.1^{+82.9}_{-60.4}{\rm~MeV}\,,

\displaystyle\Gamma(\eta_{1}(1855)\to K_{1}(1270)\bar{K}+c.c.)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 30.4^{+40.1}_{-19.3}{\rm~MeV}\,,

where
\Gamma(\eta_{1}(1855)\xrightarrow{a,b,c}\eta\eta^{\prime})
denote the partial decay widths of the processes depicted in Fig.
1
(a,b,c), respectively. We simply sum over them to obtain
\Gamma_{\rm sum}\approx 132^{+126}_{-~81}
MeV. Considering that there still exist some other possible decay channels, our results support the interpretation of the
\eta_{1}(1855)
as the
\bar{s}sg
hybrid meson of
I^{G}J^{PC}=0^{+}1^{-+}
. Moreover, the decay processes depicted in Fig.
1
(b,c) with the
\eta^{(\prime)}
mesons produced by the QCD axial anomaly enhance the
\eta\eta^{\prime}
decay mode, and this possible relationship to the QCD axial anomaly implies the hybrid nature of the
\eta_{1}(1855)
.

It is interesting to compare our results to those of Ref.
Dong:2022cuw
. In this paper we explain the
\eta_{1}(1855)
as the
\bar{s}sg
hybrid meson of
I^{G}J^{PC}=0^{+}1^{-+}
, and its partial decay width into the
K^{*}(892)\bar{K}
channel was estimated to be around 98.1 MeV. In Ref.
Dong:2022cuw
the authors explained the
\eta_{1}(1855)
as the
K\bar{K}_{1}(1400)
hadronic molecule, and this partial decay width was estimated to be only around 0.9 MeV. This significant difference may be useful when examining the nature of the
\eta_{1}(1855)
.

Based on the interpretation of the
\eta_{1}(1855)
as the
\bar{s}sg
hybrid meson of
I^{G}J^{PC}=0^{+}1^{-+}
, we have also investigated its partner state, that is the
\bar{q}qg
(
q=u/d
) hybrid meson of
I^{G}J^{PC}=0^{+}1^{-+}
. Assuming its mass to be around
1600\pm 100
MeV, we calculate its partial decay widths into the
\eta\eta^{\prime}
and
a_{1}(1260)\pi
channels. The obtained results are summarized in Table
1
, and this is our first scheme, labelled “Scheme-I”.

Besides, we have considered another scheme, labelled “Scheme-II”, where the
\eta_{1}(1855)
is interpreted as the
\bar{q}qg
hybrid meson of
I^{G}J^{PC}=0^{+}1^{-+}
. In this case its partner state is the
\bar{s}sg
hybrid meson of
I^{G}J^{PC}=0^{+}1^{-+}
. Assuming their masses to be around
1855\pm 9^{+6}_{-1}
MeV and
2100\pm 100
MeV, we calculate their partial decay widths into the
\eta\eta^{\prime}
,
a_{1}(1260)\pi
,
f_{1}(1285)\eta
,
f_{1}(1420)\eta
,
K^{*}(892)\bar{K}
,
K_{1}(1270)\bar{K}
, and
K_{1}(1400)\bar{K}
channels. During the calculations we have used
f_{a_{1}(1260)}=238\pm 10
MeV
Yang:2007zt
. The obtained results are summarized in Table
1
. It seems also possible to interpret the
\eta_{1}(1855)
as the
\bar{q}qg
(
q=u/d
) hybrid meson of
I^{G}J^{PC}=0^{+}1^{-+}
, but this explanation is less favored considering: a) its total width is already larger than 250 MeV and there still exist some other possible decay channels not investigated, and b) the
\pi_{1}(1600)
is a good isovector hybrid candidate whose isoscalar partner may have a similar mass.

To differentiate the above two schemes, and more important, to verify whether the
\eta_{1}(1855)
is a hybrid meson or not, we propose to further search the other decay modes and its partner states in future BESIII, Belle-II, GlueX, LHC, and PANDA experiments.
