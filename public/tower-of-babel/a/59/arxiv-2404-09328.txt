Existence and regularity results for space-time fractional integro-differential equation of Kirchhoff type with memory
Lalit Kumar, Sivaji Ganesh Sista, Konijeti Sreenadh

Abstract
This paper analyses a Kirchhoff type quasilinear space-time fractional integro-differential equation with memory ( 𝒦 α s ) (\mathcal{K}^{s}_{\alpha}) . Various a priori bounds are derived in different norms on the solution of the considered equation. Utilizing these a priori bounds, existence and uniqueness of the weak solution to the proposed model are proved. Furthermore, regularity results on the solution of ( 𝒦 α s ) (\mathcal{K}^{s}_{\alpha}) are established. The contribution made in this work provides a framework for further investigation of such types of partial integro-differential equation ( 𝒦 α s ) (\mathcal{K}^{s}_{\alpha}) .



Keywords:

Time-fractional derivative, Kirchhoff type diffusion, Fractional Laplacian, Memory effect, Galerkin method, Fractional Sobolev space.

35K55, 35R11, 47G20, 35B44, 35Q91.

Introduction

The purpose of the current work is to study the following space-time fractional Kirchhoff type diffusion equation with memory term.
Let
\Omega\subset\mathbb{R}^{d}
be an open and bounded domain with smooth boundary
\partial\Omega
and
[0,T]
be a fixed finite time interval. For each
(\alpha,s)\in(0,1)\times(0,1)
, find
u:=u(x,t):\Omega\times[0,T]\rightarrow\mathbb{R}
that satisfies

\displaystyle\partial^{\alpha}_{t}u+M\left(\|u(t)\|^{2}_{X_{0}(\Omega)}\right)(-\Delta)^{s}u

\displaystyle=f(x,t)+\int_{0}^{t}b(x,t,\tau)u(\tau)~d\tau~\text{in}~\Omega\times(0,T],

(
\mathcal{K}^{s}_{\alpha}
)

\displaystyle u(x,0)

\displaystyle=u_{0}(x)~~~\text{in}~~~\Omega,

\displaystyle u(x,t)

\displaystyle=0~~~\text{in}~~~\Omega^{c}\times[0,T],

where

\|u(t)\|^{2}_{X_{0}(\Omega)}=\int_{\left(\mathbb{R}^{d}\times\mathbb{R}^{d}\right)\backslash\left(\Omega^{c}\times\Omega^{c}\right)}\frac{(u(x,t)-u(y,t))^{2}}{|x-y|^{d+2s}}~dxdy.

The notation

in (

K
s

α

[
10
,
5

that is given by

\partial^{\alpha}_{t}u(x,t)=\frac{d}{dt}\int_{0}^{t}k(t-\tau)(u(\tau)-u_{0})~d\tau,

(1.1)

with

k(t)=\frac{t^{-\alpha}}{\Gamma(1-\alpha)}.

The fractional Laplacian operator
[
12
,
13
]
in (

K
s

α

(-\Delta)^{s}u(x,t)=C_{d,s}~P.~V.\int_{\mathbb{R}^{d}}\frac{u(x,t)-u(y,t)}{|x-y|^{d+2s}}~dy~~~~\forall~(x,t)\in\Omega\times[0,T],

(1.2)

where

denotes the principal value and

Initial condition

, source function

Kirchhoff type diffusion coefficient occurs in various physical and biological processes. For instance, diffusion of a bacteria in a jar where increase in population density subject to spreading of bacteria

3
Existence and uniqueness of weak solutions to
(\mathcal{K}^{s}_{\alpha})

In this section, we prove the existence and uniqueness of the weak solution to the problem (

K
s

α

) by Galerkin method. In this method, first we consider the weak formulation (
3.1
) of finding its solutions in a finite dimensional subspace of
X_{0}(\Omega)
. Then the existence Theorem
2.4
for fractional differential equation provides a Galerkin sequence of solutions of finite dimensional problems. We derive various types of a priori bounds on the Galerkin sequence of solutions that help us to apply compactness Lemma
2.5
to ensure the convergence in
L^{2}(0,T;L^{2}(\Omega))
. Further using Lemma
2.6
, we prove that the Galerkin sequence also converges in
L^{2}(0,T;X_{0}(\Omega))
. As a consequence of these convergence results, we pass the limit inside the finite dimensional problems to conclude the existence of the weak solutions to (

K
s

α

). Uniqueness of the weak solution is proved by using Lipschitz continuity of the diffusion coefficient
M
.
