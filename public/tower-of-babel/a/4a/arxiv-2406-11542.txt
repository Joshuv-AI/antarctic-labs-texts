Gradient potential estimates for elliptic double obstacle problems with Orlicz growth
Qi Xiong, Zhenqiu Zhang, Lingwei Ma

Abstract
In this paper,we consider the solutions of the elliptic double obstacle problems with Orlicz growth involving measure data. Some pointwise estimates for the approximable solutions to these problems are obtained in terms of fractional maximal operators. Furthermore, we establish pointwise and oscillation estimates for the gradients of solutions via the non-linear Wolff potentials, which in turn yield C 1 , α C^{1,\alpha} -regularity of solutions. Mathematics Subject classification (2010): 35B45; 35R05; 35B65. Keywords: double obstacle problems; Wolff potential estimates; Restricted fractional maximal functions.

We consider the double obstacle problems with Orlicz growth , which are closely connected to measure data problems of the type

-\operatorname{div}\left({a}(x,Du)\right)=\mu\quad\quad\mbox{in}\ \ \ \Omega,\\

Next, we summarize our main results. We begin by presenting some definitions, notations and assumptions.

Definition 1.1
.

A function
G:[0,+\infty)\rightarrow[0,+\infty)
is called a Young function if it is convex and
G(0)=0
.

Definition 1.2
.

Assume that G is a Young function, the Orlicz class
K^{G}(\Omega)
is the set of all measurable functions
u:\Omega\rightarrow\mathbb{R}
satisfying

\int_{\Omega}G(|u|)\operatorname{d}\!\xi<\infty.

The Orlicz space
L^{G}(\Omega)
is the linear hull of the Orlicz class
K^{G}(\Omega)
with the Luxemburg norm

\|u\|_{L^{G}(\Omega)}:=\inf\left\{\alpha>0:\ \ \int_{\Omega}G\left(\frac{|u|}{\alpha}\right)\operatorname{d}\!\xi\leqslant 1\right\}.

Furthermore, the Orlicz-Sobolev space
W^{1,G}(\Omega)
is defined as

W^{1,G}(\Omega)=\left\{u\in L^{G}(\Omega)\cap W^{1,1}(\Omega)\ |\ Du\in L^{G}(\Omega)\right\}.

The space
W^{1,G}(\Omega)
, equipped with the norm

\|u\|_{W^{1,G}(\Omega)}:=\|u\|_{L^{G}(\Omega)}+\|Du\|_{L^{G}(\Omega)},
is a Banach space. Clearly,
W^{1,G}(\Omega)=W^{1,p}(\Omega)
, the standard Sobolev space, if
G(t)=t^{p}
with
p\geqslant 1
.

Note that for the Luxemburg norm there holds the inequality

\|u\|_{L^{G}(\Omega)}\leqslant\int_{\Omega}G(|u|)\operatorname{d}\!\xi+1.

The subspace
W_{0}^{1,G}(\Omega)
is the closure of
C_{0}^{\infty}(\Omega)
in
W^{1,G}(\Omega)
. The above properties about Orlicz space can be found in
[
31
]
.

For every
k>0
we let

T_{k}(s):=\left\{\begin{array}[]{r@{\ \ }c@{\ \ }ll}s\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ if\ \ |s|\leqslant k\,,\\[1.42271pt]
k\ sgn(s)\ \ \ \ \ \ \ if\ \ |s|>k\,.\\[1.42271pt]
\end{array}\right.

Moreover, for given Dirichlet boundary data
h\in W^{1,G}(\Omega)
, we define

\mathcal{T}^{1,G}_{h}(\Omega):=\left\{u:\Omega\rightarrow\mathbb{R}\ measurable:T_{k}(u-h)\in W_{0}^{1,G}(\Omega)\ \ for\ all\ k>0\right\}.

We now give the definition of approximable solutions.

.

Suppose that obstacle functions

\psi_{1}\leqslant u\leqslant\psi_{2}
a.e. on

if there exist functions

f_{i}\in(W^{1,G}(\Omega))^{\prime}\cap L^{1}(\Omega)\ \ with\ \ f_{i}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\ast}}{{\rightharpoonup}}\mu\ in\ \mathcal{M}_{b}(\Omega)\ \ as\ i\rightarrow+\infty

satisfies

\limsup_{i\rightarrow+\infty}\int_{B_{R}(x_{0})}|f_{i}|dx\leqslant|\mu|(\overline{B_{R}(x_{0})})

and solutions

for

The existence of approximating solutions, converging as defined above, has been proved in our previous work

for the inequalities (
