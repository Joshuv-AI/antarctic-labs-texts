Corrections to the elastic proton-proton analyzing power parametrization at high energies
A.A. Poblaguev

Abstract
The HJET Polarized Atomic Hydrogen Gas Jet Target polarimeter (HJET) polarimeter was designed to measure the absolute polarization of the proton beams at the Relativistic Heavy Ion Collider. In these measurements, the small scattering angle elastic p ​ p pp single A N ​ ( t ) A_{N}(t) and double A N ​ N ​ ( t ) A_{NN}(t) spin analyzing powers can be precisely determined. The experimental accuracy achieved at HJET requires corrections to the A N ​ ( t ) A_{N}(t) parametrization, conventionally used for such studies. In this paper we evaluate the corrections to the analyzing powers due to (i) the differences between the electromagnetic and hadronic form factors and (ii) the m p 2 / s m_{p}^{2}/s terms in the elastic spin-flip p ​ p pp electromagnetic amplitude. The corresponding alterations of the evaluated hadronic spin-flip amplitudes are about the same as the experimental uncertainties of the HJET measurements. The proposed corrections may have implications for the elastic p ​ p pp forward real-to-imaginary amplitude ratios ρ \rho determined in unpolarized p ​ p pp experiments.

I
Introduction

The Polarized Atomic Hydrogen Gas Jet Target
bib:ABS
polarimeter (HJET) is employed to measure the absolute polarization of proton beams at the Relativistic Heavy Ion Collider (RHIC). For that, the vertically polarized proton beam is elastically scattered at small angles (Fig.
1
) on the vertically polarized target (the jet) with well-determined polarization
|P_{j}|=0.957\pm 0.001
and the beam and jet spin correlated asymmetries of the recoil protons [Eq. (
20
)] are studied.

The major upgrade of HJET in 2015, along with the development of new methods in data analysis, allowed us to reduce the systematic uncertainties of the beam polarization measurements to a
\sigma^{\text{syst}}_{P}/P

\lesssim

0.5\%

bib:PSTP2017
level. Such a small systematic uncertainty of measurements, combined with large statistics of approximately
2\times 10^{9}
elastic
pp
events per RHIC run accumulated in 2015 (
E_{\text{lab}}
=
100
GeV) and 2017 (
E_{\text{lab}}
=
255
GeV), allowed us to precisely measure the single
A_{\text{N}}
and double
A_{\text{NN}}
spin analyzing powers
bib:Convention
in the Coulomb-nuclear interference (CNI) region.

Generally,
A_{\text{N}}(s,t)
and
A_{\text{NN}}(s,t)
are functions of the invariant variables
s
, center-of-mass energy squared, and
t
, 4-momentum transfer squared. An important part of the experimental study of the analyzing power is isolation of the hadronic spin-flip amplitudes. The theoretical basis for such studies was developed in Refs.
bib:KL
;
bib:BGL
. An update
bib:BKLST
for the RHIC spin program provided a parametrization of
A_{\text{N}}(s,t)
which was used in all previous experimental evaluations
bib:HJET06
;
bib:HJET09
;
bib:STAR13
of the hadronic spin-flip amplitudes in high energy near-forward elastic
pp
scattering.

Recently it was pointed out
bib:AbsorptiveCorr
that analyzing power
A_{N}(t)
given in Ref.
bib:BKLST
was derived with some simplifications, which might be essential for the experimental accuracy achieved at HJET:

(i)
it was implicitly assumed that the electromagnetic form factor is equal to the hadronic form factor
\exp(Bt/2)
, and

(ii)
the elastic
pp
electric form factor,
G_{E}^{pp}
, was approximated,
G_{E}^{pp}
​=​
G_{E}^{2}(t)
, by an electric form factor
G_{E}(t)
determined in electron-proton scattering experiments. The absorptive corrections, due to the initial and final state inelastic hadronic interactions between the colliding protons
bib:AbsorptiveCorr
, were not considered in Ref.
bib:BKLST
.

After this paper was accepted for publication, a theoretical evaluation of the absorptive corrections in elastic
pp
scattering was given in Ref.
bib:AbsorptiveCorr
. These new results were not taken into account below.

Here, we analyze the effect of the possible corrections to the
A_{\text{N}}(t)
parametrization on the results of the recent HJET measurements. The evaluated alteration of the measured hadronic spin-flip amplitudes suggests including the corrections in the data analysis. Also it was found that the discussed corrections may be important for experimental determination of the real-to-imaginary ratio
\rho
of the
pp
forward elastic scattering amplitude.

Parametrization of the CNI analyzing powers at high energies

Elastic

\displaystyle\phi_{1}(s,t)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\langle++|M|++\rangle,

\displaystyle\phi_{2}(s,t)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\langle++|M|--\rangle,

\displaystyle\phi_{3}(s,t)

\displaystyle\langle+-|M|+-\rangle,

\displaystyle\phi_{4}(s,t)

\displaystyle\langle+-|M|-+\rangle,

\displaystyle\phi_{5}(s,t)

\displaystyle=

\phi_{i}=\phi_{i}^{\text{h}}+\phi_{i}^{\text{em}}\exp(i\delta_{C}).

(2)

For very low

\phi_{+}(s,t)=\left[\phi_{1}(s,t)+\phi_{3}(s,t)\right]/2

(4)

term. According to the optical theorem,

\text{Im\,}\phi_{+}^{\text{h}}(s,0)=\frac{\sigma_{\text{tot}}(s)\,s}{8\pi}\,\sqrt{1-4m_{p}^{2}/s},

where

\displaystyle\rho(s)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\text{Re\,}\phi_{+}^{\text{h}}(s,0)\,/\,\text{Im\,}\phi_{+}^{\text{h}}(s,0),

(7)

\displaystyle t_{c}(s)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-8\pi\alpha/\sigma_{\text{tot}}(s)\approx-1.84\times 10^{-3}\,\text{GeV}^{2},

(8)

Similarly, hadronic single and double spin-flip amplitudes may be parametrized by the dimensionless factors

r_{5}(s)=\frac{m_{p}\,\phi_{5}^{\text{h}}}{\sqrt{-t}\,\mbox{Im}\,\phi_{+}^{\text{h}}}=R_{5}+iI_{5}

(9)

and

r_{2}(s)=\frac{\phi_{2}^{\text{h}}}{2\,\text{Im\,}\phi_{+}^{\text{h}}}=R_{2}+iI_{2},

(10)

Using the expression for the elastic

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{-4\pi(d\sigma/dt)^{-1}}{s(s-4m_{p}^{2})}\,\text{Im\,}\left[\phi_{5}^{*}\left(\phi_{1}+\phi_{2}+\phi_{3}-\phi_{4}\right)\right]

(12)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{\sqrt{-t}}{m_{p}}\,\,\frac{(t_{c}/t)\,f_{\text{N}}^{0}+f_{\text{N}}^{1}}{f_{\text{cs}}(t)},

where

\displaystyle f_{\text{N}}^{0}(r_{5})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\varkappa\,(1-\rho\delta_{C})-2(I_{5}-\delta_{C}R_{5}),

(13)

\displaystyle f_{\text{N}}^{1}(r_{5})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-2(R_{5}-\rho I_{5}),

(14)

\displaystyle f_{\text{cs}}(t)~

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left(\frac{t_{c}}{t}\right)^{2}-2(\rho+\delta_{C})\frac{t_{c}}{t}+1+\rho^{2}.

(15)

In Eq. (

\mu_{p}-1
=
1.793
is the proton’s anomalous magnetic moment.
The

(16)

while the dependence on
r_{2}
is negligible.

Similarly
bib:BKLST
,

\displaystyle A_{\text{NN}}(t)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{4\pi(d\sigma/dt)^{-1}}{s(s-4m^{2})}\,\left[2|\phi_{5}|^{2}+\text{Re\,}\left(\phi_{1}\phi_{2}^{*}-\phi_{3}\phi_{4}^{*}\right)\right]

(17)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{(t_{c}/t)\,f_{\text{NN}}^{0}+f_{\text{NN}}^{1}}{f_{\text{cs}}(t)},

\displaystyle f_{\text{NN}}^{0}(r_{2})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-2(R_{2}+\delta_{C}I_{2}),

(18)

\displaystyle f_{\text{NN}}^{1}(r_{2})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 2I_{2}+2\rho R_{2}-(\rho\varkappa-4R_{5})\frac{\varkappa t_{c}}{2m_{p}^{2}}.

IV
Corrections to the analyzing powers

To calculate corrections to Eq. (
12
), it is convenient to use the scaled amplitudes

\varphi_{i}(s,t)=\phi_{i}(s,t)/\text{Im\,}\phi_{+}^{\text{h}}(s,t).

(21)

Since a possible dependence of
\rho
,
r_{2}
, and
r_{5}
on
t
may be neglected in the CNI region, the scaled hadronic amplitudes can be approximated by

\displaystyle\varphi_{1}^{\text{h}}=\varphi_{3}^{\text{h}}

\displaystyle=\rho(s)+i,

(22)

\displaystyle\varphi_{2}^{\text{h}}

\displaystyle=2r_{2}(s),

\displaystyle\varphi_{4}^{\text{h}}

\displaystyle=r_{4}(s)\times(-t/m_{p}^{2})\approx 0,

\displaystyle\varphi_{5}^{\text{h}}

\displaystyle=r_{5}(s)\times\sqrt{-t}/m_{p}.

For the electromagnetic amplitudes, we should include the corrections of order of
m_{p}^{2}/s
which can be significant for
E_{\text{Lab}}
=
100
GeV. Using the following expressions for the proton’s electromagnetic form factors
bib:Sachs
;
bib:BKLST
,

F_{1}=\frac{G_{E}-G_{M}t/4m_{p}^{2}}{1-t/4m_{p}^{2}},\quad\varkappa F_{2}=\frac{G_{M}-G_{E}}{1-t/4m_{p}^{2}}

(23)

and neglecting the
t/s
terms, one can derive from Ref.
bib:BGL

\displaystyle\varphi_{1}^{\text{em}}=\varphi_{3}^{\text{em}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\varphi_{0}^{\text{em}}\times(1\!-\!2m_{p}^{2}/s)/\sqrt{1\!-\!4m_{p}^{2}/s},\hskip 25.00003pt

\displaystyle-\varphi_{2}^{\text{em}}=\varphi_{4}^{\text{em}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\varphi_{0}^{\text{em}}F_{\varkappa}^{2}\!\times\!(1\!-\!2m_{p}^{2}/s)/\sqrt{1\!-\!4m_{p}^{2}/s},

(24)

\displaystyle\varphi_{5}^{\text{em}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\varphi_{0}^{\text{em}}\,\left(F_{\varkappa}-\frac{\sqrt{-t}}{2m_{p}}\,\frac{2m_{p}^{2}}{s-4m_{p}^{2}}\right).

The following shorthand was used

\displaystyle\varphi_{0}^{\text{em}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{t_{c}(s)}{t}\times F_{1}^{2}(t)\exp(-Bt/2)

(25)

\displaystyle F_{1}~

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1-\mu_{p}t/4m_{p}^{2}}{1-t/4m_{p}^{2}}\times\left(1+r_{E}^{2}t/6\right),

(26)

\displaystyle F_{\varkappa}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{\sqrt{-t}}{2m_{p}}\,\frac{\varkappa F_{2}\,}{F_{1}}=\frac{\sqrt{-t}}{2m_{p}}\,\frac{\varkappa}{1\!-\!\mu_{p}t/4m_{p}^{2}}.

(27)

The proton’s electric form factor
G_{E}(t)
was approximated in (
26
) by the proton charge radius
r_{E}
=
\langle r_{E}^{2}\rangle^{1/2}
. In Eq. (
27
), we did not distinguish between proton electric
r_{E}
and magnetic
r_{M}
radii.

The electromagnetic and hadronic form factors difference can be realized by the substitution
t_{c}

\rightarrow

t_{c}+bt
where

b/t_{c}=\frac{d}{dt}\left[F_{1}^{2}(t)e^{-Bt/2}\right]_{t=0}.

(28)

Since the electric form factor
G_{E}(t)
in the dipole form
bib:GD
;
bib:Cahn

G_{D}(t)~=\left(1-t/\Lambda^{2}\right)^{-2},~~~\Lambda^{2}=0.71\,\text{GeV}^{2}

(29)

was commonly used in the elastic
pp
data analysis, it is convenient to explicitly isolate the corresponding term
b_{D}
in (
28
)

b=b_{D}+b_{\text{nf}},

(30)

where

b_{D}/t_{c}=\frac{d}{dt}\left(G^{2}_{D}(t)e^{-Bt/2}\right)\big|_{t=0}=\left(\frac{4}{\Lambda^{2}}-\frac{B}{2}\right).

(31)

For the RHIC beam energies,

\displaystyle\text{100\,GeV:}~~~b_{D}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left(-0.06\pm 0.19\right)\times 10^{-3},

(32)

\displaystyle\text{255\,GeV:}~~~b_{D}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left(+0.31\pm 0.19\right)\times 10^{-3}.

(33)

The errors here correspond to the systematic uncertainties in the values of
B(s)

bib:Bartenev
.

For
b_{\text{nf}}
one finds

b_{\text{nf}}/t_{c}=r_{E}^{2}/3-4/\Lambda^{2}-\varkappa/2m_{p}^{2}.

(34)

Currently, PDG
bib:PDG
gives two values of proton charge radius

\displaystyle r_{ep}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 0.8751\pm 0.0061\,\text{fm},

(35)

\displaystyle r_{\mu p}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 0.84086\pm 0.00026\pm 0.00029\,\text{fm},

(36)

obtained in three kinds of measurements: with atomic hydrogen, with electron scattering off hydrogen, and with muonic hydrogen.
The discrepancy between the methods is not resolved yet. Assuming
r_{E}
=
0.858\pm 0.017
fm, one obtains

b_{\text{nf}}=\left(0.64\pm 0.46\right)\times 10^{-3}.

(37)

Approximating
\varphi_{0}^{\text{em}}
=
(t_{c}/t)e^{bt/t_{c}}
, one finds a correction to the denominator (
15
) of the analyzing power expressions

\displaystyle f_{\text{cs}}(t,\rho)

\displaystyle\rightarrow

\displaystyle f_{\text{cs}}(t,\rho-b+\varkappa^{2}t_{c}/4m_{p}^{2})

\displaystyle b^{2}-2b\delta_{C}-2\rho\varkappa^{2}t_{c}/4m_{p}^{2}+\dots

\displaystyle f_{\text{cs}}(t,\rho-b_{D}-b_{\text{cs}}).

The term

contribution to

in (

,

, and

\displaystyle f_{\text{cs}}(t,\rho)

\displaystyle\to

\displaystyle f_{\text{cs}}(t,\rho-b_{D}),

(41)

\displaystyle f^{0}_{\text{N}}

\displaystyle\to

\displaystyle f^{0}_{\text{N}}-2m_{p}^{2}/s,

(42)

\displaystyle f^{1}_{\text{N}}

\displaystyle\to

\displaystyle f^{1}_{\text{N}}+\varkappa\,\left(b_{D}+b_{\text{nf}}+b_{\varkappa}\right),

VI
Possible effect of the absorptive correction

A dependence of the measured
r_{5}
on the absorptive corrections could be readily estimated if the corresponding modification of the electromagnetic form factor
\mathcal{F}^{\text{em}}(t)

of an elastic
pp
amplitude can be approximated in the CNI region by a linear function of
t
,

\mathcal{F}^{\text{em}}(t)\rightarrow\mathcal{F}^{\text{em}}(t)\times\left[1+a(s)t/t_{c}\right].

(51)

Generally,
a(s)
is spin dependent. It can be effected by the substitutions

b_{\text{nf}}\to b_{\text{nf}}
+
a_{\text{nf}}
,

b_{\varkappa}\to b_{\varkappa}
+
a_{\text{sf}}
-
a_{\text{nf}}
,
where
a_{\text{nf}}(s)
and
a_{\text{sf}}(s)
are absorptive corrections to nonflip and spin-flip amplitudes, respectively.
The dominant absorptive corrections to
r_{5}
and
r_{2}
can be written as

\displaystyle\Delta_{\text{a}}R_{5}=a_{\text{sf}}\varkappa/2,\phantom{0}

\displaystyle\Delta_{\text{a}}I_{5}=-a_{\text{nf}}\delta_{C}\varkappa/2\approx 0,

(52)

\displaystyle\Delta_{\text{a}}R_{2}=0,\phantom{a_{\text{sf}}\varkappa/2}

\displaystyle\Delta_{\text{a}}I_{2}=a_{\text{nf}}\frac{\varkappa^{2}t_{c}}{4m_{p}^{2}}\approx 0.

(53)

As it was underlined above, the correction, such as given in Eq. (
51
), does not modify
f_{\text{cs}}(t,\rho)
but specifies the systematic errors in the experimental determinations of
\rho
. In case of large corrections, say
|a_{\text{nf}}+b_{\text{cs}}|\gtrsim 0.003
, the results of all forward unpolarized proton-proton scattering measurements should be revised and, consequently, a new global fit of
\rho(s)
and
\sigma_{\text{tot}}(s)
should be carried out.

VII
Conclusions

In this paper, the corrections to the analyzing powers given in Ref.
bib:BKLST
were studied. For the experimental results already published, Eqs. (
48
) and (
49
) allows one to evaluate with sufficient accuracy the corrections to the measured single spin-flip amplitude parameter
r_{5}
.

The improved expressions for
A_{\text{N}}(t)
and
A_{\text{NN}}(t)
could be written in the same form as in Ref.
bib:BKLST
(if neglecting the absorptive correction terms
\Delta^{a}_{\text{N}}=\Delta^{a}_{\text{NN}}=0
)

\displaystyle\frac{m_{p}}{\sqrt{-t}}\,A_{\text{N}}(t)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{\left[\varkappa^{\prime}(1-\rho^{\prime}\delta_{C})-2(I_{5}-\delta_{C}R_{5})\right]\,t^{\prime}_{c}/t-2(R_{5}-\rho^{\prime}I_{5})+\Delta^{a}_{\text{N}}\varkappa}{\left(t_{c}/t\right)^{2}-2(\tilde{\rho}+\delta_{C})\,t_{c}/t+1+\tilde{\rho}^{2}},

(54)

\displaystyle A_{\text{NN}}(t)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{-2(R_{2}+\delta_{C}I_{2})\,t^{\prime}_{c}/t+2(I_{2}+\rho^{\prime}R_{2})-(\rho^{\prime}\varkappa-4R_{5})\,\varkappa t_{c}/2m_{p}^{2}+\Delta^{a}_{\text{NN}}\varkappa t/m_{p}^{2}}{\left(t_{c}/t\right)^{2}-2(\tilde{\rho}+\delta_{C})t_{c}/t+1+\tilde{\rho}^{2}},

(55)

but with the following modification of some parameters:

\displaystyle t^{\prime}_{c}/t

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle t_{c}/t+\left(r_{E}^{2}/3-B/2-\varkappa/2m_{p}^{2}\right)t_{c},\hskip 50.00008pt

(56)

\displaystyle\rho^{\prime}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\rho+\left(r_{E}^{2}/3-4/\Lambda^{2}-\varkappa/2m_{p}^{2}-\varkappa^{2}/4m_{p}^{2}\right)t_{c},

(57)

\displaystyle\tilde{\rho}\phantom{{}^{\prime}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\rho-\left(4/\Lambda^{2}-B/2\right)t_{c},

(58)

\displaystyle\varkappa^{\prime}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\varkappa/(1\!-\!\mu_{p}t/4m_{p}^{2})-2m_{p}^{2}/(s\!-\!4m_{p}^{2}).

(59)

The published HJET results
bib:HJET19
were obtained using Eqs. (
54
)–(
59
) without absorptive corrections.

The double spin-flip amplitude terms in (
54
) and the term
|\varphi_{t}^{\text{h}}|^{2}
in (
55
) were dropped off because they are negligible for the HJET experimental accuracy and, also, are comparable with the omitted corrections of order of
(m_{p}^{2}/s)^{2}
and
t/s
. The
(m_{p}^{2}/s)^{2}
corrections (
24
) to nonflip amplitudes
\varphi_{1,3}
were neglected in Eq. (
54
). It should be noted that for experimental uncertainties similar to those at HJET these corrections become noticeable if
\sqrt{s}\!\lesssim\!5\,\text{GeV}
.

The absorptive corrections are currently undetermined [no values of
a(s)
are published yet], but once calculated may be introduced by the following substitutions:

\displaystyle r_{E}^{2}/3

\displaystyle\to

\displaystyle r_{E}^{2}/3+a_{\text{nf}}/t_{c},

(60)

\displaystyle\Delta^{a}_{\text{N\phantom{N}}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle a_{\text{sf}}-a_{\text{nf}},

(61)

\displaystyle\Delta^{a}_{\text{NN}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 4R_{5}\,\left(a_{\text{sf}}-a_{\text{nf}}\right)-\rho\varkappa\,\left(a_{\text{df}}-a_{\text{nf}}\right),

(62)

where
a_{\text{df}}(s)
is the absorptive correction (
51
) to the double spin-flip electromagnetic form factor. The absorptive corrections may also affect the results of determination of
\rho
in unpolarized elastic
pp
scattering.
