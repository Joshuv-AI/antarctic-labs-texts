Steenrod operations on the modular invariants
Nguyễn Sum

Abstract
. In this paper, we compute the action of the mod p p Steenrod operations on the modular invariants of the linear groups with p p an odd prime number.

Primary 55S10; Secondary 55S05

Introduction

Fix an odd prime
p
. Let
A_{p^{n}}
be the alternating group on
p^{n}
letters. Denote by
\Sigma_{p^{n},p}
a Sylow
p
-subgroup of

p
-group of rank

\displaystyle\mbox{Res}(E^{n},\Sigma_{p^{n},p}):H^{*}(B\Sigma_{p^{n},p})\longrightarrow H^{*}(BE^{n}),

\displaystyle\mbox{Res}(E^{n},A_{p^{n}}):H^{*}(BA_{p^{n}})\longrightarrow H^{*}(BE^{n}),

induced by the regular permutation representation

of

(see Mùi

[
4
]
). Here and throughout the paper, we assume that the coefficients are taken in the prime field
\mathbb{Z}/p
. Using modular invariant theory of linear groups, Mùi proved in
[
3
,
4
]

that

\displaystyle\mbox{ImRes}(E^{n},\Sigma_{p^{n},p})=E(U_{1},\ldots,U_{n})\otimes P(V_{1},\ldots,V_{n}),

\displaystyle\mbox{ImRes}(E^{n},A_{p^{n}})=E(\tilde{M}_{n,0},\ldots,\tilde{M}_{n,n-1})\otimes P(\tilde{L}_{n},Q_{n,1},\ldots,Q_{n,n-1}),

Here and in what follows,
E(.,\ldots,.)
and
P(.,\ldots,.)
are the exterior and polynomial algebras over
\mathbb{Z}/p
generated by the variables indicated.
\tilde{L}_{n},\,Q_{,s}
are the Dickson invariants of dimensions
p^{n},\,2(p^{n}-p^{s})
, and
\tilde{M}_{n,s},,U_{k},\,V_{k}
are the Mùi invariants of dimensions

Let
\mathcal{A}
be the mod
p

\tau_{s},\,\xi_{i}
be the Milnor elements of dimensions
2p^{s}-1,\,2p^{i}-2

\mathcal{A}_{*}

7

\mathcal{A}_{*}=E(\tau_{0},\tau_{1},\ldots)\otimes P(\xi_{1},\xi_{2},\ldots).

Then

\tau_{S}\xi^{R}=\tau_{s_{0}}\ldots\tau_{s_{k}}\xi^{r_{1}}\ldots\xi^{r_{m}}
, with

,

. Let

\mathcal{A}
has a basis consisting all operations

For

is nothing but the Steenrod operation

.

Since
H^{*}(BG),\ G=E^{n},\,\Sigma_{p^{n},p}
or

, is an

-module (see
[

The purpose of the paper is to study the module structures of

Here by the length of a sequence

P^{r}
on

and

The analogous results for

[

1.
Preliminaries

As is well-known
H^{*}(BE^{n})=E(x_{1},\ldots,x_{n})\otimes P(y_{1},\ldots,y_{n})
where
\dim x_{i}=1,\,y_{i}=\beta x_{i}
with
\beta
the Bockstein homomorphism. Following Dickson
[
2
]
and Mùi
[
3
]

define

\displaystyle[e_{1},\ldots,e_{k}]=\det(y_{i}^{p^{e_{j}}}),

\displaystyle[1;e_{2},\ldots,e_{k}]=\begin{vmatrix}x_{1}&\cdots&x_{k}\\
y_{1}^{p^{e_{2}}}&\cdots&y_{k}^{p^{e_{2}}}\\
\vdots&\cdots&\vdots\\
y_{1}^{p^{e_{k}}}&\cdots&y_{k}^{p^{e_{k}}}\end{vmatrix}.

for every sequence of non-negative integers
(e_{1},\ldots,e_{k}),\,1\leqslant k\leqslant n
. We set

\displaystyle L_{k,s}=[0,\ldots,\hat{s},\ldots,k],\,L_{k}=L_{k,k}=[0,\ldots,k-1],\,L_{0}=1,

\displaystyle M_{k,s}=[1;0,\ldots,\hat{s},\ldots,k-1],\,0\leqslant s<k\leqslant n.

Then
\tilde{L}_{n},\,Q_{n,s},\,\tilde{M}_{n,s},\,U_{k},\,V_{k}
are defined by

\displaystyle\tilde{L}_{n}=L_{n}^{h},\,h=(p-1)/2,\,Q_{n,s}=L_{n,s}/L_{n},\,0\leqslant s\leqslant n,

\displaystyle\tilde{M}_{k,s}=M_{n,s}L_{n}^{h-1},\,U_{k}=M_{k,k-1}L_{k-1}^{h-1},\,V_{k}=L_{k}/L_{k-1},\,1\leqslant k\leqslant n.

Note that
Q_{n,0}=\tilde{L}_{n}^{2}
,
Q_{n,n}=1
for any
n>0
.

Let X be a topological space. Then we have the Steenrod power map

P_{n}:H^{q}(X)\,\longrightarrow H^{p^{n}q}(EA_{p^{n}}\underset{A_{p^{n}}}{\times}X^{p^{n}}),

which sends
u
to
1\otimes u^{p^{n}}
at the cochain level (see
[
13
, Chap. VII]
). We also have the diagonal homomorphism

d_{n}^{*}:H^{*}(EA_{p^{n}}\underset{A_{p^{n}}}{\times}X^{p^{n}})\longrightarrow\,H^{*}(BE^{n})\otimes H^{*}(X)

induced by the diagonal map of
X
, the inclusion
E^{n}\subset A_{p^{n}}
and the Künneth formula.

d_{n}^{*}P_{n}
has the following fundamental properties.

Proposition 1.1

(Mùi
[
3
,
4
]
)
.

(i)

d_{n}^{*}P_{n}
is natural monomorphism preserving
cup product up to a sign, more precisely

d^{*}_{n}P_{n}(uv)=(-1)^{nhqr}d^{*}_{n}P_{n}ud^{*}_{n}P_{n}v\ ,

where
q=\dim u,\,r=\dim v,\,h=(p-1)/2.

(ii)

d^{*}_{n}P_{n}=d^{*}_{n-s}P_{n-s}d^{*}_{s}P_{s}\ ,\ 0\leqslant s\leqslant n.

(iii)
For
H^{*}(E^{1})=E(x)\otimes P(y)
, we have

\displaystyle d_{n}^{*}P_{n}x=(-h!)^{n}U_{n+1}=(h!)^{n}\Big(\tilde{L}_{n}x+\sum_{s=0}^{n-1}(-1)^{s+1}\tilde{M}_{n,s}y^{p^{s}}\Big),

\displaystyle d_{n}^{*}P_{n}y=V_{n+1}=(-1)^{n}\sum_{s=0}^{n}(-1)^{s}Q_{n,s}y^{p^{s}}.

where
U_{n+1}=U_{n+1}(x_{1},\ldots,x_{n},x,y_{1},\ldots,y_{n},y)
,
V_{n+1}=V_{n+1}(y_{1},\ldots,y_{n},y)
.

The following is a description of
