Theorem 1

Fix

Theorem 2

Lemma 14
.

In

, with

, the following holds w.v.h.p.:

P1

P2

Proof.

In the respective cases, the probability that there exists a set

with more edges than claimed can be bounded by

P1

\displaystyle\sum_{s=\log^{1/2}n}^{n/\log^{2}n}\binom{n}{s}\binom{\binom{s}{2}}{3s}p^{3s}

\displaystyle\leq\sum_{s=\log^{1/2}n}^{n/\log^{2}n}\left(\frac{ne}{s}\cdot\left(\frac{s^{2}ec\log n}{6sn}\right)^{3}\right)^{s}

\displaystyle\leq\sum_{s=\log^{1/2}n}^{\log n}\frac{1}{n^{s}}+\sum_{s=\log n}^{n/\log^{2}n}\left(\frac{c^{3}}{2\log n}\right)^{s}

\displaystyle=o(n^{-r}).

P2

\displaystyle\sum_{s=e\rho^{1/2}c\log n}^{\rho n}\binom{n}{s}\binom{\binom{s}{2}}{e\rho^{1/2}cs\log n}p^{e\rho^{1/2}cs\log n}
