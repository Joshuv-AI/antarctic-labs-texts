Design interpretable experience of dynamical feed forward machine learning model for forecasting NASDAQ
Pouriya Khalilian, Sara Azizi, Mohammad Hossein Amiri, Javad T. Firouzjaee

Abstract
National Association of Securities Dealers Automated Quotations(NASDAQ) is an American stock exchange based. It is one of the most valuable stock economic indices in the world and is located in New York City pagano2008quality . The volatility of the stock market and the influence of economic indicators such as crude oil, gold, and the dollar in the stock market, and NASDAQ shares are also affected and have a volatile and chaotic nature firouzjaee2022lstm . In this article, we have examined the effect of oil, dollar, gold, and the volatility of the stock market in the economic market, and then we have also examined the effect of these indicators on NASDAQ stocks. Then we started to analyze the impact of the feedback on the past prices of NASDAQ stocks and its impact on the current price. Using PCA and Linear Regression algorithm, we have designed an optimal dynamic learning experience for modeling these stocks. The results obtained from the quantitative analysis are consistent with the results of the qualitative analysis of economic studies, and the modeling done with the optimal dynamic experience of machine learning justifies the current price of NASDAQ shares. Keywords: NASDAQ, Oil, US Dollar, Gold, Stock market, Machine Learning, Dynamical feed forward model, lasso, PCA



I
Introduction

There is a dynamic and complex relationship between economic variables that has attracted the attention of many researchers, business people, and policy makers.
All these variables have experienced significant changes over time, therefore, it is necessary to find a periodic relationship for them. Many economists and scientists seek to find a global perspective to examine the relationships between oil prices, gold, the US dollar, and stock prices. Crude oil is an important resource in the development and economic stability of all countries in the world. Estimating the price of crude oil is one of the topics of concern for many market participants and global policy-makers.
Natural resources such as oil and minerals are precious wealth that can play an important and vital role in the economic development of a country
su2021does
. However, the resource curse paradox states that countries with natural resources have slower economic growth than countries without resources. Many studies show that the abundance of natural resources has a negative correlation with economic growth
jianqiang2020exacerbating
;
naseer2020double
. Countries like the United States and Australia are exceptions to this, but in general, it can be said that non-resource-rich countries have higher overall growth. For example, Asian economies and Western European countries experience higher economic growth rates.
Crude oil accounts for about 40% of the world’s energy consumption and has the largest share in the commodity market. Uncertainty in the price of oil, especially in the economy of oil-dependent countries, is a destructive and destabilizing factor in the macro-economy, which can hinder sustainable economic growth in the long run. Oil crises such as oil embargo, war, recession and world economic crises and epidemics, etc., have caused an increase in oil price volatility, which in turn has played an effective role in the economic growth of countries.

On the other hand, gold also plays the role of a valuable financial asset or economic security against economic volatility. With the occurrence of the Covid-19 pandemic crisis, the price of gold faced fluctuations of 26% in 2020. Hence, it can be concluded that macroeconomic structures and business cycles are related to gold and oil price fluctuations
guan2021volatility
.
A recent data set from 2000 to 2020 includes two crises and critical economic situations (the global economic crisis in 2008 and the ongoing Covid-19 pandemic in 2020), showing that in July 2008, oil prices reached their highest level of 145.18 dollars per barrel. However, with the outbreak of the pandemic, the price of oil reached an all-time low of $37.63 per barrel. On the other hand, even though the price of crude oil was falling during the pandemic, the price of gold rose dramatically, reaching a peak of $2,060 per ounce in August 2020. In the months that followed, the price of gold fell sharply, falling below $1,800 per ounce in November 2020. These fluctuations for Gold were more than expected. Volatility and movement of prices in the crises of the last two decades have prompted many economists to examine the effects of volatility in oil and gold on the economic growth of resource-producing countries
guan2021volatility
.
In Ref.
guan2021volatility
, Lu Guan et al. show that regardless of the status of oil or gold exporting countries, volatility in the oil and gold markets undermines their real GDP growth. However, in the short term, gold price fluctuations may cause its growth in the world markets. They stated that the increase in growth may be due to the fact that 12 of the 18 countries they chose as a sample are gold producers. These countries may take advantage of market volatility when gold prices rise. Oil fluctuations in short-term periods have not had a significant impact on GDP growth. This is probably because oil contracts are mostly long-term contracts.
In Ref.

The author found a negative relationship between oil prices and stocks, a negative relationship between oil and the US dollar rate, and a negative relationship between the US dollar and gold prices. In addition, he states that there is a close relationship between the price of oil and the price of gold.

However, Krugman focuses on the relationship between the portfolio investment preferences of oil exporters and exchange rate movements. He states that the increase in the price of oil will lead to more investment in the portfolio of oil exporters. Krugman believes that the exchange rate changes are basically determined by the movement of the current account, and as a result, if the increase in oil prices leads to a weakening of the country’s current account, then the exchange rate will decrease. Many other studies show that the impact of the dollar on the international price of oil is significant in the long run. Monetary policy and interest rate changes can also be factors in creating and intensifying the negative correlation between exchange rates and prices.

The price of oil, the dollar, and the stock market, along with the import of American oil and default insurance premiums of companies, can play a role in the price of gold and its changes. Following the increase in oil prices, business costs increase and the resulting profit decreases even for non-oil companies. The profit and commercial information of the listed companies are transferred to the stock markets, and as a result, the decrease in profit will lead to a decrease in the value of the stock market.

LASSO

The LASSO regression analysis method in statistics and machine learning combines variable selection and regularization to improve the predictability and understandability of the produced statistical model. Since its initial introduction by Tibshirani
tibshirani1996regression
, it has gradually found use in the energy sector, including in the consumption of electricity, the cost of electricity, and the cost of natural gas. The LASSO approach alters the cost function by adding a penalty term that minimizes the estimated value of the regression coefficients and prevents the inflation of standard errors that is frequently observed in the presence of multicollinearity. LASSO minimizes the residual sum of squares when choosing variables, provided that the total of the absolute values of the coefficients is less than a constant. More particularly:

\hat{\beta}^{L}=argmin\left\{\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left(y_{i}-\alpha-\sum_{i=1}^{n}\beta_{j}x_{ij}\right)^{2}\right\},

(1)

provided that
\sum_{j=1}^{p}|\hat{\beta}^{L}_{j}|\leq c(Constant)
.

This issue can be expressed as follows:

\hat{\beta}^{L}=argmin\left\{\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left(y_{i}-\alpha-\sum_{i=1}^{n}\beta_{j}x_{ij}\right)^{2}+\lambda\sum_{j}|\beta_{j}|\right\},

(2)

where
\lambda>0
is selected such that
\sum_{j=1}^{p}|\hat{\beta}^{L}_{j}|=c(Constant)
, and each
\lambda
is equivalent to a different LASSO parameter c
miao2017influential
.
Some of the regression coefficients decrease to zero when the LASSO value is sufficiently small. As a result, for each LASSO parameter, the LASSO algorithm only chooses a subset of the regression coefficients.
The amount of shrinkage that is applied to the estimations is controlled by the LASSO parameter
c>0
. Tibshirani
tibshirani1996regression
solves each relevant LASSO parameter using quadratic programming methods. For all values of
c
, Osborne also created a ”homotopy” method for parameter estimation
osborne2000lasso
.

Principal Component Analysis(PCA)

An unsupervised learning approach called principal component analysis is used in machine learning to reduce dimensionality.
With the use of orthogonal transformation, it is a statistical process that transforms the observations of correlated features into a set of linearly uncorrelated data.
The objective of PCA, one of the most established and widely used techniques in data analysis, is to extract low-dimensional subspaces from high-dimensional data while retaining as much diversity and unpredictability as feasible
zare2018extension
.
Three key issues are frequently addressed by PCA: 1) Dimensionality reduction for big, high-dimensional data sets, also known as low-rank subspace approximation
cunningham2015linear
.
2) Subspace learning for applications in machine learning
jiang2011linear

3) Protect the low-rank matrix from invalid data, such as missing samples
candes2011robust
;
wright2009robust
.

When there are lots of data and some of them are irrelevant, this strategy can help minimize the quantity of data storage needed while also speeding up and improving the functionality of machine learning algorithms.
The concept of PCA has traditionally been introduced algebraically through covariance matrices.
In fact, this approach seeks a set of factors that are invariably linked to one another.
PCA will significantly reduce complexity with little information loss by combining them into a new parameter.
It will be possible to find a simpler explanation for the original data set if the majority of the variance in the original data can be explained by just a few new variables.
By displaying which important variables are correlated, PCA may produce new physical insights beyond describing the statistical domain
francis1999introduction
.

Many people think that feedback loop tendencies can be seen in financial markets. The idea of feedback loops was initially devised as a theory to explain economic concepts, but it is now widely used in behavioral finance and capital markets theory, among other fields of finance.
During difficult times, the value of feedback in the financial markets increases considerably. Markets frequently become erratic during times of uncertainty because humans have a tendency to overreact to feelings of greed and fear. The fear that occurs during abrupt market corrections is a good example of this. Positive feedback occurs when the outcome strengthens the initial act. Positive feedback loops can drive the price of security well above its fundamentals during asset bubbles.
Positive feedback frequently alludes to investors’ propensity for herd mentality, which when used to buy or sell assets can turn into irrational enthusiasm. Investor elation can turn into unreasonable exuberance when a cycle of favorable feedback lasts too long; this can cause asset bubbles and eventually cause a market disaster.
Although these additional acts can also have positive effects, if they are not reined in, they frequently have negative effects. An investor who makes money right away after buying a stock could overestimate their own trading skills and undervalue luck or other market circumstances. This could eventually result in overconfidence while choosing investments.
