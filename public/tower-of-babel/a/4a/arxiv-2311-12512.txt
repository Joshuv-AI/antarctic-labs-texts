Abstract
. Let k k be an algebraically closed field of positive characteristic, and G G a simple algebraic group over k k . Under the assumption that the characteristic is a good prime for G G , we determine a maximal G G -stable subvariety 𝒰 ′ \mathcal{U}^{\prime} of the variety of the unipotent elements in G G such that for all u ∈ 𝒰 ′ u\in\mathcal{U}^{\prime} any two A 1 A_{1} -subgroups of G G containing u u are G G -conjugate. This result establishes to what degree an analogue of the Jacobson–Morozov Theorem for Lie algebras is valid for simple algebraic groups defined over fields of (good) positive characteristic.

Let

The above question was motivated by recent progress on the analogous question concerning nilpotent orbits in Lie algebras. Set

Assuming

This result can be found for classical

[

We return to our consideration of unipotent elements in

3.
Classical Groups

Let
