Singular properties of QED vacuum response to applied quasi-constant electromagnetic fields
Stefan Evans, Johann Rafelski

Abstract
Employing the Bogoliubov coefficient summation method and introducing the gyromagnetic ratio g ≠ 2 g\neq 2 we derive an explicit functional form of ℑ ​ 𝔪 ​ V g EHS \mathfrak{Im}V^{\mathrm{EHS}}_{g} , the imaginary part of Euler-Heisenberg-Schwinger (EHS) type effective action. We show that ℑ ​ 𝔪 ​ V g EHS \mathfrak{Im}V^{\mathrm{EHS}}_{g} is periodic in g g for any (quasi-)constant electromagnetic field configuration, and equal to the imaginary part obtained using a periodic in g g Ramanujan integrand in the proper time representation of V g EHS V^{\mathrm{EHS}}_{g} . This validates the Ramanujan representation of V g EHS V^{\mathrm{EHS}}_{g} for both real and imaginary parts and allows writing the effective action in a suitably modified Schwinger proper time format. As a function of the ratio b / a b/a between ℬ → b {\mathcal{B}}\to b and ℰ → a {\mathcal{E}}\to a covariant generalizations of EM fields, we explore the singular properties of ℑ ​ 𝔪 ​ V g EHS \mathfrak{Im}V^{\mathrm{EHS}}_{g} at g = 2 ± 4 k , k = 0 , ± 1 , ± 2 … g=2\pm 4k,k=0,\pm 1,\pm 2\ldots involving the pseudoscalar a ​ b ≡ ℰ → ⋅ ℬ → ab\equiv\vec{\mathcal{E}}\cdot\vec{\mathcal{B}} in perturbative and nonperturbative behavior. We study the e − ​ e + e^{-}e^{+} -decay vacuum instability, incorporating the physical value of g − 2 g-2 vertex diagrams when summing infinite irreducible loops. We obtain an effective expansion parameter χ b = α ​ b / 2 ​ a \chi_{b}=\alpha b/2a ( α = e 2 / 4 ​ π \alpha=e^{2}/4\pi ), characterizing the onset of nonperturbative in g − 2 g-2 suppression of vacuum instability. We demonstrate the χ b \chi_{b} domains for which perturbative expansion in α \alpha breaks down: The EM vacuum subject to critical electric field strength is stabilized in magnetic-dominated ‘magnetar’ environments. Considering separately the case of ℰ {\mathcal{E}} and ℬ {\mathcal{B}} fields, we generalize to all g g the temperature representation of the V g EHS V^{\mathrm{EHS}}_{g} effective action.

II.1
Proper time evaluation

We summarize the proper time formulation of EHS effective action with
g\neq 2
, which turns out to be limited to the domain
|g|\leq 2
. Schwinger
Schwinger:1951nm
in his manifestly covariant and gauge invariant approach employed the ‘squared’ Dirac equation, the product of the Dirac equation with its negative mass counterpart. To incorporate anomalous magnetic moment in this approach Kruglov extended the second order fermion
Morgan:1995
;
Espin:2013
;
Espin:2015bja
wave equation to
g\neq 2
, referred to as the KGP formulation
Steinmetz:2018ryf

\displaystyle\Big((i\partial_{\mu}-eA_{\mu})^{2}-m^{2}-\frac{g}{2}\frac{e}{2}\sigma^{\mu\nu}F_{\mu\nu}\Big)\Psi=0\;,

(1)

where

\sigma^{\mu\nu}=\frac{i}{2}[\gamma^{\mu},\gamma^{\nu}]
,
F_{\mu\nu}
denotes the EM tensor, and

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\sigma^{\mu\nu}F_{\mu\nu}=i\gamma^{5}\vec{\Sigma}\cdot\vec{\mathcal{E}}-\vec{\Sigma}\cdot\vec{\mathcal{B}}\;,

(2)

with Pauli-Dirac matrices
\vec{\Sigma}=\gamma^{5}\gamma^{0}\vec{\gamma}
.

Kruglov used Eq. (
1
) to formulate a
g
-dependent modification to Schwinger’s proper time evolution operator Eq. (2.33) in
Schwinger:1951nm
, with ‘Hamiltonian’

\displaystyle H_{\mathrm{KGP}}=

\displaystyle\;\Pi^{2}-\frac{g}{2}\frac{e}{2}\sigma_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu}\;,

(3)

where
\Pi_{\mu}=p_{\mu}-eA_{\mu}
. The resulting spin
1/2
action with
g\neq 2

\displaystyle V^{\mathrm{EHS}}_{g}=

\displaystyle\,\frac{i}{2}\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{du}{u}e^{-im^{2}u}\mathrm{tr}\left<x\right|e^{-iH_{\mathrm{KGP}}u}\left|x\right>

(4)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\;\frac{1}{32\pi^{2}}\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{du}{u^{3}}e^{-im^{2}u}\frac{e^{2}u^{2}ab\;\mathrm{tr}\;\!e^{iu(g/2)e\sigma F/2}}{\sinh(eau)\sin(ebu)}\;,

where the pre-factor follows the units of Schwinger where
\alpha=e^{2}/4\pi
, for a review see
Dunne:2004nc
. The electromagnetic field invariants are obtained from the eigenvalues (
\pm a,\,\pm ib
) of EM tensor

\displaystyle a^{2}-b^{2}=\vec{\mathcal{E}}\,^{2}-\vec{\mathcal{B}}\,^{2}\equiv 2S\;,\quad a^{2}b^{2}=(\vec{\mathcal{E}}\cdot\vec{\mathcal{B}}\,)^{2}\equiv P^{2}\;.

(5)

Eigenvalue
a
is ‘electric-like’, following

follows

Evaluation of Eq. (

\displaystyle V^{\mathrm{EHS}}_{g}=\frac{1}{8\pi^{2}}\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{du}{u^{3}}e^{-i(m^{2}-i\epsilon)u}F(eau,ebu,\frac{g}{2})\;,

\displaystyle F(x,y,\frac{g}{2})=\frac{x\cosh(\frac{g}{2}x)}{\sinh(x)}\frac{y\cos(\frac{g}{2}y)}{\sin(y)}-1\;,\qquad\left|\frac{g}{2}\right|\leq 1\;.

(6)

However, Eq. (

) is convergent only for
|g|\leq 2
. When

\displaystyle\!\!\!\!\!\lim_{u\to\infty}e^{-i(m^{2}-i\epsilon)u}\frac{eau\cosh(\frac{g}{2}eau)}{\sinh(eau)}\frac{ebu\cos(\frac{g}{2}ebu)}{\sin(ebu)}\neq 0\;.

(7)

The

. Therefore when we refer to Kruglov action we now will write

u\to-is

|g|\leq 2
, but not for
|g|>2
. Even so, we note that allowing for arbitrary field configurations and field strengths the proper time convergence issue for

We observe further that this divergence cannot be alleviated by renormalization; the subtraction -1 in Eq. (

\displaystyle F(x,y,\frac{g}{2})\to F(x,y,\frac{g}{2})-\frac{x^{2}-y^{2}}{6}\Big(3\left(\frac{g}{2}\right)^{2}-1\Big)\;,

\displaystyle\left|\frac{g}{2}\right|\leq 1\,;

III.1
Klein-Gordon-Pauli solution

In order to use the Bogoliubov coefficient summation procedure to obtain the spin

\displaystyle\Big(-(i\partial_{\mu}-eA_{\mu})^{2}+m^{2}-ge\sigma({\mathcal{B}}-i\lambda{\mathcal{E}})\Big)\Psi=0\;,

(17)

where

in Eq. (

\displaystyle\sigma=\pm 1/2\;,\qquad\lambda=\pm 1\;.

(18)

\sigma
is the spin projection along the axis parallel to

and
{\mathcal{B}}
, and

and
{\mathcal{B}}=b=|\vec{\mathcal{B}}|

-direction.

The wave function

contains the Weyl 2-spinor

\displaystyle\Psi=\psi X\;,\qquad X=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\begin{pmatrix}1\\
\pm 1\end{pmatrix}\;.

(19)

For static homogenous

and
{\mathcal{B}}
pointing in the
x
-direction, the 4-potential is given by

\displaystyle A^{\mu}=(0,-{\mathcal{E}}t,0,{\mathcal{B}}y)\;,

IV
Singular and nonperturbative properties

This section describes several properties of
V^{\mathrm{EHS}}_{g}
that rely on the nonperturbative treatment of the magnetic moment. Without doubt other interesting properties will appear in the future; this should be considered an initial consideration of the opportunities opened up by applying a nonperturbative method to achieve resummation of diagrams, see figure

represents.

IV.4
Temperature representation

The format of periodic in
g
function
V^{\mathrm{EHS}}_{g}
now available can be applied to extend prior work on the temperature representation of EHS action
Muller:1977mm
;
PauchyHwang:2009rz
;
Labun:2012jf
. First we consider the electric-dominated action. Like the proper time integration method for inserting
g\neq 2
in Eq. (
II.1
), the temperature representation is on first sight restricted to the domain
|g|\leq 2
, see Eq. (
16
) in section
II
, or Eq. (13) of
Labun:2012jf
.

To extend this result to arbitrary
g
, we recall the conversion between effective action with
|g|>2
to an equivalent form periodically reset to
|g_{k}|\leq 2
, Eq. (
54
). This allows for a convergent result in both the proper time and the temperature representation integrands. Thus the prior result for
|g|\leq 2
in Eq. (13) of Ref.
Labun:2012jf
requires only replacement
g\to g_{k}
in order to describe all possible magnetic moments. Consequently,
V^{\mathrm{EHS}}_{g}
for
g=2+4k
values (
k=0,\pm 1,\pm 2,\ldots
) corresponds to the (spin inverted) bosonic distributions, while for
g=0+4k
the representation is Fermionic.

We consider separately the case of magnetic-dominated fields. The procedure for deriving the corresponding temperature representation follows closely to the electric case summarized in section
II
. We start with the proper time integral form of
V^{\mathrm{EHS}}_{g}
given by Eq. (
III.3
), allowing for any
g
. In the vanishing electric field limit, with rotation of the integration contour
u\to-iu
, Eq. (
III.3
) becomes

\displaystyle V^{\mathrm{EHS}}_{g}=-\frac{1}{8\pi^{2}}\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{du}{u^{3}}e^{-m^{2}u}\Big(\frac{ebu\cosh(\frac{g_{k}}{2}ebu)}{\sinh(ebu)}-1\Big)\;,

(83)

which we write in terms of the meromorphic expansion

\displaystyle\frac{ebs\cos(\frac{g_{k}}{2}ebs)}{\sin(ebs)}-1=-\frac{e^{2}b^{2}u^{2}}{3}\Big(\frac{3g_{k}^{2}}{8}-\frac{1}{2}\Big)

(84)

\displaystyle\;\qquad\qquad-2e^{2}b^{2}u^{4}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^{n}\cos(\frac{g_{k}}{2}n\pi)}{n^{2}\pi^{2}(u^{2}+n^{2}\pi^{2}/e^{2}b^{2})}\;.

We plug Eq. (
84
) into Eq. (
83
), remove the charge renormalization contribution, and exchange summation with integration to obtain

V^{\mathrm{EHS}}_{g}=\frac{e^{2}b^{2}}{4\pi^{2}}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\int_{0}^{\infty}du\,u\,e^{-m^{2}u}\frac{(-1)^{n}\cos(\frac{g_{k}}{2}n\pi)}{n^{2}\pi^{2}(u^{2}+n^{2}\pi^{2}/e^{2}b^{2})}\;.

(85)

Substituting
u\to n\pi u/eb
and exchanging summation with integration again,

\displaystyle V^{\mathrm{EHS}}_{g}=

\displaystyle\;\frac{e^{2}b^{2}}{8\pi^{2}}\int_{0}^{\infty}du\frac{2u}{u^{2}+1}

(86)

\displaystyle\;\times\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}e^{-n\pi m^{2}u/eb}\frac{(-1)^{n}\cos(\frac{g_{k}}{2}n\pi)}{n^{2}\pi^{2}}\;.

Integrating Eq. (
86
) by parts,

V^{\mathrm{EHS}}_{g}=v\int_{0}^{\infty}\!\!\!du\ln[u^{2}+1]\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}e^{-n\beta u}\frac{(-1)^{n}\cos(\frac{g_{k}}{2}n\pi)}{n\pi}\;,

(87)

where now

\beta=\pi m^{2}/eb\;,\qquad v=\frac{m^{4}}{8\pi^{2}\beta}\;.

(88)

Summing Eq. (
87
) over
n
we obtain

\displaystyle V^{\mathrm{EHS}}_{g}=

\displaystyle\;-v\int_{0}^{\infty}du\ln[u^{2}+1]\frac{1}{2}\sum_{\pm}\ln[1+e^{-\beta u}e^{\pm i\frac{g_{k}}{2}\pi}]\;.

(89)

Comparing the magnetic field action Eq. (
89
) to the electric case Eq. (13) of
Labun:2012jf
(Eq. (
16
) in section
II
here), the
g
-dependent logarithmic terms on the RHS are identical within the domain
|g|\leq 2
. Thus in this
g
domain the two expressions obey the same statistical model representation. Given that our conversion
g\to g_{k}
is equal for electric and magnetic fields, the periodic
|g|>2
extensions of these two statistical representations are also equal. The one difference is between the spectral function terms describing density of the virtual particles:
\ln[u^{2}+1]
appears in the
{\mathcal{B}}
-dominated integrand, compared to
\ln(u^{2}-1+i\varepsilon)
in the
{\mathcal{E}}
field case.

V
Summary, Conclusions and Outlook

We generalized the Euler-Heisenberg-Schwinger (EHS) effective action to arbitrary values of the gyromagnetic ratio
g
. We have demonstrated a cusp singularity in the vicinity of
g=\pm 2,\pm 6,\ldots
. For arbitrary quasi-constant field configurations we have shown periodicity in
V^{\mathrm{EHS}}_{g}
as a function of
g
recognized before for the pure electric
{\mathcal{E}}

Evans:2022fsu
, and pure magnetic
{\mathcal{B}}
field cases
Rafelski:2012ui
.

The nonperturbative in
g

V^{\mathrm{EHS}}_{g}
in the presence of an anomalous magnetic moment

was conjectured in our prior work
Rafelski:2012ui
;
Evans:2022fsu
. In this work using the Bogoliubov coefficient summation method
Nikishov:1979ez
;
Kim:2008yt
we were able prove in section
III
this singular behavior dominated by nonvanishing pseudoscalar
\vec{\mathcal{E}}\cdot\vec{\mathcal{B}}
. This feature escaped prior attention since considered for pure electric
{\mathcal{E}}
, or pure magnetic
{\mathcal{B}}
fields, the effective action
V^{\mathrm{EHS}}_{g}
is smooth and differentiable at
g=\pm 2
. This exact summation procedure resolves the effective action for both
|g|\leq 2
and
|g|>2
, where analytical continuation between the two domains is otherwise not possible due to the cusp.

We have shown that the sharpness of the cusp in
\mathfrak{Im}V^{\mathrm{EHS}}_{g}
at
g=\pm 2
is dependent on the EM fields in a nonperturbative fashion, and occurs for nonzero
\vec{\mathcal{E}}\cdot\vec{\mathcal{B}}
, based on the nonperturbative discontinuity in
dV^{\mathrm{EHS}}_{g}/dg
shown in Eq. (
IV.1
), section
IV.1
. We believe that the importance of nonvanishing pseudoscalar
\vec{\mathcal{E}}\cdot\vec{\mathcal{B}}
is echoed by the relatively strong coupling of two photons to the singlet pseudoscalar
0^{-}
-para-positronium and the related fast decay channel
\tau_{s}=0.124
ns; to be compared to
\tau_{t}=142
ns for the triplet ortho-positronium coupling to odd number of photons.

We have explored some of the nonperturbative properties of
V^{\mathrm{EHS}}_{g}
. Most interesting is the cusp singularity for magnetically dominated fields
\mathfrak{Im}V^{\mathrm{EHS}}_{g}
capability to heavily suppress the pair production, see figure
3
in section
IV.1
. We presented explicit dependence of this suppression effect exploring several important values of EM fields
{\mathcal{E}}\to a
,
{\mathcal{B}}\to b
, figure
