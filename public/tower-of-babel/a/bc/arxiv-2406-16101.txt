Zejun Huang, Zhenhua Lyu

Abstract
Given a positive integer t t , let P t , 2 P_{t,2} be the digraph consisting of t t directed paths of length 2 with the same initial and terminal vertices. In this paper, we study the maximum size of P t + 1 , 2 P_{t+1,2} -free digraphs of order n n , which is denoted by e ​ x ​ ( n , P t + 1 , 2 ) ex(n,P_{t+1,2}) . The Turán type problem plays an important role in graph theory. It concerns the largest possible number of edges in graphs without given subgraphs and the extremal graphs achieving that maximum number of edges. The study of these problems can be dated back to 1907, when Mantel
[
18

The systematic investigation of extremal problems on digraphs was initiated by Brown and Harary
[
4

. They determined the precise maximum size and extremal digraphs for digraphs avoiding a complete digraph of a given order. They also studied digraphs avoiding a tournament, a direct sum of two tournaments, or a digraph on at most 4 vertices where every two vertices are joined by at least one arc. H
