,

align
,

,
aligned
,

split
, and others except the obsolete environment

eqnarray
.

Theorem 3.1
.

Content of your theorem.

In the proof below, we would like to show you how to align the math
formulas:

Proof of Theorem
3.1
.
Please refer to the following example and align your math formulas:

\displaystyle{\theta}_{\varepsilon}\wedge d{\theta}_{\varepsilon}^{n-1}

\displaystyle=(\theta_{0}+\varepsilon\alpha)\wedge(d(\theta_{0}+\varepsilon\alpha))^{n-1}

\displaystyle=(\theta_{0}+\varepsilon\alpha)\wedge(d\theta_{0})^{n-1}+\theta_{0}\wedge d\theta_{0}^{n-1}-\varepsilon d\left(\alpha\wedge\theta_{0}\wedge d\theta_{0}^{n-2}\right)

\displaystyle\qquad+\theta_{0}\wedge d\theta_{0}^{n-1}+\varepsilon\alpha\wedge d\theta_{0}^{n-1}

\displaystyle=\theta_{0}\wedge d\theta_{0}^{n-1}-\varepsilon d\left(\alpha\wedge\theta_{0}\wedge d\theta_{0}^{n-2}\right),

(3.1)

It also can be aligned in the following way:

{\theta}_{\varepsilon}\wedge d{\theta}_{\varepsilon}^{n-1}=(\theta_{0}+\varepsilon\alpha)\wedge(d(\theta_{0}+\varepsilon\alpha))^{n-1}\\
=(\theta_{0}+\varepsilon\alpha)\wedge(d\theta_{0})^{n-1}+\theta_{0}\wedge d\theta_{0}^{n-1}-\varepsilon d\left(\alpha\wedge\theta_{0}\wedge d\theta_{0}^{n-2}\right)\\
+\theta_{0}\wedge d\theta_{0}^{n-1}+\varepsilon\alpha\wedge d\theta_{0}^{n-1}\\
=\theta_{0}\wedge d\theta_{0}^{n-1}-\varepsilon d\left(\alpha\wedge\theta_{0}\wedge d\theta_{0}^{n-2}\right),

(3.2)

Here is other example if the math expression in [ ] exceeds one
line:

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{T}|u_{0}(t)|^{2}dt

\displaystyle\leq\delta^{-1}\left[\int_{0}^{T}(\beta(t)+\gamma(t))\,dt\vphantom{\left(\int_{0}^{T}\right)^{\frac{2}{p}}}\right.

\displaystyle\left.+T^{\frac{2(p-1)}{p}}\left(\int_{0}^{T}|\dot{u}_{0}(t)|^{p}\,dt\right)^{\frac{2}{p}}+T^{\frac{2(p-1)}{p}}\left(\int_{0}^{T}|\dot{u}_{0}(t)|^{p}\,dt\right)^{\frac{2}{p}}\right].

(3.3)

Please use the displaystyle if your formulas fully
occupy a paragraph, while use textstyle among the text.

For two equations:

\displaystyle A

\displaystyle=\theta_{0}\wedge d\theta_{0}^{n-1}-\varepsilon d\left(\alpha\wedge\theta_{0}\wedge d\theta_{0}^{n-2}\right),

\displaystyle B

\displaystyle=\theta_{1}\wedge d\theta_{1}^{n-1}-\varepsilon d\left(\alpha\wedge\theta_{1}\wedge d\theta_{1}^{n-2}\right).
