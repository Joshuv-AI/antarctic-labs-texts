The Jacobi Operator and its Donoghue m m -Functions
Fritz Gesztesy, Mateusz Piorkowski, Jonathan Stanfill

Abstract
. In this paper we construct Donoghue m m -functions for the Jacobi differential operator in L 2 ​ ( ( − 1 , 1 ) , ( 1 − x ) α ​ ( 1 + x ) β ​ d ​ x ) L^{2}\big((-1,1);(1-x)^{\alpha}(1+x)^{\beta}dx\big) , associated to the differential expression τ α , β = − ( 1 − x ) − α ​ ( 1 + x ) − β ​ ( d / d ​ x ) ​ ( ( 1 − x ) α + 1 ​ ( 1 + x ) β + 1 ) ​ ( d / d ​ x ) , x ∈ ( − 1 , 1 ) , α , β ∈ ℝ , \displaystyle\begin{split}\tau_{\alpha,\beta}=-(1-x)^{-\alpha}(1+x)^{-\beta}(d/dx)\big((1-x)^{\alpha+1}(1+x)^{\beta+1}\big)(d/dx),&\\ x\in(-1,1),\;\alpha,\beta\in{\mathbb{R}},\end{split} whenever at least one endpoint, x = ± 1 x=\pm 1 , is in the limit circle case.
