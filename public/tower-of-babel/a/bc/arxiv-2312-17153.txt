The m m th-order Eulerian Numbers
Tian-Xiao He

Abstract
We define the m m th-order Eulerian numbers with a combinatorial interpretation. The recurrence relation of the m m th-order Eulerian numbers, the row generating function and the row sums of the m m th-order Eulerian triangle are presented. We also define the m m th-order Eulerian fraction and its alternative form. Some properties of the m m th-order Eulerian fractions are represented by using differentiation and integration. An inversion relationship between second-order Eulerian numbers and Stirling numbers of the second kind is given. Finally, we give the exact expression of the values of the m m th-order Eulerian numbers. AMS Subject Classification: 05A15, 05A05, 15B36, 15A06, 05A19, 11B73, 11B83. Key Words and Phrases: Eulerian numbers, second-order Eulerian numbers, m m th-order Eulerian numbers, Eulerian fraction, m m th-order Eulerian fraction, Stirling numbers.

Theorem 2.3

Let
T^{(m)}_{n,k}
be the
m
th-order Eulerian numbers defined in Definition
2.1
. Denote by

S_{m;n}(t)=\sum^{n-1}_{k=0}T^{(m)}_{n,k}t^{k},

(15)

the
m
th-order Eulerian polynomials, where
S_{1;n}(t)
are the classical Eulerian polynomials. Then

S_{m;n+1}(t)=(1+mnt)S_{m;n}(t)+t(1-t)S^{\prime}_{m;n}(t).

(16)

Proof.

From (
15
), we have

S^{\prime}_{m;k}(t)=\sum^{n-1}_{k=0}kT^{(m)}_{n,k}t^{k-1},

t(1-t)S^{\prime}_{m;k}(t)=\sum^{n-1}_{k=0}kT^{(m)}_{n,k}t^{k}-\sum^{n}_{k=1}(k-1)T^{(m)}_{n,k-1}t^{k},

and

\displaystyle(1+mnt)S_{m;n}(t)=(1+mnt)\sum^{n-1}_{k=0}T^{(m)}_{n,k}t^{k}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum^{n-1}_{k=0}T^{(m)}_{n,k}t^{k}+\sum^{n}_{k=1}mnT^{(m)}_{n,k-1}t^{k}.

Hence, noting (
1
), we have

\displaystyle(1+mnt)S_{m;n}(t)+t(1-t)S^{\prime}_{m;n}(t)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum^{n-1}_{k=0}(k+1)T^{(m)}_{n,k}t^{k}+\sum^{n}_{k=1}(mn-k+1)T^{(m)}_{n,k-1}t^{k}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum^{n}_{k=0}\left((k+1)T^{(m)}_{n,k}+(mn-k+1)T^{(m)}_{n,k-1}\right)t^{k}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum^{n}_{k=0}T^{(m)}_{n+1,k}t^{k},

which yields (
16
).

From the last expression and noting

Theorem 3.2

To prove (

\displaystyle\frac{d}{dt}\left(\frac{t}{(1-t)^{mn+1}}\right)S_{m;n}(t)+\frac{t}{(1-t)^{mn+1}}S^{\prime}_{m;n}(t)

From (
