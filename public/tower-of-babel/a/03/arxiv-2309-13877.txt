Abstract




A remark on isolated symplectic singularities with trivial local fundamental group

Yoshinori Namikawa

Recently, Bellamy et al.[BBFJLS] constructed an infinite series of 4-dimensional isolated symplectic sngularities
with trivial local fundamental group, inspired by a question of Beauville [Be]. In this short note, we introduce an easy construction of isolated
symplectic singularities (of any dimension) with trivial local fundamental group. We will use a toric hyperkaehler construction ([BD], [Go], [HS], [Ko]).

Let
n
be a positive integer with
n\geq 3
and
let
\mathbf{a}:=(a_{1},...,a_{n})
be an
n
-tuple of nonzero integers such that
\mathrm{GCD}(a_{i},a_{j})=1
for any

i\neq j
.
Then
\mathbf{C}^{*}
acts on the affine space
\mathbf{C}^{2n}
by

(z_{1},...,z_{n},w_{1},...,w_{n})\to(t^{a_{1}}z_{1},...,t^{a_{n}}z_{n},t^{-a_{1}}w_{1},...,t^{-a_{n}}w_{n})

By rearranging the order of
z_{1},...,z_{n},w_{1},...,w_{n}
, we may assume that
a_{i}
are all positive and

a_{1}\leq a_{2}\leq...\leq a_{n}
.
Define a symplectic form
\omega
on
\mathbf{C}^{2n}
by

\omega:=\sum_{1\leq i\leq n}dw_{i}\wedge dz_{i}.

Then the
\mathbf{C}^{*}
-action on
(\mathbf{C}^{2n},\omega)
is a Hamiltonian action and
its moment map
\mu
is given by

\mathbf{C}^{2n}\to\mathbf{C}\>\>\>(z_{1},...,z_{n},w_{1},...,w_{n})\to\sum_{1\leq i\leq n}a_{i}z_{i}w_{i}.

Define
Y(\mathbf{a},0):=\mu^{-1}(0)//\mathbf{C}^{*}
and let
\pi:\mu^{-1}(0)\to Y(\mathbf{a},0)
be the quotient map.
Put
\bar{0}:=\pi(0)
.

Y(\mathbf{a},0)

\bar{0}

Proof

for all

.

Both

n

\mu^{-1}(0)

Assume that

As we will see below Claim 2, there is a partial resolution

(resp.

Y(\alpha,+)

\square

Claim 2
.
\pi_{1}(Y(\mathbf{a},0)-\{\bar{0}\})=1
.

Proof
. By the proof of Claim 1, it suffices to show that
\pi_{1}(\mu^{-1}(0)-\pi^{-1}(\bar{0}))=1
.

\mu^{-1}(0)
is a hypersurface of
\mathbf{C}^{2n}
with an isolated singularity at
0
.
Note that
\dim\mu^{-1}(0)=2n-1
. By [M, Theorem 5.2] the local fundamental group of

is trivial.
Moreover,

\mu^{-1}(0)
is stable under the scaling
\mathbf{C}^{*}
-action

(z_{1},...,z_{n},w_{1},...,w_{n})\to(tz_{1},...,tz_{n},tw_{1},...,tw_{n}).

Then, by [ibid] together with this
\mathbf{C}^{*}
-action, we have

\pi_{1}(\mu^{-1}(0)-\{0\})=1
.
On the other hand,

\mathrm{Codim}_{\mu^{-1}(0)}F\cup G=n-1\geq 2.

Since
\mu^{-1}(0)-\pi^{-1}(\bar{0})
is obtained from the complex manifold

\pi_{1}(\mu^{-1}(0)-\pi^{-1}(\bar{0}))\cong\pi_{1}(\mu^{-1}(0)-\{0\})=1.

\square

For a character

We put

\mu^{-1}(0)^{\alpha-ss}:=\{p\in\mu^{-1}(0)|\>\>p\>\mathrm{is}\>\alpha\mathrm{-semistable}\}.

. By definition, there is a quotient map

One can write

.

\mu^{-1}(0)^{\alpha-ss}

m(\alpha)=0

m(\alpha)<0
.

\mu^{-1}(0)^{+}

, and

\mu^{-1}(0)^{-}

\mu^{-1}(0)^{\alpha-ss}

and

Now one can check

\mu^{-1}(0)^{+}=\mu^{-1}(0)-F,\>\>\>\mu^{-1}(0)^{-}=\mu^{-1}(0)-G.

The inclusion maps
\mu^{-1}(0)^{+}\subset\mu^{-1}(0)
and

\mu^{-1}(0)^{-}\subset\mu^{-1}(0)
respectively induce maps

and

\nu^{-}:Y(\mathbf{a},-)\to Y(\mathbf{a},0)
.
It is easily checked that

and

(\nu^{+})^{-1}(\bar{0})\cong\mathbf{P}(a_{1},...,a_{n})
,

.
Both

and

are crepant partial resolutions of

with only isolated quotient singularities.
Therefore,
\nu^{+}
and
\nu^{-}
are (projective)

-factorial terminalizations
of

.
The diagram

Y(\mathbf{a},+)\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\nu^{+}}}{{\rightarrow}}Y(\mathbf{a},0)\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\nu^{-}}}{{\leftarrow}}Y(\mathbf{a},-)

is a flop in the sense that the proper transform

of a
\nu^{+}
-negative line bundle

\nu^{-}
-ample.
Since the relative Picard numbers

has exactly two different

-factorial termnalizatons. Assume that
\mathbf{a^{\prime}}
is a different

-tuple
of poisitive integers different from

. Then

. In fact, if they coincide, then their

Summing up these facts, we get

.
Y(\mathbf{a},0)
is an isolated symplectic singularity of dimension

with
\pi_{1}(Y(\mathbf{a},0)_{reg})=1

\mathbf{a}\neq\mathbf{a}^{\prime}
, then

\square

Maximal weight
. Let
(X,\omega)
be a conical symplectc variety with coordinate ring
R
.
Let
x_{1},...,x_{k}\in R
be minimal homogeneous generators of
R
. Put
l_{i}:=\mathrm{deg}(x_{i})
.
We define

l:=\mathrm{max}\{l_{1},...,l_{k}\}

and call it the
maximal weight
of
X
.
If we fix the dimension and the maximal weight, then there are only finitely many conical symplectic varieties with such properties ([Na 1]).
In particular, when the maximal weight is
1
, such a conical symplectc variety is the closure
\bar{O}
of a nilpotent orbit
O
of
a complex semisimple Lie algebra
\mathfrak{g}
([Na 2]). The method above enables us to construct an example with an arbitrary maximal weight.

Assume that the
n
-tuple
\mathbf{a}
is of the form
(1,...,1,m)
with a positive integer
m
. It is easily checked that the
\mathbf{C}
-algebra

R:=\mathbf{C}[Y(\mathbf{a},0)]
is generated by the monomials
1
1

1

There is a relation:
z_{1}w_{1}+\cdots+z_{n-1}w_{n-1}+mz_{n}w_{n}=0
.

z_{i}w_{j}\>\>(1\leq i\leq n-1,\>1\leq j\leq n-1),\>\>z_{n}w_{n}

z_{1}^{d_{1}}\cdots z_{n-1}^{d_{n-1}}w_{n},\>\>z_{n}w_{1}^{d_{1}}\cdots w_{n-1}^{d_{n-1}}\>\>(d_{1}+...+d_{n-1}=m).

For the scaling
\mathbf{C}^{*}
-action
(z_{1},...,z_{n},w_{1},...,w_{n})\to(tz_{1},...,tz_{n},tw_{1},...,tw_{n})
,
the monomials on the first row have weight
2
and those on the second row have weight
m+1
.
When
m
is an odd integer with
m=2l-1
, one can introduce a new
\mathbf{C}^{*}
-action on
\mathbf{C}[Y(\mathbf{a},0)]

so that the monomials on the first row have weight
1
and those on the second row have weight
l
.
Then the symplectic 2-form
\omega
has weight
1
.
This means that
(Y(\mathbf{a},0),\omega)
is a conical symplectic variety of dimension
2n-2
with maximal weight

l
.

Contact structures
. A complex manifold
M
of dimension
2n-1
is called a contact complex manifold if
there exists a holomorphic line bundle
L
on
M
and a surjection
\theta:\Theta_{M}\to L
such that the
following condition hold:

If we define
F:=\mathrm{Ker}(\theta)
, then

F\times F\to L,\>\>(x,y)\to\theta([x,y])

is a nondegenerate skew symmetric form.

Here
x
and
y
are regarded as sections of
\Theta_{M}
and
[x,y]
is the bracket product of vector fields.
Such a line bundle
L
is called a contact line bundle. Then the canonical line bundle
K_{M}
of
M
satisfies

K_{M}\cong L^{-n}
. In particular, if
M
is a Fano manifold, then
L
is an ample line bundle.
The LeBrun-Salamon conjecture asserts that, if
M
is a contact Fano manifold, then

M
is isomorphic to the projectivization
\mathbf{P}(O_{min})
of the minimal nilpotent orbit
O_{min}
of a complex simple Lie algebra

\mathfrak{g}
. The conjecture is true if
\dim M\leq 5
or
b_{2}(M)>1
(cf. [Ye], [Dr], [KPSW]).

Let
(M,L)
be a contact Fano manifold. Then the contact structure on
M
induces a symplectic form

\omega_{(L^{-1})^{\times}}
on
(L^{-1})^{\times}:=L^{-1}-(0\mathrm{-section})
. Since
L^{-1}
is negative, we can contract the
0
-section of
L^{-1}
to get an affine variety
X
with an isolated singularity
0
. Then
\omega_{(L^{-1})^{\times}}
determines
a symplectic form
\omega
on
X-\{0\}
. Since
L^{-1}
has a natural
\mathbf{C}^{*}
-action,

X
also has a
\mathbf{C}^{*}
-action and
(X,\omega)
becomes a conical symplectic variety with
wt(\omega)=1
.
By construction,
\mathbf{C}^{*}
acts on
X-\{0\}
freely.
Conversely, assume that
X
is a conical symplectic variety with an isolated
singularity at
0\in X
such that the
\mathbf{C}^{*}
-action is
free
on
X-\{0\}
. Embed
X
in an affine space
\mathbf{C}^{k}

by using the minimal homogeneous generators of the coordinate ring
R
of
X
. Denote by
l_{1},...,l_{k}
the weights of the
generators. Let
f:Z\to X
be the weighted blowing-up of
X
with respect to
(l_{1},...,l_{k})
. Then
M:=f^{-1}(0)
is
a contact Fano manifold (cf. [Na 1, §2], [Na 3, Lemma 2.1]). As the LeBrun-Salamon conjecture holds true if
b_{2}(M)>1
or
\dim M\leq 5
, we are interested in
the case
b_{2}(M)=1
and
\dim M\geq 7
. Then, since
f
is a resolution such that
\mathrm{Exc}(f)
has 2-nd Betti number

1
,
X
must be
Q
-factorial with

\dim X\geq 8
. However, our examples
Y(A,0)
are all non-
Q
-factorial because they have non-trivial projective
Q
-factorial terminalizations. In this sense, it would be interesting to find a new example of a
Q
-factorial conical symplectic variety
X
of
\dim X\geq 8
with an isolated singularity.
