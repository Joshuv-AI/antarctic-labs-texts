A useful lemma for calculating the Hausdorff dimension of certain sets in Engel expansions
Lei Shang

Abstract
. Let { s n } \{s_{n}\} and { t n } \{t_{n}\} be two sequences of positive real numbers. Under some mild conditions on { s n } \{s_{n}\} and { t n } \{t_{n}\} , we give the precise formula of the Hausdorff dimension of the set 𝔼 ( { s n } , { t n } ) := { x ∈ ( 0 , 1 ) : s n < d n ( x ) ≤ s n + t n , ∀ n ≥ 1 } , \mathbb{E}(\{s_{n}\},\{t_{n}\}):=\Big\{x\in(0,1):s_{n}<d_{n}(x)\leq s_{n}+t_{n},\forall n\geq 1\Big\}, where d n ​ ( x ) d_{n}(x) denotes the digit of the Engel expansion of x x . This result improves the Lemma 2.6 of Shang and Wu (2021JNT), and is very useful for calculating the Hausdorff dimension of certain sets in Engel expansions.

1.
Introduction

Let
T:[0,1)\to[0,1)
be the
Engel expansion map
defined by
T(0):=0
and

T(x):=x\left\lceil\frac{1}{x}\right\rceil-1,\ \ \ \forall x\in(0,1),

where
\lceil y\rceil
denotes the least integer not less than
y
. Denote by
T^{k}
the
k
th iteration of
T
. For
x\in(0,1)
, if
x
is rational, then there exists
n\in\mathbb{N}
such that
T^{n}(x)=0
; if
x
is irrational, then
T^{n}(x)>0
for all
n\geq 1
. Then every irrational number
x\in(0,1)
admits an infinite series with the form

x=\frac{1}{d_{1}(x)}+\frac{1}{d_{1}(x)d_{2}(x)}+\cdots+\frac{1}{d_{1}(x)\cdots d_{n}(x)}+\cdots

(1.1)

by letting
d_{1}(x):=\lceil 1/x\rceil
and
d_{n+1}(x):=d_{1}(T^{n}(x))
for all
n\geq 1
. The expression (
1.1
) is called the
Engel expansion
of
x
and the integers
d_{n}(x)
are called the
digits
of the Engel expansion of
x
. It was remarked in
[
1
, p. 7]
that
d_{n+1}(x)\geq d_{n}(x)\geq 2
for all
n\geq 1
and
d_{n}(x)\to\infty
as
n\to\infty
.
We refer the reader to Galambos
[
4
]
for more information of Engel expansions.

Let
\{s_{n}\}
and
\{t_{n}\}
be sequences of positive real numbers. Write

\mathbb{E}(\{s_{n}\},\{t_{n}\}):=\Big\{x\in(0,1):s_{n}<d_{n}(x)\leq s_{n}+t_{n},\forall n\geq 1\Big\}.

We will prove the following result.

Lemma A
.

Assume that: (1)
s_{n}\geq t_{n}\geq 2
; (2)
s_{n+1}\geq s_{n}+t_{n}
; (3)
\lim_{n\to\infty}s_{n}=\infty
. Then

\displaystyle\dim_{\mathrm{H}}\mathbb{E}(\{s_{n}\},\{t_{n}\})=\liminf_{n\to\infty}\frac{\sum_{k=1}^{n}\log t_{k}}{\sum_{k=1}^{n+1}\log s_{k}+\log s_{n+1}-\log t_{n+1}}.

(1.2)

We remark that a similar result was obtained by Liao and Rams
[
6
]
for a class of infinite iterated function systems, while the Engel expansion system does not belong to their setting, see
[
5
,
6
]
.
We also point out that the result of Lemma A would be very useful for calculating the Hausdorff dimension of certain sets in Engel expansions, see for example
[
3
,
7
,
8
,
10
]
.

The paper is organized as follows. Section 2 is devoted to several definitions and basic properties of the Engel expansion. The proof of Lemma A will be given in Section 3.

2.
Preliminaries

Definition 2.1
.

A finite sequence
(\sigma_{1},\cdots,\sigma_{n})\in\mathbb{N}^{n}
is said to be
admissible
if there exists
x\in(0,1)
such that
d_{k}(x)=\sigma_{k}
for all
1\leq k\leq n
. An infinite sequence
(\sigma_{1},\cdots,\sigma_{k},\cdots)\in\mathbb{N}^{\mathbb{N}}
is said to be
admissible
if there exists
x\in(0,1)
such that for all
n\geq 1
,
d_{k}(x)=\sigma_{k},\forall 1\leq k\leq n
.

Denote by
\Sigma_{n}
the collection of all admissible sequences with length
n
and by
\Sigma
that of all infinite admissible sequences.
The following result gives a characterisation of admissible sequences.

Proposition 2.2

(
[
4
]
)
.

(\sigma_{1},\cdots,\sigma_{n})\in\Sigma_{n}
if and only if
2\leq\sigma_{1}\leq\cdots\leq\sigma_{n}
;

(\sigma_{1},\cdots,\sigma_{n},\cdots)\in\Sigma
if and only if

\sigma_{n+1}\geq\sigma_{n}\geq 2,\ \forall n\geq 1\ \ \ \ \ \text{and}\ \ \ \ \ \lim_{n\to\infty}\sigma_{n}=\infty.

Definition 2.3
.

Let
(\sigma_{1},\cdots,\sigma_{n})\in\Sigma_{n}
. We call

I_{n}(\sigma_{1},\cdots,\sigma_{n}):=\big\{x\in(0,1):d_{1}(x)=\sigma_{1},\cdots,d_{n}(x)=\sigma_{n}\big\}

the
cylinder
of order
n
associated to
(\sigma_{1},\dots,\sigma_{n})
.

We use
|I|
to denote the diameter of an interval
I
.

Proposition 2.4

(
[
4
]
)
.

Let
(\sigma_{1},\cdots,\sigma_{n})\in\Sigma_{n}
. Then
I_{n}(\sigma_{1},\cdots,\sigma_{n})=[A_{n},B_{n})
, where

A_{n}:=\frac{1}{\sigma_{1}}+\cdots+\frac{1}{\sigma_{1}\sigma_{2}\cdots\sigma_{n-1}}+\frac{1}{\sigma_{1}\sigma_{2}\cdots\sigma_{n-1}\sigma_{n}}

and

B:=\frac{1}{\sigma_{1}}+\cdots+\frac{1}{\sigma_{1}\sigma_{2}\cdots\sigma_{n-1}}+\frac{1}{\sigma_{1}\sigma_{2}\cdots\sigma_{n-1}(\sigma_{n}-1)}.

Moreover,

\left|I_{n}(\sigma_{1},\sigma_{2},\cdots,\sigma_{n})\right|=\frac{1}{\sigma_{1}\sigma_{2}\cdots\sigma_{n-1}\sigma_{n}(\sigma_{n}-1)}.

Proposition 2.5

(
[
2
, Proposition 4.1]
)
.

Suppose
\mathbb{F}
can be covered by
\mathcal{N}_{n}
sets of diameter at most
\delta_{n}
with
\delta_{n}\to 0
as
n\to\infty
. Then

\dim_{\rm H}\mathbb{F}\leq\liminf_{n\to\infty}\frac{\log\mathcal{N}_{n}}{-\log\delta_{n}}.

Proposition 2.6

(
[
2
, Example 4.6]
)
.

Let
[0,1]=\mathbb{E}_{0}\supset\mathbb{E}_{1}\supset\cdots
be a decreasing sequence of sets and
\mathbb{E}=\bigcap_{n\geq 0}\mathbb{E}_{n}
. Assume that each
\mathbb{E}_{n}
is a union of a finite number of disjoint closed intervals (called basic intervals of order
n
) and each basic interval in
\mathbb{E}_{n-1}
contains
m_{n}
intervals of
\mathbb{E}_{n}
which are separated by gaps of lengths at least
\varepsilon_{n}
.
If
m_{n}\geq 2
and
\varepsilon_{n-1}>\varepsilon_{n}>0
, then

\dim_{\mathrm{H}}\mathbb{E}\geq\liminf_{n\to\infty}\frac{\log(m_{1}m_{2}\cdots m_{n-1})}{-\log(m_{n}\varepsilon_{n})}.

3.
Proof of Lemma A

In this section, we will give the proof of Lemma A. Assume that: (1)
s_{n}\geq t_{n}\geq 2
; (2)
s_{n+1}\geq s_{n}+t_{n}
; (3)
\lim_{n\to\infty}s_{n}=\infty
. Let

\mathcal{D}_{n}:=\big\{(\sigma_{1},\cdots\sigma_{n})\in\mathbb{N}^{n}:s_{k}<\sigma_{k}\leq s_{k}+t_{k},\forall 1\leq k\leq n\big\}.

Note that
\sigma_{1}>s_{1}\geq 2
and
\sigma_{k+1}>s_{k+1}\geq s_{k}+t_{k}>\sigma_{k}
, so
\sigma_{n}\geq\cdots\geq\sigma_{1}\geq 2
. That is to say,
(\sigma_{1},\cdots\sigma_{n})
is admissible. For
(\sigma_{1},\cdots\sigma_{n})\in\mathcal{D}_{n}
, let

J_{n}(\sigma_{1},\cdots,\sigma_{n}):=\bigcup_{s_{n+1}<j\leq s_{n+1}+t_{n+1}}cl(I_{n+1}(\sigma_{1},\cdots,\sigma_{n},j)),

where
cl(\cdot)
denotes the closure of a set. By Proposition
2.4
, we know that
J_{n}(\sigma_{1},\cdots,\sigma_{n})
is a closed interval, which is called the
basic interval
of order
n
. Write

\mathbb{E}_{n}:=\bigcup_{(\sigma_{1},\cdots,\sigma_{n})\in\mathcal{D}_{n}}J_{n}(\sigma_{1},\cdots,\sigma_{n})

(3.1)

with the convention
\mathbb{E}_{0}:=[0,1]
. Then

\mathbb{E}(\{s_{n}\},\{t_{n}\})=\bigcap_{n=0}^{\infty}\mathbb{E}_{n}.

For the upper bound of
\dim_{\rm H}\mathbb{E}(\{s_{n}\},\{t_{n}\})
, for any
n\geq 1
, we derive from (
3.1
) that
\{J_{n}(\sigma_{1},\cdots,\sigma_{n}):(\sigma_{1},\cdots,\sigma_{n})\in\mathcal{D}_{n}\}
is a cover of
\mathbb{E}(\{s_{n}\},\{t_{n}\})
. Then

\displaystyle\mathcal{N}_{n}:=\#\mathcal{D}_{n}=(\lfloor s_{1}+t_{1}\rfloor-\lfloor s_{1}\rfloor)\cdots(\lfloor s_{n}+t_{n}\rfloor-\lfloor s_{n}\rfloor)\leq 2^{n}t_{1}\cdots t_{n}.

For any
(\sigma_{1},\cdots\sigma_{n})\in\mathcal{D}_{n}
, by Proposition
2.4
, we see that

\displaystyle|J_{n}(\sigma_{1},\cdots,\sigma_{n})|

\displaystyle=\sum_{j=\lfloor s_{n+1}\rfloor+1}^{\lfloor s_{n+1}+t_{n+1}\rfloor}|I(\sigma_{1},\cdots,\sigma_{n},j)|=\frac{1}{\sigma_{1}\cdots\sigma_{n}}\sum_{\lfloor s_{n+1}\rfloor+1}^{\lfloor s_{n+1}+t_{n+1}\rfloor}\frac{1}{j(j-1)}.

Note that
s_{n+1}-1\geq s_{n+1}/2
and
s_{n+1}+t_{n+1}\geq s_{n+1}
, so

\displaystyle\sum_{j=\lfloor s_{n+1}\rfloor+1}^{\lfloor s_{n+1}+t_{n+1}\rfloor}\frac{1}{j(j-1)}=\frac{1}{\lfloor s_{n+1}\rfloor}-\frac{1}{\lfloor s_{n+1}+t_{n+1}\rfloor}\leq\frac{1}{s_{n+1}-1}-\frac{1}{s_{n+1}+t_{n+1}}\leq\frac{4t_{n+1}}{s_{n+1}^{2}}.

Since
\sigma_{k}\geq s_{k}
, we have

\displaystyle|J_{n}(\sigma_{1},\cdots,\sigma_{n})|\leq\frac{1}{s_{1}\cdots s_{n}}\cdot\frac{4t_{n+1}}{s_{n+1}^{2}}=:\delta_{n}.

From Proposition
2.5
, we conclude that

\displaystyle\dim_{\mathrm{H}}\mathbb{E}(\{s_{n}\},\{t_{n}\})

\displaystyle\leq\liminf_{n\to\infty}\frac{\log\mathcal{N}_{n}}{-\log\delta_{n}}

\displaystyle\leq\liminf_{n\to\infty}\frac{n\log 2+\log(t_{1}\cdots t_{n})}{\log(s_{1}\cdots s_{n+1})+\log s_{n+1}-\log t_{n+1}-\log 4}

\displaystyle=\liminf_{n\to\infty}\frac{\sum_{k=1}^{n}\log t_{k}}{\sum_{k=1}^{n+1}\log s_{k}+\log s_{n+1}-\log t_{n+1}}.

For the lower bound of
\dim_{\rm H}\mathbb{E}(\{s_{n}\},\{t_{n}\})
, by the structure of basic intervals, we deduce that each basic interval of order
n-1
contains

\frac{t_{n}}{2}<\lfloor t_{n}\rfloor\leq m_{n}:=\lfloor s_{n}+t_{n}\rfloor-\lfloor s_{n}\rfloor<t_{n}+1<2t_{n}

basic intervals of order
n
. Next we will estimate the gaps between two basic intervals with the same order. For two adjacent sequences
(\sigma_{1},\cdots,\sigma_{n})
and
(\sigma^{\prime}_{1},\cdots,\sigma^{\prime}_{n})
in
\mathcal{D}_{n}
, we deduce that the cylinders
J_{n}(\sigma_{1},\cdots,\sigma_{n})
and
J_{n}(\sigma^{\prime}_{1},\cdots,\sigma^{\prime}_{n})
are different and then
J_{n}(\sigma_{1},\cdots,\sigma_{n})
is on the left-hand or right-hand side of
J_{n}(\sigma^{\prime}_{1},\cdots,\sigma^{\prime}_{n})
. Without loss of generality, we assume that
J_{n}(\sigma_{1},\cdots,\sigma_{n})
is on the left-hand side of
J_{n}(\sigma^{\prime}_{1},\cdots,\sigma^{\prime}_{n})
. Then

\Pi_{1}:=\bigcup_{\sigma_{n}\leq j\leq\lfloor s_{n+1}\rfloor}I_{n+1}(\sigma_{1},\cdots,\sigma_{n},j)\ \text{and}\ \Pi_{2}:=\bigcup_{j\geq\lfloor s_{n+1}+t_{n+1}\rfloor+1}I_{n+1}(\sigma^{\prime}_{1},\cdots,\sigma^{\prime}_{n},j)

are the gap of
J_{n}(\sigma_{1},\cdots,\sigma_{n})
and
J_{n}(\sigma^{\prime}_{1},\cdots,\sigma^{\prime}_{n})
. For
\Pi_{1}
, we have

\displaystyle|\Pi_{1}|

\displaystyle=\sum_{\sigma_{n}\leq j\leq\lfloor s_{n+1}\rfloor}|I_{n+1}(\sigma_{1},\cdots,\sigma_{n},j)|

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\sigma_{1}\cdots\sigma_{n}}\sum_{\sigma_{n}\leq j\leq\lfloor s_{n+1}\rfloor}\frac{1}{j(j-1)}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\sigma_{1}\cdots\sigma_{n}}\left(\frac{1}{\sigma_{n}-1}-\frac{1}{\lfloor s_{n+1}\rfloor}\right)

\displaystyle\geq\frac{1}{2s_{1}\cdots 2s_{n}}\left(\frac{1}{s_{n}+t_{n}-1}-\frac{1}{s_{n+1}-1}\right).

For
\Pi_{2}
, we obtain

\displaystyle|\Pi_{2}|

\displaystyle=\sum_{j\geq\lfloor s_{n+1}+t_{n+1}\rfloor+1}|I_{n+1}(\sigma^{\prime}_{1},\cdots,\sigma^{\prime}_{n},j)|

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\sigma^{\prime}_{1}\cdots\sigma^{\prime}_{n}}\sum_{j\geq\lfloor s_{n+1}+t_{n+1}\rfloor+1}\frac{1}{j(j-1)}

\displaystyle\geq\frac{1}{2s_{1}\cdots 2s_{n}}\cdot\frac{1}{s_{n+1}+t_{n+1}}.

Note that
s_{n+1}\geq s_{n}+t_{n}
, so

\displaystyle\frac{1}{s_{n}+t_{n}-1}-\frac{1}{s_{n+1}-1}+\frac{1}{s_{n+1}+t_{n+1}}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{s_{n}+t_{n}-1}-\frac{t_{n+1}+1}{(s_{n+1}-1)(s_{n+1}+t_{n+1})}

\displaystyle\geq\frac{1}{s_{n}+t_{n}-1}\cdot\left(1-\frac{t_{n+1}+1}{s_{n+1}+t_{n+1}}\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{s_{n}+t_{n}-1}\cdot\frac{s_{n+1}-1}{s_{n+1}+t_{n+1}}.

Since
s_{n}+t_{n}-1<2s_{n}
,
s_{n+1}-1\geq s_{n+1}/2
and
s_{n+1}+t_{n+1}\leq 2s_{n+1}
, we see that the length of the gap of
J(\sigma_{1},\cdots,\sigma_{n})
and
J(\sigma^{\prime}_{1},\cdots,\sigma^{\prime}_{n})
is at least

\displaystyle|\Pi_{1}|+|\Pi_{2}|

\displaystyle\geq\frac{1}{2s_{1}\cdots 2s_{n}}\cdot\frac{1}{s_{n}+t_{n}-1}\cdot\frac{s_{n+1}-1}{s_{n+1}+t_{n+1}}

\displaystyle\geq\frac{1}{2s_{1}\cdots 2s_{n}}\cdot\frac{1}{8s_{n}}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2^{n+3}}\cdot\frac{1}{s_{1}\cdots s_{n}s_{n}}:=\varepsilon_{n}.

It follows from Proposition
2.6
that

\displaystyle\dim_{\mathrm{H}}\mathbb{E}(\{s_{n}\},\{t_{n}\})

\displaystyle\geq\liminf_{n\to\infty}\frac{\log(m_{1}m_{2}\cdots m_{n-1})}{-\log(m_{n}\varepsilon_{n})}

\displaystyle\geq\liminf_{n\to\infty}\frac{-n\log 2+\sum_{k=1}^{n}\log t_{k}}{(n+5)\log 2+\sum_{k=1}^{n+1}\log s_{k}+\log s_{n+1}-\log t_{n+1}}

\displaystyle=\liminf_{n\to\infty}\frac{\sum_{k=1}^{n}\log t_{k}}{\sum_{k=1}^{n+1}\log s_{k}+\log s_{n+1}-\log t_{n+1}}.
