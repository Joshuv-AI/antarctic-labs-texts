Elemental Patterns from the Erdős Straus Conjecture
Kyle Bradford

Abstract
. This paper makes the following conjecture: For every prime p p there exists a positive integer x x with ⌈ p 4 ⌉ ≤ x ≤ ⌈ p 2 ⌉ \left\lceil\frac{p}{4}\right\rceil\leq x\leq\left\lceil\frac{p}{2}\right\rceil and a positive divisor d | x 2 d|x^{2} so that either (1) d mod ( 4 ​ x − p ) ≡ − p ​ x d\bmod\left(4x-p\right)\equiv-px or (2) d ≤ x d\leq x and d mod ( 4 ​ x − p ) ≡ − x d\bmod\left(4x-p\right)\equiv-x . Furthermore this paper proves that the solutions to these modular equations are in one-to-one correspondence with the solutions of the diophantine equation used in the Erdős Straus conjecture.

2.
New Results

The results in this paper are very subtle but quite illuminating. Ultimately, for each prime
p
, I build both necessary and sufficient conditions to describe the solutions of (
1
) solely through its smallest solution value,
x
. The following proposition and corollary derive a sufficient condition for finding type I solutions to (
1
).

Proposition 1
.

Suppose for a prime
p
there exists a positive integer
x
with
\left\lceil\frac{p}{4}\right\rceil\leq x\leq\left\lceil\frac{p}{2}\right\rceil
and a positive divisor
d|x^{2}
so that
d\bmod\left(4x-p\right)\equiv-px
.

Then letting

\displaystyle y

\displaystyle=\frac{px+d}{4x-p}

\displaystyle z

\displaystyle=\frac{p\left(x+p\left(\frac{x^{2}}{d}\right)\right)}{4x-p}

we see that
x,y
and
z
are positive integers with
x\leq y\leq z
and
p\nmid y
.

Corollary 1
.

Suppose for a prime
p
there exists a positive integer
x
with
\left\lceil\frac{p}{4}\right\rceil\leq x\leq\left\lceil\frac{p}{2}\right\rceil
and a positive divisor
d|x^{2}
so that
d\bmod\left(4x-p\right)\equiv-px
.

Then we have met a sufficient condition to find a type I solution to (
1
).

The following proposition and corollary derive a sufficient condition for finding type II solutions to (
1
).

Proposition 2
.

Suppose for a prime
p
there exists a positive integer
x
with
\left\lceil\frac{p}{4}\right\rceil\leq x\leq\left\lceil\frac{p}{2}\right\rceil
and a positive divisor
d|x^{2}
so that
d\leq x
and
d\bmod\left(4x-p\right)\equiv-x
.

Then letting

\displaystyle y

\displaystyle=\frac{p(x+d)}{4x-p}

\displaystyle z

\displaystyle=\frac{p\left(x+\frac{x^{2}}{d}\right)}{4x-p}

we see that
x,y
and
z
are positive integers with
x\leq y\leq z
and
p|y
.

Corollary 2
.

Suppose for a prime
p
there exists a positive integer
x
with
\left\lceil\frac{p}{4}\right\rceil\leq x\leq\left\lceil\frac{p}{2}\right\rceil
and a positive divisor
d|x^{2}
so that
d\leq x
and
d\bmod\left(4x-p\right)\equiv-x
.

Then we have met a sufficient condition to find a type II solution to (
1

The following two propositions derive the necessary conditions for finding type I and type II solutions to (
1

Proposition 3
.

Suppose for a prime
p
there exist positive integers
x\leq y\leq z
that satisfy (
1

p\nmid y
.

Then it is necessarily true that
\left\lceil\frac{p}{4}\right\rceil\leq x\leq\left\lceil\frac{p}{2}\right\rceil
and a positive divisor
d|x^{2}
exists so that

\displaystyle(1)

\displaystyle\quad d\bmod\left(4x-p\right)\equiv-px

\displaystyle(2)

\displaystyle\quad y=\frac{px+d}{4x-p}

\displaystyle(3)

\displaystyle\quad z=\frac{p\left(x+p\left(\frac{x^{2}}{d}\right)\right)}{4x-p}.

Proposition 4
.

Suppose for a prime
p
there exist positive integers
x\leq y\leq z
that satisfy (
1
) and
p|y
.

Then it is necessarily true that
\left\lceil\frac{p}{4}\right\rceil\leq x\leq\left\lceil\frac{p}{2}\right\rceil
and a positive divisor
d|x^{2}
exists so that

\displaystyle(1)

\displaystyle\quad d\leq x

\displaystyle(2)

\displaystyle\quad d\bmod\left(4x-p\right)\equiv-x

\displaystyle(3)

\displaystyle\quad y=\frac{p(x+d)}{4x-p}

\displaystyle(4)

\displaystyle\quad z=\frac{p\left(x+\frac{x^{2}}{d}\right)}{4x-p}.

Indeed I have now developed both the necessary and sufficient conditions for solving the Erdős Straus conjecture. The solutions are in one-to-one correspondence with the modular identities in propositions
1
and
2
. This is a key result because it reduces the conjecture to one dimension. That is to say, for every prime
p
we need to find at least one pair,
x
and
d

p
to prove the Erdős Straus conjecture. I summarize this in the following conjecture.

Conjecture 1
.

For every prime
p
there exists a positive integer
x
with
\left\lceil\frac{p}{4}\right\rceil\leq x\leq\left\lceil\frac{p}{2}\right\rceil
and a positive divisor
d|x^{2}
so that either

(1)
d\bmod\left(4x-p\right)\equiv-px

or

(2)
d\leq x
and
d\bmod\left(4x-p\right)\equiv-x
.

Proof.

Proposition
1

Let
p
be a prime,
x
be a positive integer with
\left\lceil\frac{p}{4}\right\rceil\leq x\leq\left\lceil\frac{p}{2}\right\rceil
and
d
be a positive divisor
d|x^{2}
so that
d\bmod\left(4x-p\right)\equiv-px
.

It should be clear that
p\neq 2
because if
p=2
, then both
x
and
d
must be
1
by definition and we cannot have
1\bmod 2\equiv 0
.

First, note that
x
is a positive integer by definition.

Next, note that if
d\bmod\left(4x-p\right)\equiv-px
, then
(px+d)\bmod\left(4x-p\right)\equiv 0
. This implies that
(4x-p)|(px+d)
. By definition,
p,x
and
d
are all positive, so
px+d
is positive. Because
\left\lceil\frac{p}{4}\right\rceil\leq x\leq\left\lceil\frac{p}{2}\right\rceil
, it should be clear that
4x-p
is also positive. Letting

y=\frac{px+d}{4x-p}

we see that
y
is a positive integer. We also see that
p\nmid y
. To show this we assume, for the sake of contradiction, that
p|y
. This implies that
p|(px+d)
which further implies that
p|d
. If
p|d
, then
p|x^{2}
; however, from the definition of
x
, we see that
x<p
.
p
cannot divide
x
or
x^{2}
. This creates a contradiction, implying that
p\nmid y
.

Finally, note that
\left(\frac{p^{2}x^{2}}{d}\right)d=p^{2}x^{2}=(-px)^{2}
in
\mathbb{Z}
, so we get the following modular equation:

(2)

\left(\frac{p^{2}x^{2}}{d}\right)\bmod(4x-p)\cdot d\bmod(4x-p)\equiv(-px)\bmod(4x-p)\cdot(-px)\bmod(4x-p)

Recall that
d\bmod(4x-p)\equiv-px
, so this equation becomes:

(3)

\left(\frac{p^{2}x^{2}}{d}\right)\bmod(4x-p)\cdot(-px)\bmod(4x-p)\equiv(-px)\bmod(4x-p)\cdot(-px)\bmod(4x-p)

When
p\neq 2
, we see that
-px
and
4x-p
are coprime. To show this let
m
be a positive integer so that
m|(-px)
and
m|(4x-p)
. We necessarily see that
m
must divide
(-4)(-px)+(-p)(4x-p)=p^{2}
. This makes
m=1,p
or
p^{2}
. For sake of contradiction, assume that
m=p
. This implies that
p|(4x-p)
, which further implies that
p|x
, but this is impossible as
x\leq\left\lceil\frac{p}{2}\right\rceil
. Next, for the sake of contradiction, assume that
m=p^{2}
. This implies that
p^{2}|(-px)
, which further implies that
p|x
. Again, this is impossible. We conclude that
m=1
, so we have that
-px
and
4x-p
are indeed coprime. We see that
(-px)\bmod(4x-p)
is a unit, with an inverse element in the group
\left(\mathbb{Z}/\penalty(4x-p)\mathbb{Z}\right)^{\times}
. Applying this inverse element on the right to either side of (
3
), we have that
\left(\frac{p^{2}x^{2}}{d}\right)\bmod(4x-p)\equiv-px
. We see then that
\left(px+\left(\frac{p^{2}x^{2}}{d}\right)\right)\bmod(4x-p)\equiv 0
. This implies that
(4x-p)|\left(px+\left(\frac{p^{2}x^{2}}{d}\right)\right)
. Note that
p,x,d
and
4x-p
are positive integers. Letting

z=\frac{p\left(x+p\left(\frac{x^{2}}{d}\right)\right)}{4x-p}

we see that
z
is a positive integer.

To finish this proof we show that
x\leq y\leq z
.

First consider when
x<\left\lceil\frac{p}{2}\right\rceil
. We see that

x\leq\frac{p}{2}+\frac{d}{4x}

This implies that
4x^{2}<2px+d
,
x(4x-p)<px+d
and

x<\frac{px+d}{4x-p}\leq y

Next consider when
x=\left\lceil\frac{p}{2}\right\rceil
. Because
p\neq 2
, we have that
x=\frac{p+1}{2}
. We see that

\displaystyle y

\displaystyle=\frac{p\left(\frac{p+1}{2}\right)+d}{4\left(\frac{p+1}{2}\right)-p}

\displaystyle=\frac{p(p+1)+2d}{2(p+2)}

\displaystyle=\frac{p+1}{2}+\frac{d-(p+1)}{p+2}

Because
y
is an integer, we see that
(p+2)|(d-(p+1))
. For
0<d<(p+1)
, we see that

-1<\frac{d-(p+1)}{p+2}<0

This implies that
d\geq(p+1)
and
x=\frac{p+1}{2}\leq y
.

In either scenario we are guaranteed to have
x\leq y
.

Because
d|x^{2}
, we see that
d\leq x^{2}\leq px
. This implies that
d^{2}\leq p^{2}x^{2}
. We see then that

d\leq\left(\frac{p^{2}x^{2}}{d}\right)

We see then that

\frac{px+d}{4x-p}\leq\frac{px+\left(\frac{p^{2}x^{2}}{d}\right)}{4x-p}

We see that
y\leq z
.

Proposition
3

Let
p
be prime and let
x\leq y\leq z
be positive integers that satisfy (
1
) with
p\nmid y
.

First, it was shown in
[
3
]
that a necessary condition for type I solutions is that
\left\lceil\frac{p}{4}\right\rceil\leq x\leq\left\lceil\frac{p}{2}\right\rceil
.

Next, slightly changing the notation in
[
3
]

m=\gcd(x,y,z),a=\gcd(x,y)/m,b=\gcd(x,z)/m
and
c=\gcd(y,z)/m

For this type I solution, let

.

In
[

]
it was shown for type I solutions that

p=\frac{4abcm-a}{b+c}.

This makes

\displaystyle d

\displaystyle=(4x-p)y-px
