1.
Introduction

The Hardy inequality plays an important role in analysis and in the theory of partial differential equations. In
\mathbb{R}^{N},
it reads as follows:

(1)

\int\limits_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}\frac{|u|^{p}}{|x|^{p}}\leq c\int\limits_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}|\nabla u|^{p},\quad\forall\ u\in C_{c}^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}^{N}),

where the constant
c
is independent of
u.
When we restrict
u
to be in the space
W_{0}^{1,p}(\mathbb{R}^{N})
, with
p<N,
or in
W_{0}^{1,p}(\mathbb{R}^{N}\setminus\{0\})
, with
p\geq N
, (
1
) holds; in that case the smallest possible choice for
c
becomes
\left(\frac{p}{N-p}\right)^{p},
and this constant is never achieved (see for example
[
8
,
2
]
). Hence there is a scope to get an improvement in the above inequality. Brezis-Vázquez
[
8
]
have shown for the case
p=2
, that we can add an
L^{2}
-term in the left hand side of (
1
) even with the best constant. This was further generalized in many directions, for example, see
[
2
,
5
,
3
,
1
,
7
,
10
,
12
,
4
,
25
,
15
,
24
,
16
]
.

There are many results in literature on this subject in the context of a complete Riemannian manifolds
(M,g).
An important result in this direction is due to Kombe-Oz̈aydin
[
18
]
. They proved that for a nonnegative function
\rho
with
|\nabla\rho|_{g}=1
and
\Delta\rho\geq\frac{C}{\rho}
, the following holds

\left(\frac{C+1+\alpha-p}{p}\right)^{p}\int\limits_{M}\frac{|u|^{p}\rho^{\alpha}}{\rho^{p}}\leq\int\limits_{M}|\nabla u|_{g}^{p}\rho^{\alpha}\quad\mbox{ for any }u\in C_{c}^{\infty}(M).

Moreover, for a bounded domain
\Omega
with smooth boundary and in the case
p=2
, they also proved that the above inequality still holds for any
u
in
C_{c}^{\infty}(\Omega)
, if we add a reminder term of the form
C_{1}\left(\int\limits_{\Omega}|\nabla u|^{q}\rho^{q\alpha/2}\right)^{2/q}
in the left hand side of the equation, where
1<q<2
and
C=C(N,q,\Omega)
. D’Ambrosio-Dipierro
[
13
]
proved another version of Hardy inequality, where the restriction
|\nabla\rho|=1
is not there. They showed that if
\Omega
is an open set in
M
and there is a
\rho:\Omega\to[0,\infty)
such that
\rho\in W^{1,p}_{loc}(\Omega)
with
\Delta_{p}\rho\leq 0
weakly, then
\frac{|\nabla\rho|}{\rho}\in L^{p}_{loc}
, and for any
u\in C_{c}^{\infty}(\Omega)
, the following holds:

\left(\frac{p-1}{p}\right)^{p}\int\limits_{\Omega}\frac{|u|^{p}|\nabla\rho|_{g}^{p}}{\rho^{p}}\leq\int\limits_{\Omega}|\nabla u|_{g}^{p}.

They discussed, in details, the advantage and applications of this kind of Hardy inequality.
For other related results, the reader may refer to
[
19
,
26
,
20
,
23
,
14
,
6
]
and the references therein.
As of now, there is no known result regarding Hardy inequality with a reminder term as prescribed by D’Ambrosio-Dipierro
[
13
]
. We wish to address this problem in this paper by proving a slightly more general version of the result.

An interesting feature of our method is that the results of
[
13
]
and
[
18
]
follow immediately. Also the proof becomes much simpler (see
Theorem

1
) provided no reminder term is expected. The formulation allows greater control over the constant, and, at least in the Euclidean case, we can get the inequality with the best constants. Here we mainly focus on the improved Hardy inequality in all its generality. We refrain ourselves from studying the special cases exclusively, although that is very much possible to use our results to achieve improvement of the inequalities obtained in
[
13
]
.

Throughout the article,
(M,g)
stands for a fixed oriented Riemannian manifold. We shall often use the notation
\left<X,Y\right>
to denote
g(X,Y)
for any two vector fields
X
and
Y
. We use the symbol
dV_{g}
to denote the volume form, however, the symbol will be often dropped when there is no scope for confusion.

Now, we state the main results of this paper, which we shall prove in Section 2. The first one is the following Hardy inequality, which, in the particular case
\alpha=-\beta
, is proven in
[
13
]
. However, our proof is much more simpler.

Theorem 1
.

Let
1<p<\infty
,
\alpha,\beta\in\mathbb{R}
,
M
be a complete Riemannian manifold,
\Omega
be a domain in
M
with boundary
\partial\Omega
(possibly empty). Let there exist a function
\rho:\Omega\to(0,\infty)
with
|\nabla\rho|_{g}\rho^{\frac{-p+\alpha}{p}}\in L^{p}_{loc}(\Omega)
such that

(2)

\frac{1}{p-1+\beta}\int\limits_{\Omega}\left<\nabla\xi,|\nabla\rho|^{p-2}\nabla\rho\right>\rho^{-p+1+\alpha}\geq\int\limits_{\Omega}\frac{|\nabla\rho|^{p}}{\rho^{p-\alpha}}\xi,\quad\forall\ \xi\in C_{c}^{1}(\Omega)\mbox{ with }\xi\geq 0.

Then, for any
u\in W^{1,p}_{0}(\Omega)
, we have the following inequality:

\int\limits_{\Omega}|\nabla u|_{g}^{p}\rho^{\alpha}dV_{g}\geq\left(\frac{|p-1+\beta|}{p}\right)^{p}\int\limits_{\Omega}\frac{|u|^{p}|\nabla\rho|_{g}^{p}}{\rho^{p}}\rho^{\alpha}dV_{g}.

Theorem 2
.

Let
p,\alpha,\beta,M,\Omega
and
\rho
be as in
Theorem

1
. Let the constant
C=C(p)
be as in
Lemma

10
for
p\geq 2
and in the case
p<2
,
C(p):=2^{p-3}p(p-1)
.
Further, in the case
p<2
, assume that for any
\xi\in C_{c}^{1}(\Omega)\mbox{ with }\xi\geq 0

(3)

\begin{cases}\rho^{\frac{-p+1-\beta}{p}}\in W^{1,p}_{loc}(\Omega),\\
(p-1+\beta)\int\limits_{\Omega}\left<\nabla\xi,|\nabla\rho|^{p-2}\nabla\rho\right>\rho^{\alpha+\beta}\geq\int\limits_{\Omega}\frac{|\nabla\rho|^{p}}{\rho}\rho^{\alpha+\beta}\xi.\end{cases}

If there exist functions

V:\Omega\to[0,\infty)
and
\varphi\in W^{1,p}_{loc}(\Omega)
such that for any
\xi\in C_{c}^{1}(\Omega)
,

(4)

\int\limits_{\Omega}\left<|\nabla\varphi|^{p-2}\nabla\varphi,\nabla\xi\right>\varphi^{-p+1}\rho^{\alpha}-(p-1)\int\limits_{\Omega}|\nabla\varphi|^{p}\xi\rho^{\alpha}\varphi^{-p}-(p-1+\beta)\int\limits_{\Omega}\left<|\nabla\varphi|^{p-2}\nabla\varphi,\nabla\rho\right>\xi\varphi^{-p+1}\rho^{-1+\alpha}\\
\geq\int\limits_{\Omega}\frac{V}{C(p)}\xi\rho^{\alpha},

then for any
u\in W^{1,p}_{0}(\Omega)
, we have the following inequality:

(5)

\int\limits_{\Omega}|\nabla u|_{g}^{p}\rho^{\alpha}dV_{g}\geq\left(\frac{|p-1+\beta|}{p}\right)^{p}\int\limits_{\Omega}\frac{|u|^{p}|\nabla\rho|_{g}^{p}}{|\rho|^{p}}\rho^{\alpha}dV_{g}+\int\limits_{\Omega}V|u|^{p}\rho^{\alpha}dV_{g}.

Before moving further, let us observe that the hypotheses (
2
), (
3
), (
4
) can be thought of as weak formulations of certain problems; we explain this in the following using the Green’s theorem (see
Lemma

9
).
Consider the following condition on
\rho
:

(6)

\frac{-1}{p-1+\beta}\Delta_{p}\rho\geq\frac{\alpha+\beta}{p-1+\beta}\frac{|\nabla\rho|^{p}}{\rho}.

In the special case
\alpha=-\beta
, this is just the
p
-superharmonicity condition. We rewrite this as

\frac{-1}{p-1+\beta}\rho^{-p+1+\alpha}\Delta_{p}\rho+\frac{p-1-\alpha}{p-1+\beta}\rho^{-p+\alpha}|\nabla\rho|^{p}\geq\frac{|\nabla\rho|^{p}}{\rho^{p-\alpha}},

and then multiply both sides by a test function
\xi\in C_{c}^{1}(\Omega)
, integrate over
\Omega
, and then apply the product rule of divergence operator to get

\frac{-1}{p-1+\beta}\int\limits_{\Omega}\mbox{div}\left(\rho^{-p+1+\alpha}|\nabla\rho|^{p-2}\nabla\rho\right)\xi\geq\int\limits_{\Omega}\frac{|\nabla\rho|^{p}}{\rho^{p-\alpha}}\xi.

An application of Green’s theorem then gives (
2
). Thus (
2
) is an weak formulation of (
6
). Similarly, the second condition of (
3
) can be interpreted as the weak formulation of

\frac{-1}{p-1+\beta}\Delta_{p}\rho\geq\frac{p-1+\alpha+2\beta}{p-1+\beta}\frac{|\nabla\rho|^{p}}{\rho},

and (
4
) can be regarded as a weak formulation of

\Delta_{p}\varphi+(p-1+\alpha+\beta)\left<|\nabla\varphi|^{p-2}\nabla\varphi,\frac{\nabla\rho}{\rho}\right>+\frac{V}{C(p)}\varphi^{p-1}\leq 0.

Now, we discuss some immediate consequences of
Theorem

2
. The following results says that if
\left(\frac{|p-1+\beta|}{p}\right)^{p}
is the best constant in (
5
), then (
4
) has no solution when
V=\frac{|\nabla\rho|^{p}}{\rho^{p}}
.

Corollary 3
.

Let
p,\alpha,\beta,\rho
be as in
Theorem

2
, and
V=C\frac{|\nabla\rho|}{\rho}
be such that there exists some
\varphi
for which (
4
) is satisfied. Then the constant
\left(\frac{|p-1+\beta|}{p}\right)^{p}
in (
5
) is not sharp.

Remark 4
.

The above result says nothing about when the constant
\left(\frac{|p-1+\beta|}{p}\right)^{p}
in (
5
) is sharp. However it is easy to see that the constant is sharp if and only if we can never have
V=C\frac{\nabla\rho}{\rho}
in (
5
) for any
C>0
. So the question of whether the constant is sharp is related to necessity of (
4
) in (
5
); whereas result
Theorem

2
concerns with the sufficiency part. In some very particular case the condition may also be necessary as can be seen from
[
22
, Theorem 1]
.

We discuss some special cases of
Theorem

2
. In the Euclidean setup, we get the following results as a corollary. Let us take
M=\mathbb{R}^{N}
,
\rho=|x|^{d}
. Then
\nabla\rho=d|x|^{d-2}x
and we get the following corollary:

Corollary 5
.

Assume
1<p<\infty
,
d\neq 0
,
\alpha,\beta\in\mathbb{R}
be such that
p-1+\beta\neq 0
,

\frac{\alpha+\beta}{p-1+\beta}\leq 0\quad\mbox{ and }\quad\frac{(N+(d-1)(p-1))}{d(p-1+\beta)}\leq 0,

and

\frac{(N+(d-1)(p-1))}{d(p-1+\beta)}\leq-1\quad\mbox{ when }p<2.

Assume further that there exists a function
V:\mathbb{R}^{N}\to\mathbb{R}
such that the following problem admits a weak solution
\varphi\in W^{1,p}_{loc}(\Omega)
:

(7)

\Delta_{p}\varphi+\frac{d(p-1+\alpha+\beta)}{|x|^{2}}|\nabla\varphi|^{p-2}\nabla\varphi\cdot x+\frac{V}{C(p)}\varphi^{p-1}\leq 0.

Consider the two cases:

(i)
\Omega\subseteq\mathbb{R}^{N}
,
p<N+\alpha d
with

(p-1+\beta)d<N-p\quad\mbox{ when }p<2,

and

(ii)
\Omega\subseteq\mathbb{R}^{N}\setminus\{0\}
,
p\geq N+\alpha d
.

Then, in both cases, we have the following inequality:

\int\limits_{\Omega}|\nabla u|_{g}^{p}|x|^{\alpha d}dx\\
\geq\left(\frac{|d(p-1+\beta)|}{p}\right)^{p}\int\limits_{\Omega}\frac{|u|^{p}}{|x|^{p-\alpha d}}dx+\int\limits_{\Omega}V|u|^{p}|x|^{\alpha d},\,\,\forall\,u\in W^{1,p}_{0}(\Omega).

Remark that in the case
\alpha=\beta=0,d=-1
and
\Omega=\mathbb{R}^{N}
,
[
9
, Proposition 1.2]
implies that (
7
) has no solution in
\mathbb{R}^{N}
for any
1<p<N
.

Now, let us consider the so-called critical case:
p=N\geq 2
. Set
\Omega:=B_{1}(0)\subset\mathbb{R}^{N}
,
\rho=-\log|x|
. We have

Corollary 6
.

Let
N\geq 2
,
B_{1}(0)
denote the unit ball in
\mathbb{R}^{N}
,
\alpha,\beta\in\mathbb{R}
be such that
p-1+\beta\neq 0
and

\frac{\alpha+\beta}{N-1+\beta}\leq 0.

Assume further that there exists a function
V:B_{1}(0)\subset\mathbb{R}^{N}\to\mathbb{R}
such that the following problem admits a weak solution
\varphi\in W^{1,p}_{loc}(B_{1}(0)):

\Delta_{N}\varphi-\frac{(N-1+\alpha+\beta)|\nabla\varphi|^{N-2}}{|x|^{2}\log x}\nabla\varphi\cdot x+\frac{V}{C(N)}\leq 0.

Then for any
u\in W^{1,N}_{0}(B_{1}(0))
, we have

\int\limits_{B_{1}(0)}|\nabla u|^{N}|\log|x||^{\alpha}dx\geq\left(\frac{|N-1+\beta|}{N}\right)^{N}\int\limits_{B_{1}(0)}\frac{|u|^{N}}{|x|^{N}|\log|x||^{N-\alpha}}dx\\
+\int\limits_{B_{1}(0)}V|u|^{N}|\log|x||^{\alpha}dx.

Consider the case
M=\mathbb{H}^{N}:=\mathbb{R}^{N-1}\times(0,\infty)
and set
\rho(x):=x_{N}
. This gives
|\nabla\rho|=1
and
\Delta_{p}\rho=0
. This implies

Corollary 7
.

Let
1<p<\infty
,
\alpha,\beta\in\mathbb{R}
. Let the constant
C=C(p)
be as in
Theorem

2
. Assume

(\alpha+\beta)(p-1+\beta)\leq 0.

Further, in the case
p<2
, assume that

(p-1+\alpha+2\beta)(p-1+\beta)\leq 0.

If there exists a positive
p
-harmonic function

\varphi\in W^{1,p}_{loc}(\mathbb{H}^{N})
such that
(p-1+\alpha+\beta)\frac{\partial\varphi}{\partial x_{N}}\leq 0
,
then for any
u\in W^{1,p}_{0}(\mathbb{H}^{N})
, we have the following inequality:

\int\limits_{\mathbb{H}^{N}}|\nabla u|_{g}^{p}x_{N}^{\alpha}dx\geq\left(\frac{|p-1+\beta|}{p}\right)^{p}\int\limits_{\mathbb{H}^{N}}\frac{|u|^{p}}{x_{N}^{p-\alpha}}dx\\
-C(p)(p-1+\alpha+\beta)\int\limits_{\mathbb{H}^{N}}\frac{\partial\varphi}{\partial x_{N}}\frac{|\nabla\varphi|^{p-2}}{\varphi^{p-1}}x_{N}^{\alpha+1}|u|^{p}dx.

Remark 8
.

The conditions (
2
) in
Theorem

1
and (
4
) in
Theorem

2
may seem artificially imposed at first glance. However, in the most commonly used form of Hardy inequality, that is in the setup of

with

, (

) holds automatically with proper choice of

. In case of Riemannian manifolds validity of

]
.

In the particular case

,
\Omega=\mathbb{R}^{N}
, and when

is radial, (
4
) is actually a necessary condition too for

to hold. This can be seen from some symmetrization argument (to reduce the condition to its one-dimensional analogue) and

.

2.
Proof of the theorems

The proof of the following lemma can be found in
[
11
, Theorem III.7.6.]
for the case
p=2
. The proof of this version can also be done similarly as the essence of the proof lies in the Stokes theorem and in the product rule of divergence operator:

\mbox{div}(fX)=f\mbox{div}(X)+\left<\nabla f,X\right>.

Lemma 9

(Green’s Formula)
.

Let
p>1
,
M
be complete, oriented Riemannian manifold,
\Omega
a domain in

M
with smooth boundary. Let
f\in C^{2}(\Omega)
,
\xi\in C_{c}^{1}(\Omega)
. Then, we have

\int\limits_{\Omega}<|\nabla f|^{p-2}\nabla f,\nabla\xi>=-\int\limits_{\Omega}\xi\Delta_{p}f.

The following result plays a key role in the proof of the theorem.

Lemma 10
.

Let
x\in M
and
X_{x},Y_{x}\in T_{x}M
be two tangent vectors. Then, for
p\geq 2
, there is a constant
C=C(p)>0
such that

|X_{x}+Y_{x}|_{g}^{p}-|X_{x}|_{g}^{p}\geq C(p)|Y_{x}|_{g}^{p}+p|X_{x}|_{g}^{p-2}<X_{x},Y_{x}>,

and for
1\leq p\leq 2
,

|X_{x}+Y_{x}|_{g}^{p}-|X_{x}|_{g}^{p}\geq\frac{p(p-1)}{2}\frac{|Y_{x}|^{2}}{||X_{x}|+|Y_{x}||^{2-p}}+p|X_{x}|^{p-2}<X_{x},Y_{x}>.

In general, for
1<p<\infty
, we have

|X_{x}+Y_{x}|_{g}^{p}-|X_{x}|_{g}^{p}\geq p|X_{x}|^{p-2}<X_{x},Y_{x}>.

Proof.

For the case
p\geq 2
, refer to
[
21
, Chapter 12]
and recall that any
N
-dimensional Hilbert space is isomorphic to
\mathbb{R}^{N}
. We give a proof of the case
1\leq p\leq 2
, which is adapted from
[
17
, Lemma 1]
.

In the following calculation, we omit the point
x
for better readability. Consider the function

f(t):=|X+tY|^{p}.

It can be easily verified that, for
t\in(0,1)
,
f^{\prime\prime}(t)\geq p(p-1)|Y|^{2}|X+tY|^{p-2}
.
Using this, Taylor’s theorem, the fact that
p-2\leq 0
, we get

\displaystyle|X+Y|^{p}=\

\displaystyle f(1)

\displaystyle=\

\displaystyle f(0)+f^{\prime}(0)+\int\limits_{0}^{1}(1-t)f^{\prime\prime}(t)dt

\displaystyle\geq\

\displaystyle|X|^{p}+p|X|^{p-2}\left<X,Y\right>

\displaystyle+p(p-1)\int\limits_{0}^{1}(1-t)|Y|^{2}|X+tY|^{p-2}dt

\displaystyle\geq\

\displaystyle|X|^{p}+p|X|^{p-2}\left<X,Y\right>+\frac{p(p-1)}{2}|Y|^{2}||X|+|Y||^{p-2}.

This proves the lemma. ∎

We now present the proof of our first main result.

Proof of
Theorem

1
.

By a density argument, we can always assume that
u\in C_{c}^{1}(\Omega)
. Set
w(x)=u(x)\rho^{\frac{-p+1-\beta}{p}}(x)
in
\Omega
. Then

\nabla u=\rho^{\frac{p-1+\beta}{p}}\nabla w+\frac{p-1+\beta}{p}\rho^{\frac{-1+\beta}{p}}w\nabla\rho.

Note that if
\int\limits_{\Omega}|\nabla u(x)|_{g}^{p}\rho^{\alpha}dV_{g}=\infty
, then we have nothing to prove, so we assume it to be finite. In the following calculation, we need the term
\frac{|u|^{p}|\nabla\rho|_{g}^{p}}{\rho^{p-\alpha}}
to be integrable over
\Omega
; this is indeed true, as
u\in C_{c}^{1}(\Omega)
and
\frac{|\nabla\rho|_{g}^{p}}{\rho^{p-\alpha}}\in L^{1}_{loc}(\Omega)
according to hypothesis. Using
Lemma

10
and (
2
), we have

\displaystyle\int\limits_{\Omega}

\displaystyle|\nabla u(x)|_{g}^{p}\rho^{\alpha}dV_{g}-\left(\frac{|p-1+\beta|}{p}\right)^{p}\int\limits_{\Omega}\frac{|u(x)|^{p}|\nabla\rho(x)|_{g}^{p}}{|\rho(x)|^{p}}\rho^{\alpha}dV_{g}

\displaystyle=\

\displaystyle\int\limits_{\Omega}\left(\left|\rho^{\frac{p-1+\beta}{p}}\nabla w+\frac{p-1+\beta}{p}\frac{w\nabla\rho}{\rho^{\frac{1-\beta}{p}}}\right|^{p}_{g}-\left(\frac{|p-1+\beta|}{p}\right)^{p}\left|\frac{w\nabla\rho}{\rho^{\frac{1-\beta}{p}}}\right|_{g}^{p}\right)\rho^{\alpha}

\displaystyle\geq\

\displaystyle p\left(\frac{|p-1+\beta|}{p}\right)^{p-2}\left(\frac{p-1+\beta}{p}\right)\int\limits_{\Omega}\left|\frac{w\nabla\rho}{\rho^{\frac{1-\beta}{p}}}\right|^{p-2}\left<\rho^{\frac{p-1+\beta}{p}}\nabla w,\frac{w\nabla\rho}{\rho^{\frac{1-\beta}{p}}}\right>\rho^{\alpha}

\displaystyle=\

\displaystyle\left(\frac{|p-1+\beta|}{p}\right)^{p-2}\left(\frac{p-1+\beta}{p}\right)\int\limits_{\Omega}\left<\nabla|w|^{p},|\nabla\rho|^{p-2}\nabla\rho\right>\rho^{\alpha+\beta}

\displaystyle=\

\displaystyle\left(\frac{|p-1+\beta|}{p}\right)^{p-2}\left(\frac{p-1+\beta}{p}\right)\int\limits_{\Omega}\left<\nabla(\rho^{-p+1-\beta}|u|^{p}),|\nabla\rho|^{p-2}\nabla\rho\right>\rho^{\alpha+\beta}

\displaystyle=\

\displaystyle\left(\frac{|p-1+\beta|}{p}\right)^{p-2}\left(\frac{p-1+\beta}{p}\right)\int\limits_{\Omega}\left<\nabla|u|^{p},|\nabla\rho|^{p-2}\nabla\rho\right>\rho^{-p+1+\alpha}

\displaystyle-\left(\frac{|p-1+\beta|}{p}\right)^{p}p\int\limits_{\Omega}\frac{|\nabla\rho|^{p}}{\rho^{p-\alpha}}|u|^{p}

\displaystyle\geq\

\displaystyle 0.

In the last line of the above calculation, we have used the hypothesis by using the fact that
|u|^{p}\in C_{c}^{1}(\Omega)
as
p>1
.
∎

Next, we proceed with the proof of our second main result.

Proof of
Theorem

2
.

As in the proof of
Theorem

1
, we shall assume

and we set

We need to estimate the term

\begin{split}I:=\ &\int\limits_{\Omega}|\nabla u(x)|_{g}^{p}\rho^{\alpha}dV_{g}\\
&-\left(\frac{|p-1+\beta|}{p}\right)^{p}\int\limits_{\Omega}\frac{|u(x)|^{p}|\nabla\rho(x)|_{g}^{p}}{|\rho(x)|^{p}}\rho^{\alpha}dV_{g}\\
=\ &\int\limits_{\Omega}\left(\left|\rho^{\frac{p-1+\beta}{p}}\nabla w+\left(\frac{p-1+\beta}{p}\right)\frac{w\nabla\rho}{\rho^{\frac{1-\beta}{p}}}\right|^{p}_{g}\right.\\
&\left.-\left(\frac{|p-1+\beta|}{p}\right)^{p}\left|\frac{w\nabla\rho}{\rho^{\frac{1-\beta}{p}}}\right|_{g}^{p}\right)\rho^{\alpha}.\end{split}

Let
p\geq 2
. Using
Lemma

10
and (

), we get

\displaystyle I\geq\

\displaystyle C(p)\int\limits_{\Omega}\left|\rho^{\frac{p-1+\beta}{p}}\nabla w\right|^{p}\rho^{\alpha}+p\left(\frac{|p-1+\beta|}{p}\right)^{p-2}\left(\frac{p-1+\beta}{p}\right)

\displaystyle\times\int\limits_{\Omega}\left|\frac{w\nabla\rho}{\rho^{\frac{1-\beta}{p}}}\right|^{p-2}\left<\rho^{\frac{p-1+\beta}{p}}\nabla w,\frac{w\nabla\rho}{\rho^{\frac{1-\beta}{p}}}\right>\rho^{\alpha}

\displaystyle=\

\displaystyle C(p)\int\limits_{\Omega}\left|\nabla w\right|^{p}\rho^{p-1+\alpha+\beta}+\left(\frac{|p-1+\beta|}{p}\right)^{p-2}\left(\frac{p-1+\beta}{p}\right)

\displaystyle\times\int\limits_{\Omega}\left<\nabla|w|^{p},|\nabla\rho|^{p-2}\nabla\rho\right>\rho^{\alpha+\beta}

(8)

\displaystyle\geq\

\displaystyle C(p)\int\limits_{\Omega}\left|\nabla w\right|^{p}\rho^{p-1+\alpha+\beta}.

The last line in the above calculation follows from (
2
). Now, set
\psi=\frac{w}{\varphi}
. Then
\nabla w=\varphi\nabla\psi+\psi\nabla\varphi
. We have, from
Lemma

10
and (
4
),

\displaystyle\int\limits_{\Omega}

\displaystyle\rho^{p-1+\alpha+\beta}|\nabla w|_{g}^{p}

\displaystyle=\

\displaystyle\int\limits_{\Omega}\rho^{p-1+\alpha+\beta}|\varphi\nabla\psi+\psi\nabla\varphi|_{g}^{p}

\displaystyle\geq\

\displaystyle C(p)\int\limits_{\Omega}\rho^{p-1+\alpha+\beta}|\varphi\nabla\psi|_{g}^{p}

\displaystyle+\int\limits_{\Omega}p\rho^{p-1+\alpha+\beta}|\psi\nabla\varphi|^{p-2}<\psi\nabla\varphi,\varphi\nabla\psi>+\int\limits_{\Omega}\rho^{p-1+\alpha+\beta}|\psi\nabla\varphi|_{g}^{p}

\displaystyle\geq\

\displaystyle\int\limits_{\Omega}\left<\rho^{p-1+\alpha+\beta}\varphi|\nabla\varphi|^{p-2}\nabla\varphi,\nabla|\psi|^{p}\right>+\int\limits_{\Omega}\rho^{p-1+\alpha+\beta}|\psi\nabla\varphi|_{g}^{p}

\displaystyle=\

\displaystyle\int\limits_{\Omega}\left<|\nabla\varphi|^{p-2}\nabla\varphi,\nabla(\varphi|\psi|^{p})\right>\rho^{p-1+\alpha+\beta}

\displaystyle=\

\displaystyle\int\limits_{\Omega}\left<|\nabla\varphi|^{p-2}\nabla\varphi,\nabla(|u|^{p}\varphi^{-p+1}\rho^{-p+1-\beta})\right>\rho^{p-1+\alpha+\beta}

\displaystyle=\

\displaystyle\int\limits_{\Omega}\left<|\nabla\varphi|^{p-2}\nabla\varphi,\nabla(|u|^{p})\right>\varphi^{-p+1}\rho^{\alpha}-(p-1)\int\limits_{\Omega}|\nabla\varphi|^{p}|u|^{p}\rho^{\alpha}\varphi^{-p}

\displaystyle-(p-1+\beta)\int\limits_{\Omega}\left<|\nabla\varphi|^{p-2}\nabla\varphi,\nabla\rho\right>|u|^{p}\varphi^{-p+1}\rho^{-1+\alpha}

(9)

\displaystyle\geq\

\displaystyle\int\limits_{\Omega}\frac{V}{C(p)}|u|^{p}\rho^{\alpha}.

Combining (
2
) and (
2
), we get the desired result.

Now let us consider the case
p<2
. Using
Lemma

10
and (
2
), we get

\displaystyle I\geq\

\displaystyle\int\limits_{\Omega}\frac{p(p-1)}{2}\frac{\left|\rho^{\frac{p-1+\beta}{p}}\nabla w\right|^{2}\rho^{\alpha}}{\left|\left|\frac{p-1+\beta}{p}\frac{w\nabla\rho}{\rho^{\frac{1-\beta}{p}}}\right|+\left|\rho^{\frac{p-1+\beta}{p}}\nabla w\right|\right|^{2-p}}

\displaystyle+p\left|\frac{p-1+\beta}{p}\frac{w\nabla\rho}{\rho^{\frac{1-\beta}{p}}}\right|^{p-2}\left<\frac{p-1+\beta}{p}\frac{w\nabla\rho}{\rho^{\frac{1-\beta}{p}}},\rho^{\frac{p-1+\beta}{p}}\nabla w\right>\rho^{\alpha}

\displaystyle=\

\displaystyle\int\limits_{\Omega}\frac{p(p-1)}{2}\frac{\rho^{1+\alpha+\beta}\left|\nabla w\right|^{2}}{\left|\left|\frac{p-1+\beta}{p}w\nabla\rho\right|+\left|\rho\nabla w\right|\right|^{2-p}}

\displaystyle+\left(\frac{|p-1+\beta|}{p}\right)^{p-2}\left(\frac{p-1+\beta}{p}\right)\int\limits_{\Omega}\left<|\nabla\rho|^{p-2}\nabla\rho,\nabla|w|^{p}\right>\rho^{\alpha+\beta}

(10)

\displaystyle\geq\

\displaystyle\frac{p^{3-p}(p-1)}{2}\int\limits_{\Omega}\frac{\rho^{p-1+\alpha+\beta}|\nabla w|^{2}}{\left||(p-1+\beta)w\rho^{-1}\nabla\rho|+p|\nabla w|\right|^{2-p}}.

Now, we estimate the last term using Hölder’s inequality, convexity of
t\mapsto t^{p}
in the following:

\displaystyle\int\limits_{\Omega}

\displaystyle\rho^{p-1+\alpha+\beta}|\nabla w|^{p}

\displaystyle=\

\displaystyle\int\limits_{\Omega}\left(\frac{\rho^{p-1+\alpha+\beta}|\nabla w|^{2}}{\left||(p-1+\beta)w\rho^{-1}\nabla\rho|+p|\nabla w|\right|^{2-p}}\right)^{\frac{p}{2}}

\displaystyle\times\left(\rho^{\frac{(p-1+\alpha+\beta)(2-p)}{2}}\left||(p-1+\beta)w\rho^{-1}\nabla\rho|+p|\nabla w|\right|^{\frac{2p-p^{2}}{2}}\right)

\displaystyle\leq\

\displaystyle\left(\int\limits_{\Omega}\left(\frac{\rho^{p-1+\alpha+\beta}|\nabla w|^{2}}{\left||(p-1+\beta)w\rho^{-1}\nabla\rho|+p|\nabla w|\right|^{2-p}}\right)\right)^{\frac{p}{2}}

\displaystyle\times\left(\int\limits_{\Omega}\left(\rho^{\frac{(p-1+\alpha+\beta)(2-p)}{2}}\left||(p-1+\beta)w\rho^{-1}\nabla\rho|+p|\nabla w|\right|^{\frac{2p-p^{2}}{2}}\right)^{\frac{2}{2-p}}\right)^{\frac{2-p}{2}}

\displaystyle=\

\displaystyle\left(\int\limits_{\Omega}\frac{\rho^{p-1+\alpha+\beta}|\nabla w|^{2}}{\left||(p-1+\beta)w\rho^{-1}\nabla\rho|+p|\nabla w|\right|^{2-p}}\right)^{\frac{p}{2}}

\displaystyle\times\left(\int\limits_{\Omega}\rho^{p-1+\alpha+\beta}\left||(p-1+\beta)w\rho^{-1}\nabla\rho|+p|\nabla w|\right|^{p}\right)^{\frac{2-p}{2}}

\displaystyle\leq\

\displaystyle\left(\int\limits_{\Omega}\frac{\rho^{p-1+\alpha+\beta}|\nabla w|^{2}}{\left||(p-1+\beta)w\rho^{-1}\nabla\rho|+p|\nabla w|\right|^{2-p}}\right)^{\frac{p}{2}}

(11)

\displaystyle\times\left(2^{p-1}|p-1+\beta|^{p}\int\limits_{\Omega}\rho^{p-1+\alpha+\beta}|w\rho^{-1}\nabla\rho|^{p}\right.

\displaystyle\left.+p^{p}2^{p-1}\int\limits_{\Omega}\rho^{p-1+\alpha+\beta}|\nabla w|^{p}\right)^{\frac{2-p}{2}}.

Observing that since we have assumed (
3
), we can apply
Theorem

1
to get

\displaystyle\int\limits_{\Omega}\rho^{p-1+\alpha+\beta}|\nabla w|^{p}\leq

\displaystyle\ \left(\int\limits_{\Omega}\frac{\rho^{p-1+\alpha+\beta}|\nabla w|^{2}}{\left||(p-1+\beta)w\rho^{-1}\nabla\rho|+p|\nabla w|\right|^{2-p}}\right)^{\frac{p}{2}}

\displaystyle\times\left(p^{p}2^{p}\int\limits_{\Omega}\rho^{p-1+\alpha+\beta}|\nabla w|^{p}\right)^{\frac{2-p}{2}}.

After setting
\psi=\frac{w}{\varphi}
, so that
\nabla w=\varphi\nabla\psi+\psi\nabla\varphi
and then applying
Lemma

10
and (
4
), we get

\displaystyle\frac{p^{3-p}(p-1)}{2}

\displaystyle\int\limits_{\Omega}\frac{\rho^{p-1+\alpha+\beta}|\nabla w|^{2}}{\left||(p-1+\beta)w\rho^{-1}\nabla\rho|+p|\nabla w|\right|^{2-p}}

\displaystyle\geq\

\displaystyle 2^{p-3}p(p-1)\int\limits_{\Omega}\rho^{p-1+\alpha+\beta}|\nabla w|^{p}

\displaystyle=\

\displaystyle 2^{p-3}p(p-1)\int\limits_{\Omega}\rho^{p-1+\alpha+\beta}|\varphi\nabla\psi+\psi\nabla\varphi|^{p}

\displaystyle\geq\

\displaystyle 2^{p-3}p^{2}(p-1)\int\limits_{\Omega}\rho^{p-1+\alpha+\beta}|\psi\nabla\varphi|^{p-2}\left<\psi\nabla\varphi,\varphi\nabla\psi\right>

\displaystyle+2^{p-3}p(p-1)\int\limits_{\Omega}\rho^{p-1+\alpha+\beta}|\psi\nabla\varphi|^{p}

\displaystyle=\

\displaystyle 2^{p-3}p(p-1)\int\limits_{\Omega}\left<\rho^{p-1+\alpha+\beta}\varphi|\nabla\varphi|^{p-2}\nabla\varphi,\nabla|\psi|^{p}\right>

\displaystyle+2^{p-3}p(p-1)\int\limits_{\Omega}\rho^{p-1+\alpha+\beta}|\psi\nabla\varphi|^{p}

\displaystyle=\

\displaystyle 2^{p-3}p(p-1)\int\limits_{\Omega}\left<|\nabla\varphi|^{p-2}\nabla\varphi,\nabla(\varphi|\psi|^{p})\right>\rho^{p-1+\alpha+\beta}

\displaystyle\geq\

\displaystyle\int\limits_{\Omega}V|u|^{p}\rho^{\alpha}.

The last line follows following the same equalities as in (
2
).
This with (
2
) proves the theorem. ∎
