Abstract
We examine indefinite integral involving of arbitrary power x x , multiplied by three spherical Bessel functions of the first kind j h , j k j_{h},j_{k} , and j l j_{l} with integer order h , k , l ≥ 0 h,k,l\geq 0 and an exponential. Then we add some conditions for smooth calculation in considering the general and elementary exact evaluation. Thus, by measuring their equivalents, we can verify their accuracy.

Spherical Bessel Functions literature

Spherical Bessel functions are well-known to account for the problems with circular symmetry. In spherical coordinate, if one solve Helmholtz’s and Laplacian’s equation the solution yield the following differential equation

\displaystyle\frac{d^{2}y}{dx^{2}}+\frac{2}{x}\frac{dy}{dx}+\bigg[1+\frac{l(l+1)}{x^{2}}\bigg]y=0.

(2)

\displaystyle\frac{d}{dx}\bigg[x^{l+1}y_{l}(x)\bigg]=x^{l+1}y_{l-1}(x),\qquad\frac{d}{dx}\bigg[x^{-1}y_{l}(x)\bigg]=x^{-1}y_{l+1}(x),
