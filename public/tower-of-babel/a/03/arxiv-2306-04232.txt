Non-minimaxity of debiased shrinkage estimators
Yuzo Maruyama, Akimichi Takemura

Abstract
We consider the estimation of the p p -variate normal mean of X ∼ N p ​ ( θ , I ) X\sim N_{p}(\theta,I) under the quadratic loss function. We investigate the decision theoretic properties of debiased shrinkage estimator, the estimator which shrinks towards the origin for smaller ‖ x ‖ 2 \|x\|^{2} and which is exactly equal to the unbiased estimator X X for larger ‖ x ‖ 2 \|x\|^{2} . Such debiased shrinkage estimator seems superior to the unbiased estimator X X , which implies minimaxity. However we show that it is not minimax under mild conditions.

Introduction

Let
X
have a
p
-variate normal distribution

\mathcal{N}_{p}(\theta,I_{p})
.
We consider the problem of estimating the mean vector
\theta
under
the loss function

\displaystyle L(\theta,\hat{\theta})=\|\hat{\theta}-\theta\|^{2}=\sum_{i=1}^{p}(\hat{\theta}_{i}-\theta_{i})^{2}.

(1.1)

The risk function of an estimator
\hat{\theta}(X)
is

R(\theta,\hat{\theta})=\mathrm{E}\bigl[\|\hat{\theta}(X)-\theta\|^{2}\bigr]=\int_{R^{p}}\frac{\|\hat{\theta}(x)-\theta\|^{2}}{(2\pi)^{p/2}}\exp\left(-\frac{\|x-\theta\|^{2}}{2}\right)\mathrm{d}x.

The usual unbiased estimator
X
has the constant risk
p
and is
minimax for
p\in\mathbb{N}
.

Stein 1956
showed that there are orthogonally equivariant
estimators of the form

\displaystyle\hat{\theta}_{\phi}(X)=\left(1-\frac{\phi(\|X\|^{2})}{\|X\|^{2}}\right)X

(1.2)

which dominate
X
when
p\geq 3
.

James and Stein 1961

gave an explicit dominating procedure

\hat{\theta}_{\mathrm{JS}}(X)=\left(1-\frac{p-2}{\|X\|^{2}}\right)X,

(1.3)

called the James-Stein estimator.
Further, as shown in
Baranchik 1964
, the James-Stein estimator is inadmissible since
the positive-part estimator

\hat{\theta}_{\mathrm{JS}}^{+}(X)=\max\left(0,1-\frac{p-2}{\|X\|^{2}}\right)X

(1.4)

dominates
\hat{\theta}_{\mathrm{JS}}
.
For a class of general shrinkage estimators

given by (
1.2
),

B.1
and
B.2
}
where

\phi^{\prime}(w)\geq 0
for all
w\geq 0
.

Further

\displaystyle\mathrm{E}\bigl[\|\hat{\theta}_{\phi}-\theta\|^{2}\bigr]=p+\mathrm{E}\left[r_{\phi}(\|X\|^{2})\right],

(1.5)

where

\displaystyle r_{\phi}(w)=\frac{\phi(w)}{w}\left\{\phi(w)-2(p-2)\right\}-4\phi^{\prime}(w).

(1.6)

Hence the shrinkage factor
\phi(w)

,

We see that {

and

A series of papers,

.
On the other hand,

\displaystyle\mathrm{E}\left[\left(1-\frac{\phi(\|X\|^{2})}{\|X\|^{2}}\right)X\right]-\theta=-\mathrm{E}\left[\frac{\phi(\|X\|^{2})}{\|X\|^{2}}X\right],
