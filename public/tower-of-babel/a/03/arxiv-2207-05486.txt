Latest results on semileptonic and electroweak penguin decays at Belle II
M. WELSCH on behalf of the Belle II Collaboration

Abstract
The Belle II Collaboration presents four new analyses: The measurement of | V u b | |V_{\mathit{{u}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{}}}\mathit{{b}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{}}}}| from B → π e ν e \mathit{{B}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{}}}\mspace{1mu}\to\mspace{1mu}\mathit{{\pi}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{}}}\mathit{{e}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{}}}\mathit{{\nu}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{\!e}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{}}} decays with a fit to the differential q 2 q^{2} spectrum, the determination of | V c b | |V_{\mathit{{c}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{}}}\mathit{{b}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{}}}}| using a fit to the differential w w distribution from B 0 → D ∗ ⁣ − ℓ + ν ℓ \mathit{{B}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{0}}}\mspace{1mu}\to\mspace{1mu}\mathit{{D}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{*-}}}\mathit{{\ell}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{+}}}\mathit{{\nu}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{\ell}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{}}} decays, and the measurement of q 2 q^{2} moments in inclusive B → X c ℓ ν ℓ \mathit{{B}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{}}}\mspace{1mu}\to\mspace{1mu}\mathit{{X}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{c}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{}}}\mathit{{\ell}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{}}}\mathit{{\nu}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{\ell}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{}}} decays. In all these analyses tag-side B \mathit{{B}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{}}} meson is reconstructed in a fully hadronic decay chain. The last result are branching fraction measurement of B → K ∗ ℓ + ℓ − \mathit{{B}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{}}}\mspace{1mu}\to\mspace{1mu}\mathit{{K}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{\ast}}}\mathit{{\ell}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{+}}}\mathit{{\ell}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{-}}} decays using an untagged approach.

{\mathit{{\Upsilon}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{}}}}{\mathit{{\left({4S}\right)}}}

Measurement of
q^{2}
Moments in
\mathit{{B}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{}}}\mspace{1mu}\to\mspace{1mu}\mathit{{X}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{c}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{}}}\mathit{{\ell}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{}}}\mathit{{\nu}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{\ell}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{}}}
Decays

We present measurements of the first to fourth raw and central
q^{2}
moments of
\mathit{{B}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{}}}\mspace{1mu}\to\mspace{1mu}\mathit{{X}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{c}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{}}}\mathit{{\ell}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{}}}\mathit{{\nu}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{\ell}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{}}}
decays as functions of lower
q^{2}
threshold from
1.5\text{\,}\mathrm{G}\mathrm{e}\mathrm{V}^{2}\mathrm{/}\mathrm{c}^{4}
up to
8.5\text{\,}\mathrm{G}\mathrm{e}\mathrm{V}^{2}\mathrm{/}\mathrm{c}^{4}
.
Lepton energy and hadronic mass moments have been used to determine
|V_{\mathit{{c}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{}}}\mathit{{b}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{}}}}|
and parameters of the Heavy-Quark-Expansion (HQE) up to
\mathcal{O}(1/m_{\mathit{{b}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{}}}}^{3})
in a model independent way using a global fit.
At higher order, the determination of the HQE is complicated by a proliferation of non-perturbative matrix elements.
Ref.
[
9
]
outlines an alternative approach to determine
|V_{\mathit{{c}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{}}}\mathit{{b}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{}}}}|
from inclusive decays avoiding this proliferation of HQE parameters by exploiting reparameterization invariance.
However reparameterization invariance is not retained by the lepton energy and hadronic mass moments.
Thus, the measurement of
q^{2}
moments is proposed.

Semileptonic decays are reconstructed in an inclusive approach without assuming exclusive hadronic charmed resonances in the the
\mathit{{B}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{}}}\mspace{1mu}\to\mspace{1mu}\mathit{{X}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{}}}\mathit{{\ell}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{}}}\mathit{{\nu}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{\ell}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{}}}
decay.
The hadronic
\mathit{{X}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{}}}
system is identified with the remaining charged particles and photons not used in the tag-side
\mathit{{B}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{}}}
meson and signal lepton reconstruction.

q^{2}

The remaining background is subtracted directly in the calculation of the

moments by assigning a signal probability as event weight

\displaystyle\langle q^{2n}\rangle=\frac{\sum w_{i}q^{2}_{i,\mathrm{calib}}}{\sum w_{j}}\times\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{calib}}\times\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{gen}}.

(2)

components.

5
Branching Fraction Measurements of
\mathit{{B}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{}}}\mspace{1mu}\to\mspace{1mu}\mathit{{K}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{\ast}}}\mathit{{\ell}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{+}}}\mathit{{\ell}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{-}}}
Decays

The decay
\mathit{{B}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{}}}\mspace{1mu}\to\mspace{1mu}\mathit{{K}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{\ast}}}\mathit{{\ell}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{+}}}\mathit{{\ell}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{-}}}
involves a
\mathit{{b}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{}}}\mspace{1mu}\to\mspace{1mu}\mathit{{s}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{}}}
transition mediated by a flavour changing neutral current which is forbidden at tree level and thus, highly suppressed.
The decay is sensitive to new physics contributions, which might enhance or suppress the decay amplitude or modify the angular distributions.
The here reported branching fractions measurements are a first step towards a measurement of
R(\mathit{{K}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{\ast}}})
at Belle II.
Recent determinations of
R(\mathit{{K}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{\ast}}})
show tensions with the SM expectation
[
10
,
11
,
12
]
.

The analysis considers three decay modes:
\mathit{{B}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{0}}}\mspace{1mu}\to\mspace{1mu}\mathit{{K}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{*0}}}(\mspace{1mu}\to\mspace{1mu}\mathit{{K}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{+}}}\mathit{{\pi}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{-}}})\mathit{{\ell}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{+}}}\mathit{{\ell}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{-}}}
and
\mathit{{B}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{+}}}\mspace{1mu}\to\mspace{1mu}\mathit{{K}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{*+}}}(\mspace{1mu}\to\mspace{1mu}\mathit{{K}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{S}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{0}}}\mathit{{\pi}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{+}}},\mspace{1mu}\to\mspace{1mu}\mathit{{K}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{+}}}\mathit{{\pi}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{0}}})\mathit{{\ell}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{+}}}\mathit{{\ell}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{-}}}
.
The second
\mathit{{B}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{}}}
meson in the
{\mathit{{\Upsilon}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{}}}}{\mathit{{\left({4S}\right)}}}
decay is not explicitly reconstructed.
The number of signal decays is extracted from a 2D maximum likelihood fit to the beam-constrained mass
M_{\mathrm{bc}}=\sqrt{s/4-p_{\mathit{{B}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{}}}}^{\ast 2}}
and the energy difference
\Delta E=(E_{\mathit{{B}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{}}}}^{\ast}-\sqrt{s}/2
).

We measure
\mathcal{B}(\mathit{{B}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{}}}\mspace{1mu}\to\mspace{1mu}\mathit{{K}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{\ast}}}\mathit{{\mu}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{+}}}\mathit{{\mu}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{-}}})=(1.28\pm 0.29^{+0.08}_{-0.07})\times 10^{-6}
,
\mathcal{B}(\mathit{{B}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{}}}\mspace{1mu}\to\mspace{1mu}\mathit{{K}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{\ast}}}\mathit{{e}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{+}}}\mathit{{e}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{-}}})=(1.04\pm 0.48^{+0.09}_{-0.09})\times 10^{-6}
and
\mathcal{B}(\mathit{{B}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{}}}\mspace{1mu}\to\mspace{1mu}\mathit{{K}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{\ast}}}\mathit{{\ell}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{+}}}\mathit{{\ell}{}_{\mspace{-3mu}\scriptstyle{}}^{\mspace{-1mu}\scriptstyle{-}}})=(1.22\pm 0.28^{+0.08}_{-0.07})\times 10^{-6}
with the first and second uncertainty being statistical and systematic, respectively.
