Abstract
In this paper, we define and study CR-lightlike submanifolds of a golden semi-Riemannian manifold. We investigate some properties of geodesic CR-submanifolds of a golden semi-Riemannian manifold. Moreover, we obtain many interesting results for totally geodesic and totally umbilical CR-submanifolds on a golden Riemannian manifold. A non-trivial example of CR-lightlike submanifold of a golden semi-Riemannian manifold is also constructed.

In this paper, we study CR-lightlike submanifold of a golden semi-Riemannian manifold. The paper is organized as follows:

In section 2, we define a golden structure manifold and CR-lightlike submanifolds of a golden Riemannian manifold. In section 4, we investigate some properties of totally umbilical CR-lightlike submanifolds. Totally Umbilical CR-lightlike submanifolds.

Definition 4.1

A lightlike submanifold
(M,g)
of a semi-Riemannian manifold
(\overline{M},{g})
is said to be totally umbilical in
\overline{M}
if there is a smooth transversal vector field
H\in\Gamma(tr(TM))
on
M,
called the transversal curvature vector field of
M,
such that

h(X,Y)=H{g}(X,Y),

where
X,Y\in\Gamma(TM).

Using (
2.3
),
M
is a totally umbilical if and only if there exists smooth vector fields
H^{l}\in\Gamma(ltr(TM))
and
H^{s}(X,Y)\in\Gamma(S(TM^{\bot})),
such that

h^{l}(X,Y)=H^{l}{g}(X,Y),\;\;h^{s}(X,Y)=H^{s}{g}(X,Y).
