Representations of p p -adic groups over coefficient rings
Marie-France Vignéras

Abstract
. Motivated by the Langlands program in representation theory, number theory and geometry, the theory of representations of a reductive p p -adic group over a coefficient ring different from the field of complex numbers has been widely developped during the last two decades. This article provides a survey of basic results obtained in the 21st century.
