C 0 C^{0} Lagrangian monodromy
Noah Porcelli

Abstract
We prove that (under appropriate orientation assumptions), the action of a Hamiltonian homeomorphism ϕ \phi on the cohomology of a relatively exact Lagrangian fixed by ϕ \phi is the identity. This extends results of Hu-Lalonde-Leclercq [ 7 ] and the author [ 10 ] in the setting of Hamiltonian diffeomorphisms. We also prove a similar result regarding the action of ϕ \phi on relative cohomology.

1.1
Extension to the

Definition 1.4
.

A
Hamiltonian homeomorphism
is a homeomorphism
\theta\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}M\to M

It is known due to work of Eliashberg-Gromov

The dynamical behaviour of Hamiltonian homeomorphisms is very different to Hamiltonian diffeomorphisms: for example, the Arnold conjecture fails (badly) for Hamiltonian homeomorphisms

While Question

The proof of Theorem

1.2
Extension to the relative setting

Assume

\phi

\phi

acts as the identity on cohomology, it does not immediately imply the map induced on relative cohomology is the identity, though Conjecture

Theorem 1.7

Let

\tilde{\phi}
.

1.

\phi
acts as the identity on
H^{*}(M,L;\mathbb{Z}/2)
.

2.

If
L
is Spin,
\phi
acts as the identity on both
H^{*}(M,L;\mathbb{Z})
and
K^{*}(M,L)
.

The proof is similar to the proof of Theorem
1.5
, but whereas the proof of Theorem
1.5
uses a moduli space of holomorphic curves with domain

,

, along with the evaluation map on the boundary of

,
ev\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}\mathcal{P}\times\partial Z_{C}\to M
, the proof of Theorem

uses the evaluation map on the whole of

,
ev\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}\mathcal{P}\times Z_{C}\to M
.
