A note on degenerate poly-Bernoulli numbers and polynomials
Dae San Kim, Taekyun Kim

Abstract
. In this paper, we consider the degenerate poly-Bernoulli polynomials and present new and explicit formulas for computing them in terms of the degenerate Bernoulli polynomials and Stirling numbers of the second kind.

1.
Introduction

For
\lambda\in\mathbb{C}
, L. Carlitz considered the degenerate
Bernoulli polynomials given by the generating function

(1.1)

\frac{t}{\left(1+\lambda t\right)^{\frac{1}{\lambda}}-1}\left(1+\lambda t\right)^{\frac{x}{\lambda}}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\beta_{n}\left(x\mid\lambda\right)\frac{t^{n}}{n!},\quad\left(\text{see \cite[cite]{[\@@bibref{Number}{key-6,key-12,key-19}{}{}]}}\right).

When
x=0
,
\beta_{n}\left(\lambda\right)=\beta_{n}\left(0\mid\lambda\right)

are called the degenerate Bernoulli numbers.

Thus, by (
1.1
), we get

(1.2)

\beta_{n}\left(x\mid\lambda\right)=\sum_{l=0}^{n}\binom{n}{l}\beta_{l}\left(\lambda\right)\left(x\mid\lambda\right)_{n-l},

where
\left(x\mid\lambda\right)_{n}=x\left(x-\lambda\right)\left(x-2\lambda\right)\cdots\left(x-\lambda\left(n-1\right)\right)
.

The classical polylograithm function
\mathrm{Li}_{k}
is

(1.3)

\mathrm{Li}_{k}\left(x\right)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{x^{n}}{n^{k}},\quad\left(k\in\mathbb{Z}\right),\quad\left(\text{see \cite[cite]{[\@@bibref{Number}{key-10,key-11,key-12,key-13,key-14,key-15,key-16,key-17,key-18}{}{}]}}\right).

From (
1.1
), we note that

(1.4)

\displaystyle\mathrel{\phantom{=}}{}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\lim_{\lambda\rightarrow 0}\beta_{n}\left(x\mid\lambda\right)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

\displaystyle=\lim_{\lambda\rightarrow 0}\frac{t}{\left(1+\lambda t\right)^{\frac{1}{\lambda}}-1}\left(1+\lambda t\right)^{\frac{x}{\lambda}}

\displaystyle=\frac{t}{e^{t}-1}e^{xt}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}B_{n}\left(x\right)\frac{t^{n}}{n!},

where
B_{n}\left(x\right)
are called the Bernoulli polynomials
(see
[

,

,

,

,

,

,

,

,

,

,

,

,

]
).

Thus, by (
1.4
), we get

(1.5)

\lim_{\lambda\rightarrow 0}\beta_{n}\left(x\mid\lambda\right)=B_{n}\left(x\right),\quad\left(n\geq 0\right).

In
[
4
,
10
]
, the poly-Bernoulli polynomials are given
by

(1.6)

\frac{\mathrm{Li}_{k}\left(1-e^{-t}\right)}{e^{t}-1}e^{xt}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}B_{n}^{\left(k\right)}\left(x\right)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

For
k=1
, we have

(1.7)

\frac{\mathrm{Li}_{1}\left(1-e^{-t}\right)}{e^{t}-1}e^{xt}=\frac{t}{e^{t}-1}e^{xt}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}B_{n}\left(x\right)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

By (
1.4
) and (
1.7
), we get
B_{n}^{\left(1\right)}\left(x\right)=B_{n}\left(x\right)
.

The Stirling numbers of the second kind are given by

(1.8)

x^{n}=\sum_{l=0}^{n}S_{2}\left(n,l\right)\left(x\right)_{l},\quad\left(\text{see \cite[cite]{[\@@bibref{Number}{key-1,key-2,key-3,key-4,key-5,key-6,key-7,key-8,key-9,key-10,key-11,key-12,key-13,key-14,key-15,key-16,key-17,key-18,key-19,key-20,key-21,key-22,key-23,key-24,key-25,key-26,key-27}{}{}]}}\right).

and the Stirling numbers of the first kind are defined by

(1.9)

\left(x\right)_{n}=x\left(x-1\right)\cdots\left(x-n+1\right)=\sum_{l=0}^{n}S_{1}\left(n,l\right)x^{l},\quad\left(n\geq 0\right).

The purpose of this paper is to construct the degenerate poly-Bernoulli
polynomials and present new and explicit formulas for computing them
in terms of the degenerate Bernoulli polynomials and Stirling
numbers of the second kind.

2.
Degenerate poly-Bernoulli numbers and polynomials

For
\lambda\in\mathbb{C}
,
k\in\mathbb{Z}
, we consider the degenerate
poly-Bernoulli polynomials given by the generating function

(2.1)

\frac{\mathrm{Li}_{k}\left(1-e^{-t}\right)}{\left(1+\lambda t\right)^{\frac{1}{\lambda}}-1}\left(1+\lambda t\right)^{\frac{x}{\lambda}}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\beta_{n}^{\left(k\right)}\left(x\mid\lambda\right)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

When
x=0
,
\beta_{n}^{\left(k\right)}\left(\lambda\right)=\beta_{n}^{\left(k\right)}\left(0\mid\lambda\right)

are called the degenerate poly-Bernoulli numbers. Note that
\beta_{n}^{\left(1\right)}\left(x\mid\lambda\right)=\beta_{n}\left(x\mid\lambda\right)

and
\lim_{\lambda\rightarrow 0}\beta_{n}^{\left(k\right)}\left(x\mid\lambda\right)=B_{n}^{\left(k\right)}\left(x\right)
.

From (
2.1
), we can derive the following equation:

(2.2)

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\beta_{n}^{\left(k\right)}\left(x\mid\lambda\right)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

\displaystyle=\left(\frac{\mathrm{Li}_{k}\left(1-e^{-t}\right)}{\left(1+\lambda t\right)^{\frac{1}{\lambda}}-1}\right)\left(1+\lambda t\right)^{\frac{x}{\lambda}}

\displaystyle=\left(\sum_{l=0}^{\infty}\beta_{l}^{\left(k\right)}\left(\lambda\right)\frac{t^{l}}{l!}\right)\left(\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\left(x\mid\lambda\right)_{m}\frac{t^{m}}{m!}\right)

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left(\sum_{l=0}^{n}\binom{n}{l}\beta_{l}^{\left(k\right)}\left(\lambda\right)\left(x\mid\lambda\right)_{n-l}\right)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

Thus, by (
2.2
), we get

(2.3)

\beta_{n}^{\left(k\right)}\left(x\mid\lambda\right)=\sum_{l=0}^{n}\binom{n}{l}\beta_{l}^{\left(k\right)}\left(\lambda\right)\left(x\mid\lambda\right)_{n-l}.

Now, we observe that

(2.4)

\displaystyle\mathrel{\phantom{=}}{}\frac{\mathrm{Li}_{k}\left(1-e^{-t}\right)}{\left(1+\lambda t\right)^{\frac{1}{\lambda}}-1}\left(1+t\right)^{\frac{x}{\lambda}}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\beta_{n}^{\left(k\right)}\left(x\mid\lambda\right)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

\displaystyle=\frac{\left(1+t\right)^{\frac{x}{\lambda}}}{\left(1+\lambda t\right)^{\frac{1}{\lambda}}-1}\underset{\left(k-2\right)\text{times}}{\int_{0}^{t}\underbrace{\frac{1}{e^{y}-1}\int_{0}^{y}\frac{1}{e^{y}-1}\int_{0}^{y}\cdots\frac{1}{e^{y}-1}\int_{0}^{y}}}\frac{y}{e^{y}-1}dy\cdots dy.

From (
2.4
), we have

(2.5)

\displaystyle\mathrel{\phantom{=}}{}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\beta_{n}^{\left(2\right)}\left(x\mid\lambda\right)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

\displaystyle=\frac{\left(1+t\right)^{\frac{x}{\lambda}}}{\left(1+\lambda t\right)^{\frac{1}{\lambda}}-1}\int_{0}^{t}\frac{y}{e^{y}-1}dy

\displaystyle=\frac{\left(1+t\right)^{\frac{x}{\lambda}}}{\left(1+\lambda t\right)^{\frac{1}{\lambda}}-1}\sum_{l=0}^{\infty}\frac{B_{l}}{l!}\int_{0}^{t}y^{l}dy

\displaystyle=\left(\frac{t}{\left(1+\lambda t\right)^{\frac{1}{\lambda}}-1}\left(1+\lambda t\right)^{\frac{x}{\lambda}}\right)\left(\sum_{l=0}^{\infty}\frac{B_{l}}{l+1}\frac{t^{l}}{l!}\right)

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left\{\sum_{l=0}^{n}\binom{n}{l}\frac{B_{l}}{l+1}\beta_{n-l}\left(x\mid\lambda\right)\right\}\frac{t^{n}}{n!},

where
B_{n}=B_{n}\left(0\right)
are Bernoulli numbers.

By comparing the coefficients on both sides of (
2.5
),
we obtain the following theorem.

Theorem 2.1
.

For
n\geq 0
, we have

\displaystyle\beta_{n}^{\left(2\right)}\left(x\mid\lambda\right)

\displaystyle=\sum_{l=0}^{n}\binom{n}{l}\frac{B_{l}}{l+1}\beta_{n-l}\left(x\mid\lambda\right)

\displaystyle=\beta_{n}\left(x\mid\lambda\right)-\frac{n}{4}\beta_{n-1}\left(x\mid\lambda\right)+\sum_{l=2}^{n}\binom{n}{l}\frac{B_{l}}{l+1}\beta_{n-l}\left(x\mid\lambda\right).

Moreover,

\beta_{n}^{\left(k\right)}\left(x\mid\lambda\right)=\sum_{l=0}^{n}\binom{n}{l}\beta_{l}^{\left(k\right)}\left(\lambda\right)\left(x\mid\lambda\right)_{n-l}.

By (
2.4
), we easily get

(2.6)

\displaystyle\mathrel{\phantom{=}}{}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\beta_{n}^{\left(k\right)}\left(x\mid\lambda\right)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

\displaystyle=\frac{\mathrm{Li}_{k}\left(1-e^{-t}\right)}{\left(1+\lambda t\right)^{\frac{1}{\lambda}}-1}\left(1+t\right)^{\frac{x}{\lambda}}

\displaystyle=\frac{t}{\left(1+\lambda t\right)^{\frac{1}{\lambda}}-1}\left(1+t\right)^{\frac{x}{\lambda}}\frac{\mathrm{Li}_{k}\left(1-e^{-t}\right)}{t}.

We observe that

(2.7)

\displaystyle\frac{1}{t}\mathrm{Li}_{k}\left(1-e^{-t}\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{t}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{n^{k}}\left(1-e^{-t}\right)^{n}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{t}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{\left(-1\right)^{n}}{n^{k}}n!\sum_{l=n}^{\infty}S_{2}\left(l,n\right)\frac{\left(-t\right)^{l}}{l!}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{t}\sum_{l=1}^{\infty}\sum_{n=1}^{l}\frac{\left(-1\right)^{n+l}}{n^{k}}n!S_{2}\left(l,n\right)\frac{t^{l}}{l!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{l=0}^{\infty}\sum_{n=1}^{l+1}\frac{\left(-1\right)^{n+l+1}}{n^{k}}n!\frac{S_{2}\left(l+1,n\right)}{l+1}\frac{t^{l}}{l!}.

From (
2.6
) and (
2.7
), we have

(2.8)

\displaystyle\mathrel{\phantom{=}}{}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\beta_{n}^{\left(k\right)}\left(x\mid\lambda\right)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

\displaystyle=\left(\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\beta_{m}\left(x\mid\lambda\right)\frac{t^{m}}{m!}\right)\left(\sum_{l=0}^{\infty}\left(\sum_{p=1}^{l+1}\frac{\left(-1\right)^{p+l+1}}{p^{k}}p!\frac{S_{2}\left(l+1,p\right)}{l+1}\right)\frac{t^{l}}{l!}\right)

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left\{\sum_{l=0}^{n}\binom{n}{l}\left(\sum_{p=1}^{l+1}\frac{\left(-1\right)^{p+l+1}p!}{p^{k}}\frac{S_{2}\left(l+1,p\right)}{l+1}\right)\beta_{n-l}\left(x\mid\lambda\right)\right\}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

By comparing the coefficients on both sides of (
2.8
),
we obtain the following theorem.

Theorem 2.2
.

For
n\geq 0
, we have

\beta_{n}^{\left(k\right)}\left(x\mid\lambda\right)=\sum_{l=0}^{n}\binom{n}{l}\left(\sum_{p=1}^{l+1}\frac{\left(-1\right)^{p+l+1}p!}{p^{k}}\frac{S_{2}\left(l+1,p\right)}{l+1}\right)\beta_{n-l}\left(x\mid\lambda\right).

It is easy to show that

(2.9)

\displaystyle\mathrel{\phantom{=}}\frac{\mathrm{Li}_{k}\left(1-e^{-t}\right)}{\left(1+\lambda t\right)^{\frac{1}{\lambda}}-1}\left(1+\lambda t\right)^{\frac{x+1}{\lambda}}-\frac{\mathrm{Li}_{k}\left(1-e^{-t}\right)}{\left(1+\lambda t\right)^{\frac{1}{\lambda}}-1}\left(1+\lambda t\right)^{\frac{x}{\lambda}}

\displaystyle=\left(1+\lambda t\right)^{\frac{x}{\lambda}}\mathrm{Li}_{k}\left(1-e^{-t}\right)

\displaystyle=\left(\sum_{l=0}^{\infty}\left(x\mid\lambda\right)_{l}\frac{t^{l}}{l!}\right)\left(\sum_{m=1}^{\infty}\frac{\left(1-e^{-t}\right)^{m}}{m^{k}}\right)

\displaystyle=\left(\sum_{l=0}^{\infty}\left(x\mid\lambda\right)_{l}\frac{t^{l}}{l!}\right)\left(\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\frac{\left(1-e^{-t}\right)^{m+1}}{\left(m+1\right)^{k}}\right)

\displaystyle=\left(\sum_{l=0}^{\infty}\left(x\mid\lambda\right)_{l}\frac{t^{l}}{l!}\right)\left(\sum_{p=1}^{\infty}\left(\sum_{m=0}^{p-1}\frac{\left(-1\right)^{m+p+1}}{\left(m+1\right)^{k}}\left(m+1\right)!S_{2}\left(p,m+1\right)\right)\frac{t^{p}}{p!}\right)

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left\{\sum_{p=1}^{n}\sum_{m=0}^{p-1}\frac{\left(-1\right)^{m+p+1}}{\left(m+1\right)^{k}}\left(m+1\right)!S_{2}\left(p,m+1\right)\binom{n}{p}\left(x\mid\lambda\right)_{n-p}\right\}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

On the other hand,

(2.10)

\displaystyle\mathrel{\phantom{=}}{}\frac{\mathrm{Li}_{k}\left(1-e^{-t}\right)}{\left(1+\lambda t\right)^{\frac{1}{\lambda}}-1}\left(1+\lambda t\right)^{\frac{x+1}{\lambda}}-\frac{\mathrm{Li}_{k}\left(1-e^{-t}\right)}{\left(1+\lambda t\right)^{\frac{1}{\lambda}}-1}\left(1+\lambda t\right)^{\frac{x}{\lambda}}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left\{\beta_{n}^{\left(k\right)}\left(x+1\mid\lambda\right)-\beta_{n}^{\left(k\right)}\left(x\mid\lambda\right)\right\}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

Therefore, by (
2.9
) and (
2.10
), we obtain the following
theorem.

Theorem 2.3
.

For
n\geq 1
, we have

\displaystyle\mathrel{\phantom{=}}{}\beta_{n}^{\left(k\right)}\left(x+1\mid\lambda\right)-\beta_{n}^{\left(k\right)}\left(x\mid\lambda\right)

\displaystyle=\sum_{p=1}^{n}\left(\sum_{m=0}^{p-1}\frac{\left(-1\right)^{m+k+1}}{\left(m+1\right)^{k}}\left(m+1\right)!S_{2}\left(k+m+1\right)\right)\binom{n}{p}\left(x\mid\lambda\right)_{n-p}.

Now, we note that

(2.11)

\displaystyle\mathrel{\phantom{=}}{}\frac{\mathrm{Li}_{k}\left(1-e^{-t}\right)}{\left(1+\lambda t\right)^{\frac{1}{\lambda}}-1}\left(1+\lambda t\right)^{\frac{x}{\lambda}}

\displaystyle=\frac{\mathrm{Li}_{k}\left(1-e^{-t}\right)}{\left(1+\lambda t\right)^{\frac{d}{\lambda}}-1}\sum_{a=0}^{d-1}\left(1+\lambda t\right)^{\frac{l+x}{\lambda}}

\displaystyle=\left(\frac{\mathrm{Li}_{k}\left(1-e^{-t}\right)}{t}\right)\frac{1}{d}\sum_{a=0}^{d-1}\frac{dt}{\left(1+\lambda t\right)^{\frac{d}{\lambda}}-1}\left(1+\lambda t\right)^{\frac{l+x}{\lambda}}

\displaystyle=\sum_{l=0}^{\infty}\left(\sum_{p=1}^{l+1}\frac{\left(-1\right)^{p+l+1}}{p^{k}}p!\frac{S_{2}\left(l+1,p\right)}{l+1}\right)\frac{t^{l}}{l!}

\displaystyle\mathrel{\phantom{=}}{}\times\sum_{a=0}^{d-1}\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\beta_{m}\left(\left.\frac{l+x}{d}\right|\frac{\lambda}{d}\right)d^{m-1}\frac{t^{m}}{m!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{a=0}^{d-1}\left(\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left(\sum_{l=0}^{n}\sum_{p=1}^{l+1}\binom{n}{l}\frac{\left(-1\right)^{p+l+1}}{p^{k}}p!\frac{S_{2}\left(l+1,p\right)}{l+1}\beta_{n-l}\left(\left.\frac{l+x}{d}\right|\frac{\lambda}{d}\right)d^{n-l-1}\right)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}\right)

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left\{\sum_{a=0}^{d-1}\sum_{l=0}^{n}\sum_{p=1}^{l+1}\binom{n}{l}\frac{\left(-1\right)^{p+l+1}}{p^{k}}p!\frac{S_{2}\left(l+1,p\right)}{l+1}\beta_{n-l}\left(\left.\frac{l+x}{d}\right|\frac{\lambda}{d}\right)d^{n-l-1}\right\}\frac{t^{n}}{n!},

where
d
is a fixed positive integer.

On the other hand,

(2.12)

\displaystyle\mathrel{\phantom{=}}{}\frac{\mathrm{Li}_{k}\left(1-e^{-t}\right)}{\left(1+\lambda t\right)^{\frac{1}{\lambda}}-1}\left(1+\lambda t\right)^{\frac{x}{\lambda}}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\beta_{n}^{\left(k\right)}\left(x\mid\lambda\right)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

Therefore, by (
2.11
) and (
2.12
), we obtain the following
theorem.

Theorem 2.4
.

For
n\geq 0
,
d\in\mathbb{N}
and
k\in\mathbb{Z}
,
we have

\displaystyle\mathrel{\phantom{=}}{}\beta_{n}^{\left(k\right)}\left(x\mid\lambda\right)

\displaystyle=\sum_{a=0}^{d-1}\sum_{l=0}^{n}\sum_{p=1}^{l+1}\binom{n}{l}\frac{\left(-1\right)^{p+l+1}}{p^{k}}p!\frac{S_{2}\left(l+1,p\right)}{l+1}\beta_{n-l}\left(\left.\frac{l+x}{d}\right|\frac{\lambda}{d}\right)d^{n-l-1}.

From (
2.4
), we can derive the following equation:

(2.13)

\displaystyle\mathrel{\phantom{=}}{}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\beta_{n}^{\left(k\right)}\left(x+y\mid\lambda\right)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

\displaystyle=\frac{\mathrm{Li}_{k}\left(1-e^{-t}\right)}{\left(1+\lambda t\right)^{\frac{1}{\lambda}}-1}\left(1+\lambda t\right)^{\frac{x+y}{\lambda}}

\displaystyle=\left(\frac{\mathrm{Li}_{k}\left(1-e^{-t}\right)}{\left(1+\lambda t\right)^{\frac{1}{\lambda}}-1}\left(1+t\lambda\right)^{\frac{x}{\lambda}}\right)\left(1+\lambda t\right)^{\frac{y}{\lambda}}

\displaystyle=\left(\sum_{l=0}^{\infty}\beta_{l}^{\left(k\right)}\left(x\mid\lambda\right)\frac{t^{l}}{l!}\right)\left(\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\left(y\mid\lambda\right)_{m}\frac{t^{m}}{m!}\right)

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left(\sum_{l=0}^{n}\binom{n}{l}\beta_{l}^{\left(k\right)}\left(x\mid\lambda\right)\left(y\mid\lambda\right)_{n-l}\right)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

Therefore, by (
2.13
), we obtain the following theorem.

Theorem 2.5
.

For
n\geq 0
, we have

\beta_{n}^{\left(k\right)}\left(x+y\mid\lambda\right)=\sum_{l=0}^{n}\binom{n}{l}\beta_{l}^{\left(k\right)}\left(x\mid\lambda\right)\left(y\mid\lambda\right)_{n-l}.

Remark
.

\displaystyle\mathrel{\phantom{=}}\frac{d}{dx}\beta_{n}^{\left(k\right)}\left(x\mid\lambda\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{d}{dx}\sum_{l=0}^{n}\binom{n}{l}\beta_{n-l}^{\left(k\right)}\left(\lambda\right)\left(x\mid\lambda\right)_{l}

\displaystyle=\sum_{l=0}^{n}\binom{n}{l}\beta_{n-l}^{\left(k\right)}\left(\lambda\right)\sum_{j=0}^{l-1}\frac{1}{x-\lambda j}\prod_{i=0}^{l-1}\left(x-\lambda i\right)

\displaystyle=\sum_{l=0}^{n}\binom{n}{l}\beta_{n-l}^{\left(k\right)}\left(\lambda\right)\sum_{j=0}^{l-1}\prod_{\begin{subarray}{c}i=0\\
i\neq j\end{subarray}}^{l-1}\left(x-\lambda i\right).
