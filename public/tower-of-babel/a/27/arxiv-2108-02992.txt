Large population games with interactions through controls and common noise: convergence results and equivalence between o ​ p ​ e ​ n open – l ​ o ​ o ​ p loop and c ​ l ​ o ​ s ​ e ​ d closed – l ​ o ​ o ​ p loop controls
Mao Fabrice Djete

Abstract
In the presence of a common noise, we study the convergence problems in mean field game (MFG) and mean field control (MFC) problem where the cost function and the state dynamics depend upon the joint conditional distribution of the controlled state and the control process. In the first part, we consider the MFG setting. We start by recalling the notions of m ​ e ​ a ​ s ​ u ​ r ​ e measure – v ​ a ​ l ​ u ​ e ​ d valued MFG equilibria and of approximate c ​ l ​ o ​ s ​ e ​ d closed – l ​ o ​ o ​ p loop Nash equilibria associated to the corresponding N N –player game. Then, we show that all convergent sequences of approximate c ​ l ​ o ​ s ​ e ​ d closed – l ​ o ​ o ​ p loop Nash equilibria, when N → ∞ , N\to\infty, converge to m ​ e ​ a ​ s ​ u ​ r ​ e measure – v ​ a ​ l ​ u ​ e ​ d valued MFG equilibria. And conversely, any m ​ e ​ a ​ s ​ u ​ r ​ e measure – v ​ a ​ l ​ u ​ e ​ d valued MFG equilibrium is the limit of a sequence of approximate c ​ l ​ o ​ s ​ e ​ d closed – l ​ o ​ o ​ p loop Nash equilibria. In other words, m ​ e ​ a ​ s ​ u ​ r ​ e measure – v ​ a ​ l ​ u ​ e ​ d valued MFG equilibria are the accumulation points of the approximate c ​ l ​ o ​ s ​ e ​ d closed – l ​ o ​ o ​ p loop Nash equilibria. Previous work has shown that m ​ e ​ a ​ s ​ u ​ r ​ e measure – v ​ a ​ l ​ u ​ e ​ d valued MFG equilibria are the accumulation points of the approximate o ​ p ​ e ​ n open – l ​ o ​ o ​ p loop Nash equilibria. Therefore, we obtain that the limits of approximate c ​ l ​ o ​ s ​ e ​ d closed – l ​ o ​ o ​ p loop Nash equilibria and approximate o ​ p ​ e ​ n open – l ​ o ​ o ​ p loop Nash equilibria are the same. In the second part, we deal with the MFC setting. After recalling the c ​ l ​ o ​ s ​ e ​ d closed – l ​ o ​ o ​ p loop and o ​ p ​ e ​ n open – l ​ o ​ o ​ p loop formulations of the MFC problem, we prove that they are equivalent. We also provide some convergence results related to approximate c ​ l ​ o ​ s ​ e ​ d closed – l ​ o ​ o ​ p loop Pareto equilibria.

Our study in this paper treats of extended MFG (or MFG of controls) by giving some convergence and equivalence results.

As mentioned in the beginning, our primarily goal is to make a rigorous connection between the

shows that the limits of

–

–

[

[

The second main result for the MFGC is

In other words, considering measure–valued MFG equilibrium (or weak MFG) is equivalent to considering approximate strong Markovian MFG equilibrium. The approximate strong Markovian MFG equilibrium has the advantage to be close to the usual notion considered in the literature. The only difference is for the approximate strong Markovian MFG equilibrium, the optimality is not an exact one but an

(i)

Remark 2.6

(i)
A reader familiar with MFG of controls would expect another shape for an

.

Denote by

(3.2)

Also, for every
f\in C^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{n}),

(3.3)

and for each

3.1.2
