Abstract
. Finding functions, particularly permutations, with good differential properties has received a lot of attention due to their varied applications. For instance, in combinatorial design theory, a correspondence of perfect c c -nonlinear functions and difference sets in some quasigroups was recently shown by Anbar et al. (J. Comb. Des. 31(12):1-24, 2023). Additionally, in a recent manuscript by Pal et al. (Adv. Math. Communications, to appear), a very interesting connection between the c c -differential uniformity and boomerang uniformity, when c = − 1 c=-1 , was pointed out, showing that they are the same for an odd APN permutation, sparking yet more interest in the construction of functions with low c c -differential uniformity. We investigate the c c -differential uniformity of some classes of permutation polynomials. As a result, we add four more classes of permutation polynomials to the family of functions that only contains a few (non-trivial) perfect c c -nonlinear functions over finite fields of even characteristic. Moreover, we include a class of permutation polynomials with low c c -differential uniformity over the field of characteristic 3 3 . To solve the involved equations over finite fields, we use various number theoretical techniques, in particular, we find explicitly many Walsh transform coefficients and Weil sums that may be of an independent interest.

Let

to denote the multiplicative group of nonzero elements of

. If

\mathbb{F}_{q}
to itself, by using Lagrange’s interpolation formula, one can uniquely express it as a polynomial in
\mathbb{F}_{q}[X]
of degree at most

is a permutation polynomial of
\mathbb{F}_{q}
if the mapping
X\mapsto F(X)
is a bijection on
\mathbb{F}_{q}
. Permutation polynomials over finite fields are of great interest due to their numerous applications in coding theory
[
4
,
14
]
, combinatorial design theory
[
5
]
, cryptography
[
17
,
21
]
, and other areas of mathematics and engineering. These polynomials with some desired properties such as low differential uniformity, high algebraic degree and high nonlinearity act as important candidates in designing cryptographically strong S-boxes and hence in providing secure communication.

Borisov et al.
[
2
]
introduced the concept of the multiplicative differentials of the form
(F(cX),F(X))
and exploited this new class of differentials to attack certain existing ciphers. In 2020, Ellingsen et al.
[
6
]
gave the notion of

-differential uniformity. For any function
F:\mathbb{F}_{q}\to\mathbb{F}_{q}
and for any

, the (multiplicative)

-derivative of

with respect to

{}_{c}\Delta_{F}(X,a):=F(X+a)-cF(X)

of the equation
{}_{c}\Delta_{F}(X,a)=b
. The
c
-differential uniformity of

, denoted by
{}_{c}\Delta_{F}
, is given by
{}_{c}\Delta_{F}:=\max\{_{c}\Delta_{F}(a,b):a,b\in\mathbb{F}_{q}\hskip 5.69046pt\text{and}\hskip 5.69046pta\neq 0\hskip 5.69046pt\text{if}\hskip 5.69046ptc=1\}.
When

, we call

a perfect
c
-nonlinear (P
c
N) function and when

-nonlinear (AP

N) function. Note that for monomial functions,

Recently, the authors in
[

pointed out a connection between the

-differential uniformity (cDU) and combinatorial designs by showing that the graph of a P

N function is a difference set in a quasigroup. Difference sets give rise to symmetric designs, which are known to construct optimal self complementary codes. Some types of designs also have applications in secret sharing and visual cryptography. Moreover, recently, Pal and Stănică

-DDT entries (without the first row/column). In view of the applications stated above, the construction of functions, particularly permutations, with low

\displaystyle\mathcal{W}_{f}(w)^{2}
