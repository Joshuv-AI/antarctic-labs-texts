On a conjecture of Feigin, Wang and Yoshinaga
Hiraku Kawanoue

Abstract
. We settled a conjecture of Feigin, Wang and Yoshinaga, appeared in the preprint “Integral expressions for derivations of multiarrangements”.



This is a short report about a conjecture
of Feigin, Wang and Yoshinaga in
[
1
]
.

In the preprint
[
1
]
,
Feigin, Wang and Yoshinaga constructed a candidate of basis
for the module of logarithmic vector fields of
the extended Catalan arrangement

\operatorname{Cat}(B_{2},m)
of type
B_{2}

in the following way. First
they define

{f_{i}^{m}}(x,y)=\int_{0}^{x}t^{2i}(t^{2}-x^{2})^{m}(t^{2}-y^{2})^{m}\,dt\qquad(i,m\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}).

Set
p=2m+i
. Then
{f_{i}^{m}}(x,y)
is expressed as

{f_{i}^{m}}(x,y)=\sum_{0\leq k\leq m}c_{m,i,k}x^{2p-2k+1}y^{2k}.

Next, they define
\widetilde{f_{i}^{m}}(x,y)
by deforming
{f_{i}^{m}}(x,y)

as

\widetilde{f_{i}^{m}}(x,y)=\sum_{0\leq k\leq m}c_{m,i,k}(x+p-k)_{2p-2k+1}(y+p-m)_{k}(y+m-p+k-1)_{k},

where
(\alpha)_{k}=\alpha\cdot(\alpha-1)\cdot\dotsb\cdot(\alpha-k+1)
.
Using this
\widetilde{f_{i}^{m}}(x,y)
, they define

\widetilde{\eta_{i}^{m}}=\widetilde{f_{i}^{m}}(x,y)\partial_{x}+\widetilde{f_{i}^{m}}(y,x)\partial_{y}.

Note that if

\widetilde{\eta_{0}^{m}},\widetilde{\eta_{1}^{m}}\in D(\operatorname{Cat}(B_{2},m))
,
then they form a basis for
D(\operatorname{Cat}(B_{2},m))
. Actually,
since
\deg\widetilde{\eta_{i}^{m}}=4m+2i+1
and

\operatorname{Cat}(B_{2},m))
is defined by

\varphi=(x+m)_{2m+1}(y+m)_{2m+1}(x+y+m)_{2m+1}(x-y+m)_{2m+1}=0,

there exists a constant
C
such that
the Saito determinant is of the form

|\operatorname{M}(\widetilde{\eta_{0}^{m}},\widetilde{\eta_{1}^{m}})|=\widetilde{f_{0}^{m}}(x,y)\widetilde{f_{1}^{m}}(y,x)-\widetilde{f_{0}^{m}}(y,x)\widetilde{f_{1}^{m}}(x,y)=C\varphi.

Comparing the coefficients of
x^{6m+3}y^{2m+1}
in each side, we have

C=-c_{m,0,m}c_{m,1,0}=-(-1)^{m}\int_{0}^{1}(s^{2}-1)^{m}ds\cdot\int_{0}^{1}s^{2m+2}(s^{2}-1)^{m}ds\neq 0.

Thus, by Saito’s criterion,

\widetilde{\eta_{0}^{m}}
and
\widetilde{\eta_{1}^{m}}
form a basis
for
D(\operatorname{Cat}(B_{2},m))
.

Since

\widetilde{f_{i}^{m}}(x,y)\in((x+p-m)_{2p-2m+1})\subset((x+m)_{2m+1})
,
it is clear that

\widetilde{\eta_{i}^{m}}(x)\in((x+m)_{2m+1}),\quad\widetilde{\eta_{i}^{m}}(y)\in((y+m)_{2m+1}).

Suppose we know that

\widetilde{\eta_{i}^{m}}(x+y)\in((x+y+m)_{2m+1}).

Then, since

\widetilde{f_{i}^{m}}(y,x)
is odd in
x
and even in
y
,
it follows that

\widetilde{\eta_{i}^{m}}(x-y)=\widetilde{f_{i}^{m}}(x,y)-\widetilde{f_{i}^{m}}(y,x)=\widetilde{f_{i}^{m}}(x,-y)+\widetilde{f_{i}^{m}}(-y,x)\in((x-y+m)_{2m+1}).

Therefore the following conjecture
of Feigin, Wang and Yoshinaga
[
1
]

implies that

\widetilde{\eta_{0}^{m}}
and
\widetilde{\eta_{1}^{m}}
form a basis
for
D(\operatorname{Cat}(B_{2},m))
.

Conjecture

(Conjecture 5.5 in
[
1
]
)
.

For
i,m\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}
, we have

\widetilde{f_{i}^{m}}(x+y)+\widetilde{f_{i}^{m}}(x+y)\in((x+y+m)_{2m+1}).

We give an affirmative answer to this conjecture.

(1) We write down the expansion of

\widetilde{f_{i}^{m}}(x,y)
explicitly. We have

\displaystyle{f_{i}^{m}}(x,y)=\int_{0}^{x}t^{2i}(t^{2}-x^{2})^{m}(t^{2}-y^{2})^{m}\,dt

\displaystyle=x^{4m+2i+1}\int_{0}^{1}s^{i}(s-1)^{m}(s-(y/x)^{2})^{m}\,\dfrac{ds}{2\sqrt{s}}

\displaystyle=\dfrac{1}{2}x^{4m+2i+1}\sum_{0\leq u\leq m}\binom{m}{u}(-1)^{u}(y/x)^{2(m-u)}\int_{0}^{1}s^{u+i-1/2}(1-s)^{m}\,ds

\displaystyle=\dfrac{1}{2}\sum_{0\leq u\leq m}\binom{m}{u}(-1)^{u}x^{2m+2i+2u+1}y^{2m-2u}B(u+i+1/2,m+1)

\displaystyle=\dfrac{1}{2}\sum_{0\leq u\leq m}\binom{m}{u}(-1)^{u}x^{2m+2i+2u+1}y^{2m-2u}\dfrac{m!}{(m+i+u+1/2)_{m+1}}.

By definition of

\widetilde{f_{i}^{m}}(x,y)
, it follows that

2\widetilde{f_{i}^{m}}(x,y)=\sum_{0\leq u\leq m}F_{i,m,u}
,
where

\displaystyle F_{i,m,u}

\displaystyle=\binom{m}{u}\dfrac{(-1)^{u}m!}{(m+i+u+1/2)_{m+1}}

\displaystyle\phantom{=}(x+m+i+u)_{2m+2i+2u+1}(y+m+i)_{m-u}(y-i-u-1)_{m-u}.

(2) We construct a recurrence formula for

\widetilde{f_{i}^{m}}(x,y)
. For this purpose,
we denote a partial sum of
\widetilde{f_{i}^{m}}(x,y)

as

S_{i,m,l}=\sum_{l\leq u\leq m}F_{i,m,u}
,
and set

M_{i,m,l}=-2S_{i+1,m,l}+(x^{2}+y^{2}-(i+m+1)^{2}-i^{2})S_{i,m,l}-\dfrac{2i-1}{2m+2}S_{i-1,m+1,l+1}.

Lemma 1
.

\displaystyle M_{i,m,l}

\displaystyle=\binom{m}{l}\dfrac{(-1)^{l}m!}{(m+i+l+1/2)_{m+1}}\{y^{2}+l(2m+2i+l+2)-i^{2}\}

\displaystyle\phantom{=}(x+m+i+l)_{2m+2i+2l+1}(y+m+i)_{m-l}(y-i-l-1)_{m-l}.

Proof.

Proof by descending induction on
l
.

The case
l=m+1
is clear since

S_{\ast,m,m+1}=S_{\ast,m+1,m+2}=\binom{m}{m+1}=0
.

Let
l\leq m
and assume the formula holds for
M_{i,m,l+1}
.
Since

M_{i,m,l}=-2F_{i+1,m,l}+(x^{2}+y^{2}-(i+m+1)^{2}-i^{2})F_{i,m,l}-\dfrac{2i-1}{2m+2}F_{i-1,m+1,l+1}+M_{i,m,l+1},

we have

\displaystyle M_{i,m,l}=-2\binom{m}{l}\dfrac{(-1)^{l}m!}{(m+i+l+3/2)_{m+1}}(x+m+i+l+1)_{2m+2i+2l+3}(y+m+i+1)_{m-l}

\displaystyle(y-i-l-2)_{m-l}+(x^{2}+y^{2}-(i+m+1)^{2}-i^{2})\binom{m}{l}\dfrac{(-1)^{l}m!}{(m+i+l+1/2)_{m+1}}

\displaystyle(x+m+i+l)_{2m+2i+2l+1}(y+m+i)_{m-l}(y-i-l-1)_{m-l}-\dfrac{2i-1}{2m+2}\binom{m+1}{l+1}

\displaystyle\dfrac{(-1)^{l+1}(m+1)!}{(m+i+l+3/2)_{m+2}}(x+m+i+l+1)_{2m+2i+2l+3}(y+m+i)_{m-l}(y-i-l-1)_{m-l}

\displaystyle+\binom{m}{l+1}\dfrac{(-1)^{l+1}m!}{(m+i+l+3/2)_{m+1}}(y^{2}+(l+1)(2m+2i+l+3)-i^{2})\cdot

\displaystyle(x+m+i+l+1)_{2m+2i+2l+3}(y+m+i)_{m-l-1}(y-i-l-2)_{m-l-1}

\displaystyle=\binom{m}{l}\dfrac{(-1)^{l}m!}{(m+i+l+3/2)_{m+2}}(x+m+i+l)_{2m+2i+2l+1}(y+m+i)_{m-l-1}(y-i-l-2)_{m-l-1}

\displaystyle\Bigl[-2(i+l+1/2)\{x^{2}-(m+i+l+1)^{2}\}\{y^{2}-(m+i+1)^{2}\}

\displaystyle+(m+i+l+3/2)(x^{2}+y^{2}-(i+m+1)^{2}-i^{2})\{y^{2}-(l+i+1)^{2}\}

\displaystyle+\dfrac{2i-1}{2}\dfrac{m+1}{l+1}\{x^{2}-(m+i+l+1)^{2}\}\{y^{2}-(l+i+1)^{2}\}

\displaystyle-\dfrac{m-l}{l+1}(i+l+1/2)(y^{2}+(l+1)(2m+2i+l+3)-i^{2})\cdot\{x^{2}-(m+i+l+1)^{2}\}\Bigr].

One can show that

\Bigl[\cdots\Bigr]=(m+i+l+3/2)\{y^{2}-(i+l+1)^{2}\}(y^{2}+l(2m+2i+l+2)-i^{2}),

which verifies the formula for
M_{i,m,l}
.
∎

Proposition 1
.

\dfrac{2i-1}{2m+2}\widetilde{f_{i-1}^{m+1}}(x,y)=\Bigl\{x^{2}+y^{2}-(i+m+1)^{2}-i^{2}\Bigr\}\widetilde{f_{i}^{m}}(x,y)-2\widetilde{f_{i+1}^{m}}(x,y).

Proof.

By applying Lemma
1
with
l=0
, we have

M_{i,m,0}=\dfrac{m!}{(m+i+1/2)_{m+1}}(x+m+i)_{2m+2i+1}(y+m+i)_{m+1}(y-i)_{m+1}.

On the other hand, we have

F_{i-1,m+1,0}=\dfrac{(m+1)!}{(m+i+1/2)_{m+2}}(x+m+i)_{2m+2i+1}(y+m+i)_{m+1}(y-i)_{m+1}.

It follows that

\dfrac{2i-1}{2m+2}F_{i-1,m+1,0}=M_{i,m,0}
,
and hence

\displaystyle\dfrac{2i-1}{2m+2}S_{i-1,m+1,0}

\displaystyle=M_{i,m,0}+\dfrac{2i-1}{2m+2}S_{i-1,m+1,1}

\displaystyle=-2S_{i+1,m,0}+\Bigl\{x^{2}+y^{2}-(i+m+1)^{2}-i^{2}\Bigr\}S_{i,m,0}.

Now the assertion is clear since

S_{i,m,0}=2\widetilde{f_{i}^{m}}(x,y)
.
∎

Lemma 2
.

\displaystyle\sum_{t=a}^{\infty}\dfrac{(b+t)_{2t}}{(z+t)_{2t+2}}=\dfrac{(b+a)_{2a}}{(z+b)(z+a-1)_{2a}(z-b-1)}\quad(a,b\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0})
.

Proof.

Proof by descending induction on
a
.

First consider the case
a>b
. Then, we have

(b+t)_{2t}=0
for any
t\geq a
. Therefore
both sides of the asserted formula is
0
.

Assume that the assertion holds for
a+1
.
By the induction hypothesis, we have

\displaystyle\sum_{t=a}^{\infty}\dfrac{(b+t)_{2t}}{(z+t)_{2t+2}}=\dfrac{(b+a)_{2a}}{(z+a)_{2a+2}}+\dfrac{(b+a+1)_{2a+2}}{(z+b)(z+a)_{2a+2}(z-b-1)}

\displaystyle=\dfrac{(b+a)_{2a}}{(z+b)(z+a)_{2a+2}(z-b-1)}\Bigl\{(z+b)(z-b-1)+(b+a+1)(b-a)\Bigr\}

\displaystyle=\dfrac{(b+a)_{2a}(z+a)(z-a-1)}{(z+b)(z+a)_{2a+2}(z-b-1)}=\dfrac{(b+a)_{2a}}{(z+b)(z+a-1)_{2a}(z-b-1)}.\qed

For
i\in\mathbb{Z}
and
m,k,l\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}
, we define

N_{i,m,k,l}=-2U_{i+1,m,k,l}+\Bigl\{\Bigl(k+\dfrac{1}{2}\Bigr)^{2}+y^{2}-(i+m+1)^{2}-i^{2}\Bigr\}U_{i,m,k,l}-\dfrac{2i-1}{2m+2}U_{i-1,m+1,k,l}.

where

U_{i,m,k,l}=\sum_{t=l}^{k}G_{i,m,k,t}
and

\displaystyle G_{i,m,k,t}=-(i-1/2)_{2i+m-k}(y+m-k-1/2)_{2m-2k}(m+t)_{m}(k+t)_{2t}

\displaystyle\phantom{=-}(i+2m+t+1/2)_{t}(i+m+k+1/2)_{k-t}(y+m+k+1/2)_{k-t}(y-m-t-3/2)_{k-t}.

Lemma 3
.

\displaystyle N_{i,m,k,l}

\displaystyle=\dfrac{(m+l)_{m+1}}{m+1}(k+l)_{2l}(i-1/2)_{2i+m-k}(i+m+k+1/2)_{k-l}(i+2m+l+1/2)_{l-1}

\displaystyle\phantom{=}(y+m+k+1/2)_{k-l}(y+m-k-1/2)_{2m-2k}(y-m-l-3/2)_{k-l}

\displaystyle\phantom{=}\Bigl\{(y^{2}-i^{2})(i+3m+l+5/2)+(i+m+l+1/2)(i+m-k+1/2)(i+m+k+3/2)\Bigr\}.

Proof.

Proof by descending induction on
l
.
First consider the case
l>k
. Then,
we have
U_{\ast,\ast,k,l}=0
and hence

N_{i,m,k,l}=0
. We also have
(k+l)_{2l}=0
.
Therefore both sides of the asserted formula is
0
.

Assume that the assertion holds for
l+1
.
By definition, we have

\displaystyle N_{i,m,k,l}

\displaystyle=N_{i,m,k,l+1}+

\displaystyle\phantom{=}\Bigl[-2G_{i+1,m,k,l}+\Bigl\{\Bigl(k+\dfrac{1}{2}\Bigr)^{2}+y^{2}-(i+m+1)^{2}-i^{2}\Bigr\}G_{i,m,k,l}-\dfrac{2i-1}{2m+2}G_{i-1,m+1,k,l}\Bigr].

By the induction hypothesis, it follows that

\displaystyle N_{i,m,k,l}=\dfrac{(m+l+1)_{m+1}}{m+1}(k+l+1)_{2l+2}(i-1/2)_{2i+m-k}(i+m+k+1/2)_{k-l-1}(i+2m+l+3/2)_{l}

\displaystyle(y+m+k+1/2)_{k-l-1}(y+m-k-1/2)_{2m-2k}(y-m-l-5/2)_{k-l-1}

\displaystyle\Bigl\{(y^{2}-i^{2})(i+3m+l+7/2)+(i+m+l+3/2)(i+m-k+1/2)(i+m+k+3/2)\Bigr\}

\displaystyle-2\Bigl[-(i+1/2)_{2i+m-k+2}(y+m-k-1/2)_{2m-2k}(m+l)_{m}(k+l)_{2l}

\displaystyle(i+2m+l+3/2)_{l}(i+m+k+3/2)_{k-l}(y+m+k+1/2)_{k-l}(y-m-l-3/2)_{k-l}\Bigr]

\displaystyle+\Bigl\{\Bigl(k+\dfrac{1}{2}\Bigr)^{2}+y^{2}-(i+m+1)^{2}-i^{2}\Bigr\}\Bigl[-(i-1/2)_{2i+m-k}(y+m-k-1/2)_{2m-2k}(m+l)_{m}(k+l)_{2t}

\displaystyle(i+2m+l+1/2)_{l}(i+m+k+1/2)_{k-l}(y+m+k+1/2)_{k-l}(y-m-l-3/2)_{k-l}\Bigr]

\displaystyle-\dfrac{2i-1}{2m+2}\Bigl[-(i-3/2)_{2i+m-k-1}(y+m-k+1/2)_{2m-2k+2}(m+l+1)_{m+1}(k+l)_{2l}

\displaystyle(i+2m+l+3/2)_{l}(i+m+k+1/2)_{k-l}(y+m+k+3/2)_{k-l}(y-m-l-5/2)_{k-l}\Bigr]

\displaystyle=\dfrac{(m+l)_{m}}{m+1}(k+l)_{2l}(i-1/2)_{2i+m-k}(i+m+k+1/2)_{k-l-1}(i+2m+l+1/2)_{l-1}

\displaystyle(y+m+k+1/2)_{k-l-1}(y+m-k-1/2)_{2m-2k}(y-m-l-5/2)_{k-l-1}

\displaystyle\Bigl[(m+l+1)(k+l+1)(k-l)(i+2m+l+3/2)\Bigl\{(y^{2}-i^{2})(i+3m+l+7/2)

\displaystyle+(i+m+l+3/2)(i+m-k+1/2)(i+m+k+3/2)\Bigr\}+2(m+1)(i+1/2)(-i-m+k-1/2)

\displaystyle(i+2m+l+3/2)(i+m+k+3/2)(y+m+l+3/2)(y-m-l-3/2)

\displaystyle-\Bigl\{\Bigl(k+\dfrac{1}{2}\Bigr)^{2}+y^{2}-(i+m+1)^{2}-i^{2}\Bigr\}(m+1)(i+2m+3/2)(i+m+l+3/2)

\displaystyle(y+m+l+3/2)(y-m-l-3/2)+(m+l+1)(y+m-k+1/2)(y-m+k-1/2)

\displaystyle(i+2m+l+3/2)(i+m+l+3/2)(y+m+k+3/2)(y-m-k-3/2)\Bigr]

\displaystyle=\dfrac{(m+l)_{m}}{m+1}(k+l)_{2l}(i-1/2)_{2i+m-k}(i+m+k+1/2)_{k-l-1}(i+2m+l+1/2)_{l-1}

\displaystyle(y+m+k+1/2)_{k-l-1}(y+m-k-1/2)_{2m-2k}(y-m-l-5/2)_{k-l-1}

\displaystyle\Bigl[\ l(i+m+l+3/2)(y+m+l+3/2)(y-m-l-3/2)

which verifies the assertion for

Proposition 2
.

For
k\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}
, we have

\displaystyle 2\widetilde{f_{i}^{m}}(-1/2-k,y)=-(i-1/2)_{2i+m-k}(y+m-k-1/2)_{2m-2k}\sum_{t=0}^{k}(m+t)_{m}(k+t)_{2t}

\displaystyle(i+2m+t+1/2)_{t}(i+m+k+1/2)_{k-t}(y+m+k+1/2)_{k-t}(y-m-t-3/2)_{k-t}.

Proof.

Notice that
({\rm RHS})=U_{i,m,k,0}
.
We denote
({\rm LHS})
as
T_{i,m,k}
.

First consider the case
m=0
. By definition, we have

\displaystyle U_{i,0,k,0}

\displaystyle=-(i-1/2)_{2i-k}(y-k-1/2)_{-2k}\sum_{t=0}^{k}(k+t)_{2t}

\displaystyle(i+t+1/2)_{t}(i+k+1/2)_{k-t}(y+k+1/2)_{k-t}(y-t-3/2)_{k-t}

\displaystyle=-\dfrac{(i+k+1/2)_{2i+1}}{i+1/2}\Bigl\{y^{2}-(k+1/2)^{2}\Bigr\}\sum_{t=0}^{k}\dfrac{(k+t)_{2t}}{(y+t+1/2)_{2t+2}}.

By applying Lemma
2
with

a=0
,
b=k\geq 0
and
z=y+1/2
,
we have

\displaystyle U_{i,0,k,0}

\displaystyle=-\dfrac{(i+k+1/2)_{2i+1}}{i+1/2}\Bigl\{y^{2}-(k+1/2)^{2}\Bigr\}\dfrac{1}{(y+k+1/2)(y-k-1/2)}

\displaystyle=-\dfrac{(i+k+1/2)_{2i+1}}{i+1/2},

and it follows that

T_{i,0,k}=F_{i,0,0}|_{x=-1/2-k}=\dfrac{(-1/2-k+i)_{2i+1}}{i+1/2}=-\dfrac{(i+k+1/2)_{2i+1}}{i+1/2}=U_{i,0,k,0}.

Next assume
m\geq 1
. Since
T_{i,m,k}=S_{i,m,0}|_{x=-1/2-k}
,
Proposition
1
implies that

\dfrac{2i-1}{2m+2}T_{i-1,m+1,k}=\Bigl\{\Bigl(k+\dfrac{1}{2}\Bigr)^{2}+y^{2}-(i+m+1)^{2}-i^{2}\Bigr\}T_{i,m,k}-2T_{i+1,m,k}.

On the other hand,
since
N_{i,m,k,0}=0
by Lemma
3
, we have

-2U_{i+1,m,k,0}+\Bigl\{\Bigl(k+\dfrac{1}{2}\Bigr)^{2}+y^{2}-(i+m+1)^{2}-i^{2}\Bigr\}U_{i,m,k,0}-\dfrac{2i-1}{2m+2}U_{i-1,m+1,k,0}=0.

Therefore,
T_{i,m,k}
and
U_{i,m,k,0}
satisfy the same recurrence formula,
and they coincide when
m=0
. Therefore we can conclude that

T_{i,m,k}=U_{i,m,k,0}
.
∎

(4) We determine the factors of

Proposition 3
.

The following holds.

(a)
There exists a constant
A_{i,m}
depending only on
i
and
m
such that

\displaystyle 2\widetilde{f_{i}^{m}}(x,y)

\displaystyle\equiv\phantom{-}A_{i,m}(x+2m+i)_{3m+2i+1}(x+m-1/2)_{m}

\displaystyle\equiv-A_{i,m}(y+2m+i)_{3m+2i+1}(y+m-1/2)_{m}\mod{(x+y+m)}.

(b)
There exists a constant
B_{i,m}
depending only on
i
and
m
such that

\displaystyle 2\widetilde{f_{i}^{m}}(x,y)

\displaystyle\equiv\phantom{-}B_{i,m}(x+m+i)_{3m+2i+1}(x-1/2)_{m}

\displaystyle\equiv-B_{i,m}(y+m+i)_{3m+2i+1}(y-1/2)_{m}\mod{(x+y-m)}.

Proof.

(a) Since
F_{i,m,u}
is divided by

\displaystyle(x+m+i+u)_{2m+2i+2u+1}(y-i-u-1)_{m-u}

\displaystyle=(-1)^{m-u}(x+m+i+u)_{2m+2i+2u+1}(-y+i+m)_{m-u}

\displaystyle\equiv(-1)^{m-u}(x+m+i+u)_{2m+2i+2u+1}(x+2m+i)_{m-u}\mod{(x+y+m)}

\displaystyle=(-1)^{m-u}(x+2m+i)_{3m+2i+u+1}=(-1)^{m-u}(x+2m+i)_{3m+2i+1}(x-m-i-1)_{u},

we have

S_{i,m,0}\in((x+2m+i)_{3m+2i+1})+(x+y+m)
.

Since Proposition
2
guarantees

2\widetilde{f_{i}^{m}}(-1/2-k,k-m+1/2)=0\quad(0\leq k<m),

we have

S_{i,m,0}\in((x+m-1/2)_{m})+(x+y+m)
.
It follows that

S_{i,m,0}\in((x+2m+i)_{3m+2i+1}(x+m-1/2)_{m})+(x+y+m).

Since
\deg S_{i,m,0}\leq 4m+2i+1
, there exists a constant

A_{i,m}
such that

S_{i,m,0}\equiv A_{i,m}(x+2m+i)_{3m+2i+1}(x+m-1/2)_{m}\mod{(x+y+m)}.
