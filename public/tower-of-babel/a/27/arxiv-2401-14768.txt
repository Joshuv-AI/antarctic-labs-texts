On Mixed Cages of Girth 6
Gabriela Araujo-Pardo, Lydia Mirabel Mendoza-Cadena

Abstract
A [ z , r ; g ] [z,r;g] -mixed cage is a mixed graph of minimum order such that each vertex has z z in-arcs, z z out-arcs, r r edges, and it has girth g g . We present an infinite family of mixed graphs with girth 6. This construction also provides an upper bound on the minimum order of mixed cages of girth 6. Additionally, we introduce a lower bound on the minimum order for any mixed cage. Keywords: mixed cages; girth; biaffine plane; projective plane; order bounds

-mixed cages, for certain pairs of

We describe the construction of digraph

.

For all

prime and

Proof.

Parameters
\frac{p+1}{2}

4

For all

Suppose that
K
is a mixed cycle so that
|K|<6
. It is not difficult to see that
K
cannot have length 2 nor 3.

We show that
|K|\neq 4
. As we only have arcs between lines (points) that share the first same coordinate (one in vertex is in
B_{q}

B^{\prime}_{q}

B_{q}

B^{\prime}_{q}

B_{q}

|K|\neq 5

Conclusions

We provided a general lower bound for the minimum order of
[z,r;g]
-mixed cages for any value of
z,r
, and
g
.

We obtained an infinity family of mixed graphs of girth 6 using the biaffine plane. We also tried to generate families using the projective plane, but this seems a more challenging problem. Furthermore, we conjecture that this is not possible as the graphs generated by projective planes are already quite dense (those are already undirected cages of girth 6). In this sense, the Galois field of order 4 that generated the
[1,3;6]
-mixed cage is quite special. Its symmetrical structure allowed us to safely remove points and lines, and add the missing arcs.

There is still a long path to find families of girth 5 and 6 that provide better upper bounds.
