Some results on the maximal chromatic polynomials of 2 2 -connected k k -chromatic graphs
Yan Yang

Abstract
In 2015, Brown and Erey conjectured that every 2 2 -connected graph G G on n n vertices with chromatic number k ≥ 4 k\geq 4 has at most ( x − 1 ) k − 1 ​ ( ( x − 1 ) n − k + 1 + ( − 1 ) n − k ) (x-1)_{k-1}\big((x-1)^{n-k+1}+(-1)^{n-k}\big) proper x x -colorings for all x ≥ k x\geq k . Engbers, Erey, Fox, and He proved this conjecture for x = k x=k . In this paper, we prove Brown and Erey’s conjecture under the condition that either the clique number of G G is k k , or the independent number of G G is 2 2 . Keywords: graph coloring, chromatic polynomial, k k -chromatic, independent number. Mathematics Subject Classification (2010): 05C15, 05C31

Introduction

Two

[

in 1912 with the hope of proving the Four Color Conjecture. For a book devoted to this topic, see
[

In this paper we focus on the maximum chromatic polynomials of
k
-chromatic graphs. We denote

the

of

vertices respectively,

the complete graph on
n

If

A graph is

-\frac{(n-k)(n-k-1)}{2}(x-1)^{n-k-2}+(-1)^{n-k-1}(n-k-2)x<0
, then we have

P(G,x)<(x-2)_{k-2}\big((x-1)^{n-k+2}-(x-1)\big)\leq f_{n,k}(x).

the theorem holds.

The proof is completed.
