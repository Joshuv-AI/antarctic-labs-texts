Pair Production of the Open Superstrings from the Parallel-dressed D3-branes in the Compact Spacetime


Abstract
We employ the boundary state formalism to compute the pair creation rate of the open superstrings from the interaction of two parallel D3-branes. The branes live in the partially compact spacetime. In addition, they have been dressed with the internal gauge potentials and the Kalb-Ramond field.

As a special case, the pair production rate

The associated boundary state to the D

-brane

We begin with the following sigma-model

\displaystyle S=

\displaystyle-

\displaystyle\frac{1}{4\pi\alpha^{\prime}}\int_{\Sigma}{\rm d}^{2}\sigma\left(\sqrt{-h}h^{ab}G_{\mu\nu}\partial_{a}X^{\mu}\partial_{b}X^{\nu}+\varepsilon^{ab}B_{\mu\nu}\partial_{a}X^{\mu}\partial_{b}X^{\nu}\right)

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2\pi\alpha^{\prime}}\int_{\partial\Sigma}{\rm d}\sigma\left(A_{\alpha}\partial_{\sigma}X^{\alpha}\right).

A_{\alpha}=-\frac{1}{2}F_{\alpha\beta}X^{\beta}

We employ the flat worldsheet

G_{\mu\nu}=\eta_{\mu\nu}=\rm{diag}{(-1,1,\cdots,1)}

Note that the devoted indices to the

\alpha,\beta\in\{0,\alpha_{\rm n},\beta_{\rm n},\alpha_{\rm c},\beta_{\rm c}\}

\{\alpha_{\rm n},\beta_{\rm n}\}\cup\{\alpha_{\rm c},\beta_{\rm c}\}=\{1,2,\cdots,p\}

Some of the brane directions and

For the perpendicular directions

The bosonic portions of the boundary state equations

\displaystyle\left(\partial_{\tau}X^{\alpha}+\mathcal{F}^{\alpha}\;_{\beta}\partial_{\sigma}X^{\beta}-B^{\alpha}\;_{i}\partial_{\sigma}X^{i}\right)_{\tau=0}|B_{x}\rangle=0,

\displaystyle\left(X^{i}-y^{i}\right)_{\tau=0}|B_{x}\rangle=0,

(2.2)

where
\mathcal{F}_{\alpha\beta}=\partial_{\alpha}A_{\beta}-\partial_{\beta}A_{\alpha}-B_{\alpha\beta}

On the closed string boundary we applied

\left(\delta X^{i}\right)_{\tau=0}=\left(X^{i}-y^{i}\right)_{\tau=0}

The parameters

s represent the

p
-branes. The second equation
implies that

The solution of the equation of motion of closed string is

\displaystyle X^{\mu}(\sigma,\tau)=x^{\mu}+2\alpha^{\prime}p^{\mu}\tau+2L^{\mu}\sigma+\frac{i}{2}\sqrt{2\alpha^{\prime}}\sum_{m\neq 0}\frac{1}{m}\left(\alpha^{\mu}_{m}\;e^{-2im\left(\tau-\sigma\right)}+\tilde{\alpha}^{\mu}_{m}\;e^{-2im\left(\tau+\sigma\right)}\right).

(2.3)

is zero and
N^{\mu}R^{\mu}
, respectively.
If the
x^{\mu}
-direction is compact
it has the radius
R^{\mu}
. Thus, around this direction,
the closed string possesses a winding number
N^{\mu}
.
Note that we assume the time direction
x^{0}

is non-compact.

By combining Eqs. (
2.2

(
2.3

\displaystyle\left[\alpha^{\alpha}_{n}+\tilde{\alpha}^{\alpha}_{-n}-\mathcal{F}^{\alpha}\;_{\beta}\left(\alpha^{\beta}_{n}-\tilde{\alpha}^{\beta}_{-n}\right)\right]|B_{x}\rangle=0,

\displaystyle\left(\alpha^{\prime}p^{\alpha}+\mathcal{F}^{\alpha}\;_{\beta}L^{\beta}\right)|B_{x}\rangle=0,

\displaystyle\left(\alpha^{i}_{n}-\tilde{\alpha}^{i}_{-n}\right)|B_{x}\rangle=0,

\displaystyle\left(x^{i}-y^{i}\right)|B_{x}\rangle=0,

\displaystyle L^{i}|B_{x}\rangle=0.

(2.4)

The last equation implies that the closed string cannot
wind around the compact directions which are
perpendicular to the brane.

The action (
2.1
)
is invariant under the global worldsheet

\partial_{+}X^{\mu}\left(\sigma,\tau\right)\to i\eta\Psi^{\mu}_{+}\left(\sigma,\tau\right)

\displaystyle\left(\Psi^{i}_{-}+i\eta\Psi^{i}_{+}\right)_{\tau=0}|B_{\Psi},\eta\rangle=0,

GSO projection. In terms of the fermions oscillators

\displaystyle\left(\Psi^{\alpha}_{q}-i\eta\mathcal{Q}^{\alpha}\;_{\beta}\tilde{\Psi}^{\beta}_{-q}\right)|B_{\Psi},\eta\rangle=0,

\displaystyle\left(\Psi^{i}_{q}+i\eta\tilde{\Psi}^{i}_{-q}\right)|B_{\Psi},\eta\rangle=0,

(NS-NS) sector. The orthogonal matrix

\mathcal{Q}
possesses the definition

\mathcal{Q}=\left({\bf 1}-\mathcal{F}\right)^{-1}\left({\bf 1}+\mathcal{F}\right)
.

Now we solve Eqs. (
2.4

For the bosonic part, the solution of
equations (

\displaystyle|B_{x}\rangle

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{T_{p}}{2}\sqrt{\det\left({\bf 1}-\mathcal{F}\right)}\prod_{i}\delta\left(x^{i}-y^{i}\right)|p^{i}=0\rangle

(2.7)

\displaystyle\times

\displaystyle\exp{\left(-\sum^{\infty}_{m=1}\frac{1}{m}\alpha^{\mu}_{-m}\mathcal{S}_{\mu\nu}\tilde{\alpha}^{\nu}_{-m}\right)}|0\rangle_{\alpha}|0\rangle_{\tilde{\alpha}}\prod_{\alpha}|p^{\alpha}\rangle,

\mathcal{S}^{\mu}\;_{\nu}=\left(\mathcal{Q}^{\alpha}\;_{\beta}\;,-\delta^{i}\;_{j}\right)

-brane.

Eqs. (

\displaystyle|B_{\Psi},\eta\rangle_{\rm NS}

\displaystyle\exp\left(i\eta\sum_{r=1/2}^{\infty}b^{\mu}_{-r}\mathcal{S}_{\mu\nu}\tilde{b}^{\nu}_{-r}\right)|0\rangle,

(2.8)

\displaystyle|B_{\Psi},\eta\rangle_{\rm R}

\displaystyle\left[\det\left({\bf 1}-\mathcal{F}\right)\right]^{-1/2}\;\exp\left(i\eta\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}d^{\mu}_{-n}\mathcal{S}_{\mu\nu}\tilde{d}^{\nu}_{-n}\right)|B_{\Psi},\eta\rangle^{(0)}_{\rm R}.

3
The D
p
-branes interaction

The overlap of the boundary states,
via the closed string propagator,
gives the tree-level interaction amplitude
between two parallel-dressed branes

\mathcal{A}_{\rm NS-NS(R-R)}=_{\rm NS(R)}\langle B_{1}|D|B_{2}\rangle_{\rm NS(R)}
.
The closed string propagator is as follows

\displaystyle D

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{\alpha^{\prime}}{4\pi}\int_{|z|\leq 1}\frac{{\rm d}^{2}z}{|z|^{2}}z^{L_{0}}\bar{z}^{\bar{L}_{0}},

(3.1)

where
L_{0}
and
\tilde{L}_{0}

the right- and left-moving total Virasoro
generators, including the
bosonic, fermionic, conformal ghosts and

The total interaction amplitude, via the exchange of

\mathcal{A}_{\rm{cylinder}}=\mathcal{A}_{\rm NS-NS}+\mathcal{A}_{\rm R-R}

Thus, we acquire

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}_{\rm{cylinder}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{2^{\left[\frac{p+1}{2}\right]-3}\;T_{p}^{2}}{\left(2\pi\right)^{9-p}}\alpha^{\prime}\left[\det{\left({\bf 1}-\mathcal{F}_{1}\right)}\det{\left({\bf 1}-\mathcal{F}_{2}\right)}\right]^{1/2}\int^{\infty}_{0}{\rm d}t\bigg\{\left(\frac{\pi}{\alpha^{\prime}t}\right)^{d_{i_{\rm n}}/2}

(3.2)

\displaystyle\times

\displaystyle\exp{\left(-\frac{Y^{2}_{\rm n}}{4\alpha^{\prime}t}\right)}\prod_{i_{\rm c}}\Theta_{3}\left(\frac{y_{1}^{i_{\rm c}}-y_{2}^{i_{\rm c}}}{2\pi R_{i_{\rm c}}}\bigg|\frac{i\alpha^{\prime}t}{\pi R_{i_{\rm c}}^{2}}\right)\left[\eta\left(it\right)\right]^{3\left[\frac{p+1}{2}\right]-12}

\displaystyle\times

\displaystyle\left(\sum_{\{N^{\alpha_{\rm c}}\}}\bigg[\left(2\pi\right)^{p+1}\prod_{\alpha}\delta\left(p^{\alpha}_{1}-p^{\alpha}_{2}\right)\exp{\left(-\frac{t}{\alpha^{\prime}}l^{\alpha_{\rm c}}l^{\beta_{\rm c}}\left(\delta_{\alpha_{\rm c}\beta_{\rm c}}+\mathcal{F}^{\alpha}_{1\;\alpha_{\rm c}}\mathcal{F}_{2\;\alpha\beta_{\rm c}}\right)\right)}\bigg]\right)

\displaystyle\times

\displaystyle\bigg(-\Theta_{2}(0|it)^{4-[(p+1)/2]}\prod^{[\frac{p-1}{2}]}_{a=0}\Theta_{2}(\nu_{a}|it)+\Theta_{3}(0|it)^{4-[(p+1)/2]}\prod^{[\frac{p-1}{2}]}_{a=0}\Theta_{3}(\nu_{a}|it)

\displaystyle\times

\displaystyle-\Theta_{4}(0|it)^{4-[(p+1)/2]}\prod^{[\frac{p-1}{2}]}_{a=0}\Theta_{4}(\nu_{a}|it)\bigg)\prod^{[\frac{p-1}{2}]}_{a=0}\frac{\sin(\pi\nu_{a})}{\Theta_{1}(\nu_{a}|it)}\bigg\},

The branes distance along the non-compact

2.4

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}_{\rm{cylinder}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{T_{3}^{2}\alpha^{\prime}}{(2\pi)^{6}}\sqrt{\det({\bf 1}-\mathcal{F}_{1})\det({\bf 1}-\mathcal{F}_{2})}\int^{\infty}_{0}{\rm d}t\bigg\{\left(\frac{\pi}{\alpha^{\prime}t}\right)^{d_{i_{\rm n}}/2}

\displaystyle\exp{\left(-\frac{Y^{2}_{\rm n}}{4\alpha^{\prime}t}\right)}\prod_{i_{\rm c}}\Theta_{3}\left(\frac{y_{1}^{i_{\rm c}}-y_{2}^{i_{\rm c}}}{2\pi R_{i_{\rm c}}}\bigg|\frac{i\alpha^{\prime}t}{\pi R^{2}_{i_{\rm c}}}\right)\left[\eta(it)\right]^{-6}

\displaystyle\times

\displaystyle\left(\sum_{\{N^{\alpha_{\rm c}}\}}\left(2\pi\right)^{4}\prod_{\alpha}\delta(p^{\alpha}_{1}-p^{\alpha}_{2})\exp{\left(-\frac{t}{\alpha^{\prime}}l^{\alpha_{\rm c}}l^{\beta_{\rm c}}\left(\delta_{\alpha_{\rm c}\beta_{\rm c}}+\mathcal{F}^{\alpha}_{1\;\alpha_{\rm c}}\mathcal{F}_{2\;\alpha\beta_{\rm c}}\right)\right)}\right)

\displaystyle\left[\;\Theta^{2}_{1}\left(\frac{\nu_{0}+\nu_{1}}{2}\bigg|it\right)\;\Theta^{2}_{1}\left(\frac{\nu_{0}-\nu_{1}}{2}\bigg|it\right)\right]\prod^{1}_{a=0}\frac{\sin{\left(\pi\nu_{a}\right)}}{\Theta_{1}\left(\nu_{a}|it\right)}\bigg\},

4
The rate of open superstring pair creation

The Jacobi transformation

will be
used to calculate the open superstring one-loop interaction
amplitude from the cylinder one. This
enables us to understand the nature of the foregoing

However, the annulus amplitude possesses the feature

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}_{\rm annulus}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{T_{3}^{2}\pi^{d_{i_{\rm n}}/2}}{(2\pi)^{6}\alpha^{\prime(d_{i_{\rm n}}-2)/2}}\sqrt{\det({\bf 1}-\mathcal{F}_{1})\det({\bf 1}-\mathcal{F}_{2})}\int^{\infty}_{0}{\rm d}t^{\prime}\bigg\{

\displaystyle\times

\displaystyle\exp{\left(-t^{\prime}Y^{2}_{\rm n}/4\alpha^{\prime}\right)}t^{\prime(d_{i_{\rm n}}-8)/2}\;\prod_{i_{\rm c}}\Theta_{3}\left(\frac{y_{1}^{i_{\rm c}}-y_{2}^{i_{\rm c}}}{2\pi R_{i_{\rm c}}}\bigg|\frac{i\alpha^{\prime}}{t^{\prime}\pi R^{2}_{i_{\rm c}}}\right)

\displaystyle\left[\sum_{\{N^{\alpha_{\rm c}}\}}\left(2\pi\right)^{4}\prod_{\alpha}\delta(p^{\alpha}_{1}-p^{\alpha}_{2})\exp{\left(-\frac{1}{t^{\prime}\alpha^{\prime}}l^{\alpha_{\rm c}}l^{\beta_{\rm c}}\left(\delta_{\alpha_{\rm c}\beta_{\rm c}}+\mathcal{F}^{\alpha}_{1\;\alpha_{\rm c}}\mathcal{F}_{2\;\alpha\beta_{\rm c}}\right)\right)}\right]

\displaystyle\times

\displaystyle\frac{\sin(\pi\nu_{0})\sin(\pi\nu_{1})}{\sinh{(\pi\nu_{0}t^{\prime})}\sinh{(\pi\nu_{1}t^{\prime})}}\left[\cosh{(\pi\nu_{0}t^{\prime})}-\cosh{(\pi\nu_{1}t^{\prime})}\right]^{2}\prod^{\infty}_{m=1}\mathcal{C}_{m}(t^{\prime})\bigg\},

\displaystyle\mathcal{C}_{m}(t^{\prime})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{\left[1-2\cosh{(\pi t^{\prime}(\nu_{0}+\nu_{1}))}e^{-2m\pi t^{\prime}}+e^{-4m\pi t^{\prime}}\right]^{2}}{{\left(1-e^{-2m\pi t^{\prime}}\right)^{4}}\left[1-2\cosh{(2\pi t^{\prime}\nu_{0})}e^{-2m\pi t^{\prime}}+e^{-4m\pi t^{\prime}}\right]}

\displaystyle\times

\displaystyle\frac{\left[1-2\cosh{(\pi t^{\prime}(\nu_{0}-\nu_{1}))}e^{-2m\pi t^{\prime}}+e^{-4m\pi t^{\prime}}\right]^{2}}{1-2\cosh{(2\pi t^{\prime}\nu_{1})}e^{-2m\pi t^{\prime}}+e^{-4m\pi t^{\prime}}}.

\displaystyle\frac{2T_{3}^{2}\pi^{d_{i_{\rm n}}/2}}{\upsilon_{0}V_{4}(2\pi)^{6}\alpha^{\prime(d_{i_{\rm n}}-2)/2}}\sqrt{\det({\bf 1}-\mathcal{F}_{1})\det({\bf 1}-\mathcal{F}_{2})}

(4.3)

\displaystyle\sum_{k}\bigg\{\left(-1\right)^{k+1}\exp{\left(-kY^{2}_{\rm n}/4\upsilon_{0}\alpha^{\prime}\right)}\left(\frac{k}{\upsilon_{0}}\right)^{(d_{i_{\rm n}}-8)/2}

\displaystyle\times

\displaystyle\prod_{i_{\rm c}}\Theta_{3}\left(\frac{y_{1}^{i_{\rm c}}-y_{2}^{i_{\rm c}}}{2\pi R_{i_{\rm c}}}\bigg|\frac{i\alpha^{\prime}\upsilon_{0}}{k\pi R^{2}_{i_{\rm c}}}\right)\bigg[\sum_{\{N^{\alpha_{\rm c}}\}}\left(2\pi\right)^{4}\prod_{\alpha}\delta(p^{\alpha}_{1}-p^{\alpha}_{2})

\displaystyle\times

\displaystyle\exp{\left(-\frac{\upsilon_{0}}{k\alpha^{\prime}}l^{\alpha_{\rm c}}l^{\beta_{\rm c}}\left(\delta_{\alpha_{\rm c}\beta_{\rm c}}+\mathcal{F}^{\alpha}_{1\alpha_{\rm c}}\mathcal{F}_{2\alpha\beta_{\rm c}}\right)\right)}\bigg]\frac{\sinh(\pi\upsilon_{0})\sin(\pi\nu_{1})}{\sinh{(\frac{k\pi\nu_{1}}{\upsilon_{0}})}}

5
Conclusions

In the context of the superstring theory
we introduced a boundary state, associated
with a D
p
-brane, in which some of the longitudinal and
transverse directions have been compacted on tori.
The brane has been dressed with electric
and magnetic fields. The closed superstring
amplitude was also presented. Afterward, we
changed the cylinder amplitude to the annulus one.

The 1-loop annulus amplitude enabled us
to compute the decay rate of two parallel D3-branes system
through the pair production of the open superstrings.
Since the background fields on the branes are
arbitrary and different, the foregoing rate
found a generalized form. However, for clarity
we chose special electric and magnetic fields on the branes.
Thus, we observed that for
small electric fields the pair production rate is
independent of the branes distance in the
compact subspace. In addition, in comparison with the
non-compact spacetime, this specific configuration
demonstrated that the compactification enhances the value
of the pair creation rate. Besides, the magnetic field
also enhances the rate. Moreover,
for acquiring a nonzero pair production rate,
the electric fields are clearly
needed to induce a polarized region between the branes.
