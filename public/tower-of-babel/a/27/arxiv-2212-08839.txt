Convergence of the tamed-Euler–Maruyama method for SDEs with discontinuous and polynomially growing drift
Kathrin Spendier, Michaela Szölgyenyi

Abstract
Numerical methods for SDEs with irregular coefficients are intensively studied in the literature, with different types of irregularities usually being attacked separately. In this paper we combine two different types of irregularities: polynomially growing drift coefficients and discontinuous drift coefficients. For SDEs that suffer from both irregularities we prove strong convergence of order 1 / 2 1/2 of the tamed-Euler–Maruyama scheme from [ 10 ] . Keywords: stochastic differential equations, discontinuous and polynomially growing drift, tamed-Euler–Maruyama scheme, strong convergence rate Mathematics Subject Classification (2020): 60H10, 65C30

Introduction

We consider time-homogeneous stochastic differential equations (SDEs),

\displaystyle dX_{t}

\displaystyle=\mu(X_{t})dt+\sigma(X_{t})dW_{t},\quad t\in[0,T],\quad X_{0}=\xi,

(1)

where
\xi\in\mathbb{R}
,
\mu,\sigma\colon\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}
are measurable functions,
T\in(0,\infty)
,
W=(W_{t})_{t\in[0,T]}
is a
standard Brownian motion on the filtered probability space
(\Omega,\mathcal{F},\mathbb{P},(\mathbb{F}_{t})_{t\in[0,T]})
, where the filtration satisfies the usual conditions.

The novelty in this paper is that we allow
\mu
to be piecewise Lipschitz and polynomially growing at the same time. Note that the explicit Euler–Maruyama approximation fails to satisfy moment bounds for SDEs with superlinearly growing coefficients.
Therefore, the following numerical method from
[
10
]
is used for approximating the solution of SDE (
1
). Let
N\in\mathbb{N}
and define the equidistant time grid
0=t_{0}<t_{1}<\dots<t_{N}=T
with
t_{k+1}-t_{k}=\delta
for all
k\in\{0,\dots,N-1\}
.
Denote for all
t\in[0,T]
,
{\underline{t}}:=\max\{t_{k}\colon t\geq t_{k}\}
.
The time-continuous tamed-Euler–Maruyama (EM) scheme is given by
X^{(\delta)}_{0}=\xi
and

\displaystyle X^{(\delta)}_{t}=X^{(\delta)}_{\underline{t}}+\frac{\mu(X^{(\delta)}_{\underline{t}})(t-{\underline{t}})}{1+\delta|\mu(X^{(\delta)}_{\underline{t}})|}+\sigma(X^{(\delta)}_{\underline{t}})(W_{t}-W_{{\underline{t}}}),\quad t\in[0,T].

(2)

In this method the drift part
\mu(X^{(\delta)}_{\underline{t}})\delta
is “tamed” by the factor
1/(1+\delta|\mu(X^{(\delta)}_{\underline{t}})|)
, which makes it bounded by
1
, preventing the drift from becoming extraordinarily large.

In case the coefficients
\mu
and
\sigma
are Lipschitz, it is well known that SDE (
1

1/2
. In this paper we consider two different types of irregularities of the drift coefficient that have been studied separately in the literature.

2
Convergence result

Assumption 1

We assume the following on the coefficients of SDE (
1
).

(i-1)

The drift coefficient
\mu\colon\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}
is piecewise Lipschitz on
[\zeta_{1},\zeta_{m}]
with
m\in\mathbb{N}
discontinuities in the points
\zeta_{1}<\ldots<\zeta_{m}\in\mathbb{R}
.

(i-2)

The drift coefficient
\mu\colon\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}
is globally one-sided Lipschitz on
(-\infty,\zeta_{1})
and
(\zeta_{m},\infty)
, i.e there exists a constant
L_{\mu}\in(0,\infty)
such that for all
x,y\in(-\infty,\zeta_{1})
or
(\zeta_{m},\infty)
,
(x-y)(\mu(x)-\mu(y))\leq L_{\mu}|x-y|^{2}
, and
\mu\in C^{1}
on each of the intervals
(-\infty,\zeta_{1}),(\zeta_{1},\zeta_{2}),...,(\zeta_{m},\infty)
with, wherever it exists, at most polynomially growing derivative.

(ii)

The diffusion coefficient
\sigma\colon\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}
is Lipschitz continuous, lies in
\mathcal{C}^{1}
,
\sigma^{\prime}
is Lipschitz, and for all
k\in\{1,\dots,m\}
,
\sigma(\zeta_{k})\neq 0
.

In the following, for any one-sided or globally Lipschitz function
f
we denote its one-sided or global Lipschitz constant by
L_{f}
.
Note that by
[
29
, Lemma 2.2]
, piecewise Lipschitz functions grow at most linearly. We denote for any function that grows at most linearly by
c_{f}
the smallest constant such that
|f(x)|\leq c_{f}(1+|x|)
.

Theorem 2.1
.

Let Assumption 1 hold.
Then there exists
C^{(\text{tEM})}\in(0,\infty)
such that for all
\delta\in(0,1)
sufficiently small,

\bigg(\mathbb{E}\bigg[\sup_{t\in[0,T]}|X_{t}-X^{(\delta)}_{t}|^{2}\bigg]\bigg)^{\!1/2}\leq C^{(\text{tEM})}\delta^{1/2}.

2.1
Structure of the proof

Our convergence proof is based on a transformation trick from
[
14
,
17
]
in combination with ideas from
[
20
]
and
[
10
]
.
Let
Z=G(X)
, where

is a specific transformation, which we are going to introduce in Section
2.2
. Due to the fact that

will be invertible and bi-Lipschitz, we have
X=G^{-1}(Z)
and get that

\displaystyle\bigg(\mathbb{E}\bigg[\sup_{t\in[0,T]}|X_{t}-X^{(\delta)}_{t}|^{2}\bigg]\bigg)^{\!1/2}\leq L_{G^{-1}}\bigg(\mathbb{E}\bigg[\sup_{t\in[0,T]}|Z_{t}-G(X^{(\delta)}_{t})|^{2}\bigg]\bigg)^{\!1/2}.

(3)

By the triangle inequality,

\displaystyle\bigg(\mathbb{E}\bigg[\sup_{t\in[0,T]}|Z_{t}-G(X^{(\delta)}_{t})|^{2}\bigg]\bigg)^{\!1/2}

\displaystyle\leq\bigg(\mathbb{E}\bigg[\sup_{t\in[0,T]}|Z_{t}-Z^{(\delta)}_{t}|^{2}\bigg]\bigg)^{\!1/2}

(4)

\displaystyle+\bigg(\mathbb{E}\bigg[\sup_{t\in[0,T]}|Z^{(\delta)}_{t}-G(X^{(\delta)}_{t})|^{2}\bigg]\bigg)^{\!1/2},

where
Z^{(\delta)}
is defined as in (
2
) with
\mu,\sigma
replaced by
\tilde{\mu},\tilde{\sigma}
. For estimating the first error term in (
4
), we basically apply the result from
[
10
]
.
We need to show that the tamed-EM approximation
Z^{(\delta)}
converges to
Z
with strong rate
1/2
.
For estimating the second error term in (
4
), we extend ideas from
[
20
]
for the estimation of discontinuity crossing probabilities of the tamed-EM scheme and do a case by case analysis.

Properties of the transformed SDE

All the material we use in this subsection is presented in

We will apply a transform

Lemma 2.4

Let Assumption 1 hold. Then there exists a constant
