Abstract
We say that a graph is k k -mixing if it is possible to transform any k k -coloring into any other via a sequence of single vertex recolorings keeping a proper coloring all along. Cereceda, van den Heuvel and Johnson proved that deciding if a graph is 3 3 -mixing is co-NP-complete and left open the case k ≥ 4 k\geq 4 . We prove that for every k ≥ 4 k\geq 4 , k k -mixing is co-NP-hard.
