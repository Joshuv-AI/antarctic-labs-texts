Theoretical study of N ⁡ ( 1535 ) N(1535) and Σ ∗ ​ ( 1 / 2 − ) \Sigma^{*}(1/2^{-}) in the Cabibbo-favored process Λ c + → p ​ K ¯ 0 ​ η \Lambda_{c}^{+}\to p\bar{K}^{0}\eta
Ying Li, Si-Wei Liu, En Wang, De-Min Li, Li-Sheng Geng, Ju-Jun Xie

Abstract
Motivated by the recent experimental measurements, we have investigated the Cabibbo-favored process Λ c + → p ​ K ¯ 0 ​ η \Lambda_{c}^{+}\to p\bar{K}^{0}\eta , where the N ⁡ ( 1535 ) N(1535) resonance is dynamically generated from the S S -wave pseudoscalar meson-octet baryon interactions within the chiral unitary approach. The contributions from the intermediate N ⁡ ( 1650 ) N(1650) and the predicted low-lying baryon Σ ∗ ​ ( 1 / 2 − ) \Sigma^{*}(1/2^{-}) are also considered. In addition, a Breit-Wigner amplitude for the N ⁡ ( 1535 ) N(1535) resonance is checked. By comparing with the measured η ​ p \eta p , K ¯ 0 ​ η \bar{K}^{0}\eta , and p ​ K ¯ 0 p\bar{K}^{0} invariant mass squared distributions, our results support the interpretation of N ⁡ ( 1535 ) N(1535) as a dynamically generated state. Furthermore, we demonstrate that, with the contribution from Σ ∗ ​ ( 1 / 2 − ) \Sigma^{*}(1/2^{-}) taken into account, the calculated invariant mass spectrum agrees with the Belle measurements. Future precise measurements of the Λ c + → p ​ K ¯ 0 ​ η \Lambda_{c}^{+}\to p\bar{K}^{0}\eta process can further elucidate the existence of the low-lying baryon Σ ∗ ​ ( 1 / 2 − ) \Sigma^{*}(1/2^{-}) .

\displaystyle|\mathcal{T}^{N(1535)}+\mathcal{T}^{N(1650)}e^{i\phi}

\displaystyle+

\displaystyle\mathcal{T}^{\Sigma^{*}(1/2^{-})}e^{i\phi^{\prime}}|^{2},

\displaystyle|\mathcal{T}^{\rm D}|^{2}

(26)

\Sigma^{*}(1/2^{-})

With all the above ingredients, one can write the double differential widths of the process
\Lambda^{+}_{c}\to p\bar{K}^{0}\eta

\displaystyle\frac{d^{2}\Gamma}{dM^{2}_{\eta p}dM^{2}_{p\bar{K}^{0}}}=\frac{1}{(2\pi)^{3}}\frac{1}{32M^{3}_{\Lambda^{+}_{c}}}|\mathcal{T}|^{2},

(27)

\displaystyle\frac{d^{2}\Gamma}{dM^{2}_{\eta p}dM^{2}_{\eta\bar{K}^{0}}}=\frac{1}{(2\pi)^{3}}\frac{1}{32M^{3}_{\Lambda^{+}_{c}}}|\mathcal{T}|^{2}.

(28)

Then one could easily obtain the invariant mass squared distributions
d\Gamma/dM^{2}_{\eta p}
,
d\Gamma/dM^{2}_{p\bar{K}^{0}}
, and
d\Gamma/dM^{2}_{\eta\bar{K}^{0}}

27
) and (
28

M_{12}

\displaystyle\left(M_{23}^{2}\right)_{\min}=\left(E_{2}^{*}+E_{3}^{*}\right)^{2}-\left(\sqrt{E_{2}^{*2}-m_{2}^{2}}+\sqrt{E_{3}^{*2}-m_{3}^{2}}\right)^{2},

\displaystyle\left(M_{23}^{2}\right)_{\max}=\left(E_{2}^{*}+E_{3}^{*}\right)^{2}-\left(\sqrt{E_{2}^{*2}-m_{2}^{2}}-\sqrt{E_{3}^{*2}-m_{3}^{2}}\right)^{2},

where
E_{2}^{*}

\displaystyle E_{2}^{*}=\dfrac{M_{12}^{2}-m_{1}^{2}+m_{2}^{2}}{2M_{12}},

\displaystyle E_{3}^{*}=\dfrac{M_{\Lambda^{+}_{c}}^{2}-M_{12}^{2}-m_{3}^{2}}{2M_{12}},

(29)

with
m_{1}
,

IV
Summary

Recently, the Belle Collaboration reported their amplitude analysis of the
\Lambda_{c}^{+}\to pK_{S}^{0}\eta
and found the resonant structures corresponding to
N(1535)
and
N(1650)
in the
\eta p
invariant mass squared distribution, and a near-threshold enhancement in the
p{K}_{S}^{0}
invariant mass squared distribution, which provides an important lab to explore the properties of the low-lying excited
N(1535)
state and to search for the predicted
\Sigma^{*}(1/2^{-})
resonance.

In this work, we have investigated the process
\Lambda_{c}^{+}\to p\bar{K}^{0}\eta
by considering the contributions from the
N(1535)
,
N(1650)
, and
\Sigma^{*}(1/2^{-})
. We have adopted two models for the contribution of the
N(1535)
; one is the molecular picture, where the
N(1535)
is dynamically generated from the
S
-wave pseudoscalar meson-octet baryon interactions, and the other one is the Breit-Wigner form to treat the
N(1535)
as a genuine baryon state. By fitting to the Belle measurements, one finds that the contribution from the
\Sigma^{*}(1/2^{-})
is crucial to well describing the Belle measurements, which implies that the present experimental data support the existence of the
\Sigma^{*}(1/2^{-})
.
Furthermore, although the two models with different
N(1535)
amplitudes could give a reasonable description of the experimental
\eta p
,
\bar{K}^{0}\eta
, and
p\bar{K}^{0}
invariant mass squared distributions, the model regarding
N(1535)
as s dynamically generated state could give a better description of the
N(1535)
peak in the
\eta p
invariant mass squared distribution.
Furthermore, we have also estimated the ratio
R=\mathcal{B}(\Lambda_{c}^{+}\to N(1535)\bar{K}^{0})/\mathcal{B}(\Lambda_{c}^{+}\to N(1650)\bar{K}^{0})=0.14\pm 0.03
and
0.75\pm 0.12
for the molecular picture and the genuine baryon state of the
N(1535)
, respectively, which could be tested experimentally in the future, for instance in the process
\Lambda_{c}^{+}\to p\pi^{0}K^{0}_{S}
with a large branching fraction of
\mathcal{B}=(1.87\pm 0.13\pm 0.05)\%

[
66
]
.
