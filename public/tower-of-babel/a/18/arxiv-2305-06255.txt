Quasi-Isomorphisms of Commutative DG Rings and Divided Power Structures
Amnon Yekutieli

Abstract
. We prove that a quasi-isomorphism f : A → B f:A\to B between commutative DG rings, where B B admits a divided power structure, can be factored as f = f ~ ∘ e f=\tilde{f}\circ e , where e : A → B ~ e:A\to\tilde{B} is a split injective quasi-isomorphism, and f ~ : B ~ → B \tilde{f}:\tilde{B}\to B is a surjective quasi-isomorphism. This result is used in our work on a DG approach to the cotangent complex, and our work on the derived category of commutative DG rings.
