On Locally symmetric 3 3 -dimensional Riemannian Lie Groups.


Abstract
. In this paper, we use the powerful tool Milnor bases to classify all the 3 − 3- dimensional connected and locally symmetric Riemannian Lie Groups by solving system of polynomial equations of structure constants of each Lie algebra . Moreover, we showed that E 0 ​ ( 2 ) E_{0}(2) , is the only Lie group with locally symmetric left invariant Riemannian metrics which are not symmetric.
