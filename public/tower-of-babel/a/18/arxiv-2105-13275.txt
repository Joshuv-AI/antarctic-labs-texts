Relativistic hydrodynamics with the parity anomaly
Napat Poovuttikul

Abstract
We consider the hydrodynamic regime of a 2+1 dimensions QFT with the parity anomaly. Beyond the known constraints from positivity of entropy production, we show that the anomaly inflow mechanism, from a corresponding bulk SPT phase, together with thermodynamic consistency of equilibrium partition functions, restricts the form of non-dissipative transport coefficients. This included the known form of quantised Hall conductivity, which is fixed to be σ x ​ y = e 2 / 2 ​ h \sigma_{xy}=e^{2}/2h , along with new constraints on other three non-dissipative parity-odd transport coefficients.

IV
Discussions

Let us reiterate new results in the work. Perhaps the most striking features of this analysis is the existence of equilibrium Hall conductivity which is fixed to,
\sigma_{xy}=\tilde{\chi}_{E}=\theta e^{2}/2h
for
\theta=\pm\pi
, which is a hallmark of topological insulator. Any naive EFT that claims to be in the same universality class as boundary of topological insulator would have to put in this condition, either by hand or certain microscopic inputs. The consistency of anomaly inflow and equilibrium partition function not only obtained this result purely from macroscopic point of view but also put restrictions the other non-disspative transport coefficients. From (
14
), we saw that, if the parity anomaly is the only source of broken time-reversal symmetry, then

(i)

The pressure response to magnetic field
\tilde{\chi}_{B}=-(\partial p/\partial\rho)\theta
is fixed by thermodynamic functions and
\theta=\pm\pi
.

(ii)

The pressure response to vorticity
\tilde{\chi}_{\Omega}
and current responds to perpendicular temperature gradient
\tilde{\chi}_{T}
are forced to vanish.

Measurements of these quantities could served as additional indication whether or not a system of interest could have parity anomaly. A very short interlude on APS index theorem and anomaly inflow

For self-containment sake, we will briefly outlined reasons why free massless pseudoreal fermion is anomalous as well as how APS index theorem provide a derivation of anomaly inflow mechanism for parity anomaly. All of these information are explained in more details in

Start by considering a prototype of parity anomaly: the (massless) Dirac fermion in

(25)

From this, we can obtain the partition function of free massless Dirac fermion

Z_{\psi}=\det(i\not{D})=\prod_{k}\lambda_{k}

(26)

which, just like any QFT partition function, is infinite. To make sense of it, we add a regulator by adding

S_{reg}=\int d^{3}x\chi\left(i\not{D}+iM\right)\chi

(27)

for
\chi
be a 2-components scalars that satisfies
(i\not{D}+iM)\chi=0
. The resulting regulated action is

Z_{reg}=\prod_{k}\frac{\lambda_{k}}{\lambda_{k}+iM}=\exp\left(\sum_{k}\log\left(\frac{\lambda_{k}}{\lambda_{k}+iM}\right)\right)

(28)

Even when
M\to+\infty
, this theory regulated theory turns out to not be invariant under time-reversal as the partition function is not real. To check the possible imaginary part, we can consider the imaginary part of the exponent

\displaystyle\text{Im }\sum_{k}\log\left(\frac{\lambda_{k}}{\lambda_{k}+iM}\right)

\displaystyle=\sum_{k}\left[-\log(\lambda_{k}+iM)+\log(\lambda_{k}-iM)\right]\,,

(29)

\displaystyle=\sum_{k}\tan^{-1}\left(\frac{M}{\lambda_{k}}\right)\,,

\displaystyle=\frac{\pi}{2}\left[\sum_{\lambda_{k}>0}1-\sum_{\lambda_{k}<0}1\right]\qquad\text{as }M\to+\infty

The last term in the
[..]
is the APS
\boldsymbol{\eta}-
invariant. Computing
\boldsymbol{\eta}-
invariant requires another kind of regulator but we will not talk about that for now. What is interesting is that we can then write the regulated partition function as

(30)

where
|Z_{\psi}|
is the modulus of the (regulated)partition function. We can see from there that the time reversal,
