Introduction

This paper studies the Klein-Gordon equation coupled with Born-Infeld theory with critical growth

\displaystyle\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}-\Delta u+V(x)u-(2\omega+\phi)\phi u=\lambda f(u)+|u|^{4}u,&\text{in}\ \mathbb{R}^{3},\\

In the past decades, many people have studied this system through using variational methods, and have also obtained existence of nontrivial solutions under different assumptions. Let us recall some previous results which give an inspiration to the presence research.

The first result is due to d’Avenia and Pisani, in which the existence of infinitely many radially symmetric solution for the following form
