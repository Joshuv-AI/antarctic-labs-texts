Mixing and C ​ P C\!P violation in D 0 → K − ​ π + {{D}^{0}}\!\rightarrow{{{K}^{-}}{{\pi}^{+}}} decays arXiv: 2106.02014
Tommaso Pajero, and Michael Joseph Morello

Abstract
We review the experimental methods to measure mixing and time-dependent C ​ P C\!P violation in D 0 {D}^{0} decays into two hadrons. While these phenomena are usually neglected for D 0 → K − ​ π + {{D}^{0}}\!\rightarrow{{K}^{-}}{{\pi}^{+}} decays, this approximation is not always justified. In particular, it produces a bias on the measurement of the parameter y C ​ P y_{{C\!P}} , when this is performed by relying on D 0 → K − ​ π + {{D}^{0}}\!\rightarrow{{{K}^{-}}{{\pi}^{+}}} decays as a normalisation channel, whose size is around 40% of the precision of the current world average. Finally, we estimate the sensitivity to the weak mixing phases achievable by studying D 0 → K − ​ π + {{D}^{0}}\!\rightarrow{{{K}^{-}}{{\pi}^{+}}} and untagged D → K − ​ π + {D}\!\rightarrow{{{K}^{-}}{{\pi}^{+}}} decays, where D D stands for either of the D 0 {D}^{0} and D ¯ 0 {\kern 1.79993pt\overline{\kern-1.79993ptD}}{}^{0} mesons. Contrary to Cabibbo-suppressed D 0 → h + ​ h − {{D}^{0}}\!\rightarrow{{h}^{+}}{{h}^{-}} decays, these decay channels allow to measure these phases without final-state dependent nuisance contributions from the decay amplitudes, but their sensitivity is lower by a factor of six.

2.1
{D}^{0}
mixing

An arbitrary linear combination of
{D}^{0}
and
{\kern 1.79993pt\overline{\kern-1.79993ptD}}{}^{0}
flavour eigenstates,

a(t)|{{D}^{0}}\rangle+b(t)|{{\kern 1.79993pt\overline{\kern-1.79993ptD}}{}^{0}}\rangle,

(1)

evolves in time according to the Schrödinger equation,

i\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}\begin{pmatrix}a(t)\\
b(t)\end{pmatrix}=\bm{H}\begin{pmatrix}a(0)\\
b(0)\end{pmatrix},

(2)

where
\bm{H}
is the
2\times 2
effective Hamiltonian governing the dynamics of the
{D}^{0}
–
{\kern 1.79993pt\overline{\kern-1.79993ptD}}{}^{0}

system.
2
2

2

Natural units are employed throughout,
\hbar=c=1
.

This Hamiltonian can be expressed in terms of two Hermitian matrices as
\bm{H}\equiv\bm{M}-\tfrac{i}{2}\bm{\Gamma}
.
The
C\!PT
symmetry, which is assumed hereafter, implies
H_{11}=H_{22}=M-\tfrac{i}{2}\Gamma
, with
M
and
\Gamma
the mass and decay width of the
{D}^{0}
meson.
The transition amplitudes between
{D}^{0}
and
{\kern 1.79993pt\overline{\kern-1.79993ptD}}{}^{0}
mesons,

\langle{{D}^{0}}|H|{{\kern 1.79993pt\overline{\kern-1.79993ptD}}{}^{0}}\rangle=M_{12}-\tfrac{i}{2}\Gamma_{12},\qquad\langle{{\kern 1.79993pt\overline{\kern-1.79993ptD}}{}^{0}}|H|{{D}^{0}}\rangle=M^{\ast}_{12}-\tfrac{i}{2}\Gamma^{\ast}_{12},

(3)

depend on the dispersive and absorptive mixing amplitudes,
M_{12}
and
\Gamma_{12}
, which correspond to off-shell and on-shell transitions, respectively.
They are parametrised in terms of two
C\!P
-even mixing parameters,

x_{12}\equiv\frac{2\lvert M_{12}\rvert}{\Gamma},\qquad y_{12}\equiv\frac{\lvert\Gamma_{12}\rvert}{\Gamma},

(4)

and one
C\!P
-odd weak phase,

\phi_{12}\equiv\arg\left(\frac{M_{12}}{\Gamma_{12}}\right).

(5)

The additional global phase shared by
M_{12}
and
\Gamma_{12}
depends on the phase conventions for quarks and mesons and is unobservable.
The parameters
x_{12}
and
y_{12}
are equal to the absolute value of normalised mass and decay-width differences of the eigenstates of
\bm{H}
,
x\equiv\Delta m/\Gamma
and
y\equiv\Delta\Gamma/(2\Gamma)
, up to corrections quadratic in
\sin\phi_{12}
.
