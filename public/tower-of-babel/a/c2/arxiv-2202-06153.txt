A q q -deformation of enriched P P -partitions
Darij Grinberg \addressmark 1, Ekaterina A. Vassilieva \addressmark 2

Abstract
We introduce a q q -deformation that generalises in a single framework previous works on classical and enriched P P -partitions. In particular, we build a new family of power series with a parameter q q that interpolates between Gessel’s fundamental ( q = 0 q=0 ) and Stembridge’s peak quasisymmetric functions ( q = 1 q=1 ) and show that it is a basis of QSym \operatorname{QSym} when q ∉ { − 1 , 1 } q\notin\{-1,1\} . Furthermore we build their corresponding monomial bases parametrised with q q that cover our previous work on enriched monomials and the essential quasisymmetric functions of Hoffman.

Introduced by Stanley in
