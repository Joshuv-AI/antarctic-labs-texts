Projective Self-dual polygons in higher dimensions
Ana C. Chavez Caliz

Abstract
Motivated by a question from V. Arnold about self-dual curves in projective spaces, we study ℳ m , n , k {\cal M}_{m,n,k} : the moduli space of m m -self-dual n n -gons in ℙ k \mathbb{P}^{k} . This paper lays out an explicit construction of self-dual polygons, and for specific cases of n n and m m , provides the dimension of ℳ m , n , k {\cal M}_{m,n,k} . We include a conjecture about the Pentagram map in higher dimensions that generalizes Clebsch’s theorem, which states that every pentagon in ℝ ​ ℙ 2 \mathbb{RP}^{2} is invariant under the Pentagram map.
