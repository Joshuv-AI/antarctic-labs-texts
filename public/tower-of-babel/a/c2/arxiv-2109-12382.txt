Implications of light Z ′ Z^{\prime} on semileptonic B ( B s ) → T { K 2 ∗ ( 1430 ) B(B_{s})\to T\{K_{2}^{*}(1430) ( f 2 ′ ( 1525 ) ) } ℓ + ℓ − (f_{2}^{\prime}(1525))\}\ell^{+}\ell^{-} decays at large recoil
Manas K. Mohapatra, Anjan Giri

Abstract
We probe the rare semileptonic decays B ( s ) → K 2 ∗ ​ ( 1430 ) ​ ( f 2 ′ ​ ( 1525 ) ) ​ ℓ + ​ ℓ − B_{(s)}\to K_{2}^{*}(1430)(f_{2}^{\prime}(1525))\ell^{+}\ell^{-} proceeding via b → s ​ ℓ ​ ℓ b\to s\ell\ell transition in the presence of a light Z ′ Z^{\prime} boson. We employ the presence of an additional vector type interaction and constrain the new physics coupling parameter using the existing experimental measurements on R K R_{K} and R K ∗ R_{K^{*}} observables. To understand the sensitivity of the new physics coupling, we investigate the impact of this coupling on various physical observables such as differential branching ratio, the forward-backward asymmetry, the lepton polarization asymmetry, the angular observable P 5 ′ P_{5}^{\prime} , and the lepton universality parameters such as the ratio of the branching ratio R f 2 ′ ​ ( K 2 ∗ ) R_{f_{2}^{\prime}(K_{2}^{*})} and some important Q parameters of B ( s ) → K 2 ∗ ​ ( 1430 ) ​ ( f 2 ′ ​ ( 1525 ) ) ​ ℓ + ​ ℓ − B_{(s)}\to K_{2}^{*}(1430)(f_{2}^{\prime}(1525))\ell^{+}\ell^{-} processes at large recoil. We find some noticeable differences of the observables in the presence of light Z ′ Z^{\prime} contribution.

According to our best understanding the standard model (SM), although a successful theory, is not enough to explain some key puzzles such as matter-antimatter asymmetry in the universe, dark matter, dark energy, hierarchy problem, neutrino mass and so on. IV
New physics Analysis

A heavy
Z^{\prime}
boson, in the tree level exchange with flavor changing neutral current transition mediated by
b\to s\ell^{+}\ell^{-}
parton level, is the most obvious candidate in the NP contribution. There are different scenarios which are responsible for muonic four-fermion
b\to s\mu^{+}\mu^{-}
NP operators and are given as follows:

\displaystyle(\rm I):

\displaystyle[\bar{s}\gamma_{\mu}P_{L}b][\bar{\mu}\gamma^{\mu}\mu],

\displaystyle(\rm II):

\displaystyle[\bar{s}\gamma_{\mu}P_{L}b][\bar{\mu}\gamma^{\mu}P_{L}\mu],

\displaystyle(\rm III):

\displaystyle[\bar{s}\gamma_{\mu}\gamma_{5}b][\bar{\mu}\gamma^{\mu}\mu].

(24)

However, the scenarios
\rm(I)
and
\rm(II)
display the
Z^{\prime}
boson to couple with the quark sector
\bar{s}_{L}-b_{L}-Z^{\prime}
and the lepton sector
Z^{\prime}-\bar{\mu}-\mu
vectorially whereas it couples axial-vectorially in the scenario
\rm(III)
. Having said that we exclude the scenario
\rm(III)
as it is strongly rejected by the
R_{K}
measurement. The
Z^{\prime}
boson must transform as a singlet or triplet under
SU(2)_{L}
gauge group as it couples to left handed quarks. In the case of triplet
Calibbi:2015kma
;
Crivellin:2015lwa
;
Boucenna:2016wpr
, a new gauge boson
W^{\prime}
can contribute to
B\to D^{(*)+}\tau^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\tau}
mediated by
b\to c
quark level transition where the deviation in the measurement has been observed in the Ref.
Belle:2015qfa
;
LHCb:2015gmp
. In the case of singlet under
SU(2)_{L}
gauge group, this
Z^{\prime}
gauge boson associate with an extension of abelian
U(1)^{\prime}
group to the SM.
Many works have been proposed in this model with the scenario
C_{9}^{\mu\mu}(NP)
= -
C_{10}^{\mu\mu}(NP)
where the Wilson coefficients are
q^{2}
-independent. However, on the other hand, it is very interesting to consider a light
Z^{\prime}
which can also address
b\to s\mu^{+}\mu^{-}
data
Datta:2017pfz
;
Datta:2017ezo
;
Alok:2017sui
. If
2m_{\mu}<m_{Z^{\prime}}<m_{B}
, a resonance state can be obtained in the dimuon invariant mass. Moreover to say that since no signature for such kind of state has been observed in the dimuon invariant mass, we consider the typical
Z^{\prime}
mass less than
2m_{\mu}
i.e 200
\rm MeV
in our analysis. For the coupling
\bar{s}b
with the light
Z^{\prime}
, the general form of the flavor changing vertex
\bar{s}bZ^{\prime}
is considered as
Alok:2017sui

\displaystyle F(q^{2})\bar{s}\gamma^{\mu}P_{L}bZ_{\mu}^{\prime},

(25)

where the form of the form factor
F(q^{2})
can be written as

\displaystyle F(q^{2})=a_{L}^{bs}+g_{L}^{bs}\frac{q^{2}}{m_{B}^{2}}+...

(26)

The leading order term
a_{L}^{bs}
given in the above equation is severely constrained by
B\to K\nu\bar{\nu}
and can be neglected, and we consider the coupling
g_{L}^{bs}
only. Thus the
q^{2}
-dependent NP Wilson coefficients for
b\to s\mu^{+}\mu^{-}
transition are given as

\displaystyle C_{9}^{\mu\mu}(NP)=\mathcal{G}\frac{g^{L}_{bs}q^{2}/m_{B}^{2}(g^{L}_{\mu\mu}+g^{R}_{\mu\mu})}{q^{2}-m_{Z^{\prime}}^{2}},\hskip 14.22636ptC_{10}^{\mu\mu}(NP)=-\mathcal{G}\frac{g^{L}_{bs}q^{2}/m_{B}^{2}(g^{L}_{\mu\mu}-g^{R}_{\mu\mu})}{q^{2}-m_{Z^{\prime}}^{2}},

(27)

where
\mathcal{G}=\frac{\pi}{\sqrt{2}G_{F}\alpha V_{tb}V_{ts}^{*}}
.
It has been pointed out in Ref.
Alok:2017sui
that one can explain the B anomalies as good as in the case of heavy
Z^{\prime}
boson. It is clearly reported that except
R_{K^{*}}
measurement in the low
q^{2}
bin range, the light
Z^{\prime}
with pure vector coupling to muon can easily accommodate the clean observables
R_{K}^{[1,6]}
and
R_{K^{*}}^{[1.1,6]}
data given in Table - I of Ref.
Datta:2017ezo
. Since we assume the NP exist in muonic mode of
b\to s\ell^{+}\ell^{-}
transition, the NP coupling
C_{9}^{\mu\mu}(NP)
is considered in our analysis where the light
Z^{\prime}
couple with muon vectorially under the condition
g^{L}_{\mu\mu}=g^{R}_{\mu\mu}=g_{\mu\mu}
.

The long-standing discrepancy between theory and experiment that concerns with the anomalous magnetic dipole moment of muon i.e

VI
Conclusion

Inspired by the anomalies present in
B\to(K,K^{*})\ell^{+}\ell^{-}
and
B_{s}\to\phi\mu^{+}\mu^{-}
decays proceeding via
b\to s\ell^{+}\ell^{-}
flavor changing neutral current quark level interaction, we scrutinize the semileptonic decays of
B\to K_{2}^{*}(1430)
and
B_{s}\to f_{2}^{\prime}(1525)
with the charged leptons (
\ell=\mu,e)
in the presence of SM and the light
Z^{\prime}
model. Assuming the NP present in muon mode of lepton pair in the final state, we constrain the NP coupling by considering the experimental data associated with the clean observable
R_{K}
in the range
1.1<q^{2}<6.0

\rm GeV^{2}
and
R_{K^{*}}
in the central
q^{2}
region [1.1,6.0] with the performance of
\chi^{2}
fit. In the presence of effective Hamiltonian for
b\to s\ell\ell
transition, we provide a detailed study of the behavior of various physical observables such as differential branching ratio, lepton polarization fraction, forward-backward asymmetry, the angular observable
P_{5}^{\prime}
, and LFU sensitive parameter as the ratio of branching ratios in
B\to K_{2}^{*}
and
B_{s}\to f_{2}^{\prime}
transition with
\mu
mode to e mode in the final state in the SM as well as in the presence of light
Z^{\prime}
. The other observables that are very sensitive to lepton flavor universality also draw attention to probe on few
Q_{i}
parameters corresponding to the longitudinal polarization fraction (
Q_{F_{L}}
), forward-backward asymmetry (
Q_{A_{FB}}
) and the angular observable
P_{5}^{\prime}
(
Q_{5}^{\prime}
). With the
q^{2}
dependent NP coupling we give the integrated predictions of all the above discussed prominent observables pertaining to
B\to K_{2}^{*}\ell^{+}\ell^{-}
and
B_{s}\to f_{2}^{\prime}\ell^{+}\ell^{-}
decays at different
q^{2}
bin regions that compatible with LHCb experiment. In this study all the observables are investigated by considering the form factors obtained from light cone sum rule approach.

We observed in our analysis that the differential branching ratio is reduced as compared to SM and notice that no significant deviation for this observable in both exclusive
B\to K_{2}^{*}\ell^{+}\ell^{-}
and
B_{s}\to f_{2}^{\prime}\ell^{+}\ell^{-}
processes in the presence of light
Z^{\prime}
boson. In the observables the longitudinal fraction and the angular observable
P_{5}^{\prime}
, we get a remarkable contribution in the new physics analysis in both the decay modes. The deviations observed at the LFU parameters such as
R_{f_{2}^{\prime}}
and
R_{K_{2}^{*}}
are clearly distinguishable and as a complementary decay channel, both can provide an insight into the
R_{f_{2}^{\prime}}
and
R_{K_{2}^{*}}
anomalies which could be observed in the LHCb experiment. On the other hand, we also look into the
Q_{i}
parameters which are very sensitive to LFUV and found that all the observables have profound deviations from the SM contribution. As the
B\to K_{2}^{*}\ell^{+}\ell^{-}
and
B_{s}\to f_{2}^{\prime}\ell^{+}\ell^{-}
decay processes have received less attention unlike
B\to(K,K^{*})\ell^{+}\ell^{-}
and
B_{s}\to\phi\mu^{+}\mu^{-}
decays mediated by
b\to s\ell\ell
quark level transition , it is very important to acquire more data sample from the experiments in order to understand the significance of new physics contributions.
