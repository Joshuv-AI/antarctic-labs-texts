New cases of the Strong Stanley conjecture
Per Alexandersson, Ryan Mickler

Abstract
. We make progress towards understanding the structure of Littlewood–Richardson coefficients g λ , μ ν g_{\lambda,\mu}^{\nu} for products of Jack symmetric functions. Building on recent results of the second author, we are able to prove new cases of a conjecture of Stanley in which certain families of these coefficients can be expressed as a product of upper or lower hook lengths for every box in each of the partitions. In particular, we prove that conjecture in the case of a rectangular union, i.e. for g μ , σ ¯ μ ∪ m n g_{\mu,\bar{\sigma}}^{\mu\cup m^{n}} where σ ¯ \bar{\sigma} is the complementary partition of σ = μ ∩ m n \sigma=\mu\cap m^{n} in the rectangular partition m n m^{n} . We give a formula for these coefficients through an explicit prescription of such choices of hooks. Lastly, we conjecture an analogue of this conjecture of Stanley holds in the case of shifted Jack functions.

\lambda

s
. Similarly,
{\color[rgb]{0,0,1}C^{s}_{\lambda}}

Note that this definition makes sense even when
s\notin\lambda
.

Let
\lambda

Since these are sets of boxes, we may define their intersection,

and union
{\color[rgb]{0,0,1}\lambda\cup\mu}
,
and these are the Young diagrams for some integer partitions.

1.2.
Jack symmetric functions and structure constants

There are two popular versions of the Jack symmetric functions; the Jack
P
functions,
and the
integral form
Jack
J
functions—the latter has integer coefficients
in the monomial basis. We shall only use the latter functions.
The book
[
Mac95
]
is recommended as a reference on this topic.
For example, the
J_{\lambda}(\mathbf{x};\alpha)
for
\lambda\,\vdash\,3
,
expanded in the monomial basis are as follows:

\displaystyle J_{3}(\mathbf{x};\alpha)

\displaystyle=(1+3\alpha+2\alpha^{2})m_{3}+(3+3\alpha)m_{21}+6m_{111}

\displaystyle J_{21}(\mathbf{x};\alpha)

\displaystyle=(2+\alpha)m_{21}+6m_{111}

\displaystyle J_{111}(\mathbf{x};\alpha)

\displaystyle=6m_{111}.

The expansions in the power-sum basis are

\displaystyle J_{3}(\mathbf{x};\alpha)

\displaystyle=2\alpha^{2}p_{3}+3\alpha p_{21}+p_{111}

\displaystyle J_{21}(\mathbf{x};\alpha)

\displaystyle=-\alpha p_{3}+(\alpha-1)p_{21}+p_{111}

\displaystyle J_{111}(\mathbf{x};\alpha)

\displaystyle=2p_{3}-3p_{21}+p_{111}.

Let
{\color[rgb]{0,0,1}g_{\mu\nu}^{\gamma}(\alpha)}\in\mathbb{Q}(\alpha)
be the Jack Littlewood–Richardson constants,
determined via the relation

(3)

J_{\lambda}J_{\mu}=\sum_{\nu}g_{\lambda\mu}^{\nu}(\alpha)J_{\nu}.

We shall often omit the dependence on
\alpha
, and just write
g_{\lambda\mu}^{\nu}
.
Observe that these are closely related to the classical Littlewood–Richardson
coefficients for Schur functions, denoted
{\color[rgb]{0,0,1}c_{\lambda\mu}^{\nu}}
.
The Jack polynomials form an orthogonal basis with respect to
the
\alpha
-deformed Hall inner product,
\langle\,\cdot\,,\,\cdot\,\rangle
.
This is the inner product on symmetric functions with the property that

\langle p_{\lambda},p_{\mu}\rangle=\begin{cases}z_{\lambda}\alpha^{\ell(\lambda)}&\text{if $\lambda=\mu$}\\
0&\text{otherwise,}\end{cases}

and where
n!/{\color[rgb]{0,0,1}z_{\mu}}
is the number of permutations of size
n
with cycle type
\mu
.

Theorem 1.2

(Jack norm formula, see
[
Sta89
, 5.8]
)
.

(4)

\lVert J_{\lambda}\rVert^{2}=\langle J_{\lambda},J_{\lambda}\rangle=\ \prod_{b\in\lambda}{h^{\scriptscriptstyle{\mathrm{U}}}_{\lambda}(b)}{h^{\scriptscriptstyle{\mathrm{L}}}_{\lambda}(b)}.

1.3.
The Stanley Conjectures

In
[
Sta89
]
, several remarkable conjectures were stated regarding the
structure of the Jack Littlewood–Richardson coefficients.
These conjectures are stated in terms of the quantities

\langle J_{\mu}J_{\nu},J_{\lambda}\rangle=g_{\mu\nu}^{\lambda}\lVert J_{\lambda}\rVert^{2}
, herein referred to as
Stanley structure coefficients
,
but can be easily restated in terms of the Littlewood–Richardson coefficients using (
4
).

Conjecture 1.3

(Stanley conjecture, see
[
Sta89
, 8.3]
)
.

The Stanley structure coefficients are non-negative integer polynomials in
\alpha
, that is,

\langle J_{\mu}J_{\nu},J_{\lambda}\rangle\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}[\alpha].

In general, we use the terminology of a
strong
form of the Stanley conjecture to refer to any conjecture that proposes an explicit form for
\langle J_{\mu}J_{\nu},J_{\lambda}\rangle
which is
manifestly
a non-negative polynomial in
\alpha
. Stanley conjectured such a form in the case
c_{\mu\nu}^{\lambda}=1
.

Conjecture 1.4

(Strong Stanley Conjecture, see
[
Sta89
, 8.5]
)
.

If
c_{\mu\nu}^{\lambda}=1
, then the corresponding Stanley structure coefficient has the form

\langle J_{\mu}J_{\nu},J_{\lambda}\rangle=\left(\prod_{b\in\mu}\tilde{h}_{\mu}(b)\right)\left(\prod_{b\in\nu}\tilde{h}_{\nu}(b)\right)\left(\prod_{b\in\lambda}\tilde{h}_{\lambda}(b)\right),

where
\tilde{h}_{\sigma}(b)
is a choice of either
{h^{\scriptscriptstyle{\mathrm{U}}}_{\sigma}(b)}
or
{h^{\scriptscriptstyle{\mathrm{L}}}_{\sigma}(b)}
for each box
b
.
Moreover, one chooses
{h^{\scriptscriptstyle{\mathrm{L}}}_{\,}(b)}
and
{h^{\scriptscriptstyle{\mathrm{U}}}_{\,}(b)}
exactly
|\lambda|
times each in the above expression.

Stanley proved Conjecture
1.4
in an important special case.

Theorem 1.5

(Jack Pieri rule, see
[
Sta89
, 6.1]
)
.

Let
\lambda/\mu
be a horizontal
r
-strip, then the Stanley
structure coefficient is given by the following expression

\langle J_{\mu}J_{(r)},J_{\lambda}\rangle=\left(\prod_{b\in\mu}A_{\mu\lambda}(b)\right)\left(\prod_{b\in(r)}{h^{\scriptscriptstyle{\mathrm{U}}}_{(r)}(b)}\right)\left(\prod_{b\in\lambda}A^{\prime}_{\mu\lambda}(b)\right),

where

A_{\mu\lambda}(b)\coloneqq\begin{cases}{h^{\scriptscriptstyle{\mathrm{L}}}_{\mu}(b)}&\text{ if $\lambda/\mu$ does not contain a box in the same column as $b$}\\
{h^{\scriptscriptstyle{\mathrm{U}}}_{\mu}(b)}&\text{ otherwise,}\end{cases}

and
A^{\prime}
is given by the same formula as
A
, except with upper and lower looks exchanged.

In 2013, another case of Conjecture
1.4
was
proven using a technique involving vertex operators.

(Strong Stanley conjecture, rectangular case, see
[
CJ13

.

Let
\mu\subseteq m^{n}
.
The Stanley structure coefficient in the rectangular case has the following expression as a product of lower/upper hook lengths:

(5)

\langle J_{\mu}J_{\bar{\mu}},J_{m^{n}}\rangle=\left(\prod_{b\in\mu}{h^{\scriptscriptstyle{\mathrm{L}}}_{\mu}(b)}\right)\left(\prod_{b\in\bar{\mu}}{h^{\scriptscriptstyle{\mathrm{U}}}_{\bar{\mu}}(b)}\right)\left(\prod_{b\in m^{n}}D_{\mu,m^{n}}(b)\right),

b\in m^{n}

(6)

{h^{\scriptscriptstyle{\mathrm{L}}}_{m^{n}}(b)}&\text{ otherwise}.\end{cases}

Lemma 3.2
.

For
\mu
as before, and

with

Proof.

We start by noting that for any

, we have

indicates that we have dropped the zero at
u=[{s^{{{\texttt{+}}}}}]

We then use the expansion Lemma

to write the terms in this ratio as

T^{\prime}_{(\mu+s)\cup m^{n}}([{s^{{{\texttt{+}}}}}])=-[{s^{{{\texttt{+}}}}}]\times\frac{\prod_{b\in R^{s}_{\mu\cup m^{n}}}{h^{\scriptscriptstyle{\mathrm{U}}}_{\mu\cup m^{n}}(b)}}{\prod_{b\in R^{s}_{(\mu+s)\cup m^{n}}}{h^{\scriptscriptstyle{\mathrm{U}}}_{(\mu+s)\cup m^{n}}(b)}}\times\frac{\prod_{b\in C^{s}_{\mu\cup m^{n}}}{h^{\scriptscriptstyle{\mathrm{L}}}_{\mu\cup m^{n}}(b)}}{\prod_{b\in C^{s}_{(\mu+s)\cup m^{n}}}{h^{\scriptscriptstyle{\mathrm{L}}}_{(\mu+s)\cup m^{n}}(b)}}.

T^{\prime}_{(\mu+s)}([{s^{{{\texttt{+}}}}}])=-[{s^{{{\texttt{+}}}}}]\times\frac{\prod_{b\in R^{s}_{\mu}}{h^{\scriptscriptstyle{\mathrm{U}}}_{\mu}(b)}}{\prod_{b\in R^{s}_{\mu+s}}{h^{\scriptscriptstyle{\mathrm{U}}}_{\mu+s}(b)}}\times\frac{\prod_{b\in C^{s}_{\mu}}{h^{\scriptscriptstyle{\mathrm{L}}}_{\mu}(b)}}{\prod_{b\in C^{s}_{\mu+s}}{h^{\scriptscriptstyle{\mathrm{L}}}_{\mu+s}(b)}}.

Taking the ratio of these these two equalities, we arrive at the result.

4.
Conjectures involving shifted Jack functions

Is there an analog of (
8
) for shifted Jack structure constants?
In
[
AF19
]
, it is conjectured that for shifted Jack functions, we have that

\langle{J^{\#}_{\mu}}{J^{\#}_{\nu}},{J^{\#}_{\lambda}}\rangle
are Laurent polynomials in
\alpha
with non-negative
integer coefficients.
One can then ask if a shifted analog of Stanley’s conjecture holds.
Let us first set

{\color[rgb]{0,0,1}H^{U}_{\lambda}}\coloneqq\prod_{b\in\lambda}h^{U}_{\lambda}(b),\quad{\color[rgb]{0,0,1}H^{L}_{\lambda}}\coloneqq\prod_{b\in\lambda}h^{L}_{\lambda}(b).

The definition of the Jack Littlewood–Richardson coefficients

{\color[rgb]{0,0,1}g_{\mu\nu}^{\lambda}(\alpha)}
are then defined via

{J^{\#}_{\mu}}{J^{\#}_{\nu}}=\sum_{\lambda}g_{\mu\nu}^{\lambda}(\alpha){J^{\#}_{\lambda}}\text{ or equivalently }{P^{\#}_{\mu}}{P^{\#}_{\nu}}=\sum_{\lambda}g_{\mu\nu}^{\lambda}(\alpha)\frac{H^{L}_{\lambda}}{H^{L}_{\mu}\cdot H^{L}_{\mu}}{P^{\#}_{\lambda}},

where
{J^{\#}_{\mu}}=H^{L}_{\mu}{P^{\#}_{\mu}}
.
When
|\mu|+|\nu|=|\lambda|
, we recover the “non-shifted” coefficients in (
3
),
but we may have non-zero coefficients also when
|\mu|+|\nu|>|\lambda|
.
The quantity
c_{\mu\nu}^{\lambda}(\alpha)\coloneqq g_{\mu\nu}^{\lambda}(\alpha)\frac{H^{L}_{\lambda}}{H^{L}_{\mu}\cdot H^{L}_{\mu}}

is then a deformation of the classical Littlewood–Richardson coefficients for Schur functions,
and their shifted generalization.
The inner product for the shifted Jack polynomials are then given by

\langle{J^{\#}_{\mu}}{J^{\#}_{\nu}},{J^{\#}_{\lambda}}\rangle=H^{L}_{\lambda}\cdot H^{U}_{\lambda}\cdot g_{\mu\nu}^{\lambda}(\alpha),

as in Subsection
1.3
.

Conjecture 4.1

(Shifted strong Stanley conjecture)
.

If the shifted Littlewood–Richardson coefficient
c_{\mu\nu}^{\lambda}(1)
is equal to
1
,
then the (shifted) structure coefficient has the following form:

\langle{J^{\#}_{\mu}}{J^{\#}_{\nu}},{J^{\#}_{\lambda}}\rangle=\left(\prod_{b\in\mu}\tilde{h}_{\mu}(b)\right)\left(\prod_{b\in\nu}\tilde{h}_{\nu}(b)\right)\left(\prod_{b\in\lambda}\tilde{h}_{\lambda}(b)\right),

where
\tilde{h}_{\sigma}(b)
is a choice of either
{h^{\scriptscriptstyle{\mathrm{U}}}_{\sigma}(b)}
or
{h^{\scriptscriptstyle{\mathrm{L}}}_{\sigma}(b)}
for each box
b
.
Moreover, one chooses
{h^{\scriptscriptstyle{\mathrm{U}}}_{\sigma}(b)}
exactly
|\lambda|

times each in the above expression
and
{h^{\scriptscriptstyle{\mathrm{L}}}_{\sigma}(b)}
is chosen
|\mu|+|\nu|
times.
