Etale cohomologies of quadrics over ℝ {\mathbb{R}}
Nobuaki Yagita

Abstract
. In this paper, we study etale cohomologies of quadrics over ℝ {\mathbb{R}} . An element in the etale cohomology is called algebraic, if it is in the image of the cycle map from the Chow ring. In this paper, we compute the etale cohomology of norm quadrics, and give examples which have many non-algebraic elements.

Let

{\mathbb{R}}

In this paper the

The cohomology operation of the etale theory means that of the motivic theory

H_{et}^{*}(X;{\mathbb{Z}}/2)\cong lim_{N\to\infty}\tau^{N}H^{*,*+N}(X;{\mathbb{Z}}/2)

It is well known ([Vo1], [Vo2])

H_{et}^{*}(Spec({\mathbb{C}});{\mathbb{Z}}/2)\cong{\mathbb{Z}}/2,\quad H^{*,*^{\prime}}(Spec({\mathbb{C}});{\mathbb{Z}}/2)\cong{\mathbb{Z}}/2[\tau],

0\not=\tau\in H^{0,1}(Spec({\mathbb{R}});{\mathbb{Z}}/2)\cong{\mathbb{Z}}/2

\rho=-1\in{\mathbb{R}}^{*}/({\mathbb{R}}^{*})^{2}\cong K_{1}^{M}({\mathbb{R}})/2\cong H_{et}^{1}(Spec({\mathbb{R}});{\mathbb{Z}}/2).

We recall the cycle map from the Chow ring to the etale cohomology

cl/2:CH^{*}(X)/2\to H^{2*}_{et}(X;{\mathbb{Z}}/2).

This map is also written as

H^{2*,*}(X;{\mathbb{Z}}/2)\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\times\tau^{*}}}{{\to}}H^{2*,2*}(X;{\mathbb{Z}}/2).

We say that
x\in H_{et}^{*}(X;{\mathbb{Z}}/2)
is algebraic if

x\in Im(cl/2)
(for
{\mathbb{Z}}/2
coefficients).
