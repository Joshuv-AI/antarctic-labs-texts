Production of the triply heavy Ω c ​ c ​ c \Omega_{ccc} and Ω b ​ b ​ b \Omega_{bbb} baryons at e + ​ e − e^{+}e^{-} colliders
Su-Zhi Wu a, Pei Wu b, You-Wei Li a

Abstract
Nonrelativistic quantum chromodynamics (NRQCD) factorization formulism is an important approach to investigate the production of the heavy quarkonium. In this paper, we study the production of the Ω c ​ c ​ c \Omega_{ccc} and Ω b ​ b ​ b \Omega_{bbb} at the e + ​ e − e^{+}e^{-} collider, using the NRQCD factorization formulism. We do the full calculation of the total and differential cross sections of the processes e + ​ e − → γ ∗ / Z ∗ → Ω Q ​ Q ​ Q + Q ¯ + Q ¯ + Q ¯ e^{+}e^{-}\rightarrow\gamma^{*}/Z^{*}\rightarrow\Omega_{QQQ}+\bar{Q}+\bar{Q}+\bar{Q} , in the leading order at the e + ​ e − e^{+}e^{-} colliders with different energies. In this paper, Ω Q ​ Q ​ Q \Omega_{QQQ} means the triply heavy baryon Ω c ​ c ​ c \Omega_{ccc} or Ω b ​ b ​ b \Omega_{bbb} . And Q Q denotes the c c or b b quark. The results show that it is hard to observe them at the e + ​ e − e^{+}e^{-} colliders directly.

|\Psi(0,0)|^{2}
,
\alpha^{2}(q)
and
\alpha_{s}^{4}(\mu)
.
So the numerical results can be changed by one even two orders using
different the wave function at the origin of the triply heavy
baryons, different running coupling constants and different energy
scale choices. We can get the conclusion from the numerical results shown in Figs.
2
and
3
, that the production cross sections of the
\Omega_{ccc}
and
\Omega_{bbb}
take their maximum values at
\sqrt{s}=m_{Z}
.
From the numerical results in Table
1
, we know that it is impossible to observe
\Omega_{ccc}
at SuperKEKB, if there is no other models to produce them at the
e^{+}e^{-}
colliders. Also, we see there are around
32-198
events for the production of
\Omega_{ccc}
and
7-20
events for the production of
\Omega_{bbb}
, at CEPC with
16\,ab^{-1}
integrated luminosity running at
91.2\,{\rm GeV}
.

In this work, we also calculate the production cross section of the
\Omega_{ccc}
using the same parameters as in Ref.
Baranov and Slad 2004
. And the numerical result is
0.20(2)\times 10^{-2}\,{\rm fb}
, which is about an order of magnitude smaller than the one
0.0404(4)\,{\rm fb}
in Ref.
Baranov and Slad 2004
. This huge difference may can be explained by the approximation adopted in Ref.
Baranov and Slad 2004
.

IV
Summary

We have studied the production of the
\Omega_{ccc}
and
\Omega_{bbb}
at the SuperKEKB and the CEPC. From the numerical results, we conclude that it is impossible to find the triply heavy baryons at SuperKEKB, and it is hard to find
\Omega_{ccc}
and
\Omega_{bbb}
at the CEPC because of the few events.
