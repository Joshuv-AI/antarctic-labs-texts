2.
Deformation of equilateral triangle

2.1.
Deformation principle for equilateral triangle

If some triangle points lie on a circle (line) or are equivalent in the case of an equilateral triangle, then in the general case of an arbitrary triangle they are connected by some natural relations. Thus, triangle geometry can be seen as a deformation of the equilateral triangle geometry.

This principle partially describes why there exists so many relations between the Kimberling centers
X_{i}
. Further we will present several examples of the facts arising from this principle.
