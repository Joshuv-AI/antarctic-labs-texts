Abstract
. The reciprocity conjecture in the Langlands program links motives to automorphic forms. The proof of Fermat’s Last Theorem by Wiles [ 180 , 169 ] introduced new tools to study reciprocity. This survey reports on developments using these ideas (and their generalizations) in the last three decades.

5.
Potential Modularity

One new idea which emerged in Taylor’s paper

The obstruction to doing such a

At the cost of proving a weaker result, this gives a huge amount of extra flexibility that has proved
remarkably useful. Taylor’s first application of this idea was to prove the Fontaine–Mazur

(even over the original field

Local deformation rings at

One difficulty with understanding local deformation rings

Beyond analyzing the local deformation rings themselves, Kisin crucially found a way
