On a problem involving the squares of odd harmonic numbers


Abstract
We introduce a full solution to a problem considered by Wang and Chu concerning series involving the squares of finite sums of the form 1 + 1 3 + ⋯ + 1 2 ​ n − 1 1+\frac{1}{3}+\cdots+\frac{1}{2n-1} . Our proof involves techniques from the theory of colored multiple zeta values.

The infinite series

\lambda\in\mathbb{N}
we have

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{n}\binom{2n}{n}^{2}\frac{O_{n}}{1+2n-2\lambda}=-\ln(2)A(\lambda)+\frac{16^{\lambda}}{2\lambda^{2}\binom{2\lambda}{\lambda}^{2}}\sum_{k=0}^{\lambda-1}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{k}\binom{2k}{k}^{2}kA(k).

(23)

Proof.

To determine a recursion for (
20
), we start from

\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}

\displaystyle\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{n}\binom{2n}{n}^{2}\frac{O_{n}}{1+2n-2\lambda}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{n}\binom{2n}{n}^{2}\frac{O_{n+1}-\frac{1}{2n+1}}{1+2n-2\lambda}

(24)

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{n}\binom{2n}{n}^{2}\frac{O_{n+1}}{1+2n-2\lambda}-\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{n}\binom{2n}{n}^{2}\frac{1}{(2n+1)(1+2n-2\lambda)}.

For the second term at the right-hand side we use (
21
) and the well known series

\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{n}\binom{2n}{n}^{2}\frac{1}{2n+1}=\frac{4G}{\pi}.

Using partial fractions, we then obtain:

\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{n}\binom{2n}{n}^{2}\frac{1}{(2n+1)(1+2n-2\lambda)}=\frac{1}{2\lambda}A(\lambda)-\frac{1}{2\lambda}\frac{4G}{\pi}.

For the first term at the right-hand side we use reindexing:

\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{n}\binom{2n}{n}^{2}\frac{O_{n+1}}{1+2n-2\lambda}=4\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{n}\binom{2n}{n}^{2}O_{n}\frac{n^{2}}{(2n-1)^{2}(1+2n-2\lambda-2)}.

The last factor has the following partial fraction expansion:

\frac{n^{2}}{(2n-1)^{2}(1+2n-2\lambda-2)}=\frac{1}{16\lambda^{2}}\frac{(1+2\lambda)^{2}}{2n+1-2\lambda-2}-\frac{1}{16\lambda^{2}}\frac{1+4\lambda}{2n-1}-\frac{1}{8\lambda}\frac{1}{(2n-1)^{2}}

leading to the following two known series (see
[
22
]
)

\displaystyle-\frac{1+4\lambda}{16\lambda^{2}}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{n}\binom{2n}{n}^{2}\frac{O_{n}}{2n-1}=-\frac{1+4\lambda}{16\lambda^{2}}\frac{2\ln(2)}{\pi},\hskip 28.45274pt

\displaystyle-\frac{1}{8\lambda}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{n}\binom{2n}{n}^{2}\frac{O_{n}}{(2n-1)^{2}}=-\frac{1}{2\lambda}\frac{G-\ln(2)}{\pi},\hskip 28.45274pt

and the series

\frac{(1+2\lambda)^{2}}{16\lambda^{2}}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{n}\binom{2n}{n}^{2}\frac{O_{n}}{1+2n-2\lambda-2}.

Setting

C(m):=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{n}\binom{2n}{n}^{2}\frac{O_{n}}{1+2n-2m}

(
24
) can be rewritten as:

C(\lambda+1)=\frac{4\lambda^{2}}{(1+2\lambda)^{2}}C(\lambda)+\frac{2}{(1+2\lambda)^{2}}\left(\lambda A(\lambda)+\frac{\ln(2)}{\pi}\right)\ \ \mbox{with}\ \ C(1)=\frac{2\ln(2)}{\pi}.

To solve this recurrence, note that

z(\lambda):=2\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{\lambda}\binom{2\lambda}{\lambda}^{2}\lambda^{2}C(\lambda)

is a solution of the recurrence

z(\lambda+1)=z(\lambda)+\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{\lambda}\binom{2\lambda}{\lambda}^{2}\left(\lambda A(\lambda)+\frac{\ln(2)}{\pi}\right)\ \ \ \text{with}\ \ \ z(0)=0.

An explicit evaluation for this solution is given by

z(\lambda)=\sum_{k=0}^{\lambda-1}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{k}\binom{2k}{k}^{2}\left(kA(k)+\frac{\ln(2)}{\pi}\right),

leading to:

C(\lambda)=\frac{16^{\lambda}}{2\lambda^{2}\binom{2\lambda}{\lambda}^{2}}\sum_{k=0}^{\lambda-1}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{k}\binom{2k}{k}^{2}\left(kA(k)+\frac{\ln(2)}{\pi}\right)

(25)

which is equivalent with (
23
).
∎

Theorem 3
.

For all
\lambda\in\mathbb{N}
we have

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{n}\binom{2n}{n}^{2}\frac{O_{n}}{(1+2n-2\lambda)^{2}}=\frac{16^{\lambda}}{\lambda^{2}\binom{2\lambda}{\lambda}^{2}}\left(\frac{G-\ln(2)}{\pi}+\sum_{k=1}^{\lambda-1}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{k}\binom{2k}{k}^{2}D(k)\right)

(26)

where
C(m)
is given by (
25
) and

D(m):=\frac{1}{2m+1}\left(mC(m)-\frac{2m-1}{4}A(m)-\frac{\ln(2)}{\pi}\right)+\frac{m}{2}B(m).

Proof.

In this case we start from:

\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}

\displaystyle\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{n}\binom{2n}{n}^{2}\frac{O_{n}}{(1+2n-2\lambda)^{2}}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{n}\binom{2n}{n}^{2}\frac{O_{n+1}-\frac{1}{2n+1}}{(1+2n-2\lambda)^{2}}

(27)

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{n}\binom{2n}{n}^{2}\frac{O_{n+1}}{(1+2n-2\lambda)^{2}}-\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{n}\binom{2n}{n}^{2}\frac{1}{(2n+1)(1+2n-2\lambda)^{2}}.

The rest of the proof is very similar to the proof of the previous theorem, but in this case we need the following two results:

\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{n}\binom{2n}{n}^{2}\frac{1}{(1+2n-2\lambda)^{2}}=B(\lambda)

and

\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{n}\binom{2n}{n}^{2}\frac{O_{n}}{1+2n-2\lambda}=C(\lambda).

Let us define

E(m):=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{n}\binom{2n}{n}^{2}\frac{O_{n}}{(1+2n-2m)^{2}}

then the corresponding recurrence relation can be written as:

(\lambda+1)^{2}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{\lambda+1}\binom{2\lambda+2}{\lambda+1}^{2}E(\lambda+1)=\lambda^{2}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{\lambda}\binom{2\lambda}{\lambda}^{2}E(\lambda)+\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{\lambda}\binom{2\lambda}{\lambda}^{2}D(\lambda)

with
D(m)
as in the statement of the theorem and with
E(1)=\frac{4G-4\ln(2)}{\pi}
.
∎

Theorem 4
.

For all
\lambda\in\mathbb{N}
we have

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{n}\binom{2n}{n}^{2}\frac{O_{n}^{2}}{1+2n-2\lambda}=\frac{16^{\lambda}}{\lambda^{2}\binom{2\lambda}{\lambda}^{2}}\left(\frac{48G-\pi^{2}-24\ln^{2}(2)}{48\pi}+\sum_{k=1}^{\lambda-1}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{k}\binom{2k}{k}^{2}F(k)\right)

(28)

where
C(m)
is given by (
25
) and

F(m):=mC(m)+\frac{1}{4}A(m)-\frac{\pi^{2}+24\ln^{2}(2)}{48\pi}.

Proof.

We start with

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{n}\binom{2n}{n}^{2}\frac{O_{n}^{2}}{1+2n-2\lambda}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{n}\binom{2n}{n}^{2}\frac{\left(O_{n+1}-\frac{1}{2n+1}\right)^{2}}{1+2n-2\lambda}.

We proceed in the same way as before. After expanding using partial fractions, we need the sums of some additional series:

\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{n}\binom{2n}{n}^{2}\frac{1}{(2n+1)^{2}}=-\frac{16\mathcal{G}}{\pi}+\frac{3\pi^{2}}{8}+\frac{\ln^{2}(2)}{2}\quad\text{(see \eqref{eq:5})},

\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{n}\binom{2n}{n}^{2}\frac{O_{n}^{2}}{(2n-1)^{2}}=-\frac{8G}{\pi}-\frac{8G\ln(2)}{\pi}-\frac{48\mathcal{G}}{\pi}+\frac{9\pi^{2}}{8}+\frac{\pi}{6}+\frac{4\ln^{2}(2)}{\pi}+\frac{3\ln^{2}(2)}{2}

(see Theorem
1
), and

\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{n}\binom{2n}{n}^{2}\frac{O_{n}}{(2n-1)^{3}}=-\frac{4G(\ln(2)+2)}{\pi}-\frac{32\mathcal{G}}{\pi}+\frac{6\ln(2)}{\pi}+\frac{3\pi^{2}}{4}+\ln^{2}(2).

The sum of this last series can be found by reindexing:

\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}

\displaystyle\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{n}\binom{2n}{n}^{2}\frac{O_{n}}{(2n-1)^{3}}=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{n}\frac{4(2n-1)^{2}}{n^{2}}\binom{2n-2}{n-1}^{2}\frac{O_{n-1}+\tfrac{1}{2n-1}}{(2n-1)^{3}}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{4}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{n}\binom{2n}{n}^{2}\frac{O_{n}}{(n+1)^{2}(2n+1)}+\frac{1}{4}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{n}\binom{2n}{n}^{2}\frac{1}{(n+1)^{2}(2n+1)^{2}}.

In addition to the series used before, here we also need these:

\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{n}\binom{2n}{n}^{2}\frac{1}{(n+1)^{i}}\ (i=1,2)\quad\text{(see \cite[cite]{[\@@bibref{}{Nimbran2015}{}{}]})},

\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{n}\binom{2n}{n}^{2}\frac{O_{n}}{(n+1)^{i}}\ (i=1,2)\quad\text{(see \cite[cite]{[\@@bibref{}{WangChu2022}{}{}]})},

and

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{n}\binom{2n}{n}^{2}\frac{O_{n}}{2n+1},

which is given in (
17
). Defining

G(m):=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{n}\binom{2n}{n}^{2}\frac{O_{n}^{2}}{1+2n-2m}

we find the same type of recurrence for
G
.
∎

Theorem 5
.

For
\lambda\in\mathbb{N}
,

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{n}\binom{2n}{n}^{2}\frac{O_{n}^{2}}{(1+2n-2\lambda)^{2}}=\frac{16^{\lambda}}{\lambda^{2}\binom{2\lambda}{\lambda}^{2}}\left(K+\sum_{k=1}^{\lambda-1}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{k}\binom{2k}{k}^{2}H(k)\right)

(29)

with

K:=\frac{1}{4}\left(-\frac{8G}{\pi}-\frac{8G\ln(2)}{\pi}-\frac{48\mathcal{G}}{\pi}+\frac{9\pi^{2}}{8}+\frac{\pi}{6}+\frac{4\ln^{2}(2)}{\pi}+\frac{3\ln^{2}(2)}{2}\right)

and

H(m):=\frac{2m(G(m)-C(m))+C(m)-A(m)}{2(2m+1)}+\frac{\pi^{2}+24\ln^{2}(2)}{24(2m+1)\pi}+\frac{1}{4}B(m)+mE(m).

Proof.

The method is the same as in the proof of the previous theorems. We start with:

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{n}\binom{2n}{n}^{2}\frac{O_{n}^{2}}{(1+2n-2\lambda)^{2}}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{n}\binom{2n}{n}^{2}\frac{\left(O_{n+1}-\frac{1}{2n+1}\right)^{2}}{(1+2n-2\lambda)^{2}}.

Expanding the square in the numerator and reindexing the series containing
O_{n}
leads to a sum of three series:

\displaystyle 4\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}

\displaystyle\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{n}\binom{2n}{n}^{2}O_{n}^{2}\frac{n^{2}}{(2n-1)^{2}(1+2n-2\lambda-2)^{2}},

\displaystyle-8\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}

\displaystyle\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{n}\binom{2n}{n}^{2}O_{n}\frac{n^{2}}{(2n-1)^{3}(1+2n-2\lambda-2)^{2}},

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}

\displaystyle\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{n}\binom{2n}{n}^{2}\frac{1}{(2n+1)^{2}(1+2n-2\lambda-2)^{2}}.

We now use partial fractions as before. One of the series we obtain is the series (
29
) with
\lambda
replaced by
\lambda+1
. All the other ones we have already encountered in the previous theorems.
Setting

J(m):=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{n}\binom{2n}{n}^{2}\frac{O_{n}^{2}}{(1+2n-2m)^{2}},

we again find that
J
satisfies a first order recurrence:

(\lambda+1)^{2}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{\lambda+1}\binom{2\lambda+2}{\lambda+1}^{2}J(\lambda+1)=\lambda^{2}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{\lambda}\binom{2\lambda}{\lambda}^{2}J(\lambda)+\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{\lambda}\binom{2\lambda}{\lambda}^{2}H(\lambda)

with
J(1)=4K
by Theorem
1
. Solving this recurrence in the same way as in Theorem
2
leads to the desired result.
∎

Theorem 7
.

For any integer
p\geq 0
, we have

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\binom{2n}{n}\frac{z^{2n+1}}{(2n+1)^{p+1}}=\frac{\theta}{2}\frac{\ln^{p}(2\sin\theta)}{p!}+\frac{1}{4p!}\sum_{j=1}^{p}(-1)^{j-1}\binom{p}{j}\ln^{p-j}(2\sin\theta){\rm Ls}_{j+1}(2\theta),

(36)

where
\theta:=\arcsin(2z)
and

\displaystyle{\rm Ls}_{j}(\theta):=-\int_{0}^{\theta}\ln^{j-1}\left(2\sin\frac{t}{2}\right)\,dt.

(37)

Proof.

We begin with the elementary Maclaurin series expansion

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\binom{2n}{n}\frac{z^{2n+1}}{2n+1}=\frac{1}{2}\arcsin(2z),

(38)

which is the
p=0
case. Now, from (
38
), one deduces for
p>0
that

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\binom{2n}{n}\frac{z^{2n+1}}{(2n+1)^{p+1}}=\frac{1}{2(p-1)!}\int_{0}^{z}\frac{\ln^{p-1}\left(\frac{z}{t}\right)\arcsin(2t)}{t}dt

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2(p-1)!}\int_{0}^{z}\frac{\ln^{p-1}\left(\frac{4z}{4t}\right)\arcsin(2t)}{4t}d(4t)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2(p-1)!}\sum_{j=0}^{p-1}\binom{p-1}{j}(-1)^{j}\ln^{p-1-j}(4z)\int_{0}^{z}\frac{\ln^{j}(4t)\arcsin(2t)}{4t}d(4t)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2(p-1)!}\sum_{j=0}^{p-1}\binom{p-1}{j}\frac{(-1)^{j}}{j+1}\ln^{p-1-j}(4z)\int_{0}^{z}\arcsin(2t)d\ln^{j+1}(4t)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2(p-1)!}\sum_{j=0}^{p-1}\binom{p-1}{j}\frac{(-1)^{j}}{j+1}\ln^{p-1-j}(4z)\int_{0}^{\theta}xd\ln^{j+1}(2\sin x)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2(p-1)!}\sum_{j=0}^{p-1}\binom{p-1}{j}\frac{(-1)^{j}}{j+1}\ln^{p-1-j}(4z)\left(\theta\ln^{j+1}(2\sin\theta)-\int_{0}^{\theta}\ln^{j+1}(2\sin x)dx\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2(p-1)!}\theta\ln^{p}(2\sin\theta)\sum_{j=0}^{p-1}\binom{p-1}{j}\frac{(-1)^{j}}{j+1}+\frac{1}{4p!}\sum_{j=1}^{p}(-1)^{j-1}\binom{p}{j}\ln^{p-j}(2\sin\theta){\rm Ls}_{j+1}(2\theta).

Then, noting the fact that

\sum_{j=0}^{p-1}\binom{p-1}{j}\frac{(-1)^{j}}{j+1}=\frac{1}{p},

we obtain the desired evaluation.
∎

Setting
p\leq 3
in (
36

(with
\theta:=\arcsin(2z)

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\binom{2n}{n}\frac{z^{2n+1}}{2n+1}=\frac{1}{2}\theta,

(39)

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\binom{2n}{n}\frac{z^{2n+1}}{(2n+1)^{2}}=\frac{1}{2}\theta\ln(2\sin\theta)+\frac{1}{4}{\rm Cl}_{2}(2\theta),

(40)

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\binom{2n}{n}\frac{z^{2n+1}}{(2n+1)^{3}}=\frac{1}{4}\theta\ln^{2}(2\sin\theta)+\frac{1}{4}\ln(2\sin\theta){\rm Cl}_{2}(2\theta)-\frac{1}{8}{\rm Ls}_{3}(2\theta),

(41)

where we used the relation
{\rm Ls}_{2}(\theta)={\rm Cl}_{2}(\theta)
and for any positive integer
m
the Clausen function
{\rm Cl}_{m}(\theta)
is defined
as follows, for all

Remark 2

The series in (

\mathbb{Q}
-combination of the series treated in

]
. We offer a sketch of a proof,
as below, based on this alternate approach.

First, for all

\displaystyle t_{k}({\boldsymbol{\sl{s}}}):=\sum_{k\geq k_{1}>\dots>k_{d}>0}\frac{1}{(2k_{1}-1)^{s_{1}}\cdots(2k_{d}-1)^{s_{d}}}.

They satisfy the stuffle relations. For example,

\displaystyle t_{k}(1)^{2}=2t_{k}(1,1)+t_{k}(2).

Since
O_{k}=t_{k}(1)
the

\displaystyle\sum_{k>0}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{k}\binom{2k}{k}^{2}\frac{O_{k}^{2}}{(2k-1)^{2}}=2\sum_{k>0}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{k}\binom{2k}{k}^{2}\frac{t_{k}(1,1)}{(2k-1)^{2}}+\sum_{k>0}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{k}\binom{2k}{k}^{2}\frac{t_{k}(2)}{(2k-1)^{2}}.

{\boldsymbol{\sl{s}}}=(s_{1},\dots,s_{d})\in\mathbb{N}^{d}

\displaystyle\,\sum_{k>0}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{k}\binom{2k}{k}^{2}\frac{t_{k}({\boldsymbol{\sl{s}}})}{(2k-1)^{m}}

\displaystyle\,\sum_{k>k_{2}>\cdots>k_{d}>0}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{k}\binom{2k}{k}^{2}\frac{1}{(2k-1)^{s_{1}+m}(2k_{2}-1)^{s_{2}}\cdots(2k_{d}-1)^{s_{d}}}

\displaystyle\,+\sum_{k>k_{1}>\cdots>k_{d}>0}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{k}\binom{2k}{k}^{2}\frac{1}{(2k-1)^{m}(2k_{1}-1)^{s_{1}}\cdots(2k_{d}-1)^{s_{d}}},
