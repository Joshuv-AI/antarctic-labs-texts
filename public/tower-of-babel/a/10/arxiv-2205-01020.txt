This comment refutes the claim made by A. Jadczyk and A.M. Schlichtinger in arXiv:2203.14555 that the photon position operator with commuting components proposed in 1999 does not have the properties required for a photon position operator.

acceptable states. For mass positive definite particles this little group is
the three-dimensional rotation group, while for the zero mass photons it is a
single rotation that can be called
\widehat{J}_{3}

gauge transformations. This is discussed in
Weinberg

WignerLittleGroup
