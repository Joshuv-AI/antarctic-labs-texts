The influence of the couplings on the Z-production at ILC in the Randall - Sundrum model


Abstract
An attempt is made to present the influence of the couplings on e − ​ e + e^{-}e^{+} scattering process with polarized initial beams in the Randall-Sundrum (RS) model. We have studied the contribution of radion, Higgs and gauge bosons on the Z-production cross-section at International Linear Colliders (ILC). The cross-section depends strongly on the polarization of e − e^{-} , e + e^{+} initial beams, the center of mass energy s \sqrt{s} and model interaction parameters. The results indicate that with the influence of the couplings, including radion and Higgs in RS model, the cross-sections for the Z production are enhanced, in which the u, t-channels have given dominated contributions.

A review of Randall-Sundrum model and the mixing of Higgs-radion

The RS model consists of one extra dimension bounded by two 3-branes, this can be viewed as an

kr_{c}\simeq 11-12

\overline{M}_{Pl}^{2}=M_{5}^{3}/k
is derived from the 5D action. The scale of physical phenomena on the IR-brane is given by
\Lambda_{\phi}\equiv\overline{M}_{Pl}e^{-kr_{c}\pi}

few TeV. The mass of the
n^{th}

, with

[
23

. The coupling strength of the graviton KK states to the SM fields is

Gravitational fluctuations around the background metric will be defined by replacing:

\eta_{\mu\nu}\rightarrow\eta_{\mu\nu}+\epsilon h_{\mu\nu}(x,y)\qquad b_{0}\rightarrow b_{0}+b(x).

(2)

The gravity-matter interactions have been obtained
[

\mathcal{L}_{int}=-\dfrac{1}{\hat{\Lambda}_{W}}\Sigma_{n\neq 0}h^{n}_{\mu\nu}T^{\mu\nu}-\dfrac{\phi_{0}}{\Lambda_{\phi}}T^{\mu}_{\mu},

IV
Conclusion

In this paper, we have studied the contribution of the couplings on the Z production at ILC in the RS model. The results show that u, t- channels have given the main contribution in high energy
due to the electron exchanges
. With the available value of parameters
f_{4}^{V},f_{5}^{V}

(V=\gamma,Z)
, the total cross-section can be reached the observable value. The total cross-section for
e^{+}e^{-}\rightarrow ZZ\rightarrow l^{-}l^{+}q\overline{q}
process is larger than that for
e^{+}e^{-}\rightarrow\gamma Z\rightarrow\gamma l^{-}l^{+}(\gamma q\overline{q})
under the same conditions. This is due to
hZZ,\phi ZZ
couplings which are much larger than
\gamma Zh,\gamma Z\phi
vertices.

Finally, it is worth noting that the anomalous couplings of the SM gauge boson (
\gamma,Z
)
have been expected to be larger compared to the radion, Higgs contribution
. This result fits into the previous work Ref.
[
16
]
.
