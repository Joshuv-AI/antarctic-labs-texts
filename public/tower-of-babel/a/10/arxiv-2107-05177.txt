Asymptotic stability of planar stationary solution to a 2D hyperbolic-elliptic coupled system of the radiating gas in half space
Minyi Zhang, Changjiang Zhu

Abstract
This paper is concerned with the asymptotic stability of planar stationary solution to an initial-boundary value problem for a two-dimensional hyperbolic-elliptic coupled system of the radiating gas in half space. We show that the solution to the problem converges to the corresponding planar stationary solution as time tends to infinity under small initial perturbation. These results are proved by the standard L 2 L^{2} -energy method. Moreover, we prove that the solution ( u , q ) (u,q) converges to the corresponding planar stationary solution at the rate t − α / 2 − 1 / 4 t^{-\alpha/2-1/4} for non-degenerate case, and t − 1 / 4 t^{-1/4} for degenerate case. The proof is based on the time and space weighted energy method. Keywords: Hyperbolic-elliptic coupled system; the radiating gas; planar stationary solution; L 2 L^{2} -energy method; asymptotic stability. AMS subject classifications: 35L65; 76N15; 35M33; 35B40.

\displaystyle+C\int_{\mathbbm{R}}\int_{\mathbbm{R}_{+}}(|\nabla{v}|+\bar{u}_{x})|v_{y}||v_{yy}||\nabla{v}_{yy}|+|\nabla{v}_{y}||v_{yy}||\nabla{v}_{yy}|+|v_{y}||\nabla{v}_{yy}|^{2}+(|\nabla{v}|+\bar{u}_{x})|v_{yyy}||\nabla{v}_{yy}|)\,\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y

\displaystyle:=\sum_{i=1}^{14}II_{i}.

Using
(
3.8
)
,
(
2.5
)
,
(
2.4
)
,
(
4.58
)
,
(
4.59
)
and the
\mathrm{H\ddot{o}lder}
inequality and the right-hand side can be estimated as

\displaystyle II_{1}\leq C\|\nabla{v}\|_{L_{x}^{2}(L_{y}^{\infty})}\|v_{yy}\|_{L_{x}^{\infty}(L_{y}^{2})}\|v_{yy}\|_{L^{2}}\leq CE(\tau)D(\tau)^{2}(1+\tau)^{-2},

\displaystyle II_{2}\leq C\|v_{y}\|_{L^{\infty}}\|\nabla{v}_{y}\|_{L^{2}}\|v_{yy}\|_{L^{2}}\leq CE(\tau)D(\tau)^{2}(1+\tau)^{-2},

\displaystyle II_{3}\leq C\|v_{y}\|_{L^{\infty}}^{2}\|v_{y}\|_{L^{2}}\|v_{yy}\|_{L^{2}}\leq C\|v_{y}\|_{L^{\infty}}^{2}\|v_{yy}\|_{L^{2}}\leq CE(\tau)D(\tau)^{2}(1+\tau)^{-2},

\displaystyle II_{4}+II_{8}\leq C\|\nabla{v}\|_{L^{\infty}}\|\nabla{v}_{yy}\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+C\|\bar{u}_{xx}v_{yy}\|_{L^{2}}\|\nabla{v}_{yy}\|_{L^{2}}\leq C(\delta+\varepsilon_{3})\|\nabla{v}_{yy}\|_{L^{2}}^{2},

\displaystyle II_{5}+II_{12}\leq C\|\nabla{v}_{y}\|_{L_{x}^{2}(L_{y}^{\infty})}\|\nabla{v}_{y}\|_{L_{x}^{\infty}(L_{y}^{2})}\|\nabla{v}_{yy}\|_{L^{2}}\leq CE(\tau)D(\tau)^{2}(1+\tau)^{-\frac{9}{4}},

\displaystyle II_{6}\leq C\|v_{yy}\|_{L_{x}^{2}(L_{y}^{\infty})}\|\nabla^{2}{v}\|_{L_{x}^{\infty}(L_{y}^{2})}\|\nabla{v}_{yy}\|_{L^{2}}\leq CE(\tau)D(\tau)^{2}(1+\tau)^{-2},

\displaystyle II_{7}\leq C\|v_{y}\|_{L^{\infty}}\|\nabla^{2}{v}_{y}\|_{L^{2}}\|\nabla{v}_{yy}\|_{L^{2}}\leq CE(\tau)D(\tau)^{2}(1+\tau)^{-\frac{9}{4}},

\displaystyle II_{9}\leq C\|{v}_{y}\|_{L^{\infty}}\|v_{y}\|_{L^{2}}\|\nabla{v}_{yy}\|_{L^{2}}\leq CE(\tau)D(\tau)^{2}(1+\tau)^{-2},

\displaystyle II_{13}+II_{14}\leq C(\|\nabla{v}\|_{L^{\infty}}+\|\bar{u}_{x}\|_{L^{\infty}})\|\nabla{v}_{yy}\|_{L^{2}}\leq C(\delta+\varepsilon_{3})\|\nabla{v}_{yy}\|_{L^{2}}^{2}.

4.71
)

\displaystyle C(\delta+\varepsilon_{3})\|\nabla{v}_{yy}\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+CE(\tau)D(\tau)^{2}(1+\tau)^{-2}.

(4.72)

Notice that the first term of

)
can absorbed in the fifth term on the left-hand side of

)
if

and

\displaystyle\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}(\|v_{yy}(t)\|_{L^{2}}^{2}\!+\!\|\nabla{v}_{yy}(t)\|_{L^{2}}^{2})\!+\!\|\sqrt{\bar{u}_{x}}v_{yy}(\tau)\|_{L^{2}}^{2}\!+\!\|\sqrt{\bar{u}_{x}}\nabla{v}_{yy}(\tau)\|_{L^{2}}^{2}\!+\!\|\nabla{v}_{yy}(\tau)\|_{L^{2}}^{2}\!+\!\int_{\mathbbm{R}}|\nabla{v}_{yy}(0,y,\tau)|^{2}\,\mathrm{d}y

(4.73)

\displaystyle\leq CE(\tau)D(\tau)^{2}(1+\tau)^{-2}.

Multiplying
(
4.73
)
by
(1+t)^{\gamma+2}
and integrating the resulting inequality over
\mathbbm{R}_{+}\times\mathbbm{R}\times[0,t]
, for any
\gamma\geq 0
, we obtain

\displaystyle(1+t)^{\gamma+2}(\|v_{yy}(t)\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\|\nabla{v}_{yy}(t)\|_{L^{2}}^{2})+\int_{0}^{t}(1+\tau)^{\gamma+2}(\|\sqrt{\bar{u}}_{x}v_{yy}(\tau)\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\|\sqrt{\bar{u}}_{x}\nabla{v}_{yy}(\tau)\|_{L^{2}}^{2})\,\mathrm{d}\tau

(4.74)

\displaystyle+\int_{0}^{t}(1+\tau)^{\gamma+2}\|\nabla{v}_{yy}(\tau)\|_{L^{2}}^{2}\,\mathrm{d}\tau+\int_{0}^{t}(1+\tau)^{\gamma+2}\int_{\mathbbm{R}}|\nabla{v}_{yy}(0,y,\tau)|^{2}\,\mathrm{d}y\mathrm{d}\tau

\displaystyle C\left(M_{0}^{2}+(\gamma+2)\int_{0}^{t}(1+\tau)^{\gamma+1}(\|v_{yy}(\tau)\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\|\nabla{v}_{yy}(\tau)\|_{L^{2}}^{2})\,\mathrm{d}\tau+N(t)\int_{0}^{t}(1+\tau)^{\gamma}D(\tau)^{2}\,\mathrm{d}\tau\right).

By utilizing
(
4.70
)
, taking
\gamma=\alpha+\varepsilon
in
(

)

\displaystyle(1+t)^{\alpha+2+\varepsilon}\|v_{yy}(t)\|_{H^{1}}^{2}+\int_{0}^{t}(1+\tau)^{\alpha+2+\varepsilon}(\|\sqrt{\bar{u}}_{x}v_{yy}(\tau)\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\|\sqrt{\bar{u}}_{x}\nabla{v}_{yy}(\tau)\|_{L^{2}}^{2})\,\mathrm{d}\tau

(4.75)

\displaystyle+\int_{0}^{t}(1+\tau)^{\alpha+2+\varepsilon}\|\nabla{v}_{yy}(\tau)\|_{L^{2}}^{2}\,\mathrm{d}\tau+\int_{0}^{t}(1+\tau)^{\alpha+2+\varepsilon}\int_{\mathbbm{R}}|\nabla{v}_{yy}(0,y,t)|^{2}\,\mathrm{d}y\mathrm{d}\tau

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C\left(M_{0}^{2}+(\alpha+2+\varepsilon)\int_{0}^{t}(1+\tau)^{\alpha+1+\varepsilon}\|v_{yy}(\tau)\|_{H^{1}}^{2}\,\mathrm{d}\tau+N(t)\int_{0}^{t}(1+\tau)^{\alpha+\varepsilon}D(\tau)^{2}\,\mathrm{d}\tau\right)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C(1+t)^{\varepsilon}M_{\alpha}^{2}+CN(t)\int_{0}^{t}(1+\tau)^{\alpha+\varepsilon}D(\tau)^{2}\,\mathrm{d}\tau.

Finally, we need to treat the last term in
(
4.70
)
and
(
4.75
)
. We add
(
4.52
)
,
(
4.70
)
and
(
4.75
)
to obtain

\displaystyle\sum_{l=0}^{2}(1+t)^{\alpha+l+\varepsilon}\|\partial_{y}^{l}v\|_{H^{3-l}}^{2}+\sum_{l=0}^{2}\int_{0}^{t}(1+\tau)^{\alpha+l+\varepsilon}\|\partial_{y}^{l}\nabla v\|_{H^{2-l}}^{2}\,\mathrm{d}\tau

(4.76)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C(1+t)^{\varepsilon}M_{\alpha}^{2}+CN(t)\int_{0}^{t}(1+\tau)^{\alpha+\varepsilon}D(\tau)^{2}\,\mathrm{d}\tau.

Lemma 4.7
.

Under the same assumptions as Lemma
4.6
, it holds that

\displaystyle(1+t)^{\alpha+1+\varepsilon}(\|{\rm{div}}{p}_{y}(t)\|_{H^{2}}^{2}+\|p_{1y}(t)\|_{H^{2}}^{2}+\|p_{2}(t)\|_{H^{3}}^{2})\leq C(1+t)^{\varepsilon}M_{\alpha}^{2},

(4.77)

for
t\in[0,T]
and any
\varepsilon>0
.

Proof.

Rewrite
{\rm{div}}\partial_{y}\eqref{eq-vp-rd}_{2}
in the form
\Delta{\rm{div}}{p}_{y}-{\rm{div}}{p}_{y}=\Delta v_{y}
, and then square this equation. Consequently, integrating the resulting equation over
\mathbbm{R}_{+}\times\mathbbm{R}
, using
(
4.66
)
, we have

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbbm{R}}\int_{\mathbbm{R}_{+}}((\Delta{\rm{div}}{p}_{y})^{2}+2|\nabla{\rm{div}}{p}_{y}|^{2}+({\rm{div}}{p}_{y})^{2})\,\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y

(4.78)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbbm{R}}\int_{\mathbbm{R}_{+}}(\Delta v_{y})^{2}\,\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y-2\int_{\mathbbm{R}}{\rm{div}}{p}_{xy}(0,y,t){\rm{div}}{p}_{y}(0,y,t)\,\mathrm{d}y

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbbm{R}}\int_{\mathbbm{R}_{+}}(\Delta v_{y})^{2}\,\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y+2u_{-}\int_{\mathbbm{R}}{\rm{div}}{p}_{xy}(0,y,t)v_{xy}(0,y,t)\,\mathrm{d}y

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbbm{R}}\int_{\mathbbm{R}_{+}}(\Delta v_{y})^{2}\,\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y+\delta\int_{\mathbbm{R}}{\rm{div}}{p}_{xy}(0,y,t)\,\mathrm{d}y+C\delta^{-1}\int_{\mathbbm{R}}(v_{xy}(0,y,t))^{2}\,\mathrm{d}y

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbbm{R}}\int_{\mathbbm{R}_{+}}(\Delta v_{y})^{2}\,\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y+\delta\int_{\mathbbm{R}}\int_{\mathbbm{R}_{+}}\left(({\rm{div}}{p}_{xy})^{2}+({\rm{div}}{p}_{xxy})^{2}\right)\,\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y+C\int_{\mathbbm{R}}(v_{xy}^{2}+v_{xxy}^{2})\,\mathrm{d}y.

Notice that the term
{\rm{div}}{p}_{xy}
on the right-hand side of
(
4.78
)
can be absorbed in the second term on the left if
\delta
is sufficiently small. Combining the results of Lemma
4.6
, we just need to get the estimate of
\delta\|{\rm{div}}{p}_{xxy}\|_{L^{2}}^{2}
. We know that

\delta\|{\rm{div}}{p}_{xxy}\|_{L^{2}}^{2}\leq C\delta\|\Delta{\rm{div}}{p}_{y}\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+C\delta\|{\rm{div}}{p}_{yyy}\|_{L^{2}}^{2},

and the former term can be absorbed in the first term of
(
4.78
)
. Therefore, for some small but fixed
\delta
, the inequality
(
4.78
)
can be rewritten as

\int_{\mathbbm{R}}\int_{\mathbbm{R}_{+}}((\Delta{\rm{div}}{p}_{y})^{2}+2|\nabla{\rm{div}}{p}_{y}|^{2}+({\rm{div}}{p}_{y})^{2})\,\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y\leq C\|\nabla{v}_{y}\|_{H^{1}}^{2}+C\delta\|{\rm{div}}{p}_{yyy}\|_{L^{2}}^{2}.

(4.79)

Now we estimate the last term
\|{\rm{div}}{p}_{yyy}\|_{L^{2}}
. Rewriting
\nabla\eqref{eq-rd2-dengjia}_{2y}
in the form
\nabla{\rm{div}}{p}_{yy}-\nabla p_{2y}=\nabla v_{yy}
and squaring it, we obtain

|\nabla{\rm{div}}{p}_{yy}|^{2}+|\nabla p_{2yy}|^{2}+|\nabla p_{2y}|^{2}-2\{\nabla{\rm{div}}{p}_{y}\cdot\nabla p_{2y}\}_{y}=|\nabla v_{yy}|^{2}-2\nabla p_{1xy}\cdot\nabla p_{2yy}.

(4.80)

Integrating the above equation over
\mathbbm{R}_{+}\times\mathbbm{R}
, we can get

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbbm{R}}\int_{\mathbbm{R}_{+}}(|\nabla{\rm{div}}{p}_{yy}|^{2}+|\nabla p_{2yy}|^{2}+|\nabla p_{2y}|^{2})\,\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y

(4.81)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbbm{R}}\int_{\mathbbm{R}_{+}}|\nabla v_{yy}|^{2}\,\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y-2\int_{\mathbbm{R}}\int_{\mathbbm{R}_{+}}(p_{1xxy}p_{2xyy}+p_{1xyy}p_{2yyy})\,\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbbm{R}}\int_{\mathbbm{R}_{+}}|\nabla v_{yy}|^{2}\,\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y+\int_{\mathbbm{R}}\int_{\mathbbm{R}_{+}}|\nabla{\rm{div}}{p}_{y}|^{2}\,\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y,

where we have used the fact that

2p_{1xxy}p_{2xyy}+2p_{1xyy}p_{2yyy}\leq(p_{1xxy}+p_{2xyy})^{2}+(p_{1xyy}+p_{2yyy})^{2}=|\nabla{\rm{div}}{p}_{y}|^{2}.

Adding
(
4.81
)
and
(
4.79
)
, for some small
\delta
, we can obtain

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbbm{R}}\int_{\mathbbm{R}_{+}}(|\nabla^{2}{\rm{div}}{p}_{y}|^{2}+|\nabla{\rm{div}}{p}_{y}|^{2}+({\rm{div}}{p}_{y})^{2}+|\nabla p_{2yy}|^{2}+|\nabla p_{2y}|^{2})\,\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y\leq C\|\nabla{v}_{y}\|_{H^{1}}^{2}.

(4.82)

Here, we have used the fact that
|\nabla^{2}{\rm{div}}{p}_{y}|^{2}\leq C(\Delta{\rm{div}}{p}_{y})^{2}+C|\nabla{\rm{div}}{p}_{yy}|^{2}
. According to
(
3.3
)
, we can deduce that

\displaystyle\|\nabla^{k}p_{1y}\|_{L^{2}}^{2}\leq C(\|\nabla^{k}{\rm{div}}{p}_{xy}\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\|\nabla^{k}v_{xy}\|_{L^{2}}^{2})\leq C\|\nabla{v}_{y}\|_{H^{1}}^{2},\quad k=0,1,

(4.83)

\displaystyle\|\nabla^{k}p_{2}\|_{L^{2}}^{2}\leq C(\|\nabla^{k}{\rm{div}}{p}_{y}\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\|\nabla^{k}v_{y}\|_{L^{2}}^{2})\leq C\|\nabla{v}_{y}\|_{H^{1}}^{2}+C\|\nabla^{k}{v}_{y}\|_{L^{2}}^{2},\quad k=0,1,2.

Furthermore, using the relation between
p_{1}
and
p_{2}
, that is
(
3.4
)
and
{\rm{div}}{p}=p_{1x}+p_{2y}
, we have

\displaystyle p_{2xxx}=p_{1xxy}={\rm{div}}{p}_{xy}-p_{2xyy}={\rm{div}}{p}_{xy}-p_{1yyy},

(4.84)

\displaystyle p_{2xxy}=p_{1xyy}={\rm{div}}{p}_{yy}-p_{2yyy}.

It follows from
(
4.82
)
that

\|\nabla^{3}p_{2}\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\|\nabla^{2}p_{1y}\|_{L^{2}}^{2}\leq C\|\nabla{v}_{y}\|_{H^{1}}^{2}.

(4.85)

Thus, combining

\displaystyle(1+t)^{\alpha+1+\varepsilon}(\|{\rm{div}}{p}_{y}\|_{H^{2}}^{2}+\|p_{1y}\|_{H^{2}}^{2}+\|p_{2}\|_{H^{3}}^{2})\leq C(1+t)^{\varepsilon}M_{\alpha}^{2},
