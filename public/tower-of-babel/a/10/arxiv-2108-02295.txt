Claus Hertling and Makiko Mase

Abstract
. A paper of the first author and Zilke proposed seven combinatorial problems around formulas for the characteristic polynomial and the exponents of an isolated quasihomogeneous singularity. The most important of them was a conjecture on the characteristic polynomial. Here the conjecture is proved, and some of the other problems are solved, too. In the cases where also an old conjecture of Orlik on the integral monodromy holds, this has implications on the automorphism group of the Milnor lattice. The combinatorics used in the proof of the conjecture consists of tuples of orders on sets { 0 , 1 , … , n } \{0,1,...,n\} with special properties and may be of independent interest.

Definition 1.1
.

Let
M\subset{\mathbb{N}}=\{1,2,3,...\}
be a finite nonempty subset.
Its

is a pair
(H_{M},h_{M})
with

a
{\mathbb{Z}}
-lattice of rank
\sum_{m\in M}\varphi(m)
and

an automorphism with characteristic polynomial

\prod_{m\in M}\Phi_{m}
(
\Phi_{m}

and with a cyclic generator

\displaystyle H_{M}=\bigoplus_{j=1}^{rkM}{\mathbb{Z}}\cdot h_{M}^{j-1}(e_{1}).

(
1.1
)

(H_{M},h_{M})
is unique up to isomorphism.

\Aut_{S^{1}}(H_{M},h_{M})
denotes the group of all automorphisms of

which commute with

and which have all eigenvalues in
S^{1}
.

Definition 1.2
.

A finite set

is enriched as follows to a
directed graph
(M,E(M))
with set of vertices
M

and set of oriented edges

An edge goes from

Theorem 1.3

[

Let

A weight system

-lattice of some rank

\displaystyle p_{ch,h_{Mil}}

\displaystyle=\prod_{m\in{\mathbb{N}}}\Phi_{m}^{\psi(m)}

\displaystyle M_{1}\supset M_{2}\supset...\supset M_{l_{\psi}}\neq\emptyset,\quad\psi(m)=|\{j\in\{1,...,l_{\psi}\}\,|\,m\in M_{j}\}|.
