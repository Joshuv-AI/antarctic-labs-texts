. Note that Leibnitz formula for

,
g(A)=\sum_{y}g_{y}A^{y}
,

,

.

Now we are ready to prove (

). First, we begin with
x=-1
:

\displaystyle[p^{-1},q^{y}]

\displaystyle=p^{-1}q^{y}-q^{y}p^{-1}\text{, so}

\displaystyle p[p^{-1},q^{y}]p

\displaystyle=q^{y}p-pq^{y}=-[p,q^{y}]=-cyq^{y-1}.

Thus, now it will be checked that right-hand side of (

) for
x=-1
equals
-cyq^{y-1}
when it is left- and right-multiplied by
p
:

\displaystyle\partial_{p}^{k}p^{-i}

\displaystyle=(-i)\cdots(-i-k+1)p^{-i-k}=(-1)^{k}\frac{(i+k-1)!}{(i-1)!}p^{-i-k}\text{, so}

\displaystyle\partial_{p}^{k}p^{-1}

\displaystyle=(-1)^{k}k!p^{-k-1};

\displaystyle\text{let }A

\displaystyle:=-\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{(-c)^{k}}{k!}(\partial_{p}^{k}p^{-1})(\partial_{q}^{k}q^{y})\text{, then}

\displaystyle pAp

\displaystyle=-p\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{(-c)^{k}}{k!}(-1)^{k}k!p^{-k-1}y(y-1)\cdots(y-k+1)q^{y-k}p

\displaystyle=-\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}c^{k}y(y-1)\cdots(y-k+1)p^{-k}\left(pq^{y-k}-[p,q^{y-k}]\right)

\displaystyle=-\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}c^{k}y(y-1)\cdots(y-k+1)\left(p^{-k+1}q^{y-k}-c(y-k)p^{-k}q^{y-k-1}\right)

\displaystyle=-c^{1}yp^{0}q^{y-1}-\underbrace{\sum_{k=2}^{\infty}c^{k}y(y-1)\cdots(y-k+1)p^{-k+1}q^{y-k}}_{\text{this term equals\ldots}}

\displaystyle\quad+\underbrace{\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}c^{k+1}y(y-1)\cdots(y-k)p^{-k}q^{y-k-1}}_{\text{\ldots this term}}

\displaystyle=-cyq^{y-1}\text{; then,}

\displaystyle A

\displaystyle=-p^{-1}cyq^{y-1}p^{-1}=p^{-1}p[p^{-1},q^{y}]pp^{-1}=[p^{-1},q^{y}].

\displaystyle[p^{x+z},q^{y}]

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad+p^{x}(\partial_{p}^{k}p^{z})(\partial_{q}^{k}q^{y})\Big)

\displaystyle\quad-\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{(-c)^{k}}{k!}\left((\partial_{p}^{k}p^{x})p^{z}(\partial_{q}^{k}q^{y})+p^{x}(\partial_{p}^{k}p^{z})(\partial_{q}^{k}q^{y})\right)

\displaystyle=-\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}k=2\\
(k+l\mapsto k)\end{subarray}}^{\infty}\sum_{l=1}^{k-1}\frac{(-c)^{k}}{(k-l)!l!}(\partial_{p}^{k-l}p^{x})(\partial_{p}^{l}p^{z})(\partial_{q}^{k}q^{y})

\displaystyle\quad-\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{(-c)^{k}}{k!}\Big(\underbrace{(\partial_{p}^{k}p^{x})(\partial_{p}^{0}p^{z})}_{l=0}+\underbrace{(\partial_{p}^{0}p^{x})(\partial_{p}^{k}p^{z})}_{l=k}\Big)(\partial_{q}^{k}q^{y})
