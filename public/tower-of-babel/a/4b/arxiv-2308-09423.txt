Overconvergent prismatic cohomology
Andreas Langer

Abstract
. In this note I define an overconvergent version of prisms and prismatic cohomology as introduced by Bhatt and Scholze and show that overconvergent prismatic cohomology specialises to p p -adic cohomologies, like Monsky-Washnitzer resp. rigid cohomology for smooth varieties over a perfect field, the de Rham cohomology of smooth weak formal schemes over a perfectoid ring and the étale cohomology of its generic fibre. Besides, I give an overconvergent version of the complex A ​ Ω A\Omega of Bhatt-Morrow-Scholze and relate it to overconvergent prismatic cohomology.

Perfect dagger prisms, perfectoid dagger algebras and the structure sheaf of the overconvergent prismatic site
