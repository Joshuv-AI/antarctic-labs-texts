Abstract
. Let G = ( V , E ) G=(V,E) be a connected finite graph. We study a system of non-Abelian multiple vortex equations on G G . We establish a necessary and sufficient condition for the existence and uniqueness of solutions to the non-Abelian multiple vortex equations.
