black-boxes and free software

Integrated natural resources modelling and management (INRMM)
[

frequently exploits chains of nontrivial data-transformation models (

Those

Semantics and design diversity

Silent faults
[
62
]
are a critical class of software errors altering computation output without evident symptoms – such as computation premature interruption (exceptions, error messages, …), obviously unrealistic results or computation patterns (e.g. noticeably shorter/longer or endless computations). As it has been underlined, “many scientific results are corrupted, perhaps fatally so, by undiscovered mistakes in the software used to calculate and present those results”
[
63
]
.
