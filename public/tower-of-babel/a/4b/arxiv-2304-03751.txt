Abstract
. In this paper we describe the categories 𝕃 R , [ ℝ R ] \mathbb{L}_{R},\,[\mathbb{R}_{R}] whose objects are left [right] ideals of a Noetherian ring R R with unity and morphisms are appropriate R R -linear transformations. Further it is shown that these are preadditive categories with zero object and are full subcategories of the R R - modulue category with the property that these are categories with subobjects and the morphisms admits factorization property.
