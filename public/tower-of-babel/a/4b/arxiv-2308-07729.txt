Decay rates for mild solutions of QGE with critical fractional dissipation in L 2 ​ ( ℝ 2 ) L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{2})
Jamel Benameur

Abstract
In [ 2 ] the authors proved some asymptotic results for the global solution of critical Quasi-geostrophic equation with a condition on the decay of θ 0 ^ \widehat{\theta_{0}} near at zero. In this paper, we prove that this condition is not necessary. Fourier analysis and standard techniques are used.

Introduction

In this paper, we consider the Quasi-Geostrophic Equations with critical fractional dissipation defined on
\mathbb{R}^{2}
by:

(1)\left\{\begin{array}[]{l}\partial_{t}\theta+|D|\theta+u_{\theta}\nabla\theta=0\,in\,\mathbb{R}^{+}\times\mathbb{R}^{2}\\
\theta(0)=\theta^{0}\,in\,\mathbb{R}^{2},\end{array}\right.

where
\theta(x,t)
denotes the potential temperature, and
u_{\theta}(x,t)
stands for the fluid
velocity field. Here, we define

u_{\theta}=(\partial_{2}(-\Delta)^{1/2}\theta,-\partial_{1}(-\Delta)^{1/2}\theta)

and, as a result, one infers

{\rm div}\,u_{\theta}=0\,{\rm and}\,|\widehat{u_{\theta}}|=|\widehat{\theta}|.

Furthermore, the fractional Laplacian
(-\Delta)^{1/2}=|D|
is defined by

(-\Delta)^{1/2}f=\mathcal{F}^{-1}(|\xi|\widehat{f}(\xi))

for
all
f\in S^{\prime}(\mathbb{R}^{2})
such that
\widehat{f}
is given by a local integrable function. If
\theta_{0}
is more regular, we have the Duhamel formula

\theta(t,x)=e^{-t|D|}\theta_{0}(x)-\int_{0}^{t}e^{-(t-z)|D|}{\rm div}\,(\theta u_{\theta})(z,x)dz.
