The
n

\displaystyle C_{n}({X})=\{{(x_{1},\dots,x_{n})\in X^{n}}|{x_{i}\neq x_{j}\text{ if }i\neq j}\}.

The homology groups of the unordered configuration spaces

The cohomology algebra of unordered configuration spaces over closed manifolds was computed by Félix and Tanré in
[
3

with coefficients

6

The main result of this paper is the description of the
