Subordination Involving Gauss Hypergeometric Function
Anish Kumar and Sourav Das

Abstract
. The primary objective of this work is to obtain some sufficient conditions so that normalized Gauss hypergeometric function satisfies exponential starlikeness and convexity in the unit disk. Moreover, conditions on parameter of this function has been derived for being Janowski convexity and starlikeness with the help of differential subprdination. Results established in this work are presumably new and their significance is illustrated by several consequences.
