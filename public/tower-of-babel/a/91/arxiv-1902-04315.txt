Implications of JLA data for k − k- essence model of dark energy with given equation of state


Abstract

The constraints are expressed in terms of parameters ( w a , w b w_{a},w_{b} ) appearing in the chosen functional form for w ⁡ ( z ) w(z) , corresponding to each of the above mentioned models. Dark energy with varying equation of state may be
realised theoretically
in terms of dynamics of a scalar field (
\phi

One class of such scalar field
models, called ‘Quintessence’, is described in terms of standard canonical Lagrangian of
the form
L=X-V(\phi)

There also exist alternative class of models involving Lagrangians with
non-canonical kinetic terms as

is a function of

k-
essence models, have interesting phenomenological consequences
different from those of quintessence models. Another motivation for considering

in low energy effective string theory. Such theories with non-canonical kinetic terms
was first proposed by Born and Infeld to get rid of the infinite self-energy of the electrons

[

[

\phi\equiv\phi(t)

V

The constancy of the potential ensures existence of a scaling relation,

XF_{X}^{2}=Ca^{-6}

C=
constant), in a Friedmann-Lemaitre-Robertson-Walker (FLRW) background space-time with scale factor
a

We exploited the scaling relation and observational constraints
on the parameters
w_{a},w_{b}

to reconstruct the forms of the function
F(X)

for the different varying dark energy models.

In the context of
k-

40

small fluctuations in the background energy density.
Imaginary value of the sound speed (

Also from causality, it requires the speed of propagation of

c_{s}^{2}<1

However, it was pointed out in

42

43

perturbations on classical backgrounds is admissible
and no causal paradoxes arise. This implies

k-
essence theories.
For each of the varying dark energy models considered here,
we find

This has been found over the entire redshift range

For each of the varying DE models, we have found the regions in

implying that observational data allow values of
parameters (
w_{a},w_{b}

For example, for BA and Logarithmic models

It is allowed only at

For each of the models we have found the point in

The form of the
k-

\displaystyle 1+2X\frac{L_{XX}}{L_{X}}>0

(17)

Now introducing

\displaystyle c_{s}^{2}

\displaystyle\equiv

\displaystyle\left[1+2X\frac{L_{XX}}{L_{X}}\right]^{-1}

(18)

plays the role of sound speed squared for propagation of small perturbations.
However, in the context of spatially flat FRW universe, with small perturbations, neglecting vector perturbations
which decay as

written as

\displaystyle ds^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(1+2\Phi)dt^{2}-a^{2}(t)[(1-2\Phi)\delta_{ij}+h_{ij}]dx^{i}dx^{j}

(19)

where
\Phi
(the gravitational Newtonian potential) is the scalar perturbation
and
h_{ij}
is traceless transverse perturbations. From the standard results of cosmological
perturbation theory
[
75
,
76
,
77
]
, it follows that
perturbations in the k-essence field
\delta\phi

which are gauge invariant are connected with the scalar metric perturbations
and the dynamics of cosmological perturbation may described by the action of the
form

\displaystyle S_{c}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\int d^{3}xd\eta\left[\left(\frac{dv}{d\eta}\right)^{2}-c_{s}^{2}(\nabla v)^{2}-m_{c}^{2}v^{2}\right]

,
{\cal H}=(1/a)(da/d\eta)

m_{c}^{2}\equiv-(1/z)(d^{2}z/d\eta^{2})
with

representing sound speed squared for propagation of small perturbations in Eq. (

\displaystyle c_{s}^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{dp/dX}{d\rho/dX}

(21)

is also obtained in
[
71
]

F(X)
of the scaling relation Eq. (

\displaystyle c_{s}^{2}

\displaystyle\frac{F_{X}}{2XF_{XX}+F_{X}}

Neglecting the contribution to the present day energy density due to radiation

\displaystyle\omega(z)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-1+\frac{1}{3}\frac{\big[E(z)^{2}-\Omega_{m}^{0}(1+z)^{3}\big]^{\prime}(1+z)}{E(z)^{2}-\Omega_{m}^{0}(1+z)^{3}}

\displaystyle q(z)=-\frac{a\ddot{a}}{\dot{a}^{2}}\quad\mbox{and}\quad j(z)=\frac{\dddot{a}a^{2}}{\dot{a}^{3}}
