The linear-non-linear substitution 2 2 -monad
Martin Hyland, Christine Tasson

Abstract
We introduce a general construction on 2 2 -monads. We develop background on maps of 2 2 -monads, their left semi-algebras, and colimits in 2 2 -category. Then, we introduce the construction of a colimit induced by a map of 2 2 -monads, show that we obtain the structure of a 2 2 -monad and give a characterisation of its algebras. Finally, we apply the construction to the map of 2 2 -monads between free symmetric monoidal and the free cartesian 2 2 -monads and combine them into a linear-non-linear 2 2 -monad.
