Partition models, Permutations of infinite sets without fixed points, and weak forms of 𝖠𝖢 \mathsf{AC}
Amitayu Banerjee

Abstract
. We study new relations of the following statements with weak choice principles in 𝖹𝖥 \mathsf{ZF} (Zermelo–Fraenkel set theory without the Axiom of Choice ( 𝖠𝖢 \mathsf{AC} )) and 𝖹𝖥𝖠 \mathsf{ZFA} ( 𝖹𝖥 \mathsf{ZF} with the axiom of extensionality weakened to allow the existence of atoms). • There does not exist an infinite Hausdorff space X X such that every infinite subset of X X contains an infinite compact subset. • For every set X X there is a set A A such that there exists a choice function on the collection [ A ] 2 [A]^{2} of two-element subsets of A A and satisfying | X | ≤ | 2 A 2 | |X|\leq|2^{A^{2}}| . • If a field has an algebraic closure then it is unique up to isomorphism. • For every infinite set X X , there exists a permutation of X X without fixed points. • 𝗏𝖣𝖢𝖯 \mathsf{vDCP} (Van Douwen’s Choice Principle). • Any infinite locally finite connected graph has a spanning subgraph omitting K 2 , n K_{2,n} for any 2 < n ∈ ω 2<n\in\omega . • Any infinite locally finite connected graph has a spanning m m -bush for any even integer m ≥ 4 m\geq 4 . We also study the new status of different weak choice principles in the finite partition model (a type of permutation model of 𝖹𝖥𝖠 + ¬ 𝖠𝖢 \mathsf{ZFA+\neg AC} ) introduced by Benjamin Baker Bruce in 2016.

.

Define the following set.

(1)

\prod_{i\in I}^{weak}G_{i}=\left\{f:I\rightarrow\bigcup_{i\in I}G_{i}\;\middle|\;(\forall i\in I)f(i)\in G_{i},f(i)=1_{G_{i}}\text{ for all but finitely many }i\right\}.
