Classification of qubit cellular automata on hypercubic lattices
Andrea Pizzamiglio, Alessandro Bisio, Paolo Perinotti

Abstract
We classify Quantum Cellular Automata whose cells are qubits , on hypercubic lattices ℤ s \mathbb{Z}^{s} , with the von Neumann neighborhood scheme, in terms of realizability as finite-depth quantum circuits. We show the most general structure of such automata and use its characterisation to simulate a few steps of evolution and evaluate the rate of entanglement production between one cell and its surroundings.
