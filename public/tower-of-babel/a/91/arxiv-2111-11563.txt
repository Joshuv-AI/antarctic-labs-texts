Multivariable de Rham representations, Sen theory and p p -adic differential equations
O. Brinon & B. Chiarellotto & N. Mazzari

Abstract
. Let K K be a complete valued field extension of 𝐐 p \QQ_{p} with perfect residue field. We consider p p -adic representations of a finite product G K , Δ = G K Δ G_{K,\Delta}=G_{K}^{\Delta} of the absolute Galois group G K G_{K} of K K . This product appears as the fundamental group of a product of diamonds. We develop the corresponding p p -adic Hodge theory by constructing analogues of the classical period rings 𝖡 dR \B_{\dR} and 𝖡 HT \B_{\HT} , and multivariable Sen theory. In particular, we associate to any p p -adic representation V V of G K , Δ G_{K,\Delta} an integrable p p -adic differential system in several variables 𝖣 𝖽𝗂𝖿 ⁡ ( V ) \D_{\dif}(V) . We prove that this system is trivial if and only if the representation V V is de Rham. Finally, we relate this differential system to the multivariable overconvergent ( φ , Γ ) (\varphi,\Gamma) -module of V V constructed by Pal and Zábrádi in [ 20 ] , along classical Berger’s construction [ 5 ] .

3.6.

C_{\Delta}
-representations

We fix terminology and notation that will be used hereafter.

Definition

Let

B
a topological ring endowed with a continuous action of
G

B
-representation
of
G
is a topological module of finite type
W
endowed with a continuous and semi-linear action of
G
,
i.e.
such that

for all

,

and

. We say that

is free (resp. projective) of rank
d
when the underlying
B
-module is. We denote by
\Rep_{B}(G)
(resp.
\Rep_{B}^{\free}(G)
, resp.
\Rep_{B}^{\pr}(G)
) the category of
B
-representations with
G
-equivariant maps (resp. the full subcategory of free, resp. projective
B
-representations of finite rank).

Remark 3.7

If
W
is a free
B
-representation of rank
d
of
G
, and
\mathfrak{B}
is a basis of
W
over
B
, we can denote by

acting on
W

for all

is a continuous

-representation structure. Moreover, changing the basis precisely amounts to replace the cocycle by a cohomologous one. This means that isomorphism classes of free

-representations of rank

are in bijection with the continuous cohomology set

Fix

. Let

be an open normal subgroup, and put

3.30.
Generalized Sen operators

Let

be a free

-representation of rank

of
\Gamma_{K,\Delta}
.

Definition
.

The Sen operators of

are the maps

given by

\varphi_{\alpha}(y)=\lim\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}\gamma\in\Gamma_{K_{\alpha}}\\
\gamma\to\Id\end{subarray}}\frac{\gamma(y)-y}{\log(\chi(\gamma))}.

Note that

for all
\alpha\in\Delta
. As
\Gamma_{K,\Delta}
is commutative (since
\Gamma_{K}
is), the operators

\End_{K_{\Delta,\infty}}(Y)

\varphi_{\alpha}
commutes with the action of
\Gamma_{K,\Delta}
by construction.

These operators describe the infinitesimal action of

on

. More precisely, we have

Proposition 3.31
.

(cf
[
22
, Theorem 4]
). For all

, there is an open subgroup
\Gamma_{K,\Delta,y}\lhd\Gamma_{K,\Delta}
such that for all
\alpha\in\Delta
and
\gamma\in\Gamma_{K,\alpha}\cap\Gamma_{K,\Delta,y}

\gamma(y)=\exp\big(\log(\chi(\gamma))\varphi_{\alpha}\big)y.

Corollary 3.32
.

The set of elements in

on which the action of
\Gamma_{K,\Delta}
is finite (i.e. factors through a finite quotient) is
\bigcap\limits_{\alpha\in\Delta}\Ker(\varphi_{\alpha})
.

Proof.

Let

. By proposition
3.31
, there exists an open subgroup
\Gamma_{K,\Delta,y}\lhd\Gamma_{K,\Delta}
such that for all
\alpha\in\Delta
and
\gamma\in\Gamma_{K,\alpha}\cap\Gamma_{K,\Delta,y}
, we have

, so that the action of

on

. Conversely, if the action of

on

is finite, there exists a open normal subgroup

Lemma 5.21

Proof.

The
\mathbf{A}_{K,\Delta}^{\dagger}
-module
\D^{\dagger}(T)

generating the unit ideal in

r_{T}\in\QQ_{>0}

a_{i}\in\mathbf{A}_{K,\Delta}^{(0,r_{T}]}
and there exists a

-basis
(x_{i,j})_{1\leq j\leq d}
of
\D^{\dagger}(T)_{a_{i}}

\D^{(0,r_{T}]}(T)_{a_{i}}

. By theorem

T
over
