Exact Analytical Taub-NUT like solution in f ⁡ ( T ) f(T) gravity
Joshua G. Fenwick, Masoud Ghezelbash

Abstract
We construct exact analytical Taub-NUT solutions in the context of f ⁡ ( T ) f(T) gravity. We study the physical properties of the solutions, and compare them with those of the Taub-NUT solution in Einstein gravity.

As one would expect, the Kerr/CFT correspondence is not only a peculiar property of extremal black holes but also non-extremal Kerr black holes. However, at the near-horizon region of the non-extremal Kerr black holes, one cannot indicate any conformal symmetries. In other words, the conformal symmetries are not the symmetries of the non-extremal Kerr black hole geometry (as they are for the case of the extremal Kerr black holes).
