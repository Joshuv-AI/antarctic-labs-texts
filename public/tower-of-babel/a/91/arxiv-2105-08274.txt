Weighted Sylvester sums on the Frobenius set
Takao Komatsu, Yuan Zhang

Abstract
Let a a and b b be relatively prime positive integers. In this paper the weighted sum ∑ n ∈ NR ⁡ ( a , b ) λ n − 1 ​ n m \sum_{n\in{\rm NR}(a,b)}\lambda^{n-1}n^{m} is given explicitly or in terms of the Apostol-Bernoulli numbers, where m m is a nonnegative integer, and NR ⁡ ( a , b ) {\rm NR}(a,b) denotes the set of positive integers nonrepresentable in terms of a a and b b . Keywords: Frobenius problem, weighted sums, Sylvester sums, Apostol-Bernoulli numbers



1
Introduction

The
Frobenius Problem
is to determine the largest positive integer that is NOT representable as a nonnegative integer combination of given positive integers that are coprime (see
[
13
]
for general references).

Given positive integers
a_{1},\dots,a_{m}
with
\gcd(a_{1},\dots,a_{m})=1
, it is well-known that all sufficiently large
n
the equation

a_{1}x_{1}+\cdots+a_{m}x_{m}=n

(1)

has a solution with nonnegative integers
x_{1},\dots,x_{m}
.

The
Frobenius number

F(a_{1},\dots,a_{m})
is the LARGEST integer
n
such that (
1
) has no solution in nonnegative integers.
For
m=2
, we have

F(a,b)=(a-1)(b-1)-1

(Sylvester (1884)
[
17
]
). For
m\geq 3
, exact determination of the Frobenius number is difficult.
The Frobenius number cannot be given by closed formulas of a certain type (Curtis (1990)
[
6
]
), the problem to determine
F(a_{1},\dots,a_{m})
is NP-hard under Turing reduction (see, e.g., Ramírez Alfonsín
[
13
]
). Nevertheless, the Frobenius number for some special cases are calculated (e.g.,
[
12
,
14
,
16
]
). One convenient formula is by Johnson
[
9
]
. One analytic approach to the Frobenius number can be seen in
[
4
,
10
]
.
Some formulae for the Frobenius number in three variables can be seen in
[
19
]
.

For given
a
and
b
with
\gcd(a,b)=1
, let
{\rm NR}(a,b)
denote the set of nonnegative integers nonrepresentable in term of
a
and
b
, namely the set of all those nonnegative integers
n
which cannot be expressed in the form
n=ax+by
, where
x
and
y
are nonnegative integers.

There are many kinds of problems related to the Frobenius problem. The problems for the number of solutions (e.g.,
[
18
]
), and the sum of integer powers of the gaps values in numerical semigroups (e.g.,
[
5
,
8
,
7
]
) are popular.
One of other famous problems is about the so-called
Sylvester sums

\sum_{n\in{\rm NR}(a,b)}n^{m}
, where
m
is a nonnegative integer (see, e.g.,
[
20
]
and references therein).
Recently in
[
3
]
, one of more general cases is considered by giving the largest integer, the number of integers and the sum of integers whose number of representation is less than or equal to
k
. When
k=1
, the problem is reduced to the classical case.

In this paper, we consider the weighted sum

S_{m}^{(\lambda)}(a,b):=\sum_{n\in{\rm NR}(a,b)}\lambda^{n-1}n^{m}\quad(\lambda\neq 0)\,.

Sylvester
[
17
]
showed that
S_{0}^{(1)}(a,b)=(a-1)(b-1)/2
, and
Brown and Shuie showed
[
5
]
that

S_{1}^{(1)}(a,b)=\frac{1}{12}(a-1)(b-1)(2ab-a-b-1)\,.

Rødseth
[
15
]
obtained a general formula for
S_{m}^{(1)}
in terms of Bernoulli numbers and deduced

S_{2}^{(1)}(a,b)=\frac{1}{12}(a-1)(b-1)ab(ab-a-b)\,.

Tuenter
[
20
]
also investigated
S_{m}^{(1)}
by taking a different approach. He established relations between Sylvester sums and the power sums over the natural numbers.
Wang and Wang
[
21
]
considered the alternating Sylvester sums

T_{m}(a,b)=\sum_{n\in{\rm NR}(a,b)}(-1)^{n}n^{m}

by using Bernoulli and Euler numbers.

The purpose of this paper is to give an explicit expression for
S_{m}^{(\lambda)}(a,b)
. For
m=1
, we can give the following formula.

Theorem 1
.

For
\lambda\neq 0
with
\lambda^{a}\neq 1
and
\lambda^{b}\neq 1
,

S_{1}^{(\lambda)}(a,b)=\frac{1}{(\lambda-1)^{2}}+\frac{ab\lambda^{ab-1}}{(\lambda^{a}-1)(\lambda^{b}-1)}-\frac{(\lambda^{ab}-1)\bigl((a+b)\lambda^{a+b}-a\lambda^{a}-b\lambda^{b}\bigr)}{\lambda(\lambda^{a}-1)^{2}(\lambda^{b}-1)^{2}}\,.

We also give a general expression of
S_{m}^{(\lambda)}(a,b)
in terms of the Apostol-Bernoulli numbers. The alternating Sylvester sums in
[
21
]
can be also expressed as
T_{m}(a,b)=-S_{m}^{(-1)}(a,b)
.

The main new results (Theorems
2
and
3
below) cover all values of
m
and
\lambda
, and express
S_{m}^{(\lambda}(a,b)
in terms of the Apostol-Bernoulli numbers.
In case
m=1
and
\lambda^{a}\not=1
the expressions reduce to those given explicitly in Theorem
1
.

2
An explicit expression for
m=1

As in
[
5
]
, define

f(x)=\sum_{n=0}^{ab-a-b}\bigl(1-r(n)\bigr)x^{n}\,,

where
r(n)
denotes the number of representations of
n
in the form
n=sa+tb
, where
s
and
t
are nonnegative integers.
Since
r(n)=0
or
1
for
0\leq n\leq ab-1
, we have

\displaystyle f^{\prime}(\lambda)

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=1}^{ab-a-b}n\bigl(1-r(n)\bigr)\lambda^{n-1}=\sum_{1\leq n\leq ab-a-b\atop r(n)=0}n\lambda^{n-1}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n\in{\rm NR}(a,b)}\lambda^{n-1}n=S_{1}^{(\lambda)}(a,b)\,.

We use the following fact from
[
5
]
.

Lemma 1
.

f(x)=\frac{g(x)}{h(x)}\,,

where

g(x)=\sum_{k=1}^{b-1}\frac{x^{ak}-x^{k}}{x-1}\quad\hbox{and}\quad h(x)=\sum_{k=0}^{b-1}x^{k}\,.

Suppose that
\lambda\neq 1\neq\lambda^{a}
. Then

h(\lambda)=\frac{\lambda^{b}-1}{\lambda-1}

and

\displaystyle h^{\prime}(\lambda)=\sum_{k=0}^{b-1}k\lambda^{k-1}

\displaystyle=\frac{b\lambda^{b-1}}{\lambda-1}-\frac{\lambda^{b}-1}{(\lambda-1)^{2}}\,.

Also, we have

g(\lambda)=\frac{(\lambda^{ab}-1)(\lambda-1)-(\lambda^{a}-1)(\lambda^{b}-1)}{(\lambda^{a}-1)(\lambda-1)^{2}}

and

\displaystyle g^{\prime}(\lambda)

\displaystyle=\frac{(ab+1)\lambda^{ab}-ab\lambda^{ab-1}-(a+b)\lambda^{a+b+1}+a\lambda^{a-1}+b\lambda^{b-1}-1}{(\lambda^{a}-1)(\lambda-1)^{2}}

\displaystyle\quad-\frac{a\lambda^{a-1}}{\lambda^{a}-1}g(\lambda)-\frac{2}{\lambda-1}g(\lambda)\,.

Hence, we finally get

\displaystyle S_{1}^{(\lambda)}(a,b)

\displaystyle=f^{\prime}(\lambda)=\frac{g^{\prime}(\lambda)h(\lambda)-g(\lambda)h^{\prime}(\lambda)}{\bigl(h(\lambda)\bigr)^{2}}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{(\lambda-1)^{2}}+\frac{ab\lambda^{ab-1}}{(\lambda^{a}-1)(\lambda^{b}-1)}-\frac{(\lambda^{ab}-1)\bigl((a+b)\lambda^{a+b}-a\lambda^{a}-b\lambda^{b}\bigr)}{\lambda(\lambda^{a}-1)^{2}(\lambda^{b}-1)^{2}}\,.

In particular, for
\lambda=2
, we have the following.

Corollary 1
.

\displaystyle\sum_{n\in{\rm NR}(a,b)}2^{n-1}n

\displaystyle=1+\frac{ab2^{ab-1}}{(2^{a}-1)(2^{b}-1)}

\displaystyle\quad-\frac{(2^{ab}-1)\bigl((a+b)2^{a+b}-2^{a}a-2^{b}b\bigr)}{2(2^{a}-1)^{2}(2^{b}-1)^{2}}\,.

For example, for
a=3
and
b=17
,

\displaystyle S_{1}^{(2)}(3,17)

\displaystyle=2^{0}\cdot 1+2^{1}\cdot 2+2^{3}\cdot 4+2^{4}\cdot 5+2^{6}\cdot 7+2^{7}\cdot 8+2^{9}\cdot 10

\displaystyle\quad+2^{10}\cdot 11+2^{12}\cdot 13+2^{13}\cdot 14+2^{15}\cdot 16+2^{18}\cdot 19+2^{21}\cdot 22

\displaystyle\quad+2^{24}\cdot 25+2^{27}\cdot 28+2^{30}\cdot 31

\displaystyle=37515351605\,.

From Theorem
1
(or the above Corollary),

\displaystyle S_{1}^{(2)}(3,17)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{(2-1)^{2}}+\frac{3\cdot 17\cdot 2^{3\cdot 17-1}}{(2^{3}-1)(2^{17}-1)}

\displaystyle\quad-\frac{(2^{3\cdot 17}-1)\bigl((3+17)2^{3+17}-3\cdot 2^{3}-17\cdot 2^{17}\bigr)}{2(2^{3}-1)^{2}(2^{17}-1)^{2}}

\displaystyle=37515351605\,.

Similarly, by replacing
2
by another value, we can obtain that

\displaystyle S_{1}^{(5)}(3,17)

\displaystyle=900879734470832437423896\,,

\displaystyle S_{1}^{(1/2)}(3,17)

\displaystyle=\frac{8822132865}{1073741824}\,,

\displaystyle S_{1}^{(-1)}(3,17)

\displaystyle=408\,,

\displaystyle S_{1}^{(-5/3)}(3,17)

\displaystyle=\frac{760508529478902941119864}{205891132094649}\,,

\displaystyle S_{1}^{(\pm\sqrt{2})}(3,17)

\displaystyle=34250061\pm 6965604\sqrt{2}\,.

3
Weighted sums of higher power

Since

\displaystyle f^{\prime\prime}(x)

\displaystyle=\frac{g^{\prime\prime}(x)}{h(x)}-\frac{2g^{\prime}(x)h^{\prime}(x)+h(x)^{\prime\prime}(x)}{\bigl(h(x)\bigr)^{2}}+\frac{2g(x)\bigl(h^{\prime}(x)\bigr)^{2}}{\bigl(h(x)\bigr)^{3}}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=2}^{ab-a-b}n(n-1)\bigl(1-r(n)\bigr)x^{n-2}\,,

we get

xf^{\prime\prime}(x)+f^{\prime}(x)=\sum_{n=0}^{ab-a-b}n^{2}\bigl(1-r(n)\bigr)x^{n-1}\,.

Hence,

S_{2}^{(\lambda)}(a,b)=\lambda f^{\prime\prime}(\lambda)+f^{\prime}(\lambda)\,.

For simplicity, put
X_{1}=(a+b)\lambda^{a+b}-a\lambda^{a}-b\lambda^{b}
and
X_{2}=(a+b)^{2}\lambda^{a+b}-a^{2}\lambda^{a}-b^{2}\lambda^{b}
. Since

f^{\prime}(\lambda)=\frac{1}{(\lambda-1)^{2}}+\frac{ab\lambda^{ab-1}}{(\lambda^{a}-1)(\lambda^{b}-1)}-\frac{(\lambda^{ab}-1)X_{1}}{\lambda(\lambda^{a}-1)^{2}(\lambda^{b}-1)^{2}}\,,

we get

\displaystyle f^{\prime\prime}(\lambda)

\displaystyle=-\frac{2}{(\lambda-1)^{3}}+\frac{ab(ab-1)\lambda^{ab-2}}{(\lambda^{a}-1)(\lambda^{b}-1)}-\frac{2ab\lambda^{ab-2}X_{1}}{(\lambda^{a}-1)^{2}(\lambda^{b}-1)^{2}}

\displaystyle\quad-\frac{(\lambda^{ab}-1)(X_{2}-X_{1})}{\lambda^{2}(\lambda^{a}-1)^{2}(\lambda^{b}-1)^{2}}+\frac{2(\lambda^{ab}-1)X_{1}}{\lambda^{3}(\lambda^{a}-1)^{3}(\lambda^{b}-1)^{3}}\,.

Therefore, we obtain

\displaystyle S_{2}^{(\lambda)}(a,b)

\displaystyle=-\frac{\lambda+1}{(\lambda-1)^{2}}+\frac{a^{2}b^{2}\lambda^{ab-1}}{(\lambda^{a}-1)(\lambda^{b}-1)}-\frac{2ab\lambda^{ab}X_{1}+(\lambda^{ab}-1)X_{2}}{\lambda(\lambda^{a}-1)^{2}(\lambda^{b}-1)^{2}}

\displaystyle\quad+\frac{2(\lambda^{ab}-1)X_{1}}{\lambda^{2}(\lambda^{a}-1)^{3}(\lambda^{b}-1)^{3}}\,.

Similarly, we see that

\displaystyle S_{3}^{(\lambda)}(a,b)

\displaystyle=\lambda^{2}f^{\prime\prime\prime}(\lambda)+3\lambda f^{\prime\prime}(\lambda)+f^{\prime}(\lambda)\,,

\displaystyle S_{4}^{(\lambda)}(a,b)

\displaystyle=\lambda^{3}f^{(4)}(\lambda)+6\lambda^{2}f^{\prime\prime\prime}(\lambda)+7\lambda f^{\prime\prime}(\lambda)+f^{\prime}(\lambda)\,,

\displaystyle S_{5}^{(\lambda)}(a,b)

\displaystyle=\lambda^{4}f^{(5)}(\lambda)+10\lambda^{3}f^{(4)}(\lambda)+25\lambda^{2}f^{\prime\prime\prime}(\lambda)+15\lambda f^{\prime\prime}(\lambda)+f^{\prime}(\lambda)\,.

4
Apostol-Bernoulli numbers

Though we may obtain explicit expressions of
S_{m}^{(\lambda)}(a,b)
for small positive integers
m
, it is hard to obtain the formula for large
m
. In this section, using the so-called Apostol-Bernoulli numbers, we give an expression of
S_{m}^{(\lambda)}(a,b)
for general positive integral
m
.

Apostol-Bernoulli polynomials
\mathcal{B}_{n}(x,\lambda)
are defined by the generating function (
[
1
, p.165, (3.1)]
)

\frac{ze^{xz}}{\lambda e^{z}-1}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\mathcal{B}_{n}(x,\lambda)\frac{z^{n}}{n!}\quad(|z+\log\lambda|<2\pi)\,.

(2)

When
\lambda=1
in (
2
),
B_{n}(x)=\mathcal{B}_{n}(x,1)
are the classical Bernoulli numbers. When
x=0
in (
2
),
\mathcal{B}_{n}(\lambda)=\mathcal{B}_{n}(0,\lambda)
are
Apostol-Bernoulli numbers
(
[
11
, Definition 1.2]
), defined by

\frac{z}{\lambda e^{z}-1}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\mathcal{B}_{n}(\lambda)\frac{z^{n}}{n!}\quad(|z+\log\lambda|<2\pi)\,.

(3)

They seem to be also called
\lambda
-Bernoulli numbers.
When
\lambda=1
, the generating function of the left-hand side in (
3
) is exactly the same as that of the classical Bernoulli numbers
B_{n}
. But it does not imply that
\mathcal{B}_{n}(1)=B_{n}
on the right-hand side though quite a few authors misunderstand. In fact, as seen in
[
1
, p.165]
, the first several values are given by

\displaystyle\mathcal{B}_{0}(\lambda)=0,\quad\mathcal{B}_{1}(\lambda)=\frac{1}{\lambda-1},\quad\mathcal{B}_{2}(\lambda)=-\frac{2\lambda}{(\lambda-1)^{2}},\quad\mathcal{B}_{3}(\lambda)=\frac{3\lambda(\lambda+1)}{(\lambda-1)^{3}},

\displaystyle\mathcal{B}_{4}(\lambda)=-\frac{4\lambda(\lambda^{2}+4\lambda+1)}{(\lambda-1)^{4}},\quad\mathcal{B}_{5}(\lambda)=\frac{5\lambda(\lambda^{3}+11\lambda^{2}+11\lambda+1)}{(\lambda-1)^{5}}\,.

But,

B_{0}=1,~B_{1}=-\frac{1}{2},~B_{2}=\frac{1}{6},~B_{3}=0,~B_{4}=-\frac{1}{30},~B_{5}=0,~B_{6}=\frac{1}{42},~\dots\,.

For
\lambda\neq 1
, Apostol Bernoulli polynomials
\mathcal{B}_{n}(x,\lambda)
can be expressed explicitly by

\mathcal{B}_{n}(x,\lambda)=\sum_{k=1}k\binom{n}{k}\sum_{j=0}^{k-1}(-1)^{j}\lambda^{j}(\lambda-1)^{-j-1}j!\left\{k-1\atop j\right\}x^{n-k}\quad(n\geq 0)

(4)

(
[
11
, Remark 2.6]
), where the Stirling numbers of the second kind
\left\{n\atop k\right\}
are given by

\left\{n\atop k\right\}=\frac{1}{k!}\sum_{j=0}^{k}(-1)^{k-j}\binom{k}{j}j^{n}\,.

When
x=0
in (
4
), Apostol-Bernoulli numbers
\mathcal{B}_{n}(\lambda)
have an explicit expression in terms of the Stirling numbers of the second kind (
[
1
, p.166, (3.7)]
,
[
11
, p.510, (3)]
)
1
1

1

In both references, the sum begins from
j=1
. However, the value for
n=1
does not match the correct one
\mathcal{B}_{1}(\lambda)=1/(\lambda-1)
.
.

\mathcal{B}_{n}(\lambda)=n\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}(-1)^{j}\lambda^{j}(\lambda-1)^{-j-1}j!\left\{n-1\atop j\right\}\quad(n\geq 0)

(5)

We use the similar approach by Rødseth in (
[
15
]
).
Let
n
,
r
and
s
be integers with

r\equiv n\pmod{a}\quad(0\leq r<a),\qquad bs\equiv r\pmod{a}\quad(0\leq s<a)\,.

Notice that

\displaystyle n\in{\rm NR}(a,b)

\displaystyle\Longleftrightarrow\exists t\in\mathbb{Z}~(1\leq t\leq\left\lfloor bs/a\right\rfloor),~n=-at+bs

\displaystyle\Longleftrightarrow\exists k\in\mathbb{Z}~(0\leq k\leq(bs-r)/a-1),~n=ak+r\,.

Note that the case
\lambda=1
is discussed in (
[
15
]
).
Since

S_{m}^{(\lambda)}(a,b)=\sum_{r=0}^{a-1}\sum_{k=0}^{\frac{bs-r}{a}-1}\lambda^{ak+r-1}(ak+r)^{m}\,,

for
\lambda\neq 1
, we have

\displaystyle\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}S_{m}^{(\lambda)}(a,b)\frac{z^{m}}{m!}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\lambda}\sum_{r=0}^{a-1}\sum_{k=0}^{\frac{bs-r}{a}-1}(\lambda e^{z})^{ak+r}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\lambda}\frac{1}{(\lambda e^{z})^{a}-1}\left(\sum_{r=0}^{a-1}(\lambda e^{z})^{bs}-\sum_{r=0}^{a-1}(\lambda e^{z})^{r}\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\lambda}\frac{1}{(\lambda e^{z})^{a}-1}\left(\sum_{s=0}^{a-1}(\lambda e^{z})^{bs}-\sum_{r=0}^{a-1}(\lambda e^{z})^{r}\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\lambda}\frac{az}{(\lambda e^{z})^{a}-1}\frac{bz}{(\lambda e^{z})^{b}-1}\frac{(\lambda e^{z})^{ab}-1}{abz^{2}}-\frac{1}{\lambda}\frac{1}{\lambda e^{z}-1}\,.

(6)

Assume that
\lambda^{a}\neq 1
and
\lambda^{b}\neq 1
.
The second term (without sign) of the right-hand side is equal to

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\lambda}\frac{1}{\lambda e^{z}-1}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\lambda z}\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\mathcal{B}_{m}(\lambda)\frac{z^{m}}{m!}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\lambda}\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\frac{\mathcal{B}_{m}(\lambda)}{m}\frac{z^{m-1}}{(m-1)!}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\lambda}\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\frac{\mathcal{B}_{m+1}(\lambda)}{m+1}\frac{z^{m}}{m!}\quad(\mathcal{B}_{0}(\lambda)=0)\,.

The first term is divided into two parts. One part (without sign) is given as

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\lambda}\frac{1}{abz^{2}}\frac{az}{(\lambda e^{z})^{a}-1}\frac{bz}{(\lambda e^{z})^{b}-1}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\lambda}\frac{1}{abz^{2}}\left(\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}\mathcal{B}_{i}(\lambda^{a})a^{i}\frac{z^{i}}{i!}\right)\left(\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\mathcal{B}_{j}(\lambda^{b})b^{i}\frac{z^{j}}{j!}\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\lambda}\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\sum_{i=0}^{m}\binom{m}{i}a^{i-1}b^{m-i-1}\mathcal{B}_{i}(\lambda^{a})\mathcal{B}_{m-i}(\lambda^{b})\frac{z^{m-2}}{m!}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\lambda}\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{(m+1)(m+2)}\sum_{i=0}^{m+2}\binom{m+2}{i}a^{i-1}b^{m-i+1}\mathcal{B}_{i}(\lambda^{a})\mathcal{B}_{m-i+2}(\lambda^{b})\frac{z^{m}}{m!}\,.

Another part is given as

\displaystyle\frac{\lambda^{ab-1}}{abz^{2}}\frac{az}{(\lambda e^{z})^{a}-1}\frac{bz}{(\lambda e^{z})^{b}-1}e^{abz}

\displaystyle=\lambda^{ab-1}\left(\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}a^{k}b^{k}\frac{z^{k}}{k!}\right)

\displaystyle\quad\times\left(\sum_{\ell=0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{(\ell+1)(\ell+2)}\sum_{i=0}^{\ell+2}\binom{\ell+2}{i}a^{i-1}b^{\ell-i+1}\mathcal{B}_{i}(\lambda^{a})\mathcal{B}_{\ell-i+2}(\lambda^{b})\frac{z^{\ell}}{\ell!}\right)

\displaystyle=\lambda^{ab-1}\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\sum_{\ell=0}^{m}\binom{m}{\ell}\frac{1}{(\ell+1)(\ell+2)}

\displaystyle\quad\times\sum_{i=0}^{\ell+2}\binom{\ell+2}{i}a^{m-\ell+i-1}b^{m-i+1}\mathcal{B}_{i}(\lambda^{a})\mathcal{B}_{\ell-i+2}(\lambda^{b})\frac{z^{m}}{m!}\,.

Comparing the coefficients on both sides of (
6
), we get the following expression.

Theorem 2
.

For
\lambda\neq 0
with
\lambda^{a}\neq 1
and
\lambda^{b}\neq 1

\displaystyle S_{m}^{(\lambda)}(a,b)

\displaystyle=\lambda^{ab-1}\sum_{\ell=0}^{m}\sum_{i=0}^{\ell+2}\binom{\ell+2}{i}\binom{m}{\ell}\frac{a^{m-\ell+i-1}b^{m-i+1}}{(\ell+1)(\ell+2)}\mathcal{B}_{i}(\lambda^{a})\mathcal{B}_{\ell-i+2}(\lambda^{b})

\displaystyle\quad-\frac{1}{(m+1)(m+2)\lambda}\sum_{i=0}^{m+2}\binom{m+2}{i}a^{i-1}b^{m-i+1}\mathcal{B}_{i}(\lambda^{a})\mathcal{B}_{m-i+2}(\lambda^{b})

\displaystyle\quad-\frac{\mathcal{B}_{m+1}(\lambda)}{(m+1)\lambda}\,.
