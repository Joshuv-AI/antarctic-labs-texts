Phase diagrams of superconducting topological surface states
W. H. Zhao, L. L. Ding, B. W. Zhou, J. Y. Wu, Y. Bai, Z. Y. Man, X. Luo

Abstract
In this paper, we present a detailed study on the phase diagrams of superconducting topological surface states, especially, focusing on the interplay between crystalline symmetry and topology of the effective BdG Hamiltonian. We show that for the 4 × 4 4\times 4 kinematic Hamiltonian of the normal state, a mirror symmetry ℳ \mathcal{M} can be defined, and for the ℳ \mathcal{M} -odd pairings, the classification of the 8 × 8 8\times 8 BdG Hamiltonian is ℤ ⊕ ℤ \mathbb{Z}\oplus\mathbb{Z} , and the time-reversal symmetry is broken intrinsically. The topological non-trivial phase can support chiral Majorana edge modes, and can be realized in the thin films of iron-based superconductor such as FeSeTe. Key words: symmetry, phase diagrams, topology, surface states

Model Hamiltonian

We choose the Nambu basis as
\Psi_{N}=(\psi_{1\uparrow\textbf{q}},\psi_{1\downarrow\textbf{q}},\psi_{2\uparrow\textbf{q}},\psi_{2\downarrow\textbf{q}},\psi^{{\dagger}}_{1\uparrow-\textbf{q}},\psi^{{\dagger}}_{1\downarrow-\textbf{q}},\psi^{{\dagger}}_{2\uparrow-\textbf{q}},\psi^{{\dagger}}_{2\downarrow-\textbf{q}})^{T}

\end{array}\right),

(1)

\displaystyle\tilde{h}_{0}(\textbf{q})_{4\times 4}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\textbf{q}\cdot{\bm{\sigma}}\chi_{z}-\mu\sigma_{0}\chi_{0}+t\sigma_{0}\chi_{x}+\lambda\sigma_{z}\chi_{0}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left(\begin{array}[]{cccc}\lambda-\mu&q_{1}-\mathrm{i}q_{2}&t&0\\
q_{1}+\mathrm{i}q_{2}&-\lambda-\mu&0&t\\

0&t&-q_{1}-\mathrm{i}q_{2}&-\lambda-\mu\\

\displaystyle\tilde{\Delta}_{4\times 4}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\mathrm{i}\Delta\sigma_{y}\chi_{z}-\mathrm{i}\Delta_{s}\sigma_{y}\chi_{x}-\mathrm{i}\Delta_{t}\sigma_{x}\chi_{y}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left(\begin{array}[]{cccc}0&\Delta&0&-\Delta_{s}-\Delta_{t}\\
-\Delta&0&\Delta_{s}-\Delta_{t}&0\\
0&-\Delta_{s}+\Delta_{t}&0&-\Delta\\
\Delta_{s}+\Delta_{t}&0&\Delta&0\\

Symmetry analysis

3.1
Particle-hole symmetry

The BdG Hamiltonian
(\ref{h})
has a particle-hole symmetry
\mathcal{C}h(\textbf{q})\mathcal{C}^{-1}=-h(-\textbf{q})
with
\mathcal{C}=\sigma_{0}\chi_{0}\tau_{x}\mathcal{K}

\tau_{i}
is the Pauli matrix that acts on the particle-hole space, and
\mathcal{K}

3.2
Inversion symmetry

For the normal state Hamiltonian
\tilde{h}_{0}(\textbf{q})_{4\times 4}
(
2

\mathcal{I}

\mathcal{I}\tilde{h}_{0}(\textbf{q})_{4\times 4}\mathcal{I}^{-1}=\tilde{h}_{0}(-\textbf{q})_{4\times 4}

\mathcal{I}
is

\mathcal{I}=\sigma_{0}\chi_{x}.

(14)

Under
\mathcal{I}

\mathcal{I}\tilde{\Delta}_{4\times 4}\mathcal{I}^{-1}=-\mathrm{i}\Delta\sigma_{y}\chi_{z}-\mathrm{i}\Delta_{s}\sigma_{y}\chi_{x}+\mathrm{i}\Delta_{t}\sigma_{x}\chi_{y},

(15)

namely,
\Delta
and
\Delta_{t}
terms are
\mathcal{I}
-odd while
\Delta_{s}
term is
\mathcal{I}
-even.

The inversion symmetry
\mathcal{I}
can be generalized to the BdG Hamiltonian (
1

\mathcal{P}_{\pm}=\left(\begin{array}[]{cccc}\mathcal{I}&0\\
0&\pm\mathcal{I}\\
\end{array}\right),

namely,
\mathcal{P}_{+}=\sigma_{0}\chi_{x}\tau_{0}

\mathcal{P}_{-}=\sigma_{0}\chi_{x}\tau_{z}

\{\mathcal{P}_{-},\mathcal{C}\}=0

7
Conclusion

In this paper, we present a detailed study on the topological superconductivity near the
\Gamma
point of the topological surface states on the top and bottom surfaces. Without external magnetic field, we find a novel mechanism for spontaneous time-reversal symmetry breaking in such systems. Because of the emergent mirror symmetry between the two topological Dirac surface states of the thin film of a topological insulator, there are only two momentum independent pairing terms favored by the mirror symmetry, namely, one is the intra-surface spin singlet pairing
\Delta
, and the other is the inter-surface spin triplet pairing
\Delta_{t}
. When both of the pairings and the inter-surface hopping are non-zero, the time reversal symmetry is broken spontaneously. Here, because of the mirror symmetry, the topological classification is
\mathbb{Z}\oplus\mathbb{Z}
. The non-trivial topological band structure of the BdG Hamiltonian with non-zero bulk Chern number provides stable chiral Majorana edge modes propagating along the edge, which may pave the way for realizing topological quantum computation based on Majorana fermions
[
35
]
. The models we discussed are realizable in the thin films of iron-based topological superconductors such as FeTeSe with reachable experimental techniques. The chiral Majorana modes could be detected by STM similar to the case of the Majorana zero modes
[
29
]
, or by the thermo-conductivity. We also discuss the effects of a relative phase between
\Delta
and
\Delta_{t}
. The difference in the intra-surface pairing on each surface breaks the mirror symmetry, while the imaginary part of
\Delta_{t}
preserves the mirror symmetry.
