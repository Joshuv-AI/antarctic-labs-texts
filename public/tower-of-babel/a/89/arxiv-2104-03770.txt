Comment on “Unconventional enhancement of ferromagnetic interactions in Cd-doped GdFe 2 Zn 20 single crystals studied by ESR and 57 Fe Mössbauer spectroscopies”
Paul C. Canfield 1,2 , Sergey L. Bud’ko 1,2 , Andriy Palasyuk 1 , and Tyler J. Slade 1,2

Abstract
In the recent publication, Phys. Rev. B 102 , 144420 (2020), Cabrera-Baez et al. present a study of the effects of Cd-substitution for Zn in the ferromagnetic compound GdFe 2 Zn 20 . As part of this paper they claim that for GdFe 2 Zn 18.6 Cd 1.4 the effective moment of Gd is reduced by 25% and the saturated moment of Gd is reduced by over 40%. We regrew representative members of the GdFe 2 Zn 20-x Cd x series and did not find any such reductions. In addition, we measured several crystals from the growth batch that was used by Cabrera-Baez et al. and did not see such reductions. Although there is a modest increase in T C T_{C} with Cd substitution, there is no significant change in the Gd effective moment or the saturated moment associated with the low temperature ferromagnetic state.
