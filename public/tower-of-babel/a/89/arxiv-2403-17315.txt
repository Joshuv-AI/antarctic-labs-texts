Arithmetic properties of multiplier polynomials for certain polynomial maps
Yuya Murakami, Kaoru Sano, Kohei Takehira

Abstract
. We investigate the arithmetic properties of the multiplier polynomials for certain 1 1 -parameter families of polynomials. In particular, we prove integrality theorems of multiplier polynomials for z d + c z^{d}+c , ( z − c ) ​ z d + c (z-c)z^{d}+c and z d + 1 + c ​ z z^{d+1}+cz . As a corollary, we obtain the uniform upper bound on the naive height of parabolic parameters of unicritical polynomials. Moreover, we determined the quadratic parabolic parameters for z 2 + c z^{2}+c . We also conditionally list parabolic parameters for z 2 + c z^{2}+c of fixed degrees.



1.
Introduction

For a polynomial
f
of degree
d
over an integral domain
\mathcal{O}
and a positive integer
k
,

f^{\circ k}
denotes the
k
-th iterated composition
f\circ f\circ\cdots\circ f
.
The
n
-th
dynatomic polynomial

\Phi_{n}^{\ast}(z)
(or
\Phi_{f,n}^{\ast}(z)
for clarity) of
f
is

(1.1)

\Phi_{n}^{\ast}(z)=\prod_{k|n}(f^{\circ k}(z)-z)^{\mu(n/k)},

where
\mu
is the Möbius function
(see
[
Sil07
, Theorem 4.5]
).

The degree of
\Phi_{n}^{\ast}(z)
is
d_{n}=\sum_{k|n}d^{k}\mu(\frac{n}{k})
.
For a root
\alpha\in\overline{\Frac(\mathcal{O})}
of
\Phi_{n}^{\ast}
, the multiplier
\omega_{n}(\alpha)
is the derivative
(f^{\circ n})^{\prime}(\alpha)
.
By the chain rule, it is written as

(1.2)

\omega_{n}(\alpha)=\prod_{i=0}^{n-1}f^{\prime}(f^{\circ i}(\alpha)),

where
f^{\circ 0}(z)
is regarded as
z
.
The
n
-th
multiplier polynomial

\delta_{n}(x)
of
f(z)=\sum_{i=0}^{d}a_{i}z^{i}\in\mathcal{O}[z]
is the monic polynomial in

satisfying

(1.3)

\delta_{n}(x)^{n}=\prod_{\Phi_{n}^{\ast}(\alpha)=0}(x-\omega_{n}(\alpha)),

where
\delta_{n}
is indeed an element of
\mathbb{Z}[a_{i}:0\leq i\leq d][x]
(cf
[
VH92
]
).
Let
\Phi^{\mathrm{cyc}}_{i}(x)
be the
i
-th cyclotomic polynomial.
For positive integers
m,n
such that
m\mid n
and
m<n
, the quantity
\Delta_{n,m}
is defined by

(1.4)

\Delta_{n,m}=\Resultant_{x}(\Phi^{\mathrm{cyc}}_{n/m}(x),\delta_{m}(x)).

In addition, we define the quantity
\Delta_{n,n}
by

(1.5)

\delta_{n}(1)=\Delta_{n,n}\prod_{m\mid n,m\neq n}\Delta_{n,m}.

In
[
MV95
, Section 3, Table 1]
, the quantity
\Delta_{n,m}
for

and

is listed.
Following the list, it is conjectured that
\Delta_{n,m}
for

is a monic integral polynomial in
4c
in
[

, Excercise 4.12 (c)**]
.
A generalization of this conjecture for unicritical polynomial maps is proved in Huguin’s thesis
[

]
.
Our first main theorem is to reprove this conjecture using purely algebraic techniques. In particular, the behavior near infinity was analyzed without relying on calculus, instead utilizing the Newton polygon.

Theorem 1.1
.

Let
f_{c}(z)=z^{d}+c
for an integer
d\geq 2
.
Then for all integers
n,m
with
m|n
and
m<n
,
there is a polynomial
\Psi_{n,m}(t)\in\mathbb{Z}[t]
such that

(1.6)

\Delta_{n,m}(c)=\Psi_{n,m}(d^{d}c^{d-1}).

Moreover, the polynomial
(-1)^{e_{n,m}}\Psi_{n,m}(t)
is monic, where
d_{m}=\sum_{k|m}d^{k}\mu\left(\frac{m}{k}\right)
,
\varphi(n)
is Euler’s totient function, and

(1.7)

\displaystyle e_{n,m}\coloneq\begin{cases}\varphi(n)\frac{d_{m}}{m}&\text{ if }n\neq m\\
\frac{d_{n}}{n}+\varphi(n)\sum_{m\mid n,m<n}\frac{d_{m}}{m}&\text{ if }n=m.\end{cases}

When
n>2

\varphi(n)
since

is even.

1.1

Theorem 1.2

(

Fundamental facts on Number fields

For a number field

-adic absolute value or the archimedean absolute value

For

\frac{1}{[L:K]}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}w\in M_{L}\\

(cf

)

Let

Let

\left\{a\in\overline{K}\ |\ [K(a):K]\leq D\text{ and }H(a)<B\right\}

Throughout this section, the set of critical points of a polynomial

Recall that the Multibrot set is defined by

The following fact might be well-known to experts, but we describe it for the reader’s convenience.

Lemma 5.1
