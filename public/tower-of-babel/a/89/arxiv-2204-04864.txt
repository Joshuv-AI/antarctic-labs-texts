Discrete Vector-Valued Nonuniform Gabor Frames
Lalit Kumar Vashisht, Hari Krishan Malhotra

Abstract
. Gabor frames have interested many mathematicians and physicists due to their potential applications in time-frequency analysis, in particular, signal processing. A Gabor system is a collection of vectors which is obtained by applying modulation and shift operators to non-zero functions in signal spaces. In many applications, for example, signal processing related to Gabor systems, the corresponding shifts may not be uniform. That is, the set associated with shifts may not be a group under usual addition. We analyze discrete vector-valued nonuniform Gabor frames (DVNUG frames, in short) in discrete vector-valued nonuniform signal spaces, where the indexing set associated with shifts may not be a subgroup of real numbers under usual addition, but a spectrum which is based on the theory of spectral pairs. First, we give necessary and sufficient conditions for the existence of DVNUG Bessel sequences in discrete vector-valued nonuniform signal spaces in terms of Fourier transformations of the modulated window sequences. We provide a characterization of DVNUG frames in discrete vector-valued nonuniform signal spaces. It is shown that DVNUG frames are stable under small perturbation of window sequences associated with given discrete vector-valued nonuniform Gabor systems. We observed that the arithmetic mean sequences associated with window sequences of a given DVNUG frame collectively constitutes a discrete nonuniform Gabor frame. Finally, we discuss an interplay between window sequences of DVNUG systems and their corresponding coordinates.

Technical Tools

This section provides basic notation and results about Bessel sequences, discrete frames and basic tools related with nonuniform signal spaces. We begin with the definition of a discrete Hilbert frame in separable Hilbert spaces.

2.1.
Discrete Hilbert Frames

Let
\mathcal{H}
be a real or complex separable, finite or infinite dimensional Hilbert space with respect to an inner product
\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle
. The norm induced by
\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle
is given by
||g||_{\mathcal{H}}=\sqrt{\langle g,g\rangle}
,
g\in\mathcal{H}
. A collection
\{g_{k}\}_{k\in\mathbb{I}}
of vectors in
\mathcal{H}
, where
\mathbb{I}
is a countable index set, is called a
discrete Hilbert frame
(or simply
discrete frame
) for
\mathcal{H}
if for some positive real constants
a_{0}
and
b_{0}
, the following holds

(2.1)

\displaystyle a_{o}\|g\|_{\mathcal{H}}^{2}\leq\sum_{k\in\mathbb{I}}|\langle g,g_{k}\rangle|^{2}\leq b_{o}\|g\|_{\mathcal{H}}^{2},\ g\in\mathcal{H}.

The constants
a_{0}
and
b_{0}
are called
lower
and
upper frame bounds
of
\{g_{k}\}_{k\in\mathbb{I}}
, respectively. If
a_{0}=b_{0}
, then
\{g_{k}\}_{k\in\mathbb{I}}
is called
tight discrete Hilbert frame
and if
a_{0}=b_{0}=1
then it is called
Parseval discrete Hilbert frame
for
\mathcal{H}
. If in (
2.1
) only right hand side inequality holds then
\{g\}_{k\in\mathbb{I}}
is called
Bessel sequence
with Bessel bound
b_{0}
. Associated with a Bessel sequence
\{g_{k}\}_{k\in\mathbb{I}}
, the map
\Theta:\mathcal{H}\to\mathcal{H}
given by
\Theta:g\to\sum_{k\in\mathbb{I}}\langle g,g_{k}\rangle g_{k}
is called
frame operator
of
\{g_{k}\}_{k\in\mathbb{I}}
. It is bounded and linear; and invertible on
\mathcal{H}
if
\{g_{k}\}_{k\in\mathbb{I}}
is a frame for
\mathcal{H}
. Thus, if
\{g_{k}\}_{k\in\mathbb{I}}
is a frame for
\mathcal{H}
, then each vector
g
of
\mathcal{H}
can be expressed as series, not necessarily unique,
g=\Theta\Theta^{-1}g=\sum_{k\in\mathbb{I}}\langle\Theta^{-1}g,g_{k}\rangle g_{k}
. This series expansion is useful in signal reconstruction and their analysis; and take over many areas of analysis for hot topic for research. Here, we would like to mention that frames are also used in, however this list in incomplete: sampling theory
[
1
]
, signal processing
[
3
,
11
,
12
]
, iterated function system
[
45
]
, quantum physics
[
29
]
, wavelet theory and multiresolution analysis
[
7
,
22
,
41
,
42
,
48
,
49
]
, also see many references therein.

The following basic results about Bessel sequence will be used in sequel.

Theorem 2.1
.

[
5
, p. 75]

A sequence
\{g_{k}\}_{k\in\mathbb{I}}
of vectors in a Hilbert space
\mathcal{H}
is a Bessel sequence with Bessel bound
b_{0}
if and only if the pre-frame operator
\mathcal{T}:\ell^{2}(\mathbb{I})\to\mathcal{H}
,

\mathcal{T}(\{a_{k}\}_{k\in\mathbb{I}})=\sum\limits_{k\in\mathbb{I}}a_{k}g_{k}

is bounded operator from
\ell^{2}(\mathbb{I})
into
\mathcal{H}
with
\|\mathcal{T}\|^{2}\leq b_{0}
.

Proposition 2.2
.

[
5
, p. 107]

Let
\{g_{k}\}_{k\in\mathbb{I}}
be a sequence of vectors in
\mathcal{H}
, such that
there exist a constant
b_{0}>0
satisfying

\displaystyle\Big\|\sum a_{k}g_{k}\Big\|_{\mathcal{H}}^{2}\leq b_{0}\sum|a_{k}|^{2},

for all finite sequences
\{a_{k}\}
, then the series
\sum_{k\in\mathbb{I}}a_{k}g_{k}
converges for every
\{a_{k}\}_{k\in\mathbb{I}}\in\ell^{2}(\mathbb{I})
, further
\{g_{k}\}_{k\in\mathbb{I}}
is a Bessel sequence with Bessel bound
b_{0}
.

Lemma 2.3
.

[
5
, p. 94]

For any
b>0
and any constant
c_{0}\in\mathbb{R}
,
\{\sqrt{b}e^{2\pi ibl(\xi+c_{0})}\}_{l\in\mathbb{Z}}
is a complete orthonormal system of
L^{2}(0,\frac{1}{b})
.

2.2.
Discrete Vector-valued Nonuniform Signal Spaces

In this section, we give necessary background for structure of discrete vector-valued nonuniform signal spaces and some related basic results. As is standard,
symbols
\mathbb{N},\mathbb{Z},\mathbb{R}
and
\mathbb{C}
signify, the set of natural numbers, integers, real numbers and complex numbers respectively. For
S\in\mathbb{N}
, the
S
-copies of
\mathbb{C}
denoted as
\mathbb{C}^{S}
is the complex euclidean space with norm
||\mathbb{X}||_{\mathbb{C}^{S}}=\sqrt{\sum\limits_{k=1}^{S}\big|[\mathbb{X}]_{k}\big|^{2}}
for
{\mathbb{X}}=\big(\big[{\mathbb{X}}\big]_{1},\big[{\mathbb{X}}\big]_{2},\dots,\big[{\mathbb{X}}\big]_{S}\big)\in\mathbb{C}^{S},
where
[{\mathbb{X}}]_{j}
denotes the
j^{t}h
element of vector
{\mathbb{X}}
. The arithmetic mean of vector
\mathbb{X}
, defined as
\text{$\mu$}_{\mathbb{X}}=\frac{1}{S}\sum\limits_{k=1}^{S}[\mathbb{X}]_{k}
, which is a scalar valued giving the average value of all the entries of the given vector. Let
N\in\mathbb{N}
and
r
be an odd integer coprime with
N
such that
1\leq r\leq 2N-1
, assume
\Lambda=\{0,\frac{r}{N}\}+2\mathbb{Z}
. The
discrete vector-valued nonuniform signal space
, denoted by
\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})
, is defined as

\displaystyle\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S}):=\Big\{\mathbb{Z}=\{\mathbb{Z}(\lambda)\}_{\lambda\in\Lambda}=\Bigg\{\begin{bmatrix}[\mathbb{Z}(\lambda)]_{1}\vskip 5.0pt\\
[\mathbb{Z}(\lambda)]_{2}\\
\vdots\vskip 5.0pt\\
[\mathbb{Z}(\lambda)]_{S}\end{bmatrix}\Bigg\}_{\lambda\in\Lambda}\subset\mathbb{C}^{S}:\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}\lambda\in\Lambda\\
k=1,2,\dots,S\end{subarray}}\big|\big[\mathbb{Z}(\lambda)\big]_{k}\big|^{2}<\infty\Big\}.

The space
\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})
is a Hilbert space with respect to the inner product:

\displaystyle\langle\mathbb{Z},\mathbb{W}\rangle

\displaystyle=\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}\lambda\in\Lambda\\
k=1,2,\dots,S\end{subarray}}[\mathbb{Z}(\lambda)]_{k}\overline{[\mathbb{W}(\lambda)]_{k}},\ \ \ \mathbb{Z},\,\mathbb{W}\in\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S}).

Its associated norm is given by

\displaystyle||\mathbb{Z}||_{\ell^{2}}

\displaystyle=\sqrt{\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}\lambda\in\Lambda\\
k=1,2,\dots,S\end{subarray}}\big|\big[\mathbb{Z}(\lambda)\big]_{k}\big|^{2}},\ \ \mathbb{Z}\,\in\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S}).

Note for
N=1
and
S=1
, this space becomes
\ell^{2}(\mathbb{Z},\mathbb{C})
, the standard discrete signal space. The arithmetic mean sequence of vector-valued nonuniform sequence
\mathbb{Z}\in\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})
is denoted as
\text{$\mu$}_{\mathbb{Z}}=\{\text{$\mu$}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\lambda)\}_{\lambda\in\Lambda}
, where

\displaystyle\text{$\mu$}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\lambda)=\frac{1}{S}\sum\limits_{k=1}^{S}[\mathbb{Z}(\lambda)]_{k},\,\,\lambda\in\Lambda.

Clearly,
\text{$\mu$}_{\mathbb{Z}}\in\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C})
. The sum of vector-valued sequence
\mathbb{Z}\in\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})
is defined as

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{\lambda\in\Lambda}\mathbb{Z}(\lambda)=\begin{bmatrix}\sum\limits_{\lambda\in\Lambda}\big[\mathbb{Z}(\lambda)\big]_{1}\vskip 5.0pt\\
\sum\limits_{\lambda\in\Lambda}\big[\mathbb{Z}(\lambda)\big]_{2}\\
\vdots\vskip 5.0pt\\
\sum\limits_{\lambda\in\Lambda}\big[\mathbb{Z}(\lambda)\big]_{S}\\
\end{bmatrix},

provided each coordinate series converges in
\mathbb{C}
. Now we consider some important operators related to our work. Let
\lambda\in\Lambda,M\in\mathbb{N}
be fixed,
m\in\{0,1,\dots,M-1\}
and
\mathbb{Z}\in\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})
, we have

(1)

Shift operator
:
R_{2N\lambda}:\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})\to\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})
,

\displaystyle R_{2N\lambda}\mathbb{Z}=\Bigg\{\begin{bmatrix}\big[\mathbb{Z}(\lambda^{\prime}-2N\lambda)\big]_{1}\vskip 5.0pt\\
\big[\mathbb{Z}(\lambda^{\prime}-2N\lambda)\big]_{2}\\
\vdots\vskip 5.0pt\\
\big[\mathbb{Z}(\lambda^{\prime}-2N\lambda)\big]_{S}\end{bmatrix}\Bigg\}_{\lambda^{\prime}\in\Lambda},\ \mathbb{Z}\in\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S}).

(2)

Modulation operator
:
E_{\frac{m}{M}}:\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})\to\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})
,

\displaystyle E_{\frac{m}{M}}\mathbb{Z}=\Bigg\{\begin{bmatrix}e^{2\pi i\frac{m}{M}\lambda^{\prime}}\big[\mathbb{Z}(\lambda^{\prime})\big]_{1}\vskip 5.0pt\\
e^{2\pi i\frac{m}{M}\lambda^{\prime}}\big[\mathbb{Z}(\lambda^{\prime})\big]_{2}\\
\vdots\vskip 5.0pt\\
e^{2\pi i\frac{m}{M}\lambda^{\prime}}\big[\mathbb{Z}(\lambda^{\prime})\big]_{S}\end{bmatrix}\Bigg\}_{\lambda^{\prime}\in\Lambda}.

For any Lebesgue measurable subset
G\subset\mathbb{R}
,
L^{2}(G)
denotes the space of all square integrable functions on
G
, which forms a Hilbert space with inner product
\langle f_{1},f_{2}\rangle=\int\limits_{G}f_{1}(\xi)\overline{f_{2}(\xi)}\,d\xi,\,\,f_{1},f_{2}\in L^{2}(G)
.

Gabardo and Nashed
[
16
]
considered the following definition in the study of nonuniform wavelets in the space
L^{2}(\mathbb{R})
.

Definition 2.4
.

[
16
]

Let
\Omega\subset\mathbb{R}
be measurable and
\Lambda\subset\mathbb{R}
a countable subset. If the collection
\{|\Omega|^{-\frac{1}{2}}e^{2\pi i\lambda\cdot}\chi_{\Omega}(\cdot)\}_{\lambda\in\Lambda}
forms complete orthonormal system for
L^{2}(\Omega)
, where
\chi_{\Omega}
is indicator function on
\Omega
and
|\Omega|
is Lebesgue measure of
\Omega
, then the pair

is a

.

Example 2.5
.

[
16
]

Let
N\in\mathbb{N}
,
r
be an odd integer coprime to
N
such that
1\leq r\leq 2N-1
, and let
\Lambda=\left\{0,\frac{r}{N}\right\}+2\mathbb{Z}
and
\Omega=[0,\frac{1}{2}[\cup[\frac{N}{2},\frac{N+1}{2}[
. Then,

is a spectral pair.

Now, we define the corresponding vector-valued nonuniform function space of a given discrete vector-valued nonuniform signal space
\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})
, the Fourier transformation and its elementary properties.
Let
\Lambda=\{0,\frac{r}{N}\}+2\mathbb{Z}
, where
N\in\mathbb{N}
,
r
is an odd integer coprime with
N
satisfying
1\leq r\leq 2N-1
and
\Omega=[0,\frac{1}{2}[\cup[\frac{N}{2},\frac{N+1}{2}[
. The vector-valued nonuniform function space
L^{2}(\Omega,\mathbb{C}^{S})
is defined as follows:

\displaystyle L^{2}(\Omega,\mathbb{C}^{S})=\Big\{\mathbb{F}=\mathbb{F}(\xi)=\begin{bmatrix}\big[\mathbb{F}(\xi)\big]_{1}\vskip 5.0pt\\
\big[\mathbb{F}(\xi)\big]_{2}\\
\vdots\vskip 5.0pt\\
\big[\mathbb{F}(\xi)\big]_{S}\end{bmatrix}:[\mathbb{F}(\xi)]_{k}:\Omega\to\mathbb{C},\int\limits_{\Omega}\big|\big[\mathbb{F}(\xi)\big]_{k}\big|^{2}<\infty,1\leq k\leq S\Big\}.

The space
L^{2}(\Omega,\mathbb{C}^{S})
is a Hilbert space with respect to the inner product defined by

\displaystyle\langle\mathbb{F},\mathbb{G}\rangle

\displaystyle=\sum\limits_{k=1}^{S}\int\limits_{\Omega}\big[\mathbb{F}(\xi)\big]_{k}\overline{\big[\mathbb{G}(\xi)\big]_{k}}\,d\xi,\ \ \mathbb{F},\mathbb{G}\in L^{2}(\Omega,\mathbb{C}^{S}).

This inner product induces the following norm:

\displaystyle||\mathbb{F}||_{L^{2}}

\displaystyle=\sqrt{\sum\limits_{k=1}^{S}\int\limits_{\Omega}\Big|\big[\mathbb{F}(\xi)\big]_{k}\Big|^{2}},\ \mathbb{F}\in L^{2}(\Omega,\mathbb{C}^{S}).

The map
\mathcal{F}:\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})\to L^{2}(\Omega,\mathbb{C}^{S})
defined by

\displaystyle\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{Z})=\begin{bmatrix}\sum\limits_{\lambda\in\Lambda}\big[\mathbb{Z}(\lambda)\big]_{1}\,e^{2\pi i\lambda\xi}\vskip 5.0pt\\
\sum\limits_{\lambda\in\Lambda}\big[\mathbb{Z}(\lambda)\big]_{2}\,e^{2\pi i\lambda\xi}\\
\vdots\vskip 5.0pt\\
\sum\limits_{\lambda\in\Lambda}\big[\mathbb{Z}(\lambda)\big]_{S}\,e^{2\pi i\lambda\xi}\end{bmatrix}

is called the

of

. The Fourier transform

is well defined, since

is a spectral pair by Example

, so each coordinate series will converge in

. Further

is bijective and its inverse, that is, inverse Fourier transform
\mathcal{F}^{-1}:L^{2}(\Omega,\mathbb{C}^{S})\to\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})
is given by

\displaystyle\mathcal{F}^{-1}(\mathbb{F})=\begin{bmatrix}\int\limits_{\Omega}\big[\mathbb{F}(\xi)\big]_{1}\,e^{-2\pi i\lambda\xi}\,d\xi\vskip 5.0pt\\
\int\limits_{\Omega}\big[\mathbb{F}(\xi)\big]_{2}\,e^{-2\pi i\lambda\xi}\,d\xi\\
\vdots\vskip 5.0pt\\
\int\limits_{\Omega}\big[\mathbb{F}(\xi)\big]_{S}\,e^{-2\pi i\lambda\xi}\,d\xi\end{bmatrix}.

Remark 2.6
.

It can be easily observed that
2N\Lambda\subset 2\mathbb{Z}\subset\Lambda
and
\Lambda+2N\lambda=\Lambda
for any

Now we give some identities related to the Fourier transform. Let

and

. Then,

(1)

\mathcal{F}(R_{2N\lambda}\mathbb{Z})(\xi)=e^{4\pi iN\lambda\xi}\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{Z})(\xi).

(2)

\langle\mathbb{Z},\mathbb{W}\rangle_{\ell^{2}}=\langle\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{Z}),\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{W})\rangle_{L^{2}}
(

).

(3)

||\mathbb{Z}||_{\ell^{2}}=||\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{Z})||_{L^{2}}
(

).

(4)

\mathcal{F}\big(E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}\mathbb{W}\big)(\xi)=e^{4\pi iN\lambda(\frac{m}{M}+\xi)}\mathcal{F}\big(E_{\frac{m}{M}}\mathbb{W}\big)(\xi)
.

3.
Frames of Discrete Vector-valued Nonuniform Gabor Systems

This section deals with discrete vector-valued nonuniform Gabor frames in discrete vector-valued nonuniform signal spaces.
We begin with the definition of a discrete vector-valued nonuniform Gabor system in
\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})
.

Definition 3.1
.

Let
N,M,S\in\mathbb{N},P\in\mathbb{N}\cup\{0\}
and
r
be an odd integer coprime with
N
such that
1\leq r\leq 2N-1
. Let
\Lambda=\left\{0,\frac{r}{N}\right\}+2\mathbb{Z}
,
T_{1}=\{0,1,\dots,M-1\},T_{2}=\{0,1,\dots,P\}
and
\{\mathbb{W}_{j}\}_{j\in T_{2}}\subset\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})
. A collection of sequences of the form

(3.1)

\displaystyle\mathcal{G}(\Lambda,T_{1},T_{2},\mathbb{W}_{j}):=\big\{E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}\mathbb{W}_{j}:\lambda\in\Lambda,m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\big\}

is called a
discrete vector-valued nonuniform Gabor system
(DVNUG system, in short) for discrete vector-valued nonuniform signal space
\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})
; and
\mathbb{W}_{j}

(j\in T_{2})
are called
window sequences
.

Definition 3.2
.

The collection of sequences
\mathcal{G}(\Lambda,T_{1},T_{2},\mathbb{W}_{j})
, as defined in (
3.1
), is called a
discrete vector valued nonuniform Gabor frame
(DVNUG frame, in short) for
\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})
, if there exist constants
0<\alpha\leq\beta<\infty
such that

(3.2)

\displaystyle\alpha||\mathbb{Z}||_{\ell^{2}}^{2}\leq\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\Big|\langle\mathbb{Z},E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}\mathbb{W}_{j}\rangle\Big|^{2}\leq\beta||\mathbb{Z}||_{\ell^{2}}^{2}\,\,\text{for all}\,\,\mathbb{Z}\in\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S}).

The scalars
\alpha
and
\beta
, respectively, known as l
ower frame bound
and
upper frame bound
. If only the right-hand side inequality in (
3.2
) holds, then we say that
\mathcal{G}(\Lambda,T_{1},T_{2},\mathbb{W}_{j})
is a
DVNUG Bessel sequence
with Bessel bound
\beta
.

Before giving the operators associated with DVNUG frames for discrete vector-valued nonuniform signal space, we consider the space

\displaystyle\ell^{2}(\Lambda,T_{1},T_{2},\mathbb{C}):=\Big\{\{a_{\lambda,m,j}\}_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\subset\mathbb{C}:\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}|a_{\lambda,m,j}|^{2}<\infty\Big\}.

This space is a Hilbert space with respect to the following inner product.

(3.3)

\displaystyle\Big\langle\{a_{\lambda,m,j}\}_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}},\{b_{\lambda,m,j}\}_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\Big\rangle

\displaystyle=\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}a_{\lambda,m,j}\overline{b_{\lambda,m,j}},

for
\{a_{\lambda,m,j}\}_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}
,
\{b_{\lambda,m,j}\}_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\in\ell^{2}(\Lambda,T_{1},T_{2},\mathbb{C})
.
The norm associated with the inner product given in (
3.3
) is given by the following formula:

\displaystyle||\{a_{\lambda,m,j}\}_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}||_{\ell^{2}}

\displaystyle=\sqrt{\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}|a_{\lambda,m,j}|^{2}},\ \ \ \{a_{\lambda,m,j}\}_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\in\ell^{2}(\Lambda,T_{1},T_{2},\mathbb{C}).

If
\mathcal{G}(\Lambda,T_{1},T_{2},\mathbb{W}_{j})
is a discrete vector valued nonuniform Bessel sequence with Bessel bound
\beta
, then the map
\mathcal{T}:\ell^{2}(\Lambda,T_{1},T_{2},\mathbb{C})\to\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})
defined by

\displaystyle\mathcal{T}\Big(\{a_{\lambda,m,j}\}_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\Big)=\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}a_{\lambda,m,j}E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}\mathbb{W}_{j},\ \ \{a_{\lambda,m,j}\}_{\lambda\in\Lambda,\,j\in S}\in\ell^{2}(\Lambda,T_{1},T_{2},\mathbb{C})

is called the
pre-frame operator
of
\mathcal{G}(\Lambda,T_{1},T_{2},\mathbb{W}_{j})
. The pre-frame operator
\mathcal{T}
is linear and bounded with
||\mathcal{T}||\leq\sqrt{\beta}
. The Hilbert-adjoint operator of
\mathcal{T}
is
\mathcal{T}^{*}:\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})\to\ell^{2}(\Lambda,T_{1},T_{2},\mathbb{C})
is called the
analysis operator
, is given by
\mathcal{T}^{*}(\mathbb{Z})=\{\langle\mathbb{Z},E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}\mathbb{W}_{j}\rangle\}_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}
,
\mathbb{Z}\in\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})
. The composition
\Xi=\mathcal{T}\mathcal{T}^{*}:\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})\to\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})
given by

\displaystyle\Xi(\mathbb{Z})=\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\langle\mathbb{Z},E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}\mathbb{W}_{j}\rangle E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}\mathbb{W}_{j},\,\,\mathbb{Z}\in\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S}),

is called the
frame operator
of
\mathcal{G}(\Lambda,T_{1},T_{2},\mathbb{W}_{j})
. The frame operator
\Xi
is bounded and linear; if
\mathcal{G}(\Lambda,T_{1},T_{2},\mathbb{W}_{j})
is a DVNUG frame for
\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})
, then
\Xi
is invertible on
\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})
.

Throughout the entire paper, unless otherwise stated, we assume
N
,
M
,
S\in\mathbb{N}
,
P\in\mathbb{N}\cup\{0\}
and
r
be an odd integer coprime with
N
such that
1\leq r\leq 2N-1
,
\Lambda=\left\{0,\frac{r}{N}\right\}+2\mathbb{Z},\,\Omega=[0,\frac{1}{2}[\cup[\frac{N}{2},\frac{N+1}{2}[
,
T_{1}=\{0,1,\dots,M-1\}
,
T_{2}=\{0,1,\dots,P\}
and
\mathcal{G}(\Lambda,T_{1},T_{2},\mathbb{W}_{j})=\big\{E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}\mathbb{W}_{j}:\lambda\in\Lambda,m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\big\}
, where
\mathbb{W}_{j}\in\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})
.

The following result provides a sufficient condition for the existence of DVNUG Bessel sequences in discrete vector-valued nonuniform signal spaces, with explicit Bessel bounds, in terms of Fourier transformations of the modulated window sequences.

Theorem 3.3
.

Let
\{\mathbb{W}_{j}\}_{j\in T_{2}}\subset\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})
. For
m\in T_{1}
,
j\in T_{2}
and a.e.
\xi\in\Omega
, let

\displaystyle\Big|\Big|\mathcal{F}(E_{\frac{m}{M}}\mathbb{W}_{j})(\xi)\Big|\Big|_{\mathbb{C}^{S}}\leq B_{0}<\infty,

for some positive constant
B_{0}
. Then,
\mathcal{G}(\Lambda,T_{1},T_{2},\mathbb{W}_{j})
is a DVNUG Bessel sequence with Bessel bound
2^{(M+P)}B_{0}^{2}S
.

Proof.

Let
\{a_{\lambda,m,j}\}
be any finite sequence in
\ell^{2}(\Lambda,T_{1},T_{2},\mathbb{C})
. Then, we have

\displaystyle\Big|\Big|\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}a_{\lambda,m,j}E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}\mathbb{W}_{j}\Big|\Big|_{\ell^{2}}

\displaystyle=\Big|\Big|\mathcal{F}\Big(\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}a_{\lambda,m,j}E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}\mathbb{W}_{j}\Big)\Big|\Big|_{L^{2}}

\displaystyle\leq\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1}\\
j\in T_{2}\end{subarray}}\Big|\Big|\sum\limits_{\lambda\in\Lambda}a_{\lambda,m,j}e^{4\pi iN\lambda(\frac{m}{M}+\xi)}\mathcal{F}(E_{\frac{m}{M}}\mathbb{W}_{j})(\xi)\Big|\Big|_{L^{2}}

\displaystyle=\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1}\\
j\in T_{2}\end{subarray}}\sqrt{\sum\limits_{k=1}^{S}\int\limits_{\Omega}\Big|\sum\limits_{\lambda\in\Lambda}a_{\lambda,m,j}e^{4\pi iN\lambda(\frac{m}{M}+\xi)}\Big[\mathcal{F}(E_{\frac{m}{M}}\mathbb{W}_{j})(\xi)\Big]_{k}\Big|^{2}d\,\xi}

(3.4)

\displaystyle=B_{0}\sqrt{S}\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1}\\
j\in T_{2}\end{subarray}}\sqrt{\int\limits_{\Omega}\Big|\sum\limits_{\lambda\in\Lambda}a_{\lambda,m,j}e^{4\pi iN\lambda(\frac{m}{M}+\xi)}\Big|^{2}d\,\xi}.

Using (
3
) and the fact
\{e^{4\pi iN\lambda(\frac{m}{M}+\xi)}\}_{\lambda\in\Lambda}
is orthonormal system in
L^{2}(\Omega)
, we have

(3.5)

\displaystyle\Big|\Big|\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}a_{\lambda,m,j}E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}\mathbb{W}_{j}\Big|\Big|_{\ell^{2}}

\displaystyle\leq B_{0}\sqrt{S}\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1}\\
j\in T_{2}\end{subarray}}\sqrt{\sum\limits_{\lambda\in\Lambda}|a_{\lambda,m,j}|^{2}}.

Therefore by (
3.5
) and Lemma
2.7
, we get

\displaystyle\Big|\Big|\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}a_{\lambda,m,j}E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}\mathbb{W}_{j}\Big|\Big|^{2}_{\ell^{2}}

\displaystyle\leq\Bigg(B_{0}\sqrt{S}\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1}\\
j\in T_{2}\end{subarray}}\sqrt{\sum\limits_{\lambda\in\Lambda}|a_{\lambda,m,j}|^{2}}\Bigg)^{2}

(3.6)

\displaystyle\leq 2^{(M+P)}B_{0}^{2}S\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1}\\
j\in T_{2}\end{subarray}}\sum\limits_{\lambda\in\Lambda}|a_{\lambda,m,j}|^{2}.

By invoking Proposition
2.2
and Ineq. (
3
), we conclude that
\mathcal{G}(\Lambda,T_{1},T_{2},\mathbb{W}_{j})
is a Bessel sequence with the desired Bessel bound.
∎

The next result gives a necessary conditions for DVNUG Bessel sequence in discrete vector-valued nonuniform signal spaces.

Theorem 3.5
.

Let
\{\mathbb{W}_{j}\}_{j\in T_{2}}\subset\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})
be such that
\mathcal{G}(\Lambda,T_{1},T_{2},\mathbb{W}_{j})
is a DVNUG Bessel sequence with Bessel bound
B_{0}
for
\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})
. Then, for
m\in T_{1}
,
j\in T_{2}
and
a.e.\,\xi\in\Omega
, we have,

\displaystyle\Big|\Big|\mathcal{F}(E_{\frac{m}{M}}\mathbb{W}_{j})(\xi)\Big|\Big|_{\mathbb{C}^{S}}\leq 2\sqrt{NB_{0}}.

Proof.

Let
m_{0}\in T_{1}
,
j_{0}\in T_{2}
be arbitrarily fixed elements. Let
f(\xi)
be an any element of
L^{2}(0,\frac{1}{4N})
. Then, by Lemma
2.3
,
\{\sqrt{4N}e^{2\pi i(4N)l(\xi+\frac{m_{0}}{M})}\}_{l\in\mathbb{Z}}
is a complete orthonormal system of
L^{2}(0,\frac{1}{4N})
, so there exists sequence
c_{l}
such that

(3.7)

\displaystyle f(\xi)=\sum\limits_{l\in\mathbb{Z}}c_{l}\sqrt{4N}e^{2\pi i(4N)l(\xi+\frac{m_{0}}{M})},

satisfying
\int\limits_{0}^{\frac{1}{4N}}|f(\xi)|^{2}\,d\xi=\sum\limits_{l\in\mathbb{Z}}|c_{l}|^{2}<\infty
.

Define sequence
\{a_{\lambda,m,j}\}_{{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}}
as follows:

(3.8)

\displaystyle a_{\lambda,m,j}=\begin{cases}c_{l},&\text{if}\,\,\lambda=2l,\ l\in\mathbb{Z},m=m_{0},j=j_{0};\\
0,&\text{otherwise}.\end{cases}

Obviously
\{a_{\lambda,m,j}\}_{{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}}\in\ell^{2}(\Lambda,T_{1},T_{2},\mathbb{C})
.
Since,
\mathcal{G}(\Lambda,T_{1},T_{2},\mathbb{W}_{j})
is a Bessel sequence with Bessel bound
B_{0}
, so by Theorem
2.1
, the associated pre-frame operator
\mathcal{T}:\ell^{2}(\Lambda,T_{1},T_{2},\mathbb{C})\to\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})
is bounded and satisfies

(3.9)

\displaystyle||T\{a_{\lambda,m,j}\}||_{\ell^{2}}^{2}\leq B_{0}\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}|a_{\lambda,m,j}|^{2}.

Using (
3.8
), we compute

\displaystyle||T\{a_{\lambda,m,j}\}||_{\ell^{2}}^{2}

\displaystyle=\Big|\Big|\sum\limits_{l\in\mathbb{Z}}c_{l}E_{\frac{m_{0}}{M}}R_{4Nl}\mathbb{W}_{j_{0}}\Big|\Big|_{\ell^{2}}^{2}

\displaystyle=\Big|\Big|\mathcal{F}\Big(\sum\limits_{l\in\mathbb{Z}}c_{l}E_{\frac{m_{0}}{M}}R_{4Nl}\mathbb{W}_{j_{0}}\Big)\Big|\Big|_{L^{2}}^{2}

\displaystyle=\Big|\Big|\sum\limits_{l\in\mathbb{Z}}c_{l}e^{4\pi iN(2l)(\frac{m_{0}}{M}+\xi)}\mathcal{F}(E_{\frac{m_{0}}{M}}\mathbb{W}_{j_{0}})(\xi)\Big|\Big|_{L^{2}}^{2}

\displaystyle=\int\limits_{\Omega}\sum\limits_{k=1}^{S}\Big|\sum\limits_{l\in\mathbb{Z}}c_{l}e^{4\pi iN(2l)(\frac{m_{0}}{M}+\xi)}\Big[\mathcal{F}(E_{\frac{m_{0}}{M}}\mathbb{W}_{j_{0}})(\xi)\Big]_{k}\Big|^{2}\,d\xi

\displaystyle=\sum\limits_{k=1}^{S}\int\limits_{0}^{\frac{1}{2}}\Big|\sum\limits_{l\in\mathbb{Z}}c_{l}e^{4\pi iN(2l)(\frac{m_{0}}{M}+\xi)}\Big|^{2}\Bigg(\Big|\Big[\mathcal{F}(E_{\frac{m_{0}}{M}}\mathbb{W}_{j_{0}})(\xi)\Big]_{k}\Big|^{2}+\Big|\Big[\mathcal{F}(E_{\frac{m_{0}}{M}}\mathbb{W}_{j_{0}})\big(\xi+\frac{N}{2}\big)\Big]_{k}\Big|^{2}\Bigg)\,d\xi

\displaystyle=\sum\limits_{k=1}^{S}\sum\limits_{t=0}^{2N-1}\int\limits_{0}^{\frac{1}{4N}}\Big|\sum\limits_{l\in\mathbb{Z}}c_{l}e^{4\pi iN(2l)(\frac{m_{0}}{M}+\xi)}\Big|^{2}\Bigg(\Big|\Big[\mathcal{F}(E_{\frac{m_{0}}{M}}\mathbb{W}_{j_{0}})(\xi+\frac{t}{4N})\Big]_{k}\Big|^{2}

(3.10)

\displaystyle\quad+\Big|\Big[\mathcal{F}(E_{\frac{m_{0}}{M}}\mathbb{W}_{j_{0}})\Big(\xi+\frac{N}{2}+\frac{t}{4N}\Big)\Big]_{k}\Big|^{2}\Bigg)\,d\xi.

Using (
3.7
), (
3.8
), (
3.9
) and (
3
), we have

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{k=1}^{S}\sum\limits_{t=0}^{2N-1}\int\limits_{0}^{\frac{1}{4N}}|f(\xi)|^{2}\Bigg(\Big|\Big[\mathcal{F}(E_{\frac{m_{0}}{M}}\mathbb{W}_{j_{0}})(\xi+\frac{t}{4N})\Big]_{k}\Big|^{2}+\Big|\Big[\mathcal{F}(E_{\frac{m_{0}}{M}}\mathbb{W}_{j_{0}})\Big(\xi+\frac{N}{2}+\frac{t}{4N}\Big)\Big]_{k}\Big|^{2}\Bigg)\,d\xi

\displaystyle\leq 4NB_{0}\int\limits_{0}^{\frac{1}{4N}}|f(\xi)|^{2}\,d\xi.

As
f(\xi)
is an arbitrary element of
L^{2}(0,\frac{1}{4N})
, we conclude that for
a.e.\,\xi\in[0,\frac{1}{4N}[
,

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{k=1}^{S}\sum\limits_{t=0}^{2N-1}\Big|\Big[\mathcal{F}(E_{\frac{m_{0}}{M}}\mathbb{W}_{j_{0}})(\xi+\frac{t}{4N})\Big]_{k}\Big|^{2}

\displaystyle+\Big|\Big[\mathcal{F}(E_{\frac{m_{0}}{M}}\mathbb{W}_{j_{0}})\Big(\xi+\frac{N}{2}+\frac{t}{4N}\Big)\Big]_{k}\Big|^{2}\leq 4NB_{0},

which entails

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{k=1}^{S}\Big|\Big[\mathcal{F}(E_{\frac{m}{M}}\mathbb{W}_{j})(\xi)\Big]_{k}\Big|^{2}\leq 4NB_{0}\,\,\,\,\text{for}\,\,a.e.\,\xi\in\Omega,m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}.

This concludes the proof. ∎

.

.

,

0\end{bmatrix}\ \quad\text{and}\ \quad\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{W}_{1})=\begin{bmatrix}0\vskip 5.0pt\\
\sqrt{2N}\chi_{[0,\frac{1}{4N}[}(\xi)\end{bmatrix}.

, so it can be easily checked, for a.e.

(3.13)

2.3
and (
3.7

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\Big|\langle\mathbb{Z},E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}\mathbb{W}_{j}\rangle\Big|^{2}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2}\Bigg(\int\limits_{0}^{\frac{1}{4N}}\big|[\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{Z})(\xi)]_{1}\big|^{2}d\xi+\int\limits_{0}^{\frac{1}{4N}}\big|[\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{Z})(\xi)]_{1}\big|^{2}d\xi+\int\limits_{0}^{\frac{1}{4N}}\big|[\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{Z})(\xi)]_{2}\big|^{2}d\xi+\int\limits_{0}^{\frac{1}{4N}}\big|[\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{Z})(\xi)]_{2}\big|^{2}d\xi\Bigg)

(3.14)

Now, if we choose

The following lemma plays a crucial role in characterization of DVNUG frames for discrete vector-valued nonuniform signal spaces.

Lemma 3.8
.

Assume
\{\mathbb{W}_{j}\}_{j\in T_{2}}\subset\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})
. For
m\in T_{1}
,
j\in T_{2}
,
k\in\{1,2,\dots,S\}
and
\xi\in[0,\frac{1}{4N}[
, define matrices

\displaystyle{\mathcal{A}}_{j,m,k}(\xi),\quad{\mathcal{B}}_{j,m,k}(\xi),\quad\text{and}\quad\ {\mathcal{M}}_{m,k}(\xi),

respectively, as follows.

\displaystyle{\mathcal{A}}_{j,m,k}(\xi)=\tiny{\begin{bmatrix}\Big[\mathcal{F}(E_{\frac{m}{M}}\mathbb{W}_{j})(\xi)\Big]_{k}\vskip 5.0pt\\
\Big[\mathcal{F}(E_{\frac{m}{M}}\mathbb{W}_{j})(\xi+\frac{1}{4N})\Big]_{k}\\
\vdots\vskip 5.0pt\\
\Big[\mathcal{F}(E_{\frac{m}{M}}\mathbb{W}_{j})(\xi+\frac{2N-1}{4N})\Big]_{k}\vskip 5.0pt\\
\Big[\mathcal{F}(E_{\frac{m}{M}}\mathbb{W}_{j})(\xi+\frac{N}{2})\Big]_{k}\vskip 5.0pt\\
\Big[\mathcal{F}(E_{\frac{m}{M}}\mathbb{W}_{j})(\xi+\frac{N}{2}+\frac{1}{4N})\Big]_{k}\\
\vdots\vskip 5.0pt\\
\Big[\mathcal{F}(E_{\frac{m}{M}}\mathbb{W}_{j})(\xi+\frac{N}{2}+\frac{2N-1}{4N})\Big]_{k}\end{bmatrix}_{4N\times 1}},\,\,\mathcal{B}_{j,m,k}(\xi)=\tiny{\begin{bmatrix}e^{4\pi ir\xi}\Big[\mathcal{F}(E_{\frac{m}{M}}\mathbb{W}_{j})(\xi)\Big]_{k}\vskip 5.0pt\\
e^{4\pi ir(\xi+\frac{1}{4N})}\Big[\mathcal{F}(E_{\frac{m}{M}}\mathbb{W}_{j})(\xi+\frac{1}{4N})\Big]_{k}\\
\vdots\vskip 5.0pt\\
e^{4\pi ir(\xi+\frac{2N-1}{4N})}\Big[\mathcal{F}(E_{\frac{m}{M}}\mathbb{W}_{j})(\xi+\frac{2N-1}{4N})\Big]_{k}\vskip 5.0pt\\
e^{4\pi ir\xi}\Big[\mathcal{F}(E_{\frac{m}{M}}\mathbb{W}_{j})(\xi+\frac{N}{2})\Big]_{k}\vskip 5.0pt\\
e^{4\pi ir(\xi+\frac{1}{4N})}\Big[\mathcal{F}(E_{\frac{m}{M}}\mathbb{W}_{j})(\xi+\frac{N}{2}+\frac{1}{4N})\Big]_{k}\\
\vdots\vskip 5.0pt\\
e^{4\pi ir(\xi+\frac{2N-1}{4N})}\Big[\mathcal{F}(E_{\frac{m}{M}}\mathbb{W}_{j})(\xi+\frac{N}{2}+\frac{2N-1}{4N})\Big]_{k}\end{bmatrix}_{4N\times 1}}

and,

(3.16)

\displaystyle\mathcal{M}_{m,k}(\xi)=\begin{bmatrix}\mathcal{A}_{0,m,k}(\xi)&\mathcal{B}_{0,m,k}(\xi)&\mathcal{A}_{1,m,k}(\xi)&\mathcal{B}_{1,m,k}(\xi)\cdots&\mathcal{A}_{P,m,k}(\xi)&\mathcal{B}_{P,m,k}(\xi)\end{bmatrix}_{4N\times 2(P+1)}.

For
m\in T_{1}
,
j\in T_{2}
and a.e.
\xi\in\Omega
, assume that

(3.17)

\displaystyle\Big|\Big|\mathcal{F}(E_{\frac{m}{M}}\mathbb{W}_{j})(\xi)\Big|\Big|_{\mathbb{C}^{S}}\leq B_{0}<\infty,

for some constant
B_{0}
. Then, for any
\mathbb{Z}\in\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})
, we have

\displaystyle 4N\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\Big|\langle\mathbb{Z},E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}\mathbb{W}_{j}\rangle\Big|^{2}=\sum\limits_{m\in T_{1}}\int\limits_{0}^{\frac{1}{4N}}\Big|\Big|\sum\limits_{k=1}^{S}\mathcal{M}_{m,k}^{*}(\xi)\mathcal{V}_{\mathbb{Z}_{k}}(\xi)\Big|\Big|_{\mathbb{C}^{2(P+1)}}^{2}\,d\xi,

where
\mathcal{M}_{m,k}^{*}(\xi)
is conjugate transpose of matrix
\mathcal{M}_{m,k}(\xi)
and

(3.18)

\displaystyle\mathcal{V}_{\mathbb{Z}_{k}}(\xi)=\begin{bmatrix}\big[\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{Z})(\xi)\big]_{k}\vskip 5.0pt\\
\big[\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{Z})(\xi+\frac{1}{4N})\big]_{k}\\
\vdots\vskip 5.0pt\\
\big[\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{Z})(\xi+\frac{2N-1}{4N})\big]_{k}\vskip 5.0pt\\
\big[\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{Z})(\xi+\frac{N}{2})\big]_{k}\vskip 5.0pt\\
\big[\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{Z})(\xi+\frac{N}{2}+\frac{1}{4N})\big]_{k}\\
\vdots\vskip 5.0pt\\
\big[\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{Z})(\xi+\frac{N}{2}+\frac{2N-1}{4N})\big]_{k}\\
\end{bmatrix}_{4N\times 1},\,\,\,\text{for}\,\,k\in\{1,2,\dots,S\}.

Proof.

Let
\mathbb{Z}\in\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})
be arbitrary. For
m\in T_{1}
,
j\in T_{2}
and
\xi\in[0,\frac{1}{2}[
, define

(3.19)

\displaystyle X_{m,j}(\xi)=\sum\limits_{k=1}^{S}\Bigg(\big[\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{Z}(\xi))\big]_{k}\overline{\big[\mathcal{F}(E_{\frac{m}{M}}\mathbb{W}_{j})(\xi)\big]_{k}}+\big[\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{Z}(\xi+\frac{N}{2}))\big]_{k}\overline{\big[\mathcal{F}(E_{\frac{m}{M}}\mathbb{W}_{j})(\xi+\frac{N}{2})\big]_{k}}\Bigg).

Then, for
\xi\in[0,\frac{1}{4N}[
,
m\in T_{1}
, it is easy to see that

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{k=1}^{S}\mathcal{M}_{m,k}^{*}(\xi)\mathcal{V}_{\mathbb{Z}_{k}}(\xi)=\begin{bmatrix}\sum\limits_{t=0}^{2N-1}X_{m,0}(\xi+\frac{t}{4N})\vskip 5.0pt\\
\sum\limits_{t=0}^{2N-1}e^{-4\pi ir(\xi+\frac{t}{4N})}X_{m,0}(\xi+\frac{t}{4N})\vskip 5.0pt\\
\sum\limits_{t=0}^{2N-1}X_{m,1}(\xi+\frac{t}{4N})\vskip 5.0pt\\
\sum\limits_{t=0}^{2N-1}e^{-4\pi ir(\xi+\frac{t}{4N})}X_{m,1}(\xi+\frac{t}{4N})\\
\vdots\vskip 5.0pt\\
\sum\limits_{t=0}^{2N-1}X_{m,P}(\xi+\frac{t}{4N})\vskip 5.0pt\\
\sum\limits_{t=0}^{2N-1}e^{-4\pi ir(\xi+\frac{t}{4N})}X_{m,P}(\xi+\frac{t}{4N})\end{bmatrix}_{2(P+1)\times 1}.

Also,

(3.20)

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{m\in T_{1}}\int\limits_{0}^{\frac{1}{4N}}\Big|\Big|\sum\limits_{k=1}^{S}\mathcal{M}_{m,k}^{*}(\xi)\mathcal{V}_{\mathbb{Z}_{k}}(\xi)\Big|\Big|_{\mathbb{C}^{2(P+1)}}^{2}\,d\xi

\displaystyle=\sum\limits_{m\in T_{1}}\sum\limits_{j\in T_{2}}\int\limits_{0}^{\frac{1}{4N}}\Bigg(\Big|\sum\limits_{t=0}^{2N-1}X_{m,j}(\xi+\frac{t}{4N})\Big|^{2}+\Big|\sum\limits_{t=0}^{2N-1}e^{-\pi ir\frac{t}{N}}X_{m,j}(\xi+\frac{t}{4N})\Big|^{2}\Bigg)\,d\xi.

Now consider,

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\Big|\langle\mathbb{Z},E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}\mathbb{W}_{j}\rangle\Big|^{2}

\displaystyle=\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\Big|\langle\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{Z})(\xi),e^{4\pi iN\lambda(\frac{m}{M}+\xi)}\mathcal{F}(E_{\frac{m}{M}}\mathbb{W}_{j})(\xi)\rangle\Big|^{2}

\displaystyle=\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\Big|\int\limits_{\Omega}\sum\limits_{k=1}^{S}[\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{Z})(\xi)]_{k}\overline{[\mathcal{F}(E_{\frac{m}{M}}\mathbb{W}_{j})(\xi)]_{k}}e^{-4\pi iN\lambda(\frac{m}{M}+\xi)}d\xi\Big|^{2}

\displaystyle=\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\l\in\mathbb{Z}\end{subarray}}\Big|\int\limits_{\Omega}\sum\limits_{k=1}^{S}[\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{Z})(\xi)]_{k}\overline{[\mathcal{F}(E_{\frac{m}{M}}\mathbb{W}_{j})(\xi)]_{k}}e^{-4\pi iN(2l)(\frac{m}{M}+\xi)}d\xi\Big|^{2}

(3.21)

\displaystyle+\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\l\in\mathbb{Z}\end{subarray}}\Big|\int\limits_{\Omega}\sum\limits_{k=1}^{S}[\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{Z})(\xi)]_{k}\overline{[\mathcal{F}(E_{\frac{m}{M}}\mathbb{W}_{j})(\xi)]_{k}}e^{-4\pi iN(\frac{r}{N}+2l)(\frac{m}{M}+\xi)}d\xi\Big|^{2}.

Using (
3.19
) and (
3
), we have

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\Big|\langle\mathbb{Z},E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}\mathbb{W}_{j}\rangle\Big|^{2}

\displaystyle=\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\l\in\mathbb{Z}\end{subarray}}\Big|\int\limits_{0}^{\frac{1}{2}}X_{m,j}(\xi)e^{-4\pi iN(2l)(\frac{m}{M}+\xi)}d\xi\Big|^{2}+\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\l\in\mathbb{Z}\end{subarray}}\Big|\int\limits_{0}^{\frac{1}{2}}X_{m,j}(\xi)e^{-4\pi iN(\frac{r}{N}+2l)(\frac{m}{M}+\xi)}\,d\xi\Big|^{2}

\displaystyle=\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\l\in\mathbb{Z}\end{subarray}}\Big|\int\limits_{0}^{\frac{1}{4N}}\sum\limits_{t=0}^{2N-1}X_{m,j}(\xi+\frac{t}{4N})e^{-2\pi i(4N)l(\frac{m}{M}+\xi)}d\xi\Big|^{2}

(3.22)

\displaystyle+\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\l\in\mathbb{Z}\end{subarray}}\Big|\int\limits_{0}^{\frac{1}{4N}}\sum\limits_{t=0}^{2N-1}X_{m,j}(\xi+\frac{t}{4N})e^{-4\pi ir(\frac{m}{M}+\xi+\frac{t}{4N})}e^{-2\pi i(4N)l(\frac{m}{M}+\xi)}d\xi\Big|^{2}.

By invoking Lemma
2.3
, Ineq. (
3.17
) and (
3
), we have

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\Big|\langle\mathbb{Z},E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}\mathbb{W}_{j}\rangle\Big|^{2}

(3.23)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{4N}\Bigg(\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1}\atop j\in T_{2}\end{subarray}}\int\limits_{0}^{\frac{1}{4N}}\Big|\sum\limits_{t=0}^{2N-1}X_{m,j}(\xi+\frac{t}{4N})\Big|^{2}\,d\xi+\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1}\atop j\in T_{2}\end{subarray}}\int\limits_{0}^{\frac{1}{4N}}\Big|\sum\limits_{t=0}^{2N-1}X_{m,j}(\xi+\frac{t}{4N})e^{-i\pi\frac{r}{N}t}\Big|^{2}\,d\xi\Bigg).

Equations (
3.20
) and (

) give the desired result.

The following result characterizes DVNUG frames in discrete vector-valued nonuniform signal spaces.

Theorem 3.9
.

Suppose
\{\mathbb{W}_{j}\}_{j\in T_{2}}\subset\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})
satisfies

\displaystyle\Big|\Big|\mathcal{F}(E_{\frac{m}{M}}\mathbb{W}_{j})(\xi)\Big|\Big|_{\mathbb{C}^{S}}\leq B_{0}<\infty\ \ \text{for}\ m\in T_{1},\ j\in T_{2},\ \text{a.e.}\ \xi\in\Omega,

where
B_{0}
is a positive constant. Let
\mathcal{M}_{m,k}(\xi)
be
4\times 2(P+1)
matrices for
m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2},a.e.\,\xi\in[0,\frac{1}{4N}[
, as defined in (
3.16
). Then,

\mathcal{G}(\Lambda,T_{1},T_{2},\mathbb{W}_{j})
is a DVNUG frame for discrete vector-valued nonuniform signal space
\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})
with lower frame bound
A_{0}>0
if and only if

(3.24)

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{m\in T_{1}}\Big|\Big|\sum\limits_{k=1}^{S}\mathcal{M}_{m,k}^{*}(\xi)\mathbb{C}_{k}\Big|\Big|_{\mathbb{C}^{2(P+1)}}^{2}\geq 4NA_{0}\sum\limits_{k=1}^{S}||\mathbb{C}_{k}||_{\mathbb{C}^{4N}}^{2},\,\,a.e.\,\,\xi\in\Big(0,\frac{1}{4N}\Big)

for all
\mathbb{C}_{k}\in\mathbb{C}^{4N}
,
k=1,2,\dots,S
.

Proof.

Since
\Big|\Big|\mathcal{F}(E_{\frac{m}{M}}\mathbb{W}_{j})(\xi)\Big|\Big|_{\mathbb{C}^{S}}\leq B_{0}<\infty
, for
m\in T_{1}
,
j\in T_{2}
, a.e.
\xi\in\Omega
, so by Lemma
3.8
, for each
\mathbb{Z}\in\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})
, we have,

(3.25)

\displaystyle 4N\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\Big|\langle\mathbb{Z},E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}\mathbb{W}_{j}\rangle\Big|^{2}=\sum\limits_{m\in T_{1}}\int\limits_{0}^{\frac{1}{4N}}\Big|\Big|\sum\limits_{k=1}^{S}\mathcal{M}_{m,k}^{*}(\xi)\mathcal{V}_{\mathbb{Z}_{k}}(\xi)\Big|\Big|_{\mathbb{C}^{2(P+1)}}^{2}\,d\xi,

where
\mathcal{V}_{\mathbb{Z}_{k}}
defined as in (
3.18
).

Assume first that
\mathcal{G}(\Lambda,T_{1},T_{2},\mathbb{W}_{j})
is a DVNUG frame for
\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})
with lower frame bound
A_{0}
, say. Let
f(\xi)\in L^{2}(0,\frac{1}{4N})
be an arbitrary element. For
k\in\{1,2,\dots,S\}
, let

(3.26)

\displaystyle\mathbb{C}_{k}=\begin{bmatrix}[\mathbb{C}_{k}]_{1}\vskip 5.0pt\\
[\mathbb{C}_{k}]_{2}\\
\vdots\vskip 5.0pt\\
[\mathbb{C}_{k}]_{2N}\vskip 5.0pt\\
\,\,\,\,\,\,[\mathbb{C}_{k}]_{2N+1}\\
\vdots\vskip 5.0pt\\
[\mathbb{C}_{k}]_{4N}\end{bmatrix}_{4N\times 1}\in\mathbb{C}^{4N}.

Define
\mathbb{Z}\in\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})
in term of its Fourier transform,
\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{Z}(\xi))=\begin{bmatrix}[\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{Z}(\xi))]_{1}\vskip 5.0pt\\
[\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{Z}(\xi))]_{2}\\
\vdots\vskip 5.0pt\\
[\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{Z}(\xi))]_{S}\end{bmatrix}\in L^{2}(\Omega,\mathbb{C}^{S})
, as follows:

For any fixed
k_{0}\in\{1,2,\dots,S\}
,
if
\xi\in[0,\frac{1}{2}[
, then there exists unique
t^{\prime}\in\{0,1,\dots,2N-1\}
such that
\xi-\frac{t^{\prime}}{4N}\in[0,\frac{1}{4N}[
, so define

\displaystyle[\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{Z})(\xi)]_{k_{0}}=[\mathbb{C}_{k_{0}}]_{t^{\prime}+1}f(\xi-\frac{t^{\prime}}{4N});

and if
\xi\in[\frac{N}{2},\frac{N+1}{2}[
, there exists
t^{\prime\prime}\in\{0,1,\dots,2N-1\}
such that
\xi-\frac{t^{\prime\prime}}{4N}-\frac{N}{2}\in[0,\frac{1}{4N}[
, so define

\displaystyle[\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{Z})(\xi))]_{k_{0}}=[\mathbb{C}_{k_{0}}]_{2N+1+t^{\prime\prime}}f(\xi-\frac{t^{\prime\prime}}{4N}-\frac{N}{2}).

Thus, for
k\in\{1,2,\dots,S\},t\in\{0,1,\dots,2N-1\}
and
\xi\in[0,\frac{1}{4N}[
, we have

(3.27)

\displaystyle\Big[\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{Z})(\xi+\frac{t}{4N})\Big]_{k}

\displaystyle=[\mathbb{C}_{k}]_{t+1}f(\xi),

(3.28)

\displaystyle\Big[\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{Z})(\xi+\frac{t}{4N}+\frac{N}{2})\Big]_{k}

\displaystyle=[\mathbb{C}_{k}]_{2N+t+1}f(\xi).

Now, for
k\in\{1,2,\dots,S\}
and
\xi\in[0,\frac{1}{4N}[
, we define
\mathcal{V}_{\mathbb{Z}_{k}}(\xi)
as follows

(3.29)

\displaystyle\mathcal{V}_{\mathbb{Z}_{k}}(\xi)=\begin{bmatrix}\big[\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{Z})(\xi)\big]_{k}\vskip 5.0pt\\
\big[\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{Z})(\xi+\frac{1}{4N})\big]_{k}\\
\vdots\vskip 5.0pt\\
\big[\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{Z})(\xi+\frac{2N-1}{4N})\big]_{k}\vskip 5.0pt\\
\big[\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{Z})(\xi+\frac{N}{2})\big]_{k}\vskip 5.0pt\\
\big[\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{Z})(\xi+\frac{N}{2}+\frac{1}{4N})\big]_{k}\\
\vdots\vskip 5.0pt\\
\big[\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{Z})(\xi+\frac{N}{2}+\frac{2N-1}{4N})\big]_{k}\\
\end{bmatrix}_{4N\times 1}.

By (
3.26
), (
3.27
), (
3.28
) and (
3.29
), for
k\in\{1,2,\dots,S\}
and
\xi\in[0,\frac{1}{4N}[
, we have

(3.30)

\displaystyle\mathcal{V}_{\mathbb{Z}_{k}}(\xi)=\mathbb{C}_{k}f(\xi).

Now, using (
3.30
) and (
3.25
), we have

\displaystyle\int\limits_{0}^{\frac{1}{4N}}|f(\xi)|^{2}\sum\limits_{m\in T_{1}}\Big|\Big|\sum\limits_{k=1}^{S}\mathcal{M}_{m,k}^{*}(\xi)\mathbb{C}_{k}\Big|\Big|_{\mathbb{C}^{2(P+1)}}^{2}\,d\xi

\displaystyle=\int\limits_{0}^{\frac{1}{4N}}\sum\limits_{m\in T_{1}}\Big|\Big|\sum\limits_{k=1}^{S}\mathcal{M}_{m,k}^{*}(\xi)\mathcal{V}_{\mathbb{Z}_{k}}(\xi)\Big|\Big|_{\mathbb{C}^{2(P+1)}}^{2}\,d\xi

(3.31)

\displaystyle=4N\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\Big|\langle\mathbb{Z},E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}\mathbb{W}_{j}\rangle\Big|^{2}.

Therefore, by (
3
) and the hypothesis that
\mathcal{G}(\Lambda,T_{1},T_{2},\mathbb{W}_{j})
is a frame with lower frame bound
A_{0}
, we get

\displaystyle\int\limits_{0}^{\frac{1}{4N}}|f(\xi)|^{2}\sum\limits_{m\in T_{1}}\Big|\Big|\sum\limits_{k=1}^{S}\mathcal{M}_{m,k}^{*}(\xi)\mathbb{C}_{k}\Big|\Big|_{\mathbb{C}^{2(P+1)}}^{2}\,d\xi

\displaystyle\geq 4NA_{0}||\mathbb{Z}||_{\ell^{2}}^{2}

\displaystyle=4NA_{0}||\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{Z})||_{L^{2}}^{2}

(3.32)

\displaystyle=4NA_{0}\sum\limits_{k=1}^{S}\sum\limits_{t=0}^{2N-1}\Bigg(\int\limits_{0}^{4N}\Big(\Big|\Big[\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{Z})(\xi+\frac{t}{4N})\Big]_{k}\Big|^{2}+\Big|\Big[\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{Z})(\xi+\frac{t}{4N}+\frac{N}{2})\Big]_{k}\Big|^{2}\Bigg)\,d\xi.

Using (
3.27
), (
3.28
) and (
3
), we have

\displaystyle\int\limits_{0}^{\frac{1}{4N}}|f(\xi)|^{2}\sum\limits_{m\in T_{1}}\Big|\Big|\sum\limits_{k=1}^{S}\mathcal{M}_{m,k}^{*}(\xi)\mathbb{C}_{k}\Big|\Big|_{\mathbb{C}^{2(P+1)}}^{2}\,d\xi\geq 4NA_{0}\sum\limits_{k=1}^{S}\int\limits_{0}^{\frac{1}{4N}}||\mathbb{C}_{k}||_{\mathbb{C}^{4N}}^{2}|f(\xi)|^{2}\,d\xi.

Since
f(\xi)
is an arbitrary element of
L^{2}(0,\frac{1}{4N})
, we conclude for
a.e.\,\xi\in(0,\frac{1}{4N})
, that

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{m\in T_{1}}\Big|\Big|\sum\limits_{k=1}^{S}\mathcal{M}_{m,k}^{*}(\xi)\mathbb{C}_{k}\Big|\Big|_{\mathbb{C}^{2(P+1)}}^{2}\geq 4NA_{0}\sum\limits_{k=1}^{S}||\mathbb{C}_{k}||_{\mathbb{C}^{4N}}^{2}.

Conversely, assume that (
3.24
) holds. As
\Big|\Big|\mathcal{F}(E_{\frac{m}{M}}\mathbb{W}_{j})(\xi)\Big|\Big|_{\mathbb{C}^{S}}\leq B_{0}<\infty
, for
m\in T_{1}
,
j\in T_{2}
, a.e.
\xi\in\Omega
, so by Theorem
3.3
,
\mathcal{G}(\Lambda,T_{1},T_{2},\mathbb{W}_{j})
form a DVNUG Bessel sequence with Bessel bound
2^{M+P}B_{0}^{2}S
, so we only need to verify the lower frame inequality. From (
3.25
), for each
\mathbb{Z}\in\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})
, we have

(3.33)

\displaystyle 4N\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\Big|\langle\mathbb{Z},E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}\mathbb{W}_{j}\rangle\Big|^{2}

\displaystyle=\sum\limits_{m\in T_{1}}\int\limits_{0}^{\frac{1}{4N}}\Big|\Big|\sum\limits_{k=1}^{S}\mathcal{M}_{m,k}^{*}(\xi)\mathcal{V}_{\mathbb{Z}_{k}}(\xi)\Big|\Big|_{\mathbb{C}^{2(P+1)}}^{2}\,d\xi,

where
\mathcal{V}_{\mathbb{Z}_{k}}(\xi)
as defined in (
3.18
).
Therefore, by (
3.24
) and (
3.33
), we have

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\Big|\langle\mathbb{Z},E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}\mathbb{W}_{j}\rangle\Big|^{2}

\displaystyle\geq A_{0}\int\limits_{0}^{\frac{1}{4N}}\sum\limits_{k=1}^{S}||\mathcal{V}_{\mathbb{Z}_{k}}(\xi)||_{\mathbb{C}^{4N}}^{2}\,d\xi

\displaystyle=A_{0}||\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{Z})||_{L^{2}}^{2}

\displaystyle=A_{0}||\mathbb{Z}||_{\ell^{2}}^{2}\,\,,

which completes the proof.
∎

4.
Perturbation of Discrete Vector-Valued Nonuniform Gabor Frames

Perturbation theory is one of important branch of both pure mathematics and physical science, which have been studied extensively, see
[
31
]
for technical details. Under perturbation of frames, it is important that their fundamental properties are preserved. Some fundamental results about perturbation of Gabor frames can be found in
[
5
,
14
]
. The following result shows that DVNUG frames are stable under small perturbation.

Theorem 4.1
.

Let
\mathcal{G}(\Lambda,T_{1},T_{2},\mathbb{W}_{j})
be a DVNUG frame for
\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})
with frames bounds
A_{0}
and
B_{0}
. Let
\{\mathbb{V}_{j}\}_{j\in T_{2}}\subset\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})
, and
\theta
be a positive real constant satisfying

(4.1)

\displaystyle\Big|\Big|\mathcal{F}E_{\frac{m}{M}}(\mathbb{W}_{j}+\mathbb{V}_{j})(\xi)\Big|\Big|_{\mathbb{C}^{S}}\leq\theta<2^{M+P}\theta^{2}S<A_{0},

for
m\in T_{1}
,
j\in T_{2}
,
a.e.\,\xi\in\Omega
. Then,
\mathcal{G}(\Lambda,T_{1},T_{2},\mathbb{V}_{j})

is also a DVNUG frame for
\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})
with bounds
\big(\sqrt{A_{0}}-\sqrt{2^{(M+P)}\theta^{2}S}\big)^{2}
and
\big(2^{(M+P+1)}\theta^{2}S+2B_{0}\big)
.

Proof.

For any
\mathbb{Z}\in\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})
, using Lemma
2.7
, we have

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\Big|\langle\mathbb{Z},E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}\mathbb{V}_{j}\rangle\Big|^{2}

(4.2)

\displaystyle=\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\Big|\langle\mathbb{Z},E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}(\mathbb{V}_{j}+\mathbb{W}_{j})\rangle-\langle\mathbb{Z},E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}\mathbb{W}_{j}\rangle\Big|^{2}

(4.3)

\displaystyle\leq 2\Bigg(\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\Big|\langle\mathbb{Z},E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}(\mathbb{V}_{j}+\mathbb{W}_{j})\rangle\Big|^{2}+\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\Big|\langle\mathbb{Z},E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}\mathbb{W}_{j}\rangle\Big|^{2}\Bigg).

Also, by (
4.1
) and Theorem
3.3
, we have

(4.4)

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\Big|\langle\mathbb{Z},E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}(\mathbb{W}_{j}+\mathbb{V}_{j})\rangle\Big|^{2}\leq 2^{(M+P)}\theta^{2}S||\mathbb{Z}||^{2}.

Now, by the given hypothesis, (
4.3
) and (
4.4
), we obtain

(4.5)

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\Big|\langle\mathbb{Z},E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}\mathbb{V}_{j}\rangle\Big|^{2}\leq\big(2^{(M+P+1)}\theta^{2}S+2B_{0}\big)||\mathbb{Z}||^{2}.

By invoking inequalities (
4.2
) and (
4.4
), we have

\displaystyle\sqrt{\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\Big|\langle\mathbb{Z},E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}\mathbb{V}_{j}\rangle\Big|^{2}}

\displaystyle=\sqrt{\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\Big|\langle\mathbb{Z},E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}(\mathbb{V}_{j}+\mathbb{W}_{j})\rangle-\langle\mathbb{Z},E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}\mathbb{W}_{j}\rangle\Big|^{2}}

\displaystyle\geq\sqrt{\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\Big|\langle\mathbb{Z},E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}\mathbb{W}_{j}\rangle\Big|^{2}}-\sqrt{\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\Big|\langle\mathbb{Z},E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}(\mathbb{V}_{j}+\mathbb{W}_{j})\Big|^{2}}

\displaystyle\geq\sqrt{A_{0}||\mathbb{Z}||^{2}}-\sqrt{(2^{(M+P)}\theta^{2}S)||\mathbb{Z}||^{2}}

(4.6)

\displaystyle=\big(\sqrt{A_{0}}-\sqrt{2^{(M+P)}\theta^{2}S}\big)||\mathbb{Z}||,\ \ \mathbb{Z}\in\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S}).

From (
4.5
) and (
4
), we conclude that
\mathcal{G}(\Lambda,T_{1},T_{2},\mathbb{V}_{j})
is a DVNUG frame for
\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})
with the desired frame bounds.
∎

And, for

5.
Interplay Between Window Sequences of Discrete Vector-Valued Nonuniform System and Their Corresponding Coordinates

In real world, many times it is convenient to work with compact data set due to various reasons like incomplete data points or the given data set is so huge and the each particual detail of the given data is not so much important, for example if we have data set of last one thousand days of temperature
\mathbb{T}_{d}
which is calculated in three times in a day, like morning
m_{d}
, afternoon
a_{d}
and night
n_{d}
, where
d
denotes the day, that is,
\mathbb{T}_{d}=\begin{bmatrix}m_{d}\\
a_{d}\\
n_{d}\end{bmatrix},

and we are working in a problem where we need last thousand days’s temperature but not specifically three times in a day, or may be in our data points some entries are missing, then we can work with average data points of temperature
\text{$\mu$}_{\mathbb{T}_{d}}=\frac{1}{3}(m_{d}+a_{d}+n_{d})
. The problem becomes more critical if we have nonuniform data set for example if we are taking temperature of each 2 hours and half an hour later of each 2 hours, i.e.,
2k,2k+\frac{1}{2},k=0,1,\dots
. This motivate us to find frame condition for arithmetic mean of window sequences associated with a given DVNUG frame for the discrete vector-valued nonuniform signal space
\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})
. In this direction, we have the following result.

Theorem 5.1
.

Let
\{\mathbb{W}_{j}\}_{j\in T_{2}}\in\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})
be such that
\mathcal{G}(\Lambda,T_{1},T_{2},\mathbb{W}_{j})
is a DVNUG frame for
\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})
with frame bounds
A_{0}
and
B_{0}
. Then,
\mathcal{G}(\Lambda,T_{1},T_{2},\text{$\mu$}_{\mathbb{W}_{j}})
is DVNUG frame of
\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C})
with frame bounds
\frac{A_{0}}{S}
and
\frac{B_{0}}{S}
.

Proof.

Let
x=\{x(\lambda)\}_{\lambda\in\Lambda}\in\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C})
be an arbitrary element. Define
\mathbb{Z}\in\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})
as follows

\displaystyle\mathbb{Z}=\Bigg\{\begin{bmatrix}[\mathbb{Z}(\lambda)]_{1}\vskip 5.0pt\\
[\mathbb{Z}(\lambda)]_{2}\\
\vdots\vskip 5.0pt\\
[\mathbb{Z}(\lambda)]_{S}\end{bmatrix}\Bigg\}_{\lambda\in\Lambda}=\Bigg\{\begin{bmatrix}x(\lambda)\vskip 5.0pt\\
x(\lambda)\\
\vdots\vskip 5.0pt\\
x(\lambda)\end{bmatrix}\Bigg\}_{\lambda\in\Lambda}.

Clearly,
||\mathbb{Z}||_{\ell^{2}}=\sqrt{S}||x||_{\ell^{2}}
. Since
\mathcal{G}(\Lambda,T_{1},T_{2},\mathbb{W}_{j})
is a DVNUG farme with bounds
A_{0}
and
B_{0}
, we have

(5.1)

\displaystyle SA_{0}||x||^{2}\leq\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\Big|\langle\mathbb{Z},E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}\mathbb{W}_{j}\rangle\Big|^{2}\leq SB_{0}||x||^{2},\ x\in\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}).

Now consider,

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\Big|\langle\mathbb{Z},E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}\mathbb{W}_{j}\rangle\Big|^{2}

\displaystyle=S^{2}\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\Bigg|\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}k=1,\lambda^{\prime}\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}^{S}[\mathbb{Z}(\lambda^{\prime})]_{k}\frac{1}{S}\overline{E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}[\mathbb{W}_{j}(\lambda^{\prime})]_{k}}\Bigg|^{2}

\displaystyle=S^{2}\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\Bigg|\sum\limits_{\lambda^{\prime}\in\Lambda}x(\lambda^{\prime})\overline{E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}\frac{1}{S}\sum\limits_{k=1}^{S}[\mathbb{W}_{j}(\lambda^{\prime})]_{k}}\Bigg|^{2}

\displaystyle=S^{2}\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\Bigg|\sum\limits_{\lambda^{\prime}\in\Lambda}x(\lambda^{\prime})\overline{E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}\text{$\mu$}_{\mathbb{W}_{j}}(\lambda^{\prime})}\Bigg|^{2}

(5.2)

\displaystyle=S^{2}\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\Big|\langle x,E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}\text{$\mu$}_{\mathbb{W}_{j}}\rangle\Big|^{2}.

Using (
5.1
) and (
5
), we have

\displaystyle\frac{A_{0}}{S}||x||^{2}\leq\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\Big|\langle\mathbb{Z},E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}\mathbb{W}_{j}\rangle\Big|^{2}\leq\frac{B_{0}}{S}||x||^{2},\ x\in\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}).

This concludes the result.
∎

For different types of relations of frames of matrix-valued wave packet systems and its associated atomic wave packets, we refer to

30
]
.

Theorem 5.2
.

Let
\{\mathbb{W}_{j}\}_{j\in T_{2}}\subset\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})
. Define
S\times(P+1)
window matrix,
\mathbb{M}=\begin{bmatrix}\mathbb{W}_{0}&\mathbb{W}_{1}\,\,\cdots\,\,\mathbb{W}_{P}\end{bmatrix}
, that is,

\displaystyle\mathbb{M}(\lambda)=\begin{bmatrix}\big[\mathbb{W}_{0}(\lambda)\big]_{1}&\big[\mathbb{W}_{1}(\lambda)\big]_{1}&\cdots&\big[\mathbb{W}_{P}(\lambda)\big]_{1}\vskip 5.0pt\\
\big[\mathbb{W}_{0}(\lambda)\big]_{2}&\big[\mathbb{W}_{1}(\lambda)\big]_{2}&\cdots&\big[\mathbb{W}_{P}(\lambda)\big]_{2}\\
\vdots&\vdots&\vdots&\vdots\vskip 5.0pt\\
\big[\mathbb{W}_{0}(\lambda)\big]_{S}&\big[\mathbb{W}_{1}(\lambda)\big]_{S}&\cdots&\big[\mathbb{W}_{P}(\lambda)\big]_{S}\end{bmatrix}_{S\times(P+1)}\,\,\,\,\,\text{for}\,\,\,\,\lambda\in\Lambda,

where
\big[\mathbb{M}\big]_{l,l^{\prime}}=[\mathbb{W}_{(l^{\prime}-1)}]_{l}
is
l
th row and
l^{\prime}
th column of matrix
\mathbb{M}
with
1\leq l\leq S,1\leq l^{\prime}\leq P+1
.
Then the following holds.

(i)

If
\mathcal{G}(\Lambda,T_{1},T_{2},\mathbb{W}_{j})
is a DVNUG frame for
\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})
with bounds
A_{0}
and
B_{0}
, then each row of the window matrix
\mathbb{M}
generates a discrete nonuniform Gabor frame for
\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C})
. To be precise, for each
l_{0}\in\{1,2,\dots,S\}
, the system

\displaystyle\{E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}[\mathbb{M}]_{l_{0}l^{\prime}}:\lambda\in\Lambda,m\in T_{1},1\leq l^{\prime}\leq P+1\}

constitutes a discrete nonuniform Gabor frame for
\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C})
. But, the converse is not true.

(ii)

\mathcal{G}(\Lambda,T_{1},T_{2},\mathbb{W}_{j})
is DVNUG Bessel sequence in
\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})
if and only if each element
[\mathbb{M}]_{ll^{\prime}}
of window matrix
\mathbb{M}
generate discrete nonuniform Gabor Bessel sequence in
\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C})
. More precisely, for each
1\leq l\leq S,1\leq l^{\prime}\leq P+1
, the system

\displaystyle\{E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}[\mathbb{M}]_{ll^{\prime}}:\lambda\in\Lambda,m\in T_{1}\}

is a discrete nonuniform Gabor Bessel sequence in
\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C})
.

Proof.

(i)
: Fix
l_{0}\in\{1,2,\cdots,S\}
, and let
z_{l_{0}}=\{z_{l_{0}}(\lambda)\}_{\lambda\in\Lambda}\in\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C})
be arbitrary.
Define a vector
\mathbb{Z}_{0}=\Bigg\{\begin{bmatrix}\big[\mathbb{Z}_{0}(\lambda)\big]_{1}\vskip 5.0pt\\
\big[\mathbb{Z}_{0}(\lambda)\big]_{2}\\
\vdots\\
\big[\mathbb{Z}_{0}(\lambda)\big]_{S}\end{bmatrix}\Bigg\}_{\lambda\in\Lambda}
in
\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})

as follows:

(5.3)

\displaystyle\big[\mathbb{Z}_{0}(\lambda)\big]_{k}=\begin{cases}z_{l_{0}}(\lambda),&\lambda\in\Lambda,k=l_{0},\\
0,&\lambda\in\Lambda,k\in\{1,2,\cdots,S\}\setminus l_{0}.\end{cases}

Clearly,
||\mathbb{Z}_{0}||_{\ell^{2}}=||z_{l_{0}}||_{\ell^{2}}
. Let
A_{0}
and
B_{0}
be frame bounds for
\mathcal{G}(\Lambda,T_{1},T_{2},\mathbb{W}_{j})
. Then,

(5.4)

\displaystyle A_{0}||\mathbb{Z}_{0}||^{2}\leq\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\Big|\langle\mathbb{Z}_{0},E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}\mathbb{W}_{j}\rangle\Big|^{2}\leq B_{0}||\mathbb{Z}_{0}||^{2}.

Using (
5.3
) and (
5.4
), we have

(5.5)

\displaystyle A_{0}||z_{l_{0}}||^{2}\leq\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\Big|\langle z_{l_{0}},E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}[\mathbb{W}_{j}]_{l_{0}}\rangle\Big|^{2}\leq B_{0}||z_{l_{0}}||^{2}.

Using the following relation, which is by hypothesis,

\displaystyle\{[\mathbb{W}_{j}]_{l_{0}}:j\in T_{2}\}=\{[\mathbb{M}]_{l_{0},l^{\prime}}:1\leq l^{\prime}\leq P+1\},

and (
5.5
), we conclude that
\{E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}[\mathbb{M}]_{l_{0},l^{\prime}}:\lambda\in\Lambda,m\in T_{1},1\leq l^{\prime}\leq P+1\}
is a discrete nonuniform frame for
\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C})
with bounds
A_{0}
and
B_{0}
.

To show that converse is not true. Consider a sequence
\{w_{j}\}_{j\in T_{2}}\in\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C})
with the property that
\{E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}w_{j}:\lambda\in\Lambda,m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\}
is a discrete nonuniform frame for
\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C})
. Choose
S=3
, and define
\{\mathbb{W}_{j}\}_{j\in T_{2}}\subset\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{3})
as follow:

\displaystyle\mathbb{W}_{j}(\lambda)=\begin{bmatrix}[\mathbb{W}_{j}(\lambda)]_{1}\vskip 5.0pt\\
[\mathbb{W}_{j}(\lambda)]_{2}\vskip 5.0pt\\
[\mathbb{W}_{j}(\lambda)]_{3}\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}w_{j}(\lambda)\vskip 5.0pt\\
w_{j}(\lambda)\vskip 5.0pt\\
w_{j}(\lambda)\end{bmatrix}\,\,\,\,\,\text{for}\,\,\,\,\lambda\in\Lambda,\ j\in T_{2}.

Then, each row of matrix

\displaystyle\mathbb{M}(\lambda)=\begin{bmatrix}w_{0}(\lambda)&w_{1}(\lambda)&\cdots&w_{P}(\lambda)\vskip 5.0pt\\
w_{0}(\lambda)&w_{1}(\lambda)&\cdots&w_{P}(\lambda)\vskip 5.0pt\\
w_{0}(\lambda)&w_{1}(\lambda)&\cdots&w_{P}(\lambda)\end{bmatrix}_{3\times(P+1)},\,\,\,\,\,\text{for}\,\,\,\,\lambda\in\Lambda;

that is, for each
l_{0}\in\{1,2,3\}
, the sequence
\{E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}[\mathbb{M}]_{l_{0}l^{\prime}}:\lambda\in\Lambda,m\in T_{1},1\leq l^{\prime}\leq P+1\}
is a discrete nonuniform Gabor frame for
\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C})
.

But,
\mathcal{G}(\Lambda,T_{1},T_{2},\mathbb{W}_{j})
is not a DVNUG frame for
\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{3})
. Indeed, let
\alpha_{0}
and
\beta_{0}
be frame bounds for
\mathcal{G}(\Lambda,T_{1},T_{2},\mathbb{W}_{j})
. Then, for all
\mathbb{Z}\in\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{3})
, we have

(5.6)

\displaystyle\alpha_{0}||\mathbb{Z}||^{2}\leq\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\Big|\langle\mathbb{Z},E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}\mathbb{W}_{j}\rangle\Big|^{2}\leq\beta_{0}||\mathbb{Z}||^{2}.

\displaystyle\text{Choose}\ \mathbb{Z}_{0}=\Bigg\{\begin{bmatrix}[\mathbb{Z}_{0}(\lambda)]_{1}\vskip 5.0pt\\
[\mathbb{Z}_{0}(\lambda)]_{2}\vskip 5.0pt\\
[\mathbb{Z}_{0}(\lambda)]_{3}\vskip 5.0pt\end{bmatrix}\Bigg\}_{\lambda\in\Lambda}\in\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{3}),\ \text{where}\ \mathbb{Z}_{0}(\lambda)=\begin{bmatrix}[\mathbb{Z}_{0}(\lambda)]_{1}\vskip 5.0pt\\
[\mathbb{Z}_{0}(\lambda)]_{2}\vskip 5.0pt\\
[\mathbb{Z}_{0}(\lambda)]_{3}\vskip 5.0pt\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}z(\lambda)\vskip 5.0pt\\
-z(\lambda)\vskip 5.0pt\\
0\vskip 5.0pt\end{bmatrix},\,\,\,\,\,\,\lambda\in\Lambda.

Here,
0\neq z=z(\lambda)\in\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C})
is any fixed element. Then,
\mathbb{Z}_{0}
is a non-zero vector such that

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\Big|\langle\mathbb{Z}_{0},E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}\mathbb{W}_{j}\rangle\Big|^{2}=\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\Bigg|\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}k=1\\
\lambda^{\prime}\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}^{3}\big[\mathbb{Z}_{0}(\lambda^{\prime})\big]_{k}\overline{\big[E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}\mathbb{W}_{j}(\lambda^{\prime})\big]_{k}}\Bigg|^{2}=0,

which contradicts lower inequality of (
5.6
).

(ii)
: Firstly, assume that
\mathcal{G}(\Lambda,T_{1},T_{2},\mathbb{W}_{j})
is a DVNUG Bessel sequence for
\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})
with Bessel bound
\beta
. Let
x=\{x(\lambda)\}_{\lambda\in\Lambda}\in\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C})
be an arbitrary element. Let
l_{0},l_{0}^{\prime}
are any fixed integers such that
1\leq l_{0}\leq S,1\leq l_{0}^{\prime}\leq P+1
.

Define
\mathbb{Z}_{0}=\Bigg\{\begin{bmatrix}[\mathbb{Z}_{0}(\lambda)]_{1}\vskip 5.0pt\\
[\mathbb{Z}_{0}(\lambda)]_{2}\vskip 5.0pt\\
\vdots\\
[\mathbb{Z}_{0}(\lambda)]_{S}\end{bmatrix}\Bigg\}_{\lambda\in\Lambda}\in\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})
as follows

\displaystyle[\mathbb{Z}_{0}(\lambda)]_{k}=\begin{cases}x(\lambda),&\lambda\in\Lambda,k=l_{0},\\
0,&\lambda\in\Lambda,k\in\{1,2,\dots,S\}\setminus l_{0}\end{cases}.

We compute

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\Big|\langle x,E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}[\mathbb{M}]_{l_{0}l_{0}^{\prime}}\rangle\Big|^{2}

\displaystyle=\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\Big|\langle x,E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}[\mathbb{W}_{l_{0}^{\prime}-1}]_{l_{0}}\rangle\Big|^{2}

\displaystyle=\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\Big|\langle\mathbb{Z}_{0},E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}\mathbb{W}_{l_{0}^{\prime}-1}\rangle\Big|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\Big|\langle\mathbb{Z}_{0},E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}\mathbb{W}_{j}\rangle\Big|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\beta||\mathbb{Z}_{0}||^{2}

\displaystyle=\beta||x||^{2}.

Conversely, assume now that each element
[\mathbb{M}]_{ll^{\prime}}

of window matrix
\mathbb{M}
generates DVNUG Bessel sequence for
\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C})
, so there exists positive constant
\beta_{0}
say, such that for each
1\leq l\leq S,1\leq l^{\prime}\leq P+1
, we have

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\Big|\langle x,E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}[\mathbb{M}]_{ll^{\prime}}\rangle\Big|^{2}\leq\beta_{0}||x||^{2},

for all
x=\{x(\lambda)\}_{\lambda\in\Lambda}\in\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C})
, that is,

(5.7)

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\Big|\sum\limits_{\lambda^{\prime}\in\Lambda}x(\lambda^{\prime})\overline{E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}[\mathbb{M}(\lambda^{\prime})]}_{ll^{\prime}}\Big|^{2}\leq\beta_{0}\sum\limits_{\lambda\in\Lambda}|x(\lambda)|^{2}.

Using Lemma
2.7
, for any
\mathbb{Z}=\Bigg\{\begin{bmatrix}[\mathbb{Z}(\lambda)]_{1}\vskip 5.0pt\\
[\mathbb{Z}(\lambda)]_{2}\\
\vdots\\
[\mathbb{Z}(\lambda)]_{S}\end{bmatrix}\Bigg\}_{\lambda\in\Lambda}\in\ell^{2}(\Lambda,\mathbb{C}^{S})
, we compute

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\Big|\langle\mathbb{Z},E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}\mathbb{W}_{j}\rangle\Big|^{2}

\displaystyle=\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\Big|\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}k=1,\lambda^{\prime}\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}^{S}[\mathbb{Z}(\lambda^{\prime})]_{k}[\overline{E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}\mathbb{W}_{j}(\lambda^{\prime})}]_{k}\Big|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\sum\limits_{k=1}^{S}2^{S-1}\Big|\sum\limits_{\lambda^{\prime}\in\Lambda}[\mathbb{Z}(\lambda^{\prime})]_{k}\overline{E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}[\mathbb{W}_{j}(\lambda^{\prime})}]_{k}\Big|^{2}

(5.8)

\displaystyle=2^{S-1}\sum\limits_{j\in T_{2}}\sum\limits_{k=1}^{S}\Bigg(\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\Big|\sum\limits_{\lambda^{\prime}\in\Lambda}[\mathbb{Z}(\lambda^{\prime})]_{k}\overline{E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}[\mathbb{M}(\lambda^{\prime})]}_{k,j+1}\Big|^{2}\Bigg).

Inequalities in (
5.7
) and (
5
) gives

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}m\in T_{1},j\in T_{2}\\
\lambda\in\Lambda\end{subarray}}\Big|\langle\mathbb{Z},E_{\frac{m}{M}}R_{2N\lambda}\mathbb{W}_{j}\rangle\Big|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\beta_{0}2^{S-1}\sum\limits_{j\in T_{2}}\sum\limits_{k=1}^{S}\sum\limits_{\lambda\in\Lambda}\big|[\mathbb{Z}(\lambda)]_{k}\big|^{2}

\displaystyle=\beta_{0}2^{S-1}\sum\limits_{j\in T_{2}}||\mathbb{Z}||_{\ell^{2}}^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\beta_{0}2^{S-1}(P+1)||\mathbb{Z}||_{\ell^{2}}^{2}.

This concludes the proof.
∎

Remark 5.3
.

One may observed that result given in Theorem
5.2
(
ii
) is not true in case of DVNUG frames for
