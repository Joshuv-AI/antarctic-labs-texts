Lemma 2.1
.

For
x\geqslant{}1

\psi(x)=-\sum_{1\leqslant{}|h|\leqslant{}H}\Phi\left(\frac{h}{H+1}\right)\frac{e(hx)}{2\pi ih}+R_{H}(x),

where

The second one is a direct corollary of

Lemma 2.2

S_{\delta}=S_{\delta}(H,M,N):=\sum_{h\sim H}\sum_{m\sim M}\sum_{n\sim N}a_{h,n}b_{m}e\left(X\frac{M^{\beta}N^{\gamma}}{H^{\alpha}}\frac{h^{\alpha}}{m^{\beta}n^{\gamma}+\delta}\right),

3.
A key estimate

Define

\mathfrak{S}_{\delta}^{f}(x,D):=\sum_{D<d\leqslant 2D}\frac{f(d)}{d}\psi\left(\frac{x}{d+\delta}\right).

From
f=\textrm{id}\ast g
, we get

(3.1)

\frac{f(d)}{d}=\sum_{m|d}\frac{g(m)}{m},

so that we can decompose
\mathfrak{S}_{\delta}^{f}(x,D)
into bilinear forms

(3.2)

\mathfrak{S}_{\delta}^{f}(x,D)=\sum_{D<mn\leqslant 2D}\frac{g(m)}{m}\psi\left(\frac{x}{mn+\delta}\right).

In this section we will get the following estimation of
\mathfrak{S}_{\delta}^{f}(x,D)
which will play a key role in the proof of the main theorem.

Proposition 3.1
.

Let
f=\textup{id}\ast g
be an arithmetic function,

\delta\geqslant 0
be a fixed constant,

(\kappa,\lambda)
be an exponent pair.
Then

(3.3)

\mathfrak{S}_{\delta}^{f}(x,D)\ll(x^{\kappa}D^{-\kappa+\lambda})^{{1}/(1+\kappa)}+x^{\kappa}D^{-2\kappa+\lambda}\log x+x^{-1}D^{2}

uniformly for
1\leqslant D\leqslant x
, if
g(n)\ll 1
;

(3.4)

\mathfrak{S}_{\delta}^{f}(x,D)\ll D^{\varepsilon}\big((x^{\kappa}D^{-\kappa+\lambda})^{{1}/(1+\kappa)}+x^{\kappa}D^{-2\kappa+\lambda}\log x+x^{-1}D^{2}\big)

uniformly for
1\leqslant D\leqslant x
, if
g(n)\ll 1
.

Proof.

From (
3.1
), then we can be written (
3.2
) as

\mathfrak{S}_{\delta}^{f}(x,D)=\sum_{m\leqslant 2D}\frac{g(m)}{m}\sum_{D/m<n\leqslant 2D/m}\psi\left(\frac{x}{mn+\delta}\right).

Applying lemma
2.1
and noticing that
0<\Phi(t)<1
for
0<|t|<1
and
g(m)\ll m^{\varepsilon}
for
m\geqslant 1
,
we can derive

\mathfrak{S}_{\delta}^{f}(x,D)\ll\sum_{m\leqslant 2D}\frac{g(m)}{m}\bigg(\frac{(D/m)}{H}+\sum_{1\leqslant h\leqslant H}\frac{1}{h}\bigg|\sum_{D/m<n\leqslant 2D/m}e\bigg(\frac{hx}{mn+\delta}\bigg)\bigg|\bigg)

for
1\leqslant H\leqslant D/m
.
Applying the exponent pair
(\kappa,\lambda)
to the sum over
n
,
we find that

\begin{split}\mathfrak{S}_{\delta}^{f}(x,D)&\ll\sum_{m\leqslant 2D}\frac{g(m)}{m}\bigg(\frac{(D/m)}{H}+\sum_{1\leqslant h\leqslant H}\frac{1}{h}\bigg\{\bigg(\frac{hx}{D^{2}/m}\bigg)^{\kappa}(D/m)^{\lambda}+\frac{(D^{2}/m)}{hx}\bigg\}\bigg)\\
&\ll\sum_{m\leqslant 2D}\frac{g(m)}{m}\bigg((D/m)H^{-1}+x^{\kappa}H^{\kappa}(D^{2}/m)^{-\kappa}(D/m)^{\lambda}+x^{-1}(D^{2}/m)\bigg)\end{split}

for all
H\in[1,D/m]
. Optimising the parameter
H
over
[1,D/m]
we obtain

\mathfrak{S}_{\delta}^{f}(x,D)\ll\sum_{m\leqslant 2D}\frac{g(m)}{m}\big((x^{\kappa}D^{\lambda-\kappa}m^{-\lambda})^{1/(1+\kappa)}+x^{\kappa}(D^{2}/m)^{-\kappa}(D/m)^{\lambda}+x^{-1}(D^{2}/m)\big),

which implies (
3.3
) and (
3.3
).
∎

4.
Proof of Theorem
1.1

Let
f=\text{id}\ast g
and
N_{f}\in[1,x^{\frac{1}{3}})
be a parameter to be chosen later.
Firstly, we write

(4.1)

\sum_{n\leqslant x}\frac{f([x/n])}{[x/n]}=S_{f}^{{\dagger}}(x)+S_{f}^{\sharp}(x)

with

S_{f}^{{\dagger}}(x):=\sum_{n\leqslant N_{f}}\frac{f([x/n])}{[x/n]},\qquad S_{f}^{\sharp}(x):=\sum_{N_{f}<n\leqslant x}\frac{f([x/n])}{[x/n]}.

Secondly, we bound
S_{f}^{\sharp}(x)
.
Put
m=[x/n]
. Then
x/(m+1)<n\leqslant x/m
.
Thus

(4.2)

\begin{split}S_{f}^{\sharp}(x)&=\sum_{N_{f}<n\leqslant x}\frac{f([x/n])}{[x/n]}=\sum_{m\leqslant x/N_{f}}\frac{f(m)}{m}\sum_{x/(m+1)<n\leqslant x/m}1\\
&=\sum_{m\leqslant x/N_{f}}\frac{f(m)}{m}\left(\frac{x}{m}-\psi\left(\frac{x}{m}\right)-\frac{x}{m+1}+\psi\left(\frac{x}{m+1}\right)\right)\\
&=x\sum_{m\geqslant{}1}\frac{f(m)}{m^{2}(m+1)}-{R}_{0}^{f}(x)+{R}_{1}^{f}(x)+O(N_{f}),\end{split}

where

R_{\delta}^{f}(x):=\sum_{N_{f}<m\leqslant{x}/{N}_{f}}\frac{f(m)}{m}\psi\left(\frac{x}{m+\delta}\right)

for
\delta=0,1
, and we have used the following bounds

\begin{split}&x\sum_{m>{x}/{N_{f}}}\frac{f(m)}{m^{2}(m+1)}\ll N_{f},\qquad\sum_{m\leqslant N_{f}}\frac{f(m)}{m}\psi\left(\frac{x}{m+\delta}\right)\ll N_{f}.\end{split}

Writing
D_{j}:=x/(2^{j}N_{f})
, we have
N_{f}\leqslant D_{j}\leqslant x/N_{f}\leqslant x
for
0\leqslant j\leqslant\log(x/N_{f}^{2})/\log 2
.
Thus we can apply (
3.3
) of Proposition
3.1
with
(\kappa,\lambda)=(1/2,1/2)
to get

\begin{split}|R_{\delta}^{f}(x)|&\leqslant\sum_{0\leqslant j\leqslant{\log(x/N_{f}^{2})}/{\log 2}}|\mathfrak{S}_{\delta}^{f}(x,D_{j})|\\
&\ll\sum_{0\leqslant j\leqslant{\log(x/N_{f}^{2})}/{\log 2}}(x^{1/3}+x^{1/{2}}D_{j}^{-1/2}\log x+x^{-1}D_{j}^{2})\\
&\ll(x^{1/3}+x^{1/{2}}N_{f}^{-1/2}+xN_{f}^{-2})\log x.\end{split}

Inserting this into (
4.2
) and taking
N_{f}=x^{1/3}
, we derive

(4.3)

S_{f}^{\sharp}(x)=x\sum_{m\geqslant{}1}\frac{f(m)}{m^{2}(m+1)}+O(x^{1/3}\log x).

Finally,
{f(d)}/{d}=\sum_{mn=d}{g(m)}/{m}\leqslant\sum_{m|d}\frac{1}{m}\leqslant\log d

implies
S_{f}^{{\dagger}}(x)\ll N_{f}\log x
.
Inserting this and (
4.3
) into (
4.1
), we get (
1.9
).
