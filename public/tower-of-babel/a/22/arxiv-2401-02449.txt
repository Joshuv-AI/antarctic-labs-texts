Nonrigid Surface Registration: Gradient Calculations


Abstract




1
Rigid registration

The goal of rigid registration is to align a source surface
\mathcal{X}
to a target surface
\mathcal{Y}
.
The alignment process involves iteratively transforming
\mathcal{X}
closer and closer to
\mathcal{Y}
, such that

\mathcal{X}=\mathcal{Z}^{0}\rightarrow\mathcal{Z}^{1}\rightarrow\mathcal{Z}^{2}\rightarrow...\rightarrow\mathcal{Z}^{T}=\mathcal{Y}
.
In other words, in each iteration we seek a rotation matrix
\tilde{\mathbf{R}}
and a translation vector
\tilde{\mathbf{t}}
such that
\mathcal{X}^{t+1}=\tilde{\mathbf{R}}(\mathbf{R}^{t}\mathcal{X}+\mathbf{t}^{t})+\tilde{\mathbf{t}}=\tilde{\mathbf{R}}\mathcal{X}^{t}+\tilde{\mathbf{t}}
,
which corresponds to minimizing the error function

w_{1}\sum_{i=1}^{N}||\Pi_{\mathcal{Y}}(\mathbf{z}_{i}^{t+1})-\mathbf{z}_{i}^{t+1}||_{2}^{2}+w_{2}\sum_{i=1}^{N}||\tilde{\mathbf{R}}\mathbf{x}_{i}^{t}+\tilde{\mathbf{t}}-\mathbf{z}_{i}^{t+1}||_{2}^{2}

{\mathbf{x}_{1},\mathbf{x}_{2},...,\mathbf{x}_{n}}
and
{\mathbf{z}_{1},\mathbf{z}_{2},...,\mathbf{z}_{n}}
are sets of points sampled from the surfaces
\mathcal{X}
and
\mathcal{Z}
,
and
\Pi_{\mathcal{Y}}(\mathbf{z}_{i})
denotes the closest point on
\mathcal{Y}
to the point
\mathbf{z}_{i}
.

w_{1}
and
w_{2}
are simple weighting factors.
By applying two simplifications, we can trace the minimization problem back to solving a system of linear equations.
First, there is nonlinearity in
\Pi_{\mathcal{Y}}(\mathbf{z}_{i}^{t+1})
, so we use the previous estimate
\Pi_{\mathcal{Y}}(\mathbf{z}_{i}^{t})
which is constant
1
1

1

For efficient closest point lookup, a k-d tree has to be built in advance.
.
Second, there is also nonlinearity in
\tilde{\mathbf{R}}
.
Assuming small rotations, we can linearize the rotation matrices using
\cos(\cdot)\approx 1
and
\sin(\cdot)\approx 0
:

\tilde{\mathbf{R}}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\alpha&-\sin\alpha&0\\
\sin\alpha&\cos\alpha&0\\
0&0&1\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}\cos\beta&0&\sin\beta\\
0&1&0\\
-\sin\beta&0&\cos\beta\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}1&0&0\\
0&\cos\gamma&-\sin\gamma\\
0&\sin\gamma&\cos\gamma\end{bmatrix}

\tilde{\mathbf{R}}\approx\begin{bmatrix}1&-\gamma&\beta\\
\gamma&1&-\alpha\\
-\beta&\alpha&1\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}1&0&0\\
0&1&0\\
0&0&1\end{bmatrix}+\begin{bmatrix}0&-\gamma&\beta\\
\gamma&0&-\alpha\\
-\beta&\alpha&0\end{bmatrix}

By introducing
\tilde{\mathbf{r}}=\begin{bmatrix}\alpha&\beta&\gamma\end{bmatrix}
, we can substitute
\tilde{\mathbf{R}}
with
\mathbf{I}+\tilde{\mathbf{r}}\times
.
Adding Tikhonov regularization might be beneficial in ensuring that the rotations remain small.

1.1
Gradient calculations

We have to minimize the following function with respect to
\tilde{\mathbf{r}}
,
\tilde{\mathbf{t}}
, and
\mathbf{z}_{j}^{t+1}
:

\argmin_{\tilde{\mathbf{r}},\tilde{\mathbf{t}},\mathbf{z}_{j}^{t+1}}w_{1}\sum_{i=1}^{N}||\Pi_{\mathcal{Y}}(\mathbf{z}_{i}^{t})-\mathbf{z}_{i}^{t+1}||_{2}^{2}+w_{2}\sum_{i=1}^{N}||\mathbf{x}_{i}^{t}+\tilde{\mathbf{r}}\times\mathbf{x}_{i}^{t}+\tilde{\mathbf{t}}-\mathbf{z}_{i}^{t+1}||_{2}^{2}

•

Minimizing w.r.t.
\tilde{\mathbf{r}}
:

\frac{\partial}{\partial\tilde{\mathbf{r}}}\sum_{i=1}^{N}||\mathbf{x}_{i}^{t}+\tilde{\mathbf{r}}\times\mathbf{x}_{i}^{t}+\tilde{\mathbf{t}}-\mathbf{z}_{i}^{t+1}||_{2}^{2}=0

\sum_{i=1}^{N}\mathbf{x}_{i}^{t}\times(\mathbf{x}_{i}^{t}+\tilde{\mathbf{r}}\times\mathbf{x}_{i}^{t}+\tilde{\mathbf{t}}-\mathbf{z}_{i}^{t+1})=\mathbf{0}

-\sum_{i=1}^{N}\mathbf{X}_{i}^{t}\mathbf{X}_{i}^{t}\tilde{\mathbf{r}}+\sum_{i=1}^{N}\mathbf{X}_{i}^{t}\tilde{\mathbf{t}}-\sum_{i=1}^{N}\mathbf{X}_{i}^{t}\mathbf{z}_{i}^{t+1}=\mathbf{0}

•

Minimizing w.r.t.
\tilde{\mathbf{t}}
:

\frac{\partial}{\partial\tilde{\mathbf{t}}}\sum_{i=1}^{N}||\mathbf{x}_{i}^{t}+\tilde{\mathbf{r}}\times\mathbf{x}_{i}^{t}+\tilde{\mathbf{t}}-\mathbf{z}_{i}^{t+1}||_{2}^{2}=0

\sum_{i=1}^{N}\mathbf{x}_{i}^{t}+\tilde{\mathbf{r}}\times\mathbf{x}_{i}^{t}+\tilde{\mathbf{t}}-\mathbf{z}_{i}^{t+1}=\mathbf{0}

-\sum_{i=1}^{N}\mathbf{X}_{i}^{t}\tilde{\mathbf{r}}+N\tilde{\mathbf{t}}-\sum_{i=1}^{N}\mathbf{z}_{i}^{t+1}=-\sum_{i=1}^{N}\mathbf{x}_{i}^{t}

•

Minimizing w.r.t.
\mathbf{z}_{j}^{t+1}
:

\frac{\partial}{\partial\mathbf{z}_{j}^{t+1}}(w_{1}\sum_{i=1}^{N}||\Pi_{\mathcal{Y}}(\mathbf{z}_{i}^{t})-\mathbf{z}_{i}^{t+1}||_{2}^{2}+w_{2}\sum_{i=1}^{N}||\mathbf{x}_{i}^{t}+\tilde{\mathbf{r}}\times\mathbf{x}_{i}^{t}+\tilde{\mathbf{t}}-\mathbf{z}_{i}^{t+1}||_{2}^{2})=0

\frac{w_{1}}{w_{2}}(\mathbf{z}_{j}^{t+1}-\Pi_{\mathcal{Y}}(\mathbf{z}_{j}^{t}))+\mathbf{z}_{j}^{t+1}-\mathbf{x}_{j}^{t}-\tilde{\mathbf{r}}\times\mathbf{x}_{j}^{t}-\tilde{\mathbf{t}}=\mathbf{0}

\mathbf{X}_{j}^{t}\tilde{\mathbf{r}}-\tilde{\mathbf{t}}+\left(1+\frac{w_{1}}{w_{2}}\right)\mathbf{z}_{j}^{t+1}=\frac{w_{1}}{w_{2}}\Pi_{\mathcal{Y}}(\mathbf{z}_{j}^{t})+\mathbf{x}_{j}^{t}

The result is a system of 6 + 3N linear equations with the same number of unknowns.

\mathbf{x}=\begin{bmatrix}\tilde{\mathbf{r}}\\
\tilde{\mathbf{t}}\\
\mathbf{z}_{1}^{t+1}\\
\mathbf{z}_{2}^{t+1}\\
\vdots\\
\mathbf{z}_{N}^{t+1}\end{bmatrix}

\mathbf{b}=\begin{bmatrix}\mathbf{0}\\
-\sum_{i=1}^{N}\mathbf{x}_{i}^{t}\\
\frac{w_{1}}{w_{2}}\Pi_{\mathcal{Y}}(\mathbf{z}_{1}^{t})+\mathbf{x}_{1}^{t}\\
\frac{w_{1}}{w_{2}}\Pi_{\mathcal{Y}}(\mathbf{z}_{2}^{t})+\mathbf{x}_{2}^{t}\\
\vdots\\
\frac{w_{1}}{w_{2}}\Pi_{\mathcal{Y}}(\mathbf{z}_{N}^{t})+\mathbf{x}_{N}^{t}\end{bmatrix}

\mathbf{A}=\begin{bmatrix}\mathbf{A}_{rr}&\mathbf{A}_{tr}&\mathbf{A}_{z_{1}r}&\mathbf{A}_{z_{2}r}&\dots&\mathbf{A}_{z_{N}r}\\
\mathbf{A}_{rt}&\mathbf{A}_{tt}&\mathbf{A}_{z_{1}t}&\mathbf{A}_{z_{2}t}&\dots&\mathbf{A}_{z_{N}t}\\
\mathbf{A}_{rz_{1}}&\mathbf{A}_{tz_{1}}&\mathbf{A}_{z_{1}z_{1}}&\mathbf{0}&\dots&\mathbf{0}\\
\mathbf{A}_{rz_{2}}&\mathbf{A}_{tz_{2}}&\mathbf{0}&\mathbf{A}_{z_{2}z_{2}}&\dots&\mathbf{0}\\
\vdots&\vdots&\vdots&\vdots&\ddots&\vdots\\
\mathbf{A}_{rz_{N}}&\mathbf{A}_{tz_{N}}&\mathbf{0}&\mathbf{0}&\dots&\mathbf{A}_{z_{N}z_{N}}\end{bmatrix}

where

\mathbf{A}_{rr}=-\sum_{i=1}^{N}\mathbf{X}_{i}^{t}\mathbf{X}_{i}^{t}

\mathbf{A}_{tr}=\sum_{i=1}^{N}\mathbf{X}_{i}^{t}

\mathbf{A}_{z_{i}r}=-\begin{bmatrix}\mathbf{X}_{1}^{t}&\mathbf{X}_{2}^{t}&\dots&\mathbf{X}_{N}^{t}\end{bmatrix}

\mathbf{A}_{rt}=-\sum_{i=1}^{N}\mathbf{X}_{i}^{t}

\mathbf{A}_{tt}=N\mathbf{I}

\mathbf{A}_{z_{i}t}=-\begin{bmatrix}\mathbf{I}&\mathbf{I}&\dots&\mathbf{I}\end{bmatrix}

\mathbf{A}_{rz_{j}}=\begin{bmatrix}\mathbf{X}_{1}^{t}\\
\mathbf{X}_{2}^{t}\\
\dots\\
\mathbf{X}_{N}^{t}\end{bmatrix}

\mathbf{A}_{tz_{j}}=-\begin{bmatrix}\mathbf{I}\\
\mathbf{I}\\
\dots\\
\mathbf{I}\end{bmatrix}

\mathbf{A}_{z_{i}z_{j}}=\left(1+\frac{w_{1}}{w_{2}}\right)\begin{bmatrix}\mathbf{I}&\mathbf{0}&\dots&\mathbf{0}\\
\mathbf{0}&\mathbf{I}&\dots&\mathbf{0}\\
\vdots&\vdots&\ddots&\vdots\\
\mathbf{0}&\mathbf{0}&\dots&\mathbf{I}\\
\end{bmatrix}

2
As-rigid-as-possible registration

In each iteration we also compute the local rotation matrices
\tilde{\mathbf{R}}_{i}
:

w_{1}\sum_{i=1}^{N}||\Pi_{\mathcal{Y}}(\mathbf{z}_{i}^{t+1})-\mathbf{z}_{i}^{t+1}||_{2}^{2}+w_{2}\sum_{i=1}^{N}||\tilde{\mathbf{R}}\mathbf{x}_{i}^{t}+\tilde{\mathbf{t}}-\mathbf{z}_{i}^{t+1}||_{2}^{2}+w_{3}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\sum_{k\in\mathcal{N}(i)}||\tilde{\mathbf{R}}_{i}(\mathbf{x}_{k}^{t}-\mathbf{x}_{i}^{t})-(\mathbf{z}_{k}^{t+1}-\mathbf{z}_{i}^{t+1})||_{2}^{2}

We apply the same linearization to the local rotation matrices as we do to the global rotation matrix.
Connectivity has to be defined among the points of the source surface, resulting a graph (or a mesh specifically) with graph Laplacian
\mathbf{L}
.
The neighbours of point
i
are denoted by
\mathcal{N}(i)
.

2.1

We have to minimize the following function with respect to
\tilde{\mathbf{r}}
,
\tilde{\mathbf{t}}
,
\tilde{\mathbf{r}}_{j}

\mathbf{z}_{j}^{t+1}
:

\displaystyle\argmin_{\tilde{\mathbf{r}},\tilde{\mathbf{t}},\tilde{\mathbf{r}}_{j},\mathbf{z}_{j}^{t+1}}

\displaystyle w_{1}\sum_{i=1}^{N}||\Pi_{\mathcal{Y}}(\mathbf{z}_{i}^{t+1})-\mathbf{z}_{i}^{t+1}||_{2}^{2}+w_{2}\sum_{i=1}^{N}||\mathbf{x}_{i}^{t}+\tilde{\mathbf{r}}\times\mathbf{x}_{i}^{t}+\tilde{\mathbf{t}}-\mathbf{z}_{i}^{t+1}||_{2}^{2}+

\displaystyle+

\sum_{i=1}^{N}\mathbf{x}_{i}^{t}\times(\mathbf{x}_{i}^{t}+\tilde{\mathbf{r}}\times\mathbf{x}_{i}^{t}+\tilde{\mathbf{t}}-\mathbf{z}_{i}^{t+1})=\mathbf{0}

-\sum_{i=1}^{N}\mathbf{X}_{i}^{t}\mathbf{X}_{i}^{t}\tilde{\mathbf{r}}+\sum_{i=1}^{N}\mathbf{X}_{i}^{t}\tilde{\mathbf{t}}-\sum_{i=1}^{N}\mathbf{X}_{i}^{t}\mathbf{z}_{i}^{t+1}=\mathbf{0}

•

Minimizing w.r.t.
\tilde{\mathbf{t}}

\frac{\partial}{\partial\tilde{\mathbf{t}}}\sum_{i=1}^{N}||\mathbf{x}_{i}^{t}+\tilde{\mathbf{r}}\times\mathbf{x}_{i}^{t}+\tilde{\mathbf{t}}-\mathbf{z}_{i}^{t+1}||_{2}^{2}=0

\sum_{i=1}^{N}\mathbf{x}_{i}^{t}+\tilde{\mathbf{r}}\times\mathbf{x}_{i}^{t}+\tilde{\mathbf{t}}-\mathbf{z}_{i}^{t+1}=\mathbf{0}

-\sum_{i=1}^{N}\mathbf{X}_{i}^{t}\tilde{\mathbf{r}}+N\tilde{\mathbf{t}}-\sum_{i=1}^{N}\mathbf{z}_{i}^{t+1}=-\sum_{i=1}^{N}\mathbf{x}_{i}^{t}

•

Minimizing w.r.t.
\tilde{\mathbf{r}}_{j}
:

Minimizing w.r.t.
\mathbf{z}_{j}^{t+1}
:

\displaystyle\frac{\partial}{\partial\mathbf{z}_{j}^{t+1}}(

\displaystyle w_{1}\sum_{i=1}^{N}||\Pi_{\mathcal{Y}}(\mathbf{z}_{i}^{t})-\mathbf{z}_{i}^{t+1}||_{2}^{2}+w_{2}\sum_{i=1}^{N}||\mathbf{x}_{i}^{t}+\tilde{\mathbf{r}}\times\mathbf{x}_{i}^{t}+\tilde{\mathbf{t}}-\mathbf{z}_{i}^{t+1}||_{2}^{2}+

\displaystyle+

3
Accelerating the convergence: point-to-plane error

In this case, we also measure the distance from the tangent plane belonging to the closest point, adding an extra term with weighting factor
w_{4}
:

w_{4}(\mathbf{n}_{i}^{T}(\Pi_{\mathcal{Y}}(\mathbf{z}_{i}^{t})-\mathbf{z}_{i}^{t+1}))^{2}

where
\mathbf{n}_{i}
is the surface normal at point
i
. The gradient of this term w.r.t
\mathbf{z}_{j}^{t+1}
is

2w_{4}(\mathbf{n}_{i}\circ\mathbf{n}_{i})(\mathbf{z}_{i}^{t+1}-\Pi_{\mathcal{Y}}(\mathbf{z}_{i}^{t}))^{2}
