A dissipative logarithmic-Laplacian type of plate equation: asymptotic profile and decay rates Thanks: ikehatar@hiroshima-u.ac.jp
Ruy Coimbra Charão, Alessandra Piske

Abstract
We introduce a new model of the logarithmic type of wave like plate equation with a nonlocal logarithmic damping mechanism. We consider the Cauchy problem for this new model in 𝐑 n {\bf R}^{n} , and study the asymptotic profile and optimal decay rates of solutions as t → ∞ t\to\infty in L 2 L^{2} -sense. The operator L L considered in this paper was first introduced to dissipate the solutions of the wave equation in the paper studied by Charão-Ikehata [ 5 ] . We will discuss the asymptotic property of the solution as time goes to infinity to our Cauchy problem, and in particular, we classify the property of the solutions into three parts from the viewpoint of regularity of the initial data, that is, diffusion-like, wave-like, and both of them.

We consider in this work a new model of evolution equations based on an operator
L
, that combines the composition of logarithm function with the Laplace operator as follows:

\displaystyle u_{tt}+Lu+(I+L)^{-1}u_{t}=0,\ \ \ (t,x)\in(0,\infty)\times{\bf R}^{n},

(1.1)

\displaystyle u(0,x)=u_{0}(x),\quad u_{t}(0,x)=u_{1}(x),\ \ \ x\in{\bf R}^{n},

(1.2)

where the linear operator

L:D(L)\subset L^{2}({\bf R}^{n})\to L^{2}({\bf R}^{n})

is defined by

D(L):=\left\{f\in L^{2}({\bf R}^{n})\,\bigm|\,\int_{{\bf R}^{n}}(\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{2}|\hat{f}(\xi)|^{2}d\xi<+\infty\right\},

and for
f\in D(L)
,

(Lf)(x):={\cal F}_{\xi\to x}^{-1}\left(\log(1+|\xi|^{2})\hat{f}(\xi)\right)(x).

Here, one has just denoted the Fourier transform
{\cal F}_{x\to\xi}(f)(\xi)
of
f(x)
by

{\cal F}_{x\to\xi}(f)(\xi)=\hat{f}(\xi):=\displaystyle{\int_{{\bf R}^{n}}}e^{-ix\cdot\xi}f(x)dx,\quad\xi\in{\bf R}^{n},

as usual with
i:=\sqrt{-1}
, and
{\cal F}_{\xi\to x}^{-1}
expresses its inverse Fourier transform.
Since the operator
L
is non-negative and self-adjoint in
L^{2}({\bf R}^{n})
(see
[
5
]
), the square root

L^{1/2}:D(L^{1/2})\subset L^{2}({\bf R}^{n})\to L^{2}({\bf R}^{n})

can be defined, and is also nonnegative and self-adjoint with its domain

D(L^{1/2})=\left\{f\in L^{2}({\bf R}^{n})\,\bigm|\,\int_{{\bf R}^{n}}\log(1+|\xi|^{2})|\hat{f}(\xi)|^{2}d\xi<+\infty\right\}.

Note that
D(L^{1/2})
becomes Hilbert space with its graph norm

\|v\|_{D(L^{1/2})}:=\left(\|v\|^{2}+\|L^{1/2}v\|^{2}\right)^{1/2},

where the
L^{2}({\bf R}^{n})
-norm is defined by

\|\cdot\|:=\|\cdot\|_{L^{2}({\bf R})}.

It is easy to check that

H^{s}({\bf R}^{n})\hookrightarrow D(L^{1/2})\hookrightarrow L^{2}({\bf R}^{n})

for
s>0
.

Symbolically writing, one can see

L=\log(I-\Delta),

where
\Delta
is the usual Laplace operator defined on
H^{2}({\bf R}^{n})
.

As for the existence of the unique solution to problem (
1.1
)-(
1.2
) we discuss, on the next section, by employing the standard Lumer-Phillips Theorem.

The associated energy inequality to the system (
1.1
)-(
1.2

E_{u}(t)\leq E_{u}(0),

(1.3)

where

E_{u}(t):=\frac{1}{2}\left(\|u_{t}(t,\cdot)\|_{L^{2}}^{2}+\|\log^{1/2}(I-\Delta)u(t,\cdot)\|_{L^{2}}^{2}\right).

The inequality (
1.3
) implies that the the total energy is a non increasing function in time because of the existence of some kind of dissipative term
(I+L)^{-1}u_{t}

In order to introduce our main results we should define function spaces with respect to the logarithmic Laplace operator
L
such that for
\delta\geq 0

Y^{\delta}=\{f\in L^{2}({\bf R}^{n});\int_{{\bf R}_{\xi}^{n}}(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{\delta}|\hat{f}(\xi)|^{2}d\xi<\infty\}

\|f\|_{Y^{\delta}}:=\left(\int_{{\bf R}_{\xi}^{n}}(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{\delta}|\hat{f}(\xi)|^{2}d\xi\right)^{1/2}

for
f\in Y^{\delta}
.

Remark 1.1

Due to the fact that
\log(1+|\xi|^{2})\leq|\xi|^{2}
for all
\xi\in{\bf R}^{n}
, one notices
H^{\delta}({\bf R}^{n})\subset Y^{\delta}\subset L^{2}
for
\delta\geq 0

Furthermore, we set

I_{0,l}:=\sqrt{\|u_{0}\|_{1,1}^{2}+\|u_{1}\|_{1,1}^{2}+\|u_{0}\|_{Y^{l+1}}^{2}+\|u_{1}\|_{Y^{l}}^{2}}\quad(l\geq 0),

and

P_{j}:=\int_{{\bf R}^{n}}u_{j}(x)dx\quad(j=0,1).

Our results read as follows.

Theorem 1.1

Let
n\geq 1
and
l\geq 1
. If
(u_{0},u_{1})\in(L^{1,1}({\bf R}^{n})\cap Y^{l+1})\times(L^{1,1}({\bf R}^{n})\cap Y^{l})
, then there exists a constant
C>0
, which is independent of
t,u_{0},u_{1}
such that

\|u(t,\cdot)-{\cal F}_{\xi\rightarrow x}^{-1}(\varphi_{1}(t,\xi))(\cdot)\|_{2}

\leq\left\{\begin{matrix}CI_{0,l}^{2}t^{-\frac{n+2}{4}}&\text{ if }l\geq 1\text{ and }n\leq 2;\text{ if }n\geq 3\text{ and }l\geq n/2,\\
CI_{0,l}^{2}t^{-\frac{l+1}{2}}&\text{ if }n\geq 4\text{ and }n/2-1<l\leq n/2;\text{ if }n=3\text{ and }1\leq l\leq 3/2,\end{matrix}\right.

for
t\gg 1
, where

\varphi_{1}(t,\xi):=e^{-t\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))}(P_{0}+P_{1}).

Theorem 1.2

Let
n\geq 5
and
l\geq 1
. If
(u_{0},u_{1})\in(L^{1,1}({\bf R}^{n})\cap Y^{l+1})\times(L^{1,1}({\bf R}^{n})\cap Y^{l})
, then there exists a constant
C>0
, which is independent of
t,u_{0},u_{1}
such that

\|u(t,\cdot)-{\cal F}_{\xi\rightarrow x}^{-1}(\varphi_{2}(t,\xi))(\cdot)\|_{2}

\leq\left\{\begin{matrix}CI_{0,l}^{2}t^{-\frac{n}{4}}&\text{if }1\leq l<n/2-1\text{ and }5\leq n\leq 8;\text{ if }n>8\text{ and }n/2-3<l<n/2-1,\\
CI_{0,l}^{2}t^{-\frac{l+3}{2}}&\text{ if }n>8\text{ and }1\leq l\leq n/2-3,\end{matrix}\right.

for
t\gg 1
, where

\varphi_{2}(t,\xi):=e^{-\frac{t}{2\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}\frac{\sin(\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}t)}{\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}\hat{u}_{1}(\xi)+e^{-\frac{t}{2\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}\cos(\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}t)\hat{u}_{0}(\xi).

Theorem 1.3

Let
n\geq 4
and
l=\frac{n}{2}-1

(u_{0},u_{1})\in(L^{1,1}({\bf R}^{n})\cap Y^{l+1})\times(L^{1,1}({\bf R}^{n})\cap Y^{l})

In Theorems above one has assumed

Lemma 2.4

Let

2.1
Existence and Uniqueness

We note that the Cauchy problem (
1.1
)-(
1.2
) is equivalent to

\left\{\begin{matrix}(I+L)u_{tt}+L(I+L)u+u_{t}=0,\\
u(0,x)=u_{0}(x),\\
u_{t}(0,x)=u_{1}(x).\end{matrix}\right.

(2.2)

By taking the inner product of the equation in (
2.2
) by
u_{t}
, we obtain

\frac{1}{2}\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}\left(\|u_{t}(t,\cdot)\|^{2}+\|L^{1/2}u_{t}(t,\cdot)\|^{2}+\|Lu(t,\cdot)\|^{2}+\|L^{1/2}u(t,\cdot)\|^{2}\right)+\|u_{t}(t,\cdot)\|^{2}=0.

We define the total energy as

E(t):=\|u_{t}(t,\cdot)\|^{2}+\|L^{1/2}u_{t}(t,\cdot)\|^{2}+\|Lu(t,\cdot)\|^{2}+\|L^{1/2}u(t,\cdot)\|^{2}.

Then, we can observe that
E(t)
is a non-increasing function.

Associated to (
2.2
) one can choose the following energy space

X=Y^{2}\times Y^{1}.

Now, at least formally, from (
2.2
) one can write

u_{tt}=-(I+L)^{-1}(L^{2}+L)u-(I+L)^{-1}u_{t}=-(I+L)^{-1}(L^{2}+L+I)u-(I+L)^{-1}(u_{t}-u).

Then if we define
v=u_{t}
and
U(t)=\begin{pmatrix}u\\
v\end{pmatrix}
we can reduce the second order equation of (
2.2
) to a system of the first order as follows:

\displaystyle\frac{\mathrm{d}U}{\mathrm{d}t}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\begin{pmatrix}u_{t}\\
-(I+L)^{-1}(L^{2}+L+I)u-(I+L)^{-1}(u_{t}-u)\end{pmatrix}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\begin{pmatrix}0&I\\
-(I+L)^{-1}(L^{2}+L+I)&0\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}u\\
v\end{pmatrix}+\begin{pmatrix}0\\
(I+L)^{-1}(u-v)\end{pmatrix}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\begin{pmatrix}0&I\\
-A&0\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}u\\
v\end{pmatrix}+\begin{pmatrix}0\\
(I+L)^{-1}(u-v)\end{pmatrix}.

Thus, the first order evolution equation to
U
can be written as

\frac{\mathrm{d}U}{\mathrm{d}t}=BU+JU,\quad U(0)=(u_{0},u_{1}),

(2.3)

where formally the operator
A
is given by

Lemma 2.7

(2.4)

Proof.

Lemma 2.8

D(A)\subset Y^{3}
and there exists

Proof.
Let

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle|(y,\psi)|+|(L^{1/2}y,L^{1/2}\psi)|\leq\|y\|\|\psi\|+\|L^{1/2}y\|\|L^{1/2}\psi\|

\displaystyle\|\hat{y}\|\|\hat{\psi}\|+\|(\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{1/2}\hat{y}\|\|(\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{1/2}\hat{\psi}\|

\displaystyle\frac{1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}{4}|\hat{u}_{t}|^{2}+\rho(\xi)^{2}(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))|\hat{u}|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}{4}|\hat{u}_{t}|^{2}+\frac{1}{4}\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))|\hat{u}|^{2}.

Thus, the last estimate implies

\displaystyle E(t,\xi)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle E_{0}(t,\xi)+\rho(\xi)(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))\text{Re}(\hat{u}_{t}\overline{\hat{u}})+\frac{\rho(\xi)}{2}|\hat{u}|^{2}

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle E_{0}(t,\xi)+\rho(\xi)(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))\text{Re}(\hat{u}_{t}\overline{\hat{u}})

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\left(\frac{1}{2}-\frac{1}{4}\right)(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))|\hat{u}_{t}|^{2}+\left(\frac{1}{2}-\frac{1}{4}\right)\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))|\hat{u}|^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{4}(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))|\hat{u}_{t}|^{2}+\frac{1}{4}\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))|\hat{u}|^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}E_{0}(t,\xi),

for
t>0
and
\xi\in{\bf R}_{\xi}^{n}
. These imply the desired estimates.

\Box

Now, we need the next lemma.

Lemma 3.3

It is true that

\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}E(t,\xi)+\frac{\rho(\xi)}{2}E(t,\xi)\leq 0,\text{ }t>0,\text{ }\xi\in{\bf R}_{\xi}^{n}.

Proof.
By definitions of
E(t,\xi)
,
F(t,\xi)
,
R(t,\xi)
,
\rho(\xi)
and (

\displaystyle\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}E(t,\xi)+\frac{\rho(\xi)}{2}E(t,\xi)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle R(t,\xi)-F(t,\xi)+\frac{\rho(\xi)}{2}E(t,\xi)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle R(t,\xi)-F(t,\xi)+\frac{3\rho(\xi)}{2}E_{0}(t,\xi)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left(\frac{7}{4}\rho(\xi)(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))-1\right)|\hat{u}_{t}|^{2}

\displaystyle-

\displaystyle\frac{1}{4}\rho(\xi)\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))|\hat{u}|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle-\frac{1}{8}|\hat{u}_{t}|^{2}-\frac{1}{4}\rho(\xi)\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))|\hat{u}|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle 0

for
t>0
and
{\bf R}_{\xi}^{n}
. This implies the desired statement.

4
Asymptotic profile of solutions

Applying the Fourier transform on the problem (
2.2
) one obtain the associated problem in Fourier space:

\displaystyle(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))\hat{u}_{tt}+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))\hat{u}+\hat{u}_{t}=0,

\displaystyle\hat{u}(0,\xi)=\hat{u}_{0}(\xi),

(4.1)

\displaystyle\hat{u}_{t}(0,\xi)=\hat{u}_{1}(\xi).

The characteristic roots of the associated polynomial to the equation in (
4
) are given by

\lambda_{\pm}=\frac{-1\pm\sqrt{1-4\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{2}}}{2(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))}.

We observe that there exists a unique real number
\delta>0
such that

1-4\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{2}\left\{\begin{matrix}\geq 0&\text{ for}|\xi|\leq\delta,\\
<0&\text{ for}|\xi|>\delta.\end{matrix}\right.

(4.2)

In fact, the function
f(r)=1-4\log(1+r^{2})(1+\log(1+r^{2}))^{2}
is decreasing for
r\geq 0
, continuous and

f(0)=1,f(1)=1-4\log 2\cdot(1+\log 2)^{2}<0.

Therefore, by the mean value theorem there exists a unique number
\delta\in(0,1)
that satisfies (
4.2
). The same theorem guarantees us the existence of
0<\eta<\delta
such that

\frac{1}{2}\leq\sqrt{1-4\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{2}}\leq 1,

(4.3)

whenever
|\xi|\leq\eta.

The next lemma is very important to get sharp estimates. In the following notation
A\approx B
means that
c_{1}A\leq B\leq c_{2}A
for some positive constants
c_{1},c_{2}
.

Lemma 4.1

It holds that

(i).

\lambda_{+}\approx-\log(1+|\xi|^{2})
,

(ii).

\lambda_{-}\approx-1
,

(iii).

\lambda_{+}+\lambda_{-}\approx-1
,

whenever
|\xi|\leq\delta
. And, in particular, in the case of
|\xi|\leq\eta
, one has

(iv).

\lambda_{+}-\lambda_{-}\approx 1.

Remark 4.1

Note that the constants
c_{1}
and
c_{2}
appeared in Lemma
4.1
may depend on
\delta
or
\eta
. We also note that the four items in Lemma
4.1
simultaneously hold on the zone
\{|\xi|\leq\eta\}
because of
\eta<\delta
.

Proof.

(i).

First, we observe that
0\leq 4\log^{2}(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{4}
for all
\xi\in{\bf R}_{\xi}^{n}
. For this reason, one has

\displaystyle 1-4\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\left[1-2\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{2}\right]^{2}.

And, for
|\xi|\leq\delta
we have

\displaystyle\sqrt{1-4\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{2}}\leq 1-2\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{2}.

This estimate implies that

\lambda_{+}=\frac{-1+\sqrt{1-4\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{2}}}{2(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))}\leq-\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})).

Now, because of
1\leq 1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})
we obtain the upper bound to
\lambda_{+}
in the case of
|\xi|\leq\delta
:

\lambda_{+}\leq-\log(1+|\xi|^{2}).

On the other hand, for
|\xi|\leq\delta
, one has
0\leq 1-4\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{2}\leq 1
, so that

\displaystyle 1-4\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{2}\leq\sqrt{1-4\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{2}}.

Hence one can get the following lower bound on
\lambda_{+}
such that

-2\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))\leq\frac{-1+\sqrt{1-4\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{2}}}{2(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))}=\lambda_{+}.

Therefore, we can get

-2K_{\delta}\log(1+|\xi|^{2})\leq\lambda_{+},\quad|\xi|\leq\delta,

with
K_{\delta}=:1+\log(1+\delta^{2})
.
These imply the desired statement of (i).

(ii).

Since
0\leq\sqrt{1-4\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{2}}\leq 1
in the region
|\xi|\leq\delta
,

\frac{-1}{1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}\leq\frac{-1-\sqrt{1-4\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{2}}}{2(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))}\leq\frac{-1}{2(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))}.

Therefore,

-1\leq\lambda_{-}\leq\frac{-1}{2K_{\delta}}.

(iii).

To prove this item we observe that
\lambda_{+}+\lambda_{-}=\frac{-1}{1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}
. Hence,

-1\leq\lambda_{+}+\lambda_{-}\leq-\frac{1}{K_{\delta}},

for
|\xi|\leq\delta
. And we obtain the equivalence
\lambda_{+}+\lambda_{-}\approx-1.

(iv).

We observe that we have chosen
\eta>0
in (
4.3
) such that

\frac{1}{2}\leq\sqrt{1-4\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{2}}\leq 1

for all
|\xi|\leq\eta.

Thus, one can deduce

\frac{1}{2K_{\delta}}\leq\frac{1}{2(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))}\leq\frac{\sqrt{1-4\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{2}}}{1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}\leq\frac{1}{1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}\leq 1.

This estimate shows the desired equivalence
\lambda_{+}-\lambda_{-}\approx 1.

\displaystyle=\int_{|\xi|\leq\eta}(1+|\xi|^{2})^{-2t}|A_{j}(\xi)-iB_{j}(\xi)|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle=\omega_{n}(L+M)^{2}\|u_{j}\|_{1,1}^{2}\int_{0}^{\eta}(1+r^{2})^{-2t}r^{n+1}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq\omega_{n}(L+M)^{2}\|u_{j}\|_{1,1}^{2}\int_{0}^{1}(1+r^{2})^{-2t}r^{n+1}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq C\|u_{j}\|_{1,1}^{2}t^{-\frac{n+2}{2}}

for
t\gg 1
due to Lemma
2.1
. Consequently, for
t\gg 1
we have

\int_{|\xi|\leq\eta}|F_{1}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi\leq C\|u_{0}\|_{1,1}^{2}t^{-\frac{n+2}{2}}\quad\mbox{ and }\quad\int_{|\xi|\leq\eta}|F_{2}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi\leq C\|u_{1}\|_{1,1}^{2}t^{-\frac{n+2}{2}}.

In order to get an estimate on the function
F_{3}(t,\xi)
we also use the simple inequality
\log(1+r^{2})\leq r^{2}
for all
r\geq 0
. Then, by using the items (i) and (iv) of Lemma
2.6
we obtain that

\displaystyle\int_{|\xi|\leq\eta}|F_{3}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle=\int_{|\xi|\leq\eta}e^{-2t\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))}\left(\frac{\lambda_{+}}{\lambda_{+}-\lambda_{-}}\right)^{2}|\hat{u}_{0}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq C\int_{|\xi|\leq\eta}e^{-2t\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}\log^{2}(1+|\xi|^{2})|\hat{u}_{0}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq C\omega_{n}\|u_{0}\|_{1}^{2}\int_{0}^{\eta}(1+r^{2})^{-2t}r^{n+3}dr

\displaystyle\leq C\omega_{n}\|u_{0}\|_{1}^{2}\int_{0}^{1}(1+r^{2})^{-t}r^{n+3}dr

\displaystyle\leq C\omega_{n}\|u_{0}\|_{1}^{2}t^{-\frac{n+4}{2}},\quad t\gg 1,

where one has just used that
|\hat{u}_{0}(\xi)|\leq\|u_{0}\|_{1}
for all
\xi\in\mbox{\boldmath$R$}^{n}
and the simple fact that

\left|\frac{\lambda_{\pm}}{\lambda_{+}-\lambda_{-}}\right|\leq C,\quad|\xi|\leq\eta,

with
C>0
a constant.

To estimate the function
F_{4}(t,\xi)
we need the elementary inequality

|e^{-a}-1|\leq|a|,\quad a\geq 0.

(4.9)

Thus, we have

\displaystyle\int_{|\xi|\leq\eta}|F_{4}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle=\int_{|\xi|\leq\eta}e^{-2t\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))}\left(\frac{\lambda_{-}}{\lambda_{-}-\lambda_{+}}\right)^{2}\left(e^{-t(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))\lambda_{+}^{2}}-1\right)^{2}|\hat{u}_{0}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq C\int_{|\xi|\leq\eta}e^{-2t\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))}\left(e^{-t(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))\lambda_{+}^{2}}-1\right)^{2}|\hat{u}_{0}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq Ct^{2}\int_{|\xi|\leq\eta}e^{-2t\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))}(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{2}\lambda_{+}^{4}|\hat{u}_{0}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq Ct^{2}\|u_{0}\|_{1}^{2}\int_{|\xi|\leq\eta}(1+|\xi|^{2})^{-2t}\log^{4}(1+|\xi|^{2})d\xi

\displaystyle\leq Ct^{2}\|u_{0}\|_{1}^{2}\int_{|\xi|\leq\eta}(1+|\xi|^{2})^{-2t}|\xi|^{8}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq\omega_{n}Ct^{2}\|u_{0}\|_{1}^{2}t^{-\frac{n+8}{2}}

\displaystyle=C_{n}\|u_{0}\|_{1}^{2}t^{-\frac{n+4}{2}},\quad t\gg 1

because of
1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})\leq 1+\log 2
, for
|\xi|\leq\eta<1
, where we also used the fact that
|\lambda_{+}|\leq C\log(1+|\xi|^{2})
for
|\xi|\leq\eta
. The constant
C_{n}>0
depends only on
n
.

Now, let
D=1-4\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{2}
. Then we observe that
D\geq 0
for
|\xi|\leq\eta
and

\displaystyle 1-(\lambda_{+}-\lambda_{-})=\frac{1}{1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}\frac{2\log(1+|\xi|^{2})+\log^{2}(1+|\xi|^{2})+4\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{2}}{1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})+\sqrt{D}}.

In particular,
1-(\lambda_{+}-\lambda_{-})
is positive and there exists a constant
C_{\eta}>0
such that

|1-(\lambda_{+}-\lambda_{-})|\leq C_{\eta}\log(1+|\xi|^{2})

for all
|\xi|\leq\eta
, where
\eta
is defined in (
4.3
).

At this point we use the inequalities (
4.9
) and
(a-b)^{2}\leq 2a^{2}+2b^{2}
to get
the next estimate to the remainder function
F_{5}(t,\xi)
.

\displaystyle\int_{|\xi|\leq\eta}

\displaystyle|F_{5}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi=\int_{|\xi|\leq\eta}e^{-2t\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))}\frac{\left(e^{-t(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))\lambda_{+}^{2}}-(\lambda_{+}-\lambda_{-})\right)^{2}}{(\lambda_{+}-\lambda_{-})^{2}}|\hat{u}_{1}|d\xi

\displaystyle\leq 2\int_{|\xi|\leq\eta}e^{-2t\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))}\frac{\left(e^{-t(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))\lambda_{+}^{2}}-1\right)^{2}}{(\lambda_{+}-\lambda_{-})^{2}}|\hat{u}_{1}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle+2\int_{|\xi|\leq\eta}e^{-2t\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))}\frac{\left(1-(\lambda_{+}-\lambda_{-})\right)^{2}}{(\lambda_{+}-\lambda_{-})^{2}}|\hat{u}_{1}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq C\int_{|\xi|\leq\eta}e^{-2t\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))}\frac{t^{2}(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{2}\lambda_{+}^{4}}{(\lambda_{+}-\lambda_{-})^{2}}|\hat{u}_{1}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle+2C_{\eta}\int_{|\xi|\leq\eta}e^{-2t\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))}\log^{2}(1+|\xi|^{2})|\hat{u}_{1}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq Ct^{2}\int_{|\xi|\leq\eta}e^{-t\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}\log^{4}(1+|\xi|^{2})|\hat{u}_{1}|^{2}d\xi+2C_{\eta}\int_{|\xi|\leq\eta}e^{-t\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}\log^{2}(1+|\xi|^{2})|\hat{u}_{1}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq Ct^{2}\|u_{1}\|_{1}^{2}\int_{|\xi|\leq 1}(1+|\xi|^{2})^{-t}|\xi|^{8}d\xi+2C_{\eta}\|u_{1}\|_{1}^{2}\int_{|\xi|\leq 1}(1+|\xi|^{2})^{-t}|\xi|^{4}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq C_{n}\|u_{1}\|_{1}^{2}t^{-\frac{n+4}{2}}+2C_{\eta,n}\|u_{1}\|_{1}^{2}t^{-\frac{n+4}{2}},\quad t\gg 1.

Finally, by (ii) of Lemma
4.1
one has
\lambda_{-}\approx-1
on the region
|\xi|\leq\eta
, so that there exists constants
c_{1},c_{2}>0
such that
c_{1}\leq 2(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))\lambda_{-}^{2}\leq c_{2}
whenever
|\xi|\leq\eta
. Therefore, it follows that

\displaystyle\int_{|\xi|\leq\eta}|F_{6}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle=\int_{|\xi|\leq\eta}e^{-2t\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))}H_{2}^{2}(t,\xi)d\xi

\displaystyle\leq\int_{|\xi|\leq\eta}e^{-2t\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))}e^{-2t(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))\lambda_{-}^{2}}\frac{1}{(\lambda_{-}-\lambda_{+})^{2}}|\hat{u}_{1}|d\xi

\displaystyle+\int_{|\xi|\leq\eta}e^{-2t\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))}e^{-2t(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))\lambda_{-}^{2}}\left(\frac{\lambda_{+}}{\lambda_{-}-\lambda_{+}}\right)^{2}|\hat{u}_{0}|d\xi

\displaystyle\leq Ce^{-c_{2}t}\int_{|\xi|\leq\eta}e^{-t\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}|\hat{u}_{1}|^{2}d\xi+Ce^{-c_{2}t}\int_{|\xi|\leq\eta}e^{-t\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}\log^{2}(1+|\xi|^{2})|\hat{u}_{0}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq Ce^{-c_{2}t}\|u_{1}\|_{1}^{2}\int_{|\xi|\leq\eta}(1+|\xi|^{2})^{-t}d\xi+Ce^{-c_{2}t}\|u_{0}\|_{1}^{2}\int_{|\xi|\leq\eta}e^{-t\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}|\xi|^{4}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq C\|u_{1}\|_{1}^{2}t^{-\frac{n}{2}}e^{-c_{2}t}+C\|u_{0}\|_{1}^{2}t^{-\frac{n+4}{2}}e^{-c_{2}t},\qquad t\gg 1.

By combining the above estimates for
F_{j}(t,\xi),\;j=1,\cdots,6,
with equation (
4.8
), we obtain that the solution
\hat{u}(t,\xi)
given by (
4.1
) has the following asymptotic property.

Proposition 4.1

Let
n\geq 1
and
\eta>0
given by (
4.3
). For
(u_{0},u_{1})\in L^{1,1}({\bf R}^{n})\times L^{1,1}({\bf R}^{n})
the solution
\hat{u}(t,\xi)
to problem (
4
) satisfies

\int_{|\xi|\leq\eta}|\hat{u}(t,\xi)-\varphi_{1}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi\leq C\Big(\|u_{0}\|_{1,1}^{2}t^{-\frac{n+2}{2}}+\|u_{1}\|_{1,1}^{2}t^{-\frac{n+2}{2}}+\|u_{0}\|_{1}^{2}t^{-\frac{n+4}{2}}

+\|u_{1}\|_{1}^{2}t^{-\frac{n+4}{2}}+\|u_{1}\|_{1}^{2}t^{-\frac{n+2}{2}}+\|u_{1}\|_{1}^{2}t^{-\frac{n}{2}}e^{-c_{2}t}+\|u_{0}\|_{1}^{2}t^{-\frac{n+4}{2}}e^{-c_{2}t}\Big),\quad t\gg 1

where
\varphi_{1}(t,\xi)
is defined in (
4.7
) and
C
is a positive constant that depends only on
\eta
and
n
.

\square

(ii) Estimates on the low-middle frequency zone
\eta\leq|\xi|\leq\delta
:

When one obtains sharp estimates on the region middle frequency zone
\eta\leq|\xi|\leq\delta
it should be noted that according to (
4.2
) the characteristics roots
\lambda_{\pm}
are still real-valued. Then we can rewrite the solution
\hat{u}(t,\xi)
as follows.

\displaystyle\hat{u}(t,\xi)

\displaystyle=e^{-\frac{t}{2(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))}}\left[\cosh(c(\xi)t)\hat{u}_{0}+\frac{\sinh(c(\xi)t)}{2(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))c(\xi)}\hat{u}_{0}+\frac{\sinh(c(\xi)t)}{c(\xi)}\hat{u}_{1}\right]

where

c(\xi):=\frac{\sqrt{1-4\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{2}}}{2(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))}.

We remember that
\delta
is given in (
4.2
) and our choice for
\eta
is such that

\sqrt{1-4\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{2}}\geq\frac{1}{2}

when
|\xi|\leq\eta
(see (
4.3
)), and this is the decreasing function on
|\xi|
. Thus, in the case for
\eta\leq|\xi|\leq\delta
, one has

c(\xi)\leq\frac{1}{4(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))}.

Then, due to the fact that
\cosh a\leq e^{a}
for all
a\geq 0
we may estimate for
t>0

\displaystyle\int_{\eta\leq|\xi|\leq\delta}e^{-\frac{t}{1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}\cosh^{2}(c(\xi)t)|\hat{u}_{0}(\xi)|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq\int_{\eta\leq|\xi|\leq\delta}e^{-\frac{t}{1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}e^{2c(\xi)t}|\hat{u}_{0}(\xi)|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq\int_{\eta\leq|\xi|\leq\delta}e^{-\frac{t}{1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}e^{\frac{t}{2(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))}}|\hat{u}_{0}(\xi)|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle=\int_{\eta\leq|\xi|\leq\delta}e^{-\frac{t}{2(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))}}|\hat{u}_{0}(\xi)|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq e^{-C_{\delta}t}\int_{\eta\leq|\xi|\leq\delta}|\hat{u}_{0}(\xi)|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq e^{-C_{\delta}t}\|u_{0}\|_{2}^{2},

with
C_{\delta}=\frac{1}{2(1+\log(1+\delta^{2}))}
.

By combining Lemma
2.5
with ideas used to get the previous estimate, we can obtain
L^{2}
-estimates for the other two terms of the solution
\hat{u}(t,\xi)
in the middle frequency zone. In fact, we have

\displaystyle\int_{\eta\leq|\xi|\leq\delta}e^{-\frac{t}{1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}\frac{\sinh^{2}(c(\xi)t)}{c(\xi)^{2}}|\hat{u}_{1}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq\int_{\eta\leq|\xi|\leq\delta}e^{-\frac{t}{1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}t^{2}e^{2c(\xi)t}|\hat{u}_{1}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq t^{2}e^{-C_{\delta}t}\|u_{1}\|_{2}^{2},

and

\displaystyle\int_{\eta\leq|\xi|\leq\delta}e^{-\frac{t}{1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}\frac{\sinh^{2}(c(\xi)t)}{4(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{2}c(\xi)^{2}}|\hat{u}_{0}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq t^{2}\int_{\eta\leq|\xi|\leq\delta}\frac{e^{-\frac{t}{1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}}{4(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{2}}e^{2c(\xi)t}|\hat{u}_{0}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq C_{\eta}t^{2}e^{-C_{\delta}t}\|u_{0}\|_{2}^{2}

for all
t>0
. Therefore, the following estimate on the middle frequency zone holds for
t>0

\displaystyle\int_{\eta\leq|\xi|\leq\delta}|\hat{u}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq\int_{\eta\leq|\xi|\leq\delta}e^{-\frac{t}{1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}\cosh^{2}(c(\xi)t)|\hat{u}_{0}(\xi)|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle+\int_{\eta\leq|\xi|\leq\delta}e^{-\frac{t}{1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}\frac{\sinh^{2}(c(\xi)t)}{c(\xi)^{2}}|\hat{u}_{1}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle+\int_{\eta\leq|\xi|\leq\delta}e^{-\frac{t}{1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}\frac{\sinh^{2}(c(\xi)t)}{4(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{2}c(\xi)^{2}}|\hat{u}_{0}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq e^{-C_{\delta}t}\|u_{0}\|_{2}^{2}+C_{\eta}t^{2}e^{-C_{\delta}t}\|u_{0}\|_{2}^{2}+t^{2}e^{-C_{\delta}t}\|u_{1}\|_{2}^{2}.

(4.10)

By summarizing the above estimates in the middle frequency zone one can conclude the next result.

Lemma 4.2

Let
n\geq 1
and
u_{0},u_{1}\in L^{2}({\bf R}^{n})
. Then the solution
\hat{u}(t,\xi)
to the problem (
4
) satisfies

\int_{\eta\leq|\xi|\leq\delta}|\hat{u}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi\leq e^{-C_{\delta}t}\|u_{0}\|_{2}^{2}+C_{\eta}t^{2}e^{-C_{\delta}t}\|u_{0}\|_{2}^{2}+t^{2}e^{-C_{\delta}t}\|u_{1}\|_{2}^{2}

(4.11)

for
t>0
.

\square

4.2
Estimates on the high frequency zone
|\xi|\geq\delta

On the zone of high frequency the characteristics roots are complex-valued (see
4.2
), and are given by

\lambda_{\pm}=-a(\xi)\pm ib(\xi),

where

a(\xi)=\frac{1}{2(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))}\text{\; and\; }b(\xi)=\frac{\sqrt{4\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{2}-1}}{2(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))}.

(4.12)

Then the solution
\hat{u}(t,\xi)
to problem (
4
) in the high frequency zone is explicitly given by

\hat{u}(t,\xi)=e^{-a(\xi)t}\cos(b(\xi)t)\hat{u}_{0}+\frac{a(\xi)}{b(\xi)}e^{-a(\xi)t}\sin(b(\xi)t)\hat{u}_{0}+\frac{1}{b(\xi)}e^{-a(\xi)t}\sin(b(\xi)t)\hat{u}_{1}.

(4.13)

(i) Estimate on the high-middle frequency zone
\delta\leq|\xi|\leq\sqrt{e-1}
.

In this region, the function
a(\xi)
is equivalent to a constant, that is
\frac{1}{4}\leq a(\xi)\leq\frac{1}{2}.

Moreover, we can see that
\displaystyle{\frac{1}{b(\xi)}}
converges to
+\infty
when
|\xi|\rightarrow\delta^{+}
according to (
4.2
). However, we remember that
\sin a\leq a
for all
a\geq 0
. Thus

\sin(b(\xi)t)\leq b(\xi)t

for all
\xi\in{\bf R}^{n}
and
t\geq 0
.
By combining these properties together with the solution formula (
4.13
) one can obtain the following estimate for
t>0
, which implies the exponential decay in such region.

\int_{\delta\leq|\xi|\leq\sqrt{e-1}}|\hat{u}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi\leq e^{-\frac{t}{2}}\|u_{0}\|_{2}^{2}+\frac{1}{4}t^{2}e^{-\frac{t}{2}}\|u_{0}\|_{2}^{2}+t^{2}e^{-\frac{t}{2}}\|u_{1}\|_{2}^{2}.

(4.14)

(ii) Estimate on the high frequency zone
|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}

The estimates on this zone are more delicate and the derivation is one of essential contributions in our paper. We first need to rewrite the solution formula given by (
4.13
) into a more suitable expression.

First we observe that for
|\xi|\geq\delta
, in particular, for
|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}
, it holds that

b(\xi)\leq\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}.

Then the mean value theorem implies, for
|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}
, that

\cos(b(\xi)t)=\cos(\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}t)-\sin(\theta(t,\xi))\left[b(\xi)-\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}\right]t,

with
\theta(t,\xi)=\alpha b(\xi)t+(1-\alpha)\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}t
for some
\alpha\in(0,1)
.

Similarly,

\sin(b(\xi)t)=\sin(\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}t)+\cos(\eta(t,\xi))\left[b(\xi)-\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}\right]t,

with
\eta(t,\xi)=\gamma b(\xi)t+(1-\gamma)\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}t
for some
\gamma\in(0,1)
.

Thus, one can rewrite
\hat{u}(t,\xi)
as follows:

\displaystyle\hat{u}(t,\xi)

\displaystyle=e^{-a(\xi)t}\cos(\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}t)\hat{u}_{0}+te^{-a(\xi)t}\sin(\theta(\xi,t)\left[\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}-b(\xi)\right]\hat{u}_{0}

\displaystyle+\frac{a(\xi)}{b(\xi)}e^{-a(\xi)t}\sin(b(\xi)t)\hat{u}_{0}+\frac{1}{b(\xi)}e^{-a(\xi)t}\sin(\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}t)\hat{u}_{1}

\displaystyle+te^{-a(\xi)t}\frac{1}{b(\xi)}\cos(\eta(\xi,t))\left[b(\xi)-\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}\right]\hat{u}_{1}.

(4.15)

We introduce an important function to be the asymptotic profile on the zone of high frequency for the solution
\hat{u}(t,\xi)
given by (
4.2
) as follows

\displaystyle\varphi_{2}(t,\xi):=e^{-\frac{t}{2\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}\frac{\sin(\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}t)}{\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}\hat{u}_{1}(\xi)+e^{-\frac{t}{2\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}\cos(\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}t)\hat{u}_{0}(\xi).

(4.16)

In the following part, one will prove that the function
\varphi_{2}(t,\xi)
is asymptotic profile for the solution
\hat{u}(t,\xi)
in the high frequency region
|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}
. Then we obtain the following difference between the solution and the profile

\displaystyle\hat{u}(t,\xi)-\varphi_{2}(t,\xi)

\displaystyle=\left(e^{-a(\xi)t}-e^{-\frac{t}{2\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}\right)\cos(\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}t)\hat{u}_{0}+\frac{a(\xi)}{b(\xi)}e^{-a(\xi)t}\sin(b(\xi)t)\hat{u}_{0}

\displaystyle+te^{-a(\xi)t}\sin(\theta(\xi,t))\left[\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}-b(\xi)\right]\hat{u}_{0}

\displaystyle+e^{-a(\xi)t}\left(\frac{1}{b(\xi)}-\frac{1}{\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}\right)\sin(\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}t)\hat{u}_{1}

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}\left(e^{-a(\xi)t}-e^{-\frac{t}{2\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}\right)\sin(\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}t)\hat{u}_{1}

\displaystyle+te^{-a(\xi)t}\frac{1}{b(\xi)}\cos(\eta(\xi,t))\left[b(\xi)-\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}\right]\hat{u}_{1}.

(4.17)

Then, the following functions

\displaystyle G_{1}(t,\xi)=\left(e^{-a(\xi)t}-e^{-\frac{t}{2\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}\right)\cos(\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}t)\hat{u}_{0},

\displaystyle G_{2}(t,\xi)=te^{-a(\xi)t}\sin(\theta(\xi,t))\left[\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}-b(\xi)\right]\hat{u}_{0},

\displaystyle G_{3}(t,\xi)=\frac{a(\xi)}{b(\xi)}e^{-a(\xi)t}\sin(b(\xi)t)\hat{u}_{0},

\displaystyle G_{4}(t,\xi)=e^{-a(\xi)t}\left(\frac{1}{b(\xi)}-\frac{1}{\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}\right)\sin(\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}t)\hat{u}_{1},

\displaystyle G_{5}(t,\xi)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}\left(e^{-a(\xi)t}-e^{-\frac{t}{2\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}\right)\sin(\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}t)\hat{u}_{1},

\displaystyle G_{6}(t,\xi)=te^{-a(\xi)t}\frac{1}{b(\xi)}\cos(\eta(\xi,t))\left[b(\xi)-\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}\right]\hat{u}_{1}

are the remainder terms that appear in (
4.2
).

From now, let us estimates these
6
-remainders in the following lines.

We note that on the region such that
|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}
one has
1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})\leq 2\log(1+|\xi|^{2})
. Also, by Lemma
2.4
with
c=1
and
a=-1
one has

\frac{e^{\frac{-t}{1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}}{(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{\nu}}\leq Ct^{-\nu},\quad t>0,\;\;\xi\in{\bf R}^{n},

(4.18)

for a fixed
\nu>0
. The above two inequalities will be used to get the next series of estimates for the functions
G_{j}(t,\xi),\,j=1\cdots,6
.

The first estimate is concerned with the function
G_{1}(t,\xi)
.

\displaystyle\int_{|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}}|G_{1}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle=\int_{|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}}\left(e^{-a(\xi)t}-e^{-\frac{t}{2\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}\right)^{2}\cos^{2}(\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}t)|\hat{u}_{0}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle=\int_{|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}}\hskip-5.69046pte^{\frac{-t}{1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}\left(1-e^{\frac{-t}{2\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))}}\right)^{2}\cos^{2}(\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}t)|\hat{u}_{0}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq\int_{|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}}e^{\frac{-t}{1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}\left(1-e^{\frac{-t}{2\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))}}\right)^{2}|\hat{u}_{0}|^{2}d\xi.

The next inequalities conclude the estimate to the function
G_{1}(t,\xi)
.

\displaystyle\int_{|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}}|G_{1}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq t^{2}\int_{|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}}\frac{e^{\frac{-t}{1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}}{4\log^{2}(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{2}}|\hat{u}_{0}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq t^{2}\int_{|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}}\frac{e^{\frac{-t}{1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}}{(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{4}}|\hat{u}_{0}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle=t^{2}\int_{|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}}\frac{e^{\frac{-t}{1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}}{(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{5+l}}(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{l+1}|\hat{u}_{0}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq Ct^{2}t^{-(l+5)}\int_{|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}}(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{l+1}|\hat{u}_{0}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq Ct^{-(l+3)}\|u_{0}\|_{Y^{l+1}}^{2},

for all
t>0
and
l\geq 0
, where we have used the inequalities (
4.9
), (
4.18
) and the fact that
\log(1+|\xi|^{2})\geq 1
on the high frequency zone.

For
|\xi|\geq\delta
, we introduce the auxiliary function
R(t,\xi)
defined by

\displaystyle R(t,\xi)=\sqrt{1-\frac{1}{4\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{2}}},

(4.19)

which is well defined due to (
4.2
). Additionally, one notes that for
|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}
we have the following estimate

\displaystyle|\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}-b(\xi)|

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{4(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{2}\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}\left(1+R(t,\xi)\right)}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{4(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{2}\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}.

Thus for
t>0
and
l\geq 0
we get

\displaystyle\int_{|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}}|G_{2}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle=t^{2}\int_{|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}}e^{-2a(\xi)t}\sin^{2}(\theta(\xi,t))\left[\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}-b(\xi)\right]^{2}|\hat{u}_{0}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq t^{2}\int_{|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}}\frac{e^{-\frac{t}{1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}}{16(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{4}\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}|\hat{u}_{0}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq t^{2}\int_{|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}}\frac{e^{-\frac{t}{1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}}{8(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{5}}|\hat{u}_{0}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq t^{2}\int_{|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}}\frac{e^{-\frac{t}{1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}}{8(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{6+l}}(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{l+1}|\hat{u}_{0}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle=Ct^{2}t^{-(6+l)}\int_{|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}}(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{l+1}|\hat{u}_{0}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq Ct^{-(l+4)}\|u_{0}\|_{Y^{l+1}}^{2},

where one has just used the fact that
1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})\geq 2
for
|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}
and (
4.18
).

Another important property is that the function
|\xi|\mapsto\displaystyle{\frac{1}{4\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{2}}}
is decreasing, and it converges to zero as
|\xi|\rightarrow+\infty
. Hence, it follows that

\displaystyle 1\leq\frac{1}{1-\frac{1}{4\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{2}}}=\frac{1}{R(t,\xi)^{2}}\leq\frac{16}{15}

(4.20)

for
|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}
.

From the above inequality one can obtain estimates of the
L^{2}-
norms of each functions
G_{3}(t,\cdot)
,
G_{4}(t,\cdot)
and
G_{5}(t,\cdot)
for
t>0
. In fact, (
4.20
) implies that

\displaystyle\int_{|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}}|G_{3}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle=\int_{|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}}\left(\frac{a(\xi)}{b(\xi)}\right)^{2}e^{-2a(\xi)t}\sin^{2}(b(\xi)t)|\hat{u}_{0}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle=\int_{|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}}\frac{e^{-\frac{t}{1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}\sin^{2}(b(\xi)t)|\hat{u}_{0}|^{2}}{4\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{2}R(t,\xi)^{2}}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq\frac{16}{15}\int_{|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}}\frac{e^{-\frac{t}{1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}}{4\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{2}}|\hat{u}_{0}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq\frac{8}{15}\int_{|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}}\frac{e^{-\frac{t}{1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}}{(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{3}}|\hat{u}_{0}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle=\frac{8}{15}\int_{|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}}\frac{e^{-\frac{t}{1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}}{(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{4+l}}(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{l+1}|\hat{u}_{0}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq Ct^{-(l+4)}\int_{|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}}(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{l+1}|\hat{u}_{0}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq Ct^{-(4+l)}\|u_{0}\|_{Y^{l+1}}^{2},

where we have used the fact that
4\log(1+|\xi|^{2})\geq 2+2\log(1+|\xi|^{2})
for
|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}
.

To get an estimate for the
L^{2}
-norm of
G_{4}(t,\cdot)
we first observe the following identity:

\displaystyle\frac{1}{b(\xi)}-\frac{1}{\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}=\frac{1}{4\log^{3/2}(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{2}R(t,\xi)\left(1+R(t,\xi)\right)}.

holds for
|\xi|\geq\delta
, where
R(t,\xi)
is given by (
4.19
).

By using the above identity, the estimate (
4.20
) and the fact that
1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})\leq 2\log(1+|\xi|^{2})
for
|\xi|\leq\sqrt{e-1}
, we can arrive at the following
L^{2}
-estimate to the function
G_{4}(t,\cdot)
:

\displaystyle\int_{|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}}|G_{4}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle=\int_{|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}}e^{-2a(\xi)t}\left(\frac{1}{b(\xi)}-\frac{1}{\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}\right)^{2}\sin^{2}(\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}t)|\hat{u}_{1}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{15}\int_{|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}}\frac{e^{-\frac{t}{(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))}}}{\log^{3}(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{4}}\sin^{2}(\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}t)|\hat{u}_{1}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq\frac{8}{15}\int_{|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}}\frac{e^{-\frac{t}{(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))}}}{(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{7}}|\hat{u}_{1}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq Ct^{-(l+7)}\|u_{1}\|_{Y^{l}}^{2}.

Similarly to the previous estimate for
G_{1}(t,\cdot)
one obtains

\displaystyle\int_{|\xi\geq\sqrt{e-1}}|G_{5}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle=\int_{|\xi\geq\sqrt{e-1}}\frac{1}{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}\left(e^{-\frac{t}{1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}-e^{-\frac{t}{2\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}\right)^{2}\sin^{2}(\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}t)|\hat{u}_{1}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle=\int_{|\xi\geq\sqrt{e-1}}\frac{e^{-\frac{t}{1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}}{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}\left(1-e^{-\frac{t}{2\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))}}\right)^{2}\sin^{2}(\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}t)|\hat{u}_{1}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq t^{2}\int_{|\xi\geq\sqrt{e-1}}\frac{e^{-\frac{t}{1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}}{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}\frac{1}{4\log^{2}(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{2}}|\hat{u}_{1}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq 2t^{2}\int_{|\xi\geq\sqrt{e-1}}\frac{e^{-\frac{t}{1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}}{(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{5}}|\hat{u}_{1}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq Ct^{-(l+3)}\|u_{1}\|_{Y^{l}}^{2}.

Finally, we observe that

\displaystyle\frac{b(\xi)-\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}{b(\xi)}=

\displaystyle\frac{-1}{4\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{2}R(t,\xi)(1+R(t,\xi))}

for
|\xi|\geq\delta
, where
R(t,\xi)
is given by (
4.19
). Thus, for
|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}
it holds that

\left|\frac{b(\xi)-\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}{b(\xi)}\right|^{2}\leq\frac{1}{15\log^{2}(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{4}}.

Hence, one has

\displaystyle\int_{|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}}|G_{6}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle=t^{2}\int_{|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}}\left(\frac{b(\xi)-\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}{b(\xi)}\right)^{2}e^{-\frac{t}{1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}\cos^{2}(\eta(\xi,t))|\hat{u}_{1}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{15}t^{2}\int_{|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}}\frac{e^{-\frac{t}{1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}}{\log^{2}(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{4}}\cos^{2}(\eta(\xi,t))|\hat{u}_{1}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq\frac{4}{15}t^{2}\int_{|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}}\frac{e^{-\frac{t}{1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}}{(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{6}}|\hat{u}_{1}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq Ct^{-(l+4)}\|u_{1}\|_{Y^{l}}^{2},\quad t>0.

The estimates for
G_{j}(t,\xi)
(
j=1,\cdots,6
) together with the identity (
4.2
) provide the following result.

Proposition 4.2

Let
n\geq 1
,
l\geq 0
and
(u_{0},u_{1})\in Y^{l+1}\times Y^{l}
. Then there exists a positive constant
C
, which is independent of
t,u_{0}
and
u_{1}
, such that

\displaystyle\int_{|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}}

\displaystyle\left|\hat{u}(t,\xi)-\varphi_{2}(t,\xi)\right|^{2}d\xi\leq C\left(\|u_{0}\|_{Y^{l+1}}^{2}+\|u_{1}\|_{Y^{l}}^{2}\right)t^{-(l+3)}

for
t\geq 0
, where
\varphi_{2}(t,\xi)
is given by (
4.16
).

\square

4.3
Estimates on the whole space
{\bf R}^{n}

In this subsection, we consider three special functions:
\varphi_{1}(t,\xi)
,
\varphi_{2}(t,\xi)
, which are given by (
4.7
) and (
4.16
), and

\displaystyle\varphi(\xi,t)

\displaystyle=\varphi_{1}(t,\xi)+\varphi_{2}(t,\xi)

defined for
\xi\in{\bf R}_{\xi}^{n}
.

We will prove that, under certain conditions, each of them is an asymptotic profile as
t\rightarrow\infty
of the solution
\hat{u}(t,\xi)
in
{\bf R}_{\xi}^{n}
.

Lemma 4.3

Let
n\geq 1
and
(u_{0},u_{1})\in(L^{1,1}({\bf R}^{n})\cap Y^{l+1})\times(L^{1,1}({\bf R}^{n})\cap Y^{l})
. Then there exists a constant
C>0
, which is independent of
t,u_{0},u_{1}
such that

\displaystyle\int_{{\bf R}^{n}}|\hat{u}(t,\xi)-\varphi(t,\xi)

\displaystyle|^{2}d\xi\leq C\left(t^{-\frac{n+2}{2}}+t^{-(l+3)}\right)I_{0,l}^{2}

for
t\gg 1
, where

I_{0,l}:=\sqrt{\|u_{0}\|_{1,1}^{2}+\|u_{1}\|_{1,1}^{2}+\|u_{0}\|_{Y^{l+1}}^{2}+\|u_{1}\|_{Y^{l}}^{2}}.

(4.21)

Proof.
On the region
|\xi|\leq\sqrt{e-1}
, the function
\log(1+|\xi|^{2})
is positive and bounded by
1
, then for
t\geq 0
it holds that

\frac{-t}{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}\leq-t.

We also have
\sin a\leq a
for all
a\geq 0
. Having this in mind we can get, for
t\geq 0
, the estimates

\displaystyle\int_{|\xi|\leq\sqrt{e-1}}|\varphi_{2}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq 2\int_{|\xi|\leq\sqrt{e-1}}e^{-\frac{t}{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}\frac{\sin^{2}(\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}t)}{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}|\hat{u}_{1}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle+2\int_{|\xi|\leq 1}e^{-\frac{t}{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}\cos^{2}(\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}t)|\hat{u}_{0}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq 2t^{2}e^{-t}\int_{|\xi|\leq\sqrt{e-1}}|\hat{u}_{1}|^{2}d\xi+2e^{-t}\int_{|\xi|\leq 1}|\hat{u}_{0}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq 2t^{2}e^{-t}\|u_{1}\|_{2}^{2}+2e^{-t}\|u_{0}\|_{2}^{2}.

(4.22)

On the other hand, one knows that

e^{-2t\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))}\leq e^{-2t\log(1+|\xi|^{2})},

because of
1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})\geq 1
. Then

\displaystyle\int_{\eta\leq|\xi|}|\varphi_{1}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle=|P_{0}+P_{1}|^{2}\int_{|\xi|\geq\eta}e^{-2t\log(1+|\xi|^{2})(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq|P_{0}+P_{1}|^{2}\int_{|\xi|\geq\eta}(1+|\xi|^{2})^{-2t}d\xi

\displaystyle=|P_{0}+P_{1}|^{2}\int_{\eta\leq|\xi|\leq 1}(1+|\xi|^{2})^{-2t}d\xi+|P_{0}+P_{1}|^{2}\int_{|\xi|\geq 1}(1+|\xi|^{2})^{-2t}d\xi

\displaystyle=\omega_{n}|P_{0}+P_{1}|^{2}\int_{\eta}^{1}(1+r^{2})^{-2t}r^{n-1}dr+\omega_{n}|P_{0}+P_{1}|^{2}\int_{1}^{\infty}(1+r^{2})^{-2t}r^{n-1}dr

\displaystyle\leq C|P_{0}+P_{1}|^{2}\left((1+\eta^{2})^{-t}+\frac{2^{-t}}{t-1}\right)

\displaystyle\leq C\left(\|u_{0}\|_{1}^{2}+\|u_{1}\|_{1}^{2}\right)\left((1+\eta^{2})^{-t}+\frac{2^{-t}}{t-1}\right)

with a generous constant
C>0
for
t\gg 1
due to Lemmas
2.2
and
2.3
. We also note that both above estimates are of exponential type.

Under these preparations we can get the desired estimate in the statement. At first, one notices that

\displaystyle|\hat{u}(t,\xi)-\varphi(t,\xi)|

\displaystyle=|\hat{u}(t,\xi)-\varphi_{1}(t,\xi)-\varphi_{2}(t,\xi)|\leq|\hat{u}(t,\xi)-\varphi_{1}(t,\xi)|+|\varphi_{2}(t,\xi)|.

So, it holds that

\displaystyle|\hat{u}(t,\xi)-\varphi(t,\xi)|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq 2|\hat{u}(t,\xi)-\varphi_{1}(t,\xi)|^{2}+2|\varphi_{2}(t,\xi)|^{2}

(4.23)

Similarly,

\displaystyle|\hat{u}(t,\xi)-\varphi(t,\xi)|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq 2|\hat{u}(t,\xi)-\varphi_{2}(t,\xi)|^{2}+2|\varphi_{1}(t,\xi)|^{2}.

(4.24)

Also, one has

\displaystyle|\hat{u}(t,\xi)-\varphi(t,\xi)|\leq|\hat{u}(t,\xi)|+|\varphi_{1}(t,\xi)|+|\varphi_{2}(t,\xi)|.

By applying Young’s inequality, we obtain

\displaystyle|\hat{u}(t,\xi)-\varphi(t,\xi)|^{2}\leq 2|\hat{u}(t,\xi)|^{2}+4|\varphi_{1}(t,\xi)|^{2}+4|\varphi_{2}(t,\xi)|^{2}.

(4.25)

Let us apply the estimates (
4.3
) on the region
|\xi|\leq\eta
, (
4.24
) on the region
|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}
and (
4.3
) on the middle frequency region
\eta\leq|\xi|\leq\sqrt{e-1}
, respectively. Then one can proceed the estimates as follows.

\displaystyle\int_{{\bf R}^{n}}|\hat{u}(t,\xi)-\varphi(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle=\int_{|\xi|\leq\eta}|\hat{u}(t,\xi)-\varphi(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi+\int_{\eta\leq|\xi|\leq\sqrt{e-1}}|\hat{u}(t,\xi)-\varphi(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle+\int_{|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}}|\hat{u}(t,\xi)-\varphi(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq 2\int_{|\xi|\leq\eta}|\hat{u}(t,\xi)-\varphi_{1}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi+2\int_{|\xi|\leq\eta}|\varphi_{2}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle+2\int_{\eta\leq|\xi|\leq\sqrt{e-1}}|\hat{u}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi+4\int_{\eta\leq|\xi|\leq\sqrt{e-1}}|\varphi_{1}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle+4\int_{\eta\leq|\xi|\leq\sqrt{e-1}}|\varphi_{2}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi+2\int_{|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}}|\hat{u}(t,\xi)-\varphi_{2}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle+2\int_{|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}}|\varphi_{1}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq 2\int_{|\xi|\leq\eta}|\hat{u}(t,\xi)-\varphi_{1}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi+2\int_{|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}}|\hat{u}(t,\xi)-\varphi_{2}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle+4\int_{|\xi|\leq\eta}|\varphi_{2}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi+4\int_{|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}}|\varphi_{1}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle+2\int_{\eta\leq|\xi|\leq\sqrt{e-1}}|\hat{u}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi+4\int_{\eta\leq|\xi|\leq\sqrt{e-1}}|\varphi_{1}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle+4\int_{\eta\leq|\xi|\leq\sqrt{e-1}}|\varphi_{2}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle=2\int_{|\xi|\leq\eta}|\hat{u}(t,\xi)-\varphi_{1}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi+2\int_{|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}}|\hat{u}(t,\xi)-\varphi_{2}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle+4\int_{|\xi|\leq\sqrt{e-1}}|\varphi_{2}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi+4\int_{|\xi|\geq\eta}|\varphi_{1}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle+2\int_{\eta\leq|\xi|\leq\sqrt{e-1}}|\hat{u}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi.

(4.26)

Furthermore, Propositions 4.1 and 4.2 tell us that

\displaystyle\int_{|\xi|\leq\eta}|\hat{u}(t,\xi)-\varphi_{1}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi\leq CI_{0}^{2}t^{-\frac{n+2}{2}},

\displaystyle\int_{|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}}|\hat{u}(t,\xi)-\varphi_{2}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi\leq CI_{0}^{2}t^{-(l+3)}

for
t\gg 1
. By combining the previous two estimates with Propositions
4.1
and
4.2
and Lemma
4.2
we can conclude the desired estimate

\int_{{\bf R}^{n}}|\hat{u}(t,\xi)-\varphi(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi\leq\tilde{C}I_{0}^{2}(t^{-\frac{n+2}{2}}+t^{-(l+3)})\quad t\gg 1.

\Box

Lemma 4.4

Let
u_{0},u_{1}\in L^{1}({\bf R}^{n})
and the function
\varphi_{1}(t,\xi)
is defined in (
4.7
). Then there exists positive constants
C_{1},C_{2}
, depending only on dimension
n
, such that

C_{1}|P_{0}+P_{1}|^{2}t^{-\frac{n}{2}}\leq\int_{{\bf R}_{\xi}^{n}}|\varphi_{1}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi\leq C_{2}(\|u_{0}\|_{1}^{2}+\|u_{1}\|_{1}^{2})t^{-\frac{n}{2}}

(4.27)

for
t\gg 1
.

Proof.
The function
\varphi_{1}(t,\xi)
satisfies

|\varphi_{1}(t,\xi)|\leq|P_{0}+P_{1}|(1+|\xi|^{2})^{-t}

for
\xi\in{\bf R}_{\xi}^{n}
, since
1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})\geq 1.

By using Lemmas
2.1
and
2.2
, we immediately concluded that

\displaystyle\int_{{\bf R}_{\xi}^{n}}|\varphi_{1}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq|P_{0}+P_{1}|^{2}\int_{{\bf R}_{\xi}^{n}}(1+|\xi|^{2})^{-2t}d\xi

\displaystyle=\omega_{n}|P_{0}+P_{1}|^{2}\int_{0}^{1}(1+r^{2})^{-2t}r^{n-1}dr+\omega_{n}|P_{0}+P_{1}|^{2}\int_{1}^{\infty}(1+r^{2})^{-2t}r^{n-1}dr

\displaystyle\leq C\omega_{n}|P_{0}+P_{1}|^{2}\left(t^{-\frac{n}{2}}+\frac{2^{-t}}{t-1}\right)

\displaystyle\leq C\omega_{n}(\|u_{0}\|_{1}^{2}+\|u_{1}\|_{1}^{2})t^{-\frac{n}{2}}

for
t\gg 1
.

On the other hand, for
|\xi|\leq\eta
, we have
1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})\leq 1+\log(1+|\eta|^{2})=k_{\eta}
. Thus,

|\varphi(t,\xi)|\geq|P_{0}+P_{1}|(1+|\xi|^{2})^{-k_{\eta}t}

for
|\xi|\leq\eta
. First, we choose
t_{0}>0
such that, for all
t>t_{0}
it holds that
t^{-\frac{1}{2}}\leq\eta
, and

\frac{1}{e^{4k_{\eta}}}\leq\left(1+\frac{1}{t}\right)^{-2k_{\eta}t}\leq 1.

Such
t_{0}
exists, because one has

\lim_{t\rightarrow\infty}\left(1+\frac{1}{t}\right)^{-2k_{\eta}t}=\frac{1}{e^{2k_{\eta}}}.

For this choice, we can compute as follows:

\displaystyle\int_{{\bf R}_{\xi}^{n}}|\varphi_{1}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\geq\int_{|\xi|\leq\eta}|\varphi_{1}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi\geq\omega_{n}|P_{0}+P_{1}|^{2}\int_{0}^{\eta}(1+r^{2})^{-2k_{\eta}t}r^{n-1}dr

\displaystyle\geq\omega_{n}|P_{0}+P_{1}|^{2}\int_{0}^{t^{-\frac{1}{2}}}(1+r^{2})^{-2k_{\eta}t}r^{n-1}dr

\displaystyle\geq\omega_{n}|P_{0}+P_{1}|^{2}\left(1+\frac{1}{t}\right)^{-2k_{\eta}t}\int_{0}^{t^{-\frac{1}{2}}}r^{n-1}dr

\displaystyle=\frac{\omega_{n}}{n}|P_{0}+P_{1}|^{2}\left(1+\frac{1}{t}\right)^{-2k_{\eta}t}t^{-\frac{n}{2}}

\displaystyle\geq\frac{\omega_{n}e^{-4k_{\eta}}}{n}|P_{0}+P_{1}|^{2}t^{-\frac{n}{2}}

for
t>t_{0}
.

\Box

Lemma 4.5

Let
n\geq 1
,
l\geq 0
and
(u_{0},u_{1})\in Y^{l+1}\times Y^{l}
. Then there exists a constant
C>0
, which is independent of
u_{0},u_{1}
and
t
, such that

\int_{{\bf R}_{\xi}^{n}}|\varphi_{2}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi\leq CI_{0,l}^{2}t^{-(l+1)}

for all
t>0
, where
I_{0,l}
is given in (
4.21
).

Proof.
By definition of
\varphi_{2}(t,\xi)
in (
4.16
), we have

\displaystyle|\varphi_{2}(t,\xi)|\leq e^{-\frac{t}{2\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}\frac{|\sin(\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}t)|}{\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}|\hat{u}_{1}|+e^{-\frac{t}{2\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}|\cos(\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}t)||\hat{u}_{0}|.

Hence, the Young’s inequality enable us to get

\displaystyle|\varphi_{2}(t,\xi)|^{2}\leq 2e^{-\frac{t}{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}\frac{\sin^{2}(\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}t)}{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}|\hat{u}_{1}|^{2}+2e^{-\frac{t}{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}\cos^{2}(\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}t)|\hat{u}_{0}|^{2}.

(4.28)

It follows from (
4.3
), we get

\displaystyle\int_{|\xi|\leq\sqrt{e-1}}|\varphi_{2}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq 2t^{2}e^{-t}\|u_{1}\|_{2}^{2}+2e^{-t}\|u_{0}\|_{2}^{2},\quad t>0.

(4.29)

On the high frequency zone
|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}
it holds that

\displaystyle\frac{1}{1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}\leq\frac{1}{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}\leq\frac{2}{1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}.

(4.30)

By using the inequality (
4.30
) and the estimates (
4.18
) and (
4.28
), one can obtain

\displaystyle\int_{|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}}|\varphi_{2}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq 2\int_{|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}}e^{-\frac{t}{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}\frac{\sin^{2}(\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}t)}{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}|\hat{u}_{1}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle+2\int_{|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}}e^{-\frac{t}{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}\cos^{2}(\sqrt{\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}t)|\hat{u}_{0}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq 4\int_{|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}}e^{-\frac{t}{1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}\frac{1}{1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}|\hat{u}_{1}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle+2\int_{|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}}e^{-\frac{t}{1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}|\hat{u}_{0}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle=4\int_{|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}}\frac{e^{-\frac{t}{1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}}{(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{l+1}}(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{l}|\hat{u}_{1}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle+2\int_{|\xi|\geq\sqrt{e-1}}\frac{e^{-\frac{t}{1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2})}}}{(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{l+1}}(1+\log(1+|\xi|^{2}))^{l+1}|\hat{u}_{0}|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq 4Ct^{-(l+1)}\|u_{1}\|_{Y^{l}}^{2}+2Ct^{-(l+1)}\|u_{0}\|_{Y^{l+1}}^{2},\quad t>0.

(4.31)

By combining estimates (
4.29
) and (
4.3
) one can conclude

\int_{{\bf R}_{\xi}^{n}}|\varphi_{2}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi\leq Ct^{-(l+1)}(\|u_{1}\|_{Y^{l}}^{2}+\|u_{0}\|_{Y^{l+1}}^{2}),\quad t\gg 1

for some generous constant
C>0
, independent of
u_{0},u_{1}
and
t
.

\Box

4.4
Proof of Theorems
1.1
,
1.2
and
1.3

Now, let us prove our main Theorems
1.1
,
1.2
, and
1.3
at a stroke in the following paragraph.

First, it follows from Lemma
4.3
we have the estimate

\int_{{\bf R}_{\xi}^{n}}|\hat{u}(t,\xi)-\varphi(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi\leq CI_{0}^{2}\left(t^{-\frac{n+2}{2}}+t^{-(l+3)}\right)=:P_{n}(t),

(4.32)

where
I_{0}
is defined by (
4.21
).

Next, since we can write as
\hat{u}(t,\xi)-\varphi_{1}(t,\xi)=\hat{u}(t,\xi)-\varphi(t,\xi)+\varphi_{2}(t,\xi)
, and
\hat{u}(t,\xi)-\varphi_{2}(t,\xi)=\hat{u}(t,\xi)-\varphi(t,\xi)+\varphi_{1}(t,\xi)
, from Lemmas
4.5
and
4.4
one has

\int_{{\bf R}_{\xi}^{n}}|\hat{u}(t,\xi)-\varphi_{1}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi\leq 2\int_{{\bf R}_{\xi}^{n}}|\hat{u}(t,\xi)-\varphi(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi+2\int_{{\bf R}_{\xi}^{n}}|\varphi_{2}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi

\leq 2CI_{0}^{2}\left(t^{-\frac{n+2}{2}}+t^{-(l+3)}+t^{-(l+1)}\right)\leq 4CI_{0}^{2}\left(t^{-\frac{n+2}{2}}+t^{-(l+1)}\right)=:M_{n}(t),

(4.33)

and

\int_{{\bf R}_{\xi}^{n}}|\hat{u}(t,\xi)-\varphi_{2}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi\leq 2\int_{{\bf R}_{\xi}^{n}}|\hat{u}(t,\xi)-\varphi(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi+2\int_{{\bf R}_{\xi}^{n}}|\varphi_{1}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi

\leq 2CI_{0}^{2}\left(t^{-\frac{n+2}{2}}+t^{-(l+3)}+t^{-\frac{n}{2}}\right)\leq 4CI_{0}^{2}\left(t^{-(l+3)}+t^{-\frac{n}{2}}\right)=:Q_{n}(t).

(4.34)

By an asymptotic profile we mean the part of
\hat{u}(t,\xi)
that decays with the slowest time rate. According to Lemmas
4.4
and
4.5
we know that

\int_{{\bf R}_{\xi}^{n}}|\varphi_{1}(t,\xi)|^{2}\leq CI_{0}^{2}t^{-\frac{n}{2}},\quad\int_{{\bf R}_{\xi}^{n}}|\varphi_{2}(t,\xi)|^{2}\leq CI_{0}^{2}t^{-(l+1)},\quad\int_{{\bf R}_{\xi}^{n}}|\varphi(t,\xi)|^{2}\leq CI_{0}^{2}(t^{-\frac{n}{2}}+t^{-(l+1)}).

Therefore, the asymptotic profile of the solution
\hat{u}(t,\xi)
as
t\to+\infty
is

(i).

\varphi_{1}(t,\xi)
if
\frac{n}{2}<l+1
(compare with (
4.33
)),

(ii).

\varphi(t,\xi)
if
\frac{n}{2}=l+1
(compare with (
4.32
)),

(iii).

\varphi_{2}(t,\xi)
if
\frac{n}{2}>l+1
(compare with (
4.34
)).

In the final part of this subsection we will discuss the decay rate of
P_{n}(t),M_{n}(t)
and
Q_{n}(t)
related to the differences between the solution
\hat{u}(t,\xi)
and the suitable asymptotic profiles.

Let
l\geq 1
as this is necessary for the existence and uniqueness of the solution (see Theorem 2.2). For this purpose we introduce a (critical) value
l^{*}(n)
on the regularity
l\geq 1
of the initial data such that

l^{*}(n):=\frac{n}{2}-1.

Let us prove Theorems
1.1
,
1.2
and
1.3
as follows.

(i).

The case for
l^{*}(n)<l
.

First, we consider
l=1
. In this case,
\varphi_{1}(t,\xi)
is asymptotic profile for
n<4
.

\bullet
If
n\leq 2
, then
\frac{n+2}{2}\leq 2=l+1
, and so
M_{n}(t)\leq 8CI_{0}^{2}t^{-\frac{n+2}{2}}
.

\bullet
If
n=3
, then
2=l+1<\frac{n+2}{2}=\frac{5}{2}
. Thus
M_{n}(t)\leq 8CI_{0}^{2}t^{-(l+1)}=8CI_{0}^{2}t^{-2}
.

Now let us consider the case
l>1
.

\bullet
In this case, the rate
t^{-(l+1)}
is better than
t^{-2}
. Therefore, if
n\leq 2
, we have
M_{n}(t)\leq 8CI_{0}^{2}t^{-\frac{n+2}{2}}
.

\bullet
If
n>2
and
\frac{n}{2}\leq l
, then
l>1
and
M_{n}(t)\leq 8CI_{0}^{2}t^{-\frac{n+2}{2}}
.

\bullet
If
n\geq 4
and
\frac{n}{2}-1<l<\frac{n}{2}
, we obtain
l>1
and
M_{n}(t)\leq 8CI_{0}^{2}t^{-(l+1)}
.

\bullet
For
n=3
, we need
1<l\leq\frac{3}{2}
, in order that
M_{n}(t)\leq 8CI_{0}^{2}t^{-(l+1)}
.

These observations together with the Plancherel Theorem imply the desired statement of Theorem
1.1
.

(ii).

The case for
l^{*}(n)>l
.

If
l=1
,
\varphi_{2}(t,\xi)
is asymptotic profile for
n>4
.

\bullet
If
4<n\leq 8
, then
\frac{n}{2}\leq 4=l+3
. So
Q_{n}(t)\leq 8CI_{0}^{2}t^{-\frac{n}{2}}
.

\bullet
For
n>8
, we have
\frac{n}{2}>4=l+3
and
Q_{n}(t)\leq 8CI_{0}^{2}t^{-(l+3)}=8CI_{0}^{2}t^{-4}
.

By assuming
l>1
, it is necessary that
n>2l+2>4
.

\bullet
If
4<n\leq 8
, then
\frac{n}{2}\leq 4<l+3
. Therefore,
Q_{n}(t)\leq 8CI_{0}^{2}t^{-\frac{n}{2}}
.

\bullet
For
n>8
and
\frac{n}{2}-3<l<\frac{n}{2}-1
, we have
l>1
and
Q_{n}(t)\leq 8CI_{0}^{2}t^{-\frac{n}{2}}
.

\bullet
If
n>8
and
1<l\leq\frac{n}{2}-3
, we obtain
Q_{n}(t)\leq 8CI_{0}^{2}t^{-(l+3)}
.

These observations together with the Plancherel Theorem imply the desired statement of Theorem
1.2
.

(iii).

The case for
l^{*}(n)=l
.

This condition implies that
\frac{n+2}{2}=l+2<l+3
. Then we have
P_{n}(t)\leq 2CI_{0}^{2}t^{-\frac{n+2}{2}}
. Due to
l\geq 1
, this estimate holds only for
n\geq 4
.

These observations together with the Plancherel Theorem imply the desired statement of Theorem
1.3
.

4.5
Optimal decay rates of the solution

In this section we prove Theorems
1.4
,
1.5
and
1.6
based on previously obtained decay estimates.

We have already used the decomposition such as

\hat{u}(t,\xi)=\hat{u}(t,\xi)-\varphi(t,\xi)+\varphi_{1}(t,\xi)+\varphi_{2}(t,\xi),

where
\varphi(t,\xi)=\varphi_{1}(t,\xi)+\varphi_{2}(t,\xi)
with
\varphi_{1}(t,\xi)
and
\varphi_{2}(t,\xi)
are given by (
4.7
), (
4.16
). Since
u_{0}
and
u_{1}
have the required regularity in Lemmas
4.3
,
4.4
and
4.5
, one can get

\displaystyle\int_{{\bf R}_{\xi}^{n}}|\hat{u}(t,\xi)|^{2}dx

\displaystyle\leq 4\int_{{\bf R}_{\xi}^{n}}|\hat{u}(t,\xi)-\varphi(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi+4\int_{{\bf R}_{\xi}^{n}}|\varphi_{1}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi+4\int_{{\bf R}_{\xi}^{n}}|\varphi_{2}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq 4CI_{0}^{2}(t^{-\frac{n+2}{2}}+t^{-(l+3)}+t^{-\frac{n}{2}}+t^{-(l+1)})\quad(t\gg 1)

\displaystyle\leq KI_{0}^{2}(t^{-\frac{n}{2}}+t^{-(l+1)})=:R_{n}(t)

(4.35)

with some constant
K>0
.

In the same way as we did in the previous subsection, we compare
\displaystyle{\frac{n}{2}}
and
(l+1)
in order to obtain the decay rate of the solution.

(i).

If
l\geq 1
and
n\leq 3
, then
\displaystyle{\frac{n}{2}}<2\leq l+1
. So
R_{n}(t)\leq 2KI_{0}^{2}t^{-\frac{n}{2}}
.

(ii).

If
n>4
and
l>\displaystyle{\frac{n}{2}}-1
, we have
R_{n}(t)\leq 2KI_{0}^{2}t^{-\frac{n}{2}}
.

(iii).

If
n\geq 4
and
1\leq l\leq\displaystyle{\frac{n}{2}}-1
, then
l+1\leq\displaystyle{\frac{n}{2}}
. Thus
R_{n}(t)\leq 2KI_{0}^{2}t^{-(l+1)}
.

The last item (iii) combined with the expression (
4.5
) and Plancherel Theorem completes the proof of Theorem
1.4
.

\Box

Items (i) and (ii) give us conditions for the decay rate of the solution to be better than
t^{-\frac{n}{2}}
. Furthermore, we may prove that this rate is optimal under these same conditions. Recalling the fact that the condition
(u_{0},u_{1})\in Y^{2}\times Y^{1}
is necessary for the existence and uniqueness of the solution
u(t,x)
to problem (1.1)-(1.2) according to Theorem
2.1
, we can prove Theorems
1.5
and
1.6
at a stroke.

Proof of Theorems
1.5
and
1.6
.

We first observe the case of
(u_{0},u_{1})\in Y^{l+1}\times Y^{l}
with
l>\displaystyle{\frac{n-2}{2}}
.
On the one hand,

\displaystyle|\hat{u}(t,\xi)|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\left(|\hat{u}(t,\xi)-\varphi(t,\xi)|+|\varphi(t,\xi)|\right)^{2}

\displaystyle\leq 2|\hat{u}(t,\xi)-\varphi(t,\xi)|^{2}+2|\varphi(t,\xi)|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq 4|\hat{u}(t,\xi)-\varphi(t,\xi)|^{2}+4|\varphi_{1}(t,\xi)|^{2}+4|\varphi_{2}(t,\xi)|^{2}.

So,

\displaystyle\int_{{\bf R}_{\xi}^{n}}|\hat{u}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq 4\int_{{\bf R}_{\xi}^{n}}|\hat{u}(t,\xi)-\varphi(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi+4\int_{{\bf R}_{\xi}^{n}}|\varphi_{1}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi+4\int_{{\bf R}_{\xi}^{n}}|\varphi_{2}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\leq CI_{0,l}^{2}\left(t^{-\frac{n+2}{2}}+t^{-\frac{n}{2}}+t^{-(l+1)}\right)

\displaystyle\leq CI_{0,l}^{2}t^{-\frac{n}{2}},

since
l+1>\displaystyle{\frac{n}{2}}
, due to Lemmas
4.4
,
4.5
and
4.3
. Thus, the upper bound estimates in Theorems
1.5
and
1.6
can be proved by choosing
l=1
and
l=\displaystyle{\frac{n-2}{2}}+\varepsilon
, respectively.

On the other hand, since one has
|\varphi(t,\xi)|\leq|\varphi(t,\xi)-\hat{u}(t,\xi)|+|\hat{u}(t,\xi)|
and
|\varphi_{1}(t,\xi)|\leq|\varphi_{1}(t,\xi)+\varphi_{2}(t,\xi)|+|\varphi_{2}(t,\xi)|
, by using Young’s inequality, we obtain

\displaystyle|\hat{u}(t,\xi)|^{2}

\displaystyle\geq\frac{1}{2}|\varphi(t,\xi)|^{2}-|\varphi(t,\xi)-\hat{u}(t,\xi)|^{2}

\displaystyle\geq\frac{1}{4}|\varphi_{1}(t,\xi)|^{2}-\frac{1}{2}|\varphi_{2}(t,\xi)|^{2}-|\varphi(t,\xi)-\hat{u}(t,\xi)|^{2}.

By using the estimates just obtained in Lemmas
4.4
,
4.5
and
4.3
, one can obtain the following expression:

\displaystyle\int_{{\bf R}_{\xi}^{n}}|\hat{u}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\geq\frac{1}{4}\int_{{\bf R}_{\xi}^{n}}|\varphi_{1}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi-\frac{1}{2}\int_{{\bf R}_{\xi}^{n}}|\varphi_{2}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi-\int_{{\bf R}_{\xi}^{n}}|\hat{u}(t,\xi)-\varphi(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\geq C_{1}|P_{0}+P_{1}|^{2}t^{-\frac{n}{2}}-CI_{0,l}^{2}t^{-(l+1)}-CI_{0,l}^{2}t^{-\frac{n+2}{2}}-CI_{0,l}^{2}t^{-(l+3)}

\displaystyle=t^{-\frac{n}{2}}\left(C_{1}|P_{0}+P_{1}|^{2}-CI_{0}^{2}t^{-\frac{2l-n+2}{2}}-CI_{0}^{2}t^{-1}-CI_{0}^{2}t^{-\frac{2l-n+6}{2}}\right).

(4.36)

If
\frac{n}{2}<l+1
, then
\frac{2l-n+2}{2}>0
and
\frac{2l-n+6}{2}>0
, because of
l+1<l+3
. Hence, one has

\lim_{t\rightarrow\infty}\left(CI_{0}^{2}t^{-\frac{2l-n+6}{2}}+CI_{0}^{2}t^{-1}+CI_{0}^{2}t^{-\frac{2l-n+2}{2}}\right)=0,

so that there exists
t_{1}\gg 1
such that

CI_{0}^{2}t^{-\frac{2l-n+6}{2}}+CI_{0}^{2}t^{-1}+CI_{0}^{2}t^{-\frac{2l-n+2}{2}}\leq\frac{C_{1}}{2}|P_{0}+P_{1}|^{2}

for all
t\geq t_{1}
in the case of
|P_{1}+P_{0}|\neq 0
. That is, for
t\geq t_{1}
it holds that

C_{1}|P_{0}+P_{1}|^{2}-CI_{0}^{2}t^{-\frac{2l-n+6}{2}}-CI_{0}^{2}t^{-1}-CI_{0}^{2}t^{-\frac{2l-n+2}{2}}\geq\frac{C_{1}}{2}|P_{0}+P_{1}|^{2}.

Therefore, one can arrive at the crucial estimate

\displaystyle\int_{{\bf R}_{\xi}^{n}}|\hat{u}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi

\displaystyle\geq\frac{C_{1}}{2}|P_{0}+P_{1}|^{2}t^{-\frac{n}{2}}

(4.37)

for
t\geq t_{1}
because of (
4.5
). By choosing
l=1
in Theorem
1.5
, and
l=\displaystyle{\frac{n-2}{2}}+\varepsilon
in Theorem
1.6
, one can get the desired estimates.
