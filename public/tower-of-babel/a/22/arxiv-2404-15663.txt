4-connected 1-planar chordal graphs are Hamiltonian-connected
Licheng Zhang, Yuanqiu Huang, Shengxiang Lv, Fengming Dong

Abstract
Tutte proved that 4-connected planar graphs are Hamiltonian. It is unknown if there is an analogous result on 1 1 -planar graphs. In this paper, we characterize 4-connected 1-planar chordal graphs, and show that all such graphs are Hamiltonian-connected. A crucial tool used in our proof is a characteristic of 1-planar 4-trees. Keywords: 1-planar graph, chordal graph, Hamiltonian-connected MSC: 05C10, 05C40, 05C45, 05C62
