Effective Computation of Traces, Determinants, and ζ \zeta -Functions for Sturm–Liouville Operators Thanks: K.K. was supported by the Baylor University Summer Sabbatical Program. Thanks: Appeared in J. Funct. Anal. 276 , 520–562 (2019).
Fritz Gesztesy, Klaus Kirsten

Abstract
. The principal aim in this paper is to develop an effective and unified approach to the computation of traces of resolvents (and resolvent differences), Fredholm determinants, ζ \zeta -functions, and ζ \zeta -function regularized determinants associated with linear operators in a Hilbert space. In particular, we detail the connection between Fredholm and ζ \zeta -function regularized determinants. Concrete applications of our formalism to general (i.e., three-coefficient) regular Sturm–Liouville operators on bounded intervals with various (separated and coupled) boundary conditions, and Schrödinger operators on a half-line, are provided and further illustrated with an array of examples.

The prime motivation for writing this paper originally was to detail the relationship between Fredholm

\zeta

The case of traces of resolvents (and resolvent differences), Fredholm determinants,

In Section

\displaystyle=-(d/dz)\text{\rm ln}\big({\det}_{{\mathcal{H}}}\big((A-zI_{{\mathcal{H}}})(A-z_{0}I_{{\mathcal{H}}})^{-1}\big)\big),

Next, following the proof of

Lemma 2.2
.

Assume Hypothesis
2.1

\lambda_{k}\in\sigma(A)
then

{\det}_{{\mathcal{H}}}\big(I_{{\mathcal{H}}}-(z-z_{0})(A-z_{0}I_{{\mathcal{H}}})^{-1}\big)\underset{z\to\lambda_{k}}{=}(\lambda_{k}-z)^{m_{a}(\lambda_{k})}[C_{k}+O(\lambda_{k}-z)],\quad C_{k}\neq 0,

(2.4)

that is, the multiplicity of the zero
of the Fredholm determinant
{\det}_{{\mathcal{H}}}\big(I_{{\mathcal{H}}}-(z-z_{0})(A-z_{0}I_{{\mathcal{H}}})^{-1}\big)

at

equals the algebraic multiplicity of the eigenvalue

of

.

In addition, denote the spectrum of

\lambda_{k}\neq\lambda_{k^{\prime}}

\displaystyle\begin{split}{\det}_{{\mathcal{H}}}(I_{{\mathcal{H}}}-(z-z_{0})(A-z_{0}I_{{\mathcal{H}}})^{-1})&=\prod_{k\in{\mathbb{N}}}\big[1-(z-z_{0})(\lambda_{k}-z_{0})^{-1}\big]^{m_{a}(\lambda_{k})}\\

(2.5)

with absolutely convergent products in (
2.5

Hypothesis 2.3
