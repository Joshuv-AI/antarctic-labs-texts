Explicit Constructions for Poncelet Polygons
Leah Wrenn Berman, Gábor Gévay

Abstract
We study the geometric structure of Poncelet n n -gons from a projective point of view. In particular we present explicit constructions of Poncelet n n -gons for certain n n and derive algebraic characterisations in terms of bracket polynomials. Via the derivations in [ 1 ] that connect Poncelet polygons to movable ( N 4 ) (N_{4}) -configurations, the results of this article can be used to construct a large class of specific movable ( N 4 ) (N_{4}) -configurations, the trivial celestial 4-configurations , which up to this point were all thought to be rigid and to require regular polygons for their construction.

If
