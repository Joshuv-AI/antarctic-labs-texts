Theorem 7.1
.

Let
(Q,\mathfrak{n})
be a regular local ring of dimension
d+1
,
g\in\mathfrak{n}^{i}\setminus\mathfrak{n}^{i+1}
,
i\geq 2
. Let

be a MCM

-module. Now if

. Furthermore,
G(M)
is Cohen-Macaulay if and only if

.

Proof.

By
2.3
we can assume that

has infinite residue field.

We first consider the case when dim
M=2
because if dim
M\leq 1
there is nothing to prove.

Let
\underline{x}=x_{1},x_{2}
be a maximal
\phi
-superficial sequence (see
2.22
). Set

,
M_{2}=M/\underline{x}M
,
J=(x_{1},x_{2})
and
(Q^{\prime},(y))=(Q/(\underline{x}),\mathfrak{n}/(\underline{x}))
.

Clearly,

is a DVR and so
M_{2}\cong Q^{\prime}/(y^{i(M)})\oplus\ldots\oplus Q^{\prime}/(y^{i(M)})\oplus Q^{\prime}/(y^{i(M)+1})
, because

. This implies

. Notice that since
\underline{x}
is
\phi
-superficial sequence,

and
i(M)=i(M_{1})=i(M_{2})
.

Since dim
M_{1}=1
. We can write
h
-polynomial of
{M_{1}}

with all the coefficients non-negative (see

(1)).

Now if we set

h_{0}(M_{1})=h_{1}(M_{1})=\ldots=h_{i(M)-1}(M_{1})=\mu(M)
.

Therefore

, because

and all its coefficients are non-negative (see
2.26
(1)).

Since
M=coker(\phi)
, for

we get

\mathfrak{m}^{n}M/{\mathfrak{m}^{n+1}M}\cong\mathfrak{m}^{n}(Q)^{\mu(M)}/{\mathfrak{m}^{n+1}(Q)^{\mu(M)}}.

Now if
h
-polynomial of

So for all

we have

(n+1)h_{0}(M)+nh_{1}(M)+\ldots+h_{n}(M)=\binom{n+2}{n}\mu(M).

Now since

, we get from (
\dagger
)

h_{0}(M)=h_{1}(M)=\ldots=h_{i(M)-1}(M)=\mu(M)

So we have,
h_{n}(M)=h_{n}({M_{1}})
for all

.

From Singh’s equality (

) we have

\mathfrak{m}^{n+1}M:x_{1}=\mathfrak{m}^{n}M\ \text{for}\ n=0,\ldots,i(M)-1.

So we have

(5)

\mathfrak{m}^{n+1}M\cap x_{1}M=x_{1}\mathfrak{m}^{n}M\ \text{for}\ n=0,\ldots,i(M)-1.

Since
h_{n}(M_{1})=h_{n}(M_{2})
for

,
from Singh’s equality (
2.6
)

(6)

\mathfrak{m}^{n+1}{M_{1}}:x_{2}=\mathfrak{m}^{n}{M_{1}}\ \text{for}\ n=0,\ldots,i(M)-1

Now we have
\mathfrak{m}^{n+1}M\cap JM=J\mathfrak{m}^{n}M\ \text{for}\ n=0,\ldots,i(M)-1
.
In fact, if

Corollary 7.2
.

Let
Q=k[[x_{1},\ldots,x_{d+1}]]
. Let

be a
Q
-module with minimal presentation

\phi_{i}

and det
(\phi_{i(M)}+\phi_{i(M)+1})\neq 0
then depth
G(M)\geq d-1
and

h_{M}(z)=\mu(M)(1+z+\ldots+z^{i(M)-1})+z^{s}
where

.

Proof.

After row and column reduction we can assume that first
\mu(M)-1
rows and columns of
\phi_{i(M)}
form an invertible matrix.

We first consider the case when dim
M=2
because if dim
M\leq 1

\underline{x}=x_{1},x_{2}
be a maximal
\phi
-superficial sequence (see
2.22
). Set
