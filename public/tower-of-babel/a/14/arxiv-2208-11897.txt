New characterizations of ruled real hypersurfaces in complex projective space
Juan de Dios Pérez, David Pérez-López

Abstract
We consider real hypersurfaces M \displaystyle M in complex projective space equipped with both the Levi-Civita and generalized Tanaka-Webster connections. For any nonnull constant k \displaystyle k and any symmetric tensor field of type (1,1) L \displaystyle L on M \displaystyle M we can define two tensor fields of type (1,2) on M \displaystyle M , L F ( k ) \displaystyle L_{F}^{(k)} and L T ( k ) \displaystyle L_{T}^{(k)} , related to both connections. We study the behaviour of the structure operator ϕ \displaystyle\phi with respect to such tensor fields in the particular case of L = A \displaystyle L=A , the shape operator of M \displaystyle M , and obtain some new characterizations of ruled real hypersurfaces in complex projective space.

\displaystyle k
-th Cho operator, torsion operator, ruled real hypersurfaces.
