Harmonically m m -convex set-valued function
Gabriel Santana 1 , Maira Valera-López 1∗

Abstract
. This research aimed to introduce the concept of harmonically m m -convex set-valued functions, which is obtained from the combination of two definitions: harmonically m m -convex functions and set-valued functions. In this work some properties and characteristics are developed, as well as a inequality of the Hermite-Hadamard type for such functions.

tF(b)+m(1-t)F\left(\frac{a}{m}\right)\subset F\left(\frac{ab}{ta+(1-t)b}\right),

and

tF(a)+m(1-t)F\left(\frac{b}{m}\right)\subset F\left(\frac{ab}{tb+(1-t)a}\right),

for every
t\in[0,1]
,
m\in(0,1]
and
a,b\in D
. Integrating both sides of (
3.2
) on
[0,1]
with respect to
t
, we get that

(3.3)

\int_{0}^{1}tF(b)+m(1-t)F\left(\frac{a}{m}\right)dt\subset\int_{0}^{1}F\left(\frac{ab}{ta+(1-t)b}\right)dt.

Integrating the left side of (
3.3
) we have:

\int_{0}^{1}tF(b)+m(1-t)F\left(\frac{a}{m}\right)dt=\frac{F(b)+mF\left(\frac{a}{m}\right)}{2}.

By the Aumann integral definition we get, that integral on the right hand of (
3.3
) is defined as:

\int_{0}^{1}F\left(\frac{ab}{ta+(1-t)b}\right)dt=\left\{\int_{0}^{1}f\left(\frac{ab}{ta+(1-t)b}\right)dt:f(x)\in F(x)\wedge t\in[0,1]\right\}.

But,

\int_{0}^{1}f\left(\frac{ab}{ta+(1-t)b}\right)dt=\frac{ab}{b-a}\int_{a}^{b}\frac{f(x)}{x^{2}}dx,

then

\left\{\frac{ab}{b-a}\int_{a}^{b}\frac{f(x)}{x^{2}}dx:f(x)\in F(x)\wedge x\in[a,b]\right\}=\frac{ab}{b-a}\int_{a}^{b}\frac{F(x)}{x^{2}}dx.

In consecuense:

\frac{F(b)+mF\left(\frac{a}{m}\right)}{2}\subseteq\frac{ab}{b-a}\int_{a}^{b}\frac{F(x)}{x^{2}}dx.

Similarly, we have that:

\frac{F(a)+mF\left(\frac{b}{m}\right)}{2}\subseteq\frac{ab}{b-a}\int_{a}^{b}\frac{F(x)}{x^{2}}dx,

so the required result is obtained.

\min\left(\inf\left(\frac{F(a)+mF\left(\frac{b}{m}\right)}{2}\right),\inf\left(\frac{mF\left(\frac{a}{m}\right)+F(b)}{2}\right)\right)\subseteq\frac{ab}{b-a}\int_{a}^{b}\frac{F(x)}{x^{2}}dx.

∎
