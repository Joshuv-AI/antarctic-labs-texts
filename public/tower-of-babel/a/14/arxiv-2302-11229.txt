Abstract
In this work we study one of the most appealing string theory motivated models, we present a tachyonic inflationary model in the recently proposed symmetric teleparallel framework, and examine constraints on tachyon inflation with the exponential potential along with the reheating for a chosen f ⁡ ( Q ) f(Q) gravity model. Considering a reheating phase parametrized by a number of e-folds N r ​ e , N_{re}, a temperature T r ​ e T_{re} , and an equation of state ω r ​ e \omega_{re} , we relate the reheating parameters as functions of the exponantial tachyon potential, f ⁡ ( Q ) f(Q) model, and the spectral index n s n_{s} parameters. We argue that our model predicts inflationary e-folds bounded as 50 ≤ N ≤ 64 50\leq N\leq 64 . While for the reheating phase, wide ranges of reheating e-folds numbers and temperatures can be obtained as we increase ω r ​ e \omega_{re} towards the value 1 / 4 1/4 according to recent Planck Data.
