On Beurling Measure Algebras
Ross Stokke

Abstract
We show how the measure theory of regular compacted-Borel measures defined on the δ \delta -ring of compacted-Borel subsets of a weighted locally compact group ( G , ω ) (G,\omega) provides a compatible framework for defining the corresponding Beurling measure algebra ℳ ⁡ ( G , ω ) {\cal M}(G,\omega) , thus filling a gap in the literature.
