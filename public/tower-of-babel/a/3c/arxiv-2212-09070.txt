Zhonghua Li and Lu Yan

Abstract
In this paper, we study the generating functions of multiple t t -star values with an arbitrary number of blocks of twos, which are based on the results of the corresponding generating functions of multiple t t -harmonic star sums. These generating functions can deduce an explicit expression of multiple t t -star values. As applications, we obtain some evaluations of multiple t t -star values with one-two-three or more general indices.

multiple

-star value, multiple
t
-harmonic star sum, alternating multiple
t
-value, generating function.

Introduction

Let
\mathbb{C}
,
\mathbb{R}
and
\mathbb{N}
be the set of complex numbers, real numbers and positive integers, respectively. And let
\mathbb{N}_{0}=\mathbb{N}\cup\{0\}
. A finite sequence
\boldsymbol{s}=(s_{1},\ldots,s_{r})
of positive integers is called an index. If
s_{1}>1
, the multiple zeta value
\zeta(\boldsymbol{s})
and the multiple zeta-star value
\zeta^{\star}(\boldsymbol{s})
are defined respectively by (cf.
[
10
,
23
]
)

\displaystyle\zeta(\boldsymbol{s})\equiv\zeta(s_{1},\ldots,s_{r}):=\sum_{k_{1}>\cdots>k_{r}\geq 1}\frac{1}{k_{1}^{s_{1}}\cdots k_{r}^{s_{r}}},

\displaystyle\zeta^{\star}(\boldsymbol{s})\equiv\zeta^{\star}(s_{1},\ldots,s_{r}):=\sum_{k_{1}\geq\cdots\geq k_{r}\geq 1}\frac{1}{k_{1}^{s_{1}}\cdots k_{r}^{s_{r}}}.

In 2012, D. Zagier
[
24
]
obtained explicit evaluation formulas for
\zeta(\{2\}^{a},3,\{2\}^{b})
and
\zeta^{\star}(\{2\}^{a},3,\{2\}^{b})
by establishing the generating functions, where
a,b\in\mathbb{N}_{0}
and the
\{s\}^{a}
denotes the sequence obtained by repeating
s
exactly
a
times. After that, other proofs of Zagier’s evaluation formulas have appeared. For example, see
[
12
,
13
,
14
]
for the proof of the formula for
\zeta(\{2\}^{a},3,\{2\}^{b})
, and
[
9
]
for the proof of the formula for
\zeta^{\star}(\{2\}^{a},3,\{2\}^{b})
. Especially in 2017, Kh. Hessami Pilehrood and T. Hessami Pilehrood
[
6
]
gave another proof of the theorem of Zagier by using a new representation of the generating function of
\zeta^{\star}(\{2\}^{a},3,\{2\}^{b})
. Later in
[
7
]
, Kh. Hessami Pilehrood and T. Hessami Pilehrood studied the generating function of multiple zeta-star values with an arbitrary number of blocks of twos. As applications, they
[
8
]
provided another way to derive some known evaluations of multiple zeta-star values, and gave new explicit evaluations of
\zeta^{\star}(\{\{2\}^{a},3,\{2\}^{a},1\}^{d})
and
\zeta^{\star}(\{\{2\}^{a},3,\{2\}^{a},1\}^{d},\{2\}^{a+1})
, where
a\in\mathbb{N}_{0}
and
d\in\mathbb{N}
. Note that there have been numerous contributions on evaluations of multiple zeta values and multiple zeta-star values, for example, see
[
1
,
2
,
9
,
17
,
19
,
23
,
25
]
and the references therein.

In this paper, we consider the evaluations of multiple
t
-star values. Recall from
[
11
]
that for an index
\boldsymbol{s}=(s_{1},\ldots,s_{r})
with
s_{1}>1
, Hoffman’s multiple
t
-value
t(\boldsymbol{s})
and multiple
t
-star value
t^{\star}(\boldsymbol{s})
are defined by

\displaystyle t(\boldsymbol{s})

\displaystyle\equiv t(s_{1},\ldots,s_{r}):=\sum_{k_{1}>\cdots>k_{r}\geq 1\atop k_{i}:\text{odd}}\frac{1}{k_{1}^{s_{1}}\cdots k_{r}^{s_{r}}}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k_{1}>\cdots>k_{r}\geq 1}\frac{1}{(2k_{1}-1)^{s_{1}}\cdots(2k_{r}-1)^{s_{r}}},

\displaystyle t^{\star}(\boldsymbol{s})

\displaystyle\equiv t^{\star}(s_{1},\ldots,s_{r}):=\sum_{k_{1}\geq\cdots\geq k_{r}\geq 1\atop k_{i}:\text{odd}}\frac{1}{k_{1}^{s_{1}}\cdots k_{r}^{s_{r}}}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k_{1}\geq\cdots\geq k_{r}\geq 1}\frac{1}{(2k_{1}-1)^{s_{1}}\cdots(2k_{r}-1)^{s_{r}}},

respectively. Here if the index is empty, we treat the values
t(\emptyset)=t^{\star}(\emptyset)=1
. By the method of establishing the generating functions, T. Murakami
[
18
]
obtained the evaluation of
t(\{2\}^{a},3,\{2\}^{b})
and S. Charlton
[
3

. In a recent paper
[
14
]
, L. Lai, C. Lupu and D. Orr provided another proof of Murakami’s result of

. In
[
15
]
, Z. Li and Z. Wang found evaluations of
t^{\star}(\{2\}^{a},3,\{2\}^{b})
and
t^{\star}(\{2\}^{a},1,\{2\}^{b})
.

In this paper, we study the generating functions of multiple
t
-star values with an arbitrary number of blocks of twos. Inspired by
[
7
]
, we first consider the generating functions of multiple
t
-harmonic star sums which is a finite version of multiple
t
-star values. Then we prove that the limit transfer from the generating functions of multiple
t
-harmonic star sums to the generating functions of multiple
t
-star values is correct. Therefore, we can obtain explicit expressions of the generating functions of multiple
t
-star values. As applications, we get similar evaluation formulas as in
[
15
]
for
t^{\star}(\{2\}^{a},3,\{2\}^{b})
and
t^{\star}(\{2\}^{a},1,\{2\}^{b})
by another way, and show that the multiple
t
-star values with more general index can be evaluated by the weighted sum formulas of alternating multiple
t
-values. Recall from
[
20

that for
\boldsymbol{s}=(s_{1},\ldots,s_{r})\in\mathbb{N}^{r}
and
\boldsymbol{\sigma}=(\sigma_{1},\ldots,\sigma_{r})\in\{\pm 1\}^{r}

(s_{1},\sigma_{1})\neq(1,1)

t
-values are defined by

\displaystyle t(\boldsymbol{s};\boldsymbol{\sigma})\equiv t(s_{1},\ldots,s_{r};\sigma_{1},\ldots,\sigma_{r}):=\sum_{k_{1}>\cdots>k_{r}\geq 1}\frac{\sigma_{1}^{k_{1}}\cdots\sigma_{r}^{k_{r}}}{(2k_{1}-1)^{s_{1}}\cdots(2k_{r}-1)^{s_{r}}}.

This paper is organized as follows. In Section
2
, we introduce the multiple
t
-harmonic star sums and give their generating functions. In Section
3
, we obtain the generating functions of multiple
t
-star values and deduce an explicit expression of multiple

-star values with an arbitrary number of blocks of twos. As applications, we get some evaluations of multiple
t

4
.

The following notations will be used throughout the paper. For any index
\boldsymbol{s}=(s_{1},\ldots,s_{r})
and
k,m\in\mathbb{N}_{0}
, we define that

V_{k,m}^{\#}(\boldsymbol{s})=\begin{cases}\frac{2k-1}{2m-1}\sum_{k\geq l_{1}\geq\cdots\geq l_{r}\geq m}\frac{2^{\triangle(k,l_{1})+\triangle(l_{1},l_{2})+\cdots+\triangle(l_{r},m)}}{(2l_{1}-1)^{s_{1}}\cdots(2l_{r}-1)^{s_{r}}}&\text{if\;}\boldsymbol{s}\neq\emptyset\text{\;and\;}k\geq m,\\
&\\
\left(2\cdot\frac{2k-1}{2m-1}\right)^{\triangle(k,m)}&\text{otherwise},\end{cases}

(1.1)

where

\triangle(k,m)=\begin{cases}0&\text{if\;}k=m,\\
1&\text{if\;}k\neq m.\end{cases}

Also, for any
c\in\mathbb{N}_{0}
, define

\delta(c)=\begin{cases}2&\text{if\;}c=0,\\
1&\text{if\;}c=1,\\
0&\text{if\;}c\geq 3.\end{cases}

2
Generating functions of multiple
t
-harmonic star sums

In this section, let
n
be a fixed positive integer. For any index
\boldsymbol{s}=(s_{1},\ldots,s_{r})
, we define the multiple
t
-harmonic star sum
t^{\star}_{n}(\boldsymbol{s})
by

\displaystyle t^{\star}_{n}(\boldsymbol{s}):=\sum_{n\geq k_{1}\geq\cdots\geq k_{r}\geq 1}\frac{1}{(2k_{1}-1)^{s_{1}}\cdots(2k_{r}-1)^{s_{r}}}.

Here we set
t^{\star}_{n}(\emptyset)=1
. For
d\in\mathbb{N}_{0}
,
\boldsymbol{c}=(c_{1},\ldots,c_{d})\in\mathbb{N}^{d}
and
\boldsymbol{z}=(z_{0},z_{1},\ldots,z_{d})\in\mathbb{C}^{d+1}
, we denote the generating function of multiple
t
-harmonic star sums with an arbitrary number of blocks of twos as

\displaystyle G_{n}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})=\sum_{a_{0},a_{1},\ldots,a_{d}\geq 0}t^{\star}_{n}(\{2\}^{a_{0}},c_{1},\{2\}^{a_{1}},\ldots,c_{d},\{2\}^{a_{d}})z_{0}^{2a_{0}}z_{1}^{2a_{1}}\cdots z_{d}^{2a_{d}},

Recall that the
\{s\}^{a}
denotes the sequence obtained by repeating
s
exactly
a
times.

The following theorem gives an explicit expression of the generating function of multiple
t
-harmonic star sums.

Theorem 2.1
.

For any
n\in\mathbb{N}
,
d\in\mathbb{N}_{0}
,
\boldsymbol{c}=(c_{1},\ldots,c_{d})\in(\mathbb{N}\setminus\{2\})^{d}
and
\boldsymbol{z}=(z_{0},z_{1},\ldots,z_{d})\in\mathbb{C}^{d+1}
with
|z_{j}|<1,j=0,1,\ldots,d
, we have

\displaystyle G_{n}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})=\frac{n\binom{2n}{n}}{2^{4n-2}}\sum_{n\geq k_{0}\geq k_{1}\geq\cdots\geq k_{d}\geq 1}\binom{2n-1}{n-k_{0}}\prod_{i=0}^{d}\frac{(-1)^{k_{i}\delta_{i}}(2k_{i}-1)^{\delta_{i}-1}}{(2k_{i}-1)^{2}-z_{i}^{2}}V_{k_{i-1},k_{i}}^{\#}(\{1\}^{c_{i}-3}),

(2.1)

where
k_{-1}=0
,
\delta_{i}=\delta(c_{i})+\delta(c_{i+1})
with
c_{0}=1
and
c_{d+1}=0
.

We first give some corollaries of Theorem
2.1
in Subsection
2.1
. Then we prepare some preliminary lemmas in Subsection
2.2
. Finally, we prove Theorem
2.1
in Subsection
2.3
.

2.1
Corollaries of Theorem
2.1

Taking
\boldsymbol{c}=(\{3,1\}^{d})
in (
2.1
), we get the following corollary.

Corollary 2.2
.

For any
n\in\mathbb{N}
,
d\in\mathbb{N}_{0}
and any complex numbers
z_{0},z_{1},\ldots,z_{2d}
with
|z_{j}|<1,j=0,1,\ldots,2d
, we have

\displaystyle\sum_{a_{0},a_{1},\ldots,a_{2d}\geq 0}t^{\star}_{n}(\{2\}^{a_{0}},3,\{2\}^{a_{1}},1,\{2\}^{a_{2}},\ldots,3,\{2\}^{a_{2d-1}},1,\{2\}^{a_{2d}})z_{0}^{2a_{0}}z_{1}^{2a_{1}}\cdots z_{2d}^{2a_{2d}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{n\binom{2n}{n}}{2^{4n-2}}\sum_{n\geq k_{0}\geq\cdots\geq k_{2d}\geq 1}\binom{2n-1}{n-k_{0}}(2k_{2d}-1)^{2}\prod_{i=0}^{2d}\frac{(-1)^{k_{i}}}{(2k_{i}-1)^{2}-z_{i}^{2}}\left(2\cdot\frac{2k_{i-1}-1}{2k_{i}-1}\right)^{\triangle(k_{i-1},k_{i})},

where
k_{-1}=0
.

Removing the case of
z_{u}=0
for
0\leq u\leq d
, we have

Corollary 2.3
.

Let
n\in\mathbb{N}
,
d\in\mathbb{N}_{0}
,
c_{1},\ldots,c_{d}\in\mathbb{N}\setminus\{2\}
and
z_{0},z_{1},\ldots,z_{d}
be complex numbers with
|z_{j}|<1,j=0,1,\ldots,d
. Then for any integer
u
with
0\leq u\leq d
, we have

\displaystyle\sum_{a_{0},a_{1},\ldots,a_{d}\geq 0\atop a_{u}\geq 1}t^{\star}_{n}(\{2\}^{a_{0}},c_{1},\{2\}^{a_{1}},\ldots,c_{d},\{2\}^{a_{d}})z_{0}^{2a_{0}}z_{1}^{2a_{1}}\cdots z_{d}^{2a_{d}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle z_{u}^{2}\cdot\frac{n\binom{2n}{n}}{2^{4n-2}}\sum_{n\geq k_{0}\geq\cdots\geq k_{d}\geq 1}\binom{2n-1}{n-k_{0}}\frac{1}{(2k_{u}-1)^{2}}\prod_{i=0}^{d}\frac{(-1)^{k_{i}\delta_{i}}(2k_{i}-1)^{\delta_{i}-1}}{(2k_{i}-1)^{2}-z_{i}^{2}}V_{k_{i-1},k_{i}}^{\#}(\{1\}^{c_{i}-3}),

where
k_{-1}=0
,
\delta_{i}=\delta(c_{i})+\delta(c_{i+1})
with
c_{0}=1
and
c_{d+1}=0
.

Proof.
We observe that

\displaystyle\sum_{a_{0},a_{1},\ldots,a_{d}\geq 0\atop a_{u}\geq 1}t^{\star}_{n}(\{2\}^{a_{0}},c_{1},\{2\}^{a_{1}},\ldots,c_{d},\{2\}^{a_{d}})z_{0}^{2a_{0}}z_{1}^{2a_{1}}\cdots z_{d}^{2a_{d}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle G_{n}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})-G_{n}(\boldsymbol{c};z_{0},\ldots,z_{u-1},0,z_{u+1},\ldots,z_{d}),

(2.2)

where
\boldsymbol{c}=(c_{1},\ldots,c_{d})
and
\boldsymbol{z}=(z_{0},z_{1},\ldots,z_{d})
. Hence we get the desired result by Theorem
2.1
.
∎

Expanding the right-hand side of (
2.1
), we obtain the following result.

Corollary 2.4
.

For any
n\in\mathbb{N}
,
d\in\mathbb{N}_{0}
,
c_{1},\ldots,c_{d}\in\mathbb{N}\setminus\{2\}
and
a_{0},a_{1},\ldots,a_{d}\in\mathbb{N}_{0}
, we have

\displaystyle t^{\star}_{n}(\{2\}^{a_{0}},c_{1},\{2\}^{a_{1}},\ldots,c_{d},\{2\}^{a_{d}})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{n\binom{2n}{n}}{2^{4n-2}}\sum_{n\geq k_{0}\geq k_{1}\geq\cdots\geq k_{d}\geq 1}\binom{2n-1}{n-k_{0}}\prod_{i=0}^{d}\frac{(-1)^{k_{i}\delta_{i}}}{(2k_{i}-1)^{2a_{i}-\delta_{i}+3}}V_{k_{i-1},k_{i}}^{\#}(\{1\}^{c_{i}-3}),

where
k_{-1}=0
,
\delta_{i}=\delta(c_{i})+\delta(c_{i+1})
with
c_{0}=1
and
c_{d+1}=0
.

Proof.
Applying the power series expansion

\frac{1}{(2k_{i}-1)^{2}-z_{i}^{2}}=\sum\limits_{a_{i}=0}^{\infty}\frac{z_{i}^{2a_{i}}}{(2k_{i}-1)^{2a_{i}+2}}

for
i=0,1,\ldots,d
and comparing the coefficients of
z_{0}^{2a_{0}}z_{1}^{2a_{1}}\cdots z_{d}^{2a_{d}}
in (
2.1
), we get the result.
∎

2.2
Preliminary Lemmas

Similar to
[
7
, (2.5)]
and
[
9
, (2.1), (2.2)]
, we prove several finite sum formulas that will be used in the proof of Theorem
2.1
.

Lemma 2.5
.

For any
n\in\mathbb{N}
and
l\in\mathbb{N}_{0}
, we have

\displaystyle\sum_{k=l+1}^{n}(2k-1)\binom{2n-1}{n-k}=(n-l)\binom{2n-1}{n-l}

(2.3)

and

\displaystyle\sum_{k=l+1}^{n}(-1)^{k}\binom{2n-1}{n-k}=\frac{(-1)^{l+1}(n-l)}{2n-1}\binom{2n-1}{n-l}.

(2.4)

Proof.
We may assume that
n\geq l+1
. For any integer
k
with
l+1\leq k\leq n
, we observe that

\displaystyle(2k-1)\binom{2n-1}{n-k}=I(n;k+1)-I(n;k)

(2.5)

with

\displaystyle I(n;j)=-(n+j-1)\binom{2n-1}{n-j}

for
l+1\leq j\leq n
and
I(n;n+1)=0
. Summing both sides of (
2.5
) over
k
from
l+1
to
n
, (
2.3
) follows easily.

Similarly, to prove (
2.4
), we set

\displaystyle J(n;j)=\frac{(-1)^{j-1}(n+j-1)}{2n-1}\binom{2n-1}{n-j}

for
l+1\leq j\leq n
and
J(n;n+1)=0
. Then it is easy to see that for any integer
k
with
l+1\leq k\leq n
,

\displaystyle(-1)^{k}\binom{2n-1}{n-k}=J(n;k+1)-J(n;k).

Then summing both sides of the above equation over
k
from
l+1
to
n
, we obtain (
2.4
).
∎

Lemma 2.6
.

For any
n,l\in\mathbb{N}
and
c\in\mathbb{N}_{0}
, we have

\displaystyle\sum_{k=l}^{n}\frac{(-1)^{k}}{2k-1}\binom{2n-1}{n-k}V_{k,l}^{\#}(\{1\}^{c})=\frac{(-1)^{l}}{(2n-1)^{c+1}}\binom{2n-1}{n-l}.

(2.6)

Proof.
We may assume that
n\geq l
. The proof is by induction on
c
. For
c=0
, it is enough to show

\displaystyle\sum_{k=l}^{n}\frac{(-1)^{k}}{2k-1}\binom{2n-1}{n-k}\left(2\cdot\frac{2k-1}{2l-1}\right)^{\triangle(k,l)}=\frac{(-1)^{l}}{2n-1}\binom{2n-1}{n-l}.

(2.7)

In fact, the left-hand side of (
2.7
) is

\frac{(-1)^{l}}{2l-1}\binom{2n-1}{n-l}+\frac{2}{2l-1}\sum_{k=l+1}^{n}(-1)^{k}\binom{2n-1}{n-k}.

Applying (
2.4
), we find the left-hand side of (
2.7
) becomes

\frac{(-1)^{l}}{2l-1}\binom{2n-1}{n-l}+\frac{2(-1)^{l+1}(n-l)}{(2l-1)(2n-1)}\binom{2n-1}{n-l},

which exactly is
\frac{(-1)^{l}}{2n-1}\binom{2n-1}{n-l}
.

If
c\geq 1
, the left-hand side of (
2.6
) is

\sum_{k=l}^{n}\frac{(-1)^{k}}{2k-1}\binom{2n-1}{n-k}\cdot\frac{2k-1}{2l-1}\sum_{k\geq l_{1}\geq\cdots\geq l_{c}\geq l}\frac{2^{\triangle(k,l_{1})+\triangle(l_{1},l_{2})+\cdots+\triangle(l_{c},l)}}{(2l_{1}-1)\cdots(2l_{c}-1)}.

Changing the order of the summations, the left-hand side of (
2.6
) becomes

\sum_{n\geq l_{1}\geq\cdots\geq l_{c}\geq l}\left\{\sum_{k=l_{1}}^{n}\frac{(-1)^{k}2^{\triangle(k,l_{1})}}{2l_{1}-1}\binom{2n-1}{n-k}\right\}\frac{2^{\triangle(l_{1},l_{2})+\cdots+\triangle(l_{c},l)}}{(2l-1)(2l_{2}-1)\cdots(2l_{c}-1)},

which is

\sum_{n\geq l_{1}\geq\cdots\geq l_{c}\geq l}\left\{\sum_{k=l_{1}}^{n}\frac{(-1)^{k}}{2k-1}\binom{2n-1}{n-k}\left(2\cdot\frac{2k-1}{2l_{1}-1}\right)^{\triangle(k,l_{1})}\right\}\frac{2^{\triangle(l_{1},l_{2})+\cdots+\triangle(l_{c},l)}}{(2l-1)(2l_{2}-1)\cdots(2l_{c}-1)}.

Using (
2.7
) to deal with the part in the curly brace, we find the left-hand side of (
2.6
) is

\sum_{n\geq l_{1}\geq\cdots\geq l_{c}\geq l}\frac{(-1)^{l_{1}}}{2n-1}\binom{2n-1}{n-l_{1}}\cdot\frac{2^{\triangle(l_{1},l_{2})+\cdots+\triangle(l_{c},l)}}{(2l-1)(2l_{2}-1)\cdots(2l_{c}-1)},

which is just

\frac{1}{2n-1}\sum_{l_{1}=l}^{n}\frac{(-1)^{l_{1}}}{2l_{1}-1}\binom{2n-1}{n-l_{1}}V_{l_{1},l}^{\#}(\{1\}^{c-1}).

Then the result follows from the inductive hypothesis for
c-1
.
∎

2.3
Proof of Theorem
2.1

We prove Theorem
2.1
by induction on
n+d
. When
n=1
, we have

\displaystyle G_{1}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})=\sum_{a_{0},a_{1},\ldots,a_{d}\geq 0}z_{0}^{2a_{0}}\cdots z_{d}^{2a_{d}}=\prod_{i=0}^{d}\frac{1}{1-z_{i}^{2}},

and the right-hand side of (
2.1
) is

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\prod_{i=0}^{d}\frac{(-1)^{\delta_{i}}}{1-z_{i}^{2}}\cdot(-2)=(-1)^{1+\delta_{0}+\delta_{1}+\cdots+\delta_{d}}\prod_{i=0}^{d}\frac{1}{1-z_{i}^{2}}=\prod_{i=0}^{d}\frac{1}{1-z_{i}^{2}}.

Hence (
2.1
) is true for
n=1
.

When
d=0
, since for
n>1
,

t_{n}^{\star}(\{2\}^{a_{0}})=\sum_{k=0}^{a_{0}}\frac{t_{n-1}^{\star}(\{2\}^{k})}{(2n-1)^{2(a_{0}-k)}},

we have a recursive formula

G_{n}(;z_{0})=\frac{(2n-1)^{2}}{(2n-1)^{2}-z_{0}^{2}}G_{n-1}(;z_{0}).

Using the above formula repeatedly, we get

\displaystyle G_{n}(;z_{0})=\prod_{j=2}^{n}\frac{(2j-1)^{2}}{(2j-1)^{2}-z_{0}^{2}}G_{1}(;z_{0})=[(2n-1)!!]^{2}\prod_{j=1}^{n}\frac{1}{(2j-1)^{2}-z_{0}^{2}}.

By the method of partial fractional decomposition, we have

\displaystyle G_{n}(;z_{0})=

\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{n}\frac{2(-1)^{k-1}(2k-1)}{(2n-2k)!!(2n+2k-2)!!}\cdot\frac{[(2n-1)!!]^{2}}{(2k-1)^{2}-z_{0}^{2}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{n\binom{2n}{n}}{2^{4n-2}}\sum_{k=1}^{n}\binom{2n-1}{n-k}\frac{(-1)^{k}(2k-1)^{2}}{(2k-1)^{2}-z_{0}^{2}}\cdot\frac{-2}{2k-1}.

So (
2.1
) is proved for
d=0
.

Now assume that
n>1
and
d>0
. Since

\displaystyle t^{\star}_{n}(\{2\}^{a_{0}},c_{1},\{2\}^{a_{1}},\ldots,c_{d},\{2\}^{a_{d}})=

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{a_{0}}\frac{1}{(2n-1)^{2a_{0}-2k}}t^{\star}_{n-1}(\{2\}^{k},c_{1},\{2\}^{a_{1}},\ldots,c_{d},\{2\}^{a_{d}})

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{(2n-1)^{2a_{0}+c_{1}}}t^{\star}_{n}(\{2\}^{a_{1}},c_{2},\{2\}^{a_{2}},\ldots,c_{d},\{2\}^{a_{d}}),

we find that

\displaystyle G_{n}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})=

\displaystyle\sum_{a_{0},\ldots,a_{d}\geq 0}\sum_{k=0}^{a_{0}}\frac{1}{(2n-1)^{2a_{0}-2k}}t^{\star}_{n-1}(\{2\}^{k},c_{1},\{2\}^{a_{1}},\ldots,c_{d},\{2\}^{a_{d}})z_{0}^{2a_{0}}\cdots z_{d}^{2a_{d}},

\displaystyle+\sum_{a_{0}\geq 0}\frac{z_{0}^{2a_{0}}}{(2n-1)^{2a_{0}+c_{1}}}G_{n}(\boldsymbol{c}^{-};\boldsymbol{z}^{-}),

where
\boldsymbol{c}^{-}=(c_{2},\ldots,c_{d})
and
\boldsymbol{z}^{-}=(z_{1},z_{2},\ldots,z_{d})
. Then it is easy to get the following recursive formula

\displaystyle G_{n}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})=\frac{(2n-1)^{2}}{(2n-1)^{2}-z_{0}^{2}}G_{n-1}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})+\frac{(2n-1)^{2-c_{1}}}{(2n-1)^{2}-z_{0}^{2}}G_{n}(\boldsymbol{c}^{-};\boldsymbol{z}^{-}).

(2.8)

Let
\widetilde{G}_{n}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})
denote the right-hand side of (
2.1
). Therefore according to (
2.8
), we need to prove

\displaystyle\frac{(2n-1)^{2}}{(2n-1)^{2}-z_{0}^{2}}G_{n-1}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})-\widetilde{G}_{n}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})=-\frac{(2n-1)^{2-c_{1}}}{(2n-1)^{2}-z_{0}^{2}}G_{n}(\boldsymbol{c}^{-};\boldsymbol{z}^{-}).

(2.9)

By the induction hypothesis for
G_{n-1}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})
, the left hand-side of (
2.9
) is

\displaystyle\frac{(2n-1)^{2}}{(2n-1)^{2}-z_{0}^{2}}\frac{(n-1)\binom{2n-2}{n-1}}{2^{4n-6}}\sum_{n-1\geq k_{0}\geq\cdots\geq k_{d}\geq 1}\binom{2n-3}{n-k_{0}-1}

\displaystyle\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\times\prod_{i=0}^{d}\frac{(-1)^{k_{i}\delta_{i}}(2k_{i}-1)^{\delta_{i}-1}}{(2k_{i}-1)^{2}-z_{i}^{2}}V_{k_{i-1},k_{i}}^{\#}(\{1\}^{c_{i}-3})

\displaystyle-\frac{n\binom{2n}{n}}{2^{4n-2}}\sum_{n\geq k_{0}\geq\cdots\geq k_{d}\geq 1}\binom{2n-1}{n-k_{0}}\prod_{i=0}^{d}\frac{(-1)^{k_{i}\delta_{i}}(2k_{i}-1)^{\delta_{i}-1}}{(2k_{i}-1)^{2}-z_{i}^{2}}V_{k_{i-1},k_{i}}^{\#}(\{1\}^{c_{i}-3}),

which is

\displaystyle\sum_{n\geq k_{0}\geq\cdots\geq k_{d}\geq 1}\prod_{i=0}^{d}\frac{(-1)^{k_{i}\delta_{i}}(2k_{i}-1)^{\delta_{i}-1}}{(2k_{i}-1)^{2}-z_{i}^{2}}V_{k_{i-1},k_{i}}^{\#}(\{1\}^{c_{i}-3})

\displaystyle\quad\quad\quad\times\left\{\frac{(2n-1)^{2}}{(2n-1)^{2}-z_{0}^{2}}\cdot\frac{(n-1)\binom{2n-2}{n-1}}{2^{4n-6}}\binom{2n-3}{n-k_{0}-1}-\frac{n\binom{2n}{n}}{2^{4n-2}}\binom{2n-1}{n-k_{0}}\right\}.

As the part in braces can be simplified as

-\frac{n\binom{2n}{n}}{2^{4n-2}}\binom{2n-1}{n-k_{0}}\cdot\frac{(2k_{0}-1)^{2}-z_{0}^{2}}{(2n-1)^{2}-z_{0}^{2}},

the left-hand side of (
2.9
) becomes

\displaystyle\frac{2}{(2n-1)^{2}-z_{0}^{2}}\cdot\frac{n\binom{2n}{n}}{2^{4n-2}}\sum_{n\geq k_{0}\geq k_{1}\geq\cdots\geq k_{d}\geq 1}\binom{2n-1}{n-k_{0}}\frac{(-1)^{k_{0}(1+\delta(c_{1}))}(2k_{0}-1)^{\delta(c_{1})}}{2k_{0}-1}

\displaystyle\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\times\prod_{i=1}^{d}\frac{(-1)^{k_{i}\delta_{i}}(2k_{i}-1)^{\delta_{i}-1}}{(2k_{i}-1)^{2}-z_{i}^{2}}V_{k_{i-1},k_{i}}^{\#}(\{1\}^{c_{i}-3}).

(2.10)

Let
\sum\nolimits_{k_{0}}
denote the inner sum over
k_{0}
in (
2.3
), that is

\displaystyle\sum\nolimits_{k_{0}}=\sum_{k_{0}=k_{1}}^{n}\binom{2n-1}{n-k_{0}}\frac{(-1)^{k_{0}(1+\delta(c_{1}))}(2k_{0}-1)^{\delta(c_{1})}}{2k_{0}-1}V_{k_{0},k_{1}}^{\#}(\{1\}^{c_{1}-1}).

If
c_{1}=1
,
\delta(c_{1})=1
, we obtain

\displaystyle\sum\nolimits_{k_{0}}=

\displaystyle\sum_{k_{0}=k_{1}}^{n}\binom{2n-1}{n-k_{0}}\left(2\cdot\frac{2k_{0}-1}{2k_{1}-1}\right)^{\triangle(k_{0},k_{1})}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\binom{2n-1}{n-k_{1}}+\frac{2}{2k_{1}-1}\sum_{k_{0}=k_{1}+1}^{n}\binom{2n-1}{n-k_{0}}(2k_{0}-1).

Then by (
2.3
), we have

\sum\nolimits_{k_{0}}=\binom{2n-1}{n-k_{1}}+\frac{2n-2k_{1}}{2k_{1}-1}\binom{2n-1}{n-k_{1}}=\frac{2n-1}{2k_{1}-1}\binom{2n-1}{n-k_{1}}.

Using the above result together with (
2.3
), we find the left-hand side of (
2.9
) equals

\displaystyle-\frac{2n-1}{(2n-1)^{2}-z_{0}^{2}}\cdot\frac{n\binom{2n}{n}}{2^{4n-2}}\sum_{n\geq k_{1}\geq\cdots\geq k_{d}\geq 1}\binom{2n-1}{n-k_{1}}\frac{(-1)^{k_{1}(1+\delta(c_{2}))}(2k_{1}-1)^{\delta(c_{2})}}{(2k_{1}-1)^{2}-z_{1}^{2}}\cdot\frac{-2}{2k_{1}-1}

\displaystyle\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\times\prod_{i=2}^{d}\frac{(-1)^{k_{i}\delta_{i}}(2k_{i}-1)^{\delta_{i}-1}}{(2k_{i}-1)^{2}-z_{i}^{2}}V_{k_{i-1},k_{i}}^{\#}(\{1\}^{c_{i}-3}),

which by the inductive hypothesis for
G_{n}(\boldsymbol{c}^{-};\boldsymbol{z}^{-})
is

-\frac{2n-1}{(2n-1)^{2}-z_{0}^{2}}G_{n}(\boldsymbol{c}^{-};\boldsymbol{z}^{-}).

Hence the theorem is proved in this case.

If
c_{1}\geq 3
,
\delta(c_{1})=0
, by Lemma
2.6
, we get

\displaystyle\sum\nolimits_{k_{0}}=\sum_{k_{0}=k_{1}}^{n}\binom{2n-1}{n-k_{0}}\frac{(-1)^{k_{0}}}{2k_{0}-1}V_{k_{0},k_{1}}^{\#}(\{1\}^{c_{1}-3})=\frac{(-1)^{k_{1}}}{(2n-1)^{c_{1}-2}}\binom{2n-1}{n-k_{1}}.

Combining this formula with (
2.3
), we obtain the left-hand side of (
2.9
) is

\displaystyle-\frac{(2n-1)^{2-c_{1}}}{(2n-1)^{2}-z_{0}^{2}}\cdot\frac{n\binom{2n}{n}}{2^{4n-2}}\sum_{n\geq k_{1}\geq\cdots\geq k_{d}\geq 1}\binom{2n-1}{n-k_{1}}\frac{(-1)^{k_{1}(1+\delta(c_{2}))}(2k_{1}-1)^{\delta(c_{2})}}{(2k_{1}-1)^{2}-z_{1}^{2}}\cdot\frac{-2}{2k_{1}-1}

\displaystyle\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\times\prod_{i=2}^{d}\frac{(-1)^{k_{i}\delta_{i}}(2k_{i}-1)^{\delta_{i}-1}}{(2k_{i}-1)^{2}-z_{i}^{2}}V_{k_{i-1},k_{i}}^{\#}(\{1\}^{c_{i}-3}),

which by the inductive hypothesis for
G_{n}(\boldsymbol{c}^{-};\boldsymbol{z}^{-})
is

-\frac{(2n-1)^{2-c_{1}}}{(2n-1)^{2}-z_{0}^{2}}G_{n}(\boldsymbol{c}^{-};\boldsymbol{z}^{-}).

Therefore, we conclude that
G_{n}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})=\widetilde{G}_{n}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})
. This completes the proof.
∎

3.1
Lemmas

Similar to
[
7
, Lemma 4.1]
, we have the following result.

Lemma 3.5
.

Let
d\in\mathbb{N}_{0}
,
\boldsymbol{c}=(c_{1},\ldots,c_{d})\in(\mathbb{N}\setminus\{2\})^{d}
with
c_{1}\geq 3
, and let
\boldsymbol{z}=(z_{0},z_{1},\ldots,z_{d})\in\mathbb{C}^{d+1}

with
|z_{j}|<1,j=0,1,\ldots,d
. Then

\displaystyle\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}G_{n}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})=G(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z}),

(3.3)

and the convergence is uniform in any closed region
E
:
|z_{0}|\leq u_{0}<1,|z_{1}|\leq u_{1}<1,\ldots,|z_{d}|\leq u_{d}<1
.

Proof.
For
|z|<1
, we find that

\displaystyle\prod_{k=m}^{n}\left(1-\frac{z^{2}}{(2k-1)^{2}}\right)^{-1}=\sum_{l=0}^{\infty}t_{n,m}^{\star}(\{2\}^{l})z^{2l}.

(3.4)

Here we define that, for integers
n\geq m>0
and
\boldsymbol{s}=(s_{1},\ldots,s_{r})\in\mathbb{N}^{r}
,

\displaystyle t_{n,m}^{\star}(\boldsymbol{s})=t_{n,m}^{\star}(s_{1},\ldots,s_{r})=\sum_{n\geq k_{1}\geq\cdots\geq k_{r}\geq m}\frac{1}{(2k_{1}-1)^{s_{1}}\cdots(2k_{r}-1)^{s_{r}}}.

We also set
t^{\star}_{n,m}(\emptyset)=1
. If
s_{1}>1
, we may let
n
tend to infinity and define

t_{\infty,m}^{\star}(\boldsymbol{s})=\sum_{k_{1}\geq\cdots\geq k_{r}\geq m}\frac{1}{(2k_{1}-1)^{s_{1}}\cdots(2k_{r}-1)^{s_{r}}}.

If
d=0
, using (
3.4
), we have

\displaystyle G_{n}(;z_{0})=\prod_{k=1}^{n}\left(1-\frac{z_{0}^{2}}{(2k-1)^{2}}\right)^{-1}

and

\displaystyle G(;z_{0})=\prod_{k=1}^{\infty}\left(1-\frac{z_{0}^{2}}{(2k-1)^{2}}\right)^{-1}.

Hence the lemma is true for
d=0
.

Now assume that
d\geq 1
. Using (
3.4
), we get

\displaystyle G_{n}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{n\geq k_{1}\geq\cdots\geq k_{d}\geq 1}\frac{\prod\limits_{k=k_{1}}^{n}\left(1-\frac{z_{0}^{2}}{(2k-1)^{2}}\right)^{-1}\prod\limits_{k=k_{2}}^{k_{1}}\left(1-\frac{z_{1}^{2}}{(2k-1)^{2}}\right)^{-1}\cdots\prod\limits_{k=1}^{k_{d}}\left(1-\frac{z_{d}^{2}}{(2k-1)^{2}}\right)^{-1}}{(2k_{1}-1)^{c_{1}}\cdots(2k_{d}-1)^{c_{d}}}.

Setting

\displaystyle G_{n}^{\infty}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{n\geq k_{1}\geq\cdots\geq k_{d}\geq 1}\frac{\prod\limits_{k=k_{1}}^{\infty}\left(1-\frac{z_{0}^{2}}{(2k-1)^{2}}\right)^{-1}\prod\limits_{k=k_{2}}^{k_{1}}\left(1-\frac{z_{1}^{2}}{(2k-1)^{2}}\right)^{-1}\prod\limits_{k=1}^{k_{d}}\left(1-\frac{z_{d}^{2}}{(2k-1)^{2}}\right)^{-1}}{(2k_{1}-1)^{c_{1}}\cdots(2k_{d}-1)^{c_{d}}},

then

\displaystyle|G_{n}^{\infty}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})-G_{n}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\sum_{n\geq k_{1}\geq\cdots\geq k_{d}\geq 1}\frac{\prod\limits_{k=k_{1}}^{n}\left(1-\frac{|z_{0}|^{2}}{(2k-1)^{2}}\right)^{-1}\prod\limits_{k=k_{2}}^{k_{1}}\left(1-\frac{|z_{1}|^{2}}{(2k-1)^{2}}\right)^{-1}\prod\limits_{k=1}^{k_{d}}\left(1-\frac{|z_{d}|^{2}}{(2k-1)^{2}}\right)^{-1}}{(2k_{1}-1)^{c_{1}}\cdots(2k_{d}-1)^{c_{d}}}

\displaystyle\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\times\left|\prod\limits_{k=n+1}^{\infty}\left(1-\frac{z_{0}^{2}}{(2k-1)^{2}}\right)^{-1}-1\right|.

(3.5)

Assume that
|z_{0}|\leq u_{0}<1,|z_{1}|\leq u_{1}<1,\ldots,|z_{d}|\leq u_{d}<1
. Using the infinite product formula for the sine function

\frac{\sin\pi z}{\pi z}=\prod\limits_{k=1}^{\infty}\left(1-\frac{z^{2}}{k^{2}}\right),

we obtain

\displaystyle\sum_{n\geq k_{1}\geq\cdots\geq k_{d}\geq 1}\frac{\prod\limits_{k=k_{1}}^{n}\left(1-\frac{|z_{0}|^{2}}{(2k-1)^{2}}\right)^{-1}\prod\limits_{k=k_{2}}^{k_{1}}\left(1-\frac{|z_{1}|^{2}}{(2k-1)^{2}}\right)^{-1}\prod\limits_{k=1}^{k_{d}}\left(1-\frac{|z_{d}|^{2}}{(2k-1)^{2}}\right)^{-1}}{(2k_{1}-1)^{c_{1}}\cdots(2k_{d}-1)^{c_{d}}}

\displaystyle<

\displaystyle\prod_{i=0}^{d}\frac{\pi|z_{i}|}{\sin(\pi|z_{i}|)}t_{n}^{\star}(\boldsymbol{c})<\prod_{i=0}^{d}\frac{\pi u_{i}}{\sin(\pi u_{i})}t^{\star}(\boldsymbol{c}).

(3.6)

Using (
3.4
), we get

\displaystyle\left|\prod\limits_{k=n+1}^{\infty}\left(1-\frac{z_{0}^{2}}{(2k-1)^{2}}\right)^{-1}-1\right|=\left|\sum_{l=1}^{\infty}t_{\infty,n+1}^{\star}(\{2\}^{l})z_{0}^{2l}\right|<\sum_{l=1}^{\infty}t_{\infty,n+1}^{\star}(\{2\}^{l}).

Note that

\displaystyle t_{\infty,n+1}^{\star}(\{2\}^{l})

\displaystyle=\sum_{k_{1}\geq\cdots\geq k_{l}\geq n+1}\frac{1}{(2k_{1}-1)^{2}\cdots(2k_{l}-1)^{2}}

\displaystyle<\left(\sum_{k=n+1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{(2k-1)^{2}}\right)^{l}<\left(\int_{n}^{\infty}\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{(2x-1)^{2}}\right)^{l}=\left(\frac{1}{2(2n-1)}\right)^{l},

then we have

\displaystyle\left|\prod\limits_{k=n+1}^{\infty}\left(1-\frac{z_{0}^{2}}{(2k-1)^{2}}\right)^{-1}-1\right|<\sum_{l=1}^{\infty}\left(\frac{1}{2(2n-1)}\right)^{l}=\frac{1}{4n-3}.

(3.7)

Using (
3.1
), (
3.1
) and (
3.7
), we get

\displaystyle\left|G_{n}^{\infty}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})-G_{n}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})\right|<\frac{1}{4n-3}\prod_{i=0}^{d}\frac{\pi u_{i}}{\sin(\pi u_{i})}t^{\star}(\boldsymbol{c})\rightarrow 0,

as
n\rightarrow\infty
on the closed region
E
. Similarly, we have

\displaystyle\left|G(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})-G_{n}^{\infty}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})\right|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\sum_{k_{1}\geq\cdots\geq k_{d}\geq 1\atop k_{1}>n}\frac{\prod\limits_{k=k_{1}}^{\infty}\left(1-\frac{|z_{0}|^{2}}{(2k-1)^{2}}\right)^{-1}\prod\limits_{k=k_{2}}^{k_{1}}\left(1-\frac{|z_{1}|^{2}}{(2k-1)^{2}}\right)^{-1}\prod\limits_{k=1}^{k_{d}}\left(1-\frac{|z_{d}|^{2}}{(2k-1)^{2}}\right)^{-1}}{(2k_{1}-1)^{c_{1}}\cdots(2k_{d}-1)^{c_{d}}}

\displaystyle<

\displaystyle\prod_{i=0}^{d}\frac{\pi u_{i}}{\sin(\pi u_{i})}\left(t^{\star}(\boldsymbol{c})-t_{n}^{\star}(\boldsymbol{c})\right)\rightarrow 0,

as
n\rightarrow\infty
on the closed region
E
. Therefore, we finish the proof.
∎

Inspired by
[
16
, Lemma 4.2]
, we get the following lemma.

Lemma 3.6
.

Let
a,b,C\in\mathbb{R}
with
b>1,C>0
, and let
W_{k}
be real numbers satisfying

|W_{k}|<\frac{C\log^{a}(2k+1)}{k^{b}}

for any
k\in\mathbb{N}
. Then we have

\displaystyle\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}\sum_{k=1}^{n}|W_{k}|\left(\frac{2}{\pi}-\frac{n}{2^{4n-2}}\binom{2n}{n}\binom{2n-1}{n-k}\right)=0.

Proof.
In
[
21
]
, J. Wallis presented the following formula

\displaystyle\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}\left[\frac{(2n)!!}{(2n-1)!!}\right]^{2}\frac{1}{2n+1}=\frac{\pi}{2},

and a proof of the Wallis formula relies on evaluation of integrals of powers of
\sin x
, that is, let
I_{n}=\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\sin^{n}x\mathrm{d}x
, by repeated partial integration and the value range of
\sin x
, we get

\displaystyle I_{n}=\frac{n-1}{n}I_{n-2}\quad\text{and}\quad I_{2n+1}<I_{2n}<I_{2n-1}

respectively. Hence we obtain

\displaystyle\frac{(2n)!!}{(2n+1)!!}<\frac{(2n-1)!!}{(2n)!!}\frac{\pi}{2}<\frac{(2n-2)!!}{(2n-1)!!}.

(3.8)

Using the squeeze theorem, we can deduce the Wallis formula.

As

\displaystyle\frac{2}{\pi}-\frac{n}{2^{4n-2}}\binom{2n}{n}\binom{2n-1}{n-k}

\displaystyle=\frac{2}{\pi}-\frac{[(2n-1)!!]^{2}}{(2n-2k)!!(2n+2k-2)!!}

\displaystyle\geq\frac{2}{\pi}-\frac{[(2n-1)!!]^{2}}{(2n-2)!!(2n)!!},

we use (
3.8
) to get

\frac{2}{\pi}-\frac{n}{2^{4n-2}}\binom{2n}{n}\binom{2n-1}{n-k}>0.

On the other hand, by (
3.8
), we have

\displaystyle\frac{2}{\pi}-\frac{n}{2^{4n-2}}\binom{2n}{n}\binom{2n-1}{n-k}

\displaystyle<\frac{2}{\pi}-\frac{2}{\pi}\frac{[(2n)!!]^{2}}{(2n+1)(2n-2k)!!(2n+2k-2)!!}.

(3.9)

The right-hand side of (
3.9
) is
\frac{2}{\pi}-\frac{2}{\pi}\frac{2(n+k)}{2n+1}\frac{\binom{n}{k}}{\binom{n+k}{k}}
, which implies that

\displaystyle\frac{2}{\pi}-\frac{n}{2^{4n-2}}\binom{2n}{n}\binom{2n-1}{n-k}<\frac{2}{\pi}\left(1-\frac{\binom{n}{k}}{\binom{n+k}{k}}\right).

By the proof of
[
16
, Lemma 4.2]
, we have

\displaystyle 1-\frac{\binom{n}{k}}{\binom{n+k}{k}}\leq\frac{2k^{2}}{n}.

Hence we get

\displaystyle\frac{2}{\pi}-\frac{n}{2^{4n-2}}\binom{2n}{n}\binom{2n-1}{n-k}<\frac{4k^{2}}{\pi n}.

The subsequent proof is basically the same as the proof of
[
16
, Lemma 4.2]
. Let
\lambda=\min\{\frac{b-1}{2},\frac{1}{2}\}
, we deduce that

\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{n}|W_{k}|\left(\frac{2}{\pi}-\frac{n}{2^{4n-2}}\binom{2n}{n}\binom{2n-1}{n-k}\right)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{4C}{\pi n}\sum_{k=1}^{\lfloor n^{\lambda}\rfloor}\frac{\log^{a}(2k+1)}{k^{b-2}}+\frac{2C}{\pi}\sum_{k=\lfloor n^{\lambda}\rfloor+1}^{\infty}\frac{\log^{a}(2k+1)}{k^{b}}.

Since
b>1
, the last term becomes
0
when
n
tends to infinity. If
b\geq 2
, then

\displaystyle\frac{4C}{\pi n}\sum_{k=1}^{\lfloor n^{\lambda}\rfloor}\frac{\log^{a}(2k+1)}{k^{b-2}}\leq\frac{4C}{\pi n}\sum_{k=1}^{\sqrt{n}}\log^{a}(2k+1)\leq\frac{4C\log^{a}(2\sqrt{n}+1)}{\pi\sqrt{n}}\rightarrow 0

as
n\rightarrow\infty
. If
1<b<2
, then
\lambda=\frac{b-1}{2}
. Since
\lambda(3-b)=\frac{1}{2}(b-1)(3-b)\leq\frac{1}{2}
, we have

\displaystyle\frac{4C}{\pi n}\sum_{k=1}^{\lfloor n^{\lambda}\rfloor}\frac{\log^{a}(2k+1)}{k^{b-2}}\leq\frac{4Cn^{\lambda(3-b)}\log^{a}(2n^{\lambda}+1)}{\pi n}\leq\frac{4C\log^{a}(2\sqrt{n}+1)}{\pi\sqrt{n}}\rightarrow 0\quad

as
n\rightarrow\infty
. Therefore, we prove the lemma.
∎

Lemma 3.7
.

For
k_{0}\in\mathbb{N}
,
d\in\mathbb{N}_{0}
,
\boldsymbol{c}=(c_{1},\ldots,c_{d})\in(\mathbb{N}\setminus\{2\})^{d}
with
c_{1}\geq 3
,
\boldsymbol{z}=(z_{0},z_{1},\ldots,z_{d})\in\mathbb{C}^{d+1}

with
|z_{j}|<1,j=0,1,\ldots,d
, let

\displaystyle\widetilde{G}_{k_{0}}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})=\sum_{k_{0}\geq k_{1}\geq\cdots\geq k_{d}\geq 1}\prod_{i=0}^{d}\frac{(-1)^{k_{i}\delta_{i}}(2k_{i}-1)^{\delta_{i}-1}}{(2k_{i}-1)^{2}-z_{i}^{2}}V_{k_{i-1},k_{i}}^{\#}(\{1\}^{c_{i}-3}),

(3.10)

where
k_{-1}=0
,
\delta_{i}=\delta(c_{i})+\delta(c_{i+1})
with
c_{0}=1
and
c_{d+1}=0
. Then there exist
a,b,C\in\mathbb{R}
with
b>1
and
C>0
such that for all
k_{0}\in\mathbb{N}
,

\left|\widetilde{G}_{k_{0}}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})\right|<\frac{C\log^{a}(2k_{0}+1)}{k_{0}^{b}}.

Proof.
For
i=1,2,\ldots,d
, if
c_{i}\in\{1,3\}
, we have

\displaystyle V_{k_{i-1},k_{i}}^{\#}(\{1\}^{c_{i}-3})=\left(2\cdot\frac{2k_{i-1}-1}{2k_{i}-1}\right)^{\triangle(k_{i-1},k_{i})}\leq 2\cdot\frac{2k_{i-1}-1}{2k_{i}-1},

and if
c_{i}>3
, we have

\displaystyle V_{k_{i-1},k_{i}}^{\#}(\{1\}^{c_{i}-3})

\displaystyle=\frac{2k_{i-1}-1}{2k_{i}-1}\sum_{k_{i-1}\geq l_{1}\geq\cdots\geq l_{c_{i}-3}\geq k_{i}}\frac{2^{\triangle(k_{i-1},l_{1})+\triangle(l_{1},l_{2})+\cdots+\triangle(l_{c_{i}-3},k_{i})}}{(2l_{1}-1)\cdots(2l_{c_{i}-1}-1)}

\displaystyle<\frac{2k_{i-1}-1}{2k_{i}-1}\cdot C_{1}\log^{c_{i}-3}(2k_{i-1}+1),

where
C_{1}>2
is some positive constant. Then we can express the above in a unified form

\displaystyle V_{k_{i-1},k_{i}}^{\#}(\{1\}^{c_{i}-3})<\frac{2k_{i-1}-1}{2k_{i}-1}\cdot C_{1}\log^{c_{i}-1}(2k_{0}+1).

Notice that
\delta_{0}=1
,
\delta_{d}\in\{2,3\}
and
\delta_{i}\in\{0,1,2\}
for
i=1,2,\ldots,d-1
. So we find

\displaystyle\left|\widetilde{G}_{k_{0}}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})\right|

\displaystyle=\frac{2}{(2k_{0}-1)|(2k_{0}-1)^{2}-z_{0}^{2}|}

\displaystyle\qquad\times\left|\sum_{k_{0}\geq k_{1}\geq\cdots\geq k_{d}\geq 1}\prod_{i=1}^{d}\frac{(-1)^{k_{i}\delta_{i}}(2k_{i}-1)^{\delta_{i}-1}}{(2k_{i}-1)^{2}-z_{i}^{2}}V_{k_{i-1},k_{i}}^{\#}(\{1\}^{c_{i}-3})\right|

\displaystyle<C_{2}\cdot\frac{\log^{c_{1}+\cdots+c_{d}-d}(2k_{0}+1)}{(2k_{0}-1)^{2}-|z_{0}|^{2}}\sum_{k_{0}\geq k_{1}\geq\cdots\geq k_{d}\geq 1}\prod_{i=1}^{d}\frac{2k_{i}-1}{(2k_{i}-1)^{2}-|z_{i}|^{2}},

where
C_{2}
is a positive constant. Let
|z_{\max}|=\max\{|z_{1}|,\ldots,|z_{d}|\}
, then

\displaystyle\left|\widetilde{G}_{k_{0}}(\boldsymbol{c};\boldsymbol{z})\right|

\displaystyle<C_{3}\cdot\frac{\log^{c_{1}+\cdots+c_{d}-d}(2k_{0}+1)}{(2k_{0}-1)^{2}-|z_{0}|^{2}}\left(\sum_{k=1}^{k_{0}}\frac{1}{2k-1-|z_{\max}|^{2}}\right)^{d}

\displaystyle<C_{4}\cdot\frac{\log^{c_{1}+\cdots+c_{d}}(2k_{0}+1)}{(2k_{0}-1)^{2}-|z_{0}|^{2}}

\displaystyle<C_{5}\cdot\frac{\log^{c_{1}+\cdots+c_{d}}(2k_{0}+1)}{k_{0}^{2}},

where
C_{3},C_{4}
and
C_{5}
are positive constants. Therefore by setting
a=c_{1}+\cdots+c_{d}
,
b=2
and
C=C_{5}
, we conclude the result.
∎
