Long Range Energy-energy Correlator at the LHC
Yuxun Guo, Xiaohui Liu, Feng Yuan

Abstract
We study the forward-backward azimuthal angular correlations of hadrons in association with multi-particle production in the central rapidity region in proton-proton collisions at the LHC. We apply the nucleon energy-energy correlator framework, where the spinning gluon distribution introduces a nontrivial cos ⁡ ( 2 ​ ϕ ) \cos(2\phi) asymmetries. We will demonstrate that the fundamental helicity structure of QCD amplitudes predicts a unique power counting rule: cos ⁡ ( 2 ​ ϕ ) \cos(2\phi) asymmetry starts at 𝒪 ⁡ ( α s 2 ) {\cal O}(\alpha_{s}^{2}) order for dijet, 𝒪 ⁡ ( α s ) {\cal O}(\alpha_{s}) for three jet and 𝒪 ⁡ ( 1 ) {\cal O}(1) for four (and more) jet productions. Our results will help us to understand the long standing puzzle of nearside ridge behavior observed in high multiplicity events of p ​ p pp collisions at the LHC.

Discussion and Conclusion.

Before conclusion, we would like to comment on the azimuthal
\cos(2\phi)
asymmetry in multiple jets production beyond dijet case. First, let us examine the case for three jet production. Applying a similar equation of (
5
) and the helicity amplitude rule
[
27
,
28
]
, it is straightforward to show that
\hat{\sigma}_{2}
vanishes at the tree-level for
gg\to ggg
too. At one-loop order, it will receive contributions from the interference between
{\cal A}^{(1)}(++++-)
and
{\cal A}^{(0)}(--++-)

\langle\cos(2\phi)\rangle
for three jet production at
\alpha_{s}
order.

For four jet production, however,
\cos(2\phi)
asymmetry starts at the leading order, where
\hat{\sigma}_{2}
comes from, e.g.,
{\cal A}^{(0)}(++++--){\cal A}^{(0)*}(--++--)
and these are the amplitudes that contribute to
\hat{\sigma}_{0}
too. Therefore. the
\cos(2\phi)
asymmetry is order 1. The same conclusion holds for five and more jets production. In Table

\cos(2\phi)

The
\alpha_{s}
hierarchy of
\langle\cos(2\phi)\rangle
depending on the number of jets is a unique prediction from the NEEC gluon distributions and the helicity structure of parton scattering amplitudes in QCD. It is crucial to test this prediction and pave the way to decipher the underlying mechanism for the nearside ridge in high multiplicity events of
pp
collisions. In particular, the higher multiplicity events may favor a higher number of jets, where the
\cos(2\phi)
asymmetries will be larger, in qualitative agreement with the experiment observation
[

.

In summary, we studied forward-backward azimuthal angular correlations in NEEC measurements in association with multi-jet production in the central rapidity region at the LHC.
We demonstrated a peculiar prediction for multi-jet production, as a result of double helicity-flip for the
\cos(2\phi)
asymmetry and the helicity structures of parton scattering amplitudes in QCD, where
\langle\cos(2\phi)\rangle
starts at
{\cal O}(\alpha_{s}^{2})
for dijet,
{\cal O}(\alpha_{s})
for three jet, and
{\cal O}(1)

A.1

Virtual contributions come from two-loop amplitude of
A^{(2)}(++++)

A^{(1)}(++--)

\hat{\sigma}_{2}^{(2)}

\hat{\sigma}_{2}^{(2)}

35

\left|\mathcal{M}(\mu;\{p\})\right>=4\pi\alpha_{S}(\mu)\sum_{n=0}\left(\frac{\alpha_{S}(\mu)}{4\pi}\right)^{n}\left|\mathcal{M}^{(n)}(\mu;\{p\}\right>\ ,

\mathcal{M}^{abcd}(\mu;\{p\})=\sum_{i}\mathcal{C}_{i}^{abcd}\mathcal{M}_{i}(\mu;\{p\})\ ,

\left|\mathcal{M}\right>=(\mathcal{M}_{1},\mathcal{M}_{2},\cdots)

\displaystyle\left<\mathcal{M}|\mathcal{M}\right>

\displaystyle\sum_{abcd}\sum_{i,j}\mathcal{M}_{i}^{*}(\mu;\{p\})\mathcal{C}_{i}^{abcd*}\mathcal{C}_{j}^{abcd}\mathcal{M}_{j}(\mu;\{p\})

\displaystyle\sum_{i,j}\mathcal{M}_{i}^{*}(\mu;\{p\}){\cal C\!C}_{ij}\mathcal{M}_{j}(\mu;\{p\})\ ,

\displaystyle\left|\mathcal{M}_{m}^{(1)}(\mu^{2};\{p\})\right>_{\rm{R.S.}}

\displaystyle\bm{I}^{(1)}(\epsilon,\mu;\{p\})\left|\mathcal{M}_{m}^{(0)}(\mu^{2};\{p\})\right>_{\rm{R.S.}}

\displaystyle\bm{I}^{(1)}(\epsilon,\mu;\{p\})\left|\mathcal{M}_{m}^{(1)}(\mu^{2};\{p\})\right>_{\rm{R.S.}}

\displaystyle\bm{I}^{(2)}(\epsilon,\mu;\{p\})\left|\mathcal{M}_{m}^{(0)}(\mu^{2};\{p\})\right>_{\rm{R.S.}}

\displaystyle\left|\mathcal{M}_{m}^{(2)\rm{fin}}(\mu^{2};\{p\})\right>_{\rm{R.S.}}\ ,
