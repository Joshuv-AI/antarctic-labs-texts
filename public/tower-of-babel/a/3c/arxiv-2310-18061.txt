Abstract
Building upon the comprehensive framework outlined in the author’s recent collaborative book 1 , this manuscript delivers a brief overview of the 1 + 4 1+4 -dimensional de Sitter (dS 4 ) group, its accompanying Lie algebra, and the corresponding (co-)adjoint orbits, with the latter assuming significance as potential classical elementary systems within the framework of dS 4 .

A discrete symmetry and energy ambiguity

Let us associate with any

\not{x}
as given below:

\displaystyle\not{x}=x^{\alpha}\gamma_{\alpha}=\begin{pmatrix}\textbf{1}x^{0}&-\textbf{x}\\

where, in the scalar-vector representation of a quaternion,
\textbf{x}=(x^{4},\vec{x})\in\mathbb{H}
(equivalently, in the Euclidean metric notations,

and the collection of
4\times 4

\not{x}

\displaystyle\not{x}^{\prime}={g}\not{x}{g}^{-1}=\begin{pmatrix}\textbf{1}x^{\prime 0}&-\textbf{x}^{\prime}\\
{\textbf{x}}^{\prime{\scriptscriptstyle\bigstar}}&-\textbf{1}x^{\prime 0}\end{pmatrix}\,.

(2.8)

An intriguing aspect worth emphasizing here is that within the context of Sp
(2,2)
, the group action of
\gamma^{0}
aligns with a discrete symmetry:

\not{x}\;\mapsto\;\not{x}^{\prime}=\gamma^{0}\not{x}\left(\gamma^{0}\right)^{-1}=\begin{pmatrix}\textbf{1}x^{0}&\textbf{x}\\
-{\textbf{x}}^{\scriptscriptstyle\bigstar}&-\textbf{1}x^{0}\end{pmatrix}\,.

(2.9)

Under this discrete symmetry, the dS

4
