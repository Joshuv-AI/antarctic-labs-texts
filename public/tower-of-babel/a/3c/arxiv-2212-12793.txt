On the path partition of graphs
Mekkia Kouider, Mohamed Zamime

Abstract
Let G \displaystyle G be a graph of order n \displaystyle n . The maximum and minimum degree of G \displaystyle G are denoted by Δ \displaystyle\Delta and δ \displaystyle\delta respectively. The path partition number μ ⁡ ( G ) \displaystyle\mu(G) of a graph G \displaystyle G is the minimum number of paths needed to partition the vertices of G \displaystyle G . Magnant, Wang and Yuan conjectured that μ ⁡ ( G ) ≤ max ⁡ { n δ + 1 , ( Δ − δ ) ​ n ( Δ + δ ) } . \displaystyle\mu(G)\leq\max\left\{\frac{n}{\delta+1},\frac{\left(\Delta-\delta\right)n}{\left(\Delta+\delta\right)}\right\}. In this work, we give a positive answer to this conjecture, for Δ ≥ 2 ​ δ \displaystyle\Delta\geq 2\delta .

In this work, we deal with the partition problem. The cover

Theorem 4

\displaystyle G

\displaystyle n
