A Resolution of the Diagonal for Smooth Toric Varieties
Reginald Anderson

Abstract
. Beilinson gave a resolution of the diagonal for complex projective spaces, which Bayer-Popescu-Sturmfels generalized to what they refer to as unimodular projective toric varieties. The unimodular condition in Bayer-Popescu-Sturmfels’ convention is more restrictive than being smooth. In the smooth, non-unimodular setting, extra vertices appear in the finite cellular complex which Bayer-Popescu-Sturmfels previously used to resolve the diagonal for unimodular projective toric varieties. We use the floor function to assign monomial labelings in a convex manner, and show that this assignment is compatible with the graded algebra involving the irrelevant ideal to give a resolution of the diagonal for a smooth projective toric variety.

1.
Introduction

The derived category
\mathrm{D}^{b}_{\mathrm{Coh}}(X_{\Sigma})
forms part of the B-side of homological mirror symmetry. Beilinson’s resolution of the diagonal
[
Beilinson1978
]
was crucial in understanding
\mathrm{D}^{b}_{\mathrm{Coh}}(\mathbb{P}^{n})
, since it gives strong, full exceptional collections of line bundles on complex projective spaces, which yields a description of any object of
\mathrm{D}^{b}_{\mathrm{Coh}}(\mathbb{P}^{n})
in terms of the elements of a given exceptional collection. Since
\mathbb{P}^{n}
is a smooth projective toric variety, we can ask for a generalization of Beilinson’s resolution of the diagonal to any toric variety. However, this will be too broad, so we will restrict our attention to smooth toric varieties. Steps in this direction were started by Bayer-Popescu-Sturmfels, who generalized Beilinson’s resolution of the diagonal to what they call “unimodular” toric varieties, which is more restrictive than being smooth. For instance,
\mathbb{P}^{2}
blown up at a torus-invariant point, and then blown-up again along the exceptional divisor is no longer unimodular. Also, any non-trivially weighted projective space is no longer unimodular. Since there are many smooth projective toric varieties which are not unimodular in the sense of
[
bayer-popescu-sturmfels
]
, here we resolve the diagonal for a smooth projective toric variety. That is, in this paper, we study the bounded derived category of coherent sheaves
\mathrm{D}^{b}_{\mathrm{Coh}}(X_{\Sigma})
on a smooth projective toric variety
X_{\Sigma}
over an algebraically closed field
\Bbbk
. In particular, we produce several cellular resolutions
[
miller-sturmfels
]
of the diagonal for such a variety using a hyperplane arrangement
\mathcal{H}
, which we intersect with the real affine span
\mathbb{R}L
of a lattice
L
, and quotient by the additive action of
L
to get a finite cellular complex
\mathcal{H}_{L}/L
, subject to a perturbation term
\epsilon
. This paper focuses on the following theorem.

Theorem 1.1
.

The complex
(\mathcal{F}^{\bullet}_{\mathcal{H}_{L}^{\epsilon}/L},\partial^{\epsilon})
gives a resolution of
\mathcal{O}_{\Delta}
as an object of
D^{b}_{Coh}(X_{\Sigma}\times X_{\Sigma})
for
X_{\Sigma}
a smooth toric variety.

The methods of
[
bayer-popescu-sturmfels
]
run into trouble with extra vertices which appear in the smooth, non-unimodular setting. We use the floor function to give a new way to label vertices in a convex manner, and take care to show that we do yield the desired resolution.

Recently, there have been several teams of researchers interested in resolutions of the diagonal for smooth projective toric varieties and for simplicial toric varieties viewed as toric Deligne-Mumford stacks
[
hanlon2024resolutionstoricsubvarietiesline
]
,
[
Brown_2024
]
,
[
favero2022homotopypathalgebras
]
. Each approach takes a different viewpoint:
[
hanlon2024resolutionstoricsubvarietiesline
]
is most aligned with the perspective of wrapped Fukaya categories;
[
Brown_2024
]
most aligns with the perspective of semigroups; and
[
favero2022homotopypathalgebras
]
uses the perspective of homotopy path algebras on a stratified real torus. Our approach generalizes the approach of Bayer-Popescu-Sturmfels in
[
bayer-popescu-sturmfels
]
. Our approach gives a resolution in families, sensitive to a perturbation term
\epsilon
which allows us to deform the finite cellular complex and resolution which we construct. Furthermore, while the constructions in
[
hanlon2024resolutionstoricsubvarietiesline
]
and
[
Brown_2024
]
do not depend on the higher-dimensional cones of the toric fan
\Sigma
beyond the rays, for non-unimodular toric varieties (in the sense of Bayer-Popescu-Sturmfels), the construction here gives a virtual resolution of
\mathcal{O}_{\Delta}
, modulo torsion from the irrelevant ideal of
X_{\Sigma}\times X_{\Sigma}
, which depends on the maximal cones of the fan
\Sigma
. Therefore, the approach taken here is novel and constitutes a fundamentally different approach from the others. The approach given here was also generalized to a virtual resolution of the diagonal for toric Deligne-Mumford stacks which are a global quotient of a smooth projective toric variety by a finite group in
[
anderson2023resolutiondiagonaltoricdelignemumford
]
.

Outline

In Section 2, we recall background for coherent sheaves on a smooth projective toric variety, and recall the constructions of Bayer-Popescu-Sturmfels for resolving the diagonal of a unimodular projective toric variety. In Section 3, we prove the main Theorem

. In Section 4, we provide an example to illustrate the main result.
