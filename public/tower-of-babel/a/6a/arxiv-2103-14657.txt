From hydro to jet quenching, coalescence and hadron cascade: a coupled approach to solving the R A ​ A ⊗ v 2 R_{AA}\otimes v_{2} puzzle
Wenbin Zhao, Weiyao Ke, Wei Chen, Tan Luo, Xin-Nian Wang

Abstract
Hydrodynamics and jet quenching are responsible for the elliptic flow v 2 v_{2} and suppression of large transverse momentum ( p T p_{T} ) hadrons, respectively, two of the most important phenomena leading to the discovery of a strongly coupled quark-gluon plasma (QGP) in high-energy heavy-ion collisions. A consistent description of the hadron suppression factor R A ​ A R_{AA} and v 2 v_{2} , especially at intermediate p T p_{T} , however, remains a challenge. We solve this long-standing R A ​ A ⊗ v 2 R_{AA}\otimes v_{2} puzzle by including quark coalescence for hadronization and final state hadron cascade in the coupled linear Boltzmann transport-hydro model that combines concurrent jet transport and hydrodynamic evolution of the bulk medium. We illustrate that quark coalescence and hadron cascade, two keys to solving the puzzle, also lead to a splitting of v 2 v_{2} for pions, kaons and protons in the intermediate p T p_{T} region. We demonstrate for the first time that experimental data on R A ​ A R_{AA} , v 2 v_{2} and their hadron flavor dependence from low to intermediate and high p T p_{T} in high-energy heavy-ion collisions can be understood within this coupled framework.
