About Opposition and Duality in Paraconsistent Type Theory
Juan C. Agudelo-Agudelo, Andrés Sicard-Ramírez

Abstract
A paraconsistent type theory (an extension of a fragment of intuitionistic type theory by adding ‘opposite types’ ) is here extended by adding co-function types. It is shown that, in the extended paraconsistent type system, the opposite type constructor can be viewed as an involution operation that transforms each type into its dual type. Moreover, intuitive interpretations of opposite and co-function types under different interpretations of types are discussed.

In
\mathsf{PL^{S}_{0}}

\supset,\wedge,\vee,\forall
and

\Leftrightarrow

valid. The following strong equivalences are derivable in
\mathsf{PL^{S}_{0}}
(where
superscripts on quantified variables denote their sorts):

\displaystyle\mathop{\neg}(A\wedge B)

\displaystyle\mathbin{\Leftrightarrow_{\mathsf{s}}}\mathop{\neg}A\vee\mathop{\neg}B,

\displaystyle\mathop{\neg}(\forall x^{s})A

\displaystyle\mathbin{\Leftrightarrow_{\mathsf{s}}}(\exists x^{s})\mathop{\neg}A,

\displaystyle\mathop{\neg}(A\vee B)

\displaystyle\mathbin{\Leftrightarrow_{\mathsf{s}}}\mathop{\neg}A\wedge\mathop{\neg}B,

\displaystyle\mathop{\neg}(\exists x^{s})A

\displaystyle\mathop{\neg}\mathop{\neg}A

the contexts of the premises can be different); while the symmetry

constructive negation whose rules are based on the understanding of
negation as falsity, and on the notion of refutation which is dual to
the notion of proof. The distinction between additive and
multiplicative logical constants in
\mathsf{PL^{S}_{1}}
is at least not
evident. Although a reviewer pointed us out that the unique general
elimination rule for co-implication leads to a multiplicative version
of the connective, while the two standard elimination rules leads to
an additive version of the connective, the connection between
multiplicativity/additivity (which are concepts usually defined when
working with sequent calculus) and
general-elimination-rules/standard-elimination-rules (which are
concepts usually defined in natural deduction systems presented in
standard format, that is, where the rules are presented without
entailment relations nor contexts) is not clear for us. In

[

]
, where a natural deduction system (in standard
format) for intuitionistic linear logic is proposed, all elimination
rules (for additive and multiplicative logical constants) are
general. The additivity of logical constants is formalised by adding
labels to the assumptions in context-sharing rules.

As it is pointed out in

Duality is an important general theme that has manifestations in
almost every area of mathematics. Over and over again, it turns out
that one can associate with a given mathematical object a related,

object that helps one to understand the properties of
the object one started with. Despite the importance of duality in
mathematics, there is no single definition that covers all instances

Although there is not a general definition of duality in mathematics,

[
4

In mathematics, a

mathematical structures into other concepts, theorems or structures,
in a one-to-one fashion, often (but not always) by means of an
involution operation: if the dual of
A

of
B
is
A

the dual of
A

A
