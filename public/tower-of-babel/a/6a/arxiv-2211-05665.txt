On Erdős–Dushnik–Miller theorem without AC
Amitayu Banerjee, Alexa Gopaulsingh

Abstract
. In 𝖹𝖥𝖠 \mathsf{ZFA} (Zermelo-Fraenkel set theory with the Axiom of Extensionality weakened to allow the existence of atoms), we prove that the strength of the proposition 𝖤𝖣𝖬 \mathsf{EDM} (“If G = ( V G , E G ) G=(V_{G},E_{G}) is a graph such that V G V_{G} is uncountable, then for all coloring f : [ V G ] 2 → { 0 , 1 } f:[V_{G}]^{2}\rightarrow\{0,1\} either there is an uncountable set monochromatic in color 0 0 , or there is a countably infinite set monochromatic in color 1”) is strictly between 𝖣𝖢 ℵ 𝟣 \mathsf{DC_{\aleph_{1}}} (where 𝖣𝖢 ℵ 𝟣 \mathsf{DC_{\aleph_{1}}} is Dependent Choices for ℵ 1 \aleph_{1} , a weak choice form stronger than Dependent Choices ( 𝖣𝖢 \mathsf{DC} )) and Kurepa’s principle (“Any partially ordered set such that all of its antichains are finite and all of its chains are countable is countable”). Among other new results, we study the relations of 𝖤𝖣𝖬 \mathsf{EDM} with 𝖡𝖯𝖨 \mathsf{BPI} (Boolean Prime Ideal Theorem), 𝖱𝖳 \mathsf{RT} (Ramsey’s Theorem), De Bruijn–Erdős theorem for n n -colorings, Kőnig’s Lemma and several other weak choice forms. Moreover, we answer a part of a question raised by Lajos Soukup.

Proposition 3.7
.

(
\mathsf{ZF}+\mathsf{WOAM}
)

\mathsf{RT}
holds for any locally countable connected graph

If

\mathsf{WOAM}
, there exists an amorphous subset

. Fix some

. Let
V_{0}=\{r\}
. For each

, define
