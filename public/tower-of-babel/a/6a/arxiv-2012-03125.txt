Internal Neighbourhood Structures III: Finite Sum of Subobjects
Partha Pratim Ghosh

Abstract
. The notion of an internal preneighbourhood space on a finitely complete category with finite coproducts and a proper ( E , M ) (\textsf{E},\textsf{M}) -factorisation system such that for each object X X the set of M -subobjects of X X is a complete lattice was initiated in [ 5 ] . The notion of a closure operator, closed morphism and its near allies investigated in [ 6 ] . The present paper provides structural conditions on the triplet ( 𝔸 , 𝖤 , 𝖬 ) (\mbox{${\mathbb{A}}$},\mathsf{E},\mathsf{M}) (with 𝔸 {\mathbb{A}} lextensive) equivalent to the set of M -subobjects of an object closed under finite sums. Equivalent conditions for the set of closed embeddings (closed morphisms) closed under finite sums is also provided. In case when lattices of admissible subobjects (respectively, closed embeddings) are closed under finite sums, the join semilattice of admissible subobjects (respectively, closed embeddings) of a finite sum is shown to be a biproduct of the component join semilattices. Finally, it is shown whenever the set of closed morphisms is closed under finite sums, the set of proper (respectively, separated) morphisms are also closed under finite sums. This leads to equivalent conditions for the full subcategory of compact (respectively, Hausdorff) preneighbourhood spaces to be closed under finite sums.
