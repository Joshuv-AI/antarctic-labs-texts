Effect of Rare-earth Element Substitution in Superconducting R 3 Ni 2 O 7 Under Pressure
Zhiming Pan, Chen Lu, Fan Yang, Congjun Wu

Abstract
Recently, high temperature ( T c ≈ 80 T_{c}\approx 80 K) superconductivity (SC) has been discovered in La 3 Ni 2 O 7 (LNO) under pressure. Question arises whether the transition temperature T c T_{c} could be further enhanced under suitable conditions. A possible route for realizing higher T c T_{c} is element substitution. Similar SC could appear in rare-earth (RE) R 3 Ni 2 O 7 (RNO, R=RE element) material series under pressure. The electronic properties in the RNO materials are dominated by the Ni 3 ​ d 3d orbitals in the bilayer NiO 2 plane. In the strong coupling limit, the SC could be fully characterized by a bilayer single 3 ​ d x 2 − y 2 3d_{x^{2}-y^{2}} -orbital t t - J ∥ J_{\parallel} - J ⟂ J_{\perp} model. Under RE element substitution from La to RE element, the lattice constant decreases and the electronic hopping increases, leading to stronger superexchanges between the 3 ​ d x 2 − y 2 3d_{x^{2}-y^{2}} orbitals. Based on the slave-boson mean-field theory, we explore the pairing nature and the evolution of T c T_{c} in RNO materials. Consequently, it is found that the element substitution does not alter the pairing nature, i.e. the inter-layer s s -wave pairing is always favored in RNO. However, the T c T_{c} increases from La to Sm and a nearly doubled T c T_{c} is achieved for SmNO. This work provides evidence for possible higher T c T_{c} R 3 Ni 2 O 7 materials, which may be realized in further experiments.

Slave boson mean field theory

In the slave boson mean field theory

Lee et al. 2006

is the spinon creation operator and

is the holon annihilation operator.
The super exchange term can be decoupled in the hopping and pairing channel, i.e., for the inter-layer one,

\displaystyle J_{\perp}\bm{S}_{x^{2}i1}\cdot\bm{S}_{x^{2}i2}

(4)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\Big[\chi_{i\perp}\big(f_{i1\uparrow}^{\dagger}f_{i2\uparrow}+f_{i1\downarrow}^{\dagger}f_{i2\downarrow}\big)+\text{h.c.}-\frac{8}{3J_{\perp}}|\chi_{i\perp}|^{2}\Big]

\displaystyle-\Big[\Delta_{i\perp}\big(f_{i1\uparrow}^{\dagger}f_{i2\downarrow}^{\dagger}-f_{i1\downarrow}^{\dagger}f_{i2\uparrow}^{\dagger}\big)+\text{h.c.}-\frac{8}{3J_{\perp}}|\Delta_{i\perp}|^{2}\Big],

and similar decomposition for the intra-layer super exchange.
In the mean-field ansatz, the hopping and pairing order parameters are represented by their mean-field values

\displaystyle\chi_{ij}^{(\alpha)}=

\displaystyle\frac{3}{8}J_{\parallel}\langle f_{j\alpha\uparrow}^{\dagger}f_{i\alpha\uparrow}+f_{j\alpha\downarrow}^{\dagger}f_{i\alpha\downarrow}\rangle\equiv\chi_{j-i}^{(\alpha)},

Discussion

The superconducting pairing and
T_{c}
in the RNO materials are explored based on the bilayer single
3d_{x^{2}-y^{2}}
orbital
t
-
J_{\parallel}
-
J_{\perp}
model.
The numerical simulation shows that Sm
3
Ni
2
O
7
could exhibit the largest superconducting
T_{c}
.
The scenario proposed here differs from previous weak coupling analysis based on RPA
Zhang et al. 2023e
.
There, the pairing strength as well as superconducting temperature decrease from La to Sm element substitution, and LNO is already the optimal high-
T_{c}
material in the RNO series.
In our strong coupling picture, the superconducting pairing is mainly generated from the effective inter-layer spin superexchange
J_{\perp}
.

J_{\perp}
naturally increases as the inter-layer hopping strength increases under RE element substitution, and the superconductivity would be enhanced.

As we emphasized in the previous work
Lu et al. 2023a
and further confirmed in this work,
the high-
T_{c}
superconductivity is driven by the strong interlayer spin exchange
J_{\perp}
for the
3d_{x^{2}-y^{2}}
orbitals in the RNO candidates.
The inter-layer
s
-wave superconducting pairing in the
3d_{x^{2}-y^{2}}
orbital is favored in the relevant parameter regime, and the intra-layer hopping severs as the mobile engine.
Such a situation is distinct from the
d
-wave pairing in the single-layer curprate
Kotliar and Liu 1988
and multilayer cuprates where the inter-layer exchange
J_{\perp}
is naturally smaller than
J_{\parallel}

Ubbens and Lee 1994
;
Kuboki and A. Lee 1995
;
Maly et al. 1996
;
Nazarenko and Dagotto 1996
;
Medhi et al. 2009
.
The wavefunctions of
3d_{x^{2}-y^{2}}
orbital nearly lie within the plane and the interlayer overlap between
3d_{x^{2}-y^{2}}
orbitals is small.
The strong
J_{\perp}>J_{\parallel}
is hard to be directly generated from the much smaller inter-layer hopping of single
3d_{x^{2}-y^{2}}
orbitals compared to the intra-layer one.
In the RNO materials, inter-layer overlap of
3d_{z^{2}}
orbital wavefunctions persists strong inter-layer hopping.
Strong
J_{\perp}
could be realized for
3d_{z^{2}}
orbitals and is further transmitted to
3d_{x^{2}-y^{2}}
orbitals under strong Hund’s coupling.

Although we focus on the reduced single
3d_{x^{2}-y^{2}}
orbital model, the effect of hidden
3d_{z^{2}}
degrees of freedom plays an important role.

3d_{z^{2}}
electrons are nearly localized within the plane and has strong inter-layer hopping, inducing the strong inter-layer super-exchange
J_{\perp}
.
There also exist inter-layer pairings for the almost half-filled
3d_{z^{2}}
orbitals.
However, the inter-layer
3d_{z^{2}}
pairings are also nearly localized.
Even worse,
3d_{z^{2}}
orbitals are nearly half-filled and holon condensation temperature is quite low due to its low density.

3d_{z^{2}}
orbitals do not contribute to the superconducting transport behavior directly.
On the contrary, the Hund’s rule combines the two
E_{g}
orbitals into the spin-triplet states,
and induces effective strong inter-layer couplings
J_{\perp}
between
3d_{x^{2}-y^{2}}
orbitals.

While the simple single orbital model characterizes the most relevant physics of the superconducting LNO material
Lu et al. 2023a
,
further theoretical work and experimental verification are necessary to understand the interplay between
3d_{x^{2}-y^{2}}
and
3d_{z^{2}}
orbitals.
Moreover, the nature of the superconducting in
3d_{x^{2}-y^{2}}
and
3d_{z^{2}}
orbitals is different from each other.
A comprehensive analysis takes into account both degrees of freedom will be helpful.
Possible chemical doping approach may alter the superconducting behavior and further increase the
