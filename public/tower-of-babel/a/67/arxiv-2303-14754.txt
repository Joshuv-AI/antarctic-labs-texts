Categories with dependent arrows
Iosif Petrakis

Abstract
We present an abstract, categorical formulation of dependent functions in a fundamental manner and independently from the Sigma-construction. For that, we define first the notion of a category with family-arrows, or a fam -category. A ( fam , Σ ) (\textnormal{{fam}},\Sigma) -category is a fam -category with Sigma-objects, where a ( fam , Σ ) (\textnormal{{fam}},\Sigma) -category with a terminal object is exactly a type-category of Pitts, or a category with attributes of Cartmell. We introduce categories with dependent arrows, or dep -categories, and we show that every ( fam , Σ ) (\textnormal{{fam}},\Sigma) -category is a dep -category in a canonical way. The notion of a Sigma-object in a dep -Category is affected by the existence of dependent arrows, and we show that every ( fam , Σ ) (\textnormal{{fam}},\Sigma) -category is a ( dep , Σ ) (\textnormal{{dep}},\Sigma) -category in a canonical way. Keywords : Category theory, dependent type theory, categories with attributes
