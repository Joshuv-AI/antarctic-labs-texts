Hadronic production of Ξ b ​ c \Xi_{bc} with the intrinsic heavy-quark content at a fixed-target experiment at the LHC
Hong-Tai Li a, Xu-Chang Zheng a, Jiang Yan a, Xing-Gang Wu a, Gu Chen b

Abstract
In this paper, we make a detailed study on the hadronic production of the Ξ b ​ c \Xi_{bc} baryon at a fixed target experiment at the LHC (After@LHC). In estimating the production cross sections, the ( g + g ) (g+g) , ( g + c ) (g+c) and ( g + b ) (g+b) production mechanisms are considered. For the initial heavy quarks, in addition to the extrinsic component, we also consider the intrinsic component. It is found that the ( g + c ) (g+c) and ( g + b ) (g+b) production mechanisms give sizable contributions to the Ξ b ​ c \Xi_{bc} production, and the ( g + b ) (g+b) mechanism dominates the production. The results show that there are about 3.40 × 10 5 3.40\times 10^{5} Ξ b ​ c \Xi_{bc} events that can be produced per year at the After@LHC if the integrated luminosity of the After@LHC can be up to 2 ​ fb − 1 2\,{\rm fb}^{-1} per year. Moreover, the intrinsic heavy quarks can have significant impact on the production, which inversely makes the intrinsic component be possibly tested at the After@LHC.

IV
Conclusions

In the present paper, we have studied the hadronic production of the
\Xi_{bc}
baryon at the suggested fixed-target experiment After@LHC. The integrated cross sections and the differential distributions (
d\sigma/dp_{t}
and
d\sigma/dy
) for the
\Xi_{bc}
production are obtained, and the main theoretical uncertainties for the cross sections are analyzed.

For the initial partons, in addition to the
(g+g)
fusion mechanism, the
(g+c)
and
(g+b)
collision mechanisms are also considered. It is found that the
(g+c)
and
(g+b)
mechanisms give sizable contributions to the
\Xi_{bc}
production, and the
(g+b)
