The Ω c ​ c \Omega_{cc} resonances with negative parity in the chiral constituent quark model
Jin-Bao Wang, Gang Li, Cheng-Rong Deng, Chun-Sheng An, Ju-Jun Xie

Abstract
Spectrum of the low-lying Ω c ​ c \Omega_{cc} resonances with negative parity, which are assumed to be dominated by s ​ c ​ c ​ q ​ q ¯ sccq\bar{q} pentaquark components, is investigated using the chiral constituent quark model. Energies of the Ω c ​ c \Omega_{cc} resonances are obtained by considering the hyperfine interaction between quarks by exchanging Goldstone boson. Possible s ​ c ​ c ​ q ​ q ¯ sccq\bar{q} configurations with spin-parity 1 / 2 − 1/2^{-} , 3 / 2 − 3/2^{-} and 5 / 2 − 5/2^{-} are taken into account. Numerical results show that the lowest Ω c ​ c \Omega_{cc} resonances with negative parity may lie at 4050 ± 100 4050\pm 100 MeV. In addition, the transitions of the Ω c ​ c \Omega_{cc} resonance to a pseudoscalar meson and a ground baryon state are also investigated within the chiral Lagrangian approach. We expect that these Ω c ​ c \Omega_{cc} resonances could be observed in the D ​ Ξ c D\Xi_{c} channel by future experiments.

II.1
Hamiltonian and wave functions

In present work, the constituent quark model is employed to study

\Omega_{cc}

\Omega_{cc}

eigenvalue

The parameter

has been taken to be

As discussed in details in Ref.

In this work, the value of

\displaystyle H_{eff}^{Pqq}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{j}\bar{\psi}_{j}\gamma_{\mu}^{j}\gamma_{5}^{j}\psi_{j}\partial^{\mu}\phi_{m}\,,

(8)

\displaystyle H_{eff}^{Vqq}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\sum_{j}\bar{\psi}_{j}(a\gamma_{\mu}^{j}+\frac{ib\sigma_{\mu\nu}k_{M}^{\nu}}{2m_{j}})\phi_{m}^{\mu}\psi_{j}\,,

(9)

respectively, where the summation on
j

\psi_{j}
represents the quark field, and

\phi_{m}
and
\phi_{m}^{\mu}
are the pseudoscalar and vector meson fields.

is the constituent mass of the
jth

and

8

(

\displaystyle T_{d}^{Pqq}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{j}\left(\frac{\omega_{M}}{E_{f}+M_{f}}\sigma_{j}\cdot\vec{P}_{f}+\frac{\omega_{M}}{E_{i}+M_{i}}\sigma_{j}\cdot\vec{P}_{i}-\sigma_{j}\cdot\vec{k}_{M}+\frac{\omega_{M}}{2\mu_{q}}\sigma_{j}\cdot\vec{p}_{j}\right)X^{j}_{P}\exp\{-i\vec{k}_{M}\cdot\vec{r}_{j}\}\,,

(10)

\displaystyle T^{Vqq}_{d,T}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{j}\left\{i\frac{b^{\prime}}{2m_{j}}\vec{\sigma}_{j}\cdot(\vec{k}_{M}\times\vec{\epsilon})+\frac{a}{2\mu_{q}}\vec{p}_{j}\cdot\vec{\epsilon}\right\}X_{V}^{j}\exp\{-i\vec{k}_{M}\cdot\vec{r}_{j}\}\,,

(11)

\displaystyle T^{Vqq}_{d,L}

\displaystyle\sum_{j}\frac{aM_{V}}{|\vec{k}_{M}|}X_{V}^{j}\exp\{-i\vec{k}_{M}\cdot\vec{r}_{j}\}\,,

Up to now, there are no solid experimental data for the

\Omega_{cc}
resonances, while theoretical investigations on the
doubly heavy baryon resonances have been intensively taken using
various of approaches, such as the constituent quark
model
Lu:2017meb
;
Xiao:2017udy
;
Ebert:2002ig
;
Albertus:2006ya
;
Faessler:2009xn
;
Karliner:2014gca
;
Li:2019tbn
,
QCD sum rules
Chen:2017sbg
, chiral perturbation
theory
Guo:2017vcf
;
Yao:2018ifh
;
Li:2017pxa
, the unitarized
coupled channel approach
Yan:2018zdt
, and the lattice QCD
calculations
Mathur:2018rwu
, etc. The corresponding obtained
energies for the
P
-wave
\Omega_{cc}
in a three-quark picture are
around
4000-4200
MeV in most of the literatures, and one may note
that in Ref.
Yan:2018zdt
, the
S
-wave interactions between
pseudo-Nambu-Goldstone bosons (
\pi
,
K
and
\eta
) and the

J^{P}=1/2^{+}
ground state doubly charmed baryons in the energy
region around the corresponding thresholds are investigated, two
quasistable narrow
J^{P}=1/2^{-}

\Omega_{cc}
are predicted to
lie at the energy below
4200
MeV, and their strong decay mode is
predicted to be only the
\Omega_{cc}\pi^{0}
, which is isospin
breaking channel. Therefore, the two obtained
\Omega_{cc}
resonances
in a meson-baryon picture should be very narrow.

One may also study the spectrum of low-lying

\Omega_{cc}
resonances with negative parity using the chiral
constituent quark model in a three-quark
picture
Charmhyperons
. As
P
-wave states whose parity are
negative, there are three possible
\Omega_{cc}
configurations:

\displaystyle|scc,1\rangle

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle[21]_{X}[21]_{FS}[21]_{F}[21]_{S}[1^{3}]_{C}\,,

\displaystyle|scc,2\rangle

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle[21]_{X}[21]_{FS}[3]_{F}[21]_{S}[1^{3}]_{C}\,,

(34)

\displaystyle|scc,3\rangle

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle[21]_{X}[21]_{FS}[21]_{F}[3]_{S}[1^{3}]_{C}\,,

whose spin-parity quantum number

may be
1/2^{-}
or
3/2^{-}
for
the first two configurations, and
1/2^{-}
,
3/2^{-}
or
5/2^{-}

quark model as in Ref.
Charmhyperons
lead to the following
values for the energies of the three
\Omega_{cc}

E_{1}=4219\,\mathrm{MeV},\hskip 4.26773ptE_{2}=4246\,\mathrm{MeV},\hskip 4.26773ptE_{3}=4257\,\mathrm{MeV},

respectively. Consequently, the energies of low-lying

states in the five-quark picture are lower than those in the
three-quark picture, this conclusion is the same as that for the

\Omega^{*}
resonances

.

In Ref.
Ebert:2002ig
, a relativistic quark model was applied
to study the spectrum of doubly heavy baryons. Considering the
\Omega_{cc}

resonances to be dominated by three-quark components, it was obtained that the

, a three-quark model was employed

In any case, we can conclude that the
\Omega_{cc}
resonances should lie at
a energy below
4200

Yan:2018zdt

From Fig.

\Omega_{cc}

3/2^{-}

IV
Summary

In present work, we investigate the spectrum of low-lying

\Omega_{cc}
resonances with negative parity as pentaquark states,
using the chiral constituent quark model within a five-quark
picture. We obtain ten pentaquark states with spin-parity

J^{P}=1/2^{-},~3/2^{-},~5/2^{-}
, which lie at
4050\pm 100
MeV.
Most of the obtained states are above the isospin breaking decay
channel
\pi\Omega_{cc}
, but below the other meson-baryon
channels. So we just try to calculate the flavor, spin, orbital, and
color overlap factor for the final
MB
states and the residual
three-quark-meson configurations of the
\Omega_{cc}
states after
the annihilation of the quark-antiquark
q\bar{q}\rightarrow M
.
It is found that several ones of the presently obtained
\Omega_{cc}

may couple strongly to
D\Xi_{c}
or
\bar{K}\Xi_{cc}
channels.
One may expect that
these calculations here could be compared with the future
experimental measurements which are likely to be done by Belle II
and/or LHCb.
