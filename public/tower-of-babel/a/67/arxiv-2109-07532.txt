Finding Efficient Domination for ( S 1 , 2 , 5 , S 3 , 3 , 3 ) (S_{1,2,5},S_{3,3,3}) -Free Chordal Bipartite Graphs in Polynomial Time
Andreas Brandstädt

Abstract
A vertex set D D in a finite undirected graph G G is an efficient dominating set ( e.d.s. for short) of G G if every vertex of G G is dominated by exactly one vertex of D D . The Efficient Domination (ED) problem, which asks for the existence of an e.d.s. in G G , is known to be ℕ ​ ℙ \mathbb{NP} -complete for chordal bipartite graphs as well as for P 7 P_{7} -free graphs, and even for very restricted H H -free bipartite graph classes such as for K 1 , 4 K_{1,4} -free bipartite graphs as well as for C 4 C_{4} -free bipartite graphs while it is solvable in polynomial time for P 8 P_{8} -free bipartite graphs as well as for S 1 , 3 , 3 S_{1,3,3} -free bipartite graphs and for S 1 , 1 , 5 S_{1,1,5} -free bipartite graphs. Here we show that ED can be solved in polynomial time for ( S 1 , 2 , 5 , S 2 , 3 , 3 ) (S_{1,2,5},S_{2,3,3}) -free chordal bipartite graphs.

The notion of efficient domination was introduced by Biggs

3

The Exact Cover Problem asks for a subset

[

In
[
1
,
2
]

\mathbb{NP}
-complete.

For a set

For

, let

denote the chordless path with

denote the complete graph with

vertices (clearly,

).
For

, let

For indices

,

(u,r_{1},\ldots,r_{i})

(u,s_{1},\ldots,s_{j})

(u,t_{1},\ldots,t_{k})

, chair is

In

-free chordal unipolar graphs and thus, in general, for

-free graphs.
In

-free graphs (which leads to a dichotomy).

A bipartite graph

-free for every

-complete for

-free bipartite graphs.
Moreover, ED is

-complete for planar bipartite graphs

]

-complete for

-free bipartite graphs and for

-free bipartite graphs.

In

In

bipartite graphs). Moreover, Lu and Tang
