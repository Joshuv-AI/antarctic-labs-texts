The global B − L B-L symmetry in the flavor-unified SU ⁡ ( N ) {\rm SU}(N) theories
Ning Chen, Ying-nan Mao, Zhaolong Teng

Abstract
We study the origin of the global B − L B-L symmetry in a class of flavor-unified theories with gauge groups of SU ⁡ ( N ≥ 6 ) {\rm SU}(N\geq 6) . In particular, we focus on the SU ⁡ ( 8 ) {\rm SU}(8) theory which can minimally embed three-generational SM fermions non-trivially. A reformulation of the third law for the flavor sector proposed by Georgi is useful to manifest the underlying global symmetries. The ’t Hooft anomaly matching and the generalized neutrality conditions for Higgs fields play the key roles in defining the B − L B-L symmetry. Based on the global B − L B-L symmetry, we count the Higgs fields that can develop the VEVs and the massless sterile neutrinos in the SU ⁡ ( 8 ) {\rm SU}(8) theory. We also prove that a global B − L B-L symmetry can always be defined in any SU ⁡ ( N ≥ 6 ) {\rm SU}(N\geq 6) theory when it is spontaneously broken to the SM gauge symmetry.
