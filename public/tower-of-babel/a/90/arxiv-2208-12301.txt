The Calkin algebra, Kazhdan’s property (T), and strongly self-absorbing C ∗ \mathrm{C}^{*} -algebras Thanks: Partially supported by NSERC. Thanks: ORCID iD https://orcid.org/0000-0001-7703-6931
Ilijas Farah

Abstract
. It is well-known that the relative commutant of every separable nuclear C ∗ \mathrm{C}^{*} -subalgebra of the Calkin algebra has a unital copy of Cuntz algebra 𝒪 ∞ \mathcal{O}_{\infty} . We prove that the Calkin algebra has a separable C ∗ \mathrm{C}^{*} -subalgebra whose relative commutant has no simple, unital, and noncommutative C ∗ \mathrm{C}^{*} -subalgebra. On the other hand, the corona of every stable, separable C ∗ \mathrm{C}^{*} -algebra that tensorially absorbs the Jiang–Su algebra 𝒵 {\mathcal{Z}} has the property that the relative commutant of every separable C ∗ \mathrm{C}^{*} -subalgebra contains a unital copy of 𝒵 {\mathcal{Z}} . Analogous result holds for other strongly self-absorbing C ∗ \mathrm{C}^{*} -algebras. As an application, the Calkin algebra is not isomorphic to the corona of the stabilization of the Cuntz algebra 𝒪 ∞ \mathcal{O}_{\infty} , any other Kirchberg algebra, or even the corona of the stabilization of any unital, 𝒵 {\mathcal{Z}} -stable C ∗ \mathrm{C}^{*} -algebra.

1.2.

-algebras

A unital

-algebra
