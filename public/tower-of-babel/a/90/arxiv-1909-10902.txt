On the Bruhat-Tits stratification for GU(2,2) type Rapoport-Zink space: unramified case
Haining Wang

Abstract
. In this note we study the supersingular locus of the GU ⁡ ( 2 , 2 ) \mathrm{GU}(2,2) Shimura variety modulo a prime which is unramified in the imaginary quadratic extension. The supersingular locus of this Shimura variety can be related to the basic Rapoport-Zink space whose special fibre is described by the Bruhat-Tits stratification. The description for this supersingular locus in the case where the prime is inert in imaginary quadratic field is already known to Howard and Pappas by exploiting the exceptional isomorphism. Our method is more direct without using the exceptional isomorphism.

1.
Introduction

1.1.
Motivation

In this note we study the supersingular locus of the
\mathrm{GU}(2,2)
type Shimura variety at an odd prime
p
which is unramified in the imaginary quadratic field. When
p
is odd and inert in the corresponding imaginary quadratic field, the results in this note are already known to Howard and Pappas
[
HP14
]
. Their method exploits the exceptional isomorphism between
\mathrm{SU}(2,2)
and
\mathrm{Spin}(4,2)
. In this note, we show that one can work directly with the unitary group. For the
\mathrm{GU}(2,2)
type Shimura variety, the supersingular locus agrees with the so-called basic locus for the Newton stratification and thus the problem of describing the supersingular locus is equivalent to the problem of describing the underlying reduced scheme of the basic Rapoport-Zink space according to the Rapoport-Zink uniformizaiton theorem. Our description is via the so-called
Bruhat-Tits stratification
. This terminology comes from the pioneering work of
[
Vol10
]
and
[
VW10
]
where the supersingular locus of the
\mathrm{GU}(1,n-1)
type Shimura variety is studied. The Bruhat-Tits stratification roughly speaking comes from classifying the relative position between the lattices coming from the Dieudonné modules of the
p
-divisible groups parametrized by the Rapoport-Zink space and the lattices representing the faces of the Bruhat-Tits building of the automorphism group of the isocrystal with additional structures associated to the base point of the Rapoport-Zink space. Our direct approach has certain advantage over the approach via the exceptional isomorphism: one can expect to use the results in this article to describe the
\mathrm{GU}(2,2)
type Rapoport-Zink spaces with parahoric level structures using a similar approach as in
[
Wang19
]
. On the other hand, the
\mathrm{GSpin}
type Rapoport-Zink space used by Howard and Pappas is of Hodge type and the construction of the Rapoport-Zink space with parahoric level structure is not constructed yet. Our approach is also modelled over the group theoretic approach of
[
GH15
]
and can be seen as a translation of their result from the equi-characteristic case to the present mixed characteristic case.

1.2.
Integral model of Shimura variety

Let
E
be an imaginary quadratic extension of
\mathbb{Q}
with ring of integers
\mathcal{O}_{E}
and let
*
be the non-trivial automorphism of
E
over
\mathbb{Q}
. Let
V
be a Hermitian space with Hermitian form
(\cdot,\cdot)
over
E
of signature
(2,2)
. We denote by
G=\mathrm{GU}(V)
the group of unitary similitudes of
V
. We consider the Hodge cocharacter
h:\mathbb{G}_{m}\rightarrow G(\mathbb{C})
sending
z\in\mathbb{C}^{\times}
to
\text{diag}(z,z,1,1)
.

We assume there is an
\mathcal{O}_{E}
lattice
\Lambda\subset V
which is selfdual with respect to
(\cdot,\cdot)
.
Note
h
defines a decomposition of
V_{\mathbb{C}}=V_{1}\oplus V_{2}
where
V_{1}
is the subspace of
V
that
h(z)
acts by
z
and
V_{2}
is the subspace of
V
that
h(z)
acts by
\bar{z}
. We fix an open compact subgroup
U^{p}
of
G(\mathbb{A}^{p}_{f})
that we assume is sufficiently small and we define
U_{p}=G(\mathbb{Z}_{p})
. Finally we let
U=U_{p}U^{p}
.

Given the PEL datum
(E,*,V,(\cdot,\cdot),h,\Lambda)
cf.
[
Ko92
]
, we consider the following moduli problem
\mathfrak{S}h_{U^{p}}
over
\mathbb{Z}_{(p)}
. For a scheme
S
over
\mathbb{Z}_{(p)}
,
\mathfrak{S}h_{U^{p}}(S)
classifies the set of isomorphism classes of the quadruple
(A,\iota,\lambda,\eta)
where

-

A
is an abelian scheme of relative dimension
4
over
S
;

-

\lambda:A\rightarrow A^{\vee}
is a polarization which is
\mathcal{O}_{E}
-linear and of degree prime to
p
;

-

\iota:\mathcal{O}_{E}\rightarrow\End_{S}(A)
is a morphism such that
\lambda\circ\iota(a^{*})\circ\lambda^{-1}=\iota(a)^{\vee}
and satisfies the Kottwitz condition

(1.1)

\mathrm{det}(\iota(a);\mathrm{Lie}(A))=\mathrm{det}(a;V_{1})

for all
a\in\mathcal{O}_{E}
. Note this equality has to be interpreted as the way explained in
[
Ko92
, page 390]
;

-

\eta:V\otimes_{\mathbb{Q}}{\mathbb{A}}^{(p)}_{f}\rightarrow V^{(p)}(A)
is a
U^{p}
-orbit of
E\otimes_{\mathbb{Q}}\mathbb{A}^{(p)}_{f}
-linear isomorphisms which respects the Weil-pairing on the righthand side and the form
(\cdot,\cdot)
on the lefthand side. Here
V^{(p)}(A)=\prod_{p^{\prime}\neq p}T_{p^{\prime}}(A)\otimes\mathbb{A}^{(p)}_{f}
is the prime to
p
-part of the rational Tate module of
A
.

Let
\mathbb{F}
be an algebraically closed field containing
\mathbb{F}_{p}
. In this note we are concerned with the structure of the supersingular locus
\mathrm{Sh}^{ss}_{U^{p}}
in
\mathfrak{S}h_{U^{p}}\otimes\mathbb{F}
considered as a reduced closed subscheme. The structure depends on whether
p
is split or
p
is inert in
E
. Indeed, our starting point of analyzing the supersingular locus is the uniformization theorem of Rapoport-Zink
[
RZ96
, Theorem 6.1]
which takes the following form

(1.2)

\alpha:I(\mathbb{Q})\backslash\mathcal{N}_{\text{red}}\times G(\mathbb{A}^{p}_{f})/U^{p}\xrightarrow{\sim}\mathrm{Sh}^{ss}_{U^{p}}.

Here
\mathcal{N}_{\text{red}}
is the underlying reduced scheme of a Rapoport-Zink space
\mathcal{N}
and
I
is an inner form of
G
. When
p
is split in
E
, then the Rapoport-Zink space is of EL type and when
p
is inert in
E
, then the Rapoport-Zink space is of PEL type. We will discuss the two cases separately in the next two subsections. First, we fix a base point
(\bf{A},\boldsymbol{\iota},\boldsymbol{\lambda},\boldsymbol{\eta})
over
\mathbb{F}
in
\mathrm{Sh}^{ss}_{U^{p}}
. Let
E_{p}=E\otimes\mathbb{Q}_{p}
. We pass
\bf{A}
to its associated
p
-divisible group
(\mathbb{X},\iota,\lambda)
with
\iota:E_{p}\rightarrow\End(\mathbb{X})_{\mathbb{Q}}
and
\lambda:\mathbb{X}\rightarrow\mathbb{X}^{\vee}
induced by the above PEL structure on
\bf{A}
. Let
W_{0}=W(\mathbb{F})
be the Witt ring of
\mathbb{F}
and let
K_{0}=W(\mathbb{F})_{\mathbb{Q}}
be the fraction field of
W_{0}
.

1.3.
EL type

In the case
p
is split,
E_{p}=\mathbb{Q}_{p}\times\mathbb{Q}_{p}
and
\mathbb{X}=\mathbb{X}_{0}\times\mathbb{X}_{1}
. Let
(\mathrm{Nilp})
be the category of
W_{0}
-schemes S, with
p
locally nilpotent on
S
. Then we are led to consider the Rapoport-Zink space
\mathcal{N}
which is the set valued functor that associates a scheme
S\in(\mathrm{Nilp})
the set of isomorphism classes of the pair
(X,\rho_{X})
where

-

X
is a two dimensional
p
-divisible group over
S
;

-

\rho_{X}:X\times\bar{S}\rightarrow\mathbb{X}_{0}\times\bar{S}
is a quasi-isogeny over
\bar{S}
which is the locus of
S
where
p=0
.

This functor is representable by a smooth formal scheme
\mathcal{N}
over
\mathrm{Spf}(W_{0})
. The formal scheme
\mathcal{N}
decomposes into connected components
\mathcal{N}=\bigsqcup_{i\in\mathbb{Z}}\mathcal{N}(i)
where
\mathcal{N}(i)
is the sub formal scheme given by the condition that
\rho_{X}
has height
i
. Then the underlying reduced scheme
\mathcal{N}_{red}(0)
of
\mathcal{N}(0)
can be described by the so called
Bruhat-Tits stratification
. For the
p
-divisible group
\mathbb{X}_{0}
, we consider its automorphism group
J_{b}
whose derived group is isomorphic to
\mathrm{SL}_{B_{p}}(2)
for the quaternion division algebra
B_{p}
over
\mathbb{Q}_{p}
. The Bruhat-Tits building of this group over
\mathbb{Q}_{p}
is a tree with two kinds of vertices correspond to the nodes
\{1,3\}
and
\{0,2\}
in the affine Dynkin diagram of type
\widetilde{A}_{3}
. They correspond to two different kinds of strata in the Burhat-Tits stratification. Our main result about this Rapoport-Zink space is the following theorem which one can find a more precise version in Theorem
2.21
.

Theorem 1
.

Let
\mathcal{M}=\mathcal{N}_{red}(0)
. We have a stratification

\mathcal{M}=\mathcal{M}^{\circ}_{\{1,3\}}\sqcup\mathcal{M}_{\{0,2\}}

called the Bruhat-Tits stratification. The subscheme
\mathcal{M}^{\circ}_{\{1,3\}}
is open and dense in
\mathcal{M}
and
\mathcal{M}_{\{0,2\}}
is the set of superspecial points. The irreducible components of
\mathcal{M}
are isomorphic to
\mathbb{P}^{1}
.

1.4.
PEL type

In the case
p
is inert, then
E_{p}=\mathbb{Q}_{p^{2}}
and we denote by
\psi_{0},\psi_{1}
the two embeddings of
\mathbb{Q}_{p^{2}}
in
K_{0}
. We consider the following set valued functor
\mathcal{N}
on
(\mathrm{Nilp})
. To a scheme
S\in(\mathrm{Nilp})
,
\mathcal{N}
classifies the set of isomorphism classes of the quadruple
(X,\lambda_{X},\iota_{X},\rho_{X})
where

-

X
is a two dimensional
p
-divisible group over
S
;

-

\lambda_{X}:X\rightarrow X^{\vee}
is a prime to
p
polarization over
S
;

-

\iota_{X}:\mathbb{Z}_{p^{2}}\rightarrow\End_{S}(X)
satisfies

\mathrm{det}(T-\iota(a);\mathrm{Lie}(X))=(T-\psi_{0}(a))^{2}(T-\psi_{1}(a))^{2}

in
\mathcal{O}_{S}[T]
;

-

\rho_{X}:X\times\bar{S}\rightarrow\mathbb{X}\times\bar{S}
is a quasi-isogeny where
\bar{S}
is the locus of
S
where
p=0
.

This moduli space is representable by a smooth formal scheme
\mathcal{N}
. The formal scheme
\mathcal{N}
can be decomposed into
\mathcal{N}=\bigsqcup_{i\in\mathbb{Z}}\mathcal{N}(i)
where the open and closed sub formal scheme
\mathcal{N}(i)
is isomorphic to
\mathcal{N}(0)
. Denote by
\mathcal{M}=\mathcal{N}_{red}(0)
the underlying reduced scheme of
\mathcal{N}(0)
. Then our main result concerns the structure of
\mathcal{M}
. We again consider the group
J_{b}
of automorphisms of
\mathbb{X}
that preserve the additional structures. This group is isomorphic to a different unitary group of degree
4
. The Bruhat-Tits building of the derived group of
J_{b}
is obtained as the fixed point of the Bruhat-Tits building of the group
\mathrm{SL}(4)
over
K_{0}
under the Frobenius action. This action on the affine Dynkin diagram of type
\widetilde{A}_{3}
fixes the nodes
1,3
and exchanges the nodes
0
and
2
. If we label the vertices of the base alcove of the building of
\mathrm{SL}(4)
over
K_{0}
according to the nodes on the affine Dynkin diagram of type
\widetilde{A}_{3}
, then the strata of the Bruhat-Tits stratification correspond to the vertices of the building of
J_{b}
over
\mathbb{Q}_{p}
attached to the nodes
1
,
3
and
\{0,2\}
as well as the edge corresponding to
\{1,3\}
. The main result is the following and one can find more details in Theorem
3.41
.

Theorem 2
.

The scheme
\mathcal{M}=\mathcal{N}_{red}(0)
is pure of dimension
2
and admits the Bruhat-Tits stratification

\mathcal{M}=\mathcal{M}^{\circ}_{\{1\}}\sqcup\mathcal{M}^{\circ}_{\{3\}}\sqcup\mathcal{M}^{\circ}_{\{1,3\}}\sqcup\mathcal{M}_{\{2\}}.

The irreducible components of the closures of
\mathcal{M}^{\circ}_{\{1\}}
and
\mathcal{M}^{\circ}_{\{3\}}
are isomorphic to the Fermat hypersurface
x^{p+1}_{0}+x^{p+1}_{1}+x^{p+1}_{2}+x^{p+1}_{3}=0
. The irreducible components of the closure of
\mathcal{M}^{\circ}_{\{1,3\}}
are isomorphic to
\mathbb{P}^{1}
and
\mathcal{M}_{\{2\}}
consists of superspecial points.

Lemma 2.2
.

The group

can be identified with

.

Proof.

By definition (

Denote by

\mathcal{M}(\mathbb{F})=\{M\subset N_{0}:pM\subset^{2}VM\subset^{2}M,\mathrm{vol}(M)=0\}.

Remark 1

The next lemma shows that vertex lattices correspond to superspecial Dieudonné modules.

Lemma 2.6

Let

Proposition 3.35
.

The functors
\mathcal{M}_{L_{1}}
and
\mathcal{M}_{L_{3}}
are representable by closed subschemes of

. Moreover they are projective schemes.

Proof.

by
[

is closed subscheme of a projective scheme by

Theorem 3.36
.

The maps
f_{1}:\mathcal{M}_{L_{1}}\rightarrow Y^{(-)}_{V(L_{1})}
and
f_{3}:\mathcal{M}_{L_{3}}\rightarrow Y^{(+)}_{V(L_{3})}

Proof.

∎

4.
Bruhat-Tits stratification of affine Deligne-Lusztig varieties

In this final section we would like point out how our results fit in the results proved in Görtz and He
[
GH15
]
in terms of the affine Deligne-Lusztig varieties.

4.1.
Affine Deligne Lusztig variety

Let
F
be a finite extension of
\mathbb{Q}_{p}
and
\breve{F}
be the completion of the maximal unramified extension of
F
. Let
G
be a connected reductive group over
F
and we write
\breve{G}
its base change to
\breve{F}
. Then
\breve{G}
is quasi-split and we choose a maximal split torus
S
and denote by
T
its centralizer. We know
T
is a maximal torus and we denote by
N
its normalizer. The relative Weyl group is defined to be
W=N(\breve{F})/T(\breve{F})
. This is a finite group. Let
\Gamma
be the Galois group of
\breve{F}
and we have the following Kottwitz homomorphism
[
RR96
]
:

\kappa_{G}:G(\breve{F})\rightarrow X_{*}(\breve{G})_{\Gamma}.

Denote by
\widetilde{W}
the Iwahori Weyl group of
\breve{G}
which is by definition
\widetilde{W}=N(\breve{F})/T(\breve{F})_{1}
where
T(\breve{F})_{1}
is the kernel of the Kottwitz homomorphism for
T(\breve{F})
. Let
\breve{\mathfrak{B}}(G)
be the Bruhat-Tits building of
G
over
\breve{F}
. The choice of
S
determines an standard apartment
\breve{\mathfrak{A}}
which
\widetilde{W}
acts on by affine transformations. We fix a
\sigma
-invariant alcove
\mathfrak{a}
and a special vertex of
\mathfrak{a}
. Inside the Iwahori Weyl group
\widetilde{W}
, there is a copy of the affine Weyl group
W_{a}
which can be identified with
N(\breve{F})\cap G(\breve{F})_{1}/T(\breve{F})_{1}
where
G(\breve{F})_{1}
is the kernel of the Kottwitz morphism for
G(\breve{F})
. The group
\widetilde{W}
is not quite a Coxeter group while
W_{a}
is generated by the affine reflections denoted by
\tilde{\mathbb{S}}
and
(\widetilde{W},\tilde{\mathbb{S}})
form a Coxeter system. We in fact have
\widetilde{W}=W_{a}\rtimes\Omega
where
\Omega
is the normalizer of a fixed base alcove
\mathfrak{a}
and more canonically
\Omega=X_{*}(T)_{\Gamma}/X_{*}(T_{sc})_{\Gamma}
where
T_{sc}
is the preimage of
T\cap G_{der}
in the simply connected cover
G_{sc}
of
G_{der}
.

Let
\mu\in X_{*}(T)
be a minuscule cocharacter of
G
over
\breve{F}
and
\lambda
its image in
X_{*}(T)_{\Gamma}
. We denote by
\tau
the projection of
\lambda
in
\Omega
. The
admissible subset
of
\widetilde{W}
is defined to be

\Adm(\mu)=\{w\in\widetilde{W};w\leq x(\lambda)\text{ for some }x\in W\}.

Here
\lambda
is considered as a translation element in
\widetilde{W}
. Let
K\subset\tilde{\mathbb{S}}
and
\breve{K}
its corresponding parahoric subgroup. Let
\widetilde{W}_{K}
be the subgroup defined by
N(\breve{F})\cap\breve{K}/T(\breve{F})_{1}
. We have the identification
\breve{K}\backslash G(\breve{F})/\breve{K}=\widetilde{W}_{K}\backslash\widetilde{W}/\widetilde{W}_{K}
. Therefore we can define a relative position map

(4.1)

\begin{split}\mathrm{inv}:&G(\breve{F})/\breve{K}\times G(\breve{F})/\breve{K}\rightarrow\widetilde{W}_{K}\backslash\widetilde{W}/\widetilde{W}_{K}\\
&(g,h)\rightarrow\breve{K}g^{-1}h\breve{K}.\\
\end{split}

Let
b\in G(\breve{F})
be an element whose image in
B(G)
, the
\sigma
-conjugacy class of
G(\breve{F})
, lies in the subsets
B(G,\mu)
of
neutrally acceptable elements
see
[
Rap05
, 4.5, 4.6]
. For
w\in\widetilde{W}_{K}\backslash\widetilde{W}/\widetilde{W}_{K}
and
b\in G(\breve{F})
, we define the
affine Deligne-Lusztig variety

to be the set

X_{w}(b)=\{g\in G(\breve{F})/\breve{K};\mathrm{inv}(g,b\sigma(g))=w\}.

Thanks to the work of
[
BS17
]
and
[
Zhu17
]
, this set can be viewed as an ind-closed-subscheme in the affine flag variety
\breve{G}/\breve{K}
. In this note, we only consider it as a set. The Rapoport-Zink space is not directly related to the affine Deligne-Lusztig variety but rather to the following union of affine Deligne-Lusztig varieties

X(\mu,b)_{K}=\{g\in G(\breve{F})/\breve{K};g^{-1}b\sigma(g)\in\breve{K}w\breve{K},w\in\Adm(\mu)\}.

We recall the group
J_{b}
is defined by the
\sigma
-centralizer of
b
that is

J_{b}(R)=\{g\in G(R\otimes_{F}\breve{F});g^{-1}b\sigma(g)=b\}

for any
F
-algebra
R
. In the following we will assume that
b
is
basic
and in this case
J_{b}
is an inner form of
G
see
[
RR96
]
.

4.2.
Coxeter type ADLV

We define
\Adm^{K}(\mu)
to be the image of
\Adm(\mu)
in
\widetilde{W}_{K}\backslash\widetilde{W}/\widetilde{W}_{K}
and
{}^{K}\widetilde{W}
to be the set of elements of minimal length in
\widetilde{W}_{K}\backslash\widetilde{W}
. We define the set
\mathrm{EO}^{K}(\mu)=\Adm^{K}(\mu)\cap^{K}\widetilde{W}
. For

, we set

\mathrm{supp}_{\sigma}(w\tau)=\bigcup_{n\in\mathbb{Z}}(\tau\sigma)^{n}(\mathrm{supp}(w)).

If the length
l(w)
of
w
agrees with the cardinality of
\mathrm{supp}_{\sigma}(w\tau)/\langle\tau\sigma\rangle
, we say
w\tau
is a
\sigma
-Coxeter element. We denote by
\mathrm{EO}^{K}_{\sigma,\mathrm{cox}}(\mu)
the subset of
\mathrm{EO}^{K}(\mu)
such that
w
is a
\sigma
-Coxeter element and
\mathrm{supp}_{\sigma}(w)
is not
\widetilde{\mathbb{S}}
. A
K
-stable piece
is a subset of
G(\breve{F})
of the form
\breve{K}\cdot_{\sigma}\breve{I}w\breve{I}
where
\cdot_{\sigma}

\sigma

. Then we define the Ekedahl-Oort stratum attached to

X_{K,w}(b)=\{g\in G(\breve{F})/\breve{K};g^{-1}b\sigma(g)\in\breve{K}\cdot_{\sigma}IwI\}.

Then by
[
GH15
]
we have the following Ekedahl-Oort stratification

(4.2)

X(\mu,b)_{K}=\bigcup_{w\in\mathrm{EO}^{K}(\mu)}X_{K,w}(b).

The case when

(4.3)

X(\mu,b)_{K}=\bigcup_{w\in\mathrm{EO}^{K}_{\sigma,\mathrm{cox}}(\mu)}X_{K,w}(b)

is particular interesting and when this happens we say the datum
(G,\mu,K)
is of Coxeter type. The datum
(G,\mu,K)
being Coxeter type or not depends only on the associated datum
(\widetilde{W},\lambda,K,\sigma)
where
\lambda
is the image of
\mu\in X_{*}(T)_{I}
and
\sigma
is the induced automorphism of the Frobenius
\sigma
on
\widetilde{W}
. The set of
(G,\mu,K)
is classified in
[
GH15
]
Theorem 5.11. This includes the two cases we studied in the previous sections.

-

The
p
-split case corresponds to the datum

(\tilde{A}_{3},\omega^{\vee}_{2},{\mathbb{S}},id)

where
\sigma
acts on the affine Dynkin diagram by the identity.

-

The
p
-inert case corresponds to the datum

(\tilde{A}_{3},\omega^{\vee}_{2},{\mathbb{S}},\sigma_{0})

where
\sigma
acts on the affine Dynkin diagram by fixing the nodes

and
2
and interchanging the nodes
1
and
3
.

4.3.
Bruhat-Tits stratification of ADLV

Now we assume that
K
is a maximal proper subset of
\tilde{\mathbb{S}}
such that
\sigma(K)=K
. Consider the following set

\mathcal{J}=\{\Sigma\subset\tilde{\mathbb{S}};\emptyset\neq\Sigma\text{ is }\tau\sigma\text{-stable}\text{ and }d(v)=d(v^{\prime})\text{ for every }v,v^{\prime}\in\Sigma\}.

where

is the distance between
v
and the unique vertex not in
K
.
In fact every

corresponds to a

and we write
w
as

. If
(G,\mu,K)
is of Coxeter type, for any

,

(4.4)

X_{K,w_{\Sigma}}(b)=\bigcup_{i\in J_{b}/J_{b}\cap\breve{K}_{\tilde{\mathbb{S}}-\Sigma}}iX(w_{\Sigma}).

Here
\breve{K}_{\tilde{\mathbb{S}}-\Sigma}
is the parahoric subgroup associated to the set
\tilde{\mathbb{S}}-\Sigma
and
