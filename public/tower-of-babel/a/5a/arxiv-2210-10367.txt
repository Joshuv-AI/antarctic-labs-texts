Axis thermal expansion switching in transition-metal zirconides T ​ r Tr Zr 2 by tuning the c / a c/a ratio
Hiroto Arima 1, Md. Riad Kasem 1, Yoshikazu Mizuguchi 1

Abstract
This study examines the temperature-dependent evolution of the lattice constants for various CuAl 2 -type compounds, including NiZr 2 , (Co,Rh,Ir)Zr 2 , (Fe,Co,Rh,Ir)Zr 2 , and (Co,Ni,Cu,Rh,Ir)Zr 2 , in the pursuit of negative or zero thermal expansion. Results reveal that NiZr 2 has positive thermal expansion, while the other compounds exhibit uniaxial negative thermal expansion along the c c -axis contraction. The study suggests that the c c -axis thermal expansion can be controlled by manipulating the c / a c/a ratio through T ​ r Tr -site substitution, providing a design principle for achieving negative thermal expansion of the c c -axis and potentially zero thermal expansion in a single compound in T ​ r Tr Zr 2 compounds.
