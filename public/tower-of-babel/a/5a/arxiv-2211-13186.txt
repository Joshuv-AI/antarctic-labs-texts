Relevant to top-down ℳ {\cal M} -theory dual of large- N N thermal QCD-like theories at int ermediate coupling, induced by the G 2 G_{2} structure supported by the closed M 7 M_{7} - a warped product of the ℳ {\cal M} -theory circle and a non-Kähler six-fold which is the warped product of the thermal circle with a non-Einsteinian deformation of T 1 , 1 T^{1,1} - we obtain explicitly (Almost) Contact (3) (Metric)Structure(s) and a three-tuple of S ​ U ​ ( 3 ) SU(3) structures along the “transverse” six-fold induced by the AC(3)MS on M 7 M_{7} . The aforementioned ACS do(es) not yield a Contact Structure. These results are expected to be very useful in the context of classification of non-supersymmetric geometries relevant to top-down holographic thermal QCD at intermediate coupling.
