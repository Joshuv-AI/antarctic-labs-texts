R_{\lambda_{k},\tau}\left(x_{k},\frac{\xi_{k}}{\xi_{k}\mid}\right)
is an isometry, it follows that

\displaystyle 0<\|u\|_{p}=

\displaystyle\left\|R_{\lambda_{k},\tau}\left(x_{k},\frac{\xi_{k}}{\left|\xi_{k}\right|}\right)u\right\|_{p}

(2.7)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left\|\left(ST_{\sigma}-K\right)R_{\lambda_{k},\tau}\left(x_{k},\frac{\xi_{k}}{\left|\xi_{k}\right|}\right)u\right\|_{p}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\left\|ST_{\sigma}R_{\lambda_{k},\tau}\left(x_{k},\frac{\xi_{k}}{\left|\xi_{k}\right|}\right)u\right\|{p}+\left\|{KR_{\lambda_{k},\tau}}\left(x_{k},\frac{\xi_{k}}{\left|\xi_{k}\right|}\right)u\right\|_{p}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\left\|R_{\lambda_{k},\tau}\left(x_{k},\frac{\xi_{k}}{\left|\xi_{k}\right|}\right)^{-1}T_{\sigma}R_{\lambda_{k},\tau}\left(x_{k},\frac{\xi_{k}}{\left|\xi_{k}\right|}\right)u\right\|_{p}\|S\|+\left\|KR_{\lambda_{k},\tau}\left(x_{k},\frac{\xi_{k}}{\left|\xi_{k}\right|}\right)u\right\|_{p}.

Now, using the fact that
K
is a compact operator and Proposition
2.3
, it follows that

\left\|KR_{\lambda_{k},\tau}\left(x_{k},\frac{\xi_{k}}{\left|\xi_{k}\right|}\right)u\right\|_{p}\rightarrow 0

as
k\rightarrow\infty
. Then, by Equation (
2.7
),

\displaystyle\|u\|_{p}\leq\|S\|\left|\sigma_{\infty}\right|\|u\|_{p}.

(2.8)

Thus, Equation (
2.4
) and Equation (
2.8
) give the contradiction that

\frac{1}{\|S\|}\leq\left|\sigma_{\infty}\right|\leq\frac{1}{2\|S\|},

which completes the proof of the theorem.

The preceding theorem can be generalized to the following theorem.

Theorem 2.6
.

Let
\sigma\in M_{\rho,\Lambda}^{m},
where
m\in(-\infty,\infty)
, and let

be a Fredholm operator for some

Then

is an
M
-elliptic operator.

Proof

By Theorem 1.6 in
[
18
]
, the operators
T_{\sigma}:H^{s,p}\rightarrow H_{\Lambda}^{s-m,p},J_{-s}:H_{\Lambda}^{s,p}\rightarrow L^{p}\left(\mathbb{R}^{n}\right),J_{s}:L^{p}\left(\mathbb{R}^{n}\right)\rightarrow H_{\Lambda}^{s,p},J_{m-s}:H^{s-m,p}\rightarrow L^{p}\left(\mathbb{R}^{n}\right)
and

are bounded linear operators. Here

is a pseudo-differential operator with symbol
\sigma_{m}\in M_{\rho,\Lambda}^{-m}
, where

\sigma_{m}(\xi)=(\Lambda(\xi))^{-m},\quad\xi\in\mathbb{R}^{n}.

Let

\displaystyle J_{m-s}T_{\sigma}J_{s}=T_{\tau}.

(2.9)

Then

T_{\tau}:L^{p}\left(\mathbb{R}^{n}\right)\rightarrow L^{p}\left(\mathbb{R}^{n}\right),

where
\tau\in M_{\rho,\Lambda}^{0}.
Since

is bijective, it follows that

is Fredholm and
M
-elliptic for all

So, by Theorem
2.4
,

is

-elliptic. By Equation (

, is bijective and product of two

is
M

3.
Gårding’s Inequality for
M
-elliptic operators

We begin with a definition.

Definition 3.1
.

Let
\sigma\in M_{\rho,\Lambda}^{m}
, where
m\in\mathbb{R}
. Then
\sigma
is said to be strongly
M
- elliptic if there exist positive constants
C
and
R
for which

\operatorname{Re}(\sigma(x,\xi))\geq C\,\Lambda(\xi)^{m},\hskip 8.5359pt|\xi|\geq R.

Theorem 3.2
.

(Gårding’s Inequality For M-elliptic Operators)

Let
\sigma\in M_{\rho,\Lambda}^{2m}
, where
m\in\mathbb{R}
, be strongly
M
- elliptic symbol. Then we can find positive constants
C^{\prime}
and
C_{s}
for every real number
s\geq\frac{\rho}{2}
such that

\operatorname{Re}(T_{\sigma}\,\varphi,\varphi)\geq C^{\prime}\,\|\varphi\|_{m,2,\Lambda}^{2}-C_{s}\,\|\varphi\|_{m-s,2,\Lambda}^{2},\hskip 8.5359pt\varphi\in\mathcal{S}.

To prove this theorem, we need the following three lemmas.

Lemma 3.3
.

Let
\sigma\in S_{\rho,\Lambda}^{0}
and
F
be a
C^{\infty}
- function on the complex plane
\mathbb{C}
. Then
F\circ\sigma\in S_{\rho,\Lambda}^{0}
.

Proof

We need to prove that for any two multi-indices
\alpha
and
\beta
, we can find a positive constant
C_{\alpha,\beta}
such that

\displaystyle|(\partial_{x}^{\alpha}\partial_{\xi}^{\beta}(F\circ\sigma))(x,\xi)|\leq C_{\alpha,\beta}\,\Lambda(\xi)^{-\rho|\beta|},\hskip 8.5359ptx,\xi\in\mathbb{R}^{n}.

(3.1)

Let
\alpha
and
\beta
be two multi-indices such that
|\alpha+\beta|=0
, i.e.,
|\alpha|=0
and
|\beta|=0
. Since
\sigma\in S_{\rho,\Lambda}^{0}
, so we can find a positive constant
C
such that

|\sigma(x,\xi)|\leq C,\hskip 8.5359ptx,\xi\in\mathbb{R}^{n}.

Thus,
F\circ\sigma
is a bounded and
C^{\infty}
function on
\mathbb{R}^{n}\times\mathbb{R}^{n}
. So we can find another positive constant
C_{0}
such that

|(F\circ\sigma)(x,\xi)|\leq C_{0},\hskip 8.5359ptx,\xi\in\mathbb{R}^{n}.

Hence Equation (
3.1
) is true for all multi-indices
\alpha
and
\beta
with
|\alpha+\beta|=0
.
Now, suppose that Equation (
3.1
) is true for all
C^{\infty}
functions
F
on
\mathbb{C}
,
\sigma\in S_{\rho,\Lambda}^{0}
and multi-indices
\alpha
and
\beta
with
|\alpha+\beta|\leq k
. Let
\alpha
and
\beta
be multi-indices with
|\alpha+\beta|=k+1
. We first suppose that

\partial_{x}^{\alpha}\,\partial_{\xi}^{\beta}=\partial_{x}^{\alpha}\,\partial_{\xi}^{\gamma}\,\partial_{\xi_{j}}

for some multi-index
\gamma
with
|\alpha+\gamma|=k
and for some
j=1,2,...,n
. Then, by chain rule,

(\partial_{x}^{\alpha}\,\partial_{\xi}^{\beta}(F\circ\sigma))(x,\xi)=\partial_{x}^{\alpha}\,\partial_{\xi}^{\gamma}\{(F_{1}\circ\sigma)\,\partial_{\xi_{j}}\sigma+(F_{2}\circ\sigma)\,\partial_{\xi_{j}}\sigma\}(x,\xi)

for all
x
and
\xi
in
\mathbb{R}^{n}
, where
F_{1}
and
F_{2}
are the partial derivatives of
F
with respect to the first and second variables respectively. Now, by Leibniz’s formula and the induction hypothesis, we can find two positive constants
C_{\delta_{0},\delta}
and
C_{\alpha,\delta_{0},\gamma,\delta,j}
such that

\displaystyle|(\partial_{x}^{\alpha}\,\partial_{\xi}^{\gamma}\{(F_{1}\circ\sigma)\,\partial_{\xi_{j}}\sigma\})(x,\xi)|

\displaystyle\leq\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}\delta_{0}\leq\alpha\\
\delta\leq\gamma\end{subarray}}{\alpha\choose\delta_{0}}\,{\gamma\choose\delta}\,|(\partial_{x}^{\delta_{0}}\,\partial_{\xi}^{\delta}(F_{1}\circ\sigma))(x,\xi)|\,|(\partial_{x}^{\alpha-\delta_{0}}\,\partial_{\xi}^{\gamma-\delta}\,(\partial_{\xi_{j}}\sigma))(x,\xi)|

\displaystyle\leq\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}\delta_{0}\leq\alpha\\
\delta\leq\gamma\end{subarray}}{\alpha\choose\delta_{0}}\,{\gamma\choose\delta}\,C_{\delta_{0},\delta}\,\Lambda(\xi)^{-\rho|\delta|}\,C_{\alpha,\delta_{0},\gamma,\delta,j}\,\Lambda(\xi)^{-\rho(|\gamma|-|\delta|+1)}

\displaystyle\leq C_{\alpha,\gamma,j}\,\Lambda(\xi)^{-\rho(|\gamma|+1)}

\displaystyle=C_{\alpha,\gamma,j}\,\Lambda(\xi)^{-\rho(|\beta|)},\hskip 8.5359ptx,\xi\in\mathbb{R}^{n},

where

C_{\alpha,\gamma,j}=\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}\delta_{0}\leq\alpha\\
\delta\leq\gamma\end{subarray}}{\alpha\choose\delta_{0}}\,{\gamma\choose\delta}\,C_{\delta_{0},\delta}\,C_{\alpha,\delta_{0},\gamma,\delta,j}.

Similarly, we can find another positive constant
C_{\alpha,\gamma,j}^{\prime}
such that

|(\partial_{x}^{\alpha}\,\partial_{\xi}^{\gamma}\{(F_{2}\circ\sigma)\,\partial_{\xi_{j}}\sigma\})(x,\xi)|\leq C_{\alpha,\gamma,j}^{\prime}\,\Lambda(\xi)^{-\rho(|\beta|)},\hskip 8.5359ptx,\xi\in\mathbb{R}^{n}.

Hence

|(\partial_{x}^{\alpha}\,\partial_{\xi}^{\beta}(F\circ\sigma))(x,\xi)|\leq(C_{\alpha,\gamma,j}+C_{\alpha,\gamma,j}^{\prime})\,\Lambda(\xi)^{-\rho(|\beta|)},\hskip 8.5359ptx,\xi\in\mathbb{R}^{n}.

Now, we assume that

\partial_{x}^{\alpha}\,\partial_{\xi}^{\beta}=\partial_{x}^{\gamma}\,\partial_{x_{j}}\,\partial_{\xi}^{\beta}

for some multi-index
\gamma
with
|\gamma+\beta|=k
and for some
j=1,2,...,n
. Then, as before, we can find a positive constant
C_{\alpha,\gamma,j}^{\prime\prime}
such that

|(\partial_{x}^{\alpha}\,\partial_{\xi}^{\beta}(F\circ\sigma))(x,\xi)|\leq C_{\alpha,\gamma,j}^{\prime\prime}\,\Lambda(\xi)^{-\rho(|\beta|)},\hskip 8.5359ptx,\xi\in\mathbb{R}^{n}.

So, by the principle of mathematical induction, Equation (
3.1
) follows.

Lemma 3.4
.

|\gamma|=0

3.3

3.3

C^{\infty}

F

\mathbb{C}

3.3
) follows.

3.2

(3.4)

\partial_{x}^{\alpha}\,\partial_{\xi}^{\beta}=\partial_{x}^{\alpha}\,\partial_{\xi}^{\beta_{0}}\,\partial_{\xi_{j}}

3.4

on

,

\gamma
with

. Let

\displaystyle=\partial_{x}^{\alpha}\,\partial_{\xi}^{\beta}(\xi^{\gamma}\,\partial_{\xi}^{\gamma_{0}}\,\partial_{\xi_{i}}(F\circ\sigma)(x,\xi)))

Lemma 3.5
.

Let
\sigma
be a strongly
M
-elliptic symbol in
M_{\rho,\Lambda}^{2m}
, where
m\in\mathbb{R}
. Then we can find two positive constants
\eta
and
\kappa
such that

\operatorname{Re}(\sigma(x,\xi))\geq\eta\,(\Lambda(\xi))^{2m}-\kappa\,(\Lambda(\xi))^{2m-\rho},\hskip 8.5359ptx,\xi\in\mathbb{R}^{n}.
