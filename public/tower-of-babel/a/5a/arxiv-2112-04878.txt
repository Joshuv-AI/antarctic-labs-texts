The pollution to the K ​ π K\pi -puzzle from the isospin-breaking π 0 − η − η ′ \pi^{0}\!\!-\!\eta\!-\!\eta^{\prime} mixing effect
Zhen-Hua Zhang

Abstract
The influence of the isospin-breaking π 0 − η − η ′ \pi^{0}\!\!-\!\eta\!-\!\eta^{\prime} mixing effect on the C ​ P CP -asymmetries of B → K ​ π B\to K\pi processes is examined for the first time. It is found that this mixing effect brings a large uncertainty both to the C ​ P CP -asymmetry sum rule of B → K ​ π B\to K\pi processes and the C ​ P CP -asymmetry difference of the B + → K + ​ π 0 B^{+}\to K^{+}\pi^{0} and B 0 → K + ​ π − B^{0}\to K^{+}\pi^{-} , obscuring the significance of the K ​ π K\pi -puzzle. This uncertainty can be so large that it is even possible to explain the K ​ π K\pi -puzzle by the π 0 − η − η ′ \pi^{0}\!\!-\!\eta\!-\!\eta^{\prime} mixing effect alone .



I
Brief introduction to the
K\pi
-puzzle

It is believed that the decay processes
B\to K\pi
are good probes for new physics (NP) beyond the Standard Model (SM), as the tree-level amplitudes are suppressed, making them more sensitive to the potentially NP contributions.
Based on the isospin consideration, the amplitudes of the aforementioned weak decays
B\to K\pi
are related through
Nir and Quinn 1991
;
Lipkin et al. 1991
;
Gronau 1991

\mathcal{A}_{B^{+}\to K^{0}\pi^{+}}+\sqrt{2}\mathcal{A}_{B^{+}\to K^{+}\pi^{0}}=\mathcal{A}_{{B^{0}}\to K^{+}\pi^{-}}+\sqrt{2}\mathcal{A}_{{B^{0}}\to K^{0}\pi^{0}},

(1)

from which a sum rule between
CP
asymmetries of
B\to K\pi
processes can be deduced
Gronau 2005
:

A_{CP}^{K^{+}\pi^{-}}+\frac{\mathcal{B}(K^{0}\pi^{+})}{\mathcal{B}(K^{+}\pi^{-})}\frac{\tau_{0}}{\tau_{+}}A_{CP}^{K^{0}\pi^{+}}=\frac{2\mathcal{B}(K^{+}\pi^{0})}{\mathcal{B}(K^{+}\pi^{-})}\frac{\tau_{0}}{\tau_{+}}A_{CP}^{K^{+}\pi^{0}}+\frac{2\mathcal{B}(K^{0}\pi^{0})}{\mathcal{B}(K^{+}\pi^{-})}\frac{\tau_{0}}{\tau_{+}}A_{CP}^{K^{0}\pi^{0}}.

(2)

This
CP
-asymmetry sum rule can be further simplified into a more crude relation between the
CP
asymmetries of
B^{+}\to K^{+}\pi^{0}
and
B^{0}\to K^{+}\pi^{-}

Gronau and Rosner 1999
:

A_{CP}^{K^{+}\pi^{0}}-A_{CP}^{K^{+}\pi^{-}}\approx 0,

(3)

which is clearly in contradiction to the latest world average of the
CP
-asymmetry difference between these two aforementioned processes
Duh et al. 2013
;
Lees et al. 2013
;
Aaltonen et al. 2014
;
Aaij et al. 2018
;
Amhis et al. 2021
;
Aaij et al. 2021
,

\Delta A_{CP}(K\pi)\equiv A_{CP}^{K^{+}\pi^{0}}-A_{CP}^{K^{+}\pi^{-}}=0.115\pm 0.014.

(4)

This is basically a short version of the long-standing “
K\pi
-puzzle”.

The
K\pi
-puzzle has gained constant theoretical attentions, especially those works on the exploration of the possibility of NP
Buras et al. 2003
;
Buras et al. 2004a
;
Buras et al. 2004b
;
Barger et al. 2004
;
Baek et al. 2005
;
Chang et al. 2009
;
Fleischer et al. 2018
;
Beaudry et al. 2018
;
Bhattacharya et al. 2021
.
Meanwhile, there are also works attempting to understand it within SM
Bauer et al. 2006
;
Cheng and Chua 2009
;
Cheng et al. 2015
;
Zhou et al. 2017
;
Kundu et al. 2021
.
In this paper, we will try to examine the role of the isospin breaking effect –the
\pi^{0}\!\!-\!\eta\!-\!\eta^{\prime}
mixing effect– in the
K\pi
-puzzle.

II
the pollution of the
\pi^{0}\!\!-\!\eta\!-\!\eta^{\prime}
mixing effects to the
K\pi
-puzzle

The basic idea is very simple.
Since there are
\pi^{0}
s in the final states of these
B\to K\pi
decay processes, the isospin-breaking
\pi^{0}\!\!-\!\eta\!-\!\eta^{\prime}
mixing effect
Gross et al. 1979
;
Leutwyler 1996
;
Feldmann et al. 1999
;
Escribano and Royo 2020
;
Kordov et al. 2021
takes place.
Although this effect seems to be negligible at first sight as it is small
Gronau et al. 2006
–perhaps that is why the
\pi^{0}\!\!-\!\eta\!-\!\eta^{\prime}
mixing effect has never been put on the table dealing with the
K\pi
-puzzle– this kind of isospin-breaking effects could potentially affect
CP

Zhang et al. 2020

Within the context of the
\pi^{0}\!\!-\!\eta\!-\!\eta^{\prime}

\pi^{0}

\pi_{3}

\eta

\eta^{\prime}

\epsilon^{\prime}

\pi^{0}
and
\eta

\eta^{\prime}

;
Feldmann et al. 1999

and

\pi^{0}\!\!-\!\eta\!-\!\eta^{\prime}

III
Conclusion

In conclusion, the contribution of the isospin-breaking
\pi^{0}\!\!-\!\eta\!-\!\eta^{\prime}
mixing effect to the
CP
asymmetries of
B\to K\pi
is investigated for the first time in this paper.
It is found that the
\pi^{0}\!\!-\!\eta\!-\!\eta^{\prime}
mixing effect pollutes the
K\pi
-puzzle more dramatically than the naive expectation.
This pollution is imbedded in the parameter
\Delta_{\text{IB}}
, which brings quite a large uncertainty to the
CP
-asymmetry sum rule of
B\to K\pi
and the
CP
-asymmetry difference of the
B^{+}\to K^{+}\pi^{0}
and
B^{0}\to K^{+}\pi^{-}
.
The analysis of this paper shows that it is even possible to explain the
K\pi
-puzzle by the
\pi^{0}\!\!-\!\eta\!-\!\eta^{\prime}
mixing effect
alone
.
Consequently, the implications of the
K\pi
-puzzle should be reconsidered.
