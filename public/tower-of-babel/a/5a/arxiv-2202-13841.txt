Abstract
Let h , k ≥ 2 h,k\geq 2 be integers. A set A A of positive integers is called asymptotic basis of order k k if every large enough positive integer can be written as the sum of k k terms from A A . A set of positive integers A A is said to be a B h ​ [ g ] B_{h}[g] -set if every positive integer can be written as the sum of h h terms from A A at most g g different ways. In this paper we prove the existence of B h ​ [ 1 ] B_{h}[1] sets which are asymptotic bases of order 2 ​ h 2h by using probabilistic methods. 2010 Mathematics Subject Classification: 11B34, 11B75. Keywords and phrases: additive number theory, general sequences, additive representation function, Sidon sets.

(i)

Let

(ii)

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{(|n+M|+1)^{\alpha}}\cdot\frac{1}{n^{\beta}}\ll\frac{1}{(|M|+1)^{\alpha+\beta-1}}.

(iii)

. Then, for every integer

\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}(z_{1},\dots{},z_{t})\in(\mathbb{Z}^{+})^{t}\\
z_{1}+\dots{}+z_{s}-(z_{s+1}+\dots{}+z_{t})=M\end{subarray}}\frac{1}{(z_{1}\cdots{}z_{t})^{\frac{4h-3}{4h-1}}}\ll\frac{1}{(|M|+1)^{1-\frac{2t}{4h-1}}}.

3.1
Proof of Lemma 4

We estimate the sums by integral according to the well known
Euler integral formula.

\sum_{n=1}^{M-1}\frac{1}{n^{\alpha}}\cdot\frac{1}{(M-n)^{\beta}}=\sum_{n=1}^{\lfloor M/2\rfloor}\frac{1}{n^{\alpha}}\cdot\frac{1}{(M-n)^{\beta}}+\sum_{n=\lfloor M/2\rfloor+1}^{M-1}\frac{1}{n^{\alpha}}\cdot\frac{1}{(M-n)^{\beta}}

\ll\frac{1}{M^{\beta}}\sum_{n=1}^{\lfloor M/2\rfloor}\frac{1}{n^{\alpha}}+\frac{1}{M^{\alpha}}\cdot\sum_{n=\lfloor M/2\rfloor+1}^{M-1}\frac{1}{(M-n)^{\beta}}\ll\frac{1}{M^{\beta}}\int_{0}^{M}\frac{dx}{x^{\alpha}}+\frac{1}{M^{\alpha}}\int_{0}^{M}\frac{dx}{x^{\beta}}\ll\frac{1}{M^{\alpha+\beta-1}},

which proves (i).

Moreover, if
M\geq 0
,

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{(|n+M|+1)^{\alpha}}\cdot\frac{1}{n^{\beta}}=\sum_{n=1}^{M}\frac{1}{(n+M+1)^{\alpha}}\cdot\frac{1}{n^{\beta}}+\sum_{n=M+1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{(n+M+1)^{\alpha}}\cdot\frac{1}{n^{\beta}}

\leq\frac{1}{(M+1)^{\alpha}}\sum_{n=1}^{M}\frac{1}{n^{\beta}}+\sum_{n=M+1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{n^{\alpha+\beta}}

\ll\frac{1}{(M+1)^{\alpha}}\int_{0}^{M+1}\frac{dx}{x^{\beta}}+\int_{M+1}^{\infty}\frac{dx}{x^{\alpha+\beta}}\ll\frac{1}{(|M|+1)^{\alpha+\beta-1}}.

Furthermore, if
M<0
,

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{(|n+M|+1)^{\alpha}}\cdot\frac{1}{n^{\beta}}=\sum_{n=1}^{-\lfloor M/2\rfloor}\frac{1}{(|M+n|+1)^{\alpha}}\cdot\frac{1}{n^{\beta}}+\sum_{n=-\lfloor M/2\rfloor+1}^{-M}\frac{1}{(|M+n|+1)^{\alpha}}\cdot\frac{1}{n^{\beta}}

+\sum_{n=-M+1}^{-2M}\frac{1}{(|M+n|+1)^{\alpha}}\cdot\frac{1}{n^{\beta}}+\sum_{n=-2M+1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{(|M+n|+1)^{\alpha}}\cdot\frac{1}{n^{\beta}}

\ll\frac{1}{|M|^{\alpha}}\sum_{n=1}^{-\lfloor M/2\rfloor}\frac{1}{n^{\beta}}+\frac{1}{|M|^{\beta}}\sum_{n=-\lfloor M/2\rfloor+1}^{-M}\frac{1}{(|M+n|+1)^{\alpha}}+\frac{1}{|M|^{\beta}}\sum_{n=-M+1}^{-2M}\frac{1}{(|M+n|+1)^{\alpha}}

+\sum_{n=-M+1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{n^{\alpha+\beta}}\ll\frac{1}{|M|^{\alpha}}\int_{0}^{-M}\frac{dx}{x^{\beta}}+\frac{1}{|M|^{\beta}}\int_{0}^{-M}\frac{dx}{x^{\alpha}}+\frac{1}{|M|^{\beta}}\int_{0}^{-M}\frac{dx}{x^{\alpha}}+\int_{-M}^{\infty}\frac{dx}{x^{\alpha+\beta}}

\ll\frac{1}{(|M|+1)^{\alpha+\beta-1}},

which proves (ii).

Next, we prove (iii) by induction on
l
. The statement is clear for
l=1
. Assume that it is true for
l-1\leq 2h-1
. Then by (i),

\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}(z_{1},\dots{},z_{l})\in(\mathbb{Z}^{+})^{l}\\
z_{1}+\dots{}+z_{l}=M\end{subarray}}\frac{1}{(z_{1}\cdots{}z_{l})^{\frac{4h-3}{4h-1}}}=\sum_{z_{l}=1}^{M-1}\frac{1}{z_{l}^{\frac{4h-3}{4h-1}}}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}(z_{1},\dots{},z_{l-1})\in(\mathbb{Z}^{+})^{l-1}\\
z_{1}+\dots{}+z_{l-1}=M-z_{l}\end{subarray}}\frac{1}{(z_{1}\cdots{}z_{l-1})^{\frac{4h-3}{4h-1}}}

\ll\sum_{z_{l}=1}^{M-1}\frac{1}{z_{l}^{\frac{4h-3}{4h-1}}}\cdot\frac{1}{(M-z_{l})^{1-\frac{2(l-1)}{4h-1}}}\ll\frac{1}{M^{1-\frac{2l}{4h-1}}},

which gives (iii).

Finally, if either
s=0
, or
t=s
, the statement of (iv) follows immediately from (iii). Otherwise, one can assume that
0<s<t
. It follows from (ii) and (iii) that

\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}(z_{1},\dots{},z_{t})\in(\mathbb{Z}^{+})^{t}\\
z_{1}+\dots{}+z_{s}-(z_{s+1}+\dots{}+z_{t})=M\end{subarray}}\frac{1}{(z_{1}\cdots{}z_{t})^{\frac{4h-3}{4h-1}}}

=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left(\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}(z_{1},\dots{},z_{s})\in(\mathbb{Z}^{+})^{s}\\
z_{1}+\dots{}+z_{s}=n+M\end{subarray}}\left(\frac{1}{(z_{1}\cdots{}z_{s})^{\frac{4h-3}{4h-1}}}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}(z_{s+1},\dots{},z_{t})\in(\mathbb{Z}^{+})^{t-s}\\
z_{s+1}+\dots{}+z_{t}=n\end{subarray}}\frac{1}{(z_{s+1}\cdots{}z_{t})^{\frac{4h-3}{4h-1}}}\right)\right)

\ll\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}(z_{1},\dots{},z_{s})\in(\mathbb{Z}^{+})^{s}\\
z_{1}+\dots{}+z_{s}=n+M\end{subarray}}\frac{1}{(z_{1}\cdots{}z_{s})^{\frac{4h-3}{4h-1}}}\cdot\frac{1}{n^{1-\frac{2(t-s)}{4h-1}}}\ll\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{(|n+M|+1)^{1-\frac{2s}{4h-1}}}\cdot\frac{1}{n^{1-\frac{2(t-s)}{4h-1}}}

\ll\frac{1}{(|M|+1)^{1-\frac{2t}{4h-1}}},

which proves (iv). The proof of Lemma 4 is completed.

\mathbb{E}(Y^{(\overline{\beta},\overline{\gamma},\overline{\delta},\overline{\mu},\overline{\nu})}_{u,v,h,B}(n))\ll\sum_{m=1}^{-\lfloor K/2\rfloor}\frac{1}{m^{1-\frac{2u}{4h-1}}}\cdot\frac{1}{(|m+K|+1)^{\frac{2u-1}{4h-1}}}\cdot\frac{1}{(n-m)^{\frac{2u-1}{4h-1}}}

+\sum_{m=-\lfloor K/2\rfloor+1}^{n-1}\frac{1}{m^{1-\frac{2u}{4h-1}}}\cdot\frac{1}{(|m+K|+1)^{\frac{2u-1}{4h-1}}}\cdot\frac{1}{(n-m)^{\frac{2u-1}{4h-1}}}.

If
1\leq m<-K/2
, then
|m+K|\geq|K/2|\geq\frac{n}{6}
. It follows from (i) of Lemma 4 that

\sum_{m=1}^{-\lfloor K/2\rfloor}\frac{1}{m^{1-\frac{2u}{4h-1}}}\cdot\frac{1}{(|m+K|+1)^{\frac{2u-1}{4h-1}}}\cdot\frac{1}{(n-m)^{\frac{2u-1}{4h-1}}}\ll\sum_{m=1}^{-\lfloor K/2\rfloor}\frac{1}{m^{1-\frac{1}{4h-1}}}\cdot\frac{1}{n^{\frac{2u-1}{4h-1}}}\cdot\frac{1}{(n-m)^{\frac{2u-1}{4h-1}}}

\ll\frac{1}{n^{\frac{2u-1}{4h-1}}}\cdot\sum_{m=1}^{-\lfloor K/2\rfloor}\frac{1}{m^{1-\frac{1}{4h-1}}}\cdot\frac{1}{(n-m)^{\frac{2u-1}{4h-1}}}\ll\frac{1}{n^{\frac{2u-1}{4h-1}}\cdot n^{\frac{2u-2}{4h-1}}}=O(1).

On the other hand, by
-\frac{K}{2}\geq\frac{n}{6}
,

\sum_{m=-\lfloor K/2\rfloor}^{n-1}\frac{1}{m^{1-\frac{2u}{4h-1}}}\cdot\frac{1}{(|m+K|+1)^{\frac{2u-1}{4h-1}}}\cdot\frac{1}{(n-m)^{\frac{2u-1}{4h-1}}}

\ll\frac{1}{n^{1-\frac{2u}{4h-1}}}\cdot\sum_{m=-\lfloor K/2\rfloor}^{n-1}\frac{1}{(|m+K|+1)^{\frac{2u-1}{4h-1}}}\cdot\frac{1}{(n-m)^{\frac{2u-1}{4h-1}}}.

We know that
|K|\leq nh
, thus in the above sum,
1\leq|m+K|+1\leq(1+h)n
and
1\leq n-m\leq n-1
. Thus a positive integer among the integers
n-m
and
|m+K|+1
appear, as the non-largest, at most three times. Then,

\frac{1}{n^{1-\frac{2u}{4h-1}}}\cdot\sum_{m=-\lfloor K/2\rfloor}^{n-1}\frac{1}{(|m+K|+1)^{\frac{2u-1}{4h-1}}}\cdot\frac{1}{(n-m)^{\frac{2u-1}{4h-1}}}\leq\frac{3}{n^{1-\frac{2u}{4h-1}}}\cdot\sum_{k=1}^{(h+1)n}\frac{1}{k^{\frac{2u-1}{4h-1}}}\cdot\frac{1}{k^{\frac{2u-1}{4h-1}}}

\ll\frac{1}{n^{1-\frac{2u}{4h-1}}}\cdot\int_{0}^{(h+1)n}\frac{dx}{x^{\frac{4u-2}{4h-1}}}\ll\frac{n^{1-\frac{4u-2}{4h-1}}}{n^{1-\frac{2u}{4h-1}}}=O(1).

Case 3.

0<u<v<2h
. Then,

\mathbb{E}(Y^{(\overline{\beta},\overline{\gamma},\overline{\delta},\overline{\mu},\overline{\nu})}_{u,v,h,B}(n))\ll

\displaystyle\ll\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}(z_{1},\dots{},z_{2h})\in(\mathbb{Z}^{+})^{2h}\\
z_{1}+\dots{}+z_{2h}=n\end{subarray}}\frac{1}{(z_{1}\cdots{}z_{2h})^{\frac{4h-3}{4h-1}}}\cdot\frac{1}{(|z_{1}+\dots{}+z_{u}-(z_{u+1}+\dots{}+z_{v})+K|+1)^{\frac{2v-1}{4h-1}}}

\displaystyle\ll\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}(m_{1},m_{2})\in(\mathbb{Z}^{+})^{2}\\
m_{1}+m_{2}<n\end{subarray}}\left(\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}(z_{1},\dots{},z_{u})\in(\mathbb{Z}^{+})^{u}\\
z_{1}+\dots{}+z_{u}=m_{1}\end{subarray}}\left(\frac{1}{(z_{1}\cdots{}z_{u})^{\frac{4h-3}{4h-1}}}\cdot\left(\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}(z_{u+1},\dots{},z_{v})\in(\mathbb{Z}^{+})^{v-u}\\
z_{u+1}+\dots{}+z_{v}=m_{2}\end{subarray}}\frac{1}{(z_{u+1}\cdots{}z_{v})^{\frac{4h-3}{4h-1}}}\right)\cdot\right.\right.

\displaystyle\left.\left.\cdot\frac{1}{(|m_{1}-m_{2}+K|+1)^{\frac{2v-1}{4h-1}}}\cdot\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}(z_{v+1},\dots{},z_{2h})\in(\mathbb{Z}^{+})^{2h-v}\\
z_{v+1}+\dots{}+z_{2h}=n-m_{1}-m_{2}\end{subarray}}\frac{1}{(z_{v+1}\cdots{}z_{2h})^{\frac{4h-3}{4h-1}}}\right)\right).

It follows from (iv) of Lemma 4 that

\mathbb{E}(Y^{(\overline{\beta},\overline{\gamma},\overline{\delta},\overline{\mu},\overline{\nu})}_{u,v,h,B}(n))\ll

\displaystyle\ll\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}(m_{1},m_{2})\in(\mathbb{Z}^{+})^{2}\\
m_{1}+m_{2}<n\end{subarray}}\left(\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}(z_{1},\dots{},z_{u})\in(\mathbb{Z}^{+})^{u}\\
z_{1}+\dots{}+z_{u}=m_{1}\end{subarray}}\left(\frac{1}{(z_{1}\cdots{}z_{u})^{\frac{4h-3}{4h-1}}}\cdot\right.\right.

\displaystyle\left.\left.\cdot\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}(z_{u+1},\dots{},z_{v})\in(\mathbb{Z}^{+})^{v-u}\\
z_{u+1}+\dots{}+z_{v}=m_{2}\end{subarray}}\frac{1}{(z_{u+1}\cdots{}z_{v})^{\frac{4h-3}{4h-1}}}\frac{1}{(|m_{1}-m_{2}+K|+1)^{\frac{2v-1}{4h-1}}}\cdot\frac{1}{(n-m_{1}-m_{2})^{\frac{2v-1}{4h-1}}}\right)\right)

\displaystyle\ll\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}(m_{1},m_{2})\in(\mathbb{Z}^{+})^{2}\\
m_{1}+m_{2}<n\end{subarray}}\left(\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}(z_{1},\dots{},z_{u})\in(\mathbb{Z}^{+})^{u}\\
z_{1}+\dots{}+z_{u}=m_{1}\end{subarray}}\left(\frac{1}{(z_{1}\cdots{}z_{u})^{\frac{4h-3}{4h-1}}}\cdot\frac{1}{m_{2}^{1-\frac{2(v-u)}{4h-1}}}\cdot\frac{1}{(|m_{1}-m_{2}+K|+1)^{\frac{2v-1}{4h-1}}}\right.\right.

\displaystyle\left.\left.\cdot\frac{1}{(n-m_{1}-m_{2})^{\frac{2v-1}{4h-1}}}\right)\right)

=\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}(m_{1},m_{2})\in(\mathbb{Z}^{+})^{2}\\
m_{1}+m_{2}<n\end{subarray}}\frac{1}{m_{1}^{1-\frac{2u}{4h-1}}}\cdot\frac{1}{m_{2}^{1-\frac{2(v-u)}{4h-1}}}\cdot\frac{1}{(|m_{1}-m_{2}+K|+1)^{\frac{2v-1}{4h-1}}}\cdot\frac{1}{(n-m_{1}-m_{2})^{\frac{2v-1}{4h-1}}}.

Now, it is enough to prove that this sum is

O\left(\frac{n^{\frac{1}{4h-1}}}{(\log n)^{4h}}\right)
.
It follows from (i) of Lemma 4 that

\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}(m_{1},m_{2})\in(\mathbb{Z}^{+})^{2}\\
m_{1}+m_{2}<n\end{subarray}}\frac{1}{m_{1}^{1-\frac{2u}{4h-1}}}\cdot\frac{1}{m_{2}^{1-\frac{2(v-u)}{4h-1}}}\cdot\frac{1}{(n-m_{1}-m_{2})^{\frac{2v-1}{4h-1}}}

\leq\sum_{m_{1}=1}^{n-1}\frac{1}{m_{1}^{1-\frac{2u}{4h-1}}}\cdot\sum_{m_{2}=1}^{n-m_{1}-1}\frac{1}{m_{2}^{1-\frac{2(v-u)}{4h-1}}}\cdot\frac{1}{(n-m_{1}-m_{2})^{\frac{2v-1}{4h-1}}}

\ll\sum_{m_{1}=1}^{n-1}\frac{1}{m_{1}^{1-\frac{2u}{4h-1}}}\cdot\frac{1}{(n-m_{1})^{\frac{2u-1}{4h-1}}}\ll n^{\frac{1}{4h-1}},

then

\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}(m_{1},m_{2})\in(\mathbb{Z}^{+})^{2}\\
m_{1}+m_{2}<n\\
|m_{1}-m_{2}+K|>(\log n)^{16h^{2}}\end{subarray}}\frac{1}{m_{1}^{1-\frac{2u}{4h-1}}}\cdot\frac{1}{m_{2}^{1-\frac{2(v-u)}{4h-1}}}\cdot\frac{1}{(|m_{1}-m_{2}+K|+1)^{\frac{2v-1}{4h-1}}}\cdot\frac{1}{(n-m_{1}-m_{2})^{\frac{2v-1}{4h-1}}}

\ll\frac{n^{\frac{1}{4h-1}}}{(\log n)^{4h}}.

Thus it is enough to show that

S=\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}(m_{1},m_{2})\in(\mathbb{Z}^{+})^{2}\\
m_{1}+m_{2}<n\\
|m_{1}-m_{2}+K|\leq(\log n)^{16h^{2}}\end{subarray}}\frac{1}{m_{1}^{1-\frac{2u}{4h-1}}}\cdot\frac{1}{m_{2}^{1-\frac{2(v-u)}{4h-1}}}\cdot\frac{1}{(|m_{1}-m_{2}+K|+1)^{\frac{2v-1}{4h-1}}}\cdot\frac{1}{(n-m_{1}-m_{2})^{\frac{2v-1}{4h-1}}}.

\ll\frac{n^{\frac{1}{4h-1}}}{(\log n)^{4h}}

(7)

We have three subcases.

Subcase 3.1

|K|<10(\log n)^{64h^{3}}
. Then

\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}(m_{1},m_{2})\in(\mathbb{Z}^{+})^{2}\\
m_{1}+m_{2}<n\\
|m_{1}-m_{2}+K|\leq(\log n)^{16h^{2}}\end{subarray}}\frac{1}{m_{1}^{1-\frac{2u}{4h-1}}}\cdot\frac{1}{m_{2}^{1-\frac{2(v-u)}{4h-1}}}\cdot\frac{1}{(|m_{1}-m_{2}+K|+1)^{\frac{2v-1}{4h-1}}}\cdot\frac{1}{(n-m_{1}-m_{2})^{\frac{2v-1}{4h-1}}}

=\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}(m_{1},m_{2})\in(\mathbb{Z}^{+})^{2}\\
m_{1}+m_{2}<n\\
|m_{1}-m_{2}+K|\leq(\log n)^{16h^{2}}\\
m_{1}<100(\log n)^{64h^{3}}\end{subarray}}\frac{1}{m_{1}^{1-\frac{2u}{4h-1}}}\cdot\frac{1}{m_{2}^{1-\frac{2(v-u)}{4h-1}}}\cdot\frac{1}{(|m_{1}-m_{2}+K|+1)^{\frac{2v-1}{4h-1}}}\cdot\frac{1}{(n-m_{1}-m_{2})^{\frac{2v-1}{4h-1}}}

+\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}(m_{1},m_{2})\in(\mathbb{Z}^{+})^{2}\\
m_{1}+m_{2}<n\\
|m_{1}-m_{2}+K|\leq(\log n)^{16h^{2}}\\
m_{1}\geq 100(\log n)^{64h^{3}}\end{subarray}}\frac{1}{m_{1}^{1-\frac{2u}{4h-1}}}\cdot\frac{1}{m_{2}^{1-\frac{2(v-u)}{4h-1}}}\cdot\frac{1}{(|m_{1}-m_{2}+K|+1)^{\frac{2v-1}{4h-1}}}\cdot\frac{1}{(n-m_{1}-m_{2})^{\frac{2v-1}{4h-1}}}

=S_{1}+S_{2}.

In
S_{1}
, there are
O((\log n)^{64h^{3}})
possibilities for
m_{1}
, and for a fixed
m_{1}
, there are
O((\log n)^{16h^{2}})
possibilities for
m_{2}
. Since every term in
S_{1}
is bounded, thus

S_{1}=O((\log n)^{64h^{3}+16h^{2}})=O\left(\frac{n^{\frac{1}{4h-1}}}{(\log n)^{4h}}\right).

If
m_{1}\geq 100(\log n)^{64h^{3}}
, then
|K|\leq 10(\log n)^{64h^{3}}
and
|m_{1}-m_{2}+K|\leq(\log n)^{16h^{2}}
, which implies that
m_{1}\asymp m_{2}
, and so

S_{2}\ll\sum_{m_{1}=1}^{n-1}\frac{1}{m_{1}^{1-\frac{2u}{4h-1}}}\cdot\frac{1}{m_{1}^{1-\frac{2(v-u)}{4h-1}}}\cdot(\log n)^{16h^{2}}

because for a fixed
m_{1}
, there are
O((\log n)^{16h^{2}})
possibilities for
m_{2}
and

\frac{1}{(|m_{1}-m_{2}+K|+1)^{\frac{2v-1}{4h-1}}}\cdot\frac{1}{(n-m_{1}-m_{2})^{\frac{2v-1}{4h-1}}}=O(1).

Then

S_{2}\ll\sum_{m_{1}=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{m_{1}^{2-\frac{2v}{4h-1}}}\cdot(\log n)^{16h^{2}}\ll(\log n)^{16h^{2}}=O\left(\frac{n^{\frac{1}{4h-1}}}{(\log n)^{4h}}\right)

because
v\leq 2h-1
.

Subase 3.2

,

because for a fixed
m_{1}

O((\log n)^{16h^{2}})
possibilities for
m_{2}
and
v\leq 2h-1
.

Subcase 3.3
