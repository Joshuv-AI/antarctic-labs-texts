C λ \displaystyle C_{\lambda} - Extended oscillator algebra and d \displaystyle d -orthogonal polynomials
Fethi Bouzeffour ∗,⋄ , Wissem Jedidi §,⋄

Abstract




Abstract:
In this paper we first construct an analytic realization of the
\displaystyle C_{\lambda}
-extended oscillator algebra with the help of difference-differential operators.
Secondly, we study families of
\displaystyle d
-orthogonal polynomials which are extensions of the Hermite and Laguerre polynomials. The underlying algebraic framework allowed us a systematic derivation of their main properties such as recurrence relations, difference-differential equations, lowering and rising operators and generating functions. Finally, we use these polynomials to construct a realization of the
\displaystyle C_{\lambda}
-extended oscillator by block matrices.

Keywords
: vector orthogonal polynomials, deformed oscillator algebra .

Introduction

In
[
20
]
, Quesne defined the
\displaystyle C_{\lambda}
-extended oscillator algebra (
\displaystyle\lambda
being an integer bigger than
\displaystyle 1
) as the associative algebra generated by four elements
\displaystyle a_{-},

\displaystyle a_{+},

\displaystyle N
,
\displaystyle s
that obey the commutation relations

\displaystyle\displaystyle[a_{-},\,a_{+}]=1+\sum_{i=1}^{\lambda-1}\,\beta_{i}\;s^{i},\qquad s^{\lambda}=1,

(1)

\displaystyle\displaystyle[N,\,a_{-}]=-a_{-},\qquad[N,\,a_{+}]=a_{+},\qquad[N,\,s]=0,

(2)

\displaystyle\displaystyle a_{-}s=\varepsilon_{\lambda}\;s\;a_{-}\qquad a_{+}s=\varepsilon_{\lambda}^{-1}\;s\;a_{+},\qquad\varepsilon_{\lambda}=e^{\frac{2i\pi}{\lambda}},

(3)

\displaystyle\displaystyle N^{*}=N,\qquad a_{+}^{*}=a_{-},\qquad s^{*}=s^{-1},

(4)

where the constants structure
\displaystyle\beta_{1},\dots,\,\beta_{\lambda-1}
are complex numbers restricted by the conditions

\overline{\beta_{i}}=\beta_{\lambda-i},\quad i=1,\,\dots,\lambda.

(5)

The connection between orthogonal polynomials and harmonic oscillator as well as the quantum algebra is well known
[
28
,
21
]

The oscillator algebra of creation, annihilation, and number operators plays a central role in the investigation of many class of orthogonal polynomials, and provides a useful tool to derive their operational properties such as recurrence relations, difference equation, lowering and rising operators and generating functions. It particular,

\displaystyle\lambda=2

\displaystyle C_{\lambda}

[

\displaystyle a_{-},\,a_{+},\,R
,
\displaystyle 1

\displaystyle\displaystyle[a_{-},\,a_{+}]

\displaystyle\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\displaystyle 1+2\nu R,\qquad R^{2}=1,

\displaystyle\displaystyle\{a_{\pm},R\}

\displaystyle\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\displaystyle 0,\qquad[1,\,a_{\pm}]=[1,\,R]=0.

A realization of the Calogero–Vasiliev algebra working in the

\displaystyle\Psi=\Psi(x)

\displaystyle[N,a_{-}^{n}]=-na_{-}^{n-1},\qquad[N,a_{+}^{n}]=na_{+}^{n-1}.

In particular, it holds that

\displaystyle\displaystyle[a_{-}^{n\lambda},a_{+}]=n\lambda a_{-}^{n\lambda-1}\quad\text{and}\text{\hskip 10.22217pt}[a_{-},a_{+}^{n\lambda}]=n\lambda a_{+}^{n\lambda-1}.

\displaystyle u

\displaystyle P

\displaystyle[x]

[
