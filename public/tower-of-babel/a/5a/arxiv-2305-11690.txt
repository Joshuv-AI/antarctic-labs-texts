Local regularity for nonlocal double phase equations in the Heisenberg group Thanks: ∗ Corresponding author.
Yuzhou Fang, Chao Zhang and Junli Zhang ∗

Abstract
. We prove interior boundedness and Hölder continuity for the weak solutions of nonlocal double phase equations in the Heisenberg group ℍ n \mathbb{H}^{n} . This solves a problem raised by Palatucci and Piccinini et. al. in 2022 and 2023 for nonlinear integro-differential problems in the Heisenberg group ℍ n \mathbb{H}^{n} . Our proof of the a priori estiamtes bases on the spirit of De Giorgi-Nash-Moser theory, where the important ingredients are Caccioppoli-type inequality and Logarithmic estimate. To achieve this goal, we establish a new and crucial Sobolev-Poincaré type inequality in local domain, which may be of independent interest and potential applications.

Introduction

In this paper, we are interested in local behaviour of the weak solutions to nonlocal double phase problem in the Heisenberg group
{{\mathbb{H}}^{n}}
, whose prototype is

\mathrm{P.V.}\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}\left[\frac{|u(\xi)-u(\eta)|^{p-2}(u(\xi)-u(\eta))}{\|\eta^{-1}\circ\xi\|^{Q+sp}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}+a(\xi,\eta)\frac{|u(\xi)-u(\eta)|^{q-2}(u(\xi)-u(\eta))}{\|\eta^{-1}\circ\xi\|^{Q+tq}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}\right]\,d\eta=0\quad\text{in }\Omega,

where
1<p\leq q<\infty
,
s,t\in(0,1)
,

is the homogeneous dimension and

\mathbb{H}^{n}
(

first established the interior boundedness and Hölder continuity via employing the De Giorge-Nash-Moser iteration; see also

Functional setting

\displaystyle\leq CN2^{i(Q+sp+tq)}\left(\frac{\sigma^{sp-\frac{sp(p-1)}{q-1}}k_{i}^{p-1}}{r_{j+1}^{sp}}+a^{+}_{j+1}\frac{\sigma^{tq-sp}k_{i}^{q-1}}{r_{j+1}^{tq}}+\frac{\overline{G}(k_{i})}{k_{i}}\right)

\displaystyle\leq CN2^{i(Q+sp+tq)}\frac{\overline{G}(k_{i})}{k_{i}}.

Therefore,

J_{2}\leq CN2^{i(Q+sp+tq)}\overline{G}(k_{i})A_{i}.

(4.24)

On the other hand, making use of Lemma
2.8
with
u:=w_{i}
yields that

\displaystyle A^{\frac{1}{\gamma}}_{i+1}\overline{G}(k_{i}-k_{i+1})

\displaystyle\leq\left(\fint_{B^{i+1}}\left(\left|\frac{w_{i}}{r^{s}_{j+1}}\right|^{p}+a^{+}_{j+1}\left|\frac{w_{i}}{r^{t}_{j+1}}\right|^{q}\right)^{\gamma}\,d\xi\right)^{\frac{1}{\gamma}}

\displaystyle\leq CN\left(\frac{D_{1}(\hat{\rho}_{i},\rho_{i+1})}{r_{j+1}^{sp}}+\frac{\widetilde{D}_{1}(\hat{\rho}_{i},\rho_{i+1})}{r_{j+1}^{tq}}\right)\fint_{\hat{B}^{i}}\int_{\hat{B}^{i}}\frac{H(\xi,\eta,|w_{i}(\xi)-w_{i}(\eta)|)}{\|\eta^{-1}\circ\xi\|^{Q}_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}}\,d\xi d\eta

\displaystyle\quad+CN(1+D_{2}(\hat{\rho}_{i},\rho_{i+1})+\widetilde{D}_{2}(\hat{\rho}_{i},\rho_{i+1}))\fint_{\hat{B}^{i}}\left|\frac{w_{i}}{r^{s}_{j+1}}\right|^{p}+a^{-}_{j+1}\left|\frac{w_{i}}{r^{t}_{j+1}}\right|^{q}\,d\xi.

(4.25)

Thanks to the definitions of
D_{1},D_{2},\widetilde{D}_{1},\widetilde{D}_{2}
and
\hat{\rho}_{i},\rho_{i+1}
, we from
\hat{\rho}_{i}\approx\rho_{i+1}\approx r_{j+1}
and
\hat{\rho}_{i}-\rho_{i+1}=2^{-i-3}r_{j+1}
calculate

\frac{D_{1}(\hat{\rho}_{i},\rho_{i+1})}{r_{j+1}^{sp}}\leq C2^{i(Q+sp+p)},\quad\frac{\widetilde{D}_{1}(\hat{\rho}_{i},\rho_{i+1})}{r_{j+1}^{tq}}\leq C2^{i(Q+tq+q)}

and

D_{2}(\hat{\rho}_{i},\rho_{i+1})\leq C2^{i(Q+sp)},\quad\widetilde{D}_{2}(\hat{\rho}_{i},\rho_{i+1})\leq C2^{i(Q+tq)}.

It is easy to obtain

\fint_{\hat{B}^{i}}\left|\frac{w_{i}}{r^{s}_{j+1}}\right|^{p}+a^{-}_{j+1}\left|\frac{w_{i}}{r^{t}_{j+1}}\right|^{q}\,d\xi\leq C\fint_{{B}^{i}}\overline{G}(w_{i})\,d\xi\leq C\overline{G}(k_{i})A_{i}.

(4.26)

It follows from (
4
)–(
4.26
) that

A^{\frac{1}{\gamma}}_{i+1}\overline{G}(2^{-i-1}\varepsilon\omega(r_{j}))=A^{\frac{1}{\gamma}}_{i+1}\overline{G}(k_{i}-k_{i+1})\leq CN^{2}2^{i2(Q+2q)}\overline{G}(k_{i})A_{i}\leq CN^{2}2^{i2(Q+2q)}\overline{G}(\varepsilon\omega(r_{j}))A_{i}

and further

A_{i+1}\leq CN^{2\gamma}2^{i2(Q+3q)\gamma}A^{\gamma}_{i},

where
\gamma=\min\left\{\frac{p^{*}_{s}}{p},\frac{q^{*}_{t}}{q}\right\}>1
and
C
depends on
n,p,q,s,t,\alpha,[a]_{\alpha},\|a\|_{L^{\infty}},\beta
.

Now if
A_{0}
fulfills

\displaystyle A_{0}=\frac{|2B_{j+1}\cap\{u_{j}\leq 2\varepsilon\omega(r_{j})\}|}{|2B_{j+1}|}\leq(CN^{2\gamma})^{-\frac{1}{\gamma-1}}2^{-\frac{2\gamma(Q+3q)}{(\gamma-1)^{2}}}=:\mu,

(4.27)

then by Lemma
3.4
we deduce
A_{i}\rightarrow 0
as
i\rightarrow\infty
. This means

u_{j}\geq\varepsilon\omega(r_{j})\quad\text{a.e. in}\ B_{j+1},

which together with (
4.17
) leads to

\mathop{\rm osc}\limits_{B_{j+1}}u\leq(1-\varepsilon)\omega(r_{j})=(1-\varepsilon)\sigma^{-\beta}\omega(r_{j+1}).

Finally, choosing
\beta\in\left(0,\frac{sp}{q-1}\right)
small enough such that

\sigma^{\beta}\geq 1-\varepsilon=1-\sigma^{\frac{sp}{q-1}-\beta},

then
\mathrm{osc}_{B_{j+1}}u\leq\omega(r_{j+1})
, and
\beta
depends on
n,p,q,s,t,\alpha,[a]_{\alpha},\|a\|_{L^{\infty}}
and

. Indeed, due to (

), it yields that

by picking

with

in (
4.27
). That is, we could select

to ensure the condition (
4.27
) does hold true. Now we finish the proof.
