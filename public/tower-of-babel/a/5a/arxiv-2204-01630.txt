Abstract
The first aim of this paper is to examine existence, uniqueness and regularity for the Cahn-Hilliard-Cook (CHC) equation in space dimension d ≤ 3 d\leq 3 . By applying a spectral Galerkin method to the infinite dimensional equation, we elaborate the well-posedness and regularity of the finite dimensional approximate problem. The key idea lies in transforming the stochastic problem with additive noise into an equivalent random equation. The regularity of the solution to the equivalent random equation is obtained, in one dimension, with the aid of the Gagliardo-Nirenberg inequality and done in two and three dimensions, by the energy argument. Further, the approximate solution is shown to be strongly convergent to the unique mild solution of the original CHC equation, whose spatio-temporal regularity can be attained by similar arguments. In addition, a fully discrete approximation of such problem is investigated, performed by the spectral Galerkin method in space and the backward Euler method in time. The previously obtained regularity results of the problem help us to identify strong convergence rates of the fully discrete scheme. Key words: Cahn-Hilliard-Cook equation, spatio-temporal regularity, spectral Galerkin method, backward Euler method, strong convergence rates.

\beta\in[0,\gamma]
,

\displaystyle{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{\|Z(t)-Z(s)\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H^{\beta})}\leq C|t-s|^{\min\{\frac{1}{2},\frac{\gamma-\beta}{4}\}}.}}

(3.5)

Therefore, it suffices to treat (
3.2
). In the next two subsections, we will use Galerkin’s method and energy arguments to address this issue.

3.1
Useful inequalities

In this part, we collect some useful inequalities, which play an important role in our error analysis below. We first introduce the following embedding inequalities,

\displaystyle{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{H^{1}\subset H^{\frac{d}{3}}}}\subset L^{6}(D)\quad\text{and}{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{\quad H^{\delta}}}\subset C(D;\mathbb{R}),\quad\text{ for }\delta>\tfrac{d}{2},\;d\in\{1,2,3\}.

(3.6)

In light of the definition of
P
, it is not difficult to check

\displaystyle\|Pv\|_{L^{q}}\leq{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}(1+|D|^{-\frac{1}{q}})}\|v\|_{L^{q}},\;q\geq 2.

(3.7)

This together with (
3.6
) implies (see
[
46
]
), for any
\delta>\frac{d}{2}
,

\displaystyle\begin{split}\|A^{-\frac{\delta}{2}}Px\|\leq C\|x\|_{L^{1}},\;\|A^{-\frac{1}{2}}Px\|\leq C\|x\|_{L^{\frac{6}{5}}},\;\forall x\in L^{2}(D).\end{split}

(3.8)

Additionally, we also use the fact that,
the norm
\|\cdot\|_{2}
is equivalent on
H^{2}
to
the standard Sobolev norm
\|\cdot\|_{H^{2}(D)}
(see
[
47
,
50
]
) to derive, for any
f,g\in H^{2}
,

\displaystyle\|fg\|_{H^{2}(D)}\leq C\|f\|_{H^{2}(D)}\|g\|_{H^{2}(D)}\leq C{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{\|f\|_{2}\,\|g\|_{2}}}.

(3.9)

Based on the above preparations, we derive the following inequality.

Lemma 3.2

Let
g:L^{4}\rightarrow H
be the Nemytskij operator of a polynomial of second degree. The following estimates hold
for any
\iota\in(\frac{1}{2},1)
and
d=1
,

\displaystyle\|g(u)v\|_{{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{\iota}}}\leq C\|v\|_{1}(1+\|u\|_{\iota}^{2}),\text{ for }\;u\in{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{H^{\iota}}},v\in H^{1},

(3.10)

and
for any
\iota\in(\frac{d}{2},2)
,
d=2,3
,

\displaystyle\|g(u)v\|_{1}\leq C\|v\|_{1}(1+\|u\|_{\iota}^{2}),for\;u\in H^{\iota},v\in H^{1}.

(3.11)

Proof of Lemma
3.2
.

First, we consider the case
d=1
. The Hölder inequality, (
3.6
) and the equivalence of norms in
H^{\iota}
and
H^{\iota}(D)
yield that for
u,v\in H^{\iota}
,

\displaystyle\begin{split}\|g(u)v\|^{2}_{\iota}&\leq\|g(u)v\|^{2}+C\int_{D}\int_{D}\frac{|g(u(\xi_{1}))v(\xi_{1})-g(u(\xi_{2}))v(\xi_{2})|^{2}}{|\xi_{1}-\xi_{2}|^{2\iota+1}}\,\text{d}\xi_{1}\,\text{d}\xi_{2}\\
&\leq C\|v\|^{2}(1+\|u\|_{V}^{4})+C(1+\|u\|_{V}^{4})\|v\|^{2}_{\iota}+C\|u\|^{2}_{\iota}(1+\|u\|_{V}^{2})\|v\|^{2}_{V}\\
&\leq C(1+\|u\|_{V}^{4}+\|u\|^{4}_{\iota})(\|v\|^{2}_{V}+\|v\|_{\iota}^{2})\\
&\leq C(1+\|u\|^{4}_{\iota})\|v\|_{\iota}^{2}.\end{split}

(3.12)

The above estimate assures (
3.10
).
For the case
d=2,3
, we first obtain

\displaystyle\begin{split}\|g(u)v\|_{1}&\leq C(\|g(u)v\|+\|v\nabla g(u)\|+\|g(u)\nabla v\|)\\
&\leq C(1+\|u\|^{2}_{V})\|v\|+\|v\nabla g(u)\|+C\|v\|_{1}(1+\|u\|_{V}^{2}).\end{split}

(3.13)

Further,
owing to (
3.6
) and the fact that the Sobolev embedding theorem tells us
{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{H^{s}}}\subset L^{p}
, for
0<s<1
and
p=\frac{2d}{d-2s}
,
there exists a constant
\delta
given by

{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{\delta=\frac{2\iota-2}{2-\iota}}}

for
d=2
and
\delta=1
for
d=3
such that, for
\iota>\frac{d}{2},d=2,3

\displaystyle\|v\nabla u\|\leq C\|v\|_{L^{\frac{2(2+\delta)}{\delta}}}\|\nabla u\|_{L^{2+\delta}}\leq C\|v\|_{1}\|u\|_{H^{\iota}}

(3.14)

Therefore,

\displaystyle\begin{split}\|v\nabla g(u)\|&\leq C\|v\nabla u\|(1+\|u\|_{V})\leq C\|v\|_{1}(1+\|u\|_{\iota}^{2}).\end{split}

(3.15)

This together with (
3.6
) and (
3.13
) can show (
3.11
). Hence this ends the proof.

\square

3.2
The semidiscrete Galerkin approximation

In this part, we use the Galerkin method to approximate the problem (
3.2
). For
n\in\mathbb{N}
, we define a finite dimensional subspace of
{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{H}}
by

\displaystyle H_{n}={\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{span\{e_{0},e_{1},e_{2}\cdots,e_{n}\}}}

(3.16)

and the projection operator
P_{n}:{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{H^{\alpha}}}\rightarrow H_{n}
by
P_{n}\xi={\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{\sum_{i=0}^{n}\big<\xi,e_{i}\big>e_{i}}}
, for
\forall\xi\in H^{\alpha}
,
\alpha\geq-2
. Here
\{e_{i}\}_{i=0}^{n}
are the
n+1
first eigenvectors of the dominant linear operator
A
in the space
H
. It is not difficult to find that the operators
A
and
P_{n}
commute, but
P_{n}P\neq P
. Moreover,

\displaystyle\|(I-P_{n})\varphi\|\leq\lambda_{n+1}^{-\frac{\alpha}{2}}|\varphi|_{\alpha},\;\forall\varphi\in H^{\alpha},\;\alpha>0.

(3.17)

Then
the Galerkin approximation of (
3.2
) is given by

\displaystyle\begin{split}\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\frac{\text{d}v^{n}}{\text{d}t}+A^{2}v^{n}+P_{n}APF(v^{n}+{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Z^{n}}})=0,&\;\text{ in }\;D\times(0,T],\\
v^{n}(0)={\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{P_{n}PX_{0}}},&\;\text{ in }\;D,\end{array}\right.\end{split}

(3.18)

where
{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Z^{n}(t)=P_{n}Z(t)}}
.
It is clear that the problem (
3.18
) admits a unique solution
given by

\displaystyle v^{n}(t)=E(t)P_{n}PX_{0}-\int_{0}^{t}E(t-s)P_{n}APF(v^{n}(s)+{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Z^{n}(s)}})\,\text{d}s,\quad t\in[0,T].

(3.19)

Here
Z^{n}(t)
enjoys the similar spatio-temporal regularity as
Z(t)
:

\displaystyle\sup_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\mathbb{E}\Big[\sup_{s\in[0,T]}\big\|{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Z^{n}(t)}}\big\|_{\gamma}^{p}\Big]<\infty,

(3.20)

and for
\beta\in[0,\gamma]
,

\displaystyle\sup_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\|{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Z^{n}(t)-Z^{n}(s)}}\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H^{\beta})}\leq C|t-s|^{\min\{\frac{1}{2},\frac{\gamma-\beta}{4}\}}.

(3.21)

Setting
X^{n}(t):=v^{n}(t)+{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Z^{n}}}(t)
, we will show that the sequence
\{X^{n}(\cdot)\}
is Cauchy in
C_{W}([0,T];H)
, where
C_{W}([0,T];H)
is a Banach space consisting of all continuous mappings
G\colon[0,T]\rightarrow L^{2}(\Omega,\mathcal{F},\mathbb{P};H)
, endowed with the norm

\displaystyle\|G\|_{C_{W}([0,T];H)}=\Big(\sup_{s\in[0,T]}\mathbb{E}\big[\|G(s)\|^{2}\big]\Big)^{\frac{1}{2}}.

(3.22)

To prove that the limit of
\{X^{n}(t)\}
in
C_{W}([0,T];H)
is the required mild solution of (
1.2
),
let us start with a priori estimate of
X^{n}(t)
.

Lemma 3.3

Let
v^{n}(t)
be a solution of (
3.18
) and let
X^{n}(t):=v^{n}(t)+{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Z^{n}(t)}}
.
If Assumptions
2.1
-
2.4
are valid, then there exists a constant
C=C(T,\gamma,p)
such that

\displaystyle\sup_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\mathbb{E}\Big[\sup_{s\in[0,T]}\|X^{n}(s)\|^{p}\Big]\leq C<\infty.

(3.23)

Its proof is not new and has been already used in the literature. We keep it here for completeness.

Proof of Lemma
3.3
.

Multiplying both sides of (
3.18
) by
A^{-1}v^{n}
and using (
2.11
) and the chain rule
and the fact yield

\displaystyle\begin{split}|v^{n}(t)|_{-1}^{2}\leq&|{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{P_{n}PX_{0}}}|_{-1}^{2}-2\int_{0}^{t}|v^{n}(s)|_{1}^{2}\,\text{d}s-2\int_{0}^{t}\big<F(v^{n}(s)+{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Z^{n}(s)}},v^{n}(s)\big>\,\text{d}s\\
\leq&|X_{0}|_{-1}^{2}-2\int_{0}^{t}|v^{n}(s)|_{1}^{2}\,\text{d}s-2\int_{0}^{t}\|v^{n}(s)\|^{4}_{L^{4}}\,\text{d}s+2\int_{0}^{t}\|v^{n}(s)\|^{2}_{L^{2}}\,\text{d}s\\
&-2\int_{0}^{t}{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{\big<3(v^{n}(s))^{2}Z^{n}(s)+3(Z^{n}(s))^{2}v_{n}(s)+(Z^{n}(s))^{3}-Z^{n}(s),v^{n}(s)\big>}}\,\text{d}s\\
\leq&|X_{0}|_{-1}^{2}-2\int_{0}^{t}|v^{n}(s)|_{1}^{2}\,\text{d}s-2\int_{0}^{t}\|v^{n}(s)\|^{4}_{L^{4}}\,\text{d}s+2\int_{0}^{t}\|v^{n}(s)\|^{2}_{L^{2}}\,\text{d}s\\
&{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{+6\int_{0}^{t}\|v^{n}(s)\|_{L^{4}}^{3}\|Z^{n}(s)\|_{L^{4}}\,\text{d}s+6\int_{0}^{t}\|v^{n}(s)\|_{L^{4}}\|Z^{n}(s)\|_{L^{4}}^{2}\,\text{d}s}}\\
&{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{+\int_{0}^{t}\|v^{n}(s)\|_{L^{4}}^{2}\|Z^{n}(s)\|_{L^{4}}^{2}\,\text{d}s+\int_{0}^{t}\|v^{n}(s)\|\|Z^{n}(s)\|\,\text{d}s}}\\
\leq&|X_{0}|_{-1}^{2}-2\int_{0}^{t}|v^{n}(s)|_{1}^{2}\,\text{d}s-\int_{0}^{t}\|v^{n}(s)\|^{4}_{L^{4}}\,\text{d}s+C\int_{0}^{t}(1+\|{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Z^{n}(s)}}\|_{L^{4}}^{4})\,\text{d}s,\end{split}

(3.24)

where Young’s inequality was also used in the last inequality.
Therefore,

\displaystyle 2\int_{0}^{t}|v^{n}(s)|_{1}^{2}\,\text{d}s+\int_{0}^{t}\|v^{n}(s)\|_{L^{4}}^{4}\,\text{d}s\leq|X_{0}|^{2}_{-1}+C\int_{0}^{t}(1+{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{\|Z^{n}(s)\|_{L^{4}}^{4}}})\,\text{d}s,

(3.25)

which in combination with (
3.6
) and (
3.20
) leads to

\displaystyle\begin{split}\mathbb{E}\Big[\int_{0}^{t}|v^{n}(s)|_{1}^{2}\,\text{d}s\Big]^{p}+\mathbb{E}\Big[\int_{0}^{t}\|v^{n}(s)\|_{L^{4}}^{4}\,\text{d}s\Big]^{p}&\leq C\Big(\mathbb{E}\big[|X_{0}|_{-1}^{2p}\big]+\int_{0}^{t}\big(1+\mathbb{E}\big[\|{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Z^{n}(s)}}\|_{V}^{4p}\big]\big)\;\text{d}s\Big)\\
&\leq C\Big(\mathbb{E}\big[|X_{0}|_{-1}^{2p}\big]+\int_{0}^{t}\big(1+\sup_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\mathbb{E}\big[{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{\|Z^{n}(s)\|_{\gamma}^{4p}}}\big]\big)\;\text{d}s\Big)\\
&<\infty.\end{split}

(3.26)

Now we are in the position to give the a priori estimate of
\|v^{n}\|
.
Taking inner product of (
3.18
) by
v^{n}(t)
and using the chain rule and the self-adjointness of
A
, one can find that

\displaystyle\begin{split}\|v^{n}(t)\|^{2}=&\|P_{n}PX_{0}\|^{2}-2\int_{0}^{t}\|Av^{n}(s)\|^{2}\,\text{d}s+2\int_{0}^{t}{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{\big<F\big(v^{n}(s)+Z^{n}(s)\big),-Av^{n}(s)\big>\,\text{d}s}}\\
=&{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{\|P_{n}PX_{0}\|^{2}-2\int_{0}^{t}\|Av^{n}(s)\|^{2}\,\text{d}s+2\int_{0}^{t}\big<\big(v^{n}(s))^{3}-v^{n}(s)\big),-Av^{n}(s)\big>\,\text{d}s}}\\
&+2\int_{0}^{t}\big<{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{3(v^{n}(s))^{2}Z^{n}(s)+3v^{n}(s)(Z^{n}(s))^{2}+(Z^{n}(s))^{3}-Z^{n}(s)}},{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{-Av^{n}(s)}}\big>\,\text{d}s\\
\leq&\|X_{0}\|^{2}-2\int_{0}^{t}\|Av^{n}(s)\|^{2}\,\text{d}s-\tfrac{3}{2}\int_{0}^{t}|(v^{n}(s))^{2}|_{1}^{2}\,\text{d}s+2\int_{0}^{t}|v^{n}(s)|_{1}^{2}\,\text{d}s\\
&+{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{2\int_{0}^{t}\big(\|{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Z^{n}(s)}}\|_{V}^{2}\|v^{n}(s)\|^{2}_{L^{4}}+\|v^{n}(s)\|\|{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Z^{n}(s)}}\|_{V}^{2}+\|{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Z^{n}(s)}}\|_{L^{6}}^{3}+\|{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Z^{n}(s)}}\|)\|Av\|\,\text{d}s}}\\
\leq&\|X_{0}\|^{2}-\int_{0}^{t}\|Av^{n}(s)\|^{2}\,\text{d}s-\tfrac{3}{2}\int_{0}^{t}|(v^{n}(s))^{2}|_{1}^{2}\,\text{d}s+2\int_{0}^{t}|v^{n}(s)|_{1}^{2}\,\text{d}s\\
&+C\int_{0}^{t}\big(\|{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Z^{n}(s)}}\|_{V}^{4}\|v^{n}(s)\|^{4}_{L^{4}}+\|v^{n}(s)\|\|{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Z^{n}(s)}}\|_{V}^{4}+\|{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Z^{n}(s)}}\|_{L^{6}}^{6}+\|{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Z^{n}(s)}}\|^{2})\,\text{d}s.\end{split}

(3.27)

Hence, we have

\displaystyle\begin{split}&\|v^{n}(t)\|^{2}+\int_{0}^{t}\!\!\|Av^{n}(s)\|^{2}\,\text{d}s+\tfrac{3}{2}\int_{0}^{t}\!\!|(v^{n}(s))^{2}|_{1}^{2}\,\text{d}s\\
&\leq C\|X_{0}\|^{2}+C\int_{0}^{t}\!\!\big(1+\|{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Z^{n}(s)}}\|_{V}^{6}\big)\big(1+\|v^{n}(s)\|^{4}_{L^{4}}\big)\,\text{d}s.\end{split}

(3.28)

Further, from (
3.26
), (
3.20
) and the Hölder inequality,
it follows that for any
n\in\mathbb{N}
,

\displaystyle\begin{split}\mathbb{E}\Big[\sup_{s\in[0,T]}&\|v^{n}(s)\|^{2p}\Big]+\mathbb{E}\Big[\int_{0}^{T}\|Av^{n}(s)\|^{2}\,\text{d}s\Big]^{p}+\mathbb{E}\Big[\int_{0}^{T}|(v^{n}(s))^{2}|_{1}^{2}\,\text{d}s\Big]^{p}\\
\leq&C(p)~\mathbb{E}[\|X_{0}\|^{2p}]+C(p,T)\Big(1+\mathbb{E}\Big[\sup_{s\in[0,T]}\|{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Z^{n}(s)}}\|_{\gamma}^{12p}\Big]\Big)^{\frac{1}{2}}\Big(1+\mathbb{E}\Big[\int_{0}^{T}\|v^{n}(s)\|^{4}_{L^{4}}\,\text{d}s\Big]^{2p}\Big)^{\frac{1}{2}}\\
\leq&C(p,T,X_{0}).\end{split}

(3.29)

This together with (
3.20
) finishes the proof of this lemma.
\square

Moreover, we provide a stronger moment bound and a temporal regularity result of
X^{n}(t)
,
which will play an important role in the proof of the existence and uniqueness of the mild solution
X(t)
.

Lemma 3.4

Let
v^{n}(t)
be the solution of (
3.18
)
and

X^{n}(t)=v^{n}(t)+{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Z^{n}(t)}}
.
If Assumptions
2.1
-
2.4
are valid, then we obtain

\displaystyle\sup_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\sup_{s\in[0,T]}\|X^{n}(s)\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H^{\gamma})}<\infty,

(3.30)

and for any
\beta\in[0,\gamma]
,

\displaystyle\sup_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\|X^{n}(t)-X^{n}(s)\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H^{\beta})}\leq C|t-s|^{\min\{\frac{1}{2},\frac{\gamma-\beta}{4}\}}.

(3.31)

Proof of Lemma
3.4
.

The proof of this lemma in dimension
1
can be given by
following the steps of the proof of
[
17
, Proposition 3.1]
.
In what follows we focus on the proof of (
3.30
)-(
3.31
)
in dimensions two and three.
For the case

\gamma\in[3,4]
,
please refer to
[
46
]
.
Therefore, it suffices to show the case

\gamma\in(\frac{d}{2},3]
.
By taking any fixed number

\delta_{0}\in(\frac{3}{2},2)
,
we consider two possibilities:
either

\gamma\in(\frac{d}{2},\delta_{0}]

or

\gamma\in(\delta_{0},3]
.
, we introduce a Lyapunov functional
J(u)
, defined by

\displaystyle J(u)=\frac{1}{2}\|\nabla u\|^{2}+\int_{D}\Phi(u)\,\text{d}x,

(3.32)

where
\Phi
is the primitive of
F
vanishing at zeros, i.e.,
\Phi(s)=\int_{0}^{s}F(t)\,\text{d}t,s\in\mathbb{R}
.
We multiply (
3.18
) by
A^{-1}\dot{v}^{n}
to arrive at

\displaystyle\begin{split}\big|\dot{v}^{n}(t)\big|_{-1}^{2}+\tfrac{1}{2}\tfrac{\text{d}|v^{n}(t)|_{1}^{2}}{\text{d}t}+\big<F(v^{n}(t)+{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Z^{n}(t)}}),\;\dot{v}^{n}(t)\big>=0,\end{split}

(3.33)

where
v^{n}(\cdot)
is a solution of (
3.18
) and
\dot{v}^{n}(\cdot)

stands for the time derivative of
v^{n}(\cdot)
.

Using the fact
\Phi^{\prime}(u)=F(u)
and
P\dot{v}_{n}=\dot{v}_{n}
yields

\displaystyle\begin{split}&-\big<F(v^{n}(t)+{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Z^{n}(t))}},\;\dot{v}^{n}(t)\big>\\
=&-\big<F(v^{n}(t)),\;\dot{v}^{n}(t)\big>-\big<{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{3(v^{n}(t))^{2}Z^{n}(t)+3v^{n}(t)(Z^{n}(t))^{2}+(Z^{n}(t))^{3}-Z^{n}(t)}},\;\dot{v}^{n}(t)\big>\\
\leq&-\tfrac{\text{d}}{\text{d}t}\!\int_{D}\!\!\!\Phi(v^{n}(t))\,\text{d}x+\big|P\big({\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{3(v^{n}(t))^{2}Z^{n}(t)+3v^{n}(t)(Z^{n}(t))^{2}+(Z^{n}(t))^{3}-Z^{n}(t)}}\big)\big|_{1}|\dot{v}^{n}(t)|_{-1}.\end{split}

(3.34)

To treat the above expression, we use (
3.6
) and (
3.14
) with
\kappa=\gamma
to obtain, for
\gamma>\frac{d}{2}
,
d=2,3
,

\displaystyle\begin{split}\big|P\big(3(v^{n})^{2}{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Z^{n}}}&+3v^{n}{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{(Z^{n})^{2}}}+{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{(Z^{n})^{3}-Z^{n}}}\big)\big|_{1}\\
&{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{\leq\big\|\nabla\big(3(v^{n})^{2}{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Z^{n}}}+3v^{n}{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{(Z^{n})^{2}}}+{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{(Z^{n})^{3}-Z^{n}}}\big)\big\|}}\\
&\leq C\big(\|(v^{n})^{2}\|_{1}\;\|Z^{n}\|_{V}+\|(v^{n})^{2}\nabla Z^{n}\|+\|v^{n}\|_{1}\;\|Z^{n}\|_{V}^{2}\\
&\quad+\|v^{n}\|_{V}\|Z^{n}\|_{1}\;\|Z^{n}\|_{V}+\|Z^{n}\|\;\|Z^{n}\|_{V}^{2}+\|Z^{n}\|_{1}\big)\\
&{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{\leq C\big(\|(v^{n})^{2}\|_{1}\;\|Z^{n}\|_{\gamma}+\|(v^{n})^{2}\|_{1}\|Z^{n}\|_{\gamma}+\|v^{n}\|_{1}\;\|Z^{n}\|_{\gamma}^{2}}}\\
&{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{\quad+\|Av^{n}\|\;\|Z^{n}\|^{2}_{\gamma}+\|Z^{n}\|_{\gamma}^{3}+\|Z^{n}\|_{\gamma}\big)}}\\
&\leq C(\gamma)\big(1+\|(v^{n})^{2}\|_{1}+\|Av^{n}\|\big)\big(1+\|Z^{n}\|_{\gamma}^{3}\big)\end{split}

(3.35)

Therefore,

\displaystyle\begin{split}-\big<F(v^{n}(t)+Z^{n}),\;\dot{v}^{n}(t)\big>\leq&-\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}t}\int_{D}\Phi(v^{n}(t))\,\text{d}x+\frac{1}{2}\|\dot{v}^{n}(t)\|_{-1}^{2}\\
&+C(\gamma)\big(1+\|(v^{n}(t))^{2}\|_{1}+\|Av^{n}(t)\|\big)^{2}\big(1+\|Z^{n}(t)\|_{\gamma}^{3}\big)^{2}.\end{split}

(3.36)

The above estimate together with (
3.33
) and (
3.32
) yields

\displaystyle\begin{split}J(v^{n}(t))\leq J({\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{P_{n}PX_{0}}})+C(\gamma,T)\Big(1+\int_{0}^{t}\!\big(\|(v^{n}(s))^{2}\|_{1}^{2}+\|Av^{n}(s)\|^{2}\big)\,\text{d}s\Big)\Big(1+\sup_{s\in[0,T]}\big\|{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Z^{n}(s)}}\big\|_{\gamma}^{6}\Big).\end{split}

(3.37)

Then applying
(
3.29
), (
3.26
), (
3.32
)
and
(
3.20
)
infers that

\displaystyle\sup_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\sup_{s\in[0,T]}\|v^{n}(s)\|_{L^{2p}(\Omega;H^{1})}<\infty,

(3.38)

which together with (
3.4
) arrives at, for
d=2,3
,

\displaystyle\sup_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\sup_{s\in[0,T]}\|F(X^{n}(s))\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H)}<\infty.

(3.39)

Hence, when (
2.16
) is fulfilled with
\gamma\in(\frac{d}{2},\delta_{0}]
, we utilize
(
2.5
) with
\mu=0
and
1+\frac{\gamma}{2}
to show

\displaystyle\begin{split}\|v^{n}(t)\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H^{\gamma})}&\leq\|E(t)P_{n}PX_{0}\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H^{\gamma})}+\Big\|\int_{0}^{t}E(t-s)P_{n}APF(X^{n}(s))\,\text{d}s\Big\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H^{\gamma})}\\
&\leq\|X_{0}\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H^{\gamma})}+C\int_{0}^{t}(t-s)^{-\frac{2+\gamma}{4}}\,\text{d}s\sup_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\sup_{s\in[0,T]}\|F(X^{n}(s))\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H)}\\
&\leq C(T,\gamma,X_{0},p).\end{split}

(3.40)

For the temporal regularity of
v^{n}
, we apply (
2.5
), (
2.6
) and
(
3.39
) to obtain, for
\beta\in[0,\gamma]
and

\displaystyle\sup_{s\in[0,T]}\|F(X(s))\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H^{\ell(\gamma)})}<\infty,

(3.79)

and for
t\geq s
,

\displaystyle\|F(X(t))-F(X(s))\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H^{l(\gamma)-2})}\leq C(t-s)^{\frac{1}{8}},

(3.80)

where

l(\gamma)=\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}2,&for\;\gamma\in(\frac{d}{2},2],\\
3,&for\;\gamma\in(2,3],\\
4,&for\;\gamma\in(3,4],\end{array}\right.\;and\quad\ell(\gamma)=\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}0,&for\;\gamma\in(\frac{d}{2},2],\\
2,&for\;\gamma\in(2,4].\end{array}\right.

(3.81)

Proof of Lemma
3.7
.

The result (
3.79
) can be easily shown by using (
3.6
), (
3.9
) and (
3.65
).
Next we show (
3.80
). The case
\gamma\in(\frac{d}{2},2]
can be proved by utilizing (
3.66
), (
2.13
) and (
3.6
). For the case
\gamma\in(2,3]
, we first utilize the same arguments used in the proof of (
3.11
) to obtain

\displaystyle\|F(X(t))-F(X(s))\|_{1}\leq(1+\|X(t)\|_{\gamma}^{2}+\|X(s)\|_{\gamma}^{2})\|X(t)-X(s)\|_{1}.

(3.82)

This together with (
3.65
) and (
3.66
) shows the case
\gamma\in(2,3]
. Similarly, by employing (
3.9
) and (
3.66
), one can show the case
\gamma\in(3,4]
. Hence, the proof of this lemma is complete.
\square

4
The fully discrete approximation

In this section, we consider a full discretization of the CHC equation and show the maximal-type moment bounds of the solution to the fully discrete problem, which will be used later to the convergence analysis. Throughout the proofs,
C
denotes a generic nonnegative constant that is independent of the discretization parameters
N
and
k
and may change from lines to lines.

Let

k=T/M
,
M\in\mathbb{N}^{+}

be a time step-size and

t_{m}=mk,\,m\in\{0,1,\cdots,M\}
.
For

N\in\mathbb{N}
,
we define a finite dimensional subspace of
H
by

H_{N}:=span\{{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{e_{0}}},e_{1},e_{2},\cdots,e_{N}\}

and the projection

P_{N}:H^{\alpha}\rightarrow H_{N}

such that

P_{N}\xi=\sum_{j=0}^{N}\big<\xi,e_{j}\big>e_{j}
,
for

\xi\in H^{\alpha}
,

\alpha\in\mathbb{R}
.
It is not difficult to deduce that

\displaystyle\|(I-P_{N})\phi\|\leq C\lambda_{N}^{-\frac{\alpha}{2}}|\phi|_{\alpha},\;\forall\phi\in H^{\alpha},\;\alpha>0.

(4.1)

Then the fully discrete approximation of the problem (
1.2
) is to find
X_{m}^{M,N}\in H_{N}
such that

\displaystyle X_{m}^{M,N}-X_{m-1}^{M,N}+kA^{2}X_{m}^{M,N}+kP_{N}AF(X_{m}^{M,N})=P_{N}\Delta W_{m},\;X_{0}^{M,N}=P_{N}X_{0},\;

(4.2)

where we write
\Delta W_{m}=W(t_{m})-W(t_{m-1})
for brevity.
By introducing a family of operators
\{{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{E_{k,N}^{m}}}\}_{m=1}^{M}
:

\displaystyle E_{k,N}^{m}v=(I+kA^{2})^{-m}P_{N}v={\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{\sum_{j=0}^{N}(1+k\lambda_{j}^{2})^{-m}\big<v,e_{j}\big>e_{j},\;\forall v\in H}},

(4.3)

the solution of (
4.2
) can be written as

\displaystyle X_{m}^{M,N}=E_{k,N}^{m}P_{N}X_{0}-k\sum_{j=1}^{m}E_{k,N}^{m-j+1}P_{N}AF(X_{j}^{M,N})+\sum_{j=1}^{m}E_{k,N}^{m-j+1}P_{N}\Delta W_{j}.

(4.4)

Noting that the above implicit scheme works on the space
H_{N}
and that the mapping

A^{2}+kP_{N}AF(\cdot)
obeys a kind of monotonicity condition in the Hilbert space
\big(H_{N},(\cdot,\cdot)_{-1}\big)
,
one can see that the implicit scheme (
4.2
) is well-posed in
\big(H_{N},(\cdot,\cdot)_{-1}\big)
.

The next theorem offers a priori moment bounds for the fully discrete approximation.

Theorem 4.1

Let
k=T/M,M\in\mathbb{N}^{+}
be a time step-size and
X_{m}^{M,N}
be the solution of (
4.2
).
Suppose Assumptions

2.1
-
2.4
are valid,
then there
exists a positive constants
k_{0}
such that for all

k\leq k_{0}
,
N\in\mathbb{N}

and
\forall\,p\geq 1
,

\displaystyle\sup_{1\leq m\leq M}\|X_{m}^{M,N}\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H^{\kappa})}<\infty,\;\kappa=\min\big\{\gamma,\tfrac{d}{2}+\tfrac{1}{4}\big\}.

(4.5)

Before showing it, we need to introduce some smoothing properties of
E_{k\textsterling\textlnot N}^{m}
,
which is a variant of (
2.5
) and (
2.7
) and can be proved by a slight modification of the proof of
[
46
, Lemma 3.2]
.

Lemma 4.2

Under Assumption
2.1
, the following estimates for
E_{k}^{m}
hold.

(i) Let
\mu\in[0,2]
. There exists a constant
C
such that

\displaystyle\|A^{\mu}E_{k,N}^{m}v\|

\displaystyle\leq Ct_{m}^{-\frac{\mu}{2}}\|v\|,\quad\forall v\in H,\;m=1,2,3,\cdots,M.

(4.6)

(ii) There exists a constant
C
such that

\displaystyle\Big(k\sum_{j=1}^{m}\|AE_{k,N}^{j}v\|^{2}\Big)^{\frac{1}{2}}\leq C\|v\|,\quad\forall v\in H.

(4.7)

In addition, we need the following lemma concerning a strong moment bounds of the discrete stochastic convolution.
To arrive at it, we extend the classical factorization method used in the continuous setting
[
19
, Chapter 5]
.

Lemma 4.3

For any
\mu\in\left(\frac{d}{2},\min\{\gamma,2\}\right)
, it holds that

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\Big[\sup_{1\leq m\leq M}\Big\|\sum_{i=1}^{m}E_{k,N}^{m-i+1}P_{N}\Delta W_{i}\Big\|_{\mu}^{p}\Big]\leq C(\|A^{\frac{\gamma-2}{2}}PQ^{\frac{1}{2}}\|^{p}_{\mathcal{L}_{2}(H)}+\|(I-P)Q^{\frac{1}{2}}\|^{p}_{\mathcal{L}_{2}(H)})<\infty.

(4.8)

Proof of Lemma
4.3
.

For
i\in\{1,2,\cdots,M\}
, we define

\tilde{E}_{k,N}(t)=E_{k,N}^{i},\quad t\in(t_{i-1},t_{i}].

(4.9)

In this case
\tilde{E}_{k,N}(t)
is not continuous with respect to
t
and does not have the semigroup property.
However, we have a kind of weak semigroup property as follows:

\tilde{E}_{k,N}(t)=E_{k,N}^{i}=E_{k,N}^{j}E_{k,N}^{i-j}=E_{k,N}^{j}\tilde{E}_{k,N}(t-t_{j}),\quad 0\leq t_{j}\leq t_{i-1}<t\leq t_{i}.

(4.10)

Also, we define a continuous version of the discrete stochastic convolution as

\displaystyle\begin{split}\mathcal{O}_{k,N}(t):=\int_{0}^{t}\tilde{E}_{k,N}(t-s)\textmd{d}W(s).\end{split}

(4.11)

It is not difficult to see that
\mathcal{O}_{k,N}(t_{m})=\sum_{i=1}^{m}E_{k,N}^{m-i+1}P_{N}\Delta W_{i}
.
We shall first extend the classical factorization method in the continuous setting
[
19
, Chapter 5]
,
which is based on the following elementary identity

\displaystyle\int_{\sigma}^{t}(t-s)^{\alpha-1}(s-\sigma)^{-\alpha}\,\text{d}s=\frac{\pi}{\sin\pi\alpha},\;0\leq\sigma\leq t,\;0<\alpha<1.

(4.12)

In what follows we suppose
\alpha\in(0,\frac{1}{2})
and
p_{0}>\frac{1}{2\alpha}
.
Using the identity and the stochastic Fubini theorem we obtain

\displaystyle\mathcal{O}_{k,N}(t_{m})=

\displaystyle\frac{\sin\pi\alpha}{\pi}\int_{0}^{t_{m}}\int_{\sigma}^{t_{m}}\tilde{E}_{k,N}(t_{m}-\sigma)(t_{m}-s)^{\alpha-1}(s-\sigma)^{-\alpha}\,\text{d}s\,\text{d}W(\sigma)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{\sin\pi\alpha}{\pi}\int_{0}^{t_{m}}\int_{0}^{s}\tilde{E}_{k,N}(t_{m}-\sigma)(t_{m}-s)^{\alpha-1}(s-\sigma)^{-\alpha}\,\text{d}W(\sigma)\,\text{d}s.

(4.13)

Further, one can recall (
4.10
) and deduce that

\displaystyle\begin{split}\mathcal{O}_{k,N}(t_{m})=&\frac{\sin\pi\alpha}{\pi}\sum_{j=1}^{m}\int_{t_{j-1}}^{t_{j}}\int_{0}^{s}\tilde{E}_{k,N}(t_{m}-\sigma)(t_{m}-s)^{\alpha-1}(s-\sigma)^{-\alpha}\,\text{d}W(\sigma)\,\text{d}s\\
=&\frac{\sin\pi\alpha}{\pi}\sum_{j=1}^{m}\int_{t_{j-1}}^{t_{j}}E_{k,N}^{m-j}(t_{m}-s)^{\alpha-1}\int_{0}^{s}\tilde{E}_{k,N}(t_{j}-\sigma)(s-\sigma)^{-\alpha}\,\text{d}W(\sigma)\,\text{d}s\\
=&\frac{\sin\pi\alpha}{\pi}\sum_{j=1}^{m}\int_{t_{j-1}}^{t_{j}}E_{k,N}^{m-j}(t_{m}-s)^{\alpha-1}\Theta_{\alpha}(s)\,\text{d}s,\end{split}

(4.14)

where we denote
\lceil s\rceil=t_{j}
for
s\in(t_{j-1},t_{j}]
and

\displaystyle\Theta_{\alpha}(s):=\int_{0}^{s}\tilde{E}_{k,N}(\lceil s\rceil-\sigma)(s-\sigma)^{-\alpha}\,\text{d}W(\sigma).

(4.15)

By the Hölder inequality and the stability of
E_{k,N}
, we derive that, for
p_{0}>\frac{1}{2\alpha}
with
\alpha\in(0,\frac{1}{2})
,

\displaystyle\begin{split}\|\mathcal{O}_{k,N}(t_{m})\|_{\mu}^{2p_{0}}\leq&C\left(\sum_{j=1}^{m}\int_{t_{j-1}}^{t_{j}}\|E_{k,N}^{m-j}\|^{\frac{2p_{0}}{2p_{0}-1}}_{\mathcal{L}(H)}(t_{m}-s)^{\frac{2p_{0}(\alpha-1)}{2p_{0}-1}}\,\text{d}s\right)^{2p_{0}-1}\int_{0}^{t_{m}}\|\Theta_{\alpha}(s)\|_{\mu}^{2p_{0}}\,\text{d}s\\
\leq&C\int_{0}^{t_{m}}\|\Theta_{\alpha}(s)\|_{\mu}^{2p_{0}}\,\text{d}s.\end{split}

(4.16)

Using the Burkholder-Davis-Gundy-type inequality and (
4.6
),
we derive that, for
\mu\in\left(\frac{d}{2},\min\{\gamma,2\}\right)
,

\displaystyle\begin{split}\mathbb{E}&\Big[\sup_{1\leq m\leq M}\|\mathcal{O}_{k,N}(t_{m})\|_{\mu}^{2p_{0}}\Big]\leq C\int_{0}^{T}\mathbb{E}\left[\|\Theta_{\alpha}(s)\|_{\mu}^{2p_{0}}\right]\,\text{d}s\\
&\quad\leq C\int_{0}^{T}\left(\int_{0}^{s}(s-\sigma)^{-2\alpha}\Big(\|A^{\frac{\mu}{2}}\tilde{E}_{k,N}(\lceil s\rceil-\sigma)Q^{\frac{1}{2}}\|_{\mathcal{L}_{2}(H)}^{2}+\|(I-P)Q^{\frac{1}{2}}\|^{2}_{\mathcal{L}_{2}(H)}\Big)\,\text{d}\sigma\right)^{p_{0}}\,\text{d}s\\
&\quad\leq C\int_{0}^{T}\left(\int_{0}^{s}(s-\sigma)^{-2\alpha}\Big((s-\sigma)^{-\frac{\max\{\mu-\gamma+2,0\}}{2}}\|A^{\frac{\gamma-2}{2}}PQ^{\frac{1}{2}}\|_{\mathcal{L}_{2}(H)}^{2}+\|(I-P)Q^{\frac{1}{2}}\|^{2}_{\mathcal{L}_{2}(H)}\Big)\,\text{d}\sigma\right)^{p_{0}}\,\text{d}s\\
&\quad\leq C\int_{0}^{T}\left(\int_{0}^{s}(s-\sigma)^{-2\alpha-\frac{\max\{\mu-\gamma+2,0\}}{2}}\,\text{d}\sigma\right)^{p_{0}}\Big(\|A^{\frac{\gamma-2}{2}}PQ^{\frac{1}{2}}\|_{\mathcal{L}_{2}(H)}^{2p_{0}}+\|(I-P)Q^{\frac{1}{2}}\|^{2p_{0}}_{\mathcal{L}_{2}(H)}\Big)\,\text{d}s<\infty,\end{split}

(4.17)

where we require that
\alpha<\frac{1}{2}-\frac{\max\{\mu-\gamma+2,0\}}{4}
.
\square

At the moment, we are ready to prove Theorem
4.1
.

Proof of Theorem
4.1
.

We begin with showing the momet boundedness of

Z_{m}^{M,N}:=E_{k,N}^{m}{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{P_{N}}}(I-P)X_{0}+\sum_{i=1}^{m}E_{k,N}^{m-i+1}P_{N}\Delta W_{i}.

Thanks to Lemma
4.3
and applying (
4.6
) with
\mu=0
give

\displaystyle\begin{split}\mathbb{E}\Big[\sup_{1\leq m\leq M}\|Z_{m}^{M,N}\|_{\gamma}^{p}\Big]\leq&C\mathbb{E}\Big[\sup_{1\leq m\leq M}\|E_{k,N}^{m}{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{P_{N}}}(I-P)X_{0}\|_{\gamma}^{p}\Big]+\mathbb{E}\Big[\sup_{1\leq m\leq M}\Big\|\sum_{i=1}^{m}E_{k,N}^{m-i+1}P_{N}\Delta W_{i}\Big\|_{\gamma}^{p}\Big]\\
\leq&C\mathbb{E}\big[\|X_{0}\|_{\gamma}^{p}\big]+C(\|A^{\frac{\gamma-2}{2}}PQ^{\frac{1}{2}}\|^{p}_{\mathcal{L}_{2}(H)}+\|(I-P)Q^{\frac{1}{2}}\|^{p}_{\mathcal{L}_{2}(H)})<\infty,\end{split}

(4.18)

Similarly to the continuous case, we only need to consider the spatial regularity of the solution to the following problem

\displaystyle Y_{m}^{M,N}:=X_{m}^{M,N}-{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Z_{m}^{M,N}}}=E_{k,N}^{m}PX_{0}-k\sum_{i=1}^{m}E_{k,N}^{m+1-i}AF(Y_{i}^{M,N}+{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Z_{i}^{M,N}}}).

(4.19)

It is straightforward to verify that
Y_{m}^{M,N}
for
m\in\{1,2,\cdots,M\}
satisfies

\displaystyle\frac{Y_{m}^{M,N}-Y_{m-1}^{M,N}}{k}+A^{2}Y_{m}^{M,N}=-P_{N}APF(Y_{m}^{M,N}+{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Z_{m}^{M,N}}}),\;Y_{0}^{M,N}=P_{N}{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{P}}X_{0}.

(4.20)

Multiplying both sides of (
4.20
) by
A^{-1}Y_{m}^{M,N}
and applying the same arguments used in the proof of (
3.24
), we have

\displaystyle\begin{split}|Y_{m}^{M,N}|_{-1}^{2}\leq&|Y_{m-1}^{M,N}|_{-1}^{2}-2k|Y_{m}^{M,N}|_{1}^{2}-2k\big<F(Y_{m}^{M,N}+{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Z_{m}^{M,N})}},Y_{m}^{M,N}\big>\\
=&|Y_{m-1}^{M,N}|_{-1}^{2}-2k|Y_{m}^{M,N}|_{1}^{2}-2k\big<(Y_{m}^{M,N})^{3}-Y_{m}^{M,N},Y_{m}^{M,N}\big>\\
&-2k\big<{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{3(Y_{m}^{M,N})^{2}Z_{m}^{M,N}+3Y_{m}^{M,N}(Z_{m}^{M,N})^{2}+(Z_{m}^{M,N})^{3}-Z_{m}^{M,N}}},Y_{m}^{M,N}\big>\\
\leq&|Y_{m-1}^{M,N}|_{-1}^{2}-2k|Y_{m}^{M,N}|_{1}^{2}-2k\|Y_{m}^{M,N}\|_{L_{4}}^{4}+2k\|Y_{m}^{M,N}\|^{2}\\
&+2k\Big(3\|Y_{m}^{M,N}\|_{L^{4}}^{3}\|Z_{m}^{M,N}\|_{L^{4}}+3\|Y_{m}^{M,N}\|^{2}_{L^{4}}\|Z_{m}^{M,N}\|^{2}_{L^{4}}\\
&+\|Y_{m}^{M,N}\|_{L^{4}}\|Z_{m}^{M,N}\|^{3}_{L^{4}}+\|Y_{m}^{M,N}\|_{L^{2}}\|Z_{m}^{M,N}\|_{L^{2}}\Big)\\
\leq&|Y_{m-1}^{M,N}|_{-1}^{2}-2k|Y_{m}^{M,N}|_{1}^{2}-k\|Y_{m}^{M,N}\|_{L^{4}}^{4}+Ck(1+\|{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Z_{m}^{M,N}}}\|_{L^{4}}^{4}).\end{split}

(4.21)

After repeated application, this yields

\displaystyle|Y_{m}^{M,N}|_{-1}^{2}+2\sum_{j=1}^{m}k|Y_{j}^{M,N}|_{1}^{2}+\sum_{j=1}^{m}k\|Y_{j}^{M,N}\|_{L^{4}}^{4}\leq C+\|X_{0}\|_{-1}^{2}+Ck\sum_{j=1}^{m}\|{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Z_{j}^{M,N}}}\|_{L^{4}}^{4},

(4.22)

which together with (
4.18
) yields

\displaystyle\begin{split}\mathbb{E}\Big[\Big(k\sum_{j=1}^{M}|Y_{j}^{M,N}|_{1}^{2}\Big)^{p}\Big]+\mathbb{E}\Big[\Big(k\sum_{j=1}^{M}\|Y_{j}^{M,N}\|_{L^{4}}^{4}\Big)^{p}\Big]\leq&C(T,p,X_{0})\big(1+\mathbb{E}\Big[\Big(k\sum_{j=1}^{M}\|{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Z_{j}^{M,N}}}\|_{L^{4}}^{4}\Big)^{p}\Big]\big)\\
\leq&C(T,p,X_{0})\Big(1+k\sum_{j=1}^{M}\mathbb{E}\big[\|{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Z_{j}^{M,N}}}\|_{\kappa}^{4p}\big]\Big)<\infty.\end{split}

(4.23)

Next we consider the bound of
\|Y_{m}^{M,N}\|
.
By taking the inner product on both sides of (
4.20
)
with
Y_{m}^{M,N}
and using integration by parts formula,
we have

\begin{split}\|Y_{m}^{M,N}\|^{2}\leq&\|Y_{m-1}^{M,N}\|^{2}-2k\|AY_{m}^{M,N}\|^{2}-2k\big<P_{N}AF(Y_{m}^{M,N}+{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Z_{m}^{M,N}}}),Y_{m}^{M,N}\big>\\
\leq&\|Y_{m-1}^{M,N}\|^{2}-2k\|AY_{m}^{M,N}\|^{2}-\tfrac{3}{2}k\|\nabla(Y_{m}^{M,N})^{2}\|^{2}+2k\|\nabla Y_{m}^{M,N}\|^{2}\\
&-2k\big<{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{3(Y_{m}^{M,N})^{2}Z_{m}^{M,N}+3Y_{m}^{M,N}(Z_{m}^{M,N})^{2}+(Z_{m}^{M,N})^{3}-Z_{m}^{M,N}}},AY_{m}^{M,N}\big>\\
\leq&\|Y_{m-1}^{M,N}\|^{2}-k\|AY_{m}^{M,N}\|^{2}-\tfrac{3}{2}k\|\nabla(Y_{m}^{M,N})^{2}\|^{2}+2k\|\nabla Y_{m}^{M,N}\|^{2}\\
&+Ck(\|Y_{m}^{M,N}\|_{L^{4}}^{4}\|Z_{m}^{M,N}\|_{V}^{2}+\|Y_{m}^{M,N}\|^{2}\|{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Z_{m}^{M,N}}}\|_{V}^{4}+\|{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Z_{m}^{M,N}}}\|_{L^{6}}^{6}+\|{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Z_{m}^{M,N}}}\|^{2}).\end{split}

(4.24)

By summation on
m
and using (
4.22
), (
3.6
) and the fact
Y_{0}^{M,N}=P_{N}PX_{0}
, we deduce that for
\kappa=\min\{\gamma,\frac{d}{2}+\frac{1}{4}\}
,

\displaystyle\begin{split}\|Y_{m}^{M,N}\|^{2}&+\sum_{j=1}^{m}k\|AY_{j}^{M,N}\|^{2}+\frac{3}{2}\sum_{j=1}^{m}k\|\nabla(Y_{j}^{M,N})^{2}\|^{2}\\
\leq&\|X_{0}\|^{2}+2k\sum_{j=1}^{m}|Y_{j}^{M,N}|_{1}^{2}+C\Big(1+\sup_{1\leq j\leq M}\|{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Z_{j}^{M,N}}}\|_{V}^{6}\Big)\big(1+\sum_{j=1}^{m}k\|Y_{j}^{M,N}\|_{L^{4}}^{4}\big)\\
\leq&C\Big(1+\sup_{1\leq j\leq M}\|{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Z_{j}^{M,N}}}\|_{\kappa}^{6}\Big)\Big(1+\sum_{j=1}^{m}k\|Y_{j}^{M,N}\|_{L^{4}}^{4}\Big).\end{split}

(4.25)

Therefore, by using (
4.18
) and (
4.23
), it enables us to obtain

\displaystyle\Big(\mathbb{E}\big[\sup_{1\leq m\leq M}\|Y_{m}^{M,N}\|^{2p}\big]\Big)^{\frac{1}{p}}+\Big\|k\sum_{j=1}^{M}\|AY_{j}^{M,N}\|^{2}\Big\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;\mathbb{R})}+\Big\|k\sum_{j=1}^{M}\|\nabla(Y_{j}^{M,N})^{2}\|^{2}\Big\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;\mathbb{R})}<\infty.

(4.26)

Now we focus on the boundedness of
Y_{m}^{M,N}
in the norm
L^{6}
. Similarly to the continuous case, we also consider two cases: either
d=1
or
d=2,3
. For
d=1
, we follow the similar skill used in the proof of
[
17
, Lemma 4.4]
to derive

\displaystyle\begin{split}\mathbb{E}\Big[\sup_{1\leq m\leq M}\|Y_{m}^{M,N}\|^{p}_{L^{6}}\Big]\leq C(p,T,\gamma,X_{0}).\end{split}

(4.27)

For
d=2,3
, we introduce the difference operator
\overline{\partial}Y_{m}^{M,N}:=\frac{Y_{m}^{M,N}-Y^{M,N}_{m-1}}{k}
and multiply (
4.20
) by
A^{-1}\overline{\partial}Y_{m}^{M,N}
to obtain

\displaystyle\begin{split}\big|\overline{\partial}Y_{m}^{M,N}\big|_{-1}^{2}&+\big<AY_{m}^{M,N},\overline{\partial}Y_{m}^{M,N}\big>+\big<F(Y_{m}^{M,N}),\overline{\partial}Y_{m}^{M,N}\big>\\
&=-\big<{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{3(Y_{m}^{M,N})^{2}Z_{m}^{M,N}+3Y_{m}^{M,N}(Z_{m}^{M,N})^{2}+(Z_{m}^{M,N})^{3}-Z_{m}^{M,N}}},\overline{\partial}Y_{m}^{M,N}\big>.\end{split}

(4.28)

Additionally, by using (
3.32
),
one can find that

\displaystyle J(Y_{m}^{M,N})-J(Y_{m-1}^{M,N})=\big<AY_{m}^{M,N},\overline{\partial}Y_{m}^{M,N}\big>+\big<F(Y_{m}^{M,N}),\overline{\partial}Y_{m}^{M,N}\big>+\big<Y_{m}^{M,N}-Y_{m-1}^{M,N},\overline{\partial}Y_{m}^{M,N}\big>.

(4.29)

Then, the above estimates together with the fact
\overline{\partial}Y_{m}^{M,N}\in\dot{H}
yield

\displaystyle\begin{split}&k\big|\overline{\partial}Y_{m}^{M,N}\big|_{-1}^{2}+J(Y_{m}^{M,N})-J(Y_{m-1}^{M,N})\\
&=-k\big<{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{3(Y_{m}^{M,N})^{2}Z_{m}^{M,N}+3Y_{m}^{M,N}(Z_{m}^{M,N})^{2}+(Z_{m}^{M,N})^{3}-Z_{m}^{M,N}}},\overline{\partial}Y_{m}^{M,N}\big>+k\big<Y_{m}^{M,N}-Y_{m-1}^{M,N},\overline{\partial}Y_{m}^{M,N}\big>\\
&\leq k|{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{P\big(3(Y_{m}^{M,N})^{2}Z_{m}^{M,N}+3Y_{m}^{M,N}(Z_{m}^{M,N})^{2}+(Z_{m}^{M,N})^{3}-Z_{m}^{M,N}}}\big)|_{1}^{2}+k|Y_{m}^{M,N}-Y_{m-1}^{M,N}|_{1}^{2}+\frac{k}{2}|\overline{\partial}Y_{m}^{M,N}|_{-1}^{2}\\
&\leq C(\gamma)(1+k\|(Y_{m}^{M,N})^{2}\|_{1}^{2}+k\|AY_{m}^{M,N}\|^{2})(1+\|Z_{m}^{M,N}\|^{3}_{\gamma})^{2}+2k(|Y_{m}^{M,N}|_{1}^{2}+|Y_{m-1}^{M,N}|_{1}^{2})+\frac{k}{2}|\overline{\partial}Y_{m}^{M,N}|_{-1}^{2},\end{split}

(4.30)

where in the last inequality we applied the similar arguments used in (
3.35
).
Then summation on
m
and applying (
4.23
) and (
4.26
) lead to

\displaystyle\begin{split}\mathbb{E}\Big[\sup_{1\leq m\leq M}\big(J(Y_{m}^{M,N})\big)^{p}\Big]&\leq C(\gamma,T)\Big(1+\mathbb{E}\Big[\Big(k\sum_{j=1}^{M}\|(Y_{j}^{M,N})^{2}\|_{1}^{2}\Big)^{2p}\\
&\quad+\Big(k\sum_{j=1}^{M}\|AY_{j}^{M,N}\|^{2}\Big)^{2p}\Big]\Big)^{\tfrac{1}{2}}\Big(1+\mathbb{E}\big[\big(\sup_{1\leq j\leq M}\|{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Z_{j}^{M,N}}}\|^{12p}_{\gamma}\big)\big]\Big)^{\tfrac{1}{2}}\\
&\quad+\mathbb{E}\Big[k\sum_{j=1}^{M}\|AY_{j}^{M,N}\|^{2}\Big]^{p}+\mathbb{E}\left[\left(J(P_{N}PX_{0})\right)^{p}\right]+C\mathbb{E}\left[|P_{N}PX_{0}|_{1}^{2p}\right]\\
&\leq C(\gamma,p,T).\end{split}

(4.31)

The above estimate together with (
4.26
) and the fact
|Y_{m}^{M,N}|_{1}^{2}{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{-2\|Y_{m}^{M,N}\|^{2}}}\leq 2J(Y_{m}^{M,N})
yields

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\Big[\sup_{1\leq m\leq M}|Y_{m}^{M,N}|_{1}^{2p}\Big]<\infty,

(4.32)

which in combination with (
4.27
) and the fact
Y_{m}^{M,N}\in\dot{H}
arrives at, for
d=1,2,3
,

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\Big[\sup_{1\leq m\leq M}\|Y_{m}^{M,N}\|_{L^{6}}^{p}\Big]<\infty.

(4.33)

Therefore,
by (
4.6
) and (
4.18
), we have, for
\kappa=\min\{\gamma,\frac{d}{2}+\frac{1}{4}\}
,

\displaystyle\begin{split}\|Y_{m}^{M,N}\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H^{\kappa})}\leq&\|E_{k,N}^{m}PX_{0}\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H^{\kappa})}+\big\|\sum_{i=1}^{m}kE_{k,N}^{m-i+1}APF(Y_{i}^{M,N}+{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Z_{i}^{M,N}}})\big\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H^{\kappa})}\\
\leq&\|X_{0}\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H^{\kappa})}+\sum_{i=1}^{m}kt_{m-i+1}^{-\frac{2+\kappa}{4}}\|F(Y_{i}^{M,N}+{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Z_{i}^{M,N}}})\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H)}\\
\leq&\|X_{0}\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H^{\gamma})}+\sum_{i=1}^{m}kt_{m-i+1}^{-\frac{2+\kappa}{4}}\sup_{1\leq i\leq M}\big(\|Y_{i}^{M,N}\|^{3}_{L^{3p}(\Omega;L^{6})}+\|{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Z_{i}^{M,N}}}\|^{3}_{L^{3p}(\Omega;L^{6})}+1\big)\\
\leq&C(p,T,\gamma,X_{0}).\end{split}

(4.34)

Hence this finishes the proof of this theorem.
\square

5
Strong convergence rates of the full discretization

In this part, we follow the approach in
[
46
]
to derive the error estimates of the fully discrete approximation of the stochastic problem (
1.2
).
The convergence analysis heavily relies on the moment bound obtained in section 4 and the corresponding deterministic error estimates.

The next theorem states the main result of this paper, concerning strong convergence rates of the full discretizaiton scheme.

Theorem 5.1

Let
X(t)
be the mild solution of (
1.2
) and
X_{m}^{M,N}
be the solution of (
4.2
). Suppose
Assumptions
2.1
-
2.4
are valid, then
there exist two positive constants
C
and
k_{0}
such that for all
k\leq k_{0}
and
\forall p\geq 1
,

\displaystyle\sup_{1\leq m\leq M}\|X(t_{m})-X_{m}^{M,N}\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H)}\leq C(\lambda_{N}^{-\frac{\gamma}{2}}+k^{\frac{\gamma}{4}}).

(5.1)

To show this theorem, we introduce an auxiliary problem,

\displaystyle\widetilde{X}_{m}^{M,N}-\widetilde{X}_{m-1}^{M,N}+kA^{2}\widetilde{X}_{m}^{M,N}+kP_{N}AF(X(t_{m}))=P_{N}\Delta W_{m},\;X_{0}^{M,N}={\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{P_{N}}}X_{0},

(5.2)

whose solution can be recasted as

\displaystyle{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{\widetilde{X}_{m}^{M,N}={\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{E_{k,N}^{m}P_{N}}}X_{0}-k\sum_{j=1}^{m}E_{k,N}^{m-j+1}APF(X(t_{j}))+Z_{m}^{M,N}}}.

(5.3)

Owning to (
4.6
), (
4.7
) and (
4.18
), one can derive that, for
\kappa=\min\{\gamma,\frac{d}{2}+\frac{1}{4}\}
and any
m\in\{1,2,\cdots,M\}
,

\displaystyle\begin{split}\|\widetilde{X}_{m}^{M,N}\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H^{\kappa})}\leq&\|{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{E_{k,N}^{m}P_{N}X_{0}}}\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H^{\kappa})}+k\sum_{j=1}^{m}\|E_{k,N}^{m-j+1}APF(X(t_{j}))\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H^{\kappa})}+\|{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Z_{m}^{M,N}}}\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H^{\kappa})}\\
\leq&C\|X_{0}\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H^{\kappa})}+Ck\sum_{j=1}^{m}t_{m-j+1}^{-\frac{2+\kappa}{4}}\|F(X(t_{j}))\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H)}+C\|A^{\frac{\gamma-2}{2}}Q^{\frac{1}{2}}\|_{\widetilde{\mathcal{L}}_{2}(H)}\\
\leq&C\big(1+\|X_{0}\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H^{\gamma})}+k\sum_{j=1}^{m}t_{m-j+1}^{-\frac{2+\kappa}{4}}\sup_{s\in[0,T]}\|F(X(s))\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H)}\big)<\infty.\end{split}

(5.4)

Hence, we can decompose the considered error
\|X(t_{m})-X_{m}^{M,N}\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H)}
into two parts:

\displaystyle\|X(t_{m})-X_{m}^{M,N}\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H)}\leq\|X(t_{m})-\widetilde{X}_{m}^{M,N}\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H)}+\|\widetilde{X}_{m}^{M,N}-X_{m}^{M,N}\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H)}.

(5.5)

These two error terms are separately handled in the forthcoming two subsections.
Plugging (
5.28
) and (
5.28
)
into (
5.5
) gives the desired assertion (
5.1
).

5.1
Error estimates for
\|X(t_{m})-\widetilde{X}_{m}^{M,N}\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H)}

In what follows, we use the corresponding deterministic error estimates to bound
\|X(t_{m})-\widetilde{X}_{m}^{M,N}\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;\dot{H})}

in the semigroup framework, which is usually applied in the error analysis of numerical approximation of SPDEs
with globally Lipschitz coefficients.

Lemma 5.2

Let
X(t)
be the mild solution of (
1.2
) and
\widetilde{X}_{m}^{M,N}
be the solution of (
5.2
). Suppose
Assumptions
2.1
-
2.4
are valid,
then there exist two positive constants
C
and
k_{0}
such that for all
k\leq k_{0}
and
\forall p\geq 1
,

\displaystyle\|X(t_{m})-\widetilde{X}_{m}^{M,N}\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H)}\leq C(\lambda_{N}^{-\frac{\gamma}{2}}+k^{\frac{\gamma}{4}}).

(5.6)

Its proof is given below.
First, we define the fully discrete approximation operator

\displaystyle\Psi_{k}^{M,N}(t):=E(t)-E_{k,N}^{m},\;\;\;t\in[t_{m-1},t_{m}),\;m\in\{1,2,\cdots,M\}.

(5.7)

In the following lemma, we give some results on the error operator
\Psi_{k}^{M,N}(t)
, which are crucial in the error estimates of the fully discrete approximation and can be shown by a slight
modification of the proof of
[
46
, Lemma 6.2]
.

Lemma 5.3

Under Assumption
2.1
, the following estimates for
\Psi^{M,N}_{k}(t)
hold for
t\in[0,T]
.

(i) Let
\beta\in[0,4]
. There exists a constant
C
such that, for
t>0
,

\displaystyle\|\Psi_{k}^{M,N}(t)v\|

\displaystyle\leq C(\lambda_{N}^{-\frac{\beta}{2}}+k^{\frac{\beta}{4}})|v|_{\beta},\quad\forall x\in{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{H^{\beta}}}.

(5.8)

(ii) Let
\alpha\in[0,2]
. There exists a constant
C
such that, for
t>0
,

\displaystyle\|\Psi_{k}^{M,N}(t)v\|

\displaystyle\leq C(\lambda_{N}^{-\frac{4-\alpha}{2}}+k^{\frac{4-\alpha}{4}})t^{-1}|v|_{-\alpha},\quad\forall v\in{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{H^{-\alpha}}}.

(5.9)

(iii)
Let
\nu\in[0,4]
. There exists a constant
C
such that, for
t>0
,

\displaystyle\Big(\int_{0}^{t}\|\Psi_{k}^{M,N}(s)v\|^{2}\,\mathrm{d}s\Big)^{\frac{1}{2}}\leq C(\lambda_{N}^{-\frac{\nu}{2}}+k^{\frac{\nu}{4}})|v|_{\nu-2},\quad v\in{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{H^{\nu-2}}}.

(5.10)

(v)
Let
\varrho\in[0,2]
. There exists a constant
C
such that, for
t>0
,

\displaystyle\Big\|\int_{0}^{t}\Psi_{k}^{M,N}(s)v\,\mathrm{d}s\Big\|\leq C(\lambda_{N}^{-\frac{4-\varrho}{2}}+k^{\frac{4-\varrho}{4}})|v|_{-\varrho},\quad v\in{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{H^{-\varrho}}}.

(5.11)

Subsequently, we are well-prepared to show Lemma
5.2
.

Proof of Lemma
5.2
.

Subtracting (
5.3
) from
(
3.64
), the error
X(t_{m})-\widetilde{X}_{m}^{M,N}
reads

\displaystyle\begin{split}\|X(t_{m})-&\widetilde{X}_{m}^{M,N}\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H)}=\|(E(t_{m})-E_{k,N}^{m})X_{0}\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H)}\\
&+\Big\|\int_{0}^{t_{m}}E(t_{m}-s)APF(X(s))\,\text{d}s-k\sum_{j=1}^{m}E_{k,N}^{m-j+1}APF(X(t_{j}))\Big\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H)}\\
&+\Big\|\sum_{j=1}^{m}\int_{t_{j-1}}^{t_{j}}\big(E(t_{m}-s)-E_{k,N}^{m-j+1}\big)\,\text{d}W(s)\Big\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H)}\\
=:&I+J+K.\end{split}

(5.12)

Subsequently,
I,J,K
will be treated separately.
For the first term
I
, we employ (
5.8
) with
\beta=\gamma
to obtain

\displaystyle I\leq C(\lambda_{N}^{-\frac{\gamma}{2}}+k^{\frac{\gamma}{4}})\|X_{0}\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H^{\gamma})}.

(5.13)

To handle
J
, we decompose it into three terms as follows

\displaystyle\begin{split}J\leq&\Big\|\sum_{j=1}^{m}\int_{t_{j-1}}^{t_{j}}E(t_{m}-s)AP\big(F(X(s))-F(X(t_{j}))\big)\,\text{d}s\Big\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H)}\\
&+\Big\|\int_{0}^{t_{m}}\Psi_{k}^{M,N}(t_{m}-s)APF(X(t_{m}))\,\text{d}s\Big\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H)}\\
&+\sum_{j=1}^{m}\int_{t_{j-1}}^{t_{j}}\|\Psi_{k}^{M,N}(t_{m}-s)AP\big(F(X(t_{j}))-F(X(t_{m}))\big)\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H)}\,\text{d}s\\
=:&J_{1}+J_{2}+J_{3}.\end{split}

(5.14)

Next we treat the above three terms separately.
To deal with
J_{1}
, we first note that, for
s\in[t_{j-1},t_{j})
,

\displaystyle X(t_{j})=E(t_{j}-s)X(s)-\int_{s}^{t_{j}}E(t_{j}-\sigma)AF(X(\sigma))\,\text{d}\sigma+\int_{s}^{t_{j}}E(t_{j}-\sigma)\,\text{d}W(\sigma),

(5.15)

and then apply Taylor’s formula to split
J_{1}
into four terms:

\displaystyle\begin{split}J_{1}\leq&\Big\|\sum_{j=1}^{m}\int_{t_{j-1}}^{t_{j}}E(t_{m}-s)APF^{\prime}(X(s))(E(t_{j}-s)-I)X(s)\,\text{d}s\Big\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H)}\\
&+\Big\|\sum_{j=1}^{m}\int_{t_{j-1}}^{t_{j}}E(t_{m}-s)APF^{\prime}(X(s))\int_{s}^{t_{j}}E(t_{j}-\sigma)AF(X(\sigma))\,\text{d}\sigma\,\text{d}s\Big\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H)}\\
&+\Big\|\sum_{j=1}^{m}\int_{t_{j-1}}^{t_{j}}E(t_{m}-s)APF^{\prime}(X(s))\int_{s}^{t_{j}}E(t_{j}-\sigma)\,\text{d}W(\sigma)\,\text{d}s\Big\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H)}\\
&+\Big\|\sum_{j=1}^{m}\int_{t_{j-1}}^{t_{j}}E(t_{m}-s)APR_{F}(X(s),X(t_{j}))\,\text{d}s\Big\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H)}\\
=:&J_{11}+J_{12}+J_{13}+J_{14},\end{split}

(5.16)

where
R_{F}(X(s),X(t_{j}))
is a remainder term, given by

R_{F}(X(s),X(t_{j}))=\int_{0}^{1}F^{\prime\prime}\big(X(s)+\lambda(X(t_{j})-X(s))\big)(X(t_{j})-X(s),X(t_{j})-X(s))(1-\lambda)\,\text{d}\lambda.

Owing to (
3.65
), (
3.8
) and (
2.5
) with
\mu=\frac{2+\delta_{0}}{2}
,
we deduce, for any fixed
\delta_{0}\in(\frac{3}{2},2)
and
\gamma\in(\frac{d}{2},4]
,

\displaystyle\begin{split}J_{11}&\leq C\sum_{j=1}^{m}\int_{t_{j-1}}^{t_{j}}(t_{m}-s)^{-\frac{2+\delta_{0}}{4}}\|A^{-\frac{\delta_{0}}{2}}PF^{\prime}(X(s))(E(t_{j}-s)-I)X(s)\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H)}\,\text{d}s\\
&\leq C\sum_{j=1}^{m}\int_{t_{j-1}}^{t_{j}}(t_{m}-s)^{-\frac{2+\delta_{0}}{4}}\|F^{\prime}(X(s))(E(t_{j}-s)-I)X(s)\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;L^{1})}\,\text{d}s\\
&\leq C\sum_{j=1}^{m}\int_{t_{j-1}}^{t_{j}}(t_{m}-s)^{-\frac{2+\delta_{0}}{4}}\Big(1+\|X(s)\|^{2}_{L^{4p}(\Omega;L^{4})}\Big)\Big\|(E(t_{j}-s)-I)X(s)\Big\|_{L^{2p}(\Omega;H)}\,\text{d}s\\
&\leq Ck^{\frac{\gamma}{4}}\int_{0}^{t_{m}}(t_{m}-s)^{-\frac{2+\delta_{0}}{4}}\,\text{d}s\Big(1+\sup_{s\in[0,T]}\|X(s)\|^{3}_{L^{4p}(\Omega;H^{\gamma})}\Big)\\
&\leq Ck^{\frac{\gamma}{4}}.\end{split}

(5.17)

Using the similar approach as used in the proof of (
5.17
), with (
3.79
) used instead we obtain,

\displaystyle\begin{split}J_{12}&\leq C\sum_{j=1}^{m}\int_{t_{j-1}}^{t_{j}}\int_{s}^{t_{j}}(t_{m}-s)^{-\frac{2+\delta_{0}}{4}}\big\|F^{\prime}(X(s))E(t_{j}-\sigma)AF(X(\sigma))\big\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;L^{1})}\,\text{d}\sigma\,\text{d}s\\
&\leq C\sum_{j=1}^{m}\int_{t_{j-1}}^{t_{j}}\int_{s}^{t_{j}}(t_{m}-s)^{-\frac{2+\delta_{0}}{4}}(t_{j}-\sigma)^{-\frac{2-{\ell(\gamma)}}{4}}(1+\|X(s)\|^{2}_{L^{4p}(\Omega;L^{4})})\|F(X(\sigma))\|_{L^{2p}(\Omega;H^{\ell(\gamma)})}\,\text{d}\sigma\,\text{d}s\\
&\leq Ck^{\frac{2+\ell(\gamma)}{4}}\int_{0}^{t_{m}}(t_{m}-s)^{-\frac{2+\delta_{0}}{4}}\text{d}s\Big(1+\sup_{s\in[0,T]}\|X(s)\|^{2}_{L^{4p}(\Omega;H^{\gamma})}\Big)\sup_{s\in[0,T]}\|F(X(s))\|_{L^{2p}(\Omega;H^{\ell(\gamma)})}\\
&\leq Ck^{\frac{\gamma}{4}},\end{split}

(5.18)

where

\ell(\gamma)
is a piecewise function defined by
\ell(\gamma)=0\text{ for }\gamma\in(\frac{d}{2},2]\text{ and }\ell(\gamma)=2\text{ for }\gamma\in(2,4].

To bound
J_{13}
, we introduce an indicator function defined by

\chi_{[s,t_{j})}(t)=1\text{ for }t\in[s,t_{j})\text{ and }\chi_{[s,t_{j})}(t)=0\text{ for }t\not\in[s,t_{j})
and then employ the stochastic Fubini theorem (see
[
19
, Theorem 4.18]
) and the Burkholder-Davis-Gundy-type inequality to obtain

\displaystyle\begin{split}J_{13}=&\Big\|\sum_{j=1}^{m}\int_{t_{j-1}}^{t_{j}}\int_{t_{j-1}}^{t_{j}}\chi_{[s,t_{j})}(\sigma)E(t_{m}-s)APF^{\prime}(X(s))E(t_{j}-\sigma)\,\text{d}W(\sigma)\,\text{d}s\Big\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H)}\\
=&\Big\|\sum_{j=1}^{m}\int_{t_{j-1}}^{t_{j}}\int_{t_{j-1}}^{t_{j}}\chi_{[s,t_{j})}(\sigma)E(t_{m}-s)APF^{\prime}(X(s))E(t_{j}-\sigma)\,\text{d}s\text{d}W(\sigma)\Big\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H)}\\
\leq&\Big(\sum_{j=1}^{m}\int_{t_{j-1}}^{t_{j}}\Big\|\int_{t_{j-1}}^{t_{j}}\chi_{[s,t_{j})}(\sigma)E(t_{m}-s)APF^{\prime}(X(s))E(t_{j}-\sigma)Q^{\frac{1}{2}}\,\text{d}s\Big\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;\mathcal{L}_{2}(H))}^{2}\,\text{d}\sigma\Big)^{\frac{1}{2}}.\end{split}

(5.19)

Further, employing (
3.65
), (
3.11
), (
3.10
) with
\iota=\kappa=\min\{\gamma,\frac{d}{2}+\frac{1}{4}\}
, (
2.5
) with
\mu=\frac{2+\eta}{2}
and (
2.7
) with
\varrho=\frac{\max\{3-\gamma,0\}}{2}
and using the Hölder inequality, one can find that

\displaystyle\begin{split}J_{13}\leq&Ck^{\frac{1}{2}}\Big(\sum_{j=1}^{m}\int_{t_{j-1}}^{t_{j}}\int_{t_{j-1}}^{t_{j}}\sum_{l=1}^{\infty}\|E(t_{m}-s)APF^{\prime}(X(s))E(t_{j}-\sigma)Q^{\frac{1}{2}}\eta_{l}\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H)}^{2}\,\text{d}s\,\text{d}\sigma\Big)^{\frac{1}{2}}\\
\leq&Ck^{\frac{1}{2}}\Big(\sum_{j=1}^{m}\int_{t_{j-1}}^{t_{j}}\int_{t_{j-1}}^{t_{j}}(t_{m}-s)^{-\frac{2-\eta}{2}}\sum_{l=1}^{\infty}{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{\|F^{\prime}(X(s))E(t_{j}-\sigma)Q^{\frac{1}{2}}\eta_{l}\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H^{\eta})}^{2}}}\,\text{d}s\text{d}\sigma\Big)^{\frac{1}{2}}\\
\leq&Ck^{\frac{1}{2}}\Big(\sum_{j=1}^{m}\int_{t_{j-1}}^{t_{j}}(t_{m}-s)^{-\frac{2-\eta}{2}}\Big(1+\|X(s)\|^{4}_{L^{2p}(\Omega;H^{\kappa})}\Big)\,\text{d}s\int_{t_{j-1}}^{t_{j}}\sum_{l=1}^{\infty}{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{\|E(t_{j}-\sigma)Q^{\frac{1}{2}}\eta_{l}\|_{1}^{2}}}\text{d}\sigma\Big)^{\frac{1}{2}}\\
\leq&{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Ck^{\frac{1}{2}}\Big(\int_{0}^{t_{m}}(t_{m}-s)^{-\frac{2-\eta}{2}}\,\text{d}s\Big)^{\frac{1}{2}}\Big(1+\sup_{s\in[0,T]}\|X(s)\|^{4}_{L^{2p}(\Omega;H^{\gamma})}\Big)^{\frac{1}{2}}}}\\
&\qquad\qquad{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{\sup_{j\in\{1,2,\cdots,M\}}\Big(\sum_{l=1}^{\infty}\int_{t_{j-1}}^{t_{j}}\big(\|A^{\frac{1}{2}}E(t_{j}-\sigma)PQ^{\frac{1}{2}}\eta_{l}\|^{2}+\big<Q^{\frac{1}{2}}\eta_{l},e_{0}\big>^{2}\big)\,\text{d}\sigma\Big)^{\frac{1}{2}}}}\\
\leq&{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Ck^{\frac{4-\max\{3-\gamma,0\}}{4}}\big(\sum_{l=1}^{\infty}\|A^{\frac{\gamma-2}{2}}PQ^{\frac{1}{2}}\eta_{l}\|^{2}+\big<Q^{\frac{1}{2}}\eta_{l},e_{0}\big>^{2}\big)^{\frac{1}{2}}}}\\
\leq&{\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{Ck^{\frac{\gamma}{4}}(\|A^{\frac{\gamma-2}{2}}PQ^{\frac{1}{2}}\|_{\mathcal{L}_{2}(H)}+\|(I-P)Q^{\frac{1}{2}}\|_{\mathcal{L}_{2}(H)}),}}\end{split}

(5.20)

where
\eta:=\min\{\gamma,\frac{2}{3}\}
, and
\{\eta_{l}\}_{l\in\mathbb{N}^{+}}
is the eigenfunction of
Q
.
Now we are in the position to handle the term
J_{14}
.
It follows from (
3.66
) and (
3.65
) that

\displaystyle\begin{split}J_{14}&\leq C\sum_{j=1}^{m}\int_{t_{j-1}}^{t_{j}}\!\!(t_{m}-s)^{-\frac{2+\delta_{0}}{4}}\\
&\qquad\Big\|\int_{0}^{1}\!\!F^{\prime\prime}\big(X(s)+\lambda(X(t_{j})-X(s))\big)(X(t_{j})-X(s),X(t_{j})-X(s))\,\text{d}\lambda\Big\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;L^{1})}\,\text{d}s\\

(5.21)

where in the first inequality we used similar arguments as in (
5.17
). Thus, putting the above four estimates together results in, for

\displaystyle J_{1}\leq Ck^{\frac{\gamma}{4}}.

(5.22)

Concerning the term
J_{2}
, by applying (
3.79
) and (
5.11
) with
\varrho=2-\ell(\gamma)
, one can observe that

\displaystyle\begin{split}J_{2}&\leq C(\lambda_{N}^{-\frac{2+\ell(\gamma)}{2}}+k^{\frac{2+\ell(\gamma)}{4}})\|F(X(t_{m}))\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H^{\ell(\gamma)})}\\
&\leq C(\lambda_{N}^{-\frac{2+\ell(\gamma)}{2}}+k^{\frac{2+\ell(\gamma)}{4}})\sup_{s\in[0,T]}\|F(X(s))\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H^{\ell(\gamma)})}\leq C(\lambda_{N}^{-\frac{\gamma}{2}}+k^{\frac{\gamma}{4}}),\end{split}

(5.23)

where
\ell(\gamma)=0
, for
\gamma\in(\tfrac{d}{2},2]
and
\ell(\gamma)=2
, for
\gamma\in(2,4]
by (
3.81
).
With regard to
J_{3}
, we employ (
5.9
) with
\alpha=4-l(\gamma)

and (
3.80
) to infer

\displaystyle\begin{split}J_{3}&\leq C(\lambda_{N}^{-\frac{l(\gamma)}{2}}+k^{\frac{l(\gamma)}{4}})\sum_{j=1}^{m}\int_{t_{j-1}}^{t_{j}}(t_{m}-s)^{-1}\|F(X(t_{j})-F(X(t_{m}))\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H^{l(\gamma)-2})}\,\text{d}s\\
&\leq C(\lambda_{N}^{-\frac{l(\gamma)}{2}}+k^{\frac{l(\gamma)}{4}})\sum_{j=1}^{m-1}\int_{t_{j-1}}^{t_{j}}(t_{m}-s)^{-1}t_{m-j}^{\frac{1}{8}}\,\text{d}s\\
&\leq C(\lambda_{N}^{-\frac{\gamma}{2}}+k^{\frac{\gamma}{4}}){\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{\int_{0}^{t_{m-1}}(t_{m}-s)^{-1+\frac{1}{8}}\,\text{d}s}}\\
&\leq C(\lambda_{N}^{-\frac{\gamma}{2}}+k^{\frac{\gamma}{4}}),\end{split}

(5.24)

where

l(\gamma)=2
, for
\gamma\in(\tfrac{d}{2},2]
,
l(\gamma)=3
, for
\gamma\in(2,3]
and
l(\gamma)=4
, for
\gamma\in(3,4]
by (
3.81
).
This combined with (
5.22
) and (
5.23
) leads to

\displaystyle J\leq C(\lambda_{N}^{-\frac{\gamma}{2}}+k^{\frac{\gamma}{4}}).

(5.25)

To bound
K
,
utilizing the Burkholder-Davis-Gundy-type inequality and (
5.10
)
with
\nu=\gamma
arrives at

\displaystyle\begin{split}K\leq&C\Big(\sum_{j=1}^{m}\int_{t_{j-1}}^{t_{j}}\|\Psi_{k}^{M,N}(t_{m}-s)Q^{\frac{1}{2}}\|^{2}_{\mathcal{L}_{2}(H)}\text{d}s\Big)^{\frac{1}{2}}\\
\leq&C(\lambda_{N}^{-\frac{\gamma}{2}}+k^{\frac{\gamma}{4}}){\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{(\|A^{\frac{\gamma-2}{2}}PQ^{\frac{1}{2}}\|_{\mathcal{L}_{2}(H)}+\|(I-P)Q^{\frac{1}{2}}\|_{\mathcal{L}_{2}(H)}).}}\end{split}

(5.26)

At last, gathering the estimates of
I
,
J
and
K
together implies

\displaystyle\|X(t_{m})-\widetilde{X}_{m}^{M,N}\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;\dot{H})}\leq C(\lambda_{N}^{-\frac{\gamma}{2}}+k^{\frac{\gamma}{4}}).

(5.27)

Hence this ends the proof of this lemma.
\square

5.2
Error estimates for
\|\widetilde{X}_{m}^{M,N}-X_{m}^{M,N}\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H)}

This part is devoted to the bound of the second error term in (
5.5
).
The bound of the first error term together with the moment bounds of the fully discrete approximation and the solution of the auxiliary problem (
5.2
) plays a key role in our convergence analysis.

Lemma 5.4

Let
X_{m}^{M,N}
and
\widetilde{X}_{m}^{M,N}
be the solutions of (
4.2
) and (
5.2
), respectively. Suppose
Assumptions
2.1
-
2.4
are valid, then
there exist two positive constants
C
and
k_{0}
such that for all
k\leq k_{0}
and
\forall p\geq 1
,

\displaystyle\|X_{m}^{M,N}-\widetilde{X}_{m}^{M,N}\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H)}\leq C(\lambda_{N}^{-\frac{\gamma}{2}}+k^{\frac{\gamma}{4}}).

(5.28)

Proof of Lemma
5.4
.

Note first that the error
\widetilde{e}_{m}^{M,N}:=X_{m}^{M,N}-\widetilde{X}_{m}^{M,N}\in\dot{H}
is the solution of the following error problem

\displaystyle\widetilde{e}_{m}^{M,N}-\widetilde{e}_{m-1}^{M,N}+kA^{2}\widetilde{e}_{m}^{M,N}=-kP_{N}APF(X_{m}^{M,N})+kP_{N}APF(X(t_{m})),\quad\widetilde{e}_{0}^{M,N}=0,

(5.29)

which can be reformulated as

\displaystyle\widetilde{e}_{m}^{M,N}=k\sum_{j=1}^{m}E_{k,N}^{m-j+1}P_{N}AP(F(X(t_{j}))-F(X_{j}^{M,N})).

(5.30)

Multiplying both sides of (
5.29
) by
A^{-1}\widetilde{e}_{m}^{M,N}
and using (
2.13
) yield

\displaystyle\begin{split}\big<\widetilde{e}_{m}^{M,N}-&\widetilde{e}_{m-1}^{M,N},A^{-1}\widetilde{e}_{m}^{M,N}\big>+k|\widetilde{e}_{m}^{M,N}|_{1}^{2}\\
&=k\big<F(\widetilde{X}_{m}^{M,N})-F(X_{m}^{M,N}),\widetilde{e}_{m}^{M,N}\big>+k\big<F(X(t_{m}))-F(\widetilde{X}_{m}^{M,N}),\widetilde{e}_{m}^{M,N}\big>\\
&\leq\tfrac{3k}{2}\|\widetilde{e}_{m}^{M,N}\|^{2}+\tfrac{k}{2}\|F(X(t_{m}))-F(\widetilde{X}_{m}^{M,N})\|^{2}\\
&\leq\tfrac{3k}{2}\|\widetilde{e}_{m}^{M,N}\|^{2}+Ck\|X(t_{m})-\widetilde{X}_{m}^{M,N}\|^{2}(1+\|X(t_{m})\|^{4}_{V}+\|\widetilde{X}_{m}^{M,N}\|^{4}_{V}).\end{split}

(5.31)

Noting that
\|\widetilde{e}_{m}^{M,N}\|^{2}\leq|\widetilde{e}_{m}^{M,N}|_{1}|\widetilde{e}_{m}^{M,N}|_{-1}
and
\frac{1}{2}(|\widetilde{e}_{m}^{M,N}|_{-1}^{2}-|\widetilde{e}^{M,N}_{m-1}|_{-1}^{2})\leq\big<\widetilde{e}_{m}^{M,N}-\widetilde{e}^{M,N}_{m-1},A^{-1}\widetilde{e}_{m}^{M,N}\big>
,
we further arrive at

\displaystyle\begin{split}\tfrac{1}{2}(|\widetilde{e}_{m}^{M,N}|_{-1}^{2}&-|\widetilde{e}_{m-1}^{M,N}|_{-1}^{2})+k|\widetilde{e}_{m}^{M,N}|_{1}^{2}\\
&\leq\big<\widetilde{e}_{m}^{M,N}-\widetilde{e}^{M,N}_{m-1},A^{-1}\widetilde{e}_{m}^{M,N}\big>+k|\widetilde{e}_{m}^{M,N}|_{1}^{2}\\
&\leq\tfrac{k}{2}|\widetilde{e}_{m}^{M,N}|^{2}_{1}+\tfrac{9k}{8}|\widetilde{e}_{m}^{M,N}|^{2}_{-1}+Ck\|X(t_{m})-\widetilde{X}_{m}^{M,N}\|^{2}\big(1+\|X(t_{m})\|^{4}_{V}+\|\widetilde{X}_{m}^{M,N}\|^{4}_{V}\big).\end{split}

(5.32)

By iteration and using the Gronwall inequality, we have

\displaystyle|\widetilde{e}_{m}^{M,N}|_{-1}^{2}+k\sum_{j=1}^{m}|\widetilde{e}^{M,N}_{j}|_{1}^{2}\leq Ck\sum_{j=1}^{m}\|\widetilde{X}_{j}^{M,N}-X(t_{j})\|^{2}\big(1+\|\widetilde{X}_{j}^{M,N}\|_{V}^{4}+\|X(t_{j})\|_{V}^{4}\big).

(5.33)

Further, taking (
3.65
), (
5.4
),
(
3.6
) and (
5.27
) into account,
it follows that, for
\kappa=\min\{\gamma,\frac{d}{2}+\frac{1}{4}\}

\displaystyle\begin{split}\Big\|k\sum_{j=1}^{m}|\widetilde{e}_{j}^{M,N}|_{1}^{2}\Big\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;\mathbb{R})}&\leq Ck\sum_{j=1}^{m}\Big\|\|\widetilde{X}_{j}^{M,N}-X(t_{j})\|^{2}\big(1+\|\widetilde{X}_{j}^{M,N}\|_{V}^{4}+\|X(t_{j})\|_{V}^{4}\big)\Big\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;\mathbb{R})}\\
&\leq Ck\sum_{j=1}^{m}\|\widetilde{X}_{j}^{M,N}-X(t_{j})\|_{L^{4p}(\Omega;H)}^{2}\big(1+\|\widetilde{X}_{j}^{M,N}\|_{L^{8p}(\Omega;H^{\kappa})}^{4}+\|X(t_{j})\|_{L^{8p}(\Omega;H^{\kappa})}^{4}\big)\\
&\leq C(\lambda_{N}^{-\frac{\gamma}{2}}+k^{\frac{\gamma}{4}})^{2}.\end{split}

(5.34)

In analogy with (
3.59
), we split
\big\|\widetilde{e}_{m}^{M,N}\big\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H)}

into two terms:

\displaystyle\begin{split}\|\widetilde{e}^{M,N}_{m}\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H)}\leq&k\sum_{j=1}^{m}\Big\|E_{k,N}^{m-j+1}P_{N}AP\big(F(X(t_{j}))-F(\widetilde{X}^{M,N}_{j})\big)\Big\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H)}\\
&+k\Big\|\sum_{j=1}^{m}E_{k,N}^{m-j+1}P_{N}AP\big(F(\widetilde{X}_{j}^{M,N})-F(X_{j}^{M,N})\big)\Big\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H)}\\
=:&L_{1}+L_{2}.\end{split}

(5.35)

Following similar arguments used in the proof of (
5.34
), we obtain, for
\kappa=\min\{\gamma,\frac{d}{2}+\frac{1}{4}\}

\displaystyle\begin{split}L_{1}&\leq Ck\sum_{j=1}^{m}t_{m-j+1}^{-\frac{1}{2}}\|F(X(t_{j}))-F(\widetilde{X}_{j}^{M,N})\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;H)}\\
&\leq Ck\sum_{j=1}^{m}t_{m-j+1}^{-\frac{1}{2}}\|X(t_{j})-\widetilde{X}_{j}^{M,N}\|_{L^{2p}(\Omega;H)}\Big(1+\|X(t_{j})\|^{2}_{L^{4p}(\Omega;V)}+\|\widetilde{X}_{j}^{M,N}\|^{2}_{L^{4p}(\Omega;V)}\Big)\\
&\leq C(\lambda_{N}^{-\frac{\gamma}{2}}+k^{\frac{\gamma}{4}})k\sum_{j=1}^{m}t_{m-j+1}^{-\frac{1}{2}}\Big(1+\sup_{s\in[0,T]}\|X(s)\|^{2}_{L^{4p}(\Omega;H^{\kappa})}+\sup_{1\leq j\leq M}\|\widetilde{X}_{j}^{M,N}\|^{2}_{L^{4p}(\Omega;H^{\kappa})}\Big)\\
&\leq C(\lambda_{N}^{-\frac{\gamma}{2}}+k^{\frac{\gamma}{4}}).\end{split}

(5.36)

To bound the term
L_{2}
, we apply (
3.10
) with
\iota=\kappa=\min\{\gamma,\frac{d}{2}+\frac{1}{4}\}
, and (
3.11
) with
\iota=\kappa=\min\{\gamma,\frac{d}{2}+\frac{1}{4}\}
to obtain, for
\eta=\min\{\gamma,\frac{2}{3}\}

\displaystyle\begin{split}&\|A^{\frac{\eta}{2}}P\big(F(\widetilde{X}_{j}^{M,N})-F(X_{j}^{M,N})\big)\|\\
=&\left\|A^{\frac{\eta}{2}}P\int_{0}^{1}F^{\prime}\big(X(s)+\lambda(\widetilde{X}_{j}^{M,N})-F(X_{j}^{M,N})\big)\big(\widetilde{X}_{j}^{M,N})-F(X_{j}^{M,N}\big)(1-\lambda)\,\text{d}\lambda\right\|\\
\leq&C\|e_{j}^{M,N}\|_{1}\big(1+\|\widetilde{X}_{j}^{M,N}\|_{\kappa}^{2}+\|X_{j}^{M,N}\|_{\kappa}^{2}\big),\end{split}

(5.37)

where we also used the fact
\eta<\iota=\kappa=\min\{\gamma,\frac{d}{2}+\frac{1}{4}\}
in dimension one and
\eta<1
in dimensions two and three.
Then, by (
5.34
), (
5.4
), (
4.5
) and the fact
\widetilde{e}_{j}^{M,N}\in\dot{H}^{1}
, one can find that, for
\eta=\min\{\gamma,\frac{2}{3}\}
and
\kappa=\min\{\gamma,\frac{d}{2}+\frac{1}{4}\}
,

\displaystyle\begin{split}L_{2}\leq&C\Big\|k\sum_{j=1}^{m}t_{m-j+1}^{-\frac{2-\eta}{4}}\|A^{\frac{\eta}{2}}P\big(F(\widetilde{X}_{j}^{M,N})-F(X_{j}^{M,N})\big)\|\Big\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;\mathbb{R})}\\
\leq&C\Big\|k\sum_{j=1}^{m}t_{m-j+1}^{-\frac{2-\eta}{4}}|\widetilde{e}_{j}^{M,N}|_{1}\,\big(1+\|\widetilde{X}_{j}^{M,N}\|_{\kappa}^{2}+\|X_{j}^{M,N}\|_{\kappa}^{2}\big)\Big\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;\mathbb{R})}\\
\leq&C\Big\|\Big(k\sum_{j=1}^{m}|\widetilde{e}_{j}^{M,N}|^{2}_{1}\Big)^{\frac{1}{2}}\Big(k\sum_{j=1}^{m}t_{m-j+1}^{-\frac{2-\eta}{2}}\big(1+\|\widetilde{X}_{j}^{M,N}\|_{\kappa}^{4}+\|X_{j}^{M,N}\|_{\kappa}^{4}\big)\Big)^{\frac{1}{2}}\Big\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;\mathbb{R})}\\
\leq&C\Big\|k\sum_{j=1}^{m}|\widetilde{e}_{j}^{M,N}|_{1}^{2}\Big\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;\mathbb{R})}^{\frac{1}{2}}\Big\|k\sum_{j=1}^{m}t_{m-j+1}^{-\frac{2-\eta}{2}}\big(1+\|\widetilde{X}_{j}^{M,N}\|_{\kappa}^{4}+\|X_{j}^{M,N}\|_{\kappa}^{4}\big)\Big\|_{L^{p}(\Omega;\mathbb{R})}^{\frac{1}{2}}\\
\leq&C(\lambda_{N}^{-\frac{\gamma}{2}}+k^{\frac{\gamma}{4}}).\end{split}

(5.38)

Finally, inserting (
5.36
) and (
5.38
) into (
5.35
)
shows (
5.28
) and finishes the proof.

\square
