Introduction

Since order weakly compact operators play important role in positive operators, our aim in this manuscript is to introduce and study a new class of operators as almost order-weakly compact operators and we establish some of its relationships with others known operators. Under some conditions, we show that the adjoint of any almost order-weakly compact operator is so. Every compact and weakly compact operators are almost order-weakly compact operator, but the converse in general not holds.
