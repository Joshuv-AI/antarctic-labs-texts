Abstract
We revisit the free field construction of the deformed W W -algebra by Frenkel and Reshetikhin [ Comm. Math. Phys. 197 (1998), 1–32], where the basic W W -current has been identified. Herein, we establish a free field construction of higher W W -currents of the deformed W W -algebra associated with the twisted affine Lie algebra A 2 ​ N ( 2 ) A_{2N}^{(2)} . We obtain a closed set of quadratic relations and duality, which allows us to define deformed W W -algebra 𝒲 x , r ​ ( A 2 ​ N ( 2 ) ) {\mathcal{W}}_{x,r}\big(A_{2N}^{(2)}\big) using generators and relations.
