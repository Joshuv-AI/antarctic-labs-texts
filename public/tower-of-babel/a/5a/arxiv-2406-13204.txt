Abstract
In this paper, we study the normalized solutions of the Schrödinger system with trapping potentials { − Δ ​ u 1 + V 1 ​ ( x ) ​ u 1 − λ 1 ​ u 1 = μ 1 ​ u 1 3 + β ​ u 1 ​ u 2 2 + κ ​ u 2 ​ in ​ ℝ 3 , − Δ ​ u 2 + V 2 ​ ( x ) ​ u 2 − λ 2 ​ u 2 = μ 2 ​ u 2 3 + β ​ u 1 2 ​ u 2 + κ ​ u 1 ​ in ​ ℝ 3 , u 1 ∈ H 1 ( ℝ 3 ) , u 2 ∈ H 1 ( ℝ 3 ) , \begin{cases}-\Delta u_{1}+V_{1}(x)u_{1}-\lambda_{1}u_{1}=\mu_{1}u_{1}^{3}+\beta u_{1}u_{2}^{2}+\kappa u_{2}~\hbox{in}~\mathbb{R}^{3},\\ -\Delta u_{2}+V_{2}(x)u_{2}-\lambda_{2}u_{2}=\mu_{2}u_{2}^{3}+\beta u_{1}^{2}u_{2}+\kappa u_{1}~\hbox{in}~\mathbb{R}^{3},\\ u_{1}\in H^{1}(\mathbb{R}^{3}),u_{2}\in H^{1}(\mathbb{R}^{3}),\end{cases} under the constraint ∫ ℝ 3 u 1 2 = a 1 2 , ∫ ℝ 3 u 2 2 = a 2 2 , \int_{\mathbb{R}^{3}}u_{1}^{2}=a_{1}^{2},~\int_{\mathbb{R}^{3}}u_{2}^{2}=a_{2}^{2}, where μ 1 , μ 2 , a 1 , a 2 , β > 0 \mu_{1},\mu_{2},a_{1},a_{2},\beta>0 , κ ∈ ℝ \kappa\in\mathbb{R} , V 1 ​ ( x ) V_{1}(x) and V 2 ​ ( x ) V_{2}(x) are trapping potentials, and λ 1 , λ 2 \lambda_{1},\lambda_{2} are lagrangian multipliers, this is a typical L 2 L^{2} -supercritical case in ℝ 3 \mathbb{R}^{3} . We obtain the existence of solutions to this system by minimax theory on the manifold for κ = 0 \kappa=0 and κ ≠ 0 \kappa\neq 0 respectively.

Lemma 2.2

For any

Remark 2.1

\begin{split}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{3}}\mu_{1}u_{1}^{4}+\mu_{2}u_{2}^{4}+2\beta u_{1}^{2}u_{2}^{2}&\leq(\mu_{1}+\beta)\int_{\mathbb{R}^{3}}u_{1}^{4}+(\mu_{2}+\beta)\int_{\mathbb{R}^{3}}u_{2}^{4}\\
