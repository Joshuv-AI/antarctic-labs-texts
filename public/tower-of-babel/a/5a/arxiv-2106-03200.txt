Weighted Hardy Type inequalities with Robin Boundary Conditions
Ismail Kombe and Abdullah Yener

Abstract
. In this paper we establish a general weighted Hardy type inequality for the p − p- Laplace operator with Robin boundary condition. We provide various concrete examples to illustrate our results for different weights. Furthermore, we present some Heisenberg-Pauli-Weyl type inequalities with boundary terms on balls with radius R R at the origin in ℝ n \mathbb{R}^{n} .



1.
Introduction

The classical Hardy inequality states that

(1.1)

\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\left|\nabla\phi\left(x\right)\right|^{p}dx\geq\left|\frac{n-p}{p}\right|^{p}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\frac{\left|\phi\left(x\right)\right|^{p}}{\left|x\right|^{p}}dx,

and holds for all
\phi\in C_{0}^{\infty}\left(\mathbb{R}^{n}\right)
if
1<p<n
, and for all
\phi\in C_{0}^{\infty}\left(\mathbb{R}^{n}\backslash\left\{0\right\}\right)
if
p>n
. The constant
|(n-p)/p|^{p}
in the right-hand side of
1.1
is sharp but not
achieved. This inequality plays an important role in many areas such as
analysis, geometry and mathematical physics. Owing to this, there is a vast
amount of literature on the Hardy type inequalities together with their
variations and generalizations.

For instance, V.G. Maz’ya
[
15
]
proved the following weighted
integral inequality

(1.2)

\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}|x_{n}|^{p-1}|\nabla\phi\left(x\right)|^{p}dx\geq\frac{1}{(2p)^{p}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\frac{|\phi\left(x\right)|^{p}}{\left(x_{n-1}^{2}+x_{n}^{2}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}}dx,

where
\phi\in C_{0}^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
.

In the paper
[
5
]
, E.B. Davies proved the following extension of
Hardy’s inequality for convex domains
\Omega\subset\mathbb{R}^{n},

n\geq 2,

(1.3)

\int_{\Omega}\left|\nabla\phi\left(x\right)\right|^{2}dx\geq\frac{1}{4}\int_{\Omega}\frac{\left|\phi\left(x\right)\right|^{2}}{\delta^{2}\left(x\right)}dx,

where
\phi\in C_{0}^{\infty}\left(\Omega\right)
. Here

(1.4)

\delta\left(x\right)=\mathrm{dist}\,\left(x,\partial\Omega\right)=\min_{y\in\partial\Omega}\left|x-y\right|

is the distance function to the boundary
\partial\Omega
. Moreover the
constant
\frac{1}{4}
is sharp and not achieved. We refer the interested
reader to the monographs
[
13
]
,
[
5
]
,
[
2
]
,
[
10
]
,
[
17
]
and the paper
[
9
]
.

On the other hand, very little work has been done on Hardy type inequalities
with Robin boundary conditions. However, there has been some initiation in
this area of interest. In an interesting paper, H. Kovařik and A. Laptev

[
12
]
proved, among other results, the following Hardy
inequality for Laplace operators with Robin boundary conditions,

(1.5)

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}\left|\nabla\phi\right|^{2}dx+\int_{\partial\Omega}\sigma\left|\phi\right|^{2}d\nu\geq\frac{1}{4}

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}\left(\delta\left(x\right)+\frac{1}{2\sigma\left(p\left(x\right)\right)}\right)^{-2}\left|\phi\right|^{2}dx

\displaystyle+

\displaystyle\frac{1}{4}\int_{\Omega}\left(R_{in}+\frac{1}{2\sigma\left(p\left(x\right)\right)}\right)^{-2}\left|\phi\right|^{2}dx

\displaystyle+

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\int_{\partial\Omega}\left(R_{in}+\frac{1}{2\sigma\left(y\right)}\right)^{-1}\left|\phi\right|^{2}d\nu,

where
\Omega\subset\mathbb{R}^{n}
is a bounded convex domain with smooth
boundary
\partial\Omega
,
\phi\in H^{1}(\Omega)
,
0\leq\sigma\in L^{\infty}\left(\partial\Omega\right)
,
d\nu
is the surface measure on
\partial\Omega
and
R_{in}:=\text{sup}\{\delta(x):x\in\Omega\}
. Here
p\left(x\right)
is the projection function
p:\Omega\setminus S\to\partial\Omega
defined by
p\left(x\right):=y\in\partial\Omega\ :\,\delta\left(x\right)=|x-y|,\quad x\in\Omega\setminus S
, where
S\subset\Omega
the subset of points in
\Omega

for which there exist at least two points
y_{1},y_{2}\in\partial\Omega
so
that the minimum in (
1.4
) is achieved.

Later, T. Ekholm, H. Kovařik and A. Laptev
[
6
]

studied the best constant in a Hardy inequality for the
p-
Laplace operator
on convex domains with Robin boundary conditions. Moreover, there has also
been an interest regarding Hardy type inequalities for functions which does
not vanish on the boundary of a given domain, see e.g.
[
1
]
,

[
3
]
,
[
4
]
,
[
19
]
and
[
16
]
.

In light of the results mentioned above, it is natural to investigate under
what conditions the following general weighted Hardy-type inequalities with
boundary terms are valid

(1.6)

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}a\left(x\right)\left|\nabla\phi\right|^{p}dx

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}b\left(x\right)\left|\phi\right|^{p}dx+\int_{\partial\Omega}\beta\left(x\right)\left|\phi\right|^{p}d\nu.

Within this frame work, the main objective of this article is to study the
general weighted Hardy type inequalities for the
p-
Laplace operators with
Robin boundary conditions. We should emphasize that our unifying method is
quite practical and constructive to obtain several weighted Hardy, Maz’ya
and Heisenberg-Pauli-Weyl type inequalities with boundary terms.

2.
Weighted Hardy Type Inequalities With Boundary Terms

In what follows,
\Omega
represents smooth bounded domains contained in
\mathbb{R}^{n},

\partial_{\nu}u
denotes the exterior normal derivative of
u
at
the boundary of
\Omega
and
d\nu
is the surface measure on
\partial\Omega
. The generic point is
x=\left(x_{1},\ldots,x_{n}\right)\in\mathbb{R}^{n}
and
r=\left|x\right|=\sqrt{x_{1}^{2}+\cdots+x_{n}^{2}}.

We denote by
B_{R}=\left\{x\in\mathbb{R}^{n}:\left|x\right|<R\right\}
the open ball at the origin with
radius
R
in
\mathbb{R}^{n}
. The boundary is the sphere
\partial B_{R}=\left\{x\in\mathbb{R}^{n}:\left|x\right|=R\right\}
.

We are now ready to state the main result of this paper.

Theorem 2.1
.

Let
\Omega
be a smooth bounded domain contained in
\mathbb{R}^{n}.
Let
a\in C^{1}\left(\Omega\right)
be a nonnegative function,
u\in C^{\infty}\left(\Omega\right)
be a positive function,
b\in L_{\text{loc}}^{1}\left(\Omega\right)
and
\beta\in L_{\text{loc}}^{1}\left(\partial\Omega\right)
such that

(2.1)

-\nabla\cdot\left(a\left(x\right)\left|\nabla u\right|^{p-2}\nabla u\right)\geq b\left(x\right)u^{p-1}\qquad\text{a.e. in }\Omega

and

(2.2)

a\left(x\right)\left|\nabla u\right|^{p-2}\partial_{\nu}u=\beta\left(x\right)u^{p-1}\qquad\text{a.e. at }\partial\Omega,

where
\partial_{\nu}u
denotes the exterior normal derivative of
u
at
the boundary of
\Omega.
There exists a positive constant
c_{p}=c\left(p\right)
such that; if
p\geq 2,
then

(2.3)

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}a\left(x\right)\left|\nabla\phi\right|^{p}dx

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}b\left(x\right)\left|\phi\right|^{p}dx+c_{p}\int_{\Omega}a\left(x\right)\Big|\nabla\Big(\frac{\phi}{u}\Big)\Big|^{p}u^{p}dx

\displaystyle+\int_{\partial\Omega}\beta\left(x\right)\left|\phi\right|^{p}d\nu

and if
1<p<2,
then

(2.4)

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}a\left(x\right)\left|\nabla\phi\right|^{p}dx

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}b\left(x\right)\left|\phi\right|^{p}dx+c_{p}\int_{\Omega}a\left(x\right)\frac{\left|\nabla\left(\frac{\phi}{u}\right)\right|^{2}u^{2}}{\left(\left|\frac{\phi}{u}\nabla u\right|+\left|\nabla\left(\frac{\phi}{u}\right)\right|u\right)^{2-p}}dx

\displaystyle+\int_{\partial\Omega}\beta\left(x\right)\left|\phi\right|^{p}d\nu

holds for all
\phi\in C^{\infty}\left(\Omega\right).
Here,
d\nu
is
the surface measure on
\partial\Omega
.

Proof.

For any
\phi\in C^{\infty}\left(\Omega\right)
we now set
\varphi:=\frac{\phi}{u},
where
0<u\in C^{\infty}\left(\Omega\right).
Thus,

\left|\nabla\phi\right|^{p}=\left|\varphi\nabla u+u\nabla\varphi\right|^{p}.

We now use the following convexity inequality:

\left|\xi+\eta\right|^{p}\geq|\xi|^{p}+p|\xi|^{p-2}\xi\cdot\eta+c_{p}|\eta|^{p},\qquad\forall\xi,\eta\in\mathbb{R}^{n},\quad p\geq 2,

where
c_{p}
is a positive constant depending only on
p,
see
[
14
]
. As an immediate consequence of the above inequality with the
vectors
\xi=\varphi\nabla u
and
\eta=u\nabla\varphi,
we obtain

(2.5)

\left|\nabla\phi\right|^{p}\geq\left|\nabla u\right|^{p}\left|\varphi\right|^{p}+u\left|\nabla u\right|^{p-2}\nabla u\cdot\nabla\left(\left|\varphi\right|^{p}\right)+c_{p}\left|\nabla\varphi\right|^{p}u^{p}.

Multiplying the inequality
\left(\ref{e4}\right)
by
a\left(x\right)

on both sides, and then applying integration by parts over
\Omega
for the
middle term, yields

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}a\left(x\right)\left|\nabla\phi\right|^{p}dx

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}a\left(x\right)\left|\nabla u\right|^{p}\left|\varphi\right|^{p}dx-\int_{\Omega}\nabla\cdot\left(a\left(x\right)u\left|\nabla u\right|^{p-2}\nabla u\right)\left|\varphi\right|^{p}dx

\displaystyle+\int_{\partial\Omega}a\left(x\right)u\left|\nabla u\right|^{p-2}\left|\varphi\right|^{p}\partial_{\nu}ud\nu+c_{p}\int_{\Omega}a\left(x\right)\left|\nabla\varphi\right|^{p}u^{p}dx

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\int_{\Omega}\nabla\cdot\left(a\left(x\right)\left|\nabla u\right|^{p-2}\nabla u\right)u\left|\varphi\right|^{p}dx

\displaystyle+c_{p}\int_{\Omega}a\left(x\right)\left|\nabla\varphi\right|^{p}u^{p}dx+\int_{\partial\Omega}a\left(x\right)u\left|\nabla u\right|^{p-2}\left|\varphi\right|^{p}\partial_{\nu}ud\nu.

It therefore follows from
\left(\ref{de1}\right)
and
\left(\ref{de2}\right)
that

\int_{\Omega}a\left(x\right)\left|\nabla\phi\right|^{p}dx\geq\int_{\Omega}b\left(x\right)u^{p}\left|\varphi\right|^{p}dx+c_{p}\int_{\Omega}a\left(x\right)\left|\nabla\varphi\right|^{p}u^{p}dx+\int_{\partial\Omega}\beta\left(x\right)u^{p}\left|\varphi\right|^{p}d\nu.

Finally, making the variable change
\varphi=\frac{\phi}{u}
in the above
integrals, one has the desired inequality for
p\geq 2:

\int_{\Omega}a\left(x\right)\left|\nabla\phi\right|^{p}dx\geq\int_{\Omega}b\left(x\right)\left|\phi\right|^{p}dx+c_{p}\int_{\Omega}a\left(x\right)\Big|\nabla\Big(\frac{\phi}{u}\Big)\Big|^{p}u^{p}dx+\int_{\partial\Omega}\beta\left(x\right)\left|\phi\right|^{p}d\nu.

The inequality
\left(\ref{e3}\right)
can be proved in the same spirit,
and in this case we apply the following convexity inequality:

\left|\xi+\eta\right|^{p}\geq\left|\xi\right|^{p}+p\left|\xi\right|^{p-2}\xi\cdot\eta+c_{p}\frac{\left|\eta\right|^{2}}{\left(\left|\xi\right|+\left|\eta\right|\right)^{2-p}},\qquad\forall\xi,\eta\in\mathbb{R}^{n},\quad 1<p<2,

where
c_{p}=c\left(p\right)>0,
see
[
14
]
. We shall omit the
details.
∎

2.1

As we stated earlier, our method is quite practical to construct various

domains in
\mathbb{R}^{n}
. To do this, we need to identify suitable model functions
a

Corollary 2.1
.

Let

\displaystyle\int_{B_{R}}\left|x\right|^{\alpha}\left|\nabla\phi\right|^{p}dx

\displaystyle-\left(\frac{n+\alpha-p}{p}\right)^{p-1}R^{\alpha-p+1}\int_{\partial B_{R}}|\phi|^{p}d\nu

for all

On the other hand, by applying Theorem

\displaystyle\left(\frac{n+\alpha+\gamma-p}{p}\right)^{p}\int_{B_{R}}\left|x\right|^{\alpha-p}\sinh^{\gamma}\left|x\right||\phi|^{p}dx

\displaystyle-\left(\frac{n+\alpha+\gamma-p}{p}\right)^{p-1}\frac{\sinh^{\gamma}R}{R^{p-\alpha-1}}\int_{\partial B_{R}}|\phi|^{p}d\nu

for all

.

Remark 2.3
.

Note that one can also apply Theorem

on some domains

\Omega
in
\mathbb{R}^{n}
with piecewisely smooth boundary
\partial\Omega.
The following is
the first corollary in this direction.

Suppose that

a\equiv 1\text{\quad and\quad}u=x_{1}r,

then we derive the following inequality:

Corollary 2.12
.

Let
\Omega=\left\{x\in B_{R}:x_{1}>0\right\}.
For all
\phi\in C^{\infty}(\Omega),
there holds

\int_{\Omega}\left|\nabla\phi\right|^{2}dx\geq-\left(n+1\right)\int_{\Omega}\frac{\phi^{2}}{\left|x\right|^{2}}dx+\int_{\partial\Omega}\left(\frac{1}{R}-\frac{1}{x_{1}}\right)\phi^{2}d\nu.

When we take the following pair

a=\left(\frac{1}{x_{1}^{2}}+\frac{1}{x_{2}^{2}}+\cdots+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{2}}\right)^{\frac{2-p}{p}}\text{\quad and\quad}u=\left(x_{1}x_{2}\ldots x_{n}\right)^{1/p}

in Theorem
\ref{thm2.1},
we have the subsequent weighted
L^{p}
Hardy
type inequality with boundary terms.

Corollary 2.13
.

Let
1<p<\infty\
and
\Omega=\left\{x\in B_{R}:x_{i}>0,\text{ }i=1,2,\ldots,n\right\}.
Then, for all
\phi\in C^{\infty}\left(\Omega\right),
we have

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}\left(\frac{1}{x_{1}^{2}}+\frac{1}{x_{2}^{2}}+\cdots+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{2}}\right)^{\frac{2-p}{2}}\left|\nabla\phi\right|^{p}dx

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{p^{p}}\int_{\Omega}\left(\frac{1}{x_{1}^{2}}+\frac{1}{x_{2}^{2}}+\cdots+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{2}}\right)|\phi|^{p}dx

\displaystyle-\frac{1}{p^{p-1}}\int_{\partial\Omega}\left(\frac{1}{x_{1}^{2}}+\frac{1}{x_{2}^{2}}+\cdots+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{2}}\right)|\phi|^{p}d\nu

\displaystyle+\frac{n}{Rp^{p-1}}\int_{\partial\Omega}|\phi|^{p}d\nu.

We now set the pair as

a=x_{1}^{p-1}\text{\quad and\quad}u=\left(\log\frac{R}{x_{1}}\right)^{\frac{p-1}{p}}.

Hence, we derive the power logarithmic
L^{p}
Hardy type inequality.

Corollary 2.14
.

Let
p>1
and
\Omega=\left\{x\in B_{R}:x_{1}>0\right\}.

Then, for all
\phi\in C^{\infty}\left(\Omega\right),
we have

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}x_{1}^{p-1}\left|\nabla\phi\right|^{p}dx

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\left(\frac{p-1}{p}\right)^{p}\int_{\Omega}\frac{|\phi|^{p}}{x_{1}\left(\log\frac{R}{x_{1}}\right)^{p}}dx

\displaystyle+\left(\frac{p-1}{p}\right)^{p-1}\frac{1}{R}\int_{\partial\Omega}\frac{R-x_{1}}{\left(\log\frac{R}{x_{1}}\right)^{p-1}}|\phi|^{p}d\nu.

Inspired by the inequality of Maz’ya (
1.2
) and considering the
functions

a=x_{n}^{p-1}\text{\quad and\quad}u=\left(x_{n-1}^{2}+x_{n}^{2}\right)^{-\frac{1}{2p}},

we obtain the following Maz’ya type inequality with a boundary term.

Corollary 2.15
.

Let
p>1
and
\Omega=\left\{x\in B_{R}:x_{n}>0\right\}.

Then, for all
\phi\in C^{\infty}\left(\Omega\right),
we have

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}x_{n}^{p-1}\left|\nabla\phi\right|^{p}dx

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{p^{p}}\int_{\Omega}\frac{|\phi|^{p}}{(x_{n-1}^{2}+x_{n}^{2})^{p/2}}dx

\displaystyle-\frac{1}{Rp^{p-1}}\int_{\partial\Omega}\frac{x_{n}^{p-1}(x_{n-1}^{2}+x_{n}^{2}-Rx_{n})}{\left(x_{n-1}^{2}+x_{n}^{2}\right)^{\frac{p}{2}}}|\phi|^{p}d\nu.

Another consequence of the Theorem
2.1
with the special functions

a=e^{\alpha x_{1}^{2}x_{2}^{2}\dots x_{n}^{2}}\text{\quad and\quad}u=e^{-\alpha x_{1}^{2}x_{2}^{2}\dots x_{n}^{2}}

is the following inequality.

Corollary 2.16
.

For all
\phi\in C^{\infty}\left(B_{R}\right)
and
\alpha>0,
we have

\displaystyle\int_{B_{R}}e^{\alpha x_{1}^{2}x_{2}^{2}\dots x_{n}^{2}}\left|\nabla\phi\right|^{2}dx\geq

\displaystyle 2\alpha\int_{B_{R}}e^{\alpha x_{1}^{2}x_{2}^{2}\dots x_{n}^{2}}\left(x_{1}^{2}x_{2}^{2}\dots x_{n}^{2}\right)\left(\frac{1}{x_{1}^{2}}+\cdots+\frac{1}{x_{n}^{2}}\right)|\phi|^{2}dx

\displaystyle-\frac{2\alpha n}{R}\int_{\partial B_{R}}e^{\alpha x_{1}^{2}x_{2}^{2}\dots x_{n}^{2}}\left(x_{1}^{2}\dots x_{n}^{2}\right)|\phi|^{2}d\nu.

Heisenberg-Pauli-Weyl Inequality

Finally, we turn our attention to the classical Heisenberg-Pauli-Weyl
inequality which states that

(2.8)

\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}|\nabla f(x)|^{2}dx\right)\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}|x|^{2}|f(x)|^{2}dx\right)\geq\frac{n^{2}}{4}\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}|f(x)|^{2}dx\right)^{2}

for all
f\in L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
. It has been studied in various
contexts and we refer to
[
8
]
and
[
7
]
for an
overview of the history and the relevance of this inequality.

It is worth mentioning here that Theorem
2.1
can be
applied to obtain the local Heisenberg-Pauli-Weyl-type inequalities with
boundary terms. In order to be more precise, we now take
p=2
in Theorem
2.1
and choose the following functions

a\equiv 1\text{\quad and\quad}u=e^{-\alpha\left|x\right|^{2}},\quad\alpha>0.

This allows the derivation of the inequality

\int_{B_{R}}\left|\nabla\phi\right|^{2}dx\geq-4\alpha^{2}\int_{B_{R}}\left|x\right|^{2}\phi^{2}dx+2\alpha n\int_{B_{R}}\phi^{2}dx-2\alpha R^{2}\int_{\partial B_{R}}\phi^{2}d\nu.

Optimizing in
\alpha
, we find the local Heisenberg-Pauli-Weyl type
inequality with a boundary term.

Corollary 2.17
.

For all
\phi\in C^{\infty}\left(B_{R}\right),
we have

\left(\int_{B_{R}}\left|\nabla\phi\right|^{2}dx\right)\left(\int_{B_{R}}\left|x\right|^{2}\phi^{2}dx\right)\geq\frac{1}{4}\left(n\int_{B_{R}}\phi^{2}dx-R^{2}\int_{\partial B_{R}}\phi^{2}d\nu\right)^{2}.

On the other hand, by setting the functions

a\equiv 1\text{\quad and\quad}u=e^{-\alpha\left|x\right|},\quad\alpha>0,

we shall deduce from Theorem
2.1
that

\int_{B_{R}}\left|\nabla\phi\right|^{2}dx\geq-\alpha^{2}\int_{B_{R}}\phi^{2}dx+\alpha\left(\left(n-1\right)\int_{B_{R}}\frac{\phi^{2}}{\left|x\right|}dx-\int_{\partial B_{R}}\phi^{2}d\nu\right).

Hence, a very similar argument applies to obtain the following version of
the Heisenberg-Pauli-Weyl type inequality.

Corollary 2.18
.

For every
\phi\in C^{\infty}(B_{R}),
one has

\left(\int_{B_{R}}\left|\nabla\phi\right|^{2}dx\right)\left(\int_{B_{R}}\phi^{2}dx\right)\geq\frac{1}{4}\left(\left(n-1\right)\int_{B_{R}}\frac{\phi^{2}}{\left|x\right|}dx-\int_{\partial B_{R}}\phi^{2}d\nu\right)^{2}.

Considering the functions

a\equiv 1\text{\quad and\quad}u=e^{-\alpha x_{n}^{2}},\quad\alpha>0,

as a consequence of Theorem
2.1
, we readily have

\int_{B_{R}}\left|\nabla\phi\right|^{2}dx\geq-4\alpha^{2}\int_{B_{R}}x_{n}^{2}|\phi|^{2}dx+2\alpha\left(\int_{B_{R}}|\phi|^{2}dx-\int_{\partial B_{R}}x_{n}^{2}|\phi|^{2}d\nu\right).

Arguing as above, we obtain the following inequality.

Corollary 2.19
.

For every
\phi\in C^{\infty}(B_{R}),
one has

\left(\int_{B_{R}}\left|\nabla\phi\right|^{2}dx\right)\left(\int_{B_{R}}x_{n}^{2}|\phi|^{2}dx\right)\geq\frac{1}{4}\left(\int_{B_{R}}|\phi|^{2}dx-\int_{\partial B_{R}}x_{n}^{2}|\phi|^{2}d\nu\right)^{2}.

Finally, when we take the pair as

a\equiv 1\text{\quad and\quad}u=e^{-\alpha\left(x_{n-1}^{2}+x_{n}^{2}\right)},\quad\alpha>0,

we get

\displaystyle\int_{B_{R}}\left|\nabla\phi\right|^{2}dx\geq

\displaystyle-4\alpha^{2}\int_{B_{R}}\left(x_{n-1}^{2}+x_{n}^{2}\right)|\phi|^{2}dx+4\alpha\int_{B_{R}}|\phi|^{2}dx

\displaystyle-\frac{2\alpha}{R}\int_{\partial B_{R}}\left(x_{n-1}^{2}+x_{n}^{2}\right)|\phi|^{2}d\nu.

Hence, with a similar approach, we achieve the following inequality.

Corollary 2.20
.

For all
\phi\in C^{\infty}\left(B_{R}\right),
we have

\left(\int_{B_{R}}\left|\nabla\phi\right|^{2}dx\right)\left(\int_{B_{R}}\left(x_{n-1}^{2}+x_{n}^{2}\right)|\phi|^{2}dx\right)\geq\frac{A}{4},

where
A=\left(2\int_{B_{R}}|\phi|^{2}dx-\frac{1}{R}\int_{\partial B_{R}}\left(x_{n-1}^{2}+x_{n}^{2}\right)|\phi|^{2}d\nu\right)^{2}
.
