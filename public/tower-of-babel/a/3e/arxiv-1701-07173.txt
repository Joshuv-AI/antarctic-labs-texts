K S 0 − K L 0 K_{S}^{0}-K_{L}^{0} asymmetries in D D -meson decays
Di Wang 1 , Fu-Sheng Yu 1,2 , Peng-Fei Guo 1 , and Hua-Yu Jiang 1

Abstract
The K S 0 − K L 0 K_{S}^{0}-K_{L}^{0} asymmetries in the D D meson decays, induced by the interference between the Cabibbo-favored and the doubly Cabibbo-suppressed amplitudes, can help to understand the dynamics of charm decays. All possible processes of two-body non-leptonic D D decays into one neutral kaon and another pseudoscalar or vector meson are considered. We study the K S 0 − K L 0 K_{S}^{0}-K_{L}^{0} asymmetries and the branching fractions of corresponding processes in the factorization-assisted topological-amplitude approach in which significant flavor S ​ U ​ ( 3 ) SU(3) symmetry breaking effects are included. The branching fractions of K L 0 K_{L}^{0} modes are predicted. It is first found that the K S 0 − K L 0 K_{S}^{0}-K_{L}^{0} asymmetries in the D 0 D^{0} -meson decays are shifted by the D 0 − D ¯ 0 D^{0}-\overline{D}^{0} mixing parameter y D ≃ 0.006 y_{D}\simeq 0.006 , to be 0.113 ± 0.001 0.113\pm 0.001 for all the relevant D 0 D^{0} decay modes. Our results on K S 0 − K L 0 K_{S}^{0}-K_{L}^{0} asymmetries are consistent with the current data and could be tested by experiments in the future.

(3)

where

is a small complex parameter indicating the indirect

violating effect, with the value of

In order to study the

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}(D\to\overline{K}^{0}f)=\mathcal{T}_{\rm CF}e^{i(\phi_{\rm CF}+\delta_{\rm CF})},~~~~~\mathcal{A}(D\to K^{0}f)=\mathcal{T}_{\rm DCS}\,e^{i(\phi_{\rm DCS}+\delta_{\rm DCS})},

where

\begin{split}\mathcal{A}(D\rightarrow K_{S}^{0}f)&=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2(1+|\epsilon|^{2})}}\left[(1+\epsilon^{*})\,\mathcal{T}_{\rm DCS}\,e^{i(\phi_{\rm DCS}+\delta_{\rm DCS})}-(1-\epsilon^{*})\,\mathcal{T}_{\rm CF}\,e^{i(\phi_{\rm CF}+\delta_{\rm CF})}\right],\\
\mathcal{A}(D\rightarrow K_{L}^{0}f)&=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2(1+|\epsilon|^{2})}}\left[(1+\epsilon^{*})\,\mathcal{T}_{\rm DCS}\,e^{i(\phi_{\rm DCS}+\delta_{\rm DCS})}+(1-\epsilon^{*})\,\mathcal{T}_{\rm CF}\,e^{i(\phi_{\rm CF}+\delta_{\rm CF})}\right].\end{split}

For convenience, we define the ratio between the DCS and CF amplitudes as

\displaystyle R(f)

\displaystyle={|(1-\epsilon^{*})-(1+\epsilon^{*})\,r_{f}\,e^{i(\phi_{f}+\delta_{f})}|^{2}-|(1-\epsilon^{*})+(1+\epsilon^{*})\,r_{f}\,e^{i(\phi_{f}+\delta_{f})}|^{2}\over|(1-\epsilon^{*})-(1+\epsilon^{*})\,r_{f}\,e^{i(\phi_{f}+\delta_{f})}|^{2}+|(1-\epsilon^{*})+(1+\epsilon^{*})\,r_{f}\,e^{i(\phi_{f}+\delta_{f})}|^{2}}

\displaystyle=-2\,r_{f}\frac{\cos(\phi_{f}+\delta_{f})(1-|\epsilon|^{2})+2\sin(\phi_{f}+\delta_{f})Im(\epsilon)}{|1-\epsilon^{*}|^{2}+|1+\epsilon^{*}|^{2}\,r_{f}^{2}}

Amplitude decompositions in the FAT approach

The factorization-assisted topological-amplitude (FAT) approach works well for the charm decays
FAT1
;
FAT2
. It is based on the topological amplitudes according to the weak currents. There are four types of topological diagrams for the two-body nonleptonic
D
meson decays at the tree level
qaz
: the color-favored tree emission amplitude
T
, the color-suppressed tree emission amplitude
C
, the
W
-exchange amplitude
E
, and the
W
-annihilation amplitude
A
, as shown in Fig.
1
. Then the hypothesis of factorization is used, to calculate each topological
amplitude which is factorized into two parts: the short-distance Wilson coefficients and the long-distance hadronic matrix elements. The large nonperturbative and nonfactorizable contributions are parametrized to be determined by the experimental data. In this way, most
SU(3)
flavor symmetry breaking effects are included.

\displaystyle Q_{1}=\bar{u}_{\alpha}\gamma_{\mu}(1-\gamma_{5})q_{2\beta}\bar{q}_{1\beta}\gamma^{\mu}(1-\gamma_{5})c_{\alpha},~~~~~Q_{2}=\bar{u}_{\alpha}\gamma_{\mu}(1-\gamma_{5})q_{2\alpha}\bar{q}_{1\beta}\gamma^{\mu}(1-\gamma_{5})c_{\beta},

\displaystyle T[C]

\displaystyle=\frac{G_{f}}{\sqrt{2}}V_{CKM}a_{1}(\mu)[a_{2}(\mu)]f_{P_{2}}(m^{2}_{D}-m^{2}_{P_{1}})F_{0}^{D\rightarrow P_{1}}(m^{2}_{P_{2}}),

\displaystyle E

\displaystyle=\frac{G_{f}}{\sqrt{2}}V_{CKM}C_{2}(\mu)\chi^{E}_{q,s}e^{i\phi^{E}_{q,s}}f_{D}m^{2}_{D}\Big(\frac{f_{P_{1}}f_{P_{2}}}{f_{\pi}^{2}}\Big),

(21)

\displaystyle A

\displaystyle=\frac{G_{f}}{\sqrt{2}}V_{CKM}C_{1}(\mu)\chi^{A}_{q,s}e^{i\phi^{A}_{q,s}}f_{D}m^{2}_{D}\Big(\frac{f_{P_{1}}f_{P_{2}}}{f_{\pi}^{2}}\Big),

with

\displaystyle a_{1}(\mu)

\displaystyle=C_{2}(\mu)+\frac{C_{1}(\mu)}{N_{c}},\quad a_{2}(\mu)=C_{1}(\mu)+C_{2}(\mu)[\frac{1}{N_{c}}+\chi^{C}e^{i\phi^{C}}],

, to describe the

and

;

and

In the end, the major nonperturbative and nonfactorizable contributions are involved in these universal parameters, and most

Besides, the penguin contributions are not included in the CF and DCS decays, are smaller than the tree diagrams, and hence are neglected in this paper. In the following discussions, the CKM matrix elements are specified out of each topological diagram to denote the CF and DCS amplitudes. So we will use the same symbols for the topological diagrams with and without CKM matrix elements, so there will be no ambiguity.

V
Conclusions

The effect of interference between the CF and DCS amplitudes results in the
K^{0}_{S}-K^{0}_{L}
asymmetries in
D\to K^{0}_{S,L}f
decays.
We present the formulas of the
K^{0}_{S}-K^{0}_{L}
asymmetries,
R(f)
, and calculate them in the FAT approach in which significant nonperturbative effects and the
SU(3)
asymmetry breaking effects are involved. The branching fractions of the decay modes with
K_{L}^{0}
are predicted.
The results of
R(D^{0}\to K^{0}_{S,L}\pi^{0})
and
R(D^{+}\to K^{0}_{S,L}\pi^{+})
are in agreement with experimental data. We first predict the
K^{0}_{S}-K^{0}_{L}
asymmetries in the decay modes with vector mesons in the final states.
Furthermore, we first consider the effect of
D^{0}-\overline{D}^{0}
mixing in the study of
K^{0}_{S}-K^{0}_{L}
asymmetries in neutral
D

, where

D^{0}-\overline{D}^{0}
mixing parameter, with the value of
(0.62\pm 0.08)\%
and cannot be neglected. Our predictions will be tested by the future experiments with higher precision, like BESIII. Besides, we find all the
K^{0}_{S}-K^{0}_{L}
