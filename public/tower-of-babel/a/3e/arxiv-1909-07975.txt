An Elementary Proof of the Twin Prime Conjecture
Berndt

Abstract
Zusammenfassung It’s well known that every prime number p ≥ 5 p\geq 5 has the form 6 ​ k − 1 6k-1 or 6 ​ k + 1 6k+1 . We’ll call k k the generator of p p . Twin primes are distinghuished due to a common generator for each pair. Therefore it makes sense to search for the Twin Primes on the level of their generators. This paper present a new approach to prove the Twin Prime Conjecture by a sieve method to extract all Twin Primes on the level of the Twin Prime Generators. We define the ω p n \omega_{p_{n}} –numbers x x as numbers for which holds that 6 ​ x − 1 6x-1 and 6 ​ x + 1 6x+1 are coprime to the prime p n p_{n} . By dint of the average distance δ ¯ ​ ( p n ) \bar{\delta}(p_{n}) between the ω p n \omega_{p_{n}} –numbers we can prove the Twin Prime Conjecture indirectly.
