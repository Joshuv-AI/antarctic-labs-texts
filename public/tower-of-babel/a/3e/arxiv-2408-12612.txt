(53)

We also define

f_{2}\equiv\frac{\partial f}{\partial z_{2}}\text{, }f_{22}\equiv\frac{\partial^{2}f}{\partial z_{2}^{2}}\text{, }\tilde{f}=f\left(\tilde{z}_{2},\cdots,\tilde{z}_{n-2}\right)\text{, }\tilde{f}_{22}=\left.\frac{\partial^{2}f}{\partial z_{2}^{2}}\right|_{\left(\tilde{z}_{2},\cdots,\tilde{z}_{n-2}\right)}.

\displaystyle=\left(\frac{k_{12}}{M}\right)^{2m+q}\left(-\sum_{i\neq 2,n}\frac{k_{i}^{T}}{\tilde{x}_{i}-\tilde{x}_{2}}\right)^{N-2m-2q}\left(2m\right)!\left(\tilde{f}_{22}\right)^{q+2m}

\displaystyle=\left(\frac{k_{12}}{M}\right)^{2m+q}\left(\tilde{k}^{T}\right)^{N-2m-2q}\left(2m\right)!\left(\tilde{f}_{22}\right)^{q+2m}

(55)

Finally, with the saddle point, we can calculate the
HSSA
to be

\displaystyle\mathcal{T}^{\left(N,2m,2q\right)}

\displaystyle=\left(\prod_{i=3}^{n-2}\int_{0}^{1}dz_{i}\text{ }z_{i}^{i-2-N}\right)\int_{0}^{1}dz_{2}\left(\frac{\partial^{2m}\tilde{u}}{\partial z_{2}^{2m}}\frac{\left(z_{2}-\tilde{z}_{2}\right)^{2m}}{\left(2m\right)!}\right)e^{-Kf}

(56)

\displaystyle\simeq\frac{1}{\left(2m\right)!}\frac{\partial^{2m}\tilde{u}}{\partial z_{2}^{2m}}\left(\prod_{i=3}^{n-2}\tilde{z}_{i}^{i-2-N}\right)\int_{0}^{1}dz_{2}\left(z_{2}-\tilde{z}_{2}\right)^{2m}e^{-Kf\left(z_{2}\right)}

(57)

\displaystyle\simeq\frac{1}{\left(2m\right)!}\frac{\partial^{2m}\tilde{u}}{\partial z_{2}^{2m}}\left(\prod_{i=3}^{n-2}\tilde{z}_{i}^{i-2-N}\right)\int_{0}^{\infty}dz_{2}\left(z_{2}-\tilde{z}_{2}\right)^{2m}e^{-Kf\left(z_{2}\right)}

(58)

\displaystyle=\frac{2\sqrt{\pi}}{m!}\left(\prod_{i=3}^{n-2}\tilde{z}_{i}^{i-2-N}\right)\frac{e^{-K\tilde{f}}}{\left|\tilde{k}\right|^{2m+1}}\left.\frac{\partial^{2m}u}{\partial z_{2}^{2m}}\right|_{z_{i}=\tilde{z}_{i}}

\displaystyle=2\sqrt{\pi}e^{-K\tilde{f}}\left|\tilde{k}\right|^{N-1}\left(\prod_{i=3}^{n-2}\tilde{z}_{i}^{i-2-N}\right)\frac{\left(2m\right)!}{m!}\left(\frac{-1}{2M}\right)^{2m+q}\left(\frac{2K\tilde{f}_{22}}{\left(\sum_{i\neq 2,n}\frac{k_{i}^{T}}{\tilde{x}_{i}-\tilde{x}_{2}}\right)^{2}}\right)^{m+q}

where

.

IV.1
Bjorken Scaling

These stringy scaling behaviors are reminiscent of deep
inelastic
scattering of electron and proton
where the
two structure functions

and
W_{2}(Q^{2},\nu)
scale, and become not functions of

2
kinematics variables
Q^{2}
and
\nu
independently but only of their

reduces
from
2
to

.

That is, the structure functions scale as

MW_{1}(Q^{2},\nu)\rightarrow F_{1}(x),\text{ \ \ }\nu W_{2}(Q^{2},\nu)\rightarrow F_{2}(x)

(79)

where
x
is the
Bjorken variable
and
M
is the proton mass.

Moreover, due to the spin-
\frac{1}{2}
assumption of quark,
Callan
and Gross
derived the relation

2xF_{1}(x)=F_{2}(x).

(80)

,

,
