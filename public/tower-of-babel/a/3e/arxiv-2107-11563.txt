For any graph

For two vertices

The

of a vertex

The maximum and minimum degree of graph

is denoted by
\Delta(G)
and
\delta(G),
respectively. An
edge-signing
is an
edge-weighted graph obtained by signing
-1
and
1
to each edge of

G.
In this case, the sign of the edge

For the specific sign

of

The

\{\lambda_{i}\}(1\leq i\leq n,n=|V(G)|)
is the multiset of the eigenvalues of
A^{\sigma}.
In addition, define

\rho(G^{\sigma})
as the maximum value of

where

A graph is called
