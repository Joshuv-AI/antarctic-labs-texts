Hecke Action on Tamely Ramified Eisenstein Series over ℙ 1 \mathbb{P}^{1}
Tahsin Saffat

Abstract
. We study the space of automorphic functions for the rational function field 𝔽 q ​ ( t ) \mathbb{F}_{q}(t) tamely ramified at three places. Eisenstein series are functions induced from the maximal torus. The space of Eisenstein series generates a trimodule for the affine Hecke algebra. We conjecture a generators and relations description of this module and prove the conjecture when G = PGL ⁡ ( 2 ) G=\mathrm{PGL}(2) and SL ⁡ ( 3 ) \mathrm{SL}(3) .
