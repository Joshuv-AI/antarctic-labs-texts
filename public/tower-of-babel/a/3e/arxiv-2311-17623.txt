Supremal inequalities for convex M-estimators with applications to complete and quick convergence
Dietmar Ferger

Abstract
Let m ^ n \hat{m}_{n} be an M M -estimator for a parameter m m based on a sample of size n ∈ ℕ n\in\mathbb{N} . We derive exponential and polynomial upper bounds for the tail-probabilities of sup k ≥ n | m ^ k − m | \sup_{k\geq n}|\hat{m}_{k}-m| according as a boundedness- or a moment-condition is fulfilled. This enables us to derive rates of r r -complete convergence and also to show r r -qick convergence in the sense of Strasser.

Exponential tail-bounds

In order to derive exponential inequalities for the tail-probabilities of the suprema we need that the following boundedness-assumption is fulfilled:
For every
x\neq 0
there exist reals
a(x)<b(x)
such that

a(x)\leq D^{+}h(X_{i},m+x)\leq b(x)\text{ a.s. for all }1\leq i\leq n.

(5)

Let
D^{+}f
and

.
It is well known that

D^{-}M(m)\leq 0\leq D^{+}M(m).

(6)

Confer Corollary 7.2 of Ferger

(7)

\displaystyle\mathbb{P}(\inf_{k\geq n}(\hat{m}_{k}-m)<-x)\leq\exp\{-2(b(-x)-a(-x))^{-2}nD^{+}M(m-x)^{2}\}\quad\forall\;x>0.
