Strict comparison and stable rank one
Huaxin Lin

Abstract
Let A A be a σ \sigma -unital finite simple C ∗ C^{*} -algebra which has strict comparison property. We show that if the canonical map Γ \Gamma from the Cuntz semigroup to certain lower semi-continuous affine functions is surjective, then A A has tracial approximate oscillation zero and stable rank one. Equivalently, if A A has an almost unperforated and almost divisible Cuntz semigroup, then A A has stable rank one and tracial approximate oscillation zero.

Let

A

It turns out that the only unital

If

of separable amenable simple

-algebras is mainly for those

-stable

-algebras.

Suppose that
A
is a unital separable simple
C^{*}
-algebra  with non-trivial 2-quasi-traces.
Let

In the case that

the algebra of

is the tracial state of

is
just the normalized rank of

Denote by

the set of all normalized 2-quasi-traces of

For each

where

We say that

C^{*}

-algebras.

(see

,

Return to the

-algebra

The notion of stable rank was introduced to

Let

\sigma

This paper is organized as follows. Section 2 is a preliminary.

(1)
