Blocks whose defect groups are Suzuki 2 2 -groups
Charles W. Eaton

Abstract
We classify up to Morita equivalence all blocks whose defect groups are Suzuki 2 2 -groups. The classification holds for blocks over a suitable discrete valuation ring as well as for those over an algebraically closed field, and in fact holds up to basic Morita equivalence. As a consequence Donovan’s conjecture holds for Suzuki 2 2 -groups. A corollary of the proof is that Suzuki Sylow 2 2 -subgroups of finite groups with no nontrivial odd order normal subgroup are trivial intersection. Keywords: Morita equivalence; finite groups; block theory; Suzuki groups; Donovan’s conjecture

Introduction

Let
p
be a prime and

[

]

]

Following

24
]
a Suzuki

-group is a non-abelian

-group

Donovan’s conjecture holds for Suzuki
2
-groups. In fact, there are only finitely many of basic Morita equivalence classes of blocks with defect group a given Suzuki
