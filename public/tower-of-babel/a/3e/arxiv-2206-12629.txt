Lepton mass matrix from double covering of A 4 A_{4} modular flavor symmetry
Hiroshi Okada, Yuta Orikasa

Abstract
We study a double covering of modular A 4 A_{4} flavor symmetry in which we construct lepton models in cases of canonical seesaw and radiative seesaw models. Through χ \chi square numerical analysis, we show some predictions for the cases, depending on normal and inverted hierarchies.
