Abstract
. For a proper submodule N N of a finitely generated module M M over a Noetherian ring, the product of prime ideals which occur in a regular prime extension filtration of M M over N N is defined as its generalized prime ideal factorization in M M . In this article, we find conditions for a product of prime ideals to be the generalized prime ideal factorization of a submodule of some module. We show that a power of a prime ideal occurs in a generalized prime ideal factorization only if it is not equal to its lesser powers. Also, we show that 𝔭 1 r 1 ⋯ 𝔭 n r n {{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}}^{r_{1}}\cdots{{\mathfrak{p}}_{n}}^{r_{n}} is a generalized prime ideal factorization if and only if for each 1 ≤ i ≤ n 1\leq i\leq n , 𝔭 i r i {{\mathfrak{p}}_{i}}^{r_{i}} is the generalized prime ideal factorization of some submodule of a module.



1.
Introduction

Throughout this article
R

Let

-module

is a submodule of

such that

is a
\mathfrak{p}
-prime submodule of

, then we say

is a
\mathfrak{p}
-prime extension of
N
in

and denote it as
N\overset{\mathfrak{p}}{\subset}K
. In this case,
\mathrm{Ass}(K/N)=\{\mathfrak{p}\}

[
4
, Theorem 1]
. A
\mathfrak{p}
-prime extension

of

is said to be maximal in

if there is no
\mathfrak{p}
-prime extension

of
N
in

-prime extension of

[

\mathfrak{p}
-prime extension of

in

A filtration of submodules
\mathcal{F}:N=M_{0}\overset{{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}}{\subset}M_{1}\subset\cdots\overset{{\mathfrak{p}}_{n}}{\subset}M_{n}=M
is called a regular prime extension (RPE) filtration of

-prime extension of

[
2
]
and it is also noted that RPE filtrations are weak prime decompositions defined by Dress in
[
1
]
. The set of prime ideals which occur in the RPE filtration

is precisely
\mathrm{Ass}(M/N)

Lemma 1.1
.

[
2
, Proposition 14]

Let
N=M_{0}\overset{{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}}{\subset}M_{1}\subset\cdots\overset{{\mathfrak{p}}_{n}}{\subset}M_{n}=M
be a filtration of submodules such that each
M_{i-1}\overset{{\mathfrak{p}}_{i}}{\subset}M_{i}
is a maximal
{\mathfrak{p}}_{i}
-prime extension. Then
\mathrm{Ass}(M/M_{i-1})=\{{\mathfrak{p}}_{i},\ldots,{\mathfrak{p}}_{n}\}
for
1\leq i\leq n
. In particular,
\mathrm{Ass}(M/N)=\{{\mathfrak{p}}_{1},\ldots,{\mathfrak{p}}_{n}\}
.

The submodules occurring in an RPE filtration are characterized as below.

Lemma 1.2
.

[
3
, Lemma 3.1]

Let
N
be a proper submodule of an
R
-module
M
. If
N=M_{0}\overset{{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}}{\subset}M_{1}\subset\cdots\overset{{\mathfrak{p}}_{n}}{\subset}M_{n}=M
is an RPE filtration of

M_{i}=\{x\in M\mid{{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}}\cdots{{\mathfrak{p}}_{i}}x\subseteq N\}=(N:{{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}}\cdots{{\mathfrak{p}}_{i}})

.

It is proved that the number of times a prime ideal occurs in any RPE filtration of

is unique
[
2
, Theorem 22]
. So if

{{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}}\cdots{{\mathfrak{p}}_{n}}
is uniquely defined for

in

and it is called the generalized prime ideal factorization of

. Generalized prime ideal factorization of submodules is defined and its various properties are studied in

]

In general, a product of prime ideals need not be a generalized prime ideal factorization of some submodule of a module.

Example 1.3
.

Let
R=\frac{k[x,y,z]}{(xy-z^{2},x^{2}-yz)}

\mathfrak{p}
be the prime ideal
(\overline{x},\overline{z})

-module

and a submodule

In this article, for a product of prime ideals

2.
Conditions for ideals to be generalized prime ideal factorization of submodules

We need the following lemmas.

Lemma 2.1
.

Let
N
be a proper submodule of an
R
-module
M
. If a submodule
K
of
M
lies in an RPE filtration of
M
over
N
, then
{\mathcal{P}}_{M}(N)={\mathcal{P}}_{M}(K){\mathcal{P}}_{K}(N)
.

Proof.

If a submodule
K
of
M
lies in an RPE filtration of
M
over
N
, then we have an RPE filtration

N=N_{0}\overset{{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}}{\subset}N_{1}\subset\cdots\overset{{\mathfrak{p}}_{r}}{\subset}N_{r}\overset{{\mathfrak{p}}_{r+1}}{\subset}\cdots\overset{{\mathfrak{p}}_{n}}{\subset}N_{n}=M,

where
N_{r}=K
for some
r
. So
{\mathcal{P}}_{M}(N)={{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}}\cdots{{\mathfrak{p}}_{n}}
. Then

N=N_{0}\overset{{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}}{\subset}N_{1}\subset\cdots\overset{{\mathfrak{p}}_{r}}{\subset}N_{r}=K\text{ and }K=N_{r}\overset{{\mathfrak{p}}_{r+1}}{\subset}N_{r+1}\subset\cdots\overset{{\mathfrak{p}}_{n}}{\subset}N_{n}=M

are RPE filtrations, and therefore,
{\mathcal{P}}_{K}(N)={{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}}\cdots{{\mathfrak{p}}_{r}}
and
{\mathcal{P}}_{M}(K)={\mathfrak{p}}_{r+1}\cdots{\mathfrak{p}}_{n}
. This proves the lemma.
∎

Lemma 2.2
.

[
2
, Lemma 20]

Let
N
be a proper submodule of
M
and
N=M_{0}\subset\cdots\subset M_{i-1}\overset{{\mathfrak{p}}_{i}}{\subset}M_{i}\overset{{\mathfrak{p}}_{i+1}}{\subset}M_{i+1}\subset\cdots\subset M_{n}=M
be an RPE filtration of
M
over
N
. If
{\mathfrak{p}}_{i+1}\not\subseteq{\mathfrak{p}}_{i}
for some
i
, then there exists a submodule
K_{i}
of
M
such that
N=M_{0}\subset\cdots\subset M_{i-1}\overset{{\mathfrak{p}}_{i+1}}{\subset}K_{i}\overset{{\mathfrak{p}}_{i}}{\subset}M_{i+1}\subset\cdots\subset M_{n}

=

M
is an RPE filtration of
M
over
N
.

Remark 2.3
.

Suppose a product of prime ideals
{{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}}\cdots{{\mathfrak{p}}_{r}}
is
{\mathcal{P}}_{M}(N)
for some module
M
and a submodule
N
of
M
. For every reordering of
{{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}},\dots,{{\mathfrak{p}}_{r}}
with
{\mathfrak{p}}_{i}\not\subset{\mathfrak{p}}_{j}
for
i<j
, by applying Lemma
2.2
for a sufficient number of times, we can get an RPE filtration
N\overset{{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}}{\subset}N_{1}\overset{{\mathfrak{p}}_{2}}{\subset}N_{2}\subset\cdots\subset N_{r-1}\overset{{\mathfrak{p}}_{r}}{\subset}N_{r}=M
.

Proposition 2.4
.

For a prime ideal
\,\mathfrak{p}
and a positive integer
r
, if
\,{\mathfrak{p}}^{r}
is the generalized prime ideal factorization of a submodule of an
R
-module, then
{\mathfrak{p}}^{r}\neq{\mathfrak{p}}^{r-1}
.

Proof.

Suppose
M
is an
R
-module and
N
is a submodule of
M
with
{\mathcal{P}}_{M}(N)={\mathfrak{p}}^{r}
. Then we have an RPE filtration

N\overset{\mathfrak{p}}{\subset}N_{1}\overset{\mathfrak{p}}{\subset}N_{2}\subset\cdots\subset N_{r-1}\overset{\mathfrak{p}}{\subset}N_{r}=M.

If
{\mathfrak{p}}^{r}={\mathfrak{p}}^{r-1}
, then by Lemma
1.2
,
M=N_{r}=\{x\in M\mid{\mathfrak{p}}^{r}x\subseteq N\}=\{x\in M\mid{\mathfrak{p}}^{r-1}x\subseteq N\}=N_{r-1}
, a contradiction.
∎

Example
1.3
shows that the converse of the above result is not true.

Corollary 2.5
.

Let
\mathfrak{p}
be a prime ideal in
R
and
r
be a positive integer. Then
{\mathcal{P}}_{R}({{\mathfrak{p}}}^{r})={{\mathfrak{p}}}^{r}
if and only if
{\mathfrak{p}}^{r}\neq{\mathfrak{p}}^{r-1}
and
\mathrm{Ass}(R/{{\mathfrak{p}}^{r}})=\{\mathfrak{p}\}
.

Proof.

If
{\mathfrak{p}}^{r}\neq{\mathfrak{p}}^{r-1}
and
\mathrm{Ass}(R/{{\mathfrak{p}}^{r}})=\{\mathfrak{p}\}
, then by
[
5
, Corollary 2.16]
,
{\mathcal{P}}_{R}({{\mathfrak{p}}}^{r})={{\mathfrak{p}}}^{r}
. Conversely if
{\mathcal{P}}_{R}({{\mathfrak{p}}}^{r})={{\mathfrak{p}}}^{r}
, then we have an RPE filtration
{{\mathfrak{p}}}^{r}\overset{\mathfrak{p}}{\subset}{\mathfrak{a}}_{1}\overset{\mathfrak{p}}{\subset}{\mathfrak{a}}_{2}\subset\cdots\subset{\mathfrak{a}}_{r-1}\overset{\mathfrak{p}}{\subset}{\mathfrak{a}}_{r}=R
. By Lemma
1.1
,
\mathrm{Ass}(R/{{\mathfrak{p}}^{r}})=\{\mathfrak{p}\}
and by Proposition
2.4
,
{\mathfrak{p}}^{r}\neq{\mathfrak{p}}^{r-1}
.
∎

Corollary 2.6
.

Let
{\mathfrak{p}}_{1},\dots,{\mathfrak{p}}_{n}
be distinct prime ideals in

and

be positive integers. If there exists an

-module

Reorder

of

Theorem 2.7

Let
{\mathfrak{p}}_{1},\dots,{\mathfrak{p}}_{n}

be positive integers. If there exists an

Let

\{{\mathfrak{p}}_{1},\dots,{\mathfrak{p}}_{n}\}

\overset{{\mathfrak{p}}_{n-1}}{\subset}L^{(n-1)}_{r_{n-1}}\overset{{\mathfrak{p}}_{n}}{\subset}L^{(n)}_{1}\overset{{\mathfrak{p}}_{n}}{\subset}L^{(n)}_{2}\subset\cdots\overset{{\mathfrak{p}}_{n}}{\subset}L^{(n)}_{r_{n}-1}\overset{{\mathfrak{p}}_{n}}{\subset}L^{(n)}_{r_{n}}=M

of

over

. Then for every

{{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}}^{r_{1}}\cdots{{\mathfrak{p}}_{n}}^{r_{n}}x\subseteq N
[Lemma

{{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}}^{r_{1}}\cdots{{\mathfrak{p}}_{n-1}}^{r_{n-1}}{{\mathfrak{p}}_{n}}^{r_{n}-1}={{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}}^{r_{1}}\cdots{{\mathfrak{p}}_{n}}^{r_{n}}

{{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}}^{r_{1}}\cdots{{\mathfrak{p}}_{n-1}}^{r_{n-1}}

{{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}}^{r_{1}}\cdots{{\mathfrak{p}}_{i}}^{r_{i}-1}\cdots{{\mathfrak{p}}_{n}}^{r_{n}}\neq{{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}}^{r_{1}}\cdots{{\mathfrak{p}}_{n}}^{r_{n}}

The above result is not true if

The next lemma is about the regular prime extensions on direct sums.

Lemma 2.9
.

Let
N
,
N^{\prime}
be submodules of modules
M
,
M^{\prime}
respectively and

{\mathfrak{p}}
-prime extension in

(i)

If

-prime extension in

{\mathfrak{p}}
-prime extension in

{\mathfrak{p}}
-prime extension in

.

Proof.

(i)
We have

. Therefore,

. Then

and

. This implies

. Since

and

is a
{\mathfrak{p}}
-prime extension in

, we get

. Hence,

.

Let

such that

. Suppose

. Then either

or

. If

, since

is a
{\mathfrak{p}}
-prime extension, we get

. If

is a
{\mathfrak{p}}

-prime extension in

.

. Let

. Then for every

, and therefore,

. Therefore,

\mathfrak{p}
-prime extension of

(ii)

-prime extension in

. Hence,
N\oplus N^{\prime}\overset{\mathfrak{p}}{\subset}K\oplus N^{\prime}
is a regular
{\mathfrak{p}}
-prime extension in
M\oplus M^{\prime}
.
∎

Lemma 2.10
.

Let
N_{1},\dots,N_{n}
be submodules of
R
-modules
M_{1},\dots,M_{n}
respectively and let
{\mathfrak{p}}
be a maximal element in
\cup_{i=1}^{n}\mathrm{Ass}(M_{i}/N_{i})
. For
1\leq i\leq n
, let
K_{i}
be the regular
{\mathfrak{p}}
-prime extension of
N_{i}
in
M_{i}
if
\mathfrak{p}\in\mathrm{Ass}(M_{i}/N_{i})
and
K_{i}=N_{i}
otherwise. Then
{\oplus_{i=1}^{n}{N}_{i}}\overset{\mathfrak{p}}{\subset}{\oplus_{i=1}^{n}{K}_{i}}
is a regular
{\mathfrak{p}}
-prime extension in
{\oplus_{i=1}^{n}{M}_{i}}
.

Proof.

Since
\mathrm{Ass}\Big({\dfrac{\oplus_{i=1}^{n}{M}_{i}}{\oplus_{i=1}^{n}{N}_{i}}}\Big)=\bigcup\limits_{i=1}^{n}{\mathrm{Ass}\Big(\dfrac{M_{i}}{N_{i}}\Big)}
the proof follows by induction on
n
using Lemma
2.9
.
∎

Proposition 2.11
.

Let
N
,
N^{\prime}
be proper submodules of modules
M
,
M^{\prime}
respectively. If
{\mathcal{P}}_{M}(N)={{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}}^{r_{1}}\cdots{{\mathfrak{p}}_{k}}^{r_{k}}
and
{\mathcal{P}}_{M^{\prime}}(N^{\prime})={{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}}^{s_{1}}\cdots{{\mathfrak{p}}_{k}}^{s_{k}}
for some non-negative integers
r_{i}
and
s_{i}
with either
r_{i}>0
or
s_{i}>0
, then
{\mathcal{P}}_{M\oplus M^{\prime}}(N\oplus N^{\prime})={{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}}^{t_{1}}\cdots{{\mathfrak{p}}_{k}}^{t_{k}}
, where
t_{i}=\max\{r_{i},s_{i}\}
for
i=1,\dots,k
.

Proof.

We prove by induction on
k
. If
{\mathcal{P}}_{M}(N)={\mathfrak{p}}^{r}
and
{\mathcal{P}}_{M^{\prime}}(N^{\prime})={\mathfrak{p}}^{s}
for some prime ideal
\mathfrak{p}
. Without loss of generality, we assume
r\geq s
. Then we have RPE filtrations

N\overset{\mathfrak{p}}{\subset}N_{1}\overset{\mathfrak{p}}{\subset}N_{2}\subset\cdots\subset N_{s}\overset{\mathfrak{p}}{\subset}N_{s+1}\subset\cdots\subset N_{r}=M

N^{\prime}\overset{\mathfrak{p}}{\subset}N_{1}^{\prime}\overset{\mathfrak{p}}{\subset}N_{2}^{\prime}\subset\cdots\subset N_{s}^{\prime}=M^{\prime}.

Then by Lemma
2.9
,

N\oplus N^{\prime}\overset{\mathfrak{p}}{\subset}N_{1}\oplus N_{1}^{\prime}\overset{\mathfrak{p}}{\subset}N_{2}\oplus N_{2}^{\prime}\overset{\mathfrak{p}}{\subset}\cdots\overset{\mathfrak{p}}{\subset}N_{s}\oplus N_{s}^{\prime}=N_{s}\oplus M^{\prime}\overset{\mathfrak{p}}{\subset}N_{s+1}\oplus M^{\prime}\\
\overset{\mathfrak{p}}{\subset}\cdots\overset{\mathfrak{p}}{\subset}N_{r}\oplus M^{\prime}=M\oplus M^{\prime}

is an RPE filtration. Therefore,
{\mathcal{P}}_{M\oplus M^{\prime}}(N\oplus N^{\prime})={\mathfrak{p}}^{r}
. Hence, the result is true for
k=1
.

Now let
k>1
. By reordering
{{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}},\dots,{{\mathfrak{p}}_{k}}
we assume that
{\mathfrak{p}}_{i}\not\subset{\mathfrak{p}}_{j}
whenever
i<j
. Then
{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}
is maximal in
\mathrm{Ass}(M/N)\cup\mathrm{Ass}(M^{\prime}/N^{\prime})
. By Remark
2.3
, there exists RPE filtrations

N\overset{{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}}{\subset}N_{1}\overset{{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}}{\subset}N_{2}\subset\cdots\overset{{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}}{\subset}N_{r_{1}}\overset{{\mathfrak{p}}_{2}}{\subset}K_{1}\subset\cdots\subset M

N^{\prime}\overset{{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}}{\subset}N_{1}^{\prime}\overset{{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}}{\subset}N_{2}^{\prime}\subset\cdots\overset{{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}}{\subset}N_{s_{1}}^{\prime}\overset{{\mathfrak{p}}_{2}}{\subset}K_{1}^{\prime}\subset\cdots\subset M^{\prime}.

So we have
{\mathcal{P}}_{M}(N_{r_{1}})={{\mathfrak{p}}_{2}}^{r_{2}}\cdots{{\mathfrak{p}}_{k}}^{r_{k}}
and
{\mathcal{P}}_{M^{\prime}}(N_{s_{1}}^{\prime})={{\mathfrak{p}}_{2}}^{s_{2}}\cdots{{\mathfrak{p}}_{k}}^{s_{k}}
. By induction assumption,
{\mathcal{P}}_{M\oplus M^{\prime}}(N_{r_{1}}\oplus N_{s_{1}}^{\prime})={{\mathfrak{p}}_{2}}^{t_{2}}\cdots{{\mathfrak{p}}_{k}}^{t_{k}}
, where
t_{i}=\max\{r_{i},s_{i}\}
for
i=2,\dots,k
. By the previous paragraph, we have
{\mathcal{P}}_{N_{r_{1}}\oplus N_{s_{1}}^{\prime}}(N\oplus N^{\prime})={{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}}^{t_{1}}
, where
t_{1}=\max\{r_{1},s_{1}\}
. Then by Lemma
2.10
,
N_{r_{1}}\oplus N_{s_{1}}^{\prime}
lies in an RPE filtration of
M\oplus M^{\prime}
over
N\oplus N^{\prime}
. So by Lemma
2.1
, we get
{\mathcal{P}}_{M\oplus M^{\prime}}(N\oplus N^{\prime})={\mathcal{P}}_{M\oplus M^{\prime}}(N_{r_{1}}\oplus N_{s_{1}}^{\prime})

{\mathcal{P}}_{N_{r_{1}}\oplus N_{s_{1}}^{\prime}}(N\oplus N^{\prime})={{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}}^{t_{1}}\cdots{{\mathfrak{p}}_{k}}^{t_{k}}
.
∎

Using induction, we have the following theorem.

Theorem 2.12
.

Let
N_{1},\dots,N_{n}
be proper submodules of
R
-modules
M_{1},\dots,M_{n}
respectively. If
{\mathcal{P}}_{M_{i}}(N_{i})={{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}}^{r_{i_{1}}}\cdots{{\mathfrak{p}}_{k}}^{r_{i_{k}}}
for
i=1,\dots,n
and
r_{i_{j}}\geq 0
, then
{\mathcal{P}}_{\oplus_{i=1}^{n}{M}_{i}}(\bigoplus\limits_{i=1}^{n}{N}_{i})={{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}}^{s_{1}}\cdots{{\mathfrak{p}}_{k}}^{s_{k}}
, where
s_{j}=\max\{r_{1_{j}},\dots,r_{n_{j}}\}
for
j=1,\dots,k
.

Now we get the following sufficient condition for the existence of an
R
-module
M
and a submodule
N
of
M
with
{\mathcal{P}}_{M}(N)={{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}}^{r_{1}}\cdots{{\mathfrak{p}}_{n}}^{r_{n}}
.

Corollary 2.13
.

Let
{\mathfrak{p}}_{1},\dots,{\mathfrak{p}}_{n}
be distinct prime ideals in
R
and
r_{1},\dots,r_{n}
be positive integers. If
{{\mathfrak{p}}_{i}}^{r_{i}}\neq{{\mathfrak{p}}_{i}}^{r_{i}-1}
and
\mathrm{Ass}(R/{{\mathfrak{p}}_{i}}^{r_{i}})=\{{{\mathfrak{p}}_{i}}\}
for every
1\leq i\leq n
, then
{\mathcal{P}}_{R^{n}}({{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}}^{r_{1}}\oplus\cdots\oplus{{\mathfrak{p}}_{n}}^{r_{n}})={{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}}^{r_{1}}\cdots{{\mathfrak{p}}_{n}}^{r_{n}}
.

Proof.

By Corollary
2.5
,
{\mathcal{P}}_{R}({{\mathfrak{p}}_{i}}^{r_{i}})={{\mathfrak{p}}_{i}}^{r_{i}}
for every
1\leq i\leq n
. Then by Theorem
2.12
,
{\mathcal{P}}_{R^{n}}({{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}}^{r_{1}}\oplus\cdots\oplus{{\mathfrak{p}}_{n}}^{r_{n}})={{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}}^{r_{1}}\cdots{{\mathfrak{p}}_{n}}^{r_{n}}
.
∎

The next theorem gives a necessary and sufficient condition for the existence of an
R
-module
M
with a submodule
N
such that
{\mathcal{P}}_{M}(N)={{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}}^{r_{1}}\cdots{{\mathfrak{p}}_{n}}^{r_{n}}
.

Theorem 2.14
.

Let
{\mathfrak{p}}_{1},\dots,{\mathfrak{p}}_{n}
be distinct prime ideals in
R
and
r_{1},\dots,r_{n}
be positive integers. There exists an
R
-module
M
and a submodule
N
of
M
with
{\mathcal{P}}_{M}(N)={{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}}^{r_{1}}\cdots{{\mathfrak{p}}_{n}}^{r_{n}}
if and only if there exist
R
-modules
M_{i}
and submodules
N_{i}
of
M_{i}
such that
{\mathcal{P}}_{M_{i}}(N_{i})={{\mathfrak{p}}_{i}}^{r_{i}}
,
1\leq i\leq n
.

Proof.

If there exist
R
-modules
M_{i}
and submodules
N_{i}
of
M_{i}
such that
{\mathcal{P}}_{M_{i}}(N_{i})={{\mathfrak{p}}_{i}}^{r_{i}}
,
1\leq i\leq n
, then by Theorem
2.12
, for
N={\oplus_{i=1}^{n}{N}_{i}}
and
M={\oplus_{i=1}^{n}{M}_{i}}
we have
{\mathcal{P}}_{M}(N)={{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}}^{r_{1}}\cdots{{\mathfrak{p}}_{n}}^{r_{n}}
.

Conversely if there exists an
R
-module
M
and a submodule
N
of
M
with
{\mathcal{P}}_{M}(N)={{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}}^{r_{1}}\cdots{{\mathfrak{p}}_{n}}^{r_{n}}
, then taking
M_{i}=L^{(i)}_{r_{i}}
and
N_{i}=L^{(i-1)}_{r_{i-1}}
in the RPE filtration

N=L^{(0)}\overset{{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}}{\subset}L^{(1)}_{1}\overset{{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}}{\subset}L^{(1)}_{2}\overset{{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}}{\subset}\cdots\overset{{\mathfrak{p}}_{1}}{\subset}L^{(1)}_{r_{1}}\overset{{\mathfrak{p}}_{2}}{\subset}L^{(2)}_{1}\subset\cdots\\
\overset{{\mathfrak{p}}_{n-1}}{\subset}L^{(n-1)}_{r_{n-1}}\overset{{\mathfrak{p}}_{n}}{\subset}L^{(n)}_{1}\overset{{\mathfrak{p}}_{n}}{\subset}L^{(n)}_{2}\subset\cdots\overset{{\mathfrak{p}}_{n}}{\subset}L^{(n)}_{r_{n}}=M,

we get
{\mathcal{P}}_{M_{i}}(N_{i})={{\mathfrak{p}}_{i}}^{r_{i}}
,
1\leq i\leq n
.
∎
