additive bases of abelian groups of rank 2
Weidong Gao, Yuanlin Li, Yongke Qu, Qinghong Wang

Abstract
. Let G G be a finite abelian group and p p be the smallest prime dividing | G | |G| . Let S S be a sequence over G G . We say that S S is regular if for every proper subgroup H ⊊ G H\subsetneq G , S S contains at most | H | − 1 |H|-1 terms from H H . Let 𝖼 0 ​ ( G ) \mathsf{c}_{0}(G) be the smallest integer t t such that every regular sequence S S over G G of length | S | ≥ t |S|\geq t forms an additive basis of G G , i.e., ∑ ( S ) = G \sum(S)=G . Lemma 3.1
.

Let

We proceed by induction on

|\sum(B_{0})|\geq p|B_{0}|+1

Next, let

or

Lemma 3.2

Let

\sum\nolimits_{0}(T)=\{{g_{1}},0\}+\cdots+\{{g_{k}},0\}.

|\langle\supp(T_{1}),\supp(S_{1})\rangle/\langle\mbox{supp}(S_{1})\rangle|\geq 2p.

So we can always choose a subsequence
T_{1}|S\bm{\cdot}S_{1}^{[-1]}
such that

|\langle\supp(T_{1}),\supp(S_{1})\rangle/\langle\mbox{supp}(S_{1})\rangle|\geq 2p
and
|T_{1}|=2p-1
.

Finally, we verify Property
(3)
holds. Let
S_{1}^{\prime}|S_{1}
be a subsequence of
S_{1}
. Then
\langle\mbox{supp}(S_{1}^{\prime})\rangle\leq\langle\supp(S_{1})\rangle
and thus

|\langle\supp(T_{1}),\supp(S_{1}^{\prime})\rangle/\langle\mbox{supp}(S_{1}^{\prime})|\geq|\langle\supp(T_{1}),\supp(S_{1})\rangle/\langle\mbox{supp}(S_{1})\rangle|\geq 2p
.

Note that
g\notin\langle\mbox{supp}(S_{1})\rangle
for every
g|T_{1}
. Thus
g\notin\langle\mbox{supp}(S_{1}^{\prime})\rangle
for every
g|T_{1}
. By Corollary
3.3
(2) and Lemma
2.3
(2), we have
|\sum_{0}(T_{1}\bm{\cdot}S_{1}^{\prime})|\geq(|T_{1}|+1)|\sum_{0}(S_{1}^{\prime})|\geq 2p(|S_{1}^{\prime}|+1)
, implying Property
(3)
. In summary, we have found a subsequence
T_{1}
(together with
S_{1}
) satisfying all three properties. This completes the proof of
Claim
.

Next we distinguish the remaining proof into the following two cases according to the value of
\mathsf{h}(S^{\prime})
.

Case 1.

\mathsf{h}(S^{\prime})\leq 2p-2
.

Let
t\geq 0
be the maximal integer such that
S^{\prime}
has a factorization

S^{\prime}=A_{1}\bm{\cdot}\ldots\bm{\cdot}A_{t}\bm{\cdot}T

with
A_{i}\subseteq G
,
|A_{i}|\leq 6p+1
and
|\sum_{0}(A_{i})|\geq p|A_{i}|+2
. By the maximality of
t
and Lemma
3.1
, we have
|\mbox{supp}(T)|\leq 6p(p+1)
. Thus
|T|\leq|\mbox{supp}(T)|\cdot\mathsf{h}(S^{\prime})\leq 12p(p^{2}-1)
. Since
|T_{i}\bm{\cdot}S_{i}|\geq 4p-2
for each
i\in[1,\lambda]
, we have
\lambda\leq\frac{|S|-|S^{\prime}|}{4p-2}
. Similarly,
t\geq\frac{|S^{\prime}|-|T|}{6p+1}
. By Lemma
2.3
(2),
|\sum\nolimits_{0}(T)|\geq|T|+1
. Since
{\rm st}(\sum\nolimits_{0}(S))=\{0\}
, by Lemma
2.1
, we have

\displaystyle|\sum\nolimits_{0}(S)|=

\displaystyle|\sum_{i=1}^{\lambda}\sum\nolimits_{0}(T_{i}\bm{\cdot}S_{i})+\sum_{j=1}^{t}\sum\nolimits_{0}(A_{j})+\sum\nolimits_{0}(T)|

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{\lambda}(|\sum\nolimits_{0}(T_{i}\bm{\cdot}S_{i})|-1)+\sum_{j=1}^{t}(|\sum\nolimits_{0}(A_{j})|-1)+|\sum\nolimits_{0}(T)|

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{\lambda}(2p|S_{i}|+2p-1)+\sum_{j=1}^{t}(p|A_{j}|+1)+|T|+1

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{\lambda}(2p|T_{i}\bm{\cdot}S_{i}|-(2p-1)^{2})+p(|S^{\prime}|-|T|)+t+|T|+1

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle 2p(|S|-|S^{\prime}|)-(2p-1)^{2}\lambda+(p+\frac{1}{6p+1})(|S^{\prime}|-|T|)+|T|+1

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle(p+1/2)(|S|-|S^{\prime}|)+(p+\frac{1}{6p+1})(|S^{\prime}|-|T|)+|T|+1

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle(p+\frac{1}{6p+1})(|S|-|T|)+|T|+1

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle n\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ (\mbox{as }|T|\leq 12p(p^{2}-1)),

yielding a contradiction. We note that the last inequality was obtained by using a similar calculation as in (
3.1
).

Case 2.

\mathsf{h}(S^{\prime})\geq 2p-1
.

There exists
g_{\lambda+1}\in G
such that
\mathsf{v}_{g_{\lambda+1}}(S^{\prime})=\mathsf{h}(S^{\prime})\geq 2p-1
. Let
S_{\lambda+1}=S_{\langle g_{\lambda+1}\rangle}^{\prime}
and
U=S^{\prime}\bm{\cdot}S_{\lambda+1}^{[-1]}
. Then

S=(T_{1}\bm{\cdot}S_{1})\bm{\cdot}(T_{2}\bm{\cdot}S_{2})\bm{\cdot}\ldots\bm{\cdot}(T_{\lambda}\bm{\cdot}S_{\lambda})\bm{\cdot}S_{\lambda+1}\bm{\cdot}U.

We next distinguish the rest proof into the following two subcases.

Subcase 2.1.

|U|\leq 2p-2
.

By the choice of
S_{1}
, we have
|S_{\lambda+1}|\leq|S_{1}|
. Since
|T_{i}\bm{\cdot}S_{i}|\geq 4p-2
for each
i\in[2,\lambda]
, we have
\lambda-1\leq\frac{|S|-|S_{1}\bm{\cdot}T_{1}|-|S_{\lambda+1}|-|U|}{4p-2}
. By Lemma
2.3
(2),
|\sum\nolimits_{0}(S_{\lambda+1}\bm{\cdot}U)|\geq|S_{\lambda+1}\bm{\cdot}U|+1
. Since
{\rm st}(\sum_{0}(S))=\{0\}
, by Lemma
2.1
, we have

\displaystyle|\sum\nolimits_{0}(S)|=

\displaystyle|\sum\nolimits_{0}(T_{1}\bm{\cdot}S_{1})+\sum_{i=2}^{\lambda}\sum\nolimits_{0}(T_{i}\bm{\cdot}S_{i})+\sum\nolimits_{0}(S_{\lambda+1}\bm{\cdot}U)|

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle(|\sum\nolimits_{0}(T_{1}\bm{\cdot}S_{1})|-1)+\sum_{i=2}^{\lambda}(|\sum\nolimits_{0}(T_{i}\bm{\cdot}S_{i})|-1)+|\sum\nolimits_{0}(S_{\lambda+1}\bm{\cdot}U)|

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle(2p|S_{1}|+2p-1)+\sum_{i=2}^{\lambda}(2p|S_{i}|+2p-1)+|S_{\lambda+1}\bm{\cdot}U|+1

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle(p+1/2)|S_{1}|+\sum_{i=2}^{\lambda}(2p|T_{i}\bm{\cdot}S_{i}|-(2p-1)^{2})+(p+1/2)|S_{\lambda+1}|+2p

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(p+1/2)(|S_{1}|+|S_{\lambda+1}|)+2p(|S|-|T_{1}\bm{\cdot}S_{1}|-|S_{\lambda+1}|-|U|)-(2p-1)^{2}({\lambda}-1)+2p

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle(p+1/2)(|S_{1}|+|S_{\lambda+1}|)+(p+1/2)(|S|-|T_{1}\bm{\cdot}S_{1}|-|S_{\lambda+1}|-|U|)+2p

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(p+1/2)(|S|-|T_{1}|-|U|)+2p

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle(p+1/2)(n/p+p-2-(4p-3))+2p

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle n\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ (\mbox{as }n\geq 6p^{3}-3p^{2}-p),

yielding a contradiction.

Subcase 2.2.

|U|\geq 2p-1
.

Let
H=\langle\mbox{supp}(U),g_{\lambda+1}\rangle
. If
|H/\langle g_{\lambda+1}\rangle|\geq 2p
, then by a similar method as used in the proof of
Claim
, we can find a subsequence
T_{\lambda+1}|U
with
|T_{\lambda+1}|=2p-1
such that
S_{\lambda+1}
and
T_{\lambda+1}
satisfy Properties
(1)-(3)
, yielding a contradiction to the maximality of
\lambda
. Therefore, we must have
|H/\langle g_{\lambda+1}\rangle|\leq 2p-1
. Since
\langle g_{\lambda+1}\rangle
is a cyclic subgroup of
G
, we have
|G/\langle g_{\lambda+1}\rangle|\geq n_{1}\geq 2p
. Thus
H\neq G
. Therefore,

|H|\leq n/p\mbox{ \ \ \ \ and \ \ \ \ }|S^{\prime}|\leq|S_{H}|\leq n/p-1\ (\mbox{as }S^{\prime}|S_{H}\mbox{ and }S\mbox{ is regular}).

Suppose that
\mbox{supp}(S_{i})\nsubseteq H
for some
i\in[1,\lambda]
. Then let
S_{i}^{\prime}=S_{i}\bm{\cdot}g_{i}^{[-(p-1)]}
. We have

S=\left(\prod_{1\leq j\neq i\leq\lambda}(T_{j}\bm{\cdot}S_{j})\right)\bm{\cdot}(T_{i}\bm{\cdot}S_{i}^{\prime})\bm{\cdot}(g_{i}^{[p-1]}\bm{\cdot}S^{\prime}).

Let
U^{\prime}|U
with
|U^{\prime}|=p-1
. By Corollary
3.3
(1) and Lemma
2.3
(2),
|\sum_{0}(U^{\prime}\bm{\cdot}g_{\lambda+1}^{[2p-1]})|\geq(|U^{\prime}|+1)|\sum_{0}(g_{\lambda+1}^{[2p-1]})|=2p^{2}
. By Lemma
2.3
(1),
{\rm st}(\sum_{0}(S^{\prime}))=\{0\}
. Note that

\sum\nolimits_{0}(S^{\prime})=\sum\nolimits_{0}(S^{\prime}\bm{\cdot}(U^{\prime}\bm{\cdot}g_{\lambda+1}^{[2p-1]})^{[-1]})+\sum\nolimits_{0}(U^{\prime}\bm{\cdot}g_{\lambda+1}^{[2p-1]}).

By Lemma
2.1
and Lemma
2.3
(2),
|\sum_{0}(S^{\prime})|\geq|\sum_{0}(S^{\prime}\bm{\cdot}(U^{\prime}\bm{\cdot}g_{\lambda+1}^{[2p-1]})^{[-1]})|+|\sum_{0}(U^{\prime}\bm{\cdot}g_{\lambda+1}^{[2p-1]})|-1\geq|S^{\prime}\bm{\cdot}(U^{\prime}\bm{\cdot}g_{\lambda+1}^{[2p-1]})^{[-1]}|+2p^{2}=|S^{\prime}|+2p^{2}-3p+2
. Therefore, by Corollary
3.3
(1),

|\sum\nolimits_{0}(g_{i}^{[p-1]}\bm{\cdot}S^{\prime})|\geq(|g_{i}^{[p-1]}|+1)|\sum\nolimits_{0}(S^{\prime})|\geq p|\sum\nolimits_{0}(S^{\prime})|\geq p|S^{\prime}|+2p^{3}-3p^{2}+2p.

As in Subcase 2.1, we have
\lambda-1\leq\frac{|S|-|T_{i}\bm{\cdot}S_{i}|-|S^{\prime}|}{4p-2}
. Since
{\rm st}(\sum_{0}(S))=\{0\}
, by Lemma
2.1
,

\displaystyle|\sum\nolimits_{0}(S)|=

\displaystyle|\sum_{1\leq j\neq i\leq\lambda}\sum\nolimits_{0}(T_{j}\bm{\cdot}S_{j})+\sum\nolimits_{0}(T_{i}\bm{\cdot}S_{i}^{\prime})+\sum\nolimits_{0}(g_{i}^{[p-1]}\bm{\cdot}S^{\prime})|

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\sum_{1\leq j\neq i\leq\lambda}(|\sum\nolimits_{0}(T_{j}\bm{\cdot}S_{j})|-1)+(|\sum\nolimits_{0}(T_{i}\bm{\cdot}S_{i}^{\prime})|-1)+|\sum\nolimits_{0}(g_{i}^{[p-1]}\bm{\cdot}S^{\prime})|

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\sum_{1\leq j\neq i\leq\lambda}(2p|T_{j}\bm{\cdot}S_{j}|-(2p-1)^{2})+(2p|S_{i}^{\prime}|+2p-1)+p|S^{\prime}|+2p^{3}-3p^{2}+2p

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 2p(|S|-|S_{i}\bm{\cdot}T_{i}|-|S^{\prime}|)-(2p-1)^{2}(\lambda-1)+2p(|S_{i}|-p+1)

\displaystyle+p|S^{\prime}|+2p^{3}-3p^{2}+4p-1

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle(p+1/2)(|S|-|S_{i}\bm{\cdot}T_{i}|-|S^{\prime}|)+2p|S_{i}|+p|S^{\prime}|+2p^{3}-5p^{2}+6p-1

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(p+1/2)|S|+(p-1/2)|S_{i}|-(p+1/2)|T_{i}|-|S^{\prime}|/2+2p^{3}-5p^{2}+6p-1

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle(p+1/2)(n/p+p-2)-(2p-1)-(n/p-1)/2+2p^{3}-5p^{2}+6p-1

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle n,

yielding a contradiction.

Next assume that
\mbox{supp}(S_{i})\subseteq H
for every
i\in[1,\lambda]
. Let

S^{\prime\prime}=S_{1}\bm{\cdot}\ldots\bm{\cdot}S_{\lambda}\bm{\cdot}S^{\prime}.

Then
S^{\prime\prime}|S_{H}
. Let

n^{\prime}=|H|\leq n/p.

Since
S^{\prime\prime}=S_{1}\bm{\cdot}\ldots\bm{\cdot}S_{\lambda}\bm{\cdot}S^{\prime}
with
|S_{i}|\geq|T_{i}|
for
i\in[1,\lambda]
, we have
|S^{\prime\prime}|\geq(|S|+|S^{\prime}|)/2\geq((n/p+p-2)+|U|+|S_{\lambda+1}|)/2\geq(n^{\prime}+|U|+|S_{\lambda+1}|+1)/2
.

Let
T=h_{1}\bm{\cdot}h_{2}\bm{\cdot}S_{\lambda+1}
where
h_{1}\bm{\cdot}h_{2}|U
. Let
t\geq 0
be the maximal integer such that
S^{\prime\prime}
has a new factorization

S^{\prime\prime}=A_{1}\bm{\cdot}\ldots\bm{\cdot}A_{t}\bm{\cdot}W\bm{\cdot}T

where each
A_{i}
is a
2
-zero-sum free
3
-subset of
G
, and
W
is a subsequence of
S^{\prime\prime}
which contains no
2
-zero-sum free
3
-subset of
G
. Then

(3.2)

3t+|W|+|S_{\lambda+1}|+2=|S^{\prime\prime}|\geq(n^{\prime}+|U|+|S_{\lambda+1}|+1)/2

and

\mbox{supp}(W)\subseteq\{u_{1},-u_{1},u_{2},-u_{2}\}

for some distinct elements
u_{1},u_{2}\in H
. By Lemma
3.4
,
|\sum_{0}(A_{i})|\geq 7
for
i\in[1,t]
.

We now show that
|W|>|U|
. Since
S^{\prime\prime}\subseteq H
and
|H|=n^{\prime}
, we must have

\sum\nolimits_{0}(S^{\prime\prime})\subsetneq H,\mbox{\ \ \ and thus\ \ \ }|\sum\nolimits_{0}(S^{\prime\prime})|\leq n^{\prime}-1.

For otherwise,
\sum_{0}(S^{\prime\prime})=H
and thus
{\rm st}(\sum_{0}(S^{\prime\prime}))\supseteq H\neq\{0\}
. However, by Lemma
2.3
(1),
{\rm st}(\sum_{0}(S^{\prime\prime}))=\{0\}
, yielding a contradiction. By Corollary
3.3
(1) and Lemma
2.3
(2),
|\sum\nolimits_{0}(T)|\geq 3(|S_{\lambda+1}|+1)
. It follows from Lemma
2.1
and
2.3
(2) that

\displaystyle n^{\prime}-1\geq

\displaystyle|\sum\nolimits_{0}(S^{\prime\prime})|=|\sum_{i=1}^{t}\sum\nolimits_{0}(A_{i})+\sum\nolimits_{0}(W)+\sum\nolimits_{0}(T)|

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{t}(|\sum\nolimits_{0}(A_{i})|-1)+(|\sum\nolimits_{0}(W)|-1)+|\sum\nolimits_{0}(T)|

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{t}6+|W|+3(|S_{\lambda+1}|+1)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 6t+|W|+3|S_{\lambda+1}|+3

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 2|S^{\prime\prime}|-|W|+|S_{\lambda+1}|-1

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle n^{\prime}+|U|+2|S_{\lambda+1}|-|W|\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ (\mbox{by }\eqref{S''}).

This gives

|W|\geq|U|+2|S_{\lambda+1}|+1>|U|.

Note that
W|S^{\prime\prime}\bm{\cdot}S_{\lambda+1}^{[-1]}=S_{1}\bm{\cdot}\ldots\bm{\cdot}S_{\lambda}\bm{\cdot}U
. In view of Property (2), if
u_{1}
lies in
\supp(S_{j})
for some
j\leq\lambda+1
, then
\mathsf{v}_{\pm u_{1}}(S^{\prime\prime})=\mathsf{v}_{\pm u_{1}}(S_{j})
. Therefore,
u_{1}\in\supp(U)
if and only if
\mathsf{v}_{\pm u_{1}}(U)=\mathsf{v}_{\pm u_{1}}(S^{\prime\prime})
, meaning all terms equal to
u_{1}
or
-u_{1}
in
S^{\prime\prime}
occur in
U
provided that
u_{1}\in\supp(U)
; and likewise for
-u_{1}
,
u_{2}
and
-u_{2}
. Since
W|S^{\prime\prime}
and
\mbox{supp}(W)\subseteq\{u_{1},-u_{1},u_{2},-u_{2}\}
, we have
W|u_{1}^{\mathsf{v}_{u_{1}}(S^{\prime\prime})}\bm{\cdot}(-u_{1})^{\mathsf{v}_{-u_{1}}(S^{\prime\prime})}\bm{\cdot}u_{2}^{\mathsf{v}_{u_{2}}(S^{\prime\prime})}\bm{\cdot}(-u_{2})^{\mathsf{v}_{-u_{2}}(S^{\prime\prime})}
. If
u_{1}\bm{\cdot}u_{2}|W
and
u_{1}\bm{\cdot}u_{2}|U
, then by what we just proved,
u_{1}^{\mathsf{v}_{u_{1}}(S^{\prime\prime})}\bm{\cdot}(-u_{1})^{\mathsf{v}_{-u_{1}}(S^{\prime\prime})}\bm{\cdot}u_{2}^{\mathsf{v}_{u_{2}}(S^{\prime\prime})}\bm{\cdot}(-u_{2})^{\mathsf{v}_{-u_{2}}(S^{\prime\prime})}|U
, so
W|U
, implying
|W|\leq|U|
, yielding a contradiction to
|W|>|U|
. Therefore, without loss of generality, we may assume that
u_{1}|W
and
u_{1}\notin\supp(U)
. Thus
u_{1}\in\mbox{supp}(S_{j})
for some
j\leq\lambda+1
. Since the sequence
W
is disjoint from
S_{\lambda+1}
by construction, it follows that
u_{1}\in\supp(S_{j})
for some
j\leq\lambda
. In view of Property (2), we conclude that
-u_{1}\in\mbox{supp}(S_{j})
and
v\notin\langle u_{1}\rangle\leq\langle g_{j}\rangle
for every
v\in\mbox{supp}(S_{\lambda+1})
. Write
W=W_{1}\bm{\cdot}W_{2}
with

W_{1}|u_{1}^{[\mathsf{v}_{u_{1}}(S^{\prime\prime})]}\bm{\cdot}(-u_{1})^{[\mathsf{v}_{-u_{1}}(S^{\prime\prime})]}\mbox{ \ \ and \ \ }W_{2}|u_{2}^{[\mathsf{v}_{u_{2}}(S^{\prime\prime})]}\bm{\cdot}(-u_{2})^{[\mathsf{v}_{-u_{2}}(S^{\prime\prime})]}.

Now fix
v|S_{\lambda+1}
. Let
V=W_{1}\bm{\cdot}v
and
T^{\prime}=T\bm{\cdot}v^{[-1]}
. We obtain another factorization

S^{\prime\prime}=A_{1}\bm{\cdot}\ldots\bm{\cdot}A_{t}\bm{\cdot}V\bm{\cdot}W_{2}\bm{\cdot}T^{\prime}.

Since
v\notin\langle u_{1}\rangle
, by Corollary
3.3
(1) and Lemma
2.3
(2),
|\sum\nolimits_{0}(V)|\geq 2(|W_{1}|+1)
. Note that

(3.3)

|S^{\prime\prime}|=3t+|V|+|W_{2}|+|T|-1=3t+|W_{1}|+|W_{2}|+|S_{\lambda+1}|+2.

As before, we obtain

\displaystyle n^{\prime}-1\geq

\displaystyle|\sum\nolimits_{0}(S^{\prime\prime})|=|\sum_{i=1}^{t}\sum\nolimits_{0}(A_{i})+\sum\nolimits_{0}(V)+\sum\nolimits_{0}(W_{2})+\sum\nolimits_{0}(T^{\prime})|

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{t}(|\sum\nolimits_{0}(A_{i})|-1)+(|\sum\nolimits_{0}(V)|-1)+(|\sum\nolimits_{0}(W_{2})|-1)+|\sum\nolimits_{0}(T^{\prime})|

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{t}6+2|W_{1}|+1+|W_{2}|+3|S_{\lambda+1}|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 6t+2|W_{1}|+|W_{2}|+3|S_{\lambda+1}|+1

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 2|S^{\prime\prime}|-|W_{2}|+|S_{\lambda+1}|-3\ \ \ \ \ \ \ (\mbox{by }\eqref{W_1})

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle n^{\prime}+|U|+2|S_{\lambda+1}|-|W_{2}|-2\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ (\mbox{by }\eqref{S''}),

implying

|W_{2}|\geq|U|+2|S_{\lambda+1}|-1>|U|.

Next we consider
W_{2}
. Since
\supp(W_{2})\subseteq\{u_{2},-u_{2}\}
, if
u_{2}\in\supp(U)
, then as above, we obtain
W_{2}|U
, implying
|W_{2}|\leq|U|
, giving a contradiction. Thus
u_{2}\notin\supp(U)
. As above, we conclude that
\{\pm u_{2}\}\subseteq\mbox{supp}(S_{j^{\prime}})
for some
j^{\prime}\leq\lambda
and
z\notin\langle u_{2}\rangle\leq\langle g_{j^{\prime}}\rangle
for every
z\in\mbox{supp}(S_{\lambda+1})
. Since
|S_{\lambda+1}|\geq 2p-1\geq 5
, we can choose
z|S_{\lambda+1}\bm{\cdot}v^{[-1]}
. Define
V=W_{1}\bm{\cdot}v
,
Z=W_{2}\bm{\cdot}z
and
T^{\prime\prime}=T\bm{\cdot}v^{[-1]}\bm{\cdot}z^{[-1]}
. Thus we obtain another factorization

S^{\prime\prime}=A_{1}\bm{\cdot}\ldots\bm{\cdot}A_{t}\bm{\cdot}V\bm{\cdot}Z\bm{\cdot}T^{\prime\prime}.

As before, we obtain

\displaystyle n^{\prime}-1\geq

\displaystyle|\sum\nolimits_{0}(S^{\prime\prime})|=|\sum_{i=1}^{t}\sum\nolimits_{0}(A_{i})+\sum\nolimits_{0}(V)+\sum\nolimits_{0}(Z)+\sum\nolimits_{0}(T^{\prime\prime})|

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{t}(|\sum\nolimits_{0}(A_{i})|-1)+(|\sum\nolimits_{0}(V)|-1)+(|\sum\nolimits_{0}(Z)|-1)+|\sum\nolimits_{0}(T^{\prime\prime})|

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{t}6+2|W_{1}|+1+2|W_{2}|+1+3(|S_{\lambda+1}|-1)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 6t+2|W_{1}|+2|W_{2}|+3|S_{\lambda+1}|-1

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 2|S^{\prime\prime}|+|S_{\lambda+1}|-5\ \ \ \ \ \ \ (\mbox{by }\eqref{W_1})

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle n^{\prime}+|U|+2|S_{\lambda+1}|-4\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ (\mbox{by }\eqref{S''})

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle n^{\prime}\ \ \ \ \ \ (\mbox{as }|U|\geq 2p-1\geq 5),

yielding a contradiction. In all cases we have found contradictions. Thus we must have
\sum(S)=G
. This completes the proof. ∎
