Abstract
. Let 𝔽 q \mathbb{F}_{q} be a finite field with q q elements. Let n n be a positive integer with radical r ​ a ​ d ​ ( n ) rad(n) , namely, the product of distinct prime divisors of n n . Let

Let

There is a classical remarkable criterion on irreducible binomials over
\mathbb{F}_{q}
.

Lemma 2.1
.

[
8
, Theorem 3.75]

Assume that

is a positive integer and
\eta\in\mathbb{F}_{q}^{\ast}
. Then the binomial

is irreducible over

if and only if all the following conditions are satisfied:

(ii)

(iii) if

In fact, if

The following lemmas are concerned with the explicit factorization of

Lemma 3.1
.

Let

(1)
Then

\gcd(q-1,\frac{q^{w}-1}{q-1})=\gcd(q-1,w)=\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}1,&\mbox{ if $w\nmid(q-1)$,}\\

\gcd(q-1,\frac{q^{w^{2}}-1}{q-1})=\gcd(q-1,w^{2})=\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}1,&\mbox{ if $w\nmid(q-1)$,}\\

(2)
If

v_{w}(\frac{q^{w}-1}{q-1})=1

(1) Since

.

\gcd(q-1,\frac{q^{w^{2}}-1}{q-1})=\gcd(q-1,w^{2})=1

or

(2) If
