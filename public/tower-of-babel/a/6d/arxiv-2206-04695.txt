Jackiw-Teitelboim Gravity from the Karch-Randall Braneworld Preprint: today
Hao Geng a, Andreas Karch b, Carlos Perez-Pardavila b, Suvrat Raju c, Lisa Randall a, Marcos Riojas b, Sanjit Shashi b

Abstract
In this letter, we show that Jackiw-Teitelboim (JT) gravity can be naturally realized in the Karch-Randall braneworld. Notably the role of the dilaton in JT gravity is played by the radion in a suitably orbifolded version of the setup. In the classical entanglement entropy calculation, there is an apparent degeneracy of Ryu-Takayanagi surfaces. We demonstrate how quantum fluctuations of the radion/dilaton resolves this would-be classical puzzle regarding entanglement wedge reconstruction.

III
Realizing JT Gravity

In the original wedge holography papers
Akal et al. 2020
;
Miao 2020
;
Geng et al. 2020
;
Geng et al. 2021a
the branes are treated as rigid. This assumption is rooted in the original Karch-Randall setup where we just have one brane and we can take the brane to be sitting at an orbifold fixed point to model an EOW brane in analog to O-planes in string theory
Polchinski 2007
;
Johnson 2005
. However, reminding ourselves of the Randall-Sundrum I (RS1) setup
Randall and Sundrum 1999a
with two RS branes, the relative distance between the branes is a dynamical variable called the radion, which has important phenomenological implications
Arkani-Hamed et al. 2001
. Hence in the case of wedge holography, we should not neglect the possibility of brane fluctuations.

In this section, we consider small brane fluctuations (relative to the bulk AdS
3
curvature scale) and derive the effective action for the coupling between these small fluctuations and the brane-localized graviton mode by doing dimensional reduction. While brane fluctuations can be neglected in higher dimensions, we will see that this small contribution plays an important role when the bulk is AdS
3
. The detailed derivation and a more careful analysis will be presented in
Geng
.

To be precise, we will consider two branes with tensions
T_{1}
and
T_{2}
sitting at
r=r_{1}+\delta\phi_{1}(x)
and
r=r_{2}+\delta\phi_{2}(x)
slices of the following bulk geometry

ds^{2}=dr^{2}+\cosh^{2}(r)g_{ab}(x)dx^{a}dx^{b}\,,

(6)

with the relationships
T_{1}=-\tanh r_{1}>0
and
T_{2}=\tanh r_{2}>0
satisfied and
\delta\phi_{1},\delta\phi_{2}\ll 1
(the AdS length scale has been set to one). The wedge runs from
r=r_{1}+\delta\phi_{1}(x)
to
r=r_{2}+\delta\phi_{2}(x)
, and the metric ansatz Equ. (
6
) describes the lowest 2d graviton mode (the higher modes would depend on the coordinate
r
as
g_{ab}(r,x)
).

To obtain the low-energy effective action, we have to plug the above ansatz of the metric and brane locations into Equ. (
5

\mathcal{O}(\delta\phi^{2})

S_{\text{eff}}=S_{0}-\frac{1}{16\pi G_{3}}\int d^{2}x\sqrt{-g}\phi(x)\Big[R[g]+2\Big]+S_{\text{dilaton}}\,,

(7)

\phi(x)=\delta\phi_{2}(x)-\delta\phi_{1}(x)

In analogy to the Randall-Sundrum scenario, to isolate the dynamics of the radion we have to orbifold our setup. We can project out either
\delta\phi_{1}(x)

\delta\phi_{2}(x)

\delta\phi_{2}(x)

(11)

\phi(x)=\delta\phi_{2}(x)

IV
A Puzzle and Its Resolution

A canonical quantity to study in AdS
3
wedge holography is the entanglement entropy between the two asymptotic defects which support the dual quantum mechanics (see Fig.
2b
). This can be calculated using the Ryu-Takayanagi (RT) formula
Ryu and Takayanagi 2006a
;
Ryu and Takayanagi 2006b
by studying the RT surfaces that connect the two branes
Akal et al. 2020
;
Geng et al. 2020
. Choosing global AdS
2
for those AdS
2
slices, the bulk metric Equ. (
4
) can be written as

ds^{2}=dr^{2}+\cosh^{2}(r)\big[-\cosh^{2}(\eta)d\tau^{2}+d\eta^{2}\big]\,,

(16)

if we ignore the brane fluctuations
\delta\phi_{i}(x)
. The RT surface can be parametrized as
\eta=\eta(r)
,
\tau=const.
which leads to the area functional

A=\int_{r_{1}}^{r_{2}}dr\sqrt{1+\cosh^{2}(r)\eta^{\prime}(r)^{2}}\,.

(17)

It is easy to show that, with the boundary term carefully taken into account, the variation of the area functional vanishes if and only if
\eta^{\prime}(r)=0
. Therefore, any constant
\eta
surface is an RT surface and they all have equal area
A=r_{2}-r_{1}
(see Fig.
4
for an illustration). According to this result, there would be no definite RT surface for the entanglement entropy between the two defects and as a corollary there would be no definite entanglement wedge for either of the defects. This would be problematic because it would preclude a holographic interpretation from the point of view of bulk (or more precisely, entanglement wedge
Jafferis et al. 2016
) reconstruction.

Fortunately, this puzzling result occurs only when the branes are treated as rigid. We expect brane fluctuations to lift the degeneracy of the RT surfaces. Because the brane fluctuation is in general time-dependent, we should use the HRT formula
Hubeny et al. 2007
to compute the entanglement entropy from the wedge. However, this would be complicated as the result is heavily dependent on properly treating the boundary condition near the brane.

This challenge of computing entanglement entropy with brane fluctuations is readily met if we instead do it using our effective braneworld theory Equ. (
11
). In that case we are just computing the entanglement entropy between the two cutoff boundaries of the nearly-AdS
2
space. By solving the equation of motion of the dilaton and 2d metric, we have that

\displaystyle ds^{2}_{2d}

\displaystyle=-\cosh^{2}(\eta)d\tau^{2}+d\eta^{2}\,,

(18)

\displaystyle\phi(x)

\displaystyle=\phi_{r}\cosh\eta\cos\tau\,,

(19)

where
\phi_{r}
is a constant. Thus, using the maxmin prescription
Wall 2014
of the HRT formula we have

S_{\text{EE}}=\max_{\tau}\min_{\eta}\Big(\frac{\phi_{0}+\phi(x)}{4G_{3}}\Big)=\frac{\phi_{0}+\phi_{r}}{4G_{3}}\,,

(20)

where the minimization has a solution
\eta=0
,
\tau=0
. As a result, from the bulk AdS
3
perspective Equ. (
16
), the RT surface is just the
\eta=0
surface which sits precisely in the middle of the bulk (i.e. the middle green surface in Fig.
4
). Thus, the entanglement wedge of either defect is the half bulk region next to it, which is consistent with the symmetry between the two defects.

V
Conclusions

In this letter, we studied wedge holography when the bulk is three-dimensional. We found that it is necessary to consider brane fluctuations, identified as the radion mode in the braneworld language
Randall and Sundrum 1999a
;
Randall and Sundrum 1999b
, for the apparent consistency of the holographic setup. We found that the low-energy dynamics of the brane fluctuations with orbifold projection is precisely Jackiw-Teitelboim gravity.
