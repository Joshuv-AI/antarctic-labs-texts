Discrete dynamics in cluster integrable systems from geometric R R -matrix transformations
Terrence George, Sanjay Ramassamy

Abstract
Cluster integrable systems are a broad class of integrable systems modelled on bipartite dimer models on the torus. Many discrete integrable dynamics arise by applying sequences of local transformations, which form the cluster modular group of the cluster integrable system. This cluster modular group was recently characterized by the first author and Inchiostro. There exist some discrete integrable dynamics that make use of non-local transformations associated with geometric R R -matrices. In this article we characterize the generalized cluster modular group – which includes both local and non-local transformations – in terms of extended affine symmetric groups. We also describe the action of the generalized cluster modular group on the spectral data associated with cluster integrable systems.

There are two local transformations of weighted bipartite graphs embedded in

\displaystyle\phi

\displaystyle\mapsto(w_{\rho})_{\rho\in\Sigma(1)}.

In the course of proving Theorem

.

3.

Let

Let

denote the moduli space parameterizing spectral data associated with

2.

We show that generalized cluster transformations act on the spectral data in almost the same way, except that the action on

Corollary 6.3
.

The kernel of
\overline{\lambda}
