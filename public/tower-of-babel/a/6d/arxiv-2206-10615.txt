Abstract




Nodal solutions for Logarithmic weighted
N
-Laplacian problem with exponential
nonlinearities

Abstract.

In this article, we study the following problem

-\textmd{div}(\omega(x)|\nabla u|^{N-2}\nabla u)=\lambda\ f(x,u)\quad\mbox{ in }\quad B,\quad u=0\quad\mbox{ on }\quad\partial B,

where
B
is the unit ball of
\mathbb{R^{N}}
,
N\geq 2
and
w(x)

a singular weight of logarithm type. The reaction source

f(x,u)
is a radial function with respect to
x
and is subcritical or critical with respect to a maximal growth of exponential type. By using the constrained minimization in Nehari set coupled with the quantitative deformation lemma and degree theory, we prove the existence of nodal solutions.

Keywords:
Weighted Sobolev space,
N
-Laplacian operator, Critical exponential growth, Nodal solutions.

Introduction and Main results

In this paper, we consider the following elliptic problem involving
logarithmic weighted
N
-Laplacian:

\displaystyle(P_{\lambda})~~~~\left\{\begin{array}[]{rclll}-\textmd{div}(\omega(x)|\nabla u|^{N-2}\nabla u)&=&\lambda\ f(x,u)&\mbox{in}&B\\

where
B=B(0,1)
is the unit open ball in
\mathbb{R}^{N}
,
N>2
,
\lambda
is a positive parameter, the weight function

is given by

\omega(x)=\Big(\log\frac{1}{|x|}\Big)^{\beta(N-1)}~~\mbox{or}~~~\omega(x)=\Big(\log\frac{e}{|x|}\Big)^{\beta(N-1)}\beta\in[0,1).

(1.1)

We assume that the nonlinearity
f(x,t):\overline{B}\times\mathbb{R}\rightarrow\mathbb{R}
is a radial in
x
, continuous function and behaves like
\exp\{\alpha t^{\frac{N}{(N-1)(1-\beta)}}\}~~\mbox{as}~~t\rightarrow+\infty~~
, for some
\alpha>0
and
\beta\in[0,1)
.

Such an equation may arise in many fields of physics, such
as in non-Newtonian fluids, reaction diffusion problem, turbulent
flows in porous media and image treatment
[
1
,
3
,
18
,
20
]
. Here we just give some references which are close to the problem we consider in this note.

Without the weight

, the problem
(P_{\lambda})
has been widely studied by several
authors with different nonlinearities.
G. M. Figueredo and F.B. M. Nunes in
[
13
]
, consider the following equation

-\textmd{div}(a(|\nabla u|^{p})|\nabla u|^{p-2}\nabla u)=f(u)~~~\mbox{ in }~~~\Omega,~~u=0~~~\mbox{ on }~~~\partial\Omega,

(1.2)

where

is bounded,

, the nonlinearity

is a superlinear continuous function with exponential subcritical or exponential critical growth and the function

is

. By using the minimization argument and deformation lemma, the authors proved the existence of a least energy nodal solutions for the equation (

) with two nodal domains.

Recently, when

, X. sun and Y. song, see
[

, studied the problem (

) in an open smooth bounded domain in the Heisenberg group

. We also mention the work of Y. Zhang, Y Yang and S. Liang, see

\displaystyle\left\{\begin{array}[]{rclll}-\textmd{div}(\omega(x)|\nabla u|^{N-2}\nabla u)&=&\displaystyle f(x,u)&\mbox{in}&B\\

where the weight function

is given in (

) and the nonlinearity

is positive, have been investigated in literatures
(see

,

,

]
and the references therein). We notice that,
the influence of weights on limiting inequalities of Trudinger-Moser type has been studied with some detail in

and as consequence the weights have an important impact in the Sobolev norm.

Let
\Omega\subset\mathbb{R}^{N}
be a bounded domain and
w\in L^{1}(\Omega)
be a nonnegative function, the weighted sobolev space is defined as

W_{0}^{1,N}(\Omega,w)=cl\{u\in C_{0}^{\infty}(\Omega)/\int_{B}|\nabla u|^{{}^{N}}w(x)dx<\infty\}

For different proprieties and embedding results for the weighted Sobolev spaces, we can refer to
[
16
]
.

Since the logarithmic weights have a particular significance and considered as the limiting situations of the embedding of the spaces

W_{0}^{1,N}(\Omega,w)
, we will consider the weight defined by (
1.1
).
We then, restrict our attention to radial functions and consider the subspace

\mathbf{E}=W_{0,rad}^{1,N}(B,w)=cl\{u\in C_{0,rad}^{\infty}(B)~~|~~\int_{B}|\nabla u|^{{}^{N}}\omega(x)dx<\infty\}

endowed with the norm

\|u\|=\displaystyle\big(\int_{B}|\nabla u|^{N}\omega(x)dx\big)^{\frac{1}{N}}.

The choice of the weight induced in (
1.1
) and the space
\mathbf{E}
are also motivated by the following exponential inequalities.

Theorem 1.1

[
7
]

Let
\beta\in[0,1)
and let
w
be given by (
1.1
), then

\int_{B}e^{|u|^{\gamma}}dx<+\infty,~~\forall~~u\in\mathbf{E},~~\mbox{if and only if}~~\gamma\leq\gamma_{N,\beta}=\frac{N}{(N-1)(1-\beta)}=\frac{N^{\prime}}{1-\beta}

(1.3)

and

\sup_{\begin{subarray}{c}u\in\mathbf{E}\\
\|u\|\leq 1\end{subarray}}\int_{B}~e^{\alpha|u|^{\gamma_{N,\beta}}}dx<+\infty~~~~\Leftrightarrow~~~~\alpha\leq\alpha_{N,\beta}=N[\omega^{\frac{1}{N-1}}_{N-1}(1-\beta)]^{\frac{1}{1-\beta}},

(1.4)

where
\omega_{N-1}
is the area of the unit sphere

in
\mathbb{R}^{N}
and

is the H
\ddot{o}
lder conjugate of

.

Let
\gamma:=\gamma_{N,\beta}=\displaystyle\frac{N^{\prime}}{1-\beta}
, in view of inequalities (

) and (

), we say that

has subcritical growth at

if

\lim_{s\rightarrow+\infty}\frac{|f(x,s)|}{e^{\alpha s^{\gamma}}}=0,~~~~\mbox{for all}~~\alpha>0

(1.5)

and

if there exists some

\lim_{s\rightarrow+\infty}\frac{|f(x,s)|}{e^{\alpha s^{\gamma}}}=0,~~~\forall~\alpha>\alpha_{0}~~\mbox{and}~~~~\lim_{s\rightarrow+\infty}\frac{|f(x,s)|}{e^{\alpha s^{\gamma}}}=+\infty,~~\forall~\alpha>\alpha_{0}.\\

(1.6)

In this paper, we deal with problem
(P_{\lambda})
under subcritical and critical growth nonlinearities.
Furthermore, we suppose that

satisfies the following hypothesis:

(V_{1})

f:B\times\mathbb{R}\rightarrow\mathbb{R}
is

and radial in

.

(

There exist

(V_{3})

.

(V_{4})

\displaystyle\lim_{t\rightarrow 0}\frac{|f(x,t)|}{|t|^{N-1}}=0.

We give an example of such nonlinearity. The nonlinearity
f(x,t)=|t|^{N-1}t+|t|^{N}t\exp(\alpha|t|^{\gamma})
satisfies the assumptions
(V_{1})
,
(V_{2})
,
(V_{3})
and
(V_{4})
.

We will consider the following definition of solutions.

Definition 1.1

We say that a function
u\in\mathbf{E}
is a weak solution of the problem
(P_{\lambda})
if

\int_{B}|\nabla u|^{N-2}\nabla u.\nabla\varphi~w(x)dx=\lambda\int_{B}f(x,u)\varphi dx,~~\forall~\varphi\in\mathbf{E}.

Let
\mathcal{J_{\lambda}}:\mathbf{E}\rightarrow\mathbb{R}
be the functional given by

\mathcal{J_{\lambda}}(u)=\frac{1}{N}\int_{B}|\nabla u|^{N}w(x)dx-\lambda\int_{B}F(x,u)dx,

(1.7)

where

F(x,t)=\displaystyle\int_{0}^{t}f(x,s)ds.

The energy functional
\mathcal{J_{\lambda}}
is well defined and of class
C^{1}
since there exist
a,~C>0
positive constants and there exists
t_{1}>1
such for that

|f(x,t)|\leq Ce^{a~t^{\gamma}},~~~~~~\forall|t|>t_{1},

whenever the nonlinearity
f(x,t)
is critical or subcritical at
+\infty
.

It is quite clear that finding non trivial weak solutions to the problem
(P_{\lambda})
is equivalent to finding non-zero critical points of the functional
\mathcal{J_{\lambda}}
. Moreover, we have

\langle\mathcal{J}^{\prime}_{\lambda}(u),\varphi\rangle=\mathcal{J_{\lambda}^{\prime}}(u)\varphi=\int_{B}\big(\omega(x)~|\nabla u|^{N-2}\nabla u~\nabla\varphi\big)~dx-\lambda\int_{B}f(x,u)~\varphi~dx~,~~\varphi\in\mathbf{E}\cdot

We define the Nehari set as

\displaystyle\ \mathcal{N}_{\lambda}:=\{u\in\mathbf{E}:\langle\mathcal{J}^{\prime}_{\lambda}(u),u^{+}\rangle=\langle\mathcal{J}^{\prime}_{\lambda}(u),u^{-}\rangle=0,u^{+}\neq 0,u^{-}\neq 0\},

where
u^{+}=\max\{u(x),0\}
,
u^{-}=\min\{u(x),0\}
.

It’s easy to verify the following decomposition

\mathcal{J}_{\lambda}(u)=\mathcal{J}_{\lambda}(u^{+})+\mathcal{J}_{\lambda}(u^{-}),

and

\langle\mathcal{J}^{\prime}_{\lambda}(u),u^{+}\rangle=\langle\mathcal{J}^{\prime}_{\lambda}(u^{+}),u^{+}\rangle~~\mbox{and}~~\langle\mathcal{J}^{\prime}_{\lambda}(u),u^{-}\rangle=\langle\mathcal{J}^{\prime}_{\lambda}(u^{-}),u^{-}\rangle

We also give the following definitions of the so called nodal solutions and least energy sign-changing solution of problem
(P_{\lambda})
.

Definition 1.2

•

\upsilon\in\mathbf{E}
is called nodal or sign-changing solution of problem
(P_{\lambda})
if
\upsilon
is a solution of problem
(P_{\lambda})
and
\upsilon^{\pm}\neq 0~~\mbox{a.e in}~~B
.

•

\upsilon\in\mathbf{E}

is called least energy sign-changing solution of problem
(P_{\lambda})
if
\upsilon
is a sign-changing solution of
(P_{\lambda})
and

\mathcal{J_{\lambda}}(\upsilon)=\displaystyle\inf\{\mathcal{J_{\lambda}}(u):\mathcal{J^{\prime}_{\lambda}}(u)=0,u^{\pm}\neq 0~~\mbox{a.e in}~~B\}

Influenced by the works cited above, we try to find a minimize of the energy functional
\mathcal{J_{\lambda}}
over the following minimization problem,

\displaystyle c_{\lambda}=\inf_{u\in\mathcal{N}_{\lambda}}\mathcal{J_{\lambda}}(u)

Our approach is based on the
Nehari manifold method, which was introduced in
[
15
]
and is by now a well-
established and useful tool in finding solutions of problems with a variational
structure, see
[
14
]
.

To our best knowledge, there are few results for the nodal solutions to the N-weighted Lapalace equation with critical exponential nonlinearity on the weighted Sobolev space
\mathbf{E}
.

Now, we give our main results as follows:

,
