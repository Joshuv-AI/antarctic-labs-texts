Algebraic Structures In Closed Superstring Field Theory, Homotopy Transfer And Effective Actions
Ranveer Kumar Singh

Abstract
A consistent action for heterotic and type II superstring field theory was recently proposed by Sen. We give an algebraic formulation of this action in terms of certain twisted L ∞ L_{\infty} -algebra. We further show that Sen’s Wilsonian effective superstring field action can be obtained using homotopy transfer and the effective theory also possesses the algebraic structure of a twisted L ∞ L_{\infty} -algebra.

We close this section by listing two potential future directions.

1.

The present formulation of Wilsonian effective action in terms of homotopy transfer is only for the classical string field action, i.e., the string vertices are calculated using only genus zero correlators of the string worldsheet. The quantum superstring field action includes all higher genus contributions to the string vertex and the main identity (
24
) also receives contributions from integrals over other patches of the moduli space. The formulation of the quantum superstring action will require appropriate generalisation of the twisted
L_{\infty}
-algebra to
quantum twisted
L_{\infty}
-algebra
in analogy to the standard
L_{\infty}

[
4

13

. It would also be desirable to understand the quantum Wilsonian effective action in terms of homotopy transfer.

2.

A successful formulation of quantum superstring field theory action and quantum Wilsonian effective action in terms of a quantum twisted
L_{\infty}
-algebra opens up the possibility of computing higher-derivative corrections to low energy supergravity using algebraic methods. It would be extremely interesting if homotopy transfer could be applied to reproduce known higher-derivative corrections to low energy supergravity.

{thm}
