On Projective representations of direct product of groups
Sumana Hatui

Abstract
. Let G = G 1 × G 2 G=G_{1}\times G_{2} be a finite group. By [ 8 , Theorem 2.3, p. 20] , the second cohomology group H 2 ​ ( G , ℂ × ) {\mathrm{H}}^{2}(G,\mathbb{C}^{\times}) is isomorphic to H 2 ( G 1 , ℂ × ) × H 2 ( G 2 , ℂ × ) × Hom ( G 1 / G 1 ′ ⊗ ℤ G 2 / G 2 ′ , ℂ × ) . {\mathrm{H}}^{2}(G_{1},\mathbb{C}^{\times})\times{\mathrm{H}}^{2}(G_{2},\mathbb{C}^{\times})\times{\mathrm{Hom}}(G_{1}/G_{1}^{\prime}\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}}G_{2}/G_{2}^{\prime},\mathbb{C}^{\times}). A 2 2 -cocycle α \alpha of G G is called a bilinear cocycle if the corresponding cohomology class [ α ] [\alpha] of H 2 ​ ( G , ℂ × ) {\mathrm{H}}^{2}(G,\mathbb{C}^{\times}) lies in Hom ( G 1 / G 1 ′ ⊗ ℤ G 2 / G 2 ′ , ℂ × ) {\mathrm{Hom}}(G_{1}/G_{1}^{\prime}\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}}G_{2}/G_{2}^{\prime},\mathbb{C}^{\times}) . In this article, our aim is to construct an irreducible complex projective representation ρ \rho of G G for bilinear cocycles α \alpha . If G 1 G_{1} is any abelian p p -group and G 2 G_{2} is an elementary abelian p p -group, then we give a construction of ρ \rho for bilinear cocycles α \alpha of G G . For a subgroup H H of G G of index ≤ p 2 \leq p^{2} , we also count the number of cohomology classes [ α ] [\alpha] for which the irreducible projective representations behave the same while restricting on H H . Finally, we consider any p p -group G = G 1 × G 2 G=G_{1}\times G_{2} , and we discuss how the above construction helps us to describe an irreducible α \alpha -representation of G G when [ α ] [\alpha] is of order p p or G 2 / G 2 ′ G_{2}/G_{2}^{\prime} is elementary abelian. We also discuss several examples as an application of the above results.

1.
Introduction

For a finite group
G
, the second cohomology group
{\mathrm{H}}^{2}(G,\mathbb{C}^{\times})
is called the Schur multiplier of
G
.
For a
2
-cocycle
\alpha
of
G
,
[\alpha]\in{\mathrm{H}}^{2}(G,\mathbb{C}^{\times})
denotes the cohomology class of
\alpha
contains all the
2
-cocycles cohomologous to
\alpha
.
A complex projective representation of
G
corresponding to the
2
-cocycle
\alpha
is a map
\rho:G\to GL(V)
satisfying

\rho(1)=id_{V},~\rho(gh)=\alpha(g,h)\rho(g)\rho(h),g,h\in G,

where
V
is a complex vector space. Then we say
\rho
is an
\alpha
-representation of
G
; see
[
6

for the details.

\mathrm{Irr}^{\alpha}(G)
denotes the set of all irreducible
\alpha
-representations of
G
up to linear equivalence.

If
G=G_{1}\times G_{2}
, then
by
[
8
, Theorem 2.3, p. 20]
, we have

(1.0.1)

\displaystyle{\mathrm{H}}^{2}(G,\mathbb{C}^{\times})\cong{\mathrm{H}}^{2}(G_{1},\mathbb{C}^{\times})\times{\mathrm{H}}^{2}(G_{2},\mathbb{C}^{\times})\times{\mathrm{Hom}}(G_{1}/G_{1}^{\prime}\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}}G_{2}/G_{2}^{\prime},\mathbb{C}^{\times}).

A

-cocycle
\alpha

[\alpha]
of

belongs to
{\mathrm{Hom}}(G_{1}/G_{1}^{\prime}\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}}G_{2}/G_{2}^{\prime},\mathbb{C}^{\times})

For convenience, we use the notation

instead of the abelian tensor product
G_{1}/G_{1}^{\prime}\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}}G_{2}/G_{2}^{\prime}
without further reference.
If the cohomology class

{\mathrm{H}}^{2}(G_{1},\mathbb{C}^{\times})\times{\mathrm{H}}^{2}(G_{2},\mathbb{C}^{\times})

are linearly equivalent to the elements of the form

But this fact is not known if

. Hence, in this article, we give a construction of the elements of

for the

-groups

for bilinear cocycles

of
