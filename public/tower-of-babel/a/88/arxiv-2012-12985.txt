AN IDEAL PROOF FOR FUJISAWA’S RESULT AND ITS GENERALIZATION


Abstract
We give a generalization of Fujisawa’s theorem in [ 2 ] . Our proof of the generalized theorem is purely algebraic and it is much simpler than his proof. A key theorem for the generalized theorem is one of a key ingredient for the construction of the Steenbrink isomorphism in a recent preprint [ 9 ] .
