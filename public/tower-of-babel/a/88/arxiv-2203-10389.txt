On m-quasi-ideals in m-regular ordered semigroups
Susmita Mallick

Abstract
In this paper we characterize left(right) ideals, bi-ideals and quasi-ideals of an ordered semigroup by an index m m and give some important interplays between these ideals. The concept of m-regularity of an ordered semigroups has been introduced. Moreover m-regular ordered semigroups are characterized by their m-quasi-ideals and the fact that for any m-regular ordered semigroups A A , the set Q A Q_{A} of all m-quasi-ideals of A A , with multiplication defined by: Q 1 ∘ Q 2 = ( Q 1 Q 2 ] Q_{1}\circ Q_{2}=(Q_{1}Q_{2}] , for all Q 1 , Q 2 ∈ Q A Q_{1},Q_{2}\in Q_{A} , is a m-regular semigroup is obtained here.
