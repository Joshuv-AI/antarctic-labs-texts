On the effect of localized and delocalized quasiparticles on a nonlinear conductivity of superconductors
A. Gurevich

Abstract
Recently Deyo et al. [Phys. Rev. B 106 , 104502 (2022)] suggested that the surface resistance R s ​ ( H 0 ) R_{s}(H_{0}) of a superconductor under strong electromagnetic field can be affected by field-induced quasiparticle bound states at the surface.
