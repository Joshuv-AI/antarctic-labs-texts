The spectral gluing theorem revisited
Dario Beraldo

Abstract




Abstract.
We strengthen the
gluing theorem
(the main result of
[
AG18
]
) occurring on the spectral side of the geometric Langlands conjecture. The gluing theorem of
[
AG18
]
provides a fully faithful embedding of
{\operatorname{IndCoh}}_{\mathcal{N}}({{\operatorname{LS}}_{G}})
into
\mathsf{Glue}_{P}I(G,P)
, a DG category glued out of “Fourier coefficients" parametrized by the standard parabolic subgroups of
G
. Our refinement explicitly identifies the essential image of such embedding. The key idea is to introduce, for each inclusion
{Q\subseteq P}
, a
mixed
Fourier coefficient
I(G,{Q\subseteq P})
. Formation of the latter is covariant in
P
and contravariant in
Q
, hence the assignment
[{Q\subseteq P}]\rightsquigarrow I(G,{Q\subseteq P})
is a functor out of
twisted arrows
of standard parabolics. Our theorem states that
{\operatorname{IndCoh}}_{\mathcal{N}}({{\operatorname{LS}}_{G}})
is the limit of such functor.

Keywords.
Geometric Langlands program; quasi-smooth schemes; derived algebraic geometry; formal completions; Springer fibers; ind-coherent sheaves.

2.1. Shift of grading

Consider the
\infty

2.1.1.

The definition of

•

Example 2.1.2

If

Example 2.1.3

Let

2.1.4.

The latter example shows that the shift/unshift automorphisms do not commute with the forgetful functor

2.1.5.

Recall that

Example 2.1.6

Let

2.2.2.

We say that a DG scheme

Step 4.

4.2.13

P

w
