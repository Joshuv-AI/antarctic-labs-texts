Moduli of Rank Two Semistable Sheaves on Rational Fano Threefolds of the Main Series


Abstract
. In this paper we investigate the moduli spaces of semistable coherent sheaves of rank two on the projective space ℙ 3 {\mathbb{P}^{3}} and the following rational Fano manifolds of the main series - the three-dimensional quadric X 2 X_{2} , the intersection of two 4-dimensional quadrics X 4 X_{4} and the Fano manifold X 5 X_{5} of degree 5. For the quadric X 2 X_{2} , the boundedness of the third Chern class c 3 c_{3} of rank two semistable objects in D b ​ ( X 2 ) \mathrm{D}^{b}(X_{2}) , including sheaves, is proved. An explicit description is given of all the moduli spaces of semistable sheaves of rank two on X 2 X_{2} , including reflexive ones, with a maximal third class c 3 ≥ 0 c_{3}\geq 0 . These spaces turn out to be irreducible smooth rational manifolds in all cases, except for the following two: ( c 1 , c 2 , c 3 ) = ( 0 , 2 , 2 ) (c_{1},c_{2},c_{3})=(0,2,2) or (0,4,8). Several new infinite series of rational components of the moduli spaces of semistable sheaves of rank two on ℙ 3 {\mathbb{P}^{3}} , X 2 X_{2} , X 4 X_{4} and X 5 X_{5} are constructed, as well as a new infinite series of irrational components on X 4 X_{4} . The boundedness of the class c 3 c_{3} is proved for c 1 = 0 c_{1}=0 and any c 2 > 0 c_{2}>0 for stable reflexive sheaves of general type on manifolds X 4 X_{4} and X 5 X_{5} .

Introduction

In 1980, R. Hartshorne, while investigating in
[
16
]
the spectra of stable
reflexive coherent sheaves of rank two on the projective space

\mathbb{P}^{3}

c_{3}
of these sheaves
for fixed first and second Chern classes
c_{1}
and
c_{2}

The exact estimates he obtained for the class

have the form (see
[
16

)

(1)

c_{3}\leq c^{2}_{2}-c_{2}+2,\ \ \ \text{if}\ \ \ c_{1}=0;\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ c_{3}\leq c^{2}_{2}\ \ \ \text{if}\ \ \ c_{1}=-1.

In the same work, the irreducibility, smoothness and rationality of the moduli spaces of such sheaves
with
c_{1}=-1
, arbitrary
c_{2}>0
and maximum
c_{3}=c^{2}_{2}
are proved.
In 2018, B. Schmidt in
[
28

, investigating the properties of tilt stability in the derived category
\mathrm{D}^{b}(\mathbb{P}^{3})

proved that the estimates (
1

sheaves of rank two on
\mathbb{P}^{3}

space for
-1\leq c_{1}\leq 0
,
c_{2}>0
and maximal
c_{3}

obtained that these spaces are irreducible smooth
rational projective varieties. It is not difficult to see that
the moduli spaces of reflexive sheaves described by Hartshorne

We also note that quite recently in the 2023 work
[
30

generalized the above results to the case of sheaves on
\mathbb{P}^{3}
of all ranks from 0 to 4.
