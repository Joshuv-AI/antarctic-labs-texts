Persistence property and the local well-posedness of the modified Camassa-Holm equation in critical Besov equation
Zhen He, Zhaoyang Yin

Abstract
In this paper, we first establish the local well-posednesss for the Cauchy problem of a modified Camassa-Holm (MOCH) equation in critical Besov spaces B p , 1 1 p B^{\frac{1}{p}}_{p,1} with 1 ≤ p < + ∞ . 1\leq p<+\infty. The obtained results improve considerably the recent result in [ 23 ] . Then we show the persiscence property of MOCH. 2020 Mathematics Subject Classification : 35Q30, 35Q84, 76B03, 76D05. Keywords : A modified Camassa-Holm equation; local well-posedness problem ; Persistence property.
