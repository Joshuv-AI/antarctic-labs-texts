Trisections and Ozsváth-Szabó Cobordism Invariants
William E. Olsen

Abstract
. Given a smooth, compact four-manifold X X viewed as a cobordism from the empty set to its connected boundary, we demonstrate how to use the data of a trisection map π : X 4 → ℝ 2 \pi:X^{4}\to\mathbb{R}^{2} to compute the induced cobordism maps on Heegaard Floer homology associated to X X .

[

The second feature we’d like to highlight, and perhaps most important in the author’s opinion, is that Theorem

Trisections as singular fibrations

(3.1)

\partial[\mathbf{x},i]=\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}y\in\mathbb{T}_{\bm{\alpha}}\cap\mathbb{T}_{\bm{\beta}}\\

Clearly, the plus, minus, and infinity variations are related by a short exact sequence

0\to CF^{-}(\mathcal{H},\mathfrak{s})\to CF^{\infty}(\mathcal{H},\mathfrak{s})\to CF^{+}(\mathcal{H},\mathfrak{s})\to 0

\cdots\to HF^{+}_{\ast+1}(Y,\mathfrak{s})\xrightarrow{\delta}HF^{-}_{\ast}(Y,\mathfrak{s})\to HF^{\infty}_{\ast}(Y,\mathfrak{s})\to\cdots
