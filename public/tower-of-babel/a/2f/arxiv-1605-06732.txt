2.1
Work space

We define the homogeneous fractional Sobolev space
\mathcal{D}^{\alpha,2}(\mathbb{R}^{3})
as follows

\mathcal{D}^{\alpha,2}(\mathbb{R}^{3})=\Big\{u\in L^{2_{\alpha}^{\ast}}(\mathbb{R}^{3})\,\,\Big|\,\,|\xi|^{\alpha}\widehat{u}(\xi)\in L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{3})\Big\}

which is the completion of

under the norm

\|u\|_{\mathcal{D}^{\alpha,2}}=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{3}}|(-\Delta)^{\frac{\alpha}{2}}u|^{2}\,{\rm d}x=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{3}}|\xi|^{2\alpha}|\widehat{u}(\xi)|^{2}\,{\rm d}\xi

The fractional Sobolev space
H^{\alpha}(\mathbb{R}^{3})
can be described by means of the Fourier transform, i.e.

H^{\alpha}(\mathbb{R}^{3})=\Big\{u\in L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{3})\,\,\Big|\,\,\int_{\mathbb{R}^{3}}(|\xi|^{2\alpha}|\widehat{u}(\xi)|^{2}+|\widehat{u}(\xi)|^{2})\,{\rm d}\xi<+\infty\Big\}.

In this case, the inner product and the norm are defined as

(u,v)=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{3}}(|\xi|^{2\alpha}\widehat{u}(\xi)\overline{\widehat{v}(\xi)}+\widehat{u}(\xi)\overline{\widehat{v}(\xi)})\,{\rm d}\xi

\|u\|_{H^{\alpha}}=\bigg(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{3}}(|\xi|^{2\alpha}|\widehat{u}(\xi)|^{2}+|\widehat{u}(\xi)|^{2})\,{\rm d}\xi\bigg)^{\frac{1}{2}},

From Plancherel’s theorem we have
\|u\|_{2}=\|\widehat{u}\|_{2}
and
\||\xi|^{\alpha}\widehat{u}\|_{2}=\|(-\Delta)^{\frac{\alpha}{2}}u\|_{2}
. Hence

\|u\|_{H^{\alpha}}=\bigg(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{3}}(|(-\Delta)^{\frac{\alpha}{2}}u(x)|^{2}+|u(x)|^{2})\,{\rm d}x\bigg)^{\frac{1}{2}},\quad\forall u\in H^{\alpha}(\mathbb{R}^{3}).

In terms of finite differences, the fractional Sobolev space
H^{\alpha}(\mathbb{R}^{3})
also can be defined as follows

H^{\alpha}(\mathbb{R}^{3})=\Big\{u\in L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{3})\,\,\Big|\,\,\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|}{|x-y|^{\frac{3}{2}+\alpha}}\in L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{3}\times\mathbb{R}^{3})\Big\}

endowed with the natural norm

\|u\|_{H^{\alpha}}=\bigg(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{3}}|u|^{2}\,{\rm d}x+\int_{\mathbb{R}^{3}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{3}}\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{2}}{|x-y|^{3+2\alpha}}\,{\rm d}x\,{\rm d}y\bigg)^{\frac{1}{2}}.

Also, in light of Proposition 3.4 and Proposition 3.6 in
[
28
]
, we have

\|(-\Delta)^{\frac{\alpha}{2}}u\|_{2}^{2}=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{3}}|\xi|^{2\alpha}|\widehat{u}(\xi)|^{2}\,{\rm d}\xi=\frac{1}{C(\alpha)}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{3}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{3}}\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|^{2}}{|x-y|^{3+2\alpha}}\,{\rm d}x\,{\rm d}y.

It is well known that

is continuously embedded into

(7)

It is easy to show that problem (

\displaystyle\leq\Big(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{3}}|u(x)|^{\frac{12}{3+2s}}\,{\rm d}x\Big)^{\frac{3+2s}{6}}\Big(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{3}}|v(x)|^{2_{s}^{\ast}}\,{\rm d}x\Big)^{\frac{1}{2_{s}^{\ast}}}

\displaystyle\leq S_{s}^{\frac{1}{2}}\Big(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{3}}|u(x)|^{\frac{12}{3+2s}}\,{\rm d}x\Big)^{\frac{3+2s}{6}}\|v\|_{\mathcal{D}^{s,2}}\leq S_{s}^{\frac{1}{2}}C\|u\|_{H^{s}}^{2}\|v\|_{\mathcal{D}^{s,2}},

where using the following fact that

Definition 2.4

(i)

(15)
