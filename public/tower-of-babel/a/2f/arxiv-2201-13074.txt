Nuclear spin-lattice relaxation studies of Cu 2 O
Yutaka Itoh

Abstract
We report the 63 Cu and 65 Cu nuclear spin-lattice relaxation rate measurements of cuprous oxide Cu 2 O in a zero field Cu nuclear quadrupole resonance at T T = 77 − - 325 K. From the detailed isotopic measurements of the relaxation rates, we successfully estimated a finite magnetic relaxation rate W M 63 {}^{63}W_{M} and a predominant nuclear quadrupole relaxation rate W Q 63 {}^{63}W_{Q} . W Q 63 {}^{63}W_{Q} changed as T 2.1 T^{2.1} , while W M 63 {}^{63}W_{M} changed as T 1.6 T^{1.6} or T β exp ( − Δ / T ) T^{\beta}\mathrm{exp}(-\it\Delta/T) with β \beta = 0.6(3) and Δ \it\Delta = 190(62) K. The nuclear spin scattering process due to a non-degenerate Fermi gas was discussed as a possible candidate of the magnetic relaxation.
