Hjorth’s reflection argument
Grigor Sargsyan

Abstract
In [ 7 ] , Hjorth, assuming 𝖠𝖣 + 𝖹𝖥 + 𝖣𝖢 {\sf{AD+ZF+DC}} , showed that there is no sequence of length ω 2 \omega_{2} consisting of distinct 𝚺 2 1 \mathbf{\Sigma}^{1}_{2} -sets. We show that the same theory implies that for n ≥ 0 n\geq 0 , there is no sequence of length δ 2 ​ n + 2 1 \mathbf{\delta}^{1}_{2n+2} consisting of distinct 𝚺 2 ​ n + 2 1 \mathbf{\Sigma}^{1}_{2n+2} sets. The theorem settles Question 30.21 of [ 16 ] , which was also conjectured by Kechris in [ 17 ] (see Conjecture in Chapter 4 of [ 17 ] and the last paragraph of Chapter 4 of [ 17 ] ).



A central theme in descriptive set theory is the study of the complexity of various natural processes in terms of their ordinal lengths. In this line of thought, it is often shown that some ordinal is unreachable via processes of certain complexity. For example, there is no analytic well-founded relation of length
\omega_{1}

{\mathbb{R}}
