Some algebraic surfaces with canonical map of degree 10, 12, 14
NGUYEN BIN

Abstract
Surfaces of general type with canonical map of degree d d bigger than 8 8 have bounded geometric genus and irregularity. In particular the irregularity is at most 2 2 if d ≥ 10 d\geq 10 . In the present paper, the existence of surfaces with d = 10 d=10 and all possible irregularities, surfaces with d = 12 d=12 and irregularity 1 1 and 2 2 , and surfaces with d = 14 d=14 and irregularity 0 0 and 1 1 is proven, by constructing these surfaces as ℤ 2 3 \mathbb{Z}_{2}^{3} -covers of certain rational surfaces. These results together with the construction by C. Rito of a surface with d = 12 d=12 and irregularity 0 0 show that all the possibilities for the irregularity in the cases d = 10 d=10 , d = 12 d=12 can occur, whilst the existence of a surface with d = 14 d=14 and irregularity 2 2 is still an open problem.
