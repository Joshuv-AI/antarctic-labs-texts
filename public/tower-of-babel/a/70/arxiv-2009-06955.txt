Abstract
Let G G be a graph and C C a finite set of colours. A vertex colouring f : V ⁡ ( G ) → C f:V(G)\to C is complete provided that for any two distinct colours c 1 , c 2 ∈ C c_{1},c_{2}\in C there is v 1 ​ v 2 ∈ E ⁡ ( G ) v_{1}v_{2}\in E(G) such that f ⁡ ( v i ) = c i f(v_{i})=c_{i} , i = 1 , 2 i=1,2 . The achromatic number of G G is the maximum number achr ⁡ ( G ) \mathrm{achr}(G) of colours in a proper complete vertex colouring of G G . In the paper it is proved that if q ≥ 41 q\geq 41 is an odd integer, then the achromatic number of the Cartesian product of K 6 K_{6} and K q K_{q} is 2 ​ q + 3 2q+3 .

∎

There are still only few graph classes

[

[

Some papers are devoted to the achromatic number of graphs constructed by graph operations. So, Hell and Miller
[

considered

In this paper we are interested in the achromatic number of the Cartesian product
