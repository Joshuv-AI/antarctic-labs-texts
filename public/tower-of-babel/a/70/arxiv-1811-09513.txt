Finiteness of cohomology groups of stacks of shtukas as modules over Hecke algebras, and applications
Cong Xue

Abstract




Abstract.
In this paper we prove that the cohomology groups with compact support of stacks of shtukas are modules of finite type over a Hecke algebra. As an application, we extend the construction of excursion operators, defined in
[
Laf18
]
on the space of cuspidal automorphic forms, to the space of automorphic forms with compact support. This gives the Langlands parametrization for some quotient spaces of the latter, which is compatible with the constant term morphism.

Keywords.
Stacks of shtukas;
\ell
-adic cohomology; Hecke algebra

14G35; 11F70

Introduction

Let
X
be a smooth projective geometrically connected curve over a finite field
{\mathbb F}_{q}
. We denote by
F
its function field,
\mathbb A
the ring of adèles of
F
and
\mathbb O
the ring of integral adèles.

Let
G
be a connected split reductive group over
{\mathbb F}_{q}
.

Let
\Xi
be a cocompact subgroup in
Z_{G}(F)\backslash Z_{G}(\mathbb A)
, where
Z_{G}
is the center of
G
. Then the quotient
Z_{G}(F)\backslash Z_{G}(\mathbb A)/Z_{G}(\mathbb O)\Xi
is finite. Let
N\subset X
be a finite subscheme. We denote by
\mathcal{O}_{N}
the ring of functions on
N
and
K_{N}:=\operatorname{Ker}(G(\mathbb O)\rightarrow G(\mathcal{O}_{N}))
.

Let
\ell
be a prime number not dividing
q
. Let
E
be a finite extension of
\mathbb{Q}_{\ell}
containing a square root of
q
. Let
\mathcal{O}_{E}
be the ring of integers of
E
.
We denote by
C_{c}(G(F)\backslash G(\mathbb A)/K_{N}\Xi,E)
the vector space of automophic forms with compact support.

Let
u
be a place in
X\smallsetminus N
. Let
\mathcal{O}_{u}
be the complete local ring at
u
and let
F_{u}
be its field of fractions. Let
\mathscr{H}_{G,u}:=C_{c}(G(\mathcal{O}_{u})\backslash G(F_{u})/G(\mathcal{O}_{u}),E)
be the Hecke algebra of
G
at the place
u
.
The algebra
\mathscr{H}_{G,u}
acts on
C_{c}(G(F)\backslash G(\mathbb A)/K_{N}\Xi,E)
by convolution on the right.
The following proposition was already known by some experts:

Proposition 1
.

For any place
u
of
X\smallsetminus N
, the vector space
C_{c}(G(F)\backslash G(\mathbb A)/K_{N}\Xi,E)
is a
\mathscr{H}_{G,u}
-module of finite type.

Due to the lack of reference, in Section 1 we recall the proof of Proposition
1
for
G=SL_{2}
to illustrate the idea of the proof in the general case.

Let
\widehat{G}
be the Langlands dual group of
G
over
E
(i.e. the reductive group whose roots and weights are the coroots and coweights of
G
, and vice-versa). Let
I
be a finite set and
W
be a finite dimensional
E
-linear representation of
\widehat{G}^{I}
. Associated to these data, in
[
Var04
]
Varshavsky defined the stack classifying
G
-shtukas (denoted by
\operatorname{Cht}_{G,N,I,W}
) over
(X\smallsetminus N)^{I}
and
its
\ell
-adic cohomology groups with compact support in any degree
j\in{\mathbb Z}
(denoted by
H_{G,N,I,W,{\overline{x}}}^{j}
,
where
{\overline{x}}
is a geometric point of
(X\smallsetminus N)^{I}
).
In particular, when
I=\emptyset
and
W=\bf 1
(the one-dimensional trivial representation of the trivial group
\widehat{G}^{\emptyset}
), the cohomology group
H_{G,N,\emptyset,{\bf 1},{\overline{x}}}^{0}
coincides with
C_{c}(G(F)\backslash G(\mathbb{A})/K_{N}\Xi,E)
.

The cohomology group
H_{G,N,I,W,{\overline{x}}}^{j}
is equipped with an action of
\mathscr{H}_{G,u}
by Hecke correspondences on the stacks of shtukas. Suppose that
{\overline{x}}
satisfies the condition in
2.1.15
(for example
{\overline{x}}
can be a geometric generic point of
X^{I}
).
One main result in this paper is

Theorem 2
.

For any place
u
of
X\smallsetminus N
, the cohomology group
H_{G,N,I,W,{\overline{x}}}^{j}
is of finite type as
\mathscr{H}_{G,u}
-module.

Since
H_{G,N,\emptyset,{\bf 1},{\overline{x}}}^{0}=C_{c}(G(F)\backslash G(\mathbb{A})/K_{N}\Xi,E)
, Theorem
2
is a generalization of Proposition
1
.
The proof of Theorem
2
is given in Section 2. The idea is similar to the case of automorphic forms explained in Section 1. In addition, we use the constant term morphisms of the cohomology groups of stacks of shtukas and the contractibility of deep enough Harder-Narasimhan strata established in
[
Xue20
]
.

As an application, in Section 3, we extend the excursion operators, defined in
[
Laf18
]
on the vector space of cuspidal automorphic forms
C_{c}^{\operatorname{cusp}}(G(F)\backslash G(\mathbb{A})/K_{N}\Xi,E)
, to
C_{c}(G(F)\backslash G(\mathbb{A})/K_{N}\Xi,E)
.

Concretely, fix an algebraic closure
{\overline{F}}
of
F
. Let
\eta^{I}
be the generic point of
X^{I}
and fix a geometric point
{\overline{\eta^{I}}}
over
\eta^{I}
. The cohomology group
H_{G,N,I,W,{\overline{\eta^{I}}}}^{j}
is equipped with an action of
\pi_{1}(\eta^{I},{\overline{\eta^{I}}})
and an action of the partial Frobenius morphisms.
In
[
Laf18
]
, V. Lafforgue

(1)

defined a Hecke-finite part of
H_{G,N,I,W,{\overline{\eta^{I}}}}^{j}
(denoted by
H_{G,N,I,W,{\overline{\eta^{I}}}}^{j,\,\operatorname{Hf}}
). Then he used Drinfeld’s lemma (for
\mathcal{O}_{E}
-modules of finite type) to construct an action of
\operatorname{Gal}({\overline{F}}/F)^{I}
on
H_{G,N,I,W,{\overline{\eta^{I}}}}^{j,\,\operatorname{Hf}}
.

(2)

with the help of (1), he constructed the excursion operators acting on
C_{c}^{\operatorname{cusp}}(G(F)\backslash G(\mathbb{A})/K_{N}\Xi,E)
.

(3)

with the help of (2), he obtained a canonical decomposition of
C_{c}^{\operatorname{cusp}}(G(F)\backslash G(\mathbb{A})/K_{N}\Xi,{\overline{{\mathbb Q}_{\ell}}})
indexed by Langlands parameters.

In this paper,

(1’)

thanks to Theorem
2
, we apply a variant of Drinfeld’s lemma (for
\mathscr{H}_{G,u}
-modules of finite type) to
H_{G,N,I,W,{\overline{\eta^{I}}}}^{j}
and obtain an action of
\operatorname{Weil}({\overline{F}}/F)^{I}
on
H_{G,N,I,W,{\overline{\eta^{I}}}}^{j}
(Proposition
3.2.15
).

(2’)

with the help of (1’),
we construct the excursion operators acting on
C_{c}(G(F)\backslash G(\mathbb{A})/K_{N}\Xi,E)
in Section
3.4
. When restricted to
C_{c}^{\operatorname{cusp}}(G(F)\backslash G(\mathbb{A})/K_{N}\Xi,E)
, these excursion operators coincide with those defined in (2).

(3’)

Let
\mathscr{I}
be a finite codimensional ideal of
\mathscr{H}_{G,u}
.
With the help of (2’),
we obtain a canonical decomposition of the quotient vector space

C_{c}(G(F)\backslash G(\mathbb{A})/K_{N}\Xi,{\overline{{\mathbb Q}_{\ell}}})/\mathscr{I}\cdot C_{c}(G(F)\backslash G(\mathbb{A})/K_{N}\Xi,{\overline{{\mathbb Q}_{\ell}}})

indexed by
Langlands parameters (Theorem
3.6.7
).

More generally, in Section
3.7
, we construct the excursion operators acting on the cohomology groups of stacks of shtukas, which are compatible with the action of the Hecke algebras and the action of the Galois group. Moreover, there is a canonical decomposition similar to (3’) for some quotient vector spaces of the cohomology groups.

In Section 4, we show that the action of the excursion operators commutes with the constant term morphisms. As a consequence, the parametrization in (3’) is compatible with the constant term morphisms.

1. Example of automorphic forms for
SL_{2}

In this section, we consider the cohomology group of stacks of shtukas without paws, i.e. the space of automorphic forms.
In this section, let
N=\emptyset
and
G=SL_{2}
.

1.0.1.

Let
\operatorname{Bun}_{G}
be the classifying stack of
G
-bundles over
X
. For
G=SL_{2}
, it is the classifying stack of rank
2
vector bundles on
X
with trivial determinant. It is well-known that (see
[
Laf18
, rem. 8.21]
for more details)

G(F)\backslash G(\mathbb A)/G(\mathbb O)=\operatorname{Bun}_{G}({\mathbb F}_{q}).

Thus

C_{c}(G(F)\backslash G(\mathbb A)/G(\mathbb O),E)=C_{c}(\operatorname{Bun}_{G}({\mathbb F}_{q}),E).

It is also well-known that the action of
\mathscr{H}_{G,u}
by convolution on
C_{c}(G(F)\backslash G(\mathbb A)/G(\mathbb O),E)
coincides with the action of
\mathscr{H}_{G,u}
by Hecke correspondences on
C_{c}(\operatorname{Bun}_{G}({\mathbb F}_{q}),E)
.

1.0.2.

The stack
\operatorname{Bun}_{G}
has a Harder-Narasimhan stratification. For
G=SL_{2}
, we have

\operatorname{Bun}_{G}=\varinjlim_{d\in{\mathbb Z}_{\geq 0}}\operatorname{Bun}_{G}^{\leq(d,-d)},

where
\operatorname{Bun}_{G}^{\leq(d,-d)}
is the open substack of
\operatorname{Bun}_{G}
defined by the condition that the degree of any line subbundle of the rank
2
vector bundle is
\leq d
.
For any
d_{1},d_{2}\in{\mathbb Z}_{\geq 0}
such that
d_{1}\leq d_{2}
, we have an open immersion
\operatorname{Bun}_{G}^{\leq(d_{1},-d_{1})}\hookrightarrow\operatorname{Bun}_{G}^{\leq(d_{2},-d_{2})}
. It induces an inclusion
C_{c}(\operatorname{Bun}_{G}^{\leq(d_{1},-d_{1})}({\mathbb F}_{q}),E)\subset C_{c}(\operatorname{Bun}_{G}^{\leq(d_{2},-d_{2})}({\mathbb F}_{q}),E)
by extension by zero.
Thus the vector space
C_{c}(\operatorname{Bun}_{G}({\mathbb F}_{q}),E)
has a filtration
F_{G}^{\bullet}
indexed by
d\in{\mathbb Z}_{\geq 0}
:

F_{G}^{d}:=C_{c}(\operatorname{Bun}_{G}^{\leq(d,-d)}({\mathbb F}_{q}),E),

with associated graded quotients (for
d\geq 1
)

gr_{G}^{d}:=F_{G}^{d}/F_{G}^{d-1}=C_{c}(\operatorname{Bun}_{G}^{=(d,-d)}({\mathbb F}_{q}),E),

where
\operatorname{Bun}_{G}^{=(d,-d)}
is the closed substack of
\operatorname{Bun}_{G}^{\leq(d,-d)}
defined by the condition that for the canonical Harder-Narasimhan filtration
0\subset\mathcal{L}\subset\mathcal{E}
of the rank
2
vector bundle
\mathcal{E}
, we have
\operatorname{deg}\mathcal{L}=d
. (When
d=0
, we have
\operatorname{Bun}_{G}^{=(0,0)}=\operatorname{Bun}_{G}^{\leq(0,0)}
which classifies the semistable vector bundles.)

Since every
F_{G}^{d}
has finite dimension, Proposition
1
is a direct consequence of Lemma
1.0.4
below.

1.0.3.

Let
l:=\operatorname{deg}(u)
. Let
g
be the genus of
X
. Let
d_{0}=\operatorname{max}\{0,g-1\}+l
.

Lemma 1.0.4
.

We have

C_{c}(\operatorname{Bun}_{G}({\mathbb F}_{q}),E)=\mathscr{H}_{G,u}\cdot F_{G}^{d_{0}}.

Proof.
Applying successively Lemma
1.0.8
below to
d_{0}+1
,
d_{0}+2
,
d_{0}+3
,
\cdots
, we deduce that for any
d>d_{0}
, we have
F_{G}^{d}\subset\mathscr{H}_{G,u}\cdot F_{G}^{d_{0}}.
This implies Lemma
1.0.4
.

\square

.)

As a consequence, if

Moreover, by Lemma

1.6

1.6

\square

2.4.4.
