Global existence and Blow-up for the 1D damped compressible Euler equations with time and space dependent perturbation
Yuusuke Sugiyama

Abstract
In this paper, we consider the 1D Euler equation with time and space dependent damping term − a ⁡ ( t , x ) ​ v -a(t,x)v . It has long been known that when a ⁡ ( t , x ) a(t,x) is a positive constant or 0 0 , the solution exists globally in time or blows up in finite time, respectively. We prove that those results are invariant with respect to time and space dependent perturbations. We suppose that the coefficient a a satisfies the following condition | a ⁡ ( t , x ) − μ 0 | ≤ a 1 ​ ( t ) + a 2 ​ ( x ) , |a(t,x)-\mu_{0}|\leq a_{1}(t)+a_{2}(x), where μ 0 ≥ 0 \mu_{0}\geq 0 and a 1 a_{1} and a 2 a_{2} are integrable functions with t t and x x . Under this condition, we show the global existence and the blow-up with small initial data, when μ 0 > 0 \mu_{0}>0 and μ = 0 \mu=0 respectively.

Introduction

1.1
Problem

In this paper, we consider the following Cauchy problem of the compressible Euler equation with time and space dependent damping

\displaystyle\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}u_{t}-v_{x}=0,\\
v_{t}+p(u)_{x}=-a(t,x)v,\\
(u(0,x),v(0,x))=(1+\varepsilon\varphi(x),\varepsilon\psi(x)).\end{array}\right.

Here

is the Lagrangian spatial variable and
t\in\mathbb{R}_{+}
is time.
u=u(t,x)
and

are the real valued unknown functions, which stand for the specific volume and the fluid velocity.

\varepsilon

In the case with

1.1

In the case with

We assume that the flow is barotropic ideal gases. Namely the pressure

\displaystyle p(u)=\frac{u^{-\gamma}}{\gamma}\ \ \mbox{for}\ \gamma>1.

(1.5)

For initial data, in order to avoid the singularity of

such that for all

\displaystyle u(0,x)\geq\delta_{0}.

The local existence and the uniqueness of solutions of (

initial data (e.g. Friedrichs
[
7
]
, Lax
[
12
]
and Li and Wu
[

If

such that

1.2
Assumption on

We impose that the damping coefficient is a integrable perturbation with a constant

In that is, for the damping coefficient

\displaystyle|a(t,x)-\mu_{0}|+|a_{x}(t,x)|+|a_{t}(t,x)|\leq a_{1}(t)+a_{2}(x),

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\infty}a_{1}(t)dt+\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}a_{2}(x)dx=C_{a}<\infty,

\displaystyle x\dfrac{d}{dx}a_{2}(x)\leq 0.

\displaystyle a(t,x)\geq 0.

The typical example of

\displaystyle a(t,x)=\mu_{0}+\mu_{1}(1+t)^{-\lambda_{1}}+\mu_{2}(1+|x|)^{-\lambda_{2}},

where

,
