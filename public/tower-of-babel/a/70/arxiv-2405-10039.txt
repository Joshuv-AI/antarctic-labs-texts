High-Scale SUSY from Sgoldstino Inflation


Abstract
We review a number of unimodular no-scale supergravity models with F-term SUSY breaking which support technically natural de Sitter vacua. A variant of these models develops a stage of inflection-point inflation which can be realized for subplanckian field values consistently with the observational data. For central value of the spectral index n s n_{\rm s} , the necessary tuning is of the order of 10 − 6 10^{-6} , the tensor-to-scalar ratio is tiny whereas the running of n s n_{\rm s} is around − 3 ⋅ 10 − 3 -3\cdot 10^{-3} . Our proposal is compatible with high-scale SUSY and the results of LHC on the Higgs boson mass. Published in PoS CORFU 2023 , 055 (2024).

2.1
SUSY breaking within SUGRA

Within global SUSY, the scalar potential of a gauge-singlet
superfield
Z
,
V_{\rm SUSY}
, is positive semi-definite, since

V_{\rm SUSY}=|F_{Z}|^{2}~~\mbox{with}~~F_{Z}=\partial_{Z}W.

(1)

Here
W=W(Z)
is an holomorphic function named superpotential.
Spontaneous SUSY breaking occurs when
\langle{F_{Z}}\rangle\neq 0
which
results to
\langle{V_{\rm SUSY}}\rangle^{1/4}>0
. The non discovery of SUSY
in LHC dictates
\langle{F_{Z}}\rangle^{1/2}>1~{\mbox{\rm TeV}}
. On the other hand,

\langle{F_{Z}}\rangle^{1/2}
may be identified with the cosmological constant

\Lambda_{\rm CC}\simeq 2.3~{\rm meV}
. Therefore, we obtain an
inconsistency. The spontaneous SUSY breaking is accompanied with
the presence of a massless fermion named goldstino and for this
reason its SUSY partner,
Z
, is called sgoldstino.

Within local SUSY – i.e. SUGRA – the F-term scalar potential is
given by

V_{\rm SUGRA}=e^{G}\left(G^{ZZ^{*}}G_{Z}G_{Z^{*}}-3\right)~~\mbox{where}~~G:=K+\ln|W|^{2}

(2)

is the Kähler-invariant function and
K=K(Z,Z^{*})
the Kähler potential.
Also
G_{ZZ^{*}}=K_{ZZ^{*}}=\partial_{Z}\partial_{Z^{*}}K
is the Kähler metric and
K^{ZZ^{*}}=K_{ZZ^{*}}^{-1}
. In this context, SUSY is
broken again when
\langle{F^{Z}}\rangle\neq 0
where
F^{Z}=e^{G/2}K^{ZZ^{*}}G_{Z^{*}}
which may occur with
\langle{V_{\rm SUGRA}}\rangle\simeq 0
. This mechanism is accompanied with the absorption
of the goldstino by the gravitino,
\widetilde{G}
, which acquires mass
according to “super-Higgs” mechanism

m_{3/2}=\langle{e^{G/2}}\rangle=\langle{e^{K/2}W}\rangle=\langle{G_{Z{Z^{*}}}F^{Z}\bar{F}^{Z^{*}}-V_{\rm SUGRA}}\rangle^{1/2}/\sqrt{3}.

(3)

Based on this formalism we can obtain several models of SUSY
breaking
[
7
,
9
,
10
,
8
]
. One of the key
ingredients for the successful implementation of this scenario is
the determination of a naturally realistic vacuum for
V_{\rm SUGRA}
which may be Minkowski for
\langle{V_{\rm SUGRA}}\rangle=0
or de
Sitter for
\langle{V_{\rm SUGRA}}\rangle>0
.

2.2
Minkowski Vacua In no-scale SUGRA

Within no-scale SUGRA
[
1

direction which naturally yields
V_{\rm SUGRA}=0

V_{\rm SUGRA}=0

0=e^{K}\left(g_{K}^{-1}\left|\partial_{Z}W+WK_{Z}\right|^{2}-3|W|^{2}\right),~~\mbox{where}~~g_{K}^{-1}=K_{ZZ^{*}}^{-1}=K^{ZZ^{*}}

(4)

Z=Z^{*}
is stable – this assumption
can be verified a posteriori. Indeed, solving Eq. (
4

W=W_{0}(Z)

\frac{dW_{0}}{dZW_{0}}=\pm\sqrt{3g_{K}}-K_{Z}~~\Rightarrow~~W_{0}^{\pm}=m\exp\left(\pm\int dZ\sqrt{3g_{K}}-\int dZK_{Z}\right).

(5)

with
{}^{\prime}=d/dZ
. E.g., if we select the Kähler potential:

•

K=-3\ln(T+T^{*})

[
11

W_{0}^{-}=m

•

K=|Z|^{2}

totally novel result

Taking as inputs the parameters above we can investigate the

(a) The slow-roll parameters.

They are
found to be

\epsilon=\left(\frac{V_{{\rm I},\widehat{z}}}{\sqrt{2}V_{{\rm I}}}\right)^{2}\simeq\frac{v_{1}+\delta z(2v_{2}+3\delta zv_{3})}{\sqrt{2}J_{0}v_{0}}~~\mbox{and}~~\eta=\frac{V_{{\rm I},\widehat{z}\widehat{z}}}{V_{\rm I}}\simeq\frac{2(v_{2}+3\delta zv_{3})}{J_{0}^{2}v_{0}}\,.

The realization of IPI is delimited by the condition

{\footnotesize\sf max}\{\epsilon(\widehat{z}),|\eta(\widehat{z})|\}\leq 1,

(33)

which is saturated for
\delta z=\delta z_{\rm f}
found as follows

\eta\left(\delta z_{\rm f}\right)\simeq 1~~\Rightarrow~~\delta z_{\rm f}\simeq-(J_{0}^{2}v_{0}+2v_{2})/6v_{3}<0.

(34)

J_{0}^{2}v_{0}\gg v_{2}

\delta z_{\rm f}<0
or

(b) The number of e-foldings
{N_{\star}}

k_{\star}=0.05/{\rm Mpc}

{N_{\star}}=\int_{\widehat{z}_{\rm f}}^{\widehat{z}_{\star}}d\widehat{z}\frac{V_{\rm I}}{V_{{\rm I},\widehat{z}}}=\frac{f_{N\star}-f_{N{\rm f}}}{p_{N}}~~\mbox{where}~~p_{N}=\frac{\sqrt{3v_{1}v_{3}}}{J_{0}^{2}v_{0}}\>\>\mbox{and}\>\>f_{N}(z)=\arctan\frac{v_{2}+3zv_{3}}{\sqrt{3v_{1}v_{3}}}.

k_{\star}

5.3
Inflaton Decay and Reheating

Soon after the end of IPI, the (canonically normalized) sgoldstino

\widehat{\updelta z}=\langle{J}\rangle{\updelta}z~~\mbox{with}~~{\updelta}z=z-{\rm v}~~\mbox{and}~~\langle{J}\rangle=\sqrt{\frac{N}{2}}\frac{1}{\langle{\omega}\rangle}

(41)

settles into a phase of damped oscillations abound the minimum
reheating the universe at a temperature

T_{\rm rh}=\left({72/5\pi^{2}g_{\rm rh*}}\right)^{1/4}{\Gamma}_{{\updelta}z}^{1/2}m_{\rm P}^{1/2}\>\>\mbox{where $g_{\rm rh*}=106.75$ and}\>\>\>{\Gamma}_{{\updelta}z}\simeq{\Gamma}_{3/2}+{\Gamma}_{\theta}+{\Gamma}_{\tilde{h}}

(42)

the total decay width,
{\Gamma}_{{\updelta}z}
, of
\widehat{\updelta z}
with the individual
decay widths are found to be

{\Gamma}_{3/2}\simeq\frac{\langle{\omega}\rangle^{-\sqrt{3N}}m_{z}^{5}}{96\pi m^{2}m_{\rm P}^{2}},~~{\Gamma}_{\theta}\simeq\frac{m_{z}^{3}}{16N\pi{\rm v}m_{\rm P}}~~\mbox{and}~~{\Gamma}_{\tilde{h}}=\frac{N\widehat{\mu}^{2}}{16\pi m_{\rm P}^{2}}m_{z}\,.

(43)

They express decay of
\widehat{\updelta z}
into gravitinos, pseudo-sgoldstinos
and higgsinos via the
\mu
term respectively. Note that
{\Gamma}_{\tilde{h}}

becomes rather enhanced for large
N
’s. Thanks to the high
m_{z}

and
\widehat{\mu}
values, no moduli problem arises in this context
since
T_{\rm rh}\sim 1~{\mbox{\rm PeV}}\gg 1~{\rm MeV}
.

7
Conclusions

We proposed a SUGRA model with just one gauge-singlet chiral
superfield (the Goldstino) that offers at once:

•

Tiny cosmological constant in the low-energy vacuum at the
cost of a fine tuned parameter;

•

Inflection-point inflation resulting to an adjustable
n_{\rm s}
,
a small
r
and a sizable
a_{\rm s}\sim-10^{-3}
;

•

Spontaneous SUSY breaking at the scale
\widetilde{m}\sim 100~{\mbox{\rm EeV}}
,
which is consistent with the Higgs boson mass measured at LHC
within high-scale SUSY.

It would be interesting to investigate the following issues:

•

The generation of primordial black holes, which is currently
under debate
[
24
,
25
]
during an ultra slow-roll phase.
Here we did not address the question of how
z
reaches
z_{0}
.
Since
z_{\star}<z_{0}
, we assumed that the slow-roll approximation
offers a reliable description of IPI. This is true if
z
lies
initially near
z_{0}
with a small enough kinetic energy density

[
23
]
.

•

The candidacy of intermediate-scale lightest neutralino with
mass
M_{1}\sim{\mbox{\rm EeV}}
in the interval
T_{\rm rh}<M_{1}<T_{\rm max}
as a
cold dark matter candidate adapting the production mechanism of
WIMPZILLAS
[
26
]
.

•

The realization baryogenesis via non-thermal leptogenesis
taking into account similar attempts – see e.g. Ref.
[
27
]
.

•

The reconciliation of our proposal with swampland
conjectures
[
28
]
– see e.g. Ref.
[
29
]
for relevant
modifications which may render our setting more friendly with the
string ultraviolet completions.
