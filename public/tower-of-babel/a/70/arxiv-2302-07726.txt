Tension between implications from PREX-2 data and gravitational tidal response on dense matter equation of state
\firstname Vivek Baruah \lastname Thapa \fnsep, \firstname Monika \lastname Sinha

Abstract
Recently an improved value of neutron skin thickness of Pb 208 {}^{208}\text{Pb} was reported in Lead Radius EXperiment-2 (PREX-2) to be R skin = R n − R p = ( 0.283 ± 0.071 ) R_{\text{skin}}=R_{n}-R_{p}=(0.283\pm 0.071) fm which corresponds to high estimations of nuclear symmetry energy ( E sym E_{\text{sym}} ) and its slope ( L sym L_{\text{sym}} ). The updated values of E sym E_{\text{sym}} and L sym L_{\text{sym}} commensurating to the neutron star observable estimations lie exterior to the astrophysical observed range. The higher values of L sym L_{\text{sym}} at n 0 n_{0} deduced from recent PREX-2 data correlates to matter being easily deformable (yielding higher radius values) around intermediate matter densities leading to higher values of Λ ~ \tilde{\Lambda} creating a tension between the terrestrial and astrophysical observations. In this study, we exploit this tension to constrain the Δ \Delta -scalar meson coupling parameter space.

Hadronic CDF model
