Study of J / ψ J/{\psi} → {\to} D s , d ​ V D_{s,d}V decays with perturbative QCD approach
Yueling Yang, Junfeng Sun, Jie Gao, Qin Chang, Jinshu Huang, Gongru Lu

Abstract
Inspired by the recent measurements on two-body nonleptonic J / ψ J/{\psi} weak decay at BESIII, the charm-changing J / ψ J/{\psi} → {\to} D s , d ​ V D_{s,d}V weak decays are studied with perturbative QCD approach, where V V denotes ρ {\rho} and K ∗ K^{\ast} vector mesons. It is found that branching ratio for J / ψ J/{\psi} → {\to} D s ​ ρ D_{s}{\rho} decay can reach up to 𝒪 ⁡ ( 10 − 9 ) {\cal O}(10^{-9}) , which is within the potential measurement capability of the future high-luminosity experiments.

The following are some comments.

(1)
As it is aforementioned, the
J/{\psi}

here are dominated by the color-favored factorizable contributions
and insensitive to nonfactorizable contributions. So, generally,
branching ratio for a given

{\to}

has the same order of magnitude even with different
phenomenological models.

(2)
There is a clear hierarchical pattern among branching ratios,
mainly resulting from the hierarchical structure of CKM
factors in Eq.(
2
), i.e.,

{\cal B}r(J/{\psi}{\to}D_{s}{\rho})\,{\gg}\,{\cal B}r(J/{\psi}{\to}D_{s}K^{\ast})\,{\sim}\,{\cal B}r(J/{\psi}{\to}D_{d}{\rho})\,{\gg}\,{\cal B}r(J/{\psi}{\to}D_{d}K^{\ast}).

(45)

In addition, because nonfactorizable contributions are
suppressed by both small
C_{2}
and color factor
1/N_{c}

there is an approximate relationship,

\frac{{\cal B}r(J/{\psi}{\to}D_{s}K^{\ast})}{{\cal B}r(J/{\psi}{\to}D_{s}{\rho})}\ {\approx}\ \frac{{\cal B}r(J/{\psi}{\to}D_{d}K^{\ast})}{{\cal B}r(J/{\psi}{\to}D_{d}{\rho})}\ {\approx}\ {\lambda}^{2}\,\frac{f^{2}_{K^{\ast}}}{f^{2}_{\rho}}.

(46)

{\to}

D_{s}{\rho}

{\sim}

,
which is well within the measurement capability of the future
high-luminosity experiments, such as super tau-charm factory,
LHC and SuperKEKB.

(4)
There are many uncertainties on branching ratios.
The first uncertainty from scale
t

by the inclusion of higher order corrections to HME and an

The second uncertainty from wave function models or parameter

The third uncertainty is dominated by decay constants

Our results just provide an order of magnitude estimation

IV
Summary

Within the standard model, the
J/{\psi}
meson can decay via
the weak interaction, besides the strong and electromagnetic
interactions. With anticipation of copious
J/{\psi}
data samples
at the future high-luminosity experiments and gradual improvement
of particle identification techniques, we investigated the charm-changing

J/{\psi}

{\to}

D_{s,d}{\rho}
,
D_{s,d}K^{\ast}
weak decays
with pQCD approach.
It is found that the estimated branching ratio for the color-
and CKM-favored
J/{\psi}

{\to}

D_{s}{\rho}
decay can be
up to
{\cal O}(10^{-9})
, which is very likely to be measured
in the future.
