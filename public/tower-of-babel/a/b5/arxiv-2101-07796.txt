Zeraoulia Rafik

Abstract
This note is an attempt with the open conjecture 8 8 proposed by the authors of [ 1 ] which states : Assume α , β , λ ∈ [ 0 , ∞ ) \alpha,\beta,\lambda\in[0,\infty) . Then every positive solution of the difference equation : z n + 1 = α + z n ​ β + z n − 1 ​ λ z n − 2 , n = 0 , 1 , … z_{n+1}=\frac{\alpha+z_{n}\beta+z_{n-1}\lambda}{z_{n-2}},\quad n=0,1,\ldots is bounded if and only if β = λ \beta=\lambda . We will use a construction of sub-energy function and properties of Todd’s difference equation to disprove that conjecture in general.
