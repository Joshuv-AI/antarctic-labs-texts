Study of the 7 Be( d d , 3 He) 6 Li* reaction at 5 MeV/u
Sk M. Ali 1, D. Gupta 1, K. Kundalia 1, S. Maity 1, Swapan K Saha 1, O. Tengblad 2, J.D. Ovejas 2, A. Perea 2, I. Martel 3, J. Cederkall 4, J. Park 4, A. M. Moro 5

Abstract
The measurement of the 7 Be( d d , 3 He) 6 Li* transfer cross section at 5 MeV/u is carried out. The population of the 2.186 MeV excited state of 6 Li in this reaction channel is observed for the first time. The experimental angular distributions have been analyzed in the finite range DWBA and coupled-channel frameworks. The effect of the 7 Be( d d , 3 He) 6 Li reaction on both the 6 Li and 7 Li abundances are investigated at the relevant big-bang nucleosynthesis energies. The excitation function is calculated by TALYS and normalized to the experimental data. The S S factor of the ( d d , 3 He) channel from the present work is about 50 % \% lower than existing data at nearby energies. At big-bang energies, the S S factor is about three orders of magnitude smaller than that of the ( d , p d,p ) channel. The ( d d , 3 He) reaction rate is found to have a less than 0.1 % \% effect on the 6,7 Li abundances.
