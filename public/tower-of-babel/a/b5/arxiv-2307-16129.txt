Hitting with probability one for stochastic heat equations with additive noise
Robert C. Dalang, Fei Pu

Abstract
We study the hitting probabilities of the solution to a system of d d stochastic heat equations with additive noise subject to Dirichlet boundary conditions. We show that for any bounded Borel set with positive d − 6 d-6 -dimensional capacity, the solution visits this set almost surely.

.

Case 1:

,

\displaystyle|\lambda_{i}(t,x)-\lambda_{i}(t,y)|

\displaystyle=\left|\int_{0}^{1}\sum\limits_{k=1}\limits^{\infty}e^{-\pi^{2}k^{2}t}(\phi_{k}(x)-\phi_{k}(y))\phi_{k}(v)u_{0}^{i}(v)dv\right|

\displaystyle\leqslant 2N\sum\limits_{k=1}\limits^{\infty}e^{-\pi^{2}k^{2}t}|\sin(k\pi x)-\sin(k\pi y)|

\displaystyle\leqslant 2N\pi\sum\limits_{k=1}\limits^{\infty}ke^{-\pi^{2}k^{2}t_{0}}|x-y|\leqslant c_{1}N|x-y|.

(3.11)

Case 2:

,

\displaystyle=\left|\int_{0}^{1}\sum\limits_{k=1}\limits^{\infty}(e^{-\pi^{2}k^{2}t}-e^{-\pi^{2}k^{2}s})\phi_{k}(x)\phi_{k}(v)u_{0}^{i}(v)dv\right|

\displaystyle\leqslant 2N\pi^{2}(t-s)\sum\limits_{k=1}\limits^{\infty}k^{2}e^{-\pi^{2}k^{2}t_{0}}\leqslant c_{2}N(t-s),

\displaystyle(\lambda_{i}(t,x)-\lambda_{i}(s,y))^{2}\leqslant\tilde{c}\,N^{2}((t-s)^{2}+(x-y)^{2}).

Using the upper bound on the joint probability density function of

\displaystyle p_{t,x;s,y}^{u}(z_{1},z_{2})

\displaystyle=p_{t,x;s,y}^{v}(z_{1}-\lambda(t,x),z_{2}-\lambda(s,y))

\displaystyle\leqslant\frac{c}{[\Delta((t,x);(s,y))]^{d/2}}\exp\left(-\frac{\|z_{1}-\lambda(t,x)-z_{2}+\lambda(s,y)\|^{2}}{c\,\Delta((t,x);(s,y))}\right)

which proves

Therefore, in the case

(4.2)

Proof.

Due to (4.1), we have

\displaystyle\mbox{P}^{u_{0}}\{\mathcal{A}_{k}|\mathscr{F}_{S_{k-1}}\}

\displaystyle=\mbox{P}^{u_{0}}\{\exists\,t\in[S_{k-1},S_{k-1}+T_{1}\circ\theta_{S_{k-1}}],\ \mbox{s.t.}\ X(t)\in\mathcal{A}|\mathscr{F}_{S_{k-1}}\}

\displaystyle=\mbox{P}^{u_{0}}\{\exists\,t\in[0,T_{1}\circ\theta_{S_{k-1}}],\ \mbox{s.t.}\ X(t+S_{k-1})\in\mathcal{A}|\mathscr{F}_{S_{k-1}}\}

\displaystyle=\mbox{E}^{X(S_{k-1})}[\bm{1}_{\{\exists\,t\in[0,T_{1}],\ \mbox{s.t.}\ X(t)\in\mathcal{A}\}}]

\displaystyle\geqslant c,

(4.3)

where the inequality is due to (

\displaystyle\mbox{P}^{u_{0}}\{\exists\,t\geqslant 0,\,\,\,\mbox{s.t.}\,\,\,X(t)\in\mathcal{A}\}

\displaystyle\quad\geqslant\mbox{P}^{u_{0}}\left(\bigcup\limits_{k=1}\limits^{n}\mathcal{A}_{k}\right)=1-\mbox{P}^{x}\left(\bigcap\limits_{k=1}\limits^{n}\mathcal{A}_{k}^{c}\right)

\displaystyle\quad=1-\mbox{E}^{u_{0}}\left[\left(\prod\limits_{k=1}\limits^{n-1}\bm{1}_{\mathcal{A}_{k}^{c}}\right)\mbox{P}^{x}\{\mathcal{A}_{n}^{c}|\mathscr{F}_{S_{n-1}}\}\right]

\displaystyle\quad=1-\mbox{E}^{u_{0}}\left[\left(\prod\limits_{k=1}\limits^{n-1}\bm{1}_{\mathcal{A}_{k}^{c}}\right)(1-\mbox{P}^{x}\{\mathcal{A}_{n}|\mathscr{F}_{S_{n-1}}\})\right]

(4.4)

where we repeat the argument to get the last inequality. Letting
