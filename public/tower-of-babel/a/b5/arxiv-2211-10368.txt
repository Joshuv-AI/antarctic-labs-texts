The norm residue symbol for formal Drinfeld modules
Marwa Ala Eddine

Abstract
. In this paper, we study the Kummer pairing associated with formal Drinfeld modules having stable reduction of height one. We give an explicit description of the pairing à la Kolyvagin , in terms of the logarithm of the formal Drinfeld module, a certain derivation, torsion points and the trace. The results obtained give a generalization of the results of Anglès [ 1 ] proved for Carlitz modules, and of Bars and Longhi [ 2 ] proved for sign-normalized rank one Drinfeld modules. It also presents an extension of our previous formulas proved in [ 3 ] to arbitrary finite extensions of local fields containing enough torsion points. Keywords: formal Drinfeld modules, explicit reciprocity laws, local fields, class field theory, derivations 2020 Mathematics Subject Classification: 11F85, 11S31

\operatorname{D}_{L,v_{n}}(x^{m})=mx^{m-1}\operatorname{D}_{L,v_{n}}(x)\penalty\ \penalty\ \forall x\in\mathcal{O}_{L}\penalty\ \penalty\ \text{and }\penalty\ \penalty\ \forall m\geq 1.

Lemma 3.4

Suppose
\rho
is such that
(x,x)_{\rho,L,n}=0
for all

\alpha\in\mathfrak{p}_{L}\setminus\{0\}
and let

be a unit of

(3.11)

(\alpha u,u)_{L,n}=\operatorname{T}_{L|K}((1-u)\operatorname{D}_{L,v_{n}}(\alpha))\cdot_{\rho}v_{n}.

Proof.

We have

\displaystyle(\alpha u,u)_{L,n}

\displaystyle=(\alpha u,\frac{\alpha u}{\alpha})_{L,n}

\displaystyle=(\alpha u,\alpha u)_{L,n}-(\alpha u,\alpha)_{L,n}

\displaystyle=(\alpha,\alpha)_{L,n}-(\alpha u,\alpha)_{L,n}

\displaystyle=(\alpha-\alpha u,\alpha)_{L,n}

\displaystyle=\operatorname{T}_{L|K}(\lambda_{\rho}(\alpha-\alpha u)\Psi_{L,v_{n}}(\alpha))\cdot_{\rho}v_{n}

by Proposition
2.3
. Let
\gamma=\alpha(1-u)
, we will show that

(3.12)

\operatorname{T}_{L|K}(\lambda_{\rho}(\gamma)\Psi_{L,v_{n}}(\alpha))\cdot_{\rho}v_{n}=\operatorname{T}_{L|K}(\gamma\Psi_{L,v_{n}}(\alpha))\cdot_{\rho}v_{n}.

By the hypothesis on the valuations, we have
\mu(\gamma)>\frac{nm_{0}}{q}+\frac{1}{q-1}
. Hence

\displaystyle\mu(\lambda_{\rho}(\gamma)-\gamma)

\displaystyle=\mu(\sum_{i\geq 1}c_{i}\gamma^{q^{i}})

\displaystyle\geq\min_{i\geq 1}\{\mu(c_{i})+q^{i}\mu(\gamma)\}

\displaystyle>\min_{i\geq 1}\{-i+q^{i}(\frac{nm_{0}}{q}+\frac{1}{q-1})\}

\displaystyle\geq nm_{0}+\frac{1}{q-1}

Therefore, we can write
\lambda_{\rho}(\gamma)-\gamma=\eta^{n}\delta
, where
\delta
is an element of
\mathfrak{p}_{L,1}

\operatorname{T}_{L|K}((\lambda_{\rho}(\gamma)-\gamma)\Psi_{L,v_{n}}(\alpha))\cdot_{\rho}v_{n}=0

∎

Proposition 3.5

Suppose

Let

, then, by Lemma

\displaystyle(\gamma(x+y)u,u)_{L,n}
