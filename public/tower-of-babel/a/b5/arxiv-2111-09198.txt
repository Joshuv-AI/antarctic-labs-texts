Geometry of ∗ * - k k -Ricci-Yamabe soliton and gradient ∗ * - k k -Ricci-Yamabe soliton on Kenmotsu manifolds
Santu Dey, Soumendu Roy

Abstract
. The goal of the current paper is to characterize ∗ * - k k -Ricci-Yamabe soliton within the framework on Kenmotsu manifolds. Here, we have shown the nature of the soliton and find the scalar curvature when the manifold admitting ∗ * - k k -Ricci-Yamabe soliton on Kenmotsu manifold. Next, we have evolved the characterization of the vector field when the manifold satisfies ∗ * - k k -Ricci-Yamabe soliton. Also we have embellished some applications of vector field as torse-forming in terms of ∗ * - k k -Ricci-Yamabe soliton on Kenmotsu manifold. Then, we have studied gradient ∗ \ast - η \eta -Einstein soliton to yield the nature of Riemannian curvature tensor. We have developed an example of ∗ * - k k -Ricci-Yamabe soliton on 5-dimensional Kenmotsu manifold to prove our findings.
