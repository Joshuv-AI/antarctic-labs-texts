Abstract
In this paper we shall introduced the class of d − C ​ S d-CS spaces and so we shall obtained topological approach to large Kasahara spaces. This class include complete symmetric spaces, complete quasi b b -metric spaces and complete b b -spaces, but not include d ∗ d^{*} -complete topological spaces and d d -complete topological spaces. Further will be presented fixed theorem for nonlinear extended-contraction defined on this spaces, which generalize earlier results obtaned by Browder, Matkowski, Jachymski, Matkowski and Światkowski, Aran d - elović and Kečkić and Alshehri, Aran d - elović and Shahzad. Also, we obtain the fixed point theorem for nonlinear extended-contraction defined on quasi-metric spaces which extended recent result’s of Pasicki, obtained for pseudo-metric spaces.

2.1
Fixed point theory

Let

Let

(1)

is contraction if there exists
\alpha\in[0,1)
such that

d(f(x),f(y))\leq\alpha d(x,y),

(2.1)

for all

(2)

\varphi(r)<r

(3)

Definition 2.5
.

Let
