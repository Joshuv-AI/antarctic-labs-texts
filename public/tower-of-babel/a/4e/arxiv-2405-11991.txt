Let
\mathcal{G}

2.1
.

Recall
that a
t

\mathcal{G}
is a pair of strictly (i.e. closed under isomorphisms) full additive subcategories

(\mathcal{G}^{\leq 0},\mathcal{G}^{\geq 0})
satisfying the following conditions:

(T1)
\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{G}}(X,Y[-1])=0
for all
X\in\mathcal{G}^{\leq 0}
and
Y\in\mathcal{G}^{\geq 0}
.

(T2)
\mathcal{G}^{\leq 0}
is closed under the functor
[1]
, and

\mathcal{G}^{\geq 0}
is closed under the functor
[-1]
.

(T3) for each
X\in\mathcal{G}
, there is an exact triangle

A\rightarrow X\rightarrow B[-1]\rightarrow A[1]
with
A\in\mathcal{G}^{\leq 0}
and

B\in\mathcal{G}^{\geq 0}
.

For

\mathcal{G}^{\leq n}=\mathcal{G}^{\leq 0}[-n]
and
\mathcal{G}^{\geq n}=\mathcal{G}^{\geq 0}[-n]

2.2

Let

\mathcal{D}^{\leq 0}=\{\mathbf{X}_{\bullet}\mid\mathbf{X}_{\bullet}\cong\mathbf{Y}_{\bullet}\ \text{where}\ Y^{n}=0\ \text{for}\ n>0\}\ \text{and}

\mathcal{D}^{\geq 0}=\{\mathbf{X}_{\bullet}\mid\mathbf{X}_{\bullet}\cong\mathbf{Y}_{\bullet}\ \text{where}\ Y^{n}=0\ \text{for}\ n<0\}.
