Abstract
. The es-splitting operation for binary matroids is a natural generalization of Slater’s n n -line splitting operation on graphs. In this paper, we characterize the closure operator of the es-splitting binary matroid M X e M^{e}_{X} in terms of the closure operator of the original binary matroid M M . We also characterize the flats of the es-splitting binary matroid M X e M^{e}_{X} in terms of the flats of the original binary matroid M M .
