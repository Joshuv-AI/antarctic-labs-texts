A note on the filered decomposition theorem
Zebao Zhang

Abstract




Abstract.

We generalize the logarithmic decomposition theorem of Deligne-Illusie to a filtered version. There are two applications. The easier one provides a mod
p
proof for a vanishing theorem in characteristic zero. The deeper one gives rise to a positive characteristic analogue of a theorem of Deligne on the mixed Hodge structure attached to complex algebraic varieties.

Keywords
Decomposition theorem, mixed Fontaine-Laffaille complex, spectral sequence, vanishing theorem, weight filtration.

MSC
14F40, 14G17, 18G40.

(K^{*},W,\mathrm{Fil}):=R\Gamma(X,(\Omega^{*}_{\overline{X}/\mathbb{C}}(\log D),W,\mathrm{Fil}))

is a Hodge complex. Denote by
\{E^{i,j}_{r},d_{r}^{i,j}\}_{r\geq 0}
the spectral sequence of

. Then:

-

-
