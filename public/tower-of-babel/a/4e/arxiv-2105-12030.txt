General Cubic Interacting Vertex for Massless Integer Higher Spin Fields


Abstract
We consider a massless higher spin field theory within the BRST approach and construct a general off-shell cubic vertex corresponding to irreducible higher spin fields of helicities s 1 , s 2 , s 3 s_{1},s_{2},s_{3} . Unlike the previous works on cubic vertices, which do not take into account of the trace constraints, we use the complete BRST operator, including the trace constraints that describe an irreducible representation with definite integer helicity. As a result, we generalize the cubic vertex found in [ 14 ] and calculate the new contributions to the vertex, which contain additional terms with a smaller number space-time derivatives of the fields as well as the terms without derivatives.

5
Conclusion

To summarize, we have constructed generic cubic vertices for an
irreducible gauge-invariant Lagrangian formulation of massless
totally-symmetric higher spin fields with arbitrary helicities

s_{1}

s_{2}

s_{3}
