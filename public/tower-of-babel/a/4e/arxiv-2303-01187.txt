1.
Introduction

Let
C
be a smooth affine curve over an algebraically closed field
k
of characteristic
p>0
. The structure of the étale fundamental group of
C
is not quite well understood. The base point which will be omitted in the notation for étale the fundamental group is the generic geometric point of
C
. This profinite group
\pi_{1}^{\e}(C)
is not topologically finitely generated. Hence even though we know (
[
16
]
,
[
6
]
) all the finite quotients of
\pi_{1}^{\e}(C)
, they don’t determine the group. There have been attempts to understand
\pi_{1}^{\e}(C)
in various ways (
[
13
]
and
[
1
]
). For a survey on coverings and the étale fundamental group see
[
8
]
.

One way to address the problem is to understand which embedding problems for
\pi_{1}^{\e}(C)
have solutions (see Section
2
for definition). An embedding problem
(\beta:\Gamma\longrightarrow G,\alpha:\pi^{\e}_{1}(C)\longrightarrow G)
for
\pi_{1}^{\e}(C)
consists of surjective group homomorphisms
\alpha
and
\beta
with
\Gamma
a finite group. Let
H
be the kernel of
\beta
. It was shown by Pop (
[
12
, Theorem B]
) and Harbater (
[
7
, Corollary 4.6]
) that when
H
is a quasi-
p
group (i.e. generated by its Sylow-
p
subgroups), then the embedding problem has
\card(k)
many solutions. But when
H
is prime-to-
p
the number of solutions is finite. Although it is not clear when a solution exists and how many distinct solutions there are for the embedding problem. In
[
9
]
and
[
10
]
solutions to prime-to-
p
embedding problems restricted to certain open subgroups of
\pi_{1}^{\e}(C)
were investigated.
When
H\cong(\mathbb{Z}/m\mathbb{Z})^{r}
where
m
is coprime to
p
and
r\geq 1
, then
H
is a
G
-representation over
\mathbb{Z}/m\mathbb{Z}
. We show that the solutions to the embedding problems with kernel
H
are in bijection with those
G
-subrepresentations of
P_{m}(U)
which are isomorphic to
H
(see Theorem
3.5
and Corollary
3.6
). Here the
G
-Galois étale cover
U\longrightarrow C
is determined by
\alpha:\pi^{\e}_{1}(C)\longrightarrow G
and
P_{m}(U)
is a generalization of the
m
-torsion of the Picard group associated to
U
(see Definition
3.1
and Section
3.1
for
G
-action on
P_{m}
). This result has some consequences for “effective subgroups” for an embedding problem (Corollary
4.1
).

When the Galois group of the cover
U\longrightarrow C
associated with the above embedding problem is a cyclic
p
-group, we obtain some more concrete results on when they have solutions and how many distinct solutions exist (see Theorem
5.4
).
In
[
5
]
, for a prime number
l
other than
p
,
(\mathbb{Z}/l\mathbb{Z})^{\oplus n}\rtimes\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}
-Galois covers of
\mathbb{P}^{1}_{k}
ramified only at infinity with minimal genus were constructed. Here, for a finite abelian group
H
, we describe some conditions (Corollaries
5.3
,
5.6
) for existence of
H\rtimes\mathbb{Z}/p^{a}\mathbb{Z}
-Galois covers of smooth connected projective curves étale away from a finite set of points, dominating a given
\mathbb{Z}/p^{a}\mathbb{Z}
-Galois cover.

Recall that if the characteristic of
k
is zero, then
\pi_{1}^{\e}(C)
is the free profinite group over
2g_{C}+r_{C}-1
elements, where
g_{C}
is the genus of the smooth completion

of
C
and

is the number of closed points in

. The results described in Sections
2
and

hold even in characteristic zero case. But perhaps one could also obtain those results directly using group theory.

Lemma 5.1
.

Every nontrivial irreducible
\mathbb{F}_{l}
-representation of
\mathbb{Z}/p^{a}\mathbb{Z}
is of dimension
d_{b}
for some

.

Proof.

Let
G
be the cyclic group of order

and
\sigma
be its generator. Clearly
\sigma^{p^{a}}=1_{G}
implies that the minimal polynomial of
\sigma
divides

x^{p^{a}}-1=(x-1)\prod_{b=1}^{a}Q_{p^{b}}(x)=(x-1)\prod_{b=1}^{a}\prod_{i=1}^{\frac{p^{b-1}(p-1)}{d_{b}}}P_{bi}(x),

where

is the monic irreducible polynomial of a primitive

-th root of unity (in

) over
\mathbb{Q}
,

are irreducible factors (over
\mathbb{F}_{l}
) of

(
[
11
]
, Theorem 2.47) of degree

. Let
M
be a nontrivial irreducible
G
-representation. Then
M
is a simple
\mathbb{F}_{l}[x]
-module where multiplication by

is the nontrivial action by
\sigma
. Hence
M\cong\mathbb{F}_{l}[x]/P_{bi}(x)
for some
i\in\{1,\ldots,\frac{p^{b-1}(p-1)}{d_{b}}\},b\in\{1,\ldots,a\}
, by structure theorem for modules over PID. Since
M
is a nontrivial representation

\mathbb{F}_{l}[x]/(x-1)
is ruled out. Hence the dimension of

is

.
∎

Note
.

Every nontrivial irreducible
\mathbb{F}_{l}
-representation of
\mathbb{Z}/p^{a}\mathbb{Z}

\mathbb{F}_{l}[x]/P_{bi}(x)
for some

and

Theorem 5.2
.

Let
G
be a cyclic group of order
p^{a}
,
\sigma
be a generator of
G
,
l
be a prime number other than
p
,
H=(\mathbb{Z}/l\mathbb{Z})^{n}
,
n_{0}
(resp.

,

) be the dimension of
P_{l}(V\setminus S_{V})^{G}
(resp.
\ker(Q_{p^{b}}(\sigma))\subset P_{l}(V\setminus S_{V})

\mathbb{F}_{l}

can be expressed as
n=u+\Sigma_{b=1}^{a}v_{b}d_{b}
for non-negative integers

,
v_{b}\leq n_{b}/d_{b},\forall b\leq a
if and only if the embedding problem
(\beta:H\rtimes_{\theta}G\twoheadrightarrow G,\alpha:\pi_{1}^{\e}(X\setminus S_{X})\twoheadrightarrow G)
has a proper solution for some group homomorphism
\theta:G\longrightarrow\Aut(H)
.

Proof.

By Lemma
5.1
,
P_{l}(V\setminus S_{V})\cong(\mathbb{F}_{l})^{n_{0}}\oplus(\oplus_{b=1}^{a}\oplus_{i=1}^{p^{b-1}(p-1)/d_{b}}(\mathbb{F}_{l}[x]/P_{bi}(x))^{\gamma_{bi}})
as
\mathbb{F}_{l}[G]
-modules, where the non-negative integers
\gamma_{bi}
satisfy
\Sigma_{i=1}^{p^{b-1}(p-1)/d_{b}}\gamma_{bi}d_{b}=n_{b}
.

Since
\gcd(|H|,|G|)=1
, any extension of
G
by
H
with respect to some
G
-action

must be equivalent to
H\rtimes_{\theta}G
. Note that any basis of
H
over
\mathbb{F}_{l}
is of type T1 consisting of elements of order

.

If
n
can be expressed as above, then
H
is identified with a
G
-stable subspace
(\mathbb{F}_{l})^{u}\oplus(\oplus_{b=1}^{a}\oplus_{i=1}^{p^{b-1}(p-1)/d_{b}}(\mathbb{F}_{l}[x]/P_{bi}(x))^{\gamma^{\prime}_{bi}})
of

where
\Sigma_{i=1}^{p^{b-1}(p-1)/d_{b}}\gamma^{\prime}_{bi}=v_{b}
and the embedding problem
(\beta:H\rtimes_{\theta}G\twoheadrightarrow G,\alpha:\pi_{1}^{\e}(X\setminus S_{X})\twoheadrightarrow G)
has a proper solution by Theorem
3.5
where
\theta
depends on the choice of identification of

.

Conversely, if an embedding problem
(\beta:H\rtimes_{\theta}G\twoheadrightarrow G,\alpha:\pi_{1}^{\e}(X\setminus S_{X})\twoheadrightarrow G)
has a proper solution, then
H=\ker(\beta)
must be a
\mathbb{F}_{l}[G]
-submodule of
P_{l}(V\setminus S_{V})
(Theorem
3.5
). So
H\cong(\mathbb{F}_{l})^{u}\oplus(\oplus_{b=1}^{a}\oplus_{i=1}^{p^{b-1}(p-1)/d_{b}}(\mathbb{F}_{l}[x]/P_{bi}(x))^{\gamma^{\prime}_{bi}})
where

,
0\leq\gamma^{\prime}_{bi}\leq\gamma_{bi}
. Put
v_{b}=\Sigma_{i=1}^{p^{b-1}(p-1)/d_{b}}\gamma^{\prime}_{bi}
. Then
v_{b}\leq n_{b}/d_{b}
and
n=u+\Sigma_{b=1}^{a}\Sigma_{i=1}^{p^{b-1}(p-1)/d_{b}}\gamma^{\prime}_{bi}d_{b}=u+\Sigma_{b=1}^{a}v_{b}d_{b}
.
∎

We can replace
