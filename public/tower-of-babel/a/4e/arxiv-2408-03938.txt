\textup{Re}(s)=1
.

Lemma 2.4
.

Let
f\in\mathcal{C}_{w}(\kappa)
. Select a real number
t_{1}
with
|t_{1}|\leq(\log x)^{\kappa}
that maximizes
|F(1+1/\log x+it_{1})|
. If
|t|\leq(\log x)^{\kappa}
, then

|F(1+1/\log x+it)|\ll_{\kappa,A_{0}}(\log x)^{\kappa\sqrt{2/\pi}}\Big(\frac{\log x}{1+|t-t_{1}|\log x}+(\log\log x)^{2}\Big)^{\kappa(1-\sqrt{2/\pi})}.

Proof.

This is similar to the proof of
[
6
, Lemma 4.1]
, except in our use of (
2.1
). If
|t-t_{1}|\geq 1/\log x
, then the stated estimate is immediate from
Lemma

2.1
with
\sigma=1+1/\log x
. We now suppose that
(\log x)^{\kappa}\gg|t-t_{1}|\geq 1/\log x
. By the maximality of
t_{1}
, we have
|F(1+1/\log x+it)|\leq|F(1+1/\log x+it_{1})|
. In light of this, we set
\tau=|t_{1}-t|/2
.

From the inequality
\cos^{2}(\tau\log n)\leq|\cos(\tau\log n)|
combined with the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality and (
2.1
), we find that

\displaystyle\log|F(1+\tfrac{1}{\log x}+it)|

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{2}(\log|F(1+\tfrac{1}{\log x}+it_{1})|+\log|F(1+\tfrac{1}{\log x}+it)|)

\displaystyle=\textup{Re}\Big(\sum_{n\geq 2}\frac{a_{f}(n)\Lambda(n)}{n^{1+1/\log x}\log n}\cdot n^{i(t_{1}+t)/2}\cdot\frac{n^{i\tau}+n^{-i\tau}}{2}\Big)

\displaystyle\leq\sum_{n\geq 2}\frac{|a_{f}(n)|\Lambda(n)}{n^{1+1/\log x}\log n}|\cos(\tau\log n)|

\displaystyle\leq\Big(\sum_{n\geq 2}\frac{\cos^{2}(\tau\log n)\Lambda(n)}{n^{1+1/\log x}\log n}\Big)^{\frac{1}{2}}\Big(\sum_{n\geq 2}\frac{|a_{f}(n)|^{2}\Lambda(n)}{n^{1+1/\log x}\log n}\Big)^{\frac{1}{2}}

\displaystyle\leq\Big(\sum_{p\leq x}\frac{|\cos(\tau\log p)|}{p}+O(1)\Big)^{\frac{1}{2}}\Big(\sum_{n\geq 2}\frac{|a_{f}(n)|^{2}\Lambda(n)}{n^{1+1/\log x}\log n}\Big)^{\frac{1}{2}}.

We apply
Lemma

2.3
with
\sigma=1+1/\log x
and use partial summation to conclude that

\sum_{n\geq 2}\frac{|a_{f}(n)|^{2}\Lambda(n)}{n^{1+1/\log x}\log n}\leq\kappa^{2}\log\log x+O_{\kappa,A_{0}}(1).

By
[
8
, Lemma 2.3]
, there exists a constant
\Cr{Lem2.4_{l}arge}>0
such that if

y=\max\{\exp((\log\log x)^{2}),\exp(1/|\tau|)\},

then

(2.2)

\sum_{n\leq x}\frac{\cos^{2}(\tau\log n)\Lambda(n)}{n\log n}\leq\sum_{p\leq x}\frac{|\cos(\tau\log p)|}{p}+O(1)\leq\frac{2}{\pi}\log\frac{\log x}{\log y}+\log\log y+O(1).

Combining the above estimates yields the lemma.
∎

Lemma

2.4
leads us to the following Lipschitz estimate.

Lemma 2.5
.

Let
f\in\mathcal{C}_{w}(\kappa)
, and let
t_{1}
be as in
Lemma

2.4
. If
1\leq\omega\leq x^{1/3}
, then

\Big|\frac{1}{x}\sum_{n\leq x}\frac{f(n)}{n^{it_{1}}}-\frac{\omega}{x}\sum_{n\leq x/\omega}\frac{f(n)}{n^{it_{1}}}\Big|\\
\ll\Big(\frac{\log\omega+(\log\log x)^{2}}{\log x}\Big)^{\min\{1,\kappa(1-\sqrt{2/\pi})\}}(\log x)^{\kappa-1}\log\Big(\frac{\log x}{1+\log\omega}\Big).

Proof.

First, let
g\in\mathcal{C}_{w}(\kappa)
, and let
t_{0}\in[-(\log x)^{\kappa},(\log x)^{\kappa}]
be chosen so that the absolute value of the Dirichlet series for
g
at
s=1+1/\log x+it
is maximized when
t=t_{0}
. We will demonstrate that if
1\leq\omega\leq x^{\frac{1}{3}}
, then

(2.3)

\displaystyle\Big|\frac{1}{x^{1+it_{0}}}\sum_{n\leq x}g(n)-\frac{1}{(x/\omega)^{1+it_{0}}}\sum_{n\leq x/\omega}g(n)\Big|

\displaystyle\ll\Big(\frac{\log\omega+(\log\log x)^{2}}{\log x}\Big)^{\min\{1,\kappa(1-\sqrt{\frac{2}{\pi}})\}}(\log x)^{\kappa-1}\log\Big(\frac{\log x}{1+\log\omega}\Big).

To achieve this, we proceed just as in the proof of
[
6
, Theorem 1.5]
, except that we appeal to
Lemma

2.4
in place of
[
6
, Lemma 4.1]
.

Now, given
f\in\mathcal{C}_{w}(\kappa)
, let
t_{1}\in[-(\log x)^{\kappa},(\log x)^{\kappa}]
be chosen so that
|F(1+1/\log x+it)|
is maximized when
t=t_{1}
. We apply (
2.3
) with
g(n)=f(n)/n^{it_{1}}
. The Dirichlet series for

is then

. By construction, the value

in (

∎

2.2.
Extending Granville–Harper–Soundararajan

For functions

, the following (slightly weakened) form of Halász’s theorem is proved in
[
6
, Corollary 1.2]
: If
0<\lambda\leq\kappa
is such that
\sum_{n\geq 1}|f(n)|n^{-1-\sigma}\ll 1/\sigma^{\lambda}
for all

, then

\sum_{n\leq x}f(n)\ll_{\kappa,\lambda}(1+M)e^{-M}x(\log x)^{\lambda-1}+\frac{x}{\log x}(\log\log x)^{\kappa},

where

is defined by

(2.4)

\max_{|t|\leq(\log x)^{\kappa}}\Big|\frac{F(1+1/\log x+it)}{1+1/\log x+it}\Big|=:e^{-M}(\log x)^{\lambda}.

We now record the analogue of
[
6
, Corollary 2.8]
for
f\in\mathcal{C}_{w}(\kappa)
.

Lemma 2.6
.

If
f\in\mathcal{C}_{w}(\kappa)
and

is defined by

\max_{|t|\leq(\log x)^{\kappa}}\Big|\frac{F(1+1/\log x+it)}{1+1/\log x+it}\Big|:=e^{-M}(\log x)^{\kappa},

then

\sum_{n\leq x}f(n)\ll_{\kappa,A_{0}}(1+M)e^{-M}x(\log x)^{\kappa-1}+\frac{x}{\log x}(\log\log x)^{2}.

Sketch of proof.

The proofs in
[
6
]
carry over without noteworthy change to the situation where

; this is already noted in
[
6
]
. Instead of applying a pointwise bound for
\lambda_{f}(n)
(as one may do when
f\in\mathcal{C}(\kappa)
), one must use the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality to relate sums of
\lambda_{f}(n)
to the corresponding
L^{2}
estimates. One then applies (
2.1
). A prototype for such a calculation can be found in our proof of
Lemma

2.4
.
∎

We now justify the assertion in
Section

1
that (
1.11
) constitutes the “trivial bound.”

Lemma 2.7
.

If
f\in\mathcal{C}_{w}(\kappa)
and
x\geq 2
, then
\sum_{n\leq x}f(n)\ll_{\kappa,A_{0},m}x(\log x)^{\kappa-1}
.

Proof.

Applying
Lemma

2.1
with
\sigma=1+1/\log x
, we find that
|F(1+1/\log x+it)|\ll_{\kappa,A_{0}}(1+\log x)^{\kappa}
. Therefore, the quantity
M
in
Lemma

2.6
is at least
-o_{\kappa,A_{0}}(1)
as

. It follows that
(1+M)e^{-M}\ll_{\kappa,A_{0}}1
, and the desired result now follows from

2.6
.
∎

Let
\phi\in\mathbb{R}
. Our next lemma relates the mean values of
f(n)
to those of
f(n)/n^{i\phi}
.

Lemma 2.8
.

If
f\in\mathcal{C}_{w}(\kappa)
and
\phi\in\mathbb{R}
, then

\displaystyle\Big|\sum_{n\leq x}f(n)-\frac{x^{i\phi}}{1+i\phi}\sum_{n\leq x}\frac{f(n)}{n^{i\phi}}\Big|\ll x\frac{(\log\log x)^{\kappa}\log\log(e+|\phi|)}{\log x}\exp\Big(\sum_{n\leq x}\frac{|a_{f}(n)-n^{i\phi}|\Lambda(n)}{n\log n}\Big).

Proof.

If
h
is a nonnegative multiplicative function and
h\in\mathcal{C}_{w}(\alpha),
then by
Lemma

2.6
,

(2.5)

\displaystyle\sum_{n\leq x}h(n)

\displaystyle\ll\frac{x}{\log x}(1+M)\max_{|t|\leq(\log x)^{\alpha}}\Big|\frac{H(1+1/\log x+it)}{1+1/\log x+it}\Big|+\frac{x}{\log x}(\log\log x)^{\alpha}

\displaystyle\ll\frac{x}{\log x}(\log\log x)\sum_{n\geq 1}\frac{h(n)}{n^{1+\frac{1}{\log x}}}+\frac{x}{\log x}(\log\log x)^{\alpha}

\displaystyle\ll\frac{x}{\log x}(\log\log x)\sum_{n\leq x}\frac{h(n)}{n}+\frac{x}{\log x}(\log\log x)^{\alpha}.

Define
g(n)=\sum_{d\mid n}f(d)
. Note that
a_{f}(n)=a_{g}(n)+1
; since
|a_{g}(n)|^{2}\leq 2(|a_{f}(n)|^{2}+1)
, we have that

\sum_{x<n\leq ex}\frac{|a_{g}(n)|^{2}\Lambda(n)}{n}\leq 2\sum_{x<n\leq ex}\frac{|a_{f}(n)|^{2}\Lambda(n)}{n}+2\sum_{x<n\leq ex}\frac{\Lambda(n)}{n}\leq 2(\kappa+1)+O\Big(\frac{A_{0}}{\log(ex)}\Big).

It follows that
g\in\mathcal{C}_{w}(2(\kappa+1)).
We have
\sum_{n\leq x}n^{i\phi}=\frac{x^{1+i\phi}}{1+i\phi}+O(\min\{1+|\phi|^{2},x\})
by partial summation. Unfolding the convolution and applying (
2.5
) for
h=|g|
, we deduce

(2.6)

\displaystyle\sum_{n\leq x}f(n)n^{i\phi}

\displaystyle=\sum_{d\leq x}g(d)d^{i\phi}\sum_{n\leq x/d}n^{i\phi}

\displaystyle=\frac{x^{i\phi}}{1+i\phi}\sum_{d\leq x}\frac{g(d)}{d}+O\Big((1+\phi^{2})\sum_{d\leq x/(1+\phi^{2})}|g(d)|+x\sum_{x/(1+\phi^{2})<n\leq x}\frac{|g(d)|}{d}\Big)

\displaystyle=\frac{x^{1+i\phi}}{1+i\phi}\sum_{d\leq x}\frac{g(d)}{d}+O\Big(\frac{x\log(e+|\phi|)\log\log x}{\log x}\sum_{d\leq x}\frac{|g(d)|}{d}+\frac{x}{\log x}(\log\log x)^{\kappa+1}\Big).

We note that

\displaystyle\sum_{n\leq x}\frac{|g(d)|}{d}\ll\sum_{d\geq 2}\frac{|g(d)|}{d^{1+\frac{1}{\log(ex)}}}\ll\exp\Big(\sum_{n\geq 2}\frac{|a_{g}(n)|\Lambda(n)}{n^{1+\frac{1}{\log(ex)}}\log n}\Big)\ll\exp\Big(\sum_{n\leq x}\frac{|a_{f}(n)-1|\Lambda(n)}{n\log n}\Big).

By taking
\phi=0
in (
2.6
), we find that

\sum_{n\leq x}f(n)=x\sum_{d\leq x}\frac{g(d)}{d}+O\Big(\sum_{d\leq x}|g(d)|+\frac{x}{\log x}(\log\log x)^{\kappa+1}\Big).

Using (
2.5
) once again to bound the error term and substituting the result into (
2.6
), we conclude that

\Big|\sum_{n\leq x}f(n)n^{i\phi}-\frac{x^{i\phi}}{1+i\phi}\sum_{n\leq x}f(n)\Big|\ll x\frac{(\log\log x)^{\kappa}\log\log(e+|\phi|)}{\log x}\exp\Big(\sum_{n\leq x}\frac{|a_{f}(n)-1|\Lambda(n)}{n\log n}\Big).

The lemma follows from the above display by replacing
f(n)
with
f(n)/n^{i\phi}
.
∎

The next lemma provides us with a convenient alternative formulation of
Lemma

2.8
.

Lemma 2.9
.

Let
f\in\mathcal{C}_{w}(\kappa)
and
y_{0}\geq 1
. Define
N\geq 1
by

\displaystyle\Big|\sum_{n\leq e^{y_{0}}}f(n)\Big|=\frac{e^{y_{0}}y_{0}^{\kappa-1}}{N},

and assume that
N\leq y_{0}^{1/10}
.
There exists a real
\phi=\phi(y_{0})
such that
|\phi|\ll N
and

(2.7)

\Big|\sum_{n\leq e^{y_{0}}}\frac{f(n)}{n^{i\phi}}-\frac{1+i\phi}{e^{i\phi y_{0}}}\sum_{n\leq e^{y_{0}}}f(n)\Big|\ll e^{y_{0}}y_{0}^{\kappa-1-\frac{1}{5}}.

If
a_{f}(n)\in\mathbb{R}
for all
n\geq 1
, then there exists a constant
=\Cr{phi_{r}eal}(\kappa,A_{0},m)>0
such that

(2.8)

\displaystyle|\phi|\leq\Cr{phi_{r}eal}\frac{N^{6/\kappa}}{y_{0}}.

Proof.

A stronger result follows from
[
9
, Lemma 3.3]
when
\kappa=1
, so we may assume that
\kappa\geq 2
.
Let
S
denote the left side of (
2.7
). Select
|\phi|\ll N
so as to maximize the quantity

(2.9)

\displaystyle\max_{|t|\leq y_{0}^{\kappa}}\Big|\frac{F(1+1/y_{0}+it)}{1+1/y_{0}+it}\Big|=\Big|\frac{F(1+1/y_{0}+i\phi)}{1+1/y_{0}+i\phi}\Big|:=e^{-M}y^{\kappa}_{0}.

(The choice of
\phi
will depend on
y_{0}
.) Our hypothesis on
N
implies that,
|\phi|\ll y_{0}^{1/10}
. By
Lemma

2.8
(with
x
replaced by
e^{y_{0}}
), we have that

(2.10)

S\ll N\cdot e^{y_{0}}\frac{(\log y_{0})^{\kappa}\log\log(e+|\phi|)}{y_{0}}\exp\Big(\sum_{n\leq e^{y_{0}}}|a_{f}(n)n^{-i\phi}-1|\frac{\Lambda(n)}{n\log n}\Big).

To bound the exponential, we apply the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality to obtain

\displaystyle\sum_{n\leq e^{y_{0}}}|a_{f}(n)n^{-i\phi}-1|\frac{\Lambda(n)}{n\log n}

\displaystyle\leq\Big(\sum_{2\leq n\leq e^{y_{0}}}\frac{\Lambda(n)}{n\log n}\Big)^{\frac{1}{2}}\Big(\sum_{n\leq e^{y_{0}}}|1-a_{f}(n)n^{-i\phi}|^{2}\frac{\Lambda(n)}{n\log n}\Big)^{\frac{1}{2}}

\displaystyle=\Big(\sum_{2\leq n\leq e^{y_{0}}}\frac{\Lambda(n)}{n\log n}\Big)^{\frac{1}{2}}\Big(\sum_{2\leq n\leq e^{y_{0}}}\frac{(|a_{f}(n)|^{2}+1)\Lambda(n)}{n\log n}-2\sum_{2\leq n\leq e^{y_{0}}}\frac{\mathrm{Re}(a_{f}(n)n^{-i\phi})\Lambda(n)}{n\log n}\Big)^{1/2}.

It follows from (
2.1
), dyadic decomposition, and partial summation that

(2.11)

\sum_{2\leq n\leq e^{y_{0}}}\frac{|a_{f}(n)|^{2}\Lambda(n)}{n\log n}\leq\kappa^{2}\log y_{0}+O(1),\qquad\sum_{2\leq n\leq e^{y_{0}}}\frac{\Lambda(n)}{n\log n}\leq\log y_{0}+O(1),

so we arrive at

(2.12)

\displaystyle\sum_{n\leq e^{y_{0}}}|a_{f}(n)n^{-i\phi}-1|\frac{\Lambda(n)}{n\log n}

\displaystyle\leq(\log{y_{0}}+O(1))^{1/2}\Big((\kappa^{2}+1)\log{y_{0}}-2\sum_{n\leq e^{y_{0}}}\frac{\mathrm{Re}(a_{f}(n)n^{-i\phi})\Lambda(n)}{n\log n}\Big)\Big)^{1/2}.

We now provide a lower bound for the sums

\sum_{n\leq e^{y_{0}}}\frac{\mathrm{Re}(a_{f}(n)n^{-i\phi})\Lambda(n)}{n\log n}.

By
Lemma

2.6
, we have

\frac{e^{y_{0}}y_{0}^{\kappa-1}}{N}\ll\Big|\sum_{n\leq e^{y_{0}}}f(n)\Big|\ll(1+M)e^{-M}e^{y_{0}}y_{0}^{\kappa-1},

in which case

(2.13)

\displaystyle M\leq\log N+2\log\log N.

On the other hand, by (
2.9
) and Dirichlet series manipulations, we have the bounds

(2.14)

\displaystyle e^{-M}y_{0}^{\kappa}=\Big|\frac{F(1+1/y_{0}+i\phi)}{1+1/y_{0}+i\phi}\Big|

\displaystyle\ll\frac{\exp(\log|F(1+1/y_{0}+i\phi)|)}{1+|\phi|}

\displaystyle\ll\frac{1}{1+|\phi|}\exp\Big(\sum_{n\geq 2}\frac{\mathrm{Re}(a_{f}(n)n^{-i\phi})\Lambda(n)}{n^{1+\frac{1}{y_{0}}}\log n}\Big)

\displaystyle\asymp\frac{1}{1+|\phi|}\exp\Big(\sum_{n\leq e^{y_{0}}}\frac{\mathrm{Re}(a_{f}(n)n^{-i\phi})\Lambda(n)}{n\log n}\Big).

We combine (
2.13
), (
2.14
), and the bounds
N\ll y_{0}^{1/10}\leq y_{0}^{\kappa}
to conclude that

(2.15)

\exp\Big(\sum_{n\leq e^{y_{0}}}\frac{\mathrm{Re}(a_{f}(n)n^{-i\phi})\Lambda(n)}{n\log n}\Big)\gg\frac{y_{0}^{\kappa-1/10}}{(\log y_{0})^{2}}.

The bound (
2.7
) now follows from (
2.10
), (
2.12
), (
2.15
), and the bound
N\ll y_{0}^{1/10}
.

Suppose that
a_{f}(n)
is real-valued. The Cauchy–Schwarz inequality, (
2.2
), (
2.11
), (
2.13
), and (
2.14
) yield

\displaystyle\kappa\log{y_{0}}-\log{N}-2\log\log N

\displaystyle\leq\sum_{n\leq e^{y_{0}}}\frac{a_{f}(n)\cos(\phi\log n)\Lambda(n)}{n\log n}

\displaystyle\leq\Big(\sum_{n\leq e^{y_{0}}}\frac{a_{f}(n)^{2}\Lambda(n)}{n\log n}\Big)^{\frac{1}{2}}\Big(\sum_{n\leq e^{y_{0}}}\frac{\cos^{2}(\phi\log n)\Lambda(n)}{n\log n}\Big)^{\frac{1}{2}}

\displaystyle\leq(\kappa^{2}\log y_{0}+O(1))^{\frac{1}{2}}\Big(\frac{2}{\pi}\log y_{0}+\Big(1-\frac{2}{\pi}\Big)\log\max\{\Cr{Lem2.4_{l}arge}(\log y_{0})^{2},|\phi|^{-1}\}+O(1)\Big)^{\frac{1}{2}}.

In particular, the bounds

\max\{\Cr{Lem2.4_{l}arge}(\log y_{0})^{2},|\phi|^{-1}\}\gg y_{0}(N(\log N)^{2})^{-\frac{2\pi}{(\pi-2)\kappa}}\gg y_{0}N^{-6/\kappa}

hold. Since
N\leq y_{0}^{1/10}
, we would obtain a contradiction if
\Cr{Lem2.4_{l}arge}(\log y_{0})^{2}
were the maximum. This yields (
2.8
), as desired.
∎

3.1.
Convexity bounds for

We require bounds for

just to the right of the line
\textup{Re}(s)=1
. By
[

, we have that

(3.1)

|L(\tfrac{1}{2},\pi)|\ll|L(\tfrac{3}{2},\pi)|^{2}\mathfrak{C}^{\frac{1}{4}}.

The bound
L(\frac{3}{2}+it,\pi)\ll 1
follows from
Lemma

2.1
applied to

. If we change
\frac{1}{2}
to
\frac{1}{2}+it
on the left side of (

), the effect on the right side is that the numbers
\mu_{\pi}(j)
shift by

\mathfrak{C}
is replaced by
\mathfrak{C}(it)

(3.2)

|L(\tfrac{1}{2}+it,\pi)|\ll\mathfrak{C}^{\frac{1}{4}}(1+|t|)^{\frac{m}{4}}.

We use

again to achieve the bound

(3.3)

|L(\sigma+it,\pi)|\ll\mathfrak{C}^{\frac{1-\sigma}{2}}(\log\mathfrak{C})^{\kappa(2\sigma-1)}(1+|t|)^{\frac{m(1-\sigma)}{2}},\qquad 1/2\leq\sigma\leq 1.

3.2.
Partial sums and
L
-functions

We now prove a standard estimate on the partial sums of
\lambda_{\pi}(n)
when
L(s,\pi)\in\mathfrak{S}(m)
. We will make no attempt to optimize the exponents.

Lemma 3.1
.

If
L(s,\pi)\in\mathfrak{S}(m)
(in particular,
L(s,\pi)
has no pole), then

\sum_{n\leq x}\lambda_{\pi}(n)\ll x^{1-\frac{1}{m+14}}((\log x)^{\frac{\kappa^{2}}{2}}+\mathfrak{C}^{\frac{1}{4}}),\qquad x\geq 3.

Proof.

Let
c>1
,
y>0
, and
\lambda>0
. Let
K\geq 0
be an integer. A straightforward Perron integral calculation (see
[
13
, Equation 4.1]
) reveals that

\sum_{n\leq x}\lambda_{\pi}(n)=\frac{1}{2\pi i}\int_{c-i\infty}^{c+i\infty}L(s,\pi)\frac{x^{s}}{s}\Big(\frac{e^{\lambda s}-1}{\lambda s}\Big)^{K}ds+O\Big(\sum_{x<n\leq e^{K\lambda}x}|\lambda_{\pi}(n)|\Big).

We choose
K=\lfloor m/4\rfloor+3
and
\lambda=x^{-2/(m+14)}
. It suffices to take
x
large enough so that
\lambda\leq 1/K
, in which case
e^{K\lambda}-1\leq 2K\lambda
. Thus, using these bounds and the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality, we deduce from
Lemma

2.2
that

\sum_{x<n\leq e^{K\lambda}x}|\lambda_{\pi}(n)|\ll(e^{K\lambda}-1)^{\frac{1}{2}}x(\log x)^{\frac{\kappa^{2}}{2}}\ll\lambda^{\frac{1}{2}}x(\log x)^{\frac{\kappa^{2}}{2}}.

It follows from (
3.3
) that if we push the contour to the line
\textup{Re}(s)=1/2
, then

\frac{1}{2\pi i}\int_{c-i\infty}^{c+i\infty}L(s,\pi)\frac{x^{s}}{s}\Big(\frac{e^{\lambda s}-1}{\lambda s}\Big)^{K}ds\ll\mathfrak{C}^{\frac{1}{4}}x^{\frac{1}{2}}\lambda^{-K}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}(1+|t|)^{m/4-K}dt\ll\mathfrak{C}^{\frac{1}{4}}x^{\frac{1}{2}}\lambda^{-K}.

We obtain the lemma by combining our two estimates.
∎

The next lemma relates the mean values of
\lambda_{\pi}(n)/n^{i\phi}
to a suitable average of
L(s,\pi)
.

Lemma 3.2
.

Let
L(s,\pi)\in\mathfrak{S}(m)
. Let
\phi\in\mathbb{R}
and
T\geq 0
. If
0\leq\lambda<\frac{1}{m+15}
, then

\sqrt{2\pi T}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\Big(\frac{1}{e^{y}}\sum_{n\leq e^{y}}\frac{\lambda_{\pi}(n)}{n^{i\phi}}\Big)\exp\Big(\lambda y-\frac{T}{2}y^{2}\Big)dy=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{L(1-\lambda+i\phi+it,\pi)}{1-\lambda+it}\exp\Big(-\frac{t^{2}}{2T}\Big)dt.

Proof.

By
Lemma

3.1
and partial summation, we have that if
\textup{Re}(s)\geq 1-\frac{1}{m+15}
, then

\lim_{x\to\infty}\sum_{n\geq x}\frac{\lambda_{\pi}(n)}{n^{s}}=s\lim_{x\to\infty}\int_{x}^{\infty}\frac{-1}{t^{1+s}}\Big(\sum_{n\leq t}\lambda_{\pi}(n)\Big)dt\ll_{\pi}|s|\lim_{x\to\infty}\int_{x}^{\infty}\frac{t^{1-\frac{1}{m+14}}(\log t)^{\kappa^{2}/2}}{t^{1+\textup{Re}(s)}}dt=0.

Therefore, for
0\leq\lambda<\frac{1}{m+15}
, we can compute the Fourier transform

) is satisfied. Therefore, if
F_{1}
and

\displaystyle F_{1}(y)

\displaystyle:=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{a_{\pi}(n)\Lambda(n)}{n^{1+y+i(\phi+\xi)}\log n}v(e^{\frac{\log{n}}{\log X}}),

\displaystyle:=-\sum_{|1+i(\phi+\xi)-\rho|\leq K/\log X}U((1+y+i(\phi+\xi)-\rho)\log X),

then

(5.6)

\displaystyle\log\Big|\frac{L(1-\lambda+i(\phi+\xi),\pi)}{L(1+\lambda+i(\phi+\xi),\pi)}\Big|=\mathrm{Re}(F_{1}(-\lambda)-F_{1}(\lambda)+F_{2}(-\lambda)-F_{2}(\lambda))+O\Big(\frac{\lambda y_{0}e^{4k}}{B}\Big).\hskip-10.81204pt

By the mean value theorem, there exists
y_{1}\in[-\lambda,\lambda]
such that

By our choices of
u
and

(see (

)), we have that

\displaystyle F_{1}^{\prime}(y_{1})=-\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{a_{\pi}(n)\Lambda(n)}{n^{1+y_{1}+i(\xi+\phi)}}v(e^{\frac{\log n}{\log X}})\ll\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{|a_{\pi}(n)|\Lambda(n)}{n^{1-\lambda}}v(e^{\frac{\log{n}}{\log X}})\ll\sum_{n\leq X}\frac{|a_{\pi}(n)|\Lambda(n)}{n^{1-\lambda}}.

Our choice of
X
in (
5.2
) and the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality ensure that

\displaystyle F_{1}^{\prime}(y_{1})\ll\sum_{n\leq X}\frac{|a_{\pi}(n)|\Lambda(n)}{n}\ll\Big(\sum_{n\leq X}\frac{|a_{\pi}(n)|^{2}\Lambda(n)}{n}\Big)^{\frac{1}{2}}\Big(\sum_{n\leq X}\frac{\Lambda(n)}{n}\Big)^{\frac{1}{2}}.

It then follows from (
1.10
), dyadic decomposition, and Mertens’s theorem that

F_{1}(\lambda)-F_{1}(-\lambda)=2\lambda F_{1}^{\prime}(y_{1})\ll\lambda\log X\ll 1.

Thus, (
5.6
) reduces to

\displaystyle\log\Big|\frac{L(1-\lambda+i(\phi+\xi),\pi)}{L(1+\lambda+i(\phi+\xi),\pi)}\Big|

\displaystyle=\mathrm{Re}(F_{2}(-\lambda)-F_{2}(\lambda))+O\Big(\frac{\lambda y_{0}e^{4k}}{B}+1\Big).\hskip-5.69054pt

It follows from (
5.1
) that there exists a constant
=\Cr{B_{s}cale}(A_{0},\kappa,m,\delta)\geq 1
such that

\frac{\lambda y_{0}}{8}\leq\mathrm{Re}(F_{2}(-y)-F_{2}(y))+\Cr{B_{s}cale}\frac{\lambda y_{0}e^{4k}}{B}.\hskip-5.69054pt

Consequently, there exists a constant
=\Cr{B_{c}onst}(A_{0},\kappa,m,\delta)\geq 1
such that if

(5.7)

\displaystyle k=\delta^{-1}+2,\quad B=\Cr{B_{c}onst}e^{4k},

then

(5.8)

\displaystyle\frac{\lambda y_{0}}{16}

\displaystyle\leq\mathrm{Re}(F_{2}(-\lambda)-F_{2}(\lambda))\hskip-5.69054pt

Next, we estimate the right-hand side of (
5.8
). If
|y|\leq\lambda
, then by (
4.3
) and the fundamental theorem of calculus, we find that

\displaystyle\frac{\partial}{\partial y}F_{2}(y)

\displaystyle=-\sum_{|1+i(\phi+\xi)-\rho|\leq K/\log X}\frac{\partial}{\partial y}U((1+y+i(\phi+\xi)-\rho)\log X)

\displaystyle=\sum_{|1+i(\phi+\xi)-\rho|\leq K/\log X}\frac{\widehat{u}(1-(1+y+i(\phi+\xi)-\rho)\log X)}{1+y+i(\phi+\xi)-\rho}.

It follows that

(5.9)

\displaystyle F_{2}(-\lambda)-F_{2}(\lambda)=\sum_{|1+i(\phi+\xi)-\rho|\leq K/\log X}-\int_{-\lambda}^{\lambda}\frac{\widehat{u}(1-(1+y+i(\phi+\xi)-\rho)\log X)}{1+y+i(\phi+\xi)-\rho}dy.\hskip-3.98337pt

Combining (

\displaystyle\frac{\lambda y_{0}}{16}\leq\int_{-\lambda}^{\lambda}\sum_{|1+i(\phi+\xi)-\rho|\leq K/\log X}-\mathrm{Re}{\Big(\frac{\widehat{u}(1-(1+y+i(\phi+\xi)-\rho)\log X)}{1+y+i(\phi+\xi)-\rho}\Big)}dy.

Defining

\displaystyle S_{1}(y):=\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}|1+i(\phi+\xi)-\rho|\leq K/\log X\\
|1+y+i(\phi+\xi)-\rho|<1/\log X\end{subarray}}-\mathrm{Re}{\Big(\frac{\widehat{u}(1-(1+y+i(\phi+\xi)-\rho)\log X)}{1+y+i(\phi+\xi)-\rho}\Big)}dy

and

S_{2}(y):=\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}|1+i(\phi+\xi)-\rho|\leq K/\log X\\
|1+y+i(\phi+\xi)-\rho|\geq 1/\log X\end{subarray}}-\mathrm{Re}{\Big(\frac{\widehat{u}(1-(1+y+i(\phi+\xi)-\rho)\log X)}{1+y+i(\phi+\xi)-\rho}\Big)}dy,

we restate (
5.10
) as

(5.11)

\displaystyle\frac{\lambda y_{0}}{16}\leq\int_{-\lambda}^{\lambda}S_{1}(y)dy+\int_{-\lambda}^{\lambda}S_{2}(y)dy.

First, consider

. If
|1+y+i(\phi+\xi)-\rho|\leq 1/\log X
, then it follows from the expansion
\widehat{u}(1-(1+y+i(\phi+\xi)-\rho)\log X)=1+O(|1+y+i(\phi+\xi)-\rho|\log X)

\displaystyle S_{1}(y)=-\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}|1+i(\phi+\xi)-\rho|\leq K/\log X\\
|1+y+i(\phi+\xi)-\rho|<1/\log X\end{subarray}}\frac{1+y-\beta}{|1+y+i(\phi+\xi)-\rho|^{2}}+O\Big(\log X\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}|1+i(\phi+\xi)-\rho|\leq K/\log X\\

\displaystyle\int_{-\lambda}^{\lambda}S_{1}(y)dy

\displaystyle+O\Big(\lambda(\log X)\sup_{-\lambda\leq y\leq\lambda}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}|1+i(\phi+\xi)-\rho|\leq K/\log X\\
|1+y+i(\phi+\xi)-\rho|<1/\log X\end{subarray}}1\Big)

\displaystyle=\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}\rho\\

\displaystyle+O\Big(\#\Big\{\rho\colon|1+i(\phi+\xi)-\rho|\leq\frac{K}{\log X}\Big\}\Big),
