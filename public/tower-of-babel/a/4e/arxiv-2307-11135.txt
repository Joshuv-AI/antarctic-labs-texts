Introduction

Let
\displaystyle\mathfrak{H}
be complex Hilbert space and
\displaystyle\mathfrak{B}(\mathfrak{H})
be the
\displaystyle C^{*}
-algebra of all bounded linear operator on
\displaystyle\mathfrak{H}
.
An operator
\displaystyle\mathfrak{T}\in\mathfrak{B}(\mathfrak{H})
is said to be
positive
if
\displaystyle\left<\mathfrak{T}\xi,\xi\right>\geq 0
holds for all
\displaystyle\xi\in\mathfrak{H}
. We write
\displaystyle\mathfrak{T}\geq 0
if
\displaystyle\mathfrak{T}
is positive.

The numerical radius of
\displaystyle\mathfrak{T}\in\mathfrak{B}(\mathfrak{H})
is defined by

w(\mathfrak{T})=\sup\{|\lambda|:\lambda\in W(\mathfrak{T})\}=\sup\{|\left<\mathfrak{T}\xi,\xi\right>|:\xi\in\mathfrak{H},\left\|x\right\|=1\}.

It is well known that
\displaystyle w(\cdot)
defines a norm on
\displaystyle\mathfrak{B}(\mathfrak{H})
, which is equivalent to the usual operator norm
\displaystyle\left\|\cdot\right\|
.
In fact, for any
\displaystyle\mathfrak{T}\in\mathfrak{B}(\mathfrak{H})
,

\frac{1}{2}\left\|\mathfrak{T}\right\|\leq w(\mathfrak{T})\leq\left\|\mathfrak{T}\right\|.

(1)

Also, if
\displaystyle\mathfrak{T}\in\mathfrak{B}(\mathfrak{H})
is normal, then
\displaystyle w(\mathfrak{T})=\left\|\mathfrak{T}\right\|
.

An important inequality for
\displaystyle w(\mathfrak{T})
is the power
inequality stating that
\displaystyle w(\mathfrak{T}^{n})\leq(w(\mathfrak{T}))^{n}
for every natural numbers
\displaystyle n
.

Several numerical radius inequalities improving the inequalities in (
3.9
) have been
recently given in
Dragomir 2006a
;
Dragomir 2008
;
Kittaneh 1988
;
Rashid 2019
;
Rashid and Altaweel 2021
;
Rashid and Altaweel 2022
;
Rashid 2023
. For instance,
Kittaneh 2003
;
Kittaneh 2005
proved that for any
\displaystyle\mathfrak{A}\in\mathfrak{B}(\mathfrak{H})
,

w(\mathfrak{A})\leq\frac{1}{2}\left\||\mathfrak{A}|^{2}+|\mathfrak{A}^{*}|\right\|\leq\frac{1}{2}\left(\left\|\mathfrak{A}\right\|+\left\|\mathfrak{A}^{2}\right\|^{1/2}\right).

(2)

where
\displaystyle|\mathfrak{A}|=\sqrt{\mathfrak{A}^{*}\mathfrak{A}}
is the absolute value of
\displaystyle\mathfrak{A}
, and

\frac{1}{4}\left\|\mathfrak{A}^{*}\mathfrak{A}+\mathfrak{A}\mathfrak{A}^{*}\right\|\leq w^{2}(\mathfrak{A})\leq\frac{1}{2}\left\|\mathfrak{A}^{*}\mathfrak{A}+\mathfrak{A}\mathfrak{A}^{*}\right\|.

(3)

Also, in the same paper, it was shown that

\left\|\mathfrak{A}+\mathfrak{B}\right\|^{2}\leq\left\||\mathfrak{A}|^{2}+|\mathfrak{B}|^{2}\right\|+\left\||\mathfrak{A}^{*}|^{2}+|\mathfrak{B}^{*}|^{2}\right\|.

(4)

Mohammad El-Haddad 2007
established the generalizations of inequality (
2
) and the second inequality (
3
)
as follows:

w^{r}(\mathfrak{A})\leq\frac{1}{2}\left\||\mathfrak{A}|^{2r\lambda}+|\mathfrak{A}^{*}|^{2r(1-\lambda)}\right\|

(5)

and

w^{2r}(\mathfrak{A})\leq\left\|\lambda|\mathfrak{A}|^{2r}+(1-\lambda)|\mathfrak{A}^{*}|^{2r}\right\|,

(6)

where
\displaystyle 0<\lambda<1
and
\displaystyle r\geq 1
.

A general numerical radius inequality has been established by
Kittaneh 2005
, it has been proved that
if
\displaystyle\mathfrak{A},\mathfrak{B},\mathfrak{C},\mathfrak{D},\mathfrak{T},\mathfrak{S}\in\mathfrak{B}(\mathfrak{H})
, then

w(\mathfrak{A}\mathfrak{T}\mathfrak{B}+\mathfrak{C}\mathfrak{S}\mathfrak{D})\leq\frac{1}{2}\left\|\mathfrak{A}|\mathfrak{T}^{*}|^{2(1-\alpha)}\mathfrak{A}^{*}+\mathfrak{B}^{*}|\mathfrak{T}|^{2\alpha}\mathfrak{B}+\mathfrak{C}|\mathfrak{S}^{*}|^{2(1-\alpha)}\mathfrak{C}^{*}+\mathfrak{D}^{*}|\mathfrak{S}|^{2\alpha}\mathfrak{D}\right\|

(7)

for all
\displaystyle\alpha\in(0,1)
.

Although several open problems relating to numerical radius inequalities for bounded linear operators remain unsolved, work on establishing numerical radius inequalities for a number of bounded linear operators has begun (see, for example,
Gustafson and Rao 1997
and
Rashid 2019
;
Rashid and Altaweel 2021
;
Rashid and Altaweel 2022
;
Rashid 2023
).
If
\displaystyle\mathfrak{A},\mathfrak{B}\in\mathfrak{B}(\mathfrak{H})
, then

w(\mathfrak{A}\mathfrak{B})\leq 4w(\mathfrak{A})w(\mathfrak{B}).

In the case that
\displaystyle NM=MN
, we have

w(\mathfrak{A}\mathfrak{B})\leq 2w(\mathfrak{A})w(\mathfrak{B}).

Moreover, if
\displaystyle N
and
\displaystyle M
are normal, then

w(\mathfrak{A}\mathfrak{B})\leq w(\mathfrak{A})w(\mathfrak{B}).

Recently,
Dragomir 2009
proved that if
\displaystyle N,M\in\mathfrak{B}(\mathfrak{H})
and
\displaystyle r\geq 1
, then

w^{r}(\mathfrak{B}^{*}\mathfrak{A})\leq\frac{1}{2}\left\||\mathfrak{A}|^{2r}+|\mathfrak{B}^{*}|^{2r}\right\|.

(8)

Shebrawi and Albadawi 2009
discovered a fascinating numerical radius inequality, it has
been shown that if
\displaystyle\mathfrak{A},X,\mathfrak{B}\in\mathfrak{B}(\mathfrak{H})
, then

w^{r}(\mathfrak{A}^{*}X\mathfrak{B})\leq\frac{1}{2}\left\|(\mathfrak{A}^{*}|X^{*}|^{2\nu}\mathfrak{A})^{r}+(\mathfrak{B}^{*}|X|^{2(1-\nu)}\mathfrak{B})^{r}\right\|,\,\,r\geq 1,0<\nu<1.

(9)

Very recently,
Aldolat and Al-Zoubi 2016
, showed that if

\displaystyle\mathfrak{A}_{i},\mathfrak{B}_{i},\mathfrak{X}_{i}\in\mathfrak{B}(\mathfrak{H})
(
\displaystyle i=1,2,\cdots,n
),
\displaystyle m\in\mathbb{N}
and
\displaystyle\phi
and
\displaystyle\psi

are non-negative functions
on
\displaystyle[0,\infty)
which are continuous such that
\displaystyle\phi(t)\psi(t)=t
for all
\displaystyle t\in[0,\infty)
, then

w^{r}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n}\mathfrak{X}_{i}\mathfrak{A}_{i}^{m}\mathfrak{B}_{i}\right)\leq\frac{n^{2r-1}}{2m}\sum_{j=1}^{m}\left\|\sum_{i=1}^{n}(E_{i,j})^{r}+(W_{i,j})^{r}\right\|

(10)

for
\displaystyle r\geq 1
, where
\displaystyle E_{i,j}=\mathfrak{X}_{i}\phi^{2}\left(\left|\mathfrak{A}_{i}^{j*}\right|\right)\mathfrak{X}_{i}^{*}
and
\displaystyle W_{i,j}=(\mathfrak{A}_{i}^{m-j}\mathfrak{B}_{i})^{*}\psi^{2}\left(\left|\mathfrak{A}_{i}^{j}\right|\right)\mathfrak{A}_{i}^{m-j}\mathfrak{B}_{i}
.

The goal of this study is to develop significant extensions of these inequalities based on the classic convexity inequalities for nonnegative real numbers and some operator inequalities. For the sum of two operators, usual operator norm inequalities and a related numerical radius inequality are also provided. In specifically, if
\displaystyle i=1,2,\cdots n\in\mathbb{N}
,
\displaystyle\mathfrak{A}_{i},\mathfrak{B}_{i}
, and
\displaystyle\mathfrak{X}_{i}
are bounded linear operators, we estimate the numerical radius to
\displaystyle\sum_{j=1}^{m}\mathfrak{X}_{i}\mathfrak{A}_{i}^{m}\mathfrak{B}_{i}
for some
\displaystyle m\in\mathbb{N}
.

\displaystyle\mathfrak{A}_{i},\mathfrak{B}_{i}
and
\displaystyle\mathfrak{X}_{i}
are bounded
linear operators (
\displaystyle i=1,2,\cdots n\in\mathbb{N}
) , then we estimate the numerical radius
to
\displaystyle\sum_{j=1}^{m}\mathfrak{X}_{i}\mathfrak{A}_{i}^{m}\mathfrak{B}_{i}
for some
\displaystyle m\in\mathbb{N}
.

The following lemma is a straightforward application of Jensen’s inequality about the convexity or concavity of certain power functions.
Schlömilch’s inequality for the weighted means of non-negative real numbers is a specific example of this inequality.

Lemma 3.1
.

Let
\displaystyle\sigma,\tau>0
and
\displaystyle 0\leq\alpha\leq 1
. Then

\sigma^{\alpha}\tau^{1-\alpha}\leq\alpha\sigma+(1-\alpha)\tau\leq\left(\alpha\sigma^{r}+(1-\alpha)\tau^{r}\right)^{\frac{1}{r}}\quad\mbox{for}\quad r\geq 1.

(34)

The following result was established by
Kittaneh and Manasrah 2010
, which is a refinement of the scalar Young inequality.

Lemma 3.2
.

Let
\displaystyle\sigma,\tau>0
, and
\displaystyle p,q>1
such that
\displaystyle\frac{1}{p}+\frac{1}{q}=1
. Then

\sigma\tau+r_{0}(\sigma^{\frac{p}{2}}-\tau^{\frac{q}{2}})^{2}\leq\frac{\sigma^{p}}{p}+\frac{\tau^{q}}{q},

(35)

where
\displaystyle r_{0}=\min\{\frac{1}{p},\frac{1}{q}\}
.

Manasrah and Kittaneh have generalized (
35
) in
Manasrah and Kittaneh 2015
, as follows:

Lemma 3.3
.

Let
\displaystyle\sigma,\tau>0
, and
\displaystyle p,q>1
such that
\displaystyle\frac{1}{p}+\frac{1}{q}=1
. Then for
\displaystyle m=1,2,\cdots,
we have

(\sigma^{\frac{1}{p}}\tau^{\frac{1}{q}})^{m}+r_{0}^{m}(\sigma^{\frac{m}{2}}-\tau^{\frac{m}{2}})^{2}\leq\left(\frac{\sigma^{r}}{p}+\frac{\tau^{r}}{q}\right)^{\frac{m}{r}},\quad r\geq 1

(36)

where
\displaystyle r_{0}=\min\{\frac{1}{p},\frac{1}{q}\}
. In particular, if
\displaystyle p=q=2
, then

(\sqrt{\sigma\tau})^{m}+\frac{1}{2^{m}}(\sigma^{\frac{m}{2}}-\tau^{\frac{m}{2}})^{2}\leq 2^{-\frac{m}{r}}(\sigma^{r}+\tau^{r})^{\frac{m}{r}}.

(37)

For
\displaystyle m=1
, and
\displaystyle p=q=2
, we have

\sqrt{\sigma\tau}+\frac{1}{2}(\sqrt{\sigma}-\sqrt{\tau})^{2}\leq 2^{-\frac{1}{r}}(\sigma^{r}+\tau^{r})^{\frac{1}{r}}.

(38)

The convexity of the function
\displaystyle\psi(t)=t^{r}
,
\displaystyle r\geq 1
leads to the following lemma, which deals with positive real numbers.

Lemma 3.4
.

Let
\displaystyle\sigma_{i},i=1,\cdots,n
be positive real numbers. Then

\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n}\sigma_{i}\right)^{r}\leq n^{r-1}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\sigma_{i}^{r}\quad\mbox{for}\,\,r\geq 1.

(39)

Theorem 3.5
.

Let
\displaystyle\mathfrak{A}_{i},\mathfrak{C}_{i},\\
D_{i}\in\mathfrak{B}(\mathfrak{H})
,
\displaystyle(i=1,\cdots,n)
,
\displaystyle m\in\mathbb{N}
. Then

w^{r}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n}\mathfrak{D}_{i}\mathfrak{C}_{i}^{m}\mathfrak{A}_{i}\right)\leq\frac{n^{r-1}}{2m}\sum_{j=1}^{m}\left\|\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left(|\mathfrak{C}_{i}^{j}\mathfrak{A}_{i}|^{2r}+|(\mathfrak{D}_{i}\mathfrak{C}_{i}^{m-j})^{*}|^{2r}\right)\right\|

(40)

for all
\displaystyle r\geq 1
.

Proof.

Let
\displaystyle\xi\in\mathfrak{H}
be any unit vector. Then by Lemma
2.5
, Lemma
3.1
and Lemma

3.4
, we obtain that

\displaystyle\displaystyle\left|\left<\sum_{i=1}^{n}\mathfrak{D}_{i}\mathfrak{C}_{i}^{m}\mathfrak{A}_{i}\xi,\xi\right>\right|^{r}

\displaystyle\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\displaystyle\frac{1}{m}\sum_{j=1}^{m}\left|\left<\sum_{i=1}^{n}\mathfrak{D}_{i}\mathfrak{C}_{i}^{m-j}\mathfrak{C}_{i}^{j}\mathfrak{A}_{i}\xi,\xi\right>\right|^{r}

\displaystyle\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\displaystyle\frac{1}{m}\sum_{j=1}^{m}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left|\left<\mathfrak{D}_{i}\mathfrak{C}_{i}^{m-j}\mathfrak{C}_{i}^{j}\mathfrak{A}_{i}\xi,\xi\right>\right|\right)^{r}

This implies that

\displaystyle\displaystyle\left|\left<\sum_{i=1}^{n}\mathfrak{D}_{i}\mathfrak{C}_{i}^{m}\mathfrak{A}_{i}\xi,\xi\right>\right|^{r}

\displaystyle\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\displaystyle\frac{n^{r-1}}{m}\sum_{j=1}^{m}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left|\left<\mathfrak{D}_{i}\mathfrak{C}_{i}^{m-j}\mathfrak{C}_{i}^{j}\mathfrak{A}_{i}\xi,\xi\right>\right|^{r}

\displaystyle\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\displaystyle\frac{n^{r-1}}{m}\sum_{j=1}^{m}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left<|\mathfrak{C}_{i}^{j}\mathfrak{A}_{i}|^{2}\xi,\xi\right>^{\frac{r}{2}}\left<|(\mathfrak{D}_{i}\mathfrak{C}_{i}^{m-j})^{*}|^{2}\xi,\xi\right>^{\frac{r}{2}}

\displaystyle\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\displaystyle\frac{n^{r-1}}{m}\sum_{j=1}^{m}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left<|\mathfrak{C}_{i}^{j}\mathfrak{A}_{i}|^{2r}\xi,\xi\right>^{\frac{1}{2}}\left<|(\mathfrak{D}_{i}\mathfrak{C}_{i}^{m-j})^{*}|^{2r}\xi,\xi\right>^{\frac{1}{2}}

\displaystyle\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\displaystyle\frac{n^{r-1}}{2m}\sum_{j=1}^{m}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left<\left(|\mathfrak{C}_{i}^{j}\mathfrak{A}_{i}|^{2r}+|(\mathfrak{D}_{i}\mathfrak{C}_{i}^{m-j})^{*}|^{2r}\right)\xi,\xi\right>

Taking the supremum over all unit vectors
\displaystyle\xi\in\mathfrak{H}
, we get the result.
∎

Theorem 3.8
.

Let
\displaystyle\mathfrak{A}_{i},\mathfrak{C}_{i},\mathfrak{D}_{i}\in\mathfrak{B}(\mathfrak{H})
,
\displaystyle(i=1,\cdots,n)
,
\displaystyle m\in\mathbb{N}
and
\displaystyle 0\leq\alpha\leq 1
. Then

w\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n}\mathfrak{D}_{i}\mathfrak{C}_{i}^{m}\mathfrak{A}_{i}\right)\leq\frac{1}{2m}\sum_{j=1}^{m}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left\|\alpha|\mathfrak{C}_{i}^{j}\mathfrak{A}_{i}|^{\frac{2r}{\alpha}}+(1-\alpha)|(\mathfrak{D}_{i}\mathfrak{C}_{i}^{m-j})^{*}|^{\frac{2r}{1-\alpha}}\right\|^{\frac{1}{2r}}

(42)

for all
\displaystyle r\geq 1
.

Proof.

Let
\displaystyle\xi\in\mathfrak{H}
be any unit vector. Then by Lemma
2.5
, Lemma
3.1
and Lemma

3.4
, we obtain

\displaystyle\displaystyle\left|\left<\sum_{i=1}^{n}\mathfrak{D}_{i}\mathfrak{C}_{i}^{m}\mathfrak{A}_{i}\xi,\xi\right>\right|

\displaystyle\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\displaystyle\frac{1}{m}\sum_{j=1}^{m}\left|\left<\sum_{i=1}^{n}\mathfrak{D}_{i}\mathfrak{C}_{i}^{m-j}\mathfrak{C}_{i}^{j}\mathfrak{A}_{i}\xi,\xi\right>\right|

\displaystyle\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\displaystyle\frac{1}{m}\sum_{j=1}^{m}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left<|\mathfrak{C}_{i}^{j}\mathfrak{A}_{i}|^{2}\xi,\xi\right>^{\frac{1}{2}}\left<|(\mathfrak{D}_{i}\mathfrak{C}_{i}^{m-j})^{*}|^{2}\xi,\xi\right>^{\frac{1}{2}}

Hence

\displaystyle\displaystyle\left|\left<\sum_{i=1}^{n}\mathfrak{D}_{i}\mathfrak{C}_{i}^{m}\mathfrak{A}_{i}\xi,\xi\right>\right|

\displaystyle\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\displaystyle\frac{1}{m}\sum_{j=1}^{m}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left(\left<|\mathfrak{C}_{i}^{j}\mathfrak{A}_{i}|^{2}\xi,\xi\right>\left<|(\mathfrak{D}_{i}\mathfrak{C}_{i}^{m-j})^{*}|^{2}\xi,\xi\right>\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}

\displaystyle\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\displaystyle\frac{1}{m}\sum_{j=1}^{m}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left(\left<|\mathfrak{C}_{i}^{j}\mathfrak{A}_{i}|^{\frac{2}{\alpha}}\xi,\xi\right>^{\alpha}\left<|(\mathfrak{D}_{i}\mathfrak{C}_{i}^{m-j})^{*}|^{\frac{2}{1-\alpha}}\xi,\xi\right>^{1-\alpha}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}

\displaystyle\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\displaystyle\frac{1}{m}\sum_{j=1}^{m}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left(\alpha\left<|\mathfrak{C}_{i}^{j}\mathfrak{A}_{i}|^{\frac{2}{\alpha}}\xi,\xi\right>^{r}+(1-\alpha)\left<|(\mathfrak{D}_{i}\mathfrak{C}_{i}^{m-j})^{*}|^{\frac{2}{1-\alpha}}\xi,\xi\right>^{r}\right)^{\frac{1}{2r}}

\displaystyle\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\displaystyle\frac{1}{m}\sum_{j=1}^{m}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left<\left(\alpha|\mathfrak{C}_{i}^{j}\mathfrak{A}_{i}|^{\frac{2r}{\alpha}}+(1-\alpha)|(\mathfrak{D}_{i}\mathfrak{C}_{i}^{m-j})^{*}|^{\frac{2r}{1-\alpha}}\right)\xi,\xi\right>^{\frac{1}{2r}}.

Taking the supremum over all unit vectors
\displaystyle\xi\in\mathfrak{H}
, we deduce the desired result.
∎

Theorem 3.9
.

Let
\displaystyle\mathfrak{A}_{i},\mathfrak{C}_{i},\mathfrak{D}_{i}\in\mathfrak{B}(\mathfrak{H})
,
\displaystyle(i=1,\cdots,n)
,
\displaystyle m\in\mathbb{N}

\displaystyle r_{0}=\min\{\frac{1}{p},\frac{1}{q}\}

Proof.

Let
\displaystyle\xi\in\mathfrak{H}
be any unit vector. Then by Lemma
2.5
, Lemma
3.2
and Lemma

3.4
, we obtain

\displaystyle\displaystyle\left|\left<\sum_{i=1}^{n}\mathfrak{D}_{i}\mathfrak{C}_{i}^{m}\mathfrak{A}_{i}\xi,\xi\right>\right|^{2}

\displaystyle\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\displaystyle\frac{1}{m}\sum_{j=1}^{m}\left|\left<\sum_{i=1}^{n}\mathfrak{D}_{i}\mathfrak{C}_{i}^{m-j}\mathfrak{C}_{i}^{j}\mathfrak{A}_{i}\xi,\xi\right>\right|^{2}

\displaystyle\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\displaystyle\frac{n}{m}\sum_{j=1}^{m}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left|\left<\mathfrak{D}_{i}\mathfrak{C}_{i}^{m-j}\mathfrak{C}_{i}^{j}\mathfrak{A}_{i}\xi,\xi\right>\right|^{2}

\displaystyle\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\displaystyle\frac{n}{m}\sum_{j=1}^{m}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left<|\mathfrak{C}_{i}^{j}\mathfrak{A}_{i}|^{2}\xi,\xi\right>\left<|(\mathfrak{D}_{i}\mathfrak{C}_{i}^{m-j})^{*}|^{2}\xi,\xi\right>

\displaystyle\displaystyle\frac{n}{m}\sum_{j=1}^{m}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left(\frac{1}{p}\left<|\mathfrak{C}_{i}^{j}\mathfrak{A}_{i}|^{2p}\xi,\xi\right>+\frac{1}{q}\left<|(\mathfrak{D}_{i}\mathfrak{C}_{i}^{m-j})^{*}|^{2q}\xi,\xi\right>\right)

\displaystyle\displaystyle\left|\left<\sum_{i=1}^{n}\mathfrak{D}_{i}\mathfrak{C}_{i}^{m}\mathfrak{A}_{i}\xi,\xi\right>\right|^{2}

\displaystyle\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\displaystyle\frac{n}{m}\sum_{j=1}^{m}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left<\left(\frac{1}{p}|\mathfrak{C}_{i}^{j}\mathfrak{A}_{i}|^{2p}+\frac{1}{q}|(\mathfrak{D}_{i}\mathfrak{C}_{i}^{m-j})^{*}|^{2q}\right)\xi,\xi\right>

Taking the supremum over all unit vectors
\displaystyle\xi\in\mathfrak{H}

Theorem 3.13
.

\displaystyle\displaystyle\left|\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left<\mathfrak{X}_{i}\mathfrak{A}_{i}^{m}\mathfrak{B}_{i}\xi,\xi\right>\right|^{2r}

\displaystyle\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\displaystyle\frac{n^{2r-1}}{m}\sum_{j=1}^{m}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left<\left(\frac{1}{p}S_{i,j}^{pr}+\frac{1}{q}T_{i,j}^{qr}\right)\xi,\xi\right>

\displaystyle\displaystyle-

\displaystyle\displaystyle r_{0}\frac{n^{2r-1}}{m}\sum_{j=1}^{m}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left(\left<S_{i,j}^{r}\xi,\xi\right>^{\frac{p}{2}}-\left<T_{i,j}^{r}\xi,\xi\right>^{\frac{q}{2}}\right)^{2}.

Taking the supremum over all unit vectors
\displaystyle\xi\in\mathfrak{H}
, we deduce the desired result.
∎

Theorem 3.17
.

Let
\displaystyle\mathfrak{A}_{i},\mathfrak{B}_{i},\mathfrak{X}_{i}\in\mathfrak{B}(\mathfrak{H})
,
\displaystyle(i=1,\cdots,n)
,
\displaystyle m,k\in\mathbb{N}
,
\displaystyle p,q>1

\displaystyle\frac{1}{p}+\frac{1}{q}=1

\displaystyle\psi
and
\displaystyle\phi
be as in

3.12
. Then for all
\displaystyle r\geq 1
, we have

w^{2k}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n}\mathfrak{X}_{i}\mathfrak{A}_{i}^{m}\mathfrak{B}_{i}\right)\leq\frac{n^{2k-1}}{m}\sum_{j=1}^{m}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left\|\frac{1}{p}S_{i,j}^{pr}+\frac{1}{q}T_{i,j}^{qr}\right\|^{\frac{k}{r}}-r_{0}^{k}\inf_{\left\|x\right\|=1}\omega(\xi),

(51)

where
\displaystyle r_{0}=\min\{\frac{1}{p},\frac{1}{q}\}
,
\displaystyle S_{i,j}=\mathfrak{X}_{i}\psi^{2}\left(\left|\mathfrak{A}_{i}^{j*}\right|\right)\mathfrak{X}_{i}^{*}
,
\displaystyle T_{i,j}=\left(\mathfrak{A}_{i}^{m-j}\mathfrak{B}_{i}\right)^{*}\phi^{2}\left(\left|\mathfrak{A}_{i}^{j}\right|\right)\mathfrak{A}_{i}^{m-j}\mathfrak{B}_{i}

Proof.

Let
\displaystyle\xi\in\mathfrak{H}
be any unit vector. Then by Lemma
3.3
, Lemma

3.4
and Lemma
3.12
, we obtain

\displaystyle\displaystyle\left|\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left<\mathfrak{X}_{i}\mathfrak{A}_{i}^{m}\mathfrak{B}_{i}\xi,\xi\right>\right|^{2k}

\displaystyle\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\displaystyle\frac{1}{m}\sum_{j=1}^{m}\left|\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left<\mathfrak{X}_{i}\mathfrak{A}_{i}^{m-j}\mathfrak{A}_{i}^{j}\mathfrak{B}_{i}\xi,\xi\right>\right|^{2k}

\displaystyle\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\displaystyle\frac{1}{m}\sum_{j=1}^{m}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left|\left<\mathfrak{X}_{i}\mathfrak{A}_{i}^{m-j}\mathfrak{A}_{i}^{j}\mathfrak{B}_{i}\xi,\xi\right>\right|\right)^{2k}

\displaystyle\displaystyle\frac{n^{2k-1}}{m}\sum_{j=1}^{m}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left|\left<\mathfrak{X}_{i}\mathfrak{A}_{i}^{m-j}\mathfrak{A}_{i}^{j}\mathfrak{B}_{i}\xi,\xi\right>\right|^{2k}

\displaystyle\displaystyle\frac{n^{2k-1}}{m}\sum_{j=1}^{m}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left|\left<\mathfrak{A}_{i}^{j*}\mathfrak{X}_{i}^{*}x,\mathfrak{A}_{i}^{m-j}\mathfrak{B}_{i}x\right>\right|^{2k}
