Generalized Mahler measures of Laurent polynomials
Subham Roy

Abstract
. Following the work of Lalín and Mittal on the Mahler measure over arbitrary tori, we investigate the definition of the generalized Mahler measure for all Laurent polynomials in two variables when they do not vanish on the integration torus. We establish certain relations between the standard Mahler measure and the generalized Mahler measure of such polynomials. Later we focus our investigation on a tempered family of polynomials originally studied by Boyd, namely Q r ​ ( x , y ) = x + 1 x + y + 1 y + r Q_{r}(x\mathchar 59\relax y)=x+\frac{1}{x}+y+\frac{1}{y}+r with r ∈ ℂ ​ ; r\in\mathbb{C}\mathord{\mathchar 59\relax} and apply our results to this family. For the r = 4 r=4 case, we explicitly calculate the generalized Mahler measure of Q 4 Q_{4} over any arbitrary torus in terms of special values of the Bloch–Wigner dilogarithm. Finally, we extend our results to the several variable settings.

7.
Conclusion

There are several directions for further exploration. The most immediate question one can ask is how to evaluate
\mathrm{m}_{a\mathchar 59\relax b}(Q_{r})
when

A primary observation in this case is that the integration path is not necessarily closed (and in most cases it is not). This turns out to be a challenging problem since the integration path in this case cannot be easily identified as a cycle in the homology group. We face a similar obstacle while evaluating the Mahler measure of
Q_{r}(x\mathchar 59\relax y)
on the bounded connected components when the number of roots of
Q_{r}(a\mathchar 59\relax y)
(counting multiplicity) (or

) inside

(or
\mathbb{T}_{a}^{1}

(when considered as a polynomial in either
x
or
y

n

A different direction would be to consider the family of rational polynomials

Our method of proof for Theorems
1.3
and
1.5
extends to this type of rational polynomials when

is a monomial, which essentially recovers Theorems
1.2
and

The expression of
