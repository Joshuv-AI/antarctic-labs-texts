Cyber-Physical-Systems and Secrecy Outage Probability: Revisited
Makan Zamanipour

Abstract
This paper technically explores the secrecy rate Λ \Lambda and a maximisation problem over the concave version of the secrecy outage probability (SOP) as 𝕄 ​ ax Δ ⁡ ℙ ​ 𝓇 ​ ( Λ ≥ λ ) \mathop{{\rm\mathbb{M}ax}}\limits_{\Delta}{\rm\;}\mathbb{P}\mathscr{r}\big(\Lambda\geq\lambda\big) . We do this from a generic viewpoint even though we use a traditional Wyner’s wiretap channel for our system model − - something that can be extended to every kind of secrecy modeling and analysis. We consider a Riemannian mani-fold for it and we mathematically define a volume for it as 𝕍 ​ ℴ ​ 𝓁 ​ { Λ } \mathbb{V}\mathscr{ol}\big\{\Lambda\big\} . Through achieving a new bound for the Riemannian mani-fold and its volume, we subsequently relate it to the number of eigen-values existing in the relative probabilistic closure. We prove in-between some novel lemmas with the aid of some useful inequalities such as the Finsler’s lemma, the generalised Young’s inequality, the generalised Brunn-Minkowski inequality, the Talagrand’s concentration inequality. We additionally propose a novel Markov decision process based reinforcement learning algorithm in order to find the optimal policy in relation to the eigenvalue distributions − - something that is extended to a possibilisitically semi-Markov decision process for the case of periodic attacks.

How can we guarantee highly adequate relaxations over the principle of SOP?
