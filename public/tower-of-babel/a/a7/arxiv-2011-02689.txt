On Interacting Higher-Spin Bosonic Gauge Fields


Abstract
We examine interacting bosonic higher-spin gauge fields in the BRST-antifield formalism. Assuming that an interacting action S S is a deformation of the free action with a deformation parameter g g , we solve the master equation ( S , S ) = 0 (S,S)=0 from the lower orders in g g . It is shown that, choosing a certain cubic interaction as the first-order deformation, we can solve the master equation and obtain an action containing all orders in g g . The anti-ghost number of the obtained action is less than or equal to two. Furthermore we show that the obtained action is lifted to that of interacting bosonic higher-spin gauge fields on anti-de Sitter spaces.

String theory may be regarded as a spontaneous symmetry-breaking phase

There are obstacles to constructing

In

.
To avoid this,

For cubic vertices, the allowed number of derivatives is clarified by using a light-cone formulation
