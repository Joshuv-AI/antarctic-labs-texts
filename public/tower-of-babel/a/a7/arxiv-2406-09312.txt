Abstract
. In this article, we will explore the fundamental concepts, including various basic concepts on d d -complex manifolds, along with several differential operators and examine the relationships between them. A d d -Kähler manifold is a d d -complex manifold equipped with a metric that satisfies a specific condition. We prove the Hodge decomposition theorem on compact d d -Kähler manifolds, which establishes a crucial relationship between certain de-Rham cohomology groups and Dolbeault cohomology groups on a compact d d -Kähler manifold .

Remark 3.18
.

Considering the de-Rham complex

We have the associated 1-symbol sequence

0\rightarrow\wedge^{0}{T^{\star}}^{\mathcal{C}}_{X}(X)\xrightarrow{\;\sigma_{1}(d_{\mathcal{C}})(\nu)\;}\wedge^{1}{T^{\star}}^{\mathcal{C}}_{X}(X)\xrightarrow{\;\sigma_{1}(d_{\mathcal{C}})(\nu)\;}\wedge^{2}{T^{\star}}^{\mathcal{C}}_{X}(X)\xrightarrow{\;\sigma_{1}(d_{\mathcal{C}})(\nu)\;}\dots.

Let

be a smooth section such that

, and

\displaystyle\sigma_{1}(d_{\mathcal{C}})_{U_{i}}(\nu_{U_{i}})(s_{U_{i}})(x)

\displaystyle=d\Big(\sqrt{-1}\big(f_{U_{i}}-f_{U_{i}}(x)\big)s_{U_{i}}\Big)(x)

\displaystyle=\sqrt{-1}d\big(f_{U_{i}}-f_{U_{i}}(x)\big)(x)\wedge s_{U_{i}}(x)+\sqrt{-1}\big(f_{U_{i}}-f_{U_{i}}(x)\big)(x)(d(s_{U_{i}})(x))

\displaystyle=\sqrt{-1}\nu_{U_{i}}(x)\wedge s_{U_{i}}(x)

and

\displaystyle\Big(\sigma_{1}(d_{\mathcal{C}})_{U_{i}}(\nu_{U_{i}})\circ\sigma_{1}(d_{\mathcal{C}})_{U_{i}}(\nu_{U_{i}})\Big)s_{U_{i}}(x)

\displaystyle=\sigma_{1}(d_{\mathcal{C}})_{U_{i}}(\nu_{U_{i}})\sqrt{-1}\big(d(f_{U_{i}})\wedge s_{U_{i}}\big)(x)

\displaystyle=\sqrt{-1}\nu_{U_{i}}(x)\wedge i\nu_{U_{i}}(x)\wedge s_{U_{i}}(x)

\displaystyle=0

Similarly, for the Dolbeault complex

Here,

\sigma_{1}(\overline{\partial})(\nu_{U_{i}})(s_{U_{i}})(x)=\sqrt{-1}\nu_{U_{i}}^{(0,1)}(x)\wedge(s_{U_{i}})(x)

for
\nu=\{\nu_{U_{i}}\}_{i\in I}=\{d(f_{U_{i}})\}_{i\in I}\in({T^{\star}}^{\mathcal{C}}_{X}\otimes\mathcal{L})(X)
and

\displaystyle\Big(\sigma_{1}(\overline{\partial})(\nu_{U_{i}})\circ\sigma_{1}(\overline{\partial})(\nu_{U_{i}})\Big)s_{U_{i}}(x)

\displaystyle=\sigma_{1}(\overline{\partial})(\nu_{U_{i}})\sqrt{-1}\big(d(f_{U_{i}})^{(0,1)}\wedge s_{U_{i}}\big)(x)

\displaystyle=\sqrt{-1}\nu_{U_{i}}^{(0,1)}(x)\wedge i\nu_{U_{i}}^{(0,1)}(x)\wedge s_{U_{i}}(x)

Let

(1)
