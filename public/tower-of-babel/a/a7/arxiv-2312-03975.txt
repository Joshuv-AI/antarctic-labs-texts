The classification of Boolean degree 1 1 functions in high-dimensional finite vector spaces
Ferdinand Ihringer

Abstract
We classify the Boolean degree 1 1 functions of k k -spaces in a vector space of dimension n n (also known as Cameron-Liebler classes ) over the field with q q elements for n ≥ n 0 ​ ( k , q ) n\geq n_{0}(k,q) . This also implies that two-intersecting sets with respect to k k -spaces do not exist for n ≥ n 0 ​ ( k , q ) n\geq n_{0}(k,q) . Our main ingredient is the Ramsey theory for geometric lattices.

•

Determine the smallest value for

Extend the argument to closely related structures,
