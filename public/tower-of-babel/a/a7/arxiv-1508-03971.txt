Theorem 2.1

Let

Proof:
Let

and

G_{1}
and

be a clique in

. Suppose that

is not a maximal complete subgraph in

such that

is a proper subgraph of

is a proper subgraph of

,

or

. Suppose

, which is a contradiction as

Conversely, let

. Suppose that

and

is a clique in

. If

. This implies that

is a clique in
G_{2}
as

is a clique in

. Let

. Then by the definition of

, one can observe that the induced subgraph of

is complete in

, which is a contradiction as

. Similarly we can prove that

. Let

be the induced subgraph of

and

be the induced subgraph of

is a maximal complete subgraph of

,

and

respectively. Otherwise, if

then there is a maximal complete subgraph

Theorem 2.3

Let

,
G_{2}
be two graphs. If

, then

is complete if and only if either

Proof:
Let
G=G_{1}+G_{2}

Conversely, suppose that

is complete and

,
\mathcal{K}_{G_{2}}=\{Y_{1},Y_{2},\dots,Y_{m}\}
. By Corollary

, it follows that

has exactly
nm

be the set of all cliques of

. Then
Q
is the vertex set of

. Arranging the elements of

in the matrix form

, we have

Q_{21}&Q_{22}&Q_{23}&\dots&Q_{2m}\\
\vdots&\vdots&\vdots&\ddots&\vdots\\

Let

,

be any two elements in

. Since

,

, it follows that

are cliques in

. Since

is complete,

,

in
\mathbb{N}
