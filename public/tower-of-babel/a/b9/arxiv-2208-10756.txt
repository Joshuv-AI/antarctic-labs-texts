What can we learn from the electromagnetic properties of hidden-charm molecular pentaquarks with single strangeness?
Fu-Lai Wang 1,2,3, Hong-Yan Zhou 1,2, Zhan-Wei Liu 1,2,3, Xiang Liu 1,2,3

Abstract
Inspired by the observation of the P c ​ s ​ ( 4459 ) P_{cs}(4459) and P ψ ​ s Λ ​ ( 4338 ) P_{\psi s}^{\Lambda}(4338) , we systematically investigate the magnetic moments, the transition magnetic moments, and the radiative decay behaviors of the S S -wave isoscalar Ξ c ( ′ ) D ¯ ( ∗ ) \Xi_{c}^{(\prime)}\bar{D}^{(*)} molecular pentaquark states in this work. Our quantitative investigation shows that their electromagnetic properties can provide important hint to decode the inner structure of these discussed isoscalar Ξ c ( ′ ) D ¯ ∗ \Xi_{c}^{(\prime)}\bar{D}^{*} molecular pentaquarks. As the potential research issue, we suggest future experiment to focus on the electromagnetic properties of these isoscalar Ξ c ( ′ ) D ¯ ( ∗ ) \Xi_{c}^{(\prime)}\bar{D}^{(*)} molecular pentaquarks, which are a typical hidden-charm molecular pentaquark system with single strangeness.
