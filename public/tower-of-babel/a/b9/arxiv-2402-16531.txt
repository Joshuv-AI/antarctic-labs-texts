Dunford–Pettis type properties of locally convex spaces
S. Gabriyelyan

Abstract
In 1953, Grothendieck introduced and studied the Dunford–Pettis property (the D ​ P DP property) and the strict Dunford–Pettis property (the strict D ​ P DP property). The D ​ P DP property of order p ∈ [ 1 , ∞ ] p\in[1,\infty] for Banach spaces was introduced by Castillo and Sanchez in 1993. Being motivated by these notions, for p , q ∈ [ 1 , ∞ ] p,q\in[1,\infty] , we define the strict Dunford–Pettis property of order p p (the strict D ​ P p DP_{p} property) and the sequential Dunford–Pettis property of order ( p , q ) (p,q) (the sequential D ​ P ( p , q ) DP_{(p,q)} property). We show that a locally convex space (lcs) E E has the D ​ P DP property iff the space E E endowed with the Grothendieck topology τ Σ ′ \tau_{\Sigma^{\prime}} has the weak Glicksberg property, and E E has the strict D ​ P p DP_{p} property iff the space ( E , τ Σ ′ ) (E,\tau_{\Sigma^{\prime}}) has the p p -Schur property. We also characterize lcs with the sequential D ​ P ( p , q ) DP_{(p,q)} property. Some permanent properties and relationships between Dunford–Pettis type properties are studied. Numerous (counter)examples are given. In particular, we give the first example of an lcs with the strict D ​ P DP property but without the D ​ P DP property and show that the completion of even normed spaces with the D ​ P DP property may not have the D ​ P DP property.

\tfrac{1}{\lambda}\leq b_{n}=\|x_{n}-x\|_{\ell_{p}}\leq\lambda\quad\mbox{ for every $n\in\omega$}.

(5.3)

Then (

Now, by Proposition 2.1.3 of
[

]

[
3
]
), there are a basic subsequence

of

and a linear topological isomorphism

such that

R(x_{n_{k}}-x)=a_{k}e_{k}\quad\mbox{ for every $k\in\omega$, where }\;a_{k}:=b_{n_{k}}=\|x_{n_{k}}-x\|_{\ell_{p}},

and such that the subspace
\overline{\{x_{n_{k}}-x\}_{k\in\omega}}^{\,\ell_{p}}
is complemented in
\ell_{p}
.

By (
5.3
), the map
Q:\ell_{p}\to\ell_{p}
defined by
Q(\xi_{k}):=(a_{k}\xi_{k})
,
(\xi_{k})\in\ell_{p}
, is a topological linear isomorphism. Hence, replacing

by

by

for every

and
a_{k}=1
for every

. Therefore we obtained that there are a weakly null sequence
\{y_{k}=x_{n_{k}}\}_{k\in\omega}
in

and a topological linear isomorphism

such that

R(y_{k})=e_{k}\quad\mbox{ for every $k\in\omega$},

(5.4)

and such that the subspace

is complemented in
\ell_{p}
. Let

)

contains an open subset of

for some
