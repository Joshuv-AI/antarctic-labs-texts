Two examples based on the properties of discrete measures
Sergey P. Suetin

Abstract
. In the paper we represent two examples which are based on the properties of discrete measures. In the first part of the paper we prove that for each probability measure μ \mu , supp ⁡ μ = [ − 1 , 1 ] \operatorname{supp}{\mu}=[-1,1] , which logarithmic potential is a continuous function on [ − 1 , 1 ] [-1,1] there exists a (discrete) measure σ = σ ⁡ ( μ ) \sigma=\sigma(\mu) , supp ⁡ σ = [ − 1 , 1 ] \operatorname{supp}{\sigma}=[-1,1] , with the following property. Let { P n ​ ( x , σ ) } \{P_{n}(x;\sigma)\} be the sequence of polynomials orthogonal with respect to σ \sigma . Then 1 n ​ χ ​ ( P n ​ ( ⋅ , σ ) ) ​ → ∗ ​ μ \dfrac{1}{n}\chi(P_{n}(\cdot;\sigma))\overset{*}{\to}\mu , n → ∞ n\to\infty , where χ ⁡ ( ⋅ ) \chi(\cdot) is zero counting measure for the corresponding polynomial. The construction of the measure σ \sigma is based of the properties of weighted Leja points. In the second part we give an example of a compact set and a sequence of discrete measures supported on that compact set with the following property. The sequence of measures converges in weak- ∗ * topology to the equilibrium measure for the compact set but the corresponding sequence of the logarithmic potentials does not converge to the equilibrium potential in any neighbourhood of the compact set. 2.

In this section we construct two examples of compact sets and sequences of discrete measures supported on those compact sets with the following property. These sequences converge in weak-
*
topology to the equilibrium measures for the compact sets but the corresponding sequences of the logarithmic potentials do not converge to the equilibrium potentials in any neighbourhood of the compact sets.

The construction is based on the following well-known property of the logarithmic capacity (see.
[
1
, Chapter VII, § 1, Theorem 2]
).
Let
P_{n}(z)=z^{n}+\dotsb
be an arbitrary monic polynomial of degree
{n}
. Then for each non generated compact set
F\subset\mathbb{C}
and the corresponding
E=\left\{z:P_{n}(z)\in{F}\right\}
we have that

(14)

\operatorname{cap}(E)=\operatorname{cap}(F)^{1/n}.

In particularly for the case of a disk
F=\{w:|w|\leqslant{\rho^{n}}\}
we obtain

\operatorname{cap}\left\{z:\bigl|P_{n}(z)\bigr|\leqslant{\rho^{n}}\right\}=\rho.

The following statement was given by Herbert Stahl in
[
7
, p. 166]
but without details.

Proposition 2
.

Let
\Gamma=\{z:|z|=1\}
be the unit circle,
\{\mu_{n}\}
be the sequence of probability measures associated with the zeros of monic polynomials
\{z^{n}-1\}
:

\mu_{n}=\frac{1}{n}\sum_{j=1}^{n}\delta_{z_{n,j}}

(
\{z_{n,j}\}
are the roots of degree
n
from
1
).
Let
\lambda_{\Gamma}
be the equilibrium measure for the unit circle
\Gamma
. Then
\mu_{n}\overset{*}{\to}\lambda_{\Gamma}
as
n\to\infty
, but

V^{\mu_{n}}(z)\overset{\operatorname{cap}}{\not\rightarrow}V^{\lambda_{\Gamma}}(z)

in any neighbourhood of the compact set
\Gamma
.

Proof.

Let fix an arbitrary
\varepsilon>0
and consider the set

E_{n}:=\left\{z:|z|\geqslant 1,\bigl|V^{\mu_{n}}(z)-V^{\lambda_{\Gamma}}(z)\bigr|\geqslant\varepsilon\right\},\quad\text{where}\quad V^{\lambda_{\Gamma}}(z)=\log\frac{1}{|z|},\quad|z|\geqslant 1.

We have that

V^{\mu_{n}}(z)-V^{\lambda_{\Gamma}}(z)=\frac{1}{n}\sum_{j=1}^{n}\log\frac{1}{|z-z_{n,j}|}-\log\frac{1}{|z|}\\
=\frac{1}{n}\log\frac{|z|^{n}}{|z^{n}-1|}.

Therefore

\displaystyle E_{n}

\displaystyle=\left\{z:|z|\geqslant 1,\biggl|\frac{1}{n}\log\frac{|z^{n}|}{|z^{n}-1|}\biggr|\geqslant\varepsilon\right\}\supset\left\{z:|z|\geqslant 1,\frac{1}{n}\log\frac{|z^{n}|}{|z^{n}-1|}\geqslant\varepsilon\right\}

\displaystyle=\left\{z:|z|\geqslant 1,\,|z^{n}-1|\leqslant|z^{n}|{e^{-n\varepsilon}}\right\}\supset\left\{z:|z^{n}|\geqslant 1,\,|z^{n}-1|\leqslant{e^{-n\varepsilon}}\right\}

\displaystyle=\left\{z:p_{n}(z)\in F_{n}\right\}=:\widetilde{E}_{n},

where
F_{n}=\{w:|w|\geqslant 1,|w-1|\leqslant{e^{-n\varepsilon}}\}
,
w=p_{n}(z)\equiv{z^{n}}
.
Let
\rho>1
and
K_{\rho}:=\{z:|z|\leqslant\rho\}
. Then for all natural
n
large enough,
n\geqslant n_{0}
, we have that
\widetilde{E}_{n}\subset K_{\rho}
. Hence
E_{n}\cap K_{\rho}\supset\widetilde{E}_{n}
for
n\geqslant n_{0}
. Therefore from (
14
) it follows that

\operatorname{cap}(E_{n}\cap K_{\rho})\geqslant\operatorname{cap}{\widetilde{E}_{n}}=\operatorname{cap}(F_{n})^{1/n}.

It is easy to see that
\displaystyle\frac{1}{4}e^{-n\varepsilon}<\operatorname{cap}(F_{n})<e^{-n\varepsilon}
. Thus

\operatorname{cap}\left(E_{n}\cap K_{\rho}\right)\geqslant\left(\frac{1}{4}\right)^{1/n}e^{-\varepsilon}\not\rightarrow 0\quad\text{as $n\to 0$}.

\square
∎

Proposition 3
.

Let
K=[-1,1]
,
\{\mu_{n}\}
be the sequence of probability measures associated with Chebyshev polynomials of first kind for
K
. Let
\lambda_{K}
be the equilibrium measure for the segment
K
.
Then
\mu_{n}\overset{*}{\to}\lambda_{K}
as
n\to\infty
, but

V^{\mu_{n}}(z)\overset{\operatorname{cap}}{\not\rightarrow}V^{\lambda_{K}}(z)

in any neighbourhood of the compact set
K
.

Proof.

Let fix an arbitrary
\varepsilon>0
and consider the set

E_{n}=\left\{z:\bigl|V^{\mu_{n}}(z)-V^{\lambda_{K}}(z)\bigr|\geqslant\varepsilon\right\},

where
\displaystyle V^{\lambda_{K}}(z)=\log\frac{2}{|\varphi(z)|}
,
\displaystyle\varphi(z)=z+(z^{2}-1)^{1/2}
. We obtain that

\displaystyle E_{n}

\displaystyle=\left\{z:\biggl|\frac{1}{n}\log\frac{1}{|T_{n}(z)|}-\log\frac{2}{|\varphi(z)|}\biggr|\geqslant\varepsilon\right\}=\left\{z:\biggl|\frac{1}{n}\log\biggl|\frac{\varphi(z)^{n}}{2^{n}T_{n}(z)}\biggr|\biggr|\geqslant\varepsilon\right\}

\displaystyle\supset\left\{z:\frac{1}{n}\log\biggl|\frac{\varphi(z)^{n}}{2^{n}T_{n}(z)}\biggr|\geqslant\varepsilon\right\}=\left\{z:\biggl|\frac{\varphi(z)^{n}}{2^{n}T_{n}(z)}\biggr|\geqslant e^{n\varepsilon}\right\}

\displaystyle=\left\{z:\biggl|\frac{2^{n}T_{n}(z)}{\varphi(z)^{n}}\biggr|\leqslant e^{-n\varepsilon}\right\}=\left\{z:\bigl|T_{n}(z)\bigr|\leqslant 2^{-n}|\varphi(z)^{n}|e^{-n\varepsilon}\right\}

\displaystyle\supset\left\{z:\bigl|T_{n}(z)\bigr|\leqslant 2^{-n}e^{-n\varepsilon}\right\}=\left\{z:T_{n}(z)\in F_{n}\right\}=:\widetilde{E}_{n},

where
F_{n}:=\{w:|w|\leqslant 2^{-n}e^{-n\varepsilon}\}
.

Let
K_{\rho}:=\{z:|\varphi(z)|\leqslant\rho\}
,
\rho>1
, be the closer of an ellipse. It is well-known that for the Chebyshev polynomials
T_{n}(z)=z^{n}+\dotsb
the explicit representation is valid

T_{n}(z)=\frac{1}{2^{n}}\left(\varphi(z)^{n}+\varphi(z)^{-n}\right),\quad z\notin K.

From this it follows that
\widetilde{E}_{n}\subset K_{\rho}
for all
n
large enough,
n\geqslant n_{0}
. Hence
E_{n}\cap K_{\rho}\supset\widetilde{E}_{n}
for
n\geqslant n_{0}
.
Therefore from (
14
) it follows that

\operatorname{cap}(E_{n}\cap K_{\rho})\geqslant\operatorname{cap}(\widetilde{E}_{n})=\operatorname{cap}(F_{n})^{1/n}.

Since
\operatorname{cap}(F_{n})^{1/n}=e^{-\varepsilon}/2
then

\operatorname{cap}\left(E_{n}\cap K_{\rho}\right)\geqslant\frac{1}{2}e^{-\varepsilon}\not\rightarrow 0\quad\text{as
$n\to 0$}.

\square
∎
