Operations on spectral partition Lie algebras and TAQ cohomology
Adela YiYu Zhang

Abstract
. We determine all natural operations and their relations on the homotopy groups of spectral partition Lie algebras, which coincide with 𝔽 p \mathbb{F}_{p} -linear topological André-Quillen cohomology operations at any prime. We construct unary operations and a shifted restricted Lie algebra structure on the homotopy groups of spectral partition Lie algebras. Then we prove a composition law for the unary operations, as well as a compatibility condition between unary operations and the shifted Lie bracket with restriction up to a unit for the restriction. Comparing with Brantner-Mathew’s result on the ranks of the homotopy groups of free spectral partition Lie algebras, we deduce that these generate all natural operations, thereby also recovering unpublished results of Kriz and Basterra-Mandell on 𝔽 p \mathbb{F}_{p} -linear TAQ cohomology operations. As a corollary, we determine the structure of natural operations on mod p p 𝕊 \mathbb{S} -linear TAQ cohomology.



1.
Introduction

Spectral partition Lie algebras are the key objects in the emerging field of spectral formal moduli problems in characteristic
p
. Work of Brantner and Mathew
[
BM19
]
showed that there is an equivalence of
\infty
-categories between spectral formal moduli problems over
\mathbb{F}_{p}
and spectral partition Lie algebras, generalizing the characteristic 0 phenomenon studied by Drinfeld
[
Dri
]
, Pridham
[
Pri10
]
, Lurie
[
Lur11
]
, and many others. In particular, this equivalence establishes spectral partition Lie algebras over
\mathbb{F}_{p}
as divided power algebras Koszul dual to non-unital
\mathbb{E}_{\infty}
-
\mathbb{F}_{p}
-algebras.

Since spectral partition Lie algebras are algebras over a certain monad
\mathrm{Lie}^{\pi}_{\mathbb{F}_{p},\mathbb{E}_{\infty}}
, the homotopy groups of free spectral partition Lie algebras
\mathrm{Lie}^{\pi}_{\mathbb{F}_{p},\mathbb{E}_{\infty}}(\Sigma^{i_{1}}\mathbb{F}_{p}\oplus\cdots\oplus\Sigma^{i_{k}}\mathbb{F}_{p})
parametrize all natural
k
-ary operations on the homotopy groups of spectral partition Lie algebras as
(i_{1},\ldots,i_{k})
varies. In
[
BM19
, Theorem 1.20]
, Brantner and Mathew obtained bases for homotopy groups of free spectral partition Lie algebras on single generators via an isotropy spectral sequence studied by Arone, Dwyer, and Lesh
[
ADL13
]
. Then they propagated the result to multiple generators by means of an EHP sequence and a decomposition of the partition complex developed by Arone and Brantner in
[
AB21
]
. Nonetheless, this method did not provide explicit descriptions of the nature of the operations, nor were the relations among the operations clarified.

On the other hand,
\mathbb{F}_{p}
-linear TAQ cohomology
\mathrm{TAQ}^{*}_{\mathbb{F}_{p}}(R;\mathbb{F}_{p})
of
\mathbb{E}_{\infty}
-
\mathbb{F}_{p}
-algebras
R
(
Definition

2.5
), first constructed by Kriz and Basterra
[
Bas99
]
, has representing objects trivial square-zero extensions
[
Law20
, 1.8.2]
. Hence reduced
\mathbb{F}_{p}
-linear TAQ cohomology groups of trivial algebras
\mathbb{F}_{p}\oplus\Sigma^{i_{1}}\mathbb{F}_{p}\oplus\cdots\oplus\Sigma^{i_{k}}\mathbb{F}_{p}
parametrize all natural
k
-ary operations. By
[
BM19
, Proposition 5.35]
, there is an isomorphism

\pi_{*}(\mathrm{Lie}^{\pi}_{\mathbb{F}_{p},\mathbb{E}_{\infty}}(\Sigma^{i_{1}}\mathbb{F}_{p}\oplus\cdots\oplus\Sigma^{i_{k}}\mathbb{F}_{p}))\oplus\mathbb{F}_{p}\cong\mathrm{TAQ}^{-*}_{\mathbb{F}_{p}}(\mathbb{F}_{p}\oplus\Sigma^{-i_{1}}\mathbb{F}_{p}\oplus\cdots\oplus\Sigma^{-i_{k}}\mathbb{F}_{p};\mathbb{F}_{p}).

Hence natural operations on the homotopy groups of spectral partition Lie algebras agree with cohomology operations on the (reduced)
\mathbb{F}_{p}
-linear TAQ cohomology of
\mathbb{E}_{\infty}
-
\mathbb{F}_{p}
-algebras. In unpublished work, Kriz computed the
\mathbb{F}_{2}
-linear TAQ cohomology on a connective generator in
[
Kri93
, Theorem 1.10]
. Around the same time, Basterra and Mandell announced a computation of unary operations and their relations as the Koszul dual to Dyer-Lashof operations on
\mathbb{F}_{p}
-linear TAQ cohomology of connective objects for
p>2
and observed a shifted restricted Lie algebra structure, but a proof never appeared.

In this paper, we use a dual bar spectral sequence and the machinery of classical Koszul duality developed by Priddy
[
Pri70
]
to identify the structure of weight powers of
p
operations on the homotopy groups of spectral partition Lie algebras and
\mathbb{F}_{p}
-linear TAQ cohomology. Roughly speaking, this structure is given by a collection of unstable Ext groups over the Dyer-Lashof algebra, with composition product given by a sheared Yoneda product. The verification of the law of composition makes use of a general result of Brantner
[
Br17
]
that demonstrates the compatibility of the algebraic Koszul duality on the
E^{2}
-page of the (dual) bar spectral sequence with the
\infty
-categorical monadic Koszul duality that the
E^{\infty}
-page assembles to. These operations are stable under suspension, and in the colimit we obtain the universal algebra of unary operations, which is the Koszul dual of the Dyer-Lashof algebra.

Then we construct a shifted Lie bracket on the homotopy groups of spectral partition Lie algebras and use a homotopy fixed points spectral sequence to detect a restriction map on this shifted Lie bracket. The restriction on an odd degree homotopy class when
p>2
and any homotopy class when
p=2
coincides with the bottom unary operation up to a unit. Furthermore, brackets of unary operations that are not iterations of the restriction always vanish. Comparing with Brantner and Mathew’s additive computation of the homotopy groups of the free spectral partition Lie algebras on finitely many generators
[
BM19
, Theorem 1.20]
, we conclude that these are all the natural operations and obtain the target category in the sense that it records all algebraic structure on the homotopy groups of spectral partition Lie algebras.

As an immediate corollary, we determine the structure of operations on the mod
p
TAQ cohomology
\mathrm{TAQ}^{*}_{\mathbb{S}}(-;\mathbb{F}_{p})
of
\mathbb{E}_{\infty}
-
\mathbb{S}
-algebras. The
\mathbb{S}
-linear mod
p
TAQ cohomology operations consist of
\mathbb{F}_{p}
-linear TAQ cohomology operations, as well as mod
p
Steenrod operations. The relations among the unary operations are given by the Adem relations and the Nishida relations on mod
p
cohomology
[
Nis68
]
.

Remark 1.3
.

The shifted Lie brackets on the mod
p

p
, as was shown by Antolín-Camerena
[
AC20
]
and Kjaer
[

]
. The difference lies in that all unary operations on the mod
p

Remark 1.4

To identify the unit

on a degree

.

Remark 1.5

Recently, Konovalov

Since the functor

has representing object the trivial square-zero extension

For any tuple

Remark 1.7
.

In parallel to the theory of spectral partition Lie algebras, Brantner and Mathew developed a derived version called
partition Lie algebras
, which are algebras over a certain monad
\mathrm{Lie}^{\pi}_{\mathbb{F}_{p},\Delta}
on the derived category of chain complexes over
\mathbb{F}_{p}
. They showed that coconnective, finite type partition Lie algebras over
\mathbb{F}_{p}
serve as the Koszul dual to complete local Noetherian simplicial commutative
\mathbb{F}_{p}
-algebras
[
BM19
, Theorem 1.11 and 1.13]
. They then computed the ranks of homotopy groups of free partition Lie algebras in
[
BM19
, Theorem 1.16]
. While partition Lie algebras are more delicate than their spectral counterparts studied in this paper, we are hopeful that a similar strategy can be employed to obtain relations among the operations on the homotopy groups of partition Lie algebras. This will require as input the structure of natural operations on the homotopy groups of simplicial and cosimplicial commutative
\mathbb{F}_{p}
-algebras.

Note that for
p=2
, Goerss identified all operations and their relations on the homotopy groups of coconnective partition Lie algebras in
[
Goe90
, Theorem H]
via a Quillen spectral sequence and a Hilton-Milnor type argument, with input the structure of unary operations on the homotopy groups simplicial commutative
\mathbb{F}_{2}
-algebras by the works of Cartan
[
Car58
]
, Bousfield
[
Bou68
]
, Dwyer
[
Dwy80
]
. For
p>2
, a basis of unary operations on the homotopy groups simplicial commutative
\mathbb{F}_{p}
-algebras was computed by Nakaoka in the simplicial case
[
Nak57
,
Nak58
]
, and the quadratic relations among the operations were determined by Bousfield in
[
Bou68
, Theorem 8.9]
. In the cosimplicial case, Priddy determined the unary operations and their relations in
[
Pri73
]
at all primes.

Conventions

We denote by
\mathrm{Sp}
the
\infty
-category of spectra.
We use
\mathbb{F}_{p}
for both the field
\mathbb{F}_{p}
and the Eilenbarg-MacLane spectrum representing
\mathbb{F}_{p}
.
The grading convention is homological unless for TAQ cohomology groups or otherwise stated.

A weighted graded
\mathbb{F}_{p}
-module (resp.
\mathbb{F}_{p}
-module spectrum)

\mathbb{N}
-indexed collection of
\mathbb{Z}
-graded
\mathbb{F}_{p}
-modules (resp.
\mathbb{F}_{p}
-module spectra)

. The weight grading of an element

is
w
. Morphisms are weight preserving morphisms of graded
\mathbb{F}_{p}
-modules.
We assume that every object is graded and weighted whenever it makes sense. For instance,
\mathrm{Mod}_{\mathbb{F}_{p}}
stands for the ordinary category of weighted graded
\mathbb{F}_{p}
-modules, and
\mathrm{Mod}_{\mathbb{F}_{p}}(\mathrm{Sp})
the
\infty
-category of weighted graded module-spectra over
\mathbb{F}_{p}
(which is equivalent to the derived category of chain complexes over
\mathbb{F}_{p}
equipped with a weight grading). If the context is clear, we will call objects in either category
\mathbb{F}_{p}
-modules. The Day convolution
\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}
makes

(resp.
\mathrm{Mod}_{\mathbb{F}_{p}}(\mathrm{Sp})

x\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}y=(-1)^{|x||y|}y\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}x
for the symmetric monoidal product
\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}
depends only on the internal grading and not the weight grading.

Similarly, a shifted Lie algebra
L
over
\mathbb{F}_{p}
is a weighted graded
\mathbb{F}_{p}
-module

equipped with a shifted Lie bracket
[-,-]:L_{m}\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}L_{n}\rightarrow L_{m+n-1}
that adds weights, as well as satisfying graded commutativity
[x,y]=(-1)^{|x||y|}[y,x]
and the graded Jacobi identity

(-1)^{|x||z|}[x,[y,z]]+(-1)^{|y||x|}[y,[z,x]]+(-1)^{|z||y|}[z,[x,y]]=0.

If
p=2
, then we further require that
[x,x]=0
for all
x
. If
p=3
, then we further require that
[x,[x,x]]=0
for all
x
. Note that in general an unweighted Lie algebra over
\mathbb{F}_{p}
does not admit a weight decomposition.
We use
\pi_{n}(-)
to denote the functor taking the
n
th homotopy group of a spectrum or a simplicial
\mathbb{F}_{p}
-module, as well as the functor taking the
n
th homology group of a chain complex over
\mathbb{F}_{p}
.

We use
\mathbb{E}_{\infty}
and
\mathbb{E}^{\mathrm{nu}}_{\infty}
to denote respectively the unital and non-unital commutative operad in
\mathrm{Sp}
, and
\mathbb{E}^{\mathrm{nu}}_{\infty}\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}\mathbb{F}_{p}
the nonunital commutative operad in
\mathrm{Mod}_{\mathbb{F}_{p}}(\mathrm{Sp})
. Often we abuse notations and denote by
O
the monad associated to the free
O
-algebra functor when

.

2.1.
Spectral Lie operad

We begin with a brief review of the spectral Lie operad
\partial_{*}(\mathrm{Id})
.
Ching
[
Chi05
]
and Salvatore
[
Sal98
]
showed that the Goowillie derivatives
\{\partial_{n}(\mathrm{Id})\}
of the identity functor
\mathrm{Id}:\mathrm{Top}_{*}\rightarrow\mathrm{Top}_{*}
form an operad
\partial_{*}(\mathrm{Id})
in spectra. This operad is Koszul dual to the nonunital commutative operad
\mathbb{E}^{\mathrm{nu}}_{\infty}
via the operadic bar construction

\partial_{*}(\mathrm{Id})\simeq\mathbb{D}\mathrm{Bar}(1,\mathbb{E}_{\infty}^{\mathrm{nu}},1).

For a description of the operadic bar construction, see
[
Chi05
]
for a topological model using trees and
[
Br17
, Appendix D]
for an
\infty
-categorical construction along with a comparison with the topological model.

The
n
th-derivative
\partial_{n}(\mathrm{Id})
admits an explicit description due to Arone and Mahowald
[
AM99
]
, following the work of Johnson
[
Joh95
]
. Let
P_{n}
be the poset of partitions of the set
\underline{n}=\{1,2,\ldots,n\}
ordered by refinements, equipped with a
\Sigma_{n}
-action induced from that on
\underline{n}
. Denote by
\hat{0}
the discrete partition and
\hat{1}
the partition
\{\underline{n}\}
. Set
\Pi_{n}=P_{n}-\{\hat{0},\hat{1}\}
. Regarding a poset
P
as a category, we obtain via the nerve construction a simplicial set
N_{\bullet}(P)
. The
partition complex

\Sigma|\Pi_{n}|^{\diamond}
, the reduced-unreduced suspension of the geometric realization of
N_{\bullet}(\Pi_{n})
, is modeled by the simplicial set

N_{\bullet}(P_{n})/(N_{\bullet}(P_{n}-\hat{0})\cup N_{\bullet}(P_{n}-\hat{1}))

for
n\geq 2
and the simplicial 0-circle
S^{0}
for
n=1
.
Then there is an equivalence

\partial_{n}(\mathrm{Id})\simeq\mathbb{D}(\Sigma|\Pi_{n}|^{\diamond})

of spectra with
\Sigma_{n}
-action, where
\mathbb{D}
denotes the Spanier-Whitehead dual of a spectrum.

2.2.
Spectral partition Lie algebras

Motivated by the theory of classical operadic Koszul duality
[
GK94
]
, the natural next step is to formulate a Koszul duality theorem between suitable categories of algebras over the Koszul dual pair
\mathbb{E}^{\mathrm{nu}}_{\infty}
and
\partial_{*}(\mathrm{Id})
. Partial progress was achieved by Ching and Harper in
[
CH19
]
, following a general conjecture by Francis and Gaitsgory
[
FG12
]
. Recent work of Brantner and Mathew
[
BM19
]
on spectral partition Lie algebras completely resolved the question over
\mathbb{F}_{p}
, and we will give a very brief summary of their results.

Let
\mathrm{Mod}^{\mathrm{ft}}_{\mathbb{F}_{p}}\subset\mathrm{Mod}_{\mathbb{F}_{p}}(\mathrm{Sp})
be the subcategory spanned by
\mathbb{F}_{p}
-modules of
finite type
, i.e.
\mathbb{F}_{p}
-modules with degree-wise finite-dimensional homotopy groups. Denote by
\mathrm{Mod}^{\mathrm{ft}}_{\mathbb{F}_{p},\leq 0}\subset\mathrm{Mod}^{\mathrm{ft}}_{\mathbb{F}_{p}}
the subcategory spanned by coconnective objects. Let
\mathbb{E}^{\mathrm{nu}}_{\infty}\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}\mathbb{F}_{p}
be the nonunital commutative operad in
\mathrm{Mod}_{\mathbb{F}_{p}}(\mathrm{Sp})
. There is an adjunction
\mathrm{Alg}_{\mathbb{E}^{\mathrm{nu}}_{\infty}\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}\mathbb{F}_{p}}(\mathrm{Mod}_{\mathbb{F}_{p}}(\mathrm{Sp}))
\mathrm{Mod}_{\mathbb{F}_{p}}(\mathrm{Sp})
,
\mathrm{cot}
\mathrm{sqz}

where the functor sqz sends an object
M
to the trivial
\mathbb{E}^{\mathrm{nu}}_{\infty}\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}\mathbb{F}_{p}
-algebra
M
. The restriction of this adjunction to the subcategory
\mathrm{Mod}^{\mathrm{ft}}_{\mathbb{F}_{p},\leq 0}
defines a sifted-colimit-preserving monad
(M\mapsto\mathrm{cot}(\mathrm{sqz}(M)^{\vee})^{\vee})
on
\mathrm{Mod}^{\mathrm{ft}}_{\mathbb{F}_{p},\leq 0}
.

Definition 2.1
.

[
BM19
, Definition 5.32]

The
spectral partition Lie monad

\mathrm{Lie}^{\pi}_{\mathbb{F}_{p},\mathbb{E}_{\infty}}
is the unique sifted-colimit-preserving monad

\mathrm{Lie}^{\pi}_{\mathbb{F}_{p},\mathbb{E}_{\infty}}:\mathrm{Mod}_{\mathbb{F}_{p}}(\mathrm{Sp})\rightarrow\mathrm{Mod}_{\mathbb{F}_{p}}(\mathrm{Sp})

extending the monad
(M\mapsto\mathrm{cot}(\mathrm{sqz}(M)^{\vee})^{\vee})
on
\mathrm{Mod}^{\mathrm{ft}}_{\mathbb{F}_{p},\leq 0}
,

Algebras over the monad
\mathrm{Lie}^{\pi}_{\mathbb{F}_{p},\mathbb{E}_{\infty}}
are called
spectral partition Lie algebras
. The free spectral partition Lie algebras on bounded above objects admit an explicit description.

Proposition 2.2
.

[
BM19
, Proposition 5.35]

For

bounded above, we have

\mathrm{Lie}^{\pi}_{\mathbb{F}_{p},\mathbb{E}_{\infty}}(V)\simeq|\mathrm{Bar}_{\bullet}(\mathrm{id},\mathbb{E}^{\mathrm{nu}}_{\infty}\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}\mathbb{F}_{p},V^{\vee})|^{\vee}\simeq\bigoplus_{n\geq 1}\big((\partial_{n}(\mathrm{id})\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}\mathbb{F}_{p})\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}(V)^{\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}n}\big)^{h\Sigma_{n}}.

The above formula makes it clear that spectral partition Lie algebras are
not
algebras over the spectral Lie operad, as the structural map of an algebra

over the spectral Lie operad in
\mathrm{Mod}_{\mathbb{F}_{p}}(\mathrm{Sp})
is given by

\mathrm{Free}^{\partial_{*}(\mathrm{Id})\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}\mathbb{F}_{p}}(L)\simeq\bigoplus_{n\geq 1}\big((\partial_{n}(\mathrm{id})\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}\mathbb{F}_{p})\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}(L)^{\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}n}\big)_{h\Sigma_{n}}\rightarrow L.

Heuristically, spectral partition Lie algebras are the dual of divided power coalgebras over the cooperad
\mathrm{Bar}(1,\mathbb{E}^{\mathrm{nu}}_{\infty}\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}\mathbb{F}_{p},1)
, and hence candidates for the Koszul dual of
\mathbb{E}^{\mathrm{nu}}_{\infty}
-
\mathbb{F}_{p}
-algebras. To formulate the precise Koszul duality statement, we need to introduce one more condition.

Definition 2.3
.

An
\mathbb{E}_{\infty}
-
\mathbb{F}_{p}
-algebra

if

(1).
\pi_{0}(A)
is a complete local Noetherian ring;

(2).

is a finitely-generated module over
\pi_{0}(A)
for all

Now we can state a special case of the main results by Brantner and Mathew.

Theorem 2.4
.

[
BM19
, Theorem 1.19]

There is an equivalence of
\infty
-categories between complete local Noetherian
\mathbb{E}_{\infty}
-
\mathbb{F}_{p}
-algebras and the
\infty
-category of coconnective spectral partition Lie algebras of finite type.

-

2.4.
Operations

The goal of this paper is to understand natural operations and their relations on the homotopy groups of spectral partition Lie algebras and mod
p
TAQ cohomology. First we record a few general remarks about operations on algebras over a monad, adapted from Lawson’s excellent survey
[
Law20
, section 1.4]
and
[
Rez12
, section 3]
on the theory of operations for algebras over operads.

Given a monad
\mathbf{T}
on the
\infty
-category
\mathrm{Mod}_{\mathbb{F}_{p}}(\mathrm{Sp})
, we define an
operation
on
\mathbf{T}
-algebras to be a natural transformation
\pi_{m}(-)\rightarrow\pi_{n}(-)
of functors
\mathrm{Alg}_{\mathbf{T}}\rightarrow\mathrm{Sets}
for some
m,n
. Here
\mathrm{Alg}_{\mathbf{T}}=\mathrm{Alg}_{\mathbf{T}}(\mathrm{Mod}_{\mathbb{F}_{p}}(\mathrm{Sp}))
is the
\infty
-category of
\mathbf{T}
-algebras over
\mathrm{Mod}_{\mathbb{F}_{p}}(\mathrm{Sp})
. Let
\mathrm{Op}(m;n)
be the set of operations for fixed
m,n
.
It follows from the universal property of free algebras that for any
\mathbf{T}
-algebra
A
,

\pi_{m}(A)\cong\mathrm{Map}_{\mathrm{Alg}_{\mathbf{T}}}(\mathrm{Free}^{\mathbf{T}}(\Sigma^{m}\mathbb{F}_{p}),A).

Hence
\mathrm{Free}^{\mathbf{T}}(\Sigma^{m}\mathbb{F}_{p})
is the representing object for the functor
\pi_{m}(-)
on
\mathrm{Alg}_{\mathbf{T}}
.

By the Yoneda Lemma, the set of operations
\mathrm{Op}(m;n)
, or equivalently natural transformations
\pi_{m}(-)\rightarrow\pi_{n}(-)
in
\mathrm{Alg}_{\mathbf{T}}
, is isomorphic to
\pi_{n}(\mathrm{Free}^{\mathbf{T}}(\Sigma^{m}\mathbb{F}_{p}))
. Explicitly, given an operation
\alpha\in\pi_{n}(\mathrm{Free}^{\mathbf{T}}(\Sigma^{m}\mathbb{F}_{p}))
and a class
x\in\pi_{m}(A)
with
A
a
\mathbf{T}
-algebra, we obtain a class
\alpha(x)
in
\pi_{n}(A)
via the pullback

\pi_{m}(A)\cong\mathrm{Map}_{\mathrm{Alg}_{\mathbf{T}}}(\mathrm{Free}^{\mathbf{T}}(\Sigma^{m}\mathbb{F}_{p}),A)\xrightarrow{\alpha^{*}}\mathrm{Map}_{\mathrm{Alg}_{\mathbf{T}}}(\mathrm{Free}^{\mathbf{T}}(\Sigma^{n}\mathbb{F}_{p}),A)\cong\pi_{n}(A).

Therefore, to understand the unary operations on
\mathbf{T}
-algebras and their relations, we need to first compute
\pi_{*}(\mathrm{Free}^{\mathbf{T}}(\Sigma^{m}\mathbb{F}_{p}))
as an algebra for all
m
. Then we need to understand the composition product on unary operations

\pi_{n}(\mathrm{Free}^{\mathbf{T}}(\Sigma^{m}\mathbb{F}_{p}))\times\pi_{m}(\mathrm{Free}^{\mathbf{T}}(\Sigma^{l}\mathbb{F}_{p}))\rightarrow\pi_{n}(\mathrm{Free}^{\mathbf{T}}(\Sigma^{l}\mathbb{F}_{p}))

for all
l,m,n
, which corresponds to composing two natural transformations
\pi_{l}(-)\rightarrow\pi_{m}(-)
and
\pi_{m}(-)\rightarrow\pi_{n}(-)
of functors on
h\mathrm{Alg}_{\mathbf{T}}
. In general, natural
k
-ary operations
\prod_{l=1}^{k}\pi_{i_{l}}(-)\rightarrow\pi_{n}(-)
are parametrized by the homotopy groups

\pi_{n}(\mathrm{Free}^{\mathbf{T}}(\Sigma^{i_{1}}\mathbb{F}_{p}\oplus\cdots\oplus\Sigma^{i_{k}}\mathbb{F}_{p}))

for all
k
-tuples
(i_{1},\ldots,i_{k})
.

Remark 2.6
.

Since taking homotopy groups yields an equivalence between the homotopy category associated with
\mathrm{Mod}_{\mathbb{F}_{p}}(\mathrm{Sp})
and
\mathrm{Mod}_{\mathbb{F}_{p}}
, the monad
\mathbf{T}
on
\mathrm{Mod}_{\mathbb{F}_{p}}(\mathrm{Sp})
descends to a monad
\pazocal{T}

Remark 5.11

The interaction between the unary operations and the shifted Lie bracket on the homotopy groups of spectral partition Lie algebras differ from that on the homology of spectral Lie algebras. It was shown in

6.
The algebraic structure of the homotopy groups of spectral partition Lie algebras

In this section, we deduce all natural operations on the homotopy groups of spectral partition Lie algebras and mod
p
TAQ (co)homology. First we show that composition product of unary operations of weight
p^{k}
on the homotopy groups of spectral partition Lie algebras agrees, up to a shearing, with the Yoneda product on the
E^{2}
-page of the dual bar spectral sequence. This makes use a general result of Brantner (
[

6.1.

A convenient way to encode the structure of unary operations of weight

Proposition 7.2
.

The Steenrod operations commute with the bracket via the usual Cartan formula

Sq^{a}[x,y]=\sum_{i}[Sq^{i}(x),Sq^{a-i}(y)],\,\,\mathrm{for\ }p=2,

P^{a}[x,y]=\sum_{i}[P^{i}(x),P^{a-i}(y)],\,\,\,\,\beta P^{a}[x,y]=\sum_{i}([\beta P^{i}(x),P^{a-i}(y)]+[P^{i}(x),\beta P^{a-i}(y)])

for
p>2
. They commute with unary
\mathbb{F}_{p}
-linear TAQ cohomology operations
R^{i}
via the Nishida relations on mod
p
cohomology, i.e.,

Sq^{a}R^{-|x|+1}(x)=\sum\binom{j-c}{a-2c}R^{a+j+1-c}Sq^{c}(x)+\sum_{l<k,l+k=a}[Sq^{l}(x),Sq^{k}(x)],

Sq^{a}R^{b}(x)=\sum\binom{b-1-c}{a-2c}R^{a+b-c}Sq^{c}(x),\,\,\,\,b>-|x|+1

for
p=2
. For
p>2
we have

\displaystyle P^{n}\beta R^{j}(x)=

\displaystyle(-1)^{n-i}\sum_{i}\binom{(j-i)(p-1)}{n-pi}\beta R^{n+j-i}P^{i}(x)

\displaystyle+(-1)^{n-i}\sum_{i}\binom{(j-i)(p-1)-1}{n-pi-1}R^{n+j-i}\beta P^{i}(x),

P^{n}R^{j}(x)=(-1)^{n-i}\sum_{i}\binom{(j-i)(p-1)-1}{n-pi}R^{n+j-i}P^{i}(x)

for all

\displaystyle P^{n}R^{j}(x)=

\displaystyle(-1)^{n-i}\sum_{i}\binom{(j-i)(p-1)-1}{n-pi}R^{n+j-i}P^{i}(x)

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{\lambda_{|x|}}\sum_{I,\sigma\in\Sigma_{p},\sigma(1)=1}[[\cdots[[P^{i_{\sigma(1)}}(x),P^{i_{\sigma(2)}}(x)],P^{i_{\sigma(3)}}(x)]\cdots],P^{i_{\sigma(p)}}(x)]

Remark 7.3
.

Note that the commuting relations between the Steenrod operations and the TAQ cohomology operations
R^{i}
coincide with the Adem relations for Steenrod algebras, thereby reinforcing the heuristics that the operations
R^{i}
are extended Steenrod operations.

Proof of Proposition 6.2.

We will derive the commuting relations from the
E^{2}
-page of the dual bar spectral sequence on a single cohomology class in degree
j
.
Since free functors preserve colimits, it follows from the limiting case of Proposition
and
that the dual bar spectral sequence converging to

\overline{\mathrm{TAQ}}^{-*}_{\mathbb{F}_{p}}(\overline{\mathrm{TAQ}}_{\mathbb{F}_{p}}(\Sigma^{j}\mathbb{F}_{p}\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}\mathbb{F}_{p})\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}\mathbb{F}_{p})\cong\pi_{*}(\mathrm{Lie}^{\pi}_{\mathbb{F}_{p},\mathbb{E}_{\infty}}(\mathrm{Lie}^{\pi}_{\mathbb{F}_{p},\mathbb{E}_{\infty}}(\Sigma^{-j}\mathbb{F}_{p}\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}\mathbb{F}_{p}))\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}\mathbb{F}_{p})

collapses on the
E^{2}
-page with

E^{\infty}\cong E^{2}\cong\mathrm{Free}^{\mathrm{Lie}^{s,\rho}_{\pazocal{P}}}(\mathrm{Free}^{\mathrm{Lie}^{s,\rho}_{\pazocal{P}}}(\Sigma^{-j}\pazocal{A})\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}\mathbb{F}_{p}).

Note that the Steenrod operations are the linear dual of the Dyer-Lashof operations
[
May72
]
, which satisfy the Cartan formula with respect to the polynomial product (
Definition

3.2
). Since the generator of the shifted Lie bracket on the
E^{2}
-page is represented by the linear dual of the polynomial product on the
E^{1}
-page, it commutes with the Steenrod operations via the Cartan formula. On the other hand, the operations
R^{i}=(Q^{i-1})^{*}
come from the linear dual of the Dyer-Lashof operations
Q^{i-1}
, so the Steenrod operations commute with
R^{i}
via the Nishida relations on cohomology. When
p=2
the relations are worked out explicitly, for example, by Miller in

[
Mil16
]
. The Nishida relations for applying a Steenrod operation to the bottom operation on
x
involves an extra bracket term because the bottom operation is the restriction on
x
.

For
p>2
, the Nishida relations on cohomology can be read off from Theorem 3 and its corollary in Nishida’s original paper
[
Nis68
]
:

P^{n}\beta R^{j}=(-1)^{n-i}\sum_{i}\binom{(j-i)(p-1)}{n-pi}\beta R^{n+j-i}P^{i}+(-1)^{n-i}\sum_{i}\binom{(j-i)(p-1)-1}{n-pi-1}R^{n+j-i}\beta P^{i},

P^{n}R^{j}=(-1)^{n-i}\sum_{i}\binom{(j-i)(p-1)-1}{n-pi}R^{n+j-i}P^{i}.

Analogous to the case
p=2
, when
x
is a class in odd degree, the Nishida relations for the Steenrod action on the bottom class
P^{n}R^{(-|x|+1)/2}(x)
involve Lie bracket terms in addition to monomials of unary operations, since
\lambda_{|x|}R^{(-|x|+1)/2}(x)
is the restriction on
x
.

In order to determine the extra bracket terms, we need an explicit expression for the restriction map on an odd class. In the setting of unshifted graded
\mathbb{F}_{p}
-modules, this is worked out by Fresse in
[
Fre00
, Remark 1.2.8]
. Note that there is an embedding of the Lie operad into the associative operad Assoc. Furthermore, there is an identity

(13)

\sum_{\sigma\in\Sigma_{p}}X_{\sigma(1)}\cdots X_{\sigma(p)}=\sum_{\sigma\in\Sigma_{p},\sigma(1)=1}\langle\langle\cdots\langle\langle X_{\sigma(1)},X_{\sigma(2)}\rangle,X_{\sigma(3)}\rangle\cdots\rangle,X_{\sigma(p)}\rangle

in the associative operad, where
\langle x,y\rangle=xy-yx
is the commutator. For
x\in V
in even degree, the
p
th power on
x
is given by

\sum_{\sigma\in\Sigma_{p}}X_{\sigma(1)}\cdots X_{\sigma(p)}\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}x^{\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}p}\in(\mathrm{Assoc}(p)\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}V^{\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}p})^{\Sigma_{p}}\cong(\mathrm{Assoc}(p)\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}V^{\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}p})_{\Sigma_{p}}.

Using the identity (
13
), we can pull back the
p
th power on
x
along the embedding

(\mathrm{Lie}(p)\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}V^{\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}p})^{\Sigma_{p}}\hookrightarrow(\mathrm{Assoc}(p)\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}V^{\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}p})^{\Sigma_{p}}.

The resulting element is the restriction on
x
in the free restricted Lie algebra on
V
, i.e.,

(14)

x^{\{p\}}=\Big(\sum_{\sigma\in\Sigma_{p},\sigma(1)=1}[[\cdots[[X_{\sigma(1)},X_{\sigma(2)}],X_{\sigma(3)}]\cdots],X_{\sigma(p)}]\Big)\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}x^{\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}p}\in(\mathrm{Lie}(p)\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}V^{\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}p})^{\Sigma_{p}}.

Since we are working with shifted graded
\mathbb{F}_{p}
-modules, the commutator in the shifted graded associative algebra is
\langle x,y\rangle=xy-(-1)^{(|y|-1)(|x|-1)}yx
. If
x,y
are both in odd degrees, then
\langle x,y\rangle=xy-yx
. Hence the identity (
13
) pulls back to the restriction map (
14
) on an odd class
x
in the free shifted graded restricted Lie algebra over
\mathbb{F}_{p}
. Now we apply the Steenrod operation
P^{n}
to the
p
th power on
x
and use the Cartan formula. Note that the Steenrod operations
P^{a}
raises degree by an even number, so none of the signs are altered. Pulling back to the free shifted restricted Lie algebra, we deduce that the bracket terms in the Nishida relation for
P^{n}R^{(-|x|+1)/2}(x)
consists of terms

\frac{1}{\lambda_{|x|}}\sum_{\sigma\in\Sigma_{p},\sigma(1)=1}[[\cdots[[P^{i_{\sigma(1)}}(x),P^{i_{\sigma(2)}}(x)],P^{i_{\sigma(3)}}(x)]\cdots],P^{i_{\sigma(p)}}(x)]

for all nondecreasing sequences
0\leq i_{1}\leq i_{2}\leq\ldots\leq i_{p}
with
i_{1}+i_{2}+\cdots+i_{p}=n
.
∎
