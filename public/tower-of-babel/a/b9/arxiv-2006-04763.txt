Abstract.
The paper deals with the existence and uniqueness of a non-trivial solution to non-homogeneous
\displaystyle p(x)-
laplacian equations, managed by non polynomial growth operator in the framework of variable exponent Sobolev spaces on Riemannian manifolds. The mountain pass Theorem is used.
