Hidden Ergodic Ornstein-Uhlenbeck Process and Adaptive Filter
Yury A. Kutoyants Le Mans University, Le Mans, France National Research University “MPEI”, Moscow, Russia Tomsk State University, Tomsk, Russia

Abstract
The model of partially observed linear stochastic differential equations depending on some unknown parameters is considered. An approximation of the unobserved component is proposed. This approximation is realized in three steps. First an estimator of the method of moments of unknown parameter is constructed. Then this estimator is used for defining the One-step MLE-process and finally the last estimator is substituted to the equations of Kalman-Bucy (K-B) filter. The solution of obtained K-B equations provide us the approximation (adaptive K-B filter). The asymptotic properties of all mentioned estimators and MLE and Bayesian estimators of the unknown parameters are described. The asymptotic efficiency of the proposed adaptive filter is shown.

Solution of Riccati equation

Let us denote

\displaystyle r\left(\vartheta\right)=\left(a\left(\vartheta\right)^{2}+\frac{f\left(\vartheta\right)^{2}b\left(\vartheta\right)^{2}}{\sigma^{2}}\right)^{1/2},\qquad\quad\gamma_{*}\left(\vartheta\right)=\frac{\sigma^{2}\left[r\left(\vartheta\right)-a\left(\vartheta\right)\right]}{f\left(\vartheta\right)^{2}}.

Recall that in the case of constant parameters (
5
)-(
6
) the
Riccati equation (
8
) has an explicite solution
[
1
]

\displaystyle\gamma\left(\vartheta,t\right)=e^{-2r\left(\vartheta\right)t}\left[\frac{1}{\gamma\left(\vartheta,0\right)-\gamma_{*}\left(\vartheta\right)}+\frac{f\left(\vartheta\right)^{2}}{2r\left(\vartheta\right)\sigma^{2}}\left(1-e^{-2r\left(\vartheta\right)t}\right)\right]^{-1}+\gamma_{*}\left(\vartheta\right),

(11)

where we suppose that
\gamma\left(\vartheta,0\right)\not=\gamma_{*}\left(\vartheta\right)
. According to (
11

there exists a constant

\displaystyle\left|\gamma\left(\vartheta,t\right)-\gamma_{*}\left(\vartheta\right)\right|\leq C\,e^{-2r\left(\vartheta\right)t}\longrightarrow 0,\qquad{\rm as}\quad t\longrightarrow\infty.

This exponential convergence allows us in some
problems without loss of generality consider the stationary regime supposing
\gamma\left(\vartheta,0\right)=\gamma_{*}\left(\vartheta\right)
. This essentially simplifies the
calculations.

If
m_{0}\sim{\cal N}\left(0,\gamma_{*}\left(\vartheta\right)\right)
, i.e.,
the initial value
\gamma\left(\vartheta,0\right)=\gamma_{*}\left(\vartheta\right)
, then we have the equalities

\displaystyle\frac{\partial\gamma\left(\vartheta,t\right)}{\partial t}\equiv 0,\qquad\gamma\left(\vartheta,t\right)\equiv\gamma_{*}\left(\vartheta\right)\qquad{\rm for}\quad{\rm all}\quad t>0.

The value

is a positive solution

\displaystyle-2a\left(\vartheta\right)z-\frac{z^{2}f\left(\vartheta\right)^{2}}{\sigma^{2}}+b\left(\vartheta\right)^{2}=0,

which is obtained from the Riccati equation (

\displaystyle-2a\left(\vartheta\right)\gamma_{*}\left(\vartheta\right)-\frac{f\left(\vartheta\right)^{2}\gamma_{*}\left(\vartheta\right)^{2}}{\sigma^{2}}+b\left(\vartheta\right)^{2}=0.

Introduce one more notation
\Gamma\left(\vartheta\right)=\gamma_{*}\left(\vartheta\right)\sigma^{-2}f\left(\vartheta\right)^{2}=r\left(\vartheta\right)-a\left(\vartheta\right)

In this case the system (
7

8

\displaystyle{\rm d}m\left(\vartheta,t\right)=-\left[a\left(\vartheta\right)+\gamma\left(\vartheta\right)\right]m\left(\vartheta,t\right){\rm d}t+\gamma\left(\vartheta\right)f\left(\vartheta\right)^{-1}{\rm d}X_{t},\quad m\left(\vartheta,0\right)=m_{0}.

MLE and BE

We are given a two-dimensional diffusion process
\left\{X_{t},Y_{t},\;t\geq 0\right\}

\displaystyle{\rm d}Y_{t}=-\,a\left({\vartheta}\right)Y_{t}\,{\rm d}t+b\left({\vartheta}\right)\;{\rm d}V_{t},\qquad Y_{0},\;

\displaystyle{\rm d}X_{t}=\;f\left({\vartheta}\right)Y_{t}\,{\rm d}t+\;\sigma\,{\rm d}W_{t},\quad\quad X_{0}=0,

where
\left\{V_{t},W_{t},\;t\geq 0\right\}
are two independent Wiener
processes, the functions
a\left(\cdot\right)
,
b\left(\cdot\right)
,

f\left(\cdot\right)
and the constant
\sigma\neq 0
are known and
{\vartheta}
is a
finite-dimensional (unknown) parameter. The random variable
Y_{0}

is
{\mathfrak{F}}_{0}
-measurable and Gaussian.

Suppose as usual that only one component
X^{T}=\left\{X_{t},\;0\leq t\leq T\right\}
is observed and we have to estimate the parameter
{\vartheta}
. Our goal is to study the estimators of this parameter in the
asymptotic of large samples

.

This model of observations allows us to study estimators in the five
different situations:

F.

f\left(\vartheta\right)=\vartheta,\quad a\left(\vartheta\right)=a,\quad b\left(\vartheta\right)=b
,

A.

f\left(\vartheta\right)=f,\quad a\left(\vartheta\right)=\vartheta,\quad b\left(\vartheta\right)=b
,

B.

f\left(\vartheta\right)=f,\quad a\left(\vartheta\right)=a,\quad b\left(\vartheta\right)=\vartheta
,

AB.

f\left(\vartheta\right)=f,\quad a\left(\vartheta\right)=\theta_{1},\quad b\left(\vartheta\right)=\theta_{2}
, i.e.,

\vartheta=\left(\theta_{1},\theta_{2}\right)^{\top}
.

AF.

f\left(\vartheta\right)=\theta_{2},\quad a\left(\vartheta\right)=\theta_{1},\quad b\left(\vartheta\right)=b
.

The MLE
\hat{\vartheta}_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}
and BE
\tilde{\vartheta}_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}
are defined by

\displaystyle L(\hat{\vartheta}_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T},X^{T})=\sup_{\vartheta\in\Theta}L\left(\vartheta,X^{T}\right),\qquad\qquad\tilde{\vartheta}_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}=\frac{\int_{\Theta}\vartheta p\left(\vartheta\right)L\left(\vartheta,X^{T}\right){\rm d}\vartheta}{\int_{\Theta}p\left(\vartheta\right)L\left(\vartheta,X^{T}\right){\rm d}\vartheta}.

(13)

where

\displaystyle L\left(\vartheta,X^{T}\right)=\exp\left\{\int_{0}^{T}\frac{M\left(\vartheta,s\right)}{\sigma^{2}}\,{\rm d}X_{s}-\int_{0}^{t}\frac{M\left(\vartheta,s\right)^{2}}{2\sigma^{2}}{\rm d}s\right\}.

(14)

Here

and

\Theta

To construct the MLE of the parameter
\vartheta
we have to calculate the
random function

\left\{M\left(\vartheta,t\right),\;0\leq t\leq T\right\},\;\vartheta\in\Theta

by the equation

\displaystyle{\rm d}M\left(\vartheta,t\right)=-r\left(\vartheta\right)M\left(\vartheta,t\right)\,{\rm d}t+\Gamma\left(\vartheta\right){\rm d}X_{t},\qquad\quad M\left(\vartheta,0\right).

It will be convenient to consider the cases
{\bf F,A,B}
and
{\bf AB,AF}

separately. Therefore we study first the estimation of one-dimensional
parameter and then we consider the cases
{\bf AB,AF}
(two-dimensional parameter).

Suppose that the parameter
\vartheta
is one-dimensional and
\Theta=\left(\alpha,\beta\right)
. Introduce the Fisher information

\displaystyle{\rm I}\left(\vartheta\right)=\frac{\dot{a}\left(\vartheta\right)^{2}}{2a\left(\vartheta\right)}-\frac{2\dot{a}\left(\vartheta\right)\;\dot{r}\left(\vartheta\right)}{r\left(\vartheta\right)+a\left(\vartheta\right)}+\frac{\dot{r}\left(\vartheta\right)^{2}}{2r\left(\vartheta\right)},\qquad r\left(\vartheta\right)=\Bigl(a\left(\vartheta\right)^{2}+\sigma^{-2}f\left(\vartheta\right)^{2}b\left(\vartheta\right)^{2}\Bigr)^{1/2}.

Under regularity conditions given in the theorem below we have the lower bound
on the mean square risks of all estimators (Hajek-Le Cam’s bound)
[
9
]

\displaystyle\lim_{\nu\rightarrow 0}\mathop{\underline{\lim}}\limits_{T\rightarrow\infty}\sup_{\left|\vartheta-\vartheta_{0}\right|\leq\nu}T\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\left|\bar{\vartheta}_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}-\vartheta\right|^{2}\geq{\rm I}\left(\vartheta_{0}\right)^{-1}.

We call the estimator
\bar{\vartheta}_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}
asymptotically efficient if for all

\vartheta_{0}\in\Theta

\displaystyle\lim_{\nu\rightarrow 0}\lim_{T\rightarrow\infty}\sup_{\left|\vartheta-\vartheta_{0}\right|\leq\nu}T\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\left|\bar{\vartheta}_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}-\vartheta\right|^{2}={\rm I}\left(\vartheta_{0}\right)^{-1}.

Theorem 1
.

Suppose that the following conditions hold.

1.

The functions
a\left(\vartheta\right)
,
b\left(\vartheta\right)
,

f\left(\vartheta\right)
,
\vartheta\in\left[\alpha,\beta\right]
twice
continuously differentiable on
\bar{\Theta}
.

2.

These functions satisfy the conditions:

b\left(\vartheta\right)\neq 0
,
f\left(\vartheta\right)\neq 0
and

a\left(\vartheta\right)>0
for all

.

3.

For all
\nu>0
we have

\displaystyle\inf_{\vartheta_{0}\in\Theta}\inf_{\left|\vartheta-\vartheta_{0}\right|>\nu}\Bigl(\left|a(\vartheta)-a\left(\vartheta_{0}\right)\right|+\left|r(\vartheta)-r\left(\vartheta_{0}\right)\right|\Bigr)>0.

The following condition holds

\displaystyle\inf_{\vartheta\in\Theta}\Bigl(\left|\dot{a}\left(\vartheta\right)\right|+\left|\dot{r}\left(\vartheta\right)\right|\Bigr)>0.

Then the MLE

\hat{\vartheta}_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}
and BE
\tilde{\vartheta}_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}
are uniformly consistent,
uniformly on compacts
\mathbb{K}\subset\Theta
asymptotically normal

\displaystyle\sqrt{T}\left(\hat{\vartheta}_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}-\vartheta_{0}\right)\Longrightarrow\zeta\sim{\cal N}\left(0,{\rm I}\left(\vartheta_{0}\right)^{-1}\right),\quad\sqrt{T}\left(\tilde{\vartheta}_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}-\vartheta_{0}\right)\Longrightarrow\zeta,

polynomial moments converge: for any
p\geq 2

\displaystyle\lim_{T\rightarrow\infty}T^{p/2}\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta_{0}}\left|\hat{\vartheta}_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}-\vartheta_{0}\right|^{p}=\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta_{0}}\left|\zeta\right|^{p},\qquad\lim_{T\rightarrow\infty}T^{p/2}\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta_{0}}\left|\tilde{\vartheta}_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}-\vartheta_{0}\right|^{p}=\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta_{0}}\left|\zeta\right|^{p},

and the both estimators are asymptotically efficient.

For the proof see Theorem 3.1 in
[
16
]
.

Case F
,i.e.,\vartheta=f
.

Consider the particular case
\vartheta=f
. Then
r\left(\vartheta\right)=\left(a^{2}+{b^{2}\vartheta^{2}}{\sigma^{-2}}\right)^{1/2}
and the
Fisher information is

\displaystyle{\rm I}\left(\vartheta\right)=\frac{b^{4}\vartheta^{2}}{2\sigma^{4}r\left(\vartheta\right)^{3}}.

Hence by Theorem
1
the MLE and BE are consistent, asymptotically
normal

\displaystyle\sqrt{T}\left(\hat{\vartheta}_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}-\vartheta_{0}\right)\Longrightarrow{\cal N}\left(0,\frac{2\sigma^{4}r\left(\vartheta_{0}\right)^{3}}{b^{4}\vartheta_{0}^{2}}\right),\qquad\sqrt{T}\left(\tilde{\vartheta}_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}-\vartheta_{0}\right)\Longrightarrow{\cal N}\left(0,\frac{2\sigma^{4}r\left(\vartheta_{0}\right)^{3}}{b^{4}\vartheta_{0}^{2}}\right)

and are asymptotically efficient.

Case A
,i.e.,\vartheta=a
.

We have

and the

\displaystyle{\rm I}\left(\vartheta\right)=\frac{\left[r\left(\vartheta\right)^{2}-\vartheta^{2}\right]^{2}+r\left(\vartheta\right)\vartheta\left[r\left(\vartheta\right)-\vartheta\right]^{2}}{2\vartheta r\left(\vartheta\right)^{3}\left[r\left(\vartheta\right)+\vartheta\right]}=\frac{\Gamma\left(\vartheta\right)^{2}\left[\left(r\left(\vartheta\right)+\vartheta\right)^{2}+r\left(\vartheta\right)\vartheta\right]}{2\vartheta r\left(\vartheta\right)^{3}\left[r\left(\vartheta\right)+\vartheta\right]},

(15)

the both
estimators are asymptotically normal and asymptotically efficient. Say,

\displaystyle\sqrt{T}\left(\hat{\vartheta}_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}-\vartheta_{0}\right)\Longrightarrow{\cal N}\left(0,{\rm I}\left(\vartheta_{0}\right)^{-1}\right).

Case B
,i.e.,\vartheta=b
.

The Fisher information is

\displaystyle{\rm I}\left(\vartheta\right)=\frac{\vartheta^{2}f^{4}}{2r\left(\vartheta\right)^{3}\sigma^{4}}

and the MLE and BE have the corresponding asymptotic properties.

3
Method of moments estimators.

Consider the partially observed system

\displaystyle{\rm d}X_{t}

\displaystyle=f\,Y_{t}\,{\rm d}t+\sigma\,{\rm d}W_{t},\qquad\qquad\;X_{0},\quad\qquad 0\leq t\leq T,

(16)

\displaystyle{\rm d}Y_{t}

\displaystyle=-a\,Y_{t}\,{\rm d}t+b\,{\rm d}V_{t},\qquad\qquad Y_{0},\qquad\quad t\geq 0,

(17)

The process
X_{t},t\geq 0
is not ergodic and that is why for construction of
method of moments estimators we do not use integrals like

\displaystyle\frac{1}{T}\int_{0}^{T}g\left(X_{t}\right){\rm d}t.

At the beginning we suppose that
\vartheta=\left(f,a,b\right)\in\Theta
. The set
\Theta
is a bounded, convex, closed subset of
{\cal R}^{3}
and
the values of
a
are positive and separated from zero. For simplicity of
exposition we suppose that
T
is integer. The construction of estimators
follow the similar algorithms first used in the work
[
13
]
.

Introduce the notation

\displaystyle R_{1,T}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{T}\sum_{k=1}^{T}\left[X_{k}-X_{k-1}\right]^{2},\qquad\qquad\quad\Phi_{1}\left(\vartheta\right)=\frac{f^{2}b^{2}}{a^{3}}\left[e^{-a}-1+a\right]+\sigma^{2},

\displaystyle R_{2,T}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{T}\sum_{k=2}^{T}\left[X_{k}-X_{k-1}\right]\left[X_{k-1}-X_{k-2}\right],\qquad\quad\Phi_{2}\left(\vartheta\right)=\frac{f^{2}b^{2}}{2a^{3}}\left[1-e^{-a}\right]^{2},

\displaystyle K_{1,1}\left(\vartheta\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{{2f^{4}b^{4}}}{{{a}^{6}}}\left[e^{-{a}}-1+{a}\right]^{2}+\frac{f^{4}b^{4}e^{4a}\left[1-e^{-a}\right]^{3}}{a^{6}\left[1+e^{-a}\right]}

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad+\frac{4f^{2}\sigma^{2}b^{2}}{a^{3}}\left[e^{-a}-1+a\right]+{2\sigma^{4}},

\displaystyle R_{T}

\displaystyle=\left(R_{1,T},R_{2,T}\right)^{\top},\quad\qquad\Phi\left(\vartheta\right)=\left(\Phi_{1}\left(\vartheta\right),\Phi_{2}\left(\vartheta\right)\right)^{\top},\qquad\xi_{*}=\left(\xi_{1},\xi_{2}\right)^{\top},

\displaystyle{\bf K}\left(\vartheta\right)

\displaystyle=\left(\begin{array}[]{cc}K_{1,1}\left(\vartheta\right)&K_{1,2}\left(\vartheta\right)\\
K_{2,1}\left(\vartheta\right)&K_{2,2}\left(\vartheta\right)\\
\end{array}\right),\quad R_{T}=\left(R_{1,T},R_{2,T}\right)^{\top},\;\Phi\left(\vartheta\right)=\left(\Phi_{1}\left(\vartheta\right),\Phi_{2}\left(\vartheta\right)\right)^{\top}.

Here
\xi_{*}\sim{\cal N}\left(0,{\bf K}\left(\vartheta_{0}\right)\right)
and
the calculation of other terms of the matrix
{\bf K}\left(\vartheta\right)

we will discuss later.

In the work
[
17
]
the method of moments estimators (MME)
f_{T}^{*},a_{T}^{*}

and
b_{T}^{*}
were proposed and studied for the one-dimensional parameters

\vartheta=f
,
\vartheta=a
and
\vartheta=b
respectively. The MMEs are

\displaystyle f_{T}^{*}

\displaystyle=\left(\frac{a^{3}}{b^{2}\left[e^{-a}-1+a\right]T}\sum_{k=1}^{T}\left(\left[X_{k}-X_{k-1}\right]^{2}-\sigma^{2}\right)\right)^{1/2},

\displaystyle b_{T}^{*}

\displaystyle=\left(\frac{a^{3}}{f^{2}\left[e^{-a}-1+a\right]T}\sum_{k=1}^{T}\left(\left[X_{k}-X_{k-1}\right]^{2}-\sigma^{2}\right)\right)^{1/2},

\displaystyle a_{T}^{*}

\displaystyle=H\left(\frac{1}{f^{2}b^{2}T}\sum_{k=1}^{T}\left(\left[X_{k}-X_{k-1}\right]^{2}-\sigma^{2}\right)\right),

where
H\left(y\right)
is a function inverse to
y=h\left(x\right)=x^{-3}\left(x-1+e^{-x}\right)
. Below we show that
h\left(x\right),x>0
is strictly
decreasing function.

It was shown in
[
17
]
that these estimators are consistent and for any

p\geq 1

\displaystyle\sup_{f\in\mathbb{K}}T^{p/2}\mathbf{E}_{f}\left|f_{T}^{*}-f\right|^{p}\leq C,\;\sup_{b\in\mathbb{K}}T^{p/2}\mathbf{E}_{b}\left|b_{T}^{*}-b\right|^{p}\leq C,\;\sup_{a\in\mathbb{K}}T^{p/2}\mathbf{E}_{a}\left|a_{T}^{*}-a\right|^{p}\leq C,

(18)

For construction of One-step MLE-process we need just the estimates (
18
)
only.

The proposition below allows us to prove the asymptotic normality of these
estimators and to consider the two-dimensional case too.

Proposition 1
.

The statistic
R_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}
has the following properties:

1.

Uniformly
on
\Theta
it converges to
\Phi\left(\vartheta\right)
,
i.e., for any
\nu>0

\displaystyle\lim_{T\rightarrow\infty}\sup_{\vartheta\in\Theta}\mathbf{P}_{\vartheta}\left(\left\|R_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}-\Phi\left(\vartheta\right)\right\|\geq\nu\right)=0.

2.

Is uniformly
on compacts
\mathbb{K}\subset\Theta
asymptotically normal

\displaystyle\sqrt{T}\left(R_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}-\Phi\left(\vartheta\right)\right)\Longrightarrow\xi_{*},

(19)

3.

The moments converge: for any
p>0
uniformly on
\mathbb{K}

\displaystyle\lim_{T\rightarrow\infty}T^{p/2}\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\left\|R_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}-\Phi\left(\vartheta\right)\right\|^{p}=\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\left|\xi_{*}\right|^{p},

and there exists a constant
C>0
such that

\displaystyle\sup_{\vartheta\in\mathbb{K}}T^{p/2}\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\left\|R_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}-\Phi\left(\vartheta\right)\right\|^{p}\leq C.

(20)

Proof.

Using the equality (
16
) we write

\displaystyle R_{1,T}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{T}\sum_{k=1}^{T}\left[X_{k}-X_{k-1}\right]^{2}=\frac{1}{T}\sum_{k=1}^{T}\left[\int_{k-1}^{k}{\rm d}X_{s}\right]^{2}

\displaystyle=\frac{f^{2}}{T}\sum_{k=1}^{T}\eta_{k}^{2}+\frac{2f\sigma}{T}\sum_{k=1}^{T}\eta_{k}\left[W_{k}-W_{k-1}\right]+\frac{\sigma^{2}}{T}\sum_{k=1}^{T}\left[W_{k}-W_{k-1}\right]^{2},

where

\displaystyle\eta_{k}=\int_{k-1}^{k}Y_{t}\;{\rm d}t.

We have

\displaystyle\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta_{0}}R_{1,T}

\displaystyle=\frac{f^{2}}{T}\sum_{k=1}^{T}\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\eta_{k}^{2}+\sigma^{2}

because
Y^{T}
and
W^{T}
are independent.

For the process
Y^{T}
we have the equality

\displaystyle Y_{t}=Y_{0}e^{-at}+b\int_{0}^{t}e^{-a\left(t-s\right)}{\rm d}V_{s}.

Hence

\displaystyle\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}Y_{t}Y_{s}

\displaystyle=\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta_{0}}Y_{0}^{2}e^{-{a}\left(t+s\right)}+b^{2}e^{-{a}\left(t+s\right)}\int_{0}^{t\wedge s}e^{2{a}r}{\rm d}r

\displaystyle=\left[d^{2}-\frac{b^{2}}{2a}\right]e^{-{a}\left(t+s\right)}+\frac{b^{2}}{2a}e^{-{a}\left|t-s\right|}

and

\displaystyle\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\eta_{k}^{2}

\displaystyle=\int_{k-1}^{k}\int_{k-1}^{k}\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}Y_{t}Y_{s}\,{\rm d}s{\rm d}t

\displaystyle=\left[d^{2}-\frac{b^{2}}{2a}\right]\left(\int_{k-1}^{k}e^{-{a}t}{\rm d}t\right)^{2}+\frac{b^{2}}{2a}\int_{k-1}^{k}\int_{k-1}^{k}e^{-{a}\left|t-s\right|}{\rm d}s{\rm d}t

\displaystyle=\left[\frac{d^{2}}{a^{2}}-\frac{b^{2}}{2a^{3}}\right]\left[e^{a}-1\right]^{2}e^{-2{a}k}+\frac{b^{2}}{{a}^{3}}\left[e^{-{a}}-1+{a}\right].

Therefore

\displaystyle\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}R_{1,T}

\displaystyle=\left[e^{a}-1\right]^{2}\frac{f^{2}\left[2ad^{2}-b^{2}\right]}{{2a^{3}}T}\sum_{k=1}^{T}e^{-2{a}k}+\frac{f^{2}b^{2}}{{a}^{3}}\left[e^{-{a}}-1+{a}\right]+\sigma^{2}

\displaystyle=\frac{f^{2}b^{2}}{{a}^{3}}\left[e^{-{a}}-1+{a}\right]+\sigma^{2}+r_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T},\qquad\left|r_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}\right|\leq\frac{C}{T}.

For simplicity of calculations we suppose that the process
Y_{t},t\geq 0
is
stationary. Then

\displaystyle\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}R_{1,T}

\displaystyle=\frac{f^{2}b^{2}}{{a}^{3}}\left[e^{-{a}}-1+{a}\right]+\sigma^{2}.

The covariance is (
j<k
)

\displaystyle\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\eta_{k}\eta_{j}

\displaystyle=b^{2}\int_{k-1}^{k}\int_{j-1}^{j}\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}Y_{t}Y_{s}{\rm d}s{\rm d}t=b^{2}\int_{k-1}^{k}\int_{j-1}^{j}e^{-a\left(t+s\right)}\int_{-\infty}^{t\wedge s}e^{2au}{\rm d}u{\rm d}s{\rm d}t

\displaystyle=\frac{b^{2}}{2a}\int_{k-1}^{k}\int_{j-1}^{j}e^{-a\left|t-s\right|}{\rm d}s{\rm d}t=\frac{b^{2}e^{a}}{a^{3}}\left(1-e^{-a}\right)^{2}\,e^{-a\left|k-j\right|}.

Hence

\displaystyle\sup_{\vartheta\in\Theta}\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\eta_{k}\eta_{j}\leq C\,e^{-a\left|k-j\right|}

(21)

and this Gaussian sequence
\eta_{1},\eta_{2},\ldots
is exponentially mixing.
By the law of large numbers

\displaystyle R_{1,T}\longrightarrow\Phi_{1}\left(\vartheta\right)=\frac{f^{2}b^{2}}{{a}^{3}}\left[e^{-{a}}-1+{a}\right]+\sigma^{2},

as
T\rightarrow\infty
.

Then we can write

\displaystyle\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\left(R_{1,T}-\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}R_{1,T}\right)^{2}

\displaystyle\qquad=\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\left(\frac{f^{2}}{T}\sum_{k=1}^{T}\left[\eta_{k}^{2}-\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\eta_{k}^{2}\right]+\frac{2f\sigma}{T}\sum_{k=1}^{T}\eta_{k}\zeta_{k}+\frac{\sigma^{2}}{T}\sum_{k=1}^{T}\left(\zeta_{k}^{2}-1\right)\right)^{2}

\displaystyle\qquad=\frac{f^{4}}{T^{2}}\sum_{k=1}^{T}\sum_{j=1}^{T}\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\left(\left[\eta_{k}^{2}-\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\eta_{k}^{2}\right]\left[\eta_{j}^{2}-\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\eta_{j}^{2}\right]\right)+\frac{4f^{2}\sigma^{2}}{T^{2}}\sum_{k=1}^{T}\sum_{j=1}^{T}\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\bigl(\eta_{k}\eta_{j}\zeta_{k}\zeta_{j}\bigr)

\displaystyle\qquad\quad+\frac{\sigma^{4}}{T^{2}}\sum_{k=1}^{T}\sum_{j=1}^{T}\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\left(\zeta_{k}^{2}-1\right)\left(\zeta_{j}^{2}-1\right)

\displaystyle\qquad=\frac{f^{4}}{T^{2}}\sum_{k=1}^{T}\sum_{j=1}^{T}\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\left(\left[\eta_{k}^{2}-\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\eta_{k}^{2}\right]\left[\eta_{j}^{2}-\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\eta_{j}^{2}\right]\right)+\frac{4f^{2}\sigma^{2}}{T^{2}}\sum_{k=1}^{T}\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\eta_{k}^{2}

\displaystyle\qquad\quad+\frac{\sigma^{4}}{T^{2}}\sum_{k=1}^{T}\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\left(\zeta_{k}^{2}-1\right)^{2}

\displaystyle\qquad\leq\frac{f^{4}}{T^{2}}\sum_{k=1}^{T}\sum_{j=1}^{T}\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\left(\left[\eta_{k}^{2}-\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\eta_{k}^{2}\right]\left[\eta_{j}^{2}-\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\eta_{j}^{2}\right]\right)+\frac{4f^{2}\sigma^{2}b^{2}}{a^{3}T}\left[a-1+e^{-a}\right]+\frac{2\sigma^{4}}{T}.

For the last sum using once more the presentation (
17
) we obtain the
exponential bound like (
21
) as follows. Note that

\displaystyle\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\left(\left[\eta_{k}^{2}-\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\eta_{k}^{2}\right]\left[\eta_{j}^{2}-\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\eta_{j}^{2}\right]\right)=\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\left[\eta_{k}^{2}\eta_{j}^{2}\right]-\left(\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\left(\eta_{1}^{2}\right)\right)^{2},

where
\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\eta_{1}^{2}={b^{2}}{{a}^{-3}}\left[e^{-{a}}-1+{a}\right]
.

We have

\displaystyle\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\left[\eta_{k}^{2}\eta_{j}^{2}\right]

\displaystyle=\int_{k-1}^{k}\int_{k-1}^{k}\int_{j-1}^{j}\int_{j-1}^{j}\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}Y_{t}Y_{s}Y_{r}Y_{q}\;{\rm d}t{\rm d}s{\rm d}r{\rm d}q

\displaystyle=b^{4}\int_{k-1}^{k}\int_{k-1}^{k}\int_{j-1}^{j}\int_{j-1}^{j}e^{-a\left(t+s+r+q\right)}\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}I\left(t\right)I\left(s\right)I\left(r\right)I\left(q\right)\;{\rm d}t{\rm d}s{\rm d}r{\rm d}q.

Suppose that
j\leq k-1
. Then using the elementary properties of the stochastic
integral we can write

\displaystyle\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}I\left(t\right)I\left(s\right)I\left(r\right)I\left(q\right)

\displaystyle=\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\Bigl(I\left(r\right)I\left(q\right)\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\Bigl(I\left(t\right)I\left(s\right)|{\mathscr{F}}_{r\vee q}^{Y}\Bigr)\Bigr)

\displaystyle=\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\Bigl(I\left(r\right)I\left(q\right)\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\Bigl(I\left(s\wedge t\right)^{2}|{\mathscr{F}}_{r\vee q}^{Y}\Bigr)\Bigr)

\displaystyle=\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\Bigl(I\left(r\right)I\left(q\right)\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\Bigl(\left[I\left(s\wedge t\right)-I\left(r\vee q\right)+I\left(r\vee q\right)\right]^{2}|{\mathscr{F}}_{r\vee q}^{Y}\Bigr)\Bigr)

\displaystyle=\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\Bigl(I\left(r\right)I\left(q\right)\Bigl(\int_{r\vee q}^{s\wedge t}e^{2au}{\rm d}u+I\left(r\vee q\right)^{2}\Bigr)\Bigr)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{4a^{2}}\left[e^{2a\left(s\wedge t\right)}-e^{2a\left(r\vee q\right)}\right]e^{2a\left(r\wedge q\right)}+\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\Bigl(I\left(r\wedge q\right)I\left(r\vee q\right)^{3}\Bigr)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{4a^{2}}\left[e^{2a\left[\left(s\wedge t\right)+\left(r\wedge q\right)\right]}-e^{2a\left(r+q\right)}\right]+\frac{3}{4a^{2}}e^{2a\left(r+q\right)}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{4a^{2}}\left[e^{2a\left[\left(s\wedge t\right)+\left(r\wedge q\right)\right]}+2e^{2a\left(r+q\right)}\right].

Here we denoted
\sigma
-algebra
{\mathscr{F}}_{t}^{Y}=\sigma\left(Y_{s},s\leq t\right)
and used the equality

\displaystyle\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\Bigl(I\left(r\wedge q\right)I\left(r\vee q\right)^{3}\Bigr)=\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\Bigl(I\left(r\wedge q\right)\left[I\left(r\vee q\right)-I\left(r\wedge q\right)+I\left(r\wedge q\right)\right]^{3}\Bigr)

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad=3\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\Bigl(I\left(r\wedge q\right)\Bigr)^{2}\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\Bigl(\left[I\left(r\vee q\right)-I\left(r\wedge q\right)\right]^{2}\Bigr)+\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\Bigl(I\left(r\wedge q\right)\Bigr)^{4}

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad=\frac{3}{4a^{2}}e^{2a\left(r\wedge q\right)}\left[e^{2a\left(r\vee q\right)}-e^{2a\left(r\wedge q\right)}\right]+\frac{3}{4a^{2}}e^{4a\left(r\wedge q\right)}=\frac{3}{4a^{2}}e^{2a\left(r+q\right)}.

Therefore

\displaystyle e^{-a\left(t+s+r+q\right)}\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}I\left(t\right)I\left(s\right)I\left(r\right)I\left(q\right)=\frac{1}{4a^{2}}e^{-a\left|t-s\right|-a|r-q|}+\frac{1}{2a^{2}}e^{-a\left(t+s\right)}e^{a\left(r+q\right)}

and

\displaystyle\int_{k-1}^{k}\int_{k-1}^{k}\int_{j-1}^{j}\int_{j-1}^{j}e^{-a\left(t+s+r+q\right)}\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}I\left(t\right)I\left(s\right)I\left(r\right)I\left(q\right)\;{\rm d}t{\rm d}s{\rm d}r{\rm d}q

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad=\frac{1}{4a^{2}}\int_{k-1}^{k}\int_{k-1}^{k}\int_{j-1}^{j}\int_{j-1}^{j}e^{-a\left|t-s\right|-a|r-q|}\;{\rm d}t{\rm d}s{\rm d}r{\rm d}q

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad\qquad+\frac{1}{2a^{2}}\int_{k-1}^{k}\int_{k-1}^{k}\int_{j-1}^{j}\int_{j-1}^{j}e^{-a\left(t+s-r-q\right)}\;{\rm d}t{\rm d}s{\rm d}r{\rm d}q

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad=\frac{{1}}{{{a}^{6}}}\left[e^{-{a}}-1+{a}\right]^{2}+\frac{e^{2a}\left[1-e^{-a}\right]^{4}}{2a^{6}}\;e^{-2a\left|k-j\right|}.

Hence if
j\leq k-1
then

\displaystyle\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\left(\left[\eta_{k}^{2}-\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\eta_{k}^{2}\right]\left[\eta_{j}^{2}-\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\eta_{j}^{2}\right]\right)=\frac{b^{4}e^{2a}\left[1-e^{-a}\right]^{4}}{2a^{6}}\;e^{-2a\left|k-j\right|}\leq C\;e^{-2a\left|k-j\right|}.

If
k=j
then

\displaystyle\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\eta_{k}^{4}=3\left(\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\eta_{k}^{2}\right)^{2}=\frac{{3b^{4}}}{{{a}^{6}}}\left[e^{-{a}}-1+{a}\right]^{2}

and

\displaystyle\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\left[\eta_{k}^{2}-\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\eta_{k}^{2}\right]^{2}=\frac{{2b^{4}}}{{{a}^{6}}}\left[e^{-{a}}-1+{a}\right]^{2}.

Therefore

\displaystyle\frac{1}{T}\sum_{k=1}^{T}\sum_{j=1}^{T}\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\left(\left[\eta_{k}^{2}-\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\eta_{k}^{2}\right]\left[\eta_{j}^{2}-\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\eta_{j}^{2}\right]\right)

\displaystyle\qquad\quad\quad=\frac{{2b^{4}}}{{{a}^{6}}}\left[e^{-{a}}-1+{a}\right]^{2}+\frac{b^{4}e^{2a}\left[1-e^{-a}\right]^{4}}{2a^{6}T}\sum_{k=1}^{T}\sum_{j\not=k}^{T}e^{-2a\left|k-j\right|}

\displaystyle\qquad\quad\quad\longrightarrow\frac{{2b^{4}}}{{{a}^{6}}}\left[e^{-{a}}-1+{a}\right]^{2}+\frac{b^{4}e^{4a}\left[1-e^{-a}\right]^{3}}{a^{6}\left[1+e^{-a}\right]}.

Here we used the relations

\displaystyle\sum_{j=1,j\not=k}^{T}e^{-2a\left|k-j\right|}=\frac{2e^{2a}}{1-e^{-2a}}-\frac{e^{-2ak}+e^{-2a\left(T-k+1\right)}}{1-e^{-2a}}

and

\displaystyle\frac{\left[1-e^{-a}\right]^{4}}{\left(1-e^{-a}\right)\left(1+e^{-a}\right)}=\frac{\left[1-e^{-a}\right]^{3}}{\left(1+e^{-a}\right)}.

Therefore we obtained the estimate

\displaystyle\sup_{\vartheta\in\mathbb{K}}\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\Bigl(R_{1,T}-\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}R_{1,T}\Bigr)^{2}\leq\frac{C}{T}

and the convergence

\displaystyle{T}\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\Bigl(R_{1,T}-\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}R_{1,T}\Bigr)^{2}

\displaystyle\longrightarrow\frac{{2f^{4}b^{4}}}{{{a}^{6}}}\left[e^{-{a}}-1+{a}\right]^{2}+\frac{f^{4}b^{4}e^{4a}\left[1-e^{-a}\right]^{3}}{a^{6}\left[1+e^{-a}\right]}

\displaystyle\qquad+\frac{4f^{2}\sigma^{2}b^{2}}{a^{3}}\left[e^{-a}-1+a\right]+{2\sigma^{4}}=K_{1,1}\left(\vartheta\right).

The estimate (
20

inequality for time series. As the random variables

Change the variables:

Theorem 2
.

Suppose that the following conditions hold.

1.

The functions

\inf_{\vartheta\in\Theta}|\dot{\Psi}\left(\vartheta\right)|>0.

\inf_{\vartheta\in\Theta}\left(\left|\dot{a}\left(\vartheta\right)\right|+\left|\dot{r}\left(\vartheta\right)\right|\right)>0

Then One-step MLE-process
\vartheta_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}^{\star}\left(v\right),\varepsilon_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}<v\leq 1
with
\delta\in\left(1/2,1\right)
is uniformly
on compacts

\displaystyle\lim_{T\rightarrow\infty}\sup_{\vartheta_{0}\in\mathbb{K}}\mathbf{P}_{\vartheta_{0}}\left(\sup_{\varepsilon_{0}\leq v\leq 1}\left|\vartheta_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}^{\star}\left(v\right)-\vartheta_{0}\right|>\nu\right)=0,

asymptotically normal

\displaystyle\sqrt{T}\left(\vartheta_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}^{\star}\left(v\right)-\vartheta_{0}\right)\Longrightarrow\zeta_{v}\sim{\cal N}\left(0,v^{-1}{\rm I}\left(\vartheta_{0}\right)^{-1}\right),

and the moments converge: for any

\displaystyle T^{p/2}\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta_{0}}\left|\vartheta_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}^{\star}\left(v\right)-\vartheta_{0}\right|^{p}\longrightarrow\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta_{0}}\left|\zeta_{v}\right|^{p}.

The random process
\eta_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}\left(v\right)=v\sqrt{T{\rm I}\left(\vartheta_{0}\right)}\left(\vartheta_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}^{\star}\left(v\right)-\vartheta_{0}\right),\varepsilon_{0}\leq v\leq 1

) to the Wiener process

W\left(v\right),\varepsilon_{0}\leq v\leq 1
.

Proof.

Let us denote
{\rm I}_{m}=\inf_{\vartheta\in\Theta}{\rm I}\left(\vartheta\right)>0
, suppose that
\varepsilon_{T}<\varepsilon_{0}
and consider the
probability

\displaystyle\mathbf{P}_{\vartheta_{0}}\left(\sup_{\varepsilon_{0}\leq v\leq 1}\left|\vartheta_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}^{\star}\left(v\right)-\vartheta_{0}\right|>\nu\right)\leq\mathbf{P}_{\vartheta_{0}}\left(|\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}-\vartheta_{0}|>\frac{\nu}{3}\right)

\displaystyle\quad+\mathbf{P}_{\vartheta_{0}}\left(\frac{1}{{\rm I}_{m}T\left(\varepsilon_{0}-\varepsilon_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}\right)}\sup_{\varepsilon_{0}\leq v\leq 1}\left|\int_{\varepsilon_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}^{v}\frac{\dot{M}(\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}},uT)}{\sigma}{\rm d}\bar{W}_{uT}\right|\geq\frac{\nu}{3}\right)

\displaystyle\quad+\mathbf{P}_{\vartheta_{0}}\left(\frac{1}{{\rm I}_{m}\left(\varepsilon_{0}-\varepsilon_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}\right)}\int_{\varepsilon_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}^{1}\frac{\left|\dot{M}(\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}},uT)\left[M(\vartheta_{0},uT)-M(\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}},uT)\right]\right|}{\sigma^{2}}{\rm d}u\geq\frac{\nu}{3}\right).

For the first probability due to (
23
) we have

\displaystyle\mathbf{P}_{\vartheta_{0}}\left(\left|\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}-\vartheta_{0}\right|>\frac{\nu}{3}\right)\leq\frac{9}{\nu^{2}}\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta_{0}}|\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}-\vartheta_{0}|^{2}\leq\frac{C}{\nu^{2}\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}\rightarrow 0.

The second probability is estimated as follows

\displaystyle\mathbf{P}_{\vartheta_{0}}\left(\sup_{\varepsilon_{0}\leq v\leq 1}\left|\int_{\varepsilon_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}^{v}\frac{\dot{M}(\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}},uT)}{\sigma}{\rm d}\bar{W}_{uT}\right|\geq\frac{\nu{\rm I}_{m}T\left(\varepsilon_{0}-\varepsilon_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}\right)}{3}\right)

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\leq\frac{C}{\nu^{2}{\rm I}_{m}^{2}T\left(\varepsilon_{0}-\varepsilon_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}\right)^{2}}\int_{\varepsilon_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}^{1}\frac{\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta_{0}}\dot{M}(\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}},uT)^{2}}{\sigma^{2}}{\rm d}u

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\leq\frac{C}{\nu^{2}{\rm I}_{m}^{2}T\left(\varepsilon_{0}-\varepsilon_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}\right)^{2}}\longrightarrow 0.

Further

\displaystyle\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta_{0}}\left|\dot{M}(\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}},uT)\left[M\left(\vartheta_{0},uT\right)-M(\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}},uT)\right]\right|

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad=\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta_{0}}\left|\dot{M}(\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}},uT)\dot{M}\left(\bar{\vartheta}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}},uT\right)\left[\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}-\vartheta_{0}\right]\right|

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad\leq\left(\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta_{0}}\left|\dot{M}(\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}},uT)\dot{M}\left(\bar{\vartheta}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}},uT\right)\right|^{2}\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta_{0}}[\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}-\vartheta_{0}]^{2}\right)^{1/2}

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad\leq C\left(\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta_{0}}[\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}-\vartheta_{0}]^{2}\right)^{1/2}\leq CT^{-\delta/2}\rightarrow 0.

Therefore the last probability tends to zero as well. All estimates are
uniform on compacts.

The estimator
\vartheta_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}^{\star}\left(v\right)
has the representation

\displaystyle\sqrt{T}\left(\vartheta_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}^{\star}\left(v\right)-\vartheta_{0}\right)=\sqrt{T}\left(\vartheta_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}^{\star}\left(v\right)-\vartheta_{0}\right)+\frac{\sqrt{T}}{{\rm I}\bigl(\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}\bigr)\left({t-{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}}\right)}\int_{{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}}^{t}\frac{\dot{M}(\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}},s)}{\sigma}{\rm d}\bar{W}_{s}

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad+\frac{\sqrt{T}}{{\rm I}\bigl(\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}\bigr)\left({t-{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}}\right)}\int_{{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}}^{t}\frac{\dot{M}(\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}},s)}{\sigma^{2}}\left[M(\vartheta_{0},s)-M(\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}},s)\right]{\rm d}s

\displaystyle\qquad=\frac{1}{{\rm I}\bigl(\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}\bigr)\left({v-\varepsilon_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}\right)}\frac{1}{\sqrt{T}}\int_{{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}}^{t}\frac{\dot{M}(\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}},s)}{\sigma}{\rm d}\bar{W}_{s}

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad+\frac{\sqrt{T}\left(\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}-\vartheta_{0}\right)}{{\rm I}\bigl(\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}\bigr)}\left({\rm I}\bigl(\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}\bigr)-\frac{1}{\left({t-{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}}\right)}\int_{{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}}^{t}\frac{\dot{M}(\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}},s)\dot{M}(\bar{\vartheta}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}},s)}{\sigma^{2}}{\rm d}s\right).

The stochastic integral has the following asymptotics

\displaystyle\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta_{0}}\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{T}}\int_{{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}}^{t}\frac{\dot{M}(\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}},s)}{\sigma}{\rm d}\bar{W}_{s}-\frac{1}{\sqrt{T}}\int_{{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}}^{t}\frac{\dot{M}(\vartheta_{0},s)}{\sigma}{\rm d}\bar{W}_{s}\right)^{2}

\displaystyle\qquad=\frac{1}{{T}}\int_{{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}}^{t}\frac{\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta_{0}}\left[\dot{M}(\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}},s)-\dot{M}(\vartheta_{0},s)\right]^{2}}{\sigma^{2}}{\rm d}s\leq C\frac{t}{T}\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta_{0}}\left[\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}-\vartheta_{0}\right]^{2}\leq C\frac{t}{T}T^{-\delta}\rightarrow 0

and for a fixed
v

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\sqrt{T}}\int_{{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}}^{vT}\frac{\dot{M}(\vartheta_{0},s)}{\sigma}{\rm d}\bar{W}_{s}\Longrightarrow{\cal N}\left(0,v{\rm I}\left(\vartheta_{0}\right)\right)

because by the law of large numbers

\displaystyle\frac{1}{{T}}\int_{{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}}^{vT}\frac{\dot{M}(\vartheta_{0},s)^{2}}{\sigma^{2}}{\rm d}s\longrightarrow v{\rm I}\left(\vartheta_{0}\right).

Therefore

\displaystyle\frac{1}{{\rm I}\left(\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}\right)\left(v-\varepsilon_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}\right)\sqrt{T}}\int_{{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}}^{t}\frac{\dot{M}(\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}},s)}{\sigma}{\rm d}\bar{W}_{s}\Longrightarrow{\cal N}\left(0,v^{-1}{\rm I}\left(\vartheta_{0}\right)^{-1}\right).

(30)

For the last term we write

\displaystyle\int_{{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}}^{t}\frac{\dot{M}(\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}},s)\dot{M}(\bar{\vartheta}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}},s)}{\sigma^{2}}{\rm d}s

\displaystyle\qquad\quad=\int_{{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}}^{t}\frac{\dot{M}(\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}},s)^{2}}{\sigma^{2}}{\rm d}s+\left(\bar{\vartheta}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}-\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}\right)\int_{{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}}^{t}\frac{\dot{M}(\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}},s)\ddot{M}(\bar{\bar{\vartheta}}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}},s)}{\sigma^{2}}{\rm d}s,

where
\left|\bar{\vartheta}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}-\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}\right|\leq|\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}-\vartheta_{0}|
.
Hence

\displaystyle\frac{\sqrt{T}\left(\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}-\vartheta_{0}\right)}{{\rm I}\bigl(\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}\bigr)}\left({\rm I}\bigl(\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}\bigr)-\frac{1}{\left({t-{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}}\right)}\int_{{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}}^{t}\frac{\dot{M}(\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}},s)\dot{M}(\bar{\vartheta}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}},s)}{\sigma^{2}}{\rm d}s\right)

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad=\frac{\sqrt{T}\left(\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}-\vartheta_{0}\right)}{{\rm I}\bigl(\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}\bigr)}\left({\rm I}\bigl(\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}\bigr)-\frac{1}{\left({t-{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}}\right)}\int_{{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}}^{t}\frac{\dot{M}(\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}},s)^{2}}{\sigma^{2}}{\rm d}s\right)

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad\qquad+\frac{\sqrt{T}\left(\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}-\vartheta_{0}\right)\left(\bar{\vartheta}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}-\vartheta_{0}\right)}{{\rm I}\bigl(\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}\bigr)\left(t-\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}\right)}\int_{{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}}^{t}\frac{\dot{M}(\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}},s)\ddot{M}(\bar{\bar{\vartheta}}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}},s)}{\sigma^{2}}{\rm d}s.

\displaystyle\sqrt{T}\left|\left(\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}-\vartheta_{0}\right)\left(\bar{\vartheta}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}-\vartheta_{0}\right)\right|\leq\sqrt{T}\left|\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}-\vartheta_{0}\right|^{2}=O\left(T^{1/2-\delta}\right)\longrightarrow 0.

\displaystyle{\rm I}\bigl(\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}\bigr)-\frac{1}{\left({t-{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}}\right)}\int_{{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}}^{t}\frac{\dot{M}(\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}},s)^{2}}{\sigma^{2}}{\rm d}s={\rm I}\bigl(\vartheta_{0}\bigr)-\frac{1}{\left({t-{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}}\right)}\int_{{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}}^{t}\frac{\dot{M}(\vartheta_{0},s)^{2}}{\sigma^{2}}{\rm d}s+O\left(\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}-\vartheta_{0}\right).

\displaystyle\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta_{0}}\left(\frac{1}{\left({t-{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}}\right)}\int_{{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}}^{t}\left[\frac{\dot{M}(\vartheta_{0},s)^{2}}{\sigma^{2}}-{\rm I}\bigl(\vartheta_{0}\bigr)\right]{\rm d}s\right)^{2}\leq\frac{C}{t}

and we obtain

\displaystyle\frac{\sqrt{T}\left(\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}-\vartheta_{0}\right)}{{\rm I}\bigl(\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}\bigr)}\left({\rm I}\bigl(\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}\bigr)-\frac{1}{\left({t-{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}}\right)}\int_{{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}}}^{t}\frac{\dot{M}(\vartheta^{*}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}},s)\dot{M}(\bar{\vartheta}_{\tau_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}},s)}{\sigma^{2}}{\rm d}s\right)\longrightarrow 0.

This convergence and (

The convergence of moments follows from (
23
) and boundedness of all

.

It is proved that the process
\eta_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}\left(v\right),\varepsilon_{0},\leq v\leq 1

\displaystyle\eta_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}\left(v\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\sigma\sqrt{{\rm I}\left(\vartheta_{0}\right)T}}\int_{0}^{vT}\dot{M}\left(\vartheta_{0},s\right){\rm d}\bar{W}_{s}+h_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}\left(v\right),

\displaystyle\qquad=\frac{\lambda_{1}^{2}}{\sigma^{2}{{\rm I}\left(\vartheta_{0}\right)T}}\int_{0}^{v_{1}T}\dot{M}\left(\vartheta_{0},s\right)^{2}{\rm d}s+\frac{\lambda_{2}^{2}}{\sigma^{2}{{\rm I}\left(\vartheta_{0}\right)T}}\int_{0}^{v_{2}T}\dot{M}\left(\vartheta_{0},s\right)^{2}{\rm d}s

because

\displaystyle\langle\lambda,\eta_{\vphantom{\widetilde{T}}T}\rangle\Longrightarrow\langle\lambda,W\rangle=\lambda_{1}W\left(v_{1}\right)+\lambda_{2}W\left(v_{2}\right).

We have the same partially observed system

f\left(\cdot\right),a\left(\cdot\right),b\left(\cdot\right)

1
. The adaptive filter
is given by (
38
) and we would like to know if the error of
approximation
\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\left|m_{t,T}^{\star}-m\left(\vartheta,t\right)\right|^{2}
is asymptotically minimal? As before we propose a lower
minimax bound on the risks of all estimators
\bar{m}_{t}
supposing that their
calculation is based on the observations up to time
t
, i.e., these
estimators depend on
X^{t}=\left(X_{s},0\leq s\leq t\right)
.

By Theorem
1
the MLE and BE are consistent, asymptotically normal and
the polynomial moments converge uniformly on compacts
\mathbb{K}\subset\Theta
. Moreover, as it follows from Theorem 3.2.2 in
[
9
]
the BE admits the
representation similar to (
31
)

\displaystyle\sqrt{t}\left(\tilde{\vartheta}_{t}-\vartheta_{0}\right)=\frac{1}{\sigma{\rm I}\left(\vartheta_{0}\right)\sqrt{t}}\int_{0}^{t}\dot{M}\left(\vartheta_{0},s\right)\,{\rm d}\bar{W}_{s}+o\left(1\right).

(43)

Denote

\displaystyle S^{\star}\left(\vartheta_{0}\right)^{2}=K_{1}\left(\vartheta_{0}\right)^{2}+K_{2}\left(\vartheta_{0}\right)^{2}+R_{1,2}\left(\vartheta_{0}\right).

Theorem 4
.

Let the conditions of Theorem
1
be fulfilled. Then
we have the following lower minimax bound: for any estimator
\bar{m}_{t,T}
of

m\left(\vartheta,t\right)
(below
t=vT
)

\displaystyle\lim_{\nu\rightarrow 0}\mathop{\underline{\lim}}\limits_{T\rightarrow\infty}\sup_{\left|\vartheta-\vartheta_{0}\right|\leq\nu}t\,\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\left|\bar{m}_{t,T}-m\left(\vartheta,t\right)\right|^{2}\geq S^{\star}\left(\vartheta_{0}\right)^{2}.

Proof.

The given below proof is based on the proof of Theorem 1.9.1 in
[
9
]
.
We have the elementary estimate

\displaystyle\sup_{\left|\vartheta-\vartheta_{0}\right|\leq\nu}\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\left|\bar{m}_{t,T}-m\left(\vartheta,t\right)\right|^{2}\geq\int_{\vartheta_{0}-\nu}^{\vartheta_{0}+\nu}\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\left|\bar{m}_{t,T}-m\left(\vartheta,t\right)\right|^{2}p_{\nu}\left(\vartheta\right){\rm d}\vartheta.

Here the function
p_{\nu}\left(\vartheta\right),\vartheta_{0}-\nu<\vartheta<\vartheta_{0}+\nu
is a positive continuous density. If we denote

\tilde{m}_{t}
Bayesian estimator of
m\left(\vartheta,t\right)
, which
corresponds to this density
p_{\nu}\left(\cdot\right)
, then

\displaystyle\tilde{m}_{t}=\int_{\vartheta_{0}-\nu}^{\vartheta_{0}+\nu}m\left(\theta,t\right)p_{\nu}\left(\theta|X^{t}\right){\rm d}\theta,\qquad p_{\nu}\left(\theta|X^{t}\right)=\frac{p_{\nu}\left(\theta\right)L\left(\theta,X^{t}\right)}{\int_{\vartheta_{0}-\nu}^{\vartheta_{0}+\nu}p_{\nu}\left(\theta\right)L\left(\theta,X^{t}\right){\rm d}\theta}

and

\displaystyle\int_{\vartheta_{0}-\nu}^{\vartheta_{0}+\nu}\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\left|\bar{m}_{t,T}-m\left(\vartheta,t\right)\right|^{2}p_{\nu}\left(\vartheta\right){\rm d}\vartheta\geq\int_{\vartheta_{0}-\nu}^{\vartheta_{0}+\nu}\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\left|\tilde{m}_{t}-m\left(\vartheta,t\right)\right|^{2}p_{\nu}\left(\vartheta\right){\rm d}\vartheta.

The asymptotic behavior of BE
\tilde{m}_{t}
can be described as follows (below
\theta_{u}=\vartheta+\varphi_{t}u,\varphi_{t}=t^{-1/2},\mathbb{U}_{\nu}=\left(\sqrt{t}\left(\vartheta_{0}-\nu-\vartheta\right),\sqrt{t}\left(\vartheta_{0}+\nu-\vartheta\right)\right)
)

\displaystyle\tilde{m}_{t}

\displaystyle=\frac{\int_{\vartheta_{0}-\nu}^{\vartheta_{0}+\nu}m\left(\theta,t\right){p_{\nu}\left(\theta\right)L\left(\theta,X^{t}\right)}{\rm d}\theta}{\int_{\vartheta_{0}-\nu}^{\vartheta_{0}+\nu}p_{\nu}\left(\theta\right)L\left(\theta,X^{t}\right){\rm d}\theta}=\frac{\int_{\mathbb{U}_{\nu}}m\left(\theta_{u},t\right){p_{\nu}\left(\theta_{u}\right)L\left(\theta_{u},X^{t}\right)}{\rm d}u}{\int_{\mathbb{U}_{\nu}}p_{\nu}\left(\theta_{u}\right)L\left(\theta_{u},X^{t}\right){\rm d}u}

\displaystyle=m\left(\vartheta,t\right)+\varphi_{t}\dot{m}\left(\vartheta,t\right)\frac{\int_{\mathbb{U}_{\nu}}u{p_{\nu}\left(\theta_{u}\right)\frac{L\left(\theta_{u},X^{t}\right)}{L\left(\vartheta,X^{t}\right)}}{\rm d}u}{\int_{\mathbb{U}_{\nu}}p_{\nu}\left(\theta_{u}\right)\frac{L\left(\theta_{u},X^{t}\right)}{L\left(\vartheta,X^{t}\right)}{\rm d}u}\left(1+o\left(1\right)\right)

\displaystyle=m\left(\vartheta,t\right)+\varphi_{t}\dot{m}\left(\vartheta,t\right)\frac{\int_{\mathbb{U}_{\nu}}up_{\nu}\left(\vartheta\right)Z_{t}\left(u\right){\rm d}u}{\int_{\mathbb{U}_{\nu}}p_{\nu}\left(\vartheta\right)Z_{t}\left(u\right){\rm d}u}\left(1+o\left(1\right)\right).

Hence

\displaystyle\sqrt{t}\left(\tilde{m}_{t}-m\left(\vartheta,t\right)\right)

\displaystyle=\dot{m}\left(\vartheta,t\right)\frac{\int_{\mathbb{U}_{\nu}}uZ_{t}\left(u\right){\rm d}u}{\int_{\mathbb{U}_{\nu}}Z_{t}\left(u\right){\rm d}u}\left(1+o\left(1\right)\right)

(44)

\displaystyle=\dot{m}\left(\vartheta,t\right)\frac{\Delta_{t}\left(\vartheta,X^{t}\right)}{{\rm I}\left(\vartheta\right)}\left(1+o\left(1\right)\right).

(45)

Here

\displaystyle\Delta_{t}\left(\vartheta,X^{t}\right)=\frac{1}{\sigma\sqrt{t}}\int_{0}^{t}\dot{M}\left(\vartheta,s\right)\,{\rm d}\bar{W}_{s}\Longrightarrow\Delta\sim{\cal N}\left(0,{\rm I}\left(\vartheta\right)\right)

Recall that

\displaystyle Z_{t}\left(u\right)\Longrightarrow Z\left(u\right)=\exp\left(u\Delta\left(\vartheta\right)-\displaystyle\frac{u^{2}}{2}{\rm I}\left(\vartheta\right)\right),\qquad\frac{\int_{{\cal R}}uZ\left(u\right){\rm d}u}{\int_{{\cal R}}Z\left(u\right){\rm d}u}=\frac{\Delta\left(\vartheta\right)}{{\rm I}\left(\vartheta\right)}.

Moreover the uniform on compacts

\mathbb{K}\subset\left(\vartheta_{0}-\nu,\vartheta_{0}+\nu\right)
convergence of
moments

\displaystyle t\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\left(\tilde{m}_{t}-m\left(\vartheta,t\right)\right)^{2}\rightarrow\lim_{t\rightarrow\infty}t\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\left[\dot{m}\left(\vartheta,t\right)^{2}(\tilde{\vartheta}_{t}-\vartheta)^{2}\right]=S^{\star}\left(\vartheta\right)^{2}

(46)

The detailed proof of written above relations can be found in the proofs of

Therefore

\displaystyle t\int_{\vartheta_{0}-\nu}^{\vartheta_{0}+\nu}\mathbf{E}_{\vartheta}\left|\tilde{m}_{t}-m\left(\vartheta,t\right)\right|^{2}p_{\nu}\left(\vartheta\right){\rm d}\vartheta\longrightarrow\int_{\vartheta_{0}-\nu}^{\vartheta_{0}+\nu}S^{\star}\left(\vartheta\right)^{2}p_{\nu}\left(\vartheta\right){\rm d}\vartheta

and as

∎
