Abstract
. Let G G be a connected graph with vertex set V ⁡ ( G ) V(G) and edge set E ⁡ ( G ) E(G) . Pebbling shift is a deletion of two pebbles from a vertex and a placement of one pebble to a neighbouring vertex. The vertex cover set, D v ​ c D_{vc} for graph G G is the subset of V ⁡ ( G ) V(G) such that every edge in G G has minimum one end in D v ​ c D_{vc} . A detour monophonic path is considered to be a longest chordless path between two non adjacent vertices x x and y y . A detour monophonic vertex cover pebbling number, μ v ​ c ​ ( G ) , {\mu}_{vc}(G), is a minimum number of pebbles require to cover all the vertices of the vertex cover set of G G with at least one pebble each on them after the transformation of pebbles by using detour monophonic paths. We determine the detour monophonic vertex cover pebbling number (DMVCPN) of Cycle, Path, Fan and Wheel graphs. Keywords: detour monophonic pebbling, vertex cover set, detour monophonic vertex cover pebbling. AMS Subject Classification: 05C12, 05C25, 05C38, 05C76, 05C99
