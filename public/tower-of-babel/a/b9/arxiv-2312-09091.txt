Abstract
Nobody has discovered any perfect cuboid and there is no formula to deliver all possible Euler bricks. During investigations of famous open problems related to the perfect cuboid and Euler brick; I have found some new important conjectures on Pythagorean triples and biquadratic Diophantine equation [ 4 ] which are reduced & \& equivalence form for perfect cuboid and Euler brick problems. The details of the conjectures have been provided in Sections 2 - 3 . If any perfect cuboid (i.e. primitive) exists it will be only among the solutions of six conjectures and all the primitive Euler bricks are only among the solutions of next three conjectures [ 4 ] . For example, if any odd n ∈ ℕ n\in\mathbb{N} satisfy n = e 2 − f 2 = g 2 − h 2 = k 2 − l 2 n=e^{2}-f^{2}=g^{2}-h^{2}=k^{2}-l^{2} and e 2 ​ f 2 = g 2 ​ h 2 + k 2 ​ l 2 e^{2}f^{2}=g^{2}h^{2}+k^{2}l^{2} ; then we can discover a perfect cuboid of type 1 as { e 2 − f 2 , 2 ​ g ​ h , 2 ​ k ​ l , g 2 + h 2 , k 2 + l 2 , 2 ​ e ​ f , e 2 + f 2 } \{e^{2}-f^{2},2gh,2kl,g^{2}+h^{2},k^{2}+l^{2},2ef,e^{2}+f^{2}\} having ( e 2 − f 2 , 2 ​ g ​ h , 2 ​ k ​ l ) (e^{2}-f^{2},2gh,2kl) as its edges; ( g 2 + h 2 , k 2 + l 2 , 2 ​ e ​ f ) (g^{2}+h^{2},k^{2}+l^{2},2ef) as its face diagonals and e 2 + f 2 e^{2}+f^{2} as its body diagonal where e , f , g , h , k , l ( > 1 ) ∈ ℕ e,f,g,h,k,l~(>1)\in\mathbb{N} . Keywords: Pythagorean Triple Conjecture; Biquadratic Diophantine Equation Conjecture; Euler Brick; Perfect Cuboid; Number Theory Conjecture.

The research topic on perfect cuboid and Euler brick appeared several

research topic for a PhD theses in 2000 in Europe and 2004 in

because of its simplicity. As an example, This research topic appeared
in a journal run by undergraduates similar to HCMR

]

Noam Elkies told Oliver Knill

parametrizing Euler bricks is the intersection in

x^{2}+y^{2}=c^{2}
,
z^{2}+x^{2}=b^{2}
,
y^{2}+z^{2}=a^{2}
which happens to be a
K3

surface of maximal rank, so quite closely related to much of Knill’s
own recent work in number theory. Adding the condition

have no nontrivial rational points but nobody knows how to prove such
a thing.” Noam also remarked that

parametrization would only lead to a finite number of perfect Cuboids
as a consequence of Mordells theorem. There seems however no reason to
be known which would tell whether there are maximally finitely many
primitive perfect cuboids”.

Conclusion

All the perfect cuboids (if any) belong to the the solutions of first
six conjectures (and hence consequently to the the solutions of last
three conjectures) and all the Euler bricks can be generated from the
the solutions of the last three conjectures
[
4
]
. Also,
perfect cuboid (if any) can be discovered from these three types of
Euler bricks only. Moreover, all the Euler bricks (body cuboid);
available in Table given by Maurice Kraitchik, Rathbun and Granlund as
well as Rathbun
[
24
,
25
,

]
; can be classified as
the Euler bricks first type, Euler bricks second type and Euler bricks
third type. In the range of odd edge less than 1000, there are 11
primitive Euler bricks. These are 8 Euler bricks second type, 3 Euler
bricks third type and no Euler bricks of first type.
