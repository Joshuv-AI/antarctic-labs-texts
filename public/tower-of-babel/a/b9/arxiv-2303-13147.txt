Estimates for the Kolmogorov widths of an intersection of two balls in a mixed norm
A. A. Vasil’eva

Abstract
Order estimates for the Kolmogorov widths of an intersection of two finite‐dimensional balls in a mixed norm under some conditions on the parameters are obtained.



1
Introduction

In this paper, a problem of order estimates for the Kolmogorov widths of an intersection of two finite‐dimensional balls in a mixed norm is studied.

First we give necessary definitions and notation.

Let
m,\,k\in\mathbb{N}
,
1\leqslant p<\infty
,
1\leqslant\theta<\infty
. By
l_{p,\theta}^{m,k}
we denote the space
\mathbb{R}^{mk}
with the norm

\|(x_{i,j})_{1\leqslant i\leqslant m,\,1\leqslant j\leqslant k}\|_{l_{p,\theta}^{m,k}}=\left(\sum\limits_{j=1}^{k}\left(\sum\limits_{i=1}^{m}|x_{i,j}|^{p}\right)^{\theta/p}\right)^{1/\theta}.

For
p=\infty
or
\theta=\infty
, the definition is modified naturally.

By
B_{p,\theta}^{m,k}
we denote the unit ball of the space
l_{p,\theta}^{m,k}
. If
k=1
, we write
l_{p}^{m}:=l_{p,\theta}^{m,1}
and
B_{p}^{m}:=B_{p,\theta}^{m,1}
.

Let
X
be a normed space, and let
M\subset X
,
n\in\mathbb{Z}_{+}
. The Kolmogorov
n
-widts of the set
M
in the space
X
is defined as follows:

d_{n}(M,\,X)=\inf_{L\in{\cal L}_{n}(X)}\sup_{x\in M}\inf_{y\in L}\|x-y\|;

here
{\cal L}_{n}(X)
is the family of all subspaces in
X

with dimension at most
n
. For details, see, e.g.,
[
2
,
3
,
1
]
.

Exact values of the widths
d_{n}(B_{p}^{m},\,l_{q}^{m})

(for

Naturally arises the question of estimating the Kolmogorov widths of an intersection of finite‐dimension balls in a mixed norm. The result can be employed in estimating the widths of an intersection of weighted Besov classes or Besov classes with dominating mixed smoothness. Here we consider the case of two balls

Given sets

f_{1}

f_{2}:\ X\times Y\rightarrow\mathbb{R}_{+}

f_{1}(x,\,y)\underset{y}{\lesssim}f_{2}(x,\,y)
(or

for all

if
f_{1}(x,\,y)\underset{y}{\lesssim}f_{2}(x,\,y)
and
f_{2}(x,\,y)\underset{y}{\lesssim}f_{1}(x,\,y)
.

.

(see

•

(1)

•

if

\displaystyle d_{n}(B^{m,k}_{p,\theta},\,l_{q,\sigma}^{m,k})\underset{q,\sigma}{\asymp}\begin{cases}1,&n\leqslant m^{\frac{2}{q}}k^{\frac{2}{\sigma}},\\
\left(n^{‐\frac{1}{2}}m^{\frac{1}{q}}k^{\frac{1}{\sigma}}\right)^{\lambda_{p,q}},&m^{\frac{2}{q}}k^{\frac{2}{\sigma}}\leqslant n\leqslant mk^{\frac{2}{\sigma}},\\

(2)

•

if
\max\{p,\,\theta\}\geqslant 2

\displaystyle d_{n}(B^{m,k}_{p,\theta},\,l_{q,\sigma}^{m,k})\underset{q,\sigma}{\asymp}\begin{cases}1,&n\leqslant m^{\frac{2}{q}}k^{\frac{2}{\sigma}},\\
\left(n^{‐\frac{1}{2}}m^{\frac{1}{q}}k^{\frac{1}{\sigma}}\right)^{\lambda_{\theta,\sigma}},&m^{\frac{2}{q}}k^{\frac{2}{\sigma}}\leqslant n\leqslant km^{\frac{2}{q}},\\

(3)

Theorem 1
.

Let
m
,
k\in\mathbb{N}
,
n\in\mathbb{Z}_{+}
,
n\leqslant\frac{mk}{2}
,
2\leqslant q<\infty
,
2\leqslant\sigma<\infty
,
1\leqslant p_{i}\leqslant q
,
1\leqslant\theta_{i}\leqslant\sigma
,
\nu_{i}>0
,
i=1,\,2
. We define the values
\Phi_{j}(m,\,k,\,n)=\Phi_{j}(m,\,k,\,n;\,p_{1},\,p_{2},\,\theta_{1},\,\theta_{2},\,q,\,\sigma,\,\nu_{1},\,\nu_{2})
(
j=1,\,\dots,\,5
) as follows:

1.

\Phi_{j}(m,\,k,\,n)=\nu_{j}d_{n}(B^{m,k}_{p_{j},\theta_{j}},\,l^{m,k}_{q,\sigma})
for
j=1,\,2
;

2.

if there exists
\tilde{\lambda}\in[0,\,1]
such that
\frac{1}{2}=\frac{1‐\tilde{\lambda}}{p_{1}}+\frac{\tilde{\lambda}}{p_{2}}
, we define the number
\tilde{\theta}
by
\frac{1}{\tilde{\theta}}=\frac{1‐\tilde{\lambda}}{\theta_{1}}+\frac{\tilde{\lambda}}{\theta_{2}}
and set
\Phi_{3}(m,\,k,\,n)=\nu_{1}^{1‐\tilde{\lambda}}\nu_{2}^{\tilde{\lambda}}d_{n}(B^{m,k}_{2,\tilde{\theta}},\,l^{m,k}_{q,\sigma})
; otherwise, we set
\Phi_{3}(m,\,k,\,n)=+\infty
;

3.

if there exists
\tilde{\mu}\in[0,\,1]
such that
\frac{1}{2}=\frac{1‐\tilde{\mu}}{\theta_{1}}+\frac{\tilde{\mu}}{\theta_{2}}

\tilde{p}

\frac{1}{\tilde{p}}=\frac{1‐\tilde{\mu}}{p_{1}}+\frac{\tilde{\mu}}{p_{2}}

\Phi_{4}(m,\,k,\,n)=\nu_{1}^{1‐\tilde{\mu}}\nu_{2}^{\tilde{\mu}}d_{n}(B^{m,k}_{\tilde{p},2},\,l^{m,k}_{q,\sigma})

if there exist

Then

d_{n}(\nu_{1}B^{m,k}_{p_{1},\theta_{1}}\cap\nu_{2}B^{m,k}_{p_{2},\theta_{2}},\,l^{m,k}_{q,\sigma})\underset{q,\sigma}{\asymp}\min_{1\leqslant j\leqslant 5}\Phi_{j}(m,\,k,\,n).
