Lemma 2.8
.

Suppose that
G^{\prime}\subset G
is a finite index open subgroup. Then a

Proof.

(1)

If

Conversely, for any compact open subgroup

\mathrm{res}^{G}_{G^{\prime}}\operatorname{ind}_{K}^{G}W\cong\bigoplus_{g\in G^{\prime}\backslash G/K}\operatorname{ind}_{gKg^{-1}\cap G^{\prime}}^{G^{\prime}}W^{g}.

So

is finitely generated — in fact finitely presented — as a representation of

. It follows that

is finitely generated as a representation of

.

(2)

We showed in (1) that

is finitely presented as a representation of

for any finite rank

of a compact open subgroup

. It follows from Lemma

(1) that any

.

Conversely, suppose that

is finitely presented as a representation of

. By the first part, it is finitely

\operatorname{ind}_{K}^{G}W\rightarrow V
. Since the first term is
