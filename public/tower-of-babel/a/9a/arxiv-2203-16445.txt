Holographic Entanglement Entropy in T ​ T ¯ T\bar{T} -deformed CFTs
M. R. Setare, S. N. Sajadi

Abstract
In this work, we study the holographic entanglement entropy of two dimensional T ​ T ¯ T\bar{T} -deformed conformal field theory. We compute the correction due to the deformation up to the leading order of the deformation parameter in the framework of New massive gravity, General minimal massive gravity, and Exotic general massive gravity. We also apply Song’s prescription to obtain the entanglement entropy for deformed theories. In each cases, we find agreement between the results.

Entanglement Entropy for NMG

Among massive gravity models in three dimensions, a well known one is that of the new massive gravity. This model is equivalent to the three-dimensional Fierz-Pauli action for a massive spin
-2
field at the linearized level. In addition, NMG preserves parity symmetry which is not the case for the topological massive gravity (TMG).
The action of NMG is presented as follows

S_{NMG}=\dfrac{1}{8\pi G}\int d^{3}x\sqrt{-g}\left[R-2\lambda-\dfrac{1}{m^{2}}(R^{\mu\nu}R_{\mu\nu}-\dfrac{3}{8}R^{2})\right]

(2)

\displaystyle N_{\mu\nu}=-\dfrac{1}{2}\nabla^{2}Rg_{\mu\nu}-\dfrac{1}{2}\nabla_{\mu}\nabla_{\nu}R+2\nabla^{2}R_{\mu\nu}+4R_{\mu a\nu b}R^{ab}-\dfrac{3}{2}RR_{\mu\nu}-R_{\alpha\beta}R^{\alpha\beta}g_{\mu\nu}+\dfrac{3}{8}R^{2}g_{\mu\nu},

(3)

To obtain the renormalized action we should add the generalized Gibbons-Hawking boundary term for NMG and the counter-term to the action (

(4)

where

\displaystyle K_{\mu\nu}

\displaystyle=-\dfrac{1}{2}(n_{\mu;\nu}+n_{\nu;\mu}),\;\gamma^{\mu\nu}=g^{\mu\nu}-n^{\mu}n^{\nu},\;\hat{f}^{ab}=f^{\mu\nu}\gamma_{\mu}{}^{a}\gamma_{\nu}{}^{b}\;f_{\mu\nu}=\dfrac{2}{m^{2}}\left(R_{\mu\nu}-\dfrac{1}{4}Rg_{\mu\nu}\right).
