Normal subgroups of iterated wreath products of symmetric groups and alternating with symmetric groups
Ruslan V. Skuratovskii

Abstract
Normal subgroups and there properties for finite and infinite iterated wreath products S n 1 ≀ … ≀ S n m S_{n_{1}}\wr\ldots\wr S_{n_{m}} , n , m ∈ ℕ n,m\in\mathbb{N} and A n ≀ S n A_{n}\wr S_{n} are founded. The special classes of normal subgroups and there orders are investigated. Special classes of normal subgroups are investigated and their generators are found and presented in the form of Kaloujnine tables. Further, the monolith (i.e. the unique minimal normal subgroup) of these wreath products is investigated. Inverse limit of wreath product of permutation groups is found. Key words : normal subgroups, iterated wreath product of symmetric groups, normal subgroups of wreath product of symmetric group and alternating group. MSC : 20D10, 20F05, 20B05, 20B25, 20B22, 20B07, 20E08, 20E28, 20B35.
