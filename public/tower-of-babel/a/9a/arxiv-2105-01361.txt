Kerr-Schild Form of the Exact Metric for a Constantly Moving Kerr Black Hole and Null Gravitational Deflection
WEIJUN LI, ZHONGWEN FENG, XIA ZHOU, XUELING MU, GUANSHENG HE

Abstract
The exact metric of a moving Kerr black hole with an arbitrary constant velocity is derived in Kerr-Schild coordinates. We then calculate the null equatorial gravitational deflection caused by a radially moving Kerr source up to the second post-Minkowskian order, acting as an application of the weak field limit of the metric. The bending angle of light is found to be consistent with the result given in the previous works.

We adopt the Lorentz boosting technique

(t,~x,~y,~z)

The Kerr-Schild form of the Kerr metric in the comoving frame

\displaystyle ds^{2}=-\,dT^{2}+dX^{2}+dY^{2}+dZ^{2}+\frac{2MR^{3}}{R^{4}+a^{2}Z^{2}}

\displaystyle\hskip 28.0pt\times\left[dT+\frac{ZdZ}{R}+\frac{R\,(XdX+YdY)-a\,(XdY-YdX)}{R^{2}+a^{2}}\right]^{2},

(1)

where
\frac{X^{2}+Y^{2}}{R^{2}+a^{2}}+\frac{Z^{2}}{R^{2}}=1
, and

(2)

\Lambda^{\mu}_{\nu}=\left(\begin{array}[]{cccc}\gamma&-v_{1}\gamma&-v_{2}\gamma&-v_{3}\gamma\\

4
Summary

In this paper, we have worked out the exact metric of a moving Kerr black hole with an arbitrary constant velocity in Kerr-Schild coordinates via the Lorentz boosting technique. As an application of the weak field limit of this solution, the null gravitational deflection in the equatorial plane of a radially moving Kerr black hole up to the second post-Minkowskian order has been computed, in accordance with the result presented in the previous works. Considering the commonness of the relative motion of the source of light, the observer, and the deflector, this new solution of the Einstein field equations might be helpful to study the strong- and weak-field physics of a moving gravitational system.
