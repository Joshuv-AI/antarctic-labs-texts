Muon g − 2 g-2 in U ​ ( 1 ) μ − τ U(1)_{\mu-\tau} Symmetric Gauged Radiative Neutrino Mass Model Preprint: KIAS-P21026, APCTP Pre2021 - 016
Dong Woo Kang, Jongkuk Kim, Hiroshi Okada

Abstract
We explore muon anomalous magnetic moment (muon g − 2 g-2 ) in a scotogenic neutrino model with a gauged lepton number symmetry U ​ ( 1 ) μ − τ U(1)_{\mu-\tau} . In this model, a dominant muon g − 2 g-2 contribution comes not from an additional gauge sector but from a Yukawa sector. In our numerical Δ ​ χ 2 \Delta\chi^{2} analysis, we show that our model is in favor of normal hierarchy with some features. We demonstrate one benchmark point, satisfying muon g − 2 g-2 at the best fit value 25.1 × 10 − 10 25.1\times 10^{-10} .

We have proposed a gauged

neutrino mass model which can address the muon
g-2
through the Yukawa sector without relying on the new gauge sector.
The neutrino mass matrix is induced at one-loop level and participated in five neutral fermions run inside the loop.
Through our numerical
\Delta\chi^{2}
