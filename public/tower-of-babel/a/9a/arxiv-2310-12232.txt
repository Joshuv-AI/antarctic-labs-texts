On integrability of the one-dimensional Hubbard model
A. Melikyan

Abstract
We find a family of solutions to Zamolodchikov’s tetrahedral algebra corresponding to the fermionic R R -operator for the free fermion model of the difference type in one of the spectral parameters, construct an extension of the R R -operator for a system of two spins satisfying the Yang-Baxter equation, and find the local charges. We also construct a twisted monodromy operator, which leads to the one-dimensional Hubbard model.
