Additive approximation algorithm for geodesic centers in δ \delta -hyperbolic graphs
Dibyayan Chakraborty, Yann Vaxès

Abstract
For an integer k ≥ 1 k\geq 1 , the objective of k k -Geodesic Center is to find a set 𝒞 \mathcal{C} of k k isometric paths such that the maximum distance between any vertex v v and 𝒞 \mathcal{C} is minimised. Introduced by Gromov, δ \delta -hyperbolicity measures how treelike a graph is from a metric point of view. Our main contribution in this paper is to provide an additive O ⁡ ( δ ) O(\delta) -approximation algorithm for k k -Geodesic Center on δ \delta -hyperbolic graphs. On the way, we define a coarse version of the pairing property introduced by Gerstel & Zaks (Networks, 1994) and show it holds for δ \delta -hyperbolic graphs. This result allows to reduce the k k -Geodesic Center problem to its rooted counterpart, a main idea behind our algorithm. We also adapt a technique of Dragan & Leitert, (TCS, 2017) to show that for every k ≥ 1 k\geq 1 , k k - Geodesic Center is NP-hard even on partial grids.

In case of trees, the same algorithmic approach leads to an exact polynomial time algorithm. Indeed, since trees are
0
-hyperbolic, Lemma
13
implies that Algorithm
2
computes a rooted
(r,R)
-cover
\mathcal{C}
of size
2k-1
such that there is no
(r^{\prime},R^{\prime})
-cover with
R^{\prime}<R.
By Lemma
12
, an optimal

-cover of size

can be transformed into a rooted

-cover of size

Hence,

Since trees satisfy the pairing property, any

-cover of size

Hence, in case of trees,

NP-hardness for partial grids

In this section we prove
theorem

2
. Our proof is an adaptation of the NP-hardness of

-

on planar bipartite graphs proved by Dragan & Leitert (Corollary 8,

]

Lemma 16
.

Let
G
be a graph and
H=G_{\ell}
for some
\ell\geq 1
. Then for integers
m,k
, if
G
has an
m
-cover of size
k
then
H
has an
\left(m\ell+\left\lfloor\ell/2\right\rfloor\right)
-cover of size
k
.

Proof.

Let
\mathcal{C}
be an
m
-cover of
G
of size
k
and
\mathcal{C}^{\prime}
be a set of paths in
H
which are
\ell
-subdivision of the paths in
\mathcal{C}
. Clearly, all paths in
\mathcal{C}^{\prime}
consists of isometric paths in
H
. Let
u
be an original vertex of
H
and
P\in\mathcal{C}
be a path such that
d(u,V(P))\leq m
in
G
. Let
P^{\prime}\in\mathcal{C}^{\prime}
be the
\ell
-subdivision of
P
. Clearly,
d(u,V(P^{\prime}))\leq m\ell
. Now consider a vertex
u\in V(H)
which is not a vertex of
G
. Hence there exists an original vertex
u^{\prime}\in V(H)
with
d(u,u^{\prime})\leq\left\lfloor\ell/2\right\rfloor
. Let
P\in\mathcal{C}
be a path such that
d(u^{\prime},V(P))\leq m
in
G
and
P^{\prime}\in\mathcal{C}^{\prime}
be the
\ell
-subdivision of
P
. Then
d(u,V(P^{\prime}))\leq d(u,u^{\prime})+d(u^{\prime},V(P^{\prime}))\leq m\ell+\left\lfloor\ell/2\right\rfloor
.
∎

Let
G
be a graph and
H=G_{\ell}
for some
\ell\geq 1
. For an isometric path
P
of
H
between two original vertices

, let

denote the
(u,v)
-isometric path in

such that
P
is an
\ell
-subdivision of
G(P)
. Intuitively,

is the original isometric path whose

-subdivision created

in

.

Lemma 17
.

Let
G
be a graph and let
H=G_{\ell}
for some
\ell\geq 1
. Let

be an isometric path of
H
between two original vertices

. Let

be an original vertex of

such that
d(w,V(P))<(r+1)\ell

Proof.

Let
w^{\prime}\in V(P)
be a vertex which is closest to

in
H
and
P^{\prime}
be an
(w,w^{\prime})
-isometric path in

. Clearly,

is an original vertex and therefore

exists. Observe that, the number of original vertices in

is at most

. (Otherwise length of

is at least

in

is at most

in

.
∎

[

reduced the NP-complete

[

]
to show that

has an isometric path with eccentricity at most

there are two special cut vertices

of

To prove our result, we modify the graph

6
Conclusion

Constant factor approximability of
k
-
Geodesic Center
in general graphs for
k\geq 2
remains open. From both application and theoretical point of views, approximability of
k
-
Geodesic Center
on planar graphs is an important open question. An approximation scheme even for
MESP
on planar graphs is open. Studying whether
k
-
Geodesic Center
(for arbitrary
k
) is fixed-parameter tractable with respect to tree-width or hyperbolicity are interesting research questions. Polynomial solvability of
k
-
Geodesic Center
in superclasses of trees (e.g. outerplanar graphs), solid grids (i.e. partial grids where internal faces have unit area), are open as well.
For graphs with bounded isometric path complexity (including hyperbolic graphs),
IPC
admits a constant factor approximation algorithm
[
10
]
. It would be interesting to explore if
k
-
Geodesic Center
admits an additive approximation algorithm on graphs with bounded isometric path complexity? Finally, investigating the approximability or fixed-parameter tractability of
k
-
Geodesic Center
on weighted graphs are interesting directions.
