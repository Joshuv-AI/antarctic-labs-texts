1.
Introduction and statements of results

Let
f(x)\in{\mathbf{Z}}[x]
be a monic irreducible polynomial of degree
n
over the field
{\mathbf{Q}}
of rational numbers. Let
K={\mathbf{Q}}(\theta)
be an algebraic number field with
\theta
a root of

and
O_{K}
be its ring of algebraic integers. We denote the index of the subgroup
{\mathbf{Z}}[\gamma]
in

by
\mathop{\mathrm{ind}}\gamma
. An algebraic number field is called monogenic if
\mathop{\mathrm{ind}}\alpha=1
for some
\alpha\in O_{K}
, i.e.,
O_{K}={\mathbf{Z}}[\alpha]
. In this case
\{1,\alpha,\cdots,\alpha^{n-1}\}
is an integral basis of
K
; such an integral basis of
K
is called a power integral basis of
K
. If there does not exist any
\alpha\in O_{K}
for which
\mathop{\mathrm{ind}}\alpha=1
, then
K
is non-monogenic. One of the fundamental problems in algebraic number theory is the determination of the monogeneity of an algebraic number field. The problem of testing the monogeneity of number
fields and constructing power integral bases have been intensively studied (cf.
[
1
]
,
[
2
]
,
[
5
]
,
[
6
]
,
[
11
]
,
[
15
]
). In
1984,
Funakura
[
5
]
gave necessary and sufficient conditions on those integers
m
for which the number field
{\mathbf{Q}}(m^{1/4})
is monogenic.
In
2017
, Gaál and Remete
[
6
]
studied monogenity of algebraic number fields of the type
{\mathbf{Q}}(m^{1/n}),
where
3\leq n\leq 9
and
m
is square free.
In
[
7
]
, Gaál studied monogenity of number fields defined by some sextic irreducible trinomials. For a rational prime
p
, if the prime ideal factorization of an ideal
pO_{K}
is known, then Engstorm
[
4
]
determined the highest power of
p
dividing the index
i(K)
of the field
K
of degree less than or equal to
7.

In this paper, let
p
be a rational prime and
K={\mathbf{Q}}(\theta)
be an algebraic number field with
\theta
a root of an irreducible quadrinomial
f(x)=x^{6}+ax^{m}+bx+c\in{\mathbf{Z}}[x]
with
m\in\{2,3,4,5\},
then we provide some sufficient conditions involving only
a,~b,~c
and
m
for which
K
is non-monogenic. Also, we provide the highest power of
p
dividing the index of the field
K.

As an application of our results, we provide a family of algebraic number fields which are non-monogenic.

In what follows, for any integer
r
and a rational prime
p,

v_{p}(r)
will denote the highest power of
p
dividing r. Also,
i(K)
will stand for the index of the field

defined by

Note that if for some prime

then the field
K
is non-monogenic.

Precisely, we prove the following result.

Theorem 1.1
.

Let
K={\mathbf{Q}}(\theta)

Corollary 1.2

Let

f(x)

{\mathbf{Z}}[x]

O_{K}

Definition 2.1

The Gauss valuation of the field
{\mathbf{Q}}_{p}(x)
of rational functions in an indeterminate

{\mathbf{Q}}_{p}
and is defined on
{\mathbf{Q}}_{p}[x]

v_{p,x}(a_{0}+a_{1}x+a_{2}x^{2}+.....+a_{s}x^{s})=\displaystyle\min\{v_{p}(a_{i}),1\leq i\leq s\},a_{i}\in{\mathbf{Q}}_{p}.

Definition 2.2
.

Let
p
be a rational prime. Let
\phi(x)\in{\mathbf{Z}}_{p}[x]
be a monic polynomial which is irreducible modulo
p
and
f(x)\in{\mathbf{Z}}_{p}[x]
be a monic polynomial not divisible by
\phi(x)
. Let
\displaystyle\sum_{i=0}^{n}a_{i}(x)\phi(x)^{i}

\deg a_{i}(x)<\deg\phi(x)

\phi(x)
-expansion of

obtained on dividing it by the successive powers of
\phi(x)
. For each non-zero term

and form the set

S=\{(i,~v_{p,x}(a_{n-i}(x)))\mid 0\leq i\leq n,~a_{n-i}(x)\neq 0\}.

The
\phi
-Newton polygon of

with respect to
p
is the polygonal path formed by the lower edges along the convex hull of the points of set
S
. The slopes of the edges form a strictly increasing sequence; these slopes are non-negative as

is a monic polynomial with coefficients in
{\mathbf{Z}}_{p}
.

Definition 2.3
.

Let
\phi(x)\in{\mathbf{Z}}_{p}[x]
be a monic polynomial which is irreducible modulo a rational prime
p
having a root
\alpha
in the algebraic closure
\widetilde{{\mathbf{Q}}}_{p}
of
{\mathbf{Q}}_{p}
. Let

with
\phi(x)
-expansion
\phi(x)^{n}+a_{n-1}(x)\phi(x)^{n-1}+\cdots+a_{0}(x)
such that
\overline{f}(x)
is a power of
\overline{\phi}(x)
. Suppose that the
\phi
-Newton polygon of

consists of a single edge, say
S
, having positive slope denoted by
\frac{l}{e}
with

coprime, i.e.,

\min\bigg\{\frac{v_{p,x}(a_{n-i}(x))}{i}~\mid~1\leq i\leq n\bigg\}=\frac{v_{p,x}(a_{0}(x))}{n}=\frac{l}{e}

so that

and

has coefficients in
{\mathbf{Z}}_{p}
and hence

with respect to
