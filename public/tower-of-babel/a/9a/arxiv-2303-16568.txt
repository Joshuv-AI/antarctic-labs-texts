Quark mixing angles and weak CP-violating phase vs quark masses: potential approach
1

Abstract




Abstract:

It is shown that following experimentally viable expressions for quark mixing angles
\theta_{12}
,
\theta_{23}
,
\theta_{13}
and CP-violating phase
\delta
:
\sin\theta_{12}=\sqrt{m_{d}/|m_{s}|}
,
\sin\theta_{23}=2\,|m_{s}|/m_{b}
,
\sin\theta_{13}\approx 2\,m_{d}/m_{b}
,
\tan\delta=m_{b}^{2}\,m_{c}/6\,m_{t}\,m_{s}^{2}

may be derived as stable points of certain 4-th power in
V_{CKM}
flavor-invariant potentials built with traces of 3x3 quark up and down mass matrices and the Jarlskog invariant. There is no fine-tuning, potentials’ dimensionless constants are the integer numbers not above 10.

PACS numbers: 12.15.Ff

Keywords: Standard Model, quark sector, CKM matrix
