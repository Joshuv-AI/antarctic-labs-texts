Determination of the Gluonic D-term and Mechanical Radii of Proton from Experimental data
Wei Kou, Rong Wang, Xurong Chen

Abstract
We apply a “color” tripole ansatz for describing the D D -term of the proton. By fitting the experimental data of the vector meson J/ ψ \psi and ϕ \phi photoproductions near the thresholds, we firstly obtained the gluonic D D -term of the proton. D g ​ ( 0 ) D_{g}(0) is estimated to be − 2.16 ± 0.42 -2.16\pm 0.42 for J/ ψ \psi and − 1.31 ± 0.48 -1.31\pm 0.48 for ϕ \phi , and the mechanical root mean square radius of proton is estimated to be 0.61 ± 0.29 0.61\pm 0.29 fm for ϕ \phi and 0.42 ± 0.11 0.42\pm 0.11 fm for J/ ψ \psi .



I
Introduction

Recently in the study of hadron physics, the mechanical properties and
the quantum chromodynamics (QCD)
Gross and Wilczek 1973a
;
Gross and Wilczek 1973b
;
Gross and Wilczek 1974
structure of the proton
are among the hot topics under discussions.
The proton is composed of quarks and gluons according to
the quark model and the modern QCD theory.
Although one can break the atomic nucleus to get the nucleons inside,
we cannot observe the individual quarks and gluons by breaking up the nucleons.
This is because of the color confinement of QCD theory.
Nevertheless the proton structure can be revealed
in the asymptotic region under high energy transfer.
There are some theoretical ways to envisage the internal structure of the proton.
The QCD factorization theory indicates that the scattering amplitudes
can be expressed in terms of the generalized parton distribution (GPD)
Ji 1997a
;
Müller et al. 1994
;
Radyushkin 1996

in the high-
Q^{2}
region. Although less rigorous, there are some phenomenal models
which also successfully explain parts of physical processes.

The basic mechanical properties of proton can be deduced from
the gravitational form factors (GFFs) of Energy-Momentum-Tensor (EMT)
Ji 1997b
;
Pagels 1966
;
Belitsky and Radyushkin 2005
.
Matrix elements of EMT contain basic mechanical properties such as mass and angular momentum.
Over the past two decades people have gradually understood the origins of the proton mass and spin
within the framework of quantum field theory and from the prior experimental studies.
But much less is known about the
D
-term of the proton,
which is one of the GFFs that reflects the shear force and the pressure inside the proton
Polyakov 2003
;
Polyakov and Schweitzer 2018
.
The old fashion models on the
D
-term were performed in the bag model
Ji et al. 1997

and the chiral quark soliton model (
\chi
QSM)
Petrov et al. 1998
.
In 2018 V. Burkert, et. al.
Burkert et al. 2018
adopted GPD measurement
from deeply virtual Compton scattering (DVCS)
Ji 1997b
;
Ji 1997a

to firstly give a direct mapping of the pressure distribution
inside proton with the extracted
D
-term
D_{q}(0)
of quark.
In the following year, P. Shanahan and W. Detmold gave the gluonic and total
D
-term
by Lattice QCD (LQCD) calculation
Shanahan and Detmold 2019a
;
Shanahan and Detmold 2019b
.
Hatta et. al. have analyzed the
D
-term
by studying the photoproduction process of heavy quarkomium
from the holographic QCD with Operator Product Expansion theory
Hatta and Yang 2018
;
Boussarie and Hatta 2020
;
Hatta and Strikman 2021
.
H. Dutrieux et. al. apply the artificial neural network technique
recently to analyze the DVCS process in extracting the
D
-term of the proton
Dutrieux et al. 2021
.

On experimental side, the extraction of the gluonic
D
-term is rarely mentioned in the literatures.
The exclusive leptoproduction of vector mesons
ep\rightarrow e^{\prime}\gamma^{*}p\rightarrow e^{\prime}p^{\prime}V

can broadly unveil the details of the structure of proton.
The proton has only the up and down valence components,
and the light mesons can be used to detect only these partons in proton.
But in the Regge limit, the heavy quarkonium like J/
\psi\ \mathrm{and}\ \Upsilon

can be used to probe the gluon part of the proton.
It is similar to the method of extracting the gravitational radius of the proton
from the GFFs of EMT
Kharzeev 2021
;
Wang et al. 2021
.
In this work, we apply the vector meson
\phi,\ \mathrm{J}/\psi
near-threshold photoproduction data
Mibe et al. 2005
;
Ali et al. 2019

to extract the gluons’ contribution of
D
-term
D_{g}(0)
.
Then we present the internal pressure shear force distributions of the proton with the obtained
D
-term.

The organization of the paper is as follows.
The EMT and
D
-term of the proton are briefly introduced in Sec.
II
.
The result on the parameterized

II
Energy-momentum tensor and
\bf D
-term

The QCD EMT is the conserved current associated with the space-time translational symmetry based on Noether’s theorem.
The total tensor satisfies the conservation law:

\displaystyle\partial^{\mu}\hat{T}_{\mu\nu}=0,

(1)

\displaystyle\hat{T}_{\mu\nu}=\sum_{q}\hat{T}_{\mu\nu}^{q}+\hat{T}_{\mu\nu}^{g}.

The covariant normalization condition is set as

\left\langle p^{\prime}\mid p\right\rangle=2p^{0}(2\pi)^{3}\delta^{(3)}\left(\bm{p}^{\prime}-\bm{p}\right)
,
where the state
|\bm{p}\rangle
is one particle state.
The form factors of EMT depend the kinematic variables
P=\frac{1}{2}\left(p^{\prime}+p\right),\Delta=p^{\prime}-p,t=\Delta^{2}
.
As the proton is the focus of this paper, the matrix element of the QCD EMT of the Spin-
\frac{1}{2}
hadron is defined as
Polyakov and Schweitzer 2018
,

\displaystyle\left\langle p^{\prime},s^{\prime}\left|\hat{T}_{\mu\nu}^{a}(x)\right|p,s\right\rangle=\bar{u}^{\prime}\left[A^{a}(t)\frac{\gamma_{\{\mu}P_{\nu\}}}{2}\right.

(2)

\displaystyle\left.+B^{a}(t)\frac{iP_{\{\mu}\sigma_{\nu\}\rho}\Delta^{\rho}}{4m}\right.\left.+D^{a}(t)\frac{\Delta_{\mu}\Delta_{\nu}-g_{\mu\nu}\Delta^{2}}{4m}\right.

\displaystyle+m\bar{c}^{a}(t)g_{\mu\nu}\bigg]ue^{i\left(p^{\prime}-p\right)x},

where the normalization of the spinor is
\bar{u}(p,s)u(p,s)=2m
,
with the notation
a_{\{\mu}b_{\nu\}}=a_{\mu}b_{\nu}+a_{\nu}b_{\mu}
.
The individual quark and gluon form factors
A^{a}(t),\ B^{a}(t),\ D^{a}(t)

and
\bar{c}^{a}(t)
depend on the renormalization scale
Polyakov and Schweitzer 2018
.
From the lattice QCD calculation
Alexandrou et al. 2017
, the
B^{a}(t)
is suggested to be zero for the gluon.

The other GFFs besides the
D
-term form factor contain the information of the mass and the spin distributions of the proton.
In this paper we mainly focus on the
D
-term form factors which can describe the coupling between the vector meson and the proton.
The GFFs related to the vector production is discussed in Ref.
Hatta and Strikman 2021
.
The tripole parametrization ansatz for the
D
-term is
suggested by the perturbative counting rule at large
t

Tanaka 2018
;
Hatta and Strikman 2021
,

D_{q,g}(t)=\frac{D_{q,g}(0)}{\left(1-t/m_{D}^{2}\right)^{3}}.

(3)

The tripole form contributes more to the scattering amplitude in the small
t
region.

III
Extraction of
\bf{D_{g}(0)}

Usually both GFF
A(t)
and GFF
D(t)
couple to the vector meson photoproduction.

D
-term dominates at
t
approaching zero and it has the steeper
t
-dependence.
To extract the
D
-term from the vector meson near-threshold photoproduction data,
we assume that
Hatta and Yang 2018
,

\frac{d\sigma}{dt}\sim D^{2}(t)=\frac{D_{g}^{2}(0)}{\left(1-t/m_{D}^{2}\right)^{6}}.

(4)

Since the form factors are evaluated at a large scale
\mu^{2}=Q^{2}
,
in an approximation we can use the following asymptotic results,

\displaystyle A_{q}(0)

\displaystyle\approx\frac{n_{f}}{4C_{F}+n_{f}},\ \quad A_{g}(0)

\displaystyle\approx\frac{4C_{F}}{4C_{F}+n_{f}},\ \quad D_{q}(0)

\displaystyle\approx\frac{n_{f}}{4C_{F}}D_{g}(0),

(5)

where
C_{F}=\frac{N_{c}^{2}-1}{2N_{c}}
is the Casimir operator with
N_{c}=3

and
n_{f}=3
represents the number of light flavors in the proton.
The aim of the analysis is to extract
D(0)
, therefore we use the third row in Eq. (
5
),
which relates the quark
D
-term and the gluon
D
-term with
D_{q}(0)=\frac{9}{16}D_{g}(0)
.
In analyzing the experimental data of the photoproductions of
\phi
and J/
\psi
,
we assume that only the gluon component of the proton are involved in the color interaction near the threshold.
This is reasonable given that s and c quarks in the
nucleon are generated by the gluon splitting
g\to s\bar{s}/c\bar{c}

Frankfurt and Strikman 2002
.
The gluon
D(0)
is directly regarded as the total one, as
D_{g}(0)=D(0)
, because of the weak gravitational field approximation
Kharzeev 2021
.
For
\phi
and J/
\psi
meson productions, Eq. (
4
) is used to
fit the experimental data
Mibe et al. 2005
;
Ali et al. 2019
.
The cutoff parameter
m_{D}
is also a free parameter to be determined by the fits.

IV
Pressure and shear force distribution inside the proton

In the previous section, the values of
D_{g}(0)
are given by analyzing the experimental data.
Generally,
D(t)
contains the information of the internal pressure and shear force distributions
of the proton. Using the tripole parameterization for
D_{g}(t)
and
-t=K^{2}
, we get,

D_{g}(K)=\frac{D_{g}(0)}{\left(1+\frac{K^{2}}{m_{D}^{2}}\right)^{3}}.

(6)

The Fourier transform of Eq. (
6
) gives the
D
-term in the three-dimensional coordinate space (
E=m_{N}
), as,

\displaystyle\tilde{D}_{g}(r)=D_{g}(0)\int\frac{d^{3}K}{2E(2\pi)^{3}}\frac{e^{-iK\cdot r}}{\left(1+\frac{K^{2}}{m_{D}^{2}}\right)^{3}}=\frac{D_{g}(0)}{64\pi m_{N}}\left(\frac{1}{m_{D}}+r\right)m_{D}^{4}\exp(-m_{D}r).

(7)

The pressure distribution
p(r)
and shear force
s(r)
inside the proton then can be computed with
\tilde{D}_{g}(r)

Polyakov and Schweitzer 2018
:

\displaystyle p(r)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{3}\frac{1}{r^{2}}\frac{d}{dr}r^{2}\frac{d}{dr}\tilde{D}_{g}(r)=\frac{D_{g}(0)}{192\pi m_{N}}(m_{D}r-3)m_{D}^{5}\exp(-m_{D}r),

(8)

\displaystyle s(r)

\displaystyle=-\frac{1}{2}\frac{d}{dr}\frac{1}{r}\frac{d}{dr}\tilde{D}_{g}(r)=-\frac{D_{g}(0)}{128\pi m_{N}}m_{D}^{5}r\exp(-m_{D}r).

According to Eq. (
7
–
8
), we calculate the pressure and shear force distributions inside the proton
with the associated uncertainties.
The uncertainties come from the uncertainties of the parameters
D_{g}(0)
and
m_{D}
.

8

D

and

Perevalova et al. 2016

\frac{2}{3}s(r)+p(r)>0.

(9)

Of course, our results satisfy the inequality. We also use the parameters from fitting data of J/
\psi

VI
Discussions and Summary

One important outcome of the QCD factorization theorem is
that for the process dominated by the two-gluon
exchange, the
t
-dependence should reach a universal limit which is independent
of the flavor of the quark constituents of the meson
Brodsky et al. 1994
;
Frankfurt and Strikman 2002
,
at large
Q^{2}
and fixed
x
.
The mechanism for such universality is the transverse squeezing
of the meson wave function. From LQCD calculation
Shanahan and Detmold 2019a
;
Shanahan and Detmold 2019b
,
the authors concluded that the gluons play an important role
in the internal dynamics of the proton, which is quite distinct from that of quarks.
Hence in this limit the
t
-dependence of the amplitude is given solely by the
gluons’ contribution.
Therefore it is a reasonable assumption to extract the gluonic GFFs
from the differential cross section data of the vector meson photoproductions
in the region close to the threshold energy.

In this work, the gluons’ contribution to the proton
D
-term
and the pressure distribution inside the proton are determined
from the vector meson photoproduction experiments near the threshold.
The vector meson mass dependence of the gluonic
D
-term is
shown in FIG.
2
.
Our speculation for the dependence is that
the gluonic
D
-term is scale-dependent
and different meson probes correspond to different energy scales.
We note that a recent work presents a determination of the shear force distribution in the proton
using a DVCS experiment which measures the GPDs of the proton
Burkert et al. 2021
.
Our work sketches an alternative method beside the DVCS experiment
in studying the
D
-term, pressure and shear forces inside the proton.
More precise data at JLab and future EIC in US
Accardi et al. 2016
;
Abdul Khalek et al. 2021
and China
Chen 2018
;
Chen et al. 2020
;
Anderle et al. 2021

are of importance for probing the
D
-term form factor and enhancing our understanding of the QCD structure of the proton.
