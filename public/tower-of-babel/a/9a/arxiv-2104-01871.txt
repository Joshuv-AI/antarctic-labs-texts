Radiative Interactions between New Non-Abelian Gauge Sector and the Standard Model
Takaaki Nomura, Hiroshi Okada

Abstract
We discuss one loop generation of the term connecting gauge fields from a local hidden S ​ U ​ ( 2 ) H SU(2)_{H} and the standard model U ​ ( 1 ) Y U(1)_{Y} introducing an S ​ U ​ ( 2 ) H SU(2)_{H} doublet fermion with non-zero hypercharge and a scalar field in adjoint representation. Then we obtain a kinetic mixing term between S ​ U ​ ( 2 ) H SU(2)_{H} and U ​ ( 1 ) Y U(1)_{Y} gauge fields after the adjoint scalar field developing vacuum expectation value. We illustrate such a concrete scenario introducing a dark matter model in an ultraviolet (UV) completion with local S ​ U ​ ( 2 ) H SU(2)_{H} symmetry where the scalar doublet is our dark matter candidate and its stability is guaranteed by remnant Z 2 Z_{2} symmetry from S ​ U ​ ( 2 ) H SU(2)_{H} . Relic density of dark matter is calculated focusing on the case in which dark matter annihilate into known particles via S ​ U ​ ( 2 ) H SU(2)_{H} gauge interactions with radiatively induced kinetic mixing.
