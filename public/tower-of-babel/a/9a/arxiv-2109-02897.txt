Baryonic B B meson decays
Xiaotao Huang, Yu-Kuo Hsiao, Jike Wang, Liang Sun

Abstract
We review the two and three-body baryonic B B decays with the dibaryon ( 𝐁 ​ 𝐁 ¯ ′ {\bf B\bar{B}^{\prime}} ) as the final states. Accordingly, we summarize the experimental data of the branching fractions, angular asymmetries, and C ​ P CP asymmetries. Using the W W -boson annihilation (exchange) mechanism, the branching fractions of B → 𝐁 ​ 𝐁 ¯ ′ B\to{\bf B\bf\bar{B}^{\prime}} are shown to be interpretable. In the approach of perturbative QCD counting rules, we study the three-body decay channels. In particular, we review the C ​ P CP asymmetries of B → 𝐁 ​ 𝐁 ¯ ′ ​ M B\to{\bf B\bar{B}^{\prime}}M , which are promising to be measured by the LHCb and Belle II experiments. Finally, we remark the theoretical challenges in interpreting ℬ ⁡ ( B − → p ​ p ¯ ​ ρ − ) {\cal B}(B^{-}\to p\bar{p}\rho^{-}) and ℬ ⁡ ( B − → p ​ p ¯ ​ μ − ​ ν ¯ μ ) {\cal B}(B^{-}\to p\bar{p}\mu^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\mu}) .

IV
Discussions and Conclusions

The theoretical results in Table
2
can agree well with the data,
which is based on the factorization, pQCD counting rules, and baryonic form factors.
In particular, several
CP
asymmetries are predicted as large as 10-20%,
promising to be measured by LHCb and Belle II
Belle-II:2018jsg
;
LHCb:2018roe
.
It is reasonable to extend the theoretical approach to

B\to{\bf B}_{c}{\bf\bar{B}^{\prime}}
and
B\to{\bf B\bar{B}^{\prime}}M_{c}

Hsiao:2019wyd
;
Hsiao:2016amt
;
Chen:2008sw
,
where
{\bf B}_{c}(M_{c})
denotes a baryon (meson) containing a charm quark. As a consequence,

({\cal B},{\cal A}_{FB})
can also be expalined (see Table
3
).
Nonetheless,

{\cal B}(B^{-}\to p\bar{p}\rho^{-},\rho^{-}\to\pi^{-}\pi^{0})=(28.8\pm 2.1)\times 10^{-6}
and

{\cal B}(B^{-}\to p\bar{p}\mu^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\mu})=(1.04\pm 0.24\pm 0.12)\times 10^{-4}

we have predicted are not verified by the observations
Geng:2007cw
;
Geng:2011tr
;
pdg
;
LHCb:2019cgl
,

\displaystyle{\cal B}(B^{-}\to p\bar{p}\pi^{-}\pi^{0})=(4.6\pm 1.3)\times 10^{-6}\,,

\displaystyle{\cal B}(B^{-}\to p\bar{p}\mu^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\mu})=(5.27^{+0.23}_{-0.24}\pm 0.21\pm 0.15)\times 10^{-6}\,,

(21)

where the amplitude of
B^{-}\to p\bar{p}\mu^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\mu}

is given by

\displaystyle{\cal M}(B^{-}\to p\bar{p}\ell\bar{\nu}_{\ell})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{G_{F}}{\sqrt{2}}V_{ub}\langle p\bar{p}|\bar{u}\gamma_{\mu}(1-\gamma_{5})b|B^{-}\rangle\bar{\ell}\gamma^{\mu}(1-\gamma_{5})\nu_{\ell}\,.

(22)

Since
B\to p\bar{p}\rho
and
B^{-}\to p\bar{p}\mu^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\mu}

are seen to be associated with the
B\to p\bar{p}
transition form factors,
which are inferred to cause the overestimations.
Besides,
{\cal B}(B^{-}\to p\bar{p}\mu^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\mu})

inconsistent with the data can be partly due to the inconsistent determination of
|V_{ub}|

between the inclusive and exclusive
B
decays.

As the final remark,
since the predictions of
{\cal B}(B^{-}\to p\bar{p}\rho^{-})
and

{\cal B}(B^{-}\to p\bar{p}\mu^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\mu})
are shown to deviate from
the observations by the factors of 6 and 20, respectively,
the theoretical approach is facing some difficulties.
Therefore, the re-examination should be performed elsewhere.
