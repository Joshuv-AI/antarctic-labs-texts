The Dirichlet problem for degenerate fully nonlinear elliptic equations on Riemannian manifolds
Ri-Rong Yuan

Abstract
We derive the existence of C 1 , 1 C^{1,1} -solutions to the Dirichlet problem for degenerate fully nonlinear elliptic equations on Riemannian manifolds under appropriate assumptions.

Introduction

Let
(M,g)
be a compact Riemannian manifold of dimension
n\geq 2

with smooth boundary
\partial M
,
\bar{M}:=M\cup\partial M
,
with the Levi-Civita connection
\nabla
. Let

be a smooth symmetric

containing positive cone, i.e.

\Gamma_{n}:=\{\lambda\in\mathbb{R}^{n}:\mbox{ each component }\lambda_{i}>0\}\subseteq\Gamma

In this paper,
we consider the Dirichlet problem

\displaystyle f(\lambda(\nabla^{2}u+\chi))=\psi\mbox{ in }M,\,

\displaystyle u=\varphi\mbox{ on }\partial M

\displaystyle\inf_{M}\psi=\sup_{\partial\Gamma}f,

with respect to

\sup_{\partial\Gamma}f=\sup_{\lambda_{0}\in\partial\Gamma}\limsup_{\lambda\rightarrow\lambda_{0}}f(\lambda).

In addition, some standard assumptions are imposed as follows:

\displaystyle f\in C^{\infty}(\Gamma)\cap C(\overline{\Gamma}),\mbox{ where }\overline{\Gamma}=\Gamma\cup\partial\Gamma,

(1.3)

\displaystyle f_{i}(\lambda):=\frac{\partial f}{\partial\lambda_{i}}(\lambda)>0\mbox{ in }\Gamma,\,

\displaystyle\forall 1\leq i\leq n,

(1.4)

\displaystyle f\mbox{ is concave in }\Gamma.

(1.5)

For

and

[
7
]

In a series of papers

See also the Survey

For more progress and open problems on degenerate nonlinear elliptic equations, we also refer the readers to

manifolds subject to

\displaystyle(-\kappa_{1},\cdots,-\kappa_{n-1})\in\overline{\Gamma}_{\infty}\mbox{ in }\partial M.

Here

\displaystyle f(\lambda(\nabla^{2}\underline{u}+\chi))>\psi\mbox{ in }\bar{M},\quad\underline{u}=\varphi\mbox{ on }\partial M.
