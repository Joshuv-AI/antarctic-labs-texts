Weyl groupoids and superalgebraic sets.
Ian M. Musson

Abstract
This paper is a contribution to the study of the geometry of algebras related the Weyl groupoid initiated in [ 9 ] . The Nullstellensatz gives a bijection between radical ideals of such an algebra and their zero loci, the superalgebraic sets. Such sets are exactly the (Zariski) closed sets that are invariant under the action of a suitable groupoid, and the smallest superalgebraic set containing a given closed set can be described explicitly. Here we give several examples of superalgebraic sets. We also give several characterizations of Laurent supersymmetric polynomials. These adapt to unite several definitons of one of the algebras of interest, J ⁡ ( G ) J(G) that may be found in the literature.
