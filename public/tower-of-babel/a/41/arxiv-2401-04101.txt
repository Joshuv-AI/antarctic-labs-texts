A generalization of perfect codes in the presence of star multiset transpositions


Abstract
Let 0 < ℓ ∈ ℤ 0<\ell\in\mathbb{Z} . The notion of an efficient dominating set or perfect code S S of a graph G G is generalized to that of an efficient dominating ℓ \,{}^{\ell} -set or perfect ℓ code, of the graph G G , meaning that each vertex v v of V ⁡ ( G ) ∖ S V(G)\setminus S has exactly ℓ \ell neighbors in S S , instead of just one neighbor. Such generalization is applied to star j j -set transposition graphs based on permutations of multisets with each symbol repeated j j times, ( j ∈ { ℓ , ℓ − 1 } j\in\{\ell,\ell-1\} ). In such vertex-transitive graphs this approach produces total colorings, efficient dominating sets, also called perfect codes, etc.
