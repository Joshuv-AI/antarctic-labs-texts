However, the energy density of dark matter is expressed as the product of particle number density and the mass of dark matter. Now, we examine the BEC dark matter as non-relativistic bosons engaged in a two-particle interaction within a quantum system. As discussed earlier, BEC recognized as a state of matter, emerges when a dilute Bose gas undergoes significant cooling to attain extremely low temperatures.
According to experimental observations of the Einstein and Bose, as the temperature approaches absolute zero, the waves associated with the particles eventually overlap. This phenomenon results in the merging of elementary particles into a single quantum state, termed Bose-Einstein condensation.
