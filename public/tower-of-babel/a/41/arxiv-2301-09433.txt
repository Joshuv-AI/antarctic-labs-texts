On the Clean Graph of a Ring
Randhir Singh, S. C. Patekar

Abstract
Let R R be a ring (not necessarily commutative ring) with identity. The clean graph C ​ l ​ ( R ) Cl(R) of a ring R R is a graph with vertices in the form of ordered pair ( e , u ) (e,u) , where e e is an idempotent of the ring R R and u u is a unit of the ring R R . Two distinct vertices ( e , u ) (e,u) and ( f , v ) (f,v) are adjacent if and only if e ​ f = f ​ e = 0 ef=fe=0 or u ​ v = v ​ u = 1 uv=vu=1 . In this paper, we determine the Wiener index, Matching number of the clean graph of the ring ℤ n \mathbb{Z}_{n} . Keywords: Idempotent, Unit, Clean Graph, Wiener index, Matching Number. 2020 AMS Subject Classification: 05C70, 05C09, 13A70, 16U60, 16U40.

Introduction

Studying the graphs associated with the algebraic structure to understand various properties is an interesting and classical technique. In this way, various graph parameters can be studied using algebraic properties for better understanding. For example, one can study graphs associated with ring
R
with unity.

The concept of the zero divisor graph of a commutative ring was introduced by Beck
[
2
]

[

modified the definition of the zero divisor graph of a commutative ring with unity as follows: let

consist of non-zero zero divisors of the commutative ring
R
with unity and two distinct vertices

. Many researchers are attracted to study the graphs associated with algebraic structure.

Proposition 3.1

For any non negative integer

Proof.

To find number of self invertible element

x^{2}\equiv 1(mod~2^{m})\text{ and }x^{2}\equiv 1(mod\prod\limits_{i=1}^{k}{p_{i}}^{\alpha_{i}})

The solutions of

1.

.

2.

3.

The solution set of equation

[

The solutions of

Therefore, by Chinese remainder theorem,

|U^{\prime}(\mathbb{Z}_{n})|=\begin{cases}2^{k}&\text{ if }m=0\text{ or }m=1\\

∎
