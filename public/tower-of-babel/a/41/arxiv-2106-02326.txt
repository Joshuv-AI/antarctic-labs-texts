Fast Extra Gradient Methods for Smooth Structured Nonconvex-Nonconcave Minimax Problems
Sucheol Lee, Donghwan Kim

Abstract
Modern minimax problems, such as generative adversarial network and adversarial training, are often under a nonconvex-nonconcave setting, and developing an efficient method for such setting is of interest. Recently, two variants of the extragradient (EG) method are studied in that direction. First, a two-time-scale variant of the EG, named EG+, was proposed under a smooth structured nonconvex-nonconcave setting, with a slow 𝒪 ⁡ ( 1 / k ) \mathcal{O}(1/k) rate on the squared gradient norm, where k k denotes the number of iterations. Second, another variant of EG with an anchoring technique, named extra anchored gradient (EAG), was studied under a smooth convex-concave setting, yielding a fast 𝒪 ⁡ ( 1 / k 2 ) \mathcal{O}(1/k^{2}) rate on the squared gradient norm. Built upon EG+ and EAG, this paper proposes a two-time-scale EG with anchoring, named fast extragradient (FEG), that has a fast 𝒪 ⁡ ( 1 / k 2 ) \mathcal{O}(1/k^{2}) rate on the squared gradient norm for smooth structured nonconvex-nonconcave problems; the corresponding saddle-gradient operator satisfies the negative comonotonicity condition. This paper further develops its backtracking line-search version, named FEG-A, for the case where the problem parameters are not available. The stochastic analysis of FEG is also provided.

Conclusion

This paper proposed a two-time-scale and anchored extragradient method, named FEG, for smooth structured nonconvex-nonconcave problems. The proposed FEG has an accelerated
\mathcal{O}(1/k^{2})
rate,
with respect to the squared gradient norm,
for the Lipschitz continuous and negative comonotone operators for the first time. The FEG also has value for smooth convex-concave problems,
compared to existing works.
We further studied its backtracking line-search version, named FEG-A, for the smooth structured nonconvex-nonconcave problems and studied its stochastic version, named S-FEG, for smooth convex-concave problems. We leave

and applying to more realistic problems

\displaystyle+\frac{b_{k}}{2L_{k}^{2}\alpha_{k}\beta_{k}(1-\beta_{k})}(\|\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k+1}-\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k+1/2}\|^{2}-L_{k}^{2}\alpha_{k}^{2}\|(1-\beta_{k})\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k}-\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k+1/2}\|^{2})

\displaystyle\Big(a_{k}-\frac{b_{k}(1-2\beta_{k})\rho_{k}}{\beta_{k}}-\frac{b_{k}(1-\beta_{k})\alpha_{k}}{2\beta_{k}}\Big)\|\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k}\|^{2}+\Big(\frac{b_{k}}{2L_{k}^{2}\alpha_{k}\beta_{k}(1-\beta_{k})}+\frac{b_{k}\rho_{k}}{\beta_{k}}-a_{k+1}\Big)\|\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k+1}\|^{2}

\displaystyle-\Big(\frac{b_{k}}{L_{k}^{2}\alpha_{k}\beta_{k}(1-\beta_{k})}-\frac{b_{k}\alpha_{k}}{\beta_{k}(1-\beta_{k})}\Big)\mathop{\langle\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k+1},\,\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k+1/2}\rangle}\nolimits

\displaystyle\quad(\because a_{k}=\frac{b_{k}(1-\beta_{k})}{2\beta_{k}}(\alpha_{k}+2\rho_{k})-b_{k}\rho_{k}=\frac{b_{k}(1-\beta_{k})\alpha_{k}}{2\beta_{k}}+\frac{b_{k}(1-2\beta_{k})\rho_{k}}{\beta_{k}}.)

\displaystyle a_{k+1}

\displaystyle=\frac{b_{k}}{2\beta_{k}(1-\beta_{k})}\alpha_{k}+\frac{b_{k}\rho_{k}}{\beta_{k}}.\qquad(\because b_{k+1}=\frac{b_{k}}{1-\beta_{k}}.)

Note that

and

are the sufficient conditions for

for all
k\geq 1

∎

D.2

In this section, we consider the following class of stochastic methods:

\displaystyle\bm{z}_{k+1/2}

\displaystyle-\frac{b_{k}}{L^{2}\alpha_{k}\beta_{k}(1-\beta_{k})}\mathop{\langle\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k+1},\,\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k+1/2}\rangle}\nolimits+\frac{b_{k}\alpha_{k}}{\beta_{k}(1-\beta_{k})}\mathop{\langle\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k+1},\,\tilde{\bm{F}}\bm{z}_{k+1/2}\rangle}\nolimits

\displaystyle+\frac{b_{k}\alpha_{k}}{\beta_{k}}\mathop{\langle\tilde{\bm{F}}\bm{z}_{k}-\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k},\,\tilde{\bm{F}}\bm{z}_{k+1/2}\rangle}\nolimits

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{b_{k}(1-\beta_{k})\alpha_{k}}{2\beta_{k}}(\|\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k}\|^{2}-\|\tilde{\bm{F}}\bm{z}_{k}\|^{2})+\frac{b_{k}(1-L^{2}\alpha_{k}^{2})}{2L^{2}\alpha_{k}\beta_{k}(1-\beta_{k})}\|\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k+1}\|^{2}

\displaystyle+\frac{b_{k}(1-L^{2}\alpha_{k}^{2})}{2L^{2}\alpha_{k}\beta_{k}(1-\beta_{k})}\|\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k+1/2}\|^{2}+\frac{b_{k}\alpha_{k}}{2\beta_{k}(1-\beta_{k})}(\|\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k+1/2}\|^{2}-\|\tilde{\bm{F}}\bm{z}_{k+1/2}\|^{2})

\displaystyle-\frac{b_{k}(1-L^{2}\alpha_{k}^{2})}{L^{2}\alpha_{k}\beta_{k}(1-\beta_{k})}\mathop{\langle\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k+1},\,\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k+1/2}\rangle}\nolimits+\frac{b_{k}\alpha_{k}}{\beta_{k}(1-\beta_{k})}\mathop{\langle\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k+1},\,\tilde{\bm{F}}\bm{z}_{k+1/2}-\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k+1/2}\rangle}\nolimits

\displaystyle+\frac{b_{k}\alpha_{k}}{\beta_{k}}\mathop{\langle\tilde{\bm{F}}\bm{z}_{k}-\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k},\,\tilde{\bm{F}}\bm{z}_{k+1/2}\rangle}\nolimits

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{b_{k}(1-\beta_{k})\alpha_{k}}{2\beta_{k}}(\|\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k}\|^{2}-\|\tilde{\bm{F}}\bm{z}_{k}\|^{2})+\frac{b_{k}\alpha_{k}}{2\beta_{k}(1-\beta_{k})}(\|\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k+1/2}\|^{2}-\|\tilde{\bm{F}}\bm{z}_{k+1/2}\|^{2})

\displaystyle+\frac{b_{k}(1-L^{2}\alpha_{k}^{2})}{2L^{2}\alpha_{k}\beta_{k}(1-\beta_{k})}\|\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k+1}-\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k+1/2}\|^{2}

\displaystyle+\frac{b_{k}\alpha_{k}}{\beta_{k}(1-\beta_{k})}\mathop{\langle\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k+1},\,\tilde{\bm{F}}\bm{z}_{k+1/2}-\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k+1/2}\rangle}\nolimits+\frac{b_{k}\alpha_{k}}{\beta_{k}}\mathop{\langle\tilde{\bm{F}}\bm{z}_{k}-\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k},\,\tilde{\bm{F}}\bm{z}_{k+1/2}\rangle}\nolimits

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\frac{b_{k}(1-\beta_{k})\alpha_{k}}{2\beta_{k}}(\|\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k}\|^{2}-\|\tilde{\bm{F}}\bm{z}_{k}\|^{2})+\frac{b_{k}\alpha_{k}}{2\beta_{k}(1-\beta_{k})}(\|\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k+1/2}\|^{2}-\|\tilde{\bm{F}}\bm{z}_{k+1/2}\|^{2})

\displaystyle+\frac{b_{k}\alpha_{k}}{\beta_{k}(1-\beta_{k})}\mathop{\langle\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k+1},\,\tilde{\bm{F}}\bm{z}_{k+1/2}-\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k+1/2}\rangle}\nolimits+\frac{b_{k}\alpha_{k}}{\beta_{k}}\mathop{\langle\tilde{\bm{F}}\bm{z}_{k}-\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k},\,\tilde{\bm{F}}\bm{z}_{k+1/2}\rangle}\nolimits.

Then by taking expectation on the both sides and using the fact
\mathbb{E}[\mathop{\langle\tilde{\bm{F}}\bm{z}_{k}-\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k},\,\tilde{\bm{F}}\bm{z}_{k+1/2}\rangle}\nolimits]=\mathbb{E}[\mathop{\langle\tilde{\bm{F}}\bm{z}_{k}-\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k},\,\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k+1/2}\rangle}\nolimits]
, we get

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}[V_{k}]-\mathbb{E}[V_{k+1}]\geq

\displaystyle-\frac{b_{k}(1-\beta_{k})\alpha_{k}}{2\beta_{k}}\sigma_{k}^{2}-\frac{b_{k}\alpha_{k}}{2\beta_{k}(1-\beta_{k})}\sigma_{k+1/2}^{2}

\displaystyle+\frac{b_{k}\alpha_{k}}{\beta_{k}(1-\beta_{k})}\mathbb{E}[\mathop{\langle\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k+1},\,\tilde{\bm{F}}\bm{z}_{k+1/2}-\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k+1/2}\rangle}\nolimits]+\frac{b_{k}\alpha_{k}}{\beta_{k}}\mathbb{E}[\mathop{\langle\tilde{\bm{F}}\bm{z}_{k}-\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k},\,\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k+1/2}\rangle}\nolimits]

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle-\frac{b_{k}\alpha_{k}(1+2L\alpha_{k})}{2\beta_{k}}\Big((1-\beta_{k})\sigma_{k}^{2}+\frac{1}{1-\beta_{k}}\sigma_{k+1/2}^{2}\Big)

where the last inequality follows from Lemma
D.2
.
∎

D.3
Proof of Theorem
6.1

Note that S-FEG is equivalent to (
Class S-FEG
) with
\alpha_{k}=\frac{1}{L}
and
\beta_{k}=\frac{1}{k+1}
.
It is straightforward to verify that the given
\{\alpha_{k}\}_{k\geq 0}
and
\{\beta_{k}\}_{k\geq 0}
satisfy the conditions in Lemma
D.1
for all
k\geq 0
. By noting that

\displaystyle a_{k}=\frac{b_{k}(1-\beta_{k})\alpha_{k}}{2\beta_{k}}=\frac{k^{2}}{2L}\qquad\text{and}

\displaystyle b_{k}=\frac{1}{1-\beta_{k-1}}b_{k-1}=\Big(\prod_{i=1}^{k-1}\frac{1}{1-\beta_{i}}\Big)b_{1}=k,

Lemma
D.1
implies that

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}[V_{k}]

\displaystyle=\mathbb{E}[V_{k}-V_{0}]

\displaystyle=\sum_{l=0}^{k-1}(\mathbb{E}[V_{l+1}]-\mathbb{E}[V_{l}])

\displaystyle\leq\Big(\frac{L^{2}\alpha_{0}^{3}}{2}+L\alpha_{0}^{2}\Big)\sigma_{0}^{2}+\sum_{l=1}^{k-1}\frac{b_{l}\alpha_{l}(1+2L\alpha_{l})}{2\beta_{l}}\Big((1-\beta_{l})\sigma_{l}^{2}+\frac{1}{1-\beta_{l}}\sigma_{l+1/2}^{2}\Big)

\displaystyle=\frac{3}{2L}\sigma_{0}^{2}+\sum_{l=1}^{k-1}\frac{3}{2L}(l^{2}\sigma_{l}^{2}+(l+1)^{2}\sigma_{l+1/2}^{2})

\displaystyle\overset{\text{let}}{=}\sigma_{\text{total}}^{2}.

Therefore, noting that
\mathbb{E}[V_{k}]=\mathbb{E}\Big[\frac{k^{2}}{2L}\|\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k}\|^{2}-k\mathop{\langle\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k},\,\bm{z}_{0}-\bm{z}_{k}\rangle}\nolimits\Big]
, we get

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\Big[\frac{k^{2}}{2L}\|\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k}\|^{2}\Big]

\displaystyle\leq\mathbb{E}[k\mathop{\langle\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k},\,\bm{z}_{0}-\bm{z}_{k}\rangle}\nolimits]+\sigma_{\text{total}}^{2}

\displaystyle=\mathbb{E}[k\mathop{\langle\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k},\,\bm{z}_{0}-\bm{z}_{*}\rangle}\nolimits+k\mathop{\langle\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k},\,\bm{z}_{*}-\bm{z}_{k}\rangle}\nolimits]+\sigma_{\text{total}}^{2}

\displaystyle=\mathbb{E}[k\mathop{\langle\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k},\,\bm{z}_{0}-\bm{z}_{*}\rangle}\nolimits]+\sigma_{\text{total}}^{2}\qquad(\because\text{monotonicity of $\bm{F}$})

\displaystyle\leq\mathbb{E}\Big[\frac{k^{2}}{4L}\|\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k}\|^{2}+L\|\bm{z}_{0}-\bm{z}_{*}\|^{2}\Big]+\sigma_{\text{total}}^{2}.\qquad(\because\text{Young's inequality}.)

As a result, we get
\mathbb{E}\Big[\frac{k^{2}}{4L}\|\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k}\|^{2}\Big]\leq L\|\bm{z}_{0}-\bm{z}_{*}\|^{2}+\frac{3}{2L}\left[\sigma_{0}^{2}+\sum_{l=1}^{k-1}(l^{2}\sigma_{l}^{2}+(l+1)^{2}\sigma_{l+1/2}^{2})\right]
and, by dividing the both sides by
\frac{k^{2}}{4L}
, we get

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}[\|\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k}\|^{2}]\leq\frac{4L^{2}\|\bm{z}_{0}-\bm{z}_{*}\|^{2}}{k^{2}}+\frac{6}{k^{2}}\left[\sigma_{0}^{2}+\sum_{l=1}^{k-1}(l^{2}\sigma_{l}^{2}+(l+1)^{2}\sigma_{l+1/2}^{2})\right].

In addition, if

\sigma_{0}^{2}\leq\frac{\epsilon}{6}
,

\sigma_{k}^{2}\leq\frac{\epsilon}{6k}
and
\sigma_{k+1/2}^{2}\leq\frac{\epsilon}{6(k+1)}
for all
k\geq 1
, then we have

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}[\|\bm{F}\bm{z}_{k}\|^{2}]

\displaystyle\leq\frac{4L^{2}\|\bm{z}_{0}-\bm{z}_{*}\|^{2}}{k^{2}}+\frac{6}{k^{2}}\left[\frac{\epsilon}{6}+\sum_{l=1}^{k-1}\Big(\frac{\epsilon l}{6}+\frac{\epsilon(l+1)}{6}\Big)\right]

\displaystyle=\frac{4L^{2}\|\bm{z}_{0}-\bm{z}_{*}\|^{2}}{k^{2}}+\frac{\epsilon}{k^{2}}\sum_{l=0}^{k-1}(2l+1)

\displaystyle=\frac{4L^{2}\|\bm{z}_{0}-\bm{z}_{*}\|^{2}}{k^{2}}+\frac{\epsilon}{k^{2}}\sum_{l=0}^{k-1}((l+1)^{2}-l^{2})

\displaystyle=\frac{4L^{2}\|\bm{z}_{0}-\bm{z}_{*}\|^{2}}{k^{2}}+\epsilon.

∎
