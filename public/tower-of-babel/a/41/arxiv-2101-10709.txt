Symmetries, Spinning Particles and the TCFH of D=4,5 Minimal Supergravities


Abstract
We find that spinning particles with suitable couplings propagating in certain supersymmetric backgrounds of D = 4 D=4 , N = 2 N=2 and D = 5 D=5 , N = 1 N=1 minimal supergravities are invariant under symmetries generated by the twisted covariant form hierarchies of these theories. We also compare our results with the symmetries of spinning particles generated by Killing-Yano forms which are responsible for the separability properties of some gravitational backgrounds.

The 2-form bilinears of both

D=5

The existence of a principal CCKY form on a background implies the separability of the geodesic equations and some of the classical field equations, see e.g.
[
1
,
2
]
. Therefore one should not expect that the backgrounds we have investigated exhibit similar separability properties unless they admit additional symmetries, e.g. additional rotation or axial symmetries. To give an explicit example consider the solution of

K_{3}
, one should not expect that the geodesic equations of the 5-dimensional solution to be separable.
