Lemma 1

\displaystyle\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}\ell_{1}\cdots\ell_{k}=n\\

∎

Lemma 2
.

For any
h\geq 1
, we have

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{\tau_{k}(nh)}{n^{s}}=\zeta^{k}(s)A_{h}(s),\ (\sigma>1),

where

Proof.

By multiplicativity and Euler products, we have

\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{\tau_{k}(nh)}{n^{s}}

\displaystyle=\prod_{p\mid h}\left(\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\frac{\tau_{k}(p^{j+\nu_{p}(h)})}{p^{js}}\right)\prod_{p\nmid h}\left(\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\frac{\tau_{k}(p^{j})}{p^{js}}\right)

(2.9)

\displaystyle=\prod_{p\mid h}\frac{\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\binom{k+j+\nu_{p}(h)-1}{k-1}\frac{1}{p^{js}}}{\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\frac{\tau_{k}(p^{j})}{p^{js}}}\prod_{p}\left(\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\frac{\tau_{k}(p^{j})}{p^{js}}\right).

By a hypergeometric relation, we have

(2.10)

\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\binom{k+j+\nu_{p}(h)-1}{k-1}\frac{1}{p^{js}}=\binom{k+\nu_{p}(h)-1}{k-1}{}_{2}F_{1}(1,k+\nu_{p}(h);1+\nu_{p}(h);p^{-s}).

This, together with

\prod_{p}\left(\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\frac{\tau_{k}(p^{j})}{p^{js}}\right)=\zeta^{k}(s),

give (

∎

h\geq 1
