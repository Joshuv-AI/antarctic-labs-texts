Abstract.

The article is dedicated to the memory of
Oleg Nikolaevich Vvedenskii (1937-1981).
The results obtained by O. N. Vvedenskii are presented, as well as selected new results
of the authors, which develop the study of arithmetic algebraic geometry in the directions of
crystalline cohomology, fundamental groups of schemes, torsors, dualities.
Elements of ontology of Vvedenskii’s research are also given.
A continuation of the review of Vvedenskii’s results, as well as a review of new selected results, including variants of Smith–Minkowski–Siegel mass formula and Drinfeld shtukas, will be presented in the second part of the paper.

Keywords:
Abelian variety, Picard variety, local field, duality, etale (étale) topology, fundamental group of a scheme, formal group
