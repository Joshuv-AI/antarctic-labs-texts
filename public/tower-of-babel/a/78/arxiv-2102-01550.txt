Introduction and main results

In this paper we are concerned with a class of parabolic equations with
non-standard growth conditions.
Let
\Omega

\mathbb{R}^{n}

We study solutions to the equation

(1.1)

Throughout the paper we suppose that the functions

The aim of this paper is to establish the continuity of bounded weak solutions
for a wide class of parabolic equations with generalized Orlicz growth. In terms
of the function

\int\limits_{0}^{\varepsilon}f\left(x_{0},t_{0},\frac{(\varepsilon+s)\varepsilon^{j}\omega\,\lambda(\rho)}{\rho}\right)ds\leqslant\frac{2q\ln 2}{2q\ln 2+\ln\frac{1}{2\varepsilon}}\int\limits_{0}^{1}f\left(x_{0},t_{0},\frac{(\varepsilon+s)\varepsilon^{j}\omega\,\lambda(\rho)}{\rho}\right)ds.

(5.8)

\int\limits_{0}^{\sigma}f\left(x_{0},t_{0},\frac{(\varepsilon+s)\varepsilon^{j}\omega\,\lambda(\rho)}{\rho}\right)ds\\
=\sigma\int\limits_{0}^{1}f\left(x_{0},t_{0},\frac{(\varepsilon+\sigma s)\varepsilon^{j}\omega\,\lambda(\rho)}{\rho}\right)ds\geqslant\sigma\int\limits_{0}^{1}f\left(x_{0},t_{0},\frac{(\varepsilon+s)\varepsilon^{j}\omega\,\lambda(\rho)}{\rho}\right)ds.

\displaystyle f(x_{0},t_{0},\gamma{\rm w})

(5.9)

\displaystyle\geqslant\frac{1}{\gamma{\rm w}}\,\frac{1}{\psi(x_{0},t_{0},{\rm w})}=\frac{1}{\gamma g(x_{0},t_{0},{\rm w})}\geqslant\frac{{\rm w}}{\gamma q\,\mathcal{G}(x_{0},t_{0},{\rm w})}=\frac{1}{\gamma q}\,f(x_{0},t_{0},{\rm w}),

\geqslant\int\limits_{0}^{\varepsilon}f\left(x_{0},t_{0},\frac{(\varepsilon+s)\varepsilon^{j}\omega\,\lambda(\rho)}{\rho}\right)ds+\sum\limits_{i=0}^{\mathcal{J}-1}\int\limits_{2^{i}\varepsilon}^{2^{i+1}\varepsilon}f\left(x_{0},t_{0},\frac{(\varepsilon+s)\varepsilon^{j}\omega\,\lambda(\rho)}{\rho}\right)ds.

To estimate the second term on the right-hand side of the last inequality

5.9

\displaystyle\int\limits_{2^{i}\varepsilon}^{2^{i+1}\varepsilon}f\left(x_{0},t_{0},\frac{(\varepsilon+s)\varepsilon^{j}\omega\,\lambda(\rho)}{\rho}\right)ds=2^{i}\int\limits_{0}^{\varepsilon}f\left(x_{0},t_{0},\frac{(2^{i}s+(2^{i}+1)\varepsilon)\varepsilon^{j}\omega\,\lambda(\rho)}{\rho}\right)ds

\displaystyle\geqslant\frac{1}{2q}\int\limits_{0}^{\varepsilon}f\left(x_{0},t_{0},\frac{(s+\varepsilon)\varepsilon^{j}\omega\,\lambda(\rho)}{\rho}\right)ds,\quad i=0,1,\ldots,\mathcal{J}-1.

\int\limits_{0}^{1}f\left(x_{0},t_{0},\frac{(\varepsilon+s)\varepsilon^{j}\omega\,\lambda(\rho)}{\rho}\right)ds\geqslant\left(1+\frac{\mathcal{J}}{2q}\right)\int\limits_{0}^{\varepsilon}f\left(x_{0},t_{0},\frac{(s+\varepsilon)\varepsilon^{j}\omega\,\lambda(\rho)}{\rho}\right)ds,

from which the required (
5.8
