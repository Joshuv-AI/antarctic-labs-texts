C*-ALGEBRAIC SMALE MEAN VALUE CONJECTURE AND DUBININ-SUGAWA DUAL MEAN VALUE CONJECTURE
K. MAHESH KRISHNA

Abstract




Abstract
: Based on Smale mean value conjecture
[Bull. Amer. Math. Soc., 1981]
and Dubinin-Sugawa dual mean value conjecture
[Proc. Japan Acad. Ser. A Math. Sci., 2009]
we formulate the following conjectures.

C*-algebraic Smale Mean Value Conjecture : Let
\mathcal{A}
be a commutative C*-algebra. Let
P(z)=(z-a_{1})\cdots(z-a_{n})
be a polynomial of degree
n\geq 2
over
\mathcal{A}
,
a_{1},\dots,a_{n}\in\mathcal{A}
. If
z\in\mathcal{A}
is not a critical point of
P
, then there exists a critical point
w\in\mathcal{A}
of
P
such that

\displaystyle\frac{\|P(z)-P(w)\|}{\|z-w\|}\leq 1\|P^{\prime}(z)\|

or

\displaystyle\frac{\|P(z)-P(w)\|}{\|z-w\|}\leq\frac{n-1}{n}\|P^{\prime}(z)\|=\frac{\operatorname{deg}(P)-1}{\operatorname{deg}(P)}\|P^{\prime}(z)\|.

C*-algebraic Dubinin-Sugawa Dual Mean Value Conjecture : Let
\mathcal{A}
be a commutative C*-algebra. Let
P(z)=(z-a_{1})\cdots(z-a_{n})
be a polynomial of degree
n\geq 2
over
\mathcal{A}
,
a_{1},\dots,a_{n}\in\mathcal{A}
. If
z\in\mathcal{A}
is not a critical point of
P
, then there exists a critical point
w\in\mathcal{A}
of
P
such that

\displaystyle\frac{\|P^{\prime}(z)\|}{\operatorname{deg}(P)}=\frac{\|P^{\prime}(z)\|}{n}\leq\frac{\|P(z)-P(w)\|}{\|z-w\|}.

We show that (even a strong form of) C*-algebraic Smale mean value conjecture and C*-algebraic Dubinin-Sugawa dual mean value conjecture hold for degree 2 C*-algebraic polynomials over commutative C*-algebras.

Keywords
: C*-algebra, Smale mean value theorem, Smale mean value conjecture, Dubinin-Sugawa dual mean value theorem, Dubinin-Sugawa dual mean value conjecture.

: 46L05, 30C10.

2.
C*-algebraic Smale Mean Value Conjecture

Let
\mathcal{A}
be a C*-algebra. For
P(z)\coloneqq(z-a_{1})(z-a_{2})\cdots(z-a_{n})
for all
z\in\mathcal{A}
with
a_{1},a_{2},\dots,a_{n}\in\mathcal{A}
, we define

\displaystyle P^{\prime}(z)=\sum_{j=1}^{n}(z-a_{1})\cdots\widehat{(z-a_{j})}\cdots(z-a_{n}),\quad\forall z\in\mathcal{A}

where the term with cap is missing. We state C*-algebraic version of Conjecture
1.3
as follows.

Conjecture 2.1
.

(C*-algebraic Smale Mean Value Conjecture)
Let
\mathcal{A}
be a commutative C*-algebra. Let
P(z)\coloneqq(z-a_{1})\cdots(z-a_{n})
be a polynomial of degree
n\geq 2
over
\mathcal{A}
,
a_{1},\dots,a_{n}\in\mathcal{A}
. If
z\in\mathcal{A}
is not a critical point of
P
(i.e.,
P^{\prime}(z)\neq 0
), then there exists a critical point
w\in\mathcal{A}
of
P
such that

\displaystyle\frac{\|P(z)-P(w)\|}{\|z-w\|}\leq 1\|P^{\prime}(z)\|

or

\displaystyle\frac{\|P(z)-P(w)\|}{\|z-w\|}\leq\frac{n-1}{n}\|P^{\prime}(z)\|=\frac{\operatorname{deg}(P)-1}{\operatorname{deg}(P)}\|P^{\prime}(z)\|.

We can formulate a stronger conjecture than Conjecture
2.1
as follows.

Conjecture 2.2
.

(C*-algebraic Smale Mean Value Conjecture - strong form)

Let
\mathcal{A}
be a commutative C*-algebra. Let
P(z)\coloneqq(z-a_{1})\cdots(z-a_{n})
be a polynomial of degree
n\geq 2
over
\mathcal{A}
,
a_{1},\dots,a_{n}\in\mathcal{A}
. If
z\in\mathcal{A}
is not a critical point of
P
, then there exists a critical point
w\in\mathcal{A}
of
P
such that

(5)

\displaystyle(P(z)-P(w))(P(z)-P(w))^{*}\leq(z-w)P^{\prime}(z)P^{\prime}(z)^{*}(z-w)^{*}

or

(6)

\displaystyle(P(z)-P(w))(P(z)-P(w))^{*}

\displaystyle\leq\left(\frac{n-1}{n}\right)^{2}(z-w)P^{\prime}(z)P^{\prime}(z)^{*}(z-w)^{*}

\displaystyle=\left(\frac{\operatorname{deg}(P)-1}{\operatorname{deg}(P)}\right)^{2}(z-w)P^{\prime}(z)P^{\prime}(z)^{*}(z-w)^{*}.

Proposition 2.3
.

Conjecture
2.2
implies Conjecture
2.1
.

Proof.

Taking norm in Inequality (
5
) we get

\displaystyle\|P(z)-P(w)\|^{2}

\displaystyle=\|(P(z)-P(w))(P(z)-P(w))^{*}\|\leq\|(z-w)P^{\prime}(z)P^{\prime}(z)^{*}(z-w)^{*}\|

\displaystyle=\|(z-w)P^{\prime}(z)\|^{2}\leq\|z-w\|^{2}\|P^{\prime}(z)\|^{2}.

Similarly by taking norm in Inequality (
6
) we get the second inequality in Conjecture
2.1
.
∎

Theorem 2.4
.

Conjecture
2.1
holds for degree 2 C*-algebraic polynomials over C*-algebras.

Proof.

We prove that Conjecture
2.2
holds and hence Conjecture
2.1
holds. Let
\mathcal{A}
be a commutative C*-algebra and let
P(z)\coloneqq(z-a)(z-b)
,
a,b\in\mathcal{A}
. Then
P^{\prime}(z)=2z-(a+b)
for all
z\in\mathcal{A}
. Let
z\in\mathcal{A}
be such that
P^{\prime}(z)\neq 0
. Note that
P^{\prime}
has only one zero, namely
c\coloneqq\frac{a+b}{2}
. To show Inequality (
6
), we need to show that

\displaystyle(P(z)-P(c))(P(z)-P(c))^{*}\leq\frac{1}{4}(z-c)P^{\prime}(z)P^{\prime}(z)^{*}(z-c)^{*}.

Define
x\coloneqq z-a
and
y\coloneqq z-b
. Then

\displaystyle z-c=z-\frac{a+b}{2}=\frac{z-a+z-b}{2}=\frac{x+y}{2},

\displaystyle P^{\prime}(z)=z-a+z-b=x+y,

\displaystyle P(c)=\left(\frac{a+b}{2}-a\right)\left(\frac{a+b}{2}-b\right)=\frac{-(b-a)^{2}}{4}=\frac{-(x-y)^{2}}{4},

and

\displaystyle(P(z)-P(c))(P(z)-P(c))^{*}=\left(xy+\frac{(x-y)^{2}}{4}\right)\left(xy+\frac{(x-y)^{2}}{4}\right)^{*}=\frac{(x+y)^{2}(x^{*}+y^{*})^{2}}{16}.

Therefore

\displaystyle\frac{1}{4}(z-c)P^{\prime}(z)P^{\prime}(z)^{*}(z-c)^{*}-(P(z)-P(c))(P(z)-P(c))^{*}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{4}\left(\frac{x+y}{2}\right)(x+y)(x^{*}+y^{*})\left(\frac{x^{*}+y^{*}}{2}\right)-\frac{1}{16}(x+y)^{2}(x^{*}+y^{*})^{2}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{16}((x+y)^{2}(x^{*}+y^{*})^{2}-(x+y)^{2}(x^{*}+y^{*})^{2})=0.

∎

Theorem 2.6
.

Conjecture
2.5
holds for degree 2 C*-algebraic polynomials.

Proof.

We continue from the proof of Theorem
2.4
. Let
z\in\mathcal{A}
be not a zero of
P^{\prime}
. We have
P^{\prime\prime}(z)=2
. Hence

\displaystyle\|P^{\prime\prime}(z)\|=2,\quad\|P^{\prime}(z)\|=\|x+y\|,

\displaystyle\|P(z)-P(c)\|^{2}

\displaystyle=\|(P(z)-P(c))(P(z)-P(c))^{*}\|=\frac{1}{16}\|(x+y)^{2}(x^{*}+y^{*})^{2}\|

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{16}\|(x+y)^{2}((x+y)^{2})^{*}\|=\frac{1}{16}\|(x+y)^{2}\|^{2}\leq\frac{1}{16}\|x+y\|^{4}.

Therefore

\displaystyle\frac{\|P^{(2)}(z)\|}{2!}\frac{\|P(z)-P(c)\|^{2-1}}{\|P^{\prime}(z)\|^{2}}=\frac{2}{2}\frac{\left(\frac{\|(x+y)^{2}\|}{4}\right)}{\|x+y\|^{2}}\leq\frac{1}{4}\frac{\|x+y\|^{2}}{\|x+y\|^{2}}=\frac{1}{4}=4^{2-1}.
