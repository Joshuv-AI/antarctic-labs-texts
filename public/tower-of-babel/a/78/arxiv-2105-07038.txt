Low Diameter Monochromatic Covers of Complete Multipartite Graphs
Sean English, Connor Mattes, Grace McCourt, Michael Phillips

Abstract
Let the diameter cover number, D r t ​ ( G ) D^{t}_{r}(G) , denote the least integer d d such that under any r r -coloring of the edges of the graph G G , there exists a collection of t t monochromatic subgraphs of diameter at most d d such that every vertex of G G is contained in at least one of the subgraphs. We explore the diameter cover number with two colors and two subgraphs when G G is a complete multipartite graph with at least three parts. We determine exactly the value of D 2 2 ​ ( G ) D_{2}^{2}(G) for all complete tripartite graphs G G , and almost all complete multipartite graphs with more than three parts.

Introduction

Given a hypergraph

Let

Note that the classical König’s Theorem

the only exact result is for the case

7

.

An equivalent formulation of Ryser’s Conjecture involves coverings of edge-colored graphs with monochromatic connected components. More specifically, a
monochromatic connected subgraph cover
of an edge colored graph

Conjecture 1.2

For every graph

For a brief sketch of the proof that these two conjectures are equivalent, see Section

Phrasing Ryser’s Conjecture in terms of edge colorings leads to many interesting questions and connections. In particular, this phrasing of Ryser’s Conjecture sheds light on the fact that the conjecture fits into the large body of work on Ramsey theory. In particular, Ryser’s Conjecture is closely related to problems involving finding large monochromatic components in edge-colored graphs. Given an

]

[

1.2
Equivalent Formulation

In this section, we provide a brief proof that conjectures
1.1
and
1.2

First, let us assume Conjecture

r

corresponds to a vertex cover of

, and thus
\mathrm{tc}_{r}(G)\leq\tau(H)
. Furthermore, given a matching
M\subseteq E(H)
,
M=\{e_{1},e_{2},\dots,e_{k}\}
for each edge
e_{i}
in

, there is at least one vertex
v_{i}\in V(G)
such that
v_{i}
is in the intersection of all components that are contained in
e_{i}
. Consider two vertices,
v_{i}
and
v_{j}
,
i\neq j
, in the intersection of all components that are contained in
e_{i}
and
e_{j}
, respectively. If
v_{i}v_{j}\in E(G)
, say of color

, then in order for

and

to be disjoint, one of them must not contain a component of color

are maximal collections of components. Thus,

is an independent set and so

. Then Conjecture

implies that

\mathrm{tc}_{r}(G)\leq\tau(H)\leq(r-1)\nu(H)\leq(r-1)\alpha(G),

and thus Conjecture
1.1
implies Conjecture
1.2
.

Now let us assume Conjecture
1.2
holds. Let
H
be an
r
-partite hypergraph with partition
V(H)=V_{1}\cup V_{2}\cup\dots\cup V_{r}
. Define a graph
G
with
V(G)=E(H)
. For each pair

, add the edge

to

if
u\cap v\neq\emptyset
as edges of
H
. Color this edge color

, where

is the smallest integer such that

is non-empty. Given a monochromatic component cover of

, say
G_{1},G_{2},\dots,G_{k}\subseteq G
, where

is color

, there is a unique vertex

of

in

such that for all

. Thus the vertex

in

covers every edge corresponding to a vertex in

. Since

. Furthermore, given non-adjacent vertices

\tau(H)\leq\mathrm{tc}_{r}(G)\leq(r-1)\alpha(G)\leq(r-1)\nu(H),

1.3
For Any Complete Multipartite Graph
G

Note that adding edges can only decrease the tree cover number, so it suffices to show this for complete bipartite graphs. Let

A_{2,2}\neq\emptyset
and
A_{2,1}\cup A_{3,1}\neq\emptyset
by Corollary
4.4
. Thus if
y^{\prime}\notin A_{2,1}\cup A_{3,1}
, those two monochromatic subgraphs form the desired cover.

Now suppose
y^{\prime}\in A_{3,1}
. Consider the red star at
y^{\prime}
along with the red
C_{5}
blow up formed by
x
,
A_{2,2}
,
x^{\prime}
,
y
, and
(A_{2,1}\cup A_{3,1})\setminus\{y^{\prime}\}
. This gives the desired cover unless
(A_{2,1}\cup A_{3,1})\setminus\{y^{\prime}\}=\emptyset
, in which case we replace the
C_{5}
blow up with the red star at
x^{\prime}
. Thus we have the desired cover.

Therefore
y^{\prime}\in A_{2,1}
. The second half of the property can be proven by switching the roles of
x
and
x^{\prime}
in the above argument.
∎

Concluding Results

In this paper we were able to determine the diameter cover number exactly for complete tripartite graphs, and for some classes of complete multipartite graphs, whenever two colors are used and two subgraphs are allowed. It would however be interesting to have a more thorough idea of the diameter cover number for other complete multipartite graphs.
