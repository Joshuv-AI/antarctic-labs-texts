A note on ”The Klein-Gordon oscillator in (1+2)-dimensions Gürses space-time backgrounds (Ann. Phys. (N. Y.) 404 (2019) 1)”
Omar Mustafa, Faizuddin Ahmed

Abstract
Abstract: We revisit and discuss the KG-oscillator in the ( 1 + 2 ) (1+2) -dimensional Gürses space-time studied in Ref. Re1 (F. Ahmed, Ann. Phys. (N. Y.) 404 (2019) 1). The modified oscillator frequency i.e., ω ~ 2 = ( Ω 2 ​ E 2 + η 2 ) \tilde{\omega}^{2}=(\Omega^{2}\,E^{2}+\eta^{2}) appeared in the eigenvalue equation is an energy-dependent parameter, and consequently, the results are accurately reported here. Moreover, we show some interesting spectroscopic features indulged within the very nature of Gürses space-time for the KG-Gürses oscillators. PACS numbers : 05.45.-a, 03.50.Kk, 03.65.-w Keywords: Klein-Gordon oscillators, Gürses space-time, vorticity-energy correlations.



In a recent paper, one of us
Re1
has investigated the Klein-Gordon (KG) particles in the
(1+2)
-dimensions Gürses space-time background
Re2
described by the metric

ds^{2}=-dt^{2}+dr^{2}-2\,\Omega\,r^{2}\,dt\,d\theta+r^{2}\,\left(1-\Omega^{2}\,r^{2}\right)\,d\theta^{2}=g_{\mu\nu}\,dx^{\mu}\,dx^{\nu};\text{ }\mu,\nu=0,1,2.

(1)

Under such space-time metric, the covariant and contravariant metric
tensors, respectively, read

g_{\mu\nu}=\left(\begin{tabular}[]{ccc}$-1\vskip 3.0pt plus 1.0pt minus 1.0pt$&$0$&$-\Omega r^{2}$\\
$0$&$1\vskip 3.0pt plus 1.0pt minus 1.0pt$&$0$\\
$-\Omega r^{2}$&$\,0$&$\,r^{2}\left(1-\Omega^{2}r^{2}\right)$\end{tabular}\right)\Longleftrightarrow g^{\mu\nu}=\left(\begin{tabular}[]{ccc}$\left(\Omega^{2}r^{2}-1\right)$&$0\vskip 3.0pt plus 1.0pt minus 1.0pt$&$-\Omega$\\
$0$&$1\vskip 3.0pt plus 1.0pt minus 1.0pt$&$0$\\
$-\Omega$&$\,0$&$\,1/r^{2}$\end{tabular}\right)\text{ };\text{ \ }g=\det\left(g_{\mu\nu}\right)=-r^{2}.

(2)

Then the corresponding KG-equation is given by

\frac{1}{\sqrt{-g}}\partial_{\mu}\left(\sqrt{-g}g^{\mu\nu}\partial_{\nu}\Psi\right)=m^{2}\Psi.

(3)

At this point, the KG-oscillator is introduced through Moshinsky and Szczepaniak recipe
Re3
so that the momentum operator takes its non-minimal coupling form

p_{\mu}\longrightarrow p_{\mu}+i\mathcal{F}_{\mu}\Longrightarrow\partial_{\mu}\longrightarrow\partial_{\mu}+\mathcal{F}_{\mu},

(4)

where
\mathcal{F}_{\mu}=\left(0,\mathcal{F}_{r},0\right)=\left(0,m\,\omega\,r,0\right)
as proposed in
Re3
;
Re4
;
Re5
;
Re6
;
Re8
. We shall, however, define
\mathcal{F}_{\mu}=\left(0,\eta\,r,0\right)
to avoid the confusion between
m^{2}\equiv\left(mc^{2}\right)^{2}
in (
3
) and
m
in
\mathcal{F}_{\mu}=\left(0,m\,\omega r,0\right)
which identifies the non-relativistic mass of the quantum particle (as in Schrödinger equation). To retrieve Ahmed’s results, we substitute
\eta=m\,\omega
, therefore. Under such settings, one rewrites (
3
) as

\frac{1}{\sqrt{-g}}\left(\partial_{\mu}+\mathcal{F}_{\mu}\right)\left[\sqrt{-g}\,g^{\mu\nu}\left(\partial_{\nu}-\mathcal{F}_{\nu}\right)\Psi\right]=m^{2}\Psi,

(5)

to obtain

\left\{-\partial_{t}^{2}+\left(\Omega\,r\,\partial_{t}-\frac{1}{r}\partial_{\theta}\right)^{2}+\partial_{r}^{2}+\frac{1}{r}\partial_{r}-\eta^{2}r^{2}-2\eta-m^{2}\right\}\Psi=0.

(6)

Then the substitution of
\Psi=\Psi\left(t,r,\theta\right)=\exp\left(-i\,E\,t+i\,\ell\,\theta\right)\,\psi\left(r\right)
(where
\ell
denotes the magnetic quantum number
\ell=0,\pm 1,\pm 2,\cdots
) would result in

\psi^{{}^{\prime\prime}}\left(r\right)+\frac{1}{r}\psi^{{}^{\prime}}\left(r\right)+\left(\lambda-\tilde{\omega}^{2}r^{2}-\frac{\ell^{2}}{r^{2}}\right)\psi\left(r\right)=0,

(7)

where

\lambda=E^{2}-2\,\Omega\,\ell\,E-m^{2}-2\,\eta,\text{ }\tilde{\omega}=\sqrt{\Omega^{2}\,E^{2}+\eta^{2}}.

(8)

We observe that equation (
7
) is in the form of the two-dimensional radial Schrödinger oscillator and admits the textbook solution

\psi\left(r\right)\sim r^{\left|\ell\right|}\exp\left(-\frac{\left|\tilde{\omega}\right|r^{2}}{2}\right)L_{n_{r}}^{\left|\ell\right|}\left(\left|\tilde{\omega}\right|r^{2}\right)

(9)

as the eigenfunctions, and

\lambda=2|\tilde{\omega}|\left(2n_{r}+|\ell|+1\right)

(10)

as the eigenvalues, where
n_{r}=0,1,2,\cdots
is the radial quantum number. Consequently, using (
8
) and (
10
), one would obtain the following eigenvalue polynomial equation

E^{2}_{n_{r},\ell}-2\,\Omega\,\ell\,E_{n_{r},\ell}-C_{n_{r},\ell}^{2}=0,

(11)

where

\text{ \ }C_{n_{r},\ell}^{2}=2|\tilde{\omega}|\,\left(2n_{r}+|\ell|+1\right)+m^{2}+2\,\eta

(12)

which is exactly the same expression as equation (20), along with (22), reported in Ref.
Re1
using the parametric Nikiforov-Uvarov method.

However, the following discussion was missing in Ref.
Re1
which we feel necessary to explain. The modified oscillator frequency
\tilde{\omega}
, given by (
8
) with
|\tilde{\omega}|=|\Omega\,E|\sqrt{1+\eta^{2}/\Omega^{2}E^{2}}
, is actually an energy-dependent parameter, and
C_{n_{r},\ell}
’s of (
12
) are given by

C_{n_{r},\ell}^{2}=C_{n_{r},\ell}^{2}\left(E_{n_{r},\ell}\right)=2\,\sqrt{\Omega^{2}\,E^{2}_{n_{r},\ell}+\eta^{2}}\left(2\,n_{r}+|\ell|+1\right)+m^{2}+2\,\eta.

(13)

Therefore, for any
n_{r}\geq 0
the results starting from equations (25) to (30) in Ref.
Re1
seem insecure (since they are in an incomplete form). For example,
E_{0,\ell}
given in equation (25) in the original work is explicitly given by

E_{0,\ell}=\Omega\,\ell+\sqrt{\Omega^{2}\,\ell^{2}+2\,\sqrt{\Omega^{2}\,E_{0,\ell}^{2}+\eta^{2}}\left(|\ell|+1\right)+m^{2}+2\,\eta}

(14)

for
n_{r}=0
and so on. Actually, the eigenvalue equation (
11
) is a polynomial of
E_{n_{r},\ell}
given by

\displaystyle E^{2}_{n_{r},\ell}-2\,\Omega\,\ell\,E_{n_{r},\ell}-m^{2}-2\,\eta=2\,\sqrt{\Omega^{2}\,E^{2}_{n_{r},\ell}+\eta^{2}}\,\left(2\,n_{r}+|\ell|+1\right)

\displaystyle\Rightarrow

\displaystyle\left(E^{2}_{n_{r},\ell}-2\,\Omega\,\ell\,E_{n_{r},\ell}-m^{2}-2\,\eta\right)^{2}=4\,\left(\Omega^{2}\,E^{2}_{n_{r},\ell}+\eta^{2}\right)\,\left(2\,n_{r}+|\ell|+1\right)^{2}

\displaystyle\Rightarrow

\displaystyle f_{4}\,E^{4}_{n_{r},\ell}+f_{3}\,E^{3}_{n_{r},\ell}+f_{2}\,E^{2}_{n_{r},\ell}+f_{1}\,E_{n_{r},\ell}+f_{0}=0

(15)

where
f_{i}\quad(i=0\ldots 4)
are non-zero parameter. The real solution of the above polynomial (
15
) gives us the exact energy eigenvalue expression
E_{n_{r},\ell}
of the KG-oscillator field associated with the modes
\left\{n_{r},\ell\right\}
in the Gürses space-time.

Nevertheless, it would be interesting to mention that the Gürses space-time metric (
1
) provides the KG-oscillators (hence KG-Gürses oscillators) as a natural Gürses space-time byproduct. Namely, setting
\eta=0
(i.e., retrieving the usual momentum operator in (
4
)) would result that
\tilde{\omega}^{2}=\Omega^{2}E^{2}
in (
7
) and the corresponding quadratic energy form of equation (
11
) now reads

E^{2}-2\,\Omega\,\ell\,E-2\,|\Omega\,E|\,\left(2\,n_{r}+|\ell|+1\right)-m^{2}=0.

(16)

This energy relation is by itself interesting in the sense that
E
denotes the energies for both KG-particles and anti-particles,
i. e.,

E=E_{+}=+|E|
for particles and
E=E_{-}=-|E|
for anti-particles. Moreover, the vorticity parameter
\Omega
denotes rotation of the Gürses universe so that
\Omega=\pm|\Omega|
to imply that
\Omega_{+}=|\Omega|
and
\Omega_{-}=-|\Omega|
. Under such assumptions, one would obtain

E_{\pm}^{2}-2\,\Omega_{\pm}E_{\pm}\,\tilde{n}_{+}-m^{2}=0;\;\tilde{n}_{+}=2n_{r}+\left|\ell\right|+\ell\,+1,

(17)

for
\left|\Omega E\right|=\Omega_{\pm}E_{\pm}
, and

E_{\pm}^{2}+2\,\Omega_{\mp}E_{\pm}\,\tilde{n}_{-}\,-m^{2}=0;\;\tilde{n}_{-}=2n_{r}+\left|\ell\right|-\ell\,+1.

(18)

for
\left|\Omega E\right|=-\Omega_{\mp}E_{\pm}
. Which would
allow us to cast

E_{\pm}=\Omega_{\pm}\,\tilde{n}_{+}\pm\sqrt{\Omega^{2}\tilde{n}_{+}^{2}+m^{2}}\Rightarrow\left\{\begin{tabular}[]{l}$E_{+}=\Omega_{\pm}\,\tilde{n}_{+}+\sqrt{\Omega^{2}\tilde{n}_{+}^{2}+m^{2}}$\\
$E_{-}=\Omega_{\pm}\,\tilde{n}_{+}-\sqrt{\Omega^{2}\tilde{n}_{+}^{2}+m^{2}}$\end{tabular}\right.,

(19)

for
\left|\Omega E\right|=\Omega_{\pm}E_{\pm}
and

E_{\pm}=-\Omega_{\mp\,}\tilde{n}_{-}\,\pm\sqrt{\Omega^{2}\tilde{n}_{-}^{2}+m^{2}}\Rightarrow\left\{\begin{tabular}[]{l}$E_{+}=-\Omega_{-\,}\tilde{n}_{-}\,+\sqrt{\Omega^{2}\tilde{n}_{-}^{2}+m^{2}}$\\
$E_{-}=-\Omega_{+\,}\tilde{n}_{-}\,-\sqrt{\Omega^{2}\tilde{n}_{-}^{2}+m^{2}}$\end{tabular}\right..

(20)

However, it is more interesting to express the energies in terms of
\Omega_{+}
and
\Omega_{-}
so that

E_{\pm}^{\left(\Omega_{+}\right)}=\pm\left|\Omega\right|\,\tilde{n}_{\pm}\pm\sqrt{\Omega^{2}\tilde{n}_{\pm}^{2}+m^{2}},

(21)

for positive vorticity parameter, and

E_{\pm}^{\left(\Omega_{-}\right)}=\pm\left|\Omega\right|\,\tilde{n}_{\mp}\pm\sqrt{\Omega^{2}\tilde{n}_{\mp}^{2}+m^{2}}.

(22)

for negative vorticity parameter. Notably, we observe that
\tilde{n}_{\pm}\left(\ell=\pm\left|\ell\right|\right)=\tilde{n}_{\mp}\left(\ell=\mp\left|\ell\right|\right)
which would in effect introduce
the so called vorticity-energy correlations so that
E_{\pm}^{\left(\Omega_{+}\right)}\left(\ell=\pm\left|\ell\right|\right)=E_{\pm}^{\left(\Omega_{-}\right)}\left(\ell=\mp\left|\ell\right|\right)
.

On the other hand, the inclusion of the coupling
\xi
of the gravitational
field with the background curvature
R=2\Omega^{2}
would imply that

E_{\pm}^{\left(\Omega_{+}\right)}=\pm\left|\Omega\right|\,\tilde{n}_{\pm}\pm\sqrt{\Omega^{2}\tilde{n}_{\pm}^{2}+m^{2}+2\xi\Omega^{2}},

(23)

for the energies with positive vorticity in (
21
), and

E_{\pm}^{\left(\Omega_{-}\right)}=\pm\left|\Omega\right|\,\tilde{n}_{\mp}\pm\sqrt{\Omega^{2}\tilde{n}_{\mp}^{2}+m^{2}+2\xi\Omega^{2}}.

(24)

for the energies with negative vorticity in (
22
). We again observe
the vorticity-energy correlations
E_{\pm}^{\left(\Omega_{+}\right)}\left(\ell=\pm\left|\ell\right|\right)=E_{\pm}^{\left(\Omega_{-}\right)}\left(\ell=\mp\left|\ell\right|\right)

for the energies in (
23
) and (
24
).

Finally, the current note is intended to show some interesting spectroscopic features indulged within the Gürses spacetime structure, especially for the KG-Gürses equation. It is worthwhile mentioning that in the original work
Re1
, the vorticity parameter
\Omega
is chosen to be positive and forgot to interpreted the modified oscillator frequency
\tilde{\omega}
for which the exact energy is now possible to obtain from the polynomial equation (
15
) (but not the equation (21) in
Re1
written in an incomplete form). In this note, we have shown that since the modified oscillator frequency
\tilde{\omega}
given by (
8
) is an energy-dependent parameter, and hence, the exact energy expression of the KG-oscillator can be obtained by solving the polynomial equation (
15
) of the real solution. Consequently, the results reported in the original paper
Re1
for different modes, such as
n_{r}=0,1,2
are insecure and incomplete.
