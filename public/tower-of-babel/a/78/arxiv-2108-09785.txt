Partial skew Dyck paths—a kernel method approach
Helmut Prodinger

Abstract
. Skew Dyck are a variation of Dyck paths, where additionally to steps ( 1 , 1 ) (1,1) and ( 1 , − 1 ) (1,-1) a south-west step ( − 1 , − 1 ) (-1,-1) is also allowed, provided that the path does not intersect itself. Replacing the south-west step by a red south-east step, we end up with decorated Dyck paths. We analyze partial versions of them where the path ends on a fixed level j j , not necessarily at level 0. We exclusively use generating functions and derive them with the celebrated kernel method. In the second part of the paper, a dual version is studied, where the paths are read from right to left. In this way, we have two types of up-steps, not two types of down-steps, as before. A last section deals with the variation that the negative territory (below the x x -axis) is also allowed. Surprisingly, this is more involved in terms of computations.

Note that the syntactic rules of forbidden patterns

and

The following recursions can be read off immediately from the diagram:

\displaystyle f_{0}=1,\quad f_{i+1}=zf_{i}+zg_{i},\quad i\geq 0,

\displaystyle g_{i}=zf_{i+1}+zg_{i+1}+zh_{i+1},\quad i\geq 0,

\displaystyle h_{i}=zh_{i+1}+zg_{i+1},\quad i\geq 0.

And now it is time to introduce the promised
bivariate
generating functions:

F(z,u)=\sum_{i\geq 0}f_{i}(z)u^{i},\quad G(z,u)=\sum_{i\geq 0}g_{i}(z)u^{i},\quad H(z,u)=\sum_{i\geq 0}h_{i}(z)u^{i}.

Again, often we just write
F(u)
instead of
F(z,u)
and treat
z
as a ‘silent’ variable. Summing the recursions leads to

\displaystyle\sum_{i\geq 0}u^{i}f_{i+1}

\displaystyle=\sum_{i\geq 0}u^{i}zf_{i}+\sum_{i\geq 0}u^{i}zg_{i},

\displaystyle\sum_{i\geq 0}u^{i}g_{i}

\displaystyle=\sum_{i\geq 0}u^{i}zf_{i+1}+\sum_{i\geq 0}u^{i}zg_{i+1}+\sum_{i\geq 0}u^{i}zh_{i+1},

\displaystyle\sum_{i\geq 0}u^{i}h_{i}

\displaystyle=\sum_{i\geq 0}u^{i}zh_{i+1}+\sum_{i\geq 0}u^{i}zg_{i+1}.

This can be rewritten as

\displaystyle\frac{1}{u}(F(u)-1)

\displaystyle=zF(u)+zG(u),

\displaystyle G(u)

\displaystyle=\frac{z}{u}(F(u)-1)+\frac{z}{u}(G(u)-G(0))+\frac{z}{u}(H(u)-H(0)),

\displaystyle H(u)

\displaystyle=\frac{z}{u}(G(u)-G(0))+\frac{z}{u}(H(u)-H(0)).

This is a typical application of the kernel method. For a gentle example-driven introduction to the kernel method, see
[
8
]
. First,

\displaystyle F(u)

\displaystyle=\frac{z^{2}uG(0)+z^{2}uH(0)+z^{2}u-u-z^{3}+2z}{-{z}^{3}-u+2z+z{u}^{2}-{z}^{2}u},

\displaystyle G(u)

\displaystyle=\frac{z(H(0)-uzH(0)+z^{2}+G(0)-zuG(0)-zu)}{-{z}^{3}-u+2z+z{u}^{2}-{z}^{2}u},

\displaystyle H(u)

\displaystyle=\frac{z(-uzH(0)-z^{2}-zuG(0)+G(0)-z^{2}H(0)+H(0)-z^{2}G(0))}{-{z}^{3}-u+2z+z{u}^{2}-{z}^{2}u}.

The denominator factors as
z(u-r_{1})(u-r_{2})
, with

r_{1}=\frac{1+z^{2}+\sqrt{1-6z^{2}+5z^{4}}}{2z},\quad r_{2}=\frac{1+z^{2}-\sqrt{1-6z^{2}+5z^{4}}}{2z}.

Note that
r_{1}r_{2}=2-z^{2}
.
Since the factor
u-r_{2}
in the denominator is “bad,” it must also cancel in the numerators. From this
we conclude as a first step

G(0)=\frac{1-2z^{2}H(0)-3z^{2}-\sqrt{1-6z^{2}+5z^{4}}}{2z^{2}},

and by further simplification

H(0)=\frac{1-4z^{2}+z^{4}+(z^{2}-1)\sqrt{1-6z^{2}+5z^{4}}}{2-z^{2}}.

Thus (with
W=\sqrt{1-6z^{2}+5z^{4}}=\sqrt{(1-z^{2})(1-5z^{2})}
)

\displaystyle F(u)

\displaystyle=\frac{-1-z^{2}-W}{2z(u-r_{1})}=\frac{1+z^{2}+W}{2zr_{1}(1-u/r_{1})},

\displaystyle G(u)

\displaystyle=\frac{-1+z^{2}+W}{2z(u-r_{1})}=\frac{1-z^{2}-W}{2zr_{1}(1-u/r_{1})},

\displaystyle H(u)

\displaystyle=\frac{-1+3z^{2}+W}{2z(u-r_{1})}=\frac{1-3z^{2}-W}{2zr_{1}(1-u/r_{1})}.

The total generating function is

S(u)=F(u)+G(u)+H(u)=\frac{3-3z^{2}-W}{2zr_{1}(1-u/r_{1})}.

The coefficient of
u^{j}z^{n}
in
S(u)
counts the partial paths of length
n
, ending at level
j
.
We will write
s_{j}=[u^{j}]S(u)
.
Furthermore

\displaystyle f_{j}=[u^{j}]F(u)

\displaystyle=[u^{j}]\frac{1+z^{2}+W}{2zr_{1}(1-u/r_{1})},

\displaystyle g_{j}=[u^{j}]G(u)

\displaystyle=[u^{j}]\frac{1-z^{2}-W}{2zr_{1}(1-u/r_{1})},

\displaystyle h_{j}=[u^{j}]H(u)

\displaystyle=[u^{j}]\frac{1-3z^{2}-W}{2zr_{1}(1-u/r_{1})}.

At this stage, we are only interested in

s_{j}=f_{j}+g_{j}+h_{j}=[u^{j}]\frac{3-3z^{2}-W}{2zr_{1}(1-u/r_{1})}=\frac{3-3z^{2}-W}{2zr_{1}^{j+1}},

which is the generating function of all (partial) paths ending at level
j
. Parity considerations give us that only coefficients
[z^{n}]s_{j}
are non-zero if
n\equiv j\bmod 2
.
To make this more transparent, we set

P(z)=zr_{1}=\frac{1+z^{2}+\sqrt{1-6z^{2}+5z^{4}}}{2},

and then

s_{j}=f_{j}+g_{j}+h_{j}=z^{j}\frac{3-3z^{2}-W}{2P^{j+1}}.

Now we read off coefficients. We do this using residues and contour integration. The path of integration, in both variables
x
resp.
v
is a
small circle or an equivalent contour.

\displaystyle[z^{2m+j}]s_{j}

\displaystyle=[z^{2m}]\frac{3-3z^{2}-W}{2P^{j+1}}=[x^{m}]\frac{3-3x-\sqrt{1-6x+5x^{2}}}{2\Big(\frac{1+x-\sqrt{1-6x+5x^{2}}}{2}\Big)^{j+1}}

\displaystyle=[x^{m}]\frac{3-3\frac{v}{1+3v+v^{2}}-\frac{1-v^{2}}{1+3v+v^{2}}}{2\big(\frac{v(v+2)}{1+3v+v^{2}}\big)^{j+1}}

\displaystyle=[x^{m}]\frac{(1+v)(1+2v)}{v^{j+1}(v+2)^{j+1}}(1+3v+v^{2})^{j}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2\pi i}\oint\frac{dx}{x^{m+1}}\frac{(1+v)(1+2v)}{v^{j+1}(v+2)^{j+1}}(1+3v+v^{2})^{j}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2\pi i}\oint\frac{dv}{v^{m+1}}\frac{(1+v)(1+2v)(1-v^{2})}{v^{j+1}(v+2)^{j+1}}(1+3v+v^{2})^{m-1+j}

\displaystyle=[v^{m+j+1}]\frac{(1+v)^{2}(1+2v)(1-v)}{(v+2)^{j+1}}(1+3v+v^{2})^{m-1+j}.

Note that

(1+v)^{2}(1+2v)(1-v)=-9+27(v+2)-29(v+2)^{2}+13(v+2)^{3}-2(v+2)^{4};

consequently

\displaystyle[v^{k}]

\displaystyle\frac{(1+v)^{2}(1+2v)(1-v)}{(v+2)^{j+1}}

\displaystyle=-9\frac{1}{2^{j+1+k}}\binom{-j-1}{k}+27\frac{1}{2^{j+k}}\binom{-j}{k}-29\frac{1}{2^{j-1+k}}\binom{-j+1}{k}

\displaystyle+13\frac{1}{2^{j-2+k}}\binom{-j+2}{k}-2\frac{1}{2^{j-3+k}}\binom{-j+3}{k}=:\lambda_{j;k}.

With this abbreviation we find

[v^{m+j+1}]\frac{(1+v)^{2}(1+2v)(1-v)}{(v+2)^{j+1}}(1+3v+v^{2})^{m-1+j}=\sum_{k=0}^{m+j+1}\lambda_{j;k}\binom{m-1+j;1,3,1}{m+j+1-k}.

This is not extremely pretty but it is

and as good as it gets.

4.
Counting red edges

We can use an extra variable,
w
, to count additionally the red edges that occur in a path. We use the same
letters for generating functions. Eventually, the coefficient
[z^{n}u^{j}w^{k}]S
is the number of (partial) paths consisting of
n
steps, leading
to level
j
, and having passed
k
red edges. The endpoint of the original skew path has then coordinates
(n-2k,j)
. The computations are very similar, and we only
sketch the key steps.

f_{0}=1,\quad f_{i+1}=zf_{i}+zg_{i},\quad i\geq 0,

g_{i}=zf_{i+1}+zg_{i+1}+zh_{i+1},\quad i\geq 0,

h_{i}=wzh_{i+1}+wzg_{i+1},\quad i\geq 0;

\displaystyle\frac{1}{u}(F(u)-1)

\displaystyle=zF(u)+zG(u),

\displaystyle G(u)

\displaystyle=\frac{z}{u}(F(u)-1)+\frac{z}{u}(G(u)-G(0))+\frac{z}{u}(H(u)-H(0)),

\displaystyle H(u)

\displaystyle=\frac{wz}{u}(G(u)-G(0))+\frac{wz}{u}(H(u)-G(0));

\displaystyle F(u)

\displaystyle=\frac{z^{2}uG(0)+z^{2}uH(0)+z^{2}u-u-wz^{3}+z+wz}{-w{z}^{3}-u+z+wz+z{u}^{2}-w{z}^{2}u},

\displaystyle G(u)

\displaystyle=\frac{z(H(0)-uzH(0)+wz^{2}+G(0)-zuG(0)-zu)}{-w{z}^{3}-u+z+wz+z{u}^{2}-w{z}^{2}u},

\displaystyle H(u)

\displaystyle=\frac{wz(-uzH(0)-z^{2}-zuG(0)+G(0)-z^{2}H(0)+H(0)-z^{2}G(0))}{-w{z}^{3}-u+z+wz+z{u}^{2}-w{z}^{2}u}.

The denominator factors as
z(u-r_{1})(u-r_{2})
, with

\displaystyle r_{1}

\displaystyle=\frac{1+wz^{2}+\sqrt{1-(4+2w)z^{2}+(4w+w^{2})z^{4}}}{2z},

\displaystyle r_{2}

\displaystyle=\frac{1+wz^{2}-\sqrt{1-(4+2w)z^{2}+(4w+w^{2})z^{4}}}{2z}.

Note the factorization
1-(4+2w)z^{2}+(4w+w^{2})z^{4}=(1-z^{2}w)(1-(4+w)z^{2})
.
Since the factor
u-r_{2}
in the denominator is “bad,” it must also cancel in the numerators. From this
we eventually find, with the abbreviation

W=\sqrt{1-(4+2w)z^{2}+(4w+w^{2})z^{4}}\,
)

\displaystyle F(u)

\displaystyle=\frac{-1-wz^{2}-W}{2z(u-r_{1})},

\displaystyle G(u)

\displaystyle=\frac{-1+wz^{2}+W}{2z(u-r_{1})},

\displaystyle H(u)

\displaystyle=\frac{-1+(2+w)z^{2}+W}{2z(u-r_{1})}.

The total generating function is

S(u)=F(u)+G(u)+H(u)=\frac{-2-w+z^{2}(w+w^{2})+wW}{2z(u-r_{1})}.

The special case
u=0
(return to the
x
-axis) is to be noted:

S(0)=\frac{-2-w+z^{2}(w+w^{2})+wW}{-2zr_{1}}=\frac{1-wz^{2}-W}{2z^{2}}.

Since there are only even powers of
z
in this function, we replace
x=z^{2}
and get

\displaystyle S(0)

\displaystyle=\frac{1-wx-\sqrt{1-(4+2w)x+(4w+w^{2})x^{2}}}{2x}

\displaystyle=1+x+(w+2)x^{2}+(w^{2}+4w+5)x^{3}+(w^{3}+6w^{2}+15w+14)x^{4}+\cdots.

Compare the factor
(w^{2}+4w+5)
with the earlier drawing of the 10 paths.

There is again a substitution that allows for better results:

z=\frac{v}{1+(2+w)v+v^{2}},\quad\text{then}\quad S(0)=1+v.

Reading off coefficients can now be done using modified trinomial coefficients:

\binom{n;1,2+w,1}{k}=[t^{k}]\bigl(1+(2+w)t+t^{2}\bigr)^{n}.

Again, we use contour integration to extract coefficients:

\displaystyle[x^{n}](1+v)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2\pi i}\oint\frac{dx}{x^{n+1}}(1+v)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2\pi i}\oint\frac{dx}{v^{n+1}}\frac{1-v^{2}}{(1+(2+w)v+v^{2})^{2}}(1+(2+w)v+v^{2})^{n+1}(1+v)

\displaystyle=[v^{n}](1-v)(1+v)^{2}(1+(2+w)v+v^{2})^{n-1}

\displaystyle=\binom{n-1;1,2+w,1}{n}+\binom{n-1;1,2+w,1}{n-1}

\displaystyle\qquad-\binom{n-1;1,2+w,1}{n-2}-\binom{n-1;1,2+w,1}{n-3}.

Now we want to count the average number of red edges. For that, we differentiate
S(0)
w.r.t.
w
, followed by

.
This leads to

\frac{-1+6x-5x^{2}+(1+3x)\sqrt{1-6x+5x^{2}}}{2(1-x)(1-5x)}.

A simple application of singularity analysis leads to

\frac{\frac{1}{2\sqrt{5}}[x^{n}]\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-5x}}}{-\sqrt{5}[x^{n}]\sqrt{1-5x}}\sim\frac{n}{5}.

So, a random path consisting of

steps has about

red steps, on average.

In the example of Figure 2, the exact average is

\displaystyle[w^{0}]S(0)

\displaystyle=\frac{1-\sqrt{1-4x}}{2x},

\displaystyle[w^{1}]S(0)

\displaystyle=\frac{1-2x-\sqrt{1-4x}}{2\sqrt{1-4x}},

\displaystyle[w^{2}]S(0)

\displaystyle=\frac{x^{3}}{(1-4x)^{3/2}},

\displaystyle[w^{3}]S(0)

\displaystyle=\frac{x^{4}(1-2x)}{(1-4x)^{5/2}},

\displaystyle[w^{4}]S(0)

\displaystyle=\frac{x^{5}(1-4x+5x^{2})}{(1-4x)^{7/2}},\quad\&\text{c}.
