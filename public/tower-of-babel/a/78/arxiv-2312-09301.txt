Non-vanishing of Kolyvagin systems and Iwasawa theory
Ashay Burungale, Francesc Castella, Giada Grossi, Christopher Skinner

Abstract
. Let E / ℚ E/\mathbb{Q} be an elliptic curve and p p an odd prime. In 1991 Kolyvagin conjectured that the system of cohomology classes for the p p -adic Tate module of E E derived from Heegner points on E E over ring class fields of an imaginary quadratic field K K is non-trivial. In this paper we prove Kolyvagin’s conjecture in the cases where p p is a prime of good ordinary reduction for E E that splits in K K and a p p -adic anticyclotomic Iwasawa Main Conjecture for E / K E/K holds. In particular, our results cover many cases where p p is an Eisenstein prime, complementing W. Zhang’s progress towards the conjecture. Our methods also yield a proof of a refinement of Kolyvagin’s conjecture expressing the divisibility index of the p p -adic Heegner point Kolyvagin system in terms of the Tamagawa numbers of E E , as conjectured by W. Zhang in 2014, as well as proofs of analogous results for the Kolyvagin systems obtained from the cyclotomic Euler system of Beilinson–Kato elements.

Relation to previous works

Comparison with
[

Swe20

.

The approach used by W. Zhang in his breakthrough work on Kolyvagin’s conjecture is based on the principle of level-raising and rank-lowering, where rank refers to that of an associated mod
p

p
Selmer group associated to a weight two elliptic newform implies the

-value. This rank zero
implication
is a consequence of the (integral) cyclotomic Main Conjecture for the newform, as established by Skinner and Urban
[
SU14
]
under the hypotheses of
[
Zha14b
]
(by working mod
p^{m}
, some of the ramification hypotheses were removed in
[
Swe20
]
). It is precisely at this stage that Iwasawa theory enters into the approach of W. Zhang, albeit only implicitly. In contrast, the strategy in this paper is inherently Iwasawa-theoretic, based on the variation of Heegner points along the anticyclotomic
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
-extension.
Moreover, a rational Main Conjecture suffices for its implementation.
The approach of
[
Zha14b
,
Swe20
]
essentially excludes the Eisenstein case.
However, while the results of this paper on Kolyvagin’s conjecture are for
p
split in
K
, the results of
[
Zha14b
,
Swe20
]
apply also when
p
is inert in
K
.

•

Remark 1.1.1

\rho_{0}(\mathrm{H}^{1}_{\mathcal{F}(n_{i})}(K,T^{(m)})^{\pm})\geq 1

(g)

From (g), we get that

-torsion in the case where the character

is attached to a height one prime ideal of

different from

.

Moreover, by Lemma
2.2.4
and the choice (

(2.8)

j+\mathrm{length}_{R}(M(1))=\operatorname{ind}(\kappa_{1})<k-\length_{R}(M(1))<k,\ \ \ j=\operatorname{ind}(\kappa_{n_{\rho}}^{(k)}).

In order to prove the theorem, we need to show that

.
By (

\operatorname{ind}(\kappa_{n_{\rho}}^{(k)})<k

Let
\mathcal{N}^{\prime}=\mathcal{N}^{2k-1}

As

In this section we work with the cyclotomic Iwasawa algebra over

be the unique

3.1.
The Kolyvagin system of Kato classes

{\rm tor}_{\mathbb{Q}}

in the isogeny class of

For a character

(3.1)

Let

We define a

where
\mathbb{Q}^{\Sigma}

The local conditions of interest in this setting are the following:

•

For a finite prime

•

The
relaxed condition
at
\ell

\mathrm{H}^{1}_{\rm rel}(\mathbb{Q}_{\ell},V_{\alpha})=\mathrm{H}^{1}(\mathbb{Q}_{\ell},V_{\alpha})

•

The

at
\ell

\mathrm{H}^{1}_{\rm str}(\mathbb{Q}_{\ell},V_{\alpha})=\mathrm{H}^{1}(\mathbb{Q}_{\ell},V_{\alpha})

The corresponding local conditions for

For

•

The

For

we put

\mathrm{H}^{1}_{\mathcal{F}_{\circ}}(\mathbb{Q}_{\ell},V_{\alpha}):=\begin{cases}\mathrm{H}^{1}_{\circ}(\mathbb{Q}_{p},V_{\alpha})&\textrm{if $\ell=p$,}\\[1.99997pt]
\mathrm{H}^{1}_{\mathcal{F}}(\mathbb{Q}_{\ell},V_{\alpha})&\textrm{else,}\end{cases}

and let

also denote the Selmer structures similarly defined on

,

, and

.

Given a Selmer structure

, and

\mathrm{H}^{1}_{\mathcal{F}(n)}(\mathbb{Q}_{\ell},T_{\alpha})=\begin{cases}\mathrm{H}^{1}_{\rm tr}(\mathbb{Q}_{\ell},T_{\alpha})&\text{if }\ell\mid n,\\[1.99997pt]
\mathrm{H}^{1}_{\mathcal{F}}(\mathbb{Q}_{\ell},T_{\alpha})&\text{if }\ell\nmid n.\end{cases}

Also, for

(so

be the Selmer structure on

We define the Bloch–Kato Selmer structure

Later on, in the proof of Proposition

\displaystyle\geq\operatorname{ind}(\kappa_{n_{\rho}})+\sum_{i=1}^{\rho}d_{i}(\mathrm{H}^{1}_{\mathcal{F}^{*}})+\sum_{i=\rho+1}^{s}d_{i}(\mathrm{H}^{1}_{\mathcal{F}^{*}})\geq\length_{R}(\mathrm{H}^{1}_{\mathcal{F}^{*}}).
