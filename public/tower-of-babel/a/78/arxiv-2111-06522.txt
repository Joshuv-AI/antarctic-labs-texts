Abstract
Let ( 𝒞 , 𝔼 , 𝔰 ) (\mathscr{C},\mathbb{E},\mathfrak{s}) be an Ext -finite, Krull-Schmidt and k k -linear n n -exangulated category with k k a commutative artinian ring. In this note, we define two additive subcategories 𝒞 r \mathscr{C}_{r} and 𝒞 l \mathscr{C}_{l} of 𝒞 \mathscr{C} in terms of the representable functors from the stable category of 𝒞 \mathscr{C} to the category of finitely generated k k -modules. Moreover, we show that there exists an equivalence between the stable categories of these two full subcategories. Finally, we give some equivalent characterizations on the existence of Auslander-Reiten n n -exangles via determined morphisms. These results unify and extend their works by Jiao–Le for exact categories, Zhao–Tan–Huang for extriangulated categories, Xie–Liu–Yang for n n -abelian categories. Keywords: n n -exangulated categories; Auslander-Reiten n n -exangles; n n -abelian categories; ( n + 2 ) (n+2) -angulated categories; determined morphisms; extriangulated categories 2020 Mathematics Subject Classification: 18G80; 18E10; 18G50

For any pair of objects

-extension

when we indicate

{}_{A}0_{C}=0\in\mathbb{E}(C,A)

\mathbb{E}
-extension
. For any pair of
\mathbb{E}
-extensions
{}_{A}\delta_{C}
and
{}_{A^{\prime}}\delta{{}^{\prime}}_{C^{\prime}}

\delta\oplus\delta^{\prime}\in\mathbb{E}(C\oplus C^{\prime},A\oplus A^{\prime})

.

For any

and
c\in\mathscr{C}(C^{\prime},C)
,
\mathbb{E}(C,a)(\delta)\in\mathbb{E}(C,A^{\prime})\ \ \text{and}\ \ \mathbb{E}(c,A)(\delta)\in\mathbb{E}(C^{\prime},A)
are simply denoted by
a_{\ast}\delta
and
c^{\ast}\delta
, respectively.

Let
{}_{A}\delta_{C}
and
{}_{A^{\prime}}\delta{{}^{\prime}}_{C^{\prime}}
be any pair of
\mathbb{E}
-extensions. A

(a,c)\colon\delta\to{\delta}{{}^{\prime}}
of extensions is a pair of morphisms

and

in
\mathscr{C}
, satisfying the equality

.

Definition 2.1
.

[
10
, Definition 2.7]

Let
\bf{C}_{\mathscr{C}}
be the category of complexes in
\mathscr{C}
. As its full subcategory, define
{\bf{C}}^{n+2}_{\mathscr{C}}
to be the category of complexes in
\mathscr{C}
whose components are zero in the degrees outside of
\{0,1,\ldots,n+1\}
. Namely, an object in
{\bf{C}}^{n+2}_{\mathscr{C}}
is a complex
X_{\bullet}=\{X_{i},d^{X}_{i}\}
of the form

X_{0}\xrightarrow{d^{X}_{0}}X_{1}\xrightarrow{d^{X}_{1}}\cdots\xrightarrow{d^{X}_{n-1}}X_{n}\xrightarrow{d^{X}_{n}}X_{n+1}.

We write a morphism
f_{\bullet}\colon X_{\bullet}\to Y_{\bullet}
simply
f_{\bullet}=(f_{0},f_{1},\ldots,f_{n+1})
, only indicating the terms of degrees
0,\ldots,n+1
.

Definition 2.2
.

[
10
, Definition 2.11]

By Yoneda lemma, any extension
\delta\in\mathbb{E}(C,A)
induces natural transformations

\delta_{\sharp}\colon\mathscr{C}(-,C)\Rightarrow\mathbb{E}(-,A)\ \ \text{and}\ \ \delta^{\sharp}\colon\mathscr{C}(A,-)\Rightarrow\mathbb{E}(C,-).

For any
X\in\mathscr{C}
, these
(\delta_{\sharp})_{X}
and
\delta^{\sharp}_{X}
are given as follows.

(1)

(\delta_{\sharp})_{X}\colon\mathscr{C}(X,C)\to\mathbb{E}(X,A)\ :\ f\mapsto f^{\ast}\delta
.

(2)

\delta^{\sharp}_{X}\colon\mathscr{C}(A,X)\to\mathbb{E}(C,X)\ :\ g\mapsto g_{\ast}\delta
.

We simply denote
(\delta_{\sharp})_{X}(f)
and
\delta^{\sharp}_{X}(g)
by
\delta_{\sharp}(f)
and
\delta^{\sharp}(g)
, respectively.

Definition 2.3
.

[
10
, Definition 2.9]

Let
\mathscr{C},\mathbb{E},n
be as before. Define a category
\AE:=\AE^{n+2}_{(\mathscr{C},\mathbb{E})}
as follows.

(1)

A pair

\AE

and

-attached

(d_{0}^{X})_{\ast}\delta=0~~\textrm{and}~~(d^{X}_{n})^{\ast}\delta=0.

We also denote it by

X_{0}\xrightarrow{d_{0}^{X}}X_{1}\xrightarrow{d_{1}^{X}}\cdots\xrightarrow{d_{n-2}^{X}}X_{n-1}\xrightarrow{d_{n-1}^{X}}X_{n}\xrightarrow{d_{n}^{X}}X_{n+1}\overset{\delta}{\dashrightarrow}.

(2)

For such pairs
\langle X_{\bullet},\delta\rangle
and
\langle Y_{\bullet},\rho\rangle

Definition 2.4
.

[
10
, Definition 2.13]

An
n
-exangle
is an object

in
\AE

(1)

The following sequence of functors

\mathscr{C}(X_{n+1},-)\xrightarrow{\mathscr{C}(d^{X}_{n},\ -)}\cdots\xrightarrow{\mathscr{C}(d^{X}_{0},\ -)}\mathscr{C}(X_{0},-)\xrightarrow{~\delta^{\sharp}~}\mathbb{E}(X_{n+1},-)

.

[

Let
