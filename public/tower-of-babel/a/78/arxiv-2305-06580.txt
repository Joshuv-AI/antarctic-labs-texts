Abstract
. In proving Rellich inequalities in the framework of equalities, N. Bez, S. Machihara, and T. Ozawa obtained some interesting norm inequalities in the spirit of Evans and Lewis that compare the standard Laplacian with its radial and spherical components. In this paper we give a simple unified proof and a strict improvement of these Evans-Lewis inequalities in the subtle dimension three case. Our approach is robust and explains clearly the occurrence of the sharp constant.

1.
Introduction

Denote by
|\cdot|
,
\|\cdot\|_{2}
and
\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle
the usual notations for the Euclidean distance in
\mathbb{R}^{n}
, the
L^{2}
-norm on
\mathbb{R}^{n}
and the inner product for
L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
.
Recall the Rellich inequality:

\|\Delta f\|_{2}^{2}\geq{\mathbf{R}}_{n}^{2}\left\|\frac{f}{|x|^{2}}\right\|_{2}^{2},

(1.1)

where
{\mathbf{R}}_{n}=\frac{n(n-4)}{4}
.
See Rellich
[
Rel56
]
and
[
DH98
,
Yaf99
,
AGS06
,
TZ07
,
KO09
,
GM11
,
CM12
,
Avk18
,
Caz20
,
DLP21
,
KPP21
,
MNSS21
,
BGR22
]

for various extensions in various contexts.
In particular, (
1.1
) for
n=4
becomes trivial.
If we wish to allow general functions in the Sobolev space
H^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
,
it is also necessary to restrict (
1.1
) to
n\geq 5
.
For the Laplace operator
\Delta
, we have the standard decomposition

\Delta=\Delta_{r}+\frac{1}{|x|^{2}}\Delta_{{\mathbb{S}}^{n-1}}=:\Delta_{r}+\Delta_{s},

where
\Delta_{r}=\partial_{r}^{2}+\frac{n-1}{|x|}\partial_{r}
,
with
\partial_{r}=\frac{x}{|x|}\cdot\nabla
being the radial derivative,
and
\Delta_{{\mathbb{S}}^{n-1}}
denotes the Laplace-Beltrami operator on
{\mathbb{S}}^{n-1}
.
Introduce the spherical derivatives

L_{j}=\partial_{j}-\frac{x_{j}}{|x|}\partial_{r},\quad j=1,\cdots,n.

Thus we recognize the following relation

\frac{1}{|x|^{2}}\Delta_{{\mathbb{S}}^{n-1}}=\sum_{j=1}^{n}L_{j}^{2}.

Note that
-\sum_{j=1}^{n}L_{j}^{2}
is non-negative on
L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
.

Recently in
[
MOW17
,
BMO23
]
,
Machihara-Ozawa-Wadade and Bez-Machihara-Ozawa took the unusual perspective of understanding (
1.1
) in the framework of equalities,
and found the following elegant results:
for
n\geq 2
and
f\in C_{0}^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}^{n}\backslash\{0\})
,

\|\Delta f\|_{2}^{2}=\left\|\Delta_{r}f\right\|_{2}^{2}+\left\|\sum_{j=1}^{n}L_{j}^{2}f\right\|_{2}^{2}+2{\mathbf{R}}_{n}\sum_{j=1}^{n}\left\|\frac{L_{j}f}{|x|}\right\|_{2}^{2}+2\left\langle-\sum_{j=1}^{n}L_{j}^{2}f_{*},f_{*}\right\rangle,

(1.2)

where

f_{*}=\partial_{r}f+\frac{n-4}{2}\frac{f}{|x|}.

The last two terms in (
1.2
) are non-negative for
n\geq 4
, i.e.,

\text{{\rm Re}}\,\langle\Delta_{r}f,\Delta_{s}f\rangle\geq 0,

or equivalently,

\|\Delta f\|^{2}_{2}\geq\|\Delta_{r}f\|^{2}_{2}+\|\Delta_{s}f\|^{2}_{2}.

(1.3)

These inequalities were first noticed by Evans and Lewis in
[
EL05
]
.
See
[
Hua23
]
for a simpler proof not relying on the Bez-Machihara-Ozawa-Wadade equalities (
1.2
).

Unexpectedly, the Evans-Lewis inequalities (
1.3
) for
n=3
are very subtle.

Theorem 1.1

(Bez-Machihara-Ozawa)
.

For
n=3
and
f\in C_{0}^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}^{3}\backslash\{0\})
, we have

\|\Delta f\|^{2}_{2}\geq\|\Delta_{r}f\|^{2}_{2}

(1.4)

and

\|\Delta f\|^{2}_{2}\geq\left(\frac{5}{8}\right)^{2}\|\Delta_{s}f\|^{2}_{2}.

(1.5)

Both (
1.4
) and (
1.5
) are sharp.
Our aim here is to give a simple unified proof of Theorem
1.1
,
explaining the occurrence of the constant
\left(\frac{5}{8}\right)^{2}
in a visible way.
The byproducts of our machinery include a strict improvement of (
1.4
)
and a weighted version of (
1.3
) for
n=3
that interpolates (
1.5
) and the improved form of (
1.4
).

Theorem 1.2
.

For
n=3
and
f\in C_{0}^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}^{3}\backslash\{0\})
, we have

\|\Delta f\|^{2}_{2}\geq\|\Delta_{r}f\|^{2}_{2}+\frac{1}{4}\|\Delta_{s}f\|^{2}_{2}.

(1.6)

More generally, for
k_{1}\in[0,1]
,
k_{2}\in[0,25/64]
, and
9k_{1}+64k_{2}\leq 25
, we have

\|\Delta f\|^{2}_{2}\geq k_{1}\|\Delta_{r}f\|^{2}_{2}+k_{2}\|\Delta_{s}f\|^{2}_{2}.

(1.7)

Remark 1.3
.

It is desirable to extend Theorems
1.1
-
1.2
to an abstract framework, say, on homogeneous groups
as in Ruzhansky and Suragan
[

,
RS19
]
.
The latter monograph also contains an excellent account of Hardy and Rellich inequalities.
We choose not to bother ourselves in this short paper to review such a huge literature.

Recall the following spectral computation of norms (

\left\|\sum_{j=1}^{3}L_{j}^{2}f\right\|_{2}^{2}=\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\mu_{k}^{2}\left\|\frac{{\mathbb{P}}_{k}f}{|x|^{2}}\right\|_{2}^{2},

(2.1)

\sum_{j=1}^{3}\left\|\frac{L_{j}f}{|x|}\right\|_{2}^{2}=\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\mu_{k}\left\|\frac{{\mathbb{P}}_{k}f}{|x|^{2}}\right\|_{2}^{2},

Here
