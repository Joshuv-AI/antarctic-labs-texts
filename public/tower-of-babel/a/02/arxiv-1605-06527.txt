Generic freeness in frame bundle prolongations of C ∞ C^{\infty} actions
Scot Adams

Abstract
. Let a real Lie group G G act on a C ∞ C^{\infty} real manifold M M . Assume that the action is C ∞ C^{\infty} and that every nontrivial element of G G has a nowhere dense fixpoint set in M M . We show that, in some higher order frame bundle F F of M M , there exists a G G -invariant meager subset Z Z of F F such that the G G -action on F \ Z F\backslash Z is free. A similar result holds for submanifold jet bundles.

1.
Introduction

P. Olver’s freeness conjecture (in his words) asserts: “If a Lie
group acts effectively on a manifold, then, for some
n<\infty
, the
action is free on [a nonempty] open subset of the jet bundle of order
n
.”
For
local
freeness, see
Theorem 6.14 of
[
Olver
]
,
Theorem 12.1 of
[
A3
]

and Theorem
3.3
below.
For
C^{\infty}
actions, the freeness question
was resolved in
[
A1
]
by a countexample for the additive group
{\mathbb{Z}}
of integers.
Then, in
[
A2
]
,
we exposed a
C^{\omega}
counterexample, also an action of
{\mathbb{Z}}
.
Using Lemma 17.3 of
[
A1
]
,
and following the proof of Theorem 18.1 in
[
A1
]
,
we see that this
C^{\omega}
action of
{\mathbb{Z}}
induces to a
C^{\omega}
counterexample for any
connected Lie group with noncompact center.
There is also a converse: By Corollary 3.2 of
[
A4
]
,
the frame bundle freeness conjecture holds
(in its
C^{\infty}
form and, consequently, in its
C^{\omega}
form)
for any connected Lie group with compact center.
So, for many important Lie groups, the frame bundle freeness conjecture holds;
for many others, frame bundle freeness
(and, consequently, submanifold jet bundle freeness) fails.
It remains open whether a Lie group exists
for which the submanifold jet bundle freeness conjecture holds.

For general Lie groups, we now turn our attention to
generic
freeness.
In Theorem 20 of
[
AdOlv
]
,
we proved that any effective
C^{\omega}
action of a connected real Lie group
on a connected
C^{\omega}
real manifold has a frame bundle prolongation
that is generically free in some nonempty open subset of the total space.
In Theorem
9.1
below,
we generalize this result to
C^{\infty}
actions
of arbitrary Lie groups on arbitrary
C^{\infty}
real manifolds
and prove generic freeness on the entire total space.

In Theorem 26 of
[
AdOlv
]
,
we proved a
C^{\omega}
generic freeness result for submanifold jet bundles,
though only for connected groups,
and only on a nonempty open set in the total space.
In Corollary
9.2
below,
we improve that to
C^{\infty}
, to arbitrary groups, and to the entire total space.

The results in Sections 3-7 are elementary.
Our main results are Theorem
8.1

and Theorem
9.1
.

On any principal bundle, the structure group action is

Throughout this paper, a subset of a topological space acquires, without comment,

.
For all

.

.

be a manifold.
We will denote the tangent bundle of

;

-action on

.

.

.

3.
Miscellaneous results

LEMMA 3.1
.

Let a Lie group
G
have a
C^{\infty}
action on a manifold
M
.
Let
k\in{\mathbb{N}}
,
X:={\mathcal{F}}^{k}M
,
W:={\mathcal{F}}^{k-1}M
,
x\in X
.
Let
\sigma:X\to W
be the canonical map,
w:=\sigma(x)
,
v\in T_{w}W
.
Then
\hbox{Stab}_{G}(x)\subseteq\hbox{Stab}_{G}(v)
.

Proof.

Let
g\in\hbox{Stab}_{G}(x)
be given.
We wish to show that
g\in\hbox{Stab}_{G}(v)
.

Let
y:=\Psi_{M}^{1,k-1}(x)\in{\mathcal{F}}^{1}_{w}W
.
Let
r:=\hbox{dim\,}T_{w}W
.
Identify
y\in{\mathcal{F}}^{1}_{w}W
with an ordered basis of
T_{w}W
,
and then fix
u_{1},\ldots,u_{r}\in T_{w}W
such that
y=(u_{1},\ldots,u_{r})
.
Then
\{u_{1},\ldots,u_{r}\}
is a basis for
T_{w}W
,
and so, as
v\in T_{w}W
, choose
c_{1},\ldots,c_{r}\in{\mathbb{R}}
such that
v=c_{1}u_{1}+\cdots+c_{r}u_{r}
.
Since
g\in\hbox{Stab}_{G}(x)
, we see that
gx=x
.
By naturality, the map
\Psi_{M}^{1,k-1}:X\to{\mathcal{F}}^{1}W
is
G
-equivariant.
So, since
gx=x
, we get
gy=y
.
So, since
y=(u_{1},\ldots,u_{r})
,
we get
(gu_{1},\ldots,gu_{r})=(u_{1},\ldots,u_{r})
.
Then

gv=g(c_{1}u_{1}+\cdots+c_{r}u_{r})=c_{1}(gu_{1})+\cdots+c_{r}(gu_{r})=c_{1}u_{1}+\cdots+c_{r}u_{r}=v
,
and so
g\in\hbox{Stab}_{G}(v)
, as desired.
∎

LEMMA 3.2
.

Let a Lie group
G
have a
C^{\infty}
action on a manifold
M
.
Let

,

.

.

For all

.
Then
G_{p}
is discrete,

is discrete,

Also, by Lemma 4.1 of

]

(with

),

.
Then
\ker[(df)_{a}]

(df)_{a}:T_{a}G\to T_{ap}M

∎

.

Let a Lie group

C^{\infty}

.

.

.

If

.
We assume

By Theorem 12.1 of
[

]
,

-invariant dense open subset

-action on

.
