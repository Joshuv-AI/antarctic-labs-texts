Abstract
From the literature, it is known that the choice of basis functions in h ​ p \mathchar 29032\mathchar 29040 -FEM heavily influences the computational cost in order to obtain an approximate solution. Depending on the choice of the reference element, suitable tensor product like basis functions of Jacobi polynomials with different weights lead to optimal properties due to condition number and sparsity. This paper presents biorthogonal basis functions to the primal basis functions mentioned above. The authors investigate hypercubes and simplices as reference elements, as well as the cases of H 1 \mathchar 29000^{\mathchar 28721} and H ⁡ ( curl ) \mathchar 29000\delimiter 67273472\Curl\delimiter 84054785 . The functions can be expressed sums of tensor products of Jacobi polynomials with maximal two summands.

Finite elements, Numerical methods for partial differential equations, orthogonal polynomials
