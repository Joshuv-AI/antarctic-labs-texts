Evaluation of some alternating series involving the binomial coefficients C ⁡ ( 4 ​ n , 2 ​ n ) C(4n,2n)


Abstract
We evaluate in closed form several alternating infinite series involving the binomial coefficients C ⁡ ( 4 ​ n , 2 ​ n ) C(4n,2n) and C ⁡ ( 4 ​ n + 2 , 2 ​ n + 1 ) C(4n+2,2n+1) in the denominator. One of our results generalizes an identity that was obtained experimentally by Sprugnoli in 2006.

1
Motivation

By using a bisection formula and a Computer Algebra System, R. Sprugnoli
[
10
]
evaluated the constant

\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}{(-1)^{n}\binom{4n}{2n}^{-1}}\approx.84660943.

According to Sprugnoli, his result is a rather complicated formula, containing complex numbers; which he was however able to reduce to the following expression, containing only real quantities:

\displaystyle\begin{split}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}&(-1)^{n}\binom{4n}{2n}^{-1}\\
&\hskip-19.91684pt=\frac{16}{17}+\frac{2\sqrt{34}}{289}\!\left(\frac{2(\sqrt{17}-2)}{\sqrt{\sqrt{17}-1}}\arctan\!\Big(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{\sqrt{17}-1}}\Big)+\frac{\sqrt{17}+2}{\sqrt{\sqrt{17}+1}}\ln\!\Big({\frac{{\sqrt{\sqrt{17}+1}-\sqrt{2}}}{{\sqrt{\sqrt{17}+1}+\sqrt{2}}}}\Big)\!\right)\!.\end{split}

(1)

In this paper, among other things, we will generalize Sprugnoli’s identity (
1
).
In particular, we will show that

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^{n-1}}{n^{2}}\binom{4n}{2n}^{-1}x^{2n}=8\Big(\arctanh^{2}\!{\sqrt{y}}-\arctan^{2}\!{\sqrt{y}}\Big),\qquad|x|\leq 4,

where

y=y(x)=\frac{\sqrt{x^{2}+16}-4}{x}.

For
|x|\leq 4
, we also have

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^{n-1}}{n}\binom{4n}{2n}^{-1}x^{2n}=4\sqrt{y}\,\,\frac{y^{2}-1}{y^{2}+1}\left(\frac{{\arctan{\sqrt{y}}}}{{y+1}}+\frac{{\arctanh{\sqrt{y}}}}{y-1}\right)

and

\begin{split}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(-1)^{n-1}\binom{4n}{2n}^{-1}x^{2n}&=\frac{4y^{2}}{(y^{2}+1)^{2}}+\left(\frac{{(y-1)^{3}}}{(y^{2}+1)^{2}}-\frac{8(y-1)y^{2}}{(y^{2}+1)^{3}}\right)\sqrt{y}\arctan{\sqrt{y}}\\
&\quad+\left(\frac{{(y+1)^{3}}}{(y^{2}+1)^{2}}-\frac{{8(y+1)y^{2}}}{(y^{2}+1)^{3}}\right)\sqrt{y}\arctanh{\sqrt{y}}.\end{split}

(2)

Sprugnoli’s result can be obtained by setting
x=1
in (
2
) in view of
\arctanh x=\frac{1}{2}\ln\!\big({\frac{1+x}{1-x}}\big)
.

Furthermore, evaluating the above series for carefully selected values of
x
will produce additional interesting identities.
For instance, since
y(3)=1/3
, we immediately obtain

\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(-1)^{n-1}\frac{9^{n}}{n^{2}}\binom{4n}{2n}^{-1}

\displaystyle=-\frac{{2\pi^{2}}}{9}+2\ln^{2}\!\big(2+\sqrt{3}\big),

\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(-1)^{n-1}\frac{9^{n}}{n}\binom{4n}{2n}^{-1}

\displaystyle=-\frac{{2\sqrt{3}\,\pi}}{15}+\frac{{4\sqrt{3}}}{5}\ln\big(2+\sqrt{3}\big),

\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(-1)^{n-1}9^{n}\binom{4n}{2n}^{-1}

\displaystyle=\frac{9}{25}+\frac{{4\sqrt{3}\,\pi}}{375}+\frac{{22\sqrt{3}}}{125}\ln\big(2+\sqrt{3}\big).

In addition, since
y({1}/{\sqrt{3}})=(2-\sqrt{3})^{2}
and
\arctan(2-\sqrt{3})={\pi}/{12}
, we have

\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^{n-1}}{n^{2}3^{n}}\binom{4n}{2n}^{-1}

\displaystyle=-\frac{\pi^{2}}{18}+\frac{1}{2}\ln^{2}3,

\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^{n-1}}{n3^{n}}\binom{4n}{2n}^{-1}

\displaystyle=-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{21}\,\pi+\frac{2}{7}\ln 3,

\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^{n-1}}{3^{n}}\binom{4n}{2n}^{-1}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{49}-\frac{10\sqrt{3}}{1029}\pi+\frac{27}{343}\ln 3.

We note that since
\binom{4n}{2n}=(2n+1)C_{2n}
, where
C_{2n}
are Catalan numbers, our results could be stated equivalently in terms of the Catalan numbers. Similar series were studied recently by the authors
[
1
,
2
]
, Chu and Esposito
[
7
]
,
Bhandari
[
4
]
, Chen
[
6
]
, and Stewart
[
11
]
, among others.

We will also establish connections with the Fibonacci and Lucas numbers. As usual, the Fibonacci numbers
F_{n}

and the Lucas numbers
L_{n}
are defined, for
n\in\mathbb{Z}
, through the recurrence relations

F_{n}=F_{n-1}+F_{n-2}
,
n\geq 2
, with initial values
F_{0}=0
,
F_{1}=1
and
L_{n}=L_{n-1}+L_{n-2}
with
L_{0}=2
,
L_{1}=1
.
For negative subscripts we have
F_{-n}=(-1)^{n-1}F_{n}
and
L_{-n}=(-1)^{n}L_{n}
. They possess the Binet’s formulas

F_{n}=\frac{\alpha^{n}-\beta^{n}}{\alpha-\beta},\qquad L_{n}=\alpha^{n}+\beta^{n},\qquad n\in\mathbb{Z},

with
\alpha=(1+\sqrt{5})/2
being the golden ratio and
\beta=-1/\alpha
.
For more information we refer to the On-Line Encyclopedia of Integer Sequences
[
9
]
where the sequences

(F_{n})_{n\geq 0}
and
(L_{n})_{n\geq 0}
are indexed as entries A0100045 and A000032, respectively.

Main results

Theorem 1
.

For
|x|\leq 4
and

y=y(x)=\frac{{\sqrt{x^{2}+16}-4}}{x},

(3)

the following series identities hold true:

\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^{n-1}}{n^{2}}\binom{4n}{2n}^{-1}x^{2n}=8\left(\arctanh^{2}{\sqrt{y}}-\arctan^{2}{\sqrt{y}}\right)\,,

(4)

\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^{n-1}}{n}\binom{4n}{2n}^{-1}x^{2n}=\frac{4\sqrt{y}\,(y^{2}-1)}{y^{2}+1}\left({\frac{\arctan{\sqrt{y}}}{y+1}+\frac{\arctanh\sqrt{y}}{{y-1}}}\right)\!,

(5)

and

\begin{split}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(-1)^{n-1}\binom{4n}{2n}^{-1}x^{2n}&=\frac{4y^{2}}{(y^{2}+1)^{2}}+\left({\frac{{(y-1)^{3}}}{{(y^{2}+1)^{2}}}-\frac{{8(y-1)y^{2}}}{(y^{2}+1)^{3}}}\right)\sqrt{y}\arctan{\sqrt{y}}\\
&\quad+\left({\frac{{(y+1)^{3}}}{{(y^{2}+1)^{2}}}}-\frac{8(y+1)y^{2}}{(y^{2}+1)^{3}}\right)\sqrt{y}\arctanh{\sqrt{y}}.\end{split}

(6)

Proof.

We start with the identity
[
8
, Identity (13)]

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{2^{2n}}{n^{2}}\binom{2n}{n}^{-1}x^{2n}=2\arcsin^{2}x,\quad|x|\leq 2.

(7)

Let
x
be a non-zero real variable. It is not difficult to show that

\Re\Big({\arcsin\big(x\sqrt{i}\,\big)}\Big)=\arctan\sqrt{\frac{{\sqrt{1+x^{4}}-1}}{{x^{2}}}}

(8)

and

\Im\Big({\arcsin\big(x\sqrt{i}\,\big)}\Big)=\arctanh\sqrt{\frac{{\sqrt{1+x^{4}}-1}}{{x^{2}}}},

(9)

where
\Re(X)
and
\Im(X)
denote, respectively, the real and the imaginary part of
X
and
i=\sqrt{-1}
is the imaginary unit.
Writing
x\sqrt{i}
for
x
in (
7
) and taking the real part using (
8
) and (
9
)
and the fact that
\Re(f^{2})=(\Re f)^{2}-(\Im f)^{2}
for arbitrary
f
gives after replacing
x^{2}
with
x

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^{n-1}4^{2n}}{n^{2}}\binom{4n}{2n}^{-1}x^{2n}=8\arctanh^{2}\sqrt{\frac{\sqrt{1+x^{2}}-1}{x}}-8\arctan^{2}\sqrt{\frac{\sqrt{1+x^{2}}-1}{x}}.

Identity (
4
) follows immediately after replacing
x
by
x/4
and simplifying.
Noting that

x\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{1-y^{2}}{1+y^{2}}y,

and differentiating (
4
), after some algebra, the other two results follow.
∎

Remark 2
.

We emphasize that in the formulas of Theorem
1
, the arctangent and inverse hyperbolic tangent functions
can have a complex number as an argument. For instance, as
y(4i)=i
, from (
4
) we have

\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{16^{n}}{n^{2}}\binom{4n}{2n}^{-1}

\displaystyle=8\left(\arctan^{2}\sqrt{i}+\arctan^{2}\sqrt{-i}\right)

\displaystyle=-2\left(\ln^{2}\Big(\frac{i}{\sqrt{2}-1}\Big)+\ln^{2}\Big(\frac{i}{1-\sqrt{2}}\Big)\right)

\displaystyle=\pi^{2}-4\ln^{2}\big(\sqrt{2}-1\big).

More generally,

\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\binom{4n}{2n}^{-1}\frac{x^{2n}}{n^{2}}

\displaystyle=8\left(\arctan^{2}\sqrt{y(ix)}-\arctanh^{2}\sqrt{y(ix)}\right)

\displaystyle=8\left(\arctan^{2}\sqrt{y(ix)}+\arctan^{2}\big(i\sqrt{y(ix)}\big)\right)

\displaystyle=8\left(\arctan^{2}\sqrt{y(ix)}-\frac{1}{4}\ln^{2}\Big(\frac{1+\sqrt{y(ix)}}{1-\sqrt{y(ix)}}\Big)\right)

or

\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\binom{4n}{2n}^{-1}\frac{x^{2n}}{n^{2}}

\displaystyle=8\left(\arctan^{2}\sqrt{Y_{1}(x)}-\arctanh^{2}\sqrt{Y_{1}(x)}\right)

\displaystyle=8\left(\arctan^{2}\sqrt{Y_{1}(x)}-\frac{1}{4}\ln^{2}\Big(\frac{1+\sqrt{Y_{1}(x)}}{1-\sqrt{Y_{1}(x)}}\Big)\right)\!,

where

Y_{1}(x)=\frac{4-\sqrt{16-x^{2}}}{x}\cdot i.

Remark 3
.

Inverting (
3

x(y)=\frac{8y}{1-y^{2}};

so that (

) is equivalent to

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{{(-1)^{n-1}8^{2n-1}}}{{n^{2}}}\binom{4n}{2n}^{-1}\left({\frac{y}{{1-y^{2}}}}\right)^{2n}=\arctanh^{2}\sqrt{y}-\arctan^{2}\sqrt{y}

(10)

which holds for all

, i.e., for all

or

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}{\frac{{(-1)^{n-1}8^{2n-1}}}{n}\binom{4n}{2n}^{-1}}\left(\frac{y}{1-y^{2}}\right)^{2n}=\frac{\sqrt{y}(1-y^{2})}{2(1+y^{2})}\left(\frac{\arctanh\sqrt{y}}{1-y}-\frac{\arctan\sqrt{y}}{1+y}\right),

(11)

as well as

\begin{split}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}&(-1)^{n-1}8^{2n}\binom{4n}{2n}^{-1}\left({\frac{y}{{1-y^{2}}}}\right)^{2n}\\
&\qquad\qquad=(1-y)(1+4y+y^{2})\frac{1-y^{2}}{{(1+y^{2})^{3}}}\sqrt{y}\arctanh\sqrt{y}\\
&\qquad\qquad\quad\,-(1+y)(1-4y+y^{2})\frac{1-y^{2}}{{(1+y^{2})^{3}}}\sqrt{y}\arctan\sqrt{y}+\frac{4y^{2}}{(1+y^{2})^{2}}.\end{split}

(12)

Setting

and using the fact that

Theorem 9
.

For

\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^{n}}{(2n+1)^{2}}\binom{4n+2}{2n+1}^{-1}x^{2n+1}=4\arctanh\sqrt{y}\,\arctan\sqrt{y},

(17)

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(-1)^{n}

\displaystyle\binom{4n+2}{2n+1}^{-1}x^{2n+1}

\displaystyle\qquad=\frac{2y(1-y^{2})}{(1+y^{2})^{2}}+\frac{(1+y)(1-y)^{2}(y^{2}+4y+1)}{(1+y^{2})^{3}}\sqrt{y}\arctan\sqrt{y}

\displaystyle\qquad\quad+\frac{(1-y)(1+y)^{2}(y^{2}-4y+1)}{(1+y^{2})^{3}}\sqrt{y}\arctanh\sqrt{y}.

(18)

Proof.

Use exactly the same ideas as in Theorem
1
. Write
x\sqrt{i}
for
x
in (

)

) and (

Theorem 14
.

For all

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}

\displaystyle(-1)^{n+1}\frac{4n+1}{4n+3}O_{2n}^{(2)}C_{2n}x^{2n}

\displaystyle=\frac{\arctan{\sqrt{z}}+\arctanh{\sqrt{z}}}{24\sqrt{2x^{3}}}\Big(\arctan^{2}{\sqrt{z}}-4\arctan{\sqrt{z}}\arctanh{\sqrt{z}}+\arctanh^{2}{\sqrt{z}}\Big),

In addition, we have for all

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}

\displaystyle(-1)^{n+1}\frac{4n+3}{4n+5}O_{2n}^{(2)}C_{2n}x^{2n}

\displaystyle=\frac{\arctan{\sqrt{z}}-\arctanh{\sqrt{z}}}{24\sqrt{2x^{5}}}\Big(\arctan^{2}{\sqrt{z}}+4\arctan{\sqrt{z}}\arctanh{\sqrt{z}}+\arctanh^{2}{\sqrt{z}}\Big).

Proof.

Starting with (
23
) and noting that
(\sqrt{i})^{-3}=-\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(1+i)
we can write

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(-1)^{n}\frac{4n+1}{4n+3}\frac{O_{2n}^{(2)}C_{2n}}{2^{4n+1}}x^{4n}+i\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(-1)^{n}\frac{4n+3}{4n+5}\frac{O_{2n+1}^{(2)}C_{2n+1}}{2^{4n+3}}x^{4n+2}=g(x)f^{3}(x)

with

g(x)=-\frac{1+i}{6\sqrt{2}}{x^{-3}}\qquad\mbox{and}\qquad f(x)=\arcsin(\sqrt{i}x).

Next, we take the real and imaginary parts for the product on the right-hand side using

\Re f^{3}=(\Re f)^{3}-3\Re f(\Im f)^{2}

and

\Im f^{3}=3(\Re f)^{2}\Im f-(\Im f)^{3}.

This yields

\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(-1)^{n}\frac{4n+1}{4n+3}\frac{O_{2n}^{(2)}C_{2n}}{16^{n}}\,x^{4n}=-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{6x^{3}}\left((\Re f)^{3}+(\Im f)^{3}-3\,\Re f\,\Im f(\Re f+\Im f)\right)

\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(-1)^{n}\frac{4n+3}{4n+5}\frac{O_{2n+1}^{(2)}C_{2n+1}}{16^{n}}\,x^{4n}=-\frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3x^{5}}\left((\Re f)^{3}-(\Im f)^{3}+3\,\Re f\,\Im f(\Re f-\Im f)\right)\!.

Make the transformation
x\mapsto 2\sqrt{x}
and simplify while using (
8

5
Conclusion

This paper was motivated by a challenging and mysterious identity stated without a formal proof by Sprugnoli in 2006.
We have shown that his identity is a special case of a much lager class of identities involving
\binom{4n}{2n}

in the denominators. We have also covered similar series (with and without Fibonacci numbers) and derived various interesting identities.
In our next paper on the topic we will study some related series using the same approach.
