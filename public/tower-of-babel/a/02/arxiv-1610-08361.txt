The Henon-Heiles system defined on canonically deformed space-time


Abstract
In this article we provide canonically deformed classical Henon-Heiles system. Further we demonstrate that for proper value of deformation parameter θ \theta there appears chaos in the model.

2
Classical Henon-Heiles model

As it was already mentioned the Henon-Heiles system is defined by the following Hamiltonian function

\displaystyle H(p,x)\;=\;\frac{1}{2}\sum_{i=1}^{2}\;\left(p_{i}^{2}+x_{i}^{2}\right)+x_{1}^{2}x_{2}-\frac{1}{3}x_{2}^{3}\;,

(4)

with canonical variables
(p_{i},x_{i})
satisfying

\{\;x_{i},x_{j}\;\}=0=\{\;p_{i},p_{j}\;\}\;\;\;,\;\;\;\{\;x_{i},p_{j}\;\}=\delta_{ij}\;,

(5)

i.e., it describes the system of two nonlineary coupled one-dimensional harmonic oscillator models. One can check that the corresponding
canonical equations of motion take the form

\displaystyle\dot{p}_{1}\;=\;-\frac{\partial H}{\partial x_{1}}\;=\;-x_{1}-2x_{1}x_{2}\;\;\;,\;\;\;\dot{x}_{i}\;=\;\frac{\partial H}{\partial p_{i}}\;=\;p_{i}\;,

(6)

\displaystyle\dot{p}_{2}\;=\;-\frac{\partial H}{\partial x_{2}}\;=\;-x_{2}-x_{1}^{2}+x_{2}^{2}\;,

(7)

while the proper Newton equations look as follows

\left\{\begin{array}[]{rcl}\ddot{x}_{1}&=&-x_{1}-2x_{1}x_{2}\\[5.0pt]
\ddot{x}_{2}&=&-x_{2}-x_{1}^{2}+x_{2}^{2}\;.\end{array}\right.

(8)

Besides, it is easy to see that the conserved in time total energy of the model is given by

\displaystyle E_{\rm tot}\;=\;\frac{1}{2}\sum_{i=1}^{2}\;\left(\dot{x}_{i}^{2}+x_{i}^{2}\right)+x_{1}^{2}x_{2}-\frac{1}{3}x_{2}^{3}\;.

(9)

In order to analyze the discussed system we find numerically the
Poincare maps in two dimensional phase space
(x_{2},p_{2})

x_{1}=0

E_{\rm tot}=0.03125

E_{\rm tot}=0.125
,
E_{\rm tot}=0.15125
and
E_{\rm tot}=0.16245

Figures 1 - 6

Final remarks

In this article we provide the canonically deformed Henon-Heiles system, i.e., we define the proper Hamiltonian function as well as we derive the corresponding equations of motion. We also demonstrate (with use of the Poincare section method) that for deformation
parameter
\theta=0.5
and for particular values of control parameter
E_{\rm{tot}}
the analyzed model becomes chaotic.

As a next step of presented here investigations one can consider the canonical deformation of so-called generalized Henon-Heiles systems given by the following Hamiltonian function

\displaystyle H(p,x)\;=\;\frac{1}{2}\left(p_{1}^{2}+p_{2}^{2}\right)+\delta x_{1}^{2}+(\delta+\Omega)x_{2}^{2}+\alpha x_{1}^{2}x_{2}+\alpha\beta x_{2}^{3}\;,

(36)

with arbitrary coefficients
\alpha
,
\beta
,
\delta
and
\Omega
respectively. It should be noted that the properties of commutative models described by function (
36
)
are quite interesting. For example, it is well-known (see e.g.
[
27
]
-
[
30
]
and references therein) that such systems remain integrable only in the Sawada-Kotera case: with
\beta=1/3
and

\Omega=0
, in the KdV case: with
\beta=2
and arbitrary
\Omega
as well as in the Kaup-Kupershmidt case: with
\beta=16/3

\Omega=15\delta

the works in this direction already started and are in progress.
