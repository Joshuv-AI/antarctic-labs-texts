Investigations of c c -Differential Uniformity of Permutations with Carlitz Rank 3
Jaeseong Jeong 1 , Namhun Koo 2 , Soonhak Kwon 1 Email: wotjd012321@naver.com, nhkoo@ewha.ac.kr, shkwon@skku.edu 1 Applied Algebra and Optimization Research Center, Sungkyunkwan University, Suwon, Korea 2 Institute of Mathematical Sciences, Ewha Womans University, Seoul, Korea

Abstract
The c c -differential uniformity is recently proposed to reflect resistance against some variants of differential attack. Finding functions with low c c -differential uniformity is attracting attention from many researchers. For even characteristic, it is known that permutations of low Carlitz rank have good cryptographic parameters, for example, low differential uniformity, high nonlinearity, etc. In this paper we show that permutations with low Carlitz rank have low c c -differential uniformity. We also investigate c c -differential uniformity of permutations with Carlitz rank 3 in detail. Keywords. c c -Differential Uniformity, Carlitz rank, Permutation, Mathematics Subject Classification(2020) 94A60, 06E30

3
Upper bound on
c
-differential uniformity of permutations with low Carlitz rank

There are many differentially
4
-uniform permutations obtained from modifying some points in the multiplicative inverse function(see
[
10

\mathbb{F}_{2^{n}}
. In this section we give an upper bound of

-differential uniformity of such differentially
4

Theorem 3.1
.

Let
F
and
G
be permutations on
\mathbb{F}_{2^{n}}
such that
F(x)=G(x)
for all
x\in\mathbb{F}_{2^{n}}\setminus P
for some nonempty
P\subseteq\mathbb{F}_{2^{n}}
. If
c\neq 1
then
{}_{c}\Delta_{F}\leq{}_{c}\Delta_{G}+\#P
.

Proof.

Let
P_{a}=P\cup\{x+a:x\in P\}
. It is enough to show that
{}_{c}\Delta_{F}(a,b)\leq\frac{\#P_{a}}{2}+{}_{c}\Delta_{F}(a,b)
for all
a\in\mathbb{F}_{2^{n}}^{*}
and
b\in\mathbb{F}_{2^{n}}
.
Then for
x\in\mathbb{F}_{2^{n}}\setminus P_{a}
, all solutions of
{}_{c}D_{a}F(x)=b
are also solutions of
{}_{c}D_{a}G(x)=b
since
{}_{c}D_{a}F(x)={}_{c}D_{a}G(x)
. Thus we have
{}_{c}D_{a}F(x)=b
has at most
{}_{c}\Delta_{G}
solutions in
\mathbb{F}_{2^{n}}\setminus P_{a}
.

Let
x\in P_{a}
. If
{}_{c}D_{a}F(x)=F(x+a)+cF(x)=F(x)+cF(x+a)={}_{c}D_{a}F(x+a)
then we have
0=F(x+a)+cF(x)+F(x)+cF(x+a)=(c+1)(F(x+a)+F(x))
. Since
F
is a permutation, we have
c=1
, which contradicts to the assumption.
This means that

_{c}D_{a}F(x)\neq{}_{c}D_{a}F(x+a)\text{ for all }x\in P_{a}.

(2)

If there are
\frac{\#P_{a}}{2}+1
solutions in
P_{a}
of
{}_{c}D_{a}F(x)=b
for some
a,b\in\mathbb{F}_{2^{n}}
then there is
x\in P_{a}
such that both
x
and
x+a
are solutions of
{}_{c}D_{a}F(x)=b
, a contradiction to
{}_{c}D_{a}F(x)\neq{}_{c}D_{a}F(x+a)
for all
x\in P_{a}
. Hence there are at most
\frac{\#P_{a}}{2}
solutions in
P_{a}
of
{}_{c}D_{a}F(x)=b
.

Therefore,
{}_{c}\Delta_{F}(a,b)\leq{}_{c}\Delta_{G}(a,b)+\frac{\#P_{a}}{2}
for all
a,b\in\mathbb{F}_{2^{n}}
, which completes the proof.
∎

\mathbb{F}_{4}
. By Lemma

. Therefore, we get
{}_{c}\Delta_{F}(\gamma,b)\leq 3
for all

with

.

If
a=\gamma

,

,

If

Theorem 4.7
.

Let
n
be even and
\gamma\in\mathbb{F}_{4}\setminus\mathbb{F}_{2}
. If
c\in\mathbb{F}_{4}\setminus\mathbb{F}_{2}
, then

{}_{c}\Delta_{F}=\begin{cases}3&\text{if }n\equiv 2\pmod{4},\\
4&\text{if }n\equiv 4\pmod{8},\\
5&\text{if }n\equiv 0\pmod{8}.\end{cases}

Proof.

By Theorem
4.4
we have
{}_{c}\Delta_{F}=5
if and only if
n\equiv 0\pmod{8}
. Otherwise we have
3\leq\ _{c}\Delta_{F}\leq 4
by Theorem
4.3
and Theorem
4.4
. By Lemma
2.5
, it is sufficient to consider the case

.

(Case 1) Assume that

. By Lemma
4.6
we have
{}_{c}\Delta_{F}(a,b)\leq 3
for all

and

, since
\tr\left(\frac{c\gamma}{c^{2}+\gamma}\right)=\tr(\gamma^{2})=1
. Hence it is sufficient to consider the case that

4.4

•

{}_{c}D_{a}F(0)\neq{}_{c}D_{a}F(1)

•

{}_{c}D_{a}F(0)\neq{}_{c}D_{a}F(a+1)
since

•

{}_{c}D_{a}F(0)\neq{}_{c}D_{a}F(a+\gamma)

{}_{c}D_{a}F(0)=\ _{c}D_{a}F(a+\gamma)

, a contradiction to
a\in\mathbb{F}_{2^{n}}\setminus\mathbb{F}_{4}

•

{}_{c}D_{a}F(1)\neq{}_{c}D_{a}F(\gamma)

•

{}_{c}D_{a}F(1)\neq{}_{c}D_{a}F(a)
since if
{}_{c}D_{a}F(1)=\ _{c}D_{a}F(a)

{}_{c}D_{a}F(a)\neq{}_{c}D_{a}F(a+1)

Corollary 4.8
.

Let
n
be even and
\gamma\in\mathbb{F}_{4}\setminus\mathbb{F}_{2}
. If
c\in\mathbb{F}_{2^{n}}\setminus\mathbb{F}_{4}
, then
3\leq{}_{c}\Delta_{F}\leq 4
. Furthermore,
{}_{c}\Delta_{F}=3
if
\tr(c\gamma)=\tr(c\gamma^{2})=\tr(c^{-1}\gamma)=\tr(c^{-1}\gamma^{2})=\tr\left(\frac{c}{(c+\gamma)^{2}}\right)=\tr\left(\frac{c\gamma^{2}}{(c+\gamma)^{2}}\right)=\tr\left(\frac{c\gamma}{(c+\gamma^{2})^{2}}\right)=\tr\left(\frac{c}{(c+\gamma^{2})^{2}}\right)=1.

Proof.

By Theorem
4.4
if
\gamma\in\mathbb{F}_{4}\setminus\mathbb{F}_{2}
then
{}_{c}\Delta_{F}=5
if and only if
c\in\mathbb{F}_{4}\setminus\mathbb{F}_{2}
and
n\equiv 0\pmod{8}
. Hence if
c\in\mathbb{F}_{2^{n}}\setminus\mathbb{F}_{4}
then we have
3\leq{}_{c}\Delta_{F}\leq 4
using Theorem
4.3
. If
\gamma\in\mathbb{F}_{4}\setminus\mathbb{F}_{2}
and
c\in\mathbb{F}_{2^{n}}\setminus\mathbb{F}_{4}
then we have
c\not\in\{\gamma,\gamma+1,\gamma^{-1},(\gamma+1)^{-1}\}
. Using
\gamma\in\mathbb{F}_{4}\setminus\mathbb{F}_{2}
and
\tr(1)=0
we check all trace conditions in Corollary
4.5

\begin{array}[]{ll}\tr\left(\frac{\gamma}{c(\gamma+1)}\right)=\tr(c^{-1}\gamma^{-1})=1&\tr(c^{-1}\gamma^{-1})=1\\

Concluding Remark

In this paper, we study
c
-differential uniformity of permutations with low Carlitz rank. We show that a permutation of Carlitz rank
m
has
c
-differential uniformity at most
m+2
. Hence we can see that a permutation of low Carlitz rank has low
c
-differential uniformity. We observe that this upper bound
m+2
on
c
-differential uniformity of permutations with Carlitz rank
m
is tight when
1\leq m\leq 3
. In particular, we investigate
c
-differential uniformity of permutations of the form
Inv\circ(0,1,\gamma)
, which have the same
c
-differential uniformity with some permutations with Carlitz rank
3
. We can see that
3\leq\ _{c}\Delta_{F}\leq 5
, and we characterize the case
{}_{c}\Delta_{F}=5
and give a sufficient condition for
{}_{c}\Delta_{F}=3
. We also give a refined chracterization of
{}_{c}\Delta_{F}
for the special case that
n
is even and
\gamma\in\mathbb{F}_{4}\setminus\mathbb{F}_{2}
.

The proof of an upper bound on
c
-differential uniformity of permutations with Carlitz rank
m
is based on the fact that they are affine equivalent (of degree one) to the inverse function with
m
modified points. Since all permutations modifying a small set of points from
Inv
has low Carlitz rank, we already show that they also have low
c
-differential uniformity. In future studies, we investigate the
c
-differential uniformity of them in detail.
