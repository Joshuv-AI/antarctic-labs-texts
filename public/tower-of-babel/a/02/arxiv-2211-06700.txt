Hypersurfaces in spaces of constant curvature satisfying a particular Roter type equation
Ryszard Deszcz, Małgorzata Głogowska, Marian Hotloś, Katarzyna Sawicz

Abstract
. Let M M be a hypersurface isometrically immersed in an ( n + 1 ) (n+1) -dimensional semi-Riemannian space of constant curvature, n > 3 n>3 , such that its shape operator 𝒜 {\mathcal{A}} satisfies 𝒜 3 = ϕ ​ 𝒜 2 + ψ ​ 𝒜 + ρ ​ I ​ d {\mathcal{A}}^{3}=\phi{\mathcal{A}}^{2}+\psi{\mathcal{A}}+\rho I\!d , where ϕ \phi , ψ \psi and ρ \rho are some functions on M M and I ​ d I\!d is the identity operator. The main result of this paper states that on the set U U of all points of M M at which the square 𝒮 2 \mathcal{S}^{2} of the Ricci operator 𝒮 \mathcal{S} of M M is not a linear combination of 𝒮 \mathcal{S} and I ​ d I\!d , the Riemann-Christoffel curvature tensor R R of M M is a linear combination of some Kulkarni-Nomizu products formed by the metric tensor g g , the Ricci tensor S S and the tensor S 2 S^{2} of M M , i.e., the tensor R R satisfies on U U some Roter type equation. Moreover, the ( 0 , 4 ) (0,4) -tensor R ⋅ S R\cdot S is on U U a linear combination of some Tachibana tensors formed by the tensors g g , S S and S 2 S^{2} . In particular, if M M is a hypersurface isometrically immersed in the ( n + 1 ) (n+1) -dimensional Riemannian space of constant curvature, n > 3 n>3 , with three distinct principal curvatures and the Ricci operator 𝒮 \mathcal{S} with three distinct eigenvalues then the Riemann-Christoffel curvature tensor R R of M M also satisfies a Roter type equation of this kind.
