\displaystyle\operatorname{reg}(M)

\displaystyle=\mbox{max}\,\{j-i\ |\ \operatorname{Tor}_{i}(M,{\mathbb{K}})_{j}\neq 0\}

\displaystyle\operatorname{depth}(M)

\displaystyle=\mbox{min}\,\{i\ |\ \operatorname{Ext}^{i}({\mathbb{K}},M)\neq 0\}.

The following lemmas are often used to compute the depth and regularity of a module or ideal.

\operatorname{depth}(S_{G}/K)=\operatorname{depth}(S_{G\backslash w_{1}}/I_{G\backslash w_{1}})=1+\operatorname{depth}(S_{G_{1}*(G_{2}\backslash w_{1})}/I_{G_{1}*(G_{2}\backslash w_{1})}).

To compute the
\operatorname{depth}(S_{G_{1}*(G_{2}\backslash w_{1})}/I_{G_{1}*(G_{2}\backslash w_{1})})
,
let
T^{*}_{1}=T_{1}
,
T^{\prime*}_{1}=T^{\prime}_{1}
,
T^{*}_{2}=\max\{|W_{2,1}|,\\
|W_{2,2}|-1,|W_{2,3}|,\ldots,|W_{2,k_{2}}|\}
,
T^{\prime*}_{2}=\max\{|W_{2,1}|-1,|W_{2,2}|-1,|W_{2,3}|,\ldots,|W_{2,k_{2}}|\}
and
t^{\prime}=|\{i:T^{\prime*}_{i}\neq T^{*}_{i}-1\}|
.
We distinguish into the following two subcases:

(i) If
t=1
, then
T^{\prime}_{1}=T_{1}-1
. In this case, for the graph
G_{1}*(G_{2}\backslash w_{1})
, we have
t^{\prime}\leq 1
. Thus, by the above case
(1)
and by induction, we have

\displaystyle\operatorname{depth}(S_{G\backslash w_{1}}/I_{G\backslash w_{1}})

\displaystyle=\begin{cases}\operatorname{depth}(S_{G_{1}}/I_{G_{1}})+\operatorname{depth}(S_{G_{2}\backslash w_{1}}/I_{G_{2}\backslash w_{1}}),&\text{if $t^{\prime}=0$,}\\
\operatorname{depth}(S_{G_{1}}/I_{G_{1}})+\operatorname{depth}(S_{G_{2}\backslash w_{1}}/I_{G_{2}\backslash w_{1}})-1,&\text{if $t^{\prime}=1$,}\end{cases}

\displaystyle\geq\operatorname{depth}(S_{G_{1}}/I_{G_{1}})+\operatorname{depth}(S_{G_{2}\backslash w_{1}}/I_{G_{2}\backslash w_{1}})-1.

(ii) If
t=2
, then
T^{\prime}_{1}\neq T_{1}-1
. In this case, for the graph
G_{1}*(G_{2}\backslash w_{1})
, we have
t^{\prime}\geq 1
. By induction, we have

\operatorname{depth}(S_{G_{1}*(G_{2}\backslash w_{1})}/I_{G_{1}*(G_{2}\backslash w_{1})})=\operatorname{depth}(S_{G_{1}}/I_{G_{1}})+\operatorname{depth}(S_{G_{2}\setminus w_{1}}/I_{G_{2}\setminus w_{1}})-1.

In both cases we obtain

\displaystyle\operatorname{depth}(S_{G}/K)

\displaystyle=1+\operatorname{depth}(S_{G_{1}*(G_{2}\backslash w_{1})}/I_{G_{1}*(G_{2}\backslash w_{1})})

\displaystyle\geq 1+(\operatorname{depth}(S_{G_{1}}/I_{G_{1}})+\operatorname{depth}(S_{G_{2}\backslash w_{1}}/I_{G_{2}\backslash w_{1}})-1)

\displaystyle=\operatorname{depth}(S_{G_{1}}/I_{G_{1}})+(1+(|W_{2}|-1)-T^{*}_{2})

\displaystyle\geq\operatorname{depth}(S_{G_{1}}/I_{G_{1}})+(|W_{2}|-T_{2})

\displaystyle=\operatorname{depth}(S_{G_{1}}/I_{G_{1}})+\operatorname{depth}(S_{G_{2}}/I_{G_{2}})-1

where the second inequality holds because of
T^{*}_{2}\leq T_{2}
.
Applying Lemma
1.2
(
1
) and Lemma
1.5
to the exact sequence (
1
), we obtain the desired results.

∎

Next, we are ready to prove another major result of this subsection.

Theorem 3.10
.

Suppose that
G=(G_{1},f_{1})*(G_{2},f_{2})
, where
G_{i}=F_{k_{i}}^{W_{i}}(K_{n_{i}})
is a
k_{i}
-fan graph of

on the set
W_{i}
with

and
k_{i}\geq 1
for

.

be the neighbor of
f_{i}
in
G_{i}
and
t=|\{i\in[2]:\operatorname{reg}(S_{G_{i}\backslash v_{i}}/I_{G_{i}\backslash v_{i}})\neq\operatorname{reg}(S_{G_{i}}/I_{G_{i}})\}|
. We have

(1)

If
t\leq 1
, then
\operatorname{reg}(S_{G}/I_{G})=\operatorname{reg}(S_{G_{1}}/I_{G_{1}})+\operatorname{reg}(S_{G_{2}}/I_{G_{2}})

(2)

\operatorname{reg}(S_{G}/I_{G})=\operatorname{reg}(S_{G_{1}}/I_{G_{1}})+\operatorname{reg}(S_{G_{2}}/I_{G_{2}}),

(3)

and

\operatorname{reg}(S_{G}/I_{G})=\operatorname{reg}(S_{G_{1}}/I_{G_{1}})+\operatorname{reg}(S_{G_{2}}/I_{G_{2}})-1.

(2) If

\operatorname{reg}(S_{G_{i}\backslash v_{i}}/I_{G_{i}\backslash v_{i}})\neq\operatorname{reg}(S_{G_{i}}/I_{G_{i}})
