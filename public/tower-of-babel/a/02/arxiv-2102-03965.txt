Stable diffeomorphism classification of some unorientable 4-manifolds
Arun Debray

Abstract
. Kreck’s modified surgery theory reduces the classification of closed, connected 4-manifolds, up to connect sum with some number of copies of S 2 × S 2 S^{2}\times S^{2} , to a series of bordism questions. We implement this in the case of unorientable 4-manifolds M M and show that for some choices of fundamental groups, the computations simplify considerably. We use this to solve some cases in which π 1 ​ ( M ) \pi_{1}(M) is finite of order 2 mod 4: under an assumption on cohomology, there are nine stable diffeomorphism classes for which M M is pin + , one stable diffeomorphism class for which M M is pin - , and four stable diffeomorphism classes for which M M is neither. We also determine the corresponding stable homeomorphism classes.
