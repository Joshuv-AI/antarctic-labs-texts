Log adjunction: moduli part
V.V. Shokurov

Abstract
Upper moduli part of adjunction is introduced and its basic property are discussed. The moduli part satisfies the BP in the case of rational multiplicities and is nef in the maximal case.

\mathcal{B}^{\prime}_{X^{\prime}/Z^{\prime}},\mathcal{B}^{\prime}_{X^{\prime}/Y^{\prime}},\mathcal{M}_{Y,Z}

denotes respectively the moduli part of adjunction for

(X^{\prime}/Z^{\prime},B^{\prime}),(X^{\prime}/Y^{\prime},B^{\prime}),(Y/Z,\mathbb{B}^{\prime}{}_{\mathrm{div}})
.
However, in nonstable situation we consider a moduli part and
divisorial part of adjunction as b-divisors.
Since
\mathcal{B}^{\prime}_{X^{\prime}/Y^{\prime}}\leq\mathcal{D}{}^{\mathrm{mm}}
, it is enough to verify that

\mathcal{M}_{Y/Z}\leq 0
for appropriate canonical b-divisors of
Y,Z

(cf. Example
1
, (2)).

By definition, construction and the lc property of
\mathbb{B}^{\prime}{}_{\mathrm{div}}
we can suppose that

\mathbb{B}^{\prime}{}_{\mathrm{div}}\leq\overline{D}
and
=\overline{0}
over
Y\setminus D

(cf. Theorem
2
).
By General property (2) after increasing we can suppose that
the divisorial part of adjunction
\mathbb{D}^{\prime}
for
(Y/Z,\mathbb{B}^{\prime}{}_{\mathrm{div}})
is

\overline{D_{Z}}
.
Since
Y/Z
is toroidal

\mathcal{M}_{Y/Z}=\mathbb{K}_{Y}+\mathbb{B}^{\prime}{}_{\mathrm{div}}-g^{\circ}(\mathbb{K}_{Z}+\overline{D_{Z}})=\mathbb{K}_{Y}+\mathbb{B}^{\prime}{}_{\mathrm{div}}-\mathbb{K}_{Y}-\overline{D}=\mathbb{B}^{\prime}{}_{\mathrm{div}}-\overline{D}\leq 0

holds.
This concludes the proof of maximal property.

In other words,
\mathcal{D}{}^{\mathrm{mm}}
is the positive b-divisor of
a relative lc b-divisor.
