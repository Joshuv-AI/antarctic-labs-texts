Abstract
The theory of path homology for digraphs was developed by Alexander Grigor’yan, Yong Lin, Yuri Muranov, and Shing-Tung Yau. In this paper, we consider the differential algebras on digraphs and define the parametrized homology of digraphs as an analog of the path homology. We prove the functoriality of the parametrized homology of digraphs in Theorem 4.2 . We also prove some Künneth-type formulae for the parametrized homology of digraphs in Theorem 4.4 .

\displaystyle{\rm Inf}_{\bullet}(G,\alpha,q)

\displaystyle:=

\displaystyle{\rm Inf}_{\bullet}(\Lambda_{*}(G;R),\Lambda_{*}(V;R),\alpha,q),

\displaystyle{\rm Sup}_{\bullet}(G,\alpha,q)

\displaystyle:=

\displaystyle{\rm Sup}_{\bullet}(\Lambda_{*}(G;R),\Lambda_{*}(V;R),\alpha,q).

We define the
(\alpha,q)
-parametrized path homology groups
of

by

\displaystyle H_{*}(G,\alpha,q):=H_{*}(\Lambda_{*}(G;R),\Lambda_{*}(V;R),\alpha,q).

(4.5)

We have an induced morphism of homology groups

\displaystyle\beta_{*}:H_{*}(G,\alpha,q)\longrightarrow H_{*}(G,\alpha,q-r).

(4.6)

Similarly, for any
\bar{\alpha}\in\wedge^{p}R^{*}(V)
, where
p

\bar{\beta}\in\wedge^{r}R^{*}(V)
, where

there is are chain complexes

\displaystyle:=

\displaystyle{\rm Sup}_{\bullet}(G,\bar{\alpha},q)

\displaystyle:=

\displaystyle{\rm Sup}_{\bullet}(\Lambda_{*}(G;R),\Lambda_{*}(V;R),\bar{\alpha},q).

(\bar{\alpha},q)
-parametrized path up-homology groups
of

\displaystyle H^{*}(G,\bar{\alpha},q):=H^{*}(\Lambda_{*}(G;R),\Lambda_{*}(V;R),\bar{\alpha},q).

(4.7)

(4.8)

Remark 2
:

If
G
is transitive, then for any
\alpha\in\wedge^{p}R_{*}(V)
, where
p
is odd, we have

\displaystyle{\rm Inf}_{\bullet}(G,\alpha,q)={\rm Sup}_{\bullet}(G,\alpha,q)

and for any
\bar{\alpha}\in\wedge^{p}R^{*}(V)

\displaystyle{\rm Inf}_{\bullet}(G,\bar{\alpha},q)={\rm Sup}_{\bullet}(G,\bar{\alpha},q).

Remark 3
:

Even for a digraph with a small number of vertices, the rank of the parametrized homology could be large. We do not give any examples explicitly since for the reason of computational complexity.

4.2
Some Künneth-type formulae for the parametrized homology of digraphs

Let
V
and
V^{\prime}
be two disjoint vertex sets.
Let
G=(V,D,s,t)
and
G^{\prime}=(V^{\prime},D^{\prime},s^{\prime},t^{\prime})
be two digraphs. The
join
of
G
and
G^{\prime}
is a digraph

\displaystyle G*G^{\prime}=(V\sqcup V^{\prime},D*D^{\prime},s*s^{\prime},t*t^{\prime})

where

\displaystyle D*D^{\prime}=D\sqcup D^{\prime}\sqcup\{(v,v^{\prime})\mid v\in D,v^{\prime}\in D^{\prime}\}

and for any
e\in D*D^{\prime}
,

\displaystyle(s*s^{\prime})(e)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\begin{cases}s(e)&{\rm~if~}e\in D,\\
s^{\prime}(e)&{\rm~if~}e\in D^{\prime},\\
v&{\rm~if~}e=(v,v^{\prime}){\rm~where~}v\in V{\rm~and~}v^{\prime}\in V^{\prime},\end{cases}

\displaystyle(t*t^{\prime})(e)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\begin{cases}t(e)&{\rm~if~}e\in D,\\
t^{\prime}(e)&{\rm~if~}e\in D^{\prime},\\
v^{\prime}&{\rm~if~}e=(v,v^{\prime}){\rm~where~}v\in V{\rm~and~}v^{\prime}\in V^{\prime}.\end{cases}

Let
n
and
m
be nonnegative integers. We have an
R
-bilinear map

\displaystyle*:\Lambda_{n}(V;R)\times\Lambda_{m}(V^{\prime};R)\longrightarrow\Lambda_{n+m+1}(V\sqcup V^{\prime};R)

sending a pair
(v_{0}\ldots v_{n},v^{\prime}_{0}\ldots v^{\prime}_{m})
of an elementary
n
-path
v_{0}\ldots v_{n}
on
V
and an elementary

m
-path

on
V^{\prime}
to the elementary
(n+m+1)

v_{0}\ldots v_{n}v^{\prime}_{0}\ldots v^{\prime}_{m}

on
V\sqcup V^{\prime}
. The restriction of (
4.13
) to the paths on
G
and
G^{\prime}
induces an
R
-bilinear map

\displaystyle*:\Lambda_{n}(G;R)\times\Lambda_{m}(G^{\prime};R)\longrightarrow\Lambda_{n+m+1}(G*G^{\prime};R).

(4.14)

For any

and a unique
