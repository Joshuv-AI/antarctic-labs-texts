1.
Introduction

We study a mixed local and nonlocal functional whose prototype is

\displaystyle\mathcal{E}_{0}(v;\Omega)\coloneqq\iint_{\mathcal{C}_{\Omega}}\dfrac{|v(x)-v(y)|^{p}}{|x-y|^{n+sp}}\,dxdy+\int_{\Omega}a(x)|Dv|^{q}\,dx,

(1.1)

where
\Omega\subset\mathbb{R}^{n}

(n\geq 2)
is a bounded domain,

\displaystyle\mathcal{C}_{\Omega}\coloneqq(\mathbb{R}^{n}\times\mathbb{R}^{n})\setminus((\mathbb{R}^{n}\setminus\Omega)\times(\mathbb{R}^{n}\setminus\Omega)),

and

\displaystyle s\in(0,1),\quad 1<p\leq q.

(1.2)

Here, the modulating coefficient function
a:\Omega\rightarrow\mathbb{R}
is measurable and bounded such that

\displaystyle 0\leq a(x)\leq\|a\|_{L^{\infty}}\quad(x\in\Omega).

(1.3)

The problem under consideration exhibits a purely nonlocal feature on
\{a(x)=0\}
, but both local and nonlocal features on
\{a(x)>0\}
.
Note that in light of (
1.2
), the
W^{1,q}
-energy has a higher regularization effect than that of the
W^{s,p}
-energy.
In this respect, our problem is deeply related to the following two topics which have been intensively studied currently: mixed local and nonlocal problems and double phase problems.

Mixed local and nonlocal linear operators, like
-\triangle+(-\triangle)^{s}
, naturally appear in the Lévy process; in particular, nonlocal operators, like
(-\triangle)^{s}
, are related to a special case of the so-called purely jump process.
There have been many researches on regularity for elliptic and parabolic equations involving these two kinds of linear operators; we refer to
[
11
,
12
,
13
]
for nonlocal equations and to
[
2
,
17
,
18
,
19
,
34
]
for mixed local and nonlocal equations, respectively.
Note that in those papers, Harnack’s inequality is proved for a solution which is nonnegative in the whole domain
\mathbb{R}^{n}
. This positivity assumption on
\mathbb{R}^{n}
cannot be relaxed, as shown in
[
38
]
.

Later in
[
29
,
30
]
, Hölder regularity and Harnack’s inequality were established for fractional
p
-Laplacian type equations via the approach using nonlocal Caccioppoli estimates and logarithmic estimates. In particular, a new form of Harnack’s inequality was obtained in
[
29
]
without an extra positivity assumption, which is an extension of the results in
[
39
]
. In
[
23
]
, the results in
[
29
,
30
]
generalized to functions in a fractional De Giorgi class, regarding a wider class of nonlinear nonlocal problems with lower order terms. The approach used in
[
23
]
involves improved Caccioppoli estimates and nonlocal isoperimetric type inequalities. We refer to
[
40
,
41
,
43
,
44
]
for more on regularity results for fractional
p
-Laplacian type problems.

Subsequently, in
[
35
]
the purely analytic approaches in
[
29
,
30
]
were applied to mixed local and nonlocal
p
-Laplacian type equations.
For more on regularity and other qualitative behaviors, see
[
5
,
6
,
10
,
26
,
36
,
47
]
. In particular, the maximal regularity in
[
26
]
was achieved for general problems modeled after

\displaystyle v\mapsto\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\dfrac{|v(x)-v(y)|^{p}}{|x-y|^{n+sp}}\,dxdy+\int_{\Omega}|Dv|^{q}\,dx,\qquad sp<q.

Such mixed local and nonlocal problems have an anisotropic feature, and they naturally link to other kinds of problems, namely nonlocal problems with nonstandard growth. Recently, the methods and results in
[
29
,
30
]
have been extended to nonlocal problems with various nonstandard growth conditions
[

,

,

,

,

,

]
; see also
[

,
15
,

]
for the extensions of the methods in
[
23
]
. Specifically, in
[
9
]
local boundedness and Hölder continuity results were proved for the nonlocal double phase problem

v\mapsto\iint_{\mathcal{C}_{\Omega}}\left(\frac{|v(x)-v(y)|^{p}}{|x-y|^{n+sp}}+a(x,y)\frac{|v(x)-v(y)|^{q}}{|x-y|^{n+sp}}\right)\,dxdy,\qquad 0<s\leq t<1<p\leq q.

They are analogous to those for the local double phase problem

v\mapsto\int_{\Omega}(|Dv|^{p}+a(x)|Dv|^{q})\,dx,\qquad 1<p<q.

We refer to
[
3
,
4
,

,

,

,

]
and references therein for comprehensive regularity results, including gradient regularity, for local double phase problems.
Of particular interest to the present paper are the low regularity results in
[
3
,
20
,
24
]
, which we summarize as follows:

•

If
a(\cdot)\in L^{\infty}_{{\operatorname{loc}}}(\Omega)
and

\displaystyle\begin{cases}p\leq q\leq\frac{np}{n-p}&\text{when }\ p<n,\\
p\leq q<\infty&\text{when }\ p=n,\end{cases}

then
u\in L^{\infty}_{{\operatorname{loc}}}(\Omega)
.

•

If
p\leq n
,
u\in L^{\infty}_{\mathrm{loc}}(\Omega)
and

\displaystyle a(\cdot)\in C^{0,\alpha}_{{\operatorname{loc}}}(\Omega),\quad q\leq p+\alpha,

then
u\in C^{0,\gamma}_{{\operatorname{loc}}}(\Omega)
for some
\gamma\in(0,1)
.

•

If
u
is nonnegative in a ball
B_{9R}\subset\Omega
, and

\left\{\begin{aligned} u\in L^{\infty}_{\mathrm{loc}}(\Omega),\,\,a(\cdot)\in C^{0,\alpha}_{{\operatorname{loc}}}(\Omega),\,\,q\leq p+\alpha\quad&\text{when }\;p\leq n,\\
a(\cdot)\in C^{0,\alpha}_{{\operatorname{loc}}}(\Omega),\,\,\frac{q}{p}\leq 1+\frac{\alpha}{n}\quad&\text{when }\;p>n,\end{aligned}\right.

then

\sup_{B_{R}}u\leq c\inf_{B_{R}}u

for some

.

In this paper, we prove the local boundedness, Hölder continuity and Harnack’s inequality for minimizers of (
1.1
) under the natural assumptions on
s,p,q
and
a(\cdot)
which are analogous to those for local double phase problems. To the best of our knowledge, each of our results are the first one that deals with the functional (
1.1
) to make a systematic study on the regularity of its minimizers in the literature. Moreover, Harnack’s inequality stated in Theorem
1.4
below is a new result even when
a(\cdot)
is constant. One of main points in our problem is the interplay between local and nonlocal phenomena in the double phase structure, which gives rise to several difficulties in combining the local theory with the nonlocal theory.
Indeed, the approaches in the present paper are different from those in
[
9
,
26
]
.
On one hand, since the second term in (
1.1
) is of a local nature, we could not derive the same form of the logarithmic estimate as in
[
9
, Lemma 5.1]
. On the other hand, since (
1.1
) features purely nonlocal behaviors on the set
\{a(x)=0\}
, we could not compare (
1.1
) with a local problem as in
[
26
]
.
We thus develop a different method motivated from the ones in
[
1
,
3
,
23
]
, whose crucial tools include the expansion of positivity results described in Lemmas
4.2
and
4.3
below. For fractional
p
-Laplacian type problems, analogous results are proved in
[
23
, Lemma 6.3]
, but their proofs are not directly applicable to our double phase setting.
To overcome this difficulty, we first make use of the local boundedness of minimizers and the Hölder continuity of
a(\cdot)
to establish an improved Caccioppoli estimate given in Lemma
4.1
below. Then we prove Lemma
4.2
by considering two alternatives, say “the nonlocal phase” and “the mixed phase”.
Moreover, for Harnack’s inequality, we take into account the case that
sp>n
as well, where optimal assumptions on
s,p,q
and
a(\cdot)
accordingly change. We also describe the precise nonlocal contribution of minimizers to our results via nonlocal tails.

1.1
.
Assumptions and main results

We actually consider a general functional of the type

\displaystyle\mathcal{E}(u;\Omega)\coloneqq\iint_{\mathcal{C}_{\Omega}}|u(x)-u(y)|^{p}K_{sp}(x,y)\,dxdy+\int_{\Omega}a(x)F(x,Du)\,dx,

where
F:\Omega\times\mathbb{R}^{n}\rightarrow\mathbb{R}
is a Carathéodory function such that

\displaystyle\Lambda^{-1}|\xi|^{q}\leq F(x,\xi)\leq\Lambda|\xi|^{q}

(1.4)

for some
\Lambda>1
, and
K_{sp}:\mathbb{R}^{n}\times\mathbb{R}^{n}\rightarrow\mathbb{R}
is a symmetric kernel with order
(s,p)
; i.e., it is a measurable function satisfying

\displaystyle\dfrac{\Lambda^{-1}}{|x-y|^{n+sp}}\leq K_{sp}(x,y)=K_{sp}(y,x)\leq\dfrac{\Lambda}{|x-y|^{n+sp}}

(1.5)

for a.e.
(x,y)\in\mathbb{R}^{n}\times\mathbb{R}^{n}
.

Let us introduce relevant function spaces which will be used throughout the paper. We denote by

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}(\Omega)\coloneqq\left\{v:\mathbb{R}^{n}\rightarrow\mathbb{R}\,\,\Big|\,\,v|_{\Omega}\in L^{p}(\Omega)\,\,\text{and}\,\,\mathcal{E}_{0}(v;\Omega)<\infty\right\},

(1.6)

and

\displaystyle L^{p-1}_{sp}(\mathbb{R}^{n})\coloneqq\left\{v:\mathbb{R}^{n}\rightarrow\mathbb{R}\,\,\Big|\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\dfrac{|v(x)|^{p-1}}{(1+|x|)^{n+sp}}\,dx<\infty\right\}.

(1.7)

Then we define minimizers of the functional
\mathcal{E}
as follows.

Definition 1.1
.

We say that
u\in\mathcal{A}(\Omega)
is a minimizer of
\mathcal{E}
if

\displaystyle\mathcal{E}(u;\Omega)\leq\mathcal{E}(v;\Omega)

(1.8)

for any measurable function
v:\mathbb{R}^{n}\rightarrow\mathbb{R}
with
v=u
a.e. in
\mathbb{R}^{n}\setminus\Omega
.

The first theorem is about the local boundedness of minimizers in the case that
sp\leq n
.

Theorem 1.2

(Local boundedness)
.

Assume (
1.2
)–(
1.5
) for the functional
\mathcal{E}
. Suppose that

s,p,q
satisfy

\displaystyle\begin{cases}p\leq q\leq\frac{np}{n-sp}\quad&\text{when}\,\,\,sp<n,\\
p\leq q<\infty\quad&\text{when}\,\,\,sp=n.\end{cases}

(1.9)

Then every minimizer
u\in\mathcal{A}(\Omega)\cap L^{p-1}_{sp}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
of
\mathcal{E}
is locally bounded in
\Omega
.

The second theorem is concerned with the Hölder continuity of bounded minimizers in the case that
sp\leq n
.
For this, we additionally assume the Hölder continuity of
a(\cdot)
:

\displaystyle|a(x)-a(y)|\leq[a]_{\alpha}|x-y|^{\alpha},\quad\alpha\in(0,1]

(1.10)

for every
x,y\in\Omega
.

Theorem 1.3

(Hölder continuity)
.

Assume (
1.2
), (
1.4
) and (
1.5
) for the functional
\mathcal{E}
. Suppose that (
1.10
) holds for
a(\cdot):\Omega\rightarrow\mathbb{R}
. Let
s,p
and
q
satisfy
sp\leq n
and

q\leq sp+\alpha.

Then every minimizer
u\in\mathcal{A}(\Omega)\cap L^{p-1}_{sp}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
of
\mathcal{E}
which is locally bounded in
\Omega
is locally Hölder continuous in
\Omega
. Moreover, for any open set
\Omega^{\prime}\Subset\Omega
, there exists
\gamma\in(0,1)
depending only on
n,s,p,q,\Lambda,\alpha,[a]_{\alpha}
and
\|u\|_{L^{\infty}(\Omega^{\prime})}
such that
u\in C^{\gamma}_{{\operatorname{loc}}}(\Omega^{\prime})
.

Finally, in order to state a nonlocal version of Harnack’s inequality, we define the tail as follows:

{\mathrm{Tail}}(v;x_{0},R)\coloneqq\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}\setminus B_{R}(x_{0})}\dfrac{|v(x)|^{p-1}}{|x-x_{0}|^{n+sp}}\,dx.

(1.11)

For any subset
\Omega_{0}\Subset\Omega
, let us denote

\displaystyle\mathtt{data}(\Omega_{0})\coloneqq\begin{cases}n,s,p,q,\Lambda,\alpha,[a]_{\alpha},\|u\|_{L^{\infty}(\Omega_{0})}&\quad\text{when}\quad sp\leq n,\\
n,s,p,q,\Lambda,\alpha,[a]_{\alpha},[u]_{W^{s,p}(\Omega_{0})}&\quad\text{when}\quad sp>n.\end{cases}

Then we have the following.

Theorem 1.4

(Harnack’s inequality)
.

Assume (
1.2
), (
1.4
) and (
1.5
) for the functional
\mathcal{E}
. Suppose that (
1.10
) for
a(\cdot)
. Let
s,p,q
and
\alpha
satisfy

\displaystyle\begin{cases}q\leq sp+\alpha\quad&\text{when}\,\,\,sp\leq n,\\
q\leq p+\frac{p\alpha}{n}+\frac{(s-1)pq}{n}\quad&\text{when}\,\,\,sp>n.\end{cases}

(1.12)

Let
u\in\mathcal{A}(\Omega)\cap L^{p-1}_{sp}(\mathbb{R}^{n})

\mathcal{E}
which is nonnegative in a ball

\displaystyle\sup_{B_{R}}u\leq c\inf_{B_{R}}u+c\left[R^{sp}{\mathrm{Tail}}(u_{-};x_{0},2R)\right]^{\frac{1}{p-1}}

We can obtain the following result for general minimizers by combining the results of Theorems

First, we show a Caccioppoli type estimate with tail, which plays an important role throughout the paper.

Lemma 3.1
.

Let
u\in\mathcal{A}(\Omega)\cap L^{p-1}_{sp}(\Omega)
be a minimizer of
\mathcal{E}
under the assumptions (
1.2
)-(
1.5
). Then for any ball
B_{2r}=B_{2r}(x_{0})\Subset\Omega
and
0<\rho<\sigma\leq r
we have

\displaystyle\int_{B_{\rho}}

\displaystyle\int_{B_{\rho}}\dfrac{|w_{\pm}(x)-w_{\pm}(y)|^{p}}{|x-y|^{sp}}\dfrac{dxdy}{|x-y|^{n}}+\int_{B_{\rho}}a(x)|Dw_{\pm}|^{q}\,dx

\displaystyle+\int_{B_{\rho}}w_{\pm}(x)\left[\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\dfrac{w_{\mp}^{p-1}(y)}{|x-y|^{n+sp}}\,dy\right]\,dx

\displaystyle\leq\dfrac{c}{(\sigma-\rho)^{p}}\int_{B_{\sigma}}\int_{B_{\sigma}}\dfrac{|w_{\pm}(x)+w_{\pm}(y)|^{p}}{|x-y|^{(s-1)p}}\dfrac{dxdy}{|x-y|^{n}}+\dfrac{c}{(\sigma-\rho)^{q}}\int_{B_{\sigma}}a(x)\left|w_{\pm}\right|^{q}\,dx

\displaystyle+\frac{c\sigma^{n+sp}}{(\sigma-\rho)^{n+sp}}[{\mathrm{Tail}}(w_{\pm};\sigma)]\int_{B_{\sigma}}w_{\pm}\,dx

for some
c=c(n,s,p,q,\Lambda)
, where
w_{\pm}\coloneqq(u-k)_{\pm}
with
k\geq 0
.

Proof.

We only prove the estimate for
w_{+}
, since the proof of the one for
w_{-}
is similar. Choose two radii
\rho_{1},\sigma_{1}
satisfying
\rho\leq\rho_{1}<\sigma_{1}\leq\sigma
and then a cut-off function
\phi\in C^{\infty}_{0}(B_{\frac{\sigma_{1}+\rho_{1}}{2}})
satisfying
0\leq\phi\leq 1
,
\phi=1
on
B_{\rho_{1}}
and
|D\phi|\leq\frac{4}{\sigma_{1}-\rho_{1}}
. We test (
1.8
) with
v=u-\phi w_{+}
. Then, since
u=v
in
\mathbb{R}^{n}\setminus B_{\sigma}
, we have

\displaystyle\begin{split}0&\leq\iint_{\mathcal{C}_{\Omega}}(|v(x)-v(y)|^{p}-|u(x)-u(y)|^{p})K_{sp}(x,y)\,dxdy\\
&\quad+\int_{\Omega}a(x)(F(x,Dv)-F(x,Du))\,dx\\
&\leq\int_{B_{\sigma}}\int_{B_{\sigma}}(|v(x)-v(y)|^{p}-|u(x)-u(y)|^{p})K_{sp}(x,y)\,dxdy\\
&\quad+2\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}\setminus B_{\sigma}}\int_{B_{\sigma}}(|v(x)-v(y)|^{p}-|u(x)-u(y)|^{p})K_{sp}(x,y)\,dxdy\\
&\quad+\int_{B_{\sigma}}a(x)(F(x,Dv)-F(x,Du))\,dx\\
&\eqqcolon I_{1}+I_{2}+I_{3}.\end{split}

(3.1)

Both
I_{1}
and
I_{2}
are estimated in the same way as in the proof of
[
23
, Proposition 7.5]
:

\displaystyle\begin{split}I_{1}+I_{2}&\leq c\int_{B_{\sigma_{1}}\setminus B_{\rho_{1}}}\int_{B_{\sigma_{1}}\setminus B_{\rho_{1}}}\dfrac{|w_{+}(x)-w_{+}(y)|^{p}}{|x-y|^{sp}}\dfrac{dxdy}{|x-y|^{n}}\\
&\quad+\dfrac{c}{(\sigma_{1}-\rho_{1})^{p}}\int_{B_{\sigma}}\int_{B_{\sigma}}\dfrac{\left|w_{+}(x)+w_{+}(y)\right|^{p}}{|x-y|^{(s-1)p}}\dfrac{dxdy}{|x-y|^{n}}\\

&\quad-\frac{1}{c}\int_{B_{\rho_{1}}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\dfrac{w_{-}(y)^{p-1}w_{+}(x)}{|x-y|^{sp}}\dfrac{dydx}{|x-y|^{n}}\\
&\quad+c\frac{\sigma^{n+sp}}{(\sigma_{1}-\rho_{1})^{n+sp}}\left[{\mathrm{Tail}}(w_{+};x_{0},\sigma)\right]\int_{B_{\sigma}}w_{+}\,dx\end{split}

(3.2)

with

.

For

, we note that

, which implies

\displaystyle\begin{split}I_{3}&=\int_{A^{+}(k,\sigma_{1})}a(x)(F(x,Dv)-F(x,Du))\,dx\\

\displaystyle=|Du-(D\phi)w_{+}-\phi(Dw_{+})|^{q}

\displaystyle=|(1-\phi)Dw_{+}-(D\phi)w_{+}|^{q}

for a constant

For

On the other hand, since

\displaystyle I_{2}\leq cr^{n-sp}\left(M^{p}+a^{-}_{B_{r}(z)}r^{sp-q}M^{q}+M{\mathrm{Tail}}(u_{-};z,r)\right).

\displaystyle{\mathrm{Tail}}(u_{+};z,r)

\displaystyle\leq c\left(g_{B_{r}(z)}(M)+{\mathrm{Tail}}(u_{-};z,r)\right).
