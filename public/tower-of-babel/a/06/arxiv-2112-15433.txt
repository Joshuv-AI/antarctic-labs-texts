Abstract
. Grätzer and Lakser asked in the 1971 Transactions of the American Mathematical Society if the pseudocomplemented distributive lattices in the amalgamation class of the subvariety generated by 𝟐 n ⊕ 𝟏 {\bf 2}^{n}\oplus{\bf 1} can be characterized by the property of not having a ∗ * -homomorphism onto 𝟐 i ⊕ 𝟏 {\bf 2}^{i}\oplus{\bf 1} for 1 < i < n 1<i<n . In this article, this question is answered. If you want to know the answer, you will have to read it (or skip to the last section).

A

if and only if

The class of pseudocomplemented distributive lattices is a variety,
\mathcal{B}_{\omega}
. Each subvariety besides
\mathcal{B}_{\omega}
is either
\mathcal{B}_{-1}
(the trivial variety) or
\mathcal{B}_{n}
for

, a variety generated by
{\bf 2}^{n}\oplus{\bf 1}

[

Let

be a variety, and let

be in
\mathcal{V}
. It is an

of

if for all

and

in

and for all one-to-one homomorphisms

and
\alpha_{1}:A\to B_{1}

\mathcal{V}
and one-to-one homomorphisms

and
\beta_{1}:B_{1}\to C
such that
\beta_{0}\circ\alpha_{0}=\beta_{1}\circ\alpha_{1}
. The
amalgamation class
of
\mathcal{V}
,

, is the family of all amalgamation bases of
