Comment on “Quantum trapping and rotational self-alignment in triangular Casimir microcavities”
Zhentao Zhang

Abstract
Küçüköz et al ., Sci. Adv. 10 , eadn1825 (2024) reported an experiment, which shows that conducting plates in the misaligned system separated by a liquid may experience transverse interactions at small separations. We point out that the effects might have been examined in detail in theoretical studies, New J. Phys. 24 , 113036 (2022) and Phys. Lett. A 495 , 129304 (2024).
