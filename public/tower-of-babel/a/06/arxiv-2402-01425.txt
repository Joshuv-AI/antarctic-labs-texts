(4.18)

Replacing

\pounds_{\mathbf{X}}\varsigma

(4.19)

Operating
h^{\prime}

Case I: If

5.
\boldsymbol{\eta}
-Ricci-Yamabe solitons with conformal vector fields

The concept of
\boldsymbol{\eta}
-Ricci-Yamabe soliton comes as a generalization of Ricci-Yamabe soliton and is defined by
[
18
]

\pounds_{\mathbf{X}}g+2\alpha_{1}S+(2\lambda_{1}-\beta_{1}r)g+2\nu_{1}\boldsymbol{\eta}\otimes\boldsymbol{\eta}=0,

(5.1)

where
\alpha_{1},\beta_{1},\nu_{1}
are constant. If
\nu_{1}=0
, then
\boldsymbol{\eta}
-Ricci-Yamabe soliton reduces to a Ricci-Yamabe soliton and for
\nu_{1}\neq 0
, it is called proper
\boldsymbol{\eta}
-Ricci-Yamabe soliton.
This soliton turns into

(i)
\boldsymbol{\eta}
-Ricci soliton if
\alpha_{1}=1,\beta_{1}=0
,

(ii)
\boldsymbol{\eta}
-Yamabe soliton if
\alpha_{1}=0,\beta_{1}=1
,

(iii)
\boldsymbol{\eta}
-Einstein soliton if
\alpha_{1}=1,\beta_{1}=-1
.

Several authors have studied
\boldsymbol{\eta}
-Ricci solitons and Ricci-Yamabe solitons, including (
[
1
]
,
[
2
]
,
[
4
]
,
[
6
]
,
[
19
]
) and many others.

Assume that the
(\verb"k",\boldsymbol{\mu})^{\prime}
-akm
\mathbf{N}^{2m+1}
admits an
\boldsymbol{\eta}
-Ricci-Yamabe soliton with
CVF
. Then from (
5.1
) we have

(\pounds_{\mathbf{X}}g)(U_{1},V_{1})+2\alpha_{1}S(U_{1},V_{1})+(2\lambda_{1}-\beta_{1}r)g(U_{1},V_{1})+2\nu_{1}\boldsymbol{\eta}(U_{1})\boldsymbol{\eta}(V_{1})=0.

(5.2)

Using (
1.1
) in (
5.2
), we get

(5.3)

Setting
U_{1}=V_{1}=\varsigma
in (
5.3

(5.4)

which is a constant. Again, contracting
U_{1}
and

In view of (

(5.6)

and

\nu_{1}=-2m\alpha_{1}(\verb"k"+1).

(5.7)

It is known that in a
(\verb"k",\boldsymbol{\mu})^{\prime}
-akm,
r=2m(\verb"k"-2m)
, a constant. Hence with the help of (
1.5

\rho=0

\alpha_{1}S(U_{1},V_{1})=(\frac{\beta_{1}}{2}r-\lambda_{1})g(U_{1},V_{1})-\nu_{1}\boldsymbol{\eta}(U_{1})\boldsymbol{\eta}(V_{1}),

which represents an

.

If a
(\verb"k",\boldsymbol{\mu})^{\prime}
-akm admits an
\boldsymbol{\eta}
-Ricci-Yamabe soliton with

If a
(\verb"k",\boldsymbol{\mu})^{\prime}

\boldsymbol{\eta}

\lambda_{1}

If a
