Can we understand the decay width of the T c ​ c + T_{cc}^{+} state?
Xi-Zhe Ling, Ming-Zhu Liu, Li-Sheng Geng, En Wang, Ju-Jun Xie

Abstract
Inspired by the recent discovery of a doubly charmed tetraquark state T c ​ c + T_{cc}^{+} by the LHCb Collaboration, we employ the effective Lagrangian approach to investigate the decay width of T c ​ c + → D + ​ D 0 ​ π 0 / D 0 ​ D 0 ​ π + T_{cc}^{+}\to D^{+}D^{0}\pi^{0}/D^{0}D^{0}\pi^{+} and T c ​ c + → D 0 ​ D + ​ γ T_{cc}^{+}\to D^{0}D^{+}\gamma with the assumption that T c ​ c + T_{cc}^{+} is an isoscalar D ​ D ∗ DD^{\ast} molecule. We show that both the T c ​ c → D ​ D ​ π T_{cc}\to DD\pi and T c ​ c → D ​ D ​ γ T_{cc}\to DD\gamma modes contribute to the decay width of T c ​ c T_{cc} , with the former being dominant. The resulting total decay width of about Γ = 63 \Gamma=63 keV is smaller than the experimental decay width obtained from the Breit-Wigner fit of the LHCb data, Γ = 410 ± 165 ± 43 − 38 + 18 \Gamma=410\pm 165\pm 43^{+18}_{-38} keV, while close to the number obtained from the alternative unitary analysis, Γ = 48 ± 2 − 14 + 0 \Gamma=48\pm 2^{+0}_{-14} keV, which supports the molecular nature of T c ​ c T_{cc} .



I
Introduction

Mesons made of a pair of quark and anti-quark and baryons made of three quarks can be well understood in the conventional quark model
Gell-Mann 1964
. Although Quantum ChromoDynamics (QCD) allows for other quark configurations, such as tetraquark, pentaquark, and hexaquark states, their existence were not experimentally confirmed until the charged tetraquark state
Z_{c}(4430)
, with the minimum quark content
c\bar{c}u\bar{d}
, was discovered in 2007 by the Belle Collaboration
Choi et al. 2008
. In 2015, the LHCb Collaboration reported the first pentaquark states
P_{c}(4380)
and
P_{c}(4450)

Aaij et al. 2015
, while the latter was shown to be a superposition of two states,
P_{c}(4440)
and
P_{c}(4457)

Aaij et al. 2019
. In 2020, the LHCb Collaboration discovered the first fully heavy tetraquark state
X(6900)

Aaij et al. 2020a
.
It should be noted that all of these exotic states carry hidden charm number. The first open charm tetraquark states,
X_{0}(2866)
and
X_{1}(2904)
, were only discovered in 2020 by the LHCb Collaboration
Aaij et al. 2020b
.

Since forty years ago, a series of pioneer works have already investigated the likely existence of
QQ\bar{q}\bar{q}
tetraquark states in the quark model, which showed that the stability of a
QQ\bar{q}\bar{q}
tetraquark depends on the mass ratio of
m_{Q}/m_{\bar{q}}

Ader et al. 1982
;
Zouzou et al. 1986
;
Lipkin 1986
;
Heller and Tjon 1987
;
Carlson et al. 1988
;
Silvestre-Brac and Semay 1993
;
Semay and Silvestre-Brac 1994
.
As the ratio is larger, such a multiquark state is more stable. However, due to the uncertainty of
m_{c}/m_{\bar{q}}
, whether the mass of the
cc\bar{q}\bar{q}
tetraquark state is above or below the
DD^{\ast}
mass threshold is unsettled. Later, meson exchange potentials were employed to study the likely existence of
DD^{\ast}
molecules
Manohar and Wise 1993
;
Pepin et al. 1997
, where due to the unknown parameters the existence of
DD^{\ast}
bound states is also uncertain. In 2002, a
doubly charmed baryon was discovered by the SELEX Collaboration
Mattson et al. 2002
, which motivated further theoretical studies on
cc\bar{q}\bar{q}
tetraquark states
Gelman and Nussinov 2003
;
Vijande et al. 2004
.
In 2003,
X(3872)
was discovered by the Belle Collaboration
Choi et al. 2003
, which opened a new era in hadron physics. One of the most promising interpretations of
X(3872)
is a
D\bar{D}^{\ast}
bound state. This has further stimulated theoretical studies on
D^{(*)}D^{(*)}
molecules, i.e.,
T_{cc}

Janc and Rosina 2004
;
Navarra et al. 2007
;
Vijande et al. 2007
;
Ebert et al. 2007
;
Yang et al. 2009
;
Molina et al. 2010
;
Li et al. 2013
;
Feng et al. 2013
;
Luo et al. 2017
;
Wang 2018
;
Junnarkar et al. 2019
;
Maiani et al. 2019
;
Liu et al. 2019
.
In 2017, the LHCb collaboration reported the doubly charmed baryon
\Xi_{cc}

Aaij et al. 2017
, which allows the
cc
-diquark mass precisely extracted by the mass of
\Xi_{cc}
and predicts a doubly charmed compact tetraquark state above the
DD^{*}
mass threshold by 8 MeV
Karliner and Rosner 2017
.
Taking into account the heavy quark symmetry between
\Xi_{cc}
and
T_{cc}
, in Refs.
Mehen 2017
;
Eichten and Quigg 2017
a doubly charmed compact tetraquark state above the
DD^{\ast}
mass threshold was also predicted.

Very recently, the LHCb Collaboration reported the discovery of a doubly charmed tetraquark state
T_{cc}^{+}
with
I(J^{P})=0(1^{+})
, which is found in the
D^{0}D^{0}\pi^{+}
invariant mass spectrum
Aaij et al. 2021a
. Its binding energy with respect to the
D^{\ast+}D^{0}
mass threshold is found to be
\delta=273\pm 61\pm 5^{+11}_{-14}
keV and decay width is
\Gamma=410\pm 165\pm 43^{+18}_{-38}
keV. In the unitarized Breit-Wigner profile that takes into account the effect of mass threshold, the binding energy and decay width change to
\delta=360\pm 40^{+4}_{-0}
keV and
\Gamma=48\pm 2^{+0}_{-14}
keV, respectively
Aaij et al. 2021b
.
The
T_{cc}
state could be either a compact tetraquark state or a hadronic molecule of
DD^{*}
. Since the
T_{cc}
mass is below the mass threshold of
D^{\ast+}D^{0}
by 273 keV, the molecular picture seems more appealing.

In the present work, we revisit the molecular picture for the
T_{cc}^{+}
state. In particular, we study its hadronic and radiative decays to check whether one can obtain a decay width in reasonable agreement with the LHCb measurement. This can serve as a highly nontrivial check on its nature.

II
Theoretical formalism

In our previous work
Liu et al. 2019
, we predicted the existence of two doubly charmed hadronic molecules,
DD^{\ast}
and
D^{\ast}D^{\ast}
, with
I(J^{P})=0(1^{+})
, in the One-Boson-Exchange(OBE) model where the cutoff
\Lambda=
1.01 GeV was fixed by reproducing the binding energy of
X(3872)
, assumed to be a
\bar{D}^{\ast}D
bound state. With the cutoff
\Lambda=
1.01 GeV we obtained one
DD^{\ast}
bound state below the
DD^{\ast}
mass threshold by 3 MeV, which is quite close to the mass of
T_{cc}
. Actually, the experimental mass of
T_{cc}
can be obtained with a slightly smaller cutoff, i.e.,
\Lambda=0.943
GeV, as shown in Ref.
Liu et al. 2020
. Therefore, in the OBE model one can regard the newly observed doubly charmed tetraquark state
T_{cc}
as a
DD^{\ast}
bound state. In the following, we focus on its decay mechanism.
Since
T_{cc}
is doubly charmed, it cannot decay into charmonium states and light mesons and the allowed strong decay modes should contain open charm mesons. However, as the
T_{cc}
mass is below the
DD^{*}
threshold, the decay can only proceed via an off-shell
D^{*}
, which
would heavily suppress its decay width. Weak and radiative decays are also possible, but they are much smaller in comparison with the strong decay.

In this work, we assume that
T_{cc}^{+}
is generated by couple channels
D^{\ast+}D^{0}
and
D^{\ast 0}D^{+}
, and it then can decay into
D^{+}D^{0}\pi^{0}/D^{0}D^{0}\pi^{+}
and
D^{+}D^{0}\gamma
via the tree-level diagrams, as shown in Fig.
1
. In the following, we employ the effective Lagrangian approach to calculate the partial decay widths of
T_{cc}\to D^{+}D^{0}\pi^{0}/D^{0}D^{0}\pi^{+}
and
D^{+}D^{0}\gamma
.

The interaction between the
T_{cc}
state and the
DD^{\ast}
pair is described by the following effective Lagrangian
Dong et al. 2008

\displaystyle\mathcal{L}_{T_{cc}}(x)=ig_{T_{cc}}T_{cc}^{\mu}(x)\int dy\Phi(y^{2})D(x+\omega_{D^{\ast}}y)D^{\ast}_{\mu}(x-\omega_{D}y),

(1)

where
\omega_{D^{\ast}}=\frac{m_{D^{\ast}}}{m_{D^{\ast}}+m_{D}}
and
\omega_{D}=\frac{m_{D}}{m_{D^{\ast}}+m_{D}}
are the kinematic parameters with
m_{D^{\ast}}
and
m_{D}
the masses of
D
and
D^{*}
, and
g_{T_{cc}}
is the coupling between
T_{cc}
and the
{D}^{\ast}D
component. The correlation function
\Phi(y^{2})
is introduced to reflect the distribution of the two constituent hadrons in a molecule, which also renders the Feynman diagrams
ultraviolet finite. Here we choose the Fourier transformation of the correlation
function in form of a Gaussian function

\displaystyle\Phi(p^{2})=e^{-p_{E}^{2}/\Lambda^{2}},

(2)

where
\Lambda
is a size parameter, which is expected to be around 1 GeV
Huang et al. 2020
;
Ling et al. 2021
, and
P_{E}
is the Euclidean momentum. The coupling of
g_{T_{cc}}
can be estimated by reproducing the binding energy of the
T_{cc}
state via the compositeness condition
Weinberg 1963
;
Salam 1962
;
Hayashi et al. 1967
. The condition indicates that the coupling constant can be determined from the fact that the renormalization constant of the wave function of a composite particle should be zero.

For a spin-1 meson, the self energy can be divided into a transverse part and a longitudinal part, i.e.,

\displaystyle\Sigma^{\mu\nu}=g^{\mu\nu}_{\bot}\Sigma^{T}(k_{0}^{2})+\frac{p^{\mu}p^{\nu}}{p^{2}}\Sigma^{L}(k_{0}^{2}),

(3)

with
g^{\mu\nu}_{\bot}=g^{\mu\nu}-\frac{p^{\mu}p^{\nu}}{p^{2}}
.
The compositeness condition can then be estimated from the transverse part of the self energy

Z_{T_{cc}}=1-\frac{d\Sigma_{T_{cc}}^{T}(k_{0}^{2})}{d{k}_{0}^{2}}|_{{{k}_{0}=m_{T_{cc}}}}=0.

(4)

III
Summary and outlook

We studied the decay width of the
T_{cc}^{+}

state in the effective Lagrangian approach. Assuming that the
T_{cc}^{+}
state is a hadronic molecule of
DD^{*}
, we obtained a partial decay width of
T_{cc}\to DD\pi
of about 53 keV and a radiative decay width of about 10 keV. Their sum is much smaller than the central experimental value of the Breit-Wigner fit, but agrees with that of the unitary fit. We argued that although the experimental binding energy favors a molecular interpretation for the
T_{cc}
state, a complete understanding of its decay width is still missing.

The discovery of the doubly charmed tetraquark state
T_{cc}^{+}
may open up another new era for hadron physics, in the same way that the discovery of
X(3872)
did
Choi et al. 2003
. For open charm exotic baryons, it is very reasonable to expect a complete multiplet of doubly charmed
D^{(\ast)}\Sigma_{c}^{\ast}
hadronic molecules, which are more bound than their hidden charm
\bar{D}^{(\ast)}\Sigma_{c}^{\ast}
counterparts
Liu et al. 2020
, consistent with Refs.
Dong et al. 2021
;
Chen et al. 2021a
;
Chen et al. 2021b
. From SU(3) symmetry, one may expect the existence of
D^{\ast}D_{s}
or
D^{\ast}D_{s}
molecules. However, these two systems are found difficult to bind, at least from the perspective of OBE models
Li et al. 2013
. In a series of recent works
Sanchez Sanchez et al. 2018
;
Martinez Torres et al. 2019
;
Wu et al. 2019
;
Wu et al. 2021
, we predicted one
DDK
bound state with isospin 1/2, spin-parity
0^{-}
, and a minimum quark content of
cc\bar{s}\bar{q}
, which can be regraded as the strangeness partner of
T_{cc}
. All these remain to be further studied in more detail both theoretically and experimentally.
