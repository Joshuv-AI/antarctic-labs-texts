Sequences of multiple products and cohomology classes for foliations of complex curves
A. Zuevsky

Abstract
. The idea of transversality is explored in the construction of cohomology theory associated to adapted sequences of multiple products of rational functions associated to vertex algebra cohomology of codimension one foliations on complex curves. Explicit formulas for cohomology invariants results from consideration transversality conditions applied to sequences of multiple products for elements of cochain transversal complexes defined for codimension one foliations. AMS Classification: 53C12, 57R20, 17B69
