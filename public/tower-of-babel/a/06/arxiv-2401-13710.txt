Abstract
. We introduce the class of split regular Hom-Lie superalgebras as the natural extension of the one of split Hom-Lie algebras and Lie superalgebras, and study its structure by showing that an arbitrary split regular Hom-Lie superalgebra 𝔏 {\mathfrak{L}} is of the form 𝔏 = U + ∑ j I j {\mathfrak{L}}=U+\sum_{j}I_{j} with U U a linear subspace of a maximal abelian graded subalgebra H H and any I j I_{j} a well described (split) ideal of 𝔏 {\mathfrak{L}} satisfying [ I j , I k ] = 0 [I_{j},I_{k}]=0 if j ≠ k j\neq k . Under certain conditions, the simplicity of 𝔏 {\mathfrak{L}} is characterized and it is shown that 𝔏 {\mathfrak{L}} is the direct sum of the family of its simple ideals. Keywords : Hom-Lie superalgebra, Root, Root space, Structure theory. 2000 MSC : 17A60, 17A70, 17B22.

Introduction and first definitions

The motivation to study Hom-Lie structures are related to physics and to deformations of Lie algebras (see for instance
[
1

,
13
,

,

,

\mathbb{Z}_{2}
-graded space with a bracket for which the super Jacobi identity is twisted by a homomorphism. This class was introduced by Ammar and Makhlouf in
[
1
]
, where they have shown that the supercommutator bracket defined using the multiplication in a Hom-associative superalgebra leads naturally to a Hom-Lie superalgebra. Hom-Lie superalgebras are generalizations of Lie superalgebras, Lie algebras as well as Hom-Lie algebras, and also they are a particular case of
\Gamma
-graded quasi-Lie algebras introduced by Larsson and Silvestrov in
[
15
]
, who also have shown its relation with discrete and deformed vector fields and differential calculus. We also note that an analogous notion has been considered in other categories like Hom-associative algebras, Hom-alternative algebras, Hom-Leibniz algebras, etc. (see
[
1
,
12
,
16
,
17
,
18
,
20
,
23
]
).

In the present paper we introduce the class of split regular Hom-Lie superalgebras as the natural extension of the one of split regular Hom-Lie algebras (see
[
2
]
) and study its structure presenting them as a direct sum of adequate ideals. We also characterize a simple split regular split Hom-Lie superalgebra which together a relation of equivalence defined in its root system allow us to obtain a final expression as direct sum of simple ideals. Here it is interesting to mention the recent references
[
9
,
10
,
11
,
24
]
where different classes of split Hom-algebras are also studied from the viewpoint of their inner structures.

We briefly describe the content of this paper. In Section 2 we develop connection of roots techniques which becomes the main tool in our study. In Section 3 we apply all of the machinary introduced in the previous section to show that a split regular Hom-Lie superalgebra
{\mathfrak{L}}
is of the form
{\mathfrak{L}}=U+\sum_{j}I_{j}
with

satisfying

Definition 1.1
.

A
Hom-Lie superalgebra

{\mathfrak{L}}
is a
{\hbox{\hueca Z}}_{2}
-graded algebra
{\mathfrak{L}}={\mathfrak{L}}_{\bar{0}}\oplus{\mathfrak{L}}_{\bar{1}}
over a base field
𝕂
endowed with a bilinear product

[\cdot,\cdot]:{\mathfrak{L}}\times{\mathfrak{L}}\to{\mathfrak{L}}

and an even superspace homomorphism
\phi:{\mathfrak{L}}\to{\mathfrak{L}},
satisfying

[x,y]=-(-1)^{{\bar{i}}{\bar{j}}}[y,x]

(1)

(-1)^{{\bar{i}}{\bar{k}}}[[x,y],\phi(z)]+(-1)^{{\bar{i}}{\bar{j}}}[[y,z],\phi(x)]+(-1)^{{\bar{j}}{\bar{k}}}[[z,x],\phi(y)]=0,

(2)

for all homogeneous elements
x\in{\mathfrak{L}}_{{\bar{i}}},y\in{\mathfrak{L}}_{\bar{j}},z\in{\mathfrak{L}}_{\bar{k}},
with
{\bar{i}},{\bar{j}},{\bar{k}}\in{\hbox{\hueca Z}}_{2},
called
skew-supersymmetry
and
super Hom-Jacobi identity
, respectively. Furthermore, if
\phi
is an superalgebra automorphism
{\mathfrak{L}}
is called a
regular Hom-Lie superalgebra
.

Note that
{\mathfrak{L}}_{\bar{0}}
is a Hom-Lie algebra called the even or bosonic part of
\mathfrak{L}
while
{\mathfrak{L}}_{\bar{1}}
is called the odd or fermonic part of
\mathfrak{L}
. The usual regularity concepts will be understood in the graded sense. A
graded subalgebra

A
of
{\mathfrak{L}}
is a graded subspace
A=A_{\bar{0}}\oplus A_{\bar{1}}
of
{\mathfrak{L}}
such that
[A,A]\subset A
and
\phi(A)=A
. An
ideal

I
of
{\mathfrak{L}}
is a graded subalgebra
I=I_{\bar{0}}\oplus I_{\bar{1}}
of
{\mathfrak{L}}
satisfying
[I,{\mathfrak{L}}]\subset I.
A Hom-Lie superalgebra
\mathfrak{L}
is called
simple
if
[{\mathfrak{L}},{\mathfrak{L}}]\neq 0
and its only (graded) ideals are
\{0\}
and
{\mathfrak{L}}
. Throughout this paper we consider regular Hom-Lie superalgebras and denote by
\mathbb{N}_{0}
the set of all non-negative integers and by
\mathbb{Z}
the set of all integers. Finally, we emphasize that the regular Hom-Lie superalgebras
{\mathfrak{L}}
are considered of arbitrary dimension and over an arbitrary base field
𝕂
.
Let us introduce the class of split algebras in the framework of regular Hom-Lie superalgebras in a similar way to the cases of Hom-Lie algebras, Lie algebras and Lie superalgebras, among other classes of algebras. We begin by considering a maximal abelian graded subalgebra
H=H_{\bar{0}}\oplus H_{\bar{1}}
among the abelian graded subalgebras of
\mathfrak{L}.

For any

(5)

(21)

From here, if we have

Equation (

{\mathfrak{L}}_{\epsilon\alpha}\subset I

Now, for

From Equations (

.

Therefore, it just remains to study the case in which there exists
\alpha\in\Lambda

\alpha\in\Lambda_{\bar{0}}\cap\Lambda_{\bar{1}}.

We begin with the following observation.

If there exists some

.

Indeed, by arguing as above with the fact that

. So

From here, as

Given now any

From Equations (

for some

Theorem 4.2
.

Let
{\mathfrak{L}}
be a split regular Hom-Lie superalgebra of maximal length, root multiplicative satisfying
\mathcal{Z}({\mathfrak{L}})=0
and
H=\sum_{\alpha\in\Lambda}[{\mathfrak{L}}_{\alpha},{\mathfrak{L}}_{-\alpha}].
Then
{\mathfrak{L}}
is the direct sum of the family of its minimal ideals, each one being a simple split regular Hom-Lie superalgebra having all its nonzero roots connected.

Proof.

By Corollary
3.1
,
{\mathfrak{L}}=\bigoplus_{[\alpha]\in\Lambda/\sim}{\mathfrak{L}}_{[\alpha]}
is the direct sum of the ideals

{\mathfrak{L}}_{[\alpha]}=H_{[\alpha]}\oplus V_{[\alpha]}=\Bigl(\sum\limits_{\beta\in[\alpha]}[{\mathfrak{L}}_{\beta},{\mathfrak{L}}_{-\beta}]\Bigr)\oplus\Bigl(\bigoplus\limits_{\beta\in[\alpha]}{\mathfrak{L}}_{\beta}\Bigr)

having any
{\mathfrak{L}}_{[\alpha]}
its root system
[\alpha]
with all of its roots connected. Taking into account the facts
[\alpha]=-[\alpha]
and
[{\mathfrak{L}}_{[\alpha]},{\mathfrak{L}}_{[\alpha]}]\subset{\mathfrak{L}}_{[\alpha]}
(see Proposition
3.1
-1) we easily deduce that
[\alpha]
has all of its roots connected through roots in
[\alpha]
. We also have that any of the
{\mathfrak{L}}_{[\alpha]}
is root-multiplicative as consequence of the root-multiplicativity of
{\mathfrak{L}}
and trivially is regular. Finally, is clear that
{\mathfrak{L}}_{[\alpha]}
is of maximal length and
{\mathcal{Z}}_{{\mathfrak{L}}_{[\alpha]}}({\mathfrak{L}}_{[\alpha]})=0
(where
{\mathcal{Z}}_{{\mathfrak{L}}_{[\alpha]}}({\mathfrak{L}}_{[\alpha]})
denotes de center of
{\mathfrak{L}}_{[\alpha]}
in
{\mathfrak{L}}_{[\alpha]}
) as consequence of
[{\mathfrak{L}}_{[\alpha]},{\mathfrak{L}}_{[\gamma]}]=0
if
[\alpha]\neq[\gamma]
(Theorem
3.2
) and
{\mathcal{Z}}({\mathfrak{L}})=0
. We can apply Theorem
4.1
to any
{\mathfrak{L}}_{[\alpha]}
so as to conclude
{\mathfrak{L}}_{[\alpha]}
is simple. It is clear that the decomposition
{\mathfrak{L}}=\bigoplus_{[\alpha]\in\Lambda/\sim}{\mathfrak{L}}_{[\alpha]}
satisfies the assertions of the theorem.
∎

Remark 4.1
.

We finally note that the results in the present paper also hold
for non regular Hom-Lie superalgebras
{\mathfrak{L}}
if we introduce the class of split non regular Hom-Lie superalgebras by asking for
H
not only the condition of being a maximal abelian subalgebra of
{\mathfrak{L}}
but also the one of satisfying that
\phi|_{H}
is a linear bijection from
H
onto
H.

Example 4.1
.

Let us consider the real
{\mathbb{Z}}_{2}
-graded vector space

{\mathfrak{L}}={\mathfrak{L}}_{\bar{0}}\oplus{\mathfrak{L}}_{\bar{1}}
, with
basis

\{e_{1},e_{2}\}\cup\{h_{n},x_{n},y_{n}:n\in{\mathbb{N}},n\geq 2\}

of
{\mathfrak{L}}_{\bar{0}}
and

\{e_{3}\}\cup\{f_{n},g_{n}:n\in{\mathbb{N}},n\geq 2\}

of
{\mathfrak{L}}_{\bar{1}}
given in Example
1.1
. Here the nonzero products on these elements
were induced by the relations:

[e_{2},e_{1}]=e_{1},\hskip 8.5359pt[h_{n},x_{n}]=2n^{2}x_{n},\hskip 8.5359pt[h_{n},y_{n}]=-\frac{2}{n^{2}}y_{n},

[x_{n},y_{n}]=h_{n},\hskip 8.5359pt[y_{n},g_{n}]=\frac{1}{n}f_{n},\hskip 8.5359pt[x_{n},f_{n}]={n}g_{n},

[h_{n},f_{n}]=-\frac{1}{n}f_{n},\hskip 8.5359pt[h_{n},g_{n}]={n}g_{n},\hskip 8.5359pt[g_{n},f_{n}]=h_{n},\hskip 8.5359pt[g_{n},g_{n}]=-2n^{2}x_{n},

[f_{n},f_{n}]=\frac{2}{n^{2}}y_{n}.

If we now consider the superspace homomorphism

\phi^{\prime}:{\mathfrak{L}}\to{\mathfrak{L}}

defined by

\phi^{\prime}(e_{1})=e_{1}
,
\phi^{\prime}(e_{2})=e_{2}
,
\phi^{\prime}(e_{3})=0
and

\phi^{\prime}(h_{n})=h_{n}
,
\phi^{\prime}(x_{n})=n^{2}x_{n}
,
\phi^{\prime}(y_{n})=\frac{1}{n^{2}}y_{n}
,
\phi^{\prime}(f_{n})=\frac{1}{n}f_{n}
,
\phi^{\prime}(g_{n})=ng_{n}

for any
n\geq 2
,
we have that

{\mathfrak{L}}={\mathfrak{L}}_{\bar{0}}\oplus{\mathfrak{L}}_{\bar{1}}
also becomes a split Hom-Lie superalgebra, with a root spaces decomposition as in Equation (
3
), which is not regular since
\phi^{\prime}
is not an automorphism of
({\mathfrak{L}},[\cdot,\cdot])
. However,
\phi^{\prime}|_{H}
is a linear bijection from
H
onto
H.
From here, Remark
4.1
allows us to assert that
{\mathfrak{L}}
admits an analogous decomposition as sum of ideals as the one given in Equation (
14
).
