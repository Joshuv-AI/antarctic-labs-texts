Proving Rho Meson be a Dynamical Gauge Boson of Hidden Local Symmetry
Koichi Yamawaki

Abstract
The rho meson has long been successfully identified with a dynamical gauge boson of the Hidden Local Symmetry (HLS) H local H_{\rm local} in the nonlinear sigma model G / H G/H gauge equivalent to the model having the symmetry G global × H local G_{\rm global}\times H_{\rm local} , with G = [ S ​ U ​ ( 2 ) L × S ​ U ​ ( 2 ) R ] ≃ O ⁡ ( 4 ) , H = S ​ U ​ ( 2 ) V ≃ O ⁡ ( 3 ) G=[SU(2)_{L}\times SU(2)_{R}]\simeq O(4),H=SU(2)_{V}\simeq O(3) , however under a hitherto unproven assumption that its kinetic term is dynamically generated, together with an ad hoc choice of the auxiliary field parameter “ a = 2 a=2 ”. We prove this assumption, thereby solving the long-standing mystery: The rho meson kinetic term is generated simply by the large N N limit of the Grassmannian model G / H = O ⁡ ( N ) / [ O ⁡ ( N − 3 ) × O ⁡ ( 3 ) ] G/H=O(N)/[O(N-3)\times O(3)] gauge equivalent to O ​ ( N ) global × [ O ⁡ ( N − 3 ) × O ⁡ ( 3 ) ] local O(N)_{\rm global}\times[O(N-3)\times O(3)]_{\rm local} , extrapolated to N = 4 N=4 , O ​ ( 4 ) global × O ​ ( 3 ) local O(4)_{\rm global}\times O(3)_{\rm local} , with all the phenomenologically successful “ a = 2 a=2 results” i.e., ρ \rho -universality, KSRF relation and the Vector Meson Dominance, realized independently of the parameter “ a a ”. This in turn establishes validity of the large N N dynamics at quantitative level directly by the experiments. The relevant cutoff reads Λ ≃ 4 ​ π ​ F π \Lambda\simeq 4\pi F_{\pi} for N = 4 N=4 , which is regarded as a matching scale of the HLS as a “magnetic dual” to QCD. Skyrmion is stabilized by such a dynamically generated rho meson without recourse to the underlying QCD, further signal of the duality. The unbroken phase with massless rho meson may be realized as a novel chiral restored hadronic phase in hot/dense QCD.
