Pentagon equations, Voronoï tilings and pure braid groups invariant
Illia E. Rohozhkin

Abstract
We construct ( 2 ​ n − 4 ) × ( 2 ​ n − 4 ) (2n-4)\times(2n-4) matrices corresponding to the motion of points on standard round sphere from the point of view of Delaunay triangulations. We define homomorphism from spherical pure braids on n n strands to the product of these matrices for n > 5 n>5 . Keywords: spherical braid group, Delaunay triangulation, matrices, photography method, pentagon equation. MSC: 57M25, 57M27.

7
Further directions

Among further directions which may appear in further papers is knots invariants constructing.

A braid closure allows to construct knot from the braid. Also it is possible to transform one braid to another by a sequence of Markov movements. The first move is conjugation. The second move is adding a strand to the braid and twisting it once with the last strand.

Having a braids invariant in matrices, we need to find some characteristics of the matrix that are invariant under the first and second Markov movements. It can be a trace or determinant of the matrix. This will allow us to construct an invariant
for knots.
