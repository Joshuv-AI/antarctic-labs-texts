What kind of linearly distributive category do polynomial functors form?
David I. Spivak, Priyaa Varshinee Srinivasan

Abstract
This paper has two purposes. The first is to extend the theory of linearly distributive categories by considering the structures that emerge in a special case: the normal duoidal category ( 𝖯𝗈𝗅𝗒 , 𝓎 , ⊗ , ⊲ ) ({\sf Poly},\mathcal{y},\otimes,\triangleleft) of polynomial functors under Dirichlet and substitution product. This is an isomix LDC which is neither ∗ * -autonomous nor fully symmetric. The additional structures of interest here are a closure for ⊗ \otimes and a co-closure for ⊲ \triangleleft , making 𝖯𝗈𝗅𝗒 {\sf Poly} a bi-closed LDC, which is a notion we introduce in this paper. The second purpose is to use 𝖯𝗈𝗅𝗒 {\sf Poly} as a source of examples and intuition about various structures that can occur in the setting of LDCs, including duals, cores, linear monoids, and others, as well as how these generalize to the non-symmetric setting. To that end, we characterize the linearly dual objects in 𝖯𝗈𝗅𝗒 {\sf Poly} : every linear polynomial has a right dual which is a representable. It turns out that the linear and representable polynomials also form the left and right cores of 𝖯𝗈𝗅𝗒 {\sf Poly} . Finally, we provide examples of linear monoids, linear comonoids, and linear bialgebras in 𝖯𝗈𝗅𝗒 {\sf Poly} .
