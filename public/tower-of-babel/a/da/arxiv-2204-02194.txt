Given a sequence

, its binomial transform is the sequence
\{b_{n}\}

\displaystyle b_{n}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}a_{k},

(1)

with inversion

\displaystyle a_{n}=\sum_{i=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}(-1)^{n-k}b_{k}.

(2)

Writing for brevity
\nabla b_{n}=b_{n}-b_{n-1}
and
\nabla^{0}b_{n}=b_{n}

Lemma 2.1
.

Suppose the sequences
\{a_{n}\}
and
\{b_{n}\}
are defined by the formula (
1
).
Then for every two integers
0\leq m\leq n,
we have

\displaystyle\binom{n}{m}\nabla^{m}b_{n}=\sum_{j=0}^{n}\binom{n}{j}\binom{j}{n-m}(-1)^{n-j}b_{j}

(3)

or , in a shorter form

\displaystyle\nabla^{m}b_{n}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{m}{k}(-1)^{k}b_{n-k}.

(4)

Proof.

The proof of (
4
) is by induction.

When
m=0
this is clearly true. We take
1\leq m<n
. We do the proof for
m+1.

\displaystyle\nabla^{m+1}b_{n}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{m}{k}(-1)^{k}\nabla b_{n-k}=

\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{m}\binom{m}{k}(-1)^{k}b_{n-k}+\binom{m}{0}b_{n}-\sum_{k=0}^{m-1}\binom{m}{k}(-1)^{k}b_{n-k-1}-(-1)^{m}b_{n-m-1}=

\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{m}\binom{m+1}{k}(-1)^{k}b_{n-k}+\binom{m}{0}b_{n}+(-1)^{m+1}b_{n-m-1}=\sum_{k=0}^{m+1}\binom{m+1}{k}(-1)^{k}b_{n-k}

∎

Lemma 2.2
.

Suppose the sequences
\{a_{n}\}
and
\{b_{n}\}
are defined by the formula (
1
).Then for every two integers
0\leq m\leq n
we have

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\binom{k}{m}a_{k}=\binom{n}{m}\nabla^{m}b_{n}

(5)

or , in a shorter form

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n-m}{k-m}a_{k}=\nabla^{m}b_{n}.

(6)

Proof.

The RHS of (
6

4

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{m}{k}(-1)^{k}b_{n-k}=\sum_{k=0}^{m}\binom{m}{k}(-1)^{k}b_{n-k}=\sum_{k=0}^{m}\sum_{l=0}^{n-k}\binom{m}{k}\binom{n-k}{l}(-1)^{k}a_{l}=

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{m}\sum_{l=0}^{n}\binom{m}{k}\binom{n-k}{l}(-1)^{k}a_{l}{=}\sum_{l=0}^{n}\sum_{k=0}^{m}\binom{m}{k}\binom{n-k}{l}(-1)^{k}a_{l}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle Gould:3.49}}{{=}}

\displaystyle\sum_{l=0}^{n}\binom{n-m}{l-m}a_{l}.

∎

Lemma 2.3
.

For any two integers
1\leq m\leq n.

\displaystyle\sum_{k=m}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\binom{k}{m}\frac{(-1)^{k}}{k}=\frac{(-1)^{m}}{m}.

(7)

Proof.

The LHS of (
7

\displaystyle\sum_{k=m}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\binom{k}{m}\frac{(-1)^{k}}{k}=\binom{n}{m}\sum_{k=m}^{n}\binom{n-m}{k-m}\frac{(-1)^{k}}{k}=\binom{n}{m}(-1)^{m}\sum_{j=0}^{n-m}\binom{n-m}{j}\frac{(-1)^{j}}{m+j}=

\displaystyle=\binom{n}{m}\frac{(-1)^{m}}{m}\sum_{j=0}^{n-m}\binom{n-m}{j}\frac{(-1)^{j}m}{m+j}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle Gould:1.41}}{{=}}\binom{n}{m}\frac{(-1)^{m}}{m}\frac{1}{\binom{n}{n-m}}=\frac{(-1)^{m}}{m}.
