Large CP violation in charmed baryon decays
Xiao-Gang He, Chia-Wei Liu

Abstract
We present a systematic numerical study of CP violation in two-body weak decays of antitriplet charmed baryons. In the standard model, the Δ ​ c = 1 \Delta c=1 quark level interactions inducing CP violation for the relevant decays can be grouped into two types, proportional to the CKM matrix elements λ q = V u ​ q ​ V c ​ q ∗ \lambda_{q}=V_{uq}V^{*}_{cq} with q = d q=d and q = b q=b , respectively. Recent studies have shown that with S ​ U ​ ( 3 ) F SU(3)_{F} flavor symmetry, the decay amplitudes, including their strong phases, can be determined by data if contributions from λ b \lambda_{b} are neglected. However, terms proportional to λ b \lambda_{b} must be retained to create interferences that induce CP violation. Some of the λ b \lambda_{b} terms can be recovered in the framework of S ​ U ​ ( 3 ) F SU(3)_{F} flavor symmetry, and the CP-violating rate asymmetry A C ​ P A_{CP} is predicted to be of the order of 𝒪 ⁡ ( 10 − 4 ) {\cal O}(10^{-4}) . We find that final state re-scattering effects, which link λ d \lambda_{d} and λ b \lambda_{b} terms, can help recover the missing terms. The re-scattering effects can enhance CP violation by an order of magnitude, with A C ​ P ​ ( Ξ c 0 → p ​ K − ) − A C ​ P ​ ( Ξ c 0 → Σ + ​ π − ) A_{CP}(\Xi_{c}^{0}\to pK^{-})-A_{CP}(\Xi_{c}^{0}\to\Sigma^{+}\pi^{-}) being as large as 1.87 × 10 − 3 1.87\times 10^{-3} . This makes it promising to observe CP violation for the first time in baryon decays.



The study of CP violation is fundamentally important. It is closely related to the baryon asymmetry in our universe (BAU)
Sakharov:1967dj
. BAU suggests the need to go beyond the standard model (SM), as the CP violation source from Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa (CKM) mixing matrix
Kobayashi:1973fv

observed at LHCb
LHCb:2019hro
;
LHCb:2022lry

Bause:2022jes

Cheng:2019ggx
;
Bediaga:2022sxw
;
