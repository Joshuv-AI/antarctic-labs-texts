On the discrete analogues of Appell function F 4 F_{4}
Ravi Dwivedi, Vivek Sahai

Abstract
In this paper, we study the Appell function F 4 F_{4} from discrete point of view. In particular, we obtain regions of convergence, difference-differential equations, finite and infinite summation formulas and a list of recursion relations satisfied by the discrete analogues of Appell function F 4 F_{4} . AMS Subject Classification: 33C65. Keywords: Appell functions, discrete hypergeometric functions.

\displaystyle F_{4}(a,b;c,c^{\prime};x,y)

\displaystyle=\sum_{m,n=0}^{\infty}\frac{(a)_{m+n}\,(b)_{m+n}}{(c)_{m}\,{(c^{\prime})}_{n}}\,\frac{x^{m}\,y^{n}}{m!\,n!},\quad\sqrt{|x|}+\sqrt{|y|}<1,

(1.1)

where the shifted factorial
(a)_{l}

(a)_{l}=\begin{cases}1,&\text{ if }l=0,\\

In this paper, we study two distinct discrete analogues of Appell function

\displaystyle\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}

\displaystyle=\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}(a,b;c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y)

\displaystyle=\sum_{m,n\geq 0}\frac{(a)_{m+n}\,(b)_{m+n}\,(-1)^{mk_{1}}\,(-t_{1})_{mk_{1}}\,(-1)^{nk_{2}}\,(-t_{2})_{nk_{2}}}{(c_{1})_{m}\,(c_{2})_{n}\,m!\,n!}\ x^{m}\,y^{n};

(1.3)

\displaystyle\mathcal{F}^{(2)}_{4}

\displaystyle=\mathcal{F}^{(2)}_{4}(a,b;c_{1},c_{2};t,k,x,y)

\displaystyle=\sum_{m,n\geq 0}\frac{(a)_{m+n}\,(b)_{m+n}\,(-1)^{(m+n)k}\,(-t)_{(m+n)k}}{(c_{1})_{m}\,(c_{2})_{n}\,m!\,n!}\ x^{m}\,y^{n}.

Discrete Appell function
\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}

Here, and in subsequent sections, we explore the discrete Appell function
\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}

For some fixed values of
k_{1}
and
k_{2}
, the discrete Appell function
\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}
reduces into some classical functions of two variables. In particular, we have

\displaystyle\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}(a,b;c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},0,0,x,y)

\displaystyle=F_{4}(a,b;c_{1},c_{2};x,y),

(2.1)

\displaystyle\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}(a,b;c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},1,0,x,y)

\displaystyle=F_{{0}:{1},{1}}^{{2}:{1},{0}}\left(\begin{array}[]{ccc}a,b:&-t_{1},&-\\
-:&c_{1},&c_{2}\end{array};-x,\ y\right),

\displaystyle\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}(a,b;c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},0,1,x,y)

\displaystyle=F_{{0}:{1},{1}}^{{2}:{0},{1}}\left(\begin{array}[]{ccc}a,b:&-,&-t_{2}\\
-:&c_{1},&c_{2}\end{array};x,\ -y\right),

\displaystyle\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}(a,b;c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},1,1,x,y)

\displaystyle=F_{{0}:{1},{1}}^{{2}:{1},{1}}\left(\begin{array}[]{ccc}a,b:&-t_{1},&-t_{2}\\
-:&c_{1},&c_{2}\end{array};-x,\ -y\right),

where
F_{{l^{\prime}}:{m^{\prime}},{n^{\prime}}}^{{l}:{m},{n}}
is the Kampé de Fériet function defined by,
[
13
]

\displaystyle F_{{l^{\prime}}:{m^{\prime}},{n^{\prime}}}^{{l}:{m},{n}}\left(\begin{array}[]{ccc}A:&B,&C\\
D:&E,&F\end{array};x,\ y\right)

\displaystyle=\displaystyle\sum_{k,p\geq 0}\ \frac{\prod_{i=1}^{l}(a_{i})_{k+p}\,\prod_{i=1}^{m}(b_{i})_{k}\,\prod_{i=1}^{n}(c_{i})_{p}}{\prod_{i=1}^{l^{\prime}}(d_{i})_{k+p}\,\prod_{i=1}^{m^{\prime}}(e_{i})_{k}\,\prod_{i=1}^{n^{\prime}}(f_{i})_{p}}\ \frac{x^{k}\,y^{p}}{k!\,p!},

(2.10)

where
A
denote the sequence of complex numbers
a_{1}
, …,
a_{l}
, etc. and
d_{i},e_{i},f_{i}\neq 0,-1,\dots
.

Now, we examine the convergence of the discrete Appell function
\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}
. Let
\mathcal{A}_{m,n}x^{m}\,y^{n}
be the general term of
\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}
. Then

\displaystyle\left|\mathcal{A}_{m,n}x^{m}\,y^{n}\right|

\displaystyle=\left|\frac{(a)_{m+n}\,(b)_{m+n}\,(-t_{1})_{mk_{1}}\,(-t_{2})_{nk_{2}}}{(c_{1})_{m}\,(c_{2})_{n}\,m!\,n!}\ x^{m}\,y^{n}\right|

\displaystyle<\left|\frac{\Gamma(c_{1})\,\Gamma(c_{2})}{\Gamma(a)\,\Gamma(b)\,\Gamma(-t_{1})\,\Gamma(-t_{2})}\right|

\displaystyle\quad\times\left|\frac{\Gamma(a+m+n)\,\Gamma(b+m+n)\,\Gamma(-t_{1}+mk_{1})\,\Gamma(-t_{2}+nk_{2})}{\Gamma(c_{1}+m)\,\Gamma(c_{2}+n)\,m!\,n!}\right|\,|x|^{m}\,|y|^{n}.

(2.11)

For sufficiently large
m
and
n
, the limiting value of Stirling formula
\lim_{n\to\infty}\Gamma(\lambda+n)=\sqrt{(}2\pi)\,n^{\lambda+n-\frac{1}{2}}\,e^{-n}
yields

\displaystyle\left|\mathcal{A}_{m,n}x^{m}\,y^{n}\right|

\displaystyle<\left|\frac{2\pi\,\Gamma(c_{1})\,\Gamma(c_{2})}{\Gamma(a)\,\Gamma(b)\,\Gamma(-t_{1})\,\Gamma(-t_{2})}\right|

\displaystyle\quad\times\left|(m+n)^{a+b-2}\,m^{1-c_{1}}\,n^{1-c_{2}}\,(mk_{1})^{mk_{1}-t_{1}-\frac{1}{2}}\,(nk_{2})^{nk_{2}-t_{2}-\frac{1}{2}}e^{-(mk_{1}+nk_{2})}\right|

\displaystyle\quad\times\left|\left(\frac{(m+n)!}{m!\,n!}\right)^{2}x^{m}\,y^{n}\right|.

(2.12)

Let
N>\left|\frac{2\pi\,\Gamma(c_{1})\,\Gamma(c_{2})}{\Gamma(a)\,\Gamma(b)\,\Gamma(-t_{1})\,\Gamma(-t_{2})}\right|
. Then

\displaystyle\left|\mathcal{A}_{m,n}x^{m}\,y^{n}\right|

\displaystyle<\frac{N\,(mk_{1})^{mk_{1}-t_{1}-\frac{1}{2}}\,(nk_{2})^{nk_{2}-t_{2}-\frac{1}{2}}}{(m+n)^{2-a-b}\,m^{c_{1}-1}\,n^{c_{2}-1}\,e^{(mk_{1}+nk_{2})}}\,\left|\left(\frac{(m+n)!}{m!\,n!}\right)^{2}x^{m}\,y^{n}\right|

\displaystyle<\frac{N\,(mk_{1})^{mk_{1}-t_{1}-\frac{1}{2}}\,(nk_{2})^{nk_{2}-t_{2}-\frac{1}{2}}}{(m+n)^{2-a-b}\,m^{c_{1}-1}\,n^{c_{2}-1}\,e^{(mk_{1}+nk_{2})}}\,(\sqrt{|x|}+\sqrt{|y|})^{2(m+n)}.

(2.13)

For
k_{1},k_{2}\in\mathbb{N},t_{1},t_{2}\in\mathbb{C}
and
\sqrt{|x|}+\sqrt{|y|}<1
,
\left|\mathcal{A}_{m,n}x^{m}\,y^{n}\right|\to 0
as
m,n\to\infty
. Hence the discrete Appell function
\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}
converges absolutely.

Let
\Theta_{t}:=t\,\rho_{t}\,\Delta_{t}
be the discrete analogue of
\theta=t\,\frac{d}{dt}
, where
\Delta_{t}f(t)=f(t+1)-f(t)
and
\rho_{t}\,f(t)=f(t-1)
. Then, we have

\displaystyle\Theta_{t}\,((-1)^{nk}\,(-t)_{nk})

\displaystyle=n\,k\,(-1)^{nk}\,(-t)_{nk}.

(2.14)

Next, we obtain the difference equations satisfied by
\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}
. We have

\displaystyle\Theta_{t_{1}}\left(\frac{1}{k_{1}}\Theta_{t_{1}}+c_{1}-1\right)\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}

\displaystyle=\sum_{m,n\geq 0}\frac{(a)_{m+n}\,(b)_{m+n}\,(-1)^{mk_{1}}\,(-t_{1})_{mk_{1}}\,(-1)^{nk_{2}}\,(-t_{2})_{nk_{2}}}{(c_{1})_{m}\,(c_{2})_{n}\,m!\,n!}\ x^{m}\,y^{n}\,mk_{1}\,(c_{1}+m-1)

\displaystyle=k_{1}\,\sum_{m,n\geq 0}\frac{(a)_{m+n+1}\,(b)_{m+n+1}\,(-1)^{(m+1)k_{1}}\,(-t_{1})_{(m+1)k_{1}}\,(-1)^{nk_{2}}\,(-t_{2})_{nk_{2}}}{(c_{1})_{m}\,(c_{2})_{n}\,m!\,n!}\ x^{m+1}\,y^{n}

\displaystyle=k_{1}\,\sum_{m,n\geq 0}(a+m+n)\,(b+m+n)\,(-1)^{k_{1}}\,(-t_{1})_{k_{1}}\,x\,\rho_{t_{1}}^{k_{1}}

\displaystyle\quad\times\frac{(a)_{m+n}\,(b)_{m+n}\,(-1)^{mk_{1}}\,(-t_{1}+k_{1})_{mk_{1}}\,(-1)^{nk_{2}}\,(-t_{2})_{nk_{2}}}{(c_{1})_{m}\,(c_{2})_{n}\,m!\,n!}\ x^{m}\,y^{n}

\displaystyle=k_{1}\,(-1)^{k_{1}}\,(-t_{1})_{k_{1}}\,x\,\rho_{t_{1}}^{k_{1}}\,\left(\frac{1}{k_{1}}\Theta_{t_{1}}+\frac{1}{k_{2}}\Theta_{t_{2}}+a\right)\,\left(\frac{1}{k_{1}}\Theta_{t_{1}}+\frac{1}{k_{2}}\Theta_{t_{2}}+b\right)\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}.

(2.15)

Thus, we arrive at

\displaystyle\left[\Theta_{t_{1}}\left(\frac{1}{k_{1}}\Theta_{t_{1}}+c_{1}-1\right)\right.

\displaystyle\quad\left.-k_{1}\,(-1)^{k_{1}}\,(-t_{1})_{k_{1}}\,x\,\rho_{t_{1}}^{k_{1}}\,\left(\frac{1}{k_{1}}\Theta_{t_{1}}+\frac{1}{k_{2}}\Theta_{t_{2}}+a\right)\,\left(\frac{1}{k_{1}}\Theta_{t_{1}}+\frac{1}{k_{2}}\Theta_{t_{2}}+b\right)\right]\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}=0.

(2.16)

Similarly

\displaystyle\left[\Theta_{t_{2}}\left(\frac{1}{k_{2}}\Theta_{t_{2}}+c_{2}-1\right)\right.

\displaystyle\quad\left.-k_{2}\,(-1)^{k_{2}}\,(-t_{2})_{k_{2}}\,y\,\rho_{t_{2}}^{k_{2}}\,\left(\frac{1}{k_{1}}\Theta_{t_{1}}+\frac{1}{k_{2}}\Theta_{t_{2}}+a\right)\,\left(\frac{1}{k_{1}}\Theta_{t_{1}}+\frac{1}{k_{2}}\Theta_{t_{2}}+b\right)\right]\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}=0.

(2.17)

We now give some of the integral representations of the discrete Appell function
\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}
.

\displaystyle\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}(a,b;c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\Gamma(a)}\int_{0}^{\infty}e^{-u}\,u^{a-1}

\displaystyle\quad\times F_{{0}:{1};{1}}^{{1}:{k};{k}}\left(\begin{array}[]{ccc}b:&\frac{-t_{1}}{k},\dots,\frac{-t_{1}+k-1}{k};&\frac{-t_{2}}{k},\dots,\frac{-t_{2}+k-1}{k}\\
:&c_{1};&c_{2}\end{array};(-k)^{k}\,ux,(-k)^{k}\,uy\right)du;

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\Gamma(b)}\int_{0}^{\infty}e^{-u}\,u^{b-1}

\displaystyle\quad\times F_{{0}:{1};{1}}^{{1}:{k};{k}}\left(\begin{array}[]{ccc}a:&\frac{-t_{1}}{k},\dots,\frac{-t_{1}+k-1}{k};&\frac{-t_{2}}{k},\dots,\frac{-t_{2}+k-1}{k}\\
-:&c_{1};&c_{2}\end{array};(-k)^{k}\,ux,(-k)^{k}\,uy\right)du.

To prove (

(a)_{m+n}=\frac{\Gamma(a+m+n)}{\Gamma(a)}
and the integral of

\displaystyle\Gamma(a+m+n)=\int_{0}^{\infty}e^{-u}\,u^{a+m+n-1}\,du.

(2.22)

3
Differential and difference formulae

Theorem 3.1
.

The following difference and differential formulas are satisfied by discrete Appell function
\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}
, where
\theta=x\frac{\partial}{\partial x},\phi=y\frac{\partial}{\partial y}
:

\displaystyle(\Delta_{t_{1}})^{r}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}(a,b;c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},1,k_{2},x,y)

\displaystyle=\frac{(a)_{r}\,(b)_{r}\,x^{r}}{(c_{1})_{r}}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}(a+r,b+r;c_{1}+r,c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},1,k_{2},x,y);

(3.1)

\displaystyle(\Delta_{t_{2}})^{r}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}(a,b;c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},1,x,y)

\displaystyle=\frac{(a)_{r}\,(b)_{r}\,y^{r}}{(c_{2})_{r}}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}(a+r,b+r;c_{1},c_{2}+r;t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},1,x,y);

(3.2)

\displaystyle(\theta)^{r}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}(a,b;c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y)

\displaystyle=\frac{(-1)^{rk_{1}}\,(a)_{r}\,(b)_{r}\,(-t_{1})_{rk_{1}}\,x^{r}}{(c_{1})_{r}}\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}(a+r,b+r;c_{1}+r,c_{2};t_{1}-rk_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y);

(3.3)

\displaystyle(\phi)^{r}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}(a,b;c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y)

\displaystyle=\frac{(-1)^{rk_{2}}\,(a)_{r}\,(b)_{r}\,(-t_{2})_{rk_{2}}\,y^{r}}{(c_{2})_{r}}\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}(a+r,b+r;c_{1},c_{2}+r;t_{1},t_{2}-rk_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y).

(3.4)

Proof.

The action of difference operator
\Delta_{t_{1}}
on the discrete Appell function
\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}
, when
k_{1}=1
, yields

\displaystyle(\Delta_{t_{1}})\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}(a,b;c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},1,k_{2},x,y)

\displaystyle=\sum_{m,n\geq 0}\frac{(a)_{m+n}\,(b)_{m+n}\,(-1)^{m-1}\,m\,(-t_{1})_{m-1}\,(-1)^{nk_{2}}\,(-t_{2})_{nk_{2}}}{(c_{1})_{m}\,(c_{2})_{n}\,m!\,n!}\ x^{m}\,y^{n}

\displaystyle=\sum_{m,n\geq 0}\frac{(a)_{m+n+1}\,(b)_{m+n+1}\,(-1)^{m}\,(-t_{1})_{m}\,(-1)^{nk_{2}}(-t_{2})_{nk_{2}}}{(c_{1})_{m+1}\,(c_{2})_{n}\,m!\,n!}\ x^{m+1}\,y^{n}

\displaystyle=\frac{a\,b\,x}{c_{1}}\sum_{m,n\geq 0}\frac{(a+1)_{m+n}\,(b+1)_{m+n}\,(-1)^{m}\,(-t_{1})_{m}\,(-1)^{nk_{2}}\,(-t_{2})_{nk_{2}}}{(c_{1}+1)_{m}\,(c_{2})_{n}\,m!\,n!}\ x^{m}\,y^{n}

\displaystyle=\frac{a\,b\,x}{c_{1}}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}(a+1,b+1;c_{1}+1,c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},1,k_{2},x,y).

(3.5)

Now, acting the difference operator twice gives

\displaystyle(\Delta_{t_{1}})^{2}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}(a,b;c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},1,k_{2},x,y)

\displaystyle=\frac{(a)_{2}\,(b)_{2}\,x^{2}}{(c_{1})_{2}}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}(a+2,b+2;c_{1}+2,c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},1,k_{2},x,y).

(3.6)

In general,

\displaystyle(\Delta_{t_{1}})^{r}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}(a,b;c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},1,k_{2},x,y)

\displaystyle=\frac{(a)_{r}\,(b)_{r}\,x^{r}}{(c_{1})_{r}}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}(a+r,b+r;c_{1}+r,c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},1,k_{2},x,y).

(3.7)

It completes the proof of (
3.1
). Similarly the other difference and differential formulae can be proved.
∎

Besides, some other differential formulas are stated in the following theorem.

Theorem 3.2
.

The following differential formulas hold for the discrete Appell function
\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}
:

\displaystyle\left(\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\right)^{r}\left[x^{b+r-1}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}(a,b;c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,xy)\right]

\displaystyle=x^{b-1}\,(b)_{r}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}(a,b+r;c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,x\,y);

(3.8)

\displaystyle\left(\frac{\partial}{\partial y}\right)^{r}[y^{b+r-1}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}(a,b;c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x\,y,y)]

\displaystyle=y^{b-1}\,(b)_{r}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}(a,b+r;c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x\,y,y);

(3.9)

\displaystyle\left(\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\right)^{r}[x^{a+r-1}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}(a,b;c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,xy)]

\displaystyle=x^{a-1}\,(a)_{r}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}(a+r,b;c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,xy);

(3.10)

\displaystyle\left(\frac{\partial}{\partial y}\right)^{r}[y^{a+r-1}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}(a,b;c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},xy,y)]

\displaystyle=y^{a-1}\,(a)_{r}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}(a+r,b;c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},xy,y);

(3.11)

\displaystyle\left(\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\right)^{r}[x^{c_{1}-1}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}(a,b;c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y)]

\displaystyle=(-1)^{r}\,(1-c_{1})_{r}\,x^{c_{1}-r-1}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}(a,b;c_{1}-r,c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y);

(3.12)

\displaystyle\left(\frac{\partial}{\partial y}\right)^{r}[y^{c_{2}-1}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}(a,b;c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y)]

\displaystyle=(-1)^{r}\,y^{c_{2}-r-1}\,(1-c_{2})_{r}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}(a,b;c_{1},c_{2}-r;t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y).

(3.13)

Proof.

To prove (
3.8
), we start with

\displaystyle\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\left[x^{b}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}(a,b;c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,x\,y)\right]

\displaystyle=\sum_{m,n\geq 0}\frac{(a)_{m+n}\,(b)_{m+n}\,(-1)^{mk_{1}}\,(-t_{1})_{mk_{1}}\,(-1)^{nk_{2}}\,(-t_{2})_{nk_{2}}}{(c_{1})_{m}\,(c_{2})_{n}\,m!\,n!}\,\frac{\partial}{\partial x}x^{b+m+n}\,y^{n}

\displaystyle=\sum_{m,n\geq 0}\frac{(a)_{m+n}\,(b)_{m+n}\,(-1)^{mk_{1}}\,(-t_{1})_{mk_{1}}\,(-1)^{nk_{2}}\,(-t_{2})_{nk_{2}}}{(c_{1})_{m}\,(c_{2})_{n}\,m!\,n!}\,(b+m+n)\,x^{b+m+n-1}\,y^{n}

\displaystyle=x^{b-1}\,b\sum_{m,n\geq 0}\frac{(a)_{m+n}\,(b+1)_{m+n}\,(-1)^{mk_{1}}\,(-t_{1})_{mk_{1}}\,(-1)^{nk_{2}}\,(-t_{2})_{nk_{2}}}{(c_{1})_{m}\,(c_{2})_{n}\,m!\,n!}\,x^{m}\,y^{n}

\displaystyle=x^{b-1}\,b\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}(a,b+1;c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y).

(3.14)

Performing the same operation twice, we get

\displaystyle\left(\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\right)^{2}\left[x^{b+1}\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}(a,b;c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y)\right]

\displaystyle=x^{b-1}\,(b)_{2}\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}(a,b+2;c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y).

(3.15)

Inductively, this gives

\displaystyle\left(\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\right)^{r}\left[x^{b+r-1}\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}(a,b;c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y)\right]

\displaystyle=x^{b-1}\,(b)_{r}\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}(a,b+r;c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y).

(3.16)

It completes the proof. The other results can also be proved in a similar manner.
∎

4
Recursion Formulae

Theorem 4.1
.

The following recursion formulas hold for the discrete Appell function
\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}
:

\displaystyle\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}(a+s,b;c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y)

\displaystyle=\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}(a,b;c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y)

\displaystyle\quad+\frac{(-1)^{k_{1}}\,(-t_{1})_{k_{1}}\,b\,x}{c_{1}}\sum_{r=1}^{s}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}(a+r,b+1;c_{1}+1,c_{2};t_{1}-k_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y)

\displaystyle\quad+\frac{(-1)^{k_{2}}\,(-t_{2})_{k_{2}}\,b\,y}{c_{2}}\sum_{r=1}^{s}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{4}(a+r,b+1;c_{1},c_{2}+1;t_{1},t_{2}-k_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y);

(4.1)
