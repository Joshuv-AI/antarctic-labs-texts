1. Introduction
— The exploration of quark-gluon structure in hadrons is a cross-cutting edge issue between particle physics and medium-high energy nuclear physics in recent years. In which quarks and gluons, called partons, are the fundamental degrees of freedom of quantum chromodynamics (QCD). Although the parton can not be directly observed, the QCD factorization theorem allows one to express the information of the parton inside the nucleon in terms of nonperturbative functions
Collins:1989gx

Berger:1979du

2. Theoretical Framework
— If one wants to use the typical probability expression of quantum mechanics to describe the measurable properties of a given hadron, the first thing one needs to do is finding the WF. Each element of the WF fock-space decomposition represents the probability amplitude of finding
n
components in the hadron. However, the PDFs describe the longitudinal momentum distribution parton of hadron. To derive the
a_{0}(980)
-meson leading-twist PDF, the following expression can be used
Cui:2020tdf

\displaystyle\mathpzc q^{a_{0}}(x,\zeta)=\int_{|{\bf k}_{\bot}|^{2}\leq\zeta^{2}}d^{2}{\bf k}_{\bot}|\psi_{a_{0}}(x,{\bf k}_{\bot})|^{2}.

(2)

where
\psi_{a_{0}}(x,\textbf{k}_{\bot})
is WF and
\zeta
stands for the scale. In order to calculate the PDF, the connection between distribution amplitudes and distribution function can be established to achieve the purpose. Exploiting this relationship, one can predict
a_{0}(980)
-meson leading-twist PDFs with the LCHO model based on BHL description
BHL
. The LCHO model of the
a_{0}(980)
-meson leading-twist WF is denoted by

\displaystyle\psi_{a_{0}}(x,{\bf k}_{\bot})=\sum_{\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2}}\chi_{a_{0}}^{\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2}}(x,{\bf k}_{\bot})\psi^{R}_{a_{0}}(x,{\bf k}_{\bot}),

(3)

with
{\bf k}_{\bot}
is transverse momentum. What’s more,
\lambda_{1}
and
\lambda_{2}
are the helicities of the two constituent quark. The spin-space WF
\chi_{a_{0}}^{\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2}}(x,{\bf k}_{\bot})
comes from the Wigner-Melosh rotation. The different forms for
\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2}
can also be found in Ref.
Huang:1994dy
. Thus the sum of the spin-space WF have the following form

\displaystyle\sum_{\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2}}\chi_{a_{0}}^{\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2}}(x,{\bf k}_{\bot})=\frac{m_{q}^{2}}{\sqrt{{\bf k}^{2}_{\bot}+m_{q}^{2}}},

(4)

with
m_{q}=m_{u}=m_{d}
. On the other hand, the BHL description
Guo:1991eb
;
Huang:1994dy
proposed the assumption that the valence Fock WF depends only on the energy variable
\epsilon
outside the shell. At the same time, the connection between the equal-time WF in the rest frame and the light-cone wave function in the infinite frame by equating the energy propagator
\epsilon=M^{2}-(\sum_{i=1}^{n}k_{i})^{2}
is proposed. The propagators in different frames are as follws

\displaystyle\epsilon_{1}=M^{2}-\left(\sum\limits_{i=1}^{n}q^{0}_{i}\right)^{2},

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{i=1}^{n}{\bf q}^{i}=0

\displaystyle\epsilon_{2}=M^{2}-\bigg(\sum\limits_{i=1}^{n}\frac{{\bf k}^{2}_{\bot i}+m^{2}_{i}}{x_{i}}\bigg),

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{i=1}^{n}{\bf k}^{2}_{\bot i}=0,

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{i=1}^{n}x^{i}=1.

(5)

For the two-particle system, one can get

\displaystyle{\bf q}\leftrightarrow\frac{{\bf k}^{2}_{\bot i}+m^{2}_{q}}{4x(1-x)}-m^{2}_{q}

(6)

where
q^{0}_{1}=q^{0}_{2}
. Besides, there is approximate connection between
\psi_{\rm CM}({\bf q})
and
\psi_{\rm LC}(x,{\bf k})
,

\displaystyle\psi_{\rm CM}({\bf q}^{2})\leftrightarrow\psi_{\rm LC}\bigg(\frac{{\bf k}^{2}_{\bot}+m^{2}_{q}}{4x(1-x)}-m^{2}_{q}\bigg)

(7)

Based on the approximation for bound state solution of the meson quark model, the WF of the harmonic oscillator model in the rest frame is expressed as

\displaystyle\psi_{\rm CM}({\bf q}^{2})=A\exp\bigg(-\frac{{\bf q}^{2}}{2\beta^{2}}\bigg)

(8)

Combining Eqs. (
7
) and (
8
), the spatial WF is

\displaystyle\psi^{R}_{a_{0}}(x,{\bf k}_{\bot})=A_{a_{0}}\varphi_{a_{0}}(x)\exp\left[-\frac{{\bf k}^{2}_{\bot}+m_{q}^{2}}{8\beta_{a_{0}}^{2}x\bar{x}}\right].

(9)

where
A_{a_{0}}
is the normalization constant and
\varphi_{a_{0}}(x)=(x\bar{x})^{\alpha_{a_{0}}}C_{1}^{3/2}(2x-1)
. Thus, one can get the
a_{0}(980)
-meson leading-twist WF

\displaystyle\psi_{a_{0}}(x,{\bf k}_{\bot})=\frac{m_{q}^{2}A_{a_{0}}\varphi_{a_{0}}(x)}{\sqrt{{\bf k}^{2}_{\bot}+m_{q}^{2}}}\exp\left[-\frac{{\bf k}^{2}_{\bot}+m_{q}^{2}}{8\beta_{a_{0}}^{2}x\bar{x}}\right].

(10)

Furthermore, the
a_{0}(980)
-meson leading-twist valence-quark distribution function can be obtained by integrating over the squared transverse momentum, i.e. Eq. (
2
), which leads to the following formula

\displaystyle\mathpzc q^{a_{0}}(x,\zeta)=\frac{A^{2}_{a_{0}}m_{q}^{2}(1-2x)^{2}(x\bar{x})^{2\alpha_{a_{0}}}}{512\pi^{5}}

\displaystyle\quad\times\left\{\textrm{Ei}\left[\sqrt{\frac{m_{q}^{2}+\zeta^{2}}{4\beta_{a_{0}}^{2}x\bar{x}}}\right]-\textrm{Ei}\left[\sqrt{\frac{m_{q}^{2}}{4\beta_{a_{0}}^{2}x\bar{x}}}\right]\right\},

(11)

with
\textrm{Ei}(x)=-\int^{\infty}_{-z}\frac{e^{-t}}{t}dt
. It guarantees the relationship

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{1}dx\mathpzc q^{a_{0}}(x,\zeta)=1

(12)

which is in agreement with the cases of pion and kaon. For further research, the process-independent effective charge is used to redesign the process-dependent-charge alternative and implement evolution to integrate the one-loop Dokshitzer-Gribov-Lipatov-Altarelli-Parisi (DGLAP) equations with describing the evolution of quarks or gluons fragmenting into hadrons and identifying only a hadron at a time. The relevant explanation can be found in Ref.
Cui:2020tdf

\displaystyle\langle x^{n}\mathpzc q^{a_{0}}\rangle_{\zeta}=\int^{1}_{0}x^{n}\mathpzc q^{a_{0}}(x,\zeta)dx

Then, the ratio of Mellin moments is also a point of interest, and its expression is as follows

\displaystyle\mathpzc x^{n}_{~a_{0}}(\zeta,\zeta_{k})=\frac{\langle x^{n}\mathpzc q^{a_{0}}\rangle_{\zeta}}{\langle x^{n}\mathpzc q^{a_{0}}\rangle_{\zeta_{k}}}

Another significant physical quantity associated with

-meson PDF is its LCDA. The relationship between the

-meson leading-twist LCDA and the WF is

\displaystyle\phi_{a_{0}}(x,\zeta)=\int_{|{\bf k}_{\bot}|^{2}\leq\zeta^{2}}\frac{d^{2}{\bf k}_{\bot}}{16\pi^{3}}\psi_{a_{0}}(x,{\bf k}_{\bot}).

\displaystyle\phi_{a_{0}}(x,\zeta)=\frac{A_{a_{0}}m_{q}\beta_{a_{0}}}{4\sqrt{2}\pi^{3/2}}\sqrt{x\bar{x}}\varphi_{a_{0}}(x)

\displaystyle\quad\times\left\{\textrm{Erf}\left[\sqrt{\frac{m_{q}^{2}+\zeta^{2}}{8\beta_{a_{0}}^{2}x\bar{x}}}\right]-\textrm{Erf}\left[\sqrt{\frac{m_{q}^{2}}{8\beta_{a_{0}}^{2}x\bar{x}}}\right]\right\},
