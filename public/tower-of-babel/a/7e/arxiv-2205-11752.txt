On

Given a function

Let

Let us define the Ornstein-Uhlenbeck semigroup

as

\displaystyle T_{t}f(x)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\left(1-e^{-2t}\right)^{d/2}}\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{d}}e^{-\frac{e^{-2t}(\left|x\right|^{2}+\left|y\right|^{2})-2e^{-t}\left\langle x,y\right\rangle}{1-e^{-2t}}}f(y)\gamma_{d}(dy)

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\pi^{d/2}(1-e^{-2t})^{d/2}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{d}}e^{-\frac{|y-e^{-t}x|^{2}}{1-e^{-2t}}}f(y)dy

w(x,t)=\displaystyle e^{-t}u^{(n)}(x,t)
. Now using the fundamental theorem of calculus,

\displaystyle\frac{\partial^{n}}{\partial t^{n}}P_{t}(\mathcal{D}_{\beta}f)(x)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{e^{t}}{c_{\beta}}\int_{0}^{+\infty}s^{-\beta-1}\Delta_{s}^{k}(w(x,\cdot),t)ds

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{e^{t}}{c_{\beta}}\int_{0}^{+\infty}s^{-\beta-1}\int_{0}^{s}\frac{\partial}{\partial r}\Delta_{r}^{k}(w(x,\cdot),t)dr\,ds.

Then, using classical Hardy’s inequality (
1.26
), and Lemma
2.1
,

\displaystyle\left|\frac{\partial^{n}}{\partial t^{n}}P_{t}\mathcal{(}{D}_{\beta}f)(x)\right|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{e^{t}}{c_{\beta}}\int_{0}^{+\infty}s^{-\beta-1}\int_{0}^{s}\left|\frac{\partial}{\partial r}\Delta_{r}^{k}(w(x,\cdot),t)\right|drds

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{e^{t}}{c_{\beta}\beta}\int_{0}^{+\infty}r\left|\frac{\partial}{\partial r}\Delta_{r}^{k}(w(x,\cdot),t)\right|r^{-\beta-1}dr

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{ke^{t}}{c_{\beta}\beta}\int_{0}^{+\infty}r^{-\beta}|\Delta_{r}^{k-1}(w^{\prime}(x,\cdot),t+r)|dr

and by Minkowski’s integral inequality (
1.18
) we get

\displaystyle\left\|\frac{\partial^{n}}{\partial t^{n}}P_{t}(\mathcal{D}_{\beta}f)\right\|_{p(\cdot),\gamma_{d}}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C\frac{ke^{t}}{\beta c_{\beta}}\int_{0}^{+\infty}r^{-\beta}\|\Delta_{r}^{k-1}(w^{\prime},t+r)\|_{p(\cdot),\gamma_{d}}dr.

Now, by analogous argument as above, Lemma
2.1
and again Leibnitz’s differentiation rule for the product, give us

\displaystyle\|\Delta_{r}^{k-1}(w^{\prime},t+r)\|_{p(\cdot),\gamma_{d}}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{k,p(\cdot)}\sum_{j=0}^{k}{k\choose j}r^{k-1}e^{-(t+r)}\|u^{(k+n-j)}(\cdot,t+r)\|_{p(\cdot),\gamma_{d}},

and this implies that

\displaystyle\left\|\frac{\partial^{n}}{\partial t^{n}}P_{t}(\mathcal{D}_{\beta}f)\right\|_{p(\cdot),\gamma_{d}}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{k,p(\cdot)}e^{t}\frac{k}{c_{\beta}\beta}\int_{0}^{+\infty}r^{-\beta}\big(\sum_{j=0}^{k}{k\choose j}r^{k-1}e^{-(t+r)}\|u^{(k+n-j)}(\cdot,t+r)\|_{p(\cdot),\gamma_{d}}dr

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle C_{k,p(\cdot)}\frac{k}{c_{\beta}\beta}\sum_{j=0}^{k}{k\choose j}\int_{0}^{+\infty}r^{k-\beta-1}e^{-r}\|u^{(k+n-j)}(\cdot,t+r)\|_{p(\cdot),\gamma_{d}}dr.

Thus,

\displaystyle\left\|t^{n-(\alpha-\beta)}\left\|\frac{\partial^{n}}{\partial t^{n}}P_{t}(\mathcal{D}_{\beta}f)\right\|_{p(\cdot),\gamma_{d}}\right\|_{q(\cdot),\mu}

\displaystyle\quad\quad\leq C_{k,p(\cdot)}\frac{k}{c_{\beta}\beta}\sum_{j=0}^{k}{k\choose j}\left\|t^{n-(\alpha-\beta)}\int_{0}^{+\infty}r^{k-\beta-1}e^{-r}\|u^{(k+n-j)}(\cdot,t+r)\|_{p(\cdot),\gamma_{d}}dr\right\|_{q(\cdot),\mu}.

Now, for each
1\leq j\leq k
,

0<\alpha-\beta+k-j\leq\alpha
and by lemma
1.2

\displaystyle\left\|t^{n-(\alpha-\beta)}\int_{0}^{\infty}r^{k-\beta-1}e^{-r}\|u^{(k+n-j)}(\cdot,t+r)\|_{p(\cdot),\gamma_{d}}dr\right\|_{q(\cdot),\mu}

\displaystyle\quad\quad\quad\leq C_{p(\cdot)}\left\|t^{n-(\alpha-\beta)}\|u^{(n+k-j)}(\cdot,t)\|_{p(\cdot),\gamma_{d}}\int_{0}^{+\infty}r^{k-\beta-1}e^{-r}dr\right\|_{q(\cdot),\mu}

\displaystyle\quad\quad\quad=C_{p(\cdot)}\Gamma(k-\beta)\left\|t^{n+(k-j)-(\alpha-\beta+k-j)}\|u^{(n+k-j)}(\cdot,t)\|_{p(\cdot),\gamma_{d}}\right\|_{q(\cdot),\mu}<\infty,

as
f\in B_{p(\cdot),q(\cdot)}^{\alpha}(\gamma_{d})\subset B_{p(\cdot),q(\cdot)}^{\alpha-\beta+(k-j)}(\gamma_{d})
for any
1\leq j\leq k
.

Now, for the case
j=0
,

\displaystyle\left\|t^{n-(\alpha-\beta)}\int_{0}^{+\infty}r^{k-\beta-1}e^{-r}\|u^{(n+k)}(\cdot,t+r)\|_{p(\cdot),\gamma_{d}}dr\right\|_{q(\cdot),\mu}

\displaystyle\quad\quad\quad\leq\left\|t^{n-(\alpha-\beta)}\int_{0}^{t}r^{k-\beta-1}e^{-r}\|u^{(n+k)}(\cdot,t+r)\|_{p(\cdot),\gamma_{d}}dr\right\|_{q(\cdot),\mu}

\displaystyle\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad+\left\|t^{n-(\alpha-\beta)}\int_{t}^{+\infty}r^{k-\beta-1}e^{-r}\|u^{(n+k)}(\cdot,t+r)\|_{p(\cdot),\gamma_{d}}dr\right\|_{q(\cdot),\mu}

\displaystyle\quad\quad\quad=(I)+(II).

Using Lemma
1.2
, and
k>\beta
,

\displaystyle(I)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{p(\cdot)}\left\|t^{n-(\alpha-\beta)}\int_{0}^{t}r^{k-\beta-1}\|u^{(n+k)}(\cdot,t)\|_{p(\cdot),\gamma_{d}}dr\right\|_{q(\cdot),\mu}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle C_{p(\cdot)}\left\|t^{n-(\alpha-\beta)}\|u^{(n+k)}(\cdot,t)\|_{p(\cdot),\gamma_{d}}\int_{0}^{t}r^{k-\beta-1}dr\right\|_{q(\cdot),\mu}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{C_{p(\cdot)}}{k-\beta}\left\|t^{n+k-\alpha}\|u^{(n+k)}(\cdot,t)\|_{p(\cdot),\gamma_{d}}\right\|_{q(\cdot),\mu}<\infty,

since
f\in B_{p(\cdot),q(\cdot)}^{\alpha}(\gamma_{d})
and
n+k>\alpha
.

For the second term, using Lemma
1.2
and Hardy’s inequality (
1.25
)

\displaystyle(II)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{p(\cdot)}\left\|t^{n-(\alpha-\beta)}\int_{t}^{+\infty}r^{k-\beta-1}\|u^{(n+k)}(\cdot,r)\|_{p(\cdot),\gamma_{d}}dr\right\|_{q(\cdot),\mu}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{p(\cdot)}C_{q(\cdot)}\left\|r^{n+k-\alpha}\|u^{(n+k)}(\cdot,r)\|_{p(\cdot),\gamma_{d}}\right\|_{q(\cdot),\mu}<\infty,

since
f\in B_{p(\cdot),q(\cdot)}^{\alpha}(\gamma_{d})
. Therefore,
\mathcal{D}_{\beta}f\in B_{p(\cdot),q(\cdot)}^{\alpha-\beta}(\gamma_{d})
.

Moreover,

\displaystyle\|\mathcal{D}_{\beta}f\|_{B_{p(\cdot),q(\cdot)}^{\alpha-\beta}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\|\mathcal{D}_{\beta}f\|_{p(\cdot),\gamma_{d}}+\left\|t^{n-(\alpha-\beta)}\left\|\frac{\partial^{n}}{\partial t^{n}}P_{t}\mathcal{D}_{\beta}f\right\|_{p(\cdot),\gamma_{d}}\right\|_{q(\cdot),\mu}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C^{1}_{k,p(\cdot)}\|f\|_{p(\cdot),\gamma_{d}}+\frac{k}{c_{\beta}\beta}\sum_{j=0}^{k}{k\choose j}C^{2}_{p(\cdot),q(\cdot)}\left\|r^{n-\alpha}\left\|\frac{\partial^{n}}{\partial r^{n}}P_{r}f\right\|_{p(\cdot),\gamma_{d}}\right\|_{q(\cdot),\mu}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{p(\cdot),q(\cdot)}\|f\|_{B_{p(\cdot),q(\cdot)}^{\alpha}}

∎
