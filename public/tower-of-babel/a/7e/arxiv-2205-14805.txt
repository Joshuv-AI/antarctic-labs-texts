Some identities on λ \lambda -analogues of r r -Stirling numbers of the second kind Thanks: * are corresponding authors
DAE SAN KIM, Hyekyung Kim*, Taekyun Kim*

Abstract
. Recently, the λ \lambda -analogues of r r -Stirling numbers of the first kind were studied by Kim-Kim. The aim of this paper is to introduce the λ \lambda -analogues of r r -Stirling numbers of the second kind and to investigate some properties, recurrence relations and certain identities on those numbers. We also introduce the λ \lambda -analogues of Whitney-type r r -Stirling numbers of the second and derive similar results to the case of the λ \lambda -analogues of r r -Stirling numbers of the second kind. In addition, we consider the λ \lambda -analogues of Dowling polynomials and deduce a Dobinski-like formula.

Introduction

Carlitz [3] initiated a study of the degenerate Bernoulli and Euler polynomials and numbers, which are degenerate versions of the Bernoulli and Euler polynomials and numbers.
In recent years, studying degenerate versions of special numbers and polynomials regained interests of some mathematicians. They have been explored with various tools and many fascinating results have been revealed. It is remakable that this quest for degenerate versions is not just restricted to polynomials but also extended to transcendental functions, like gamma functions. In addition, it also led to the introduction of

\lambda

The degenerate Stirling numbers of the first kind and of the second kind, which are degenerate versions of the Stirling numbers of the first kind and of the second kind, appear frequently when we study degenerate versions of some special numbers and polynomials. Throughout this paper, let
\lambda
be any nonzero real number. The generalized falling factorial sequence is defined by

(1)

(x)_{0,\lambda}=1,\quad(x)_{n,\lambda}=x(x-\lambda)\cdots(x-(n-1)\lambda),\quad(n\geq 1),\quad(\mathrm{see}\ [2,6,8,9,11]).

It is known that the degenerate exponential functions are defined by

(2)

e_{\lambda}^{x}(t)=(1+\lambda t)^{\frac{x}{\lambda}}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(x)_{n,\lambda}\frac{t^{n}}{n!},\quad(\mathrm{see}\ [3,7,10]).

When
x=1
, we use the notation as
e_{\lambda}(t)=e_{\lambda}^{1}(t)
.

For
n\geq 0
, the Stirling numbers of the first kind are defined by

(3)

(x)_{n}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}S_{1}(n,k)x^{k},\quad(\mathrm{see}\ [1,2,4,7,12,13]),

where the falling factorial sequence is given by

(x)_{0}=1,\quad(x)_{n}=x(x-1)\cdots(x-n+1),\quad(n\geq 1).

As the inversion formula of (
3
), the Stirling numbers of the second kind are defined as

(4)

x^{n}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}{n\brace k}(x)_{k},\quad(\mathrm{see}\ [4,9,12]).

The
\lambda
-analogues of Stirling numbers of the first kind are defined by

(5)

(x)_{n,\lambda}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}S_{1,\lambda}(n,k)x^{k},\quad(n\geq 0),\quad(\mathrm{see}\ [9]).

For
r\in\mathbb{N}\cup\{0\}
, the
\lambda
-analogues of
r
-Stirling numbers of the first kind are defined by

(6)

(x+r)_{n,\lambda}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}S_{1,\lambda}^{(r)}(n,k)x^{k},\quad(\mathrm{see}\ [9]).

Also, the
\lambda
-analogues of unsigned
r
-Stirling numbers of the first kind are given by

(7)

\langle x+r\rangle_{n,\lambda}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}{n+r\brack k+r}_{r,\lambda}x^{k},\quad(n\geq 0),\quad(\mathrm{see}\ [9]).

Note that
\displaystyle\lim_{\lambda\rightarrow 1}{n+r\brack k+r}_{r,\lambda}={n+r\brack k+r}_{r}\displaystyle
are the ordinary unsigned
r
-Stirling numbers of the first kind.

2.
\lambda
-analogues of
r
-Stirling numbers of the second kind

First, we consider the
\lambda
-analogues of Stirling numbers of the second kind as the inversion formula of (
5
), which are defined by

(8)

x^{n}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}{n\brace k}_{\lambda}(x)_{k,\lambda},\quad(n\geq 0).

From (
8
), we note that

(9)

\displaystyle e^{xt}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}x^{n}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}\sum_{k=0}^{n}{n\brace k}_{\lambda}(x)_{k,\lambda}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\bigg(\sum_{n=k}^{\infty}{n\brace k}_{\lambda}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}\bigg)(x)_{k,\lambda}.

On the other hand, by (
1
), we get

(10)

\displaystyle e^{xt}

\displaystyle=\big(e^{\lambda t}-1+1\big)^{\frac{x}{\lambda}}=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\binom{\frac{x}{\lambda}}{k}(e^{\lambda t}-1)^{k}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\lambda^{-k}\frac{1}{k!}\big(e^{\lambda t}-1\big)^{k}(x)_{k,\lambda}.

From (
9
) and (
10
), we note that

(11)

\frac{1}{\lambda^{k}}\frac{1}{k!}\big(e^{\lambda t}-1\big)^{k}=\sum_{n=k}^{\infty}{n\brace k}_{\lambda}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

Note that
\displaystyle\lim_{\lambda\rightarrow 0}{n\brace k}_{\lambda}={n\brace k}\displaystyle
are the Stirling numbers of the second kind which are defined by

x^{n}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}{n\brace k}(x)_{k},\quad(n\geq 0).

For
r\in\mathbb{N}\cup\{0\}
, we consider the
\lambda
-analogues of
r
-Stirling numbers of the second kind as the inversion formula of (
6
) which are defined by

(12)

(x+r)^{n}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}{n+r\brace k+r}_{r,\lambda}(x)_{k,\lambda},\quad(n\geq 0).

From (
12
), we note that

(13)

\displaystyle e^{(x+r)t}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(x+r)^{n}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\bigg(\sum_{k=0}^{n}{n+r\brace k+r}_{r,\lambda}(x)_{k,\lambda}\bigg)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\bigg(\sum_{n=k}^{\infty}{n+r\brace k+r}_{r,\lambda}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}\bigg)(x)_{k,\lambda}.

On the other hand, by (
1
), we get

(14)

\displaystyle e^{(x+r)t}

\displaystyle=e^{rt}e^{xt}=e^{rt}(e^{\lambda t}-1+1)^{\frac{x}{\lambda}}

\displaystyle=e^{rt}\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\binom{\frac{x}{\lambda}}{k}(e^{\lambda t}-1)^{k}=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{k}(e^{\lambda t}-1)^{k}e^{rt}\frac{1}{\lambda^{k}}(x)_{k,\lambda}.

From (
13
) and (
14
), we note that

(15)

\frac{1}{\lambda^{k}}\frac{1}{k!}\big(e^{\lambda t}-1\big)^{k}e^{rt}=\sum_{n=k}^{\infty}{n+r\brace k+r}_{r,\lambda}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

Theorem 1
.

The generating function of the
\lambda
-analogues of
r
-Stirling numbers of the second kind is given by

\frac{1}{\lambda^{k}}\frac{1}{k!}\big(e^{\lambda t}-1\big)^{k}e^{rt}=\sum_{n=k}^{\infty}{n+r\brace k+r}_{r,\lambda}\frac{t^{n}}{n!},\quad(k\geq 0).

Note that
\displaystyle\lim_{\lambda\rightarrow 1}{n+r\brace k+r}_{r,\lambda}={n+r\brace k+r}_{r}\displaystyle
are the ordinary
r
-Stirling numbers of the second kind which are defined by

(x+r)^{n}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}{n+r\brace k+r}_{r}(x)_{k},\quad(n\geq 0),\quad(\mathrm{see}\ [\ ]).

Let
\triangle
be the difference operator with

\triangle f(x)=f(x+1)-f(x).

Then we have

(16)

\triangle^{k}f(x)=\sum_{l=0}^{k}\binom{k}{l}(-1)^{k-l}f(x+l).

From Theorem 1, we note that

(17)

\displaystyle\sum_{n=k}^{\infty}{n+r\brace k+r}_{r,\lambda}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\lambda^{k}}\frac{1}{k!}\big(e^{\lambda t}-1\big)^{k}e^{rt}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\lambda^{k}}\frac{1}{k!}\sum_{l=0}^{k}\binom{k}{l}(-1)^{k-l}e^{(l\lambda+r)t}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\bigg(\frac{1}{\lambda^{k}}\frac{1}{k!}\sum_{l=0}^{k}\binom{k}{l}(-1)^{k-l}(l\lambda+r)^{n}\bigg)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

Let us take
f(x)=\big(\frac{x}{\lambda}\big)^{n}
in (
8
). Then we have

(18)

\displaystyle\triangle^{k}\bigg(\frac{r}{\lambda}\bigg)^{n}

\displaystyle=\sum_{l=0}^{k}\binom{k}{l}(-1)^{k-l}\bigg(l+\frac{r}{\lambda}\bigg)^{n}

\displaystyle=\sum_{l=0}^{k}\binom{k}{l}(-1)^{k-l}(\lambda l+r)^{n}\lambda^{-n}.

By (
17
) and (
18
), we get

(19)

\sum_{n=k}^{\infty}{n+r\brace k+r}_{r,\lambda}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\lambda^{n-k}\frac{1}{k!}\triangle^{k}\bigg(\frac{r}{\lambda}\bigg)^{n}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

Theorem 2
.

For
k\geq 0
, we have

\lambda^{n-k}\frac{1}{k!}\triangle^{k}\bigg(\frac{r}{\lambda}\bigg)^{n}=\left\{\begin{array}[]{ccc}{n+r\brace k+r}_{r,\lambda},&\textrm{if $n\geq k$,}\\
0,&\textrm{if $0\leq n<k$}.\end{array}\right.

By Theorem 1, we get

(20)

\displaystyle\sum_{n=k}^{\infty}{n+r\brace k+r}_{r,\lambda}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\lambda^{k}}\frac{1}{k!}\big(e^{\lambda t}-1\big)^{k}e^{rt}

\displaystyle=\sum_{l=k}^{\infty}{l\brace k}_{\lambda}\frac{t^{l}}{l!}\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}r^{m}\frac{t^{m}}{m!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=k}^{\infty}\bigg(\sum_{l=k}^{n}{l\brace k}_{\lambda}r^{n-l}\binom{n}{l}\bigg)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

Therefore, by comparing the coefficients on both sides of (
20
), we obtain the following theorem.

Theorem 3
.

For
n,k\in\mathbb{Z}
with
n\geq k\geq 0
, we have

{n+r\brace k+r}_{r,\lambda}=\sum_{l=k}^{n}\binom{n}{l}{l\brace k}_{\lambda}r^{n-l}.

For
n\geq 1
, we have

\displaystyle(x+r)^{n+1}

\displaystyle=(x+r)^{n}(x+r)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}{n+r\brace k+r}_{r,\lambda}(x+r)(x)_{k,\lambda}

(21)

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{n}{n+r\brace k+r}_{r,\lambda}(x-k\lambda+k\lambda+r)(x)_{k,\lambda}.

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{n}{n+r\brace k+r}_{r,\lambda}(x)_{k+1,\lambda}+\sum_{k=0}^{n}{n+r\brace k+r}_{r,\lambda}(x)_{k,\lambda}(\lambda k+r)

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=1}^{n+1}{n+r\brace k-1+r}_{r,\lambda}(x)_{k,\lambda}+\sum_{k=0}^{n}(\lambda k+r){n+r\brace k+r}_{r,\lambda}(x)_{k,\lambda}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{n+1}\bigg({n+r\brace k-1+r}_{r,\lambda}+(\lambda k+r){n+r\brace k+r}_{r,\lambda}\bigg)(x)_{k,\lambda}.

On the other hand, by (
12
), we get

(22)

(x+r)^{n+1}=\sum_{k=0}^{n+1}{n+1+r\brace k+r}_{r,\lambda}(x)_{k,\lambda}.

Therefore, by (
21
) and (
22
), we obtain the following theorem.

Theorem 4
.

For
n,k\in\mathbb{Z}
with
n\geq k\geq 1
, we have

{n+1+r\brace k+r}_{r,\lambda}={n+r\brace k-1+r}_{r,\lambda}+(\lambda k+r){n+r\brace k+r}_{r,\lambda}.

Now, we observe that

(23)

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\lambda^{k}}\frac{1}{k!}\big(e^{\lambda t}-1\big)^{k}\frac{1}{\lambda^{m}}\frac{1}{m!}\big(e^{\lambda t}-1\big)^{m}e^{rt}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\lambda^{k+m}}\frac{1}{(k+m)!}(e^{\lambda t}-1)^{k+m}e^{rt}\frac{(k+m)!}{k!m!}

\displaystyle=\binom{k+m}{k}\sum_{n=m+k}^{\infty}{n+r\brace k+m+r}_{r,\lambda}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

On the other hand, by (
15
), we get

(24)

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\lambda^{k}}\frac{1}{k!}\big(e^{\lambda t}-1\big)^{k}\frac{1}{\lambda^{m}}\frac{1}{m!}(e^{\lambda t}-1)^{m}e^{rt}

\displaystyle=\sum_{l=k}^{\infty}{l\brace k}_{\lambda}\frac{t^{l}}{l!}\sum_{j=m}^{\infty}{j+r\brace m+r}_{r,\lambda}\frac{t^{j}}{j!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=m+k}^{\infty}\sum_{l=k}^{n-m}{l\brace k}_{\lambda}{n-l+r\brace m+r}_{r,\lambda}\binom{n}{l}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

Therefore, by (
23
) and (
24
), we obtain the following theorem.

Theorem 5
.

For
m,n,k\geq 0
with
n\geq m+k
, we have

\binom{m+k}{k}{n+r\brace k+m+r}_{r,\lambda}=\sum_{l=k}^{n-m}\binom{n}{l}{l\brace k}_{\lambda}{n-l+r\brace m+r}_{r,\lambda}.

From the definition of the
\lambda
-analogues of the Stirling numbers of the second kind, we have

(25)

\displaystyle\sum_{n=k}^{\infty}{n\brace k}_{\lambda}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\lambda^{k}}\frac{1}{k!}\big(e^{t}-1\big)^{k}=\frac{1}{\lambda^{k}}\frac{1}{k!}\big(e^{\lambda t}-1\big)^{k}e^{rt}e^{-rt}

\displaystyle=\sum_{l=k}^{\infty}{l+r\brace k+r}_{r,\lambda}\frac{t^{l}}{l!}\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}(-r)^{m}\frac{t^{m}}{m!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=k}^{\infty}\bigg(\sum_{l=k}^{n}\binom{n}{l}{l+r\brace k+r}_{r,\lambda}(-1)^{n-l}r^{n-l}\bigg)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

Therefore, by comparing the coefficients on both sides of (
25
) we obtain the following theorem.

Theorem 6
.

For
n,k\geq 0
with
n\geq k
, we have

{n\brace k}_{\lambda}=\sum_{l=k}^{n}\binom{n}{l}{l+r\brace k+r}_{r,\lambda}(-1)^{n-l}r^{n-l}.

For
m\in\mathbb{N}
, the higher-order Bernoulli polynomials are defined by

(26)

\bigg(\frac{t}{e^{t}-1}\bigg)^{m}e^{xt}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}B_{n}^{(m)}(x)\frac{t^{n}}{n!},\quad(\mathrm{see}\ [1,3,7]).

From (
26
), we note that

(27)

\displaystyle\sum_{n=k}^{\infty}{n+r\brace k+r}_{r,\lambda}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}=\frac{1}{\lambda^{k}}\frac{1}{k!}\big(e^{\lambda t}-1\big)^{k}e^{rt}

\displaystyle\quad=\frac{1}{t^{m}}\frac{1}{\lambda^{k+m}}\frac{(k+m)!}{k!}\frac{1}{(k+m)!}(e^{\lambda t}-1)^{k+m}\bigg(\frac{\lambda t}{e^{\lambda t}-1}\bigg)^{m}e^{rt}

\displaystyle\quad=\binom{k+m}{k}\frac{m!}{t^{m}}\sum_{l=k+m}^{\infty}{l\brace k+m}_{\lambda}\frac{t^{l}}{l!}\bigg(\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}B_{j}^{(m)}\frac{r}{\lambda}\bigg)\frac{\lambda^{j}t^{j}}{j!}

\displaystyle\quad=\binom{k+m}{k}\sum_{l=k}^{\infty}{l+m\brace k+m}_{\lambda}\frac{m!l!}{(l+m)!}\frac{t^{l}}{l!}\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}B_{j}^{(m)}\bigg(\frac{r}{\lambda}\bigg)\frac{\lambda^{j}t^{j}}{j!}

\displaystyle\quad=\binom{k+m}{k}\sum_{n=k}^{\infty}\bigg(\sum_{l=k}^{n}\frac{\binom{n}{l}}{\binom{l+m}{l}}{l+m\brace k+m}_{\lambda}\lambda^{n-l}B_{n-l}^{(m)}\bigg(\frac{r}{\lambda}\bigg)\bigg)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

Therefore, by comparing the coefficients on both sides of (
27
), we obtain the following theorem.

Theorem 7
.

For
n,k\geq 0
with
n\geq k
, we have

\frac{{n+r\brace k+r}_{r,\lambda}}{\binom{k+m}{k}}=\sum_{l=k}^{n}\frac{\binom{n}{l}}{\binom{l+m}{l}}{l+m\brace k+m}_{\lambda}B_{n-l}^{(m)}\bigg(\frac{r}{\lambda}\bigg)\lambda^{n-l}.

3.
Further Remarks

For
m,n\geq 0
, we define
\lambda
-analogues of the Whitney-type Stirling numbers of the second kind as

(28)

(mx+1)^{n}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}W_{m,\lambda}(n,k)m^{k}(x)_{k,\lambda},\quad(n\geq 0).

From (
28
), we note that

(29)

\displaystyle e^{(mx+1)t}

\displaystyle=\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}(mx+1)^{n}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\sum_{k=0}^{n}W_{m,\lambda}(n,k)m^{k}(x)_{k,\lambda}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\sum_{n=k}^{\infty}W_{m,\lambda}(n,k)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}m^{k}(x)_{k,\lambda}.

On the other hand, by (
11
), we get

(30)

\displaystyle e^{(mx+1)t}

\displaystyle=e^{t}\big(e^{\lambda mt}-1+1\big)^{\frac{x}{\lambda}}=e^{t}\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\binom{\frac{x}{\lambda}}{k}(e^{\lambda mt}-1)^{k}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\bigg(\frac{1}{\lambda^{k}}\frac{1}{k!}\bigg(\frac{e^{\lambda mt}-1}{m}\bigg)^{k}e^{t}\bigg)m^{k}(x)_{k,\lambda}.

Therefore, by (
29
) and (
30
), we obtain the generating function
W_{m,\lambda}(n,k),\ (n,k\geq 0)
.

Theorem 8
.

For
k\geq 0
, we have

\frac{1}{k!}\frac{1}{\lambda^{k}}\bigg(\frac{e^{\lambda mt}-1}{m}\bigg)^{k}e^{t}=\sum_{n=k}^{\infty}W_{m,\lambda}(n,k)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

From Theorem 8, we note that

(31)

\displaystyle\sum_{n=k}^{\infty}{n+1\brace k+1}_{\lambda}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}=\frac{d}{dt}\bigg\{\sum_{n=k}^{\infty}{n+1\brace k+1}_{\lambda}\frac{t^{n+1}}{(n+1)!}\bigg\}

\displaystyle=\frac{d}{dt}\bigg(\frac{1}{(k+1)!}\frac{1}{\lambda^{k+1}}(e^{\lambda t}-1)^{k+1}\bigg)=\frac{1}{k!}\frac{1}{\lambda^{k}}(e^{\lambda t}-1)^{k}e^{t}e^{(\lambda-1)t}

\displaystyle=\sum_{l=k}^{\infty}W_{1,\lambda}(l,k)\frac{t^{l}}{l!}\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}(\lambda-1)^{j}\frac{t^{j}}{j!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=k}^{\infty}\bigg(\sum_{l=k}^{n}W_{1,\lambda}(l,k)(\lambda-1)^{n-l}\binom{n}{l}\bigg)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

Therefore, by comparing the coefficients on both sides of (
31
), we obtain the following theorem.

Theorem 9
.

For
n,k\geq 0
with
n\geq k
, we have

\sum_{l=k}^{n}\binom{n}{l}W_{1,\lambda}(l,k)(\lambda-1)^{n-l}={n+1\brace k+1}_{\lambda}.

Now, we consider the
\lambda
-analogues of Dowling polynomials which are defined by

(32)

d_{m,\lambda}(n,x)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}W_{m,\lambda}(n,k)x^{k},\quad(n\geq 0).

Thus, by (
32
), we get

(33)

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}d_{m,\lambda}(n,x)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\sum_{k=0}^{n}W_{m,\lambda}(n,k)x^{k}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

(34)

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}x^{k}\sum_{n=k}^{\infty}W_{m,\lambda}(n,k)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

\displaystyle=e^{t}\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}x^{k}\frac{1}{k!}\frac{1}{\lambda^{k}}\bigg(\frac{e^{\lambda mt}-1}{m}\bigg)^{k}

\displaystyle=e^{t}e^{x(\frac{e^{\lambda mt}-1}{\lambda m})}.

Theorem 10
.

For
m\in\mathbb{N}
, we have

e^{t}e^{x(\frac{e^{\lambda mt}-1}{\lambda m})}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}d_{m,\lambda}(n,x)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

When
x=1
,
d_{m,\lambda}(n,1)=d_{m,\lambda}(n)
are called the
\lambda
-analogues of Dowling numbers.

We define the
\lambda
-analogues of Bell polynomials by

(35)

e^{\frac{x}{\lambda}(e^{\lambda t}-1)}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\phi_{n,\lambda}(x)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

Thus, we easily get
\displaystyle\phi_{n,\lambda}(x)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}{n\brace k}_{\lambda}x^{k},\ (n\geq 0)\displaystyle
. When
x=1,\ \phi_{n,\lambda}(1)=\phi_{n,\lambda}
are called the
\lambda
-analogues of Bell numbers.

From Theorem 10, we note that

(36)

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}d_{m,\lambda}(n,x)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

\displaystyle=e^{t}e^{x(\frac{e^{\lambda mt}-1}{\lambda m})}=e^{-\frac{x}{\lambda m}}e^{t}e^{x\frac{e^{\lambda mt}}{\lambda m}}

\displaystyle=e^{-\frac{x}{\lambda m}}\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{e^{\lambda mkt+t}}{\lambda^{k}m^{k}}\frac{x^{k}}{k!}

\displaystyle=e^{-\frac{x}{\lambda m}}\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{x^{k}}{k!m^{k}\lambda^{k}}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(\lambda mk+1)^{n}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\bigg(e^{-\frac{x}{\lambda m}}\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{x^{k}}{k!m^{k}\lambda^{k}}(\lambda mk+1)^{n}\bigg)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

Therefore, by comparing the coefficients on both sides of (
36
), we obtain the following Dobinski-like formula.

Theorem 11
.

For
n\geq 0
, we have

d_{m,\lambda}(n,x)=e^{-\frac{x}{\lambda m}}\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{x^{k}}{k!m^{k}\lambda^{k}}(\lambda mk+1)^{n}.

For
r\in\mathbb{N}
,
m,n\geq 0
, we consider the
\lambda
-analogues of the Whitney- type
r
-Stirling numbers of the second kind defined by

(37)

(mx+r)^{n}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}W_{m,\lambda}^{(r)}(n,k)m^{k}(x)_{k,\lambda},\quad(n\geq 0).

From (
37
), we note that

(38)

\displaystyle e^{(mx+r)t}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(mx+r)^{n}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\bigg(\sum_{k=0}^{n}W_{m,\lambda}^{(r)}(n,k)m^{k}(x)_{k}\bigg)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\bigg(\sum_{n=k}^{\infty}W_{m,\lambda}^{(r)}(n,k)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}\bigg)m^{k}(x)_{k}.

On the other hand, by (
1
), we get

(39)

\displaystyle e^{(mx+r)t}

\displaystyle=e^{rt}(e^{m\lambda t}-1+1)^{\frac{x}{\lambda}}=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\binom{\frac{x}{\lambda}}{k}(e^{m\lambda t}-1)^{k}e^{rt}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{k!}\frac{1}{\lambda^{k}}(e^{m\lambda t}-1)^{k}e^{rt}(x)_{k,\lambda}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\bigg(\frac{1}{k!}\frac{1}{\lambda^{k}}\bigg(\frac{e^{m\lambda t}-1}{m}\bigg)^{k}e^{rt}\bigg)m^{k}(x)_{k,\lambda}.

By (
38
) and (
39
), we get

(40)

\frac{1}{k!}\frac{1}{\lambda^{k}}\bigg(\frac{e^{\lambda mt}-1}{m}\bigg)^{k}e^{rt}=\sum_{n=k}^{\infty}W_{m,\lambda}^{(r)}(n,k)\frac{t^{n}}{n!},

where
k
is a nonnegative integer.

Therefore, by (
40
), we obtain the following theorem.

Theorem 12
.

For
k\geq 0
, we have

\frac{1}{k!}\frac{1}{\lambda^{k}}\bigg(\frac{e^{\lambda mt}-1}{m}\bigg)^{k}e^{rt}=\sum_{n=k}^{\infty}W_{m,\lambda}^{(r)}(n,k)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

From (
15
) and Theorem 11, we have

W_{1,\lambda}^{(r)}(n,k)={n+r\brace k+r}_{r,\lambda},\ W_{1,\lambda}^{(0)}(n,k)={n\brace k}_{\lambda},\ \mathrm{and}\ W_{m,\lambda}^{(1)}(n,k)=W_{m,\lambda}(n,k),\quad(n,k\geq 0).

Now, we observe that

(41)

\displaystyle\sum_{n=k}^{\infty}W_{m,\lambda}^{(r)}(n,k)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}=\frac{1}{k!}\frac{1}{\lambda^{k}}\bigg(\frac{e^{\lambda mt}-1}{m}\bigg)^{k}e^{rt}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{k!}\frac{1}{\lambda^{k+\alpha}}\bigg(\frac{e^{\lambda mt}-1}{m}\bigg)^{k+\alpha}e^{t}\frac{1}{t^{\alpha}}\bigg(\frac{\lambda mt}{e^{\lambda mt}-1}\bigg)^{\alpha}e^{(r-1)t}

\displaystyle=\frac{(k+\alpha)!}{k!}\frac{1}{t^{\alpha}}\frac{1}{(k+\alpha)!}\frac{1}{\lambda^{k+\alpha}}\bigg(\frac{e^{\lambda mt}-1}{m}\bigg)^{k+\alpha}e^{t}\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}B_{j}^{(\alpha)}\bigg(\frac{r-1}{m\lambda}\bigg)\lambda^{j}m^{j}\frac{t^{j}}{j!}

\displaystyle=\frac{\alpha!}{t^{\alpha}}\binom{k+\alpha}{k}\sum_{l=k+\alpha}^{\infty}W_{m,\lambda}(l,k+\alpha)\frac{t^{l}}{l!}\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}B_{j}^{(\alpha)}\bigg(\frac{r-j}{m\lambda}\bigg)\lambda^{j}m^{j}\frac{t^{j}}{j!}

\displaystyle=\binom{k+\alpha}{k}\bigg(\sum_{l=k}^{\infty}\frac{W_{m,\lambda}(l+\alpha,k+\alpha)}{\binom{l+\alpha}{l}}\frac{t^{l}}{l!}\bigg)\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}B_{j}^{(\alpha)}\bigg(\frac{r-j}{m\lambda}\bigg)\lambda^{j}m^{j}\frac{t^{j}}{j!}

\displaystyle=\binom{k+\alpha}{k}\sum_{n=k}^{\infty}\bigg(\sum_{l=k}^{n}\frac{\binom{n}{l}}{\binom{l+\alpha}{l}}W_{m,\lambda}(l+\alpha,k+\alpha)B_{n-l}^{(\alpha)}\bigg(\frac{r-n+l}{m\lambda}\bigg)\lambda^{n-l}m^{n-l}\bigg)\frac{t^{n}}{n!},

where
\alpha
is a positive integer.

Comparing the coefficients on both sides of (
41
), we have the following theorem.

Theorem 13
.

For
n,k\in\mathbb{N}\cup\{0\}
and
\alpha\in\mathbb{N}
, we have

\binom{k+\alpha}{k}^{-1}W_{m,\lambda}^{(r)}(n,k)=\sum_{l=k}^{n}\frac{\binom{n}{l}}{\binom{l+\alpha}{l}}W_{m,\lambda}(l+\alpha,k+\alpha)B_{n-l}^{(\alpha)}\bigg(\frac{r-n+l}{m\lambda}\bigg)\lambda^{n-l}m^{n-l}.

4.
Conclusion

In this paper, we introduced the
\lambda
-analogues of
r
-Stirling numbers of the second which appear as the coefficients when powers of
x+r
are expressed in terms of the degenerate falling factorial sequence. We obtained some properties, recurrence relations and certain identities on such numbers. We also introduced the
\lambda
-analogues of Whitney-type
r
-Stirling numbers of the second and derived similar results to the case of the
\lambda
-analogues of
r
-Stirling numbers of the second. Furthermore, the
\lambda
-analogues of Dowling polynomials were introduced as a natural extension of the analogues of Whitney-type Stirling numbers of the second kind and a Dobinski-like formula for them was deduced.

It is one of our future projects to continue to study analogues of some special numbers and polynomials and to find their applications in physics, science and engineering.
