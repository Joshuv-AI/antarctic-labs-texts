Towards Realistic SUSY Grand Unification for Extended MSSM
Sibo Zheng

Abstract
Low-energy supersymmetric models such as MSSM, NMSSM and MSSM with vectorlike fermion are consistent with perturbative unification. While the non-minimal extensions naturally explain Higgs mass and dark matter in the low energy region, it is unclear how seriously they are constrained in the ultraviolet region. Our study shows that OPEN i ) i) , In the case of embedding MSSM into SU ⁡ ( 5 ) \rm{SU}(5) , the fit to SM fermion masses requires a singlet S S , which leads to unviable embedding of NMSSM into SU ⁡ ( 5 ) \rm{SU}(5) because such S S feeds singlet N N a mass of order unification scale as well. OPEN i ​ i ) ii) , Similar result holds in the case of embedding NMSSM into SO ⁡ ( 10 ) \rm{SO}(10) , where S S is replaced by some Higgs fields responsible for SO ⁡ ( 10 ) \rm{SO}(10) breaking. OPEN i ​ i ​ i ) iii) , On the contrary, for the embedding of MSSM with 16 16 -dimensional vectorlike fermions into SO ⁡ ( 10 ) \rm{SO}(10) , the Higgs field responsible for the vectorlike mass of order TeV scale can evade those problems the singlet N N encounters because of an intermediate mass scale in the 126 126 -dimensional Higgs field.

V
Conclusion

According to the observed Higgs mass at the LHC and the dark matter direct detection limits,
the conventional MSSM - the simplest natural SUSY that is consistent with unification - is under more pressure than ever.
Such stress can be greatly relaxed in the extended MSSM models such as NMSSM and VMSSM which retain the unification and are still simple.
In this paper, following the assumptions that
W_{Y}
is fixed by the SM matter content and its TeV-scale extension,
and they receive their masses from
W_{\text{SB}}
through Higgs mechanism,
we have studied the embeddings of these three models into SUSY GUTs.

First of all, we discussed the MSSM, where the realistic
\rm{SU}(5)
and
\rm{SO}(10)
realizations
serve as benchmark solutions to the SM flavor issue and neutrino masses.
Then, we utilize the benchmark MSSM as guidance to the embedding of NMSSM and VMSSM.
We have found that the embedding of NMSSM is not viable
due to a large mount of mixings between the singlet
N
and the Higgs fields responsible for the GUT symmetry breaking.
But the problem can be evaded in the VMSSM,
because the Higgs field
\rho
which provides
16
-dimensional VL mass of order TeV scale
can avoid the same problems the singlet
N
encounters due to the intermediate mass scale in the
126
-dimensional Higgs.
