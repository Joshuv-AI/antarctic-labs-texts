Instability of hairy black holes in regularized
4-dimensional Einstein-Gauss-Bonnet gravity

Abstract




WUCG-22-04

In regularized 4-dimensional Einstein-Gauss-Bonnet (EGB) gravity derived from
a Kaluza-Klein reduction of higher-dimensional EGB theory,
we study the existence and stability of black hole (BH) solutions on a static
and spherically symmetric background.
We show that asymptotically-flat hairy BH solutions realized for
a spatially-flat maximally symmetric internal space are unstable
against linear perturbations for any rescaled GB coupling constant.
This instability is present for the angular propagation of even-parity perturbations
both in the vicinity of an event horizon and at spatial infinity.
There is also a strong coupling problem associated with the
kinetic term of even-parity perturbations vanishing everywhere.

I
Introduction

There have been numerous attempts for constructing gravitational
theories beyond General Relativity
(GR)
Copeland et al. 2006
;
De Felice and Tsujikawa 2010
;
Clifton et al. 2012
;
Joyce et al. 2015
;
Heisenberg 2019
.
This is not only motivated by the construction of an ultraviolet compete theory
of gravity but also by the firm observational evidence of dark matter
and dark energy. Moreover, the dawn of gravitational-wave
astronomy
Abbott et al. 2016
started
to offer a new opportunity for probing the physics of strong gravity regimes
in the vicinity of black holes (BHs) and neutron stars (NSs)
Berti et al. 2015
;
Barack et al. 2019
.
It is important to scrutinize the possible deviation from GR
on the cosmological and strong gravitational backgrounds.

GR is the 4-dimensional spacetime theory that leads to conserved and
symmetric field equations of motion with derivatives up to second order
in the metric tensor. One way of modifying the gravitational equations in GR
is to add new degrees of freedom such as scalar/vector fields coupled to gravity.
For example, Horndeski theories
Horndeski 1974
;
Deffayet et al. 2011
;
Kobayashi et al. 2011
;
Charmousis et al. 2012a

are the most general scalar-tensor theories whose equations of motion
contain derivatives of the scalar field and metric tensor up to second order.
In such second-order gravitational theories, one can avoid an Ostrogradski
instability
Ostrogradsky 1850
arising from the Hamiltonian unbounded from below.

The other way of constructing alternative theories of gravity is
to consider spacetime dimensions higher than 4.
In this vein, Lovelock
Lovelock 1971
derived the gravitational
field equations of motion with 2-rank symmetric tensors satisfying
the conserved and second-order properties.
In 4 dimensions, GR is the unique theory of gravity
being compatible with such properties
Lovelock 1972
.
In spacetime dimensions higher than 4, there is a new term
known as the Gauss-Bonnet (GB) term defined by

{\cal R}_{\rm GB}^{2}={\cal R}^{2}-4{\cal R}_{AB}{\cal R}^{AB}+{\cal R}_{ABCD}{\cal R}^{ABCD}\,,

(1)

where
{\cal R}
,
{\cal R}_{AB}
, and
{\cal R}_{ABCD}
are
the Ricci scalar, Ricci tensor, and Riemann tensors in

D>4
dimensions, respectively
Padmanabhan and Kothawala 2013
;
Fernandes et al. 2022
.
In 4 spacetime dimensions, the field equations of motion
following from the Lagrangian
L=\sqrt{-g}\,\hat{\alpha}{\cal R}_{\rm GB}^{2}

(where
g
is a determinant of the metric tensor and

\hat{\alpha}
is a GB coupling) identically vanish by
reflecting the fact that the GB curvature invariant
reduces to a topological surface term.

If we rescale the GB coupling constant as
\hat{\alpha}\to\alpha/(D-4)

and take the limit
D\to 4
, it is possible to extract non-trivial contributions
of the higher-dimensional GB term in 4 dimensions
Glavan and Lin 2020
.
This theory is known as 4D-Einstein-Gauss-Bonnet (4DEGB) gravity,
which has received much attention over the past
few years (see Ref.
Fernandes et al. 2022
for a review).
In the original version proposed by Glavan and Lin
Glavan and Lin 2020
,
there are some ill-defined terms in the field equations that lead to the divergence of second-order perturbation equations about a Minkowski
background
Arrechea et al. 2021
;
Arrechea et al. 2020
.
Furthermore, the
D\to 4
limit gives rise to unphysical divergences
in the on-shell action
Mahapatra 2020
.
In Refs.
Gürses et al. 2020
;
Gurses et al. 2020
,
it was also argued that a simple rescaling of
the GB coupling originally suggested by Glavan and Lin does not
yield covariant equations of a massless graviton in
4 dimensions.

However, there are several possibilities for constructing regularized 4DEGB
theories that can evade the aforementioned problems of original
4DEGB gravity.
One of such improved theories arises from a conformal regularization
introducing a counter-term to eliminate
divergent parts of the theory
Fernandes et al. 2020
;
Hennigar et al. 2020
.
The other is a Kaluza-Klein reduction of the higher-dimensional
EGB theory with a scalar field
\phi
characterizing the size of a
maximally symmetric internal space
Lu and Pang 2020
;
Kobayashi 2020
.
In the former and latter approaches, the 4-dimensional actions
obtained from the rescaling
\hat{\alpha}\to\alpha/(D-4)

belong to subclasses of shift-symmetric and
non-shift-symmetric Horndeski theories,
respectively. For a spatially-flat internal space with the
Kaluza-Klein reduction, the 4-dimensional effective action
coincides with the one derived from the conformal regularization.
There is yet another regularization scheme in which the
temporal diffeomorphism invariance is explicitly
broken
Aoki et al. 2020
.

The regularized 4DEGB theories have been applied to the search for
static and spherically symmetric BH solutions.
In the original 4DEGB gravity, there exists an exact BH
solution
Glavan and Lin 2020
analogous to the one
obtained in the framework of higher-dimensional EGB
theories
Boulware and Deser 1985
;
Wheeler 1986

(see also Refs.
Konoplya and Zinhailo 2020
;
Guo and Li 2020
;
Fernandes 2020
;
Konoplya and Zhidenko 2020a
;
Hegde et al. 2020
;
Ghosh and Maharaj 2020
;
Ghosh and Kumar 2020
;
Hosseini Mansoori 2021
;
Wei and Liu 2020
;
Mishra 2020
;
Singh and Siwach 2020
;
Eslam Panah et al. 2020
;
Yang et al. 2020
;
Zeng et al. 2020
;
Dadhich 2020
;
Shaymatov et al. 2020
;
Aragón et al. 2020
;
Atamurotov et al. 2021
;
Sengupta 2022
;
Jaryal and Chatterjee 2022
;
Bravo-Gaete et al. 2022

for related works).
The same kind of solutions is also present in two regularized versions of
4DEGB theories mentioned above
Lu and Pang 2020
;
Fernandes et al. 2021
.
In the conformal regularization, this is the unique asymptotically-flat
BH solution allowed in the theory.

To study the linear stability of static and spherically symmetric BHs
in 4 dimensions, we need to classify perturbations into the odd- and
even-parity sectors depending on the types of parities under the rotation
in two-dimensional sphere
(\theta,\varphi)

Regge and Wheeler 1957
;
Zerilli 1970
.
In Horndeski theories, the BH stability
against odd- and even-parity perturbations for a time-independent
scalar field was originally addressed by Kobayashi
et al.

Kobayashi et al. 2012
;
Kobayashi et al. 2014

(see also Refs.
De Felice et al. 2011
;
Motohashi and Suyama 2011
;
Kase et al. 2014
;
Tattersall and Ferreira 2018
;
Kase et al. 2020
;
Khoury et al. 2020
).
Except for the propagation of even-parity modes along the
angular direction, Kobayashi
et al.
derived conditions for the absence
of ghost/Laplacian instabilities in high frequency limits.
In 4DEGB gravity, the stability of BHs has been discussed
in Refs.
Konoplya and Zhidenko 2020b
;
Cuyubamba 2021
;
Langlois et al. 2022a
;
Langlois et al. 2022b

by paying particular attention to the behavior of perturbations
along the radial direction.

The recent general analysis of BH and NS perturbations in Horndeski
theories
Kase and Tsujikawa 2022

shows that the angular propagation of even-parity modes
with large multipoles
\ell\gg 1

plays an important role to exclude some hairy BH solutions by the
instability in the vicinity of an event horizon
Minamitsuji et al. 2022a
.
This instability can arise for BHs with static scalar hair whose kinetic
term
X
is a non-vanishing constant
X_{s}
on the horizon
Minamitsuji et al. 2022b
.
For example, the derivative coupling with an Einstein tensor gives rise to
exact hairy BH solutions
Rinaldi 2012
;
Anabalon et al. 2014
;
Minamitsuji 2014

with
X_{s}\neq 0
, but they are prone to either ghost or Laplacian instabilities.
To recognize such properties, the propagation speeds of even-parity perturbations
for large angular momentum modes are crucial.

In this paper, we study the existence and stability of BHs
against odd- and even-parity perturbations in regularized 4DEGB gravity
derived from the Kaluza-Klein reduction of higher-dimensional EGB theory.
Our analysis also accommodates hairy BHs present in 4DEGB theory
with the conformal regularization. We will show that asymptotically-flat hairy
BH solutions present in such regularized 4DEGB theories are excluded by
the linear instability and strong coupling problems.

II
Black hole solutions in regularized 4DEGB theories

We first briefly revisit the regularized Kaluza-Klein reduction of
D
-dimensional
EGB gravity on a
(D-4)
-dimensional maximally symmetric space
with constant curvature
\lambda
.
In this setup, the
D
-dimensional metric is expressed in the form

{\rm d}s_{D}^{2}={\rm d}s_{4}^{2}+{\rm e}^{-2\phi}{\rm d}\sigma_{D-4}^{2}\,,

(2)

where
{\rm d}s_{4}^{2}
is the line element of 4-dimensional spacetime,
and
{\rm d}\sigma_{D-4}^{2}
is the line element of an internal space
whose Riemann tensor is given by

{\cal R}_{abcd}=\lambda(g_{ac}g_{bd}-g_{ad}g_{bc})

The scalar field
\phi

depends only on the 4-dimensional
coordinates
x^{\mu}=(x^{0},x^{1},x^{2},x^{3})

We consider the action of
D

{\cal S}=\frac{1}{16\pi G_{D}}\int{\rm d}^{D}x\sqrt{-g}\left({\cal R}+\hat{\alpha}{\cal R}_{\rm GB}^{2}\right)\,,

(3)

D

Substituting the metric ansatz (
2

reduced action in 4 dimensions

;
Kobayashi 2020

(see also Refs.
Van Acoleyen and Van Doorsselaere 2011
;
Charmousis et al. 2012b

\displaystyle{\cal S}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{16\pi G}\int{\rm d}^{4}x\sqrt{-g}\,{\rm e}^{-(D-4)\phi}\biggl[R-(D-4)(D-5)\left(2X-\lambda{\rm e}^{2\phi}\right)+\hat{\alpha}R_{\rm GB}^{2}

(4)

\displaystyle-2\hat{\alpha}(D-4)(D-5)\left(2G^{\mu\nu}\nabla_{\mu}\phi\nabla_{\nu}\phi-\lambda R{\rm e}^{2\phi}\right)

\displaystyle-4\hat{\alpha}(D-4)(D-5)(D-6)\left\{X\square\phi+(D-5)X^{2}\right\}

\displaystyle-\hat{\alpha}(D-4)(D-5)(D-6)(D-7)\left(4\lambda X{\rm e}^{2\phi}-\lambda^{2}{\rm e}^{4\phi}\right)\biggr]\,,

We performed a volume integral with respect to the internal space

Since the GB combination is topological in 4 dimensions, we can

-(16\pi G)^{-1}\int{\rm d}^{4}x\sqrt{-g}\,\hat{\alpha}R_{\rm GB}^{2}

4

To extract contributions of the higher-dimensional GB term, we rescale
the coupling constant to be
\hat{\alpha}=\alpha/(D-4)

original prescription of Glavan and Lin.
Taking the

{\cal S}=\int{\rm d}^{4}x\sqrt{-g}\left[R+\alpha\left\{4G^{\mu\nu}\nabla_{\mu}\phi\nabla_{\nu}\phi-\phi R_{\rm GB}^{2}-8X\square\phi+8X^{2}-2\lambda{\rm e}^{2\phi}\left(R-12X+3\lambda{\rm e}^{2\phi}\right)\right\}\right]\,.

(5)

Here and in the following, we use the unit
(16\pi G)^{-1}=1
.
For the spatially-flat internal space (
\lambda=0

5

the one derived by a counter term regularization with

Fernandes et al. 2020

Hennigar et al. 2020

The regularized 4DEGB gravity (

Horndeski theories given by the action
Horndeski 1974
;
Deffayet et al. 2011
;
Kobayashi et al. 2011
;
Charmousis et al. 2012a

\displaystyle{\cal S}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int{\rm d}^{4}x\sqrt{-g}\,\biggl[G_{2}-G_{3}\square\phi+G_{4}R+G_{4,X}\left\{(\square\phi)^{2}-(\nabla_{\mu}\nabla_{\nu}\phi)(\nabla^{\mu}\nabla^{\nu}\phi)\right\}+G_{5}G^{\mu\nu}\nabla_{\mu}\nabla_{\nu}\phi

(6)

\displaystyle-\frac{1}{6}G_{5,X}\left\{(\square\phi)^{3}-3(\square\phi)\,(\nabla_{\mu}\nabla_{\nu}\phi)(\nabla^{\mu}\nabla^{\nu}\phi)+2(\nabla^{\mu}\nabla_{\alpha}\phi)(\nabla^{\alpha}\nabla_{\beta}\phi)(\nabla^{\beta}\nabla_{\mu}\phi)\right\}\biggr]\,,

\displaystyle G_{2}=\alpha\left(8X^{2}+24\lambda X{\rm e}^{2\phi}-6\lambda^{2}{\rm e}^{4\phi}\right)\,,\qquad G_{3}=8\alpha X\,,

\displaystyle G_{4}=1+4\alpha X-2\alpha\lambda{\rm e}^{2\phi}\,,\qquad G_{5}=4\alpha\ln|X|\,.

(7)

Here, we used the fact that the term

For

In this case, the theory belongs to a subclass of shift-symmetric

We search for hairy asymptotically-flat BH solutions where the metric

With this boundary condition of

Then, at large distances, the leading-order contribution to

\lambda=0\,.

(14)

In other words the asymptotic flatness requires

BH solutions in this case.

For
\lambda=0

On using the expansions (
13

J=\sqrt{h/f}\,\alpha(f^{\prime}+2\phi^{\prime}f)[1-h(1+r\phi^{\prime})^{2}]\to 0

J=\sqrt{\frac{h}{f}}\alpha\left(f^{\prime}+2\phi^{\prime}f\right)j=0\,.

Since

, and

Among the two branches of Eq. (

\phi^{\prime}=\frac{1}{r}\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{h}}-1\right)\,,

The other branch,

.

III
Instability of hairy black holes

We now address the linear stability of asymptotically-flat hairy BHs
present for
\lambda=0
. We study the behavior of odd- and even-parity
perturbations on the background solution (
20
) with (

).
The quantities relevant to the stability against odd-parity perturbations

\displaystyle{\cal G}

\displaystyle\equiv

\displaystyle 2G_{4}+2h\phi^{\prime 2}G_{4,X}-h\phi^{\prime 2}\left(G_{5,\phi}+{\frac{f^{\prime}h\phi^{\prime}G_{5,X}}{2f}}\right)\,,

\displaystyle\equiv

\displaystyle 2G_{4}+h\phi^{\prime 2}G_{5,\phi}-h\phi^{\prime 2}\left(\frac{1}{2}h^{\prime}\phi^{\prime}+h\phi^{\prime\prime}\right)G_{5,X}\,,

IV
Conclusions

We have studied the existence and stability of BH solutions
on a static and spherically symmetric background
in regularized 4DEGB gravity obtained from a Kaluza-Klein
reduction of higher-dimensional EGB theory.
The regularized 4DEGB theory derived by the rescaling
of the GB coupling constant
\hat{\alpha}\to\alpha/(D-4)

belongs to a subclass of Horndeski
theories given by the action (
5
).
On using the background Eq. (
11
) of the scalar field,
we showed that hairy asymptotically-flat BH solutions
respecting the boundary conditions (
13
) at spatial infinity
can exist only for a spatially-flat internal space (
\lambda=0
).

For
\lambda=0
, there is a unique hairy BH described by the
metric components (
20
) with the scalar derivative (
17
).
This solution has two horizons located at
r_{\pm}=M\pm\sqrt{M^{2}-\alpha}
,
where the GB coupling is in the range
-8M^{2}<\alpha<M^{2}
for
the existence of an outer horizon
r_{+}
.
We found that the absence of ghost/Laplacian instabilities
in the odd-parity sector requires that
0<\alpha<4(3\sqrt{3}-5)M^{2}
.
For this hairy BH solution, the scalar field has a non-vanishing
kinetic term
X_{s}
on the horizon. This property mostly arises from
the existence of a derivative coupling term
4\alpha X

in
G_{4}
. Since there is no standard kinetic term
X
in
G_{2}
,
we do not have the no-hair branch
\phi^{\prime}(r)=0
for
\alpha\neq 0
.

For the BH solution (
20
) with (
17
), we showed that the quantity

{\cal K}
associated with the no-ghost condition of even-parity perturbations
vanishes everywhere. Hence there is a strong coupling
problem in the even-parity sector. The product
{\cal F}{\cal K}B_{2}
is
a non-vanishing finite value, but the leading-order term of
{\cal F}{\cal K}B_{2}

is negative in the vicinity of an outer horizon.
This means that, even if
{\cal F}
is a positive finite value, we have

B_{2}\to-\infty
as
{\cal K}\to+0
.
Then, the angular Laplacian instability of even-parity perturbations is
present around the horizon. At spatial infinity the squared propagation
speed
c_{r2,{\rm even}}^{2}
of
\delta\phi
divided by
B_{2}
is given by

c_{r2,{\rm even}}^{2}/B_{2}=-2+{\cal O}(M/r)
, so
either
c_{r2,{\rm even}}^{2}
or
B_{2}
is negative.
Hence there is also the Laplacian instability of even-parity perturbations
far away from the horizon.
We note that, for
\alpha=r_{+}^{2}=M^{2}
, the BH has a single horizon,
but this solution is excluded by the radial Laplacian instability of odd-parity
perturbations in the vicinity of the horizon (i.e.,
{\cal F}<0
).

Our argument of the instability of BHs is valid for the effective 4-dimensional
action (
5
) that belongs to a subclass of Horndeski theories.
To discuss the stability of higher-dimensional BH solutions present
in
D>4
EGB theories
Boulware and Deser 1985
;
Cai 2002
, we need
to reformulate BH perturbations on the higher-dimensional background
along the line of Ref.
Kodama and Ishibashi 2003
.
In the presence of an electromagnetic Lagrangian
-F_{AB}F^{AB}/4
,
it is known that there are exact charged BH solutions in
D>4

dimensions
Wiltshire 1986
;
Wiltshire 1988
.
In 4DEGB gravity reduced from
D>4
EGB theory
with the rescaling
\hat{\alpha}\to\alpha/(D-4)
,
we also have hairy charged BHs
Fernandes 2020
;
Hegde et al. 2020
;
Eslam Panah et al. 2020

whose metric components are the same as those derived
in the context of 4D gravity with a
conformal anomaly
Cai et al. 2010
;
Cai 2014
.
It will be of interest to investigate the stability of such charged BH solutions
against linear perturbations, along the line of Refs.
Kase et al. 2018a
;
Kase et al. 2018b
;
Heisenberg et al. 2018

performed in particular classes of vector-(scalar)-tensor theories.
