Abstract
In these proceedings, we present transverse momentum dependence of the mid-rapidity slope of directed flow ( d ​ v 1 / d ​ y | y = 0 dv_{1}/dy|_{y=0} ) for π + \pi^{+} and K S 0 K_{S}^{0} in Au + Au collisions at s N ​ N \sqrt{s_{NN}} = 3.0, 3.2, 3.5, and 3.9 GeV. Both π + \pi^{+} and K S 0 K_{S}^{0} show negative v 1 v_{1} slope at low p T p_{T} ( p T < 0.6 p_{T}<0.6 GeV/ c c ). Collision energy dependence of v 1 v_{1} slope and p T p_{T} -integrated v 2 v_{2} for π ± \pi^{\pm} , K S 0 K_{S}^{0} , and Λ \Lambda are also presented. A comparison to JAM model calculations indicates that spectator shadowing can lead to anti-flow at low p T p_{T} . In addition, a breaking of the Number of Constitute Quark (NCQ) scaling of elliptic flow ( v 2 v_{2} ) is observed at s N ​ N \sqrt{s_{NN}} = 3.2 GeV, which implies the dominance of hadronic degrees of freedom occurs in collisions at s N ​ N \sqrt{s_{NN}} = 3.2 GeV and below.

3.1
Anti-flow of Kaon

The anti-flow of kaon was first observed by E895 Collaboration at 6 A GeV

It was attributed to the repulsive potential associated with the strange quark in

We have observed anti-flow behavior in kaons and pions for

< 0.6 GeV/
c

= 3.0, 3.2, 3.5, and 3.9 GeV using the fixed target data from STAR.
