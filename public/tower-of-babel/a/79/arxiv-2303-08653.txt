On the robustness of posterior means
Jiafeng Chen

Abstract




Consider an observation
X
whose likelihood is
X\mid\theta\sim\mathcal{N}(\theta,\sigma^{2})
for some known

\sigma^{2}
. There are two priors for
\theta
, denoted by
G_{0}
and
G_{1}
. Suppose both
priors have mean zero and have finite variances bounded by
V>0
. Consider the decision
problem of estimating
\theta
under squared error, with
L(a,\theta)=(a-\theta)^{2}
. For
the Bayesian with prior
G_{1}
, the Bayes decision rule is the posterior mean
\mathbb{E}_{G_{1}}[\theta\mid X]
under the prior
G_{1}
. This decision rule attains Bayes risk under the
prior
G_{0}

R(G_{1},\sigma;G_{0})\equiv\mathbb{E}_{\theta\sim G_{0}}\left[{(\mathbb{E}_{G_{1}}[\theta\mid X]-\theta)^{2}}\right].

We can think of
R(G_{1},\sigma;G_{0})
as a measure of decision quality under
disagreement. It measures the quality of
G_{1}
’s decision from
G_{0}
’s point of view.
When
G_{1}\neq G_{0}
, how large can
R(G_{1},\sigma;G_{0})
be?

This note bounds
R(G_{1},\sigma;G_{0})
uniformly over
G_{1},G_{0},\sigma
.

Since

R(G_{1},\sigma;G_{0})\leq 2\left({\mathbb{E}_{G_{0}}[\mathbb{E}_{G_{1}}[\theta\mid X]^{2}]+\mathbb{E}_{G_{0}}[\theta^{2}]}\right)\leq 2V+2\mathbb{E}_{G_{0}}[\mathbb{E}_{G_{1}}[\theta\mid X]^{2}],

it thus suffices to bound
\mathbb{E}_{G_{0}}[\mathbb{E}_{G_{1}}[\theta\mid X]^{2}]

modulo constants. That is, it suffices to bound the 2-norm
\lVert\mathbb{E}_{G_{1}}[\theta\mid X]\rVert^{2}
under the law
X\sim\mathcal{N}(0,\sigma^{2})\star G_{0}
.

The rest of the note shows that this quantity is uniformly bounded over all
G_{0},G_{1},\sigma^{2}>0
. Specifically,
Lemma

1
shows that for all
G_{0},G_{1}
that are mean zero and have variance bounded by
V
,
\mathbb{E}_{G_{0}}[\mathbb{E}_{G_{1}}[\theta\mid X]^{2}]
is bounded by a constant that depends only on
(V,\sigma^{2})
. This bound is
large when
\sigma^{2}
is large. To improve this bound,
Theorem

1
then shows that,
if
G_{1}
additionally satisfies some conditions on its tail behavior,
\mathbb{E}_{G_{0}}[\mathbb{E}_{G_{1}}[\theta\mid X]^{2}]
is bounded by a constant that depends only on
V
and the tail condition—and
does not depend on
\sigma
.

Lemma 1
.

Suppose
G_{0},G_{1}
have mean zero and variances bounded by
V
, then

\mathbb{E}_{G_{0}}[\mathbb{E}_{G_{1}}[\theta\mid X]^{2}]\leq 6V+4\sigma^{2}

uniformly over
G_{0},G_{1},\sigma^{2}
.

Proof.

Let
f_{G,\sigma}(x)=\int f_{X}(x\mid\theta)\,G(d\theta)
.

Jiang 2020
(Lemma 1) shows that

\left({\frac{f^{\prime}_{G,\sigma}(x)}{f_{G,\sigma}(x)}}\right)^{2}\leq\frac{1}{\sigma^{2}}\log\left({\frac{1}{2\pi\sigma^{2}f^{2}_{G,\sigma}(x)}}\right).

Plugging in the bound (
2
) in
Lemma

2
, we have that
pointwise in
X

\left({\mathbb{E}_{G_{1}}[\theta\mid X]-X}\right)^{2}=\left({\sigma^{2}\frac{f^{\prime}_{G_{1},\sigma}(x)}{f_{G_{1},\sigma}(x)}}\right)^{2}\leq\sigma^{4}\frac{1}{\sigma^{2}}\frac{X^{2}+V}{\sigma^{2}}=X^{2}+V,

where the first equality is due to Tweedie’s formula.

Now, note that

(\mathbb{E}_{G_{1}}[\theta\mid X])^{2}\leq 2\left({(\mathbb{E}_{G_{1}}[\theta\mid X]-X)^{2}+X^{2}}\right).

Hence,

\mathbb{E}_{G_{0}}\left[{\mathbb{E}_{G_{1}}[\theta\mid X])^{2}}\right]\leq 2\mathbb{E}_{G_{0}}[2X^{2}+V]\leq 2(2(V+\sigma^{2})+V)=6V+4\sigma^{2}.\qed

To show a more powerful bound, we require a stronger condition on the tails of
G_{1}
and
derive bounds that are independent of
\sigma
but are dependent on the tail conditions.
In particular, assume

\max\left({1-G_{1}(s),G_{1}(-s)}\right)\leq C_{G_{1}}s^{-k}

for some

and
C_{G_{1}}>0

s>0

\mathbb{E}_{G_{1}}[\theta^{2}\mid X]
exists almost surely. Note that if

can be taken to be

and
C_{G_{1}}
can be taken to
be

by Markov’s inequality. In the rest of the proof, we let
C_{t}<\infty
denote a
positive constant that depends only on
t
. Occurrences of

C_{t}
might correspond to different constant values.

Lemma 2
.

Suppose
G_{1}
has mean zero and variance bounded by
V
. Let

f_{G_{1},\sigma}(x)\equiv\int f_{X}(x\mid\theta)\,G_{1}(d\theta).

Then,

f_{G_{1},\sigma}(x)\geq\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}\sigma}\exp\left({-\frac{x^{2}+V}{2\sigma^{2}}}\right)\text{, or }\frac{1}{f_{G_{1},\sigma}(x)}\leq\sqrt{2\pi}\sigma\exp\left({\frac{x^{2}+V}{2\sigma^{2}}}\right).

Proof.

Observe that, by Jensen’s inequality,

f_{G_{1},\sigma}(x)\equiv\int f_{X}(x\mid\theta)\,G_{1}(d\theta)\geq\exp\int\log f_{X}(x\mid\theta)\,G_{1}(d\theta).

We compute

\log f_{X}(x\mid\theta)=\log\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}\sigma}-\frac{1}{2\sigma^{2}}(x-\theta)^{2}.

Note that
\mathbb{E}_{\theta\sim G_{1}}[(x-\theta)^{2}]=x^{2}-2x\mathbb{E}_{G_{1}}[\theta]+\mathbb{E}_{G_{1}}\theta^{2}\leq x^{2}+V
.
Thus (
2
) follows. ∎

Lemma 3
.

For all
x\geq 0
,
\overline{\Phi}(x)\leq\frac{1}{2}e^{-x^{2}/2}
.

Proof.

Note that
\overline{\Phi}(0)=\frac{1}{2}
and thus the bound holds with equality at
x=0
.
Differentiate,

\overline{\Phi}^{\prime}(x)=-\varphi(x)\quad\frac{d}{dx}\frac{1}{2}e^{-x^{2}/2}=-\frac{x}{2}e^{-x^{2}/2}=(-x\sqrt{\pi/2})\varphi(x)

For
x\in[0,\sqrt{2/\pi}]
,

\frac{d}{dx}\overline{\Phi}(x)\leq\frac{d}{dx}\frac{1}{2}e^{-x^{2}/2}\implies\overline{\Phi}(x)\leq\frac{1}{2}e^{-x^{2}/2}.

Note that since Mill’s ratio is bounded by
1/x
, we have that for all
x>0

\overline{\Phi}(x)\leq\varphi(x)/x.

Take
x>\sqrt{2/\pi}
, we have that

\overline{\Phi}(x)\leq\varphi(x)\sqrt{\frac{\pi}{2}}=\frac{1}{2}e^{-x^{2}/2}.

Hence the inequality holds for all
x\geq 0
.
∎
