Thermodynamically Consistent Diffuse Interface Model for Cell Adhesion and Aggregation
Lingyue Shen, Ping Lin, Zhiliang Xu, Shixin Xu

Abstract
A thermodynamically consistent phase-field model is introduced for simulating multicellular deformation, aggreggation under flow conditions. In particular, a Lennard-Jones type potential is proposed under phase field framework for cell-cell, cell-wall interactions. A second-order accurate in both space and time C 0 C^{0} finite element method is proposed to solved the model governing equations. Various numerical tests confirm the convergence, energy stability, and nonlinear mechanical properties of cells of the proposed scheme. Vesicles with different adhesion are also used to explain the pathological risk for patient with sickle cell disease.

Model Derivation

Derivation of the model in this paper is based on the energy dissipation law which holds ubiquitously in physical systems involving irreversible processes
[
46
,
47
,
48
,
49
,
44
]
. This law states that for an isothermal and closed system the rate of change of the energy of the system is equal to the dissipation of the energy as follows:

\frac{d}{dt}E^{\rm total}=-\triangle\leq 0~,

(1)

where
E^{\rm total}
is the total energy of the system, which is the sum of the kinetic energy
\mathcal{K}
and the Helmholtz free energy
\mathcal{F}
of the system, and
\triangle
is the rate of energy dissipation, which in fact is entropy production. Eq. (
1
) can be easily derived via the combination of
the First and Second Laws of Thermodynamics. The choices of the total energy functional and the dissipation functional, together with the kinematic
(transport) relations of the variables employed in the system, determine all the physical
and mechanical considerations and assumptions for the problem
[
50
]
. A general technique to determine these relations of variables may be found in
[
38
,
44
]
.

\delta_{\epsilon_{i}}\mathcal{P}_{i}^{n}:{\nabla}\mathbf{u}^{n+\frac{1}{2}}+\xi\epsilon^{2}\nabla\cdot((\phi_{i}^{n})^{2}\nabla\lambda_{i}^{n+\frac{1}{2}})=0~,\end{array}\right.

with boundary conditions on
\partial\Omega_{w}
,

\displaystyle\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\kappa\dot{\phi}_{i}^{n+\frac{1}{2}}=-L_{i}^{n+\frac{1}{2}}~,\\
L_{i}^{n+\frac{1}{2}}=\kappa_{B}\partial_{n}f_{i}^{n+\frac{1}{2}}+\mathcal{M}_{s}\epsilon\frac{S(\phi_{i}^{n+\frac{1}{2}})-S_{0_{i}}}{S_{0_{i}}}\partial_{n}\phi_{i}^{n+\frac{1}{2}}~,\\
-l_{s}^{-1}u_{\tau_{j}}^{n+\frac{1}{2}}=\bm{\tau_{j}}\cdot(\eta^{n}({\nabla}\mathbf{u}^{n+\frac{1}{2}}+({\nabla}\mathbf{u}^{n+\frac{1}{2}})^{T})+\sum_{i}\lambda_{i}^{n+\frac{1}{2}}\delta_{\epsilon_{i}}\mathcal{P}_{i}^{n})\cdot\bm{n}\\
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~-\sum_{i}L_{i}^{n+\frac{1}{2}}\partial_{\tau_{j}}\phi_{i}^{n+\frac{1}{2}}~,~~j=1,2,\\
f_{i}^{n+\frac{1}{2}}=0~,\\
\partial_{n}\lambda_{i}^{n+\frac{1}{2}}=0~,\end{array}\right.

with

(\cdot)^{n+\frac{1}{2}}=\frac{(\cdot)^{n}+(\cdot)^{n+1}}{2}
and
\mathcal{P}_{i}^{n}=I-\mathbf{n}^{n}_{m}\otimes\mathbf{n}^{n}_{m}
with
\mathbf{n}_{m}^{n}=\frac{\nabla\phi_{i}^{n}}{|\nabla\phi_{i}^{n}|}
and

\displaystyle\left\{\begin{array}[]{l}f(\phi_{i}^{n+1},\phi_{i}^{n})=-\epsilon\Delta\phi_{i}^{n+\frac{1}{2}}+\frac{1}{4\epsilon}((\phi_{i}^{n+1})^{2}+(\phi_{i}^{n})^{2}-2)(\phi_{i}^{n+1}+\phi_{i}^{n})~,\\
g(\phi_{i}^{n+1},\phi_{i}^{n})=\left(-\Delta f_{i}^{n+\frac{1}{2}}+\frac{1}{\epsilon^{2}}\left((\phi_{i}^{n+1})^{2}+(\phi_{i}^{n})^{2}+\phi_{i}^{n+1}\phi_{i}^{n}-1\right)f_{i}^{n+\frac{1}{2}}\right)~,\\
H_{i}^{n}=q_{1}(\phi_{i}^{n}+1)^{2}\sum_{j\neq i}\left[(\phi_{j}^{n+\frac{1}{2}}+1)^{2}\right]-q_{2}((\phi_{i}^{n})^{2}-1)^{2}\sum_{j\neq i}\left[((\phi_{j}^{n+\frac{1}{2}})^{2}-1)^{2}\right]~,\\
H_{i}^{n+1}=q_{1}(\phi_{i}^{n+1}+1)^{2}\sum_{j\neq i}\left[(\phi_{j}^{n+\frac{1}{2}}+1)^{2}\right]-q_{2}((\phi_{i}^{n+1})^{2}-1)^{2}\sum_{j\neq i}\left[((\phi_{j}^{n+\frac{1}{2}})^{2}-1)^{2}\right]~,\\
f_{w}(\phi^{n})=q_{w_{1}}(\phi_{i}^{n}+1)^{2}(\phi_{w}+1)^{2}-q_{w_{2}}((\phi_{i}^{n})^{2}-1)((\phi_{w})^{2}-1)~.\end{array}\right.

Thus we have

\displaystyle\frac{H_{i}^{n+1}-H_{i}^{n}}{\phi_{i}^{n+1}-\phi_{i}^{n}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\phi_{i}^{n+1}-\phi_{i}^{n}}\left({q_{1}}(\phi_{i}^{n+1}+1)^{2}\sum_{j\neq i}\left[(\phi_{j}^{n+\frac{1}{2}}+1)^{2}\right]-q_{2}((\phi_{i}^{n+1})^{2}-1)^{2}\sum_{j\neq i}\left[((\phi_{j}^{n+\frac{1}{2}})^{2}-1)^{2}\right]\right.

(97)

\displaystyle-\left.q_{1}(\phi_{i}^{n}+1)^{2}\sum_{j\neq i}\left[(\phi_{j}^{n+\frac{1}{2}}+1)^{2}\right]+q_{2}((\phi_{i}^{n})^{2}-1)^{2}\sum_{j\neq i}\left[(\phi_{j}^{n+\frac{1}{2}})^{2}-1\right]\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle q_{1}(\phi_{i}^{n+1}+\phi_{i}^{n}+2)\sum_{j\neq i}\left[(\phi_{j}^{n+\frac{1}{2}}+1)^{2}\right]

\displaystyle-q_{2}(\phi_{i}^{n+1}+\phi_{i}^{n})((\phi_{i}^{n+1})^{2}+(\phi_{i}^{n})^{2}-2)\sum_{j\neq i}\left[((\phi_{j}^{n+\frac{1}{2}})^{2}-1)^{2}\right]

Similarly,

\displaystyle\frac{f_{w}(\phi_{i}^{n+1})-f_{w}(\phi_{i}^{n})}{\phi_{i}^{n+1}-\phi_{i}^{n}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle q_{w_{1}}(\phi_{i}^{n+1}+\phi_{i}^{n}+2)(\phi_{w}+1)^{2}

(98)

\displaystyle-q_{w_{2}}(\phi_{i}^{n+1}+\phi_{i}^{n})((\phi_{i}^{n+1})^{2}+(\phi_{i}^{n})^{2}-2)(\phi_{w}^{2}-1)^{2}

and

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{\triangle t}{Re}\left(-2\|(\eta^{n})^{1/2}\mathbf{D}^{n+\frac{1}{2}}_{\eta}\|^{2}-\mathcal{M}\sum_{i}^{N}\|{\nabla}\mu_{i}^{n+\frac{1}{2}}\|^{2}-\xi\sum_{i}^{N}\|\ \epsilon\phi_{i}^{n}\nabla\lambda_{i}^{n+\frac{1}{2}}\|^{2}\right.

\displaystyle\left.-\frac{1}{\kappa}\|\sum_{i}^{N}L(\phi_{i}^{n+\frac{1}{2}})\|_{w}^{2}-\|l_{s}^{-1/2}\mathbf{u}_{\tau}^{n+\frac{1}{2}}\|^{2}_{w}\right)~,

3.2
Fully discrete
C^{0}

The spatial discretization using
C^{0}
finite element is straight forward. Let
\Omega
be the domain of interest with a Lipschitz-continuous boundary
\partial\Omega
. Let
{\mathbf{W}_{b}}^{h}\subset\mathbf{W}_{b}
be a finite element space with respect to the triangulation of the domain
\Omega
. The fully discrete scheme of the system is to find

\left(\{\Phi_{h}\}^{n+1},\{\bm{U}\}_{h}^{n+1},\{F_{h}\}^{n+1},\{\Lambda_{h}\}^{n+1},\{p_{h}\}^{n+1},\{\mathbf{u}_{h}\}^{n+1}\right)\in\mathbf{W}_{b}^{h},

such that for any
(\psi_{1,h},...,\psi_{N,h},\chi_{1,h},...,\chi_{N,h},\zeta_{1,h},...,\zeta_{N,h},\Theta_{1,h},...,\Theta_{N,h},q_{h},\mathbf{v}_{h})\in\mathbf{W}_{b}^{h}
, the following scheme holds.

\displaystyle\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\int_{\Omega}\left(\frac{\mathbf{u}_{h}^{n+1}-\mathbf{u}_{h}^{n}}{\Delta t}+(\mathbf{u}_{h}^{n+\frac{1}{2}}\cdot{\nabla})\mathbf{u}_{h}^{n+\frac{1}{2}}+\frac{1}{Re}{\nabla}P_{h}^{n+\frac{1}{2}}\right)\cdot\mathbf{v}_{h}d\bm{x}\\
=-\int_{\Omega}\frac{1}{Re}(\eta_{h}^{n}({\nabla}\mathbf{u}_{h}^{n+\frac{1}{2}}+({\nabla}\mathbf{u}_{h}^{n+\frac{1}{2}})^{T})):{\nabla}\mathbf{v}_{h}d\bm{x}\\
~~~~~~~~~~+\sum_{i}\int_{\Omega}\frac{1}{Re}\mu_{i,h}^{n+\frac{1}{2}}{\nabla}\phi_{h}^{n+\frac{1}{2}}\cdot\mathbf{v}_{h}d\bm{x}-\sum_{i}\int_{\Omega}\frac{1}{Re}\lambda_{i,h}^{n+\frac{1}{2}}\mathcal{P}_{i,h}^{n}\delta_{i,h,\epsilon}:\mathbf{v}_{h}d\bm{x}\\
~~~~~~~~~~+\int_{\partial\Omega_{w}}\frac{1}{Re}\mathbf{n}\cdot(\eta_{h}^{n}({\nabla}\mathbf{u}_{h}^{n+\frac{1}{2}}+({\nabla}\mathbf{u}_{h}^{n+\frac{1}{2}})^{T})+\sum_{i}\lambda_{i,h}^{n+\frac{1}{2}}\mathcal{P}_{i,h}^{n}\delta_{i,\epsilon})\cdot\mathbf{v}_{h}d\bm{x}~,\\
\int_{\Omega}({\nabla}\cdot\mathbf{u}_{h}^{n+\frac{1}{2}})q_{h}d\bm{x}=0~,\\[8.53581pt]
\int_{\Omega}(\frac{\phi_{i,h}^{n+1}-\phi_{i,h}^{n}}{\Delta t}+(\mathbf{u}_{h}^{n+\frac{1}{2}}\cdot{\nabla})\phi_{i,h}^{n+\frac{1}{2}})\psi_{i,h}d\bm{x}=-\int_{\Omega}\mathcal{M}{\nabla}\mu_{i,h}^{n+\frac{1}{2}}{\nabla}\psi_{i,h}d\bm{x}~,\\[8.53581pt]
\int_{\Omega}\mu_{i,h}^{n+\frac{1}{2}}\chi_{i,h}d\bm{x}=\int_{\Omega}\left(\kappa_{B}\frac{1}{\epsilon^{2}}((\phi_{i,h}^{n+1})^{2}+(\phi_{i,h}^{n})^{2}+\phi_{i,h}^{n+1}\phi_{i,h}^{n}-1)f_{i,h}^{n+\frac{1}{2}}\right.\\
~~~~~~~~~~\left.+\mathcal{M}_{s}\frac{(S(\phi_{i,h}^{n+\frac{1}{2}})-S(\phi_{i,h,0}))}{S(\phi_{i,h,0})}(\frac{1}{4\epsilon}((\phi_{i,h}^{n+1})^{2}+(\phi_{i,h}^{n})^{2}-2)(\phi_{i,h}^{n+1}+\phi_{i,h}^{n}))\right)\chi_{i,h}d\bm{x}\\
~~~~~~~~~~+\int_{\Omega}(\kappa_{B}{\nabla}f_{i,h}^{n+\frac{1}{2}}+\mathcal{M}_{s}\epsilon\frac{(S(\phi_{i,h}^{n+\frac{1}{2}})-S(\phi_{i,h,0}))}{S(\phi_{i,h,0})}{\nabla}\phi_{i,h}^{n+\frac{1}{2}})\cdot{\nabla}\chi_{i,h}d\bm{x}\\
~~~~~~~~~~+\int_{\Omega}\alpha\frac{f_{w}(\phi_{i,h}^{n+1})-f_{w}(\phi_{i,h}^{n})}{\phi_{i,h}^{n+1}-\phi_{i,h}^{n}}\chi_{i,h}d\bm{x}+\int_{\Omega}\alpha\frac{(H_{i,h}^{n+1}-H_{i,h}^{n})}{\phi_{i,h}^{n+1}-\phi_{i,h}^{n}}\chi_{i,h}d\bm{x}\\
~~~~~~~~~~-\int_{\partial\Omega_{w}}(\kappa_{B}\partial_{\mathbf{n}}f_{i,h}^{n+\frac{1}{2}}+\mathcal{M}_{s}\epsilon\frac{(S(\phi_{i,h}^{n+\frac{1}{2}})-S(\phi_{i,h,0}))}{S(\phi_{i,h})}\partial_{\mathbf{n}}\phi_{i,h}^{n+\frac{1}{2}})\chi_{i,h}ds~,\\
\int_{\Omega}f_{i,h}^{n+\frac{1}{2}}\zeta_{i,h}=\int_{\Omega}\epsilon{\nabla}\phi_{i,h}^{n+\frac{1}{2}}\cdot{\nabla}\zeta_{i,h}+\int_{\Omega}\frac{1}{\epsilon}((\phi_{i,h}^{n+\frac{1}{2}})^{2}-1)\phi_{i,h}^{n+\frac{1}{2}}\zeta_{i,h}d\bm{x}\\
~~~~~~~~~~~-\int_{\partial\Omega_{w}}\varepsilon\partial_{\mathbf{n}}\phi_{i,h}^{n+\frac{1}{2}}\zeta_{i,h}d\bm{x}~,\\
\int_{\Omega}\delta_{i,h,\epsilon}\mathcal{P}_{i,h}^{n}:{\nabla}\mathbf{u}_{i,h}^{n+\frac{1}{2}}\Theta_{i,h}d\bm{x}-\int_{\Omega}\xi\epsilon^{2}((\phi_{i,h}^{n})^{2}\nabla\lambda_{i,h}^{n+\frac{1}{2}})\cdot{\nabla}\Theta_{i,h}d\bm{x}+\int_{\partial\Omega_{w}}\xi\epsilon^{2}((\phi_{i,h}^{n})^{2}\partial_{\mathbf{n}}\lambda_{i,h}^{n+\frac{1}{2}})\Theta_{i,h}d\bm{x}=0~.\end{array}\right.

5
Conclusions

In this paper, a thermodynamically consistent diffuse interface (phase-field) model for describing cell-wall, cell-cell interaction and aggregation is derived based on the energy variation method. The interactions of cellular and wall structures are modeled by introducing a new interaction energy with the help of the phase-field function.

Then an efficient scheme using
C^{0}
finite element spatial discretization and the mid-point temporal discretization is proposed to solve the obtained model equations. Thanks to the mid-point temporal discretization, the obtained numerical scheme is unconditionally energy stable. The model and parameters are calibrated with experimental data on cell deformation under different stretch force. Then effects of the adhesion strength on cell-wall and cell-cell interaction are studied. In the end, the calibrated model is used to model red blood cell motion near the vessel bifurcation. The results are consistent with the experimental observation.
