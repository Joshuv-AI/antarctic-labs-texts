On Lipschitz continuity and smoothness up to the boundary of solutions of hyperbolic Poisson’s equation
Miodrag Mateljević, Nikola Mutavdžić

Abstract
We solve the Dirichlet problem u | 𝔹 n = φ , \left.u\right|_{\mathbb{B}^{n}}=\varphi, for hyperbolic Poisson’s equation Δ h ​ u = μ \Delta_{h}u=\mu where φ ∈ L 1 ​ ( ∂ 𝔹 n ) \varphi\in L_{1}(\partial\mathbb{B}^{n}) and μ \mu is a measure that satisfies a growth condition. Next we present a short proof for Lipschitz continuity of solutions of certain hyperbolic Poisson’s equations, previously established at [ 3 ] . In addition, we investigate some alternative assumptions on hyperbolic Laplacian, which are connected with Riesz’s potential. Also, local Hölder continuity is proved for solution of certain hyperbolic Poisson’s equations. We show that, if u u is hyperbolic harmonic in the upper half-space, then ∂ u ∂ y ​ ( x 0 , y ) → 0 , y → 0 + \frac{\partial u}{\partial y}(x_{0},y)\to 0,y\to 0^{+} , when boundary function f f of the functions u u is differentiable at the boundary point x 0 x_{0} . As a corollary, we show C 1 ​ ( ℍ n ¯ ) C^{1}(\overline{\mathbb{H}^{n}}) smoothness of a hyperbolic harmonic function, which is reproduced from the C c 1 ​ ( ℝ n − 1 ) C_{c}^{1}(\mathbb{R}^{n-1}) boundary values.

Let us consider the following Dirichlet boundary problem
