General form of second main theorem on generalized p p -Parabolic manifolds for arbitrary closed subschemes
Duc Quang Si

Abstract
By introducing the notion of distributive constant for a family of closed subschemes, we establish a general form of the second main theorem for algebraic non-degenerate meromorphic mappings from a generalized p p -Parabolic manifold into a projective variety with arbitrary families of closed subschemes. As its consequence, we give a second main theorem for such meromorphic mappings intersecting arbitrary hypersurfaces with an explicitly truncation level for the counting functions.

In order to state our result, we first recall the notion of the Weil function of a closed subscheme on a projective variety due to K. Yamanoi

Let

It implies that

\displaystyle\|\int_{M\langle r\rangle}\max_{K\in\mathcal{K}}\sum_{j\in K}\epsilon_{Y_{j}}(A)\lambda_{Y_{j}}(f(x))\lambda_{Y_{j}}(f)\sigma\leq\|\int_{M\langle r\rangle}\max_{K\in\mathcal{K}}\sum_{j\in K}\lambda_{F_{j,K}}(f)\sigma.

Also, applying Theorem

&+c\left(m_{0}(\mathfrak{L};r,s)+{\rm Ric}_{p}(r,s)+\kappa\log^{+}\mathcal{Y}\left(r^{2}\right)+k\log^{+}r\right),\end{split}

where

).

Corollary 20
.

Let
V\subset{\mathbb{P}}^{N}({\mathbb{C}})
be a smooth complex projective variety of dimension
n\geq 1
and
(M,\tau,\omega)
be an
p
-Parabolic manifold. Let
f:M\rightarrow V
be an algebraically nondegenerate meromorphic mapping which satisfies the conditions
(\mathcal{A}_{1})
and
(\mathcal{A}_{2})
. Let
\{Q_{1},\ldots,Q_{q}\}
be a family of
q
hypersurfaces of
{\mathbb{P}}^{N}({\mathbb{C}})
with the distributive constant with respect to
V
equal to
\delta
. Let
d={\rm lcm}(\deg Q_{1},\ldots,\deg Q_{q})
. Then, for any
\epsilon>0
and
r>s>0
, we have

\displaystyle\|(q-\delta(n+1)-\epsilon)T_{f}(r,s)\leq

\displaystyle\sum_{j=1}^{q}\frac{1}{\deg Q_{j}}N_{f}^{[m]}(r,s;Q_{j})

\displaystyle+c\left(m_{0}(\mathfrak{L};r,s)+{\rm Ric}_{p}(r,s)+\kappa\log^{+}\mathcal{Y}\left(r^{2}\right)+\kappa\log^{+}r\right),

where

[x]
we denote the biggest integer not exceed the real number
x
.

Sketch of the proof.

As usual argument, we may assume that all
Q_{j}\ (1\leq j\leq q)

d
and
V\cap\bigcap_{j=1}^{q}Q_{j}=\emptyset

From (
16
), we have

\displaystyle\begin{split}&\|\ \sum_{j=1}^{q}m_{f}(r,Q_{j})\leq\delta(n+1)dT_{f}(r,s)\\
&-\frac{\delta(n+1)}{uH_{Y}(u)}N_{ramF}(r,s)+\frac{(2n+1)D\delta(n+1)}{u}\sum_{j=1}^{q}m_{f}\left(r,Q_{j}\right)\\
&+\frac{\delta(n+1)}{uH_{Y}(u)}\left(\frac{H_{Y}(u)\left(H_{Y}(u)-1\right)}{2}+\epsilon^{\prime}\right)\bigl[m_{0}(\mathfrak{L};r,s)+{\rm Ric}_{p}(r,s)\\
&+\kappa\log^{+}\mathcal{Y}\left(r^{2}\right)+\kappa\log^{+}(r)\bigl]+\epsilon^{\prime}\frac{\delta(n+1)}{H_{Y}(u)u}T_{F}(r,s)+O(1).\end{split}

(21)

Similar as (4.14) in
Qmm
, we have

\displaystyle\frac{\delta(n+1)}{duH_{Y}(u)}N_{ramF}(r,s)

\displaystyle\geq\frac{1}{d}\sum_{j=1}^{q}[N_{f}(r,s;Q_{j})-N_{f}^{[n_{u}]}(r,s;Q_{j})]

\displaystyle-\frac{\delta(2n+1)(n+1)D}{du}\sum_{j=1}^{q}N_{f}(r,s;Q_{j}).

21

\displaystyle\begin{split}&\|\quad d(q-\delta(n+1))T_{f}(r,s)\leq\sum_{j=1}^{q}N_{f}^{[n_{u}]}\left(r,s;Q_{j}\right)\\

We now choose
u
the smallest integer such that

\displaystyle u>d(2n+1)(n+1)qd^{n}\deg(V)\epsilon^{-1}\geq d(2n+1)(n+1)qD\epsilon^{-1},

and take

(25)
