On a conjecture of Gowers and Wolf
Daniel Altman

Abstract
Gowers and Wolf have conjectured that, given a set of linear forms { ψ i } i = 1 t \{\psi_{i}\}_{i=1}^{t} each mapping ℤ D \mathbb{Z}^{D} to ℤ \mathbb{Z} , if s s is an integer such that the functions ψ i s + 1 , … , ψ t s + 1 \psi_{i}^{s+1},\ldots,\psi_{t}^{s+1} are linearly independent, then averages of the form 𝔼 𝒙 ​ ∏ i = 1 t f ⁡ ( ψ i ​ ( 𝒙 ) ) \mathbb{E}_{\boldsymbol{x}}\prod_{i=1}^{t}f(\psi_{i}(\boldsymbol{x})) may be controlled by the Gowers U s + 1 U^{s+1} -norm of f f . We prove (a stronger version of) this conjecture.

Theorem 1.1
.

Let
\Psi=(\psi_{1},\ldots,\psi_{t})
be a collection of linear forms each mapping
\mathbb{Z}^{D}
to
\mathbb{Z}
, and let

be an integer such that the polynomials
\psi_{1}^{s+1},\ldots,\psi_{t}^{s+1}
are linearly independent. For

, let
f_{i}:[-N,N]\to\mathbb{C}
be functions bounded in magnitude by 1 (and defined to be zero outside of
[-N,N]
). For all
\varepsilon>0
there exists
\delta>0
such that if
\min_{i}||f_{i}||_{U^{s+1}[-N,N]}\leq\delta
, then

\left|\mathbb{E}_{\boldsymbol{x}\in[-N,N]^{D}}\prod_{i=1}^{t}f_{i}(\psi_{i}(\boldsymbol{x}))\right|\leq\varepsilon.

The strategy of proof employed is the same as the general ‘arithmetic regularity lemma and counting lemma’ strategy put forward in
[
5
]
. Unfortunately, the counting lemma
[
5
, Theorem 1.11]
applies only to systems of linear forms which satisfy the flag condition (indeed this is where

(a_{i})_{i=1}^{t}

1.1
Notation, conventions

The following notation and conventions are taken from related papers in the literature.

As in
[
9
, Appendix B]
, define Gowers norms on subsets of additive groups as follows. Let
Z
be an abelian group and let
A\subset Z
. For any function
f:A\to\mathbb{C}
and for all
s\geq 1
define the Gowers uniformity norm
||f||_{U^{s+1}(A)}
by

||f||_{U^{s+1}(A)}^{2^{s+1}}=\mathbb{E}_{\vbox{\Let@\restore@math@cr\default@tag\halign{\hfil$\m@th\scriptstyle#$&$\m@th\scriptstyle{}#$\hfil\cr&x,h:x+\omega\cdot h\in A\\
&\text{for all } \omega\in\lx@text@lbrace 0,1\lx@text@rbrace^{s+1}\crcr}}}\prod_{\omega\in\{0,1\}^{s+1}}\mathcal{C}^{|\omega|}f(x+\omega\cdot h),

where the
x
are elements of
Z
, the
h
are vectors in
Z^{s+1}
,
\mathcal{C}
is the complex conjugation operator and
|\omega|=\sum_{i=1}^{s+1}\omega_{i}
. As is noted in
[
9
, Appendix B]
, we have the relation

||f||_{U^{s+1}{(A)}}=||f1_{A}||_{U^{s+1}{(Z)}}/||1_{A}||_{U^{s+1}{(Z)}}.

As in
[
5
]
, we will use
o_{A\to\infty;M}(X)
as shorthand for a quantity bounded in magnitude by
c_{M}(A)X
where
c_{M}(A)\to 0
as
A\to\infty
for
M
fixed. We will also use slight variations on this notation which may be translated analogously.

A strengthened result in the flag case

The goal of this section is to establish the following theorem.

Theorem 2.1
.

Let
\Psi=(\psi_{1},\ldots,\psi_{t})
be a collection of linear forms each mapping
\mathbb{Z}^{D}
to
\mathbb{Z}
such that
\Psi
satisfies the flag condition. Let

be an integer such that the polynomials
\psi_{1}^{s+1},\ldots,\psi_{t}^{s+1}
are linearly independent. For
i=1,\ldots t
, let

be functions bounded in magnitude by 1 (and defined to be zero outside of

be a subset of

whose convex hull in

is a polytope with

|K|=\Theta_{\Psi,s,D,t}(N^{D})
. Let

be such that
\Psi(K)+(c,c,\ldots,c)\subset[N]^{t}
. For all

there exists

(which depends on

and

and

\min_{i}||f_{i}||_{U^{s+1}[N]}\leq\delta

\left|\mathbb{E}_{\boldsymbol{x}\in K}\prod_{i=1}^{t}f_{i}(\psi_{i}(\boldsymbol{x})+c)\right|\leq\varepsilon.

Theorem
2.1
is
[
5
, Theorem 1.13]

[

The additional features in Theorem

1.

we accommodate averages over large convex polytopes in

2.

we accommodate shifts of systems of linear forms,

3.

we obtain a result for averages comprising

-norm less than

In addition, we amend two parts of the argument in
[

1.

We give an explicit and quantitative argument for the case in which

The following inequality holds:

\displaystyle|\mathbb{E}_{n\in[N]}|f_{j}(n)|^{2}|^{2}

We claim that there is a sequence of scalars

Let
(a_{i})_{i=1}^{t}

on

.

The following inequality holds:

||\tilde{f}_{i}||_{U^{s+1}[2aN]}\leq||f_{i}||_{U^{s+1}[-N,N]}+o_{N\to\infty}(1).

We compute directly from the definition of Gowers norms (cf. Section

\displaystyle||\tilde{f}_{i}||_{U^{s+1}[2aN]}^{2^{s+1}}

\displaystyle\left|\mathbb{E}_{\boldsymbol{x}\in[-N,N]^{D}}\prod_{i=1}^{t}f_{i}(\psi_{i}(\boldsymbol{x}))\right|

\displaystyle=\left|\mathbb{E}_{\boldsymbol{x}\in K}\prod_{i=1}^{t}\tilde{f}_{i}(a_{i}\psi_{i}(\boldsymbol{x})+aN)\right|.

, which is zero when
|x|>(1+1/\sqrt{N})\varepsilon N/2
, and which linearly interpolates otherwise (
\chi_{0}
is the Fourier transform of a de la Vallée Poussin kernel). It is standard that
||\hat{\chi}_{0}||_{1}=O_{\varepsilon}(1)
(one may see this by writing
\chi_{0}
as the convolution of suitably normalised characteristic functions of intervals).
Define
\chi
by
\chi(x)=\chi_{0}((x-c)/q)
for all
x=c\ (\mo q)
and zero otherwise. Then we have
||\hat{\chi}||_{1}=O_{\varepsilon}(1)
and furthermore that
1_{P}-\chi
is supported on a set of size
o_{\varepsilon}(N)
. The former property and Fourier inversion yield that

||\chi f||_{U^{s+1}[N]}\leq||\hat{\chi}||_{1}\sup_{\theta}||e(\theta\cdot)f||_{U^{s+1}[N]}=O_{\varepsilon}(||f||_{U^{s+1}[N]}),

using that the
U^{s+1}[N]
-norm is linear phase invariant (see
[
10
, Exercise 1.3.21]
). The latter property yields that
||(1_{P}-\chi)f||_{U^{s+1}[N]}=o_{\varepsilon}(1)
. We are ready to conclude:

||1_{P}f||_{U^{s+1}[N]}\leq||\chi f||_{U^{s+1}[N]}+||(1_{P}-\chi)f||_{U^{s+1}[N]}=O_{\varepsilon}(||f||_{U^{s+1}[N]})+o_{\varepsilon}(1).

∎
