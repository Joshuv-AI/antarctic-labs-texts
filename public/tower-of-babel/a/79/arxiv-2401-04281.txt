S. B. Dubovichenko, A.S. Tkachenko, R. Ya. Kezerashvili

Abstract
We explicitly present the comparison of the results for the astrophysical S − S- factor and reaction rate for the 6 Li( p ​ ; ​ γ p\mathord{\mathchar 59\relax}\gamma ) 7 Be capture process at astrophysical energies, presented in Phys. Rev. Phys. Rev. C 105 , 065806 (2022) and Phys. Rev. C 108 . 065801 (2023) obtained within the famework of potential models. We demonstrate that both potential model approaches describe successfully the astropgisical S − S- factor and reaction rate similtaneusly and reproduce the LUNA Collaboration [Phys. Rev. C 102 , 052802(R) (2020)] results.
