CONSTRUCTIONS OF k k -g-FUSION FRAMES AND THEIR DUALS IN HILBERT SPACES
Vahid Sadri, Reza Ahmadi, Asghar Rahimi

Abstract
. Frames for operators or k k -frames were recently considered by G a ˘ \breve{\mbox{a}} vruta (2012) in connection with atomic systems. Also generalized frames are important frames in the Hilbert space of bounded linear operators. Fusion frames, which are a special case of generalized frames have various applications. This paper introduces the concept of generalized fusion frames for operators aka k k -g-fusion frames and we get some results for characterization of these frames. We further discuss on duals and Q-duals in connection with k k -g-fusion frames. Also we obtain some useful identities for these frames. We also give several methods to construct k k -g-fusion frames. The results of this paper can be used in sampling theory which are developed by g-frames and especially fusion frames. In the end, we discuss the stability of a more general perturbation for k k -g-fusion frames.

2.
k
-g- Fusion Frames

We define the space
\mathscr{H}_{2}:=(\sum_{j\in\mathbb{J}}\oplus H_{j})_{\ell_{2}}
by

(2)

\displaystyle\mathscr{H}_{2}=\big\{\{f_{j}\}_{j\in\mathbb{J}}\ :\ f_{j}\in H_{j},\ \sum_{j\in\mathbb{J}}\|f_{j}\|^{2}<\infty\big\}

with the inner product defined by

\langle\{f_{j}\},\{g_{j}\}\rangle=\sum_{j\in\mathbb{J}}\langle f_{j},g_{j}\rangle.

It is clear that
\mathscr{H}_{2}
is a Hilbert space with pointwise operations.

Definition 3
.

Let
W=\{W_{j}\}_{j\in\mathbb{J}}
be a collection of closed
subspaces of
H
,
\{v_{j}\}_{j\in\mathbb{J}}
be a family of
weights, i.e.
v_{j}>0
,
\Lambda_{j}\in\mathcal{B}(H,H_{j})
for each

j\in\mathbb{J}
and
k\in\mathcal{B}(H)
. We say
\Lambda:=(W_{j},\Lambda_{j},v_{j})
is a
k
-g- fusion frame for
H
if there exist

0<A\leq B<\infty
such that for each
f\in H
,

(3)

\displaystyle A\|k^{*}f\|^{2}\leq\sum_{j\in\mathbb{J}}v_{j}^{2}\|\Lambda_{j}\pi_{W_{j}}f\|^{2}\leq B\|f\|^{2}.

When
k=id_{H}
, we get the g-fusion frame for
H
. Throughout this paper,
\Lambda
will be a triple
(W_{j},\Lambda_{j},v_{j})
with
j\in\mathbb{J}
unless otherwise noted.
We say
\Lambda
is a Parseval
k
-g-fusion frame whenever

\sum_{j\in\mathbb{J}}v_{j}^{2}\|\Lambda_{j}\pi_{W_{j}}f\|^{2}=\|k^{*}f\|^{2}.

The synthesis and the analysis operators in the
k
-g-fusion frames are defined by

\displaystyle T_{\Lambda}

\displaystyle:\mathscr{H}_{2}\longrightarrow H\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ ,\ \ \ \ \ \ T_{\Lambda}^{*}:H\longrightarrow\mathscr{H}_{2}

\displaystyle T_{\Lambda}(\{f_{j}\}_{j\in\mathbb{J}})

\displaystyle=\sum_{j\in\mathbb{J}}v_{j}\pi_{W_{j}}\Lambda_{j}^{*}f_{j}\ \ \ ,\ \ \ \ T_{\Lambda}^{*}(f)=\{v_{j}\Lambda_{j}\pi_{W_{j}}f\}_{j\in\mathbb{J}}.

Thus, the
k
-g-fusion frame operator is given by

S_{\Lambda}f=T_{\Lambda}T^{*}_{\Lambda}f=\sum_{j\in\mathbb{J}}v_{j}^{2}\pi_{W_{j}}\Lambda^{*}_{j}\Lambda_{j}\pi_{W_{j}}f

and

(4)

\langle S_{\Lambda}f,f\rangle=\sum_{j\in\mathbb{J}}v_{j}^{2}\|\Lambda_{j}\pi_{W_{j}}f\|^{2},

for all
f\in H
. Therefore,

(5)

\langle Akk^{*}f,f\rangle\leq\langle S_{\Lambda}f,f\rangle\leq\langle Bf,f\rangle

or

(6)

Akk^{*}\leq S_{\Lambda}\leq BI.

Hence, we conclude that:

Proposition 1
.

Let
\Lambda
be a g-fusion Bessel sequence for
H
. Then
\Lambda
is a
k
-g-fusion frame for
H
if and only if there exists
A>0
such that
S_{\Lambda}\geq Akk^{*}
.

Remark 1
.

In
k
-g-fusion frames, like
k
-frames and
k
-fusion frames, the
k
-g-fusion operator is not invertible. But,
if
k\in\mathcal{B}(H)
has closed range, then the operator
S_{\Lambda}
is an invertible operator on a subspace of
\mathcal{R}(k)\subset H
.
Indeed, suppose that
f\in\mathcal{R}(k)
, then

\|f\|^{2}=\|(k^{\dagger}|_{\mathcal{R}(k)})^{*}k^{*}f\|^{2}\leq\|k^{\dagger}\|^{2}\|k^{*}f\|^{2}.

Thus, we have

A\|k^{\dagger}\|^{-2}\|f\|^{2}\leq\langle S_{\Lambda}f,f\rangle\leq B\|f\|^{2},

which implies that
S_{\Lambda}:\mathcal{R}(k)\rightarrow S_{\Lambda}(\mathcal{R}(k))
is a homeomorphism, furthermore, for each
f\in S_{\Lambda}(\mathcal{R}(k))
we have

B^{-1}\|f\|^{2}\leq\langle(S_{\Lambda}|_{\mathcal{R}(k)})^{-1}f,f\rangle\leq A^{-1}\|k^{\dagger}\|^{2}\|f\|^{2}.

Remark 2
.

Since
S_{\Lambda}\in\mathcal{B}(H)
is positive and self-adjoint and
\mathcal{B}(H)
is a
C^{*}
-algabra, then
S^{-1}_{\Lambda}
is positive and self-adjoint too whenever
k\in\mathcal{B}(H)
has closed range. Now, for each
f\in S_{\Lambda}(\mathcal{R}(k))
we can write

\displaystyle\langle kf,f\rangle

\displaystyle=\langle kf,S_{\Lambda}S_{\Lambda}^{-1}f\rangle

\displaystyle=\langle S_{\Lambda}(kf),S_{\Lambda}^{-1}f\rangle

\displaystyle=\langle\sum_{j\in\mathbb{J}}v_{j}^{2}\pi_{W_{j}}\Lambda^{*}_{j}\Lambda_{j}\pi_{W_{j}}kf,S_{\Lambda}^{-1}f\rangle

\displaystyle=\sum_{j\in\mathbb{J}}v_{j}^{2}\langle S_{\Lambda}^{-1}\pi_{W_{j}}\Lambda^{*}_{j}\Lambda_{j}\pi_{W_{j}}kf,f\rangle.

Theorem 3.6
.

Let
\Lambda
be a Parseval

. If

and

\displaystyle=kk^{*}(S_{\Lambda,\mathbb{I}}+S_{\Lambda,\mathbb{I}^{c}})+S^{2}_{\Lambda,\mathbb{I}}+S^{2}_{\Lambda,\mathbb{I}^{c}}

Lemma 5
.

(

Let U be a Linear operator on a Banach space X and assume that there exist

\displaystyle\frac{1-\lambda_{1}}{1+\lambda_{2}}\|x\|\leq\|Ux\|\leq\frac{1+\lambda_{1}}{1-\lambda_{2}}\|x\|

\displaystyle\frac{1-\lambda_{2}}{1+\lambda_{1}}\|x\|\leq\|U^{-1}x\|\leq\frac{1+\lambda_{2}}{1-\lambda_{1}}\|x\|

Theorem 4.1
