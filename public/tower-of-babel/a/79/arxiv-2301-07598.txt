Counting equivariant sheaves on K3 surfaces
Yunfeng Jiang, Hao Max Sun

Abstract
. We study the equivariant sheaf counting theory on K3 surfaces with finite group actions. Let 𝒮 = [ S / G ] {\mathcal{S}}=[S/G] be a global quotient stack, where S S is a K3 surface and G G is a finite group acting as symplectic homomorphisms on S S . We show that the Joyce invariants counting Gieseker semistable sheaves on 𝒮 {\mathcal{S}} are independent on the Bridgeland stability conditions. As an application we prove the multiple cover formula of Y. Toda for the counting invariants for semistable sheaves on local K3 surfaces with a symplectic finite group action.

Introduction

Let
S
be a smooth projective surface over
{\mathbb{C}}
and

the local K3 surface. The sheaf counting theory of

A multiple cover formula for the Joyce invariants for

The multiple cover formula is essential to the calculation of the generating series of the

The multiple cover formula of Toda was generalized and proved to be true for étale K3 gerbes in

Both the generating series of the

1.1.
Sheaf counting on
[S/G]

Let
S
be a smooth projective K3 surface and
G
a finite group acting on
S
as symplectic morphisms. We consider the surface Deligne-Mumford stack
{\mathcal{S}}=[S/G]
. The action of
G
on
S
only has isolated fixed points, and the classification of the number of fixed points corresponding to different finite group actions are given in
[
9
, Chapter 15]
. Thus all the stacky locus of
{\mathcal{S}}=[S/G]
are stacky points, and they are given by
ADE
type singularities on the coarse moduli space
\overline{{\mathcal{S}}}=S/G
. Let
\sigma:Y\to S/G
be the minimal resolution. It is a crepant resolution and the exceptional curves over a singular point in
S/G
are given by
ADE
type Dynkin diagrams.

Let
\mbox{Coh}({\mathcal{S}})
be the abelian category of coherent sheaves on
{\mathcal{S}}
, which is by definition the category of
G
-equivariant sheaves on
S
. We fix an ample divisor
\omega
on
\overline{{\mathcal{S}}}
.
Similar to the K3 surface, we define the orbifold Mukai vector
v_{\orb}(E)
for any object
E\in\mbox{Coh}({\mathcal{S}})
as:

v_{\orb}(E):=\widetilde{\Ch}(E)\cdot\sqrt{\widetilde{\td}_{{\mathcal{S}}}}=(\widetilde{\Ch}_{0}(E),\widetilde{\Ch}_{1}(E),\widetilde{\Ch}_{0}(E)+\widetilde{\Ch}_{2}(E))\in H^{*}_{\CR}({\mathcal{S}})

where
\widetilde{\Ch}
is the orbifold Chern character and
\widetilde{\td}
is the orbifold Todd class.
Here
H^{*}_{\CR}({\mathcal{S}})
is the Chen-Ruan cohomology of
{\mathcal{S}}
. Let

\Gamma_{0}^{G}
be the group of all the orbifold Mukai vectors.

We take

{\mathcal{M}}(\mbox{Coh}({\mathcal{S}}))
to be the moduli stack of coherent sheaves on
{\mathcal{S}}
, which is an algebraic stack locally of finite type over
{\mathbb{C}}
.
We work on the moduli stack
{\mathcal{M}}_{\omega}(v_{\orb})
of
\omega
-Gieseker semistable sheaves

E\in\mbox{Coh}({\mathcal{S}})
with
v_{\orb}(E)=v_{\orb}\in\Gamma_{0}^{G}
.
In general, one takes a generating sheaf
\Xi
on
{\mathcal{S}}=[S/G]
as in
[
20
]
and define the moduli stack of semistable sheaves on
{\mathcal{S}}=[S/G]

using the modified Gieseker stability defined by
\Xi
. Since
{\mathcal{S}}=[S/G]
only has isolated stacky points, the modified Gieseker stability is equivalent to the
\omega
-Gieseker stability.

There is a Hall algebra structure on
\mbox{Coh}({\mathcal{S}})
and we denote it by
{\mathcal{H}}(\mbox{Coh}({\mathcal{S}}))
, see §
2.4
. Thus we have an element

\delta_{\omega,{\mathcal{S}}}:=[{\mathcal{M}}_{\omega}(v_{\orb})\hookrightarrow{\mathcal{M}}(\mbox{Coh}({\mathcal{S}}))]\in{\mathcal{H}}(\mbox{Coh}({\mathcal{S}}))

We take its logarithm as:

(1.1.1)

\epsilon_{\omega,{\mathcal{S}}}(v_{\orb}):=\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}\ell\geq 1,v_{1}+\cdots+v_{\ell}=v_{\orb},v_{i}\in\Gamma_{0}^{G}\\
\overline{\chi}_{\omega,v_{i}}(m)=\overline{\chi}_{\omega,v_{\orb}}(m)\end{subarray}}\frac{(-1)^{\ell-1}}{\ell}\delta_{\omega,{\mathcal{S}}}(v_{1})\star\cdots\star\delta_{\omega,{\mathcal{S}}}(v_{\ell})

where
\overline{\chi}_{\omega,v_{\orb}}(m)
is the reduced Hilbert polynomial.

Let

C({\mathcal{S}}):=\mathop{\rm Im}(v_{\orb}:\mbox{Coh}({\mathcal{S}})\to\Gamma_{0}^{G}).

Then the Joyce invariants are defined as follows.
If
v_{\orb}\in C({\mathcal{S}})
, we define

(1.1.2)

J^{\omega}(v_{\orb})=\lim_{q^{\frac{1}{2}}\to 1}(q-1)P_{q}(\epsilon_{\omega,{\mathcal{S}}}(v_{\orb}))

where
P_{q}(-)
is the Poincaré polynomial of the stack. The Joyce invariants are independent to the polarization
\omega
.

Generalizing the construction in
[
3
]
, there exist Bridgeland stability conditions
\sigma=({\mathcal{Z}},{\mathcal{A}})
on the bounded derived category
D^{b}(\mbox{Coh}({\mathcal{S}}))
of coherent sheaves on
{\mathcal{S}}
.
Here Bridgeland stability condition
\sigma=({\mathcal{Z}},{\mathcal{A}})
is a pair, where
{\mathcal{A}}
is the heart of a bounded
t
-structure on
D^{b}(\mbox{Coh}({\mathcal{S}}))
, and
{\mathcal{Z}}:K({\mathcal{A}})\to{\mathbb{C}}
is the central charge satisfying certain conditions, see §
2.6
for more details.
We define
{\mathcal{M}}^{\sigma}(v_{\orb})
to be the moduli stack of
\sigma
-semistable objects
E\in{\mathcal{A}}
with
v_{\orb}(E)=v_{\orb}
.
The heart
{\mathcal{A}}
is an abelian category.
Let
{\mathcal{H}}({\mathcal{A}})
be the Hall algebra of
{\mathcal{A}}
. Then similar to (
1.1.1
), we replace the moduli stack there by the moduli stack of
\sigma
-semistable objects in
{\mathcal{A}}
and define the Joyce invariants

J^{\sigma}(v_{\orb})
in the same way.
Our first main result is:

Theorem 1.1
.

(Theorem
2.8
)
The Joyce invariant
J^{\sigma}(v_{\orb})
is independent to the stability conditions. Moreover

J^{\sigma}(v_{\orb})=J^{\omega}(v_{\orb}).

We prove Theorem
1.1
in the remaining subsections in §
2

1.2.
Multiple cover formula

We generalize the above counting invariants to the local orbifold K3 surfaces. Let
{\mathfrak{X}}:={\mathcal{S}}\times{\mathbb{C}}
be the local orbifold K3 surface, which is a Calabi-Yau threefold Deligne-Mumford stack. Let

\pi:{\mathfrak{X}}\to{\mathbb{C}}
be the projection and let

\mbox{Coh}_{\pi}({\mathfrak{X}})\subset\mbox{Coh}({\mathfrak{X}})
be the subcategory of coherent sheaves supported on the fibers of
\pi
.
Let
{\mathcal{M}}(\mbox{Coh}_{\pi}({\mathfrak{X}}))
be the moduli stack of objects in
\mbox{Coh}_{\pi}({\mathfrak{X}})
, which is an algebraic stack locally of finite type.
Still fix the polarization
\omega
, and let
{\mathcal{M}}_{\omega,{\mathfrak{X}}}(v_{\orb})
be the moduli stack of
\omega
-Gieseker semistable sheaves

E\in\mbox{Coh}_{\pi}({\mathfrak{X}})
with
v_{\orb}(E)=v_{\orb}\in\Gamma_{0}^{G}
.
Let
{\mathcal{H}}(\mbox{Coh}_{\pi}({\mathfrak{X}}))
be the Hall algebra. We have

\delta_{\omega,{\mathfrak{X}}}:=[{\mathcal{M}}_{\omega,{\mathfrak{X}}}(v_{\orb})\hookrightarrow{\mathcal{M}}(\mbox{Coh}_{\pi}({\mathfrak{X}}))]\in{\mathcal{H}}(\mbox{Coh}_{\pi}({\mathfrak{X}}))

Its logarithm is:

(1.2.1)

\epsilon_{\omega,{\mathfrak{X}}}(v_{\orb}):=\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}\ell\geq 1,v_{1}+\cdots+v_{\ell}=v_{\orb},v_{i}\in\Gamma_{0}^{G}\\
\overline{\chi}_{\omega,v_{i}}(m)=\overline{\chi}_{\omega,v_{\orb}}(m)\end{subarray}}\frac{(-1)^{\ell-1}}{\ell}\delta_{\omega,{\mathfrak{X}}}(v_{1})\star\cdots\star\delta_{\omega,{\mathfrak{X}}}(v_{\ell})

The Joyce invariants counting semistable coherent sheaves in
\mbox{Coh}_{\pi}({\mathfrak{X}})
are given by

J_{{\mathfrak{X}}}^{\omega}(v_{\orb})=\lim_{q^{\frac{1}{2}}\to 1}(q-1)P_{q}(\epsilon_{\omega,{\mathfrak{X}}}(v_{\orb}))\in{\mathbb{Q}}.

Our second result is the multiple cover formula of such invariants:

Theorem 1.2
.

(Theorem
3.7
)
We have a multiple cover formula for
J_{{\mathfrak{X}}}(v_{\orb})
:

J_{{\mathfrak{X}}}(v_{\orb})=\sum_{k|v_{\orb},k\geq 1}\frac{1}{k^{2}}\chi(\Hilb^{n,\mathfrak{m}}({\mathcal{S}}))

where the data
n,\mathfrak{m}
are determined by

\frac{1}{k}v_{\orb}=(r,(\beta,\mathfrak{m}),n)
, and
\Hilb^{n,\mathfrak{m}}({\mathcal{S}})
is the Hilbert scheme of the orbifold K3 surface

{\mathcal{S}}
with data
(n,\mathfrak{m})
.

We prove Theorem
1.2
by working on the sheaf counting invariants on the compactification

\overline{{\mathfrak{X}}}={\mathcal{S}}\times{\mathbb{P}}^{1}
and
\overline{Z}=Y\times{\mathbb{P}}^{1}
.
For the crepant resolution
Y\to S/G
. From
[
5
]

There exists a derived equivalence

\Phi:D(\mbox{Coh}({\mathcal{S}}))\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\sim}}{{\longrightarrow}}D(\mbox{Coh}(Y)).

The equivalence induces the following equivalence:

\Phi:D(\mbox{Coh}_{\pi}(\overline{{\mathfrak{X}}}))\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\sim}}{{\longrightarrow}}D(\mbox{Coh}_{\pi}(\overline{Z}))

where
\mbox{Coh}_{\pi}(\overline{{\mathfrak{X}}})\subset\mbox{Coh}(\overline{{\mathfrak{X}}})
(rep.
\mbox{Coh}_{\pi}(\overline{Z})\subset\mbox{Coh}(\overline{Z})
) is the subcategory of
coherent sheaves on
\overline{{\mathfrak{X}}}
(rep.
\overline{Z}
) supported on the fibers under
\pi:\overline{{\mathfrak{X}}}\to{\mathbb{P}}^{1}
(rep.
\pi:\overline{Z}\to{\mathbb{P}}^{1}
).
Then we define the Joyce invariants
\overline{J}_{\overline{{\mathfrak{X}}}}(v_{\orb})
(rep.
\overline{J}_{\overline{Z}}(v_{Y})
) on the categories above. The invariants satisfy the relation

\overline{J}_{\overline{{\mathfrak{X}}}}(v_{\orb})=2J_{{\mathfrak{X}}}(v_{\orb})
.
We prove the automorphic property for the invariants
J_{{\mathfrak{X}}}(v_{\orb})
, and show that it is the same as
J_{Z}(v_{Y})
under the
isomorphism
\Phi_{*}:H^{*}_{\CR}({\mathcal{S}})\to H^{*}(Y)
such that
\Phi_{*}(v_{\orb})=v_{Y}

J_{Z}(v_{Y})
in

]
,
[

2.1.
Symplectic action on K3 surfaces

Let
S
be a smooth projective K3 surface, and
G
a finite group which acts on
S
as symplectic morphisms.
We consider the surface Deligne-Mumford stack
{\mathcal{S}}:=[S/G]
. From
[
9
, Chapter 15]
, the action only has isolated fixed points which are all ADE type singularities on the coarse moduli space

\overline{{\mathcal{S}}}=S/G
. For instance, when
G=\mu_{2}
, there are totally
8
isolated
A_{1}
-type singularities, and this is called the Nikulin involution.
More basic properties of the symplectic action on the K3 surface
S
can be found in
[
9
, Chapter 15]
.

2.2.
Mukai lattice

For the K3 surface
S
with
H^{1}(S,{\mathcal{O}}_{S})=0
, the Mukai lattice is define by:

\widetilde{H}(S,{\mathbb{Z}}):=H^{0}(S,{\mathbb{Z}})\oplus H^{2}(S,{\mathbb{Z}})\oplus H^{4}(S,{\mathbb{Z}})

and for
v_{i}=(r_{i},\beta_{i},n_{i})\in\widetilde{H}(S,{\mathbb{Z}})
(
i=1,2
), the Mukai paring is defined as:

(2.2.1)

\langle v_{1},v_{2}\rangle=\beta_{1}\beta_{2}-r_{1}n_{2}-r_{2}n_{1}.

There is a weight two Hodge structure on
\widetilde{H}(S,{\mathbb{Z}})\otimes{\mathbb{C}}
, which is given by:

\begin{cases}\widetilde{H}^{2,0}(S):=H^{2,0}(S);\\
\widetilde{H}^{1,1}(S):=H^{0,0}(S)\oplus H^{1,1}(S)\oplus H^{2,2}(S);\\
\widetilde{H}^{0,2}(S):=H^{0,2}(S).\end{cases}

Define:

\Gamma_{0}:=\widetilde{H}(S,{\mathbb{Z}})\cap\widetilde{H}^{1,1}(S)={\mathbb{Z}}\oplus NS(S)\oplus{\mathbb{Z}}.

The Mukai vector
v(E)\in\Gamma_{0}
for any
E\in D^{b}(\mbox{Coh}(S))
is defined by

v(E):=\Ch(E)\sqrt{\td_{S}}=(\Ch_{0}(E),\Ch_{1}(E),\Ch_{0}(E)+\Ch_{2}(E)).

Riemann-Roch theorem tells us that

\chi(E,F)=-\langle v(E),v(F)\rangle.

2.3.
Orbifold Mukai vector

Let
{\mathcal{S}}=[S/G]
be the surface Deligne-Mumford stack given by the symplectic action of
G

. We consider the Chen-Ruan cohomology

H^{*}_{\CR}({\mathcal{S}},{\mathbb{Q}})=\bigoplus_{i\in I_{{\mathcal{S}}}}H^{*-2\age({\mathcal{S}}_{i})}({\mathcal{S}}_{i},{\mathbb{Q}})

. We define the orbifold Mukai vector

for any

2.6.
Bridgeland stability conditions

We will construct the Bridgeland stability conditions on

D^{b}(\mbox{Coh}({\mathcal{S}}))
.

Definition 2.2
.

A Bridgeland stability condition on
D^{b}(\mbox{Coh}({\mathcal{S}}))
is a pair

\sigma=(Z,{\mathcal{A}})
consisting of the heart of a bounded
t
-structure

{\mathcal{A}}\subset D^{b}(\mbox{Coh}({\mathcal{S}}))
and a group homomorphism map (called

such that the following are

(1)

Z
satisfies the following positivity property for any
0\neq E\in{\mathcal{A}}
:

Z(E)\in\{re^{i\pi\phi}:r>0,0<\phi\leq 1\}.

(2)

Every

{\mathcal{A}}
has a Harder-Narasimhan filtration in
{\mathcal{A}}
with
respect to
\nu_{\sigma}
-stability, here the slope
\nu_{\sigma}

of an object
