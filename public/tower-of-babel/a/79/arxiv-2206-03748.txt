Normalized solutions for the Klein Gordon-Dirac system
Vittorio Coti Zelati, Margherita Nolasco

Abstract
. We prove the existence of a stationary solution for the system describing the interaction between an electron coupled with a massless scalar field (a photon). We find a solution, with fixed L 2 L^{2} -norm, by variational methods, as a critical point of an energy functional.



1.
Introduction

We study the interaction electron-photon analyizing the Euler-Lagrange
equations for a system consisting of a spinor field coupled with a
massless scalar field. More precisely our system consists of the Dirac
equation coupled with a massless Klein-Gordon equation, and look for
normalized and stationary solutions of the following system

(1.1)

\begin{cases}(-i\gamma^{\mu}\partial_{\mu}+m-s\varphi)\psi=0&\text{in }\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{R}^{3}\\
\partial^{\mu}\partial_{\mu}\varphi=4\pi s(\psi,\beta\psi)&\text{in }\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{R}^{3}\end{cases},

where
\psi\colon\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{R}^{3}\to\mathbb{C}^{4}
,
\varphi\colon\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{R}^{3}\to\mathbb{R}
,
m>0
is the mass of the electron,
s>0
is the
coupling constant,
\gamma^{\mu}
are the
4\times 4
Dirac matrices

\gamma^{0}=\beta=\begin{pmatrix}I&0\\
0&-I\end{pmatrix}\qquad\gamma^{k}=\begin{pmatrix}0&\sigma^{k}\\
-\sigma^{k}&0\end{pmatrix},\quad k=1,\ldots,3

and
\sigma^{k}
are the
2\times 2
Pauli matrices

\sigma^{1}=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\
-1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad\sigma^{2}=\begin{pmatrix}0&-i\\
i&0\end{pmatrix},\quad\sigma^{3}=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\
0&-1\end{pmatrix},

and
(z,w)=\sum_{i=1}^{4}\bar{z}_{i}w_{i}
the scalar
product between
z
,
w\in\mathbb{C}^{4}
.

This problem is closely related to the one studied in

[
6
]
, and we will prove existence of a solution of
(
1.1
) with essentially the same methods
developed in that article (see also
[
2
]
).

More precisely we prove existence of stationary, normalized solutions
of this system, that is solutions
(\omega,\psi)
of the following
problem:

(1.2)

\begin{cases}(-i\boldsymbol{\alpha}\cdot\nabla+m\beta-s\varphi\beta)\psi=\omega\psi&\text{in }\mathbb{R}^{3}\\
-\Delta\varphi=4\pi(\psi,\beta\psi)&\text{in }\mathbb{R}^{3}\\
\left|\psi\right|_{2}^{2}=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{3}}\lvert\psi(t,x)\rvert^{2}\,dx=1\end{cases},

where
\alpha_{i}=\beta\gamma_{i}
,
i=1,\ldots,3
. From

-\Delta\varphi=4\pi(\psi,\beta\psi)
we deduce that

\varphi=(\psi,\beta\psi)*\frac{1}{\lvert x\rvert}

and hence our problem reduces to

(1.3)

\begin{cases}(-i\boldsymbol{\alpha}\cdot\nabla+m\beta-s(\psi,\beta\psi)*\frac{1}{\lvert x\rvert}\beta)\psi=\omega\psi&\text{in }\mathbb{R}^{3}\\
\left|\psi\right|_{2}^{2}=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{3}}\lvert\psi(t,x)\rvert^{2}\,dx=1\end{cases}.

Our result is

Theorem 1.4
.

For all
s\in(0,\frac{1}{8\pi})
, there exists
\omega\in(0,m)
and
\psi\in H^{1/2}(\mathbb{R}^{3},\mathbb{C}^{4})

solutions of problem (
1.3
).

In the article
[
3
]
the authors prove
using critical point theory the existence of one stationary solution
of equation (
1.1
) but do not prescribe its

L^{2}
-norm.

We will find such a solution as a critical point of the functional

I(\psi)=\frac{1}{2}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{3}}(H\psi,\psi)-\frac{s}{4}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{3}\times\mathbb{R}^{3}}\frac{(\psi,\beta\psi)(x)(\psi,\beta\psi)(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}

restricted on the manifold
\left|\psi\right|_{2}^{2}=1
.
Here

H=-i\boldsymbol{\alpha}\cdot\nabla+m\beta.

The functional
I
is strongly indefinite, and, following the method
introduced in
[
2
,
6
]
, the solution will
be found via a
\min
-
\max
procedure consisting in minimizing the
supremum of
I
over subspaces of dimension
1
in the positive energy
subspace of the linear operator
H
, see proposition

2.13
. Let us remark here that we know very few
results on the existence of
normalized
solutions for Dirac
equation (and more generally for strongly indefinite problems – one
of these is
[
1
]
).

1.1.
Notation and background results

We let
\lvert u\rvert^{p}_{p}=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{3}}\lvert u(x)\rvert^{p}
,

(u\,|\,v)=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{3}}u(x)v(x)
.

Let us recall some well known facts on the Dirac operator
H
(see for
more details
[
7
]
):
H
is a first order, self-adjoint
operator on
H^{1}(\mathbb{R}^{3},\mathbb{C}^{4})
with purely
absolutely continuous spectrum given by

\sigma(H)=(-\infty,-m]\cup[m,+\infty).

The orthogonal projectors
\Lambda_{\pm}
on the positive and negative
energies subspaces are such that

H\Lambda_{\pm}=\Lambda_{\pm}H=\pm\sqrt{-\Delta+m^{2}}\Lambda_{\pm}=\pm\Lambda_{\pm}\sqrt{-\Delta+m^{2}}

and hence

\int(\psi(x),H\psi(x))\,dx=\left|(-\Delta+m^{2})^{1/4}\Lambda_{+}\psi\right|_{2}^{2}-\left|(-\Delta+m^{2})^{1/4}\Lambda_{-}\psi\right|_{2}^{2}.

We will denote
X=H^{1/2}(\mathbb{R}^{3},\mathbb{C}^{4})
,
X_{\pm}=\Lambda_{\pm}X
,
\Sigma=\bigl\{\,\psi\in X\bigm|\left|\psi\right|_{2}=1\,\bigr\}

and
\Sigma_{\pm}=\bigl\{\,\psi\in X_{\pm}\bigm|\left|\psi\right|_{2}=1\,\bigr\}
.

We have also that

\displaystyle\hat{H}=\mathcal{F}H\mathcal{F}^{-1}=\boldsymbol{\alpha}\cdot p+m\beta

\displaystyle U\hat{H}U^{-1}=\lambda(p)\beta

\displaystyle\hat{\Lambda}_{\pm}=\mathcal{F}\Lambda_{\pm}\mathcal{F}^{-1}=U^{-1}\left(\frac{\mathsf{I}\pm\beta}{2}\right)U=\frac{1}{2}\left(\mathsf{I}\pm\frac{m}{\lambda(p)}\beta\pm\frac{1}{\lambda(p)}\boldsymbol{\alpha}\cdot p\right)

where

\displaystyle\mathcal{F}\psi(p)=\hat{\psi}(p)\left(=\frac{1}{(2\pi)^{3/2}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{3}}\esp^{-ipx}\psi(x)\,dx\quad\text{for all $v\in\mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R}^{3})$}\right),

\displaystyle\lambda(p)=\sqrt{\lvert p\rvert^{2}+m^{2}},

\displaystyle U=u_{+}(p)\mathsf{I}+u_{-}(p)\beta\frac{\boldsymbol{\alpha}\cdot p}{\lvert p\rvert},

\displaystyle U^{-1}=u_{+}(p)\mathsf{I}-u_{-}(p)\beta\frac{\boldsymbol{\alpha}\cdot p}{\lvert p\rvert},

\displaystyle u_{\pm}(p)=\sqrt{\frac{1}{2}\left(1\pm\frac{m}{\lambda(p)}\right)}.

Let, for
\phi
and
\psi\in H^{1/2}(\mathbb{R}^{3},C^{4})

\langle\phi\,|\,\psi\rangle=\int\sqrt{\lvert p\rvert^{2}+m^{2}}(\hat{\phi}(p),\hat{\psi}(p))\,dp

and

\lVert\psi\rVert^{2}=\langle\psi\,|\,\psi\rangle.

We have that

\langle\Lambda_{+}\phi\,|\,\Lambda_{-}\psi\rangle=(\Lambda_{+}\phi\,|\,\Lambda_{-}\psi)=0.

Let us recall that, since
\mathcal{F}\frac{1}{\lvert x\rvert}=\sqrt{\frac{2}{\pi}}\frac{1}{\lvert p\rvert^{2}}
, for all
f\in L^{1}(\mathbb{R}^{3})\cap L^{3/2}(\mathbb{R}^{3})

(1.5)

\int\frac{f(x)\bar{f}(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}=4\pi\int\frac{\lvert\hat{f}(p)\rvert}{\lvert p\rvert^{2}}\geq 0

and that for all
\rho\in L^{1}(\mathbb{R}^{3})
,
\psi\in H^{1/2}(\mathbb{R}^{3},\mathbb{C}^{4})

(1.6)

\int\frac{\rho(x)\lvert\psi\rvert^{2}(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}\leq\kappa\lvert\rho\rvert_{1}\left|(-\Delta)^{1/4}\psi\right|_{2}^{2}\leq\kappa\lvert\rho\rvert_{1}\lVert\psi\rVert^{2}

(
\kappa=\frac{\pi}{2}
) and also that

(1.7)

\int\frac{\lvert f_{n}\rvert(x)\lvert g_{n}\rvert(x)\lvert h_{n}\rvert(y)\lvert v\rvert(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}\to 0

when
f_{n}
,
g_{n}
.
h_{n}
and
v\in H^{1/2}
,
f_{n}
,
g_{n}

bounded,
h_{n}\rightharpoonup 0
.

2.
Maximization

Let
I\colon H^{1/2}(\mathbb{R}^{3},\mathbb{C}^{4})\to\mathbb{R}

I(\psi)=\frac{1}{2}\lVert\Lambda_{+}\psi\rVert^{2}-\frac{1}{2}\lVert\Lambda_{-}\psi\rVert^{2}-\frac{s}{4}\int\frac{(\psi,\beta\psi)(x)(\psi,\beta\psi)(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}.

Let us fix
w\in\Sigma_{+}
and let

B_{1}=\bigl\{\,\eta\in X_{-}\bigm|\left|\eta\right|_{2}<1\,\bigr\}.

We will look, given
w
, for a maximizer of the functional
J_{w}

defined on
B_{1}

J_{w}(\eta)=I(a(\eta)w+\eta)=\frac{1}{2}\lVert a(\eta)w\rVert^{2}-\frac{1}{2}\lVert\eta\rVert^{2}-\frac{s}{4}\int\frac{(\psi,\beta\psi)(x)(\psi,\beta\psi)(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}

where
a(\eta)=\sqrt{1-\left|\eta\right|_{2}^{2}}
and
\psi=a(\eta)w+\eta\in\Sigma
.

We have that
da(\eta)[\xi]=-a(\eta)^{-1}(\eta\,|\,\xi)
and hence
the derivative of
J_{w}
is given, for all
\xi\in X_{-}
, by

(2.1)

dJ_{w}(\eta)[\xi]=dI(a(\eta)w+\xi)[da(\eta)[\xi]w+\xi]=dI(\psi)[h]\\
=\langle a(\eta)w\,|\,da(\eta)[\xi]w\rangle-\langle\eta\,|\,\xi\rangle-s\int\frac{(\psi,\beta\psi)(x)(\psi,\beta h)(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}\\
=-(\eta\,|\,\xi)\lVert w\rVert^{2}-\langle\eta\,|\,\xi\rangle-s\int\frac{(\psi,\beta\psi)(x)(\psi,\beta h)(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}

(here
h=da(\eta)[\xi]w+\xi
) and we have, in particular

dJ_{w}(\eta)[\eta]=-\left|\eta\right|_{2}^{2}\lVert w\rVert^{2}-\lVert\eta\rVert^{2}-s\int\frac{(\psi,\beta\psi)(x)(\psi,\beta(da(\eta)[\eta]w+\eta))(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}.

Lemma 2.2
.

For all
w\in\Sigma_{+}
and
\eta\in B_{1}
we have

(2.3)

\lVert\eta\rVert^{2}\leq a(\eta)^{2}\lVert w\rVert^{2}-2J_{w}(\eta),

and for all
\eta\in B_{1}
such that
\left|\eta\right|_{2}^{2}\geq\frac{1}{2}
and
J_{w}(\eta)\geq 0
we have that

(2.4)

dJ_{w}(\eta)[\eta]\leq-\frac{1}{2}(1-4s\kappa)m<0

Proof.

We have, thanks to (
1.5
) that for

\eta\in B_{1}
and
\psi=a(\eta)w+\eta

\frac{1}{2}\lVert\eta\rVert^{2}\leq\frac{1}{2}\lVert\eta\rVert^{2}+\frac{s}{4}\int\frac{(\psi,\beta\psi)(x)(\psi,\beta\psi)(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}=\frac{1}{2}\lVert a(\eta)w\rVert^{2}-J_{w}(\eta)

and (
2.3
) follows.

From (
2.3
) follows that
\lVert\eta\rVert\leq a(\eta)\lVert w\rVert
if
J_{w}(\eta)\geq 0
, hence we have, if

\left|\eta\right|_{2}^{2}>\frac{1}{2}
:

\displaystyle dJ_{w}(\eta)[\eta]

\displaystyle=-\left|\eta\right|_{2}^{2}\lVert w\rVert^{2}-\lVert\eta\rVert^{2}-s\int\frac{(\psi,\beta\psi)(x)(\psi,\beta\psi)(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}

\displaystyle\quad+s\int\frac{(\psi,\beta\psi)(x)(w,\beta w)(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}

\displaystyle\quad+sa(\eta)^{-1}\int\frac{(\psi,\beta\psi)(x)(\eta,\beta w)(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}

\displaystyle\leq-\left|\eta\right|_{2}^{2}\lVert w\rVert^{2}-\lVert\eta\rVert^{2}+s\kappa(\lVert w\rVert^{2}+a(\eta)^{-1}\lVert\eta\rVert\lVert w\rVert)

\displaystyle\leq-\frac{1}{2}\lVert w\rVert^{2}-\lVert\eta\rVert^{2}+2s\kappa\lVert w\rVert^{2}<-\frac{1}{2}\left(1-4s\kappa\right)\lVert w\rVert^{2}

\displaystyle\leq-\frac{1}{2}\left(1-4s\kappa\right)m\left|w\right|_{2}^{2}=-\frac{1}{2}\left(1-4s\kappa\right)m

where we have used (
1.5
) and
(
1.6
).
∎

Remark 2.5
.

It follows from the Lemma
2.2
that if
\eta_{n}

is a Palais-Smale sequence for
J_{w}
such that
J_{w}(\eta_{n})\geq 0
, then
\left|\eta_{n}\right|_{2}^{2}<\frac{1}{2}

Lemma 2.6
.

Let
\eta_{n}\in B_{1}
be a Palais-Smale sequence for
J_{w}
,
that is

J_{w}(\eta_{n})\to c\geq 0,\qquad dJ_{w}(\eta_{n})\to 0.

Then
\eta_{n}
converges, up to a subsequence, to a critical
point
\eta
of
J_{w}
.

Proof.

Follows form the Lemma
2.2
and Remark

2.5
that
\left|\eta_{n}\right|_{2}^{2}<\frac{1}{2}
and that

\lVert\eta_{n}\rVert
is bounded, hence
\eta_{n}\rightharpoonup\eta
(up to a
subsequence).

From

o(1)=dJ_{w}(\eta_{n})[\eta_{n}-\eta]=-(\eta_{n}\,|\,\eta_{n}-\eta)\lVert w\rVert^{2}-\langle\eta_{n}\,|\,\eta_{n}-\eta\rangle\\
-s\int\frac{(\psi_{n},\beta\psi_{n})(x)(\psi_{n},\beta(-a(\eta_{n})^{-1}(\eta_{n}\,|\,\eta_{n}-\eta)w+\eta_{n}-\eta))(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}

we deduce that

\left|\eta_{n}-\eta\right|_{2}^{2}\lVert w\rVert^{2}+\lVert\eta_{n}-\eta\rVert^{2}=-(\eta\,|\,\eta_{n}-\eta)\lVert w\rVert^{2}-\langle\eta\,|\,\eta_{n}-\eta\rangle\\
+sa(\eta_{n})^{-1}(\eta_{n}\,|\,\eta_{n}-\eta))\int\frac{(\psi_{n},\beta\psi_{n})(x)(\psi_{n},\beta w)(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}\\
-s\int\frac{(\psi_{n},\beta\psi_{n})(x)(\psi_{n},\beta(\eta_{n}-\eta))(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}+o(1).

We have that

\displaystyle\int\frac{(\psi_{n},\beta\psi_{n})(x)(\psi_{n},\beta(\eta_{n}-\eta))(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}

\displaystyle=\int\frac{(\psi_{n},\beta(\eta_{n}-\eta))(x)(\psi_{n},\beta(\eta_{n}-\eta))(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}

\displaystyle\qquad+\int\frac{(\psi_{n},\beta\eta)(x)(\psi_{n},\beta(\eta_{n}-\eta))(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}

\displaystyle\qquad+a(\eta_{n})\int\frac{(\psi_{n},\beta w)(x)(\psi_{n},\beta(\eta_{n}-\eta))(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}

\displaystyle=\int\frac{(\psi_{n},\beta(\eta_{n}-\eta))(x)(\psi_{n},\beta(\eta_{n}-\eta))(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}+o(1)

and

\displaystyle\left|\int\frac{(\psi_{n},\beta\psi_{n})(x)(\psi_{n},\beta a(\eta_{n})w)(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}\right|

\displaystyle\leq\kappa\lVert\psi_{n}\rVert\lVert a(\eta_{n})w\rVert

\displaystyle\leq\kappa(2\lVert a(\eta_{n})w\rVert^{2}+\lVert\eta_{n}\rVert^{2})\leq 3\kappa a(\eta_{n})^{2}\lVert w\rVert^{2}.

Since
\left|\eta_{n}\right|_{2}^{2}<\frac{1}{2}
we have

a(\eta_{n})^{-2}(\eta_{n}\,|\,\eta_{n}-\eta)=a(\eta_{n})^{-2}\left|\eta_{n}-\eta\right|_{2}^{2}+o(1)

and we deduce that

\left|\eta_{n}-\eta\right|_{2}^{2}\lVert w\rVert^{2}+\lVert\eta_{n}-\eta\rVert^{2}\leq 3s\kappa\left|\eta_{n}-\eta\right|_{2}^{2}\lVert w\rVert^{2}\\
-s\int\frac{(\psi_{n},\beta(\eta_{n}-\eta))(x)(\psi_{n},\beta(\eta_{n}-\eta))(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}+o(1)\\
\leq 3s\kappa\left|\eta_{n}-\eta\right|_{2}^{2}\lVert w\rVert^{2}+o(1)

and
\eta_{n}\to\eta
, with
\eta
critical point of
J_{w}
.
∎

Lemma 2.7
.

Let
\eta\in X_{-}
a critical point of
J_{w}
such that
J_{w}(\eta)\geq 0
.

Then there exists
\delta>0
such that

d^{2}J_{w}(\eta)[\xi,\xi]\leq-\delta\lVert\xi\rVert^{2}\qquad\text{ for all }\xi\in X_{-}.

Proof.

In order to compute the second derivative se denote
\psi=a(\eta)w+\eta
and
h=da(\eta)[\xi]w+\xi
and observe that

d^{2}a(\eta)[\xi,\zeta]=-a(\eta)^{-1}\left((\xi\,|\,\zeta)+\frac{(\eta\,|\,\xi)(\eta,\zeta)}{1-\left|\eta\right|_{2}^{2}}\right).

Then

\displaystyle d^{2}J_{w}(\eta)[\xi,

\displaystyle\xi]=d^{2}I(\psi)[h,h]+dI(\psi)[d^{2}a(\eta)[\xi,\xi]w]

\displaystyle=\langle da(\eta)[\xi]w\,|\,da(\eta)[\xi]w\rangle-\langle\xi\,|\,\xi\rangle-s\int\frac{(\psi,\beta\psi)(x)(h,\beta h)(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}

\displaystyle\quad-2s\int\frac{(\psi,\beta h)(x)(\psi,\beta h)(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}+a(\eta)d^{2}a(\eta)[\xi,\xi]\langle w\,|\,w\rangle

\displaystyle\quad-s\int\frac{(\psi,\beta\psi)(x)(\psi,\beta d^{2}a(\eta)[\xi.\xi]w)(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}

\displaystyle=-\left|\xi\right|_{2}^{2}\lVert w\rVert^{2}+s\left|\xi\right|_{2}^{2}\int\frac{(\psi,\beta\psi)(x)(w,\beta w)(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}-\lVert\xi\rVert^{2}

\displaystyle\quad-s\int\frac{(\psi,\beta\psi)(x)(\xi,\beta\xi)(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}+2s\frac{(\eta\,|\,\xi)}{1-\left|\eta\right|_{2}^{2}}a(\eta)\Gamma(\xi)

\displaystyle\quad-2s\int\frac{(\psi,\beta h)(x)(\psi,\beta h)(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}+s\left(\frac{\left|\xi\right|_{2}^{2}}{1-\left|\eta\right|_{2}^{2}}+\frac{(\eta\,|\,\xi)^{2}}{(1-\left|\eta\right|_{2}^{2})^{2}}\right)a(\eta)\Gamma(\eta)

where we have set

\Gamma(\zeta)=\int\frac{(\psi,\beta\psi)(x)(w,\beta\zeta)(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}.

Since
\eta
is a critical point for
J_{w}
, we have that

d^{2}J_{w}(\eta)[\xi,\xi]=d^{2}J_{w}(\eta)[\xi,\xi]+2\frac{(\eta\,|\,\xi)}{1-\left|\eta\right|_{2}^{2}}dJ_{w}(\eta)[\xi]\\
+\left(\frac{\left|\xi\right|_{2}^{2}}{1-\left|\eta\right|_{2}^{2}}+3\frac{(\eta,\xi)^{2}}{(1-\left|\eta\right|_{2}^{2})^{2}}\right)dJ_{w}(\eta)[\eta].

We have that

\displaystyle 0

\displaystyle=dJ_{w}(\eta)[\eta]=-\left|\eta\right|_{2}^{2}\lVert w\rVert^{2}-\lVert\eta\rVert^{2}+s\frac{\left|\eta\right|_{2}^{2}}{1-\left|\eta\right|_{2}^{2}}\int\frac{(\psi,\beta\psi)(x)(w,\beta w)(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}

\displaystyle-s\int\frac{(\psi,\beta\psi)(x)(\eta,\beta\eta)(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}-s\left(\frac{1-2\left|\eta\right|_{2}^{2}}{1-\left|\eta\right|_{2}^{2}}\right)a\Gamma(\eta)

\displaystyle\leq-\left|\eta\right|_{2}^{2}(1-\kappa)\lVert w\rVert-(1-\kappa)\lVert\eta\rVert-s\left(\frac{1-2\left|\eta\right|_{2}^{2}}{1-\left|\eta\right|_{2}^{2}}\right)a\Gamma(\eta)

which implies that
\Gamma(\eta)<0
. After some semplification
we get

\displaystyle d^{2}J_{w}(\eta)[\xi,\xi]

\displaystyle\leq-Q(1-s\kappa)\lVert w\rVert^{2}-\frac{\left|\xi\right|_{2}^{2}}{1-\left|\eta\right|_{2}^{2}}(1-s\kappa)\lVert\eta\rVert^{2}

\displaystyle\quad-(1-s\kappa)\lVert\xi+R\eta\rVert^{2}

\displaystyle\leq-\frac{\left|\xi\right|_{2}^{2}}{1-\left|\eta\right|_{2}^{2}}(1-s\kappa)\lVert\eta\rVert^{2}-(1-s\kappa)\left(\frac{1}{3}\lVert\xi\rVert^{2}-\frac{1}{2}R^{2}\lVert\eta\rVert^{2}\right)

\displaystyle\leq-\frac{1}{3}(1-s\kappa)\lVert\xi\rVert^{2}

where

\displaystyle R=\frac{(\eta,\xi)}{1-\left|\eta\right|_{2}^{2}}

\displaystyle Q=\left|\xi\right|_{2}^{2}+2R(\eta,\xi)-\left|\eta\right|_{2}^{2}\left(\frac{\left|\xi\right|_{2}^{2}}{1-\left|\eta\right|_{2}^{2}}+R^{2}\right).

Remark that, since
\left|\eta\right|_{2}^{2}<\frac{1}{2}
, we have

Q\geq\left(\frac{\left|\xi\right|_{2}}{1-\left|\eta\right|_{2}^{2}}+R^{2}\right)(1-2\left|\eta\right|_{2}^{2})>0

and

\frac{\left|\xi\right|_{2}^{2}}{1-\left|\eta\right|_{2}^{2}}-\frac{1}{2}\frac{(\eta\,|\,\xi)^{2}}{(1-\left|\eta\right|_{2}^{2})^{2}}\geq\frac{\left|\xi\right|_{2}^{2}(2-3\left|\eta\right|_{2}^{2})}{2(1-\left|\eta\right|_{2}^{2})^{2}}>\frac{\left|\xi\right|_{2}^{2}}{4(1-\left|\eta\right|_{2}^{2})^{2}}>0.

∎

We let, for all
w\in\Sigma_{+}

\mathcal{E}(w)=\sup_{\eta\in B_{1}}J_{w}(\eta).

Lemma 2.8
.

For all
w\in\Sigma_{+}
we have

0<\frac{1}{4}(2-s\kappa)m\leq\frac{1}{4}(2-s\kappa)\lVert w\rVert^{2}\leq\mathcal{E}(w)\leq\frac{1}{2}\lVert w\rVert^{2}.

Proof.

We have that

\displaystyle\mathcal{E}(w)

\displaystyle\geq J_{w}(0)=\frac{1}{2}\lVert w\rVert^{2}-\frac{s}{4}\int\frac{(w,\beta w)(x)(w,\beta w)(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}

\displaystyle\geq\frac{1}{4}(2-s\kappa)\lVert w\rVert^{2}\geq\frac{1}{4}(2-s\kappa)m\left|w\right|_{2}^{2}=\frac{1}{4}(2-s\kappa)m

and, for all
\eta\in B_{1}
, we have

J_{w}(\eta)=\frac{1}{2}\lVert a(\eta)w\rVert^{2}-\frac{1}{2}\lVert\eta\rVert^{2}-\frac{s}{4}\int\frac{(\psi,\beta\psi)(x)(\psi,\beta\psi)(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}\leq\frac{1}{2}\lVert w\rVert^{2}.

∎

Proposition 2.9
.

For every
w\in\Sigma_{+}
there is a unique
\eta(w)\in B_{1}

such that

J_{w}(\eta(w))=\max_{\eta\in B_{1}}J_{w}(\eta)=\mathcal{E}(w).

\eta(w)
is a critical point of
J_{w}
on
B_{1}
such that

\left|\eta(w)\right|_{2}<\frac{1}{2}
and

(2.10)

\lVert\eta(w)\rVert^{2}+m\leq\lVert a(\eta)w\rVert^{2},\qquad\lVert\eta(w)\rVert^{2}\leq\frac{s}{2}\kappa\lVert w\rVert^{2}.

Moreover the map

w\in X_{+}\setminus\{0\}\mapsto\gamma(w)=\eta(\left|w\right|_{2}^{-1}w)\in B_{1}

is smooth.

Proof.

We can find, by lemma
2.8
and using Ekeland’s
variational principle a maximizing Palais-Smale sequence

\eta_{n}
at positive level.

Then, by lemma
2.6
,
\eta_{n}\to\eta
(up to a
subsequence), with

dJ_{w}(\eta)=0,\qquad J_{w}(\eta)=\mathcal{E}(w).

From

\mathcal{E}(w)=\frac{1}{2}a(\eta)^{2}\lVert w\rVert^{2}-\frac{1}{2}\lVert\eta\rVert^{2}-\frac{s}{4}\int\frac{(\psi,\beta\psi)(x)(\psi,\beta\psi)(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}\\
\geq\frac{1}{2}\lVert w\rVert^{2}-\frac{s}{4}\int\frac{(w,\beta w)(x)(w,\beta w)(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}

we deduce, using Lemma
A.1
in the appendix:

w_{n}
is bounded. Follows
from
2.9
that also
\eta_{n}=\eta(w_{n})
and

\psi_{n}=a(\eta_{n})w_{n}+\eta_{n}
are bounded in
X
. Letting

\omega_{n}=a(\eta_{n})^{-1}dI(\psi_{n})[w_{n}]
we have that

dI_{s}(\psi_{n})[h]-\omega_{n}(\psi_{n}\,|\,h)=0\qquad\text{for all }h\in X.

We can assume that (up to a subsequence)
\psi_{n}\rightharpoonup\psi
in
X

and that
\omega_{n}\to\omega
. Then we have that for all
h\in X

dI_{s}(\psi_{n})[h]-\omega_{n}(\psi_{n}\,|\,h)\\
=\langle\psi_{n}\,|\,\Lambda_{+}h\rangle-\langle\psi_{n}\,|\,\Lambda_{-}h\rangle-s\int\frac{(\psi_{n},\beta\psi_{n})(x)(\psi_{n},\beta h)(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}-\omega_{n}(\psi_{n}\,|\,h)\\
\to 0

since, by (
1.7
), we have that

\int\frac{(\psi_{n},\beta\psi_{n})(x)(\psi_{n}-\psi,\beta h)(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}\to 0.

As a consequence we have that

dI_{s}(\psi)[h]-\omega(\psi\,|\,h)=0\qquad\text{for
all }h\in X.

The weak convergence does not, however, preserve the
L^{2}
norm, so
we only know that
\left|\psi\right|_{2}\leq\left|\psi_{n}\right|_{2}=1
(it could
even be that
\psi=0
).

To conclude we will now apply the concentration-compactness principle,
see
[
4
,
5
]
. First of all let us show that no
vanishining occurs. By contradiction, assume that

\limsup_{n\to+\infty}\sup_{y\in\mathbb{R}^{3}}\int_{B(y,1)}\lvert\psi_{n}\rvert^{2}=0.

Then we know, see
[
4
]
or
[
Willem96
, Lemma 1.21]
,
that
\psi_{n}\to 0
in
L^{p}(\mathbb{R}^{3})
for
2<p<6
.

Since

\int\frac{(\psi_{n},\beta\psi_{n}(x))(\psi_{n},\beta\psi_{n}(y))}{\lvert x-y\rvert}\leq\int\frac{\lvert\psi_{n}(x)\rvert^{2}\lvert\psi_{n}(y)\rvert^{2}}{\lvert x-y\rvert}\leq C\left|\psi_{n}\right|_{\frac{12}{5}}^{4}\to 0

we deduce, using (
2.10
), (
2.13
)
and lemma
2.15
that

\displaystyle 0

\displaystyle=dI_{s}(\psi_{n})[\psi_{n}]-\omega_{n}\left|\psi_{n}\right|_{2}^{2}

\displaystyle=\lVert a(\eta_{n})w_{n}\rVert^{2}-\lVert\eta_{n}\rVert^{2}-\omega_{n}\left|\psi_{n}\right|_{2}^{2}-s\int\frac{(\psi_{n},\beta\psi_{n}(x))(\psi_{n},\beta\psi_{n}(y))}{\lvert x-y\rvert}

\displaystyle=\lVert a(\eta_{n})w_{n}\rVert^{2}-\lVert\eta_{n}\rVert^{2}-m\left|\psi_{n}\right|_{2}^{2}+(m-\omega_{n})+o(1)

\displaystyle\geq(m-\omega_{n})+o(1)>0

for
n
large enough, a contradiction which shows that vanishing does
not occur.

Then we know from the concentration compactness principle, that there
exists
p\geq 1
functions
\phi_{1}
, …,
\phi_{p}\in X
,
critical points for
I_{s}
under the constraint
\left|\psi\right|_{2}^{2}=\mu_{i}
(hence satisfying (
2.14
) with

\omega=\lim_{n}\omega_{n}>0
) and
p
sequences of points
x_{i,n}\in\mathbb{R}^{3}
,
i=1
, …,
p
such that
\lvert x_{i,n}-x_{j,n}\rvert\to+\infty
for all
i\neq j
as
n\to+\infty
and

\lVert\psi_{n}-\sum_{i=i}^{{}^{p}}\phi_{i}(\cdot-x_{i,n})\rVert\to 0\quad\text{as }n\to+\infty.

From this follows also that
\left|\psi_{n}\right|_{2}^{2}=1=\sum_{i=1}^{p}\mu_{i}
.

We then observe that

\displaystyle\lVert\Lambda_{+}\psi_{n}\rVert^{2}-

\displaystyle\lVert\Lambda_{-}\psi_{n}\rVert^{2}=\langle\psi_{n}\,|\,\Lambda_{+}\psi_{n}-\Lambda_{-}\psi_{n}\rangle

\displaystyle=\langle\psi_{n}-\sum_{i=i}^{{}^{p}}\phi_{i}(\cdot-x_{i,n})\,|\,\Lambda_{+}\psi_{n}-\Lambda_{-}\psi_{n}\rangle

\displaystyle\qquad+\sum_{i=i}^{{}^{p}}\langle\phi_{i}(\cdot-x_{i,n})\,|\,\Lambda_{+}\psi_{n}-\Lambda_{-}\psi_{n}\rangle

\displaystyle=\sum_{i=i}^{{}^{p}}\left(\langle\Lambda_{+}\phi_{i}(\cdot-x_{i,n})\,|\,\psi_{n}\rangle-\langle\Lambda_{-}\phi_{i}(\cdot-x_{i,n})\,|\,\psi_{n}\rangle\right)+o(1)

\displaystyle=\sum_{i=i}^{{}^{p}}\left(\lVert\Lambda_{+}\phi_{i}\rVert^{2}-\lVert\Lambda_{-}\phi_{i}\rVert^{2}\right)+o(1)

and also

\int\frac{(\psi_{n},\beta\psi_{n}(x))(\psi_{n},\beta\psi_{n}(y))}{\lvert x-y\rvert}=\sum_{i=1}^{p}\int\frac{(\phi_{i},\beta\phi_{i}(x))(\phi_{i},\beta\phi_{i}(y))}{\lvert x-y\rvert}+o(1).

Finally we have that

(2.17)

e(s)=I_{s}(\psi_{n})+o(1)=\sum_{i=1}^{p}I_{s}(\phi_{i})+o(1)

Let, for
i=1,\ldots,n
,
\tilde{\psi}_{i}=\left|\phi_{i}\right|_{2}^{-1}\phi_{i}=\mu_{i}^{-1/2}\phi_{i}\in\Sigma
. We have that

I_{s}(\phi_{i})=I_{s}(\sqrt{\mu_{i}}\tilde{\psi}_{i})=\mu_{i}I_{s\mu_{i}}(\tilde{\psi}_{i})

and

0=dI_{s}(\phi_{i})[h]-\omega(\phi_{i}\,|\,h)=\sqrt{\mu_{i}}\left(dI_{s\mu_{i}}(\tilde{\psi}_{i})[h]-\omega(\tilde{\psi}_{i}\,|\,h)\right)\qquad\text{for all }h\in X,

Follows from proposition
2.13
that
\tilde{w}_{i}=\left|\Lambda_{+}\tilde{\psi}_{i}\right|_{2}^{-1}\Lambda_{+}\tilde{\psi}_{i}\in\Sigma_{+}
is a critical point for
\mathcal{E}_{s\mu_{i}}
and

\mathcal{E}_{s\mu_{i}}(\tilde{w}_{i})=I_{s\mu_{i}}(\tilde{\psi}_{i})
.

Since

\mathcal{E}_{s\mu_{i}}(\tilde{w}_{i})\geq e(s\mu_{i})

we deduce from (
2.16
) that

e(s)=\sum_{i=1}^{p}I_{s}(\phi_{i})=\sum_{i=1}^{p}\mu_{i}I_{s\mu_{i}}(\tilde{\psi}_{i})\geq\sum_{i=1}^{p}\mu_{i}e(s\mu_{i})>\sum_{i=1}^{p}\mu_{i}e(\frac{1}{\mu_{i}}s\mu_{i})=e(s)\sum_{i=1}^{p}\mu_{i},

a contradiction if
p>1
.

Since there is no vanishing or dichotomy, our sequence
\psi_{n}

converges strongly in
X
to a critical point
\psi\in X
of
(
2.14
) such that
\left|\psi\right|_{2}=1
and
the theorem follows.

∎

Appendix A
A useful lemma

This lemma is similar to
[
6
, Lemma 2.9]
. We give here
a slightly different proof.

Lemma A.1
.

For all
\psi=\sqrt{1-\left|w\right|_{2}^{2}}w+\eta
,
w\in\Sigma_{+}
,
\eta\in X_{-}
we have

\displaystyle\int\frac{(\psi,\beta\psi)(x)(\psi,\beta\psi)(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}

\displaystyle\geq\int\frac{(w,\beta w)(x)(w,\beta w)(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}-2\kappa\left|\eta\right|_{2}^{2}\lVert w\rVert^{2}

\displaystyle\qquad-14a(\eta)^{2}\kappa(\lVert w\rVert^{2}-m\left|w\right|_{2}^{2})-18\kappa\lVert\eta\rVert^{2}

Proof.

We have

\displaystyle\int\frac{(\psi,\beta\psi)(x)(\psi,\beta\psi)(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}=a(\eta)^{4}\int\frac{(w,\beta w)(x)(w,\beta w)(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}

\displaystyle\qquad+4a(\eta)^{3}\int\frac{(w,\beta w)(x)(w,\beta\eta)(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}+3a(\eta)^{2}\int\frac{(w,\beta w)(x)(\eta,\beta\eta)(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}

\displaystyle\qquad+3a(\eta)^{2}\int\frac{(w,\beta\eta)(x)(w,\beta\eta)(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}+4a(\eta)\int\frac{(\eta,\beta\eta)(x)(w,\beta\eta)(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}

\displaystyle\qquad+\int\frac{(\eta,\beta\eta)(x)(\eta,\beta\eta)(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}

\displaystyle\geq a(\eta)^{4}\int\frac{(w,\beta w)(x)(w,\beta w)(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}+4a(\eta)^{3}\int\frac{(w,\beta w)(x)(w,\beta\eta)(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}

\displaystyle\qquad-3a(\eta)^{2}\kappa\lVert\eta\rVert^{2}+4a(\eta)\int\frac{(\eta,\beta\eta)(x)(w,\beta\eta)(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}

We have

\displaystyle\left|\int\frac{(w,\beta w)(x)(w,\beta\eta)(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}\,dx\,dt\right|

\displaystyle=(2\pi)^{3/2}\sqrt{\frac{2}{\pi}}\left|\int\frac{\mathcal{F}[(w,\beta w)]\mathcal{F}[(w,\beta\eta)]}{\lvert p\rvert^{2}}\,dp\right|

\displaystyle\leq(2\pi)^{3/2}\sqrt{\frac{2}{\pi}}\lvert\mathcal{F}[(w,\beta w)]\rvert_{\infty}\left|\int\frac{\lvert\mathcal{F}[(w,\beta\eta)]\rvert}{\lvert p\rvert^{2}}\,dp\right|

\displaystyle\leq\sqrt{\frac{2}{\pi}}\lvert(w,\beta w)\rvert_{1}\left|\int\frac{\lvert\mathcal{F}[(w,\beta\eta)]\rvert}{\lvert p\rvert^{2}}\,dp\right|

\displaystyle\leq\sqrt{\frac{2}{\pi}}\left|w\right|_{2}^{2}\left|\int\frac{\lvert\mathcal{F}[(w,\beta\eta)]\rvert}{\lvert p\rvert^{2}}\,dp\right|

\displaystyle\leq\sqrt{\frac{2}{\pi}}\int\frac{dp}{\lvert p\rvert^{2}}\left(\frac{1}{(2\pi)^{3/2}}\int\lvert(\hat{w}(p-q),\beta\hat{\eta}(q))\rvert\,dq\right)

and

\left|\int\frac{(\eta,\beta\eta)(x)(w,\beta\eta)(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}\,dx\,dt\right|\\
\leq\left|\eta\right|_{2}^{2}\sqrt{\frac{2}{\pi}}\int\frac{dp}{\lvert p\rvert^{2}}\left(\frac{1}{(2\pi)^{3/2}}\int\lvert(\hat{w}(p-q),\beta\hat{\eta}(q))\rvert\,dq\right)

so that

4a(\eta)^{3}\int\frac{(w,\beta w)(x)(w,\beta\eta)(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}+4a(\eta)\int\frac{(\eta,\beta\eta)(x)(w,\beta\eta)(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}\\
\leq 4a(\eta)\sqrt{\frac{2}{\pi}}\int\frac{dp}{\lvert p\rvert^{2}}\left(\frac{1}{(2\pi)^{3/2}}\int\lvert(\hat{w}(p-q),\beta\hat{\eta}(q))\rvert\,dq\right)

Since

\displaystyle(\hat{w}(p-q),\beta\hat{\eta}(q))

\displaystyle=(\hat{\Lambda}_{+}(p-q)\hat{w}(p-q),\beta\hat{\Lambda}_{-}(q)\hat{\eta}(q))

\displaystyle=(\hat{w}(p-q),\hat{\Lambda}_{+}(p-q)\beta\hat{\Lambda}_{-}(q)\hat{\eta}(q))

we compute

\displaystyle 4\hat{\Lambda}_{+}(p-q)

\displaystyle\beta\hat{\Lambda}_{-}(q)

\displaystyle=\left(\mathsf{I}+\frac{m\beta}{\lambda(p-q)}+\frac{\boldsymbol{\alpha}\cdot(p-q)}{\lambda(p-q)}\right)\beta\left(\mathsf{I}-\frac{m\beta}{\lambda(q)}-\frac{\boldsymbol{\alpha}\cdot q}{\lambda(q)}\right)

\displaystyle=\beta\left(1-\frac{m^{2}}{\lambda(q)\lambda(p-q)}\right)-\mathsf{I}\left(\frac{m}{\lambda(q)}-\frac{m}{\lambda(p-q)}\right)

\displaystyle\qquad-\beta\boldsymbol{\alpha}\cdot\left(\frac{q}{\lambda(q)}+\frac{p-q}{\lambda(p-q)}\right)-\frac{m\boldsymbol{\alpha}\cdot(q+(p-q))}{\lambda(q)\lambda(p-q)}

\displaystyle\qquad+\frac{\beta}{\lambda(q)\lambda(p-q)}(\boldsymbol{\alpha}\cdot(p-q)\boldsymbol{\alpha}\cdot q)

\displaystyle=\beta\left(1-\frac{m^{2}}{\lambda(q)\lambda(p-q)}\right)-\mathsf{I}\left(\frac{m}{\lambda(q)}-\frac{m}{\lambda(p-q)}\right)

\displaystyle\qquad-\beta\boldsymbol{\alpha}\cdot\left(\frac{q}{\lambda(q)}+\frac{p-q}{\lambda(p-q)}\right)-\frac{m\boldsymbol{\alpha}\cdot p}{\lambda(q)\lambda(p-q)}+\frac{\beta\boldsymbol{\Sigma}\cdot(p-q)\boldsymbol{\Sigma}\cdot q}{\lambda(q)\lambda(p-q)}

where

\Sigma_{i}=\begin{pmatrix}\sigma_{i}&0\\
0&\sigma_{i}\end{pmatrix}

We now estimate the different terms. First of all, from

m(\lvert p-q\rvert+\lvert q\rvert)\leq\lambda(q)\lambda(p-q)\leq\lvert q\rvert\lvert p-q\rvert+m(\lvert q\rvert+\lvert p-q\rvert)+m^{2},

and

\left|\lvert q\rvert\lambda(p-q)-\lvert p-q\rvert\lambda(q)\right|\leq m\lvert p\rvert

we deduce that

\left|\frac{\lvert q\rvert}{\lambda(q)}-\frac{\lvert p-q\rvert}{\lambda(p-q)}\right|=\left|\frac{\lvert q\rvert\lambda(p-q)-\lvert p-q\rvert\lambda(q)}{\lambda(q)\lambda(p-q)}\right|\leq\frac{m\lvert p\rvert}{\lambda(q)\lambda(p-q)}

and

\displaystyle\left|\frac{m}{\lambda(q)}-\frac{m}{\lambda(p-q)}\right|

\displaystyle=m\frac{\lvert\lambda(p-q)-\lambda(q)\rvert}{\lambda(q)\lambda(p-q)}=m\frac{\lvert m^{2}+\lvert p-q\rvert^{2}-m^{2}-\lvert q\rvert^{2}\rvert}{\lambda(q)\lambda(p-q)(\lambda(q)+\lambda(p-q))}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{\lvert\lvert p-q\rvert-\lvert q\rvert\rvert}{\lambda(q)+\lambda(p-q)}\leq\frac{\lvert p\rvert}{\lambda(q)+\lambda(p-q)}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{\lvert p\rvert}{(\lambda(q)+m)^{1/2}(\lambda(p-q)+m)^{1/2}}.

Then

\displaystyle\left|\frac{\lambda(q)\lambda(p-q)-m^{2}}{\lambda(q)\lambda(p-q)}\right|

\displaystyle\leq\frac{\lvert q\rvert\lvert p-q\rvert+m(\lvert q\rvert+\lvert p-q\rvert)}{\lambda(q)\lambda(p-q)}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{\lvert q\rvert\lvert p-q\rvert}{\lambda(q)\lambda(p-q)}+\frac{m\lvert p\rvert}{\lambda(q)\lambda(p-q)}+2\frac{m\lvert p-q\rvert}{\lambda(q)\lambda(p-q)}

and

\displaystyle\left|\frac{q}{\lambda(q)}+\frac{p-q}{\lambda(p-q)}\right|

\displaystyle\leq\left|\frac{\lvert q\rvert}{\lambda(q)}-\frac{\lvert p-q\rvert}{\lambda(p-q)}\right|+2\frac{\lvert p-q\rvert}{\lambda(p-q)}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{m\lvert p\rvert}{\lambda(q)\lambda(p-q)}+2\frac{\lvert p-q\rvert}{\lambda(p-q)}.

Since

\left|\frac{\beta\boldsymbol{\Sigma}\cdot(p-q)\boldsymbol{\Sigma}\cdot q}{\lambda(q)\lambda(p-q)}\right|\leq\frac{\lvert q\rvert\lvert p-q\rvert}{\lambda(q)\lambda(p-q)}

we finally have

4\lvert(\hat{w}(p-q),\beta\hat{\eta}(q))\rvert\\
\leq\Bigl(\frac{3\lvert q\rvert\lvert p-q\rvert+3m\lvert p\rvert+2m\lvert p-q\rvert}{\lambda(q)\lambda(p-q)}+\frac{2\lvert p-q\rvert}{\lambda(p-q)}\Bigr)\lvert\hat{w}(p-q)\rvert\lvert\hat{\eta}(q)\rvert\\
+\frac{\lvert p\rvert}{(\lambda(q)+m)^{1/2}(\lambda(p-q)+m)^{1/2}}\lvert\hat{w}(p-q)\rvert\lvert\hat{\eta}(q)\rvert

Let us analyse the different terms:

\displaystyle\sqrt{\frac{2}{\pi}}\int\frac{dp}{\lvert p\rvert^{2}}

\displaystyle\left(\frac{1}{(2\pi)^{3/2}}\int\frac{\lvert p-q\rvert\lvert\hat{w}(p-q)\rvert}{\lambda(p-q)}\frac{\lvert q\rvert\lvert\hat{\eta}(q)\rvert}{\lambda(q)}\,dq\right)

\displaystyle=\sqrt{\frac{2}{\pi}}\int\frac{1}{\lvert p\rvert^{2}}\mathcal{F}\left[\mathcal{F}^{-1}\left[\frac{\lvert p\rvert\lvert\hat{w}(p)\rvert}{\lambda(p)}\right]\mathcal{F}^{-1}\left[\frac{\lvert p\rvert\lvert\hat{\eta}(p)\rvert}{\lambda(p)}\right]\right]\,dp

\displaystyle=\int\frac{1}{\lvert x\rvert}\mathcal{F}^{-1}\left[\frac{\lvert p\rvert\lvert\hat{w}(p)\rvert}{\lambda(p)}\right]\mathcal{F}^{-1}\left[\frac{\lvert p\rvert\lvert\hat{\eta}(p)\rvert}{\lambda(p)}\right]\,dx

\displaystyle\leq\left|\frac{1}{\lvert x\rvert^{1/2}}\mathcal{F}^{-1}\left[\frac{\lvert p\rvert\lvert\hat{w}(p)\rvert}{\lambda(p)}\right]\right|_{2}\left|\frac{1}{\lvert x\rvert^{1/2}}\mathcal{F}^{-1}\left[\frac{\lvert p\rvert\lvert\hat{\eta}(p)\rvert}{\lambda(p)}\right]\right|_{2}

\displaystyle\leq\kappa\left|(-\Delta)^{1/4}\mathcal{F}^{-1}\left[\frac{\lvert p\rvert\lvert\hat{w}(p)\rvert}{\lambda(p)}\right]\right|_{2}\left|(-\Delta)^{1/4}\mathcal{F}^{-1}\left[\frac{\lvert p\rvert\lvert\hat{\eta}(p)\rvert}{\lambda(p)}\right]\right|_{2}

\displaystyle\leq 2\kappa\sqrt{\lVert w\rVert^{2}-m\left|w\right|_{2}^{2}}\sqrt{\lVert\eta\rVert^{2}-m\left|\eta\right|_{2}^{2}}

since

\left|(-\Delta)^{1/4}\mathcal{F}^{-1}\left[\frac{\lvert p\rvert\lvert\hat{w}(p)\rvert}{\lambda(p)}\right]\right|_{2}^{2}=\int\frac{\lvert p\rvert^{3}\lvert\hat{w}(p)\rvert^{2}}{\lambda(p)^{2}}\,dp\\
\leq 2\int(\sqrt{\lvert p\rvert^{2}+m^{2}}-m)\lvert\hat{w}(p)\rvert^{2}\,dp=2(\lVert w\rVert^{2}-m\left|w\right|_{2}^{2})

and

\displaystyle\sqrt{\frac{2}{\pi}}\int\frac{dp}{\lvert p\rvert^{2}}

\displaystyle\left(\frac{1}{(2\pi)^{3/2}}\int\frac{\lvert p-q\rvert\lvert\hat{w}(p-q)\rvert}{\lambda(p-q)}\frac{m\lvert\hat{w}(q)\rvert}{\lambda(q)}\,dq\right)

\displaystyle\sqrt{\frac{2}{\pi}}\int\frac{dp}{\lvert p\rvert^{2}}

\displaystyle\left(\frac{1}{(2\pi)^{3/2}}\int\lvert p\rvert\frac{\lvert\hat{w}(p-q)\rvert}{(\lambda(p-q)+m)^{1/2}}\frac{\lvert\hat{\eta}(q)\rvert}{(\lambda(q)+m)^{1/2}}\,dq\right)

(we have used the fact that
\frac{mp^{2}}{p^{2}+m^{2}}\leq\frac{5}{2}(\sqrt{p^{2}+m^{2}}-m)
.)

Finally we have

\displaystyle\sqrt{\frac{2}{\pi}}\int\frac{dp}{\lvert p\rvert^{2}}

\displaystyle\left(\frac{1}{(2\pi)^{3/2}}\int\frac{\lvert p-q\rvert\lvert\hat{w}(p-q)\rvert\lvert\hat{w}(q)\rvert}{\lambda(p-q)}\,dq\right)

\displaystyle\leq\kappa\left|(-\Delta)^{1/4}\mathcal{F}^{-1}\left[\frac{\lvert p\rvert\lvert\hat{w}(p)\rvert}{\lambda(p)}\right]\right|_{2}\left|(-\Delta)^{1/4}\mathcal{F}^{-1}\left[\lvert\hat{\eta}(p)\rvert\right]\right|_{2}

\displaystyle\leq\sqrt{2}\kappa\lVert\eta\rVert\sqrt{\lVert w\rVert^{2}-m\left|w\right|_{2}^{2}}.

We now collect the terms:

\displaystyle 4a(\eta)\sqrt{\frac{2}{\pi}}

\displaystyle\qquad+2\sqrt{m}\kappa a(\eta)\left|\eta\right|_{2}\sqrt{\lVert w\rVert^{2}-m\left|w\right|_{2}^{2}}

\displaystyle\qquad+2\sqrt{2}\kappa a(\eta)\lVert\eta\rVert\sqrt{\lVert w\rVert^{2}-m\left|w\right|_{2}^{2}}

\displaystyle\leq 14a(\eta)^{2}\kappa(\lVert w\rVert^{2}-m\left|w\right|_{2}^{2})+15\kappa\lVert\eta\rVert^{2}

to deduce that

\displaystyle\int\frac{(\psi,\beta\psi)(x)(\psi,\beta\psi)(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}

\displaystyle-\int\frac{(w,\beta w)(x)(w,\beta w)(y)}{\lvert x-y\rvert}

\displaystyle\geq-2\kappa\left|\eta\right|_{2}^{2}\lVert w\rVert^{2}-3a(\eta)^{2}\kappa\lVert\eta\rVert^{2}

\displaystyle\quad-4a(\eta)\sqrt{\frac{2}{\pi}}\int\frac{dp}{\lvert p\rvert^{2}}\left(\frac{1}{(2\pi)^{3/2}}\int\lvert(\hat{w}(p-q),\beta\hat{\eta}(q))\rvert\,dq\right)

\displaystyle\geq-14a(\eta)^{2}\kappa(\lVert w\rVert^{2}-m\left|w\right|_{2}^{2})-2\kappa\left|\eta\right|_{2}^{2}\lVert w\rVert^{2}-18\kappa\lVert\eta\rVert^{2}

∎
