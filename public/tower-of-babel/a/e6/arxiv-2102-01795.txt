IV
Conclusion

We discussed the possibility that the weak SU(2) gauge symmetry works for the source of explicit breaking terms of a global symmetries suggested for a quintessential axion. We find that it is possible in supersymmetric standard models if the vacuum expectaion value of the quintessential axion field is about
1.48\times 10^{8\,}\,\textrm{GeV}
.
