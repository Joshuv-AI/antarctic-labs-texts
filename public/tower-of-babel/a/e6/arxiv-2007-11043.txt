Definition 2.1

A closed subspace

Theorem 2.5

2.4

. It follows from

(3.8)

\displaystyle C+C||u_{n}||

\displaystyle\geqslant J(u_{n})-\dfrac{1}{\theta}\left\langle J^{\prime}(u_{n}),u_{n}\right\rangle
