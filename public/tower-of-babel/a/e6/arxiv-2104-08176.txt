Abstract
The manuscript contains elegant extensions of the fundamental variational principles: Brøndsted’s and Ekeland’s. On the other hand, we get general and precise version of the Takahashi and the Caristi fixed point theorems. The results are based on the notion of istance for uniform spaces.



The main results of this paper are theorems of variational type for uniform spaces. Their proofs
involve a kind of order relation
y\mbox{$\preccurlyeq$}x
defined by
\psi(y)+q(y,x)-\psi(x)\leq 0
.
Here
q
, called istance, is a special mapping satisfying the triangle inequality (see Definition
2
).
In consequence we get natural and more general versions of Brøndsted’s variational principle (Theorem

6
) and of Ekeland’s one (Theorem
10
). On the other hand, we obtain a good-looking extension
of the Caristi and Takahashi fixed point theorems (Theorem
8
).

Many advanced results for uniform spaces are known. The idea is to replace metric by a family of semimetrics
(see e.g.
[
12
]
), pseudometrics (see e.g.
[
9
]
) or quasimetris (see e.g.
[
5
]
); and further,
real-valued mappings by vector-valued functions (see e.g.
[
6
]
,
[
13
]
). Our theorems look more classical
while the assumptions on order relation (only transitivity) and
q
are weak. Theorem

and in some
sense similar result of Vályi
[
14

are compared further.

Definition 2

Let

-istance

\displaystyle q(z,x)\leq q(z,y)+q(y,x),\quad\mbox{$x,y,z\in X$},

(1a)

\displaystyle q(\cdot,x)\mbox{ is lower semicontinuous,}\quad x\in X,

(1b)

\displaystyle\mbox{each Cauchy sequence in }\mbox{$(X,q)$}\mbox{ is a Cauchy sequence in }\mbox{$(X,\mathcal{U})$}.

(1c)

Proposition 3

Let
(X,\mathcal{U})

\mathcal{U}
-istance

q(x,y)=0\mbox{ and }q(y,x)=0\mbox{ yield }x=y,\quad\mbox{$x,y\in X$}.

(2)

Assume

and

x_{2k}=y

Let
(X,\mathcal{U})

\mathcal{U}
-istance in

\psi\colon X\to\mathbb{R}

,

x\in\{z\in X\colon\psi(z)\leq\psi(y)\}

is the unique smallest element of

\quad\square

\beta=\inf\{\psi(z)\colon z\in X\}\mbox{, }B=\{z\in X\colon\psi(z)=\beta\}.

Theorem 7

(i)
