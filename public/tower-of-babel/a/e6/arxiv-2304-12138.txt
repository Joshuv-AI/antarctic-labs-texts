Abstract
Let k k be a perfect field of prime characteristic p p , G G a finite group scheme over k k , and V V a finite-dimensional G G -module. Let S = Sym V S=\mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}V be the symmetric algebra with the standard grading. Let M M be a ℚ \mathbb{Q} -graded S S -finite S S -free ( G , S ) (G,S) -module, and L L be its S S -reflexive graded ( G , S ) (G,S) -submodule. Generalizing the result of P. Symonds and the first author, we describe the Frobenius limit FL ( L G ) \mathop{\mathrm{FL}}(L^{G}) of the S G S^{G} -module L G L^{G} .
