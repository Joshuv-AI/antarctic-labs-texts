Countably-categorical Boolean rings with distinguished ideals
Andrew B. Apps

Abstract
We describe and classify countable Boolean rings with finitely many distinguished ideals whose elementary theory is countably categorical. This extends the description by Macintyre and Rosenstein and subsequent authors of countably categorical Boolean algebras with finitely many distinguished ideals. Following Pierce, our classification takes a topological approach using the language of PO systems (partially ordered sets with a distinguished subset) and topological Boolean algebras. We discuss how our findings link with previous results, but the paper is otherwise self-contained.

The problem of describing

closed subsets of a Stone space. We also generalise by considering
Boolean rings which may not have an identity rather than just Boolean

Theorem 5.1

There is a natural bijection between isomorphism

-categorical Boolean rings with

Suppose first that
\boldsymbol{J}^{(n)}
are distinguished ideals
of the countable Boolean ring
R
, corresponding to closed subsets

\boldsymbol{C}^{(n)}

X
of
R
, such that

is
\omega
-categorical. Let

\mathscr{D}
be the sub-TBA of
2^{X}
generated by
\boldsymbol{C}^{\{n\}}
,
with atoms
\{X_{p}\mid p\in P\}
, so that
P
is a finite PO system

-partition of

of

are

. In addition,

∎

Remark 2

If we restrict to Boolean algebras, we obtain a natural bijection
between the isomorphism classes of countable

-categorical

distinguished ideals, and the isomorphism

Corollary 1

Let
\boldsymbol{J}^{(n)}
be distinguished ideals of the countable
Boolean ring
R
with Stone space

such that
(R,\boldsymbol{J}^{(n)})

is
\omega
-categorical. Let

∎

Remark 3

We note that for

,

and
