Introduction and preliminaries

In 1946, Gabor

Controlled frames in Hilbert spaces have been introduced by P. Balazs

\ast
-
K

We begin this section with the following definition.

Definition 2.1
.

Let
K\in End_{\mathcal{A}}^{\ast}(\mathcal{H})
and
C,C^{{}^{\prime}}\in GL^{+}(\mathcal{H})
. A family of adjointable operators
\{T_{i}\}_{i\in I}
on a Hilbert
\mathcal{A}
-module
\mathcal{H}
over a unital
C^{\ast}
-algebra is said to be a
(C,C^{{}^{\prime}})
-controlled
\ast
-K-operator frame for
End_{\mathcal{A}}^{\ast}(\mathcal{H})
, if there exist two strictly non zero elements
A
and
B
in
\mathcal{A}
such that

(2.1)

A\langle K^{\ast}x,K^{\ast}x\rangle_{\mathcal{A}}A^{\ast}\leq\sum_{i\in I}\langle T_{i}Cx,T_{i}C^{{}^{\prime}}x\rangle_{\mathcal{A}}\leq B\langle x,x\rangle_{\mathcal{A}}B^{\ast},x\in\mathcal{H}.

The elements
A
and
B
are called respectively lower and upper bounds of the
(C,C^{{}^{\prime}})
-controlled
\ast
-K-operator frame.

If
A\langle K^{\ast}x,K^{\ast}x\rangle_{\mathcal{A}}A^{\ast}=\sum_{i\in I}\langle T_{i}Cx,T_{i}C^{{}^{\prime}}x\rangle_{\mathcal{A}}
, the
(C,C^{{}^{\prime}})
-controlled
\ast
-K-operator frame is called
A
-tight.

If
A=1_{\mathcal{A}}
, it is called a normalized tight
(C,C^{{}^{\prime}})
-controlled
\ast
-K-operator frame or a Parseval
(C,C^{{}^{\prime}})
-controlled
\ast
-K-operator frame.

Example 2.2
.

Let
\mathcal{H}=l_{2}(\mathbb{C})=\bigg\{\{a_{n}\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\subset\mathbb{C};\;\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}}|a_{n}|^{2}<\infty\bigg\}
be a Hilbert space with respect the inner product,

\langle\{a_{n}\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}},\{b_{n}\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\rangle=\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}}a_{n}\bar{b_{n}},

equiped with the norm,

\|\{a_{n}\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\|_{l_{2}(\mathbb{C})}=(\sum_{n\in\mathbb{N}}|a_{n}|^{2})^{\frac{1}{2}}.

We consider the
\mathbb{C}^{\ast}
-algebra
\mathcal{A}=\bigg\{\{a_{n}\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\subset\mathbb{C};\;\underset{n\in\mathbb{N}}{max}|a_{n}|<\infty\bigg\}
, equiped with the involution,

\{a_{n}\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\longrightarrow\{a_{n}\}^{\ast}_{n\in\mathbb{N}}=\{\bar{a_{n}}\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\quad for\;all\quad\{a_{n}\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\subset\mathcal{A}

Let the map,

\displaystyle\mathcal{H}\times\mathcal{H}

\displaystyle\longrightarrow\mathcal{A}

\displaystyle(\{a_{n}\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}},\{b_{n}\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}})

\displaystyle\longrightarrow\langle\{a_{n}\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}},\{b_{n}\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\rangle_{\mathcal{A}}=\bigg\{\frac{a_{n}\overline{b_{n}}}{n}\bigg\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}}

Wich is an inner product and equiped with it,
\mathcal{H}
is a Hilbert
\mathcal{A}
-modules.

Let

\displaystyle\Lambda_{k}:\mathcal{H}

\displaystyle\longrightarrow\mathcal{H}

\displaystyle\{a_{n}\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}}

\displaystyle\longrightarrow\bigg\{\frac{a_{n}\delta_{n}^{2k+1}}{\sqrt{2k+1}}\bigg\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}},

where

\Lambda_{k}

\Lambda_{k}(\{a_{n}\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}})=(0,0,...,\frac{a_{2k+1}}{\sqrt{2k+1}},0,...)\quad For\;all\quad\{a_{n}\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\subset\mathcal{H}.\\

Hence the family
\{\Lambda_{k}\}_{k\in\mathbb{N}}
is bounded and linear.

For
\alpha,\beta>0

\displaystyle C:\mathcal{H}

\displaystyle\longrightarrow\mathcal{H}

\displaystyle\{a_{n}\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}}

\displaystyle\longrightarrow\{\alpha a_{n}\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}},

and

\displaystyle C^{{}^{\prime}}:\mathcal{H}

\displaystyle\longrightarrow\mathcal{H}

\displaystyle\{a_{n}\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}}

\displaystyle\longrightarrow\{\beta a_{n}\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}},

It’s easy to see that
C

C^{{}^{\prime}}
are bounded, linear and invertible operators.

Furthermore,

\displaystyle\sum_{k\in\mathbb{N}}\langle\Lambda_{k}C(\{a_{n}\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}}),\Lambda_{k}C^{{}^{\prime}}(\{a_{n}\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}})\rangle_{\mathcal{A}}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k\in\mathbb{N}}\langle\Lambda_{k}(\{\alpha a_{n}\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}}),\Lambda_{k}(\{\beta a_{n}\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}})\rangle_{\mathcal{A}}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k\in\mathbb{N}}\langle\bigg\{\frac{\alpha a_{n}\delta_{n}^{2k+1}}{\sqrt{2k+1}}\bigg\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}},\bigg\{\frac{\beta a_{n}\delta_{n}^{2k+1}}{\sqrt{2k+1}}\bigg\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\rangle_{\mathcal{A}}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k\in\mathbb{N}}\alpha\beta\bigg\{\frac{|a_{n}|^{2}\delta_{n}^{2k+1}}{(2k+1)n}\bigg\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}}

\displaystyle=\alpha\beta\bigg\{\frac{|a_{2k+1}|^{2}}{(2k+1)^{2}}\bigg\}_{k\in\mathbb{N}}

Then the family
\{\Lambda_{k}\}_{k\in\mathbb{N}}
is a
\ast
-
(C,C^{{}^{\prime}})
-controlled Bessel sequence, but is not a
(C,C^{{}^{\prime}})
-controlled operator frame.

Indeed,

\displaystyle\sum_{k\in\mathbb{N}}\langle\Lambda_{k}C\{a_{n}\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}},\Lambda_{k}C^{{}^{\prime}}\{a_{n}\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\rangle_{\mathbb{A}}

\displaystyle\leq\alpha\beta\bigg\{\frac{|a_{2k+1}|^{2}}{(2k+1)}\bigg\}_{k\in\mathbb{N}}

\displaystyle\leq\alpha\beta\bigg\{\frac{|a_{k}|^{2}}{k}\bigg\}_{k\in\mathbb{N}}

\displaystyle=\alpha\beta\langle\{a_{n}\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}},\{a_{n}\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\rangle_{\mathbb{A}}

\displaystyle=\sqrt{\alpha\beta}Id_{\mathbb{A}}\langle\{a_{n}\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}},\{a_{n}\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\rangle_{\mathbb{A}}\sqrt{\alpha\beta}Id_{\mathcal{A}}

But if
\{a_{n}\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\in\mathcal{H}
and

\langle\Lambda_{k}\{a_{n}\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}},\Lambda_{k}\{a_{n}\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\rangle_{\mathcal{A}}=\{0\}

So, we consider the operator

\displaystyle K:\mathcal{H}

\displaystyle\longrightarrow\mathcal{H}

\displaystyle\{a_{n}\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}}

\displaystyle\longrightarrow\{a_{2k+1}\}_{k\in\mathbb{N}}

\displaystyle A\langle K^{\ast}\{a_{n}\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}},K^{\ast}\{a_{n}\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\rangle_{\mathcal{A}}A^{\ast}=

\displaystyle\sqrt{\alpha\beta}\bigg\{\frac{1}{\sqrt{k}}\bigg\}_{k\in\mathbb{N}^{\ast}}\bigg\{\frac{|a_{2k+1}|^{2}}{2k+1}\bigg\}_{k\in\mathbb{N}}\sqrt{\alpha\beta}\bigg\{\frac{1}{\sqrt{k}}\bigg\}_{k\in\mathbb{N}^{\ast}}

\displaystyle=\alpha\beta\bigg\{\frac{|a_{2k+1}|^{2}}{(2k+1)^{2}}\bigg\}_{k\in\mathbb{N}}

\ast

Proposition 2.3
.

Every
(C,C^{{}^{\prime}})
-controlled
\ast
-operator frame for

\ast
-K-operator frame for

-controlled

-operator frame for

Therefore,
\{T_{i}\}_{i\in I}
is a
(C,C^{{}^{\prime}})
-controlled
\ast
- K-operator frame for
End_{\mathcal{A}}^{\ast}(\mathcal{H})
with bounds
A\|K\|^{-1}
and B.
