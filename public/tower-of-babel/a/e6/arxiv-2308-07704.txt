The properties of the S S -wave D s ​ D ¯ s D_{s}\bar{D}_{s} bound state
Jing-Juan Qi, Zhen-Yang Wang, Zhu-Feng Zhang, Xin-Heng Guo

Abstract
In this work, we investigate possible bound states of the D s ​ D ¯ s D_{s}\bar{D}_{s} system in the Bethe-Salpeter formalism in the ladder and instantaneous approximations. By numerically solving the Bethe-Salpeter equation with a kernel that includes the contributions from ϕ \phi and J / ψ J/\psi exchanges, we confirm the existence of a bound state in the D s ​ D ¯ s D_{s}\bar{D}_{s} system. We further investigate the partial decay widths of the D s ​ D ¯ s D_{s}\bar{D}_{s} bound state into D ​ D ¯ D\bar{D} , η c ​ η \eta_{c}\eta , and J / ψ ​ ω J/\psi\omega , finding that these partial widths are sensitive to the parameter α \alpha in our model. Notably, we observe that the dominant decay channel for the D s ​ D ¯ s D_{s}\bar{D}_{s} bound state is that into D ​ D ¯ D\bar{D} .

At the tree level, the
t
-channel interaction kernel for the BS equation in the so-called ladder approximation is

K(P,p,q)=(2\pi)^{4}\delta^{4}(p_{1}+p_{2}-q_{1}-q_{2})g_{D_{s}D_{s}\phi}^{2}(p_{1}^{\mu}+q_{1}^{\mu})(p_{2}^{\nu}+q_{2}^{\nu})\Delta_{\mu\nu}(p_{1}-q_{1}),

Since the the strong interaction vertices are determined by the physical particles and the off-shell exchanged particles, it is necessary to introduce the form factor
F(k)
to account for the off-shell effects of the
t
-channel exchanged particles. The form factor is defined as

F(k,m_{V})=\frac{\Lambda^{2}-m_{V}^{2}}{\Lambda^{2}-k^{2}},

(7)

where
\Lambda
is the cutoff in the form factor which value should be not far from the physical mass of the exchanged particle. The cutoff
\Lambda
can be further reparameterized as
\Lambda=m_{V}+\alpha\Lambda_{\text{QCD}}
with
\Lambda_{\text{QCD}}=220

\alpha
being of order of unity. The value of
\alpha

6

4

\begin{split}\chi_{P}(p)&=\frac{-1}{\left[(\lambda_{1}P_{0}+p_{0})^{2}-\omega_{1}^{2}+i\epsilon\right]\left[(\lambda_{2}P_{0}-p_{0})^{2}-\omega_{2}^{2}+i\epsilon\right]}\\

\end{split}

(8)

for each exchanged meson with mass

and coupling

\begin{split}\chi_{P}(\mathbf{p})&=2\pi i\frac{E_{1}+E_{2}}{2E_{1}E_{2}(P_{0}-E_{1}-E_{2})(P_{0}+E_{1}+E_{2})}\\

D_{s}\bar{D}_{s}
bound state. Before this, we need to obtaine the physical BS wave function, which means we need to normalize the BS wave function. The normalization condition for the BS wave function is

&=i\int\frac{d^{4}pd^{4}q}{(2\pi)^{8}}\bar{\chi}_{P}(p)\frac{\partial}{\partial P_{0}}\left\{(2\pi)^{4}\delta^{4}(p-q)\left[(\lambda_{1}P_{0}+p_{0})^{2}-\omega_{1}^{2}\right]\left[(\lambda_{2}P_{0}-p_{0})^{2}-\omega_{2}^{2}\right]\right\}\chi_{P}(q),\\

\end{split}

(11)

From the above equation we can see that all the poles come from the BS wave function

8

\begin{split}\chi_{P}(p)&=\frac{-1}{\left[(\lambda_{1}P_{0}+p_{0})^{2}-\omega_{1}^{2}\right]\left[(\lambda_{2}P_{0}-p_{0})^{2}-\omega_{2}^{2}\right]}\frac{1}{2\pi i}\frac{2\omega_{1}\omega_{2}(P_{0}-\omega_{1}-\omega_{2})(P_{0}+\omega_{1}+\omega_{2})}{\omega_{1}+\omega_{2}}\chi_{P}(\mathbf{p}).\end{split}

(12)

Then

\begin{split}&i\int\frac{d^{4}pd^{4}q}{(2\pi)^{8}}\bar{\chi}_{P}(p)\frac{\partial}{\partial P^{0}}I_{P}(p,q)\chi_{P}(q)=-\int\frac{d|\mathbf{p}|}{(2\pi)^{4}}\frac{8\omega_{1}\omega_{2}P_{0}|\mathbf{p}|^{2}}{\omega_{1}+\omega_{2}}\bar{\chi}_{P}^{2}(|\mathbf{p}|).\end{split}

For the second part of the normalization equation (
10

\begin{split}&i\int\frac{d^{4}pd^{4}q}{(2\pi)^{8}}\bar{\chi}_{P}(p)\frac{\partial}{\partial P^{0}}\bar{K}_{P}(p,q)\chi_{P}(q)=\frac{4\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2}P_{0}}{(2\pi)^{6}}\int d|\mathbf{p}|d|\mathbf{q}||\mathbf{p}||\mathbf{q}|g^{2}_{D_{s}D_{s}V}\bar{\chi}_{P}(|\mathbf{p}|)T\chi_{P}(|\mathbf{p}|),\end{split}

The coupling constants and form factors associated with strong interaction vertices play an important role in studying the possible bound states of the

Since the exchanged particle is not on shell, it is necessary to introduce the form factor

t

V
Summary

In this work we have studied the possible bound states of the
D_{s}\bar{D}_{s}
system in the BS equation, and calculated the partial decay widths of these possible bound states to
D\bar{D}
,
\eta_{c}\eta
and
J/\psi\omega
final states. To accomplish this, we have applied the ladder and instantaneous approximations in the the kernel which is induced by the contributions of
\phi
and
J/\psi
exchange diagrames. Our calculations have revealed that the
D_{s}\bar{D}_{s}
system can be formed as bound states when both the
\phi
and
J/\psi
exchange contributions are considered, while it would not be formed as a bound state when only one of these exchange diagrames were taken into account. The possible bound states of the
D_{s}\bar{D}_{s}
system for bounding energy
E_{b}
= 5, 15, 30 MeV have been presented in Fig.
1
.

We have applied the numerical solutions for the BS wave functions to calculate the partial decay widths of the
D_{s}\bar{D}_{s}
bound states to
D\bar{D}
,
\eta_{c}\eta
and
J/\psi\omega
final states. The partial decay widths to
D\bar{D}
,
\eta_{c}\eta
and
J/\psi\omega
final states have been presented in Figs.
2
,
3
, and
4
, respectively. Our results have indicated that the partial decay widths depend on
\alpha
and are sensitive to the value of
\alpha
in some regions. We gave found that the
D_{s}\bar{D}_{s}
bound state decays dominantly to the
D\bar{D}
final state. Thus if we neglect the contributions of the partial decay widths of the
D_{s}\bar{D}_{s}
bound state from other channels like light meosns and photon radiations decays, the total decay width of the
D_{s}\bar{D}_{s}
bound state can be estimated to be 30.67
\sim
61.15 MeV, 54.56
\sim
98.14 MeV, and 76.42
\sim
127.35 MeV for bounding energy
E_{b}
= 5, 15, and 30 MeV, respectively, indicating the decay width of the
D_{s}\bar{D}_{s}
bound state increases with the binding energy increase. Hopefully, our understanding about the properties of the
D_{s}\bar{D}_{s}
bound state will be tested in the experimental exploration.
