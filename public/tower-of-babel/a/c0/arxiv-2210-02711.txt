On Andreae’s Ubiquity Conjecture
Johannes Carmesin

Abstract
A graph H H is ubiquitous if for every graph G G that for every natural number n n contains n n vertex-disjoint H H -minors contains infinitely many vertex-disjoint H H -minors. Andreae conjectured that every locally finite graph is ubiquitous. We give a disconnected counterexample to this conjecture. It remains open whether every connected locally finite graph is ubiquitous.
