Decay properties of Axially Symmetric D-solutions to the Steady Incompressible Magnetohydrodynamic Equations
Shangkun Weng, Yan Zhou

Abstract
In this paper, we investigate the decay properties of axially symmetric solutions to the steady incompressible magnetohydrodynamic equations in ℝ 3 \mathbb{R}^{3} with finite Dirichlet integral. We first derive the decay rates of general D-solutions to the axisymmetric MHD equations. In the special case where the magnetic field only has the swirl component 𝐡 ⁡ ( r , z ) = h θ ​ ( r , z ) ​ 𝐞 θ {\bf h}(r,z)=h_{\theta}(r,z){\bf e_{\theta}} , we obtain better decay rates. The last result examines the decay rates along the axis O ​ z Oz also within the special class of D-solutions with only swirl magnetic field. The main tool in this paper is the combination of the scaling argument, the Brezis-Gallouet inequality and the weighted energy estimate.

Introduction

In this paper, we investigate the decay properties of axially symmetric solutions to the steady Magnetohydrodynamics equations (MHD), which are listed as follows.

\left\{\begin{array}[]{l}\textbf{u}\cdot\nabla\textbf{u}+\nabla p=\textbf{h}\cdot\nabla\textbf{h}+\triangle\textbf{u}+\textbf{f},\\
\textbf{u}\cdot\nabla\textbf{h}-\textbf{h}\cdot\nabla\textbf{u}=\triangle\textbf{h}+\rm{curl}\,\textbf{g},\\
\nabla\cdot\textbf{u}=\nabla\cdot\textbf{h}=0,\end{array}\right.

(1.1)

with the additional condition at infinity

\displaystyle\lim\limits_{|x|\to\infty}\textbf{u}(x)=\lim\limits_{|x|\to\infty}\textbf{h}(x)=0,

(1.2)

and the finite Dirichlet integral

\int_{\mathbb{R}^{3}}|\nabla\textbf{u}(x)|^{2}+|\nabla{\bf h}(x)|^{2}dx<+\infty.

(1.3)

\displaystyle\frac{C}{d}\bigg(\frac{C}{|z|^{\frac{1}{2}(1+\tau)}}\bigg)^{1/2}\bigg(\frac{C}{d^{2}|z|^{(\frac{5}{21}(1+2\tau))^{-}}}\bigg)^{\frac{1}{2}}\leq\frac{C}{d^{2}|z|^{(\frac{31}{84}+\frac{41}{84}\tau)^{-}}},

(5.11)

Together with previous decay results on

\displaystyle|h_{\theta}(r,z)|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C(M)(\rho+1)^{-(\frac{31}{168}+\frac{41}{168}\tau)^{-}},\quad\forall(r,z)\in\mathbb{R}_{+}\times\mathbb{R},\rho=\sqrt{r^{2}+z^{2}}.

In general case where

Step 3.
For the special case where the flow has zero swirl

\displaystyle(u_{r}\partial_{r}+u_{z}\partial_{z})\Omega+\partial_{z}\Pi^{2}=\bigg(\partial_{r}^{2}+\frac{3}{r}\partial_{r}+\partial_{z}^{2}\bigg)\Omega+\frac{1}{r}(\partial_{z}f_{r}-\partial_{r}f_{z}),

\Omega\coloneqq\frac{\omega^{u}_{\theta}(r,z)}{r}

[
35
]
for
\Omega
to derive the estimate
\int_{\mathbb{R}^{3}}\rho^{d_{1}}|\nabla\Omega|^{2}dx

\displaystyle\bigg|\int_{\mathbb{R}^{3}}\phi^{2}\rho^{d_{1}}\Omega\partial_{z}\Pi^{2}dx\bigg|

\displaystyle C\|\Pi\|_{L^{\infty}}\|\Omega\|_{L^{2}}(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{3}}\rho^{2d_{1}}|\partial_{z}\Pi|^{2}dx)^{1/2}

Thus

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{3}}\rho^{\frac{1+\tau}{2}}|\nabla\Omega(r,z)|^{2}dx<\infty.

(5.13)

To derive the estimate of

\displaystyle 0

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{2}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{3}}\partial_{r}(\phi^{2}\rho^{d_{2}})\frac{|\partial_{z}\Omega|^{2}}{r}dx-\int_{\mathbb{R}^{3}}\partial_{z}(\phi^{2}\rho^{d_{2}})\partial_{z}\Omega(u_{r}\partial_{r}+u_{z}\partial_{z})\Omega dx

\displaystyle-\int_{\mathbb{R}^{3}}\phi^{2}\rho^{d_{2}}\partial_{z}^{2}\Omega(u_{r}\partial_{r}+u_{z}\partial_{z})\Omega dx-\int_{\mathbb{R}^{3}}\partial_{z}(\phi^{2}\rho^{d_{2}})\partial_{z}\Omega\cdot\partial_{z}\Pi^{2}dx

\displaystyle|K_{1}|+|K_{2}|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle|K_{6}|
