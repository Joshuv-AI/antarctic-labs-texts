Bounds on Binary Niederreiter-Rosenbloom-Tsfasman LCD codes
Welington Santos

Abstract
Linear complementary dual codes (LCD codes) are codes whose intersections with their dual codes are trivial. These codes were introduced by Massey in 1992. LCD codes have wide applications in data storage, communication systems and cryptography. Niederreiter-Rosenbloom-Tsfasman LCD codes (NRT-LCD codes) were introduced by Heqian, Guangku and Wei as a generalization of LCD codes for the NRT metric space M n , s ​ ( 𝔽 q ) M_{n,s}(\mathbb{F}_{q}) . In this paper, we study LCD [ n × s , k ] [n\times s,k] , the maximum minimum NRT distance among all binary [ n × s , k ] [n\times s,k] NRT-LCD codes. We prove the existence (non-existence) of binary maximum distance separable NRT-LCD codes in M 1 , s ​ ( 𝔽 2 ) M_{1,s}(\mathbb{F}_{2}) . We present a linear programming bound for binary NRT-LCD codes in M n , 2 ​ ( 𝔽 2 ) M_{n,2}(\mathbb{F}_{2}) . We also give two methods to construct binary NRT-LCD codes.

An
[s,k]

.

Coding theory has also been developed with respect to alternative metrics. One of those metrics is the Niederreiter Rosenbloom-Tsfasman (NRT for short) metric which was introduced in

The NRT metric is more appropriate for the correction of random errors that occur with high probability in the first coordinates. Since its definition several coding-theoretic questions with respect to the Niederreiter Rosenbloom-Tsfasman metric have been investigated, such as bounds

Preliminaries

2.1
Niederreiter Rosenbloom-Tsfasman metric codes

Let
M_{n,s}(\mathbb{F}_{q})
denote the set of all
n\times s
matrices over the finite field
\mathbb{F}_{q}
.

Given a matrix
v=[v_{i,j}]_{n\times s}\in M_{n,s}(\mathbb{F}_{q})
, we write
v=[v_{1};v_{2};\ldots;v_{n}]
or
v=[w_{1}\mid w_{2}\mid\cdots\mid w_{s}]
, where
v_{i}=[v_{i,1},\ldots,v_{i,s}]
is the
i
-th row of
v
, and
w_{j}=[w_{1,j},w_{2,j},\ldots;w_{n,j}]
is the
j
-th column of
v
.

The Niederreiter-Rosenbloom-Tsfasman (for short, NRT) weight of
v
is given by

\rho(v)=\sum_{i=1}^{n}\rho(v_{i})

(1)

where,

\displaystyle\rho(v_{i})

\displaystyle=\left\{\begin{array}[]{cl}\max\left\{j:v_{i,j}\neq 0\right\}&\text{if}\ v_{i}\neq 0\\
0&\text{if}\ v_{i}=0.\end{array}\right.

for
i=1,2,\ldots,n
.

The NRT metric is defined by
d_{N}(u,v)=\rho(u-v)
, where
u,v\in M_{n,s}(\mathbb{F}_{q})
. We note that for
s=1
, that is, for
v\in M_{n,1}\cong\mathbb{F}^{n}_{q}
the NRT metric becomes the usual Hamming metric.

Given a subset
\mathcal{C}\subseteq M_{n,s}(\mathbb{F}_{q})
the minimum NRT distance of
\mathcal{C}
is defined by:

d_{N}(\mathcal{C})=\min\left\{d_{N}(v,u):d_{N}(v,u)\neq 0\right\}.

(5)

We sometimes write
d_{N}
rather than
d_{N}(\mathcal{C})
if the code is clear from the context.

Given
\mathcal{C}\subseteq M_{n,s}(\mathbb{F}_{q})
it is easy to check that
d_{H}(\mathcal{C})\leq d_{N}(\mathcal{C})
, where
d_{H}
is the minimum Hamming distance of
\mathcal{C}
.

Definition 1
.

An
[n\times s,k,d_{N}]
NRT metric code is a linear subspace
\mathcal{C}\subseteq M_{n,s}(\mathbb{F}_{q})
of dimension
k
and minimum NRT distance
d_{N}
.

Note that the minimum distance of NRT metric code
\mathcal{C}\subseteq M_{n,s}(\mathbb{F}_{q})
is given by
d_{N}(\mathcal{C})=\min\{\rho(v):v\in\mathcal{C},v\neq 0\}
. Moreover, we have the following singleton bound for
[n\times s,k,d_{N}]
NRT metric codes

d_{N}(\mathcal{C})\leq ns-k+1.

(6)

An NRT metric code with
d_{N}(\mathcal{C})=ns-k+1
is called maximum distance separable code.

2.2
Niederreiter-Rosenbloom-Tsfasman LCD codes

Niederreiter-Rosenbloom-Tsfasman linear complementary dual (NRT-LCD) codes were defined in
H.Xu
. In order to study NRT-LCD codes, we introduce the following definitions.

Definition 2
.

The dual code
\mathcal{C}^{\perp_{N}}
of an
[n\times s,k,d_{N}]
NRT metric code
\mathcal{C}
is defined as

\mathcal{C}^{\perp_{N}}=\{u\in M_{n,s}(\mathbb{F}_{q})\mid\langle u,v\rangle_{N}=0\text{ for all }v\in\mathcal{C}\},

where for
u=[u_{1};\ldots;u_{n}],v=[v_{1};\ldots;v_{n}]\in M_{n,s}(\mathbb{F}_{q})

\langle u,v\rangle_{N}=\sum_{i=1}^{n}\langle u_{i},v_{i}\rangle_{N}\text{, }\langle u_{i},v_{i}\rangle_{N}=\sum_{j=1}^{s}u_{i,j}u_{i,s-j+1}.

(7)

Definition 3
.

(
H.Xu
)
An
[n\times s,k,d_{N}]
NRT metric code
\mathcal{C}
is called NRT linear complementary dual code (NRT-LCD code for short) if
\mathcal{C}\cap\mathcal{C}^{\perp_{N}}=\{0\}
.

Definition 4
.

(
Marka
)
Let

is defined by

, where

is denoted by

.

Definition 5
.

(
InvariantSantos
)
Let
A=[A_{1}\mid A_{2}\mid\cdots\mid A_{n}]\in M_{k,ns}(\mathbb{F}_{q})
, where
A_{i}
is a
k\times s
matrix for
i=1,2,\ldots,n
. The ordered flip of

is defined by

Oflip(A)=[flip(A_{1})\mid flip(A_{2})\mid\cdots\mid flip(A_{n})].

The transpose of

is denoted by

A^{{\dagger}}=[Oflip(A)]^{T}=\left[\begin{array}[]{c}A^{\circ}_{1}\\
A^{\circ}_{2}\\
\vdots\\
A^{\circ}_{n}\end{array}\right].

(8)

Note that, if
n=1
then,
A^{{\dagger}}=A^{\circ}
.

.

(

)

Definition 8

6
Conclusion

In this paper, we studied the maximum minimum distance of a binary NRT-LCD code. In particular, we proved the existence of binary maximum distance separable
[1\times s,k]
NRT-LCD codes for the cases where
k
is even or
s
and
k
are odd. We derived conditions on
n
for the existence of binary
[n\times 2,k]
NRT-LCD codes. Finally, we present a linear programming bound for binary
[n\times 2,k]
NRT-LCD codes and two new constructions. It would be interesting to find construction methods of maximum distance separable codes with small hulls for the parameters that we can not construct maximum distance separable NRT-LCD code.
