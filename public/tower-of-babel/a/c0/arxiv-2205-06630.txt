M’hamed Ghiati 1∗ , Mohamed Rossafi 2 , Mohammed Mouniane 3 , Hatim Labrigui 4 and Abdeslam Touri 5

Abstract
. The frame theory is dynamic and exciting with various pure and applied mathematics applications. In this paper, we introduce and study the concept of Controlled Continuous ∗ \ast - g g -Frames in Hilbert C ∗ C^{\ast} -Modules, which is a generalization of discrete controlled ∗ \ast - g g -Frames in Hilbert C ∗ C^{\ast} -Modules. Also, we give some properties.

Proposition 2.3
.

Let
\Lambda=\{\Lambda_{w}\in End_{\mathcal{A}}^{\ast}(U,V_{w}):w\in\Omega\}

From the definition of
\Lambda

\Theta

\Gamma
, we have for any

\displaystyle\bigg\|\int_{\Omega}\Gamma_{\omega}\Lambda^{\ast}_{\omega}\theta_{w}xd\mu(w)\bigg\|^{2}

\displaystyle=\underset{y\in U,\|y\|\leq 1}{\sup}\bigg\|\langle\int_{\Omega}\Gamma_{\omega}\Lambda^{\ast}_{\omega}\theta_{w}xd\mu(w),y\rangle\bigg\|^{2}

\displaystyle=\underset{y\in U,\|y\|\leq 1}{\sup}\bigg\|\int_{\Omega}\langle\Gamma_{\omega}\theta_{w}xd\mu(w),\Lambda_{\omega}y\rangle\bigg\|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\underset{y\in U,\|y\|\leq 1}{\sup}\bigg\|\int_{\Omega}\langle\Gamma_{\omega}\theta_{w}x,\Gamma_{\omega}\theta_{w}x\rangle d\mu(w)\bigg\|\bigg\|\int_{\Omega}\langle\Lambda_{\omega}y,\Lambda_{\omega}y\rangle d\mu(w)\bigg\|.

On other hand, we have

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}\langle\Gamma_{\omega}\theta_{w}x,\Gamma_{\omega}\theta_{w}x\rangle d\mu(w)

\displaystyle=\int_{\Omega}|\Gamma_{\omega}|^{2}\langle\theta_{w}x,\theta_{w}x\rangle d\mu(w)

\displaystyle\leq\|\Gamma_{\omega}\|^{2}_{\infty}\int_{\Omega}\langle\theta_{w}x,\theta_{w}x\rangle d\mu(w)

\displaystyle\leq\|\Gamma_{\omega}\|^{2}_{\infty}B_{\Theta}\langle x,x\rangle B^{\ast}_{\Theta}.

Hence

\displaystyle\bigg\|\int_{\Omega}\Gamma_{\omega}\Lambda^{\ast}_{\omega}\theta_{w}xd\mu(w)\bigg\|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\underset{y\in U,\|y\|\leq 1}{\sup}\|\Gamma_{\omega}\|^{2}_{\infty}\|B_{\Theta}\|^{2}\|\langle x,x\rangle\|\|B_{\Lambda}\|^{2}\|\langle y,y\rangle\|

\displaystyle=\|\Gamma_{\omega}\|^{2}_{\infty}\|B_{\Theta}\|^{2}\|\langle x,x\rangle\|\|B_{\Lambda}\|^{2},

\|L_{\Gamma,\Lambda,\Theta}\|\leq\|\Gamma_{\omega}\|^{2}_{\infty}\|B_{\Theta}\|^{2}\|B_{\Lambda}\|^{2}
