On the Iwasawa invariants of BDP Selmer groups and BDP p p -adic L L -functions
Antonio Lei, Katharina Müller, Jiacheng Xia

Abstract
. Let p p be an odd prime. Let f 1 f_{1} and f 2 f_{2} be weight 2 2 Hecke eigen cusp forms with isomorphic residual Galois representations at p p . Greenberg–Vatsal and Emerton–Pollack–Weston showed that if p p is a good ordinary prime for the two forms, the Iwasawa invariants of their p p -primary Selmer groups and p p -adic L L -functions over the cyclotomic ℤ p \mathbb{Z}_{p} -extension of ℚ \mathbb{Q} are closely related. The goal of this article is to generalize these results to the anticyclotomic setting. More precisely, let K K be an imaginary quadratic field where p p splits. Suppose that the generalized Heegner hypothesis holds with respect to both ( f 1 , K ) (f_{1},K) and ( f 2 , K ) (f_{2},K) . We study relations between the Iwasawa invariants of the BDP Selmer groups and the BDP p p -adic L L -functions of f 1 f_{1} and f 2 f_{2} .

The goal of this subsection is to prove an integral version of Corollary

[
