On the modularity of elliptic curves over the cyclotomic ℤ p \mathbb{Z}_{p} -extension of some real quadratic fields
Xinyao Zhang

Abstract
. The modularity of elliptic curves always intrigues number theorists. Recently, Thorne had proved a marvelous result that for a prime p p , every elliptic curve defined over a p p -cyclotomic extension of ℚ \mathbb{Q} is modular. The method is to use some automorphy lifting theorems and study non-cusp points on some specific elliptic curves by Iwasawa theory for elliptic curves. Since the modularity of elliptic curves over real quadratic was proved, one may ask whether it is possible to replace ℚ \mathbb{Q} with a real quadratic field K K . Following Thorne’s idea, we give some assumptions first and prove the modularity of elliptic curves over the ℤ p \mathbb{Z}_{p} -extension of some real quadratic fields.

Lemma 3.5

Let

E_{i}(\mathbb{Q})[l^{\infty}]=E_{i}(K_{\infty})[l^{\infty}]

Proof.

Considering the Galois representation
\bar{\rho}_{E,l}:G_{\mathbb{Q}}\rightarrow\operatorname{GL_{2}}(\mathbb{F}_{l})

[
Tho19
]

f_{E_{i}}(0)\sim\left(\prod_{v~\mathrm{bad}}c_{v}^{(p)}\right)\left(\prod_{v\mid p}\left|\tilde{E_{i}}_{,v}\left(\mathbb{F}_{v}\right)_{p}\right|^{2}\right)\left|\operatorname{Sel}(E_{i}/K)_{p}\right|/\left|E_{i}(K)_{p}\right|^{2}.

As

Case 2.

In this case, we have the formula [
