Groups having 11 11 or 12 12 cyclic subgroups
Khyati Sharma, A. Satyanarayana Reddy

Abstract
A group is n n -cyclic if it has exactly n n cyclic subgroups. In this paper, we classify 11 11 -cyclic groups and 12 12 -cyclic groups.



Keywords
: n-cyclic group, Cyclic subgroup, Sylow theorem, Maximal subgroup

Mathematics Subject Classification
: 20D20 . 20D25
