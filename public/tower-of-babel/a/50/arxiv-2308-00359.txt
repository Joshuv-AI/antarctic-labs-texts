Abstract
In this paper, the ∂ ¯ \overline{\partial} -steepest descent method and Bäcklund transformation are used to study the asymptotic stability of solitons to the Cauchy problem of focusing Hirota equation i ​ q t + α ⁡ ( 2 ​ | q | 2 ​ q + q x ​ x ) + i ​ β ​ ( q x ​ x ​ x + 6 ​ | q | 2 ​ q x ) = 0 , \displaystyle iq_{t}+\alpha(2|q|^{2}q+q_{xx})+i\beta(q_{xxx}+6|q|^{2}q_{x})=0, q ​ ( x , 0 ) = q 0 ​ ( x ) , \displaystyle q(x,0)=q_{0}(x), where q 0 ∈ H 1 ​ ( ℝ ) ∩ L 2 , s ​ ( ℝ ) , s ∈ ( 1 2 , 1 ] . q_{0}\in H^{1}(\mathbb{R})\,\cap\,L^{2,s}(\mathbb{R}),s\in(\frac{1}{2},1].
