\acute{o}

\displaystyle\omega_{Q}^{1/\bar{p}_{2}-1/\bar{p}_{1}}\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}\frac{|y|_{h}^{-Q/p}}{\max(1,|y|_{h}^{Q})}dy=\omega_{Q}^{1/\bar{p}_{2}-1/\bar{p}_{1}}\frac{\omega_{Q}Q}{(Q-Q/p)Q/p},

Prove completion.
