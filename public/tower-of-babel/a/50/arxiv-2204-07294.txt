Systematic analysis of the proton mass radius based on photoproduction of vector charmoniums
Xiao-Yun Wang, Fancong Zeng, Quanjin Wang

Abstract
In this work, the cross-section of the reaction γ ​ p → V ⁡ ( J / ψ , ψ ⁡ ( 2 ​ S ) ) ​ p \gamma p\rightarrow V(J/\psi,\psi(2S))p from the production threshold to medium energy is studied and systematically analyzed within two gluon exchange model. The obtained numerical results are in agreement with experimental data and other theoretical predictions. Under the assumption of the scalar form factor of dipole form, the value of proton mass radius is calculated as 0.55 ± 0.09 0.55\pm 0.09 fm and 0.77 ± 0.12 0.77\pm 0.12 fm from the fit to the predicted J / ψ J/\psi and ψ ⁡ ( 2 ​ S ) \psi(2S) differential cross-section, respectively. Finally the average value of proton mass radius is estimated to be ⟨ R m 2 ⟩ = 0.67 ± 0.11 \sqrt{\left<R^{2}_{m}\right>}=0.67\pm 0.11 fm. Moreover, one find that extracting mass radius from the near-threshold differential cross-section of heavy quarkoniums is always affected by large | t | m ​ i ​ n |t|_{min} . These obtained results may provide important theoretical reference for understanding of nucleon structure and future relevant experiments.

INTRODUCTION

The proton radius is a big inspiration in understanding the proton structure, and it can be measured by using lepton as probe.
Usually, the proton radius can be estimated by colliding the nucleus with high-energy electrons and observing the angles and energies of these electrons scattered from the nucleus. In the past decade, several groups have given their results

0.8409

ParticleDataGroup:2020ssz

0.817
fm
Cui:2021skn
, respectively. In fact, it is theoretically possible to use graviton as a probe to determine the proton radius, which is usually called proton mass radius. Since the interaction of gravitons and proton scattering is very weak, far beyond the measurement limit of current experiments, it is difficult to directly measure the proton mass radius experimentally. In recent study, mass radius is described by the scalar gravitational form factors (GFFs) of the energy momentum tensor trace of quantum chromodynamics (QCD)
Kobzarev:1962wt
;
Pagels:1966zza
;
Ji:1996nm

Wang:2019mza
;
Ji:1996nm
. Under an assumption of the scalar form factor of dipole form, the proton mass radius can be extracted via the near-threshold photoproduction data of vector quarkoniums
Kharzeev:2021qkd
;
Fujii:1999xn
.

In recent years, researchers have made relevant calculations and studies on the proton mass radius using vector meson photoproduction data.
In ref.
Guo:2021ibg
, the gluonic contributions to the quantum anomalous energy, mass radius, spin, and mechanical pressure in the proton are studied by analyzing the near-threshold production of heavy quarkonium, and proton mass radius is calculated as 0.68 fm. In the charm quark energy region, due to the mass of

Therefore, one need to first consider the theoretical calculation and prediction of charmoniums (

\psi(2S)

II
FORMALISM

II.1
two gluon exchange model

The two gluon exchange model is based on the photon fluctuation into the quark-antiquark pair (
\gamma\rightarrow c+\bar{c}
) and the double gluon exchange between the nucleon state and the quark-antiquark pair. Here the
c\bar{c}
fluctuation of the photon treated as a colour dipole. Finally the dipole forms into final vector meson. The picture of the double gluon exchange is illustrated in Fig.
1
. Here, we describe the process

\gamma(q)+p(p)\rightarrow V(q_{1})+p(p_{1})

(1)

where
V=J/\psi
,
\psi(2S)
.

Figure 1:
The schematic Feynman diagram of the two-gluon exchange for
V(J/\psi,\psi(2S))
production.

Due to the hard scale in the heavy quarkonium production, the exclusive vector meson photoproduction amplitude is given by

Ryskin:1992ui
;
Brodsky:1994kf
;
Ryskin:1995hz

\begin{split}\mathcal{T}=&\frac{i\sqrt{2}\pi^{2}}{3}m_{q}\alpha_{s}e_{q}f_{V}F_{2g}(t)\\
&\left[\frac{xg\left(x,Q_{0}^{2}\right)}{m_{q}^{4}}+\int_{Q_{0}^{2}}^{+\infty}\frac{dl^{2}}{m_{q}^{2}\left(m_{q}^{2}+l^{2}\right)}\frac{\partial xg\left(x,l^{2}\right)}{\partial l^{2}}\right].\end{split}

(2)

The amplitude is normalized and
\frac{d\sigma}{dt}=\alpha|\mathcal{T}|^{2}
.
The
J/\psi
and
\psi(2S)
photoproduction differential cross-section at low momentum transfer are purely diffeactive and given as
Sibirtsev:2004ca
;
Zeng:2020coc

\displaystyle\frac{d\sigma}{dt}=\frac{\pi^{3}\Gamma^{V}_{e^{+}e^{-}}\alpha_{s}}{6\alpha m_{q}^{5}}\left[xg\left(x,m_{V}^{2}\right)\right]^{2}\exp(bt),

(3)

where
x=m_{V}^{2}/W^{2}
,
W
is the center-of-mass energy of the
\gamma p
collision;
t
is the momentum transfer.

\alpha_{s}=0.5
is the strong running coupling constant
Xu:2020uaa
,
\alpha=1/137
is the electromagnetic coupling constant,
m_{q}=1.27
GeV is the mass of
c
quark.

\Gamma^{V}_{e^{+}e^{-}}
is the radiative decay of vector meson.
In this paper, we set
\Gamma_{e^{+}e^{-}}^{J/\psi}=5.547
keV and
\Gamma_{e^{+}e^{-}}^{\psi(2S)}=2.33
keV from PDG
ParticleDataGroup:2018ovx
.
The factor
xg\left(x,m_{V}^{2}\right)
is the gluon distribution function at
Q^{2}=m_{V}^{2}
, which is parameterized using a simple function form
xg\left(x,m_{V}^{2}\right)=A_{0}x^{A_{1}}(1-x)^{A_{2}}

Pumplin:2002vw
. In this paper, the photoproduction of
J/\psi
and
\psi(2S)
will use the same gluon distribution function.

The last exponential factor in Eq.
3
usually describes the differential cross-section of vector meson at low momentum transfer.
The exponential slope
b
for
J/\psi
has little vary with
W
, which is given as
Zeng:2020coc

\displaystyle b^{J/\psi}(W)=b_{0}^{J/\psi}+0.46\cdot In(W/W_{0}^{J/\psi})

(4)

and fixed the slope
b_{0}^{J/\psi}=1.67\pm 0.38
GeV
-2
at the energy
W_{0}^{J/\psi}=4.58
GeV
GlueX:2019mkq
.
For
b^{\psi(2S)}(W)
, we
use the standard form based on the Regge phenomenology
Hentschinski:2020yfm
;
Cepila:2019skb

\displaystyle b^{\psi(2S)}(W)=b_{0}^{\psi(2S)}+4\alpha^{\prime}(0)~In(W/W_{0}),~~W_{0}=90~\mathrm{GeV}

(5)

the parameters have been determined from a fit to HERA data that
b_{0}^{\psi(2S)}=4.86
, and
\alpha^{\prime}(0)=0.151

Hentschinski:2020yfm
.

The total cross-section is obtained by integrating the differential cross-section (Eq.
3
)
over the allowed kinematical range from
t_{min}
to
t_{max}
,
which can be written as

\displaystyle\sigma=\int_{t_{min}}^{t_{max}}dt~\left(\frac{d\sigma}{dt}\right)

(6)

here, the limiting values
t_{min}
and
t_{max}
are

\displaystyle t_{max}\left(t_{min}\right)=\left[\frac{m_{1}^{2}-m_{3}^{2}-m_{2}^{2}+m_{4}^{2}}{2W}\right]^{2}-\left(p_{1\mathrm{cm}}\mp p_{3\mathrm{cm}}\right)^{2}

(7)

The center-of-mass energies and momenta of the incoming photon and vector meson are

\displaystyle p_{i\mathrm{cm}}=\sqrt{E_{i\mathrm{cm}}^{2}-m_{i}^{2}}~~(i=1,3),

(8)

\displaystyle E_{1\mathrm{cm}}=\frac{W^{2}+m_{1}^{2}-m_{2}^{2}}{2W},~\mathrm{and}~E_{3\mathrm{cm}}=\frac{W^{2}+m_{3}^{2}-m_{4}^{2}}{2W}.

(9)

In addition, one noticed that in refs.
Kharzeev:1995ij
;
Kharzeev:1998bz
;
Gryniuk:2016mpk
, the real part of the
J/\psi-p
scattering amplitude is of great success in vector meson photoproduction around the threshold, which indicate that the real part of the
scattering amplitude is in general important at low energies. Considering the trace terms in the OPE can no longer be neglected at low energies, more theoretical research on the two gluon exchange model and related physical mechanisms is still needed.

II.2
GFFs and mass radius

The mass radius can be defined in terms of the scalar gravitational form factors
G(t)
,
the definition of the mass radius is given by
Miller:2018ybm
;
Kharzeev:2021qkd

\displaystyle\left<R^{2}_{m}\right>\equiv\frac{6}{M}\left.\frac{dG(t)}{dt}\right|_{t=0}

(10)

with
G(0)=M
.
And the scalar gravitational form factor is defined as

\displaystyle G(t)=\frac{M}{(1-t/m_{s}^{2})^{2}}

(11)

in which
m_{s}
is a free parameter.
According to the definition, the mass radius is
connected to the dipole parameter
m_{s}
as

\displaystyle\left<R^{2}_{m}\right>=\frac{12}{m_{s}^{2}}

(12)

The differential cross-section of the photoproduction of the quarkonium can be described with the GFFs, which is written as
Kharzeev:2021qkd
;
Mamo:2019mka
;
Hatta:2019lxo
;
Frankfurt:2002ka

\displaystyle\frac{d\sigma}{dt}\propto G^{2}(t)

(13)

Therefore, it is an effective way to extract proton mass radius by learning the near-threshold photoproduction data of charmoniums.

III.2
proton mass radius

Under the assumption of the scalar form factor of dipole form, the proton mass radius can be extracted from the near-threshold differential cross-section of vector charmoniums.
In this work, the differential cross-section of
\gamma p\rightarrow\psi(2S)p

are predicted by two gluon exchange model. We choose the c.m. energy

2

|t|_{min}

13

For

Note that, the proton mass radius mainly depends on the slope of the vector meson threshold photoproduction cross-section, and is hardly affected by the cross-section size. However, one found that although the slope

at the threshold changes very slowly (which can be verified by Eq.

Actually, the mainly reason is the rapidly varies of
|t|_{min}

|t|
at the threshold equals

\displaystyle|t|_{thr}=|t|_{min}(W_{thr})=|t|_{max}(W_{thr})=\frac{M_{N}M_{V}^{2}}{M_{N}+M_{V}}

By analyzing the formula, it can be obtained that when the mass of the vector meson is heavier, the corresponding

For some light vector mesons (such as
\phi

|t|_{min}

IV
Summary

We have reproduced the total and differential cross-section
of the reaction
\gamma p\rightarrow J/\psi p
from the production threshold to medium energy (
W
near
400
GeV) with the two gluon exchange model encountering a parameterized gluon distribution function. By fitting the experimental data, one get the parameters of the gluon distribution function and the result show that the two gluon exchange model depicts well both the differential and total cross-section of
J/\psi
in a wide energy range.
Subsequently, one estimate the photoproduction of
\psi(2S)
meson using the gluon distribution determined by
J/\psi
photoproduction. The comparison between our theoretical prediction and
\psi(2S)
experimental data is well.
Naturally, the proton mass radius is extracted by using the predicted differential cross-section of charmoniums (
J/\psi
and
\psi(2S)
). After system analysis, the average value of the proton mass radius is estimated to be
0.67\pm 0.11
fm. This value is smaller than proton charge and magnetic radius, but is basically close to the mass radius value given by other theoretical groups
Wang:2021dis
;
Guo:2021ibg
;
Kharzeev:2021qkd
.
We also find that extracting mass radius from the near-threshold differential cross-section is always affected by large
|t|_{min}
. This requires us to take special care in extracting mass radius through the near-threshold cross-section of heavy vector quarkoniums photoproduction. Of course, more accurate experimental measurement data for the photo/electro-production of charmoniums is still needed, which can not only be realized in the JLab experiment
GlueX:2019mkq
, but also within the capabilities of EicC and US-EIC facility
Anderle:2021wcy
;
Accardi:2012qut
.
Moreover, the results of charmoniums photoproduction will provide an important theoretical reference for Ultraperipheral collisions (UPCs)
Klein:2016yzr
;
Klein:2019avl
;
Wang:2020stx
;
Xie:2020wfe
.
Therefore, it would also be interesting to investigate the production of charmoniums in
ep
and
pA
collisions according to the actual situation of EIC and UPCs experiments.
