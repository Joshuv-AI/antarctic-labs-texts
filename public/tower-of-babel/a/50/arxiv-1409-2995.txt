A Combined Fit on the Annihilation Corrections in B u , d , s B_{u,d,s} → \to P ​ P PP Decays Within QCDF
Qin Chang, Junfeng Sun, Yueling Yang, Xiaonan Li

Abstract
Motivated by the possible large annihilation contributions implied by recent CDF and LHCb measurements on nonleptonic annihilation B B -meson decays, and the refined experimental measurements on hadronic B B -meson decays, we study the strength of annihilation contributions within QCD factorization (QCDF) in this paper. With the available measurements of two-body B u , d , s B_{u,d,s} → {\to} π ​ π {\pi}{\pi} , π ​ K {\pi}K , K ​ K KK decays, a comprehensive fit on the phenomenological parameters X A i , f X_{A}^{i,f} (or ρ A i , f {\rho}_{A}^{i,f} and ϕ A i , f {\phi}_{A}^{i,f} ) which are used to parameterize the endpoint singularity in annihilation amplitudes is performed with the statistical χ 2 \chi^{2} approach. It is found that (1) flavor symmetry breaking effects are hardly to be distinguished between X A , s i X_{A,s}^{i} and X A , d i X_{A,d}^{i} due to the large experimental errors and theoretical uncertainties, where X A , s i X_{A,s}^{i} and X A , d i X_{A,d}^{i} are related to the nonfactorization annihilation contributions in B s B_{s} and B u , d B_{u,d} decay, respectively. So X A , s i X_{A,s}^{i} ≃ {\simeq} X A , d i X_{A,d}^{i} is a good approximation by now. (2) In principle, parameter X A f X_{A}^{f} which is related to the factorization annihilation contributions and independent of the initial state can be regarded as the same variable for B u , d , s B_{u,d,s} decays. (3) Numerically, two solutions are found, one is ( ρ A i , ϕ A i [ ∘ ] ) (\rho_{A}^{i},\phi_{A}^{i}[^{\circ}]) = = ( 2.98 − 0.86 + 1.12 , − 105 − 24 + 34 ) (2.98^{+1.12}_{-0.86},-105^{+34}_{-24}) and ( ρ A f , ϕ A f [ ∘ ] ) (\rho_{A}^{f},\phi_{A}^{f}[^{\circ}]) = = ( 1.18 − 0.23 + 0.20 , − 40 − 8 + 11 ) (1.18^{+0.20}_{-0.23},-40^{+11}_{-8}) , the other is ( ρ A i , ϕ A i [ ∘ ] ) (\rho_{A}^{i},\phi_{A}^{i}[^{\circ}]) = = ( 2.97 − 0.90 + 1.19 , − 105 − 24 + 32 ) (2.97^{+1.19}_{-0.90},-105^{+32}_{-24}) and ( ρ A f , ϕ A f [ ∘ ] ) (\rho_{A}^{f},\phi_{A}^{f}[^{\circ}]) = = ( 2.80 − 0.21 + 0.25 , 165 − 3 + 4 ) (2.80^{+0.25}_{-0.21},165^{+4}_{-3}) . Obviously, nonfactorization annihilation parameter X A i X_{A}^{i} is generally unequal to factorization annihilation parameter X A f X_{A}^{f} , which differ from the traditional treatment. With the fitted parameters, all results for observables of B u , d , s B_{u,d,s} → \to π ​ π {\pi}{\pi} , π ​ K {\pi}K , K ​ K KK decays are in good agreement with experimental data.

In summary, we studied the nonfactorizable and factorizable
annihilation contributions to

\to

,

\pi K
,
KK

To clarify the independence of annihilation parameters

and
X_{A}^{f}
and the possible flavor symmetry breaking effects
therein, a statistical
\chi^{2}

B_{u,d}
and

It is found that (1)
X_{A}^{i}
and
X_{A}^{f}
are independent parameters,
which differs from the traditional treatment with annihilation parameters
and verifies the proposal of Ref.
zhu2
.
(2) The flavor symmetry breaking effects might be small for nonleptonic

B_{u,d}
and
B_{s}
decays by now due to the large experimental
errors and theoretical uncertainties.
With the simplifications
X_{A,s}^{i}

=

X_{A,d}^{i}
and

=

X_{A,d}^{f}

measurements on
B_{u,d,s}

\to

\pi\pi
,
\pi K
,

,

,
