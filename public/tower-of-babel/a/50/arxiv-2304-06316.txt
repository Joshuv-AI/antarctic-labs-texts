Asymptotics for k k -crank of k k -colored partitions


Abstract
In this paper, we obtain asymptotic formulas for k k -crank of k k -colored partitions. Let M k ​ ( a , c , n ) M_{k}(a,c;n) denote the number of k k -colored partitions of n n with a k k -crank congruent to a a mod c c . For the cases k = 2 , 3 , 4 k=2,3,4 , Fu and Tang derived several inequality relations for M k ​ ( a , c , n ) M_{k}(a,c;n) using generating functions. We employ the Hardy-Ramanujan Circle Method to extend the results of Fu and Tang. Furthermore, additional inequality relations for M k ​ ( a , c , n ) M_{k}(a,c;n) have been established, such as logarithmic concavity and logarithmic subadditivity. Introduction

The objective of this paper is to derive an asymptotic formula for the

Let

[

derived an asymptotic formula for

[
10

For further asymptotics and inequalities related to ranks and cranks, see, for example,

Building on earlier research in the domain of ranks and cranks, the exploration of

[
16
]
, the exploration of

-colored partitions has emerged as a fresh area of interest, leading to the introduction of the

Following this, by examining the generating function

\displaystyle\quad\times\frac{2i}{(q_{1})_{\infty}^{k}}\sum_{m\in\mathbb{Z}}\frac{(-1)^{m}e^{-\pi ih^{\prime}\omega_{1}}q_{1}^{\frac{l}{2c_{1}}+\frac{m(m+1)}{2}}}{1-e^{-2\pi ih^{\prime}\omega_{1}}q_{1}^{m+\frac{l}{c_{1}}}},

which completes the proof.
∎

3
Asymptotic formula for
M_{k}(a,c;n)

The following theorem plays a crucial role in proving the asymptotic formula.

Lemma 3.1
.

Let
n,m,p,D\in\mathbb{Z}
with
(D,p)=1
, and
0\leq\sigma_{1}<\sigma_{2}\leq p
. Then there exist constants
C_{1}
and
C_{2}
such that:

(1) We have

\displaystyle|F|:=\left|\sum_{h\pmod{p}^{*}\atop\sigma_{1}\leq Dh^{\prime}\leq\sigma_{2}}\omega_{h,p}^{k}e^{\frac{2\pi i}{p}(-hn+h^{\prime}m)}\right|\leq C_{1}\cdot(24n+k,p)^{\frac{1}{2}}p^{\frac{1}{2}+\varepsilon}.

(3.1)

(2) We have

\displaystyle\left|\frac{\sin\left(\frac{\pi a}{c}\right)}{(-1)^{ap+1}}\sum_{h\pmod{p}^{*}\atop\sigma_{1}\leq Dh^{\prime}\leq\sigma_{2}}\frac{\omega_{h,p}^{k}}{\sin\left(\frac{\pi ah^{\prime}}{c}\right)}e^{-\frac{\pi ia^{2}p_{1}h^{\prime}}{c}}e^{\frac{2\pi i}{p}(-hn+h^{\prime}m)}\right|\leq C_{2}\cdot(24n+k,p)^{\frac{1}{2}}p^{\frac{1}{2}+\varepsilon}.

(3.2)

The constants
C_{1}
and
C_{2}
are independent of
a
and
p
.

Proof.
According to the calculations of Andrews
[
2
, p. 482]
and defining
\theta:=(h,24)
, we have

\displaystyle\omega_{h,p}^{k}=(-i)^{k}\left(\frac{-p}{h}\right)^{k}\exp\left(2k\pi i\frac{(4p^{2}+3p-1)h-(p^{2}-1)h^{\prime}}{24p}\right),

when
p
is even and
hh^{\prime}\equiv-1\pmod{24p/\theta}
.
Additionally,

\displaystyle\omega_{h,p}^{k}=\exp\left(\frac{-k\pi i(p-1)}{4}\right)\left(\frac{-h}{p}\right)^{k}\exp\left(2k\pi i\frac{(p^{2}-1)h-(p^{2}-1)h^{\prime}}{24p}\right)

when
p
is odd and
hh^{\prime}\equiv-1\pmod{24p/\theta}
.

Hence,
F
can be expressed as:

\displaystyle\sum_{h\pmod{p}^{*}\atop\sigma_{1}\leq Dh^{\prime}\leq\sigma_{2}}(-i)^{k}\left(\frac{-p}{h}\right)^{k}\exp\left(2\pi i\frac{\left((4p^{2}+3p-1)k-24n\right)h-\left((p^{2}-1)k-24m\right)h^{\prime}}{24p}\right)

when
p
is even and
hh^{\prime}\equiv-1\pmod{24p/\theta}
.
For odd
p
and
hh^{\prime}\equiv-1\pmod{24p/\theta}
, it can be written as:

\displaystyle\sum_{h\pmod{p}^{*}\atop\sigma_{1}\leq Dh^{\prime}\leq\sigma_{2}}\exp\left(\frac{-k\pi i(p-1)}{4}\right)

\displaystyle\left(\frac{-h}{p}\right)^{k}

\displaystyle\times\exp\left(2\pi i\frac{\left((p^{2}-1)k-24n\right)h-\left((p^{2}-1)k-24m\right)h^{\prime}}{24p}\right).

We have

\displaystyle|F|=O\left(\sum_{h\pmod{p}^{*}\atop\sigma_{1}\leq Dh^{\prime}\leq\sigma_{2}}\exp\left(2\pi i\frac{\left((4p^{2}+3p-1)k-24n\right)h-\left((p^{2}-1)k-24m\right)h^{\prime}}{24p}\right)\right)

for even
p
.
When
p
is odd,

\displaystyle|F|=O\left(\sum_{h\pmod{p}^{*}\atop\sigma_{1}\leq Dh^{\prime}\leq\sigma_{2}}\exp\left(2\pi i\frac{\left((p^{2}-1)k-24n\right)h-\left((p^{2}-1)k-24m\right)h^{\prime}}{24p}\right)\right)

By utilizing an estimate of Sali’e
[
28
, eq. (5)]
, we have

\displaystyle|F|

\displaystyle=\begin{cases}O\left(p^{\frac{1}{2}+\varepsilon}\left((4p^{2}+3p-1)k-24n,24p\right)\right),&p~\text{even},\\[5.0pt]
O\left(p^{\frac{1}{2}+\varepsilon}\left((p^{2}-1)k-24n,24p\right)\right),&p~\text{odd},\end{cases}

\displaystyle=\begin{cases}O\left(p^{\frac{1}{2}+\varepsilon}\left(24n+k,p\right)\right),&p~\text{even},\\[5.0pt]
O\left(p^{\frac{1}{2}+\varepsilon}\left(24n+k,p\right)\right),&p~\text{odd}.\end{cases}

We have verified that (
3.2
) holds. We define
\overline{c}:=c
if
p
is odd and
\overline{c}:=2c
if
p
is even. It is clear that
\frac{e^{-\frac{\pi ia^{2}p_{1}h^{\prime}}{c}}}{\sin\left(\frac{\pi ah^{\prime}}{c}\right)}
depends only on the residue class of
h^{\prime}(\text{mod}~\overline{c})
. We can then express (
3.2
) as:

\frac{\sin\left(\frac{\pi a}{c}\right)}{(-1)^{ap+1}}\sum_{c_{j}}\frac{e^{-\frac{\pi ia^{2}p_{1}h^{\prime}}{c}}}{\sin\left(\frac{\pi ah^{\prime}}{c}\right)}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}h\pmod{p}^{*}\\
\sigma_{1}\leq Dh^{\prime}\leq\sigma_{2}\\
h^{\prime}\equiv c_{j}~(\text{mod}~\overline{c})\end{subarray}}\omega_{h,p}^{k}e^{\frac{2\pi i}{p}(-hn+h^{\prime}m)},

where
c_{j}
runs through a set of primitive residues
(\text{mod}~\overline{c})
. The inner sum can be rewritten as

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\overline{c}}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}h\pmod{p}^{*}\\
\sigma_{1}\leq Dh^{\prime}\leq\sigma_{2}\end{subarray}}\omega_{h,p}^{k}e^{\frac{2\pi i}{p}(-hn+h^{\prime}m)}

\displaystyle\sum_{r~~(\text{mod}~\overline{c})}e^{\frac{2\pi ir}{\overline{c}}(h^{\prime}-c_{j})}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\overline{c}}\sum_{r~~(\text{mod}~\overline{c})}e^{-\frac{2\pi irc_{j}}{\overline{c}}}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}h\pmod{p}^{*}\\
\sigma_{1}\leq Dh^{\prime}\leq\sigma_{2}\end{subarray}}\omega_{h,p}^{k}e^{\frac{2\pi i}{p}(-hn+h^{\prime}(m+\frac{pr}{\overline{c}}))}.

Applying (
3.1
) effortlessly yields (
3.2
), thus completing the proof.
∎

Consider the expression

C_{k}\left(e^{\frac{2\pi ia}{c}};q\right)=1+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}A_{k}\left(\frac{a}{c};n\right)q^{n}.

We derive the subsequent asymptotic formulas for the coefficient
A_{k}\left(\frac{a}{c};n\right)
.

Proposition 3.2
.

Given
\varepsilon>0
, suppose
0<a<c
are coprime integers,
c
is odd, and
n
is a positive integer. Then, for
k\leq 12
, we obtain

\displaystyle A_{k}\left(\frac{a}{c};n\right)

\displaystyle=2\pi i\left(\frac{k}{24n-k}\right)^{\frac{k}{4}}\sum_{1\leq p\leq N\atop c\mid p}\frac{B_{a,c,p,k}(-n,0)}{p}I_{\frac{k}{2}}\left(\frac{\pi\sqrt{k(24n-k)}}{6p}\right)+\frac{4\cdot 24^{\frac{k}{4}}\pi\sin\left(\frac{\pi a}{c}\right)}{\left(24n-k\right)^{\frac{k}{4}}}

\displaystyle\quad\times\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}p,r\\
c\nmid p\\
\delta_{a,c,p,r,k}^{j}>0\\
j\in\{+,-\}\end{subarray}}\frac{\left(\delta_{a,c,p,r,k}^{j}\right)^{\frac{k}{4}}D_{a,c,p,k}(-n,m_{a,c,p,r}^{j})}{p}I_{\frac{k}{2}}\left(\frac{\pi}{p}\sqrt{\frac{2\delta_{a,c,p,r,k}^{j}(24n-k)}{3}}\right)+O(n^{\varepsilon}).

Proof.
Applying Cauchy’s theorem, we obtain for
n>0

A_{k}\left(\frac{a}{c};n\right)=\frac{1}{2\pi i}\int_{\mathcal{C}}\frac{C_{k}\left(e^{\frac{2\pi ia}{c}};q\right)}{q^{n+1}}dq,

where
\mathcal{C}
represents an arbitrary path inside the unit circle encircling
0
counterclockwise. Choosing a circle with radius
e^{-\frac{2\pi}{n}}
and parametrization
q=e^{-\frac{2\pi}{n}+2\pi it}
for
0\leq t\leq 1
, we get

A_{k}\left(\frac{a}{c};n\right)=\int_{0}^{1}C_{k}\left(e^{\frac{2\pi ia}{c}};e^{-\frac{2\pi}{n}+2\pi it}\right)e^{2\pi-2\pi int}dt.

We define

\vartheta^{\prime}_{h,p}:=\frac{1}{p\left(\widetilde{p}_{1}+p\right)},~~~~~\vartheta^{\prime\prime}_{h,p}:=\frac{1}{p\left(\widetilde{p}_{2}+p\right)},

where
\frac{h_{1}}{\widetilde{p}_{1}}<\frac{h}{p}<\frac{h_{2}}{\widetilde{p}_{2}}
are adjacent Farey fractions in the Farey sequence of order
N:=\lfloor n^{1/2}\rfloor
.
From the theory of Farey fractions, we know that

\frac{1}{p+\widetilde{p}_{j}}\leq\frac{1}{N+1}~~~~(j=1,2).

Now, we decompose the path of integration along Farey arcs
-\vartheta^{\prime}_{h,p}\leq\Phi\leq\vartheta^{\prime\prime}_{h,p}
, where
\Phi=t-\frac{h}{p}
and
0\leq h<p\leq N
with
(h,p)=1
. From this decomposition of the path we can rewrite the integral along these arcs:

A_{k}\left(\frac{a}{c};n\right)=\sum_{h,p}e^{-\frac{2\pi ihn}{p}}\int_{-\vartheta^{\prime}_{h,p}}^{\vartheta^{\prime\prime}_{h,p}}C_{k}\left(e^{\frac{2\pi ia}{c}};e^{\frac{2\pi i}{p}(h+iz)}\right)e^{\frac{2\pi nz}{p}}d\Phi,

where
z=\frac{p}{n}-p\Phi i
.
We insert our transformation formula from Proposition
2.1
into the integral and obtain

\displaystyle A_{k}\left(\frac{a}{c};n\right)

\displaystyle=i\sin\left(\frac{\pi a}{c}\right)\sum_{h,p\atop c\mid p}\omega_{h,p}^{k}\frac{(-1)^{ap+1}}{\sin\left(\frac{\pi ah^{\prime}}{c}\right)}e^{-\frac{\pi ia^{2}p_{1}h^{\prime}}{c}-\frac{2\pi ihn}{p}}

\displaystyle\quad\times\int_{-\vartheta^{\prime}_{h,p}}^{\vartheta^{\prime\prime}_{h,p}}z^{\frac{k}{2}-1}e^{\frac{2\pi z}{p}\left(n-\frac{k}{24}\right)+\frac{k\pi}{12pz}}C_{k}\left(e^{\frac{2\pi iah^{\prime}}{c}};q_{1}\right)d\Phi

\displaystyle\quad-4i\sin\left(\frac{\pi a}{c}\right)\sum_{h,p\atop c\nmid p}\omega_{h,p}^{k}(-1)^{ap+l}e^{-\frac{\pi ia^{2}p_{1}h^{\prime}}{cc_{1}}+\frac{2\pi ialh^{\prime}}{cc_{1}}-\frac{2\pi ihn}{p}}

\displaystyle\quad\times\int_{-\vartheta^{\prime}_{h,p}}^{\vartheta^{\prime\prime}_{h,p}}z^{\frac{k}{2}-1}e^{\frac{2\pi z}{p}\left(n-\frac{k}{24}\right)+\frac{k\pi}{12pz}}q_{1}^{-\frac{l^{2}}{2c_{1}^{2}}}C_{k}\left(ah^{\prime},\frac{lc}{c_{1}},c;q_{1}\right),

and we call them
\Sigma_{1}
and
\Sigma_{2}
respectively.
To estimate
\Sigma_{1}
, we first consider the principal part of
C_{k}\left(e^{\frac{2\pi iah^{\prime}}{c}};q_{1}\right)
with respect to the
q_{1}
variable. Using (
1.1
), we obtain

\displaystyle C_{k}\left(e^{\frac{2\pi iah^{\prime}}{c}};q_{1}\right)

\displaystyle=1+\sum_{r=1}^{\infty}\sum_{m=-\infty}^{\infty}M_{k}(m,r)e^{\frac{2\pi iah^{\prime}m}{c}}q_{1}^{r}

\displaystyle=1+\sum_{r=1}^{\infty}\sum_{s~~(\text{mod}~c)}\sum_{t=-\infty}^{\infty}M_{k}(s+tc,r)e^{\frac{2\pi iah^{\prime}m}{c}}q_{1}^{r}.

Letting

a_{k}(r,s):=\sum_{t=-\infty}^{\infty}M_{k}(s+tc,r),

we obtain

\displaystyle C_{k}\left(e^{\frac{2\pi iah^{\prime}}{c}};q_{1}\right)=1+\sum_{r=1}^{\infty}\sum_{s~~(\text{mod}~c)}a_{k}(r,s)e^{\frac{2\pi ih^{\prime}}{c}m_{r,s}}q_{1}^{r},

(3.3)

where
m_{r,s}\in\mathbb{Z}
and

\sum_{s~(\text{mod}~c)}a_{k}(r,s)=p_{k}(r)

for
r\geq 1
.
Therefore,
\Sigma_{1}
part can be expressed as

\Sigma_{1}=S_{1}+S_{2}.

Here

\displaystyle S_{1}=i\sin\left(\frac{\pi a}{c}\right)\sum_{h,p\atop c\mid p}\omega_{h,p}^{k}\frac{(-1)^{ap+1}}{\sin\left(\frac{\pi ah^{\prime}}{c}\right)}e^{-\frac{\pi ia^{2}p_{1}h^{\prime}}{c}-\frac{2\pi ihn}{p}}\int_{-\vartheta^{\prime}_{h,p}}^{\vartheta^{\prime\prime}_{h,p}}z^{\frac{k}{2}-1}e^{\frac{2\pi z}{p}\left(n-\frac{k}{24}\right)+\frac{k\pi}{12pz}}d\Phi

(3.4)

and

\displaystyle S_{2}=

\displaystyle i\sin\left(\frac{\pi a}{c}\right)\sum_{h,p\atop c\mid p}\omega_{h,p}^{k}\frac{(-1)^{ap+1}}{\sin\left(\frac{\pi ah^{\prime}}{c}\right)}e^{-\frac{\pi ia^{2}p_{1}h^{\prime}}{c}-\frac{2\pi ihn}{p}}

\displaystyle\times\int_{-\vartheta^{\prime}_{h,p}}^{\vartheta^{\prime\prime}_{h,p}}z^{\frac{k}{2}-1}e^{\frac{2\pi z}{p}\left(n-\frac{k}{24}\right)+\frac{k\pi}{12pz}}\sum_{r=1}^{\infty}p_{k}(r)e^{\frac{2\pi ih^{\prime}}{c}m_{r,s}}q_{1}^{r}d\Phi.

(3.5)

In order to bound
S_{2}
, we split the integral in the following way

\int_{-\vartheta^{\prime}_{h,p}}^{\vartheta^{\prime\prime}_{h,p}}=\int_{-\frac{1}{p(N+p)}}^{\frac{1}{p(N+p)}}+\int_{-\frac{1}{p(\widetilde{p}_{1}+p)}}^{-\frac{1}{p(N+p)}}+\int_{\frac{1}{p(N+p)}}^{\frac{1}{p(\widetilde{p}_{2}+p)}},

Consequently,
S_{2}
can be decomposed into three integrals:

S_{2}=S_{21}+S_{22}+S_{23}.

For example,

\displaystyle S_{21}=

\displaystyle i\sin\left(\frac{\pi a}{c}\right)\sum_{h,p\atop c\mid p}\omega_{h,p}^{k}\frac{(-1)^{ap+1}}{\sin\left(\frac{\pi ah^{\prime}}{c}\right)}e^{-\frac{\pi ia^{2}p_{1}h^{\prime}}{c}-\frac{2\pi ihn}{p}}

\displaystyle\times\int_{-\frac{1}{p(N+p)}}^{\frac{1}{p(N+p)}}z^{\frac{k}{2}-1}e^{\frac{2\pi z}{p}\left(n-\frac{k}{24}\right)+\frac{k\pi}{12pz}}\sum_{r=1}^{\infty}p_{k}(r)e^{\frac{2\pi ih^{\prime}}{c}m_{r,s}}q_{1}^{r}d\Phi.

One can easily verify that
\frac{p}{n}\leq|z|\leq\sqrt{\frac{2}{n}}
, hence

|z|^{\frac{k}{2}-1}\leq\begin{cases}p^{-\frac{1}{2}}n^{\frac{1}{2}},&k=1,\\
\left(\frac{2}{n}\right)^{\frac{k}{4}-\frac{1}{2}},&2\leq k\leq 12.\end{cases}

Taking the absolute value of
S_{21}
, it is same as the result of Rolon
[
27

for
k=1

|S_{21}|=O(n^{\varepsilon})
. By
[
14

\operatorname{Re}(\frac{1}{z})<p

2\leq k\leq 12

\operatorname{Re}(z)=\frac{p}{n}

\frac{p}{2}\leq\operatorname{Re}(\frac{1}{z})<p
then

\displaystyle\left|S_{21}\right|

\displaystyle\leq\sum_{r=1}^{\infty}\sum_{p\atop c\mid p}p_{k}(r)\left|(-1)^{ap+1}\sin\left(\frac{\pi a}{c}\right)\sum_{h}\frac{\omega_{h,p}^{k}}{\sin\left(\frac{\pi ah^{\prime}}{c}\right)}e^{-\frac{\pi ia^{2}p_{1}h^{\prime}}{c}-\frac{2\pi ihn}{p}+\frac{2\pi ih^{\prime}}{c}m_{r,s}}\right|

\displaystyle\quad\times\left(\frac{2}{n}\right)^{\frac{k}{4}-\frac{1}{2}}e^{2\pi+\frac{k\pi}{12}}e^{-\pi r}\int_{-\frac{1}{p(N+p)}}^{\frac{1}{p(N+p)}}d\Phi.

Applying Lemma
3.1
, we may bound this by

\displaystyle\widehat{C}\sum_{r=1}^{\infty}p_{k}(r)e^{-\pi r}\sum_{p}p^{\varepsilon-\frac{1}{2}}\left(24n+k,p\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}n^{-\frac{k}{4}}

\displaystyle\leq\widehat{C}_{1}\sum_{p}p^{\varepsilon-\frac{1}{2}}\left(24n+k,p\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}n^{-\frac{k}{4}}\leq\widehat{C}_{1}\sum_{p\leq N}p^{\varepsilon-\frac{1}{2}}\sum_{d\mid p\atop d\mid 24n+k}d^{\frac{1}{2}}n^{-\frac{k}{4}}

\displaystyle\leq\widehat{C}_{1}n^{-\frac{k}{4}}\sum_{d\mid 24n+k\atop d\leq N}d^{\frac{1}{2}}\sum_{p\leq N/d}(pd)^{\varepsilon-\frac{1}{2}}\leq\widehat{C}_{2}\sum_{d\mid 24n+k\atop d\leq N}d^{-\frac{1}{2}}N^{\varepsilon+\frac{1}{2}}n^{-\frac{k}{4}}

\displaystyle\leq\widehat{C}_{2}\sum_{d\mid 24n+k\atop d\leq N}d^{-\frac{1}{2}}n^{\frac{1-k}{4}+\frac{\varepsilon}{2}}=O(n^{\varepsilon}),

where
\widehat{C},\widehat{C}_{1}
and
\widehat{C}_{2}
are constants. Here we bound trivially

\sum_{d|24n+k}d^{-\frac{1}{2}}<\sum_{d|24n+k}1=\sigma_{0}(24n+k)=o(n^{\epsilon})

and choose
0<\epsilon<\varepsilon/2
, where
\sigma_{0}(n)
denotes the number of the divisors of
n
and satisfies
\sigma_{0}(n)=o(n^{\epsilon})

[
5
, p.296]
.
Therefore
|S_{21}|=O(n^{\varepsilon})
for
k\leq 12
.

Both
S_{22}
and
S_{23}

S_{22}
. We can rewrite the integral as follows:

\int_{-\frac{1}{p(\widetilde{p}_{1}+p)}}^{-\frac{1}{p(N+p)}}=\sum_{\ell=\widetilde{p}_{1}+p}^{N+p-1}\int_{-\frac{1}{p\ell}}^{-\frac{1}{p(\ell+1)}},

\displaystyle\left|S_{22}\right|

\displaystyle\leq\left|\sum_{r=1}^{\infty}\sum_{p\atop c\mid p}p_{k}(r)\sum_{\ell=\widetilde{p}_{1}+p}^{N+p-1}\int_{-\frac{1}{p\ell}}^{-\frac{1}{p(\ell+1)}}z^{\frac{k}{2}-1}e^{\frac{2\pi z}{p}\left(n-\frac{k}{24}\right)+\frac{k\pi}{12pz}}q_{1}^{r}d\Phi\right.

\displaystyle\quad\left.\times(-1)^{ap+1}\sin\left(\frac{\pi a}{c}\right)\sum_{h}\frac{\omega_{h,p}^{k}}{\sin\left(\frac{\pi ah^{\prime}}{c}\right)}e^{-\frac{\pi ia^{2}p_{1}h^{\prime}}{c}-\frac{2\pi ihn}{p}+\frac{2\pi ih^{\prime}}{c}m_{r,s}}\right|=:A_{k}.

\displaystyle A_{k}

\displaystyle=\left|\sum_{r=1}^{\infty}\sum_{p\atop c\mid p}p_{k}(r)\sum_{\ell=N+1}^{N+p-1}\int_{-\frac{1}{p\ell}}^{-\frac{1}{p(\ell+1)}}z^{\frac{k}{2}-1}e^{\frac{2\pi z}{p}\left(n-\frac{k}{24}\right)+\frac{k\pi}{12pz}}q_{1}^{r}d\Phi\right.

\displaystyle\quad\left.\times(-1)^{ap+1}\sin\left(\frac{\pi a}{c}\right)\sum_{h\atop N<\widetilde{p}_{1}+p\leq\ell}\frac{\omega_{h,p}^{k}}{\sin\left(\frac{\pi ah^{\prime}}{c}\right)}e^{-\frac{\pi ia^{2}p_{1}h^{\prime}}{c}-\frac{2\pi ihn}{p}+\frac{2\pi ih^{\prime}}{c}m_{r,s}}\right|.

Now by the theory of Farey fractions we have

\widetilde{p}_{1}\equiv-h^{\prime}~~(\text{mod}~p),~\widetilde{p}_{2}\equiv h^{\prime}~~(\text{mod}~p),~N-p\leq\widetilde{p}_{i}\leq N,

for
i=1,2
. One can easily calculate that

\sum_{\ell=N+1}^{N+p-1}\int_{-\frac{1}{p\ell}}^{-\frac{1}{p(\ell+1)}}d\Phi\leq\frac{2}{p\sqrt{n}}.

Therefore, by applying Lemma
3.1
, we can bound
S_{22}
in a manner similar to
S_{21}
. As a result, we obtain

We now turn our attention to
S_{1}
.
The integral is split in the following way:

\int_{-\vartheta^{\prime}_{h,p}}^{\vartheta^{\prime\prime}_{h,p}}=\int_{-\frac{1}{pN}}^{\frac{1}{pN}}-\int_{-\frac{1}{pN}}^{-\frac{1}{p(\widetilde{p}_{1}+p)}}-\int_{\frac{1}{p(\widetilde{p}_{2}+p)}}^{\frac{1}{pN}}.

, we write the integral as:

Substituting into

\displaystyle S_{12}=\sum_{p\atop c\mid p}\sum_{\ell=N}^{\widetilde{p}_{1}+p-1}\int_{-\frac{1}{p\ell}}^{-\frac{1}{p(\ell+1)}}z^{\frac{k}{2}-1}e^{\frac{2\pi z}{p}\left(n-\frac{k}{24}\right)+\frac{k\pi}{12pz}}d\Phi\frac{i\sin\left(\frac{\pi a}{c}\right)}{(-1)^{ap+1}}\sum_{h}\frac{\omega_{h,p}^{k}}{\sin\left(\frac{\pi ah^{\prime}}{c}\right)}e^{-\frac{\pi ia^{2}p_{1}h^{\prime}}{c}-\frac{2\pi ihn}{p}}

Now, given the condition

\ell\leq\widetilde{p}1+p-1\leq N+p-1
, which restricts the summation over

\displaystyle|S_{12}|

\displaystyle\leq\sum_{p\atop c\mid p}\sum_{\ell=N}^{N+p-1}\int_{-\frac{1}{p\ell}}^{-\frac{1}{p(\ell+1)}}\left|z^{\frac{k}{2}-1}e^{\frac{2\pi z}{p}\left(n-\frac{k}{24}\right)+\frac{k\pi}{12pz}}\right|d\Phi

\displaystyle\quad\times\left|\sin\left(\frac{\pi a}{c}\right)(-1)^{ap+1}\sum_{h\atop\ell\leq\widetilde{p}_{1}+p-1\leq N+p-1}\frac{\omega_{h,p}^{k}}{\sin\left(\frac{\pi ah^{\prime}}{c}\right)}e^{-\frac{\pi ia^{2}p_{1}h^{\prime}}{c}-\frac{2\pi ihn}{p}}\right|

\displaystyle=O(n^{\varepsilon}).

Similarly, we have
