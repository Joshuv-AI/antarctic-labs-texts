2.
Convex Approximation

Taking into account that

\lambda\geq 1

E_{n}^{(+2)}(f)=\inf\{\|f-P\|_{\infty};P\in{\mathcal{P}}_{n}(\mathbb{R}),P^{\prime\prime}(x)\geq 0,\forall x\in[-1,1]\},

it is natural to consider the following open question.

Open Question 1.

There exists finite the limit

\lim_{n\to\infty}n^{\lambda}E_{n}^{(+2)}(|x|^{\lambda})

\lambda\geq 1

Note that since by
[

E_{n}(f)={\mathcal{O}}(n^{-\lambda})\quad\mbox{ iff }E_{n}^{(+2)}(f)={\mathcal{O}}(n^{-\lambda}),\quad n\to\infty,

it easily follows that the sequence
(n^{\lambda}E_{n}^{(+2)}(|x|^{\lambda}))_{n\in\mathbb{N}}

\lambda\geq 1
