Shalika newforms for GL( n n )
Takeo Okazaki

Abstract
. Let ( π , V ) (\pi,V) be a generic irreducible representation of a general linear group over a p p -adic field. Jacquet, Piatetski-Shapiro, and Shalika gave an open compact subgroup K K , so that the subspace V K V^{K} consisting of v ∈ V v\in V fixed by K K is one-dimensional. If π \pi has a Shalika model Λ \Lambda , then we call vectors in Λ ⁡ ( V ) \Lambda(V) the Shalika forms of π \pi , and those in Λ ⁡ ( V K ) \Lambda(V^{K}) the Shalika newforms. In this article, we give a method to determine all values of the Shalika newforms on the mirabolic subgroup in the case where π \pi is supercuspidal. Using this result, we give another Shalika form with nice properties, which is not fixed by K K in the case where the character defining the Shalika model is ramified.
