In the H 2 ​ ( Be 10 , He 3 ) ​ Li 9 {{}^{\makebox[3.98613pt][r]{$\scriptstyle 2$}}_{\makebox[3.98613pt][r]{$\scriptstyle$}}\mathrm{H}}({{}^{\makebox[7.97226pt][r]{$\scriptstyle 10$}}_{\makebox[7.97226pt][r]{$\scriptstyle$}}\mathrm{Be}},{{}^{\makebox[3.98613pt][r]{$\scriptstyle 3$}}_{\makebox[3.98613pt][r]{$\scriptstyle$}}\mathrm{He}}){{}^{\makebox[3.98613pt][r]{$\scriptstyle 9$}}_{\makebox[3.98613pt][r]{$\scriptstyle$}}\mathrm{Li}} reaction the Li 9 {}^{\makebox[3.98613pt][r]{$\scriptstyle 9$}}_{\makebox[3.98613pt][r]{$\scriptstyle$}}\mathrm{Li} ground-state ( 3 / 2 − 3/2^{-} ) and its first excited state (2.69 MeV, 1 / 2 − 1/2^{-} ) were identified in the low-energy region of its excitation spectrum. The energy spectrum of Li 8 {}^{\makebox[3.98613pt][r]{$\scriptstyle 8$}}_{\makebox[3.98613pt][r]{$\scriptstyle$}}\mathrm{Li} populated in the H 2 ​ ( Be 10 , He 4 ) ​ Li 8 {{}^{\makebox[3.98613pt][r]{$\scriptstyle 2$}}_{\makebox[3.98613pt][r]{$\scriptstyle$}}\mathrm{H}}({{}^{\makebox[7.97226pt][r]{$\scriptstyle 10$}}_{\makebox[7.97226pt][r]{$\scriptstyle$}}\mathrm{Be}},{{}^{\makebox[3.98613pt][r]{$\scriptstyle 4$}}_{\makebox[3.98613pt][r]{$\scriptstyle$}}\mathrm{He}}){{}^{\makebox[3.98613pt][r]{$\scriptstyle 8$}}_{\makebox[3.98613pt][r]{$\scriptstyle$}}\mathrm{Li}} reaction shows the strong peak which corresponds to the excitation of the second excited state of Li 8 {}^{\makebox[3.98613pt][r]{$\scriptstyle 8$}}_{\makebox[3.98613pt][r]{$\scriptstyle$}}\mathrm{Li} (2.25 MeV, 3 + 3^{+} ). Introduction

When conducting multi-parameter experiments dedicated to the
study of neutron drip-line nuclei obtained with very low yield in nuclear
reactions, the choice of reference reaction(s) is an important issue.
Such additional measurements are necessary in order to check:
(i) whether the particle detectors work correctly,
(ii) how well the carried out Monte-Carlo (MC) simulations reflect
the real experimental conditions and
(iii) how accurately are estimated the main characteristics of
the experiment, such as the spectrum calibration and resolution assumed over
the measured excitation energy of the studied nucleus,
the detection efficiency of reaction products, and so on.
All together it can provide a new reliable information
(level scheme, spin–parity data, decay channels etc.)
for the unknown exotic systems.

Recently, the
{}^{\makebox[3.98613pt][r]{$\scriptstyle 7$}}_{\makebox[3.98613pt][r]{$\scriptstyle$}}\mathrm{H}
and
{}^{\makebox[3.98613pt][r]{$\scriptstyle 6$}}_{\makebox[3.98613pt][r]{$\scriptstyle$}}\mathrm{H}

the

).
The absolute values of the cross sections are well predicted by the modern
shell-model calculation with spectroscopic factor SF
\sim\!\!1.7
for the

{{}^{\makebox[7.97226pt][r]{$\scriptstyle 10$}}_{\makebox[7.97226pt][r]{$\scriptstyle$}}\mathrm{Be}}=p+{{}^{\makebox[3.98613pt][r]{$\scriptstyle 9$}}_{\makebox[3.98613pt][r]{$\scriptstyle$}}\mathrm{Li}}_{\text{g.s.}}
configuration.
In the energy spectrum of
{}^{\makebox[3.98613pt][r]{$\scriptstyle 8$}}_{\makebox[3.98613pt][r]{$\scriptstyle$}}\mathrm{Li}
derived from the deuteron pickup reaction

{{}^{\makebox[3.98613pt][r]{$\scriptstyle 2$}}_{\makebox[3.98613pt][r]{$\scriptstyle$}}\mathrm{H}}({{}^{\makebox[7.97226pt][r]{$\scriptstyle 10$}}_{\makebox[7.97226pt][r]{$\scriptstyle$}}\mathrm{Be}},{{}^{\makebox[3.98613pt][r]{$\scriptstyle 4$}}_{\makebox[3.98613pt][r]{$\scriptstyle$}}\mathrm{He}}){{}^{\makebox[3.98613pt][r]{$\scriptstyle 8$}}_{\makebox[3.98613pt][r]{$\scriptstyle$}}\mathrm{Li}}
,
a strong peak attributed to the second excited state of
{}^{\makebox[3.98613pt][r]{$\scriptstyle 8$}}_{\makebox[3.98613pt][r]{$\scriptstyle$}}\mathrm{Li}

(2.25 MeV,
3^{+}
) was observed.
Also, the presence of an unresolved third excited state at 3.21 MeV (
1^{+}
)
is possible in the measured
{}^{\makebox[3.98613pt][r]{$\scriptstyle 8$}}_{\makebox[3.98613pt][r]{$\scriptstyle$}}\mathrm{Li}
spectrum.
The absence of the ground and the first excited states of
{}^{\makebox[3.98613pt][r]{$\scriptstyle 8$}}_{\makebox[3.98613pt][r]{$\scriptstyle$}}\mathrm{Li}
in
the measured spectrum is explained in complete agreement with the theoretical
concepts of the structures of the
{}^{\makebox[7.97226pt][r]{$\scriptstyle 10$}}_{\makebox[7.97226pt][r]{$\scriptstyle$}}\mathrm{Be}
ground state and

{}^{\makebox[3.98613pt][r]{$\scriptstyle 8$}}_{\makebox[3.98613pt][r]{$\scriptstyle$}}\mathrm{Li}
levels at low excitation energy.

In general, these and the previous
results
Bezbakh et al. 2020
;
Muzalevskii
et al. 2021
;
Nikolskii et al. 2022
of
the first experiments carried out with secondary beams at the ACCULINNA-2
facility show a great potential for the studies of light exotic nuclei
being close to and beyond the nuclear drip lines.
