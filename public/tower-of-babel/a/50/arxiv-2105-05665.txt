W − W- exchange contributions in hadronic decays of bottom baryon Λ b \Lambda_{b}
Fayyazuddin

Abstract
The nonleptonic decays Λ b → Σ c ∗ ⁣ − ​ π + , Ξ c ∗ 0 ​ K 0 \Lambda_{b}\rightarrow\Sigma_{c}^{*-}\pi^{+},\Xi_{c}^{*0}K^{0} and Λ b → Δ 0 ​ D 0 , Σ ∗ ⁣ − ​ D 0 + \Lambda_{b}\rightarrow\Delta^{0}D^{0},\Sigma^{*-}D_{0}^{+} are studied. In addition, the decays Λ b → Ξ c 0 ​ K 0 , Σ − ​ D s + \Lambda_{b}\rightarrow\Xi_{c}^{0}K^{0},\Sigma^{-}D_{s}^{+} are analyzed. For all these decays the dominant contribution comes from W − W- exchange, and for the decay Λ b → Λ c + ​ π − \Lambda_{b}\rightarrow\Lambda_{c}^{+}\pi^{-} , in addition to factorization, baryon pole contribution to the p p -wave (parity conserving) decay amplitude B B is discussed.

Another aspect of this paper is the analyze the non-leptonic decays

For the decays
\Lambda_{b}\rightarrow\Xi_{c}^{0}K^{0},\Sigma^{-}D_{s}^{+},

B=B_{\text{pole}}

\Lambda_{b}\rightarrow\Lambda_{c}^{+}\pi^{-},

B=B_{\text{factorization}}+B_{\text{pole}},

B_{\text{pole}}=g_{\Lambda_{c}\pi^{-}\Sigma_{Q}^{+}}\frac{1}{m_{\Sigma_{Q}^{*}}-m_{\Lambda_{c}}}\left\langle\Lambda_{c}^{*}\right|H_{W}^{PC}\left|\Sigma_{Q}^{+}\right\rangle.

For the decay
\Lambda_{b}\rightarrow\Xi_{c}^{0}K^{0}

(29)

and for the decay
\Lambda_{b}\rightarrow\Sigma^{-}D_{s}^{+};

\left\langle\Lambda_{c}^{*}\right|H_{W}^{PC}\left|\Sigma_{b}^{+}\right\rangle=\left\langle\Xi_{c}^{0}\right|H_{W}^{PC}\left|\Xi_{b}^{0\prime}\right\rangle=\left\langle\Sigma^{-}\right|H_{W}^{PC}\left|\Sigma_{b}^{-}\right\rangle=\left[\frac{G_{F}}{\sqrt{2}}V_{cb}V_{ud}\right]\sqrt{6}d^{\prime}
