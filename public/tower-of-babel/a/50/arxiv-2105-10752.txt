Abstract
We construct a symmetric invertible binary pairing function F ⁡ ( m , n ) F(m,n) on the set of positive integers with a property of F ⁡ ( m , n ) = F ⁡ ( n , m ) F(m,n)=F(n,m) . Then we provide a complete proof of its symmetry and bijectivity, from which the construction of symmetric invertible binary pairing functions on any custom set of integers could be seen.

4
Conclusions

In this paper, we indirectly introduce a general method to construct a symmetric binary pairing function mapping

onto
D
, where
D
is a set of integers, illuminated by the construction of Cantor pairing function. Specially we present two pairing functions mapping

onto
\mathbb{N^{+}}
and
\mathbb{N}\times\mathbb{N}
onto
\mathbb{N}
respectively, the former of which has been given a complete demonstration with the technique of set theory. These two pairing functions both have a property of symmetry shown as
F(m,n)=F(n,m)
if denoted by

. They are invertible and the corresponding inverse formulas are given.
