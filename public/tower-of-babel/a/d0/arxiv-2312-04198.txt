Distributed Formation Maneuver Control Using Complex Laplacian
Xu Fang, Lihua Xie

Abstract
This paper studies the problem of distributed formation maneuver control of multi-agent systems via complex Laplacian. We will show how to change the translation, scaling, rotation, and also the shape of formation continuously by only tuning the positions of the leaders in both 2-D and 3-D spaces, where the rotation of formation in 3-D space is realized by changing the yaw angle, pitch angle, and roll angle of formation sequentially. Compared with real-Laplacian-based methods, the first advantage of the proposed complex-Laplacian-based approach is that each follower requires fewer neighbors and lesser communication. The second advantage is that non-convex and non-generic nominal configurations are allowed and the uniqueness of the complex-constraint-based target formation can be guaranteed by the non-collocated nominal agents. The third advantage is that more formation shapes can be realized by only tuning the positions of the leaders. Two simulation examples are given to illustrate the theoretical results.

II-C

Complex Constraint

The distance between nominal agent
i
and nominal agent
j
is denoted by
d_{ij}\!=\!\|r_{j}-r_{i}\|_{2}
.
In a constant nominal formation
(\mathcal{G},r)
, if each nominal follower
i
and its two neighbors
j,k
are not collocated, i.e.,
d_{ij},d_{ik}\neq 0
, the position relationship among each nominal follower
i
and its two neighbors

can be described by a complex constraint, i.e.,

w_{ij}(r_{j}-r_{i})+w_{ik}(r_{k}-r_{i})=0,\ i\in\mathcal{V}_{F},

(2)

where the weights
w_{ij},w_{ik}\in\mathbb{C}
are designed as

w_{ij}=\frac{(r_{j}-r_{i})^{H}}{d_{ij}^{2}},\ w_{ik}=-\frac{(r_{k}-r_{i})^{H}}{d_{ik}^{2}}.

(3)

It yields from (
2
) that

(w_{ij}+w_{ik})r_{i}-w_{ij}r_{j}-w_{ik}r_{k}=0.

(4)

We can aggregate the complex constraint of each nominal follower from (
4
) into a matrix form, i.e.,

W_{f}r=\mathbf{0},

(5)

where
W_{f}\!\in\!\mathbb{C}^{(n\!-\!m)\times n}
,
r=(r_{1}^{H},\cdots,r_{n}^{H})^{H}
, and

\begin{array}[]{ll}&[W_{f}]_{ij}\!=\!\left\{\!\begin{array}[]{lll}\sum\limits_{k\in\mathcal{N}_{i}}w_{ik},&i\in\mathcal{V}_{F},\ j=i,\\
\ \ 0,&i\in\mathcal{V}_{F},\ j\notin\mathcal{N}_{i},\ j\neq i,\\
-w_{ij},&i\in\mathcal{V}_{F},\ j\in\mathcal{N}_{i},\ j\neq i.\\
\end{array}\right.\end{array}

(6)

Based on the leader group
r_{L}
and follower group
r_{F}
in
(\mathcal{G},r)
, (
5
) becomes

W_{fl}r_{L}+W_{f\!f}r_{F}=\mathbf{0},

(7)

where

,
W_{f\!f}\in\mathbb{C}^{(n\!-\!m)\times(n\!-\!m)}
are complex matrices with

. If the complex matrix

is invertible, the follower group

is uniquely determined by the leader group

.

A nominal formation

III-A

Control Protocol of the Leaders

Assumption 1
.

Only the leaders have access to their positions and target positions, and the target velocity of each leader

such that

Only the leader group knows the parameters

in (

aims at reaching its target position

where
\dot{p}_{i}^{*}\!=\!\dot{x}_{i}^{*}\!+\!\dot{y}_{i}^{*}\iota
.
\tanh(\cdot)
is the hyperbolic tangent function in
\mathbb{R}
. (

) is equivalent to

\left\{\!\!\begin{array}[]{lll}\dot{x}_{i}\!=\!-\tanh(x_{i}\!-\!x_{i}^{*})+\dot{x}_{i}^{*},\\
\dot{y}_{i}\!=\!-\tanh(y_{i}\!-\!y_{i}^{*})+\dot{y}_{i}^{*}.\end{array}\right.

(17)

Lemma 2
.

Under Assumption
1
, the leader group achieves their control objective (
13
) under control protocol (
16
).

Proof.

Denote by
e_{i}\!=\!p_{i}\!-\!p^{*}_{i}

the tracking error of leader

. Consider a Lyapunov function

V_{1}=\sum\limits_{i=1}^{m}\frac{1}{2}[(x_{i}\!-\!x_{i}^{*})^{2}+(y_{i}\!-\!y_{i}^{*})^{2}].

(18)

We have

\begin{array}[]{ll}\hskip-5.69046pt\dot{V}_{1}&\!=\!-\sum\limits_{i=1}^{m}[(x_{i}\!-\!x_{i}^{*})\tanh(x_{i}\!-\!x_{i}^{*})\!+\!(y_{i}\!-\!y_{i}^{*})\tanh(y_{i}\!-\!y_{i}^{*})].\end{array}

(19)

Note that
(x_{i}\!-\!x_{i}^{*})\tanh(x_{i}\!-\!x_{i}^{*}),(y_{i}\!-\!y_{i}^{*})\tanh(y_{i}\!-\!y_{i}^{*})>0
if
x_{i}\!-\!x_{i}^{*},y_{i}\!-\!y_{i}^{*}\neq 0
. Thus,

. Then, the conclusion follows.

Proof.

Under Assumption

, the complex matrix

It yields from (

The controller of each follower in (

\begin{array}[]{ll}&\digamma\!(p_{i})\!=\!\left\{\!\begin{array}[]{lll}\frac{p_{i}}{\|p_{i}\|_{2}},&p_{i}\neq 0,\\
0,&p_{i}=0.\end{array}\right.\end{array}

Then, the control protocol of each follower

is designed as

v_{i}\!=\!\gamma_{{ijk}}+\hskip-8.5359pt\sum\limits_{i,l\in\mathcal{N}_{g},g\in\mathcal{V}_{f}}\hskip-14.22636pt\gamma_{{gil}}+\alpha_{2}\digamma\!\!\left(\gamma_{{ijk}}+\hskip-8.5359pt\sum\limits_{i,l\in\mathcal{N}_{g},g\in\mathcal{V}_{f}}\hskip-14.22636pt\gamma_{{gil}}\right),

where

\alpha_{2}\in\mathbb{R}
is the control gain and

\begin{array}[]{ll}&\gamma_{ijk}\!=\!(w_{ij}\!+\!w_{ik})^{H}[w_{ij}(p_{j}\!-\!p_{i})\!+\!w_{ik}(p_{k}\!-\!p_{i})],\ j,k\in\mathcal{N}_{i},\\
&\gamma_{gil}=w_{gi}^{H}w_{gi}(p_{g}\!-\!p_{i})\!+\!w_{gi}^{H}w_{gs}(p_{g}\!-\!p_{l}).\end{array}

(28)

To implement distributed localization protocol (

Remark 1

For follower

If follower

VII
Conclusion

This work studies the distributed formation maneuver control via complex Laplacian in both 2-D and 3-D spaces. The translation, scaling, rotation, and also the shape of formation can be changed continuously by only tuning the positions of the leaders, where the followers have no information of the time-varying target formation.
The proposed distributed control protocol can drive the agents to their target positions. In addition, the inter-agent collision can be avoided by tuning the time-varying maneuver parameters.
