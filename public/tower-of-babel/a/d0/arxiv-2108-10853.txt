Holographic drag force in non-conformal plasma
Tolga Domurcukgul

Abstract
In this study, the gauge/string duality is used to investigate the dynamics of a moving heavy quark in a strongly coupled, non-conformal plasma. The drag force in this non-conformal model is smaller than that of 𝒩 = 4 \mathcal{N}=4 supersymmetric Yang-Mills (SYM) plasma and decreases as the level of non-conformality is increased. At intermediate temperatures, the world-sheet temperature, which is derived numerically by calculating the world-sheet horizon, deviates from its conformal value, but at high temperatures, it tends to its conformal value.

2
Non-conformal holographic model

The action of 5-dimensional Einstein gravity coupled to a scalar field is given by

\displaystyle S=\frac{2}{\kappa_{5}^{2}}\int d^{5}x\sqrt{-g}\left[\frac{1}{4}\mathcal{R}-\frac{1}{2}\left(\nabla\phi\right)^{2}-V(\phi)\right]\,,

(1)

where
\kappa_{5}
is the five-dimensional Newton constant and
V(\phi)
is a potential encoding the details of the dual gauge theory. In
Attems:2016ugt
, the following potential has been considered

\displaystyle L^{2}V=-3-\frac{3}{2}\phi^{2}-\frac{1}{3}\phi^{4}+\left(\frac{1}{3\phi_{M}^{2}}+\frac{1}{2\phi_{M}^{4}}\right)\phi^{6}-\frac{1}{12\phi_{M}^{4}}\phi^{8}\,.

(2)

which is characterized by a single parameter,
\phi_{M}
. This non-trivial relatively simple potential has a maximum at
\phi=0
and a minimum at
\phi=\phi_{M}
corresponding to two AdS solutions at UV and IR fixed points. The radii of these two solutions are related as

L_{\rm IR}=\frac{1}{1+\frac{1}{6}\phi_{M}^{2}}L\,.

(3)

Since
L_{\rm IR}<L
, the number of degrees of freedom is smaller at IR.

By parametrizing the vacuum metric as

ds^{2}=e^{2A(r)}\left(-dt^{2}+d{\bf x}^{2}\right)+dr^{2}\,,

(4)

the vacuum solution can be analytically obtained for arbitrary
\phi_{M}

\displaystyle e^{2A}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{\Lambda^{2}L^{2}}{\phi^{2}}\,\left(1-\frac{\phi^{2}}{\phi_{M}^{2}}\right)^{\frac{\phi_{M}^{2}}{6}+1}\,e^{-\frac{\phi^{2}}{6}}\,,

(5)

\displaystyle\phi(r)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{\Lambda L\,e^{-r/L}}{\sqrt{1+\frac{\Lambda^{2}L^{2}}{\phi_{M}^{2}}e^{-2r/L}}}\,.

(6)

It is shown that the arbitrary parameter
\Lambda
is responsible for breaking the conformal invariance explicitly
Attems:2016ugt
.

The black brane solution of action
1
can be calculated numerically by the following ansatz in the Eddington-Finkelstein coordinate

ds^{2}=e^{2A}\left(-h(\phi)d\tilde{t}\,^{2}+d{\bf x}^{2}\right)-2e^{A+B}L\,d\tilde{t}\,d\phi\,,

(7)

where
\phi
considered being the radial coordinate such that the boundary and the horizon are located at
\phi=0
and
\phi=\phi_{H}
, respectively. In fact, the value of the scalar field at the horizon,
\phi_{H}
characterizes the black-brane solution and its thermodynamic properties explicitly. The dual gauge theory is conformal both at the UV and at the IR. Therefore the high and low temperature behavior of thermodynamical variables must match with the relativistic conformal theory. The entropy ratio to its conformal value,
s/s_{con}

\phi_{M}

1(b)

(L_{IR}/L)^{3}
at low temperatures. The speed of sound reaches its conformal value both at high and low temperatures while deviating from its conformal value at intermediate temperatures, which can be interpreted as a measure of the non-conformality of the gauge theory. The detail of the numerical procedure is presented in
Attems:2016ugt

3
Classical trailing string

This section studied a heavy quark moving through an infinite volume of quark-gluon plasma with a fixed velocity
v
at a finite temperature
T
. The heavy quark feels a drag force and consequently losses energy. In the dual theory, the dynamic of this quark can be described by an open string whose endpoint is attached to the UV boundary of AdS space and moves at the constant speed of
v
with a string tail into the AdS bulk
Herzog:2006gh
;
Gubser:2006bz
.

The dynamics of the string is described by the Nambu-Goto action

S_{NG}=\,{1\over 2\pi\alpha^{\prime}}\int d\sigma d\tau\,\sqrt{-\det\gamma_{\alpha\beta}},

(8)

where
\alpha^{\prime}=l_{s}^{2}
is the square of string’s fundamental length and
\gamma_{\alpha\beta}
is the induced metric of the world-sheet defined as

\gamma_{\alpha\beta}=\,G_{MN}\,\partial_{\alpha}X^{M}\,\partial_{\beta}X^{M}\,,

(9)

where
G_{MN}
are the metric component of Eq. (
7
) in the Poincare coordinate

G_{tt}(\phi)=-\,h(\phi)\,e^{2A(\phi)}\,\,\,,G_{xx}(\phi)=e^{2A(\phi)}\,\,\,,G_{\phi\phi}(\phi)=\frac{L^{2}}{h(\phi)}e^{2B(\phi)}\,.

(10)

Henceforward we assume
L=1
for simplicity. By considering the following ansatz for the string embedding function in the static gauge
(\tau=t\,,\sigma=\phi)

X^{M}(\tau,\sigma)=(t,x=vt+\xi(\phi),0,0,\phi)\,,

(11)

the induced metric on the world-sheet becomes

\gamma_{\alpha\beta}=e^{2A}\left(\begin{array}[]{cc}-(h-v^{2})&v\,\xi^{\prime}\\
v\,\xi^{\prime}&\frac{e^{2(B-A)}}{h}+\xi^{\prime 2}\\
\end{array}\right)\,,

(12)

leads to the corresponding Lagrangian

\mathcal{L}=-\frac{e^{A(\phi)}}{2\pi\alpha^{\prime}}\sqrt{e^{2A(\phi)}h(\phi)\xi^{\prime}(\phi)^{2}-\frac{v^{2}e^{2B(\phi)}}{h(\phi)}+e^{2B(\phi)}}\,.

(13)

Since the Lagrangian does not depend on the
\xi
explicitly, the string equation of motion simplifies as

\frac{1}{2\pi\alpha^{\prime}}\frac{e^{2A(\phi)}h(\phi)\xi^{\prime}(\phi)}{\sqrt{e^{2(B(\phi)-A(\phi))}-v^{2}\,\frac{e^{2(B(\phi)-A(\phi))}}{h(\phi)}+h(\phi)\xi^{\prime}(\phi)^{2}}}\,=\,const\equiv\pi_{\xi}\,,

(14)

where
\pi_{\xi}
is the constant of motion. From above, the equation for
\xi^{\prime}
can be obtained as

\xi^{\prime}(\phi)=\pm\frac{2\pi\alpha^{\prime}\pi_{\xi}e^{B(\phi)-A(\phi)}}{h(\phi)}\,\sqrt{\frac{h(\phi)-v^{2}}{e^{4A(\phi)}h(\phi)-\left(2\pi\alpha^{\prime}\pi_{\xi}\right)^{2}}}\,.

(15)

The numerator and denominator inside the square root are positive near the boundary and negative around the horizon. Requiring the real values for the string profile,
\xi^{\prime}
means that the numerator and the denominator have to change their sign at the same point denoted by
\phi_{s}

\displaystyle h(\phi_{s})-v^{2}=0\,,

\displaystyle e^{4A(\phi_{s})}h(\phi_{s})-\left(2\pi\alpha^{\prime}\pi_{\xi}\right)^{2}=0\,.

(16)

From the above equations, the constant of the equation of motion can be determined as

\displaystyle\pi_{\xi}=\frac{1}{2\pi\alpha^{\prime}}\,v\,e^{2A(\phi_{s})}\,.

(17)

Substituting the
\pi_{\xi}
into Eq. (
15
) yields to

\xi^{\prime}(\phi)=\pm\frac{ve^{2A(\phi_{s})}e^{B(\phi)-A(\phi)}}{h(\phi)}\,\sqrt{\frac{h(\phi)-h(\phi_{s})}{e^{4A(\phi)}h(\phi)-e^{4A(\phi_{s})}h(\phi_{s})}}\,.

(18)

The square root is now well-defined for the region outside the horizon
(0<\phi<\phi_{H})
, and this equation can be solved numerically to obtain the string profile.

3.1
Drag force

The drag force, which indicates the dissipation of quark momentum into the plasma, is given by

F_{drag}=-\pi^{1}_{x}\,,

(19)

where
\pi^{1}_{x}
is the momentum that is lost by flowing from the string to the horizon.
The canonical momentum densities associated with the string can be obtained from varying the action with respect to the derivatives of the embedding functions

\displaystyle\left(\begin{array}[]{c}\pi^{0}_{t}\\
\pi^{0}_{x}\end{array}\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2\pi\alpha^{\prime}}\frac{e^{4A(\phi)}}{\sqrt{-\det\gamma_{\alpha\beta}}}\,\left(\begin{array}[]{c}-e^{2\left(B(\phi)-A(\phi)\right)}-h(\phi)\,\xi^{\prime}(\phi)^{2}\\
e^{2\left(B(\phi)-A(\phi)\right)}\,v\,/\,h(\phi)\end{array}\right),

\displaystyle\left(\begin{array}[]{c}\pi^{1}_{t}\\
\pi^{1}_{x}\end{array}\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2\pi\alpha^{\prime}}\frac{e^{4A(\phi)}}{\sqrt{-\det\gamma_{\alpha\beta}}}\,\left(\begin{array}[]{c}v\,\xi^{\prime}(\phi)\,h(\phi)\\
-\xi^{\prime}(\phi)\,h(\phi)\end{array}\right).

Integrating the
\pi^{0}_{t}
and
\pi^{0}_{x}
along the string gives us the total energy and the total momentum in the direction of motion of the string, respectively. While,
\pi^{1}_{t}
and
\pi^{1}_{x}
are the energy and momentum flow along the string and similar to the case of
\mathcal{N}=4
SYM plasma,
\pi^{1}_{t}=-v\,\pi^{1}_{x}
.
By substituting the string solution, Eq. (
18
) in the Eq. (
3.1
), it is evident that the
\pi^{1}_{x}
equals the constant of the equation of motion,
\pi_{\xi}
. This means that if we pull the quark with a constant velocity, the fraction of energy flows at a given point along the string,
\pi^{1}_{t}
is constant. Using the Eq. (
17
), and Eq. (
19
) the drag force is

F_{drag}=-\frac{1}{2\pi\alpha^{\prime}}\,v\,e^{2A(\phi_{s})}\,,

(30)

which is the momentum flow along the string at point
\phi_{s}
. If we use the metric of AdS-Sch, the drag force for
\mathcal{N}=4
SYM plasma can be obtained as
Herzog:2006gh
;
Gubser:2006bz
;
CasalderreySolana:2006rq

F_{drag}^{SYM}=-\frac{\pi\,T^{2}\,\sqrt{\lambda}}{2}\frac{v}{\sqrt{1-v^{2}}}\,,

(31)

where we have used the relation
L^{4}=\lambda\,\alpha^{\prime 2}
. In the case of non-conformal background, the first equation of Eq. (
3

\phi_{s}
and then the drag force can be calculated using Eq. (
30

\mathcal{N}=4

World-sheet temperature

One can reparametrize the world-sheet coordinate, Eq. (
11
) as follows

\displaystyle\tau

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle t+K(\phi)\,,

\displaystyle x

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle vt+vK(\phi)+\xi(\phi)\,.

(32)

Therefore, the background induced metric, Eq. (
12
) becomes

\gamma_{\alpha\beta}=e^{2A}\left(\begin{array}[]{cc}-(h-v^{2})&v\,(\xi^{\prime}+v\,K^{\prime})-h\,K^{\prime}\\
v\,(\xi^{\prime}+v\,K^{\prime})-h\,K^{\prime}&\,\,\,\frac{e^{2(B-A)}}{h}+(\xi^{\prime}+v\,K^{\prime})^{2}-h\,K^{\prime 2}\\
\end{array}\right)\,.

(33)

By choosing a particular ansatz of the form of

\displaystyle K^{\prime}(\phi)=\frac{v\,\xi^{\prime}}{h-v^{2}}\,,

(34)

and using Eq. (
17
), and Eq. (
18
), the induced metric diagonalizes as

\gamma_{\alpha\beta}=e^{2A(\phi)}\left(\begin{array}[]{cc}-(h(\phi)-v^{2})&0\\
0&\frac{e^{2(B(\phi)+A(\phi))}}{e^{4A(\phi)}\,h(\phi)-e^{4A(\phi_{s})}\,v^{2}}\\
\end{array}\right)\,,

(35)

which describe the metric of a two-dimensional world-sheet blackhole with a horizon radius of
\phi_{s}
. The Hawking temperature of the world-sheet blackhole indicated as
T_{wsh}
is

\displaystyle T_{wsh}=\frac{1}{4\pi}\sqrt{e^{2A(\phi_{s})-2B(\phi_{s})}\,h^{\prime}(\phi_{s})(4\,v^{2}\,A^{\prime}(\phi_{s})+h^{\prime}(\phi_{s}))}\,.

In the conformal limit, AdS-Schwarzschild metric, the world-sheet temperature depends on the blackhole temperature and the velocity of quark as

\displaystyle T_{wsh}^{conf}=\frac{T}{\sqrt{\gamma}}\,.

(37)

Summary

This paper studied the dynamics of a heavy quark moving through a strongly coupled plasma with broken conformal symmetry using the gauge/gravity duality.
We considered a holographic five-dimensional model consisting of Einstein gravity coupled to a scalar field with a non-trivial potential corresponding to a dual four-dimensional non-conformal gauge theory that exhibits a renormalization group flow between two different types of fixed points (located at UV and IR) at zero temperature
Attems:2016ugt
. The parameter
\phi_{M}
indicates the deviation from conformality, as shown in Fig.
1
. According to the AdS/CFT dictionary, the heavy quark is associated with a string attached to the boundary of the AdS space. The string equation of motion was solved numerically for different values of parameter
\phi_{M}
, and then the drag force was computed by obtaining the corresponding conjugate momenta. Our results indicate that the drag force in this non-conformal plasma is smaller than
\mathcal{N}=4
super-Yang-Mills plasma. By increasing the level of non-conformality (the value of parameter
\phi_{M}
), the drag force decreases, see Fig.
2
. The ratio of drag force to its conformal limit reaches one at high temperatures. At finite temperature, this ratio is smaller than one for different values of
\phi_{M}
but increases slowly by increasing the velocity of quark, see Fig.
3
.

The world-sheet horizon was also calculated, and it was shown that the drag force is equivalent to the energy flow at the world-sheet horizon. The corresponding world-sheet temperature has been calculated and compared with its conformal limit. Results confirm that the deviation from the conformal limit is dominated at the intermediate plasma temperature.
