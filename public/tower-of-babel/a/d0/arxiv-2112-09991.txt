The coarse Pimsner-Voiculescu sequence
Ulrich Bunke

Abstract
We derive the Pimsner-Voiculescu sequence calculating the K K -theory of a C ∗ C^{*} -algebra with ℤ {\mathbb{Z}} -action using constructions with equivariant coarse K K -homology theory. We then investigate to which extend this idea extends to more general equivariant coarse homology theories.
