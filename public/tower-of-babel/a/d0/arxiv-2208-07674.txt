Graded components of the Lie algebra associated with the lower central series of a right-angled Coxeter group Thanks: The study was supported by the Russian Science Foundation grant No. 21-71-00049, https://rscf.ru/project/21-71-00049/
Y. Veryovkin

Abstract
. The lower central series of the rgiht-angled Coxeter group RC 𝒦 \mbox{\it RC\/}_{\mathcal{K}} and the corresponding graded Lie algebra L ⁡ ( RC 𝒦 ) L(\mbox{\it RC\/}_{\mathcal{K}}) associated with the lower central series of a right-angled Coxeter group are studied. Relations are obtained in the graded components of the Lie algebra L ⁡ ( RC 𝒦 ) L(\mbox{\it RC\/}_{\mathcal{K}}) . A basis of the fourth graded component L ⁡ ( RC 𝒦 ) L(\mbox{\it RC\/}_{\mathcal{K}}) for groups with at most 4 4 generators was described.



1.
Introduction

The right-angled Coxeter group
\mbox{\it RC\/}_{\mathcal{K}}
is a group with
m
generating
g_{1},\ldots,g_{m}
relations
g_{i}^{2}=1
for all
i\in\{1,\ldots,m\}
, as well as the relations
g_{i}g_{j}=g_{j}g_{i}
for some pairs
\{i,j\}
. Such a group can be defined by a graph
\mathcal{K}^{1}
with
m
vertices, where if the corresponding generators commute, then a pair of vertices is connected by an edge. Also of interest is the lower central series of the group, with the help of which the Lie algebra associated with group is constructed. This paper is devoted to the description of the Lie algebra associated with the right-angled Coxeter group (associated Lie algebra).

For the right-angled Artin groups
\mbox{\it RA\/}_{\mathcal{K}}
(which differ from the right-angled Coxeter groups
\mbox{\it RC\/}_{\mathcal{K}}
by the absence of the relations
g_{i}^{2}=1
), the associated Lie algebra
L(\mbox{\it RA\/}_{\mathcal{K}})
was completely calculated in
[
4
]
, see also
[
11
]
,
[
8
]
. Namely, an isomorphism between the Lie algebra
L(\mbox{\it RA\/}_{\mathcal{K}})
and the Lie graph algebra (over
\mathbb{Z}
) corresponding to the graph
\mathcal{K}^{1}
was proved.

In the case of right-angled Coxeter groups, in some special cases, factor groups
\gamma_{1}(\mbox{\it RC\/}_{\mathcal{K}})/\gamma_{n}(\mbox{\it RC\/}_{\mathcal{K}})
were studied for some
n
(see works
[
9
]
,
[
10
]
). For right-angled Coxeter groups, in contrast to right-angled Artin groups, the problem of describing the associated Lie algebra
L(\mbox{\it RC\/}_{\mathcal{K}})
is much more difficult, since there is no isomorphism between the algebra
L(\mbox{\it RC\/}_{\mathcal{K}})
and the Lie graph algebra
L_{\mathcal{K}}
over
\mathbb{Z}_{2}
(see
[
7
, Example 4.3]
). In this paper
[
7
]
an epimorphism of Lie algebras
L_{\mathcal{K}}\rightarrow L(\mbox{\it RC\/}_{\mathcal{K}})
is constructed and in some cases its kernel is described, and for an arbitrary group
\mbox{\it RC\/}_{\mathcal{K}}
a combinatorial description of the bases of the first three graded components of the Lie algebra.

In this paper
[
12
]
the dimensions of the successive factors of the members of the lower central series are calculated and their basis is constructed for the free product of direct sums of cyclic groups of order
2
, which is a subset of right-angled Coxeter groups. We construct a basis for the
4
-th graded component of the associated Lie algebra for Coxeter groups with
3
and
4
generators (the associated Lie algebra is completely described for
2
generators, see
[
7
, Proposition 4.4]
). Unlike the bases constructed in
[
12
]
, the bases constructed in this paper consist entirely of simple nested commutators.
