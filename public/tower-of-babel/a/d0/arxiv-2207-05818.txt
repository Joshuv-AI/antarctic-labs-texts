High-precision mass measurement of 24 Si and a refined determination of the r ​ p rp process at the A = 22 A=22 waiting point
D. Puentes, Z. Meisel, G. Bollen, A. Hamaker, C. Langer, E. Leistenschneider, C. Nicoloff, W.-J. Ong, M. Redshaw, R. Ringle, C. S. Sumithrarachchi, J. Surbrook, A. A. Valverde, I. T. Yandow

Abstract
We report a high precision mass measurement of Si 24 {}^{24}{\rm Si} , performed with the LEBIT facility at the National Superconducting Cyclotron Laboratory. The atomic mass excess, 10 753.8 10\;753.8 (37) keV, is a factor of 5 more precise than previous results. This substantially reduces the uncertainty of the Al 23 ​ ( p , γ ) 24 ​ Si {}^{23}{\rm Al}(p,\gamma)^{24}{\rm Si} reaction rate, which is a key part of the rapid proton capture ( r ​ p rp ) process powering Type I X-ray bursts. The updated rate constrains the onset temperature of the ( α , p ) (\alpha,p) process at the Mg 22 {}^{22}{\rm Mg} waiting-point to a precision of 9%.



Type I X-ray bursts occur at astrophysical sites consisting of a binary star system where a neutron star accretes H/He-rich matter from a companion star, leading to nuclear burning on the neutron star surface
Lewin et al. 1993
;
Schatz and Rehm 2006
. As the temperature increases, the hot CNO cycle proceeds until the triple-
\alpha

rp
)

and (
\alpha,p
)
Wallace and Woosley 1981

Neutron Star Interior Composition Explorer
(NICER)

In addition to the astrophysical conditions, the main determinant of the shape of the light curve is the nuclear physics involved

), (
\alpha,\gamma
) and (
\alpha,p
) reaction rates and slow

and multi-zone

;
Zamfir et al. 2012

;
Parikh et al. 2008

Cyburt et al. 2016
. The dramatic variations in the simulated light curve due to these reaction rate uncertainties can make it difficult to extract astrophysical parameters from comparisons with observed bursting events.

We assessed the impact of the new
{}^{24}{\rm Si}

\alpha p
-process by calculating the flow at the
{}^{22}{\rm Mg}

{}^{23}{\rm Al}(p,\gamma)
and
{}^{22}{\rm Mg}(\alpha,p)

{}^{22}{\rm Mg}
, 3.88 s

of
{}^{23}{\rm Al}

The flow into the

\lambda_{\alpha,p}/\left(\lambda_{p,\gamma}+\lambda_{\alpha,p}\right)

, where
X_{i}
and
A_{i}
are the mass fraction and mass number of hydrogen and helium for the
{}^{23}{\rm Al}(p,\gamma)
and
{}^{22}{\rm Mg}(\alpha,p)
reactions, respectively
Merz and Meisel 2021
.
W
is a weight factor, which is the equilibrium abundance of the nuclide as determined by the Saha equation
Meisel et al. 2018
;
Brown 2015
. Since the
{}^{22}{\rm Mg}

\beta^{+}
-decay rate is too slow to compete, only strong and electromagnetic interactions are involved in the flow, and the density dependence cancels. For
X_{i}
, we take the conditions determined by Ref.
Fisker et al. 2008
for the X-ray burst ignition region, where
X_{\rm H}=0.03
and
X_{\rm He}=0.31

{}^{24}{\rm Si}
using Penning trap mass spectrometry, resulting in a mass excess of
