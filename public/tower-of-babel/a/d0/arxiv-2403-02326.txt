Approximate Controllability for Nonautonomous Integrodifferential Equations with State-dependent Delay
Mamadou Abdoul DIOP, Mohammed ELGHANDOURI, Khalil EZZINBI ∗

Abstract
. We study the existence of mild solutions and the approximate controllability for nonautonomous integrodifferential equations with state-dependent delay. We assume the approximate controllability of the linear part, then we use the resolvent operators theory to prove the approximate controllability of the nonlinear case. An example of one-dimensional heat equation with memory is given to illustrate our basic results.

Controllability is one of the most important fundamental concepts of mathematical control theory, which plays an important role in deterministic and stochastic systems. Its history began with the case of the finite dimension, its extension to the infinite dimension case has known a very important development since the works of Hector Fattorini in 1971, David Russell in 1978 and Jacques-Louis Lions in the late 1970s, see
[
35

37

and a space of control functions

by the following differential equation:

Proof.

is compact for all

, we can show that there exist non-negatives continuous functions

and

such that

\displaystyle\bar{K}(t)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\displaystyle\int_{s}^{t}\displaystyle\int_{s}^{r}M_{1}(t,r)\bar{K}(w)dwdr+\displaystyle\int_{s}^{t}\displaystyle\int_{s}^{r}\displaystyle\int_{s}^{w}M_{2}(t,r,w)\bar{K}(l)dldwdr.

As

,

is continuous in

. It follows then from Fubini’s Theorem that

\displaystyle\bar{K}(t)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\displaystyle\int_{s}^{t}\bar{K}(w)\displaystyle\int_{w}^{t}M_{1}(t,r)drdw+\displaystyle\int_{s}^{t}\displaystyle\int_{s}^{r}\bar{K}(l)\displaystyle\int_{l}^{r}M_{2}(t,r,w)dwdldr

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\displaystyle\int_{s}^{t}\bar{K}(w)\displaystyle\int_{w}^{t}M_{1}(t,r)drdw+\displaystyle\int_{s}^{t}\bar{K}(l)\displaystyle\int_{l}^{t}\displaystyle\int_{l}^{r}M_{2}(t,r,w)dwdrdl.

Let

, then

\displaystyle\bar{K}(t)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\displaystyle\int_{s}^{t}\left[\bar{M}_{1,a}+\bar{M}_{2,a}\right]\bar{K}(w)dw\hskip 2.84544pt\text{ for }\hskip 2.84544pt0\leq s\leq t\leq a,

where

\bar{M}_{1,a}=\sup\limits_{0\leq w\leq t\leq a}\displaystyle\int_{w}^{t}M_{1}(t,r)dr

and

,

for

. By Grönwall Lemma, we get that

for

. Since

is arbitrary, we obtain that

for each

, i.e,

on

for

, and

. Thus, assumption
\bf\huge(H_{0})
holds.

. By

\displaystyle=
