Rational fibered Cubic fourfolds
Hanine AWADA

Abstract
. Some classes of cubic fourfolds are birational to fibrations over ℙ 2 \mathbb{P}^{2} , where the fibers are rational surfaces. This is the case for cubics containing a plane (resp. an elliptic ruled surface), where the fibers are quadric surfaces (resp. del Pezzo sextic surfaces). It is known that the rationality of these cubic hypersurfaces is related to the rationality of these surfaces over the function field of ℙ 2 \mathbb{P}^{2} and to the existence of rational (multi)sections of the fibrations. We study, in the moduli space of cubic fourfolds, the intersection of the divisor 𝒞 8 \mathcal{C}_{8} (resp. 𝒞 18 \mathcal{C}_{18} ) with 𝒞 14 \mathcal{C}_{14} , 𝒞 26 \mathcal{C}_{26} and 𝒞 38 \mathcal{C}_{38} , whose elements are known to be rational cubic fourfolds. We provide descriptions of the irreducible components of these intersections and give new explicit examples of rational cubics fibered in (quartic, quintic) del Pezzo surfaces or in quadric surfaces over ℙ 2 \mathbb{P}^{2} . We also investigate the existence of rational sections for these fibrations. Under some mild assumptions on the singularities of the fibers, these properties can be translated in terms of Brauer classes on certain surfaces.

Introduction

A cubic fourfold is a smooth cubic hypersurface
X\subset\mathbb{P}^{5}
, that is the vanishing locus of a degree 3 homogeneous polynomial in 6 variables. Determining the rationality (or not) of
X
is a very challenging open problem in algebraic geometry.

The fourfold
X
is called
special
if it contains an algebraic surface
S
which is not homologous to a complete intersection. Hassett
[
12
]
defines the loci
\mathcal{C}_{d}
of special cubic fourfolds of discriminant
d

\mathcal{C}

d>6
and
d\equiv 0,2\pmod{6}
. The values
d
hence make up an infinite sequence of integers. Moreover, for an infinite, proper subset of the set of the divisors
\mathcal{C}_{d}
, the Hodge structure of the nonspecial cohomology of the cubic fourfold is essentially the Hodge structure of the primitive cohomology of a K3 surface. This K3 surface is said to be
associated
to the special cubic fourfold. Hassett
[
12
, Section 5]
shows that special cubic fourfolds of discriminant
d
have an associated K3 surface if and only if
d\nmid 4
, 9, or any odd prime
p\equiv 2\pmod{3}
. A natural suspicion is that any rational smooth cubic fourfold ought to have an associated K3 surface. For now, cubic fourfolds in
\mathcal{C}_{14}
,
\mathcal{C}_{26}
,
\mathcal{C}_{38}
and
\mathcal{C}_{42}
are proved to be rational (see
[
8
]
,
[
18
]
,
[
6
]
or very recently
[
19
]
).

In this paper, we are interested in classes of cubic fourfolds that are birational to surface fibrations over
\mathbb{P}^{2}
. This is the case for cubics in
\mathcal{C}_{8}
(resp.
\mathcal{C}_{18}
) containing a plane (resp. an elliptic ruled surface). The projection from the plane (resp. the linear system of quadrics through the elliptic ruled surface) gives the cubic a structure of quadric (resp. sextic del Pezzo) surface fibration over
\mathbb{P}^{2}

In fact, Hassett

identifies countably many divisors in

that displays the blow-up

of

\mathbb{P}^{2}
. Such a fibration is rational over the function field
\mathbb{C}(\mathbb{P}^{2})
when it has a rational section, and the codimension two loci described in
[
1
]

proprieties (see

- we will call these

of a
\mathbb{P}^{2}
-bundle

over a smooth degree two K3 surface

and the class

\mathbb{P}^{1}
