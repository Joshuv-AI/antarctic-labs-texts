Toward a twistor action for chiral higher-spin gravity
Tung Tran

Abstract
A covariant twistor action for chiral higher-spin theory in (A)dS and flat space is constructed in term of a holomorphic Chern-Simons theory on twistor space. The action reproduces all known cubic vertices of chiral higher-spin theory in flat space. The spacetime action of the holomorphic Chern-Simons theory in flat space is also obtained.

. In order to retain locality, which is one of the crucial features of field theories, one often has to give up unitarity. As a consequence, local higher-spin theories with propagating

In this work, we propose a covariant twistor action for the chiral HSGRA in (A)dS in response to the quest of covariantising the light-cone action of chiral HSGRA. Note that the proposed twistor action for chiral HSGRA is not fully determined as we do not know the explicit expression of what we call

However, the twistor Chern-Simons action allows us to compute all three-point scattering amplitudes of chiral HSGRA. We also present the spacetime action of the holomorphic Chern-Simons theory in flat space. As a consistency check, we find that its cubic vertices, after projecting to the light-cone gauge, match with the ones in

Bengtsson et al. 1987

The twistor theory

II.1
Twistor geometry/correspondence

Let
\mathcal{PT}
be the twistor space associated with a conformally flat Euclidean spacetime
{\cal M}
with cosmological constant
\Lambda

\displaystyle ds^{2}=\frac{dx_{\mu}dx^{\mu}}{(1+\Lambda x^{2})^{2}}=\Omega^{2}dx_{\mu}dx^{\mu}\,,\qquad\mu=1,2,3,4\,.

(1)

\mathcal{PT}
is defined as an open subset of
\mathbb{P}^{3}

2

Huggett and Tod 1986
;
Jiang 2008
;
Adamo 2013

Adamo 2018

\displaystyle\mathcal{PT}=\left\{\mathcal{Z}^{A}:=(\lambda^{\alpha},\mu^{\dot{\alpha}})\,\big|\,I_{AB}\mathcal{Z}^{A}\hat{\mathcal{Z}}^{B}\neq 0\right\}\,.

(2)

Here,
\mathcal{Z}^{A}
are homogeneous coordinates of
\mathbb{P}^{3}
. On
\mathcal{PT}
, there is a quaternionic
conjugation that maps
\mathcal{Z}^{A}
to its dual twistor
\hat{\mathcal{Z}}^{A}=(\hat{\lambda}^{\alpha},\hat{\mu}^{\dot{\alpha}})
where

\displaystyle\hat{\lambda}^{\alpha}=(-\overline{\lambda^{1}},\overline{\lambda^{0}})\,,\qquad\hat{\mu}^{\dot{\alpha}}=(-\overline{\mu^{\dot{1}}},\overline{\mu^{\dot{0}}})\,.

(3)

Note that
\overline{\lambda^{1}}
is simply the complex conjugation of
\lambda^{1}
. Furthermore,
I_{AB}
is known as the infinity twistor that specifies the conformal factor
\Omega
in (
1

Penrose and MacCallum 1972

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}I^{AB}\epsilon_{ABCD}=I_{CD}\,,\qquad I_{AC}I^{BC}=\Lambda\delta_{A}{}^{B}\,.

(4)

The infinity twistor has the following representatives:

\displaystyle I^{AB}=\begin{pmatrix}\Lambda\epsilon^{\alpha\beta}&0\\
0&\epsilon^{\dot{\alpha}\dot{\beta}}\end{pmatrix}\,,\quad I_{AB}=\begin{pmatrix}\epsilon_{\alpha\beta}&0\\
0&\Lambda\epsilon_{\dot{\alpha}\dot{\beta}}\end{pmatrix}\,,

(5)

which induces a Poisson structure

;
Ward 1980

\displaystyle\widetilde{\Pi}=I^{AB}\overleftarrow{\partial_{A}}\wedge\overrightarrow{\partial_{B}}=\Lambda\frac{\overleftarrow{\partial}}{\partial\lambda^{\alpha}}\wedge\frac{\overrightarrow{\partial}}{\partial\lambda_{\alpha}}+\frac{\overleftarrow{\partial}}{\partial\mu^{\dot{\alpha}}}\wedge\frac{\overrightarrow{\partial}}{\partial\mu_{\dot{\alpha}}}\,,

(6)

on
\mathcal{PT}

\mathcal{PT}

;
Adamo et al. 2017

\displaystyle f\star g

\displaystyle:=fe^{\ell_{p}\widetilde{\Pi}}\wedge g=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{\ell_{p}^{k}}{k!}f\,\widetilde{\Pi}^{k}\,g\,,

(7)

where
\ell_{p}
is some natural length scale that plays the role of a deformation parameter. At
k=1
, we recover the standard Poisson bracket

\displaystyle\{f,g\}_{\mathcal{PT}}=f\,\widetilde{\Pi}\,g\,.

(8)

Note that we will sometime suppress the
\wedge
-products to shorten our expressions.

II.2
The twistor action

In constructing the twistor action for chiral HSGRA, it is useful to define the following Euler operator
Hähnel and McLoughlin 2017
;
Adamo et al. 2017

\displaystyle\hat{\Sigma}=\mathcal{Z}^{A}\frac{\partial}{\partial\mathcal{Z}^{A}}\,,

(9)

to measure the weight in
\mathcal{Z}
of any twistor expression. For instance,
\hat{\Sigma}\,D^{3}\mathcal{Z}=4
, where

\displaystyle D^{3}\mathcal{Z}=\epsilon_{ABCD}\mathcal{Z}^{A}d\mathcal{Z}^{B}\wedge d\mathcal{Z}^{C}\wedge d\mathcal{Z}^{D}\,

(10)

is the canonical measure on
\mathcal{PT}
. Then, our proposed twistor action for chiral HSGRA in (anti)de-Sitter space is

\displaystyle\begin{split}\bm{S}[\mathbb{A}]&=S_{hCS}+S_{c}=\int D^{3}\mathcal{Z}\,L[\mathbb{A}]+S_{c}\,.\end{split}

(11)

Here,

\displaystyle\begin{split}L[\mathbb{A}]&=\text{Tr}\big[\mathbb{A}\star\mathbb{A}+\frac{2}{3}\mathbb{A}\star\mathbb{A}\star\mathbb{A}\big]\\
&=\text{Tr}\big[\sum_{h\in\mathbb{Z}}\mathbb{A}_{-h}\star\bar{\partial}\mathbb{A}_{h}+\frac{2}{3}\sum_{h_{i}\in\mathbb{Z}}\mathbb{A}_{h_{1}}\star\mathbb{A}_{h_{2}}\star\mathbb{A}_{h_{3}}\big]\,\end{split}

(12)

is the Lagrangian that obeys the constraint

\displaystyle\hat{\Sigma}\,L[\mathbb{A}]=-4\,,

(13)

so that we have a well-defined integral on
\mathcal{PT}

3

Bittleston and Skinner 2020

\mathcal{PT}

\displaystyle\bar{\partial}=d\hat{Z}^{A}\frac{\partial}{\partial\hat{Z}^{A}}\,,\quad\text{and}\quad\mathbb{A}=\sum_{h\in\mathbb{Z}}\mathbb{A}_{h}\,,

(14)

where
\mathbb{A}_{h}\in\Omega^{0,1}(\mathcal{PT},\text{End}(E)\otimes\mathcal{O}(2h-2))\,

Lastly, the term

\displaystyle\delta\mathcal{Z}^{A}=\sum_{h\in\mathbb{Z}}\{\mathcal{Z}^{A},\xi_{h}\}\,,\qquad\xi_{h}\in\Gamma(\mathcal{PT},\mathcal{O}(2h-2))

III
Scattering amplitudes

All 3-point tree-level amplitudes of chiral HSGRA in (A)dS can be computed as follows. By doing integration by parts, we observe that
I^{AB}\partial_{A}\partial_{B}f=0
. Hence, we can reduce the number of the star-products by one in each term of the action
S_{hCS}
. Therefore, (
11
) can be cast into the following form

\displaystyle\bm{S}=\int\text{Tr}\big[\sum_{h}\mathbb{A}_{-h}\bar{\partial}\mathbb{A}_{h}+\frac{2}{3}\sum_{h_{i}}\mathbb{A}_{h_{1}}\mathbb{A}_{h_{2}}\star\mathbb{A}_{h_{3}}\big]+S_{c}^{\prime}\,

(16)

where
S_{c}^{\prime}
accounts for
S_{c}
in (
11
) and the remnants of
\partial/\partial\mathcal{Z}^{A}
when acting on the holomorphic measure
D^{3}\mathcal{Z}
. The twistor representative of the
(0,1)
-form connection
\mathbb{A}_{h}
on
\mathcal{PT}
is chosen to be
Hähnel and McLoughlin 2017
;
Adamo et al. 2017

\displaystyle\begin{split}\mathbb{A}_{h_{i}}&=\int_{\mathbb{C}}\frac{dt_{i}}{t_{i}^{2h_{i}-1}}\bar{\delta}^{2}(t_{i}\lambda-\lambda_{i})e^{t_{i}[\mu\tilde{\lambda}_{i}]}\,,\end{split}

(17)

in terms of the on-shell four-momentum
k_{i}^{\alpha\dot{\alpha}}=\lambda_{i}^{\alpha}\tilde{\lambda}_{i}^{\dot{\alpha}}
, which is a null vector on the tangent space of
(A)dS_{4}
. Here,

\displaystyle\bar{\delta}(az-b)=\frac{1}{2\pi i}d\bar{z}\frac{\partial}{\partial\bar{z}}\Big(\frac{1}{az-b}\Big)

(18)

is a
(0,1)
-form holomorphic delta function
Witten 2004
. Parametrizing
\lambda_{\alpha}=(1,z)
and
\lambda_{\alpha}^{\prime}=(b,a)
, we define

\displaystyle\bar{\delta}(\langle\lambda\lambda^{\prime}\rangle)=\frac{1}{2\pi i}d\overline{\lambda^{\dot{\alpha}}}\frac{\partial}{\partial\overline{\lambda^{\dot{\alpha}}}}\frac{1}{\langle\lambda\lambda^{\prime}\rangle}\,.

(19)

Finally, the projective version of the holomorphic delta function is defined by
Hähnel and McLoughlin 2017

\displaystyle\bar{\delta}_{m}(\lambda,\lambda^{\prime})=\Big[\frac{\langle\xi\lambda\rangle}{\langle\xi\lambda^{\prime}\rangle}\Big]^{m}\bar{\delta}(\langle\lambda\lambda^{\prime}\rangle)=\int_{\mathbb{C}}\frac{dt}{t^{m}}\bar{\delta}^{2}(t\lambda-\lambda^{\prime}),

(20)

which explains the origin of the twistor representative (
17
). Here, the degree of
t_{i}
(or the helicity
h_{i}
) essentially defines the weight in
\lambda
of
\mathbb{A}_{h_{i}}
. Furthermore, the crucial difference between our setup and the setup in
Hähnel and McLoughlin 2017
;
Adamo et al. 2017
is that there is a scalar field corresponding to
h=0
, which is essential for quantum consistency of chiral HSGRA.

It was shown in
Nagaraj and Ponomarev 2019
that the plane wave solutions for higher-spin fields have the same structures with the ones in flat space. This explains why we can use the momentum eigenstates (
17
) in (A)dS.

A simple computation shows that

\displaystyle\mathbb{A}_{h_{2}}\star\mathbb{A}_{h_{3}}\sim\frac{\ell_{p}^{k}}{k!}t_{2}^{d_{k,h_{2}}}t_{3}^{d_{k,h_{3}}}\Big([23]+\Lambda\left\langle\frac{\partial}{\partial\lambda_{2}}\frac{\partial}{\partial\lambda_{3}}\right\rangle\Big)^{k}\,,

(21)

where
d_{k,h_{i}}=k+1-2h_{i}
. Following the steps in
Adamo et al. 2017
, we rewrite
D^{3}\mathcal{Z}
as
\frac{d^{4}\mathcal{Z}}{\text{Vol}\,{\mathbb{C}}^{*}}
, which allows us to integrate out
\mu
and
\lambda
variables to obtain 4-dimensional momentum delta-function
\delta^{4}(P)
. Note that

\displaystyle\begin{split}\left\langle\frac{\partial}{\partial\lambda_{2}}\frac{\partial}{\partial\lambda_{3}}\right\rangle\delta^{4}(P)&=-[23]\Box_{P}\delta^{4}(P)\,,\\
\text{where}\quad\Box_{P}&=\frac{1}{2}\frac{\partial}{\partial P^{\alpha\dot{\alpha}}}\frac{\partial}{\partial P_{\alpha\dot{\alpha}}}\,.\end{split}

(22)

Furthermore, we use the conventions where
\langle ab\rangle=a^{\alpha}b_{\alpha}
and
[ab]=a^{\dot{\alpha}}b_{\dot{\alpha}}
. From (
13
), we can read off the constraint between the number of derivatives in (
21
) and helicities of the external states:

\displaystyle k=h_{1}+h_{2}+h_{3}-1\,.

(23)

Integrating over
\mu
, we obtain

\begin{split}{\cal M}^{\Lambda}_{3}&(h_{1},h_{2},h_{3})=\frac{\ell_{p}^{k}}{k!}\int d^{2}\lambda dt_{1}dt_{2}dt_{3}t_{1}^{1-2h_{1}}t_{2}^{d_{k,h_{2}}}t_{3}^{d_{k,h_{3}}}\\
&\left([23]+\Lambda\left\langle\frac{\partial}{\partial\lambda_{2}}\frac{\partial}{\partial\lambda_{3}}\right\rangle\right)^{k}\bar{\delta}^{2}(t_{1}\tilde{\lambda}_{1}+t_{2}\tilde{\lambda}_{2}+t_{3}\tilde{\lambda}_{3})\\
&\times\bar{\delta}^{2}(t_{1}\lambda-\lambda_{1})\bar{\delta}^{2}(t_{2}\lambda-\lambda_{2})\bar{\delta}^{2}(t_{3}\lambda-\lambda_{3})\,.\end{split}

(24)

The integrals over
\lambda
and
t_{i}
variables can be performed trivially as in
Hähnel and McLoughlin 2017
;
Adamo et al. 2017
. For instance, we can use
\text{Vol}\,\mathbb{C}^{*}
to fix
t_{1}=1
. Then, the integration over
\lambda
gives us delta functions on the support at

\displaystyle t_{2}=\frac{\langle 23\rangle}{\langle 31\rangle}\,,\quad t_{3}=\frac{\langle 32\rangle}{\langle 12\rangle}\,.

(25)

From here, it is a simple computation to integrate over
t_{2}
and
t_{3}
. After a few more steps of manipulating spinors using momentum conservation, we arrive at:

\displaystyle\begin{split}{\cal M}_{3}^{\Lambda}(h_{1},&h_{2},h_{3})=\frac{\big[\ell_{p}(1-\Lambda\Box_{P})\big]^{h_{1}+h_{2}+h_{3}-1}}{\Gamma[h_{1}+h_{2}+h_{3}]}\delta^{4}(P)\\
&\times[12]^{h_{1}+h_{2}-h_{3}}[23]^{h_{2}+h_{3}-h_{1}}[31]^{h_{3}+h_{1}-h_{2}}\,.\end{split}

(26)

Note that we do not need to fix the kinetic part of the above 3-pt amplitudes by symmetry as in

Nagaraj and Ponomarev 2019
;
Nagaraj and Ponomarev 2020
. Furthermore, in the flat limit where
\Lambda\rightarrow 0
, we obtain the standard
\overline{\text{MHV}}_{3}
amplitudes, which enables us to read off the cubic coupling constants:

\displaystyle\mathcal{C}_{h_{1},h_{2},h_{3}}=\frac{\ell_{p}^{h_{1}+h_{2}+h_{3}-1}}{\Gamma[h_{1}+h_{2}+h_{3}]}\,,\quad h_{1}+h_{2}+h_{3}>0\,.

(27)

A nice feature about our twistor construction is that the coupling constant
\mathcal{C}_{h_{1},h_{2},h_{3}}
is built in, and there is no need to derive it dynamically as in
Metsaev 1991a
;
Metsaev 1991b
;
Ponomarev and Skvortsov 2017
.

Using the map between the spinor-helicity formalism and the light-cone formalism, see e.g.
Chalmers and Siegel 1999
;
Bengtsson 2016
where

\displaystyle i]=2^{1/4}\binom{\bar{p}_{i}\beta_{i}^{-1/2}}{-\beta_{i}^{1/2}}\,,\qquad i\rangle=2^{1/4}\binom{p_{i}\beta_{i}^{-1/2}}{-\beta_{i}^{1/2}}\,,

(28)

we can express the square and angle brackets as

\displaystyle[ij]=\sqrt{\frac{2}{\beta_{i}\beta_{j}}}\overline{\mathbb{P}}_{ij}\,,\qquad\langle ij\rangle=\sqrt{\frac{2}{\beta_{i}\beta_{j}}}\mathbb{P}_{ij}\,.

(29)

Note that
\overline{\mathbb{P}}_{ij}=\bar{p}_{i}\beta_{j}-\bar{p}_{j}\beta_{i}
with
\bm{p}_{i}=(\beta_{i},p^{-}_{i},p_{i},\bar{p}_{i})
is the momentum of the external field that has helicity
h_{i}
. Then, we can show that the above 3-pt amplitudes in the flat limit reduce to

\displaystyle{\cal M}_{3}^{\Lambda\rightarrow 0}=\mathcal{C}_{h_{1},h_{2},h_{3}}\frac{\overline{\mathbb{P}}_{23}^{h_{1}+h_{2}+h_{3}}}{\beta_{1}^{h_{1}}\beta_{2}^{h_{2}}\beta_{3}^{h_{3}}}\,,

(30)

which are the correct cubic vertices obtained previously in
Bengtsson et al. 1983
;
Metsaev 1991a
;
Metsaev 1991b
. As a remark, it would be interesting to establish the map between (
26
) and the cubic vertices in (A)dS found by Metsaev in
Metsaev 2018
.

IV
Spacetime action in flat space

In this section, we obtain the spacetime action of the holomorphic Chern-Simons action in flat space where
\mathbb{M}:=\lim_{\Lambda\rightarrow 0}\mathcal{M}
from the action (
11
). To simplify the problem, we assume that all deformations are sufficiently small so that they will not affect the complex structures on twistor space to avoid the complication that arises from Kodaira’s theory, see details in
Kodaira 2005

First of all, notice that the twistor space in this case reduces to the usual flat/undeformed twistor space where

\displaystyle\mathbb{PT}=\left\{Z^{A}=(\lambda^{\alpha},\mu^{\dot{\alpha}}:=F^{\dot{\alpha}}(\lambda,x))\,\big|\,\lambda_{\alpha}\neq 0\right\}\,.

\displaystyle N_{X}:=T(\mathbb{PT})|_{\mathbb{P}^{1}}/T(\mathbb{P}^{1})\simeq\mathcal{O}(1)\oplus\mathcal{O}(1)\,.

(32)

The correspondence between
\mathbb{PT}
and

\displaystyle\mu^{\dot{\alpha}}=F^{\dot{\alpha}}(x,\lambda)=x^{\alpha\dot{\alpha}}\lambda_{\alpha}\,,

;

\displaystyle x^{\alpha\dot{\alpha}}=\frac{\lambda^{\alpha}\hat{\mu}^{\dot{\alpha}}-\hat{\lambda}^{\alpha}\mu^{\dot{\alpha}}}{\langle\lambda\hat{\lambda}\rangle}\,.

\displaystyle\bar{\partial}_{0}

\displaystyle=\langle\lambda\hat{\lambda}\rangle\lambda_{\alpha}\frac{\partial}{\partial\hat{\lambda}_{\alpha}}\,,\quad

\displaystyle\bar{\partial}_{\dot{\alpha}}

\displaystyle=-\lambda^{\alpha}\partial_{\alpha\dot{\alpha}}\,,

\displaystyle\bar{e}^{0}

\displaystyle=\frac{\langle\hat{\lambda}d\hat{\lambda}\rangle}{\langle\lambda\hat{\lambda}\rangle^{2}}\,,\qquad\qquad

\displaystyle\partial_{\dot{\alpha}}

\displaystyle:=-\frac{\hat{\lambda}^{\alpha}}{\langle\lambda\hat{\lambda}\rangle}\partial_{\alpha\dot{\alpha}}\,,

\displaystyle e^{0}

\displaystyle:=\langle\lambda d\lambda\rangle\,,

\displaystyle e^{\dot{\alpha}}

\displaystyle:=\lambda_{\alpha}dx^{\alpha\dot{\alpha}}\,.

Here,

When
\partial_{\dot{\alpha}}
-vector field acting on any

Using the basis (

\displaystyle\mathbb{A}=\mathbb{A}_{0}\bar{e}^{0}+\mathbb{A}_{\dot{\alpha}}\bar{e}^{\dot{\alpha}}\,.

(51)

Furthermore, we have the following rules:

\displaystyle\partial_{0}\mathbin{\raisebox{0.0pt}{\scalebox{1}[-1]{$\lnot$}}}e^{0}

\displaystyle=1\,,\qquad\quad\bar{\partial}_{0}\mathbin{\raisebox{0.0pt}{\scalebox{1}[-1]{$\lnot$}}}\bar{e}^{0}=1\,,

(52a)

Note that to reduce the number of

\displaystyle S[\mathbb{A}]=\int_{\mathbb{PS}}\text{Tr}\big[\sum_{h\in\mathbb{Z}}\mathbb{A}_{-h}\bar{\partial}\mathbb{A}_{h}+\frac{2}{3}\sum_{h_{i}\in\mathbb{Z}}\mathbb{A}_{h_{1}}\mathbb{A}_{h_{2}}\ast\mathbb{A}_{h_{3}}\big]\,.

A simple computation results in

\displaystyle\mathcal{L}_{\partial_{\dot{\alpha}}}\mathbb{A}=(\partial_{\dot{\alpha}}\mathbb{A}_{0}+\mathbb{A}_{\dot{\alpha}})\bar{e}^{0}+\partial_{\dot{\alpha}}\mathbb{A}_{\dot{\beta}}\bar{e}^{\dot{\beta}}\,,

where

V
Discussion

In this letter, we have constructed a covariant action for chiral HSGRA in (A)dS from a Chern-Simons action on twistor space. The twistor origin of chiral HSGRA indicates that it must be integrable and one-loop exact. It is intriguing to ask whether we can have a world-sheet description for the chiral HSGRA to gain control over non-locality issues the moment we step outside the self-dual sectors. It will be a crucial step in finding a higher-spin theory with unitary completion where we have total control of non-local interactions.

One of the fundamental questions the results of this letter can address is whether having a covariant form for interacting higher-spin theories is an advantage. As in other covariant formulations of higher-spin theories, the twistor construction provides us a clear view about higher-spin symmetry that governs the chiral HSGRA. The covariant action of the chiral HSGRA also enable us to see some vertices in terms of spacetime derivatives that can not be obtained using Fronsdal’s approach. However, note that the computation of scattering amplitudes of the action (
69
) is more involved compared to the calculations in the light-cone gauge even at tree-level.

Contrary to the old folklore, recent developments in constructing higher-spin interactions by means of the light-front formalism or free differential algebra show that there exists a smooth deformation between the vertices in flat space and (A)dS
Metsaev 2018
;
Sharapov and Skvortsov 2022
. In this paper, we also confirm that such deformation exists at the level of the twistor action (
11
).

Lastly, chiral HSGRA is known to admit
U(N)
,
O(N)
and
USp(N)
gaugings
Metsaev 1991a
;
Metsaev 1991b
;
Skvortsov et al. 2020
, see also
Konstein and Vasiliev 1990
. From this perspective, it is plausible that chiral HSGRA will become the self-dual part of the HS-IKKT matrix model with truncated higher-spin spectrum
Steinacker 2016
;
Sperling and Steinacker 2018
;
Sperling and Steinacker 2019
;
Steinacker and Tran 2022
in the deep quantum regime where spacetime coordinates no longer commute. We postpone the study of finding the connection between chiral HSGRA and HS-IKKT for future work.
