Current algebras on S 3 S^{3} of complex Lie algebras ( Revised version )
Tosiaki Kori

Abstract
This is a full revised version of the previous same titled article. The 2-cocycle of the central extension of the current algebra on S 3 S^{3} is taken the place of a new quarternion valued one, that is defined by the boundary Dirac operator. And we added a discussion on the symmetric invariant bilinear forms associated to the central extension 𝔤 ^ \widehat{\mathfrak{g}} . A current algebra is a Lie algebra of smooth mappings of a given manifold into a Lie algebra, or its central extension. Affine Kac-Moody algebra is an example where the manifold is S 1 ⊂ 𝐂 ∖ { 0 } S^{1}\subset\mathbf{C}\setminus\{0\} . We shall extend this theory to a Lie algebra of smooth mappings of S 3 ⊂ 𝐂 2 ∖ { 0 } S^{3}\subset\mathbf{C}^{2}\setminus\{0\} into a complex simple Lie algebra 𝔤 \mathfrak{g} . Let ℒ \mathcal{L}\, be the 𝐂 − \mathbf{C}- algebra generated by the Laurent polynomial type harmonic spinors over 𝐂 2 ∖ { 0 } \mathbf{C}^{2}\setminus\{0\} . Here we mean ”harmonic” as the zero-mode of the Dirac operator on 𝐂 2 \mathbf{C}^{2} . The real part 𝒦 \mathcal{K} of ℒ \mathcal{L}\, becomes a commutative real subalgebra of ℒ \mathcal{L}\, . For a simple Lie algebra 𝔤 \mathfrak{g} , the 𝔤 \mathfrak{g} -current algebra is defined as the ( real ) Lie algebra ℒ ​ 𝔤 \mathcal{L}\mathfrak{g} that is generated by ℒ ⊗ 𝐑 𝔤 \mathcal{L}\otimes_{\mathbf{R}}\mathfrak{g} . For the Cartan subalgebra 𝔥 \mathfrak{h} of 𝔤 \mathfrak{g}\, , 𝒦 ​ 𝔥 = 𝒦 ⊗ 𝐑 𝔥 \,\mathcal{K}\mathfrak{h}=\mathcal{K}\otimes_{\mathbf{R}}\mathfrak{h} becomes a Cartan subalgebra of ℒ ​ 𝔤 \mathcal{L}\mathfrak{g} . We have the weight space decomposition of ℒ ​ 𝔤 \mathcal{L}\mathfrak{g} with respect to the Cartan subalgebra 𝒦 ​ 𝔥 \mathcal{K}\mathfrak{h} . We introduce a quarternion valued 2-cocycle on the 𝔤 \mathfrak{g} -current algebra ℒ ​ 𝔤 \mathcal{L}\mathfrak{g} . Then we have the central extenstion ℒ ​ 𝔤 ⊕ 𝐇 ​ c \mathcal{L}\mathfrak{g}\oplus\mathbf{H}{\rm c} associated to the 2-cocycle. Adjoining a derivation coming from the radial vector field ∂ ∂ n \frac{\partial}{\partial n} on S 3 S^{3} we obtain the second central extension 𝔤 ^ = ℒ ​ 𝔤 ⊕ 𝐇 ​ c ⊕ 𝐑 ​ d \,\widehat{\mathfrak{g}}=\mathcal{L}\mathfrak{g}\oplus\mathbf{H}{\rm c}\oplus\mathbf{R}{\rm d} . We shall investigate the root space decomposition and the Chevalley generators of 𝔤 ^ \,\widehat{\mathfrak{g}}\, .

Lemma 5.9
