Single-particle properties of the near-threshold proton-emitting resonance in 11 B
Nguyen Le Anh, Bui Minh Loc, Naftali Auerbach, Vladimir Zelevinsky

Abstract
The excitation function of proton elastic scattering from 10 Be at keV energy is calculated using the self-consistent Skyrme Hartree-Fock in the continuum method. The calculation successfully reproduces the narrow near-threshold proton-emitting resonance ( E x = 11.4 E_{x}=11.4 MeV, Γ = 6 \Gamma=6 keV, and quantum number J π = 1 / 2 + J^{\pi}=1/2^{+} ) in 11 B relevant to the β \beta -delayed proton emission of 11 Be. This supports the recent experimental result of Y. Ayyad et al. at the ReA3 re-accelerator facility of the National Superconducting Cyclotron Laboratory (NSCL) at the Michigan State University. The resonance is interpreted as the s 1 / 2 s_{1/2} single-proton resonance state in the Skyrme Hartree-Fock mean-field theory.

Conclusion.
The experimental result

11

\beta
-delayed proton emission of
11
Be is interpreted as the

single-particle resonance predicted by the Skyrme HF calculation. Its simple structure is given by [

The calculated width of this resonance is
\Gamma=6
keV and

. The experiment in Ref.
Ayyad et al. 2022
is crucial for localizing the exact position of this resonance. The final result of calculations does not depend on the choice of the version of the Skyrme interactions. The Skyrme HF calculation is an excellent tool for describing the scattering process including low-lying single-particle resonances in the keV-energy region.
