Sterile Neutrinos at MAPP in the B − L B-L Model
Frank F. Deppisch, Suchita Kulkarni, Wei Liu

Abstract
The possibility of searching for right-handed neutrinos at the MoEDAL’s Apparatus for Penetrating Particles (MAPP) detector is investigated in this work. In particular, pair-production of right-handed (RH) neutrinos N N from either a B − L B-L gauge boson Z ′ Z^{\prime} , as well as Standard Model (SM) Z Z boson are considered. Under a no-background assumption, we show that the MAPP detector can be sensitive to active-sterile neutrino mixing strengths as low as V μ ​ N 2 ≈ 10 − 12 V_{\mu N}^{2}\approx 10^{-12} for multiple choices of m N / m Z ′ m_{N}/m_{Z^{\prime}} values, when taking the B − L B-L gauge coupling g B − L = 10 − 3 g_{B-L}=10^{-3} near its current limit. The SM Z Z boson portal can reach a similar sensitivity, when the effective mixing between the B − L B-L and SM gauge boson is α ≈ 0.01 \alpha\approx 0.01 .

Introduction

The lightness of active neutrinos is a strong indication that the mass generation mechanism responsible is different from that of the other fermions, and is based on physics beyond the Standard Model (SM). A simple, ultraviolet-complete model based on the additional gauge group
U(1)_{B-L}

Mohapatra:1980qe
(baryon minus lepton number) can be employed which incorporates a seesaw mechanism with three right-handed Majorana neutrinos. It has a SM-singlet Higgs field
\chi
which acquires a vacuum expectation value that spontaneously breaks the
B-L
symmetry leading to a massive
Z^{\prime}
boson and heavy right-handed (RH) Majorana neutrinos
N_{i}
.

The
Z^{\prime}
gauge boson decays into pairs of RH neutrinos providing an efficient means of their production, even if the active-sterile mixing
V_{lN}
between the mostly sterile (under SM interactions) RH neutrinos and the active neutrinos is small. In this case, distinctive displaced vertex signatures at the Large Hadron Collider (LHC) are produced as the RH neutrino must decay via the small active-sterile mixing corresponding to long lifetimes. This production process
pp\to Z^{\prime}\to NN
at a hadron collider is controlled by the
B-L
coupling
g_{B-L}
, the mass
m_{Z^{\prime}}
of the heavy gauge boson and the masses
m_{N_{i}}
of the heavy neutrinos.

The primary exotic particle, the heavy gauge boson, can be searched for in different ways. LHC searches for a heavy resonance in dilepton final states put a bound
m_{Z^{\prime}}>4.5
TeV
Aaboud:2017buh
for a
B-L
gauge coupling equal to that of the SM
Z
boson. While the
B-L
breaking scale
\Lambda_{B-L}=M_{Z^{\prime}}/(2g_{B-L})
is constrained to be larger than 3.45 TeV from LEP-II
Heeck:2014zfa
;
Cacciapaglia:2006pk
;
Anthony:2003ub
;
LEP:2003aa
;
Carena:2004xs
, these limits fail when
m_{Z^{\prime}}
becomes too small as they assume an effective interaction with the
Z^{\prime}
being integrated out. Limits on the full
(m_{Z^{\prime}},g_{B-L})
parameter space can be derived using the Constraints On New Theories Using Rivet (CONTUR) tool for
g_{B-L}<10^{-3}
approximately for a
Z^{\prime}
above 1 GeV
Amrith:2018yfb
;
Butterworth:2016sqg
. Dark photon searches in various probes can be recast using the Darkcast tool
Ilten:2018crw
, see Figs.
1
and
2
.

Limits on the heavy RH neutrino sector can be derived from sterile neutrino searches, which are based on the SM production and decay mechanisms, see, e.g., Refs.
Bolton:2019pcu
;
Abdullahi:2022jlv
;
Bolton:2022pyf
and references therein. As mentioned, the RH neutrinos are long-lived if the active-sterile mixing
V_{lN}
is small which is generically expected in a canonical Type-I seesaw mechanism of neutrino mass generation. Here, the mass scale
m_{\nu}
of light neutrinos is related to that of the RH neutrinos as
m_{\nu}\sim V_{lN}^{2}m_{N}
. With sub-eV light neutrinos, as required by
0\nu\beta\beta
and Tritium beta decay experiments
Kraus:2004zw
;
Aseev:2011dq
;
Deppisch:2016rox
as well as cosmological observations
Ade:2015xua
, the active-sterile neutrino mixing is very small,
V_{lN}^{2}\approx 10^{-10}-10^{-12}
, for
m_{N}\approx 10-100
GeV. The RH neutrino decay lengths are then of the order of centimetres to metres and models of neutrino mass generation around the electroweak scale thus clearly motivate searches at the lifetime frontier. We should mention however that this is not necessarily the case in extended scenarios such as the inverse seesaw mechanism where the active-sterile neutrino mixing is large but the RH neutrinos are quasi-Dirac states, see, e.g., Ref.
Deppisch:2015qwa
and references therein.

Searches for displaced vertices at the LHC and in dedicated experiments have gained significant attention, see, e.g., Refs.

MammenAbraham:2020hex

2
B-L
gauge model and its phenomenology

2.1
Model setup

The
U(1)_{B-L}
gauge model adds an Abelian gauge field
B^{\prime}_{\mu}
, a SM-singlet scalar field
\chi
and three RH neutrinos
N_{i}
to the SM. The full gauge group is
SU(3)_{c}\times SU(2)_{L}\times U(1)_{Y}\times U(1)_{B-L}
, under which the scalar and RH neutrino fields
\chi
and
N_{i}
have
B-L
charges
Y_{B-L}(\chi)=+2
and
Y_{B-L}(N_{i})=-1
, respectively, but are otherwise singlets. The SM fermions have their canonical
Y_{B-L}
quantum numbers. The scalar sector of the Lagrangian is given by

\displaystyle{\cal L}

\displaystyle\supset(D^{\mu}H)^{\dagger}(D_{\mu}H)+(D^{\mu}\chi)^{\dagger}D_{\mu}\chi

\displaystyle-\left[m^{2}(H^{\dagger}H)+\mu^{2}(\chi^{*}\chi)+\lambda_{1}(H^{\dagger}H)^{2}+\lambda_{2}(\chi^{*}\chi)^{2}+\lambda_{3}(H^{\dagger}H)(\chi^{*}\chi)\right],

(1)

where
H
is the SM Higgs doublet and
D_{\mu}
is the covariant derivative. The latter includes the
U(1)_{B-L}
term
g_{B-L}Y_{B-L}
, with the extra gauge coupling
g_{B-L}
. The fermionic part of the Lagrangian adds the terms

\displaystyle{\cal L}

\displaystyle\supset i\overline{\nu_{Ri}}\gamma_{\mu}D^{\mu}\nu_{Ri}-y_{ij}^{\nu}\overline{L_{i}}\nu_{Rj}\tilde{H}-y_{ij}^{M}\overline{\nu^{c}_{Ri}}\nu_{Rj}\chi+\text{h.c.},

(2)

with a summation over the fermion generations
i,j=1,2,3
implied. The first term is the kinetic energy density of the RH gauge eigenstates
\nu_{R_{i}}
and the other two terms describe the Yukawa couplings with the SM lepton doublets
L_{i}
, and between the RH neutrinos, respectively. Here,
\tilde{H}=i\sigma^{2}H^{\ast}
and the Yukawa matrices
y^{\nu}
and
y^{M}
are a priori arbitrary. The RH neutrino masses are generated by the breaking of the
B-L
gauge symmetry when
\chi
acquires its vacuum expectation value
\langle\chi\rangle
.

2.2
Active-sterile neutrino mixing

The mixing between the active and RH neutrinos is induced by the Dirac mass matrix
m_{D}=y^{\nu}v/\sqrt{2}
, with the SM Higgs vacuum expectation value
v
. The full
6\times 6
mass matrix in the
(\nu_{L},\nu_{R}^{c})
basis is

\displaystyle{\cal M}=\begin{pmatrix}0&m_{D}\\
m_{D}&M_{R}\end{pmatrix},

(3)

with

\displaystyle m_{D}=\frac{y^{\nu}}{\sqrt{2}}v,\quad M_{R}=\sqrt{2}y^{M}\langle\chi\rangle.

(4)

In the canonical seesaw case,
M_{R}\gg m_{D}
, the light and heavy neutrino mass matrices are

\displaystyle m_{\nu}\sim-m_{D}M^{-1}_{R}m^{T}_{D},\quad m_{N}\sim M_{R},

(5)

with the flavour and mass eigenstates connected by

\displaystyle\begin{pmatrix}\nu_{L}\\
\nu_{R}\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}V_{LL}&V_{LR}\\
V_{RL}&V_{RR}\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}\nu\\
N\end{pmatrix}.

(6)

The above are written schematically in terms of
3\times 3
block matrices and three-component field vectors. For example, the top left element is the SM charged current lepton mixing matrix
V_{LL}=U_{\text{PMNS}}
, apart from small corrections due to non-unitarity. Likewise,
V_{LR}
describes the admixture of the RH neutrinos in the light states. We mainly consider the case of a single generation of light and RH neutrino where the above reduce to a
2\times 2
form. We thus assume that generations decouple and the Yukawa coupling matrix is diagonal (
i=e,\mu,\tau
),

\displaystyle y^{\nu}_{ii}=\frac{\sqrt{2}m_{N_{i}}V_{iN}}{v},

(7)

where
V_{eN}
,
V_{\mu N}
,
V_{\tau N}
are the active-sterile mixing per generation.

2.3
Gauge mixing

In our previous paper
Deppisch:2019kvs
, we did not include a potential kinetic and mass mixing between the SM hypercharge and
B-L
gauge bosons. We here incorporate these effects by including the terms
Dev:2021otb

\displaystyle{\cal L}\supset-\frac{\epsilon}{2}B^{\mu\nu}B^{\prime}_{\mu\nu}+m^{2}_{ZZ^{\prime}}Z^{\mu}Z^{\prime}_{\mu},

(8)

where
B^{\mu\nu}
and
B^{\prime}_{\mu\nu}
are the field strength tensors of SM hypercharge
U(1)_{Y}
and
U(1)_{B-L}
, respectively. The mass mixing term violates the model’s gauge symmetry but may be present if the
U(1)_{B-L}
and electroweak symmetry breaking are not independent of each other. We treat both
\epsilon
and
m_{ZZ^{\prime}}^{2}
as effective, independent parameters at the electroweak scale. The mass
mixing induces a field mixing angle
\alpha
,

\displaystyle\tan\alpha=\frac{m^{2}_{ZZ^{\prime}}}{m_{Z^{\prime}}^{2}-m_{Z}^{2}}.

(9)

In combination, this results in coupling of the SM-like gauge boson to the
B-L
fermion current
J_{B-L}^{\mu}

Lindner:2018kjo
,

\displaystyle{\cal L}\supset-\sin\theta_{ZZ^{\prime}}\cdot Z_{\mu}J_{B-L}^{\mu},

(10)

with the overall mixing angle

\displaystyle\sin\theta_{ZZ^{\prime}}\approx\tan\alpha+\frac{\epsilon\sin\theta_{W}}{m_{Z^{\prime}}^{2}/m_{Z}^{2}-1},

(11)

valid for small
\epsilon
and
\alpha
. Here,
\theta_{W}
is the electroweak mixing angle. The SM and the
B-L
Higgs also mix, induced by the term
\lambda_{3}(H^{\dagger}H)(\chi^{*}\chi)
in Eq. (
2.1
), but this is not relevant for our analysis.

2.4
Right-handed neutrino production and decay

We study the displaced decays of RH neutrinos produced via a
Z^{\prime}
boson,
pp\to Z^{\prime}\to NN
. Both
Z^{\prime}
and
N
have very small widths and in the narrow width approximation we have

\displaystyle\sigma(pp\to Z^{\prime}\to NN\to N+\text{displaced vertex})=

\displaystyle\sigma(pp\to Z^{\prime})\times\text{BR}(Z^{\prime}\to NN)\times\text{BR}(N\to\text{visible}).

(12)

Thus, we consider a final state consisting of a single displaced vertex with charged SM particles. The partial decay width of
Z^{\prime}\to NN
(one generation) is

\displaystyle\Gamma(Z^{\prime}\to NN)=\frac{1}{6}\frac{g_{B-L}^{2}}{4\pi}m_{Z^{\prime}}\left(1-\frac{4m_{N}^{2}}{m_{Z^{\prime}}^{2}}\right)^{3/2}.

(13)

The total
Z^{\prime}
decay width can be similarly calculated by summing over all SM fermions,
Z^{\prime}\to\bar{f}f
and the RH neutrinos
Deppisch:2019kvs
. This simple approximation does not consider hadronization of quark pairs which could be captured using the vector meson dominance assumption
Fujiwara:1984mp
.

Alternatively, we consider production of RH neutrinos through the SM
Z
via gauge mixing,

\displaystyle\sigma(pp\to Z\to NN\to N+\text{displaced vertex})=

\displaystyle\sigma(pp\to Z)\times\text{BR}(Z\to NN)\times\text{BR}(N\to\text{visible}).

(14)

The cross section is
\sigma(pp\to Z)\approx 2111
pb at the 14 TeV LHC
Ogul:2017zjd
, and 1981 pb at the 13 TeV LHC
ATLAS:2016fij
. The branching ratio of
Z
is

\displaystyle\text{BR}(Z\to NN)=\frac{\Gamma(Z\to NN)}{\Gamma(Z_{\text{SM}})+\Gamma(Z\to NN)},

(15)

with

\Gamma(Z_{\text{SM}})\approx 2.50
GeV
ParticleDataGroup:2018ovx
and

\displaystyle\Gamma(Z\to NN)=\frac{1}{6}\frac{g^{2}_{B-L}\sin^{2}\theta_{ZZ^{\prime}}}{4\pi}m_{Z}\left(1-\frac{4m_{N}^{2}}{m_{Z}^{2}}\right)^{3/2}.

(16)

We note that the RH neutrino production cross section is independent of the active-sterile neutrino mixing in this case as well. In addition to the gauge mixing contribution above, there is in principle also a contribution
\sigma(pp\to Z\to NN)\propto V_{lN}^{4}
due to non-unitarity effects but we neglect this for the small active-sterile mixing strengths considered.

As discussed above, we assume flavour-diagonal active-sterile neutrino mixing and we concentrate on one generation of RH neutrinos mixing dominantly with muon flavour through
V_{\mu N}
. Therefore, the
N
has three-body decays
N\to\mu^{\pm}q\bar{q}
and
N\to\mu^{+}\mu^{-}\nu_{\mu}
through off-shell SM
W^{\pm(*)},Z^{(*)}
. The corresponding branching ratios have been calculated in Ref.
Deppisch:2018eth
. Approximately, the resulting decay length for
m_{N}\lesssim m_{Z}
is

\displaystyle L_{N}\approx 0.025~\text{m}\cdot\frac{10^{-12}}{V_{\mu N}^{2}}\cdot\left(\frac{100~\text{GeV}}{m_{N}}\right)^{5}.

(17)

For such small active-sterile mixing, as expected in the canonical seesaw mechanism, e.g.,
V^{2}_{\mu N}\approx 10^{-8}
for
m_{N}=3
GeV, the decay length is of the order of meters, potentially detectable in a displaced vertex experiment such as MAPP.

4
Conclusion

In this work, we have discussed the potential to search for RH neutrinos at the MAPP detector facility. Within the framework of the
U(1)_{B-L}
model, the RH neutrinos are pair-produced, mediated by either the
B-L
gauge boson
Z^{\prime}
or the SM
Z
boson. We have performed an analysis of searching for displaced RH neutrinos from such processes at the MAPP-2 detectors, requiring the RH neutrinos to decay within the decay volume of the detector, namely the MAPP-1 design at the LHC Run-3, and the proposed MAPP-2 design at the HL-LHC. The MAPP-1 detector is too small to have an appreciable sensitivity in our model. Since the MAPP-2 detector is placed roughly 50 meters away from the interaction point and it is protected by rock shielding, the background events are considered to be negligible. We obtain a sensitivity to probe RH neutrino with masses around
m_{N}\lesssim 20-30
GeV. For the
Z^{\prime}
mediated channel, taking the
B-L
gauge coupling
g_{B-L}
at its current experimental limits, i.e.,
g_{B-L}\approx 10^{-3}
, we show that the MAPP-2 detector can probe the interesting seesaw region of light neutrino mass generation where the active-sterile mixing is
V^{2}_{\mu N}\approx 10^{-12}
in such a case if the
Z^{\prime}
boson mass is
m_{Z^{\prime}}\approx(2.5-3.5)\times m_{N}
. The sensitivity of MAPP-2 is interesting as it is located between that of other displaced vertex searches
Acharya:2022nik
and can specifically probe the seesaw regime for such RH neutrinos. We here compare the sensitivity of MAPP-2 with that of the similar proposed detectors CODEX-b, FASER-2 and MATHUSLA. When it comes to the SM
Z
mediated channel, the cross section is controlled by the gauge mixing parameter
\alpha=g_{B-L}\sin\theta_{ZZ^{\prime}}
and the seesaw regime is probed as long as
\alpha\gtrsim 0.005
.

V^{2}_{\mu N}
and coupling with the SM
Z
and

B-L
