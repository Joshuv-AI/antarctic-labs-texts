Poincaré Extension of Möbius Transformations
\name Vladimir V. Kisil

Abstract
Given sphere preserving (Möbius) transformations in n n -dimensional Euclidean space one can use the Poincaré extension to obtain sphere preserving transformations in a half-space of n + 1 n+1 dimensions. The Poincaré extension is usually provided either by an explicit formula or by some geometric construction. We investigate its algebraic background and describe all available options. The solution is given in terms of one-parameter subgroups of Möbius transformations acting on triples of quadratic forms. To focus on the concepts, this paper deals with the Möbius transformations of the real line only.



keywords

Möbius transformation, Poincaré extension, quadratic
forms, Cayley–Klein geometries.
