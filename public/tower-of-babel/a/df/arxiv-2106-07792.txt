Anisotropic Kondo line defect and ODE/IM correspondence
Jingxiang Wu

Abstract
We study the anisotropic Kondo line defects in products of chiral S ​ U ​ ( 2 ) SU(2) WZW models. We propose an ODE/IM correspondence for the anisotropic Kondo problems by considering the four-dimensional Chern Simons theory in the trigonometric case. We verify the claim both by explicit perturbative calculations in the ultraviolet and by exact WKB analysis in the infrared. In doing so, we derived the infrared dynamics of a large class of anisotropic line defects.
