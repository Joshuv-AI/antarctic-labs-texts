Magnetic transition in the J eff = 1 / 2 J_{\textrm{eff}}=1/2 Kagomé system Sm 3 Sb 3 Zn 2 O 14
Vinod Kumar, S. Kundu, M. Baenitz, A. V. Mahajan

Abstract
We present a study of Sm 3 Sb 3 Zn 2 O 14 (SSZO) through magnetization and specific heat capacity C p ​ ( T ) C_{p}(T) measurements. SSZO contains well separated planes of Sm 3+ magnetic ions on a Kagomé lattice. Though our magnetic susceptibility (where the data are limited down to 2 K) does not show any anomaly, the C p ​ ( T ) C_{p}(T) data which have been taken down to 0.35 K reveal a broad maximum at T ∼ 1.5 T\sim 1.5 K followed by a sharp peak at T ∼ 0.5 T\sim 0.5 K indicative of short-range correlations and long-range order, respectively.

IV
Conclusion

In summary, we have prepared a single phase polycrystalline sample of Sm
3
Sb
3
Zn
2
O
14
which has magnetic Kagomé planes formed by Sm
3+
ions. From the Curie constant determined from the low-temperature susceptibility data, we find an effective moment consistent with a pseudo
S=1/2
state. The small value of
\theta_{CW}
(-1.4 K) from the low-temperature Curie-Weiss fit suggests weak antiferromagnetic interactions. Specific heat data (which were taken down to 0.35 K; lower than the 1.8 K limit of
\chi(T)
data) show a sharp anomaly at about 0.5 K which indicates LRO (or possibly a glassy state). Low temperature ac susceptibility measurements might help resolve this. It would also be useful to do local probe measurements like muon spin relaxation to validate this conclusion and to further check for the prevalence of fluctuations in the low-temperature regime.
