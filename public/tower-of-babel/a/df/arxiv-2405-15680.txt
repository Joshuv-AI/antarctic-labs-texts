Introduction

In this paper we prove results on the difference between a normalized
Jensen functional and the sum of other normalized Jensen functionals for
convex function.

Jensen functional is:

J_{n}\left(f,\mathbf{x},\mathbf{p}\right)=\sum_{i=1}^{n}p_{i}f\left(x_{i}\right)-f\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n}p_{i}x_{i}\right).
