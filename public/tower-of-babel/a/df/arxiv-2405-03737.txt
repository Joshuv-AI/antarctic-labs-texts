“Theorem 1. In a Stäckel space, and only in such, there exists a

\begin{split}&\overset{\circ}{g}{}^{ir}=f^{-1}_{\sigma\nu}(u)h^{ir}_{\nu}\left(\overset{\nu}{u}\right),\\

h^{i_{\nu}r_{\nu}}

h^{i_{0}r_{\nu}}

was never published.

Therefore I cannot criticize the “result” of V. Shapovalov, because there is
