Induced Turán problem in bipartite graphs
Maria Axenovich, Jakob Zimmermann

Abstract
The classical extremal function for a graph H H , ex ⁡ ( K n , H ) {\rm ex}(K_{n},H) is the largest number of edges in a subgraph of K n K_{n} that contains no subgraph isomorphic to H H . Note that defining ex ⁡ ( K n , H 9 ind ) {\rm ex}(K_{n},H{\rm\shortminus{ind}}) by forbidding induced subgraphs isomorphic to H H is not very meaningful for a non-complete H H since one can avoid it by considering a clique. For graphs F F and H H , let ex ⁡ ( K n , { F , H 9 ind } ) {\rm ex}(K_{n},\{F,H{\rm\shortminus{ind}}\}) be the largest number of edges in an n n -vertex graph that contains no subgraph isomorphic to F F and no induced subgraph isomorphic to H H . Determining this function asymptotically reduces to finding either ex ⁡ ( K n , F ) {\rm ex}(K_{n},F) or ex ⁡ ( K n , H ) {\rm ex}(K_{n},H) , unless H H is a biclique or both F F and H H are bipartite. Here, we consider the bipartite setting, ex ⁡ ( K n , n , { F , H 9 ind } ) {\rm ex}(K_{n,n},\{F,H{\rm\shortminus{ind}}\}) when K n K_{n} is replaced with K n , n K_{n,n} , F F is a biclique, and H H is a bipartite graph. Our main result, a strengthening of a result by Sudakov and Tomon, implies that for any d ≥ 2 d\geq 2 and any K d , d K_{d,d} -free bipartite graph H H with each vertex in one part of degree either at most d d or a full degree, so that there are at most d − 2 d-2 full degree vertices in that part, one has ex ⁡ ( K n , n , { K t , t , H 9 ind } ) = o ⁡ ( n 2 − 1 / d ) {\rm ex}(K_{n,n},\{K_{t,t},H{\rm\shortminus{ind}}\})=o(n^{2-1/d}) . This provides an upper bound on the induced Turán number for a wide class of bipartite graphs and implies in particular an extremal result for bipartite graphs of bounded VC-dimension by Janzer and Pohoata.

The question about extremal properties of graphs with forbidden induced subgraphs is not new and was studied a lot in the framework of Erdős-Hajnal conjecture
[
9
]
, asserting that any graph

[

]
, see also
[
2
]
for a more quantitative version. For graphs with bounded VC-dimension, it was shown that the conjecture holds by Nguyen, Scott, and Seymour
[

]
, see also a paper by Fox, Pach, and Suk
[

]
. In addition, the so-called Ramsey-Turán number

[

[

[

Lemma 7.1. in Du, Girão, Hunter, McCarty, and Scott

Lemma 1
.

Let
F
be a graph,
H
be a non-empty graph, and
r=\min\{\chi(H),\chi(F)\}
.

1.

Let
r\geq 3
.
If
H
is a complete multipartite graph, then
{\rm ex}(n,\{F,H{\rm\shortminus{ind}}\})=(1-1/(r-1))\binom{n}{2}(1+o(1))
.
Otherwise
{\rm ex}(n,\{F,H{\rm\shortminus{ind}}\})=(1-1/(\chi(F)-1))\binom{n}{2}(1+o(1)).
In either case
{\rm ex}(n,\{F,H{\rm\shortminus{ind}}\})\in\{{\rm ex}(n,H)(1+o(1)),{\rm ex}(n,F)(1+o(1))\}
.

2.

Let
r=2
and
\chi(H)>2
. Then

{\rm ex}(n,F)/2\leq{\rm ex}(n,\{F,H{\rm\shortminus{ind}}\})\leq{\rm ex}(n,F)
.

3.

Let
r=2
,
\chi(F)>2
, and
H
be not complete bipartite. Then

{\rm ex}(n,\{F,H{\rm\shortminus{ind}}\})={\rm ex}(n,F)(1+o(1))
.

Proof.

1.

We have that
F,H\not\subseteq T_{r-1}(n)
, where
T_{r-1}(n)
is the Turán graph with
r-1

{\rm ex}(n,\{F,H{\rm\shortminus{ind}}\})\geq||T_{r-1}(n)||=(1-1/(r-1))\binom{n}{2}(1+o(1))
.
If
H
is not a complete multipartite graph, then
H
is not an induced subgraph of any complete multipartite graph. In particular,
H
is not an induced subgraph of
T_{\chi(F)-1}(n)
. Since
F\not\subseteq T_{\chi(F)-1}(n)
, we have
{\rm ex}(n,\{F,H{\rm\shortminus{ind}}\})\geq||T_{\chi(F)-1}(n)||=(1-1/(\chi(F)-1))\binom{n}{2}(1+o(1))
.

Since
{\rm ex}(n,\{F,H{\rm\shortminus{ind}}\})\leq{\rm ex}(n,F)
, using Erdős-Stone Theorem
we have that

{\rm ex}(n,\{F,H{\rm\shortminus{ind}}\})\leq(1-1/(\chi(F)-1))\binom{n}{2}(1+o(1))
.
If
r=\chi(H)<\chi(F)
and
H
is complete
r

{\rm ex}(n,\{F,H{\rm\shortminus{ind}}\})\leq{\rm ex}(n,T_{r}(t))
, for sufficiently large

. Indeed, if

, then either some part of

induces a subgraph of

that contains

contains an induced copy of

. So,
{\rm ex}(n,\{F,H{\rm\shortminus{ind}}\})\leq(1-1/(r-1))\binom{n}{2}(1+o(1))
.

2.

Let

be bipartite and

not bipartite. Then any

-free bipartite graph doesn’t contain

as a subgraph, so by taking a densest bipartite subgraph of an

-free

. In addition,
{\rm ex}(n,\{F,H{\rm\shortminus{ind}}\})\leq{\rm ex}(n,F).

3.

If

is bipartite, but not complete bipartite, and

, we have that

contains neither

as an induced subgraph, nor

{\rm ex}(n,\{F,H{\rm\shortminus{ind}}\})\geq||T_{r-1}(n)||={\rm ex}(n,F)(1+o(1))

Since

Lemma 2

(Hypergraph Removal Lemma)
.

For any
\epsilon>0
, any integers

, there is

hyperedges to destroy all

Lemma 3

(Kővari-Sós-Turán Theorem,
[
26
]
)
.

If

is a bipartite graph, such that

doesn’t contain a complete bipartite subgraph with

||G||\leq(y_{2}-1)^{1/y_{1}}(|Y_{1}|-y_{1}+1)|Y_{2}|^{1-1/y_{1}}+(y_{1}-1)|Y_{2}|

Lemma 4

(Reduction Lemma)
.

For every
\epsilon\in(0,1)
there is

such that for every

such that for every integer

there is an induced subgraph

vertices such that
||G^{\prime}||\geq\frac{2c}{5}m^{1+\epsilon}
and

is

.

Lemma 5

Let

\epsilon n^{2-1/d}
edges, for some positive
\epsilon
. By considering the max cut of
G
we may assume
G
to be bipartite.
Let
t=\max\{|V^{\prime}|,|U^{\prime}|\}
.
If
G
contains a copy of
K_{t,t}
, it contains a copy of
H
as a subgraph.
Otherwise, by Theorem
1
,
G
contains a copy of
W(k,d,0)
, that in turn contains a copy of
H
.
∎

Proof of Theorem
3
.

Let
k
and
d
be integers such that
t\geq d\geq 3
.
Let
G
be a bipartite graph on partite sets
A,B
of size
n/2
each and having VC-dimension at most
d
with respect to
A
.
Assume that
K_{t,t}\nsubseteq G
and
||G||\geq\epsilon n^{2-\frac{1}{d}}
for some positive constant
\epsilon
.
Then Theorem
1
yields that
G
contains an induced copy of
W(d+1,d,d-2)
with body in
A
, that, by Lemma
6
(2),
contains an induced copy of the incidence graph of a hypergraph with vertex set
[d+1]
and edge set
2^{[d+1]}
.
However, by the definition of VC-dimension, this implies that the VC-dimension of
G
is at least
d+1
, a contradiction.
∎

4
Concluding remarks

In this paper, we provide an upper bound on the largest number of edges in a bipartite graph with
n
vertices in each part, not containing a given bipartite graph
H
as as induced subgraph and not containing a large but fixed biclique, for any
H
that is
K_{d,d}
-free and such that any vertex in one part has degree either at most
d
or a full degree,
d\geq 2
. It remains interesting to determine the induced Turán function of an arbitrary bipartite graph and a bi-clique.
In addition, it would be very nice to resolve a conjecture of Conlon and Lee,
[
6
]
that states that for any
K_{d,d}
-free bipartite graph
H
with maximum degree at most
d
in one part, there are positive constants
C
and
\delta
such that
{\rm ex}(n,H)\leq Cn^{2-1/d-\delta}
.

Lemma
1
shows that in most cases
{\rm ex}(n,\{F,H{\rm\shortminus{ind}}\})
is equal to either
{\rm ex}(n,F)(1+o(1))
or
{\rm ex}(n,H)(1+o(1))
.
There are pairs of graphs
F
and
H
for which it doesn’t hold. For example, consider
F=K_{t,t}
and
H=P_{\ell}
, the path on
\ell
edges,
\ell>1
.
Then
(t-1)n+o(n)\leq{\rm ex}(n,\{K_{t,t},P_{\ell}{\rm\shortminus{ind}}\}\})\leq t^{c}n
, where the lower bound can be seen by considering pairwise vertex-disjoint union of cliques on
2t-1
vertices each and the upper bound follows from a result by Scott, Seymour, and Spirkl
[
32
]
mentioned in the introduction.
On the other hand,
{\rm ex}(n,K_{t,t})\geq cn^{2-2/{t+1}}
and
{\rm ex}(n,P_{\ell})\leq\ell n
. So, when
t
is much larger than
\ell
, we see that

ex(n,\{K_{t,t},P_{\ell}{\rm\shortminus{ind}}\}\})\not\in\{{\rm ex}(n,P_{\ell})(1+o(1)),{\rm ex}(n,K_{t,t})(1+o(1))\}.

However, it is not clear whether

{\rm ex}(n,\{F,H{\rm\shortminus{ind}}\})\in\{\Theta({\rm ex}(n,F)),\Theta({\rm ex}(n,H))\}

for all graphs
F
and
H
.
In the manuscript by Hunter, Milojević, Sudakov, and Tomon
[
20
]
the authors independently state a conjecture similar to our question:

{\rm ex}(n,\{K_{t,t},H{\rm\shortminus{ind}}\})\leq C(H,t){\rm ex}(n,H),

for a constant
C(H,t)
depending only on
H
and
t
.

Note that instead of
{\rm ex}(K_{n},\{K_{t,t},H{\rm\shortminus{ind}}\})
, one can consider
{\rm ex}(K_{n},\{K_{t,t},\mathcal{H}{\rm\shortminus{ind}}\})
for a class of graphs
\mathcal{H}
. When
\mathcal{H}
is the set of all subdivisions of
H
, this problem attracted a lot of attention, starting with a work of Kühn and Osthus
[
27
]
. Recently, the problem has been considered by Girão and Hunter
[
18
]
, McCarty
[
29
]
, Bourneuf, Bucić, Cook, and Davies
[
5
]
, as well as by Du, Girão, Hunter, McCarty, and Scott
[
8
]
.

It is also not clear in general whether
{\rm ex}(K_{n},\{F,H{\rm\shortminus{ind}}\})=O({\rm ex}(K_{n,n},\{F,H{\rm\shortminus{ind}}\}))
. In the non-induced setting it holds by considering a maximal balanced cut.
We would like to conclude with a question whether our main result holds for
K_{n}
instead of
K_{n,n}
:
Is it true that for any non-negative integers
k,r,d,t
with
d\geq r+2
, we have
{\rm ex}(K_{n},\{K_{t,t},W(k,d,r){\rm\shortminus{ind}}\})=o(n^{2-\frac{1}{d}})
?
