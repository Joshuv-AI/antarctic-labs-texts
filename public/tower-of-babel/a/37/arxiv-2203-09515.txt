Highly uniform prime number theorems
Ikuya Kaneko, Jesse Thorner

Abstract
. We prove a highly uniform version of the prime number theorem for a certain class of L L -functions. The range of x x depends polynomially on the analytic conductor, and the error term is expressed in terms of an optimization problem depending explicitly on the available zero-free region. The class contains the Rankin–Selberg L L -function L ⁡ ( s , π × π ′ ) L(s,\pi\times\pi^{\prime}) associated to cuspidal automorphic representations π \pi and π ′ \pi^{\prime} of GL m \mathrm{GL}_{m} and GL m ′ \mathrm{GL}_{m^{\prime}} , respectively. Our main result implies the first uniform prime number theorems for such L L -functions (with analytic conductor uniformity) in complete generality.

Lemma 6.1

If

Proof.

By (
1.4

\eta_{\pi\times\pi^{\prime}}(x)\geq\inf_{u\geq 0}\phi_{x}(u),\qquad\phi_{x}(u)=\frac{\Cr{ZFR}\log x}{(m+m^{\prime})\log(C(\pi)C(\pi^{\prime}))+m(m+m^{\prime})u}+u.

Note that
\lim_{u\to\infty}\phi_{x}(u)=\infty

\displaystyle\phi_{x}(u)

\displaystyle\geq\begin{cases}\phi_{x}(u_{0})&\mbox{if $\displaystyle x>\exp\Big(\frac{m+m^{\prime}}{\Cr{ZFR}m}(\log(C(\pi)C(\pi^{\prime})))^{2}\Big)$,}\\
\phi_{x}(0)&\mbox{if $\displaystyle 3\leq x\leq\exp\Big(\frac{m+m^{\prime}}{\Cr{ZFR}m}(\log(C(\pi)C(\pi^{\prime})))^{2}\Big)$}\end{cases}\vskip 2.84526pt

\displaystyle=\begin{cases}\displaystyle 2\Big(\frac{\Cr{ZFR}\log x}{m(m+m^{\prime})}\Big)^{\frac{1}{2}}-\frac{\log(C(\pi)C(\pi^{\prime}))}{m}&\mbox{if $\displaystyle x>\exp\Big(\frac{m+m^{\prime}}{\Cr{ZFR}m}(\log(C(\pi)C(\pi^{\prime})))^{2}\Big)$,}\\
\displaystyle\frac{\Cr{ZFR}\log x}{(m+m^{\prime})\log(C(\pi)C(\pi^{\prime}))}&\mbox{if $\displaystyle 3\leq x\leq\exp\Big(\frac{m+m^{\prime}}{\Cr{ZFR}m}(\log(C(\pi)C(\pi^{\prime})))^{2}\Big)$}\end{cases}
