Genus Field and Extended Genus Field of an Elementary Abelian Extension of Global Fields.
Juan Carlos Hernandez–Bocanegra, Gabriel Villa–Salvador

Abstract
. In the present work we give the construction of the genus field and the extended genus field of an elementary abelian l l -extension of a field of rational functions, where l l is a prime number. In the Kummer case, if K K is contained in a cyclotomic funtion field, the construction is given using Leopoldt’s ideas by means of Dirichlet characters. Following the definition of Anglès and Jaulent of extended Hilbert class field, we obtain the extended genus field of an elementary abelian l l -extension of a field of rational functions.

1.
Introduction

The study of
genus fields
began with C.F. Gauss in
[
7
]
, in the context of binary quadratic forms. H. Hasse
[
8
]
studied the extended genus field theory for quadratic number field, using characters and class field theory. It was H. W. Leopoldt
[
11
]
who determined the extended genus field
K_{\mathfrak{gex}}
of an abelian extension
K
of
\mathbb{Q}
, and studied the arithmetic of
K
using Dirichlet characters, generalizing Hasse’s work.

For an arbitrary finite extension
K/\mathbb{Q}
, the genus field
K_{\mathfrak{ge}}
of
K
is the composition of
K
and the maximum abelian extension
k^{*}
of
\mathbb{Q}
contained in the Hilbert class field of
K
, that is,
K_{\mathfrak{ge}}=Kk^{*}
. This definition was given by A. Fröhlich
[
6
]
. Similarly, the extended genus field
K_{\mathfrak{gex}}
of
K
, is defined as
K_{\mathfrak{gex}}=KL
where
L/\mathbb{Q}
the maximal abelian extension contained in the extended Hilbert class field of
K
.

For a number field
K
, the
Hilbert class field

K_{H}
of
K
is defined as the maximum abelian extension of
K
unramified at any prime. The
extended Hilbert class field

K_{H^{+}}
of
k
is defined as the maximum abelian extension of
K
unramified at every finite prime, these definitions are without any ambiguity. We have that
K\subseteq K_{\mathfrak{ge}}\subseteq K_{H}
and
\textrm{Gal}(K_{H}/K)
is isomorphic to the class group
Cl_{K}
of
K
. The genus field
K_{\mathfrak{ge}}
corresponds to a subgroup
G_{K}
of
Cl_{K}
. We have
\textrm{Gal}(K_{\mathfrak{ge}}/K)\cong Cl_{K}/G_{K}
. The degree
[K_{\mathfrak{ge}}:K]
is called the
genus number
of
K
and the group
\textrm{Gal}(K_{\mathfrak{ge}}/K)
is known as the
genus group
. Similarly for
K\subseteq K_{\mathfrak{gex}}\subseteq K_{H^{+}}
.

M. Ishida
[
10
]
described the genus field
K_{\mathfrak{ge}}
for any finite extension
K
of
\mathbb{Q}
. X. Zhang
[
20
]
gave a simple expression of
K_{\mathfrak{ge}}
for any number field, using Hilbert’s ramification theory.

In global function fields, there are several definitions of the Hilbert class field depending on the aspect you want to study. Following the ideas of number fields, the definition of the Hilbert class field of a global function field
K
over
\mathbb{F}_{q}
as the maximum unramified abelian extension of
K
, turns out that it contains all constant extensions
K_{m}\coloneqq K\mathbb{F}_{q^{m}}
for any positive integer
m
. Therefore, the direct definition of Hilbert class field as the maximum unramified abelian extension, has the disadvantage of being of infinite degree over the base field.

M. Rosen
[
17
]
gave a definition of the Hilbert class field of
K
. For a fixed finite non-empty set
S_{\infty}
of prime divisors of
K
, the Hilbert class field of
K
is defined as the maximum unramified abelian extension of
K
such that the primes in
S_{\infty}
are totally decomposed. Using this definition, a suitable concept of genus field can be given, as in the case of number fields. It was R. Clement
[
5
]
the first to consider the extended genus field of a cyclic extension
K
of the field of rational functions
k=\mathbb{F}_{q}(T)
of degree a prime number
l
dividing
q-1
. Using class field theory she found the genus field of
K
. S. Bae and J.K. Koo
[
2
]
generalized Clement’s result using Fröhlich’s methods.

G. Peng
[
15
]
described explicitly the genus field of a cyclic Kummer extension of prime degree of a congruence function field using Rosen’s definitions of Hilbert class field. Later on, S. Hu and Y. Li
[
9
]
described the genus field of an Artin-Schreier extension of a field of rational functions.

In
[
12
]
and
[
13
]
, the theory of genus fields was developed for a congruence funtion field using Rosen’s definition of Hilbert class field. The method used is based on Leopoldt’s ideas using Dirichlet’s characters when
K
is contained in a cyclotomic funtion field. If
K
is not contained in a cyclotomic extension and
\mathfrak{p}_{\infty}
is tamely ramified, we consider a suitable extension of constants of
K
and then proceed as before to find
K_{\mathfrak{ge}}
. The method can be used as an application to find explicitly
K_{\mathfrak{ge}}
of an extension
K/k
of prime degree extension that divides
q-1
(Kummer) or
l=p
, where
p
is the characteristic (Artin- Schreier) and also when
K/k
is a
p
-cyclic extension (Witt). Later on, this method was used in
[
4
]
to describe
K_{\mathfrak{ge}}
explicitly when
K/k
is a cyclic extension of degree
l^{n}
, where
l
is a prime number and
l^{n}\big|q-1
under several restrictions.
In
[
14
]
the genus field of a finite and separable extension
K
of
k=\mathbb{F}_{q}(T)
is described as
K_{\mathfrak{ge}}=Kk^{*}
where
k^{*}
is the composition of two fields
k_{1}
and
k_{2}
following the method of Ishida
[
10
]
.

The study of the genus fields of abelian extensions of
k
has been considered recently in
[
3
]
,
[
4
]
,
[
12
]
,
[
13
]
. In
[
3
]
the genus field
K_{ge}
of a finite abelian extension
K
of
k=\mathbb{F}_{q}(T)
is described, in a much more transparent way than in
[
12
]
.

In the present work we characterize the genus field and the extended genus field of an elementary abelian extension
K
of degree
l^{m}
of a rational congruence function field
k=\mathbb{F}_{q}(T)
, where
K=K_{1}\cdots K_{m}
and
\textrm{Gal}(K/k)\cong C_{l}^{m}
, where
C_{l}
denotes the cyclic group of order
l
. Using the ideas obtained in
[
3
]
, the genus field is described when
l\big|(q-1)
and when
l\nmid(q-1)
. The main result of this work describes the genus field and the extended genus field of
K
explicitly. The proof of the main result is constructive beginning with the study of the genus field of a cyclic extension
K/k
of degree
l
; this is Peng’s result
[
15
]
. We generalize Peng’s results to an elementary abelian
l
-extension of degree
l^{m}
.

B. Anglès and J.-F. Jaulent presented in
[
1
]
the general theory of the extended genus field of global fields, either function or numeric. E. Ramírez, M. Rzedowski and G. Villa described in
[
16
]
the extended genus field using a different concept from the one defined by Anglès and Jaulent.

In this paper we are interested in describing the extended genus field for an elementary abelian
l
-extension of degree
l^{m}
. We use the class field theory approach of genus theory given by Anglès and Jaulent and its connection with Dirichlet characters given M. Rzedowski and G. Villa in
[
19
]
, where it is shown that if
K
is contained in a cyclotomic function field, then
K_{\mathfrak{gex}}
is the same field for both approaches. Furthermore, in our situation, the elementary abelian
l
-extensions, the extended genus field obtained by means of the Anglès-Jaulent approach is the same that the one given by Ramírez, Rzedowski and Villa. For more general extensions the extended genus fields might be a different.

2.
Preliminaries and notation

We use the following notation. Let
k=\mathbb{F}_{q}(T)
be a rational congruence funtion field,
\mathbb{F}_{q}
denoting the finite field of
q
elements. Let
R_{T}=\mathbb{F}_{q}[T]
be the ring of polynomials, that is,
R_{T}
is the ring of integers of
k
and let
R_{T}^{+}\coloneqq\{P\in R_{T}\;|\;P\;\text{is monic and irreducible}\}
. The elements of
R_{T}^{+}
are the
finite primes
of
k
and
\mathfrak{p}_{\infty}=\infty
, the pole of the principal divisor
(T)
in
k
, is the
infinite prime
. For
N\in R_{T}\setminus\{0\}
,
\Lambda_{N}
denotes the
N
-torsion of the Carlitz module and
k(\Lambda_{N})
denotes the
N
-th cyclotomic funtion field. The
R_{T}
-module
\Lambda_{N}
is cyclic and
\lambda_{N}
denotes a generator of
\Lambda_{N}
as
R_{T}
-module.

For a finite Galois extension
L/K
of function fields, if
\mathfrak{p}
is a place of
K
and
\mathfrak{P}
is a place above
\mathfrak{p}
, the decomposition and the inertia groups are denoted by
D_{\mathfrak{P}/\mathfrak{p}}(L|K)
and
I_{\mathfrak{P}/\mathfrak{p}}(L|K)
respectively. For any finite extension
K/k
we will use the symbol
S_{\infty}(K)
to denote either one prime or the set of all primes in
K
above
\mathfrak{p}_{\infty}
. Given a cyclotomic function field, a
Dirichlet character
is a group homomorphism
\chi:(R_{T}/(N))^{*}\longrightarrow\mathbb{C}^{*}
and we define the conductor
F_{\chi}
of
\chi
, as the monic polynomial of minimum degree such that
\chi
can be defined modulo
F_{\chi}
,
\chi:(R_{T}/(F_{\chi}))^{*}\longrightarrow\mathbb{C}^{*}
.

Definition 2.1
.

Let
X
be any group of Dirichlet characters. Let
N
be the least common multiple of
\{F_{\chi}\;|\;\chi\in X\}
. Thus

is a subgroup of
\widehat{G_{N}}

. Then

is called

.

For any character

, let

be the canonical decomposition. We have that

has conductor a power of

. We also have that
F_{\chi}=\prod_{P\in R_{T}^{+}}F_{\chi_{P}}
. If
X
is a group of Dirichlet characters, we write
X_{P}\coloneqq\{\chi_{P}\;|\;\chi\in X\}
for

. If

is any extension of

,

and

.

The ramification index of

in

decomposes in

. Furthermore, the inertia and decomposition groups of

coincide. In

\mathfrak{p}_{\infty}
(if

) and the polynomials

Let

as the set of prime divisors dividing

in

Definition 2.2
.

Let

. The genus field

Let

Definition 2.4
.

A Kummer extension is an extension

such that

Let

2.1.
Extended Genus Field

In this section we recall the concept of extended genus field for a geometric extension of congruence function fields
K/k
according to
[
19
]
, where it is obtained the extended genus field of an abelian extension of a rational function field following the definition of Anglès and Jaulent
[

]
.

Let
k=\mathbb{F}_{q}(T)
be a global rational function field. Let
\mathfrak{p}_{\infty}
be the infinite prime of
k
, that is, the pole of
T
and let
k_{\infty}\cong\mathbb{F}_{q}((\frac{1}{T}))
be the completion of

at
\mathfrak{p}_{\infty}
. For

,

can be written uniquely as:

x=\Big(\frac{1}{T}\Big)^{n_{x}}\lambda_{x}\varepsilon_{x}\hskip 8.5359pt\text{with}\hskip 8.5359ptn_{x}\in\mathbb{Z},\hskip 8.5359pt\lambda_{x}\in\mathbb{F}_{q}\hskip 8.5359pt\text{and}\hskip 8.5359pt\varepsilon_{x}\in U_{\infty}^{(1)}

where
U_{\infty}^{(1)}=1+\mathfrak{p}_{\infty}
is the group of 1-th units of
k
.

Definition 2.7
.

The
sign function

\phi_{\infty}:k^{*}_{\infty}\longrightarrow\mathbb{F}_{q}^{*}
is defined as
\phi_{\infty}(x)=\lambda_{x}
for
x\in k_{\infty}^{*}
. The value
\phi_{\infty}(x)
is called the “sign” of
x
.

Definition 2.8
.

Let
L
be a finite separable extension of
k_{\infty}
. We define the sign of
L^{*}
by
\phi_{L}\coloneq\phi_{\infty}\circ N_{L/k_{\infty}}:L^{*}\longrightarrow\mathbb{F}_{q}^{*}
, where

is the norm from
L
to
k_{\infty}
.

Let
\mathcal{O}_{L}=\{x\in L\;|\;v_{\mathfrak{p}}(x)\geq 0,\;\text{for all}\;\mathfrak{p}\nmid\mathfrak{p}_{\infty}\}
,
I_{L}
the group of fractional ideals,
P_{L}=\{(x)=x\mathcal{O}_{L}\;|\;x\in L^{*}\}
and
C_{L}=I_{L}/P_{L}

L^{+}=\{x\in L^{*}\;|\;\phi_{L_{\mathfrak{p}}}(x)=1,\;\text{for all}\;\mathfrak{p}\big|\mathfrak{p}_{\infty}\}

We define the following subgroups of the group of idèles

U_{L}\coloneq\prod_{v\in\mathcal{P}_{\infty}}L_{v}^{*}\times\prod_{v\notin\mathcal{P}_{\infty}}U_{L_{v}},

and

For

we denote by

the Hilbert class field of the global function field

\mathrm{Gal}(L_{H}/L)\cong C_{L}\cong J_{L}/U_{L}L^{*}.

.

Let

,
\mathbb{P}_{L}
denotes the set of prime divisors of

3.
Genus field when
K/k
is a Kummer extension

3.1.
Genus field when
K
is a cyclotomic function field

Let
K/k
be an elementary abelian
l
-extension. Let
K=K_{1}\cdots K_{m}
where
K_{j}/k
is a cyclic extension of degree
l
,
1\leq j\leq m
. When
K/k
is a Kummer extension, each
K_{j}
can be written as a radical extension as follows:
K_{j}=k(\sqrt[l]{\gamma_{j}D_{j}})
for all
1\leq j\leq m
, where
\gamma_{j}\in\mathbb{F}_{q}^{*}
and
D_{j}\in R_{T}=\mathbb{F}_{q}[T]
is an
l
-power free monic polynomial. We obtain from
[
18
, Corolario 9.5.12]
, that
K_{j}=k(\sqrt[l]{\gamma_{j}D_{j}})\subseteq k(\Lambda_{D_{j}})
if and only if
\gamma_{j}\equiv(-1)^{\deg\>D_{j}}\bmod{(\mathbb{F}_{q}^{*})^{l}}
which is equivalent to
\xi_{j}:=(-1)^{\deg\>D_{j}}\gamma_{j}\in(\mathbb{F}_{q}^{*})^{l}
. There are two subcases, namely, whether or not
K
is contained in a cyclotomic function field.

First we consider the case when
K
