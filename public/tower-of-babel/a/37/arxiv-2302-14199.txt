On ψ 5 5 {}_{5}\psi_{5} identities of Bailey
Aritram Dhar

Abstract
. In this paper, we provide proofs of two ψ 5 5 {}_{5}\psi_{5} summation formulas of Bailey using a ϕ 4 5 {}_{5}\phi_{4} identity of Carlitz. We show that in the limiting case, the two ψ 5 5 {}_{5}\psi_{5} identities give rise to two ψ 3 3 {}_{3}\psi_{3} summation formulas of Bailey. Finally, we prove the two ψ 3 3 {}_{3}\psi_{3} identities using a technique initially used by Ismail to prove Ramanujan’s ψ 1 1 {}_{1}\psi_{1} summation formula and later by Ismail and Askey to prove Bailey’s very-well-poised ψ 6 6 {}_{6}\psi_{6} sum.
