Jacob’s ladders, Hardy-Littlewood integral (1918) and new asymptotic functional equations for Euler’s Gamma function together with the tenth equivalent of the Fermat-Wiles theorem
Jan Moser

Abstract
. In this paper new Γ \Gamma -functional is constructed upon the basis of the set of almost linear increments of the Hardy-Littlewood integral. This functional generates a Γ \Gamma -equivalent of the Fermat-Wiles theorem and also new set of factorization formulae for Euler’s Γ \Gamma -function.
