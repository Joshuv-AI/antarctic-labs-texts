Dependence of the coarse wiring profile on the choice of parameter
Ruth Raistrick

Abstract
There are two subgraphs X , Y X,Y of the 2 2 -dimensional integer grid such that for any k < l k<l there is an infinite subset I ⊂ ℕ I\subset\mathbb{N} such that the k k -wiring profile of X X into Y Y grows quadratically on I I while the l l -wiring profile of X X into Y Y grows linearly on I I . This resolves Question 7.8 of Barrett-Hume.

Introduction

In
[
BH21
]
, the authors introduce coarse wirings as a coarse geometric version of thick embeddings defined by Kolmogorov-Barzdin and later considered by Gromov-Guth
[
KB93
,
GG12
]
.

Definition 1.1
.

Let
\Gamma
be a finite graph and let
Y
be a graph. A
wiring
of
\Gamma
into
Y
is a continuous map
f:\Gamma\to Y
which maps vertices to vertices and edges to unions of edges.

A wiring
f
is a
coarse
k
-wiring
if

1.

the restriction of
f
to
V\Gamma
is
\leq k
-to-
1
, i.e.
|\{v\in V\Gamma\mid f(v)=w\}|\leq k
for all
w\in VY
; and

2.

each edge
e\in EY
is contained in at most
k
of the paths in
\mathcal{P}
.

We consider the
image
of a wiring
\textrm{im}(f)
to be the subgraph of
Y
consisting of all vertices and edges in the image of
f
. The
volume
of a wiring
\textrm{vol}(f)
is the number of vertices in its image. We denote by
\textrm{wir}^{k}(\Gamma\to Y)
the minimal volume of a coarse
k
-wiring of
\Gamma
into
Y
. If no such coarse
k
-wiring exists, we say
\textrm{wir}^{k}(\Gamma\to Y)=+\infty
.

Let
X
and
Y
be graphs. The
k
-
wiring profile
of
X
into
Y
is the function

\textrm{wir}^{k}_{X\to Y}(n)=\max\{\textrm{wir}^{k}(\Gamma\to Y)\mid\Gamma\leq X,\ |\Gamma|\leq n\}.

For the graphs considered in
[
BH21
]

\textrm{wir}^{k}_{X\to Y}(n)
does not depend (up to a natural asymptotic equivalence of functions) on the parameter
k
, provided it is chosen to be sufficiently large. We recall that given two functions
f,g:\mathbb{N}\to\mathbb{N}
we write
f\lesssim g
if there exists some constant
C
such that
f(n)\leq Cg(Cn)+C
holds for every
n
. We write
f\simeq g
if
f\lesssim g
and
g\lesssim f
.

The authors ask whether there exist pairs of graphs where for every
k
, there is some
l>k
such that
\textrm{wir}^{k}_{X\to Y}(n)\not\simeq\textrm{wir}^{l}_{X\to Y}(n)

[
BH21
, Question 7.8]
. The goal of this paper is to prove that there are.

Theorem 1.2
.

There are two subgraphs
X,Y
of the
2
-dimensional integer grid such that, for every
k\geq 2
there is an infinite subset
I_{k}\subset\mathbb{N}
such that for all
n\in I_{k}

(a)

\textrm{wir}^{k-1}_{X\to Y}(n)\geq\left(\frac{n}{k(k+1)}\right)^{2}
, and

(b)

\textrm{wir}^{k}_{X\to Y}(n)\leq 2n
.

The remainder of the paper is split into three short sections. The first contains the definitions of the graphs
X
and
Y
, the second contains the proof of part (a) of the main theorem, while the third contains the proof of part (b).
