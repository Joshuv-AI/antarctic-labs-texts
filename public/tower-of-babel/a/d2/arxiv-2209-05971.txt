Affine BPS algebras, W algebras, and the cohomological Hall algebra of 𝔸 2 \mathbb{A}^{2} Thanks: I was supported by the European Research Council starting grant “Categorified Donaldson–Thomas theory” No. 759967 and a Royal Society University Research Fellowship during the writing of this paper.
Ben Davison

Abstract
. We introduce affinizations and deformations of the BPS Lie algebra associated to a tripled quiver with its canonical cubic potential, and use them to precisely determine the T T -equivariant cohomological Hall algebra 𝒜 𝔸 2 T \CoHA_{\mathbb{A}^{2}}^{T} of compactly supported coherent sheaves on 𝔸 ℂ 2 \mathbb{A}_{\mathbb{C}}^{2} , acted on by a torus T T . In particular we show that this algebra is spherically generated for all T T .

1.2.
Donaldson–Thomas theory

Donaldson–Thomas theory
[
Tho00
]
, is a theory that assigns to a projective Calabi–Yau threefold
X
, a Chern class
\upalpha\in\HO(X,\mathbb{Z})
, and a stability condition
\upzeta

\omega^{\upzeta}_{\upalpha}
“counting”
\upzeta

X

\upalpha

\mathcal{M}_{\upalpha}^{\upzeta}(X)
of such sheaves will generally have strictly positive dimension, i.e. there are infinitely many such sheaves, and so
\omega_{\upalpha}^{\upzeta}
is defined by first constructing a virtual fundamental class of the right expected dimension (zero) and then taking its degree.

Subsequently it was realised by Kai Behrend
[
Beh09
]
that every quasi-projective complex scheme
Y
carries a canonical
\mathbb{Z}
-valued constructible function
\upnu_{Y}
, and the above virtual count can be computed as the Euler characteristic of
Y=\mathcal{M}_{\upalpha}^{\upzeta}(X)
, weighted by
\upnu_{Y}
. Furthermore, in the event that a scheme
Y
can be written
1

as the critical locus of a function

d

, its
\upnu_{Y}
-weighted Euler characteristic is equal to the Euler characteristic of the vanishing cycle cohomology
\HO(U,{}^{\mathfrak{p}}\!\upphi_{f}\mathbb{Q}^{\vir})

, the cohomological shift of the locally constant sheaf on

. Firstly, these facts give us a way to define DT invariants for noncompact moduli spaces, and secondly, they suggest a natural (partial) “categorification” of the DT invariant: instead of considering the

1.3.

Let

1.4.
Results

The algebra
W_{1+\infty}
is defined to be the universal central extension of the Lie algebra of algebraic differential operators on the complex torus
\mathbb{C}^{*}
. A basis for
W_{1+\infty}
is provided by the set

\{z^{m}D^{n}\;\lvert\;m\in\mathbb{Z},n\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}\}\cup\{c\}

where we set

, and

accounts for the central extension, which will not feature in this paper. We use this basis to consider

as a Lie algebra over
\mathbb{Q}
. We define

(1)

W_{1+\infty}^{+}=\mathrm{Span}_{\mathbb{Q}}(z^{m}D^{n}\;\lvert\;m\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 1},n\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}),

which is a Lie subalgebra of
W_{1+\infty}
. The Lie bracket is given by

(2)

[z^{m}D^{a},z^{n}D^{b}]=z^{m+n}((D+n)^{a}D^{b}-D^{a}(D+m)^{b}).

The Lie algebra
W^{+}_{1+\infty}
is
\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}
-graded, with
n
th graded piece spanned by the operators
z^{n}D^{a}
for
a\geq 0
. The Lie algebra
W^{+}_{1+\infty}
has a
\mathbb{Z}
-filtration given by setting
3
3

3

F_{i}W^{+}_{1+\infty}=\mathrm{Span}(z^{m}D^{a}\;\lvert\;0\leq a\leq(i+2)/2,m\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 1}).

We denote by
\Gr^{F}_{\bullet}\!W_{1+\infty}^{+}
the associated graded Lie algebra. From (
2
), the induced Lie bracket on
\Gr^{F}_{\bullet}\!W_{1+\infty}^{+}
is nontrivial; it is given by

So the associated graded Lie algebra

with skew-symmetric form

(for various

3.10.
Affinizing BPS Lie algebras

For pairs
(\tilde{Q},\tilde{W})
of a tripled quiver with potential there is extra structure on the CoHA

, ultimately derived from a factorization structure on the stack of representations of the Jacobi algebra. We briefly describe this here; full details for more general 3-Calabi–Yau completions of 2-Calabi–Yau categories will appear in joint work with Hennecart, Kinjo, Schiffmann and Vasserot. In the special case in which

is the one-loop quiver, i.e. the case we need for the main results of this paper, this structure can be extracted from
[

]
via dimensional reduction.

Under the isomorphism

from (

[

, Lem.4.1]
) that for

and

be disjoint open balls in

\displaystyle\HO(\mathfrak{M}^{U^{\prime}\coprod U^{\prime\prime}}(\tilde{Q}),{}^{\mathfrak{p}}\!\upphi_{\Tr(\tilde{W})}\mathbb{Q}^{\vir})

\displaystyle\cong\HO(\mathfrak{M}^{U^{\prime}}(\tilde{Q}),{}^{\mathfrak{p}}\!\upphi_{\Tr(\tilde{W})}\mathbb{Q}^{\vir})\otimes\HO(\mathfrak{M}^{U^{\prime\prime}}(\tilde{Q}),{}^{\mathfrak{p}}\!\upphi_{\Tr(\tilde{W})}\mathbb{Q}^{\vir})

3.11.
Affinized finite type BPS algebras

Let
Q

\hat{\mathfrak{g}}_{\tilde{Q},\tilde{W}}
. We start with an easy lemma:

Lemma 3.15
.

Let
\mathcal{H}
be an algebra object in
\Heis\lmod_{\mathbb{Z}}
, such that there is an isomorphism of underlying
\Heis_{\mathbb{Z}}
-modules

\mathcal{H}\cong\bigoplus_{m\in 2\cdot\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 1}}\Gr_{\bullet}^{F}(V_{[m]})[-2]^{\oplus a_{m}}

for integers

. Then up to isomorphism,
\mathcal{H}
is determined by
\mathcal{H}^{0}
, the summand of
\mathcal{H}
of cohomological degree zero, considered as an ordinary associative algebra. Similarly, if
\mathfrak{g}
is a Lie algebra object in
\Heis\lmod_{\mathbb{Z}}
such that there is an isomorphism of underlying
\Heis_{\mathbb{Z}}
-modules

\mathfrak{g}\cong\bigoplus_{m\in 2\cdot\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 1}}\Gr_{\bullet}^{F}(V_{[m]})[-2]^{\oplus b_{m}}

for integers
b_{m}
, then
\mathfrak{g}
is determined up to isomorphism by
\mathfrak{g}^{0}
, considered as an ordinary Lie algebra.

Proof.

Under the conditions of the lemma,
\mathcal{H}
is concentrated in cohomological degrees indexed by
2\!\cdot\!\mathbb{N}
. Moreover, the lowering operator

is injective for

\mathcal{H}^{\geq 2}
is spanned by elements

, with

, on which

acts by sending

are not both of cohomological degree zero,
\upalpha\star\upbeta
is determined by

, and the result follows by induction. The proof for
\mathfrak{g}
is identical.
∎

Given a
\mathbb{N}^{Q_{0}}
-graded Lie algebra
\mathfrak{g}
we form the
\mathbb{N}^{Q_{0}}
-graded Lie algebra
\mathfrak{g}\otimes_{\mathbb{Q}}\mathbb{Q}[D]

\mathbb{Q}[D]
-linear extension. We make this into a
\Heis_{Q}
-module by setting

, for

\mathbb{N}^{Q_{0}}
-degree

. The
\Heis_{Q}
-module
\mathfrak{g}\otimes_{\mathbb{Q}}\mathbb{Q}[D]

\Heis
-module structure via (

\mathbb{Z}
-graded by (doubling the)

-degree. For

a finite type ADE Dynkin diagram with associated Lie algebra
\mathfrak{g}
and negative half
