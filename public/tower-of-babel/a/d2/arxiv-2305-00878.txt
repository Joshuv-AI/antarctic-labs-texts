On the Continuous embeddings between the fractional Hajłasz-Orlicz-Sobolev spaces
Azeddine BAALAL, Mohamed BERGHOUT, EL-Houcine OUALI

Abstract
. Let G G be an Orlicz function and let α , β , s \alpha,\beta,s be positive real numbers. 1.
Introduction

A metric-measure space
(X,d,\mu)
is a metric space
(X,d)
with a Borel measure
\mu
such that
0<\mu(B(x,r))<\infty
for all
x\in X
and all
r\in\left(0,\infty\right)
. We will always assume
that metric spaces have at least two points. Sobolev spaces on metric measure spaces have been studied during the last two decades see e.g
[
5
,
9
,
11
]
. The theory was generalized to Orlicz-Sobolev spaces on metric-measure spaces in
[
2
,
13
,
18
,
19
,
20
]
. These spaces play an important role in the so-called area of strongly nonlinear potential theory
[
2
]
. Continuous and compact embeddings play a central role in the theory of Sobolev spaces. Those issues have been investigated by many researchers, we refer to
[
1
,
12
,
6
,
23
,
3
,
9
,
10
,
14
,
15
,
8
]
.

The objective of this paper is to study the continuous embeddings of the fractional Hajłasz-Orlicz-Sobolev spaces.

Let
(X,d,\mu)
be a metric-measure space and
G
be an Orlicz function (cf. Section
2
for precise definition). We define the Orlicz space
L^{G}(X,\mu)
as follows

L^{G}(X,\mu):=\left\{u:X\rightarrow\mathbb{R}\textit{ measurable; }\Phi_{G}(u)<+\infty\right\},

where the modular
\Phi_{G}
is defined as follows

\displaystyle\Phi_{G}(u):=\int_{X}G(|u(x)|)d\mu(x).

The Orlicz space
L^{G}(X,\mu)
is a Banach space with the following norm, called the Luxemburg norm

\|u\|_{L^{G}(X,\mu)}:=\inf\left\{\lambda>0;\Phi_{G}\left(\frac{u}{\lambda}\right)\leq 1\right\}.

Let
\alpha,s>0
, we define the fractional order Orlicz-Sobolev space
W_{s}^{\alpha,G}(X,d,\mu)
as follows

W_{s}^{\alpha,G}(X,d,\mu):=\left\{u\in L^{G}(X,\mu);\Phi^{\alpha,G}_{s}(u)<+\infty\right\},

where the modular
\Phi^{\alpha,G}_{s}
is defined as

\displaystyle\Phi^{\alpha,G}_{s}(u):=\iint_{0<d(x,y)<1}G\left(\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|}{d(x,y)^{\alpha}}\right)\frac{d\mu(y)d\mu(x)}{d(x,y)^{s}}.

The space
W_{s}^{\alpha,G}(X,d,\mu)
is a Banach space with the following norm

\|u\|_{W_{s}^{\alpha,G}(X,d,\mu)}:=\|u\|_{L^{G}(X,\mu)}+[u]_{W_{s}^{\alpha,G}(X,d,\mu)},

(1.1)

where
[.]_{W_{s}^{\alpha,G}(X,d,\mu)}
is the Gagliardo seminorm, defined by

[u]_{W_{s}^{\alpha,G}(X,d,\mu)}:=\inf\left\{\lambda>0;\Phi^{\alpha,G}_{s}\left(\frac{u}{\lambda}\right)\leq 1\right\}.

(1.2)

Furthermore, we say that
u\in\dot{W}_{s}^{\alpha,G}(X,d,\mu)
if
u
is finite almost everywhere and
[u]_{W_{s}^{\alpha,G}(X,d,\mu)}<\infty
.

Let
\beta>0
, we say that a function
v\in L^{G}(X,\mu)
belongs to the fractional Hajłasz-Orlicz-Sobolev space
M^{\beta,G}(X,d,\mu)
if there exists a non-negative function
h\in L^{G}(X,\mu)
, called a generalized
\beta
-gradient of
v
, such that

\left|v(x)-v(y)\right|\leq d^{\beta}(x,y)(h(x)+h(y))\quad\text{for}\ \mu\text{-almost all}\ x,y\in X.

We equip the space
M^{\beta,G}(X,d,\mu)
with the norm

\left\|v\right\|_{M^{\beta,G}(X,d,\mu)}:=\|v\|_{L^{G}(X,\mu)}+\inf\|h\|_{L^{G}(X,\mu)},

where the infimum is taken over all the generalized
\beta
-gradients of
v
. When
G(t)=t^{p}
with
p>1
, those spaces were introduced by Hajłasz for
\alpha=1
(see
[
9
]
) and by Yang for
\alpha\neq 1
(see
[
21
]
). For more details on these spaces, we refer to
[
3
,
10
,
11
,
9
,
22
,
17
,
21
]
.

The classical Sobolev embedding theorems for
W^{s,p}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
have different character when
sp<n
,
sp=n
, or
sp>n
, see
[
6
]
. Therefore, in the metric-measure context, in order to prove embedding theorems, we need a condition that would be the counterpart of the dimension of the space. It turns out that such a condition is provided by the lower bound and upper bound for the growth of the measure.

We say that the metric-measure space
(X,d,\mu)
is lower Ahlfors
s
-regular if there exists some positive constant
b
such that

\mu(B(z,r))\geq\frac{1}{b}r^{s}\text{ for }r\in(0,1],z\in X.

(1.3)

On the other hand, we say that
(X,d,\mu)
is upper Ahlfors
s
-regular if there is some positive constant
b
such that

\mu(B(z,r))\leq br^{s}\text{ for }r\in(0,1],z\in X.

(1.4)

For example, let
\theta>2
and

\Omega:=\left\{(x,y)\in\mathbb{R}^{2}:0<y<1\text{ and }|x|<y^{\theta-1}\right\}.

Then the metric-measure space
(\Omega,d_{\mathbb{R}^{2}},\lambda_{2})
is lower
\theta
-regular, see
[
8
]
. Here we regard
\Omega
as a metric-measure space with the Euclidean metric
d_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}
, and the Lebesgue measure
\lambda_{2}
.

The first main result of this paper is the Hölder regularity for the Sobolev functions when
\alpha p_{0}>s
, see Theorem
3.1
and Theorem
3.2
. The second main result of our paper is a continuous embedding between the spaces
W_{s}^{\alpha,G}(X,d,\mu)
and
M^{\beta,G}(X,d,\mu)
, see Theorem
3.3
.

This paper is organized as follows. In Section
2
, we give some useful properties for Orlicz functions. The proof of the main results is given in Section
3
.

2.
Orlicz functions and some preliminaries results

In this section, we prove several useful properties of the Orlicz functions, which will be used throughout this paper. We start by recalling the definition of the well-known Orlicz functions.

Definition 2.1
.

A function
G:\mathbb{R}_{+}\rightarrow\mathbb{R}_{+}
is called an Orlicz function if it has the following properties :

\left(H_{1}\right)

G
is continuous, convex, increasing and
G(0)=0
.

\left(H_{2}\right)

G
satisfies the
\Delta_{2}
condition, that is, there exists
C>2
such that
G(2x)\leq CG(x)
for all
x\in\mathbb{R}_{+}
.

\left(H_{3}\right)

G
is super-linear at zero, that is
\displaystyle\lim_{x\rightarrow 0}\frac{G(x)}{x}=0
.

It is easy to check that Orlicz functions fulfill the following basic properties. See
[
7
]
for a proof of these facts.

\left(P_{1}\right)

(Regularity)
G
is Lipschitz continuous.

\left(P_{2}\right)

G
can be represented in the form
\displaystyle G(x)=\int_{0}^{x}g(s)ds,

where
g
is a non-decreasing right continuous function.

\left(P_{3}\right)

(Subadditivity) Given
a,b\in\mathbb{R}_{+}
,

G(a+b)\leq\frac{C}{2}(G(a)+G(b))
, where
C>0
is the constant in the
\Delta_{2}
condition.

\left(P_{4}\right)

For any
0<b<1
and
a>0
, it holds
G(ab)\leq bG(a)
.

A typical example of the Orlicz function is
G(t)=t^{p}
with
p>1
, we refer to
[
16
]
for more examples of Orlicz functions.

Throughout this paper, we assume that

1<p_{0}:=\inf_{s>0}\frac{sg(s)}{G(s)}\leq p^{0}:=\sup_{s>0}\frac{sg(s)}{G(s)}<+\infty.

(2.1)

Lemma 2.2
.

Let
G
be an Orlicz function and assume that
(\ref{2eq1})
is satisfied. Then the following inequalities hold.

G(\gamma t)\geq\gamma^{p_{0}}G(t)\textit{ for all }t>0\textit{ and all }\gamma\geq 1,

(2.2)

G(\gamma t)\geq\gamma^{p^{0}}G(t)\textit{ for all }t>0\textit{ and all }\gamma\in(0,1],

(2.3)

G(\gamma t)\leq\gamma^{p^{0}}G(t)\textit{ for all }t>0\textit{ and all }\gamma\geq 1,

(2.4)

G(t)\leq\gamma^{p_{0}}G\left(\frac{t}{\gamma}\right)\textit{ for all }t>0\textit{ and all }\gamma\in(0,1].

(2.5)

For
\gamma\neq 1
, the proof of the lemma is given in
[
4
, Lemma 2.2]
. For the convenience of the reader we present it here.

Proof.

If
\gamma=1
, the previous inequalities are trivial. Suppose that
\gamma\neq 1
,
since
p_{0}\leq\frac{tg(t)}{G(t)}
for all
t>0
, it follows that for letting
\gamma>1
, we have

\log\left(G(\gamma t)\right)-\log\left(G(t)\right)=\int_{t}^{\gamma t}\frac{g(\tau)}{G(\tau)}d\tau\geq\int_{t}^{\gamma t}\frac{p_{0}}{\tau}d\tau=\log\left(\gamma^{p_{0}}\right).

Thus, we deduce

G(\gamma t)\geq\gamma^{p_{0}}G(t)\text{ for all }t>0\text{ and }\gamma>1.

(2.6)

Now, we show that
G(\gamma t)\leq\gamma^{p^{0}}G(t)\quad
for all
t>0
and
\gamma>1
.
Indeed, since
p^{0}\geq\frac{tg(t)}{G(t)}
for all
t>0
, it follows that for all
\gamma>1
, we have

\log(G(\gamma t))-\log(G(t))=\int_{t}^{\gamma t}\frac{g(\tau)}{G(\tau)}d\tau\leq\int_{t}^{\gamma t}\frac{p^{0}}{\tau}d\tau=\log\left(\gamma^{p^{0}}\right).

Thus, we deduce

G(\gamma t)\leq\gamma^{p^{0}}G(t)\text{ for all }t>0\text{ and }\gamma>1.

(2.7)

By the same argument in the proof of (
2.6
) and (
2.7
), we have

G(\gamma t)\geq\gamma^{p^{0}}G(t)\textit{ for all }t>0\textit{ and }\gamma\in(0,1),

(2.8)

and

G(t)\leq\gamma^{p_{0}}G\left(\frac{t}{\gamma}\right)\textit{ for all }t>0\textit{ and }\gamma\in(0,1).

(2.9)

This concludes the proof.
∎

Proposition 2.3
.

Let
s,\alpha>0
,
G
be an Orlicz function and suppose that
(X,d,\mu)
is an upper Ahlfors
\theta
-regular space, where
\alpha p^{0}<\theta-s
. Then,
W_{s}^{\alpha,G}(X,d,\mu)=L^{G}(X,\mu)
with equivalent norms.

Proof.

Let
u\in L^{G}(X,\mu)
and
\lambda>0
, then by using the Fubini’s Theorem and the inequality (
2.4
) we have

\displaystyle\Phi^{\alpha,G}_{s}\left(\frac{u}{\lambda}\right)

\displaystyle=\iint_{0<d(x,y)<1}G\left(\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|}{\lambda d(x,y)^{\alpha}}\right)\frac{d\mu(y)d\mu(x)}{d(x,y)^{s}}

\displaystyle\leq\iint_{0<d(x,y)<1}G\left(\frac{2|u(x)|}{2\lambda d(x,y)^{\alpha}}+\frac{2|u(y)|}{2\lambda d(x,y)^{\alpha}}\right)\frac{d\mu(y)d\mu(x)}{d(x,y)^{s}}

\displaystyle\leq 2^{p^{0}}\int_{X}\int_{B(x,1)\backslash\{x\}}G\left(\frac{|u(x)|}{\lambda d(x,y)^{\alpha}}\right)\frac{d\mu(y)d\mu(x)}{d(x,y)^{s}}

\displaystyle+2^{p^{0}}\int_{X}\int_{B(y,1)\backslash\{y\}}G\left(\frac{|u(y)|}{\lambda d(x,y)^{\alpha}}\right)\frac{d\mu(x)d\mu(y)}{d(x,y)^{s}}

\displaystyle\leq 2^{p^{0}+1}\int_{X}G\left(\frac{|u(x)|}{\lambda}\right)\int_{B(x,1)\backslash\{x\}}\frac{1}{d(x,y)^{s+\alpha p^{0}}}d\mu(y)d\mu(x)

\displaystyle\leq 2^{s+\alpha p^{0}+p^{0}+1}\int_{X}G\left(\frac{|u(x)|}{\lambda}\right)\left(\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\mu\left(B\left(x,2^{-k}\right)\right)2^{k(s+\alpha p^{0})}\right)d\mu(x)

\displaystyle\leq b2^{s+\alpha p^{0}+p^{0}+1}\int_{X}G\left(\frac{|u(x)|}{\lambda}\right)d\mu(x)\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}2^{k(\alpha p^{0}+s-\theta)}

\displaystyle=\frac{b2^{s+\alpha p^{0}+p^{0}+1}}{1-2^{\alpha p^{0}+s-\theta}}\int_{X}G\left(\frac{|u(x)|}{\lambda}\right)d\mu(x),

and this implies that

[u]_{W^{\alpha,G}_{s}(X,d,\mu)}\leq\beta\|u\|_{L^{G}(X,d,\mu)},\textit{ with }\beta=\frac{b2^{s+\alpha p^{0}+p^{0}+1}}{1-2^{\alpha p^{0}+s-\theta}},

where we have used the decomposition
B(x,1)\backslash\{x\}=

\bigcup_{k=0}^{\infty}B\left(x,2^{-k}\right)\backslash B\left(x,2^{-(k+1)}\right).

This completes the proof.
∎

In the sequel, let
(X,d,\mu)
be a lower Ahlfors s-regular space and let
B
be the ball with the center
z\in X
and the radius
R>0
.

The median value of a measurable function
u
finite a.e. on
B
is defined by

m_{u}(B)=\max\left\{t\in\mathbb{R}:\mu(\{x\in B:u(x)<t\})\leq\frac{\mu(B)}{2}\right\}.

From
[
8
]
, we have the following result.

Proposition 2.4
.

Let
u
be a measurable function finite a.e. on
B
, then

(a)

if
c\in\mathbb{R}
, then
m_{u}(B)-c=m_{u-c}(B)
,

(b)

if
c>0
, then
cm_{u}(B)=m_{cu}(B)
.

Lemma 2.5
.

Let
G
be an Orlicz function and let
u
be a measurable function finite a.e. Then, for all
c\in\mathbb{R}
, we have

\left|m_{u}(B)-c\right|\leq G^{-1}\left(2\fint_{B}G\left(|u(x)-c|\right)d\mu(x)\right).

(2.10)

Proof.

Let us first prove the following inequality

\left|m_{u}(B)\right|\leq m_{|u|}(B).

(2.11)

If
m_{u}(B)>0
, then we have

\left\{x\in B:|u(x)|<m_{u}(B)\right\}\subseteq\left\{x\in B:u(x)<m_{u}(B)\right\},

thus the inequality (
2.11
) follows.

If
m_{u}(B)\leq 0
, then we have

\left\{x\in B:|u(x)|<\left|m_{u}(B)\right|\right\}\subseteq\left\{x\in B:u(x)>m_{u}(B)\right\},

(2.12)

and by the definition of the median value we have

\mu\left(\left\{x\in B:u(x)\leq m_{u}(B)\right\}\right)\geq\frac{\mu(B)}{2}.

(2.13)

Hence from the inclusion (
2.12
) and the inequality (
2.13
) we get (
2.11
).

Now, let us show the following inequality

m_{|u-c|}(B)\leq G^{-1}\left(2\fint_{B}G\left(|u(x)-c|\right)d\mu(x)\right).

(2.14)

Let
\eta>2
and
\delta=\fint_{B}G\left(|u(x)-c|\right)d\mu(x)
. Then by general Chebyshev’s inequality, we have

\displaystyle\mu\left(\left\{x\in B:|u(x)-c|\geq G^{-1}\left(\eta\delta\right)\right\}\right)

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{\eta\delta}\int_{B}G\left(|u(x)-c|\right)d\mu(x)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\eta}\mu(B)<\frac{1}{2}\mu(B).

Thus,

m_{|u-c|}(B)\leq G^{-1}\left(\eta\fint_{B}G\left(|u(x)-c|\right)d\mu(x)\right).

Then by passing to the limit
\eta\rightarrow 2
we get (
2.14
).

Hence, by Proposition
2.4
and the inequalities (
2.11
) and (
2.14
) we obtain the result.
∎

Corollary 2.6
.

Let
G
be an Orlicz function and let
u
be a measurable function finite a.e. Then for all
t
such that
|u(t)|<\infty
we get

G\left(\left|m_{u}(B(z,r))-u(t)\right|\right)\leq 2br^{-s}\int_{B(z,r)}G\left(|u(t)-u(y)|\right)d\mu(y),

(2.15)

where
z\in X
and
r\leq 1
.

Now let us prove the following lemma.

Lemma 2.7
.

Let
G
be an Orlicz function and let
u\in\dot{W}_{s}^{\alpha,G}(X,d,\mu)
for some
\alpha>0
. Then, there exists a null set
H
such that
\lim_{r\rightarrow 0}m_{u}(B(x,r))=u(x)
for all
x\in X\backslash H
.

Proof.

Let us consider the following set

H=\left\{x\in X:\int_{B(x,1)}G\left(\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|}{d(x,y)^{\alpha}}\right)\frac{d\mu(y)}{d(x,y)^{s}}=+\infty\text{ or }|u(x)|=+\infty\right\}.

By the Fubini’s Theorem, we have
\displaystyle\int_{B(x,1)}G\left(\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|}{d(x,y)^{\alpha}}\right)\frac{d\mu(y)}{d(x,y)^{s}}\in L^{1}(X,\mu)
, then
\mu(H)=0
. Hence, for all
x\in X\backslash H
and
r\leq 1
, by (
2.15
) and
(
2.5
)
we have

\displaystyle G\left(\left|u(x)-m_{u}(B(x,r))\right|\right)

\displaystyle\leq 2br^{-s}\int_{B(x,r)}G\left(|u(x)-u(y)|\right)d\mu(y)

\displaystyle\leq 2br^{-s}\int_{B(x,r)}G\left(\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|}{d(x,y)^{\alpha}}\right)d(x,y)^{\alpha p_{0}+s}\frac{d\mu(y)}{d(x,y)^{s}}

\displaystyle\leq 2br^{\alpha p_{0}}\int_{B(x,1)}G\left(\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|}{d(x,y)^{\alpha}}\right)\frac{d\mu(y)}{d(x,y)^{s}}.

Since
\displaystyle 2br^{\alpha p_{0}}\int_{B(x,1)}G\left(\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|}{d(x,y)^{\alpha}}\right)\frac{d\mu(y)}{d(x,y)^{s}}
tends to
0
as
r
tends to
0
, the proof follows.
∎

.

Let
G
be an Orlicz function and let
\alpha>0

,

\displaystyle G\left(\left|m_{u}\left(B\left(z,r_{1}\right)\right)-m_{u}\left(B\left(z,r_{2}\right)\right)\right|\right)

\displaystyle\leq 4b^{2}2^{s+\alpha p_{0}}[u]_{W^{\alpha,G}_{s}(X,d,\mu)}^{p_{0}}\left(r_{1}^{\alpha p_{0}-s}+\frac{r_{2}^{\alpha p_{0}}}{r_{1}^{s}}\right)

if

, and

\displaystyle G\left(\left|m_{u}\left(B\left(z,r_{1}\right)\right)-m_{u}\left(B\left(z,r_{2}\right)\right)\right|\right)

(2.17)

\displaystyle\leq 4b^{2}2^{s+\alpha p_{0}}[u]_{W^{\alpha,G}_{s}(X,d,\mu)}^{p^{0}}\left(r_{1}^{\alpha p_{0}-s}+\frac{r_{2}^{\alpha p_{0}}}{r_{1}^{s}}\right)

.

Proof.

By using the inequalities (

\displaystyle G\left(\left|m_{u}\left(B\left(z,r_{1}\right)\right)-m_{u}\left(B\left(z,r_{2}\right)\right)\right|\right)

(2.18)

\displaystyle=4b^{2}\frac{\left(r_{1}+r_{2}\right)^{s}}{r_{2}^{s}}\frac{\left(r_{1}+r_{2}\right)^{\alpha p_{0}}}{r_{1}^{s}}\iint_{0<d(x,y)<1}G\left(\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|}{d(x,y)^{\alpha}}\right)\frac{d\mu(y)d\mu(x)}{d(x,y)^{s}}

[u]_{W^{\alpha,G}_{s}(X,d,\mu)}=0

[u]_{W^{\alpha,G}_{s}(X,d,\mu)}\in(0,1]

\displaystyle\times[u]_{W^{\alpha,G}_{s}(X,d,\mu)}^{p_{0}}\iint_{0<d(x,y)<1}G\left(\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|}{[u]_{W^{\alpha,G}_{s}(X,d,\mu)}d(x,y)^{\alpha}}\right)\frac{d\mu(y)d\mu(x)}{d(x,y)^{s}}

\displaystyle\leq 4b^{2}2^{s+\alpha p_{0}}[u]_{W^{\alpha,G}_{s}(X,d,\mu)}^{p_{0}}\left(r_{1}^{\alpha p_{0}-s}+\frac{r_{2}^{\alpha p_{0}}}{r_{1}^{s}}\right).

[u]_{W^{\alpha,G}_{s}(X,d,\mu)}\geq 1

2.4
) we have

\displaystyle G\left(\left|m_{u}\left(B\left(z,r_{1}\right)\right)-m_{u}\left(B\left(z,r_{2}\right)\right)\right|\right)

\displaystyle\leq 4b^{2}2^{s+\alpha p_{0}}\left(r_{1}^{\alpha p_{0}-s}+\frac{r_{2}^{\alpha p_{0}}}{r_{1}^{s}}\right)

\displaystyle\times\iint_{0<d(x,y)<1}G\left([u]_{W^{\alpha,G}_{s}(X,d,\mu)}\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|}{[u]_{W^{\alpha,G}_{s}(X,d,\mu)}d(x,y)^{\alpha}}\right)\frac{d\mu(y)d\mu(x)}{d(x,y)^{s}}

\displaystyle\leq 4b^{2}2^{s+\alpha p_{0}}\left(r_{1}^{\alpha p_{0}-s}+\frac{r_{2}^{\alpha p_{0}}}{r_{1}^{s}}\right)

\displaystyle\times[u]_{W^{\alpha,G}_{s}(X,d,\mu)}^{p^{0}}\iint_{0<d(x,y)<1}G\left(\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|}{[u]_{W^{\alpha,G}_{s}(X,d,\mu)}d(x,y)^{\alpha}}\right)\frac{d\mu(y)d\mu(x)}{d(x,y)^{s}}

\displaystyle\leq 4b^{2}2^{s+\alpha p_{0}}[u]_{W^{\alpha,G}_{s}(X,d,\mu)}^{p^{0}}\left(r_{1}^{\alpha p_{0}-s}+\frac{r_{2}^{\alpha p_{0}}}{r_{1}^{s}}\right).

This concludes the proof.
∎

Proposition 2.9
.

Let
G
be an Orlicz function such that
G^{-1}(xy)\leq x^{\frac{1}{p_{0}}}G^{-1}(y)
if
x\leq 1
, and
G^{-1}(xy)\leq x^{\frac{1}{p^{0}}}G^{-1}(y)
if
x\geq 1
and let
\alpha>0
. Then for all
z\in X
,
R\in\left(0,\frac{2}{3}\right)
,
i,j\in\mathbb{N}
,
0\leq i<j
and
u\in\dot{W}_{s}^{\alpha,G}(X,d,\mu)
we have

\displaystyle\left|m_{u}\left(B\left(z,\frac{R}{2^{j}}\right)\right)-m_{u}\left(B\left(z,\frac{R}{2^{i}}\right)\right)\right|

(2.19)

\displaystyle\leq A[u]_{W^{\alpha,G}_{s}(X,d,\mu)}\sum_{l=i}^{j-1}G^{-1}\left(\frac{R^{\alpha p_{0}-s}}{2^{l(\alpha p_{0}-s)}}\right),

where
A=\displaystyle\max\left\{\left(4^{s+1}b^{2}\right)^{\frac{1}{p_{0}}}\left(1+2^{\alpha p_{0}}\right)^{\frac{1}{p_{0}}},\left(4^{s+1}b^{2}\right)^{\frac{1}{p^{0}}}\left(1+2^{\alpha p_{0}}\right)^{\frac{1}{p^{0}}}\right\}
.

Proof.

Let us take
r_{1}=\displaystyle\frac{R}{2^{l+1}}
and
r_{2}=\displaystyle\frac{R}{2^{l}}
, where
l\in\mathbb{N}
. Then, by the proposition
2.8
, we get

\displaystyle G\left(\left|m_{u}\left(B\left(z,\frac{R}{2^{l+1}}\right)\right)-m_{u}\left(B\left(z,\frac{R}{2^{l}}\right)\right)\right|\right)

\displaystyle\leq 4b^{2}2^{s+\alpha p_{0}}[u]_{W^{\alpha,G}_{s}(X,d,\mu)}^{p_{0}}\left(\frac{1}{2^{\alpha p_{0}-s}}\frac{R^{\alpha p_{0}-s}}{2^{l(\alpha p_{0}-s)}}+2^{s}\frac{R^{\alpha p_{0}-s}}{2^{l(\alpha p_{0}-s)}}\right)

\displaystyle=4^{s+1}b^{2}(1+2^{\alpha p_{0}})[u]_{W^{\alpha,G}_{s}(X,d,\mu)}^{p_{0}}\frac{R^{\alpha p_{0}-s}}{2^{l(\alpha p_{0}-s)}}

if
[u]_{W^{\alpha,G}_{s}(X,d,\mu)}\leq 1
, and

\displaystyle G\left(\left|m_{u}\left(B\left(z,\frac{R}{2^{l+1}}\right)\right)-m_{u}\left(B\left(z,\frac{R}{2^{l}}\right)\right)\right|\right)

\displaystyle\leq 4b^{2}2^{s+\alpha p_{0}}[u]_{W^{\alpha,G}_{s}(X,d,\mu)}^{p^{0}}\left(\frac{1}{2^{\alpha p_{0}-s}}\frac{R^{\alpha p_{0}-s}}{2^{l(\alpha p_{0}-s)}}+2^{s}\frac{R^{\alpha p_{0}-s}}{2^{l(\alpha p_{0}-s)}}\right)

\displaystyle=4^{s+1}b^{2}(1+2^{\alpha p_{0}})[u]_{W^{\alpha,G}_{s}(X,d,\mu)}^{p^{0}}\frac{R^{\alpha p_{0}-s}}{2^{l(\alpha p_{0}-s)}}

if
[u]_{W^{\alpha,G}_{s}(X,d,\mu)}\geq 1
.

Then in both cases, we have

\left|m_{u}\left(B\left(z,\frac{R}{2^{l+1}}\right)\right)-m_{u}\left(B\left(z,\frac{R}{2^{l}}\right)\right)\right|\leq A[u]_{W^{\alpha,G}_{s}(X,d,\mu)}G^{-1}\left(\frac{R^{\alpha p_{0}-s}}{2^{l(\alpha p_{0}-s)}}\right)

where
A=\max\left\{\left(4^{s+1}b^{2}\right)^{1/p_{0}}\left(1+2^{\alpha p_{0}}\right)^{1/p_{0}},\left(4^{s+1}b^{2}\right)^{1/p^{0}}\left(1+2^{\alpha p_{0}}\right)^{1/p^{0}}\right\}
.

Hence, by the triangle inequality we have

\displaystyle\left|m_{u}\left(B\left(z,\frac{R}{2^{j}}\right)\right)-m_{u}\left(B\left(z,\frac{R}{2^{i}}\right)\right)\right|

\displaystyle\leq\sum_{l=i}^{j-1}\left|m_{u}\left(B\left(z,\frac{R}{2^{l+1}}\right)\right)-m_{u}\left(B\left(z,\frac{R}{2^{l}}\right)\right)\right|

\displaystyle\leq A[u]_{W^{\alpha,G}_{s}(X,d,\mu)}\sum_{l=i}^{j-1}G^{-1}\left(\frac{R^{\alpha p_{0}-s}}{2^{l(\alpha p_{0}-s)}}\right),

which completes the proof.
∎

3.
Continuous embeddings

This section is aimed at proving some results concerning continuous embeddings between the fractional order Orlicz-Sobolev space defined on metric-measure spaces
W_{s}^{\alpha,G}(X,d,\mu)
, and the fractional Hajłasz-Orlicz-Sobolev space
M^{\beta,G}(X,d,\mu)
where
G
is an Orlicz function.

Theorem 3.1
.

Let
G
be an Orlicz function such that
G^{-1}(xy)\leq x^{\frac{1}{p_{0}}}G^{-1}(y)
if
x\leq 1
and
G^{-1}(xy)\leq x^{\frac{1}{p^{0}}}G^{-1}(y)
if
x\geq 1
, and let
s,\alpha>0
such that
\alpha p_{0}>s
. Then, there exists a constant
C>0
such that any
u\in\dot{W}_{s}^{\alpha,G}(X,d,\mu)
equals a.e. to a continuous function
\widetilde{u}
and we have the following inequality

\displaystyle\sup_{0<d(x,y)<\frac{1}{3}}\frac{\left|\widetilde{u}(x)-\widetilde{u}(y)\right|}{d(x,y)^{\alpha-\frac{s}{p_{0}}}}\leq C[u]_{W_{s}^{\alpha,G}(X,d,\mu)}.

(3.1)

Proof.

Let
z\in X
and
0\leq R<\frac{1}{3}
. Since
\alpha p_{0}>s
, the inequality (
2.19
) implies that for all
i,j\in\mathbb{N}
such that
0\leq i<j
, we have

\left|m_{u}\left(B\left(z,\frac{R}{2^{i}}\right)\right)-m_{u}\left(B\left(z,\frac{R}{2^{j}}\right)\right)\right|\leq\widetilde{A}[u]_{W^{\alpha,G}_{s}(X,d,\mu)}\frac{R^{\alpha-\frac{s}{p_{0}}}}{2^{i\left(\alpha-\frac{s}{p_{0}}\right)}},

(3.2)

where
\tilde{A}=G^{-1}(1)A/\displaystyle\left(1-2^{\frac{s}{p_{0}}-\alpha}\right)>0
. Therefore, we get that
\left\{m_{u}\left(B\left(z,\frac{R}{2^{k}}\right)\right)\right\}_{k=0}^{\infty}
is a Cauchy sequence, thus is convergent. By Lemma
2.7
we have that
m_{u}\left(B\left(x,\frac{R}{2^{k}}\right)\right)
converges to
u(x)
for every
x\in X\backslash H
, where
\mu(H)=0
.

Let us take
i=0
in (
3.2
) and pass to the limit
j\rightarrow\infty
, then we get for all
z\in X\backslash H

\left|m_{u}(B(z,R))-u(z)\right|\leq\widetilde{A}[u]_{W^{\alpha,G}_{s}(X,d,\mu)}R^{\alpha-\frac{s}{p_{0}}}.

(3.3)

Let
z,w\in X\backslash H
be such that
0<d(z,w)<\frac{1}{3}
and let
R=d(z,w)
. Then, by (
3.3
) we have

\displaystyle|u(z)-u(w)|

\displaystyle\leq\left|u(z)-m_{u}(B(z,R))\right|+\left|u(w)-m_{u}(B(w,R))\right|

(3.4)

\displaystyle+\left|m_{u}(B(z,R))-m_{u}(B(w,R))\right|

\displaystyle\leq 2\widetilde{A}[u]_{W^{\alpha,G}_{s}(X,d,\mu)}d(z,w)^{\alpha-\frac{s}{p_{0}}}

\displaystyle+\left|m_{u}(B(z,R))-m_{u}(B(w,R))\right|.

By the inequalities (
2.10
) and (
2.15
) we have

\displaystyle\left|m_{u}(B(z,R))-m_{u}(B(w,R))\right|

(3.5)

\displaystyle\leq G^{-1}\left(2\fint_{B(z,R)}G\left|u(x)-m_{u}(B(w,R))\right|)d\mu(x)\right)

\displaystyle\leq G^{-1}\left(2\fint_{B(z,R)}2bR^{-s}\int_{B(w,R)}G(|u(x)-u(y)|)d\mu(y)d\mu(x)\right)

\displaystyle\leq G^{-1}\left(4b^{2}R^{-2s}\int_{B(z,R)}\int_{B(w,R)}G(|u(x)-u(y)|)d\mu(y)d\mu(x)\right).

Then by using the inequalities (
3.5
) and (
2.5
) with
\gamma=d(x,y)^{\alpha}\in(0,1]
and the estimate
d(x,y)\leq d(x,z)+d(z,w)+d(w,y)\leq 3R
, we obtain

\displaystyle\left|m_{u}(B(z,R))-m_{u}(B(w,R))\right|

(3.6)

\displaystyle\leq G^{-1}\left(4b^{2}R^{-2s}\int_{B(z,R)}\int_{B(w,R)}G\left(\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|}{d(x,y)^{\alpha}}\right)d(x,y)^{\alpha p_{0}+s}\frac{d\mu(y)d\mu(x)}{d(x,y)^{s}}\right)

\displaystyle\leq G^{-1}\left(4b^{2}3^{s+\alpha p_{0}}R^{\alpha p_{0}-s}\int_{B(z,R)}\int_{B(w,R)}G\left(\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|}{d(x,y)^{\alpha}}\right)\frac{d\mu(y)d\mu(x)}{d(x,y)^{s}}\right)

\displaystyle\leq Bd(z,w)^{\alpha-\frac{s}{p_{0}}}G^{-1}\left(\iint_{d(x,y)<1}G\left(\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|}{d(x,y)^{\alpha}}\right)\frac{d\mu(y)d\mu(x)}{d(x,y)^{s}}\right),

where
B=\max\left\{\left(4b^{2}3^{s+\alpha p_{0}}\right)^{\frac{1}{p_{0}}},\left(4b^{2}3^{s+\alpha p_{0}}\right)^{\frac{1}{p^{0}}}\right\}
.

If
[u]_{W^{\alpha,G}_{s}(X,d,\mu)}=0
, the inequality (
3.1
) is trivial.

We suppose that
[u]_{W^{\alpha,G}_{s}(X,d,\mu)}\in(0,1]
, then from the inequalities (
3.6
) and (
2.5
) we get that

\displaystyle\left|m_{u}(B(z,R))-m_{u}(B(w,R))\right|

\displaystyle\leq Bd(z,w)^{\alpha-\frac{s}{p_{0}}}

\displaystyle\times G^{-1}\left(\iint_{d(x,y)<1}G\left(\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|}{[u]_{W^{\alpha,G}_{s}(X,d,\mu)}d(x,y)^{\alpha}}\right)[u]_{W^{\alpha,G}_{s}(X,d,\mu)}^{p_{0}}\frac{d\mu(y)d\mu(x)}{d(x,y)^{s}}\right)

\displaystyle\leq Bd(z,w)^{\alpha-\frac{s}{p_{0}}}[u]_{W^{\alpha,G}_{s}(X,d,\mu)}

\displaystyle\times G^{-1}\left(\iint_{d(x,y)<1}G\left(\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|}{[u]_{W^{\alpha,G}_{s}(X,d,\mu)}d(x,y)^{\alpha}}\right)\frac{d\mu(y)d\mu(x)}{d(x,y)^{s}}\right)

\displaystyle\leq G^{-1}(1)Bd(z,w)^{\alpha-\frac{s}{p_{0}}}[u]_{W^{\alpha,G}_{s}(X,d,\mu)}.

Now we suppose that
[u]_{W^{\alpha,G}_{s}(X,d,\mu)}\geq 1
, then from the inequalities (
3.6
) and (
2.4
) we get that

\displaystyle\left|m_{u}(B(z,R))-m_{u}(B(w,R))\right|

\displaystyle\leq Bd(z,w)^{\alpha-\frac{s}{p_{0}}}

\displaystyle\times G^{-1}\left(\iint_{d(x,y)<1}G\left([u]_{W^{\alpha,G}_{s}(X,d,\mu)}\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|}{[u]_{W^{\alpha,G}_{s}(X,d,\mu)}d(x,y)^{\alpha}}\right)\frac{d\mu(y)d\mu(x)}{d(x,y)^{s}}\right)

\displaystyle\leq Bd(z,w)^{\alpha-\frac{s}{p_{0}}}

\displaystyle\times G^{-1}\left(\iint_{d(x,y)<1}G\left(\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|}{[u]_{W^{\alpha,G}_{s}(X,d,\mu)}d(x,y)^{\alpha}}\right)[u]_{W^{\alpha,G}_{s}(X,d,\mu)}^{p^{0}}\frac{d\mu(y)d\mu(x)}{d(x,y)^{s}}\right)

\begin{aligned} &\leq Bd(z,w)^{\alpha-\frac{s}{p_{0}}}[u]_{W^{\alpha,G}_{s}(X,d,\mu)}\\
&\times G^{-1}\left(\iint_{d(x,y)<1}G\left(\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|}{[u]_{W^{\alpha,G}_{s}(X,d,\mu)}d(x,y)^{\alpha}}\right)\frac{d\mu(y)d\mu(x)}{d(x,y)^{s}}\right)\\
&\leq G^{-1}(1)Bd(z,w)^{\alpha-\frac{s}{p_{0}}}[u]_{W^{\alpha,G}_{s}(X,d,\mu)}.\end{aligned}

Then in both cases we have

\left|m_{u}(B(z,R))-m_{u}(B(w,R))\right|\leq G^{-1}(1)Bd(z,w)^{\alpha-\frac{s}{p_{0}}}[u]_{W^{\alpha,G}_{s}(X,d,\mu)}.

Then, by (
3.4
) we get

|u(z)-u(w)|\leq C[u]_{W^{\alpha,G}_{s}(X,d,\mu)}d(z,w)^{\alpha-\frac{s}{p_{0}}}

for all
z,w\in X\backslash H
such that
d(z,w)<\frac{1}{3}
, where
C=2\widetilde{A}+G^{-1}(1)B
.

Now, we extend
u
to a function
\widetilde{u}
which is continuous on
X
and satisfies (
3.1
).

Since
0<\mu(B(x,r))<\infty
for all
x\in X
and all
r\in\left(0,\infty\right)
, then the set
X\backslash H
is dense in
X
. Hence, for
x\in H
, there exists
x_{n}\in X\backslash H
converging to
x
.

Then,

\left|u\left(x_{n}\right)-u\left(x_{m}\right)\right|\leq C[u]_{W^{\alpha,G}_{s}(X,d,\mu)}d\left(x_{n},x_{m}\right)^{\alpha-\frac{s}{p_{0}}}.

Therefore,
\left\{u\left(x_{n}\right)\right\}_{n=1}^{\infty}
is a Cauchy sequence in
\mathbb{R}
, thus is convergent, let
\widetilde{u}(x)
denote its limit (which is independent of the choice of the sequence converging to
x
). For
x\in X\backslash H
we put
\widetilde{u}(x)=u(x)
. Let us fix
x,y\in X
such that
d(x,y)<\frac{1}{3}
and let us take
x_{n},y_{n}\in X\backslash H
such that
x_{n}\rightarrow x
and
y_{n}\rightarrow y
.

Then,

u\left(x_{n}\right)\rightarrow\widetilde{u}(x)\quad\textit{ and }\quad u\left(y_{n}\right)\rightarrow\widetilde{u}(y).

Hence,

\displaystyle\left|\widetilde{u}(x)-\widetilde{u}(y)\right|

\displaystyle\leq\left|u\left(x_{n}\right)-\widetilde{u}(x)\right|+\left|u\left(y_{n}\right)-\widetilde{u}(y)\right|+\left|u\left(x_{n}\right)-u\left(y_{n}\right)\right|

\displaystyle\leq\left|u\left(x_{n}\right)-\widetilde{u}(x)\right|+\left|u\left(y_{n}\right)-\widetilde{u}(y)\right|

\displaystyle+C[u]_{W^{\alpha,G}_{s}(X,d,\mu)}d\left(x_{n},y_{n}\right)^{\alpha-\frac{s}{p_{0}}}.

Then, by passing to the limit
n\rightarrow\infty
we get

\left|\widetilde{u}(x)-\widetilde{u}(y)\right|\leq C[u]_{W^{\alpha,G}_{s}(X,d,\mu)}d(x,y)^{\alpha-\frac{s}{p_{0}}},

which completes the proof.
∎

Theorem 3.2
.

Let
G
be an Orlicz function such that
G^{-1}(xy)\leq x^{\frac{1}{p_{0}}}G^{-1}(y)
if
x\leq 1
and
G^{-1}(xy)\leq x^{\frac{1}{p^{0}}}G^{-1}(y)
if
x\geq 1
, and let
s,\alpha>0
such that
\alpha p_{0}>s
. Then there is a positive constant
\widetilde{C}
such that for all
u\in W_{s}^{\alpha,G}(X,d,\mu)
we have

\left\|\widetilde{u}\right\|_{C^{0,\alpha-\frac{s}{p_{0}}}(X,d)}\leq\widetilde{C}\|u\|_{W_{s}^{\alpha,G}(X,d,\mu)},

(3.7)

where
\widetilde{u}
is a continuous representative of
u
.

Proof.

Let
z\in X
, then there exists
w\in B(z,\frac{1}{3})
such that

G\left(\left|\widetilde{u}(w)\right|\right)\leq\frac{1}{\mu(B(z,\frac{1}{3}))}\int_{B(z,\frac{1}{3})}G\left(|u(y)|\right)d\mu(y).

(3.8)

If
\|u\|_{L^{G}(X,\mu)}=0
, the inequality (
3.7
) is trivial.

We suppose that
\|u\|_{L^{G}(X,\mu)}\in(0,1]
, then by (
3.8
) and (
2.5
) we have

\displaystyle\left|\widetilde{u}(w)\right|

\displaystyle\leq G^{-1}\left(\frac{1}{\mu(B(z,\frac{1}{3}))}\int_{B(z,\frac{1}{3})}G\left(|u(y)|\right)d\mu(y)\right)

\displaystyle\leq G^{-1}\left(\frac{1}{\mu(B(z,\frac{1}{3}))}\int_{B(z,\frac{1}{3})}G\left(\frac{|u(y)|}{\|u\|_{L^{G}(X,\mu)}}\right)\|u\|_{L^{G}(X,\mu)}^{p_{0}}d\mu(y)\right)

\displaystyle\leq G^{-1}\left(b3^{s}\int_{B(z,\frac{1}{3})}G\left(\frac{|u(y)|}{\|u\|_{L^{G}(X,\mu)}}\right)\|u\|_{L^{G}(X,\mu)}^{p_{0}}d\mu(y)\right)

\displaystyle\leq C_{1}\|u\|_{L^{G}(X,\mu)}G^{-1}\left(\int_{B(z,\frac{1}{3})}G\left(\frac{|u(y)|}{\|u\|_{L^{G}(X,\mu)}}\right)d\mu(y)\right)

\displaystyle\leq C_{1}G^{-1}(1)\|u\|_{L^{G}(X,\mu)},

where
C_{1}=\max\left\{b^{\frac{1}{p_{0}}}3^{\frac{s}{p_{0}}},b^{\frac{1}{p^{0}}}3^{\frac{s}{p^{0}}}\right\}.

Now we suppose that
\|u\|_{L^{G}(X,\mu)}\geq 1
, then by (
3.8
) and (
2.4
) we have

\displaystyle\left|\widetilde{u}(w)\right|

\displaystyle\leq G^{-1}\left(\frac{1}{\mu(B(z,\frac{1}{3}))}\int_{B(z,\frac{1}{3})}G\left(|u(y)|\right)d\mu(y)\right)

\displaystyle=G^{-1}\left(\frac{1}{\mu(B(z,\frac{1}{3}))}\int_{B(z,\frac{1}{3})}G\left(\|u\|_{L^{G}(X,\mu)}\frac{|u(y)|}{\|u\|_{L^{G}(X,\mu)}}\right)d\mu(y)\right)

\displaystyle\leq G^{-1}\left(b3^{s}\int_{B(z,\frac{1}{3})}G\left(\frac{|u(y)|}{\|u\|_{L^{G}(X,\mu)}}\right)\|u\|_{L^{G}(X,\mu)}^{p^{0}}d\mu(y)\right)

\displaystyle\leq C_{1}\|u\|_{L^{G}(X,\mu)}G^{-1}\left(\int_{B(z,\frac{1}{3})}G\left(\frac{|u(y)|}{\|u\|_{L^{G}(X,\mu)}}\right)d\mu(y)\right)

\displaystyle\leq C_{1}G^{-1}(1)\|u\|_{L^{G}(X,\mu)}.

Then in both cases, we have

\left|\widetilde{u}(w)\right|\leq C_{2}\|u\|_{L^{G}(X,\mu)},

(3.9)

where
C_{2}=C_{1}G^{-1}(1).
Then, by Theorem
3.1
and the inequality (
3.9
), we have

\displaystyle\left|\widetilde{u}(z)\right|

\displaystyle\leq\left|\widetilde{u}(z)-\widetilde{u}(w)\right|+\left|\widetilde{u}(w)\right|

\displaystyle\leq C[u]_{W_{s}^{\alpha,G}(X,d,\mu)}d(z,w)^{\alpha-\frac{s}{p_{0}}}+C_{2}\|u\|_{L^{G}(X,\mu)}

\displaystyle\leq C_{3}\|u\|_{W_{s}^{\alpha,G}(X,d,\mu)},

where
C_{3}=\max\left\{C3^{\frac{s}{p_{0}}-\alpha},C_{2}\right\}
. Hence,

\left\|\widetilde{u}\right\|_{C(X,d)}\leq C_{3}\|u\|_{W_{s}^{\alpha,G}(X,d,\mu)}.

By using Theorem
3.1
we can control the Hölder semi-norm of
\widetilde{u}
for all
z,w
such that
d(z,w)<\frac{1}{3}
. Now, let us estimate the whole Hölder’s semi-norm.

Let
z,w\in X
such that
d(z,w)\geq\frac{1}{3}
, then

\displaystyle\left|\widetilde{u}(z)-\widetilde{u}(w)\right|

\displaystyle\leq 2\left\|\widetilde{u}\right\|_{C(X,d)}

(3.10)

\displaystyle=2.3^{\alpha-\frac{s}{p_{0}}}\left\|\widetilde{u}\right\|_{C(X,d)}\frac{1}{3^{\alpha-\frac{s}{p_{0}}}}

\displaystyle\leq 2.3^{\alpha-\frac{s}{p_{0}}}C_{3}\|u\|_{W_{s}^{\alpha,G}(X,d,\mu)}d(z,w)^{\alpha-\frac{s}{p_{0}}}.

Then by Theorem
3.1
and (
3.10
) we have

\sup_{x\neq y\in X}\frac{\left|\widetilde{u}(x)-\widetilde{u}(y)\right|}{d(x,y)^{\alpha-\frac{s}{p_{0}}}}\leq C_{4}\|u\|_{W_{s}^{\alpha,G}(X,d,\mu)},

where
C_{4}=\max\left\{C,2.3^{\alpha-\frac{s}{p_{0}}}C_{3}\right\}.

Hence, we have

\displaystyle\left\|\widetilde{u}\right\|_{C^{0,\alpha-\frac{s}{p_{0}}}(X,d)}

\displaystyle=\left\|\widetilde{u}\right\|_{C(X,d)}+\sup_{x\neq y\in X}\frac{\left|\widetilde{u}(x)-\widetilde{u}(y)\right|}{d(x,y)^{\alpha-\frac{s}{p_{0}}}}

\displaystyle\leq C_{3}\|u\|_{W_{s}^{\alpha,G}(X,d,\mu)}+C_{4}\|u\|_{W_{s}^{\alpha,G}(X,d,\mu)}

\displaystyle=\widetilde{C}\|u\|_{W_{s}^{\alpha,G}(X,d,\mu)},

where
\widetilde{C}=C_{3}+C_{4}
.

The proof is complete.
∎

Theorem 3.3
.

Let
\alpha>0
,
G
be an Orlicz function such that
G^{-1}(xy)\leq x^{\frac{1}{p_{0}}}G^{-1}(y)
if
x\leq 1
and
G^{-1}(xy)\leq x^{\frac{1}{p^{0}}}G^{-1}(y)
if
x\geq 1
and suppose that
(X,d,\mu)
is a lower Ahlfors
\theta
-regular space, where
\theta\geq 0
and
\alpha p_{0}>\theta-s
. Then, the continuous embedding
W_{s}^{\alpha,G}(X,d,\mu)\hookrightarrow M^{\beta,G}(X,d,\mu)
holds, where
\beta=\alpha+(s-\theta)/p_{0}
.

Proof.

Let
u\in W_{s}^{\alpha,G}(X,d,\mu)
and let us consider the following set

H=\left\{x\in X:\int_{B(x,1)}G\left(\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|}{d(x,y)^{\alpha}}\right)\frac{d\mu(y)}{d(x,y)^{s}}=+\infty\text{ or }|u(x)|=+\infty\right\}.

It is clear that
\mu(H)=0
. Let
\xi,\eta\in X\backslash H
.

If
d(\xi,\eta)<\frac{1}{2}
, then from the inequalities (
2.10
) and (
2.5
) we have

\displaystyle|u(\xi)-u(\eta)|
