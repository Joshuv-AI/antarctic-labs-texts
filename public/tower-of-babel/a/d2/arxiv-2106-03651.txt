On the inverse problem of identifying an unknown coefficient in a space-time fractional differential equation
Mine Aylin Bayrak 1,∗, Ali Demir 1

Abstract
In this study, we focus on identifying solution and an unknown space-dependent coefficient in a space-time fractional differential equation by employing fractional Taylor series method. The substantial advantage of this method is that we don’t take any over-measured data into account. Consequently, we determine the solution and unknown coefficient more precisely. The presented examples illustrate that outcomes of this method are in high agreement with the exact ones of the corresponding problem. Moreover, it can be implemented and applied effectively comparing with other methods.

Definition 1.
The Riemann-Liouville fractional integral of order
\alpha~(\alpha\geqslant 0)
is given as

\displaystyle J^{\alpha}f(x)=\frac{1}{\Gamma(\alpha)}\int_{0}^{x}(x-t)^{\alpha-1}f(t)dt,~\alpha>0,~x>0,

(5)

\displaystyle J^{0}f(x)=f(x).

(6)

Definition 2.
The Liouville-Caputo fractional derivative of order
\alpha
is given as

\displaystyle D^{\alpha}f(x)=J^{n-\alpha}D^{n}f(x)=\int_{0}^{x}(x-t)^{n-\alpha-1}\frac{d^{n}}{dt^{n}}f(t)dt,~n-1<\alpha<n,~x>0,

(7)

where
D^{n}
denotes the ordinary derivative of order
n

Definition 3.
The
\alpha^{th}
order derivative of
u(x,t)
in Liouville-Caputo sense is given as

\displaystyle D_{t}^{\alpha}u(x,t)=\left\{\begin{array}[]{lr}\frac{1}{\Gamma(n-\alpha)}\int_{0}^{t}(t-\xi)^{n-\alpha-1}\frac{\partial^{n}u(x,\xi)}{\partial t^{n}}d\xi,~n-1<\alpha<n,\\
\frac{\partial^{n}u(x,t)}{\partial t^{n}}~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~,\alpha=n\in N.\end{array}\right.

Definition 4.
An
(\alpha,\beta)
-fractional Taylor series is defined as follows
[
18
]
:

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{i+j=0}^{\infty}g_{i,j}t^{i\alpha}x^{j\beta}=\underbrace{g_{0,0}}_{i+j=0}+\underbrace{g_{1,0}t^{\alpha}+g_{0,1}x^{\beta}}_{i+j=1}+...+\underbrace{\sum\limits_{k=0}^{n}g_{n-k,k}t^{(n-k)\alpha}x^{k\beta}}_{i+j=n}+...

(11)

where
g_{i,j},i,j\epsilon N
are the coefficients of the series.

Lemma 5.
Let
u(x,t)
has a fractional Taylor series representation as (
11
) for
(x,t)\epsilon[0,R_{x})\times[0,R_{t})
. If
D_{t}^{r\alpha}D_{x}^{s\beta}u(x,t)\epsilon((0,R_{x})\times(0,R_{t}))
for
r,s\epsilon N
, then

\displaystyle D_{t}^{r\alpha}u(x,t)=\sum\limits_{i+j=0}^{\infty}g_{i+r,j}\frac{\Gamma((i+r)\alpha+1)}{\Gamma(i\alpha+1)}t^{i\alpha}x^{j\beta}

(12)

\displaystyle D_{x}^{s\beta}u(x,t)=\sum\limits_{i+j=0}^{\infty}g_{i,j+s}\frac{\Gamma((j+s)\beta+1)}{\Gamma(j\beta+1)}t^{i\alpha}x^{j\beta}

3
Fractional Taylor series method

In order to determine the diffusion coefficient
p(x)
of time in the space-time fractional diffusion problem (
1
)-(
4
), in the series form we plug the fractional Taylor series of
u=u(x,t)
and
p=p(x)
into (
1
)-(
4
) which leads to:

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{i+j=0}^{\infty}g_{i+1,j}\frac{\Gamma((i+1)\alpha+1)}{\Gamma(i\alpha+1)}t^{i\alpha}x^{j\beta}=\sum\limits_{i+j=0}^{\infty}g_{i,j+2}\frac{\Gamma((j+2)\beta+1)}{\Gamma(j\beta+1)}t^{i\alpha}x^{j\beta}

\displaystyle+\sum\limits_{k=0}^{\infty}p_{k}\Big\{\sum\limits_{i+j=0}^{\infty}\frac{t^{i\alpha}x^{(j+k)\beta}}{\Gamma(i\alpha+1)\Gamma((j+k)\beta+1)}\Big\}

(14)

Making two series on both sides of above equation equal to each other, the unknown coefficients in the fractional Taylor series of
p(x)
are acquired.

Conclusion

In this research, the identification of an unknown space-time coefficient in a space-time fractional differential equation with initial and Neumann-like boundary conditions by fractional Taylor series method is investigated. First, the solution of the equation is established by employing fractional Taylor series method with the help of initial condition and Neumann-like boundary condition at the final point. Later, the unknown space-dependent coefficient is identified in the series form without using any over-measured data which is substantial advantage of this method. Future work will be on the construction of unknown coefficient or source term in space-dependent fractional differential equations with various boundary conditions.
