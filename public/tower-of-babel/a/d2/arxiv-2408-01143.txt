Type-II seesaw of a non-holomorphic modular A 4 A_{4} symmetry
Takaaki Nomura, Hiroshi Okada

Abstract
We search for predictability of lepton masses and mixing patterns of type-II seesaw scenario in a non-holomorphic modular A 4 A_{4} symmetry recently proposed by Qu and Ding. We propose three types of minimum predictive models with different assignments of modular weight, satisfying the neutrino oscillation data in Nufit 5.2. The cosmological bound on the sum of neutrino mass is stringent to our models and CMB bound ∑ D ν ≤ 0.12 \sum D_{\nu}\leq 0.12 eV can be satisfied by one of three models playing an important role in discriminating them.

Neutrino mass matrix

The mass matrix of neutrino comes from the second term in Eq.(

Pontecorvo-Maki-Nakagawa-Sakata (PMNS) mixing matrix

Here, we define dimensionless neutrino mass matrix;
m_{\nu}\equiv\kappa\tilde{m}_{\nu}

\displaystyle({\rm NH}):\ \kappa^{2}=\frac{|\Delta m_{\rm atm}^{2}|}{\tilde{D}_{\nu_{3}}^{2}-\tilde{D}_{\nu_{1}}^{2}},\quad({\rm IH}):\ \kappa^{2}=\frac{|\Delta m_{\rm atm}^{2}|}{\tilde{D}_{\nu_{2}}^{2}-\tilde{D}_{\nu_{3}}^{2}},

\displaystyle\Delta m_{\rm sol}^{2}={\kappa^{2}}({\tilde{D}_{\nu_{2}}^{2}-\tilde{D}_{\nu_{1}}^{2}}).

IV
Summary and discussion

We have searched for predictability of lepton masses and mixing of type-II scenario in a non-holomorphic modular
A_{4}
symmetry.
We have found three types of minimum models in addition to the Qu and Ding model,
satisfying the neutrino oscillation data in Nufit 5.2.
However, if we impose the cosmological bound
\sum D_{\nu}\leq 0.12
eV, the only normal hierarchical model with
r=-1
is survived. Furthermore, we have sharp predictions in this case as follows:
There are two localized regions;
D_{\nu_{1}}\approx(0.02,\ 0.09-0.13)
eV and
\langle m_{ee}\rangle\approx(0.02,\ 0.08-0.12)
eV.
We also found four main localized regions

\alpha_{31}\approx(0-60,\ 160-200,\ 310-360)
deg and
\alpha_{21}\approx(10-30,\ 50-80,\ 270-310,\ 330-350)
deg.
There exist four localized regions; sum
D_{\nu}\approx(0.1,\ 0.28-0.4)
eV
and
\delta_{CP}\rangle\approx(80-100,\ 120-140,\ 220-240,\ 260-280)
deg.
This model is well-tested by upcoming experiments in near future.
