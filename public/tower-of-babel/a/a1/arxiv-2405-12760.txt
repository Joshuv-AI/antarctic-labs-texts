Mixing and C ​ P {C\!P} violation in beauty and charm at LHCb
Jordy Butter, on behalf of the LHCb Collaboration

Abstract
These proceedings discuss recent measurements by the LHCb experiment on mixing and C ​ P {C\!P} violation with beauty and charm mesons, as presented at the Moriond QCD 2024 conference. All discussed measurements show agreement with the Standard Model.



1
Beauty measurements

The study of
{B}
hadrons provides a great laboratory to study the CKM sector of the Standard Model (SM) and look for manifestations of physics beyond the SM.
First, direct determinations of the weak angle
\gamma
using
{{B}^{\pm}}\rightarrow D^{*}h^{\pm}
and
{{B}^{0}}\rightarrow DK^{*}(892)^{0}
decays are discussed.
Afterwards, mixing-induced
{C\!P}
violation is studied using
B^{0}_{s}\!\rightarrow D_{s}^{\mp}K^{\pm}
,
{{B}^{0}_{s}}\rightarrow{{J\mskip-3.0mu/\mskip-2.0mu\psi}}{{K}^{+}}{{K}^{-}}
and
{{B}^{0}}\rightarrow\psi(\rightarrow{\ell^{+}}{\ell^{-}}){{K}^{0}_{\mathrm{\scriptscriptstyle S}}}(\rightarrow{{\pi}^{+}}{{\pi}^{-}})
decays, probing the CKM angles
\gamma
,
\beta_{s}
and
\beta
, respectively.
Finally, a measurement of
{\Delta\Gamma_{{s}}}
is shown.

1.1
Direct determinations of the CKM angle
\gamma

The experimental sensitivity to the CKM angle
\gamma
is constrained most notably by
{{B}^{\pm}}\rightarrow D{{K}^{\pm}}
decays, where
D
is an admixture of
{{D}^{0}}
and
{{\kern 1.99997pt\overline{\kern-1.99997ptD}{}}{}^{0}}
mesons and the interference between
b\rightarrow c
and
b\rightarrow u
amplitudes is used to measure
\gamma
.
In these decays, the differences in the
D
-decay Dalitz-plot distributions between
{{{B}^{+}}}
and
{{{B}^{-}}}
decays are due to the
{C\!P}
-violating phase
\gamma
, which is probed via time-integrated analyses.

These proceedings show two
\gamma
measurements using
{{B}^{\pm}}\rightarrow D^{*}h^{\pm}
decays, where
D^{*}\rightarrow D\gamma/{{\pi}^{0}}
and
D\rightarrow{{K}^{0}_{\mathrm{\scriptscriptstyle S}}}h^{+}h^{-}
, using proton-proton data collected between 2011 and 2012 (Run 1) and between 2015 and 2018 (Run 2), corresponding to an integrated luminosity of 3 and
6\mbox{\,fb}^{-1}
, respectively.
The rich interference structure in
D\rightarrow{{K}^{0}_{\mathrm{\scriptscriptstyle S}}}h^{+}h^{-}
decays, with
h={\pi,K}
, is explored through a model-independent approach, not relying on a
D
decay amplitude model.
The interference effects are studied by measuring the yields of the
{{{B}^{+}}}
and
{{{B}^{-}}}
decays in bins of
D
decay
Dalitz phase space.
The key difference between these two measurements is that one fully reconstructs the final-state particles
[
1
]
, whereas the other does not reconstruct the neutral
\gamma
or
{{\pi}^{0}}
coming from the
D^{*}
decay
[
2
]
.
The values of
\gamma=(69^{+13}_{-14})^{\circ}
and
\gamma=(92^{+21}_{-17})^{\circ}
are found for the fully and the partially reconstructed analysis, respectively.

Additionally, two measurements using
{{B}^{0}}\rightarrow DK^{*}(892)^{0}
decays are presented, using Run 1 and Run 2 data.
Despite its relatively small branching fraction, the study of the
{{B}^{0}}\rightarrow DK^{*}(892)^{0}
decay provides a precise determination of
\gamma
due to the relatively similar magnitude of the interfering amplitudes.
The first analysis studies interference effects using
D\rightarrow{{K}^{0}_{\mathrm{\scriptscriptstyle S}}}h^{+}h^{-}
decays by measuring the
{{B}^{0}}
and
{{\kern 1.79993pt\overline{\kern-1.79993ptB}{}}{}^{0}}
decay yields across the
D
phase space
[
3
]
.
These yields are used to determine the weak phase
\gamma=(49^{+22}_{-19})^{\circ}
, the amplitude ratio
r_{{B}^{0}}=0.271^{+0.065}_{-0.066}
and the strong phase
\delta_{{B}^{0}}=(236^{+19}_{-21})^{\circ}
.
The other
{{B}^{0}}\rightarrow DK^{*0}
measurement studies final states with
D\rightarrow K\pi(\pi\pi)
,
\pi\pi(\pi\pi)
and
KK

[
4
]
.
Invariant mass fits are used to measure asymmetries and ratios of the
{{B}^{0}}
and
{{\kern 1.79993pt\overline{\kern-1.79993ptB}{}}{}^{0}}
decay rates, which are functions of
\gamma
,
r_{{B}^{0}}
and
\delta_{{B}^{0}}
.
Four solutions are found, but a combination with the previously described analysis favours the values
\gamma=(63.3\pm 7.2)^{\circ}
,
r_{{B}^{0}}=0.233\pm 0.016
and
\delta_{{B}^{0}}=(191.9\pm 6.0)^{\circ}
.

\gamma
–
r_{{{B}^{0}}}^{DK^{*}}

1.2
Mixing-induced
{C\!P}
violation

In certain
{B}
hadron decays
{C\!P}
violation manifests itself in the interference between the mixed and the unmixed decay amplitudes.
This creates a time-dependent
{C\!P}
asymmetry, dependent on the complex phases of the CKM matrix.

A well-known example of this type of
{C\!P}
violation is the study of
B^{0}_{s}\!\rightarrow D_{s}^{\mp}K^{\pm}
decays, where the interfering amplitudes have a comparable magnitude and a weak-phase difference of
\gamma-2\beta_{s}
.
This analysis has recently been performed using Run 2 data
[
5
]
and makes use of the measurement of the
{{B}^{0}_{s}}
–
{{\kern 1.79993pt\overline{\kern-1.79993ptB}{}}{}^{0}_{s}}
mixing frequency
\Delta m_{s}
with
B^{0}_{s}\!\rightarrow D_{s}^{-}\pi^{+}
decays to calibrate detector effects and for the value of
\Delta m_{s}

[
6
]
.
First, a likelihood fit is performed to the invariant mass of the
{{B}^{0}_{s}}
and
{{D}^{-}_{s}}
mesons to separate the signal from the background.
Afterwards, a decay-time fit is performed to extract a set of five
{C\!P}
observables.
The corresponding
{C\!P}
asymmetry plot is provided in Fig.
3
, where the shift between the two lines is a manifestation of
{C\!P}
violation.
The decay-time fit requires a good understanding of the decay-time acceptance, the resolution of the decay-time measurement and the flavour tagging, the main detector effects diluting the sensitivity to
{C\!P}
violation.
Finally, assuming
\phi_{s}=-2\beta_{s}
and using the LHCb combination of
\phi_{s}

[
7
]
, the measured
{C\!P}
observables are used to find the CKM angle
\gamma=(74\pm 11)^{\circ}
, the strong-phase difference
\delta=(346.9\pm 6.6)^{\circ}
and the amplitude ratio
r_{D_{s}K}=0.327\pm 0.038
.
This is the most precise determination of
\gamma
with
{{B}^{0}_{s}}
mesons and agrees with the LHCb combination of
\gamma

[
8
]
.

The most important constraint for the CKM angle
\beta_{s}
is the study of
{{B}^{0}_{s}}\rightarrow{{J\mskip-3.0mu/\mskip-2.0mu\psi}}\phi
decays, where the measurable
{C\!P}
-violating phase
\phi_{s}
is equal to
-2\beta_{s}
in the SM, neglecting subleading loop contributions.
The corresponding Run 2 measurement is performed using
{{B}^{0}_{s}}\rightarrow{{J\mskip-3.0mu/\mskip-2.0mu\psi}}{{K}^{+}}{{K}^{-}}
decays near the
\phi(2010)
resonance
[
7
]
.
The measurement requires a flavour-tagged, time-dependent angular analysis, leading to the most precise determination of
{C\!P}
violating phase
\phi_{s}
, the
{{B}^{0}_{s}}
decay-width difference
{\Delta\Gamma_{{s}}}
and the difference in the average
{{B}^{0}_{s}}
and
{{B}^{0}}
decay width,
{\Gamma_{{s}}}-{\Gamma_{{d}}}
, to date.
This measurement finds
\phi_{s}=-0.039\pm 0.022\pm 0.006\mathrm{\,rad}
,
{\Delta\Gamma_{{s}}}=0.0845\pm 0.0044\pm 0.0024{\mathrm{\,ps}}^{-1}
and
{\Gamma_{{s}}}-{\Gamma_{{d}}}=-0.0056^{+0.0013}_{-0.0015}\pm 0.0014{\mathrm{\,ps}}^{-1}
, where the uncertainties are statistical and systematic.
The result is compatible with
{C\!P}
symmetry, other
\phi_{s}
measurements and the SM expectation.

Also, the most precise single determination of the CKM angle
\beta
is presented, using
{{B}^{0}}\rightarrow\psi(\rightarrow{\ell^{+}}{\ell^{-}}){{K}^{0}_{\mathrm{\scriptscriptstyle S}}}(\rightarrow{{\pi}^{+}}{{\pi}^{-}})
decays collected in Run 2
[
9
]
.
The considered final states are
{{J\mskip-3.0mu/\mskip-2.0mu\psi}}(\rightarrow{\mu^{-}}{\mu^{+}}){{K}^{0}_{\mathrm{\scriptscriptstyle S}}}
,
\phi(2S)(\rightarrow{\mu^{-}}{\mu^{+}}){{K}^{0}_{\mathrm{\scriptscriptstyle S}}}
and
{{J\mskip-3.0mu/\mskip-2.0mu\psi}}(\rightarrow e^{+}e^{-}){{K}^{0}_{\mathrm{\scriptscriptstyle S}}}
.
Due to the small
{{B}^{0}}
mass-width difference, the
{C\!P}
asymmetry is approximately
\mathcal{A}^{{C\!P}}(t)\approx S\sin(\Delta m_{d}t)-C\cos(\Delta m_{d}t)
, where
S
is equal to
\sin(2\beta)
in the SM, besides CKM-suppressed contributions from penguin topologies.
The determination of the
{C\!P}
observables requires a flavour-tagged and time-dependent analysis, resulting in
S_{\psi{{K}^{0}_{\mathrm{\scriptscriptstyle S}}}}=0.717\pm 0.013\pm 0.008
and
C_{\psi{{K}^{0}_{\mathrm{\scriptscriptstyle S}}}}=0.008\pm 0.012\pm 0.003
.
Figure
3
shows the measured
{C\!P}
asymmetry as a function of
{{B}^{0}}
decay time.

1.3
Measurement of
\Delta\Gamma_{s}

The measurements of the
{{B}^{0}_{s}}
decay-width difference
\Delta\Gamma_{s}
using
{{B}^{0}_{s}}\rightarrow{{J\mskip-3.0mu/\mskip-2.0mu\psi}}\phi
decays show a tension between the LHC experiments
[
10
]
.
Consequently, performing measurements using other decay modes is important.
As such,
\Delta\Gamma_{s}
is determined as the decay-with difference between
{C\!P}
-even
{{B}^{0}_{s}}\rightarrow{{J\mskip-3.0mu/\mskip-2.0mu\psi}}\eta^{\prime}
and mostly
{C\!P}
-odd
{{B}^{0}_{s}}\rightarrow{{J\mskip-3.0mu/\mskip-2.0mu\psi}}{{\pi}^{-}}{{\pi}^{+}}
decays, collected between 2011 and 2018
[
11
]
.
The yield ratio of these decays in bins of decay time depends on
{\Delta\Gamma_{{s}}}
, allowing for a
\chi^{2}
fit to obtain
{\Delta\Gamma_{{s}}}
.
Finally, the value of
\Delta\Gamma_{s}=0.087\pm 0.012\pm 0.009{\mathrm{\,ps}}^{-1}
is reported, which is compatible with the HFLAV average.

2
Charm measurements

{C\!P}
violation in
D
mesons has been observed for the first time in 2019
[
12
]
.
The compatibility of this result with SM is debated, therefore new measurements of
{C\!P}
violation in charm meson decays are crucial to clarify the picture
[
13
]
.
These proceedings discuss the time-dependent study of charm mixing and
{C\!P}
violation in
{{D}^{0}}\rightarrow{{K}^{+}}{{\pi}^{-}}
decays and the usage of the energy-test method to study
{C\!P}
violation in the
{{D}^{0}}\rightarrow{{K}^{0}_{\mathrm{\scriptscriptstyle S}}}{{K}^{-}}{{\pi}^{+}}
and
{{D}^{0}}\rightarrow{{\pi}^{-}}{{\pi}^{+}}{{\pi}^{0}}
decays.
The neutral
D
meson flavour at production in these measurements is determined by requiring it to originate from
{{D}^{*+}}\rightarrow{{D}^{0}}{{\pi}^{+}}
decays.

2.1
Charm mixing and
{C\!P}
violation with
{{D}^{0}}\rightarrow{{K}^{+}}{{\pi}^{-}}

In this measurement, the Run 2 dataset is exploited to probe mixing and
{C\!P}
-violating parameters by measuring the decay-time dependency of the ratio of wrong sign (WS)
{{D}^{0}}\rightarrow{{K}^{+}}{{\pi}^{-}}
and right sign (RS)
{{\kern 1.99997pt\overline{\kern-1.99997ptD}{}}{}^{0}}\rightarrow{{K}^{+}}{{\pi}^{-}}
decays,
R_{{K}{\pi}}^{+}(t)
, and the ratio of
{C\!P}
-conjugated processes,
R_{{K}{\pi}}^{-}(t)

[
14
]
.
The ratios are expanded as
R_{{K}{\pi}}^{\pm}(t)\approx R_{{K}{\pi}}(1\pm A_{{K}{\pi}})+\sqrt{R_{{K}{\pi}}(1\pm A_{{K}{\pi}})}(c_{{K}{\pi}}\pm\Delta c_{{K}{\pi}})(t/\tau_{{{D}^{0}}})+(c_{{K}{\pi}}^{\prime}\pm\Delta c_{{K}{\pi}}^{\prime})(t/\tau_{{{D}^{0}}})^{2}
, where
c_{{K}{\pi}}
and
c_{{K}{\pi}}^{\prime}
are sensitive to mixing parameters, while
A_{{K}{\pi}}
,
\Delta c_{{K}{\pi}}
and
\Delta c_{{K}{\pi}}^{\prime}
are sensitive
{C\!P}
violation in the decay, the mixing and their interference, respectively.
The WS-to-RS yield ratio in 18 bins of
{{D}^{0}}
decay time is used to extract
R_{K\pi}=(343.1\pm 2.0)\times 10^{-5}
,
c_{K\pi}=(51.4\pm 3.5)\times 10^{-4}
and
c^{\prime}_{K\pi}=(13\pm 4)\times 10^{-6}
.
No evidence for
{C\!P}
violation is found.

2.2
Search for
C\!P
violation using the energy-test method

A search for
{C\!P}
violation is conducted in the phase space of
{{D}^{0}}\rightarrow{{K}^{0}_{\mathrm{\scriptscriptstyle S}}}{{K}^{\pm}}{{\pi}^{\mp}}
decays
[
15
]
and
{{D}^{0}}\rightarrow{{\pi}^{-}}{{\pi}^{+}}{{\pi}^{0}}
decays
[
16
]
using the energy-test method.
This method studies local
{C\!P}
violation by checking if the phase spaces of
{{D}^{0}}
and
{{\kern 1.99997pt\overline{\kern-1.99997ptD}{}}{}^{0}}
decays follow the same underlying distribution.
A test statistic
T
is measured using the data which is compared with the
T
distribution under the null hypothesis.
The resulting p-values correspond to
70\%
for
{{D}^{0}}\rightarrow{{K}^{0}_{\mathrm{\scriptscriptstyle S}}}{{K}^{-}}{{\pi}^{+}}
,
66\%
for
{{D}^{0}}\rightarrow{{K}^{0}_{\mathrm{\scriptscriptstyle S}}}{{K}^{+}}{{\pi}^{-}}
and
62\%
for
{{D}^{0}}\rightarrow{{\pi}^{-}}{{\pi}^{+}}{{\pi}^{0}}
; showing no evidence for
{C\!P}
violation.

3
Conclusions

Great progress is made in both beauty and charm measurements in LHCb.
The experimental uncertainty on
\gamma
has dropped 3 degrees, with the latest HFLAV average providing
\gamma=(66.5^{+2.8}_{-2.9})^{\circ}

[
10
]
, whereas the indirect determination provides
\gamma=(66.3^{+0.7}_{-1.9})^{\circ}

[
17
]
.
The sensitivity gap between direct and indirect determinations of
\gamma
is narrowing, and closing this gap is one of the physics goals of LHCb.
Additionally, the most precise determinations of the CKM angles
\beta
and
\beta_{s}
are discussed.
Finally, mixing and
{C\!P}
violation in charm mesons is probed, further constraining the charm-mixing parameters and finding agreement with
{C\!P}
symmetry and the SM.
