Understanding Tieq Viet with Deep Learning Models - An Empirical Study
Nguyen Ha Thanh

Abstract
Deep learning is a powerful approach in recovering lost information as well as harder inverse function computation problems. When applied in natural language processing, this approach is essentially making use of context as a mean to recover information through likelihood maximization. Not long ago, a linguistic study called Tieq Viet was controversial among both researchers and society. We find this a great example to demonstrate the ability of deep learning models to recover lost information. In the proposal of Tieq Viet , some consonants in the standard Vietnamese are replaced. A sentence written in this proposal can be interpreted into multiple sentences in the standard version, with different meanings. The hypothesis that we want to test is whether a deep learning model can recover the lost information if we translate the text from Vietnamese to Tieq Viet .

2
Problem Formulation

Consonant phonemes in Vietnamese are represented by single or multiple characters. In
Tieq Viet
, existing phonemes are changed as the following:

•

Ch and Tr are replaced by C

•

Đ is replaced by D

•

Gh is replaced by G

•

Ph is replaced by F

•

C, and Q are replaced by K

•

Ng and Ngh are replaced by Q

•

Kh is replaced by X

•

Th is replaced by W

•

D, Gi and R are replaced by Z

•

Nh is replaced by N’

After replacement, the alphabet does not contain the letter Đ any more and is added with some letter such as F, J, W, Z. With such replacement of consonances, the ambiguities between the words are obvious. Some of the ambiguous examples are shown in Table
1
. Without understanding the context, it is impossible to restore the sentences into the standard Vietnamese.

The problem for restoring the sentences from
Tieq Viet
to standard Vietnamese can be formulated as follow:

•

C_{tieqviet}
is the character set of
Tieq Viet

•

C_{standard}
is the character set of the standard Vietnamese

•

Given a sentence
S_{tieqviet}=[c_{i}|c_{i}\in C_{tieqviet}]
obtained by converting a original
S_{standard}=[c_{i}|c_{i}\in C_{standard}]

•

The system need to restore the sentence as the original version
S_{standard}
.

Looking at Table
1
, we can see that there is no restoring rule that can be applied in every reverse conversion. An effective model solving this problem is not a simple mapping between one-to-one from characters in
C_{tieqviet}
to
C_{standard}
. In the next section, we describe our experiment in restoring sentence from
Tieq Viet
and verify the importance of context in language understanding. Given a sentence written in
Tieq Viet
, the model needs to restore the original in the standard Vietnamese.

3
Experiment

3.1
Data Preparation

The data for the experiment is crawled from Vietnamese digital news websites and converted into
Tieq Viet
by the rules described in the previous section. The dataset only contains pairs of sentences written in
Tieq Viet
and standard Vietnamese. No additional information is added into the dataset.

The training and validation set of data is divided from the dataset. The training set contains 500 pairs of sentences and the validation test contains 100 pairs of sentences.

In order to process the sentences with different length in a batch, we uniform all sentences into the same length. The maximum sequence length is set to 50 (characters), all sentence longer than 50 characters are pruned and the shorter ones are padded with

4
Conclusions

In this paper, we analyze the ability of deep learning models to recover the lost information caused by translating standard Vietnamese into
Tieq Viet
.
To remain as generality as possible, we use the simple architecture as LSTM in our experiment.
We also limit the amount of training data and testing data comparatively less than other tasks in NLP.
Our experiment suggests that deep learning models are able to recover the loss of information based on the context with good performance (96.32%).
This result also provides an empirical fact for scientific debates about
Tieq Viet
as well as other Vietnamese linguistic innovation proposals.
