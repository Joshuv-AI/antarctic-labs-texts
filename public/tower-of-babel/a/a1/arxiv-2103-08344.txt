Global weak solution to real bi-fluid system with magnetic field
Lin Ma Boling Guo Jie Shao

Abstract




Abstract.
We prove the existence of global weak solutions with finite energy to some two-fluid systems with magnetic field and the results suit for corresponding two-fluid systems. The proof method is mainly inspired by Novotný et al.
[
10
]
and Vasseur et al.
[
12
]
. For
academic~magnetic

bi\text{-}fluid~system
, we focus on the case of pressure law with ideal gases and the new ingredient is that we can make the proof more explicit without using the preposed hypotheses and remove some unnecessary conditions of the main theorem in Novotný et al.
[
10
]
. Meanwhile, the same proof method can be used for
real~magnetic

bi\text{-}fluid~system
with pressure law proposed in Vasseur et al.
[
12
]
.

2.1
Properties of the pressure function
P(\rho,n)

Recall
\rho=\alpha\rho_{+}
,
n=(1-\alpha)\rho_{-}
,
\mathfrak{P}_{+}\left(\rho_{+}\right)=\mathfrak{P}_{-}\left(\rho_{-}\right)
. Defining
\mathfrak{q}=\mathfrak{P}_{-}^{-1}\circ\mathfrak{P}_{+}
, where
\mathfrak{P}_{-}^{-1}(\cdot)=(\cdot)^{\frac{1}{\gamma^{-}}}
, then one has

\displaystyle\rho_{-}=\mathfrak{q}(\rho_{+})=\rho_{+}^{\frac{\gamma^{+}}{\gamma^{-}}},~~\rho_{+}=\mathfrak{q}^{-1}(\rho_{-})=\rho_{-}^{\frac{\gamma^{-}}{\gamma^{+}}}.

(2.1)

It follows that

\displaystyle n\rho_{+}=(1-\alpha)\rho_{-}\rho_{+}=(1-\alpha)\mathfrak{q}(\rho_{+})\rho_{+}

(2.2)

i.e.

\displaystyle\rho_{+}\mathfrak{q}\left(\rho_{+}\right)-\mathfrak{q}\left(\rho_{+}\right)\rho-n\rho_{+}=0.

(2.3)

From (
2.1
), (
2.2
) or (
2.3
), direct calculation gives

\left\{\begin{array}[]{l}\rho_{+}=\rho,~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~\text{if}~~n=0,\rho>0,\\
\mathfrak{q}(\rho_{+})=\rho_{-}=n,~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~\text{if}~~n>0,\rho=0,\\
\rho_{+}=\rho_{-}=0,~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~\quad\text{if}~~\rho=n=0.\end{array}\right.

(2.4)

For instance, if
n=0,\rho=\alpha\rho_{+}>0
, we have
\mathfrak{q}^{-1}(\rho_{-})=\rho_{+}>0
, thus
n=(1-\alpha)\rho_{-}=0
indicates that
\alpha=1
and
\rho_{+}=\rho
. For the remaining case
\rho>0,n>0
,
\rho=\alpha\rho_{+}
gives
\rho_{+}>0
. Set
f(\rho_{+}):=\rho_{+}\mathfrak{q}\left(\rho_{+}\right)-\mathfrak{q}\left(\rho_{+}\right)\rho-n\rho_{+}
,
then

\displaystyle f^{\prime}(\rho_{+})

\displaystyle=\rho_{+}\mathfrak{q}^{\prime}\left(\rho_{+}\right)+\mathfrak{q}\left(\rho_{+}\right)-\mathfrak{q}^{\prime}\left(\rho_{+}\right)\rho-n

\displaystyle=\mathfrak{q}^{\prime}(\rho_{+})\rho_{+}\left(1-\alpha\right)+\left(\mathfrak{q}\left(\rho_{+}\right)-n\right)>0,~~\text{for }\rho_{+}(1-\alpha)>0

and (
2.3
) admits a unique solution
\rho_{+}(\rho,n)
according to implicit function theorem. Consequently, for any
\rho\geqslant 0,n\geqslant 0,
(
2.3
) has a unique solution

\left\{\begin{array}[]{c}0<\rho_{+}=\rho_{+}(\rho,n)\in[\rho,\infty)\text{ if }\rho>0\text{ or }n>0\\
\rho_{+}(0,0)=0\end{array}\right\}

such that

\rho_{+}(\rho,0)=\rho,\quad\rho_{+}(0,n)=\mathfrak{q}^{-1}(n).

Based on the analysis above, we can research the properties of

.

Lemma 2.1

Assume

Proof:

(i)
\bm{\rho>0,n>0}

where we used
\rho_{+}(\delta\rho,0)=\delta\rho,\rho_{+}(0,\delta n)=\mathfrak{q}^{-1}(\delta n)
