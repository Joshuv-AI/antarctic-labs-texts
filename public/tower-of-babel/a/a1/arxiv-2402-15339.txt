Abstract
. In this article, we examine gradient type Ricci solitons and ( m , τ ) (m,\tau) -quasi Einstein solitons in generalized Robertson-Walker ( G ​ R ​ W GRW ) spacetimes. Besides, we demonstrate that in this scenario the G ​ R ​ W GRW spacetime presents the Robertson-Walker ( R ​ W RW ) spacetime and the perfect fluid ( P ​ F PF ) spacetime presents the phantom era. Consequently, we show that if a G ​ R ​ W GRW spacetime permits a gradient τ \tau - Einstein solitons, then it also represents a P ​ F PF spacetime under certain condition.
