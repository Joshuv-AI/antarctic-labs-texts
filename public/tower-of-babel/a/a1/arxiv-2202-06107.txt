Nonlinear Meissner effect in Nb 3 Sn coplanar resonators.
J. Makita 1 , C. Sundahl 2 , G. Ciovati 1,3 , C.B. Eom 2 , and A. Gurevich

Abstract
We investigated the nonlinear Meissner effect (NLME) in Nb 3 Sn thin film coplanar resonators by measuring the resonance frequency as a function of a parallel magnetic field at different temperatures. We used low rf power probing in films thinner than the London penetration depth λ ⁡ ( B ) \lambda(B) to significantly increase the field onset of vortex penetration and measure the NLME under equilibrium conditions. Contrary to the conventional quadratic increase of λ ⁡ ( B ) \lambda(B) with B B expected in s-wave superconductors, we observed a nearly linear increase of the penetration depth with B B . We concluded that this behavior of λ ⁡ ( B ) \lambda(B) is due to weak linked grain boundaries in our polycrystalline Nb 3 Sn films, which can mimic the NLME expected in a clean d-wave superconductor.

To compare the contributions of Meissner pairbreaking and weakly-coupled GBs to
\delta f(B)/f_{0}
