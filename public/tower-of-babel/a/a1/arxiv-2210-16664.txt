Aggregating regular norms
Anatoli Juditsky, Arkadi Nemirovski

Abstract
The subject of this paper is regularity-preserving aggregation of regular norms on finite-dimensional linear spaces. Regular norms were introduced in [ 5 ] and are closely related to “type 2” spaces [ 9 , Chapter 9] playing important role in 1) high-dimensional convex geometry and probability in Banach spaces, see [ 15 , 12 , 13 , 9 , 8 ] , and in 2) design of proximal first order algorithms for large-scale convex optimization with dimension-independent, or nearly so, complexity. Regularity, with moderate parameters, of a norm makes applicable, in a dimension-independent fashion, various geometric, probabilistic, and optimization-related results, thus motivating the subject of this paper—aggregations of regular norms resulting in controlled (and moderate) inflation of regularity parameters.

This paper focuses on

Theorem 2.1

(1)

(clearly, this indeed is a norm on

Finally, we have

Ellitopes.

A

Ellitope

\overline{X}\subset{\mathbf{R}}^{p}

\overline{{\cal X}}=P{\cal X}\hbox{\ with ${\cal X}$ given by (\ref{eeq1})},

(3)
