Infinitesimal CR Symmetries of Accidental CR Structures
C. Denson Hill, Joël Merker, Zhaohu Nie, Paweł Nurowski

Abstract
. In this companion paper to our article Accidental CR structures (arxiv.org, January 2023), thought of as an appendix not submitted for publication, we provide complete explicit lists of infinitesimal CR automorphisms for the concerned CR models having respective Lie algebra structures: 𝐄 I ​ I , 𝐄 I ​ I ​ I , 𝔰 ​ 𝔬 ​ ( ℓ − 1 , ℓ + 1 ) , 𝔰 ​ 𝔲 ​ ( p , q ) . {\bf E}_{II},\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ {\bf E}_{III},\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \mathfrak{so}(\ell-1,\ell+1),\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \mathfrak{su}(p,q). We start from our lists of quadric CR submanifolds M 2 ​ n + c ⊂ ℂ n + c M^{2n+c}\subset\mathbb{C}^{n+c} of codimension c > 1 c>1 which are shown to be accidental , in the sense that their CR symmetry groups are equal to (and not smaller than) the symmetry groups of the underlying real distribution structures — after forgetting the complex structure. Thanks to intensive symbolic computer explorations, we then determine embedded vector field generators of these CR symmetries Lie algebras, and we express them in extrinsic holomorphic coordinates, because intrinsic formulas would be too extended to be shown.



1.
Introduction

Any smooth manifold
M
can be locally equipped with a vector
subdistribution (subbundle)

H\subset TM
of any rank and of any codimension.
Such a pair
(M,H)
is called
almost Cauchy-Riemann

(almost CR for short) if
H
can be ‘
decorated
’

[
1
]
by a linear
operator
J\colon H\longrightarrow H

satisfying
J^{2}=-\,{\rm Id}
.
If
J
is
integrable
in the sense that
for any two sections
X,Y
of
H
:

\big([X,Y]-[JX,JY]\big)\,\in\,H,\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ J\big([X,Y]-[JX,JY]\big)=[JX,Y]+[X,JY],

then
J
is said to be
integrable
, and

(M,H,J)
is said to be a
CR manifold
.
One also calls
H=:T^{c}M
the
complex tangent bundle
,
necessarily of even rank.
We will assume positivity of CR dimension and of codimension:

1\,\leqslant\,{\rm CRdim}\,M\,:=\,\tfrac{1}{2}\,{\rm rank}\,T^{c}M,\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \dim\,M-2\,n\,=:\,c\,\geqslant\,1.

Both for a naked distribution structure
(M,H)
and
for a CR structure
(M,T^{c}M,J)
,
the two groups
G
and
G_{J}
of
automorphisms and their respective Lie algebras

\mathfrak{g}
and
\mathfrak{g}_{J}

of infinitesimal symmetries,
are presented and defined precisely
in
[
1
]
.
Of course:

G_{J}\subset G,\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \mathfrak{g}_{J}\,\subset\,\mathfrak{g},

the typical situation being that
G_{J}\subsetneqq G
and

\mathfrak{g}_{J}\subsetneqq\mathfrak{g}

are
proper inclusions
.

But (very) exceptionally,
equalities
G_{J}=G
and

\mathfrak{g}_{J}=\mathfrak{g}
can occur.
Therefore,
in
[
1
]
, such CR
structures
(M,T^{c}M,J)
for which
G_{J}=G
and

\mathfrak{g}_{J}=\mathfrak{g}

are called

accidental
, in the sense that
the generic situation that
G_{J}\subsetneqq G

is a
proper
subgroup is
"by accident" replaced with the "exceptional" equality
G_{J}=G
.

Applying purely algebraic Lie-theoretic methods,
Medori-Nacinovich
[
3
]

classified abstractly such accidental
CR structures with
\mathfrak{g}

simple
.
We would like to mention that
not all embeddings of such CR structures
into some complex space
\mathbb{C}^{N}

are known,
especially none is known in codimension
c>1
.

In this paper, which consists of
advanced extended formulas
,
our main aim is to
uncover
explicit algebraic faces of accidental
CR structures
,
by exhibiting complete lists of infinitesimal CR symmetries.

Under the form of a long ‘appendix’ not submitted for publication
in a journal,
we therefore complement our
article
[
1
]
,
which provided
a full list
embedded CR structures

in appropriate
\mathbb{C}^{N}
’s satisfying:

\bullet

they have real codimension
c>1
;

\bullet

the complex-tangential distribution
T^{c}M

is such that
[T^{c}M,T^{c}M]+T^{c}M=TM
;

\bullet

the local group
G_{J}
of CR automorphisms
of the CR structure
(M,T^{c}M,J)
is simple,
acts transitively on
M
,
and has isotropy
P
being a parabolic subgroup in
G
;

\bullet

the local symmetry group
G
of the naked real vector distribution

T^{c}M
on
M
coincides with the group
G_{J}=G

of CR automorphisms of
(M,T^{c}M,J)
, or, what is
the same,
\mathfrak{g}_{J}=\mathfrak{g}
.

In particular, the article
[
1
]

provided
striking
geometric realizations

of real simple Lie groups,
such as for example
two real forms
{\bf E_{II}}

and
{\bf E_{III}}
of the
exceptional simple complex Lie group
{\bf E_{6}}
.
These two
real simple exceptional Lie groups of dimension 78 are realized
as
groups of CR automorphisms of the corresponding CR
structures
, of CR dimension 8 and of codimension 8.

Thus, in the present paper ‘appended’
to
[
1
]
,
we show in particular two collections of 78

explicit
holomorphic vector fields
in
\mathbb{C}^{8+8}
which generate the respective
Lie algebras of infinitesimal CR symmetries
of these two embedded CR realizations of
{\bf E_{II}}

and of
{\bf E_{III}}
. We also show much more.

Background about Embedded CR Manifolds

It is known
[
4
]

that every transitive group action can be equipped
with some
real analytic
manifold structure.
It is also known that every real analytic (abstract) CR structure

(M,T^{c}M,J)
of CR dimension
n\geqslant 1
and
of codimension
c\geqslant 1

can be
embedded
as a CR-generic submanifold:

M^{2n+c}\,\subset\,\mathbb{C}^{n+c},

genericity
meaning that:

TM+JTM\,=\,T\mathbb{C}^{n+c}\big|_{M}.

Clearly:

T^{c}M\,=\,TM\cap JTM.

It is also known that there exist complex coordinates:

\big(z_{1},\dots,z_{n},\,w_{1},\dots,w_{c}\big),\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ z_{i}\,=\,x_{i}+{\scriptstyle{\sqrt{-1}}}\,y_{i},\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ w_{j}\,=\,u_{j}+{\scriptstyle{\sqrt{-1}}}\,v_{j},

in which such a CR-generic submanifold

M^{2n+c}\subset\mathbb{C}^{n+c}
is
locally graphed as:

[formula]

2\,u_{j}\,=\,\varphi_{j}\big(z,\overline{z},v\big)

with the
\varphi_{j}
’s being real analytic in some neighborhood of
the origin:

\varphi_{j}\,=\,\sum_{\alpha\in\mathbb{N}^{n}}\,\sum_{\beta\in\mathbb{N}^{n}}\,\sum_{\tau\in\mathbb{N}^{c}}\,\varphi_{j\alpha\beta\tau}\,z^{\alpha}\overline{z}^{\beta}v^{\tau},\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \big|\varphi_{j\alpha\beta\tau}\big|\,\leqslant\,C^{|\alpha|+|\beta|+|\tau|}.

When the CR structure is embedded,
the infinitesimal CR symmetries of
M

take the form
of
holomorphic
vector fields:

L\,=\,\sum_{i=1}^{n}\,Z_{i}(z,w)\,\partial_{z_{i}}+\sum_{j=1}^{c}\,W_{j}(z,w)\,\partial_{w_{j}},

whose (double) real part is
tangent

to the (extrinsic) CR-generic submanifold:

\displaystyle\mathfrak{hol}(M)

\displaystyle:=\,\big\{L\,\colon\,\,\big(L+\overline{L}\big)\big|_{M}\,\,\text{is tangent to}\,\,M\big\},

\displaystyle\mathfrak{aut}_{CR}(M)

\displaystyle:=\,2\,{\rm Re}\,\mathfrak{hol}(M).

This makes two uniquely defined
real
Lie algebras
of vector fields which are
isomorphic
. Usually,
only the holomorphic

L
is written, with the understanding that it comes
with its ‘
+\overline{L}
’,
as it is the case for
all
of the explicit formulas
exhibited
infra
.

For all accidental CR models
of
[
1
]

which have
quadratic
(see also below)
right-hand sides
\varphi_{j}=Q_{j}(z,\overline{z})

not depending
on
v
:

w_{j}+\overline{w}_{j}\,=\,Q_{j}(z,\overline{z}),

it is natural to attribute the weights:

[z^{i}]:=1=:[\overline{z}^{i}],\ \ \ \ \ \big[\partial_{z^{i}}\big]:=-1=:\big[\partial_{\overline{z}^{i}}\big],\ \ \ \ \ [w^{j}]:=2=:[\overline{w}^{j}],\ \ \ \ \ \big[\partial_{w^{j}}\big]:=-2=:\big[\partial_{\overline{w}^{j}}\big],

and it is easy to verify that
\mathfrak{hol}(M)
then splits into
a direct sum:

\mathfrak{hol}(M)\,=\,\bigoplus_{{\text{o}}\in\mathbb{Z}}\,\mathfrak{g}_{{\text{o}}},

with the graded pieces:

\mathfrak{g}_{{\text{o}}}\,=\,\big\{L\in\mathfrak{hol}(M)\colon\,\,[L]={\text{o}}\big\},

so that the elements of
\mathfrak{g}_{{\text{o}}}
are those vector fields
having only terms of constant weight
o
:

L_{{\text{o}}}\,:=\,\sum_{i=1}^{n}\,\bigg(\sum_{|\alpha|+|\tau|={\text{o}}+1}\,Z_{j\alpha\tau}\,z^{\alpha}\,w^{\tau}\bigg)\frac{\partial}{\partial{z_{i}}}+\sum_{j=1}^{c}\,\bigg(\sum_{|\alpha|+|\tau|={\text{o}}+2}\,W_{j\alpha\tau}\,z^{\alpha}\,w^{\tau}\bigg)\frac{\partial}{\partial{w_{j}}},

which satisfy that
L_{{\text{o}}}+\overline{L}_{{\text{o}}}

is tangent to
M
.

In fact, for all accidental CR models
of
[
1
]
,
all
\mathfrak{g}_{{\text{o}}}=0
are zero for
|{\text{o}}|\geqslant 3
, whence:

\mathfrak{hol}(M)\,=\,\mathfrak{g}_{-2}\oplus\mathfrak{g}_{-1}\oplus\mathfrak{g}_{0}\oplus\mathfrak{g}_{1}\oplus\mathfrak{g}_{2}\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \text{with}\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \dim\,\mathfrak{g}_{-{\text{o}}}\,=\,\dim\,\mathfrak{g}_{{\text{o}}}.

Therefore, for the CR-generic models
of
[
1
]
,
having simple accidental CR geometry,
we determined the full graded pieces

\mathfrak{g}_{{\text{o}}}
with
-2\leqslant{\text{o}}\leqslant 2

of
\mathfrak{hol}(M)
.
