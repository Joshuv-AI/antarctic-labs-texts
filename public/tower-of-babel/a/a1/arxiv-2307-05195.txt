Almost simple groups as flag-transitive automorphism groups of 2 2 -designs with λ = 2 \lambda=2
Seyed Hassan Alavi

Abstract
. In this article, we study 2 2 -designs with λ = 2 \lambda=2 admitting a flag-transitive almost simple automorphism group with socle a finite simple exceptional group of Lie type, and we prove that such a 2 2 -design does not exist. In conclusion, we present a classification of 2 2 -designs with λ = 2 \lambda=2 admitting flag-transitive and point-primitive automorphism groups of almost simple type, which states that such a 2 2 -design belongs to an infinite family of 2 2 -designs with parameter set ( ( 3 n − 1 ) / 2 , 3 , 2 ) ((3^{n}-1)/2,3,2) and X = PSL n ​ ( 3 ) X=\mathrm{PSL}_{n}(3) for some n ⩾ 3 n\geqslant 3 , or it is isomorphic to the 2 2 -design with parameter set ( 6 , 3 , 2 ) (6,3,2) , ( 7 , 4 , 2 ) (7,4,2) , ( 10 , 4 , 2 ) (10,4,2) , ( 10 , 4 , 2 ) (10,4,2) , ( 11 , 5 , 2 ) (11,5,2) , ( 28 , 7 , 2 ) (28,7,2) , ( 28 , 3 , 2 ) (28,3,2) , ( 36 , 6 , 2 ) (36,6,2) , ( 126 , 6 , 2 ) (126,6,2) or ( 176 , 8 , 2 ) (176,8,2) .

2.
Preliminaries

In this section, we state some useful facts in both design theory and group theory.

Lemma 2.1
.

Let
\mathcal{D}
be a
2
-design with parameters set
(v,k,\lambda)
. Then

(a)

r(k-1)=\lambda(v-1)
;

(b)

vr=bk
;

(c)

v\leqslant b
and
k\leqslant r
;

(d)

\lambda v<r^{2}
.

Proof.

Parts (a) and (b) follow immediately by simple counting. The inequality
v\leqslant b
is the Fisher’s inequality
[
15
, p. 57]
, and so by applying part (b), we have that
k\leqslant r
. By part (a) and (c), we easily observe that
r^{2}>r(k-1)=\lambda(v-1)>\lambda v
, and so
\lambda v<r^{2}
, as desired.
∎

Lemma
2.2
below is an elementary result on maximal subgroups of finite almost simple groups.

Lemma 2.2
.

[
3
, Lemma 2.2]

Let
G
be an almost simple group with socle
X
, and let
H
be maximal in

not containing

. Then
G=HX
and

|H|
divides
|\mathrm{Out}(X)|\cdot|H\cap X|
.

Lemma 2.3
.

Suppose that
\mathcal{D}
is block design with parameters
(v,k,\lambda)
admitting a flag-transitive and point-primitive almost simple automorphism group

Proof.

Note that

∎
