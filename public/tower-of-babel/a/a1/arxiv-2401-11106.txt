Study on the mixing of Ξ c \Xi_{c} and Ξ c ′ \Xi^{\prime}_{c} by the transition Ξ b → Ξ ( ′ ) c \Xi_{b}\to\Xi^{(^{\prime})}_{c}
Hong-Wei Ke 1 , Gang-Yang Fang 1 and Yan-Liang Shi 2

Abstract
Recently, the LHCb collaboration has observed the decays Ξ b 0 → Ξ c + ​ D s − \Xi^{0}_{b}\to\Xi^{+}_{c}D^{-}_{s} and Ξ b − → Ξ c 0 ​ D s − \Xi^{-}_{b}\to\Xi^{0}_{c}D^{-}_{s} . They measured the relative branching fractions times the ratio of beauty-baryon production cross-sections ℛ ⁡ ( Ξ b 0 Λ b ) ≡ σ ⁡ ( Ξ b 0 ) σ ⁡ ( Λ b 0 ) × B ⁡ ( Ξ b 0 → Ξ c + ​ D s − ) B ⁡ ( Λ b 0 → Λ c + ​ D s − ) \mathcal{R}(\frac{\Xi^{0}_{b}}{\Lambda_{b}})\equiv\frac{\sigma(\Xi_{b}^{0})}{\sigma(\Lambda^{0}_{b})}\times\frac{B(\Xi^{0}_{b}\to\Xi^{+}_{c}D^{-}_{s})}{B(\Lambda^{0}_{b}\to\Lambda^{+}_{c}D^{-}_{s})} and ℛ ⁡ ( Ξ b − Λ b ) ≡ σ ⁡ ( Ξ b − ) σ ⁡ ( Λ b 0 ) × B ⁡ ( Ξ b − → Ξ c 0 ​ D s − ) B ⁡ ( Λ b 0 → Λ c + ​ D s − ) \mathcal{R}(\frac{\Xi^{-}_{b}}{\Lambda_{b}})\equiv\frac{\sigma(\Xi^{-}_{b})}{\sigma(\Lambda^{0}_{b})}\times\frac{B(\Xi^{-}_{b}\to\Xi^{0}_{c}D^{-}_{s})}{B(\Lambda^{0}_{b}\to\Lambda^{+}_{c}D^{-}_{s})} . Once the ratio σ ⁡ ( Ξ b 0 ) σ ⁡ ( Λ b 0 ) \frac{\sigma(\Xi_{b}^{0})}{\sigma(\Lambda^{0}_{b})} or σ ⁡ ( Ξ b − ) σ ⁡ ( Λ b 0 ) \frac{\sigma(\Xi_{b}^{-})}{\sigma(\Lambda^{0}_{b})} is known, one can determine the relative branching fractions which can be used to exam the mixing of Ξ c \Xi_{c} and Ξ c ′ \Xi^{\prime}_{c} . In previous literature, Ξ c \Xi_{c} and Ξ c ′ \Xi^{\prime}_{c} were assumed to belong to SU(3) F antitriple and sextet, respectively. However, recent experimental measurements such as the ratio Γ ⁡ ( Ξ c ​ c → Ξ c ​ π + ) / Γ ⁡ ( Ξ c ​ c → Ξ c ′ ​ π + ) \Gamma(\Xi_{cc}\to\Xi_{c}\pi^{+})/\Gamma(\Xi_{cc}\to\Xi^{\prime}_{c}\pi^{+}) indicate the spin-flavor structures of Ξ c \Xi_{c} and Ξ c ′ \Xi^{\prime}_{c} are a mixture of Ξ c 3 ¯ \Xi^{\bar{3}}_{c} and Ξ c 6 \Xi^{6}_{c} . The exact value of mixing angle θ \theta is still under debate. In theoretical models, the mixing angle was fitted to be about 16.27 ∘ ± 2.30 ∘ 16.27^{\circ}\pm 2.30^{\circ} or 85.54 ∘ ± 2.30 ∘ 85.54^{\circ}\pm 2.30^{\circ} based on decay channels Ξ c ​ c → Ξ ( ′ ) c ​ c \Xi_{cc}\to\Xi^{(^{\prime})}_{cc} . While in lattice calculation, a small angle ( 1.2 ∘ ± 0.1 ∘ 1.2^{\circ}\pm 0.1^{\circ} ) is preferred. To address such discrepancy and test the mixing of Ξ c \Xi_{c} and Ξ c ′ \Xi^{\prime}_{c} , here we propose the analysis of semileptonic and non-leptonic decays of Ξ b → Ξ c \Xi_{b}\to\Xi_{c} and Ξ b → Ξ ′ c \Xi_{b}\to\Xi^{{}^{\prime}}_{c} . We calculate the decay rate of Ξ b → Ξ c \Xi_{b}\to\Xi_{c} and Ξ b → Ξ ′ c \Xi_{b}\to\Xi^{{}^{\prime}}_{c} based on light-front quark model and study the effect of the mixing angle on the ratios of weak decays Ξ b → Ξ c \Xi_{b}\to\Xi_{c} and Ξ b → Ξ ′ c \Xi_{b}\to\Xi^{{}^{\prime}}_{c} . In particular, we find the transition Ξ b → Ξ ′ c \Xi_{b}\to\Xi^{{}^{\prime}}_{c} can be an ideal channel to verify the mixing and extract the mixing angle because in theory the decay rate would be extremely tiny without mixing. Our calculation suggests a measurement of Ξ b → Ξ c ′ \Xi_{b}\to\Xi^{\prime}_{c} can be feasible in the near future, which will help to test flavor mixing angle θ \theta and elucidate the mechanism of decay of heavy baryons.

I
Introduction

Recently the LHCb collaboration observed the decays

\Xi^{0}_{b}\to\Xi^{+}_{c}D^{-}_{s}
and
\Xi^{-}_{b}\to\Xi^{0}_{c}D^{-}_{s}
LHCb:2023ngz
.
They measured the relative branching fractions times the ratio of the corresponding
beauty-baryon production cross-sections

\mathcal{R}(\frac{\Xi^{0}_{b}}{\Lambda_{b}})\equiv\frac{\sigma(\Xi_{b}^{0})}{\sigma(\Lambda^{0}_{b})}\times\frac{B(\Xi^{0}_{b}\to\Xi^{+}_{c}D^{-}_{s})}{B(\Lambda^{0}_{b}\to\Lambda^{+}_{c}D^{-}_{s})}=15.8\pm 1.1\pm 0.6\pm 7.7

and
\mathcal{R}(\frac{\Xi^{-}_{b}}{\Lambda_{b}})\equiv\frac{\sigma(\Xi^{-}_{b})}{\sigma(\Lambda^{0}_{b})}\times\frac{B(\Xi^{-}_{b}\to\Xi^{0}_{c}D^{-}_{s})}{B(\Lambda^{0}_{b}\to\Lambda^{+}_{c}D^{-}_{s})}=16.9\pm 1.3\pm 0.9\pm 4.3
, which suggests experimental measurement of transitions
\Xi_{b}\to\Xi_{c}
and
\Xi_{b}\to\Xi^{\prime}_{c}
can be promising in the near future. These channels can be used to probe the possible mixing of
\Xi_{c}
and
\Xi^{\prime}_{c}
states.

Historically,
\Xi_{c}
and
\Xi^{\prime}_{c}
were identified as SU(3)
F
antitriple and sextet of flavor symmetry, respectively. For these kinds of heavy baryons, usually the two light quarks
q
(denotes
u
or
d
) and
s
are assumed to form a subsystem called
diquark
Ebert:2006rp
;
Korner:1992wi
. In previous literature, a presupposition is the spin-flavor structure of
\Xi_{c}
is
[qs]_{0}c

and that of
\Xi^{{}^{\prime}}_{c}
is
[qs]_{1}c
where the subscript 0 or 1 represents the total spin of the
qs
subsystem.

However, recent experimental data such as the ratio of the branching fraction

\Xi_{cc}\to\Xi^{\prime}_{c}\pi^{+}
relative to that of the decay

\Xi_{cc}\to\Xi_{c}\pi^{+}

LHCb:2022rpd
indicate the spin-flavor structures of
\Xi_{c}
and
\Xi^{\prime}_{c}
are not pure
[qs]_{0}c
and
[qs]_{1}c
(they are denoted as
\Xi^{\bar{3}}_{c}
and
\Xi^{6}_{c}
, respectively, where the superscripts
\bar{3}
and
6
correspond to SU(3)
F
anti-triple and sextet) but mixtures of them. In this case, we can use a mixing angle
\theta
(
0<\theta<\pi
) to describe the composition of
\Xi^{\bar{3}}_{c}
and
\Xi^{6}_{c}
in flavor-spin wave functions of
\Xi_{c}
and
\Xi^{\prime}_{c}
, i.e.,
|\Xi_{c}\rangle={\rm cos}\theta\,|\Xi^{\bar{3}}_{c}\rangle+{\rm sin}\theta\,|\Xi^{6}_{c}\rangle
and
|\Xi^{\prime}_{c}\rangle=-{\rm sin}\theta\,|\Xi^{\bar{3}}_{c}\rangle+{\rm cos}\theta\,|\Xi^{6}_{c}\rangle
. When
\theta
is set to 0, i.e.,
\Xi_{c}=\Xi^{\bar{3}}_{c}
and
\Xi^{{}^{\prime}}_{c}=\Xi^{6}_{c}
, the structures of
\Xi_{c}
and
\Xi^{{}^{\prime}}_{c}
restore the original setting supposed by the authors of Ref.
Korner:1992wi
;
Ebert:2006rp
. Such mixing is a reflection of the breaking of SU(3) flavor symmetry, which is due to the mass difference between
s
and
u,d
quarks.

To determine the mixing angle between
\Xi_{c}
and
\Xi^{{}^{\prime}}_{c}
, different approaches has been proposed while a consensus has not been achieved. One approach is to analyze transition rates of these state based on phenomenological models. In Ref.
Ke:2022gxm
, we fixed the mixing angle to be
16.27^{\circ}\pm 2.30^{\circ}
or
85.54^{\circ}\pm 2.30^{\circ}
by fitting the data
\Gamma(\Xi_{cc}\to\Xi_{c}^{{}^{\prime}+}\pi^{+})/\Gamma(\Xi_{cc}\to\Xi_{c}\pi^{+})
. Geng et. al.
Geng:2022yxb
;
Geng:2022xfz
obtain the value is
24.66^{\circ}
from the mass spectra. Another approach is lattice simulation. Recent lattice calculation
Liu:2023feb
prefers
1.2^{\circ}\pm 0.1^{\circ}
, which is much smaller than those from phenomenological analysis.

To better understand the mixing mechanism and obtain a precise measurement of mixing angle of
\Xi_{c}
and
\Xi^{{}^{\prime}}_{c}
, more experimental data involving
\Xi_{c}
and
\Xi^{\prime}_{c}
are needed. Inspired by recent data from LHCb on
\Xi_{b}\to\Xi_{c}D_{s}
, here we apply light-front quark model
Jaus
;
Ji:1992yf
;
Cheng:1996if
;
Cheng:2003sm
;
Lu:2007sg
;
Li:2010bb
;
Ke:2009ed
;
Ke:2010
;
Choi:2007se
;
Ke:2009mn
;
Ke:2011fj
;
Ke:2011mu
;
Ke:2011jf
;
Chua:2018lfa
;
Yu:2017zst
;
Ke:2017eqo
;
pentaquark1
;
pentaquark2
;
Tawfiq:1998nk
;
Chang:2018zjq
;
Geng:2022xpn
framework to study the semileptonic and non-leptonic decays of
\Xi_{b}\to\Xi_{c}
and
\Xi_{b}\to\Xi^{\prime}_{c}
, and estimate the feasibility to extract the mixing angle from these channels.

The light-front quark model has been widely used to study light-quark systems. In our earlier work we extended the light-quark model to study baryon with heavy quark-diquark picture
Ke:2007tg
;
Ke:2012wa
;
Wei:2009np
. Later the three-quark picture of baryon was adopted
Ke:2019smy
;
Ke:2019lcf
;
Lu:2023rmq
. In this study we also apply the three-quark picture to analyze decays of heavy-baryon. In the calculation, we apply the spectator approximation to the light diquark components.

This paper is
organized as follows: after the introduction, in section II we discuss the form factors for the transition
\Xi_{b}\rightarrow\Xi^{(^{\prime})}_{c}

in the light-front quark model. Our numerical results for
\Xi_{b}\rightarrow\Xi^{(^{\prime})}_{c}
are presented in section
III. The section IV is devoted to our conclusion and discussions.

II
the form factors of
\Xi_{b}\to\Xi_{c}
and
\Xi_{b}\to\Xi^{{}^{\prime}}_{c}
in LFQM

The leading Feynman diagram responsible for the weak decay

\Xi_{b}\to\Xi_{c}^{(^{\prime})}

.
During this process,

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\bar{u}_{\Xi_{c}}(P^{\prime},S^{\prime}_{z})\left[\gamma_{\mu}g_{1}(q^{2})+i\sigma_{\mu\nu}\frac{q^{\nu}}{M_{\Xi_{b}}}g_{2}(q^{2})+\frac{q_{\mu}}{M_{\Xi_{b}}}g_{3}(q^{2})\right]\gamma_{5}u_{\Xi_{b}}(P,S_{z}).

IV
Conclusions and discussions

In this work we study the transition rates of
\Xi_{b}\to\Xi^{(^{\prime})}_{c}
in the light front quark model with a three-quark
picture of baryon. To correctly compute the transition rates, it is important to properly assess the spin-flavor structures of initial and final states.
In the three-quark picture, the two light quarks constitute a diquark which combine with a heavy quark to form baryons
\Xi_{b}
and
\Xi_{c}
. In previous studies, the light
qs
pair in
\Xi_{c}
(
\Xi^{{}^{\prime}}_{c}
) was are assumed be to the eigenstates (scalar or pseudovector ) of SU(3) flavor symmetry. However, experimental measurements such as the ratio
\Gamma(\Xi^{++}_{cc}\to\Xi^{+}_{c}\pi)/\Gamma(\Xi^{++}_{cc}\to\Xi^{{}^{\prime}+}_{c}\pi)

LHCb:2022rpd
suggest the spin-flavor structures of
\Xi_{c}
and
\Xi^{\prime}_{c}
should be a mixture of
\Xi^{\bar{3}}_{c}
and
\Xi^{6}_{c}
.
Therefore, here we introduce a mixing angle
\theta
to define the state
|\Xi_{c}\rangle
as
{\rm cos}\theta\,|\Xi^{\bar{3}}_{c}\rangle+{\rm sin}\theta\,|\Xi^{6}_{c}\rangle
, and
|\Xi^{{}^{\prime}}_{c}\rangle
as
-{\rm sin}\theta\,|\Xi^{\bar{3}}_{c}\rangle+{\rm cos}\theta\,|\Xi^{6}_{c}\rangle
. Because of heavy quark symmetry, we can similarly define
|\Xi_{b}\rangle
as a mixture of states with the same mixing angle:
{\rm cos}\theta\,|\Xi^{\bar{3}}_{b}\rangle+{\rm sin}\theta\,|\Xi^{6}_{b}\rangle
.

Based on proposed spin-flavor structures of
\Xi_{b}
,
\Xi_{c}
and
\Xi^{{}^{\prime}}_{c}
, we calculate semileptonic and nonleptonic decay of
\Xi_{b}\to\Xi_{c}
with specific value of mixing angle (
\theta
=
0^{\circ}
,
16.27^{\circ}
and
85.54^{\circ}
) used in previous analysis
Ke:2022gxm
. We find the decay width and the asymmetry parameter at
\theta=85.54^{\circ}
are very different with those without mixing (
\theta=0^{\circ}
). However for the mixing angle
\theta=16.27^{\circ}
, only the decay width has a significant decrease relative to the result without mixing.

Following this theoretical framework, we also evaluate the rates of semileptonic decay and nonleptonic decay of

\Xi_{b}\to\Xi^{\prime}_{c}
. Since the form factors
f^{\prime}_{i}={\rm cos}\theta{\rm sin}\,\theta(f^{v}_{i}-f^{s}_{i})
,
g^{\prime}_{i}={\rm cos}\theta{\rm sin}\,\theta(g^{v}_{i}-g^{s}_{i})
, the theoretical decay rates are proportional to the value
{\rm cos}^{2}\theta{\rm sin}^{2}\,\theta
, hence we only display results with
\theta=16.27^{\circ}
for the purpose of illustration.
Our numerical results indicate that the decay rates of these decays are about ten to twenty times smaller than the values of the transitions
\Xi_{b}\to\Xi_{c}
, which can be feasible to measure experimentally in the near future. Without a mixing of flavor eigenstates, the rates of the transitions
\Xi_{b}\to\Xi^{\prime}_{c}
should be very tiny (about the same order of the transition of
\Lambda_{b}\to\Sigma_{c}
). However, a modest mixing angle such as
\theta=16.27^{\circ}
will make the transition rate large enough for measurements, which makes these channels ideal for studying the mixing of
\Xi_{c}
and
\Xi^{\prime}_{c}
(
\Xi_{b}
and
\Xi^{\prime}_{b}
). We hope measurement of those decay channels of
\Xi_{b}\to\Xi^{\prime}_{c}
will be available in the near future, which will help us to test flavor mixing angle
\theta
and elucidate the mechanism of decay of heavy baryons.
