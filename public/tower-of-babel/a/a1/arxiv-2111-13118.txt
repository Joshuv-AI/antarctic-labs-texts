The role of q q -spin singlet pairs of physical spins in the dynamical properties of the spin- 1 / 2 1/2 Heisenberg-Ising X ​ X ​ Z XXZ chain
José M. P. Carmelo, Pedro D. Sacramento

Abstract
Dynamical correlation functions contain important physical information on correlated spin models. Here a dynamical theory suitable suitable to the Δ = 1 \Delta=1 isotropic spin- 1 / 2 1/2 Heisenberg chain in a longitudinal magnetic field is extended to anisotropy Δ > 1 \Delta>1 . The aim of this paper is the study of the ( k , ω ) (k,\omega) -plane line shape of the spin dynamical structure factor components S + ⁣ − ​ ( k , ω ) S^{+-}(k,\omega) , S − ⁣ + ​ ( k , ω ) S^{-+}(k,\omega) , and S z ​ z ​ ( k , ω ) S^{zz}(k,\omega) of the Δ > 1 \Delta>1 spin- 1 / 2 1/2 Heisenberg-Ising chain in a longitudinal magnetic field near their ( k , ω ) (k,\omega) -plane sharp peaks. However, the extension of the theory to anisotropy Δ > 1 \Delta>1 requires as a first step the clarification of the nature of a specific type of elementary magnetic configurations in terms of physical spins 1 / 2 1/2 . To reach that goal, that the spin S ​ U ​ ( 2 ) SU(2) symmetry of the isotropic Δ = 1 \Delta=1 point is in the case of anisotropy Δ > 1 \Delta>1 replaced by a continuous quantum group deformed q q -spin S ​ U q ​ ( 2 ) SU_{q}(2) symmetry plays a key role. For Δ > 1 \Delta>1 , spin projection S z S^{z} remains a good quantum number whereas spin S S is not, being replaced by the q q -spin S q S_{q} in the eigenvalue of the Casimir generator of the continuous S ​ U q ​ ( 2 ) SU_{q}(2) symmetry. Based on the isomorphism between the irreducible representations of the Δ = 1 \Delta=1 spin S ​ U ​ ( 2 ) SU(2) symmetry and Δ > 1 \Delta>1 continuous S ​ U q ​ ( 2 ) SU_{q}(2) symmetry and on their relation to the occupancy configurations of the Bethe-ansatz quantum numbers one finds that the elementary magnetic configurations under study are q q -spin neutral. This determines the form of S S matrices on which the extended dynamical theory relies. They are found in this paper to describe the scattering of n n -particles whose relation to configurations of physical spins 1 / 2 1/2 is established. Specifically, their internal degrees of freedom refer to unbound q q -spin singlet pairs of physical spins 1 / 2 1/2 described by n = 1 n=1 real single Bethe rapidities and to n = 2 , 3 , … n=2,3,... bound such q q -spin singlet pairs described by Bethe n n -strings for n > 1 n>1 . There is a relationship between the negativity and the length of the momentum k k interval of the k k dependent exponents that control the power-law line shape of the spin dynamical structure factor components near the lower threshold of a given ( k , ω ) (k,\omega) -plane continuum and the amount of spectral weight over the latter. Using such a relationship, one finds that in the thermodynamic limit the significant spectral weight contributions from Bethe n n -strings at a finite longitudinal magnetic field refer to specific two-parametric ( k , ω ) (k,\omega) -plane gapped continua in the spectra of the spin dynamical structure factor components S + ⁣ − ​ ( k , ω ) S^{+-}(k,\omega) and S z ​ z ​ ( k , ω ) S^{zz}(k,\omega) . In contrast to the Δ = 1 \Delta=1 isotropic chain, excited energy eigenstates including up to n = 3 n=3 Bethe n n -strings lead to finite spectral-weight contributions to S + ⁣ − ​ ( k , ω ) S^{+-}(k,\omega) . Most spectral weight stems though from excited energy eigenstates whose q q -spin singlet S z = S q = 0 S^{z}=S_{q}=0 pairs of physical spins 1 / 2 1/2 are all unbound. It is associated with ( k , ω ) (k,\omega) -plane continua in the spectra of the spin dynamical structure factor components that are gapless at some specific momentum values. We derive analytical expressions for the line shapes of S + ⁣ − ​ ( k , ω ) S^{+-}(k,\omega) , S − ⁣ + ​ ( k , ω ) S^{-+}(k,\omega) , and S z ​ z ​ ( k , ω ) S^{zz}(k,\omega) valid in the vicinity of ( k , ω ) (k,\omega) -plane lines of sharp peaks. Those are mostly located at and just above lower thresholds of ( k , ω ) (k,\omega) -plane continua associated with both states with only unbound q q -spin singlet pairs and states populated by such pairs and a single n = 2 n=2 or n = 3 n=3 n n -particle. Our results provide physically interesting and important information on the microscopic processes that determine the dynamical properties of the non-perturbative spin- 1 / 2 1/2 Heisenberg-Ising chain in a longitudinal magnetic field.

Where in the Bethe-ansatz solution are the unpaired physical spins
1/2

Out of the

1/2

N_{1}

1

n
-string-particles are for

n
-strings.
In the case of
S_{q}>0

solution’s quantities is stored the information on the
M=2S_{q}
unpaired physical spins
1/2

The clarification of this issue involves a squeezed space
construction

.
Each Bethe-ansatz
n
-band is found to correspond in the thermodynamic limit
to a
n
-squeezed lattice with

of such sites are not occupied by

-particles for
n=1

and by
n
-string-particles for
n>1

n
-squeezed lattice unoccupied sites.

Out of the
N
sites of the original lattice, the occupancies of

2S_{q}
such sites by the
M=2S_{q}
unpaired physical spins
1/2
remain invariant under
the squeezed space construction. This important symmetry refers to each of the local configurations
whose superposition generates
an energy eigenstate.

Information on the
M=2S_{q}
unpaired physical spins
1/2
is then found to be stored by the Bethe-ansatz solution
in the
n
-squeezed lattice’s

unoccupied sites. Those are directly related
to the
N^{h}_{n}

n
-holes. Out of the
N^{h}_{n}=2S_{q}+\sum_{n^{\prime}=n+1}^{\infty}2(n^{\prime}-n)N_{n^{\prime}}
, Eq. (
5
),
unoccupied sites of each
n
-squeezed lattice, a number
2S_{q}
of such sites is in the original lattice
singly occupied by unpaired physical spins
1/2
.

For energy eigenstates without
n
-strings, one has that

. In that case all

-holes describe the translational degrees of freedom of the

unpaired physical spins
1/2
. Such

-holes are often in the literature identified with spinons,
our results clarifying their relation to the unpaired physical spins
1/2

Concluding remarks

One of the aims of this paper is the clarification of the nature in terms of physical spins
1/2
configurations
of scientifically important and interesting elementary magnetic configurations.
We have exactly shown that such elementary magnetic configurations, that can occur either
unbound and described by

A direct consequence of the neutral
