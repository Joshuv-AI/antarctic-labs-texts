Chiral symmetry-breaking corrections to strong decays of D s ​ 0 ∗ ​ ( 2317 ) D_{s0}^{*}(2317) and D s ​ 1 ′ ( 2460 ) D_{s1}^{{}^{\prime}}(2460) in HH χ \chi PT
Jin-Yun Wu, Yong-Lu Liu, Jian-Rong Zhang, and Ming-Qiu Huang

Abstract
The strong decays of two narrow mesons D s ​ 0 ∗ ​ ( 2317 ) D_{s0}^{*}(2317) and D s ​ 1 ′ ( 2460 ) D_{s1}^{{}^{\prime}}(2460) are studied within the framework of heavy hadron chiral perturbation theory. Up to next-to-leading order in 1 / Λ χ 1/\Lambda_{\chi} , by a fit to the experimental widths of their nonstrange partners, the chiral symmetry-breaking coupling constants are extracted. The single-pion decay widths are estimated to be Γ ⁡ ( D s ​ 0 ∗ ​ ( 2317 ) → D s + ​ π 0 ) = 9.2 ± 2.3 \Gamma(D_{s0}^{*}(2317)\to D_{s}^{+}\pi^{0})=9.2\pm 2.3 KeV and Γ ( D s ​ 1 ′ ( 2460 ) → D s ∗ ⁣ + π 0 ) = 9.0 ± 2.1 \Gamma(D_{s1}^{{}^{\prime}}(2460)\to D_{s}^{*+}\pi^{0})=9.0\pm 2.1 KeV, respectively, which are consistent with the experimental constraints and comparable with other theoretical predictions. The numerical analysis shows that chiral-symmetry corrections to the decay widths are significant. Applications and predictions for the corresponding beauty mesons are also provided.

II
The chiral Lagrangian

The strong decays of excited heavy-light mesons involve the
emission of soft pions and kaons, and hence it is useful to analyze these
interactions with the chiral perturbation theory
chiraltheory
.
The octet of light pseudoscalar mesons is introduced through the definition

\Sigma=\xi^{2}={\mbox{exp}}(2i\mathcal{M}/f_{\pi})
, where

\displaystyle\mathcal{M}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\pi^{i}\lambda^{i}=\left(\begin{array}[]{ccc}\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\pi^{0}+\frac{1}{\sqrt{6}}\eta&\pi^{+}&K^{+}\\
\pi^{-}&\frac{-1}{\sqrt{2}}\pi^{0}+\frac{1}{\sqrt{6}}\eta&K^{0}\\
K^{-}&\bar{K}^{0}&-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{3}}\eta\\
\end{array}\right).

The heavy-light mesons are customarily cataloged by the total
angular momentum of the light degrees of freedom
s_{l}^{p}
(
p
denotes
the parity), which is a good quantum number because of heavy quark spin
symmetry in the heavy quark limit
m_{Q}\rightarrow\infty
. In this paper, only two doublets,

H
doublet (
0^{-},1^{-}
) and
S
doublet (
0^{+},1^{+}
), corresponding to
s_{l}^{p}=1/2^{-},1/2^{+}

are discussed, which can be respectively represented by the superfields

H_{a}=\frac{1+v{\!\!\!/\penalty}}{2}[P^{*}_{a\mu}\gamma^{\mu}-P_{a}\gamma_{5}]

(
a=u,d,s
, a light flavor index), where
P^{*}_{a\mu}
and
P_{a}

annihilate the vector and pseudoscalar mesons, and

S_{a}=\frac{1+v{\!\!\!/\penalty}}{2}[P^{{}^{\prime}}_{1a\mu}\gamma^{\mu}\gamma_{5}-P_{0a}^{*}]
for the axial-vector

P^{{}^{\prime}}_{1a\mu}
and scalar
P_{0a}^{*}
mesons.

Considering heavy quark spin-flavor symmetry and light quark chiral symmetry,
an effective Lagrangian responsible for the strong decay

S\rightarrow HM
(
M
is a light pseudoscalar meson) can be written with
these superfields. The leading order contribution in
1/\Lambda_{\chi}
and

1/m_{Q}
is

\mathcal{L}_{mix}=hTr[\bar{H}_{b}S_{a}\mathcal{A}{\!\!\!/\penalty}_{ab}\gamma_{5}]+h.c..

(6)

According to Refs.
chiral-L
;
Fajfer2006
, the corresponding chiral
symmetry breaking corrections to the Lagrangian Eq. (
6
) to next-to-leading
order in
1/\Lambda_{\chi}
read

\displaystyle\mathcal{L}_{mix}^{sb}

\displaystyle=1/\Lambda_{\chi}\{\kappa_{1}Tr[(\bar{H}S\mathcal{A}{\!\!\!/\penalty}\gamma_{5})_{ab}(m^{\xi}_{q})_{ba}]+\kappa_{2}Tr[(\bar{H}S\mathcal{A}{\!\!\!/\penalty}\gamma_{5})_{aa}(m^{\xi}_{q})_{bb}]

(7)

\displaystyle{}+\kappa_{3}Tr[\bar{H}_{a}S_{a}\mathcal{A}{\!\!\!/\penalty}_{bc}\gamma_{5}(m^{\xi}_{q})_{cb}]+\kappa_{4}Tr[\bar{H}_{c}S_{a}\mathcal{A}{\!\!\!/\penalty}_{bc}\gamma_{5}(m^{\xi}_{q})_{ab}]

\displaystyle{}+\kappa_{5}Tr[\bar{H}_{a}S_{b}iv\cdot\mathcal{D}_{bc}\mathcal{A}{\!\!\!/\penalty}_{ca}\gamma_{5}]+\kappa_{6}Tr[\bar{H}_{a}S_{b}i\mathcal{D}{\!\!\!/\penalty}_{bc}v\cdot\mathcal{A}_{ca}\gamma_{5}]\}+h.c..

Meanwhile, the effective Lagrangian responsible for
\eta-\pi^{0}
mixing,
through which the pionic decays of
D_{s0}^{*}(2317)
and

D_{s1}^{{}^{\prime}}(2460)
occur, can be described by the isospin violating piece
in the chiral Lagrangian

\displaystyle\mathcal{L}_{\eta-\pi^{0}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{m^{2}_{\pi}f^{2}_{\pi}}{4(m_{u}+m_{d})}Tr[m_{q}^{\dagger}\Sigma+\Sigma^{\dagger}m_{q}]

(8)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{m^{2}_{\pi}(m_{u}-m_{d})}{\sqrt{3}(m_{u}+m_{d})}\pi^{0}\eta+\cdots.

Herein,
\bar{H}_{a}=\gamma^{0}H^{\dagger}_{a}\gamma^{0}
,
and
\mathcal{D}_{ab}^{\mu}=\delta_{ab}\partial^{\mu}-\mathcal{V}_{ab}^{\mu}
. In the expressions,

\mathcal{V}_{\mu}=1/2(\xi^{\dagger}\partial_{\mu}\xi+\xi\partial_{\mu}\xi^{\dagger})

and

\mathcal{A}_{\mu}=i/2(\xi^{\dagger}\partial_{\mu}\xi-\xi\partial_{\mu}\xi^{\dagger})

are the light meson vector and axial currents, containing an
even number and an odd number of pseudoscalar fields, respectively.

\mathcal{D}_{ab}^{\mu}\mathcal{A}_{bc}^{\nu}=\partial^{\mu}\mathcal{A}_{ac}^{\nu}+[\mathcal{V}^{\mu},\mathcal{A}^{\nu}]_{ac}
.
And the chiral symmetry-breaking scale
\Lambda_{\chi}
is set to be
\Lambda_{\chi}=1
GeV. The

3\times 3
mass matrix is
m_{q}=diag(m_{u},m_{d},m_{s})
, and
m_{q}^{\xi}=\xi m_{q}\xi+\xi^{\dagger}m_{q}\xi^{\dagger}
.

Note that a full calculation of the strong decays should also contain, in
addition to the chiral symmetry-breaking corrections, the heavy quark
symmetry breaking corrections in
1/m_{Q}
. However, if the
1/m_{Q}
corrections are
also included, the number of free parameters to be determined will be too larger compared with the number of
experimentally measured decay rates of the
S
doublet heavy mesons, thus heavily weakens
the effectiveness of the
\chi^{2}
fitting.
Moreover, the lattice QCD studies
McNeile2004
;
Abada2004

of the strong couplings of heavy mesons indicate that these
1/m_{Q}
corrections
seem not to be significant but pointed out the importance of controlling chiral corrections.
And based on these lattice researches, chiral loop corrections to strong decays of non-strange charmed mesons in

S
doublet
have been studied in the Ref.
Fajfer2006
, obtaining pretty good results.
Therefore, we just concentrate on the chiral symmetry-breaking corrections,
while ignore the heavy quark symmetry corrections in the calculation.

Finally, we rewrite the decay amplitudes as follows:

\displaystyle\text{for $c\bar{q}$ mesons},

\displaystyle F^{i}_{ab}

\displaystyle=\frac{2h^{{}^{\prime}}}{f_{\pi}}+\frac{4\kappa_{1^{{}^{\prime}}}}{\Lambda_{\chi}f_{\pi}}(m_{u}+m_{d})+\frac{4\kappa_{4^{{}^{\prime}}}}{\Lambda_{\chi}f_{\pi}}(m_{d}-m_{u})g_{ab}

\displaystyle{}-\frac{4\kappa_{3}}{\Lambda_{\chi}f_{\pi}}(m_{d}-m_{u})\delta_{ab}-\frac{2\kappa_{5}^{{}^{\prime}}}{\Lambda_{\chi}f_{\pi}}E_{\pi^{i}},

\displaystyle\text{while for $c\bar{s}$ mesons},

\displaystyle=\frac{2h^{{}^{\prime}}}{f_{\pi}}+\frac{8\kappa_{1^{{}^{\prime}}}}{\Lambda_{\chi}f_{\pi}}m_{s}+\frac{2\kappa_{3}}{\Lambda_{\chi}f_{\pi}}(2m_{s}-m_{d}-m_{u})-\frac{2\kappa_{5}^{{}^{\prime}}}{\Lambda_{\chi}f_{\pi}}E_{\pi^{i}},

Before conducting the minimization of
\chi^{2}
to extract the unknown symmetry-breaking coupling constants
\kappa_{i}
s, we need to acquire the reasonable ranges of these parameters,
assuming the corrections to be moderate and thus maintaining the convergence of the perturbation series. Specifically, following the approach of Ref.
Stewart1998
, we assume that each correction
term change the leading order contribution by less than
30\%
.
Setting
h^{{}^{\prime}}
to be just the leading order weighted average result

h=0.44\pm 0.03
, the upper bounds of these parameters are roughly obtained:

\displaystyle|\kappa_{1}^{{}^{\prime}}|<1.35,\quad|\kappa_{3}|<1.40,\quad|\kappa_{4}^{{}^{\prime}}|<1.35,\quad|\kappa_{5}^{{}^{\prime}}|<0.50.

(18)

Here, we should be aware that though the coefficients of
\kappa_{1}^{{}^{\prime}}
,
\kappa_{3}
and
\kappa_{4}^{{}^{\prime}}
are of order

1\%
, i.e.
4(m_{d}\pm m_{u})/{\Lambda_{\chi}f_{\pi}}\sim 10^{-4}
, compared to the coefficients of
h^{{}^{\prime}}
and
\kappa_{5}^{{}^{\prime}}

for
c\bar{q}
mesons (see Eq. (
15
)),
they are at the same order for the
c\bar{s}
mesons due to the relatively large value of
m_{s}
(see Eq. (
16
)).
As a result, the upper bounds of all the
\kappa_{i}
s are of the same order.

Next, we will determine the
1/\Lambda_{\chi}
chiral symmetry-breaking
coupling constants within their bounds using the available experimental data by
\chi^{2}
fitting following the approach in Ref.
Godfrey1991
.
The
\chi^{2}
function is

\displaystyle\chi^{2}=\sum_{i=1}^{3}\frac{(\Gamma_{theo}^{(i)}-\Gamma_{exp}^{(i)})^{2}}{(\delta\Gamma_{exp}^{(i)})^{2}},

(19)

where
\Gamma_{exp}^{(i)}
and
\delta\Gamma_{exp}^{(i)}
are the experimentally measured widths and errors of
D^{*0}_{0}(2400)
,

D^{*\pm}_{0}(2400)
and
D^{{}^{\prime}0}_{1}(2430)
;
\Gamma_{theo}^{(i)}
are the numerical values corresponding to a set of given
symmetry-breaking coupling constants. Herein, as the chiral corrections are included, the leading order coupling
constant
h
is transferred to
h^{{}^{\prime}}
and should be treated as an unknown parameter now. Thus, with the transformations of the couplings
(listed in Section
III
), in total, there are five parameters to be determined.
However, only two of them are truly free as three ones are totally constrained by three experimentally measured decay widths
in the
S
doublet (listed in Table
2
). Furthermore, in the case of
c\bar{q}

S
doublet mesons, coefficients of
\kappa_{3}
and

\kappa_{4}^{{}^{\prime}}
are smaller than those of
\kappa_{1}^{{}^{\prime}}
and much smaller (of order
1\%
)
than those of
h^{{}^{\prime}}
and
\kappa_{5}^{{}^{\prime}}
. Thus considering that all the parameters are constrained
within the allowed ranges in Eq. (
18
), we can infer that almost all the contributions to the decay rates of
c\bar{q}
mesons
should be attributed to the
h^{{}^{\prime}}
,
\kappa_{5}^{{}^{\prime}}
and
\kappa_{1}^{{}^{\prime}}
terms. Therefore the minimization of
\chi^{2}

should be reliable on
h^{{}^{\prime}}
,
\kappa_{5}^{{}^{\prime}}
and
\kappa_{1}^{{}^{\prime}}
, while less reliable on

\kappa_{3}
and
\kappa_{4}^{{}^{\prime}}
. However, it should be kept in mind that the
\kappa_{3}
and
\kappa_{4}^{{}^{\prime}}
terms cannot be neglected,
since they are important to decay rates of the
c\bar{s}
doublet mesons as can be seen from Eq. (
16
).

For comparison, we do firstly the
\chi^{2}
fitting of
h^{{}^{\prime}}
by setting all
\kappa_{i}
s to be
0
.
The result is
h^{{}^{\prime}}=0.48\pm 0.02
, which nicely agrees with the weighted average in Eq. (

Then including the chiral corrections, we carry out the minimization in its five dimensional domain (
h^{{}^{\prime}}
,
\kappa_{1}^{{}^{\prime}}

\kappa_{3}
,
\kappa_{4}^{{}^{\prime}}
and
\kappa_{5}^{{}^{\prime}}
, two of which are truly free) within the bounds in Eq. (
18
).
We find that the minimum of
\chi^{2}
depends mainly on the choice of the masses of involved particles within their measured errors,
especially for
D_{0}^{*0}
,
D_{0}^{*+}
and
D_{1}^{{}^{\prime}}

h^{{}^{\prime}}
and
\kappa_{i}
s within their bounds slightly.
Therefore, we repeat the procedure of optimization with different sets of starting point of
\kappa_{i}s
and of the masses
of involved particles until we are confident that we have found the absolute minimum. The final couplings are shown in

In summary, we investigate the strong decays of
the exotic states
D_{s0}^{*}(2317)
and
D_{s1}^{{}^{\prime}}

(2460)
, within the
framework of HH
\chi
PT. Considering the chiral symmetry-breaking effects,
the effective heavy hadron chiral Lagrangian up to terms of next-to-leading
order in
1/\Lambda_{\chi}
is given.
Single-pion decay widths of charmed
heavy mesons and the corresponding beauty ones in the heavy quark
spin-flavor symmetry are calculated.
Using the existing experimental data of the non-strange partners of

D_{s0}^{*}(2317)
and
D_{s1}^{{}^{\prime}}(2460)
, the coupling constants are
estimated by minimizing
\chi^{2}
. Numerical analysis shows that our results
are consistent with the experimental constraints and comparable with the
other theoretical works in the literature. And the chiral symmetry-breaking
corrections of
c\bar{q}
(
b\bar{q}
) are small in comparison with the leading
order contributions, while those of
c\bar{s}
(
b\bar{s}
) are significant due to large mass of the strange quark.
The confirmation of such predictions is expected in the near future by
experiments at the LHCb and the hadron B factories.
