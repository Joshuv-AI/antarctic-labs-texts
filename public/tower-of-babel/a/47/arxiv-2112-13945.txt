The self-interacting Dirac fields in FLRW spacetime
Anahit Galstian and Karen Yagdjian

Abstract
We prove the existence of global in time solution with the small initial data for the semilinear equation of the spin- 1 2 \frac{1}{2} particles in the Friedmann-Lemaître-Robertson-Walker spacetime. Moreover, we also prove that if the initial function satisfies the Lochak-Majorana condition, then the global solution exists for arbitrary large initial value. The solution scatters to free solution for large time. The mass term is assumed to be complex-valued. The conditions on the imaginary part of mass are discussed by proving nonexistence of the global solutions if certain relation between scale function and the mass are fulfilled.

\displaystyle A(t)

\displaystyle:=

\displaystyle\int_{1}^{t}\left(3\ell\tau^{-1}+2\tau^{-1}|\Im(m)|\right)\,d\tau=\ln a(t)^{3}-\ln a(1)^{3}+2|\Im(m)|\ln(t),

then the inequality (
5.4
) reads

\displaystyle\frac{d}{dt}E(t)

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle K(t)E(t)^{(2+\alpha)/2}-E(t)\frac{d}{dt}A(t)\,.

Hence

\displaystyle\frac{d}{dt}\left(E(t)e^{A(t)}\right)

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle K(t)e^{-A(t)\alpha/2}\left(E(t)e^{A(t)}\right)^{(2+\alpha)/2}\,.

For the function

\displaystyle F(t)

\displaystyle:=

\displaystyle E(t)e^{A(t)}

the inequality leads to

\displaystyle\frac{d}{dt}F(t)

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle K(t)e^{-A(t)\alpha/2}F(t)^{(2+\alpha)/2}

or

\displaystyle\frac{d}{dt}F(t)^{-\alpha/2}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle-\frac{\alpha}{2}K(t)e^{-A(t)\frac{\alpha}{2}}\,.

After integration we obtain

\displaystyle F(t)^{-\frac{\alpha}{2}}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle F(1)^{-\frac{\alpha}{2}}-\frac{\alpha}{2}c_{0}\int_{1}^{t}\left(R+{\mathcal{A}}(s)\right)^{-3\frac{\alpha}{2}}e^{-A(s)\frac{\alpha}{2}}\,ds\,.

Furthermore

\displaystyle\int_{1}^{t}\left(R+{\mathcal{A}}(s)\right)^{-3\frac{\alpha}{2}}e^{-A(s)\frac{\alpha}{2}}\,ds

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int_{1}^{t}\left(R+{\mathcal{A}}(s)\right)^{-3\frac{\alpha}{2}}\left(\frac{a(1)}{a(s)}\right)^{\frac{3\alpha}{2}}s^{-\alpha|\Im(m)|}\,ds

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle J(t,m,R,\alpha,\ell)\,,

where

\displaystyle J(t,m,R,\alpha,\ell)

\displaystyle:=

\displaystyle\int_{1}^{t}\left(R+{\mathcal{A}}(s)\right)^{-3\alpha/2}s^{-\ell\frac{3\alpha}{2}-\alpha|\Im(m)|}\,ds\,.

In the case of
\ell<1
and
1=\frac{3\alpha}{2}+\alpha|\Im(m)|
we obtain

\displaystyle J(t,m,R,\alpha,\ell)

\displaystyle\approx

\displaystyle C+\int_{1}^{t}\left(s^{1-\ell}\right)^{-\frac{3\alpha}{2}}s^{-\ell\frac{3\alpha}{2}-\alpha|\Im(m)|}\,ds=C+\int_{1}^{t}s^{-1}\,ds=\ln(t)\to\infty\quad\mbox{for large}\,\,t.

In the case of
\ell<1
and
1>\frac{3\alpha}{2}+\alpha|\Im(m)|
we obtain

\displaystyle J(t,m,R,\alpha,\ell)

\displaystyle\approx

\displaystyle C+\int_{1}^{t}\left(s^{1-\ell}\right)^{-\frac{3\alpha}{2}}s^{-\ell\frac{3\alpha}{2}-\alpha|\Im(m)|}\,ds\approx C+t^{1-\frac{3\alpha}{2}-\alpha|\Im(m)|}\to\infty\quad\mbox{for large}\quad t\,.

Thus, in these two cases the solution blows up for initial data with arbitrary size.

In the case of
\ell<1
and
1<\frac{3\alpha}{2}+\alpha|\Im(m)|
we obtain

\displaystyle J(t,m,R,\alpha,\ell)

\displaystyle\approx

\displaystyle C+\int_{1}^{t}\left(s^{1-\ell}\right)^{-\frac{3\alpha}{2}}s^{-\ell\frac{3\alpha}{2}-\alpha|\Im(m)|}\,ds\leq 2C\quad\mbox{for large}\quad t\,.

Thus, the solution blows up for the large initial data. The solution blows up no later than time
T_{bu}
such that (
5.2
) holds and
E(1)
is sufficiently large.

In the case of
1<\ell
and
1=\frac{3\alpha}{2}\ell+\alpha|\Im(m)|
we obtain

\displaystyle J(t,m,R,\alpha,\ell)

\displaystyle\approx

\displaystyle C+\int_{1}^{t}\left(R+\frac{1}{\ell-1}s^{1-\ell}\right)^{-3\alpha/2}s^{-\ell\frac{3\alpha}{2}-\alpha|\Im(m)|}\,ds

\displaystyle\approx

\displaystyle C+2R^{-3\alpha/2}\ln(t)\to\infty\quad\mbox{for large}\quad t\,.

In the case of
1<\ell
and
1>\frac{3\alpha}{2}\ell+\alpha|\Im(m)|
we obtain

\displaystyle J(t,m,R,\alpha,\ell)

\displaystyle\approx

\displaystyle C+\int_{1}^{t}\left(R+\frac{1}{\ell-1}s^{1-\ell}\right)^{-3\alpha/2}s^{-\ell\frac{3\alpha}{2}-\alpha|\Im(m)|}\,ds

\displaystyle\approx

\displaystyle C+2R^{-3\alpha/2}\frac{1}{1-\ell\frac{3\alpha}{2}-\alpha|\Im(m)|}t^{1-\ell\frac{3\alpha}{2}-\alpha|\Im(m)|}\to\infty\quad\mbox{for large}\quad t\,.

Thus, in the last two cases the solution blows up for initial data with arbitrary size.

In the case of
1<\ell
and
1<\frac{3\alpha}{2}\ell+\alpha|\Im(m)|
we obtain

\displaystyle J(t,m,R,\alpha,\ell)

\displaystyle\approx

\displaystyle C+2R^{-3\alpha/2}\frac{1}{1-\ell\frac{3\alpha}{2}-\alpha|\Im(m)|}t^{1-\ell\frac{3\alpha}{2}-\alpha|\Im(m)|}\leq 2C\quad\mbox{for large}\quad t\,.

Thus, the solution blows up for the large initial data. The solution blows up no later than time
T_{bu}
such that (
5.2
) holds and

E(1)
is sufficiently large.

In the case of
\ell=1
and
1=\frac{3\alpha}{2}+\alpha|\Im(m)|
we obtain (for
\alpha\not=2/3
)

\displaystyle J(t,m,R,\alpha,\ell)

\displaystyle\approx

\displaystyle C+\int_{2}^{t}\left(\ln(s)\right)^{-\frac{3\alpha}{2}}s^{-\ell\frac{3\alpha}{2}-\alpha|\Im(m)|}\,ds=C+\int_{2}^{t}\left(\ln(s)\right)^{-\frac{3\alpha}{2}}s^{-1}\,ds

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle C+\frac{1}{1-\frac{3\alpha}{2}}\left((\ln(t))^{1-\frac{3\alpha}{2}}-(\ln(2))^{1-\frac{3\alpha}{2}}\right)\quad\mbox{for large}\quad t\,.

Hence, there is no global solution for initial data with arbitrary size.

In the case of
\ell=1
and
1<\frac{3\alpha}{2}+\alpha|\Im(m)|
we obtain

\displaystyle J(t,m,R,\alpha,\ell)

\displaystyle\approx

\displaystyle C+\int_{1}^{t}\left(\ln(s)\right)^{-\frac{3\alpha}{2}}s^{-\frac{3\alpha}{2}-\alpha|\Im(m)|}\,ds\leq 2C\quad\mbox{for large}\quad t\,.

There is no global solution for large data. The solution blows up no later than time
T_{bu}
such that (
5.2
)
holds and
E(1)
is sufficiently large.

In the case of
\ell=1
and
1>\frac{3\alpha}{2}+\alpha|\Im(m)|
we obtain

\displaystyle J(t,m,R,\alpha,\ell)

\displaystyle\approx

\displaystyle C+\int_{1}^{t}\left(\ln(s)\right)^{-\frac{3\alpha}{2}}s^{-\frac{3\alpha}{2}-\alpha|\Im(m)|}\,ds\to\infty\quad\mbox{for large}\quad t\,.

There is no global solution for initial data with arbitrary size.

Theorem
5.2
is proved.
\square
