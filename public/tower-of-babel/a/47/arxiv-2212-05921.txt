Rings whose ideals are close to automorphism-invariant
Adel Abyzov, Truong Cong Quynh, Askar Tuganbaev

Abstract
. We consider rings whose one-sided ideals are close to automorphism-invariant modules. We study rings in which every (finitely generated) right ideal is automorphism invariant and rings in which every right ideal is a finite direct sum of automorphism invariant ideals. Connections between these classes of rings, q q -ring and Σ \Sigma - q q -rings are also considered.
