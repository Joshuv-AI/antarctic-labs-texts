Muon g − 2 g-2 and CP violation in MSSM
Chengcheng Han 1

Abstract
We study the constraints of the CP violation in the muon g − 2 g-2 preferred region of the minimal supersymmetric standard model assuming a universal slepton masses within first two generations. We present two particular scenarios where the g − 2 g-2 anomaly is predicted within 2 σ \sigma level mainly through the chargino loop or the bino loop. We found that for both cases the electron EDM experiment already highly constrained the CP phase of the parameters: either the Arg[ μ M 1 ] \mu M_{1}] or Arg[ μ M 2 ] \mu M_{2}] should be smaller than 𝒪 \mathcal{O} (2-3) × 10 − 5 \times 10^{-5} . If the muon g − 2 g-2 anomaly is explained by the MSSM, a particular SUSY breaking mechanism is needed to guarantee the small CP phase of SUSY parameters. Otherwise, a tuning of 𝒪 ⁡ ( 10 − 5 ) \mathcal{O}(10^{-5}) is needed to cancel the phase in a general CP violated SUSY model.



I
Introduction

The minimal supersymmetric standard model(MSSM) as the minimal extension of the standard model in the framework of supersymmetry, remains one of the well-motivated models beyond the standard model. It not only alleviates the Higgs mass hierarchy problem but also provides a dark matter candidate as well as predicts the unification of the gauge couplings at a high energy scale. Therefore it is also one of the most important targets to search for at high-energy physics experiments. However, the large hadron collider(LHC) already set a very strong limit on the masses of SUSY particles.
