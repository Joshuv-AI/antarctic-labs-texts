explicit formulae for rank zero DT invariants and the OSV conjecture
Soheyla Feyzbakhsh

Abstract
. Fix a Calabi-Yau 3-fold X X satisfying the Bogomolov-Gieseker conjecture of Bayer-Macrì-Toda, such as the quintic 3-fold. By two different wall-crossing arguments we prove two different explicit formulae relating rank 0 Donaldson-Thomas invariants (counting torsion sheaves on X X supported on ample divisors) in terms of rank 1 Donaldson-Thomas invariants (counting ideal sheaves of curves) and Pandharipande-Thomas invariants. In particular, we prove a slight modification of Toda’s formulation of OSV conjecture for X X . When X X is of Picard rank one, we also give an explicit formula for rank two DT invariants in terms of rank zero and rank one DT invariants.

Tilt (Gieseker) stability

Given a sheaf of class
\alpha\in K(X)
with Hilbert polynomial

P_{\alpha}(t)\ :=\ \chi\big(\alpha(t)\big)\ =\ a_{d}t^{d}+a_{d-1}t^{d-1}+\dots+a_{0},

where
d\leq 3
and
a_{d}\neq 0
. Its
reduced
Hilbert polynomial is

p_{\alpha}(t)\ :=\ \frac{P_{\alpha}(t)}{a_{d}}\,.

Following Joyce
[
Joy07
, Section 4.4]
we introduce a total order
\prec
on
\{
monic polynomials
\}\sqcup\{0\}
by saying
p\prec q
if and only if

(i)

deg
\,p>\
deg
\,q
, or

(ii)

deg
\,p=\,\hskip 0.6pt
deg
\,q
and
p(t)<q(t)
for
t\gg 0
.

Our convention is that
\deg 0=0
. Then
E\in\mathrm{Coh}\hskip 0.6pt(X)
is called Gieseker (semi)stable if for all exact sequences
0\to A\to E\to B\to 0
in Coh
\hskip 0.6pt(X)
we have

p_{\hskip 0.6pt[A]}\ \,(\preceq)\,\ p_{\hskip 0.6pt[B]}.

Here
(\preceq)
means
\prec
for stability and
\preceq
(which is
\prec
or
=
) for semistability. In particular (i) ensures that Gieseker semistable sheaves are pure.

Discarding the constant term of the Hilbert polynomial before dividing by its top coefficient as before gives

(14)

\widetilde{p}_{\alpha}(t)\ :=\ p_{\alpha}(t)-\frac{a_{0}}{a_{d}}\ =\ t^{d}+\dots+\frac{a_{1}}{a_{d}}t.

It depends only on
\operatorname{ch}_{H}^{\leq 2}(\alpha)
. Then
tilt (semi)stability
on Coh
(X)
is defined by the inequalities

\widetilde{p}_{\hskip 0.6pt[A]}\ \,(\preceq)\,\ \widetilde{p}_{\hskip 0.6pt[B]}

for all exact sequences of sheaves
0\to A\to E\to B\to 0
.

Wall-crossing formula

Now assume
X
is a Calabi-Yau 3-fold, i.e.
K_{X}\cong\mathcal{O}_{X}
and
h^{1}(X,\mathcal{O}_{X})=0
. For any two objects
E,F\in\mathcal{D}(X)
, the Hirzebruch–Riemann–Roch Theorem implies that the Euler form
\chi([E],[F])=\sum_{i}(-1)^{i}\dim_{\mathbb{C}}\operatorname{Ext}^{i}(E,F)
is given in terms of their Chern characters by

\displaystyle\chi([E_{1}],[E_{2}])=\

\displaystyle\operatorname{ch}_{0}(E_{1})\operatorname{ch}_{3}(E_{2})-\operatorname{ch}_{0}(E_{2})\operatorname{ch}_{3}(E_{1})+\operatorname{ch}_{1}(E_{2})\operatorname{ch}_{2}(E_{1})-\operatorname{ch}_{1}(E_{1})\operatorname{ch}_{2}(E_{2})

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{12}c_{2}(X)\big(\operatorname{ch}_{0}(E_{1})\operatorname{ch}_{1}(E_{2})-\operatorname{ch}_{0}(E_{2})\operatorname{ch}_{1}(E_{1})\big).

For any
(b,w)\in U
and any class
v\in K(X)
with
\nu_{b,w}(v)<+\infty
, we can apply the work of Joyce-Song
[
JS12
]
to define generalised DT invariants

\mathsf{J}\hskip 0.6pt_{b,w}(v)\ \in\ \mathbb{Q}

counting
\nu_{b,w}
-semistable objects
E\in\mathcal{A}_{b}
of class
v
. In
[
FT21b
, Section 4 and Appendix C]
, these invariants have been described and shown that they are independent of the choice of
(b,w)
inside each chamber described in Proposition
2.2
for class
v
. Also the Joyce-Song wall crossing formula applies to the
\mathsf{J}\hskip 0.6pt_{b,w}(v)
under the same
\nu_{b,w}(v)<+\infty
condition.

Suppose
\ell
is a non-vertical wall for a class
v\in K(X)
with
\operatorname{ch}_{1}(v)H^{2}>0
if
\operatorname{ch}_{0}(v)=0
. Thus
\ell
passes through
\Pi(v)
if
\operatorname{ch}_{0}(v)\neq 0
; otherwise is of gradient
\operatorname{ch}_{2}(v).H\big/\!\operatorname{ch}_{1}(v).H^{2}
. Let
(b,w_{0})
be a point on the line segment
\ell\cap U
. By the local finiteness of walls of Proposition
2.2
we may choose

(15)

(b,w^{\pm})\,\in\,U\,\text{ just above and below the wall }\ell,

in the sense that
(b,w^{\pm})\not\in\ell
and between
(b,w_{-})
and
(b,w_{+})
there are no walls for
v
,
nor any of its finitely many semistable factors
, except for
\ell
. Then the wall crossing formula
[
JS12
, Equation (5.13)]
gives

\displaystyle\begin{gathered}\mathsf{J}\hskip 0.6pt_{b,w^{-}}(v)=\!\!\!\!\!\!\sum_{\begin{subarray}{l}q\geq 1,\ \alpha_{1},\ldots,\alpha_{q}\in C(\mathcal{A}_{b}):\\
\alpha_{1}+\cdots+\alpha_{q}=v\end{subarray}}\,\,\,\,\sum_{\begin{subarray}{l}\text{connected, simply-connected digraphs $\Gamma\colon$}\\
\text{vertices $\{1,\ldots,q\}$, edge $i\rightarrow j$ implies $i<j$}\end{subarray}}\\
\frac{(-1)^{q-1+\sum_{1\leq i<j\leq q}\chi(\alpha_{i},\alpha_{j})}}{2^{q-1}}\,U\left(\alpha_{1},\ldots,\alpha_{q};(b,w^{+}),(b,w^{-})\right)\!\!\!\!\!\prod_{\text{edges $i\rightarrow j$ in $\Gamma$}}\!\!\!\!\!\chi(\alpha_{i},\alpha_{j})\prod_{i=1}^{q}\mathsf{J}\hskip 0.6pt_{b,w^{+}}(\alpha_{i}).\end{gathered}

Here
C(\mathcal{A}_{\hskip 0.6ptb})
is the positive cone (
6
) and the coefficients
U\left(\alpha_{1},\ldots,\alpha_{q};(b,w^{+}),(b,w^{-})\right)
are defined as follows.

Definition 2.5
.

[
JS12

Take two points

and

satisfying (a). Otherwise define

. Now

\displaystyle U\big(\alpha_{1},\ldots,\alpha_{q};(b,w_{1}),(b,w_{2})\big)=

\displaystyle\sum_{\begin{subarray}{l}\phantom{wiggle}\\

(-1)^{\chi(\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2})+1}\chi(\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2}).

Let
(X,\mathcal{O}_{X}(1))
be a smooth polarised complex projective threefold with

\mathsf{v}=(0,D,\beta,m)\in K(X)

2
