On split Leibniz superalgebras
Antonio J. Calderón Martín

Abstract
We study the structure of arbitrary split Leibniz superalgebras. We show that any of such superalgebras 𝔏 {\mathfrak{L}} is of the form 𝔏 = 𝒰 + ∑ j I j {\mathfrak{L}}={\mathcal{U}}+\sum_{j}I_{j} with 𝒰 {\mathcal{U}} a subspace of an abelian (graded) subalgebra H H and any I j I_{j} a well described (graded) ideal of 𝔏 {\mathfrak{L}} satisfying [ I j , I k ] = 0 [I_{j},I_{k}]=0 if j ≠ k j\neq k . In the case of 𝔏 {\mathfrak{L}} being of maximal length, the simplicity of 𝔏 {\mathfrak{L}} is also characterized in terms of connections of roots. Keywords : Leibniz superalgebras, roots, root spaces, structure theory. 2010 MSC : 17A32, 17A70, 17B05.

Theorem 3.1
.
