Pre-Nichols algebras of one-parameter families of finite Gelfand-Kirillov dimension Thanks: The work was supported by CONICET
Emiliano Campagnolo

Abstract
. Between the braided vector spaces of diagonal type there exist some families whose associated Nichols algebras are infinite dimensional but with finite Gelfand-Kirillov dimension, called one-parameter families. We show that every pre-Nichols algebra of finite Gelfand-Kirillov dimension of this kind with connected diagram is necessarily the corresponding Nichols algebra, up to few exceptions. For each one of these exceptions we present a proper pre-Nichols algebra by generators and relations, and prove that it is the corresponding eminent pre-Nichols algebra. This paper essentially ends the determination of the eminent pre-Nichols algebras of diagonal type, the first step towards the classification of pre-Nichols algebras of diagonal type with finite GKdim.



1.
Introduction

Let
\Bbbk
be an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero. If
H
is a pointed Hopf algebra (that is, its coradical
H_{0}
coincides with the subalgebra generated by the group
G(H)
of group-likes elements) over
\Bbbk
then the coradical filtration
\{H_{i}\}_{i\in\mathbb{N}_{0}}
of
H
is a Hopf algebra filtration with
H_{0}=\Bbbk G(H)
, thus the associated graded object
\operatorname{gr}H
is a graded Hopf algebra and admits morphisms of Hopf algebras
\pi:\operatorname{gr}H\rightarrow\Bbbk G(H)
and
\iota:\Bbbk G(H)\rightarrow\operatorname{gr}H
with
\pi\circ\iota=\operatorname{id}
. By
[
R
, §11.7]
,
\operatorname{gr}H\simeq\Bbbk G(H)\#R
, where
R=\bigoplus_{n\geq 0}R(n)
is a graded Hopf algebra in the Yetter-Drinfeld modules category
{}^{H_{0}}_{H_{0}}\mathcal{YD}
such that
R(0)=\Bbbk 1
. Let
V:=R(1)\in{}^{H_{0}}_{H_{0}}\mathcal{YD}
: the subalgebra of
R
generated by
V
is the Nichols algebra
\mathscr{B}(V)
of
V
, which and is a graded Hopf algebra in
{}^{H_{0}}_{H_{0}}\mathcal{YD}
univocally determined by
V
.

Given a group
G
, a program (called the
Lifting Method
) for the classification of finite dimensional pointed Hopf algebras
H
such that
G(H)\simeq G
is presented in
[
AS
]
. There is a similar program when the Gelfand-Kirillov dimension is finite, which proposes to answering the following questions:

(1)

Classify all Yetter-Drinfeld modules
V
over
G
such that
\operatorname{GKdim}\mathscr{B}(V)<\infty
.

(2)

For each
V
obtained above, compute all post-Nichols algebras of
V
with
\operatorname{GKdim}
finite.

(3)

Find all Hopf algebras
H
such that
\operatorname{gr}H\simeq\mathcal{E}\#\Bbbk G
.

From the main result in
[
AnG
]
it is proved that every Nichols algebra of diagonal type with finite
\operatorname{GKdim}
has a finite root system. On the other hand in
[
H
]
all braided vector spaces
V
with connected Dynkin diagram and finite root systems were classified. Thus, combining these two results we answer (
1
) when
V
is of diagonal type; that is, we obtain the classification of all Nichols algebras of diagonal type with connected Dynkin diagram and finite
\operatorname{GKdim}
.

The problem proposed in (
2
) for diagonal type is equivalent to computing all pre-Nichols algebras with
\operatorname{GKdim}
finite.
The main results of
[
ASa
,
AnCSa
,
AnCSa2
]
give an answer for (
2
) when the Nichols algebra is finite-dimensional: they prove the existence of a pre-Nichols algebra

of

such that every other finite

-dimensional pre-Nichols algebra of

is a quotient of
\widehat{\mathscr{B}}
; that is,

is the corresponding eminent pre-Nichols algebra.

This paper gives a complete answer for (

) when

is a braided vector space of diagonal type such that

but

; these braided vector spaces are obtained by evaluating the parameter of the

[

]
in a generic value. The strategy follows the same ideas as in the series of papers

,

,

Notations

The set of

Let

The element

For each

For notation related to Hopf algebras we refer to

2.1.
Eminent pre-Nichols algebras

Let
V
be a finite-dimensional braided vector space. A pre-Nichols algebra of
V
is a Hopf quotient
\mathscr{B}
of
T(V)
by an
\mathbb{N}_{0}
-homogeneous ideal generated by elements of degree
\geq 2
; hence, there exist canonical epimorphisms
T(V)\twoheadrightarrow\mathscr{B}\twoheadrightarrow\mathscr{B}(V)
, induced by the identity of
V
.

Let
\mathfrak{Pre}(V)
be the poset whose elements are the pre-Nichols algebras of
V
and whose partial order is given by
\mathscr{B}_{1}\leq\mathscr{B}_{2}
if there exists an epimorphism
\mathscr{B}_{1}\twoheadrightarrow\mathscr{B}_{2}
induced by the identity on
V
. Therefore
T(V)
is minimum in
\mathfrak{Pre}(V)
and
\mathscr{B}(V)
is maximum in
\mathfrak{Pre}(V)
.

We denote by
\mathfrak{Pre}_{\operatorname{fGK}}(V)
the subposet of
\mathfrak{Pre}(V)
that contains all pre-Nichols algebras of
V
with finite
\operatorname{GKdim}
. Notice that
T(V)\notin\mathfrak{Pre}_{\operatorname{fGK}}(V)
if
\dim V>1
, and
\mathfrak{Pre}_{\operatorname{fGK}}(V)
is not empty if and only if
\operatorname{GKdim}\mathscr{B}(V)<\infty
. If there exists a pre-Nichols algebra
\widehat{\mathscr{B}}
that is minimal on
\mathfrak{Pre}_{\operatorname{fGK}}(V)
, we will call it the
eminent
pre-Nichols algebra of
V
.

The main results of this work determine the eminent pre-Nichols algebra for some cases when
V
is a braided vector space of diagonal type.
