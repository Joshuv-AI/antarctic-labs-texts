Abstract
. Let 𝒞 \mathcal{C} be a hyperelliptic curve y 2 = p ⁡ ( x ) y^{2}=p(x) defined over a number field K K with p ⁡ ( x ) p(x) integral of odd degree. The purpose of the present article is to prove lower and upper bounds for the 2 2 -Selmer group of the Jacobian of 𝒞 \mathcal{C} in terms of the class group of the K K -algebra K ⁡ [ x ] / ( p ⁡ ( x ) ) K[x]/(p(x)) . Our main result is a formula relating these two quantities under some mild hypothesis. We provide some examples that prove that our lower and upper bounds are as sharp as possible. As a first application, we study the rank distribution of the 2 2 -Selmer group in families of quadratic twists. Under some extra hypothesis we prove that among prime quadratic twists, a positive proportion has fixed 2 2 -Selmer group. As a second application, we study the family of octic twists of the genus 2 2 curve y 2 = x 5 + x y^{2}=x^{5}+x .

1.
Introduction

Let
K
be a number field and
\mathcal{C}
be a hyperelliptic curve over
K
given by

(1.1)

\mathcal{C}:y^{2}=p(x)=x^{d}+a_{d-1}x^{d-1}+a_{d-2}x^{d-2}+\cdots+a_{1}x+a_{0},

is divisible by all prime ideals over
2
of

Let
J
denote the Jacobian of
\mathcal{C}
and
g
its genus. By Mordell’s
theorem, we know that the abelian group
J(K)
is finitely generated.
It is an important problem to determine its rank,
namely the rank of its free part.

The standard algorithm to compute the rank of
J(K)
is the so called
“descent” method. The idea behind the
2
-descent method is that the short exact sequence

\textstyle{1\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces}
\textstyle{J[2]\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces}
\textstyle{J(\overline{K})\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces}
\scriptstyle{\times 2}
\textstyle{J(\overline{K})\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces}
\textstyle{1,}

where

denotes the
2
-torsion points on
J(\overline{K})
,
induces a short exact sequence in cohomology

J(K)/2J(K)\hookrightarrow H^{1}(\Gal_{K},J[2])
(here
\Gal_{K}
denotes the absolute Galois group
\Gal(\overline{{\mathbb{Q}}}/K)
). The so called

2
-Selmer group
, denoted by
\Sel_{2}(J)
(whose definition is
recalled in Definition
2.2
), is a subgroup of the cohomology group

H^{1}(\Gal_{K},J[2])
. It is a finite dimensional
\F_{2}
-vector space
whose understanding provides deep information of the rank of
J(K)
(its dimension equals the rank of
J(K)
plus the dimension of the elements of order
2
in the Tate-Shafarevich group).

The pioneer work of Brumer and Kramer (
[

, Proposition 7.1]
)
gives an upper bound for the order of the
2
-Selmer group of an elliptic curve

in terms of the
2

{\mathbb{Q}}[x]/(f(x))
when

is irreducible (see also
[
Sch96
]
). One is led to
expect that a similar phenomena should hold in general, namely the
order of
\Sel_{2}(J)
should be related to a ray class group of the

K
-algebra
K[x]/(p(x))
.
In
[
Li19
]
Chao Li gave not only an upper bound, but
also a lower bound of the
2
-Selmer group of a rational elliptic
curve in terms of the class group of
K[x]/(p(x))
(under some
hypothesis). Li’s result was generalized to general number fields
under less restricted hypothesis in
[
BSPT21
]
. Moreover, in

[
BSPT21
]
we provided a general framework which could be
applied to more general situations, like the case of hyperelliptic
curves
\mathcal{C}
. In the present article we pursuit this goal, obtaining a
similar result. More precisely in this work:

(1)

We obtain general bounds for
\mathrm{dim}_{\F_{2}}(\Sel_{2}(J))
.

(2)

We obtain applications related to quadratic twists of hyperelliptic curves and certain families of hyperelliptic curves.

1.1.
Bounding the
2
-Selmer group

Attached to the curve
\mathcal{C}
with equation
\mathcal{C}:y^{2}=p(x)
we consider
the étale
K
-algebra
A_{K}=K[x]/(p(x))
.
Our main result gives a lower and upper bound for
\mathrm{Sel}_{2}(J)
in terms of a
2
-class group similar to the one obtained in
[

]

as defined in §

between the classical class group

\Cl(A_{K})

(see definition

Our main result is the following.

Theorem

.

Let
K
be a number field and
\mathcal{C}/K
be a hyperelliptic
curve. Suppose that hypotheses

\dim_{\F_{2}}\Cl_{*}(A_{K},\mathcal{C})[2]-\sum_{v\mid 2}\bigl(r_{v}-1-\dim_{\F_{2}}(\mathbb{V}_{v})\bigr)\quad\\
\leq\quad\dim_{\F_{2}}\Sel_{2}(J)\quad\leq\quad\dim_{\F_{2}}\Cl_{*}(A_{K},\mathcal{C})[2]+g\,[K:{\mathbb{Q}}]\,.

The lower bound includes local correction
terms (possibly zero) at places over
2
defined as follows.
For
v\mid 2
let
K_{v}
be the completion of

at
v
and
k
its
residue field. Over
K_{v}
the polynomial

We denote

\overline{T}_{i}
be the image of

in

The space

\mathbb{V}_{v}=\langle\trace_{k_{i}/k}(\overline{T}_{i})\ :\ i=1,...,r_{v}\rangle\subset k.

Under our assumption on

The best lower bound occurs when

for all

,
in which case the difference between the upper and the lower bound

[

For example, if

is

Assuming the parity conjecture we can then deduce from our bounds the

To our knowledge, the only previous general result to bound the

2
-Selmer group of a hyperelliptic curve is due to Stoll. In

[
Sto01
]
Stoll developed a very nice algorithm to compute the

2
-Selmer group of an hyperelliptic curve, and as a Corollary of his

[

2
-Selmer group similar to the one obtained by Brumer and Kramer (see also

[
Sch95

). A similar upper bound was obtained in

[

]
in the particular situation where

is the
minimal polynomial of
\zeta_{p}+\zeta_{p}^{-1}
(where
\zeta_{p}

denotes a
p

(p-1)/2
is
a prime number. Note that our result improves theirs in the sense that
we get the same upper bound when

(although we do
not need to impose the condition

we also get a lower bound.

The proof of our main result has two key ingredients. We start considering the long exact sequence in cohomology

(1.2)

\textstyle{1\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces}
\textstyle{J(K)/2J(K)\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces}
\textstyle{\coho^{1}(\Gal_{K},J[2])\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces}
\textstyle{\coho^{1}(\Gal_{K},J)[2].}

By a result of Cassels, the cohomology group
\coho^{1}(\Gal_{K},J[2])
is
isomorphic to
(A_{K}^{\times}/(A_{K}^{\times})^{2})_{\square}
. To get the upper bound, we need to assure
that any cocycle in
\coho^{1}(\Gal_{K_{v}},J[2])
coming from

is
unramified at any odd place of
A_{K}
. Under Cassels isomorphism,
this is equivalent to proving
that the image of any
point

in
J(K_{v})
,
a priori in
(A_{K_{v}}^{\times}/(A_{K_{v}}^{\times})^{2})_{\square}
,
actually belongs
to the class of integral elements

(A_{\mathcal{O}}^{\times}/(A_{\mathcal{O}}^{\times})^{2})_{\square}

(a purely local computation, see Theorem
3.4
).
The hypotheses imposed are the ones needed for this statement to be true.

A second key ingredient is needed to get the lower bound: we need to
construct points on
J(K_{v})
. Luckily enough (by dimension reasons)
this last hard problem only needs to be done at primes dividing

2
. The spaces
\mathbb{V}_{v}
appearing in Theorem
5.15
play a
crucial role in this construction.

The first one concerns the study of quadratic twists of

.

Let
\mathcal{C}
be an hyperelliptic curve satisfying

-Selmer group for all

.

To our knowledge this is the first general result regarding

-Selmer

Remark 3.3

Since the polynomials

are prime to each other, there exists an injective map

this might not be true, for example if

and

, the image of the map

consists of pairs of

in

such that

Let

denote the ring of integers of an algebraic
closure of

denote its maximal ideal. The

A standard argument using resultants proves that

if
and only if
{\mathfrak{p}}\nmid\alpha-\beta

of

Lemma 5.14
.

With the previous notation,

\#(A_{\mathcal{O}}^{\times}/(A_{\mathcal{O}}^{\times})^{2})_{\square}=2^{g[K:{\mathbb{Q}}]}\cdot 2^{a_{3}+2a_{5}+3a_{7}+\cdots+(\frac{d-1}{2})a_{d}}

of archimedean places of

\alpha={a_{1}+a_{3}+\cdots+a_{d}+c}

\mathcal{C}
be an hyperelliptic curve over

6.2.
A family of octic twists

Let

Remark 6.4

•

Its absolute value equals
p

•

Recall that in general, if

is a tower of Galois field

(by
