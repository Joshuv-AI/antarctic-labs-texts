Mock theta functions and characters of N=3 superconformal modules III
Minoru Wakimoto

Abstract




Abstract

In this paper we study the branching functions of tensor products
of N=3 superconformal modules.

1
Introduction

It is known in
[
12
]
and
[
13
]
that the characters of
N=3 superconformal modules are written by Jacobi’s
\theta
-functions and
Mumfold’s
\vartheta
-functions and Dedekind’s
\eta
-function.
Also the mutiplication formula for classical theta functions is known
in
[
1
]
.
Then it is naturally expected that these will enable us to compute
products of N=3 characters, namely the branching functions of
tensor product representations of the N=3 superconformal algebra.
This paper is an approach to this problem.

For notations and definitions, we follow from
[
12
]
,

[
13
]
and
[
14
]
.

In section
3
, we compute explicitly the branching functions
of the tensor product representation
H(\Lambda^{[K(m),m_{2}]})\otimes H(\Lambda^{[K(m^{\prime}),m_{2}^{\prime}]})
in the case when
m=m^{\prime}=1
and
m=m^{\prime}=2
.

The characters of N=3 superconformal modules are obtained in
[
13
]

but we need to have more information about them for our study on the
branching of tensor product representations.
For this sake, in section
4
,
we make further analysis on the numerators of N=3 characters.
Making use of the results obtained in section
4
,
we discuss about the products of N=3 characters in section

5
.
