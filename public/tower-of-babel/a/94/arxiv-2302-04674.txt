On some analytic properties of nabla tempered fractional calculus
Yiheng Wei ∗, Linlin Zhao, Xuan Zhao, Jinde Cao

Abstract
Despite many applications regarding fractional calculus have been reported in literature, it is still unknown how to model some practical process. One major challenge in solving such a problem is that, the nonlocal property is needed while the infinite memory is undesired. Under this context, a new kind nabla fractional calculus accompanied by a tempered function is formulated. However, many properties of such fractional calculus needed to be discovered. From this, this paper gives particular emphasis to the topic. Some remarkable properties like the equivalence relation, the nabla Taylor formula, and the nabla Laplace transform for such nabla fractional calculus are developed and analyzed. It is believed that this work greatly enriches the mathematical theory of nabla tempered fractional calculus and provides high value and huge potential for further applications.

x:\mathbb{N}_{a+1-n}\to\mathbb{R}

{\textstyle\nabla^{n}x(k):=\sum_{i=0}^{n}(-1)^{i}\big(\begin{smallmatrix}n\\

(1)

where

\left(\begin{smallmatrix}p\\

{\textstyle{}_{a}^{\rm G}\nabla_{k}^{\alpha}x(k):=\sum_{i=0}^{k-a-1}(-1)^{i}\big(\begin{smallmatrix}\alpha\\
i\end{smallmatrix}\big)x(k-i),}

(2)

,

represents the difference operation. When

{\textstyle{}_{a}^{\rm G}\nabla_{k}^{-\alpha,w(k)}x(k)=\nabla^{n,w(k)}{}_{a}^{\mathrm{G}}\nabla_{k}^{-\alpha-n,w(k)}x(k).}

=&\hskip-6.0ptw^{-1}(k)\nabla\sum\nolimits_{j=a+1}^{k}{\frac{{({k-j+1})\overline{{}^{\alpha}}}}{{\Gamma({\alpha+1})}}{\nabla^{n}}_{a}^{\rm G}\nabla_{j}^{\alpha-n}z(j)}\\

&\hskip-6.0pt-w^{-1}(k){\sum\nolimits_{i=0}^{n-1}{[{{\nabla^{n-i-1}}{}_{a}^{\rm G}\nabla_{k}^{\alpha-n}z(k)}]}_{k=a}\frac{{({k-a})\overline{{}^{\alpha-i-1}}}}{{\Gamma({\alpha-i})}}}\\
=&\hskip-6.0ptw^{-1}(k)\nabla{}_{a}^{\rm G}\nabla_{k}^{n-\alpha-1}{}_{a}^{\rm G}\nabla_{k}^{\alpha-n}z(k)\\
&\hskip-6.0pt-w^{-1}(k){\sum\nolimits_{i=0}^{n-1}{[{{}_{a}^{\rm R}\nabla_{k}^{\alpha-i-1}z(k)}]}_{k=a}\frac{{({k-a})\overline{{}^{\alpha-i-1}}}}{{\Gamma({\alpha-i})}}}\\
=&\hskip-6.0ptw^{-1}(k){}_{a}^{\rm G}\nabla_{k}^{n-\alpha}{}_{a}^{\rm G}\nabla_{k}^{\alpha-n}z(k)\\
&\hskip-6.0pt-{\sum\nolimits_{i=0}^{n-1}\frac{w(a)}{w(k)}{[{{}_{a}^{\rm R}\nabla_{k}^{\alpha-i-1}z(k)}]}_{k=a}\frac{{({k-a})\overline{{}^{\alpha-i-1}}}}{{\Gamma({\alpha-i})}}}\\
=&\hskip-6.0ptw^{-1}(k)z(k)-{\sum\nolimits_{i=0}^{n-1}\frac{{({k-a})\overline{{}^{\alpha-i-1}}}}{{\Gamma({\alpha-i})}}\frac{w(a)}{w(k)}{[{{}_{a}^{\rm R}\nabla_{k}^{\alpha-i-1,w(k)}x(k)}]}_{k=a}},\end{array}

(27)

which implies (
23
).

For the Caputo case, one has

\begin{array}[]{rl}{}_{a}^{\rm G}\nabla_{k}^{-\alpha,w(k)}{}_{a}^{\rm C}\nabla_{k}^{\alpha,w(k)}x(k)=&\hskip-6.0ptw^{-1}(k){}_{a}^{\rm G}\nabla_{k}^{-\alpha}{}_{a}^{\rm C}\nabla_{k}^{\alpha}z(k)\\
=&\hskip-6.0ptw^{-1}(k){}_{a}^{\rm G}\nabla_{k}^{-\alpha}{}_{a}^{\rm G}\nabla_{k}^{\alpha-n}{\nabla^{n}}z(k)\\
=&\hskip-6.0ptw^{-1}(k){}_{a}^{\rm G}\nabla_{k}^{-n}{\nabla^{n}}z(k)\\
=&\hskip-6.0ptw^{-1}(k)z(k)-w^{-1}(k){\sum\nolimits_{i=0}^{n-1}{\frac{{({k-a})\overline{{}^{i}}}}{{i!}}[{{\nabla^{i}}z(k)}]}_{k=a}}\\
=&\hskip-6.0ptx(k)-{\sum\nolimits_{i=0}^{n-1}\frac{w(a)}{w(k)}{\frac{{({k-a})\overline{{}^{i}}}}{{i!}}[{{\nabla^{i,w(k)}}x(k)}]}_{k=a}},\end{array}

(28)

which leads to (
24
). All of these complete the proof.
∎

Theorem
2
gives the relation between tempered fractional difference and tempered fractional sum, which can be the generalization of
[
17
, Corollary 5]
. Along this way, letting
\beta\in(m-1,m)
,
m,n\in\mathbb{Z}_{+}
,

,

, then one has

\left\{\begin{array}[]{l}{}_{a}^{\rm R}\nabla_{k}^{n-\beta,w(k)}{}_{a}^{\rm C}\nabla_{k}^{\beta,w(k)}x(k)=\nabla^{n,w(k)}x(k),\\

By rearranging (

{\textstyle\begin{array}[]{rl}x(k)=&\hskip-6.0pt\sum\nolimits_{i=0}^{n-1}{{\frac{{({k-a})\overline{{}^{i}}}}{{i!}}}\frac{w(a)}{w(k)}[{{\nabla^{i,w(k)}}x(k)}]_{k=a}}+\sum\nolimits_{j=a+1}^{k}{{}\frac{{({k-j+1})\overline{{}^{\alpha-1}}}}{{\Gamma(\alpha)}}\frac{w(j)}{w(k)}{{}_{a}^{\rm C}\nabla_{j}^{\alpha,w(j)}}x(j)}\\

(29)

which can be regarded as

of

at

with summation reminder. The summation might be the fractional order case

In a similar way, for any

,

{\textstyle\begin{array}[]{rl}{\nabla^{m,w(k)}}x(k)=&\hskip-6.0pt\sum\nolimits_{i=m}^{n-1}{\frac{({k-a})\overline{{}^{i-m}}}{{(i-m)!}}\frac{w(a)}{w(k)}[{{\nabla^{i,w(k)}}x(k)}]_{k=a}}+\sum\nolimits_{j=a+1}^{k}{{}\frac{{({k-j+1})\overline{{}^{\alpha-m-1}}}}{{\Gamma(\alpha-m)}}\frac{w(j)}{w(k)}{}_{a}^{\rm C}\nabla_{j}^{\alpha,w(k)}x(j)}.\end{array}}

{\textstyle\begin{array}[]{rl}{}_{a}^{\rm G}\nabla_{k}^{n,w(k)}x(k)=&\hskip-6.0pt{\nabla^{n,w(k)}}x(k)+\left\{\begin{array}[]{rl}\sum\nolimits_{i=k-a}^{n}{{{({-1})}^{i}}({\begin{smallmatrix}n\\

0,&\hskip-6.0ptk\geq a+n,\end{array}\right.\end{array}}

(31)

where

Theorem 3
.

For any
x:\mathbb{N}_{a+1}\to\mathbb{R}
,
\alpha>0
,
k\in\mathbb{N}_{a+1}
,
a\in\mathbb{R}
,
w:\mathbb{N}_{a+1}\to\mathbb{R}\backslash\{0\}
,
{}_{a}^{\rm G}\nabla_{k}^{-\alpha,w(k)}x(k)
is continuous with respect to
\alpha
and one has

{\textstyle\mathop{\lim}\limits_{\alpha\to 0}{}_{a}^{\rm G}\nabla_{k}^{-\alpha,w(k)}x(k)=x(k)}.

(32)

Proof.

By using (
6
), (
9
) and the formula of summation by parts, it follows

{\textstyle\begin{array}[]{rl}{}_{a}^{\rm G}\nabla_{k}^{-\alpha,w(k)}x(k)=&\hskip-6.0ptw^{-1}(k)\sum\nolimits_{j=a+1}^{k}{{}\frac{{({k-j+1})\overline{{}^{\alpha-1}}}}{{\Gamma(\alpha+1)}}w(j)x(j)}\\
=&\hskip-6.0ptw^{-1}(k)\sum\nolimits_{j=a+1}^{k}{{}\frac{{({k-j+1})\overline{{}^{\alpha}}}}{{\Gamma(\alpha+1)}}\nabla[w(j)x(j)]}-w^{-1}(k)\big[\frac{{({k-j})\overline{{}^{\alpha}}}}{{\Gamma(\alpha)}}w(j)x(j)\big]_{j=a}^{j=k}\\
=&\hskip-6.0ptw^{-1}(k)\sum\nolimits_{j=a+1}^{k}{\frac{{({k-j+1})\overline{{}^{\alpha}}}}{{\Gamma(\alpha+1)}}\nabla[w(j)x(j)]}+\frac{{({k-a})\overline{{}^{\alpha}}}}{{\Gamma(\alpha+1)}}\frac{w(a)}{w(k)}x(a).\end{array}}

(33)

Since
\frac{{({k-j+1})\overline{{}^{\alpha}}}}{{\Gamma(\alpha+1)}}
is continuous with respect to
\alpha
for any
j\in\mathbb{N}_{a+1}^{k}
,
{}_{a}^{\rm G}\nabla_{k}^{-\alpha,w(k)}x(k)
is also continuous. Due to the fact of
\mathop{\lim}\limits_{\alpha\to 0}{\frac{{({k-j+1})\overline{{}^{\alpha}}}}{{\Gamma(\alpha+1)}}}=1
, taking limit for both sides of (
33
), one has

{\textstyle\begin{array}[]{rl}\mathop{\lim}\limits_{\alpha\to 0}{{}_{a}^{\rm G}\nabla_{k}^{-\alpha,w(k)}x(k)}=&\hskip-6.0ptw^{-1}(k)\sum\nolimits_{j=a+1}^{k}{\nabla[w(j)x(j)]}+\frac{w(a)}{w(k)}x(a)=x(k).\end{array}}

(34)

The proof is thus done.
∎

Theorem 4
.

For any

,

,

,
\alpha\in(n-1,n)

,

{\textstyle\mathop{\lim}\limits_{\alpha\to n}{}_{a}^{\rm C}\nabla_{k}^{\alpha,w(k)}x(k)={\nabla^{n,w(k)}}x(k)},k\in\mathbb{N}_{a+1},

(35)

{\textstyle\mathop{\lim}\limits_{\alpha\to n-1}{}_{a}^{\rm C}\nabla_{k}^{\alpha,w(k)}x(k)+\frac{w(a)}{w(k)}{[{{\nabla^{n-1,w(k)}}x(k)}]_{k=a}}={\nabla^{n-1,w(k)}}x(k),k\in\mathbb{N}_{a+1},}

(36)

{\textstyle\mathop{\lim}\limits_{\alpha\to n}{}_{a}^{\rm R}\nabla_{k}^{\alpha,w(k)}x(k)={\nabla^{n,w(k)}}x(k)},k\in\mathbb{N}_{a+n+1},

(37)

Proof.

Assume

{\textstyle\begin{array}[]{rl}{}_{a}^{\rm C}\nabla_{k}^{\alpha,w(k)}x(k)=&\hskip-6.0ptw^{-1}(k){}_{a}^{\rm C}\nabla_{k}^{\alpha}z(k)\\

(39)

Note that

is continuous regarding to
\alpha
for any
j\in\mathbb{N}_{a+1}^{k}

k\in\mathbb{N}_{a+1}

Taking limit for

{\textstyle\begin{array}[]{rl}\mathop{\lim}\limits_{\alpha\to n}{}_{a}^{\rm C}\nabla_{k}^{\alpha,w(k)}x(k)=&\hskip-6.0pt\mathop{\lim}\limits_{\alpha\to n}w^{-1}(k)\sum\nolimits_{j=a+1}^{k}{\frac{{({k-j+1})\overline{{}^{n-\alpha}}}}{{\Gamma({n-\alpha+1})}}{\nabla^{n+1}}z(j)}\\

(40)

{\textstyle\begin{array}[]{rl}\mathop{\lim}\limits_{\alpha\to n-1}{}_{a}^{\rm C}\nabla_{k}^{\alpha,w(k)}x(k)=&\hskip-6.0pt\mathop{\lim}\limits_{\alpha\to n-1}\{{w^{-1}(k)\sum\nolimits_{j=a+1}^{k}{\frac{{({k-j+1})\overline{{}^{n-\alpha-1}}}}{{\Gamma({n-\alpha})}}{\nabla^{n}}z(j)}}\}\\

(41)

which leads to (
36
) smoothly.

{\textstyle\begin{array}[]{l}\mathop{\lim}\limits_{\alpha\to n}\sum\nolimits_{i=0}^{n-1}{\frac{{({k-a})\overline{{}^{i-\alpha}}}}{{\Gamma({i-\alpha+1})}}}\frac{w(a)}{w(k)}{[{{\nabla^{i,w(k)}}x(k)}]_{k=a}}=\sum\nolimits_{i=0}^{n-1}\frac{\Gamma({k-a+i-n})}{\Gamma({i-n+1})\Gamma({k-a)}}\frac{w(a)}{w(k)}{[{{\nabla^{i,w(k)}}x(k)}]_{k=a}}=0.\end{array}}

Theorem 5
.

For

,

converges uniformly to

,

,

,

{\textstyle\mathop{\lim}\limits_{i\to+\infty}{}_{a}^{\rm G}\nabla_{k}^{-\alpha,w(k)}x_{i}(k)={}_{a}^{\rm G}\nabla_{k}^{-\alpha,w(k)}x(k)}.

(45)

Proof.

Due to the given condition on uniform convergence, one obtains that for any
\varepsilon>0
, there exists
N\in\mathbb{Z}_{+}
, such that
|x_{i}(k)-x(k)|<\varepsilon
, for any
i>N
,
k\in\mathbb{N}_{a+1}^{b}
. Assuming
\kappa:=\mathop{\max}\limits_{k}{}_{a}^{\rm G}\nabla_{k}^{-\alpha,w(k)}1
and
\kappa:=\varepsilon\kappa
, then for any
i>N
, one has

{\textstyle\begin{array}[]{rl}|{}_{a}^{\rm{G}}\nabla_{k}^{-\alpha,w(k)}{x_{i}}(k)-{}_{a}^{\rm{G}}\nabla_{k}^{-\alpha,w(k)}x(k)|=&\hskip-6.0pt|{w^{-1}}(k)\sum\nolimits_{j=a+1}^{k}{\frac{{(k-j+1)\overline{{}^{\alpha-1}}}}{{\Gamma\left(\alpha\right)}}w(j)[{x_{i}}(j)-x(j)]}|\\

\leq&\hskip-6.0pt\varepsilon{w^{-1}}(k)\sum\nolimits_{j=a+1}^{k}{\frac{{(k-j+1)\overline{{}^{\alpha-1}}}}{{\Gamma\left(\alpha\right)}}w(j)}\\
\leq&\hskip-6.0pt\varepsilon\kappa=\epsilon,\end{array}}

(46)

which implies (
45
).
∎

&\hskip-6.0pt+\sum\nolimits_{i=0}^{n-1}{[{{\nabla^{i,\lambda}}x({k+a-j}){\nabla^{n-i-1,\lambda}}{}_{a}^{\rm G}\nabla_{k}^{\alpha-n,\lambda}y(j)}]_{j=a}^{j=k}}\\
=&\hskip-6.0pt{}_{a}^{\rm C}\nabla_{k}^{\alpha,\lambda}x(k)\ast y(k)+\sum\nolimits_{i=0}^{n-1}{[{{\nabla^{i,\lambda}}x({k+a-j}){}_{a}^{\rm R}\nabla_{j}^{\alpha-i-1,\lambda}y(j)}]_{j=a}^{j=k}}\\
=&\hskip-6.0pt\sum\nolimits_{j=a+1}^{k}{{}_{a}^{\rm C}\nabla_{j}^{\alpha,\lambda}x(j)y({k+a+1-j})}\\
&\hskip-6.0pt+\sum\nolimits_{i=0}^{n-1}{[{\nabla^{i,\lambda}x({k+a-j}){}_{a}^{\rm R}\nabla_{j}^{\alpha-i-1,\lambda}y(j)}]_{j=a}^{j=k}}.\end{array}

All of these complete the proof.

5
Conclusions

In this paper, the nabla tempered fractional difference and fractional sum have been investigated originally. A series of analytic properties are developed with rigorous mathematical proof including the equivalence relation, the nabla Taylor formula, and the nabla Laplace transform. It is believed that this work reveals the relationship between time domain and frequency domain of nabla tempered fractional calculus and enriches the knowledge of nabla tempered fractional calculus.
