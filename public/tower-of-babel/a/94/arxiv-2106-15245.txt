Abstract
. We generalize a terminating summation formula to a unilateral nonterminating, and further, a bilateral summation formula by a property of analytic functions. The unilateral one is proved to be a q q -analogue of a F 3 4 {}_{4}F_{3} -summation formula. And, an identity unifying Jacobi’s triple product identity and the quintuple product identity is obtained as a special case of the bilateral one.

From Theorem

{}_{4}F_{3}
-summation

The hypergeometric series
{}_{r}F_{s}
is defined by
[
1
, p. 62, Eq.
(2.1.2)]
:

_{r}F_{s}\left(\begin{array}[]{c}a_{1},\ldots,a_{r}\\
b_{1},\ldots,b_{s}\end{array};x\right)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{(a_{1})_{n}\ldots(a_{r})_{n}}{(b_{1})_{n}\ldots(b_{s})_{n}}\frac{x^{n}}{n!},

where
(a)_{n}=a(a+1)\cdots(a+n-1)
for
n>0
and
(a)_{0}=1
. The

\Gamma
-function and its
q
-analogue, say, the

\Gamma_{q}
-function, are defined, respectively, by:

(3.1)

\Gamma(x)=\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}\frac{n!n^{x-1}}{(x)_{n}}\mbox{\qquad and\qquad}\Gamma_{q}(x)=\frac{(q;q)_{\infty}}{(q^{x};q)_{\infty}}(1-q)^{1-x}.

The definitions and properties of the
\Gamma
- and

\Gamma_{q}
-functions can be found in the books
[
1
]
and

[
4
]
. Note that

\lim_{q\rightarrow 1^{-}}\Gamma_{q}(x)=\Gamma(x)
.

We state the
{}_{4}F_{3}
-summation formula in the following.

Corollary
3.1
.

[
1
, p. 148, Cor.

_{4}F_{3}\left(\begin{array}[]{cccc}a,&{a\over 2}+1,&c,&d\\
&{a\over 2},&a-c+1,&a-d+1\end{array};-1\right)=\frac{\Gamma{(a-c+1)}\Gamma{(a-d+1)}}{\Gamma{(a+1)}\Gamma{(a-c-d+1)}}.

Performing the replacement:

\displaystyle\frac{(q^{c}+q^{d})}{q^{c+d}}\frac{(q^{1+a},-q^{1-c},-q^{1-d},q^{a-c-d+1};q)_{\infty}}{(-q,q^{a-c+1},q^{a-d+1},-q^{-c-d};q)_{\infty}}

respectively. After routine simplification, we have

(4.1)

\sum_{k=-\infty}^{\infty}(-1)^{k}\left(1-x^{4}q^{4k+2}\right)q^{3k^{2}-k\over 2}x^{3k}=\frac{(q,q/x^{2},x^{2};q)_{\infty}}{(x,q/x;q)_{\infty}}.

The LHS of (
4.1
) is equal to

(4.2)

\displaystyle\sum_{k=-\infty}^{\infty}(-1)^{k}q^{3k^{2}-k\over 2}x^{3k}+\sum_{k=-\infty}^{\infty}(-1)^{k+1}q^{3k^{2}+7k+4\over 2}x^{3k+4}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=-\infty}^{\infty}(-1)^{k}q^{3k^{2}-k\over 2}x^{3k}+\sum_{k=-\infty}^{\infty}(-1)^{k}q^{3k^{2}+k\over 2}x^{3k+1}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=-\infty}^{\infty}(-1)^{k}q^{k(3k-1)\over 2}x^{3k}(1+xq^{k}).

Combining (
4.1
) and (
4.2
), we obtain Corollary

4.3
. This completes the proof.

Corollary
4.4
.

(Jacobi’s triple product identity
[
1
, p. 497]
,
[
3
, p.
35]
or
[
4
, p. 15, Eq. (1.6.1)]
)

For
a\neq 0
, there holds

\sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty}(-1)^{n}q^{n(n-1)\over 2}a^{n}=(q,q/a,a;q)_{\infty}.

Proof.

Rewrite the LHS of the formula in Corollary
4.2
as

(4.3)

\displaystyle\sum_{k=-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{(s;q)_{k}}{(aq/s;q)_{k}}q^{3k^{2}-3k\over 2}\left(-\frac{a^{2}}{s}\right)^{k}-a^{2}\sum_{k=-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{(s;q)_{k}}{(aq/s;q)_{k}}q^{3k^{2}+5k\over 2}\left(-\frac{a^{2}}{s}\right)^{k}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{(s;q)_{k}}{(aq/s;q)_{k}}q^{3k^{2}-3k\over 2}\left(-\frac{a^{2}}{s}\right)^{k}-\sum_{k=-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{(s;q)_{k-1}}{(aq/s;q)_{k-1}}q^{3k^{2}-k-2\over 2}\frac{(-1)^{k-1}a^{2k}}{s^{k-1}}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{(s;q)_{k-1}}{(aq/s;q)_{k-1}}q^{3k^{2}-3k\over 2}\left(-\frac{a^{2}}{s}\right)^{k-1}\left[{1-sq^{k-1}\over 1-aq^{k}/s}\left(-{a^{2}\over s}\right)-q^{k-1}a^{2}\right]

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{(s;q)_{k-1}}{(aq/s;q)_{k-1}}q^{3k^{2}-3k\over 2}\left(-\frac{a^{2}}{s}\right)^{k-1}{-a^{2}+a^{3}q^{2k-1}\over s-aq^{k}}.

Multiply both sides of the formula in Corollary
4.2
by
s\over a
, and use (
4.3
) to get

(4.4)

\sum_{k=-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{(s;q)_{k-1}}{(aq/s;q)_{k-1}}q^{3k^{2}-3k\over 2}\left(-\frac{a^{2}}{s}\right)^{k-1}{s(-a+a^{2}q^{2k-1})\over s-aq^{k}}=\frac{(q,q/a,a;q)_{\infty}}{(q/s,aq/s;q)_{\infty}}.

We look both sides of (
4.4
) as functions of
s
. Note that
the point
s=\infty
is their removable pole, and then, the series
on the LHS of (
4.4
) is convergent uniformly in a neighborhood
of
\infty
. Let
s\rightarrow\infty
. The sum and the limit can
interchange. Thus, we have

(4.5)

\sum_{k=-\infty}^{\infty}\lim_{s\rightarrow\infty}\left(\frac{(s;q)_{k-1}}{(aq/s;q)_{k-1}}q^{3k^{2}-3k\over 2}\left(-\frac{a^{2}}{s}\right)^{k-1}{s(-a+a^{2}q^{2k-1})\over s-aq^{k}}\right)=(q,q/a,a;q)_{\infty}.

Now we compute the limitation behind the sum above. For any integer

k
,

(4.6)

\displaystyle\lim_{s\rightarrow\infty}\left(\frac{(s;q)_{k-1}}{(aq/s;q)_{k-1}}q^{3k^{2}-3k\over 2}\left(-\frac{a^{2}}{s}\right)^{k-1}{s(-a+a^{2}q^{2k-1})\over s-aq^{k}}\right)

\displaystyle=q^{3k^{2}-3k\over 2}a^{2k-2}\lim_{s\rightarrow\infty}\left(\frac{(s;q)_{k-1}}{(aq/s;q)_{k-1}}\left(-\frac{1}{s}\right)^{k-1}\right)\lim_{s\rightarrow\infty}\left({s(-a+a^{2}q^{2k-1})\over s-aq^{k}}\right)

\displaystyle=q^{3k^{2}-3k\over 2}a^{2k-2}\times q^{k^{2}-3k+2\over 2}\times\left(-a+a^{2}q^{2k-1}\right)

\displaystyle=-q^{2k^{2}-3k+1}a^{2k-1}+q^{2k^{2}-k}a^{2k}.

In (
4.5
) and (
4.6
), we have proved

(4.7)

\displaystyle-\sum_{k=-\infty}^{\infty}q^{2k^{2}-3k+1}a^{2k-1}+\sum_{k=-\infty}^{\infty}q^{2k^{2}-k}a^{2k}=(q,q/a,a;q)_{\infty}.

Combining the two sums on the LHS of (
4.7
), we arrive at
Corollary
4.4
. This ends the proof.
