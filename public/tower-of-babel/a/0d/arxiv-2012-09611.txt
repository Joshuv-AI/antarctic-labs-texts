Towards room-temperature superconductivity
Jacob Szeftel 1, Nicolas Sandeau 2, Michel Abou Ghantous 3, Muhammad El-Saba 4

Abstract
By taking advantage of a stability criterion established recently, the critical temperature T c T_{c} is reckoned with help of the microscopic parameters, characterising the normal and superconducting electrons, namely the independent-electron band structure and a repulsive two-electron force. The emphasis is laid on the sharp T c T_{c} dependence upon electron concentration and inter-electron coupling, which might offer a practical route toward higher T c T_{c} values and help to understand why high- T c T_{c} compounds exhibit such remarkable properties.
