Abstract
In this article we will consider average angles of triangle, which share the same side with regular polygons. In particular we will count average angles in the triangle, which share the same bottom side with a square with length side d = 1 d=1 .

Introduction

Whenever we are talking about something average of continuity, we use integrals.

Consider following problem:

Let us count the average number of
\left\{1,2,3,4,5\right\}
. We know, it will be
\frac{1+2+3+4+5}{5}=3
— sum of numbers divided by their quantity.

But, we can solve this problem using integral. Let
x
be variable, which represents each number and the limits of integral be from 1 to 5. Then, according to integrate rules we get:

\frac{1}{5-1}\int_{1}^{5}xdx=\frac{1}{4}\frac{x^{2}}{2}\Biggr|^{5}_{1}=\frac{25-1}{8}=3
