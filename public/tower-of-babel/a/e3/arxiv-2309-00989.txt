Equitable list coloring of planar graphs with given maximum degree
H. A. Kierstead, Alexandr Kostochka, Zimu Xiang

Abstract
If L L is a list assignment of r r colors to each vertex of an n n -vertex graph G G , then an equitable L L -coloring of G G is a proper coloring of vertices of G G from their lists such that no color is used more than ⌈ n / r ⌉ \lceil n/r\rceil times. A graph is equitably r r - choosable if it has an equitable L L -coloring for every r r -list assignment L L . In 2003, Kostochka, Pelsmajer and West (KPW) conjectured that an analog of the famous Hajnal-Szemerédi Theorem on equitable coloring holds for equitable list coloring, namely, that for each positive integer r r every graph G G with maximum degree at most r − 1 r-1 is equitably r r -choosable. The main result of this paper is that for each r ≥ 9 r\geq 9 and each planar graph G G , a stronger statement holds: if the maximum degree of G G is at most r r , then G G is equitably r r -choosable. In fact, we prove the result for a broader class of graphs—the class ℬ {\mathcal{B}} of the graphs in which each bipartite subgraph B B with | V ⁡ ( B ) | ≥ 3 |V(B)|\geq 3 has at most 2 ​ | V ⁡ ( B ) | − 4 2|V(B)|-4 edges. Together with some known results, this implies that the KPW Conjecture holds for all graphs in ℬ {\mathcal{B}} , in particular, for all planar graphs.

4
Concluding remarks

1. Our Theorem
4
establishes a statement implying the validity of both, Conjecture
1
and
Conjecture
3
, for planar graphs with maximum degree at least
9
. This suggests that possibly the following is true.

Conjecture 19
.

If
G
is an
r
-colorable graph with
\Delta(G)\leq r
then either
r
is odd and
K_{r,r}\subseteq G
or

G
is equitably

It might be interesting to determine whether Conjecture

(and Conjecture

) holds for all planar graphs. Also one could check whether Conjecture

holds in special cases (e.g.
r\leq 4
or
r\geq n/4
) for which Conjecture
1
is known to be true.

2. Our Theorem
7
deals with
class
{\mathcal{B}}
which topologically is much broader than the class of planar graphs. For example, for any graph
H
, the graph
B_{H}
obtained from
H

{\mathcal{B}}
.
Therefore, every graph is a minor of a graph in
{\mathcal{B}}
.
In particular, the acyclic chromatic number of graphs in
{\mathcal{B}}
can be arbitrarily large, while it is at most
5
for any planar graph.

So, proving
Conjecture
19
for all graphs in
{\mathcal{B}}
would be more interesting than proving it merely for planar graphs.

Another way to extend results on equitable coloring of planar graphs is to consider graphs embeddable into other surfaces. For example, it was proved in
[
9
]
that for each
r\geq 9
every graph
G
embeddable into torus or the Möbius strip with
\Delta(G)\leq r
has an equitable
r
-coloring.

3. In
[
4
]
, the following refinement of the Hajnal-Szemerédi Theorem was proved.

Theorem 20
.

For any
r\geq 1
, if for every edge

in a
graph

,
d(x)+d(y)\geq 2r+1
, then
G

has an equitable
r+1
coloring.

It would be interesting to prove a list analogue of Theorem
20
for wide classes of graphs. Is this analogue true for planar graphs? For graphs in
{\mathcal{B}}
?

4. Recall that one can extract from our proof of Theorem
7
a polynomial-time algorithm
that produces for every
graph
G\in{\mathcal{B}}
with
\Delta(G)\leq r
and every
(r+1)
-list

for

-coloring of

It is also known (see, e.g.
[

) that there is a polynomial-time algorithm for finding an equitable

-coloring of an arbitrary graph with maximum degree at most

. But we do not know a similar algorithmic analogue of
