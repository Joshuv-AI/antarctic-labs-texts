2
The GKS Formalism

Giveon-Kutasov-Seiberg revived the old fashioned worldsheet perturbative formalism to study propagation of strings and superstrings on
AdS_{3}\times S^{3}\times K3
and
AdS_{3}\times S^{3}\times T^{4}
backgrounds. They derived the corresponding spacetime conformal field theory, Virasoro and current algebras. It was an establishment of the
AdS_{3}/CFT_{2}
conjecture. (Another derivation of the same was provided by Eberhardt-Gaberdiel-Gopakumar in
[
26
]
from a different angle.)

The bosonic string on
AdS_{3}
times some compact manifold has the Lagrangian

\mathcal{L}=\frac{2l^{2}}{l_{s}^{2}}(\partial\phi\bar{\partial}\phi+e^{2\phi}\bar{\partial}\gamma\partial\gamma)

(1)

where
l_{s}
is the fundamental string length and
l
is the radius of the curvature of the hyperspace used to define
AdS_{3}

B
-field of the form

B=l^{2}e^{2\phi}d\gamma\wedge d\bar{\gamma}

(2)

has been incorporated and the
AdS_{3}
metric is

ds^{2}=l^{2}(d\phi^{2}+e^{2\phi}\partial\gamma\partial\bar{\gamma}).

(3)

The theory has affine
SL(2,R)\times SL(2,R)

J^{a}(z)J^{b}(z)=\frac{k\eta^{AB}/2}{(z-w)^{2}}+\frac{i\eta_{CD}\epsilon^{ABC}J^{D}(w)}{z-w}+...

(5)

The central charge of the model is

c=\frac{3k}{k-2}.

(6)

The current algebra has a Wakimoto free field representation

\displaystyle J^{-}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\beta,~~~J^{3}=\beta\gamma+\frac{\alpha_{+}}{2}\partial\phi,

\displaystyle J^{+}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\beta\gamma^{2}+\alpha_{+}\gamma\partial\phi+k\partial\gamma

with

\alpha_{+}^{2}=2k-4.

(8)

The relevant vertex operators are

V_{jm\bar{m}}=\gamma^{j+m}\bar{\gamma}^{j+\bar{m}}exp\left(\frac{2j}{{\alpha_{+}}}\phi\right)

(9)

with scaling dimension of

being

h=-\frac{j(j+1)}{k-2}.

(10)

The unitarity condition puts a condition on the quantum numbers

[

]
and
[

\displaystyle-\oint dzJ^{+}(z),~L_{-1}=-\oint dzJ^{-}(z)

[

AdS_{3}\times\mathcal{N}

(15)

(16)

The Virasoro generators are defined as

3
Superalgebra Doubling

With above GKS formalism we would now like to demand spacetime supersymmetry in the theory. It is well known that to have N=1 spacetime supersymmetry the worldsheet superconformal symmetry must be enhanced to N=2.

The N=1 superconformal algebra has only two generators: the energy-momentum tensor
T(z)
and super-tensor
G(z)
. Both of these have been constructed above. N=2 superconformal symmetry has four generators-the energy-momentum tensor
T(z)
, two super-tensors
G^{\pm}(z)
and a
U(1)
current
J(z)
.

GKS devised an ingenious method to get N=2 superconformal symmetry on worldsheet to get spactime supersymmetry. In particular they abandoned the usual approach of identifying the
U(1)
currents. The reason is that this usual approach, discussed in their Appendix B, suffers from the problem of superalgebra doubling. We now spell out this problem. The N=2
U(1)
current is written as sum of parts corresponding to isometries

\frac{SL(2,R)}{U(1)}\times\frac{SU(2)}{U(1)}\times U(1)^{2}\times U(1)^{4}

(25)

J(z)=J^{(1)}+J^{(2)}+J^{(3)}+J^{(4)}

(26)

with

\displaystyle J^{(1)}(z)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{2}{k}(J^{3}+i\psi^{1}\psi^{2}),~J^{(2)}(z)=\frac{2}{k}(i\chi^{1}\chi^{2}-K^{3})

\displaystyle J^{(3)}(z)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{2}{k}\psi^{3}\chi^{3},~~~~~~~~~~~~J^{(4)}(z)=i\lambda^{1}\lambda^{2}+i\lambda^{3}\lambda^{4}.

(27)

Here
J^{3}
and
K^{3}
are total
SL(2,R)
and
SU(2)
currents respectively. To construct spacetime supercharges
J(z)
is bosonised as

J(z)=i\partial(H+H_{1}+H_{2}+H_{3})

(28)

with

\displaystyle i\partial H

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{2}{k}(-i\psi^{1}\psi^{2}+i\chi^{1}\chi^{2}+J^{3}-K^{3}),~i\partial H_{1}=\frac{2}{k}\psi^{3}\chi^{3},

\displaystyle\partial H_{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\lambda^{1}\lambda^{2},~\partial H_{3}=\lambda^{3}\lambda^{4}.

(29)

The spacetime charges are

\displaystyle Q_{\alpha}^{\pm}=\oint dzS^{\pm}_{\alpha},~\bar{Q}_{\alpha}^{\pm}=\oint dz\bar{S}^{\pm}_{\alpha}

(30)

with

\displaystyle S_{\alpha}^{\pm}=e^{\pm\frac{i}{2}(H_{2}+H_{3})},~S_{\bar{\alpha}}^{\pm}=e^{\pm\frac{i}{2}(H_{2}-H_{3})}

(31)

Now the spacetime superalgebra is

\displaystyle\{Q_{\alpha}^{+},Q_{\beta}^{-}\}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\delta_{\alpha\beta}(J^{3}-K^{3}),~\{\bar{Q}_{\alpha}^{+},\bar{Q}_{\beta}^{-}\}=\delta_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}(J^{3}+K^{3})

\displaystyle\{Q_{\alpha}^{-},\bar{Q}_{\beta}^{+}\}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\{Q_{\alpha}^{+},\bar{Q}_{\bar{\beta}}^{-}\}=\gamma^{i}_{\alpha\bar{\beta}}P_{i}.

(32)

First of these equations is the Ramond sector N=4 superalgebra. With that interpretation the second of the above equations give another copy of the Ramond sector superalgebra.

For a giveN theory there should be only one (small) N=4 superconformal algebra. Here we have two copies. Why do we have two copies of the Ramond sector (small) N=4 superconformal algebra is the problem we are trying to resolve in this note.

4
The GKS Resolution

One immediate idea to interpret this superalgebra doubling is to propose that the second equation of (
3
) is the copy of superalgebra corresponding to the other chirality. GKS reject this possibility on the basis of two rather stark arguments. Firstly the left and right moving generators commute or anti-commute, that is, have zero commutators or anti-commutators with the other chirality. From third of Eqn.(
3
) it is clear that the superalgebra of two copies of the superalgebra have non-zero anti-commutators. Secondly the worldsheet and spacetime chiralities in string theory are related. Thus a single worldsheet chirality giving rise to both chiralities in the spacetime theory is not feasible. Moreover the other chirality of worldsheet too will give rise to both chiralities on the spacetime side.

The GKS solution to the Ramond superalgebra doubling is to propose that the two superalgebras are different Ramond twists
[
30
]
of the NS generators

\displaystyle T^{3}_{n}(\eta)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle T^{3}_{n}(0)-\frac{\eta kp}{2}\delta_{n,0},~T^{\pm}_{n\pm\eta}=T^{\pm}_{n}(0),

\displaystyle Q^{1}_{n+\frac{\eta}{2}}(\eta)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle Q^{1}_{\eta}(0),~~Q^{2}_{n-\frac{\eta}{2}}(\eta)=Q^{2}_{n}(0),

\displaystyle L_{n}(\eta)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle L_{n}(0)-\eta T^{3}_{n}(0)+\eta^{2}\frac{kp}{4}\delta_{n,0}.

(33)

Now since both vacua cannot be described at the same time they proposed the following procedure to restrict to single sector of the vacuum. If
J(z)
contains the combination
J^{3}-K^{3}
then the physical states are defined by restricting to the cohomology of operators
Q_{\alpha}^{\pm}
:

\displaystyle Q_{\alpha}^{\pm}|phy\rangle=0,~|phy\rangle\sim|phy\rangle+Q|anything\rangle.

(34)

Because of (
3
) such states have
J^{3}-K^{3}=0
. After that the four remaining supercharges
\bar{Q}_{\bar{\alpha}}^{\pm}
together with
J^{3}+K^{3}
form the left moving small N=4 superconformal algebra

\displaystyle\left[{L}_{m},{L}_{n}\right]

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(m-n){L}_{m+n}+\frac{{c}}{12}(m^{3}-m)\delta_{{m+n},0},

\displaystyle\left[{L}_{m},{G}_{r}^{a}\right]

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left(\frac{m}{2}-r\right){G}^{a}_{m+r},~~~\left[{L}_{m},{J}^{i}_{n}\right]=-n{J}^{i}_{m+n},

\displaystyle\{{G}^{a}_{r},{G}^{b}_{s}\}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 2\delta^{ab}{L}_{r+s}-4(r-s)\alpha^{+i}_{ab}{J}^{i}_{r+s}+\frac{{c}}{3}\left(r^{2}-\frac{1}{4}\right)\delta^{ab}\delta_{{r+s},0},

\displaystyle\left[{J}^{i}_{m},{G}^{a}_{r}\right]

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\alpha^{+i}_{ab}{G}^{b}_{m+r},~~~~\left[{J}^{i}_{m},{J}^{j}_{n}\right]=\epsilon^{ijk}{J}^{k}_{m+n}-m\frac{{k}}{2}\delta^{ij}\delta_{m+n,0},

\displaystyle\alpha^{+i}_{ab}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\left(\delta^{i}_{a}\delta^{0}_{b}-\delta^{i}_{b}\delta^{0}_{a}\right)+\frac{1}{2}\epsilon^{iab},~~~{c}=6{k}.

(35)

When the current
J(z)
contains
J^{3}+K^{3}
the roles of
Q
and
\bar{Q}
as well as
J^{3}-K^{3}
and
J^{3}+K^{3}
are reversed.

By this procedure the states of the other chirality are killed. Moreover, GKS argue, that this procedure ensures
P^{i}=0
using the unitarity argument. The projecting out of the states of other chirality by Eqn.(
34
) the worldsheet and spactime chirality correlation is restored. They also compare the projection (
34
) to the topological twist
[
31
,
32
,
33
]
of N=2 theory whose physical states are in one to one correspondence with the chiral ring of the original theory.

This resolution of the superalgebra doubling is admittedly conjectural and kills half of the spectrum in an ad hoc manner.

We assert that the two copies of the Ramond superalgebra that Giveon-Kutasov-Seiberg get in their construction are the two distinct and independent branches of the theory just like the two branches of the moduli space of the ADHM instanton sigma model. Thus these should be treated separately. In particular there is no need to kill the Hilbert space of physical states of one branch or the other.
