Boundedness of Fractional Integral operators and their commutators in vanishing generalized weighted Morrey spaces
Bilal Çekiç

Abstract




In this article, we establish some conditions for the boundedness of
fractional integral operators on the vanishing generalized weighted Morrey
spaces. We also investigate corresponding commutators generated by BMO
functions.

Keywords :
Vanishing generalized weighted Morrey spaces, fractional
integral operator, Commutator

MSC :
42B20; 42B35; 46E30

1
Introduction

Morrey spaces
L^{p,\lambda}
that play important role in the theory of
partial differential equations an in harmonic analysis were introduced by C.
Morrey
[
9
]
in 1938. Since then these spaces and various
generalizations of Morrey spaces have been extensively studied by many
authors. Mizuhara
[
8
]
introduced the generalized Morrey space
L^{p,\varphi}
and Komori and Shirai
[
6
]
defined the weighted
Morrey spaces
L^{p,\kappa}(w)
. Guliyev
[
3
]
have given the
notion of generalized weighted Morrey space
L^{p,\varphi}\left(w\right)

which can be accepted as an extension of
L^{p,\varphi}
and
L^{p,\kappa}(w)
. We refer readers to the survey
[
13
]
and to the elegant book

[
2
]
for further generalizations about these spaces and references
on recent developments in this field.

Fractional maximal operator
M_{\alpha}
and fractional integral operator
I_{\alpha}
play an important role in harmonic analysis. In recent years
many authors have studied the boundedness of these operators on Morrey type
spaces. The boundedness of fractional integral operator
I_{\alpha}
was
proved by Adams
[
1
]
on classical Morrey spaces. In
[
5
]

authours find conditions on the pair
\left(\varphi_{1},\varphi_{2}\right)
which ensure Spanne type boundedness of fractional maximal
M_{\alpha}
and fractional integral operator
I_{\alpha}
from one
generalized Morrey spaces to another generalized Morrey space. The
boundedness of fractional integral operator
I_{\alpha}
was proved by
Komori and Shirai
[
6
]
on weighted Morrey spaces. The boundedness
of
I_{\alpha,b}
was proved by Di Fazio and Ragusa
[
15
]
on
classical Morrey spaces, by Guliyev and Shukurov
[
5
]
on
generalized Morrey space and by Komori and Shirai
[
6
]
on weighted
Morrey spaces. Recently, Guliyev proved the boundedness of fractional
integral operator
I_{\alpha}
and fractional maximal operator
M_{\alpha}
and their commutators on generalized weighted Morrey space
L^{p,\varphi}\left(w\right)
in the elegant paper
[
3
]
.

The vanishing Morrey space
VL^{p,\lambda}
which has been first introduced
by Vitanza in
[
17
]
is a subspace of functions in
L^{p,\lambda}

satisfying the following condition

\underset{r\rightarrow 0}{\lim}\sup\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}x\in\mathbb{R}^{n}\\
0<t<r\end{subarray}}t^{-\frac{\lambda}{p}}\left\|f\right\|_{L^{p}\left(B\left(x,t\right)\right)}=0.

Ragusa
[
14
]
proved the boundedness of
I_{\alpha,b}
on vanishing
Morrey spaces. Persson et al.
[
12
]
proved the boundedness of
commutators of Hardy operators in vanishing Morrey spaces. Later N. Samko

[
16
]
introduced the vanishing generalized Morrey spaces
VL_{\Pi}^{p,\varphi}\left(\Omega\right)
and proved the boundedness
of
I_{\alpha}
and
M_{\alpha\text{ }}
on these spaces.

Inspired by the above papers, we introduce the vanishing generalized
weighted Morrey spaces which are suitable subspace of functions in
L^{p,\varphi}\left(w\right)
and prove the boundedness of
M_{\alpha\text{ }}
and
I_{\alpha}
from the vanishing generalized weighted Morrey
space
VL_{\Pi}^{p,\varphi}\left(\mathbb{R}^{n};w^{p}\right)
to another vanishing generalized weighted Morrey space
VL_{\Pi}^{q,\psi}\left(\mathbb{R}^{n};w^{q}\right).
Furthermore we show that the commutators of both
M_{\alpha\text{ }}
and
I_{\alpha}
are bounded in vanishing generalized
weighted Morrey spaces. In all the cases the conditions for the boundedness
are given in terms of Zygmund-type integral inequalities on
\left(\varphi,\psi\right)
, where there is no any assumption on monotonicity of
\varphi
,
\psi
in
r
.

Throughout this paper,
C,c,c_{i}
etc. are used as positive constant that
can change from one line to another.
A\lesssim B
means that
A\leq cB

with some positive constant
c
. If
A\lesssim B
and
B\lesssim A
, then we
say
A\approx B
which means
A\
and
B
are equivalent.

2
Preliminaries

Let
w
be a weight function on
\mathbb{R}^{n},
such that
w(x)>0
for almost every
x\in\mathbb{R}^{n}.
For
1\leq p<\infty
and
\Omega\subset\mathbb{R}^{n}
we denote the weighted Lebesgue space by
L^{p,w}\left(\Omega\right)
with the norm

\left\|f\right\|_{L^{p,w}\left(\Omega\right)}=\left(\int\limits_{\Omega}\left|f(x)\right|^{p}w(x)dx\right)^{1/p}<\infty.

Let
1\leq p<\infty
,
\varphi
be a positive measurable function on
\mathbb{R}^{n}\times\left(0,\infty\right)
and
w
be a weight function on
\mathbb{R}^{n}
. We denote by
L^{p,\varphi}\left(\mathbb{R}^{n};w\right)=

L^{p,\varphi}\left(w\right)
the generalized weighted
Morrey space, the space of all functions
f\in L_{loc}^{p,w}\left(\mathbb{R}^{n}\right)
with finite quasinorm

\left\|f\right\|_{L^{p,\varphi}\left(w\right)}=\underset{x\in\mathbb{R}^{n},r>0}{\sup}\frac{1}{\varphi^{\frac{1}{p}}\left(x,r\right)}\left\|f\right\|_{L^{p,w}\left(B\left(x,r\right)\right)}

where
B\left(x,r\right)
denote the open ball centered at
x
of radius
r.

The fractional integral operator (Riesz potential)
I_{\alpha}
and
fractional Maximal operator
M_{\alpha},
which play important roles in
real and harmonic analysis, are defined by

I_{\alpha}f(x)=\mathop{\displaystyle\int}\limits_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\frac{f(y)}{\left|x-y\right|^{n-\alpha}}dy,\qquad 0<\alpha<n

and

M_{\alpha}f(x)=\underset{r>0}{\sup}\frac{1}{r^{n-\alpha}}\mathop{\displaystyle\int}\limits_{B(x,r)}\left|f(y)\right|dy,\qquad 0\leq\alpha<n

where
f\in L_{loc}^{1}\left(\mathbb{R}^{n}\right)
. If
\alpha=0,
then
M\equiv M_{0}
is the Hardy-Littlewood
maximal operator.

The class of
A_{p}
weights was introduced by B. Muckenhoupt in
[
10
]
. The Muckenhoupt classes characterize the boundedness of the
Hardy-Littlewood maximal function
M
on weighted Lebesgue spaces. It is
known that,
M
is bounded on
L^{p,w}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
if and only if
w\in A_{p}
,
1<p<\infty.

Now we define Muckenhoupt class
A_{p}.
Let
1<p<\infty
and
\left|B\right|
be the Lebesgue measure of the ball
B.
A weight
w
is said
to be an
A_{p}
weight, if there exists a positive constant
c_{p}
such
that, for every ball
B\subset\mathbb{R}^{n},

\left(\mathop{\displaystyle\int}\limits_{B}w\left(x\right)dx\right)\left(\mathop{\displaystyle\int}\limits_{B}w\left(x\right)^{1-p^{\prime}}dx\right)^{p-1}\leq c_{p}\left|B\right|^{p},

when
1<p<\infty
, and for
p=1

\left(\mathop{\displaystyle\int}\limits_{B}w\left(y\right)dy\right)\leq c_{1}\left|B\right|w\left(x\right)

for a.e.
x\in B.

A weight
w
belongs to
A_{p,q}
for
1<p<q<\infty
if there exists
c_{p,q}>1
such that

\left\|w\right\|_{L^{q}(B)}\left\|w^{-1}\right\|_{L^{p^{\prime}}(B)}\leq c_{p,q}\left|B\right|^{1+\frac{1}{q}-\frac{1}{p}},

where
\frac{1}{p}+\frac{1}{p^{\prime}}=1
.
A_{p,q}
class was introduce
by B. Muckenhoupt and R. Wheeden
[
11
]
to study weighted norm
inequalities for fractional integral operators.

Following lemma gives the relation between
A_{p,q}
and
A_{p}
class.

Lemma 2.1

[
7
]
If
w^{q}\in A_{1+\frac{q}{p^{\prime}}}
with
1<p<q,
then
w\in A_{p,q}.

Lemma 2.2

[
3
]
Let
0<\alpha<n,

1<p<\frac{n}{\alpha},

\frac{1}{q}=\frac{1}{p}-\frac{\alpha}{n}
and
w\in A_{p,q}
. Then the inequality

\left\|I_{\alpha}f\right\|_{L^{q,w^{q}}\left(B\left(x,r\right)\right)}\lesssim\left\|w\right\|_{L^{q}\left(B(x,r)\right)}\mathop{\displaystyle\int}\limits_{2r}^{\infty}\left\|f\right\|_{L^{p,w^{p}}\left(B\left(x,t\right)\right)}\left\|w\right\|_{L^{q}\left(B\left(x_{,}t\right)\right)}^{-1}\frac{dt}{t}

(
2.1
)

holds for any ball
B(x,r)
and for all
f\in L_{loc}^{p,w}\left(\mathbb{R}^{n}\right).

2.1
Vanishing generalized weighted Morrey spaces

Let
\Omega
be an open set in
\mathbb{R}^{n}
and
\Pi
be an arbitrary subset of
\Omega
. Let also
w
be a
weight function on
\Omega
,
\varphi\left(x,r\right)
be a measurable
non negative function on
\Pi\times\left[0,l\right)

\left(l=diam~\Omega\right)
and positive for all
(x,t)\in\Pi\times(0,l)
.
The vanishing weighted Morrey space
VL_{\Pi}^{p,\varphi}\left(\Omega;w\right)=VL_{\Pi}^{p,\varphi}\left(w\right)
is defined as the space
of functions
f\in L_{loc}^{p,w}\left(\Omega\right)
with finite quasi
norm

\left\|f\right\|_{VL_{\Pi}^{p,\varphi}\left(w\right)}=\underset{x\in\Pi,0<r<l}{\sup}\frac{1}{\varphi^{\frac{1}{p}}\left(x,r\right)}\left\|f\right\|_{L^{p,w}\left(\widetilde{B}\left(x,r\right)\right)}

such that

\underset{r\rightarrow 0}{\lim}\text{ }\underset{x\in\Pi}{\sup}\frac{1}{\varphi^{\frac{1}{p}}\left(x,r\right)}\left\|f\right\|_{L^{p,w}\left(\widetilde{B}\left(x,r\right)\right)}=0,

(
2.2
)

where
\widetilde{B}\left(x,r\right)=B\left(x,r\right)\cap\Omega
and
1\leq p<\infty.

Naturally, it is suitable to impose the function
\varphi\left(x,r\right)

on the following conditions

\underset{r\rightarrow 0}{\lim}\text{ }\underset{x\in\Pi}{\sup}\frac{\left\|w\right\|_{L^{1}\left(\widetilde{B}(x,r)\right)}}{\varphi\left(x,r\right)}=0

(
2.3
)

and

\underset{r>1}{\inf}\text{ }\underset{x\in\Pi}{\sup}\text{ }\varphi\left(x,r\right)>0

(
2.4
)

where the last condition must be imposed when
\Omega
is unbounded. These
conditions makes
VM_{\Pi}^{p,\varphi}\left(w\right)
non trivial,
since bounded functions which have compact support belong to these spaces.

Henceforth we denote by
\varphi\in\mathfrak{B}\left(w\right)
if
\varphi\left(x,r\right)
is a measurable non negative function on
\Pi\times\left[0,l\right)
and positive for all
(x,t)\in\Pi\times(0,l)

and satisfies the condition (2.3) and (2.4).

2.2
Commutator of Fractional Integral Operator

Let
f\in L_{loc}^{1}\left(\mathbb{R}^{n}\right)
we define

\left\|f\right\|_{\ast}=\underset{x\in\mathbb{R}^{n},r>0}{\sup}\frac{1}{\left|B\left(x,r\right)\right|}\mathop{\displaystyle\int}\limits_{B\left(x,r\right)}\left|f\left(y\right)-f_{B\left(x,r\right)}\right|dy

where

f_{B\left(x,r\right)}=\frac{1}{\left|B\left(x,r\right)\right|}\mathop{\displaystyle\int}\limits_{B\left(x,r\right)}f\left(y\right)dy.

The space of functions of bounded mean oscillation (BMO) are the class of
functions whose deviation from their means over cubes is bounded. The set

BMO\left(\mathbb{R}^{n}\right)=\left\{f\in L_{loc}^{1}\left(\mathbb{R}^{n}\right):\left\|f\right\|_{\ast}<\infty\right\}

is called the function space of BMO space.

Next we shall introduce the commutators of maximal operator
M_{\alpha,b}

and the commutator of fractional integral operator
I_{\alpha,b}
.

Let
b
be a locally integrable function,
M_{\alpha,b}
and
I_{\alpha,b}

which are formed by
b
are defined by

M_{\alpha,b}f\left(x\right)=b\left(x\right)M_{\alpha}f\left(x\right)-M_{\alpha}\left(bf\right)\left(x\right)

and

I_{\alpha,b}f\left(x\right)=b\left(x\right)I_{\alpha}f\left(x\right)-I_{\alpha}\left(bf\right)\left(x\right).

Lemma 2.3

[
3
]

Let
0<\alpha<n,

1<p<\frac{n}{\alpha},

\frac{1}{q}=\frac{1}{p}-\frac{\alpha}{n},b\in BMO\left(\mathbb{R}^{n}\right)
and
w\in A_{p,q}
. Then the inequality

\left\|I_{\alpha,b}f\right\|_{L^{q,w^{q}}\left(B(x,r)\right)}\lesssim\left\|b\right\|_{\ast}\left\|w\right\|_{L^{q}\left(B(x,r)\right)}\mathop{\displaystyle\int}\limits_{2r}^{\infty}\ln\left(e+\frac{t}{r}\right)\left\|f\right\|_{L^{p,w^{p}}\left(B\left(x,t\right)\right)}\left\|w\right\|_{L^{q}\left(B\left(x,t\right)\right)}^{-1}\frac{dt}{t}

(
2.5
)

holds for any ball
B(x,r)
and for all
f\in L_{loc}^{p,w^{p}}\left(\mathbb{R}^{n}\right).

3
Main Results

3.1
Boundedness of Fractional Integral operators and their
commutators on vanishing generalized weighted Morrey space

Theorem 3.1

Let
0<\alpha<n,

1<p<\frac{n}{\alpha},

\frac{1}{q}=\frac{1}{p}-\frac{\alpha}{n},

w\in A_{p,q}
,
\varphi\in\mathcal{B}\left(w^{p}\right)

and
\psi\in

\mathcal{B}\left(w^{q}\right)
. Then the operators
M_{\alpha}
and
I_{\alpha}
are bounded from the vanishing space
VL_{\Pi}^{p,\varphi}\left(\mathbb{R}^{n};w^{p}\right)
to another vanishing space
VL_{\Pi}^{q,\psi}\left(\mathbb{R}^{n};w^{q}\right),
if

c_{\delta}:=\mathop{\displaystyle\int}\limits_{\delta}^{\infty}\sup_{x\in\Pi}\frac{\varphi^{\frac{1}{p}}\left(x,t\right)}{\left\|w\right\|_{L^{q}\left(B\left(x,t\right)\right)}}\frac{dt}{t}<\infty

(
3.1
)

for every
\delta>0,
and

\mathop{\displaystyle\int}\limits_{r}^{\infty}\frac{\varphi^{\frac{1}{p}}\left(x,t\right)}{\left\|w\right\|_{L^{q}\left(B\left(x,t\right)\right)}}\frac{dt}{t}\leq c_{0}\frac{\psi^{\frac{1}{q}}\left(x,r\right)}{\left\|w\right\|_{L^{q}\left(B\left(x,r\right)\right)}}

(
3.2
)

where
c_{0}
does not depend on
x\in\Pi
and
r>0.

Proof.
By the pointwise inequality

M_{\alpha}f(x)\leq I_{\alpha}\left(\left|f\right|\right)(x)
for
0<\alpha<n
it is sufficient to
prove the theorem only for fractional integral operator. For every
f\in VL_{\Pi}^{p,\varphi}\left(\mathbb{R}^{n};w^{p}\right)
we have to show that

\left\|I_{\alpha}f\right\|_{VL_{\Pi}^{q,\psi}\left(w^{q}\right)}\leq c\left\|f\right\|_{VL_{\Pi}^{p,\varphi}\left(w^{p}\right)}

and

\underset{r\rightarrow 0}{\lim}\sup\limits_{x\in\Pi}\frac{1}{\psi^{\frac{1}{q}}\left(x,r\right)}\left\|I_{\alpha}f\right\|_{L^{q,w^{q}}\left(B\left(x,r\right)\right)}=0.

Firstly, we prove that
I_{\alpha}f\in VL_{\Pi}^{q,\psi}\left(w^{q}\right)
when
f\in VL_{\Pi}^{p,\varphi}\left(w^{p}\right)
.
Namely,

\underset{r\rightarrow 0}{\lim}\sup\limits_{x\in\Pi}\frac{1}{\varphi^{\frac{1}{p}}\left(x,r\right)}\left\|f\right\|_{L^{p,w^{p}}\left(B\left(x,r\right)\right)}=0\Rightarrow\underset{r\rightarrow 0}{\lim}\sup\limits_{x\in\Pi}\frac{1}{\psi^{\frac{1}{q}}\left(x,r\right)}\left\|I_{\alpha}f\right\|_{L^{q,w^{q}}\left(B\left(x,r\right)\right)}=0.

Let
0<r<\delta_{0}
. To show that
\sup\limits_{x\in\Pi}\frac{1}{\psi^{\frac{1}{q}}\left(x,r\right)}\left\|I_{\alpha}f\right\|_{L^{q,w^{q}}\left(B\left(x,r\right)\right)}<\varepsilon
for
0<r<\delta_{0}
, by using the inequality
\left(2.1\right)
we can write

\frac{1}{\psi^{\frac{1}{q}}\left(x,r\right)}\left\|I_{\alpha}f\right\|_{L^{q,w^{q}}\left(B\left(x,r\right)\right)}\leq C\left[A_{\delta_{0}}\left(x,r\right)+B_{\delta_{0}}\left(x,r\right)\right]

(
3.3
)

where

A_{\delta_{0}}\left(x,r\right):=\frac{\left\|w\right\|_{L^{q}\left(B(x,r)\right)}}{\psi^{\frac{1}{q}}\left(x,r\right)}\left(\mathop{\displaystyle\int}\limits_{r}^{\delta_{0}}\frac{\varphi^{\frac{1}{p}}\left(x,t\right)}{t\text{ }\left\|w\right\|_{L^{q}(B(x,t))}}\underset{0<r<t}{\sup}\frac{1}{\varphi^{\frac{1}{p}}\left(x,r\right)}\left\|f\right\|_{L^{p,w^{p}}\left(B\left(x,r\right)\right)}dt\right)

and

B_{\delta_{0}}\left(x,r\right):=\frac{\left\|w\right\|_{L^{q}\left(B(x,r)\right)}}{\psi^{\frac{1}{q}}\left(x,r\right)}\left(\mathop{\displaystyle\int}\limits_{\delta_{0}}^{\infty}\frac{\varphi^{\frac{1}{p}}\left(x,t\right)}{t\text{ }\left\|w\right\|_{L^{q}(B(x,t))}}\underset{0<r<t}{\sup}\frac{1}{\varphi^{\frac{1}{p}}\left(x,r\right)}\left\|f\right\|_{L^{p,w^{p}}\left(B\left(x,r\right)\right)}dt\right).

Now we fix
\delta_{0}>0
such that
\sup\limits_{x\in\Pi}\underset{0<r<t}{\sup}\frac{1}{\varphi^{\frac{1}{p}}\left(x,r\right)}\left\|f\right\|_{L^{p,w^{p}}\left(B\left(x,r\right)\right)}<\frac{\varepsilon}{2Cc_{0}}
, where
c_{0}
and
C
are constants from
\left(3.2\right)
and
\left(3.3\right),
respectively. This allows to estimate
uniformly for
0<r<\delta_{0}

\sup\limits_{x\in\Pi}CA_{\delta_{0}}\left(x,r\right)<\frac{\varepsilon}{2}.

The estimation of the second term, in view the condition (3.1), we get

B_{\delta_{0}}\left(x,r\right)\leq c_{\delta_{0}}\frac{\left\|w\right\|_{L^{q}\left(B(x,r)\right)}}{\psi^{\frac{1}{q}}\left(x,r\right)}\left\|f\right\|_{VL_{\Pi}^{p,\varphi}\left(w^{p}\right)}

where
c_{\delta_{0}}
is the constant from
\left(3.1\right).
Since
\psi\in\mathcal{B}\left(w^{q}\right)
it suffices to choose
r
small
enough such that

\sup\limits_{x\in\Pi}\frac{\left\|w^{q}\right\|_{L^{1}\left(B(x,r)\right)}}{\psi\left(x,r\right)}<\left(\frac{\varepsilon}{2Cc_{\delta_{0}}\left\|f\right\|_{VL_{\Pi}^{p,\varphi}\left(w^{p}\right)}}\right)^{q}

which gives required result.

By the definition of the norm and Lemma 2.2 we have

\displaystyle\left\|I_{\alpha}f\right\|_{VL_{\Pi}^{q,\psi}\left(w^{q}\right)}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\underset{x\in\Pi,r>0}{\sup}\frac{1}{\psi^{\frac{1}{q}}\left(x,r\right)}\left\|I_{\alpha}f\right\|_{L^{q,w^{q}}\left(B\left(x,r\right)\right)}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle c\underset{x\in\Pi,r>0}{\sup}\frac{1}{\psi^{\frac{1}{q}}\left(x,r\right)}\left\|w\right\|_{L^{q}\left(B(x,r)\right)}\mathop{\displaystyle\int}\limits_{r}^{\infty}\left\|f\right\|_{{}_{L^{p,w^{p}}\left(B\left(x,t\right)\right)}}\left\|w\right\|_{L^{q}\left(B\left(x,t\right)\right)}^{-1}\frac{dt}{t}.

Thus by the condition (3.2) we get

\left\|I_{\alpha}f\right\|_{VL_{\Pi}^{q,\psi}\left(w^{q}\right)}\leq c\left\|f\right\|_{VL_{\Pi}^{p,\varphi}\left(w^{p}\right)}

which completes the proof of Theorem 3.2.

Example 3.5

and

such

Example 3.6

Let
0<\alpha<n,

1<p<\frac{n}{\alpha}
and
0<\lambda<n-\alpha p.

Moreover, let
\frac{1}{q}=\frac{1}{p}-\frac{\alpha}{n},

\varphi(x,r)=r^{\lambda}
and
\psi(x,r)=r^{\frac{\lambda q}{p}}.
Suppose that
v(y)=\left|y\right|^{\beta}.
Then
v\in A_{p}
,
1<p<\infty,

if
0<\beta<n(p-1).
From Lemma 2.1 we have
w(y)=\left|y\right|^{\frac{\beta}{q}}\in A_{p,q}
,
1<p<q<\infty
if
0<\beta<n\frac{q}{p^{\prime}}
. Thus we can obtain the following:

\left\|w\right\|_{L^{q}(B(x,r))}\approx\left\{\begin{array}[]{ccc}r^{\frac{n+\beta}{q}}&\text{when}&\left|x\right|<3r\\
r^{\frac{n}{q}}(\left|x\right|+r)^{\frac{\beta}{q}}&\text{when}&\left|x\right|\geq 3r\end{array}\right..

If
\left|x\right|<3r,
then we get for the left hand side of (3.2)

\mathop{\displaystyle\int}\limits_{r}^{\infty}\frac{\varphi^{\frac{1}{p}}\left(x,t\right)}{\left\|w\right\|_{L^{q}\left(B\left(x,t\right)\right)}}\frac{dt}{t}\lesssim r^{\frac{\lambda}{p}-\frac{n+\beta}{q}}

since
\frac{\lambda}{p}-\frac{n+\beta}{q}<0.
On the other hand, for the
right hand side of the inequality (3.2), we have

\frac{\psi^{\frac{1}{q}}\left(x,r\right)}{\left\|w\right\|_{L^{q}\left(B\left(x,r\right)\right)}}\approx r^{\frac{\lambda}{p}-\frac{n+\beta}{q}}.

If
\left|x\right|\geq 3r,
then

\mathop{\displaystyle\int}\limits_{r}^{\infty}\frac{\varphi^{\frac{1}{p}}\left(x,t\right)}{\left\|w\right\|_{L^{q}\left(B\left(x,t\right)\right)}}\frac{dt}{t}\lesssim\frac{r^{\frac{\lambda}{p}-\frac{n}{q}}+\left|x\right|^{\frac{\lambda}{p}-\frac{n}{q}}}{\left|x\right|^{\frac{\beta}{q}}}

and

\frac{\psi^{\frac{1}{q}}\left(x,r\right)}{\left\|w\right\|_{L^{q}\left(B\left(x,r\right)\right)}}\approx\frac{r^{\frac{\lambda}{p}-\frac{n}{q}}}{(\left|x\right|+r)^{\frac{\beta}{q}}}.

Obviously

\frac{r^{\frac{\lambda}{p}-\frac{n}{q}}+\left|x\right|^{\frac{\lambda}{p}-\frac{n}{q}}}{\left|x\right|^{\frac{\beta}{q}}}\lesssim\frac{r^{\frac{\lambda}{p}-\frac{n}{q}}}{(\left|x\right|+r)^{\frac{\beta}{q}}},\qquad\text{when }\left|x\right|\geq 3r.

Furthermore we have

c_{\delta}\approx\delta^{\frac{\lambda}{p}-\frac{n+\beta}{q}}.

Therefore
M_{\alpha}
and
I_{\alpha}
are bounded from the vanishing
space
VL_{\Pi}^{p,\varphi}\left(\mathbb{R}^{n};w^{p}\right)
to another vanishing space
VL_{\Pi}^{q,\psi}\left(\mathbb{R}^{n};w^{q}\right).
