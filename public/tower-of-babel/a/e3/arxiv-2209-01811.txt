Bounds on torsion of CM abelian varieties over a p p -adic field with values in a field of p p -power roots
Yoshiyasu Ozeki

Abstract
Let p p be a prime number and M M the extension field of a p p -adic field K K obtained by adjoining all p p -power roots of all elements of K K . In this paper, we show that there exists a constant C C , depending only on K K and an integer g > 0 g>0 , which satisfies the following property: If A / K A_{/K} is a g g -dimensional CM abelian variety, then the order of the torsion subgroup of A ⁡ ( M ) A(M) is bounded by C C .
