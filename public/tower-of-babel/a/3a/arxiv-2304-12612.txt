Bose-Einstein condensations of magnons in quantum magnets with spin-orbit coupling in a Zeeman field
Fadi Sun 1,2 and Jinwu Ye 1,2,3

Abstract
We study the response of a quantum magnet with spin-orbit coupling (SOC) to a Zeeman field by constructing effective actions and performing Renormalization Group (RG) analysis. There are several novel classes of quantum phase transitions at a low h c ​ 1 h_{c1} and an upper critical field h c ​ 2 h_{c2} driven by magnon condensations at commensurate (C-) or in-commensurate (IC-) momenta 0 < k 0 < π 0<k_{0}<\pi . The intermediate IC- Skyrmion crystal (IC-SkX) phase is controlled by a line of fixed points in the RG flows labeled by k 0 k_{0} . We derive the relations between the quantum spin and the order parameters of the effective actions which determine the spin-orbital structures of the IC-SkX phase. We also analyze the operator contents near h c ​ 1 h_{c1} and h c ​ 2 h_{c2} which determine the exotic excitation spectra inside the IC-SkX. The intrinsic differences between the magnon condensations at the C- and IC- momenta are explored. The two critical fields h c ​ 1 < h c ​ 2 h_{c1}<h_{c2} and the intermediate IC-SkX phase could be a generic feature to any quantum magnets with SOC in a Zeeman field. Experimental implications to some materials or cold atom systems with SOC in a Zeeman field are presented.
