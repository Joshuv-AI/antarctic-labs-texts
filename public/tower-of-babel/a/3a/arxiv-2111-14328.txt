Key Varieties for Prime ℚ \mathbb{Q} -Fano Threefolds Related with ℙ 2 × ℙ 2 \mathbb{P}^{2}\times\mathbb{P}^{2} -Fibrations. Part II
Hiromichi Takagi

Abstract
We construct a 15 15 -dimensional affine variety Π 𝔸 15 \Pi_{\mathbb{A}}^{15} with a GL 2 {\rm GL}_{2} - and ( ℂ ∗ ) 4 (\mathbb{C}^{*})^{4} -actions. We denote by Π 𝔸 14 \Pi_{\mathbb{A}}^{14} the affine variety obtained from Π 𝔸 15 \Pi_{\mathbb{A}}^{15} by setting one specified variable to 1 1 (we refer the precise definition to Definition 1.1 of the paper). Let Π ℙ 13 \Pi_{\mathbb{P}}^{13} be several weighted projectivizations of Π 𝔸 14 \Pi_{\mathbb{A}}^{14} , and Π ℙ 14 \Pi_{\mathbb{P}}^{14} the weighted cone over Π ℙ 13 \Pi_{\mathbb{P}}^{13} with a weight one coordinate added. We show that Π ℙ 13 \Pi_{\mathbb{P}}^{13} or Π ℙ 14 \Pi_{\mathbb{P}}^{14} produce, as weighted complete intersections, examples of prime ℚ \mathbb{Q} -Fano threefolds of codimension four belonging to the eight classes No.308, 501, 512, 550, 577, 872, 878, and 1766 of the graded ring database [ GRDB ] . The construction of Π 𝔸 15 \Pi_{\mathbb{A}}^{15} is based on a certain type of unprojection and is inspired by R.Taylor’s thesis [ Tay ] submitted to University of Warwick. We also show that a partial projectivization of Π 𝔸 15 \Pi_{\mathbb{A}}^{15} has a ℙ 2 × ℙ 2 \mathbb{P}^{2}\times\mathbb{P}^{2} -fibration over the affine space 𝔸 10 \mathbb{A}^{10} . To show this, we introduce another 13 13 -dimensional affine variety H 𝔸 13 H_{\mathbb{A}}^{13} whose product with an open subset of 𝔸 2 \mathbb{A}^{2} is isomorphic to a sextic cover of an open subset of Π 𝔸 15 \Pi_{\mathbb{A}}^{15} .

The aim of this paper is twofold; the construction of
\mathbb{Q}
-Fano
threefolds as in Theorem
1.2
, and the description of the
key variety
\Pi_{\mathbb{A}}^{15}
.

After obtaining preliminary results in the section
2
,
we show in the section
3
that the affine coordinate
ring of
\Pi_{\mathbb{A}}^{15}
is a domain (Proposition
3.1
)
and is Gorenstein (Proposition
3.3
) using the theory of
unprojection and following the papers
[
P1
]
and
[
Tay
]
.
In the sections
4
and
5
,
we describe a group action on
\Pi_{\mathbb{A}}^{15}
and determine
the singular locus of
\Pi_{\mathbb{A}}^{15}
, respectively. In the
section
6
, we show that the affine coordinate
ring of
\Pi_{\mathbb{A}}^{15}
is factorial (Proposition
6.3
),
and as an application of this, we describe the divisor class groups
of weighted projectivizations of
\Pi_{\mathbb{A}}^{15}
and weighted
compelete intersections in them (Proposition
6.5
). In
the section
7
, we calculate the graded
free resolution of the affine coordinate ring of
\Pi_{\mathbb{A}}^{15}

(Proposition
7.1
) and observe that it is compatible with
the data given in
[
GRDB
]
(Corollaries
7.2

and
7.3
). In the section
8
,
we show Theorem
1.2
. To investigate properties of
\Pi_{\mathbb{A}}^{15}

more, we introduce and describe another affine variety
H_{\mathbb{A}}^{13}

in the section
9
. In the section
10
,
we describe a
\mathbb{P}^{2}\times\mathbb{P}^{2}
-fibration structure
of a partial projectivization of
\Pi_{\mathbb{A}}^{15}
(Proposition

10.1
) and detailed singularities of
\Pi_{\mathbb{A}}^{15}

(Proposition
10.2
).

Lemma 3.2
