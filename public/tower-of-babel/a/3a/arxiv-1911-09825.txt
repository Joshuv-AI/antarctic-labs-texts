Virtual cycles of stable (quasi-)maps with fields
Qile Chen and Felix Janda and Rachel Webb

Abstract
. We generalize the results of Chang–Li, Kim–Oh and Chang–Li on the moduli of p p -fields to the setting of (quasi-)maps to complete intersections in arbitrary smooth Deligne–Mumford stacks with projective coarse moduli. In particular, we show that the virtual cycle of stable (quasi-)maps to a complete intersection can be recovered by the cosection localized virtual cycle of the moduli of p p -fields of the ambient space.

Given the success of the

Main definition and result

We now state precise definitions and results.

Contents of the paper

is a regular section with zero locus

. Its restriction

is canonically identified with the tautological section of
\mathscr{E}|_{N_{X/Y}}\simeq\mathscr{E}|_{E|_{X}}
.

Proof.

The deformation
\mathcal{Y}
is constructed smooth locally, so we may assume that

is an affine scheme and

splits. Let

be given by elements
(a_{1},\ldots,a_{r})\in A
; by Definition
2.1.1
this sequence is regular. By

,

is equal to

where

is the graded ring

S^{\bullet}_{(T)}=\ldots\oplus I^{2}T^{-2}\oplus IT^{-1}\oplus A\oplus AT\oplus AT^{2}\oplus\ldots.

The ideal of

is generated by

in degree 1. So while

is given by the sequence

Restricting to the fiber

and our section is the sequence

in degree 1 (

Let

be the vector bundle on

whose sheaf of sections is

or the critical locus of

Lemma 2.2.2
.

Suppose

is a smooth Deligne–Mumford stack and

is a vector bundle on

s

. Let

be the function induced by the dual of

equals

.

Proof.

Both sides can be checked étale locally, and we may therefore
assume that
Y
is a smooth scheme, and that

is trivial of
rank

on

If we write

then the function

(y,p_{1},\dotsc,p_{r})\mapsto\sum_{i=1}^{r}p_{i}\cdot s_{i}(y),

Let

be the critical locus of

The other inclusion holds if and only if for every closed
