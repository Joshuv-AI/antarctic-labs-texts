Non-Weight modules over affine Nappi-Witten Lie algebras
Priyanshu Chakraborty, Santanu Tantubay

Abstract
. In this paper, we study the representation theory of affine Nappi-Witten Lie algebra H 4 ^ \widehat{H_{4}} corresponding to the Nappi-Witten Lie algebra H 4 H_{4} . We completely classify all Cartan-free modules of rank one for the Nappi-Witten Lie algebra H 4 H_{4} . With the help of Cartan free H 4 H_{4} modules we classify all Cartan-free modules of rank one over affine Nappi Witten Lie algebra. We also give a necessary and sufficient condition for these modules to be irreducible. Finally as an application we classify Cartan free modules of rank one for affine-Virasoro Nappi-Witten Lie algebras.

1.
Introduction

There are numerous uses for two-dimensional conformal field theories (CFTs) in mathematics and physics. A significant category of CFTs include Wess-Zumino-Novikov-Witten (WZNW) models
[
12
]
. Nappi and Witten showed in
[
13
]
that a WZNW model is based on a central extension of the two-dimensional Euclidean group that describes the homogeneous four-dimensional space-time corresponding to a gravitational plane wave. The corresponding Lie algebra
H_{4}
is known as Nappi Witten Lie algebra. This Lie algebra is neither abelian nor semisimple. Nappi-Witten Lie algebra has non-degenerate symmetric bilinear form which is
H_{4}
invariant, so like affine Kac-Moody Lie algebra, one can affinize Nappi-Witten Lie algebra. The central extension of loop of
H_{4}
is known as affine Nappi-Witten Lie algebra. Now one naturally add the Virasoro Lie algebra with affine Nappi-Witten Lie algebra and construct the afiine-Virasoro Nappi-Witten Lie algebra.

The study of the representation theory of the
Nappi-witten Lie algebra
H_{4}
was started in
[
17
]
. The representation theory of affine Nappi-witten Lie algebra
\widehat{H}_{4}
was studied by
[
20
]
. Later a systematic study of representations of
\widehat{H}_{4}
was initiated in
[
14
]
, where the authors studied the structures of generalized Verma modules and gave vertex
operator algebra constructions. In
[
19
]
, the authors classified the simple restricted modules for the affine Nappi-Witten algebra. The category of weight modules with
finite dimensional weight spaces over the Nappi-Witten vertex operator algebra was studied in
[
18
]
,
here the simple modules were classified and the characters of these simple mod
ules were computed. The imaginary Verma modules and irreducible weight modules with finite weight spaces for the twisted affine Nappi-witten Lie algebra were studied in
[
15
]
and
[
16
]
respectively.

Non-weight simple modules for any Lie algebras are less understood. A class of non-weight modules for finite-dimensional simple Lie algebra of type
A_{l}
is studied by J. Nilsson in
[
5
]
, which are known as Cartan-free modules. In
[
6
]
, the author showed that Cartan-free modules exist only for type
A_{l}(l\geq 1)
and type
C_{l}(l\geq 2)
. For Witt algebra such classification was done in
[
7
]
. For other classes of Lie algebras, towards classification of Cartan free modules one can see
[
1
,
8
,
7
,
9
,
3
]
. It should mention that in
[
2
]
authors studied Cartan free module affine Kac-Moody Lie algebras, which encourage us to study this type of modules for affine Nappi-Witten Lie algebras. Recently non-weight modules for affine-Virasoro Nappi-Witten Lie algebras has been studied in
[
11
]
.

In the present paper, we study representation of affine Nappi-Witten Lie algebra. In Section
2
, we recall definitions of Nappi-Witten Lie algebra, affine Nappi-Witten Lie algebra as well as Nappi-Witten type Virasoro algebra. In Section
3
, we study Cartan-free representation of Nappi-Witten Lie algebras. We classify all possible Cartan-free modules for
H_{4}
and find out the irreducibility condition of this type of modules. In Section
4
, we consider Cartan-free modules over affine Nappi-WItten Lie algebras and find the conditions of irreduciblity of these modules. We show that most of all such modules are coming from Cartan-free modules of Nappi-Witten Lie algebras. Finally as an application of our results we recover the Cartan free modules for affine-Virasoro Nappi-Witten Lie algebras which was obtained in
[
11
]
.

2.
Notations and Preliminaries

Throughout this paper,
\mathbb{Z}
,
\mathbb{C}
, and
\mathbb{C}^{*}
denote the sets of integers, complex numbers, and nonzero complex numbers, respectively. For a Lie algebra
\mathfrak{a}
, we denote the universal enveloping algebra of

as

. All the vector spaces, algebras, and tensor products are over
\mathbb{C}
, unless it is specified.

2.1.
Nappi-Witten Lie algebras

The Nappi-Witten Lie algebra
H_{4}
is a four-dimensional vector space

H_{4}=\mathbb{C}p\oplus\mathbb{C}q\oplus\mathbb{C}r\oplus\mathbb{C}s

together with the Lie brackets

[p,q]=r,\ [s,p]=p,\;[s,q]=-q,[r,.]=0.

Let
\mathfrak{h}=\mathbb{C}s
be a Cartan subalgebra of
H_{4}
. In the next Section, we will describe a class of modules for
H_{4}
with respect to this Cartan subalgebra.

Let
(,)
be a symmetric bilinear form on
H_{4}
defined by

(p,q)=1,\;(r,s)=1,\;\text{otherwise},(\;,\;)=0.

It is easy to see that
(\;,\;)
is a non-degenerate
H_{4}
-invariant symmetric bilinear form on
H_{4}
.

2.2.
Affine Nappi-Witten Lie algebras.

Let
\mathbb{C}[t^{\pm 1}]
be the Laurent polynomial ring over
\mathbb{C}
. Now we consider the space

\widehat{H_{4}}=H_{4}\otimes\mathbb{C}[t^{\pm 1}]\oplus\mathbb{C}\mathbf{k}\oplus\mathbb{C}d

with the Lie brackets

[h_{1}\otimes t^{m},h_{2}\otimes t^{n}]=[h_{1},h_{2}]\otimes t^{m+n}+m(h_{1},h_{2})\delta_{m+n,0}\mathbf{k},\;[\widehat{H_{4}},\mathbf{k}]=0,\;[d,h_{1}\otimes t^{m}]=mh_{1}\otimes t^{m},

where
h_{1},h_{2}\in H_{4},\;m,n\in\mathbb{Z}
.

Throughout the paper, we will denote the element
h_{1}\otimes t^{n}
by
h_{1}(n)
.

2.3.
Virasoro Lie algebras

By definition, the Virasoro algebra
Vir:=\mathbb{C}\{d_{m},\mathbf{k}:m\in\mathbb{Z}\}
, with the brackets

[d_{m},d_{n}]=(n-m)d_{m+n}+\delta_{m+n,0}\frac{m^{3}-m}{12}\mathbf{k},\;[d_{m},\mathbf{k}=0],

for all
m,n\in\mathbb{Z}
.

2.4.
Affine-Virasoro Nappi-Witten Lie algebras

We define the affine-Virasoro Nappi-Witten algebra as follows:

The underlying vector space is given by

\overline{H_{4}}=H_{4}\otimes\mathbb{C}[t^{\pm 1}]\oplus\mathbb{C}\mathbf{k}\;\displaystyle{\bigoplus_{m\in\mathbb{Z}}}\mathbb{C}d_{m},

where the Lie brackets are given by:

[h_{1}\otimes t^{m},h_{2}\otimes t^{n}]=[h_{1},h_{2}]\otimes t^{m+n}+m(h_{1},h_{2})\delta_{m+n,0}\mathbf{k},\;[\overline{H_{4}},\mathbf{k}]=0,\;[d_{m},h_{1}\otimes t^{n}]=nh_{1}\otimes t^{m+n},

[d_{m},d_{n}]=(n-m)d_{m+n}+\delta_{m+n,0}\frac{m^{3}-m}{12}\mathbf{k}.

2.5.

Let
\mathfrak{a}
be any Lie algebra over
\mathbb{C}
and
\mathfrak{b}
be any finite-dimensional abelian subalgebra of
\mathfrak{a}
. Denote
\mathcal{M}(\mathfrak{a},\mathfrak{b})
by the full subcategory of

In this paper, we will study this subcategory for Nappi-Witten, affine Nappi-Witten, affine-Virasoro Nappi-Witten Lie algebras.
