Two non-perturbative α ′ \alpha^{\prime} corrected or loop corrected string cosmological solutions
Li Song, Deyou Chen

Abstract
In this paper, we present two non-perturbative string cosmological solutions without curvature singularities for the bosonic gravi-dilaton system. These solutions are general in the sense that they can straightforwardly match the perturbative solution to arbitrarily high orders in the perturbative region. The first solution includes non-perturbative α ′ \alpha^{\prime} corrections based on Hohm-Zwiebach action. We then use the simple phenomenological map between α ′ \alpha^{\prime} corrected theory and loop corrected theory in string cosmology to construct a non-perturbative loop corrected non-singular solution. Both solutions are non-singular everywhere. Therefore, the pre- and post-big-bangs are smoothly connected by these solutions.



I
Introduction

Resolving the big-bang singularity is a widely concerned problem.
In Einstein’s general theory of gravity, spacetime is generated from
the big-bang. On the other hand, in string cosmology, the story is
quite different due to the famous scale-factor duality
Veneziano:1991ek
;
Sen:1991zi
;
Tseytlin:1991wr
;
Tseytlin:1991xk
.
The scale-factor duality had been firstly obtained from the equations
of motion (EOM) of the string’s low energy gravi-dilaton effective
action. It shows that the EOM with the FLRW-like ansatz is invariant
under the transformations between the scale factor and its inversion,
namely
a\left(t\right)\leftrightarrow 1/a\left(t\right)
. Keep
in mind that the string dilaton plays a central role in this duality.
The main differences between T-duality and scale-factor duality are
that the scale-factor duality does not require the background compactified,
and it belongs to a continuous group of
O\left(d,d\right)
. Remarkably,
the scale-factor duality introduces a pre-big-bang cosmology
Gasperini:1996fu
;
Veneziano:2000pz
;
Gasperini:2002bn
;
Gasperini:2007vw
;
Gasperini:1992em
.
It implies that the story of our universe was not only born from the
initial big-bang singularity, but there exists a long evolution in
a region of pre-big-bang. Pre- and post- big-bang scenarios are disconnected
by the big-bang singularity. On the other hand, when the universe
evolves to the big-bang singularity, the growth of the string coupling

g_{s}=\exp\left(2\phi\right)
and the Hubble parameter
H\left(t\right)

invalidate the perturbative theory. The theory of quantum gravity
around this region requires the low energy effective action to include
two kinds of corrections: (1) the string curvature
scale, which includes higher-derivative
\alpha^{\prime}
corrections,
(2) the strong coupling regime, which requires the quantum loop corrections.

In literature, some phenomenological higher loop models

Gasperini:1992em
;
Gasperini:2003pb
;
Gasperini:2004ss

were proposed to resolve the big-bang singularity. As for the higher-derivative

\alpha^{\prime}
corrections, at the cost of losing the scale-factor
duality or the
O(d,d)
symmetry, some simplified models were proposed
Gasperini:1996fu
;
Easson:2003ia

to smoothly connect the Pre- and post- big-bang scenarios. One may
wonder if we can obtain a nature global picture of the non-singular
universe when the action includes the
\alpha^{\prime}
corrections
to all orders.

To answer this question, let us focus on the recent developments of
non-perturbative string cosmology to all orders in
\alpha^{\prime}
.
In refs.
Meissner:1991zj
;
Meissner:1996sa
, in addition
to manifesting the scale-factor duality in the EOM, Meissner and Veneziano
found that when the massless closed string fields only depending on
time, the low energy effective action of closed string with the zeroth
and the first orders in
\alpha^{\prime}
could be simplified and
rewritten in an
O\left(d,d\right)
invariant form. After that, Sen
proved that this result can be extended to all orders in
\alpha^{\prime}

corrections of full string field theory
Sen:1991zi
;
Sen:1991cn
.
In other words, the closed string spacetime action can be rewritten
into the
O\left(d,d\right)
covariant form to all orders in
\alpha^{\prime}

without imposing any extra constraint or symmetry. Based on these
works, Hohm and Zwiebach
Hohm:2015doa
;
Hohm:2019ccp
;
Hohm:2019jgu

demonstrated that the
O\left(d,d\right)
covariant spacetime action
can be dramatically simplified into a simple form. This result in
turn provides possible non-perturbative dS or AdS vacua to the bosonic
string theory
Hohm:2019ccp
;
Wang:2019mwi
;
Krishnan:2019mkv
;
Nunez:2020hxx
;
Bieniek:2023ubx
.
Moreover, the Hohm-Zwiebach action makes it possible to analyze the
stringy effects on the cosmological singularity and black hole singularity
seriously. In ref.
Wang:2019kez
, a set of non-singular non-perturbative
string cosmological solutions is constructed for the first time. This
set of solutions is subsequently extended to more general solutions
which can match the perturbative solution to arbitrary order in
\alpha
’
expansion
Wang:2019dcj
. More recent developments on the smoothing
cosmological and black hole singularities refer to refs.

Ying:2021xse
;
Ying:2022xaj
;
Klinkhamer:2019ocj
;
Bernardo:2019bkz
;
Bernardo:2020zlc
;
Quintin:2021eup
;
Codina:2021cxh
;
Ying:2022cix
;
Codina:2023fhy
;
Gasperini:2023tus
.

The purpose of this paper is to give non-singular non-perturbative solutions with
\alpha^{\prime}
corrections or
loop corrections. We first construct an
\alpha^{\prime}
corrected solution based on Hohm-Zwiebach action.
The solution we present is non-perturbative in the sense that it covers the whole region from the pre-big-bang to the post-big-bang and non-singular everywhere. In the perturbative region
t\to\infty
, this solution matches the perturbative solution to
arbitrary order in
\alpha^{\prime}
. So, though we currently only know the first two orders in
\alpha^{\prime}
corrections, once higher orders are calculated in the future, our solution can straightforwardly match them by fixing the parameters.
Our solution is parallel to these given in
Wang:2019dcj
.
In ref.
Wang:2019dcj
,
a phenomenological map between the
\alpha^{\prime}
corrected theory and loop corrected theory are found. With the help of this map, we then construct a non-perturbative loop corrected solution, which is non-singular everywhere and includes higher loop contributions.
So, both the
\alpha^{\prime}
corrected or the loop corrected solution we are going to construct resolve the big-bang singularity and connects the pre-big-bang
and post-big-bang scenarios smoothly.

The reminder of this paper is outlined as follows. We give a non-singular non-perturbative solution to any order in
\alpha^{\prime}
expansion in section 2. In section 3, we present a loop corrected non-singular non-perturbative solution. Section 4 is the conclusion.

II
The general
\alpha^{\prime}
corrected solution

Since we are going to find both
\alpha^{\prime}
corrected solutions and loop corrected solutions, we write the full perturbative structure of the closed string effective action,

\displaystyle I

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int\>d^{d+1}x\sqrt{-g}\bigg\{e^{-2\phi}\Big[(R+4(\partial\phi)^{2}-\frac{1}{12}{\cal H}^{2})+\frac{\text{$\alpha^{\prime}$}}{4}(R_{\mu\nu\sigma\rho}R^{\mu\nu\sigma\rho}+\cdots)+{\cal O}(\alpha^{\prime 2})\big]

(1)

\displaystyle+

\displaystyle\Big[(c_{R}^{1}R+c_{\phi}^{1}(\partial\phi)^{2}+c_{{\cal H}}^{1}{\cal H}^{2})+\alpha^{\prime}(c_{\alpha^{\prime}R}^{1}R_{\mu\nu\sigma\rho}R^{\mu\nu\sigma\rho}+\cdots)+{\cal O}(\alpha^{\prime 2})\Big]

\displaystyle+

\displaystyle e^{2\phi}\Big[(c_{R}^{2}R+c_{\phi}^{2}(\partial\phi)^{2}+c_{{\cal H}}^{2}{\cal H}^{2})+\alpha^{\prime}(c_{\alpha^{\prime}R}^{2}R_{\mu\nu\sigma\rho}R^{\mu\nu\sigma\rho}+\cdots)+{\cal O}(\alpha^{\prime 2})\Big]

\displaystyle+

\displaystyle\cdots\bigg\}.

This action contains three massless fields, the metric
g_{\mu\nu}

are not known until now. We first consider the
\alpha^{\prime}
corrected solutions. To this end, we focus on the loop tree level, i.e. the first line of the above action. In the FLRW background,

ds^{2}=-dt^{2}+a^{2}\left(t\right)\delta_{ij}dx^{i}dx^{j}.

(2)

the loop tree level action with all orders in
\alpha^{\prime}

Hohm:2019ccp
;
Hohm:2019jgu

\displaystyle I_{\alpha^{\prime}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int d^{D}x\sqrt{-g}e^{-2\phi}\left(R+4\left(\partial\phi\right)^{2}+\frac{1}{4}\alpha^{\prime}\left(R^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}R_{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}+\ldots\right)+\alpha^{\prime 2}(\ldots)+\ldots\right),

(3)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int dte^{-\Phi}\left(-\dot{\Phi}^{2}+\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\left(\alpha^{\prime}\right)^{k-1}c_{k}\mathrm{tr}\left(\dot{\mathcal{S}}^{2k}\right)\right),

(4)

The notation
\mathcal{S}
is defined as

\mathcal{S}=\left(\begin{array}[]{cc}0&a^{2}\left(t\right)\\
a^{-2}\left(t\right)&0\end{array}\right).

(5)

In the action (
4
), we can
only determine the coefficients
c_{1}=-\frac{1}{8}
and
c_{2}=\frac{1}{64}

for the bosonic string theory through the one-loop and two-loops beta

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{\dot{a}\left(t\right)}{a\left(t\right)},

\displaystyle f\left(H\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle d\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\left(-\alpha^{\prime}\right)^{k-1}2^{2\left(k+1\right)}kc_{k}H^{2k-1}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-2dH-2d\alpha^{\prime}H^{3}+\mathcal{O}\left(\alpha^{\prime 3}\right),

\displaystyle g\left(H\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle d\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\left(-\alpha^{\prime}\right)^{k-1}2^{2k+1}\left(2k-1\right)c_{k}H^{2k}

(6)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-dH^{2}-\frac{3}{2}d\alpha^{\prime}H^{4}+\mathcal{O}\left(\alpha^{\prime}\right),

where
H\left(t\right)
is the Hubble parameter. Note that

g^{\prime}(H)=Hf^{\prime}(H),\quad{\rm and}\quad g(H)=Hf(H)-\int_{0}^{H}f(x)dx,

where
f^{\prime}(H)\equiv\frac{d}{dH}f(H)
. The action (
4
)
is simplified to the Hohm-Zwiebach action

\displaystyle I_{HZ}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int dte^{-\Phi}\left(-\dot{\Phi}^{2}+g(H)-Hf(H)\right),

(7)

After variation, the EOM of Hohm-Zwiebach action (
7
) is

\displaystyle\ddot{\Phi}+\frac{1}{2}Hf\left(H\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 0,

\displaystyle\frac{d}{dt}\left(e^{-\Phi}f\left(H\right)\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 0,

\displaystyle\dot{\Phi}^{2}+g\left(H\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 0.

In the perturbative region

\displaystyle H\left(t\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{\alpha^{\prime}}}\left[\frac{t_{0}}{t}-160c_{2}\frac{t_{0}^{3}}{t^{3}}+\frac{256\left(770c_{2}^{2}+19c_{3}\right)}{3}\frac{t_{0}^{5}}{t^{5}}\right.

\displaystyle\left.-\frac{2048\left(88232c_{2}^{3}+4644c_{3}c_{2}+41c_{4}\right)}{5}\frac{t_{0}^{7}}{t^{7}}+\mathcal{O}\left(\frac{t_{0}^{9}}{t^{9}}\right)\right],

\displaystyle\Phi\left(t\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\frac{1}{2}\log\left(\beta^{2}\frac{t^{2}}{t_{0}^{2}}\right)-32c_{2}\frac{t_{0}^{2}}{t^{2}}+\frac{256\left(44c_{2}^{2}+c_{3}\right)}{3}\frac{t_{0}^{4}}{t^{4}}

(9)

\displaystyle-\frac{2048\left(6976c_{2}^{3}+352c_{3}c_{2}+3c_{4}\right)}{15}\frac{t_{0}^{6}}{t^{6}}+\mathcal{O}\left(\frac{t_{0}^{8}}{t^{8}}\right),

with

\displaystyle f\left(H\left(t\right)\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-2dH-128c_{2}d\alpha^{\prime}H^{3}+768c_{3}d\alpha^{\prime 2}H^{5}-4096c_{4}d\alpha^{\prime 3}H^{7}+\mathcal{O}\left(\alpha^{\prime 4}H^{9}\right),

\displaystyle\frac{\sqrt{d}}{t_{0}}\left[-\frac{2t_{0}}{t}+64c_{2}\frac{t_{0}^{3}}{t^{3}}-\frac{512\left(50c_{2}^{2}+c_{3}\right)}{3}\frac{t_{0}^{5}}{t^{5}}\right.

\displaystyle g\left(H\left(t\right)\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-dH^{2}-96c_{2}d\alpha^{\prime}H^{4}+640c_{3}d\alpha^{\prime 2}H^{6}-3584c_{4}d\alpha^{\prime 3}H^{8}+\mathcal{O}\left(\alpha^{\prime 4}H^{10}\right),

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{t_{0}^{2}}\left[-\frac{t_{0}^{2}}{t^{2}}+128c_{2}\frac{t_{0}^{4}}{t^{4}}-\frac{2048\left(50c_{2}^{2}+c_{3}\right)}{3}\frac{t_{0}^{6}}{t^{6}}\right.

\displaystyle\left.+\frac{8192\left(24448c_{2}^{3}+1136c_{3}c_{2}+9c_{4}\right)}{15}\frac{t_{0}^{8}}{t^{8}}+\mathcal{O}\left(\frac{t_{0}^{10}}{t^{10}}\right)\right],

where
\beta
is an integration constant,
t_{0}\equiv\frac{\sqrt{\alpha^{\prime}}}{\sqrt{2d}}

One is ready to see that this solution is singular at the non-perturbative region

From the scale factor duality,
H(t)\to-H(t)
,

,

A non-perturbative non-singular solution of the EOM (
8

\frac{\sqrt{\alpha^{\prime}}}{t}\to 0

\frac{\sqrt{\alpha^{\prime}}}{t}

are still unknown, a good solution only needs to match the first two orders in
\alpha^{\prime}

Wang:2019kez

c_{k\leq n}

n>2

is expressed in terms of

Wang:2019dcj

In this work, we give another simpler one. Every term in our solution is non-singular. Let us first define a dimensionless parameter

\tau\equiv\frac{t}{t_{0}}=\sqrt{\frac{2d}{\alpha^{\prime}}}t.

(10)

After some long calculation, we find a non-perturbative solution of the EOM (
8
),

\displaystyle H(t)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{\sqrt{\frac{2}{\alpha^{\prime}\beta^{2}\lambda_{0}}}\left(e^{-\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{\lambda_{k}}{\tau^{2k}+1}}\left(\left(\tau^{2}+1\right)^{2}\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\left(\frac{8k^{2}\lambda_{k}\tau^{4k-2}}{\left(\tau^{2k}+1\right)^{3}}-\frac{2k(2k-1)\lambda_{k}\tau^{2k-2}}{\left(\tau^{2k}+1\right)^{2}}\right)+\tau^{2}-1\right)\right)}{\left(\tau^{2}+1\right)^{3/2}}

(11)

\displaystyle\Phi(t)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\log\frac{\lambda_{0}}{1+\tau^{2}}+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{\lambda_{n}}{1+\tau^{2n}},

\displaystyle f(H(t))

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-2d\sqrt{\frac{2\beta^{2}\lambda_{0}}{\left(\tau^{2}+1\right)\alpha^{\prime}}}e^{\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{\lambda_{k}}{\tau^{2k}+1}}=-2dH(t)-2d\alpha^{\prime}H(t)^{3}+{\cal O}(\alpha^{\prime 2})

(13)

\displaystyle g(H(t))

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\frac{2d}{\alpha^{\prime}}\left(\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{2k\lambda_{k}\tau^{2k-1}}{\left(\tau^{2k}+1\right)^{2}}+\frac{\tau}{\tau^{2}+1}\right)^{2}=-dH(t)^{2}-\frac{3}{2}d\alpha^{\prime}H(t)^{4}+{\cal O}(\alpha^{\prime 2}).

Applying

and
c_{2}=1/64

t/\sqrt{\alpha^{\prime}}\to\infty
, to match the perturbative solution (
9

\displaystyle\lambda_{0}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 1/\beta^{2},\quad\lambda_{1}=0,\quad\lambda_{2}=\frac{11+1024c_{3}}{12},

\displaystyle\lambda_{3}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\frac{4}{15}(13+2816c_{3}+1536c_{4})...

(15)

It is obvious that every single term in the sums such as

\displaystyle H(t)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{\alpha^{\prime}}}\frac{\left(1-\tau^{2}\right)}{\left(1+\tau^{2}\right)^{3/2}},

III
The general loop corrected solution

After constructing the non-perturbative non-singular
\alpha^{\prime}
corrected solution, we want to find the corresponding loop corrected non-perturbative non-singular solution. We do not know the form and coefficients of the higher loop terms in the full perturbative action (
1
). For the FLRW background (
2
), an effective loop corrected action is sketched as

\displaystyle I_{\rm Loop}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int d^{d+1}x\sqrt{-g}e^{-2\phi}\left[R+4\left(\partial_{\mu}\phi\right)^{2}-V\left(e^{-\Phi\left(x\right)}\right)\right],

(17)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int dte^{-\Phi}\big[-\dot{\Phi}+dH^{2}-V(e^{-\Phi})\big],

where the
O(d,d)
non-local dilaton is
Gasperini:1992em
;
Gasperini:2003pb
,

e^{-\Phi\left(t\right)}=V_{d}\int dt^{\prime}\left|\frac{d\left(2\phi\right)}{dt^{\prime}}\right|\sqrt{-g\left(t^{\prime}\right)}e^{-2\phi\left(t^{\prime}\right)}\delta\left(2\phi\left(t\right)-2\phi\left(t^{\prime}\right)\right)=V_{d}\sqrt{-g\left(t\right)}e^{-2\phi\left(t\right)}.

(18)

The EOM is

\displaystyle 2\ddot{\Phi}_{L}-2dH_{L}^{2}-\frac{\partial V}{\partial\Phi_{L}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 0,

\displaystyle\dot{\Phi}_{L}^{2}-dH_{L}^{2}-V

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 0,

\displaystyle\dot{H}_{L}-H_{L}\dot{\Phi}_{L}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 0,

(19)

where the subscribe
L
indicates that the quantities belong to the loop corrected theory.
For an positive integer
n
and arbitrary parameters
m_{n}
,
\sigma_{n}
,
a class of solutions of the above EOM was constructed in refs
Gasperini:2003pb
;
Gasperini:2004ss
,

\displaystyle\Phi^{(n)}_{L}(t)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2n}\log\left(\frac{\sigma_{n}^{2n}}{1+\left(m_{n}t\right)^{2n}}\right),

(20)

\displaystyle H^{(n)}_{L}(t)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\sqrt{d}}\frac{m_{n}}{\sigma_{n}}e^{\Phi^{(n)}_{L}\left(t\right)}=\frac{m_{n}}{\sqrt{d}}\left[\frac{1}{1+\left(m_{n}t\right)^{2n}}\right]^{1/2n}.

with the potential

V^{(n)}_{L}=\left(\frac{m_{n}}{\sigma_{n}}\right)^{2}e^{2\Phi^{(n)}_{L}\left(t\right)}\left[\left(1-\sigma_{n}^{-2n}e^{2n\Phi^{(n)}_{L}\left(t\right)}\right)^{\frac{2n-1}{n}}-1\right].

(21)

As argued in refs
Gasperini:2003pb
;
Gasperini:2004ss
, since
the factor
e^{\Phi}
roughly represents the coupling constant, the integer
n
effectively represents the loop number. The potential indicates the non-perturbative contributions by the
n
th loop.

In ref.
Wang:2019dcj
, a map is found between the
\alpha^{\prime}
corrected theory and loop corrected theory,

\displaystyle H_{L}

\displaystyle\leftrightarrow

\displaystyle f\left(H_{\alpha^{\prime}}\right),

\displaystyle-V_{L}

\displaystyle\leftrightarrow

\displaystyle g\left(H_{\alpha^{\prime}}\right)+df\left(H_{\alpha^{\prime}}\right)^{2}

\displaystyle\Phi_{L}

\displaystyle\leftrightarrow

\displaystyle\Phi_{\alpha^{\prime}}+\Phi_{0}.

(22)

where
\Phi_{0}
is a constant. Through this map, we thus can find a loop corrected non-perturbative non-singular solution from our
\alpha^{\prime}
corrected solution (
14
). Since
e^{n\Phi_{L}^{(n)}}
indicates the contribution from the
n
-th loop,
we use eqn. (
III
) to express the solution in terms of
e^{\Phi_{L}^{(n)}}
. Then, after some tedious calculation, we obtain the loop corrected solution,

\displaystyle\Phi_{L}(t)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\Phi_{L}^{(1)}+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}e^{2n\Phi_{L}^{(n)}}

\displaystyle H_{L}(t)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{m_{1}}{\sigma_{1}}\exp\left[\Phi_{L}^{(1)}+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}e^{2n\Phi_{L}^{(n)}}\right]

\displaystyle V_{L}(\Phi_{L}^{(n)}(t))

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left(\dot{\Phi}_{L}^{(1)}+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}2n\dot{\Phi}_{L}^{(n)}e^{2n\Phi_{L}^{(n)}}\right)^{2}-\frac{dm_{1}^{2}}{\sigma_{1}^{2}}\exp\left[2\Phi_{L}^{(1)}+2\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}e^{2n\Phi_{L}^{(n)}}\right],

(23)

where we set
m_{n}=\sqrt{2d/\alpha^{\prime}}
and
\sigma_{i}
’s are free parameters to be determined by coefficients calculated from the effective low energy action. Obviously, this solution is non-singular around the non-perturbative region
t\sim 0
.

IV
Conclusion

In this paper, based on Hohm-Zwiebach action,
we first constructed a class of general
\alpha^{\prime}
corrected non-perturbative non-singular string cosmology solutions.
Currently, only the first two orders in
\alpha^{\prime}
correction are available. Our solution matches these results in the perturbative region as required. Once higher orders in
\alpha^{\prime}
correction are available in the future, we can straightforwardly fix the corresponding parameters in our solution to match the perturbative solutions. There exists a phenomenological map between
\alpha^{\prime}
corrected theory and loop corrected theory given in ref.
Wang:2019dcj
. We used this map to constructed the corresponding loop corrected solution, which is also non-singular and non-perturbative. Since both solutions are non-singular everywhere, the pre- and post-big-bangs are thus smoothly connected by them.
