Analysis of the T c ​ s ¯ ​ ( 2900 ) T_{c\bar{s}}(2900) and related tetraquark states with the QCD sum rules


Abstract
In this research, we tentatively assign the T c ​ s ¯ ​ ( 2900 ) T_{c\bar{s}}(2900) as the A ​ A ¯ A\bar{A} -type tetraquark state, and study the mass spectrum of the tetraquark states with strange and doubly strange, which have the spin-parity J P = 0 + J^{P}=0^{+} , 1 + 1^{+} and 2 + 2^{+} , in the framework of the QCD sum rules in details, where the A A denotes the axialvector diquark state. The predicted mass M = 2.92 ± 0.12 ​ GeV M=2.92\pm 0.12\,\rm{GeV} is in consistent with the experimental values M = 2.892 ± 0.014 ± 0.015 ​ GeV M=2.892\pm 0.014\pm 0.015\,\rm{GeV} and 2.921 ± 0.017 ± 0.020 ​ GeV 2.921\pm 0.017\pm 0.020\,\rm{GeV} from the LHCb collaboration and supports assigning the T c ​ s ¯ ​ ( 2900 ) T_{c\bar{s}}(2900) to be the A ​ A ¯ A\bar{A} -type scalar c ​ s ¯ ​ q ​ q ¯ c\bar{s}q\bar{q} tetraquark state. The predictions for other tetraquark states can be confronted to the experimental data in the future to diagnose the nature of the fully open flavor exotic states.
