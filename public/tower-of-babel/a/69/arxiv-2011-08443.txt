1.
Introduction

In this section, we introduce the notations and necessary prerequisites.
Let
\mathbb{B}\left(\mathscr{H}\right)
denote the
{{C}^{*}}
-algebra of all bounded linear operators on a complex Hilbert space
\mathscr{H}
with inner product
\left\langle\cdot,\cdot\right\rangle
. For
A\in\mathbb{B}\left(\mathscr{H}\right)
, let
\omega\left(A\right)
and
\left\|A\right\|
denote the numerical radius and the operator norm of
A
, respectively. Recall that
\omega\left(A\right)=\underset{\left\|x\right\|=1}{\mathop{\underset{x\in\mathscr{H}}{\mathop{\sup}}\,}}\,\left|\left\langle Ax,x\right\rangle\right|
and
\left\|A\right\|=\underset{\left\|x\right\|=1}{\mathop{\underset{x\in\mathscr{H}}{\mathop{\sup}}\,}}\,\left\|Ax\right\|
. We remark here that if
A\in\mathbb{B}\left(\mathscr{H}\right)
, and if
f
is a non-negative increasing function on
\left[0,\infty\right)
,
then
\left\|f\left(\left|A\right|\right)\right\|=f\left(\left\|A\right\|\right)
. Here
\left|A\right|
stands for the positive operator

{{\left({{A}^{*}}A\right)}^{\frac{1}{2}}}
.

It is clear that
\omega\left(\cdot\right)
defines a norm
on
\mathbb{B}\left(\mathscr{H}\right)
, which is equivalent to the operator norm
\left\|\cdot\right\|
. In fact, for every
A\in\mathbb{B}\left(\mathscr{H}\right)
,

(1.1)

\frac{1}{2}\left\|A\right\|\leq\omega\left(A\right)\leq\left\|A\right\|.

The inequalities in (
1.1
) are sharp. The first inequality becomes an equality if
{{A}^{2}}=0
. The second inequality becomes an equality if
A
is normal.

An important inequality for
\omega\left(A\right)
is the power inequality
[
1
]
stating that

(1.2)

\omega\left({{A}^{n}}\right)\leq{{\omega}^{n}}\left(A\right),

for
n=1,2,\ldots
.

In
[
6
]
, Kittaneh improved the second inequality in (
1.1
), and obtained the following result:

(1.3)

\omega\left(A\right)\leq\frac{1}{2}\left(\left\|A\right\|+{{\left\|{{A}^{2}}\right\|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right).

He also showed the following estimate, which is stronger than (
1.3
),

(1.4)

\omega\left(A\right)\leq\frac{1}{2}\left\|\left|A\right|+\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\right\|.

Another refinement of the second inequality in (
1.1
) has been established in
[
8
]
. This refinement asserts that if
A\in\mathbb{B}\left(\mathscr{H}\right)
, then

(1.5)

{{\omega}^{2}}\left(A\right)\leq\frac{1}{2}\left\|{{\left|A\right|}^{2}}+{{\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|}^{2}}\right\|.

Also, in the same paper, the author proved that

(1.6)

\frac{1}{4}\left\|{{\left|A\right|}^{2}}+{{\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|}^{2}}\right\|\leq{{\omega}^{2}}\left(A\right).

It can be easily seen that (
1.6
) improves the first inequality in (
1.1
). It should be mentioned here that upper bounds obtained in (
1.3
) and (
1.5
) are not comparable.

Nowadays, a considerable attention is dedicated to refinement and generalization of the previous discussion
[
9
,
10
,
11
,
12
,
13
]
.

Motivated by the results outlined above, it is the primary goal of the present work to establish new numerical radius inequalities for Hilbert space operators. A comparison of the established results with previously obtained results is demonstrated to show that the results presented in this paper are better than those already exist in literature.

In Section
2
, we introduce an inequality that refines both inequalities (
1.3
) and (
1.5
). Furthermore, we make a refinement of the inequality (
1.6
).

To achieve our goal, we need the following lemmas. The first lemma presented by Kittaneh in
[
5
, (19)]
, while the second lemma is given in
[
7
]
.

Lemma 1.1
.

Let
A,B\in\mathbb{B}\left(\mathscr{H}\right)
be positive operators, then

\left\|A+B\right\|\leq\frac{1}{2}\left(\left\|A\right\|+\left\|B\right\|+\sqrt{{{\left(\left\|A\right\|-\left\|B\right\|\right)}^{2}}+4{{\left\|{{A}^{\frac{1}{2}}}{{B}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right\|}^{2}}}\right).

In particular,

\left\|{{\left|A\right|}^{2}}+{{\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|}^{2}}\right\|\leq\left\|{{A}^{2}}\right\|+{{\left\|A\right\|}^{2}},

for any
A\in\mathbb{B}\left(\mathscr{H}\right)
.

Lemma 1.2
.

Let
A\in\mathbb{B}\left(\mathscr{H}\right)
and let
x,y\in\mathscr{H}
be any vector. If
f,g
are nonnegative continuous functions on
\left[0,\infty\right)
satisfying
f\left(t\right)g\left(t\right)=t,\left(t\geq 0\right)
, then

\left|\left\langle Ax,y\right\rangle\right|\leq\left\|f\left(\left|A\right|\right)x\right\|\left\|g\left(\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\right)y\right\|.

In particular,

\left|\left\langle Ax,y\right\rangle\right|\leq\sqrt{\left\langle{{\left|A\right|}^{2\left(1-v\right)}}x,x\right\rangle\left\langle{{\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|}^{2v}}y,y\right\rangle},\text{ }\left(0\leq v\leq 1\right).

2.
Numerical Radius Inequalities

Using the same strategy as in
[
3
, Theorem 1]
, we get the first result, a refinement of inequality (
1.4
).

Theorem 2.1
.

Let
A\in\mathbb{B}\left(\mathscr{H}\right)
, then

(2.1)

\omega\left(A\right)\leq\frac{1}{2}~\underset{0\leq v\leq 1}{\mathop{\min}}\,\left\|{{\left|A\right|}^{2\left(1-v\right)}}+{{\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|}^{2v}}\right\|.

Proof.

Let
x\in\mathscr{H}
be a unit vector. By employing Lemma
1.2
and the arithmetic-geometric mean inequality, we have

\displaystyle\left|\left\langle Ax,x\right\rangle\right|

\displaystyle\leq\sqrt{\left\langle{{\left|A\right|}^{2\left(1-v\right)}}x,x\right\rangle\left\langle{{\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|}^{2v}}x,x\right\rangle}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{2}\left(\left\langle{{\left|A\right|}^{2\left(1-v\right)}}x,x\right\rangle+\left\langle{{\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|}^{2v}}x,x\right\rangle\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2}\left\langle\left({{\left|A\right|}^{2\left(1-v\right)}}+{{\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|}^{2v}}\right)x,x\right\rangle

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{2}\left\|{{\left|A\right|}^{2\left(1-v\right)}}+{{\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|}^{2v}}\right\|.

Thus,

\left|\left\langle Ax,x\right\rangle\right|\leq\frac{1}{2}\left\|{{\left|A\right|}^{2\left(1-v\right)}}+{{\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|}^{2v}}\right\|.

Now, taking the supremum over
x\in\mathscr{H}
with
\left\|x\right\|=1
in the above inequality produces

(2.2)

\omega\left(A\right)\leq\frac{1}{2}\left\|{{\left|A\right|}^{2\left(1-v\right)}}+{{\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|}^{2v}}\right\|.

Taking minimum over all
v\in\left[0,1\right]
, we get

\omega\left(A\right)\leq\frac{1}{2}~\underset{0\leq v\leq 1}{\mathop{\min}}\,\left\|{{\left|A\right|}^{2\left(1-v\right)}}+{{\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|}^{2v}}\right\|,

as required.
∎

The following example shows that inequality (
2.1
) is a nontrivial improvement of inequality (
1.4
). It shows that

\frac{1}{2}~\underset{0\leq v\leq 1}{\mathop{\min}}\,\left\|{{\left|A\right|}^{2\left(1-v\right)}}+{{\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|}^{2v}}\right\|\lneqq\frac{1}{2}\left\|\left|A\right|+\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\right\|.

Example 2.1
.

Letting
A=\left[\begin{matrix}0&1&0\\
0&0&2\\
0&0&0\\
\end{matrix}\right]
. So,
\left|A\right|=\left[\begin{matrix}0&0&0\\
0&1&0\\
0&0&2\\
\end{matrix}\right]
and
\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|=\left[\begin{matrix}1&0&0\\
0&2&0\\
0&0&0\\
\end{matrix}\right]
. With a help of Matlab, we get

\frac{1}{2}\left\|\left|A\right|+\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\right\|=\frac{3}{2},

while

\frac{1}{2}~\underset{0\leq v\leq 1}{\mathop{\min}}\,\left\|{{\left|A\right|}^{2\left(1-v\right)}}+{{\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|}^{2v}}\right\|\approx 1.28.

Theorem
2.1
admits the following result.

Corollary 2.1
.

Let
A\in\mathbb{B}\left(\mathscr{H}\right)
and let
0\leq v\leq 1
. Then

\omega\left(A\right)\leq\frac{1}{4}\left({{\left\|A\right\|}^{2\left(1-v\right)}}+{{\left\|A\right\|}^{2v}}+\sqrt{{{\left({{\left\|A\right\|}^{2\left(1-v\right)}}-{{\left\|A\right\|}^{2v}}\right)}^{2}}+4{{\left\|{{\left|A\right|}^{1-v}}{{\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|}^{v}}\right\|}^{2}}}\right),

Proof.

Let
0\leq v\leq 1
. We have

\displaystyle\left\|{{\left|A\right|}^{2\left(1-v\right)}}+{{\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|}^{2v}}\right\|

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{2}\left(\left\|{{\left|A\right|}^{2\left(1-v\right)}}\right\|+\left\|{{\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|}^{2v}}\right\|+\sqrt{{{\left(\left\|{{\left|A\right|}^{2\left(1-v\right)}}\right\|-\left\|{{\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|}^{2v}}\right\|\right)}^{2}}+4{{\left\|{{\left|A\right|}^{1-v}}{{\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|}^{v}}\right\|}^{2}}}\right)

\displaystyle\text{(by Lemma \ref{17})}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2}\left({{\left\|A\right\|}^{2\left(1-v\right)}}+{{\left\|A\right\|}^{2v}}+\sqrt{{{\left({{\left\|A\right\|}^{2\left(1-v\right)}}-{{\left\|A\right\|}^{2v}}\right)}^{2}}+4{{\left\|{{\left|A\right|}^{1-v}}{{\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|}^{v}}\right\|}^{2}}}\right).

So, it follows from the inequality (
2.2
) that

\omega\left(A\right)\leq\frac{1}{4}\left({{\left\|A\right\|}^{2\left(1-v\right)}}+{{\left\|A\right\|}^{2v}}+\sqrt{{{\left({{\left\|A\right\|}^{2\left(1-v\right)}}-{{\left\|A\right\|}^{2v}}\right)}^{2}}+4{{\left\|{{\left|A\right|}^{1-v}}{{\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|}^{v}}\right\|}^{2}}}\right),

as required.
∎

Remark 2.1
.

From
[
2
, Theorem IX.2.1]
we know that, if
A,B\in\mathbb{B}\left(\mathscr{H}\right)
are positive operators, then

\left\|{{A}^{p}}{{B}^{p}}\right\|\leq{{\left\|AB\right\|}^{p}},\text{ }\left(0\leq p\leq 1\right).

Based on this inequality, one can easily infer that

\displaystyle\omega\left(A\right)

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{2}\left(\left\|A\right\|+\left\|{{\left|A\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}{{\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right\|\right)

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{2}\left(\left\|A\right\|+{{\left\|\left|A\right|\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\right\|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2}\left(\left\|A\right\|+{{\left\|{{A}^{2}}\right\|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right).

Our second result reads as follows. This result contains our promised refinement of the inequality (
1.5
).

Theorem 2.2
.

Let
A\in\mathbb{B}\left(\mathscr{H}\right)
and let
f,g
be nonnegative continuous functions on
\left[0,\infty\right)
satisfying
f\left(t\right)g\left(t\right)=t,\left(t\geq 0\right)
, then

(2.3)

\displaystyle{{\omega}^{2}}\left(A\right)

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{4}\left\|{{f}^{4}}\left(\left|A\right|\right)+{{g}^{4}}\left(\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\right)\right\|+\frac{1}{4}\left\|{{f}^{2}}\left(\left|A\right|\right){{g}^{2}}\left(\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\right)+{{g}^{2}}\left(\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\right){{f}^{2}}\left(\left|A\right|\right)\right\|

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{2}\left\|{{f}^{4}}\left(\left|A\right|\right)+{{g}^{4}}\left(\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\right)\right\|.

Proof.

On making use of Lemma
1.2
, we have

\displaystyle{{\displaystyle\left|\left\langle Ax,x\right\rangle\right|}^{2}}

\displaystyle\leq\left\langle{{f}^{2}}\left(\left|A\right|\right)x,x\right\rangle\left\langle{{g}^{2}}\left(\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\right)x,x\right\rangle

\displaystyle\leq{{\left(\frac{\left\langle{{f}^{2}}\left(\left|A\right|\right)x,x\right\rangle+\left\langle{{g}^{2}}\left(\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\right)x,x\right\rangle}{2}\right)}^{2}}

\displaystyle\text{(by the arithmetic-geometric mean inequality)}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{4}{{\left\langle\left({{f}^{2}}\left(\left|A\right|\right)+{{g}^{2}}\left(\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\right)\right)x,x\right\rangle}^{2}}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{4}\left\langle{{\left({{f}^{2}}\left(\left|A\right|\right)+{{g}^{2}}\left(\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\right)\right)}^{2}}x,x\right\rangle

\displaystyle\text{(by the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality)}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{4}\left\langle\left({{f}^{4}}\left(\left|A\right|\right)+{{g}^{4}}\left(\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\right)+{{f}^{2}}\left(\left|A\right|\right){{g}^{2}}\left(\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\right)+{{g}^{2}}\left(\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\right){{f}^{2}}\left(\left|A\right|\right)\right)x,x\right\rangle

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{4}\left\|{{f}^{4}}\left(\left|A\right|\right)+{{g}^{4}}\left(\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\right)+{{f}^{2}}\left(\left|A\right|\right){{g}^{2}}\left(\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\right)+{{g}^{2}}\left(\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\right){{f}^{2}}\left(\left|A\right|\right)\right\|

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{4}\left\|{{f}^{4}}\left(\left|A\right|\right)+{{g}^{4}}\left(\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\right)\right\|+\frac{1}{4}\left\|{{f}^{2}}\left(\left|A\right|\right){{g}^{2}}\left(\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\right)+{{g}^{2}}\left(\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\right){{f}^{2}}\left(\left|A\right|\right)\right\|

\displaystyle\text{(by the triangle inequality for the usual operator norm)}.

Thus,

{{\left|\left\langle Ax,x\right\rangle\right|}^{2}}\leq\frac{1}{4}\left\|{{f}^{4}}\left(\left|A\right|\right)+{{g}^{4}}\left(\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\right)\right\|+\frac{1}{4}\left\|{{f}^{2}}\left(\left|A\right|\right){{g}^{2}}\left(\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\right)+{{g}^{2}}\left(\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\right){{f}^{2}}\left(\left|A\right|\right)\right\|.

Now, taking the supremum over
x\in\mathscr{H}
with
\left\|x\right\|=1
in the above inequality produces

(2.4)

{{\omega}^{2}}\left(A\right)\leq\frac{1}{4}\left\|{{f}^{4}}\left(\left|A\right|\right)+{{g}^{4}}\left(\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\right)\right\|+\frac{1}{4}\left\|{{f}^{2}}\left(\left|A\right|\right){{g}^{2}}\left(\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\right)+{{g}^{2}}\left(\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\right){{f}^{2}}\left(\left|A\right|\right)\right\|.

On the other hand,

(2.5)

\displaystyle\left\|{{f}^{2}}\left(\left|A\right|\right){{g}^{2}}\left(\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\right)+{{g}^{2}}\left(\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\right){{f}^{2}}\left(\left|A\right|\right)\right\|

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2}\left\|{{\left({{f}^{2}}\left(\left|A\right|\right)+{{g}^{2}}\left(\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\right)\right)}^{2}}-{{\left({{f}^{2}}\left(\left|A\right|\right)-{{g}^{2}}\left(\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\right)\right)}^{2}}\right\|

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{2}\left\|{{\left({{f}^{2}}\left(\left|A\right|\right)+{{g}^{2}}\left(\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\right)\right)}^{2}}+{{\left({{f}^{2}}\left(\left|A\right|\right)-{{g}^{2}}\left(\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\right)\right)}^{2}}\right\|

\displaystyle=\left\|{{f}^{4}}\left(\left|A\right|\right)+{{g}^{4}}\left(\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\right)\right\|

in which the last inequality is a direct consequence of the following result (see, e.g.,
[
5
, Corollary 1]
):

(2.6)

\left\|S-T\right\|\leq\left\|S+T\right\|,

where
S,T\in\mathbb{B}\left(\mathscr{H}\right)
are two positive operators. Combining the relations (
2.4
) and (
2.5
), we get (
2.3
).
∎

) is also

Corollary 2.2
.

Let
A\in\mathbb{B}\left(\mathscr{H}\right)
, then

(2.8)

\omega\left(A\right)\leq\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{\left\|{{\left|A\right|}^{2}}+{{\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|}^{2}}\right\|+\left\|\left|A\right|\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|+\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\left|A\right|\right\|}\leq\frac{1}{2}\left(\left\|A\right\|+{{\left\|{{A}^{2}}\right\|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right).

Proof.

Observe that

(2.9)

\displaystyle\left\|\left|A\right|\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|+\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\left|A\right|\right\|

\displaystyle\leq\left\|\left|A\right|\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\right\|+\left\|\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\left|A\right|\right\|\quad\text{(by the triangle inequality for the usual operator norm)}

\displaystyle=\left\|\left|A\right|\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\right\|+\left\|{{\left(\left|A\right|\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\right)}^{*}}\right\|

\displaystyle=2\left\|\left|A\right|\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\right\|\quad\text{(since $\left\|T\right\|=\left\|{{T}^{*}}\right\|$ for any $T\in\mathbb{B}\left(\mathscr{H}\right)$)}

\displaystyle=2\left\|{{A}^{2}}\right\|\quad\text{(since $\left\|\left|A\right|\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\right\|=\left\|{{A}^{2}}\right\|$)}.

On the other hand,

\displaystyle{{\omega}^{2}}\left(A\right)

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{4}\left(\left\|{{\left|A\right|}^{2}}+{{\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|}^{2}}\right\|+\left\|\left|A\right|\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|+\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\left|A\right|\right\|\right)

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{4}\left(\left\|{{A}^{2}}\right\|+{{\left\|A\right\|}^{2}}\right)+\frac{1}{4}\left\|\left|A\right|\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|+\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\left|A\right|\right\|\quad\text{(by Lemma \ref{17})}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{4}\left(\left\|{{A}^{2}}\right\|+{{\left\|A\right\|}^{2}}\right)+\frac{1}{2}\left\|{{A}^{2}}\right\|\quad\text{(by \eqref{15})}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{4}\left({{\left\|A\right\|}^{2}}+3\left\|{{A}^{2}}\right\|\right)

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{4}\left({{\left\|A\right\|}^{2}}+2\left\|A\right\|{{\left\|{{A}^{2}}\right\|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}+\left\|{{A}^{2}}\right\|\right)\quad\text{(since $\left\|{{A}^{2}}\right\|={{\left\|{{A}^{2}}\right\|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}{{\left\|{{A}^{2}}\right\|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\leq\left\|A\right\|{{\left\|{{A}^{2}}\right\|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}$)}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{4}{{\left(\left\|A\right\|+{{\left\|{{A}^{2}}\right\|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right)}^{2}},

and yields validity of inequality (
2.8
).
∎

Remark 2.4
.

{{\omega}^{2}}\left(A\right)\leq\frac{1}{4}\left\|{{\left|A\right|}^{2}}+{{\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|}^{2}}\right\|+\frac{1}{2}\omega\left(\left|A\right|\left|A^{*}\right|\right).

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{4}\left(\left\|{{\left|A\right|}^{2}}+{{\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|}^{2}}\right\|+\omega\left(\left|A\right|\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|+\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\left|A\right|\right)\right)

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{4}\left(\left\|{{\left|A\right|}^{2}}+{{\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|}^{2}}\right\|+\omega\left(\left|A\right|\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\right)+\omega\left(\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\left|A\right|\right)\right)

\displaystyle\text{(by the triangle inequality for $\omega\left(\cdot\right)$)}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{4}\left(\left\|{{\left|A\right|}^{2}}+{{\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|}^{2}}\right\|+\omega\left(\left|A\right|\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\right)+\omega\left(\left(\left|{{A}}\right|\left|A^{*}\right|\right)^{*}\right)\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{4}\left\|{{\left|A\right|}^{2}}+{{\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|}^{2}}\right\|+\frac{1}{2}\omega\left(\left|A\right|\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\right)\quad\text{(since $\omega\left(T\right)=\omega\left(T^{*}\right)$ for any $T\in\mathbb{B}(\mathscr{H})$)}.

It is clear that inequality (
2.7
) refines the inequality (
2.10
).

Remark 2.5
.

From the inequality (
2.9
) and the first inequality in (
1.1
), we infer that

\frac{1}{4}\left\|\left|A\right|\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|+\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|\left|A\right|\right\|\leq\frac{1}{2}\left\|{{A}^{2}}\right\|\leq\omega\left({{A}^{2}}\right).

Therefore, by inequality (
2.7
), we can write

{{\omega}^{2}}\left(A\right)\leq\frac{1}{4}\left\|{{\left|A\right|}^{2}}+{{\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|}^{2}}\right\|+\omega\left({{A}^{2}}\right).

This can be regarded as a reverse of the inequality (
1.2
), when
n=2
.

We can obtain a refinement of the triangle inequality as follows.

Proposition 2.1
.

Let
A,B\in\mathbb{B}\left(\mathscr{H}\right)
, then

(2.11)

\displaystyle\left\|A+B\right\|

\displaystyle\leq\sqrt{\left\|{{\left|A\right|}^{2}}+{{\left|B\right|}^{2}}\right\|+\left\|{{A}^{*}}B+{{B}^{*}}A\right\|}

\displaystyle\leq\sqrt{{{\left\|A\right\|}^{2}}+{{\left\|B\right\|}^{2}}+2\left\|{{A}^{*}}B\right\|}

\displaystyle\leq\left\|A\right\|+\left\|B\right\|.

Proof.

We can write,

\displaystyle{{\left\|A+B\right\|}^{2}}

\displaystyle=\left\|{{\left|A\right|}^{2}}+{{\left|B\right|}^{2}}+{{A}^{*}}B+{{B}^{*}}A\right\|

\displaystyle\leq\left\|{{\left|A\right|}^{2}}+{{\left|B\right|}^{2}}\right\|+\left\|{{A}^{*}}B+{{B}^{*}}A\right\|

(2.12)

(by the triangle inequality for the usual operator norm)

\displaystyle\leq{{\left\|A\right\|}^{2}}+{{\left\|B\right\|}^{2}}+\left\|{{A}^{*}}B\right\|+\left\|{{B}^{*}}A\right\|

(by the triangle inequality for the usual operator norm)

\displaystyle={{\left\|A\right\|}^{2}}+{{\left\|B\right\|}^{2}}+\left\|{{A}^{*}}B\right\|+\left\|{{\left({{A}^{*}}B\right)}^{*}}\right\|

\displaystyle={{\left\|A\right\|}^{2}}+{{\left\|B\right\|}^{2}}+2\left\|{{A}^{*}}B\right\|\quad\text{(since $\left\|T\right\|=\left\|{{T}^{*}}\right\|$ for any $T\in\mathbb{B}\left(\mathscr{H}\right)$)}

\displaystyle\leq{{\left\|A\right\|}^{2}}+{{\left\|B\right\|}^{2}}+2\left\|{{A}^{*}}\right\|\left\|B\right\|

(by the submultiplicative property of usual operator norm)

\displaystyle={{\left\|A\right\|}^{2}}+{{\left\|B\right\|}^{2}}+2\left\|A\right\|\left\|B\right\|

\displaystyle={{\left(\left\|A\right\|+\left\|B\right\|\right)}^{2}}.

This shows that

\displaystyle{{\left\|A+B\right\|}^{2}}

\displaystyle\leq\left\|{{\left|A\right|}^{2}}+{{\left|B\right|}^{2}}\right\|+\left\|{{A}^{*}}B+{{B}^{*}}A\right\|

\displaystyle\leq{{\left\|A\right\|}^{2}}+{{\left\|B\right\|}^{2}}+2\left\|{{A}^{*}}B\right\|

\displaystyle\leq{{\left(\left\|A\right\|+\left\|B\right\|\right)}^{2}}.

Taking the square root in this inequality we obtain (
2.11
).
∎

Our refinement of the inequality (
1.6
) is presented in the following theorem.

Theorem 2.3
.

Let
A\in\mathbb{B}\left(\mathscr{H}\right)
, then

\frac{1}{4}\left\|{{\left|A\right|}^{2}}+\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|^{2}\right\|\leq\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{2{{\omega}^{4}}\left(A\right)+\frac{1}{8}\left\|{{\left(A+{{A}^{*}}\right)}^{2}}{{\left(A-{{A}^{*}}\right)}^{2}}\right\|}\leq{{\omega}^{2}}\left(A\right).

Proof.

Let
A=B+iC
be the Cartesian decomposition of
A
. Then
B

and
C
are self-adjoint and

{{\left|\left\langle Ax,x\right\rangle\right|}^{2}}={{\left\langle Bx,x\right\rangle}^{2}}+{{\left\langle Cx,x\right\rangle}^{2}}.

A little calculation shows that

(2.13)

\left\|B\right\|\leq\omega\left(A\right),

and similarly

(2.14)

\left\|C\right\|\leq\omega\left(A\right).

Now, by using Proposition
2.1
we have

\displaystyle\frac{1}{4}\left\|{{\left|A\right|}^{2}}+\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|^{2}\right\|

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2}\left\|{{B}^{2}}+{{C}^{2}}\right\|

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{{{\left\|B\right\|}^{4}}+{{\left\|C\right\|}^{4}}+2\left\|{{B}^{2}}{{C}^{2}}\right\|}

\displaystyle\text{(by the submultiplicative property of usual operator norm)}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{2{{\omega}^{4}}\left(A\right)+2\left\|{{B}^{2}}{{C}^{2}}\right\|}\quad\text{(by \eqref{27} and \eqref{28})}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{2{{\omega}^{4}}\left(A\right)+2{{\left\|B\right\|}^{2}}{{\left\|C\right\|}^{2}}}

\displaystyle\leq{{\omega}^{2}}\left(A\right)\quad\text{(by \eqref{27} and \eqref{28})}.

Thus, from the discussion above we have

\frac{1}{4}\left\|{{\left|A\right|}^{2}}+\left|{{A}^{*}}\right|^{2}\right\|\leq\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{2\left({{\omega}^{4}}\left(A\right)+\left\|{{B}^{2}}{{C}^{2}}\right\|\right)}\leq{{\omega}^{2}}\left(A\right).

This completes the proof.
∎
