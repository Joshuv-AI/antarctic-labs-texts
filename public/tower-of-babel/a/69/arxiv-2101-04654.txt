On the growth and zeros of polynomials attached to arithmetic functions
Bernhard Heim, Markus Neuhauser

Abstract
. In this paper we investigate growth properties and the zero distribution of polynomials attached to arithmetic functions g g and h h , where g g is normalized, of moderate growth, and 0 < h ⁡ ( n ) ≤ h ⁡ ( n + 1 ) 0<h(n)\leq h(n+1) . We put P 0 g , h ​ ( x ) = 1 P_{0}^{g,h}(x)=1 and P n g , h ​ ( x ) := x h ⁡ ( n ) ​ ∑ k = 1 n g ⁡ ( k ) ​ P n − k g , h ​ ( x ) . P_{n}^{g,h}(x):=\frac{x}{h(n)}\sum_{k=1}^{n}g(k)\,P_{n-k}^{g,h}(x). As an application we obtain the best known result on the domain of the non-vanishing of the Fourier coefficients of powers of the Dedekind η \eta -function. Here, g g is the sum of divisors and h h the identity function. Kostant’s result on the representation of simple complex Lie algebras and Han’s results on the Nekrasov–Okounkov hook length formula are extended. The polynomials are related to reciprocals of Eisenstein series, Klein’s j j -invariant, and Chebyshev polynomials of the second kind.

-function. Let

Since the definition of

Let us start with the Nekrasov–Okounkov hook length formula
[

]

Let

-function.
Let

The Nekrasov–Okounkov hook length formula (

The identity (

Note that the

are integer-valued polynomials of degree

From the formula it follows that

for all real

.

The second example is of a more artificial nature, discovered recently

\displaystyle\left|P_{j}^{g,h}\left(x\right)\right|

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\left|\frac{x+g\left(2\right)h\left(j-1\right)}{h\left(j\right)}\right|\left|P_{j-1}^{g,h}\left(x\right)\right|-\left|P_{j}^{g,h}-\frac{x+g\left(2\right)h\left(j-1\right)}{h\left(j\right)}P_{j-1}^{g,h}\left(x\right)\right|

\displaystyle>

\displaystyle\left(\frac{\left|x\right|}{h\left(j\right)}-\frac{\left|g\left(2\right)\right|h\left(j-1\right)}{h\left(j\right)}-\varepsilon\frac{\left|x\right|}{h\left(j\right)}\right)\left|P_{j-1}^{g,h}\left(x\right)\right|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{\left(1-\varepsilon\right)\left|x\right|-\left|g\left(2\right)\right|h\left(j-1\right)}{h\left(j\right)}\left|P_{j-1}^{g,h}\left(x\right)\right|

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\frac{\left(1-\varepsilon\right)\left|x\right|-\left|g\left(2\right)\right|h\left(j\right)}{h\left(j\right)}\left|P_{j-1}^{g,h}\left(x\right)\right|

for

\left|x\right|>\kappa\,\,h(n-1)
.
Note that for

\left|x\right|>\kappa\,h(n-1)

we have

\left(1-\varepsilon\right)\left|x\right|-g\left(2\right)h\left(j\right)>\left(\frac{1}{T_{\varepsilon}}+\left|g\left(2\right)\right|\right)h\left(n-1\right)-g\left(2\right)h\left(j\right)>0.

Thus,

\left|P_{j-1}^{g,h}\left(x\right)\right|<\frac{h\left(j\right)}{\left(1-\varepsilon\right)\left|x\right|-g\left(2\right)h\left(j\right)}\left|P_{j}^{g,h}\left(x\right)\right|.

We use this inequality and obtain

\displaystyle\left|P_{n-k}^{g,h}\left(x\right)\right|

\displaystyle<

\displaystyle\left|P_{n-k+1}^{g,h}\left(x\right)\right|\frac{h\left(n-k+1\right)}{\left(1-\varepsilon\right)\left|x\right|-\left|g\left(2\right)\right|h\left(n-k+1\right)}<\ldots

\displaystyle<

\displaystyle\left|P_{n-1}^{g,h}\left(x\right)\right|\prod_{j=1}^{k-1}\frac{h\left(n-j\right)}{\left(1-\varepsilon\right)\left|x\right|-\left|g\left(2\right)\right|h\left(n-j\right)}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\left|P_{n-1}^{g,h}\left(x\right)\right|\left(\frac{h\left(n-1\right)}{\left(1-\varepsilon\right)\left|x\right|-\left|g\left(2\right)\right|h\left(n-1\right)}\right)^{k-1}

for

\left|x\right|>\kappa\,\,h(n-1)\geq\kappa\,\,h(n-k)

for all
2\leq k\leq n
by assumption.
Using this, we can now estimate the sum by

\displaystyle\sum_{k=2}^{n-1}\left|g\left(k+1\right)-g\left(2\right)g\left(k\right)\right|\left|P_{n-1-k}^{g,h}\left(x\right)\right|

\displaystyle<

\displaystyle\left|P_{n-1}^{g,h}\left(x\right)\right|\sum_{k=2}^{n-1}\left|g\left(k+1\right)-g\left(2\right)g\left(k\right)\right|\left(\frac{h\left(n-1\right)}{\left(1-\varepsilon\right)\left|x\right|-\left|g\left(2\right)\right|h\left(n-1\right)}\right)^{k}

and we obtain

\displaystyle\left|P_{n}^{g,h}\left(x\right)-\frac{x+g\left(2\right)h\left(n-1\right)}{h\left(n\right)}P_{n-1}^{g,h}\left(x\right)\right|

\displaystyle<

\displaystyle\frac{\left|x\right|}{h\left(n\right)}\left|P_{n-1}^{g,h}\left(x\right)\right|\sum_{k=2}^{n-1}\left|g\left(k+1\right)-g\left(2\right)g\left(k\right)\right|\left(\frac{h\left(n-1\right)}{\left(1-\varepsilon\right)\left|x\right|-\left|g\left(2\right)\right|h\left(n-1\right)}\right)^{k}.

Estimating the sum using the
assumption from the theorem we obtain

\displaystyle\sum_{k=2}^{n-1}\left|g\left(k+1\right)-g\left(2\right)g\left(k\right)\right|\left(\frac{h\left(n-1\right)}{\left(1-\varepsilon\right)\left|x\right|-\left|g\left(2\right)\right|h\left(n-1\right)}\right)^{k}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle G_{2}\left(\frac{h\left(n-1\right)}{\left(1-\varepsilon\right)\left|x\right|-\left|g\left(2\right)\right|h\left(n-1\right)}\right)\leq G_{2}\left(T_{\varepsilon}\right)\leq\varepsilon

since

\left|x\right|>\kappa\,\,h(n-1)=\frac{\kappa\,\,h(n-1)}{1-\varepsilon}\left(\frac{1}{T_{\varepsilon}}+\left|g\left(2\right)\right|\right)

which is equivalent to

\frac{\left(1-\varepsilon\right)\left|x\right|}{h\left(n-1\right)}-\left|g\left(2\right)\right|>\frac{1}{T_{\varepsilon}}

and
G_{2}
is increasing on
\left[0,R\right)

as
\left|g\left(k+1\right)-g\left(2\right)g\left(k\right)\right|\geq 0
for all
k\in\mathbb{N}
.
∎
