Spin effects in spherical harmonic modes of gravitational waves from eccentric compact binary inspirals
Kaushik Paul, Chandra Kant Mishra

Abstract
We compute the leading and subleading spin effects through the second post-Newtonian order (2PN) in spherical harmonic modes of gravitational waveforms from inspiralling compact binaries in noncircular orbits with nonprecessing components. The two spin couplings, linear-in-spin (spin-orbit) and quadratic-in-spin (spin-spin), that appear in 2PN waveforms are computed with desired accuracy and explicit expressions for relevant modes are derived. The modes that have spin corrections through 2PN include ( ℓ , | m | ) (\ell,|m|) = ( ( 2 , 2 ) , ( 2 , 1 ) , ( 3 , 3 ) , ( 3 , 2 ) , ( 3 , 1 ) , ( 4 , 3 ) , ( 4 , 1 ) ) ((2,2),\,(2,1),\,(3,3),\,(3,2),\,(3,1),\,(4,3),\,(4,1)) modes. Additionally, two m = 0 m=0 modes— ( 2 , 0 ) (2,0) and ( 3 , 0 ) (3,0) —also contribute to the 2PN order. Closed-form expressions for these modes for compact binaries in general orbits as well as in elliptical orbits are provided. While the general orbit results can be used to study signals from binaries in orbits of arbitrary shape and nature, elliptical orbit results are applicable to systems with arbitrary eccentricities. We also express the elliptical orbit results as leading eccentric corrections to the circular results. Our prescription represents, the first, fully analytical treatment that combines spins, eccentricity and higher modes together and completes computation of spin effects through 2PN order. These should find immediate applications in inspiral-merger-ringdown modeling for eccentric mergers including the effect of nonprecessing spins and higher modes as well as in parameter estimation analyses employing an inspiral waveform.

=
(m_{1}m_{2}/M^{2})
is the symmetric mass ratio parameter and
\delta
=
(m_{1}-m_{2})/M
, with
m_{1}
,
m_{2}
being component masses. (Note that the above definitions lead to
\delta=\pm\sqrt{1-4\nu}
depending upon the sign of the difference
m_{1}-m_{2}

\langle\rangle
) around indices indicate that expressions are symmetric and trace-free in indices they enclose. Additionally,
r=|\bm{x}|
, is the binary’s relative separation and
\dot{r}=({\bm{x}\cdot\bm{v})/r}
the radial component of the relative velocity vector.
Finally, the parameters
\kappa_{1,2}
characterize the spin-induced mass quadrupole moment of the two components, defined such that for black holes
\kappa_{1,2}=1
.

II.3
Equations of motion

Equations of motion, which help writing mode expressions completely in terms of position and velocity variables, have been computed with the accuracy desired in
(
Buonanno et al. 2013
)
. Similar to the source moments these have contributions from SO and SS effects. These are expressed in terms of vectors along the relative separation (
\bm{x}
) and relative velocity (
\bm{v}
) and involve suitable combinations of spin vectors introduced in
(
Kidder 1995
;
Buonanno et al. 2013
)

We reproduce these here for the convenience of the reader. Again, we split the related expression in terms of NS, SO, and SS pieces for clarity in presentation. The structure for EoM (or simply acceleration,
{\bm{a}}
) is as follows:

\displaystyle\bm{a}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\bm{a}^{{\rm{NS}}}+\bm{a}^{{\rm{SO}}}+\bm{a}^{{\rm{SS}}}\,,

(12)

with,

\displaystyle\bm{a}^{{\rm{NS}}}

\displaystyle=-\frac{GM}{r^{3}}\bm{x}-\frac{1}{c^{2}}\frac{GM}{r^{3}}\Bigg\{\bigg[(1+3\nu)v^{2}-\frac{3}{2}\nu\dot{r}^{2}

\displaystyle-\frac{4GM}{r}\left(1+\frac{\nu}{2}\right)\bigg]\bm{x}\,-\,4r\dot{r}\Big(1-\frac{\nu}{2}\Big)\bm{v}\Bigg\}+\mathcal{O}\left(\frac{1}{c^{4}}\right),

(13a)

\displaystyle\bm{a}^{{\rm{SO}}}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{c^{3}}\frac{GM}{r^{3}}\left(\frac{GM}{r}\right)\Bigg\{\left[\left(\frac{\bm{x}\times\bm{v}}{r}\right)\cdot\left(\frac{12\bm{S}}{GM^{2}}\right.\right.

\displaystyle\left.\left.+\frac{6\delta\bm{\Sigma}}{GM^{2}}\right)\right]\bm{x}-r\bigg[\bm{v}\times\bigg(\frac{7\bm{S}}{GM^{2}}+\frac{3\delta\bm{\Sigma}}{GM^{2}}\bigg)\bigg]

\displaystyle+3\dot{r}\bigg[\bm{x}\times\bigg(\frac{3\bm{S}}{GM^{2}}+\frac{\delta\bm{\Sigma}}{GM^{2}}\bigg)\bigg]\Bigg\}+\mathcal{O}\left(\frac{1}{c^{5}}\right)\,,

(13b)

\displaystyle\bm{a}^{{\rm{SS}}}

\displaystyle=-{3\over 2}\frac{1}{c^{4}}\frac{GM}{r^{3}}\left(\frac{GM}{r}\right)^{2}\Bigg[\bm{x}\,\bigg(\frac{{\bm{S}_{0}^{+}}}{GM^{2}}\cdot\frac{{\bm{S}_{0}^{-}}}{GM^{2}}\bigg)\,

\displaystyle+\bigg(\bm{x}\cdot\frac{{\bm{S}_{0}^{+}}}{GM^{2}}\bigg)\frac{{\bm{S}_{0}^{-}}}{GM^{2}}+\bigg(\bm{x}\cdot\frac{{\bm{S}_{0}^{-}}}{GM^{2}}\bigg)\frac{{\bm{S}_{0}^{+}}}{GM^{2}}\,

\displaystyle-\frac{5\bm{x}}{r^{2}}\,\bigg(\bm{x}\cdot\frac{{\bm{S}_{0}^{+}}}{GM^{2}}\bigg)\bigg(\bm{x}\cdot\frac{{\bm{S}_{0}^{-}}}{GM^{2}}\bigg)\Bigg]+\mathcal{O}\left(\frac{1}{c^{6}}\right)\,.

(13c)

Note again, the NS part above is 1PN accurate. This again is keeping in mind the computation of the (
\ell
=2,
m
=1) mode which has leading (1PN) and subleading (2PN) contributions. For all other modes, contributing to the 2PN waveform Newtonian acceleration in the NS piece together with SO and SS pieces should suffice. Below we summarize notation of
Buonanno et al. 2013
used in expressing the EoM.
The set of spin vectors (
\bm{S_{0}^{+}}
,
\bm{S_{0}^{-}}

{\bm{S}_{1}}
,
{\bm{S}_{2}}
), component masses (

\displaystyle\bm{S}^{+}_{0}=\frac{M}{m_{1}}\bigg(\frac{\kappa_{1}}{\kappa_{2}}\bigg)^{1/4}\left(1+\sqrt{1-\kappa_{1}\kappa_{2}}\right)^{1/2}{\bm{S_{1}}}

\displaystyle\ +\frac{M}{m_{2}}\bigg(\frac{\kappa_{2}}{\kappa_{1}}\bigg)^{1/4}\left(1-\sqrt{1-\kappa_{1}\kappa_{2}}\right)^{1/2}{\bm{S_{2}}}\,,

(14a)

\displaystyle\bm{S}^{-}_{0}=\frac{M}{m_{2}}\bigg(\frac{\kappa_{2}}{\kappa_{1}}\bigg)^{1/4}\left(1+\sqrt{1-\kappa_{1}\kappa_{2}}\right)^{1/2}{\bm{S_{2}}}

\displaystyle\ +\frac{M}{m_{1}}\bigg(\frac{\kappa_{1}}{\kappa_{2}}\bigg)^{1/4}\left(1-\sqrt{1-\kappa_{1}\kappa_{2}}\right)^{1/2}{\bm{S_{1}}}\,.

(14b)

Additionally, expressions for
\ddot{r}
and
\dot{v}

\displaystyle\dot{v}

\displaystyle=\frac{\bm{a}\cdot\bm{v}}{v}\,,

III
