Logarithmic TC via the Infinite Root Stack and the Beilinson Fiber Square
Federico Binda, Tommy Lundemo, Alberto Merici, Doosung Park

Abstract
. We apply our previous results on “saturated descent” to express a wide range of logarithmic cohomology theories in terms of the infinite root stack. Examples include the log cotangent complex, Rognes’ log topological cyclic homology, and Nygaard-complete log prismatic cohomology. As applications, we show that the Nygaard-completion of the site-theoretic log prismatic cohomology coincides with the definition arising from log TC {\rm TC} , and we establish a log version of the TC {\rm TC} -variant of the Beilinson fiber square of Antieau–Mathew–Morrow–Nikolaus.

(1)

The last of these approaches has a fruitful generalization to the context of structured ring spectra (

Let us first discuss the perspective of (3). Following the work of Talpo–Vistoli

It is

[

show that

(a)

3.13.
The infinite root stack

We give a very brief recollection of the construction of the (

Let

is an fpqc-stack over

fibered in groupoids, determined by the following property: Given a solid arrow diagram

{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\sqrt[\infty]{({\cal X},{\cal M})}}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces{\rm Spf}(A)}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces{\cal X},}
\scriptstyle{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces f}

and an isomorphism relating its pre-composition with

(denoted

induced by the log structure and

[
30
, Definition 3.3 and Lemma 3.12]
and Diao–Yao
[

, Section 13.1]
.

Remark 3.14
.

We need the assignment

to satisfy quasisyntomic descent. Here,
{\rm Div}_{{\rm Spf}(A)}
is the category of pairs
(L,s)
with
L
a line bundle and

a global section. To see this, we consider for a quasisyntomic cover

the diagram

{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces{\rm Div}_{{\rm Spf}(A)}}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\lim_{\Delta}{\rm Div}_{{\rm Spf}(B^{\otimes_{A}\bullet})}}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces{\rm lim}_{n}{\rm Div}_{{\rm Spec}(A/p^{n})}}

Here, the vertical maps are equivalences by Grothendieck’s existence theorem, while the bottom map is an equivalence by fpqc-descent. Hence the top map is an equivalence, as desired (note that quasisyntomic descent for

is also recorded in

{\rm lPoly}_{(A/I,M)}^{\wedge}\to{\cal D}(A),\qquad(R,P)\mapsto{{\mathbbl{\Delta}}}_{(R,P)/(A,M)},

where
{{\mathbbl{\Delta}}}_{(R,P)/(A,M)}
is the site-theoretic definition of log prismatic cohomology of
[

, Section 4.2]
. By left Kan extension, this gives a functor

{{\mathbbl{\Delta}}}_{(-,-)/(A,M)}\colon{\rm Ani}({\rm PreLog}_{(A/I,M)^{\wedge}})\to{\cal D}(A)

that we shall refer to as
derived log prismatic cohomology
.

5.2.
Derived log prismatic cohomology via the infinite root stack

Suppose now that
(A,I)
is a perfect prism. We could have equally well worked with a pre-log prism
(A,I,M)
with
M
a perfect monoid by
[
15
, Proposition 4.5]
and
[
6
, Proposition 4.7]
. Extending the present discussion to more general base prisms would require additional effort.

By quasisyntomic descent, we may apply Definition
3.20
to define
{{\mathbbl{\Delta}}}_{\sqrt[\infty]{{\rm Spf}(R,P)}/A}
for
(R,P)\in{\rm lQSyn}_{A/I}
.

Lemma 5.3
.

With notation as above, assume further that
(R,P)\in{\rm lQSyn}_{A/I}^{\rm qsyn}
. Then there is a natural equivalence
{{\mathbbl{\Delta}}}_{(R,P)/A}\simeq{{\mathbbl{\Delta}}}_{\sqrt[\infty]{{\rm Spf}(R,P)}/A}
relating the derived log prismatic cohomology of
(R,P)
to that of the infinite root stack
\sqrt[\infty]{{\rm Spf}(R,P)}
.

Proof.

We can compute
{{\mathbbl{\Delta}}}_{\sqrt[\infty]{{\rm Spf}(R,P)}/A}
in terms of the ordinary prismatic cohomology of the saturated Čech nerve (
3.3
) associated to
P\to P_{\rm perf}
. This takes the form

(5.1)

{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces R\langle P_{\rm perf}\rangle\!\!}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\!R\widehat{\otimes}_{A/I\langle P_{\rm perf}\rangle}A/I\langle P_{\rm perf}\oplus_{P}^{\rm sat}P_{\rm perf}\rangle\!\!}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\!\cdots,}

see (
3.7
). Ignoring the question of Nygaard filtrations for now, our remaining task is to argue that
{{\mathbbl{\Delta}}}_{(R,P)/A}
can be computed by the same diagram. For this, we denote by
R\langle P\rangle^{\otimes^{\rm sat}\bullet+1}
the diagram (
5.1
), while we write
P_{\rm perf}^{\oplus^{\rm sat}_{P}\bullet+1}
for the diagram (
3.1
) associated to
\varphi\colon P\to P_{\rm perf}
. We then observe that there are canonical maps

(5.2)

{{\mathbbl{\Delta}}}_{(R,P)/A}\xrightarrow{}{{\mathbbl{\Delta}}}_{(R\langle P\rangle^{\otimes^{\rm sat}\bullet+1},P_{\rm perf}^{\oplus^{\rm sat}_{P}\bullet+1})/A}\xleftarrow{}{{\mathbbl{\Delta}}}_{R\langle P\rangle^{\otimes^{\rm sat}\bullet+1}/A},

where the right-hand term computes
{{\mathbbl{\Delta}}}_{\sqrt[\infty]{{\rm Spf}(R,P)}/A}
. We claim that both maps are equivalences on totalizations. For this, we may first reduce modulo
A/I
and argue as in
[
6
, Proof of Corollary 4.22]
and commute the base-change past the totalization. Then we use the (derived) Hodge–Tate comparison
[
15
, Proposition 4.5]
to reduce checking that (
5.2
) are equivalences by replacing (log) prismatic cohomology with the (log) cotangent complex. This is true, by saturated descent for the log cotangent complex
[
6
, Theorem 4.12]
on one hand, and the fact that the
p
-complete log cotangent complex does not see semiperfect monoids on the other
[
6
, Proposition 4.7]
. Thus the maps (
5.2
) are equivalences.
∎

Let us write
\sqrt[\infty]{{{\mathbbl{\Delta}}}}_{(-,-)/A}
for the left Kan extension of
{{\mathbbl{\Delta}}}_{\sqrt[\infty]{\rm Spf}(-,-)/A}
from
p
-complete polynomial
A/I
-algebras to all animated
p
-complete
A/I
-algebras. We have the following generalization of
[
12
, Theorem 13.5]
:

Theorem 5.4
.

Let
(A,I)
be a perfect prism. The two functors

\sqrt[\infty]{{{\mathbbl{\Delta}}}}_{(-,-)/A},\quad{{{\mathbbl{\Delta}}}}_{(-,-)/A}\colon{\rm Ani}({\rm PreLog}_{A/I}^{\wedge})\to{\cal D}(A)

are canonically equivalent.

Proof.

By Lemma
5.3
, they agree for
p
-complete polynomial pre-log rings over
A/I
, and so the result follows from their definitions as left Kan extensions.
∎

We next want to claim that the equivalence of Theorem
5.4
is compatible with Nygaard filtrations. To see this, we will first introduce an alternative definition for the Nygaard filtration on the site-theoretic log-prismatic cohomology, which deviates from that pursued by Koshikawa–Yao
[
15
, Section 5.5]
, inspired by the equivalences (
5.2
). We explain in Section
5.9
that the two filtrations are equivalent.

The repletion/exactification of the multiplication map
P_{\rm perf}^{\oplus\bullet+1}\to P_{\rm perf}
is canonically isomorphic to the projection

P_{\rm perf}^{\oplus^{\rm ex}\bullet+1}\cong P_{\rm perf}\oplus(P_{\rm perf}^{\rm gp})^{\oplus\bullet}\to P_{\rm perf}.

Similarly, the repletion/exactification of the multiplication map
P_{\rm perf}^{\oplus_{P}\bullet+1}\to P_{\rm perf}
is canonically isomorphic to the projection

P_{\rm perf}^{\oplus_{P}^{\rm ex}\bullet+1}\cong P_{\rm perf}\oplus(P_{\rm perf}^{\rm gp}/P^{\rm gp})^{\oplus\bullet}\to P_{\rm perf}.

This is canonically isomorphic to the saturated coproducts
P_{\rm perf}^{\oplus_{P}^{\rm sat}\bullet+1}\cong P_{\rm perf}\oplus(P_{\rm perf}^{\rm gp}/P^{\rm gp})^{\oplus\bullet}
(cf.
[
6
, Section 4]
or
[
22
]
) and receives a map from
P_{\rm perf}^{\oplus^{\rm ex}\bullet+1}
by means of the quotient map
P_{\rm perf}^{\rm gp}\to P_{\rm perf}^{\rm gp}/P^{\rm gp}
. If
(A,I)
is a perfect prism and
(R,P)\in{\rm lPoly}_{A/I}^{\wedge}
, the right-hand side of (
5.2
) then participates in a further equivalence

(5.3)

{{\mathbbl{\Delta}}}_{R\langle P\rangle^{\otimes^{\rm sat}\bullet+1}/A}\xrightarrow{\simeq}{{\mathbbl{\Delta}}}_{R\langle P\rangle^{\otimes^{\rm sat}\bullet+1}/A\langle P_{\rm perf}^{\oplus^{\rm ex}\bullet+1}\rangle},

as
A/I\langle P_{\rm perf}^{\oplus^{\rm ex}\bullet+1}\rangle
is a different choice of levelwise perfectoid mapping (indeed, levelwise surjecting) to the levelwise quasiregular semiperfectoid ring
R\langle P\rangle^{\otimes^{\rm sat}\bullet+1}
. We make the following definition:

Definition 5.5
.

Let
(A,I)
be a perfect prism and let
(R,P)\in{\rm lPoly}_{A/I}^{\wedge}
. With notation as in the proof of Theorem
5.4
, we define the
Nygaard filtration
to be

{\rm Fil}_{N}^{\geq i}{{\mathbbl{\Delta}}}_{(R,P)/A}:={\rm Tot}({\rm Fil}^{\geq i}_{N}{{\mathbbl{\Delta}}}_{R\langle P\rangle^{\otimes^{\rm sat}\bullet+1}/A\langle P_{\rm perf}^{\oplus^{\rm ex}\bullet+1}\rangle}),

that is, the totalization of the cosimplicial object
{\rm Fil}^{\geq i}_{N}{{\mathbbl{\Delta}}}_{R\langle P\rangle^{\otimes^{\rm sat}\bullet+1}/A\langle P_{\rm perf}^{\oplus^{\rm ex}\bullet+1}\rangle}
. For a general
p
-complete animated pre-log
A/I
-algebra, we define the Nygaard filtration by left Kan extension.

We now work towards the proof of Theorem
1.3
, largely inspired by the proof strategy of
[
5
, Theorem 13.1]
. Let us first recall from
[
6
, Construction 3.9]
that, given a perfectoid ring
A/I
, we define the functor

{\rm Ani}({\rm lPoly}_{A/I}^{\wedge})\to{\cal D}(A)^{\wedge}_{(p,I)},\quad(R,P)\mapsto\widehat{{{\mathbbl{\Delta}}}}^{\rm nc}_{(R,P)/A}

by left Kan extension of Nygaard-completed log prismatic cohomology
\widehat{{{\mathbbl{\Delta}}}}_{(R,P)/A}
from the log polynomial case.

Lemma 5.6
.

Let
(R,P)\in{\rm lPoly}_{A/I}^{\wedge}
be a
p
-complete polynomial pre-log
A/I
-algebra. Then there is an equivalence
{{{\mathbbl{\Delta}}}}_{(R,P)/A}\xrightarrow{\simeq}\widehat{{{\mathbbl{\Delta}}}}_{(R,P)/A}^{\rm nc}
of
(p,I)
-complete objects in
{\cal D}(A)
.

Proof.

By Lemma
5.3
(and the equivalences (
5.2
) in particular), we may compute
{{\mathbbl{\Delta}}}_{(R,P)/A}
as the totalization of the cosimplicial diagram
{{\mathbbl{\Delta}}}_{R\langle P\rangle^{\otimes^{\rm sat}\bullet+1}}
. By
[
6
, Corollary 4.22]
, the same is true for
\widehat{{{\mathbbl{\Delta}}}}_{(R,P)/A}^{\rm nc}
.
∎

While left somewhat implicit in the proof, we find it worthwhile to highlight that the equivalence of Lemma

comes to life from the levelwise equivalences

{{\mathbbl{\Delta}}}_{R\langle P\rangle^{\otimes^{\rm sat}\bullet+1}}\xrightarrow{\simeq}\widehat{{{\mathbbl{\Delta}}}}^{\rm nc}_{R\langle P\rangle^{\otimes^{\rm sat}\bullet+1}}

of
[
5
, Theorem 13.1]
and saturated descent for both constructions.

Corollary 5.7
.

For any
p
-complete animated pre-log
A/I
-algebra
(R,P)
, there is an equivalence
{{\mathbbl{\Delta}}}_{(R,P)/A}\xrightarrow{\simeq}\widehat{{{\mathbbl{\Delta}}}}^{\rm nc}_{(R,P)/A}
. ∎

We now match the various Nygaard filtrations in this picture. The Nygaard filtration

is also defined by left Kan extension, so that its Nygaard completion recovers

for log quasiregular semiperfectoid
A/I

. We observe that this coincides with the

, obtained by setting

(5.4)

\widetilde{{\rm Fil}}_{N}^{\geq i}\widehat{{{\mathbbl{\Delta}}}}_{(R,P)/A}^{\rm nc}:={\rm Tot}({\rm Fil}^{\geq i}_{N}\widehat{{{\mathbbl{\Delta}}}}^{\rm nc}_{R\langle P\rangle^{\otimes^{\rm sat}\bullet+1}/A\langle P_{\rm perf}^{\oplus^{\rm ex}\bullet+1}\rangle})

and left Kan extending in general. These filtrations coincide: indeed, the saturated descent of

Lemma 5.8
.

Let

be log quasiregular semiperfectoid. There is a map

{{\mathbbl{\Delta}}}_{(S,Q)}\to\widehat{{{\mathbbl{\Delta}}}}_{(S,Q)}

which exhibits the target as the Nygaard-completion of the source.

Proof.

Let
(A,I)
be a perfect prism with a map

, there is an equivalence

. By construction, the filtration of Definition

is compatible with (

We expect there to be a variant of Lemma

in the log setting, cf.

.

Proof of Theorem

7.
A log variant of the Beilinson fiber square

In this final section, we prove Theorems
1.5
and
1.6
from the introduction. Recall from
[
1
, Section 2]
(and in particular
[
1
, Proof of Theorem 2.12]
) that the
{\rm TC}
-variant of the Beilinson fiber square comes to life by studying the effect of the map
X\otimes_{\mathbb{S}}{\mathbb{Z}}^{\rm triv}\to X\otimes_{{\mathbb{S}}}{\rm THH}({\mathbb{F}}_{p})
on
{\rm TC}
and
{\rm TC}^{-}
(cf.
[
1
, Proposition 2.8]
). Unsurprisingly, the proof of Theorem
1.5
arises from their results applied to the cyclotomic spectrum

. To this end, we begin with the following analog of
[
1
, Theorem 2.12]
:

Proposition 7.1
.

Let
(R,P)
be a pre-log ring. There is a square of the form

{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces{\rm TC}((R,P);{\mathbb{Z}}_{p})}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces{\rm TC}((R\otimes_{\mathbb{S}}{\mathbb{F}}_{p},P);{\mathbb{Z}}_{p})}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces{\rm HC}^{-}((R,P);{\mathbb{Z}}_{p})}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces{\rm HP}((R,P);{\mathbb{Z}}_{p}).}

which becomes cartesian after inverting
p
.

Proof.

This follows from replacing
{\rm THH}(R;{\mathbb{Z}}_{p})
with
{\rm THH}((R,P);{\mathbb{Z}}_{p})
in
[
1
, Proof of Theorem 2.12]
and using basic properties of log
{\rm THH}
established in
[
8
]
. We spell this out below.

We build a commutative square

{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces{\rm TC}((R,P);{\mathbb{Z}}_{p})}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces{\rm TC}({\rm THH}(R,P)\otimes_{\mathbb{S}}{\mathbb{Z}}^{\rm triv};{\mathbb{Z}}_{p})}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces{\rm TC}((R\otimes_{{\mathbb{S}}}{\mathbb{F}}_{p},P);{\mathbb{Z}}_{p})}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces({\rm THH}((R,P);{\mathbb{Z}}_{p})\otimes_{{\mathbb{S}}}{\mathbb{Z}}^{\rm triv})^{hS^{1}}}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces({\rm THH}((R,P);{\mathbb{Z}}_{p})\otimes_{{\mathbb{S}}}{\mathbb{Z}}^{\rm triv})^{tS^{1}}}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces{\rm HC}^{-}((R,P);{\mathbb{Z}}_{p})}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces{\rm HP}((R,P);{\mathbb{Z}}_{p}).}

We claim that both squares are cartesian after inverting
p
, and that the top vertical arrow is an equivalence after inverting
p
, yielding the result.

We first treat the top square. There is an equivalence

{\rm TC}((R\otimes_{\mathbb{S}}{\mathbb{F}}_{p},P);{\mathbb{Z}}_{p})\simeq{\rm TC}({\rm THH}(R,P)\otimes_{{\mathbb{S}}}{\rm THH}({\mathbb{F}}_{p});{\mathbb{Z}}_{p})

[

(with

[
1

applies to say that the upper square is cartesian after inverting
p
.

We now treat the bottom square. It is induced by the

-equivariant map
