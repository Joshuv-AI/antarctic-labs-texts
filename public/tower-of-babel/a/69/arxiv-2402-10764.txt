Nearly-optimal effective stability estimates around Diophantine tori of Hölder Hamiltonians
Santiago Barbieri, Gerard Farré

Abstract
We prove that the solutions of Hölder-differentiable Hamiltonian systems, associated to initial conditions in a small ball of radius ρ > 0 \rho>0 around a Lagrangian, ( γ , τ ) − (\gamma,\tau)- Diophantine, quasi-periodic torus, are stable over a time t stab ≃ 1 / ( | ρ | 1 + ℓ − 1 τ + 1 ​ | ln ⁡ ρ | ℓ − 1 ) t^{\text{stab}}\simeq 1/(|\rho|^{1+\frac{\ell-1}{\tau+1}}|\ln\rho|^{\ell-1}) , where ℓ > 2 ​ d + 1 , ℓ ∈ ℝ \ell>2d+1,\ell\in\mathbb{R} , is the regularity, and d d is the number of degrees of freedom. In the finitely differentiable case (for integer ℓ \ell ), this result improves the previously known effective stability bounds around Diophantine tori. Moreover, by a previous work based on the Anosov-Katok construction, it is known that for any ε > 0 \varepsilon>0 there exists a C ℓ C^{\ell} -Hamiltonian, with ℓ ≥ 3 \ell\geq 3 , admitting a sequence of solutions starting at distance ρ n → 0 \rho_{n}\to 0 from a ( γ , τ ) (\gamma,\tau) -Diophantine torus that diffuse in a time of order t n diff ≃ 1 / ( | ρ n | 1 + ℓ − 1 τ + 1 + ε ) t^{\text{diff}}_{n}\simeq 1/(|\rho_{n}|^{1+\frac{\ell-1}{\tau+1}+\varepsilon}) . Therefore the stability estimates that we show are optimal up to an arbitrarily small polynomial correction. Keywords. Hamiltonian systems, Quasi-periodic invariant tori, Effective stability, Nekhoroshev Theory.
