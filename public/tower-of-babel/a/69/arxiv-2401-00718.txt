Generalised Local Fractional Hermite-Hadamard Type Inequalities on Fractal Sets
Peter Olamide Olanipekun

Abstract
Fractal geometry and analysis constitute a growing field, with numerous applications, based on the principles of fractional calculus. Fractals sets are highly effective in improving convex inequalities and their generalisations. In this paper, we establish a generalized notion of convexity. By defining generalised ϕ h − s \phi_{h-s} convex functions, we extend the well known concepts of generalised convex functions, P P -functions, Breckner s s -convex functions, h h -convex functions amongst others. With this definition, we prove Hermite-Hadamard type inequalities for generalized ϕ h − s \phi_{h-s} convex mappings onto fractal sets. Our results are then applied to probability theory.

Theorem 2.7
.

Suppose

. Define
K_{\alpha}(\lambda,s):=\rho_{\alpha s}(\lambda^{\frac{1}{s}})+\rho_{\alpha s}((1-\lambda)^{\frac{1}{s}})

, then

is non decreasing on
(0,\infty)

Proof.

Let

such that
\phi(x)>0

f(\lambda^{\frac{1}{s}}\phi(x)+(1-\lambda)^{\frac{1}{s}}\phi(x))\leq K_{\alpha}(\lambda,s)f(\phi(x))

Clearly, the function

given by

. Consequently, we have

\displaystyle f(t\phi(x))\leq K_{\alpha}(\lambda,s)f(\phi(x))\hskip 10.00002pt\hskip 10.00002pt\textnormal{for all}\hskip 10.00002ptt\in[2^{1-\frac{1}{s}},1]\hskip 10.00002pt\textnormal{and}\hskip 10.00002pt\phi(x)>0.

(2)

If
t\in[2^{2\left(1-\frac{1}{s}\right)},1]
then

lies in the interval

Choose

Hermite-Hadamard-type Inequalities on Fractal Sets

We now establish some inequalities for generalised
\phi_{h-s}
convex functions on fractal sets.

Theorem 3.1
.

(Generalised Hermite-Hadamard inequality).

Let
f:I\subseteq\mathbb{R}\rightarrow\mathbb{R}^{\alpha}
. Suppose that
f\in C_{\alpha}(I)
is a generalised
\phi_{h-s}
convex function. Then for all
t\in[0,1]
and
\phi(a),\phi(b)\in I\subseteq\mathbb{R}
, the inequality holds

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2^{\alpha}\Gamma(1+\alpha)\rho_{\alpha s}\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)}f\left(\frac{\phi(a)+\phi(b)}{2}\right)\leq\frac{{}_{\phi(a)}{\mathcal{J}}_{\phi(b)}^{\alpha}f(\phi(x))}{(\phi(b)-\phi(a))^{\alpha}}\leq\left(f(\phi(a))+f(\phi(b))\right){}_{0}\mathcal{J}_{1}^{\alpha}\rho_{\alpha s}(t).

(4)

Proof.

Using the assumption of generalised
\phi_{h-s}
convexity on
f
, one observes that for all
t\in[0,1]
and
\phi(a),\phi(b)\in I\subseteq\mathbb{R}

\displaystyle f\left(\frac{\phi(a)+\phi(b)}{2}\right)

\displaystyle=f\left(\frac{1}{2}\left(t\phi(a)+(1-t)\phi(b)+(1-t)\phi(a)+t\phi(b)\right)\right)

\displaystyle\leq\rho_{\alpha s}(1/2)f(t\phi(a)+(1-t)\phi(b))+\rho_{\alpha s}(1/2)f(t\phi(b)+(1-t)\phi(a)).

(5)

Choosing
x\in I
such that
\phi(x)=t\phi(a)+(1-t)\phi(b)
and noting that
t\phi(b)+(1-t)\phi(a)=\phi(a)+\phi(b)-\phi(x)
,
a local fractional integration of (
5
) with respect to
t
on
[0,1]
yields the first inequality in (
4
).
To prove the second inequality, we use the generalised
\phi_{h-s}
convexity of
f
to estimate the functional
\mathcal{J}^{\alpha}f(\phi(x))
on
(\phi(a),\phi(b))\subseteq I
. Noting that
{}_{0}\mathcal{J}^{\alpha}_{1}(\rho_{\alpha s}(t)-\rho_{\alpha s}(1-t))=0
, we have

\displaystyle{}_{\phi(a)}\mathcal{J}^{\alpha}_{\phi(b)}f(\phi(x))

\displaystyle=(\phi(b)-\phi(a))^{\alpha}\,\,{}_{0}\mathcal{J}^{\alpha}_{1}f(t\phi(a)+(1-t)\phi(b))

\displaystyle\leq(\phi(b)-\phi(a))^{\alpha}\,\,{}_{0}\mathcal{J}^{\alpha}_{1}\left[\rho_{\alpha s}(t)f(\phi(a))+\rho_{\alpha s}(1-t)f(\phi(b))\right]

\displaystyle=(\phi(b)-\phi(a))^{\alpha}[f(\phi(a))+f(\phi(b))]\,\,{}_{0}\mathcal{J}^{\alpha}_{1}\rho_{\alpha s}(t).

This completes the proof.
∎

.

Let
\phi:[a,b]\rightarrow(0,\infty)
and
f:I\subseteq\mathbb{R}\rightarrow\mathbb{R}^{\alpha}
be such that
f\in C_{\alpha}(I)
.
Then for
0\leq\lambda\leq 1
, we have

\displaystyle{}_{0}\mathcal{J}_{1}^{\alpha}f\left((1-t)\phi(a)+t\phi(b)\right)=(1-\lambda)^{\alpha}{}_{0}\mathcal{J}_{1}^{\alpha}f\left((1-t)((1-\lambda)\phi(a)+\lambda\phi(b))+t\phi(b)\right)

\displaystyle\hskip 10.00002pt+\lambda^{\alpha}{}_{0}\mathcal{J}_{1}^{\alpha}f\left((1-t)\phi(a)+t((1-\lambda)\phi(a)+\lambda\phi(b))\right)

(6)

Proof.

It is easy to see that equality (
6
) holds for
\lambda=0
and
\lambda=1
. Now, suppose
\lambda\in(0,1)
. The change of variable

u=\lambda(1-t)+t
yields

\displaystyle{}_{0}\mathcal{J}_{1}^{\alpha}\,\,f\left((1-t)((1-\lambda)\phi(a)+\lambda\phi(b))+t\phi(b)\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\Gamma(\alpha+1)}\frac{1}{(1-\lambda)^{\alpha}}\int_{\lambda}^{1}f\left((1-u)\phi(a)+u\phi(b)\right)\,(du)^{\alpha}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{(1-\lambda)^{\alpha}}\,\,{}_{0}\mathcal{J}_{\lambda}^{\alpha}\,\,f\left((1-u)\phi(a)+u\phi(b)\right).

(7)

On the other hand, the change of variable
u=\lambda t
yields

\displaystyle{}_{0}\mathcal{J}_{1}^{\alpha}\,\,f\left((1-t)\phi(a)+t((1-\lambda)\phi(a)+\lambda\phi(b))\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}\frac{1}{\lambda^{\alpha}}\int_{0}^{\lambda}f((1-u)\phi(a)+u\phi(b))\,\,(du)^{\alpha}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\lambda^{\alpha}}\,\,{}_{0}\mathcal{J}_{\lambda}^{\alpha}\,\,f((1-u)\phi(a)+u\phi(b)).

(8)

Combining (
7
) and (
8
) gives the identity (
6
). This completes the proof.

∎

Theorem 3.6
.

Let
f:I\subseteq\mathbb{R}\rightarrow\mathbb{R}^{\alpha}
. Suppose that
f\in C_{\alpha}(I)
is a generalised
\phi_{h-s}
convex function. If
\rho_{\alpha s}(t)\leq t^{\alpha}
for all
t\in[0,1]
then for all
\phi(a),\phi(b)\in I\subseteq\mathbb{R}
with
\phi(a)\leq\phi(b)
and any
\lambda\in[0,1]
, the following inequality holds

\displaystyle\frac{f\left(\frac{\phi(a)+\phi(b)}{2}\right)}{\Gamma(\alpha+1)}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{\rho_{\alpha s}(1-\lambda)}{\Gamma(\alpha+1)}f\left(\frac{(1-\lambda)\phi(a)+(\lambda+1)\phi(b)}{2}\right)+\frac{\rho_{\alpha s}(\lambda)}{\Gamma(\alpha+1)}f\left(\frac{(2-\lambda)\phi(a)+\lambda\phi(b)}{2}\right)

\displaystyle\leq\frac{{}_{\phi(a)}\mathcal{J}_{\phi(b)}^{\alpha}f(x)}{(\phi(b)-\phi(a))^{\alpha}}

\displaystyle\leq\left[f((1-\lambda)\phi(a)+\lambda^{\alpha}f(\phi(a))+\lambda\phi(b))+(1-\lambda)^{\alpha}f(\phi(b))\right]\,\,{}_{0}\mathcal{J}_{1}^{\alpha}\,\rho_{\alpha s}(x)

\displaystyle\leq[f(\phi(a))+f(\phi(b))]\,\,{}_{0}\mathcal{J}_{1}^{\alpha}\,\rho_{\alpha s}(x).

Proof.

Estimate (
4
) and the generalised
\phi_{h-s}
convexity of
f
imply

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(\alpha+1)}f\left(\frac{(1-\lambda)\phi(a)+(1+\lambda)\phi(b)}{2}\right)=\frac{1}{\Gamma(\alpha+1)}f\left(\frac{(1-\lambda)\phi(a)+\lambda\phi(b)}{2}+\frac{\phi(b)}{2}\right)

\displaystyle\leq\frac{2^{\alpha}\rho_{\alpha s}(1/2)}{\Gamma(\alpha+1)}\int_{0}^{1}f\Big((1-t)[(1-\lambda)\phi(a)+\lambda\phi(b)]+t\phi(b)\Big)(dt)^{\alpha}

\displaystyle\leq\Big(f[(1-\lambda)\phi(a)+\lambda\phi(b)]+f(\phi(b))\Big)\,{}_{0}\mathcal{J}_{1}^{\alpha}\rho_{\alpha s}(t)

(9)

where we have used the fact that
0^{\alpha}\leq 2^{\alpha}\rho_{\alpha s}(1/2)\leq 1^{\alpha}
. Using again estimate (
4
) and the generalised
\phi_{h-s}
convexity of
f
we have

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(\alpha+1)}f\left(\frac{(2-\lambda)\phi(a)+\lambda\phi(b)}{2}\right)=\frac{1}{\Gamma(\alpha+1)}f\left(\frac{\lambda\phi(a)}{2}+\frac{(1-\lambda)\phi(a)+\lambda\phi(b)}{2}\right)

\displaystyle\leq\frac{2^{\alpha}\rho_{\alpha s}(1/2)}{\Gamma(\alpha+1)}\int_{0}^{1}f\Big((1-t)\phi(a)+t[(1-\lambda)\phi(a)+\lambda\phi(b)]\Big)(dt)^{\alpha}

\displaystyle\leq\Big(f(\phi(a))+f[(1-\lambda)\phi(a)+\lambda\phi(b)]\Big)\,\,{}_{0}\mathcal{J}_{1}^{\alpha}\rho_{\alpha s}(t).

(10)

Multiply the estimates (
9
) by
(1-\lambda)^{\alpha}
and (
10
) by
\lambda^{\alpha}
, and add the resulting double estimates. Calling upon Lemma
3.5
, we find

\displaystyle\frac{(1-\lambda)^{\alpha}}{\Gamma(\alpha+1)}f\left(\frac{(1-\lambda)\phi(a)+(\lambda+1)\phi(b)}{2}\right)+\frac{\lambda^{\alpha}}{\Gamma(\alpha+1)}f\left(\frac{(2-\lambda)\phi(a)+\lambda\phi(b)}{2}\right)

\displaystyle\leq 2^{\alpha}\rho_{\alpha s}(1/2)\,\,{}_{0}\mathcal{J}_{1}^{\alpha}\,f\big((1-t)\phi(a)+t\phi(a)\big)

\displaystyle\leq\Big(f[(1-\lambda)\phi(a)+\lambda\phi(b)]+(1-\lambda)^{\alpha}f(\phi(b))+\lambda^{\alpha}f(\phi(a))\Big){}_{0}\mathcal{J}_{1}\,\rho_{\alpha s}(t).

(11)

We use the generalised
\phi_{h-s}
convexity of
f
to obtain the estimates

\displaystyle(1-\lambda)^{\alpha}f\left(\frac{(1-\lambda)\phi(a)+(\lambda+1)\phi(b)}{2}\right)+\lambda^{\alpha}f\left(\frac{(2-\lambda)\phi(a)+\lambda\phi(b)}{2}\right)

\displaystyle\geq\rho_{\alpha s}(1-\lambda)f\left(\frac{(1-\lambda)\phi(a)+(\lambda+1)\phi(b)}{2}\right)+\rho_{\alpha s}(\lambda)f\left(\frac{(2-\lambda)\phi(a)+\lambda\phi(b)}{2}\right)

\displaystyle\geq f\left((1-\lambda)\left(\frac{(1-\lambda)\phi(a)+(\lambda+1)\phi(b)}{2}\right)+\lambda\left(\frac{(2-\lambda)\phi(a)+\lambda\phi(b)}{2}\right)\right)

\displaystyle=f\left(\frac{\phi(a)+\phi(b)}{2}\right).

(12)

Accordingly, the condition
\rho_{\alpha}(x)\leq x^{\alpha}
for all
x,s\in[0,1]
yields

\displaystyle\Big(f[(1-\lambda)\phi(a)+\lambda\phi(b)]+(1-\lambda)^{\alpha}f(\phi(b))+\lambda^{\alpha}f(\phi(a))\Big)\,{}_{0}\mathcal{J}^{\alpha}_{1}\rho_{\alpha s}(t)

\displaystyle\leq\Big(\rho_{\alpha s}(1-\lambda)f(\phi(a))+\rho_{\alpha s}(\lambda)f(\phi(b))+(1-\lambda)^{\alpha}f(\phi(b))+\lambda^{\alpha}f(\phi(a))\Big)\,{}_{0}\mathcal{J}^{\alpha}_{1}\rho_{\alpha s}(t)

\displaystyle=\Big(f(\phi(a))+f(\phi(b))\Big)\,{}_{0}\mathcal{J}^{\alpha}_{1}\rho_{\alpha s}(t).

(13)

To complete the proof, we combine estimates (
11
), (
12
) and (
13
) and note that a suitable change of variable yield
{}_{0}\mathcal{J}_{1}^{\alpha}\,f\big((1-t)\phi(a)+t\phi(a)\big)=\frac{{}_{\phi(a)}\mathcal{J}_{\phi(b)}^{\alpha}f(x)}{(\phi(b)-\phi(a))^{\alpha}}
.

∎

Next, we obtain average refinements of Hermite-Hadamard inequalities by using the generalised notion of convexity and assuming that
\rho_{s}:[0,1]\rightarrow(0,\infty)
is linear and multiplicative (c.f.

[
15
,
4
,
20
,
51
]
. Note that a multiplicative function
\rho
satisfies
\rho(1)=1
. An example of a multiplicative, linear function is
\rho_{s}(x)=x
).

Theorem 3.7
.

Let
f:I\rightarrow\mathbb{R}^{\alpha}
. Suppose that
f\in C_{\alpha}(I)
is a generalised
\phi_{h-s}
convex function and
\rho_{s}:[0,1]\rightarrow(0,\infty)
is linear and multiplicative. Then for all
t\in[0,1]
, the following inequalities hold

\displaystyle X_{[1-t,t,C]}\leq f((1-t)\phi(a)+t\phi(b))\leq X_{[1-t,t,c]}

(14)

where c=
min{
t, 1-t
}
, C=
max{
t, 1-t
}
and

X_{[u,v,\varrho]}:=\rho_{\alpha s}(u)f(\phi(a))+\rho_{\alpha s}(v)f(\phi(b))-\rho_{\alpha s}(2\varrho)\left(\frac{f(\phi(a))+f(\phi(b))}{2}-f\left(\frac{\phi(a)+\phi(b)}{2}\right)\right)

with
0\leq u,v,\varrho\leq 1
. Furthermore, the double inequality hold

\displaystyle X_{[1,1,2C]}-f(\phi(x))\leq f(\phi(a)+\phi(b)-\phi(x))\leq X_{[1,1,2c]}-f(\phi(x))

(15)

where, in particular,
c=\textnormal{min}\left\{\frac{\phi(b)-\phi(x)}{\phi(b)-\phi(a)},\frac{\phi(x)-\phi(a)}{\phi(b)-\phi(a)}\right\}
and
C=\textnormal{max}\left\{\frac{\phi(b)-\phi(x)}{\phi(b)-\phi(a)},\frac{\phi(x)-\phi(a)}{\phi(b)-\phi(a)}\right\}
.

Moreover, the refined Hermite-Hadamard inequality also holds

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(\alpha+1)}\int_{0}^{1}X_{[1,\,1,\,2C_{1}+2C_{2}]}(dt)^{\alpha}\,\,

\displaystyle\leq f\left(\phi(a)+\phi(b)-\frac{1}{2}(\phi(u)+\phi(v))\right)+\frac{{}_{\phi(v)}\mathcal{J}_{\phi(u)}f}{(\phi(v)-\phi(u))^{\alpha}}\,\,

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{\Gamma(\alpha+1)}\int_{0}^{1}X_{[1,\,1,\,2c_{1}+2c_{2}]}(dt)^{\alpha}\,\,

where
C_{1}:=C_{1}(t)=(1-t)\textnormal{max}\left\{t,1-t\right\}
,
C_{2}:=C_{2}(t)=t\,\textnormal{max}\left\{t,1-t\right\}
,
c_{1}:=c_{1}(t)=(1-t)\textnormal{min}\left\{t,1-t\right\}
and
c_{2}:=c_{2}(t)=t\,\textnormal{min}\left\{t,1-t\right\}
.

Proof.

Let
t\in[0,\frac{1}{2}]
, then
c=t
. The linearity of
\rho_{s}
guarantees that

\displaystyle X_{[1-t,t,c]}

\displaystyle=\rho_{\alpha s}(1-2t)f(\phi(a))+\rho_{\alpha s}(2t)f\left(\frac{\phi(a)+\phi(b)}{2}\right)

\displaystyle\geq f\left((1-2t)\phi(a)+2t\left(\frac{\phi(a)+\phi(b)}{2}\right)\right)

\displaystyle=f((1-t)\phi(a)+t\phi(b)).

(16)

The case
t\in[\frac{1}{2},1]
is proved by replacing
t
by
1-t
in the argument above. Next, we use the generalised
\phi_{h-s}
convexity of
f
to estimate

\displaystyle f(\phi(a))

\displaystyle=f\left[\frac{1}{t+1}((t+1)\phi(a)-t\phi(b))+\frac{t}{t+1}\phi(b)\right]

\displaystyle\leq\rho_{\alpha s}\left(\frac{1}{t+1}\right)f((t+1)\phi(a)-t\phi(b))+\rho_{\alpha s}\left(\frac{t}{t+1}\right)f(\phi(b)).

The latter together with the multiplicative property of
\rho_{s}
imply the estimate

\displaystyle\rho_{\alpha s}(t+1)f(\phi(a))-\rho_{\alpha s}(t)f(\phi(b))\leq f((t+1)\phi(a)-t\phi(b)).

(17)

On the other hand, if
t\in[0,\frac{1}{2}]
(the case
t\in[\frac{1}{2},1]
follows by a similar argument) then
C=1-t
. Calling upon estimate (
17
), we have

\displaystyle f((1-t)\phi(a)+t\phi(b))=f\left((2-2t)\frac{\phi(a)+\phi(b)}{2}-(1-2t)f(\phi(b))\right)

\displaystyle\geq\rho_{\alpha s}(1+(1-2t))f\left(\frac{\phi(a)+\phi(b)}{2}\right)-\rho_{\alpha s}(1-2t)f(\phi(b))

\displaystyle=\rho_{\alpha s}(1-t)f(\phi(a))+\rho_{\alpha s}(t)f(\phi(b))-\rho_{\alpha s}(2C)\left(\frac{f(\phi(a))+f(\phi(b))}{2}-f\left(\frac{\phi(a)+\phi(b)}{2}\right)\right).

(18)

Combining estimates (
18
) and (
16
) proves the double inequality (
14
).

Choose
x\in[a,b]
such that
\phi(a)\leq\phi(x)\leq\phi(b)
and
\phi(x)=t\phi(a)+(1-t)\phi(b)
where
t=\frac{\phi(b)-\phi(x)}{\phi(b)-\phi(a)}
. Note that
c=\textnormal{min}\left\{\frac{\phi(b)-\phi(x)}{\phi(b)-\phi(a)},\frac{\phi(x)-\phi(a)}{\phi(b)-\phi(a)}\right\}
. Applying estimate (
16
) twice, we have

\displaystyle f(\phi(a)+\phi(b)-\phi(x))

\displaystyle=f((1-t)\phi(a)+t\phi(b))

\displaystyle\leq X_{[1-t,t,c]}=X_{[1-t,1-(1-t),c]}

\displaystyle=X_{[1,1,c]}-\rho_{\alpha s}(t)f(\phi(a))-\rho_{\alpha s}(1-t)f(\phi(b))

\displaystyle\leq X_{[1,1,2c]}-f(\phi(x)).

(19)

Note that by using (
18
) instead of (
16
), the following reverse estimate of (
19
) holds

X_{[1,1,2C]}-f(\phi(x))\leq f(\phi(a)+\phi(b)-\phi(x)).

Combining the latter with (
19
) proves estimate (
15
).

We extend (
19
) to estimates with two parameters. The generalised
\phi_{h-s}
convexity of
f
implies

\displaystyle f\left(\phi(a)+\phi(b)-((1-t)\phi(x)+t\phi(y))\right)

\displaystyle=f\left((1-t)(\phi(a)+\phi(b)-\phi(x))+t(\phi(a)+\phi(b)-\phi(y))\right)

\displaystyle\leq\rho_{\alpha s}(1-t)f(\phi(a)+\phi(b)-\phi(x))+\rho_{\alpha s}(t)f(\phi(a)+\phi(b)-\phi(y)).

The latter together with (
19
) yield

\displaystyle f\left(\phi(a)+\phi(b)-((1-t)\phi(x)+t\phi(y))\right)

\displaystyle\leq\rho_{\alpha s}(1-t)\left(X_{[1,1,2c]}-f(\phi(x))\right)+\rho_{\alpha s}(t)\left(X_{[1,1,2c]}-f(\phi(y))\right)

\displaystyle=X_{[1-t,t,2c_{1}]}+X_{[t,1-t,2c_{2}]}-\rho_{\alpha s}(1-t)f(\phi(x))-\rho_{\alpha s}(t)f(\phi(y))

(20)

where
c_{1}:=c_{1}(t)=(1-t)\textnormal{min}\left\{t,1-t\right\}
and
c_{2}:=c_{2}(t)=t\,\textnormal{min}\left\{t,1-t\right\}
.

The generalised
\phi_{h-s}
convexity of
f
implies

\displaystyle f\left(\phi(a)+\phi(b)-\frac{1}{2}(\phi(x)+\phi(y))\right)

\displaystyle=f\left(\frac{1}{2}(\phi(a)+\phi(b)-((1-t)\phi(x)+t\phi(y)))+\frac{1}{2}(\phi(a)+\phi(b)-(t\phi(x)+(1-t)\phi(y)))\right)

\displaystyle\leq\rho_{\alpha s}\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)f\left(\phi(a)+\phi(b)-((1-t)\phi(x)+t\phi(y))\right)+\rho_{\alpha s}\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)f\left(\phi(a)+\phi(b)-(t\phi(x)+(1-t)\phi(y))\right)

Combining the latter with (
20
) and using the multiplicative property of
\rho_{s}
, we have

\displaystyle f\left(\phi(a)+\phi(b)-\frac{1}{2}(\phi(x)+\phi(y))\right)

\displaystyle\leq X_{[1-t,t,2c_{1}]}+X_{[t,1-t,2c_{2}]}-\rho_{\alpha s}\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)(f(\phi(x))+f(\phi(y)))

\displaystyle=f(\phi(a))+f(\phi(b))-\frac{1}{2}[f(\phi(x))+f(\phi(y))]-4\rho_{\alpha s}(c_{1}+c_{2})\left(\frac{f(\phi(a))+f(\phi(b))}{2}-f\left(\frac{\phi(a)+\phi(b)}{2}\right)\right)

(21)

Similarly, the following reverse estimate holds

\displaystyle f\left(\phi(a)+\phi(b)-\frac{1}{2}(\phi(x)+\phi(y))\right)

\displaystyle\geq f(\phi(a))+f(\phi(b))-\frac{1}{2}[f(\phi(x))+f(\phi(y))]-4\rho_{\alpha s}(C_{1}+C_{2})\left(\frac{f(\phi(a))+f(\phi(b))}{2}-f\left(\frac{\phi(a)+\phi(b)}{2}\right)\right)

(22)

where
C_{1}:=C_{1}(t)=(1-t)\textnormal{max}\left\{t,1-t\right\}
and
C_{2}:=C_{2}(t)=t\,\textnormal{max}\left\{t,1-t\right\}
.
Combining estimates (
21
) and (
22
) and applying a local fractional integration w.r.t
t
on
[0,1]
yield

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(\alpha+1)}\int_{0}^{1}X_{[1,\,1,\,2C_{1}+2C_{2}]}(dt)^{\alpha}-\frac{{}_{\phi(v)}\mathcal{J}_{\phi(u)}f}{(\phi(v)-\phi(u))^{\alpha}}\,\,\,\,

\displaystyle\leq f\left(\phi(a)+\phi(b)-\frac{1}{2}(\phi(u)+\phi(v))\right)

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{\Gamma(\alpha+1)}\int_{0}^{1}X_{[1,\,1,\,2c_{1}+2c_{2}]}(dt)^{\alpha}-\frac{{}_{\phi(v)}\mathcal{J}_{\phi(u)}f}{(\phi(v)-\phi(u))^{\alpha}}\,\,\,\,

where we have used the change of variables
\phi(x)=t\phi(u)+(1-t)\phi(v)
and
\phi(y)=(1-t)\phi(u)+t\phi(v)
with
u,v\in I
. This completes the proof.

3.2
Application to Probability

Let
\chi
be a random variable whose probability density function
\mathfrak{p}:[t_{1},t_{2}]\rightarrow[0,1]^{\alpha}
is generalised
\phi_{h-s}
convex with a cummulative distribution function given by

{\bf P}_{\alpha}(\chi\leq x)=\mathcal{F}_{\alpha}(x):=\frac{1}{\Gamma(\alpha+1)}\int_{t_{1}}^{x}\mathfrak{p}(\theta)\,(d\theta)^{\alpha}

and the fractional normalising condition
\frac{1}{\Gamma(\alpha+1)}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\mathfrak{p}(\theta)\,(d\theta)^{\alpha}=1
. Define the functional

\mathcal{E}_{\alpha s}(\chi):=\frac{1}{\Gamma(\alpha+1)}\int_{t_{1}}^{t_{2}}\rho_{\alpha s}(\theta)\,\mathfrak{p}(\theta)\,(d\theta)^{\alpha}

where
\rho_{\alpha s}:[0,\infty)\rightarrow[0,\infty)^{\alpha}
is as in the previous sections. With
\rho_{\alpha s}(\theta)=\theta^{\alpha}
and
\rho_{\alpha s}(\theta)=\theta^{\alpha s}
, the functional
\mathcal{E}_{\alpha s}({\chi})
reduces to generalised expectation and
s
-moments of fractional order respectively. In particular,
\mathcal{E}_{\alpha s}(\chi)
satisfies

\mathcal{E}_{\alpha s}(x)=\frac{1}{\Gamma(\alpha+1)}\int_{t_{1}}^{x}\theta^{\alpha}\,d\mathcal{F}_{\alpha}(\theta)=t_{2}^{\alpha}-\frac{1}{\Gamma(\alpha+1)}\int_{t_{1}}^{t_{2}}\mathcal{F}_{\alpha}(\theta)\,(d\theta)^{\alpha}.

Interested readers may see
[
23
,
1
]
for more information on probability using fractional calculus.

Theorem 3.8
.

Under the assumption of Theorem
3.1
, the following inequalities hold

\displaystyle\frac{2^{\alpha(s-1)}}{\Gamma(\alpha+1)}{\bf P}_{\alpha}\Big(\chi\leq\frac{\phi(a)+\phi(b)}{2}\Big)

\displaystyle\leq\frac{(\phi(b))^{\alpha}-\mathcal{E}_{\alpha}(\chi)}{(\phi(b)-\phi(a))^{\alpha}\Gamma(\alpha+1)}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{\Gamma(\alpha s+1)}{\Gamma(1+\alpha(s+1))}\Big({\bf P}_{\alpha}(\chi\leq\phi(a))+{\bf P}_{\alpha}(\chi\leq\phi(b))\Big).

Proof.

Follows easily by using Theorem
3.1
and the identity
\frac{1}{\Gamma(\alpha+1)}\int_{0}^{1}t^{\alpha s}\,(dt)^{\alpha}-\frac{\Gamma(\alpha s+1)}{\Gamma(\alpha(s+1)+1)}=0^{\alpha}.
