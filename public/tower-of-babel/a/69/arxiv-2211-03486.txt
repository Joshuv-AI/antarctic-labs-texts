Kinetic description of ion transport in the system “ionic solution – porous environment”
Tokarchuk M

Abstract
A kinetic approach based on a modified chain of BBGKI equations for nonequilibrium particle distribution functions was used to describe the ion transfer processes in the ionic solution – porous medium system. A generalized kinetic equation of the revised Enskog–Vlasov–Landau theory for the nonequilibrium ion distribution function in the model of charged solid spheres is obtained, taking into account attractive short-range interactions for the ionic solution – porous medium system.

A generalized kinetic equation of the revised Enskog–Vlasov–Landau theory for the nonequilibrium ion distribution function in the model of charged solid spheres is obtained,

Two cases of construction of kinetic equations depending on the initial conditions of the interaction of ionic solution particles and porous matrix are considered. In the case of non-interacting subsystems (at the initial moment of time),
the modified boundary conditions of the BBGKI chain of equations do not include quasi-equilibrium coordinate

In the case of interacting subsystems (at the initial moment of time),
modified boundary conditions in the chain of equations BBGKI include vase-equilibrium coordinate (pair and higher-order) functions describing the correlations between solution ions and porous matrix particles.
For this case,
a generalized kinetic equation of the revised Enskog–Vlasov–Landau theory for the nonequilibrium ion distribution function in the model of charged solid spheres is obtained,
taking into account attractive short-range interactions for the ionic solution – porous medium system.
