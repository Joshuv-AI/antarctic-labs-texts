Introduction and statement of main result

In this article, we are devoted to the following biharmonic Choquard type equation

\Delta^{2}u-\beta\Delta u=\lambda u+(I_{\mu}*F(u))f(u),\quad\mbox{in}\ \ \mathbb{R}^{4},

(1.1)

with prescribed mass

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{4}}|u|^{2}dx=c^{2},\quad u\in H^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{4}),

The study of (

(H_{2})

\displaystyle F(z)=\int_{0}^{|z|}f(t)dt

To look for standing wave solutions

If

For the nonlinear Schrödinger equation with normalization constraint
