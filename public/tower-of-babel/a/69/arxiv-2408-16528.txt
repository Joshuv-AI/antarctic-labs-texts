Electromagnetic responses of a nonextensive quark-gluon plasma
Bing-feng Jiang, Jun Chen, De-fu Hou

Abstract
Based on the nonextensive statistical mechanics and the gluon polarization tensor obtained from kinetic theory, we derive the longitudinal and transverse gluon self-energies for the quark-gluon plasma(QGP). The electric permittivity ε \varepsilon and the magnetic permeability μ M \mu_{M} are evaluated from the gluon self-energies through which the real part of the square of the refraction index Re ​ n 2 {\rm Re}\,n^{2} and the Depine-Lakhtakia index n D ​ L n_{DL} are investigated. The real part of ε \varepsilon displays a frequency pole ω d = p \omega_{d}=p , which is just the position of the frequency inflexion of the imaginary part of ε \varepsilon . The nonextensive parameter q q significantly affects the real and imaginary parts of ε \varepsilon in the spacelike region ω < p \omega<p , while the frequency pole ω d = p \omega_{d}=p remains unchanged as q q increases. The magnetic permeability, Re ​ n 2 {\rm Re}\,n^{2} and the Depine-Lakhtakia index n D ​ L n_{DL} diverge at frequency ω m \omega_{m} . As q q increases, the pole frequency ω m \omega_{m} shifts to large frequency region. The Depine-Lakhtakia index n D ​ L n_{DL} becomes negative in a quite large frequency region ω ∈ [ ω c , ω m ] \omega\in[\omega_{c},\omega_{m}] . When q q increases, the frequency range for n D ​ L < 0 n_{DL}<0 becomes wider. Nevertheless, there are no propagating modes for the negative refraction. In addition, as momentum p p increases, the electric permittivity, the magnetic permeability, Re ​ n 2 {\rm Re}\,n^{2} and n D ​ L n_{DL} are sensitive to the change of p p , which indicates the importance of the spatial dispersion in the electromagnetic responses of the QGP.

V
Summary

We have worked out the electric permittivity
\varepsilon(\omega,k)
and the magnetic permeability

in terms of the nonextensive distribution functions associated with the polarization tensor derived from kinetic theory, through which we have derived the real part of the square of the refractive index

and the Depine-Lakhtakia index

. We have investigated the effects of nonextensivity on the electromagnetic responses of the QGP. The real and imaginary parts of the electric permittivity have changed noticeably in the spacelike region as

increases, but the pole frequency of the real part and inflexion frequency of the imaginary part remain unchanged.
\mu_{M}(\omega,k)

and

Though

In addition, momentum

The refractive index can directly relate to phenomenology of ultrarelativistic heavy-ion collisions.
Based on the refractive index at finite temperature or density, some groups have studied the medium responses due to the Cherenkov gluon radiation induced by the parton jet traveling through the hot QGP to explain the double peak structure of the experimental dihadron correlation function in the away side

;
dremin

By incorporating polarization and absorption effects with a complex index of refraction,
some authors have investigated the radiation energy loss of an energetic charge, which is helpful for understanding the jet quenching phenomena

Generally, negative refraction of light will lead to some special medium properties, such as modified Snell’s law, perfect lens, reversed Doppler shift, and so on
ramakrishna
. If the QGP has a negative refraction index, the fast parton may induce unique Cherenkov radiation phenomenon in the medium.
Unlike the normal refraction medium, the radiation from the fast parton is emitted in the opposite direction to the parton’s motion direction
ramakrishna
, which may cause unusual energy deposit and medium response.
Therefore, the negative refraction might result in some novel effect on jet quenching observables. In addition, the negative refraction might have an impact on the photon production
in ultrarelativistic heavy ion collisions. These are interesting issues deserving further
investigations.
