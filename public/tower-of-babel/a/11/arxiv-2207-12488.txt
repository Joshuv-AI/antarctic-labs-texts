The objective of our proposal is to study the photoionization of Chlorine, Sulfur, and Phosphorus ions
using the unique high resolution capability of 10.0.1 beamline in the ALS. These species are
relevant in astrophysics and as a benchmark for state of the art theoretical models of the quantum
properties of ions. We also propose to test a technique to quench the metastable states from the
ion-beam based on recent experimental evidence and on a well known technique in chemistry.
The work of this proposal may lay the foundations for a definite solution to control the
metastable component of the initial ion-beam.
