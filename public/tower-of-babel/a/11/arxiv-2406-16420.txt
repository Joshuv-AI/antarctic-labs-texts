Local Limit Theorems for q q -Multinomial and Multiple Heine Distributions
Malvina Vamvakari

Abstract
In this work we establish local limit theorems for q q -multinomial and multiple Heine distributions. Specifically, the pointwise convergence of the q q -multinomial distribution of the first kind, as well as for its discrete limit, the multiple Heine distribution, to a multivariate Stieltjes-Wigert type distribution, are provided.
