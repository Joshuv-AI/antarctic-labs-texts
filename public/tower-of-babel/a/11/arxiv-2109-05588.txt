Vanishing short multiplets in rank one 4d/5d SCFTs
Jaewon Song

Abstract
We study the short multiplet spectrum in 4d 𝒩 = 2 \mathcal{N}=2 superconformal theories of low rank using the full superconformal indices and the selection rules from the superconformal representation theory. We find a universal expression for the leading terms for the superconformal index of rank one H 0 , H 1 , H 2 , D 4 , E 6 , E 7 H_{0},H_{1},H_{2},D_{4},E_{6},E_{7} theories. From this result, we argue that certain short multiplets appear in the operator product expansions involving stress-tensor, conserved current, and Coulomb branch operator vanish. We also apply the same procedure to 5d superconformal theories and find that E 1 E_{1} theory has vanishing short multiplets analogous to that of the H 1 H_{1} theory.

In this section, we compute the superconformal indices for the 4d
{\cal N}=2
rank 1 SCFTs. We will mostly use two different representations of the indices, one of which is given in terms of fugacities
(p,q,t)
as

\displaystyle{\cal I}(p,q,t)={\rm Tr}(-1)^{F}p^{j_{1}+j_{2}+\frac{r}{2}}q^{j_{2}-j_{1}+\frac{r}{2}}t^{R-\frac{r}{2}}\ ,

(6)

where
j_{1,2}
denotes Cartans for the Lorentz group
SU(2)_{1}\times SU(2)_{2}
and
R,r
denote the Cartans for the
SU(2)_{R}\times U(1)_{r}
. The index gets contributions from the states satisfying
\Delta\equiv E-2j_{2}-2R-r/2=0
and the fugacities satisfy

\displaystyle|p|<1,\quad|q|<1,\quad|t|<1,\quad\left|\frac{pq}{t}\right|<1\ .

(7)

We sometimes use an alternative parametrization

\displaystyle p=\mathfrak{t}^{3}y\ ,\quad q=\mathfrak{t}^{3}/y\ ,\quad t=\mathfrak{t}^{4}/v\ ,

(8)

with
|y|=|v|=1
and
|\mathfrak{t}|<1
, which is useful when expanding the index as a power series of

. Then the trace formula for the index can be written as

\displaystyle{\cal I}(\mathfrak{t},y,v)={\rm Tr}(-1)^{F}\mathfrak{t}^{2(E+j_{2})}y^{2j_{1}}v^{-R+\frac{r}{2}}\ .

(9)

One can take a simplification limit

to obtain the Macdonald index, which counts the operators in the so-called Schur sector

. One can further take

;

The superconformal index counts the degeneracies of short multiplets up to recombination
Kinney:2005ej
;
Bhattacharya:2008zy
. Therefore for a given index, it is not always possible to extract the true short multiplet spectrum. On the other hand, we know the existence of certain universal multiplets such as stress-tensor (
{\cal T}
), conserved-current (
{\cal J}
), Coulomb branch (
{\cal E}_{r}
) and their OPE selection rules. Combining both information (sometimes with a plausible assumption), we are able to show that certain short multiplets are absent in the theory.

The procedure we take is as follows
Liendo:2015ofa
;
Song:2015wta
;
Agarwal:2018zqi
: Suppose we find the index of the form

\displaystyle{\cal I}={\rm PE}\left[{\cal I}(A)+{\cal I}(B)-{\cal I}(C)+\ldots\right]\ ,

(11)

where
{\cal I}(A),{\cal I}(B),{\cal I}(C)
denotes the index for the short multiplets
A,B,C
, respectively (it may include a possibly minus sign). The multiplets
A
or
B
can be taken as universal ones such as stress-tensor, Coulomb branch, or conserved current. For this case, the contribution to the index is unambiguously coming from such universal multiplets. On the other hand, it is not uniquely specified which short multiplet contribute to
{\cal I}(C)
in general. This term may come from the vanishing of
C
multiplet or come from another multiplet
C^{\prime}
that contributes to
{\cal I}(C^{\prime})=-{\cal I}(C)
.
However, if we once figure out the selection rules for the products of
A
and
B
’s, such as
A\times A
,
A\times B
,
B\times B
or products of powers of
A
and
B
, one may look for the multiplet
C
among these OPEs. Among the short multiplets that appear in these OPEs, if we find a unique multiplet
C
that gives rise to
{\cal I}(C)
, we can confidently claim that
C
should disappear in the theory.
3
3

3

One may find such
C
only at a multiple product of
A
and
B
, for example
n
-fold product
A^{n}=A\times\cdots\times A
. Indeed, such phenomenon happens for higher-rank theories
Agarwal:2018zqi
. In such cases, one finds that the operator
C
cannot appear in
A^{i}
for
i<n
.
It does not completely rule out the possibility of having both multiplets
C
and
C^{\prime}
so that we reproduce the index. However, it yields additional multiplets that contribute to higher-order with even larger cancellations. Therefore we claim the
C
multiplet vanishes in this case, which is supported by the bootstrap results.

Instead of computing the full selection rules for the OPEs, we take a short-cut by computing superconformal characters for the superconformal multiplets, say
\chi(A)
and
\chi(B)
. Then we take the product
\chi(A)\times\chi(B)
and decompose it into a sum over superconformal characters:
\chi(A)\times\chi(B)=\sum_{p}\chi(p)
where
p
collectively denotes the quantum numbers for the superconformal multiplets. This is
not
the full selection rule for the OPE for the product of two local operators in
A
and
B
multiplets, since it misses contributions from singular pieces. Nevertheless, it turns out this is sufficient for us since what we need to find is an existence of certain multiplets in the OPE that can potentially contribute to the index. One may worry that singular contribution might give rise to additional short multiplet that might contribute to the same index, but this does not happen generically since it changes the scaling dimension. This does not fully justify our method, but we find our method agrees with the analysis based on OPE whenever the OPE for the supermultiplets are available.

’s denotes the Cartans for the flavor symmetry group. The trace gets non-vanishing contributions only from the states with
\Delta\equiv E-2j_{1}-3R=0
.

Short multiplets in 5d SCFT are classified by Cordova-Dumitrescu-Intriligator (CDI)
Cordova:2016emh
and also by Buican-Hayling-Papageorgakis (BHP)
Buican:2016hpb
. The superconformal index for the conserved current (
{\cal J}=C_{1}[0,0]_{3}^{(2)}
in the CDI notation and
{\cal J}={\cal D}[0,0;2]
in the BHP notation) and the stress-tensor (
{\cal T}=B_{2}[0,0]_{3}^{(0)}
in CDI and
{\cal T}={\cal B}[0,0;0]
in BHP) multiplets are given as
Buican:2016hpb

\displaystyle{\cal I}_{\cal J}={\cal I}_{{\cal D}[0,0;2]}=\frac{x^{2}}{(1-xy)(1-x/y)}\ ,\quad{\cal I}_{\cal T}={\cal I}_{{\cal B}[0,0;0]}=\frac{x^{3}\chi_{\mathbf{2}}(y)}{(1-xy)(1-x/y)}\ ,

(58)

where
\chi_{\mathbf{n}}(y)
denotes the character for the
n
-dimensional representation of
SU(2)
. The denominator comes from the conformal descendants generated by derivatives. We summarize the indices for the short multiplets below.

•

The
{\cal A}
-type (BHP)/
A
-type (CDI) multiplets:

\displaystyle{\cal I}_{{\cal A}[d_{1},d_{2};R]}=(-1)^{d_{2}+1}\frac{x^{d_{1}+d_{2}+R+4}}{(1-xy)(1-x/y)}\chi_{d_{1}+1}(y)

(59)

Here
d_{1},d_{2}
are the Dynkin labels for the
SU(2)
representations, which are twice as that of the spins
(d_{i}=2j_{i})
.

•

The
{\cal B}
-type (BHP)/
B
-type (CDI) multiplets:

\displaystyle{\cal I}_{{\cal B}[d_{1},0;R]}=\frac{x^{d_{1}+R+3}}{(1-xy)(1-x/y)}\chi_{d_{1}+2}(y)

(60)

•

The
{\cal D}
-type (BHP)/
C
-type (CDI) multipets:

\displaystyle{\cal I}_{{\cal D}[0,0;R]}=\frac{x^{R}}{(1-xy)(1-x/y)}

(61)

We can follow the same procedure as in the case of 4d theory, namely combining the information from the index and the selection rules to deduce the vanishing OPE coefficients. As far as the author is aware, unlike the case of 4d
{\cal N}=2
theories, the (mixed) OPEs in 5d superconformal theories are still not carried out in detail. (See
Chang:2017cdx
;
Bobev:2017jhk
;
Baume:2019aid
for the work on 5d superconformal blocks and bootstrap.) Here we resort to a simple group theory argument, which is sufficient for our purpose.

3.2
E_{1}
theory

The five-dimensional rank 1 superconformal theories are discovered by Seiberg
Seiberg:1996bd
, which are realized as UV fixed points of
SU(2)
gauge theory of
N_{f}\leq 7
fundamental hypermultiplets. They are known to exhibit enhanced global symmetry of
E_{N_{f}+1}
and such an enhancement of symmetry is verified using the superconformal index
Kim:2012gu
;
Bashkirov:2012re
;
Hwang:2014uwa
and via study of the instanton operators
Tachikawa:2015mha
.

Here, let us focus on the minimal case of
N_{f}=0
. The leading terms in the superconformal index of the
E_{1}
theory can be written as

\displaystyle{\cal I}_{E_{1}}={\rm PE}\left[\frac{\chi_{\mathbf{3}}(a)x^{2}+\chi_{\mathbf{2}}(y)x^{3}-x^{4}-\chi_{\mathbf{3}}(a)\chi_{\mathbf{2}}(y)x^{5}-\left(\chi_{\mathbf{3}}(a)+1+\chi_{\mathbf{3}}(y)\right)x^{6}+\ldots}{(1-xy)(1-x/y)}\right]\ .

(62)

We see that the first two terms of the index comes from the conserved current (
x^{2}
) and the stress-tensor (
x^{3}
) multiplets. From the
x^{4},x^{5},x^{6}
terms, we see that certain short multiplets that appear in
{\cal J}\times{\cal J}
,
{\cal J}\times{\cal T}
and
{\cal T}\times{\cal T}
should disappear.

The
-x^{4}
term in (
62
) can be reproduced by the absence of the
{\cal D}[0,0;4]
multiplet which is generally present in the OPE of
{\cal J}\times{\cal J}={\cal D}[0,0;2]\times{\cal D}[0,0;2]
. Therefore, we have the following OPE coefficient vanish:

\displaystyle{\cal D}[0,0;4]\in{\cal J}\times{\cal J}\ \longrightarrow\ \lambda\left[{\cal J},{\cal J},{\cal D}[0,0;4]\right]\Big|_{{\cal I}_{2}}=0\ ,

(63)

where
{\cal I}_{2}=\mathbf{1}
is the singlet of the
SU(2)
flavor.

The
-x^{5}\chi_{\mathbf{3}}(a)\chi_{\mathbf{2}}(y)
term can be obtained either by the absence of the
{\cal B}[0,0;2]
multiplet or an extra
SU(2)
triplet of
{\cal A}[1,0;0]
multiplet. But we find that
{\cal B}[0,0;2]
multiplet exists in the OPE of
{\cal T}\times{\cal J}={\cal B}[0,0;0]\times{\cal D}[0,0;2]
but not
{\cal A}[1,0;0]
, the short multiplet
{\cal B}[0,0;2]
should vanish in this theory. Hence, we have

\displaystyle\begin{split}{\cal B}[0,0;2]\in{\cal T}\times{\cal J},~~{\cal A}[1,0;0]\notin{\cal T}\times{\cal J}\,\longrightarrow\ \lambda\left[{\cal T},{\cal J},{\cal B}[0,0;2]\right]=0\ .\end{split}

(64)

The terms at order
x^{6}
has two parts: One from
{\cal J}^{3}
and the other from
{\cal T}^{2}
. The
{\cal J}^{3}={\cal D}[0,0;2]^{3}
part arises from
{\cal D}[0,0;4]\times{\cal D}[0,0;4]
which includes
{\cal D}[0,0;6]
, and this is the only short multiplet that gives rise to
-\chi_{\mathbf{3}}(a)x^{6}
. Therefore, the following OPE coefficients should vanish

\displaystyle\begin{split}{\cal D}[0,0;6]\in{\cal J}^{3}\ \longrightarrow\ \lambda\left[{\cal J},{\cal D}[0,0;4],{\cal D}[0,0;6]\right]\Big|_{\textrm{adj}}=0\ .\end{split}

(65)

The term
-\chi_{\mathbf{3}}(y)x^{6}
can be obtained from two short multiplets: vanishing of
{\cal B}[1,0;2]
or addition of
{\cal A}[2,0;0]
multiplet. The former appears in the OPE of
{\cal T}\times{\cal T}
, but the latter does not. Therefore, we find the vanishing OPE

\displaystyle{\cal B}[1,0;2]\in{\cal T}\times{\cal T},~~{\cal A}[2,0;0]\notin{\cal T}\times{\cal T}\,\longrightarrow\,\lambda\left[{\cal T},{\cal T},{\cal B}[1,0;2]\right]=0\ .

(66)

The singlet term
-x^{6}
can come from the vanishing of
{\cal A}[0,1;1]
or addition of
{\cal A}[0,0;2]
or addition of
{\cal A}[0,2;0]
. However among them only
{\cal A}[0,1;1]
multiplet can appear in
{\cal T}\times{\cal T}
. Therefore, we have

\displaystyle{\cal A}[0,1;1]\in{\cal T}\times{\cal T}\ \longrightarrow\ \lambda\left[{\cal T},{\cal T},{\cal A}[0,1;1]\right]=0\ .

(67)

The vanishing structure is quite similar to that of the 4d rank one theories we considered. It would be interesting to see whether we have some kind of universality for other 5d rank one SCFTs.
Let us notice that we also have
{\cal J}^{3}\big|_{\textrm{adj}}\sim 0
, which is identical to that of the
H_{1}
theory in 4d. In fact, a similar relation
{\cal J}^{2n+1}\big|_{\textrm{adj}}\sim 0
holds for all the
(A_{1},D_{2n+1})
AD theory, which has exactly
SU(2)
flavor symmetry
Agarwal:2018zqi
. It would be interesting to find a 5d analog of such AD theories. One natural candidate would be
SU(N)_{N}
pure YM theory, which has exactly
SU(2)
flavor symmetry
Bergman:2013aca
;
Tachikawa:2015mha
.
