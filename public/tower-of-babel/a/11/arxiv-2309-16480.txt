A Liouville theorem of V ​ T VT -harmonic map heat flow
Xiangzhi Cao

Abstract
We proved an Liouville theorem for Backward V ​ T VT -harmonic map heat flow from evolution manifolds into generalized regular ball. Among others, we also proved an Liouville theorem for V ​ T VT -harmonic map heat flow from complete manifolds into generalized regular ball.

Jost-Yau

investigated the existence of Hermitian harmonic maps from Hermitian manifolds into compact nonpositively curved Riemannian manifolds by using the heat flow method. The study of such kind of maps is more difficult than that of harmonic map, since generally no variation structure can be employed. The absence of variation structure often leads to the lack of monotonicity inequality, which is a challenge for studying its blow-up behaviour (e.g. energy identity) and proving the existence using heat flows.
