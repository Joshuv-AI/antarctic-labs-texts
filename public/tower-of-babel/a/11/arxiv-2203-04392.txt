Abstract
. A graph is vertex-transitive if its automorphism group acts transitively on vertices of the graph. A vertex-transitive graph is a Cayley graph if its automorphism group contains a subgroup acting regularly on its vertices. Proposition 5

Let

Suppose that

, where
T\leq A
.
Let

, where

acts transitively

. Suppose that

, where

, a contradiction. Thus

acts regularly on

and

is a Cayley graph on

.
∎
