2.1
Cochain complexes

A cochain complex,
(A^{{}^{\tiny\bullet}},d^{{}^{\tiny\bullet}})

A^{{}^{\tiny\bullet}}

(A_{{}_{\tiny\bullet}},d_{{}_{\tiny\bullet}})

H({\cal C})\ \overset{def.}{=}\ \text{Ker}({\cal Q})/\ \text{Im}({\cal Q}).

(8)
