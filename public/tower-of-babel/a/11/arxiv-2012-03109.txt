Elastic Properties and Glass Forming Ability of the Zr 50 Cu 40 Ag 10 Metallic Alloy
Ramil M. Khusnutdinoff 1,2a and Anatolii V. Mokshin 1,2b

Abstract
The elastic properties of the Zr 50 Cu 40 Ag 10 metallic alloy, such as the bulk modulus B B , the shear modulus G G , the Young’s modulus E E and the Poisson’s ratio σ \sigma , are investigated by molecular dynamics simulation in the temperature range T = 250 − 2000 T=250-2000 K and at an external pressure of p = 1.0 p=1.0 bar. It is shown that the liquid-glass transition is accompanied by a considerable increase in the shear modulus G G and the Young’s modulus E E (by more than 50 % 50\% ). The temperature dependence of the Poisson’s ratio exhibits a sharp fall from typical values for metals of approximately 0.32 − 0.33 0.32-0.33 to low values (close to zero), which are characteristic for brittle bulk metallic glasses. Non-monotonic temperature dependence of the longitudinal and transverse sound velocity near the liquid-glass transition is also observed. The glass forming ability of the alloy is evaluated in terms of the fragility index m m . As found, its value is m ≈ 64 m\approx 64 for the Zr 50 Cu 40 Ag 10 metallic glass, that is in a good agreement with the experimental data for the Zr-Cu-based metallic glasses.

Interest to the amorphous metallic alloys is provoked by the obvious advantages of their potential applications in various fields of techniques, engineering and materials science. Amorphous alloys or metallic glasses are a new class of materials with unique physical and mechanical properties, which differ from the properties of their crystalline analogs
