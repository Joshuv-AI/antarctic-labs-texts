Uniform Stabilization of the Petrovsky-Wave Nonlinear coupled system with strong damping
Akram Ben Aissa*

Abstract
. This paper concerns the well-posedness and uniform stabilization of the Petrovsky-Wave Nonlinear coupled system with strong damping. Existence of global weak solutions for this problem is established by using the Galerkin method. Meanwhile, under a clever use of the multiplier method, we estimate the total energy decay rate.

Introduction

For simplicity reasons, we omit the space variable
x

Our main interest lies in the following system of the coupled Petrovsky-wave system of the type

(1.1)

\left\{\begin{aligned} &u^{\prime\prime}_{1}+\Delta^{2}u_{1}-a(x)\Delta u_{2}-g_{1}(\Delta u^{\prime}_{1})=0,\ &x\in\Omega,t\geq 0&\\
&u^{\prime\prime}_{2}-\Delta u_{2}-a(x)\Delta u_{1}-g_{2}(\Delta u^{\prime}_{2})=0\ &x\in\Omega,t\geq 0&\\
&\Delta u_{1}=u_{1}=u_{2}=0,\ &x\in\Gamma,t\geq 0&\\
&u_{i}(x,0)=u_{i}^{0}(x),\,\,\,u^{\prime}_{i}(x,0)=u_{i}^{1}(x),\ &x\in\Omega,\,\,\,\,\,i=1,2.&\\
\end{aligned}\right.

Here
\Omega
is a bounded domain of
\mathbb{R}^{n}
with regular boundary
\Gamma
.

When

, the Petrovsky equation was treated by Komornik
[
8
]
,
where he used semigroup approach for setting the well possedness and
he studied the strong stability

Recently, Bahlil et al.

5

\left\{\begin{array}[]{lll}u_{1}^{\prime\prime}+a(x)u_{2}+\Delta^{2}u_{1}-g_{1}(u_{1}^{\prime}(x,t))=f_{1}(u_{1},u_{2}),&in&\Omega\times\mathbb{R}^{+},\\
u_{2}^{\prime\prime}+a(x)u_{1}-\Delta u_{2}-g_{2}(u_{2}^{\prime}(x,t))=f_{2}(u_{1},u_{2}),&in&\Omega\times\mathbb{R}^{+},\\
\partial_{\nu}u_{1}=u_{1}=v=u_{2}=0&on&\Gamma\times\mathbb{R}^{+},\end{array}\right.

for

do not necessarily having a polynomial growth near the origin, by using Faedo-Galerkin method to prove the existence and uniqueness of solution and established energy decay results depending on

.
Guesmia

7

consider the problem (
1.2
) without Source

and

In this paper, we prove the global existence of weak solutions of the problem (

12

)
we use some technique from

The paper is organized as follows. In section 2 we present some assumptions and technical lemmas. In section 3 we prove the existence and the uniqueness of a global solution. In section 4 we prove the energy estimates.

\begin{split}&\int_{S}^{T}\frac{\varphi{(E)}}{E}\int_{\Omega^{-}}|\Delta u_{1}|.|g_{1}(\Delta u^{\prime}_{1})|+|\nabla u^{\prime}_{1}|^{2}\,dx\,dt+\int_{S}^{T}\frac{\varphi{(E)}}{E}\int_{\Omega^{-}}|\Delta u_{2}|.|g_{2}(\Delta u^{\prime}_{2})|+|\nabla u^{\prime}_{2}|^{2}\,dx\,dt\\
