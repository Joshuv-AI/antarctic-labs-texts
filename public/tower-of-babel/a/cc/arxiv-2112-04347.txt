Analytical model of low mass strange stars in 2+1 spacetime
Masum Murshid 1, Nilofar Rahman 1, Irina Radinschi 2, Mehedi Kalam 1

Abstract
The low mass compact stars are quite fascinating objects to study for their enigmatic behaviour. In this paper, we have modeled this kind of low mass strange stars based on the Heintzmann ansatz [ 1 ] in ( 2 + 1 ) (2+1) dimension. Attractive anisotropic force plays a significant role to restrict the upper mass limit (which is comparatively low) of the strange star. We have applied our model to some low mass strange stars. Our model could be useful to predict the important parameters of the low mass strange stars.

(2+1)

Some Physical Properties

Matching condition

The exterior spacetime of the static spherically symmetric compact object is
assumed to be described by the BTZ metric as follow

ds^{2}=-(-M_{0}-\Lambda r^{2})dt^{2}+\frac{dr^{2}}{-M_{0}-\Lambda r^{2}}+r^{2}d\phi^{2}.

(15)

The parameter
M_{0}
is the conserved charge associated with asymptotic invariance under the time displacements.
The continuity of
g_{tt}
and
g_{rr}
at surface
(r=R)
and vanishing of
normal pressure at surface yield

\displaystyle e^{2\nu(R)}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-M_{0}-\Lambda R^{2},

(16)

\displaystyle e^{-2\mu(R)}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-M_{0}-\Lambda R^{2},

(17)

\displaystyle 0

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{\Lambda}{2\pi}+\frac{\nu^{\prime-2\mu(R)}}{2\pi R}.

(18)

Solving these above three equations (eqn.-
16
-
18

A=\frac{1}{3\sqrt{3}}\sqrt{-\frac{\left(3M_{0}+2\Lambda R^{2}\right){}^{3}}{\left(M_{0}+\Lambda R^{2}\right){}^{2}},}

(19)

a=\frac{\Lambda}{3M_{0}+2\Lambda R^{2}},

(20)

\displaystyle C

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{\sqrt{3}}{\Lambda R^{2}}\sqrt{\frac{M_{0}+2\Lambda R^{2}}{3M_{0}+2\Lambda R^{2}}}

\displaystyle\times\left[M_{0}\left(4\Lambda R^{2}+2\right)+2M_{0}^{2}+\Lambda R^{2}\left(2\Lambda R^{2}+1\right)\right]

The total mass of the compact object of radius
R

Behavior of energy density and pressure

For a physically acceptable model, energy density and radial pressure both
should be monotonically decreasing function in

\displaystyle\frac{d\rho}{dr}

\displaystyle-\frac{3a^{2}r}{\pi\left(ar^{2}+1\right)^{3}\left(4ar^{2}+1\right)^{5/2}}

Energy conditions

The energy conditions such as null energy condition (NEC), weak energy
condition (WEC), strong energy condition (SEC) and dominant energy condition
(DEC) should be satisfied at every point in the interior of the compact star
simultaneously. These energy conditions are as follows:

i) NEC:
\rho+p_{i}\geq 0

ii) WEC:
\rho+p_{i}\geq 0

\rho\geq 0

iii) SEC:
\rho+p_{i}\geq 0

\rho+p_{r}+2p_{t}\geq 0

iv) DEC:
\rho>|p_{i}|

Z_{s}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-2u}}-1=\frac{1}{\sqrt{1+r\Lambda-\frac{3ar\left[1+C\left(1+4ar^{2}\right)^{-\frac{1}{2}}\right]}{2\left(1+ar^{2}\right)}}}-1

Generalized TOV Equation

The generalized TOV equation for anisotropic system is written as

\frac{d}{dr}\left(p_{r}-\frac{\Lambda}{2\pi}\right)+\nu^{\prime}(\rho+p_{r})+\frac{1}{r}(p_{r}-p_{t})=0.

(31)

This equation represents the equilibrium condition of the system under
gravitational force (
F_{g}

F_{h}
) and anisotropic force
(
F_{a}

F_{h}+F_{g}+F_{a}=0,

(32)

where

\displaystyle F_{g}=-\nu^{\prime}(\rho+p_{r}),

\displaystyle F_{a}=\frac{1}{r}(p_{t}-p_{r})

and

\displaystyle F_{h}=-\frac{d}{dr}\left(p_{r}-\frac{\Lambda}{2\pi}\right)

The following form of the gravitational force (
F_{g}
), hydrostatic force (

) and anisotropic force (

\displaystyle F_{g}=\frac{9a^{2}r}{4\pi\left(ar^{2}+1\right)^{3}\left(4ar^{2}+1\right)^{3/2}}

\displaystyle\times\left[C\left(14a^{2}r^{4}+ar^{2}-1\right)\right.

\displaystyle\left.+\left(4ar^{2}+1\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}\left(4a^{2}r^{4}-11ar^{2}-3\right)\right],

(33)

\displaystyle F_{h}=\frac{3a^{2}r}{2\pi\left(ar^{2}+1\right)^{3}\left(4ar^{2}+1\right)^{3/2}}

\displaystyle\times\left[C\left(-18a^{2}r^{4}+3ar^{2}+3\right)\right.

\displaystyle\left.+\left(4ar^{2}+1\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}\left(-4a^{2}r^{4}+19ar^{2}+5\right)\right],
