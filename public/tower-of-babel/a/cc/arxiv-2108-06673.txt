Diagram automorphisms and canonical bases
for quantized enveloping algebras

Abstract
. Let X X be a Cartan datum of symmetric type, with an admissible automorphism σ \sigma on X X , and X ¯ \underline{X} the Cartan datum induced from ( X , σ ) (X,\sigma) . Let 𝐔 q − \mathbf{U}_{q}^{-} (resp. 𝐔 ¯ q − \underline{\mathbf{U}}_{q}^{-} ) be the negative part of the quantized enveloping algebra associated to X X (resp. X ¯ \underline{X} ). Lusztig constructed the canonical basis 𝐁 \mathbf{B} of 𝐔 q − \mathbf{U}_{q}^{-} and the canonical signed basis ℬ ¯ ~ \widetilde{\underline{\mathcal{B}}} of 𝐔 ¯ q − \underline{\mathbf{U}}_{q}^{-} by making use of the geometric theory of quivers. By normalizing the sign of ℬ ¯ ~ \widetilde{\underline{\mathcal{B}}} , he obtained the canonical basis 𝐁 ¯ \underline{\mathbf{B}} of 𝐔 ¯ q − \underline{\mathbf{U}}_{q}^{-} , and a natural bijection 𝐁 σ ​ ∼ → ​ 𝐁 ¯ \mathbf{B}^{\sigma}\,\raise 2.0pt\hbox{$\underrightarrow{\sim}$}\,\underline{\mathbf{B}} . In this paper, assuming the existence of 𝐁 \mathbf{B} for 𝐔 q − \mathbf{U}_{q}^{-} , we construct the canonical basis 𝐁 ¯ \underline{\mathbf{B}} of 𝐔 ¯ q − \underline{\mathbf{U}}_{q}^{-} , and a bijection 𝐁 σ ​ ∼ → ​ 𝐁 ¯ \mathbf{B}^{\sigma}\,\raise 2.0pt\hbox{$\underrightarrow{\sim}$}\,\underline{\mathbf{B}} , by an elementary method, subject to the condition that the order of σ \sigma is odd. In the case where the order is even, we obtain the corresponding result for the canonical signed basis.



Introduction

Let
X
be the Cartan datum corresponding to
a Kac-Moody algebra
\mathfrak{g}
, and
\mathbf{U}_{q}^{-}
the negative part of
the quantized enveloping algebra
\mathbf{U}_{q}=\mathbf{U}_{q}(\mathfrak{g})
.
Let
\sigma:X\to X
be the diagram automorphism of
X
.
Then
\sigma
induces an algebra automorphism
\sigma:\mathbf{U}_{q}^{-}\to\mathbf{U}_{q}^{-}
.
In the case where
\sigma
is admissible (see 2.1 for the definition),
the pair
(X,\sigma)
induces a Cartan datum
\underline{X}
, which
corresponds to the orbit algebra
\mathfrak{g}^{\sigma}
of
\mathfrak{g}
.
Let
\underline{\mathbf{U}}_{q}^{-}
be the negative part of the quantized enveloping algebra

\underline{\mathbf{U}}_{q}
associated to
\underline{X}
.

In the case where
X
is a symmetric Cartan datum, the canonical basis

\mathbf{B}
of
\mathbf{U}_{q}^{-}
was constructed by Lusztig [L1] by making use of
the geometric theory of quivers.
In [L2], this result was generalized to an arbitrary Cartan datum
\underline{X}
.
The outline of the proof is as follows; for a given Cartan datum
\underline{X}
,
there exists a Cartan datum
X
of symmetric type, and
an admissible diagram automorphism
\sigma:X\to X
such that the Cartan datum induced
from
(X,\sigma)
is isomorphic to
\underline{X}
. Let
\mathbf{B}
be the canonical basis of
\mathbf{U}_{q}^{-}
.
Then
\sigma
gives a permutation of
\mathbf{B}
, and we denote by
\mathbf{B}^{\sigma}
the set
of
\sigma
-fixed elements in
\mathbf{B}
.
In the first step,
by making use of the geometric theory of quivers with automorphisms,
he constructed the canonical signed basis
\widetilde{\underline{\mathcal{B}}}
of
\underline{\mathbf{U}}_{q}^{-}

(see 1.18), and
proved that there exists a natural bijection
\widetilde{\mathbf{B}}^{\sigma}\,\raise 2.0pt\hbox{$\underrightarrow{\sim}$}\,\widetilde{\underline{\mathcal{B}}}
,
where
\widetilde{\mathbf{B}}=\mathbf{B}\sqcup-\mathbf{B}
,
and
\widetilde{\underline{\mathcal{B}}}=\underline{\mathcal{B}}\sqcup-\underline{\mathcal{B}}
for some
basis
\underline{\mathcal{B}}
of
\underline{\mathbf{U}}_{q}^{-}
([L2, Thm. 14.2.3]).
In the second step, he proved the existence of the canonical
basis
\underline{\mathbf{B}}
of
\underline{\mathbf{U}}_{q}^{-}
such that

\widetilde{\underline{\mathcal{B}}}=\underline{\mathbf{B}}\sqcup-\underline{\mathbf{B}}
and constructed a natural bijection

\mathbf{B}^{\sigma}\,\raise 2.0pt\hbox{$\underrightarrow{\sim}$}\,\underline{\mathbf{B}}
([L2, Thm. 14.4.3, 19.2.3]),
by normalizing the sign of
\widetilde{\underline{\mathbf{B}}}
in terms of
the theory of crystals due to Kashiwara [K].

In this paper, we take up a similar problem, but from a different point
of view. This is a generalization of [SZ1,2], where the case
X
is finite or
affine type was discussed. We consider
X
of symmetric type, with an automorphism
\sigma
on
X
,
and
\underline{X}
induced from
(X,\sigma)
as before. Assuming the existence of
the canonical basis
\mathbf{B}
for
\mathbf{U}_{q}^{-}
, we shall construct the
canonical signed basis
\widetilde{\underline{\mathcal{B}}}
of

\underline{\mathbf{U}}_{q}^{-}
and a natural bijection
\widetilde{\mathbf{B}}^{\sigma}\,\raise 2.0pt\hbox{$\underrightarrow{\sim}$}\,\widetilde{\underline{\mathcal{B}}}
in an elementary way,
without appealing the geometric theory, nor the theory of crystal basis.
In the case where the order of
\sigma
is odd, we obtain the canonical basis
\underline{\mathbf{B}}

of
\underline{\mathbf{U}}_{q}^{-}

and a natural bijection
\mathbf{B}^{\sigma}\,\raise 2.0pt\hbox{$\underrightarrow{\sim}$}\,\underline{\mathbf{B}}
.

The main ingredient for our approach is the isomorphism

\Phi:{}_{\mathbf{A}^{\prime}}\underline{\mathbf{U}}_{q}^{-}\,\raise 2.0pt\hbox{$\underrightarrow{\sim}$}\,\mathbf{V}_{q}
as discussed in [SZ1, SZ2], where

X
is assumed to be finite type or affine type.
We generalize those results to the following situation.
Let
X
be a Cartan datum of arbitrary type, with an admissible
automorphism
\sigma
on
X
. Then the pair
(X,\sigma)
induces
a Cartan datum
\underline{X}
. We consider the quantized enveloping algebra

\mathbf{U}_{q}^{-}
(resp.
\underline{\mathbf{U}}_{q}^{-}
) associated to
X
(resp.
\underline{X}
).
Here we assume that the order of
\sigma

is a power of a prime number
p
. Let
\mathbf{A}=\mathbf{Z}[q,q^{-1}]
, and

\mathbf{A}^{\prime}=\mathbf{F}[q,q^{-1}]
, where
\mathbf{F}=\mathbf{Z}/p\mathbf{Z}
is the finite
field of
p
-elements.
Let
{}_{\mathbf{A}}\mathbf{U}_{q}^{-}
be Lusztig’s integral form of
\mathbf{U}_{q}^{-}
,
and set
{}_{\mathbf{A}^{\prime}}\mathbf{U}_{q}^{-}=\mathbf{A}^{\prime}\otimes_{\mathbf{A}}{}_{\mathbf{A}}\mathbf{U}_{q}^{-}
.

\sigma
acts naturally on
{}_{\mathbf{A}^{\prime}}\mathbf{U}_{q}^{-}
, and we denote by

{}_{\mathbf{A}^{\prime}}\mathbf{U}_{q}^{-,\sigma}
the
\sigma
-fixed point subalgebra of
{}_{\mathbf{A}^{\prime}}\mathbf{U}_{q}^{-}
.
The
\mathbf{A}^{\prime}
-algebra
\mathbf{V}_{q}
is defined as
\mathbf{V}_{q}={}_{\mathbf{A}^{\prime}}\mathbf{U}_{q}^{-,\sigma}/J
,
where

such that

\sigma(x)\neq x
(see 3.2). The
\mathbf{A}^{\prime}
-algebra
{}_{\mathbf{A}^{\prime}}\underline{\mathbf{U}}_{q}^{-}

is defined similarly to the case of
\mathbf{U}_{q}^{-}
.
In turn, we give an axiomatic definition of the canonical basis
for
\mathbf{U}_{q}^{-}
(see 1.12) by focussing some properties of the canonical basis
in the symmetric case. It is shown that the canonical basis is
unique if it exists. We show, in Theorem 3.4, that
if the canonical (signed) basis
\mathbf{B}
exists for
\mathbf{U}_{q}^{-}
, then there exists an isomorphism

\Phi:{}_{\mathbf{A}^{\prime}}\underline{\mathbf{U}}_{q}^{-}\,\raise 2.0pt\hbox{$\underrightarrow{\sim}$}\,\mathbf{V}_{q}
of
\mathbf{A}^{\prime}
-algebras.
Moreover, in Theorem 4.18, under the assumption that
p
is odd,
we construct the canonical basis
\underline{\mathbf{B}}
of
\underline{\mathbf{U}}_{q}^{-}
from
\mathbf{B}
,
by making use of this isomorphism,
and show that there exists a natural bijection
\mathbf{B}^{\sigma}\,\raise 2.0pt\hbox{$\underrightarrow{\sim}$}\,\underline{\mathbf{B}}
.
In the case where
p=2
, we only obtain the canonical signed basis
(see Remark 4.19).

Returning to the original problem, we consider
X
of symmetric type, and

\sigma:X\to X
such that
\underline{X}
is induced from
(X,\sigma)
.
In this case, the order of
\sigma
is not necessarily a prime power,
but one can find a sequence

of Cartan data,
and an automorphism
\sigma_{i}:X_{i}\to X_{i}
such that
X_{i+1}
is isomorphic
to the Cartan datum induced from
(X_{i},\sigma_{i})
and that the order of
\sigma_{i}

is a prime power, with
\sigma=\sigma_{k-1}\cdots\sigma_{1}\sigma_{0}
.
In the case where the order of
\sigma
is odd, since
the canonical basis
\mathbf{B}
exists for
X_{0}=X
, by the repeated use of Theorem 4.18,
one can find the canonical basis
\underline{\mathbf{B}}
of
X_{k}=\underline{X}
, and a bijection

\mathbf{B}^{\sigma}\,\raise 2.0pt\hbox{$\underrightarrow{\sim}$}\,\underline{\mathbf{B}}
(Theorem 4.27).
While in the case where the order of
\sigma
is even,
we obtain the corresponding result concerning with the canonical signed basis.

In the course of the proof of Theorem 3.4, one needs to show that
\Phi
is
a homomorphism. In [SZ1], where
X
is finite or affine type,
this was proved by case by case verification,
by making use of PBW-bases. In our case, we can not use PBW-bases. Instead,
in this paper we prove this by a purely combinatorial argument,
in a uniform way. The discussion here is in some sense simpler,
and more transparent than the direct computation in [SZ1].
