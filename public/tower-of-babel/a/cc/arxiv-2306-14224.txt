Long run stochastic control problems with general discounting
Łukasz Stettner

Abstract
Controlled discrete time Markov processes are studied first with long run general discounting functional. It is shown that optimal strategies for average reward per unit time problem are also optimal for average generally discounting functional. Then long run risk sensitive reward functional with general discounting is considered. When risk factor is positive then optimal value of such reward functional is dominated by the reward functional corresponding to the long run risk sensitive control. In the case of negative risk factor we get an asymptotical result, which says that optimal average reward per unit time control is nearly optimal for long run risk sensitive reward functional with general discounting, assuming that risk factor is close to 0 0 . For this purpose we show in Appendix upper estimates for large deviations of weighted empirical measures, which are of independent interest.

Introduction

Let
(X_{n})

(\Omega,F,(F_{n}))
taking values in a locally compact separable metric space
E

\sigma
field
{\cal E}
. The process has transition operator (kernel)
\mathbb{P}^{a}(x,\cdot)
, where
x\in E
and
a\in U
a fixed compact set of control parameters. We shall assume that the mapping
E\times U\ni(x,a)\mapsto\mathbb{P}^{a}(x,D)
is Borel measurable for
D\in{\cal E}
. Markov process is controlled using sequence
V=(a_{0},a_{1},\ldots,a_{n}\ldots)
such that

is

adapted. We denote by

V
and starting at time
k

we simply write
\mathbb{E}_{x}^{V}
. We want to maximize the following long run average generally discounted functional

J_{k,x}^{g}(V)=\liminf_{n\to\infty}\left(\sum_{i=k}^{n+k-1}\phi(i)\right)^{-1}\mathbb{E}_{k,x}^{V}\left[\sum_{i=k}^{n+k-1}\phi(i)c(X_{i-k},a_{i-k})\right],

\phi(0)=1

\phi(n+k)\geq\phi(n)\phi(k)
, for

.
We shall assume furthermore that
\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\phi(k)=\infty
.
Generally we have in mind generalized discount factors such that

\phi(n+k)>\phi(n)\phi(k)
, for
n,k\in\mathbb{N}
.
Typical example for such discount rate is a generalized hyperbolic discount factor of the form
\phi(i)=(1+hi)^{-{r\over h}}
, where

and
{r\over h}\leq 1
. It plays an important role in economics see e.g.
Loew1992
,
Prelec2004
,
Rohde2010
,
Al2006
and references therein. The problems with non-exponential discounting are time inconsistent (see sections 7 and 17 of
Bjork2021
for examples of such problems or
Bau2021
). General hyperbolic discounting was considered in the case of repeated games in
Obara2017
. Finite horizon consumption and portfolio decisions with stochastic

Zoua
. The necessity to use hyperbolic discounting working with market date was shown in
Salois
. It appears that working with far distant futures we have to consider hyperbolic discounting as was noted in the paper
Anch
. Further motivations to study hyperbolic discount one can find in
Pen
. In the paper we shall study long time behaviour of functional (
1
) and the formulated below functional (
2
) with general discounting. We shall compare with the case
\phi\equiv 1

Moreover we assume that the mapping
E\times U\ni(x,a)\mapsto c(x,a)

I_{k,x}^{\gamma,g}(V)=\liminf_{n\to\infty}\left(\gamma\sum_{i=k}^{n+k-1}\phi(i)\right)^{-1}\ln\left(\mathbb{E}_{k,x}^{V}\left[\exp\left\{\gamma\sum_{i=k}^{n+k-1}\phi(i)c(X_{i-k},a_{i-k})\right\}\right]\right)

1

The purpose of the paper is to show that under certain assumptions, usually for the computational purposes, when we make suitable discretizations of the problems, the problems can be fully or partially solved using undiscouted versions of the functionals (
1
) and (
2
).
In the undiscounted case, when
\phi\equiv 1
instead of (
1
) we maximize average reward per unit time functional

J_{x}(V)=\liminf_{n\to\infty}{1\over n}\mathbb{E}_{x}^{V}\left[\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}c(X_{i},a_{i})\right],

(3)

I_{x}^{\gamma}(V)=\liminf_{n\to\infty}{1\over n\gamma}\ln\left(\mathbb{E}_{x}^{V}\left[\exp\left\{\gamma\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}c(X_{i},a_{i})\right\}\right]\right).

(4)

We shall study reward functionals (
1
)-(
2
) comparing them to (
3
)-(
4
) respectively. Our fundamental assumption will be so called uniform ergodicity condition

\sup_{x,x^{\prime}\in E}\sup_{a,a^{\prime}\in U}\sup_{D\in{\cal E}}\mathbb{P}^{a}(x,D)-\mathbb{P}^{a^{\prime}}(x^{\prime},D)=\Delta<1.

(A.1)

The assumption (
A.1

u:E\to U

Another important assumption is a weak continuity of the controlled transition kernels (sometimes called Feller property of the controlled kernels) that is

the\ \ mapping\ \ E\times U\ni(x,a)\mapsto\mathbb{P}^{a}f(x)\ \ is\ \ continuous,

(A.2)

where
\mathbb{P}^{a}f(x)=\int_{E}f(y)\mathbb{P}^{a}(x,dy)
for

- the space of continuous bounded functions on

. Under assumption (

) and (
A.2
) the functional (
3
) was studied in particular in
HerLas1996
(see also the references therein). The functional (

DiMSte1999
and in
DiMSte2000
. Under the above assumptions bounded solutions to so called Bellman equations corresponding to such functionals were obtained. Boundedness of solutions to the Bellman equations plays an important role in the analysis of problems with general discount factor. We are aware of the fact that (
4

Jas2007
using Bellman inequalities,
PitSte2016
for portfolio applications,
WeiChen2021
for risk sensitive nonzero sum games,

1
) or (

We first follow this approach studying suitable Bellman equations for controlled state - time processes. Then we use the results on undiscounted problems to study discounted functionals (
1

DV1

2
Long run average discounted functional problem.

Let

v^{g}(k,x):=\sup_{V}J_{k,x}^{g}(V)

(5)

be the value function of the reward functional (
1
). We have the following Bellman equation corresponding to this functional

w(i,x)=\sup_{a\in U}\left[\phi(i)(c(x,a)-\lambda(i))+\int_{E}w(i+1,y)\mathbb{P}^{a}(x,dy)\right]

(6)

for
i\geq k
.
We are looking for a sequence
(\lambda(i))
and continuous bounded function
w
for which (
6
) is satisfied. For this purpose we introduce the span norm
\|f\|_{sp}:=\sup_{x\in E}f(x)-\inf_{x^{\prime}\in E}f(x^{\prime})
, for
f\in C(E)
and the quotient space
C_{sp}=C(\mathbb{N}\times E)/\penalty\sim
, where for

. We endow the space

with the norm

.
One can show that

with the norm

is a Banach space. We have

Theorem 1
.

Under (
A.1
) and (
A.2
) there are solutions
w\in C_{sp}
and sequence
(\lambda(i))
to the equation (
6
). Moreover

v^{g}(k,x)=\liminf_{n\to\infty}{\sum_{i=k}^{n+k-1}\lambda(i)\phi(i)\over\sum_{i=k}^{n+k-1}\phi(i)}

(7)

and the above limit does not depend on

and
x\in E
. The optimal strategy
\hat{V}
for the reward functional

is of the form
\hat{V}=(\hat{u}_{k}(X_{0}),\hat{u}_{k+1}(X_{1}),\ldots\hat{u}_{k+i}(X_{i}),\ldots)
, where
\hat{u}_{i}

i\geq k
is a Borel measurable selector for which equality on the right hand side of (
6

Proof.

Consider the transformation
\Phi
of the space
C_{sp}
defined as follows for
i\geq k
,
w\in C_{sp}

\Phi w(i,x):=\sup_{a\in U}\left[\phi(i)c(x,a)+\int_{E}w(i+1,y)\mathbb{P}^{a}(x,dy)\right].

(8)

Let
w_{1},w_{2}\in C_{sp}
. For
x,x^{\prime}\in E
,
i\geq k
, by (
A.2
) there are

\Phi w_{1}(i,x)=\phi(i)c(x,a_{1})+\int_{E}w_{1}(i+1,y)\mathbb{P}^{a_{1}}(x,dy)

(9)

\Phi w_{2}(i,x^{\prime})=\phi(i)c(x^{\prime},a_{2})+\int_{E}w_{2}(i+1,y)\mathbb{P}^{a_{2}}(x^{\prime},dy).

(10)

Then by (
9
) and (
10
) we have

\displaystyle\Phi w_{1}(i,x)-\Phi w_{2}(i,x)-(\Phi w_{1}(i,x^{\prime})-\Phi w_{2}(i,x^{\prime}))\leq

\displaystyle\int_{E}(w_{1}(i+1,y)-w_{2}(i+1,y))\left(\mathbb{P}^{a_{1}}(x,dy)-\mathbb{P}^{a_{2}}(x^{\prime},dy)\right)\leq

\displaystyle\sup_{y\in E}(w_{1}(i+1,y)-w_{2}(i+1,y))\left(\mathbb{P}^{a_{1}}(x,H)-\mathbb{P}^{a_{2}}(x^{\prime},H)\right)-

\displaystyle\inf_{y\in E}(w_{1}(i+1,y)-w_{2}(i+1,y))\left(\mathbb{P}^{a_{1}}(x,H^{c})-\mathbb{P}^{a_{2}}(x^{\prime},H^{c})\right)=

\displaystyle\|w_{1}(i+1,\cdot)-w_{2}(i+1,\cdot)\|_{sp}\left(\mathbb{P}^{a_{1}}(x,H)-\mathbb{P}^{a_{2}}(x^{\prime},H)\right)\leq

\displaystyle\|w_{1}(i+1,\cdot)-w_{2}(i+1,\cdot)\|_{sp}\Delta,

(11)

where

comes from the Hahn decomposition of

Therefore we have that

\mid\|\Phi w_{1}-\Phi w_{2}\|\mid\leq\Delta\mid\|w_{1}-w_{2}\|\mid

(12)

and there is a fixed point
w\in C_{sp}
such that
\|w-\Phi w\|_{sp}=0
, which means that there is a sequence
(\lambda(i))
such that
w(i,x)+\lambda(i)\phi(i)=\Phi w(i,x)
for
x\in E
and
i\geq k
. Consequently

and
(\lambda(i))
form a solution to (
6
). Fix

and define
\bar{w}(i,x):=w(i,x)-w(i,x_{0})
, for
i\geq k
and
x\in E
. Since

we therefore have that
\sup_{i\geq k}\sup_{x\in E}|\bar{w}(i,x)|\leq\mid\|w\|\mid<\infty
. Consequently for
\tilde{\lambda}(i)\phi(i):=\lambda(i)\phi(i)+w(i,x_{0})-w(i+1,x_{0})

\bar{w}(i,x)+\tilde{\lambda}(i)\phi(i)=\sup_{a\in U}\left[\phi(i)c(x,a)+\int_{E}\bar{w}(i+1,y)\mathbb{P}^{a}(x,dy)\right]

(13)

and for any control
