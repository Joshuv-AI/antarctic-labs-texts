Abstract
. In their classical paper, Erdős, Goodman and Pósa [ 6 ] studied the representation of a graph with vertex set [ n ] [n] by a family of subsets S 1 , … , S n S_{1},\dots,S_{n} with the property that { i , j } \{i,j\} is an edge if and only if S i ∩ S j ≠ ∅ S_{i}\cap S_{j}\neq\emptyset . In this note, we consider a similar representation of bounded degree r r -uniform hypergraphs and establish some bounds for a corresponding problem.



1.
Introduction

A set
S

represents
an
r
-uniform hypergraph

if there is a family
(S_{v})_{v\in V(G)}
of subsets of

such that for any

\displaystyle\{v_{1},\dots,v_{r}\}\in E(G)\iff\left|\bigcap_{i=1}^{r}S_{v_{i}}\right|\geq 1.

One can observe that any

-uniform hypergraph can be represented by a finite set and similar to
[
6

-uniform hypergraph

that
represents

G

The study of representing graphs (the case where

[

]
.
In

For graphs

. On the other hand, in
[

.
In

, these results were extended to

-uniform hypergraphs.

The related concept of

-represents

-uniform graph

\displaystyle\{v_{1},\dots,v_{r}\}\in E(G)\iff\left|\bigcap_{i=1}^{r}S_{v_{i}}\right|\geq k.

The

-uniform hypergraph

-represents

It may be natural to ask the following question: given a graph

-represents

Lemma 2.1

(Matching Decomposition)

If

\displaystyle E(\mathcal{L})=\{\{e,f\}\subseteq E(G):e\neq f\text{ and }e\cap f\neq\emptyset\}.

.

{r\choose 2}
matchings
M_{i}
with some edge of
M_{i}
intersecting
\{v_{1},\dots,v_{r}\}
in a set of size at least two. Thus,
a_{i}=r
for all but at most
{r\choose 2}
matchings in
I_{2}
and, by
Proposition

2.4
,
a_{i}\geq 2
for the remaining matchings.

Consequently, for every
\{v_{1},\dots,v_{r}\}\notin E(G)
,

(13)

\displaystyle\left|\bigcap_{j=1}^{r}S_{v_{j}}\right|=\sum_{i=1}^{L}\left|\bigcap_{j=1}^{r}R(v_{j},i)\right|

\displaystyle<(1+\varepsilon)\left(\left(L-\tbinom{r}{2}\right)p^{r}t+\tbinom{r}{2}p^{2}t\right).

It remains to show
\left|\cap_{j=1}^{r}S_{v_{j}}\right|<k
. Indeed, for large enough
k
and large enough
L
, the ratio of
\left|\cap_{j=1}^{r}S_{v_{j}}\right|
for a non-edge to an edge is,

\displaystyle\left(\frac{1+\varepsilon}{1-\varepsilon}\right)\frac{(L-\tbinom{r}{2})p^{r}t+\tbinom{r}{2}p^{2}t}{pt}<3\left(L\cdot\frac{1}{4L}+\frac{\binom{r}{2}}{(4L)^{\frac{1}{r-1}}}\right)<\frac{5}{6}.

Thus, for every
\{v_{1},\dots,v_{r}\}\notin E(G)
, in view of
Eq.

13
,

\displaystyle\left|\bigcap_{j=1}^{r}S_{v_{j}}\right|=\sum_{i=1}^{L}\left|\bigcap_{j=1}^{r}R(v_{j},i)\right|

\displaystyle<(1+\varepsilon)\left(\left(L-\tbinom{r}{2}\right)p^{r}t+\tbinom{r}{2}p^{2}t\right)<\frac{5}{6}(1-\varepsilon)pt<k.

Thus,
G
can be
k
-
represented
by the set
S_{1}\cup S_{2}\cup\dots\cup S_{L}
and for
A=577
, we have

\tilde{\theta}(G)\leq\theta_{k}(G)\leq Lt\leq A(r+1)L^{1+\frac{r}{r-1}}\log n=A(r+1)L^{2+\frac{1}{r-1}}\log n.

∎

Proof of Lower Bound

The proof of the lower bound extends the approach used in

.
Fix

n_{0}
is a large enough integer. Assume that
n\geq n_{0}
. Let
\mathcal{H}^{(r)}(n,\Delta)
be the collection of
r
-uniform graphs on the vertex set
[n]
with bounded degree
\Delta
, and let
\mathcal{M}^{(r)}(n)
be the collection of all
almost perfect
matchings of
r
-tuples on
[n]
. Each union of
\Delta
matchings from
\mathcal{M}^{(r)}(n)
is a graph on
[n]

\Delta\leq n
, and consequently,

(14)

\displaystyle|\mathcal{H}^{(r)}(n,\Delta)|\geq{|\mathcal{M}^{(r)}(n)|\choose\Delta}\geq\left(\frac{|\mathcal{M}^{(r)}(n)|}{\Delta}\right)^{\Delta}\geq\left(\frac{|\mathcal{M}^{(r)}(n)|}{n}\right)^{\Delta}.

Claim 3.1
.

For
r\geq 3
and
n\geq n_{0}
,

\displaystyle|\mathcal{M}^{(r)}(n)|\geq\left(\frac{n}{er}\right)^{n/2}

Proof.

Let
n=qr+s
where
0\leq s<r
and
q=\lfloor n/r\rfloor
. We have that
|\mathcal{M}^{(r)}(n)|
is at least

\displaystyle\frac{1}{q!}{n\choose r}{n-r\choose r}\cdots{r+s\choose r}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{q!}\frac{n!}{(r!)^{q}s!}\geq\frac{n!}{(n/r)!(r^{r})^{n/r}r!}.

We use that
n!\geq\sqrt{2\pi n}(n/e)^{n}
and consequently, we have, for
n\geq n_{0}(r)

\displaystyle\frac{n!}{(n/r)!(r^{r})^{n/r}r!}\geq\left(\frac{n}{er}\right)^{n}\frac{\sqrt{2\pi n}}{r^{r}}\frac{1}{(n/r)!}\geq\left(\frac{n}{er}\right)^{n}.\frac{1}{(n/r)!}\geq\left(\frac{n}{er}\right)^{n/2}.

∎

Proof of

1.2
.

In view of

4.
Concluding Remarks

In this note we established upper and lower bounds
on
\tilde{\theta}(G)
that differ by a factor of
O(\Delta^{2})
, i.e.

\Omega(\Delta\log n)\leq\tilde{\theta}(G)\leq O(\Delta^{3}\log n)
.
Closing the gap between these bounds is a problem
of interest.
Further, since the lower bound in
Theorem

1.2

is nonconstructive
it would be interesting to find an explicit construction
that matches or improves our lower bound.
