Existence of solution for Hilfer fractional differential problem with nonlocal boundary condition
Hanan A. Wahash, Mohammed S. Abdo, Satish K. Panchal, Sandeep P. Bhairat

Abstract
. This paper is devoted to study the existence of a solution to Hilfer fractional differential equation with nonlocal boundary condition. We use the equivalent integral equation to study the considered Hilfer differential problem with nonlocal boundary condition. The Mönch type fixed point theorem and the measure of the noncompactness technique are the main tools in this study. We demonstrate the existence of a solution with a suitable illustrative example.

Since

Now, applying

By Lemma

To prove the existence of solutions for the problem at hand, let us make the
following hypotheses.

(H1)

The function

(H2)

f:J_{2}\times E\rightarrow E

(H3)

The inequalities

\displaystyle\mathcal{G}

\displaystyle:

\displaystyle\mathcal{=}\big(\frac{1}{\Gamma(\gamma)}\frac{\left(\Lambda_{q,\mu,\gamma}\right)^{\frac{1}{q}}}{\left(c+d-A\right)}\sum_{k=1}^{m}\frac{\lambda_{k}}{\Gamma(\mu)}(\tau_{k}-a)^{\gamma+\mu-1}

\displaystyle+\big)\frac{1}{\Gamma(\gamma)}\left|\frac{d}{\left(c+d-A\right)}\right|\frac{\left(\Delta_{q,\mu,\gamma}\right)^{\frac{1}{q}}}{\Gamma(1-\gamma+\mu)}+\frac{\left(\Lambda_{q,\mu,\gamma}\right)^{\frac{1}{q}}}{\Gamma(\mu)}\big)(b-a)^{\mu}\big)\left\|\rho\right\|_{L^{p}}<1,

\displaystyle=\big(\frac{m}{\Gamma(\gamma)}\frac{(b-a)^{\gamma-1}}{\left(c+d-A\right)}\sum_{k=1}^{m}\frac{\lambda_{k}(\tau_{k}-a)^{\mu}}{\Gamma(\mu+1)}

\displaystyle\left\|\big((\mathcal{T}z_{n})(t)-(\mathcal{T}z)(t)\big)(t-a)^{1-\gamma}\right\|

\displaystyle+\frac{(t-a)^{1-\gamma}}{\Gamma(\mu)}\int_{a}^{t}(t-s)^{\mu-1}\left\|f(s,z_{n}(s))-f(s,z(s))\right\|ds

An application of Theorem

.
