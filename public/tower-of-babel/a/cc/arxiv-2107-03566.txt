Maximal pronilfactors and a topological Wiener-Wintner theorem
Yonatan Gutman & Zhengxing Lian

Abstract
. For strictly ergodic systems, we introduce the class of CF-Nil( k k ) systems: systems for which the maximal measurable and maximal topological k k -step pronilfactors coincide as measure-preserving systems. Weiss’ theorem implies that such systems are abundant in a precise sense. We show that the CF-Nil ( k ) (k) systems are precisely the class of minimal systems for which the k k -step nilsequence version of the Wiener-Wintner average converges everywhere. As part of the proof we establish that pronilsystems are coalescent both in the measurable and topological categories. In addition, we characterize a CF-Nil ( k ) (k) system in terms of its ( k + 1 ) (k+1) -th dynamical cubespace . In particular, for k = 1 k=1 , this provides for strictly ergodic systems a new condition equivalent to the property that every measurable eigenfunction has a continuous version.
