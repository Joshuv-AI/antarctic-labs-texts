Root Cross Z Z -Complementary Pairs with Large ZCZ Width
Shibsankar Das, Adrish Banerjee, Zilong Liu

Abstract
In this paper, we present a new family of cross Z Z -complementary pairs (CZCPs) based on generalized Boolean functions and two roots of unity. Our key idea is to consider an arbitrary partition of the set { 1 , 2 , ⋯ , n } \{1,2,\cdots,n\} with two subsets corresponding to two given roots of unity for which two truncated sequences of new alphabet size determined by the two roots of unity are obtained. We show that these two truncated sequences form a new q q -ary CZCP with flexible sequence length and large zero-correlation zone width. Furthermore, we derive an enumeration formula by considering the Stirling number of the second kind for the partitions and show that the number of constructed CZCPs increases significantly compared to the existing works.

II
Preliminaries

II-A

Cross
Z
-Complementary Pairs (CZCPs)

Given two
q
-ary sequences
\textbf{{x}}=\big[x_{0},x_{1},\cdots,x_{N-1}\big]
and
\textbf{{y}}=\big[y_{0},y_{1},\cdots,y_{N-1}\big]
of equal length
N
over
\mathbb{Z}_{q}
, their aperiodic cross-correlation function (ACCF) at a time-shift
\tau
is defined by

\rho_{\textbf{{x}},\textbf{{y}}}(\tau):=\left\{\begin{array}[]{cl}\sum\limits_{k=0}^{N-1-\tau}\omega_{q}^{x_{k}-y_{k+\tau}};&\penalty\ 0\leq\tau\leq N-1;\\
\sum\limits_{k=0}^{N-1+\tau}\omega_{q}^{x_{k-\tau}-y_{k}};&\penalty\ -(N-1)\leq\tau<0;\\
0;&\penalty\ \penalty\ \mid\tau\mid\geq N,\end{array}\right.

(1)

where
\omega_{q}=e^{\frac{2\pi\sqrt{-1}}{q}}
and
q\ (\geq 2)
is a positive integer. When the two sequences are identical, i.e.,
\textbf{{x}}=\textbf{{y}}
,
\rho_{\textbf{{x}},\textbf{{y}}}(\tau)
is called an aperiodic auto-correlation function (AACF) of
x
. For simplicity, the AACF of
x
will be written as
\rho_{\textbf{{x}}}(\tau)
.

Definition
1
(
[
1
]
)

Let
(\textbf{{x}},\textbf{{y}})
be a sequence pair with sequence length
N
. For an integer
Z
, let
\mathcal{T}_{1}=\{1,2,\cdots,Z\}
and
\mathcal{T}_{2}=\{N-Z,N-Z+1,\cdots,N-1\}
. Then,
(\textbf{{x}},\textbf{{y}})
is said to be an
(N,Z)
-CZCP if it satisfies the symmetric zero (out-of-phase) AACF sums for the time-shifts over
\mathcal{T}_{1}\cup\mathcal{T}_{2}
and zero ACCF sums for the time-shifts over
\mathcal{T}_{2}
. In short, an
(N,Z)
-CZCP satisfies the following two conditions.

\displaystyle C_{1}:\rho_{\textbf{{x}}}(\tau)+\rho_{\textbf{{y}}}(\tau)=0,\ \forall\ |\tau|\in\mathcal{T}_{1}\cup\mathcal{T}_{2};

\displaystyle C_{2}:\rho_{\textbf{{x}},\textbf{{y}}}(\tau)+\rho_{\textbf{{y}},\textbf{{x}}}(\tau)=0,\ \forall\ |\tau|\in\mathcal{T}_{2}.

(2)

Throughout this paper, an
m\times n
matrix with all zero entries is denoted by
\textbf{O}_{m\times n}
and an
m\times n
matrix with all one entries is denoted by
\textbf{1}_{m\times n}
.

Definition
2

For a given
(N,Z)
-CZCP, the cross
Z
-complementary ratio
(\text{CZC}_{\text{ratio}})
is defined by

\text{CZC}_{\text{ratio}}=Z/Z_{\text{max}}
, where
Z_{\text{max}}
is the theoretical maximum value of
Z
.

Clearly,
\text{CZC}_{\text{ratio}}\leq 1
as
Z\leq Z_{\text{max}}
. In
[
1
]
, it has been shown that
Z_{\text{max}}=N/2
.

Definition
3
(
[
1
]
)

An
(N,Z)
-CZCP is called a perfect
(N,Z)
-CZCP if
Z=\frac{N}{2}
when
N
is an even positive integer.

Clearly, the maximum ZCZ width is achieved for a perfect CZCP. Therefore, they are definitely optimal CZCPs.

II-B

Generalized Boolean Functions (GBFs)

A
\mathbb{Z}_{q}
-valued function from

-linear combination of the

k

x_{1},x_{2},\cdots,x_{n}
refers to a monomial of degree
k
for
n
variables
x_{1},x_{2},\cdots,x_{n}\in\mathbb{Z}_{2}
. For each GBF of
n
-variables, one can associate it with a finite sequence of length
2^{n}
. Let
f(x_{1},x_{2},\ldots,x_{n})
be a GBF and
\mathbf{u}_{i}\equiv(u_{i,1},u_{i,2},\ldots,u_{i,n})
be the binary vector
representation of the index
i
, where
0\leq i\leq 2^{n}-1

i=\sum_{l=1}^{n}u_{i,l}2^{l-1}
. Let
f_{i}=f(\mathbf{u}_{i})=f(u_{i,1},u_{i,2},\ldots,u_{i,n})

\forall\ i
.
The
\mathbb{Z}_{q}
-valued sequence associated with

is denoted by
f
and is defined by
\textbf{{f}}=\big[f_{0},f_{1},\ldots,f_{2^{n}-1}\big]
. Clearly, the sequence

is a

-ary sequence of length

. The complex-valued sequence with

-th roots of unity elements associated with

\psi_{q}(\textbf{{f}})
and defined by
\psi_{q}(\textbf{{f}})=\big[\omega_{q}^{f_{0}},\omega_{q}^{f_{1}},\cdots,\omega_{q}^{f_{2^{n}-1}}\big]
. It is worth mentioning that the elements

’s represent data to be communicated, and the complex-valued sequence

represents the complex modulated sequence with
q
-th roots of unity. We will use the following notation throughout the manuscript,

\rho^{(q)}_{f,g}(\tau)\equiv\rho_{\psi_{q}(\textbf{{f}}),\psi_{q}(\textbf{{g}})}(\tau).

(3)

The truncated sequence corresponding to the GBF

is denoted by
\textbf{{f}}^{(L)}
in which the first and the last

. For example, we consider a binary sequence
\textbf{{f}}=(1100111101)
of length

\textbf{{f}}^{(3)}=(\underbrace{110}0111\underbrace{101})=(0111)
