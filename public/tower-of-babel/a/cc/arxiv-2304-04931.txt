The influence of scalar unparticle on the production of multiple photons in the Randall- Sundrum model


Abstract
The influence of scalar unparticle on the production of multiple photons in the Randall- Sundrum model at muon colliders with the center of mass energy s = 3 − 10 \sqrt{s}=3-10 TeV is evaluated in detail. The results indicate that the total cross-sections depend strongly on the polarization of the muon and antimuon beams, the collision energy s \sqrt{s} , the scaling dimension d U d_{U} of the unparticle operator 𝒪 U \mathcal{O}_{U} and the energy scale Λ U \Lambda_{U} . Numerical evaluation shows that the cross - sections for the production of four - photons at the final state are much larger than the associated production of two - photons with the unparticle under the same conditions.

II
The mixing of Higgs - radion and the scalar unparticle in Randall-Sundrum model

II.1
The mixing of Higgs - radion

The RS model is based on a 5D spacetime with two three-branes: the UV brane and the IR brane. All the SM and dark matter (DM) fields, excepted for gravity, are assumed at IR brane. Gravity lives on the second three-branes. The RS model consider a non-factorizable 5-dimensional metric in the form

S=S_{\text{gravity }}+S_{\mathrm{IR}}+S_{\mathrm{UV}}.

IV
Conclusion

In this paper, the influence of scalar unparticles on production of multiple photons at muon-muon conlliders in the Randall- Sundrum model is studied in detail. The results indicate that the total cross-sections depend strongly on the polarization of the muon and antimuon beams, the collision energy
\sqrt{s}
, the scaling dimension
d_{U}
and the energy scale
\Lambda_{U}
. Numerical evaluation shows that at multi- TeV muon colliders, the cross - sections for the production of four - photons are much larger than the associated production of two- photons with the scalar unparticle at the final state under the same conditions. This is due to the scalar unparticle propagator in the s - channel gives main contribution on the considered processes.

Finally, we note that in this work we have only considered on a theoretical basis, other problems concerning the effect of the unparticle on production of new particles in muon conllisions need to be studied in the future.
