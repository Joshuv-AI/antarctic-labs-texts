On large sieve inequalities involving p p th powers of trigonometric polynomials
S. NORVIDAS

Abstract




Abstract
. In this paper, we extend the large sieve type estimates to sums involving
p
th powers of trigonometric polynomials. An approach to such estimates that does not rely on the usual
L^{2}
-technique is given. Our method is based on comparing the norm and the spectral radius of convolution operators on a normed space of trigonometric polynomials.

1
Introduction

We denote by
T_{N}
the set of trigonometric polynomials of degree at most
N

s(x)=\sum_{k=-N}^{N}c_{k}e^{ikx}

with a positive integer
N
and
c_{k}\in\mathbb{C}
,
k=-N,\dots,N
. Suppose that
x_{1}<x_{2}<\dots<x_{r}
,
r>1
, is any sequence in
\mathbb{T}=(-\pi,\pi]
such that

\min\Bigl\{x_{j+1}-x_{j},j=1,\dots,r-1;\ 2\pi-(x_{r}-x_{1})\Bigr\}=\delta>0.

(1)

The usual large sieve inequality states that

\sum_{j=1}^{r}|s(x_{j})|^{2}\leq\Bigl(\frac{N}{2\pi}+\frac{1}{\delta}\Bigr)\int_{\mathbb{T}}|s(x)|^{2}\,dx.

(2)

See Selberg
[
8
, p. 221]
, but note the different notation. Gallagher
[
4
]
has given a simple derivation of the large sieve inequalities. It turned out that the method of
[
4
]
can be applied to
L^{p}(\mathbb{T})
-norm. More precisely, in
[
3
, p. 96]
was proved that if
s\in T_{N}
and
2\leq p<\infty
, then

\sum_{j=1}^{r}|s(x_{j})|^{p}\leq\Delta_{p}p^{1/2}\max\biggl(N,\,\frac{4\pi}{\delta}\biggr)\Bigl(\sum_{k=-N}^{N}|c_{k}|^{q}\Bigr)^{p/q},

(3)

where
\Delta_{p}
is an absolute constant and
1/p+1/q=1
. Next, in
[
6
, p. 533]
the inequality (3) was extended for all
0<p<\infty
as follows. Let
\Psi
be a convex, nonnegative, and nondecreasing function in
[0,\infty)
. Then for any
s\in T_{N}
,

\sum_{j=1}^{r}\Psi(|s(x_{j})|^{p})\leq\Bigl(\frac{N}{\pi}+\frac{1}{\delta}\Bigr)\int_{\mathbb{T}}\Psi\Bigl(|s(x)|^{p}(p+1)e/2\Bigr)\,dx,

(4)

whenever
-\pi<x_{1}<x_{2}<\dots<x_{r}\leq\pi
and
\delta
is defined by (1). If
\Psi(t)=t
,
0\leq t<\infty
, then (4) implies

\sum_{j=1}^{r}|s(x_{j})|^{p}\leq\Bigl(\frac{N}{\pi}+\frac{1}{\delta}\Bigr)\frac{(p+1)e}{2}\|s\|^{p}_{L^{p}(\mathbb{T})}.

(5)

In
[
5
, p. 164]
the estimate (4) was extended to the case of generalized trigonometric polynomials. In particular, for usual trigonometric polynomials , the inequality (5) was improved as follows:

\sum_{j=1}^{r}|s(x_{j})|^{p}\leq\Bigl(\frac{N+1}{2\pi}+\frac{1}{\delta}\Bigr)\frac{(p+1)e}{2}\|s\|^{p}_{L^{p}(\mathbb{T})}.

(6)

Note that inequalities (2)-(6) are also called forward Marcinkiewicz-Zygmund inequalities (see, e.g.,
[
2
]
).

In this paper, we will develop an approach to inequalities of the type (3) and (5)-(6) that not use the usual
L^{2}
-technique. Our approach is based on the spectral theory of convolution operators on
T_{N}
.

The main result is given in the following theorem. Note that in the sequel,
[x]
denotes the integer part of a positive number
x
. Also we use the notation
\Gamma(\cdot)
for the standard gamma function.

THEOREM 1

.
Let
\{x_{j}\}_{j=1}^{r}
be a sequence in
\mathbb{T}
that satisfies (1). If
s\in T_{N}
and
1\leq p<\infty
, then

\sum_{j=1}^{r}|s(x_{j})|^{p}\leq\frac{pN\sigma(\delta;N)}{2\sqrt{\pi}}\cdot\frac{\Gamma\Bigl(p/2\Bigr)}{\Gamma\Bigl(p/2+1/2\Bigr)}\|s\|^{p}_{L^{p}(\mathbb{T})}

(7)

with

\displaystyle\sigma(\delta;N)=\begin{array}[]{\{}{rl}.\frac{\pi}{N\delta},\ \ \ \ if&\frac{\pi}{N\delta}\in\mathbb{Z},\\
1+\biggl[\frac{\pi}{N\delta}\biggr],&\mbox{otherwise.}\end{array}

If
p
is a positive integer, then the quantity

\frac{\Gamma\Bigl(p/2\Bigr)}{\Gamma\Bigl(p/2+1/2\Bigr)}

can be calculated directly by using the relations between
\Gamma\Bigl(p/2\Bigr)
and
\Gamma\Bigl(p/2+1/2\Bigr)
.

COROLLARY 2

.
Let
l
be a positive integer. Then under the conditions of Theorem 1 it follows that:

(i) if
p=2l
, then

\sum_{j=1}^{r}|s(x_{j})|^{p}\leq\frac{pN\sigma(\delta;N)}{2\sqrt{\pi}}\cdot\frac{\Gamma\Bigl(p/2\Bigr)}{\Gamma\Bigl(p/2+1/2\Bigr)}\|s\|^{p}_{L^{p}(\mathbb{T})}=\frac{pN\sigma(\delta;N)\cdot 2^{l-1}(l-1)!}{\pi\Bigl(1\cdot 3\cdot 5\cdot\cdots\cdot(2l-1)\Bigr)}\|s\|^{p}_{L^{p}(\mathbb{T})};

(10)

(ii) if
p=2l+1
, then

\sum_{j=1}^{r}|s(x_{j})|^{p}\leq\frac{pN\sigma(\delta;N)}{2\sqrt{\pi}}\cdot\frac{\Gamma\Bigl(p/2\Bigr)}{\Gamma\Bigl(p/2+1/2\Bigr)}\|s\|^{p}_{L^{p}(\mathbb{T})}=\frac{pN\sigma(\delta;N)\cdot\Bigl(1\cdot 3\cdot 5\cdot\cdots\cdot(2l-1)\Bigr)}{2^{l+1}\cdot l!}\|s\|^{p}_{L^{p}(\mathbb{T})}.

(11)

COROLLARY 3

.
Assume that
s\in T_{N}
,
\|s\|_{L^{p}(\mathbb{T})}=1
,
p\geq 1
, and
\{x_{j}\}_{j=1}^{r}
satisfies (1).

(i) If
\frac{\pi}{N\delta}\in\mathbb{Z}
, then

\sum_{j=1}^{r}|s(x_{j})|^{p}<\frac{p+1}{\delta}.

(12)

(ii) If
\frac{\pi}{N\delta}\not\in\mathbb{Z}
, then

\sum_{j=1}^{r}|s(x_{j})|^{p}<(p+1)\biggl(\frac{N}{\pi}+\frac{1}{\delta}\biggr).

(13)

For each

PROPOSITION 4

.
Let
u\in L^{q}(\mathbb{T})

(17)

(-1)^{m}\tau_{m}>0,

(18)

Let

). Thus,

v(x)=\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}}a_{k}e^{ikx\pi/N}=\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}}a_{k}\cos\Bigl(\frac{\pi}{N}kx\Bigr)

(24)

with

\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}}|a_{k}|<\infty,

(25)

where

a_{k}=\int_{-N}^{N}v(t)e^{-ikt\pi/N}\,dt=2\int_{0}^{N}v(t)\cos\Bigl(\frac{\pi}{N}kt\Bigr)\,dt.

(26)

We claim that

(-1)^{k}a_{k}>0

for all

. Combining (19) with (25), we see that

a_{k}=-\frac{2N}{\pi k}\int_{0}^{N}v^{\prime}(t)\sin\Bigl(\frac{\pi}{N}kt\Bigr)\,dt=-\frac{2N}{\pi k}\ \sum_{j=0}^{k-1}I_{j},

(28)

where

(-1)^{j}I_{j}>0\hskip 11.74988pt{\text{and}}\hskip 11.74988pt|I_{j}|<|I_{j+1}|

for all

(-1)^{k-1}\sum_{j=0}^{k-1}I_{j}>0.

In light of (27) this proving the claim (26).

For

\tau_{m}=\sum_{j\in\mathbb{Z}}a_{m+2jN}.

(31)

From (24), we see that the series in (30) converges absolutely. Next, from (26) it follows that, for each

Finally, for any

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\widehat{u}(n)}=v(n)=\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}}a_{k}e^{ik\pi n/N}=\sum_{m=-N+1}^{N}\biggl(\sum_{j\in\mathbb{Z}}a_{m+2jN}e^{i(m+2jN)\pi n/N}\Biggr)

.
