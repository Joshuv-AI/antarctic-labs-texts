Abstract
. In this work we present a theorem concerning two convex bodies K 1 , K 2 ⊂ ℝ n K_{1},K_{2}\subset\mathbb{R}^{n} , where n ≥ 3 n\geq 3 , and two families of their sections determined by tangent planes of two spheres S i ⊂ int K i S_{i}\subset\operatorname*{int}K_{i} , i = 1 , 2 i=1,2 . The theorem considers pairs of parallel supporting planes Π 1 \Pi_{1} , Π 2 \Pi_{2} for S 1 S_{1} and S 2 S_{2} , respectively, such that these planes correspond in the following sense: the outer normal vectors of the supporting half-spaces determined by Π 1 \Pi_{1} , Π 2 \Pi_{2} are identical in direction. Under this condition, if the sections Π 1 ∩ K 1 \Pi_{1}\cap K_{1} , Π 2 ∩ K 2 \Pi_{2}\cap K_{2} are translations of each other for every such pair of corresponding planes, then the theorem states: (1) if S 1 S_{1} , S 2 S_{2} have the same radius, then the bodies K 1 K_{1} and K K are translations of each other; (2) if S 1 S_{1} , S 2 S_{2} have distinct radii, then both K 1 K_{1} and K K must be spheres.
