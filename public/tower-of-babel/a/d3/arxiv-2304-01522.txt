High temperature expansion of double scaled SYK
Kazumi Okuyama

Abstract
We study the high temperature (or small inverse temperature β \beta ) expansion of the free energy of double scaled SYK model. We find that this expansion is a convergent series with a finite radius of convergence. It turns out that the radius of convergence is determined by the first zero of the partition function on the imaginary β \beta -axis. We also show that the semi-classical expansion of the free energy obtained from the saddle point approximation of the exact result is consistent with the high temperature expansion of the free energy.

Introduction

The Sachdev-Ye-Kitaev (SYK) model is a very useful toy model for the
study of quantum gravity

Sachdev1993
;
Kitaev1
;
Kitaev2
;
Polchinski:2016xgd
;

At low energy, the SYK model is described by the Schwarzian mode
and it is holographically dual to the Jackiw-Teitelboim gravity

Jackiw:1984je
;
Teitelboim:1983ux
.

One can go beyond the low energy limit
by taking a certain double scaling limit of the SYK model

Cotler:2016fpe
, which we call the DSSYK model in this paper.
The Hamiltonian of the
SYK model is given by the random
p
-body interaction
of
N
Majorana fermions and the DSSYK model is defined by the limit

\displaystyle N,p\to\infty\quad\text{with}\quad\lambda=\frac{2p^{2}}{N}:~\text{fixed}.

(1)

It turns out that the partition function and the correlation functions
of DSSYK model reduce to the computation of the intersection numbers of
chord diagrams.
For instance, the partition function is schematically written as

\displaystyle Z(\beta)=\langle\tr e^{-\beta H}\rangle_{J}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{\beta^{2n}}{(2n)!}\sum_{\text{chord diagrams}}q^{\#\text{intersections}},

(2)

where
\langle\cdots\rangle_{J}
represents the average over the random coupling
J

and

\displaystyle q=e^{-\lambda}.

(3)

This counting problem of chord diagrams
is exactly solvable using the technique of the transfer matrix

Berkooz:2018jqr
.
One can take various limits of the parameters such as
\lambda
and
\beta

to study the bulk dual of DSSYK.
For instance, the semi-classical, small
\lambda
limit of DSSYK
was recently considered in
Goel:2023svz
.

2
Small
\beta
expansion of free energy

As shown in
Berkooz:2018jqr
, the partition function of DSSYK
is given by

\displaystyle Z(\beta)=\langle 0|e^{\beta T}|0\rangle,

(4)

where the transfer matrix
T

q
-deformed oscillator
A_{\pm}

\displaystyle T=A_{-}+A_{+}.

(5)

A_{\pm}

|n\rangle

they create or annihilate the chords

\displaystyle A_{-}|n\rangle=\sqrt{\frac{1-q^{n}}{1-q}}|n-1\rangle,\quad A_{+}|n\rangle=\sqrt{\frac{1-q^{n+1}}{1-q}}|n+1\rangle.

(6)

As shown in
Berkooz:2018jqr
,

T
can be diagonalized by the
q
-Hermite polynomial, from which one can derive
the integral representation of the partition function

\displaystyle Z(\beta)=\int_{0}^{\pi}\frac{d\theta}{2\pi}(q;q)_{\infty}(e^{2\mathrm{i}\theta};q)_{\infty}(e^{-2\mathrm{i}\theta};q)_{\infty}\exp\left(\frac{2\beta\cos\theta}{\sqrt{1-q}}\right),

(7)

q
-Pochhammer symbol

\displaystyle(a;q)_{\infty}=\prod_{n=0}^{\infty}(1-aq^{n}).

(8)

Note that

\beta

\displaystyle Z(-\beta)=Z(\beta).

(9)

From (

\langle 0|T^{2n}|0\rangle

\displaystyle Z(\beta)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{\beta^{2n}}{(2n)!}\langle 0|T^{2n}|0\rangle.

The moment

\displaystyle\langle 0|T^{2n}|0\rangle

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{(1-q)^{n}}\sum_{j=-n}^{n}(-1)^{j}q^{\frac{1}{2}j(j-1)}\binom{2n}{n+j}.

We are interested in small
\beta

\displaystyle\log Z(\beta)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{\beta^{2n}}{(2n)!}k_{2n}(q).

\mathcal{F}_{k}(u)
in (
41
)

\displaystyle F_{k}\left(\frac{2u}{\cos u}\right)=\mathcal{F}_{k}(u).

(45)

To summarize, we find that the semi-classical small
\lambda

expansion obtained from
the saddle point approximation of the exact result is
consistent with the small
\beta
expansion
of free energy in (
16
) after rescaling

\beta\to\beta\lambda^{-\frac{1}{2}}
.
In other words, there is no order-of-limit problem between the small

\lambda
expansion and the small
\beta
expansion
and we find the agreement of
\mathcal{F}_{k}(u)
and
F_{k}(\beta)
in (
45
).

Finally, we comment on the interpretation of
A(1)

we found in section
3
.
The analytic continuation of
\beta
to the imaginary value

\beta=\mathrm{i}t
corresponds to the analytic continuation of

u
to the imaginary value
u=\mathrm{i}s
.
Then the relation (
37
) becomes

\displaystyle t=\frac{2s}{\cosh s}.

(46)

As we increase
t
from zero, this relation
ceases to have a solution above the maximum of the
right hand side.
Thus, it is natural that
A(1)=\max\{2s/\cosh s\}
sets the value of
the radius of convergence when
q=1
,
if we regard the rescaled combination

\beta\sqrt{1-q}
as the expansion parameter.

6
Conclusions and outlook

In this paper, we have studied the high temperature expansion of the free
energy of DSSYK.
We found that the radius of convergence of this expansion is determined by the first
zero of the partition function along the imaginary
\beta
-axis.
We have also computed the first few terms
of the semi-classical expansion of free energy and found that they
are consistent with the small
\beta
expansion at finite
q
.

There are many interesting open questions.
From the analysis of the Padé approximation of the small
\beta

expansion and the numerical evaluation of the exact
Z(\beta)
in
(
7
), we find that there is no Hawking-Page transition for the partition
function of DSSYK and the high and the low temperature regimes are smoothly
connected.
On the other hand, it is speculated that
the UV completion of the SYK model
involves stringy degrees of freedom
Maldacena:2016hyu
;
Goel:2021wim
.
Our result suggests that there is no Hagedorn
growth of the degrees of freedom in DSSYK at high energy.
In fact, the eigenvalue spectrum of the transfer matrix

T
in (
5
) is bounded from above.
It would be interesting to understand the stringy degrees of freedom in
DSSYK better.

In general, we are still lacking a clear understanding of the bulk
spacetime picture of DSSYK.
In particular, we would like to understand the
bulk dual of DSSYK at finite
\lambda
, away from the semi-classical limit.
As discussed in
Avdoshkin:2019trj
,
the moment
\langle 0|T^{2n}|0\rangle
can be expanded as a sum over the Dyck paths
and the limit shape of the Dyck paths are obtained for the case of
the ordinary (not
q
-deformed) oscillators
Avdoshkin:2019trj
.
It would be interesting to find the limit shape of the Dyck paths for the

q
-oscillator case,
which might be related to the Brownian motion of the boundary particle
discussed in
Berkooz:2022mfk
.
We leave this as an interesting future problem.
