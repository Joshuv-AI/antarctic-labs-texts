Abstract
. In this paper, we prove a lemma on logarithmic derivative for holomorphic curves from annuli into Kähler compact manifolds. As its application, a second main theorem for holomophic curves from annuli into semi abelian varieties intersecting with only one divisor is given.

Lemma 3.2

|\xi_{1}(z)|^{2}\leq C.s(z).

Hence, by some simple computations we have

\displaystyle m_{0}(r,\xi_{1})\leq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2\pi}\int\limits_{|z|=r}\log(1+|\xi_{1}|^{2})d\theta+\int\limits_{|z|=\frac{1}{r}}\log(1+|\xi_{1}|^{2})d\theta+O(1)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{4\pi}\int\limits_{|z|=r}\log(1+s(z))d\theta+\frac{1}{4\pi}\int\limits_{|z|=r}\log(1+s(x))d\theta+O(1)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\log\left(1+\frac{1}{2\pi}\int\limits_{|z|=r}sd\theta\right)+\frac{1}{2}\log\left(1+\frac{1}{2\pi}\int\limits_{|z|=\frac{1}{r}}sd\theta\right)+O(1)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\log\left(1+\frac{1}{2\pi r}\dfrac{d}{dr}\int\limits_{1\leq|z|<r}tsdt\wedge d\theta\right)

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{2}\log\left(1+\frac{r}{2\pi}\dfrac{d}{dr}\int\limits_{1\geq|z|>\frac{1}{r}}tsdt\wedge d\theta\right)+O(1)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\log\left(1+\frac{1}{2\pi r}\dfrac{d}{dr}\int\limits_{1\leq|z|<r}f^{*}\Omega\right)+\frac{1}{2}\log\left(1+\frac{r^{3}}{2\pi}\dfrac{d}{dr}\int\limits_{1\geq|z|>\frac{1}{r}}f^{*}\Omega\right)+O(1)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\log\left(1+\frac{1}{2\pi r}(\frac{1}{R_{0}-r}\int\limits_{1\leq|z|<r}f^{*}\Omega)^{2}\right)

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{2}\log\left(1+\frac{r^{3}}{2\pi}(\frac{1}{R_{0}-r}\int\limits_{1\geq|z|>\frac{1}{r}}f^{*}\Omega)^{2}\right)+O(1),

for all
r\in[0;R_{0})
outside an
\Delta_{R_{0}}
-set. Here the last inequality comes from the fact that
\int\limits_{1\leq|z|<r}f^{*}\Omega
and
\int\limits_{1\geq|z|>\frac{1}{r}}f^{*}\Omega
are both monotone increasing functions in
r\in[1;R_{0})
and Lemma
3.2
is applied.
Moreover, we have

\int\limits_{1\leq|z|<r}f^{*}\Omega=r\frac{d}{dr}\int\limits_{1}^{r}\frac{d}{dt}\int\limits_{1\leq|z|<t}f^{*}\Omega\ \text{ and }\ \int\limits_{1\geq|z|>\frac{1}{r}}f^{*}\Omega=r\frac{d}{dr}\int\limits_{1}^{r}\frac{d}{dt}\int\limits_{1\geq|z|>\frac{1}{t}}f^{*}\Omega.

Then we have

\displaystyle\begin{split}m_{0}(r,\xi_{1})&\leq\log^{+}\left(\frac{d}{dr}\int\limits_{1}^{r}\frac{d}{dt}\int\limits_{1\leq|z|<t}f^{*}\Omega\right)+\log^{+}\left(\frac{d}{dr}\int\limits_{1}^{r}\frac{d}{dt}\int\limits_{1\geq|z|>\frac{1}{t}}f^{*}\Omega\right)\\
&\ \ \ +2\log^{+}\frac{1}{R_{0}-r}+O(1)\\
&\leq 2\log^{+}\left(\frac{d}{dr}\int\limits_{1}^{r}\frac{d}{dt}\int\limits_{\frac{1}{t}<|z|<t}f^{*}\Omega\right)+2\log^{+}\frac{1}{R_{0}-r}+O(1)\\
&\leq 2\log^{+}\left(\frac{d}{dr}T_{0,f}(r;\Omega)\right)+2\log^{+}\frac{1}{R_{0}-r}+O(1)\\
&\leq 2\log^{+}\left(\frac{1}{R_{0}-r}T_{0,f}(r;\Omega)\right)^{2}+2\log^{+}\frac{1}{R_{0}-r}+O(1)\\
&=4\log^{+}T_{0,f}(r;\Omega)+4\log^{+}\frac{1}{R_{0}-r}+O(1).\end{split}

(3.5)

Here, the fourth inequality holds because of Lemma
3.2
.

Now, we will estimate
m_{0}(r,\xi_{2})
. By Lemma
3.3
we have

\displaystyle\biggl\|\ m_{0}(r,\xi_{2})=m_{0}\left(\frac{(\varphi\circ f)^{\prime}}{\varphi\circ f}\right)\leq O\left(\log^{+}T_{0}(r,\varphi\circ f)+\log^{+}\frac{1}{R_{0}-r}\right).

(3.6)

Take
\|.\|_{1}
and
\|.\|_{2}
the Hermitian fiber metrics on
L({\mathop{\mathrm{div}}}^{0}(\varphi))
and
L({\mathop{\mathrm{div}}}^{\infty}(\varphi))
respectively. We take
\sigma_{1}\in H^{0}(N,L({\mathop{\mathrm{div}}}^{0}(\varphi)))
and
\sigma_{2}\in H^{0}\left(N,L({\mathop{\mathrm{div}}}^{\infty}(\varphi))\right)
so that
{\mathop{\mathrm{div}}}(\sigma_{1})={\mathop{\mathrm{div}}}^{0}(\varphi)
,
{\mathop{\mathrm{div}}}(\sigma_{2})={\mathop{\mathrm{div}}}^{\infty}(\varphi)
and
|\varphi|=\frac{\|\sigma_{1}\|_{1}}{\|\sigma_{2}\|_{2}}\leq\frac{1}{\|\sigma_{2}\|_{2}}
(because of compactness of
N
, we may suppose that
\|\sigma_{1}\|_{1}\leq 1
and
\|\sigma_{2}\|_{2}\leq 1
). Then we have

\displaystyle\begin{split}m_{0}(r,\varphi\circ f)&=\frac{1}{2\pi}\int\limits_{|z|=r}\log\bigl(1+|\varphi\circ f|^{2}\bigl)d\theta+\frac{1}{2\pi}\int\limits_{|z|=\frac{1}{r}}\log\bigl(1+|\varphi\circ f|^{2}\bigl)d\theta+O(1)\\
&\leq\frac{1}{2\pi}\int\limits_{|z|=r}\log\frac{1}{\|\sigma_{2}\circ f\|_{2}}d\theta+\frac{1}{2\pi}\int\limits_{|z|=\frac{1}{r}}\log\frac{1}{\|\sigma_{2}\circ f\|_{2}}d\theta+O(1)\\
&=m_{0,f}(r,{\mathop{\mathrm{div}}}^{\infty}(\varphi)).\end{split}

(3.7)

On the other hand, we have

\displaystyle\begin{split}N_{0}(r,{\mathop{\mathrm{div}}}^{\infty}(\varphi\circ f))&=N_{0}(r,{\mathop{\mathrm{div}}}(\sigma_{2}\circ f))\\
&=T_{0,f}(r,c_{1}({\mathop{\mathrm{div}}}^{\infty}(\varphi)))-m_{0,f}(r,{\mathop{\mathrm{div}}}^{\infty}(\varphi))+O(1).\end{split}

(3.8)

From (
3.7
), (
3.8
) and by Lemma
3.1
, we have

T_{0}(r,\varphi\circ f)\leq T_{0,f}(r,c_{1}({\mathop{\mathrm{div}}}^{\infty}(\varphi)))+O(1)=O(T_{0,f}(r,\Omega)).

Combining the above inequality with (
3.6
), we get

\displaystyle\biggl\|\ m_{0}(r,\xi_{2})\leq O\left(\log^{+}T_{0,f}(r,\Omega)+\log^{+}\frac{1}{R_{0}-r}\right).

(3.9)

From (
3.4
), (
3.5
) and (
3.9
) we get

\biggl\|\ m_{0}(r,\xi)\leq O(\log^{+}T_{0,f}(r,\Omega))+O\left(\log^{+}\frac{1}{R_{0}-r}\right).

The lemma is proved.
∎

4.
Proof of Second main theorem for holomorphic curves

We have the following lemma from
[
11
]
(which is a special case of
[
10
, Lemma 3.14]
).

Lemma 4.1

(see
[
11
, Lemma 4.4]
)
.

Let
M
be a semi-Abelian variety and let
D
be an algebraic divisor on
M
such that
\mathrm{St}(D)=\{0\}
.
Then there exists a smooth equivariant compactification
\overline{M}
of
M
such that the closure
\overline{D}
of
D
in
\overline{M}
is big, generally positioned.

(c) Proof of Theorem
1.2

Let
\overline{M}
be a smooth equivariant compactification of
M
which is chosen as in Lemma
4.1
. We may regard
f
as a holomorphic curve into

\overline{M}
.

As in the Section
\lx@sectionsign 2
, denote by
\mathcal{M}_{\overline{M}}^{*}
the sheaf of germs of meromorphic functions on
\overline{M}
which do not identically vanish, and the sheaf
\mathcal{U}^{1}_{\overline{M}}
is defined by

\begin{array}[]{ccccc}0\rightarrow{\mathbb{C}}^{*}\rightarrow&\mathcal{M}^{*}_{\overline{M}}&\overset{d\log}{\rightarrow}&\mathcal{U}^{1}_{\overline{M}}&\rightarrow 0.\\
&\gamma&\mapsto&d\log\gamma&\\
\end{array}

Since
M
is a semi-Abelian variety, by taking the standard coordinates from the universal cover
{\mathbb{C}}^{n}\rightarrow M
of
M
which gives automatically sections
\omega_{i}
in
H^{0}({\overline{M}},\mathcal{U}^{1}_{\overline{M}})
, we may assume that

\omega^{i}\in H^{0}({\overline{M}},\mathcal{U}^{1}_{\overline{M}})
for all
1\leq i\leq n
.

We define functions
\xi^{i}
by setting
f^{*}\omega^{i}=\xi^{i}dz
. Then by Lemma
1.1
we have

\biggl\|\ m(r,\xi^{i})\leq O(\log^{+}T_{0,f}(r,\Omega))+O\left(\log^{+}\frac{1}{R_{0}-r}\right),\ \forall 1\leq i\leq n.

If
D
is a divisor on
M
such that
\overline{D}
is generally positioned in
\overline{M}
, by Theorem
3.1
we obtain

\displaystyle\biggl\|\ m(r,\xi^{i})\leq O(\log^{+}T_{0,f}(r,c_{1}(\overline{D})))+O\left(\log^{+}\frac{1}{R_{0}-r}\right),\ \forall 1\leq i\leq n.

(4.2)

Proof of theorem
1.2
.

Without loss of generality we assume that
D
is irreducible. If
\mathrm{St}(D)\not=\{0\}
, by taking the quotient
q:M\rightarrow M/\mathrm{St}(D)
and deal with the holomorphic curve
q\circ f:{\mathbb{A}}(R_{0})\rightarrow M/\mathrm{St}(D)
and the divisor
D/\mathrm{St}(D)
, then we may reduce to the case where
D
is
irreducible and
\mathrm{St}(D)=\{0\}
. Thus we may assume that
D
is irreducible and
\mathrm{St}(D)=\{0\}
.

By Lemma
4.1
, there exists a smooth equivariant compactification
\overline{M}
of
M
, in which
\overline{D}
is generally positioned and big.

Let
J_{k}(f):{\mathbb{A}}(R_{0})\rightarrow J_{k}(\overline{M},\log\partial M)\cong\overline{M}\times{\mathbb{C}}^{nk}
be the
k
-jet lifting of
f
. We have the following claim from
[
11
, Claim 6.1]

Claim 4.3
.

There exists a number
k_{0}
such that

\displaystyle\pi_{2}\big(J_{k_{0}}(\overline{D},\log\partial M)\big)\cap\pi_{2}(\overline{J_{k_{0}}f({\mathbb{A}}(R_{0}))}^{\mathrm{Zar}})\not=\pi_{2}(\overline{J_{k_{0}}f({\mathbb{A}}(R_{0}))}^{\mathrm{Zar}}),

where
\overline{J_{k_{0}}f({\mathbb{A}}(R_{0}))}^{\mathrm{Zar}}
is the Zariski closure of
J_{k_{0}}f({\mathbb{A}}(R_{0}))
in
J_{k}(\overline{M},\log\partial M)
.

Since the proof of this claim is just follow the proof of
[
11
, Claim 6.1]
with the same lines, we will omit its proof here.

Let
\{U_{\alpha}\}
be an affine open covering of
\overline{M}

such that

L(\overline{D})|_{U_{\alpha}}\cong U_{\alpha}\times{\mathbb{C}}.

(4.4)

We take
\sigma\in H^{0}(\overline{M},L(\overline{D}))
so that
{\mathop{\mathrm{div}}}(\sigma)=\overline{D}
and take local holomorphic functions
\sigma_{\alpha}=\sigma|_{U_{\alpha}}
given by the trivialization (
4.4
).

We fix a Hermitian metric
\|\cdot\|
in
L(\overline{D})
and choose positive smooth functions
h_{\alpha}
on
U_{\alpha}
such that

\dfrac{1}{\|\sigma(x)\|}=\dfrac{h_{\alpha}(x)}{|\sigma_{\alpha}(x)|},\qquad x\in U_{\alpha}.

By Claim
4.3
, there exists a polynomial
R(w)
in variable

w=(w_{lk})\in\pi_{2}\left(\overline{J_{k_{0}}(f)({\mathbb{A}}(R_{0}))}^{\text{Zar}}\right)\cong{\mathbb{C}}^{nk_{0}}

such that

\displaystyle\pi_{2}\left(J_{k_{0}}(\overline{D},\log\partial M)\right)\cap\pi_{2}\left(\overline{J_{k_{0}}(f)({\mathbb{A}}(R_{0}))}^{\text{Zar}}\right)

\displaystyle\subset\left\{w\in\pi_{2}\left(\overline{J_{k_{0}}(f)({\mathbb{A}}(R_{0}))}^{\text{Zar}}\right)|\ R(w)=0\right\}

\displaystyle\not=\pi_{2}\left(\overline{J_{k_{0}}(f)({\mathbb{A}}(R_{0}))}^{\text{Zar}}\right).

Then we have an equation on every
U_{\alpha}\times\pi_{2}\left(\overline{J_{k_{0}}(f)({\mathbb{A}}(R_{0}))}^{\text{Zar}}\right)
of the form:

\displaystyle{}b_{\alpha 0}\sigma_{\alpha}+b_{\alpha 1}d\sigma_{\alpha}+\cdots+b_{\alpha k_{0}}d^{k_{0}}\sigma_{\alpha}=R(w),

(4.5)

where
b_{\alpha i}
are jet differentials on
U_{\alpha}
.
Therefore, in every
U_{j}\times\pi_{2}\left(\overline{J_{k_{0}}(f)({\mathbb{A}}(R_{0}))}^{\text{Zar}}\right)
, we have

\dfrac{1}{\|\sigma\|}=\dfrac{1}{|R|}\dfrac{h_{\alpha}}{|\sigma_{\alpha}|}=\dfrac{1}{|R|}\left|h_{\alpha}b_{\alpha 0}+h_{\alpha}b_{\alpha 1}\dfrac{d\sigma_{\alpha}}{\sigma_{\alpha}}+\cdots+h_{\alpha}b_{\alpha k_{0}}\dfrac{d^{k_{0}}\sigma_{\alpha}}{\sigma_{\alpha}}\right|.

Choose relatively compact open subsets
U^{\prime}_{\alpha}
of
U_{\alpha}
so that

\cup_{\alpha}U^{\prime}_{\alpha}=\overline{M}
. For every
\alpha
, there exist
positive constants
C_{\alpha}
such that

h_{\alpha}|b_{\alpha i}|\leq\sum_{\text{finite}}h_{\alpha}|b_{\alpha ilk\beta_{lk}(x)}|\cdot|w_{lk}|^{\beta_{lk}}\leq C_{\alpha}\sum_{\text{finite}}|w_{lk}|^{\beta_{lk}}

for all
x\in U^{\prime}_{\alpha}
.

By making
C_{\alpha}
larger if necessary, there exists
d_{\alpha}>0
such that for
f(z)\in U^{\prime}_{\alpha}
we have

h_{\alpha}(f(z))|b_{\alpha i}(J_{k_{0}}(f)(z))|\leq C_{\alpha}\left(1+\sum_{\underset{1\leq k\leq k_{0}}{1\leq l\leq n}}\left|w_{lk}\circ J_{k_{0}}(f)(z)\right|\right)^{d_{\alpha}}.

Setting
\xi_{(k)}=(w_{1k}(J_{k}(f)),\cdots,w_{2k}(J_{k}(f)))
and

\xi_{l(k)}=w_{lk}(J_{k}(f))
, for
f(z)\in U^{\prime}_{\alpha}
we have

\dfrac{1}{\|\sigma(f(z))\|}\leq\dfrac{1}{|R(\xi_{(1)}(z),\cdots,\xi_{(k_{0})}(z))|}\sum_{j=1}^{N}C_{\alpha}\left(1+\sum_{\underset{1\leq k\leq k_{0}}{1\leq l\leq n}}|\xi_{l(k)}(z)|\right)^{d_{\alpha}}

\quad\quad\quad\quad\times\left(1+\left|\dfrac{d\sigma_{\alpha}}{\sigma_{\alpha}}(J_{1}(f)(z))\right|+\cdots+\left|\dfrac{d^{k_{0}}\sigma_{\alpha}}{\sigma_{\alpha}}\left(J_{k_{0}}(f)(z)\right)\right|\right).

This implies that

\displaystyle m_{0,f}(r;\overline{D})

\displaystyle=\dfrac{1}{2\pi}\int_{0}^{2\pi}\log^{+}\dfrac{1}{\|\sigma(f(re^{i\theta})\|}d\theta+\dfrac{1}{2\pi}\int_{0}^{2\pi}\log^{+}\dfrac{1}{\|\sigma(f(\frac{1}{r}e^{i\theta})\|}d\theta+O(1)

\displaystyle\leq m_{0}\left(r,\dfrac{1}{R(\xi_{(1)}(z),\cdots,\xi_{(k_{0})}(z))}\right)

(4.6)

\displaystyle+O\biggl(\sum_{\underset{1\leq k\leq k_{0}}{1\leq l\leq n}}\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{0}^{2\pi}\bigl(\log^{+}|\xi_{l(k)}(re^{i\theta})|+\log^{+}|\xi_{l(k)}(\frac{1}{r}e^{i\theta})|\bigl)d\theta

\displaystyle+\sum_{\underset{1\leq k\leq k_{0}}{1\leq\leq n}}\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{0}^{2\pi}\bigl(\log^{+}\bigl|\dfrac{d^{k}\sigma_{\alpha}}{\sigma_{\alpha}}(J_{k}(f)(re^{i\theta})\bigl|+\log^{+}\bigl|\dfrac{d^{k}\sigma_{\alpha}}{\sigma_{\alpha}}(J_{k}(f)+(\frac{1}{r}e^{i\theta})\bigl)\bigl|d\theta\biggl)+O(1).

By Lemma
1.1
and by (
4.2
) we have

\displaystyle\biggl\|\ m_{0}\left(r,\dfrac{d^{k}\sigma_{j}\circ f}{\sigma_{j}\circ f}\right)=O(\log^{+}T_{0,f}(r;c_{1}(\overline{D})))+O\left(\log^{+}\frac{1}{R_{0}-r}\right).

(4.7)

Combining Lemma
1.1
, (
4.6
) and (
4.7
) we obtain

\displaystyle\biggl\|\quad m_{0,f}(r;\overline{D})=O(\log^{+}T_{0,f}(r;c_{1}(\overline{D})))+O\left(\log\frac{1}{R_{0}-r}\right).

(4.8)

We next estimate the counting function
N_{0}(r,f^{*}D)
.
We see that for all
z\in{\mathbb{A}}(R_{0})
,

{\mathrm{ord}}_{z}f^{*}D>k\Leftrightarrow J_{k}(f)(z)\in J_{k}(\overline{D},\log\partial M).

Then, from (
4.5
) we have

{\mathrm{ord}}_{z}f^{*}D-\min\{{\mathrm{ord}}_{z}f^{*}D,k_{0}\}\leq{\mathrm{ord}}_{z}{\mathop{\mathrm{div}}}_{0}(R(\xi_{(1)},\cdots,\xi_{(k_{0})})).

Thus

\displaystyle N_{0}(r,f^{*}D)-N^{[k_{0}]}_{0}(r,f^{*}D)\leq N_{0}(r,{\mathop{\mathrm{div}}}_{0}R(\xi_{(1)},\cdots,\xi_{(k_{0})})).

(4.9)

By the first main theorem and by Lemma
1.1
, we have

\displaystyle\|\ N_{0}(r,{\mathop{\mathrm{div}}}_{0}R(\xi_{(1)},\cdots,\xi_{(k_{0})}))

\displaystyle\leq T_{0}(r,R(\xi_{(1)},\cdots,\xi_{(k_{0})}))+O(1)

\displaystyle\leq O\left(\sum_{\underset{1\leq k\leq k_{0}}{1\leq l\leq n}}T_{0}(r,\xi_{l(k)})\right)+O(1)

\displaystyle=O\left(\sum_{\underset{0\leq k\leq k_{0}-1}{1\leq l\leq n}}m_{0}(r,\xi_{l(k)})\right)+O(1)

\displaystyle=O(\log^{+}T_{0,f}(r;c_{1}(\overline{D})))+O\left(\log^{+}\frac{1}{R_{0}-r}\right).

(4.10)

By (
4.8
), we have

\displaystyle T_{0,f}(r;c_{1}(\overline{D}))\leq N_{0}(r,f^{*}D)+O(1).

(4.11)

Combining (
4.9
), (
4.10
) and (
4.11
), we obtain

\biggl\|\ T_{0,f}(r;c_{1}(\overline{D}))\leq N^{[k_{0}]}(r,f^{*}D)+O(\log^{+}T_{0,f}(r;c_{1}(\overline{D})))+O\left(\log^{+}\frac{1}{R_{0}-r}\right).

The theorem is proved.
