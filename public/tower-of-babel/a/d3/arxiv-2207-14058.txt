The decay η ′ → π ​ ρ \eta^{\prime}\to\pi\rho in the chiral NJL model
M. K. Volkov 1, A. A. Pivovarov 1, K. Nurlan 1,2,3

Abstract
The branching fraction of the decay η ′ → π ​ ρ \eta^{\prime}\to\pi\rho is calculated in the chiral quark NJL model. The decay exists due to the mass difference between u u - and d d -quarks leading to π 0 − η ′ \pi^{0}-\eta^{{}^{\prime}} mixing. Two different approaches are applied: the approximate calculation of this process taking into account the transition π 0 − η ′ \pi^{0}-\eta^{{}^{\prime}} explicitly without dioganalisation and a more precise approach consisting in the diagonalisation of the singlet and octet states leading to the physical fields π 0 \pi^{0} , η \eta and η ′ \eta^{{}^{\prime}} . The obtained results are in satisfactory agreement with current experimental data.
