A Sheaf Cohomology Restriction Formula on toric complete intersections
Zhentao Lyu

Abstract
. We prove a sheaf cohomology restriction (SCORE) formula for a class of vector bundles on complete intersections in toric varieties. The formula enables one to compute cohomology products on the complete intersection X X via computations on the ambient space V V and potentially compute certain quantum corrections to the classical sheaf cohomology ring. Schematically the formula reads ( s 1 , … , s r ) X = ( s 1 , … , s r , g ) V (s_{1},...,s_{r})_{X}=(s_{1},...,s_{r},g)_{V} with g g being an explicitly described quantity derived from the monad data of the bundle.

Introduction

For a compact Kähler manifold
X
and a holomorphic vector bundle
\mathcal{E}
,
we are interested in studying the
polymology

H_{\mathcal{E}}^{*}(X):=\bigoplus_{p,q}H^{q}(X,\wedge^{p}\mathcal{E}^{*}),

where
\mathcal{E}^{*}
is the dual of
\mathcal{E}
. This is an associative algebra
[
3
]
. In the special case when
\mathcal{E}
is the tangent bundle
T_{X}
, it is the familiar cohomology of differential forms. The polymology, or classical sheaf cohomology ring, has been studied on toric varieties
[
3
]
and Grassmannians
[
5
]
, particularly motivated by the efforts to mathematically understand the physics theory of A/2-twisted gauge linear sigma models introduced by Witten
[
13
]
. See also

[
7
,
10
,
11
,
8
,
9
,
2
,
6
,
12
,
4
]
for some physics developments. The mathematical endeavor is to define quantum sheaf cohomology
QH^{*}_{\mathcal{E}}(X)
for
\mathcal{E}
with special properties, which is a “quantum correction” of
H^{*}_{\mathcal{E}}(X)
. Mathematically this has only been done on toric varieties
[
3
]
, while
[
11
]
works out a quantum restriction formula for toric complete intersections via localization techniques in physics. They motivate our study of
H^{*}_{\mathcal{E}}(X)
on hypersurfaces and complete intersections in toric varieties as well as its quantum corrections.

Let
X
be a smooth hypersurface in a smooth projective variety
V
. Consider vector bundles
\mathcal{E}^{*}_{X}
and
\mathcal{E}^{*}_{V}
on
X
and
V
respectively, with the requirement that they fit in the short exact sequence (SES)

(1)

0\to\mathcal{O}_{X}(-X)\xrightarrow{J}\mathcal{E}_{V}^{*}|_{X}\to\mathcal{E}^{*}_{X}\to 0.

We would like to compute the cohomology product

c_{X}:H^{1}(\mathcal{E}^{*}_{X})\times...\times H^{1}(\mathcal{E}^{*}_{X})\to H^{n-1}(\wedge^{n-1}\mathcal{E}^{*}_{X})

and relate it to

c_{V}:H^{1}(\mathcal{E}^{*}_{V})\times...\times H^{1}(\mathcal{E}^{*}_{V})\to H^{n}(\wedge^{n}\mathcal{E}^{*}_{V}).

Let
\sigma_{i}\in H^{1}(\mathcal{E}^{*}_{V}),i=1,...,n
, denote the image
c_{V}(\sigma_{1},...,\sigma_{n})
by
\langle\sigma_{1},...,\sigma_{n}\rangle_{V}
.
If we have a restriction map

\begin{array}[]{rll}H^{1}(\mathcal{E}^{*}_{V})&\to&H^{1}(\mathcal{E}^{*}_{X})\\
\sigma&\mapsto&\bar{\sigma}\end{array},

we would like to have a sheaf cohomology restriction (SCORE) formula in the form

(2)

\langle\bar{\sigma}_{1},...,\bar{\sigma}_{n-1}\rangle_{X}=\langle\sigma_{1},...,\sigma_{n-1},[\mathcal{E}^{*}_{X}]\rangle_{V},

with some class
[\mathcal{E}^{*}_{X}]\in H^{1}(\mathcal{E}^{*}_{V})
.

As an example, note that in the case when
\mathcal{E}^{*}_{V}
is the cotangent bundle
\Omega_{V}
, let
D_{1},...,D_{r}
be divisors of
V
, and they restrict to divisors on
X\subset V
.
Then the intersection of
D_{1}|_{X},...,D_{r}|_{X}
can be computed by

(3)

([D_{1}|_{X}],...,[D_{r}|_{X}])_{X}=([D_{1}],...,[D_{r}],[X])_{V}.

In this paper, we prove a SCORE formula for the case where
V
is an
n
dimensional smooth projective toric variety and
\mathcal{E}^{*}_{V}
is a small ‘toric’ deformation of the cotangent bundle, and
X
is a smooth complete intersection, of codimension
m
. We make use of a deformed version of the toric Euler sequence of the cotangent bundle, so that both
\mathcal{E}^{*}_{V}
and
\mathcal{E}^{*}_{X}
can be expressed in monads with terms splitting into direct sums of line bundles. This enables us to make sense of the restriction map, the comparison between
H^{n-m}(\wedge^{n-m}\mathcal{E}^{*}_{X})
and
H^{n}(\wedge^{n}\mathcal{E}^{*}_{V})
, as well as the precise form of the class
[\mathcal{E}^{*}_{X}]
inserted in the SCORE formula.
The main theorem is stated as follows:

Main Theorem

(Theorem
4.1
)
.

Let
V
be a smooth projective toric variety of dimension
n
. Let
Y_{k},k=1,...,m(m\leq n-3)
be smooth hypersurfaces in
V
defined by hyperplane setions
f_{k}\in H^{0}(V,\mathcal{O}(H_{k}))
, such that every
X_{k}:=Y_{1}\cap...\cap Y_{k}
is smooth. Let the vector bundles
\mathcal{E}^{*}_{X_{k}}
be small deformations of the cotangent bundle
\Omega_{X_{k}}
defined by the middle cohomology of (
30
) such that
H^{1}(\mathcal{E}^{*}_{X_{k}})\cong W
. Then for
\sigma_{i}\in H^{1}(\mathcal{E}^{*}_{X_{m}}),i=1,...,n-m
, their cohomology product can be computed on the ambient space
V
via the following SCORE formula, with
\gamma_{k}\in W
determined by the presentation of
\mathcal{E}^{*}_{X_{k}}
as in (
31
).

(4)

\langle\sigma_{1},\sigma_{2},...,\sigma_{n-m}\rangle_{X}=\langle\sigma_{1},\sigma_{2},...,\sigma_{n-m},\gamma_{1},...,\gamma_{m}\rangle_{V}.

The contents of the rest of the paper are as follows: In Section
2
, we set up the hypersurface case in detail and state a SCORE formula in this case. In Section
3
we prove the hypersurface case SCORE formula after introducing two double complexes than contains all the relevant information, by doing various diagram chases. In Section
4
we state and prove the Main Theorem, and prove it using induction. We set up the assumptions of the theorem in a way so that the proof of the induction step is completely analogous to the hypersurface case, with the only extra work (proof of Proposition
4.2
) being the check of the vanishing of some sheaf cohomology groups. We conclude with a remark explaining that our assumption on
H^{1}(\mathcal{E}^{*}_{X_{k}})
in the Main Theorem is not a strong constraint.
