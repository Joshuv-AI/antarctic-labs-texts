Role of IR-Improvement in Precision LHC/FCC Physics and in Quantum Gravity
S.A. Yost

Abstract
IR-improvement based on amplitude-level resummation allows one to control unintegrable results in quantum field theory with arbitrary precision in principle. We illustrate such resummation in specific examples in precision LHC and FCC physics and in quantum gravity.

\frac{1}{1-x}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}x^{n}.

(1)

Here the result of the summation is an analytic result that is well defined except for a simple pole at
x=1

x=1

One is accordingly invited, as a next step, to resum series that are already being summed to try to improve ones knowledge of the represented function.

Continuing in this way, we write for the Feynman series for a process the following:

\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}C_{n}\alpha_{s}^{n}\begin{cases}&=F_{\rm RES}(\alpha_{s})\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}B_{n}\alpha_{s}^{n},\;\text{\rm EXACT}\\
&\cong G_{\rm RES}(\alpha_{s})\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\tilde{B}_{n}\alpha_{s}^{n},\;\text{\rm APPROX.}\end{cases}

(2)

6
Summary

What we can see is that the resummation of the IR regime of quantum field theory, coupled with exact results to a given order, when done at the level of the amplitude in an exact
re-arrangement of the original Feynman series, has wide applicability for precision phenomenology. We have illustrated the range of this applicability from the current precision LHC physics to the futuristic FCC precision physics program and reliable estimates for quantum gravity by one of us (BFLW). The future of particle physics is intimately interwoven with continued progress on IR-improved quantum field theoretic predictions, when taken together with control of the attendant collinear and UV limits.
