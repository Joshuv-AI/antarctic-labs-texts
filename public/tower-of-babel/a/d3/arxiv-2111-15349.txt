Let
\phi_{1},\phi_{2}\colon G\to[0,1]
be integrable functions on a unimodular locally compact group
G
with
G=G_{0}
(that is,
G
is connected).

(1)

One has
\phi_{1}*\phi_{2}\geq\|\phi_{1}\|+\|\phi_{2}\|-m(G)
.

(2)

If
\|\phi_{1}\|+\|\phi_{2}\|-m(G)\geq 0
holds, then one has
\|f_{t}\circ(\phi_{1}*\phi_{2})\|=\|\phi_{1}\|\|\phi_{2}\|-tm(G)
for any
0\leq t\leq\|\phi_{1}\|+\|\phi_{2}\|-m(G)
.

Proof.

(1)

For any
g\in G
,

\displaystyle\phi_{1}*\phi_{2}(g)=\int_{G}\phi_{1}(g^{\prime})\phi_{2}(g^{\prime-1}g)dg^{\prime}.

One has

\displaystyle\phi_{1}(g^{\prime})\phi_{2}(g^{\prime-1}g)

\displaystyle=\phi_{1}(g^{\prime})+\phi_{2}(g^{\prime-1}g)-1+(1-\phi_{1}(g^{\prime}))(1-\phi_{2}(g^{\prime-1}g))

\displaystyle\geq\phi_{1}(g^{\prime})+\phi_{2}(g^{\prime-1}g)-1

by
\phi_{1}(g^{\prime}),\phi_{2}(g^{\prime-1}g)\leq 1
, and hence

\displaystyle\int_{G}\phi_{1}(g^{\prime})\phi_{2}(g^{\prime-1}g)dg^{\prime}\geq\int_{G}(\phi_{1}(g^{\prime})+\phi_{2}(g^{\prime-1}g)-1)dg^{\prime}=\|\phi_{1}\|+\|\phi_{2}\|-\mathrm{vol}(G).

Since
m(G)=\mathrm{vol}(G)
holds by
G=G_{0}
and Remark
2.4

(3)
, we obtain

\displaystyle\phi_{1}*\phi_{2}(g)=\int_{G}\phi_{1}(g^{\prime})\phi_{2}(g^{\prime-1}g)dg^{\prime}\geq\|\phi_{1}\|+\|\phi_{2}\|-\mathrm{vol}(G)=\|\phi_{1}\|+\|\phi_{2}\|-m(G).

(2)

Since
f_{t}\circ(\phi_{1}*\phi_{2})=\phi_{1}*\phi_{2}-t
holds by
(1)
, we obtain

\displaystyle\|f_{t}\circ(\phi_{1}*\phi_{2})\|=\|\phi_{1}*\phi_{2}-t\|=\|\phi_{1}\|\|\phi_{2}\|-tm(G)

by Lemma
2.2
.
∎

By Lemma
3.1
, the inequality (
1.4
) does not hold for any
0<t<\|\phi_{1}\|+\|\phi_{2}\|-m(G)
when

is connected and (
1.1
) does not hold.
In fact,

\displaystyle\|f_{t}\circ(\phi_{1}*\phi_{2})\|=\|\phi_{1}\|\cdot\|\phi_{2}\|-tm(G)=(\|\phi_{1}\|-t)(\|\phi_{2}\|-t)+t(\|\phi_{1}\|+\|\phi_{2}\|-m(G)-t)

holds by Lemma
3.1

(2)
and hence we have
(\|\phi_{1}\|-t)(\|\phi_{2}\|-t)<\|f_{t}\circ(\phi_{1}*\phi_{2})\|
.
