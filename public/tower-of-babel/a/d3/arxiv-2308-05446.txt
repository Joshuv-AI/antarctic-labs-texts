1.1.
Structural assumptions

In (
1.1
), the integrand
F\colon\mathds{R}^{N\times n}\to\mathds{R}
satisfies

(1.7)

\displaystyle\begin{cases}\ F\in C^{2}_{\operatorname{loc}}(\mathds{R}^{N\times n})\\[2.15277pt]
\ \Lambda^{-1}\lvert z\rvert^{p}\leqslant F(z)\leqslant\Lambda\lvert z\rvert^{p}\\[2.15277pt]
\ \lvert\partial^{2}F(z)\rvert\leqslant\Lambda\lvert z\rvert^{p-2}\\[2.15277pt]
\ \lvert\partial^{2}F(z_{1})-\partial^{2}F(z_{2})\rvert\leq\mu\left(\displaystyle\frac{\lvert z_{2}-z_{1}\rvert}{\lvert z_{2}\rvert+\lvert z_{1}\rvert}\right)\left(\lvert z_{1}\rvert^{2}+\lvert z_{2}\rvert^{2}\right)^{\frac{p-2}{2}}\end{cases}

for all
z\in\mathds{R}^{N\times n}
,
\Lambda\geqslant 1
being a positive absolute constant and
\mu\colon[0,\infty)\to[0,1]
being a concave nondecreasing function with
\mu(0)=0
. In the rest of the paper we will always assume
p\geq 2
. In order to derive meaningful regularity results, we need to update (
1.6
) to the stronger strict quasiconvexity condition

(1.8)

\displaystyle\int_{B}\left[F(z+D\varphi)-F(z)\right]\ \,{\rm d}x\geqslant\lambda\int_{B}(\lvert z\rvert^{2}+\lvert D\varphi\rvert^{2})^{\frac{p-2}{2}}\lvert D\varphi\rvert^{2}\,{\rm d}x,

holding for all
z\in\mathds{R}^{N\times n}
and
\varphi\in W^{1,p}_{0}(B,\mathds{R}^{N})
, with
\lambda
being a positive, absolute constant. Furthermore, we allow the integrand
F(\cdot)
to be degenerate elliptic in the origin. More specifically, we assume that

features degeneracy of

-Laplacian type at the origin, i. ​e.

(1.9)

\displaystyle\left|\ \frac{\partial F(z)-\partial F(0)-\lvert z\rvert^{p-2}z}{\lvert z\rvert^{p-1}}\ \right|\to 0\,\qquad\text{as}~\lvert z\rvert\to 0\,,

which means that we can find a function
\omega\colon(0,\infty)\to(0,\infty)
such that

(1.10)

\displaystyle\lvert z\rvert\leqslant\omega(s)\ \Longrightarrow\ \lvert\partial F(z)-\partial F(0)-\lvert z\rvert^{p-2}z\rvert\leqslant s\lvert z\rvert^{p-1},

for every
z\in\mathds{R}^{N\times n}
and all

. Moreover, the right-hand side term

in (

\displaystyle f\in L^{m}(\Omega,\mathds{R}^{N})\quad\mbox{with}\ \ 2>m>\begin{cases}\ 2n/(n+2)\quad&\mbox{if}\ \ n>2\\[2.15277pt]
\ 3/2\quad&\mbox{if}\ \ n=2,\end{cases}

\displaystyle f\in W^{1,p}(\Omega,\mathds{R}^{N})^{*}\qquad\mbox{and}\qquad m^{\prime}<2^{*}\leqslant p^{*}.
