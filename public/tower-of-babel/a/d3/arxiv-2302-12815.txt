Generalized divisor functions in arithmetic progressions: II
D. T. Nguyen

Abstract
. We obtain a new bound on the second moment of modified shifted convolutions of the generalized 3-fold divisor function, and show that, for applications, the modified version is sufficient.



1.
Introduction

Let
\zeta^{3}(s)=\sum_{n}\tau_{3}(n)n^{-s},\Re(s)>1
. Determining full asymptotic for the shifted convolutions

(1.1)

\sum_{1\leq n\leq N}\tau_{3}(n)\tau_{3}(n+h)

for various ranges of
h
is an important problem in number theory in the past hundred years and is still wide open. We believe, however, that (
1.1
) is too strong for applications.
What we mean by that is the following. Suppose
\mathbf{(\mathrm{\textbf{A}})}
and
\mathbf{(\mathrm{\textbf{C}})}
are two statements, possibly conjectures, with
\mathbf{(\mathrm{\textbf{A}})}\implies\mathbf{(\mathrm{\textbf{C}})}
. We say that
\mathbf{(\mathrm{\textbf{A}})}
is “too strong” for
\mathbf{(\mathrm{\textbf{C}})}
if there exists a statement
\mathbf{(\mathrm{\textbf{B}})}
such that, (i),
\mathbf{(\mathrm{\textbf{B}})}
is easier to prove than
\mathbf{(\mathrm{\textbf{A}})}
, and, (ii), the following diagram of implications

(1.2)

{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\mathbf{(\mathrm{\textbf{A}})}}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\mathbf{(\mathrm{\textbf{B}})}}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\mathbf{(\mathrm{\textbf{C}})}}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\smallsetminus}

holds. Thus, we propose the following modified weaker correlation sum

(1.3)

\sum_{n\leq X-h}\tau_{3}(n)\tau_{3}(n+h),

where the length of the sum depends on the shift
h
. We show that this sum (
1.3
) is close to its expected value in an
L^{2}
sense, and that this is enough for certain problems.

More precisely, we prove, with a power-saving error term, that the second moment of (
1.3
), namely

(
B
)

\sum_{h<X}\left(\sum_{n\leq X-h}\tau_{3}(n)\tau_{3}(n+h)-\mathrm{MT}(X,h)\right)^{2}\ll X^{3-1/100},

is small, for a certain explicit main term
\mathrm{MT}(X,h)
. The main tool used in the proof is a trigonometric method of I. M. Vinogradov.

Secondly, as an application of the above bound (

), we obtain the full asymptotic for the variance of the ternary 3-fold divisor function in arithmetic progressions, averaged over all residue classes (not necessarily coprime) and moduli: There exist computable numerical constants
c_{0},\dots,c_{8}
such that

(
C
)

\sum_{q\leq X}\sum_{1\leq a\leq q}\left(\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}n\leq X\\
n\equiv a(q)\end{subarray}}\tau_{3}(n)-\mathrm{MT}(X;q,a)\right)^{2}=X^{2}(c_{8}\log^{8}X+\cdots+c_{0})+O\left(X^{2-1/300}\right),

for some explicit main term
\mathrm{MT}(X;q,a)
. This result refines a related conjecture (see conjecture
1.1
below) about the leading order asymptotic of a similar variance and improves a previously known upper bound by the author.

Quantities of the form (

[
4
]

[
24

\sum_{1\leq q\leq N^{1/2}(\log N)^{-B}}\max_{y\leq N}\max_{(a,q)=1}\left|\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}1\leq n\leq y\\
n\equiv a(q)\end{subarray}}\Lambda(n)-\frac{y}{\varphi(y)}\right|\ll N(\log N)^{-A}

where

is the von Mangoldt function and

with

\tau_{k}(n)

[
19
]
and
\tau_{2}(n)^{2}

[
16

, where

is the

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\tau_{k}(n)n^{-s}=\zeta^{k}(s),\Re(s)>1
. Around the same time, Barban
[

[

(1963-1964), Davenport-Halberstam
[

[

\sum_{1\leq q\leq N(\log N)^{-B}}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}1\leq a\leq q\\
(a,q)=1\end{subarray}}\left|\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}1\leq n\leq N\\

giving a much wider range for

Barban-Davenport-Halberstam type inequalities have many applications in number theory. For instance, a version of this inequality (with

In 1970, Montgomery

(a,q)=1\end{subarray}}\left|\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}1\leq n\leq N\\

Conjecture 1.1

In
[

[

obtained a lower bound of the right order of magnitude for the average of this variance. In

The smoothed asymptotic (

We note that the coprimity condition

In summary, we obtain in this paper a new upper bound for the second moment of the error term of the modified shifted convolution of

2.2.
Outline of the proofs

We follow the approach of Motohashi
[

To control the error term, we prove an analog of Lavrik’s result for

\sum_{1\leq n\leq N-h}\tau_{3}(n)\tau_{3}(n+h)

To evaluate the main term, we proceed slightly different from Motohashi due to some complications involving an exponential sum in three variables. We show that the resulting sum can be evaluated, on average, thanks to an orthogonality property of the Ramanujan’s sums.
