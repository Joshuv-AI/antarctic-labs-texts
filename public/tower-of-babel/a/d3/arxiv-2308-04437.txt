Lemma 1.2
.

Assume

\displaystyle\sum_{i=0}^{r}(-1)^{i}\frac{2^{2i-1}}{r+i}\binom{r+i}{r-i}\binom{2i}{k}

Using Zeilberger’s algorithm we find the sum satisfies the following recursion:

\displaystyle\rm{SUM}[1+r]=-\frac{(k+2r)(12+k+2r)}{(-2+k-2r)(-1+k-2r)}\rm{SUM}[r]

Lemma 2.2
.

\displaystyle\sum_{j=1}^{i}(-1)^{j}2^{2j-1}\frac{2i-1}{2j-1}\binom{i+j-2}{2j-2}

Lemma 2.3
.

Assume
i\geq j\geq 2
, then

\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{i}(-1)^{k}2^{2k}\frac{2i-1}{2k-1}\binom{i+k-2}{2k-2}\binom{k}{j-1}

\displaystyle=(-1)^{i}2^{2j-2}\frac{2i^{2}-2i+j-1}{(2j-3)(j-1)}\binom{i+j-3}{i-j+1}

Note, for

The response is: If

\displaystyle-(i+j-2)(2i^{2}+2i+j-1)S(i)-(i-j+2)(2i^{2}-2i+j-1)S(i+1)

\displaystyle=-8(j-2)(j-1)\binom{1}{j-1}

Let

\displaystyle f(i)

\displaystyle=(-1)^{i}2^{2j-2}\frac{2i^{2}-2i+j-1}{(2j-3)(j-1)}\binom{i+j-3}{i-j+1}

\displaystyle-(i+j-2)(2i^{2}+2i+j-1)f(i)-(i-j+2)(2i^{2}-2i+j-1)f(i+1)+8(j-2)(j-1)\binom{1}{j-1}

\displaystyle=\frac{8}{\pi}\sin(j\pi)

Hence the formula satisfies the recursion for the sum and has the correct initial value.

Lemma 5.3
.

For

\displaystyle\sin(n\theta)

\displaystyle\cos(n\theta)

\displaystyle=\sum_{r=0}^{\lfloor(n)/2\rfloor}(-1)^{r}\binom{n}{2r}\cos^{n-2r}(\theta)\sin^{2r}(\theta)

Proof.

Note that it is equivalent to say

\displaystyle\sin(n\theta)

\displaystyle=\sum_{r=0}^{n}(-1)^{r}\binom{n}{2r+1}\cos^{n-2r-1}(\theta)\sin^{2r+1}(\theta)

\displaystyle\cos(n\theta)

\displaystyle=\sum_{r=0}^{n}(-1)^{r}\binom{n}{2r}\cos^{n-2r}(\theta)\sin^{2r}(\theta)

Since, in the equation for
\sin(n\theta)
,
r>\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor\implies\binom{n}{2r+1}=0
since then
2r+1>n
and
r\in\mathbb{Z}
, similarly for
\cos(n\theta)
. The proof then procedes by induction. True for
n=1
. Assume true for
n
and show for
n+1
.

\displaystyle\sin((n+1)\theta)

\displaystyle=\sin(n\theta+\theta)

\displaystyle=\sin(n\theta)\cos(\theta)+\cos(n\theta)\sin(\theta)

\displaystyle=\sum_{r=0}^{n+1}(-1)^{r}\binom{n+1}{2r}\cos^{n-2r}(\theta)\sin^{2r+1}(\theta)
