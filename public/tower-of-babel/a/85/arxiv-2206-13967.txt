The study of odd coloring of graphs was recently initiated by Petruševski and Škrekovski
[
19
]
,
as a relaxation of conflict-free coloring in the literature, which was first studied for hypergraphs. A
coloring of vertices of a hypergraph is

a unique color, see
[
12
]

[
5

studied the conflict-free coloring of graphs. More specifically,
a graph G has a conflict-free coloring if for any vertex
v\in V(G)
, there is a color occurs exactly once
in
N(v)
. Conflict-free colorings have been widely studied, see
[
15
,
16
,
20
]
. The odd coloring
of hypergraphs was introduced by Cheilaris et al.
[
6
]
, which requires each edge in

has some
color that occurs odd number of times on its vertices. This problem has been studied in
[

13

A number of interesting results about structures and parameters of 1-planar graphs has been obtained in recent years. Fabrici and Madaras
