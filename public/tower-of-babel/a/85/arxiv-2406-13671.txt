Topological Equivalence Theorem and Double-Copy for Chern-Simons Scattering Amplitudes
Yan-Feng Hang, Hong-Jian He, Cong Shen 1,

Abstract
We study the mechanism of topological mass-generation for 3d Chern-Simons gauge theories and propose a brand-new Topological Equivalence Theorem to connect scattering amplitudes of the physical gauge boson states to that of the transverse states under high energy expansion. We prove a general energy cancellation mechanism for N N -point physical gauge boson amplitudes, which predicts large cancellations of E 4 − L → E ( 4 − L ) − N \hskip 0.85358ptE^{4-L}\!\!\rightarrow\!E^{(4-L)-N}\! at any L L -loop level ( L ⩾ 0 L\!\geqslant\!0 ). We extend the double-copy approach to construct massive graviton amplitudes and study their structures. We newly uncover a series of strikingly large energy cancellations E 12 → E 1 E^{12}\!\!\rightarrow\!E^{1} of the tree-level four-graviton scattering amplitude under high energy expansion and establish a new correspondence between the two energy cancellations in the topologically massive Yang-Mills gauge theory and the topologically massive gravity theory. We further study the scattering amplitudes of Chern-Simons gauge bosons and gravitons in the nonrelativistic limit. Journal-ref: Research 6 (2023) 0072

3. Formulation of Topological Equivalence Theorem

The CS action from Eq.(
1
) is gauge-invariant, and using the method of Refs.
[

]
[
6

\langle 0|\hskip 0.85358pt\mathcal{F}^{a_{1}}(p_{1})\cdots\mathcal{F}^{a_{\hskip-0.85358ptN}}(p_{\hskip-0.85358ptN})\hskip 0.85358pt\Phi\hskip 0.85358pt|0\rangle\,=\,0\,,\vskip-2.84526pt

(6)

where
\,\mathcal{F}^{a}(p_{j})\!=\!-\text{i}p^{\mu}_{j}\!A^{a}_{\mu}

and the symbol
\Phi
denotes
any other on-shell physical fields after the
Lehmann-Symanzik-Zimmermann (LSZ) amputation. Since the function
\mathcal{F}^{a}
contains only
one single gauge field
A_{\mu}^{a}
,
it is straightforward to amputate each external

\hskip 0.85358pt\mathcal{F}^{a}
line by the LSZ reduction, where we impose the
on-shell condition
\,p_{j}^{2}\!=\!-m^{2}\hskip 0.85358pt

for each external line. From Eq.(
3a
) and the relation

\hskip 0.85358pt\epsilon_{\text{S}}^{\mu}\!=\epsilon_{\text{L}}^{\mu}\hskip-1.42262pt-v^{\mu}

with the residual term

v^{\mu}\!=\!\mathcal{O}(m/E)
,
we deduce the following identity:

\epsilon_{\text{S}}^{\mu}\!=\!\sqrt{2}\hskip 0.85358pt\epsilon_{\text{P}}^{\mu}-\!(\epsilon_{\text{T}}^{\mu}\hskip-0.85358pt+v^{\mu}),\,

(7)

Using Eqs.(
4c
) and (
7
),
we can reexpress the gauge-fixing function

as follows:

\mathcal{F}^{a}(p)\!=\!-\text{i}\sqrt{2}\hskip 0.85358ptm(A^{a}_{\text{P}}\!-\Omega^{a})\hskip 0.85358pt,\hskip 8.53581pt\Omega^{a}\!=\!\mbox{$\frac{\,{1}\,}{\sqrt{2\,}\,}$}(A^{a}_{\text{T}}\!+\!v^{a})\hskip 0.85358pt,

(8)

where
\,v^{a}\!=\!v^{\mu}A^{a}_{\mu}
.
Making the LSZ reduction on Eq.(
