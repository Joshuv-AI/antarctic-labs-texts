Mass Spectroscopy and Decay Properties of Ξ c ​ b \Xi_{cb} , Ξ b ​ b \Xi_{bb} Baryons Spectroscopy study of doubly heavy baryon alongwith the properties as magnetic moment, Radiative decay.
AMEE KAKADIYA, CHANDNI MENAPARA and AJAY KUMAR RAI

Abstract
Using the Hypercentral Constituent Quark Model (hCQM), the mass spectra of doubly heavy baryons Ξ c ​ b \Xi_{cb} and Ξ b ​ b \Xi_{bb} are determined. The model describes an interaction inside the baryonic system. Screened potential has been considered as confining potential with color-Coulomb potential to enumerate the masses of baryonic states. Regge trajectories have been plotted in ( J , M 2 ) (J,M^{2}) plane. The properties like magnetic moment and radiative decay width have been determined using the obtained results.

Theoretical Framework

The inter-quark interaction has been described using the Hypercentral Constituent Quark Model (hCQM) wherein the internal effects are parametrized by higher constituent quark mass. The Jacobi coordinates are employed to account for the three body interactions as
[
36
,
38
,
37
,
39
,
40
,
41
,
42
,
44
,
43
]
,

\vec{\rho}=\frac{\vec{r_{1}}-\vec{r_{2}}}{\sqrt{2}}\hskip 14.22636ptand\hskip 14.22636pt\vec{\lambda}=\frac{\vec{r_{1}}+\vec{r_{2}}-2\vec{r_{3}}}{\sqrt{6}}.

(1)

The hypercentral coordinates (hyperradias
x
and hyperangle
\xi
) in terms of Jacobi coordinates can be expressed as
[
45
]
,

x=\sqrt{\rho^{2}+\lambda^{2}}\hskip 14.22636ptand\hskip 14.22636pt\xi=arctan\left(\frac{\rho}{\lambda}\right)

(2)

The Hamiltonian, presenting the three quark bound system is
[
45
]
,

H=\frac{P^{2}}{2m}+V(x)

(3)

Here,
P
is conjugate momentum and
m
is the reduced mass of the system, which expressed as,
m=\frac{2m_{\rho}m_{\lambda}}{m_{\rho}+m_{\lambda}}
; where,
m_{\rho}=\frac{2m_{1}m_{2}}{m_{1}+m_{2}}
and
m_{\lambda}=\frac{2m_{3}(m_{1}^{2}+m_{2}^{2}+m_{1}m_{2})}{(m_{1}+m_{2})(m_{1}+m_{2}+m_{3})}

[
46
]
. Here,
m_{1}
,
m_{2}
,
m_{3}
are the masses of the constituent quarks:
m_{u}=m_{d}=0.344GeV
,
m_{c}=1.275GeV
, and
m_{b}=4.670GeV
. As the hypercentral model itself suggests,
V(x)
is non-relativistic interaction potential inside the baryonic system depending only on hyperradius x (falls in two terms i.e. spin dependent (
V_{SD}
) and spin independent (
V_{SI}
) potential term
[
48
,
47
,
36
]
).

V(x)=V_{SD}(x)+V_{SI}(x)

(4)

The expression of kinetic energy operator for the three quark system is
[
45
]
,

\frac{P{{{}_{x}}^{2}}}{2m}=-\frac{\hbar^{2}}{2m}(\Delta_{\rho}+\Delta_{\lambda})=-\frac{\hbar^{2}}{2m}\left(\frac{\partial^{2}}{\partial x^{2}}+\frac{5}{x}\frac{\partial}{\partial x}+\frac{L^{2}(\Omega)}{x^{2}}\right)

(5)

Here,
L^{2}(\Omega)
is the Grand angular operator.

The screened potential is incorporated as confining potential with the color-Coulomb potential (spin independent potential
V_{SI}(x)=V_{conf}(x)+V_{Col}(x)
)
[
49
,
50
]
.

V_{conf}(x)=a\left(\frac{1-e^{-{\mu}x}}{\mu}\right)\hskip 14.22636ptand\hskip 14.22636ptV_{Col}(x)=-\frac{2}{3}\frac{\alpha_{s}}{x}

(6)

where,
a
is the string tension, the constant
\mu
(0.07) is the the screening factor,
x
indicates the inter-quark separation and the parameter
\alpha_{s}
corresponds to the strong running coupling constant
[
37
]
.

The spin dependent part of potential
V_{SD}(x)
is
[
51
]
,

V_{SD}(x)=V_{SS}(x)(\vec{S_{\rho}}\cdot\vec{S_{\lambda}})+V_{\gamma S}(x)(\vec{\gamma}\cdot\vec{S})+V_{T}(x)\left[S^{2}-\frac{3(\vec{S}\cdot\vec{x})(\vec{S}\cdot\vec{x})}{x^{2}}\right]

(7)

which includes spin-spin, spin-orbit and tensor term respectively. The mass spectra of
\Xi_{cb}
and
\Xi_{bb}
baryons calculated by solving the Schrödinger equation in Mathematica notebook

and
\Xi_{bb}
baryons are calculated, which include all possible hyperfine states presented in Table
1

\Xi_{cb}

[

\mu_{B}=\sum_{q}\langle\Phi_{sf}|\hat{\mu}_{qz}|\Phi_{sf}\rangle;\quad\quad q=u,d,b

m_{q}^{eff}=m_{q}\left(1+\frac{\langle H\rangle}{\sum_{q}m_{q}}\right)

\langle H\rangle=M-\sum_{q}m_{q}

M

m_{q}^{eff}

m_{p}
