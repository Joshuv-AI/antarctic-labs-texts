Abstract
Reaction processes in muon catalyzed fusion ( μ \displaystyle\mu CF), ( d ​ t ​ μ ) J = v = 0 → α + n + μ + 17.6 ​ MeV \displaystyle(dt\mu)_{J=v=0}\to\alpha+n+\mu+17.6\,{\rm MeV}\> or ( α ​ μ ) n ​ l + n + 17.6 ​ MeV \displaystyle\;(\alpha\mu)_{nl}+n+17.6\,{\rm MeV} in the D-T mixture was comprehensively studied by Kamimura, Kino and Yamashita [Phys. Rev. C 107 , 034607 (2023)] by solving the d ​ t ​ μ \displaystyle dt\mu - α ​ n ​ μ \displaystyle\alpha n\mu coupled channel (CC) Schrödinger equation under a boundary condition where the muonic molecule ( d ​ t ​ μ ) J = v = 0 \displaystyle(dt\mu)_{J=v=0} was set as the initial state and the outgoing wave was in the α ​ n ​ μ \displaystyle\alpha n\mu channel. We approximate this CC framework and propose a considerably more tractable model using the T \displaystyle T -matrix method based on the Lippmann-Schwinger equation. Nuclear interactions adopted in the T \displaystyle T -matrix model are determined by reproducing the cross section of the reaction d + t → α + n + 17.6 ​ MeV \displaystyle d+t\to\alpha+n+17.6\,{\rm MeV} at low energies. The cross section of the strong-coupling rearrangement reaction is presented in a simple closed form based on our new model. This T \displaystyle T -matrix model have reproduced most of the calculated results on the above μ \displaystyle\mu CF reaction reported by Kamimura et al. (2023) and is applicable to other μ \displaystyle\mu CF systems such as ( d ​ d ​ μ ) \displaystyle(dd\mu) , ( t ​ t ​ μ ) \displaystyle(tt\mu) , ( d ​ t ​ μ ) ∗ \displaystyle(dt\mu)^{*} , ( d ​ d ​ μ ) ∗ \displaystyle(dd\mu)^{*} .

INTRODUCTION

To ensure that nuclear fusion occurs within nuclear distance (a few fms)
the Coulomb barrier between two nuclei must be overcome, which typically
requires very high temperatures.
At low temperatures, negative muons injected into a mixture of
deuterium (D) and tritium (T) can catalyze the fusion reaction

\qquad\;\;d+t\rightarrow\alpha+n+17.6\;{\rm MeV},

(1)

Following the catalyzed reaction, free muons can

\displaystyle\mu

The fusion of the muonic molecule
\displaystyle dt\mu

\displaystyle dd\mu

\displaystyle tt\mu

Recently, the

beam by utilizing
\displaystyle\mu

VI
Conclusion

Recently, a comprehensive study of the
\displaystyle\mu
CF reaction
(1.2) was performed in Ref.
Kamimura:2021msf

by solving two coupled channel (CC) Schrödinger equations
for the reactions (1.1) and (1.2).
In the present study, we have proposed a considerably more tractable
\displaystyle T
-matrix
model to simulate the CC model
Kamimura:2021msf
by considering
scenarios i) – v):

i) We reproduced the low-energy cross sections of reaction (1.1)
by using an optical-potential (OP) model (Fig. 2 and Table I).
The effect of the
\displaystyle\alpha
-
\displaystyle n
channel is considered to be
included

using the imaginary part of the optical potential.

ii) The exact
\displaystyle T
-matrix
for the reaction (1.1) was approximated by
replacing the exact CC wave function with the OP-model
wave function obtained in i).
The cross section of the reaction (1.1) was expressed by the simple
closed form (3.10) based on our model and reproduced the observed
cross section by properly
selecting
the
\displaystyle dt
-
\displaystyle\alpha n
coupling potential
(Fig. 4 and
Table IV)
.

iii) We calculated
the
\displaystyle(dt\mu)_{J=v=0}
molecular wave function including the OP-model

\displaystyle d
-
\displaystyle t
potential determined in i).

iv) We approximated the full
\displaystyle T
-matrix in Ref. [5] for the reaction (1.2)
as follows:
We replaced the CC wave function for this reaction with the
muonic
\displaystyle(dt\mu)_{J=v=0}
wave function
obtained
in iii).

v) Using the approximated
\displaystyle T
-matrix in iv), we calculated the reaction rates
to the
\displaystyle\alpha
-
\displaystyle\mu
continuum and the bound states,
fusion rates of reaction (1.2),
\displaystyle\alpha
-
\displaystyle\mu
sticking probability,
and momentum and energy spectra of muons emitted by
reaction (1.2). Most of the results obtained in Ref. [5] were well reproduced.

All the calculated results were insensitive to 20 sets of

\displaystyle d
-
\displaystyle t
potential and
\displaystyle dt
-
\displaystyle\alpha n
coupling potential.
In practical calculations,
the use of only a few sets is sufficient.

Thus, the proposed tractable
\displaystyle T
-matrix model was constructed, such that
it reproduced most of the results obtained in Ref.
Kamimura:2021msf
.
This model is applicable to other
\displaystyle\mu
CF systems such as
\displaystyle(dd\mu)
,

\displaystyle(tt\mu)
,
\displaystyle(dt\mu)^{*}
, and
\displaystyle(dd\mu)^{*}
.
