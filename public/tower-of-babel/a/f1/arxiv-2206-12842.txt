5.
Final conclusion

Let us mention in this section that the choice of the

-pairs
\{a,ak\}
with

is not random. In fact, the idea is to investigate irregular

-quadruples, which contain the pairs
\{a,ka\}
, for any positive integer

In view of the recent work done by the second author in
[
5
]
, it appears that if

i.e.

For

it is easy to check from equation (

) that there is no

-pair of the form

. On the other hand, for

whose positive solutions are defined by

, where

and

denote the

-pair

should be skipped since it has already been studied (see

]
,
[
10
]
). In short, it remains to study

, which is the aim of this paper.
