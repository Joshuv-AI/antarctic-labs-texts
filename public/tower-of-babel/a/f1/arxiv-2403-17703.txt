Residual finiteness of fundamental n \mathchar 29038 -quandles of links
Neeraj Kumar Dhanwani, Deepanshi Saraf, Mahender Singh

Abstract
. In this paper, we investigate residual finiteness and subquandle separability of quandles. The existence of these finiteness properties implies the solvability of the word problem and the generalised word problem for quandles. We prove that the fundamental n \mathchar 29038 -quandle of any link in the 3-sphere is residually finite for each n ≥ 2 \mathchar 29038\mathchar 12821\mathchar 28722 . This supplements the recent result on residual finiteness of link quandles and the classification of links whose fundamental n \mathchar 29038 -quandles are finite for some n \mathchar 29038 . We also establish several general results on these finiteness properties and give many families of quandles admitting them.
