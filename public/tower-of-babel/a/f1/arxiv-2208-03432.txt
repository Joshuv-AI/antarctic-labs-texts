Introduction

In this study, we investigate the global existence of solutions for a non-Newtonian incompressible fluid governed by the following system:

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{l}\vskip 5.69054ptu_{t}-{\rm div}\,\big(S(Du)\big)+\nabla p=-{\rm div}(u\otimes u),\qquad{\rm div}\,u=0\mbox{ in }{\mdmathbb R}^{n}_{+}\times(0,\infty),\\
