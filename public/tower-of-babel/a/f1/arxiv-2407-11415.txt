. We are devoted to the study of the following nonlinear p p -Laplacian Schrödinger equation with L p L^{p} -norm constraint { − Δ p ​ u = λ ​ | u | p − 2 ​ u + | u | r − 2 ​ u in Ω , u = 0 on ∂ Ω , ∫ Ω | u | p ​ d x = a , \displaystyle\begin{cases}&-\Delta_{p}u=\lambda|u|^{p-2}u+|u|^{r-2}u\quad\mbox{in}\quad\Omega,\\ &u=0\quad\mbox{on}\quad\partial\Omega,\\ &\int_{\Omega}|u|^{p}dx=a,\end{cases} where Δ p u = div ( | ∇ u | p − 2 ∇ u ) \Delta_{p}u=\text{div}(|\nabla u|^{p-2}\nabla u) , Ω ⊂ ℝ N \Omega\subset\mathbb{R}^{N} is an exterior domain with smooth boundary ∂ Ω ≠ ∅ \partial\Omega\neq\emptyset satisfying that ℝ N ∖ Ω \mathbb{R}^{N}\setminus\Omega is bounded, N ≥ 3 N\geq 3 , 2 ≤ p < N 2\leq p<N , p < r < p + p 2 N p<r<p+\frac{p^{2}}{N} , a > 0 a>0 and λ ∈ ℝ \lambda\in\mathbb{R} is an unknown Lagrange multiplier. 1  Introduction

In this paper we investigate the following nonlinear
p
-Laplacian Schrödinger equation with
L^{p}
-norm constraint

(1.1)

\displaystyle\begin{cases}&-\Delta_{p}u=\lambda|u|^{p-2}u+|u|^{r-2}u\quad\mbox{in}\quad\Omega,\\
&u=0\quad\mbox{on}\quad\partial\Omega,\\
&\int_{\Omega}|u|^{p}dx=a,\end{cases}

where
\Delta_{p}u=\text{div}(|\nabla u|^{p-2}\nabla u)
,
\Omega\subset\mathbb{R}^{N}
is an exterior domain with smooth boundary

satisfying that
\mathbb{R}^{N}\setminus\Omega
is bounded,
N\geq 3
,

,
p<r<p_{c}
with
p_{c}=p+\frac{p^{2}}{N}
,

and
\lambda\in\mathbb{R}
is an unknown Lagrange multiplier.

The research motivation of (
1.1
) arises from seeking the stationary solutions for reaction diffusion problems which appears in biophysics, plasma physics, and chemical reaction design. The prototype equation for these
models can be written in the form

u_{t}=-\Delta_{p}u+\lambda|u|^{p-2}u+|u|^{r-2}u,

where
u
generally stands for a concentration, the term
-\Delta_{p}u
corresponds to the diffusion with coefficient and
\lambda|u|^{p-2}u+|u|^{r-2}u
represents the reaction term related to source and loss processes, see
[
18
]
.

When
\Omega=\mathbb{R}^{N}
, we can rewrite equation (
1.1
) as follows

(1.2)

\displaystyle\begin{cases}&-\Delta_{p}u=\lambda|u|^{p-2}u+|u|^{r-2}u\quad\mbox{in}\quad\mathbb{R}^{N},\\
&\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}|u|^{p}dx=a.\end{cases}

There is a amount of research of solutions for this problem. It is well know that under the
L^{p}
-norm constraint, by the Gagliardo-Nirenberg inequality, there appears a
L^{p}
-critical exponent
p_{c}
. For
L^{p}
-subcritical growth nonlinearity, the problem of seeking solution of problem (
1.2
) can be reformulated as finding the global minimum of a variational functional on a constraint. However, when
the nonlinearity is
L^{p}
-supcritical growth, the variational functional is unbounded below under the constraint. Thus one can not obtain a solution by the methods of finding a global minimum and the method one usually uses is to establish the mountain pass geometry under the restriction.
Now, we briefly present the results of study for equation (
1.2
). By Schwarz rearrangement and
Ekeland variational principle, Zhang-Zhang
[
26
]
obtained the existence of positive radial ground states solution with
L^{p}
-subcritical growth and
L^{p}
-supcritical growth terms, they also dealt with the case where
L^{p}
-subcritical growth,
L^{p}
-critical growth and
L^{p}
-supcritical growth terms co-existed,
and they used the fountain theorem to obtain the existence of infinitely many nonradial sign-changing solutions. Inspired by Bartsch-Molle-Rizzi
[
5
]
, in which the authors studied the normalized solution for Schrödinger equation with the affect of potential,
Deng and Wu
[
10
]
investigated the existence of normalized solution in the presence of potentials
and pointed out that the solution they obtained is mountain pass type. Moreover, they in
[
9
]
also
considered the existence of solution and infinitely many solutions for (
1.2
) with Sobolev exponent and
L^{p}
-subcritical exponent. For more researches on (
1.2
), we refer the readers to
[
6
,
17
,
20
]
.

When

The study of existence of nontrivial solutions for (

Later, Yu-Tang-Zhang

In this paper, we shall use the variational approaches to study the existence of solutions for (

We consider this problem on the manifold

In order to study the solution of (

(1.5)

\displaystyle m(a)=\inf\{\mathcal{J}(u):u\in S(a)\}.

The following result shows that this global minimum does not exist.

Theorem 1.1
.

The minimization problem (
1.5
) is not attained.

When

\displaystyle\|u\|_{L^{p}(\Lambda)}=\begin{cases}(\int_{\Lambda}|u|^{p}dx)^{\frac{1}{p}}&\quad\mbox{for}\quad p\in[1,\infty),\\

We know that

is a reflexive and separable Banach space,

is continuously embedded in the Lebesgue space

for any

and compactly embedded

for any

]

\displaystyle|x-y_{n}|^{2}=|x|^{2}+|y_{n}|^{2}-2\langle x,y_{n}\rangle\geq|x|^{2}+|y_{n}|^{2}.

\displaystyle\int_{(\mathbb{R}^{N})_{n}^{-}}|w_{\lambda}(x-y_{n})|\chi(|x|)|x|dx=o_{n}(1).

In the light of (
4

\displaystyle\quad\langle\mu(w_{\lambda}(x-y_{n})),\frac{y_{n}}{|y_{n}|}\rangle

\displaystyle=\int_{(\mathbb{R}^{N})_{n}^{+}}|w_{\lambda}(x-y_{n})|^{p}\chi(|x|)\frac{\langle x,y_{n}\rangle}{|y_{n}|}dx+\int_{(\mathbb{R}^{N})_{n}^{-}}|w_{\lambda}(x-y_{n})|^{p}\chi(|x|)\frac{\langle x,y_{n}\rangle}{|y_{n}|}dx

\displaystyle\geq\frac{\tau_{0}w_{\lambda}^{p}(0)}{2^{p+3}}|B_{\tau_{0}}(0)|-\int_{(\mathbb{R}^{N})_{n}^{-}}|w_{\lambda}(x-y_{n})|^{p}\chi(|x|)|x|dx

\displaystyle=\frac{\tau_{0}w_{\lambda}^{p}(0)}{2^{p+3}}|B_{\tau_{0}}(0)|+o_{n}(1).

But

∎

Lemma 4.3

Let

For any

for any

Recall the notation of genus. Let

For any nonempty set

Next we introduce an abstract minimax theorem given by

Lemma 5.1
.

(
[
14
, Theorem 2.1]
)
Let
\Phi:W_{0,\text{rad}}^{1,p}(\Omega)\rightarrow\mathbb{R}
be an even functional of class

and the following statements hold.

is a critical value of

(ii)

\mathcal{J}|_{S_{\text{rad}}(a)}

Lemma 5.2
.

\mathcal{J}|_{S_{\text{rad}}(a)}

.

Proof.

Assume that
\{u_{n}\}\subset S_{\text{rad}}(a)

\mathcal{J}(u_{n})\rightarrow c

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}|\nabla u_{n}|^{p-2}\nabla u_{n}\nabla\varphi dx-\lambda\int_{\Omega}|u_{n}|^{p-2}u_{n}\varphi dx-\int_{\Omega}|u_{n}|^{r-2}u_{n}\varphi dx=o_{n}(1)\|\varphi\|_{W_{0}^{1,p}(\Omega)}.

Let
\varphi_{R}:\mathbb{R}^{N}\rightarrow\mathbb{R}
be a smooth function and radially symmetric such that
\varphi_{R}=1
in

,

\varphi_{R}=0
in
\mathbb{R}^{N}\setminus B_{2R}(0)

\langle\mathcal{J}_{\lambda}^{\prime}(u_{n})-\mathcal{J}_{\lambda}^{\prime}(u),\varphi_{R}(u_{n}-u)\rangle=o_{n}(1)

\displaystyle\quad\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}(V(\nabla u_{n},\nabla u)-\lambda V(u_{n},u))\varphi_{R}dx+\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}(|\nabla u_{n}|^{p-2}\nabla u_{n}-|\nabla u|^{p-2}\nabla u)(u_{n}-u)\nabla\varphi_{R}dx

\displaystyle=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}(|u_{n}|^{r-2}u_{n}-|u|^{r-2}u)(u_{n}-u)\varphi_{R}dx+o_{n}(1).

\displaystyle\leq\frac{C}{R}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}(|\nabla u_{n}|^{p-1}+|\nabla u|^{p-1})|u_{n}-u|dx

\displaystyle\int_{B_{R}(0)}V(\nabla u_{n},\nabla u)dx\leq o_{n}(1).

Proof of Theorem

From Lemma
