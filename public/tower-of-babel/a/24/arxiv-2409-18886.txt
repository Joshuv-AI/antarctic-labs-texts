Log-concavity and strong q q -log-convexity for some generalized triangular arrays
Boualam Rezig, Moussa Ahmia

Abstract
. In this paper, we provide criteria for the log-concavity of rows and the strong q q -log-convexity of the generating functions of rows in more generalized triangles. Additionally, we prove that the bi s nomial transformation not only preserves the strong q q -log-convexity property but also preserves the strong q q -log-concavity property.

x_{n}x_{m}\geq x_{n-1}x_{m+1}
in the log-concave case, and
x_{n}x_{m}\leq x_{n-1}x_{m+1}
in the log-convex case, for all
m\geq n\geq 1

[

[

[

For two polynomials with real coefficients

2.
Log-concavity of the rows

In this section, we will present a sufficient condition for the log-concavity of the rows in recursive matrices

Theorem 2.1
.

Let an infinite lower triangular array
\left[A_{n,k}\right]_{n,k\geq 0}
satisfy the recurrence

(4)

\displaystyle A_{n,k}=\gamma_{k}A_{n-1,k-2}+e_{k}A_{n-1,k-1}+f_{k}A_{n-1,k}+g_{k}A_{n-1,k+1}+h_{k}A_{n-1,k+2}

for
n\geq 1
and
0\leq k\leq 2n
, with the initial conditions
A_{0,0}=1
and
A_{0,k}=A_{0,-1}=0
for
k>0
. Assume that the nonnegative sequences
\left(\gamma_{k}\right)_{k\geq 2}

\left(e_{k}\right)_{k\geq 1}
,
\left(f_{k}\right)_{k\geq 0}
,
\left(g_{k}\right)_{k\geq 0}
and
\left(h_{k}\right)_{k\geq 0}
are all log-concave. If

(1):

2\gamma_{k}e_{k}\geq\gamma_{k-1}e_{k+1}+\gamma_{k+1}e_{k-1}
,

(2):

2\gamma_{k}f_{k}\geq\gamma_{k-1}f_{k+1}+\gamma_{k+1}f_{k-1}
,

(3):

2\gamma_{k}g_{k}\geq\gamma_{k-1}g_{k+1}+\gamma_{k+1}g_{k-1}
,

(4):

2\gamma_{k}h_{k}\geq\gamma_{k-1}h_{k+1}+\gamma_{k+1}h_{k-1}
,

(5):

2e_{k}f_{k}\geq e_{k+1}f_{k-1}+e_{k-1}f_{k+1}
and
e_{k+1}e_{k-1}\geq\gamma_{k+1}f_{k-1}
,

(6):

2e_{k}g_{k}\geq e_{k+1}g_{k-1}+e_{k-1}g_{k+1}
and
f_{k+1}e_{k-1}\geq\gamma_{k+1}g_{k-1}
,

(7):

2e_{k}h_{k}\geq e_{k+1}h_{k-1}+e_{k-1}h_{k+1}
and
g_{k+1}e_{k-1}\geq\gamma_{k+1}h_{k-1}
,

(8):

2f_{k}g_{k}\geq f_{k+1}g_{k-1}+f_{k-1}g_{k+1}
and
f_{k+1}f_{k-1}\geq e_{k+1}g_{k-1}
,

(9):

2f_{k}h_{k}\geq f_{k+1}h_{k-1}+f_{k-1}h_{k+1}
and
g_{k+1}f_{k-1}\geq e_{k+1}h_{k-1}
,

(10):

2g_{k}h_{k}\geq g_{k+1}h_{k-1}+g_{k-1}h_{k+1}
and
g_{k+1}g_{k-1}\geq f_{k+1}h_{k-1}

for all
k\geq 2
, then for any fixed
n
, the sequence
\left[A_{n,k}\right]_{0\leq k\leq 2n}
is log-concave
in
k
.

Proof.

To demonstrate that
\left[A_{n,k}\right]_{0\leq k\leq 2n}
is log-concave in
k
, it is sufficient to prove that

A_{n,k}^{2}-A_{n,k+1}A_{n,k-1}\geq 0

for any
k\geq 0
, which will be prove by induction on
n
. It is clear for
n=0
. Thus, we assume the statement holds for
1\leq n\leq m
. Now, for
n=m+1
and
0\leq k\leq m+1
, we have

\displaystyle A_{m+1,k}^{2}-A_{m+1,k+1}A_{m+1,k-1}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left[\gamma_{k}A_{m,k-2}+e_{k}A_{m,k-1}+f_{k}A_{m,k}+g_{k}A_{m,k+1}+h_{k}A_{m,k+2}\right]^{2}

\displaystyle-\left[\gamma_{k+1}A_{m,k-1}+e_{k+1}A_{m,k}+f_{k+1}A_{m,k+1}+g_{k+1}A_{m,k+2}+h_{k+1}A_{m,k+3}\right]

\displaystyle\times\left[\gamma_{k-1}A_{m,k-3}+e_{k-1}A_{m,k-2}+f_{k-1}A_{m,k}+g_{k-1}A_{m,k}+h_{k-1}A_{m,k+1}\right]

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left[{\gamma_{k}^{2}A_{m,k-2}^{2}-\gamma_{k+1}\gamma_{k-1}A_{m,k-1}A_{m,k-3}}\right]+\left[{e_{k}^{2}A_{m,k-1}^{2}-e_{k+1}e_{k-1}A_{m,k}A_{m,k-2}}\right]

\displaystyle+\left[{f_{k}^{2}A_{m,k}^{2}-f_{k+1}f_{k-1}A_{m,k+1}A_{m,k-1}}\right]+\left[{g_{k}^{2}A_{m,k+1}^{2}-g_{k+1}g_{k-1}A_{m,k+2}A_{m,k}}\right]

\displaystyle+\left[{h_{k}^{2}A_{m,k+2}^{2}-h_{k+1}h_{k-1}A_{m,k+3}A_{m,k+1}}\right]

\displaystyle+\left[{2\gamma_{k}e_{k}A_{m,k-1}A_{m,k-2}-\gamma_{k-1}e_{k+1}A_{m,k}A_{m,k-3}-\gamma_{k+1}e_{k-1}A_{m,k-1}A_{m,k-2}}\right]

\displaystyle+\left[{2\gamma_{k}f_{k}A_{m,k}A_{m,k-2}-\gamma_{k-1}f_{k+1}A_{m,k+1}A_{m,k-3}-\gamma_{k+1}f_{k-1}A^{2}_{m,k-1}}\right]

\displaystyle+\left[{2\gamma_{k}g_{k}A_{m,k+1}A_{m,k-2}-\gamma_{k-1}g_{k+1}A_{m,k+2}A_{m,k-3}-\gamma_{k+1}g_{k-1}A_{m,k}A_{m,k-1}}\right]

\displaystyle+\left[{2e_{k}f_{k}A_{m,k}A_{m,k-1}-f_{k+1}e_{k-1}A_{m,k+1}A_{m,k-2}-e_{k+1}f_{k-1}A_{m,k}A_{m,k-1}}\right]

\displaystyle+\left[{2e_{k}g_{k}A_{m,k+1}A_{m,k-1}-g_{k+1}e_{k-1}A_{m,k+2}A_{m,k-2}-e_{k+1}g_{k-1}A^{2}_{m,k}}\right]

\displaystyle+\left[{2e_{k}h_{k}A_{m,k+2}A_{m,k-1}-h_{k+1}e_{k-1}A_{m,k+3}A_{m,k-2}-e_{k+1}h_{k-1}A_{m,k+1}A_{m,k}}\right]

\displaystyle+\left[{2f_{k}g_{k}A_{m,k+1}A_{m,k}-g_{k+1}f_{k-1}A_{m,k+2}A_{m,k-1}-f_{k+1}g_{k-1}A_{m,k+1}A_{m,k}}\right]

\displaystyle+\left[{2f_{k}h_{k}A_{m,k+2}A_{m,k}-h_{k+1}f_{k-1}A_{m,k+3}A_{m,k-1}-f_{k+1}h_{k-1}A_{m,k}A^{2}_{m,k+1}}\right]

(5)

\displaystyle+\left[{2g_{k}h_{k}A_{m,k+2}A_{m,k+1}-h_{k+1}g_{k-1}A_{m,k+3}A_{m,k}-g_{k+1}h_{k-1}A_{m,k+2}A_{m,k+1}}\right].

In the following, we will examine the non-negativity of (
5
). On the one hand, from the log-concavity of

, we deduce that

(6)

A_{m,k}^{2}-A_{m,k+1}A_{m,k-1}\geq 0.

In addition, we know that the sequence

, is log-concave. Therefore, applying conditions (1)-(10) of Theorem
2.1
, we obtain that

\displaystyle\eqref{eqq2}\geq

\displaystyle e_{k+1}e_{k-1}\left(A_{m,k-1}^{2}-A_{m,k}A_{m,k-2}\right)

\displaystyle+g_{k+1}g_{k-1}\left(A_{m,k+1}^{2}-A_{m,k+2}A_{m,k}\right)

\displaystyle+h_{k+1}h_{k-1}\left(A_{m,k+2}^{2}-A_{m,k+3}A_{m,k+1}\right)

\displaystyle+\gamma_{k+1}\gamma_{k-1}\left(A_{m,k-2}^{2}-A_{m,k-1}A_{m,k-3}\right)

\displaystyle A_{n,0}=
