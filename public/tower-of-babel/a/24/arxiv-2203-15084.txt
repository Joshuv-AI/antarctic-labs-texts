Symmetric ordering and Weyl realizations for quantum Minkowski spaces
Stjepan Meljanac, Zoran Škoda, Saša Krešić–Jurić

Abstract
Symmetric ordering and Weyl realizations for non commutative quantum Minkowski spaces are reviewed. Weyl realizations of Lie deformed spaces and corresponding star products, as well as twist corresponding to Weyl realization and coproduct of momenta are presented. Drinfeld twists understood in Hopf algebroid sense are also discussed. A few examples of corresponding Weyl realizations are given. We show that for the original Snyder space there exists symmetric ordering, but no Weyl realization. Quadratic deformations of Minkowski space are considered and it is demonstrated that symmetric ordering is deformed and a generalized Weyl realization can be defined.

by construction. This map may or may not be invertible,

\varphi

If

is indeed an isomorphism of vector spaces,
then using formula (

we can transfer the product from

to

without

A general class of realizations of NC coordinates
\hat{x}_{\mu}\in U_{\theta}

\hat{x}_{\mu}=x_{\alpha}\,\varphi_{\alpha\mu}(iL_{\gamma\delta})

We point out that the results for Lie algebra type NC spaces discussed in Sections 2 and 3 cannot be applied to these quadratic deformations.
Construction of such quadratic algebras can be performed using twist operators that besides the Lorentz generators

include dilation operators
and, more generally,
L_{\mu\nu}=x_{\mu}p_{\nu}

(no summation). Another example is given in Ref.

(93)

for any different, but fixed indices
\alpha,\beta,\gamma,\delta

If, for a quadratic algebra, we define functions
