A priori error estimation for elastohydrodynamic lubrication using interior-exterior penalty approach
Peeyush Singh

Abstract
In the present study, an interior-exterior penalty discontinuous Galerkin finite element method (DG-FEM) is analysed for solving Elastohydrodynamic lubrication (EHL) line and point contact problems. The existence of discrete penalized solution is examined using Brouwer’s fixed point theorem. Furthermore, the uniqueness of solution is proved using Lipschitz continuity of the discrete solution map under light load parameter assumptions. A priori error estimates are achieved in L 2 L^{2} and H 1 H^{1} norms which are shown to be optimal in mesh size h h and suboptimal in polynomial degree p p . The validity of theoretical findings are confirmed through series of numerical experiments.



Keywords:
Elasto-hydrodynamic lubrication, Discontinuous Finite Element Method,
interior-exterior penalty method, pseudo-monotone operators, quasi-variational inequality.

Introduction

Discontinuous Galerkin finite element methods (DG-FEMs) are now widely used in scientific computation to achieve better accuracy and their ﬂexibility in handling nonuniform degrees of approximation as well as flexibility in local mesh adaptivity. While DG-FEMs have been profoundly shown to be very successful, the theory ensuring the convergence of the algorithm and the
advantages over Elastohydrodynamic Lubrication (EHL) line and point contacts problems still under development. Recently, several results
have been obtained for DG-FEMs for strongly nonlinear elliptic partial differential equations (see for example

2

6

).

This paper presents the mathematical analysis of DG-FEMs for EHL problems using interior-exterior penalty approach.
However, the idea is more generic and it can be easily extended to more general variational inequality problems too.
In this study, author demonstrates priori error estimate in the underline norm, prove existence and uniqueness of the discrete DG-FEM formulation of the EHL problem and find the optimal and suboptimal rate of convergence under discussed norm.
Recently, a nearby method to approximate the EHL solution is to solve the analogue discrete inequality using discontinuous Galerkin finite volume method (DG-FVM) approach discussed (see for example
[
5
]
) which is restricted under lower regularity assumptioms of penalized soultion of EHL point contact problem.

Exterior penalty methods has a significant role in from of conceptual perspective. One striaght way justification is, it convert inequalities to equation.
A priori estimates for finite element methods for elliptic obstacle problems were proved in
[
4

using regularization technique imposing the unilateral constraint approximately through a penalty term depending on a regularization
parameter

By motivated by the same route, in this article author prove priori error estimate of discontinuous Galerkin-finite element methods using interior-exterior penality approach.

\displaystyle+CC_{\epsilon}C_{u}h^{1/2}({\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\chi\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|}+(CC_{\epsilon}C_{u}h^{1/2}+1){\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\eta\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|})\Big){\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\xi\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|}.

(84)

Now using the coericivity property we have

\displaystyle{\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\xi\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|}^{2}\leq CC_{\epsilon}\Big(\Big(\max_{1\leq i\leq N_{h}}\frac{p_{i}}{h_{i}}\Big)^{1/2}{\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\chi\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|}^{2}

\displaystyle+CC_{\epsilon}C_{u}h^{1/2}({\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\chi\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|}+(CC_{\epsilon}C_{u}h^{1/2}+1){\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\eta\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|})\Big){\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\xi\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|}.

(85)

Hence we have the desire result.

∎

Theorem 3.10
.

Let
\epsilon\in C^{2}_{b}(\Omega\times\mathbb{R})

. Suppose
\mathcal{P}_{h}^{p}

C=C(\alpha_{*},M)

Consider the following adjoint problem

\displaystyle-\nabla(\epsilon^{*}(u)\nabla\psi)+\epsilon_{u}^{*}(u)\nabla u.\nabla\psi-\vec{\beta}\Big(\rho h_{d}+(\rho h_{d})_{u}\Big)\nabla\psi=e\text{ in }\Omega

(87)

\displaystyle\psi=0\text{ on }\partial\Omega.

(88)

Now from elliptic regularity property, there exist a unique
\psi\in H^{2}(\Omega)
which satisfies above linear elliptic problem (?) and

\displaystyle\lVert\psi\rVert_{H^{2}(\Omega)}\leq C\lVert e\rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega)}

(89)

∎

holds.

By short computation, It is easy to show that

\displaystyle\lVert e\rVert^{2}=\mathscr{B}(u;u,\psi)-\mathscr{B}(u_{h};u_{h},\psi)+\sum_{i=1}^{N_{h}}\int_{K_{i}}\Big(e\tilde{\epsilon_{u}}\nabla e-\tilde{\epsilon_{uu}}e^{2}\nabla u\Big)\nabla\psi

(90)

The first term on the right hand side of (84) is revised as

\displaystyle I=\mathscr{B}(u;u,\psi)-\mathscr{B}(u_{h};u,\psi)+\mathscr{B}(u_{h};u,\psi)-\mathscr{B}(u_{h};u_{h},\psi)

4
Conclusion

In this article, we have discuss and analyze interior-exterior penalty base discontinuous Galerkin finite element method for solving EHL line as well as point contact problems. Convergence of discrete DG solution is proved using Brouwer’s fixed point theorem. We have shown that optimal order of convergence in
L^{2}
and
H^{1}
norms is achieved in mesh size
h
theoretically. However, suboptimal order convergence is achieved in polynomial degree
p
.
