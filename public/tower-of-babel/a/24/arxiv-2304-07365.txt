Limiting Sets for Digital Cones and Suspensions
Laurence Boxer

Abstract
Cone and suspension constructions have been introduced in digital topology, modeled on those of classical topology. For digital cones and suspensions, and for some related digital images, we find ( m , n ) (m,n) -limiting sets; especially ( 0 , 0 ) (0,0) -limiting (freezing) sets. Key words and phrases: digital topology, digital image, cone, suspension, freezing set, cold set, limiting set MSC: 54B20, 54C35
