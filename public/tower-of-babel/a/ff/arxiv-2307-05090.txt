The strong vertices of bottom mesons B B , B ∗ B^{*} and bottomonia Υ \Upsilon , η b \eta_{b}
Jie Lu 1, Guo-Liang Yu 1, Zhi-Gang Wang 1, Bin Wu 1

Abstract
In this article, the strong coupling constants of vertices B ​ B ​ Υ BB\Upsilon , B ​ B ∗ ​ Υ BB^{*}\Upsilon , B ∗ ​ B ∗ ​ Υ B^{*}B^{*}\Upsilon , B ​ B ∗ ​ η b BB^{*}\eta_{b} and B ∗ ​ B ∗ ​ η b B^{*}B^{*}\eta_{b} are analyzed in the framework of QCD sum rules. In this work, all possible off-shell cases and the contributions of vacuum condensate terms including ⟨ q ¯ ​ q ⟩ \langle\overline{q}q\rangle , ⟨ q ¯ ​ g s ​ σ ​ G ​ q ⟩ \langle\overline{q}g_{s}\sigma Gq\rangle , ⟨ g s 2 ​ G 2 ⟩ \langle g_{s}^{2}G^{2}\rangle , ⟨ f 3 ​ G 3 ⟩ \langle f^{3}G^{3}\rangle and ⟨ q ¯ ​ q ⟩ ​ ⟨ g s 2 ​ G 2 ⟩ \langle\overline{q}q\rangle\langle g_{s}^{2}G^{2}\rangle are considered. The momentum dependent strong coupling constants are first calculated and then are fitted into analytical functions g ⁡ ( Q 2 ) g(Q^{2}) which are used to extrapolate into time-like regions to obtain the final values of strong coupling constants. The final results are g B ​ B ​ Υ = 40.67 − 4.20 + 7.55 g_{BB\Upsilon}=40.67^{+7.55}_{-4.20} , g B ​ B ∗ ​ Υ = 11.58 − 1.09 + 2.19 g_{BB^{*}\Upsilon}=11.58^{+2.19}_{-1.09} GeV -1 , g B ∗ ​ B ∗ ​ Υ = 57.02 − 5.31 + 5.32 g_{B^{*}B^{*}\Upsilon}=57.02^{+5.32}_{-5.31} , g B ​ B ∗ ​ η b = 23.39 − 2.30 + 4.74 g_{BB^{*}\eta_{b}}=23.39^{+4.74}_{-2.30} and g B ∗ ​ B ∗ ​ η b = 12.49 − 1.35 + 2.12 g_{B^{*}B^{*}\eta_{b}}=12.49^{+2.12}_{-1.35} GeV -1 . These strong coupling constants are important input parameters which reflect the dynamic properties of the interactions among the mesons and quarkonia.
