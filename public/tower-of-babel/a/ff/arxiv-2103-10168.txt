Conformal correlators in the critical O ⁡ ( N ) O(N) vector model
Noam Chai, Mikhail Goykhman, and Ritam Sinha

Abstract
We calculate a set of conformal correlators in the critical O ⁡ ( N ) O(N) vector model in 2 < d < 6 2<d<6 dimensions. We focus on the correlators involving the Hubbard-Stratonovich field s s , and its composite form s 2 s^{2} . In the process, we report a number of new calculations of diagrams involving the composite s 2 s^{2} operator. Through the calculation of the ⟨ s 2 ​ s 2 ​ s ⟩ \langle s^{2}s^{2}s\rangle three-point function, we shed new light on a conjectured s → − s s\rightarrow-s symmetry in the s s sector of the critical O ⁡ ( N ) O(N) vector model in d = 3 d=3 .
