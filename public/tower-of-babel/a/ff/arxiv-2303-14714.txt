On lower bounds of the order of k k -chromatic unit distance graphs
Aubrey D.N.J. de Grey; Jaan Parts (jaan_parts@mail.ru)

Abstract
Here we give refined numerical values for the minimum number of vertices of k k -chromatic unit distance graphs in the Euclidean plane.

Pritikin’s approach is based on finding a proper tiling of the plane using

In

The main idea of Gwyn-Stavrianos’ approach is to first estimate the minimum number of edges
e_{k}

v_{k}
using known relations. To do this, a tiling of the plane in
k-1

p_{k-1}
of the formation of a
mono-chromatic edge

1/p_{k-1}
gives a lower bound on the number of edges
e_{k}

For

4

k=6

\delta=\pi/(8\sqrt{3})
and colored in the fifth color, and a lemma is used that allows passing from

p_{k}\leq(1-2\delta)\;p_{k-1}

In

\phi

M_{k}(\sigma,\phi)
was determined, which takes the value 1 or 0 depending on whether an edge is mono-chromatic or not.
Instead, we took an algebraic method, calculating the integral

p_{k}=\frac{1}{2\pi S}\int_{S}d\sigma\int_{0}^{2\pi}d\phi\,M_{k}(\sigma,\phi)
