Difference Methods for Double Change Covering Designs
Amanda Lynn Chafee, Brett Stevens

Abstract
A double change covering design is a v v -set X X and an ordered list ℒ \mathcal{L} of b b blocks of size k k where every t t -set must occur in at least one block. Each pair of consecutive blocks differs by exactly two elements. A double change covering design is circular when the first and last blocks also differ by two elements. We give a recursive construction that uses 1-factorizations and expansion sets to increase v v by v + k − 2 k − 2 \frac{v+k-2}{k-2} . We construct CDCCD( 2 ​ k − 2 , k , k − 1 2k-2,k,k-1 ) and CDCCD( 2 ​ k − 1 , k , k − 2 2k-1,k,k-2 ) from single change covering designs. We use difference methods to construct five infinite families for any k ≥ 3 k\geq 3 , minimum CDCCD( c ⁡ ( 4 ​ k − 6 ) + 1 , k , c 2 ​ ( 4 ​ k − 6 ) + c c(4k-6)+1,k,c^{2}(4k-6)+c ) when c ≤ 5 c\leq 5 and CDCCD(61,4,366). Finally, from these, we use the recursion to construct twelve additional minimum DCCD.
