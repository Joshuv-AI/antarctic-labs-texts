A Practical Consideration on Convex Mutual Information
Mingxi Yin, Bingli Jiao, Senior Member, IEEE, Dongsheng Zheng, Yuli Yang, Senior Member, IEEE

Abstract
In this paper, we focus on the convex mutual information, which was found at the lowest level split in multilevel coding schemes with communications over the additive white Gaussian noise (AWGN) channel. Theoretical analysis shows that communication achievable rates (ARs) do not necessarily below mutual information in the convex region. In addition, simulation results are provided as an evidence.

In multilevel coding schemes, there are several approaches to split the bit-to-symbol mapping of one modulated symbol into different levels, each of which constructs a coded modulation problem based on their individual MIs. The overall achievable rate (AR) is obtained by the summation of ARs of all levels and is limited by the MI

III
Conclusion

The present work shows that ARs of the low level in multilevel coding are not necessarily limited by the convex MI with split signals. In the theoretical work, we have proved that ARs of repeated signals of the low level can be in a straight line when approaching zero SNR instead of along with the convex MI of the low level. Simulation results have confirmed the straight line of the AR and its possibility beyond the MI.
