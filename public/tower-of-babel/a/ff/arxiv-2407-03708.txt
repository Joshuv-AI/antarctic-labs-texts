Abstract

We examine the determination of the gluon distribution of the proton in the low-
x
, low-
Q^{2}
domain of deep inelastic electron-proton scattering (DIS).
Adopting two-gluon exchange as the dominant interaction in the low-
x
, low-
Q^{2}
domain
implying the known result of scaling of the photoabsorption cross section in terms of
the scaling variable
\eta(W^{2},Q^{2})
, we deduce a reliable result for the
gluon distribution at leading order of the perturbative QCD improved parton model that differs significantly from the widely spread results from big
collaborations based on evolving from a starting scale of
Q_{0}^{2}\cong 2
GeV
2
.
The validity of evolution upon adopting its quantitative modification at low-
Q^{2}

without any modification at larger values of
Q^{2}
leads to a quantitative improvement of
the extraction of the gluon distribution based on evolution from a starting scale of
Q^{2}

conventionally chosen as
Q^{2}=Q_{0}^{2}\cong 2
GeV
2
.

Based on two-gluon exchange as dominant interaction in photoabsorption and
