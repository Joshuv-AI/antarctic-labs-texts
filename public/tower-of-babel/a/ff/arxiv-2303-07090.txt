Abstract
. Let ( A , 𝔪 ) (A,\mathfrak{m}) be an excellent normal domain of dimension two containing a field k ≅ A / 𝔪 k\cong A/\mathfrak{m} . An 𝔪 \mathfrak{m} -primary ideal I I to be a p g p_{g} -ideal if the Rees algebra A ⁡ [ I ​ t ] A[It] is a Cohen-Macaulay normal domain. Dear Reader, while reading this paper it is a good idea to have

Let

Later

Assume
(A,\mathfrak{m})
is an excellent normal domain of dimension two containing an algebraically closed field
k\cong A/\mathfrak{m}
.
For such rings Okuma, Watanabe and Yoshida in
[
8
]
introduced (using geometric techniques) the notion of

-ideals as follows:
let
I
be an
\mathfrak{m}
-primary ideal in
A
.
The

and

An integrally closed
\mathfrak{m}
-primary ideal

-ideal. They showed that

. Furthermore

-primary

is a

-ideal. Furthermore

They also proved that if

-primary integrally closed ideal

-ideal. In a later paper

-ideal.

Motivated by this result we made the following definition in

-primary ideal

In

We now describe in brief the contents of this paper. In section two we discuss some preliminaries that we need.

3.
Ellingsrud-Skjelbred spectral sequences and an application

In this section we describe the Ellingsrud-Skjelbred spectral sequences (we follow the exposition given in
[
7
, 8.6]
). We also give an application which is crucial for us.

3.1
.

Let
S
be a commutative Noetherian ring. Let
Mod(A)
be the category of left
A
-modules.

(1) Let
G
be a finite group. Let
S[G]
be the group ring and let
Mod(S[G])
be the category of left
S[G]
-modules. Let
(-)^{G}
be the functor of
G
-fixed points. Let
H^{n}(G,-)
be the
n^{th}
right derived functor of

(-)^{G}
.

(2) Let
\mathfrak{a}
be an ideal in
S
. Let
\Gamma_{\mathfrak{a}}(-)
be the torsion functor associated to
\mathfrak{a}
. Let
H^{n}_{\mathfrak{a}}(-)
be the
n^{th}
right derived functor of

\Gamma_{\mathfrak{a}}(-)
. Usually
H^{n}_{\mathfrak{a}}(-)
is called the
n^{th}
local cohomology functor of
A
with respect to
\mathfrak{a}
.

(3) If
M\in Mod(S[G])
then note
\Gamma_{\mathfrak{a}}(M)\in Mod(S[G])
.

3.2
.

Ellingsrud-Skjelbred spectral sequences are constructed as follows: Consider the following sequence of functors

(i)\quad Mod(S[G])\xrightarrow{(-)^{G}}Mod(S)\xrightarrow{\Gamma_{\mathfrak{a}}}Mod(S),

(ii)\quad Mod(S[G])\xrightarrow{\Gamma_{\mathfrak{a}}}Mod(S[G])\xrightarrow{(-)^{G}}Mod(S)

We then notice

(a)

The above compositions are equal.

(b)

It is possible to use Grothendieck spectral sequence of composite of functors to both (i) and (ii) above; see

[
7
, 8.6.2]
.

Following Ellingsrud-Skjelbred we let
H^{n}_{\mathfrak{a}}(G,-)
denote the
n^{th}
right derived functor of this composite
functor. So by (i) and (ii) we have two first quadrant spectral sequences for each
S[G]
-module
M

(\alpha)\colon\quad\quad E_{2}^{p,q}=H^{p}_{\mathfrak{a}}(H^{q}(G,M))\Longrightarrow H^{p+g}_{\mathfrak{a}}(G,M),\ \text{and}

(\beta)\colon\quad\quad\mathcal{E}_{2}^{p,q}=H^{p}(G,H^{q}_{\mathfrak{a}}(M))\Longrightarrow H^{p+g}_{\mathfrak{a}}(G,M).

Remark 3.3
.

(1) If
S
is
\mathbb{N}
-graded ring then
S[G]
is also
\mathbb{N}
-graded (with
\deg\sigma=0
for all
\sigma\in G
).

(2) If
M
is a finitely generated graded left
S[G]
-module then
H^{n}(G,M)
are finitely generated graded
S
-module. This can be easily seen by taking a graded free resolution of
S
consisting of finitely generated free graded
S[G]
-modules.

(3) Ellingsrud-Skjelbred spectral sequences have an obvious graded analogue.

3.4
.

Application:

Setup: Let
(A,\mathfrak{m})
be a two dimensional Cohen-Macaulay local ring containing a field

. Let
\ell
be a finite Galois extension of
k

. Set

\mathfrak{n}
be maximal ideal of

G
act on

(as described in
2.1
(9)). Note

; see
2.1
(9). Let
I
be an
\mathfrak{n}
-primary ideal of

which is
G
-invariant (i.e.,
\sigma(I)=I
for each

). Let
\mathcal{R}(I)=B[It]

. Then note we have a natural action of

on
\mathcal{R}(I)

\mathcal{C}=\mathcal{R}(I)^{G}

\mathcal{R}(I)

\mathcal{C}
-module.
We prove

Theorem 3.5
.

(with hypotheses as in
3.4
.) Let
\mathfrak{M}
be the graded maximal ideal of
\mathcal{C}
. If
\mathcal{R}(I)
is Cohen-Macaulay then

(1)

(2)

(3)

\mathfrak{m}
-primary ideal
