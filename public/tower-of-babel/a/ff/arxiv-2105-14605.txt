ordinary and symbolic powers of edge ideals of weighted oriented graphs
Arindam Banerjee, Kanoy Kumar Das, S. Selvaraja

Abstract
. Let 𝒟 \mathcal{D} be a weighted oriented graph and I ⁡ ( 𝒟 ) I(\mathcal{D}) be its edge ideal. In this paper, we show that all the symbolic and ordinary powers of I ⁡ ( 𝒟 ) I(\mathcal{D}) coincide when 𝒟 \mathcal{D} is a weighted oriented certain class of tree. Finally, we give necessary and sufficient conditions for the equality of ordinary and symbolic powers of naturally oriented lines.



1.
Introduction

Symbolic powers of ideals have been studied intensely over the last two decades.
We refer the
reader to
[
4
]
for a review of results in the literature.
Let
R=k[x_{1},\ldots,x_{n}]
be the polynomial ring in
n
variables over a field
k

and
I
be a homogeneous ideal in
R
.
Then the
s
-th symbolic powers of

I^{(s)}=\bigcap\limits_{\mathfrak{p}\in\operatorname{Ass}(I)}I^{s}R_{\mathfrak{p}}\cap R
, where

\operatorname{Ass}(I)
is the set of associated prime ideals of
I
.
Comparing the ordinary and symbolic powers of an ideal becomes a natural question.
In general, the question of when the symbolic and ordinary powers of a given ideal coincide
is open. There are conditions on
I
that are equivalent to
I^{(s)}=I^{s}

for all

given by Hochster
[
6
]
when

is prime, and
generalized by Li and Swanson

]
to the case

is a radical ideal. Other sufficient conditions are known, and the question is
completely settled in certain cases. However, the above question is still open even for
monomial ideals. In this paper we study the
ordinary and symbolic powers of edge ideals of weighted oriented graphs.

Let
G=(V(G),E(G))
be a finite simple
(no loops, no multiple edges) undirected graph. A weighted oriented graph
\mathcal{D}
whose underlying graph is

,
is a triplet
(V(\mathcal{D}),E(\mathcal{D}),w)
where
V(\mathcal{D})=V(G)
,

\{\{x,y\}\mid\{x,y\}\in E(\mathcal{D})\}=E(G)
and

is a function
w:V(\mathcal{D})\longrightarrow\mathbb{N}
.
The weight of
x_{i}\in V(\mathcal{D})
is
w(x_{i})
or
w_{i}
.
An ordered pair
\{x,y\}\in E(\mathcal{D})
if there is a directed edge from the vertex
x
to
y
.
Let
\mathcal{D}=(V(\mathcal{D}),E(\mathcal{D}),w)
denote a weighted oriented graph with vertices

. By identifying the vertices with the variables in the polynomial ring

we can associate to

\mathcal{D}
a monomial ideal

generated by the set

\{x_{i}x_{j}^{w(x_{j})}\mid\{x_{i},x_{j}\}\in E(\mathcal{D})\}

The ideal

is called the

of

.
These ideals generalize the usual edge ideals of graphs, since if

for all

, then

.
The interest in studying edge ideals of weighted oriented graphs has its foundation in coding theory,
specifically in the study of Reed-Muller type codes (see
[
10
]
).
These edge ideals of weighted oriented graphs appears as initial ideals of vanishing ideals of
projective spaces over finite fields.
Since then, the researchers have been investigating the connection

In

of

As an immediate consequence, we have if

.
In
[

]
, the authors
proved that the equality of ordinary and symbolic powers of edge
ideals of weighted oriented graphs when the underlying simple graph is either a complete
graph or a complete bipartite graph.
Mandal and Pradhan studied the symbolic powers of

Recently, they showed that

\mathcal{D}
is any weighted oriented star graph or some specific weighted naturally
oriented path, then

for all
s\geq 2
,
(
[
8
]
,
[

Recall that a

More precisely, we prove the following. Let

3.
Symbolic Powers of Weighted Oriented Graphs

In this section, we will study equality of ordinary and symbolic powers of edge ideals of
certain classes of weighted oriented graphs.

The following lemma exhibits a situation when all the symbolic and ordinary powers of the edge ideals coincide.

Lemma 3.1
.

Let
\mathcal{D}
be a weighted oriented graph such that
w(v)>1
for all

v\in V(\mathcal{D})
. Then
V(\mathcal{D})
is a strong vertex cover of
\mathcal{D}
if and only if

\mathcal{D}
does not contain any source vertex. Moreover,
if
V(\mathcal{D})
is a strong vertex cover, then
I(\mathcal{D})^{(s)}=I(\mathcal{D})^{s}
for all

s\geq 1
.

Proof.

From the definition of strong vertex cover,
V(\mathcal{D})
is a strong vertex cover of

\mathcal{D}
if and only if for all
x\in V(\mathcal{D})
there is
y\in V(\mathcal{D})
such that

\{y,x\}\in E(\mathcal{D})
. In other words,
V(\mathcal{D})
is a strong vertex cover if and only
if
\mathcal{D}
does not contain any source vetices. Now, if
V(\mathcal{D})
is a strong vertex cover,
then the unique homogeneous maximal ideal is an associated prime of
I(\mathcal{D})
, and
hence by
[
3
, Lemma 3.3]
,
I(\mathcal{D})^{(s)}=I(\mathcal{D})^{s}
for all
s\geq 1
.
∎

As an immediate consequence, we have the following:

Corollary 3.2
.

Let
\mathcal{D}
be a weighted naturally oriented cycle such that

w(v)>1
for all
v\in V(\mathcal{D})
. Then
I(\mathcal{D})^{(s)}=I(\mathcal{D})^{s}
for all
s\geq 1
.

Theorem 3.3
.

Let
\mathcal{D}
be a weighted oriented graph with

w(v)>1
for all
v\in V(\mathcal{D})
. If

I(\mathcal{D})^{(s)}=I(\mathcal{D})^{s}

Proof.

We first observe that if the

\mathcal{D}
contains the vertex

, and

z
.
In view of this, we partition the strong vertex covers of
\mathcal{D}

\mathcal{C}_{1}

\mathcal{D}
which contains the vertices

and does not contain the vertex

and
\mathcal{C}_{2}
contains all the strong vertex cover of
\mathcal{D}

which contains the vertex

.
Set

By Theorem

\mathcal{C}_{2}

.
Then by Theorem

,

.

Claim:

I_{1}=(x,z^{w(z)})+I(\mathcal{D}^{\prime})
and

I_{2}=(y^{w(y)},yz^{w(z)})+I(\mathcal{D}^{\prime})

Proof of the claim:
For any
C\in\mathcal{C}_{1}

\mathcal{D}

.
Conversely, let

.
Then we must have

Lemma 3.5
.

Let
\mathcal{D}

Proof.

First we claim that
f=x_{i-1}x_{i}^{w_{i}}x_{i+1}^{2}x_{i+2}^{w_{i+2}}\in I(\mathcal{D})^{(3)}

\mathcal{D}
,

.
Suppose

Next, if

.
