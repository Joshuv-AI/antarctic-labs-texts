Supertranslation Goldstone and de Sitter Tachyons
Hongjie Chen

Abstract
Supertranslation Goldstone lies in certain “exceptional series” representations of S ​ L ​ ( 2 , ℂ ) SL(2,\mathbb{C}) . Interestingly, m 2 = − 3 m^{2}=-3 scalar tachyon in three dimensional de Sitter space also lies in the same representation. In this note, we analyze these theories, focusing on representation-theoretical aspects, and emphasize that “modulo certain polynomials”, there is a unitary representation of the corresponding symmetry group.

Supertranslation Goldstone is related to the leading order soft theorem for gravitons. One might wonder whether they furnish a unitary representation of the symmetry group

We now check that on one-particles states, this coincides with the inner product of representation

(6.32)

\ket{0}
which is annihilated by

. This means

are positive frequency modes. The physical Hilbert space is built by acting

In de Sitter space, there is a conformal class of such choice of vacua
[

]

[

is normalized according to (

One can see that if

of
