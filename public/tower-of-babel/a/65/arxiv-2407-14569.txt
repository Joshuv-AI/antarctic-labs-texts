On right π \pi -inverse ordered semigroups
A. Jamadar

Abstract
Here we introduce the notion of (left, right) π \pi - t t -simple, right π \pi -inverse ordered semigroups and discuss characterizations and relationships concerning them. Semilattice decomposition of left π \pi - t t -simple ordered semigroups has been given here. Furthermore we study an interrelation of between the generalized Green’s relations and the class of semigroups which are semilattices of right π \pi - t t -simple ordered semigroups.
