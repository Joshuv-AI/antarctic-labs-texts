Abstract
. For certain cyclic totally real number fields, we give formulas for the density of primes that satisfy a given spin relation.

Some Class Field Theory

Lemma 6.3

Lemma 6.4
.

Let
K:=K(n,\ell)
. For all
\alpha\in\mathbf{M}_{4}
, if
\star(\alpha)=1
then
\star(-\alpha)=-1
.

Proof.

By Lemma
5.2
,
(-1,-1)_{2}=-1
.

Next note that
(a,b)_{2}=(a^{\sigma},b^{\sigma})_{2}
for all
\sigma\in\operatorname{Gal}(K/\mathbb{Q})
since
2
is inert in
K
.

Assume
\star(\alpha)=1
. Then
(\alpha,\alpha^{\sigma})_{2}=1
for all nontrivial
\sigma\in\operatorname{Gal}(K/\mathbb{Q})
. Let
\sigma\in\operatorname{Gal}(K/\mathbb{Q})
be nontrivial. By bimultiplicativity of Hilbert symbols,

\displaystyle(-\alpha,-\alpha^{\sigma})_{2}

\displaystyle=(-\alpha,-1)_{2}(-\alpha,\alpha^{\sigma})_{2}

\displaystyle=(-1,-1)_{2}(\alpha,-1)_{2}(-1,\alpha^{\sigma})_{2}(\alpha,\alpha^{\sigma})_{2}.

Next observe
(\alpha,-1)_{2}=(-1,\alpha)_{2}=(-1,\alpha^{\sigma})_{2}
, the second equality coming from the Galois-invariance shown earlier in this proof. Therefore
(\alpha,-1)_{2}(-1,\alpha^{\sigma})_{2}=1
. Then since
(\alpha,\alpha^{\sigma})_{2}=1
and
(-1,-1)_{2}=-1
, we get that

(-\alpha,-\alpha^{\sigma})_{2}=-1.

Therefore
\star(-\alpha)=-1
.
