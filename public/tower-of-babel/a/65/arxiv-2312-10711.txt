A discussion on the anomalous threshold enhancement of J / ψ J/\psi – ψ ⁡ ( 3770 ) \psi(3770) couplings and X ⁡ ( 6900 ) X(6900) peak


Abstract
The attractive interaction between J / ψ J/\psi and ψ ⁡ ( 3770 ) \psi(3770) has to be strong enough to form the molecular resonant structure, i.e., X ⁡ ( 6900 ) X(6900) . We argue that since ψ ⁡ ( 3770 ) \psi(3770) decays predominantly into a D ​ D ¯ D\bar{D} pair, the interactions between J / ψ J/\psi and ψ ⁡ ( 3770 ) \psi(3770) may be significantly enhanced due to the three point D ​ D ¯ D\bar{D} loop diagram. The enhancement comes from the anomalous threshold located at t = − 1.288 t=-1.288 GeV 2 , which effect propagates into the s s -channel partial wave amplitude in the vicinity of s ≃ 6.9 \sqrt{s}\simeq 6.9 GeV. This effect may be helpful in forming the X ⁡ ( 6900 ) X(6900) peak.
