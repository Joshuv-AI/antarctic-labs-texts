RIP-based Performance Guarantee for Low Rank Matrix Recovery via L ∗ − F L_{*-F} Minimization
Yan Li, Liping Zhang

Abstract
In the undetermined linear system 𝒃 = 𝒜 ⁡ ( 𝑿 ) + 𝒔 \bm{b}=\mathcal{A}(\bm{X})+\bm{s} , vector 𝒃 \bm{b} and operator 𝒜 \mathcal{A} are the known measurements and 𝒔 \bm{s} is the unknown noise. In this paper, we investigate sufficient conditions for exactly reconstructing desired matrix 𝑿 \bm{X} being low-rank or approximately low-rank. We use the difference of nuclear norm and Frobenius norm ( L ∗ − F L_{*-F} ) as a surrogate for rank function and establish a new nonconvex relaxation of such low rank matrix recovery, called the L ∗ − F L_{*-F} minimization, in order to approximate the rank function closer. For such nonconvex and nonsmooth constrained L ∗ − F L_{*-F} minimization problems, based on whether the noise level is 0 0 , we give the upper bound estimation of the recovery error respectively. Particularly, in the noise-free case, one sufficient condition for exact recovery is presented. If linear operator 𝒜 \mathcal{A} satisfies the restricted isometry property with δ 4 ​ r < 2 ​ r − 1 2 ​ r − 1 + 2 ​ ( 2 ​ r + 1 ) \delta_{4r}<\frac{\sqrt{2r}-1}{\sqrt{2r}-1+\sqrt{2}(\sqrt{2r}+1)} , then r r - rank matrix 𝑿 \bm{X} can be exactly recovered without other assumptions. In addition, we also take insights into the regularized L ∗ − F L_{*-F} minimization model since such regularized model is more widely used in algorithm design. We provide the recovery error estimation of this regularized L ∗ − F L_{*-F} minimization model via RIP tool. To our knowledge, this is the first result on exact reconstruction of low rank matrix via regularized L ∗ − F L_{*-F} minimization.

A particular class of (

Unfortunately, problem (

\displaystyle\min_{\bm{X}}\ \|\bm{X}\|_{*}

\displaystyle\textup{s.t.}\ \mathcal{A}(\bm{X})=\bm{b},

In this study, we focus on a non-convex surrogate for rank function, i.e., the difference of nuclear norm and Frobenius norm (

In practice, since the measurement

Main Results on Constrained Minimization

II-A

Links Between Problem (
2
) and Problem (
4
)

The goal of this subsection is to study the links between the rank minimization problem (
2
) and its nonconvex surrogate minimization (
4
). In light of the characterization of locally sparse feasible solutions, we show the globally optimal solution of problem (
2
) must solve the problem (
4
) globally.

Definition 3
.

Denote
\mathcal{F}:=\{\bm{X}\ |\ \mathcal{A}(\bm{X})=\bm{b}\}
.
X\in\mathcal{F}
is called locally sparse if
\nexists Y\in\mathcal{F}\backslash\{\bm{X}\}
such that
\bm{Y}
and
\bm{X}
have a simultaneous ordered SVD and
\textup{supp}(\bm{\pi}(\sigma(\bm{Y})))\subseteq\textup{supp}(\sigma(\bm{X}))
. Denote
\mathcal{F}_{L}=\{\bm{X}\in\mathcal{F}\ |\ \bm{X}\textup{ is locally sparse}\}
as the set of locally sparse feasible solutions.

In fact, the locally sparse feasible solution is locally the sparsest feasible solution.

Lemma 4
.

For any
\bm{X}\in\mathcal{F}_{L}
, there exists
\delta_{X}>0
such that for any
\bm{Y}\in\mathcal{F}
having simultaneous ordered SVD with
\bm{X}
, if
0<\|\bm{Y}-\bm{X}\|_{F}<\delta_{X}
, we have
\textup{supp}(\sigma(\bm{X}))\subset\textup{supp}(\bm{\pi}(\sigma(\bm{Y})))
.

Proof.

Choose
\delta_{X}=\min_{i\in\textup{supp}(\sigma(\bm{X}))}\{\sigma_{i}(\bm{X})\}
. For any
\bm{Y}\in\mathcal{F}
having simultaneous ordered SVD with
\bm{X}
such that
0<\|\bm{Y}-\bm{X}\|_{F}<\delta_{X}
, we set
\bm{Z}=\bm{X}-\bm{Y}
, that is
\bm{Z}=\bm{U}_{X}\mathcal{D}(\sigma(\bm{X})-\bm{\pi}(\sigma(\bm{Y}))){\bm{V}_{X}}^{\intercal}
. For brevity, denote
\bm{z}
as
\sigma(\bm{X})-\bm{\pi}(\sigma(\bm{Y}))
, then we get

\|\bm{z}\|_{\infty}\leq\|\bm{z}\|_{2}<\min_{i\in\textup{supp}(\sigma(\bm{X}))}\{\sigma_{i}(\bm{X})\}.

This yields

\displaystyle\bm{\pi}_{i}(\sigma(\bm{Y}))

\displaystyle\geq\sigma_{i}(\bm{X})-\|\bm{z}\|_{\infty}

\displaystyle>\sigma_{i}(\bm{X})-\min_{i\in\textup{supp}(\sigma(\bm{X}))}\{\sigma_{i}(\bm{X})\}\geq 0

for any
i\in\textup{supp}(\sigma(\bm{X}))
, which implies
\textup{supp}(\sigma(\bm{X}))\subseteq\textup{supp}(\bm{\pi}(\sigma(\bm{Y})))
. Moreover, since
\bm{X}\in\mathcal{F}_{L}
, we have
\textup{supp}(\sigma(\bm{X}))\neq\textup{supp}(\bm{\pi}(\sigma(\bm{Y})))
. Hence we obtain that
\textup{supp}(\sigma(\bm{X}))\subset\textup{supp}(\bm{\pi}(\sigma(\bm{Y})))
. This completes the proof.
∎

The following results show that the optimal solution sets of problem (
4
) and problem (
2
) are contained in the locally sparse sets.

Lemma 5
.

If
\bm{X}^{*}
solves problem (
4
) globally, then
\bm{X}^{*}\in\mathcal{F}_{L}
.

Proof.

If
\bm{X}^{*}\notin\mathcal{F}_{L}
and
\bm{X}^{*}=\bm{U}_{X^{*}}\mathcal{D}(\sigma(\bm{X}^{*}))\bm{V}_{X^{*}}^{\intercal}
, then there exists
Y^{*}\in\mathcal{F}\backslash\{\bm{X}^{*}\}
such that
\bm{Y}^{*}=\bm{U}_{X^{*}}\mathcal{D}(\bm{\pi}(\sigma(\bm{Y}^{*})))\bm{V}_{X^{*}}^{\intercal}
and
\textup{supp}(\bm{\pi}(\sigma(\bm{Y})))\subseteq\textup{supp}(\sigma(\bm{X}^{*}))
. Hence we can find a small enough
\epsilon>0
such that

\sigma(\bm{X}^{*})-\epsilon\bm{\pi}(\sigma(\bm{Y}^{*}))\geq 0.

Define
\bm{Z}^{*}:=\bm{U}_{X^{*}}\mathcal{D}(\frac{\sigma(\bm{X}^{*})-\epsilon\bm{\pi}(\sigma(\bm{Y}^{*}))}{1-\epsilon})\bm{V}_{X^{*}}^{\intercal}
. By directly calculating, it yields

\mathcal{A}(\bm{Z}^{*})=\frac{1}{1-\epsilon}\mathcal{A}(\bm{X}^{*})-\frac{\epsilon}{1-\epsilon}\mathcal{A}(\bm{Y}^{*})=\bm{b}

and
\|\bm{Z}^{*}\|_{*}=\frac{1}{1-\epsilon}\|\bm{X}^{*}\|_{*}-\frac{\epsilon}{1-\epsilon}\|\bm{Y}^{*}\|_{*}
due to the non-negativity of
\sigma_{i}(\bm{X}^{*})-\epsilon\bm{\pi}_{i}(\sigma(\bm{Y}^{*}))
. Moreover, it follows from
\bm{Y}^{*}\neq\bm{X}^{*}
and
\mathcal{A}(\bm{Y}^{*})=\mathcal{A}(\bm{X}^{*})
that they are linearly independent, and hence
\bm{Y}^{*}
and
\bm{Z}^{*}
are linearly independent. This implies

\|\bm{X}^{*}\|_{F}<\epsilon\|\bm{Y}^{*}\|_{F}+(1-\epsilon)\|\bm{Z}^{*}\|_{F}.

Therefore, it is obvious that

\displaystyle\|\bm{X}^{*}\|_{*}-\|\bm{X}\|_{F}

\displaystyle>

\displaystyle\epsilon(\|\bm{Y}^{*}\|_{*}-\|\bm{Y}^{*}\|_{F})+(1-\epsilon)(\|\bm{Z}^{*}\|_{*}-\|\bm{Z}^{*}\|_{F})

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\min\{\|\bm{Y}^{*}\|_{*}-\|\bm{Y}^{*}\|_{F},\|\bm{Z}^{*}\|_{*}-\|\bm{Z}^{*}\|_{F}\},

which contradicts with the optimality of
\bm{X}^{*}
.
∎

.

\bm{X}^{*}

\bm{X}^{*}\notin\mathcal{F}_{L}

\displaystyle\bm{Z}^{*}:=\frac{1}{1-r}\bm{X}^{*}-\frac{r}{1-r}\bm{Y}^{*}=\bm{U}_{X^{*}}\mathcal{D}(\frac{\sigma(\bm{X}^{*})-r\bm{\pi}(\sigma(\bm{Y}^{*}))}{1-r})\bm{V}_{X^{*}}^{\intercal},

\bm{z}
as
\frac{\sigma(\bm{X}^{*})-r\bm{\pi}(\sigma(\bm{Y}^{*}))}{1-r}
, then
\bm{z}_{k}=0
indicates
\textup{supp}(\sigma(\bm{Z}^{*}))\subsetneqq\textup{supp}(\sigma(\bm{X}^{*}))
, which contradicts with
\bm{X}^{*}
being optimal solution of problem (
2
).
∎

Now, we are in the position to present one of our main recovery result.

Theorem 7
.

If
\bm{X}^{*}
uniquely solves problem (
2
) with
\textup{rank}(\bm{X}^{*})=s
and
\min_{i\in\textup{supp}(\sigma(\bm{X}))}\sigma_{i}(\bm{X})>\frac{2(\sqrt{s}-1)}{\textup{rank}(\bm{X})-1}\|\bm{X}^{*}\|_{F}
for any
\bm{X}\in\mathcal{F}_{L}\setminus\{\bm{X}^{*}\}
, then
\bm{X}^{*}
also uniquely solves problem (
4
).

Proof.

First, we will show that for
\bm{X}\in\mathbb{R}^{m\times n}
,
(\textup{rank}(\bm{X})-1)/2\min_{i\in\textup{supp}(\sigma(\bm{X}))}\sigma_{i}(\bm{X})\leq\|\bm{X}\|_{*}-\|\bm{X}\|_{F}\leq(\sqrt{\textup{rank}(\bm{X})}-1)\|\bm{X}\|_{F}
. This upper bound can be immediately obtained from the Cauchy-Schwartz inequality. Next, we will give the lower bound.

By directly calculating, it yields

\displaystyle\|\bm{X}\|_{*}-\|\bm{X}\|_{F}

\displaystyle=\frac{\|\bm{X}\|_{*}^{2}-\|\bm{X}\|_{F}^{2}}{\|\bm{X}\|_{*}+\|\bm{X}\|_{F}}

\displaystyle=\frac{\sum_{i\neq j}\sigma_{i}(\bm{X})\sigma_{j}(\bm{X})}{\|\bm{X}\|_{*}+\|\bm{X}\|_{F}}

\displaystyle=\frac{\sum_{i\neq j\in\textup{supp}(\sigma(\bm{X}))}\sigma_{i}(\bm{X})\sigma_{j}(\bm{X})}{\|\bm{X}\|_{*}+\|\bm{X}\|_{F}}

\displaystyle\geq\frac{(\textup{rank}(\bm{X})-1)\|\bm{X}\|_{*}\min_{i\in\textup{supp}(\sigma(\bm{X}))}\sigma_{i}(\bm{X})}{\|\bm{X}\|_{*}+\|\bm{X}\|_{F}}

\displaystyle=\frac{\textup{rank}(\bm{X})-1}{1+\frac{\|\bm{X}\|_{F}}{\|\bm{X}\|_{*}}}\min_{i\in\textup{supp}(\sigma(\bm{X}))}\sigma_{i}(\bm{X})

\displaystyle\geq\frac{\textup{rank}(\bm{X})-1}{2}\min_{i\in\textup{supp}(\sigma(\bm{X}))}\sigma_{i}(\bm{X}).

Considering above discussion, we can directly obtain

\displaystyle\|\bm{X}^{*}\|_{*}-\|\bm{X}^{*}\|_{F}

\displaystyle\leq(\sqrt{s}-1)\|\bm{X}^{*}\|_{F}

\displaystyle<\frac{\textup{rank}(\bm{X})-1}{2}\min_{i\in\textup{supp}(\sigma(\bm{X}))}\sigma_{i}(\bm{X})

\displaystyle\leq\|\bm{X}\|_{*}-\|\bm{X}\|_{F}

for any
\bm{X}\in\mathcal{F}_{L}\setminus\{\bm{X}^{*}\}
, which makes sense according to
6
. Thus, the desired result follows directly from
5
.
∎

II-B

Exact Recovery Theory

In this subsection, we obtain some theoretical results to guarantee the robust recovery through the constrained
L_{1-2}
minimization problem (
4
) and problem (
6
). Our main results not only provide the sufficient conditions of stably recovering the desired matrix
X^{o}
, but also characterize the recovery errors with these two approaches.
Before proceeding, we provide essential preliminaries and related facts which are helpful to derive stable recovery conditions. We begin with the following fundamental properties respect to the function
\|\bm{X}\|_{*}-\|\bm{X}\|_{F}
.

Lemma 8
.

Suppose
\bm{X}\in\mathbb{R}^{m\times n}\backslash\{0\}
and
\textbf{rank}(\bm{X})=r
. Let
\bm{U}\mathcal{D}(\sigma(\bm{X}))\bm{V}^{\top}
be the SVD of
\bm{X}
, that is,
\bm{X}=\sum_{i=1}^{t}\sigma_{i}(\bm{X})\bm{U}_{i}\bm{V}_{i}^{\top}
. Denote
\Lambda:=\textup{supp}(\sigma(\bm{X}))
. Then, we have

(a)

(t-\sqrt{t})\sigma_{t}(\bm{X})\leq\|\bm{X}\|_{*}-\|\bm{X}\|_{F}\leq(\sqrt{t}-1)\|\bm{X}\|_{F};

(b)

(r-\sqrt{r})\sigma_{r}(\bm{X})\leq\|\bm{X}\|_{*}-\|\bm{X}\|_{F}\leq(\sqrt{r}-1)\|\bm{X}\|_{F};

(c)

\|\bm{X}\|_{*}-\|\bm{X}\|_{F}=0
if and only if
r=1
.

Proof.

(a) We can easily get the supper bound from the Cauchy-Schwartz inequality, and it suffices to give the lower bound. Define
r_{0}=\lfloor\sqrt{t}\rfloor
. We will give

\displaystyle\|\bm{X}\|_{F}=

\displaystyle\sqrt{\sum_{i}\sigma_{i}(\bm{X})^{2}}

(9)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{r_{0}}\sigma_{i}(\bm{X})+(\sqrt{t}-r_{0})\sigma_{r_{0}+1}(\bm{X}).

To achieve this, let the left side of (
9
) be
\rho_{l}
and the right side be
\rho_{r}
. On the one hand, we have

\displaystyle\rho_{l}^{2}

\displaystyle=\sum_{i=1}^{r_{0}}\sigma_{i}(\bm{X})^{2}+\sum_{i=r_{0}+1}^{t}\sigma_{i}(\bm{X})^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\sum_{i=1}^{r_{0}}\sigma_{i}(\bm{X})^{2}+(t-r_{0})\sigma_{r_{0}+1}(\bm{X})^{2}

and on the other hand by directly calculating, we obtain

\displaystyle\rho_{r}^{2}=

\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{r_{0}}\sigma_{i}(\bm{X})^{2}+2(\sqrt{t}-r_{0})\sigma_{r_{0}+1}(\bm{X})\sum_{i=1}^{r_{0}}\sigma_{i}(\bm{X})+\sum_{i=1}^{r_{0}}\sum_{\stackrel{{\scriptstyle j=1}}{{j\neq i}}}^{r_{0}}\sigma_{i}(\bm{X})\sigma_{j}(\bm{X})+(\sqrt{t}-r_{0})^{2}\sigma_{r_{0}+1}(\bm{X})^{2}

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{r_{0}}\sigma_{i}(\bm{X})^{2}+2(\sqrt{t}-r_{0})\sigma_{r_{0}+1}(\bm{X})\sum_{i=1}^{r_{0}}\sigma_{r_{0}+1}(\bm{X})+\sum_{i=1}^{r_{0}}\sum_{\stackrel{{\scriptstyle j=1}}{{j\neq i}}}^{r_{0}}\sigma_{r_{0}+1}(\bm{X})\sigma_{r_{0}+1}(\bm{X})+(\sqrt{t}-r_{0})^{2}\sigma_{r_{0}+1}(\bm{X})^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{r_{0}}\sigma_{i}(\bm{X})^{2}+(t-r_{0})\sigma_{r_{0}+1}(\bm{X})^{2},

and hence
\rho_{l}\leq\rho_{r}
, which implies (
9
).
Then it yields that

\displaystyle\|\bm{X}\|_{*}-\|\bm{X}\|_{F}\geq

\displaystyle\|\bm{X}\|_{*}-\sum_{i=1}^{r_{0}}\sigma_{i}(\bm{X})-(\sqrt{t}-r_{0})\sigma_{r_{0}+1}(\bm{X})

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\sum_{i=r_{0}+1}^{t}\sigma_{i}(\bm{X})-(\sqrt{t}-r_{0})\sigma_{r_{0}+1}(\bm{X})

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle(t-\sqrt{t})\sigma_{t}(\bm{X}).

This yields the desired results.

(b) Note that
|\Lambda|=r
. Define
\hat{\bm{X}}:=\sum_{i\in\Lambda}\sigma_{i}(\bm{X})\bm{U}_{i}\bm{V}_{i}^{\top}
. Obviously,

\|\bm{X}\|_{*}-\|\bm{X}\|_{F}=\|\hat{\bm{X}}\|_{*}-\|\hat{\bm{X}}\|_{F},

hence we can apply (a) to get the desired results.

(c) If
\|\bm{X}\|_{*}-\|\bm{X}\|_{F}=0
, we can obtain
(r-\sqrt{r})\sigma_{r}(\bm{X})=0
by employing the relation (a) and hence
r=1
. The other direction is easy.
∎

By a simple application of the parallelogram identity, we then have the next lemma, whose proof can be found in
[
33
]
.

Lemma 9
.

For all
\bm{X},\bm{X}^{\prime}
obeying
\langle\bm{X},\bm{X}^{\prime}\rangle=0
, and
\textbf{rank}(\bm{X})\leq r
,
\textbf{rank}(\bm{X}^{\prime})\leq r^{\prime}
, we have

|\langle\mathcal{A}(\bm{X}),\mathcal{A}(\bm{X}^{\prime})\rangle|\leq\delta_{r+r^{\prime}}\|\bm{X}\|_{F}\|\bm{X}^{\prime}\|_{F},

where
\delta_{r+r^{\prime}}
is
(r+r^{\prime})
-isometry constant defined by Definition
2
.

To show the main results, the following lemma is also necessary.

Lemma 10
.

Fix positive integer
r
and
\hat{t}
. Let
m_{1},m_{2},n_{1},n_{2}
be nonnegative integers which satisfy
m_{1}+m_{2}=n_{1}+n_{2}=r
. Then for
k\in[\hat{t}]
, it holds

\displaystyle\min_{m_{1},m_{2},n_{1},n_{2}}\max\{m_{1}+n_{1}+k,m_{2}+n_{2}+2k\}=\max\{r+\lceil\frac{3}{2}k\rceil,2k\}.

(10)

Proof.

Obviously, it holds

\max\{m_{1}+n_{1}+k,m_{2}+n_{2}+2k\}\geq r+\lceil\frac{3}{2}k\rceil

(11)

for any
k\in[\hat{t}]
since
m_{1}+n_{1}+k+n_{1}+n_{2}+2k=2r+3k
.

For
k\geq 2r
. Following from
m_{1}+n_{1}\leq 2r
, it is known that
\max\{m_{1}+n_{1}+k,m_{2}+n_{2}+2k\}=m_{2}+n_{2}+2k
and then we have
\min_{m_{1},m_{2},n_{1},n_{2}}\max\{m_{1}+n_{1}+k,m_{2}+n_{2}+2k\}=2k
by setting
m_{2}=n_{2}=0
.

For
k<2r
. It is known that
r+\lceil\frac{3}{2}k\rceil\geq r+\frac{3}{2}k>2k
. In fact, we can show
\max\{m_{1}+n_{1}+k,m_{2}+n_{2}+2k\}=r+\lceil\frac{3}{2}k\rceil
by setting an appropriate
m_{1},m_{2},n_{1},n_{2}
. If
k
is odd, we set

\displaystyle m_{1}=\lceil\frac{r}{2}+\frac{k}{4}+\frac{1}{4}\rceil;

\displaystyle m_{2}=r-\lceil\frac{r}{2}+\frac{k}{4}+\frac{1}{4}\rceil;

\displaystyle n_{1}=r+\frac{k}{2}+\frac{1}{2}-\lceil\frac{r}{2}+\frac{k}{4}+\frac{1}{4}\rceil;

\displaystyle n_{2}=\lceil\frac{r}{2}+\frac{k}{4}+\frac{1}{4}\rceil-\frac{k}{2}-\frac{1}{2}.

If
k
is even, we set

\displaystyle m_{1}=\lceil\frac{r}{2}+\frac{k}{4}\rceil;

\displaystyle m_{2}=r-\lceil\frac{r}{2}+\frac{k}{4}\rceil;

\displaystyle n_{1}=r+\frac{1}{2}k-\lceil\frac{r}{2}+\frac{k}{4}\rceil;

\displaystyle n_{2}=\lceil\frac{r}{2}+\frac{k}{4}\rceil-\frac{k}{2}.

This indicates that

\min_{m_{1},m_{2},n_{1},n_{2}}\max\{m_{1}+n_{1}+k,m_{2}+n_{2}+2k\}=r+\lceil\frac{3}{2}k\rceil.

Combining the above two cases, we obtain the desired results.
∎

In view of above lemmas, we are ready to give our main results for recovery analysis via constrained
L_{1-2}
minimization problem. We first present the recovery estimation in the noise-free case, i.e., the vector
\bm{s}=0
in (
5
). The proof is based on the block
decomposition of a matrix and the technique result of
Lemma
10
. Denote
\bm{U}\mathcal{D}(\sigma(\bm{X}^{o}))\bm{V}^{\top}
as SVD of
\bm{X}^{o}
. For any fixed positive integer
r\leq t
, the best rank-r approximation matrix
\bm{X}^{o}_{r}
of
\bm{X}^{o}
is defined as
\bm{U}\mathcal{D}(\bm{x}^{r})\bm{V}^{\top}
where
\bm{x}^{r}\in\mathbb{R}^{t}
and
x^{r}_{i}=\sigma_{i}(\bm{X}^{o})
for
i\in[r]
and

otherwise.

Theorem 11
.

Let
\mathcal{A}:\mathbb{R}^{m\times n}\rightarrow\mathbb{R}^{l}
be a linear map and
\bm{b}\in\mathbb{R}^{l}
. Set the desired matrix
\bm{X}^{o}
with

\beta:=\cfrac{\sqrt{2}\delta_{\max\{r+\lceil\frac{3}{2}k\rceil,2k\}}(\sqrt{2r}+1)}{(1-\delta_{2r+k})(\sqrt{k}-1)}<1,

(12)

then we obtain that any optimal solution
\hat{\bm{X}}^{*}
of problem (

\|\bm{X}^{o}-\hat{\bm{X}}^{*}\|_{F}\leq\alpha\|\bm{X}^{o}-X_{r}^{o}\|_{F},

where

\bm{X}^{o}
is
r

\bm{X}^{o}

Proof.

Define
\bm{Z}:=\hat{\bm{X}}^{*}-\bm{X}^{o}

.

(

m_{1}+m_{2}+m_{3}=m

r_{1}:=m_{1}+n_{1}+k,~~r_{2}:=m_{2}+n_{2}+2k,~~r_{3}=\max\{r_{1},r_{2}\}.

In the sequel, we get a block decomposition of
\bm{Z}
with respect to
\bm{X}^{o}
as follows:

\bm{U}^{\top}\bm{Z}\bm{V}:=\begin{pmatrix}\bm{Z}_{11}&\bm{Z}_{12}&\bm{Z}_{13}\\
\bm{Z}_{21}&\bm{Z}_{22}&\bm{Z}_{23}\\
\bm{Z}_{31}&\bm{Z}_{32}&\bm{Z}_{33}\end{pmatrix}

(13)

with

(

Define

\bm{Z}_{21}&0&0\\
\bm{Z}_{31}&0&0\end{array}\right)\bm{V}^{\top},\quad\bm{Z}_{2}:=\bm{U}\left(\begin{array}[]{ccc}0&0&0\\

Naturally, we can decompose

\bm{Z}=\bm{Z}_{1}+\bm{Z}_{2}+\bm{Z}_{3}=\bm{Z}^{r}+\bm{Z}^{r_{c}},

(14)

where
\bm{Z}^{r}:=\bm{Z}_{1}+\bm{Z}_{2}
,
\bm{Z}^{r_{c}}:=\bm{Z}_{3}
.
It is known that
\textbf{rank}(\bm{Z}_{1})\leq m_{1}+n_{1}

\textbf{rank}(\bm{Z}_{2})\leq m_{2}+n_{2}
and
\textbf{rank}(\bm{Z}_{1}+\bm{Z}_{2})\leq 2r
. Also,
\bm{Z}_{1}
,
\bm{Z}_{2}
and
\bm{Z}_{3}
are orthogonal each other. Then, we have

\displaystyle\|\bm{X}^{o}+\bm{Z}\|_{*}-\|\bm{X}^{o}+\bm{Z}\|_{F}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\|\bm{X}^{o}_{r}+\bm{X}^{o}_{r_{c}}+\bm{Z}^{r}+\bm{Z}^{r_{c}}\|_{*}-\|\bm{X}^{o}+\bm{Z}^{r}+\bm{Z}^{r_{c}}\|_{F}

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\|\bm{X}^{o}_{r}+\bm{Z}^{r_{c}}\|_{*}-\|\bm{X}^{o}_{r_{c}}\|_{*}-\|\bm{Z}^{r}\|_{*}-\|\bm{X}^{o}\|_{F}-\|\bm{Z}^{r}\|_{F}-\|\bm{Z}^{r_{c}}\|_{F}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\|\bm{X}^{o}_{r}\|_{*}+\|\bm{Z}^{r_{c}}\|_{*}-\|\bm{X}^{o}_{r_{c}}\|_{*}-\|\bm{Z}^{r}\|_{*}-\|\bm{X}^{o}\|_{F}-\|\bm{Z}^{r}\|_{F}-\|\bm{Z}^{r_{c}}\|_{F}.

Here the last equality follows from
[

with
\bm{X}^{o}_{r}{\bm{Z}^{r_{c}}}^{\top}=0
and
{\bm{X}^{o}_{r}}^{\top}\bm{Z}^{r_{c}}=0
. Besides, we can get

\|\hat{\bm{X}}^{*}\|_{*}-\|\hat{\bm{X}}^{*}\|_{F}\leq\|\bm{X}^{o}\|_{*}-\|\bm{X}^{o}\|_{F}

due to the optimality of
\hat{\bm{X}}^{*}
. Hence we obtain

\|\bm{Z}^{r_{c}}\|_{*}-\|\bm{Z}^{r_{c}}\|_{F}\leq\|\bm{Z}^{r}\|_{*}+\|\bm{Z}^{r}\|_{F}+2\|\bm{X}^{o}_{r_{c}}\|_{*}.

(15)

Denote
\bm{P}\mathcal{D}(\sigma(\bm{Z}_{33}))\bm{Q}^{\top}
as the SVD of
\bm{Z}_{33}
where
\sigma(\bm{Z}_{33})\in\mathbb{R}^{\hat{t}}
and

,

0&0&0\\
0&0&\bm{P}\mathcal{D}(\sigma_{T_{i}}(\bm{Z}_{33}))\bm{Q}^{\top}\end{array}\right)\bm{V}^{\top}

(16)

for

. Hence
\bm{Z}_{1}
,
\bm{Z}_{2}
,
\bm{Z}_{T_{i}}
are all orthogonal to each other and

. Then, on the one hand, we obtain

\displaystyle\|\mathcal{A}(\bm{Z}_{1}+\bm{Z}_{2}+\bm{Z}_{T_{1}})\|_{2}^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\langle\mathcal{A}(\bm{Z}_{1}+\bm{Z}_{2}+\bm{Z}_{T_{1}}),\mathcal{A}(\bm{Z}_{1}+\bm{Z}_{2}+\bm{Z}_{T_{1}})\rangle

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\langle\mathcal{A}(\bm{Z}_{1}+\bm{Z}_{2}+\bm{Z}_{T_{1}}),\mathcal{A}(\bm{Z})\rangle-\langle\mathcal{A}(\bm{Z}_{1}+\bm{Z}_{2}+\bm{Z}_{T_{1}}),\mathcal{A}(\bm{Z}_{3}-\bm{Z}_{T_{1}})\rangle

\displaystyle-\langle\mathcal{A}(\bm{Z}_{1}+\bm{Z}_{2}+\bm{Z}_{T_{1}}),\mathcal{A}(\bm{Z}_{3}-\bm{Z}_{T_{1}})\rangle.

On the other hand, direct calculation yields

\displaystyle|\langle\mathcal{A}(\bm{Z}_{1}+\bm{Z}_{2}+\bm{Z}_{T_{1}}),\mathcal{A}(\bm{Z}_{3}-\bm{Z}_{T_{1}})\rangle|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle|\langle\mathcal{A}(\bm{Z}_{1}),\mathcal{A}(\bm{Z}_{3}-\bm{Z}_{T_{1}})\rangle+\langle\mathcal{A}(\bm{Z}_{2}+\bm{Z}_{T_{1}}),\mathcal{A}(\bm{Z}_{3}-\bm{Z}_{T_{1}})\rangle|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\sum_{i\geq 2}|\langle\mathcal{A}(\bm{Z}_{1}),\mathcal{A}(\bm{Z}_{T_{i}})\rangle|+\sum_{i\geq 2}|\langle\mathcal{A}(\bm{Z}_{2}+\bm{Z}_{T_{1}}),\mathcal{A}(\bm{Z}_{T_{i}})\rangle|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle(\delta_{r_{1}}\|\bm{Z}_{1}\|_{F}+\delta_{r_{2}}\|\bm{Z}_{2}+\bm{Z}_{T_{1}}\|_{F})\sum_{i\geq 2}\|\bm{Z}_{T_{i}}\|_{F}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\sqrt{2}\delta_{r_{3}}\|\bm{Z}_{1}+\bm{Z}_{2}+\bm{Z}_{T_{1}}\|_{F}\sum_{i\geq 2}\|\bm{Z}_{T_{i}}\|_{F},

where the second inequality comes from Lemma

Easily, invoking Lemma

\displaystyle|\langle\mathcal{A}(\bm{Z}_{1}+\bm{Z}_{2}+\bm{Z}_{T_{1}}),\mathcal{A}(\bm{Z}_{3}-\bm{Z}_{T_{1}})\rangle|

\displaystyle\sqrt{2}\delta_{\max\{r+\lceil\frac{3}{2}k\rceil,2k\}}\|\bm{Z}_{1}+\bm{Z}_{2}+\bm{Z}_{T_{1}}\|_{F}\sum_{i\geq 2}\|\bm{Z}_{T_{i}}\|_{F}

since the arbitrariness of

.

Hence

\displaystyle\|\mathcal{A}(\bm{Z}_{1}+\bm{Z}_{2}+\bm{Z}_{T_{1}})\|_{2}^{2}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\sqrt{2}\delta_{\max\{r+\lceil\frac{3}{2}k\rceil,2k\}}\|\bm{Z}_{1}+\bm{Z}_{2}+\bm{Z}_{T_{1}}\|_{F}\sum_{i\geq 2}\|\bm{Z}_{T_{i}}\|_{F},

Now we will give an upper bound for the right side of (

with

\sigma_{q}\leq\min_{p\in T_{i-1}}\sigma_{p}(\bm{Z}_{33})\leq\cfrac{\|\bm{Z}_{T_{i-1}}\|_{*}-\|\bm{Z}_{T_{i-1}}\|_{F}}{k-\sqrt{k}},

which implies

\|\sigma_{T_{i}}(\bm{Z}_{33})\|_{2}\leq\sqrt{k}\cfrac{\|\bm{Z}_{T_{i-1}}\|_{*}-\|\bm{Z}_{T_{i-1}}\|_{F}}{k-\sqrt{k}}.

By direct calculation, we have

\displaystyle\sum_{i=2}^{h}\|\bm{Z}_{T_{i}}\|_{F}\leq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\sqrt{k}-1}\sum_{i=2}^{h}(\|\bm{Z}_{T_{i-1}}\|_{*}-\|\bm{Z}_{T_{i-1}}\|_{F})

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\sqrt{k}-1}\sum_{i=1}^{h}(\|\bm{Z}_{T_{i}}\|_{*}-\|\bm{Z}_{T_{i}}\|_{F})

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\sqrt{k}-1}(\|\bm{Z}_{3}\|_{*}-\|\bm{Z}_{3}\|_{F})

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{\sqrt{2r}+1}{\sqrt{k}-1}\|\bm{Z}_{1}+\bm{Z}_{2}\|_{F}+\frac{2}{\sqrt{k}-1}\|\bm{X}^{o}_{r_{c}}\|_{*},

(18)

where the third inequality holds since

and the last inequality comes from (

Combining with (

\|\bm{Z}_{1}+\bm{Z}_{2}+\bm{Z}_{T_{1}}\|_{F}\leq\frac{2\beta}{(\sqrt{2r}+1)(1-\beta)}\|\bm{X}^{o}_{r_{c}}\|_{*}

(19)

since

from the assumption.
Then we have

\displaystyle\|\bm{Z}\|_{F}

\displaystyle\leq\|\bm{Z}_{1}+\bm{Z}_{2}+\bm{Z}_{T_{1}}\|_{F}+\sum_{i=2}^{h}\|\bm{Z}_{T_{i}}\|_{F}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{\sqrt{k}+\sqrt{2r}}{\sqrt{k}-1}\|\bm{Z}_{1}+\bm{Z}_{2}+\bm{Z}_{T_{1}}\|_{F}+\frac{2}{\sqrt{k}-1}\|\bm{X}^{o}_{r_{c}}\|_{*}

where the first inequality follows from (

) and the last inequality follows from (

This indicates

\|\hat{\bm{X}}^{*}-\bm{X}^{o}\|_{F}\leq\alpha\|\bm{X}^{o}_{r_{c}}\|_{*}.

Hence, we get the desired results. The special case
\textbf{rank}(\bm{X}^{o})\leq r
implies
\bm{X}^{o}=\bm{X}^{o}_{r}
and hence
\hat{\bm{X}}^{*}=\bm{X}^{o}
is trivial. Thus, we complete the proof.
∎

Naturally, by setting different values of
k
, we can get different bounds on isometry constant. When choosing
k=2r
, we obtain the following RIP condition involved in
\delta_{4r}
.

Corollary 12
.

Let
\mathcal{A}:\mathbb{R}^{m\times n}\rightarrow\mathbb{R}^{l}
be a linear map and
\bm{b}\in\mathbb{R}^{l}
. Set the desired matrix
\bm{X}^{o}
satisfies
\mathcal{A}(\bm{X}^{o})=\bm{b}
, that implies the vector
\bm{s}=0
in (
5
). For a given positive integer
r\leq t
, if linear map
\mathcal{A}
obeys

\delta_{4r}<\cfrac{\sqrt{2r}-1}{\sqrt{2r}-1+\sqrt{2}(\sqrt{2r}+1)},

(20)

then any optimal solution
\hat{\bm{X}}^{*}
of problem (
4
) satisfies

\|\bm{X}^{o}-\hat{\bm{X}}^{*}\|_{F}\leq\hat{\alpha}\|\bm{X}^{o}-X_{r}^{o}\|_{F},

where
\hat{\alpha}:=\cfrac{2+(2\sqrt{2}-2)\delta_{4r}}{(\sqrt{2r}-1)-[(\sqrt{2r}-1)+\sqrt{2}(\sqrt{2r}+1)]\delta_{4r}}
. Moreover, if
\bm{X}^{o}
is
r
-rank matrix, the unique minimizer of problem (
4
) is exactly
\bm{X}^{o}
.

Next we shall establish the recovery estimation of constrained
L_{*-F}
minimization when measurements are contaminated by noise.

Theorem 13
.

Under the assumptions of Theorem
11
except that the desired matrix
\bm{X}^{o}
satisfies

\mathcal{A}(\bm{X}^{o})+\bm{s}=\bm{b}
with perturbation
\|\bm{s}\|_{2}\leq\epsilon
, we have that the optimal solution
\bm{X}^{opt}
of problem (
6
)
satisfies

\|\bm{X}^{o}-\bm{X}^{opt}\|_{F}\leq\alpha\|\bm{X}^{o}-X_{r}^{o}\|_{F}+\bar{\alpha}\epsilon,

where

\alpha:=\cfrac{2(\sqrt{2r}+1)+2\beta(\sqrt{k}-1)}{(\sqrt{k}-1)(1-\beta)(\sqrt{2r}+1)},

and

\bar{\alpha}:=\cfrac{2(\sqrt{k}+\sqrt{2r})\sqrt{1+\delta_{2r+k}}}{(\sqrt{k}-1)(1-\beta)(1-\delta_{2r+k})}.

Moreover, if
\bm{X}^{o}
is
r
-rank matrix, then it yields

\|\bm{X}^{o}-\bm{X}^{opt}\|_{F}\leq\bar{\alpha}\epsilon.

Proof.

Let
\bm{X}^{opt}=\bm{X}^{o}+\bm{Z}
, then starting from the block decomposition of
\bm{Z}

with respect to
\bm{X}^{o}
and the fact

\|\bm{X}^{opt}\|_{*}-\|\bm{X}^{opt}\|_{F}\leq\|\bm{X}^{o}\|_{*}-\|\bm{X}^{o}\|_{F},

we repeat the arguments in the proof of Theorem
11
and obtain

\displaystyle-\sqrt{2}\delta_{\max\{r+\lceil\frac{3}{2}k\rceil,2k\}}(\|\bm{Z}_{1}+\bm{Z}_{2}+\bm{Z}_{T_{1}}\|_{F})\sum_{i\geq 2}^{h}\|\bm{Z}_{T_{i}}\|_{F}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\langle\mathcal{A}(\bm{Z}_{1}+\bm{Z}_{2}+\bm{Z}_{T_{1}}),\mathcal{A}(\bm{Z})\rangle-\|\mathcal{A}(\bm{Z}_{1}+\bm{Z}_{2}+\bm{Z}_{T_{1}})\|_{2}^{2}

and

\sum_{i=2}^{h}\|\bm{Z}_{T_{i}}\|_{F}\leq\frac{\sqrt{2r}+1}{\sqrt{k}-1}\|\bm{Z}_{1}+\bm{Z}_{2}\|_{F}+\frac{2}{\sqrt{k}-1}\|\bm{X}^{o}_{r_{c}}\|_{*}.

(21)

Easily, we can find

\displaystyle-\sqrt{2}\delta_{\max\{r+\lceil\frac{3}{2}k\rceil,2k\}}(\|\bm{Z}_{1}+\bm{Z}_{2}+\bm{Z}_{T_{1}}\|_{F})\sum_{i\geq 2}^{h}\|\bm{Z}_{T_{i}}\|_{F}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle 2\epsilon\sqrt{1+\delta_{2r+k}}\|\bm{Z}_{1}+\bm{Z}_{2}+\bm{Z}_{T_{1}}\|_{F}-(1-\delta_{2r+k})\|\bm{Z}_{1}+\bm{Z}_{2}+\bm{Z}_{T_{1}}\|_{F}^{2},

which together with (
21
) yields

\displaystyle(1-\delta_{2r+k})\|\bm{Z}_{1}+\bm{Z}_{2}+\bm{Z}_{T_{1}}\|_{F}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\sqrt{2}\delta_{\max\{r+\lceil\frac{3}{2}k\rceil,2k\}}\frac{\sqrt{2r}+1}{\sqrt{k}-1}\|\bm{Z}_{1}+\bm{Z}_{2}\|_{F}+\frac{2\sqrt{2}\delta_{\max\{r+\lceil\frac{3}{2}k\rceil,2k\}}}{\sqrt{k}-1}\|\bm{X}^{o}_{r_{c}}\|_{*}+2\sqrt{1+\delta_{2r+k}}\epsilon.

Since

\beta:=\cfrac{\sqrt{2}\delta_{\max\{r+\lceil\frac{3}{2}k\rceil,2k\}}(\sqrt{2r}+1)}{(1-\delta_{2r+k})(\sqrt{k}-1)}<1

and
\|\bm{Z}_{1}+\bm{Z}_{2}\|_{F}\leq\|\bm{Z}_{1}+\bm{Z}_{2}+\bm{Z}_{T_{1}}\|_{F},
we have

\displaystyle\|\bm{Z}_{1}+\bm{Z}_{2}+\bm{Z}_{T_{1}}\|_{F}\leq\cfrac{2\sqrt{2}\delta_{\max\{r+\lceil\frac{3}{2}k\rceil,2k\}}}{(1-\delta_{2r+k})(\sqrt{k}-1)(1-\beta)}\|\bm{X}^{o}_{r_{c}}\|_{*}+\cfrac{2\sqrt{1+\delta_{2r+k}}}{(1-\delta_{2r+k})(1-\beta)}\epsilon.

(22)

Therefore, direct calculation yields

\displaystyle\|\bm{Z}\|_{F}\leq

\displaystyle\|\bm{Z}_{1}+\bm{Z}_{2}+\bm{Z}_{T_{1}}\|_{F}+\sum_{i\geq 2}^{h}\|\bm{Z}_{T_{i}}\|_{F}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{\sqrt{k}+\sqrt{2r}}{\sqrt{k}-1}\|\bm{Z}_{1}+\bm{Z}_{2}+\bm{Z}_{T_{1}}\|_{F}+\frac{2}{\sqrt{k}-1}\|\bm{X}^{o}_{r_{c}}\|_{*}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\alpha\|\bm{X}^{o}_{r_{c}}\|_{*}+\bar{\alpha}\epsilon.

Here the first inequality comes from (
21
), and the last inequality comes from (

) and the definitions of
\alpha
and
\bar{\alpha}
. Additionally, from the above discussion, the desired result is easily obtained when
\bm{X}^{o}
is

-rank matrix. Thus, we complete the proof.
∎

III
Main Results on Unconstrained Minimization

III-A

Relation Between Problem (
4
) and Problem (
7
)

We now show that in some sense, problem (
4
) can be solved via solving problem (
7
). We note that the regularization term
\|\bm{X}\|_{*}-\|\bm{X}\|_{F}
is nonconvex and nonsmooth, hence the result is nontrivial.

Theorem 14
.

Assume linear map
\mathcal{A}
obeys
\delta_{1}<1
. Let
\{\lambda_{n}\}
be a decreasing sequence of positive numbers with
\lambda_{n}\rightarrow 0
and
\bar{\bm{X}}_{\lambda_{n}}
be the optimal solution of the problem (
7
) with
\lambda=\lambda_{n}
. If problem (
4
) is feasible, then sequence
\{\bar{\bm{X}}_{\lambda_{n}}\}
is bounded and any of its accumulation points is the optimal solution of the problem (
4
).

Proof.

Let
\bar{\bm{X}}
be any feasible point of problem (
4
), then
\mathcal{A}(\bar{\bm{X}})=\bm{b}
. Since
\bar{\bm{X}}_{\lambda_{n}}
is the optimal solution of problem (
7
) with respect to
\lambda=\lambda_{n}
, we have

\displaystyle\|\bar{\bm{X}}_{\lambda_{n}}\|_{*}-\|\bar{\bm{X}}_{\lambda_{n}}\|_{F}+\frac{1}{2\lambda_{n}}\|\mathcal{A}(\bar{\bm{X}}_{\lambda_{n}})-\bm{b}\|_{2}^{2}\leq\|\bar{\bm{X}}\|_{*}-\|\bar{\bm{X}}\|_{F}.

(23)

Since
\lambda_{n}\rightarrow 0
as
n\rightarrow\infty
, the sequences
\{\|\bar{\bm{X}}_{\lambda_{n}}\|_{*}-\|\bar{\bm{X}}_{\lambda_{n}}\|_{F}\}
and
\{\|\mathcal{A}(\bar{\bm{X}}_{\lambda_{n}})-\bm{b}\|_{2}\}
converge to zero and so they are bounded.

Next we will show that the sequence
\{\bar{\bm{X}}_{\lambda_{n}}\}
is bounded and hence it has at least one accumulation point.
Denote
\bm{U}_{\lambda_{n}}\mathcal{D}(\sigma(\bar{\bm{X}}_{\lambda_{n}}))\bm{V}_{\lambda_{n}}
as the SVD of
\bar{\bm{X}}_{\lambda_{n}}
. Define

\bar{\bm{X}}_{\lambda_{n}}^{1}:=\bm{U}_{\lambda_{n}}\mathcal{D}(\sigma^{1}(\bar{\bm{X}}_{\lambda_{n}}))\bm{V}_{\lambda_{n}},

and

\bar{\bm{X}}_{\lambda_{n}}^{2}:=\bm{U}_{\lambda_{n}}\mathcal{D}(\sigma^{2}(\bar{\bm{X}}_{\lambda_{n}}))\bm{V}_{\lambda_{n}},

where
\sigma^{1}_{i}(\bar{\bm{X}}_{\lambda_{n}})=\sigma_{i}(\bar{\bm{X}}_{\lambda_{n}})
for
i\leq 2
,
\sigma_{i}^{2}(\bar{\bm{X}}_{\lambda_{n}})=\sigma_{i}(\bar{\bm{X}}_{\lambda_{n}})
for
i>2
and others are
0
. Likewise, let us define matrices

\bm{Y}^{1}_{\lambda_{n}}:=\bm{U}_{\lambda_{n}}\mathcal{D}(\bm{y}^{1}_{\lambda_{n}})\bm{V}_{\lambda_{n}},~~\bm{Y}^{2}_{\lambda_{n}}:=\bm{U}_{\lambda_{n}}\mathcal{D}(\bm{y}^{2}_{\lambda_{n}})\bm{V}_{\lambda_{n}},

where
(\bm{y}^{1}_{\lambda_{n}})_{i}=\sigma_{i}(\bar{\bm{X}}_{\lambda_{n}})
for
i=1
,
(\bm{y}^{2}_{\lambda_{n}})_{i}=\sigma_{i}(\bar{\bm{X}}_{\lambda_{n}})
for
i>1
and others are
0
. Clearly, we have

\bar{\bm{X}}_{\lambda_{n}}=\bar{\bm{X}}_{\lambda_{n}}^{1}+\bar{\bm{X}}_{\lambda_{n}}^{2}=\bm{Y}^{1}_{\lambda_{n}}+\bm{Y}^{2}_{\lambda_{n}}.

By direct computation, it follows from Lemma
8
(b) that

\displaystyle\|\bar{\bm{X}}_{\lambda_{n}}\|_{*}-\|\bar{\bm{X}}_{\lambda_{n}}\|_{F}\geq

\displaystyle\|\bar{\bm{X}}_{\lambda_{n}}^{1}\|_{*}+\|\bar{\bm{X}}_{\lambda_{n}}^{2}\|_{*}-\|\bar{\bm{X}}_{\lambda_{n}}^{1}\|_{F}-\|\bar{\bm{X}}_{\lambda_{n}}^{2}\|_{F}

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\|\bar{\bm{X}}_{\lambda_{n}}^{1}\|_{*}-\|\bar{\bm{X}}_{\lambda_{n}}^{1}\|_{F}

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle(2-\sqrt{2})\sigma_{2}(\bar{\bm{X}}_{\lambda_{n}}),

which, together with the boundedness of the sequence
\{\|\bar{\bm{X}}_{\lambda_{n}}\|_{*}-\|\bar{\bm{X}}_{\lambda_{n}}\|_{F}\}
, implies the sequence
\{\|\bm{Y}^{2}_{\lambda_{n}}\|_{F}\}
is bounded. Hence, the inequality

\|\mathcal{A}(\bm{Y}^{2}_{\lambda_{n}})-\bm{b}\|_{2}\leq\|\mathcal{A}\|\|\bm{Y}^{2}_{\lambda_{n}}\|_{F}+\|\bm{b}\|_{2}

yields the sequence
\{\|\mathcal{A}(\bm{Y}^{2}_{\lambda_{n}})-\bm{b}\|_{2}\}
is bounded where
\|\mathcal{A}\|
is the operator norm of linear map
\mathcal{A}
. As a result, since
\{\|\mathcal{A}(\bar{\bm{X}}_{\lambda_{n}})-\bm{b}\|_{2}\}
is bounded and

\|\mathcal{A}(\bm{Y}^{1}_{\lambda_{n}})\|_{2}\leq\|\mathcal{A}(\bar{\bm{X}}_{\lambda_{n}})-b\|_{2}+\|\mathcal{A}(\bm{Y}^{2}_{\lambda_{n}})-\bm{b}\|_{2},

we obtain that the sequence
\{\|\mathcal{A}(\bm{Y}^{1}_{\lambda_{n}})\|_{2}\}
is bounded. Furthermore, since linear map
\mathcal{A}
obeys
\delta_{1}<1
, it follows from (
8
) that the sequence
\{\|\bm{Y}^{1}_{\lambda_{n}}\|_{F}\}
is also bounded. Hence, the boundedness of
\{\bar{\bm{X}}_{\lambda_{n}}\}
can be seen from
\bar{\bm{X}}_{\lambda_{n}}=\bm{Y}^{1}_{\lambda_{n}}+\bm{Y}^{2}_{\lambda_{n}}
and the boundedness of
\{\|\bm{Y}^{1}_{\lambda_{n}}\|_{F}\}
and
\{\|\bm{Y}^{2}_{\lambda_{n}}\|_{F}\}
.

Besides, the inequality (
23
) shows

\frac{1}{2}\|\mathcal{A}(\bar{\bm{X}}_{\lambda_{n}})-\bm{b}\|_{2}^{2}\leq\lambda_{n}(\|\bar{\bm{X}}\|_{*}-\|\bar{\bm{X}}\|_{F})

for any
\lambda_{n}\rightarrow 0
. Let
\bm{X}^{*}
be any accumulation point of the sequence
\{\bar{\bm{X}}_{\lambda_{n}}\}
. Then we can derive that
\mathcal{A}(\bm{X}^{*})=\bm{b}
, which combines
\|\bm{X}^{*}\|_{*}-\|\bm{X}^{*}\|_{F}\leq\|\bar{\bm{X}}\|_{*}-\|\bar{\bm{X}}\|_{F}
and the arbitrariness of
\bar{\bm{X}}
lead to
\bm{X}^{*}
being the optimal solution of problem (
4
). This completes the proof.
∎

III-B

Recovery Error Estimation for Problem (
7
)

In this subsection, we provide some theoretical investigations to guarantee the robust recovery of the regularized
L_{1-2}
minimization problem (
7
). Let us start with some powerfully technical tools used in the proof of our main results. The following lemma states an elementary geometric fact: any point in a ploytope can be represented as a convex combination of sparse vectors.

Lemma 15
.

[Sparse Representation of a Polytope
[
21
]
]
For a positive number
\alpha
and a positive integer
s
, define the polytope
T(\alpha,s)\subset\mathbb{R}^{p}
by

T(\alpha,s)=\{\bm{v}\in\mathbb{R}^{p}:\|\bm{v}\|_{\infty}\leq\alpha,\|\bm{v}\|_{1}\leq s\alpha\}.

For any
\bm{v}\in\mathbb{R}^{p}
, define the set of sparse vectors
\mathcal{U}(\alpha,s,\bm{v})\subset\mathbb{R}^{p}
by

\displaystyle\mathcal{U}(\alpha,s,\bm{v})=\{\bm{u}\in\mathbb{R}^{p}:\textup{supp}(\bm{u})\subseteq\textup{supp}(\bm{v}),\|\bm{u}\|_{0}\leq s,\|\bm{u}\|_{1}=\|\bm{v}\|_{1},\|\bm{u}\|_{\infty}\leq\alpha\}.

(24)

Then
\bm{v}\in T(\alpha,s)
if and only if
\bm{v}
is in the convex hull of
\mathcal{U}(\alpha,s,\bm{v})
. In particularly, any
\bm{v}\in T(\alpha,s)
can be expressed as

\bm{v}=\sum_{i=1}^{N}\lambda_{i}\bm{u}_{i}

for some positive integer
N
, where
\bm{u}_{i}\in\mathcal{U}(\alpha,s,\bm{v})
and

\sum_{i=1}^{N}\lambda_{i}=1\quad\text{with}\ \ 0\leq\lambda_{i}\leq 1.

(25)

The following lemma established in
[
34
]
is also necessary which give an inequality between the sums of the
\alpha
th power of two sequences of nonnegative numbers based on the inequality of their sums.

Lemma 16
.

Suppose
m\geq r
,
a_{1}\geq a_{2}\geq\cdots\geq a_{m}\geq 0
, and
\sum_{i=1}^{r}a_{i}\geq\sum_{i=r+1}^{m}a_{i}
, then for all
\alpha\geq 1
,

\sum_{j=r+1}^{m}a_{j}^{\alpha}\leq\sum_{i=1}^{r}a_{i}^{\alpha}.

More generally, suppose
a_{1}\geq a_{2}\geq\cdots\geq a_{m}\geq 0
,
\eta\geq 0
and
\sum_{i=1}^{r}a_{i}+\eta\geq\sum_{i=r+1}^{m}a_{i}
, then for all
\alpha\geq 1
,

\sum_{j=r+1}^{m}a_{j}^{\alpha}\leq r\left(\sqrt[\alpha]{\cfrac{\sum_{i=1}^{r}a_{i}^{\alpha}}{r}}+\frac{\eta}{r}\right)^{\alpha}.

With the above preparation, we may state and prove the critical results, which play key role in recovery estimation of regularized minimization.

Lemma 17
.

Let
k
be a positive integer and linear map
\mathcal{A}
obey the
tk
-order RIP with
\delta_{tk}\in(0,1)
for certain integer
t>1
. Then for any subset
T\subseteq[n]
with
|T|\leq k
and any matrix
\bm{H}\in\mathbb{R}^{m\times n}
, we have

\|\bm{h}_{T}\|_{2}\leq\frac{\beta_{1}}{\sqrt{k}}\|\bm{h}_{T^{C}}\|_{1}+\gamma_{1}\|\mathcal{A}(\bm{H})\|_{2},

(26)

where
\bm{h}\in\mathbb{R}^{d}
is the singular value vector of
\bm{H}
and

\beta_{1}=\frac{\delta_{tk}}{\sqrt{(t-1)[1-\delta_{tk}^{2}]}},\quad\gamma_{1}=\frac{2}{(1-\delta_{tk})\sqrt{1+\delta_{tk}}}.

(27)

Proof.

For given
\bm{H}\in\mathbb{R}^{m\times n}
, let
\bm{U}\mathcal{D}(\bm{h})\bm{V}^{\top}
be SVD of
\bm{H}
, and for given
t>1
, define

\displaystyle T_{1}=\left\{i\in T^{c}\big||\left(\bm{h}_{T^{c}}\right)_{i}|>\cfrac{\|\bm{h}_{T^{c}}\|_{1}}{(t-1)k}\right\},

(28)

\displaystyle T_{2}=\left\{i\in T^{c}\big||\left(\bm{h}_{T^{c}}\right)_{i}|\leq\cfrac{\|\bm{h}_{T^{c}}\|_{1}}{(t-1)k}\right\}.

(29)

Note that
T^{c}=T_{1}\cup T_{2}
which yields

\bm{h}=\bm{h}_{T}+\bm{h}_{T^{c}}=\bm{h}_{T}+\bm{h}_{T_{1}}+\bm{h}_{T_{2}}.

(30)

Besides, the fact
T_{1}\cap T=\emptyset
implies
\|\bm{h}_{T}\|_{2}\leq\|\bm{h}_{T\cup T_{1}}\|_{2}
. Thus, to show (
26
), it suffices to show that

\|\bm{h}_{T\cup T_{1}}\|_{2}\leq\frac{\beta_{1}}{\sqrt{k}}\|\bm{h}_{T^{c}}\|_{1}+\gamma_{1}\|\mathcal{A}(\bm{H})\|_{2},

(31)

where
\beta_{1}
and
\gamma_{1}
are defined as (
27
). We can apply Lemma
15
to show (
31
) to be true.

For this purpose, we first show that

|T_{1}|<(t-1)k.

(32)

Obviously, the above inequality holds if
T_{1}=\emptyset
. Otherwise, we can apply (
28
) to get that

\|\bm{h}_{T_{1}}\|_{1}>|T_{1}|\cfrac{\|\bm{h}_{T^{c}}\|_{1}}{(t-1)k}=|T_{1}|\cfrac{\|\bm{h}_{T_{1}\cup T_{2}}\|_{1}}{(t-1)k}\geq|T_{1}|\cfrac{\|\bm{h}_{T_{1}}\|_{1}}{(t-1)k},

which implies that (
32
) holds.

We now turn to show (
31
) holds. The relation
T^{c}=T_{1}\cup T_{2}
and
T_{1}\cap T_{2}=\emptyset
, along with the expression (
28
), indicates that

\displaystyle\|\bm{h}_{T_{2}}\|_{1}

\displaystyle=\|\bm{h}_{T^{c}}\|_{1}-\|\bm{h}_{T_{1}}\|_{1}

\displaystyle\leq\|\bm{h}_{T^{c}}\|_{1}-|T_{1}|\cfrac{\|\bm{h}_{T^{c}}\|_{1}}{(t-1)k}

\displaystyle=((t-1)k-|T_{1}|)\cfrac{\|\bm{h}_{T^{c}}\|_{1}}{(t-1)k}.

Since
tk
is an integer, it follows from (
32
) that
(t-1)k-|T_{1}|
is a positive integer.
Additionally, (
29
) implies that

\|\bm{h}_{T_{2}}\|_{\infty}\leq\cfrac{\|\bm{h}_{T^{c}}\|_{1}}{(t-1)k}.

By setting

\bm{v}=\bm{h}_{T_{2}},\ \alpha=\cfrac{\|\bm{h}_{T^{c}}\|_{1}}{(t-1)k},\ s=(t-1)k-|T_{1}|

and applying Lemma
15
, we have

\bm{h}_{T_{2}}=\sum_{i=1}^{N}\lambda_{i}\bm{u}_{i}

(33)

for some positive integer
N
, where
\sum_{i=1}^{N}\lambda_{i}=1
with
0\leq\lambda_{i}\leq 1
and

\bm{u}_{i}\in\mathcal{U}\left(\cfrac{\|\bm{h}_{T^{c}}\|_{1}}{(t-1)k},(t-1)k-|T_{1}|,\bm{h}_{T_{2}}\right)

(34)

with
\mathcal{U}
being defined as (
24
). Moreover, the expression (
24
) implies that

\displaystyle\|\bm{u}_{i}\|_{2}^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\|\bm{u}_{i}\|_{0}\|\bm{u}_{i}\|_{\infty}^{2}

(35)

\displaystyle\leq((t-1)k-|T_{1}|)\left(\cfrac{\|\bm{h}_{T^{c}}\|_{1}}{(t-1)k}\right)^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\cfrac{(\|\bm{h}_{T^{c}}\|_{1})^{2}}{(t-1)k}.

For simplicity, denote

\displaystyle\bm{v}_{i}:=(1+\delta_{tk})\bm{h}_{T\cup T_{1}}+\delta_{tk}\bm{u}_{i},

(36)

\displaystyle\bar{\bm{v}}_{i}:=(1-\delta_{tk})\bm{h}_{T\cup T_{1}}-\delta_{tk}\bm{u}_{i}.

(37)

The relation
\|\bm{u}_{i}\|_{0}\leq(t-1)k-|T_{1}|
combined with (
32
) and the fact
|T|\leq k
yield that
\bm{v}_{i}
and
\bar{\bm{v}}_{i}
are
tk
-sparse for

. Thus, we have

\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{N}\lambda_{i}\left[\|\mathcal{A}\left(\bm{U}\mathcal{D}(\bm{v}_{i})\bm{V}^{\top}\right)\|_{2}^{2}-\|\mathcal{A}\left(\bm{U}\mathcal{D}(\bar{\bm{v}}_{i})\bm{V}^{\top}\right)\|_{2}^{2}\right]

(38)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{N}\lambda_{i}\left[\|\mathcal{A}\left(\bm{U}\mathcal{D}(\bm{h}_{T\cup T_{1}})\bm{V}^{\top}\right)+\delta_{tk}\mathcal{A}\left(\bm{U}\mathcal{D}(\bm{u}_{i}+\bm{h}_{T\cup T_{1}})\bm{V}^{\top}\right)\|_{2}^{2}-\|\mathcal{A}\left(\bm{U}\mathcal{D}(\bm{h}_{T\cup T_{1}})\bm{V}^{\top}\right)\right.

\displaystyle\left.-\delta_{tk}\mathcal{A}\left(\bm{U}\mathcal{D}(\bm{u}_{i}+\bm{h}_{T\cup T_{1}})\bm{V}^{\top}\right)\|_{2}^{2}\right]

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{N}\lambda_{i}\left[4\delta_{tk}\left(\mathcal{A}(\bm{U}\mathcal{D}(\bm{h}_{T\cup T_{1}})\bm{V}^{\top})\right)^{\top}\mathcal{A}\left(\bm{U}\mathcal{D}(\bm{u}_{i}+\bm{h}_{T\cup T_{1}})\bm{V}^{\top}\right)\right]

\displaystyle\overset{(a)}{=}

\displaystyle 4\delta_{tk}\left(\mathcal{A}(\bm{U}\mathcal{D}(\bm{h}_{T\cup T_{1}})\bm{V}^{\top})\right)^{\top}\mathcal{A}\left(\bm{U}\mathcal{D}(\sum_{i=1}^{N}\lambda_{i}\bm{u}_{i}+\bm{h}_{T\cup T_{1}})\bm{V}^{\top}\right)

\displaystyle\overset{(b)}{=}

\displaystyle 4\delta_{tk}\left(\mathcal{A}(\bm{U}\mathcal{D}(\bm{h}_{T\cup T_{1}})\bm{V}^{\top})\right)^{\top}\mathcal{A}\left(\bm{U}\mathcal{D}(\bm{h})\bm{V}^{\top}\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 4\delta_{tk}\left(\mathcal{A}(\bm{U}\mathcal{D}(\bm{h}_{T\cup T_{1}})\bm{V}^{\top})\right)^{\top}\mathcal{A}(\bm{H})

\displaystyle\overset{(c)}{\leq}

\displaystyle 4\delta_{tk}\sqrt{1+\delta_{tk}}\|\bm{h}_{T\cup T_{1}}\|_{2}\|\mathcal{A}(\bm{H})\|_{2},

where (a) follows from (

), (b) follows from (

) and (

), (c) follows from Cauchy-Schwartz inequality and (

).

Next we will give a lower bound of the first formula in (

). That is

\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{N}\lambda_{i}\left[\|\mathcal{A}\left(\bm{U}\mathcal{D}(\bm{v}_{i})\bm{V}^{\top}\right)\|_{2}^{2}-\|\mathcal{A}\left(\bm{U}\mathcal{D}(\bar{\bm{v}}_{i})\bm{V}^{\top}\right)\|_{2}^{2}\right]

\displaystyle\overset{(a)}{\geq}

\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{N}\lambda_{i}(1-\delta_{tk})\|\bm{v}_{i}\|_{2}^{2}-\sum_{i=1}^{N}\lambda_{i}(1+\delta_{tk})\|\bar{\bm{v}}_{i}\|_{2}^{2}

\displaystyle\overset{(b)}{=}

\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{N}\lambda_{i}\left(2(\delta_{tk}-\delta_{tk}^{3})\|\bm{h}_{T\cup T_{1}}\|_{2}^{2}-2\delta_{tk}^{3}\|\bm{u}_{i}\|_{2}^{2}\right)

\displaystyle\overset{(c)}{\geq}

\displaystyle 2(\delta_{tk}-\delta_{tk}^{3})\|\bm{h}_{T\cup T_{1}}\|_{2}^{2}-2\delta_{tk}^{3}\cfrac{(\|\bm{h}_{T^{c}}\|_{1})^{2}}{(t-1)k},

where (a) follows from (
8
), (b) follows from (
36
) and (
37
), (c) follows from (
25
) and (
35
). The inclusion together with (
38
) indicates that

\displaystyle 2(\delta_{tk}-\delta_{tk}^{3})\|\bm{h}_{T\cup T_{1}}\|_{2}^{2}-2\delta_{tk}^{3}\cfrac{(\|\bm{h}_{T^{c}}\|_{1})^{2}}{(t-1)k}\leq 4\delta_{tk}\sqrt{1+\delta_{tk}}\|\bm{h}_{T\cup T_{1}}\|_{2}\|\mathcal{A}(\bm{H})\|_{2},

which is exactly

(1-\delta_{tk}^{2})\|\bm{h}_{T\cup T_{1}}\|_{2}^{2}-2\sqrt{1+\delta_{tk}}\|\bm{h}_{T\cup T_{1}}\|_{2}\|\mathcal{A}(\bm{H})\|_{2}-\delta_{tk}^{2}\cfrac{(\|\bm{h}_{T^{c}}\|_{1})^{2}}{(t-1)k}\leq 0.

Hence we have

\displaystyle\|\bm{h}_{T\cup T_{1}}\|_{2}\leq

\displaystyle\bigg[\left((2\sqrt{1+\delta_{tk}}\|\mathcal{A}(\bm{H})\|_{2})^{2}+4\delta_{tk}^{2}(1-\delta_{tk}^{2})\cfrac{(\|\bm{h}_{T^{c}}\|_{1})^{2}}{(t-1)k}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}+2\sqrt{1+\delta_{tk}}\|\mathcal{A}(\bm{H})\|_{2}\bigg]\bigg/2(1-\delta_{tk}^{2})

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\cfrac{\delta_{tk}}{\sqrt{(1-\delta_{tk}^{2})(t-1)k}}\|\bm{h}_{T^{c}}\|_{1}+\cfrac{2}{(1-\delta_{tk})\sqrt{1+\delta_{tk}}}\|\mathcal{A}(\bm{H})\|_{2},

where the last inequality follows from
\sqrt{x^{2}+y^{2}}\leq x+y
for any
x\geq 0
and
y\geq 0
.

By virtue of (
27
) and (
31
), we obtain the desired results. This completes the proof.
∎

Lemma 18
.

Let
k
be a positive integer and the desired matrix
\bm{X}^{o}
satisfy
\mathcal{A}(\bm{X}^{o})+\bm{s}=\bm{b}
with perturbation
\|\bm{s}\|_{2}\leq\epsilon
. Set
\bm{H}:=\bm{X}^{*}-\bm{X}^{o}
with
\bm{X}^{*}\in\arg\min\mathcal{J}(\bm{X})
. Denote
\bm{U}_{o}\mathcal{D}(\bm{x}^{0})\bm{V}_{o}^{\top}
and
\bm{U}_{H}\mathcal{D}(\bm{h})\bm{V}_{H}^{\top}
as the SVD of
\bm{X}^{o}
and
\bm{H}
, respectively. Define
\Omega:=\textup{supp}(\bm{x}^{o}_{\max(k)})
and
\Gamma:=\textup{supp}(\bm{h}_{\max(k)})
, then we have

\displaystyle\|\mathcal{A}(\bm{H})\|_{2}^{2}-2\epsilon\|\mathcal{A}(\bm{H})\|_{2}\leq 2\lambda(2\|\bm{X}^{o}\|_{*}+\|\bm{h}_{\Gamma}\|_{1}-\|\bm{h}_{\Gamma^{c}}\|_{1}+\|\bm{h}\|_{2})

(39)

and

\|\bm{h}_{\Gamma^{c}}\|_{1}\leq 2\|\bm{X}^{o}\|_{*}+\|\bm{h}_{\Gamma}\|_{1}+\|\bm{h}\|_{2}+\cfrac{\epsilon}{\lambda}\|\mathcal{A}(\bm{H})\|_{2}.

(40)

Proof.

According to the definition of
\bm{X}^{*}
, we obtain that
\mathcal{J}(\bm{X}^{*})\leq\mathcal{J}(\bm{X}^{o})
, i.e.,

\displaystyle\|\bm{X}^{*}\|_{*}-\|\bm{X}^{*}\|_{F}+\cfrac{1}{2\lambda}\|\mathcal{A}(\bm{X}^{*})-\bm{b}\|_{2}^{2}\leq\|\bm{X}^{o}\|_{*}-\|\bm{X}^{o}\|_{F}+\cfrac{1}{2\lambda}\|\mathcal{A}(\bm{X}^{o})-\bm{b}\|_{2}^{2},

which after simplification gives

\displaystyle\cfrac{1}{2\lambda}\left(\|\mathcal{A}(\bm{H})\|_{2}^{2}-2\langle\bm{s},\mathcal{A}(\bm{H})\rangle\right)\leq\|\bm{X}^{o}\|_{*}-\|\bm{X}^{o}\|_{F}-\|\bm{X}^{*}\|_{*}+\|\bm{X}^{*}\|_{F}.

(41)

Denote the left and right sides of (
41
) as
\rho_{l}
and
\rho_{r}
respectively. It is known from the Cauchy-Schwartz inequality that

\displaystyle\rho_{l}\geq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2\lambda}\left(\|\mathcal{A}(\bm{H})\|_{2}^{2}-2\|\bm{s}\|_{2}\|\mathcal{A}(\bm{H})\|_{2}\right)

(42)

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2\lambda}\left(\|\mathcal{A}(\bm{H})\|_{2}^{2}-2\epsilon\|\mathcal{A}(\bm{H})\|_{2}\right).

Besides, direct calculations lead to the expression of
\bm{H}+\bm{X}^{o}=\bm{A}+\bm{B}
with

\bm{A}=\bm{U}_{o}\mathcal{D}(\bm{x}^{o}_{\Omega})\bm{V}_{o}^{\top}+\bm{U}_{H}\mathcal{D}(\bm{h}_{\Omega})\bm{V}_{H}^{\top}

and

\bm{B}=\bm{U}_{o}\mathcal{D}(\bm{x}^{o}_{\Omega^{c}})\bm{V}_{o}^{\top}+\bm{U}_{H}\mathcal{D}(\bm{h}_{\Omega^{c}})\bm{V}_{H}^{\top}.

Then, we have

\displaystyle\rho_{r}=

\displaystyle\|\bm{X}^{o}\|_{*}-\|\bm{X}^{o}\|_{F}-\|\bm{H}+\bm{X}^{o}\|_{*}+\|\bm{H}+\bm{X}^{o}\|_{F}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\|\bm{X}^{o}\|_{*}-\|\bm{X}^{o}\|_{F}-\|\bm{B}\|_{*}+\|\bm{A}\|_{*}+\|\bm{H}\|_{F}+\|\bm{X}^{o}\|_{F}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\|\bm{X}^{o}\|_{*}-\|\bm{h}_{\Omega^{c}}\|_{1}+\|\bm{x}^{o}_{\Omega^{c}}\|_{1}+\|\bm{x}^{o}_{\Omega}\|_{1}+\|\bm{h}_{\Omega}\|_{1}+\|\bm{h}\|_{2}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle 2\|\bm{X}^{o}\|_{*}+\|\bm{h}_{\Gamma}\|_{1}-\|\bm{h}_{\Gamma^{c}}\|_{1}+\|\bm{h}\|_{2}.

(43)

Hence combining (
42
) with (
III-B
), we can get the desired inequalities (
39
) and (
40
) hold trivially from (
39
).
∎

Theorem 19
.

Assume that

(46)

due to

Therefore,

IV
Conclusion

In this paper, we focused on analysis of theoretical guarantees for reconstruction of low rank matrix from noisy measurements via
L_{*-F}
minimization. Firstly, we briefly presented the relationship of the optimal solution among several
L_{*-F}
minimization problems. Secondly, we gave the sufficient conditions of stable recovery and the recovery error estimation for the general constrained
L_{*-F}
minimization problems. Besides, we considered the robust low rank matrix recovery by regularized
L_{*-F}
minimization model, approximate error estimation of which was obtained under the framework of powerful RIP tools. To our knowledge, this theoretical result has not been studied before. A further issue worth considering is developing a tighter recovery error estimation for this regularized model. It is also interesting to see if the techniques in this paper can be applied in other settings.
