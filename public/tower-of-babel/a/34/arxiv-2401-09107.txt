Properties of Q 5 ​ q Q^{5}q dibaryons
Xin-Zhen Weng

Abstract
We investigate heavy flavor dibaryons with five heavy quarks Q Q ( Q = { c , b } Q=\{c,b\} ) and one light quark q q ( q = { u , d , s } q=\{u,d,s\} ), namely the Q 5 ​ q Q^{5}q dibaryons. In the framework of an extended chromomagnetic model, we systematically study the mass spectrum of these dibaryons. We find no stable state below the corresponding baryon-baryon thresholds. In addition to the analysis of the masses, we also study their two body decay properties by estimating the relative width ratios of the decay channels. We hope our study will be of help for future experiments.

For these compact systems, the interaction is provided by gluon exchange and string confinement.
The interactions include spin-independent Coulomb-type interaction and linear confinement, plus higher order terms such as spin-spin chromomagnetic interaction, tensor interaction, and spin-orbit interaction.

II
The Extended Chromomagnetic Model

In the quark model, the Hamiltonian of the
S
-wave hadron reads
Chan et al. 1978
;
Høgaasen et al. 2014

H=\sum_{i}m_{i}+H_{\text{CE}}+H_{\text{CM}}\,,

(1)

where
m_{i}
is the effective mass of
i
th quark.

H_{\text{CE}}
is the colorelectric (CE) interaction

H_{\text{CE}}=-\sum_{i<j}a_{ij}\bm{F}_{i}\cdot\bm{F}_{j}\,,

(2)

and
H_{\text{CM}}
is the chromomagnetic (CM) interaction

H_{\text{CM}}=-\sum_{i<j}v_{ij}\bm{S}_{i}\cdot\bm{S}_{j}\bm{F}_{i}\cdot\bm{F}_{j}\,,

(3)

Here,
a_{ij}
and
v_{ij}\propto\braket{\alpha(\mathbf{r})\delta^{3}(\mathbf{r})}/m_{i}m_{j}
are effective coupling constants which depend on the constituent quark masses and the spatial wave function.

\bm{S}_{i}=\bm{\sigma}_{i}/2
and
\bm{F}_{i}={\bm{\lambda}}_{i}/2
are the quark spin and color operators.

Since

\displaystyle\sum_{i<j}\left(m_{i}+m_{j}\right)\bm{F}_{i}\cdot\bm{F}_{j}

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle\left(\sum_{i}m_{i}\bm{F}_{i}\right)\cdot\left(\sum_{i}\bm{F}_{i}\right)-\frac{4}{3}\sum_{i}m_{i}\,,

(4)

and the total color operator
\sum_{i}\bm{F}_{i}
nullifies any color-singlet physical state, we can rewrite the Hamiltonian as
Weng et al. 2018

H=-\frac{3}{4}\sum_{i<j}m_{ij}V^{\text{C}}_{ij}-\sum_{i<j}v_{ij}V^{\text{CM}}_{ij}\,,

(5)

by introducing the quark pair mass parameter

m_{ij}=\left(m_{i}+m_{j}\right)+\frac{4}{3}a_{ij}\,,

(6)

where
V^{\text{C}}_{ij}\equiv\bm{F}_{i}\cdot\bm{F}_{j}
and
V^{\text{CM}}_{ij}\equiv\bm{S}_{i}\cdot\bm{S}_{j}\bm{F}_{i}\cdot\bm{F}_{j}
are the color and CM interactions between quarks.
The model parameters
m_{ij}
and
v_{ij}
can be fitted from conventional mesons and baryons.
Their values are listed in Table
1
.
In this work, we use these parameters to estimate the masses of the
S
-wave
Q^{5}q
dibaryons.

(12)

The effect of
\text{C}_{6}
term surpasses that of the spin term.
Thus the chromomagnetic interaction favors the axial-vector state over the scalar state.

The spectra of their bottom partners,
bbbbbq
dibaryons, share similar pattern.
As shown in Fig.

(c–d), the ground states are the
D(bbbbbn,24526,1^{+})
and the

The reason is that the splitting is inversely proportional to the quark masses [see Eq. (

\Delta{m}\left(Q^{5}q\right){\propto}v_{Qq}{\propto}1/m_{Q}m_{q}\,.

(13)

The bottom quark is much heavier than the charm quark, thus the mass splitting of the

dibaryons is smaller than that of the
cccccq
dibaryons.

Besides the masses, the eigenvectors can also be used to investigate the decay properties of the dibaryons.
We can transform the wave function into the
q^{3}{\otimes}q^{3}
configuration.
The
q^{3}
component can be either of color-singlet or of color-octet.
The
\ket{(qqq)^{1_{c}}{\otimes}(qqq)^{1_{c}}}
dibaryon can easily dissociate into two baryons in relative
S
-wave.
We call this kind of decay the “Okubo-Zweig-Iizuka- (OZI-)superallowed” decay.
On the other hand, the
\ket{(qqq)^{8_{c}}{\otimes}(qqq)^{8_{c}}}
dibaryon cannot fall apart without the gluon exchange.
For simplicity, we follow Refs.
Jaffe 1977a
;
Jaffe 1977b
;
Strottman 1979
;
Weng and Zhu 2022
and only consider the “OZI-superallowed” decays in this work.
In Table
7
, we transform the eigenvectors of the
cccccq
(

) dibaryons into the
ccc{\otimes}ccq
(
bbb{\otimes}bbq
) configuration.
For simplicity, we only present the color-singlet components, and rewrite the bases as a direct product of two baryons

\Psi=\sum_{i}c_{i}\Ket{\psi_{i}\left(B{\otimes}B\right)}+\cdots\,,

(14)

where
c_{i}
represents the overlap between the dibaryon and a particular baryon
\times
baryon channel.
For each decay channel, the decay width is proportional to the square of the coefficient
c_{i}
of the corresponding component in the eigenvector, and also depends on the phase space.
For two body
L
-wave decay, the partial decay width reads
Gao 1992
;
Weng et al. 2019

\Gamma_{i}=\gamma_{i}\alpha\frac{k^{2L+1}}{m^{2L}}{\cdot}|c_{i}|^{2},

(15)

where
\gamma_{i}
is a quantity related to the decay dynamics,
\alpha
is an effective coupling constant,
k
is the momentum of the final baryons in the rest frame of the initial state,
m
is the dibaryon mass, and
L
is the relative partial wave between the two baryons.
For the decay processes studied in this work,
(k/m)^{2}
’s are all of
\mathcal{O}(10^{-2})
or even smaller.
The higher wave decays are all suppressed.
Thus we only consider the
S
-wave decays.
Generally,
\gamma_{i}
depends on the spatial wave functions of the initial and final states, which are different for each decay process.
In the quark model, the spatial wave functions of the ground state
1/2^{+}
and
3/2^{+}
baryons are similar.
Thus for
each
dibaryon state, we have

\gamma_{B_{1}B_{2}}\approx\gamma_{B_{1}B_{2}^{*}}\approx\gamma_{B_{1}^{*}B_{2}}\approx\gamma_{B_{1}^{*}B_{2}^{*}}\,,

(16)

where
B_{i}
and
B_{i}^{*}
are the
1/2^{+}
and
3/2^{+}
baryons with the same flavor contents.
In Table

, we calculate the values of
k\cdot|c_{i}|^{2}
for the
cccccq
and
bbbbbq
dibaryons.
Then we compare their relative widths in Table

.

For the
cccccq
and

dibaryons, the scalar states only have one

-wave decay channel consists of two
3/2^{+}
baryons.

The axial-vector states have two
S
-wave decay channels.
From Table

\frac{\Gamma\left[D(cccccn,8516,1^{+}){\to}\Omega_{ccc}^{*}\Xi_{cc}\right]}{\Gamma\left[D(cccccn,8516,1^{+}){\to}\Omega_{ccc}^{*}\Xi_{cc}^{*}\right]}\sim 1.3\,,

(17)

\frac{\Gamma\left[D(cccccs,8617,1^{+}){\to}\Omega_{ccc}^{*}\Omega_{cc}\right]}{\Gamma\left[D(cccccs,8617,1^{+}){\to}\Omega_{ccc}^{*}\Omega_{cc}^{*}\right]}\sim 1.5\,,

(18)

\frac{\Gamma\left[D(bbbbbn,24526,1^{+}){\to}\Omega_{bbb}^{*}\Xi_{bb}\right]}{\Gamma\left[D(bbbbbn,24526,1^{+}){\to}\Omega_{bbb}^{*}\Xi_{bb}^{*}\right]}\sim 1.0\,,

and

For axial-vector states, the partial decay widths of the two channels are compatible.

\Braket{H_{\text{C}}\left(ccccbq\right)}=2m_{cc}+m_{bq}-\frac{3}{8}\delta{m}\cdot\mathrm{C}_{3}\left(cccc\right)

\text{C}_{3}

\left\{\begin{split}\delta{m}\left(ccccbn\right)=21.93~\text{MeV}\,,\\
\delta{m}\left(ccccbs\right)=16.64~\text{MeV}\,.\end{split}\right.

(23)

Note that the
\mathrm{C}_{3}(cccc)
is diagonal in the
\bar{6}_{c}{\otimes}6_{c}
and
3_{c}{\otimes}\bar{3}_{c}
color bases, with matrix elements
40/3
and
16/3

Thus

\Braket{H_{\text{C}}\left(ccccbq\right)}=\left\{\begin{split}2m_{cc}+m_{bq}-5\delta{m}~\text{for}~\bar{6}_{c}{\otimes}6_{c}\,,\\
2m_{cc}+m_{bq}-2\delta{m}~\text{for}~3_{c}{\otimes}\bar{3}_{c}\,.\end{split}\right.

(24)

The color interaction favors the
\bar{6}_{c}{\otimes}6_{c}
configuration.
Then we obtain the two-band structure in the mass spectrum.
The CM interaction is suppressed by
1/m_{Q}
and thus cannot alter the order.
Alas, the CM interaction mixes the states and gives small shift of the bands.

Before concluding this section, we would like to compare the present results with that of Refs.
Weng et al. 2021
;

.

(29)

A more detailed dynamical study of the dependence of the spectrum and wave function with respect to the quark masses would be very important for decoding the nature of dibaryons.

\displaystyle 8_{c}{\otimes}6_{c}{\otimes}3_{c}:\left\{\left[\left(QQQ\right)^{8_{c}}{\otimes}\left(Q^{\prime}Q^{\prime}\right)^{6_{c}}\right]^{\bar{3}_{c}}{\otimes}q^{3_{c}}\right\}^{1_{c}}\,,

\displaystyle 8_{c}{\otimes}\bar{3}_{c}{\otimes}3_{c}:\left\{\left[\left(QQQ\right)^{8_{c}}{\otimes}\left(Q^{\prime}Q^{\prime}\right)^{\bar{3}_{c}}\right]^{\bar{3}_{c}}{\otimes}q^{3_{c}}\right\}^{1_{c}}\,,

\displaystyle 1_{c}{\otimes}\bar{3}_{c}{\otimes}3_{c}:\left\{\left[\left(QQQ\right)^{1_{c}}{\otimes}\left(Q^{\prime}Q^{\prime}\right)^{\bar{3}_{c}}\right]^{\bar{3}_{c}}{\otimes}q^{3_{c}}\right\}^{1_{c}}\,.

(37)

Note that the last one is the dominant component of the scattering states.
For these systems, the color interaction reads

\displaystyle\Braket{H_{\text{C}}\left(Q^{3}Q^{\prime 2}q\right)}

\displaystyle={}

\displaystyle M-\frac{3}{8}\delta{m}_{\alpha}\cdot\mathrm{C}_{3}\left(QQQ\right)-\frac{3}{8}\delta{m}_{\beta}\cdot\mathrm{C}_{3}\left(Q^{\prime}Q^{\prime}\right)\,,

(38)

where

M=\frac{3m_{QQ}+2m_{Q^{\prime}Q^{\prime}}+m_{qQ}+m_{qQ^{\prime}}-m_{QQ^{\prime}}}{2}\,,

(39)

\delta{m}_{\alpha}=\frac{m_{QQ}+m_{qQ^{\prime}}-m_{qQ}-m_{QQ^{\prime}}}{4}\,,

(40)

and

\delta{m}_{\beta}=\frac{m_{Q^{\prime}Q^{\prime}}+m_{qQ}-m_{qQ^{\prime}}-m_{QQ^{\prime}}}{4}\,.

(41)

The color interaction is diagonal over the three bases.
For the
cccbbq
dibaryons,
\delta{m}_{\alpha}\sim 20~\text{MeV}
and
\delta{m}_{\beta}\sim-50~\text{MeV}
.
Thus the
\mathrm{C}_{3}(ccc)

(ccc)^{8_{c}}
cluster, while the
\mathrm{C}_{3}(bb)
term favors the bases with
(bb)^{\bar{3}_{c}}
cluster.

|\delta{m}_{\alpha}|<|\delta{m}_{\beta}|
, the
\mathrm{C}_{3}(bb)
term dominates the mass splitting while the
\mathrm{C}_{3}(ccc)
term give smaller splittings afterward.
In summary, we have

8_{c}{\otimes}\bar{3}_{c}{\otimes}3_{c}<1_{c}{\otimes}\bar{3}_{c}{\otimes}3_{c}\ll 8_{c}{\otimes}6_{c}{\otimes}3_{c}\,.

(42)

Thus the scattering states of the

For the

\delta{m}_{\alpha}\sim-50~\text{MeV}
and
\delta{m}_{\beta}\sim 20~\text{MeV}
.

1_{c}{\otimes}\bar{3}_{c}{\otimes}3_{c}\ll 8_{c}{\otimes}6_{c}{\otimes}3_{c}<8_{c}{\otimes}\bar{3}_{c}{\otimes}3_{c}\,.

(43)

Thus the scattering states of the
bbbccq
systems lie in the bottom of the spectra.
When including the CM interaction, these color bases will be mixed.
Since the splittings between
1_{c}{\otimes}\bar{3}_{c}{\otimes}3_{c}
base and other bases are quite large in the

system, the corresponding mixing effects become small.
Thus the proportions of

scattering states’ dominant components are always very large (
\sim 100\%
).
On the contrary, the splitting between the
1_{c}{\otimes}\bar{3}_{c}{\otimes}3_{c}
and
8_{c}{\otimes}\bar{3}_{c}{\otimes}3_{c}
bases is quite small in the
cccbbq
system, the corresponding mixing effect becomes pretty important.
So the proportions of
cccbbq
scattering states’ dominant components become significantly smaller (see Table

).

After identifying the scattering states, the other states are genuine dibaryons.

Thus they may be broad states.

IV
Conclusions

In this work, we have systematically studied the mass spectrum of dibaryons with five heavy quarks, in the framework of an extended chromomagnetic model, which includes both the colorelectric and chromomagnetic interactions.
To do this, we first constructed the flavor
\otimes
color
\otimes
spin wave functions of the
Q^{5}q
dibaryons to satisfy the
\mathrm{SU}(2)_{f}
isospin symmetry and the Pauli principle.
Then we systematically calculated the interaction Hamiltonian under these bases and obtain the corresponding masses.
The numerical results suggest that all states are above the corresponding baryon-baryon thresholds.
Most of them are expected to be broad states.
However, the
D(bbbbcn,21306,0^{+})
,
D(bbbbcn,21251,1^{+})
,
D(bbbbcs,21398,0^{+})
and
D(bbbbcs,21341,1^{+})
both have only one
S
-wave decay channel and they lie above the corresponding channels by only about
20~\text{MeV}
.
Due to the small phases, these states may be relatively narrow.
In addition to computing the masses, we also use the wave functions to investigate the decay properties of the dibaryons.
We hope that our studies can be of help for future experimental searches.
