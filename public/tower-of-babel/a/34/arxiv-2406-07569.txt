Δ \Delta -locally nilpotent algebras, their ideal structure and simplicity criteria
V. V. Bavula

Abstract
The class of Δ \Delta -locally nilpotent algebras (introduced in the paper) is a wide generalization of the algebras of differential operators on commutative algebras. Examples includes all the rings 𝒟 ⁡ ( A ) {\cal D}(A) of differential operators on commutative algebras (in arbitrary characteristic), all subalgebras of 𝒟 ⁡ ( A ) {\cal D}(A) that contain the algebra A A , the universal enveloping algebras of nilpotent, solvable and semi-simple Lie algebras, the Poisson universal enveloping algebra of an arbitrary Poisson algebra, iterated Ore extensions A ⁡ [ x 1 , … , x n ; δ 1 , … , δ n ] A[x_{1},\ldots,x_{n};\delta_{1},\ldots,\delta_{n}] , certain generalized Weyl algebras, and others. In [ 8 ] , simplicity criteria are given for the algebras differential operators on commutative algebras (it was a long standing problem). The aim of the paper is to describe the ideal structure of Δ \Delta -locally nilpotent algebras and as a corollary to give simplicity criteria for them (it is a generalization of the results of [ 8 ] ). Examples are considered. Key Words: Δ \Delta -locally nilpotent algebra, ring of differential operators, commutative algebra of essentially finite type, simplicity criterion, ideal, the Weyl algebra, the generalized Weyl algebra, nilpotent Lie algebra, Poisson algebra, Poisson enveloping algebra, iterated Ore extension. The canonical form of a differential operator.
For a finite set
\Lambda
, we denote by
\mathbb{N}^{\Lambda}
the direct product of
\Lambda
copies of the set of natural numbers
\mathbb{N}
. For an element
\alpha=(\alpha_{\lambda})
of
\mathbb{N}^{\Lambda}
, let
|\alpha|:=\sum_{\lambda\in\Lambda}\alpha_{\lambda}
and
(-1)^{\alpha}:=(-1)^{|\alpha|}
. For elements
\alpha,\beta\in\mathbb{N}^{\Lambda}
, we write
\beta\leq\alpha
if
\beta_{\lambda}\leq\alpha_{\lambda}
for all
\lambda\in\Lambda
. If
\beta\leq\alpha
then
{\alpha\choose\beta}:=\prod_{\lambda\in\Lambda}{\alpha_{\lambda}\choose\beta_{\lambda}}
.

Theorem 3.6

Let
A
be a finitely generated commutative algebra,
G=\{x_{\lambda}\}_{\lambda\in\Lambda}
be a finite set of generators of
A
,
{\cal D}(A)
be the algebra of differential operators on
A
, and
\{{\cal D})A)_{i}\}_{i\geq 0}
be the order filtration on
{\cal D}(A)
.

1.

Each differential operator
\delta\in{\cal D}(A)_{i}
of order
i
is uniquely determined by the elements
\{{\rm ad}^{\alpha}(\delta)*1\,|\,\alpha\in\mathbb{N}^{\Lambda}\}
where
{\rm ad}^{\alpha}=\prod_{\lambda\in\Lambda}{\rm ad}_{x_{\lambda}}^{\alpha_{\lambda}}
for
\alpha=(\alpha_{\lambda})\in\mathbb{N}^{\Lambda}
.

2.

For all elements
\alpha\in\mathbb{N}^{\Lambda}
and
\delta\in{\cal D}(A)_{i}
,

\delta(x^{\alpha})=\sum_{\beta\leq\alpha,|\beta|\leq i}(-1)^{\beta}{\alpha\choose\beta}{\rm ad}^{\beta}(\delta)*1\cdot x^{\alpha-\beta}

where
x^{\alpha-\beta}=\prod_{\lambda\in\Lambda}x_{\lambda}^{\alpha_{\lambda}-\beta_{\lambda}}
.

Proof
. 2. For the element
x_{\lambda}
, we denote by
l_{x_{\lambda}}
and
r_{x_{\lambda}}
the left and right multiplication maps by the element
x_{\lambda}
, respectively. Now,

\displaystyle\delta(x^{\alpha})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\delta x^{\alpha}*1=\delta\prod_{\lambda\in\Lambda}x_{\lambda}^{\alpha_{\lambda}}*1=\bigg(\prod_{\lambda\in\Lambda}r_{x_{\lambda}}^{\alpha_{\lambda}}\delta\bigg)*1=\bigg(\prod_{\lambda\in\Lambda}(l_{x_{\lambda}}-{\rm ad}_{x_{\lambda}})^{\alpha_{\lambda}}\delta\bigg)*1

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{\beta\leq\alpha,|\beta|\leq i}(-1)^{\beta}{\alpha\choose\beta}{\rm ad}^{\beta}(\delta)*1\cdot x^{\alpha-\beta}.

1. Statement 1 follows from statement 2: Let
\delta,\delta^{\prime}\in{\cal D}(A)_{i}
. If
\delta=\delta^{\prime}
then
{\rm ad}^{\alpha}(\delta)*1={\rm ad}^{\alpha}(\delta^{\prime})*1
for all elements
\alpha
such that
|\alpha|\leq i
. The converse follows from statement 2.
