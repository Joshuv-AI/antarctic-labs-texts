Four-point correlators of light-ray operators in CCFT
Yangrui Hu, Luke Lippstreu, Marcus Spradlin, Akshay Yelleshpur Srikant, Anastasia Volovich

Abstract
We compute the four-point correlator of two gluon light-ray operators and two gluon primaries from the four-gluon celestial amplitude in ( 2 , 2 ) (2,2) signature spacetime. The correlator is non-distributional and allows us to verify that light-ray operators appear in the OPE of two gluon primaries. We also carry out a conformal block decomposition of the terms involving the exchange of gluon operators.

The light transform

The “anti-holomorphic” light transform of an operator

\Delta

{\bar{\bf{L}}}[\mathcal{O}_{\Delta,J}](z,\bar{z})\coloneqq\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{d\bar{z}^{\prime}}{|\bar{z}^{\prime}-\bar{z}|^{2-\Delta+J}}\mathcal{O}_{\Delta,J}(z,\bar{z}^{\prime})\,.

(11)

{\bar{\bf{L}}}[\mathcal{O}_{\Delta,J}](z,\bar{z})

Conformal block decomposition

In the previous section we showed directly from the four-point correlator (
14

14

The

(and similarly for
\bar{z}_{i}

\bar{z}=\bar{z}_{3}

=-\pi\delta(\beta)\,\Big[{\cal I}_{1}(z,\bar{z})+{\cal I}_{2}(z,\bar{z})\Big],

(20)

where (we assume that
0<z,\bar{z}<1

\begin{split}{\cal I}_{1}(z,\bar{z})~=&~\frac{C(\Delta_{3}-1,\Delta_{4}-1)}{z(1-z)^{\Delta_{3}}}\,_{2}F_{1}\left[1-\Delta_{2},\Delta_{3}-1,\Delta_{3}+\Delta_{4}-2,\frac{\bar{z}-z}{1-z}\right],\\

(21)

We focus on the first term, which corresponds to the exchange of gluon operators, and leave the decomposition of the second term, which corresponds to the exchange of light-ray operators, to future work.

We proceed along the lines of

in order to massage

into a form from which the conformal block decomposition can be read off.
First we use the identity

\begin{split}{}_{2}F_{1}\left(a,b_{1},b_{1}+b_{2},\frac{x-y}{1-y}\right)=(1-y)^{a}F_{1}[a,b_{1},b_{2},b_{1}+b_{2},x,y]\,,\end{split}

(22)

and then the Burchnall-Chaundy expansion
Identity1
;
identity2
of the Appell function

=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\,\frac{(a)_{n}(b_{1})_{n}(b_{2})_{n}(c-a)_{n}}{n!(c+n-1)_{n}(c)_{2n}}\,x^{n}\,y^{n}\,_{2}F_{1}(a+n,b_{1}+n,c+2n,x)_{2}F_{1}(a+n,b_{2}+n,c+2n,y)\,,

(23)

along with the Gauss recursion relations for
{}_{2}F_{1}
, to express

\begin{split}{\cal I}_{1}(z,\bar{z})~=&~\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\sum_{k=n}^{\infty}\,a_{k,n}\,K^{21}_{34}\left[\frac{\Delta_{3}+\Delta_{4}}{2}+k,\frac{\Delta_{3}+\Delta_{4}}{2}+n-1\right].\end{split}

(24)

Here the coefficients are

\times\sum_{m=0}^{k-n}\,\frac{(1-\Delta_{2}+n)_{m}(\Delta_{4}-1+n)_{m}}{(\Delta_{3}+\Delta_{4}+2n-2)_{2m}}\,\frac{(2-\Delta_{1}+n+m)_{k-n-m}(\Delta_{3}+n+m)_{k-n-m}}{(\Delta_{3}+\Delta_{4}-1+2n+2m)_{2k-2n-2m}}

(25)

and
K^{21}_{34}[h,\bar{h}]
are the usual conformal blocks

K^{21}_{34}[h,\bar{h}]=z^{h-h_{3}-h_{4}}{}_{2}F_{1}[h-h_{12},h+h_{34},2h,z]\bar{z}^{\bar{h}-\bar{h}_{3}-\bar{h}_{4}}{}_{2}F_{1}[\bar{h}-\bar{h}_{12},\bar{h}+\bar{h}_{34},2\bar{h},\bar{z}]\,,

(26)

where

,
\bar{h}_{ij}=\bar{h}-\bar{h}_{j}
.
In the present application, operators 3 and 4 are gluon primaries with

and for these we should take

Appendix A
The four marked point integral

In this Appendix we elaborate on how to evaluate the integral (
15
),

{\cal F}(z,\bar{z}):=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}dt\,|\bar{z}-t|^{\Delta_{2}-1}|z-t|^{\Delta_{1}-1}|1-t|^{\Delta_{4}-2}\,.

(28)

Besides the singular point at
|t|=\infty
, the integrand exhibits three singular points at
t=1,z,\bar{z}
. Thus there are seemingly six different configurations which need to be analyzed:

I

\displaystyle:\bar{z}<1<z,\quad\textup{II}

\displaystyle:z<1<\bar{z}

III

\displaystyle:1<z<\bar{z},\quad\textup{IV}

\displaystyle:1<\bar{z}<z

V

\displaystyle:\bar{z}<z<1,\quad\textup{VI}

\displaystyle:z<\bar{z}<1

We will demonstrate that the result of the integral (
28
) can be brought to a form where there are only two distinct configurations. To see this, let us first evaluate
\mathcal{F}
in configuration
\textup{I}:\bar{z}<1<z
. The integral then breaks up into four regions

\displaystyle{\cal F}(z,\bar{z}){\Big|}_{\textup{I}}

\displaystyle=\int_{-\infty}^{\bar{z}}dt(\bar{z}-t)^{\Delta_{2}-1}(z-t)^{\Delta_{1}-1}(1-t)^{\Delta_{4}-2}

\displaystyle+\int_{\bar{z}}^{1}dt(t-\bar{z})^{\Delta_{2}-1}(z-t)^{\Delta_{1}-1}(1-t)^{\Delta_{4}-2}

\displaystyle+\int_{1}^{z}dt(t-\bar{z})^{\Delta_{2}-1}(z-t)^{\Delta_{1}-1}(t-1)^{\Delta_{4}-2}

\displaystyle+\int_{z}^{\infty}dt(t-\bar{z})^{\Delta_{2}-1}(t-z)^{\Delta_{1}-1}(t-1)^{\Delta_{4}-2}\,.

(29)

All of these four integrals are Gauss hypergeometric functions; explicitly

\displaystyle{\cal F}(z,\bar{z}){\Big|}_{\textup{I}}=|1-\bar{z}|^{1-\Delta_{3}}\,B(\Delta_{2},\Delta_{3}-1)_{2}F_{1}\left[1-\Delta_{1},\Delta_{3}-1,\Delta_{2}+\Delta_{3}-1,-\frac{|z-1|}{|1-\bar{z}|}\right]

\displaystyle+|1-\bar{z}|^{\Delta_{2}+\Delta_{4}-2}|z-1|^{\Delta_{1}-1}\,B(\Delta_{2},\Delta_{4}-1)\,_{2}F_{1}\left[1-\Delta_{1},\Delta_{4}-1,\Delta_{2}+\Delta_{4}-1,-\frac{|1-\bar{z}|}{|z-1|}\right]

\displaystyle+|z-1|^{\Delta_{1}+\Delta_{4}-2}|1-\bar{z}|^{\Delta_{2}-1}\,B(\Delta_{1},\Delta_{4}-1)\,_{2}F_{1}\left[1-\Delta_{2},\Delta_{4}-1,\Delta_{1}+\Delta_{4}-1,-\frac{|z-1|}{|1-\bar{z}|}\right]

\displaystyle+|z-1|^{1-\Delta_{3}}\,B(\Delta_{3}-1,\Delta_{1})\,_{2}F_{1}\left[1-\Delta_{2},\Delta_{3}-1,\Delta_{1}+\Delta_{3}-1,-\frac{|1-\bar{z}|}{|z-1|}\right]

(30)

where we have maintained the ordering between the four regions in (
29
) and the four terms in (
30
) to indicate to the reader which term arises from which region. Using standard identities and Euler transformations we can write the result more compactly
as (
16
).

An explicit computation of the integral in configuration
II
and subsequent use of the abovementioned identities reveals the identical result. Similar techniques can be applied to show that in regions
\textup{III}-\textup{VI}
the integral takes the form (
3
).
