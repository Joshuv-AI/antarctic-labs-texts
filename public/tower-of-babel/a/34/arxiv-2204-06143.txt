\displaystyle\mathcal{K}_{p,\theta^{*}}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\limsup_{|x|\to+\infty}u_{0}(x)|x|^{N-\frac{2sp+\theta^{*}}{p-1}}\leq C_{0}\mathcal{K}_{p,\theta^{*}}.

We complete the proof.
\Box

Theorem 5.2

Assume that
h\in L^{1}(B_{1})

and

p=\frac{N+2s+\tilde{\theta}}{N-2s}\hskip 18.49988pt{\rm for}\hskip 9.24994pt\tilde{\theta}\in(-2s,\,0).

(5.6)

Let
u_{0}
be a positive solution of (
5.1
) and
C_{0}\geq 1
be the constant from the Harnack inequality in Theorem
1.1
with
\theta=0
.

Then
either for some
k>0

\displaystyle\lim_{|x|\to+\infty}u_{0}(x)|x|^{N-2s}=k

or

\displaystyle\frac{\mathcal{K}_{p}}{C_{0}}\leq\liminf_{|x|\to+\infty}u_{0}(x)|x|^{\frac{2s+\tilde{\theta}}{p-1}}\leq\mathcal{K}_{p}\leq\limsup_{|x|\to+\infty}u_{0}(x)|x|^{\frac{2s+\tilde{\theta}}{p-1}}\leq C_{0}\mathcal{K}_{p}.

Proof.

From the Kelvin transformation,

u^{\sharp}(x):=|x|^{2s-N}u_{0}(\frac{x}{|x|^{2}})

is a positive solution of

\left\{\begin{array}[]{lll}(-\Delta)^{s}u^{\sharp}=(u^{\sharp})^{p}&{\rm in}\ \,B_{1}\setminus\{0\},\\[5.69054pt]
\hskip 18.49988pt\ \ u^{\sharp}=|x|^{2s-N}h^{*}&{\rm in}\ \ \mathbb{R}^{N}\setminus B_{1},\end{array}\right.

by our assumption (
5.6
) that
\tilde{\theta}=p(N-2s)-N-2s
.

From Theorem
1.1
,
u^{\sharp}
is either removable at the origin or it
verifies

\displaystyle\frac{\mathcal{K}_{p}}{C_{0}}\leq\liminf_{|x|\to 0^{+}}u^{\sharp}(x)|x|^{\frac{2s}{p-1}}\leq\mathcal{K}_{p}\leq\limsup_{|x|\to 0^{+}}u^{\sharp}(x)|x|^{\frac{2s}{p-1}}\leq C_{0}\mathcal{K}_{p},

then we have that
either

\displaystyle\lim_{|x|\to+\infty}u_{0}(x)|x|^{N-2s}=u^{\sharp}(0)

or

\displaystyle\frac{\mathcal{K}_{p}}{C_{0}}\leq\liminf_{|x|\to+\infty}u_{0}(x)|x|^{N-\frac{2sp}{p-1}}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\mathcal{K}_{p}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\limsup_{|x|\to+\infty}u_{0}(x)|x|^{N-\frac{2sp}{p-1}}\leq C_{0}\mathcal{K}_{p},

where we use
\theta^{*}=p(N-2s)-N-2s-\tilde{\theta}=0.

We complete the proof.
\Box
