Abstract
: In this paper, we show that for finite

X
and two-stage space
Y
(for example
n
-spheres
S^{n}
, homogeneous spaces and
F_{0}
-spaces), the rational homotopy type of
\operatorname{map}(X,Y)
is determined by the cohomology algebra
H^{*}(X;\mathbb{Q})
and the rational homotopy type of
Y
. From this, we deduce the existence of H-structures on a component of the mapping space
\operatorname{map}(X,Y)

X
and

\operatorname{map}(X,Y;f)\simeq\operatorname{map}(X,Y;f^{\prime})
if the corresponding
Maurer-Cartan elements
are connected by an algebra automorphism of

1.
Introduction

For two topological spaces
X
and
Y
, let
\operatorname{map}(X,Y)
be the mapping space of all (free, continuous) maps of
X
into
Y
. A fundamental problem is to describe the homotopy type of the mapping space
\operatorname{map}(X,Y)
in terms of the homotopy types of
X
and
Y
. Following the work of Haefliger
[
6
]
, Møller-Raussen
[
12
]
described the the rational homotopy classification problem for the components of some mapping spaces
\operatorname{map}(X,Y)
. In particular, Møller-Raussen gave an elegant formula for the rational homotopy type of the components of
\operatorname{map}(X,S^{n}_{\mathbb{Q}})
in terms of the cohomology algebra
H^{*}(X;\mathbb{Q})
and the Sullivan model of
S^{n}_{\mathbb{Q}}
. In fact, Møller-Raussen proved that if
X
is rationally
(n)
-coconnected space, i.e.
H^{\geq n}(X;\mathbb{Q})=0
and the rational space
Y
has the Sullivan model of the form
(\Lambda(x,y),dy=x^{m})
with
|y|\geq n
(
|y|
denotes the degree of
y
), then the rational homotopy type of the components of
\operatorname{map}(X,Y)
is depended on the cohomology algebra
H^{*}(X;\mathbb{Q})
and the Sullivan model of
Y
.

Recently, the rational homotopy type of mapping spaces seem to be well described in terms of the theory of
L_{\infty}
-algebra
[
1
,
2
,
3
,
9
]
. By using the homotopy transfer theorem for
L_{\infty}
-algebra, Buijs and Gutiérrez
[
3
]
gave an
L_{\infty}
-model for mapping space
\operatorname{map}(X,Y)
with the cohomology of
X
and the
L_{\infty}
-model of
Y
. Inspired by their work, we extend Møller-Raussen’s result by the following theorem

Theorem 1.1
.

Let
X
be a connected and finite nilpotent CW-complex, and let
Y
be a connected rational space that has the minimal Sullivan model of the form

(1.1)

(\Lambda(P\oplus Q),dP=0,dQ\subset\Lambda P),

where
P
and
Q
are graded spaces of finite type. Then the rational homotopy type of
\operatorname{map}(X,Y)
is determined by the cohomology algebra
H^{*}(X;\mathbb{Q})
and the Sullivan algebra
(\Lambda(P\oplus Q),d)
.

Remark 1.2
.

In Theorem 1.4 of
[
7
]
it is shown that it is always possible to obtain a model of
\operatorname{map}(X,Y)
of the form
(\Lambda(V\otimes H),d)
, where
(\Lambda V,d^{\prime})
is a Sullivan model of
Y
and
H=H_{\ast}(X;\mathbb{Q})
. But the differential
d^{\prime}
depends on the structure of the coalgebra model of
X
instead of the coproduct of
H_{\ast}(X;\mathbb{Q})
, i.e. the product of
H^{\ast}(X;\mathbb{Q})
.

Remark 1.3
.

Note that
S^{n}
or
\mathbb{C}P^{n}
satisfy the hypotheses of
Y
in Theorem
1.1
. More generally, the pure Sullivan algebras and the Sullivan model of homogeneous spaces and
F_{0}
-spaces also satisfy the hypotheses of
Y
in Theorem
1.1

[
4
]
.

G. Lupton and S. B. Smith showed that for a CW-complex
Y
, the space
\operatorname{map}(Y,Y;id)
is a group-like space
[
11
]
. We show that

Corollary 1.4
.

Let
Y
be as in the Theorem
1.1
. If
H^{\ast}(X;\mathbb{Q})\simeq H^{\ast}(Y;\mathbb{Q})
, then there exist a component of mapping space
\operatorname{map}(X,Y)
which is a rational H-space.

Let
X
and
Y
be as in Theorem
1.1
. Given an isomorphism
\psi\colon H^{\ast}(X;\mathbb{Q})\rightarrow H^{\ast}(X;\mathbb{Q})
between the cohomology algebra. Let
\mathsf{MC}(H^{\ast}(X;\mathbb{Q})\otimes L)
denote the
Maurer-Cartan
set of
H^{\ast}(X;\mathbb{Q})\otimes L
(see Section 3), we have

Theorem 1.5
.

If
h\otimes l\in\mathsf{MC}(H^{\ast}(X,\mathbb{Q})\otimes L)
, then
\psi(h)\otimes l\in\mathsf{MC}(H\otimes L)
and
\operatorname{map}(X,Y;f)\simeq\operatorname{map}(X,Y;f^{\prime})
, where
h\otimes l
and
\psi(h)\otimes l
represent the maps
f\colon X\rightarrow Y
and
f^{\prime}\colon X\rightarrow Y
respectively.

In the rest of the paper
X
will always denote a nilpotent finite CW-complex and
Y
will always denote a rational finite type CW-complex. We write
H^{\ast}(X)
for
H^{\ast}(X,\mathbb{Q})
.

The paper is organized as follows. In Section 2 a brief exposition of the
L_{\infty}
-model for a mapping spaces and the homotopy transfer theorem for

-algebra is given. Section 3 contains the proof of Theorem
1.1

2.
L_{\infty}
-algebra and the homotopy transfer theorem

In this section, we recall basic facts about
L_{\infty}
-algebras.

2.1.
L_{\infty}
-algebra

Definition 2.1
.

An
L_{\infty}
-algebra
(L,\{\ell_{k}\})

is a graded vector space
L
together with linear maps

\ell_{k}\colon L^{\otimes k}\to L,\quad x_{1}\otimes\cdots\otimes x_{k}\mapsto[x_{1},\cdots,x_{k}],

of degree
k-2
, for
k\geq 1
, satisfying the following two conditions:

(i)

Anti-symmetry,

[\cdots,x,y,\cdots]=-(-1)^{|x||y|}[\cdots,y,x,\cdots],

(ii)

The generalized Jacobi identity,

\sum_{i+j=n+1}\sum_{\sigma\in S(i,n-i)}\varepsilon_{\sigma}\varepsilon(-1)^{i(j-1)}[[x_{\sigma(1)}\ldots x_{\sigma(i)}],x_{\sigma(i+1)},\ldots,x_{\sigma(n)}]=0,

where
S(i,n-i)
denotes the set of
(i,n-i)
shuffles.

An
L_{\infty}
-algebra
(L,\{\ell_{k}\})
is called
minimal
if
\ell_{1}=0
.
For two
L_{\infty}
-algebras
(L,\{\ell_{k}\}_{k\geq 1}
and
(L^{\prime},\{\ell^{\prime}_{k}\}_{k\geq 1}
, an
L_{\infty}
-morphism
f\colon L\to L^{\prime}
is a family of skew-symmetric linear maps
\{f^{n}\colon L^{\otimes n}\to L^{\prime}\}
of degree
n-1
(
n\geq 1
) which satisfy an infinite sequence of equations involving the brackets

\ell_{k}
and
\ell^{\prime}_{k}
(see for instance
[
8
]
).
It is called an
L_{\infty}

quasi-isomorphism
if
f^{(1)}\colon(L,\ell_{1})\to(L^{\prime},\ell_{1}^{\prime})
is a quasi-isomorphism of complexes.

Recall that a model for a space (not necessarily connected) means that a commutative differential graded algebra whose simplicial realization has the same homotopy type as the singular simplicial approximation of its rationalization
[
13
]
. Similarly, we say an
L_{\infty}
-algebra model for a space means that an
L_{\infty}
-algebra
L
such that
\mathscr{C}^{*}(L)
is a commutative differential graded algebra model, where
\mathscr{C}^{*}(L)
is the
\emph{Chevalley-Eilenbergconstruction}
for
L_{\infty}
-algebra
[
1
]
.

Theorem 2.2
.

[
1
, Theorem 1.4]

If
(A,d_{A})
is a finite dimensional commutative differential differential graded algebra model for
X
and
(L,\{\ell^{\prime}_{k}\}_{k\geq 1})
is an
L_{\infty}
-model for
Y
, then the following
L_{\infty}
-algebra is a model of
\operatorname{map}(X,Y)
:

(A\otimes L,\{\ell_{k}\}_{k\geq 1}),

where the brackets are defined by

\ell_{1}(x\otimes a)=d_{A}(x)\otimes a\pm x\otimes\ell^{\prime}_{1}(a),

[x_{1}\otimes a_{1},\cdots,x_{r}\otimes a_{r}]=\pm x_{1}\cdots x_{r}\otimes[a_{1},\cdots,a_{r}],\quad r>1,

where
x,x_{1},\cdots,x_{r}\in A
and
a,a_{1},\cdots,a_{r}\in L
.

H-space structures of mapping space

An interesting question is to determine whether a mapping space is of the rational homotopy type of an H-space. We show

Corollary 3.4
.

Let
Y
be as in the Theorem
1.1
. If
H^{\ast}(X)\simeq H^{\ast}(Y)
, then there exist a component of mapping space
\operatorname{map}(X,Y)
which is a rational H-space.

Proof.

By Therorem
1.1
,
\operatorname{map}(X,Y)\simeq\operatorname{map}(Y,Y).
Then, there exist a component
\operatorname{map}(X,Y;f)
of
\operatorname{map}(X,Y)
such that
\operatorname{map}(X,Y;f)\simeq\operatorname{map}(Y,Y;id)
. Note that
\operatorname{map}(Y,Y;1)
is a group-like space
[
11
, Theorem 3.6]
. It follows that
\operatorname{map}(X,Y;f)
is a rational H-space.
∎

Classifying the component of the mapping spaces

Let
X
and
Y
be as in the Theorem
1.1
. A fundamental problem is to classify the components of
\operatorname{map}(X,Y)
up to homotopy type. By Theorem
1.1
, we have an
\L_{\infty}
-model
H\otimes L
for the
\operatorname{map}(X,Y)
. Recall (
[
1
, Theorem 1.5]
) that

[X,Y]\cong\mathscr{MC}(H\otimes L),

where the moduli space
\mathscr{MC}(H\otimes L)=\mathsf{MC}(H\otimes L)/\sim
the quotient set of equivalence classes of
Maurer-Cartan element
. Let
z\in\mathsf{MC}(H\otimes L)
, we have a new
\L_{\infty}
-algebra
H\otimes L^{z}

[
5
, Proposition 4.4]
. The truncated and twisted
L_{\infty}
-algebra
(H\otimes L^{z})_{\geq 0}
is an
L_{\infty}
-model for the component
\operatorname{map}(X,Y;f)
, where the
Maurer-Cartan element

z
represents the map
f\colon X\rightarrow Y
.

Given an isomorphism
\psi\colon H^{\ast}(X)\rightarrow H^{\ast}(X)
between the cohomology algebra. Consider the linear map
\psi\otimes id\colon H\otimes L\rightarrow H\otimes L
, we have

Proposition 3.6
.

If
h\otimes l\in\mathsf{MC}(H\otimes L)
, then
\psi\otimes id(h\otimes l)=\psi(h)\otimes l\in\mathsf{MC}(H\otimes L)
.

Proof.

By Theorem
1.1
, the
L_{\infty}
-structure
\{l_{k}\}_{k\geq 1}
of
H\otimes L
is depended on the product of
H
and the
L_{\infty}
-structure of
L
, i.e.
l_{k}(h\otimes l,\ldots,h\otimes l)=h\cdot_{H}h\ldots h\otimes[l,\ldots,l]_{L}
. Thus,

\displaystyle 0

\displaystyle=\psi\otimes id\big(\sum_{k\geq 0}\frac{1}{k!}l_{k}(h\otimes l,\cdots,h\otimes l)\big)

\displaystyle=\sum_{k\geq 0}\frac{1}{k!}l_{k}(\psi(h)\otimes l,\ldots,\psi(h)\otimes l)

\displaystyle=\sum_{k\geq 0}\frac{1}{k!}\psi(h\cdot_{H}h\ldots h)\otimes[l,\ldots,l].

It follows that
\psi(h)\otimes l\in\mathsf{MC}(H\otimes L)
.
∎

Let the
Maurer-Cartan elements

h\otimes l
and
\psi(h)\otimes l
repesent the maps
f
and
f^{\prime}
respectively. Note that, as graded vector spaces,

\psi\otimes id\colon(H\otimes L^{h\otimes l})_{\geq 0}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\cong}}{{\longrightarrow}}(H\otimes L^{\psi(h)\otimes l})_{\geq 0}.

It is now easily checked that this isomorphism is compatible with the
L_{\infty}
-structure, i.e.
(H\otimes L^{h\otimes l})_{\geq 0}
and
(H\otimes L^{\psi(h)\otimes l})_{\geq 0}
are isomorphism of
L_{\infty}
-algebra. Thus,

\operatorname{map}(X,Y;f)\simeq_{\mathbb{Q}}\operatorname{map}(X,Y;f^{\prime}).
