Abstract
. In this article, we consider the representation of m m -gonal forms over ℕ 0 {\mathbb{N}}_{0} . We show that any m m -gonal forms over ℕ 0 {\mathbb{N}}_{0} of rank ≥ 5 \geq 5 is almost regular and ponder the sufficiently large integers which are indeed represented over ℕ 0 {\mathbb{N}}_{0} among the integers which are locally represented. And as its consequential result, we prove a finiteness theorem for universal (original polygonal number version’s) m m -gonal forms over ℕ 0 {\mathbb{N}}_{0} .

Proposition 2.1
.

Suppose that
\max\limits_{1\leq i\leq n}\sqrt{a_{i}}\sqrt{\alpha}\leq\beta
.
Then for any
(x_{1},\cdots,x_{n})\in\mathbb{R}^{n}
satisfying

(2.2)

\begin{cases}a_{1}x_{1}^{2}+\cdots+a_{n}x_{n}^{2}=\alpha\\
a_{1}x_{1}+\cdots+a_{n}x_{n}=\beta,\end{cases}

we have that
x_{i}\geq 0
for all
1\leq i\leq n

Proof.

Note that the hyperbolic plane
a_{1}x_{1}+\cdots+a_{n}x_{n}=\beta
intersects with the sphere

a_{1}x_{1}^{2}+\cdots+a_{n}x_{n}^{2}=\alpha
only on the space
(\mathbb{R}^{+}\cup\{0\})^{n}
.
∎

We recall a simple observation that the system (
2.1

\mathbf{x}\in{\mathbb{Z}},{\mathbb{N}}_{0}
, or
{\mathbb{Z}}_{p}
if and only if

\left(B+k(m-2)-\left(\sum\limits_{i=2}^{n}a_{i}x_{i}\right)\right)^{2}+\sum\limits_{i=2}^{n}a_{1}a_{i}x_{i}^{2}=2Aa_{1}+Ba_{1}+k(m-4)a_{1}

.
The equation (

\beta_{A,B;m}^{+}+K(\mathbf{a})<\alpha_{A,B;m}^{+}

with
0\leq B\leq m-3
holds for any
A\geq N(a_{1},\cdots,a_{n})\cdot(m-2)^{2}
and
m\geq 3
.

Consequently, we may conclude that for an integer
A(m-2)+B
which is locally represented by
\left<a_{1},\cdots,a_{n}\right>_{m}
with

\begin{cases}A\geq N(a_{1},\cdots,a_{n})\cdot(m-2)^{2}\\
0\leq B\leq m-3,\end{cases}

we may take
k\in{\mathbb{N}}_{0}
satisfying (
3.2
).
Then from the first and second conditions of (
3.2
), we obtain that
A(m-2)+B
is represented by
\left<a_{1},\cdots,a_{n}\right>_{m}
over
{\mathbb{Z}}
, i.e., there is
\mathbf{x}\in{\mathbb{Z}}^{n}
for which

A(m-2)+B=a_{1}P_{m}(x_{1})+\cdots+a_{n}P_{m}(x_{n})

holds, more precisely, the diophantine system

\begin{cases}a_{1}x_{1}^{2}+\cdots+a_{n}x_{n}^{2}=2A+B+k(m-4)\\
a_{1}x_{1}+\cdots+a_{n}x_{n}=B+k(m-2)\\
\end{cases}

holds.
And from the third condition of (
3.2
), we obtain that the
\mathbf{x}
is indeed in
{\mathbb{N}}_{0}^{n}
by Proposition
2.1
.
This completes the first argument.

The Cauchy-Schwarz inequality gives that

(a_{1}+\cdots+a_{n})(a_{1}x_{1}^{2}+\cdots+a_{n}x_{n}^{2})\leq(a_{1}x_{1}+\cdots+a_{n}x_{n})^{2}.

Which induces that for a given
(a_{1},\cdots,a_{n})\in{\mathbb{N}}^{n}
, the cubic on
m
is optimal in this argument.
∎

4.
Finiteness theorem for universal
m
-gonal forms
(original polygonal number version)

We start this section by recalling the Bhargava’s escalaltor tree.
We call the smallest positive integer which is not represented by a non-universal
m
-gonal form
\left<a_{1},\cdots,a_{n}\right>_{m}
over
{\mathbb{N}}_{0}
as the
the truant of
\left<a_{1},\cdots,a_{n}\right>_{m}
.
The
escalator tree of
m
-gonal forms over
{\mathbb{N}}_{0}
is one sided tree with root
\emptyset_{m}
.
Conventionally, the truant of the
m
-gonal form
\emptyset_{m}
is considered as the smallest positive integer
1
.
If an
m
-gonal form
\left<a_{1},\cdots,a_{n}\right>_{m}
of a node is not universal (i.e., has the truant), then we connect the node with the nodes
\left<a_{1},\cdots,a_{n+1}\right>_{m}
which are escalated superforms of
\left<a_{1},\cdots,a_{n}\right>_{m}
to represent the truant of
\left<a_{1},\cdots,a_{n}\right>_{m}
.
Note that by the construction, the rank of every
m
-gonal form of depth
n
of the escalator tree is
n
.
In order to avoid the appearance of the same forms, we set assumption
a_{1}\leq\cdots\leq a_{n}\leq a_{n+1}
.
If an
m
-gonal form on a node is universal (i.e., does not have truant), then the node become a leaf of the tree.

So, universal forms of the tree appear only on the leaves and the universal
m
-gonal forms on the leaves would be kind of proper universal forms.
And one may notice that a univesal
m
-gonal form would contains at least one (proper universal)
m
-gonal form on a leaf of the escalator tree of
m
-gonal form as its subform.

Once one completes the escalator tree of
m
-gonal forms over
{\mathbb{N}}_{0}
, then one should get the
\gamma_{m;{\mathbb{N}}_{0}}
as the largest truant of the tree.

Since the smallest positive
m
-gonal number (over
{\mathbb{N}}_{0}
) is
m
except
1
, if
a_{1}+\cdots+a_{n}<m-1
, then the
m
-gonal form
\left<a_{1},\cdots,a_{n}\right>_{m}
would not represent
a_{1}+\cdots+a_{n}+1
(more precisely, all the integers between
a_{1}+\cdots+a_{n}+1
and
m-1
).
So for an
m
-gonal form
\left<a_{1},\cdots,a_{n}\right>_{m}
on a node of the escalator tree, in the case that
a_{1}+\cdots+a_{n}<m-1
, its truant would be
a_{1}+\cdots+a_{n}+1
and so its children would be forms of

\left<a_{1},\cdots,a_{n},a_{n+1}\right>_{m}

where
a_{n}\leq a_{n+1}\leq a_{1}+\cdots+a_{n}+1
.
Which gives a special observation that for a fixed depth
d
, the depth
d
of the escalator tree of
m
-gonal form take on the same forms for all sufficiently large
m
.
Especially, as an example, when
m\geq 8
, the escalator tree of
m
-gonal forms over
{\mathbb{N}}_{0}
up to
3
depth would appear as following :

\emptyset_{m}
\left<1\right>_{m}
\left<1,1\right>_{m}
\left<1,1,1\right>_{m}
\vdots
\left<1,1,2\right>_{m}
\vdots
\left<1,1,3\right>_{m}
\vdots
\left<1,2\right>_{m}
\left<1,2,2\right>_{m}
\vdots
\left<1,2,3\right>_{m}
\vdots
\left<1,2,4\right>_{m}
\vdots

Now we prove a finiteness theorem for univesal
m
-gonal forms over
{\mathbb{N}}_{0}
based on the Bhargava’s escalator tree idea.
Just before, we see a method to determine the local representability of
m
-gonal by adopting already well known local representability results of quadratic forms.

Proposition 4.1
.

Let
F_{m}(\mathbf{x})=a_{1}P_{m}(x_{1})+\cdots+a_{n}P_{m}(x_{n})
be a primitive
m
-gonal form.

(1)

When
p
is an odd prime with
p|m-2
,
F_{m}(\mathbf{x})
is universal over
{\mathbb{Z}}_{p}
.

(2)

When
m\not\equiv 0\pmod{4}
,
F_{m}(\mathbf{x})
is universal over
{\mathbb{Z}}_{2}
.

(3)

When
p
is an odd prime with
(p,m-2)=1
, an integer
N
is represented by
F_{m}(\mathbf{x})
over
{\mathbb{Z}}_{p}
if and only if the integer
8(m-2)N+(a_{1}+\cdots+a_{n})(m-4)^{2}
is represented by the diagonal quadratic form
\left<a_{1},\cdots,a_{n}\right>
over
{\mathbb{Z}}_{p}
.

(4)

When
m\equiv 0\pmod{4}
, an integer
N
is represented by
F_{m}(\mathbf{x})
over
{\mathbb{Z}}_{2}
if and only if the integer
\frac{m-2}{2}N+(a_{1}+\cdots+a_{n})\left(\frac{m-4}{4}\right)^{2}
is represented by the diagonal quadratic form
\left<a_{1},\cdots,a_{n}\right>
over
{\mathbb{Z}}_{2}
.

Proof.

See Proposition 3.1 in
[
12
]
.
∎

Remark 4.2
.

m
-gonal forms

From Theorem
