Partial-wave analysis of τ − → π − π − π + ν τ \mathup{{{\tau}}^{\scriptstyle{-}}}\to\mathup{{{\pi}}^{\scriptstyle{-}}}\mathup{{{\pi}}^{\scriptstyle{-}}}\mathup{{{\pi}}^{\scriptstyle{+}}}\mathup{{{\nu}}{}_{\scriptstyle{\!\tau}}} at BELLE
A. Rabusov \from ins:TUM, D. Greenwald \from ins:TUM, \atque S. Paul \from ins:TUM for the Belle collaboration

Abstract
We present preliminary results of a partial-wave analysis of τ − → π − π − π + ν τ \mathup{{{\tau}}^{\scriptstyle{-}}}\to\mathup{{{\pi}}^{\scriptstyle{-}}}\mathup{{{\pi}}^{\scriptstyle{-}}}\mathup{{{\pi}}^{\scriptstyle{+}}}\mathup{{{\nu}}{}_{\scriptstyle{\!\tau}}} in data from the Belle experiment at the KEKB e + ​ e − \mathup{{{e}}^{\scriptstyle{+}}}\mathup{{{e}}^{\scriptstyle{-}}} collider. We demonstrate the presence of the a 1 ​ ( 1420 ) \mathrm{a}_{1}(1420) and a 1 ​ ( 1640 ) \mathrm{a}_{1}(1640) resonances in tauon decays and measure their masses and widths. We also present validation of our findings using a model-independent approach. Our results can improve modeling in simulation studies necessary for measuring the tauon electric and magnetic dipole moments and Michel parameters.

Our event selection criteria
[
9
]
are optimized, using
simulated data, to maximize efficiency and purity, but chosen not to
distort the phase-space variables. In each event, we define two
hemispheres defined by the plane perpendicular the thrust axis of all
charged particles and photons. We require three charged particles be
present in one hemisphere, the signal hemisphere, and one be present in
the other, the tag hemisphere.

Using simulated data, we train a boosted decision tree (BDT) to remove
events from processes other than
\mathup{{{e}}^{\scriptstyle{+}}}\mathup{{{e}}^{\scriptstyle{-}}}\to\mathup{{{\tau}}^{\scriptstyle{+}}}\mathup{{{\tau}}^{\scriptstyle{-}}}
. It looks at six event
variables: the thrust, the sum of charged-particle and photon momenta
in the center-of-momentum (CM) frame, the mass and cosine of the polar
angle of the missing four-momentum in the CM frame, the total energy
measured in the Belle’s electromagnetic calorimeter, and the sum of the
energies of charged particles in the CM frame. The thrust is the most
discriminating of the variables.

We veto the presence of charged kaons in the signal hemisphere by
requiring the particles with the same charge be consistent with being
pions. We veto the presence of neutral kaons by requiring

|\sqrt{s_{i}}-m_{\mathup{{{K}}^{\scriptstyle{0}}}}|>$12\text{\,}\mathrm{M}\mathrm{e}\mathrm{V}$
,
i=1,2
.
Finally, we veto the presence of neutral pions by requiring the sum of
photon energies in the signal hemisphere to be below
480\text{\,}\mathrm{M}\mathrm{e}\mathrm{V}
.

We select
55\text{\times}{10}^{6}

We conducted a preliminary study of the systematic uncertainties on
20% of the data. We varied the neural network coefficients to study
the uncertainty on the background parameterization and find that its
tantamount to the statistical uncertainty for
m_{3\pi}>$1\text{\,}\mathrm{G}\mathrm{e}\mathrm{V}$

In conclusion, we observe the
\mathrm{a}_{1}(1420)
resonance in both conventional and
quasi-model-independent PWA. After finalizing the systematic
uncertainty studies, we will provide an updated model for the

\mathup{{{\tau}}^{\scriptstyle{-}}}\to\mathup{{{\pi}}^{\scriptstyle{-}}}\mathup{{{\pi}}^{\scriptstyle{-}}}\mathup{{{\pi}}^{\scriptstyle{+}}}\mathup{{{\nu}}{}_{\scriptstyle{\!\tau}}}
with 15 partial waves, which can be used by the TAUOLA
software library for simulating tauon decay and in future measurements
of the tau electrical and magnetic dipole moments at Belle II.
