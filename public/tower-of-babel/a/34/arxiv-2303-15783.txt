On Causal Equivalence by Tracing in String Rewriting
Vincent van Oostrom

Abstract
We introduce proof terms for string rewrite systems and, using these, show that various notions of equivalence on reductions known from the literature can be viewed as different perspectives on the notion of causal equivalence. In particular, we show that permutation equivalence classes (as known from the λ \lambda -calculus and term rewriting) are uniquely represented both by trace graphs (known from physics as causal graphs) and by so-called greedy multistep reductions (as known from algebra). We present effective maps from the former to the latter, topological multi-sorting 𝖳𝖲 \mathsf{TS} , and vice versa, the proof term algebra ⟦ ⟧ \llbracket\,\rrbracket .

We adapt the theory of permutation equivalence from term

Lemma 1

(Logicality)
.

If
{\gamma}\mathbin{:}{s}\mathrel{{\geqslant}}{t}
for some proof
term
{\gamma}
, then there is a reduction
{\gamma}^{\prime}\mathbin{:}{s}\mathrel{{\geqslant}}{t}

with
{\gamma}\mathrel{{{\equiv}}}{\gamma}^{\prime}
.

Proof.

By induction and cases on
{\gamma}
.

(empty)

the empty string
{\varepsilon}
is an empty reduction;

(letter)

a single letter
{a}
is an empty reduction;

(rule)

a single rule
\varrho
is a single step reduction from its lhs to its rhs;

(juxtaposition)

suppose we have a proof term
{\gamma}\mathrel{{:}{=}}{\gamma}_{{1}}{\gamma}_{{2}}\mathbin{:}{s}_{{1}}{s}_{{2}}\mathrel{{\geqslant}}{t}_{{1}}{t}_{{2}}

with
{\gamma}_{{i}}\mathbin{:}{s}_{{i}}\mathrel{{\geqslant}}{t}_{{i}}
.
By the IH we have reductions

{\gamma}_{{i}}^{\prime}\mathbin{:}{s}_{{i}}\mathrel{{\geqslant}}{t}_{{i}}

with
{\gamma}_{{i}}\mathrel{{{\equiv}}}{\gamma}_{{i}}^{\prime}
.
Set
{\gamma}^{\prime}
to

{\gamma}_{{1}}^{\prime}\langle{s}_{{2}}\rangle\mathbin{{\cdot}}\langle{t}_{{1}}\rangle{\gamma}_{{2}}^{\prime}
, where for a reduction
{\zeta}
and string
{u}
,

{\zeta}\langle{u}\rangle
denotes the reduction obtained
by suffixing each step of
{\zeta}
by
{u}
, and
symmetrically for
\langle{u}\rangle{\zeta}
.
One easily verifies

{\gamma}^{\prime}\mathbin{:}{s}_{{1}}{s}_{{2}}\mathrel{{\geqslant}}{t}_{{1}}{t}_{{2}}
,
and also that
{\gamma}\mathrel{{{\equiv}}}{\gamma}^{\prime}
by using (exchange) and vertical units repeatedly.
Then by repeated vertical associativity applied to
{\gamma}^{\prime}
we obtain
a reduction, except in case one or both of the
{\gamma}_{{i}}^{\prime}

is the empty reduction in which case we conclude by eliding one such by a horizontal unit.

(transitivity)

by vertically composing the reductions obtained by the IH
for the constituent proof terms, possibly followed by associating to right and
eliding empty reductions as before. ∎

The proof is effective, transforming proof

Although the logicality lemma allows

We have shown that Lévy’s notion of permutation
