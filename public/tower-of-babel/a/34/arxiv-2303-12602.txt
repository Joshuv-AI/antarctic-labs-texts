Abstract
. We look at rank two parabolic Higgs bundles over the projective line minus five points which are semistable with respect to a weight vector μ ∈ [ 0 , 1 ] 5 \mu\in[0,1]^{5} . The moduli space corresponding to the central weight μ c = ( 1 2 , … , 1 2 ) \mu_{c}=(\frac{1}{2},\dots,\frac{1}{2}) is studied in details and all singular fibers of the Hitchin map are described, including the nilpotent cone. After giving a description of fixed points of the ℂ ∗ \mathbb{C}^{*} -action we obtain a proof of Simpson’s foliation conjecture in this case. For each n ≥ 5 n\geq 5 , we remark that there is a weight vector so that the foliation conjecture in the moduli space of rank two logarithmic connections over the projective line minus n n points is false.
