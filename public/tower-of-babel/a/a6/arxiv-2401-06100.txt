Special values of p p -adic L L -functions and Iwasawa λ \lambda -invariants of Dirichlet characters
Heiko Knospe

Abstract
. We study the Iwasawa λ \lambda -invariant of Dirichlet characters χ \chi of arbitrary order for odd primes p p . From special values of the p p -adic L L -function and its derivative we derive several novel and easily computable criteria to distinguish between the cases λ = 0 \lambda=0 , λ = 1 \lambda=1 , λ = 2 \lambda=2 and λ ⩾ 3 \lambda\geqslant 3 . In particular, we look at the case when the p p -adic L L -function vanishes at s = 0 s=0 . Using formulas of Ferrero-Greenberg and Gross-Koblitz, we give conditions for λ p ​ ( χ ) > 1 \lambda_{p}(\chi)>1 and λ p ​ ( χ ) > 2 \lambda_{p}(\chi)>2 . Furthermore, we extend methods of Ernvall-Metsänkylä and Dummit et al. to calculate the λ \lambda -invariant by twisting χ \chi with characters ψ \psi of the second kind and using the values of the p p -adic L L -function at s = 2 − p , … , 0 s=2-p,\dots,0 . In addition, we leverage the value at s = 1 s=1 to compute λ p ​ ( χ ) \lambda_{p}(\chi) . The formulas are also used to obtain numerical data on the distribution of λ \lambda -invariants, where either the prime p p or the Dirichlet character χ \chi is fixed.

\omega

In our article

In the present article, we give several new formulas for the

-adic

The main results of this article are Theorem

13
]
,
[
8
]
) and can be shown using only Galois theory and class field theory.
Let
\Gamma=\text{Gal}(K_{\infty}/K)\cong\mathbb{Z}_{p}
and let
\widetilde{K}
be the compositum of all
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
-extensions of
K
. Then
G:=\mathrm{Gal}(\widetilde{K}/K)\cong\mathbb{Z}_{p}^{r_{2}+1+\delta}
with
\delta\geqslant 0
(see
[
16
]
Theorem 13.4) and
X:=\mathrm{Gal}(\widetilde{K}/K_{\infty})\cong\mathbb{Z}_{p}^{r_{2}+\delta}
. We have an exact sequence

0\longrightarrow X\longrightarrow G\longrightarrow\Gamma\longrightarrow 0.

Let
\mathfrak{p}
be a prime of
K
above
p
, and let
\mathfrak{P}
be a prime of
\widetilde{K}
that lies above
\mathfrak{p}
. We define
T=T(\mathfrak{P}/\mathfrak{p})\subset G
to be the associated inertia subgroup. We claim that projection of
T
to
\Gamma
is injective. To this end, we use the assumption that the primes
\mathfrak{p}
above
p
are fully split in
K
. Hence the local unit group
U_{\mathfrak{p}}
is isomorphic to
\mathbb{Z}_{p}^{\times}
, and the pro-
p
-part
U^{0}_{\mathfrak{p}}
is isomorphic to
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
. Class field theory then implies that the inertia group
T
is the image of
U^{0}_{\mathfrak{p}}
under the Artin map. Since
\mathfrak{p}
is ramified in the extension
K_{\infty}/K
, the inertia group
T
cannot be trivial and must be isomorphic to
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
. Hence the reduction map from
T
to
\Gamma
is injective and
T\cap X=0
, which shows that the primes above
p
are unramified in the extension
\widetilde{K}/K_{\infty}
. We thus obtain that
\widetilde{K}/K_{\infty}
is unramified everywhere. Since the Galois group
X
is isomorphic to
\mathbb{Z}_{p}^{r_{2}+\delta}
, the
\lambda_{p}
-invariant of
K
must be
\geqslant r_{2}
.
∎

4.
Rank Zero Case

We now consider the general situation when
L_{p}(0,\chi)\neq 0
. The following theorem provides criteria for the cases
\lambda_{p}(\chi)=0
,
\lambda_{p}(\chi)=1
,
\lambda_{p}(\chi)=2
and
\lambda_{p}(\chi)\geqslant 3
. Of course, part (i) is well known.

Theorem 4.1
.

Let
\chi
be a non-trivial even character of the first kind and suppose that
\chi\omega^{-1}(p)\neq 1
. Then:

(i)

\lambda_{p}(\chi)>0
if and only if
v_{p}(B_{1,\chi\omega^{-1}})=v_{p}(L_{p}(0,\chi))>0
.

(ii)

Assume that
\lambda_{p}(\chi)>0
. Then
\lambda_{p}(\chi)>1
if one of the following equivalent conditions is satisfied:

(a)

v_{p}(L_{p}^{\prime}(0,\chi))>1

(b)

v_{p}(-(1-\chi\omega^{-1}(p))B_{1,\chi\omega^{-1}}-L_{p}(1,\chi))>1

(c)

v_{p}\left(-(1-\chi\omega^{-1}(p)p^{p-1})\frac{B_{p,\chi\omega^{-1}}}{p}+(1-\chi\omega^{-1}(p))B_{1,\chi\omega^{-1}}\right)>1

(iii)

Assume that
\lambda_{p}(\chi)>1
.
Then
\lambda_{p}(\chi)>2
if and only if

\displaystyle v_{p}\left(-(1-\chi\omega^{-1}(p)p^{p-1})\frac{B_{p,\chi\omega^{-1}}}{p}+(1-\chi\omega^{-1}(p))B_{1,\chi\omega^{-1}}\right.

\displaystyle-\frac{L_{p}^{\prime}(0,\chi)}{\log_{p}(1+p)}((1+p)^{1-p}-1)\bigg)>2.

An equivalent condition is that

v_{p}\left(-(1-\chi\omega^{-1}(p))B_{1,\chi\omega^{-1}}+\frac{L_{p}^{\prime}(1,\chi)}{(1+p)\log_{p}(1+p)}\,p-L_{p}(1,\chi)\right)>2.

Proof.

The claims follow from Corollary
2.2
(i), (ii), (iii) and equations (
2.1
), (
2.2
), (
2.3
), (
2.4
), (
2.5
).
∎

2.5
.

Remark

Let us fix an odd character
\theta
, and let
K
be the associated totally imaginary field of degree
2r_{2}
. We know from Section
2
that
\lambda_{p}(\theta\omega)\geqslant 1
for all primes
p
which are completely split in
K/\mathbb{Q}
, i.e. primes
p
with
\theta(p)=1

p
-adic valuation of

or the valuation of

to determine whether
\lambda_{p}(\theta\omega)=1
or
\lambda_{p}(\theta\omega)>1
(see Theorem
3.2
(i)).

On the other hand, consider the non-split unramified primes
p
, i.e., primes with
\theta(p)\neq 0,1
. There are infinitely many such primes, but only finitely many have the property that
\lambda_{p}(\theta\omega)>0

since
v_{p}(B_{1,\theta})=0
for almost all primes
p
.

Furthermore, there are corresponding statements for the cyclotomic
\lambda
-invariants of

. By considering all odd powers
\theta
,
\theta^{3}
,
\dots
,

, we have

for completely split primes

, and

iff

. On the other hand, among the inert primes

\lambda_{p}(K)>0
.

5.
Twists by Characters of
p
-power Order

One can also leverage twists by characters of the second kind in order to compute
\lambda
-invariants.
Dummit et al.
[
3
]
used characters
\psi
of order
p^{n}
to set up a system of equations and to compute the coefficients of
\mathcal{F}_{\chi}(T)
mod
p^{n}
or mod
p^{n-1}
.
In this section, we follow the approach by Ernvall and Metsänkylä in
[
5
]
and show that the
p
-adic valuation of a
single value

L_{p}(1-k,\chi\psi)
suffices to compute
\lambda_{p}(\chi)
if
n
is large enough (
n=1
or
n=2
are mostly sufficient).

Proposition 5.1
.

Let
K/\mathbb{Q}_{p}
be a finite extension with ring of integers
\mathcal{O}
and ramification index
e
. Let
n
be a positive integer and

\pi_{n}
a uniformizing element of
\mathbb{Q}_{p}(\zeta_{p^{n}})
,
e.g.,
\pi_{n}=\zeta_{p^{n}}-1
.
Let
t_{0}\in\mathcal{O}
with
v(t_{0})\geqslant 1
, e.g.,
t_{0}=0
.
Suppose that

\mathcal{F}(T)\in\mathcal{O}[[T]]
is a power series satisfying
\mu_{p}(\mathcal{F})=0
.

(1)

\lambda_{p}(\mathcal{F})<\frac{(p-1)p^{n-1}}{e}\Longleftrightarrow v(\mathcal{F}(\pi_{n}))<\frac{1}{e}
, and in this case

\lambda_{p}(\mathcal{F})=v(\mathcal{F}(\pi_{n}))\cdot(p-1)p^{n-1}.\\

(2)

Suppose that
\lambda_{p}(\mathcal{F})>0
. Then
\lambda_{p}(\mathcal{F})<\frac{(p-1)p^{n-1}}{e}+1\Longleftrightarrow
v(\mathcal{F}(\pi_{n})-\mathcal{F}(t_{0}))<\frac{1}{e}+\frac{1}{(p-1)p^{n-1}}
, and in this case

\lambda_{p}(\mathcal{F})=v(\mathcal{F}(\pi_{n})-\mathcal{F}(t_{0}))\cdot(p-1)p^{n-1}.

(3)

Suppose that
\lambda_{p}(\mathcal{F})>2
. Then
\lambda_{p}(\mathcal{F})<\frac{(p-1)p^{n-1}}{e}+2\Longleftrightarrow
v(\mathcal{F}(\pi_{n})-\mathcal{F}(t_{0})-\mathcal{F}^{\prime}(t_{0})(\pi_{n}-t_{0}))<\frac{1}{e}+\frac{2}{(p-1)p^{n-1}}
, and in this case

\lambda_{p}(\mathcal{F})=v(\mathcal{F}(\pi_{n})-\mathcal{F}(t_{0})-\mathcal{F}^{\prime}(t_{0})(\pi_{n}-t_{0}))\cdot(p-1)p^{n-1}.

Proof.

The statement is true for
\lambda=\lambda_{p}(\mathcal{F})=0
, and we can assume that
\lambda\geqslant 1
.
Let
\mathcal{F}(T)=a_{0}+a_{1}(T-t_{0})\dots+a_{\lambda-1}(T-t_{0})^{\lambda-1}+a_{\lambda}(T-t_{0})^{\lambda}+\dots
. Since
v(t_{0})>0
, the
\lambda
-invariant does not depend on
t_{0}
. Furthermore,
v(a_{0}),\dots,v(a_{\lambda-1})\geqslant\frac{1}{e}
and
v(a_{\lambda})=0
. By assumption,
v(\pi_{n})=v(\pi_{n}-t_{0})=\frac{1}{(p-1)p^{n-1}}
. If
\lambda<\frac{(p-1)p^{n-1}}{e}
we have
v(\mathcal{F}(\pi_{n}))=\frac{\lambda}{(p-1)p^{n-1}}<\frac{1}{e}
. Vice versa, suppose that
\lambda\geqslant\frac{(p-1)p^{n-1}}{e}
.
Then
v(\mathcal{F}(\pi_{n}))\geqslant\frac{1}{e}
.
This proves (1), and parts (2) and (3) can be shown similarly by subtracting the terms of degree
0
and
1
from
\mathcal{F}(T)
.
∎

Remark
.

(1)

We can increase
n
in order to satisfy the condition on
\lambda_{p}(\mathcal{F})
, and thus compute the exact
\lambda
-invariant. In most cases,
n=1
or

are sufficient since large values of
\lambda_{p}(\chi)

(2)

,

,

,

)).

\zeta_{p^{n}}(1+p)^{-1}-1

(3)

Parts (2) and (3) of the above Proposition turn out to be useful for
\lambda>0
, small primes
p
and

or

. In our applications, the constant

The Theorem below is a slight improvement of

Theorem 5.2
.

Let

be a non-trivial Dirichlet character of the first kind, and let

be the ramification index of
\mathcal{O}_{\chi}
. Define

if

(See

,
and if this is satisfied then

(2)

\lambda_{p}(\chi)>0
. Then

. Then

\displaystyle\hskip 42.67912pt\lambda_{p}(\chi)=

Theorem 5.3
.

Let
\chi
be a non-trivial Dirichlet character of the first kind, and let
e
be the ramification index of
\mathcal{O}_{\chi}
. Let
\psi
be a character of the second kind of order
p^{n}
, where
n\geqslant 1
.

(1)

\lambda_{p}(\chi)<\frac{(p-1)p^{n-1}}{e}\Longleftrightarrow v(L_{p}(1,\chi\psi))<\frac{1}{e}
,
and if this is satisfied then

\lambda_{p}(\chi)=v(L_{p}(1,\chi\psi))\cdot(p-1)p^{n-1}.

(2)

Suppose that
\lambda_{p}(\chi)>0
. Then

\lambda_{p}(\chi)<\frac{(p-1)p^{n-1}}{e}+1\Longleftrightarrow

\displaystyle\hskip 42.67912pt\lambda_{p}(\chi)<\frac{(p-1)p^{n-1}}{e}+2\Longleftrightarrow

\displaystyle\hskip-128.0374ptv\left(L_{p}(1,\chi\psi)-L_{p}(1,\chi)-\frac{L_{p}^{\prime}(1,\chi)}{\log_{p}(1+p)}\>(\zeta_{p^{n}}-1)\right)<\frac{1}{e}+\frac{2}{(p-1)p^{n-1}},

\displaystyle\hskip 42.67912pt\lambda_{p}(\chi)=v\left(L_{p}(1,\chi\psi)-L_{p}(1,\chi)-\frac{L_{p}^{\prime}(1,\chi)}{\log_{p}(1+p)}\>(\zeta_{p^{n}}-1)\right)\cdot(p-1)p^{n-1}.
