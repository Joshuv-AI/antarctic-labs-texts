concavity property of minimal L 2 L^{2} integrals with Lebesgue measurable gain VIII: Partial linearity and log-convexity
Shijie Bao, Qi’an Guan, Zheng Yuan

Abstract
. In this article, we give some necessary conditions for the concavity property of minimal L 2 L^{2} integrals degenerating to partial linearity, a characterization for the concavity degenerating to partial linearity for open Riemann surfaces, and some relations between the concavity property for minimal L 2 L^{2} integrals and the log-convexity for Bergman kernels.

Lemma 2.17

Proof of Theorem

Proof of Theorem

We prove Theorem

. Denote that

By definition,

\tilde{G}_{k}(t)\leq\lambda(\{G_{\Omega}(\cdot,z_{0})<-t\})

Proof of Example

Let

\begin{split}\int_{\{\psi<-t\}}|F|^{2}&=\int_{\{2(k+1)\log|z|<-t\}}\bigg|\sum_{0\leq j\leq k}a_{j}z^{j}+\sum_{j>k}b_{j}z^{j}\bigg|^{2}\\

Denote that
