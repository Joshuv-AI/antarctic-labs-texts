Now by Proposition

For every

By Lemma

The proof of Corollary
1.2

Consider a braided tensor equivalence
{\rm F}:\mathscr{D}\xrightarrow{\cong}\mathscr{Z}({\rm Coh}(G,\omega))
, and let
\mathcal{F}:\mathscr{Z}({\rm Coh}(G,\omega))\twoheadrightarrow{\rm Coh}(G,\omega)
be the forgetful functor. Let

denote the subcategory of
\mathscr{D}
consisting of all objects sent to
{\rm Vec}
under

is the preimage of
{\rm Rep}(G)
under
{\rm F}

2.5
). Then
f(G,\omega,{\rm F})
is a Tannakian Lagrangian subcategory of
\mathscr{D}
, which is clearly independent of the equivalence class of
(G,\omega,{\rm F})
.
Thus, every equivalence class
[(G,\omega,{\rm F})]
gives rise to a Tannakian Lagrangian subcategory
{\rm f}([G,\omega,{\rm F}]):=f(G,\omega,{\rm F})
of
\mathscr{D}
.

Conversely, assume
{\rm Rep}(G)\subset\mathscr{D}
is a Tannakian Lagrangian subcategory of
\mathscr{D}

for some finite group scheme
G
over
k
. Then by
Theorem
1.1
, we have a braided tensor equivalence
{\rm F}:\mathscr{D}\cong\mathscr{Z}({\rm Coh}(G,\omega))
for some

\omega\in H^{3}(G,\mathbb{G}_{m})
. Thus, every Tannakian Lagrangian subcategory
{\rm Rep}(G)
of
\mathscr{D}
gives rise to an equivalence class
[(G,\omega,{\rm F})]
. Set
{\rm h}({\rm Rep}(G)):=[(G,\omega,{\rm F})]
.

We claim that the assignments
{\rm f},{\rm h}
constructed above are inverse to each other.

Given a Tannakian Lagrangian subcategory
\mathscr{E}={\rm Rep}(G)
of
\mathscr{D}
, let
A:=\mathscr{O}(G)
and
\mathscr{C}:={\rm Mod}(A)_{\mathscr{D}}
(see
2.5
). Since by Corollary
3.4
, the functor
\mathscr{D}\xrightarrow{{\rm F}}\mathscr{Z}(\mathscr{C})\xrightarrow{{\rm Forget}}\mathscr{C}
coincides with the free
A
-module functor, it follows that the category
{\rm f}({\rm h}(\mathscr{E}))=f(G,\omega,{\rm F})

consists of all objects
X
in
\mathscr{D}
such that
X\otimes A
is a multiple of
A
.
Since
A
is the regular object of
\mathscr{E}
, it follows that
\mathscr{E}\subset{\rm f}({\rm h}(\mathscr{E}))
, so

\mathscr{E}={\rm f}({\rm h}(\mathscr{E}))
(as both categories have FP dimension
|G|
). Thus,
{\rm f}\circ{\rm h}={\rm Id}
, as desired.

Given an equivalence class
[(G_{1},\omega_{1},{\rm F}_{1})]
, we have to show now that
({\rm h}\circ{\rm f})([G_{1},\omega_{1},{\rm F}_{1}])=[(G_{1},\omega_{1},{\rm F}_{1})]
.
Since
f(G_{1},\omega_{1},{\rm F}_{1})\cong{\rm Rep}(G_{1})

{\rm F}_{1}
), it follows from
[
DM
]
that there exist a finite group scheme

over

({\rm h}\circ{\rm f})([G_{1},\omega_{1},{\rm F}_{1}])={\rm h}({\rm Rep}(G_{2}))=[(G_{2},\omega_{2},{\rm F}_{2})],

{\rm F}_{2}:\mathscr{D}\cong\mathscr{Z}({\rm Coh}(G_{2},\omega_{2}))

{\rm F}_{1}(\mathscr{O}(G_{2}))=\phi^{*}(\mathscr{O}(G_{2}))=\mathscr{O}(G_{1})

5.
The proof of Corollary
1.3

Fix a finite group scheme
G
over
k
. Set
\mathscr{E}:={\rm Rep}(G)
, and retain the notation of
2.9
.

By Theorem
1.1
, we have a surjective map of sets

\alpha:\mathcal{M}_{\rm ext}(\mathscr{E})\cong H^{3}(G,\mathbb{G}_{m}),\,\,\,(\mathscr{D},\iota)\mapsto\omega,

where
\omega\in H^{3}(G,\mathbb{G}_{m})
is such that
\mathscr{D}\cong\mathscr{Z}({\rm Coh}(G,\omega))
as braided tensor categories. Moreover, it is clear that
\alpha(\mathscr{D},\iota)=1
if and only if
\mathscr{D}\cong\mathscr{Z}({\rm Coh}(G))
as braided tensor categories. Thus, it remains to show that
\alpha
is a group homomorphism.

Set
A:=\mathscr{O}(G\times G)
and
B:=\mathscr{O}(G\times G/\Delta(G))
(see Lemma
2.4
). Then
B\subset A
are exact commutative algebras in the Tannakian category

, and we have to prove that

{\rm Mod}^{0}(B)_{\mathscr{Z}({\rm Coh}(G,\omega_{1}))\boxtimes\mathscr{Z}({\rm Coh}(G,\omega_{2}))}\cong\mathscr{Z}({\rm Coh}(G,\omega_{1}\omega_{2}))

as braided tensor categories. By (
2.8
), it suffices to prove that

{\rm Mod}^{0}(B)_{\mathscr{Z}({\rm Coh}(G\times G,\omega_{1}\times\omega_{2}))}\cong\mathscr{Z}({\rm Coh}(G,\omega_{1}\omega_{2}))

To this end, note that similarly to
[
DMNO
, Example 4.11(ii)]
, one shows that exact subalgebras

of
A
are in one to one correspondence with tensor subcategories of
\mathscr{Z}({\rm Coh}(G\times G,\omega_{1}\times\omega_{2}))
, such that
B
corresponds to the image of the functor

(5.1)

{\rm Mod}^{0}(B)_{\mathscr{Z}({\rm Coh}(G\times G,\omega_{1}\times\omega_{2}))}\xrightarrow{-\otimes_{B}A}{\rm Coh}(G\times G,\omega_{1}\times\omega_{2}).

Since the image of the functor (

) is

{\rm Coh}(\Delta(G),(\omega_{1}\times\omega_{2})_{\mid\Delta(G)})\cong{\rm Coh}(G,\omega_{1}\omega_{2}),
