Abstract
There is a long history of studying Ramsey theory using the algebraic structure of the Stone-Čech compactification of discrete semigroup. It has been shown that various Ramsey theoretic structures are contained in different algebraic large sets. In this article we will deduce the combinatorial characterization of certain sets, that are the member of the idempotent ultrafilters of the closed subsemigroup of β ​ S \beta S , arising from certain Ramsey family. In a special case when S = ℕ S=\mathbb{N} , we will deduce that sets which are the members of all idempotent ultrafilters of those semigroups contain certain additive-multiplicative structures. Later we will generalize this result for weak rings, where we will show a non-commutative version of the additive-multiplicative structure.

A brief review of Topological algebra:

topological center (meaning that for any

is continuous). This is a famous
Theorem due to Ellis that if

E\left(S\right)\neq\emptyset

of

if

,

is the left ideal that does not contain any proper left ideal. Similarly,
we can define

and

.

Any compact Hausdorff right topological semigroup

\begin{array}[]{ccc}K(T)&=&\bigcup\{L:L\text{ is a minimal left ideal of }T\}\\
&=&\,\,\,\,\,\bigcup\{R:R\text{ is a minimal right ideal of }T\}.\end{array}

Given a minimal left ideal

An idempotent is minimal with respect to this relation if and only

of

. Given

Proof.

Since
\langle x_{n}\rangle_{n=1}^{\infty}
is a
\mathcal{F}

(S,+)

p\in E\left(\beta\left(\mathcal{\mathcal{F}}\right)\right)

FS(\langle x_{n}\rangle_{n=m}^{\infty})\in p

m\in p

A

\mathcal{F}^{\star}
-set for every
s,t\in S

s^{-1}A,t^{-1}A,s^{-1}At^{-1}\in p
. Let
A^{\star}=\{s\in A:-s+A\in p\}
,
then
A^{\star}\in p
. We can choose
y_{1}\in A^{\star}\cap FS(\langle x_{n}\rangle_{n=1}^{\infty})

Inductively, let
m\in\mathbb{N}

\langle y_{i}\rangle_{i=1}^{m}

\langle H_{i}\rangle_{i=1}^{m}

\mathcal{P}_{f}(\mathbb{N})

1.

For

2.

\text{If}\,y_{i}={\displaystyle\sum_{t\in H_{i}}x_{t},\,\text{then}\,\sum_{t\in H_{i}}x_{t}\in A^{\star},\,\text{and}\,AP(\langle y_{i}\rangle_{i=1}^{m})\subseteq A^{\star}}.

We observe that
\{\,\sum_{t\in H}x_{t}:\,H\in\mathcal{P}_{f}(\mathbb{N}),\,\text{min}H>\text{max}H_{m}\}\in p
.
Let us set
B=\{\,\sum_{t\in H}x_{t}:\,H\in\mathcal{P}_{f}(\mathbb{N}),\,\text{min}H>\text{max}H_{m}\}

E_{1}=FS(\langle y_{t}\rangle_{n=1}^{m})
