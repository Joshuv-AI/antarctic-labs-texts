The Conformal Manifold of S-folds in String Theory
Nikolay Bobev, Friðrik Freyr Gautason, Jesse van Muiden, [5mm] KU Leuven, Instituut voor Theoretische FysicaCelestijnenlaan 200D, B-3001 Leuven, Belgium, [5mm] University of Iceland, Science InstituteDunhaga 3, 107 Reykjavík, Iceland, [5mm] SISSAVia Bonomea 265, I 34136 Trieste, Italy, [5mm] INFN - Sezione di TriesteVia Valerio 2, I-34127 Trieste, Italy

Abstract
We continue the holographic exploration of the conformal manifold of 3d 𝒩 = 2 \mathcal{N}=2 S-fold SCFTs constructed by gauging the flavor symmetry of the Gaiotto-Witten T ⁡ [ U ⁡ ( N ) ] T[U(N)] theory. We show how to uplift the two-parameter family of AdS 4 vacua dual to this conformal manifold to 10d backgrounds of type IIB supergravity. We use these uplifted solutions to shed new light on the mysterious nature of the infinite distance limit on the conformal manifold and to study probe strings and D3-branes. This analysis uncovers an intriguing structure of the S 3 S^{3} partition function of the S-fold SCFTs which resembles the giant graviton expansion of the superconformal index of 4d 𝒩 = 4 \mathcal{N}=4 SYM. We also show how to each member of the family of supersymmetric AdS 4 vacua one can associate a consistent truncation to 4d 𝒩 = 2 \mathcal{N}=2 gauged supergravity and use this result, in conjunction with holography, to calculate the large N N partition function of the 3d S-fold SCFT on compact Euclidean manifolds. Finally, we generalize the supersymmetric AdS 4 vacua to a four-parameter family of non-supersymmetric AdS 4 solutions.

The supergravity analysis above therefore suggests that at large

It will be very interesting to explore this question further and understand better the global properties of this conformal manifold of S-fold SCFTs.

As an additional check of the holographic duality in our setup we also explore supersymmetric line operators in the S-fold SCFTs. We first study probe

As expected from supersymmetric localization, our results do not depend on the exactly marginal couplings and are thus valid along the entire conformal manifold. Our analysis also reveals the existence of asymptotically AdS

Regardless of the existence of a properly defined S-duality twisted index we can adopt a string theory perspective to shed more light on the structure of the partition function in (

4  Holographic line operators

In this section we will use the new ten-dimensional background presented in Section
2
to compute the expectation values of line operators in the dual CFT. The CFT line operators we consider are dual to fundamental strings, D1-branes, or more general
(p,q)
-strings wrapping the equator of
S^{3}
and extending into the bulk geometry. We also study the vacuum expectation value of the Wilson loop using the results from supersymmetric localization in the dual 3d CFT and find good schematic agreement with the supergravity analysis.

4.1  Gravity

We consider the on-shell action of a
(p,q)
-string that wraps the equator of
S^{3}
on the boundary of Euclidean AdS
4
. The string extends into the bulk geometry in such a way that its worldsheet spans an
\text{AdS}_{2}
subspace of
\text{AdS}_{4}
. We assume that the string sits at a fixed location along the remaining directions of the 10d background. This is done in order to retain the isometries of AdS
2
as symmetries of the string. The classical action of the
(p,q)
-string takes the form

S_{(p,q)}=\frac{1}{2\pi\ell_{s}^{2}}\int\left[\mathrm{d}^{2}\sigma\sqrt{\mathbf{p}\cdot\mathcal{M}\cdot\mathbf{p}^{T}\,\det[G_{ab}]}+i\mathbf{p}\cdot A_{2}\right]\,,

(4.1)

where
\mathbf{p}=(p,q)
determines the charge of the string,
A_{2}=(C_{2},B_{2})
gives the coupling to the background type IIB two-forms, and the matrix
\mathcal{M}
encodes the axio-dilaton, see (
2.16
)

\mathcal{M}=\mathrm{e}^{\Phi}\left(\begin{array}[]{cc}\tau\bar{\tau}&C_{0}\\
C_{0}&1\end{array}\right)\,.

(4.2)

The fundamental string has charge
\mathbf{p}=(0,1)
, which corresponds to a Wilson line in the fundamental representation in the dual CFT, while any other charge corresponds to ’t Hooft or dyonic line operators in the QFT. Extremizing the on-shell action of the
(p,q)
-string leads to a number of different solutions. Notably, only one of them is independent of the supergravity parameter
Y
that is dual to the marginal coupling in the dual SCFT. Since the expectation value of a supersymmetric Wilson loop operators in 3d
\mathcal{N}=2
theories are independent of exactly marginal couplings we expect that the on-shell action of the supersymmetric string should be independent of
Y
. We therefore assume that the other extrema for the on-shell action of the probe string break supersymmetry and we do not consider them further. The extremum we are interested in satisfies

\cos\theta=1\,,\quad\cos 2\xi_{3}=0\,,\quad\mathrm{e}^{2\varphi-T}=-\frac{p+\mathrm{e}^{-T}q}{p+\mathrm{e}^{T}q}\,.

(4.3)

The on-shell action of such a
(p,q)
-string is easily computed after using the fact that the regularized volume of the unit-radius Euclidean AdS
2
is
-2\pi
. Using (
2.23
) and the holographic dictionary we find that the expectation value of the
(p,q)
line operator scales reads

\log{\cal W}_{(p,q)}=-S_{(p,q),{\rm on-shell}}^{1}=\sqrt{\frac{2\pi N}{\sinh T}(p^{2}+q^{2}-pq\cosh T)}\,.

(4.4)

In particular for the fundamental Wilson loop we find

\log{\cal W}=\sqrt{\frac{2\pi N}{\sinh T}}\,.

(4.5)

The results (
4.4
) and (
4.5
) should be viewed as predictions for the holographic 3d
\mathcal{N}=2
SCFT. Although we have not been able to independently and rigorously compute these line operator expectation values from the dual SCFT, we nevertheless were able to estimate the WL vev using the matrix model obtained by supersymmetric localization and the Wigner semi-circle law. We now proceed to briefly describe this calculation.

4.2  Field theory

To properly calculate the vev of a supersymmetric Wilson loop for the S-fold SCFT on
S^{3}
one needs to apply supersymmetric localization and derive the analog of the matrix model result in (
3.1
) for the
S^{3}
partition function of the SCFT. We will not pursue this calculation here but will rather draw on the results of
[
2
]
and the calculation of the WL vev in the ABJM theory studied in
[
48
]
to make an educated guess about the result.

We will assume that the Wilson loop that preserves the large amount of supersymmetry wraps the great circle on
S^{3}
. Following
[
48
]
we can write this WL vev as
9
9

9

In this section we temporarily absorb a factor of
i
in the definition of
\sigma^{2}
as compared to the matrix model in (
3.1
) in order to follow standard Gaussian matrix model conventions.

\langle W_{R}\rangle=\langle\text{Tr}_{R}\,\mathrm{e}^{2\pi\sigma}\rangle\,,

(4.6)

where
R
is the representation of the Wilson loop with respect to the gauge group and
\sigma
is same variable as in the matrix model in (
3.1
). We hasten to add that this expression is a guess and should be treated with care especially since the S-fold SCFT of interest is non-Lagrangian. We proceed further with a crude approximation, namely we assume that in the large
N
limit the eigenvalues
\sigma
lay on a Wigner semi-circle distribution specified by the eigenvalue density

\rho(\sigma)=\frac{2}{\pi\mu^{2}}\sqrt{\mu^{2}-\sigma^{2}}\,,\quad\text{where}\quad-\mu<\sigma<\mu\,,

(4.7)

where
\mu
is a positive real number and we have normalized the eigenvalue density such that

\int_{-\mu}^{\mu}\rho(\sigma)d\sigma=1\,.

(4.8)

We stress that this assumption for the eigenvalue density is non-trivial since the matrix model at hand is not exactly Gaussian. With these assumptions at hand we can determine the value
\mu
by computing the expectation value of
\sigma^{2}
at large

in two alternative ways. First, we can take two derivatives of the matrix model partition function in (

) with respect to the coupling. The role of coupling is played by the CS level

\langle\sum\limits_{j}\sigma_{j}^{2}\rangle=-\frac{1}{\pi}\frac{\partial}{\partial k}\log\mathcal{Z}_{N}[T]\approx\frac{N^{2}}{4\pi\sinh T}\,,

(4.9)

where in the second relation on the right hand side we have taken the large
N
limit. Alternatively we can first take the large
N

) to find

\langle\sigma^{2}\rangle=N\int\limits_{-\mu}^{\mu}\mathrm{d}\sigma\rho(\sigma)\sigma^{2}=\frac{\mu^{2}N}{4}\,,

(4.10)

where the additional factor of
N
arises from changing from a sum over eigenvalues to an integral. Comparing the two alternative approaches we conclude that

\mu=\sqrt{\frac{N}{\pi\sinh T}}\,.

(4.11)

To calculate the Wilson line vev in the fundamental representation we now simply have to evaluate the expectation value of
\mathrm{e}^{2\pi\sigma}
using the eigenvalue density
\rho(\sigma)
and the result for
\mu
above. The result is simple and reads

\langle W_{F}\rangle=\int\limits_{-\mu}^{\mu}\mathrm{d}\sigma\rho(\sigma)\mathrm{e}^{2\pi\sigma}=\frac{1}{2\mu}I_{1}(2\pi\mu)\,,

(4.12)

where

\lim\limits_{N\rightarrow\infty}\log\langle W_{F}\rangle\approx 2\pi\mu=\sqrt{\frac{4\pi N}{\sinh T}}\,.

(4.13)

Despite the guesses we made this schematic calculation leads to a result that is of the same form as the one obtained by the probe string calculation on the gravity side and differs only by a simple numerical factor, see (

). We consider this to be a good qualitative check of holography in this setup. It will certainly be very nice to put this calculation of the WL vev on a more solid footing by a careful application of supersymmetric localization in the S-fold SCFT.

5  4d
\mathcal{N}=2
supergravity and S-fold black holes

The goal of this section is to show that for each member of the family of supersymmetric AdS
4
vacua discussed in Section
2
and Appendix
B
there is a consistent truncation of the maximal 4d
\mathcal{N}=8

[\mathop{\rm SO}(6)\times\mathop{\rm SO}(1,1)]\ltimes\mathbf{R}^{12}
gauged supergravity theory to the minimal 4d
\mathcal{N}=2
gauged supergravity.

To restrict ourselves to the supersymmetric family of AdS
4
vacua we set

z=-1\,,\quad\chi_{2}=0\,.

(5.1)

The scalar manifold is then spanned by only two real scalar fields,
x

\chi_{1}
, that parametrize the coset element

\mathcal{V}=\,\mathrm{e}^{\chi_{1}\mathfrak{h}_{1}}\cdot\mathrm{e}^{-\log\sqrt{2}\mathfrak{t}_{1}}\cdot\mathrm{e}^{\frac{1}{2}\log\frac{3+x}{4}\mathfrak{t}_{2}}\cdot\mathrm{e}^{\frac{2}{x-3}\frac{\sqrt{1-x}}{\sqrt{3+x}}[\mathfrak{r}_{2},\mathfrak{r}_{3}]}\cdot\mathrm{e}^{-\mathfrak{r}_{2}}\cdot\mathrm{e}^{-\frac{\sqrt{1-x}}{\sqrt{3+x}}\mathfrak{r}_{3}}\,.

(5.2)

In addition to these two scalar fields the other bosonic fields in the truncation are the metric and two Abelian vector fields associated to the
\rm SO(6)

g_{1}
and
g_{2}
in (
B.8

x=\frac{8}{1+\cosh(2\text{Re}\,\zeta)}-3\,,\quad\text{and}\quad\chi_{1}=\text{Im}\,\zeta\,.

(5.3)

The truncated bosonic Lagrangian then reads

\mathcal{L}=R-h_{IJ}F^{I}_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu J}-(\partial_{\zeta}\partial_{\bar{\zeta}}\mathcal{K})\zeta^{\prime}\bar{\zeta}^{\prime}+6\,,

(5.4)

where the

y\mapsto yL_{\text{AdS}}
where
L_{\text{AdS}}=\sqrt{2}/g

\begin{split}\mathcal{K}&=|\zeta|^{2}-2\log\cosh\text{Re}\,\zeta\,,\quad h=\left(\begin{matrix}{}1/4&\\
&\rho(\zeta,\bar{\zeta})/2\end{matrix}\right)\,,\\
\rho(\zeta,\bar{\zeta})&=\frac{1+\cosh 2\text{Re}\,\zeta}{(1+\cosh 2\text{Re}\,\zeta)\cosh 2\text{Re}\,\zeta+8(\text{Im}\,\zeta)^{2}}\,.\end{split}

(5.5)

There are two important features of the Lagrangian in (
5.6

F^{2}_{\mu\nu}=0
and the scalars

\chi_{1}
to an allowed constant value that corresponds to any of the two-parameter family of supersymmetric AdS
4
vacua. Therefore, we conclude that for any value of
\zeta

S=-\frac{L^{2}_{\text{AdS}}}{16\pi G_{N}}\int\star_{4}\Big(R-\frac{1}{4}F_{\mu\nu}^{1}F^{1\mu\nu}+6\Big)\,.

(5.6)

This is simply the action of the 4d Einstein-Maxwell theory with a negative cosmological constant which in turn is the bosonic sector of 4d
\mathcal{N}=2
minimal gauged supergravity. The prefactor
L^{2}_{\text{AdS}}
appears because of the coordinate rescaling performed above. The existence of this consistent supergravity truncation is compatible with the general expectation, see
[
50

51
]
and references thereof, that every supersymmetric
\mathcal{N}=2

4

\mathcal{N}=2
minimal gauged supergravity which describes holographically the universal dynamics of the energy-momentum tensor in the dual 3d
\mathcal{N}=2
SCFT. The novel feature of the construction above is that since we have a two-parameter family of AdS
4
supersymmetric vacua we are led to a two-parameter space of consistent truncations to 4d
\mathcal{N}=2
minimal gauged supergravity.

We can exploit the existence of this 4d
\mathcal{N}=2
minimal gauged supergravity truncation in the context of holography and learn about some of the properties of the dual 3d
\mathcal{N}=2
family of S-fold SCFTs. In particular, we find that the free energy of the SCFT placed on a compact smooth Euclidean 3-manifold
\mathcal{M}_{3}

F_{\mathcal{M}_{3}}=S_{\text{on-shell}}\equiv\frac{\pi L^{2}_{\text{AdS}}}{2G_{N}}\mathcal{F}\,.

(5.7)

Here
\mathcal{F}
is determined by the regularized on-shell action of an appropriate Euclidean gravitational solution of the equations of motion obtained from (

\mathcal{M}_{3}
with an accompanying Maxwell field.
Using the same arguments as in
[

\pi L^{2}_{\text{AdS}}/2G_{N}=N^{2}T/2

Using this, we find the following prediction for the dual SCFT free energy on Euclidean 3-manifolds

F_{\mathcal{M}_{3}}=\frac{N^{2}T}{2}\mathcal{F}\,.

(5.8)

There are a number of important Euclidean solutions of the 4d

•

\mathcal{M}_{3}

(5.10)

This result for the index, after an appropriate choice of ensemble and a Legendre transformation, can also be used to obtain the Bekenstein-Hawking entropy of the supersymmetric Kerr-Newman black hole.
