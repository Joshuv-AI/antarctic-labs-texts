Circular flow number of Goldberg snarks
Robert Lukoťka

Abstract
A circular nowhere-zero r r -flow on a bridgeless graph G G is an orientation of the edges and an assignment of real values from [ 1 , r − 1 ] [1,r-1] to the edges in such a way that the sum of incoming values equals the sum of outgoing values for every vertex. The circular flow number of G G is the infimum over all values r r such that G G admits a nowhere-zero r r -flow. We prove that the circular glow number of Goldberg snark G 2 ​ k + 1 G_{2k+1} is 4 + 1 / ( k + 1 ) 4+1/(k+1) , proving a conjecture of Goedgebeur, Mattiolo, and Mazzuoccolo. Keywords. ciruclar flow number, snarks.

We use balanced valuations introduced by Jaeger
[

Let

denote the number of edges with exactly one end in

Let

A

is a mapping

such that

•

b(S)\leq\partial(S)
for each

and

•

b(v)\equiv\partial(v)(\bmod\ 2)
for each

,

where
b(S)
denotes the sum of
b(v)
for all

. To avoid zeroes in denominators we will mostly consider only subsets

with
\partial(S)\neq 0

S
is such subset we use

. Note that for sets

with
\partial(S)=0
the definition of balanced valuation implies

.
A balanced valuation is

if for each
S\sqsubset G
, we have
b(S)<\partial(S)
. Note that if
G
has an orientable balanced valuation, then

is bridgeless.

Given a circular nowhere-zero
r
-flow on
G
, we can create an orientable balanced valuation

on

by taking

to be equal to the number of incoming minus number of outgoing edges of

As the maximal flow value is

one can bound the ratio of incoming and outgoing edges to
S
and which in turn can be expressed using

and
\partial(S)
. The resulting condition is not only necessary, but also sufficient.

.

\displaystyle(\partial(S)+b(S))/(\partial(S)-b(S))\leq r-1.

\displaystyle\frac{\partial(S)+b(S)}{\partial(S)-b(S)}+1=\frac{\partial(A)+\partial(B)+b(A)+b(B)}{\partial(A)+\partial(B)-b(A)-b(B)}+1
