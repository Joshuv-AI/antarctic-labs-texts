Nonlinear sequential fractional boundary value problems involving generalized ψ \psi -Caputo fractional derivatives
Nguyen Minh Dien

Abstract
This paper is devoted to study the nonlinear sequential fractional boundary value problems involving generalized ψ \psi -Caputo fractional derivatives with nonlocal boundary conditions. We investigate the Green function and some of its properties, from which we obtain a new Lyapunov-type inequality for our problem. A lower bound for the possible eigenvalues of our problem is derived. Furthermore, we apply some properties of the Green function to obtain some existence results for our problem. It is worth mentioning that our results still work with some source functions including singularities.

\displaystyle x(t)=c_{2}+\left(I_{a+}^{\beta,\psi}c_{1}\right)(t)-I_{a+}^{\alpha+\beta,\psi}f(t,x(t))=c_{2}+\frac{c_{1}}{\Gamma(\beta+1)}(\psi(t)-\psi(a))^{\beta}+I_{a+}^{\alpha+\beta,\psi}f(t,x(t)).

4
Lyapunov-type inequality and existence results

In this section, we investigate a new Lyapunov-type inequality and obtain a lower bound for the possible eigenvalues of our problem. We also present some existence results for our problem.

To obtain Lyapunov-type inquality, the following assumptions will be posed.

•

Assumption
(\mathcal{A}1)
:

There exist a positive function
q:(a,b)\to\mathbb{R}_{+}

and a positive, non-decreasing and concave function

such that

|f(t,x)|\leq q(t)|\phi(x)|

for any
t\in(a,b)
and
x\in\mathbb{R}
.

•

Assumption
(\mathcal{A}2)
:

There exists
\kappa\in(0,1)
such that

|\mathcal{G}(x)|\leq\kappa|x|

for any
x\in\mathbb{R}
.

In this section, we also use the concept of mild solutions as follows.

Definition 4.1

The function

3.1

1.1
) and (
1.2

Let

If

and the problem (
1.1
)-(
1.2
) has a nontrivial mild solution,
then

\int_{a}^{b}\psi^{\prime}(\tau)q(\tau)\hskip 2.84544pt\mathrm{d}\tau\geq\frac{1-\kappa}{G_{\max}}\frac{\|x\|}{\phi(\|x\|)},

where

\displaystyle G_{\max}=\frac{1}{\Gamma(\alpha+\beta)}(\psi(b)-\psi(a))^{\alpha+\beta-1}\max\left\{\beta^{\beta}(\alpha+\beta-1)^{\alpha+\beta-1},\frac{\beta(\alpha+\beta-1)^{\frac{\alpha+\beta-1}{\beta}}}{(\alpha+2\beta-1)^{\frac{\alpha+2\beta-1}{\beta}}}\right\}.

Remark 4.3
.

Lyapunov-type inequality obtained in Theorem
4.2

seems to be new, which is still not proposed in previous papers.

Proof
.

Using the fact that
\frac{\psi(t)-\psi(a)}{\psi(b)-\psi(a)}\leq 1
for all
t\in[a,b]
,
Lemma
2.4
and Lemma
3.3
, we obtain from (

) that

\displaystyle|x(t)|

\displaystyle\leq|\mathcal{G}(x)|+G_{\max}\int_{a}^{b}\psi^{\prime}(\tau)|f(\tau,x(\tau))|\hskip 2.84544pt\mathrm{d}\tau

\displaystyle\leq\kappa{\left|\mskip-1.80002mu\left|x\right|\mskip-1.80002mu\right|}+G_{\max}\int_{a}^{b}\psi^{\prime}(\tau)q(\tau)|\phi(x(\tau))|\hskip 2.84544pt\mathrm{d}\tau

\displaystyle\leq\kappa{\left|\mskip-1.80002mu\left|x\right|\mskip-1.80002mu\right|}+G_{\max}{\left|\mskip-1.80002mu\left|\psi^{\prime}(\cdot)q(\cdot)\right|\mskip-1.80002mu\right|}_{L^{1}(a,b)}\phi\left(\int_{a}^{b}\frac{|\psi^{\prime}(\tau)q(\tau)|}{{\left|\mskip-1.80002mu\left|\psi^{\prime}(\cdot)q(\cdot)\right|\mskip-1.80002mu\right|}_{L^{1}(a,b)}}|x(\tau)|\hskip 2.84544pt\mathrm{d}\tau\right)

\displaystyle\leq\kappa{\left|\mskip-1.80002mu\left|x\right|\mskip-1.80002mu\right|}+G_{\max}{\left|\mskip-1.80002mu\left|\psi^{\prime}(\cdot)q(\cdot)\right|\mskip-1.80002mu\right|}_{L^{1}(a,b)}\phi({\left|\mskip-1.80002mu\left|x\right|\mskip-1.80002mu\right|}),

where

\displaystyle G_{\max}=\frac{1}{\Gamma(\alpha+\beta)}(\psi(b)-\psi(a))^{\alpha+\beta-1}\max\left\{\beta^{\beta}(\alpha+\beta-1)^{\alpha+\beta-1},\frac{\beta(\alpha+\beta-1)^{\frac{\alpha+\beta-1}{\beta}}}{(\alpha+2\beta-1)^{\frac{\alpha+2\beta-1}{\beta}}}\right\}.

From the latter inequality, we obtain

\int_{a}^{b}\psi^{\prime}(\tau)q(\tau)\hskip 2.84544pt\mathrm{d}\tau\geq\frac{1-\kappa}{G_{\max}}\frac{\|x\|}{\phi(\|x\|)}.

This completes the proof of Theorem.

In the following theorem, we introduce an existence result for our problem.

Theorem 4.7
.

Let
0<\alpha,\beta\leq 1
with
\alpha+\beta>1
, and let
\psi\in C^{1}_{+}[a,b]
.
Suppose that Assumptions
(\mathcal{A}2)
and
(\mathcal{A}3)
hold.
Suppose further that
\mathcal{G}\in C(\mathbb{R},\mathbb{R})
and
\psi^{\prime}(\cdot)\kappa_{i}(\cdot)\in L^{1}(a,b)

If there exists
M>0
such that

M>\frac{1}{1-\kappa}\Psi(M)G_{\max}{\left|\mskip-1.80002mu\left|\psi^{\prime}(\cdot)\kappa_{1}(\cdot)\right|\mskip-1.80002mu\right|}_{L^{1}(a,b)}

with

, then
the problem (
1.1
) and (
1.2
) has at least one mild solution.

Proof
.

Let us define the operator

by

Sx(t)=\left(\frac{\psi(t)-\psi(a)}{\psi(b)-\psi(a)}\right)^{\beta}\mathcal{G}(x)+\int_{a}^{b}G(\tau,t)\psi^{\prime}(\tau)f(\tau,x(\tau))\hskip 2.84544pt\mathrm{d}\tau,

(4.1)

where

defined in Lemma
3.1
.
We also denote

B_{r}=\{x\in C[a,b]:{\left|\mskip-1.80002mu\left|x\right|\mskip-1.80002mu\right|}\leq r\}\ \ \text{for some}\ \ r>0.

We firstly show that
S
is completely continuous operator.
We divide the process of proof into three steps.

Step 1.

We verify that
S
is continuous operator.
Assume that
x,y\in B_{r}
for some
r>0
,
we obtain from Proposition
3.3
and Assumption

that

\displaystyle{\left|\mskip-1.80002mu\left|Sx-Sy\right|\mskip-1.80002mu\right|}

\displaystyle\leq\sup_{a\leq t\leq b}\left|\left(\frac{\psi(t)-\psi(a)}{\psi(b)-\psi(a)}\right)^{\beta}(\mathcal{G}(x)-\mathcal{G}(y))\right|+\sup_{a\leq t\leq b}\left|\int_{a}^{b}G(\tau,t)\psi^{\prime}(\tau)(f(\tau,x(\tau))-f(\tau,y(\tau)))\hskip 2.84544pt\mathrm{d}\tau\right|

\displaystyle\leq|\mathcal{G}(x)-\mathcal{G}(y)|+G_{\max}\int_{a}^{b}\psi^{\prime}(\tau)\kappa_{2}(\tau){\left|\Phi(x(\tau),y(\tau))\right|}\hskip 2.84544pt\mathrm{d}\tau

\displaystyle\leq|\mathcal{G}(x)-\mathcal{G}(y)|+G_{\max}{\left|\mskip-1.80002mu\left|\Phi(x(\cdot),y(\cdot))\right|\mskip-1.80002mu\right|}{\left|\mskip-1.80002mu\left|\psi^{\prime}(\cdot)\kappa_{2}(\cdot)\right|\mskip-1.80002mu\right|}_{L^{1}(a,b)}

\displaystyle\to 0\ \ \text{as}\ \ x\to y

due to

and

{\left|\mskip-1.80002mu\left|\Phi(x(\cdot),y(\cdot))\right|\mskip-1.80002mu\right|}\to 0
as
x\to y

Step 2.

We prove that

.
Suppose that
x\in B_{r}

3.3
and Assumption
(\mathcal{A}3)

one has

\displaystyle{\left|\mskip-1.80002mu\left|Sx\right|\mskip-1.80002mu\right|}

\displaystyle\leq\sup_{a\leq t\leq b}\left|\left(\frac{\psi(t)-\psi(a)}{\psi(b)-\psi(a)}\right)^{\beta}\mathcal{G}(x)+\int_{a}^{b}G(\tau,t)\psi^{\prime}(\tau)f(\tau,x(\tau))\hskip 2.84544pt\mathrm{d}\tau\right|

\displaystyle\leq\kappa r+\Psi(r){\left|\mskip-1.80002mu\left|\psi^{\prime}(\cdot)\kappa_{1}(\cdot)\right|\mskip-1.80002mu\right|}_{L^{1}(a,b)}.

The proof of

Step 3.

We verify that

maps bound sets into equicontinuous sets of

for some

For
