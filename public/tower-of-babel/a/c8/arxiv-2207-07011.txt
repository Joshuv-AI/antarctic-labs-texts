Abstract
. In this paper, we discuss a class of nonlinear Schrödinger equations with the power-type nonlinearity: ( i ​ ∂ ∂ t + Δ ) ​ ψ = λ ​ | ψ | 2 ​ η ​ ψ (\mathrm{i}\frac{\partial}{\partial t}+\Delta)\psi=\lambda|\psi|^{2\eta}\psi in 𝐑 N × 𝐑 + \mathbf{R}^{N}\times\mathbf{R}^{+} . Based on the Gagliardo-Nirenberg interpolation inequality, we prove the local existence and long-time behavior (continuation, finite-time blow-up or global existence, continuous dependence) of the solutions to the ( H q ) (H^{q}) super-critical Schrödinger equation. The corresponding scaling invariant space is homogeneous Sobolev H ˙ q c ​ r ​ i ​ t \dot{H}^{q_{crit}} with q c ​ r ​ i ​ t > q q_{crit}>q . Based on the estimates of the quadratic terms containing the phase derivatives used in the paper by Killip, Murphy and Visan [ 24 , SIAM J. Math. Anal. 50(3) (2018), 2681–2739] we shall study the stability with a stronger bound on the solutions to our problem. Moreover, from the arguments on virial-types presented in the paper by Killip and Visan [ 22 , Amer. J. Math. 132(2) (2010), 361–424] , a modified pseudo-conformal conservation law is proposed. The Morawetz estimate for the solutions to the problem are also presented. Mathematics Subject Classification 2000: 35B40, 35L65, 35Q55. \displaystyle\|f\|_{L^{m}(I;X)}=\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\left(\displaystyle\int_{I}\left\|f(t)\right\|^{m}_{X}dt\right)^{\frac{1}{m}}<\infty,\quad\mbox{for}~1\leq m<\infty,\\

\displaystyle\|f\|_{C^{k}(\bar{I};X)}=\sum_{j=0}^{k}\sup_{t\in\bar{I}}\left\|f^{(j)}(t)\right\|_{X}<\infty,

\displaystyle\left\|f\right\|_{W^{q,p}}=\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\displaystyle\left(\sum_{|\beta|\leq q}\int_{\mathbf{R}^{N}}|\mathscr{D}^{\beta}(f)|^{p}dx\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}<\infty,\quad\mbox{for}~1\leq p<\infty,\\

, the space

If

, the inner product and norm of

are defined by

is the conjugate term of

Next, we state a version of the Sobolev embedding by the following lemma.

Lemma 2.6

(Sobolev embedding)
.

If
N<2q<\infty
, then
H^{q}(\mathbf{R}^{N})\hookrightarrow C_{0}(\mathbf{R}^{N})
and there exists a constant positive
C^{\mathrm{SE}}=C(N,q)
such that

\left\|f\right\|_{L^{\infty}(\mathbf{R}^{N})}\leq C^{\mathrm{SE}}\left\|f\right\|_{H^{q}(\mathbf{R}^{N})}.

Proof.
For
N<2q
, suppose that

(Schwartz space) we have

\displaystyle\left\|f\right\|_{L^{\infty}(\mathbf{R}^{N})}

\displaystyle=\sup_{x\in\mathbf{R}^{N}}\left|\int_{\mathbf{R}^{N}}\widehat{f}(\xi)e^{\mathrm{i}\xi x}d\xi\right|\leq\int_{\mathbf{R}^{N}}\left|\widehat{f}(\xi)\right|d\xi=\int_{\mathbf{R}^{N}}\frac{1}{\left<\xi\right>^{q}}\left<\xi\right>^{q}\left|\widehat{f}(\xi)\right|d\xi,\quad\mbox{for}~\left<\xi\right>=(1+|\xi|^{2})^{\frac{1}{2}}.

Using the Hölder inequality we conclude that

(19)

\displaystyle\left\|f\right\|_{L^{\infty}(\mathbf{R}^{N})}

\displaystyle\leq\left(\int_{\mathbf{R}^{N}}\frac{1}{\left<\xi\right>^{2q}}d\xi\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}\left(\int_{\mathbf{R}^{N}}\left<\xi\right>^{2q}\left|\widehat{f}(\xi)\right|^{2}d\xi\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}.

Since

, this implies that
f\in H^{q}(\mathbf{R}^{N})
and

is a Schwartz function, then

. Moreover, for

, the first integral in (

\displaystyle\left\|f\right\|_{L^{\infty}(\mathbf{R}^{N})}\leq C^{\mathrm{SE}}\left(\int_{\mathbf{R}^{N}}\left<\xi\right>^{2q}\left|\widehat{f}(\xi)\right|^{2}d\xi\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}\lesssim\left\|f\right\|_{H^{q}(\mathbf{R}^{N})},

where
C=C^{\mathrm{SE}}=\left(\displaystyle\int_{\mathbf{R}^{N}}\frac{1}{\left<\xi\right>^{2q}}d\xi\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}>0
, for

.
This concludes the proof.
