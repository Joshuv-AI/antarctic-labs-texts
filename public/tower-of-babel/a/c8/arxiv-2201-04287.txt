Optimal embedding of hypercube into cylinder
Zhiyi Tang

Abstract
. We study the problem of Embedding Wirelength of n n -dimensional Hypercube Q n Q_{n} into Cylinder C 2 n 1 × P 2 n 2 C_{2^{n_{1}}}\times P_{2^{n_{2}}} , where n 1 + n 2 = n n_{1}+n_{2}=n , called EWHC. We show that such wirelength corresponding to Gray code embedding is 2 n 2 ​ ( 3 ⋅ 2 2 ​ n 1 − 3 − 2 n 1 − 1 ) + 2 n 1 ​ ( 2 2 ​ n 2 − 1 − 2 n 2 − 1 ) 2^{n_{2}}(3\cdot 2^{2n_{1}-3}-2^{n_{1}-1})+2^{n_{1}}(2^{2n_{2}-1}-2^{n_{2}-1}) . In addition, we prove that Gray code embedding is an optimal strategy of EWHC. Key words: hypercube; cylinder; optimal embedding.

2.
Preliminaries

Computations of the embedding wirelength between different graphs have been widely analyzed. In this section, we list two major approaches: edge congestion problem(ECP) and edge isoperimetric problem(EIP), and focus on EIP for hypercubes later.

2.1.
Edge congestion problem

ECP is one of the most popular tools studied in the computation of wirelength of different embeddings. With ECP, one could treat the wirelength of an embedding as a problem of some congestion summation through all edges of
H
(such as
[
2
,
6
,
8
,
10
]
).
Here we only state some necessary definitions and notations, more results about ECP are referred to
[
2
,
8
]
.

Definition 2.1

(Def.2 of
[
13
]
)
.

The
edge congestion
of an edge
e^{\prime}\in E(H)
when using an embedding
f
, denoted by
EC_{f}(e^{\prime})
, is defined as the number of
e=\{u,v\}\in E(G)
such that
e^{\prime}
is incident on
P_{f}(u,v)
. In other words,

EC_{f}(e^{\prime})=\#\{\{u,v\}\in E(G):e^{\prime}\in P_{f}(u,v)\}.

This can be extended any edge subset
Y\subset E(H)
, define

EC_{f}(Y)=\sum_{e^{\prime}\in Y}EC_{f}(e^{\prime}).

For an embedding
f
, we define an index function
\delta(e^{\prime},P_{f}(u,v))
to be one if
e^{\prime}\in P_{f}(u,v)
and zero otherwise for each
\{u,v\}\in E(G),\ e^{\prime}\in E(H)
. Then

EC_{f}(e^{\prime})
,
d_{H}(f(u),f(v))
are equivalent to
\sum_{\{u,v\}\in E(G)}\delta(e^{\prime},P_{f}(u,v))
,

\sum_{e^{\prime}\in E(H)}\delta(e^{\prime},P_{f}(u,v))
, respectively.
Consequently, the embedding wirelength of
G
into
H
is equivalent to
\sum_{e^{\prime}\in E(H)}EC_{f}(e^{\prime})
. In other words,

WL(G,H;f)=\sum_{e^{\prime}\in E(H)}EC_{f}(e^{\prime}).

Clearly, take any partition
(Y_{i})_{i=1}^{m}
of
E(H)
, then

WL(G,H;f)=\sum_{i=1}^{m}EC_{f}(Y_{i}).

In particular,
G
is a regular graph.
Based on Congestion Lemma of
[
8
]
, for each
1\leq i\leq m
, let
Y_{i}
be an edge cut of
H
such that the removal of edges of
Y_{i}
leaves
H
into 2 components. One induced vertex set is denoted by
\beta_{i}
, and let
\alpha_{i}=f^{-1}(\beta_{i})
, edge set
R_{i}=\{\{u,v\}\in E(G):u\in\alpha_{i},v\notin\alpha_{i}\}
.
Suppose
Y_{i}
satisfies the followsing conditions:

(A1)
.For each
\{u,v\}\in R_{i}
,
P_{f}(u,v)
has exactly one edge in
Y_{i}
;

(A2)
.For each
\{u,v\}\in E(G)\backslash R_{i}
,
P_{f}(u,v)
has no edge in
Y_{i}
.

Then

WL(G,H;f)=\sum_{i=1}^{m}|R_{i}|.

Since conditions (A1)(A2) imply that

\sum_{e^{\prime}\in Y_{i}}\delta(e^{\prime},P_{f}(u,v))
is one if
\{u,v\}\in R_{i}
and zero otherwise for each
\{u,v\}\in E(G)
.
What’s more, for each
1\leq i\leq m
,

EC_{f}(Y_{i})=\sum_{e^{\prime}\in Y_{i}}\sum_{\{u,v\}\in E(G)}\delta(e^{\prime},P_{f}(u,v))=\sum_{\{u,v\}\in R_{i}}\sum_{e^{\prime}\in Y_{i}}\delta(e^{\prime},P_{f}(u,v))=|R_{i}|.

It is worth mentioning that
[
10
]
studied EWHC of the case
n_{1}=2
by introducing some partition of edge set
E(C_{2^{2}}\times P_{2^{n_{2}}})
, then minimized each item with the help of ECP.

[
6
]
extended to the case
n_{1}=3
with ECP.
But for

, no one find an appropriate partition to minimize each item with ECP until now. The purpose of the paper is aim to present an alternative partition of

, then to apply EIP to solve EWHC for more general cases.

Harper(

Lemma 2.3

(
[

]
)

For

Definition 2.4

(
[

,
7
]
)
.

Take
S\subset V(Q_{n})

|S|=k

If

-subcubes,

-subcube lies in a neighbor of every

-subcube for

Letting

if

Consequently,

(3)

As a corollary, we have

\theta(n,k)=\theta(n-1,k)+k,\quad 0\leq k\leq 2^{n-1},

\theta(n+1,2k)=2\theta(n,k),\quad 0\leq k\leq 2^{n}.

Next, we introduce another powful indicator,

Definition 2.5

(
[
3

7
]
)

Given any

What’s more, for
