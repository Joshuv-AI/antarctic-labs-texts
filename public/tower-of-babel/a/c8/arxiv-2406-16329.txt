A model structure and Hopf-cyclic theory on the category of coequivariant modules over a comodule algebra
Mariko Ohara

Abstract
. Let H H be a coFrobenius Hopf algebra over a field k k . Let A A be a right H H -comodule algebra over k k . We recall that the category ℳ H \mathcal{M}^{H} of right H H -comodules admits a certain model structure whose homotopy category is equivalent to the stable category of right H H -comodules given in [ 5 ] . In the first part of this paper, we show that the category LMod A ​ ( ℳ H ) \mathrm{LMod}_{A}(\mathcal{M}^{H}) of left A A -module objects in ℳ H \mathcal{M}^{H} admits a model structure, which becomes a model subcategory of the category of A ​ # ​ H ∗ A\#H^{\ast} -modules endowed with a model structure given in [ 14 ] if H H is finite dimensional with a certain assumption. Note that LMod A ​ ( ℳ H ) \mathrm{LMod}_{A}(\mathcal{M}^{H}) is not a Frobenius category in general. We also construct a functorial cofibrant replacement by proceeding the similar argument as in [ 16 ] . Hopf-cyclic theory is refered as a theory of cyclic homology of (co)module (co)algebra over a Hopf algebra H H whose coefficients in Hopf H H -modules. In the latter half of this paper, we see that cyclic H H -comodules which give Hopf-cyclic (co)homology with coefficients in Hopf H H -modules are contructible in the homotopy category of right H H -comodules, and we investigate a Hopf-cyclic (co)homology in slightly modified setting by assuming A A a right H H -comodule k k -Hopf algebra with H H -colinear bijective antipode in stable category of right H H -comodules and give an analogue of the characteristic map.
