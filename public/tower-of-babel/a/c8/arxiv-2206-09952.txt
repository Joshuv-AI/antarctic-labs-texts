In this paper, we investigate QCD-like running coupling α s A ​ d ​ S ​ ( Q 2 ) \alpha_{s}^{AdS}(Q^{2}) and its associated β \beta -function β ⁡ ( Q 2 ) \beta(Q^{2}) in the spirit of tachyonic AdS/QCD. The function presents different properties of α s A ​ d ​ S ​ ( Q 2 ) \alpha_{s}^{AdS}(Q^{2}) at small and large values of the fifth-dimensional holographic variable z z .
