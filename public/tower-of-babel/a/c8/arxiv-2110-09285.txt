Nonstandard proof of combined sum and product structure in IP ∗ sets Keywords: IP ∗ sets, Nonstandard analysis
Sayan Goswami

Abstract
V. Bergelson and N. Hindman proved that I ​ P ∗ IP^{*} sets contain all possible finite sum and product of a sum subsystem of any sequence in ℕ \mathbb{N} . In this article, we will prove this result using Nonstandard analysis.

Ramsey theoretic study is deeply involved with the study of topological

A subset of

In

all possible

and
y_{n}=\sum_{t\in H_{n}}x_{t}
for each

. In

[

Theorem 1
.

FS\left(\langle y_{n}\rangle_{n=1}^{\infty}\right)\cup FP\left(\langle y_{n}\rangle_{n=1}^{\infty}\right)\subseteq A.
