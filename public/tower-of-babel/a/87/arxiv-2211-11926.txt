The weak Galerkin finite element method for Stokes interface problems with curved interface
Lin Yang, Qilong Zhai, Ran Zhang

Abstract
In this paper, we develop a weak Galerkin (WG) finite element scheme for the Stokes interface problems with curved interface. The conventional numerical schemes rely on the use of straight segments to approximate the curved interface and the accuracy is limited by geometric errors. Hence in our method, we directly construct the weak Galerkin finite element space on the curved cells to avoid geometric errors. For the integral calculation on curved cells, we employ non-affine transformations to map curved cells onto the reference element. The optimal error estimates are obtained in both the energy norm and the L 2 L^{2} norm. A series of numerical experiments are provided to validate the efficiency of the proposed WG method.

In Section 4 and 5, the error estimates in the energy norm and the

\displaystyle(\nabla_{w}\cdot(Q_{h}{\bf u}),\varphi)_{T}=(\mathcal{Q}_{h}(\nabla\cdot{\bf u}),\varphi)_{T}+\langle Q_{b}{\bf u}-{\bf u},\varphi{\bf n}\rangle_{\partial T\cap\Gamma}.

(3.4)

Proof.

For
T\in\mathcal{T}_{h}^{S}
and
\varphi\in P_{k-1}(T)
, according to Eq.(
2.3
), definitions of
Q_{0}
,
Q_{b}
and
\mathcal{Q}_{h}
and integration by parts , we get

\displaystyle(\nabla_{w}\cdot(Q_{h}{\bf u}),\varphi)_{T}=

\displaystyle-(Q_{0}{\bf u},\nabla\varphi)_{T}+\langle Q_{b}{\bf u},\varphi{\bf n}\rangle_{\partial T}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-({\bf u},\nabla\varphi)_{T}+\langle{\bf u},\varphi{\bf n}\rangle_{\partial T}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(\nabla\cdot{\bf u},\varphi)_{T}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(\mathcal{Q}_{h}(\nabla\cdot{\bf u}),\varphi)_{T}.

Similarly, for
T\in\mathcal{T}_{h}^{I}
and
\varphi\in P_{k-1}(T)
, using Eq.(
2.3
) to derive

\displaystyle(\nabla_{w}\cdot(Q_{h}{\bf u}),\varphi)_{T}=

\displaystyle-(Q_{0}{\bf u},\nabla\varphi)_{T}+\langle Q_{b}{\bf u},\varphi{\bf n}\rangle_{\partial T}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-({\bf u},\nabla\varphi)_{T}+\langle{\bf u},\varphi{\bf n}\rangle_{\partial T}+\langle Q_{b}{\bf u}-{\bf u},\varphi{\bf n}\rangle_{\partial T\cap\Gamma}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(\nabla\cdot{\bf u},\varphi)_{T}+\langle Q_{b}{\bf u}-{\bf u},\varphi{\bf n}\rangle_{\partial T\cap\Gamma}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(\mathcal{Q}_{h}(\nabla\cdot{\bf u}),\varphi)_{T}+\langle Q_{b}{\bf u}-{\bf u},\varphi{\bf n}\rangle_{\partial T\cap\Gamma}.

The proof of the above lemma is complete.
∎

Now, we give some important inequalities for the proof. The trace inequality and the inverse inequality are essential technique tools for the analysis. For the straight triangular cells, these inequalities have been proved in
[
33
]
. Next we extend two inequalities to the curved cells.

Lemma 3.3
.

(Inverse Inequality)
For all
T\in\mathcal{T}_{h}^{I}
,
\varphi
is the piecewise polynomial on
T
, then we have

\displaystyle\|\nabla\varphi\|_{T}\leqslant Ch_{T}^{-1}\|\varphi\|_{T}.

(3.5)

Proof.

For any
T\in\mathcal{T}_{h}^{I}
, assume that
S(T)
is the circumscribed simplex satisfying the shape regularity conditions. According to the standard inverse inequality
[
33
]
on
S(T)
, we have

\displaystyle\|\nabla\varphi\|_{T}\leqslant\|\nabla\varphi\|_{S(T)}\leqslant Ch_{S(T)}^{-1}\|\varphi\|_{S(T)}.

(3.6)

Next, consider a ball, denoted as
S
, which is contained entirely within
T
and has a diameter that is proportional to
h_{T}
. Then by the domain inverse inequality
[
33
]
, we get

\displaystyle\|\varphi\|_{S(T)}\leqslant\|\varphi\|_{S}\leqslant\|\varphi\|_{T}.

(3.7)

Substituting inequality (
3.7
) into the inequality (
3.6
) leads to

\displaystyle\|\nabla\varphi\|_{T}\leqslant Ch_{T}^{-1}\|\varphi\|_{T}.

The proof of the inverse inequality on the curved cells is complete.
∎

Lemma 3.4
.

(Trace Inequality)
For any
T\in\mathcal{T}_{h}^{I}
and
\varphi\in H^{1}(T)
, we have

\displaystyle\|\varphi\|_{e}^{2}\leqslant C\left(h_{T}^{-1}\|\varphi\|_{T}^{2}+h_{T}\|\nabla\varphi\|_{T}^{2}\right),~~e\subset\partial T.

(3.8)

Proof.

For simplify, we consider curved triangular cells. Similarly, the trace inequality holds true for polygons. For
T\in\mathcal{T}_{h}^{I}
, assume that the non-affine transformation between
T
and the reference element
\hat{T}
is as follows

\displaystyle\left\{\begin{array}[]{rl}x=\tilde{x}(\xi,\eta),&(\xi,\eta)\in\hat{T}\\
y=\tilde{y}(\xi,\eta),&(\xi,\eta)\in\hat{T}\end{array}\right.,

and denote that
\tilde{\varphi}(\xi,\eta)=\varphi(\tilde{x}(\xi,\eta),\tilde{y}(\xi,\eta))
.
\tilde{J}
is the corresponding Jacobian of the above mapping. Suppose that the edge
e
possesses parametric representations given by
x=x(t)
and
y=y(t)
,
0\leqslant t\leqslant 1
. According to the regularity conditions and Theorem 1 in
[
40
]
, every term of Eq.(
3.8
) has the following estimate.

\displaystyle\begin{split}\|\varphi\|_{e}^{2}=&\int_{e}\varphi^{2}(x,y)ds\\
=&\int_{0}^{1}\varphi^{2}(x(t),y(t))|e|dt\\
=&\int_{0}^{1}\tilde{\varphi}^{2}(\xi(t),\eta(t))|e|dt\\
\leqslant&Ch_{T}\|\tilde{\varphi}\|_{\hat{e}}^{2},\end{split}

and

\displaystyle\begin{split}\|\varphi\|_{T}^{2}=&\int_{T}\varphi(x,y)^{2}dT\\
=&\int_{\hat{T}}\tilde{\varphi}^{2}(\xi,\eta)|\tilde{J}|d\hat{T}\\
\geqslant&Ch_{T}^{2}\int_{\hat{T}}\tilde{\varphi}^{2}(\xi,\eta)d\hat{T}\\
\geqslant&Ch_{T}^{2}{h^{-1}_{\hat{T}}}\|\tilde{\varphi}\|_{\hat{T}}^{2},\end{split}

and

\displaystyle\begin{split}\|\nabla\varphi\|_{T}^{2}=&\int_{T}\left(\frac{\partial\varphi}{\partial x}(x,y)\right)^{2}+\left(\frac{\partial\varphi}{\partial y}(x,y)\right)^{2}dT\\
=&\int_{\hat{T}}\left(\frac{\partial\varphi}{\partial x}(\tilde{x}(\xi,\eta),\tilde{y}(\xi,\eta))\right)^{2}+\left(\frac{\partial\varphi}{\partial y}(\tilde{x}(\xi,\eta),\tilde{y}(\xi,\eta))\right)^{2}|\tilde{J}|d\hat{T}\\
\geqslant&\int_{\hat{T}}\left(\frac{\partial\varphi}{\partial x}(\tilde{x}(\xi,\eta),\tilde{y}(\xi,\eta))\right)^{2}+\left(\frac{\partial\varphi}{\partial y}(\tilde{x}(\xi,\eta),\tilde{y}(\xi,\eta))\right)^{2}h_{T}^{2}d\hat{T}\\
\geqslant&C\int_{\hat{T}}\left(\frac{\partial\varphi}{\partial x}\frac{\partial\tilde{x}}{\partial\xi}+\frac{\partial\varphi}{\partial y}\frac{\partial\tilde{y}}{\partial\xi}\right)^{2}+\left(\frac{\partial\varphi}{\partial x}\frac{\partial\tilde{x}}{\partial\eta}+\frac{\partial\varphi}{\partial y}\frac{\partial\tilde{y}}{\partial\eta}\right)^{2}d\hat{T}\\
=&\int_{\hat{T}}\left(\frac{\partial\tilde{\varphi}}{\partial\xi}\right)^{2}+\left(\frac{\partial\tilde{\varphi}}{\partial\eta}\right)^{2}d\hat{T}\\
\geqslant&Ch_{\hat{T}}\|\nabla\tilde{\varphi}\|_{\hat{T}}^{2}.\end{split}

Therefore, according to the trace inequality
[
33
]
on the reference element, we have

\displaystyle\begin{split}\|\varphi\|_{e}^{2}\leqslant&Ch_{T}\|\tilde{\varphi}\|_{\hat{e}}^{2}\\
\leqslant&Ch_{T}h_{\hat{T}}^{-1}\|\tilde{\varphi}\|_{\hat{T}}^{2}+Ch_{T}h_{\hat{T}}\|\nabla\tilde{\varphi}\|_{\hat{T}}^{2}\\
\leqslant&Ch_{T}^{-1}\|\varphi\|_{T}^{2}+Ch_{T}\|\nabla\varphi\|_{T}^{2}.\end{split}

The proof of the trace inequality is complete.
∎

Lemma 3.5
.

For
{\bf w}\in[H^{l+1}(\Omega)]^{2}
,
\rho\in H^{l}(\Omega)
with

\displaystyle Ch^{2l}\|{\bf w}\|_{l+1}^{2},

(3.11)

\displaystyle\sum_{T\in\mathcal{T}_{h}}h_{T}^{2s}\|\rho-\mathcal{Q}_{h}\rho\|_{T,s}^{2}

\displaystyle\leqslant

\displaystyle Ch^{2l}\|\rho\|_{l}^{2}.

(3.12)

Here
C
represents a positive constant that remains independent of both the mesh size
h
and the functions involved in the above estimates.

Proof.

For
T\in\mathcal{T}_{h}^{S}
, the inequalities (
3.10
)-(

) are same as the inequalities in

34
]
. Therefore we only consider the curved cell

. Assume that

is the circumscribed simplex satisfying the shape regularity conditions. And let

smoothly extend onto

the

projection operator onto

\displaystyle\begin{split}\sum_{T\in\mathcal{T}_{h}}\|u-Q_{0}u\|_{T}^{2}\leqslant&C\sum_{T\in\mathcal{T}_{h}}\|u-\tilde{Q}_{0}u\|_{T}^{2}\\

Similarly, we get

\displaystyle\sum_{T\in\mathcal{T}_{h}}h_{T}^{2s}\|\nabla(u-Q_{0}u)\|_{T,s}^{2}\leqslant Ch^{2(r+1)}\|u\|_{r+1}^{2}.

Therefore, the proof of Eq.(
3.10

3.11
)-(
3.12

∎

Next we give the stability analysis of the Stokes interface problems.

Lemma 3.6
.

(Inf-Sup Condition)
There are two positive constants

and

\displaystyle\sup_{{\mathbf{v}}\in V_{h}^{0}}\frac{b({\mathbf{v}},\rho)}{{|\hskip-1.4457pt|\hskip-1.4457pt|}{\mathbf{v}}{|\hskip-1.4457pt|\hskip-1.4457pt|}}\geqslant C_{1}\|\rho\|-C_{2}h|\rho|_{1},

\displaystyle\left(\sum_{T\in\mathcal{T}_{h}}Ch_{T}^{3}\|\bm{\omega}\|_{2}^{2}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}\left(\sum_{T\in\mathcal{T}_{h}}Ch_{T}^{2k-1}\|{\bf u}\|_{k+1}^{2}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}

\displaystyle\leqslant

\displaystyle Ch^{k+1}\|\bm{\omega}\|_{2}(\|{\bf u}_{1}\|_{k+1,\Omega_{1}}+\|{\bf u}_{2}\|_{k+1,\Omega_{2}}).

Next, we use same techniques and the fact that
\sum_{e\in\mathcal{E}_{h}^{S}}\langle\bm{\omega}-Q_{b}\bm{\omega},A(\nabla{\bf u})\cdot{\bf n}-A\mathbb{Q}_{h}(\nabla{\bf u})\cdot{\bf n}\rangle_{e}=0
to obtain

\displaystyle\Big|\sum_{T\in\mathcal{T}_{h}}\langle\bm{\omega}-Q_{b}\bm{\omega},A\nabla{\bf u}\cdot{\bf n}-A\mathbb{Q}_{h}(\nabla{\bf u})\cdot{\bf n}\rangle_{\partial T}\Big|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\Big|\sum_{e\in\mathcal{E}_{h}^{I}}\langle\bm{\omega}-Q_{b}\bm{\omega},A(\nabla{\bf u})\cdot{\bf n}-A\mathbb{Q}_{h}(\nabla{\bf u})\cdot{\bf n}\rangle_{e}\Big|

\displaystyle\leqslant

\displaystyle\sum_{e\in\mathcal{E}_{h}^{I}}\|\bm{\omega}-Q_{b}\bm{\omega}\|_{e}\|A\nabla{\bf u}-A\mathbb{Q}_{h}(\nabla{\bf u})\|_{e}

\displaystyle\leqslant

\displaystyle C\left(\sum_{e\in\mathcal{E}_{h}^{I}}\|\bm{\omega}-Q_{b}\bm{\omega}\|_{e}^{2}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}\left(\sum_{e\in\mathcal{E}_{h}^{I}}\|\nabla{\bf u}-\mathbb{Q}_{h}(\nabla{\bf u})\|_{e}^{2}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}

\displaystyle\leqslant

\displaystyle C\left(h^{k-\frac{1}{2}}\|{\bf u}\|_{k+1}\right)\left(\sum_{e\in\mathcal{E}_{h}^{I}}\|\bm{\omega}-Q_{b}\bm{\omega}\|_{e}^{2}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}},

where

\displaystyle\|\bm{\omega}-Q_{b}\bm{\omega}\|_{e}^{2}\leqslant\|\bm{\omega}-Q_{0}\bm{\omega}\|_{e}^{2}\leqslant\|\bm{\omega}-Q_{0}\bm{\omega}\|_{\partial T}^{2}\leqslant Ch_{T}^{3}\|\bm{\omega}\|_{2}^{2}.

Using the above inequality, we have

\displaystyle\sum_{T\in\mathcal{T}_{h}}\langle\bm{\omega}-Q_{b}\bm{\omega},A\nabla{\bf u}\cdot{\bf n}-A\mathbb{Q}_{h}(\nabla{\bf u})\cdot{\bf n}\rangle_{\partial T}\leqslant Ch^{k+1}\|\bm{\omega}\|_{2}(\|{\bf u}_{1}\|_{k+1,\Omega_{1}}+\|{\bf u}_{2}\|_{k+1,\Omega_{2}}).

Therefore,

\displaystyle\begin{split}|\ell_{1}({\bf u},Q_{h}\bm{\omega})|=&\Big|\sum_{T\in\mathcal{T}_{h}}\langle Q_{0}\bm{\omega}-Q_{b}\bm{\omega},A\nabla{\bf u}\cdot{\bf n}-A\mathbb{Q}_{h}(\nabla{\bf u})\cdot{\bf n}\rangle_{\partial T}\Big|\\
\leqslant&\Big|\sum_{T\in\mathcal{T}_{h}}\langle Q_{0}\bm{\omega}-\bm{\omega},A\nabla{\bf u}\cdot{\bf n}-A\mathbb{Q}_{h}(\nabla{\bf u})\cdot{\bf n}\rangle_{\partial T}\Big|\\
&+\Big|\sum_{T\in\mathcal{T}_{h}}\langle\bm{\omega}-Q_{b}\bm{\omega},A\nabla{\bf u}\cdot{\bf n}-A\mathbb{Q}_{h}(\nabla{\bf u})\cdot{\bf n}\rangle_{\partial T}\Big|\\
\leqslant&Ch^{k+1}\|\bm{\omega}\|_{2}(\|{\bf u}_{1}\|_{k+1,\Omega_{1}}+\|{\bf u}_{2}\|_{k+1,\Omega_{2}}).\end{split}

(6.10)

(2)For
\ell_{2}(p,Q_{h}\bm{\omega})
, we use the same method as the proof of
\ell_{1}({\bf u},Q_{h}\bm{\omega})
to get

\displaystyle\sum_{T\in\mathcal{T}_{h}}\langle Q_{0}\bm{\omega}-\bm{\omega},(p-\mathcal{Q}_{h}p){\bf n}\rangle_{\partial T}

\displaystyle\leqslant

\displaystyle\left(\sum_{T\in\mathcal{T}_{h}}\|Q_{0}\bm{\omega}-\bm{\omega}\|_{\partial T}^{2}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}\left(\sum_{T\in\mathcal{T}_{h}}\|p-\mathcal{Q}_{h}p\|_{\partial T}^{2}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}

\displaystyle\leqslant

\displaystyle\left(C\sum_{T\in\mathcal{T}_{h}}(h_{T}^{-1}\|Q_{0}\bm{\omega}-\bm{\omega}\|_{T}^{2}+h_{T}\|\nabla(Q_{0}\bm{\omega}-\bm{\omega})\|_{T}^{2})\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}

\displaystyle\left(C\sum_{T\in\mathcal{T}_{h}}(h_{T}^{-1}\|p-\mathcal{Q}_{h}p\|_{T}^{2}+h_{T}\|\nabla(p-\mathcal{Q}_{h}p)\|_{T}^{2})\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}

\displaystyle\leqslant

\displaystyle\left(\sum_{T\in\mathcal{T}_{h}}Ch_{T}^{3}\|\bm{\omega}\|_{2}^{2}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}\left(\sum_{T\in\mathcal{T}_{h}}Ch^{2k-1}\|p\|_{k}^{2}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}

\displaystyle\leqslant

\displaystyle Ch^{k+1}\|\bm{\omega}\|_{2}\|p\|_{k}.

Similarly, we have

\displaystyle\sum_{T\in\mathcal{T}_{h}}\langle\bm{\omega}-Q_{b}\bm{\omega},(p-\mathcal{Q}_{h}p){\bf n}\rangle_{\partial T}\leqslant Ch^{k+1}\|\bm{\omega}\|_{2}(\|p_{1}\|_{k,\Omega_{1}}+\|p_{2}\|_{k,\Omega_{2}}).

Therefore, for
\ell_{2}(p,Q_{h}{\bf w})
, we derive the following estimate

\displaystyle|\ell_{2}(p,Q_{h}\bm{\omega})|\leqslant Ch^{k+1}\|\bm{\omega}\|_{2}\|p\|_{k}.

(6.11)

(3)For
s(Q_{h}{\bf u},Q_{h}\bm{\omega})
, we get

\displaystyle\begin{split}&|s(Q_{h}{\bf u},Q_{h}\bm{\omega})|\\
\leqslant&\left|\sum_{T\in\mathcal{T}_{h}}Ah_{T}^{-1}\langle Q_{b}(Q_{0}{\bf u})-Q_{b}{\bf u},Q_{b}(Q_{0}\bm{\omega})-Q_{b}\bm{\omega}\rangle_{\partial T\setminus(\partial T\cap\Gamma)}\right|\\
&+\left|\sum_{T\in\mathcal{T}_{h}}Ah_{T}^{-1}\langle Q_{0}{\bf u}-Q_{b}{\bf u},Q_{0}\bm{\omega}-Q_{b}\bm{\omega}\rangle_{\partial T\cap\Gamma}\right|\\
\leqslant&C\left(\sum_{T\in\mathcal{T}_{h}}h_{T}^{-1}\|Q_{0}{\bf u}-{\bf u}\|_{\partial T\setminus(\partial T\cap\Gamma)}^{2}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}\left(\sum_{T\in\mathcal{T}_{h}}h_{T}^{-1}\|Q_{0}\bm{\omega}-\bm{\omega}\|_{\partial T\setminus(\partial T\cap\Gamma)}^{2}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}\\
&+\left(\sum_{T\in\mathcal{T}_{h}}h_{T}^{-1}\|Q_{0}{\bf u}-{\bf u}\|_{\partial T\cap\Gamma}^{2}+\|Q_{b}{\bf u}-{\bf u}\|_{\partial T\cap\Gamma}^{2}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}\\
&\left(\sum_{T\in\mathcal{T}_{h}}h_{T}^{-1}\|Q_{0}\bm{\omega}-\bm{\omega}\|_{\partial T\cap\Gamma}^{2}+\|Q_{b}\bm{\omega}-\bm{\omega}\|_{\partial T\cap\Gamma}^{2}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}\\
\leqslant&Ch^{k+1}(\|{\bf u}_{1}\|_{k+1,\Omega_{1}}+\|{\bf u}_{2}\|_{k+1,\Omega_{2}})\|\bm{\omega}\|_{2}.\end{split}

(6.12)

(4)For
\ell_{3}({\bf u},Q_{h}\bm{\omega})
, using the fact that
\sum_{i=1}^{2}\sum_{e\in\Gamma}\langle Q_{b}{\bf u}_{i}-{\bf u}_{i},Q_{b}(\nabla\bm{\omega}_{i}\cdot{\bf n}_{i})\rangle_{e}=0
, the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality, the trace inequality and the inverse inequality, we obtain

\displaystyle|\ell_{3}({\bf u},Q_{h}\bm{\omega})|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\Big|\sum_{i=1}^{2}\sum_{e\in\Gamma}\langle Q_{b}{\bf u}_{i}-{\bf u}_{i},A_{i}\nabla_{w}(Q_{h}\bm{\omega}_{i})\cdot{\bf n}\rangle_{e}\Big|

\displaystyle\leqslant

\displaystyle C\sum_{i=1}^{2}\sum_{e\in\Gamma}\|Q_{b}{\bf u}_{i}-{\bf u}_{i}\|_{e}\|\nabla_{w}(Q_{h}\bm{\omega}_{i})\cdot{\bf n}_{i}-\mathbb{Q}_{h}(\nabla\bm{\omega}_{i})\cdot{\bf n}_{i}\|_{e}

\displaystyle+C\sum_{i=1}^{2}\sum_{e\in\Gamma}\|Q_{b}{\bf u}_{i}-{\bf u}_{i}\|_{e}\|\mathbb{Q}_{h}(\nabla\bm{\omega}_{i})\cdot{\bf n}_{i}-\nabla\bm{\omega}_{i}\cdot{\bf n}_{i}\|_{e}

\displaystyle+C\sum_{i=1}^{2}\sum_{e\in\Gamma}\|Q_{b}{\bf u}_{i}-{\bf u}_{i}\|_{e}\|Q_{b}(\nabla\bm{\omega}_{i}\cdot{\bf n}_{i})-(\nabla\bm{\omega}_{i}\cdot{\bf n}_{i})\|_{e}

\displaystyle\leqslant

\displaystyle\left(\sum_{i=1}^{2}\sum_{e\in\Gamma}\|Q_{b}{\bf u}_{i}-{\bf u}_{i}\|_{e}^{2}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{2}\sum_{T\in\mathcal{T}_{h}^{i}}h_{T}^{-1}\|\nabla_{w}(Q_{h}\bm{\omega}_{i})-\mathbb{Q}_{h}(\nabla\bm{\omega}_{i})\|_{T}^{2}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}

\displaystyle+\left(\sum_{i=1}^{2}\sum_{e\in\Gamma}\|Q_{b}{\bf u}_{i}-{\bf u}_{i}\|_{e}^{2}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{2}\sum_{e\in\Gamma}\|\mathbb{Q}_{h}(\nabla\bm{\omega}_{i})-\nabla\bm{\omega}_{i}\|_{e}^{2}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}

\displaystyle+\left(\sum_{i=1}^{2}\sum_{e\in\Gamma}\|Q_{b}{\bf u}_{i}-{\bf u}_{i}\|_{e}^{2}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{2}\sum_{e\in\Gamma}\|Q_{b}(\nabla\bm{\omega}_{i}\cdot{\bf n}_{i})-(\nabla\bm{\omega}_{i}\cdot{\bf n}_{i})\|_{e}^{2}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}},

where by the trace inequality and the estimate (
3.10
), we have

\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{2}\sum_{e\in\Gamma}\|Q_{b}{\bf u}_{i}-{\bf u}_{i}\|_{e}^{2}

\displaystyle\leqslant

\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{2}\sum_{e\in\Gamma}\|Q_{0}{\bf u}_{i}-{\bf u}_{i}\|_{e}^{2}

\displaystyle\leqslant

\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{2}\sum_{T\in\mathcal{T}_{h}^{I}}C\left(h_{T}^{-1}\|Q_{0}{\bf u}_{i}-{\bf u}_{i}\|_{T}^{2}+h_{T}\|\nabla(Q_{0}{\bf u}_{i}-{\bf u}_{i})\|_{T}^{2}\right)

\displaystyle\leqslant

\displaystyle Ch^{2k+1}(\|{\bf u}_{1}\|^{2}_{k+1,\Omega_{1}}+\|{\bf u}_{2}\|^{2}_{k+1,\Omega_{2}}).

Similarly, we get the following estimates:

\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{2}\sum_{e\in\Gamma}\|\mathbb{Q}_{h}(\nabla\bm{\omega}_{i})-\nabla\bm{\omega}_{i}\|_{e}^{2}\leqslant Ch\|\bm{\omega}\|^{2}_{2},

\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{2}\sum_{e\in\Gamma}\|Q_{b}(\nabla\bm{\omega}_{i}\cdot{\bf n})-(\nabla\bm{\omega}_{i}\cdot{\bf n})\|_{e}^{2}\leqslant Ch\|\bm{\omega}\|^{2}_{2}.

Next according to Eq.(
3.2
), we take
\tau=\nabla_{w}(Q_{h}\bm{\omega})-\mathbb{Q}_{h}(\nabla\bm{\omega})
to lead to

\displaystyle\begin{split}&\|\nabla_{w}(Q_{h}\bm{\omega})-\mathbb{Q}_{h}(\nabla\bm{\omega})\|^{2}_{T}\\
\leqslant&\|Q_{b}\bm{\omega}_{i}-\bm{\omega}_{i}\|_{\partial T}\|\nabla_{w}(Q_{h}\bm{\omega})-\mathbb{Q}_{h}(\nabla\bm{\omega})\|^{2}_{\partial T}\\
\leqslant&Ch^{-1}\|Q_{b}\bm{\omega}_{i}-\bm{\omega}_{i}\|_{\partial T}\|\nabla_{w}(Q_{h}\bm{\omega})-\mathbb{Q}_{h}(\nabla\bm{\omega})\|^{2}_{T},\end{split}

therefore we have

\displaystyle\|\nabla_{w}(Q_{h}\bm{\omega})-\mathbb{Q}_{h}(\nabla\bm{\omega})\|_{T}\leqslant Ch^{-1}\|Q_{b}\bm{\omega}_{i}-\bm{\omega}_{i}\|_{\partial T}\leqslant Ch^{\frac{1}{2}}\|\bm{\omega}\|_{2}.

(6.13)

Combining the above four estimates, we get

\displaystyle|\ell_{3}({\bf u},Q_{h}\bm{\omega})|\leqslant Ch^{k+1}\|\bm{\omega}\|_{2}(\|{\bf u}_{1}\|_{k+1,\Omega_{1}}+\|{\bf u}_{2}\|_{k+1,\Omega_{2}}).

(6.14)

(5)For
\ell_{4}({\bf u},\mathcal{Q}_{h}\theta)
, by the fact that
\sum_{i=1}^{2}\sum_{e\in\Gamma}\langle Q_{b}{\bf u}_{i}-{\bf u}_{i},\mathcal{Q}_{h}(\theta{\bf n})\rangle_{e}=0
, we have

\displaystyle\begin{split}|\ell_{4}({\bf u},\mathcal{Q}_{h}\theta)|=&\Big|\sum_{i=1}^{2}\sum_{e\in\Gamma}\langle Q_{b}{\bf u}_{i}-{\bf u}_{i},(\mathcal{Q}_{h}\theta){\bf n}\rangle_{e}\Big|\\
\leqslant&\Big|\sum_{i=1}^{2}\sum_{e\in\Gamma}\langle Q_{b}{\bf u}_{i}-{\bf u}_{i},(\mathcal{Q}_{h}\theta){\bf n}-\theta{\bf n}\rangle_{e}\Big|\\
&+\Big|\sum_{i=1}^{2}\sum_{e\in\Gamma}\langle Q_{b}{\bf u}_{i}-{\bf u}_{i},\mathcal{Q}_{h}(\theta{\bf n})-\theta{\bf n}\rangle_{e}\Big|\\
\leqslant&Ch^{k+1}(\|{\bf u}_{1}\|_{k+1,\Omega_{1}}+\|{\bf u}_{2}\|_{k+1,\Omega_{2}})\|\theta\|_{1}.\end{split}

(6.15)

Combining the five estimates (
6.10
)-(
6.15
), we obtain

\displaystyle\|{\bf e}_{0}\|^{2}\leqslant

\displaystyle Ch^{k+1}(\|{\bf u}_{1}\|_{k+1,\Omega_{1}}+\|{\bf u}_{2}\|_{k+1,\Omega_{2}}+\|p_{1}\|_{k,\Omega_{1}}+\|p_{2}\|_{k,\Omega_{2}})(\|{\bf w}\|_{2}+\|\theta\|_{1})

\displaystyle+Ch{|\hskip-1.4457pt|\hskip-1.4457pt|}{\bf e}_{h}{|\hskip-1.4457pt|\hskip-1.4457pt|}\|{\bf e}_{0}\|

\displaystyle\leqslant

\displaystyle Ch^{k+1}(\|{\bf u}_{1}\|_{k+1,\Omega_{1}}+\|{\bf u}_{2}\|_{k+1,\Omega_{2}}+\|p_{1}\|_{k,\Omega_{1}}+\|p_{2}\|_{k,\Omega_{2}})\|{\bf e}_{0}\|

\displaystyle+Ch{|\hskip-1.4457pt|\hskip-1.4457pt|}{\bf e}_{h}{|\hskip-1.4457pt|\hskip-1.4457pt|}\|{\bf e}_{0}\|.

Finally, it follows from Theorem
5.1
that

\displaystyle\|{\bf e}_{0}\|\leqslant

\displaystyle Ch^{k+1}(\|{\bf u}_{1}\|_{k+1,\Omega_{1}}+\|{\bf u}_{2}\|_{k+1,\Omega_{2}}+\|p_{1}\|_{k,\Omega_{1}}+\|p_{2}\|_{k,\Omega_{2}})+Ch{|\hskip-1.4457pt|\hskip-1.4457pt|}{\bf e}_{h}{|\hskip-1.4457pt|\hskip-1.4457pt|}

\displaystyle\leqslant

\displaystyle Ch^{k+1}(\|{\bf u}_{1}\|_{k+1,\Omega_{1}}+\|{\bf u}_{2}\|_{k+1,\Omega_{2}}+\|p_{1}\|_{k,\Omega_{1}}+\|p_{2}\|_{k,\Omega_{2}}).

The proof of theorem is complete.

8
Conclusion

In this paper, we use the weak Galerkin finite element method to deal with Stokes interface problems with curved interface. We present a weak Galerkin finite element numerical scheme with two values at the interface. Based on the WG scheme, we prove that numerical solutions converge to the exact solutions at the optimal rates. Additionally, the numerical results from our examples show the optimal convergence orders are obtained in both the energy norm and the
L^{2}
norm on the triangular meshes and quadrilateral meshes. These results align with the theoretical analysis.
