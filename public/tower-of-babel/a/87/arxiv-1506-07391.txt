Abstract




HERMITE-HADAMARD TYPE INEQUALITIES FOR GENERALIZED
s
-CONVEX FUNCTIONS ON REAL LINEAR FRACTAL SET

In the paper, we establish the Hermite-Hadamard type inequalities for the generalized s-convex functions in the second sense on real linear fractal set
\mathbb{R}^{\alpha}(0<\alpha<1).

.

[
10

3
Main Results

Theorem 3.1
.

Suppose that
f:\mathbb{R_{+}}\rightarrow\mathbb{R^{\alpha}}
is a generalized
s
-convex function in the second sense, where
s\in(0,1).
Let
a,b\in[0,\infty),

a<b.
If
f\in C_{\alpha}[a,b]
, then the following inequalities hold:

[formula]

\begin{array}[]{cl}\displaystyle\frac{2^{(s-1)\alpha}}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}f\biggl(\displaystyle\frac{a+b}{2}\biggr)\leq\displaystyle\frac{{}_{a}I_{b}^{\alpha}f(x)}{(b-a)^{\alpha}}\leq\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+s\alpha)}{\Gamma(1+(s+1)\alpha)}\bigl(f(a)+f(b)\bigr).\end{array}

Proof
.

Let
x=a+b-t,
then by Lemma 2.1, we have
{}_{\frac{a+b}{2}}I_{b}^{(\alpha)}f(x)={{}_{a}}I_{\frac{a+b}{2}}^{(\alpha)}f(a+b-t).

Moreover,
f
is
s
convex in the second sense, then

\begin{array}[]{cl}{}_{a}I_{b}^{(\alpha)}f(x)&={{}_{a}}I_{\frac{a+b}{2}}^{(\alpha)}\bigl[f(x)+f(a+b-x)\bigr]\\
&\geq 2^{\alpha s}{{}_{a}}I_{\frac{a+b}{2}}^{(\alpha)}f\biggl(\displaystyle\frac{a+b}{2}\biggr)\\
&=\displaystyle\frac{2^{(s-1)\alpha}}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}(b-a)^{\alpha}f\biggl(\displaystyle\frac{a+b}{2}\biggr).\end{array}

In the other hand, let
x=b-(b-a)t
,
0\leq t\leq 1,
then we get

\begin{array}[]{cl}{}_{a}I_{b}^{(\alpha)}f(x)&=(b-a)^{\alpha}{{}_{0}}I_{1}^{(\alpha)}f\bigl[ta+(1-t)b\bigr]\\
&\leq(b-a)^{\alpha}{{}_{0}}I_{1}^{(\alpha)}\bigl[t^{\alpha s}f(a)+(1-t)^{\alpha s}f(b)\bigr]\\
&=(b-a)\bigl[f(a){{}_{0}}I_{1}^{(\alpha)}t^{\alpha s}+f(b){{}_{0}}I_{1}^{(\alpha)}(1-t)^{\alpha s}\bigr].\end{array}

From Lemma 2.3, it is easy to see that

{{}_{0}}I_{1}^{(\alpha)}t^{\alpha s}=\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+s\alpha)}{\Gamma(1+(s+1)\alpha)},

and

{{}_{0}}I_{1}^{(\alpha)}(1-t)^{\alpha s}=\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+s\alpha)}{\Gamma(1+(s+1)\alpha)}.

So,

{}_{a}I_{b}^{(\alpha)}f(x)\leq(b-a)^{\alpha}\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+s\alpha)}{\Gamma(1+(s+1)\alpha)}\bigl(f(a)+f(b)\bigr).

Therefore, we obtain

\displaystyle\frac{2^{(s-1)\alpha}}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}f\biggl(\displaystyle\frac{a+b}{2}\biggr)\leq\displaystyle\frac{{}_{a}I_{b}^{\alpha}f(x)}{(b-a)^{\alpha}}\leq\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+s\alpha)}{\Gamma(1+(s+1)\alpha)}\bigl(f(a)+f(b)\bigr).

Theorem 3.2
.

Let
f:I\rightarrow\mathbb{R^{\alpha}}
, be a differentiable function on
I^{0}
(
I^{0}
is the interior of
I
) such that
f^{(\alpha)}\in C_{\alpha}[a,b]
, where
a,b\in I^{0}
,
a<b
. If
|f^{(\alpha)}|^{q}
is generalized
s
-convex in the second sense on
[a,b]
for some fixed
s\in(0,1)
and
q\geq 1
, then

\begin{array}[]{cl}&\bigg|\displaystyle\frac{f(a)+f(b)}{2^{\alpha}}-\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}{(b-a)^{\alpha}}{{}_{a}}I_{b}^{(\alpha)}f(x)\bigg|\\
&\leq\displaystyle\frac{(b-a)^{\alpha}}{2^{\alpha}}\bigg(\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}{\Gamma(1+2\alpha)}\bigg)^{\frac{q-1}{q}}\bigg[\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+s\alpha)}{\Gamma(1+(s+1)\alpha)}+\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+\alpha)\Gamma(1+s\alpha)}{\Gamma(1+(s+2)\alpha)}\biggl(\biggl(\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\biggr)^{\alpha s}-2^{\alpha}\biggr)\bigg]^{\frac{1}{q}}\\
&\quad\times\bigg[\big|f^{(\alpha)}(a)\big|^{q}+\big|f^{(\alpha)}(b)\big|^{q}\bigg]^{\frac{1}{q}}\\
\end{array}

To show Theorem 3.2 is right, we need the following Lemma.

Lemma 3.1
.

(
[
8
]
)   Let
I\subseteq R
be an interval,
f:I^{0}\subseteq R\rightarrow R^{\alpha}
such that
f\in D_{\alpha}(I^{0})
and
f^{(\alpha)}\in C_{\alpha}[a,b]
for
a,b\in I^{0}
with
a<b
. Then the following equality holds:

\displaystyle\frac{f(a)+f(b)}{2^{\alpha}}-\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}{(b-a)^{\alpha}}{{}_{a}}I_{b}^{(\alpha)}f(x)=\displaystyle\frac{(b-a)^{\alpha}}{2^{\alpha}}\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}\int_{0}^{1}(1-2t)^{\alpha}f^{(\alpha)}\big(ta+(1-t)b\big)(dt)^{\alpha}.

Now, let us give the proof of Theorem 3.2.

Proof
.

From Lemma 3.1, it is obvious that

[formula]

\begin{array}[]{cl}&\bigg|\displaystyle\frac{f(a)+f(b)}{2^{\alpha}}-\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}{(b-a)^{\alpha}}{{}_{a}}I_{b}^{(\alpha)}f(x)\bigg|\\
\leq&\displaystyle\frac{(b-a)^{\alpha}}{2^{\alpha}}\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}\int_{0}^{1}|1-2t|^{\alpha}\big|f^{(\alpha)}(ta+(1-t)b)\big|(dt)^{\alpha}.\end{array}

Let us estimate

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}\int_{0}^{1}|1-2t|^{\alpha}\big|f^{(\alpha)}(ta+(1-t)b)\big|(dt)^{\alpha},

for
q=1
and
q>1.

Case I.

q=1.

Since
|f^{(\alpha)}|
is generalized
s
-convex on
[a,b]
in the second sense, we can know that for any
t\in[0,1]

\big|f^{(\alpha)}(ta+(1-t)b)\big|\leq t^{\alpha s}|f^{(\alpha)}(a)|+(1-t)^{\alpha s}|f^{(\alpha)}(b)|.

Then, we have,

[formula]

\begin{array}[]{cl}&\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}\int_{0}^{1}|1-2t|^{\alpha}\big|f^{(\alpha)}(ta+(1-t)b)\big|(dt)^{\alpha}\\
&\leq\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}\int_{0}^{1}|1-2t|^{\alpha}\big[t^{\alpha s}|f^{(\alpha)}(a)|+(1-t)^{\alpha s}|f^{(\alpha)}(b)|\big](dt)^{\alpha}\\
&=\biggl[|f^{(\alpha)}(a)|\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}\int_{0}^{1}t^{\alpha s}|1-2t|^{\alpha}(dt)^{\alpha}+|f^{(\alpha)}(b)|\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}\int_{0}^{1}(1-t)^{\alpha s}|1-2t|^{\alpha}(dt)^{\alpha}\biggr].\\
\end{array}

From Lemma 2.2 and lemma 2.3, it is easy to see that

[formula]

\begin{array}[]{cl}&\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}\int_{0}^{1}t^{\alpha s}|1-2t|^{\alpha}(dt)^{\alpha}\\
&=\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}\bigg[\int_{0}^{\frac{1}{2}}t^{\alpha s}(1-2t)^{\alpha}(dt)^{\alpha}+\displaystyle\int_{\frac{1}{2}}^{1}t^{\alpha s}(2t-1)^{\alpha}(dt)^{\alpha}\bigg]\\
&=\bigg[\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+s\alpha)}{\Gamma(1+(s+1)\alpha)}+\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+\alpha)\Gamma(1+s\alpha)}{\Gamma(1+(s+2)\alpha)}\biggl(\bigg(\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\biggr)^{\alpha s}-2^{\alpha}\biggr)\bigg].\end{array}

And, let
1-t=x,
then by Lemma 2.1 and (3.4), we have

[formula]

\begin{array}[]{cl}&\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}\int_{0}^{1}(1-t)^{\alpha s}|1-2t|^{\alpha}(dt)^{\alpha}\\
&=\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}\int_{0}^{1}x^{\alpha s}|1-2x|^{\alpha}(dx)^{\alpha}\\
&=\bigg[\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+s\alpha)}{\Gamma(1+(s+1)\alpha)}+\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+\alpha)\Gamma(1+s\alpha)}{\Gamma(1+(s+2)\alpha)}\biggl(\bigg(\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\biggr)^{\alpha s}-2^{\alpha}\biggr)\bigg].\end{array}

Thus, substituting (3.4) and (3.5) into (3.3), we have

[formula]

\begin{array}[]{cl}&\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}\int_{0}^{1}|1-2t|^{\alpha}\big|f^{(\alpha)}(ta+(1-t)b)\big|(dt)^{\alpha}\\
&\leq\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}{\Gamma(1+2\alpha)}\bigg[\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+s\alpha)}{\Gamma(1+(s+1)\alpha)}+\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+\alpha)\Gamma(1+s\alpha)}{\Gamma(1+(s+2)\alpha)}\biggl(\biggl(\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\biggr)^{\alpha s}-2^{\alpha}\biggr)\bigg]\\
&\quad\times\bigg[\big|f^{(\alpha)}(a)\big|^{q}+\big|f^{(\alpha)}(b)\big|\bigg].\\
\end{array}

Thus, from (3.2), we obtain

\begin{array}[]{cl}&\bigg|\displaystyle\frac{f(a)+f(b)}{2^{\alpha}}-\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}{(b-a)^{\alpha}}{{}_{a}}I_{b}^{(\alpha)}f(x)\bigg|\\
&\leq\displaystyle\frac{(b-a)^{\alpha}}{2^{\alpha}}\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}{\Gamma(1+2\alpha)}\bigg[\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+s\alpha)}{\Gamma(1+(s+1)\alpha)}+\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+\alpha)\Gamma(1+s\alpha)}{\Gamma(1+(s+2)\alpha)}\biggl(\biggl(\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\biggr)^{\alpha s}-2^{\alpha}\biggr)\bigg]\\
&\quad\times\bigg[\big|f^{(\alpha)}(a)\big|^{q}+\big|f^{(\alpha)}(b)\big|\bigg].\\
\end{array}

Case II.

q>1.

Using the generalized Hölder’s inequality(Lemma 2.4), we obtain

[formula]

\begin{array}[]{cl}&\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}\int_{0}^{1}|1-2t|^{\alpha}\big|f^{(\alpha)}(ta+(1-t)b)\big|(dt)^{\alpha}\\
&=\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}\int_{0}^{1}|1-2t|^{\alpha\frac{q-1}{q}}|1-2t|^{\alpha\frac{1}{q}}\big|f^{(\alpha)}(ta+(1-t)b)\big|(dt)^{\alpha}\\
&\leq\bigg(\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}\int_{0}^{1}|1-2t|^{\alpha}(dt)^{\alpha}\bigg)^{\frac{q-1}{q}}\bigg(\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}\int_{0}^{1}|1-2t|^{\alpha}|f^{(\alpha)}(ta+(1-t)b)|^{q}(dt)^{\alpha}\bigg)^{\frac{1}{q}}.\end{array}

It is obvious that

[formula]

\begin{array}[]{cl}&\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}\int_{0}^{1}|1-2t|^{\alpha}(dt)^{\alpha}\\
&=\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}\int_{0}^{\frac{1}{2}}(1-2t)^{\alpha}(dt)^{\alpha}+\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}\int_{\frac{1}{2}}^{1}(2t-1)^{\alpha}(dt)^{\alpha}=\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}{\Gamma(1+2\alpha)}.\end{array}

Moreover, since
|f^{(\alpha)}|^{q}
is generalized
s
-convex in the second sense on
[a,b],
then

\begin{array}[]{cl}&\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}\int_{0}^{1}|1-2t|^{\alpha}\big|f^{(\alpha)}(ta+(1-t)b)\big|^{q}(dt)^{\alpha}\\
&\leq\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}\int_{0}^{1}|1-2t|^{\alpha}\bigg(t^{\alpha s}|f^{(\alpha)}(a)|^{q}+(1-t)^{\alpha s}|f^{(\alpha)}(b)|^{q}\bigg)(dt)^{\alpha}\\
&=|f^{(\alpha)}(a)|^{q}\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}\int_{0}^{1}|1-2t|^{\alpha}t^{\alpha s}(dt)^{\alpha}+|f^{(\alpha)}(b)|^{q}\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}\int_{0}^{1}|1-2t|^{\alpha}(1-t)^{\alpha s}(dt)^{\alpha}.\\
\end{array}

From (3.3) and (3.4), it is easy to see that

\begin{array}[]{cl}&\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}\int_{0}^{1}|1-2t|^{\alpha}t^{\alpha s}(dt)^{\alpha}\\
=&\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}\int_{0}^{1}|1-2t|^{\alpha}(1-t)^{\alpha s}(dt)^{\alpha}\\
=&\bigg[\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+s\alpha)}{\Gamma(1+(s+1)\alpha)}+\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+\alpha)\Gamma(1+s\alpha)}{\Gamma(1+(s+2)\alpha)}\biggl(\biggl(\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\biggr)^{\alpha s}-2^{\alpha}\biggr)\bigg].\end{array}

So,

[formula]

\begin{array}[]{cl}&\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}\int_{0}^{1}|1-2t|^{\alpha}\big|f^{(\alpha)}(ta+(1-t)b)\big|^{q}(dt)^{\alpha}\\
=&\bigg[\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+s\alpha)}{\Gamma(1+(s+1)\alpha)}+\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+\alpha)\Gamma(1+s\alpha)}{\Gamma(1+(s+2)\alpha)}\biggl(\biggl(\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\biggr)^{\alpha s}-2^{\alpha}\biggr)\bigg]^{\frac{1}{q}}\bigg[\big|f^{(\alpha)}(a)\big|^{q}+\big|f^{(\alpha)}(b)\big|^{q}\bigg]^{\frac{1}{q}}.\end{array}

Thus, substituting (3.8) and (3.9) into (3.7), we have

\begin{array}[]{cl}&\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}\int_{0}^{1}|1-2t|^{\alpha}\big|f^{(\alpha)}(ta+(1-t)b)\big|(dt)^{\alpha}\\
\leq&\bigg(\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}{\Gamma(1+2\alpha)}\bigg)^{\frac{q-1}{q}}\bigg[\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+s\alpha)}{\Gamma(1+(s+1)\alpha)}+\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+\alpha)\Gamma(1+s\alpha)}{\Gamma(1+(s+2)\alpha)}\biggl(\biggl(\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\biggr)^{\alpha s}-2^{\alpha}\biggr)\bigg]^{\frac{1}{q}}\bigg[\big|f^{(\alpha)}(a)\big|^{q}+\big|f^{(\alpha)}(b)\big|^{q}\bigg]^{\frac{1}{q}}.\end{array}

So, from (3.2) it follows that

\begin{array}[]{cl}&\bigg|\displaystyle\frac{f(a)+f(b)}{2^{\alpha}}-\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}{(b-a)^{\alpha}}{{}_{a}}I_{b}^{(\alpha)}f(x)\bigg|\\
&\leq\displaystyle\frac{(b-a)^{\alpha}}{2^{\alpha}}\bigg(\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}{\Gamma(1+2\alpha)}\bigg)^{\frac{q-1}{q}}\bigg[\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+s\alpha)}{\Gamma(1+(s+1)\alpha)}+\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+\alpha)\Gamma(1+s\alpha)}{\Gamma(1+(s+2)\alpha)}\biggl(\biggl(\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\biggr)^{\alpha s}-2^{\alpha}\biggr)\bigg]^{\frac{1}{q}}\\
&\quad\times\bigg[\big|f^{(\alpha)}(a)\big|^{q}+\big|f^{(\alpha)}(b)\big|^{q}\bigg]^{\frac{1}{q}}.\\
\end{array}

Thus, we complete the proof of Theorem 3.2.

Theorem 3.3
.

Let
f:

I\rightarrow\mathbb{R^{\alpha}},

I\subset[0,\infty)
, be a differentiable function on
I^{0}
such that
f^{(\alpha)}\in C_{\alpha}[a,b]
, where
a,b\in I
and
a<b
. If
|f^{(\alpha)}|^{q}
is generalized
s
-convex in the second sense on
[a,b]
for some fixed
s\in(0,1)
and
q>1
, then

\begin{array}[]{cl}&\bigg|\displaystyle\frac{f(a)+f(b)}{2^{\alpha}}-\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}{(b-a)^{\alpha}}{{}_{a}}I_{b}^{(\alpha)}f(x)\bigg|\\
&\leq\displaystyle\frac{(b-a)^{\alpha}}{2^{\alpha}}\bigg[\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+\frac{q}{q-1}\alpha)}{2^{\alpha}\Gamma(1+\frac{2q-1}{q-1}\alpha)}\bigg]^{\frac{q-1}{q}}\bigg(\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+s\alpha)}{2^{\alpha}\Gamma(1+(s+1)\alpha)}\bigg)^{\frac{1}{q}}\\
&\quad\times\biggl[\biggl(|f^{(\alpha)}(a)|^{q}+\biggl|f^{(\alpha)}\biggl(\displaystyle\frac{a+b}{2}\biggr)\biggr|^{q}\biggr)^{\frac{1}{q}}+\biggl(\biggl|f^{(\alpha)}\biggl(\displaystyle\frac{a+b}{2}\biggr)\biggr|^{q}+|f^{(\alpha)}(b)|^{q}\biggr)^{\frac{1}{q}}\biggr].\end{array}

Proof
.

From Lemma 3.1, we have

[formula]

\begin{array}[]{cl}&\bigg|\displaystyle\frac{f(a)+f(b)}{2^{\alpha}}-\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}{(b-a)^{\alpha}}{{}_{a}}I_{b}^{(\alpha)}f(x)\bigg|\\
&\leq\displaystyle\frac{(b-a)^{\alpha}}{2^{\alpha}}\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}\int_{0}^{1}|1-2t|^{\alpha}|f^{(\alpha)}(ta+(1-t)b)|(dt)^{\alpha}\\
&\leq\displaystyle\frac{(b-a)^{\alpha}}{2^{\alpha}}\bigg[\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}\int_{0}^{\frac{1}{2}}(1-2t)|f^{(\alpha)}(ta+(1-t)b)|(dt)^{\alpha}\\
&\quad+\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}\int_{\frac{1}{2}}^{1}(2t-1)|f^{(\alpha)}(ta+(1-t)b)|(dt)^{\alpha}\bigg].\end{array}

Let us estimate

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}\int_{0}^{\frac{1}{2}}(1-2t)|f^{(\alpha)}(ta+(1-t)b)|(dt)^{\alpha}

and

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}\int_{\frac{1}{2}}^{1}(2t-1)|f^{(\alpha)}(ta+(1-t)b)|(dt)^{\alpha},

respectively.

Using the generalized Hölder’s inequality(Lemma 2.4), we obtain

[formula]

\begin{array}[]{cl}&\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}\int_{0}^{\frac{1}{2}}(1-2t)|f^{(\alpha)}(ta+(1-t)b)|(dt)^{\alpha}\\
&\leq\bigg(\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}\int_{0}^{\frac{1}{2}}(1-2t)^{\alpha\frac{q}{q-1}}(dt)^{\alpha}\bigg)^{\frac{q-1}{q}}\bigg(\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}\int_{0}^{\frac{1}{2}}|f^{(\alpha)}(ta+(1-t)b)|^{\alpha q}(dt)^{\alpha}\bigg)^{\frac{1}{q}}.\end{array}

It is easy to see that

[formula]

\begin{array}[]{cl}\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}\int_{0}^{\frac{1}{2}}(1-2t)^{\alpha\frac{q}{q-1}}(dt)^{\alpha}=\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}\int_{\frac{1}{2}}^{1}(1-2t)^{\alpha\frac{q}{q-1}}(dt)^{\alpha}=\frac{\Gamma(1+\frac{q}{q-1}\alpha)}{2^{\alpha}\Gamma(1+\frac{2q-1}{q-1}\alpha)}.\end{array}

Let
|f^{(\alpha)}(ta+(1-t)b)|^{q}=U(t).
Since
|f^{(\alpha)}|^{q}
is a generalized
s
convex function in the second sense , then
U(t)
is a generalized
s
convex function in the second sense , too. Thus, from the right hand side of (3.1),
it follows that

[formula]

\begin{array}[]{cl}&\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}\int_{0}^{\frac{1}{2}}|f^{(\alpha)}(ta+(1-t)b)|^{q}(dt)^{\alpha}\\
=&\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}\int_{0}^{\frac{1}{2}}U(t)(dt)^{\alpha}\\
\leq&\biggl(\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}-0\biggr)^{\alpha}\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+s\alpha)}{\Gamma(1+(s+1)\alpha)}(U(0)+U(\frac{1}{2}))\\
=&\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+s\alpha)}{2^{\alpha}\Gamma(1+(s+1)\alpha)}\biggl(\biggl|f^{(\alpha)}\biggl(\displaystyle\frac{a+b}{2}\biggr)\biggr|^{q}+|f^{(\alpha)}(b)|^{q}\biggr).\end{array}

Thus, substituting (3.12) and (3.13) into (3.11), we get

[formula]

\begin{array}[]{cl}&\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}\int_{0}^{\frac{1}{2}}(1-2t)|f^{(\alpha)}(ta+(1-t)b)|(dt)^{\alpha}\\
&\leq\bigg(\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+\frac{q}{q-1}\alpha)}{2^{\alpha}\Gamma(1+\frac{2q-1}{q-1}\alpha)}\bigg)^{\frac{q-1}{q}}\bigg(\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+s\alpha)}{2^{\alpha}\Gamma(1+(s+1)\alpha)}\bigg)^{\frac{1}{q}}\biggl(|f^{(\alpha)}(a)|^{q}+\biggl|f^{(\alpha)}\biggl(\frac{a+b}{2}\biggr)\biggr|^{q}\biggr)^{\frac{1}{q}}.\end{array}

Moreover,

\begin{array}[]{cl}\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}\int_{\frac{1}{2}}^{1}(2t-1)^{\alpha\frac{q}{q-1}}(dt)^{\alpha}=\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}\int_{\frac{1}{2}}^{1}(1-2t)^{\alpha\frac{q}{q-1}}(dt)^{\alpha}=\frac{\Gamma(1+\frac{q}{q-1}\alpha)}{2^{\alpha}\Gamma(1+\frac{2q-1}{q-1}\alpha)}.\end{array}

And, similar to the estimate of (3.13), we have

\begin{array}[]{cl}\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}\int_{\frac{1}{2}}^{1}|f^{(\alpha)}(ta+(1-t)b)|^{q}(dt)^{\alpha}\leq\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+s\alpha)}{2^{\alpha}\Gamma(1+(s+1)\alpha)}\biggl(\biggl|f^{(\alpha)}\biggl(\displaystyle\frac{a+b}{2}\biggr)\biggr|^{q}+|f^{(\alpha)}(b)|^{q}\biggr).\end{array}

So, it is analogues to the estimate of (3.11), we have

[formula]

\begin{array}[]{cl}&\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}\int_{0}^{\frac{1}{2}}(1-2t)|f^{(\alpha)}(ta+(1-t)b)|(dt)^{\alpha}\\
&\leq\bigg(\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}\int_{\frac{1}{2}}^{1}(2t-1)^{\alpha\frac{q}{q-1}}(dt)^{\alpha}\bigg)^{\frac{q-1}{q}}\bigg(\displaystyle\frac{1}{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}\int_{\frac{1}{2}}^{1}|f^{(\alpha)}(ta+(1-t)b)|^{q\alpha}(dt)^{\alpha}\bigg)^{\frac{1}{q}}\\
&\leq\bigg(\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+\frac{q}{q-1}\alpha)}{2^{\alpha}\Gamma(1+\frac{2q-1}{q-1}\alpha)}\bigg)^{\frac{q-1}{q}}\bigg(\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+s\alpha)}{2^{\alpha}\Gamma(1+(s+1)\alpha)}\bigg)^{\frac{1}{q}}\biggl(\biggl|f^{(\alpha)}\biggl(\frac{a+b}{2}\biggr)\biggr|^{q}+|f^{(\alpha)}(b)|^{q}\biggr)\biggr|^{q}\biggr)^{\frac{1}{q}}.\end{array}

Thus, combining (3.10), (3.14) and (3.15), we obtain

\begin{array}[]{cl}&\bigg|\displaystyle\frac{f(a)+f(b)}{2^{\alpha}}-\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+\alpha)}{(b-a)^{\alpha}}{{}_{a}}I_{b}^{(\alpha)}f(x)\bigg|\\
&\leq\displaystyle\frac{(b-a)^{\alpha}}{2^{\alpha}}\bigg[\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+\frac{q}{q-1}\alpha)}{2^{\alpha}\Gamma(1+\frac{2q-1}{q-1}\alpha)}\bigg]^{\frac{q-1}{q}}\bigg(\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma(1+s\alpha)}{2^{\alpha}\Gamma(1+(s+1)\alpha)}\bigg)^{\frac{1}{q}}\\
&\quad\times\biggl[\biggl(|f^{(\alpha)}(a)|^{q}+\biggl|f^{(\alpha)}\biggl(\displaystyle\frac{a+b}{2}\biggr)\biggr|^{q}\biggr)^{\frac{1}{q}}+\biggl(\biggl|f^{(\alpha)}\biggl(\displaystyle\frac{a+b}{2}\biggr)\biggr|^{q}+|f^{(\alpha)}(b)|^{q}\biggr)^{\frac{1}{q}}\biggr].\end{array}

Terefore, we complete the proof of Theorem 3.3.
