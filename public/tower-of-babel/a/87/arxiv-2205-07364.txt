Prospects for Dark Matter Search at the Super c-tau Factory
E. E. Boos, V. E. Bunichev, S. S. Trykov

Abstract
We present perspectives for searching for light dark matter production mediated by a leptophilic scalar ϕ \phi and a dark photon A ′ A^{\prime} in in experiments at the Super c-tau Factory. Based on the analysis of the associative production of mediators and τ \tau -leptons at the energies of the future collider, the possibility of searching in the non-excluded region of the parameter space was found. The obtained sensitivity curves at the 90 % 90\% C.L. for the mediators mass range below 4 ​ Ge ​ V 4\,\mathrm{\,Ge\kern-1.00006ptV} demonstrate the power of the SCTF for light dark matter search.



I
Introduction

Many gravitational, astrophysical and cosmological unexplained phenomena indicate the existence of a special kind of matter,
which we call dark matter (DM)
Zwicky:1933gu
;
Zwicky:1937zza
;
Rubin:1970zza
.
Such kind of phenomena can be explained by assuming that DM is a kind of particles, which should have some certain properties.
We follow a simplified models approach, in which it is assumed that there are DM particles of any spin and there are particles,
usually scalar or vector, mediating interactions between Standard Model (SM) particles and DM
Agrawal:2021dbo
.

Many models beyond the SM predict the existence of additional scalars
\phi

that can mediate interactions between SM particles and DM
Marshall:2010qi
;
Branco:2011iw
;
Batell:2016ove
.
Possible couplings of an interaction between additional scalars with SM fermions are constrained by the SM gauge invariance.
Now, the minimal extension of the scalar sector by mixing an additional scalar with the SM Higgs boson is strongly constrained by experiments of searching for rare flavor-changing neutral current decays of mesons, such as
B^{+}\rightarrow K^{+}\phi
and
K^{+}\rightarrow\pi^{+}\phi

Beacham:2019nyx
;
Lanfranchi:2020crw
, and by searching for heavy DM
Felcini:2018osp
;
Beacham:2019nyx
;
Lanfranchi:2020crw
;
Agrawal:2021dbo
.
Thus, the
\mathrm{\,Me\kern-1.00006ptV}-\mathrm{\,Ge\kern-1.00006ptV}
range search area comes to the fore.

In a sub-GeV scale, some BSM theories make it possible to assume that in the SM sector after spontaneous electroweak symmetry breaking, an extra scalar couples exclusively to SM leptons
Marshall:2010qi
;
Branco:2011iw
;
Batell:2016ove
.
Possible couplings with quarks turn out to be strongly suppressed.
Thus, a scalar portal for interaction between heavy-flavored SM leptons and DM is occurred.
We refer the additional scalar particle as a dark leptophilic scalar
\phi
.
A Lagrangian of that interaction is given by

\mathcal{L}_{\rm int}^{\phi}=-\xi\sum\limits_{\ell=e,\mu,\tau}\frac{m_{\ell}}{v}\bar{\ell}\phi\ell-g_{\rm D}\bar{\chi}\phi\chi,

(1)

where
\xi
is the flavor-independent coupling constant,
v=246\,\mathrm{\,Ge\kern-1.00006ptV}
is the SM Higgs vacuum expectation value, and the second term is a Lagrangian of the interaction between the dark scalar
\phi
and fermionic DM states
\chi
with a coupling constant
g_{\rm D}
.

In the
\mathrm{\,Me\kern-1.00006ptV}-\mathrm{\,Ge\kern-1.00006ptV}
mass range, if kinematically allowed, dominant decay modes of the dark leptophilic scalar are to lighter DM states and to leptons,
with partial widths given by
Abercrombie:2015wmb

\displaystyle\Gamma_{\phi}^{\chi\bar{\chi}}

\displaystyle=g_{\rm D}^{2}\frac{m_{\phi}}{8\pi}\beta_{\chi}^{3},

(2)

\displaystyle\Gamma_{\phi}^{\ell^{+}\ell^{-}}

\displaystyle=\xi^{2}\frac{m_{\ell}^{2}}{v^{2}}\frac{m_{\phi}}{8\pi}\beta_{\ell}^{3},

(3)

here
\beta_{f}=\sqrt{1-4m_{f}^{2}/m_{\phi}^{2}}
,
m_{\phi}
- mass of the mediator,
m_{f}
- mass of the mediator decay product.

Another intriguing possibility for providing a portal between the SM and DM sectors is an addition of an extra massive vector boson associated with the spontaneously broken
U_{\rm D}(1)
gauge group,
whose interaction between the SM charged fermionic current is similar to the ordinary photon of electromagnetism
Holdom:1985ag
;
Alexander:2016aln
;
Agrawal:2021dbo
.
We refer such a vector mediator as a dark photon
A^{\prime}
.
The
A^{\prime}
can have a mass in the sub-GeV mass range, and couple to the SM via kinetic mixing with the ordinary photon.
A coupling constant of the interaction between
A^{\prime}
and the SM states is suppressed by a kinetic mixing parameter
\varepsilon\ll 1
.
A Lagrangian can be written as
Okun:1982xi
;
Galison:1983pa
;
Holdom:1985ag

\mathcal{L}=\mathcal{L}_{\rm SM}+\frac{1}{2}\frac{\varepsilon}{\cos\theta_{\rm{W}}}B^{\mu\nu}F^{\prime}_{\mu\nu}+\mathcal{L}_{\rm Dark}-e_{\rm D}A^{\prime}_{\mu}\,j_{\rm DM}^{\mu},

(4)

where
F^{\prime}_{\mu\nu}\equiv\partial_{\mu}A^{\prime}_{\nu}-\partial_{\nu}A^{\prime}_{\mu}
is the dark photon gauge field
A^{\prime}_{\mu}
strength tensor,

B_{\mu\nu}\equiv\partial_{\mu}B_{\nu}-\partial_{\nu}B_{\mu}
is the SM weak hypercharge strength tensor;

e=\sqrt{4\pi\alpha_{\rm EM}}
is the
U(1)
coupling constant, and

e_{\rm D}=\sqrt{4\pi\alpha_{\rm D}}
is the
U_{\rm D}(1)
coupling constant of the interaction between
A^{\prime}
and the DM current
j^{\mu}_{\rm DM}=\bar{\chi}\gamma^{\mu}\chi
for fermionic DM states.

\mathcal{L}_{\rm Dark}
is a term containing a DM and dark photon states Lagrangians.

After spontaneous symmetry breaking in the
\mathrm{\,Me\kern-1.00006ptV}-\mathrm{\,Ge\kern-1.00006ptV}
mass range,
the main contribution of mixing by the term
B^{\mu\nu}F^{\prime}_{\mu\nu}
in the Lagrangian (
4
) is given by

(\varepsilon/2)F^{\mu\nu}F^{\prime}_{\mu\nu}
.
Mixing with a heavy
Z
boson is suppressed by a factor
1/m_{Z}^{2}
.
After diagonalization of the kinetic terms, the result of mixing leads
to the fact that the dark photon
A^{\prime}
acquires a coupling
\varepsilon e

with the electromagnetic current
j^{\mu}_{\rm EM}
:

\mathcal{L}_{\rm int}^{A^{\prime}}=-\varepsilon eA^{\prime}_{\mu}\,j_{\rm EM}^{\mu}-e_{\rm D}A^{\prime}_{\mu}\,j_{\rm DM}^{\mu}.

(5)

We consider light DM states
\chi
with mass
m_{\chi}<m_{A^{\prime}}/2
and
e_{\rm D}>e
.
Thus, if kinematically allowed, dark photon are expected to decay predominantly into invisible dark sector final states.
If no such decays are allowed, the dark photon will decay into visible SM final states, with decay partial widths given by
Abercrombie:2015wmb
;
Fabbrichesi:2020wbt

\Gamma_{A^{\prime}}^{\chi\bar{\chi}}=\frac{1}{3}\alpha_{\rm D}m_{A^{\prime}}\left(1+2\frac{m_{\chi}^{2}}{m_{A^{\prime}}^{2}}\right)\beta_{\chi},

(6)

\displaystyle\Gamma_{A^{\prime}}^{\ell^{+}\ell^{-}}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{3}\varepsilon^{2}\alpha m_{A^{\prime}}\left(1+2\frac{m_{\ell}^{2}}{m_{A^{\prime}}^{2}}\right)\beta_{\ell},

(7)

\displaystyle\Gamma_{A^{\prime}}^{\rm hadrons}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{3}\varepsilon^{2}\alpha m_{A^{\prime}}\left(1+2\frac{m_{\mu}^{2}}{m_{A^{\prime}}^{2}}\right)\beta_{\mu}\,R,

(8)

\displaystyle R

\displaystyle\equiv\frac{\sigma(e^{+}e^{-}\rightarrow{\rm hadrons})}{\sigma(e^{+}e^{-}\rightarrow\mu^{+}\mu^{-})}.

(9)

where
\beta_{f}
has the same definition as in (
2
) and (
3
).

\phi
and the dark photon
A^{\prime}
in the processes

and
e^{+}e^{-}\rightarrow\tau^{+}\tau^{-}+(A^{\prime}\rightarrow invisible)

3

7\,\mathrm{\,Ge\kern-1.00006ptV}
in the centre of mass system
that will provide a uniquely high peak luminosity of
10^{35}\,{\rm cm}^{-2}{\rm s}^{-1}

The SCTF physical program is aimed at a detailed study of the processes involving the
c
-quark and
\tau
-lepton in the final state.
For
10
years of SCTF operation, the integral luminosity of the collider will exceed
10\,{\rm ab}^{-1}

In this work we have proposed a search for invisible decays of dark leptophilic scalar and dark photon in the processes

e^{+}e^{-}\rightarrow\tau^{+}\tau^{-}\phi
and
e^{+}e^{-}\rightarrow\tau^{+}\tau^{-}A^{\prime}
, respectively, at the future SCTF.
We present the promising sensitivity on the scalar coupling constant
\xi
and the dark photon mixing strength
\varepsilon
at
\sqrt{s}=4.2,\,7\,\mathrm{\,Ge\kern-1.00006ptV}
assuming integrated luminosity of
3\,{\rm ab}^{-1}
, and
30\,{\rm ab}^{-1}
for
\sqrt{s}=7\,\mathrm{\,Ge\kern-1.00006ptV}
in the non-excluded parameter spaces below
4\,\mathrm{\,Ge\kern-1.00006ptV}
.
In addition, we have discussed the constraints on light thermal DM.
We provide the expected
90\%
C.L. sensitivity on the dimensionless DM annihilation cross section parameter
y
.
We find that the future SCTF data can expand the search light DM for the mass region
0.001\,\mathrm{\,Ge\kern-1.00006ptV}\lesssim\chi\lesssim 1\,\mathrm{\,Ge\kern-1.00006ptV}
; for the scalar, pseudo-Dirac and Majorana type of light DM can be excluded by the combined data from the future STCF and BABAR for the mass region
0.1\,\mathrm{\,Ge\kern-1.00006ptV}\lesssim\chi\lesssim 1\,\mathrm{\,Ge\kern-1.00006ptV}
.

It should be noted that the search for light dark matter in the processes of associated production with tau leptons is of particular interest, since it allows one to simultaneously search for scalar and vector mediators. As shown in Fig.
7
, the angular distributions differ significantly for mediators with different spins. By studying the angular correlations in such processes, one can determine the spin nature of mediator particles.
