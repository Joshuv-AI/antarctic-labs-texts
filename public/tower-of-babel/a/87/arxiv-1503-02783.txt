Weighted Tensor Products of Joyal Species, Graphs, and Charades
Ross STREET

Abstract
Motivated by the weighted Hurwitz product on sequences in an algebra, we produce a family of monoidal structures on the category of Joyal species. We suggest a family of tensor products for charades. We begin by seeing weighted derivational algebras and weighted Rota–Baxter algebras as special monoids and special semigroups, respectively, for the same monoidal structure on the category of graphs in a monoidal additive category. Weighted derivations are lifted to the categorical level.

Review of weighted derivations and Rota–Baxter operators

The inspiration for the family of tensor products on species came from the
\lambda
-weighted
product of Hurwitz series as discussed in
[
12
,
23
]
and their references.
They begin by defining a
derivation of weight
\lambda

on an algebra
A
over a commutative ring
k
,
with given
\lambda\in k

k
-module morphism
d\colon A\to A
satisfying

\displaystyle d(ab)=d(a)b+ad(b)+\lambda d(a)d(b).

They note the generalized Leibnitz rule

\displaystyle d^{n}(ab)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}{\sum_{j=0}^{n-k}{\binom{n}{k}\binom{n-k}{j}\lambda^{k}d^{n-j}(a)d^{k+j}(b)}}.

However, I prefer to write this in the form

\displaystyle d^{n}(ab)=\sum_{n=r+s+t}{\binom{n}{r,s,t}\lambda^{t}d^{r+t}(a)d^{s+t}(b)}

(2.1)

to emphasise the relationship to the trinomial expansion rule for

Here

\displaystyle\binom{n}{r,s,t}=\frac{n!}{r!s!t!}.

The
\lambda

can be defined by the clearly related equation

\displaystyle\big(f\cdot^{\lambda}g\big)(n)=\sum_{n=r+s+t}{\binom{n}{r,s,t}\lambda^{t}f(r+t)g(s+t)}.

(2.2)

.

For
