1.
Introduction

Let
{\mathbb{B}}(\mathscr{H})
denotes the
C^{*}
-algebra of all bounded linear operators on a complex Hilbert space
{\mathscr{H}}
with an inner product
\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle
and the corresponding norm
\|\cdot\|
. In the case when
{\rm dim}{\mathscr{H}}=n
, we identify
{\mathbb{B}}({\mathscr{H}})
with the matrix algebra
\mathbb{M}_{n}
of all
n\times n
matrices with entries in
the complex field. For
S\in{\mathbb{B}}(\mathscr{H})
, let
S=\Re(S)+i\Im(S)

be the Cartesian decomposition of
S
, where the Hermitian matrices
\Re(S)=\frac{S+S^{*}}{2}
and
\Im(S)=\frac{S-S^{*}}{2i}
are called the real and imaginary parts of
S
, respectively.
The numerical radius of
S\in{\mathbb{B}}({\mathscr{H}})
is defined by

\displaystyle w(S):=\sup\{|\langle Sx,x\rangle|:x\in{\mathscr{H}},\|x\|=1\}.

It is well known that
w(\,\cdot\,)
defines a norm on
{\mathbb{B}}({\mathscr{H}})
, which is equivalent to the usual operator norm
\|\cdot\|
. In fact, for any
S\in{\mathbb{B}}({\mathscr{H}})
,

\frac{1}{2}\|S\|\leq w(S)\leq\|S\|
;
see. Let
r(\cdot)
denote the spectral radius. It is well known that for every operator

S\in{\mathbb{B}}({\mathscr{H}})
, we have
r(S)\leq w(S)
.
In, the authors showed that

w\left(S\right)=\sup_{\theta\in\mathbb{R}}\|\Re({{\rm{e}}^{i\theta}S})\|=\sup_{\alpha^{2}+\beta^{2}=1}\|\alpha\Re S+\beta\Im S\|,

which is equali to the above definition.
For more facts about the numerical radius see and references therein.
Let
S,T,X,Y\in{\mathbb{B}}({\mathscr{H}})
. The operator matrices
\left[\begin{array}[]{cc}S&0\\
0&Y\end{array}\right]
and
\left[\begin{array}[]{cc}0&T\\
X&0\end{array}\right]
are called the diagonal and off-diagonal parts of the operator matrix
\left[\begin{array}[]{cc}S&T\\
X&Y\end{array}\right]
, respectively.

In , it has been shown that if
S
is an operator in
{\mathbb{B}}({\mathscr{H}})
, then

\displaystyle w(S)\leq\frac{1}{2}\left(\|S\|+\|S^{2}\|^{\frac{1}{2}}\right).

(1.1)

Several refinements and generalizations of inequality have been given; see Yamazaki showed that for
S\in{\mathbb{B}}({\mathscr{H}})
and
t\in[0,1]
we have

\displaystyle w(S)\leq\frac{1}{2}\left(\|S\|+w(\tilde{S}_{t})\right).

(1.2)

where
S=U|S|
is the polar decomposition of
S
and
\tilde{S}_{t}=|S|^{t}U|S|^{1-t}
. Horn et al. proved that

\displaystyle\|S+T\|\leq\||S|+|T|\|,

(1.3)

where
S,T\in{\mathbb{B}}({\mathscr{H}})
are normal. Davidson and Power proved that if
S
and
T
are positive operators in
{\mathbb{B}}({\mathscr{H}})
, then

\displaystyle\|S+T\|\leq\max\{\|S\|,\|T\|\}+\|ST\|^{\frac{1}{2}}.

(1.4)

Inequality has been generalized in . In , the author extended this inequality to the form

\displaystyle\|S+T\|\leq\max\{\|S\|,\|T\|\}+\frac{1}{2}\left(\left\||S|^{t}|T|^{1-t}\right\|+\left\||S^{*}|^{1-t}|T^{*}|^{t}\right\|\right),

(1.5)

in which
S,T\in{\mathbb{B}}({\mathscr{H}})
and
t\in[0,1]
.
In , the authors showed that a generalization of inequality as follows:

\displaystyle\|S+T\|\leq\max\{\|S\|,\|T\|\}+\frac{1}{2}\Big(\big\|f(|S|)g(|T|)\big\|+\big\|f(|S^{\ast}|)g(|T^{\ast}|)\big\|\Big),

in which
S,T\in{\mathbb{B}}({\mathscr{H}})
and
f,g
be two non-negative non-decreasing continuous functions on
[0,\infty)
such that
f(x)g(x)=x\,\,(x\geq 0)
.
Recently, Shi et al. proved the following inequality

\displaystyle\left\|S+T\right\|\leq\frac{1}{2}(\left\|S\right\|+\left\|T\right\|)+\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{(\left\|S\right\|-\left\|T\right\|)^{2}+\left\|\frac{1}{t}|S|^{r}|T|^{s}+t|S|^{1-r}|T|^{1-s}\right\|^{2}}

(1.6)

for normal operators
S,T\in{\mathbb{B}}({\mathscr{H}})
,
r,s\in[0,1]
and
t>0
.

In this paper, we give several further norm inequalities for sums of bounded linear operators. These inequalities refine and generalize inequalities. Moreover, as another application, we show a new numerical radius inequality which is a generalization.

If

Theorem 2.3
.

Let
S,T\in{\mathbb{B}}({\mathscr{H}})
be normal and
f_{1},f_{2},g_{1},g_{2}

continuous functions on
[0,\infty)

f_{1}(x)f_{2}(x)=x

\displaystyle\left\|S+T\right\|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}(\left\|S\right\|+\left\|T\right\|)+\frac{1}{2}\min_{t>0}\sqrt{(\left\|S\right\|-\left\|T\right\|)^{2}+\left\|\frac{1}{t}f_{1}(|S|)g_{1}(|T|)+tf_{2}(|S|)g_{2}(|T|)\right\|^{2}}.

Proof.

Let
S
and
T
be positive operators. Then for all
t>0
we have

\displaystyle\left\|S+T\right\|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left\|\left[\begin{array}[]{cc}S+T&0\\
0&0\end{array}\right]\right\|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left\|\left[\begin{array}[]{cc}tf_{2}(S)&g_{1}(T)\\
0&0\end{array}\right]\left[\begin{array}[]{cc}\frac{1}{t}f_{1}(S)&0\\
g_{2}(T)&0\end{array}\right]\right\|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\left\|\Re\left(\left[\begin{array}[]{cc}\frac{1}{t}f_{1}(S)&0\\
g_{2}(T)&0\end{array}\right]\left[\begin{array}[]{cc}tf_{2}(S)&g_{1}(T)\\
0&0\end{array}\right]\right)\right\|

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad(\textrm{by Lemma })

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left\|\left[\begin{array}[]{cc}S&\frac{1}{2}(\frac{1}{t}f_{1}(S)g_{1}(T)+tf_{2}(S)g_{2}(T))\\
\frac{1}{2}(tf_{2}(S)g_{2}(T)+\frac{1}{t}f_{1}(S)g_{1}(T))&T\end{array}\right]\right\|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\left\|\left[\begin{array}[]{cc}\|S\|&\frac{1}{2}\|\frac{1}{t}f_{1}(S)g_{1}(T)+tf_{2}(S)g_{2}(T)\|\\
\frac{1}{2}\|tf_{2}(S)g_{2}(T)+\frac{1}{t}f_{1}(S)g_{1}(T)\|&\|T\|\end{array}\right]\right\|

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad(\textrm{by Lemma })

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle r\left(\left[\begin{array}[]{cc}\|S\|&\frac{1}{2}\|\frac{1}{t}f_{1}(S)g_{1}(T)+tf_{2}(S)g_{2}(T)\|\\
\frac{1}{2}\|\frac{1}{t}f_{2}(S)g_{2}(T)+tf_{1}(S)g_{1}(T)\|&\|T\|\end{array}\right]\right)

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad(\textrm{since is Hermitian matrix})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}(\left\|S\right\|+\left\|T\right\|)+\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{(\left\|S\right\|-\left\|T\right\|)^{2}+\left\|\frac{1}{t}f_{1}(|S|)g_{1}(|T|)+tf_{2}(|S|)g_{2}(|T|)\right\|^{2}},

whence

\displaystyle\left\|S+T\right\|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}(\left\|S\right\|+\left\|T\right\|)+\frac{1}{2}\min_{t>0}\sqrt{(\left\|S\right\|-\left\|T\right\|)^{2}+\left\|\frac{1}{t}f_{1}(|S|)g_{1}(|T|)+tf_{2}(|S|)g_{2}(|T|)\right\|^{2}}

for positive operators
S
and
T
. Now, if
S
and
T
are normal operators, then by using inequality we have

\displaystyle\left\|S+T\right\|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\left\||S|+|T|\right\|\qquad(\textrm{by inequality })

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}(\left\||S|\right\|+\left\||T|\right\|)+\frac{1}{2}\min_{t>0}\sqrt{(\left\||S|\right\|-\left\||T|\right\|)^{2}+\left\|\frac{1}{t}f_{1}(|S|)g_{1}(|T|)+tf_{2}(|S|)g_{2}(|T|)\right\|}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}(\left\|S\right\|+\left\|T\right\|)+\frac{1}{2}\min_{t>0}\sqrt{(\left\|S\right\|-\left\|T\right\|)^{2}+\left\|\frac{1}{t}f_{1}(|S|)g_{1}(|T|)+tf_{2}(|S|)g_{2}(|T|)\right\|}.

∎

Corollary 2.4
.

Let
S,T\in{\mathbb{B}}({\mathscr{H}})
be normal,
f_{1},f_{2},g_{1},g_{2}

[0,\infty)

and

\displaystyle\left\|S+T\right\|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}(\left\|S\right\|+\left\|T\right\|)+\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{(\left\|S\right\|-\left\|T\right\|)^{2}+4\left\|f_{1}(|S|)g_{1}(|T|)\right\|\left\|f_{2}(|S|)g_{2}(|T|)\right\|}.

Proof.

If
S
and
T
are normal operators, then

\displaystyle\left\|S+T\right\|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}(\left\|S\right\|+\left\|T\right\|)+\frac{1}{2}\min_{t>0}\sqrt{(\left\|S\right\|-\left\|T\right\|)^{2}+\left\|\frac{1}{t}f_{1}(|S|)g_{1}(|T|)+tf_{2}(|S|)g_{2}(|T|)\right\|^{2}}

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}(\left\|S\right\|+\left\|T\right\|)+\frac{1}{2}\min_{t>0}\sqrt{(\left\|S\right\|-\left\|T\right\|)^{2}+\left(\frac{1}{t}\left\|f_{1}(|S|)g_{1}(|T|)\right\|+t\left\|f_{2}(|S|)g_{2}(|T|)\right\|\right)^{2}}

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}(\left\|S\right\|+\left\|T\right\|)+\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{(\left\|S\right\|-\left\|T\right\|)^{2}+4\left\|f_{1}(|S|)g_{1}(|T|)\right\|\left\|f_{2}(|S|)g_{2}(|T|)\right\|}.

The last equation follows from
\min_{t>0}h(t)=\min_{t>0}(\frac{1}{t}a+tb)=2\sqrt{ab}\,\,(a,b\geq 0)
.

For invertible normal operators we get the next result.

Corollary 2.5
.

Let
S,T\in{\mathbb{B}}({\mathscr{H}})
be invertible normal and
f,g
be two positive
continuous functions on
[0,\infty)
. Then

\displaystyle\left\|S+T\right\|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}(\left\|S\right\|+\left\|T\right\|)+\frac{1}{2}\min_{t>0}\sqrt{(\left\|S\right\|-\left\|T\right\|)^{2}+\left\|\frac{1}{t}f(|S|)g(|T|)+t|S|(f(|S|))^{-1}|T|(g(|T|))^{-1}\right\|^{2}}.

In particular,

\displaystyle\left\|S+T\right\|\leq\frac{1}{2}(\left\|S\right\|+\left\|T\right\|)+\frac{1}{2}\min_{t>0}\sqrt{(\left\|S\right\|-\left\|T\right\|)^{2}+\left\|\frac{1}{t}|S|^{r}|T|^{s}+t|S|^{1-r}|T|^{1-s}\right\|^{2}},

where
r,s\in\mathbb{R}
.

Proof.

For the first inequality, it is enough to put
f_{1}(t)=f(t)
,
f_{2}(t)=\frac{t}{f(t)}
,
g_{1}(t)=g(t)
and
g_{2}(t)=\frac{t}{g(t)}\,\,(t>0)
in Theorem . In particular, if
f(t)=t^{r}
and
g(t)=t^{s}\,\,(r,s\in\mathbb{R})
we get the second inequality.
∎

Corollary 2.6
.

Let
S,T\in{\mathbb{B}}({\mathscr{H}})
be invertible normal and
f,g
be two positive
continuous functions on
[0,\infty)
. Then

\displaystyle\left\|S+T\right\|\leq\max\{\left\|S\right\|+\left\|T\right\|\}+\left\|f(|S|)g(|T|)\right\|^{\frac{1}{2}}\left\||S|(f(|S|))^{-1}|T|(g(|T|))^{-1}\right\|^{\frac{1}{2}}.

In particular,

\displaystyle\left\|S+T\right\|\leq\max\{\left\|S\right\|,\left\|T\right\|\}+\left\||S|^{r}|T|^{1-s}\right\|^{\frac{1}{2}}\left\||S|^{1-r}|T|^{s}\right\|^{\frac{1}{2}},

(2.8)

where
r,s\in\mathbb{R}
.

Proof.

If
S,T\in{\mathbb{B}}({\mathscr{H}})
is invertible normal and
f,g
are two positive
continuous functions on
[0,\infty)
, then by using Theorem we have

\displaystyle\left\|S+T\right\|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}(\left\|S\right\|+\left\|T\right\|)+\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{(\left\|S\right\|-\left\|T\right\|)^{2}+\left\|\frac{1}{t}f(|S|)g(|T|)+t|S|(f(|S|))^{-1}|T|(g(|T|))^{-1}\right\|^{2}}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}(\left\|S\right\|+\left\|T\right\|)+\frac{1}{2}|\left\|S\right\|-\left\|T\right\||+\frac{1}{2}\left\|\frac{1}{t}f(|S|)g(|T|)+t|S|(f(|S|))^{-1}|T|(g(|T|))^{-1}\right\|

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad(\textrm{by the inequality}\,\,\sqrt{x^{2}+y^{2}}\leq\sqrt{x^{2}}+\sqrt{y^{2}})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\max\{\|S\|,\|T\|\}+\left\|\frac{1}{t}f(|S|)g(|T|)+t|S|(f(|S|))^{-1}|T|(g(|T|))^{-1}\right\|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\max\{\|S\|,\|T\|\}+\frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{1}{t}\left\|f(|S|)g(|T|)\right\|+t\left\||S|(f(|S|))^{-1}|T|(g(|T|))^{-1}\right\|\right).

Now, we take the minimum on

and we get

\displaystyle\left\|S+T\right\|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\max\{\|S\|,\|T\|\}+\frac{1}{2}\min_{t>0}\left(\frac{1}{t}\left\|f(|S|)g(|T|)\right\|+t\left\||S|(f(|S|))^{-1}|T|(g(|T|))^{-1}\right\|\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\max\{\left\|S\right\|,\left\|T\right\|\}+\left\|f(|S|)g(|T|)\right\|^{\frac{1}{2}}\left\||S|(f(|S|))^{-1}|T|(g(|T|))^{-1}\right\|^{\frac{1}{2}}.

For the second inequality, put
f(t)=t^{r}
and
g(t)=t^{1-s}\,\,(r,s\in\mathbb{R})
in the first inequality.
∎

Remark 2.7
.

If
S,T\in{\mathbb{B}}({\mathscr{H}})
are normal operator, then inequality is a refinement and generalization of inequality for normal operators. In fact, in this case

\displaystyle\left\|S+T\right\|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\max\{\left\|S\right\|,\left\|T\right\|\}+\left\||S|^{r}|T|^{1-s}\right\|^{\frac{1}{2}}\left\||S|^{1-r}|T|^{s}\right\|^{\frac{1}{2}}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\max\{\left\|S\right\|,\left\|T\right\|\}+\frac{1}{2}\left(\left\||S|^{r}|T|^{1-s}\right\|+\left\||S|^{1-r}|T|^{s}\right\|\right)

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad(\textrm{by the arithmetic-geometric inequality}),

where
r,s\in[0,1]

In the next result, we show a generalization of the inequality for arbitrary operators in
S,T\in{\mathbb{B}}({\mathscr{H}})

Theorem 2.8
.

Let
S,T\in{\mathbb{B}}({\mathscr{H}})
and
f_{1},f_{2},g_{1},g_{2}
be non-negative
continuous functions on
[0,\infty)
, in which
f_{1}(x)f_{2}(x)=x
and
g_{1}(x)g_{2}(x)=x\,\,(x\geq 0)
. Then

\displaystyle\left\|S+T\right\|\leq\frac{1}{2}(\left\|S\right\|+\left\|T\right\|)+\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{(\left\|S\right\|-\left\|T\right\|)^{2}+\max\{\alpha,\beta\}},

where
\alpha=\left\|\frac{1}{t}f_{1}(|S^{*}|)g_{1}(|T^{*}|)+tf_{2}(|S^{*}|)g_{2}(|T^{*}|)\right\|^{2}
and
\beta=\left\|\frac{1}{t}f_{1}(|S|)g_{1}(|T|)+tf_{2}(|S|)g_{2}(|T|)\right\|^{2}
.

Proof.

Let
\tilde{S}=\left[\begin{array}[]{cc}0&S\\
S^{*}&0\end{array}\right]

and

\tilde{T}=\left[\begin{array}[]{cc}0&T\\
T^{*}&0\end{array}\right]
. Then
\tilde{S}
and
\tilde{T}
are normal operators and

\|\tilde{S}+\tilde{T}\|=\left\|\left[\begin{array}[]{cc}0&S+T\\
S^{*}+S^{*}&0\end{array}\right]\right\|=\|S+T\|.

Hence, applying Theorem we get

\displaystyle\left\|S+T\right\|=\|\tilde{S}+\tilde{T}\|

(2.9)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}(\|\tilde{S}\|+\|\tilde{T}\|)+\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{(\|\tilde{S}\|-\|\tilde{T}\|)^{2}+\left\|\frac{1}{t}f_{1}(|\tilde{S}|)g_{1}(|\tilde{T}|)+tf_{2}(|\tilde{S}|)g_{2}(|\tilde{T}|)\right\|^{2}}.

Moreover, it follows from
\|\tilde{S}\|=\|S\|
,
\|\tilde{T}\|=\|T\|
and

f(|\tilde{S}|)=\left[\begin{array}[]{cc}f(|S^{*}|)&0\\
0&f(|S|)\end{array}\right],\quad f(|\tilde{T}|)=\left[\begin{array}[]{cc}f(|T^{*}|)&0\\
0&f(|T|)\end{array}\right]

that we get

\displaystyle\left\|\frac{1}{t}f_{1}(|\tilde{S}|)g_{1}(|\tilde{T}|)+tf_{2}(|\tilde{S}|)g_{2}(|\tilde{T}|)\right\|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left\|\left[\begin{array}[]{cc}\frac{1}{t}f_{1}(|{S^{*}}|)g_{1}(|{T^{*}}|)+tf_{2}(|{S^{*}}|)g_{2}(|{T^{*}}|)&0\\
0&\frac{1}{t}f_{1}(|{S}|)g_{1}(|{T}|)+tf_{2}(|{S}|)g_{2}(|{T}|)\end{array}\right]\right\|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\max\left\{\left\|\frac{1}{t}f_{1}(|{S^{*}}|)g_{1}(|{T^{*}}|)+tf_{2}(|{S^{*}}|)g_{2}(|{T^{*}}|)\right\|,\left\|\frac{1}{t}f_{1}(|{S}|)g_{1}(|{T}|)+tf_{2}(|{S}|)g_{2}(|{T}|)\right\|\right\}.

Using the recent equality and inequality we reach the desired result.
∎

3.
Some results for the numerical radius

In this section, as application of norm inequalities for sums of operators, we present some inequalities for the numerical radius.

Theorem 3.1
.

Let
S,T\in{\mathbb{B}}({\mathscr{H}})
. Then

\displaystyle w(S-T)

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\max\{2w(S),2w(T)\}-\max\left\{\left\|S\right\|,\left\|T\right\|\right\}

\displaystyle-\max\left\{\||S^{*}|^{r}|T^{*}|^{1-s}\|^{\frac{1}{2}}\||S^{*}|^{1-r}|T^{*}|^{s}\|^{\frac{1}{2}},\||S|^{r}|T|^{1-s}\|^{\frac{1}{2}}\||S|^{1-r}|T|^{s}\|^{\frac{1}{2}}\right\},

where
r,s\in[0,1]
.

Proof.

If
S,T\in{\mathbb{B}}({\mathscr{H}})
, then

\displaystyle 2\max\{w(S),w(T)\}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle w\left(\left[\begin{array}[]{cc}S&0\\
0&T\end{array}\right]\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle w\left(\left[\begin{array}[]{cc}S+T&0\\
0&T+S\end{array}\right]+\left[\begin{array}[]{cc}S-T&0\\
0&T-S\end{array}\right]\right)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle w\left(\left[\begin{array}[]{cc}S+T&0\\
0&T+S\end{array}\right]\right)+w\left(\left[\begin{array}[]{cc}S-T&0\\
0&T-S\end{array}\right]\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle w(S+T)+w(S-T),

whence

\displaystyle w(S-T)\geq 2\max\{w(S),w(T)\}-w(S+T).

It follows from
w(S+T)\leq\|S+T\|
and Corollary that

\displaystyle w(S-T)

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\max\{2w(S),2w(T)\}-w(S+T)

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\max\{2w(S),2w(T)\}-\|S+T\|

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\max\{2w(S),2w(T)\}-\max\left\{\left\|S\right\|,\left\|T\right\|\right\}

\displaystyle-\max\left\{\||S^{*}|^{r}|T^{*}|^{1-s}\|^{\frac{1}{2}}\||S^{*}|^{1-r}|T^{*}|^{s}\|^{\frac{1}{2}},\||S|^{r}|T|^{1-s}\|^{\frac{1}{2}}\||S|^{1-r}|T|^{s}\|^{\frac{1}{2}}\right\}

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad(\textrm{by Corollary }),

where
r,s\in[0,1]
.
∎

Remark 3.2
.

If
S,T\in{\mathbb{B}}({\mathscr{H}})
are normal operator, then
|S|=|S^{*}|
,
|T|=|T^{*}|
,
w(S)=\|S\|
and
w(T)=\|T\|
. This concludes that Theorem appear as

\displaystyle\|S-T\|\geq\max\left\{\left\|S\right\|,\left\|T\right\|\right\}-\||S|^{r}|T|^{1-s}\|^{\frac{1}{2}}\||S|^{1-r}|T|^{s}\|^{\frac{1}{2}}.

In particular, if
S
and
T
are positive, then for
r=s=\frac{1}{2}
we have

\displaystyle\|S-T\|\geq\max\left\{\left\|S\right\|,\left\|T\right\|\right\}-\|S^{\frac{1}{2}}T^{\frac{1}{2}}\|.

In the next result, we obtain an upper bound for the numerical radius.

Theorem 3.3
.

Let
S\in{\mathbb{B}}({\mathscr{H}})
and
f_{1},f_{2},g_{1},g_{2}
be non-negative
continuous functions on
[0,\infty)
, in which
f_{1}(x)f_{2}(x)=x
and
g_{1}(x)g_{2}(x)=x\,\,(x\geq 0)
. Then

\displaystyle w(S)\leq\frac{1}{2}\left\|S\right\|+\frac{1}{4}\max\{\alpha,\beta\},

in which
\alpha=\left\|f_{1}(|S^{*}|)g_{1}(|S|)+f_{2}(|S^{*}|)g_{2}(|S|)\right\|
and
\beta=\left\|f_{1}(|S|)g_{1}(|S^{*}|)+f_{2}(|S|)g_{2}(|S^{*}|)\right\|
.
In particular,

\displaystyle w(S)\leq\frac{1}{2}\|S\|+\frac{1}{4}\left\|f_{1}(|S^{*}|)f_{2}(|S|)+f_{2}(|S^{*}|)f_{1}(|S|)\right\|.

Proof.

Using the of
w(\cdot)
and Theorem for
t=1
we have

\displaystyle w(S)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sup_{\theta\in\mathbb{R}}\|\Re({{\rm{e}}^{i\theta}T})\|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\sup_{\theta\in\mathbb{R}}\|{{\rm{e}}^{i\theta}S}+{{\rm{e}}^{-i\theta}S^{*}}\|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{4}(\left\|S\right\|+\left\|S^{*}\right\|)+\frac{1}{4}\max\{\alpha,\beta\}\qquad(\textrm{by Theorem })

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\left\|S\right\|+\frac{1}{4}\max\{\alpha,\beta\},

in which
\alpha=\left\|f_{1}(|S^{*}|)g_{1}(|S|)+f_{2}(|S^{*}|)g_{2}(|S|)\right\|
and
\beta=\left\|f_{1}(|S|)g_{1}(|S^{*}|)+f_{2}(|S|)g_{2}(|S^{*}|)\right\|
. In the special case for
g_{1}=f_{2}
and
g_{2}=f_{1}
we get the second inequality.
∎

Theorem 3.4
.

Let
S\in{\mathbb{B}}({\mathscr{H}})
. Then

\displaystyle w(S)\leq\max\left\{\left\|\Re S\right\|,\left\|\Im S\right\|\right\}+\frac{1}{2}\||\Re S|^{\frac{1}{2}}|\Im S|^{\frac{1}{2}}\|.

Proof.

Using inequality and the definition
w(\cdot)
we have

\displaystyle w(S)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sup_{\alpha^{2}+\beta^{2}=1}\|\alpha\Re S+\beta\Im S\|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\sup_{\alpha^{2}+\beta^{2}=1}\left(\max\left\{\left\|\alpha\Re S\right\|,\left\|\beta\Im S\right\|\right\}+\max\left\{\||\alpha\Re S|^{\frac{1}{2}}|\beta\Im S|^{\frac{1}{2}}\|,\||\alpha\Re S|^{\frac{1}{2}}|\beta\Im S|^{\frac{1}{2}}\|\right\}\right)

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad(\textrm{by inequality })

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\max\left\{\left\|\Re S\right\|,\left\|\Im S\right\|\right\}+\sup_{\alpha^{2}+\beta^{2}=1}\left(\sqrt{|\alpha\beta|}\||\Re S|^{\frac{1}{2}}|\Im S|^{\frac{1}{2}}\|\right)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\max\left\{\left\|\Re S\right\|,\left\|\Im S\right\|\right\}+\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\||\Re S|^{\frac{1}{2}}|\Im S|^{\frac{1}{2}}\|.

∎
