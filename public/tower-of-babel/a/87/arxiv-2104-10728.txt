Lax representations via twisted extensions of infinite-dimensional Lie algebras: some new results
Oleg I. Morozov

Abstract
. We find new integrable partial differential equations with Lax representations generated by extensions of Lie algebras of the Kac–Moody type as well as the Lie algebra of Hamiltonian vector fields on ℝ 2 \mathbb{R}^{2} .

Introduction

Lax representations provide the basic construction that allows applications of a number of techniques for studying nonlinear partial differential equations (
pde

–

s.

The aim of the present paper is to gain a better understanding of the relationship between the structure theory of infinite-dimensional
Lie algebras and the theory of integrable systems. In particular, we construct new examples of deriving Lax representations of

pde
s from twisted extensions of some Lie algebras.

In a number of above-mentioned examples the symmetry algebras of the

\mathfrak{s}_{\diamond}\ltimes\mathfrak{q}_{N,\varepsilon}

Let the linear space

be either

(2.4)

\quad+\sum\limits_{1\leq p<q\leq k+1}(-1)^{p+q+1}\theta([X_{p},X_{q}],X_{1},...,\hat{X}_{p},...,\hat{X}_{q},...,X_{k+1}).
