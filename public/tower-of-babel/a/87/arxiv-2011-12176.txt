Large time behavior to the FENE dumbbell model of polymeric flows near equilibrium
Zhaonan Luo, Wei Luo and, Zhaoyang Yin

Abstract
In this paper we mainly study large time behavior for the strong solutions of the finite extensible nonlinear elastic (FENE) dumbbell model. There is a lot results about the L 2 L^{2} decay rate of the co-rotation model. In this paper, we consider the general case. We prove that the optimal L 2 L^{2} decay rate of the velocity is ( 1 + t ) − d 4 (1+t)^{-\frac{d}{4}} with d ≥ 2 d\geq 2 . This result improves the previous result in [ 8 ] . 2010 Mathematics Subject Classification : 35Q30, 76B03, 76D05, 76D99. Keywords : The FENE dumbbell model; L 2 L^{2} decay rate; Fourier transform.

Introduction

In this paper we investigate the large time behavior for the finite extensible nonlinear elastic (FENE) dumbbell model
[
1
,
3
]
:

\displaystyle\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}u_{t}+(u\cdot\nabla)u-\Delta u+\nabla{P}=div~\tau,~~~~~~~div~u=0,\\[4.30554pt]
\psi_{t}+(u\cdot\nabla)\psi=div_{R}[-\sigma(u)\cdot{R}\psi+\beta\nabla_{R}\psi+\nabla_{R}\mathcal{U}\psi],\\[4.30554pt]
\tau_{ij}=\int_{B}(R_{i}\nabla_{R_{j}}\mathcal{U})\psi dR,\\[4.30554pt]
u|_{t=0}=u_{0},~\psi|_{t=0}=\psi_{0},\\[4.30554pt]
(\beta\nabla_{R}\psi+\nabla_{R}\mathcal{U}\psi)\cdot{n}=0~~~~\text{on}~~~~\partial B(0,R_{0}).\\[4.30554pt]
\end{array}\right.

In
1

stands for the velocity of the polymeric liquid. The polymer particles are described by the distribution function

is the drag term. In general,
\sigma(u)=\nabla u
. For the co-rotation case,
\sigma(u)=\frac{\nabla u-(\nabla u)^{T}}{2}

In the paper we will take
\sigma(u)=\nabla u
,

and

Notice that
(u,\psi)
with

\psi_{\infty}(R)=\frac{e^{-\mathcal{U}(R)}}{\int_{B}e^{-\mathcal{U}(R)}dR}=\frac{(1-|R|^{2})^{k}}{\int_{B}(1-|R|^{2})^{k}dR},

is a trivial solution of
1
.
Let
\tilde{\psi}=\psi-\psi_{\infty}

1

\displaystyle\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}u_{t}+(u\cdot\nabla)u-\Delta u+\nabla{P}=div~\tau,~~~~~~~div~u=0,\\[4.30554pt]
\tilde{\psi}_{t}+u\cdot\nabla\tilde{\psi}+\mathcal{L}(\tilde{\psi})=div_{R}(-\nabla u\cdot{R}\tilde{\psi})+\psi_{\infty}\nabla u\cdot{R}\nabla_{R}\mathcal{U},\\[4.30554pt]
\tau_{ij}=\int_{B}(R_{i}\nabla_{R_{j}}\mathcal{U})\tilde{\psi}dR,\\[4.30554pt]
u|_{t=0}=u_{0},~\tilde{\psi}|_{t=0}=\psi_{0}-\psi_{\infty},\\[4.30554pt]
\psi_{\infty}\nabla_{R}\frac{\tilde{\psi}}{\psi_{\infty}}\cdot{n}=0~~~~\text{on}~~~~\partial B(0,1),\\[4.30554pt]
\end{array}\right.

where
\mathcal{L}(\tilde{\psi})=-div_{R}(\psi_{\infty}\nabla_{R}\frac{\tilde{\psi}}{\psi_{\infty}})

Multiplying

\displaystyle\leq C(1+t)^{-\frac{d}{2}}\int_{0}^{t}\|u\|^{2}_{L^{2}}\|\nabla\tilde{\psi}\|^{2}_{L^{2}(\mathcal{L}^{2})}ds

\displaystyle\leq C\int_{S(t)}d\xi\int_{0}^{t}\int_{B}\|\psi_{\infty}|F(\nabla u\cdot{R}\frac{\tilde{\psi}}{\psi_{\infty}})|^{2}\|_{L^{\infty}}dRds

\displaystyle\leq C(1+t)^{-\frac{d}{2}}\int_{0}^{t}\|\nabla u\|^{2}_{L^{2}}\|\tilde{\psi}\|^{2}_{L^{2}(\mathcal{L}^{2})}ds

\displaystyle\leq C(1+t)^{-\frac{d}{2}}.

\displaystyle\int_{S(t)}|\hat{u}(t,\xi)|^{2}d\xi\leq C(1+t)^{-\frac{d}{2}+1}.

) and using standard energy estimation, we have

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\frac{d}{dt}(\|\nabla^{\alpha}u\|^{2}_{L^{2}}+\|\nabla^{\alpha}\tilde{\psi}\|^{2}_{L^{2}(\mathcal{L}^{2})})+\|\nabla\nabla^{\alpha}u\|^{2}_{L^{2}}+\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\int_{B}\psi_{\infty}|\nabla_{R}\frac{\nabla^{\alpha}\tilde{\psi}}{\psi_{\infty}}|^{2}dRdx

\displaystyle=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\int_{B}\nabla^{\alpha}\nabla_{R}\cdot(\nabla uR\tilde{\psi})\frac{\nabla^{\alpha}\tilde{\psi}}{\psi_{\infty}}dRdx+\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}(\nabla^{\alpha}u\cdot\nabla)u\cdot\nabla^{\alpha}udx+\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\int_{B}(\nabla^{\alpha}u\cdot\nabla)\tilde{\psi}\nabla^{\alpha}\tilde{\psi}dRdx.

Integrating by part and using Lemma (
2.2
), Theorem (
2.4
), we get

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\int_{B}\nabla^{\alpha}\nabla_{R}\cdot(\nabla uR\tilde{\psi})\frac{\nabla^{\alpha}\tilde{\psi}}{\psi_{\infty}}dRdx=-\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\int_{B}\nabla^{\alpha}(\nabla uR\tilde{\psi})\nabla_{R}\frac{\nabla^{\alpha}\tilde{\psi}}{\psi_{\infty}}dRdx

\displaystyle\leq C(\|\nabla\nabla^{\alpha}u\|_{L^{2}}\|\tilde{\psi}\|_{L^{\infty}(\mathcal{L}^{2})})(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\int_{B}\psi_{\infty}|\nabla_{R}\frac{\nabla^{\alpha}\tilde{\psi}}{\psi_{\infty}}|^{2}dRdx)^{\frac{1}{2}}

\displaystyle+\|\nabla u\|_{L^{\infty}}\|\nabla^{\alpha}\tilde{\psi}\|_{L^{2}(\mathcal{L}^{2})})(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\int_{B}\psi_{\infty}|\nabla_{R}\frac{\nabla^{\alpha}\tilde{\psi}}{\psi_{\infty}}|^{2}dRdx)^{\frac{1}{2}})

\displaystyle\leq C{\varepsilon}_{0}(\|\nabla\nabla^{\alpha}u\|^{2}_{L^{2}}+\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\int_{B}\psi_{\infty}|\nabla_{R}\frac{\nabla^{\alpha}\tilde{\psi}}{\psi_{\infty}}|^{2}dRdx).

Using Lemma (
2.1
) with
p=3
and Theorem (
2.4
), we have

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{d}}(\nabla^{\alpha}u\cdot\nabla)u\cdot\nabla^{\alpha}udx\leq C\|\nabla u\|^{3}_{L^{3}}\leq C\|\nabla u\|^{2}_{L^{2}}\|\nabla^{2}u\|_{L^{2}}\leq C{\varepsilon}_{0}\|\nabla^{2}u\|^{2}_{L^{2}}.

Using Lemma (
2.1
), Lemma (
2.2
) and Theorem (
2.4
), we get

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\int_{B}(\nabla^{\alpha}u\cdot\nabla)\tilde{\psi}\nabla^{\alpha}\tilde{\psi}dRdx

\displaystyle\leq\|\nabla\tilde{\psi}\|_{L^{2}(\mathcal{L}^{2})}\|\nabla^{\alpha}u\|_{L^{\infty}}\|\nabla^{\alpha}\tilde{\psi}\|^{2}_{L^{2}(\mathcal{L}^{2})}

\displaystyle\leq C{\varepsilon}_{0}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\int_{B}\psi_{\infty}|\nabla_{R}\frac{\nabla\tilde{\psi}}{\psi_{\infty}}|^{2}dRdx.

Summing up all the estimates for
|\alpha|=1
, we deduce that

(3.43)

\displaystyle\frac{d}{dt}(\|\nabla u\|^{2}_{L^{2}}+\|\nabla\tilde{\psi}\|^{2}_{L^{2}(\mathcal{L}^{2})})+\|\nabla^{2}u\|^{2}_{L^{2}}+\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\int_{B}\psi_{\infty}|\nabla_{R}\frac{\nabla\tilde{\psi}}{\psi_{\infty}}|^{2}dRdx\leq 0,

which implies that

(3.44)

\displaystyle\frac{d}{dt}(\|\nabla u\|^{2}_{L^{2}}+\|\nabla\tilde{\psi}\|^{2}_{L^{2}(\mathcal{L}^{2})})+\frac{3}{1+t}\|\nabla u\|^{2}_{L^{2}}+\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\int_{B}\psi_{\infty}|\nabla_{R}\frac{\nabla\tilde{\psi}}{\psi_{\infty}}|^{2}dRdx\leq\frac{3}{1+t}\int_{S(t)}|\xi|^{2}|\hat{u}(\xi)|^{2}d\xi.

According to (
3.41
), we have

\displaystyle\frac{3}{1+t}\int_{S(t)}|\xi|^{2}|\hat{u}(\xi)|^{2}d\xi\leq 9(1+t)^{-2}\|u\|^{2}_{L^{2}}\leq C(1+t)^{-3}.

This together with (
3.44
) ensures that

(3.45)

\displaystyle\|\nabla u\|_{L^{2}}+\|\nabla\tilde{\psi}\|_{L^{2}(\mathcal{L}^{2})}\leq C(1+t)^{-1}.

Using standard energy estimation to the second equation in (
1
), we get

\displaystyle\frac{d}{dt}\|\tilde{\psi}\|^{2}_{L^{2}(\mathcal{L}^{2})}+2\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\int_{B}\psi_{\infty}|\nabla_{R}\frac{\tilde{\psi}}{\psi_{\infty}}|^{2}dRdx

\displaystyle=2\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\int_{B}\nabla_{R}\cdot(\nabla uR\tilde{\psi})\frac{\tilde{\psi}}{\psi_{\infty}}dRdx+2\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\int_{B}\nabla u\cdot{R}\nabla_{R}\mathcal{U}\tilde{\psi}dRdx.

Using Lemma (
2.2
), Lemma (
2.3
) and Theorem (
2.4
), we have

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\int_{B}\nabla_{R}\cdot(\nabla uR\tilde{\psi})\frac{\tilde{\psi}}{\psi_{\infty}}dRdx=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\int_{B}\psi_{\infty}\nabla uR\frac{\tilde{\psi}}{\psi_{\infty}}\nabla_{R}\frac{\tilde{\psi}}{\psi_{\infty}}dRdx\leq C{\varepsilon}_{0}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\int_{B}\psi_{\infty}|\nabla_{R}\frac{\tilde{\psi}}{\psi_{\infty}}|^{2}dRdx

and

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\int_{B}\nabla u\cdot{R}\nabla_{R}\mathcal{U}\tilde{\psi}dRdx=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\nabla u\cdot\tau dx\leq\delta\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\int_{B}\psi_{\infty}|\nabla_{R}\frac{\tilde{\psi}}{\psi_{\infty}}|^{2}dRdx+C_{\delta}\|\nabla u\|^{2}_{L^{2}}.

Using Lemma (
2.2
), we obtain

\displaystyle\frac{d}{dt}\|\tilde{\psi}\|^{2}_{L^{2}(\mathcal{L}^{2})}+\|\tilde{\psi}\|^{2}_{L^{2}(\mathcal{L}^{2})}\leq C_{\delta}\|\nabla u\|^{2}_{L^{2}}.

This together with (
3.45
) implies that

\displaystyle\|\tilde{\psi}\|^{2}_{L^{2}(\mathcal{L}^{2})}

\displaystyle\leq\|\tilde{\psi}_{0}\|^{2}_{L^{2}(\mathcal{L}^{2})}e^{-t}+C\int_{0}^{t}e^{-(t-s)}\|\nabla u\|^{2}_{L^{2}}ds

\displaystyle\leq C(e^{-t}+\int_{0}^{t}e^{-(t-s)}(1+s)^{-2}ds)

\displaystyle\leq C(1+t)^{-2},

which means that we prove the claim (
3.42
).
We thus complete the proof of Proposition (
3.3
).
∎

By (
3.42
), Lemma (
2.1
) and Proposition (
3.3
), we obtain the following corollary.

Corollary 3.4
.

Under the assumption of Theorem (
2.5
), for
2\leq p<\infty
, we have

(3.46)

\displaystyle\|u\|_{L^{p}}\leq C(1+t)^{-(1-\frac{1}{p})},~~\|\tilde{\psi}\|_{L^{p}(\mathcal{L}^{2})}\leq C(1+t)^{-1}.
