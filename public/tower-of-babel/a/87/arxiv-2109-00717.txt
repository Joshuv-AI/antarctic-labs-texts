Units of integral group rings of cyclic 2 2 -groups
Rifkhat Zh. Aleeev, Olga V. Mitina, Aleksandra D. Godova

Abstract
This paper is devoted to the units of integral group rings of cyclic 2 2 -groups of small orders, namely, the orders of 2 n 2^{n} for n ⩽ 7 n\leqslant 7 . Immediately we should note the issues our consideration describe in the introduction in more detail. Here we will indicate the main directions of our research. Previously, we proved that the normalized group of units of an integral group ring of a cyclic 2 2 -group of order 2 n 2^{n} contains a subgroup of finite index, which is the direct product of the subgroup of units defined by the character with the largest character field and the subgroup of units that is isomorphic to the subgroup of units of the integer group ring of the cyclic 2 2 -group of order 2 n − 1 2^{n-1} . Because of this, it is very important to study the structure of the subgroup of units defined by the character with the largest field of characters, which is the cyclotomic field 𝐐 2 n \mathbf{Q}_{2^{n}} obtained by adjoining a primitive 2 n 2^{n} th root of unity to 𝐐 \mathbf{Q} , the field of rational number. That subgroup of units of an integral group ring of a cyclic 2 2 -group is isomorphic to the subgroup of the group of units of the integer ring of the specified cyclotomic field. Therefore, the research of units of an integer group ring of a cyclic 2 2 -group is reduced to study the properties of the group of units of the integer ring of the cyclotomic field 𝐐 2 n \mathbf{Q}_{2^{n}} . In general, the group of units of the ring of integers of the circular field 𝐐 2 n \mathbf{Q}_{2^{n}} is not known completely. However, the fundamental paper of Sinnott allows us to always find a subgroup of circular units of a finite index in this group of units, and this index is equal to the class number of the maximum real subfield of the field 𝐐 2 n \mathbf{Q}_{2^{n}} . The classical Weber class number problem assumes the class number is equal to 1 1 , and this would give a coincidence of the subgroup of circular units and the group of all units of the integer ring of the cyclotomic field 𝐐 2 n \mathbf{Q}_{2^{n}} . As far as we know, the Weber problem is solved positively for all 2 n ⩽ 256 2^{n}\leqslant 256 , and under the generalized Riemann hypothesis for 2 n ⩽ 512 2^{n}\leqslant 512 . Thus, we will study of groups of circular units of integer rings of cyclotomic fields 𝐐 2 n \mathbf{Q}_{2^{n}} in large part. First of all, we note one significant point. In previous works of the authors (Aleev and Mitina) & co-authors, it was used 5 5 generates a unit subgroup of the index 2 2 modulo 2 n 2^{n} . As it turned out, Gauss noted 5 5 can be replaced by 3 3 , that is 3 3 generates a unut subgroup of the index 2 2 modulo 2 n 2^{n} . This made it possible to replace the generators of groups of circular units by simpler ones. However, this also led to the need to revise a rather extensive list of previously obtained results. Since some of the results obtained earlier were proved by complicated calculations, it was impossible to simply say they are obtained similarly. This transfer of the results took up quite a lot of space in this work. More precisely, the cases of n ∈ { 0 , 1 , 2 } n\in\{0,1,2\} are trivial, the case of n = 3 n=3 is well known, besides, in this case there is no difference what to consider 3 3 or 5 5 . For 5 5 the cases of n ∈ { 4 , 5 , 6 } n\in\{4,5,6\} were fully studied, so they were necessary to review these cases. Also, many results were obtained for an arbitrary n n , they needed to be revised also. Let’s summarize the results. We can divide our work into the following parts. Preliminary information. This is described in section 2. There we introduce three number sequences of cyclotomic fields play an essential role in further considerations. In particular, one of these sequences allows us to describe circular units. Circular units. This is the subject of section 3. First, we consider the reduction of unit group of circular unit group. Then we obtain a description of circular unit group by one of the sequences introduced earlier. Properties of units of group rings. More precisely, in section 4 we produce certain units of an integral group ring of a cyclic group of order 2 n 2^{n} . These units are determined by a character whose character field is a cyclotomic field 𝐐 2 n \mathbf{Q}_{2^{n}} . Due to the standard isomorphism, we reduce the study of such units to the study of the units of the integer ring of the cyclotomic field 𝐐 2 n \mathbf{Q}_{2^{n}} . Namely, we prove necessary to study the units of the integer ring of the cyclotomic field 𝐐 2 n \mathbf{Q}_{2^{n}} congruent to 1 1 modulo 2 2 . Congruence of circular units. In sections 5–7 we study the congruence of circular units modulo 2 2 , which plays an important role in finding the units of integral group rings of cyclic 2 2 -groups. More specifically, in section 5 a subgroup of circular units is constructed, which claims to describe all circular units that congruent to 1 1 modulo 2 2 . In section 6 we introduce a subgroup of circular units whose quotient group is an elementary Abelian 2 2 -group by the subgroup from section 5. Section 7 is devoted to proving the coincidence of subgroups from sections 5 and 6, which can be done for 2 n ⩽ 128 2^{n}\leqslant 128 . As noted earlier, Weber’s problem about the number of classes for such cases has a positive solution. Therefore, for 2 n ⩽ 128 2^{n}\leqslant 128 we can state that obtain the description of all units of integer group rings of cyclic 2 2 -groups determined by the character with the character field 𝐐 2 n \mathbf{Q}_{2^{n}} .
