Spin dynamics of the E 8 E_{8} particles
Xiao Wang, Konrad Puzniak, Karin Schmalzl, C. Balz, M. Matsuda, Akira Okutani, M. Hagiwara, Jie Ma, Jianda Wu, Bella Lake

Abstract
In this article, we report on inelastic neutron scattering measurements on a quasi-1D antiferromagnet BaCo 2 V 2 O 8 under a transverse magnetic field applied along the (0,1,0) direction. Combining results of inelastic neutron scattering experiments, analytical analysis, and numerical simulations, we precisely studied the E 8 E_{8} excitations appearing in the whole Brillouin zone at B c 1 ​ D ≈ 4.7 B_{c}^{1D}\approx 4.7 T. The energy scan at Q = ( 0 , 0 , 2 ) Q=(0,0,2) reveals a match between the data and the theoretical prediction of energies of multiple E 8 E_{8} excitations. Furthermore, dispersions of the lightest three E 8 E_{8} particles have been clearly observed, confirming the existence of the E 8 E_{8} particles in BaCo 2 V 2 O 8 . Our results lay down a concrete ground to systematically study the physics of the exotic E 8 E_{8} particles.

Unlike the classical phase transition driven by thermal fluctuations, the quantum phase transition arises at zero temperature when the system is tuned by a non-thermal parameter
