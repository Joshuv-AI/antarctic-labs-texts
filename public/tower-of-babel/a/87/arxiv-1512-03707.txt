1.2
The Logarithmic Derivative of
Z(t)
and its Integral

Let
Q(t)
be the logarithmic derivative of
Z(t)
given by

\begin{array}[]{ll}Q(t)&=\frac{\dot{\mathrm{Z}}(t)}{Z(t)}\\
&=\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}\operatorname{lnZ}(t)\\
&=i\left(\frac{\dot{\zeta}\left(\frac{1}{2}+it\right)}{\zeta\left(\frac{1}{2}+it\right)}+\frac{\Psi\left(\frac{1}{4}-\frac{i}{2t}\right)+\Psi\left(\frac{1}{4}+\frac{i}{2t}\right)-2\ln(\pi)}{4}\right)\\
&=i\left(\frac{\dot{z}(t)}{z(t)}+\frac{g^{-}(t)+g^{+}(t)-2\ln(\pi)}{4}\right)\end{array}

(10)

where

\Psi(x)=\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}x}\ln(\Gamma(x))=\frac{\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}x}\Gamma(x)}{\Gamma(x)}

(11)

is the digamma function, the logarithmic derivative of the
\Gamma
function
and
z(t)
and
g^{\pm}(t)
have been introduced to simplify the
expressions. The function

has singularities at
\pm\frac{i}{2}(4n-3)
with residues

\underset{t=\frac{i}{2}(4n-3)}{\operatorname{Res}(Q(t))}=\frac{-8\zeta(2-2n)n^{2}+4\zeta(2-2n)n}{16\zeta(2-2n)n^{2}-8\zeta(2-2n)n}=-\frac{1}{2}

(12)

and

\underset{t=-\frac{i}{2}(4n-3)}{\operatorname{Res}(Q(t))}=\frac{-2\zeta(2n-1)+4\zeta(2n-1)n}{8\zeta(2n-1)n-4\zeta(2n-1)}=\frac{1}{2}

(13)

Now, the integral of the logarithmic derivative of Z is defined by

\begin{array}[]{cl}R(t)&=\pi^{-1}\int Q(t)\mathrm{d}t\\
&=\pi^{-1}\int\frac{\dot{\mathrm{Z}}(t)}{Z(t)}\mathrm{d}t\\
&=\pi^{-1}\int\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}\operatorname{lnZ}(t)\mathrm{d}t\\
&=\pi^{-1}\int i\left(\frac{\dot{z}(t)}{z(t)}+\frac{g^{-}(t)+g^{+}(t)-2\ln(\pi)}{4}\right)\mathrm{d}t\\
&=\pi^{-1}\int i\left(\frac{\dot{\zeta}\left(\frac{1}{2}+\operatorname{it}\right)}{\zeta\left(\frac{1}{2}+it\right)}+\frac{\Psi\left(\frac{1}{4}-\frac{i}{2t}\right)+\Psi\left(\frac{1}{4}+\frac{i}{2t}\right)-2\ln(\pi)}{4}\right)\mathrm{d}t\\
&=\pi^{-1}\left(\ln\zeta\left(\frac{1}{2}+it\right)+i\vartheta(t)\right)\\
&=\pi^{-1}\left(\ln\zeta\left(\frac{1}{2}+it\right)+\frac{i}{2}\left(i\left(\ln\Gamma\left(\frac{1}{4}-\frac{it}{2}\right)-\ln\Gamma\left(\frac{1}{4}+\frac{it}{2}\right)\right)-\ln(\pi)t\right)\right)\end{array}

(14)
