Fibers of such bundles are given by elements of algebraic completion of the space of all formal series in complex parameters, sections are provided by rational functions with prescribed analytic properties. In this paper we introduce and study K-groups K ⁡ ( A ⁡ ( 𝔤 ) , X ) K(A(\mathfrak{g}),X) of twisted A ⁡ ( 𝔤 ) A(\mathfrak{g}) -bundles as equivalence classes [ ℰ ] [\mathcal{E}] of A ⁡ ( 𝔤 ) A(\mathfrak{g}) -bundle ℰ \mathcal{E} .
