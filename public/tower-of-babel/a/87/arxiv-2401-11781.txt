Kleisli categories, T T -categories and internal categories
Dominique Bourn

Abstract
. We investigate the properties of the Kleisli category K ​ l ​ T KlT of a monad ( T , λ , μ ) (T,\lambda,\mu) on a category 𝔼 \mathbb{E} and in particular the existence of (some kind of) pullbacks. This culminates when the monad is cartesian. In this case, we show that any T T -category in 𝔼 \mathbb{E} in the sense of A. Burroni coincides with a special kind of internal category in K ​ l ​ T KlT . So, it is the case in particular for T T -operads and T T -multicategories. More unexpectedly, this, in turn, sheds new lights on internal categories and n n -categories.

1.
Monads

1.1.
Basics

Let us briefly recall the basics on monads. A monad on a category
\mathbb{E}
is a triple
(T,\lambda,\mu)
of an endofunctor

and two natural transformations:

Id_{\mathbb{E}}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\lambda}}{{\longrightarrow}}T\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\mu}}{{\longleftarrow}}T^{2}

satisfying
\mu.\mu T=\mu.T\mu
and
\mu.\lambda T=1_{T}=\mu.T\lambda
. An adjoint pair
(U,F,\lambda,\epsilon):\bar{\mathbb{E}}\rightleftarrows\mathbb{E}
determines the monad
(T,\lambda,\mu)=(U.F,\lambda,U\epsilon F)

\mathbb{E}
.

A
T
-

]
on an object

\xi.\lambda_{X}=1_{X}
and

produces a
T
-algebra on

of

This construction determines the category

defined by

as left adjoint which makes

a left exact functor. The monad associated with the adjoint pair
(U^{T},F^{T})
recovers the initial monad
(T,\lambda,\mu)
. From an adjoint pair

and its associated monad

we get a comparison functor

, making the following adjoint pairs commute:

A monad
