Asymptotic distributions of the number of zeros of random polynomials in Hayes equivalence class over a finite field
Zhicheng Gao

Abstract
Hayes equivalence is defined on monic polynomials over a finite field 𝔽 q {\mathbb{F}}_{q} in terms of the prescribed leading coefficients and the residue classes modulo a given monic polynomial Q Q . We study the distribution of the number of zeros in a random polynomial over finite fields in a given Hayes equivalence class. It is well known that the number of distinct zeros of a random polynomial over 𝔽 q {\mathbb{F}}_{q} is asymptotically Poisson with mean 1. We show that this is also true for random polynomials in any given Hayes equivalence class. Asymptotic formulas are also given for the number of such polynomials when the degree of such polynomials is proportional to q q and the degree of Q Q and the number of prescribed leading coefficients are bounded by q \sqrt{q} . When Q = 1 Q=1 , the problem is equivalent to the study of the distance distribution in Reed-Solomon codes. Our asymptotic formulas extend some earlier results and imply that all words for a large family of Reed-Solomon codes are ordinary, which further supports the well-known Deep-Hole Conjecture.

Lemma 1

(b)

To minimize the above upper bound, we choose

Proof of Theorem 2

Proof of Theorem 2:

Since the case

\displaystyle t+\ell=O(\sqrt{n}),\quad q=n+O(\sqrt{n}),\quad\hbox{and }~r-k

\displaystyle=O(\sqrt{n}).

It follows from (

which is bounded away from 0.
It follows from (

\displaystyle=O\left(q^{-1/2}\right)\sum_{j=k+1}^{k+t+\ell}{n-r\choose j-r}q^{-j}

\displaystyle=o\left(q^{-r}\left(1+q^{-1}\right)^{q}\right)

\displaystyle=o\left(q^{-r}\right).

By Theorem
4
, asymptotic formula (

\displaystyle\sum_{j=k+1}^{k+t+\ell}{j\choose r}{t+\ell-1\choose k+t+\ell-j}q^{(t+\ell-k-j)/2}A_{j}(n,\gamma)=o\left({n\choose r}q^{-r}\right).

Since

, the above condition is implied by

\displaystyle\ln A_{j}(n,\gamma)+\ln{j\choose r}+\ln{t+\ell-1\choose t+\ell+k-r}-\ln{n\choose r}+\frac{t+\ell-k-j+1+2r}{2}\ln q\to-\infty.

(38)

Set

\displaystyle\delta

\displaystyle:=\frac{j}{{n}}-c=\frac{j}{{n}}-\frac{k}{q},\quad(r\leq j\leq k+t+\ell),

\displaystyle:=\frac{r}{{n}}-c=\frac{r}{{n}}-\frac{k}{q}.

By (

\displaystyle\ln{{n}\choose r}

\displaystyle\geq{n}\left((c+\delta^{\prime})\ln\frac{1}{c+\delta^{\prime}}+(1-c-\delta^{\prime})\ln\frac{1}{1-c-\delta^{\prime}}\right)-\frac{1}{2}\ln{n}-\frac{2}{3}

\displaystyle\geq{n}\left(c\ln\frac{1}{c}+(1-c)\ln\frac{1}{1-c}\right)+O(\sqrt{n}).

Noting

\displaystyle\ln{j\choose r}

\displaystyle=\ln{j\choose j-r}\leq(j-r)\ln j=O(\sqrt{n}\ln n),

\displaystyle\ln{t+\ell-1\choose t+\ell+k-r}

\displaystyle\leq\ln 2^{t+\ell-1}=O(\sqrt{n}),

we obtain

\displaystyle~~\ln{j\choose r}+\ln{t+\ell-1\choose t+\ell+k-r}-\ln{n\choose r}+\frac{t+\ell-k-j+1+2r}{2}\ln q

\displaystyle\leq-{n}\left(c\ln\frac{1}{c}+(1-c)\ln\frac{1}{1-c}\right)+O(\sqrt{n}\ln n).

6
Conclusion

For any given
D\subseteq{\mathbb{F}}_{q}
, we proved that the number of zeros in
D
of a random monic polynomial over
{\mathbb{F}}_{q}
in a given Hayes’s equivalence class is asymptotically Poisson with mean
|D|/q
. We used the generating functions defined on the group algebra of equivalence classes, Weil’s bounds on the corresponding character sums, and Li-Wan’s coordinate-sieve formula. It will be of considerable interest if the problem can be expressed as the sum of nearly independent random variables so that the Chen-Stein method can be applied to obtain the same results. Asymptotic formulas are derived when the relevant parameters satisfy some simple inequalities. Our asymptotic formulas extend those in
[
21
,
12
]
about the distance distribution in Reed-Solomon codes, and imply that all words for a large family of Reed-Solomon codes are ordinary, which further supports the
deep-hole
conjecture.
