A note on Galois groups of linearized polynomials
Peter Müller

Abstract
Let L ⁡ ( X ) L(X) be a monic q q -linearized polynomial over 𝔽 q \mathbb{F}_{q} of degree q n q^{n} , where n n is an odd prime. In [ 2 ] , Gow and McGuire showed that the Galois group of L ⁡ ( X ) / X − t L(X)/X-t over the field of rational functions 𝔽 q ​ ( t ) \mathbb{F}_{q}(t) is GL n ⁡ ( q ) \GL_{n}(q) unless L ⁡ ( X ) = X q n L(X)=X^{q^{n}} . The case of even q q remained open, but it was conjectured that the result holds too and partial results were given. In this note we settle this conjecture. In fact we use Hensel’s Lemma to give a unified proof for all prime powers q q .

In this note we extend the main result

Theorem 1
.

Let

Using Hensel’s Lemma we prove the following divisibility result for

n

be a prime.

Proposition 2

Let

n

L(X)=\sum_{i=m}^{n}a_{i}X^{q^{i}}

a_{i}\in\mathbb{F}_{q}

a_{m},a_{n}\neq 0

q^{m}\cdot(q^{m}-1)\cdot(q^{n}-1)
divides the

L(X)/X-t
over
\mathbb{F}_{q}(t)

We show how Theorem

follows from Proposition

2
. Upon replacing
t
with
t-a_{0}
one may assume that

a_{0}=0
. Let
G
be the Galois group of
L(X)/X-t
over
\mathbb{F}_{q}(t)
. It

. Furthermore,

contains a cyclic regular
subgroup of order

. (See e.g.

or

G\leq\gL_{1}(q^{n})
. The latter group has order
n\cdot(q^{n}-1)
. Suppose

G\neq\GL_{n}(q)
. Proposition
2
yields

q^{m}\cdot(q^{m}-1)\cdot(q^{n}-1)\mid n\cdot(q^{n}-1)

In the following we let

The proof of the following lemma uses Hensel’s Lemma and the

Let

(ii)

(iii)

Then

Let

\alpha

By definition,

Thus we are done once we know that

Thus

As

Remark 6
.

The main tool from group theory in the proof of Theorem
1

is
[
2
, Theorem 7]
: If a subgroup
G
of

\GL_{n}(q)
contains a Singer cycle (an element which permutes the
nonzero elements of
\mathbb{F}_{q}^{n}
cyclically), then

\GL_{n/d}(q^{d})\leq G\leq\gL_{n/d}(q^{d})
for some divisor

of

For this result Gow and McGuire refer to several much stronger results
about transitive linear groups. In particular, these sources depend
on the classification of the finite simple groups.

In
[
3
]
, Kantor gives a short proof of this result
without using the classification of the finite simple
groups. His proof is based on the classification-free paper

[
1
]
where Cameron and Kantor determine the doubly
transitive subgroups of
\PGL_{n}(q)
, which later turned out to
contain mistakes. These were fixed by Kantor in the revision

[
4
]
.
