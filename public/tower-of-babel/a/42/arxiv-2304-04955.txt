On Beckner’s Inequality for Axially Symmetric Functions on 𝕊 6 \mathbb{S}^{6}

Abstract

We prove that axially symmetric solutions to the Q Q -curvature type problem α ​ P 6 ​ u + 120 ​ ( 1 − e 6 ​ u ∫ 𝕊 6 e 6 ​ u ) = 0 on ​ 𝕊 6 \alpha P_{6}u+120(1-\frac{e^{6u}}{\int_{\mathbb{S}^{6}}e^{6u}})=0\ \ \ \ \ \mbox{on}\ \mathbb{S}^{6} must be constants, provided that 1 2 ≤ α < 1 \frac{1}{2}\leq\alpha<1 . , therefore

\displaystyle\frac{f_{k,a}(1)-f_{k,a}(\lambda)}{a^{2}}\geq(B_{k}+B_{k+1})(0.04-0.035)>0.

To sum up, by now we have proved Proposition
4.1
when

. When
