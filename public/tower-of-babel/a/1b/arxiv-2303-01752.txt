Uniform bounds on the Harish-Chandra characters
Anna Szumowicz

Abstract
. Let 𝐆 {\mathbf{G}} be a connected reductive algebraic group over a p p -adic local field F F . In this paper we study the asymptotic behaviour of the trace characters θ π \theta_{\pi} evaluated at a regular element γ \gamma of 𝐆 ⁡ ( F ) {\mathbf{G}}(F) as π \pi varies among supercuspidal representations of 𝐆 ⁡ ( F ) {\mathbf{G}}(F) . Kim, Shin and Templier [ 8 ] conjectured that θ π ​ ( γ ) deg ⁡ ( π ) \frac{\theta_{\pi}(\gamma)}{\deg(\pi)} tends to 0 0 when π \pi runs over irreducible supercuspidal representations of 𝐆 ⁡ ( F ) {\mathbf{G}}(F) with unitary central character and the formal degree of π \pi tends to infinity. For 𝐆 {\mathbf{G}} semisimple we prove that the trace character is uniformly bounded on γ \gamma under the assumption, which is expected to hold true for every 𝐆 ⁡ ( F ) {\mathbf{G}}(F) , that all irreducible supercuspidal representations of 𝐆 ⁡ ( F ) {\mathbf{G}}(F) are compactly induced from an open compact modulo center subgroup. Moreover, we give an explicit upper bound in the case of γ \gamma ellitpic.

1.
Introduction

Let
{\mathbf{G}}
be a connected reductive algebraic group over a
p
-adic local field
F
. Denote
G:={\mathbf{G}}(F)
. Let
\pi
be an admissible representation of
G
. Denote by
\mathcal{C}^{\infty}_{c}(G)
the set of smooth compactly supported functions on
G

\theta_{\pi}
is the conjugation invariant distribution on
\mathcal{C}_{c}^{\infty}(G)
defined as

\langle\theta_{\pi},f\rangle:={\rm tr}\,\pi(f)

for
f\in\mathcal{C}_{c}^{\infty}(G)
. Harish-Chandra showed that
\theta_{\pi}
is represented by a locally constant function on the set of regular semisimple elements which is locally
L^{1}
on
G
. We will still denote this function by
\theta_{\pi}
.

If
\pi
is a discrete series representation, for example is irreducible unitary supercuspidal, one can define the formal degree of
\pi
denoted by
\deg(\pi)
. It plays a similar role as the dimension of representations of compact groups.

Denote by
{\rm{Irr}^{sc}}({\mathbf{G}}(F))
the set of equivalence classes of irreducible supercuspidal representations of
G
. Kim, Shin and Templier proposed the following conjecture.

Conjecture 1.1

(
[
8
, Conjecture 4.1.]
)
.

Fix a regular semisimple element
\gamma\in G
. Then,

\lim_{\begin{subarray}{c}\pi\in{\rm{Irr}^{sc}}(G)\\
\deg(\pi)\to\infty\end{subarray}}\frac{\theta_{\pi}(\gamma)}{\deg(\pi)}=0

where
\pi\in{\rm{Irr}^{sc}}({\mathbf{G}}(F))
varies over representations whose central character is unitary.

Kim, Shin and Templier
[
8
]
proved Conjecture
1.1
in case of

\gamma
topologically unipotent,
\pi
constructed by Yu
[
16
]
and under certain assumptions on
G
. In
[
9
]
they proved Conjecture
1.1
for
\gamma
elliptic and under certain assumption on tori in
{\mathbf{G}}
which is satisfied for example when every
F
-torus in
{\mathbf{G}}
is tame and the characteristic of the residue field of
F
does not divide the order of the Weyl group of
{\mathbf{G}}
. An analogue of this conjecture for compact semisimple Lie groups can be easily verified using the Weyl character formula. See also
[
15
]
for partial results in the case of the general linear group.

As described in
[
9
]
, bounds on the trace characters are motivated by their relation with limit multiplicity formulas, Sato-Tate equidistribution and bounds towards Ramanujan.

1.1.
Uniform bounds

In this paper we prove Conjecture

for arbitrary semisimple connected
p
-adic groups for irreducible supercuspidal representations of the form

p
-adic group
{\mathbf{G}}
when

[

]
and the exhaustion results by Kim
[

and Fintzen
[

(Bushnell-Kutzko construction
[
4

) and for the classical groups (Stevens
[

]

Kim, Shin and Templier in their work
[

]

[

towards Conjecture

Theorem 1.2
.

Let
{\mathbf{G}}
be a connected semisimple group over a non-Archimedean local field

Then, for every regular semisimple element

For

). Denote by
\mathcal{P}(M)
the set of all parabolic subgroups of
{\mathbf{G}}
defined over
F
with Levi component
M
. Let
P^{\prime}\in\mathcal{P}(M)
be a parabolic subgroup with Levi decomposition
P^{\prime}=MN_{P^{\prime}}
. Any point
x\in{\mathbf{G}}(F)
can be written in the form

x=n_{P^{\prime}}(x)m_{P^{\prime}}(x)k_{P^{\prime}}(x),

where
n_{P^{\prime}}(x)\in N_{P^{\prime}}(F)
,
m_{P^{\prime}}(x)\in M(F)
and
k_{P^{\prime}}(x)\in K_{1}
. Write
X(M)
for the module of rational characters of
M
. Consider a map

H_{M}\colon M(F)\to\mathbf{a}_{M}:={\rm Hom}(X(M)_{F},{\mathbb{R}})

given by
\exp{(\langle H_{M}(m),\chi\rangle)}=|\chi(m)|
for
m\in M(F)
,
\chi\in X(M)_{F}
. We put

H_{P^{\prime}}(x):=H_{M}(m_{P^{\prime}}(x)).

The function
v_{M}
is defined as the volume of the convex hull of the projection of

\{-H_{P^{\prime}}(x)|\ \ P^{\prime}\in\mathcal{P}(M)\}

onto
\mathbf{a}_{M}/\mathbf{a}_{G}
. Remark that by definition, the function

is constant on right

-cosets, so in particular it is continuous.

By Lemma
3.2
and
[

]
,

\int_{A_{M}(\gamma)\setminus G}f(x^{-1}\gamma x)v_{M}(x)d\mu(x)=(-1)^{\dim(A_{M}(\gamma)/A_{{\mathbf{G}}})}\theta_{\pi}(f)\theta_{\pi}(\gamma)

(3.1)

for
f=\chi_{\rho}
.
By Proposition
2.1
,
\Omega(K)
has finitely many right cosets modulo
K
. By Frobenius reciprocity,

\theta_{\pi}(\chi_{\rho})={\rm tr}\,\pi(\chi_{\rho})=\dim{\rm Hom}_{K}(\rho,\pi)=\dim{\rm Hom}_{G}({\operatorname{c-Ind}}_{K}^{G}\rho,\pi)=\dim{\rm Hom}_{G}(\pi,\pi)=1.

By (

\displaystyle|\theta_{\pi}(\gamma)|

\displaystyle=\left|\int_{A_{M}(\gamma)\setminus G}\chi_{\rho}(g^{-1}\gamma g)v_{M}(g)dg\right|=\left|\int_{\Omega(K)}\chi_{\rho}(g^{-1}\gamma g)v_{M}(g)dg\right|

\displaystyle\leqslant\sum_{gK\in\Omega(K)/K}\int_{K}\left|\chi_{\rho}(k^{-1}g^{-1}\gamma gk)\right||v_{M}(gk)|dk.

Since the function

is continuous and

,

\displaystyle\leqslant\left(\sum_{g\in\Omega(K)/K}|\Delta(g^{-1}\gamma g)|_{F}^{-1}C\right)2^{\dim{\mathbf{G}}-{\rm rk}({\mathbf{G}})}|W|

\displaystyle=|\Delta(\gamma)|_{F}^{-1}|\Omega(K)/K|C2^{\dim{\mathbf{G}}-\textrm{rk}({\mathbf{G}})}|W|=:\kappa_{\gamma}

for some

∎

4.
Explicit bound for
\gamma

In this section we compute an explicit bound on
\theta_{\pi}(\gamma)
for
\gamma
elliptic.

Theorem 4.1
.

Let
{\mathbf{G}}
be a connected semisimple group over a non-Archimedean local field
F
of characteristic zero. Let
W
be the Weyl group of
{\mathbf{G}}
. Then, for every regular elliptic
\gamma\in{\mathbf{G}}(F)
and every irreducible compactly induced supercuspidal representation
\pi
we have

|\theta_{\pi}(\gamma)|\leqslant|\Delta(\gamma)|_{F}^{-1}2^{\dim{\mathbf{G}}-{\rm rk}\,{\mathbf{G}}}|W|.

Corollary 4.2
.

Let
{\mathbf{G}}
be a connected semisimple group over a non-Archimedean local field
F
of characteristic zero. Let
W
be the Weyl group of
{\mathbf{G}}
. Then, for every regular elliptic element
\gamma\in{\mathbf{G}}(F)
and every irreducible compactly induced supercuspidal representation
\pi
with
\deg(\pi)\geqslant|\Delta(\gamma)|_{F}^{-1}
we have

|\theta_{\pi}(\gamma)|\leqslant|\Delta(\gamma)|_{F}^{-\frac{1}{2}}\deg(\pi)^{\frac{1}{2}}2^{\dim{\mathbf{G}}-{\rm rk}\,{\mathbf{G}}}|W|.

This goes towards
[
9
, Conjecture 3.15. (iii)]
.

The proof of Theorem
4.1
is inspired by the proof of
[
6
, Theorem 5.8.]
. However, for
{\mathbf{G}}
non-compact some significant changes are needed.
In order to estimate
\theta_{\pi}
, we rely on Kazhdan’s orthogonality relations (Lemma
4.4
) and the following lemma.

Fix a regular elliptic element
\gamma\in{\mathbf{G}}(F)
. Let
\mathbf{T}_{0}
be an elliptic torus such that
\gamma\in\mathbf{T}_{0}(F)
. Denote by
{\mathbf{G}}^{rs}(F)
the set of regular semisimple elements in
{\mathbf{G}}(F)
. For any irreducible representation
\tau
of a compact group we write
\chi_{\tau}
for its trace.

Lemma 4.3
.

Let
S
be a closed subset of
\mathbf{T}_{0}(F)\cap{\mathbf{G}}^{rs}(F)
.
Then,
\theta_{\pi}|_{S}
is the restriction of a finite integral combination of unitary characters of
\mathbf{T}_{0}(F)
.

Proof.

For any regular elliptic
g\in\mathbf{G}(F)
write

\Omega_{g}(K):=\{x\in{\mathbf{G}}(F):\ \ x^{-1}gx\in K\}.

By Proposition
2.1
, Arthur’s formula and the local constancy of
\theta_{\pi}
, for every regular elliptic
g
there exists an open neighborhood
U_{g}
of
g
such that

\theta_{\pi}(u_{g})=\sum_{xK\in\Omega_{g}(K)/K}\chi_{\rho}(x^{-1}u_{g}x)

for every
u_{g}\in U_{g}
. Choose a finite cover
\{U_{g_{i}}\}_{i=1}^{n}
of
S
with
g_{i}\in S
.
Let
\mathcal{F}:=\bigcup_{i=1}^{n}\Omega_{g_{i}}(K)
. This set is left
\mathbf{T}_{0}(F)
-invariant. Consider the representation

\sigma:=\bigoplus_{x\in\mathbf{T}_{0}(F)\setminus\mathcal{F}/K}{\rm Ind}_{\mathbf{T}_{0}(F)\cap K^{x^{-1}}}^{\mathbf{T}_{0}(F)}\rho^{x^{-1}}

where
K^{x^{-1}}:=xKx^{-1}
and
\rho^{x^{-1}}(a):=\rho(x^{-1}ax)
for any
a\in K^{x^{-1}}
. For every
g_{i}
we have

\displaystyle\chi_{\sigma}(g_{i})

\displaystyle=\sum_{x\in\mathbf{T}_{0}(F)\setminus\mathcal{F}/K}\ \ \sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}r\in\mathbf{T}_{0}(F)/\mathbf{T}_{0}(F)\cap K^{x^{-1}},\\
g_{i}\in K^{x^{-1}}\end{subarray}}\chi_{\rho^{x^{-1}}}(g_{i})=\sum_{xK\in\Omega_{g_{i}}(K)/K}\chi_{\rho}(x^{-1}g_{i}x).

Therefore
\theta_{\pi}|_{S}=\chi_{\sigma}|_{S}
. ∎

Lemma 4.4
.

Let
{\mathbf{G}}
be a semisimple group over a non-Archimedean local field
F
. For any torus
\mathbf{T}
write

denotes the normalizer of

in

. Then, for every supercuspidal representation

\sum_{[\mathbf{T}]\subseteq{\mathbf{G}}}\frac{1}{|W_{\mathbf{T}}|}\int_{\mathbf{T}(F)\cap{\mathbf{G}}^{rs}(F)}|\Delta(t)|_{F}|\theta_{\pi}(t)|^{2}dt=1

runs over all

-conjugacy classes of maximal elliptic tori and

Proof.

Follows from the Kazhdan’s orthogonality relations
[

and
[

[
7

∎

Proof of Theorem
4.1
.

Denote by
\mathbf{T}_{0}
maximal split torus such that
\gamma\in\mathbf{T}_{0}(F)
. Let
m:=\frac{1}{2}(\dim{\mathbf{G}}-{\rm rk}\,{\mathbf{G}})
and choose a set of positive roots
\lambda_{1},\ldots,\lambda_{m}
of
\mathbf{T}_{0}
defined over the algebraic closure of

. We have

\Delta(t)=\prod_{j=1}^{m}(1-\lambda_{j}(t))(1-\lambda_{j}(t)^{-1}).

Let

Z_{i}:=\{t\in\mathbf{T}_{0}(F)|\ \ |1-\lambda_{i}(t)|_{F}<|1-\lambda_{i}(\gamma)|_{F}\}.

Let
E
be a finite extension of
F
such that every character of
\mathbf{T}_{0}
is defined over
E
. Denote by
\mathcal{O}_{E}^{\times}
the group of all invertible elements in the ring of integers of
E
. By the compactness of
\mathbf{T}_{0}(F)
,
\lambda_{j}(\mathbf{T}_{0}(F))\subseteq\mathcal{O}_{E}^{\times}
for every

. By Pontryagin duality, for every

we can find a unitary character
\alpha_{j}\colon\mathcal{O}_{E}^{\times}\to\mathbb{C}^{\times}
such that
\alpha_{j}(\lambda_{j}(\gamma))\neq 1
and
\alpha_{j}(\lambda_{j}(z))=1
for every

. Define

\beta(t):=\prod_{j=1}^{m}(1-\alpha_{j}(\lambda_{j}(t)))

for every

. Since

is a non-zero algebraic integer, there exists a Galois automorphism
\kappa

\alpha(t):=\prod_{j=1}^{m}(1-\kappa(\alpha_{j}(\lambda_{j}(t))))

satisfies

.
Since
\alpha(z)=0
for every

(4.1)

Let
dt

\mathbf{T}_{0}(F)

\int_{\mathbf{T}_{0}(F)\cap{\mathbf{G}}^{rs}(F)}|\Delta(t)|_{F}|\theta_{\pi}(t)|^{2}dt\leqslant|W|.

(4.2)

Putting (

) we get

(4.3)

Define
