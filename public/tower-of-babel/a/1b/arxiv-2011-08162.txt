1.
Introduction

For
f\in L^{2}({\mathbb{R}}^{n}),n\geq 1
and
a>0
we set

(1)

\displaystyle\hat{f}(\xi)=\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{n}}\mbox{e}^{-i\xi\cdot x}f(x)\,dx,\xi\in{\mathbb{R}}^{n},

and

S_{t}f(x)=(2\pi)^{-n}\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{n}}\mbox{e}^{i\xi\cdot x}\mbox{e}^{it|\xi|^{a}}\hat{f}(\xi)\,d\xi,\quad x\in{\mathbb{R}}^{n},t\geq 0.

We introduce Sobolev spaces
H_{s}=H_{s}({\mathbb{R}}^{n})
by setting

H_{s}=\{f\in\mathscr{S}^{\prime};\|f\|_{H_{s}}<\infty\},s\in{\mathbb{R}},

where

\|f\|_{H_{s}}=\left(\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{n}}(1+[\xi[^{2})^{s}|\hat{f}(\xi)|^{2}\,d\xi\right)^{1/2}

and
\mathscr{S}=\mathscr{S}({\mathbb{R}}^{n})
denotes the Schwartz class.
We let the sequence
\{t_{m}\}_{1}^{\infty}
have the properties that

(2)

\displaystyle 1>t_{1}>t_{2}>t_{3}>\dots>0\mbox{ and }\lim_{m\to\infty}t_{m}=0.

We shall study the problem of deciding for which sequences
\{t_{m}\}_{1}^{\infty}
and functions
f
one has

(3)

\displaystyle\lim_{m\to\infty}S_{t_{m}}f(x)=f(x)\mbox{ almost everywhere}.

For
r>0
we say that
\{t_{m}\}_{1}^{\infty}\in l^{r}
if
\sum\limits_{1}\ t_{m}^{r}<\infty
, and that
\{t_{m}\}_{1}^{\infty}\in l^{r,\infty}
if

\#\{m;t_{m}>b\}

\lesssim b^{-r}
for
b>0
.

In Sjölin and Strömberg
[
2
]
we proved that if
a>0,n\geq 1,0<s<a/2
, and

\{t_{m}\}_{1}^{\infty}\in l^{r}
for some

, then (
3
) holds if
f\in H_{s}({\mathbb{R}}^{n})
.

In the case
n=1
Dimou and Seeger
[
1
]
have proved that if
a>0,a\neq 1,0<s<a/4
and
\{t_{m}\}_{1}^{\infty}\in l^{r}
, where

, then (
3
) holds if
f\in H_{s}({\mathbb{R}}^{)}
. They also proved that here
r
cannot be replaced by
r_{1}

if

.

In Section 3 of this paper we shall improve the result from
[
2
]
mentioned above in the case

. We shall prove that
(
3

if
a>0,a\neq 1,n\geq 2,0<s<a/2
, and
\{t_{m}\}_{1}^{\infty}\in l^{r,\infty}

where

. In the proof of this result we shall use the following theorem.

Theorem 1
.

Assume

\|\sup\limits_{t\in J}|S_{t}f|\|_{2}\leq(1+C|J|^{1/2}\lambda^{a/2})\|f\|_{2}.

decreasing, there is no estimate

2.
Maximal estimates

The following theorem was proved in Sjölin and Strömberg
[
2
]
. We shall here give a new proof which is simpler than the proof in

[
2
]
.

Theorem A.
(Sjölin-Strömberg) Asssume
a>0,n\geq 1,\lambda\geq 1
, and
J
interval in
{\mathbb{R}}
. Assume also that
f\in L^{2}({\mathbb{R}}^{n})

and
\mbox{supp }\hat{f}\subset B(0;\lambda)
.

Then one has

\|\sup\limits_{t\in J}|S_{t}f|\|_{2}\leq(1+C|J|\lambda^{a})\|f\|_{2}.

Proof.

We can write

S_{t}f(x)=c\int\mbox{e}^{i\xi\cdot x}\mbox{e}^{it|\xi|^{a}}\hat{f}(\xi)\,d\xi

and
J=[t_{0},t_{0}+r]
where
r=|J|
.

We have

\mbox{e}^{it|\xi|^{a}}=\Delta+\mbox{e}^{it_{0}|\xi|^{a}}

where
\Delta=\mbox{e}^{it|\xi|^{a}}-\mbox{e}^{it_{0}|\xi|^{a}}
. It follows that

\Delta=i|\xi|^{a}\int_{t_{0}}^{t}\mbox{e}^{i|\xi|^{a}s}\,ds,

and

(4)

\displaystyle S_{t}f(x)

\displaystyle=c\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{n}}\int_{t_{0}}^{t}\mbox{e}^{i\xi\cdot x}i|\xi|^{a}\mbox{e}^{is|\xi|^{a}}\hat{f}(\xi)\,d\xi\,ds+c\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{n}}\mbox{e}^{i\xi\cdot x}\mbox{e}^{it_{0}|\xi|^{a}}\hat{f}(\xi)\,d\xi

(5)

\displaystyle=S_{1}(x,t)+S_{2}(x,t).

Hence

|S_{1}(x,t)|\lesssim\int_{t_{0}}^{t}\left|\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{n}}\mbox{e}^{i\xi\cdot x}i|\xi|^{a}\mbox{e}^{is|\xi|^{a}}\hat{f}(\xi)\,d\xi\right|\,ds

and

\sup\limits_{t\in J}|S_{1}(x,t)|\lesssim\int_{t_{0}}^{t_{0}+r}\left|\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{n}}\mbox{e}^{i\xi\cdot x}i|\xi|^{a}\mbox{e}^{is|\xi|^{a}}\hat{f}(\xi)\,d\xi\right|\,ds.

Using Minkovski’s inequality and Plancherel’s theorem we obtain

(6)

\displaystyle\left(\int\sup\limits_{t\in J}|S_{1}(x,t)|^{2}\,dx\right)^{1/2}

\displaystyle\lesssim\int_{t_{0}}^{t_{0}+r}\||\xi|^{a}\hat{f}(\xi)\|_{2}\,ds

(7)

\displaystyle\leq r\lambda^{a}\left(\int_{B(0;\lambda)}|\hat{f}(\xi)|^{2}|\right)^{1/2}\lesssim r\lambda^{a}\|f\|_{2}.

Also

\left(\int\sup\limits_{t\in J}|S_{2}(x,t)|^{2}\,dx\right)^{1/2}=\left(\int|S_{2}(x,t_{0})|^{2}\,dx\right)^{1/2}=\|f\|_{2},

and we obtain

\|\sup\limits_{t\in J}|S_{t}f|\|_{2}\leq(1+Cr\lambda^{a})\|f\|_{2},

which proves the theorem.
∎

In the following theorems let
J
denote an interval. In the one-dimensional case Dimou and Seeger obtained the following result.

Theorem B.
(Dimou -Seeger )
Assume
a>0,a\neq 1,n=1,\lambda\geq 1
. and
J\subset[0,1]
. Assume also that
f\in L^{2}({\mathbb{R}})
and

\mbox{supp }\hat{f}\subset\{\xi;\lambda/2\leq|\xi|\leq\lambda\}
.

Then one has

\|\sup\limits_{t\in J}|S_{t}f|\|_{2}\lesssim\left(1+|J|^{1/4}\lambda^{a/4}\right)\|f\|_{2}.

We have the following result.

Theorem 1
.

Assume
a>0,n\geq 1,\lambda\geq 1
, and let the interval
J\subset[0,1]
. Assume also that
f\in L^{2}({\mathbb{R}}^{n})
and

\mbox{supp }\hat{f}\subset B(0;\lambda)=\{\xi\in{\mathbb{R}}^{n};|\xi|\leq\lambda\}
. Then one has

\|\sup\limits_{t\in J}|S_{t}f|\|_{2}\leq(1+C|J|^{1/2}\lambda^{a/2})\|f\|_{2}.

Proof.

First we study the case
|J|\lambda^{a}\leq 1
. Then
(1+C|J|\lambda^{a}))\leq(1+C|J||^{1/2}\lambda^{a/2})
. Applying Theorem A we get

\|\sup\limits_{t\in J}|S_{t}f|\|_{2}\leq(1+C|J|\lambda^{a}))\|f\|_{2}\leq(1+C|J||^{1/2}\lambda^{a/2})\|f\|_{2}.

It remains to study the case
|J|\lambda^{a}>1
.

Cover
J
with intervals
J_{i},i=1,2,\dots,N
, of length
\lambda^{-a}
. We may take
N\leq|J|\lambda^{a}+1
.. Using Theorem A we obtain

(8)

\displaystyle\|\sup\limits_{t\in J}|S_{t}f|\|_{2}^{2}\leq\sum\limits_{i=1}^{N}\|\sup\limits_{t\in J_{i}}|S_{t}|f\|_{2}^{2}\lesssim\sum\limits_{i=1}^{N}\left(1+|J_{i}|\lambda^{a}\right)\|f\|_{2}^{2}\lesssim N\|f\|^{2}_{2}\leq\left(|J|\lambda^{a}+1\right)\|f\|^{2}_{2}

and the inequality in the theorem follows.

∎

We have the following extension of Theorem B.

Theorem 2
.

Assume
a>0,a\neq 1,\lambda\geq 1
and
J\subset[0,1]
. Assume also that
f\in L^{2}({\mathbb{R}})
with
\mbox{supp }\hat{f}\subset B(0,\lambda)
. Then one has

\|\sup\limits_{t\in J}|S_{t}f|\|_{2}\lesssim C\left(|J|^{1/4}\lambda^{a/4}+1\right)\|f\|_{2}.

Proof.

First we study the case
|J|\lambda^{a}\leq 1
. From Theorem A we obtain

\|\sup\limits_{t\in J}|S_{t}f|\|_{2}\lesssim\|f\|_{2}.

which proves the theorem in this case.

We then consider the case
|J|\lambda^{a}>1
, i.e.
\lambda>|J|^{-1/a}
.

We choose
k
such that
2^{-k-1}\lambda<|J|^{-1/a}\leq 2^{-k}\lambda
and then write
f=\sum\limits_{j=0}^{k}f_{j}+g
where

\mbox{supp }\hat{f}_{j}\subset\{\xi;2^{-j-1}\lambda\leq|\xi|\leq 2^{-j}\lambda\}
for
j=0,1,\dots,k
, and
\mbox{supp }\hat{g}\subset B(0;2^{-k-1}\lambda)
.

From Theorem A we conclude that

\|\sup\limits_{t\in J}|S_{t}g|\|_{2}\lesssim\left(1+|J||J|^{-1}\right)\|g\|_{2}\lesssim\|f\|_{2}

and it follows from Theorem B that

\|\sup\limits_{t\in J}|S_{t}f_{j}|\|_{2}\lesssim\left(1+|J|^{1/4}2^{-ja/4}\lambda^{a/4}\right)\|f\|_{2}\lesssim|J|^{1/4}2^{-ja/4}\lambda^{a/4}\|f\|_{2},

since
|J|2^{-ja}\lambda^{a}\geq 1
. Hence we have

\sum\limits_{j=0}^{k}\|\sup\limits_{t\in J}|S_{t}f_{j}|\|_{2}\lesssim|J|^{1/4}\lambda^{a/4}\|f\|_{2}

and the theorem follows from the above estimates.
∎

3.
A convergence result

We shall here prove a convergence result for function in
H_{s}({\mathbb{R}}^{n}),n\geq 2
, and begin with two lemmas.

Lemma 4
.

Assume
a>0,a\neq 1,n\geq 2,\lambda\geq 1
and
0<s<a/2
. Also let
(t_{m})_{1}^{\infty}\in l^{r,\infty}
, where

r=2s/(a-2s)
Then one has

\|\sup\limits_{m}|S_{t_{m}}f|\|_{2}\lesssim\lambda^{s}\|f\|_{2},

if
f\in L^{2}({\mathbb{R}}^{n})
and
\mbox{supp }\hat{f}\subset\{\xi;\lambda/2\leq|\xi|\leq\lambda\}
.

Proof.

Let
0<b<1
. We have

\|\sup\limits_{m}|S_{t_{m}}f|\|_{2}\leq\|\sup\limits_{t_{m}\leq b}|S_{t_{m}}f|\|_{2}+\|\sup\limits_{t_{m}>b}|S_{t_{m}}f|\|_{2}=T_{1}+T_{2}.

Theorem 1 gives the estimate

T_{1}\lesssim\left(b^{1/2}\lambda^{a/2}+1\right)\|f\|_{2}.

We also have
\#\{m;t_{m}>b\}\lesssim b^{-r}
and it follows that

T_{2}\lesssim b^{-r/2}\|f\|_{2}.

We then choose
b
such that

b^{1/2}\lambda^{a/2}=b^{-r/2}.

One gets

b^{1/2+r/2}=\lambda^{-a/2},

and

b=\lambda^{-a/(1+r})<1.

Hence
b^{-r/2}\geq 1
and

T_{1}\lesssim b^{1/2+r/2}\|f\|_{2}=b^{-r/2}\|f\|_{2}.

We have shown that

\|\sup\limits_{m}|S_{t_{m}}f|\|_{2}\lesssim b^{-r/2}\|f\|_{2},

where

b^{-r/2}=\lambda^{ra/2(1+r)}=\lambda^{s}.

This completes the proof of the lemma.
∎
