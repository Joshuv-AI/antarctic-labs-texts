On the accuracy of the finite volume approximations to nonlocal conservation laws
Aekta Aggarwal, Helge Holden, Ganesh Vaidya

Abstract
In this article, we discuss the error analysis for a certain class of monotone finite volume schemes approximating nonlocal scalar conservation laws, modeling traffic flow and crowd dynamics, without any additional assumptions on monotonicity or linearity of the kernel μ \displaystyle\mu or the flux f \displaystyle f . We first prove a novel Kuznetsov-type lemma for this class of PDEs and thereby show that the finite volume approximations converge to the entropy solution at the rate of Δ ​ t \displaystyle\sqrt{\Delta t} in L 1 ​ ( ℝ ) \displaystyle L^{1}(\mathbb{R}) . To the best of our knowledge, this is the first proof of any type of convergence rate for this class of conservation laws. We also present numerical experiments to illustrate this result.

The celebrated

\displaystyle\displaystyle u_{t}+\Big(u\nu(u*\mu)\Big)_{x}

Uniqueness of the entropy solution

Any two entropy solutions of the IVP (
1.4

1.5

Theorem 2.1

(Uniqueness)
.

Let
\displaystyle u,v\in C([0,T];L^{1}(\mathbb{R}))\cap(L^{\infty}_{t}\operatorname{BV}_{x})(\overline{Q}_{T})
be two entropy solutions of the IVP (
1.4
)–(
1.5
) corresponding to the initial data
\displaystyle{u_{0}}
and
\displaystyle{v_{0}}
respectively.
Then, there exists a constant

\mathcal{M}=\mathcal{M}(f,\mu,\eta,\nu,\beta,{\left\|u\right\|}_{L^{1}(Q_{T})},{\left\|v\right\|}_{L^{1}(Q_{T})},|u|_{L^{\infty}_{t}\operatorname{BV}_{x}},|v|_{L^{\infty}_{t}\operatorname{BV}_{x}},|u|_{L^{\infty}_{t}\operatorname{BV}_{x}},T)>0

{\left\|u(t,\,\cdot\,)-v(t,\,\cdot\,)\right\|}_{L^{1}(\mathbb{R})}\leq{\left\|u_{0}-v_{0}\right\|}_{L^{1}(\mathbb{R})}(1+\mathcal{M}t\exp(\mathcal{M}t)),\quad t\in[0,T].

In particular, if
\displaystyle u_{0}=v_{0},
then
\displaystyle u=v
a.e. in
\displaystyle\overline{Q}_{T}
.

Proof.

Note that the nonlocal coefficient of the flux function considered in this article is more general than the ones in all the previous results on the uniqueness of nonlinear-nonlocal conservation laws. However, the continuous dependence estimates for the entropy solution of the conservation laws (local) derived in
[
23
, Thm. 1.3]
can still be invoked to prove the desired weighted contraction estimates as in

[
5
, Thm. 4.1]

3.2

\displaystyle\varepsilon,\varepsilon_{0}\rightarrow 0.

∎

For
\displaystyle\Delta x,\Delta t>0,

\displaystyle u^{\Delta}

\displaystyle\displaystyle u^{n+1/2}_{ij}

\displaystyle\displaystyle=u^{n}_{ij}-\lambda_{x}\bigl[\mathcal{F}^{x,n}_{i+1/2,j}(u^{n}_{ij},u^{n}_{i+1,j})-\mathcal{F}^{x,n}_{i-1/2,j}(u^{n}_{i-1,j},u^{n}_{ij})\bigr],

\displaystyle\displaystyle u^{n+1}_{ij}

\displaystyle\displaystyle=u^{n+1/2}_{ij}-\lambda_{y}\bigl[\mathcal{F}^{y,n}_{i,j+1/2}(u^{n+1/2}_{ij},u^{n+1/2}_{i,j+1})-\mathcal{F}^{y,n}_{i,j-1/2}(u^{n+1/2}_{i,j-1},u^{n+1/2}_{ij})\bigr],

\displaystyle f^{2}(u)\nu^{2}(\mu^{2}*\beta^{2}(u))

\displaystyle(x_{i+1/2},y_{j})

\displaystyle(x_{i},y_{j+1/2})

\displaystyle i,j\in\mathbb{Z}

where,
\displaystyle u^{n}_{l+1/2,p}

\displaystyle u^{n}_{l,p}
and
\displaystyle u^{n}_{l+1,p}
, with

\displaystyle\mu^{1}_{i+1/2,j}=\mu^{1}(x_{i+1/2},y_{j})

\displaystyle\mu^{2}_{i+1/2,j}=\mu^{2}(x_{i+1/2},y_{j})\,.

\displaystyle\Delta t

\lambda_{x}\leq\frac{\min(1,4-6\theta_{x},6\theta_{x})}{1+6\left|f^{1}\right|_{\lip(\mathbb{R})}{\left\|\nu^{1}\right\|}_{L^{\infty}(\mathbb{R})}},\quad\quad\lambda_{y}\leq\frac{\min(1,4-6\theta_{y},6\theta_{y})}{1+6\left|f^{2}\right|_{\lip(\mathbb{R})}{\left\|\nu^{2}\right\|}_{L^{\infty}(\mathbb{R})}},\quad\quad\theta_{x},\theta_{y}\in\left(0,\frac{2}{3}\right),

\lambda_{x}\left|f^{1}\right|_{\lip(\mathbb{R})}{\left\|\nu^{1}\right\|}_{L^{\infty}(\mathbb{R})}\leq\frac{1}{2},\quad\lambda_{y}\left|f^{2}\right|_{\lip(\mathbb{R})}{\left\|\nu^{2}\right\|}_{L^{\infty}(\mathbb{R})}\leq\frac{1}{2},

with numerical fluxes
\displaystyle\mathcal{F}^{x}
or
\displaystyle\mathcal{F}^{y}
chosen as
Lax–Friedrichs flux and Godunov flux, respectively. Extension to other monotone fluxes and for higher dimensions is similar. The numerical scheme can now be shown to converge to entropy solution, see, for example,
[
1
]
.
The Kuznetsov Lemma and the theorem on error estimate presented in Section
3
can now be extended to several space dimensions using dimension splitting arguments (see
[
22
, Sec. 4.3]
) with appropriate modifications throughout the proof.

Conclusions

In this article, we have established the convergence rate estimates for scalar nonlocal nonlinear conservation laws, modeling traffic and crowd dynamics, with no additional assumptions on monotonicity/linearity of the kernel or the flux. The rate is shown to be 1/2 which is consistent with its local counterparts. It is interesting to see that the results are independent of the radius of the convolution matrix and monotonicity of the kernel. For
\displaystyle\nu(u)=\beta(u)=1,
the nonlocal conservation laws boil down to the local conservation law and hence the convergence rate
\displaystyle 1/2
is optimal due to
[
32
]
.

The extensions of these results to a general coupled system of nonlocal conservation laws, for the convergent finite volume schemes proposed in
[
1
]
and also for nonlocal conservation laws with discontinuous flux, are not straightforward and are works in progress. Furthermore, using the Kuznetsov-type lemma proved in this article, the rate at which the solutions of the nonlocal FTL (see
[
16
,
10
]
) models converge to its continuum limit, can be explored, which we aim to address in our upcoming article.
