Remark on the Adiabatic Limit of Quantum Zakharov System
Brian Choi

Abstract
This paper is concerned with the low regularity well-posedness of the adiabatic limit of the quantum Zakharov system, which is a modified nonlinear Schrödinger equation (NLSE). As the quantum parameter tends to zero, the modified NLSE formally converges to the standard NLSE, and we show this rigorously.



Key words.
Adiabatic Limit, Quantum Zakharov System, Well-posedness, NLS

Consider

\begin{split}\begin{cases}i\partial_{t}u+\Delta u-\epsilon^{2}\Delta^{2}u=-(I-\epsilon^{2}\Delta)^{-1}(|u|^{2})u,\>(x,t)\in M\times\mathbb{R}\\

Theorem 1.1
.

For all
s\geq 0
, (
1.1
) is globally well-posed in

. Furthermore the data-to-solution map is locally Lipschitz in

.

For

) on

We closely follow the method in

. Let

. For
m\in\mathbb{N}\cup\left\{0\right\}
, define the dyadic projector
\chi_{m}(\tau,k)\coloneqq\chi_{2^{m}\leq\langle\tau+w(k)\rangle<2^{m+1}}
and
\widehat{u_{2^{m}}}(\tau,k)\coloneqq\widehat{u}(\tau,k)\chi_{m}(\tau,k)

\lVert u\rVert_{L^{4}}^{2}=\lVert u^{2}\rVert_{L^{2}}\lesssim\sum_{m,n\geq 0}\lVert u_{2^{m}}u_{2^{m+n}}\rVert_{L^{2}}=\sum_{m,n\geq 0}\lVert\widehat{u_{2^{m}}}\ast\widehat{u_{2^{m+n}}}\rVert_{L^{2}}.

Since

,

\begin{split}&\widehat{u_{2^{m}}}\ast\widehat{u_{2^{m+n}}}(\tau,k)\\
=&\sum_{k_{1}\in\mathbb{Z}}\int\widehat{u_{2^{m}}}(\tau_{1},k_{1})\widehat{u_{2^{m+n}}}(\tau-\tau_{1},k-k_{1})\chi_{m}(\tau_{1},k_{1})\chi_{m+n}(\tau-\tau_{1},k-k_{1})d\tau_{1}.\end{split}

By the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality in
\tau_{1},k_{1}
, Hölder’s inequality in
l_{k}^{2}L_{\tau}^{\infty}
, and Young’s inequality,

\begin{split}\lVert\widehat{u_{2^{m}}}\ast\widehat{u_{2^{m+n}}}\rVert_{L^{2}}\leq\lVert\chi_{m}\ast\chi_{m+n}\rVert_{l^{\infty}_{k}L^{\infty}_{\tau}}^{1/2}\left|\left||\widehat{u_{2^{m}}}|^{2}\ast|\widehat{u_{2^{m+n}}}|^{2}\right|\right|_{l^{1}_{k}L^{1}_{\tau}}^{1/2}\\
\leq\lVert\chi_{m}\ast\chi_{m+n}\rVert_{l^{\infty}_{k}L^{\infty}_{\tau}}^{1/2}\lVert u_{2^{m}}\rVert_{L^{2}}\lVert u_{2^{m+n}}\rVert_{L^{2}}.\end{split}

It remains to extract a sufficient decay (in
m,n
) from
\lVert\chi_{m}\ast\chi_{m+n}\rVert_{L^{\infty}}^{1/2}
. Since

\chi_{m}\ast\chi_{m+n}(\tau,k)=\sum_{k_{1}}\int\chi_{m}(\tau_{1},k_{1})\chi_{m+n}(\tau-\tau_{1},k-k_{1})d\tau_{1},

each non-zero integral, for a fixed
k_{1}
, is at most

. It remains to count how many
k_{1}
s gives rise to non-zero integrals. For a fixed
\tau,k
, if the integral is non-zero, then

\tau_{1}+w(k_{1})=O(2^{m}),\>\tau-\tau_{1}+w(k-k_{1})=O(2^{m+n}),

and therefore

\tau+w(k_{1})+w(k-k_{1})=O(2^{m+n}).

(2.6)

It can be shown by direct computation that

where
k_{1}^{\prime}
is re-labelled to
k_{1}

s are constrained in finitely many intervals of length

. Since

To estimate

, a similar Arzelà-Ascoli argument is used. More precisely, we have

\lVert I_{4}\rVert_{H^{1}}\leq\int_{0}^{t}\left|\left|\Big(U_{\epsilon}(t-t^{\prime})-U(t-t^{\prime})\Big)(|u|^{2}u)\right|\right|_{H^{1}}dt^{\prime}=\int_{0}^{t}\left|\left|\Big(U_{\epsilon}(t^{\prime})-U(t^{\prime})\Big)(|u(t-t^{\prime})|^{2}u(t-t^{\prime}))\right|\right|_{H^{1}}dt^{\prime},

and the last term tends to zero uniformly in
t
as
\epsilon\rightarrow 0
.

Hence for all
\epsilon>0
sufficiently small,

\lVert u^{(\epsilon)}(t)-u(t)\rVert_{H^{1}}\lesssim\delta\langle t\rangle+\int_{0}^{t}\lVert u^{(\epsilon)}(t^{\prime})-u(t^{\prime})\rVert_{H^{1}}dt^{\prime},

from which the Gronwall’s inequality yields

\lVert u^{(\epsilon)}(t)-u(t)\rVert_{H^{1}}\leq C\delta\langle t\rangle e^{Ct},

for all
t\in[0,T]
for some
C>0
. Since
\delta>0
is arbitrary, the proof is complete.
∎

&\leq\frac{|\xi_{1}|^{-1/3}}{|\xi_{1}|^{2/3}(\epsilon^{2}\xi_{1}^{2}+2)}\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{d\xi_{2}}{\langle\xi_{2}\rangle^{2b}}\simeq_{b}\frac{1}{|\xi_{1}|(\epsilon^{2}\xi_{1}^{2}+2)}.\end{split}

(A.9)

On the other hand,

\begin{split}P(\xi_{2})&=(12\epsilon^{2}r^{2}+|\xi_{1}|^{2/3}(\epsilon^{2}\xi_{1}^{2}+2))(\xi_{2}-r)+12\epsilon^{2}r(\xi_{2}-r)^{2}+4\epsilon^{2}(\xi_{2}-r)^{3}\\
&\geq 4\epsilon^{2}(\xi_{2}-r)^{3}\geq 0,\end{split}

where the inequality holds since

(12\epsilon^{2}r^{2}+|\xi_{1}|^{2/3}(\epsilon^{2}\xi_{1}^{2}+2))(\xi_{2}-r)+12\epsilon^{2}r(\xi_{2}-r)^{2}\geq 0,

on
\xi_{2}\in(r,0)
. Then,

\begin{split}|\xi_{1}|^{-1/3}\int_{r}^{0}\frac{d\xi_{2}}{\langle P(\xi_{2})\rangle^{2b}}&\leq|\xi_{1}|^{-1/3}\int_{r}^{0}\frac{d\xi_{2}}{\langle 4\epsilon^{2}(\xi_{2}-r)^{3}\rangle^{2b}}\\
&=|\xi_{1}|^{-1/3}\int_{0}^{|r|}\frac{d\xi_{2}}{\langle 4\epsilon^{2}\xi_{2}^{3}\rangle^{2b}}\leq|\xi_{1}|^{-1/3}\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{d\xi_{2}}{\langle 4\epsilon^{2}\xi_{2}^{3}\rangle^{2b}}\simeq_{b,\epsilon}|\xi_{1}|^{-1/3}.\end{split}

(A.10)

Hence

|\xi_{1}|^{-1/3}\int_{r}^{0}\frac{d\xi_{2}}{\langle P(\xi_{2})\rangle^{2b}}\lesssim_{b,\epsilon}\min\Big(\frac{1}{|\xi_{1}|(\epsilon^{2}\xi_{1}^{2}+2)},|\xi_{1}|^{-1/3}\Big)

and the desired result follows by integrating with respect to
\xi_{1}
.

(iii)
. Similarly on
\xi_{2}\in(0,\infty)

|\xi_{1}|^{-1/3}\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{d\xi_{2}}{\langle P(\xi_{2})\rangle^{2b}}\lesssim_{b,\epsilon}\min\Big(\frac{1}{|\xi_{1}|(\epsilon^{2}\xi_{1}^{2}+2)},|\xi_{1}|^{-1/3}\Big),

(A.11)

where the two lower bounds of

are used to argue as (
A.9
), (
A.10
). This concludes the proof for case I.
