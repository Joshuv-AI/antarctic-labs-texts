CICY3 Families of Nonsingular Codimension two K3
Geoffrey Mboya

Abstract
. We conduct a systematic search of codimension 2 Complete Intersection Calabi–Yau threefolds (CICY3) in rank 2 toric ambient spaces and fibered by complete intersection of a quadric and a cubic in ℂℙ 4 \mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^{4} . We classify both the nonsingular ones as well as those with isolated singularities.

is also nonsingular.
∎
