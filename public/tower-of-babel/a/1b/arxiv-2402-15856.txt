Vaishali Guleria, Eshete Gebrehana, Shashank Bhatnagar

Abstract
In this work we study the E1 decay processes, P 1 3 {}^{3}P_{1} → \rightarrow S 1 3 ​ γ {}^{3}S_{1}\gamma , and S 1 3 {}^{3}S_{1} → \rightarrow P 1 3 ​ γ {}^{3}P_{1}\gamma in the framework of Bethe-Salpeter equation and calculate their decay widths. We have used algebraic forms of Salpeter wave functions obtained through analytic solutions of mass spectral equations for ground and excited states of S 1 3 {}^{3}S_{1} , and P 1 3 {}^{3}P_{1} equal mass quarkonia in approximate harmonic oscillator basis to do analytic calculations of their decay widths. These decay widths have been compared with data and other models.

where,
|\hat{q}|
is the length of the 3-D vector,
\hat{q}
, defined as

, and is a Lorentz-invariant
[

,

) variable.

The transition amplitude,
M_{fi}

M_{fi}=-2ie_{Q}\int\frac{d^{3}\hat{q}}{(2\pi)^{3}}\frac{1}{M^{2}}Tr[\alpha_{1}{\not}P\bar{\Psi}^{++}_{A}(\hat{q}^{\prime}){\not}\epsilon^{\prime\prime}\Psi^{++}_{V}(\hat{q})]

The trace over the gamma matrices can be expressed as,

[Tr]=\alpha_{1}\bigg[X^{\prime}_{1}\epsilon_{\mu\nu\alpha\beta}\epsilon^{\prime\prime}_{\mu}\epsilon^{\prime}_{\nu}\epsilon_{\alpha}P_{\beta}+X^{\prime}_{2}\epsilon_{\mu\nu\alpha\beta}k_{\mu}\epsilon_{\nu}\epsilon^{\prime}_{\alpha}\epsilon^{\prime\prime}_{\beta}+X^{\prime}_{3}\epsilon_{\mu\nu\alpha\beta}P_{\mu}k_{\nu}\epsilon^{\prime}_{\alpha}\epsilon_{\beta}+X^{\prime}_{4}\epsilon_{\mu\nu\alpha\beta}k_{\mu}P_{\nu}\epsilon_{\alpha}\epsilon^{\prime\prime}_{\beta}+X^{\prime}_{5}\epsilon_{\mu\nu\alpha\beta}P_{\mu}k_{\nu}\epsilon^{\prime}_{\alpha}\epsilon^{\prime\prime}_{\beta}\bigg],

(17)

where,

\displaystyle X^{\prime}_{1}=b_{1}a_{1}-M^{2}b_{2}a_{2}-(\frac{M^{2}}{2}-\frac{1}{2}P^{\prime}.P)b_{3}a_{1}-M^{2}\hat{q}^{2}b_{3}a_{3}+M^{2}\hat{q}^{2}b_{3}a_{7}-(\frac{M^{2}}{2}-\frac{1}{2}P^{\prime}.P)b_{4}a_{2}-\hat{q}^{2}b_{4}a_{4}

\displaystyle X^{\prime}_{2}=M^{2}b_{2}a_{2}-(M^{2}-\frac{1}{2}P^{\prime}.P)b_{3}a_{1}+M^{2}\hat{q}^{2}b_{3}a_{3}-M^{2}\hat{q}^{2}b_{3}a_{7}+\frac{M^{2}}{2}b_{4}a_{2}

\displaystyle X^{\prime}_{3}=[-1-\frac{P^{\prime}.P}{M^{2}}]b_{3}a_{1}

\displaystyle X^{\prime}_{4}=b_{6}a_{1}+(M^{2}-\frac{P^{\prime}.P}{M^{2}})b_{7}a_{2}-\hat{q}^{2}b_{7}a_{4}

\displaystyle X^{\prime}_{5}=(-M^{2}-\frac{1}{2}P^{\prime}.P)b_{3}a_{5}-\hat{q}^{2}b_{3}a_{8}.

Thus, we can write
M_{fi}
as,

\displaystyle M_{fi}=-2ie_{Q}\frac{N_{V}N_{A}}{M^{2}}[G_{1}~\epsilon_{\mu\nu\alpha\beta}\epsilon^{\prime\prime}_{\mu}\epsilon^{\prime}_{\nu}\epsilon_{\alpha}P_{\beta}+G_{2}~\epsilon_{\mu\nu\alpha\beta}k_{\mu}\epsilon_{\nu}\epsilon^{\prime}_{\alpha}\epsilon^{\prime\prime}_{\beta}+(P.\epsilon^{\prime\prime})G_{3}~\epsilon_{\mu\nu\alpha\beta}P_{\mu}k_{\nu}\epsilon^{\prime}_{\alpha}\epsilon_{\beta}+

\displaystyle(I.\epsilon^{\prime})G_{4}~\epsilon_{\mu\nu\alpha\beta}k_{\mu}P_{\nu}\epsilon_{\alpha}\epsilon^{\prime\prime}_{\beta}+(I.\epsilon)G_{5}~\epsilon_{\mu\nu\alpha\beta}P_{\mu}k_{\nu}\epsilon^{\prime}_{\alpha}\epsilon^{\prime\prime}_{\beta}],

where,

\displaystyle G_{1}=\int\frac{d^{3}\hat{q}}{(2\pi)^{3}}\alpha_{1}X^{\prime}_{1}(\hat{q})\phi_{A}(\hat{q})\phi_{V}(\hat{q})

\displaystyle G_{2}=\int\frac{d^{3}\hat{q}}{(2\pi)^{3}}\alpha_{1}X^{\prime}_{2}(\hat{q})\phi_{A}(\hat{q})\phi_{V}(\hat{q})

\displaystyle G_{3}=\int\frac{d^{3}\hat{q}}{(2\pi)^{3}}\alpha 1X^{\prime}_{3}\phi_{A}(\hat{q})\phi_{V}(\hat{q})

\displaystyle G_{4}=\int\frac{d^{3}\hat{q}}{(2\pi)^{3}}\alpha_{1}X^{\prime}_{4}\phi_{A}(\hat{q})\phi_{V}(\hat{q})

\displaystyle G_{5}=\int\frac{d^{3}\hat{q}}{(2\pi)^{3}}\alpha_{1}X^{\prime}_{5}\phi_{A}(\hat{q})\phi_{V}(\hat{q}).

is an effective 3D vector, we can write,

, where we take

is a Lorentz-invariant variable
[

), and since the polarization vector of final axial meson

\lambda

\lambda^{\prime\prime}
of A-meson, and
\lambda^{\prime}

we can write,

|\overline{M}_{fi}|^{2}=\frac{8\pi\alpha_{em}}{81}N_{A}^{2}N_{V}^{2}|G_{2}|^{2}\frac{(M^{2}-M^{\prime 2})^{2}(M^{2}+M^{\prime 2})}{M^{6}M^{\prime 2}}.

(25)

The decay width of the process (
