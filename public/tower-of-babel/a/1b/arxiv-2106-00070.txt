U U -projectors and fields of U U -invariants
K.A.Vyatkina, A.N.Panov

Abstract
We present the general construction of the U U -projector (the homomorphism of the algebra into its field of U U -invariants identical on the subalgebra of U U -invariants). It is shown how to apply U U -projector to find the systems of free generators of the fields of U U -invariants for representations of reductive groups.

Here and further,

We extend

;

2) let

\leqslant j\leqslant k

. On the other hand, for

Let
G
be as above (i.e. it is a connected reductive split group defined over a field

of zero characteristic). We consider the representation of the group

in the space

defined by the formula
R_{g}f(s)=f(g^{-1}sg)

-projector for this representation in the algebra

Let
{\mathfrak{g}}
be the Lie algebra of the group
G
.
Let
\Pi=\{{\alpha}_{1},\ldots,{\alpha}_{n}\}
be the system of simple roots in
\Delta^{+}
, and

In each fundamental representation

with the highest weight
\phi_{i}
, we choose the highest vector
v_{i}^{+}

and the lowest vector
v_{i}^{-}
. In the dual space

, we choose the highest and the lowest vectors

and

such that
(v_{i}^{-},l_{i}^{+})=(v_{i}^{+},l_{i}^{-})=1

,

, is an
U
-invariant. Denote by

the localization of the algebra

with respect to the denominator system generated by

Order the positive roots

such that
\beta_{t}<\beta_{s}
implies

. Each
\beta_{s}\in{\Delta}^{+}
is either simple (i.e.
\beta_{s}
coincides with some
{\alpha}_{\nu(s)}\in\Pi

\beta_{s}
, let us fix
{\alpha}_{\nu(s)}\in\Pi

Let us correspond to each

the matric element

d_{\beta_{s}}(g)=(gv^{+},E_{-\beta_{s}}l^{+}).

Then for any

, we obtain

D_{x}d_{\beta_{s}}(g)=-(xgv^{+},E_{-\beta_{s}}l^{+})+(gxv^{+},E_{-\beta_{s}}l^{+})=(gv^{+},xE_{-\beta_{s}}l^{+}).

(14)

If

, then as above we simplify notation

.
The formula (
14

D_{\beta_{s}}d_{\beta_{t}}(g)=0

D_{\beta_{s}}d_{\beta_{s}}(g)=\phi(H_{\beta_{s}})d_{\nu(s)}(g).

Then for

D_{\beta_{s}}Q_{\beta_{t}}(g)=\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}0,&\mbox{if}~s>t,\\

For each

.

1) The operator

Proof

2
.

1) The inequalities
d_{i}\neq 0
,

, define the open Bruhat cell

.
Let us show that the system
\{d_{\beta_{s}}(g):~1\leqslant s\leqslant m\}

w_{0}B
in

.

On the other hand,
