A noetherian criterion for sequences of modules
Wee Liang Gan, Khoa Ta

Abstract
. We prove a noetherian criterion for a sequence of modules with linear maps between them. This generalizes a noetherian criterion of Gan and Li for infinite EI categories. We apply our criterion to the linear categories associated to certain diagram algebras defined by Patzt.

1

proved a noetherian criterion for infinite EI categories. In this note, we adapt their proof to a more general setting and apply it to the linear categories studied by Patzt
[
2
]
in the context of representation stability. In contrast to

]
and

Notation 5

(i)

(ii)

For each

Lemma 6
.

Let

be an
A
-module, let

be an

-submodule of

, and let

be an

-submodule of
N
. Let

-module;

Then

Proof.

Since

is a semisimple

-module, there exists an

-submodule

Let

Our noetherian criterion is:

Let

be an

-module such that each

is finite dimensional. Let

. Assume that for each integer

(7.2)

We adapt the proof of

]

Assume on the contrary that there exists an

Define a sequence of integers

Hence, by Lemma

\dim F^{M}_{\ell_{i+1}}(N^{(\ell_{i})})<\dim F^{M}_{\ell_{i+1}}(N^{(\ell_{i+1})}).

By (

\textstyle{F^{M}_{d+1}(M)\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces}
