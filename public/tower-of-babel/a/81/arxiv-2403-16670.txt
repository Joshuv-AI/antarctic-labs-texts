Probabilistic bivariate Bell polynomials
Taekyun Kim, Dae San Kim

Abstract
. Let Y Y be a random variable whose moment generating function exists in some neighborhood of the origin. We consider the probabilistic bivariate Bell polynomials associated with Y Y and the probabilistic bivariate r r -Bell polynomials associated with Y Y . For those polynomials, we derive the recurrence relations corresponding to the ones found by Zheng and Li for the bivariate Bell polynomials and the bivariate r r -Bell polynomials.



2010 Mathematics Subject Classification
11B73; 11B83

1.
Introduction

The Stirling number of the second kind
{n\brace k}
enumerates the number of ways to partition a set of
n
objects into
k
nonempty subsets (see (
1
)). More generally, for any
r\in\mathbb{N}
, the
r
-Stirling number of the second kind
{n+r\brace k+r}_{r}
counts the number of ways to partition a set of
n+r
objects into
k+r
nonempty subsets such that the first
r
elements are in distinct subsets (see (
2
)).

Spivey discovered a remarkable recurrence relation for the Bell numbers (see (
4
)). This relation was extended to the Bell polynomials by Gould and Quaintance (see (
5
)). The corresponding ones for the bivariate Bell polynomials
\phi_{n}(x,y)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}{n\brace k}(x)_{k}y^{k}
and the bivariate
r
-Bell polynomials
\phi_{n,r}(x,y)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}{n+r\brace k+r}_{r}(x)_{k}y^{k}

were found by Zheng and Li (see (
9
), (
10
)).

Assume that
Y
is a random variable whose moment generating function exists in some neighbourhood of the origin. Let
(Y_{j})_{j\geq 1}
be a sequence of mutually independent copies of the random variable
Y
, and let
S_{k}=Y_{1}+Y_{2}+\cdots+Y_{k},\ (k\geq 1)
, with
S_{0}=0
. In this paper, we introduce bivariate extensions of the probabilistic Bell polynomials associated
Y
and the probabilistic
r
-Bell polynomials associated with
Y
, namely the probabilistic bivariate Bell polynomials associated with
Y
,
\phi_{n}^{Y}(x,y)
(see (
16
)) and the probabilistic bivariate
r
-Bell polynomials associated with
Y
,
\phi_{n,r}^{Y}(x,y)
(see (
20
)). Then we derive the recurrence relations corresponding to the ones found by Zheng and Li for the probabilistic bivariate Bell polynomials associated with
Y
(see Theorem 2.2)

\displaystyle\phi_{m+n}^{Y}(x,y)

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\sum_{j=0}^{m}\binom{m}{j}\phi_{j}^{Y}(x-k,y)(x)_{k}y^{k}\frac{1}{k!}\sum_{l_{1}+\cdots+l_{k}=n}\binom{n}{l_{1},\dots,l_{k}}E\bigg[S_{k}^{m-j}\prod_{i=1}^{k}Y_{i}^{l_{i}}\bigg],

and also for the probabilistic bivariate
r
-Bell polynomials associated with
Y
(see Theorems 2.4 and 2.5)

\displaystyle\phi_{m+n,r}^{Y}(x,y)

\displaystyle=\sum_{i=0}^{n}\sum_{k=0}^{m}\binom{n}{i}\phi_{i,r}^{Y}(x-k,y)\frac{(x)_{k}y^{k}}{k!}\sum_{j=k}^{m}\sum_{l_{1}+\cdots+l_{k}=j}\binom{m}{j}\binom{j}{l_{1},\dots,l_{k}}r^{m-j}E\bigg[S_{k}^{n-i}\prod_{i=1}^{k}Y_{i}^{l_{i}}\bigg]

\displaystyle=\sum_{i=0}^{n}\sum_{k=0}^{m}\frac{1}{k!}\binom{n}{i}(x)_{k}y^{k}\phi_{i}^{Y}(x-k,y)\sum_{j=k}^{m}\binom{m}{j}\sum_{l_{1}+\cdots+l_{k}=j}\binom{j}{l_{1},\dots,l_{k}}E\bigg[(S_{k}+r)^{n-i}\prod_{i=1}^{k}Y_{i}^{l_{i}}\bigg]r^{m-j}.

These are done by using the simple formula for Taylor series (see (
11
)). For the rest of this section, we recall the facts that are needed throughout this paper.

For
n\geq 0
, the Stirling numbers of the second kind are given by

(1)

x^{n}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}{n\brace k}(x)_{k},\quad(\mathrm{see}\ [1-29]),

where
(x)_{0}=1,\ (x)_{k}=x(x-1)\cdots(x-k+1),\ (k\geq 1)
.

The
r
-Stirling number of the second kind are defined as

(2)

(x+r)^{n}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}{n+r\brace k+r}_{r}(x)_{k},\quad(n\geq 0),\quad(\mathrm{see}\ [8,18-20]).

The Bell polynomials are given by

(3)

\phi_{n}(x)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}{n\brace k}x^{k},\quad(n\geq 0),\quad(\mathrm{see}\ [2,10,17,25,28]).

When
x=1,\ \phi_{n}=\phi_{n}(1)
are called the Bell numbers.

Spivey found the following recurrence relation for
\phi_{n}
:

(4)

\phi_{l+n}=\sum_{k=0}^{l}\sum_{i=0}^{n}k^{n-i}\binom{n}{i}{l\brace k}\phi_{i},\quad(\mathrm{see}\ [28]).

Gould and Quaintance discovered the following extension of the relation for
\phi_{n}
in (
4
) to that for
\phi_{n}(x)
:

(5)

\phi_{l+n}(x)=\sum_{k=0}^{l}\sum_{i=0}^{n}k^{n-i}\binom{n}{i}{l\brace k}\phi_{i}(x)x^{k},\quad(\mathrm{see}\ [12,28]).

For
r\in\mathbb{N}
, the
r
-Bell polynomials are defined as

(6)

\phi_{n,r}(x)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}{n+r\brace k+r}_{r}x^{k},\quad(n\geq 0),\quad(\mathrm{see}\ [18,20]).

It is known that the bivariate Bell polynomials are given by

(7)

\phi_{n}(x,y)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}{n\brace k}(x)_{k}y^{k},\quad(\mathrm{see}\ [30]).

For
r\in\mathbb{N}
, the bivariate
r
-Bell polynomials are defined as

(8)

\phi_{n,r}(x,y)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}{n+r\brace k+r}_{r}(x)_{k}y^{k},\quad(\mathrm{see}\ [30]).

Note that

\lim_{y\rightarrow 0}\phi_{n}\bigg(\frac{x}{y},y\bigg)=\phi_{n}(x),\quad\lim_{y\rightarrow 0}\phi_{n,r}\bigg(\frac{x}{y},y\bigg)=\phi_{n,r}(x),\quad(n\geq 0).

From (
7
) and (
8
), we note that

(9)

\displaystyle\phi_{m+n}(x,y)

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{m}\sum_{i=0}^{n}k^{n-i}\binom{n}{i}{m\brace k}\phi_{i}(x-k,y)(x)_{k}y^{k},

(10)

\displaystyle\phi_{m+n,r}(x,y)

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{m}\sum_{i=0}^{n}k^{n-i}\binom{n}{i}{m+r\brace k+r}_{r}\phi_{i,r}(x-k,y)(x)_{k}y^{k},\quad(\mathrm{see}\ [30]),

where
m,n
are nonnegative integers.

We observe that

(11)

\displaystyle f(x+t)

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{f^{(n)}(x)}{n!}(x+t-x)^{n}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{f^{(n)}(x)}{n!}t^{n}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{t^{n}D_{x}^{n}}{n!}f(x)=e^{tD_{x}}f(x),

where
D_{x}=\frac{d}{dx}
.

Assume that
Y
is a random variable such that the moment generating function of
Y
,

E\big[e^{tY}\big]=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}E[Y^{n}],\quad(|t|<r),

exists for some
r>0
, (see [3,16,17]).
Let
(Y_{j})_{j\geq 1}
be a sequence of mutually independent copies of the random variable
Y
, and let
S_{k}=Y_{1}+Y_{2}+\cdots+Y_{k},\ (k\geq 1)
, with
S_{0}=0
.

We recall that the probabilistic Stirling numbers of the second kind associated with
Y
are given by

(12)

{n\brace k}_{Y}=\frac{1}{k!}\sum_{i=0}^{k}\binom{k}{i}(-1)^{k-i}E\big[S_{i}^{n}\big],\quad(0\leq k\leq n),\quad(\mathrm{see}\ [3,16,17]).

The probabilistic Bell polynomials associated with
Y
are defined as

(13)

\phi_{n}^{Y}(x)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}{n\brace k}_{Y}x^{k},\quad(n\geq 0),\quad(\mathrm{see}\ [16,17,27]).

For
r\in\mathbb{N}
, the probabilistic
r
-Stirling numbers of the second kind associated with
Y
are given by

(14)

{n+r\brace k+r}_{r,Y}=\frac{1}{k!}\sum_{j=0}^{k}\binom{k}{j}(-1)^{k-j}E\big[(S_{j}+r)^{n}\big],\quad(n\geq k\geq 0),\quad(\mathrm{see}\ [16,17]).

When
Y=1
, we have

{n+r\brace k+r}_{r,Y}={n+r\brace k+r}_{r},\ (n\geq k\geq 0).

The probabilistic
r
-Bell polynomials associated with
Y
are defined as

(15)

\phi_{n,r}^{Y}(x)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}{n+r\brace k+r}_{r,Y}x^{k},\quad(n\geq 0),\quad(\mathrm{see}\ [16,17]).

When
Y=1
, we have
\phi_{n,r}^{Y}(x)=\phi_{n,r}(x),\ (n\geq 0)
.

2.
Probabilistic bivariate Bell polynomials

Let
(Y_{j})_{j\geq 1}
be a sequence of mutually independent copies of the random variable
Y
, and let

S_{0}=0,\quad S_{k}=Y_{1}+Y_{2}+\cdots+Y_{k},\ (k\geq 1).

Now, we define the
probabilistic bivariate Bell polynomials associated with
Y
by

(16)

\Big(1+y\big(E\big[e^{Yt}\big]-1\big)\Big)^{x}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\phi_{n}^{Y}(x,y)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

From (
16
), we note that

(17)

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}

\displaystyle\phi_{n}^{Y}(x,y)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}(x)_{k}y^{k}\frac{1}{k!}\Big(E\big[e^{Yt}\big]-1\Big)^{k}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}(x)_{k}y^{k}\sum_{n=k}^{\infty}{n\brace k}_{Y}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\bigg(\sum_{k=0}^{n}{n\brace k}_{Y}(x)_{k}y^{k}\bigg)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

Therefore, by comparing the coefficients on both sides of (
17
), we obtain the following theorem.

Theorem 2.1
.

For
n\geq 0
, we have

\phi_{n}^{Y}(x,y)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}{n\brace k}_{Y}(x)_{k}y^{k}.

When
Y=1,\phi_{n}^{Y}(x,y)=\phi_{n}(x,y)
.
As
\lim_{y\rightarrow 0}\phi_{n}^{Y}\big(\frac{x}{y},y\big)=\phi_{n}^{Y}(x)
, the probabilistic bivariate Bell polynomials
\phi_{n}^{Y}(x,y)
are also a bivariate extension of the probabilistic Bell polynomials
\phi_{n}^{Y}(x)
.

From (
16
), we note that

(18)

\displaystyle e^{sD_{t}}\Big(1+y\big(E\big[e^{Yt}\big]-1\big)\Big)^{x}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{s^{n}}{n!}D_{t}^{n}\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\phi_{m}^{Y}(x,y)\frac{t^{m}}{m!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\phi_{m+n}^{Y}(x,y)\frac{s^{n}}{n!}\frac{t^{m}}{m!}.

On the other hand, by (
11
), we get

(19)

\displaystyle e^{sD_{t}}\Big(1+y\big(E\big[e^{Yt}\big]-1\big)\Big)^{x}=\Big(1+y\big(E\big[e^{(s+t)Y}\big]-1\big)\Big)^{x}

\displaystyle=\Big(1+y\big(E\big[e^{Yt}\big]-1\big)+yE\big[e^{Yt}(e^{sY}-1)\big]\Big)^{x}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\binom{x}{k}\Big(1+y\big(E\big[e^{Yt}\big]-1\big)\Big)^{x-k}y^{k}\Big(E\big[e^{Yt}(e^{sY}-1)\big]\Big)^{k}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{(x)_{k}}{k!}y^{k}\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\phi_{j}^{Y}(x-k,y)\frac{t^{j}}{j!}E\Big[e^{S_{k}t}(e^{sY_{1}}-1)(e^{sY_{2}}-1)\cdots(e^{sY_{k}}-1)\Big]

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{(x)_{k}}{k!}y^{k}\sum_{n=k}^{\infty}\sum_{l_{1}+l_{2}+\cdots+l_{k}=n}\binom{n}{l_{1},l_{2},\dots,l_{k}}E\Big[e^{S_{k}t}Y_{1}^{l_{1}}Y_{2}^{l_{2}}\cdots Y_{k}^{l_{k}}\Big]\frac{s^{n}}{n!}\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\phi_{j}^{Y}(x-k,y)\frac{t^{j}}{j!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\sum_{k=0}^{n}(x)_{k}y^{k}\frac{1}{k!}\sum_{l_{1}+\cdots+l_{k}=n}\binom{n}{l_{1},\dots,l_{k}}\frac{s^{n}}{n!}\sum_{l=0}^{\infty}E\bigg[S_{k}^{l}\prod_{i=1}^{k}Y_{i}^{l_{i}}\bigg]\frac{t^{l}}{l!}\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\phi_{j}^{Y}(x-k,y)\frac{t^{j}}{j!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\sum_{k=0}^{n}\sum_{j=0}^{m}\binom{m}{j}\phi_{j}^{Y}(x-k,y)(x)_{k}y^{k}\frac{1}{k!}\sum_{l_{1}+\cdots+l_{k}=n}\binom{n}{l_{1},\dots,l_{k}}E\bigg[S_{k}^{m-j}\prod_{i=1}^{k}Y_{i}^{l_{i}}\bigg]\frac{s^{n}}{n!}\frac{t^{m}}{m!},

where
l_{1},l_{2},\dots,l_{k}
are positive integers.

Therefore, by (
18
) and (
19
), we obtain the following theorem.

Theorem 2.2
.

For
m,n\geq 0
, we have

\displaystyle\phi_{m+n}^{Y}(x,y)

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\sum_{j=0}^{m}\binom{m}{j}\phi_{j}^{Y}(x-k,y)(x)_{k}y^{k}\frac{1}{k!}\sum_{l_{1}+\cdots+l_{k}=n}\binom{n}{l_{1},\dots,l_{k}}E\bigg[S_{k}^{m-j}\prod_{i=1}^{k}Y_{i}^{l_{i}}\bigg],

where
l_{1},l_{2},\dots,l_{k}
are positive integers.

When
Y=1
, the expression in Theorem 2.2 reduces to

\displaystyle\phi_{m+n}(x,y)

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\sum_{j=0}^{m}\binom{m}{j}\phi_{j}(x-k,y)(x)_{k}y^{k}\frac{1}{k!}\sum_{l_{1}+\cdots+l_{k}=n}\binom{n}{l_{1},\dots,l_{k}}k^{m-j}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\sum_{j=0}^{m}\binom{m}{j}\phi_{j}(x-k,y)(x)_{k}y^{k}{n\brace k}k^{m-j}.

For
r\in\mathbb{N}
, we define the
probabilistic bivariate
r
-Bell polynomials associated with
Y
by

(20)

\Big(1+y\big(E\big[e^{Yt}\big]-1\big)\Big)^{x}e^{rt}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\phi_{n,r}^{Y}(x,y)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

From (
14
), we note that

(21)

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\phi_{n,r}^{Y}(x,y)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}

\displaystyle=\Big(1+y\big(E\big[e^{Yt}\big]-1\big)\Big)^{x}e^{rt}=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}(x)_{k}\frac{y^{k}}{k!}\big(E\big[e^{Yt}\big]-1\big)^{k}e^{rt}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}y^{k}(x)_{k}\sum_{n=k}^{\infty}{n+r\brace k+r}_{r,Y}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\sum_{k=0}^{n}y^{k}(x)_{k}{n+r\brace k+r}_{r,Y}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}.

Therefore, by comparing the coefficients on both sides of (
21
), we obtain the following theorem.

Theorem 2.3
.

For
n\geq 0
, we have

\phi_{n,r}^{Y}(x,y)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}{n+r\brace k+r}_{r,Y}(x)_{k}y^{k}.

When
Y=1
, we obtain

\phi_{n,r}(x,y)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}{n+r\brace k+r}_{r}(x)_{k}y^{k}.

In addition, we note that

\lim_{y\rightarrow 0}\phi_{n,r}^{Y}\bigg(\frac{x}{y},y\bigg)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}{n+r\brace k+r}_{r,Y}x^{k}=\phi_{n,r}^{Y}(x).

By using (
11
), we have

(22)

\displaystyle e^{tD_{s}}\Big(1+y\big(E\big[e^{sY}\big]-1\big)\Big)^{x}e^{rs}=\Big(1+y\big(E\big[e^{(s+t)Y}\big]-1\big)\Big)^{x}e^{(s+t)r}

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\binom{x}{k}\Big(1+y\big(E\big[e^{tY}\big]-1\big)\Big)^{x-k}e^{rt}y^{k}\Big(E\big[e^{tY}\big(e^{sY}-1\big)\big]\Big)^{k}e^{sr}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{(x)_{k}y^{k}}{k!}\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}\phi_{i,r}^{Y}(x-k,y)\frac{t^{i}}{i!}\sum_{j=k}^{\infty}\sum_{l_{1}+\cdots+l_{k}=j}\binom{j}{l_{1},\dots,l_{k}}E\bigg[e^{S_{k}t}\prod_{i=1}^{k}Y_{i}^{l_{i}}\bigg]\frac{s^{j}}{j!}e^{sr}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{(x)_{k}y^{k}}{k!}\sum_{m=k}^{\infty}\sum_{j=k}^{m}\binom{m}{j}\sum_{l_{1}+\cdots+l_{k}=j}\binom{j}{l_{1},\dots,l_{k}}E\bigg[e^{S_{k}t}\prod_{i=1}^{k}Y_{i}^{l_{i}}\bigg]r^{m-j}\frac{s^{m}}{m!}\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}\phi_{i,r}^{Y}(x-k,y)\frac{t^{i}}{i!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\sum_{l=0}^{\infty}\sum_{k=0}^{m}\frac{(x)_{k}y^{k}}{k!}\sum_{j=k}^{m}\sum_{l_{1}+\cdots+l_{k}=j}\binom{m}{j}\binom{j}{l_{1},\dots,l_{k}}r^{m-j}\frac{s^{m}}{m!}E\bigg[S_{k}^{l}\prod_{i=1}^{k}Y_{i}^{l_{i}}\bigg]\frac{t^{l}}{l!}\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}\phi_{i,r}^{Y}(x-k,y)\frac{t^{i}}{i!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\sum_{i=0}^{n}\sum_{k=0}^{m}\binom{n}{i}\phi_{i,r}^{Y}(x-k,y)\frac{(x)_{k}y^{k}}{k!}

\displaystyle\quad\quad\quad\quad\times\sum_{j=k}^{m}\sum_{l_{1}+\cdots+l_{k}=j}\binom{m}{j}\binom{j}{l_{1},\dots,l_{k}}r^{m-j}E\bigg[S_{k}^{n-i}\prod_{i=1}^{k}Y_{i}^{l_{i}}\bigg]\frac{t^{n}}{n!}\frac{s^{m}}{m!},

where
l_{1},l_{2},\dots,l_{k}
are positive integers.

On the other hand, by (
20
), we get

(23)

\displaystyle e^{tD_{s}}\Big(1+y\big(E\big[e^{sY}\big]-1\big)\Big)^{x}e^{rs}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{t^{n}}{n!}D_{s}^{n}\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\phi_{m,r}^{Y}(x,y)\frac{s^{m}}{m!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\phi_{m+n,r}^{Y}(x,y)\frac{t^{n}}{n!}\frac{s^{m}}{m!}.

Therefore, by (
22
) and (
23
), we obtain the following theorem.

Theorem 2.4
.

For
m,n\geq 0
, we have

\displaystyle\phi_{m+n,r}^{Y}(x,y)

\displaystyle=\sum_{i=0}^{n}\sum_{k=0}^{m}\binom{n}{i}\phi_{i,r}^{Y}(x-k,y)\frac{(x)_{k}y^{k}}{k!}\sum_{j=k}^{m}\sum_{l_{1}+\cdots+l_{k}=j}\binom{m}{j}\binom{j}{l_{1},\dots,l_{k}}r^{m-j}E\bigg[S_{k}^{n-i}\prod_{i=1}^{k}Y_{i}^{l_{i}}\bigg],

where
l_{1},l_{2},\dots,l_{k}
are positive integers.

When
Y=1
, the expression in Theorem 2.4 boils down to

\displaystyle\phi_{m+n,r}(x,y)

\displaystyle=\sum_{i=0}^{n}\sum_{k=0}^{m}\binom{n}{i}\phi_{i,r}(x-k,y)\frac{(x)_{k}y^{k}}{k!}\sum_{j=k}^{m}\binom{m}{j}\sum_{l_{1}+\cdots+l_{k}=j}\binom{j}{l_{1},\dots,l_{k}}r^{m-j}k^{n-i}

\displaystyle=\sum_{i=0}^{n}\sum_{k=0}^{m}\binom{n}{i}\phi_{i,r}(x-k,y)(x)_{k}y^{k}k^{n-i}{m+r\brace k+r}_{r}.

Now, we observe that

(24)

\displaystyle e^{tD_{s}}\Big(1+y\big(E\big[e^{sY}\big]-1\big)\Big)^{x}e^{rs}=\Big(1+y\big(E\big[e^{(s+t)Y}\big]-1\big)\Big)^{x}e^{r(s+t)}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{(x)_{k}y^{k}}{k!}\Big(1+y\big(E\big[e^{tY}\big]-1\big)\Big)^{x-k}E\bigg[e^{(S_{k}+r)t}\prod_{i=1}^{k}\big(e^{sY_{i}}-1\big)\bigg]e^{sr}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{(x)_{k}y^{k}}{k!}\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}\phi_{i}^{Y}(x-k,y)\frac{t^{i}}{i!}\sum_{j=k}^{\infty}\sum_{l_{1}+\cdots+l_{k}=j}\binom{j}{l_{1},\dots,l_{k}}E\bigg[e^{(S_{k}+r)t}\prod_{i=1}^{k}Y_{i}^{l_{i}}\bigg]\frac{s^{j}}{j!}e^{sr}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{(x)_{k}y^{k}}{k!}\sum_{m=k}^{\infty}\sum_{j=k}^{m}\binom{m}{j}\sum_{l_{1}+\cdots+l_{k}=j}\binom{j}{l_{1},\dots,l_{k}}E\bigg[e^{(S_{k}+r)t}\prod_{i=1}^{k}Y_{i}^{l_{i}}\bigg]r^{m-j}\frac{s^{m}}{m!}\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}\phi_{i}^{Y}(x-k,y)\frac{t^{i}}{i!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\sum_{k=0}^{m}\frac{(x)_{k}y^{k}}{k!}\sum_{j=k}^{m}\binom{m}{j}\sum_{l_{1}+\cdots+l_{k}=j}\binom{j}{l_{1},\dots,l_{k}}E\bigg[e^{(S_{k}+r)t}\prod_{i=1}^{k}Y_{i}^{l_{i}}\bigg]r^{m-j}\frac{s^{m}}{m!}\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}\phi_{i}^{Y}(x-k,y)\frac{t^{i}}{i!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\sum_{l=0}^{\infty}\sum_{k=0}^{m}\frac{(x)_{k}y^{k}}{k!}\sum_{j=k}^{m}\binom{m}{j}\sum_{l_{1}+\cdots+l_{k}=j}\binom{j}{l_{1},\dots,l_{k}}r^{m-j}\frac{s^{m}}{m!}

\displaystyle\quad\quad\quad\quad\times E\bigg[(S_{k}+r)^{l}\prod_{i=1}^{k}Y_{i}^{l_{i}}\bigg]\frac{t^{l}}{l!}\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}\phi_{i}^{Y}(x-k,y)\frac{t^{i}}{i!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\sum_{i=0}^{n}\sum_{k=0}^{m}\binom{n}{i}\frac{(x)_{k}y^{k}}{k!}\phi_{i}^{Y}(x-k,y)\sum_{j=k}^{m}\binom{m}{j}\sum_{l_{1}+\cdots+l_{k}=j}\binom{j}{l_{1},\dots,l_{k}}

\displaystyle\quad\quad\quad\quad\times E\bigg[(S_{k}+r)^{n-i}\prod_{i=1}^{k}Y_{i}^{l_{i}}\bigg]r^{m-j}\frac{s^{m}}{m!}\frac{t^{n}}{n!},

where
l_{1},l_{2},\dots,l_{k}
are positive integers.

Therefore, by (
23
) and (
24
), we obtain the following theorem.

Theorem 2.5
.

For
m,n\geq 0
, we have

\displaystyle\phi_{m+n,r}^{Y}(x,y)

\displaystyle=\sum_{i=0}^{n}\sum_{k=0}^{m}\frac{1}{k!}\binom{n}{i}(x)_{k}y^{k}\phi_{i}^{Y}(x-k,y)\sum_{j=k}^{m}\binom{m}{j}\sum_{l_{1}+\cdots+l_{k}=j}\binom{j}{l_{1},\dots,l_{k}}E\bigg[(S_{k}+r)^{n-i}\prod_{i=1}^{k}Y_{i}^{l_{i}}\bigg]r^{m-j},

where
l_{1},l_{2},\dots,l_{k}
are positive integers.

When
Y=1
, the expression in Theorem 2.5 reduces to

\displaystyle\phi_{m+n,r}(x,y)

\displaystyle=\sum_{i=0}^{n}\sum_{k=0}^{m}\binom{n}{i}(x)_{k}y^{k}\phi_{i}(x-k,y)\frac{1}{k!}\sum_{j=k}^{m}\binom{m}{j}\sum_{l_{1}+\cdots+l_{k}=j}\binom{j}{l_{1},\dots,l_{k}}r^{m-j}(r+k)^{n-i}

\displaystyle=\sum_{i=0}^{n}\sum_{k=0}^{m}\binom{n}{i}(x)_{k}y^{k}\phi_{i}(x-k,y){m+r\brace k+r}_{r}(r+k)^{n-i}.

From Theorem 2.4, we note that

(25)

\displaystyle\phi_{m+n,r}^{Y}(x)=\lim_{y\rightarrow 0}\phi_{m+n}^{Y}\bigg(\frac{x}{y},y\bigg)

\displaystyle=\lim_{y\rightarrow 0}\sum_{i=0}^{n}\sum_{k=0}^{m}\binom{n}{i}\phi_{i,r}^{Y}\bigg(\frac{x}{y}-k,y\bigg)\bigg(\frac{x}{y}\bigg)_{k}y^{k}\frac{1}{k!}

\displaystyle\quad\quad\quad\quad\times\sum_{j=k}^{m}\binom{m}{j}\sum_{l_{1}+\cdots+l_{k}=j}\binom{j}{l_{1},\dots,l_{k}}r^{m-j}E\bigg[S_{k}^{n-i}\prod_{i=1}^{k}Y_{i}^{l_{i}}\bigg]

\displaystyle=\sum_{i=0}^{n}\sum_{k=0}^{m}\binom{n}{i}\phi_{i,r}^{Y}(x)x^{k}\frac{1}{k!}\sum_{j=k}^{m}\binom{m}{j}\sum_{l_{1}+\cdots+l_{k}=j}\binom{j}{l_{1},\dots,l_{k}}r^{m-j}E\bigg[S_{k}^{n-i}\prod_{i=1}^{k}Y_{i}^{l_{i}}\bigg],

where
l_{1},l_{2},\dots,l_{k}
are positive integers.

Therefore, by (
25
), we obtain the following theorem.

Theorem 2.6
.

For
m,n\geq 0
, we have

\displaystyle\phi_{m+n,r}^{Y}(x)

\displaystyle=\sum_{i=0}^{n}\sum_{k=0}^{m}\binom{n}{i}\phi_{i,r}^{Y}(x)x^{k}\frac{1}{k!}\sum_{j=k}^{m}\binom{m}{j}\sum_{l_{1}+\cdots+l_{k}=j}\binom{j}{l_{1},\dots,l_{k}}r^{m-j}E\bigg[S_{k}^{n-i}\prod_{i=1}^{k}Y_{i}^{l_{i}}\bigg],

where
l_{1},l_{2},\dots,l_{k}
are positive integers.

From Theorem 2.5, we get

(26)

\displaystyle\phi_{m+n,r}^{Y}(x)

\displaystyle=\lim_{y\rightarrow 0}\phi_{m+n,r}^{Y}\bigg(\frac{x}{y},y\bigg)

\displaystyle=\lim_{y\rightarrow 0}\sum_{i=0}^{n}\sum_{k=0}^{m}\binom{n}{i}\bigg(\frac{x}{y}\bigg)_{k}y^{k}\phi_{i}^{Y}\bigg(\frac{x}{y}-k,y\bigg)\frac{1}{k!}\sum_{j=k}^{m}\binom{m}{j}

\displaystyle\quad\quad\quad\quad\times\sum_{l_{1}+\cdots+l_{k}=j}\binom{j}{l_{1},\dots,l_{k}}E\bigg[(S_{k}+r)^{n-i}\prod_{i=1}^{k}Y_{i}^{l_{i}}\bigg]r^{m-j}

\displaystyle=\sum_{i=0}^{n}\sum_{k=0}^{m}\binom{n}{i}x^{k}\phi_{i}^{Y}(x)\frac{1}{k!}\sum_{j=k}^{m}\binom{m}{j}\sum_{l_{1}+\cdots+l_{k}=j}\binom{j}{l_{1},\dots,l_{k}}E\bigg[(S_{k}+r)^{n-i}\prod_{i=1}^{k}Y_{i}^{l_{i}}\bigg]r^{m-j},

where
l_{1},l_{2},\dots,l_{k}
are positive integers.

Therefore, by (
26
), we obtain the following theorem.

Theorem 2.7
.

For
m,n\geq 0
, we have

\phi_{m+n,r}^{Y}(x)=\sum_{i=0}^{n}\sum_{k=0}^{m}\binom{n}{i}x^{k}\phi_{i}^{Y}(x)\frac{1}{k!}\sum_{j=k}^{m}\binom{m}{j}\sum_{l_{1}+\cdots+l_{k}=j}\binom{j}{l_{1},\dots,l_{k}}E\bigg[(S_{k}+r)^{n-i}\prod_{i=1}^{k}Y_{i}^{l_{i}}\bigg]r^{m-j},

where
l_{1},l_{2},\dots,l_{k}
are positive integers.

When
Y=1
, the expression in Theorem 2.7 boils down to

\displaystyle\phi_{m+n}(x)

\displaystyle=\sum_{i=0}^{n}\sum_{k=0}^{m}\binom{n}{i}x^{k}\phi_{i}(x)\frac{1}{k!}\sum_{j=k}^{m}\binom{m}{j}\sum_{l_{1}+\cdots+l_{k}=j}r^{m-j}(k+r)^{n-i}

\displaystyle=\sum_{i=0}^{n}\sum_{k=0}^{m}\binom{n}{i}x^{k}\phi_{i}(x){m+r\brace k+r}_{r}(k+r)^{n-i}.

3.
Conclusion

Let
Y
be a random variable such that the moment generating function of
Y
exists in a neighborhood of the origin. We introduced probabilistic extensions of the bivariate Bell polynomials and the bivariate
r
-Bell polynomials, namely the probabilistic bivariate Bell polynomials associated with
Y
and the probabilistic bivariate
r
-Bell polynomials associated with
Y
. Zheng and Li found recurrence relations for the bivariate Bell polynomials and the bivariate
r
-Bell polynomials. In this paper, by utilizing the simple fact (
11
) we obtained recurrence relations for the probabilistic bivariate Bell polynomials associated with
Y
and the probabilistic bivariate
r
-Bell polynomials associated with
Y
. These relations boil down to the
relations found by Zheng and Li when
Y=1
.

As one of our future projects, we would like to continue to study probabilistic extensions of many special polynomials and numbers and to find their applications to physics, science and engineering as well as to mathematics.
