On the Serre conjecture for Artin characters in the geometric case
Tomoyuki Abe

Abstract
Let G G be a finite group and A A be a regular local ring on which G G acts. Under certain assumptions on A A and the action, Serre defined a function a G : G → ℤ a_{G}\colon G\rightarrow\mathbb{Z} which can be viewed as a higher dimensional analogue of Artin character, and conjectured that it is associated to a ℚ ℓ \mathbb{Q}_{\ell} -rational representation of G G for any prime ℓ \ell invertible in A A . We prove this conjecture in the equal characteristic case.

Concluding remarks

3.1

There are at least two geometric constructions of the classical Artin representation in the literature:
one by Laumon
[
La
, 2.6.1]
that we have used, and the other by Katz
[
K
, 1.6]
.
These two constructions are not totally independent, and the idea of the proof of Theorem
1.6
clarifies a relation.
Let
k
be a separably closed field and put
K:=\mathrm{Frac}(k\{t\})
as before.
Let

be a finite Galois extension with Galois group

We put
\mathrm{Reg}_{G}:=f_{*}\mathbb{Z}_{\ell}

on
\mathbb{G}_{\mathrm{m},t}
(
e.g.
, canonical extension)
such that it coincides with
\mathrm{Reg}_{G}
around

and tame at
\infty

Let

be the open immersions.
Katz showed that
\mathrm{H}^{1}\bigl(\mathbb{P}^{1},j^{\prime}_{!}\mathrm{R}j_{*}(\mathrm{Reg}_{G})^{\sim}\bigr)
viewed as a
\mathbb{Z}_{\ell}[G]
-module realizes the Swan representation.
Consider
\mathcal{M}:=j^{\prime}_{!}\mathrm{R}j_{*}(\mathrm{Reg}_{G})^{\sim}\otimes\mathcal{L}(tt^{\prime})
on
\mathbb{P}^{1}_{t}\times\mathbb{P}^{1}_{t^{\prime}}
using the notations of Theorem
1.6
.
We consider

on
\mathbb{P}^{1}_{t^{\prime}}
, where
\mathrm{pr}^{\prime}\colon\mathbb{P}^{1}_{t}\times\mathbb{P}^{1}_{t^{\prime}}\rightarrow\mathbb{P}^{1}_{t^{\prime}}

Invoking
[

.
For a geometric point

Since

3.3

In Theorem

The right hand side of (

This is nothing but the (negative of) Milnor number

Note that,

In the case where

In this case,

(at

Since

It might be interesting to generalize this picture to higher dimensional situation.
