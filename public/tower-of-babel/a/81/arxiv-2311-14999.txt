ANCs of the bound states of 16 O deduced from elastic α \alpha - 12 C scattering data
Shung-Ichi Ando

Abstract
Asymptotic normalization coefficients (ANCs) of the 0 1 + 0_{1}^{+} , 0 2 + 0_{2}^{+} , 1 1 − 1_{1}^{-} , 2 1 + 2_{1}^{+} , 3 1 − 3_{1}^{-} ( l i ​ t ​ h π l_{ith}^{\pi} ) bound states of 16 O are deduced from the phase shift data of elastic α \alpha - 12 C scattering at low energies. S S matrices of elastic α \alpha - 12 C scattering are constructed within cluster effective field theory (EFT), in which both bound and resonant states of 16 O are considered. Parameters in the S S matrices are fitted to the precise phase shift data below the p p - 15 N breakup energy for the partial waves of l = 0 , 1 , 2 , 3 , 4 , 5 , 6 l=0,1,2,3,4,5,6 , and the ANCs are calculated by using the wave function normalization factors of 16 O propagators for l = 0 , 1 , 2 , 3 l=0,1,2,3 . We review the values of ANCs, which are compared with other results in the literature, and discuss uncertainties of the ANCs obtained from the elastic α \alpha - 12 C scattering data in cluster EFT.
