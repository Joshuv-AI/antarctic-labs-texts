1+2 dimensional radially symmetric wave maps revisit.
Yi Zhou

Abstract
The author gives an alternative and simple proof of the global existence of smooth solutions to the Cauchy problem for wave maps from the 1+2-dimensional Minkowski space to an arbitrary compact smooth Riemannian manifold without boundary, for arbitrary smooth, radially symmetric data. the author can also treat non-compact manifold under some additional assumptions which generalize the existing ones.



Keywords
: Cauchy problem, wave maps, global smooth solution

The main purpose of this paper is to give an alternative proof of the global existence for 1+2 dimensional wave maps with radial symmetry.
We may assume that TN is parallelizable. Let

there exists a unique, smooth solution

to the Cauchy problem (1.1)(1.2), defined for all time.

3
New div curl Lemma

The purpose of this section is to prove the following lemma3.1 and lemma3.2. We call Lemma3.2 a new div curl Lemma.

Lemma 3.1
.

Suppose that

\begin{cases}\partial_{u}F^{11}+\partial_{v}F^{12}=G^{1}\ \ (r\geq 0,0\leq t\leq T)\\
\partial_{u}F^{21}-\partial_{v}F^{22}=G^{2}\ \ (r\geq 0,0\leq t\leq T),\end{cases}

(3.1)

where
F^{11},F^{12},F^{21},F^{22}
are all nonnegative. Moreover,

\displaystyle r=0:\ F^{11}-F^{12}=F^{21}+F^{22}=0.

(3.2)

Then there hold

\displaystyle\sup_{u}\int_{|u|}^{2T-u}F^{11}(u,v)du+\sup_{v}\int_{-v}^{min\{v,2T-v\}}F^{12}(u,v)du

\displaystyle\lesssim\int_{0}^{\infty}(F^{11}+F^{12})(0,r)dr+\int_{0}^{T}\int_{0}^{\infty}|G_{1}|(t,r)drdt,

(3.3)

\displaystyle\sup_{u}\int_{|u|}^{2T-u}F^{21}(u,v)du+\sup_{v}\int_{-v}^{min\{v,2T-v\}}F^{22}(u,v)du

\displaystyle\lesssim\int_{0}^{\infty}(F^{21}+F^{22})(T,r)dr+\int_{0}^{\infty}(F^{21}+F^{22})(0,r)dr+\int_{0}^{T}\int_{0}^{\infty}|G_{2}|(t,r)drdt.

Proof
We only prove

\displaystyle\sup_{u}\int_{|u|}^{2T-u}F^{11}(u,v)du

\displaystyle\lesssim\int_{0}^{\infty}(F^{11}+F^{12})(0,r)dr+\int_{0}^{T}\int_{0}^{\infty}|G_{1}|(t,r)drdt.

The other estimates are similar. Noting(3.2), we have

\displaystyle\partial_{u}\int_{|u|}^{2T-u}F^{11}(u,v)du

\displaystyle=-F^{11}|_{v=2T-u}-F^{11}|_{v=|u|}sgn(u)+\int_{|u|}^{2T-u}\partial_{u}F^{11}dv

\displaystyle=-F^{11}|_{v=2T-u}-F^{11}|_{v=|u|}sgn(u)-\int_{|u|}^{2T-u}\partial_{v}F^{12}dv+\int_{|u|}^{2T-u}|G_{1}|dv

\displaystyle=-F^{11}|_{v=2T-u}-F^{12}|_{v=2T-u}-F^{11}|_{v=|u|}sgn(u)+F^{12}|_{v=|u|}+\int_{|u|}^{2T-u}|G_{1}|dv

\displaystyle\lesssim(F^{11}+F^{12})|_{t=0}+\int_{|u|}^{2T-u}|G_{1}|dv.

Integration in
u
yields the desired estimate.

Lemma 3.2
.

Under the assumption of Lemma3.1, we have

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{T}\int_{0}^{\infty}(F^{11}F^{22}+F^{12}F^{21})(t,r)drdt

\displaystyle\lesssim\left(\int_{0}^{\infty}(F^{11}+F^{12})(0,r)dr+\int_{0}^{T}\int_{0}^{\infty}|G_{1}(t,r)|drdt\right)

\displaystyle\cdot\left(\int_{0}^{\infty}(F^{21}+F^{22})(0,r)dr+\int_{0}^{\infty}(F^{21}+F^{22})(T,r)dr+\int_{0}^{T}\int_{0}^{\infty}|G_{2}(t,r)|drdt\right).

Proof
Similary to the proof of Lemma3.1, for
\bar{v}\leq 2T-u
, we get

\displaystyle\partial_{u}\int_{|u|}^{\bar{v}}F^{11}(u,v)dv+F^{12}(u,\bar{v})

\displaystyle\lesssim(F^{11}+F^{12})|_{u+\bar{v}=0}+\int_{|u|}^{\bar{v}}G_{1}dv.

Thus

\displaystyle F^{21}(u,v)\partial_{u}\int_{|u|}^{\bar{v}}F^{11}(u,v)dv+F^{21}(u,\bar{v})F^{12}(u,\bar{v})

\displaystyle\lesssim F^{21}(u,\bar{v})(F^{11}+F^{12})|_{u+\bar{v}=0}+\left(\int_{|u|}^{\bar{v}}G_{1}(u,v)dv\right)F^{21}(u,\bar{v}).

By the second equation in (3.1), the first term is

\displaystyle\partial_{u}\left[F^{21}(u,\bar{v})\int_{|u|}^{\bar{v}}F^{11}(u,v)dv\right]-\partial_{u}F^{21}(u,\bar{v})\int_{|u|}^{\bar{v}}F^{11}(u,v)dv

\displaystyle=\partial_{u}\left[F^{21}(u,\bar{v})\int_{|u|}^{\bar{v}}F^{11}(u,v)dv\right]-\partial_{\bar{v}}F^{22}(u,\bar{v})\int_{|u|}^{\bar{v}}F^{11}(u,v)dv

\displaystyle-G_{2}(u,\bar{v})\int_{|u|}^{\bar{v}}F^{11}(u,v)dv

\displaystyle=\partial_{u}\left[F^{21}(u,\bar{v})\int_{|u|}^{\bar{v}}F^{11}(u,v)dv\right]-\partial_{\bar{v}}\left[F^{22}(u,\bar{v})\int_{|u|}^{\bar{v}}F^{11}(u,v)dv\right]

\displaystyle+F^{22}(u,\bar{v})F^{11}(u,\bar{v})-G_{2}(u,\bar{v})\int_{|u|}^{\bar{v}}F^{11}(u,v)dv.

Therefore, we get

\displaystyle F^{22}(u,\bar{v})F^{11}(u,\bar{v})+F^{21}(u,\bar{v})F^{12}(u,\bar{v})

\displaystyle\lesssim\partial_{\bar{v}}\left[F^{22}(u,\bar{v})\int_{|u|}^{\bar{v}}F^{11}(u,v)dv\right]-\partial_{u}\left[F^{21}(u,\bar{v})\int_{|u|}^{\bar{v}}F^{11}(u,v)dv\right]

\displaystyle+G_{2}(u,\bar{v})\int_{|u|}^{\bar{v}}F^{11}(u,v)dv+F^{21}(u,\bar{v})(F^{11}+F^{12})|_{u+\bar{v}=0}+\left(\int_{|u|}^{\bar{v}}G_{1}(u,v)dv\right)F^{21}(u,\bar{v}).

Integration in the region
\left\{0\leq u+\bar{v}\leq 2T,\ \bar{v}-u\geq 0\right\}
and using Lemma3.1 for the boundary estimate, we get the desired conclusions.

\displaystyle\lesssim|\Psi|^{2}(|\Psi_{u}||\Phi_{v}|+|\Phi_{u}||\Psi_{v}|)r+|\Psi_{t}|(|\Psi||\Phi_{u}||\Phi_{v}|)r

\displaystyle=g_{1}+g_{2}.

(4.10)

We first estimate
g_{1}
. Let
\frac{1}{10}\leq\beta\leq\frac{1}{4}
, we have

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{T}\int_{0}^{\infty}|g_{1}|drdt\lesssim\left[\int_{0}^{T}\int_{0}^{\infty}\left(|\Psi_{u}|^{2}|\Phi_{v}|^{2}+|\Phi_{u}|^{2}|\Psi_{v}|^{2}\right)r^{2-\beta}drdt\right]^{\frac{1}{2}}\left[\int_{0}^{T}\int_{0}^{\infty}|\Psi|^{4}r^{\beta}drdt\right]^{\frac{1}{2}}.

(4.11)

By Sobolev-Hardy inequality,

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\infty}|\Psi|^{4}r^{\beta}dr\lesssim\left(\int_{0}^{\infty}|\Psi|^{2}r^{-\beta}dr\right)\left(\int_{0}^{\infty}|\Psi|^{2}rdr\right)^{\beta}\left(\int_{0}^{\infty}|\Psi_{r}|^{2}rdr\right)^{1-\beta},

(4.12)

thus

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{T}\int_{0}^{\infty}|g_{1}|drdt

\displaystyle\lesssim\left[\int_{0}^{T}\int_{0}^{\infty}\left(|\Psi_{u}|^{2}|\Phi_{v}|^{2}+|\Phi_{u}|^{2}|\Psi_{v}|^{2}\right)r^{2-\beta}drdt\right]^{\frac{1}{2}}\left[\int_{0}^{T}\int_{0}^{\infty}|\Psi|^{2}r^{-\beta}drdt\right]^{\frac{1}{2}}

Take
\alpha=\beta
in (
2
) and integrate for
0\leq r\leq+\infty,\ 0\leq t\leq T
. Noting (2.19), we get

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{T}\int_{0}^{\infty}r^{-\beta}\left(|\Psi|^{2}+|\hat{\Psi}|^{2}\right)drdt=\left(\int_{0}^{\infty}r^{1-\beta}\Psi\hat{\Psi}\right)|_{0}^{T}\left(q^{2}_{0}+q^{2}_{1}\right)drdt

\displaystyle-\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{\infty}r^{-\beta+\sigma}Q_{0}q_{0}dr|_{0}^{T}

\displaystyle+\left[\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{\infty}q_{0}r^{\sigma-\alpha}\int_{0}^{r}\xi^{\alpha}A_{0}q_{1}d\xi-\sigma q_{0}r^{\sigma-\alpha}\int_{0}^{r}\xi^{-\sigma-1}q_{0}(\xi)d\xi+\frac{1}{2}r^{\sigma-\alpha}A_{0}q_{0}Q_{0}drdt\right]

By Hardy’s inequality and noting (2.10), the third term in the right hand of side of the above equality can be absorbed by the left hand side, and by Sobolev-hardy inequality the first and second term is bounded by

\displaystyle\epsilon_{1}^{1-\beta/2}E^{\beta/2}_{1},

thus

\displaystyle\left(\int_{0}^{T}\int_{0}^{\infty}r^{-\beta}\left(|\Psi|^{2}+|\hat{\Psi}|^{2}\right)drdt\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}\lesssim\epsilon_{1}^{\frac{1}{2}-\frac{\beta}{4}}E_{1}^{\frac{\beta}{4}}.

(4.14)

Rewrite (4.3) as

\displaystyle\partial_{u}\left[r(\partial_{v}\Psi)^{2}\right]+\partial_{v}\left[r(\partial_{u}\Psi)^{2}\right]=2G_{1}.

(4.15)

Take
\alpha=\beta
in (
2
) and rewrite it as

\displaystyle-\partial_{v}\left(r^{1-\beta}q_{0}q_{1}\right)+\partial_{v}\left(\frac{1}{2}r^{1-\beta}(q_{0}^{2}+q_{1}^{2})\right)-\frac{1}{2}\partial_{v}(r^{-\beta+\sigma}Q_{0}q_{0})

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{2}\partial_{v}(q_{1}r^{-\beta+\sigma}Q_{0})+\frac{\beta-\sigma+1}{4}\partial_{v}(r^{2\sigma-1-\beta}Q_{0}^{2})

\displaystyle-\partial_{u}\left(r^{1-\beta}q_{0}q_{1}\right)-\partial_{u}\left(\frac{1}{2}r^{1-\beta}(q_{0}^{2}+q_{1}^{2})\right)-\frac{1}{2}\partial_{u}(r^{-\beta+\sigma}Q_{0}q_{0})

\displaystyle-\frac{1}{2}\partial_{u}(q_{1}r^{-\beta+\sigma}Q_{0})-\frac{\beta-\sigma+1}{4}\partial_{u}(r^{2\sigma-1-\beta}Q_{0}^{2})

\displaystyle=\frac{q_{0}}{2r^{\beta-\sigma}}\int_{0}^{r}\xi^{-\sigma}A_{0}q_{1}d\xi-\frac{\sigma q_{0}}{2r^{\beta-\sigma}}\int_{0}^{r}\xi^{-\sigma-1}q_{0}(\xi)d\xi+\frac{1}{2}r^{-\beta+\sigma}A_{0}q_{0}Q_{0}

\displaystyle-\frac{(1+\beta-2\sigma)(\beta-\sigma+1)}{4}r^{2\sigma-2-\alpha}Q_{0}^{2}-\frac{\beta}{2}\frac{q_{0}^{2}+q_{1}^{2}}{r^{\alpha}}

.

(4.16)

\displaystyle\partial_{v}\left(r^{1-\beta}\frac{1}{2}\left(q_{0}-q_{1}\right)^{2}+\frac{1}{2}r^{-\beta+\sigma}Q_{0}\left(q_{1}-q_{0}\right)+\frac{\beta-\sigma+1}{4}r^{2\sigma-1-\beta}Q_{0}^{2}\right)

\displaystyle-\partial_{u}\left(r^{1-\beta}\frac{1}{2}\left(q_{0}+q_{1}\right)^{2}+\frac{1}{2}r^{-\beta+\sigma}Q_{0}\left(q_{1}+q_{0}\right)+\frac{\beta-\sigma+1}{4}r^{2\sigma-1-\beta}Q_{0}^{2}\right)

\displaystyle=G_{\beta},

(4.17)

where

\displaystyle G_{\beta}:=

\displaystyle\frac{q_{0}}{2r^{\beta-\sigma}}\int_{0}^{r}\xi^{-\sigma}A_{0}q_{1}d\xi-\frac{\sigma q_{0}}{2r^{\beta-\sigma}}\int_{0}^{r}\xi^{-\sigma-1}q_{0}(\xi)d\xi+\frac{1}{2}r^{-\beta+\sigma}A_{0}q_{0}Q_{0}

\displaystyle-\frac{(1+\beta-2\sigma)(\beta-\sigma+1)}{4}r^{2\sigma-2-\alpha}Q_{0}^{2}-\frac{\beta}{2}\frac{q_{0}^{2}+q_{1}^{2}}{r^{\alpha}}.

(4.18)

We integrate above balace law over space time, and take the
\sigma
sufficiently small. By hardy inequality, we are arrive at the following estimates

\displaystyle\frac{(1+\beta-2\sigma)(\beta-\sigma+1)}{4}r^{2\sigma-2-\alpha}Q_{0}^{2}+\frac{\beta}{2}\frac{q_{0}^{2}+q_{1}^{2}}{r^{\alpha}}

\displaystyle\leq 2\left(\frac{(1+\beta-2\sigma)(\beta-\sigma+1)}{4}r^{2\sigma-2-\alpha}Q_{0}^{2}+\frac{\beta}{2}\frac{q_{0}^{2}+q_{1}^{2}}{r^{\alpha}}+\frac{\sigma q_{0}}{2r^{\beta-\sigma}}\int_{0}^{r}\xi^{-\sigma-1}q_{0}(\xi)d\xi\right)

\displaystyle\leq 2\left(\frac{\sigma q_{0}}{2r^{\beta-\sigma}}\int_{0}^{r}\xi^{-\sigma}A_{0}q_{0}(\xi)d\xi+\frac{1}{2}r^{-\beta+\sigma}A_{0}q_{0}Q_{0}\right)+C\epsilon^{1-\frac{\beta}{2}}_{1}E^{\frac{\beta}{2}}_{1}

Noting that (2.10) and energy small, the first two terms on the right can be absorbed by the left hand side, therefore

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{T}\int_{0}^{\infty}|G_{\beta}|(t,r)drdt\lesssim\epsilon_{1}^{1-\frac{\beta}{2}}E_{1}^{\frac{\beta}{2}}.

(4.20)

Noting that

\displaystyle r^{1-\beta}\lvert\Phi_{u}\rvert^{2}=\frac{1}{4}r^{1-\beta}\left(q_{0}-q_{1}\right)^{2}

\displaystyle\lesssim r^{1-\beta}\frac{1}{2}\left(q_{0}-q_{1}\right)^{2}+\frac{1}{2}r^{-\beta+\sigma}Q_{0}\left(q_{1}-q_{0}\right)+\frac{\beta-\sigma+1}{4}r^{2\sigma-1-\beta}Q_{0}^{2}

(4.21)

and

\displaystyle r^{1-\beta}\lvert\Phi_{v}\rvert^{2}=\frac{1}{4}r^{1-\beta}\left(q_{0}-q_{1}\right)^{2}

\displaystyle\lesssim r^{1-\beta}\frac{1}{2}\left(q_{0}+q_{1}\right)^{2}+\frac{1}{2}r^{-\beta+\sigma}Q_{0}\left(q_{1}+q_{0}\right)+\frac{\beta-\sigma+1}{4}r^{2\sigma-1-\beta}Q_{0}^{2}

(4.22)

We apply Lemma 3.2 to get

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{T}\int_{0}^{\infty}r^{2-\beta}\left[(\partial_{u}\Phi)^{2}(\partial_{v}\Psi)^{2}+(\partial_{u}\Psi)^{2}(\partial_{v}\Phi)^{2}\right]drdt\lesssim\epsilon_{1}^{1-\frac{\beta}{2}}E_{1}^{1+\frac{\beta}{2}}.

(4.23)

So we get

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{T}\int_{0}^{\infty}|g_{1}|drdt\lesssim\epsilon_{1}E_{1}.

(4.24)

Now we estimate
g_{2}
.

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{T}\int_{0}^{\infty}|g_{2}|drdt\lesssim\int_{0}^{T}\left(\int_{0}^{\infty}|\Psi_{t}|^{2}rdr\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}\left(\int_{0}^{\infty}|\Psi|^{2}|\Phi_{u}|^{2}|\Phi_{v}|^{2}rdr\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}dt

\displaystyle\lesssim E_{1}^{\frac{1}{2}}\int_{0}^{T}\left(\int_{0}^{\infty}|\Psi|^{2}|\Phi_{u}|^{2}|\Phi_{v}|^{2}rdr\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}dt.

(4.25)

We have

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\infty}|\Psi|^{2}|\Phi_{u}|^{2}|\Phi_{v}|^{2}rdr=\int_{0}^{\infty}|\Phi_{u}|^{2}|\Phi_{v}|^{2}d\int_{0}^{r}|\Psi|^{2}\lambda d\lambda

\displaystyle=-2\int_{0}^{\infty}\int_{0}^{r}|\Psi|^{2}\lambda d\lambda\left[(\Phi_{u}\cdot\hat{\Psi}_{u})|\Phi_{v}|^{2}+|\Phi_{u}|^{2}(\Phi_{v}\cdot\hat{\Psi}_{v})\right]

\displaystyle\leq 2\int_{0}^{\infty}dr\int_{0}^{r}|\Psi|^{2}\lambda^{-\beta}d\lambda\left[r^{1+\beta}|\Phi_{u}||\Phi_{v}|(|\hat{\Psi}_{u}||\Phi_{v}|+|\hat{\Psi}_{v}||\Phi_{u}|)\right]

\displaystyle\left(\int_{0}^{\infty}|\Phi_{u}|^{2}|\Phi_{v}|^{2}r^{3\beta}dr\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}.

Therefore we get

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{T}\int_{0}^{\infty}|g_{2}|drdt\lesssim E_{1}^{\frac{1}{2}}\left(\int_{0}^{T}\int_{0}^{\infty}|\Psi|^{2}r^{-\beta}drdt\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}
