Bounds for moments of ℓ \ell -torsion in class groups
Peter Koymans, Jesse Thorner

Abstract
. Fix a number field k k , integers ℓ , n ≥ 2 \ell,n\geq 2 , and a prime p p . For all r ≥ 1 r\geq 1 , we prove strong unconditional upper bounds on the r r -th moment of ℓ \ell -torsion in the ideal class groups of degree p p extensions of k k and of degree n n S n S_{n} -extensions of k k , improving upon results of Ellenberg, Pierce and Wood as well as GRH-conditional results of Frei and Widmer. For large r r , our results are comparable with work of Heath-Brown and Pierce for imaginary quadratic extensions of ℚ \mathbb{Q} . When r = 1 r=1 , our results are new even for the family of all quadratic extensions of ℚ \mathbb{Q} , leading to an improved upper bound for the count of degree p p D p D_{p} -extensions over ℚ \mathbb{Q} (where D p D_{p} is the dihedral group of order 2 ​ p 2p ).

Consider the subspace of

given by

(3.3)

\displaystyle V_{0}=\Big\{(x_{1},\dots,x_{r+s})\in\mathbb{R}^{r+s}\colon\sum_{i=1}^{r+s}d_{i}x_{i}=0\Big\}.

By Dirichlet’s unit theorem,

, there exists a Minkowski basis

\mathcal{F}=\Big\{\sum_{i=1}^{r+s-1}x_{i}\mathbf{u}_{i}:0\leq x_{i}<1\Big\}

be a fundamental domain of

. For all
\alpha\in K

(unique up to multiplication by a root of unity),
\mathbf{v}_{\alpha}\in\mathcal{F}
, and

such that

.

We claim that

\displaystyle\|\mathbf{u}_{i}\|\gg 1.

Indeed, if

and

\|\mathbf{u}_{i}\|=\Big(\sum_{j=1}^{r+s}(d_{j}\log|u_{i}|_{v_{j}})^{2}\Big)^{1/2}\geq\frac{\sum_{j=1}^{r+s}d_{j}\max(0,\log|u_{i}|_{v_{j}})}{\sqrt{r+s}}\geq\frac{\sum_{j=1}^{r+s}d_{j}\max(0,\log|u_{i}|_{v_{j}})}{\sqrt{d[k:\mathbb{Q}]}}.

Since

, the right hand side of the above inequality equals

3.4

We will apply Minkowski’s second theorem to

. Let

be the isomorphism that drops the last coordinate, and let
\iota
be the compositional inverse of
\pi
. We endow

with the pushforward measure, via

, of the Lebesgue measure on
\mathbb{R}^{r+s-1}
. Since

yields

\prod_{i=1}^{r+s-1}\|\mathbf{u}_{i}\|\ll\mathrm{Vol}(\mathcal{F}).

Together, (

by definition, we obtain

. Since

by
[

. Thus, there exists an effectively computable constant

partitions into

.

The Cauchy–Schwarz inequality yields

\pi_{K}^{(1)}(Z)=\sum_{(a,i)}|f^{-1}(a,i)|\leq\Big(\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}(a,i)\colon f^{-1}(a,i)\neq\varnothing\end{subarray}}1\Big)^{1/2}\Big(\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}(a,i)\colon f^{-1}(a,i)\neq\varnothing\end{subarray}}|f^{-1}(a,i)|^{2}\Big)^{1/2},

It remains to produce constant
