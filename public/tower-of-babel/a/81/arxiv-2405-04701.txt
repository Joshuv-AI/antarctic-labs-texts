Introduction: the geography, enumerative geometry, and

In this paper, a
Calabi-Yau threefold (CY3)
is a smooth,
projective threefold

{\mathbb{C}}

and

1.1.

The Hodge numbers of a CY3

CY3s with small Hodge numbers. Candelas, Constantin, and Mishra

There exist CY3s

1.2.3.
GV theory.

The genus

The curve classes of a smooth fiber of

5.1.
Review of the partition function of Banana manifolds

The
