Strong Edge-Coloring of Cubic Bipartite Graphs: A Counterexample
Daniel W. Cranston

Abstract
A strong edge-coloring φ \varphi of a graph G G assigns colors to edges of G G such that φ ⁡ ( e 1 ) ≠ φ ⁡ ( e 2 ) \varphi(e_{1})\neq\varphi(e_{2}) whenever e 1 e_{1} and e 2 e_{2} are at distance no more than 1. It is equivalent to a proper vertex coloring of the square of the line graph of G G . In 1990 Faudree, Schelp, Gyárfás, and Tuza conjectured that if G G is a bipartite graph with maximum degree 3 and sufficiently large girth, then G G has a strong edge-coloring with at most 5 colors. In 2021 this conjecture was disproved by Lužar, Mačajová, Škoviera, and Soták. Here we give an alternative construction to disprove the conjecture.

Main Result

Our Main Theorem is motivated by the special case of
k
-regular graphs where
k=3
, which is all that is
needed to disprove Conjecture

(6). However, with only a bit more work we prove the

Main Theorem
.

For every positive integer
g

, there exists a
k
-regular bipartite graph

such that

has girth at least
g

\chi^{\prime}_{s}(G)\geq 2k

We first prove the Main Theorem assuming two lemmas. We prove the lemmas below.

Proof.

Fix positive integers
g

k\geq 2
. By Lemma
2

n
is sufficiently large
then there exists a bipartite
k
-regular graph on
2n

girth at least

Since

is

|E(G)|=\frac{k}{2}|V(G)|=kn

(2k-1)\nmid|E(G)|
. Thus, Lemma

\chi^{\prime}_{s}(G)\geq 2k

∎

We consider an arbitrary edge

k
-regular graph and the
2k-2

edges that share one endpoint with

corresponding vertices form a clique. So each color in a strong edge-coloring of

this argument for every edge

a strong edge-coloring is used on at most
1/(2k-1)
of all edges. We
formalize this idea below.

Lemma 1

If

-regular and simple, for some

\varphi

(2k-1)\nmid\left|E(G)\right|

\chi^{\prime}_{s}(G)\geq 2k

Proof.

Fix a simple
k
-regular graph

\varphi

set of edges receiving the same color under

†
