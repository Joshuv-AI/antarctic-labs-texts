The Turán number of Berge- K 4 K_{4} in triple systems
András Gyárfás

Abstract
A Berge- K 4 K_{4} in a triple system is a configuration with four vertices v 1 , v 2 , v 3 , v 4 v_{1},v_{2},v_{3},v_{4} and six distinct triples { e i ​ j : 1 ≤ i < j ≤ 4 } \{e_{ij}:1\leq i<j\leq 4\} such that { v i , v j } ⊂ e i ​ j \{v_{i},v_{j}\}\subset e_{ij} for every 1 ≤ i < j ≤ 4 1\leq i<j\leq 4 . We denote by ℬ \mathcal{B} the set of Berge- K 4 K_{4} configurations. A triple system is ℬ \mathcal{B} -free if it does not contain any member of ℬ \mathcal{B} . We prove that the maximum number of triples in a ℬ \mathcal{B} -free triple system on n ≥ 6 n\geq 6 points is obtained by the balanced complete 3 3 -partite triple system: all triples { a b c : a ∈ A , b ∈ B , c ∈ C } \{abc:a\in A,b\in B,c\in C\} where A , B , C A,B,C is a partition of n n points with ⌊ n 3 ⌋ = | A | ≤ | B | ≤ | C | = ⌈ n 3 ⌉ . \left\lfloor{n\over 3}\right\rfloor=|A|\leq|B|\leq|C|=\left\lceil{n\over 3}\right\rceil.
