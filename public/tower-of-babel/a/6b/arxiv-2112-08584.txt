New puzzle in charmonium decays
Hongpeng Wang, Chang-Zheng Yuan

Abstract
By analyzing existing data on pseudoscalar charmonium decays, we obtain the ratio of the branching fractions of η c ​ ( 2 ​ S ) \eta_{c}(2S) and η c \eta_{c} decays into ten different final states with light hadrons. For the first time, we test the two existing theoretical predictions of these decays, and find that the experimental data are significantly different from both of them. The lack of observation of any decay mode with higher rate in η c ​ ( 2 ​ S ) \eta_{c}(2S) than in η c \eta_{c} decays suggests very unusual decay dynamics in pseudoscalar charmonium decays to be identified. We also report the first model-independent evaluation of the partial width of η c ​ ( 2 ​ S ) → γ ​ γ \eta_{c}(2S)\to\gamma\gamma ( 2.21 − 0.64 + 0.88 2.21_{-0.64}^{+0.88} keV) and improved determination of that of η c → γ ​ γ \eta_{c}\to\gamma\gamma ( 5.43 − 0.38 + 0.41 5.43_{-0.38}^{+0.41} keV). The latter shows a tension with the most recent lattice QCD calculation.



Charmonium states are bound state of a charmed quark (
c
) and a
charmed antiquark (
\bar{c}
). Since the discovery of the first
charmonium state, the
J/\psi
, at BNL
ting
and at
SLAC
richter
in 1974, all the charmonium states below the
open-charm threshold and a few charmonium states above the
open-charm threshold have been established; the measured spectrum
of the states agrees well with theoretical calculations based on
QCD
Brambilla:2004jw
;
review4
;
Brambilla:2014jmp
and
QCD-inspired potential models
eichten
;
godfrey
;
barnes
. On
the contrary, the decays of the charmonium states into light
hadrons which must proceed via the annihilation of the charmed
quark-antiquark pair, are still poorly known, although they are
governed by the same QCD theory.

The first calculation by Appelquist and
Politzer
Appelquist:1974zd
using perturbative QCD related
the hadronic decays of
J/\psi
and its radial excited sibling

\psi(2S)
to their leptonic decays, and predicted

Q^{V}=\frac{{\cal B}(\psi(2S)\to hadrons)}{{\cal B}(J/\psi\to hadrons)}=\frac{{\cal B}(\psi(2S)\to e^{+}e^{-})}{{\cal B}(J/\psi\to e^{+}e^{-})}.

The ratio was found to be around 12% using the branching
fractions of the leptonic decays at that time, and this was called
“12% rule” since then, although the most recent ratio is

(13.3\pm 0.3)\%

PDG
. Extending
Q^{V}
of inclusive decays
of charmonium to light hadrons to each individual hadronic final
state,
h
, Mark II experiment tested
Q^{V}_{h}
with eight final
states
Franklin:1983ve
and found two modes were severely
suppressed relative to 12% while the other six modes agree with
12% reasonably well, and the
\rho\pi
mode was suppressed by
more than an order of magnitude so this was referred to as
“
\rho\pi
puzzle”. Many theoretical explanations have been put
forth to decipher this puzzle
Mo:2006cy
, some attribute the
small
Q^{V}_{\rho\pi}
to the enhanced branching fraction of

J/\psi
decays, some to the suppressed branching fraction of

\psi(2S)
decays, and some other to some dynamics which may affect
both
\psi(2S)
and
J/\psi
decays but in a different way. Improved
measurements from BES, CLEOc, and lately BESIII experiments
confirmed the Mark II observations and tested various theoretical
models
PDG
, it is found that none of these models can solve the
“
\rho\pi
puzzle” and all the newly available data
satisfactorily
Mo:2006cy
.

As the spin-partners of
J/\psi
and
\psi(2S)
respectively, the
spin-singlets
\eta_{c}
and
\eta_{c}(2S)
may decay into light hadrons in
a similar way as their spin-triplets partners. Anselmino,
Genovese, and Predazzi assumed
Anselmino:1991es

\frac{{\cal B}(\eta_{c}(2S)\to hadrons)}{{\cal B}(\eta_{c}\to hadrons)}\approx\frac{{\cal B}(\psi(2S)\to hadrons)}{{\cal B}(J/\psi\to hadrons)}=Q^{V},

while Chao, Gu, and Tuan argued that
Chao:1996sf

Q^{P}=\frac{{\cal B}(\eta_{c}(2S)\to hadrons)}{{\cal B}(\eta_{c}\to hadrons)}\approx 1.

These two predictions deviate by a factor of seven and
should be tested with experimental data.

The theoretical work was clearly ahead of time since the
\eta_{c}(2S)

was first observed in 2002
Belle:2002bnx
and until now only
three hadronic decays of it were listed with branching fractions
and the uncertainties are more than 50%
PDG
.

By examining the experimental data available for
\eta_{c}(2S)
decays
(cited by the PDG
PDG
and those listed in the
Appendix
Data used in the analysis
(Tables
,
,
,
)
), we found an amazing fact that in most of the
cases both
\eta_{c}
and
\eta_{c}(2S)
were measured in an experiment at
the same time, so this allows a very convenient way of determining
the ratio of the branching fractions and to test the theoretical
predictions. We scrutinize the experimental measurements and
select only the reliable results and do a global fit to extract
properties related to the
\eta_{c}
and
\eta_{c}(2S)
states.

There are mainly three categories of measurements related to

\eta_{c}
and
\eta_{c}(2S)
states: two-photon processes (
\gamma\gamma\to\eta_{c}~(\eta_{c}(2S))

B

B\to K\eta_{c}~(\eta_{c}(2S))

charmonium decays (
\psi(2S)\to\gamma\eta_{c}~(\eta_{c}(2S))

experimental measurements are the ratio or the product of the
branching fractions or partial widths. With the help of
measurements of a few absolute branching fractions and the total
widths of
\eta_{c}

\Gamma_{\eta_{c}}

\eta_{c}(2S)

\Gamma_{\eta_{c}(2S)}

\eta_{c}

\eta_{c}(2S)

\gamma\gamma
(
\Gamma_{\eta_{c}\to\gamma\gamma}
and

\Gamma_{\eta_{c}(2S)\to\gamma\gamma}

The
\eta_{c}
-related measurements before 1995 were obtained by
using
\eta_{c}
mass and width that are significantly smaller than
the recent results

since the results are biased and the precision is low. The

\eta_{c}

\eta_{c}

In summary, we determined the ratios of the pseudoscalar
charmonium states
\eta_{c}(2S)
and
\eta_{c}
decay branching fractions
and found prominent discrepancy from theoretical
predictions
Anselmino:1991es
;
Chao:1996sf
. The mixing of the

J/\psi
with a nearby glueball has been proposed
Hou:1982kh

to explain the “
\rho\pi
puzzle” and the scheme has been
extended to the
\eta_{c}
case
Anselmino:1991es
;
Chao:1996sf
.
As the pseudoscalar glueballs are expected to be close to
\eta_{c}

or
\eta_{c}(2S)

Huber:2021zqk
;
Chen:2021dvn
, the mixing between
them may also play important role in the charmonium
decays
Tsai:2011dp
;
Qin:2017qes
;
Zhang:2021xvl
. The different
contribution of the open-charm loop in
\eta_{c}
and
\eta_{c}(2S)
decays
may affect the branching fraction ratio
Wang:2012wj
too.
The fact that all the known hadronic decays of
\eta_{c}(2S)
have rates
lower than
\eta_{c}
decays suggests abnormal dynamics in either

\eta_{c}(2S)
or
\eta_{c}
decays, and these may be investigated at
future experiments like BESIII
bes3_white
, Belle
II
belle2
, and LHCb
lhcb_detector
in charmonium
decays, two-photon processes, and
B
decays.
