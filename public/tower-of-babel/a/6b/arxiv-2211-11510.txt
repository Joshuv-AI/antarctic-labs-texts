Complete study of four top quark production at the LHC in the SMEFT
Hesham El Faham

Abstract
We present a study of four top quark production at the LHC in the Standard Model Effective Field Theory (SMEFT). The analysis is performed at the tree-level, including all terms from the mixed QCD-EW-coupling cross-section expansion and all the relevant dimension-six SMEFT operators. We find several cases in which formally subleading terms give rise to significant contributions. Moreover, we obtain limits on the SMEFT Wilson coefficients (WCs), including different collider setups, FCC-hh and HL-LHC, using a simplified chi-square fit. Through the latter, we also assessed the importance of including differential information in the fit.

5
Summary and conclusions

We emphasise the importance of our results in summarising the four top quark production SMEFT predictions and analysing them in each order of the QCD-EW expansion.
Our observations underline the significance of subleading terms for the 4-heavy quark operators.
Moreover, within all the dimension-six SMEFT operators, we have shown that four top quark production provides strong constraints on the 4-heavy coefficients. We also showed that including differential information further improves the bounds on the SMEFT coefficients.
