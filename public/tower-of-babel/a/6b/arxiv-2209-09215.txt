Gravity Localization on Intersecting Thick Braneworlds
Henrique Matheus Gauy, Alex E. Bernardini

Abstract
The localization of gravity for ( 5 + 1 ) (5+1) -dimensional intersecting thick braneworld models is thoroughly investigated. Departing from preliminary results for five independent proposals, from I I to V V , gravity is shown to be localized in the brane. In particular, for models from I I to I ​ I ​ I III , only the zero modes are analytically determined. On the other hand, for models I ​ V IV and V V , massive modes are all obtained: a finite number of massive states is identified for model I ​ V IV , while an infinite but discrete number of massive states bounded from below is exhibited by model V V . Considering that the discreteness of the graviton modes implies that gravity does not propagate in the co-dimensions, the naked singularities of models I ​ V IV and V V , at edges of space, are made harmless. To conclude, the phenomenological implications for sphere models, constructed out of models I ​ I ​ I III and I ​ V IV , are also discussed. According to such an overall classification, if the internal space is a sphere, model I ​ V IV is shown to exhibit normalizable modes. More relevantly, for the sphere model assembled out of model I ​ I ​ I III , the subtle property of reproducing a consistent Newtonian limit is identified.

(\mathbb{B}^{2},\bm{\hat{\sigma}})

Despite the reiterated physical appealing limitations of models

More problematically, model

The most physically appealing intersecting braneworld, model
