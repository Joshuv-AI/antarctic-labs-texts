TRIPLES AND QUADRUPLES OF CONSECUTIVE SQUARES OR NON-SQUARES IN A FINITE FIELD
STEPHEN D. COHEN

Abstract
Let 𝔽 q \mathbb{F}_{q} be the finite field of odd prime power order q q , We find explicit expressions for the number of triples { α − 1 , α , α + 1 } \{\alpha-1,\alpha,\alpha+1\} of consecutive non-zero squares in 𝔽 q \mathbb{F}_{q} and similarly for the number of triples of consecutive non-square elements. A key ingredient is the evaluation of Jacobsthal sums over general finite fields by Katre and Rajwade. This extends results of Monzingo(1985) to non-prime fields. Curiously, the same machinery alows the evaluation of the number of consecutive quadruples { α − 1 , α , α + 1 , α + 2 } \{\alpha-1,\alpha,\alpha+1,\alpha+2\} of square and non-squares over 𝔽 q \mathbb{F}_{q} , when q q is a power of 5.

finite fields, squares and non-squares Jacobsthal sums
