Search for a D ​ D ¯ D\bar{D} bound state in the Λ b → Λ ​ D ​ D ¯ \Lambda_{b}\rightarrow\Lambda D\bar{D} process
Le-Le Wei, Hong-Shen Li, En Wang, Ju-Jun Xie, De-Min Li, Yu-Xiao Li

Abstract
We have investigated the process of Λ b → Λ ​ D ​ D ¯ \Lambda_{b}\to\Lambda D\bar{D} , by taking into account the contributions from the s s -wave D ​ D ¯ D\bar{D} interaction within the coupled-channel unitary approach, and the intermediate ψ ⁡ ( 3770 ) \psi(3770) resonance. In addition to the peak of the ψ ⁡ ( 3770 ) \psi(3770) , an enhancement near the D ​ D ¯ D\bar{D} mass threshold is found in the D ​ D ¯ D\bar{D} invariant mass distributions, which should be the reflection of the D ​ D ¯ D\bar{D} bound state. We would like to encourage our experimental colleagues to measure the D ​ D ¯ D\bar{D} invariant mass distribution of the Λ b → Λ ​ D ​ D ¯ \Lambda_{b}\to\Lambda D\bar{D} process, which is crucial to search for the D ​ D ¯ D\bar{D} bound state and to understand the heavy-hadron heavy-hadron interactions.

Introduction

Although the quark model was proposed by Gell-Mann and Zweig more than half century ago
GellMann:1964nj
;
Zweig:1964jf
, it is still valid in classifying all known hadrons by now. Since the
X(3872)
was observed by the Belle Collaboration in 2003

, many charmonium-like states were reported experimentally

, and most of them cannot be explained as the conventional mesons (
q\bar{q}
) or baryons (
qqq
)
Olsen:2017bmm
;
Brambilla:2019esw
.
There are many explanations about those states, such as tetraquark states, molecular states, the conventional
c\bar{c}
mesons, or the mixing between different components
Chen:2016qju
;
Liu:2019zoy
;
Hosaka:2016pey
;
Guo:2017jvc
;
Hao:2019fjg
. However, it is surprising that many resonant structures are observed around thresholds of a pair of heavy hadrons, such as
X(3872)
and
Z_{c}(3900)^{\pm}
around the
D\bar{D}^{*}
threshold,
Z_{cs}(3985)
around the
\bar{D}_{s}D^{*}
and
\bar{D}^{*}_{s}D
thresholds, and

around
D_{s}\bar{D}_{s}
threshold. As discussed in Ref.
Dong:2020hxe
, such structures should appear at any threshold of a pair of heavy-quark and heavy-antiquark hadrons which have attractive interaction at threshold. Thus, the experimental information about the threshold structures is crucial to deeply understand the heavy-hadron heavy-hadron interactions, and the internal structures of the hidden-charm states
Wang:2018djr
;
Wang:2017mrt
.

In Ref.
Gamermann:2006nm

,
D^{0}\bar{D}^{0}

On the other hand, the decays of

, such as the processes of

\displaystyle V_{p}c\bar{c}\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}s(ud-du)

IV
Conclusions

The study of the charmonium-like states is crucial to understand the heavy-hadron heavy-hadron interactions, and also the internal structures of the hidden-charm states. One
D\bar{D}
bound state around 3700 MeV was predicted within the coupled channel unitary approach
Gamermann:2006nm
, and also the lattice investigation of the
D\bar{D}
and
D_{s}\bar{D}_{s}
scattering
Wang:2020elp
. Although our previous studies on the
e^{+}e^{-}\to J/\psi D\bar{D}
and
\gamma\gamma\to D\bar{D}
data support the existence of the
D\bar{D}
bound state, the other possibilities cannot be discarded due to the present quality of the experimental data
Wang:2019evy
;
Wang:2020elp
. Investigating the processes involving the
s
-wave
D\bar{D}
system could provide the information about the existence of the
D\bar{D}
bound state.

In this paper, we have investigated the processes
\Lambda_{b}\rightarrow\Lambda D^{0}\bar{D}^{0}
and
\Lambda_{b}\rightarrow\Lambda D^{+}D^{-}
within the coupled channel unitary approach, by taking into account the
s
-wave meson-meson interactions and the contribution from the intermediate resonance
\psi(3770)
. The
D^{0}\bar{D}^{0}
and
D^{+}D^{-}
invariant mass distributions in the
\Lambda_{b}\to\Lambda D\bar{D}
reaction are investigated, and our results show an enhancement structure near the
D\bar{D}
threshold, which should be the reflection of the
D\bar{D}
bound state. Therefore, we strongly encourage our experimental colleagues to measure the
\Lambda_{b}\rightarrow\Lambda D\bar{D}
process, which would be crucial to confirm the existence the
X(3700)
resonance, and to understand the heavy-hadron heavy-hadron interactions.
