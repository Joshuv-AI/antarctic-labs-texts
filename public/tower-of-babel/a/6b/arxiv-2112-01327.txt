A modified limited memory Nesterov’s accelerated quasi-Newton
S. Indrapriyadarsini 2 , Shahrzad Mahboubi 3 , Hiroshi Ninomiya 3 , Takeshi Kamio 4 and Hideki Asai 2

Abstract




I
Introduction

The Nesterov’s accelerated quasi-Newton (L)NAQ
[
1
]
method has shown to accelerate the conventional (L)BFGS quasi-Newton method using the Nesterov’s accelerated gradient in several neural network (NN) applications. However, the calculation of two gradients per iteration increases the computational cost. An approximation to the Nesterov’s accelerated gradient was proposed in
[
2
]
. This paper extends the study in
[
2
]
by applying the approximation to limited memory NAQ.

II
Proposed Algorithm (L-MoQ)

The weight update in NAQ is given as
{\bf w}_{k+1}={\bf w}_{k}+\mu_{k}{\bf v}_{k}+\alpha_{k}{\bf g}_{k},
where
{\bf g}_{k}
is the search direction as in (1).

{\bf g}_{k}={\bf H}_{k}{\bf\nabla E(w}_{k}+\mu_{k}{\bf v}_{k}).\vskip-2.84526pt

(1)

MoQ [2] showed that (1) can be approximated as

{\boldsymbol{\bf g}}_{k}={\bf H}_{k}[(1+\mu_{k})\nabla{\bf E(w}_{k})-\mu_{k}\nabla{\bf E(w}_{k-1})].\vskip-4.2679pt

(2)

The
\mu_{k}
value is updated using (3) where
\theta_{k+1}
is obtained by solving (4) with
\theta_{0}=1
and
\gamma=10^{-5}
.

\mu_{k}=\theta_{k}(1-\theta_{k})/({\theta_{k}}^{2}+\theta_{k+1}),\vskip-4.2679pt

(3)

{\theta_{k+1}}^{2}=(1+\theta_{k+1}){\theta_{k}}^{2}+\gamma\theta_{k+1}.\vskip-4.2679pt

(4)

In this paper, we propose limited-memory MoQ (L-MoQ) in which (2) is evaluated by the two-loop recursion using the last
m
curvature pairs
({\bf s},{\bf y})
given by,

~~~~~~~~{\bf s}_{k}={\bf w}_{k+1}-({\bf w}_{k}+\mu_{k}{\bf v}_{k})~~~{\rm and}~~\vskip-4.2679pt

{\bf y}_{k}=\nabla{\bf E(w}_{k+1})-[(1+\mu_{k})\nabla{\bf E(w}_{k})-\mu_{k}\nabla{\bf E(w}_{k-1})]).\vskip-4.2679pt
