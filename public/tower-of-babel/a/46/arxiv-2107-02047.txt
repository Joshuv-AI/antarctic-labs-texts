Next-to-leading-order study of J / ψ J/\psi angular distributions in e + ​ e − → J / ψ + η c , χ c ​ J e^{+}e^{-}\to J/\psi+\eta_{c},\chi_{cJ} at s ≈ 10.6 \sqrt{s}\approx 10.6 GeV

Abstract
In this paper, we present a detailed next-to-leading-order (NLO) study of J / ψ J/\psi angular distributions in e + ​ e − → J / ψ + η c , χ c ​ J e^{+}e^{-}\to J/\psi+\eta_{c},\chi_{cJ} ( J = 0 , 1 , 2 J=0,1,2 ) within the nonrelativistic QCD factorization (NRQCD). The numerical NLO expressions for total and differential cross sections, i.e., d ​ σ d ​ cos ⁡ θ = A + B ​ cos 2 ⁡ θ \frac{d\sigma}{d\cos\theta}=A+B\cos^{2}\theta , are both derived. With the inclusion of the newly-calculated QCD corrections to A A and B B , the α θ ( = B / A ) \alpha_{\theta}(=B/A) parameters in J / ψ + χ c ​ 0 J/\psi+\chi_{c0} and J / ψ + χ c ​ 1 J/\psi+\chi_{c1} are moderately enhanced, while the magnitude of α θ J / ψ + χ c ​ 2 {\alpha_{\theta}}_{J/\psi+\chi_{c2}} is significantly reduced; regarding the production of J / ψ + η c J/\psi+\eta_{c} , the α θ \alpha_{\theta} value remains unchanged. By comparing with experiment, we find the predicted α θ J / ψ + η c {\alpha_{\theta}}_{J/\psi+\eta_{c}} is in good agreement with the B ELLE measurement; however, α θ J / ψ + χ c ​ 0 {\alpha_{\theta}}_{J/\psi+\chi_{c0}} is still totally incompatible with the experimental result, and this discrepancy seems to hardly be cured by proper choices of the charm-quark mass, the renormalization scale, and the NRQCD matrix elements.

2
Calculation formalism

Following the NRQCD factorization, the differential cross sections of
e^{+}(p_{1})+e^{-}(p_{2})\to J/\psi(p_{3})+\eta_{c},\chi_{cJ}(p_{4})
can be generally written as

\displaystyle d\sigma=d\hat{\sigma}_{e^{+}e^{-}\to c\bar{c}[n_{1}]+c\bar{c}[n_{2}]}\langle\mathcal{O}^{J/\psi}(n_{1})\rangle\langle\mathcal{O}^{\eta_{c}(\chi_{cJ})}(n_{2})\rangle,

(2)

where
d\hat{\sigma}_{e^{+}e^{-}\to c\bar{c}[n_{1}]+c\bar{c}[n_{2}]}
is the perturbative calculable short distance coefficients, denoting the production of a configuration of
c\bar{c}[n_{1}]
intermediate state associated with
c\bar{c}[n_{2}]
. By neglecting the color-octet contributions, which are discovered to be trivial for the production of
e^{+}+e^{-}\to J/\psi+\eta_{c}(\chi_{cJ})

Braaten:2002fi
,
n_{1}=^{3}S_{1}^{1}
and
n_{2}=^{1}S_{0}^{1}(^{3}P_{J}^{1})
. The universal nonperturbative LDMEs
\langle\mathcal{O}^{J/\psi}(n_{1})\rangle
and
\langle\mathcal{O}^{\eta_{c}(\chi_{cJ})}(n_{2})\rangle
stand for the probabilities of
c\bar{c}[n_{1}]
and
c\bar{c}[n_{2}]
into
J/\psi
and
\eta_{c}(\chi_{cJ})
, respectively.

d\hat{\sigma}_{e^{+}e^{-}\to c\bar{c}[n_{1}]+c\bar{c}[n_{2}]}
can be further expressed as

\displaystyle d\hat{\sigma}_{e^{+}e^{-}\to c\bar{c}[n_{1}]+c\bar{c}[n_{2}]}=|\mathcal{M}|^{2}d\Pi_{2}=L_{\mu\nu}H^{\mu\nu}d\Pi_{2},

(3)

where
L_{\mu\nu}
and
H^{\mu\nu}
are the leptonic and hadronic tensors, respectively, and
d\Pi_{2}
is the standard two-bodies phase space.

2.1
Leptonic current

L_{\mu\nu}
can directly be calculated and obtained as

\displaystyle L_{\mu\nu}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 4\pi\alpha\textrm{Tr}[p\!\!\!/\penalty_{1}\gamma_{\mu}p\!\!\!/\penalty_{2}\gamma_{\nu}]

(4)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 4\pi\alpha s\left(-2g_{\mu\nu}+\frac{4{p_{1}}_{\mu}q_{\nu}+4{p_{1}}_{\nu}q_{\mu}-8{p_{1}}_{\mu}{p_{1}}_{\nu}}{s}\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 4\pi\alpha sl_{\mu\nu},

where
q=p_{1}+p_{2}
and
s=(p_{1}+p_{2})^{2}
.
Note that
, in calculating total cross section, the standard
l_{\mu\nu}
in equation (
4
) can be replaced equivalently with
-\frac{8}{3}g^{\mu\nu}

Gong:2009ng
, which, however, is no longer applicable for the case of differential cross section. Therefore, in order to evaluate
\frac{d\sigma}{d\cos\theta}
, we will employ a new
l_{\mu\nu}
obtained in our recent paper
Zhang:2017dia
; the following is a brief description of its derivation.

By integrating
H^{\mu\nu}
over all the final states other than
J/\psi
, we obtain the hadronic tensor
W_{h}^{\mu\nu}(p_{3},q)
, which is dependent only on
p_{3}
and
q
. Subsequently we decompose
W_{h}^{\mu\nu}(p_{3},q)
as a linear combination of the tensors constituted of
-g^{\mu\nu}
,
p_{3}
, and
q
as

\displaystyle W_{h}^{\mu\nu}(p_{3},q)=W_{1}\left(-g^{\mu\nu}+\frac{q^{\mu}q^{\nu}}{s}\right)+W_{2}\left[{\left(p_{3}-\frac{p_{3}\cdot q}{s}q\right)}^{\mu}{\left(p_{3}-\frac{p_{3}\cdot q}{s}q\right)}^{\nu}\right],

(5)

which satisfies the following relation

\displaystyle q_{\mu}W_{h}^{\mu\nu}=0.

(6)

With some calculations, the contraction of
W_{h}^{\mu\nu}
with the
l_{\mu\nu}
in equation (
4
) says

\displaystyle l_{\mu\nu}W_{h}^{\mu\nu}=4W_{1}+2|\boldsymbol{p}_{3}|^{2}(1-\cos^{2}\theta)W_{2},

(7)

where
\boldsymbol{p}_{3}
is the three momenta of
J/\psi
, and
\theta
is the angle between
\boldsymbol{p}_{3}
and the spatial momentum of
e^{-}
(or
e^{+}
) in the
e^{+}e^{-}
center-of-mass frame.

It is easy to verify that, with

\displaystyle l_{\mu\nu}=\mathcal{A}_{1}\left(-g_{\mu\nu}+\frac{q_{\mu}q_{\nu}}{s}\right)+\mathcal{A}_{2}\left[\frac{{\left(p_{3}-\frac{p_{3}\cdot q}{s}q\right)}_{\mu}{\left(p_{3}-\frac{p_{3}\cdot q}{s}q\right)}_{\nu}}{|{\boldsymbol{p}_{3}}|^{2}}\right],

(8)

where

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}_{1}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 1+\cos^{2}\theta,

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}_{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 1-3\cos^{2}\theta,

(9)

one can reproduce the results in equation (
7
). Further employing the current conservation will finally yield

\displaystyle l_{\mu\nu}=\mathcal{A}_{1}(-g_{\mu\nu})+\mathcal{A}_{2}\left(\frac{{p_{3}}_{\mu}{p_{3}}_{\nu}}{|{\boldsymbol{p}_{3}}|^{2}}\right).

(10)

With the integration over
\cos\theta
, the term involving
A_{2}
in equation (
10
) vanishes, and the first term on the right-hand side will reduce to
-\frac{8}{3}g^{\mu\nu}
. Comparing the two leptonic tensors in equations (
4
) and (
10
), one can easily find the newly-derived
l_{\mu\nu}
is related only to the hadronic-process momentum (
p_{3}
), which, by the absence of
p_{1}
and
p_{2}
, would greatly reduce the complication in computing the differential cross sections, especially at the NLO accuracy.

2.2
Cross sections

Using the leptonic tensor in equation (
10
), the differential cross sections of
e^{+}e^{-}\to J/\psi+\eta_{c},\chi_{cJ}
can be written in the following form

\displaystyle\frac{d\sigma}{d\cos\theta}=A+B\cos^{2}\theta=\kappa\left(\mathcal{C}_{1}\mathcal{A}_{1}+\mathcal{C}_{2}\mathcal{A}_{2}\right),

(11)

and then we have

\displaystyle A

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\kappa(\mathcal{C}_{1}+\mathcal{C}_{2}),

\displaystyle B

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\kappa(\mathcal{C}_{1}-3\mathcal{C}_{2}),

\displaystyle\sigma

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{8}{3}\kappa\mathcal{C}_{1}.

(12)

The universal factor
\kappa
reads

\displaystyle\kappa_{J/\psi+\eta_{c}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{2\pi\alpha^{2}\alpha_{s}^{2}|R_{1S}(0)|^{2}|R_{1S}(0)|^{2}\sqrt{s^{2}-16m_{c}^{2}s}}{81m_{c}^{2}s^{2}},

\displaystyle\kappa_{J/\psi+\chi_{cJ}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{2\pi\alpha^{2}\alpha_{s}^{2}|R_{1S}(0)|^{2}|R^{{}^{\prime}}_{1P}(0)|^{2}\sqrt{s^{2}-16m_{c}^{2}s}}{27m_{c}^{2}s^{2}}.

(13)

|R_{1S}(0)|
and
|R^{{}^{\prime}}_{1P}(0)|
are the wave functions at the origin, which can be related to the NRQCD LDMEs by the following formulae:

\displaystyle\langle\mathcal{O}^{J/\psi}(^{3}S_{1}^{1})\rangle

\displaystyle\simeq

\displaystyle 3\langle\mathcal{O}^{\eta_{c}}(^{1}S_{0}^{1})\rangle=\frac{9}{2\pi}|R_{1S}(0)|^{2},

\displaystyle\langle\mathcal{O}^{\chi_{cJ}}(^{3}P_{J}^{1})\rangle

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(2J+1)\frac{3}{4\pi}|R^{{}^{\prime}}_{1P}(0)|^{2}.

(14)

2.2.2
NLO

Due to the absence of the real-correction processes in NLO, we need only to calculate the virtual corrections, which include 60 one-loop diagrams and 20 counter-term diagrams, as illustrated in figures
1
(b)-
1
(f). We utilize the dimensional regularization with

Z_{m}

Z_{2}

\overline{MS}

Z_{g}

Z_{3}

\displaystyle\delta Z_{m}^{OS}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-3C_{F}\frac{\alpha_{s}N_{\epsilon}}{4\pi}\left[\frac{1}{\epsilon_{\textrm{UV}}}-\gamma_{E}+\textrm{ln}\frac{4\pi\mu_{r}^{2}}{m_{c}^{2}}+\frac{4}{3}\right],

\displaystyle\delta Z_{2}^{OS}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-C_{F}\frac{\alpha_{s}N_{\epsilon}}{4\pi}\left[\frac{1}{\epsilon_{\textrm{UV}}}+\frac{2}{\epsilon_{\textrm{IR}}}-3\gamma_{E}+3\textrm{ln}\frac{4\pi\mu_{r}^{2}}{m_{c}^{2}}+4\right],

\displaystyle\delta Z_{3}^{\overline{MS}}

\displaystyle\frac{\alpha_{s}N_{\epsilon}}{4\pi}(\beta_{0}-2C_{A})\left[\frac{1}{\epsilon_{\textrm{UV}}}-\gamma_{E}+\textrm{ln}(4\pi)\right],

\displaystyle\delta Z_{g}^{\overline{MS}}

In order to do the numerical calculations, we choose

4
Summary

In order to provide deeper insight into the well-known exclusive double-charmonium productions in
e^{+}e^{-}
annihilation at
B
factories, we in this paper carry out the first NLO study of the
J/\psi
angular distributions in
e^{+}e^{-}\to J/\psi+\eta_{c},\chi_{cJ}
with
J=0,1,2
based on the NRQCD factorization. The numerical results show that the newly-calculated QCD corrections moderately affect the LO predictions of
{\alpha_{\theta}}_{J/\psi+\chi_{c0}}
and
{\alpha_{\theta}}_{J/\psi+\chi_{c1}}
, while significantly reduce in magnitude the LO result of
{\alpha_{\theta}}_{J/\psi+\chi_{c2}}
. Concerning the production of
J/\psi+\eta_{c}
, the QCD corrections do not change its
\alpha_{\theta}
value. By comparisons to experiment, we find
{\alpha_{\theta}}_{J/\psi+\eta_{c}}
is consistent with the
B
ELLE
measurement; however, the existing radical incompatibility between the LO prediction of
{\alpha_{\theta}}_{J/\psi+\chi_{c0}}
and experiment becomes even worse by including the QCD corrections.
