Symmetric deformed 2D/3D Hurwitz-Kontsevich model and affine Yangian of 𝔤 ​ 𝔩 ​ ( 1 ) {\mathfrak{gl}}(1)
Wang Na†, Wu Ke‡

Abstract
Since the ( β \beta -deformed) Hurwitz Kontsevich model corresponds to the special case of affine Yangian of 𝔤 ​ 𝔩 ​ ( 1 ) {\mathfrak{gl}}(1) . In this paper, we construct two general cases of the β \beta -deformed Hurwitz Kontsevich model. We find that the W W -operators of these two models can be represented by the generators e k , f k , ψ k e_{k},\ f_{k},\psi_{k} of the affine Yangian of 𝔤 ​ 𝔩 ​ ( 1 ) {\mathfrak{gl}}(1) , and the eigenstates (the symmetric functions Y λ Y_{\lambda} and 3-Jack polynomials) can be obtained from the 3D Young diagram representation of affine Yangian of 𝔤 ​ 𝔩 ​ ( 1 ) {\mathfrak{gl}}(1) . Then we can see that the W W -operators and eigenstates are symmetric about the permutations of coordinate axes.
