Lemma 3.2

The graded commutative

-algebra

is subject to the following relations.
When

When

First note that for

\displaystyle y_{a,U}y_{b,T}

\displaystyle=\beta(\eta_{a,U})\beta(\eta_{b,T})=\beta(\beta(\eta_{a,U})\eta_{b,T})

\displaystyle=\beta(\sum_{k\in U}\eta_{a+\delta_{k-1},U-\{k\}}\eta_{b,T})

Using
