Abstract
. We study robust regularity estimates for local minimizers of nonlocal functionals with non-standard growth of ( p , q ) (p,q) -type and for weak solutions to a related class of nonlocal equations. The main results of this paper are local boundedness and Hölder continuity of minimizers and weak solutions. Our approach is based on the study of corresponding De Giorgi classes.

Introduction

The aim of this paper is to prove regularity properties of local minimizers and weak solutions to a class of nonlocal problems with non-standard growth.

Our approach for studying local minimizers and weak solutions is based on so-called De Giorgi classes. We show that minimizers of (

In the following, we discuss related literature and describe the novelty of our results.

We first comment on related results for local operators. For this purpose, we consider functionals of the following form

\int_{\Omega}f(x,u,\nabla u)\,\mathrm{d}x,

where
f
is a non-negative function which describes the growth behavior of the functional. If the function
f
satisfies the so-called
p
-growth condition, that is

|\xi|^{p}\lesssim f(x,z,\xi)\lesssim|\xi|^{p}+1\qquad\text{ for }p>1,

the literature is very rich and many regularity results have been proved. We refer the reader to the classical references
[
LU68
,
Fre75
,
GG82
]
and for a more comprehensive treatment to the books
[
Gia83
]
and
[
Giu03
]
.

Functionals with non-standard growth of
(p,q)
-type

|\xi|^{p}\lesssim f(x,z,\xi)\lesssim|\xi|^{q}+1,

where
1<p<q
, are naturally connected to Orlicz–spaces. The analysis of regularity of minimizers of functionals having non-standard growth of
(p,q)
-type
was initiated by Marcellini’s work
[
Mar89
]
, where he studies strictly convex
C^{2}
-functions
f
satisfying
(p,q)
-growth condition.

To the best of our knowledge, functionals with non-standard growth functions of the type (
1.2
) first appeared in the papers

[
Lie91
]
and
[
MN91
]
in the context of regularity results.

In the paper
[
Lie91
]
, Lieberman proves several regularity results for bounded weak solutions to a class of elliptic operators in divergence form. Furthermore, he studies quasiminimizers and proves regularity results for functions in corresponding De Giorgi classes.

In
[
MN91
]
, Moscariello and Nania prove Hölder continuity of locally bounded minimizers for growth functions satisfying (
1.2
) and local boundedness for functions with
(p,q)
-growth. Their key idea is to introduce an auxiliary function which is comparable to the growth function
f
and to prove that any function in the De Giorgi class corresponding to the auxiliary function is Hölder continuous. We adapt this idea for the proof of
Theorem

1.1
and
Theorem

1.2
to the nonlocal case, see
Section

4
.

There have been many important contributions to regularity for problems with non-standard growth of
(p,q)
-type. Papers studying local operators with non-standard growth of
(p,q)
-type are, among others,
[

,

,

,

,

,

,

For a more detailed picture on problems with non-standard growth, including double-phase problems, problems with variable exponents, and anisotropic problems, we refer the reader to the surveys
[

]
and

In the case of nonlocal operators, the energy functional is defined by (

).
Local regularity results for the fractional

-Laplacian, that is
f(t)=t^{p}
, were first established in the papers
[

DCKP16

by Di Castro, Kuusi and Palatucci. Another important contribution to regularity is the work

]
, where he studies minimizers to nonlocal energy functionals plus a possibly discontinuous

For further results on the regularity of the fractional

Lately, the interest in the analysis of nonlocal problems with non-standard growth has increased. For instance, regularity results for nonlocal double phase equations and nonlocal equation with variable exponents are proved in

As far as we know, first regularity results for fractional order Orlicz–Sobolev spaces have been proved in

by Fernández Bonder, Salort and Vivas. The authors establish regularity results for weak solutions to the Dirichlet problem for the fractional

See also

The present paper is substantially different from

where we used

.
By (

and after integrating from

Lemma 2.7
.

Lemma 2.8
.

Let

3.
De Giorgi classes

In this section, we introduce fractional order Orlicz–Sobolev spaces and define fractional De Giorgi classes governed by convex functions having non-standard growth.

Fractional order Orlicz–Sobolev spaces have been introduced in
[
FBS19
]
by Fernández Bonder and Salort. The authors prove several properties of the spaces including that the fractional order Orlicz–Sobolev space approximates some Orlicz–Sobolev space as the fractional parameter goes to
1
.
For further results concerning fractional Orlicz–Sobolev spaces, we refer the reader to
[
BO20
,
BOT20
,
ACPS21a
,
ACPS21b
,
DNFBS21
]
and the references therein.

Let
f:[0,\infty)\to[0,\infty)
be a convex increasing function satisfying (

f
q

) and
f(0)=0
. Let
s\in(0,1)
and
\Omega\subset\mathbb{R}^{d}
be open. We define the
Orlicz
and
Orlicz–Sobolev spaces
by

\begin{split}L^{f}(\Omega)&=\{u:\Omega\to\mathbb{R}~\text{measurable}:\Phi_{L^{f}(\Omega)}(u)<\infty\},\\
W^{s,f}(\Omega)&=\{u\in L^{f}(\Omega):\Phi_{W^{s,f}(\Omega)}(u)<\infty\},\\
V^{s,f}(\Omega|\mathbb{R}^{d})&=\{u\in L^{f}(\Omega):\Phi_{V^{s,f}(\Omega)}(u)<\infty\},\end{split}

where
\Phi_{L^{f}(\Omega)}
,
\Phi_{W^{s,f}(\Omega)}
and
\Phi_{V^{s,f}(\Omega|\mathbb{R}^{d})}
are
modulars
defined by

\begin{split}\Phi_{L^{f}(\Omega)}(u)&=\int_{\Omega}f(|u(x)|)\,\mathrm{d}x,\\
\Phi_{W^{s,f}(\Omega)}(u)&=(1-s)\int_{\Omega}\int_{\Omega}f\left(\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|}{|x-y|^{s}}\right)\frac{\,\mathrm{d}y\,\mathrm{d}x}{|x-y|^{d}},\\
\Phi_{V^{s,f}(\Omega|\mathbb{R}^{d})}(u)&=(1-s)\iint_{(\Omega^{c}\times\Omega^{c})^{c}}f\left(\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|}{|x-y|^{s}}\right)\frac{\,\mathrm{d}y\,\mathrm{d}x}{|x-y|^{d}}.\end{split}

Under more restrictive assumptions on
f
,
L^{f}(\Omega)
,
W^{s,f}(\Omega)
, and
V^{s,f}(\Omega|\mathbb{R}^{d})
are Banach spaces endowed with the norms

\begin{split}\|u\|_{L^{f}(\Omega)}&=\inf\{\lambda>0:\Phi_{L^{f}(\Omega)}(u/\lambda)\leq 1\},\\
\|u\|_{W^{s,f}(\Omega)}&=\|u\|_{L^{f}(\Omega)}+[u]_{W^{s,f}(\Omega)}=\|u\|_{L^{f}(\Omega)}+\inf\{\lambda>0:\Phi_{W^{s,f}(\Omega)}(u/\lambda)\leq 1\},\\
\|u\|_{V^{s,f}(\Omega|\mathbb{R}^{d})}&=\|u\|_{L^{f}(\Omega)}+[u]_{V^{s,f}(\Omega|\mathbb{R}^{d})}=\|u\|_{L^{f}(\Omega)}+\inf\{\lambda>0:\Phi_{V^{s,f}(\Omega|\mathbb{R}^{d})}(u/\lambda)\leq 1\}.\end{split}

Let us next define nonlocal tails, which capture the behavior of functions
u\in V^{s,f}(\Omega|\mathbb{R}^{d})
at large scales. For this purpose, we consider a convex and differentiable function
f
and a generalized inverse function of its derivative
f^{\prime}
. There are several definitions of generalized inverse functions in the literature, see
[
EH13
]
,
[
dLF20
]
, but we use the following definition for a generalized inverse of
f^{\prime}
:

(3.1)

(f^{\prime})^{-1}(y)=\inf\{t:f^{\prime}(t)\geq y\}.

The advantage of this definition is that (
3.1
) enjoys the following properties, which play an important role in the proof of regularity estimates.

Proposition 3.1
.

Let
f:[0,\infty)\to[0,\infty)
be a convex and differentiable function. Then

(3.2)

\displaystyle(f^{\prime}\circ(f^{\prime})^{-1})(y)\geq y\quad\text{for all }y\geq 0,

(3.3)

\displaystyle((f^{\prime})^{-1}\circ f^{\prime})(t)\leq t\quad\text{for all }t\geq 0.

Proof.

To prove (
3.2
), let
y\geq 0
and
t=(f^{\prime})^{-1}(y)
. Then, there exists a sequence
(t_{n})_{n\geq 1}
such that
t_{n}\geq t
,
t_{n}\to t
, and
f^{\prime}(t_{n})\geq y
. Since
f^{\prime}
is continuous by Darboux’s theorem, we obtain
f^{\prime}(t)\geq y
by taking the limit
n\to\infty
. Assertion (
3.3
) is obvious by definition of
(f^{\prime})^{-1}
.
∎

We define the nonlocal
f^{\prime}
-Tail by

(3.4)

\mathrm{Tail}_{f^{\prime}}(u;x_{0},R)=R^{s}(f^{\prime})^{-1}\left((1-s)R^{s}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{d}\setminus B_{R}(x_{0})}f^{\prime}\left(\frac{|u(y)|}{|y-x_{0}|^{s}}\right)\frac{\textnormal{d}y}{|y-x_{0}|^{d+s}}\right).

Note that
\mathrm{Tail}_{f^{\prime}}
coincides with the standard tail considered in
[
DCKP14
,
DCKP16
,
Coz17
]
in the special case
f(t)\asymp t^{p}
. Moreover, it is natural in the sense that the following scaling property is satisfied:

\mathrm{Tail}_{f^{\prime}}(u;x_{0},R)=\mathrm{Tail}_{f^{\prime}(\cdot/R^{s})}(u(R\cdot);x_{0}/R,1).

We claim that the nonlocal
f^{\prime}
-Tail is well-defined for functions in the fractional Orlicz–Sobolev space
V^{s,f}(\Omega|\mathbb{R}^{d})
. To this end, we define the
Legendre transform

f^{\ast}:[0,\infty)\to[0,\infty)
by
f^{\ast}(s)=\sup_{t\in[0,\infty)}(st-f(t))
. It is well known that

(3.5)

(f^{\ast}(f^{\prime}))(t)=f^{\prime}(t)t-f(t)

and that

(3.6)

st\leq f(t)+f^{\ast}(s),\quad\text{for all }t,s\geq 0.

Inequality (
3.6
) is called

.

Proposition 3.2
.

Let
q>1
,
s\in(0,1)
and
\Omega\subset\mathbb{R}^{d}
be open. Let
f:[0,\infty)\to[0,\infty)
be a convex increasing function satisfying (

f
q

) and
f(0)=0
. If

Proof.

Let

and

. We may assume that
x_{0}=0
. In order to prove finiteness of the tail, it is sufficient to show that

(3.7)

\int_{\mathbb{R}^{d}\setminus B_{R}}f^{\prime}\left(\frac{|u(y)|}{|y|^{s}}\right)\frac{\textnormal{d}y}{|y|^{d+s}}<\infty.

Since
B_{R}\subset\Omega
and
|x-y|\leq 2|y|
for

,

, we have

(3.8)

\displaystyle\infty

\displaystyle>\Phi_{L^{f}(\Omega)}(u)+\Phi_{V^{s,f}(\Omega|\mathbb{R}^{d})}(u)

\displaystyle\geq C\int_{B_{R}}f(|u(x)|)\,\mathrm{d}x+C(1-s)\iint_{B_{R}\times B_{R}^{c}}f\left(\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|}{|y|^{s}}\right)\frac{\textnormal{d}y\,\mathrm{d}x}{|y|^{d}}

by using (

f
q

Note that by
Lemma

2.3
,

2.2
and (

f

where we also used

\displaystyle\iint_{B_{R}\times B_{R}^{c}}f(|u(x)|)\frac{\textnormal{d}y\,\mathrm{d}x}{|y|^{d}(|y|^{s}\vee|y|^{sq})}+(1-s)\iint_{B_{R}\times B_{R}^{c}}f\left(\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|}{|y|^{s}}\right)\frac{\textnormal{d}y\,\mathrm{d}x}{|y|^{d}}

Altogether,

for some

. For

. Fix

and assume

Then, using (

) and

&\leq C2^{(d+sq)j}\frac{2^{(q-1)j}}{k^{q-1}}\frac{(1+(\frac{k}{\delta R^{s}})^{q})}{\frac{k}{\delta R^{s}}}Y_{j}\leq C2^{(d+2q)j}\delta^{-(q-1)}Y_{j}\end{split}

for some constant

.
Combining the estimates of

and

Using

and the inequality

\begin{split}(k_{j+1}-\tilde{k}_{j})^{(p^{\ast}-q)p/p^{\ast}}Y_{j+1}^{p/p^{\ast}}&\leq C\|\tilde{w}_{j}\|_{L^{p^{\ast}}(B_{R_{j+1}})}^{p}\\

&\leq C2^{(d+2q)j}\delta^{-(q-1)}Y_{j},\end{split}

or

where

and

. If

, then

as

then

\sup_{B_{R}}u\leq k.

We now take

with

We follow the strategy carried out in

(6.1)

Remark 7.2
.

Let us prove that the weak formulation (

\displaystyle\leq\Lambda(1-s)\iint_{(\Omega^{c}\times\Omega^{c})^{c}}f^{\prime}\left(\frac{|u(x)-u(y)|}{|x-y|^{s}}\right)\frac{|\phi(x)-\phi(y)|}{|x-y|^{s}}\frac{\,\mathrm{d}y\,\mathrm{d}x}{|x-y|^{d}}

where we used that by (

.

Theorem 7.3

Proof.

The desired result follows from a similar argument as in the proof of

. Let

,

as in

6.2

Let us estimate

. If

, then

(7.3)

B(x,y)=0.

If

and

, then by (

h

)

h\left(x,y,\frac{u(x)-u(y)}{|x-y|^{s}}\right)=h\left(x,y,\frac{w_{+}(x)+w_{-}(y)}{|x-y|^{s}}\right)\geq\frac{1}{\Lambda}f^{\prime}\left(\frac{w_{+}(x)+w_{-}(y)}{|x-y|^{s}}\right).

Thus, we obtain

(7.4)

\begin{split}B(x,y)&\geq\frac{1}{\Lambda}f^{\prime}\left(\frac{|w_{+}(x)+w_{-}(y)|}{|x-y|^{s}}\right)\frac{w_{+}(x)}{|x-y|^{s}}\eta^{q}(x)\\

&\geq\frac{1}{2\Lambda}f\left(\frac{|w_{+}(x)-w_{+}(y)|}{|x-y|^{s}}\right)\eta^{q}(x)+\frac{1}{2\Lambda}f^{\prime}\left(\frac{w_{-}(y)}{|x-y|^{s}}\right)\frac{w_{+}(x)}{|x-y|^{s}}\eta^{q}(x),\end{split}

where we used (
2.4
) and

2.3
.

If

, we prove

\begin{split}B(x,y)&\geq\frac{1}{\Lambda}f\left(\frac{|w_{+}(x)-w_{+}(y)|}{|x-y|^{s}}\right)(\eta^{q}(x)\lor\eta^{q}(y))-\varepsilon\Lambda(q-1)f\left(\frac{|w_{+}(x)-w_{+}(y)|}{|x-y|^{s}}\right)\\
&\quad-c\Lambda f\left(\frac{w_{+}(x)\lor w_{+}(y)}{|x-y|^{s}}|\eta(x)-\eta(y)|\right)\end{split}

for any
\varepsilon\in(0,1)
, where
c=c(q,\varepsilon)>0
. It is enough to prove (
7.5
) for the case
w_{+}(x)\geq w_{+}(y)
. If
\eta(x)\geq\eta(y)
, then (
7.5
) follows from

where we used (

h

) and
Lemma

2.3
. When
\eta(y)\geq\eta(x)
, we observe that

(7.6)

\begin{split}B(x,y)&=h\left(x,y,\frac{w_{+}(x)-w_{+}(y)}{|x-y|^{s}}\right)\left(\frac{w_{+}(x)-w_{+}(y)}{|x-y|^{s}}\eta^{q}(y)-\frac{w_{+}(x)}{|x-y|^{s}}(\eta^{q}(y)-\eta^{q}(x))\right)\\

2.3
