Mean Curvature Flow of High Codimension in Complex Projective Space
Artemis A. Vogiatzi

Abstract
We study the mean curvature flow of smooth m m -dimensional compact submanifolds with quadratic pinching in the Riemannian manifold ℂ ​ P n \mathbb{C}P^{n} . Our main focus is on the case of high codimension, k ≥ 2 k\geq 2 . We establish a codimension estimate that shows in regions of high curvature, the submanifold becomes approximately codimension one in a quantifiable way. This estimate enables us to prove at a singular time of the flow, there exists a rescaling that converges to a smooth codimension-one limiting flow in Euclidean space. Under a cylindrical type pinching, we show that this limiting flow is weakly convex and moves by translation. These estimates allow us to analyse the behaviour of the flow near singularities and establish the existence of the limiting flow. Lastly, we prove a decay estimate that shows that the rescaling converges smoothly to a totally geodesic limit in infinite time. This behaviour is only possible if the dimension of the submanifold is even. Our approach relies on the preservation of the quadratic pinching condition along the flow and a gradient estimate that controls the mean curvature in regions of high curvature. This result generalises the work of Pipoli and Sinestrari on the mean curvature flow of submanifolds of the complex projective space.

Introduction

Let
F_{0}\colon\mathcal{M}^{m}\rightarrow\mathbb{C}P^{n}

\mathcal{M}^{m}

\displaystyle F\colon\mathcal{M}^{m}\times[0,T)\rightarrow\mathbb{C}P^{n}

\displaystyle\begin{split}\left\{\begin{array}[]{rl}\partial_{t}F(p,t)&=H(p,t),\ \ \text{for}\ \ p\in\mathcal{M},t\in[0,T)\\

In

[

Recently, Nguyen and the author in

In the same paper, we’ve proved convergence to a codimension one limiting flow of submanifolds of higher codimension in the hyperbolic space under a suitable pinching condition.

The outline of the paper is as follows. In section 2, we give all the technical tools needed for our work and set up our notation. In section 3, we give the proof for the preservation of the quadratic pinching condition along the mean curvature flow. In section 4, we prove some gradient estimates needed for the blow up procedure. The importance of the gradient estimate is that it allows us to control the mean curvature and hence the full second fundamental form on a neighbourhood of fixed size. In section 5, we prove the codimension estimate, in the case where
H\neq 0
. This means that in regions of high curvature, the submanifold becomes codimension one quantitatively. In section 6, we prove the cylindrical estimate, which implies that the submanifold is actually cylinder-like. In section 7, we show how the codimension estimate in Riemannian manifolds actually falls into the Euclidean case and, finally, in section 8, we prove that the submanifold converges smoothly to a totally geodesic limit in infinite time.

Let

If

3.

The remaining vectors satisfy

\displaystyle\left\{\begin{array}[]{l}Je_{2r-1}=-\nu_{r}e_{2r}-\tau_{r}e_{m+2r-1},\\

It’s worth mentioning that when

When

\displaystyle R_{1}:=\sum_{\alpha,\beta}(\sum_{i,j}A_{ij}^{\alpha}A_{ij}^{\beta})^{2}+\sum_{i,j,\alpha,\beta}\left(\sum_{p}(A_{ip}^{\alpha}A_{jp}^{\beta}-A_{ip}^{\beta}A_{jp}^{\alpha})\right)^{2},

\displaystyle R_{2}:=\sum_{i,j}(\sum_{\alpha}H^{\alpha}A^{\alpha}_{ij})^{2}.

For a frame of type (B1), we easily get

\displaystyle R_{2}=\begin{cases}|\mathring{h}|^{2}|H|^{2}+\frac{1}{m}|H|^{4}&\text{ if }H\neq 0,\\
0&\text{ if }H=0.\end{cases}

The following result, proven in Section 3 in

Lemma 2.5
.

At a point where

\displaystyle 2R_{1}-2cR_{2}

\displaystyle=2|\mathring{h}|^{4}-2\left(c-\frac{2}{m}\right)|\mathring{h}|^{2}|H|^{2}-\frac{2}{m}\left(c-\frac{1}{m}\right)|H|^{4}

\displaystyle+8|\mathring{h}|^{2}|\mathring{A}^{-}|^{2}+3|\mathring{A}^{-}|^{4}.

In addition, if

\displaystyle 2R_{1}-2cR_{2}

\displaystyle\leq\left(6-\frac{2}{mc-1}\right)|\mathring{h}|^{2}|\mathring{A}^{-}|^{2}-3|\mathring{A}^{-}|^{4}

\displaystyle+\frac{2mcd}{mc-1}|\mathring{h}|^{2}+\frac{4d}{mc-1}|\mathring{A}^{-}|^{2}-\frac{2d^{2}}{mc-1}.

3
Preservation of the quadratic pinching condition

In this section, we show the quadratic pinching condition (

) in the complex projective space is preserved along the mean curvature flow, for a suitable positive constant. We define a new connection for the orthogonal decomposition of
N\mathcal{M}=E_{1}\oplus\hat{E}
where
\hat{E}
consists of normal vectors
\nu

\hat{\nabla}^{\bot}

by

\displaystyle\hat{\nabla}_{i}^{\perp}\hat{\nu}:=\nabla_{i}^{\perp}\hat{\nu}-\left\langle\nabla_{i}^{\perp}\hat{\nu},\nu_{1}\right\rangle\nu_{1}.

(
[

]

\displaystyle|\nabla^{\perp}A|^{2}

\displaystyle=\sum_{i,j,k}|\hat{\nabla}_{i}^{\perp}A^{-}_{jk}+h_{jk}\nabla_{i}^{\perp}\nu_{1}|^{2}+\sum_{i,j,k}|\langle\nabla_{i}^{\perp}A^{-}_{jk},\nu_{1}\rangle+\nabla_{i}h_{jk}|^{2}.

(3.1)

\displaystyle|\nabla^{\perp}H|^{2}

\displaystyle=|H|^{2}|\nabla^{\perp}\nu_{1}|^{2}+|\nabla|H||^{2}.

(3.2)

\displaystyle|\nabla^{\perp}A^{-}|^{2}

\displaystyle=|\hat{\nabla}^{\perp}A^{-}|^{2}+|\langle\nabla^{\perp}A^{-},\nu_{1}\rangle|^{2}.

(3.3)

It is very useful to consider the implications of the Codazzi equation for the decomposition of
\nabla_{i}^{\perp}A_{jk}
above. Projecting the Codazzi equation onto

and

\displaystyle\nabla_{i}h_{jk}+\langle\nabla_{i}^{\perp}A^{-}_{jk},\nu_{1}\rangle\quad\text{ and }\quad\hat{\nabla}_{i}^{\perp}A^{-}_{jk}+h_{jk}\nabla_{i}^{\perp}\nu_{1}

\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{m}(\nabla_{k}h_{ik}+\langle\nabla_{k}^{\perp}A^{-}_{ik},\nu_{1}\rangle)=\nabla_{i}|H|,

\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{m}(\hat{\nabla}_{k}^{\perp}A^{-}_{ik}+h_{ik}\nabla_{k}^{\perp}\nu_{1})=|H|\nabla_{i}^{\perp}\nu_{1}.

(3.5)

In order to prove that the pinching condition is preserved under the flow, we primarily follow Pipoli and Sinestrari in

Lemma 3.2

Let

(3.7)

For

Lemma 3.4

For any submanifold

We are now ready to prove the preservation of the quadratic pinching condition under the flow. We seperate the cases of codimension one and higher codimension.

Proposition 3.5

(cf.
[
31
]
, Proposition 3.6)
.

Let
\mathcal{M}_{0}
be a closed hypersurface of
\mathbb{CP}^{n}
, with

. Then, the pinching condition

\displaystyle|A|^{2}\leq\frac{1}{m-\tilde{k}}|H|^{2}+2\tilde{k}

is preserved by the mean curvature flow for every

Proof.

Let us set

with

and

. Lemma
2.3
gives

\displaystyle\left(\partial_{t}-\Delta\right)Z=

\displaystyle-2\left(|\nabla A|^{2}-c|\nabla H|^{2}\right)+2\left(|A|^{2}-c|H|^{2}\right)\left(|A|^{2}+\bar{r}\right)

\displaystyle-4\left(h_{ij}h_{j}^{p}\bar{R}_{pli}{}^{l}-h^{ij}h^{lp}\bar{R}_{pilj}\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-2\left(|\nabla A|^{2}-c|\nabla H|^{2}\right)+2Z\left(|A|^{2}+\bar{r}\right)+2d\left(|A|^{2}+\bar{r}\right)

\displaystyle-4\left(h_{ij}h_{j}{}^{p}\bar{R}_{pli}{}^{l}-h^{ij}h^{lp}\bar{R}_{pilj}\right),

(3.8)

where

\displaystyle\bar{r}=\bar{R}ic(\nu,\nu)=2(n+1).

By (
3.7
) the gradient terms in (
3
) are non-positive. Indeed, for
\tilde{k}\geq 1
we need at least

. Also, this inequality provides a condition on how large
\tilde{k}
can get. So, it suffices to consider the reaction terms. Fix an orthonormal basis tangent to
\mathcal{M}_{t}
, which diagonalizes the second fundamental form and denote
\lambda_{1}\leq\lambda_{2}\leq\cdots\leq\lambda_{m}
its eigenvalues. Recalling that the sectional curvature

\displaystyle-4\left(h_{ij}h_{j}^{p}\bar{R}_{pli}{}^{l}-h^{ij}h^{lp}\bar{R}_{pilj}\right)

\displaystyle=-4\left(\lambda_{j}^{2}\delta_{ij}\delta_{jp}\bar{R}_{plil}-\lambda_{j}\lambda_{l}\delta_{ij}\delta_{lp}\bar{R}_{pilj}\right)

\displaystyle=-4\sum_{j,l}\left(\lambda_{j}^{2}-\lambda_{j}\lambda_{l}\right)\bar{R}_{jljl}

\displaystyle=-2\sum_{j,l}\left(\lambda_{j}-\lambda_{l}\right)^{2}\bar{K}_{jl}

\displaystyle\leq-2\sum_{j,l}\left(\lambda_{j}-\lambda_{l}\right)^{2}

\displaystyle=-4m|\mathring{A}|^{2}.

Since

\displaystyle 2d\left(|A|^{2}+\bar{r}\right)-4m\left(|A|^{2}-\frac{1}{m}|H|^{2}\right)=-\frac{4}{c}\left(|A|^{2}-c|H|^{2}-\frac{c}{2}d\bar{r}\right)\leq-\frac{4}{c}Z.

By the maximum principle, we get the desired result.

\displaystyle+24\sum_{i,r\neq s\leq\frac{k}{2}}\left(\left|\mathring{A}_{i\ 2s}^{m+2r}\right|^{2}+\left|\mathring{A}_{i\ 2s}^{m+2r-1}\right|^{2}+\left|\mathring{A}_{i\ 2s-1}^{m+2r}\right|^{2}+\left|\mathring{A}_{i\ 2s-1}^{m+2r-1}\right|^{2}\right)

\displaystyle+16\sum_{i,r,s\leq\frac{m-k}{2}}\left(\left|\mathring{A}_{i\ k+2s-1}^{m+2r-1}\right|^{2}+\left|\mathring{A}_{i\ k+2s}^{m+2r}\right|^{2}+\left|\mathring{A}_{i\ k+2s}^{m+2r-1}\right|^{2}+\left|\mathring{A}_{i\ k+2s-1}^{m+2r}\right|^{2}\right)

\displaystyle\leq 8k|\mathring{A}|^{2}.

So we can say that in any case

\displaystyle III\leq 8k|\mathring{A}|^{2}.

(3.14)

In conclusion, by (
3.12
), (
3
) and (
3.14
) we get

\displaystyle P_{\alpha}=I+II+III\leq-2(m-3-4k)|\mathring{A}|^{2}.

(3.15)

Let
R=2R_{1}-2c_{m}R_{2}+P_{\alpha}
. Considering a frame of type (B1), Lemma (
2.5
) says that at any point with
g=0
, we get

\displaystyle R

\displaystyle=\left(6-\frac{2}{mc_{m}-1}\right)|\mathring{A}|^{2}|\mathring{A}^{-}|^{2}+\left(\frac{2mc_{m}d_{m}}{mc_{m}-1}-2(m-3-4k)\right)|\mathring{h}|^{2}-3|\mathring{A}^{-}|^{4}

\displaystyle+\left(\frac{4d_{m}}{mc_{m}-1}-2(m-3-4k)\right)|\mathring{A}^{-}|^{2}-\frac{2d_{m}^{2}}{mc_{m}-1}.

Note that, for our choice of
c_{m}
and
d_{m}
the coefficient of the term
|\mathring{A}|^{2}|\mathring{A}^{-}|^{2}
is negative, since
m\geq 4\tilde{k}
, while the coefficient of the term
|\mathring{h}|^{2}
is zero. Moreover, the assumptions
g=0
and
c_{m}>1/\mathrm{m}
, imply that
|\mathring{A}|^{2}\geq d_{m}
. Using this, we have

\displaystyle R\leq-3|\mathring{A}^{-}|^{4}+\left(\left(6-\frac{2}{mc_{m}-1}\right)d_{m}+\frac{4d_{m}}{mc_{m}-1}-2(m-3-4k)\right)|\mathring{A}^{-}|^{2}-\frac{2d_{m}^{2}}{mc_{m}-1}

\displaystyle=-3|\mathring{A}^{-}|^{4}+4d_{m}|\mathring{A}^{-}|^{2}+2d_{m}(d_{m}-m+3+4k).

Using
4d_{m}|\mathring{A}^{-}|^{2}\leq 3|\mathring{A}^{-}|^{4}+\frac{4}{3}d_{m}^{2}
, we conclude that

\displaystyle R\leq 2d_{m}\left(\frac{5}{3}d_{m}-m+3+4k\right).

Our choice of
d_{m}
, implies
R<0
, since
m\geq 4\tilde{k}
. Now, consider the case of a point where
g=0
and
H=0
. In this case, we have
|A|^{2}=

|\mathring{A}|^{2}=d_{m},R_{2}=0
. In addition, using Theorem 1 from
[
19
]
, we find
2R_{1}\leq 3|A|^{4}=3d_{m}^{2}
. As before, we obtain that
P_{\alpha}\leq-2(m-3-4k)|\mathring{A}|^{2}=-2(m-3-4k)d_{m}
. Therefore,

\displaystyle R\leq 3d_{m}^{2}-2(m-3-4k)d_{m},

which is negative for the
d_{m}
we chose. By the maximum principle, we get the desired result.
∎

4
Gradient Estimate

This section presents a proof of the gradient estimate for the mean curvature flow. We establish this estimate directly from the quadratic curvature bound
|A|^{2}<c_{m}|H|^{2}+d_{m}
, where
c_{m}\leq\frac{1}{m-\tilde{k}}
, without relying on the asymptotic cylindrical estimates. In fact, we demonstrate the cylindrical estimates follow as a consequence of the gradient estimates we derive here. These estimates are pointwise gradient estimates that rely solely on the mean curvature (or, equivalently, the second fundamental form) at a point and not on the maximum of curvature, as is the case with more general parabolic-type derivative estimates. Specifically, we obtain

\displaystyle\frac{16}{9(m+2)}-c_{m}>0.

This inequality enables us to combine the derivative terms in the evolution equation of
|A|^{2}-c_{m}|H|^{2}-d_{m}
with the Kato-type inequality from Lemma
3.4
.

Theorem 4.1

(cf.
[
29
]
, Theorem 4.1, cf.
[
15
]
, Section 6)
.

Let
\mathcal{M}_{t},t\in[0,T)
be a closed
m
-dimensional quadratically bounded solution to the mean curvature flow in the Riemannian manifold
\mathbb{C}P^{n}
, that is

\displaystyle|A|^{2}-c|H|^{2}-d<0,|H|>0

with
c=\frac{1}{m-\tilde{k}}
.
Then, there exists a constant
\gamma_{1}=\gamma_{1}(m,\mathcal{M}_{0})
and a constant
\gamma_{2}=\gamma_{2}(m,\mathcal{M}_{0})
, such that the flow satisfies the uniform estimate

\displaystyle|\nabla A|^{2}\leq\gamma_{1}|A|^{4}+\gamma_{2},

for every
t\in[0,T)
.

Proof.

The proof is the same in the case of hypersurfaces, from Proposition
3.5
. We choose here
\kappa_{m}=\left(\frac{16}{9(m+2)}-c\right)>0
. We will consider here the evolution equation for

\displaystyle\frac{|\nabla A|^{2}}{g^{2}},

where
g=c|H|^{2}-|A|^{2}+d>0
. Since
|A|^{2}-c|H|^{2}<0,|H|>0
and
\mathcal{M}_{0}
is compact, there exists an
\eta(\mathcal{M}_{0})>0,C_{\eta}(\mathcal{M}_{0})>0
, so that

\displaystyle\left(c-\eta\right)|H|^{2}-|A|^{2}\geq C_{\eta}>0.

(4.1)

Hence, we set

\displaystyle g=c|H|^{2}-|A|^{2}\geq\eta|H|^{2}>\frac{\eta}{c}|A|^{2}>\varepsilon_{1}|A|^{2}+\varepsilon_{2},

where
\varepsilon_{1}=\frac{\eta}{c}
and
\varepsilon_{2}>0
. From Lemma
3.4
and Theorem
3.6
and a suitable constant
d
, we get

\displaystyle\left(\partial_{t}-\Delta\right)g

\displaystyle=-2\left(c|\nabla H|^{2}-|\nabla A|^{2}\right)+2\left(cR_{2}-R_{1}\right)+P_{\alpha}

\displaystyle\geq-2\left(\Big(\frac{16}{9(m+2)}-\eta\Big)^{-1}c-1\right)|\nabla A|^{2}

\displaystyle\geq-2\Big(\frac{9(m+2)}{16}c-1\Big)|\nabla A|^{2}

\displaystyle=2\kappa_{m}\frac{9(m+2)}{16}|\nabla A|^{2},

for a suitable positive constant
\eta
. The evolution equation for
|\nabla A|^{2}
is given by

\displaystyle\left(\partial_{t}-\Delta\right)|\nabla A|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq-2|\nabla^{2}A|^{2}+c|A|^{2}|\nabla A|^{2}+d|\nabla A|^{2}.

Let
w,z
satisfy the evolution equations

\displaystyle\partial_{t}w=\Delta w+W,\quad\partial_{t}z=\Delta z+Z

then, we find

\displaystyle\left(\partial_{t}-\Delta\right)\frac{w}{z}

\displaystyle=\frac{2}{z}\left\langle\nabla\left(\frac{w}{z}\right),\nabla z\right\rangle+\frac{W}{z}-\frac{w}{z^{2}}Z

\displaystyle=2\frac{\langle\nabla w,\nabla z\rangle}{z^{2}}-2\frac{w|\nabla z|^{2}}{z^{3}}+\frac{W}{z}-\frac{w}{z^{2}}Z.

Furthermore, for any function
g
, we have by Kato’s inequality

\displaystyle\langle\nabla g,\nabla|\nabla A|^{2}\rangle

\displaystyle\leq 2|\nabla g||\nabla^{2}A||\nabla A|\leq\frac{1}{g}|\nabla g|^{2}|\nabla A|^{2}+g|\nabla^{2}A|^{2}.

We then get

\displaystyle-\frac{2}{g}|\nabla^{2}A|^{2}+\frac{2}{g}\left\langle\nabla g,\nabla\left(\frac{|\nabla A|^{2}}{g}\right)\right\rangle\leq-\frac{2}{g}|\nabla^{2}A|^{2}-\frac{2}{g^{3}}|\nabla g|^{2}|\nabla A|^{2}+\frac{2}{g^{2}}\langle\nabla g,\nabla|\nabla A|^{2}\rangle\leq 0.

Then, if we let
w=|\nabla A|^{2}
and
z=g
, with
W\leq-2|\nabla^{2}A|^{2}+c|A|^{2}|\nabla A|^{2}+d|\nabla A|^{2}
and
Z\geq 2\kappa_{m}\frac{9(m+2)}{16}|\nabla A|^{2}
, we get

\displaystyle\left(\partial_{t}-\Delta\right)\frac{|\nabla A|^{2}}{g}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{2}{g}\left\langle\nabla g,\nabla\left(\frac{|\nabla A|^{2}}{g}\right)\right\rangle+\frac{1}{g}(-2|\nabla^{2}A|^{2}+c|A|^{2}|\nabla A|^{2}

\displaystyle+d|\nabla A|^{2})-2\kappa_{m}\frac{9(m+2)}{16}\frac{|\nabla A|^{4}}{g^{2}}

\displaystyle\leq c|A|^{2}\frac{|\nabla A|^{2}}{g}+d\frac{|\nabla A|^{2}}{g}-2\kappa_{m}\frac{9(m+2)}{16}\frac{|\nabla A|^{4}}{g^{2}}.

We repeat the above computation with
w=\frac{|\nabla A|^{2}}{g},z=g,

\displaystyle W\leq c|A|^{2}\frac{|\nabla A|^{2}}{g}+d\frac{|\nabla A|^{2}}{g}-2\kappa_{m}\frac{9(m+2)}{16}\frac{|\nabla A|^{4}}{g^{2}}

and
Z\geq 0
, to get

\displaystyle\left(\partial_{t}-\Delta\right)\frac{|\nabla A|^{2}}{g^{2}}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{2}{g}\left\langle\nabla g,\nabla\left(\frac{|\nabla A|^{2}}{g^{2}}\right)\right\rangle

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{g}\left(c|A|^{2}\frac{|\nabla A|^{2}}{g}+d\frac{|\nabla A|^{2}}{g}-2\kappa_{m}\frac{9(m+2)}{16}\frac{|\nabla A|^{4}}{g^{2}}\right).

The nonlinearity then is

\displaystyle\frac{|\nabla A|^{2}}{g^{2}}\left(c|A|^{2}+d-\frac{2\kappa_{m}9(m+2)}{16}\frac{|\nabla A|^{2}}{g}\right).

Since

\displaystyle g>\varepsilon_{1}|A|^{2}+\varepsilon_{2},

there exists a constant
N
, such that

\displaystyle Ng\geq c|A|^{2}+d.

Hence, by the maximum principle, there exists a constant (with
\eta,\varepsilon_{1},\varepsilon_{2}
chosen sufficiently small so that N is sufficiently large, this estimate holds at the initial time), such that

\displaystyle\frac{|\nabla A|^{2}}{g^{2}}\leq\frac{8N}{\kappa_{m}9(m+2)}.

Therefore, we see there exists a constant
\mathcal{C}=\frac{8N}{\kappa_{m}9(m+2)}=\mathcal{C}(m,\mathcal{M}_{0})
, such that

\displaystyle\frac{|\nabla A|^{2}}{g^{2}}\leq\mathcal{C}

and from the definition of
g
, we get the result of the lemma.
∎

Theorem 4.2

(

29
]
, Theorem 4.2)
.

Let
\mathcal{M}_{t},t\in[0,T)

\gamma_{3},\gamma_{4}

.

\displaystyle\left|\partial_{t}A\right|=|\Delta A+A*A*A|\leq C|\nabla^{2}A|+C|A|^{3}\leq c_{1}|A|^{3}+c_{2}.

5
Codimension Estimates on
\mathbb{C}P^{n}

In this section, we consider
T<\infty
and we want to show that in regions of high curvature, the submanifold of
\mathbb{C}P^{n}
becomes approximately codimension one in a quantifiable sense. Our goal is to seperate the second fundamental form in the principle direction and the second fundamental form in the other directions and compute their evolution equations seperately. Later, we find estimates for the reaction and gradient terms as well as for the lower order terms, which appear due to the Riemannian ambient space. Then, we start by computing the evolution equation of the quantity
\frac{|A^{-}|^{2}}{f}
, which since in the limit the background space is Euclidean, the result will follow from the maximum principle. Here, we follow the computations of our previous paper
[
29
]
, using the quadratic bound
|A|^{2}\leq c_{m}|H|^{2}+d_{m}
, for
c_{m}=\frac{1}{m-\tilde{k}}
and
d_{m}=\frac{\tilde{k}}{m}(m-3-4k),\tilde{k}\geq 1
.

5.1
The Evolution Equation of
|A^{-}|^{2}

We start by computing the evolution equation of
|A^{-}|^{2}
. We define the tensor
A^{-}
by

\displaystyle A^{-}(X,Y)=A(X,Y)-\frac{\langle A(X,Y),H\rangle}{|H|^{2}}H,

for vector fields
X,Y
tangent to
\mathcal{M}_{t}
. The tensor
A^{-}
is well defined for
H>0
. At points where
H=0
, we define
\frac{|A^{-}|^{2}}{f}=0
, for
f=d_{m}+c_{m}|H|^{2}-|A|^{2}>0
as we will see later on. Therefore, we will need to compute the evolution equations of
|A|^{2}
and
\frac{|\langle A,H\rangle|^{2}}{|H|^{2}}.
Using (
2.6
) and the quotient rule, we have

\displaystyle\Big(\partial_{t}

\displaystyle-\Delta\Big)\frac{\sum_{i,j}|\langle A_{ij},H\rangle|^{2}}{|H|^{2}}=|H|^{-2}\left(\partial_{t}-\Delta\right)\sum_{i,j}|\langle A_{ij},H\rangle|^{2}

\displaystyle+2\sum_{k}|H|^{-2}\Big\langle\nabla_{k}|H|^{2},\nabla_{k}\frac{\sum_{i,j}|\langle A_{ij},H\rangle|^{2}}{|H|^{2}}\Big\rangle

\displaystyle-|H|^{-4}\sum_{i,j}|\langle A_{ij},H\rangle|^{2}\big(-2|\nabla^{\bot}H|^{2}+2\sum_{i,j}|\langle A_{ij},H\rangle|^{2}+2\sum_{k,\alpha,\beta}\bar{R}_{k\alpha k\beta}H^{\alpha}H^{\beta}\big).

Before computing the evolution equation of
\sum_{i,j}|\langle A_{ij},H\rangle|^{2}
, we simplify the other terms. In particular, using
\sum_{i,j}|\langle A_{ij},H\rangle|^{2}=|H|^{2}|h|^{2}
and

\displaystyle|\nabla^{\bot}H|^{2}=|H|^{2}|\nabla^{\bot}\nu_{1}|^{2}+|\nabla|H||^{2},

we write

\displaystyle 2|H|^{-4}\sum_{i,j}|\langle A_{ij},H\rangle|^{2}|\nabla^{\bot}H|^{2}=2|h|^{2}|\nabla^{\bot}\nu_{1}|^{2}+2|H|^{-2}|h|^{2}|\nabla|H||^{2},

\displaystyle-2|H|^{-4}\sum_{i,j}|\langle A_{ij},H\rangle|^{4}=-2|h|^{4},

\displaystyle 2|H|^{-4}\sum_{i,j}|\langle A_{ij},H\rangle|^{2}\sum_{k,\alpha,\beta}\bar{R}_{k\alpha k\beta}H^{\alpha}H^{\beta}=2|h|^{2}|H|^{-2}\sum_{k,\alpha,\beta}\bar{R}_{k\alpha k\beta}H^{\alpha}H^{\beta}.

As for the remaining gradient terms, we have

\displaystyle\nabla_{k}|H|^{2}=2\langle\nabla_{k}^{\bot}H,H\rangle

and

\displaystyle\nabla_{k}(|H|^{-2}\sum_{i,j}|\langle A_{ij},H\rangle|^{2})=\nabla_{k}|h|^{2}=2\sum_{i,j}h_{ij}\nabla_{k}h_{ij}.

Therefore, since
H=|H|\nu_{1}
and
\langle\nabla^{\bot}_{k}\nu_{1},\nu_{1}\rangle=0
, we have

\displaystyle 2|H|^{-2}\sum_{k}\Big\langle\nabla_{k}|H|^{2},\nabla_{k}\frac{\sum_{i,j}|\langle A_{ij},H\rangle|^{2}}{|H|^{2}}\Big\rangle

\displaystyle=8|H|^{-2}\sum_{i,j,k}\langle\nabla^{\bot}_{k}H,H\rangle h_{ij}\nabla_{k}h_{ij}

\displaystyle=8|H|^{-1}\sum_{i,j,k}\nabla_{k}|H|h_{ij}\nabla_{k}h_{ij}.

To summarise, we have shown so far that

\displaystyle\left(\partial_{t}-\Delta\right)\frac{\sum_{i,j}|\langle A_{ij},H\rangle|^{2}}{|H|^{2}}

\displaystyle=|H|^{-2}\left(\partial_{t}-\Delta\right)\sum_{i,j}|\langle A_{ij},H\rangle|^{2}-2|h|^{4}+2|h|^{2}\sum_{k}|\nabla^{\bot}_{k}\nu_{1}|^{2}

\displaystyle+2|H|^{-2}|h|^{2}|\nabla|H||^{2}+8|H|^{-1}\sum_{i,j,k}\nabla_{k}|H|h_{ij}\nabla_{k}h_{ij}

\displaystyle-2|h|^{2}|H|^{-2}\sum_{k,\alpha,\beta}\bar{R}_{k\alpha k\beta}H^{\alpha}H^{\beta}.

For the evolution equation of
\langle A_{ij},H\rangle
, we have the following lemma.

Lemma 5.1
.

The evolution equation of
|\langle A_{ij},H\rangle|^{2}
is

\displaystyle|H|^{-2}\left(\partial_{t}-\Delta\right)|\langle A_{ij},H\rangle|^{2}

\displaystyle=4|\mathring{h}_{ij}A_{ij}^{-}|^{2}+2|R_{ij}^{\bot}(\nu_{1})|^{2}+4|h|^{4}-4|H|^{-1}\mathring{h}_{ij}\nabla_{k}|H|\langle\nabla^{\bot}_{k}A_{ij}^{-},\nu_{1}\rangle

\displaystyle-8|H|^{-1}\nabla_{k}|H|h_{ij}\nabla_{k}h_{ij}-2|\nabla h|^{2}+2B^{\prime}

\displaystyle-2|\bar{R}_{ij}(\nu_{1})|^{2}-4\langle\bar{R}_{ij}(\nu_{1}),\mathring{h}_{ip}A^{-}_{jp}-\mathring{h}_{jp}A^{-}_{ip}\rangle,

\displaystyle:=2|H|^{-2}\bar{R}_{ipjq}\langle A_{pq},H\rangle\langle A_{ij},H\rangle-2|H|^{-2}\bar{R}_{kjkp}\langle A_{pi},H\rangle\langle A_{ij},H\rangle

\displaystyle+|H|^{-2}A^{\alpha}_{ij}\bar{R}_{k\alpha k\beta}H^{\beta}\langle A_{ij},H\rangle+|H|^{-2}H^{\alpha}\bar{R}_{k\alpha k\beta}A_{ij}^{\beta}\langle A_{ij},H\rangle

Proof.

Whenever
h

A^{-}
or its derivative, we may replace

with
\mathring{h}

2.1

\displaystyle\Big\langle\left(\partial_{t}-\Delta\right)^{\bot}A_{ij},H\Big\rangle

\displaystyle=\langle A_{ij},A_{pq}\rangle\langle A_{pq},H\rangle+\langle A_{iq},A_{pq}\rangle\langle A_{pj},H\rangle+\langle A_{jq},A_{pq}\rangle\langle A_{pi},H\rangle

\displaystyle+A^{\alpha}_{ij}\bar{R}_{k\alpha k\beta}\langle\nu_{\beta},H\rangle-2A^{\alpha}_{jp}\bar{R}_{ip\alpha\beta}\langle\nu_{\beta},H\rangle-2A^{\alpha}_{ip}\bar{R}_{jp\alpha\beta}\langle\nu_{\beta},H\rangle,

\displaystyle\Big\langle A_{ij},\left(\partial_{t}-\Delta\right)^{\bot}H\Big\rangle

\displaystyle=\langle A_{pq},H\rangle\langle A_{pq},A_{ij}\rangle+H^{\alpha}\bar{R}_{k\alpha k\beta}\langle\nu_{\beta},A_{ij}\rangle.

Tracing each of the equations with a copy of
\langle A_{ij},H\rangle

\displaystyle\Big\langle\left(\partial_{t}-\Delta\right)^{\bot}A_{ij},H\Big\rangle\langle A_{ij},H\rangle

Putting the above equations together and keeping in mind that

\displaystyle\Big(\left(\partial_{t}-\Delta\right)\langle A_{ij},H\rangle\Big)\langle A_{ij},H\rangle

\displaystyle-2\langle A_{ip},A_{jq}\rangle\langle A_{pq},H\rangle\langle A_{ij},H\rangle+2\bar{R}_{ipjq}\langle A_{pq},H\rangle\langle A_{ij},H\rangle

\displaystyle B

\displaystyle:=2\bar{R}_{ipjq}\langle A_{pq},H\rangle\langle A_{ij},H\rangle-2\bar{R}_{kjkp}\langle A_{pi},H\rangle\langle A_{ij},H\rangle

\displaystyle 4|H|^{-2}\langle A_{ij},A_{pq}\rangle\langle A_{pq},H\rangle\langle A_{ij},H\rangle

\displaystyle=4|H|^{-2}|H|^{2}h_{ij}h_{pq}\langle A_{ij},A_{pq}\rangle

\displaystyle=4|h|^{4}+4h_{ij}h_{pq}\langle A_{ij}^{-},A_{pq}^{-}\rangle

\displaystyle=4|h|^{4}+4\mathring{h}_{ij}\mathring{h}_{pq}\langle A_{ij}^{-},A_{pq}^{-}\rangle

\displaystyle=4|h|^{4}+4|\mathring{h}_{ij}A_{ij}^{-}|^{2}.

(5.2)

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{f}\Big(\frac{|A^{-}|^{2}}{f}\Big(-2c_{m}\sum_{k,\alpha,\beta}\bar{R}_{k\alpha k\beta}H^{\alpha}H^{\beta}-8\sum_{j,p,\alpha,\beta}\bar{R}_{jp\alpha\beta}\big(\sum_{i}A^{\alpha}_{ip}A_{ij}^{\beta}\big)\Big)\Big)

\displaystyle+2\Big\langle\nabla\frac{|A^{-}|^{2}}{f},\nabla\log f\Big\rangle.

Let us give a brief explanation of the above evolution equation. The first two lines on the right hand side are the higher order terms and the terms in the third line are Euclidean terms. The terms in the fourth and fifth line are lower order terms, that are orthogonal to the principal direction. The terms on the sixth and nineth line are gradient terms and the terms on the seventh and eighth line are lower order terms, both in the principal direction and orthogonal to the principal direction.

We begin by estimating the reaction terms. We will make use of two estimates. The first estimate is proven on page 372 in

Lemma 5.3
.

\displaystyle\sum_{i,j}|\mathring{h}_{ij}A^{-}_{ij}|^{2}+\sum_{i,j}|R_{ij}^{\perp}(\nu_{1})|^{2}\leq 2|\mathring{h}|^{2}|{A^{-}}|^{2}+\sum_{i,j}|\bar{R}_{ij}(\nu_{1})|^{2}+4|\bar{R}_{ij}(\nu_{1})||\mathring{h}||A^{-}|,

(5.12)

\displaystyle\sum_{i,j,p,q}|\langle A^{-}_{ij},A^{-}_{pq}\rangle|^{2}+\big|\hat{R}^{\perp}\big|^{2}\leq\frac{3}{2}|A^{-}|^{4}+\sum_{\alpha,\beta\geq 2}\Big(\sum_{i,j}|\bar{R}_{ij\alpha\beta}|^{2}+4|\bar{R}_{ij\alpha\beta}||A^{-}|^{2}\Big).

(5.13)

Proof.

The arguments given in
[
3
]
to prove inequality (
5.12
) are simple and short, so we will repeat them in our notation here. We will express inequality (
5.13
) so that it is an immediate consequence of Lemma 3.3 in
[
19
]
.
Fix any point
p\in\mathcal{M}
and time
t\in[0,T)
. Let
e_{1},\ldots,e_{m}
be an orthonormal basis which identifies
T_{p}\mathcal{M}\cong\mathbb{R}^{m}
at time
t
and then choose
\nu_{2},\ldots,\nu_{k}
to be a basis of the orthogonal complement of principal normal
\nu_{1}
in
N_{p}\mathcal{M}
at time
t
. For each
\beta\in\{2,\ldots,k\}
, define a matrix
A_{\beta}=\left\langle A,\nu_{\beta}\right\rangle
, whose components are given by
\left(A_{\beta}\right)_{ij}=A_{ij\beta}
.

Then
A^{-}=\sum_{\beta\geq 2}A_{\beta}\nu_{\beta}
. We also have
\mathring{h}=\langle\mathring{A},\nu_{1}\rangle
.
To prove (
5.12
), let
\lambda_{1},\ldots,\lambda_{m}
denote the eigenvalues of
\mathring{h}
. Assume the orthonormal basis is an eigenbasis of
\mathring{h}
. Now

\displaystyle\sum_{i,j}|\mathring{h}_{ij}A^{-}_{ij}|^{2}=\sum_{\beta\geq 2}\sum_{i,j,p,q}\mathring{h}_{ij}\mathring{h}_{pq}A^{\beta}_{ij}A^{\beta}_{pq}=\sum_{\beta\geq 2}\big(\sum_{i,j}\mathring{h}_{ij}A^{\beta}_{ij}\big)^{2}=\sum_{\beta\geq 2}\big(\sum_{i}\lambda_{i}A^{\beta}_{ii}\big)^{2}.

By Cauchy-Schwarz,

\displaystyle\sum_{i,j}\big|\mathring{h}_{ij}A^{-}_{ij}\big|^{2}\leq\sum_{\beta\geq 2}\big(\sum_{i}\lambda_{j}^{2}\big)\big(\sum_{i}(A^{\beta}_{ii})^{2}\big)=|\mathring{h}|^{2}\sum_{\beta\geq 2}\sum_{i}(A^{\beta}_{ii})^{2}.

(5.14)

Now, using

\displaystyle\sum_{i,j}|R^{\bot}_{ij}(\nu_{1})|^{2}=\sum_{i,j}|\bar{R}_{ij}(\nu_{1})|^{2}+\sum_{i,j,k}|\mathring{h}_{ik}A^{-}_{jk}-\mathring{h}_{jk}A^{-}_{ik}|^{2}+2\sum_{i,j,p}\langle\bar{R}_{ij}(\nu_{1}),\mathring{h}_{ip}A^{-}_{jp}-\mathring{h}_{jp}A^{-}_{ip}\rangle,

(5.15)

and (
2
) we have

\displaystyle|R_{ij}^{\perp}(\nu_{1})|^{2}

\displaystyle=\sum_{\beta\geq 2}\sum_{i,j,k}\big(\mathring{h}_{ik}A^{\beta}_{jk}-\mathring{h}_{jk}A^{\beta}_{ik}\big)^{2}+\sum_{i,j}|\bar{R}_{ij}(\nu_{1})|^{2}+2\sum_{i,j,p}\langle\bar{R}_{ij}(\nu_{1}),\mathring{h}_{ip}A^{-}_{jp}-\mathring{h}_{jp}A^{-}_{ip}\rangle

\displaystyle=\sum_{\beta\geq 2}\sum_{i,j}\big(\lambda_{i}-\lambda_{j}\big)^{2}(A^{\beta}_{ij})^{2}+\sum_{i,j}|\bar{R}_{ij}(\nu_{1})|^{2}+2\sum_{i,j,p}\langle\bar{R}_{ij}(\nu_{1}),\mathring{h}_{ip}A^{-}_{jp}-\mathring{h}_{jp}A^{-}_{ip}\rangle

\displaystyle=\sum_{\beta\geq 2}\sum_{i\neq j}\big(\lambda_{i}-\lambda_{j}\big)^{2}(A^{\beta}_{ij})^{2}+\sum_{i,j}|\bar{R}_{ij}(\nu_{1})|^{2}+2\sum_{i,j,p}\langle\bar{R}_{ij}(\nu_{1}),\mathring{h}_{ip}A^{-}_{jp}-\mathring{h}_{jp}A^{-}_{ip}\rangle.

Since
\left(\lambda_{i}-\lambda_{j}\right)^{2}\leq 2\left(\lambda_{i}^{2}+\lambda_{j}^{2}\right)\leq 2|\mathring{h}|^{2}
, we have

\displaystyle\sum_{i,j}|R_{ij}^{\perp}(\nu_{1})|^{2}\leq 2|\mathring{h}|^{2}\sum_{\beta\geq 2}\sum_{i\neq j}(A^{\beta}_{ij})^{2}+\sum_{i,j}|\bar{R}_{ij}(\nu_{1})|^{2}+4|\bar{R}_{ij}(\nu_{1})||\mathring{h}||A^{-}|.

(5.16)

Summing (
5.14
) and (
5.16
), we obtain

\displaystyle\sum_{i,j}|\mathring{h}_{ij}A^{-}_{ij}|^{2}+\sum_{i,j}|R_{ij}^{\perp}(\nu_{1})|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq|\mathring{h}|^{2}\sum_{\beta\geq 2}\sum_{i}(A^{\beta}_{ii})^{2}+2|\mathring{h}|^{2}\sum_{\beta\geq 2}\sum_{i\neq j}(A^{\beta}_{ij})^{2}+\sum_{i,j}|\bar{R}_{ij}(\nu_{1})|^{2}

\displaystyle+4|\bar{R}_{ij}(\nu_{1})||\mathring{h}||A^{-}|

\displaystyle\leq 2|\mathring{h}|^{2}|A^{-}|^{2}+\sum_{i,j}|\bar{R}_{ij}(\nu_{1})|^{2}+4|\bar{R}_{ij}(\nu_{1})||\mathring{h}||A^{-}|,

which is (
5.12
).
To establish (
5.7
), for
\alpha,\beta\in\{2,\ldots,k\}
define

\displaystyle S_{\alpha\beta}:=\operatorname{tr}\left(A_{\alpha}A_{\beta}\right)=\sum_{i,j}A^{\alpha}_{ij}A^{\beta}_{ij}\quad\text{ and }\quad S_{\alpha}:=\left|A_{\alpha}\right|^{2}=\sum_{i,j}A^{\alpha}_{ij}A^{\alpha}_{ij}.

Let
S:=S_{2}+\cdots+S_{k}=|A^{-}|^{2}
. Now

\displaystyle\sum_{i,j,p,q}|\langle A^{-}_{ij},A^{-}_{pq}\rangle|^{2}

\displaystyle=\sum_{i,j,p,q}\sum_{\alpha,\beta\geq 2}A^{\alpha}_{ij}A^{\alpha}_{pq}A^{\beta}_{ij}A^{\beta}_{pq}

\displaystyle=\sum_{\alpha,\beta\geq 2}(\sum_{i,j}A^{\alpha}_{ij}A^{\beta}_{ij})(\sum_{p,q}A^{\alpha}_{pq}A^{\beta}_{pq})

\displaystyle=\sum_{\alpha,\beta\geq 2}S_{\alpha\beta}^{2}.

In addition, recalling (
5.9
), we may write

\displaystyle\big|\hat{R}^{\perp}\big|^{2}=\sum_{\alpha,\beta\geq 2}\Big(\left|A_{\alpha}A_{\beta}-A_{\beta}A_{\alpha}\right|^{2}+\sum_{i,j}|\bar{R}_{ij\alpha\beta}|^{2}+2\sum_{i,j,p}\langle\bar{R}_{ij\alpha\beta},A_{ip}^{\alpha}A_{jp}^{\beta}-A_{jp}^{\alpha}A_{ip}^{\beta}\rangle\Big)

where
\left(A_{\alpha}A_{\beta}\right)_{ij}=\left(A_{\alpha}\right)_{ik}\left(A_{\beta}\right)_{kj}=\left(A_{\alpha}\right)_{ik}\left(A_{\beta}\right)_{jk}
denotes standard matrix multiplication and
|\cdot|
is the usual square norm of the matrix. We see that inequality (
5.13
) is equivalent to

\displaystyle\sum_{\alpha,\beta\geq 2}\left|A_{\alpha}A_{\beta}-A_{\beta}A_{\alpha}\right|^{2}+\sum_{\alpha,\beta\geq 2}S_{\alpha\beta}^{2}\leq\frac{3}{2}S^{2}.

(5.17)

Therefore, we have

\displaystyle\sum_{i,j,p,q}|\langle A^{-}_{ij},A^{-}_{pq}\rangle|^{2}+|\hat{R}^{\bot}|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{3}{2}|A^{-}|^{4}+\sum_{i,j,\alpha,\beta\geq 2}\Big(|\bar{R}_{ij\alpha\beta}|^{2}+2\sum_{p}\langle\bar{R}_{ij\alpha\beta},A_{ip}^{\alpha}A_{jp}^{\beta}-A_{jp}^{\alpha}A_{ip}^{\beta}\rangle\Big)

\displaystyle\leq\frac{3}{2}|A^{-}|^{4}+\sum_{\alpha,\beta\geq 2}\Big(\sum_{i,j}|\bar{R}_{ij\alpha\beta}|^{2}+4|\bar{R}_{ij\alpha\beta}||A^{-}|^{2}\Big).

Now if
k=2
, inequality (
5.13
) is trivial since
|\hat{R}^{\perp}|^{2}=0
and
\sum_{i,j,p,q}|\langle A^{-}_{ij},A^{-}_{pq}\rangle|^{2}=|A^{-}|^{4}
. Otherwise, if
k\geq 3
, inequality (
5.17
) follows Lemma 3.3 in
[
19
]
. This completes the proof.
∎

Lemma 5.5

(Lower bound for the reaction terms of

,

\displaystyle 2\sum_{i,j}|\mathring{h}_{ij}A^{-}_{ij}|^{2}

(5.22)

Lemma 5.6

(Reaction term estimate)
.

If

\displaystyle\sum_{i,j,p,q}|\langle A^{-}_{ij},A^{-}_{pq}\rangle|^{2}+|\hat{R}^{\perp}|^{2}+\sum_{i,j}|R_{ij}^{\perp}(\nu_{1})|^{2}\leq(1-\delta)\frac{|A^{-}|^{2}}{f}\Big(c_{m}\sum_{i,j}\left|\left\langle A_{ij},H\right\rangle\right|^{2}

\displaystyle-\sum_{i,j,p,q}\left|\left\langle A_{ij},A_{pq}\right\rangle\right|^{2}-\sum_{i,j}|R_{ij}^{\perp}|^{2}\Big)+\Big(1+(1-\delta)\frac{|A^{-}|^{2}}{f}\Big)\sum_{\alpha,\beta\geq 2}\Big(\sum_{i,j}|\bar{R}_{ij\alpha\beta}|^{2}+4|\bar{R}_{ij\alpha\beta}||A^{-}|^{2}\Big)

\displaystyle+\Big(1+(1-\delta)\frac{2|A^{-}|^{2}}{f}\Big)\Big(\sum_{i,j}|\bar{R}_{ij}(\nu_{1})|^{2}+4|\bar{R}_{ij}(\nu_{1})||\mathring{h}||A^{-}|\Big)

\displaystyle+(1-\delta)\frac{|A^{-}|^{2}}{f}\Big(\frac{1}{mc_{m}-1}d_{m}\big(|A^{-}|^{2}+2f\big)+\frac{mc_{m}}{mc_{m}-1}|\mathring{h}|^{2}d_{m}\Big).

Proof.

In view of (
5.4
) and (
5.5
), we have

\displaystyle\sum_{i,j,p,q}|\langle A^{-}_{ij},A^{-}_{pq}\rangle|^{2}+|\hat{R}^{\perp}|^{2}+\sum_{i,j}|R_{ij}^{\perp}(\nu_{1})|^{2}-(1-\delta)\frac{|A^{-}|^{2}}{f}\Big(c_{m}\sum_{i,j}\left|\left\langle A_{ij},H\right\rangle\right|^{2}

\displaystyle-\sum_{i,j,p,q}\left|\left\langle A_{ij},A_{pq}\right\rangle\right|^{2}-\sum_{i,j}|R_{ij}^{\perp}|^{2}\Big)

\displaystyle\leq\frac{3}{2}|A^{-}|^{4}+2|\mathring{h}|^{2}|A^{-}|^{2}-\frac{2(1-\delta)}{mc_{m}-1}|A^{-}|^{4}-\frac{mc_{m}(1-\delta)}{mc_{m}-1}|\mathring{h}|^{2}|A^{-}|^{2}

\displaystyle+\sum_{\alpha,\beta\geq 2}\Big(\sum_{i,j}|\bar{R}_{ij\alpha\beta}|^{2}+4|\bar{R}_{ij\alpha\beta}||A^{-}|^{2}\Big)+\sum_{i,j}|\bar{R}_{ij}(\nu_{1})|^{2}+4|\bar{R}_{ij}(\nu_{1})||\mathring{h}||A^{-}|

\displaystyle+(1-\delta)\frac{|A^{-}|^{2}}{f}\Big(\sum_{\alpha,\beta\geq 2}\Big(\sum_{i,j}|\bar{R}_{ij\alpha\beta}|^{2}+4|\bar{R}_{ij\alpha\beta}||A^{-}|^{2}\Big)+2\sum_{i,j}|\bar{R}_{ij}(\nu_{1})|^{2}+8|\bar{R}_{ij}(\nu_{1})||\mathring{h}||A^{-}|\Big)

\displaystyle+(1-\delta)\frac{|A^{-}|^{2}}{f}\Big(\frac{1}{mc_{m}-1}d_{m}\big(|A^{-}|^{2}+2f\big)+\frac{mc_{m}}{mc_{m}-1}|\mathring{h}|^{2}d_{m}\Big)

\displaystyle=\left(\frac{3}{2}-\frac{2(1-\delta)}{mc_{m}-1}\right)|A^{-}|^{4}+\left(2-\frac{mc_{m}(1-\delta)}{mc_{m}-1}\right)|\mathring{h}|^{2}|A^{-}|^{2}

\displaystyle+\Big(1+(1-\delta)\frac{|A^{-}|^{2}}{f}\Big)\sum_{\alpha,\beta\geq 2}\Big(\sum_{i,j}|\bar{R}_{ij\alpha\beta}|^{2}+4|\bar{R}_{ij\alpha\beta}||A^{-}|^{2}\Big)

\displaystyle+\Big(1+(1-\delta)\frac{2|A^{-}|^{2}}{f}\Big)\Big(\sum_{i,j}|\bar{R}_{ij}(\nu_{1})|^{2}+4|\bar{R}_{ij}(\nu_{1})||\mathring{h}||A^{-}|\Big)

\displaystyle+(1-\delta)\frac{|A^{-}|^{2}}{f}\Big(\frac{1}{mc_{m}-1}d_{m}\big(|A^{-}|^{2}+2f\big)+\frac{mc_{m}}{mc_{m}-1}|\mathring{h}|^{2}d_{m}\Big).

If
c_{m}\leq\frac{1}{m-\tilde{k}}
and

, then

\displaystyle\frac{1}{mc_{m}-1}\geq 3,\quad\text{ and }\quad\frac{mc_{m}}{mc_{m}-1}\geq 4

Therefore, if

\displaystyle\frac{3}{2}-\frac{2(1-\delta)}{mc_{m}-1}\leq\frac{3}{2}-6(1-\delta)\leq 0,

\displaystyle 2-\frac{mc_{m}(1-\delta)}{mc_{m}-1}\leq 2-4(1-\delta)\leq 0,

which gives (

Lemma 5.7

(Lower bound for Bochner term of
\left(\partial_{t}-\Delta\right)|A^{-}|^{2}
)
.

If
\frac{1}{m}<c_{m}\leq\frac{1}{m-\tilde{k}}
, then

\displaystyle 2|\hat{\nabla}^{\perp}A^{-}|^{2}

\displaystyle\geq\left(\frac{7m-18}{9(m+2)\left(mc_{m}-1\right)}-2\right)|\mathring{h}|^{2}|\nabla^{\perp}\nu_{1}|^{2}

\displaystyle+\frac{7m-18}{9(m+2)\left(mc_{m}-1\right)}(|A^{-}|^{2}+f-d_{m})|\nabla^{\perp}\nu_{1}|^{2}.

Proof.

We begin by applying Young’s inequality

\displaystyle\sum_{i,j,k}|\hat{\nabla}_{i}^{\perp}A^{-}_{jk}+\mathring{h}_{jk}\nabla_{i}^{\perp}\nu_{1}|^{2}

\displaystyle=|\hat{\nabla}^{\perp}A^{-}|^{2}+2\sum_{i,j,k}\langle\hat{\nabla}_{i}^{\perp}A^{-}_{jk},\mathring{h}_{jk}\nabla_{i}^{\perp}\nu_{1}\rangle+|\mathring{h}|^{2}|\nabla^{\perp}\nu_{1}|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq 2|\hat{\nabla}^{\perp}A^{-}|^{2}+2|\mathring{h}|^{2}|\nabla^{\perp}\nu_{1}|^{2}.

Multiplying both sides of (
5.19
) by
\frac{7m-18}{9(m+2)(mc_{m}-1)}
gives

\displaystyle\frac{7m-18}{9m(m+2)}|H|^{2}=\frac{7m-18}{9(m+2)\left(mc_{m}-1\right)}\left(f+|A^{-}|^{2}+|\mathring{h}|^{2}-d_{m}\right).

In view of (
5.29
), our observations give us that

\displaystyle\frac{7m-18}{9(m+2)\left(mc_{m}-1\right)}\left(f+|A^{-}|^{2}+|\mathring{h}|^{2}-d_{m}\right)|\nabla^{\perp}\nu_{1}|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq 2|\hat{\nabla}^{\perp}A^{-}|^{2}+2|\mathring{h}|^{2}|\nabla^{\perp}\nu_{1}|^{2}.

Subtracting the

\displaystyle\frac{7m-18}{9(m+2)\left(mc_{m}-1\right)}(f+|A^{-}|^{2}

\displaystyle-d_{m})|\nabla^{\perp}\nu_{1}|^{2}+\left(\frac{7m-18}{9(m+2)\left(mc_{m}-1\right)}-2\right)|\mathring{h}|^{2}|\nabla^{\perp}\nu_{1}|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq 2|\hat{\nabla}^{\perp}A^{-}|^{2},

(5.30)

which is the estimate of the lemma.
∎

Lemma 5.8

(Lower bound for Bochner term of
\left(\partial_{t}-\Delta\right)f

.

If

\displaystyle 2\frac{|A^{-}|^{2}}{f}(|\nabla^{\perp}A|^{2}-c_{m}|\nabla^{\perp}H|^{2})

\displaystyle\geq\left(2-\frac{18(m+2)\left(mc_{m}-1\right)}{7m-18}\right)\frac{|A^{-}|^{2}}{f}\sum_{i,j,k}|\langle\nabla_{i}^{\perp}\mathring{A}_{jk},\nu_{1}\rangle|^{2}

\displaystyle+\frac{2m}{mc_{m}-1}\left(\frac{16}{9(m+2)}-c_{m}\right)|A^{-}|^{2}|\nabla^{\perp}\nu_{1}|^{2}

\displaystyle+\frac{2m}{mc_{m}-1}\left(c_{m}-\frac{16}{9(m+2)}\right)\frac{|A^{-}|^{2}}{f}d_{m}|\nabla^{\perp}\nu_{1}|^{2}.

Proof.

Using (
3.1
) and (
3.2
), we have

\displaystyle|\nabla^{\perp}A|^{2}-c_{m}|\nabla^{\perp}H|^{2}

\displaystyle=\sum_{i,j,k}|\langle\nabla_{i}^{\perp}A^{-}_{jk},\nu_{1}\rangle+\nabla_{i}h_{jk}|^{2}-c_{m}|\nabla|H\|^{2}

\displaystyle+\sum_{i,j,k}|\hat{\nabla}_{i}^{\perp}A^{-}_{jk}+h_{jk}\nabla_{i}^{\perp}\nu_{1}|^{2}-c_{m}|H|^{2}|\nabla^{\perp}\nu_{1}|^{2}.

Note that by (
5.24

5.26
) and (
5.28

\displaystyle\sum_{i,j,k}|\langle\nabla_{i}^{\perp}A^{-}_{jk},\nu_{1}\rangle+\nabla_{i}h_{jk}|^{2}-c_{m}|\nabla|H||^{2}

\displaystyle=\sum_{i,j,k}|\langle\nabla_{i}^{\perp}\mathring{A}_{jk},\nu_{1}\rangle|^{2}-\frac{mc_{m}-1}{m}|\nabla|H||^{2}

\displaystyle\geq\left(1-\frac{9(m+2)\left(mc_{m}-1\right)}{7m-18}\right)\sum_{i,j,k}|\langle\nabla_{i}^{\perp}\mathring{A}_{jk},\nu_{1}\rangle|^{2}.

Lemma 5.9

(Upper bound for gradient term of
\left(\partial_{t}-\Delta\right)|A^{-}|^{2}
)
.

If
\frac{1}{m}<c_{m}\leq\frac{1}{m-\tilde{k}}
, then

\displaystyle 4\sum_{i,j,k}Q_{ijk}\langle A^{-}_{ij},\nabla_{k}^{\perp}\nu_{1}\rangle

\displaystyle\leq 2|\langle\nabla^{\perp}A^{-},\nu_{1}\rangle|^{2}+\left(2a_{2}+2a_{3}\frac{9\tilde{k}(m+2)}{(7m-18)(m-\tilde{k})}\right)\frac{|A^{-}|^{2}}{f}|\langle\nabla^{\perp}\mathring{A},\nu_{1}\rangle|^{2}

\displaystyle+2|A^{-}|^{2}|\nabla^{\perp}\nu_{1}|^{2}+\frac{2}{a_{2}}f|\nabla^{\perp}\nu_{1}|^{2}+\frac{2}{a_{3}}|\mathring{h}|^{2}|\nabla^{\perp}\nu_{1}|^{2}.

Proof.

Using the definition of
Q_{ijk}
, we get

\displaystyle|Q|\leq|\langle\nabla^{\perp}\mathring{A},\nu_{1}\rangle|+|\langle\nabla^{\perp}A^{-},\nu_{1}\rangle|+|H|^{-1}|\mathring{h}||\nabla|H||.

(5.31)

It easily follows from the definition of

\displaystyle f\leq\left(c_{m}-\frac{1}{m}\right)|H|^{2}\leq\frac{\tilde{k}}{m(m-\tilde{k})}|H|^{2}.

Consequently, using (
5.28
), we obtain

\displaystyle\frac{|A^{-}|^{2}}{|H|^{2}}|\nabla|H||^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{9m(m+2)}{7m-18}\frac{\tilde{k}}{m(m-\tilde{k})}\frac{|A^{-}|^{2}}{f}|\langle\nabla^{\perp}\mathring{A},\nu_{1}\rangle|^{2}

\displaystyle=\frac{9\tilde{k}(m+2)}{(7m-18)(m-\tilde{k})}\frac{|A^{-}|^{2}}{f}|\langle\nabla^{\perp}\mathring{A},\nu_{1}\rangle|^{2}.

(5.32)

Then

\displaystyle|\langle A^{-},\nabla^{\bot}\nu_{1}\rangle|^{2}=\sum_{i,j}\langle A^{-}_{ij},\nabla^{\bot}_{i}\nu_{1}\rangle^{2}\leq\sum_{i,j,k}\sum_{\beta\geq 2}(A^{\beta}_{ij})^{2}\langle\nabla^{\bot}_{k}\nu_{1},\nu_{\beta}\rangle^{2}

and (
5.31
) give

\displaystyle 4\sum_{i,j,k}Q_{ijk}\langle A^{-}_{ij},\nabla_{k}^{\perp}\nu_{1}\rangle

\displaystyle\leq 4|Q||\langle A^{-},\nabla^{\perp}\nu_{1}\rangle|

\displaystyle\leq 4\left(|\langle\nabla^{\perp}\mathring{A},\nu_{1}\rangle|+|\langle\nabla^{\perp}A^{-},\nu_{1}\rangle|+|H|^{-1}|\mathring{h}||\nabla|H||\right)|A^{-}||\nabla^{\perp}\nu_{1}|.

Now to each of these three summed terms above we apply Young’s inequality with constants
a_{1},a_{2},a_{3}>0
. Specifically, we have

\displaystyle 4|\langle\nabla^{\perp}A^{-},\nu_{1}\rangle||A^{-}||\nabla^{\perp}\nu_{1}|

\displaystyle\leq 2a_{1}|\langle\nabla^{\perp}A^{-},\nu_{1}\rangle|^{2}+\frac{2}{a_{1}}|A^{-}|^{2}|\nabla^{\perp}\nu_{1}|^{2},

\displaystyle 4|\langle\nabla^{\perp}\mathring{A},\nu_{1}\rangle||A^{-}||\nabla^{\perp}\nu_{1}|

\displaystyle=4|\langle\nabla^{\perp}\mathring{A},\nu_{1}\rangle|\frac{|A^{-}|}{\sqrt{f}}f^{\frac{1}{2}}|\nabla^{\perp}\nu_{1}|

\displaystyle\leq 2a_{2}\frac{|A^{-}|^{2}}{f}|\langle\nabla^{\perp}\mathring{A},\nu_{1}\rangle|^{2}+\frac{2}{a_{2}}f|\nabla^{\perp}\nu_{1}|^{2},

\displaystyle 4|H|^{-1}|\mathring{h}||\nabla|H|||A^{-}||\nabla^{\perp}\nu_{1}|

\displaystyle\leq 2a_{3}\frac{|A^{-}|^{2}}{|H|^{2}}\left|\nabla|H|\right|^{2}+\frac{2}{a_{3}}|\mathring{h}|^{2}|\nabla^{\perp}\nu_{1}|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq 2a_{3}\frac{9\tilde{k}(m+2)}{(7m-18)(m-\tilde{k})}\frac{|A^{-}|^{2}}{f}|\langle\nabla^{\perp}\mathring{A},\nu_{1}\rangle|^{2}+\frac{2}{a_{3}}|\mathring{h}|^{2}|\nabla^{\perp}\nu_{1}|^{2}.

Note we used (
5.1
) in the last inequality. Hence

\displaystyle 4\sum_{i,j,k}Q_{ijk}\langle A^{-}_{ij},\nabla_{k}^{\perp}\nu_{1}\rangle

\displaystyle\leq 2a_{1}|\langle\nabla^{\perp}A^{-},\nu_{1}\rangle|^{2}+\left(2a_{2}+2a_{3}\frac{9\tilde{k}(m+2)}{(7m-18)(m-\tilde{k})}\right)\frac{|A^{-}|^{2}}{f}|\langle\nabla^{\perp}\mathring{A},\nu_{1}\rangle|^{2}

\displaystyle+\frac{2}{a_{1}}|A^{-}|^{2}|\nabla^{\perp}\nu_{1}|^{2}+\frac{2}{a_{2}}f|\nabla^{\perp}\nu_{1}|^{2}+\frac{2}{a_{3}}|\mathring{h}|^{2}|\nabla^{\perp}\nu_{1}|^{2}.

(5.33)

Setting
\alpha_{1}=1
and keeping
\alpha_{2}
and
\alpha_{3}
as they are for now, we get the desired result.
∎

Lemma 5.10

(Gradient term estimate)
.

\frac{1}{m}<c_{m}\leq\frac{1}{m-\tilde{k}}

\frac{1}{m}<c_{m}\leq\frac{1}{m-\tilde{k}}

3.2

5.8

(1-\delta)

Putting these together, we get

\displaystyle 2|\nabla^{\perp}A^{-}|^{2}

\displaystyle+2(1-\delta)\frac{|A^{-}|^{2}}{f}(|\nabla^{\perp}A|^{2}-c_{m}|\nabla^{\perp}H|^{2})\geq 2|\langle\nabla^{\perp}A^{-},\nu_{1}\rangle|^{2}

\displaystyle+\left(\frac{7m-18}{9(m+2)\left(mc_{m}-1\right)}-2\right)|\mathring{h}|^{2}|\nabla^{\perp}\nu_{1}|^{2}

\displaystyle+\frac{7m-18}{9(m+2)\left(mc_{m}-1\right)}(|A^{-}|^{2}+f-d_{m})|\nabla^{\perp}\nu_{1}|^{2}

\displaystyle+(1-\delta)\left(2-\frac{18(m+2)\left(mc_{m}-1\right)}{7m-18}\right)\frac{|A^{-}|^{2}}{f}\sum_{i,j,k}|\langle\nabla_{i}^{\perp}\mathring{A}_{jk},\nu_{1}\rangle|^{2}

\displaystyle+(1-\delta)\frac{2m}{mc_{m}-1}\left(c_{m}-\frac{16}{9(m+2)}\right)\frac{|A^{-}|^{2}}{f}d_{m}|\nabla^{\perp}\nu_{1}|^{2}.

On the other hand, the first result of Lemma

\displaystyle 4\sum_{i,j,k}Q_{ijk}\langle A^{-}_{ij},\nabla_{k}^{\perp}\nu_{1}\rangle

\displaystyle|A^{-}|^{2}\leq\varepsilon f+C_{\varepsilon}\quad\text{and}\quad|A|^{2}-\frac{1}{m-\tilde{k}+1}|H|^{2}\leq\varepsilon f+C_{\varepsilon},

\forall t\in[0,T)
, where
C_{\varepsilon}=C_{\varepsilon}(n,m)
. At the first singular time, the only possible blow-up limits are codimension one shrinking round spheres, shrinking round cylinders, and translating bowl solitons. Therefore, we can classify these blowup limits as follows:

Corollary 7.2

(cf.
[
29
]
, Corollary 7.2, cf.
[
14
]
, Corollary 4.7)
.

Let
c_{m}=\frac{1}{m-\tilde{k}},\tilde{k}\geq 1
. Suppose
F_{t}\colon\mathcal{M}^{m}\rightarrow\mathbb{C}P^{n}
is a smooth solution of the mean curvature flow, compact and quadratically pinched with positive mean curvature on the maximal time interval
[0,T)
.

1.

If the singularity for
t\rightarrow T
is of type I, the only possible limiting flows under the rescaling procedure as in
[
14
]
, are the homothetically shrinking solutions associated with
\mathbb{S}^{m},\mathbb{R}^{\tilde{k}-1}\times\mathbb{S}^{m-\tilde{k}+1}
.

2.

If the singularity is of type II, then from Theorem
5.11
, the only possible blow-up limits at the first singular time are codimension one shrinking round spheres, shrinking round cylinders, and translating bowl solitons.

8
The case of infinite maximal time

In this section, we assume
T=\infty
. We primarily follow
[
17
]
. In the following result, the improvement of pinching can be obtained directly from the maximum principle, compared to the case of
T<\infty
.

Proposition 8.1
.

Let
F:\mathcal{M}^{m}\times[0,T)\rightarrow\mathbb{C}P^{n}
be a smooth solution to MCF so that
F_{0}(p)=F(p,0)
is compact and quadratically pinched.
Then, there exists a positive constant
\mathcal{C}=\mathcal{C}(m,\tilde{k},d_{m})
, depending only on the initial manifold
M_{0}
, such that

\displaystyle\frac{|\mathring{A}|^{2}}{f}\leq\mathcal{C}e^{-2d_{m}t},

for any
0\leq t<T=\infty
.

Proof.

Consider the following functions

\displaystyle Q:=-\frac{f}{2},

\displaystyle q:=\frac{1}{2}(|A|^{2}-\frac{1}{m}|H|^{2}),

where recall
f:=-|A|^{2}+c_{m}|H|^{2}+d_{m}
,
\frac{1}{m}\leq c_{m}\leq\frac{1}{m-\tilde{k}}
and
d_{m}=\frac{\tilde{k}}{m}(m-3-4k),\tilde{k}\geq 1
. In the case where
H\neq 0
, from (
2.6
), (
2.2
) and as we did in previous chapters in (
3.10
) and (
3.15
), the evolution equation of
Q
becomes

\displaystyle\left(\partial_{t}-\Delta\right)Q

\displaystyle=-(|\nabla A|^{2}-c_{m}|\nabla H|^{2})+R_{1}-c_{m}R_{2}+\frac{1}{2}P_{\alpha}

\displaystyle\leq-(|\nabla A|^{2}-c_{m}|\nabla H|^{2})+\sum_{\alpha,\beta}\big(\sum_{i,j}A^{\alpha}_{ij}A_{ij}^{\beta}\big)^{2}+\sum_{i,j,\alpha,\beta}\Big(\sum_{p}\big(A^{\alpha}_{ip}A_{jp}^{\beta}-A^{\alpha}_{jp}A_{ip}^{\beta}\big)\Big)^{2}

\displaystyle-c_{m}\sum_{i,j}\big(\sum_{\alpha}H^{\alpha}A^{\alpha}_{ij}\big)^{2}-(m-3-4k)|\mathring{A}|^{2}.

At a point where
H\neq 0
, decomposing
A
into its irreducible components according to
[
24
]
,
[
3
]
,
[
17
]
and
[
19
]
, we have

\displaystyle|A|^{2}=|\mathring{h}|^{2}+\frac{1}{m}|H|^{2}+|A^{-}|^{2},

\displaystyle\sum_{i,j}\big(\sum_{\alpha}H^{\alpha}A^{\alpha}_{ij}\big)^{2}=|\mathring{h}|^{2}|H|^{2}+\frac{1}{m}|H|^{4},

\displaystyle\sum_{\alpha,\beta}\big(\sum_{i,j}A^{\alpha}_{ij}A_{ij}^{\beta}\big)^{2}+\sum_{i,j,\alpha,\beta}\Big(\sum_{p}\big(A^{\alpha}_{ip}A_{jp}^{\beta}-A^{\alpha}_{jp}A_{ip}^{\beta}\big)\Big)^{2}\leq 3|\mathring{h}|^{2}|A^{-}|^{2}

\displaystyle+\frac{3}{2}|A^{-}|^{4}+\left(|\mathring{h}|^{2}+\frac{1}{m}|H|^{2}\right)|A|^{2}-\frac{1}{m}|A^{-}|^{2}|H|^{2}.

Therefore, we arrive at

\displaystyle\left(\partial_{t}-\Delta\right)Q

\displaystyle\leq 3|\mathring{h}|^{2}|A^{-}|^{2}+\frac{3}{2}|A^{-}|^{4}+\left(|\mathring{h}|^{2}+\frac{1}{m}|H|^{2}\right)|A|^{2}-\frac{1}{m}|A^{-}|^{2}|H|^{2}

\displaystyle-c_{m}\left(|\mathring{h}|^{2}+\frac{1}{m}|H|^{2}\right)|H|^{2}-(|\nabla A|^{2}-c_{m}|\nabla H|^{2})-(m-3-4k)|\mathring{A}|^{2}

\displaystyle=3|\mathring{h}|^{2}|A^{-}|^{2}+\frac{3}{2}|A^{-}|^{4}-\frac{1}{m}|A^{-}|^{2}|H|^{2}+\left(|\mathring{h}|^{2}+\frac{1}{m}|H|^{2}\right)(2Q+d_{m})

\displaystyle-(m-3-4k)(|\mathring{h}|^{2}+|A^{-}|^{2})-(|\nabla A|^{2}-c_{m}|\nabla H|^{2})

\displaystyle=\left(3|\mathring{h}|^{2}+\frac{3}{2}|A^{-}|^{2}-\frac{1}{m}|H|^{2}-d_{m}\right)|A^{-}|^{2}+d_{m}\left(|\mathring{h}|^{2}+|A^{-}|^{2}+\frac{1}{m}|H|^{2}\right)

\displaystyle-(m-3-4k)(|\mathring{h}|^{2}+|A^{-}|^{2})+2Q\left(|\mathring{h}|^{2}+\frac{1}{m}|H|^{2}\right)-(|\nabla A|^{2}-c_{m}|\nabla H|^{2}).

Substituting
|A|^{2}=2Q+c_{m}|H|^{2}+d_{m}
, we obtain

\displaystyle d_{m}\left(|\mathring{h}|^{2}+|A^{-}|^{2}+\frac{1}{m}|H|^{2}\right)=d_{m}(2Q+c_{m}|H|^{2}+d_{m})

and

\displaystyle-(m-3-4k)|\mathring{A}|^{2}

\displaystyle=-(m-3-4k)(|\mathring{h}|^{2}+|A^{-}|^{2})

\displaystyle=-(m-3-4k)\left(2Q+\left(c_{m}-\frac{1}{m}\right)|H|^{2}+d_{m}\right)

and hence

\displaystyle d_{m}\left(|\mathring{h}|^{2}+|A^{-}|^{2}+\frac{1}{m}|H|^{2}\right)-(m-3-4k)(|\mathring{h}|^{2}+|A^{-}|^{2})

\displaystyle=Q\left(1-\frac{m}{\tilde{k}}\right)2d_{m}+|H|^{2}\left(c_{m}-\frac{m}{\tilde{k}}\left(c_{m}-\frac{1}{m}\right)\right)d_{m}+d_{m}^{2}\left(1-\frac{m}{\tilde{k}}\right).

Also, writing

\displaystyle\frac{1}{m}|H|^{2}=\frac{1}{mc_{m}-1}(|\mathring{h}|^{2}+|A^{-}|^{2}-2Q-d_{m}),

we find

\displaystyle 3|\mathring{h}|^{2}

\displaystyle+\frac{3}{2}|A^{-}|^{2}-\frac{1}{m}|H|^{2}-d_{m}=3|\mathring{h}|^{2}+\frac{3}{2}|A^{-}|^{2}-\frac{1}{mc_{m}-1}(|\mathring{h}|^{2}+|A^{-}|^{2}-2Q-d_{m})-d_{m}

\displaystyle=\left(3-\frac{1}{mc_{m}-1}\right)|\mathring{h}|^{2}+\left(\frac{3}{2}-\frac{1}{mc_{m}-1}\right)|A^{-}|^{2}+\frac{2}{mc_{m}-1}Q-\left(1-\frac{1}{mc_{m}-1}\right)d_{m}.

For
c_{m}\leq\frac{1}{m-\tilde{k}}
, the first term on the right hand side is non positive, since
m\geq 4\tilde{k}
, an inequality also used in Proposition 3.1 in
[
17
]
and the term
-(|\nabla A|^{2}-c_{m}|\nabla H|^{2})
becomes
-\left(1-\frac{9(m+2)}{16}c\right)|\nabla A|^{2}
for
1-\frac{9(m+2)}{16}c>0
, from Theorem
4.1
. Disregarding these terms and putting things back together we conclude that

\displaystyle\left(\partial_{t}-\Delta\right)Q

\displaystyle\leq\left(\frac{3}{2}-\frac{1}{mc_{m}-1}\right)|A^{-}|^{4}-\left(1-\frac{1}{mc_{m}-1}\right)d_{m}|A^{-}|^{2}+\left(1-\frac{m}{\tilde{k}}\right)d_{m}^{2}

\displaystyle+2Q\left(|\mathring{h}|^{2}+\frac{1}{mc_{m}-1}|A^{-}|^{2}+\frac{1}{m}|H|^{2}+d_{m}\left(1-\frac{m}{\tilde{k}}\right)\right)

\displaystyle+d_{m}|H|^{2}\left(c_{m}-\frac{m}{\tilde{k}}\left(c_{m}-\frac{1}{m}\right)\right)-\left(1-\frac{9(m+2)}{16}c\right)|\nabla A|^{2}.

The term
d_{m}|H|^{2}\left(c_{m}-\frac{m}{\tilde{k}}\left(c_{m}-\frac{1}{m}\right)\right)
is negative. Indeed,

\displaystyle c_{m}-\frac{m}{\tilde{k}}\left(c_{m}-\frac{1}{m}\right)

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{m-\tilde{k}}-\frac{m}{\tilde{k}}\frac{1}{m}-\frac{1}{\tilde{k}}=\frac{-2m+3\tilde{k}}{\tilde{k}(m-\tilde{k})}<0,

since
m\geq 4\tilde{k}
. Also, the discriminant of the polynomial

\displaystyle\left(\frac{3}{2}-\frac{1}{mc_{m}-1}\right)|A^{-}|^{4}-\left(1-\frac{1}{mc_{m}-1}\right)d_{m}|A^{-}|^{2}+\left(1-\frac{m}{\tilde{k}}\right)d_{m}^{2}

is negative. Indeed,

\displaystyle D

\displaystyle=\left(1-\frac{1}{mc_{m}-1}\right)^{2}-4\left(\frac{3}{2}-\frac{1}{mc_{m}-1}\right)\left(1-\frac{m}{\tilde{k}}\right)

\displaystyle=-5+\frac{2}{mc_{m}-1}+\frac{1}{(mc_{m}-1)^{2}}-\frac{4m}{\tilde{k}(mc_{m}-1)}

\displaystyle=-\frac{3m^{2}}{\tilde{k}^{2}}+\frac{4m}{\tilde{k}}-6

\displaystyle<0,

for all
\tilde{k}\geq 1
and
c_{m}=\frac{1}{m-\tilde{k}}
, so we can disregard these terms. Therefore, whenever
H\neq 0
, the evolution equation of
Q
, becomes

\displaystyle\left(\partial_{t}-\Delta\right)Q

\displaystyle<2Q\left(|\mathring{h}|^{2}+\frac{1}{mc_{m}-1}|A^{-}|^{2}+\frac{1}{m}|H|^{2}+d_{m}\left(1-\frac{m}{\tilde{k}}\right)\right)

\displaystyle-\left(1-\frac{9(m+2)}{16}c\right)|\nabla A|^{2},

for
1-\frac{9(m+2)}{16}c>0
, from Theorem
4.1
. For the evolution equation of
q
, we have

\displaystyle\left(\partial_{t}-\Delta\right)q

\displaystyle=\left(3|\mathring{h}|^{2}+\frac{3}{2}|A^{-}|^{2}-\frac{1}{m}|H|^{2}\right)|A^{-}|^{2}-(m-3-4k)(|\mathring{h}|^{2}+|A^{-}|^{2})

\displaystyle-\left(|\nabla A|^{2}-\frac{1}{m}|\nabla H|^{2}\right)+2q\left(|\mathring{h}|^{2}+\frac{1}{m}|H|^{2}\right).

But
|\mathring{h}|^{2}+|A^{-}|^{2}=|\mathring{A}|^{2}=2q
, and also

\displaystyle 3|\mathring{h}|^{2}+\frac{3}{2}|A^{-}|^{2}-\frac{1}{m}|H|^{2}

\displaystyle=\left(3-\frac{1}{mc_{m}-1}\right)|\mathring{h}|^{2}+\left(\frac{3}{2}-\frac{1}{mc_{m}-1}\right)|A^{-}|^{2}-\frac{1}{m}|H|^{2}

\displaystyle+\frac{2}{mc_{m}-1}q.

The first three terms of the above equality are non-positive. Also, from the Kato inequality, we arrive at

\displaystyle\left(\partial_{t}-\Delta\right)q

\displaystyle\leq 2q\left(|\mathring{h}|^{2}+\frac{1}{mc_{m}-1}|A^{-}|^{2}+\frac{1}{m}|H|^{2}-\frac{m}{\tilde{k}}d_{m}\right).

Finally, for the evolution equation of
\frac{\left(\partial_{t}-\Delta\right)\frac{q}{Q}}{\frac{q}{Q}}
, we have the following computation

\displaystyle\frac{\left(\partial_{t}-\Delta\right)\frac{q}{Q}}{\frac{q}{Q}}

\displaystyle=\frac{\left(\partial_{t}-\Delta\right)q}{q}-\frac{\left(\partial_{t}-\Delta\right)Q}{Q}+2\left\langle\nabla\log\frac{q}{Q},\nabla\log Q\right\rangle

\displaystyle\leq 2\left(|\mathring{h}|^{2}+\frac{1}{mc_{m}-1}|A^{-}|^{2}+\frac{1}{m}|H|^{2}-\frac{m}{\tilde{k}}d_{m}\right)-\left(1-\frac{9(m+2)}{16}c\right)\frac{|\nabla A|^{2}}{Q}

\displaystyle-2\left(|\mathring{h}|^{2}+\frac{1}{mc_{m}-1}|A^{-}|^{2}+\frac{1}{m}|H|^{2}+d_{m}\left(1-\frac{m}{\tilde{k}}\right)\right)+2\left\langle\nabla\log\frac{q}{Q},\nabla\log Q\right\rangle

\displaystyle=-2d_{m}-\left(1-\frac{9(m+2)}{16}c\right)\frac{|\nabla A|^{2}}{Q}+2\left\langle\nabla\log\frac{q}{Q},\nabla\log Q\right\rangle.

where
1-\frac{9(m+2)}{16}c>0
, wherever
H\neq 0
. In the case of
H=0
, in the same way, we have

\displaystyle\left(\partial_{t}-\Delta\right)Q

\displaystyle\leq 2Q\left(|\mathring{h}|^{2}+\frac{1}{mc_{m}-1}|A^{-}|^{2}+d_{m}\left(1-\frac{m}{\tilde{k}}\right)\right)-\left(1-\frac{9(m+2)}{16}c\right)|\nabla A|^{2}

and

\displaystyle\left(\partial_{t}-\Delta\right)q

\displaystyle\leq 2q\left(|\mathring{h}|^{2}+\frac{1}{mc_{m}-1}|A^{-}|^{2}-\frac{m}{\tilde{k}}d_{m}\right).

Therefore, for the evolution equation of
\frac{\left(\partial_{t}-\Delta\right)\frac{q}{Q}}{\frac{q}{Q}}
, we have the following computation

\displaystyle\frac{\left(\partial_{t}-\Delta\right)\frac{q}{Q}}{\frac{q}{Q}}

\displaystyle=\frac{\left(\partial_{t}-\Delta\right)q}{q}-\frac{\left(\partial_{t}-\Delta\right)Q}{Q}+2\left\langle\nabla\log\frac{q}{Q},\nabla\log Q\right\rangle

\displaystyle\leq 2\left(|\mathring{h}|^{2}+\frac{1}{mc_{m}-1}|A^{-}|^{2}-\frac{m}{\tilde{k}}d_{m}\right)-\left(1-\frac{9(m+2)}{16}c\right)\frac{|\nabla A|^{2}}{Q}

\displaystyle-2\left(|\mathring{h}|^{2}+\frac{1}{mc_{m}-1}|A^{-}|^{2}+d_{m}\left(1-\frac{m}{\tilde{k}}\right)\right)+2\left\langle\nabla\log\frac{q}{Q},\nabla\log Q\right\rangle

\displaystyle=-2d_{m}-\left(1-\frac{9(m+2)}{16}c\right)\frac{|\nabla A|^{2}}{Q}+2\left\langle\nabla\log\frac{q}{Q},\nabla\log Q\right\rangle,

which is to the same evolution equation as in the case
H\neq 0
. Hence, by the strong maximum principle there exists this constant
\mathcal{C}
depending upon
\tilde{k},m
and
d_{m}
, such that

\displaystyle\frac{q}{Q}\leq\mathcal{C}e^{-2d_{m}t},

which completes the proof.
∎

Proposition
8.1
implies, that there exists
\tau=\tau(m,\tilde{k})
such that the inequality

\displaystyle|A|^{2}-\frac{1}{m-1}|H|^{2}-\frac{m-3-4k}{m}<0

holds for every
t\in(0,T)\cap\left[\tau d_{m}^{-1},+\infty\right)
on any solution initially satisfying (
3.9
). Therefore, for all
\tilde{k}\geq 1
, we have

\displaystyle|A|^{2}-\frac{1}{m-\tilde{k}}|H|^{2}-\frac{\tilde{k}}{m}(m-3-4k)<0.

If
T>\tau d_{m}^{-1}
, this means that at time
\tau d_{m}^{-1}
the solution satisfies the hypotheses of Theorem 1.1 in
[
31
]
. Consequently, the solution either exists forever and converges to a totally geodesic submanifold as
t\rightarrow\infty
, or else contracts to codimension one solution in finite time, from Theorem
5.11
.
