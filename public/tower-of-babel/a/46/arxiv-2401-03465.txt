Degeneration of families of projective hypersurfaces and Hodge conjecture
Johann Bouali

Abstract
We prove by induction on dimension the Hodge conjecture for smooth complex projective varieties. Let X X be a smooth complex projective variety. Then X X is birational to a possibly singular projective hypersurface, hence to a smooth projective variety E 0 E_{0} which is a component of a normal crossing divisor E = ∪ i = 0 r E i ⊂ Y E=\cup_{i=0}^{r}E_{i}\subset Y which is the singular fiber of a pencil f : Y → 𝔸 1 f:Y\to\mathbb{A}^{1} of smooth projective hypersurfaces. Using the smooth hypersurface case ( [ 5 ] theorem 1), the nearby cycle functor on mixed Hodge module with rational de Rham factor and the induction hypothesis, we prove that a Hodge class of E 0 E_{0} is absolute Hodge, more precisely the locus of Hodge classes inside the algebraic vector bundle given the De Rham cohomology of the rational deformation of E 0 E_{0} is defined over ℚ \mathbb{Q} . By [ 4 ] theorem 4, we get the Hodge conjecture for E 0 E_{0} . By the induction hypothesis we also have the Hodge conjecture for X X .
