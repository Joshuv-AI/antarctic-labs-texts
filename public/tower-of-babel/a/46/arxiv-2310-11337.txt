On the Northcott property for infinite extensions
Martin Widmer

Abstract
. We start with a brief survey on the Northcott property for subfields of the algebraic numbers ℚ ¯ \overline{\mathbb{\mathchar 29009}} . Then we introduce a new criterion for its validity (refining the author’s previous criterion), addressing a problem of Bombieri. We show that Bombieri and Zannier’s theorem, stating that the maximal abelian extension of a number field K \mathchar 29003 contained in K ( d ) \mathchar 29003^{\delimiter 67273472\mathchar 29028\delimiter 84054785} has the Northcott property, follows very easily from this refined criterion. Here K ( d ) \mathchar 29003^{\delimiter 67273472\mathchar 29028\delimiter 84054785} denotes the composite field of all extensions of K \mathchar 29003 of degree at most d \mathchar 29028 .
