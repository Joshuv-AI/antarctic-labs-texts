\pi^{-1}{{\hat{\gamma\mkern 3.0mu}\mkern-3.0mu}{}}(\gamma^{-1}(j))=-k
and
\pi^{-1}{{\hat{\gamma\mkern 3.0mu}\mkern-3.0mu}{}}(\gamma^{-1}(k))=-j
we have that
-\gamma^{-1}(k)
and
\gamma^{-1}(j)
are in the same cycle of
K(\pi)
and
\gamma^{-1}(k)
and
-\gamma^{-1}(j)
are in the same cycle of
K(\pi)
; but not in the same cycle as
-\gamma^{-1}(k)
and
\gamma^{-1}(j)
. Hence

\#((-\gamma^{-1}(k),\gamma^{-1}(j))\cdot K(\pi)\cdot(-\gamma^{-1}(j),\gamma^{-1}(k)))=\#(K(\pi))+2.

Thus

\#(\pi)+\#(\pi^{-1}{{\hat{\gamma\mkern 3.0mu}\mkern-3.0mu}{}})=\#(\pi)+\#(K(\pi))+2=2n+2.

Let
\langle\pi,{{\hat{\gamma\mkern 3.0mu}\mkern-3.0mu}{}}\rangle
be the subgroup generated by
\pi
and
{{\hat{\gamma\mkern 3.0mu}\mkern-3.0mu}{}}
. If
\langle\pi,{{\hat{\gamma\mkern 3.0mu}\mkern-3.0mu}{}}\rangle
acts transitively on
[\pm n]
then there is an integer
g\geq 0
such that

\#(\pi)+\#(\pi^{-1}{{\hat{\gamma\mkern 3.0mu}\mkern-3.0mu}{}})+\#({{\hat{\gamma\mkern 3.0mu}\mkern-3.0mu}{}})=2n+2(1-g).

Then we would have

2m+2=\#(\pi)+\#(\pi^{-1}{{\hat{\gamma\mkern 3.0mu}\mkern-3.0mu}{}})=2n-2g,

which is impossible. Hence
\langle\pi,{{\hat{\gamma\mkern 3.0mu}\mkern-3.0mu}{}}\rangle
does not act transitively on
[\pm n]
, this means that no cycle of
\pi
meets both of the cycles of
{{\hat{\gamma\mkern 3.0mu}\mkern-3.0mu}{}}
. Hence each cycle of
\pi
is contained in one of the cycles of
{{\hat{\gamma\mkern 3.0mu}\mkern-3.0mu}{}}
.

\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(n-1)m_{n-1}z^{n}

\displaystyle=z^{2}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}m_{n-1}(n-1)z^{n-2}

\displaystyle=z^{2}\frac{d}{dz}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}m_{n-1}z^{n-1}=z^{2}M^{\prime}(z).

Next, observe that

\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\sum_{k=1}^{n-1}m_{k-1}\mskip 1.5mu\overline{\mskip-1.5mu{m}\mskip-1.5mu}\mskip 1.5mu^{\prime}_{n-k}z^{n}=z\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\sum_{n=k+1}^{\infty}m_{k-1}z^{k-1}\mskip 1.5mu\overline{\mskip-1.5mu{m}\mskip-1.5mu}\mskip 1.5mu^{\prime}_{n-k}z^{n-k}

\displaystyle=z\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}m_{k-1}z^{k-1}\sum_{n=k+1}^{\infty}\mskip 1.5mu\overline{\mskip-1.5mu{m}\mskip-1.5mu}\mskip 1.5mu^{\prime}_{n-k}z^{n-k}=zM(z)\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\mskip 1.5mu\overline{\mskip-1.5mu{m}\mskip-1.5mu}\mskip 1.5mu^{\prime}_{n}z^{n}=zm(z)M(z).

This gives us the first claimed equality. Now we have

so

is analytic on

. As

Corollary 36
.

For
n\geq 1

\mskip 1.5mu\overline{\mskip-1.5mu{m}\mskip-1.5mu}\mskip 1.5mu^{\prime}_{n}=4^{n-1}-\frac{1}{2}\binom{2n}{n}.

Proof.

We have
