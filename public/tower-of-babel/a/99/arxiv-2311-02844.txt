Introduction

Let
(\mathcal{M},g)
be a smooth compact Riemannian manifold of dimension
N\geq 8
, where
g
denotes the metric tensor. We consider the following elliptic system

\displaystyle\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}-\Delta_{g}u+h(x)u=v^{p-\alpha\varepsilon},&\mbox{in}\ \mathcal{M},\\
-\Delta_{g}v+h(x)v=u^{q-\beta\varepsilon},&\mbox{in}\ \mathcal{M},\\
u,v>0,&\mbox{in}\ \mathcal{M},\end{array}\right.

where
\Delta_{g}=div_{g}\nabla
is the Laplace-Beltrami operator on
\mathcal{M}
,
h(x)
is a
C^{1}
-function on
\mathcal{M}
,
\varepsilon>0
is a small parameter,
\alpha,\beta>0
are real numbers,
(p,q)\in(1,+\infty)\times(1,+\infty)
is a pair of numbers lying on the
critical hyperbola

\frac{1}{p+1}+\frac{1}{q+1}=\frac{N-2}{N}.

(1.4)

Without loss of generality, we assume that
1<p\leq\frac{N+2}{N-2}\leq q
. Moreover, by (
1.4

p>\frac{2}{N-2}
.

In the case

,

and
\alpha=\beta=1
, system (
1
) is reduced to the following equation

-\Delta_{g}u+h(x)u=u^{2^{*}-1-\varepsilon},\quad u>0,\quad\text{in $\mathcal{M}$},

where
N\geq 3
,
2^{*}=\frac{2N}{N-2}
,
\varepsilon\in\mathbb{R}
is a small parameter. If

, where

is the scalar curvature of the manifold, equation (

) is intensively studied as the well-known

whose positive solutions

are such the scalar curvature of the conformal metric

is constant, see e.g.
[
34
,
1
,
31
,
32

, Micheletti et al.
[

first proved that (

is distinct at some point from the graph of
\frac{N-2}{4(N-1)}Scal_{g}
. Here, we say that a family of solutions

of (

) blows up at a point

if there exists a family of points
\xi_{\varepsilon}\in\mathcal{M}
such that

[
4

discovered the existence of clustered solutions which concentrate at one point in

for (

[

,
29
]
.
For more related results about (

Now, we return to the following elliptic system

\displaystyle\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}-\Delta u=|v|^{p-1}v,\quad\mbox{in}\ \Omega,\\
-\Delta v=|u|^{q-1}u,\quad\mbox{in}\ \Omega,\\
(u,v)\in\mathcal{X}_{p,q}(\Omega),\end{array}\right.

called the Lane-Emden system, where
N\geq 3
,
(p,q)
satisfies (
1.4
),
\Omega
is either a smooth bounded domain or
\mathbb{R}^{N}
, and
\mathcal{X}_{p,q}(\Omega)=\dot{W}^{2,\frac{p+1}{p}}(\Omega)\times\dot{W}^{2,\frac{q+1}{q}}(\Omega)
. System (
1
) has received remarkable attention for decades. When
\Omega=\mathbb{R}^{N}
, by the Sobolev embedding theorem, there holds

\dot{W}^{2,\frac{p+1}{p}}(\mathbb{R}^{N})\hookrightarrow\dot{W}^{1,p^{*}}(\mathbb{R}^{N})\hookrightarrow L^{q+1}(\mathbb{R}^{N}),\quad\dot{W}^{2,\frac{q+1}{q}}(\mathbb{R}^{N})\hookrightarrow\dot{W}^{1,q^{*}}(\mathbb{R}^{N})\hookrightarrow L^{p+1}(\mathbb{R}^{N}),

with

\frac{1}{p^{*}}=\frac{p}{p+1}-\frac{1}{N}=\frac{1}{q+1}+\frac{1}{N},\quad\frac{1}{q^{*}}=\frac{q}{q+1}-\frac{1}{N}=\frac{1}{p+1}+\frac{1}{N}.

Thus the following energy functional is well defined in
\mathcal{X}_{p,q}(\mathbb{R}^{N})
:

\mathcal{J}(u,v)=\int\limits_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}\nabla u\cdot\nabla vdz-\frac{1}{p+1}\int\limits_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}|v|^{p+1}dz-\frac{1}{q+1}\int\limits_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}|u|^{q+1}dz.

By applying the concentration compactness principle, Lions
[
25
]
found a positive least energy solution to (
1
) in
\mathcal{X}_{p,q}(\mathbb{R}^{N})
, which is radially symmetric and radially decreasing. Moreover, Wang
[
33
]
and Hulshof and Van der Vorst
[

]
independently proved that the uniqueness of the

, and all the positive least energy solutions
\big(U_{\delta,\xi}(z),V_{\delta,\xi}(x)\big)
given by

\big(U_{\delta,\xi}(z),V_{\delta,\xi}(z)\big)=\big(\delta^{-\frac{N}{q+1}}U_{1,0}(\delta^{-1}(z-\xi)),\delta^{-\frac{N}{p+1}}V_{1,0}(\delta^{-1}(z-\xi))\big),\quad\text{for any $\delta>0$, $\xi\in\mathbb{R}^{N}$}.

Frank et al.
[
12

established the non-degeneracy of (
1

(N+1)
-dimensional spaces of solutions in

[

For more investigations of system (
