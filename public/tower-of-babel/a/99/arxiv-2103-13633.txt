A family of projective two-weight linear codes
Ziling Heng, Dexiang Li, Jiao Du, Fuling Chen

Abstract
Projective two-weight linear codes are closely related to finite projective spaces and strongly regular graphs. In this paper, a family of q q -ary projective two-weight linear codes is presented, where q q is a power of 2. The parameters of both the codes and their duals are excellent. As applications, the codes are used to derive strongly regular graphs with new parameters and secret sharing schemes with interesting access structures.

Let

{\mathbb{F}}_{q}

12

\sum_{x\in{\mathbb{F}}_{q}}\varphi_{1}(ax)=\left\{\begin{array}[]{rl}q&\mbox{ for }a=0,\\

Let

and
\alpha
be a primitive element of it. Define the

of
{\mathbb{F}}_{q}
by

\psi_{j}(\alpha^{k})=\zeta_{q-1}^{jk}\mbox{ for }k=0,1,\cdots,q-1
, where

. In particular,
\psi_{0}
is called the trivial multiplicative character and

. The orthogonality relation of multiplicative characters (see
[
12
]
) is given by

\sum_{x\in{\mathbb{F}}_{q}^{*}}\psi_{j}(x)=\left\{\begin{array}[]{rl}q-1&\mbox{ for }j=0,\\
0&\mbox{ for }j\neq 0.\end{array}\right.

For an additive character
\varphi
and a multiplicative character

of
{\mathbb{F}}_{q}
, the

over
{\mathbb{F}}_{q}
is defined by

G(\psi,\varphi)=\sum_{x\in{\mathbb{F}}_{q}^{*}}\psi(x)\varphi(x).

We call

the quadratic Gauss sum over

for nontrivial

.

Lemma 4

For

\displaystyle\Delta(a,b,c)=\begin{cases}(q^{s}+1)(1-q)&\text{ if $c=0,{\mathrm{Tr}}_{q^{s}/q}\left(\frac{b^{q^{s}+1}}{a}\right)=0$},\\
q^{s}+1-q&\text{ if $c=0,{\mathrm{Tr}}_{q^{s}/q}\left(\frac{b^{q^{s}+1}}{a}\right)\neq 0$},\\
q^{s}+1&\begin{subarray}{c}\mbox{ if }c\neq 0,\ {\mathrm{Tr}}_{q^{s}/q}\left(\frac{b^{q^{s}+1}}{a}\right)\neq 0,\ c\neq{\mathrm{Tr}}_{q^{s}/q}\left(\frac{b^{q^{s}+1}}{a}\right),\mbox{ or }c\neq 0,\ {\mathrm{Tr}}_{q^{s}/q}\left(\frac{b^{q^{s}+1}}{a}\right)=0,\end{subarray}\\
q^{s}+1-q^{s+1}&\text{ if $c={\mathrm{Tr}}_{q^{s}/q}\left(\frac{b^{q^{s}+1}}{a}\right)\neq 0$}.\end{cases}

Lemma 5
.

The length

of

is given by

\displaystyle n=\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}(q^{s}+1)(q^{s-1}-1)&\text{ if }c=0,\\
q^{s-1}(q^{s}+1)&\text{ if }c\in{\mathbb{F}}_{q}^{*}.\end{array}\right.

Proof.

By the orthogonal relation of additive characters and Lemma
4

\displaystyle n=\frac{1}{q}\sum_{x\in{\mathbb{F}}_{q^{m}}^{*}}\sum_{y\in{\mathbb{F}}_{q}}\zeta_{p}^{{\mathrm{Tr}}_{q/p}\left(y{\mathrm{Tr}}_{q^{s}/q}(x^{q^{s}+1})+yc\right)}=\frac{q^{m}-1}{q}+\frac{1}{q}\sum_{y\in{\mathbb{F}}_{q}^{*}}\varphi(yc)\sum_{x\in{\mathbb{F}}_{q^{m}}^{*}}\varphi\left(y{\mathrm{Tr}}_{q^{s}/q}(x^{q^{s}+1})\right)=\frac{q^{m}-1}{q}+\frac{1}{q}\Delta(1,0,c).

Then the desired conclusion follows from Lemma

∎

Strongly regular graphs with new parameters

A connected graph of
N
vertices is called
strongly regular
with parameters
(N,K,\lambda,\mu)
if it is regular with valency
K
and if the number of vertices joined to two given vertices is
\lambda
or
\mu
according as the two given vertices are adjacent or non-adjacent.

Let
G=[\textbf{y}_{1},\textbf{y}_{2},\cdots,\textbf{y}_{n}]
be the generator matrix of a
[n,k]
linear code
{\mathcal{C}}
over
{\mathbb{F}}_{q}
, where
\textbf{y}_{i}\in{\mathbb{F}}_{q}^{k}
. Let
\textbf{V}={\mathbb{F}}_{q}^{k}
,
\textbf{O}=\{\langle\textbf{y}_{i}\rangle:i=1,2,\cdots,n\}
and

\Omega=\{\textbf{v}\in\textbf{V}:\langle\textbf{v}\rangle\in\textbf{O}\}
. Define a graph
G(\Omega)
with vertices the vectors of
V
and where two vertices are joint if and only if their difference is in
\Omega
. In
[
1
, Th. 3.2]
, it was proved that
G(\Omega)
is strongly regular if and only if
{\mathcal{C}}
is a projective two-weight code. Assume that the nonzero weights of
{\mathcal{C}}
are
w_{1}
and
w_{2}
. By
[
1
, Coro. 3.7]
, the parameters of
G(\Omega)
are

N=q^{k},\ K=n(q-1),\ \lambda=K^{2}+3K-q(w_{1}+w_{2})-Kq(w_{1}+w_{2})+q^{2}w_{1}w_{2},\ \mu=K^{2}+K-Kq(w_{1}+w_{2})+q^{2}w_{1}w_{2}.

By Theorems
7
and
8
, if
m=2s
,
q
is even and
c\in{\mathbb{F}}_{q}^{*}
, then
{\mathcal{C}}_{D}
is a projective
[q^{s-1}(q^{s}+1),m,q^{2s-2}(q-1)]
two-weight linear code with weight enumerator

1+(q^{s}+1)(q^{s-1}-1)z^{q^{2s-2}(q-1)}+(q^{s}+1)(q^{s}-q^{s-1})z^{q^{2s-2}(q-1)+q^{s-1}}.

Hence
{\mathcal{C}}_{D}
yields a strongly regular graph
G(\Omega)
with the following parameters:

\displaystyle N=q^{2s},\ K=q^{s-1}(q^{s}+1)(q-1),\ \lambda=q^{s-1}\left(2q-3+q^{s-1}(q-1)^{2}\right),\ \mu=(q-1)q^{s-1}(q^{s}-q^{s-1}+1).

Compared with known strongly regular graphs
[

]
,

has new parameters.
