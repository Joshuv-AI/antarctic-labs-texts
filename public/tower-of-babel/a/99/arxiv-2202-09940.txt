\mathfrak{g}{(A)}

\mathfrak{g}{(A)}
is a quasi-affine Lie algebra, which is infinite dimensional. Given a braided matrix

4.2
About proof of main result

In order to
find all quasi-affine GDDs over an arithmetic GDD
\alpha
in Table B, we have to find all quasi-affine GDDs which are GDDs adding a vertex on Vertex
1,2,\cdots,n
, respectively, of
\alpha
. For example, we find all quasi-affine GDDs which are GDDs adding a vertex on Vertex
1
. Choice
i
,
2\leq i\leq n.

Let
\beta
the GDD omitting Vertex i of
\alpha
. We find all GDDs by adding a vertex on Vertex 1 of
\beta
such that they are arithmetic. Next we put Vertex 1 into these GDDs. Finally we determine if they are quasi-affine.
