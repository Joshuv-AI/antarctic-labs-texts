Calculations in induced gravity from higher-derivative field theories
Nahomi Kan, Kiyoshi Shiraishi

Abstract
In this paper, we investigate Einstein’s gravity induced from higher-derivative scalar field theories. We develop an approach utilizing an effective theory of multiple fields for the higher-derivative theory. The expressions for induced cosmological constant and the induced gravitational constant are obtained in the present scenario of induced gravity in D D dimensions. We also show that finite values for the induced constants can be extracted in certain infinite-derivative theories.
