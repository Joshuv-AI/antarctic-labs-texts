Near-Zone Symmetries of Kerr Black Holes
Lam Hui, Austin Joyce, Riccardo Penco, Luca Santoni, Adam R. Solomon

Abstract
We study the near-zone symmetries of a massless scalar field on four-dimensional black hole backgrounds. We provide a geometric understanding that unifies various recently discovered symmetries as part of an SO ⁡ ( 4 , 2 ) {\rm SO}(4,2) group. Of these, a subset are exact symmetries of the static sector and give rise to the ladder symmetries responsible for the vanishing of Love numbers. In the Kerr case, we compare different near-zone approximations in the literature, and focus on the implementation that retains the symmetries of the static limit. We also describe the relation to spin-1 and 2 perturbations.

Effective near-zone metric:

We begin by considering the Schwarzschild case. Our starting point is

.3
Ladder in spin and finite frequency

A convenient near-zone approximation that describes the dynamics of particles of generic spin,
s
, in the limit
r_{+}\leq r\ll 1/\omega
is
Page 1976
;
Starobinskiǐ and Churilov 1974

x(x+1)\partial_{x}^{2}R+(s+1)(2x+1)\partial_{x}R+\left[-(\ell-s)(\ell+s+1)+\frac{q^{2}+isq(2x+1)}{x(x+1)}\right]R=0,

(28)

where

q\equiv r_{s}r_{+}(m\Omega_{+}-\omega)/(r_{+}-r_{-})

x\equiv\frac{r-r_{+}}{r_{+}-r_{-}}.

(29)

It is straightforward to show that eq. (
28

E^{+}_{s}=\left(\partial_{r}-\frac{ir_{s}r_{+}(\omega-m\Omega_{+})}{\Delta}\right)R\,,\qquad\qquad E^{-}_{s}=\Delta^{-s+1}\left(\partial_{r}+\frac{ir_{s}r_{+}(\omega-m\Omega_{+})}{\Delta}\right)\Delta^{s}R\,,

which generate solutions with spin
s+1
and
s-1
respectively, i.e.,

and

, where

solves (

;
Starobinskiǐ and Churilov 1974
;
Chandrasekhar 1985
in the near-zone regime by connecting solutions with consecutive spin
s
and
