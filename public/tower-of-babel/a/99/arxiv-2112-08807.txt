[
3

If

, then we say that

If

The

(respectively,

, then

and

. We define

N^{+2}(x,B)=\{z\in B\setminus(\{x\}\cup N^{+}(x,B))\,\,|\,\,yz\in A(D),y\in N^{+}(x,B)\},

Note that for a vertex

, we have

and

. Similarly, let

and

.

The

(respectively, the

) in

consisting of distinct vertices
x_{1},x_{2},\ldots,x_{m}
(
m\geq 2
) and arcs
x_{i}x_{i+1}
,

(respectively,
x_{i}x_{i+1}
,

, and

), is denoted by

(respectively,

). The

obtained from a digraph

Remark 4:
The following example of a tournament of order
2n+1=9
shows that Theorem
1.5
for
n=4
is not true. Consider a tournament

such that

, where
A=\{a_{1},a_{2}\}
and
B=\{b_{1},b_{2}\}
, with the properties

x\rightarrow\{u,v,z\}\rightarrow y
,
B\rightarrow\{u,v\}\rightarrow A
,
y\rightarrow A\cup B\rightarrow x
,

A\rightarrow z\rightarrow B
and the following
xy,uv,vz,zu,a_{1}a_{2},b_{1}b_{2},a_{1}b_{1}
,
a_{2}b_{2},b_{2}a_{1},a_{2}b_{1}

It is easy to see that

contains an
