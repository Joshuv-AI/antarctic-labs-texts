Hannah Knight

Abstract
This paper is devoted to the study of the problem of resolvents, i.e., the problem of finding the resolvent by a given equation, whose coefficients depend on several independent parameters, the number of parameters in the coefficients being as small as possible. The author connects the problem to the study of critical manifolds in the space of equation parameters.

In this article, a new method for studying resolvents is introduced. This method is based on the construction of higher critical manifolds in the spaces whose coordinates are the parameters on which the coefficients of a given equation depend. A higher critical manifold, I define to be a set of points in the space of parameters at which several roots of the equation coincide.
