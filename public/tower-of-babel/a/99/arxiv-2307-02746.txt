In recent years, finding sharp bounds for Hankel determinants for various classes of univalent functions becomes a subject of great interest in geometric function theory. Establishing invariance between two functionals of Hankel determinants for a class of functions is an important study in Geometric function theory. There are results for certain classes of functions for Hankel determinant which are invariance (see e.g.,
