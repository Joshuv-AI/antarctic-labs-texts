Dyson’s model in infinite dimensions is irreducible
Hirofumi Osada; Ryosuke Tsuboi

Abstract
Dyson’s model in infinite dimensions is a system of Brownian particles interacting via a logarithmic potential with an inverse temperature of β = 2 \beta=2 . The stochastic process is given as a solution to an infinite-dimensional stochastic differential equation. Additionally, a Dirichlet form with the sine 2 point process as a reference measure constructs the stochastic process as a functional of the associated configuration-valued diffusion process. In this paper, we prove that Dyson’s model in infinite dimensions is irreducible. Keywords: Dyson’s model, random matrices, irreducibility, diffusion process, interacting Brownian motion, infinite-dimensional stochastic differential equations, logarithmic potential, Gaussian unitary ensembles MSC2020: 60B20, 60H10, 60J40, 60J60, 60K35

Introduction

This paper considers an infinite-dimensional stochastic differential equation (ISDE) of the form

\displaystyle X_{t}^{i}-X_{0}^{i}=B_{t}^{i}+\frac{\beta}{2}\int_{0}^{t}\lim_{r\to\infty}\sum_{|X_{u}^{i}-X_{u}^{j}|<r,\ j\not=i}^{\infty}\frac{1}{X_{u}^{i}-X_{u}^{j}}du\quad(i\in\mathbb{Z}).

(1.1)

For

[
28

\displaystyle\int_{\mathrm{W}}P_{\mathbf{w}}^{m}(\mathbf{w}^{m}_{0}\in\mathbf{A}_{0}^{m},\mathbf{w}^{m}_{t}\in\mathbf{B}_{t}^{m}|\mathscr{F}_{0,t}^{m*})1_{\mathbf{A}_{0}^{m*}}(\mathbf{w}^{m*}_{0})1_{\mathbf{B}_{t}^{m*}}(\mathbf{w}^{m*}_{t})P^{m*}(d\mathbf{w})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int_{\mathrm{W}}P^{\infty}(\mathbf{w}_{0}\in\mathbf{A}_{0},\mathbf{w}_{t}\in\mathbf{B}_{t}|\mathscr{F}_{0,t}^{m*})P^{m*}(d\mathbf{w})\quad\text{ by \eqref{:52p}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle P^{\infty}(\mathbf{w}_{0}\in\mathbf{A}_{0},\mathbf{w}_{t}\in\mathbf{B}_{t})=0\quad\text{ by \eqref{:11b}}.

Using this, we obtain, for
P^{m*}
-a.s.
\mathbf{w}
,

\displaystyle P_{\mathbf{w}}^{m}(\mathbf{w}^{m}_{0}\in\mathbf{A}_{0}^{m},\,\mathbf{w}^{m}_{t}\in\mathbf{B}_{t}^{m}|\mathscr{F}_{0,t}^{m*})1_{\mathbf{A}_{0}^{m*}}(\mathbf{w}^{m*}_{0})1_{\mathbf{B}_{t}^{m*}}(\mathbf{w}^{m*}_{t})=0.

From this, we easily deduce, for
P^{m*}
-a.s.
\mathbf{w}
,

\displaystyle P_{\mathbf{w}}^{m}(\mathbf{w}^{m}_{0}\in\mathbf{A}_{0}^{m},\,\mathbf{w}^{m}_{t}\in\mathbf{B}_{t}^{m})1_{\mathbf{A}_{0}^{m*}}(\mathbf{w}^{m*}_{0})1_{\mathbf{B}_{t}^{m*}}(\mathbf{w}^{m*}_{t})=0.

(4.2)

Using (
4.2
) and Theorem
1.3
, we deduce

\displaystyle P_{\mathbf{w}}^{m}(\mathbf{w}^{m}_{0}\in\mathbf{A}_{0}^{m})1_{\mathbf{A}_{0}^{m*}}(\mathbf{w}^{m*}_{0})1_{\mathbf{B}_{t}^{m*}}(\mathbf{w}^{m*}_{t})=0\quad\text{ for $P^{m*}$-a.s.\,$\mathbf{w}$}

(4.3)

or

\displaystyle P_{\mathbf{w}}^{m}(\mathbf{w}^{m}_{t}\in\mathbf{B}_{t}^{m})1_{\mathbf{A}_{0}^{m*}}(\mathbf{w}^{m*}_{0})1_{\mathbf{B}_{t}^{m*}}(\mathbf{w}^{m*}_{t})=0\quad\text{ for $P^{m*}$-a.s.\,$\mathbf{w}$}.

(4.4)

Suppose (
4.3
).
Then, using
P^{m*}=P^{\infty}\circ(\varpi^{m*})^{-1}
, we obtain

\displaystyle P_{\mathbf{w}}^{m}(\mathbf{w}^{m}_{0}\in\mathbf{A}_{0}^{m})1_{\mathbf{A}_{0}^{m*}}(\mathbf{w}^{m*}_{0})1_{\mathbf{B}_{t}^{m*}}(\mathbf{w}^{m*}_{t})=0\quad\text{for $P^{\infty}$-a.s.\,$\mathbf{w}$}.

(4.5)

Taking
\mathscr{B}=\mathrm{W}
in (
4.1
) and using
P^{m*}=P^{\infty}\circ(\varpi^{m*})^{-1}
,
we obtain

\displaystyle P_{\mathbf{w}}^{m}(\mathbf{w}^{m}_{0}\in\mathbf{A}_{0}^{m})1_{\mathbf{A}_{0}^{m*}}(\mathbf{w}^{m*}_{0})=P^{\infty}(\mathbf{w}_{0}\in\mathbf{A}_{0}|\mathscr{F}_{0,t}^{m*})\quad\text{ for $P^{\infty}$-a.s.\,$\mathbf{w}$}.

Hence, we deduce

\displaystyle P_{\mathbf{w}}^{m}(\mathbf{w}^{m}_{0}\in\mathbf{A}_{0}^{m})1_{\mathbf{A}_{0}^{m*}}(\mathbf{w}^{m*}_{0})1_{\mathbf{B}_{t}^{m*}}(\mathbf{w}^{m*}_{t})

(4.6)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle P^{\infty}(\mathbf{w}_{0}\in\mathbf{A}_{0}|\mathscr{F}_{0,t}^{m*})1_{\mathbf{B}_{t}^{m*}}(\mathbf{w}^{m*}_{t})\quad\text{ for $P^{\infty}$-a.s.\,$\mathbf{w}$}.

From (
4.5
) and (
4.6
), we obtain

\displaystyle P^{\infty}(\mathbf{w}_{0}\in\mathbf{A}_{0}|\mathscr{F}_{0,t}^{m*})1_{\mathbf{B}_{t}^{m*}}(\mathbf{w}^{m*}_{t})=0\quad\text{ for $P^{\infty}$-a.s.\,$\mathbf{w}$}

(4.7)

Integrating (
4.7
) with respect to
P^{\infty}
, we obtain

\displaystyle\int_{\mathrm{W}}P^{\infty}(\mathbf{w}_{0}\in\mathbf{A}_{0}|\mathscr{F}_{0,t}^{m*})1_{\mathbf{B}_{t}^{m*}}(\mathbf{w}^{m*}_{t})P^{\infty}(d\mathbf{w})=0.

(4.8)

Next, suppose (
4.4
).
Then, similarly as (
4.8
), we obtain

\displaystyle\int_{\mathrm{W}}P^{\infty}(\mathbf{w}_{t}\in\mathbf{B}_{t}|\mathscr{F}_{0,t}^{m*})1_{\mathbf{A}_{0}^{m*}}(\mathbf{w}^{m*}_{0})P^{\infty}(d\mathbf{w})=0.

(4.9)

Thus, we see either (
4.8
) or (
4.9
) holds for each
m\in\mathbb{N}
.
Hence, (
4.8
) holds for infinitely many
m\in\mathbb{N}
or
(
4.9
) holds for infinitely many
m\in\mathbb{N}
.

Note that the sequence of
\sigma
-fields
\{\mathscr{F}_{0,t}^{m*}\}_{m\in\mathbb{N}}
is decreasing.
Furthermore, the sequences of sets

\{(\varpi_{0}^{m*})^{-1}(\mathbf{A}_{0}^{m*})\}_{m\in\mathbb{N}}

and

\{(\varpi_{t}^{m*})^{-1}(\mathbf{B}_{t}^{m*})\}_{m\in\mathbb{N}}

are increasing and the limits

\widetilde{\mathscr{A}}:=\bigcup_{m=1}^{\infty}(\varpi_{0}^{m*})^{-1}(\mathbf{A}_{0}^{m*})
and

\widetilde{\mathscr{B}}:=\bigcup_{m=1}^{\infty}(\varpi_{t}^{m*})^{-1}(\mathbf{B}_{t}^{m*})

are
\mathscr{C}_{\mathrm{path}}^{\infty*}
-measurable. The sets
\widetilde{\mathscr{A}}
and
\widetilde{\mathscr{B}}

contain
\mathscr{A}
and
\mathscr{B}
, respectively.
Hence, using the martingale convergence theorem and the Lebesgue convergence theorem,
we find that,
P^{\infty}
-a.s. and in
L^{1}(\mathrm{W},P^{\infty})
,

\displaystyle\lim_{m\to\infty}

\displaystyle P^{\infty}(\mathbf{w}_{0}\in\mathbf{A}_{0}|\mathscr{F}_{0,t}^{m*})1_{\mathbf{B}_{t}^{m*}}(\mathbf{w}^{m*}_{t})

(4.10)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle P^{\infty}(\mathbf{w}_{0}\in\mathbf{A}_{0}|\bigcap_{m=1}^{\infty}\mathscr{F}_{0,t}^{m*})1_{\widetilde{\mathscr{B}}}(\mathbf{w}),

\displaystyle\lim_{m\to\infty}

\displaystyle P^{\infty}(\mathbf{w}_{t}\in\mathbf{B}_{t}|\mathscr{F}_{0,t}^{m*})1_{\mathbf{A}_{0}^{m*}}(\mathbf{w}^{m*}_{0})

(4.11)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle P^{\infty}(\mathbf{w}_{t}\in\mathbf{B}_{t}|\bigcap_{m=1}^{\infty}\mathscr{F}_{0,t}^{m*})1_{\widetilde{\mathscr{A}}}(\mathbf{w}).

From Lemma
4.1
and
\bigcap_{m=1}^{\infty}\mathscr{F}_{0,t}^{m*}\subset\mathscr{C}_{\mathrm{path}}^{\infty*}
,
we deduce
P^{\infty}(\widetilde{\mathscr{A}})\in\{0,1\}
.
Furthermore,
\{\mathbf{w};\mathbf{w}_{0}\in\mathbf{A}_{0}\}\subset\widetilde{\mathscr{A}}
by construction. Hence,

\displaystyle\int_{\mathscr{A}}P^{\infty}(\mathbf{w}_{0}\in\mathbf{A}_{0}|\bigcap_{m=1}^{\infty}\mathscr{F}_{0,t}^{m*})dP^{\infty}=

\displaystyle P^{\infty}(\mathscr{A})P^{\infty}(\mathbf{w}_{0}\in\mathbf{A}_{0}).

(4.12)

Similarly, we have

\displaystyle\int_{\mathscr{B}}P^{\infty}(\mathbf{w}_{t}\in\mathbf{B}_{t}|\bigcap_{m=1}^{\infty}\mathscr{F}_{0,t}^{m*})dP^{\infty}=

\displaystyle P^{\infty}(\mathscr{B})P^{\infty}(\mathbf{w}_{t}\in\mathbf{B}_{t}).

(4.13)

Suppose
P^{\infty}(\widetilde{\mathscr{A}})=0
. Then

P^{\infty}(\mathbf{w}_{0}\in\mathbf{A}_{0})=0
because
\{\mathbf{w};\mathbf{w}_{0}\in\mathbf{A}_{0}\}\subset\widetilde{\mathscr{A}}
.
Suppose
P^{\infty}(\widetilde{\mathscr{A}})=1
.
If, in addition, (
4.8
) holds for infinitely many
m\in\mathbb{N}
,
then from (
4.8
), (
4.10
), and (
4.12
), we deduce
P^{\infty}(\mathbf{w}_{0}\in\mathbf{A}_{0})=0
.

Similarly,
P^{\infty}(\widetilde{\mathscr{B}})=0
implies
P^{\infty}(\mathbf{w}_{t}\in\mathbf{B}_{t})=0
.
If
P^{\infty}(\widetilde{\mathscr{B}})=1
and
(
4.9
) holds for infinitely many
m\in\mathbb{N}
,
then
P^{\infty}(\mathbf{w}_{t}\in\mathbf{B}_{t})=0
from (
4.9
), (
4.11
), and (
4.13
).

Combining these and recalling
\mathbf{A}=\mathbf{A}_{0}
and

\mathbf{B}=\mathbf{B}_{t}
complete the proof.
