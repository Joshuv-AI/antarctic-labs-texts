Tree Embeddings and Tree-Star Ramsey Numbers
Zilong Yan, Yuejian Peng

Abstract
We say that a graph F F can be embedded into a graph G G if G G contains an isomorphic copy of F F as a subgraph. Guo and Volkmann [ 6 ] conjectured that if G G is a connected graph with at least n n vertices and minimum degree at least n − 3 n-3 , then any tree with n n vertices and maximum degree at most n − 4 n-4 can be embedded into G G . In this paper, we give a result slightly stronger than this conjecture and obtain a sufficient and necessary condition that a tree with n n vertices and maximum degree at most n − 3 n-3 can be embedded into a connected graph G with at least n n vertices and minimum degree at least n − 3 n-3 . Our result implies that the conjecture of Guo and Volkmann is true with one exception. We also give an application to the Ramsey number of a tree versus a star.



Keywords: Tree, Embedding, Ramsey number, Tree-Star Ramsey number.

7

In

Definition 2.1

Fact 4.1

For positive integers

and

is a linear combination of

and

Proof of Fact

Necessity: If

,

and

m+n-3=k(n-1)+l(n-2)+(n-1)-(n-2)=(k+1)(n-1)+(l-1)(n-2),
