On Grundy and b -chromatic number of some families of graphs: a comparative study
Zoya Masih Manouchehr Zaker

Abstract
The Grundy and the b-chromatic number of graphs are two important chromatic parameters. The Grundy number of a graph G \displaystyle G , denoted by Γ ⁡ ( G ) \displaystyle\Gamma(G) is the worst case behavior of greedy (First-Fit) coloring procedure for G \displaystyle G and the b-chromatic number b ⁡ ( G ) \displaystyle{\rm{b}}(G) is the maximum number of colors used in any color-dominating coloring of G \displaystyle G . Because the nature of these colorings are different they have been studied widely but separately in the literature. This paper presents a comparative study of these coloring parameters. There exists a sequence { G n } n ≥ 1 \displaystyle\{G_{n}\}_{n\geq 1} with limited b-chromatic number but Γ ⁡ ( G n ) → ∞ \displaystyle\Gamma(G_{n})\rightarrow\infty . We obtain families of graphs ℱ \displaystyle\mathcal{F} such that for some adequate function f ( . ) \displaystyle f(.) , Γ ⁡ ( G ) ≤ f ⁡ ( b ⁡ ( G ) ) \displaystyle\Gamma(G)\leq f({\rm{b}}(G)) , for each graph G \displaystyle G from the family. This verifies a previous conjecture for these families.

Concluding Remarks

One of the reviewers has mentioned that the inequality
\displaystyle\Gamma(G)\leq 2m(G)
holds for all graphs
\displaystyle G
. Hence for any family of graphs in which
\displaystyle m(G)\leq{\rm b}(G)+1
, for any
\displaystyle G
from the family, we immediately obtain
\displaystyle\Gamma(G)\leq 2{\rm b}(G)+2
. As confirmed by the reviewer, few families of graphs are known satisfying this property. Trees, cacti with
\displaystyle m\geq 7
and graphs with girth at least 7, are some of such families, but we have very better comparative inequalities concerning the Grundy and the b-chromatic number of graphs in these families. We present a proof of
\displaystyle\Gamma(G)\leq 2m(G)
for any
\displaystyle G
. Let
\displaystyle C
be a Grundy coloring of
\displaystyle G
having
\displaystyle k=\Gamma(G)
colors. Then there are vertices say
\displaystyle v_{1},v_{2},\ldots,v_{k}
such that
\displaystyle d(v_{1})\geq k-1,d(v_{2})\geq k-2,\ldots,d(v_{k/2})\geq k/2
. Let
\displaystyle d_{1}\geq d_{2}\geq\ldots\geq d_{n}
be a vertex degree of
\displaystyle G
in non-increasing form. It follows that
\displaystyle d_{k/2}\geq(k/2)-1
and hence
\displaystyle m(G)\geq k/2
. Let
\displaystyle{\mathcal{F}}
be any family such that for some function
\displaystyle f(.)
and for any
\displaystyle G
from the family, one has
\displaystyle{\rm b}(G)\geq f(m(G))
. It implies that Conjecture

is valid for
\displaystyle{\mathcal{F}}
.
