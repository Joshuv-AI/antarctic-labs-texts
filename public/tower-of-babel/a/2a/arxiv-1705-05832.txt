New Directions In Cellular Automata
Abdulrhman Elnekiti

Abstract
We Propose A Novel Automaton Model which uses Arithmetic Operations as the Evolving Rules, each cell has the states of the Natural Numbers k k = ( ℕ ) (\mathbb{N}) , a radius of r r = 1 2 \frac{1}{2} and operates on an arbitrary input size. The Automaton reads an Arithmetic Expression as an input and outputs another Arithmetic Expression. In Addition, we simulate a variety of One Dimensional Cellular Automata Structures with different Dynamics including Elementary Cellular Automata.

History & Overview

A Cellular Automaton (CA) is a Discrete Dynamical System which consists of Identical Cells, each cell has a defined number of states and the cells evolve according to a local rule in a defined number of iterations. CA was introduced by Von Neumann and Ulam
[
1
]
to model natural growth processes like Seashell and Snowflakes
[
2
]
.

Stephen Wolfram has explored One-Dimensional CA where each cell has the states of [1 or 0], known as Elementary Cellular Automata (ECA)
[
3
]
. We propose an Automaton model that shows a variety of structures based on a specific arithmetic expression formed as the input.
