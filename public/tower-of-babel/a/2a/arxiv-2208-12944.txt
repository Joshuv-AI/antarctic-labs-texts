An ordinal analysis of a single stable ordinal
Toshiyasu Arai

Abstract
In this paper we give an ordinal analysis of a set theory extending 𝖪𝖯 ​ ℓ r {\sf KP}\ell^{r} with an axiom stating that ‘there exists a transitive set M M such that M ≺ Σ 1 V M\prec_{\Sigma_{1}}V ’.
