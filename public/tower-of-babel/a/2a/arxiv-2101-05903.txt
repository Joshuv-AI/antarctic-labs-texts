Erratum:”Quasi-objective coherent structure diagnostics from single trajectories” [Chaos 31, 043131 (2021)]
George Haller, Nikolas Aksamit, Alex P. Encinas-Bartos

Abstract




This erratum corrects a mistake in our previously published paper

[
1
]
. Theorem 3 is incorrect as stated because the extended
Euclidean frame changes of the form (32) are nonlinear transformations
of the extended phases space. Their Jacobians generally does not preserve
lengths and angles, and hence the scalar fields
\mathrm{TSE}
,
\overline{\mathrm{TSE}}

and
\mathrm{\overline{\mathrm{TRA}}}
are not invariant under these
transformations.

Theorem 3 can be corrected by modifying assumption (A1) for unsteady
flows as

(A3)
\left|\partial_{t}\mathbf{v}(\mathbf{x}(t),t)\right|\ll\left|\mathbf{a}(t)\right|
,

where
\mathbf{a}(t):=\ddot{\mathbf{x}}(t)
denoted the Lagrangian
acceleration. This reflects the assumption that Lagrangian time scales
dominate Eulerian time scales in geophysical flows, which originally
motivated our study. On specific flow domains, (A3) can be a priori
verified from characteristic velocity-, length- and time-scales (see,
e.g, Ref.
[
2
]
, p. 88). Assumption (A3) also eliminates
the need to use the extended phase space in our arguments and enables
us to set
\mathrm{v}_{0}=0
in our formulas. Figure 1. shows that
(A3) is satisfied on average for both the AVISO data set and the unsteady
ABC flow example we considered in
[
1
]
.

Verification of assumption (A3) for our two examples in

]
:
Probability density function (pdf) of the values of
\left|\partial_{t}\mathbf{v}(\mathbf{x}(t),t)\right|/\left|\mathbf{a}(t)\right|

for assumption (A3) in (a) the AVISO data set example (b) the unsteady
ABC flow example. The pdf is measured in probability per unit increment.

The evolution of the Lagrangian velocity
\boldsymbol{v}(t):=\mathbf{v}(\mathbf{x}(t),t)

is nearly material in frames satisfying (A3). Additionally, such frames
must be related to each other via Euclidean coordinate transformations

\mathbf{x}=\mathbf{Q}(t)\mathbf{y}+\mathbf{b}(t)
with
\left|\dot{\mathbf{Q}}\right|,\left|\dot{\mathbf{b}}\right|,\left|\ddot{\mathbf{Q}}\right|,\left|\ddot{\mathbf{b}}\right|\ll 1
,
i.e., via slowly varying (geophysical) frame changes, in order for
the
\mathrm{TSE}
,
\overline{\mathrm{TSE}}
and
\mathrm{\overline{\mathrm{TRA}}}

to return approximately the same values in the new frames. More specifically,
our revised definition of quasi-objectivity for a scalar field is
that it has to approximate the same objective quantity in all frames
related to each other by slowly varying Euclidean transformation.

In any frame satisfying (A3), therefore, the single-trajectory
diagnostics
\lambda{}_{t_{0}}^{t_{N}}(\mathbf{\boldsymbol{x}}_{0},\boldsymbol{v}(t_{0}))

and
\bar{\lambda}{}_{t_{0}}^{t_{N}}(\mathbf{\boldsymbol{x}}_{0},\boldsymbol{v}(t_{0}))

will closely approximate the corresponding objective measures (averaged
stretching exponent and averaged hyperbolicity strength) of material
elements initially aligned with
\boldsymbol{v}(t_{0})
. This implies
the quasi-objectivity of these single-trajectory diagnostics by the
above revision of the definition of Ref.
[
1
]
. A similar
statement holds for the rotation diagnostic
\bar{\alpha}_{t_{0}}^{t_{N}}(\mathbf{\boldsymbol{x}}_{0},\boldsymbol{v}(t_{0}))

under assumptions (A2)-(A3). In summary, the corrected Theorem 3 reads
as follows:

Theorem 3.

(i) Under assumption
(A3)
, the trajectory stretching exponents

(TSEs), defined as

\mathrm{TSE}_{t_{0}}^{t_{N}}(\boldsymbol{x}\mathbf{}_{0})=\frac{1}{t_{N}-t_{0}}\log\frac{\left|\dot{\mathbf{x}}(t_{N})\right|}{\left|\dot{\mathbf{x}}(t_{0})\right|},\qquad\overline{\mathrm{TSE}}_{t_{0}}^{t_{N}}(\boldsymbol{x}\mathbf{}_{0})=\frac{1}{t_{N}-t_{0}}\sum_{i=0}^{N-1}\left|\log\frac{\left|\dot{\mathbf{x}}(t_{i+1})\right|}{\left|\dot{\mathbf{x}}(t_{i})\right|}\right|,

are quasi-objective measures of trajectory stretching and hyperbolicity
strength.

(ii) Under assumptions (
A2
)-
(A3)
, the trajectory
angular velocity

(
\mathrm{\overline{\mathrm{TRA}}}
), defined
as

\displaystyle\mathrm{\overline{\mathrm{TRA}}}_{t_{0}}^{t_{N}}(\boldsymbol{x}\mathbf{}_{0})

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{t_{N}-t_{0}}\sum_{i=0}^{N-1}\cos^{-1}\frac{\left\langle\dot{\mathbf{\boldsymbol{x}}}(t_{i}),\dot{\mathbf{\boldsymbol{x}}}(t_{i+1})\right\rangle}{\left|\dot{\mathbf{\boldsymbol{x}}}(t_{i})\right|\left|\dot{\mathbf{\boldsymbol{x}}}(t_{i+1})\right|},

is a quasi-objective measure of total trajectory rotation.
