2 2 -generated axial algebras of Monster type
Clara Franchi, Mario Mainardis, Sergey Shpectorov

Abstract
. We provide the basic setup for the project, initiated by Felix Rehren in [ 20 ] , aiming at classifying all 2 2 -generated axial algebras of Monster type ( α , β ) (\alpha,\beta) over a field 𝔽 {\mathbb{F}} . Using this, we first show that every such algebra has dimension at most 8 8 , except for the case ( α , β ) = ( 2 , 1 2 ) (\alpha,\beta)=(2,\tfrac{1}{2}) , where the Highwater algebra provides examples of dimension n n , for all n ∈ ℕ ∪ { ∞ } n\in{\mathbb{N}}\cup\{\infty\} . We then classify all 2-generated axial algebras of Monster type ( α , β ) (\alpha,\beta) over ℚ ⁡ ( α , β ) {\mathbb{Q}}(\alpha,\beta) , for α \alpha and β \beta algebraically independent over ℚ {\mathbb{Q}} . Finally, we generalise the Norton-Sakuma Theorem to every primitive 2 2 -generated axial algebra of Monster type ( 1 4 , 1 32 ) (\frac{1}{4},\frac{1}{32}) over a field of characteristic zero, dropping the hypothesis on the existence of a Frobenius form.

Fusion laws

Recall

. To this aim, for two fusion laws
