Hartle-Hawking wavefunction in double scaled SYK
Kazumi Okuyama

Abstract
We compute the transition amplitude between the chord number 0 0 and ℓ \ell states in the double scaled SYK model and interpret it as a Hartle-Hawking wavefunction of the bulk gravitational theory. We observe that the so-called un-crossed matter correlators of double scaled SYK model are obtained by gluing the Hartle-Hawking wavefunctions with an appropriate weight.



1
Introduction

Sachdev-Ye-Kitaev (SYK) model
kitaev1
;
kitaev2
;
Sachdev1993
;
Maldacena:2016hyu
;
Polchinski:2016xgd

is a useful toy model for the study of quantum
gravity.
The low energy sector of SYK model
is described by the Schwarzian mode and this part of the
dynamics is holographically dual to Jackiw-Teitelboim (JT)
gravity
Jackiw:1984je
;
Teitelboim:1983ux
.
As discussed in
Cotler:2016fpe
;
Berkooz:2018qkz
;
Berkooz:2018jqr
,
one can go beyond this low energy approximation by taking
a certain double scaling limit of the SYK model.
It turns out that this double scaled SYK (DSSYK) model
is exactly solvable using the
technique of the chord diagram

Berkooz:2018qkz
;
Berkooz:2018jqr
.
However, as emphasized in
Lin:2022rbf
,
the bulk gravitational picture of DSSYK
is not well understood.
One mysterious feature of DSSYK is that
the geodesic length of bulk spacetime becomes a discrete chord number

\ell
.
It is proposed in
Lin:2022rbf
that
the chord number state
|\ell\rangle
originally introduced
in
Berkooz:2018jqr
should be identified as the
state of bulk Hilbert space
and the expression of the partition function

Z(\beta)=\langle 0|e^{-\beta T}|0\rangle
in terms of the
transfer matrix
T
acting on the chord number states has a
natural bulk gravitational interpretation.

Generalizing this interpretation in
Lin:2022rbf
,
we propose that the amplitude

\langle\ell|e^{-\beta T}|0\rangle
is interpreted as the Hartle-Hawking
(HH) wavefunction
of the bulk gravitational theory
Hartle:1983ai

and we explicitly compute
\langle\ell|e^{-\beta T}|0\rangle
.
We realize that
the HH wavefunction has been secretly appeared in
appendix C of
Berkooz:2018jqr

in the computation of the so-called un-crossed
matter correlators in DSSYK.
Thus we find a bulk gravitational interpretation
of the un-crossed correlators: these correlators are obtained by
gluing the HH wavefunctions with an appropriate weight.

This paper is organized as follows. In section
2
,
we review the solution of DSSYK in terms of the chord diagrams and
the transfer matrix
Berkooz:2018jqr
.
In section

3
, we compute the HH wavefunction

\langle\ell|e^{-\beta T}|0\rangle
.
In section
4
, we present our observation
that the un-crossed correlators in appendix C of

Berkooz:2018jqr
are written in terms of the HH wavefunctions.
In section
5
, we consider the
probability distribution
of chord number

\ell
given by the absolute value squared

|\langle\ell|e^{-\beta T}|0\rangle|^{2}

of the HH wavefunction.
Finally we conclude in section

6
with some discussion on the future problems.

2
Review of doubled scaled SYK

We first review the result of DSSYK in
Berkooz:2018jqr
.
SYK model is defined by the Hamiltonian for

N
Majorana fermions
\psi_{i}~(i=1,\cdots,N)

with all-to-all
p
-body interaction

\displaystyle H=\mathrm{i}^{p/2}\sum_{i_{1},\cdots,i_{p}=1}^{N}J_{i_{1}\cdots i_{p}}\psi_{i_{1}}\cdots\psi_{i_{p}},

(1)

where
\psi_{i}

\{\psi_{i},\psi_{j}\}=2\delta_{i,j}

and the random coupling
J_{i_{1}\cdots i_{p}}

\displaystyle\langle J_{i_{1}\cdots i_{p}}\rangle_{J}=0,\quad\langle J_{i_{1}\cdots i_{p}}^{2}\rangle_{J}=\binom{N}{p}^{-1}.

(2)

DSSYK is defined by the limit

\displaystyle N,p\to\infty\quad\text{with}\quad\lambda=\frac{2p^{2}}{N}:\text{fixed}.

(3)

As shown in
Berkooz:2018jqr
, SYK model is exactly solvable in this double scaling limit.
In the computation of the trace
\langle\tr H^{k}\rangle_{J}
,
the average over the random coupling

J
can be organized
as the so-called chord diagram arising from the Wick contraction of

J
’s and the remaining trace of Majorana fermions

of the intersection number of chords

\displaystyle\langle\tr H^{k}\rangle_{J}=\sum_{\text{chord diagrams}}q^{\#(\text{intersections})},

(4)

where
q
is given by

\displaystyle q=e^{-\lambda}.

(5)

states
\{|n\rangle\}_{n=0,1,\cdots}
. Here
|n\rangle
denotes

n

as

\displaystyle\langle n|m\rangle=\delta_{n,m},\quad 1=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}|n\rangle\langle n|.

(7)

Then the partition function

of DSSYK is written as

\displaystyle Z(\beta)=\langle\tr e^{-\beta H}\rangle_{J}=\langle 0|e^{-\beta T}|0\rangle,

(8)

where the transfer matrix
T
is written in terms
of the

-deformed oscillator
A_{\pm}
as

\displaystyle-T=A_{-}+A_{+}.

(9)

A_{\pm}
acts on the state
|n\rangle
as

\displaystyle A_{-}|n\rangle=\sqrt{[n]}|n-1\rangle,\quad A_{+}|n\rangle=\sqrt{[n+1]}|n+1\rangle,

(10)

where
[n]
is the

-integer

\displaystyle{[}n{]}=\frac{1-q^{n}}{1-q}.

(11)

From (

), one can easily show that

A_{\pm}
obey the relations

\displaystyle A_{-}A_{+}-qA_{+}A_{-}=1,\quad[A_{-},A_{+}]=q^{\widehat{N}},

(12)

where
\widehat{N}
is the number operator

\displaystyle\widehat{N}|n\rangle=n|n\rangle.

(13)

Some comments are in order here:

1.

Our
-T
in (
9
) is equal to
\hat{T}
in

Berkooz:2018jqr
, which is related to the original
T
in

Berkooz:2018jqr
by a conjugation.
Put differently, the original
T
in

Berkooz:2018jqr
is obtained from our
-T
in (
9
)
by acting it on a different basis
\{A_{+}^{n}|0\rangle\}_{n=0,1,\cdots}

\displaystyle-TA_{+}^{n}|0\rangle=A_{+}^{n+1}|0\rangle+\frac{1-q^{n}}{1-q}A_{+}^{n-1}|0\rangle,

(14)

where
-T=A_{-}+A_{+}
in (

). Note that the state
A_{+}^{n}|0\rangle

is related to
|n\rangle
as

\displaystyle A_{+}^{n}|0\rangle=\sqrt{[n]!}|n\rangle,\qquad[n]!=\prod_{k=1}^{n}[k],

(15)

and the norm of
A_{+}^{n}|0\rangle
is given by the
q
-factorial
of
n

\displaystyle\big|A_{+}^{n}|0\rangle\big|^{2}=[n]!.

(16)

This basis and normalization were adopted in
Lin:2022rbf
.

2.

We have put the minus sign in the definition of
T
in (
9
).
This minus sign does not change the final result since the spectrum of the Hamiltonian
is symmetric under the sign flip
H\to-H
.
This symmetry can be traced back to the Gaussian distribution
of the random coupling
J
, where
+J
and
-J
appear with an equal probability.

As shown in
Berkooz:2018jqr
, one can compute the partition function
in (
8
) by going to the eigenbasis of the transfer matrix
T
,
which is given by the
q
-Hermite polynomial

H_{n}(\cos\theta|q)

\displaystyle\langle\theta|n\rangle=\frac{H_{n}(\cos\theta|q)}{\sqrt{(q;q)_{n}}},

(17)

where
(a;q)_{n}
denotes the
q
-Pochhammer symbol

\displaystyle(a;q)_{n}=\prod_{k=0}^{n-1}(1-aq^{k}),

(18)

and the
q
-Hermite polynomial is defined by

\displaystyle H_{n}(\cos\theta|q)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\Bigl[~\begin{matrix}n\\
k\end{matrix}~\Bigr]_{q}e^{\mathrm{i}(n-2k)\theta},

(19)

with
\Bigl[~\begin{matrix}n\\
k\end{matrix}~\Bigr]_{q}
being the
q
-binomial coefficient

\displaystyle\Bigl[~\begin{matrix}n\\
k\end{matrix}~\Bigr]_{q}=\frac{(q;q)_{n}}{(q;q)_{k}(q;q)_{n-k}}.

(20)

Using the recurrence relation for the

q
-Hermite polynomial, one can show that
T
is diagonal in this basis

\displaystyle\langle\theta|T|n\rangle=E(\theta)\langle\theta|n\rangle,

(21)

and the eigenvalue
E(\theta)
is given by

\displaystyle E(\theta)=-\frac{2\cos\theta}{\sqrt{1-q}}.

(22)

\langle\theta|n\rangle
obeys an orthogonality relation with respect to a
certain
integration measure of
\theta

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\pi}\frac{d\theta}{2\pi}\mu(\theta)\langle n|\theta\rangle\langle\theta|m\rangle=\delta_{n,m},

(23)

where the measure factor
\mu(\theta)
is given by

\displaystyle\mu(\theta)=(q;q)_{\infty}(e^{2\mathrm{i}\theta};q)_{\infty}(e^{-2\mathrm{i}\theta};q)_{\infty}.

(24)

Then the partition function (
8
) is written as

\displaystyle\langle 0|e^{-\beta T}|0\rangle

\displaystyle=\int_{0}^{\pi}\frac{d\theta}{2\pi}\mu(\theta)\langle 0|\theta\rangle e^{-\beta E(\theta)}\langle\theta|0\rangle

(25)

\displaystyle=\int_{0}^{\pi}\frac{d\theta}{2\pi}\mu(\theta)e^{-\beta E(\theta)},

where we used
\langle\theta|0\rangle=\frac{H_{0}(\cos\theta|q)}{\sqrt{(q;q)_{0}}}=1
.
This integral was evaluated in
Berkooz:2018jqr
as

\displaystyle\langle 0|e^{-\beta T}|0\rangle=\sum_{r=0}^{\infty}(-1)^{r}q^{\frac{1}{2}r(r+1)}(2r+1)\frac{\sqrt{1-q}}{\beta}I_{2r+1}\left(\frac{2\beta}{\sqrt{1-q}}\right),

(26)

where
I_{n}(z)
denotes the modified Bessel function
of the first kind. In the next section, we will compute the more general
amplitude of
e^{-\beta T}
sandwiched between
n=0
and
n=\ell

\displaystyle\langle\ell|e^{-\beta T}|0\rangle,

(27)

which we interpret as the Hartle-Hawking (HH) wavefunction of the bulk
gravitational theory.

The HH wavefunction
\langle\ell|e^{-\beta T}|0\rangle

\displaystyle\langle\ell|e^{-\beta T}|0\rangle

\displaystyle=\int_{0}^{\pi}\frac{d\theta}{2\pi}\mu(\theta)\langle\ell|\theta\rangle e^{-\beta E(\theta)}\langle\theta|0\rangle

\displaystyle=\int_{0}^{\pi}\frac{d\theta}{2\pi}\mu(\theta)\langle\ell|\theta\rangle e^{-\beta E(\theta)},

\langle\theta|0\rangle=1

we first note that the measure factor

\mu(\theta)
is expanded as

\displaystyle\mu(\theta)

\displaystyle=-(e^{\mathrm{i}\theta}-e^{-\mathrm{i}\theta})^{2}\prod_{n=1}^{\infty}(1-q^{n})(1-e^{2\mathrm{i}\theta}q^{n})(1-e^{-2\mathrm{i}\theta}q^{n})

(30)

\displaystyle=-2\mathrm{i}\sin\theta\sum_{r\in\mathbb{Z}}(-1)^{r}q^{\frac{1}{2}r(r+1)}e^{\mathrm{i}(2r+1)\theta}.

Combining the first factor
-2\mathrm{i}\sin\theta
of (
30
)
and the Boltzmann weight
e^{-\beta E(\theta)}
, we find

\displaystyle-2\mathrm{i}\sin\theta e^{-\beta E(\theta)}

\displaystyle=\frac{\sqrt{1-q}}{\beta}\mathrm{i}\partial_{\theta}e^{-\beta E(\theta)}

(31)

\displaystyle=\frac{\sqrt{1-q}}{\beta}\mathrm{i}\partial_{\theta}\sum_{n\in\mathbb{Z}}I_{n}\left(\frac{2\beta}{\sqrt{1-q}}\right)e^{\mathrm{i}n\theta}

\displaystyle=\frac{\sqrt{1-q}}{\beta}\sum_{n\in\mathbb{Z}}(-n)I_{n}\left(\frac{2\beta}{\sqrt{1-q}}\right)e^{\mathrm{i}n\theta}.

Now, using (
19
), (

(
31
), the

\displaystyle\langle\ell|e^{-\beta T}|0\rangle

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\sqrt{(q;q)_{\ell}}}\sum_{r\in\mathbb{Z}}(-1)^{r}q^{\frac{1}{2}r(r+1)}\sum_{k=0}^{\ell}\Bigl[~\begin{matrix}\ell\\
k\end{matrix}~\Bigr]_{q}(2r+1+\ell-2k)\frac{\sqrt{1-q}}{2\beta}I_{2r+1+\ell-2k}\left(\frac{2\beta}{\sqrt{1-q}}\right).

(32)

After shifting
r\to r+k
, the sum over
k
can be performed
with the help of the
q
-binomial formula

\displaystyle(a;q)_{n}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}(-a)^{k}q^{\frac{1}{2}k(k-1)}\Bigl[~\begin{matrix}n\\
k\end{matrix}~\Bigr]_{q}.

(33)

After some algebra we find

\displaystyle\langle\ell|e^{-\beta T}|0\rangle

\displaystyle=\sum_{r\in\mathbb{Z}}(-1)^{r}q^{\frac{1}{2}r(r+1)}\frac{(q;q)_{r+\ell}}{\sqrt{(q;q)_{\ell}}(q;q)_{r}}(2r+1+\ell)\frac{\sqrt{1-q}}{2\beta}I_{2r+1+\ell}\left(\frac{2\beta}{\sqrt{1-q}}\right).

(34)

One can further simplify this expression
as follows.
Using the relation

\displaystyle\frac{(q;q)_{r+\ell}}{(q;q)_{r}}=0,\qquad(0>r\geq-\ell),

(35)

the summation in (
34
) can be divided into two parts

\displaystyle\langle\ell|e^{-\beta T}|0\rangle

\displaystyle=\left(\sum_{r\geq 0}+\sum_{r\leq-\ell-1}\right)(-1)^{r}q^{\frac{1}{2}r(r+1)}\frac{(q;q)_{r+\ell}}{\sqrt{(q;q)_{\ell}}(q;q)_{r}}(2r+1+\ell)\frac{\sqrt{1-q}}{2\beta}I_{2r+1+\ell}\left(\frac{2\beta}{\sqrt{1-q}}\right).

(36)

One can show that the two contributions
\sum_{r\geq 0}

and
\sum_{r\leq-\ell-1}

are related by the transformation
r\to-r-\ell-1
and
these two
contributions are actually equal.
Thus we can restrict the summation to
r\geq 0
and
multiply by the factor of
2
.
Finally we find

\displaystyle\langle\ell|e^{-\beta T}|0\rangle

\displaystyle=\sum_{r=0}^{\infty}(-1)^{r}q^{\frac{1}{2}r(r+1)}\frac{(q;q)_{r+\ell}}{\sqrt{(q;q)_{\ell}}(q;q)_{r}}(2r+1+\ell)\frac{\sqrt{1-q}}{\beta}I_{2r+1+\ell}\left(\frac{2\beta}{\sqrt{1-q}}\right).

(37)

This is the main result of this section.
As a consistency check, one can see that
when
\ell=0
this reduces to the known result
(
32
) of partition function, as expected.

One can generalize this computation to the more general overlap

\displaystyle\langle\ell_{1}|e^{-\beta T}|\ell_{2}\rangle=\sum_{r\in\mathbb{Z}}(-1)^{r}q^{\frac{1}{2}r(r+1)}\frac{\mathcal{C}_{\ell_{1},\ell_{2},r}}{\sqrt{(q;q)_{\ell_{1}}(q;q)_{\ell_{2}}}}(2r+1+\ell_{1}+\ell_{2})\frac{\sqrt{1-q}}{2\beta}I_{2r+1+\ell_{1}+\ell_{2}}\left(\frac{2\beta}{\sqrt{1-q}}\right),

(38)

where

is given by

5
Probability distribution of
\ell

If we naively apply the probability interpretation of quantum mechanics
to the “wavefunction of the universe”
\langle\ell|e^{-\beta T}|0\rangle
,
then its absolute value squared

|\langle\ell|e^{-\beta T}|0\rangle|^{2}
can be thought of as the probability
distribution of
\ell
.
It seems more natural to normalize
|\langle\ell|e^{-\beta T}|0\rangle|^{2}

by the partition function
Z(2\beta)=\langle 0|e^{-2\beta T}|0\rangle

\displaystyle P_{\ell}(\beta,q):=\frac{\langle 0|e^{-\beta T}|\ell\rangle\langle\ell|e^{-\beta T}|0\rangle}{\langle 0|e^{-2\beta T}|0\rangle}

(63)

in such a way that the total probability becomes unity

\displaystyle\sum_{\ell=0}^{\infty}P_{\ell}(\beta,q)=1.

(64)

Here we assumed
\beta\in\mathbb{R}
.
Using this definition of probability, one can consider various quantities
associated with this distribution. For instance
we can consider the expectation value of

\ell

\displaystyle\overline{\ell}(\beta,q):=\sum_{\ell=0}^{\infty}\ell P_{\ell}(\beta,q).

(65)

When
q=1
, the distribution of
\ell
becomes the Poisson distribution

\displaystyle P_{\ell}(\beta,1)=\frac{\beta^{2\ell}}{\ell!}e^{-\beta^{2}},

(66)

since the
q
-deformed oscillator reduces to the ordinary oscillator

a,a^{\dagger}
with
[a,a^{\dagger}]=1
,
and the Hartle-Hawking state
e^{-\beta T}|0\rangle

becomes the coherent state
e^{\beta(a+a^{\dagger})}|0\rangle
of ordinary
harmonic oscillator.
In this case, the expectation value of
\ell
is given by

\displaystyle\overline{\ell}(\beta,1)=\beta^{2}.

(67)

For
0<q<1
, we do not know the analytic form of
\overline{\ell}(\beta,q)

as a function of
\beta
and
q
. Instead, we can compute
it numerically by truncating the sum
over
r
in (
37
) up to some cut-off
r\leq r_{\text{cut}}
.

In Figure
1
, we show the plot of
P_{\ell}(\beta,q)

for various values of
\beta
and
q
.
When
\beta
is small (or at high temperature),
P_{\ell}(\beta,q)
is peaked around

\ell=0
and the large value of
\ell
is highly suppressed.
On the other hand,
when
\beta
becomes large (or at low temperature)

P_{\ell}(\beta,q)
has a maximum at a non-zero value of
\ell
.
In Figure
2
, we show the plot of the expectation value
of
\ell
as a function of
\beta
. One can see that

\overline{\ell}(\beta,q)
is a monotonically increasing function of
\beta
,
but the growing rate for
q<1
is much slower than the
q=1
case
(
67
).

6
Conclusions and outlook

In this paper, we have computed the HH wavefunction

\langle\ell|e^{-\beta T}|0\rangle
in the DSSYK model explicitly,
and we observed that the known result of un-crossed correlator of matter
fields in
Berkooz:2018jqr

is obtained by gluing the HH wavefunctions
with an appropriate weight.
This observation suggests the bulk gravitational
picture for the
2-point function in (
52
) and
for the 4-point function in (
59
).
Interestingly, the basic building block of the bulk spacetime is the chord
and the spacetime can be thought of as fabric
made of the chords (see e.g. (
60
)).
This picture is vaguely reminiscent of the random tensor network

Hayden:2016cfa
or
the bit threads
Freedman:2016zud
.
As emphasized in
Berkooz:2018qkz
;
Berkooz:2018jqr
;
Lin:2022rbf
,
in the DSSYK model
the geodesic length is replaced by the number of chords
\ell
which is naturally
quantized.
It would be very interesting to extract more information of the bulk spacetime
from the DSSYK model.

There are many interesting open questions.
The crossed 4-point function in DSSYK
is constructed in
Berkooz:2018jqr

and it involves a complicated
R
-matrix originated from the
underlying quantum group symmetry.
It would be nice if we can extract the bulk spacetime picture
of the crossed 4-point function. In particular
we would like to understand the relation
between the crossed 4-point function and
the HH wavefunction as we did for the un-crossed case.
It is also interesting to go beyond the
strict large
N
limit and compute the
1/N
corrections.
In the strict large
N
limit (
3
), we only see the bulk
spacetime with disk topology.
It would be interesting to study the higher genus corrections to
the DSSYK model and develop a technique to compute the
higher genus corrections systematically. As a first step,
it would be interesting to compute the DSSYK analogue of the
“trumpet partition function”,
which played an important role in the
matrix model of JT gravity
Saad:2019lba
.
We leave this as a very interesting future problem.
