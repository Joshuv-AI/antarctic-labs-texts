Three new classes of binomial Fibonacci sums


Abstract
In this paper, we introduce three new classes of binomial sums involving Fibonacci (Lucas) numbers and weighted binomial coefficients.

Introduction and motivation

As usual, we will use the notation
F_{n}
for the
n
th Fibonacci number and
L_{n}
for the
n
th Lucas number, respectively.
Both number sequences are defined, for
n\in\mathbb{Z}
, through the same recurrence relation
x_{n}=x_{n-1}+x_{n-2},n\geq 2,

with initial values
F_{0}=0,F_{1}=1
, and
L_{0}=2,L_{1}=1
, respectively. They possess the explicit formulas (Binet forms)

F_{n}=\frac{\alpha^{n}-\beta^{n}}{\alpha-\beta},\quad L_{n}=\alpha^{n}+\beta^{n},\quad n\in\mathbb{Z},

where
\alpha=(1+\sqrt{5})/2
is the golden section and
\beta=-1/\alpha
.
For negative subscripts one checks easily that
F_{-n}=(-1)^{n-1}F_{n}
and
L_{-n}=(-1)^{n}L_{n}
.
For more information about these famous sequences we refer, among others, to the books by Koshy
[
15
]
and Vajda
[
18
]
.
In addition, one can consult the On-Line Encyclopedia of Integer Sequences

[
21
]
where these sequences are listed under the ids A000045 and A000032, respectively.

The literature on Fibonacci numbers is very rich. Dozens of articles and problem proposals
dealing with binomial sum identities involving these sequences exist.
Classical articles on the topic are
[

,

]
, among others.
Newer contributions include
[

]
and recent articles are
[

.

This note is motivated by the problem proposal
[
9
]
where the author asked to prove the identities

\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\frac{F_{k}+L_{k}}{k+1}=\frac{F_{2n+1}+L_{2n+1}}{n+1}\quad{\text{\rm and}}\quad\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\frac{F_{k}+L_{k}}{(k+1)(k+2)}=\frac{F_{2n+2}+L_{2n+2}-2}{(n+1)(n+2)}.

A solution with a slight generalization was provided by Ventas in
[
19
]
.
Here, we introduce some generalized variants of this proposal which should be regarded as attractive complements.
More precisely, we present three presumable new classes of Fibonacci (Lucas) binomial sums possessing the same structure.
Our results follow from three recently published polynomial identities derived by Dattoli et al.
[
8
]
.
They are given by (
x\in\mathbb{C}
)

\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}(-1)^{k}\frac{1}{k+1}x^{k+1}(1+x)^{n-k}=\frac{(1+x)^{n+1}-1}{n+1},

(1)

\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}(-1)^{k}\frac{1}{k+2}x^{k+2}(1+x)^{n-k}=\frac{(1+x)^{n+2}-(n+2)x-1}{(n+1)(n+2)},

(2)

and

\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}(-1)^{k}\frac{1}{(k+1)(k+2)}x^{k+2}(1+x)^{n-k}=\frac{(n+1)x(1+x)^{n+1}-(1+x)^{n+1}+1}{(n+1)(n+2)}.

(3)

In the course of derivation we will make use of the following known results.

Lemma 1
.

For any integer
s
, we have

(-1)^{s}+\alpha^{2s}=\alpha^{s}L_{s},\qquad\mbox{and}\qquad(-1)^{s}+\beta^{2s}=\beta^{s}L_{s}.

(4)

Lemma 2
.

Let
r
and
s
be any integers. Then it holds that
[
10
]

\displaystyle L_{r+s}-L_{r}\alpha^{s}

\displaystyle=-\beta^{r}F_{s}\sqrt{5},

(5)

\displaystyle L_{r+s}-L_{r}\beta^{s}

\displaystyle=\alpha^{r}F_{s}\sqrt{5},

(6)

\displaystyle F_{r+s}-F_{r}\alpha^{s}

\displaystyle=\beta^{r}F_{s},

(7)

\displaystyle F_{r+s}-F_{r}\beta^{s}

\displaystyle=\alpha^{r}F_{s}.

(8)

2
First set of results

Theorem 1
.

If
r
,
s
and
t
are any integers and
n
is a non-negative integer, then

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\frac{1}{k+1}(-1)^{s(k+1)+t}F_{r}^{k+1}F_{s}^{n-k}F_{rn-s(k+1)-rk-t}

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad=\frac{1}{n+1}\Big((-1)^{t+1}F_{s}^{n+1}F_{r(n+1)-t}-F_{t}F_{r+s}^{n+1}\Big)

(9)

and

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\frac{1}{k+1}(-1)^{s(k+1)+1+t}F_{r}^{k+1}F_{s}^{n-k}L_{rn-s(k+1)-rk-t}

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad=\frac{1}{n+1}\Big((-1)^{t}F_{s}^{n+1}L_{r(n+1)-t}-L_{t}F_{r+s}^{n+1}\Big).

(10)

Proof.

Set
x=-F_{r}\alpha^{s}/F_{r+s}
in (
1
), use (
7
) and multiply through by
\alpha^{t}
, to obtain

\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\frac{1}{k+1}(-1)^{r(n-k)+1}F_{r}^{k+1}F_{s}^{n-k}\alpha^{k(s+r)-rn+s+t}=\frac{1}{n+1}\Big((-1)^{r(n+1)}F_{s}^{n+1}\alpha^{-r(n+1)+t}-\alpha^{t}F_{r+s}^{n+1}\Big).

Similarly, setting
x=-F_{r}\beta^{s}/F_{r+s}
in (
1
), using (
8
) and multiplying through by
\beta^{t}
, yields

\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\frac{1}{k+1}(-1)^{r(n-k)+1}F_{r}^{k+1}F_{s}^{n-k}\beta^{k(s+r)-rn+s+t}=\frac{1}{n+1}\Big((-1)^{r(n+1)}F_{s}^{n+1}\beta^{-r(n+1)+t}-\beta^{t}F_{r+s}^{n+1}\Big).

The results follow by combining these identities according to the Binet forms while applying
F_{-n}=(-1)^{n-1}F_{n}
and
L_{-n}=(-1)^{n}L_{n}
.
∎

1

\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\frac{1}{k+1}(-1)^{k}L_{n-2k-1+t}=\frac{1}{n+1}\Big(L_{n+1+t}-L_{t}\Big).

(12)

Corollary 3

\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\frac{1}{k+1}(-1)^{t}F_{n-3k-2-t}=\frac{1}{n+1}\Big((-1)^{t+1}F_{n+1-t}-F_{t}2^{n+1}\Big)

(13)

\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\frac{1}{k+1}(-1)^{t+1}L_{n-3k-2-t}=\frac{1}{n+1}\Big((-1)^{t}L_{n+1-t}-L_{t}2^{n+1}\Big).

(14)

(16)

Corollary 5

\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\frac{1}{k+1}(-1)^{t}F_{2n-4k-2-t}=\frac{1}{n+1}\Big((-1)^{t+1}F_{2n+2-t}-F_{t}3^{n+1}\Big)

(17)

\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\frac{1}{k+1}(-1)^{t+1}L_{2n-4k-2-t}=\frac{1}{n+1}\Big((-1)^{t}L_{2n+2-t}-L_{t}3^{n+1}\Big).

\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\frac{1}{k+1}(-1)^{k}F_{2n-k+1+t}=\frac{1}{n+1}\Big(F_{2n+2+t}-F_{t}\Big)

\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\frac{1}{k+1}(-1)^{n+k+1}2^{n-k}F_{n+2k+3+t}=\frac{1}{n+1}\Big((-2)^{n+1}F_{n+1+t}-F_{t}\Big)

\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\frac{1}{k+1}(-1)^{n+k+1}2^{n-k}L_{n+2k+3+t}=\frac{1}{n+1}\Big((-2)^{n+1}L_{n+1+t}-L_{t}\Big).

Corollary 8

\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\frac{1}{k+1}(-1)^{k}2^{n-k}F_{2n+k+3+t}=\frac{1}{n+1}\Big(2^{n+1}F_{2n+2+t}-F_{t}\Big)

\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\frac{1}{k+1}(-1)^{k}3^{n-k}F_{2(n+k+2)+t}=\frac{1}{n+1}\Big(3^{n+1}F_{2n+2+t}-F_{t}\Big)

Theorem 10
.

If
s
is an even integer and
t
is any integer, then

\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\frac{1}{k+1}(-1)^{k}L_{s}^{n-k}F_{s(n+k+2)+t}=\frac{1}{n+1}\Big(L_{s}^{n+1}F_{s(n+1)+t}-F_{t}\Big)

(27)

and

\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\frac{1}{k+1}(-1)^{k}L_{s}^{n-k}L_{s(n+k+2)+t}=\frac{1}{n+1}\Big(L_{s}^{n+1}L_{s(n+1)+t}-L_{t}\Big).

(28)

Proof.

Let
s
be even. Set
x=\alpha^{2s}
and
x=\beta^{2s}
, respectively, in (
1
), use
1
.
Multiply through the resulting equations by
\alpha^{t}
and
\beta^{t}
, respectively, and combine according to the Binet forms.
∎

4
Additional sum relations

In
[
8
]
the following sum relation is also proved:

\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}(-1)^{k}\frac{1}{k+2}x^{k}(1+x)^{n-k}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\frac{x^{k}}{(k+1)(k+2)}.

(39)

This relation immediately yields

\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}(-1)^{k}\frac{1}{k+2}F_{2n-k}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\frac{F_{k}}{(k+1)(k+2)},

\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}(-1)^{k}\frac{1}{k+2}L_{2n-k}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\frac{L_{k}}{(k+1)(k+2)}

or

\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}(-1)^{k}\frac{F_{2n-k}+L_{2n-k}}{k+2}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\frac{F_{k}+L_{k}}{(k+1)(k+2)}=\frac{F_{2n+2}+L_{2n+2}-2}{(n+1)(n+2)},

which provides a nice addendum to problem proposal
[
9
]
. More generally, we have sum relations of the following form.

Theorem 16
.

If
r
,
s
and
t
are any integers (
r
non-zero) and
n
is a non-negative integer, then

\displaystyle F_{r+s}^{-n}\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\frac{1}{k+2}(-1)^{r(n-k)}F_{r}^{k}F_{s}^{n-k}F_{sk-r(n-k)+t}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\frac{1}{(k+1)(k+2)}(-1)^{k}\Big(\frac{F_{r}}{F_{r+s}}\Big)^{k}F_{sk+t}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{(n+1)(n+2)}\Big(\Big(\frac{F_{s}}{F_{r}}\Big)^{2}\Big(\frac{F_{s}}{F_{r+s}}\Big)^{n}(-1)^{t+1}F_{2s+r(n+2)-t}-\Big(\frac{F_{r+s}}{F_{r}}\Big)^{2}(-1)^{t+1}F_{2s-t}\Big)

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad+\frac{1}{n+1}\frac{F_{r+s}}{F_{r}}(-1)^{s+t+1}F_{s-t}

(40)

and

\displaystyle F_{r+s}^{-n}\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\frac{1}{k+2}(-1)^{r(n-k)}F_{r}^{k}F_{s}^{n-k}L_{sk-r(n-k)+t}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\frac{1}{(k+1)(k+2)}(-1)^{k}\Big(\frac{F_{r}}{F_{r+s}}\Big)^{k}L_{sk+t}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{(n+1)(n+2)}\Big(\Big(\frac{F_{s}}{F_{r}}\Big)^{2}\Big(\frac{F_{s}}{F_{r+s}}\Big)^{n}(-1)^{t}L_{2s+r(n+2)-t}-\Big(\frac{F_{r+s}}{F_{r}}\Big)^{2}(-1)^{t}L_{2s-t}\Big)

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad+\frac{1}{n+1}\frac{F_{r+s}}{F_{r}}(-1)^{s+t}L_{s-t}.

(41)

In particular,

\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\frac{1}{k+2}(-1)^{k+1}F_{n-2k}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\frac{(-1)^{k}F_{k}}{(k+1)(k+2)}=\frac{1-F_{n+4}}{(n+1)(n+2)}+\frac{1}{n+1}

(42)

and

\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\frac{1}{k+2}(-1)^{k}L_{n-2k}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\frac{(-1)^{k}L_{k}}{(k+1)(k+2)}=\frac{L_{n+4}-3}{(n+1)(n+2)}-\frac{1}{n+1}.

(43)

Theorem 17
.

If
s
is an even integer and
t
is any integer, then

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\frac{1}{k+2}(-1)^{k}L_{s}^{n-k}F_{s(n+k)+t}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\frac{F_{2sk+t}}{(k+1)(k+2)}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{(n+1)(n+2)}\Big(L_{s}^{n+2}F_{s(n-2)+t}+(-1)^{t}F_{4s-t}\Big)+\frac{(-1)^{t}}{n+1}F_{2s-t}

(44)

and

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\frac{1}{k+2}(-1)^{k}L_{s}^{n-k}L_{s(n+k)+t}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\frac{L_{2sk+t}}{(k+1)(k+2)}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{(n+1)(n+2)}\Big(L_{s}^{n+2}L_{s(n-2)+t}-(-1)^{t}L_{4s-t}\Big)-\frac{(-1)^{t}}{n+1}L_{2s-t}.

(45)

In particular,

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\frac{1}{k+2}(-1)^{k}3^{n-k}F_{2(n+k)}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\frac{F_{4k}}{(k+1)(k+2)}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{(n+1)(n+2)}\Big(3^{n+2}F_{2(n-2)}+21\Big)+\frac{3}{n+1}

(46)

and

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\frac{1}{k+2}(-1)^{k}3^{n-k}L_{2(n+k)}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\frac{L_{4k}}{(k+1)(k+2)}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{(n+1)(n+2)}\Big(3^{n+2}L_{2(n-2)}-47\Big)-\frac{7}{n+1}.

(47)

5
Conclusion

Motivated by the author’s recent problem proposal closed forms for three new classes of binomial sums
with Fibonacci and Lucas numbers were derived. In addition, a few sum relations connected with the subject were discussed.
Extensions of the results presented this note to gibonacci or even to Horadam sequences should be possible with little effort.
This exercise is left to the interested readers.
