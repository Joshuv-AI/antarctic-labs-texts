In

Let

2.1

We start with a projective algebraic surface

Definition 2.1

Let

Let

;

(c)

In

Now we explain in general the regeneration process.
Say that the degree of the degenerated branch curve

Each of the

Outer and inner 4-points are extensions of outer and inner 3-points respectively.
A 5-point regenerates as the 4-point regenerates, but with an addition of the 5th line

21
