Groundstates for nonlinear fractional Choquard equations with general nonlinearities Thanks: Partially supported by NSFC (11101374, 11271331)
Zifei Shen, Fashun Gao, Minbo Yang

Abstract
We study the following nonlinear Choquard equation driven by fractional Laplacian: ( − Δ ) s ​ u + u = ( | x | − μ ∗ F ⁡ ( u ) ) ​ f ​ ( u ) in ​ ℝ N , (-\Delta)^{s}u+u=\big(|x|^{-\mu}\ast F(u)\big)f(u)\hskip 11.77943pt\mbox{in}\hskip 3.2436pt\mathbb{R}^{N}, where N ≥ 3 N\geq 3 , s ∈ ( 0 , 1 ) s\in(0,1) and μ ∈ ( 0 , N ) \mu\in(0,N) . By supposing that the nonlinearities satisfy the general Berestycki-Lions type conditions [ 5 ] , we are able to prove the existence of groundstates for this equation by variational methods. Introduction and main results

The aim of this paper is to consider the following nonlinear Choquard equation involving a fractional Laplacian:

\left\{\begin{array}[]{l}\displaystyle(-\Delta)^{s}u+u=\big(|x|^{-\mu}\ast F(u)\big)f(u)\hskip 11.77943pt\mbox{in}\hskip 3.2436pt\mathbb{R}^{N},\\
\displaystyle u\in H^{s}(\mathbb{R}^{N}),\end{array}\right.

(1.1)

where
N\geq 3
,
s\in(0,1)
,
\mu\in(0,N)

fractional Laplacian

[
1

21

walk models in a natural way, occur widely in physics, chemistry and biology

the fractional Laplacians, have attracted much attention from many mathematicians. When
v
is
smooth enough, it can also be computed by the following singular integral:

(-\Delta)^{s}v(x)=c_{N,s}P.V.\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}\frac{v(x)-v(y)}{|x-y|^{N+2s}}dy,

(1.3)

\mathbb{R}^{N}

[
7

\left\{\begin{array}[]{l}\displaystyle-div(y^{1-2s}\nabla w)=0\hskip 11.77943pt\mbox{in}\hskip 3.2436pt\mathbb{R}_{+}^{N+1},\\
\displaystyle\hskip 37.38692ptw(x,0)=u\hskip 11.77943pt\mbox{on}\hskip 3.2436pt\mathbb{R}^{N}.\end{array}\right.

As shown in
[
11

(-\Delta)^{s}u(x)=-\frac{1}{\kappa_{s}}\lim_{y\rightarrow 0^{+}}y^{1-2s}\frac{\partial w}{\partial y}(x,y),\hskip 23.16054pt\forall u\in H^{s}(\mathbb{R}^{N}).

(1.4)

Thus we can see that the problem (
1.1

\left\{\begin{array}[]{l}\displaystyle-div(y^{1-2s}\nabla w)=0\hskip 87.17923pt\mbox{in}\hskip 3.2436pt\mathbb{R}_{+}^{N+1},\\
\displaystyle\partial_{\nu}^{s}w=-u+\big(|x|^{-\mu}\ast F(u)\big)f(u)\hskip 35.85048pt\mbox{on}\hskip 3.2436pt\mathbb{R}^{N},\end{array}\right.

\partial_{\nu}^{s}w(x,0)\doteq-\frac{1}{\kappa_{s}}\lim_{y\rightarrow 0^{+}}y^{1-2s}\frac{\partial w}{\partial y}(x,y),\ \ \forall\ \ x\in\mathbb{R}^{N}.
