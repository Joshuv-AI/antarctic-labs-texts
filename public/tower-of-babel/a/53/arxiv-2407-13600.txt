Spherically orderable groups
S.V. Sudoplatov

Abstract
We introduce and study the class of spherically ordered groups. The notions of spherically ordered groups and their spectra of spherical orderability are introduced. Values of these spectra are found for a series of natural groups.



The work was carried out in the framework of the
State Contract of the Sobolev Institute of Mathematics, Project
No. FWNF-2022-0012.

Key words:
spherical order, group, spectrum of spherical
orderability.

Remark 2.6

Theorem

In general,
