Subgrid multiscale stabilized finite element analysis of non-Newtonian Casson model fully coupled with Advection-Diffusion-Reaction equations
B.V. Rathish Kumar, Manisha Chowdhury

Abstract
In this paper we have studied subgrid multiscale stabilized formulation with dynamic subscales for non-Newtonian Casson fluid flow model tightly coupled with variable coefficients ADR ( V ​ A ​ D ​ R VADR ) equation. The Casson viscosity coefficient is taken to be dependent upon solute mass concentration. This paper presents the stability and convergence analyses of the stabilized finite element solution. The proposed expressions of the stabilization parameters helps in obtaining optimal order of convergences. Appropriate numerical experiments have been provided.



Keywords
Non-Newtonian Casson fluid & Advection-Diffusion-Reaction equation & Subgrid multiscale stabilized method & Apriori error estimation & Aposteriori error estimation

\begin{split}\mid(\textbf{TE}^{n+1},E^{A,n+1}_{\textbf{U}})\mid&=\mid(\textbf{TE}^{n+1},\textbf{e}^{n+1})\mid+\mid(\textbf{TE}^{n+1},E^{I,n+1}_{\textbf{U}})\mid\\

(68)

\rho\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}(\|e^{n+1}_{\textbf{u}}\|^{2}-\|e^{n}_{\textbf{u}}\|^{2})+\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}(\|e^{n+1}_{c}\|^{2}-\|e^{n}_{c}\|^{2})+(2\eta_{l}-C_{1}^{\prime}-C_{2}^{\prime}\eta_{s}-\epsilon_{1}^{\prime}\mid\tau_{2}\mid-\epsilon_{2}^{\prime})\\

\boxed{\|\textbf{u}-\textbf{u}_{h}\|_{\bar{\textbf{P}}}^{2}+\|c-c_{h}\|_{\bar{\textbf{P}}}^{2}\leq C^{\prime}(\textbf{R}_{h})(h^{2}+dt^{2})}
