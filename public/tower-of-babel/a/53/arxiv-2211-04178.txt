An attempt to add Barrow entropy in f ⁡ ( R ) \displaystyle f(R) gravity
P. S. Ens, A. F. Santos

Abstract
In this work, a way to consider together two originally different corrections to the Friedmann equations is presented. The first is the Barrow entropy, which imposes a fractal structure on the black hole horizon area. While the second is the well-known f ⁡ ( R ) \displaystyle f(R) gravity, which comes from a generalization of the Einstein-Hilbert action. Using the ideas of gravity-thermodynamics conjecture, these two models are combined. Then the modified Friedmann equation is obtained. Choosing a particular f ⁡ ( R ) \displaystyle f(R) model, an application is investigated. The state parameter and the density parameters for matter and dark energy are calculated. With these results, the dynamic evolution of the universe is discussed.

\displaystyle\Omega_{M}(0)=1
,
\displaystyle\Lambda=0
.

The initial conditions are defined so that the system always starts from an inertial expansion,
\displaystyle\omega=-1/3
, to better visualize the influence of the dark energy. By default, this
\displaystyle f(R)
model produces an initial acceleration that approaches to exponential,
\displaystyle\omega=-1
, depending on the choosen values of
\displaystyle\lambda
, and eventually it stabilizes itself to a power law,
\displaystyle\omega=-2/3
, according to the parameter
\displaystyle\mu

\displaystyle\Delta=0
. Note that the presence of the Barrow modification, i.e.
\displaystyle\Delta\neq 0

\displaystyle\Delta=0.04
or higher, it remains exponential with the difference being an initial deceleration more predominant while having a sharper transition to the asymptotic state. Very large values of the
\displaystyle\Delta
parameter can bring the system collapse for the given conditions.

VI
Conclusions

From the works of Hawking and Bekenstein, black hole thermodynamics opened a window to investigate the equations of the gravitational field in a different way, i.e. using the thermodynamics laws. In this paper, gravity-thermodynamics conjecture is used to derived the Friedmann equation considering Barrow entropy. In Barrow model, it is considered the case that quantum-gravitational effects can change actual horizon area of a black hole. In other words, this model constructs a fractal horizon surface by increasing the black hole area. Taking the ideas of gravity-thermodynamics conjecture,
\displaystyle f(R)
gravity is considered and the first law of thermodynamics in this gravitational model is analyzed. The main study developed in this work was to join the Barrow entropy and
\displaystyle f(R)
gravity, and from that, analyze the dynamic evolution of the universe during different periods. Corrections for the dark energy components are imposed due to the two models. Our results show that the scalar factor and the state parameter exhibit the expected behavior both for the individual cases and for the union of the Barrow and
\displaystyle f(R)
models. The results point to a significant influence of the intricacity parameter
\displaystyle\Delta
. However, restrictions related to its values in this new context will be studied in future investigations.
