Improved DHOST Genesis
Mian Zhu, Yunlong Zheng

Abstract
We improve the DHOST Genesis proposed in Ilyas:2020zcb , such that the near scale invariant scalar power spectrum can be generated from the model itself, without invoking extra mechanism like a string gas. Besides, the superluminality problem of scalar perturbation plagued in Ilyas:2020zcb can be rescued by choosing proper DHOST action.

IV.1

In this section, we study the scalar perturbation of the current model. Applying the previous result developed in
Ilyas:2020qja

Ilyas:2020zcb

S_{2,s}=\int d\tau d^{3}x\frac{z_{s}^{2}}{2}\left[\zeta^{\prime 2}-c_{s}^{2}(\partial_{i}\zeta)^{2}\right]~,

(21)

where
\zeta
is the curvature perturbation and

\frac{z_{s}^{2}}{2a^{2}}=3+2\frac{\dot{\pi}^{2}(-g_{1}+3g_{2}\dot{\pi}^{2})+18H\gamma\dot{\pi}^{5}-6H^{2}}{(\gamma\dot{\pi}^{5}-2H)^{2}}~,

\left(-\frac{z_{s}^{2}}{2a^{2}}\right)c_{s}^{2}=1+h+\frac{2}{a}\frac{d}{dt}\frac{a\left(h_{X}\dot{\pi}^{2}-h-1\right)}{2H-\gamma\dot{\pi}^{5}}~.

Before proceeding, we mention that in generalized Galilean Genesis, the curvature perturbation

Now, as we argued in
Ilyas:2020zcb

\eta_{E}=t_{E}

Hence, the scalar power spectrum can be evaluated by

P_{\zeta}(k)=\frac{k^{3}}{2\pi^{2}}\frac{|v_{k}(\eta_{E})|^{2}}{z_{s}(\eta_{E})^{2}}~.

(29)

IV.3
Vacuum initial condition

In this section we impose the vacuum initial condition and work out the scalar spectra index. Firstly, the argument of the Bessel function
kc_{s0}\eta_{E}\ll 1
, so we can expand the Bessel function with small argument approximation:

J_{a}(z)\sim\frac{1}{\Gamma(a+1)}\left(\frac{z}{2}\right)^{a}~,~Y_{a}(z)\sim-\frac{\Gamma(a)}{\pi}\left(\frac{2}{z}\right)^{a}~,

(30)

and the Bessel

v_{k}(\eta_{H})\propto c_{2}(k)\sqrt{k\eta_{H}}(k\eta_{H})^{-\frac{3}{2}}=c_{2}(k)(k\eta_{H})^{-1}~.

Now we determine the

term from the vacuum initial condition. In the far past, the linearized action (
21

VI
Conclusion and Outlook

In this paper we improve the DHOST Genesis model
Ilyas:2020zcb
to get the scale invariance scalar power spectrum. Compared to the original Galileon Genesis model
Creminelli:2010ba
, the improved model consists three new ingredient. The deformed Horndeski term
\mathcal{L}_{H2}
provides the realization for a graceful exit from emergent universe to standard cosmology; the
\mathcal{L}_{H3}
term determines the scalar spectrum index, resulting in the scale invariance; The DHOST term
\mathcal{L}_{D}
modifies the sound speed of scalar perturbation
c_{s}^{2}
near the emergent event
t=0
without altering the background evolution, hence solves the gradient instability. The emergent universe scenario presented here is then healthy and can meet the basic observational constraint without other mechanism invoked.

There are still plenty of rooms for us to improve the current model. Firstly, most of the emergent universe models (including the one here) predict a blue tensor spectrum with
n_{t}=2
(counterexamples can be found in, say,
Cai:2016gjd
;
Nishi:2016ljg
), so updating the current model to allow more possibility for
n_{t}
would be worth investigating. Secondly, it is interesting to develop genesis models with alternative mechanism (like the curvaton mechanism
Creminelli:2010ba
;
Wang:2012bq
) to realize the scale invariance, and see whether they can be distinguished by more observational data. Thirdly, it is worthy to develop the model to make the sound speed
c_{T}^{2}
are less than
c^{2}=1
, hence evading the superluminality issue. Finally, a detailed phase space analyze can help to decide whether there is fine-tuning problem in our model.
