Hankel determinants for a perturbed Laguerre weight and Painlevé V equation
Min Chen, Yang Chen, En-Gui Fan

Abstract
In this paper, we study Hankel determinants generated from the following perturbed Laguerre weight function, w ⁡ ( x , t ) = ( x + t ) λ ​ x α ​ e − x , t > 0 , α > 0 , α + λ + 1 > 0 , x ∈ [ 0 , ∞ ) . w(x,t)=(x+t)^{\lambda}x^{\alpha}e^{-x},\;\;t>0,\;\alpha>0,\;\alpha+\lambda+1>0,\;\;x\in[0,\infty). Under the double scaling scheme, the random matrix size n → ∞ , n\rightarrow\infty, t → 0 , t\rightarrow 0, such that s = 4 ​ n ​ t s=4nt is finite, we give the uniform asymptotic approximations of Hankel determinants in terms of a solution of a third-order nonlinear differential equation, which is equivalent to a particular Painlevé V equation ( P V {\rm P_{V}} ). In fact, this P V {\rm P_{V}} equation is equivalent to the general Painlevé III equation. The asymptotic approximations of the leading coefficients and the recurrence coefficients for the corresponding orthogonal polynomials also involve the P V {\rm P_{V}} equation. The asymptotic analysis is based on our earlier results by using the Deift-Zhou nonlinear steepest descent method.

We consider Hankel determinants are defined by

D_{n}(t,\alpha,\lambda)=\det\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}_{+}}x^{j+k}w(x,t)dx\right)_{j,k=0}^{n-1},\;\;\;\mathbb{R}_{+}=(0,\infty),

w(x,t)=(x+t)^{\lambda}x^{\alpha}e^{-x},\;\;t>0,\;\alpha>0,\;\alpha+\lambda+1>0,\;\;x\in[0,\infty).

(1.2)

It is well-known that the above Hankel determinants can also be expressed by

D_{n}(t,\alpha,\lambda)=\prod_{j=0}^{n-1}h_{j}(t)=\frac{1}{n!}Z_{n}(t),

(1.3)

where

Z_{n}(t)=\int_{\mathbb{R}_{+}^{n}}\prod_{1\leq{j<k}\leq{n}}\left(x_{j}-x_{k}\right)^{2}\prod_{\ell=1}^{n}w(x_{\ell},t)dx_{\ell},

(1.4)

and

\int_{\mathbb{R}_{+}}\pi_{j}(x)\pi_{k}(x)w(x,t)dx=h_{j}(t)\delta_{jk}.

(1.6)

It is obviously that the recurrence coefficients
