Criterion for the absolute continuity of curves
in metric spaces

Abstract
It is proved that a parameterized curve in a metric space X X is absolutely continuous if and only if its composition with any Lipschitz function on X X is absolutely continuous.



Dedicated to V. G. Krotov

Keywords:

absolutely continuous curve, metric space, Lipschitz function

This article was written solely due to the brilliant report by A. I. Tyulenev presented on April 29, 2024 at a seminar led by V. G. Krotov at the Belarusian State University. There was proved a criterion for the absolute continuity of curves in metric spaces satisfying a certain doubling condition (which implies automatically finiteness of the Hausdorff dimension of the spaces under consideration).

While thinking about the necessity of the above mentioned doubling condition I was very surprised to discover it could simply be omitted. Had been informed about this, A. I. Tyulenev replied that he had also discovered this, but his proof is indirect.

Theorem 1

A continuous mapping
\gamma\colon[a,b]\to X
is absolutely continuous if and only if for any Lipschitz function
h\colon X\to\mathbb{R}
the composition
h\circ\gamma
is absolutely continuous.

The necessary part of this theorem is obvious. To prove its sufficient part, we need the following three lemmas.

\displaystyle h^{+}_{L}(x)=\inf_{x^{\prime}\in X^{\prime}}\{\kern 0.83298pth(x^{\prime})+L\rho(x^{\prime},x)\kern 0.83298pt\},\qquad x\in X,

\displaystyle h^{-}_{L}(x)=\sup_{x^{\prime}\in X^{\prime}}\{\kern 0.83298pth(x^{\prime})-L\rho(x^{\prime},x)\kern 0.83298pt\},\qquad x\in X

(2)

Note that if Lemma

A
piecewise injective curve
is any continuous mapping
\gamma\colon A\to X
that has the following properties:

a) it is defined on a compact subset

\gamma(c)=\gamma(d)

(c,d)

c) for each segment

\gamma([c,d]\cap A)
is connected.

From b) it follows that the mapping
\gamma
can take the same values at no more than two different points, and only at the ends of a ‘hole’ in its domain

A
piecewise injective modification
of the continuous mapping
\gamma\colon[a,b]\to X
is any piecewise injective curve that is a restriction of
\gamma
to some compact subset

containing the points

\gamma\colon[a,b]\to X

Proof.

. Let

Secondly, assume that
