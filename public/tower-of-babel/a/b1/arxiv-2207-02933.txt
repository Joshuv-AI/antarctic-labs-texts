On the two-dimensional time-dependent anisotropic harmonic oscillator in a magnetic field
Pinaki Patra

Abstract
A Charged harmonic oscillator in a magnetic field, Landau problems, and an oscillator in a noncommutative space, share the same mathematical structure in their Hamiltonians. We have considered a two-dimensional anisotropic harmonic oscillator (AHO) with arbitrarily time-dependent parameters (effective mass and frequencies), placed in an arbitrarily time-dependent magnetic field. A class of quadratic invariant operators (in the sense of Lewis and Riesenfeld) have been constructed. The invariant operators ( ℐ ^ \hat{\mathcal{I}} ) have been reduced to a simplified representative form by a linear canonical transformation (the group S ​ p ​ ( 4 , ℝ ) Sp(4,\mathbb{R}) ). An orthonormal basis of the Hilbert space consisting of the eigenvectors of ℐ ^ \hat{\mathcal{I}} is obtained. In order to obtain the solutions of the time-dependent Schrödinger equation corresponding to the system, both the geometric and dynamical phase-factors are constructed. Generalized Peres-Horodecki separability criterion (Simon’s criterion) for the ground state corresponding to our system has been demonstrated.

Systems under consideration

Time-dependent Phase factor and the solutions of Schrödinger equation

(142)

\Omega

Given a bipartite density operator
\hat{\rho}

VIII
Conclusions

The exact solutions of the time-dependent Schrödinger equation (SE) for an anisotropic harmonic oscillator are constructed with the help of Lewis-Riesenfeld invariant method. We had considered an invariant operator, which is quadratic in co-ordinates and momentum. The coupled first order differential equations of the coefficients in the linear combination of the constituent operators in the invariant operator are expressed in a closed matrix form. These coupled matrix equations can be solved in principle for a given set of time-dependent parameters (mass, field strength, frequencies, NC parameter - whichever is relevant). The quadratic form is expressed in a simpler form by diagonalizing the system keeping the intrinsic symplectic structure intact. The annihilation and creation operators are introduced. The eigenstates and eigenvalues are demonstrated in detail. Both the geometrical and dynamical phases are constructed, and thus Schrödinder equation is completely solved for our system. The ground state for the bipartite system is demonstrated along with its separability criterion.

The results discussed in this paper can be directly used for various types of systems. In particular, for an anisotropic oscillator in noncommutative space (for both the spatial and momentum noncommutativity), charged oscillator in a anisotropic magnetic field, for Chern-Simon model (in long wavelength limit) and of the similar types.
