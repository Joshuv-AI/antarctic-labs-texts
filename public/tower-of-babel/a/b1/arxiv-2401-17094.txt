Constructing rotatable permutations of 𝔽 2 m 3 \mathbb{F}_{2^{m}}^{3} with 3 3 -homogeneous functions
Yunwen Chi, Kangquan Li, Longjiang Qu

Abstract
In the literature, there are many results about permutation polynomials over finite fields. However, very few permutations of vector spaces are constructed although it has been shown that permutations of vector spaces have many applications in cryptography, especially in constructing permutations with low differential and boomerang uniformities. In this paper, motivated by the butterfly structure [ 31 ] and the work of Qu and Li [ 32 ] , we investigate rotatable permutations from 𝔽 2 m 3 {\mathbb{F}}_{2^{m}}^{3} to itself with d d -homogenous functions. Based on the theory of equations of low degree, the resultant of polynomials, and some skills of exponential sums, we construct five infinite classes of 3 3 -homogeneous rotatable permutations from 𝔽 2 m 3 {\mathbb{F}}_{2^{m}}^{3} to itself, where m m is odd. Moreover, we demonstrate that the corresponding permutation polynomials of 𝔽 2 3 ​ m {\mathbb{F}}_{2^{3m}} of our newly constructed permutations of 𝔽 2 m 3 {\mathbb{F}}_{2^{m}}^{3} are QM-inequivalent to the known ones.

\displaystyle=2^{2m}\left[(-1)^{0}+(-1)^{\mathrm{Tr}_{m}\left(1+\frac{1}{1+t}+\left(\frac{1}{1+t}\right)^{2}\right)}\right]

\displaystyle=2^{2m}\left[(-1)^{0}+(-1)^{1}\right]=0.

Thus Eq. (
17
) holds and Eq. (
16
) has no solution, which means
F
is a permutation.
∎

Theorem 3.3
.

Let
m
be odd and
F(x,y,z)=(f(x,y,z),f(y,z,x),f(z,x,y))
, where

{\mathbb{F}}_{2^{m}}^{3}

Proof.

It suffices to show that the equation system

\displaystyle x^{3}+yz^{2}+y^{2}z=a

(19.1)

\displaystyle y^{3}+x^{2}z+xz^{2}=b

(19.2)

\displaystyle z^{3}+xy^{2}+x^{2}y=c

(19.3)

{\mathbb{F}}_{2^{m}}^{3}

) and Eq. (

(x+d)^{3}+(y+d)^{3}=c+d^{3}.\end{cases}

Let

. Then the equation system above is equivalent to

\begin{cases}(X+Y)^{3}+Y^{3}=a+d^{3},\\
X^{3}+Y^{3}=c+d^{3}.\end{cases}

By simplifying, we get

\displaystyle X(X^{2}+XY+Y^{2})=b+c,

(20.1)

\displaystyle(X+Y)(X^{2}+XY+Y^{2})=a+b.

(20.2)

Next, we investigate the solutions of the equation system (
20
) in the case of
b+c=0
and
b+c\neq 0
.

Case 1.
If
b=c
, we get
X^{2}+XY+Y^{2}=X^{2}\left(1+\frac{Y}{X}+\frac{Y^{2}}{X^{2}}\right)=0
by Eq. (
20.1
) when

, which implies

and contradicts the fact that

is odd. Thus, we have
X=0
. Plugging it into Eq. (
20.2
), we have
Y^{3}=a+b
. Furthermore,
(d,d+(a+b)^{1/3},d+(a+b)^{1/3})

is the unique solution of the equation system (
19
).

Case 2.
If
b\neq c
, then
X\neq 0
. After dividing the left part of Eq. (
20.2
) by that of Eq. (
20.1
) and simplifying, we get

\frac{Y}{X}=\frac{a+c}{b+c}.

Plugging it into Eq. (

\left[1+\left(\frac{a+c}{b+c}\right)^{3}\right]X^{3}=a+b.

Finally, the only solution of the equation system (
19

\begin{cases}x=d+\left[\frac{(a+b)(b+c)^{3}}{(a+c)^{3}+(b+c)^{3}}\right]^{1/3},\\

To sum up,
F(x,y,z)
is a permutation of
{\mathbb{F}}_{2^{m}}^{3}
.
∎

4
Conclusion and further works

In this paper, we mainly focused on constructing several new classes of
3
-homogenous rotatable permutations from
{\mathbb{F}}_{q}^{3}
to itself. In our proofs, the resultant of polynomials and some skills of exponential sums were used.
Clearly, many directions could be further investigated, and some perspectives are listed here.
First, from the experimental results by MAGMA, we can find that there are still some
3
-homogenous rotatable permutation instances on
{\mathbb{F}}_{2^{m}}^{3}
where
m=3,5,7
. It is interesting to generalize them into infinite classes. Second,
due to the diversity of
d
-homogeneous polynomials, there surely are plenty of possibilities to enrich PPs of the form
f(x)=x^{r}h(x^{q-1})
over
{\mathbb{F}}_{q^{n}}
using the method of
[
32
]
. Therefore, constructing
d
-homogeneous permutations for more structures and higher powers deserves to be explored.
Finally, from the point of properties of permutations, the cryptographic properties of these construction results are unexplored in this paper. We believe that, if permutations with good properties can be discovered from them, they will be able to broaden the applications of multivariate permutations.
