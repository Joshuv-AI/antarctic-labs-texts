Nishant Agrawal, Yaozhong Hu

1.
Introduction

In
[
2
]
we introduced a logarithmic Euler-Maruyama scheme for a
single stochastic delay equation, which preserve positivity if the solution to the original equation is positive.
The convergence rate was also obtained for such scheme.
This scheme is important for simulation of the paths of the
equation. It plays important role in option pricing for example since
we often cannot obtain the explicit pricing value and we need to
use Monte-Carlo to complete the evaluation. Naturally our next question would be what will be the
analogous scheme for a system of stochastic delay equations and if such schemes converges.
This type of problems is very important since there is always more than one stock in the real market.
Now in more than one dimension, the problem of positive solution
and the numerical schemes which preserve the positivity are much more complicated.
In this paper
we shall extend
our work in
[
2
]
to a system of stochastic delay differential equations. The
problems of existence and uniqueness of a positive
are solved. The multi-dimensional logarithmic
Euler-Maruyama scheme are constructed which preserve the positivity of the
approximate solutions. The scheme is proved to be convergent with rate
0.5
.

\displaystyle\leq C\Big(\mathbb{E}\sup_{0\leq t\leq T}\Big|p_{i}^{\pi}(t)-p_{i}^{\pi}(\lfloor{t\rfloor})\Big|^{2p}\Big)^{1/2}\Big(\mathbb{E}\sup_{0\leq t\leq T}\Big|X_{i}^{\pi}(t)\Big|^{2p}\Big)^{1/2}

\displaystyle\qquad\quad+C\Big(\mathbb{E}\sup_{0\leq t\leq T}\Big|X_{i}^{\pi}(t)-X_{i}^{\pi}(\lfloor{t\rfloor})\Big|^{2p}\Big)^{1/2}\Big(\mathbb{E}\sup_{0\leq t\leq T}\Big|p_{i}^{\pi}(\lfloor{t\rfloor})\Big|^{2p}\Big)^{1/2}.

(3.17)

By Assumption 2 we can bound

We now bound the other two components.

(3.18)

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\sup_{0\leq t\leq T}\Big|p_{i}^{\pi}(t)-p_{i}^{\pi}(\lfloor{t\rfloor})\Big|^{2p}\leq\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}j,j\neq i\end{subarray}}^{d}\mathbb{E}\sup_{0\leq t\leq T}\Big|\int_{\lfloor{t\rfloor}}^{t}f_{ij}(v_{2}(u))du\Big|^{2p}.

By Assumption 2 it is easy to see that for some constant
C_{1}

(3.19)

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\sup_{0\leq t\leq T}\Big|p_{i}^{\pi}(t)-p_{i}^{\pi}(\lfloor{t\rfloor})\Big|^{2p}\leq C_{1}{\Delta}^{2p}.

For

, boundedness of

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\sup_{0\leq t\leq T}\Big|X_{i}^{\pi}(t)-X_{i}^{\pi}(\lfloor{t\rfloor})\Big|^{2p}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\left\{\mathbb{E}\sup_{0\leq t\leq T}\Big|X_{i}^{\pi}(t)+X_{i}^{\pi}(\lfloor{t\rfloor})\Big|^{2p}\right\}^{1/2}

\displaystyle\cdot K\left\{\mathbb{E}\sup_{0\leq t\leq T}\left[\Big|\sum_{j=1}^{d}\sum_{\lfloor{t\rfloor}\leq s<t}\ln(1+g_{ij}(v_{2}(s))Y_{j,N(s)})\Big|\right]^{2p}\right\}^{1/2}.

The first factor is bounded and now, we want to bound the second factor:

I:=\mathbb{E}\sup_{0\leq t\leq T}\left|\sum_{j=1}^{d}\sum_{\lfloor{t\rfloor}\leq s\leq t}\ln(1+g_{i,j}(v_{2}(s))Y_{j,N_{j}(s)})\right|^{2p}\,.

(We use the same

to denote different quantities in different

We write the above sum as an integral:

\displaystyle I

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\sup_{0\leq t\leq T}\Big|\sum_{j=1}^{d}\displaystyle\int_{\mathcal{J}}\displaystyle\int_{\lfloor{t\rfloor}}^{t}\ln(1+z_{j}g_{ij}(v_{2}(s))){N_{j}}(ds,dz_{j})\Big|^{2p}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\sup_{0\leq t\leq T}\Big|\sum_{j=1}^{d}\displaystyle\int_{\mathcal{J}}\displaystyle\int_{\lfloor{t\rfloor}}^{t}\ln(1+z_{j}g_{ij}(v_{2}(s)))\tilde{N_{j}}(ds,dz_{j})

\displaystyle+\sum_{j=1}^{d}\displaystyle\int_{\mathcal{J}}\displaystyle\int_{\lfloor{t\rfloor}}^{t}\ln(1+z_{j}g_{ij}(v_{2}(s)))\nu_{j}(dz_{j})ds\Big|^{2p}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C_{p}\left({\Delta}^{2p}+\mathbb{E}\sup_{0\leq t\leq T}\Big|\displaystyle\int_{\mathcal{J}}\displaystyle\int_{\lfloor{t\rfloor}}^{t}\ln(1+z_{j}g_{ij}(v_{2}(s)))\tilde{N_{j}}(ds,dz_{j})\Big|^{2p}\right)\,.

By the Burkholder–Davis–Gundy inequality, we have

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\sup_{0\leq t\leq T}\Big|\displaystyle\int_{\mathcal{J}}\displaystyle\int_{\lfloor{t\rfloor}}^{t}\ln(1+z_{j}g_{ij}(v_{2}(s)))\tilde{N_{j}}(ds,dz_{j})\Big|^{2p}

\displaystyle\qquad\quad\leq\mathbb{E}\sup_{0\leq t\leq T}\left(\displaystyle\int_{\mathcal{J}}\int_{\lfloor{t\rfloor}}^{t}\Big|\ln(1+z_{j}g_{ij}(v_{2}(s)))\Big|^{2}\nu_{j}(dz_{j})ds\right)^{p}

(3.20)

\displaystyle\qquad\quad\leq K_{p}{\Delta}^{p}\,.

Plugging above, (
3.19
), in (

) we get for some

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\sup_{0\leq t\leq T}\Big|S_{i}^{\pi}(t)-v_{i}(t)\Big|^{p}\leq K_{1}{\Delta}^{p}+K_{2}{\Delta}^{p/2}\leq K{\Delta}^{p/2}.

This proves the lemma.

Theorem 3.5
.

Assume that Assumptions (A1)–(A4) are true.
Then, there is a constant
K_{pd,T}
, independent of
\pi
such that

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\Bigg[\sup_{0\leq t\leq T}\Big[|S(t)-S^{\pi}(t)|^{p}\Big]\Bigg]\leq K_{pd,T}{\Delta}^{p/2}.

Proof

First, we we want to bound

(3.23)

\displaystyle I_{1}:=\mathbb{E}\Big(\sup_{0\leq t\leq r}|p(t)-p^{\pi}(t)|^{p}\Big)\,.

From (
3.9
), we see that when
t\in[t_{k},t_{k+1}]
,

\tilde{F}(S(t-b))=\int_{t_{k}}^{t}F(S(u-b))du+O(\Delta^{2})\,.

Thus

\exp\left(\tilde{F}(S(t-b))\right)=I+\int_{t_{k}}^{t}F(S(u-b))du+O(\Delta^{2})\,.

Thus we have a formula for

which is analogous to the one for

(Equation

\displaystyle p(t)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\Big[I+\int_{\lfloor{t\rfloor}}^{t}F(S(u-b))du+O(\Delta^{2})\Big]\prod_{k=0}^{\lfloor t\rfloor}\Big[I+\int_{t_{k}}^{t_{k+1}}F(S(u-b))du+O(\Delta^{2})\Big]

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\rho(\lfloor{t\rfloor},t)\prod_{k=0}^{\lfloor t\rfloor}\rho(t_{k},t_{k+1})\,,

where

\rho(r,s)=I+\int_{r}^{s}F(S(u-b))du+O(\Delta^{2})\,.

We can also write

(3.25)

\displaystyle p^{\pi}(t)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\rho^{\pi}(\lfloor{t\rfloor},t)\prod_{k=0}^{\lfloor t\rfloor}\rho^{\pi}(t_{k},t_{k+1})\,,

When
r,s\in[t_{k},t_{k+1}],r<s
, we have by the Lipschitz condition

(3.26)

\displaystyle|\rho(r,s)-\rho^{\pi}(r,s)|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle|F(S(t_{k}-b))-F(S^{\pi}(t_{k}-b))|(s-r)

\displaystyle+\int_{r}^{s}|F(S(u-b))-F(S^{\pi}(t_{k}-b))|du+O(\Delta^{2})

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C|S(t_{k}-b)-S^{\pi}(t_{k}-b)|+O(\Delta^{3/2})\,.

We also have

(3.27)

In the same way we have

(3.28)

|\rho(r,s)|\leq e^{C(s-r)}\,.

Thus

\displaystyle|p^{\pi}(t)-p(t)|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\left|\rho(\lfloor{t\rfloor},t)-\rho^{\pi}(\lfloor{t\rfloor},t)\right|\prod_{k=0}^{\lfloor t\rfloor}\rho^{\pi}(t_{k},t_{k+1})

\displaystyle\qquad+\sum_{\ell=0}^{\lfloor{t\rfloor}}\left|\rho(t_{\ell},t_{\ell+1})-\rho^{\pi}(t_{\ell},t_{\ell+1})\right|\rho(\lfloor{t\rfloor},t)\prod_{k=0,k\not=\ell}^{\lfloor t\rfloor}\rho^{\pi}(t_{k},t_{k+1})

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\left[C\left|S(t_{k}-b)-S^{\pi}(t_{k}-b)\right|+O(\Delta^{3/2})\right]\prod_{k=0}^{\lfloor t\rfloor}e^{C(t_{k+1}-t_{k})}

\displaystyle\qquad+\sum_{\ell=0}^{\lfloor{t\rfloor}}\left[C|S(t_{\ell}-b)-S^{\pi}(t_{\ell}-b)|+O(\Delta^{3/2})\right]\rho(\lfloor{t\rfloor},t)\prod_{k=0,k\not=\ell}^{\lfloor t\rfloor}e^{C(t_{\ell+1}-t_{\ell})}

Thus we have for some

\displaystyle I_{1}\leq C\mathbb{E}\sup_{0\leq t\leq r}\Big|S(t-b)-S^{\pi}(t-b)\Big|^{p}+K_{1}\mathbb{E}\sup_{0\leq t\leq r}\Big|v_{2}(u)-S^{\pi}(t-b)\Big|^{p}.

Then by lemma

We now bound
\mathbb{E}\sup_{0\leq t\leq r}|X(t)-X^{\pi}(t)|^{p}
.
Denote

{\Delta}Z(u)\neq 0\end{subarray}}\ln\Big(1+g_{ij}(S(u-b))Y_{j,N_{j}(u)}\Big)
