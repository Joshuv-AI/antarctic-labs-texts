Abstract
. In this paper we consider Littlewood-Paley functions defined by the semigroups associated with the operator 𝒜 = − 1 2 Δ − x ∇ \mathcal{A}=-\frac{1}{2}\Delta-x\nabla in the inverse Gaussian setting for Banach valued functions. We characterize the uniformly convex and smooth Banach spaces by using L p ​ ( R n , γ − 1 ) L^{p}(\mathbb R^{n},\gamma_{-1}) - properties of the 𝒜 \mathcal{A} -Littlewood-Paley functions. We also use Littlewood-Paley functions associated with 𝒜 \mathcal{A} to characterize the Köthe function spaces with the UMD property.

Introduction

Let
\{T_{t}\}_{t>0}
be a semigroup of operators on a measure space
(\Omega,\mu)
,
k\in\mathbb N
and
q\in(1,\infty)
. The Littlewood-Paley function
g_{k,\{T_{t}\}_{t>0}}^{q}
associated with
\{T_{t}\}_{t>0}
is defined by

g^{q}_{k,\{T_{t}\}_{t>0}}(f)(x)=\left(\int_{0}^{\infty}\left|t^{k}\partial^{k}_{t}T_{t}f(x)\right|^{q}\frac{dt}{t}\right)^{1/q},\;\;x\in\Omega,

for every
f\in L^{p}(\Omega,\mu)
,
1\leq p<\infty
.

Littlewood-Paley functions, also called
g
-functions, allow us to obtain equivalent norms in
L^{p}(\Omega,\mu)
,
1\leq p<\infty
. This fact makes that
L^{p}
-boundedness properties of operators connected with the semigroup
\{T_{t}\}_{t>0}
can be obtained in a unified way.

Stein developed in
[
30
]
the Littlewood-Paley theory for symmetric diffusion semigroups. He proved that if
\{T_{t}\}_{t>0}
is a symmetric difussion semigroup then, for every
1<p<\infty
and
k\in\mathbb N
there exists
C>0
such that

(1.1)

\frac{1}{C}\|f-E_{0}(f)\|_{p}\leq\|g_{k,\{T_{t}\}_{t>0}}^{2}(f)\|_{p}\leq C\|f\|_{p},\,\,f\in L^{p}(\Omega,\mu).

Actually
C
depends only on
p
and
k
. Here,
\displaystyle E_{0}(f)=\lim_{t\rightarrow\infty}T_{t}(f)
and
E_{0}
is the projection of
L^{p}(\Omega,\mu)
on the fixed point space of
\{T_{t}\}_{t>0}
.

Suppose that

is a Banach space. For every

we denote by

the

can be extended to

as a bounded operator from

into itself that will be also denoted by

.

If

is a positive semigroup of operators on
(\Omega,\mu)

denotes the norm in

Let

be the unit circle. For every

Here

such that, for every

\frac{1}{C}\|f\|_{L^{p}_{X}(\mathbb T)}\leq|\hat{f}(0)|+\|G^{X}(f)\|_{L^{p}(\mathbb T)}\leq C\|f\|_{L^{p}_{X}(\mathbb T)},

is isomorphic to a Hilbert space. Xu (
[
34

) considered

for every

for which one of the inequalities in (

We recall the martingale type and cotype introduced by Pisier (
[
26
]
). A Banach space

Here,

denotes the expectation and
M_{-1}=0

has martingale cotype (respectively, type)

then

(respectively

).

We define, for every martingale

with values in

)

(\mbox{respectively},\;\sup_{n\in\mathbb N}\mathbb E\|M_{n}\|_{X}^{p}\leq C\mathbb E\left[S_{q,X}(M)\right]^{p}).

The vector valued harmonic analysis is closely connected with the geometry of Banach spaces. The modulus of convexity of
X
is defined by

\delta_{X}(\varepsilon)=\inf\left\{1-\left\|\frac{a+b}{2}\right\|_{X}:a,b\in X,\,\|a\|_{X}=\|b\|_{X}=1,\,\|a-b\|_{X}=\varepsilon\right\},\quad 0<\varepsilon<2,

and the modulus of smoothness of
X
is defined by

\rho_{X}(t)=\sup\left\{\frac{\|a+tb\|_{X}+\|a-tb\|_{X}}{2}-1:a,b\in X,\,\|a\|_{X}=\|b\|_{X}=1,\,\right\},\quad t>0.

X
is said to be uniformly convex when
\delta_{X}(\varepsilon)>0
, for every
0<\varepsilon<2
, and to be uniformly smooth when
\displaystyle\lim_{t\rightarrow 0^{+}}\frac{\rho_{X}(t)}{t}=0
. If
q\in(1,\infty)
we say that
X
is
q
-uniformly convex (respectively,
q
-uniformly smooth) when there exists
C>0
such that
\delta_{X}(\varepsilon)\geq C\varepsilon^{q}
,
0<\varepsilon<2
(respectively,
\rho_{X}(t)\leq Ct^{q},\;t>0
).

The convexity and smoothness of a Banach space can be characterized by using
G^{q,X}
and
S_{q,X}

(

Let

be a complete

-finite measure space. A Banach space

consisting of equivalence classes modulus equality almost everywhere with respect to

, for almost all

(b)

For every

, the characteristic function

of

is in

.

Each Köthe function space endowed with the natural order is a Banach lattice. This lattice is
\sigma
-order complete. Furthermore, if

is an order continuous Banach lattice having a weak unity, then

is order isometric to a Köthe function space (

whose infimum is zero is norm-convergent to zero. We refer
[

]
for the main facts about Banach lattices.

We say that a Banach space has the UMD-property when for every (equivalently, for some)
1<p<\infty
there exists

such that

\left\|\sum^{n}_{k=1}\varepsilon_{k}d_{k}\right\|_{L^{p}_{X}(\Omega,\mu)}\leq C\left\|\sum^{n}_{k=1}d_{k}\right\|_{L^{p}_{X}(\Omega,\mu)},

for every martingale difference sequence

and

. UMD Banach spaces are very important in harmonic analysis. Burkholder

for every

,

, can be extended from

to

as a bounded operator from

into itself, for every (equivalently, for some)

. We refer to
[
18
, Chapters 4 and 5]
for information on UMD-spaces.

\displaystyle\leq Ce^{q(\eta+\delta)\frac{|y|^{2}-|x|^{2}}{2}}\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{e^{-t}e^{-\delta q\frac{|y-e^{-t}x|^{2}}{1-e^{-2t}}}}{(1-e^{-2t})^{\frac{nq}{2}}}\frac{dt}{t},\quad x,y\in\mathbb{R}^{n}.

Let
(x,y)\in N_{1}^{c}
.
As in
[
25
, p. 861]
we denote
a=|x|^{2}+|y|^{2}
and
b=2\langle x,y\rangle
. Also we make the change of variable
1-s=e^{-2t}
,
t\in(0,\infty)
, and we define
u(s)=\frac{|y-x\sqrt{1-s}|^{2}}{s}
,
s\in(0,1)
. We have that

\displaystyle[R_{m,k}^{\mathcal{A}}(x,y)]^{q}

\displaystyle\leq Ce^{q(\eta+\delta)\frac{|y|^{2}-|x|^{2}}{2}}\int_{0}^{1}\frac{e^{-q\delta u(s)}}{s^{\frac{nq}{2}}(-\log(1-s))\sqrt{1-s}}ds

\displaystyle\leq Ce^{q(\eta+\delta)\frac{|y|^{2}-|x|^{2}}{2}}\int_{0}^{1}\frac{e^{-q\delta u(s)}}{s^{\frac{nq}{2}+1}\sqrt{1-s}}ds.

Assume now
b\leq 0
. Then,
u(s)\geq\frac{a}{s}-|x|^{2}
,
s\in(0,1)
, and

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{1}\frac{e^{-q\delta u(s)}}{s^{\frac{nq}{2}+1}\sqrt{1-s}}ds

\displaystyle\leq e^{-q\delta|y|^{2}}\int_{0}^{1}\frac{e^{-q\delta(\frac{a}{s}-a})}{s^{\frac{nq}{2}+1}\sqrt{1-s}}ds\leq Ce^{-q\delta|y|^{2}}\int_{0}^{\infty}e^{-q\delta r}\frac{(r+a)^{\frac{nq-1}{2}}}{a^{\frac{nq}{2}}}\frac{dr}{\sqrt{r}}

\displaystyle\leq Ce^{-q\delta|y|^{2}}\int_{0}^{\infty}e^{-q\delta r}(r+1)^{\frac{nq-1}{2}}\frac{dr}{\sqrt{r}}\leq Ce^{-q\delta|y|^{2}},

because
a\geq\frac{n}{2}
. We obtain

R_{m,k}^{\mathcal{A}}(x,y)\leq Ce^{-(\eta+\delta)\frac{|x|^{2}}{2}+(\eta-\delta)\frac{|y|^{2}}{2}}.

Suppose that
b>0
. Since
\frac{1}{n}>q(1-\delta)
, we can write (see
[
25
, p. 862]
)

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{1}\frac{e^{-q\delta u(s)}}{s^{\frac{nq}{2}+1}\sqrt{1-s}}ds

\displaystyle=\int_{0}^{1}\left(\frac{e^{-u(s)}}{s^{\frac{n}{2}}}\right)^{\frac{nq-1}{n}}\frac{e^{-(q(\delta-1)+\frac{1}{n})u(s)}}{s^{\frac{3}{2}}\sqrt{1-s}}ds

\displaystyle\leq C\left(\frac{e^{-u_{0}}}{s_{0}^{\frac{n}{2}}}\right)^{\frac{nq-1}{n}}\int_{0}^{1}\frac{e^{-(q(\delta-1)+\frac{1}{n})u(s)}}{s^{\frac{3}{2}}\sqrt{1-s}}ds,

where
s_{0}=2\frac{\sqrt{a^{2}-b^{2}}}{a+\sqrt{a^{2}-b^{2}}}
and
u_{0}=u(s_{0})=\frac{|y|^{2}-|x|^{2}}{2}+\frac{|x+y||x-y|}{2}
.

We now proceed as in the proof of
[
25
, Lemma 2.3]
. Since
a^{2}-b^{2}\geq 1
, we get

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{1}\frac{e^{-(q(\delta-1)+\frac{1}{n})u(s)}}{s^{\frac{3}{2}}\sqrt{1-s}}ds

\displaystyle\leq C\frac{e^{-(q(\delta-1)+\frac{1}{n})u_{0}}}{\sqrt{s_{0}}}\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{e^{-(q(\delta-1)+\frac{1}{n})s}}{(a^{2}-b^{2})^{\frac{1}{4}}}\left(\sqrt{s}+\frac{1}{\sqrt{s}}\right)ds

\displaystyle\leq C\frac{e^{-(q(\delta-1)+\frac{1}{n})u_{0}}}{\sqrt{s_{0}}}.

Then,

\int_{0}^{1}\frac{e^{-q\delta u(s)}}{s^{\frac{nq}{2}+1}\sqrt{1-s}}ds\leq C\frac{e^{-q\delta u_{0}}}{s_{0}^{\frac{nq}{2}}}.

According to
[
25
, p. 863]
, since
|x-y||x+y|\geq n
and
s_{0}\sim\frac{\sqrt{a^{2}-b^{2}}}{a}
, we obtain

\displaystyle R_{m,k}^{\mathcal{A}}(x,y)

\displaystyle\leq C\frac{e^{(\eta+\delta)\frac{|y|^{2}-|x|^{2}}{2}-\delta u_{0}}}{s_{0}^{n/2}}=C\frac{e^{\frac{\eta}{2}(|y|^{2}-|x|^{2})-\frac{\delta}{2}|x+y||x-y|}}{s_{0}^{n/2}}

\displaystyle\leq C\Big(\frac{|x+y|}{|x-y|}\Big)^{n/2}e^{\frac{\eta}{2}(|y|^{2}-|x|^{2})-\frac{\delta}{2}|x+y||x-y|}\leq C|x+y|^{n}e^{\frac{\eta}{2}(|y|^{2}-|x|^{2})-\frac{\delta}{2}|x+y||x-y|}.

By putting together the above estimates we get, when
(x,y)\in N_{1}^{c}
,

(2.5)

R^{\mathcal{A}}_{m,k}(x,y)\leq C\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}e^{-(\eta+\delta)\frac{|x|^{2}}{2}+(\eta-\delta)\frac{|y|^{2}}{2}},&\langle x,y\rangle\leq 0,\\[5.69046pt]
|x+y|^{n}e^{\frac{\eta}{2}(|y|^{2}-|x|^{2})-\frac{\delta}{2}|x+y||x-y|},&\langle x,y\rangle>0.\end{array}\right.

Since
\left||y|^{2}-|x|^{2}\right|\leq|x+y||x-y|
,
x,y\in\mathbb{R}^{n}
, and that we have chosen
\eta-\delta<\frac{2}{p}<\eta+\delta
, we obtain (see
[
24
, p. 501]
)

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb R^{n}}e^{\frac{|x|^{2}}{p}-\frac{|y|^{2}}{p}}R^{\mathcal{A}}_{m,k}(x,y)\mathcal{X}_{N_{1}^{c}}(x,y)dy

\displaystyle\hskip-156.49014pt\leq C\left(\int_{\mathbb R^{n}}e^{\left(\frac{1}{p}-\frac{\eta+\delta}{2}\right)|x|^{2}}e^{-|y|^{2}\left(\frac{1}{p}+\frac{\eta-\delta}{2}\right)}dy+\int_{\mathbb R^{n}}|x+y|^{n}e^{-\left(\frac{\delta}{2}-\left|\frac{\eta}{2}-\frac{1}{p}\right|\right)|x-y||x+y|}dy\right)\leq C,\;\;x\in\mathbb R^{n}.

In a similar way we can see that

\sup_{y\in\mathbb R^{n}}\int_{\mathbb R^{n}}e^{\frac{|x|^{2}-|y|^{2}}{p}}R^{\mathcal{A}}_{m,k}(x,y)\mathcal{X}_{N_{1}^{c}}(x,y)dx<\infty.

It follows that the operator
R_{m,k;p}
defined by

R^{\mathcal{A}}_{m,k;p}(f)(x)=\int_{\mathbb R^{n}}e^{\frac{|x|^{2}-|y|^{2}}{p}}R^{\mathcal{A}}_{m,k}(x,y)\mathcal{X}_{N_{1}^{c}}(x,y)f(y)dy,\;\;x\in\mathbb R^{n},

is bounded from
L^{r}(\mathbb R^{n},dx)
into itself, for every
1\leq r<\infty
. Then, the operator
R^{\mathcal{A}}_{m,k}
is bounded from
L^{p}(\mathbb R^{n},\gamma_{-1})
into itself. Hence,
g_{m,k,\{T_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}\}_{t>0};\,{\rm glob}}^{q}
is bounded from
L^{p}(\mathbb R^{n},\gamma_{-1})
into itself.

We now deal with
g_{m,k,\{T_{t}\}_{t>0};\,{\rm loc}}^{q}
. We define the operator
G_{m,k,\{T^{\mathcal{A}}_{t}\}_{t>0}}
by

G_{m,k,\{T^{\mathcal{A}}_{t}\}_{t>0}}(f)(x)=t^{m+\frac{|k|}{2}}\partial_{t}^{m}\partial_{x}^{k}T_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}(f)(x),\;x\in\mathbb R^{n}\;\;\text{and}\;t>0.

We are going to see that

(a)

G_{m,k,\{T_{t}\}_{t>0}}
defines a bounded operator from
L^{q}(\mathbb R^{n},\gamma_{-1})
into
L^{q}_{L^{q}((0,\infty)\frac{dt}{t})}(\mathbb R^{n},\gamma_{-1})
.

(b)

R_{m,k}^{\mathcal{A}}(x,y)\leq\frac{C}{|x-y|^{n}}
,
(x,y)\in N_{2}
.

(c)

For every
i=1,\ldots,n
,

\left(\int_{0}^{\infty}\left|t^{m+\frac{|k|}{2}}\partial_{t}^{m}\partial_{x_{i}}\partial_{x}^{k}T_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}(x,y)\right|^{q}\frac{dt}{t}\right)^{\frac{1}{q}}\leq\frac{C}{|x-y|^{n+1}},\;\;(x,y)\in N_{2}.

This means that
G_{m,k,\{T^{\mathcal{A}}_{t}\}_{t>0}}
is a local Calderón-Zygmund operator with respect to the Banach space
L^{q}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})
in the inverse Gaussian setting. Salogni
[
28
, §3.2]
considered scalar local Calderón-Zygmund operators in the inverse Gaussian context. The properties continue being valid in a Banach valued setting.

First we show (b). Assume that
(x,y)\in N_{2}
. Then,
||x|^{2}-|y|^{2}|\leq C
and according to (
2.4
) we obtain that

R_{m,k}^{\mathcal{A}}(x,y)^{q}\leq C\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{e^{-\delta q\frac{|x-e^{-t}y|^{2}}{1-e^{-2t}}}}{(1-e^{-2t})^{\frac{nq}{2}}}\frac{e^{-\frac{qt}{2}}}{t}dt.

The argument in the proof of
[
28
, Lemma 3.3.1]
leads to

\int_{0}^{m(x)}\frac{e^{-\delta q\frac{|x-e^{-t}y|^{2}}{1-e^{-2t}}}}{(1-e^{-2t})^{\frac{nq}{2}}}\frac{e^{-\frac{qt}{2}}}{t}dt\leq C\int_{0}^{m(x)}\frac{e^{-c\frac{|x-y|^{2}}{t}}}{t^{\frac{nq}{2}+1}}dt.

Also, we have

\int_{m(x)}^{\infty}\frac{e^{-\delta q\frac{|x-e^{-t}y|^{2}}{1-e^{-2t}}}}{(1-e^{-2t})^{\frac{nq}{2}}}\frac{e^{-\frac{qt}{2}}}{t}dt\leq C\int_{m(x)}^{\infty}\frac{e^{-\frac{qt}{2}}}{(1-e^{-2t})^{\frac{nq}{2}}}\frac{dt}{t}\leq C\int_{m(x)}^{\infty}\frac{dt}{t^{\frac{nq}{2}+1}}\leq\frac{C}{(m(x))^{\frac{nq}{2}}}\leq\frac{C}{|x-y|^{nq}}.

We conclude that

R_{m,k}^{\mathcal{A}}(x,y)\leq\frac{C}{|x-y|^{n}},\;(x,y)\in N_{2}.

Thus (b) is proved. The estimation in (c) can be seen in a similar way.

Next we handle property (a). Let
f\in L^{q}(\mathbb R^{n},\gamma_{-1})
and write
\mathbb{B}_{q}=L^{q}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})
. Since
q\geq 2
, by using Hölder’s inequality we get

\displaystyle\left\|G_{m,k,\{T_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}\}_{t>0}}^{q}(f)\right\|^{q}_{L^{q}_{\mathbb{B}_{q}}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})}

\displaystyle=\int_{\mathbb R^{n}}\int_{0}^{\infty}\Big|t^{m+\frac{|k|}{2}}\partial_{t}^{m}\partial_{x}^{k}T_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}(f)(x)\Big|^{q}\frac{dt}{t}d\gamma_{-1}(x)

\displaystyle\hskip-56.9055pt=\int_{\mathbb R^{n}}\int_{0}^{\infty}\big|t^{m+\frac{|k|}{2}}\partial_{t}^{m}\partial_{x}^{k}T_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}(f)(x)\big|^{2}\sup_{t>0}\big|t^{m+\frac{|k|}{2}}\partial_{t}^{m}\partial_{x}^{k}T_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}(f)(x)\big|^{q-2}\frac{dt}{t}d\gamma_{-1}(x)

\displaystyle\hskip-56.9055pt\leq\left(\int_{\mathbb R^{n}}\big|g^{2}_{m,k,\{T_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}\}_{t>0}}(f)(x)\big|^{q}d\gamma_{-1}(x)\right)^{\frac{2}{q}}\left(\int_{\mathbb R^{n}}\big|T^{\mathcal{A}}_{m,k,*}(f)(x)\big|^{q}d\gamma_{-1}(x)\right)^{1-\frac{2}{q}}.

Here
T^{\mathcal{A}}_{m,k,*}
is the maximal operator defined by

T^{\mathcal{A}}_{m,k,*}f(x)=\sup_{t>0}\Big|t^{m+\frac{|k|}{2}}\partial_{t}^{m}\partial_{x}^{k}T_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}(f)(x)\Big|,\quad x\in\mathbb{R}^{n}.

Thus, to get (a) it is sufficient to see that
g^{2}_{m,k,\{T_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}\}_{t>0}}
and
T^{\mathcal{A}}_{m,k,*}
are bounded from
L^{q}(\mathbb R^{n},\gamma_{-1})
into itself, because these properties allow us to conclude that

\left\|G_{m,k,\{T_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}\}_{t>0}}(f)\right\|^{q}_{L^{q}_{\mathbb{B}_{q}}(\mathbb R^{n},\gamma_{-1}))}\leq C\|f\|^{2}_{L^{q}(\mathbb R^{n},\gamma_{-1})}\|f\|^{q-2}_{L^{q}(\mathbb R^{n},\gamma_{-1})}=C\|f\|^{q}_{L^{q}(\mathbb R^{n},\gamma_{-1})}.

In order to see that
g^{2}_{m,k,\{T_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}\}_{t>0}}
is bounded from
L^{q}(\mathbb R^{n},\gamma_{-1})
into itself we are going to establish the following properties:

(a’)

G_{m,k,\{T_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}\}_{t>0}}
is bounded from
L^{2}(\mathbb R^{n},\gamma_{-1})
into
L^{2}_{\mathbb{B}_{2}}(\mathbb R^{n},\gamma_{-1})
.

(b’)

\big\|t^{m+\frac{k}{2}}\partial_{t}^{m}\partial_{x}^{k}T_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}(x,y)\big\|_{\mathbb{B}_{2}}\leq\frac{C}{|x-y|^{n}}
,
(x,y)\in N_{2}
,

(c’)

For every
i=1\ldots,n
,

\Big\|t^{m+\frac{k}{2}}\partial^{m}_{t}\partial_{x_{i}}\partial_{x}^{k}T_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}(x,y)\Big\|_{\mathbb{B}_{2}}\leq\frac{C}{|x-y|^{n+1}},\quad(x,y)\in N_{2}.

Note that (b’) and (c’) coincide with (b) and (c), respectively, when
q=2
. So, we only need to prove (a’). Let
f\in L^{2}(\mathbb R^{n},\gamma_{-1})
. We have that

T^{\mathcal{A}}_{t}(f)(x)=\sum_{\ell\in\mathbb N^{n}}c_{\ell}(f)e^{-(n+|\ell|)t}\widetilde{H}_{\ell}(x),\quad x\in\mathbb R^{n}.

According to
[
29
, p. 324]
, for every
j\in\mathbb N
,

|\widetilde{H}_{j}(u)|\leq 2\sqrt{2^{j}j!}e^{-\frac{u^{2}}{2}},\quad u\in\mathbb R.

Also,

\|\widetilde{H}_{j}\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb R^{n},\gamma_{-1})}=\sqrt{\pi 2^{j}j!},\quad j\in\mathbb N.

Then, for every
\ell=(\ell_{1},\ldots,\ell_{n})\in\mathbb N^{n}
,

\displaystyle|c_{\ell}(f)|

\displaystyle\leq\frac{C}{\|\widetilde{H}_{\ell}\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb R^{n},\gamma_{-1})}}\int_{\mathbb R^{n}}|f(x)||\widetilde{H}_{\ell}(x)|e^{|x|^{2}}dx\leq C\frac{\|f\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb R^{n},\gamma_{-1})}}{\|\widetilde{H}_{l}\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb R^{n},\gamma{-1})}}.

We get, for every
\ell=(\ell_{1},\ldots,\ell_{n})\in\mathbb N^{n}
,

|c_{\ell}(f)||\widetilde{H}_{\ell}(x)|\leq Ce^{-\frac{|x|^{2}}{2}},\;x\in\mathbb R^{n}.

Hence, for every
t>0
, the series defining
T_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}(f)
is absolute and uniformly convergent in
\mathbb R^{n}
. Also, since
\frac{d}{du}\widetilde{H}_{j}(u)=-\widetilde{H}_{j+1}(u)
,
u\in\mathbb R
and
j\in\mathbb N
, we can derivate under the sum sign to obtain

\partial^{m}_{t}\partial_{x}^{k}T_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}(f)(x)=\sum_{\ell\in\mathbb N^{n}}(-1)^{|k|+n+|\ell|}c_{\ell}(f)e^{-t(n+|\ell|)}(n+|\ell|)^{m}\widetilde{H}_{\ell+k}(x),\quad x\in\mathbb R^{n},\;t>0.

By using Plancherel identity for Hermite polynomials it follows that

\displaystyle\left\|t^{m+\frac{|k|}{2}}\partial^{m}_{t}\partial_{x}^{k}T_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}(f)\right\|^{2}_{L^{2}_{\mathbb{B}_{2}}(\mathbb R^{n},\gamma_{-1})}

\displaystyle=\int^{\infty}_{0}t^{2m+|k|}\int_{\mathbb R^{n}}\big|\partial^{m}_{t}\partial_{x}^{k}T_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}(f)(x)\big|^{2}d\gamma_{-1}(x)\frac{dt}{t}

\displaystyle\hskip-99.58464pt=\int^{\infty}_{0}t^{2m+|k|}\sum_{\ell\in\mathbb N^{n}}|c_{\ell}(f)|^{2}e^{-2t(n+|\ell|)}(n+|\ell|)^{2m}\|\widetilde{H}_{\ell+k}\|^{2}_{L^{2}(\mathbb R^{n},\gamma_{-1})}\frac{dt}{t}

\displaystyle\hskip-99.58464pt=\sum_{\ell\in\mathbb N^{n}}|c_{\ell}(f)|^{2}\|\widetilde{H}_{\ell}\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb R^{n},\gamma_{-1})}^{2}\frac{\|\widetilde{H}_{\ell+k}\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb R^{n},\gamma_{-1})}^{2}}{\|\widetilde{H}_{\ell}\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb R^{n},\gamma_{-1})}^{2}}(n+|\ell|)^{2m}\int_{0}^{\infty}e^{-2t(n+|\ell|)}t^{2m+|k|}\frac{dt}{t}

\displaystyle\hskip-99.58464pt\leq C\sum_{\ell\in\mathbb N^{n}}|c_{\ell}(f)|^{2}\|\widetilde{H}_{\ell}\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb R^{n},\gamma_{-1})}^{2}\frac{\|\widetilde{H}_{\ell+k}\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb R^{n},\gamma_{-1})}^{2}}{\|\widetilde{H}_{\ell}\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb R^{n},\gamma_{-1})}^{2}}\frac{1}{(n+|\ell|)^{|k|}}

\displaystyle\hskip-99.58464pt\leq C\sum_{\ell\in\mathbb N^{n}}\frac{|c_{\ell}(f)|^{2}\|\widetilde{H}_{\ell}\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb R^{n},\gamma_{-1})}^{2}}{(n+|\ell|)^{|k|}}\prod_{j=1}^{n}\frac{(\ell_{j}+k_{j})!}{\ell_{j}!}\leq C\sum_{\ell\in\mathbb N^{n}}\frac{|c_{\ell}(f)|^{2}\|\widetilde{H}_{\ell}\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb R^{n},\gamma_{-1})}^{2}}{(n+|l|)^{|k|}}\prod_{j=1}^{n}\ell_{j}^{k_{j}}

\displaystyle\hskip-99.58464pt\leq C\sum_{\ell\in\mathbb N^{n}}|c_{\ell}(f)|^{2}\|\widetilde{H}_{\ell}\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb R^{n},\gamma_{-1})}^{2}=C\|f\|^{2}_{L^{2}(\mathbb R^{n},\gamma_{-1})}.

Thus, we obtain that
G_{m,k,\{T_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}\}_{t>0}}
is bounded from
L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})
into
L_{\mathbb{B}_{2}}^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})
. According to a vector valued
L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})
) version of
[
28
, Proposition 3.2.5]
we conclude that the operator
G_{m,k,\{T_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}\}_{t>0};\,{\rm loc}}
defined by

G_{m,k,\{T_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}\}_{t>0};\,{\rm loc}}(f)(x)=t^{m+\frac{|k|}{2}}\partial_{t}^{m}\partial_{x}^{k}T_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}(\mathcal{X}_{N_{2}}(x,\cdot)f)(x),\quad x\in\mathbb{R}^{n},\;t>0,

is bounded from
L^{r}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})
into
L^{r}_{\mathbb{B}_{2}}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})
, for every
1<r<\infty
. In other words, we have seen that the operator
g_{m,k,\{T_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}\}_{t>0};\,{\rm loc}}^{2}
is bounded from
L^{r}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})
into itself, for every
1<r<\infty
. As we had proved,
g_{m,k,\{T_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}\}_{t>0};\,{\rm glob}}^{2}
is also bounded from
L^{r}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})
into itself, for every
1<r<\infty
. Hence,
g_{m,k,\{T_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}\}_{t>0}}^{2}
is bounded from
L^{q}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})
into itself.

We now consider the maximal operator

T_{m,k,*}^{\mathcal{A}}(f)(x)=\sup_{t>0}\Big|t^{m+\frac{|k|}{2}}\partial_{t}^{m}\partial_{x}^{k}T_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}(f)(x)\Big|,\quad x\in\mathbb{R}^{n}.

We are going to see that

\mathbb{T}_{m,k,*}^{\mathcal{A}}(f)(x):=\sup_{t>0}\Big|\mathcal{M}(f)(t,x)|,\quad x\in\mathbb{R}^{n},

is bounded from
L^{q}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})
into itself, where

\mathcal{M}(f)(t,x)=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\mathbb{M}(t,x,y)f(y)dy,\quad x\in\mathbb{R}^{n},\;t>0,

and

\mathbb{M}(t,x,y)=\frac{e^{-\frac{t}{2}}}{(1-e^{-2t})^{\frac{n}{2}}}e^{(\eta+\delta)\frac{|y|^{2}-|x|^{2}}{2}}e^{-\delta\frac{|y-e^{-t}x|^{2}}{1-e^{-2t}}},\quad x,y\in\mathbb{R}^{n},\;t>0.

Here,
0<\delta<\eta<1
. We observe that, once we prove this fact, according to (
2.4
) and since
|x-ry|^{2}=|y-rx|^{2}+(1-r^{2})(|x|^{2}+|y|^{2})
,
x,y\in\mathbb{R}^{n}
,
r>0
, we can deduce that
T_{m,k,*}^{\mathcal{A}}
is bounded from
L^{q}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})
into itself.

Consider
\mathcal{M}_{\rm loc}
and
\mathcal{M}_{\rm glob}
defined by

\mathcal{M}_{\rm loc}(f)(t,x)=\mathcal{M}(\mathcal{X}_{N_{\nu}}(x,\cdot)f)(t,x)\quad\mbox{ and }\quad\mathcal{M}_{\rm glob}(f)(t,x)=\mathcal{M}(\mathcal{X}_{N_{\nu}^{c}}(x,\cdot)f)(t,x),\quad x\in\mathbb{R}^{n},\;t>0.

The operators
\mathbb{T}_{m,k,*;\,{\rm loc}}
and
\mathbb{T}_{m,k,*;\,{\rm glob}}
are defined in the obvious way. Here
\nu
will be fixed later.

We first study
\mathbb{T}_{m,k,*;\;{\rm glob}}^{\mathcal{A}}
. Let
\lambda,\alpha\in(0,\infty)
. Suppose that
(u,v)\in N_{\lambda}
, that is,
|u-v|<\lambda n\sqrt{m(u)}
. Then,

|\alpha u-\alpha v|<\alpha\lambda n\sqrt{m(u)}\leq\alpha\lambda n\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\sqrt{m(\alpha u)},&0<\alpha<1,\\[5.69046pt]
\alpha\sqrt{m(\alpha u)},&\alpha\geq 1.\end{array}\right.

Hence,
(\alpha u,\alpha v)\in N_{\alpha\lambda}
, when
0<\alpha<1
, and
(\alpha u,\alpha v)\in N_{\alpha^{2}\lambda}
, if
\alpha\geq 1
. Since
0<\delta<1
we deduce that
(\sqrt{\delta}x,\sqrt{\delta}y)\not\in N_{1}
provided that
(x,y)\not\in N_{\nu}
, with
\nu=\delta^{-1}
.

Let
\nu=\delta^{-1}
. By
[
22
, Proposition 2.1]
we can write, for every
(x,y)\in N_{\nu}^{c}
,

\displaystyle\sup_{t>0}\mathbb{M}(t,x,y)

\displaystyle\leq Ce^{(\eta+\delta)\frac{|y|^{2}-|x|^{2}}{2}}\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}e^{-\delta|y|^{2}},&\langle x,y\rangle\leq 0,\\[5.69046pt]
|x+y|^{n}e^{-\frac{\delta}{2}(|y|^{2}-|x|^{2}+|x+y||x-y|)},&\langle x,y\rangle>0,\end{array}\right.

\displaystyle\leq C\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}e^{-(\eta+\delta)\frac{|x|^{2}}{2}+(\eta-\delta)\frac{|y|^{2}}{2}},&\langle x,y\rangle\leq 0,\\[5.69046pt]
|x+y|^{n}e^{\frac{\eta}{2}(|y|^{2}-|x|^{2})-\frac{\delta}{2}|x+y||x-y|},&\langle x,y\rangle>0.\end{array}\right.

By using that
||y|^{2}-|x|^{2}|\leq|x+y||x-y|
, we obtain

\displaystyle e^{\frac{|x|^{2}}{p}-\frac{|y|^{2}}{p}}\sup_{t>0}\mathbb{M}(t,x,y)

\displaystyle\leq C\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}e^{|x|^{2}(\frac{1}{p}-\frac{\eta+\delta}{2})}e^{|y|^{2}(\frac{\eta-\delta}{2}-\frac{1}{p})},&\langle x,y\rangle\leq 0,\\[5.69046pt]
|x+y|^{n}e^{-(\frac{\delta}{2}-|\frac{\eta}{2}-\frac{1}{p}|)|x+y||x-y|},&\langle x,y\rangle>0,\end{array}\right.\quad(x,y)\in N_{\nu}^{c}.

We choose
0<\delta<\eta<1
such that
\eta-\delta<\frac{2}{p}<\eta+\delta
. It follows that (see
[
24

We recall that

,

,

Let

. We will choose

and hence

Also we get

We choose

. We write

We now take a function

,

We now study the operator
\mathcal{H}_{1}^{1}
. We have that

\displaystyle t\partial_{t}(W_{t}(x-y)-T_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}(x,y))

\displaystyle=-nt\left(\frac{W_{t}(x-y)}{2t}-\frac{T_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}(x,y)}{1-e^{-2t}}\right)

\displaystyle\quad+2t\left(\frac{|x-y|^{2}}{(2t)^{2}}W_{t}(x-y)-\frac{e^{-2t}|x-e^{-t}y|^{2}}{(1-e^{-2t})^{2}}T_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}(x,y)\right)

\displaystyle\quad+2te^{-t}\frac{\langle y,x-e^{-t}y\rangle}{1-e^{-2t}}T_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}(x,y)=:\sum_{j=1}^{3}K_{j}(t,x,y),\;\;\;\;x,y\in\mathbb R^{n},\;t>0.

We are going to estimate
\|K_{j}(\cdot,x,y)\|_{L^{q}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}
,

, for every
(x,y)\in N_{1}
. By performing the change of variable

,

, we obtain

\displaystyle\|K_{3}(\cdot,x,y)\|_{L^{q}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}^{q}

\displaystyle\leq C|y|^{q}\int_{0}^{\infty}t^{q}e^{-q(n+1)t}\frac{e^{-c\frac{|x-e^{-t}y|^{2}}{1-e^{-2t}}}}{(1-e^{-2t})^{\frac{n+1}{2}q}}\frac{dt}{t}

\displaystyle\hskip-91.04872pt\leq C|y|^{q}e^{-c(|x|^{2}-|y|^{2})}\int_{0}^{1}\frac{e^{-c\frac{|x-y|^{2}}{s}}}{s^{\frac{n-1}{2}q}}e^{-cs(|x+y|^{2}+|x-y|^{2})}\frac{ds}{-\log(1-s)},\quad x,y\in\mathbb R^{n}.

2|y|\leq(|x+y|+|x-y|)
,

,

,

Next we deal with
\|K_{j}(\cdot,x,y)\|_{L^{q}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}
,

. Let us first observe that

\displaystyle|K_{1}(t,x,y)|+|K_{2}(t,x,y)|

\displaystyle\leq C\left(\Big(1+\frac{|x-y|^{2}}{t}\Big)W_{t}(x-y)+\frac{t}{1-e^{-2t}}\Big(1+\frac{|x-e^{-t}y|^{2}}{1-e^{-2t}}\Big)T_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}(x,y)\right)

is a dense subspace of
L^{r}_{L^{q}_{X}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}(\mathbb R^{n},\gamma_{-1})
. We can write

\displaystyle\partial_{t}^{\beta}T_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}(Q_{\beta}^{\mathcal{A}}(h))(x)

\displaystyle=\int_{\mathbb R^{n}}\partial_{t}^{\beta}T_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}(x,y)Q_{\beta}^{\mathcal{A}}(h)(y)dy

\displaystyle=\int_{\mathbb R^{n}}\partial_{t}^{\beta}T_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}(x,y)\int_{0}^{\infty}s^{\beta-1}\int_{\mathbb R^{n}}\partial_{s}^{\beta}T_{s}^{\mathcal{A}}(y,z)h(s,z)dzdsdy

\displaystyle=\int_{0}^{\infty}\int_{\mathbb R^{n}}h(s,z)\int_{\mathbb R^{n}}s^{\beta-1}\partial_{s}^{\beta}T_{s}^{\mathcal{A}}(y,z)\partial_{t}^{\beta}T_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}(x,y)dydzds,\quad x\in\mathbb R^{n}\;\mbox{and}\;t>0.

By using the semigroup property we get

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb R^{n}}\partial_{s}^{\beta}T_{s}^{\mathcal{A}}(y,z)\partial_{t}^{\beta}T_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}(x,y)dy

\displaystyle=\partial_{t}^{\beta}\partial_{s}^{\beta}\int_{\mathbb R^{n}}T_{s}^{\mathcal{A}}(y,z)T_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}(x,y)dy

\displaystyle=\partial_{t}^{\beta}\partial_{s}^{\beta}T_{t+s}^{\mathcal{A}}(x,z)=\partial_{u}^{2\beta}T_{u}^{\mathcal{A}}(x,z)_{\big|u=t+s},\quad x,z\in\mathbb R^{n}\;\mbox{and}\;t,s\in(0,\infty).

We consider the operator
L_{\beta}^{\mathcal{A}}
defined by

L_{\beta}^{\mathcal{A}}(h)(t,x)=\int_{0}^{\infty}\int_{\mathbb R^{n}}h(s,z)K_{\beta}^{\mathcal{A}}(t,x;s,z)dz\frac{ds}{s},\;\;\;x\in\mathbb R^{n}\;\mbox{and}\;t>0,

where

Note that

\mathcal{L}_{q,\beta}^{\mathcal{A}}(h)(x)=\|L_{\beta}^{\mathcal{A}}(h)(\cdot,x)\|_{L^{q}_{X}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})},\;\;\;\;x\in\mathbb R^{n}.

We are going to see that
L_{\beta}^{\mathcal{A}}
is bounded from
L^{r}_{L^{q}_{X}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}(\mathbb R^{n},\gamma_{-1})
into itself, for every
1<r<\infty
. We consider
L_{\beta,{\rm loc}}^{\mathcal{A}}
and

defined in the usual way.

We first observe that, since
q^{\prime}/q\geq 1
and
\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{(st)^{\beta q}}{(s+t)^{2\beta q}}\frac{dt}{t}=\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{z^{\beta q-1}}{(1+z)^{2\beta q}}dz=B(\beta q,\beta q)
,
s>0

is a function defined on
(0,\infty)
such that

, then

\displaystyle\Big\|(st)^{\beta}F(t+s)\Big\|_{L^{q^{\prime}}_{L^{q}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}((0,\infty),\frac{ds}{s})}

\displaystyle=C\left(\int_{0}^{\infty}\left(\int_{0}^{\infty}(st)^{\beta q}|F(t+s)|^{q}\frac{dt}{t}\right)^{q^{\prime}/q}\frac{ds}{s}\right)^{1/q^{\prime}}

\displaystyle\hskip-85.35826pt\leq C\left(\int_{0}^{\infty}\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{(st)^{\beta q-1}}{(s+t)^{2\beta q}}|(t+s)^{2\beta}F(t+s)|^{q^{\prime}}dtds\right)^{1/q^{\prime}}

(3.8)

\displaystyle\hskip-85.35826pt=C\left(\int_{0}^{\infty}u^{2\beta(q^{\prime}-q)}|F(u)|^{q^{\prime}}\int_{0}^{u}((u-t)t)^{\beta q-1}dtdu\right)^{1/q^{\prime}}=C\|u^{2\beta}F\|_{L^{q^{\prime}}((0,\infty),\frac{du}{u})}.

We now study the operator
L_{\beta,{\rm glob}}^{\mathcal{A}}
.

According to (

,

(3.9)

|u^{2\beta}\partial_{u}^{2\beta}T_{u}^{\mathcal{A}}(x,z)|\leq C\frac{e^{-\frac{u}{2}}}{(1-e^{-2u})^{\frac{n}{2}}}e^{(\eta-\delta)\frac{|z|^{2}-|x|^{2}}{2}}e^{-\delta\frac{|z-e^{-u}x|^{2}}{1-e^{-2u}}},\quad x,z\in\mathbb R^{n}\;\mbox{and}\;u>0.

By using Minkowski and Hölder’s inequalities we get

\displaystyle\|L_{\beta,{\rm glob}}^{\mathcal{A}}(h)(\cdot,x)\|_{L^{q}_{X}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}

\displaystyle\leq\int_{\mathbb R^{n}}\int_{0}^{\infty}\|K_{\beta}^{\mathcal{A}}(\cdot,x;s,z)\|_{L^{q}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}\|h(s,z)\|_{X}\mathcal{X}_{N_{1}^{c}}(x,z)\frac{ds}{s}dz

\displaystyle\hskip-113.81102pt\leq\int_{\mathbb R^{n}}\Big\|K_{\beta}^{\mathcal{A}}(\cdot,x;\cdot,z)\Big\|_{L^{q^{\prime}}_{L^{q}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}((0,\infty),\frac{ds}{s})}\|h(\cdot,z)\|_{L^{q}_{X}((0,\infty),\frac{ds}{s})}\mathcal{X}_{N_{1}^{c}}(x,z)dz,\quad x\in\mathbb{R}^{n}.

By considering (
3.2
) and (
3.9
) it follows that

\displaystyle\Big\|K_{\beta}^{\mathcal{A}}(\cdot,x;\cdot,z)\Big\|_{L^{q^{\prime}}_{L^{q}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}((0,\infty),\frac{ds}{s})}

\displaystyle=\Big\|(st)^{\beta}\partial_{u}^{2\beta}T_{u}^{\mathcal{A}}(x,z)_{|u=t+s}|\Big\|_{L^{q^{\prime}}_{L^{q}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}((0,\infty),\frac{ds}{s})}

\displaystyle\hskip-113.81102pt\leq C\|u^{2\beta}\partial_{u}^{2\beta}T_{u}^{\mathcal{A}}(x,z)\|_{L^{q^{\prime}}((0,\infty),\frac{du}{u})}\leq Ce^{(\eta-\delta)\frac{|z|^{2}-|x|^{2}}{2}}\Big\|\frac{e^{-\frac{u}{2}}e^{-\delta\frac{|z-e^{-u}x|^{2}}{1-e^{-2u}}}}{(1-e^{-2u})^{\frac{n}{2}}}\Big\|_{L^{q^{\prime}}((0,\infty),\frac{du}{u})}

\displaystyle\hskip-113.81102pt\leq Ce^{(\eta-\delta)\frac{|z|^{2}-|x|^{2}}{2}}\Big\|\frac{e^{-\frac{u}{2}}e^{-\delta\frac{|z-e^{-u}x|^{2}}{1-e^{-2u}}}}{(1-e^{-2u})^{\frac{n}{2}}}\Big\|_{L^{q^{\prime}}((0,\infty),\frac{du}{u})},\quad x,z\in\mathbb{R}^{n}.

Then,

\|L_{\beta,{\rm glob}}^{\mathcal{A}}(h)(\cdot,x)\|_{L^{q}_{X}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}\leq C\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}H(x,z)\|h(\cdot,z)\|_{L^{q}_{X}((0,\infty),\frac{ds}{s})}\mathcal{X}_{N_{1}^{c}}(x,z)dz,\quad x\in\mathbb{R}^{n},

where

H(x,z)=e^{(\eta-\delta)\frac{|z|^{2}-|x|^{2}}{2}}\Big\|\frac{e^{-\frac{u}{2}}e^{-\delta\frac{|z-e^{-u}x|^{2}}{1-e^{-2u}}}}{(1-e^{-2u})^{\frac{n}{2}}}\Big\|_{L^{q^{\prime}}((0,\infty),\frac{du}{u})},\quad x,z\in\mathbb{R}^{n}.

By proceeding as in the estimation (
2.5
) we get that,

H(x,z)\leq C\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}e^{-(\eta+\delta)\frac{|x|^{2}}{2}+(\eta-\delta)\frac{|z|^{2}}{2}},&\langle x,z\rangle\leq 0,\\[5.69046pt]

Let

is bounded from

into itself.

Our objective is to established that

. Recall that the operator

(j,r,s,\ell)\in\mathbb{J}

,

, we get for every

\displaystyle\left|\widetilde{H}_{2r}\Big(\frac{x-e^{-u}z}{\sqrt{1-e^{-2u}}}\Big)-\widetilde{H}_{2r}\Big(\frac{x-z}{\sqrt{2u}}\Big)\right|

\displaystyle\hskip-142.26378pt\leq\sum_{k=1}^{n}\prod_{i=1}^{k-1}\left|\widetilde{H}_{2r_{i}}\Big(\frac{x_{i}-z_{i}}{\sqrt{2u}}\Big)\right|\left|\widetilde{H}_{2r_{k}}\Big(\frac{x_{k}-e^{-u}z_{k}}{\sqrt{1-e^{-2u}}}\Big)-\widetilde{H}_{2r_{k}}\Big(\frac{x_{k}-z_{k}}{\sqrt{2u}}\Big)\right|\prod_{i=k+1}^{n}\left|\widetilde{H}_{2r_{i}}\Big(\frac{x_{i}-e^{-u}z_{i}}{\sqrt{1-e^{-2u}}}\Big)\right|

\displaystyle\hskip-142.26378pt\leq Ce^{-c\frac{|x-z|^{2}}{u}}\sum_{k=1}^{n}\left|\frac{x_{k}-e^{-u}z_{k}}{\sqrt{1-e^{-2u}}}-\frac{x_{k}-z_{k}}{\sqrt{2u}}\right|

\displaystyle\hskip-142.26378pt\leq Ce^{-c\frac{|x-z|^{2}}{u}}\sum_{k=1}^{n}\left(|z_{k}|\sqrt{1-e^{-2u}}+|x_{k}-z_{k}|\Big|\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-e^{-2u}}}-\frac{1}{\sqrt{2u}}\Big|\right)

\displaystyle\hskip-142.26378pt\leq Ce^{-c\frac{|x-z|^{2}}{u}}\sqrt{u}(1+|z|),\quad(x,z)\in N_{1},\;u\in(0,1).

We also have that, when
u\in(0,1)

\left|\frac{e^{-(4\beta+n)u}}{(1-e^{-2u})^{\frac{n}{2}+2\beta}}-\frac{1}{(2u)^{\frac{n}{2}+2\beta}}\right|\leq\left|\frac{e^{-(4\beta+n)u}-1}{(1-e^{-2u})^{\frac{n}{2}+2\beta}}\right|+\left|\frac{1}{(1-e^{-2u})^{\frac{n}{2}+2\beta}}-\frac{1}{(2u)^{\frac{n}{2}+2\beta}}\right|\leq\frac{C}{u^{\frac{n}{2}+2\beta-1}}.

Thus, we conclude that

|I_{2}(u,x,z)|\leq C\frac{e^{-c\frac{|x-z|^{2}}{u}}}{u^{\frac{n}{2}+2\beta}}(\sqrt{u}(1+|z|)+u)\leq C(1+|x|)\frac{e^{-c\frac{|x-z|^{2}}{u}}}{u^{\frac{n}{2}+2\beta-\frac{1}{2}}},\quad(x,z)\in N_{1},\;u\in(0,1).

Also we have that

|I_{2}(u,x,z)|\leq C\left(\frac{e^{-(4\beta+n)u}}{(1-e^{-2u})^{\frac{n}{2}+2\beta}}+\frac{1}{u^{\frac{n}{2}+2\beta}}\right)\leq\frac{C}{u^{\frac{n}{2}+2\beta}},\quad x,z\in\mathbb{R}^{n},\;u\in(0,\infty).

By these estimations we get, since
\sqrt{m(x)}\sim(1+|x|)^{-1}
,
x\in\mathbb{R}^{n}
,

\displaystyle\Big\|u^{2\beta}I_{2}(u,x,z)\Big\|_{L^{q^{\prime}}((0,\infty),\frac{du}{u})}^{q^{\prime}}

\displaystyle\leq C\left((1+|x|)^{q^{\prime}}\int_{0}^{m(x)}\frac{e^{-c\frac{|x-z|^{2}}{u}}}{u^{(\frac{n}{2}-\frac{1}{2})q^{\prime}+1}}du+\int_{m(x)}^{\infty}\frac{du}{u^{\frac{n}{2}q^{\prime}+1}}\right)

\displaystyle\hskip-56.9055pt\leq C\left((1+|x|)^{q^{\prime}}m(x)^{\frac{q^{\prime}}{4}}\int_{0}^{m(x)}\frac{e^{-c\frac{|x-z|^{2}}{u}}}{u^{(\frac{n}{2}-\frac{1}{4})q^{\prime}+1}}du+\frac{1}{m(x)^{\frac{n}{2}q^{\prime}}}\right)

\displaystyle\hskip-56.9055pt\leq C\left(\frac{\sqrt{1+|x|}}{|x-y|^{n-\frac{1}{2}}}\right)^{q^{\prime}},\quad(x,z)\in N_{1}.

Hence, (
3.11
) is established.

We have seen that
L_{\beta}^{\mathcal{A}}
is bounded from
L^{r}_{L^{q}_{X}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}(\mathbb R^{n},\gamma_{-1})
into itself, with
1<r<\infty
, provided that so is
L_{\beta,{\rm loc}}
.

We are going to prove that
L_{\beta,{\rm loc}}
is bounded from
L^{r}_{L^{q}_{X}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}(\mathbb R^{n},\gamma_{-1})
into itself, for every
1<r<\infty
. We use vector valued Calderón-Zygmund theory.

We have that

|\partial_{t}^{2\beta}W_{t}(z)|\leq C\frac{e^{-c|z|^{2}/t}}{t^{n/2+2\beta}},\;\;\;\;z\in\mathbb R^{n}\;\mbox{and}\;t>0.

We get

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb R^{n}}\int_{0}^{\infty}|K_{\beta}(t,x;s,z)|\frac{ds}{s}dz

\displaystyle\leq C\int_{\mathbb R^{n}}\int_{0}^{\infty}(st)^{\beta}\frac{e^{-c\frac{|x-z|^{2}}{s+t}}}{(s+t)^{\frac{n}{2}+2\beta}}\frac{ds}{s}dz

\displaystyle\leq C\int_{\mathbb R^{n}}\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{(st)^{\beta}}{\left(1+\frac{|x-z|^{2}}{s+t}\right)^{\frac{n}{2}+2\beta}(s+t)^{\frac{n}{2}+2\beta}}\frac{ds}{s}dz

\displaystyle\leq C\int_{\mathbb R^{n}}\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{(st)^{\beta}}{\left(s+t+|x-z|^{2}\right)^{\frac{n}{2}+2\beta}}\frac{ds}{s}dz

\displaystyle\leq C\int_{0}^{\infty}(st)^{\beta}\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{\rho^{n-1}}{\left(s+t+\rho^{2}\right)^{\frac{n}{2}+2\beta}}d\rho\frac{ds}{s}

\displaystyle\leq C\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{(st)^{\beta}}{(s+t)^{2\beta}}\frac{ds}{s}=C\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{u^{\beta-1}}{(1+u)^{2\beta}}du=C,\quad x\in\mathbb R^{n}\;\mbox{and}\;t>0.

It follows that

\displaystyle\|L_{\beta}(h)\|^{q}_{L^{q}_{L^{q}_{X}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}(\mathbb R^{n},dx)}

\displaystyle=\int_{\mathbb R^{n}}\int_{0}^{\infty}\left(\int_{\mathbb R^{n}}\int_{0}^{\infty}|K_{\beta}(t,x;s,z)|\|h(s,z)\|_{X}\frac{ds}{s}dz\right)^{q}\frac{dt}{t}dx

\displaystyle\hskip-85.35826pt\leq\int_{\mathbb R^{n}}\int_{0}^{\infty}\int_{\mathbb R^{n}}\int_{0}^{\infty}|K_{\beta}(t,x;s,z)|\|h(s,z)\|_{X}^{q}\frac{ds}{s}dz\left(\int_{\mathbb R^{n}}\int_{0}^{\infty}|K_{\beta}(t,x;s,z)|\frac{ds}{s}dz\right)^{q/q^{\prime}}\frac{dt}{t}dx

\displaystyle\hskip-85.35826pt\leq C\|h\|^{q}_{L^{q}_{L^{q}_{X}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}(\mathbb R^{n},dx)},\quad h\in L^{q}_{L^{q}_{X}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}(\mathbb R^{n},dx).

For every
x,z\in\mathbb R^{n}
,
x\neq z
, we define the operator
K_{\beta}(x,z)
by

K_{\beta}(x,z)(g)(t)=\int_{0}^{\infty}K_{\beta}(t,x;s,z)g(s)\frac{ds}{s},\;\;\;\;t\in(0,\infty).

As before we have that

\int_{0}^{\infty}|K_{\beta}(t,x;s,z)|\frac{ds}{s}\leq C\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{(st)^{\beta}}{(s+t+|x-z|^{2})^{\frac{n}{2}+\beta}}\frac{ds}{s}\leq\frac{C}{|x-z|^{n}},\quad x,z\in\mathbb R^{n},\,x\neq z.

Then, for every
g\in L^{q}_{X}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})
,

\displaystyle\|K_{\beta}(x,z)(g)\|_{L^{q}_{X}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}^{q}

\displaystyle=\int_{0}^{\infty}\left|\int_{0}^{\infty}K_{\beta}(t,x;s,z)g(s)\frac{ds}{s}\right|^{q}\frac{dt}{t}

\displaystyle\hskip-56.9055pt\leq\int_{0}^{\infty}\left(\int_{0}^{\infty}K(t,x;s,z)\frac{ds}{s}\right)^{q/q^{\prime}}\int_{0}^{\infty}K_{\beta}(t,x;s,z)\|g(s)\|^{q}\frac{ds}{s}dt

\displaystyle\hskip-56.9055pt\leq\frac{C}{|x-z|^{(\frac{q}{q^{\prime}}+1)n}}\|g\|^{q}_{L^{q}_{X}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}\leq\frac{C}{|x-z|^{qn}}\|g\|^{q}_{L^{q}_{X}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})},\;\;\;\;x,z\in\mathbb R^{n},\,x\neq z.

Hence,

\|K_{\beta}(x,z)\|_{L^{q}_{X}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})\rightarrow L^{q}_{X}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}\leq\frac{C}{|x-z|^{n}},\quad x,z\in\mathbb R^{n},\,x\neq z.

In a similar way we can see that, for every
i=1,...,n
,

\|\partial_{x_{i}}K_{\beta}(x,z)\|_{L^{q}_{X}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})\rightarrow L^{q}_{X}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}\leq\frac{C}{|x-z|^{n+1}},\quad x,z\in\mathbb R^{n},\,x\neq z,

and

\|\partial_{z_{i}}K_{\beta}(x,z)\|_{L^{q}_{X}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})\rightarrow L^{q}_{X}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}\leq\frac{C}{|x-z|^{n+1}},\quad x,z\in\mathbb R^{n},\,x\neq z,

where
\partial_{x_{i}}K_{\beta}(x,z)
and
\partial_{z_{i}}K_{\beta}(x,z)
are understood as the integral operators defined by the kernels
\partial_{x_{i}}K_{\beta}(t,x;s,z)
and
\partial_{z_{i}}K_{\beta}(t,x;s,z)
, respectively.

We also have that, for every
h\in C_{c}(\mathbb R^{n})\otimes C_{c}(0,\infty)\otimes X
,

K_{\beta}(h)(t,x)=\left(\int_{\mathbb R^{n}}K_{\beta}(x,z)[h(\cdot,x)]dz\right)(t),

for almost all
(t,x)\notin\mathop{\mathrm{supp}}h
. Here the integral is understood in the
L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})
-Bochner sense.
According to vector valued Calderón-Zygmund theory ,
K_{\beta}
defines a bounded operator from
L^{r}_{L^{q}_{X}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}(\mathbb R^{n},dx)
into itself, for every
1<r<\infty
.

By
[
28
, Propositions 3.2.5 and 3.2.7]

K_{\beta,{\rm loc}}
is bounded from
L^{r}_{L^{q}_{X}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}(\mathbb R^{n},\gamma_{-1})
into itself, for every
1<r<\infty
.

The proof of (ii)
\Rightarrow
(i) is thus finished.

Assume now that

\|g_{\{P_{t}\}_{t>0}}^{1,X}(f)\|_{L^{p}_{X}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})}\leq C\|f\|_{L^{p}_{X}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})},\quad f\in L^{p}_{X}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1}).

\displaystyle G(f)(t,x,w)

\displaystyle=t\big[\partial_{t}P_{t,{\rm loc}}^{\mathcal{A}}(f(\cdot,w))(x)-\partial_{t}P_{t,{\rm loc}}(f(\cdot,w))(x)\big]

\Big\|\|G(f)(\cdot,x,w)\|_{L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}\Big\|_{L^{r}_{X}(\mathbb{R}^{n},dx)}\leq C\|f\|_{L^{r}_{X}(\mathbb{R}^{n},dx)},

\big\|g_{\{P_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}\}_{t>0};\,{\rm loc}}^{1,X}(f)-g_{\{P_{t}\}_{t>0};\,{\rm loc}}^{1,X}(f)\big\|_{L^{r}_{X}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})}\leq C\|f\|_{L^{r}_{X}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})},

for every
f\in L^{r}_{X}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})
,
1\leq r<\infty
.
We now consider the operator
\mathcal{M}_{\rm glob}

\mathcal{M}_{\rm glob}(f)(t,x,w)=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}t\partial_{t}P_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}(x,y)f(y,w)\mathcal{X}_{N_{1}^{c}}(x,y)dy,\quad x\in\mathbb{R}^{n},\;t>0,\;w\in\Omega.

) when

Then,

\Big\|\|\mathcal{M}_{\rm glob}(f)(\cdot,x,w)\|_{L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}\Big\|_{L^{r}_{X}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})}\leq C\|f\|_{L^{r}_{X}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})},

.

It follows that

\|g_{\{P_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}\}_{t>0};\,{\rm glob}}^{1,X}(f)\|_{L^{r}_{X}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})}\leq C\|f\|_{L^{r}_{X}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})},

for each
f\in L^{r}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})
and
r\in(1,\infty)
.

Since
(ii)

\displaystyle\|g_{\{P_{t}\}_{t>0};\,{\rm loc}}^{1,X}(f)\|_{L^{p}_{X}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})}

\displaystyle\leq\|g_{\{P_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}\}_{t>0};\,{\rm loc}}^{1,X}(f)-g_{\{P_{t}\}_{t>0};\,{\rm loc}}^{1,X}(f)\|_{L^{p}_{X}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})}

\displaystyle\quad+\|g_{\{P_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}\}_{t>0};\,{\rm loc}}^{1,X}(f)\|_{L^{p}_{X}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})}.

g_{\{P_{t}\}_{t>0};\,{\rm loc}}^{1,X}
is bounded from
L^{p}_{X}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})
into itself, and also from
L^{p}_{X}(\mathbb{R}^{n},dx)
into itself (see
[
28
, Proposition 3.2.5]
).

By using now the corresponding properties of invariance (see
[

\|g_{\{P_{t}\}_{t>0}}^{1,X}(f)\|_{L^{p}_{X}(\mathbb{R}^{n},dx)}\leq C\|f\|_{L^{p}_{X}(\mathbb{R}^{n},dx)},\quad f\in L^{p}_{X}(\mathbb{R}^{n},dx).

By changing

4.2.
Proof of Theorem
1.6

In
[
6
, Theorems 1.4 and 1.5]

\mathcal{R}_{i}^{\mathcal{A}}

For every
i=1,...,n
,
\mathcal{R}_{i}^{\mathcal{A}}
is bounded from
L^{p}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})
into itself, when
1<p<\infty
, and from
L^{1}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})
into
L^{1,\infty}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})
; moreover,
\mathcal{R}_{i}^{\mathcal{A}}
can be extended, in the usual way, to
L^{p}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})\otimes Y
,
1\leq p<\infty
, where
Y
is a Banach space. In
[
6
, Theorem 1.4]
it was established that
Y
has the UMD property if and only if
\mathcal{R}_{i}^{\mathcal{A}}
,
i=1,...,n
, can be extended to
L^{p}_{Y}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})
for some (equivalently, for any)
1<p<\infty
as a bounded operator from
L^{p}_{Y}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})
into itself.

Suppose that
(ii)
holds. Let
i=1,...,n
. We have that, for every
k\in\mathbb{N}^{n}
,
\partial_{x_{i}}\widetilde{H}_{k}=-\widetilde{H}_{k+e_{i}}
, where
e_{i}=(e_{ij})_{j=1}^{n}
, being

,
i=j
, and

, if

\mathcal{A}^{-1/2}f=\sum_{\ell\in\mathbb{N}^{n}}\frac{1}{\sqrt{n+|\ell|}}c_{\ell}(f)\widetilde{H}_{\ell},\quad f\in L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1}),

we have that

\mathcal{R}_{i}^{\mathcal{A}}\widetilde{H}_{k}=-\frac{1}{\sqrt{n+|k|}}\widetilde{H}_{k+e_{i}},\quad k\in\mathbb{N}^{n}.

On the other hand, we can write

P_{t}^{\mathcal{A-I}}(f)=\sum_{\ell\in\mathbb{N}^{n}}e^{-t\sqrt{n+|\ell|-1}}c_{\ell}(f)\widetilde{H}_{\ell},\quad f\in L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1}).

Then we get

\partial_{t}P_{t}^{\mathcal{A-I}}(\mathcal{R}_{i}^{\mathcal{A}}\widetilde{H}_{k})=-\frac{1}{\sqrt{n+|k|}}\partial_{t}P_{t}^{\mathcal{A-I}}(\widetilde{H}_{k+e_{i}})=e^{-t\sqrt{n+|k|}}\widetilde{H}_{k+e_{i}},\quad k\in\mathbb{N}^{n}.

and also, by considering that

P_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}(f)=\sum_{\ell\in\mathbb{N}^{n}}e^{-t\sqrt{n+|\ell|}}c_{\ell}(f)\widetilde{H}_{\ell},\quad f\in L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1}),

it follows that

\partial_{x_{i}}P_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}(\widetilde{H}_{k})=-e^{-t\sqrt{n+|k|}}\widetilde{H}_{k+e_{i}},\quad k\in\mathbb{N}^{n}.

Let us denote by
F={\rm span}\,\{\widetilde{H}_{k}\}_{k\in\mathbb{N}^{n}}
the linear space generated by
\{\widetilde{H}_{k}\}_{k\in\mathbb{N}^{n}}
. For every
f\in F
we have that

(4.1)

\partial_{x_{i}}P_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}(f)=-\partial_{t}P_{t}^{\mathcal{A-I}}(\mathcal{R}_{i}^{\mathcal{A}}(f)).

We recall that
F
is a dense subspace of
L^{p}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})
, for every
1\leq p<\infty
. It is clear that
\mathcal{R}_{i}^{\mathcal{A}}f\in F
, for every
f\in F
and
i=1,...,n
.

Since
X
is order continuous, and by taking into account that, when
n\geq 2
, 0 is not an eigenvalue of
\mathcal{A}-I
, it follows that, for every
f\in L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})\otimes X
and
g\in L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})\otimes X^{*}
,

\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\langle f(x,\cdot),g(x,\cdot)\rangle_{X,X^{*}}d\gamma_{-1}(x)=4\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\int_{0}^{\infty}\langle t\partial_{t}P_{t}^{\mathcal{A-I}}(f(\cdot,w))(x),t\partial_{t}P_{t}^{\mathcal{A-I}}(g(\cdot,w))(x)\rangle\frac{dt}{t}d\gamma_{-1}(x).

As above, by taking into account that

\big\|g_{\{P_{t}^{\mathcal{A-I}}\}_{t>0}}^{1,X^{*}}(h)\big\|_{L^{p^{\prime}}_{X^{*}}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})}\leq C\|h\|_{L^{p^{\prime}}_{X^{*}}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})},\quad h\in L^{p^{\prime}}_{X^{*}}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1}),

we obtain that, for every
f\in(L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})\cap L^{p}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1}))\otimes X
,

(4.2)

\|f\|_{L^{p}_{X}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})}\leq C\big\|g_{\{P_{t}^{\mathcal{A}-I}\}_{t>0}}^{1,X}(f)\big\|_{L^{p}_{X}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})}.

According to (
4.1
) and (
4.2
) we get

\displaystyle\big\|\mathcal{R}_{i}^{\mathcal{A}}(f)\big\|_{L^{p}_{X}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})}

\displaystyle\leq C\big\|g_{\{P_{t}^{\mathcal{A-I}}\}_{t>0}}^{1,X}(\mathcal{R}_{i}^{\mathcal{A}}(f))\big\|_{L^{p}_{X}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})}=C\big\|g_{i,\{P_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}\}_{t>0}}^{X}(f)\big\|_{L^{p}_{X}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})}

\displaystyle\leq C\|f\|_{L^{p}_{X}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})},\quad f\in F\otimes X.

By
[
6
, Theorem 1.4]
we conclude that the space
X
has the UMD property.

We observe that when
n=1
, we have that

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}}\langle(f-E_{0}(f))(x,\cdot),(g-E_{0}(g))(x,\cdot)\rangle_{X,X^{*}}d\gamma_{-1}(x)

\displaystyle\hskip-113.81102pt=4\int_{\mathbb{R}}\int_{0}^{\infty}\langle t\partial_{t}P_{t}^{\mathcal{A-I}}(f(\cdot,w))(x),t\partial_{t}P_{t}^{\mathcal{A-I}}(g(\cdot,w))(x)\rangle\frac{dt}{t}d\gamma_{-1}(x),

for every
f\in L^{2}(\mathbb{R},\gamma_{-1})\otimes X
and
g\in L^{2}(\mathbb{R},\gamma_{-1})\otimes X^{*}
, where
E_{0}(f)
is the projection of
f
into the subspace generated by
\widetilde{H}_{0}
. Property (
4.2
) can be written in the following way

\|f-E_{0}(f)\|_{L^{p}_{X}(\mathbb{R},\gamma_{-1})}\leq C\big\|g_{\{P_{t}^{\mathcal{A}-I}\}_{t>0}}^{1,X}(f)\big\|_{L^{p}_{X}(\mathbb{R},\gamma_{-1})},

for every
f\in(L^{2}(\mathbb{R},\gamma_{-1})\cap L^{p}(\mathbb{R},\gamma_{-1}))\otimes X
. Since
E_{0}(\mathcal{R}_{1}^{\mathcal{A}}(f))=0
, for
f\in F\otimes X
, with the same argument as before we can deduce that
X
is UMD when
n=1
.

Suppose now that the Banach space
X
has the UMD property. Let
i=1,...,n
. Our objective is to see that, for certain
C>0
,

(4.3)

\big\|g_{\{P_{t}^{\mathcal{A}-I}\}_{t>0}}^{1,X^{*}}(f)\big\|_{L^{p^{\prime}}_{X^{*}}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})}\leq C\|f\|_{L^{p^{\prime}}_{X^{*}}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})},\quad f\in L^{p^{\prime}}_{X^{*}}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1}),

and

(4.4)

\big\|g_{i,\{P_{t}\}_{t>0}}^{X}(f)\big\|_{L^{p}_{X}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})}\leq C\|f\|_{L^{p}_{X}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})},\quad f\in L^{p}_{X}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1}).

Let us consider first the estimation for
g_{\{P_{t}^{\mathcal{A}-I}\}_{t>0}}^{1,X^{*}}
. We define the local and global operators in the usual way and write

g_{\{P_{t}^{\mathcal{A-I}}\}_{t>0}}^{1,X^{*}}(f)\leq g_{\{P_{t}^{\mathcal{A-I}}\}_{t>0};\,{\rm glob}}^{1,X^{*}}(f)+g_{\{P_{t}^{\mathcal{A-I}}\}_{t>0};\,{\rm loc}}^{1,X^{*}}(f)-g_{\{P_{t}\}_{t>0};\,{\rm loc}}^{1,X^{*}}(f)+g_{\{P_{t}\}_{t>0};\,{\rm loc}}^{1,X^{*}}(f),

for every
f\in L_{X}^{p}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})
.

By using Minkowski inequality we get, for each
x\in\mathbb{R}^{n}
and
w\in\Omega
,

g_{\{P_{t}^{\mathcal{A-I}}\}_{t>0};\,{\rm glob}}^{1,X^{*}}(f(\cdot,w))(x)\leq C\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\mathcal{X}_{N_{1}^{c}}(x,y)|f(y,w)|\big\|t\partial_{t}P_{t}^{\mathcal{A-I}}(x,y)\big\|_{L^{2}((0,\infty)\frac{dt}{t})}dy,

and

\displaystyle\big|g_{\{P_{t}^{\mathcal{A-I}}\}_{t>0};\,{\rm loc}}^{1,X^{*}}(f(\cdot,w))(x)-g_{\{P_{t}\}_{t>0};\,{\rm loc}}^{1,X^{*}}(f(\cdot,w))(x)\big|

\displaystyle\hskip-170.71652pt\leq C\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\mathcal{X}_{N_{1}}(x,y)|f(y,w)|\big\|t\partial_{t}\big[P_{t}^{\mathcal{A-I}}(x,y)-P_{t}(x-y)\big]\big\|_{L^{2}((0,\infty)\frac{dt}{t})}dy.

We recall that

P_{t}^{\mathcal{A-I}}(x,y)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{\pi}}\int_{0}^{\infty}e^{-u}T_{t^{2}/(4u)}^{\mathcal{A-I}}(x,y)\frac{du}{\sqrt{u}},\quad x,y\in\mathbb{R}^{n},\;t>0.

We obtain, for every
x,y\in\mathbb{R}^{n}
and
t>0
,

t\partial_{t}P_{t}^{\mathcal{A-I}}(x,y)=\frac{t^{2}}{2\sqrt{\pi}}\int_{0}^{\infty}e^{-u}\partial_{v}T_{v}^{\mathcal{A}-I}(x,y)_{|v=\frac{t^{2}}{4u}}\frac{du}{u^{\frac{3}{2}}}=\frac{t}{\sqrt{\pi}}\int_{0}^{\infty}e^{-\frac{t^{2}}{4v}}\partial_{v}T_{v}^{\mathcal{A}-I}(x,y)\frac{dv}{\sqrt{v}}.

Then, Minkowski inequality leads to

\displaystyle\big\|t\partial_{t}P_{t}^{\mathcal{A-I}}(x,y)\big\|_{L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}

\displaystyle\leq C\int_{0}^{\infty}\big\|te^{-\frac{t^{2}}{4v}}\big\|_{L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}|\partial_{v}T_{v}^{\mathcal{A-I}}(x,y)|\frac{dv}{\sqrt{v}}

\displaystyle\hskip-28.45274pt\leq C\int_{0}^{\infty}|\partial_{v}T_{v}^{\mathcal{A-I}}(x,y)|dv=\big\|v\partial_{v}T_{v}^{\mathcal{A}-I}(x,y)\big\|_{L^{1}((0,\infty),\frac{dv}{v})},\quad x,y\in\mathbb{R}^{n}.

We now observe that, since
n\geq 2
, the estimation (
2.5
) is also valid when
m=1
,
k=0
,
q=1
and
\mathcal{A}
is replaced by
\mathcal{A}-I
. Then, for
0<\delta<\eta<1
, we have that

\big\|t\partial_{t}P_{t}^{\mathcal{A-I}}(x,y)\big\|_{L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}\leq C\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}e^{-(\eta+\delta)\frac{|x|^{2}}{2}+(\eta-\delta)\frac{|y|^{2}}{2}},&\langle x,y\rangle\leq 0,\\[5.69046pt]
|x+y|^{n}e^{\frac{\eta}{2}(|y|^{2}-|x|^{2})-\frac{\delta}{2}|x+y||x-y|},&\langle x,y\rangle>0.\end{array}\right.,\quad(x,y)\in N_{1}^{c}.

By choosing
\eta-\delta<\frac{2}{p^{\prime}}<\eta+\delta
we deduce that

\sup_{x\in\mathbb{R}^{n}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}e^{\frac{|x|^{2}-|y|^{2}}{p^{\prime}}}\mathcal{X}_{N_{1}^{c}}(x,y)\big\|t\partial_{t}P_{t}^{\mathcal{A-I}}(x,y)\big\|_{L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}dy<\infty,

and

\sup_{y\in\mathbb{R}^{n}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}e^{\frac{|x|^{2}-|y|^{2}}{p^{\prime}}}\mathcal{X}_{N_{1}^{c}}(x,y)\big\|t\partial_{t}P_{t}^{\mathcal{A-I}}(x,y)\big\|_{L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}dx<\infty.

It follows that the operator
g_{\{P_{t}^{\mathcal{A-I}}\}_{t>0};\,{\rm glob}}^{1,X^{*}}
is bounded from
L^{p^{\prime}}_{X^{*}}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})
into itself.

On the other hand, by proceeding as above and by taking into account that estimation (
3.6
) holds for
q=1
and
\mathcal{A}-I
(because
n\geq 2
) we obtain

\displaystyle\big\|t\partial_{t}\big[P_{t}^{\mathcal{A-I}}(x,y)-P_{t}(x-y)\big]\big\|_{L^{2}((0,\infty)\frac{dt}{t})}

\displaystyle\leq C\Big\|v\partial_{v}[T_{v}^{\mathcal{A}-I}(x,y)-W_{v}(x-y)]\Big\|_{L^{1}((0,\infty),\frac{dv}{v})}

\displaystyle\leq C\frac{\sqrt{1+|x|}}{|x-y|^{n-\frac{1}{2}}},\quad(x,y)\in N_{1}.

We deduce that

\big\|g_{\{P_{t}^{\mathcal{A-I}}\}_{t>0};\,{\rm loc}}^{1,X^{*}}(f(\cdot,w))(x)-g_{\{P_{t}\}_{t>0};\,{\rm loc}}^{1,X^{*}}(f(\cdot,w))(x)\big\|_{L^{p^{\prime}}_{X^{*}}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})}\leq C\|f\|_{L^{p^{\prime}}_{X^{*}}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})},

for every
f\in L^{p^{\prime}}_{X^{*}}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})
.

Finally, since
X^{*}
has the UMD property,
[
19
, Theorem 1.6]
leads to
g_{\{P_{t}\}_{t>0}}^{1,X^{*}}
is a bounded operator from
L^{p^{\prime}}_{X^{*}}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})
into itself.
We can also write

\displaystyle\big\|t\partial_{t}P_{t}(x-y)\big\|_{L^{2}((0,\infty)\frac{dt}{t})}

\displaystyle\leq C\int_{0}^{\infty}|\partial_{v}W_{v}(x-y)|dv\leq C\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{e^{-c\frac{|x-y|^{2}}{v}}}{v^{\frac{n}{2}+1}}dv

\displaystyle\leq\frac{C}{|x-y|^{n}}\leq C\frac{\sqrt{1+|x|}}{|x-y|^{n-\frac{1}{2}}},\quad(x,y)\in N_{1}^{c}.

Thus,
g_{\{P_{t}\}_{t>0};\,{\rm glob}}^{1,X^{*}}
, and as a consequence
g_{\{P_{t}\}_{t>0};\,{\rm loc}}^{1,X^{*}}
, are bounded operators from
L^{p^{\prime}}_{X^{*}}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})
into itself. Thus (
4.3
) is established.

In order to prove (
4.4
) we proceed in the same way. In this case we write

\displaystyle\big\|t\partial_{x_{i}}P_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}(x,y)\big\|_{L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}

\displaystyle=\Big\|\frac{t^{2}}{2\sqrt{\pi}}\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{e^{-\frac{t^{2}}{4u}}}{u^{\frac{3}{2}}}\partial_{x_{i}}T_{u}^{\mathcal{A}}(x,y)du\Big\|_{L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}

\displaystyle\leq C\int_{0}^{\infty}\big\|t^{2}e^{-\frac{t^{2}}{4u}}\big\|_{L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}|\partial_{x_{i}}T_{u}^{\mathcal{A}}(x,y)|\frac{du}{u^{\frac{3}{2}}}

\displaystyle\leq C\int_{0}^{\infty}|\partial_{x_{i}}T_{u}^{\mathcal{A}}(x,y)|\frac{du}{\sqrt{u}},\quad x,y\in\mathbb{R}^{n}.

Since

\partial_{x_{i}}T_{u}^{\mathcal{A}}(x,y)=\frac{2e^{-nu}(x_{i}-e^{-u}y_{i})}{(1-e^{-2u})^{\frac{n}{2}+1}}e^{-\frac{|x-e^{-u}y|^{2}}{1-e^{-2u}}},\quad x,y\in\mathbb{R}^{n},\,u>0,

it follows that

\displaystyle\big\|t\partial_{x_{i}}P_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}(x,y)\big\|_{L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}

\displaystyle\leq C\int_{0}^{\infty}e^{-nu}\frac{e^{-\eta\frac{|x-e^{-u}y|^{2}}{1-e^{-2u}}}}{(1-e^{-2u})^{\frac{n+1}{2}}}\frac{du}{\sqrt{u}}

\displaystyle\leq Ce^{-\eta(|x|^{2}-|y|^{2})}\int_{0}^{\infty}e^{-nu}\frac{e^{-\eta\frac{|y-e^{-u}x|^{2}}{1-e^{-2u}}}}{(1-e^{-2u})^{\frac{n+1}{2}}}\frac{du}{\sqrt{u}},\quad x,y\in\mathbb{R}^{n},

with
0<\eta<1
.

By proceeding as in the estimation of
R_{m,k}^{\mathcal{A}}
in (
2.5
) we get, for very
(x,y)\notin N_{1}
,

\big\|t\partial_{x_{i}}P_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}(x,y)\big\|_{L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}\leq C\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}e^{-\eta|x|^{2}},&\mbox{ if }\langle x,y\rangle\leq 0,\\[5.69046pt]
\displaystyle|x+y|^{n}\exp\Big(-\eta\big(\frac{|x+y||x-y|}{2}-\frac{|y|^{2}-|x|^{2}}{2}\big)\Big),&\mbox{ if }\langle x,y\rangle\geq 0.\end{array}\right.

By choosing
\frac{1}{p}<\eta<1
we can deduce, as before, that
g_{i,\{P_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}\}_{t>0};\,{\rm glob}}^{X}
is bounded from
L^{p}_{X}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})
into itself.

On the other hand, as above, we have that

\big\|t\partial_{x_{i}}[P_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}(x,y)-P_{t}(x-y)]\big\|_{L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}\leq C\int_{0}^{\infty}|\partial_{x_{i}}[T_{u}^{\mathcal{A}}(x,y)-W_{u}(x-y)]|\frac{du}{\sqrt{u}},\quad x,y\in\mathbb{R}^{n}.

We can write

\displaystyle\partial_{x_{i}}(T_{u}^{\mathcal{A}}(x,y)-W_{u}(x-y))

\displaystyle=-\frac{2(x_{i}-y_{i}e^{-u})}{1-e^{-u}}T_{u}^{\mathcal{A}}(x,y)+\frac{x_{i}-y_{i}}{u}W_{u}(x-y)

\displaystyle\hskip-113.81102pt=\frac{x_{i}-y_{i}}{u}(W_{u}(x-y)-T_{u}^{\mathcal{A}}(x,y))+\left(\frac{x_{i}-y_{i}}{u}-\frac{2(x_{i}-y_{i}e^{-u})}{1-e^{-2u}}\right)T_{u}^{\mathcal{A}}(x,y),\quad x,y\in\mathbb{R}^{n},\;u>0.

Then, we get

|\partial_{x_{i}}(T_{u}^{\mathcal{A}}(x,y)-W_{u}(x-y))|\leq C\left(\frac{e^{-nu}}{(1-e^{-2u})^{\frac{n+1}{2}}}+\frac{1}{u^{\frac{n+1}{2}}}\right)\leq\frac{C}{u^{\frac{n+1}{2}}},\quad x,y\in\mathbb{R}^{n},\;u>0.

Also, by using that

\displaystyle\left|\frac{x_{i}-y_{i}}{u}-\frac{2(x_{i}-y_{i}e^{-u})}{1-e^{-2u}}\right|

\displaystyle\leq C\left(|x-y|\left|\frac{1}{2u}-\frac{1}{1-e^{-2u}}\right|+\frac{1}{1-e^{-2u}}|x_{i}-y_{i}-(x_{i}-e^{-u}y_{i})|\right)

\displaystyle\leq C(|x-y|+|y|),\quad x,y\in\mathbb{R}^{n},\;u>0,

the estimation

e^{-\frac{|x-e^{-u}|^{2}}{1-e^{-2u}}}\leq Ce^{-c\frac{|x-y|^{2}}{u}},\quad(x,y)\in N_{1},\,u\in(0,1),

and (
3.1
), we obtain that, when
(x,y)\in N_{1}
and
u\in(0,1)
,

\displaystyle|\partial_{x_{i}}(T_{u}^{\mathcal{A}}(x,y)-W_{u}(x-y))|

\displaystyle\leq Ce^{-c\frac{|x-y|^{2}}{u}}\left(\frac{|x-y|}{u^{\frac{n}{2}}}\Big(1+|y|^{2}+\frac{|y|}{\sqrt{u}}\Big)+\frac{e^{-nu}(|x-y|+|y|)}{(1-e^{-2u})^{\frac{n}{2}}}\right)

\displaystyle\hskip-56.9055pt\leq C\frac{e^{-c\frac{|x-y|^{2}}{u}}}{u^{\frac{n-1}{2}}}\Big(1+|y|^{2}+\frac{|y|}{\sqrt{u}}\Big).

Taking into account (
3.1
), (
3.4
) and (
3.1
) for
q=1
, we get

\displaystyle\big\|t\partial_{x_{i}}[P_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}(x,y)-P_{t}(x-y)]\big\|_{L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}

\displaystyle\hskip-142.26378pt\leq C\left(\int_{0}^{m(x)}\frac{e^{-c\frac{|x-y|^{2}}{u}}}{u^{\frac{n}{2}}}\Big(1+|y|^{2}+\frac{|y|}{\sqrt{u}}\Big)du+\int_{m(x)}^{\infty}\frac{du}{u^{\frac{n}{2}+1}}\right)\leq C\frac{\sqrt{1+|x|}}{|x-y|^{n-\frac{1}{2}}},\quad(x,y)\in N_{1},

and deduce that

\big\|g_{i,\{P_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}\}_{t>0};\,{\rm loc}}^{X}(f(\cdot,w))(x)-g_{i,\{P_{t}\}_{t>0};\,{\rm loc}}^{X}(f(\cdot,w))(x)\big\|_{L^{p}_{X}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})}\leq C\|f\|_{L^{p}_{X}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})},

for every
f\in L^{p}_{X}(\mathbb{R}^{n}\gamma_{-1})
.

We now study
g_{i,\{P_{t}\}_{t>0};\,{\rm loc}}^{X}
by using the vector-valued Calderón-Zygmund theory. We consider the operator
G_{i}
defined by

G_{i}(f)(t,x,w)=t\partial_{x_{i}}P_{t}(f(\cdot,w))(x),\quad x\in\mathbb{R}^{n},\;t>0,\;w\in\Omega.

This is a convolution operator defined by the kernel

K_{t}^{i}(x)=c_{n}\frac{x_{i}t^{2}}{(t^{2}+2|x|^{2})^{\frac{n+3}{2}}},\quad x\in\mathbb{R}^{n},\;t>0,

where
c_{n}=-2^{\frac{n}{2}+1}(n+1)\pi^{-\frac{n+1}{2}}\Gamma(\frac{n+1}{2})
.
The Fourier transforms
\widehat{K_{t}^{i}}
of
K_{t}^{i}
is given by

\widehat{K_{t}^{i}}(y)=Ciy_{i}e^{-ct|y|},\quad y\in\mathbb{R}^{n},\;t>0.

Then,

\big\|\widehat{K_{t}^{i}}(y)\big\|_{L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}=C\left(\int_{0}^{\infty}y_{i}^{2}t^{2}e^{-2ct|y|}\frac{dt}{t}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}\leq C,\quad y\in\mathbb{R}^{n}.

We also have that

\displaystyle\big\|K_{t}^{i}(x)\big\|_{L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}

\displaystyle=c_{n}\left(\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{x_{i}^{2}t^{3}}{(t^{2}+2|x|^{2})^{n+3}}dt\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}

\displaystyle\leq C\left(\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{dt}{(t+|x|)^{2n+1}}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}\leq\frac{C}{|x|^{n}},\quad x\in\mathbb{R}^{n}\setminus\{0\}.

In a similar way we can see that, for
j=1,...,n
,

\big\|\partial_{x_{j}}K_{t}^{i}(x)\big\|_{L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}\leq\frac{C}{|x|^{n+1}},\quad x\in\mathbb{R}^{n}\setminus\{0\}.

By using Bourgain’s results (see
[
8
]
and
[
9
]
) we conclude that the operator
G_{i}
is bounded from
L^{p}_{X}(\mathbb{R}^{n},dx)
into
L^{p}_{X(L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t}))}(\mathbb{R}^{n},dx)
.

Here
X(L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t}))
represents the Köthe Bochner function space consisting of all the measurable functions
g:\Omega\longrightarrow L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})
such that the function

h(w)=\big\|g(w)\big\|_{L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}
,
w\in\Omega,

belongs to
X
.
X(L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t}))
is endowed with the natural norm
\|\cdot\|_{X(L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t}))}
defined by

\|g\|_{X(L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t}))}=\big\|\big\|g(\cdot,w)\big\|_{L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})}\big\|_{X},\quad g\in X(L^{2}((0,\infty),\frac{dt}{t})).

Hence, the operator
g_{i,\{P_{t}\}_{t>0}}^{X}
is bounded from
L^{p}_{X}(\mathbb{R}^{n},dx)
into itself.

According to
[
28
, Propositions 3.2.5 and 3.2.7]
,
g_{i,\{P_{t}\}_{t>0};\,{\rm loc}}^{X}
is bounded from
L^{p}_{X}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})
into itself.

We conclude that
g_{i,\{P_{t}^{\mathcal{A}}\}_{t>0}}^{X}
is bounded from
L^{p}_{X}(\mathbb{R}^{n},\gamma_{-1})
into itself. Thus the property
(ii)
is established.
