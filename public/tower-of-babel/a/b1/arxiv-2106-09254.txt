On Hook Formulas for Cylindric Skew Diagrams
Takeshi Suzuki, Yoshitaka Toyosawa

Abstract
We present a conjectual hook formula concerning the number of the standard tableaux on ”cylindric” skew diagrams. Our formula can be seen as an extension of Naruse’s hook formula for skew diagrams. Moreover, we prove our conjecture in some special cases.

The hook formula gives the number of the standard tableaux on

A generalization of the hook formula

Cylindric diagrams

For
\omega\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geqq 1}\times\mathbb{Z}_{\leqq-1}
,
we let
\mathbb{Z}\omega
denote the subgroup of (the additive group)
\mathbb{Z}^{2}
generated by
\omega
,
and define

\mathcal{C}_{\omega}=\mathbb{Z}^{2}/\mathbb{Z}\omega.

We regard
\mathbb{Z}^{2}
as a poset with the following partial order

(a,b)\leq(a^{\prime},b^{\prime})\iff\hbox{$a\leqq a^{\prime}$ and $b\leqq b^{\prime}$
as integers}.

Then, the cylinder
\mathcal{C}_{\omega}
admits an induced poset structure, namely,

x\leq y\iff\hbox{$\exists\widetilde{x},\widetilde{y}\in\mathbb{Z}^{2}$ such that $\pi(\widetilde{x})=x,\ \pi(\widetilde{y})=y$ and
$\widetilde{x}\leq\widetilde{y}$},

where
\pi:\mathbb{Z}^{2}\to\mathcal{C}_{\omega}
is the natural projection.

Definition 2.1
.

Let
(P,\leq)
be a poset.
A subset
F
of
P
is called an
order filter

if the following condition holds:

x\in F,\ x\leq y\ \Rightarrow\ y\in F.

An order filter
F
is said to be
non-trivial
if
F\neq\emptyset
nor
F\neq P
.

Definition 2.2
.

Let
\omega\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geqq 1}\times\mathbb{Z}_{\leqq-1}
.
A non-trivial order filter
{\theta}
of
\mathcal{C}_{\omega}
is called a
cylindric diagram
.
The inverse image
\pi^{-1}({\theta})\subset{\mathbb{Z}}^{2}
is called a
periodic diagram
of period
\omega
.
An element of cylindric/periodic diagram is called a
cell
.

Note that a cylindric diagram
{\theta}
is a poset and its order filter
\eta

is a cylindric diagram such thet
\eta\subset{\theta}
.

Definition 2.3
.

Let
\theta\subset\mathcal{C}_{\omega}
be a cylindric diagram and

\eta
an order filter of
\theta
.
The set-difference

\theta/\eta:=\theta\backslash\eta

is called a
cylindric skew diagram
.
The inverse image
\pi^{-1}({\theta}/\eta)\subset{\mathbb{Z}}^{2}
is called a

periodic skew diagram
of period
\omega
.

We sometimes parameterize periodic/cylindric diagrams by
\ell
-restricted partitions:

\lambda_{1}\geqq\cdots\geqq\lambda_{\ell},\quad\lambda_{1}-\lambda_{\ell}\leqq\ell.

We

Linear extensions

For two integers
a,b
,
we use the following notation:

[a,b]=\{x\in{\mathbb{Z}}\mid a\leqq x\leqq b\}.

Definition 3.1
.

For a poset
P
such that
|P|=n
,
a
linear extension
(or a
reverse standard tableau
)
of
P
is a bijection

{\varepsilon}:P\to[1,n]
satisfying

x<y\Longrightarrow{\varepsilon}(x)<{\varepsilon}(y).

Let
{\mathrm{RST}}(P)
denote the set of all linear extensions of

P
.

Let
{\lambda},\mu
be partitions such that
{\lambda}\supset\mu
and
|{\lambda}/\mu|=n
.

It is easy to see that
a bijection
{\varepsilon}:{\lambda}/\mu\to[1,n]
is a linear extension on the finite skew diagram
{\lambda}/\mu

if and only if the following conditions hold:

(1)

{\varepsilon}(a,b)>{\varepsilon}(a,b+1)
whenever
(a,b),(a,b+1)\in{\lambda}/\mu
.

(2)

{\varepsilon}(a,b)>{\varepsilon}(a+1,b)
whenever
(a,b),(a+1,b)\in{\lambda}/\mu
.

Fix
m,\ell\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geqq 1}
.

Definition 3.2
.

Let
{\lambda},\mu\in\mathcal{P}_{m,\ell}
.
A linear extension
{\varepsilon}
on
{\lambda}/\mu
is
\ell
-restricted

if it satisfies

{\varepsilon}(1,b)<{\varepsilon}(m,b-\ell)\ \text{
whenever }(1,b),(m,b-\ell)\in{\lambda}/\mu.

We denote by
{\mathrm{RST}}_{\ell}({\lambda}/\mu)
the set of all
\ell
-restricted
linear extensions of
{\lambda}/\mu
.

Note that the projection
\pi:{\mathbb{Z}}^{2}\to\mathcal{C}_{\omega}
gives a bijection

{\lambda}/\mu\to\pi({\lambda}/\mu)={\mathring{{\lambda}}}/{\mathring{\mu}}
and
that a map
{\varepsilon}:{\lambda}/\mu\to[1,n]
induces a map

{\varepsilon}:{\mathring{{\lambda}}}/{\mathring{\mu}}\to[1,n]
.

Lemma 3.3
.

Let
{\lambda},\mu\in\mathcal{P}_{m,\ell}
.
A bijection
{\varepsilon}:{\lambda}/\mu\to[1,n]

induces a linear extension on the cylindric skew diagram
{\mathring{{\lambda}}}/{\mathring{\mu}}

if and only if
{\varepsilon}\in{\mathrm{RST}}_{\ell}({\lambda}/\mu)
.
Namely, the set
{\mathrm{RST}}({\mathring{{\lambda}}}/{\mathring{\mu}})
and
{\mathrm{RST}}_{\ell}({\lambda}/\mu)

are in one to one correspondence.
