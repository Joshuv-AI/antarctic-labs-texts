Behavior of Kreiss bounded C 0 C_{0} -semigroups on a Hilbert space
L. Arnold

Abstract
. Let 0 < α ≤ 1 0<\alpha\leq 1 . We prove that a α \alpha -Kreiss bounded C 0 C_{0} semigroup ( T t ) t ≥ 0 (T_{t})_{t\geq 0} on a Hilbert space has asymptotics ‖ T t ‖ = 𝒪 ⁡ ( t α / l ​ o ​ g ​ ( t ) ) \left\|T_{t}\right\|=\mathcal{O}\big(t^{\alpha}/\sqrt{log(t)}\big) . Primary 47D06 ; Secondary 34D05, 35B45

Let
-A
be the generator of a
C_{0}
-semigroup
(T_{t})_{t\geq 0}
on a complex Banach space
X
. For
\alpha>0
, we say that
(T_{t})_{t\geq 0}
is
\alpha
-Kreiss bounded (or simply Kreiss bounded when
\alpha=1
) if
\sigma(A)\subset\overline{\mathbb{C}_{+}}=\{\lambda\in\mathbb{C}:\,\operatorname{Re}(\lambda)\geq 0\}
and

(1)

\left\|R(\lambda,A)\right\|\leq\frac{C}{(-\operatorname{Re}(\lambda))^{\alpha}},\quad\forall\operatorname{Re}(\lambda)<0.

According to
[
5
, (2.6)]
(see also
[
8
, Theorem 1.1.(1)]
) , if
(T_{t})_{t\geq 0}
is an
\alpha
-Kreiss bounded

C_{0}
-semigroup on a Hilbert space for
\alpha>0
, then
\left\|T_{t}\right\|=O(t^{\alpha})
. Let us fix
\gamma\in(0,1)
. In
[
3
]
, the authors give an example
(see
[
3
, Example 4.4.]
) of Kreiss bounded
C_{0}
-semigroup for which there
exists a constant
C>0
such that
\left\|T_{t}\right\|\geq Ct^{\gamma}
for
t\geq 1
. A natural
question (listed as an open question in
[
7
, Appendix B]
) is whether the
estimate
\left\|T_{t}\right\|=O(t^{\alpha})
, for

\alpha
-Kreiss bounded
C_{0}
-semigroup
(T_{t})_{t\geq 0}
on a Hilbert space, is sharp. In the following we answer this question for
\alpha\in(0,1]
.

Theorem 1
.

Let
0<\alpha\leq 1
and let
-A
be the generator of
\alpha
-Kreiss bounded
C_{0}
-semigroup
(T_{t})_{t\geq 0}
on a Hilbert space.
Then

(2)

\left\|T_{t}\right\|=\underset{t\rightarrow\infty}{\mathcal{O}}\Big(t^{\alpha}/\sqrt{log(t)}\Big).

The proof of the Theorem
1
is based on techniques given in
[
2
]
. We first state and prove two lemmas. It is known (see
[
4
]
or
[
9
]
) that a
C_{0}
-semigroup
(T_{t})_{t\geq 0}
on a Hilbert space, with negative generator
A
, is bounded if and only if

\left\|R(-r+i\cdot,A)x\right\|^{2}_{L^{2}(\mathbb{R},H)}\leq\frac{C}{r}\left\|x\right\|^{2},\quad r>0

and

\left\|R(-r+i\cdot,A^{*})x^{*}\right\|^{2}_{L^{2}(\mathbb{R},H)}\leq\frac{C}{r}\left\|x^{*}\right\|^{2},\quad r>0.

The following lemma gives an analogous necessary condition for a
C_{0}
-semigroup to be
\alpha
-Kreiss bounded, for
\alpha>0
.

Lemma 2
.

Let
\alpha>0
and
(T_{t})_{t\geq 0}
be a
C_{0}
-semigroup on a Hilbert space whose negative generator
A
. Assume
(T_{t})_{t\geq 0}
is
\alpha
-Kreiss bounded then

(3)

\left\|R(-r+i\cdot,A)x\right\|^{2}_{L^{2}(\mathbb{R},H)}\leq\frac{C(1+r^{\alpha})^{2}}{r^{2\alpha}}\left\|x\right\|^{2},\quad r>0

and

(4)

\left\|R(-r+i\cdot,A^{*})x^{*}\right\|^{2}_{L^{2}(\mathbb{R},H)}\leq\frac{C(1+r^{\alpha})^{2}}{r^{2\alpha}}\left\|x^{*}\right\|,\quad r>0.

Proof.

Let
x\in H
. First, we by
[
6
, formula (7.1) section 1.7]
, we have

R(-r+i\beta,A)x=-\int_{0}^{\infty}e^{i\beta s}e^{-rs}T_{s}xds,\quad r>0,\beta\in\mathbb{R}.

This means that for
r>0
,

(5)

R(-r+i\beta,A)x=-\mathcal{F}^{-1}(s\mapsto e^{-rs}T_{s}x)(\beta),

where
\mathcal{F}:L^{2}(\mathbb{R},H)\mapsto L^{2}(\mathbb{R},H)
is the Fourier-Plancherel operator. By Fourier-Plancherel Theorem

(6)

\left\|\mathcal{F}^{-1}(s\mapsto e^{-rs}T_{s}x)(\cdot)\right\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb{R},H)}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}\left\|e^{-r\cdot}T_{(\cdot)}x\right\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb{R}_{+},H)}.

The Gearhart-Prüss Theorem (see
[
1
, Theorem 5.2.1.]
) says that for each

r>0
,
\underset{t>0}{\sup}\left\|e^{-rt}T_{t}\right\|<\infty
. This implies that there exists

K>0
such that

\underset{a>1}{\sup}\left\|e^{-a\cdot}T_{(\cdot)}x\right\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb{R}_{+},H)}\leq K\left\|x\right\|

and then

(7)

\underset{a>1}{\sup}\left\|R(-a+i\beta,A)x\right\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb{R}_{+},H)}\leq\frac{K}{\sqrt{2\pi}}\left\|x\right\|.

Now, let
r>0
and
a=r+1>1
. By the resolvent identity, we have, for
\beta\in\mathbb{R}
,

\displaystyle R(-r+i\beta,A)x

\displaystyle=\big(I+(r-a)R(-r+i\beta,A)\big)R(-a+i\beta,A)x

\displaystyle=\big(I-R(-r+i\beta,A)\big)R(-a+i\beta,A)x.

Using (
1
) and (
7
):

\displaystyle\left\|R(-r+i\cdot,A)x\right\|^{2}_{L^{2}(\mathbb{R},H)}

\displaystyle\leq(1+\sup_{\beta\in\mathbb{R}}\left\|R(-r+i\beta,A)\right\|)^{2}\underset{a^{\prime}<-1}{\sup}\left\|R(-a^{\prime}+i\cdot,A)x\right\|^{2}_{L^{2}(\mathbb{R}_{+},H)}

\displaystyle\leq 2\pi K^{2}\Big(1+\frac{C}{r^{\alpha}}\Big)^{2}\left\|x\right\|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq C^{\prime}\frac{(1+r^{\alpha})^{2}}{r^{2\alpha}}\left\|x\right\|^{2}.

In the same way we obtain (
4
).

∎

The next lemma says that if
(T_{t})_{t\geq 0}
and
(T^{*}_{t})_{t\geq 0}
satisfy a kind of Cesàro condition then
\left\|T_{t}\right\|
satisfy (
2
).

Lemma 3
.

Let
\alpha\in(0,1)
and
(T_{t})_{t\geq 0}
be a
C_{0}
-semigroup on a Banach space
X
. Assume that there exists
C>0
such that for each
t>1
,

\int_{0}^{t}\left\|T_{s}x\right\|^{2}ds\leq Ct^{2\alpha}\left\|x\right\|^{2},\quad x\in X,

and

\int_{0}^{t}\left\|T^{*}_{s}x^{*}\right\|^{2}ds\leq Ct^{2\alpha}\left\|x^{*}\right\|^{2},\quad x^{*}\in X^{*}.

Then,
\left\|T_{t}\right\|=\mathcal{O}(t^{\alpha}/\sqrt{log(t)})
.

Proof.

Let
t>2
and
1<P<Q<t
. We have

\displaystyle(Q-P)^{2}|\langle T_{t}x,x^{*}\rangle|^{2}

\displaystyle=\Big|\int_{P}^{Q}\langle T_{t-s}x,T_{s}^{*}x^{*}\rangle ds\Big|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\Big(\int_{P}^{Q}\left\|T_{t-s}x\right\|^{2}ds\Big)\Big(\int_{P}^{Q}\left\|T_{s}^{*}x^{*}\right\|^{2}ds\Big)

\displaystyle\leq\Big(\int_{P}^{Q}\left\|T_{t-s}x\right\|^{2}ds\Big)\Big(\int_{0}^{Q}\left\|T_{s}^{*}x^{*}\right\|^{2}ds\Big)

\displaystyle\leq CQ^{2\alpha}\left\|x^{*}\right\|^{2}\Big(\int_{t-Q}^{t-P}\left\|T_{s}x\right\|^{2}ds\Big)

Finally taking the supremum over
\{\left\|x^{*}\right\|=1\}
one obtains, since
\alpha\in(0,1]
,

(8)

\displaystyle\frac{(Q-P)^{2}}{Q^{2}}\left\|T_{t}x\right\|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq CQ^{2(\alpha-1)}\int_{t-Q}^{t-P}\left\|T_{s}x\right\|^{2}ds

\displaystyle\leq C\int_{t-Q}^{t-P}\left\|T_{s}x\right\|^{2}ds

Now we set
L:=\left\lfloor log(t)/log(2)\right\rfloor
. For
0\leq l\leq L-1
,
Q=2^{l+1}
and
P=2^{l}
we have

\left\|T_{t}x\right\|^{2}\leq 4C\int_{t-2^{l+1}}^{t-2^{l}}\left\|T_{s}x\right\|^{2}ds

Therefore

L\left\|T_{t}x\right\|^{2}=\sum_{l=0}^{L-1}\left\|T_{t}x\right\|^{2}\leq\sum_{l=0}^{L-1}4C\int_{t-2^{l+1}}^{t-2^{l}}\left\|T_{s}x\right\|^{p}ds\leq 4C\int_{0}^{t}\left\|T_{s}x\right\|^{2}ds\leq 4C^{2}t^{2\alpha}

Hence for
t>2
,

\left\|T_{t}x\right\|\leq\frac{2Ct^{\alpha}}{\sqrt{L}}\left\|x\right\|\leq\frac{C^{\prime}t^{\alpha}}{\sqrt{log(t)}}\left\|x\right\|.

∎

We are now ready to prove Theorem
1
.

Proof of Theorem
1
.

Let
x\in H
and
r>0
. By (
5
), (
6
) and Lemma
2
,

\left\|e^{-r\cdot}T_{(\cdot)}x\right\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb{R}_{+},H)}^{2}=\left\|R(-r+i\cdot,A)x\right\|^{2}_{L^{2}(\mathbb{R},H)}\leq\frac{C(1+r^{\alpha})^{2}}{r^{2\alpha}}\left\|x\right\|^{2}.

Furthermore

\left\|e^{-r\cdot}T_{(\cdot)}x\right\|^{2}_{L^{2}(\mathbb{R}_{+},H)}=\int_{\mathbb{R}}e^{-2rs}\left\|T_{s}x\right\|^{2}ds\geq e^{-2rt}\int_{0}^{t}\left\|T_{s}x\right\|^{2}ds.

Taking
t=\frac{1}{r}
we obtain

\int_{0}^{t}\left\|T_{s}x\right\|^{2}ds\leq Ce^{2}t^{2\alpha}\big(1+\frac{1}{t^{\alpha}}\big)^{2}\left\|x\right\|^{2}.

Hence, for
t>1
,

\int_{0}^{t}\left\|T_{s}x\right\|^{2}ds\leq C^{\prime}t^{2\alpha}\left\|x\right\|^{2},

where
C^{\prime}=4e^{2}C
.
Similarly, since
(T_{t}^{*})_{t\geq 0}
is Kreiss bounded
C_{0}
-semigroup on a Hilbert space, for
t>1
and
x^{*}\in H
, we have

\int_{0}^{t}\left\|T^{*}_{s}x^{*}\right\|^{2}ds\leq C^{\prime}t^{2\alpha}\left\|x^{*}\right\|^{2}.

Finally, Lemma
3
allows us to conclude that
\|T_{t}\|=\mathcal{O}(t^{\alpha}/\sqrt{log(t)})
.

∎

Remark 4
.

(1)

When
\alpha>1
, (
8
) with
Q=2
and
P=1
gives

\left\|T_{t}x\right\|^{2}\leq C2^{\alpha}\int_{t-2}^{t-1}\left\|T_{s}x\right\|^{2}ds\leq C^{\prime}\int_{0}^{t}\left\|T_{s}x\right\|^{2}ds\leq C^{\prime\prime}t^{2\alpha}\left\|x\right\|^{2}.

Then
\left\|T_{t}\right\|=O(t^{\alpha})
, which gives another proof of
[
8
, Theorem 1.1.(1) with
g(s)=s^{\alpha}
for
\alpha>1
]
.

(2)

Let us notice that if
(T_{t})_{t\geq 0}
satisfies the
\alpha
-Kreiss bounded condition only for
\operatorname{Re}(\lambda)\in(0,1)
that is

\left\|R(\lambda,A)\right\|\leq\frac{C}{-\operatorname{Re}(\lambda)^{\alpha}},\quad\forall\operatorname{Re}(\lambda)\in(-1,0),

then the conclusion
\left\|T_{t}\right\|=\mathcal{O}(t^{\alpha}/\sqrt{log(t)})
remains true.

Let give an application of the Theorem
1
. Let
W^{2,s}(\mathbb{T}^{2})
be the second order Sobolev space equipped with the standard norm. Let
A
be the operator with domain
D(A)=W^{2,2}(\mathbb{T}^{2})\times W^{2,1}(\mathbb{T}^{2})
defined by

A=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\
-\Delta-M\frac{\partial}{\partial x}&0\end{pmatrix},

where
\Delta
is the Laplacian with
D(\Delta)=W^{2,2}(\mathbb{T}^{2})
and
M:L^{2}(\mathbb{T}^{2})\rightarrow L^{2}(\mathbb{T}^{2})
is the multiplication operator given by
M(h)(x,y)=e^{iy}h(x,y)
.
Then, it is known (see
[
8
, section 4]
for details) that
-A
generates a

C_{0}
-group
(T_{t})_{t\in\mathbb{R}}
on the Hilbert space
H=W^{1,2}(\mathbb{T}^{2})\times L^{2}(\mathbb{T}^{2})
,
satisfying
\left\|T_{t}\right\|=\underset{t\rightarrow\pm\infty}{\mathcal{O}}(|t|e^{|t|/2})
. With Theorem

1
, we are able to improve this estimate:

Proposition 5
.

Let
(T_{t})_{t\in\mathbb{R}}
be the
C_{0}
-group on
H
generated by
-A
. Then

(9)

\left\|T_{t}\right\|=\underset{t\rightarrow\pm\infty}{\mathcal{O}}\Bigg(\frac{|t|}{\sqrt{log(|t|)}}e^{|t|/2}\Bigg).

Proof.

According to
[
8
, Lemma 4.4.]
, for
\lambda\in\mathbb{C}_{+}
, with
\operatorname{Re}(\lambda)\in(0,1)
,

\left\|R\Big(\lambda,A+\frac{1}{2}\Big)\right\|\leq\frac{C}{-\operatorname{Re}(\lambda)}\quad{and}\quad\left\|R\Big(\lambda,-A+\frac{1}{2}\Big)\right\|\leq\frac{C}{-\operatorname{Re}(\lambda)}.

Then, according to Remark
4

(2)
, the semigroup
(e^{-1/2t}T_{t})_{t\geq 0}
(resp.
(e^{-1/2t}T_{-t})_{t\geq 0}
) which is generated by
-(A+\frac{1}{2})
(resp.
A+\frac{1}{2}
), satisfies
\left\|e^{-1/2t}T_{t}\right\|=\mathcal{O}(t/\sqrt{log(t)})
(resp.
\left\|e^{-1/2t}T_{-t}\right\|=\mathcal{O}(t/\sqrt{log(t)})
). This yields the estimate (
9
).

∎
