some hyperideals defined on the basis of the intersection of all n n -ary prime hyperideals
M. Anbarloei

Abstract
. Various classes of hyperideals have been studied in many papers in order to let us fully understand the structures of hyperrings in general. The purpose of this paper is the study of some hyperideals whose concept is created on the basis of the intersection of all n n -ary prime hyperideals in category of Krasner ( m , n ) (m,n) -hyperrings. In this regard we introduce notions of N N -hyperideals, δ \delta - N N -hyperideals, ( k , n ) (k,n) -absorbing δ \delta - N N -hyperideals and S S - N N -hyperideals. The overall framework of these structures is then explained, providing a number of major conclusions.

{S^{\prime}}^{-1}I\cap H\subseteq E_{s}
.
Since the inclusion
E_{s}\subseteq{S^{\prime}}^{-1}I\cap H
holds, we get
{S^{\prime}}^{-1}I\cap H=E_{s}
.
∎

We have the following corollary of Theorem
6.8
.

Corollary 6.9
.

Let
S
be an
n
-ary multiplicative subset of a commutative Krasner
(m,n)
-hyperring
H
such that
1_{H}\in S
and
I
be a hyperideal of
H
with
I\cap S=\varnothing
. If
I
is an
n
-ary
S
-
N
-hyperideal of
H
, then
S^{-1}I
is an
n
-ary
N
-hyperideal of
S^{-1}H
and
S^{-1}I\cap H=E_{s}
where
E_{s}=\{y\in H\ |\ g(y,s,1^{(n-2)})\in I\}
and
s
is an
S
-element of
I
.

Proof.

Let
I
be an
n
-ary
S
-
N
-hyperideal of
H
. Then, by Theorem
6.8
,
S^{-1}I
is an
n
-ary
S^{-1}S
-N-hyperideal of
S^{-1}H
. Suppose that
G(\frac{a_{1}}{s_{1}},\cdots,\frac{a_{n}}{s_{n}})\in S^{-1}I
for
a_{1}^{n}\in H
and
s_{1}^{n}\in S
. There there exist an
S^{-1}S
-element
\frac{u}{v}
of
S^{-1}I
such that
G(\frac{u}{v},\frac{a_{i}}{s_{i}},\frac{1_{H}}{1_{H}}^{(n-2)})\in\sqrt{0_{S^{-1}H}}^{(m,n)}
or
G(\frac{a_{1}}{s_{1}},\cdots,\frac{a_{i-1}}{s_{i-1}},\frac{u}{v},\frac{a_{i+1}}{s_{i+1}},\cdots,\frac{a_{n}}{s_{n}})\in S^{-1}I
for some
1\leq i\leq n
. Then we get the result that
S^{-1}I
is an
n
-ary
N
-hyperideal of
S^{-1}H
, as
\frac{u}{v}
is invertible. For the second assertion, we use a similar argument to that of Theorem
6.8
.
∎

From Theorem
6.8
and Corollary
6.9
, we can conclude the
following.

Theorem 6.10
.

Let
S
be an
n
-ary multiplicative subset of a commutative Krasner
(m,n)
-hyperring
H
such that
1_{H}\in S
and
I
be a hyperideal of
H
with
I\cap S=\varnothing
. Then
I
is an
n
-ary
S
-
N
-hyperideal of

S^{-1}I
is an
n
-ary
N
-hyperideal of
S^{-1}H
and
S^{-1}I\cap H=E_{s}
and
S^{-1}\sqrt{0}^{(m,n)}\cap H=F_{s^{\prime}}
where
E_{s}=\{y\in H\ |\ g(y,s,1^{(n-2)})\in I\}
and
F_{s^{\prime}}=\{z\in H\ |\ g(z,s^{\prime},1^{(n-2)})\in\sqrt{0}^{(m,n)}\}

s,s^{\prime}\in S

Proof.

\Longrightarrow
Let
I
be an
n
-ary
S
-
N
-hyperideal of
H
. Then, by Corollary
6.9
we conclude that
S^{-1}I
is an
n
-ary
N
-hyperideal of
S^{-1}H
. The rest of the claim follows by a similar argument to that of Theorem
6.8
.

\Longleftarrow
Let
S^{-1}I
be an
n
-ary
N
-hyperideal of
S^{-1}H
,
S^{-1}I\cap H=E_{s}
and
S^{-1}\sqrt{0}^{(m,n)}\cap H=F_{s^{\prime}}
where
E_{s}=\{y\in H\ |\ g(y,s,1_{H}^{(n-2)})\in I\}
and
F_{s^{\prime}}=\{z\in H\ |\ g(z,s^{\prime},1_{H}^{(n-2)})\in\sqrt{0}^{(m,n)}\}
for some

g(s,s^{\prime},1_{H}^{(n-2)})=s^{\prime\prime}

g(a_{1}^{n})\in I

G(\frac{a_{1}}{1_{H}},\cdots,\frac{a_{n}}{1_{H}})\in S^{-1}I

is an
n
-ary
S
-
N
-hyperideal of
H
.
∎
