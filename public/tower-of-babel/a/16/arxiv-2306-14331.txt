(2)

\displaystyle L_{x}(y)=x\cdot y

(3)

Definition 2.2
.

A derivation of associative algebra

is a linear transformation

d(x\cdot y)=d(x)y+xd(y)\quad\quad\forall x,y\in\mathcal{A}

We denote the set of all derivations of an associative algebra

The

is an associative algebra with respect the composition operation

[d_{1},d_{2}]=d_{1}\circ d_{2}-d_{2}\circ d_{1}.

Some properties of Quasi-Centroids and Quasi-Derivations of associative algebras

First we give some properties of center derivation algebras, quasi derivations algebras and quasi-centroid of associative algebras.

Lemma 3.1
.

Let
\mathcal{A}
be an associative algebra. Then

•

Der(\mathcal{A})C(\mathcal{A})\subset C(\mathcal{A})

•

[QDer(\mathcal{A}),QC(\mathcal{A})]\subset QC(\mathcal{A})

•

[QC(\mathcal{A}),QC(\mathcal{A})]\subset QDer(\mathcal{A})

•

Proof.

The conclusions can be easily obtained by the definitions of quasi-derivation and quasi-centroid.
∎

Proposition 3.2
.

If
\mathcal{A}
is an associative algebra, then
[C(\mathcal{A}),\ QC(\mathcal{A})]\subseteq End(\mathcal{A},\ Z(\mathcal{A}))
. Moreover, if
Z(\mathcal{A})=0
, then
[C(\mathcal{A}),\ QC(\mathcal{A})]=0
.

Proof.

Assume that
\phi_{1}\in C(\mathcal{A}),\ \phi_{2}\in QC(\mathcal{A})

and for all
x,\ y\ \in\ \mathcal{A},
we have:

[\phi_{1},\phi_{2}](x)\cdot y\ =\ \phi_{1}\ \phi_{2}(x)\cdot y-\phi_{2}\ \phi_{1}(x)\cdot y

=\ \phi_{1}(\phi_{2}(x)\cdot y)-\phi_{1}(x)\cdot\phi_{2}(y)

=\ \phi_{1}(\phi_{2}(x)\cdot y-x\cdot\phi_{2}(y))=0.

Hence,
[\phi_{1},\ \phi_{2}](x)\in Z(\mathcal{A})
and
[\phi_{1},\ \phi_{2}]\in End(A,\ Z(\mathcal{A}))
as desired. Furthermore, if
Z(\mathcal{A})\ =\ \{0\}
, it is clear that
\ [C(\mathcal{A}),\ QC(\mathcal{A})]=\{0\}.

∎

Let

n

The product of basis is expressed in terms of structure constants as follows

\displaystyle\phi(e_{i})=\sum_{j=1}^{n}a_{ji}e_{j}.

\displaystyle\sum_{p=1}^{n}a_{pi}\delta_{pj}^{q}=\sum_{p=1}^{n}a_{pj}\delta_{ip}^{q}.

(5)

It is observed that if the structure constants

of a associative algebra
\mathcal{A}
are given then in order to describe its quasi-centroid one has to solve the system of equations above with respect to

i,j=1,2,\cdots,n.
