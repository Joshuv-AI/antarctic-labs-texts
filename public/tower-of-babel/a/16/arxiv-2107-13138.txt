The Ground State Energy and Concentration of Complexity in Spherical Bipartite Models
Pax Kivimae

Abstract
We establish an asymptotic formula for the ground-state energy of the spherical pure ( p , q ) (p,q) -spin glass model for p , q ≥ 97 p,q\geq 97 . We achieve this by showing a concentration result for the complexity of critical points with values within a region of the ground state energy. More specifically, we show that the second moment of this count coincides with the square of the first moment up to a sub-exponential factor.

The method of proof is structurally similar to the approach developed in

The main new tools we need to employ in our analysis are relations that we obtain by analyzing the relevant quantities for finite

This not only allows us to remove multiple variables from our minimization, but it allows us to effectively compare this determinant to the semi-circle law by using classical matrix inequalities. In particular, both of these methods result in inequalities involving fixed limiting functions but which appear relatively opaque from this perspective.

Appendix A
Covariance Computations and Conditional Densities

In this appendix we will study the covariance structure of the Gaussian vector

(h_{N}(\textbf{n}),\nabla h_{N}(\textbf{n}),\nabla^{2}h_{N}(\textbf{n}),h_{N}(\textbf{n}(r,t)),\nabla h_{N}(\textbf{n}(r,t))),

(217)

after which we will investigate some of its conditional density functions.

We will be interested specifically in studying the case where the orthonormal frame field
(E_{i})_{i=1}^{N-2}
is induced by a pullback of a choice of an orthonormal frame field on each component of the product. That is, let us write
E^{i}=E_{i+N_{1}-1}
, and assume that
(E_{i})_{i=1}^{N_{1}-1}
is obtained from an orthonormal frame field on the factor
S^{N_{1}-1}

(E^{i})_{i=1}^{N_{2}-1}
is obtained from an orthogonal frame field on the factor
S^{N_{2}-1}
. We observe that in this case, the action of
E^{i}
commutes with
E_{j}
for each
i,j
.

With
\delta_{ij}
denoting the standard Kronecker
\delta
, we denote
\delta_{ijk}=\delta_{ij}\delta_{jk}
,
\delta_{ij\neq 1}=\delta_{ij}(1-\delta_{i1})
, etc. In addition, we employ the shorthand
x_{*}=\sqrt{1-x^{2}}
.

Lemma A.1
.

For
p,q\geq 2
,
0<\gamma<1
and
r,t\in[-1,1]
there exists an orthogonal frame field
E
, such that:

\mathbb{E}[h_{N}(\textbf{n})h_{N}(\textbf{n}(r,t))]=r^{p}t^{q}

(218)

\mathbb{E}[E_{i}h_{N}(\textbf{n})h_{N}(\textbf{n}(r,t))]=-\mathbb{E}[h_{N}(\textbf{n})E_{i}h_{N}(\textbf{n}(r,t))]=pr^{p-1}r_{*}t^{q}\delta_{1i}

(219)

\mathbb{E}[E^{i}h_{N}(\textbf{n})h_{N}(\textbf{n}(r,t))]=-\mathbb{E}[h_{N}(\textbf{n})E^{i}h_{N}(\textbf{n}(r,t))]=qt^{q-1}t_{*}r^{p}\delta_{1i}

(220)

\mathbb{E}[E_{i}h_{N}(\textbf{n})E_{j}h_{N}(\textbf{n}(r,t))]=t^{q}(pr^{p-1}\delta_{ij\neq 1}+\delta_{ij1}pr^{p-2}(pr^{2}-(p-1)))

(221)

\mathbb{E}[E^{i}h_{N}(\textbf{n})E^{j}h_{N}(\textbf{n}(r,t))]=r^{p}(qt^{q-1}\delta_{ij\neq 1}+\delta_{ij1}qt^{q-2}(qt^{2}-(q-1)))

(222)

\mathbb{E}[E^{i}h_{N}(\textbf{n})E_{j}h_{N}(\textbf{n}(r,t))]=\mathbb{E}[E_{i}h_{N}(\textbf{n})E^{j}h_{N}(\textbf{n}(r,t))]=-pqt^{q-1}r^{p-1}r_{*}t_{*}\delta_{ij1}

(223)
