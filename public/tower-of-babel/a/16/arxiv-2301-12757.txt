Spin 𝟐 2 spectrum for marginal deformations of 4d 𝓝 = 𝟐 \mathcal{N}=2 SCFTs
Sourav Roychowdhury, and Dibakar Roychowdhury

Abstract
We compute spin 2 2 spectrum associated with massive graviton fluctuations in γ \gamma -deformed Gaiotto-Maldacena background those are holographically dual to marginal deformations of 𝒩 = 2 \mathcal{N}=2 SCFTs in four dimensions. Under the special circumstances, we analytically estimate the spectra both for the γ \gamma - deformed Abelian T dual (ATD) as well as the non-Abelian T dual (NATD) cases where we retain ourselves upto leading order in the deformation parameter. Our analysis reveals a continuous spectra which is associated with the breaking of the U ⁡ ( 1 ) U(1) isometry (along the directions of the internal manifold) in the presence of the γ \gamma - deformation. We also comment on the effects of adding flavour branes into the picture and the nature of the associated spin 2 2 operators in the dual 𝒩 = 1 \mathcal{N}=1 SCFTs.

Finally, we would like comment on the dual spin 2 operator associated to the

superconformal quiver. As mentioned previously, the
