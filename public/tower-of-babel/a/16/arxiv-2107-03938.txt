Approximately dual pairs of wavelet frames
Ana Benavente, Ole Christensen, Marzieh Hasannasab

Abstract
This paper deals with structural issues concerning wavelet frames and their dual frames. It is known that there exist wavelet frames { a j / 2 ψ ( a j ⋅ − k b ) } j , k ∈ ℤ \{a^{j/2}\psi(a^{j}\cdot-kb)\}_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}} in L 2 ​ ( ℝ ) L^{2}(\mathbb{R}) for which no dual frame has wavelet structure. We first generalize this result by proving that there exist wavelet frames for which no approximately dual frame has wavelet structure. Motivated by this we show that by imposing a very mild decay condition on the Fourier transform of the generator ψ ∈ L 2 ​ ( ℝ ) , \psi\in L^{2}(\mathbb{R}), a certain oversampling { a j / 2 ψ ( a j ⋅ − k b / N ) } j , k ∈ ℤ \{a^{j/2}\psi(a^{j}\cdot-kb/N)\}_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}} indeed has an approximately dual wavelet frame; most importantly, by choosing the parameter N ∈ ℕ N\in\mathbb{N} sufficiently large we can get as close to perfect reconstruction as desired, which makes the approximate dual frame pairs perform equally well as the classical dual frame pairs in applications.



Keywords:
Wavelet frames, approximately dual frames, almost perfect reconstruction

1
Introduction

Given any
a>1
and
b\in\mathbb{R},
consider the associated
scaling operator

D_{a}
and translation operator
T_{b}
acting on
L^{2}(\mathbb{R})
by
D_{a}f(x):=a^{1/2}f(ax),

resp.,
T_{b}f(x)=f(x-b),\,x\in\mathbb{R}.
The
wavelet system
generated by a
function
\psi\in L^{2}(\mathbb{R})
is the collection of functions

\{D_{a^{j}}T_{kb}\psi\}_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}}=\{a^{j/2}\psi(a^{j}\cdot-kb)\}_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}}.

It is well-known
[
16
,
12
]
that even though a wavelet frame always
has a dual frame, there are cases where no dual frame with wavelet structure exists. Thus,
there might not exist a function
\widetilde{\psi}\in L^{2}(\mathbb{R})
such that the

perfect reconstruction formula

\displaystyle f=\sum_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}}\langle f,D_{a^{j}}T_{kb}\widetilde{\psi}\rangle D_{a^{j}}T_{kb}\psi

(1.1)

holds for all
f\in L^{2}(\mathbb{R}).
For this reason we will
consider a weakening of the condition (
1.1
); indeed, following
[
10
]
,
we say that two wavelet systems
\{D_{a^{j}}T_{kb}\psi\}_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}}
and

\{D_{a^{j}}T_{kb}\widetilde{\psi}\}_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}}
form
approximately
dual frames
if both systems form Bessel sequences, and there exists
\epsilon<1
such that

\displaystyle\left|\left|f-\sum_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}}\langle f,D_{a^{j}}T_{kb}\widetilde{\psi}\rangle D_{a^{j}}T_{kb}\psi\right|\right|\leq\epsilon\,||f||,\,\forall f\in L^{2}(\mathbb{R}).

(1.2)

Since real-life applications always involve some truncations and approximations,
approximately dual frames
with sufficiently small parameters
\epsilon

( say, smaller
than machine precision) will perform as well as the classical dual pairs of frames. At
the same time it is a significantly weaker condition to ask for
functions
\psi,\widetilde{\psi}
to generate approximately dual frames, so
giving up the perfect reconstruction property leads to a significant gain
in design freedom.
In our main result
we prove that under a very mild decay condition
on the Fourier transform of a function

\psi\in L^{2}(\mathbb{R})
that generates a wavelet
frame
\{D_{a^{j}}T_{kb}\psi\}_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}},
there
exists
N\in\mathbb{N}
such that the
oversampled wavelet system

\{D_{a^{j}}T_{kb/N}\psi\}_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}}

has an approximately dual wavelet frame; most importantly, by
choosing
N
sufficiently large, we can get as
close to perfect reconstruction as desired.

Approximate duals already appear in a number
of contexts in the literature, see
the papers
[
5
,
3
,
8
,
18
,
19
,
20
]
.
Also, the parallel case of Gabor frames
was considered in the paper
[
9
]
. Even though
some of the techniques are similar, there is a fundamental
difference between the Gabor case and the wavelet
case considered in the current paper: indeed,
in contrast to the wavelet case, a Gabor
frame always has an exact dual frames with the same
structure. For the wavelet case, there is an
extensive literature concerned with the case of
(multi)wavelets where dual frames with the same
structure exists, typically formulated within
the setting of the unitary extension principle
and its later variants.
However, these constructions are based on refinable functions and thus yield a quite limited class.
General dual wavelet frames have been considered
and characterized, e.g., in
[
21
,
4
,
22
]
,
but the literature only contains sparse information
about the case where a wavelet frames does not
have a dual with the same structure.

Our first result, stated in Section
2
, is negative and will serve
as motivation for the rest of the paper: we prove
that there exist wavelet frames, where not even an approximately dual wavelet system exist,
i.e., giving up the perfect reconstruction property by itself does not lead to
sufficient extra design freedom.
Our main new contribution, outlined above, is then developed in Section
3

2
On the existence of approximately dual wavelet frames

The purpose in this Section is to show that there exist wavelet frames for
which no approximate dual wavelet frame exists. The result will be derived
as a consequence of a couple of results that are derived in the setting of frames for a general Hilbert space
{\cal H}.
Since we are aiming at a no-go result, it is enough
to consider a special class of frames, and our results will actually be derived
in the setting of Riesz bases. The first result yields a lower bound for
the deviation from perfect reconstruction that is obtained when the dual Riesz
basis is replaced by any other Bessel sequence in the Hilbert space. For the
sake of our application to wavelet systems we will index the Riesz basis by
an arbitrary countable index set
I.

Proposition 2.1

Let
\{f_{k}\}_{k\in I}
be a Riesz basis for a Hilbert space
{\cal H},
with dual Riesz basis
\{g_{k}\}_{k\in I}
and lower frame
bound
A.
Then, for any Bessel sequence
\{\varphi_{k}\}_{k\in I}
and
any
\ell\in\mathbb{N},

\displaystyle\sup_{||f||=1}\left|\left|f-\sum_{k\in I}\langle f,\varphi_{k}\rangle f_{k}\right|\right|^{2}\geq A\,||g_{\ell}-\varphi_{\ell}||^{2}.

(2.1)

Proof.
For any
f\in{\cal H},
using that
f=\sum_{k\in I}\langle f,g_{k}\rangle f_{k},
we obtain that

\displaystyle\left|\left|f-\sum_{k\in I}\langle f,\varphi_{k}\rangle f_{k}\right|\right|^{2}=\left|\left|\sum_{k\in I}\langle f,g_{k}-\varphi_{k}\rangle f_{k}\right|\right|^{2}\geq A\sum_{k\in I}|\langle f,g_{k}-\varphi_{k}\rangle|^{2}.

Now, if

g_{\ell}=\varphi_{\ell},
the result (
2.1
) clearly holds;
otherwise, taking

f:=(g_{\ell}-\varphi_{\ell})/||g_{\ell}-\varphi_{\ell}||

yields the result.
\square

As a consequence of
Proposition
2.1
we now show that if the vectors
\{g_{k}\}_{k\in I}
in the dual Riesz basis do not
have equal norm, then a sequence

\{\varphi_{k}\}_{k\in I}
of vectors with identical norms can not be an
approximate dual frame for arbitrary small values of
\epsilon:

Corollary 2.2

Let
\{f_{k}\}_{k\in I}
be a Riesz basis with dual Riesz basis
\{g_{k}\}_{k\in I}
and lower frame
bound
A.
Assume that there exists
j,\ell\in I
such that
\delta:=||g_{j}||-||g_{\ell}||>0,
and consider any Bessel sequence
\{\varphi_{k}\}_{k\in I}
consisting of vectors with equal norm. Then

\displaystyle\sup_{||f||=1}\left|\left|f-\sum_{k\in I}\langle f,\varphi_{k}\rangle f_{k}\right|\right|^{2}\geq\frac{A\delta^{2}}{4}.

Proof.
Choose
j,\ell\in I
such that
\delta:=||g_{j}||-||g_{\ell}||>0.
If the vectors
\{\varphi_{k}\}_{k\in I}
have equal norm, then either

||g_{j}-\varphi_{j}||\geq\delta/2
or
||g_{\ell}-\varphi_{\ell}||\geq\delta/2
. Thus
the result follows directly from Proposition
2.1
.
\square

The relevance of Corollary
2.2
for the aim of the section is
immediately clear: indeed, if we take a Riesz basis
\{f_{k}\}_{k\in I}
with
wavelet structure and we aim at another wavelet system
\{\varphi_{k}\}_{k\in I}

being an approximately dual frame, then all the vectors in
\{\varphi_{k}\}_{k\in I}

have equal norm and we can apply the result in Corollary
2.2
directly.
Using this idea we will now exhibit a class of wavelet frames, where
it is impossible to get arbitrarily close to perfect reconstruction
using another wavelet system.
The frame construction itself originally appeared in
[
16
,
12
]
and was
elaborated further in
[
7
]
.

Example 2.3

Let
\{D_{2^{j}}T_{k}\psi\}_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}}
be a wavelet
orthonormal basis for
L^{2}(\mathbb{R})
. Given
\eta\in]0,1[
,
define a function
\theta
by

\displaystyle\theta=\psi+\eta D_{2}\psi.

In
[
16
]
it is proved that
\{D_{2^{j}}T_{k}\theta\}_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}}
is a
Riesz basis and that the dual Riesz basis does not have wavelet structure.
Using that the scaling operator
D_{2}
is unitary and that
\{D_{2^{j}}T_{k}\psi\}_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}}
is an orthonormal system, it follows that for any finite
scalar sequence
\{c_{j,k}\}_{(j,k)\in I},\,I\subset\mathbb{Z}\times\mathbb{Z},

\displaystyle\left|\left|\sum_{(j,k)\in I}c_{j,k}\left(D_{2^{j}}T_{k}\theta-D_{2^{j}}T_{k}\psi\right)\right|\right|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left|\left|\sum_{(j,k)\in I}c_{j,k}D_{2^{j}}T_{k}(\eta D_{2}\psi)\right|\right|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\eta\,\left|\left|\sum_{(j,k)\in I}c_{j,k}D_{2^{j+1}}T_{2k}\psi\right|\right|=\eta\left(\sum_{(j,k)\in I}|c_{j,k}|^{2}\right)^{1/2}.

Using standard perturbation results for frames (see, e.g., Theorem 22.1.1 in
[
7
]
),
it follows that the Riesz basis
\{D_{2^{j}}T_{k}\theta\}_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}}

has lower frame bound
A=(1-\eta)^{2}.
Let
\{\omega_{j,k}\}_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}}

denote
the dual Riesz basis associated with
\{D_{2^{j}}T_{k}\theta\}_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}}
.
In Example 16.1.1 in
[
7
]

it is proved that for any
j,k\in\mathbb{Z}
we have
\omega_{j,2k+1}=D_{2^{j}}T_{2k+1}\psi;

thus
||\omega_{j,2k+1}||=1
for
j,k\in\mathbb{Z}.
It is also proved that

\omega_{j,0}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(-\eta)^{n}D_{2^{j-n}}\psi
for any
j\in\mathbb{Z},

which implies that
||\omega_{j,0}||=(1-\eta^{2})^{-1/2}.
In particular, we see that

\delta:=||\omega_{0,0}||-||\omega_{0,1}||=(1-\eta^{2})^{-1/2}-1.
Now,
consider any wavelet system
\{D_{2^{j}}T_{k}\varphi\}_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}}
in
L^{2}(\mathbb{R}).

Since a wavelet system always consists of
vectors of equal norm, Corollary
2.2
immediately implies that

\displaystyle\sup_{||f||=1}\left|\left|f-\sum_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}}\langle f,D_{2^{j}}T_{k}\varphi\rangle D_{2^{j}}T_{k}\theta\right|\right|^{2}\geq\frac{(1-\eta)^{2}}{4}\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-\eta^{2}}}-1\right)^{2}.

This estimate immediately shows that for the Riesz basis
\{D_{2^{j}}T_{k}\theta\}_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}}
there is a limition on how close we can get to perfect reconstruction using
an “approximate dual wavelet system”
\{D_{2^{j}}T_{k}\varphi\}_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}}
in
L^{2}(\mathbb{R}).

\square

3
Construction of approximately dual frames

Motivated by the negative result in Example
2.3
we will now
develop a new strategy for obtaining pairs of approximately dual wavelet frames.
The key idea is to use
oversampling.
Indeed, our main result
shows that for a large class of wavelet systems

\{D_{a^{j}}T_{kb}\psi\}_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}}
we can get arbitrarily
close to perfect reconstruction by using two wavelet systems that are obtained from
the given one using oversampling.

A key step in our construction of an approximately dual wavelet frames
is to truncate the given function
\psi\in L^{2}(\mathbb{R}).
Throughout the paper,
given
\psi\in L^{2}(\mathbb{R})
and any
K>0,
define the function

\psi_{K}\in L^{2}(\mathbb{R})
by

\displaystyle\widehat{\psi_{K}}:=\widehat{\psi}\,\chi_{[-K,K]}.

(3.1)

We will need the following result, which basically estimates the Bessel bound of the wavelet
system
\{D_{a^{j}}T_{kb}(\psi-\psi_{K})\}_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}}.

Proposition 3.1

Let
a>1
and
b>0
. Let
\psi\in L^{2}(\mathbb{R})
and assume that there exist constants
C>0
and
\sigma>0

such that

\displaystyle|\widehat{\psi}(\gamma)|\leq\frac{C}{1+|\gamma|^{1+\sigma}},\ \ \,\gamma\in\mathbb{R}.

(3.2)

Then

\sup_{|\gamma|\in[1,a]}\sum_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}}|(\widehat{\psi}-\widehat{\psi_{K}})(a^{j}\gamma)(\widehat{\psi}-\widehat{\psi_{K}})(a^{j}\gamma+k/b)|\leq\frac{2C^{2}}{\sigma K^{1+2\sigma}}\left(\frac{\sigma}{K}+b\right)\frac{a^{1+\sigma}}{a^{1+\sigma}-1}.

Proof.
For notational convenience, let
p_{K}(x):=\psi(x)-\psi_{K}(x).
We first note that

\displaystyle\sup_{|\gamma|\in[1,a]}\sum_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}}|\widehat{p_{K}}(a^{j}\gamma)\widehat{p_{K}}(a^{j}\gamma+k/b)|

\displaystyle\leq\left(\sup_{|\gamma|\in[1,a],j\in\mathbb{Z}}\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}}\bigg|\widehat{p_{K}}(a^{j}\gamma+k/b)\bigg|\right)\left(\sup_{|\gamma|\in[1,a]}\sum_{j\in\mathbb{Z}}\bigg|\widehat{p_{K}}(a^{j}\gamma)\bigg|\right)

(3.3)

and
\widehat{p}_{K}(\gamma)=\widehat{\psi}(\gamma)\chi_{[-K,K]^{c}}(\gamma),\,\gamma\in\mathbb{R}.
We now proceed
with a number of estimates that finally yields the result.

Estimate of
\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}}\bigg|\widehat{p}_{N}(a^{j}\gamma+k/b)\bigg|
:

Fix
\gamma\in[-a,-1]\cup[1,a]
and
j\in\mathbb{Z}
. Then
the contribution from

a^{j}\gamma+k/b
hitting the interval
]-\infty,-K]\cup[K,\infty[
is at most

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\left|\widehat{\psi}\left(K+\frac{k}{b}\right)\right|+\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\left|\widehat{\psi}\left(-K-\frac{k}{b}\right)\right|

\displaystyle\leq 2\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{C}{\left(K+\frac{k}{b}\right)^{1+\sigma}}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{2C}{K^{1+\sigma}}+\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{2C}{\left(K+\frac{t}{b}\right)^{1+\sigma}}dt

\displaystyle=2C\left(\frac{1}{K^{1+\sigma}}+\frac{b}{\sigma K^{\sigma}}\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{2C}{\sigma K^{\sigma}}\left(\frac{\sigma}{K}+b\right).

This leads to

\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}}\bigg|\widehat{p}_{N}(a^{j}\gamma+k/b)\bigg|\leq\frac{2C}{\sigma K^{\sigma}}\left(\frac{\sigma}{K}+b\right).

(3.4)

Estimate of
\sum_{j\in\mathbb{Z}}\bigg|\widehat{p}_{N}(a^{j}\gamma)\bigg|
:

Fix
\gamma\in[-a,-1]\cup[1,a]
. Then the contribution from

a^{j}\gamma
hitting the interval
]\infty,-K]\cup[K,\infty[
is at most

\displaystyle\max\left\{\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\left|\widehat{\psi}\left(a^{j}K\right)\right|,\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\left|\widehat{\psi}\left(-a^{j}K\right)\right|\right\}

\displaystyle\leq\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\frac{C}{\left(a^{j}K\right)^{1+\sigma}}=\frac{C}{K^{1+\sigma}}\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{(a^{1+\sigma})^{j}}=\frac{C}{K^{1+\sigma}}\frac{1}{1-\frac{1}{a^{1+\sigma}}}=\frac{Ca^{1+\sigma}}{K^{1+\sigma}\left(a^{1+\sigma}-1\right)}.

This leads to

\sum_{j\in\mathbb{Z}}\bigg|\widehat{p}_{N}(a^{j}\gamma)\bigg|\leq\frac{Ca^{1+\sigma}}{K^{1+\sigma}\left(a^{1+\sigma}-1\right)}.

(3.5)

The result now follows from (
3.3
) together with (
3.4
) and
(
3.5
).

\square

Proposition
3.1
implies that if

\{D_{a^{j}}T_{kb}\psi\}_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}}
is a
frame and (
3.2
) holds, then also

\{D_{a^{j}}T_{kb}\psi_{K}\}_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}}
is a
frame whenever
K\in\mathbb{N}
is sufficiently large.
We are now ready to present our main result.

Theorem 3.2

Assume that for some
a>1,b>0,
the function
\psi\in L^{2}(\mathbb{R})
generates a wavelet frame and
that for some
C>0,\sigma>0,

\displaystyle|\widehat{\psi}(\gamma)|\leq\frac{C}{1+|\gamma|^{1+\sigma}},\ \ \,\gamma\in\mathbb{R}.

(3.6)

Given any
K\in\mathbb{N},
let
N
denote the unique odd
integer such that

\displaystyle N-2\leq 2bK<N,

(3.7)

and define
the function
\widetilde{\psi_{K}}
by

\displaystyle\widehat{\widetilde{\psi_{K}}}(\gamma)=\frac{b\widehat{\psi_{K}}(\gamma)}{N\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}}|\widehat{\psi_{K}}(a^{k}\gamma)|^{2}}.

(3.8)

Then, letting

\displaystyle\varepsilon_{K}:=\left(\frac{K+1/b}{K^{3+2\sigma}}\right)^{1/2}2C\left[(1+\frac{1}{2\sigma})\frac{a^{1+\sigma}}{a^{1+\sigma}-1}\right]^{1/2}

(3.9)

and choosing

K\in\mathbb{N}
large enough such that
\varepsilon_{K}<\sqrt{2bA},
we have

\displaystyle\left|\left|f-\sum_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}}\langle f,D_{a^{j}}T_{kb/N}\widetilde{\psi_{K}}\rangle D_{a^{j}}T_{kb/N}\psi\right|\right|\leq\frac{\varepsilon_{K}}{\sqrt{2bA}-\varepsilon_{K}}\,\left|\left|f\right|\right|,\,\forall f\in L^{2}(\mathbb{R}).

(3.10)

Proof.
Using Proposition
3.1
on the function
\psi-\psi_{K}

and with the translation parameter
b
replaced by

b/N
, (
3.7
) implies that

\displaystyle\frac{N}{b}\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}}\sum_{j\in\mathbb{Z}}|(\widehat{\psi}-\widehat{\psi_{K}})(a^{j}\gamma)(\widehat{\psi}-\widehat{\psi_{K}})(a^{j}\gamma+kN/b)|

\displaystyle\leq\frac{N}{b}\frac{2C^{2}}{\sigma K^{1+2\sigma}}\left(\frac{\sigma}{K}+\frac{b}{N}\right)\frac{a^{1+\sigma}}{a^{1+\sigma}-1}

\displaystyle=\frac{4C^{2}}{K^{2+2\sigma}}\left(\frac{N}{2b}+\frac{K}{2\sigma}\right)\frac{a^{1+\sigma}}{a^{1+\sigma}-1}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{4C^{2}}{K^{2+2\sigma}}\left(K+\frac{1}{b}+\frac{K}{2\sigma}\right)\frac{a^{1+\sigma}}{a^{1+\sigma}-1}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{4C^{2}}{K^{2+2\sigma}}\left(K+\frac{1}{b}\right)\left(1+\frac{1}{2\sigma}\right)\frac{a^{1+\sigma}}{a^{1+\sigma}-1}=:R_{K}.

Thus the wavelet system
\{D_{a^{j}}T_{kb/N}(\psi-\psi_{K})\}_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}}
is a Bessel sequence with bound
R_{K}.
Now, since the
oversampling parameter
N
is odd, the oversampling theorem by
Chui and Shi
[
13
]
shows that the wavelet system

\{D_{a^{j}}T_{kb/N}\psi\}_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}}
is a frame with lower frame
bound
AN;
hence by standard frame perturbation theory (see Cor.
22.1.5 in
[
7
]
),
\{D_{a^{j}}T_{kb/N}\psi_{K}\}_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}}
is
also a frame whenever
R_{K}<AN,
with lower frame bound
AN\left(1-\sqrt{\frac{R_{K}}{AN}}\right)^{2}.
Invoking again the
relationship between
K
and
N
in (
3.7
), we conclude
that in particular
\{D_{a^{j}}T_{kb/N}\psi_{K}\}_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}}
is a
frame whenever
R_{K}<2bAK,
with lower frame bound
AN\left(1-\sqrt{\frac{R_{K}}{2bAK}}\right)^{2}.
By construction,

\widehat{\psi}_{K}
is supported on an interval of length
2K;

hence, again due to (
3.7
) the canonical dual frame of

\{D_{a^{j}}T_{kb/N}\psi_{K}\}_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}}
is

\{D_{a^{j}}T_{kb/N}\widetilde{\psi_{K}}\}_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}},
with

\widetilde{\psi_{K}}
defined as in (
3.8
). Note that the
upper frame bound for

\{D_{a^{j}}T_{kb/N}\widetilde{\psi_{K}}\}_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}}
is
\left[AN\left(1-\sqrt{\frac{R_{K}}{2bAK}}\right)^{2}\right]^{-1}.

Thus, for any
f\in L^{2}(\mathbb{R})
we arrive at

\displaystyle\left|\left|f-\sum_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}}\langle f,D_{a^{j}}T_{kb/N}\widetilde{\psi_{K}}\rangle D_{a^{j}}T_{kb/N}\psi_{K}\right|\right|^{2}

\displaystyle=\left|\left|\sum_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}}\langle f,D_{a^{j}}T_{kb/N}\widetilde{\psi_{K}}\rangle D_{a^{j}}T_{kb/N}\psi_{K}-\sum_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}}\langle f,D_{a^{j}}T_{kb/N}\widetilde{\psi_{K}}\rangle D_{a^{j}}T_{kb/N}\psi\right|\right|^{2}

\displaystyle=\left|\left|\sum_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}}\langle f,D_{a^{j}}T_{kb/N}\widetilde{\psi_{K}}\rangle D_{a^{j}}T_{kb/N}\left(\psi-\psi_{K}\right)\right|\right|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq R_{K}\sum_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}}|\langle f,D_{a^{j}}T_{kb/N}\widetilde{\psi_{K}}|^{2}

\displaystyle=\frac{R_{K}}{AN\left(1-\sqrt{\frac{R_{K}}{2bAK}}\right)^{2}}\,||f||^{2}.

This implies that

\displaystyle\left|\left|f-\sum_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}}\langle f,D_{a^{j}}T_{kb/N}\widetilde{\psi_{K}}\rangle D_{a^{j}}T_{kb/N}\psi_{K}\right|\right|\leq\frac{\sqrt{\frac{R_{K}}{AN}}}{1-\sqrt{\frac{R_{K}}{2bAK}}}\,||f||\leq\frac{\sqrt{\frac{R_{K}}{K}}}{\sqrt{2bA}-\sqrt{\frac{R_{K}}{K}}}\,\left|\left|f\right|\right|;

the proof is now completed
by reformulating the result in terms
of
\varepsilon_{K}=\sqrt{R_{K}/K}.

\square

Remark 3.3

Theorem
3.2
yields a
very explicit relationship between the support-size
of the truncated window, measured by the parameter
K,

and the degree of approximation; in fact,
asymptotically as
K\to\infty
and for some constant

D>0,

\displaystyle\left|\left|f-\sum_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}}\langle f,D_{a^{j}}T_{kb/N}\widetilde{\psi_{K}}\rangle D_{a^{j}}T_{kb/N}\psi\right|\right|\leq\frac{D}{K^{1+\sigma}}\,||f||,\,\forall f\in L^{2}(\mathbb{R}).

(3.11)

In particular, our method allows to us get as close
as desired to perfect reconstruction using
oversampling of the given wavelet system.

Corollary 3.4

Under the assumptions in
Theorem
3.2
, given any
\epsilon>0,

choose
K\in\mathbb{N}
such that

\displaystyle\frac{\varepsilon_{K}}{\sqrt{2bA}-\varepsilon_{K}}\leq\epsilon.

Then, denoting
the synthesis operator for the oversampled frame

\{D_{a^{j}}T_{kb/N}\psi\}_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}}

bu
U_{N}
and the synthesis operator for

\{D_{a^{j}}T_{kb/N}\widetilde{\psi_{K}}\}_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}}

by

W_{N},
we have that

\displaystyle||I-U_{N}W_{N}^{*}||\leq\epsilon.

(3.12)

Let us now return to the motivating Example
2.3
, and show that
the condition in Theorem
3.2

is satisfied when we apply
the construction to the Battle-Lemarié wavelets:

Example 3.5

Let us first recall the key steps in the construction
of the Battle-Lemarié wavelets
[
2
,
23
]
; see, e.g.,
[
17
]

for more details about the construction. The B-splines
N_{m},\,m\in\mathbb{N},
are defined
recursively by
N_{1}:=\chi_{[0,1]}
and the convolution
formula
N_{m+1}=N_{m}*N_{1}.
It is well known that

\widehat{N_{m}}(\gamma)=\left(\frac{1-e^{-2\pi i\gamma}}{2\pi i\gamma}\right)^{m}
and that
\widehat{N_{m}}(2\gamma)=H_{0}(\gamma)\widehat{N_{m}}(\gamma),
where

H_{0}(\gamma)=\left(\frac{1+e^{-2\pi i\gamma}}{2}\right)^{m};

furthermore,
there exist constants
A,B>0
such that
A\leq G(\gamma):=\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}}|\widehat{N_{m}}(\gamma+k)|^{2}\leq B,\ \gamma\in\mathbb{R}.

Define the function
\varphi\in L^{2}(\mathbb{R})

by its Fourier transform
\widehat{\varphi}
via

\widehat{\varphi}(\gamma):=\frac{1}{\sqrt{G(\gamma)}}\,\widehat{N_{m}}(\gamma),\,\gamma\in\mathbb{R}.

Then
\varphi
is a scaling function; indeed, by direct calculation,

\widehat{\varphi}(2\gamma)=\widetilde{H_{0}}(\gamma)\widehat{\varphi}(\gamma),
where

\widetilde{H_{0}}(\gamma):=\sqrt{\frac{G(\gamma)}{G(2\gamma)}}\,H_{0}(\gamma).

The Battle-Lemarié wavelet
\psi
associated with the scaling function
\varphi
is given in the
Fourier domain by the formula

\displaystyle\widehat{\psi}(\gamma)=\overline{\widetilde{H_{0}}(\gamma/2+1/2)}e^{-\pi i\gamma}\widehat{\varphi}(\gamma/2)=\sqrt{\frac{G(\gamma/2+1/2)}{G(\gamma)G(\gamma/2)}}\,\left(\frac{1-\cos\pi\gamma}{\pi i\gamma}\right)^{m}e^{-\pi i\gamma}.

By construction
\{D_{2^{j}}T_{k}\psi\}_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}}
is an orthonormal basis
for
L^{2}(\mathbb{R}).
Given
\eta\in]0,1[
,
define a function
\theta
by
\theta=\psi+\eta D_{2}\psi.

Then, by Example
2.3
,
\{D_{2^{j}}T_{k}\theta\}_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}}
is a
Riesz basis and the dual Riesz basis does not have wavelet structure. Note
that

\displaystyle\widehat{\theta}(\gamma)=\widehat{\psi}(\gamma)+\frac{\eta}{\sqrt{2}}\widehat{\psi}(\gamma/2).

(3.13)

It is clear from (
3.13
) and the formula
for
\widehat{\psi}(\gamma)
that the function
\theta

satisfies the decay condition in Theorem
3.2

for
m\geq 2;
thus, we can
construct approximately dual wavelet frames based on
the Battle-Lemarié wavelets for all

m\geq 2
and any
\eta\in]0,1[,
as claimed.
\square

As already mentioned, the decay condition in Theorem
3.2
is very mild; it is much weaker than
the standard decay conditions used to guarantee
the Bessel condition for a wavelet system, see,
e.g., Theorem 15.2.3 and Lemma 15.2.5 in
[
7
]
.
However, if we remove this condition, we
can not be sure that the truncation
\psi_{K}

of the generator
\psi
for a wavelet frame generates
a wavelet frame itself, regardless
how large
K
is chosen. Based on the subsequent Lemma
3.6
we will provide a concrete
example of this in Example
3.7
.

Lemma 3.6

Let
a>1
and
b>0
. Given a function
\psi\in L^{2}(\mathbb{R})
and
K>0
,
consider the function
\psi_{K}
in (
3.1
), and assume that

\{D_{a^{j}}T_{kb/N}\psi_{K}\}_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}}
is a frame
for some
N\in\mathbb{N}.
Then there
exists
J_{0}\in\mathbb{Z}
such that

\displaystyle\inf_{|\gamma|\in[1,a]}\sum_{j=-\infty}^{J_{0}}\big|\widehat{\psi}(a^{j}{\gamma})\big|^{2}>0.

(3.14)

Proof.
Choose
J_{0}
with
K<a^{J_{0}+1}
.
Due to the compact support of
\widehat{\psi_{K}}
, we have

\inf_{|\gamma|\in[1,a]}\sum_{j\in\mathbb{Z}}\big|\widehat{\psi_{K}}(a^{j}{\gamma})\big|^{2}=\inf_{|\gamma|\in[1,a]}\sum_{j=-\infty}^{J_{0}}\big|\widehat{\psi_{K}}(a^{j}{\gamma})\big|^{2}\leq\inf_{|\gamma|\in[1,a]}\sum_{j=-\infty}^{J_{0}}\big|\widehat{\psi}(a^{j}{\gamma})\big|^{2}.

Now, since
\{D_{a^{j}}T_{kb/N}\psi_{K}\}_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}}

is a frame, a result by Chui and Shi
[
12
]
says that

\inf_{|\gamma|\in[1,a]}\sum_{j\in\mathbb{Z}}\big|\widehat{\psi}(a^{j}{\gamma})\big|^{2}>0.;

hence the desired conclusion follows.

\square

Example 3.7

Let
a=2,b=1,
and let
\{A_{n}\}_{n=0}^{\infty}
be a sequence of measurable sets
such that
\left[\frac{1}{2},1\right[=\biguplus_{n=0}^{\infty}A_{n}
, where

\biguplus
denotes a disjoint union.
Note that for
n\geq 1
, we have that

2^{-n}A_{n}\subset\left[0,\frac{1}{2}\right[
.
Following
[
15
, Example 4.5(xi)]
, consider the sets

\displaystyle B:=\biguplus_{n=0}^{\infty}2^{-n}A_{n},\,\,C:=\left[-1,-\frac{1}{2}\right[\setminus(\biguplus_{n=1}^{\infty}(2^{-n}A_{n}-1)),\,\,D:=\biguplus_{n=1}^{\infty}2^{n}(2^{-n}A_{n}-1),

and define the function
\psi\in L^{2}(\mathbb{R})
by
\widehat{\psi}:=\chi_{B\cup C\cup D}.

It is shown in
[
15
, Example 4.5(xi)]
that
\{D_{2^{j}}T_{k}\psi\}_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}}
is an orthonormal basis. Now, if
J_{0}<-1
, then

\sum_{j=-\infty}^{J_{0}}\big|\widehat{\psi}(2^{j}{\gamma})\big|^{2}=0,\ \forall\gamma\in[-2,-1[;

and if
J_{0}\geq-1
, then

\sum_{j=-\infty}^{J_{0}}\big|\widehat{\psi}(2^{j}{\gamma})\big|^{2}=0,\ \forall\gamma\in\cup_{n=J_{0}+1}^{\infty}\left(2^{-n}A_{n+1}-2\right)\left(\subset\left[-2,-1\right[\right).

Thus, regardless of the choice of
J_{0}\in\mathbb{Z}
, the condition (
3.14
)
is violated. Via Lemma
3.6
we conclude
that

\{D_{a^{j}}T_{kb/N}\psi_{K}\}_{j,k\in\mathbb{Z}}
can not be a frame, regardless how the parameters
K,N\in\mathbb{N}

are chosen.

\square
