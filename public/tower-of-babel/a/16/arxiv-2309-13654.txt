-functor

\operatorname{THH}(-/\mathbb{S}[z];\mathbb{Z}_{p}):\operatorname{Cat_{\infty}^{perf}}(\mathcal{O}_{K})\to\operatorname{Mod}_{\operatorname{THH}(\mathcal{O}_{K}/\mathbb{S}[z];\mathbb{Z}_{p})}(\operatorname{Sp}^{BS^{1}}).

Let us study this functor from
[
BMS19
, section 11]
. By the base change along
\mathbb{S}[z]\to\mathbb{S}[z^{1/{p^{\infty}}}]~|~z\mapsto z
, there is a natural equivalence

(2.4)

\operatorname{THH}(\mathcal{O}_{K}/\mathbb{S}[z])\otimes_{\mathbb{S}[z]}\mathbb{S}[z^{1/{p^{\infty}}}]\simeq\operatorname{THH}(\mathcal{O}_{K}[\pi^{1/p^{\infty}}]/\mathbb{S}[z^{1/{p^{\infty}}}]).

The natural map
\operatorname{THH}(\mathbb{S}[z^{1/{p^{\infty}}}];\mathbb{Z}_{p})\to\mathbb{S}[z^{1/{p^{\infty}}}]^{\wedge}_{p}
is an equivalence (see
[
BMS19
, Proposition. 11.7]
), we obtain equivalences

\displaystyle\operatorname{THH}(\mathcal{O}_{K}[\pi^{1/p^{\infty}}];\mathbb{Z}_{p})

\displaystyle\simeq

\displaystyle\operatorname{THH}(\mathcal{O}_{K}[\pi^{1/p^{\infty}}]/\mathbb{S}[z^{1/{p^{\infty}}}];\mathbb{Z}_{p})

\displaystyle\overset{\eqref{basechange}}{\simeq}

\displaystyle\operatorname{THH}(\mathcal{O}_{K}/\mathbb{S}[z];\mathbb{Z}_{p})\otimes_{\mathbb{S}[z]^{\wedge}_{p}}\mathbb{S}[z^{1/{p^{\infty}}}]^{\wedge}_{p}.

Since a morphism of homotopy groups
\pi_{*}(\mathbb{S}[z]^{\wedge}_{p})\to\pi_{*}(\mathbb{S}[z^{1/{p^{\infty}}}]^{\wedge}_{p})
is faithfully flat and there is an isomorphism

\pi_{*}\operatorname{THH}(\mathcal{O}_{K}[\pi^{1/p^{\infty}}];\mathbb{Z}_{p})\simeq\mathcal{O}_{K}[\pi^{1/p^{\infty}}][u]
, where
\deg u=2
(see
[
BMS19
, section. 6]
), we obtain an isomorphism

(2.5)

\pi_{*}\operatorname{THH}(\mathcal{O}_{K}/\mathbb{S}[z];\mathbb{Z}_{p})\simeq\mathcal{O}_{K}[u],

where
u
has degree
2
(see
[
BMS19
, Proposition. 11.10]
).

Proposition 2.5
.

Any dualizable object in
\operatorname{Mod}_{\operatorname{THH}(\mathcal{O}_{K}/\mathbb{S}[z];\mathbb{Z}_{p})}(\operatorname{Sp}^{BS^{1}})
is perfect.

Proof.

By the isomorphism (
2.5
),
\pi_{*}\operatorname{THH}(\mathcal{O}_{K}/\mathbb{S}[z];\mathbb{Z}_{p})
is a regular noetherian ring of finite Krull dimension concentrated in even degrees. We obtain the claim by
[
AMN18
, Theorem 2.15]
.
∎

Proposition 2.6

-linear categories and suppose

Theorem 2.9

\mathcal{T}

\mathcal{O}_{K}

Proof.

The morphism
\tilde{\varphi}^{hS^{1}}_{\mathcal{T}}
induces a morphism of
\operatorname{TP}(\mathcal{O}_{K}/\mathbb{S}[z];\mathbb{Z}_{p})
-modules:

(2.7)

By proposition
2.6

-linear isomorphism on homotopy groups:

(2.9)

for any
