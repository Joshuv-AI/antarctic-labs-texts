The non-invariance of fundamental interactions under the combination
of charge-conjugation (C) and parity (P) transformations is a
necessary condition for baryogenesis
Sakharov 1967
, a process
that dynamically generates the matter-antimatter asymmetry in the
universe. Although the Standard Model (SM) accommodates CP violation
with the Kobayashi-Maskawa
phase
Cabibbo 1963
;
Kobayashi and Maskawa 1973
, it can only explain a
matter-antimatter asymmetry that is at least ten orders of magnitude
smaller than the observed value
Barr et al. 1979
. Additional
sources of CP violation beyond the SM are expected to exist, and the
weak hadronic transitions of hyperons are another place to search
for such sources of CP
violation
Salone et al. 2022
;
Donoghue et al. 1986
.

When two or more transition amplitudes interfere with each other,
relative weak- and strong-phase contributions exist between them.
For
K\to\pi\pi

Alavi-Harati et al. 1999
;
Fanti et al. 1999
, the CP violating weak
phase comes from the interference between
S
-wave isospin

I=0
(
A_{0}
) and isospin
I=2
(
A_{2}
) amplitudes, which
correspond to
\Delta I=1/2
and
\Delta I=3/2
transitions,
respectively
Cirigliano et al. 2012
. The unforeseen large discrepancy
between the real parts of the two isospin amplitudes,
{\rm Re}(A_{0})/{\rm Re}(A_{2})=22.45\pm 0.06
, known as the
\Delta I=1/2
rule
Amhis et al. 2022
;
Manzari et al. 2021

puzzle. Various theoretical
models have been proposed to explain this large ratio, but the dual
QCD approach
Buras et al. 2014
and lattice QCD
calculation
Abbott et al. 2020

comprehensive understanding of this rule is desirable.

\phi_{\rm Y}

Lee and Yang 1957
. The ratio of decay asymmetry parameters
for the two isospin decay modes
\Lambda\to n\pi^{0}
and
\Lambda\to p\pi^{-}
,

\alpha_{\Lambda 0}/\alpha_{\Lambda-}
, is a sensitive probe to determine the contribution
of
\Delta I=3/2
transitions. In their absence, the ratio

\alpha_{\Lambda 0}/\alpha_{\Lambda-}
is predicted to be unity
Olsen et al. 1970
. A
recent BESIII result suggests that this might not be the
case
Ablikim et al. 2019
. Further studies of the isospin
amplitude in hyperon decays is required to rigorously test the
\Delta I=1/2
rule.

Moreover, contrary to kaon decays, CP-violation in hyperon decays could arise from
the interference between parity-conserving (
P
-wave) and
parity-violating (
S
-wave) amplitudes with a CP-odd weak phase.
The decay asymmetry parameters of hyperon are CP-odd and assuming CP conservation

\alpha_{\rm Y}=-\bar{\alpha}_{\rm Y}
and
\phi_{\rm Y}=-\bar{\phi}_{\rm Y}
, where
\bar{\alpha}_{\rm Y}
and
\bar{\phi}_{\rm Y}

are decay asymmetry parameters for antihyperon
\bar{Y}

Donoghue et al. 1986
.
CP symmetry, which is broken in the presence of non-negligible
weak phase contributions, is gauged by the CP-observables

A_{\rm CP}
and
\Delta\Phi_{\rm CP}

Ablikim et al. 2022a
:

A_{\rm CP}^{\rm Y}=\frac{\alpha_{\rm Y}+\bar{\alpha}_{\rm Y}}{\alpha_{\rm Y}-\bar{\alpha}_{\rm Y}}=-\tan\left(\delta_{P}-\delta_{S}\right)\tan\left(\xi_{P}-\xi_{S}\right),

(1)

\Delta\phi_{\rm CP}^{\rm Y}=\frac{\phi_{\rm Y}+\bar{\phi}_{\rm Y}}{2}=\frac{\langle\alpha\rangle}{\sqrt{1-\langle\alpha\rangle^{2}}}\cos\langle\phi\rangle\tan\left(\xi_{P}-\xi_{S}\right),

(2)

where
\langle\alpha\rangle=(\alpha_{{\rm Y}}-\bar{\alpha}_{{\rm Y}})/2
,
\langle\phi\rangle=(\phi_{{\rm Y}}-\bar{\phi}_{{\rm Y}})/2
,
\delta_{P}-\delta_{S}
denotes the strong
phase difference of the final-state interaction, and
\xi_{P}-\xi_{S}

denotes the weak phase difference. Experimentally, the weak phase
difference has been directly determined to be

(1.2\pm 3.4\pm 0.8)\times 10^{-2}
rad
Ablikim et al. 2022a
for
the decay
\Xi^{-}\to\Lambda\pi^{-}
using entangled
\Xi^{-}
and

\bar{\Xi}^{+}
produced at BESIII.

In this Letter, the process
J/\psi\to\Xi^{-}\bar{\Xi}^{+}\to\Lambda(p\pi^{-})\pi^{-}\bar{\Lambda}(\bar{n}\pi^{0})\pi^{+}
is studied
with
(10087\pm 44)\times 10^{6}

J/\psi
events
Ablikim et al. 2022b

collected by the BESIII detector. Benefiting from the
transversely-polarized hyperons and the spin correlation between
hyperon and anti-hyperons, various decay properties of
\Xi^{-}
and

\Lambda
are determined by an extended formalism that completely
describes the angular distributions of the production and decay
processes
Perotti et al. 2019
.

The design and performance of the BESIII detector
are described in Refs.
Ablikim et al. 2010
;
Huang et al. 2022

corresponding simulation, analysis framework, and software are
presented in Refs.
Ping 2008

Deng et al. 2006

Li et al. 2006

Monte Carlo (MC) samples are produced with
Geant
4-based

experimental conditions including the electron-positron collision, the
decays of the particles, and the response of the detector. A sample of
simulated events of generic
J/\psi

luminosity of data, is used to study the potential background
reactions. To eliminate experimenter bias, the central values were
blinded by using the hidden answer technique

until
all selections, fits, and uncertainty evaluations were finalized.
Simulated signal and background samples are used to verify the
analysis approaches and to study the systematic effects. Unless
otherwise indicated, the charge-conjugate channel is implied

Four charged tracks are required in the multilayer drift
chamber (MDC) within the range
\lvert\cos\theta\rvert<0.93

GeV/

Ablikim et al. 2022a

The combination with the smallest
(M_{p\pi^{-}\pi^{-}}-m_{\Xi^{-}})^{2}+(M_{p\pi^{-}}-m_{\Lambda})^{2}

M_{p\pi^{-}\pi^{-}(p\pi^{-})}
denotes the invariant mass of
p\pi^{-}\pi^{-}

(
p\pi^{-}
) and

refers to the nominal mass of

\Xi^{-}
(
\Lambda
)
Workman and Others 2022

\pi^{-}
from the
\Lambda
and
\Xi^{-}
decays being wrongly assigned

0.1\%
, which is negligible. The candidate events are
required to satisfy

MeV/
c^{2}
and
\lvert M_{p\pi^{-}\pi^{-}}-m_{\Xi^{-}}\rvert<11
MeV/
c^{2}
. The decay lengths of the
\Xi^{-}
and
\Lambda

\theta_{\Xi^{-}}
of the reconstructed
\Xi^{-}

in the

center-of-mass frame is required to satisfy

At least two photon candidates in the electromagnetic calorimeter
(EMC) are required. A photon candidate should have energy greater
than
25
MeV in the barrel region (
\lvert\cos\theta\rvert<0.8

50

A boosted decision tree (BDT) classifier

number of hits, second and Zernike moments, and deposition

90\%

55\%

\pi^{0}

\pi^{0}

J/\psi\to\Xi^{-}\pi^{+}\bar{n}\gamma\gamma

\gamma\gamma

\pi^{0}
and

\bar{\Lambda}

\Xi^{-}

\pi^{0}

candidate. If there is more than one
\pi^{0}
candidate, the candidate
with the smallest
\chi^{2}
is retained, and
\chi^{2}<200

\lvert M_{\bar{n}\gamma\gamma\pi^{+}}-m_{\bar{\Xi}^{+}}\rvert<11

Detailed studies are performed with MC simulation and data in the

\Xi^{-}
and
\bar{\Xi}^{+}

The joint angular amplitude of the full decay chain can be written in
a modular form as

\mathcal{W}(\xi;\omega)=\sum_{\mu,\nu=0}^{3}C_{\mu\nu}\sum_{\mu^{\prime},\nu^{\prime}=0}^{3}a_{\mu\mu^{\prime}}^{\Xi}a_{\nu\nu^{\prime}}^{\Xi}a_{\mu^{\prime}0}^{\Lambda}a_{\nu^{\prime}0}^{\bar{\Lambda}}.

(3)

Here
C_{\mu\nu}
is a
4\times 4
real-valued spin density matrix
describing the spin configuration of the entangled
\Xi^{-}\bar{\Xi}^{+}

pair,
a^{{\rm Y}}_{\mu\nu}
is also a
4\times 4
real-valued matrix
representing the propagation of the spin density matrix in the decays
of a spin 1/2 hyperon into a spin 1/2 baryon and a pseudoscalar,
{\rm Y}\to{\rm B}\pi
. Therefore, the distribution of the nine helicity
angles
\xi=(\theta_{\Xi},\theta_{\Lambda},\phi_{\Lambda},\theta_{\bar{\Lambda}},\phi_{\bar{\Lambda}},\theta_{p},\phi_{p},\theta_{\bar{n}},\phi_{\bar{n}})
is determined by eight global
parameters
\omega=(\alpha_{J/\psi},\Delta\Phi_{J/\psi},\alpha_{\Xi},\phi_{\Xi},\bar{\alpha}_{\Xi},\bar{\phi}_{\Xi},\alpha_{\Lambda-},\bar{\alpha}_{\Lambda 0})
. In this analysis,
{\rm Y}\to{\rm B}\pi
stands for

\Xi^{-}\to\Lambda\pi^{-}
,
\Lambda\to p\pi^{-}
and
\bar{\Lambda}\to\bar{n}\pi^{0}
.
The distribution of the helicity angle
\theta_{p}
in the
\Lambda

rest frame is written as

\frac{\mathrm{d}N}{\mathrm{d}\cos\theta_{p}}\propto 1+\alpha_{\Lambda-}\alpha_{\Xi}\cos\theta_{p}

(4)

by integrating over the remaining eight helicity angles.
The formalism of the full angular distribution and the definition of the reference system are discussed in detail in Ref.
Ablikim et al. 2022a
.

A simultaneous fit on the joint angular distribution is carried out
with the production parameters,
\alpha_{J/\psi}
and
\Delta\Phi_{J/\psi}
, and decay asymmetry parameters of

\Xi^{-}
shared between the two charge-conjugate channels. For each
channel, the probability distribution function of the eight unknown
parameters
\omega
can be defined in terms of the helicity angles

\xi

\mathcal{P}(\xi;\omega)=\mathcal{W}(\xi;\omega)\varepsilon(\xi)/\mathcal{N}(\omega),

(5)

where the normalization factor
\mathcal{N}(\omega)
is calculated with
\mathcal{N}(\omega)=\frac{1}{M}\sum_{j=1}^{M}\frac{\mathcal{W}(\xi_{j};\omega)}{\mathcal{W}(\xi_{j};\omega_{\rm gen})}
by a signal MC sample generated with parameters
\omega_{\rm gen}
.
The sum runs over all events in the generated sample
M
, which is chosen to be thirty times the yield
obtained in data after the full selection.
The log-likelihood function for
N
observed events is

\small\mathcal{S}=-\mathcal{G}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{N}\ln\mathcal{P}(\xi_{i};\omega)-\sum_{j}{\sc w_{j}}\sum_{i}^{N_{j}^{\rm bkg}}\ln\mathcal{P}(\xi_{i};\omega)\right),

(6)

where the second term in brackets with
j
from one to three
represents the three different sources of background remaining in the
final event sample. Their contributions are evaluated with the
corresponding MC samples or data events in the sideband region and
their associated weight factors
w_{j}
. The global factor,

\mathcal{G}=(N-\sum_{j}N_{j}^{\rm bkg}\times\sc{w_{j}})/(N+\sum_{j}N_{j}^{\rm bkg}\times\sc{w_{j}}^{2})
, corrects for the statistical
uncertainties in the weighted likelihood
fit
Langenbruch 2022
.

The
\mathcal{S}
function is minimized using
Minuit2
Hatlo et al. 2005
to determine the production and
decay asymmetry parameters
\omega
. The results from the
fit, as shown in Table

previous measurements, but with improved precision. In particular,

\alpha_{\Lambda 0}
is almost the same in magnitude and opposite in sign as

\bar{\alpha}_{\Lambda 0}

previous measurements.
