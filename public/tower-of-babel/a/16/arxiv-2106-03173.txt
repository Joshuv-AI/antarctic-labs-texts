Subtilings of Elnitsky Tilings for Finite Irreducible Coxeter Groups
Robert Nicolaides, Peter Rowley

Abstract
Two new Elnitsky tilings for Coxeter groups of type B \mathrm{B} are displayed as certain subtilings. Additionally, a new tiling for the non-crystallographic Coxeter group of type H 3 \mathrm{H}_{3} is obtained, described as a D 6 \mathrm{D}_{6} -subtiling.

1.

Let

be a

2.

Construct and label the first

The choice of angles and side lengths for the left hand side do not actually matter so long as we have the left hand side’s vertices forming a convex set and the edges with the same label being translations of one another.
