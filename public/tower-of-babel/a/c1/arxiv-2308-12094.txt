On the Ternary Purely Exponential Diophantine Equation ( a ​ k ) x + ( b ​ k ) y = ( ( a + b ) ​ k ) z (ak)^{x}+(bk)^{y}=((a+b)k)^{z} with Prime Powers a a and b b


Abstract
Let k k be a positive integer, and let a , b a,b be coprime positive integers with min ⁡ { a , b } > 1 \min\{a,b\}>1 . In this paper, using a combination of some elementary number theory techniques with classical results on the Nagell-Ljunggren equation, the Catalan equation and some new properties of the Lucas sequence ( A000204 in OEIS), we prove that if k > 1 k>1 and a , b a,b are both prime powers with min ⁡ { a , b } > 2 \min\{a,b\}>2 , then the equation ( a ​ k ) x + ( b ​ k ) y = ( ( a + b ) ​ k ) z (ak)^{x}+(bk)^{y}=((a+b)k)^{z} has only one positive integer solution ( x , y , z ) = ( 1 , 1 , 1 ) (x,y,z)=(1,1,1) . The above result partially proves that Conjecture 1 presented in (Acta Arith. 2018, 184 (1): 37-49) is true.
