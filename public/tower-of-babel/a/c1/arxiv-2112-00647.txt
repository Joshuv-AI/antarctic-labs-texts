Yang–Mills–Scalar–Matter Fields in the Two-Point Space
Gustavo Amilcar Saldaña Moncada

Abstract
The purpose of this work is to present a Non–Commutative Geometrical version of the Yang–Mills Theory and the Yang–Mills Scalar Matter Theory by constructing a concrete example using M. Durdevich’s theory of quantum principal bundles.



Keywords:
Quantum Principal Bundles (qpb), Yang–Mills Theory, Yang–Mills Scalar Matter Theory.

Introduction

The study of principal bundles and principal connections have applications in Topology and Differential Geometry among other areas. In Physics, it provides the natural framework to talk about Classical and Quantum Field Theory; in particular, it is possible to talk about the Yang–Mills theory and the theory of matter fields. There is a general theory that supports all the concepts presented. This was just an example.
