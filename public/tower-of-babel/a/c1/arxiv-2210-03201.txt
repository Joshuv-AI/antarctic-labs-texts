On Bloch’s map for torsion cycles over non-closed fields
Theodosis Alexandrou, Stefan Schreieder

Abstract
. We generalize Bloch’s map on torsion cycles from algebraically closed fields to arbitrary fields. While Bloch’s map over algebraically closed fields is injective for zero-cycles and for cycles of codimension at most two, we show that the generalization to arbitrary fields is only injective for cycles of codimension at most two but in general not for zero-cycles. Our result implies that Jannsen’s cycle class map in integral ℓ \ell -adic continuous étale cohomology is in general not injective on torsion zero-cycles over finitely generated fields. This answers a question of Scavia and Suzuki.

Scavia and Suzuki exhibited in

.

There is a finitely generated field

The question whether

(This question is interesting for the base change

Remark 2.1

Lemma 4.1

(1)

(2)

If

for all

(3)

Assume first that

By the Weil conjectures proven by Deligne, the group

.

The Weil conjectures imply that

[

as claimed.

is an arbitrary finitely generated field.
Let

be the absolute Galois group of

and let
\bar{k}
be an algebraic (or separable) closure of

.
Then the Hochschild–Serre spectral sequence from
[

]
yields an exact sequence

H^{1}(\operatorname{Spec}k,\mathbb{Q}_{\ell}(1))\lx@xy@svg{\hbox{\raise 2.5pt\hbox{\kern 1.0pt\hbox{\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\hbox{\vtop{\halign{\entry@#!@&&\entry@@#!@\cr&\crcr}}}\ignorespaces{\hbox{\kern-1.0pt\raise 0.0pt\hbox{\hbox{\kern 0.0pt\raise 0.0pt\hbox{\hbox{\kern 1.0pt\raise-2.5pt\hbox{$\textstyle{\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces}$}}}}}}}\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces{}{\hbox{\lx@xy@droprule}}\ignorespaces{\hbox{\kern 14.0pt\raise 0.0pt\hbox{\hbox{\kern 0.0pt\raise 0.0pt\hbox{\lx@xy@tip{1}\lx@xy@tip{-1}}}}}}{\hbox{\lx@xy@droprule}}{\hbox{\lx@xy@droprule}}{\hbox{\kern 14.0pt\raise 0.0pt\hbox{\hbox{\kern 0.0pt\raise 0.0pt\hbox{\hbox{\kern 1.0pt\raise-2.5pt\hbox{$\textstyle{}$}}}}}}}\ignorespaces}}}}\ignorespaces H^{1}(X,\mathbb{Q}_{\ell}(1))\lx@xy@svg{\hbox{\raise 2.5pt\hbox{\kern 1.0pt\hbox{\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\hbox{\vtop{\halign{\entry@#!@&&\entry@@#!@\cr&\crcr}}}\ignorespaces{\hbox{\kern-1.0pt\raise 0.0pt\hbox{\hbox{\kern 0.0pt\raise 0.0pt\hbox{\hbox{\kern 1.0pt\raise-2.5pt\hbox{$\textstyle{\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces}$}}}}}}}\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces{}{\hbox{\lx@xy@droprule}}\ignorespaces{\hbox{\kern 14.0pt\raise 0.0pt\hbox{\hbox{\kern 0.0pt\raise 0.0pt\hbox{\lx@xy@tip{1}\lx@xy@tip{-1}}}}}}{\hbox{\lx@xy@droprule}}{\hbox{\lx@xy@droprule}}{\hbox{\kern 14.0pt\raise 0.0pt\hbox{\hbox{\kern 0.0pt\raise 0.0pt\hbox{\hbox{\kern 1.0pt\raise-2.5pt\hbox{$\textstyle{}$}}}}}}}\ignorespaces}}}}\ignorespaces H^{1}(X_{\bar{k}},\mathbb{Q}_{\ell}(1))^{G},
