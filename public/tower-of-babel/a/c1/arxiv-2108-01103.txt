Democratic Lagrangians for Nonlinear Electrodynamics
Zhirayr Avetisyan, Oleg Evnin, Karapet Mkrtchyan

Abstract
We construct a Lagrangian for general nonlinear electrodynamics that features electric and magnetic potentials on equal footing. In the language of this Lagrangian, discrete and continuous electric-magnetic duality symmetries can be straightforwardly imposed, leading to a simple formulation for theories with the S ​ O ​ ( 2 ) SO(2) duality invariance. When specialized to the conformally invariant case, our construction provides a manifestly duality-symmetric formulation of the recently discovered ModMax theory. We briefly comment on a natural generalization of this approach to p p -forms in 2 ​ p + 2 2p+2 dimensions.

{\textstyle\star}H-H
.
These structures will be explored in more detail in future works.
