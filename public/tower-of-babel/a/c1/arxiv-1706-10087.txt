A Variation of the 𝒒 \boldsymbol{q} -Painlevé System with Affine Weyl Group Symmetry of Type 𝑬 𝟕 ( 𝟏 ) \boldsymbol{E_{7}^{(1)}}
Hidehito NAGAO

Abstract
Recently a certain q q -Painlevé type system has been obtained from a reduction of the q q -Garnier system. In this paper it is shown that the q q -Painlevé type system is associated with another realization of the affine Weyl group symmetry of type E 7 ( 1 ) E_{7}^{(1)} and is different from the well-known q q -Painlevé system of type E 7 ( 1 ) E_{7}^{(1)} from the point of view of evolution directions. We also study a connection between the q q -Painlevé type system and the q q -Painlevé system of type E 7 ( 1 ) E_{7}^{(1)} . Furthermore determinant formulas of particular solutions for the q q -Painlevé type system are constructed in terms of the terminating q q -hypergeometric function.
