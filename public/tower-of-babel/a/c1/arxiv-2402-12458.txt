Testing the double-logarithm asymptotic gluon density in ultraperipheral heavy ion collisions at the Large Hadron Collider
D. A. Fagundes, M.V.T. Machado

Abstract
In this work we analyze the application of the analytical gluon distribution based on the double asymptotic scaling for the photoproduction of vector mesons in coherent p ​ p pp , p ​ A pA and A ​ A AA collisions at the LHC energies using the color dipole formalism. Predictions for the rapidity distribution are presented for ρ 0 \rho^{0} and J / ψ J/\psi , ψ ⁡ ( 2 ​ S ) \psi(2S) and Υ ⁡ ( 1 ​ S ) \Upsilon(1S) photoproduction. An analysis on the uncertainties associated to different implementation of dipole-proton amplitude is performed. The vector meson photoproduction accompanied by electromagnetic dissociation is also analyzed.



I
Introduction

The exclusive production of vector mesons plays an important role both experimentally and theoretically allowing accurate studies on applications of perturbative Quantum Chromodynamics (QCD) and parton saturation physics as well. The light vector mesons on one side have no hard perturbative scale associated to the process in the photoproduction limit,
Q^{2}=0
, thus probing the non-perturbative regime of QCD. In the electroproduction case, where the virtuality
Q^{2}
of the incoming photon is sufficiently large, a perturbative approach is justified. On the other hand, quarkonium production presents the intrinsic hard scale characterized by the heavy quark masses even for
Q^{2}=0
and weak coupling methods can be successfully applied. It is well known that the transition between the perturbative QCD treatment and the soft regime can be handled by the parton saturation framework
Gribov:1983ivg
;
Mueller:1985wy
;
Mueller:2001fv
;
McLerran:1993ni
;
McLerran:1993ka
;
McLerran:1994vd
;
Ayala:1995kg
;
Ayala:1995hx
;
Iancu:2003xm
;
Gelis:2010nm
;
Morreale:2021pnn
within the QCD color dipole formalism
Nikolaev:1990ja
;
Nikolaev:1991et
;
Mueller:1993rr
;
Mueller:1994jq
. There the saturation scale,
Q_{s}
, sets the limit on the parton density phase-space to be reached in the hadron’s wavefunction. Hence, it can be viewed as the typical underlying scale in the low Bjorken-
x
dynamics in the case of light meson photoproduction and plays a key role in quarkonium production as well (see recent applications in Refs.
Henkels:2022bne
;
Cisek:2022yjj
;
Mantysaari:2022sux
;
Mantysaari:2022kdm
;
Mantysaari:2022bsp
;
Mantysaari:2021ryb
;
Kopeliovich:2021dgx
;
Kopeliovich:2022jwe
;
Cepila:2023pvh
;
Matousek:2022enl
;
Kumar:2022aly
;
Anand:2018zle
;
Xie:2022sjm
;
Henkels:2023plt
;
Mantysaari:2023xcu
;
Mantysaari:2023prg
;
Azevedo:2023fee
;
Goncalves:2023sts
;
Boroun:2023vuu
;
Boroun:2023klw
and references therein). The photon-proton or photon-nucleus scattering in such approach is characterized by the QCD color dipole amplitude representing the interaction of the Fock states of the virtual incoming photon, i.e. color singlet dipoles at leading order, with the target at rest. The
q\bar{q}
fluctuations and the transition from the dipole to meson are described by the photon and vector meson wavefunctions, respectively.

Given the shutdown of the Hadron-Electron Ring Accelerator (HERA) experiment the low-
x
studies on exclusive processes are being developed in a prolific way in the Relativistic Heavy Ion Collisions (RHIC) and at the Large Hadron Collider (LHC). In the ultraperipheral heavy ion collisions (UPCs) and in coherent proton-proton (
pp
) reactions the production process can be theoretically factorized in
terms of the high flux of photons into the hadron or nucleus projectile and the photon-target production cross section (see reviews on Refs.
Bertulani:1987tz
;
Baur:2001jj
;
Bertulani:2005ru
;
Baltz:2007kq
;
Klein:2020fmr
). Investigations of
\gamma p
or
\gamma A
interactions
at these colliders bring valuable information on the underlying QCD dynamics. A crucial information that can be extracted from the available data for meson production is the gluon distribution function,
xg(x,\mu^{2})
.

Recently, in Ref.
Fagundes:2022bzw
the gluon parton distribution function (PDF) has been evaluated by us based on the double asymptotic scaling (DAS) for gluon density
Caola:2008xr
;
Ball:2016spl
within the color dipole approach. The model has the advantage of replacing the numerical LO DGLAP evolution in the large virtualities domain by the analytical calculations from pQCD in the DAS approximation. The evolution presents similar results compared to other studies
Rezaeian:2013tka
;
Mantysaari:2018nng
using numerical DGLAP evolution. The work
Fagundes:2022bzw
has contributed to an easier modelling of dipole cross section and requires very little computational power to predict inclusive and exclusive deep inelastic scattering (DIS) observables. As far as vector meson production is concerned the calculations of
J/\psi
and
\rho
photoproduction cross sections describe the HERA data and the measurements of vector meson photoproduction in proton-proton (
pp
) collisions at the LHC. Moreover, it was shown in
Fagundes:2022bzw
that the extracted analytical gluon density is quite suitable for the high-precision HERA data and to study small-
x
physics at future colliders such as the Electron Ion Collider (EIC) and the Large Hadron Electron Collider (LHeC).

The main focus here is to investigate the robustness of the phenomenological model for the color dipole amplitude based on DAS approximation as applied to UPCs and
pp
collisions at the LHC energies. We analyze the theoretical uncertainties on the predictions of exclusive production of vector mesons in proton-proton, proton-lead and lead-lead collisions at the LHC providing predictions for the rapidity distribution,
y
. This paper is organized as follows. In Sec.
II
we present the the color dipole scattering amplitude including QCD evolution through the DAS approximation. The parameters of the model have been determined from small-
x
high precision DIS
ep
data and it describes correctly the structure functions and exclusive vector meson production. In Sec.
III
we apply the formalism in order to compute the production of vector mesons in UPCs focusing on the LHC energy regime. In the last section, the main results are discussed.

Given the DAS approximation, the QCD color dipole amplitude can be obtained. In the target rest frame, the DIS
ep
process,
\gamma^{*}p\rightarrow X
, is viewed as the interaction of a color singlet
q\bar{q}
pair with fixed transverse size
\vec{r}
off the target through gluon exchanges (gluon ladder diagrams). For instance, in the impact-parameter saturation model (IPSAT hereafter)
Kowalski:2003hm
;
Mantysaari:2018nng
;
Mantysaari:2018zdd
describing the multiple interaction of a QCD dipole probe with a dense target the
b
-dependent dipole amplitude is given by,

\displaystyle\frac{d\sigma_{q\bar{q}}}{d^{2}\vec{b}}=2\,\left[1-\exp\left(-\frac{\pi^{2}}{2\,N_{c}}r^{2}\alpha_{S}(\mu^{2})xg(x,\mu^{2})T(b)\right)\right],

(6)

where the scale is set
\mu^{2}=C/r^{2}+\mu_{0}^{2}
and
x
is the modified Bjorken variable,
x=x_{\mathrm{Bj}}\left(1+\frac{4m_{q}^{2}}{Q^{2}}\right)
, with
m_{q}
being the effective quark mass
Golec-Biernat:1998zce
;
GolecBiernat:1999qd
. Such modification describes consistently the transition from high
Q^{2}
towards the limit
Q^{2}\rightarrow 0
. The corresponding dipole cross section is thus obtained as
\hat{\sigma}(x,\vec{r})=\int d^{2}\vec{b}\,d\sigma_{q\bar{q}}/d^{2}\vec{b}
.

In eq.(
6
),
T(b)
represents the thickness function of a proton. Quite generally a Gaussian model is assumed for
T(b)
, given the exponential fall-off in the
|t|
-dependence of quarkonia production. In such case, the normalized-to-unity thickness,
T_{G}(b)
, is given by:

\displaystyle T_{G}(b)=\frac{1}{2\pi B_{G}}\exp\left(-\frac{b^{2}}{2B_{G}}\right),

(7)

where the parameter
B_{G}\simeq 4
GeV
2
is related to the average squared transverse radius of the nucleon,
\langle b^{2}\rangle=2B_{G}

Kowalski:2003hm
;
Mantysaari:2018nng
;
Mantysaari:2018zdd
.

On the other hand, in the BGK approach
Bartels:2002cj
;
Golec-Biernat:2006koa
;
Golec-Biernat:2017lfv
, the color dipole cross section takes the form

\displaystyle\hat{\sigma}(x,\vec{r})=\sigma_{0}\,\left[1-\exp\left(-\frac{\pi^{2}r^{2}\alpha_{S}(\mu^{2})xg(x,\mu^{2})/N_{c}}{\sigma_{0}}\right)\right],

(8)

where
\sigma_{0}
is now a new parameter of this model. The unintegrated gluon distribution (UGD),
{\mathcal{F}}(x,k)
, can be obtained from the dipole cross section above
Luszczak:2022fkf
;
Boroun:2023ldq
,

\displaystyle\mathcal{F}(x,k)=k^{4}\frac{\sigma_{0}}{\alpha_{S}(\mu_{0}^{2})}\,\frac{N_{c}}{8\pi^{2}}\int_{0}^{\infty}rdr\,J_{0}(kr)\left[1-\frac{\alpha_{S}(\mu_{0}^{2})}{\alpha_{S}(r)}\,\frac{\hat{\sigma}(x,r)}{\sigma_{0}}\right].

(9)

For the DIS inclusive process, the cross section for the interaction of a virtual photon with virtuality
Q^{2}
with a given polarisation off a proton target is expressed as
Kovchegov:2012mbw
:

\displaystyle\sigma_{T,L}^{\gamma^{*}p}(x,Q^{2})=\sum_{f}\int d^{2}\vec{r}\int dz\left|\psi_{T,L}^{f}(Q,r,z)\right|^{2}\hat{\sigma}(x,\vec{r}),

(10)

where
\psi_{T,L}^{f}(Q,r,z)
is the corresponding photon wave function in mixed representation for photon with transverse,
T
, or longitudinal,
L
polarization, respectively. The quark (antiquark) carries a longitudinal momentum fraction
z
(
1-z
) of the incoming photon. The summation above refers to the quark flavour
f
. The squared photon wave functions summed over the quark helicities for a given photon polarisation and quark flavour
f
are expressed by
Kovchegov:2012mbw
,

\displaystyle\left|\psi_{T}^{f}(Q,r,z)\right|^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{2N_{c}}{\pi}\alpha_{\mathrm{em}}e_{f}^{2}\left\{\left[z^{2}+(1-z)^{2}\right]\epsilon^{2}K_{1}^{2}(\epsilon r)+m_{f}^{2}K_{0}^{2}(\epsilon r)\right\},

(11)

\displaystyle\left|\psi_{L}^{f}(Q,r,z)\right|^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{8N_{c}}{\pi}\alpha_{\mathrm{em}}e_{f}^{2}Q^{2}z^{2}(1-z)^{2}K_{0}^{2}(\epsilon r).

(12)

where
K_{\nu}
are the modified Bessel functions of second kind of order
\nu=0,1
and
\epsilon=\sqrt{z(1-z)Q^{2}+m_{f}^{2}}
.

The exclusive processes can be also be described within the very same QCD color dipole picture. For example, the differential cross section of exclusive vector meson production,
\gamma^{*}p\rightarrow Vp

\displaystyle\frac{d\sigma^{\gamma p\rightarrow Vp}}{dt}=\frac{1}{16\pi}\left|\mathcal{A}^{\gamma p\rightarrow Vp}\right|^{2}\;(1+\beta^{2})\,R_{g}^{2},

\displaystyle\beta

\displaystyle\tan\left(\frac{\pi\lambda_{\mathrm{eff}}}{2}\right),\quad\text{with}\quad\lambda_{\mathrm{eff}}\equiv\frac{\partial\ln\left(\mathcal{A}_{T}^{\gamma p\rightarrow Vp}\right)}{\partial\ln(1/x)},

(14)

\displaystyle R_{g}(\lambda_{\mathrm{eff}})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{2^{2\lambda_{\mathrm{eff}}+3}}{\sqrt{\pi}}\frac{\Gamma(\lambda_{\mathrm{eff}}+5/2)}{\Gamma(\lambda_{\mathrm{eff}}+4)}.

III
Results and discussions

Let us start by calculating the rapidity distribution for coherent vector meson production in proton-proton collisions. The equivalent photon approximation (EPA) will be used. Thus, the production cross section in hadron-hadron collisions will be factorized as a convolution of photon flux,
dN_{\gamma}^{p}/d\omega
, and the photoproduction cross section,
\sigma(\gamma p\rightarrow Vp)
. The rapidity distribution is given by
Bertulani:1987tz
;
Baur:2001jj
;
Bertulani:2005ru
;
Baltz:2007kq
;
Klein:2020fmr

\displaystyle\frac{d\sigma(pp\rightarrow pVp)}{dy}=\left[\langle|{\cal{S}}|^{2}\rangle(\omega)\omega\,\frac{dN_{\gamma}^{p}(\omega)}{d\omega}\,\sigma^{\gamma p\rightarrow Vp}(\omega)\right]_{\omega=\omega^{-}}+\left[\langle|{\cal{S}}|^{2}\rangle(\omega)\omega\,\frac{dN_{\gamma}^{p}(\omega)}{d\omega}\,\sigma^{\gamma p\rightarrow Vp}(\omega)\right]_{\omega=\omega^{+}}

IV
Conclusions and Final Remarks

As a summary, in this work calculations are performed considering an analytical gluon distribution based on DAS approximation leading to predictions for the exclusive vector meson production of
\rho^{0}
,
J/\psi
,
\phi(2S)
and
\Upsilon(1S)
. The focus was on their coherent production for
pp
,
pA
and
AA
collisions at the LHC energies. This analysis extend the investigation presented in Ref.
Fagundes:2022bzw
, where the models were proposed and compared to structure functions and exclusive meson production in the HERA kinematics. It is shown that the different implementations of the dipole-proton amplitude (IPSAT-FM, BGK-FM and BGK-DAS) using such a gluon PDF leads to a sizable theoretical uncertainty in the photonuclear cross section and in rapidity distributions. Of course, other sources of uncertainties as the meson wave function and quark masses in the phenomenological models were not addressed at this stage. In numerical calculations the boosted Gaussian meson wavefunction was used and one expects change in normalization of rapidity distributions as other parametrizations are considered like the light cone Gaussian (LCG). In general, the rapidity distributions of coherent vector meson production are fairly described by the analytical gluon PDF. The electromagnetic dissociation in nucleus-nucleus collisions has also been studied. Based on the
n
\mathbf{{}_{O}^{O}}
n
generator, the rapidity distributions for coherent meson photoproduction in 0n0n, 0nXn and XnXn neutron multiplicity classes were computed. Taking into account the theoretical uncertainty the models are able to describe the recent LHC data for
J/\psi
and
\rho^{0}
production and the very recent RHIC-STAR data.
