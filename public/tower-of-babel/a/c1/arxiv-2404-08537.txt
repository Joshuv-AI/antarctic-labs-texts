The interplay between the space (manifold) and its asymptotic cones (tangent cone) is fundamental in geometric analysis. On the one hand, uniqueness of asymptotic cones is expected when there are natural equations (e.g., Einstein manifolds, minimal surfaces, solitons). On the other hand, one looks for classification of spaces, according to their asymptotic cones.
