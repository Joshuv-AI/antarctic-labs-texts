A qualitative difference appears to Newton’s laws. Their features are that nature is not only constituted by matter, but also through Faraday’s lines of force [2].

Nevertheless, although all its success, Maxwell equations contain limitations. It is a linear theory, introduces the potential fields as a subsidiary, polarization and magnetization fields by hand, passive light, others. Different subjects as condensate matter, plasma, superconductivity, astrophysics are registering such restrictions. There is a physics beyond Maxwell to be encompassed. Something is expected to be done with contemporary electromagnetism. Advances are welcomed since preserving the four Maxwell postulates.
