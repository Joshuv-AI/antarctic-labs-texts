Singularities and Accumulation of Singularities of π \pi N Scattering amplitudes


Abstract
It is demonstrated that for the isospin I = 1 / 2 I=1/2 π \pi N scattering amplitude, T I = 1 / 2 ​ ( s , t ) T^{I=1/2}(s,t) , s = ( m N 2 − m π 2 ) 2 / m N 2 s={(m_{N}^{2}-m_{\pi}^{2})^{2}}/{m_{N}^{2}} and s = m N 2 + 2 ​ m π 2 s=m_{N}^{2}+2m_{\pi}^{2} are two accumulation points of poles on the second sheet of complex s s plane, and are hence accumulation of singularities of T I = 1 / 2 ​ ( s , t ) T^{I=1/2}(s,t) . For T I = 3 / 2 ​ ( s , t ) T^{I=3/2}(s,t) , s = ( m N 2 − m π 2 ) 2 / m N 2 s={(m_{N}^{2}-m_{\pi}^{2})^{2}}/{m_{N}^{2}} is the accumulation point of poles on the second sheet of complex s s plane. The proof is valid up to all orders of chiral expansions.

The on-shell
T

\pi

s=(p+q)^{2},\quad t=(p-p^{\prime}),\quad u=(p-q^{\prime})\ ,

(1)

T(\pi^{a}+N_{i}\to\pi^{\prime}+N_{f})=\chi_{f}^{\dagger}(\delta^{aa^{\prime}}T^{+}+\dfrac{1}{2}[\tau^{a^{\prime}},\tau^{a}]T^{-})\chi_{i}\ ,

(3)

where
\tau^{a}
is Pauli matrices, and
\chi_{i}
(
\chi_{f}
) corresponds to the isospin wave function of the initial (final) nucleon state.
The amplitudes with isospins
I={1}/{2},{3}/{2}
can be written as

\begin{split}&T^{I=1/2}=T^{+}+2T^{-}\ ,\\
&T^{I=3/2}=T^{+}-T^{-}\ .\end{split}

(4)

As for the Lorentz structure, for an isospin index
I={1}/{2},{3}/{2}
,

T^{I}=\bar{u}^{(s^{\prime})}(p^{\prime})[A^{I}(s,t)+\dfrac{1}{2}(\not{q}+\not{q}^{\prime})]B^{I}(s,t)]u^{(s)}(p)\ ,

(5)

where the superscripts (
s^{\prime}
), (s) denote the spins of the Dirac spinors, and q(p) and
q^{\prime}(p^{\prime})
are the 4-momenta of initial and final pions (nucleons), respectively. Functions
A

B

s
and

Helicity amplitudes can be obtained by substituting the nucleon spinors by helicity eigenstates in the centre of mass frame

\begin{split}&T^{I}_{++}=(\dfrac{1+z_{s}}{2})^{1/2}[2m_{N}A^{I}(s,t)+(s-m_{\pi}^{2}-m_{N}^{2})B^{I}(s,t)]\ ,\\
&T^{I}_{+-}=-(\dfrac{1-z_{s}}{2})^{1/2}s^{-1/2}[(s-m_{\pi}^{2}+m_{N}^{2})A^{I}(s,t)+m_{N}(s+m_{\pi}^{2}-m_{N}^{2})B^{I}(s,t)]\ .\end{split}

(6)

The first and second subscripts of

+,-

+1/2
and
-1/2
, respectively;
z_{s}=\cos\theta

\theta
the scattering angle. Partial wave amplitudes for total angular momentum
J

\begin{split}&T^{I,J}_{++}(s)=\dfrac{1}{32\pi}\int_{-1}^{1}dz_{s}T^{I}_{++}(s,t)d^{J}_{1/2,1/2}(z_{s})\ ,\\
&T^{I,J}_{+-}(s)=\dfrac{1}{32\pi}\int_{-1}^{1}dz_{s}T^{I}_{+-}(s,t)d^{J}_{1/2,-1/2}(z_{s})\ .\end{split}

(7)

where
d^{J}
is the standard Wigner
d
-function. Substituting Eq. (
6

7

-function and the

\sqrt{\dfrac{1\pm z}{2}}d^{J}_{1/2,\pm 1/2}(z_{s})=\dfrac{1}{2}\left[P_{J+1/2}(z_{s})\pm P_{J-1/2}(z_{s})\right]\,,

\begin{split}&T^{I,J}_{++}(s)=\dfrac{1}{64\pi}[2m_{N}A_{C}^{I}(s)+(s-m_{\pi}^{2}-m_{N}^{2})B_{C}^{I}(s)]\ ,\\

F_{C}^{I}(s)=\int_{-1}^{1}dz_{s}F^{I}(s,t)[P_{J+1/2}(z_{s})+P_{J-1/2}(z_{s})]\ ,

(10)

F_{S}^{I}(s)=\int_{-1}^{1}dz_{s}F^{I}(s,t)[P_{J+1/2}(z_{s})-P_{J-1/2}(z_{s})]\ ,

The parity eigenstates can be constructed by the linear combinations of helicity amplitudes

T^{I,J}_{\pm}=T^{I,J}_{++}(s)\pm T^{I,J}_{+-}(s)\ ,

(12)

which satisfy the optical theorem for partial waves,

\mathrm{Im}T^{I,J}_{\pm}=\rho(s)|T_{\pm}^{I,J}|^{2}\ .

(13)

The kinematic factor

is defined as

\rho(s)=\dfrac{\sqrt{(s-s_{L})(s-s_{R})}}{s}\ ,

(14)
