Ungar Games on the Young-Fibonacci and the Shifted Staircase Lattices
Yunseo Choi, Katelyn Gan

Abstract
In 2023, Defant and Li introduced the Ungar move, which sends an element v v of a finite meet-semilattice L L to the meet of some subset of the elements covered by v v . More recently, Defant, Kravitz, and Williams introduced the Ungar game on L L , in which two players take turns making Ungar moves starting from an element of L L until the player that cannot make a nontrivial Ungar move loses. In this note, we settle two conjectures by Defant, Kravitz, and Williams on the Ungar games on the Young-Fibonacci lattice and the lattices of the order ideals of shifted staircases.
