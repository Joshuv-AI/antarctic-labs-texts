The asymptotic spectrum of flipped multilevel Toeplitz matrices and of certain preconditionings
M. Mazza 1 and J. Pestana 2,

Abstract
In this work, we perform a spectral analysis of flipped multilevel Toeplitz sequences, i.e., we study the asymptotic spectral behaviour of { Y 𝒏 ​ T 𝒏 ​ ( f ) } 𝒏 \{Y_{\boldsymbol{n}}T_{\boldsymbol{n}}(f)\}_{\boldsymbol{n}} , where T 𝒏 ​ ( f ) T_{\boldsymbol{n}}(f) is a real, square multilevel Toeplitz matrix generated by a function f ∈ L 1 ​ ( [ − π , π ] d ) f\in L^{1}([-\pi,\pi]^{d}) and Y 𝒏 Y_{\boldsymbol{n}} is the exchange matrix, which has 1 1 s on the main anti-diagonal. In line with what we have shown for unilevel flipped Toeplitz matrix sequences, the asymptotic spectrum is determined by a 2 × 2 2\times 2 matrix-valued function whose eigenvalues are ± | f | \pm|f| . Furthermore, we characterize the eigenvalue distribution of certain preconditioned flipped multilevel Toeplitz sequences with an analysis that covers both multilevel Toeplitz and circulant preconditioners. Finally, all our findings are illustrated by several numerical experiments.

2
Preliminaries

In this section we formalize the definition of multilevel (block) Toeplitz sequences associated with a Lebesgue integrable (matrix-valued) function. Next, we define the spectral distribution, in the sense of the eigenvalues and of the singular values, of a generic matrix sequence.
To deal with the spectral distribution of preconditioned flipped multilevel Toeplitz matrices, we introduce a class of matrix sequences (the multilevel block GLT class) that contains multilevel block Toeplitz sequences.

Whenever we use the expression

2.3
Multilevel block generalized locally Toeplitz class

In the sequel, we introduce the
*
-algebra of multilevel block generalized locally Toeplitz (GLT) matrix sequences
[
6
,
7
]
. The formal definition of this class is rather technical and involves somewhat cumbersome notation: therefore we just give and briefly discuss a few properties of the multilevel block GLT class, which are sufficient for studying the spectral features of preconditioned flipped multilevel Toeplitz matrices.

Throughout, we use the notation

\{A_{\boldsymbol{n}}\}_{\boldsymbol{n}}\sim_{\rm GLT}{\kappa(\boldsymbol{x},\boldsymbol{\theta})},\quad\ \ \kappa:[0,1]^{d}\times[-\pi,\pi]^{d}\rightarrow\mathbb{C}^{s\times s}

to indicate that the sequence
\{A_{\boldsymbol{n}}\}_{\boldsymbol{n}}
is a
d
-level
s\times s
-block GLT sequence with GLT symbol
\kappa(\boldsymbol{x},\boldsymbol{\theta})
.

Here we list five of the main features of multilevel block GLT sequences.

GLT1

Let
\{A_{\boldsymbol{n}}\}_{\boldsymbol{n}}\sim_{\rm GLT}\kappa
with
\kappa:G\rightarrow\mathbb{C}^{s\times s}
,
G=[0,1]^{d}\times[-\pi,\pi]^{d}
. Then
\{A_{\boldsymbol{n}}\}_{\boldsymbol{n}}\sim_{\sigma}(\kappa,G)
. If the matrices
A_{\boldsymbol{n}}
are Hermitian, then it also holds that
\{A_{\boldsymbol{n}}\}_{\boldsymbol{n}}\sim_{\lambda}(\kappa,G)
.

GLT2

The set of block GLT sequences forms a
*
-algebra, i.e., it is closed under linear combinations, products, inversion and conjugation. In formulae, let
\{A_{\boldsymbol{n}}\}_{\boldsymbol{n}}\sim_{\rm GLT}\kappa_{1}
and
\{B_{\boldsymbol{n}}\}_{\boldsymbol{n}}\sim_{\rm GLT}\kappa_{2}
, then

\bullet

\{\alpha A_{\boldsymbol{n}}+\beta B_{\boldsymbol{n}}\}_{\boldsymbol{n}}\sim_{\rm GLT}\alpha\kappa_{1}+\beta\kappa_{2},\quad\alpha,\beta\in\mathbb{C};

\bullet

\{A_{\boldsymbol{n}}B_{\boldsymbol{n}}\}_{\boldsymbol{n}}\sim_{\rm GLT}\kappa_{1}\kappa_{2};

\bullet

\{A^{-1}_{\boldsymbol{n}}\}_{\boldsymbol{n}}\sim_{\rm GLT}\kappa_{1}^{-1}
provided that
\kappa_{1}
is invertible a.e.;

\bullet

\{A_{\boldsymbol{n}}^{*}\}_{\boldsymbol{n}}\sim_{\rm GLT}{\kappa^{*}_{1}}.

GLT3

Any sequence of multilevel block Toeplitz matrices
\{T_{\boldsymbol{n}}(f)\}_{\boldsymbol{n}}
generated by a function
f:[-\pi,\pi]^{d}\rightarrow\mathbb{C}^{s\times s}
, with

such that

d
-level
s\times s
-block GLT sequence with symbol
\kappa(\boldsymbol{x},\boldsymbol{\theta})=f(\boldsymbol{\theta})
.

GLT4

Let
\{A_{\boldsymbol{n}}\}_{\boldsymbol{n}}\sim_{\sigma}0
. We say that
\{A_{\boldsymbol{n}}\}_{\boldsymbol{n}}
is a
zero-distributed matrix sequence
. Note that for any

the

and viceversa, i.e.,
\{A_{\boldsymbol{n}}\}_{\boldsymbol{n}}\sim_{\sigma}0

\iff

\{A_{\boldsymbol{n}}\}_{\boldsymbol{n}}\sim_{\rm GLT}O_{s}
.

GLT5

Let
\{A_{\boldsymbol{n}}\}_{\boldsymbol{n}}
be a
d
-level matrix sequence and let

be a sequence of matrix sequences that satisfies the following condition: for each

\{A_{\boldsymbol{n}}\}_{\boldsymbol{n}}\sim_{\rm GLT}\kappa

Theorem 11

Let

\displaystyle\{Y_{\boldsymbol{n}}T_{\boldsymbol{n}}(f)\}_{\boldsymbol{n}}\sim_{\lambda}(g,[-\pi,\pi]^{d}),\text{\,\,\, with \,\,\,}g=\begin{bmatrix}0&f\\
f^{\ast}&0\end{bmatrix}.

Proof.

In the case that

,

for each

10

that

In all other cases, by recalling

Theorem 12
.

Let
\{T_{\boldsymbol{n}}(f)\}_{\boldsymbol{n}}
,
T_{\boldsymbol{n}}(f)\in\mathbb{R}^{d_{\boldsymbol{n}}\times d_{\boldsymbol{n}}}

Proof.

The thesis follows from the combination of

by noticing that

Conclusions

We have shown that the asymptotic eigenvalue distribution of
\{Y_{\boldsymbol{n}}T_{\boldsymbol{n}}(f)\}_{\boldsymbol{n}}
,
where
T_{\boldsymbol{n}}(f)
is a square real multilevel Toeplitz matrix generated by
f\in L^{1}([-\pi,\pi]^{d})
and

Y_{\boldsymbol{n}}
is the exchange matrix, is governed by a
2\times 2
matrix-valued function
whose eigenvalues are
\pm|f|
. We have also investigated the asymptotic eigenvalue distribution of
preconditioned sequences
\{\mathcal{P}^{-1}_{\boldsymbol{n}}Y_{\boldsymbol{n}}T_{\boldsymbol{n}}(f)\}_{\boldsymbol{n}}
, where

\mathcal{P}_{\boldsymbol{n}}
is Hermitian positive definite,

\{\mathcal{P}_{\boldsymbol{n}}\}_{\boldsymbol{n}}\sim_{\rm GLT}h
, and
\{\Pi_{\boldsymbol{n}}U_{\boldsymbol{n}}\mathcal{P}_{\boldsymbol{n}}U_{\boldsymbol{n}}\Pi_{\boldsymbol{n}}^{T}\}_{\boldsymbol{n}}\sim_{\rm GLT}h\cdot I_{2}
with
h:[-\pi,\pi]^{d}\rightarrow\mathbb{C}
and
h\neq 0
a.e.
The latter result enables us to analyse the convergence of preconditioned MINRES for this problem at least in the two quite common cases where the preconditioners are multilevel circulant or multilevel Toeplitz matrices.
