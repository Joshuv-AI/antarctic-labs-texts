Has J-PARC E07 observed a Ξ 1 ​ s − \Xi^{-}_{1s} nuclear state? Journal: Physics Letters B
E. Friedman, A. Gal

Abstract
The Strangeness 𝒮 = − 2 {\cal S}=-2 J-PARC E07 emulsion experiment published recently two new Ξ − \Xi^{-} – 14 N capture events, IRRAWADDY and IBUKI, identified by observing weak-decay sequences of pairs of single- Λ \Lambda hypernuclei and interpreted as Ξ 1 ​ s − \Xi^{-}_{1s} and Ξ 1 ​ p − \Xi^{-}_{1p} nuclear bound states, respectively, with binding energies B Ξ − 1 ​ s B_{\Xi^{-}}^{1s} =6.27 ± \pm 0.27 MeV and B Ξ − 1 ​ p B_{\Xi^{-}}^{1p} =1.27 ± \pm 0.21 MeV. Ξ − \Xi^{-} capture events in emulsion play a major role in determining the Ξ \Xi -nuclear and the Λ ​ Λ \Lambda\Lambda potential strengths. Here we question the assignment of a Ξ 1 ​ s − \Xi^{-}_{1s} nuclear bound state to IRRAWADDY and offer an alternative assignment as a near-threshold Ξ 1 ​ p 0 \Xi^{0}_{1p} – 14 C nuclear bound state, slightly admixed with a Ξ 1 ​ p − \Xi^{-}_{1p} – 14 N nuclear bound state component corresponding to IBUKI. We also question using IBUKI’s B Ξ − 1 ​ p B_{\Xi^{-}}^{1p} value as is to determine the Ξ \Xi -nuclear potential depth. Altogether, Ξ − \Xi^{-} capture events in 12 C and 16 O should prove less ambiguous than in 14 N.
