Abstract
In this paper, we develop the new method to compute the homotopy groups of the mapping cone C f = Y ∪ f C X C_{f}=Y\cup_{f}CX beyond the metastable range by analysing the homotopy of the n n -th filtration of the relative James construction J ⁡ ( X , A ) J(X,A) for CW-pair A ↪ i X A\stackrel{{\scriptstyle i}}{{\hookrightarrow}}X , defined by B. Gray, which is homotopy equivalent to the homotopy fiber of the pinch map X ∪ i C A → Σ A X\cup_{i}CA\rightarrow\Sigma A . As an application, we compute the 5 and 6-dim unstable homotopy groups of 3-dimensional mod 2 r 2^{r} Moore spaces for all positive integers r r .
