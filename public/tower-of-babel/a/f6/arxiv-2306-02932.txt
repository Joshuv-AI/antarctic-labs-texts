Abstract
We study metric invariants of Riemannian manifolds X X defined via the 𝕋 ⋊ \mathbb{T}^{\rtimes} - stabilized scalar curvatures of manifolds Y Y mapped to X X and prove in some cases additivity of these invariants under Riemannian products X 1 × X 2 X_{1}\times X_{2} .
