Cohomology theory of Rota-Baxter pre-Lie algebras of arbitrary weights
Shuangjian Guo, Yufei Qin, Kai Wang and Guodong Zhou

Abstract
. This paper is devoted to study deformation, cohomology theory of Rota-Baxter pre-Lie algebras of arbitrary weights. First we give the notion of a new representation of a Rota-Baxter pre-Lie algebra of arbitrary weight and define the cohomology theory of a Rota-Baxter pre-Lie algebra of arbitrary weight. Then we study formal deformations by lower degree cohomology groups. Finally, we classify abelian extensions of Rota-Baxter pre-Lie algebras of arbitrary weight using the second cohomology group, and classify skeletal Rota-Baxter pre-Lie 2-algebra of arbitrary weight using the third cohomology group as applications.

Definition 1.1

\displaystyle(x,y,z)=(y,x,z),\text{ or \, equivalently},(x\cdot y)\cdot z-x\cdot(y\cdot z)=(y\cdot x)\cdot z-y\cdot(x\cdot z).

Let

\displaystyle[x,y]_{A}=x\cdot y-y\cdot x,\text{ for }x,y\in A.

Furthermore, the map

2.
Rota-Baxter operators on pre-Lie algebras

In this section, we introduce basic definitions and facts about Rota-Baxter pre-Lie algebra.

Definition 2.1
.

Let
\lambda\in{\bf k}
.
Given a pre-Lie algebra
({\mathfrak{g}},\mu=\cdot)
, it is called a Rota-Baxter pre-Lie algebra of weight
\lambda
, if there is a linear
operator
T:{\mathfrak{g}}\rightarrow{\mathfrak{g}}
subject to

(1)

\displaystyle T(a)\cdot T(b)=T\big(a\cdot T(b)+T(a)\cdot b+\lambda\ a\cdot b\big)

for arbitrary
a,~b\in{\mathfrak{g}}
, or in terms of maps

(2)

\displaystyle\mu\circ(T\otimes T)=T\circ\mu\circ({\rm Id}\otimes T+T\otimes{\rm Id})+\lambda\ T\circ\mu.

In this case,
T
is called a Rota-Baxter operator of weight
\lambda
.

Denote by
\mathsf{RBPLA_{\lambda}}
the category of Rota-Baxter pre-Lie algebras with obvious morphisms.

Inspired by
[

3.

3.1.
Cohomology of Rota-Baxter Operators

Firstly, let’s study the cohomology of Rota-Baxter
operators.

Let
({\mathfrak{g}},\mu,T)
be a
Rota-Baxter pre-Lie algebra and
(M,\cdot,T_{M})
be a Rota-Baxter bimodule over it. Recall that

2.5
and Proposition

give a new

and
a new Rota-Baxter bimodule
{}_{\rhd}M_{\lhd}
over
{\mathfrak{g}}_{\star}

Consider the Hochschild cochain complex of

with
coefficients in
{}_{\rhd}M_{\lhd}

{\mathrm{C}}^{\bullet}_{{\mathrm{PLA}}}({\mathfrak{g}}_{\star},{{}_{\rhd}M_{\lhd}})=\bigoplus\limits_{n=0}^{\infty}{\mathrm{C}}^{n}_{{\mathrm{PLA}}}({\mathfrak{g}}_{\star},{{}_{\rhd}M_{\lhd}}).

More precisely, for
n\geqslant 0

\displaystyle\partial^{n}(f)(a_{1,n+1})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum^{n}_{i=1}(-1)^{i+1}x_{i}\rhd f(x_{1},...,\hat{x}_{i},...,x_{n+1})+\sum^{n}_{i=1}(-1)^{i+1}f(x_{1},...,\hat{x}_{i},...,x_{n},x_{i})\lhd x_{n+1}

4.
Formal deformations

In this section, we will study deformations of Rota-Baxter pre-Lie algebras and interpret them via lower degree cohomology groups of Rota-Baxter pre-Lie algebras defined in last section.

4.1.
Formal deformations of Rota-Baxter pre-Lie algebras

Let
({\mathfrak{g}},\mu,T)
be a Rota-Baxter pre-Lie algebra of weight
\lambda
. Consider a 1-parameterized family:

\mu_{t}=\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}\mu_{i}t^{i},\ \mu_{i}\in{\mathrm{C}}^{2}_{\mathrm{PLA}}({\mathfrak{g}}),\quad T_{t}=\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}T_{i}t^{i},\ T_{i}\in{\mathrm{C}}^{1}_{\mathsf{RBO}}({\mathfrak{g}}).

Definition 4.1
.

A 1-parameter formal deformation of Rota-Baxter pre-Lie algebra
({\mathfrak{g}},\mu,T)
is a pair
(\mu_{t},T_{t})
which endows the flat
{\bf k}[[t]]
-module
{\mathfrak{g}}[[t]]
with a Rota-Baxter pre-Lie algebra structure over
{\bf k}[[t]]
such that
(\mu_{0},T_{0})=(\mu,T)
.

Power series
\mu_{t}
and
T_{t}
determine a 1-parameter formal deformation of Rota-Baxter pre-Lie algebra
({\mathfrak{g}},\mu,T)
if and only if for arbitrary
a,b,c\in{\mathfrak{g}}
, the following equations hold :

\displaystyle\mu_{t}(\mu_{t}(a\otimes b)\otimes c)-\mu_{t}(a\otimes\mu_{t}(b\otimes c))

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\mu_{t}(\mu_{t}(b\otimes a)\otimes c)-\mu_{t}(b\otimes\mu_{t}(a\otimes c)),

\displaystyle\mu_{t}(T_{t}(a)\otimes T_{t}(b))

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle T_{t}\Big(\mu_{t}(a\otimes T_{t}(b))+\mu_{t}(T_{t}(a)\otimes b)+\lambda\mu_{t}(a\otimes b)\Big).

By expanding these equations and comparing the coefficient of
t^{n}
, we obtain that
\{\mu_{i}\}_{i\geqslant 0}
and
\{T_{i}\}_{i\geqslant 0}
have to satisfy: for arbitrary
n\geqslant 0
,

(9)

\sum_{i=0}^{n}\mu_{i}\circ(\mu_{n-i}(a\otimes b)\otimes c)-\sum_{i=0}^{n}\mu_{i}\circ(a\otimes\mu_{n-i}(b\otimes c))=\sum_{i=0}^{n}\mu_{i}\circ(\mu_{n-i}(b\otimes a)\otimes c)-\sum_{i=0}^{n}\mu_{i}\circ(b\otimes\mu_{n-i}(a\otimes c)),

(10)

\begin{array}[]{rcl}\sum_{i+j+k=n\atop i,j,k\geqslant 0}\mu_{i}\circ(T_{j}\otimes T_{k})&=&\sum_{i+j+k=n\atop i,j,k\geqslant 0}T_{i}\circ\mu_{j}\circ({\rm Id}\otimes T_{k})\\
&&+\sum_{i+j+k=n\atop i,j,k\geqslant 0}T_{i}\circ\mu_{j}\circ(T_{k}\otimes{\rm Id})+\lambda\sum_{i+j=n\atop i,j\geqslant 0}T_{i}\circ\mu_{j}.\end{array}

Obviously, when
n=0
, the above conditions are exactly the associativity of
\mu=\mu_{0}
and Equation (
33
) which is the defining relation of Rota-Baxter operator
T=T_{0}
.

Proposition 4.2
.

Let
({\mathfrak{g}}[[t]],\mu_{t},T_{t})
be a 1-parameter formal deformation of
Rota-Baxter pre-Lie algebra
({\mathfrak{g}},\mu,T)
of weight
\lambda
. Then

(\mu_{1},T_{1})
is a 2-cocycle in the cochain complex

C_{\mathsf{RBPLA_{\lambda}}}^{\bullet}({\mathfrak{g}})
.

Proof.

When
n=1
, Equations (
9
) and (
10
) become

\displaystyle\mu_{1}(a\otimes b)\cdot c+\mu_{1}((a\cdot b)\otimes c)-a\cdot\mu_{1}(b\otimes c)-\mu_{1}(a\otimes b\cdot c)

\displaystyle=\mu_{1}(b\otimes a)\cdot c+\mu_{1}((b\cdot a)\otimes c)-b\cdot\mu_{1}(a\otimes c)-\mu_{1}(b\otimes a\cdot c),

and

\begin{array}[]{cl}&\mu_{1}(T\otimes T)-\{T\circ\mu_{1}\circ({\rm Id}\otimes T)+T\circ\mu_{1}\circ(T\otimes{\rm Id})+\lambda T\circ\mu_{1}\}\\
=&-\{\mu\circ(T\otimes T_{1})+\mu\circ(T_{1}\otimes T)-T\circ\mu\circ({\rm Id}\otimes T_{1})-T\circ\mu\circ(T_{1}\otimes{\rm Id})\}\\
&+\{T_{1}\circ\mu\circ({\rm Id}\otimes T)+T_{1}\circ\mu\circ(T\otimes{\rm Id})+\lambda T_{1}\circ\mu\}.\end{array}

Note that the first equation is exactly
\delta^{2}(\mu_{1})=0\in{\mathrm{C}}^{\bullet}_{{\mathrm{PLA}}}({\mathfrak{g}})
and that second equation is exactly to

\Phi^{2}(\mu_{1})=-\partial^{1}(T_{1})\in{\mathrm{C}}^{\bullet}_{\mathsf{RBO}}({\mathfrak{g}}).

So
(\mu_{1},T_{1})
is a 2-cocycle in
{\mathrm{C}}^{\bullet}_{\mathsf{RBPLA_{\lambda}}}({\mathfrak{g}})
.
∎

Definition 4.3
.

The 2-cocycle
(\mu_{1},T_{1})
is called the infinitesimal of the 1-parameter formal deformation
({\mathfrak{g}}[[t]],\mu_{t},T_{t})
of Rota-Baxter pre-Lie algebra
({\mathfrak{g}},\mu,T)
.

In general, we can rewrite Equation (
9
) and (
10
) as

(11)

\displaystyle\delta^{2}(\mu_{n})(a\otimes b\otimes c)=\sum_{i=1}^{n-1}(\mu_{i}\circ\mu_{n-i})(a\otimes b\otimes c)-\sum_{i=1}^{n-1}(\mu_{i}\circ\mu_{n-i})(b\otimes a\otimes c)

(12)

\begin{array}[]{rcl}\partial^{1}(T_{n})+\Phi^{2}(\mu_{n})&=&\sum_{i+j+k=n\atop 0\leqslant i,j,k\leqslant n-1}\mu_{i}\circ(T_{j}\otimes T_{k})-\sum_{i+j+k=n\atop 0\leqslant i,j,k\leqslant n-1}T_{i}\circ\mu_{j}\circ({\rm Id}\otimes T_{k})\\
&&-\sum_{i+j+k=n\atop 0\leqslant i,j,k\leqslant n-1}T_{i}\circ\mu_{j}\circ(T_{k}\otimes{\rm Id})-\sum_{i+j=n\atop 0\leqslant i,j\leqslant n-1}T_{i}\circ\mu_{j}.\end{array}

Definition 4.4
.

Let
({\mathfrak{g}}[[t]],\mu_{t},T_{t})
and
({\mathfrak{g}}[[t]],\mu_{t}^{\prime},T_{t}^{\prime})
be two 1-parameter formal deformations of Rota-Baxter pre-Lie algebra
({\mathfrak{g}},\mu,T)
. A formal isomorphism from
({\mathfrak{g}}[[t]],\mu_{t}^{\prime},T_{t}^{\prime})
to
({\mathfrak{g}}[[t]],\mu_{t},T_{t})
is a power series
\psi_{t}=\sum_{i=0}\psi_{i}t^{i}:{\mathfrak{g}}[[t]]\rightarrow{\mathfrak{g}}[[t]]
, where
\psi_{i}:{\mathfrak{g}}\rightarrow{\mathfrak{g}}
are linear maps with
\psi_{0}={\rm Id}_{\mathfrak{g}}
, such that:

(13)

\displaystyle\psi_{t}\circ\mu_{t}^{\prime}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\mu_{t}\circ(\psi_{t}\otimes\psi_{t}),

(14)

\displaystyle\psi_{t}\circ T_{t}^{\prime}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle T_{t}\circ\psi_{t}.

In this case, we say that the two 1-parameter formal deformations
({\mathfrak{g}}[[t]],\mu_{t},T_{t})
and

({\mathfrak{g}}[[t]],\mu_{t}^{\prime},T_{t}^{\prime})
are equivalent.

Given a Rota-Baxter pre-Lie algebra
({\mathfrak{g}},\mu,T)
, the power series
\mu_{t},~~T_{t}

with
\mu_{i}=\delta_{i,0}\mu,~~T_{i}=\delta_{i,0}T
make

({\mathfrak{g}}[[t]],\mu_{t},T_{t})
into a
1
-parameter formal deformation of

({\mathfrak{g}},\mu,T)
. Formal deformations equivalent to this one are called trivial.

Theorem 4.5
.

The infinitesimals of two equivalent 1-parameter formal deformations of
({\mathfrak{g}},\mu,T)
are in the same cohomology class in
{\mathrm{H}}^{\bullet}_{\mathsf{RBPLA_{\lambda}}}({\mathfrak{g}})
.

Proof.

Let
\psi_{t}:({\mathfrak{g}}[[t]],\mu_{t}^{\prime},T_{t}^{\prime})\rightarrow({\mathfrak{g}}[[t]],\mu_{t},T_{t})
be a formal isomorphism.
Expanding the identities and collecting coefficients of
t
, we get from Equations (
13
) and (
14
):

\displaystyle\mu_{1}^{\prime}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\mu_{1}+\mu\circ({\rm Id}\otimes\psi_{1})-\psi_{1}\circ\mu+\mu\circ(\psi_{1}\otimes{\rm Id}),

\displaystyle T_{1}^{\prime}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle T_{1}+T\circ\psi_{1}-\psi_{1}\circ T,

that is, we have

(\mu_{1}^{\prime},T_{1}^{\prime})-(\mu_{1},T_{1})=(\delta^{1}(\psi_{1}),-\Phi^{1}(\psi_{1}))=d^{1}(\psi_{1},0)\in{\mathrm{C}}^{\bullet}_{\mathsf{RBPLA_{\lambda}}}({\mathfrak{g}}).

∎

Definition 4.6
.

A Rota-Baxter pre-Lie algebra
({\mathfrak{g}},\mu,T)
is said to be rigid if every 1-parameter formal deformation is trivial.

Theorem 4.7
.

Let
({\mathfrak{g}},\mu,T)
be a Rota-Baxter pre-Lie algebra of weight
\lambda
. If
{\mathrm{H}}^{2}_{\mathsf{RBPLA_{\lambda}}}({\mathfrak{g}})=0
, then
({\mathfrak{g}},\mu,T)
is rigid.

Proof.

Let
({\mathfrak{g}}[[t]],\mu_{t},T_{t})
be a
1
-parameter formal deformation of
({\mathfrak{g}},\mu,T)
. By Proposition
4.2
,

(\mu_{1},T_{1})
is a
2
-cocycle. By
{\mathrm{H}}^{2}_{\mathsf{RBPLA_{\lambda}}}({\mathfrak{g}})=0
, there exists a
1
-cochain

(\psi_{1}^{\prime},x)\in{\mathrm{C}}^{1}_{\mathsf{RBPLA_{\lambda}}}({\mathfrak{g}})=C^{1}_{{\mathrm{PLA}}}({\mathfrak{g}})\oplus{\rm Hom}(k,{\mathfrak{g}})

such that

(\mu_{1},T_{1})=d^{1}(\psi_{1}^{\prime},x),

that is,
\mu_{1}=\delta^{1}(\psi_{1}^{\prime})
and
T_{1}=-\partial^{0}(x)-\Phi^{1}(\psi_{1}^{\prime})
. Let
\psi_{1}=\psi_{1}^{\prime}+\delta^{0}(x)
. Then

\mu_{1}=\delta^{1}(\psi_{1})
and
T_{1}=-\Phi^{1}(\psi_{1})
, as it can be readily seen that
\Phi^{1}(\delta^{0}(x))=\partial^{0}(x)
.

Setting
\psi_{t}={\rm Id}_{\mathfrak{g}}-\psi_{1}t
, we have a deformation
({\mathfrak{g}}[[t]],\overline{\mu}_{t},\overline{T}_{t})
, where

\overline{\mu}_{t}=\psi_{t}^{-1}\circ\mu_{t}\circ(\psi_{t}\times\psi_{t})

and

\overline{T}_{t}=\psi_{t}^{-1}\circ T_{t}\circ\psi_{t}.

It can be easily verify that
\overline{\mu}_{1}=0,\overline{T}_{1}=0
. Then

\begin{array}[]{rcl}\overline{\mu}_{t}&=&\mu+\overline{\mu}_{2}t^{2}+\cdots,\\
T_{t}&=&T+\overline{T}_{2}t^{2}+\cdots.\end{array}

By Equations (
11
) and (
12
), we see that
(\overline{\mu}_{2},\overline{T}_{2})
is still a
2
-cocyle, so by induction, we can show that

({\mathfrak{g}}[[t]],\mu_{t},T_{t})
is equivalent to the trivial extension
({\mathfrak{g}}[[t]],\mu,T).

Thus,
({\mathfrak{g}},\mu,T)
is rigid.

∎

4.2.
Formal deformations of Rota-Baxter operator with product fixed

Let
({\mathfrak{g}},\mu=\cdot,T)
be a Rota-Baxter pre-Lie algebra of weight
\lambda
. Let us consider the case where we only deform the Rota-Baxter operator with the product fixed. So
{\mathfrak{g}}[[t]]=\{\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}a_{i}t^{i}\ |\ a_{i}\in{\mathfrak{g}},\forall i\geqslant 0\}
is endowed with the product induced from that of
{\mathfrak{g}}
, say,

(\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}a_{i}t^{i})(\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}b_{j}t^{j})=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(\sum_{i+j=n\atop i,j\geqslant 0}a_{i}b_{j})t^{n}.

Then
{\mathfrak{g}}[[t]]
becomes a flat
{\bf k}[[t]]
-algebra, whose product is still denoted by
\mu
.

In this case, a 1-parameter formal deformation
(\mu_{t},T_{t})
of Rota-Baxter pre-Lie algebra
({\mathfrak{g}},\mu,T)
satisfies

\mu_{i}=0,\forall i\geqslant 1
. So Equation (
9
) degenerates and Equation (
10
) becomes

\displaystyle\mu\circ(T_{t}\otimes T_{t})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle T_{t}\circ\Big(\mu\circ({\rm Id}\otimes T_{t})+\mu\circ(T_{t}\otimes{\rm Id})+\lambda\mu\Big).

Expanding these equations and comparing the coefficient of
t^{n}
, we obtain that
\{T_{i}\}_{i\geqslant 0}
have to satisfy: for arbitrary
n\geqslant 0
,

(15)

\displaystyle\sum_{i+j=n\atop i,j\geqslant 0}\mu\circ(T_{i}\otimes T_{j})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{i+j=n\atop i,j\geqslant 0}T_{i}\circ\mu\circ({\rm Id}\otimes T_{j})+\sum_{i+j=n\atop i,j\geqslant 0}T_{i}\circ\mu\circ(T_{j}\otimes{\rm Id})+\lambda T_{n}\circ\mu.

Obviously, when
n=0
, Equation (
15
) becomes
exactly Equation (
33
) defining Rota-Baxter operator
T=T_{0}
.

When
n=1
, Equation (
15
) has the form

\mu\circ(T\otimes T_{1}+T_{1}\otimes T)=T\circ\mu\circ({\rm Id}\otimes T_{1})+T_{1}\circ\mu\circ({\rm Id}\otimes T)+T\circ\mu\circ(T_{1}\otimes{\rm Id})+T_{1}\circ\mu\circ(T\otimes{\rm Id})+\lambda T_{1}\circ\mu

which says exactly that
\partial^{1}(T_{1})=0\in{\mathrm{C}}^{\bullet}_{\mathsf{RBO}}({\mathfrak{g}})
.
This proves the following result:

Proposition 4.8
.

Let
T_{t}
be a 1-parameter formal deformation of
Rota-Baxter operator
T
of weight
\lambda
. Then

T_{1}
is a 1-cocycle in the cochain complex

{\mathrm{C}}_{\mathsf{RBO}}^{\bullet}({\mathfrak{g}})
.

This means that the cochain complex
{\mathrm{C}}^{\bullet}_{\mathsf{RBO}}({\mathfrak{g}})
controls formal deformations of Rota-Baxter operators.

5.
Abelian extensions

In this section, we study abelian extensions of Rota-Baxter pre-Lie algebras and show that they are classified by the second cohomology, as one would expect of a good cohomology theory.

Notice that a vector space
M
together with a linear transformation
T_{M}:M\to M
is naturally a Rota-Baxter pre-Lie algebra where the multiplication on
M
is defined to be
u\cdot v=0
for all
u,v\in M.

Definition 5.1
.

An abelian extension of Rota-Baxter pre-Lie algebras is a short exact sequence of morphisms of Rota-Baxter pre-Lie algebras

(16)

\displaystyle 0\to(M,\cdot,T_{M})\stackrel{{\scriptstyle i}}{{\to}}(\hat{{\mathfrak{g}}},\hat{\cdot},\hat{T})\stackrel{{\scriptstyle p}}{{\to}}({\mathfrak{g}},\cdot,T)\to 0,

that is, there exists a commutative diagram:

Definition 5.2

Let

With the above notations,

Proof.

For arbitrary

\displaystyle a\cdot(b\cdot m)-(a\cdot b)\cdot m

\displaystyle s(a)\cdot(s(b)\cdot m)-s(a\cdot b)\cdot m

\displaystyle s(b)\cdot(s(a)\cdot m)-s(b\cdot a)\cdot m

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle b\cdot(a\cdot m)-(b\cdot a)\cdot m.

{\hat{T}}(s(a))-s(T(a))\in M

We further define linear maps

(i)

\displaystyle\psi_{1}(a,b)

Proposition 5.6

The proof of other direction is similar, and the proof is finished.

We recall the cohomology of Rota-Baxter associative algebras with any weight which was first defined by W. Kai and G. Zhou

Definition 7.1
