Fields of locally compact quantum groups: continuity and pushouts
Alexandru Chirvasitu

Abstract
We prove that (a) discrete compact quantum groups (or more generally locally compact, under additional hypotheses) with coamenable dual are continuous fields over their central closed quantum subgroups, and (b) the same holds for free products of discrete quantum groups with coamenable dual amalgamated over a common central subgroup. Along the way we also show that free products of continuous fields of C ∗ C^{*} -algebras are again free via a Fell-topology characterization for C ∗ C^{*} -field continuity, recovering a result of Blanchard’s in a somewhat more general setting.



Key words:
C^{*}
-algebra; continuous field; weak containment; Fell topology; locally compact quantum group; discrete quantum group; pushout; free product with amalgamation

Fields over central locally compact quantum groups

We begin with

Theorem 2.1
.

Let
G
be an LCQG with coamenable dual and
H\leq G
a central closed quantum subgroup.

(a)

If

also has coamenable dual then

(b)

The hypothesis in

Proof
.

We treat the two clauses separately.

Part
Item

a
We mimic the proof of
[

x_{\alpha}\to x\in\widehat{H}

of characters, we have to argue that an arbitrary unitary representation

is in the Fell closure of a family of unitary representations where

acts by

. The coamenability condition ensures that

has coamenable dual and hence its regular representation weakly contains its trivial representation

. We thus similarly have the weak containment

{\bf 1}_{G}\preceq\mathrm{Ind}_{H}^{G}({\bf 1}_{H}),

and it follows that

\rho\cong\rho\otimes{\bf 1}_{G}\preceq\rho\otimes\mathrm{Ind}_{H}^{G}({\bf 1}_{H});

the latter representation is a Fell-topology limit of

\rho\otimes\mathrm{Ind}_{H}^{G}(p_{x_{\alpha}x^{-1}})

where

, hence the conclusion.

Part
Item

b
According to
[

the amenability of

entails that of

,whereas by
[

amenability and dual coamenability are equivalent for discrete quantum groups.

\blacksquare

A comment on the literature

The present side-note is devoted to an issue I believe is present in the proof of
[
7
, Theorem 3.7]
. Though apparently the problem is fixable, the proof as-is posed some difficulties (for this reader, at least). The setup is as follows: one considers an arbitrary element
a
in the dense purely algebraic pushout

A_{1}*^{\mathrm{alg}}_{C(X)}A_{2}\subset A_{1}*_{C(X)}A_{2}

and seeks to show that

is lower semicontinuous. This is done for separable

-algebras first, in
[

, Lemma 3.5]
, and then generalized to the present setting by

•

first embedding

where

and

are separable

•

citing
[

to conclude that there is an embedding

D_{1}*_{C(Y)}D_{2}\subseteq A_{1}*_{C(X)}A_{2}.

(3-1)

The problem is with this last step:
[
20
, Theorem 4.2]
proves inclusions of the form

D_{1}*_{C}D_{2}\subseteq A_{1}*_{C}A_{2}

given inclusions

C\subseteq D_{i}\subseteq A_{i},\ i=1,2.

Note that the amalgam

is the same on both sides. On the other hand, given that on the right-hand side of

one amalgamates over a (generally-speaking)

, it is not at all obvious that the natural map

is indeed an inclusion. Indeed, it certainly will not be in full generality:

.

Consider the case where

, but

is trivial (i.e.

) whereas

is not.

is then a

\blacklozenge

Note also that one cannot exhaust

and

respectively by separable

and

and naively hope for a continuity permanence property under filtered colimits

.

Consider the space

(the

This affords us a filtered-colimit description
