Betti Tate’s thesis and the trace of perverse schobers
Benjamin Gammage and Justin Hilburn

Abstract
. We propose a conjecture on the categorical trace of the 2-category of perverse schobers (expected to model the Fukaya-Fueter 2-category of a holomorphic symplectic space). By proving a Betti geometric version of Tate’s thesis, and combining it with our previous 3d mirror symmetry equivalence and the Ben-Zvi–Nadler–Preygel result on spectral traces, we are able to establish our conjecture in the simplest interesting case.



1.
Introduction

Let
\mathcal{L}_{B}(\mathbb{C}/\mathbb{C}^{\times})
be the Betti loop space
{\sf Maps}(S^{1}_{B},\mathbb{C}/\mathbb{C}^{\times})
— i.e., the moduli space of rank-1 Betti local systems on the punctured disk equipped with a flat section — and let
\mathfrak{L}\mathbb{C}=\mathbb{C}((t))
be the loop space of
\mathbb{C},
which we understand as an (ind-infinite type) ind-scheme. Let
\mathcal{S}
be the stratification of
\mathfrak{L}\mathbb{C}
by order of zero or pole at
0\in\mathbb{C}
(where we understand the constant map to 0 as having a zero of infinite order at 0): in other words,
\mathcal{S}
is the stratification of the space
\mathbb{C}((t))
of Laurent polynomials by the valuation
\mathbb{C}((t))\to\mathbb{Z}\cup\{\infty\}
taking a Laurent polynomial to the degree of its lowest-order term. The following result is the main theorem of this paper.

Theorem A

(“Betti Tate’s thesis”)
.

There is an equivalence

(1)

\mathsf{Sh}^{!}_{\mathcal{S}}(\mathfrak{L}\mathbb{C})\simeq\mathsf{IndCoh}(\mathcal{L}_{B}(\mathbb{C}/\mathbb{C}^{\times}))

between the category of (co)sheaves on
\mathfrak{L}\mathbb{C}
with singular support in conormals to strata of
\mathcal{S}
and the category of ind-coherent sheaves on
\mathcal{Y}.
This equivalence intertwines the structure of module categories for the monoidal category whose automorphic and spectral realizations are the left- and right-hand sides, respectively, of the “Betti geometric class field theory” equivalence

(2)

\mathsf{Loc}(\mathfrak{L}\mathbb{C}^{\times})\simeq\mathsf{Loc}(\mathbb{Z}\times\mathbb{C}^{\times})\simeq\mathsf{IndCoh}(B\mathbb{C}^{\times}\times\mathbb{C}^{\times})\simeq\mathsf{IndCoh}(\mathcal{L}_{B}(B\mathbb{C}^{\times})),

where
\mathsf{Loc}
denotes the category of (possibly infinite-dimensional) local systems, and the left- (resp. right-)hand side of (
2
) acts on the left- (resp. right-)hand side of (
1
) by convolution (resp. pullback and tensor).

A de Rham version of this theorem, in which the constructible-(co)sheaf category
\mathsf{Sh}_{\mathcal{S}}^{!}
is replaced by the category
{\sf D}^{!}
of D-modules, and the Betti loop space
\mathcal{L}_{B}(\mathbb{C}/\mathbb{C}^{\times})
is replaced by the de Rham loop space
\mathcal{L}_{dR}(\mathbb{C}/\mathbb{C}^{\times}):={\sf Maps}(\mathring{\mathbb{D}}_{dR},\mathbb{C}/\mathbb{C}^{\times})
(with the categories in
Equation

2
similarly replaced by their de Rham analogues), was proved in
[
20
]
. We refer to the introduction of that paper for more on the physical and mathematical background of this result, and to
[
9
]
for more about the “Betti” approach to dualities in 3d and 4d topological field theories.

1.2.
A-side traces

A

, which we interpret as a theorem about the trace of the 2-category of Ind-coherent sheaves on

Let

\mathbb{L}\subset T^{*}X

between the categorical trace of the 2-category of perverse schobers on

\mathfrak{L}X

1

1

\Lambda(\mathbb{L})\subset T^{*}\mathfrak{L}X
determined by

Unfortunately, it is not currently possible to prove

1.3.
Further remarks

We conclude the introduction with a brief explication of, and context for,
A
and
A
.
The latter conjecture sits as part of a long tradition of relating Hochschild-type invariants of (possibly higher) categories to loop spaces. In the B-model, the fundamental statement is a sort of Hochschild-Kostant-Rosenberg theorem (see for instance
[
8
]
*Corollary 1.21) identifying Hochschild homology of the category
\mathsf{QCoh}(X)
of quasicoherent sheaves on
X
with the space of functions on the (Betti) loop space
\mathcal{L}_{B}X
; a categorical analogue can be found in
[
6
]
. As we described above, this theorem was subsequently modified in
[
7
]
to allow for a nontrivial singular-support Lagrangian.

In the A-model, the fundamental result is Jones’s theorem
[
21
]
relating Hochschild homology of the category
\mathsf{Loc}(M)
of local systems on
M
to the cohomology of the free loop space
\mathfrak{L}M,
which has Fukaya-categorical incarnations as the calculation of symplectic homology of a cotangent bundle
[
28
,
1
,
2
]
. A partially-wrapped (i.e., incorporating a nonzero singular-support Lagrangian) version of this theorem is not yet in the literature, but has a conjectural relation to the
microlocal homology
of the loop space (for which see
[
25
]
*§1.4).
These theorems are what our
A
aims to categorify, with the 2-category of perverse schobers standing in for a conjectural partially wrapped 2-category of holomorphic Lagrangians in
T^{*}X.

In this 2-category, we expect that the singular-support Lagrangian
\mathbb{L}\subset T^{*}X
should determine a complex Hamiltonian function
H:T^{*}X\to\mathbb{C}
controlling the wrapping of Lagrangians, and that the contribution of this function to the symplectic action functional on the free loop space
\mathfrak{L}T^{*}X=T^{*}\mathfrak{L}X
can be used to produce a superpotential
W:T^{*}\mathfrak{L}X\to\mathbb{C}.
The right-hand side of the conjectural equivalence (
5
) is a sheaf-theoretic model for the Fukaya-Seidel type category associated to the Landau-Ginzburg model
(T^{*}\mathfrak{L}X,W).
Examples and more detailed justifications will be postponed to future work which begins to construct this 2-category.

Finally, we point out that the equivalence of categories described in (
1
) is t-exact for the obvious t-structures on both sides — which may seem odd from the perspective of geometric representation theory, where the canonical t-structure on the left-hand side of (
1
) is the
perverse
t-structure. The perverse t-structure is more natural from the perspective of
A
as well, since perverse schobers by construction are intended to categorify perverse sheaves. One can therefore ask what t-structure on the category
\mathsf{IndCoh}(\mathcal{L}_{B}(\mathbb{C}/\mathbb{C}^{\times}))
corresponds to the perverse t-structure. Conjecturally, the answer is the
perverse coherent
t-structure of
[
3
]
(see also
[
12
]
for further discussion, and
[
26
]
for another example of this phenomenon). Our
A
therefore offers some justification for the perverse coherent t-structure within the framework of 3d mirror symmetry: if one begins with a 3d mirror pair as in
Theorem

1.1
, the perverse coherent t-structure on the spectral trace category of
Theorem

1.2
should be equivalent to a perverse t-structure on the automorphic trace category of
A
.

1.4.
Categorical conventions

Throughout this paper, we work in the setting of stable
\mathbb{C}
-linear
(\infty,1)
-categories as in
[
23
,
22
]
, or equivalently pretriangulated
\mathbb{C}
-linear dg-categories as in
[
27
]
. (More general coefficients are possible.) We refer to these simply as “categories.” We work model-independently; in fact, the categories we study are all equipped with natural t-structures and the functors we describe are t-exact, so our statements and proofs may be read at the level of abelian (1,1)-categories without losing any essential information.

Proof of

The remainder of this paper is devoted to the proof of
A
. We begin in
Section

2.1
by reviewing the theory of constructible cosheaves on infinite-type Ind-schemes. We then describe the automorphic category
\mathsf{Sh}^{!}_{\mathcal{S}}(\mathfrak{L}\mathbb{C})

(\mathbb{C}^{\ell+1},\mathcal{T}_{\ell})
described in (
7
). As we shall see, the normal geometry of each stratum (with the slight exception of the smallest stratum) is equivalent, so that the case
\ell=2
will already contain the entire complexity of the situation.

Before considering
\ell=2
, we begin as a warm-up with the case
\ell=1.
In this case, the exit-path category
{\sf Exit}(\mathbb{C},\mathcal{T}_{1})
contains two isomorphism classes of objects, corresponding to the two strata
\{0\}
and
\mathbb{C}^{\times}
; the endomorphisms of the former are trivial, and the endomorphisms of the latter are generated by a single invertible element
m

S^{1}
’s worth of exit-paths from
\{0\}
to
\mathbb{C}^{\times},
or equivalently there is a monodromy-invariant exit path
\{0\}\to\mathbb{C}^{\times}.

In other words, the category
\mathsf{Sh}_{\mathcal{T}_{1}}(\mathbb{C})
is equivalent to the data of a pair of vector spaces
V,W

m\in\mathsf{aut}(W)
and a monodromy-invariant “restriction” map
r:V\to W

Example 2.5
.

Although the language of exit- and entry-path categories provides the cleanest description of the categories
\mathsf{Sh}^{!}_{\mathcal{S}}(X_{k}^{\ell}),
it may obscure some of the actual geometry underlying these categories. In order to clarify the calculations of
Proposition

2.4
for readers more accustomed to concrete sheaf-theoretic calculations, we provide a hands-on calculation of this form in the simplest case,
\ell=1.

It is a corollary of the standard recollement relations that if
\mathcal{S}
is a stratification of
X
with contractible strata, then the category
\mathsf{Sh}_{\mathcal{S}}(X)
is generated by
!
-pushforwards of constant sheaves on strata. (See
[
15
]
for applications of this result in mirror symmetry.) Similarly, when the strata are not contractible, the category may be generated by
!
-pushforwards of the “universal local system” on each stratum. In general this local system will not be concentrated in degree 0, but in our case, when each noncontractible stratum is homotopic to the
K(\pi,1)
space
S^{1},
the universal local system
L_{{\sf univ}}
has stalk
\mathbb{C}[m^{\pm}],
with monodromy given by
m.

In the
\ell=1
case of
proposition

2.4
, the category of interest, namely the category
\mathsf{Sh}_{\mathbb{C}\cup T^{*}_{0}\mathbb{C}}(\mathbb{C})
of sheaves on the stratified space
\mathbb{C}=\mathbb{C}^{\times}\sqcup\{0\},
is generated by the skyscraper sheaf
\delta_{0}=i_{!}\underline{\mathbb{C}}
and the pushforward
j_{!}L_{{\sf univ}}
of the universal local system, where we write
\{0\}\xhookrightarrow{i}\mathbb{C}\xhookleftarrow{j}\mathbb{C}^{\times}
for the respective inclusions of strata. The category may therefore be presented as modules over the algebra

(11)

A:=\mathsf{end}(i_{!}\underline{\mathbb{C}}\oplus j_{!}L_{{\sf univ}})\simeq\left(\begin{array}[]{ll}\mathbb{C}&0\\
\Gamma_{0}(\mathbb{C},j_{!}L_{{\sf univ}})&\mathbb{C}[m^{\pm}]\end{array}\right),

where we write
\Gamma_{0}(j_{!}L_{{\sf univ}})
for sections of the sheaf
i^{!}j_{!}L_{{\sf univ}},
which may be computed using the exact sequence

0\to\Gamma_{0}(\mathbb{C},j_{!}L_{{\sf univ}})\to\Gamma(\mathbb{C},j_{!}L_{{\sf univ}})\to\Gamma(\mathbb{C}^{\times},j^{*}j_{!}L_{{\sf univ}})\to 0

whose middle term is 0 and whose right-hand term is is the cohomology of the universal local system on
\mathbb{C}^{\times}.
We conclude that the bottom-left corner of (

\mathbb{C}[m^{\pm}]\xrightarrow{m-1}\mathbb{C}[m^{\pm}],

Remark 2.6
.

Proposition

2.4
gives a complete description of the categories
\mathsf{Sh}^{!}_{\mathcal{S}}(X_{k})
and

since these are obtained as filtered colimits of the categories

under the functors described. Informally, we may describe

2.4.
The Betti spectral category

To complete the proof of
A

Proposition

2.4
as the category of Ind-coherent sheaves on the Betti loop space
\mathcal{L}_{B}(\mathbb{C}/\mathbb{C}^{\times}).

Lemma 2.7
.

The loop space
\mathcal{L}_{B}(\mathbb{C}/\mathbb{C}^{\times})
admits a global quotient presentation

(12)

\mathcal{L}_{B}(\mathbb{C}/\mathbb{C}^{\times})\simeq\left({\sf Spec}\ \mathbb{C}[x,y^{\pm}]/(x(y-1))\right)/\mathbb{C}^{\times},

where the weights for the
\mathbb{C}^{\times}
action are
|x|=1,|y|=0.

Proof.

The loop space of a global quotient stack
X/G
may be described as the fiber product

\mathcal{L}_{B}(X/G)\simeq(X/G)\times_{(X\times X)/G}(X\times G)/G,

where the left map is the diagonal and the right map is
(a,p),
where
a
is the action and
p
is the projection. In other words, the loop space
\mathcal{L}_{B}(X/G)
is the
G
-quotient of
\{(x,g)\mid gx=x\}.
In the case where
X=\mathbb{C},G=\mathbb{C}^{\times},
this space is

\{(z,g)\in\mathbb{C}\times\mathbb{C}^{\times}\mid gz=z\}=\mathbb{C}\times\{1\}\cup_{(0,1)}\{0\}\times\mathbb{C}^{\times}.

This is precisely the desired presentation of
\mathcal{L}_{B}(\mathbb{C}/\mathbb{C}^{\times})

∎

Remark 2.8
.

Lemma

presents
\mathcal{L}_{B}(\mathbb{C}/\mathbb{C}^{\times})

(13)

\mathcal{L}_{B}(\mathbb{C}/\mathbb{C}^{\times})\simeq\varinjlim\left(\mathbb{C}/\mathbb{C}^{\times}\leftarrow B\mathbb{C}^{\times}\to\mathbb{C}^{\times}\times B\mathbb{C}^{\times}\right).

Since

satisfies descent for the colimit (

]
*Chapter 1, Proposition 2.5.7 of exchanging limits and colimits of presentable categories by passing to left adjoints, the colimit presentation (

\mathsf{IndCoh}(\mathcal{L}_{B}(\mathbb{C}/\mathbb{C}^{\times}))
as the category of triples
(F^{\bullet}V,W^{\bullet}\circlearrowleft\varphi,(W^{\bullet})^{\varphi}\simeq{\sf gr}^{\bullet}(V))

a graded vector space
W^{\bullet}
equipped with an automorphism

and an equivalence (of graded vector spaces) between the 1-eigenspace of

∎

Proposition 2.16

The equivalence of categories

constructed above intertwines convolution by local systems on

with tensor product by coherent sheaves pulled back from
\mathsf{IndCoh}(\mathcal{L}_{B}(B\mathbb{C}^{\times})).

Proof.

First, observe that

is a t-exact functor (for the natural t-structures defined on both sides of the equivalence), and by passing to hearts, it is sufficient to check the statement of the proposition at the level of abelian categories: this follows from
[
18
]
*Theorem A.4, which shows that passing to hearts defines a symmetric monoidal equivalence between a category of Grothendieck abelian categories with enough projective objects, and a category of derived categories of Grothendieck abelian categories with enough projectives.

Now note that an action of the abelian heart of the equivalent symmetric monoidal categories of
Equation

2
on an abelian category
\mathcal{C}
is determined by the data of a
\mathbb{C}[a^{\pm}]
-linear structure on
\mathcal{C}
together with a
\mathbb{C}[a^{\pm}]
-linear autoequivalence
\mathcal{C}\xrightarrow{\sim}\mathcal{C},
and it is straightforward to check that these agree.

Namely, on the left-hand side of (
2

a
on the degree-zero component
\mathbb{C}^{\times}\times\{0\}\subset\mathbb{C}^{\times}\times\mathbb{Z}\simeq\mathfrak{L}\mathbb{C}^{\times}

picks out the monodromy-
a
part of local systems on the strata of
\mathfrak{L}\mathbb{C}
; under the equivalence
F,
this is tensoring with the (placed-in-weight-0) skyscraper sheaf
\delta_{a}\in\mathsf{Coh}(\mathbb{C}^{\times}\times B\mathbb{C}^{\times}).
On the other hand, convolution by the universal local system
L_{{\sf univ}}[n]
on the
n

\mathfrak{L}\mathbb{C}=\varinjlim X_{k}
by
n
iterations of the pushforward

(which makes sense for

\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{C}^{\times}}(n),
the structure sheaf of
\mathbb{C}^{\times}
twisted by weight
n
.
∎
