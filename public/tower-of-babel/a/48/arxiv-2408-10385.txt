Forbidden conductors and sequences of ± 1 \pm 1 s
Maciej Radziejewski

Abstract
. We study “forbidden” conductors, i.e. numbers q > 0 q>0 satisfying algebraic criteria introduced by J. Kaczorowski, A. Perelli and M. Radziejewski, that cannot be conductors of L L -functions of degree 2 2 from the extended Selberg class. We show that the set of forbidden q q is dense in the interval ( 0 , 4 ) (0,4) , solving a problem posed in [ 6 ] . We also find positive points of accumulation of rational forbidden q q .
