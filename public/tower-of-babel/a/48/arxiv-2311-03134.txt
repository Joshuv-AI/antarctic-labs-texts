Martingale approximation of non-stationary stochastic processes
Dalibor Volný

Abstract
We generalise the martingale-coboundary representation of discrete time stochastic processes to the non-stationary case and to random variables in Orlicz spaces. Related limit theorems (CLT, invariance principle, log log law, probabilities of large deviations) are studied.



Classification:
60G10, 60G42, 28D05, 60F05

0. Introduction

Let
(\Omega,A,\mu)
be a probability space and
(X_{i})_{i\in\mathbb{Z}}

a sequence of random variables. We shall say that
(X_{i})
is stationary (we shall always
mean strictly stationary) if there exists a measurable and measure preserving
transformation
T
:
\Omega\to\Omega
such that
X_{i+1}=X_{i}\circ T
; each stationary
sequence can be represented in this way.
We shall study approximations of the sequence
(X_{i})
of the form

X_{i}=Y_{i}+U_{i}-U_{i+1}

[formula]

where
(Y_{i})
is a martingale difference sequence with a filtration
(F_{i})
.
In the stationary case we suppose that
F_{i}=T^{-i}F_{0}
,
i\in\mathbb{Z}
.
For the partial sums
S_{n}=\sum_{i=1}^{n}X_{i}
we get

S_{n}=\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}Y_{i}+U_{0}-U_{n}
hence if
Y_{i},U_{i}
are square integrable
and the norms of
U_{i}
are uniformly bounded,
then

\frac{1}{\sqrt{n}}\Big\|\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}(X_{i}-Y_{i})\Big\|_{2}\to 0

[formula]

(in the stationary case this is equivalent to Gordin’s approximation criterium from

[
Go1
]
, cf.
[
Vo
]
).
The decomposition (G1) enables us to reduce the study of the central limit theorem (CLT)
for
(X_{i})
to the study of the CLT for the martingale difference sequence

(Y_{i})
. If
(Y_{i})
and
(U_{i})
are stationary sequences with finite second moments,
(1) guarantees the Donsker invariance principle and the functional law of iterated logarithm
(cf.
[
Ha-He
]
,
[
Vo-Sa
]
). Even in the general non-stationary case
we can using (1) reduce the study of the probabilities of large deviations for
(X_{i})

to the study of
(Y_{i})
(cf.
[
Les-Vo
]
).

Gordin (
[
Go1
]
, Theorem 2) proved (1) for a stationary sequence of

X_{i}\in L^{2+\delta}
where for
p=(2+\delta)/(1+\delta)
,
\delta\geq 0
, it is

\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}\|E(X_{i}|F_{0})\|_{p}<\infty,\quad\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}\|X_{-i}-E(X_{-i}|F_{0})\|_{p}<\infty.

In
[
Vo
]
it is proved that for a stationary sequence of
X_{i}\in L^{p}

which are
F_{\infty}
measurable and
E(X_{i}|F_{-\infty})=0
,

1\leq p<\infty
(the proof was given for
p=1,2
but is valid for the other values of
p

as well), the convergence of

\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}E(X_{i}|F_{0}),\quad\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}[X_{-i}-E(X_{-i}|F_{0})]

in
L^{p}
is a necessary and sufficient condition for (1).

The idea of the martingale-coboundary decomposition was in its nonstationary form used in

[
Ph-St
,
Lemma~2.1
]
;
in their theorem, the random variables
X_{i}
are adapted to the filtration

(F_{i})
and (1) follows from the convergence of the series

\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}E(X_{k+i}|F_{k})
; the convergence is understood as the convergence of

\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}E|E(X_{k+i}|F_{k})|

(the
Y_{i}
and
U_{i}
are then integrable).

If
X_{i}=U_{i}-U_{i+1}
and the sequence of
U_{i}
is stochastically bounded then the partial
sums
S_{n}
are stochastically bounded as well and in the limit theorems we get a degenerate
case. As shown in
[
Sc
]
, in the stationary case the
S_{n}
are stochastically bounded
if and only if
X_{i}=U_{i}-U_{i+1}
. The result
was generalised to nonstationary sequences in
[
Br
]
.

The decomposition (1) applies to various interesting and important cases. Many of them were
studied in the monography
[
Ha-He
]
, applications to mixing processes
appear in
[
Du-G
]
, in
[
Le
]
the method is used to limit
theorems for nonhyperbolic toral automorphisms.

In this paper we shall give necessary and sufficient condition of (1) in the
non-stationary case. The random variables are defined in more general function
spaces. Several limit theorems (the CLT, the invariance principle, the law of iterated logarithm, the estimates of probabilities of large deviations) implied by the decomposition
are shown.
