Arrival times of Cox process with independent increments with application to prediction problems Thanks: Muneya Matsui’s research is partly supported by JSPS Grant-in-Aid for Young Scientists B (16K16023)
Muneya Matsui

Abstract
. Properties of arrival times are studied for a Cox process with independent (and stationary) increments. Under a reasonable setting the directing random measure is shown to take over independent (and stationary) increments of the process, from which the sets of arrival times and their numbers in disjoint intervals are proved to be independent (and stationary). Moreover, we derive the exact joint distribution of these quantities with Gamma random measure, whereas for a general random measure the method of calculation is presented. Based on the derived properties we consider prediction problems for the shot noise process with Cox process arrival times which trigger additive processes off. We obtain a numerically tractable expression for the predictor which works reasonably in view of numerical experiments.

Preliminaries

In this paper we consider a Cox process

Early on, properties related with arrival times of Poisson processes have been intensively

In this paper we derive properties for arrival times of Cox process

As an application we consider a prediction problem of the model (

Finally we make two remarks.
First our motivation is to construct

In our recognition the independent increments would be the best

Second Cox processes with

An additive process

increments with càdlàg path starting at

The distribution of the process

[

for more details. We will restrict ourselves to processes
having only a pure jump part (see
[

), namely
we consider

(1.2)

\displaystyle E[e^{ixV(u)}]=\exp\Big\{\int_{(0,u]\times\mathbb{R}}(e^{ixv}-1)\,\rho(d(w,v))\Big\},

where a measure

\displaystyle\int_{(0,u]\times\mathbb{R}}(|w|\wedge 1)\,\rho(d(w,v))<\infty,\qquad\mathrm{for}\ u\geq 0.

In this case the generating triplet is
(0,\rho_{u},0)
with
\rho_{u}(B):=\rho((0,u]\times B)

any Borel set

Among additive processes we frequently focus on
Lévy processes which additionally have stationary increments property, so that the
system of generating triplet of

\displaystyle{\mathbb{E}}[e^{-xV(1)}]=\exp\big\{-\gamma x+\int_{(0,\infty)}(e^{-xv}-1)\nu(dv)\big\},\quad x\geq 0

Note that subordinators could be random measures on

2.
Main result

In this section we characterize properties of the directing random measure

\eta
from those of the Cox process
N
, namely, under some reasonable setting
we specify the class of directing
measures
\eta

processes. In addition we show an order statistics-type property for the Cox
processes driven by additive or Lévy
processes. Based on those main results the method of calculating the joint distribution of arrival
times and their number is presented. Moreover,
the exact joint distribution is obtained when

random measure.

Notice that Cox process of this type can be regarded as the time-changed
Lévy process by a subordinator which we call subordination

[

). In our case
the original Lévy process is given by a homogeneous Poisson.
In the literature of subordination, part of our results are known. We
will state the detailed relation with past results after

.

‘only if part’ For the proof, we consider the LP of

\displaystyle{\mathbb{E}}\prod_{j=1}^{k}e^{-\alpha_{j}\eta(t_{j-1},t_{j}]}=\prod_{j=1}^{k}{\mathbb{E}}e^{-\alpha_{j}\eta(t_{j-1},t_{j}]},\quad\alpha_{j}\geq 0,\,j=1,\ldots,k.

\displaystyle{\mathbb{E}}\prod_{j=1}^{k}\frac{\eta(t_{j-1},t_{j}]^{\ell_{j}}}{\ell_{j}!}e^{-\eta(t_{j-1},t_{j}]}=\prod_{j=1}^{k}{\mathbb{E}}\frac{\eta(t_{j-1},t_{j}]^{\ell_{j}}}{\ell_{j}!}e^{-\eta(t_{j-1},t_{j}]},
