Uncountable almost Irredundant Sets in nonseparable C*-algebras
Clayton Suguio Hida

Abstract
. In this article, we consider the notion of almost irredundant sets: A subset 𝒳 \mathcal{X} of a C*-algebra 𝒜 \mathcal{A} is called almost irredundant if and only if for every a ∈ 𝒳 a\in\mathcal{X} , the element a a does not belong to the norm-closure of { ∑ i = 1 n λ i ​ ∏ j = 1 n i a i , j : where ​ a i , j ∈ 𝒳 ∖ { a } ​ and ​ ∑ | λ i | ≤ 1 } . \{\sum_{i=1}^{n}\lambda_{i}\prod_{j=1}^{n_{i}}a_{{i,j}}:\textrm{ where }a_{{i,j}}\in\mathcal{X}\setminus\{a\}\textrm{ and}\sum|\lambda_{i}|\leq 1\}. Since every almost irrredundant set is in particular a discrete set, it follows that the density of 𝒜 \mathcal{A} is an upper bound for the size of almost irredundant sets. We prove that under the Proper Forcing Axiom (PFA), there is an uncountable almost irredundant set in every C*-algebra with an uncountable increasing sequence of ideals. In particular, assuming PFA, every nonseparable scattered C*-algebra admits an uncountable almost irredundant set. Keywords. Almost irredundant sets, scattered C*-algebras, Martin’s Axiom (MA), Proper Forcing Axiom (PFA).

2.
Almost irredundant sets and UBABS+

In this section we study the relation between irredundance and UBABS+. We will prove that the existence of an UBABS+ of type
\eta
for some
0\leq\eta<1
is enough to guarantee the existence of an uncountable almost irredundant set.

We start by proving the following simple inequality about states and positive elements:

Lemma 2.1
.

Let
\mathcal{A}
be an unital C*-algebra. Suppose
a_{1},\cdots,a_{n}
are self adjoint elements such that
0\leq a_{i}\leq 1
and
\tau(a_{i})<\varepsilon
for
\tau
a state. Then

\tau(a_{1}\cdots a_{n})\leq\sqrt{\varepsilon}.

Proof.

The result follows from repeatedly applying the inequalities
|\tau(a)|^{2}\leq\|\tau\|\tau(a^{*}a)
and
{\tau(b^{*}a^{*}ab)\leq\|a^{*}a\|\tau(b^{*}b)}
, which holds for every positive linear functional
\tau
and every
a,b\in\mathcal{A}
(see Theorem 3.3.2 and Theorem 3.3.7 of
[
21
]
).

∎

Theorem 2.2
.

Let
\mathcal{A}
be an unital C*-algebra. Suppose
(a_{\alpha},\tau_{\alpha})_{\alpha<\omega_{1}}
is an UBABS+ of type
\eta
for some
0\leq\eta<1
.
Then
\mathcal{X}=\{a_{\alpha}:\alpha<\omega_{1}\}
is an almost irredundant set.

Proof.

Suppose
\mathcal{X}=\{a_{\alpha}:\alpha<\omega_{1}\}
is not an almost irredundant set and lets get a contradiction.
Consider
\alpha<\omega_{1}
such that
a_{\alpha}
belongs to the closure of

\{a=\sum_{i=1}^{n}\lambda_{i}\prod_{j=1}^{n_{i}}a_{\beta_{i,j}}:\textrm{ where }\beta_{i,j}\neq\alpha\textrm{ and}\sum|\lambda_{i}|\leq 1\}.

Consider
\varepsilon>0
such that
\varepsilon+\sqrt{\eta}<1
and
a=\sum_{i=1}^{n}\lambda_{i}\prod_{j=1}^{n_{i}}a_{\beta_{i,j}}
such that
\|a_{\alpha}-a\|<\varepsilon.
In particular,

|\tau_{\alpha}(a_{\alpha}-a)|<\varepsilon.

On the other hand,

|\tau_{\alpha}(a)|=|\tau_{\alpha}(\sum_{i=1}^{n}\lambda_{i}\prod_{j=1}^{n_{i}}a_{\beta_{i,j}})|=|\sum_{i=1}^{n}\lambda_{i}\tau_{\alpha}(\prod_{j=1}^{n_{i}}a_{\beta_{i,j}})|\leq\sum_{i=1}^{n}|\lambda_{i}||\tau_{\alpha}(\prod_{j=1}^{n_{i}}a_{\beta_{i,j}})|\leq

\stackrel{{\scriptstyle*}}{{\leq}}\sum_{i=1}^{n}|\lambda_{i}|\sqrt{\eta}\leq\sqrt{\eta}

where in
*
we applied Lemma
2.1
.
But then,

1=|\tau_{\alpha}(a_{\alpha})|=|\tau_{\alpha}(a_{\alpha}-a)+\tau_{\alpha}(a)|\leq|\tau_{\alpha}(a_{\alpha}-a)|+|\tau_{\alpha}(a)|<\varepsilon+\sqrt{\eta}<1

which is a contradiction.

This proves that
\mathcal{X}=\{a_{\alpha}:\alpha<\omega_{1}\}
is an almost irredundant set in
\mathcal{A}
.
∎

We would like to conclude this section with some results about UBABS+ of type
\eta=0
.

If
(a_{\alpha},\tau_{\alpha})_{\alpha<\omega_{1}}
is a UBABS+ of type
\eta=0
, then
(a_{\alpha},\tau_{\alpha})_{\alpha<\omega_{1}}
is a biorthogonal system such that
\tau_{\alpha}
is a positive linear functional and
a_{\alpha}
is a posive element for every
\alpha<\omega_{1}
. For simplicity of notation, we simply say that a
(a_{\alpha},\tau_{\alpha})_{\alpha<\omega_{1}}
is a positive biorthogonal system.

By Lemma 3.14 of
[

]
, if a C*-algebra has an uncountable positive biorthogonal system, then it contains an uncountable irredundant set. Actually, it can be proved that the existence of a positive biorthogonal system is equivalently with the following stronger version of irredundance:

Definition 2.3
.

Let
\mathcal{A}
be a C*-algebra. A subset
\mathcal{X}\subseteq\mathcal{A}_{sa}

is called h-irredundant if and only if for every
a\in\mathcal{X}
, the
hereditary C*-subalgebra of
\mathcal{A}
generated by
\mathcal{X}\setminus\{a\}

does not contain
a
.

Proposition 2.4
.

Let
\mathcal{A}
be C*-algebra and
\mathcal{X}=\{a_{\alpha}:\alpha<\kappa\}
a sequence in
\mathcal{A}_{+}
. Then
\mathcal{X}
is h-irredundant if and only if for each
\alpha<\kappa
, there is a positive functional
\tau_{\alpha}
such that

(a_{\alpha},\tau_{\alpha})_{\alpha<\omega_{1}}
is a positive biorthogonal system.

Proof.

(\Rightarrow)
Suppose
(a_{\alpha})_{\alpha<\kappa}
is h-irredundant. For each
\alpha
, consider
H_{\alpha}
the hereditary C*-subalgebra generated by
\{a_{\beta}:\beta<\kappa,\beta\neq\alpha\}
. Let
\mathcal{B}
be the C*-subalgebra generated by
(a_{\alpha})_{\alpha<\kappa}
.

For each
\alpha<\kappa
, as
H_{\alpha}\varsubsetneq\mathcal{B}
, by Theorem 5.3.1 of
[
21
]
, there is a non null positive functional
\tau_{\alpha}^{\prime}
on
\mathcal{B}
vanishing on
H_{\alpha}
. We have that

\tau_{\alpha}^{\prime}(a_{\alpha})\neq 0
. Otherwise, we would have
a_{\alpha}
in the left kernel
N_{\tau_{\alpha}^{\prime}}=\{b\in\mathcal{B}:\tau_{\alpha}^{\prime}(b^{*}b)=0\}
and since
H_{\alpha}\subset N_{\tau_{\alpha}^{\prime}}
we would get
\mathcal{B}=N_{\tau_{\alpha}^{\prime}}
, contradiction with the fact that
\tau_{\alpha}^{\prime}
is non null on
\mathcal{B}
. To conclude, consider
\tau_{\alpha}
the unique extension of
\tau_{\alpha}^{\prime}
on
\mathcal{A}
.

(\Leftarrow)
. Analogous with the proof of Lemma 3.14 of
[
12
]
: If there is a family of positive functionals
(\tau_{\alpha})_{\alpha}
separating
(a_{\alpha})_{\alpha}
, then for each
\alpha<\kappa
,
a_{\alpha}\notin N_{\tau_{\alpha}}
but
a_{\beta}\in N_{\tau_{\alpha}}
for each
\beta\neq\alpha
. Since left kernels are left ideais and in particular, hereditary C*-subalgebras, we conclude that
(a_{\alpha})_{\alpha<\kappa}
is h-irredundant.
∎

3.
Discrete set of states and UBABS+

In this section we prove that the existence of an uncountable discrete set of pure states is enough to guarantee the existence of a UBABS+ of type
\eta

Recall the following notion of excision of states:

Definition 3.1

Let
\mathcal{A}

Moreover, if

See Lemma 5.2.2 of

Proposition 3.3

Let
\mathcal{A}
be an unital C*-algebra. Suppose

such that
\tau_{\alpha}
can be excised for every

(a_{\alpha},\tau_{\beta_{\alpha}})_{\alpha<\omega_{1}}
is a UBABS+ of type

In particular, by Theorem

is an uncountable almost irredundant set. In the case where

is of real rank zero, we can assume that

is a family of projections.

Proof.

For each
\alpha<\omega_{1}
, let

be a weak*-open neighbourhood of

such that
\tau_{\beta}\notin V_{\alpha}
for every

.
Since

can be excised, by Proposition

\tau_{\alpha}\in\{\sigma:\sigma(a_{\alpha})>1-\varepsilon_{\alpha}\}\subset V_{\alpha}.

Consider an uncountable subset

and

such that

4.
Semibiorthogonal system and uncountable discrete sets of states

This section deals with positive semibiorthogonal system, discrete sets and UBABS+. We prove that under the Martin’s Axiom, every C*-algebra with an uncountable positive semibiorthogonal system admits an uncountable discrete set of states and in the case of commutative C*-algebras, we prove the existence of an UBABS+.
In the noncommutative case, we prove that under the Proper Forcing Axiom, every C*-algebra
\mathcal{A}
with an uncountable positive semibiorthogonal system of pure states has an uncountable discrete set of pure states.

Definition 4.1
.

Let
\mathcal{A}
be a C*-algebra. A positive semibiorthogonal system on
\mathcal{A}
is a sequence
(a_{\alpha},\tau_{\alpha})_{\alpha<\kappa}\in\mathcal{A}\times\mathcal{A}^{*}
such that

(1)

\tau_{\alpha}(x_{\alpha})=1

(2)

\tau_{\alpha}(a_{\beta})=0
for
\beta<\alpha
,

(3)

\tau_{\alpha}(a_{\beta})\geq 0
for all
\alpha\neq\beta
and

(4)

\tau_{\alpha}
is a positive linear functional

for every
\alpha,\beta<\kappa
. In the case where
\tau_{\alpha}
is a pure state for each
\alpha<\kappa
, we say that
(a_{\alpha},\tau_{\alpha})_{\alpha<\kappa}
is a positive semibiorthogonal system of pure states.

The following partial order will be used to apply the Martin’s Axiom:

Definition 4.2
.

Let
\mathcal{A}
be a C*-algebra. Consider
(a_{\alpha},\tau_{\alpha})_{\alpha<\kappa}\in\mathcal{A}\times\mathcal{A}^{*}
a positive semibiorthogonal system in
\mathcal{A}
.

Define the partial order
\mathbb{P}
formed by elements

and where the order is given by

:

(1)

q\subseteq p
;

(2)

For all
\beta\in q
and for all
\alpha\in p\setminus q
we have that
\tau_{\alpha}(a_{\beta})<\frac{1}{2}
.

.

Let
\mathcal{A}
be a C*-algebra. Consider
(a_{\alpha},\tau_{\alpha})_{\alpha<\kappa}\in\mathcal{A}\times\mathcal{A}^{*}
a positive semibiorthogonal system in
\mathcal{A}
and
\mathbb{P}
the partial order of Definition
4.2
. Suppose

does not have the countable chain condition (c.c.c). Then

(1)

There is an uncountable discrete set in
S(\mathcal{A})
.

(2)

If
\mathcal{A}=C(K)
and
\tau_{\alpha}
is a pure state for each
\alpha<\omega_{1}
, then
\mathcal{A}
has an UBABS+.

Proof.

Let
(p_{\xi})_{\xi<\omega_{1}}
be an uncountable antichain in

. Suppose
(p_{\xi})_{\xi<\omega_{1}}
is a

For each

is an antichain, for each

Since
