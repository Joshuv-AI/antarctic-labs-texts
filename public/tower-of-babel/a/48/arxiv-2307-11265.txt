Unique common fixed point results for four weakly commuting maps in G G -metric spaces
Talat Nazir, Sergei Silvestrov

Abstract
Using the setting of G G -metric spaces, common fixed point theorems for four maps satisfying the weakly commuting conditions are obtained for various generalized contractive conditions. Several examples are also presented to show the validity of main results. MSC 2020 Classification: 54H25, 47H10, 54E50
