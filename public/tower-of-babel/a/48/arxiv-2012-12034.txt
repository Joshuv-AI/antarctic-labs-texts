1.
Introduction and Preliminaries

Let
\mathcal{B}(\mathcal{H})
stand for the
C^{\ast}
-algebra of all bounded linear operators on a complex Hilbert space
\mathcal{H}
with inner product
\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle
and the corresponding norm
\|\cdot\|
. For
T\in\mathcal{B}(\mathcal{H})
, we denote by
\mathcal{R}(T)
,
\mathcal{N}(T)
and
T^{*}
the range, the kernel and the adjoint of
T
, respectively. For a given linear subspace
\mathcal{M}
of
\mathcal{H}
, its closure in the norm topology of
\mathcal{H}
will be denoted by
\overline{\mathcal{M}}
. Further, let
P_{\mathcal{S}}
stand for the orthogonal projection onto a closed subspace
\mathcal{S}
of
\mathcal{H}
. An operator
T\in\mathcal{B}(\mathcal{H})
is called positive if
\langle Tx,x\rangle\geq 0
for all
x\in{\mathcal{H}}
, and we then write
T\geq 0
. Furthermore, if
T\geq 0
, then the square root of
T
is denoted by

T^{1/2}
. For
T\in\mathcal{B}(\mathcal{H})
, the absolute value of
T
, denoted by
|T|
, is defined as
|T|=(T^{*}T)^{1/2}
. Throughout the article,
A
denotes a non-zero positive operator on
\mathcal{H}
. The positive operator
A
induces the following semi-inner product

\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle_{A}:\mathcal{H}\times\mathcal{H}\longrightarrow\mathbb{C},\;(x,y)\longmapsto\langle x,y\rangle_{A}:=\langle Ax,y\rangle=\langle A^{1/2}x,A^{1/2}y\rangle.

The seminorm induced by
{\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle}_{A}
is given by
{\|x\|}_{A}=\|A^{1/2}x\|
for all
x\in\mathcal{H}
. It is easy to check that
{\|\cdot\|}_{A}
is a norm if and only if
A
is injective and that the seminormed space
(\mathcal{H},{\|\cdot\|}_{A})
is complete if and only if
\overline{\mathcal{R}(A)}=\mathcal{R}(A)
. It is well-known that the semi-inner product
{\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle}_{A}
induces an inner product on the quotient space
\mathcal{H}/\mathcal{N}(A)
which is not complete unless
\mathcal{R}(A)
is closed. However, a canonical construction due to de Branges and Rovnyak
[
11
]
(see also
[
17
]
) shows that the completion of
\mathcal{H}/\mathcal{N}(A)
is isometrically isomorphic to the Hilbert space
\mathcal{R}(A^{1/2})
with the inner product

\displaystyle\langle A^{1/2}x,A^{1/2}y\rangle_{\mathbf{R}(A^{1/2})}:=\langle P_{\overline{\mathcal{R}(A)}}x,P_{\overline{\mathcal{R}(A)}}y\rangle,\quad\forall\,x,y\in\mathcal{H}.

For the sequel, the Hilbert space
\big(\mathcal{R}(A^{1/2}),\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle_{\mathbf{R}(A^{1/2})}\big)
will be simply denoted by
\mathbf{R}(A^{1/2})
. For an account of results related to
\mathbf{R}(A^{1/2})
, we refer the readers to
[
6
]
.

Let
T\in\mathcal{B}(\mathcal{H})
. We recall that an operator
S\in\mathcal{B}(\mathcal{H})
is called an
A
-adjoint of
T
if
\langle Tx,y\rangle_{A}=\langle x,Sy\rangle_{A}
for all
x,y\in\mathcal{H}
. One can observe that the existence of an
A
-adjoint of
T
is equivalent to the existence of a solution in
\mathcal{B}(\mathcal{H})
of the equation
AX=T^{*}A
.
Clearly, the existence of an
A
-adjoint operator is not guaranteed. If the set of all operators admitting
A
-adjoints is denoted by
\mathcal{B}_{A}(\mathcal{H})
, then by Douglas theorem
[
13
]
we have,

\mathcal{B}_{A}(\mathcal{H})=\left\{T\in\mathcal{B}(\mathcal{H})\,;\;\mathcal{R}(T^{*}A)\subseteq\mathcal{R}(A)\right\}.

If
T\in\mathcal{B}_{A}(\mathcal{H})
, the reduced solution of the equation

AX=T^{*}A
is a distinguished
A
-adjoint operator of
T
, which is denoted by
T^{\sharp_{A}}
and satisfies
\mathcal{R}(T^{\sharp_{A}})\subseteq\overline{\mathcal{R}(A)}
. Note that
T^{\sharp_{A}}=A^{\dagger}T^{*}A
, where
A^{\dagger}
is the Moore-Penrose inverse of
A
(see
[
5
]
). If
T\in\mathcal{B}_{A}({\mathcal{H}})
, then
T^{\sharp_{A}}\in\mathcal{B}_{A}({\mathcal{H}})
,
(T^{\sharp_{A}})^{\sharp_{A}}=P_{\overline{\mathcal{R}(A)}}TP_{\overline{\mathcal{R}(A)}}
and
((T^{\sharp_{A}})^{\sharp_{A}})^{\sharp_{A}}=T
. Moreover, if
S\in\mathcal{B}_{A}(\mathcal{H})
then
TS\in\mathcal{B}_{A}({\mathcal{H}})
and
(TS)^{\sharp_{A}}=S^{\sharp_{A}}T^{\sharp_{A}}.

For more results concerning
T^{\sharp_{A}}
, we invite the readers to see
[
4
,
5
]
.
An operator
T
is called
A
-bounded if there exists
\lambda>0
such that
\|Tx\|_{A}\leq\lambda\|x\|_{A},
for every
x\in\mathcal{H}.
In virtue of Douglas theorem, one can see that the set of all operators admitting
A^{1/2}
-adjoints, denoted by
\mathcal{B}_{A^{1/2}}(\mathcal{H})
, is same as the collection of all
A
-bounded operators, i.e.,

\mathcal{B}_{A^{1/2}}(\mathcal{H})=\left\{T\in\mathcal{B}(\mathcal{H})\,;\;\exists\,\lambda>0\,;\;\|Tx\|_{A}\leq\lambda\|x\|_{A},\;\forall\,x\in\mathcal{H}\right\}.

It is well-known that
\mathcal{B}_{A}(\mathcal{H})
and
\mathcal{B}_{A^{1/2}}(\mathcal{H})
are two subalgebras of
\mathcal{B}(\mathcal{H})
which are, in general, neither closed nor dense in
\mathcal{B}(\mathcal{H})
. Further, we have
\mathcal{B}_{A}(\mathcal{H})\subseteq\mathcal{B}_{A^{1/2}}(\mathcal{H})
(see
[
4
,
15
]
). If
T\in\mathcal{B}_{A^{1/2}}(\mathcal{H})
, then the seminorm of
T
induces by
\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle_{A}
is given by

\|T\|_{A}:=\sup_{\begin{subarray}{c}x\in\overline{\mathcal{R}(A)},\\
x\not=0\end{subarray}}\frac{\|Tx\|_{A}}{\|x\|_{A}}=\sup\big\{{\|Tx\|}_{A}\,;\,\,x\in\mathcal{H},\,{\|x\|}_{A}=1\big\}<\infty.

(1.1)

It was shown in
[
5
, Proposition 2.3]
that, for every
T\in\mathcal{B}_{A}({\mathcal{H}})
, we have

\|T^{\sharp_{A}}T\|_{A}=\|TT^{\sharp_{A}}\|_{A}=\|T\|_{A}^{2}=\|T^{\sharp_{A}}\|_{A}^{2}.

(1.2)

Furthermore, Saddi
[
23
]
introduced the concept of the
A
-numerical radius of an operator
T\in\mathcal{B}(\mathcal{H})
as follows

\displaystyle\omega_{A}(T)

\displaystyle:=\sup\left\{|\langle Tx,x\rangle_{A}|\,;\;x\in\mathcal{H},\|x\|_{A}=1\right\}.

We mention here that it may happen that
{\|T\|}_{A}
and
\omega_{A}(T)
are equal to
+\infty
for some
T\in\mathcal{B}(\mathcal{H})\setminus\mathcal{B}_{A^{1/2}}(\mathcal{H})
(see
[
15
]
). However, it was shown in
[
7
]
that
{\|\cdot\|}_{A}
and
\omega_{A}(\cdot)
are equivalent seminorms on
\mathcal{B}_{A^{1/2}}(\mathcal{H})
. More precisely, for every
T\in\mathcal{B}_{A^{1/2}}(\mathcal{H})
, the following inequalities hold

\tfrac{1}{2}\|T\|_{A}\leq\omega_{A}(T)\leq\|T\|_{A}.

(1.3)

Let
T\in\mathcal{B}_{A^{1/2}}(\mathcal{H})
. Then
\|T\|_{A}=0
if and only if
AT=0
. Furthermore,
\|Tx\|_{A}\leq\|T\|_{A}\|x\|_{A},
for every
x\in\mathcal{H}.
This implies that for all
T,S\in\mathcal{B}_{A^{1/2}}(\mathcal{H})
,
\|TS\|_{A}\leq\|T\|_{A}\|S\|_{A}.
An operator
T\in\mathcal{B}(\mathcal{H})
is called
A
-selfadjoint if
AT
is selfadjoint and it is called
A
-positive if
AT
is a positive operator. An operator
T\in\mathcal{B}_{A}(\mathcal{H})
is said to be
A
-normal if
T^{\sharp_{A}}T=TT^{\sharp_{A}}
(see
[
6
]
). For the sequel, if
A=I
then
\|T\|
,
r(T)
and
\omega(T)
denote respectively the classical operator norm, the spectral radius and the numerical radius of an operator
T
.

For any operator
T\in{\mathcal{B}}_{A}({\mathcal{H}})
, we write
\Re_{A}(T):=\frac{T+T^{\sharp_{A}}}{2}
. Following
[
25
, Theorem 2.5]
, we have that if
T\in\mathcal{B}_{A}(\mathcal{H})
then

\displaystyle\omega_{A}(T)=\displaystyle{\sup_{\theta\in\mathbb{R}}}{\left\|\Re_{A}(e^{i\theta}T)\right\|}_{A}.

(1.4)

The
A
-spectral radius of an operator
T\in\mathcal{B}_{A^{1/2}}(\mathcal{H})
was defined by the second author in
[
15
]
as

r_{A}(T):=\displaystyle\inf_{n\geq 1}\|T^{n}\|_{A}^{\frac{1}{n}}=\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}\|T^{n}\|_{A}^{\frac{1}{n}}.

(1.5)

The second equality in (
1.5
) is also proved in
[
15
, Theorem 1]
. In addition, it was shown in
[
15
]
that
r_{A}(\cdot)
satisfies the commutativity property, i.e.,

r_{A}(TS)=r_{A}(ST),\quad\forall\;T,S\in\mathcal{B}_{A^{1/2}}(\mathcal{H}).

(1.6)

Also, the following relation between the
A
-spectral radius and the
A
-numerical radius of
A
-bounded operators is also proved in
[
15
]
.

r_{A}(T)\leq\omega_{A}(T),\quad\forall\;T\in\mathcal{B}_{A^{1/2}}(\mathcal{H}).

(1.7)

Recently, many mathematicians have obtained different
A
-numerical radius inequalities of semi-Hilbertian space operators, the interested readers are invited to see
[
8
,
9
,
10
,
16
,
19
,
21
,
22
]
and references therein. Here, we obtain several new inequalities for the
A
-numerical radius of the products and the sums of semi-Hilbertian space operators. The bounds obtained here improve on the existing bounds.

2.
On inequalities for product of operators

We begin this section with the following known lemma which can be found in
[
15
]
.

Lemma 2.1
.

Let
T\in\mathcal{B}(\mathcal{H})
be an
A
-selfadjoint operator. Then,

\|T\|_{A}=\omega_{A}(T)=r_{A}(T).

Our first result reads as:

Theorem 2.2
.

Let
T,S\in\mathcal{B}_{A}(\mathcal{H})
. Then,

\displaystyle\omega_{A}(TS)\leq\|T\|_{A}\,\omega_{A}(S)+\frac{1}{2}\omega_{A}\left(TS\pm ST^{\sharp_{A}}\right).

Proof.

Let
\theta\in\mathbb{R}
. Clearly,
\Re_{A}(e^{i\theta}TS)
is an
A
-selfadjoint operator. Therefore, from Lemma
2.1
we get,

\displaystyle\left\|\Re_{A}(e^{i\theta}TS)\right\|_{A}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\omega_{A}\left(\Re_{A}(e^{i\theta}TS)\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\omega_{A}\left(\frac{1}{2}(e^{i\theta}TS+e^{-i\theta}S^{\sharp_{A}}T^{\sharp_{A}})\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\omega_{A}\left(\frac{1}{2}(e^{i\theta}TS+e^{-i\theta}TS^{\sharp_{A}}+e^{-i\theta}S^{\sharp_{A}}T^{\sharp_{A}}-e^{-i\theta}TS^{\sharp_{A}})\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\omega_{A}\left(T\Re_{A}(e^{i\theta}S)+\frac{1}{2}e^{-i\theta}(S^{\sharp_{A}}T^{\sharp_{A}}-TS^{\sharp_{A}})\right)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\omega_{A}\left(T\Re_{A}(e^{i\theta}S)\right)+\omega_{A}\left(\frac{1}{2}e^{-i\theta}(S^{\sharp_{A}}T^{\sharp_{A}}-TS^{\sharp_{A}})\right)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\left\|T\Re_{A}(e^{i\theta}S)\right\|_{A}+\frac{1}{2}\omega_{A}\left(S^{\sharp_{A}}T^{\sharp_{A}}-TS^{\sharp_{A}}\right)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\|T\|_{A}\left\|\Re_{A}(e^{i\theta}S)\right\|_{A}+\frac{1}{2}\omega_{A}\left(S^{\sharp_{A}}T^{\sharp_{A}}-TS^{\sharp_{A}}\right)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\|T\|_{A}\omega_{A}(S)+\frac{1}{2}\omega_{A}\left(S^{\sharp_{A}}T^{\sharp_{A}}-TS^{\sharp_{A}}\right).

Taking supremum over all
\theta\in\mathbb{R}
we get,

\displaystyle\omega_{A}(TS)\leq\|T\|_{A}\omega_{A}(S)+\frac{1}{2}\omega_{A}\left(S^{\sharp_{A}}T^{\sharp_{A}}-TS^{\sharp_{A}}\right).

(2.1)

Next, for
x\in\mathcal{H}
we have,

|\langle(S^{\sharp_{A}}T^{\sharp_{A}}-TS^{\sharp_{A}})x,x\rangle_{A}|=|\langle(TS-ST^{\sharp_{A}})x,x\rangle_{A}|.

This implies that
\omega_{A}\left(S^{\sharp_{A}}T^{\sharp_{A}}-TS^{\sharp_{A}}\right)=\omega_{A}\left(TS-ST^{\sharp_{A}}\right).
Thus, it follows from (
2.1
) that

\displaystyle\omega_{A}(TS)\leq\|T\|_{A}\omega_{A}(S)+\frac{1}{2}\omega_{A}\left(TS-ST^{\sharp_{A}}\right).

(2.2)

Also, replacing
T
by
iT
in (
2.2
) we get,

\displaystyle\omega_{A}(TS)\leq\|T\|_{A}\omega_{A}(S)+\frac{1}{2}\omega_{A}\left(TS+ST^{\sharp_{A}}\right).

(2.3)

The proof now follows from (
2.2
) and (
2.3
).
∎

Remark 2.3
.

It is easy to verify that (also see in
[
9
, Cor. 3.3]
)

\omega_{A}\left(TS\pm ST^{\sharp_{A}}\right)\leq 2\|T\|_{A}\omega_{A}(S).

Therefore,

\|T\|_{A}\omega_{A}(S)+\frac{1}{2}\omega_{A}\left(TS\pm ST^{\sharp_{A}}\right)\leq 2\|T\|_{A}\omega_{A}(S).

Thus, the inequality obtained in Theorem
2.2
is stronger than the well-know inequality

\omega_{A}(TS)\leq 2\|T\|_{A}\omega_{A}(S).

In order to obtain our next inequality that gives an upper bound for the
A
-numerical radius of product of two operators, we need the following lemmas. First we consider the
{2\times 2}
operator diagonal matrix
\mathbb{A}=\begin{pmatrix}A&0\\
0&A\end{pmatrix}
. Clearly,
\mathbb{A}
is a positive operator on
\mathcal{H}\oplus\mathcal{H}
. So,
\mathbb{A}
induces the following semi-inner product on
\mathcal{H}\oplus\mathcal{H}
defined as

\langle x,y\rangle_{\mathbb{A}}=\langle\mathbb{A}x,y\rangle=\langle x_{1},y_{1}\rangle_{A}+\langle x_{2},y_{2}\rangle_{A},

for all
x=(x_{1},x_{2}),y=(y_{1},y_{2})\in\mathcal{H}\oplus\mathcal{H}
. Note that if
T,S,X,Y\in\mathcal{B}_{A}(\mathcal{H})
then, it was shown in
[
8
, Lemma 3.1]
that
\begin{pmatrix}T&S\\
X&Y\end{pmatrix}\in\mathcal{B}_{\mathbb{A}}(\mathcal{H}\oplus\mathcal{H})
and

\begin{pmatrix}T&S\\
X&Y\end{pmatrix}^{\sharp_{\mathbb{A}}}=\begin{pmatrix}T^{\sharp_{A}}&X^{\sharp_{A}}\\
S^{\sharp_{A}}&Y^{\sharp_{A}}\end{pmatrix}.

(2.4)

Lemma 2.4
.

(
[
19
]
)

Let
T,S\in\mathcal{B}(\mathcal{H})
be
A
-positive operators. Then,

\omega_{\mathbb{A}}\left[\begin{pmatrix}0&T\\
S&0\end{pmatrix}\right]=\frac{1}{2}\left\|T+S\right\|_{A}.

(2.5)

Lemma 2.5
.

(
[
18
]
)

Let
T,S\in\mathcal{B}_{A^{1/2}}(\mathcal{H})
. Then,

(a)

\omega_{\mathbb{A}}\left[\begin{pmatrix}T&0\\
0&S\end{pmatrix}\right]=\max\{\omega_{A}(T),\omega_{A}(S)\}.

In particular,

\omega_{\mathbb{A}}\left[\begin{pmatrix}T&0\\
0&T\end{pmatrix}\right]=\omega_{\mathbb{A}}\left[\begin{pmatrix}T&0\\
0&T^{\sharp_{A}}\end{pmatrix}\right]=\omega_{A}(T).

(2.6)

(b)

\left\|\begin{pmatrix}0&T\\
S&0\end{pmatrix}\right\|_{\mathbb{A}}=\left\|\begin{pmatrix}T&0\\
0&S\end{pmatrix}\right\|_{\mathbb{A}}=\max\left\{\|T\|_{A},\|S\|_{A}\right\}.

Now we are in a position to obtain the following inequality.

Theorem 2.6
.

Let
T,S\in\mathcal{B}_{A}(\mathcal{H})
. Then,

\displaystyle\omega_{A}(TS)\leq\frac{1}{2}\omega_{A}(ST)+\frac{1}{4}\Big(\|T\|_{A}\|S\|_{A}+\|TS\|_{A}\Big).

(2.7)

Proof.

Let
\theta\in\mathbb{R}
. Since
\Re_{A}(e^{i\theta}TS)
is an
A
-selfadjoint operator, so by Lemma
2.1
we have,

\displaystyle\|\Re_{A}(e^{i\theta}TS)\|_{A}

\displaystyle=r_{A}[\Re_{A}(e^{i\theta}TS)]

\displaystyle=\tfrac{1}{2}r_{A}(e^{i\theta}TS+e^{-i\theta}S^{\sharp_{A}}T^{\sharp_{A}}).

(2.8)

On the other hand, we have

\displaystyle r_{A}(e^{i\theta}TS+e^{-i\theta}S^{\sharp_{A}}T^{\sharp_{A}})

\displaystyle=r_{\mathbb{A}}\left[\begin{pmatrix}e^{i\theta}TS+e^{-i\theta}S^{\sharp_{A}}T^{\sharp_{A}}&0\\
0&0\end{pmatrix}\right]

\displaystyle=r_{\mathbb{A}}\left[\begin{pmatrix}e^{i\theta}T&S^{\sharp_{A}}\\
0&0\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}S&0\\
e^{-i\theta}T^{\sharp_{A}}&0\end{pmatrix}\right]

\displaystyle=r_{\mathbb{A}}\left[\begin{pmatrix}S&0\\
e^{-i\theta}T^{\sharp_{A}}&0\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}e^{i\theta}T&S^{\sharp_{A}}\\
0&0\end{pmatrix}\right]\quad(\text{ by }\;\eqref{commut})

\displaystyle=r_{\mathbb{A}}\left[\begin{pmatrix}e^{i\theta}ST&SS^{\sharp_{A}}\\
T^{\sharp_{A}}T&e^{-i\theta}T^{\sharp_{A}}S^{\sharp_{A}}\end{pmatrix}\right].

By applying (
1.7
) we get

\displaystyle r_{A}(e^{i\theta}TS+e^{-i\theta}S^{\sharp_{A}}T^{\sharp_{A}})

\displaystyle\leq\omega_{\mathbb{A}}\left[\begin{pmatrix}e^{i\theta}ST&SS^{\sharp_{A}}\\
T^{\sharp_{A}}T&e^{-i\theta}T^{\sharp_{A}}S^{\sharp_{A}}\end{pmatrix}\right]

\displaystyle\leq\omega_{\mathbb{A}}\left[\begin{pmatrix}e^{i\theta}ST&0\\
0&e^{-i\theta}T^{\sharp_{A}}S^{\sharp_{A}}\end{pmatrix}\right]+\omega_{\mathbb{A}}\left[\begin{pmatrix}0&SS^{\sharp_{A}}\\
T^{\sharp_{A}}T&0\end{pmatrix}\right]

\displaystyle=\omega_{A}(ST)+\frac{1}{2}\|SS^{\sharp_{A}}+T^{\sharp_{A}}T\|_{A},

where the last equality follows from Lemma
2.4
together with (
2.6
). Therefore, from (
2
) we get,

\displaystyle\|\Re_{A}(e^{i\theta}TS)\|_{A}\leq\tfrac{1}{2}\omega_{A}(ST)+\tfrac{1}{4}\|SS^{\sharp_{A}}+T^{\sharp_{A}}T\|_{A}.

Hence, by taking supremum over all
\theta\in\mathbb{R}
and then using (
1.4
) we get,

\displaystyle\omega_{A}(TS)\leq\tfrac{1}{2}\omega_{A}(ST)+\tfrac{1}{4}\|SS^{\sharp_{A}}+T^{\sharp_{A}}T\|_{A},

(2.9)

If
AT=0
or
AS=0
, then the inequality (
2.7
) holds trivially. Assume that
AT\neq 0
and
AS\neq 0
. By Replacing
T
and
S
by
\sqrt{\frac{\|S\|_{A}}{\|T\|_{A}}}T
and
\sqrt{\frac{\|T\|_{A}}{\|S\|_{A}}}S
, respectively, in (
2.9
) we obtain,

\displaystyle\omega_{A}(TS)\leq\frac{1}{2}\omega_{A}(ST)+\frac{1}{4}\left\|\tfrac{\|S\|_{A}}{\|T\|_{A}}T^{\sharp_{A}}T+\tfrac{\|T\|_{A}}{\|S\|_{A}}SS^{\sharp_{A}}\right\|_{A}.

(2.10)

It is easy to see that the operator
\tfrac{\|S\|_{A}}{\|T\|_{A}}T^{\sharp_{A}}T+\tfrac{\|T\|_{A}}{\|S\|_{A}}SS^{\sharp_{A}}
is
A
-positive. So, an application of Lemma
2.1
gives

\left\|\tfrac{\|S\|_{A}}{\|T\|_{A}}T^{\sharp_{A}}T+\tfrac{\|T\|_{A}}{\|S\|_{A}}SS^{\sharp_{A}}\right\|_{A}=r_{A}\left(\tfrac{\|S\|_{A}}{\|T\|_{A}}T^{\sharp_{A}}T+\tfrac{\|T\|_{A}}{\|S\|_{A}}SS^{\sharp_{A}}\right).

(2.11)

Next,

\displaystyle r_{A}\left(\tfrac{\|S\|_{A}}{\|T\|_{A}}T^{\sharp_{A}}T+\tfrac{\|T\|_{A}}{\|S\|_{A}}SS^{\sharp_{A}}\right)

\displaystyle=r_{\mathbb{A}}\left[\begin{pmatrix}\tfrac{\|S\|_{A}}{\|T\|_{A}}T^{\sharp_{A}}T+\tfrac{\|T\|_{A}}{\|S\|_{A}}SS^{\sharp_{A}}&0\\
0&0\end{pmatrix}\right]

\displaystyle=r_{\mathbb{A}}\left[\begin{pmatrix}\sqrt{\tfrac{\|S\|_{A}}{\|T\|_{A}}}T^{\sharp_{A}}&\sqrt{\tfrac{\|T\|_{A}}{\|S\|_{A}}}S\\
0&0\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}\sqrt{\tfrac{\|S\|_{A}}{\|T\|_{A}}}T&0\\
\sqrt{\tfrac{\|T\|_{A}}{\|S\|_{A}}}S^{\sharp_{A}}&0\end{pmatrix}\right]

Further, by applying (
1.6
), we get

\displaystyle r_{A}\left(\tfrac{\|S\|_{A}}{\|T\|_{A}}T^{\sharp_{A}}T+\tfrac{\|T\|_{A}}{\|S\|_{A}}SS^{\sharp_{A}}\right)

\displaystyle=r_{\mathbb{A}}\left[\begin{pmatrix}\sqrt{\tfrac{\|S\|_{A}}{\|T\|_{A}}}T&0\\
\sqrt{\tfrac{\|T\|_{A}}{\|S\|_{A}}}S^{\sharp_{A}}&0\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}\sqrt{\tfrac{\|S\|_{A}}{\|T\|_{A}}}T^{\sharp_{A}}&\sqrt{\tfrac{\|T\|_{A}}{\|S\|_{A}}}S\\
0&0\end{pmatrix}\right]

\displaystyle=r_{\mathbb{A}}\left[\begin{pmatrix}\tfrac{\|S\|_{A}}{\|T\|_{A}}TT^{\sharp_{A}}&TS\\
S^{\sharp_{A}}T^{\sharp_{A}}&\tfrac{\|T\|_{A}}{\|S\|_{A}}S^{\sharp_{A}}S\end{pmatrix}\right].

(2.12)

In addition, we see that

\displaystyle\mathbb{A}\begin{pmatrix}\tfrac{\|S\|_{A}}{\|T\|_{A}}TT^{\sharp_{A}}&TS\\
S^{\sharp_{A}}T^{\sharp_{A}}&\tfrac{\|T\|_{A}}{\|S\|_{A}}S^{\sharp_{A}}S\end{pmatrix}

\displaystyle=\begin{pmatrix}\tfrac{\|S\|_{A}}{\|T\|_{A}}ATT^{\sharp_{A}}&ATS\\
AS^{\sharp_{A}}T^{\sharp_{A}}&\tfrac{\|T\|_{A}}{\|S\|_{A}}AS^{\sharp_{A}}S\end{pmatrix}

\displaystyle=\begin{pmatrix}\tfrac{\|S\|_{A}}{\|T\|_{A}}(TT^{\sharp_{A}})^{*}A&(S^{\sharp_{A}}T^{\sharp_{A}})^{*}A\\
(TS)^{*}A&\tfrac{\|T\|_{A}}{\|S\|_{A}}(S^{\sharp_{A}}S)^{*}A\end{pmatrix}

\displaystyle=\begin{pmatrix}\tfrac{\|S\|_{A}}{\|T\|_{A}}TT^{\sharp_{A}}&TS\\
S^{\sharp_{A}}T^{\sharp_{A}}&\tfrac{\|T\|_{A}}{\|S\|_{A}}S^{\sharp_{A}}S\end{pmatrix}^{*}\mathbb{A}.

This implies that,
\begin{pmatrix}\tfrac{\|S\|_{A}}{\|T\|_{A}}TT^{\sharp_{A}}&TS\\
S^{\sharp_{A}}T^{\sharp_{A}}&\tfrac{\|T\|_{A}}{\|S\|_{A}}S^{\sharp_{A}}S\end{pmatrix}
is an
\mathbb{A}
-selfadjoint operator. Hence, in view of Lemma
2.1
we have,

\left\|\begin{pmatrix}\tfrac{\|S\|_{A}}{\|T\|_{A}}TT^{\sharp_{A}}&TS\\
S^{\sharp_{A}}T^{\sharp_{A}}&\tfrac{\|T\|_{A}}{\|S\|_{A}}S^{\sharp_{A}}S\end{pmatrix}\right\|_{\mathbb{A}}=r_{\mathbb{A}}\left[\begin{pmatrix}\tfrac{\|S\|_{A}}{\|T\|_{A}}TT^{\sharp_{A}}&TS\\
S^{\sharp_{A}}T^{\sharp_{A}}&\tfrac{\|T\|_{A}}{\|S\|_{A}}S^{\sharp_{A}}S\end{pmatrix}\right].

(2.13)

So, it follows from (
2.11
), (
2
) and (
2.13
) that

\left\|\tfrac{\|S\|_{A}}{\|T\|_{A}}T^{\sharp_{A}}T+\tfrac{\|T\|_{A}}{\|S\|_{A}}SS^{\sharp_{A}}\right\|_{A}=\left\|\begin{pmatrix}\tfrac{\|S\|_{A}}{\|T\|_{A}}TT^{\sharp_{A}}&TS\\
S^{\sharp_{A}}T^{\sharp_{A}}&\tfrac{\|T\|_{A}}{\|S\|_{A}}S^{\sharp_{A}}S\end{pmatrix}\right\|_{\mathbb{A}}.

Finally, by applying the triangle inequality and then using Lemma
2.5
, we get

\displaystyle\left\|\tfrac{\|S\|_{A}}{\|T\|_{A}}T^{\sharp_{A}}T+\tfrac{\|T\|_{A}}{\|S\|_{A}}SS^{\sharp_{A}}\right\|_{A}

\displaystyle\leq\left\|\begin{pmatrix}\tfrac{\|S\|_{A}}{\|T\|_{A}}TT^{\sharp_{A}}&0\\
0&\tfrac{\|T\|_{A}}{\|S\|_{A}}S^{\sharp_{A}}S\end{pmatrix}\right\|_{\mathbb{A}}+\left\|\begin{pmatrix}0&TS\\
S^{\sharp_{A}}T^{\sharp_{A}}&0\end{pmatrix}\right\|_{\mathbb{A}}

\displaystyle=\max\left\{\tfrac{\|S\|_{A}}{\|T\|_{A}}\|TT^{\sharp_{A}}\|_{A},\tfrac{\|T\|_{A}}{\|S\|_{A}}\|S^{\sharp_{A}}S\|_{A}\right\}+\|TS\|_{A}

\displaystyle=\|S\|_{A}\|T\|_{A}+\|TS\|_{A}.

Therefore, we get (
2.7
) as desired by taking (
2.10
) into account.
∎

On inequalities for sum of operators

We begin this section with the following lemma.

Lemma 3.1
.

For any
x,y,z\in\mathcal{H}
, we have

\displaystyle|\langle x,y\rangle_{A}|^{2}+|\langle x,z\rangle_{A}|^{2}\leq\|x\|_{A}^{2}\Big(\max\{\|y\|_{A}^{2},\|z\|_{A}^{2}\}+|\langle y,z\rangle_{A}|\Big).

(3.1)

Proof.

First note that, by the proof of
[
14
, Th. 3]
we have,

\displaystyle|\langle x,y\rangle|^{2}+|\langle x,z\rangle|^{2}\leq\|x\|^{2}\Big(\max\{\|y\|^{2},\|z\|^{2}\}+|\langle y,z\rangle|\Big),

(3.2)

for every
x,y,z\in\mathcal{H}
. Now,

\displaystyle|\langle x,y\rangle_{A}|^{2}+|\langle x,z\rangle_{A}|^{2}

\displaystyle=|\langle A^{1/2}x,A^{1/2}y\rangle|^{2}+|\langle A^{1/2}x,A^{1/2}z\rangle|^{2}.

So, by applying (
3.2
), we obtain

\displaystyle|\langle x,y\rangle_{A}|^{2}+|\langle x,z\rangle_{A}|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\|A^{1/2}x\|^{2}\Big(\max\{\|A^{1/2}y\|^{2},\|A^{1/2}z\|^{2}\}+|\langle A^{1/2}y,A^{1/2}z\rangle|\Big).

Hence, we get (
3.1
) as required.
∎

Now, we are in a position to prove the following theorem.

Theorem 3.2
.

Let
T,S\in\mathcal{B}_{A}(\mathcal{H})
. Then

\displaystyle\omega_{A}(T+S)

\displaystyle\leq\sqrt{\frac{1}{2}\Big(\left\|TT^{\sharp_{A}}+SS^{\sharp_{A}}\right\|_{A}+\left\|TT^{\sharp_{A}}-SS^{\sharp_{A}}\right\|_{A}\Big)+\omega_{A}\left(ST^{\sharp_{A}}\right)+2\omega_{A}\left(T\right)\omega_{A}\left(S\right)}.

Proof.

Recall first that for every
t,s\in\mathbb{R}
it holds

\max\{t,s\}=\frac{1}{2}\Big(t+s+|t-s|\Big).

(3.3)

Now, let
x\in\mathcal{H}
with
\|x\|_{A}=1
. Using Lemma
3.1
we get,

\displaystyle|\langle(T

\displaystyle+S)x,x\rangle_{A}|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq|\langle x,T^{\sharp_{A}}x\rangle_{A}|^{2}+|\langle x,S^{\sharp_{A}}x\rangle_{A}|^{2}+2|\langle Tx,x\rangle_{A}|\,|\langle Sx,x\rangle_{A}|

\displaystyle\leq\max\left\{\|T^{\sharp_{A}}x\|_{A}^{2},\|S^{\sharp_{A}}x\|_{A}^{2}\right\}+|\langle ST^{\sharp_{A}}x,x\rangle_{A}|+2|\langle Tx,x\rangle_{A}|\,|\langle Sx,x\rangle_{A}|

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2}\Big(\|T^{\sharp_{A}}x\|_{A}^{2}+\|S^{\sharp_{A}}x\|_{A}^{2}+\left|\|T^{\sharp_{A}}x\|_{A}^{2}-\|S^{\sharp_{A}}x\|_{A}^{2}\right|\Big)+|\langle ST^{\sharp_{A}}x,x\rangle_{A}|

\displaystyle\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad+2|\langle Tx,x\rangle_{A}|\,|\langle Sx,x\rangle_{A}|\quad(\text{by }\,\eqref{r})

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2}\Big(\langle(TT^{\sharp_{A}}+SS^{\sharp_{A}})x,x\rangle_{A}+\left|\langle(TT^{\sharp_{A}}-SS^{\sharp_{A}})x,x\rangle_{A}\right|\Big)+|\langle ST^{\sharp_{A}}x,x\rangle_{A}|

\displaystyle\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad+2|\langle Tx,x\rangle_{A}|\,|\langle Sx,x\rangle_{A}|

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{2}\Big(\omega_{A}(TT^{\sharp_{A}}+SS^{\sharp_{A}})+\omega_{A}(TT^{\sharp_{A}}-SS^{\sharp_{A}})\Big)+\omega_{A}(ST^{\sharp_{A}})+2\omega_{A}\left(T\right)\omega_{A}\left(S\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2}\Big(\|TT^{\sharp_{A}}+SS^{\sharp_{A}}\|_{A}+\|TT^{\sharp_{A}}-SS^{\sharp_{A}}\|_{A}\Big)+\omega_{A}(ST^{\sharp_{A}})+2\omega_{A}\left(T\right)\omega_{A}\left(S\right),

where the last equality follows from Lemma
2.1
, since the operators
TT^{\sharp_{A}}\pm SS^{\sharp_{A}}
are
A
-selfadjoint. So, we infer that

\displaystyle|\langle(T

\displaystyle+S)x,x\rangle_{A}|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{2}\Big(\|TT^{\sharp_{A}}+SS^{\sharp_{A}}\|_{A}+\|TT^{\sharp_{A}}-SS^{\sharp_{A}}\|_{A}\Big)+\omega_{A}(ST^{\sharp_{A}})+2\omega_{A}\left(T\right)\omega_{A}\left(S\right).

Therefore, the desired result follows by taking supremum over all
x\in\mathcal{H}
with
\|x\|_{A}=1
in the last inequality.
∎

Our next objective is to refine the triangle inequality related to
\omega_{A}(\cdot)
. To do this, we need to recall the following lemma from
[
20
]
.

Lemma 3.3
.

Let
T_{1},T_{2},S_{1},S_{2}\in\mathcal{B}_{A^{1/2}}(\mathcal{H})
. Then,

\displaystyle r_{A}\left(T_{1}S_{1}+T_{2}S_{2}\right)

\displaystyle\leq\left\|\begin{pmatrix}\|S_{1}T_{1}\|_{A}&\sqrt{\left\|S_{1}T_{2}\right\|_{A}\left\|S_{2}T_{1}\right\|_{A}}\\
\sqrt{\left\|S_{1}T_{2}\right\|_{A}\left\|S_{2}T_{1}\right\|_{A}}&\|S_{2}T_{2}\|_{A}\end{pmatrix}\right\|.

(3.4)

Now, we are in a position to prove the following theorem which covers and generalizes a recent result proved by Abu-Omar and Kittaneh in
[
1
]
.

Theorem 3.4
.

Let
T,S\in\mathcal{B}_{A}(\mathcal{H})
. Then,

\displaystyle\omega_{A}\left(T+S\right)

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{2}\left[\omega_{A}\left(T\right)+\omega_{A}\left(S\right)+\sqrt{\left(\omega_{A}\left(T\right)-\omega_{A}\left(S\right)\right)^{2}+4\sup_{\theta\in\mathbb{R}}\left\|\Re_{A}(e^{i\theta}T)\Re_{A}(e^{i\theta}S)\right\|_{A}}\right]

\displaystyle\leq\omega_{A}\left(T\right)+\omega_{A}\left(S\right).

(3.5)

Proof.

Let
\theta\in\mathbb{R}
. It can be seen that
\Re_{A}[e^{i\theta}(T+S)]
is an
A
-selfadjoint operator. So, by Lemma
2.1
we get,

\left\|\Re_{A}[e^{i\theta}(T+S)]\right\|_{A}=r_{A}\Big(\Re_{A}[e^{i\theta}(T+S)]\Big).

By letting
T_{1}=I
,
S_{1}=\Re_{A}(e^{i\theta}T)
,
T_{2}=\Re_{A}(e^{i\theta}S)
and
S_{2}=I
in Lemma
3.3
and then using the norm monotonicity of matrices with nonnegative entries we get,

\displaystyle\left\|\Re_{A}[e^{i\theta}(T+S)]\right\|_{A}

\displaystyle=r_{A}\Big(\Re_{A}(e^{i\theta}T)+\Re_{A}(e^{i\theta}S)\Big)

\displaystyle\leq\left\|\begin{pmatrix}\|\Re_{A}(e^{i\theta}T)\|_{A}&\left\|\Re_{A}(e^{i\theta}T)\Re_{A}(e^{i\theta}S)\right\|_{A}^{1/2}\\
\left\|\Re_{A}(e^{i\theta}T)\Re_{A}(e^{i\theta}S)\right\|_{A}^{1/2}&\|\Re_{A}(e^{i\theta}S)\|_{A}\end{pmatrix}\right\|

\displaystyle\leq\left\|\begin{pmatrix}\omega_{A}(T)&\sqrt{\displaystyle\sup_{\theta\in\mathbb{R}}\left\|\Re_{A}(e^{i\theta}T)\Re_{A}(e^{i\theta}S)\right\|_{A}}\\
\sqrt{\displaystyle\sup_{\theta\in\mathbb{R}}\left\|\Re_{A}(e^{i\theta}T)\Re_{A}(e^{i\theta}S)\right\|_{A}}&\omega_{A}(S)\end{pmatrix}\right\|

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2}\left[\omega_{A}\left(T\right)+\omega_{A}\left(S\right)+\sqrt{\left(\omega_{A}\left(T\right)-\omega_{A}\left(S\right)\right)^{2}+4\sup_{\theta\in\mathbb{R}}\left\|\Re_{A}(e^{i\theta}T)\Re_{A}(e^{i\theta}S)\right\|_{A}}\right].

By taking supremum over all
\theta\in\mathbb{R}
we get,

\displaystyle\omega_{A}\left(T+S\right)

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{2}\left[\omega_{A}\left(T\right)+\omega_{A}\left(S\right)+\sqrt{\left(\omega_{A}\left(T\right)-\omega_{A}\left(S\right)\right)^{2}+4\sup_{\theta\in\mathbb{R}}\left\|\Re_{A}(e^{i\theta}T)\Re_{A}(e^{i\theta}S)\right\|_{A}}\right].

(3.6)

This proves the first inequality. Moreover,

\displaystyle\sqrt{\left(\omega_{A}\left(T\right)-\omega_{A}\left(S\right)\right)^{2}+4\sup_{\theta\in\mathbb{R}}\left\|\Re_{A}(e^{i\theta}T)\Re_{A}(e^{i\theta}S)\right\|_{A}}

\displaystyle\leq\sqrt{\left(\omega_{A}\left(T\right)-\omega_{A}\left(S\right)\right)^{2}+4\omega_{A}\left(T\right)\omega_{A}\left(S\right)}

\displaystyle=\sqrt{\left(\omega_{A}\left(T\right)+\omega_{A}\left(S\right)\right)^{2}}=\omega_{A}\left(T\right)+\omega_{A}\left(S\right).

So, by using (
3
) we easily get the second inequality.
∎

The following lemma (see in
[
23
]
) plays a crucial role in proving our next result.

Lemma 3.5
.

Let
x,y,e\in\mathcal{H}
with
\|e\|_{A}=1.
Then,

\displaystyle|\langle x,e\rangle_{A}\langle e,y\rangle_{A}|\leq\frac{1}{2}\big(|\langle x,y\rangle_{A}|+\|x\|_{A}\|y\|_{A}\big).

Now, we prove the following theorem.

Theorem 3.6
.

Let
T,S\in\mathcal{B}_{A}(\mathcal{H})
. Then

\displaystyle\omega_{A}(T+S)

\displaystyle\leq\sqrt{\omega_{A}^{2}(T)+\omega_{A}^{2}(S)+\frac{1}{2}\left\|T^{\sharp_{A}}T+SS^{\sharp_{A}}\right\|_{A}+\omega_{A}(ST)}.

Proof.

Let
x\in\mathcal{H}
be such that
\|x\|_{A}=1
. One can verify that

\displaystyle|\langle(T+S)x,x\rangle_{A}|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq|\langle Tx,x\rangle_{A}|^{2}+|\langle Sx,x\rangle_{A}|^{2}+2|\langle Tx,x\rangle_{A}|\;|\langle Sx,x\rangle_{A}|

\displaystyle=|\langle Tx,x\rangle_{A}|^{2}+|\langle Sx,x\rangle_{A}|^{2}+2|\langle Tx,x\rangle_{A}|\;|\langle x,S^{\sharp_{A}}x\rangle_{A}|.

Using Lemma
3.5
we get,

\displaystyle|\langle(T+S)x,x\rangle_{A}|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq|\langle Tx,x\rangle_{A}|^{2}+|\langle Sx,x\rangle_{A}|^{2}+\|Tx\|_{A}\|S^{\sharp_{A}}x\|_{A}+|\langle Tx,S^{\sharp_{A}}x\rangle_{A}|

\displaystyle=|\langle Tx,x\rangle_{A}|^{2}+|\langle Sx,x\rangle_{A}|^{2}+\sqrt{\langle T^{\sharp_{A}}Tx,x\rangle_{A}\langle SS^{\sharp_{A}}x,x\rangle_{A}}+|\langle STx,x\rangle_{A}|.

By using the arithmetic-geometric mean inequality we get,

\displaystyle|\langle(T+S)x,x\rangle_{A}|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\omega_{A}^{2}(T)+\omega_{A}^{2}(S)+\frac{1}{2}\left(\langle T^{\sharp_{A}}Tx,x\rangle_{A}+\langle SS^{\sharp_{A}}x,x\rangle_{A}\right)+\omega_{A}(ST)

\displaystyle=\omega_{A}^{2}(T)+\omega_{A}^{2}(S)+\frac{1}{2}\langle(T^{\sharp_{A}}T+SS^{\sharp_{A}})x,x\rangle_{A}+\omega_{A}(ST)

\displaystyle\leq\omega_{A}^{2}(T)+\omega_{A}^{2}(S)+\frac{1}{2}\omega_{A}\left(T^{\sharp_{A}}T+SS^{\sharp_{A}}\right)+\omega_{A}(ST)

\displaystyle=\omega_{A}^{2}(T)+\omega_{A}^{2}(S)+\frac{1}{2}\left\|T^{\sharp_{A}}T+SS^{\sharp_{A}}\right\|_{A}+\omega_{A}(ST),

where the last equality follows from Lemma
2.1
. So, we infer that

\displaystyle|\langle(T+S)x,x\rangle_{A}|^{2}\leq\omega_{A}^{2}(T)+\omega_{A}^{2}(S)+\frac{1}{2}\left\|T^{\sharp_{A}}T+SS^{\sharp_{A}}\right\|_{A}+\omega_{A}(ST),

for all
x\in\mathcal{H}
with
\|x\|_{A}=1
. Thus, by taking the supremum over all
x\in\mathcal{H}
with
\|x\|_{A}=1
, we get

\displaystyle\omega_{A}^{2}(T+S)\leq\omega_{A}^{2}(T)+\omega_{A}^{2}(S)+\frac{1}{2}\left\|T^{\sharp_{A}}T+SS^{\sharp_{A}}\right\|_{A}+\omega_{A}(ST).

This proves the desired result.
∎

As an application of the above theorem, we get the following corollary.

.

Let

. Then

\displaystyle\omega_{A}(T)\leq\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{{\big\|TT^{\sharp_{A}}+T^{\sharp_{A}}T\big\|}_{A}+2\omega_{A}(T^{2})}\leq\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\sqrt{\|T^{\sharp_{A}}T+TT^{\sharp_{A}}\|_{A}}.

Proof.

Clearly, the first inequality follows by taking
S=T
in Theorem
3.6
. Moreover, it is well-known that
\omega_{A}(T^{2})\leq\omega_{A}^{2}(T)
(see
[
15
]
) and
\omega_{A}^{2}(T)\leq\frac{1}{2}{\big\|TT^{\sharp_{A}}+T^{\sharp_{A}}T\big\|}_{A}
. So, we get that

\displaystyle\frac{1}{4}{\big\|TT^{\sharp_{A}}+T^{\sharp_{A}}T\big\|}_{A}+\frac{1}{2}\omega_{A}(T^{2})

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{4}{\big\|TT^{\sharp_{A}}+T^{\sharp_{A}}T\big\|}_{A}+\frac{1}{2}\omega_{A}^{2}(T)

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{4}{\big\|TT^{\sharp_{A}}+T^{\sharp_{A}}T\big\|}_{A}+\frac{1}{4}{\big\|TT^{\sharp_{A}}+T^{\sharp_{A}}T\big\|}_{A}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2}{\big\|TT^{\sharp_{A}}+T^{\sharp_{A}}T\big\|}_{A}.

.
∎

Remark 3.8
.

Note that Corollary

has been recently proved in
[

]
.

Our next improvement reads as:

Theorem 3.9
.

Let
T,S\in\mathcal{B}_{A}(\mathcal{H})
be
A
-selfadjoint. Then,

\omega_{A}(T+S)\leq\sqrt{\omega_{A}^{2}(T+{\rm i}S)+\omega_{A}(ST)+\|T\|_{A}\|S\|_{A}}\leq\omega_{A}(T)+\omega_{A}(S).

Proof.

Let
x\in\mathcal{H}
be such that
\|x\|_{A}=1.
Then we have,

\displaystyle|\langle(T+S)x,x\rangle_{A}|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle(|\langle Tx,x\rangle_{A}|+|\langle Tx,x\rangle_{A}|)^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle|\langle Tx,x\rangle_{A}|^{2}+|\langle Sx,x\rangle_{A}|^{2}+2|\langle Tx,x\rangle_{A}||\langle Sx,x\rangle_{A}|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle|\langle Tx,x\rangle_{A}+{\rm i}\langle Sx,x\rangle_{A}|^{2}+2|\langle Tx,x\rangle_{A}\langle Sx,x\rangle_{A}|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle|\langle(T+{\rm i}S)x,x\rangle_{A}|^{2}+2|\langle Tx,x\rangle_{A}\langle x,S^{\sharp_{A}}x\rangle_{A}|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle|\langle(T+{\rm i}S)x,x\rangle_{A}|^{2}+\|Tx\|_{A}\|S^{\sharp_{A}}x\|_{A}+|\langle Tx,S^{\sharp_{A}}x\rangle_{A}|

\displaystyle\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,~~(\mbox{by Lemma \ref{Lemma:3}})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle|\langle(T+{\rm i}S)x,x\rangle_{A}|^{2}+\|Tx\|_{A}\|S^{\sharp_{A}}x\|_{A}+|\langle STx,x\rangle_{A}|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\omega_{A}^{2}(T+{\rm i}S)+\|T\|_{A}\|S\|_{A}+\omega_{A}(ST).

Taking supremum over all
x\in\mathcal{H}
with
\|x\|_{A}=1
we get,

\displaystyle\omega_{A}^{2}(T+S)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\omega_{A}^{2}(T+{\rm i}S)+\|T\|_{A}\|S\|_{A}+\omega_{A}(ST).

Thus, we have the first inequality of the theorem. Now we prove the second inequality. It is not dificult to show that
\omega_{A}^{2}(T+{\rm i}S)\leq\|T\|_{A}^{2}+\|S\|_{A}^{2}.
Also we have
\omega_{A}(ST)\leq\|T\|_{A}\|S\|_{A}.
So,
\omega_{A}^{2}(T+{\rm i}S)+\|T\|_{A}\|S\|_{A}+\omega_{A}(ST)\leq(\|T\|_{A}+\|S\|_{A})^{2}.
Since
\omega_{A}(T)=\|T\|_{A}
and
\omega_{A}(S)=\|S\|_{A}
, so we get the required second inequality of the theorem.
∎

Next we obtain the inequalities for the sum of
k
operators. First we recall the following results:
For
T\in\mathcal{B}(\mathcal{H})

6

if and only if there exists a unique

such that
Z_{A}T=\widetilde{T}Z_{A}
. Here,

is defined by

. Also, it has been proved in
[

\|T\|_{A}=\|\widetilde{T}\|_{\mathcal{B}(\mathbf{R}(A^{1/2}))}\quad\text{ and }\quad\omega_{A}(T)=\omega(\widetilde{T}).

(3.7)

On the basis of the above results we obtain the following theorems.

Theorem 3.10
.

For
i=1,2,\ldots,k,
let
S_{i}\in\mathcal{B}_{A}(\mathcal{H})
. Then,

\displaystyle\omega^{4n}_{A}\left({\sum_{i=1}^{k}S_{i}}\right)

\displaystyle\leq\frac{k^{4n-1}}{4}\left[\left\|{\sum_{i=1}^{k}\left(\left(S_{i}^{\sharp_{A}}S_{i}\right)^{2n}+\left(S_{i}S_{i}^{\sharp_{A}}\right)^{2n}\right)}\right\|_{A}+2\sum_{i=1}^{k}\omega_{A}\left({\left(S_{i}^{\sharp_{A}}S_{i}\right)^{n}\left(S_{i}S_{i}^{\sharp_{A}}\right)^{n}}\right)\right],

for all
n=1,2,3,\ldots.

Proof.

Let
x\in\mathcal{H}
be such that
\|x\|=1
. Since
S_{i}\in\mathcal{B}_{A}(\mathcal{H})
, so
S_{i}\in\mathcal{B}(\mathcal{H})
. Then we have,

\displaystyle\left|\left\langle\left(\sum_{i=1}^{k}S_{i}\right)x,x\right\rangle\right|^{4n}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\left(\sum_{i=1}^{k}|\left\langle S_{i}x,x\right\rangle|\right)^{4n}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle k^{4n-1}\sum_{i=1}^{k}|\left\langle S_{i}x,x\right\rangle|^{4n}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle k^{4n-1}\sum_{i=1}^{k}\langle|S_{i}|x,x\rangle^{2n}\langle|S_{i}^{*}|x,x\rangle^{2n},\,\,~~\Big(|\left\langle S_{i}x,x\right\rangle|^{2}\leq\langle|S_{i}|x,x\rangle\langle|S_{i}^{*}|x,x\rangle\Big)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle k^{4n-1}\sum_{i=1}^{k}\langle|S_{i}|^{2n}x,x\rangle\langle|S_{i}^{*}|^{2n}x,x\rangle,\,\,~~\Big(\langle Sx,x\rangle^{r}\leq\langle S^{r}x,x\rangle,S\geq 0,r\geq 1\Big)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle k^{4n-1}\sum_{i=1}^{k}\langle|S_{i}|^{2n}x,x\rangle\langle x,|S_{i}^{*}|^{2n}x\rangle

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{k^{4n-1}}{2}\sum_{i=1}^{k}\left(\left\||S_{i}|^{2n}x\right\|\left\||S_{i}^{*}|^{2n}x\right\|+|\langle|S_{i}|^{2n}x,|S_{i}^{*}|^{2n}x\rangle|\right)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{k^{4n-1}}{2}\sum_{i=1}^{k}\left(\frac{1}{2}(\left\||S_{i}|^{2n}x\right\|^{2}+\left\||S_{i}^{*}|^{2n}x\right\|^{2})+|\langle|S_{i}|^{2n}|S_{i}^{*}|^{2n}x,x\rangle|\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{k^{4n-1}}{4}\sum_{i=1}^{k}\left\langle(|S_{i}|^{4n}+|S_{i}^{*}|^{4n})x,x\right\rangle+\frac{k^{4n-1}}{2}\sum_{i=1}^{k}|\langle|S_{i}|^{2n}|S_{i}^{*}|^{2n}x,x\rangle|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{k^{4n-1}}{4}\left\langle\left(\sum_{i=1}^{k}(|S_{i}|^{4n}+|S_{i}^{*}|^{4n})\right)x,x\right\rangle+\frac{k^{4n-1}}{2}\sum_{i=1}^{k}|\langle|S_{i}|^{2n}|S_{i}^{*}|^{2n}x,x\rangle|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{k^{4n-1}}{4}\left\|\sum_{i=1}^{k}(|S_{i}|^{4n}+|S_{i}^{*}|^{4n})\right\|+\frac{k^{4n-1}}{2}\sum_{i=1}^{k}\omega\left(|S_{i}|^{2n}|S_{i}^{*}|^{2n}\right),

where the second inequality follows from Bohr’s inequality
(
[
24
]
)
, i.e.,
if for
i=1,2,\ldots,n
,
a_{i}
be a positive real number then

\left(\sum_{i=1}^{k}a_{i}\right)^{r}\leq k^{r-1}\sum_{i=1}^{k}a_{i}^{r},r\geq 1

and the fifth inequality follows from Buzano’s inequality (
[
12
]
), i.e.,
if
x,y,e\in\mathcal{H}
with
\|e\|=1
then

|\langle x,e\rangle\langle e,y\rangle|\leq\frac{1}{2}\left(\|x\|\|y\|+|\langle x,y\rangle|\right).

Taking supremum over all
x\in\mathcal{H}
with
\|x\|=1
we get,

\displaystyle\omega^{4n}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{k}S_{i}\right)\leq\frac{k^{4n-1}}{4}\left\|\sum_{i=1}^{k}(|S_{i}|^{4n}+|S_{i}^{*}|^{4n})\right\|+\frac{k^{4n-1}}{2}\sum_{i=1}^{k}\omega\left(|S_{i}|^{2n}|S_{i}^{*}|^{2n}\right).

(3.8)

Now since
\mathcal{B}_{A}(\mathcal{H})\subseteq\mathcal{B}_{A{{}^{1/2}}}(\mathcal{H})
, so for each
i=1,2,\ldots,k
,
S_{i}\in\mathcal{B}_{A{{}^{1/2}}}(\mathcal{H}).
Therefore, there exists unique
\widetilde{S_{i}}
in
\mathcal{B}(\mathbf{R}(A^{1/2}))
such that
Z_{A}S_{i}=\widetilde{S_{i}}Z_{A}
. Now,
\mathbf{R}(A^{1/2})
being a complex Hilbert space, so (
3.8
) implies that

\displaystyle\omega^{4n}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{k}\widetilde{S_{i}}\right)

\displaystyle\leq\frac{k^{4n-1}}{4}\left\|\sum_{i=1}^{k}(|\widetilde{S_{i}}|^{4n}+|\widetilde{S_{i}}^{*}|^{4n})\right\|_{\mathcal{B}(\mathbf{R}(A^{1/2}))}+\frac{k^{4n-1}}{2}\sum_{i=1}^{k}\omega\left(|\widetilde{S_{i}}|^{2n}|\widetilde{S_{i}}^{*}|^{2n}\right).

It is well-known that for
S,T\in\mathcal{B}_{A{{}^{1/2}}}(\mathcal{H})
, we have
\widetilde{S+\lambda T}=\widetilde{S}+\lambda\widetilde{T}
and
\widetilde{ST}=\widetilde{S}\widetilde{T}
for all
\lambda\in\mathbb{C}
(see
[
17
]
). So, from the above inequality we have,

\displaystyle\omega^{4n}\left(\widetilde{\sum_{i=1}^{k}S_{i}}\right)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{k^{4n-1}}{4}\left\|\sum_{i=1}^{k}\left(\left((\widetilde{S_{i}})^{*}\widetilde{S_{i}}\right)^{2n}+\left(\widetilde{S_{i}}(\widetilde{S_{i}})^{*}\right)^{2n}\right)\right\|_{\mathcal{B}(\mathbf{R}(A^{1/2}))}

\displaystyle+\frac{k^{4n-1}}{2}\sum_{i=1}^{k}\omega\left(\left((\widetilde{S_{i}})^{*}\widetilde{S_{i}}\right)^{n}\left(\widetilde{S_{i}}(\widetilde{S_{i}})^{*}\right)^{n}\right).

Also since
(\widetilde{S_{i}})^{*}=\widetilde{S_{i}^{\sharp_{A}}}
, so

\displaystyle\omega^{4n}\left(\widetilde{\sum_{i=1}^{k}S_{i}}\right)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{k^{4n-1}}{4}\left\|\sum_{i=1}^{k}\left(\left(\widetilde{S_{i}^{\sharp_{A}}}\widetilde{S_{i}}\right)^{2n}+\left(\widetilde{S_{i}}\widetilde{S_{i}^{\sharp_{A}}}\right)^{2n}\right)\right\|_{\mathcal{B}(\mathbf{R}(A^{1/2}))}

\displaystyle+\frac{k^{4n-1}}{2}\omega\left(\left(\widetilde{S_{i}^{\sharp_{A}}}\widetilde{S_{i}}\right)^{n}\left(\widetilde{S_{i}}\widetilde{S_{i}^{\sharp_{A}}}\right)^{n}\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{k^{4n-1}}{4}\left\|\widetilde{\sum_{i=1}^{k}\left(\left(S_{i}^{\sharp_{A}}S_{i}\right)^{2n}+\left(S_{i}S_{i}^{\sharp_{A}}\right)^{2n}\right)}\right\|_{\mathcal{B}(\mathbf{R}(A^{1/2}))}

\displaystyle+\frac{k^{4n-1}}{2}\sum_{i=1}^{k}\omega\left(\widetilde{\left(S_{i}^{\sharp_{A}}S_{i}\right)^{n}\left(S_{i}S_{i}^{\sharp_{A}}\right)^{n}}\right).

Hence,

\displaystyle\omega^{4n}_{A}\left({\sum_{i=1}^{k}S_{i}}\right)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{k^{4n-1}}{4}\left\|{\sum_{i=1}^{k}\left(\left(S_{i}^{\sharp_{A}}S_{i}\right)^{2n}+\left(S_{i}S_{i}^{\sharp_{A}}\right)^{2n}\right)}\right\|_{A}

\displaystyle+\frac{k^{4n-1}}{2}\sum_{i=1}^{k}\omega_{A}\left({\left(S_{i}^{\sharp_{A}}S_{i}\right)^{n}\left(S_{i}S_{i}^{\sharp_{A}}\right)^{n}}\right).

Thus, we complete the proof.
∎

In particular considering
k=1
and
n=1
in Theorem
3.10
we get the following result.

Corollary 3.11
.

Let
S\in\mathcal{B}_{A}(\mathbb{H}).
Then

\omega_{A}^{4}(S)\leq\frac{1}{4}\left\|\left(S^{\sharp_{A}}S\right)^{2}+\left(SS^{\sharp_{A}}\right)^{2}\right\|_{A}+\frac{1}{2}\omega_{A}\left(S^{\sharp_{A}}S^{2}S^{\sharp_{A}}\right).

Next result reads as:

Theorem 3.12
.

For
i=1,2,\ldots,n,
let
S_{i}\in\mathcal{B}_{A}(\mathcal{H})
. Then

\omega_{A}^{2n}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{k}S_{i}\right)\leq\frac{k^{2n-1}}{2}\left\|\sum_{i=1}^{k}\left(\left(S_{i}^{\sharp_{A}}S_{i}\right)^{n}+\left(S_{i}S_{i}^{\sharp_{A}}\right)^{n}\right)\right\|_{A}.

Proof.

Let
x\in\mathcal{H}
with
\|x\|=1
.
S_{i}\in\mathcal{B}_{A}(\mathcal{H})
implies
S_{i}\in\mathcal{B}(\mathcal{H})
. So we have,

\displaystyle\left|\left\langle\left(\sum_{i=1}^{k}S_{i}\right)x,x\right\rangle\right|^{2n}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\left(\sum_{i=1}^{k}|\left\langle S_{i}x,x\right\rangle|\right)^{2n}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle k^{2n-1}\sum_{i=1}^{k}|\left\langle S_{i}x,x\right\rangle|^{2n}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle k^{2n-1}\sum_{i=1}^{k}\langle|S_{i}|x,x\rangle^{n}\langle|S_{i}^{*}|x,x\rangle^{n},\,\,~~\Big(|\left\langle S_{i}x,x\right\rangle|^{2}\leq\langle|S_{i}|x,x\rangle\langle|S_{i}^{*}|x,x\rangle\Big)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle k^{2n-1}\sum_{i=1}^{k}\langle|S_{i}|^{n}x,x\rangle\langle|S_{i}^{*}|^{n}x,x\rangle,\,\,~~\Big(\langle Sx,x\rangle^{r}\leq\langle S^{r}x,x\rangle,S\geq 0,r\geq 1\Big)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{k^{2n-1}}{2}\sum_{i=1}^{k}\left(\langle|S_{i}|^{n}x,x\rangle^{2}+\langle|S_{i}^{*}|^{n}x,x\rangle^{2}\right)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{k^{2n-1}}{2}\sum_{i=1}^{k}\left(\langle|S_{i}|^{2n}x,x\rangle+\langle|S_{i}^{*}|^{2n}x,x\rangle\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{k^{2n-1}}{2}\sum_{i=1}^{k}\langle\left(|S_{i}|^{2n}+|S_{i}^{*}|^{2n}\right)x,x\rangle

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{k^{2n-1}}{2}\left\langle\sum_{i=1}^{k}\left(|S_{i}|^{2n}+|S_{i}^{*}|^{2n}\right)x,x\right\rangle

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{k^{2n-1}}{2}\left\|\sum_{i=1}^{k}\left(|S_{i}|^{2n}+|S_{i}^{*}|^{2n}\right)\right\|.

Taking supremum over all
x\in\mathcal{H}
with
\|x\|=1
we get,

\displaystyle\omega^{2n}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{k}S_{i}\right)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{k^{2n-1}}{2}\left\|\sum_{i=1}^{k}\left(|S_{i}|^{2n}+|S_{i}^{*}|^{2n}\right)\right\|.

(3.9)

Now for each
i=1,2,\ldots,k
,
S_{i}\in\mathcal{B}_{A{{}^{1/2}}}(\mathcal{H}).
So, there exists unique
\widetilde{S_{i}}
in
\mathcal{B}(\mathbf{R}(A^{1/2}))
such that
Z_{A}S_{i}=\widetilde{S_{i}}Z_{A}
. Now
\mathbf{R}(A^{1/2})
being a complex Hilbert space, we have from (
3.9
) that

\displaystyle\omega^{2n}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{k}\widetilde{S_{i}}\right)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{k^{2n-1}}{2}\left\|\sum_{i=1}^{k}(|\widetilde{S_{i}}|^{2n}+|\widetilde{S_{i}}^{*}|^{2n})\right\|_{\mathcal{B}(\mathbf{R}(A^{1/2}))}.

Using the property that if
S,T\in\mathcal{B}_{A{{}^{1/2}}}(\mathcal{H})
then
\widetilde{S+\lambda T}=\widetilde{S}+\lambda\widetilde{T}
and
\widetilde{ST}=\widetilde{S}\widetilde{T}
for all
\lambda\in\mathbb{C}
, we have

\displaystyle\omega^{2n}\left(\widetilde{\sum_{i=1}^{k}S_{i}}\right)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{k^{2n-1}}{2}\left\|\sum_{i=1}^{k}\left(\left((\widetilde{S_{i}})^{*}\widetilde{S_{i}}\right)^{n}+\left(\widetilde{S_{i}}(\widetilde{S_{i}})^{*}\right)^{n}\right)\right\|_{\mathcal{B}(\mathbf{R}(A^{1/2}))}.

Also
(\widetilde{S_{i}})^{*}=\widetilde{S_{i}^{\sharp_{A}}}
, so

\displaystyle\omega^{2n}\left(\widetilde{\sum_{i=1}^{k}S_{i}}\right)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{k^{2n-1}}{2}\left\|\sum_{i=1}^{k}\left(\left(\widetilde{S_{i}^{\sharp_{A}}}\widetilde{S_{i}}\right)^{n}+\left(\widetilde{S_{i}}\widetilde{S_{i}^{\sharp_{A}}}\right)^{n}\right)\right\|_{\mathcal{B}(\mathbf{R}(A^{1/2}))}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{k^{2n-1}}{2}\left\|\widetilde{\sum_{i=1}^{k}\left(\left(S_{i}^{\sharp_{A}}S_{i}\right)^{n}+\left(S_{i}S_{i}^{\sharp_{A}}\right)^{n}\right)}\right\|_{\mathcal{B}(\mathbf{R}(A^{1/2}))}.

Hence,

\displaystyle\omega^{2n}_{A}\left({\sum_{i=1}^{k}S_{i}}\right)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{k^{2n-1}}{2}\left\|{\sum_{i=1}^{k}\left(\left(S_{i}^{\sharp_{A}}S_{i}\right)^{n}+\left(S_{i}S_{i}^{\sharp_{A}}\right)^{n}\right)}\right\|_{A}.

Hence we complete the proof.
∎

Finally we obtain the following result.

Theorem 3.13
.

For
i=1,2,\ldots,n,
let
S_{i}\in\mathcal{B}_{A}(\mathcal{H})
. Then,

\omega_{A}^{2n}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{k}S_{i}\right)\leq\frac{k^{2n-1}}{\sqrt{2}}\sum_{i=1}^{k}\omega_{A}\left(\left(S_{i}^{\sharp_{A}}S_{i}\right)^{n}+{\rm i}\left(S_{i}S_{i}^{\sharp_{A}}\right)^{n}\right).

Proof.

Let
x\in\mathcal{H}
with
\|x\|=1
. Then we have,

\displaystyle\left|\left\langle\left(\sum_{i=1}^{k}S_{i}\right)x,x\right\rangle\right|^{2n}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{k^{2n-1}}{2}\sum_{i=1}^{k}\left(\langle|S_{i}|^{2n}x,x\rangle+\langle|S_{i}^{*}|^{2n}x,x\rangle\right).

Now we observe that
|a+b|\leq\sqrt{2}|a+{\rm i}b|
for all
a,b\in\mathbb{R}.
Using this inequality we have,

\displaystyle\left|\left\langle\left(\sum_{i=1}^{k}S_{i}\right)x,x\right\rangle\right|^{2n}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{k^{2n-1}}{\sqrt{2}}\sum_{i=1}^{k}\left|\langle|S_{i}|^{2n}x,x\rangle+{\rm i}\langle|S_{i}^{*}|^{2n}x,x\rangle\right|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{k^{2n-1}}{\sqrt{2}}\sum_{i=1}^{k}\omega\left(|S_{i}|^{2n}+{\rm i}|S_{i}^{*}|^{2n}\right).

Taking supremum over all
x\in\mathcal{H}
with
\|x\|=1
we get,

\displaystyle\omega^{2n}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{k}S_{i}\right)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{k^{2n-1}}{\sqrt{2}}\sum_{i=1}^{k}\omega\left(|S_{i}|^{2n}+{\rm i}|S_{i}^{*}|^{2n}\right).

(3.10)

Now for each
i=1,2,\ldots,k
,
S_{i}\in\mathcal{B}_{A{{}^{1/2}}}(\mathcal{H}).
So, there exists unique
\widetilde{S_{i}}
in
\mathcal{B}(\mathbf{R}(A^{1/2}))
such that
Z_{A}S_{i}=\widetilde{S_{i}}Z_{A}
. Now
\mathbf{R}(A^{1/2})
being a complex Hilbert space, from (
3.10
) we get,

\displaystyle\omega^{2n}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{k}\widetilde{S_{i}}\right)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{k^{2n-1}}{\sqrt{2}}\sum_{i=1}^{k}\omega\left(|\widetilde{S_{i}}|^{2n}+{\rm i}|\widetilde{S_{i}}^{*}|^{2n}\right).

(3.11)

Using the property that if
S,T\in\mathcal{B}_{A{{}^{1/2}}}(\mathcal{H})
then
\widetilde{S+\lambda T}=\widetilde{S}+\lambda\widetilde{T}
and
\widetilde{ST}=\widetilde{S}\widetilde{T}
for all
\lambda\in\mathbb{C}
and
(\widetilde{S_{i}})^{*}=\widetilde{S_{i}^{\sharp_{A}}}
, we have form (
3.11
) that

\displaystyle\omega^{2n}\left(\widetilde{\sum_{i=1}^{k}\widetilde{S_{i}}}\right)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{k^{2n-1}}{\sqrt{2}}\sum_{i=1}^{k}\omega\left(\widetilde{(S_{i}^{\sharp_{A}}S_{i})^{n}+{\rm i}(S_{i}S_{i}^{\sharp_{A}})^{n}}\right).

Hence,

\omega_{A}^{2n}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{k}S_{i}\right)\leq\frac{k^{2n-1}}{\sqrt{2}}\sum_{i=1}^{k}\omega_{A}\left(\left(S_{i}^{\sharp_{A}}S_{i}\right)^{n}+{\rm i}\left(S_{i}S_{i}^{\sharp_{A}}\right)^{n}\right),

as required.
∎

.

Corollary 3.14
.

Let
S\in\mathcal{B}_{A}(\mathcal{H})
. Then

\omega_{A}^{2}\left(S\right)\leq\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\omega_{A}\left(S^{\sharp_{A}}S+{\rm i}~~SS^{\sharp_{A}}\right).
