In 1878, Dedekind introduced a notable criterion known as the Dedekind criterion (

Theorem 2.4

(Dedekind Criterion) Let
\theta
be an algebraic integer and
f(x)
be the minimal polynomial of
\theta
over
\mathbb{Q}.
Let
K=\mathbb{Q}(\theta)
be the corresponding number field. Let
p
be a prime and

\bar{f}(x)=\bar{f}_{1}(x)^{a_{1}}\bar{f}_{2}(x)^{a_{2}}\cdots\bar{f}_{t}(x)^{a_{t}}

be the factorization of
\bar{f}(x)
as a product of powers of distinct monic irreducible polynomials over the field
{\mathbb{F}}_{p}.
Let

be the polynomial defined as

M(x)=\frac{1}{p}(f(x)-{f}_{1}(x)^{a_{1}}{f}_{2}(x)^{a_{2}}\cdots{f}_{t}(x)^{a_{t}})\in\mathbb{Z}[x],

where

are monic lifts of
\bar{f}_{i}(x),

Then, a prime
p\nmid[\mathcal{O}_{K}:\mathbb{Z}[\theta]]
if and only if for each

does not divide
\bar{M}(x).
