Some geometric consequences of the symmetric reduction
Taiwang DENG

Abstract
. In this article, we derived some consequences to the symmetrization process developed in [ 5 ] . This consists a geometric derivation of part of the properties which uniquely determines the Kazhdan-Lusztig polynomials of type A n \displaystyle A_{n} as well as an interpretations of the last property by the decomposition theorem of [ 1 ] . Finally, the relation of geometry of graded nilpotent classes to Parabolic Kazhdan-Lusztig polynomials is discussed for the purpose of later applications to Zelevinsky’s derivatives.

The goal of the present paper is twofold. Firstly we aim to draw some consequence from our previous paper

In loc.cit., we have developed a symmetrization process to study the geometry of graded nilpotent classes.
In terms of the infinite dimensional representation theory of

In the first part of the paper, we consider the

by comparing with the combinatorial
properties of

. This will be crucial in our geometric determination of the Zelevinsky’s derivatives in
[

In the second part of the paper, we generalize the study of the symmetric case in
[
5

to the parabolic cases. In the regular cases, the main results are given in Proposition

\displaystyle S(\mathbf{a})
with
\displaystyle\mathbf{a}

We will apply the results of this paper to calculate Zelevinsky’s derivatives in

Finally, the reader may wonder why the author put such an effort into making explicit the relations between the geometry of Schubert varieties and the geometry of graded nilpotent classes. It turns out that there are deeper reasons lying behind the scenes. In fact, it follows from our discussions that a certain category of equivariant perverse sheaves embeds fully faithfully into a certain category of equivariant perverse sheaves on the space of graded nilpotent classes. This gives the dual version of the main results of

\displaystyle\displaystyle\sum_{j\neq k_{1}-1,k_{1}}[b(\Delta_{w^{-1}(j)}),e(\Delta_{j})]+[b(\Delta_{w^{-1}(k_{1}-1)}),e(\Delta_{k_{1}})]+[b(\Delta_{w^{-1}(k_{1})}),e(\Delta_{k_{1}-1})],

Lemma 3.15

\displaystyle J=\{\sigma_{i_{0}}\}\cup J_{1}

\displaystyle w_{j}\in S^{J_{1}}
