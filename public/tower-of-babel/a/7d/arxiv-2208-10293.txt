Mod p p homology of unordered configuration spaces of surfaces
Matthew Chen, Adela YiYu Zhang

Abstract
. We provide a short proof that the dimensions of the mod p p homology groups of the unordered configuration space B k ​ ( T ) B_{k}(T) of k k points in a torus are the same as its Betti numbers for p > 2 p>2 and k ≤ p k\leq p . Hence the integral homology has no p p -power torsion. The same argument works for the punctured genus g g surface with g > 0 g>0 , thereby recovering a result of Brantner-Hahn-Knudsen via Lubin-Tate theory.

[
Knu17
]

The
i
th Betti number of
B_{k}(\Sigma_{g})
and
B_{k}(\Sigma_{g,1})
are respectively equal to the dimension over
\mathbb{Q}
of
\bigoplus_{s+t=i}H_{s,t}\big(\mathrm{wt}_{k}(\mathrm{CE}(\mathfrak{g}^{\prime};\mathbb{Q}))\big)
and
\bigoplus_{s+t=i}H_{s,t}\big(\mathrm{wt}_{k}(\mathrm{CE}(\mathfrak{g}^{\prime}_{1};\mathbb{Q}))\big)
for all
i
.

Explicit formulae for the Betti numbers
\beta_{i}(B_{k}(T))
were obtained by Drummond-Cole and Knudsen in
[
DCK17
, Corollary 4.5-4.7]
and
\beta_{i}(B_{k}(\Sigma_{g,1}))
in
[
DCK17
, Proposition 3.5]
.

We will deduce the higher differentials in the spectral sequence

(3)

E^{2}_{s,t}(k)=\pi_{s}\pi_{t}\big(\mathrm{Bar}_{\bullet}\big(\mathrm{id},s\mathscr{L},\mathrm{Free}^{s\mathscr{L}}(\Sigma^{n}\mathbb{S})^{M^{+}}\big)\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}\mathbb{F}_{p}\big)(k)\Rightarrow H_{s+t}(B_{k}(M);\mathbb{F}_{p})

where
M=T
or
\Sigma_{g,1}
by combining
Proposition

2.6
with
Theorem

3.3
.

Theorem 3.4

(
Theorem

1.1
)
.

For
k\leq p
, the dimension of
H_{i}(B_{k}(T);\mathbb{F}_{p})
over
\mathbb{F}_{p}
is equal to the Betti number
\beta_{i}(B_{k}(T))
for all
i
. Hence the integral homology of
B_{k}(T)
has no
p
-power torsion for
k\leq p
.

Proof.

The isomorphism between the
\mathbb{F}_{p}
-basis of
\mathfrak{g}
and the
\mathbb{Q}
-basis of
\mathfrak{g}^{\prime}
(cf.
3.1
) induces compatible isomorphisms between the basis

\big\{\gamma_{k_{1}}(u_{1})\cdots\gamma_{k_{m}}(u_{m})\langle v_{1},\dots,v_{n}\rangle,\ u_{1},\ldots,u_{m},v_{1},\ldots v_{m}\in B\big\}

on each simplicial level of
\mathrm{CE}(\mathfrak{g};\mathbb{F}_{p})
and
\mathrm{CE}(\mathfrak{g}^{\prime};\mathbb{Q})
. Since the integral cohomology of
T
is torsion-free and
\gamma_{p}(y\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}x_{2})
in weight
p
does not receive differentials, the differentials in the two
\mathrm{CE}
complexes preserve the isomorphism in weight
k\leq p
. Furthermore, when
p=3
the simplicial objects
\mathrm{Bar}_{\bullet}(\mathrm{id},\mathrm{Lie}^{s}_{\mathbb{F}_{3}},\mathfrak{g})
and
\mathrm{Bar}_{\bullet}(\mathrm{id},\mathrm{Lie}^{s,\mathrm{op}}_{\mathbb{F}_{3}},\mathfrak{g})
are isomorphic in weight
k<p
. Hence

(4)

\dim_{\mathbb{F}_{p}}\bigoplus_{s+t=i}\mathrm{wt}_{k}H_{s,t}(\mathrm{CE}(\mathfrak{g};\mathbb{F}_{p}))=\dim_{\mathbb{Q}}\bigoplus_{s+t=i}\mathrm{wt}_{k}H_{s,t}(\mathrm{CE}(\mathfrak{g}^{\prime};\mathbb{Q}))=\beta_{i}(B_{k}(T))

for all
i
and
k\leq p
, with the right equality given by
Theorem

3.3
.

By
Proposition

2.6
.(1), the
E^{2}
-page of the weight
k<p
part of the spectral sequence (
3
) is

E^{2}_{s,t}(k)\cong\mathrm{wt}_{k}H_{s,t}(\mathrm{CE}(\mathfrak{g};\mathbb{F}_{p})).

Since
B_{k}(T)
is of finite type, the dimension of
H_{i}(B_{k}(T);\mathbb{F}_{p})
over
\mathbb{F}_{p}
is at least
\beta_{i}(B_{k}(T))
for all
i
. Therefore no higher differential can happen by
Equation

4
.

Now we tackle the case
k=p
.
First we consider
p>3
.
By
Proposition

2.6
.(2), the
E^{2}
-page of the weight
k
part of the spectral sequence (
3
) is given by

E^{2}_{*,*}(p)\cong\mathrm{wt}_{p}H_{*,*}(\mathrm{CE}(\mathfrak{g};\mathbb{F}_{p}))\oplus\mathbb{F}_{p}\{\overline{Q^{0}}|c\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}x_{2},\overline{\beta Q^{0}}|c\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}x_{2}\}.

Note that the class
\overline{\beta Q^{0}}|c\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}x_{2}\in E^{2}_{1,-2}
has total degree
-1
, so it has to killed by a class with total degree
0
and simplicial degree at least 3. There is exactly one class of total degree
0
in
\mathrm{wt}_{p}H_{*,*}(\mathrm{CE}(\mathfrak{g};\mathbb{F}_{p}))
since
\beta_{0}(B_{p}(T))=1
, which is the class
\gamma_{p}(c\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}x_{2})\in E^{2}_{p-1,1-p}
. Therefore there has to be a
d_{p-2}
-differential
\gamma_{p}(c\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}x_{2})\mapsto\overline{\beta Q^{0}}|c\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}x_{2}.

Appealing to the inequality

\sum_{i}\dim_{\mathbb{F}_{p}}H_{i}(B_{k}(T);\mathbb{F}_{p})\geq\sum_{i}\beta_{i}(B_{k}(T))\stackrel{{\scriptstyle(\ref{CEsamedim})}}{{=}}\dim_{\mathbb{F}_{p}}\bigoplus_{s,t}\mathrm{wt}_{p}H_{s,t}(\mathrm{CE}(\mathfrak{g};\mathbb{F}_{p}))=\sum_{s,t}\dim_{\mathbb{F}_{p}}E^{2}_{s,t}(p)-2,

we deduce that no other higher differential can happen.

For
p=3
, we deduce from
Remark

2.7
that the normalized complexes of
\mathrm{Bar}_{\bullet}(\mathrm{id},\mathrm{Lie}^{s}_{\mathbb{F}_{3}},\mathfrak{g})
and
\mathrm{Bar}_{\bullet}(\mathrm{id},\mathrm{Lie}^{s,\mathrm{op}}_{\mathbb{F}_{3}},\mathfrak{g})
differ in the weight 3 part in that there is a differential
\gamma_{3}(c\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}x_{2})\mapsto\overline{\beta Q^{0}}|c\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}x_{2}
in the latter but not in the former. Hence in the spectral sequence (
3
), the element

\gamma_{3}(c\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}x_{2})=[[c\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}x_{2},c\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}x_{2}],c\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}x_{2}]\in\mathrm{Lie}^{s}_{\bar{\pazocal{R}}}\circ\mathrm{Lie}^{s}_{\bar{\pazocal{R}}}\Big(H^{*}(T;\mathbb{F}_{3})\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}\mathrm{Lie}^{s}_{\bar{\pazocal{R}}}(\mathbb{F}_{3}\{x_{2}\})\Big)

with the two brackets come from different iterations of
\mathrm{Lie}^{s}_{\bar{\pazocal{R}}}
is mapped by the
d_{1}
-differential to

[[c\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}x_{2},c\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}x_{2}],c\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}x_{2}]\in\mathrm{Lie}^{s}_{\bar{\pazocal{R}}}\Big(H^{*}(T;\mathbb{F}_{3})\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}\mathrm{Lie}^{s}_{\bar{\pazocal{R}}}(\mathbb{F}_{3}\{x_{2}\})\Big)

with both brackets coming from the same iteration of
\mathrm{Lie}^{s}_{\bar{\pazocal{R}}}
. At weight 3, there are no more differentials between elements containing a unary operation and brackets. It follows that the
E^{2}
-page of the weight 3 part of the spectral sequence (
3
) has a basis given by the union of an
\mathbb{F}_{3}
-basis of
\mathrm{wt}_{3}H_{*,*}(\mathrm{CE}(\mathfrak{g};\mathbb{F}_{3}))/\mathbb{F}_{3}\{\gamma_{3}(c\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}x_{2})\}
and
\{\overline{Q^{0}}|c\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}x_{2}\}
. Combining with
Equation

4
, we see that

\sum_{i}\beta_{i}(B_{3}(T))=\dim_{\mathbb{F}_{3}}\bigoplus_{s,t}\mathrm{wt}_{3}H_{s,t}(\mathrm{CE}(\mathfrak{g};\mathbb{F}_{3}))=\sum_{s,t}\dim_{\mathbb{F}_{3}}E^{2}_{s,t}(3)\geq\sum_{i}\dim_{\mathbb{F}_{3}}H_{i}(B_{3}(T);\mathbb{F}_{3}).

Hence no higher differential can happen and equality is achieved.

Therefore the dimensions of
H_{*}(B_{k}(T);\mathbb{F}_{p})
over
\mathbb{F}_{p}
agree with the Betti numbers for
k\leq p
. Since
B_{k}(T)
is a finite complex, we further deduce that its integral homology has no
p
-power torsion for
k\leq p
.
∎

Remark 3.6
.

The
d_{p-2}
-differential
\gamma_{p}(c\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}x_{2})\mapsto\overline{\beta Q^{0}}|c\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}x_{2}
should be viewed as a universal differential, in the sense that it occurs in the universal case

, cf.
[
Zha21

. Then
\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}\Omega^{n}\mathrm{Free}^{s\mathscr{L}}(\Sigma^{n}X)\simeq X
and the spectral sequence (
2
) becomes

E^{2}_{s,t}=\pi_{s}\pi_{t}\mathrm{Bar}_{\bullet}(\mathrm{id},s\mathscr{L},\mathbb{S}^{r}\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}\mathbb{F}_{p})\Rightarrow\pi_{s+t}|\mathrm{Bar}_{\bullet}(\mathrm{id},s\mathscr{L},\mathbb{S}^{r}\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}\mathbb{F}_{p})|\cong\pi_{s,t}(\mathrm{Free}^{\mathbb{E}^{\mathrm{nu}}_{\infty}\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}\mathbb{F}_{p}}(\mathbb{S}^{r})),

where
\mathbb{E}^{\mathrm{nu}}_{\infty}\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}\mathbb{F}_{p}
is the non-unital
\mathbb{E}_{\infty}
-operad in the category of
\mathbb{F}_{p}
-module spectra. Heuristically, this is because the bottom non-vanishing mod
p
Dyer-Lashof operation on a class
x
of degree
2j
in an
\mathbb{E}^{\mathrm{nu}}_{\infty}
-
\mathbb{F}_{p}
-algebra is given by
\bar{Q}^{j}(x)=x^{\mathbin{\mathop{\otimes}\displaylimits}p}
, so
\gamma_{p}(x)
is redundant.
