Equivariant derived category of a reductive group as a categorical center
Roman Bezrukavnikov, Andrei Ionov

Abstract
We prove that the adjoint equivariant derived category of a reductive group G G is equivalent to the appropriately defined monoidal center of the torus-equivariant version of the Hecke category. We use this to give new proofs, independent of sheaf-theoretic set up, of the fact that the Drinfeld center of the abelian Hecke category is equivalent to the abelian category of unipotent character sheaves; and of a characterization of strongly-central sheaves on the torus.



1
Introduction

Let
G
be a connected reductive group, either over
\mathbb{C}
or split over a finite field. In this paper, we study the equivariant, with respect to the adjoint aciton of
G
on itself, constructible derived category of sheaves on
G
, which we denote
D^{b}(G/_{\operatorname{Ad}}G)
, not specifying the precise sheaf-theoretic setting for now. The category
D^{b}(G/_{\operatorname{Ad}}G)
is well-known to be a braided monoidal category. We relate this category to the appropriately defined center of another triangulated monoidal category
\mathcal{H}^{(1)}
, which we call the
T
-equivariant Hecke category (for the adjoint action of a maximal torus
T\subset G
).

Similarly, we relate the derived category
D^{b}_{\mathfrak{C}}(G)\subset D^{b}(G/_{\operatorname{Ad}}G)
of unipotent character sheaves on
G
to the appropriately defined center of the monodromic
T
-equivariant Hecke category with monodromic parameter
\lambda
,
\mathcal{H}^{(1)}_{\lambda}
.

The idea to relate the category of character sheaves to the categorical center of the monoidal Hecke category is not new. In
[
BFO12
]
, first named author, Finkelberg and Ostrik proved that the abelian category
\mathfrak{C}
of unipotent character sheaves is equivalent to the Drinfeld center of the abelian category of Harish-Chandra D-modules. We reprove their result and extend it to other sheaf-theoretic settings.

In
[
BN09
]
, authors prove that the appropriately defined
\infty
-category of unipotent character sheaves is the categorical center of both equivariant and monodromic versions of the
\infty
-Hecke category in the setting of
D
-modules. While this work was in final stages of preparation, preprint
[
HL23
]
appeared, where the Drinfeld center of the Hecke category is computed in a general
\infty
-categorical setting. Our approach is different in that we don’t leave the world of triangulated categories (except when considering the statement about abelian Drinfeld center). We deduce our results from a statement valid for all equivariant sheaves on the group (Theorem
2.2.1
). We were informed by David Ben-Zvi that a close statement in the
\infty
-categorical D-module setting follows from the results of
[
BGO18
]
.

In the upcoming work, second and third named authors, together with the authors of
[
HL23
]
, are planning to use this fact to define a mixed Koszul self-duality for the derived category of character sheaves. This has applications, in particular, to symmetries in Khovanov-Rozansky homology theories, and the generalizations of those theories, as in
[
GH22
]
. Two-periodic version of such a Koszul duality equivalence in characteristic 0 appears in
[
BN09
]
.

Finally, we use our results to give a new proof of a variant of the vanishing result proved in
[
Che22
]
, eliminating a restriction on the characteristic.

We also mention that the description of the asymptotic abelain category of character sheaves as a categorical center was obtained in
[
Lus14
]
,
[
Lus15
]
.

For the fact that

-operations and Proposition
