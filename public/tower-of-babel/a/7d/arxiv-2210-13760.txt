A Scattering Result of the Radial Defocusing Cubic Schrodinger Equation in the Hyperbolic Space with Rough Data
Chutian Ma

Abstract
. In this paper, we study the defocusing cubic Schrödinger equation on three dimensional hyperbolic space ℍ 3 \mathbb{H}^{3} with radial initial data in the Sobolev Space H s ​ ( 0 < s < 1 ) H^{s}(0<s<1) . Our main result is that the initial value problem is globally wellposed and scatters for 15 16 < s < 1 \frac{15}{16}<s<1 . This is an extension of the high-low method of Bourgain and the work of Staffilani and Yu to the three dimensional hyperbolic space.

2.3.
Heat-Flow Based Frequency Projection

As in
[
LLOS18
]
, heat flow based freqency projection operators are defined by

\displaystyle P_{\geq s}f=e^{s\Delta}f

\displaystyle P_{<s}f=f-P_{\geq s}f

\displaystyle P_{s}f=(-s\Delta)e^{s\Delta}f

These operators serve as a substitute of the Littlewood Paley frequency projections in the Euclidean space. Operators of the form
\chi(-\Delta)
, where
\chi
is a compactly supported bump function, might seem like a more straightforward choice. But unfortunately, the latter are not
L^{p}
bounded in general.
P_{\geq s}
can be viewed roughly as frequency localization to frequency
\leq s^{-\frac{1}{2}}
, whereas
P_{s}
is the frequency localization to frequency
\sim s^{-\frac{1}{2}}
, in the sense that they satisfy the following Bernstein inequalities:

Proposition 2.7

(Bernstein Inequality)
.

\displaystyle\|P_{<s}f\|_{L^{p}_{x}}\lesssim s^{\frac{1}{2}}\|\nabla f\|_{L^{p}_{x}}

\displaystyle\|\nabla P_{\geq s}f\|_{L^{p}_{x}}\lesssim s^{-\frac{1}{2}}\|f\|_{L^{p}_{x}}

\displaystyle\|\nabla P_{s}f\|_{L^{p}_{x}}\sim s^{-\frac{1}{2}}\|f\|_{L^{p}_{x}}
