2 P P -wave charmed baryons from QCD sum rules
Hui-Min Yang 1, Hua-Xing Chen 2

Abstract
We conduct an investigation on the 1 ​ P 1P - and 2 ​ P 2P -wave charmed baryons using the methods of QCD sum rules and light-cone sum rules within the framework of heavy quark effective theory. Our results suggest that the Λ c ​ ( 2910 ) + \Lambda_{c}(2910)^{+} , Λ c ​ ( 2940 ) + \Lambda_{c}(2940)^{+} , and Ξ c ​ ( 3123 ) + \Xi_{c}(3123)^{+} can be well interpreted as the 2 ​ P 2P -wave charmed baryons of J P = 1 / 2 − J^{P}=1/2^{-} and 3 / 2 − 3/2^{-} , belonging to the S ​ U ​ ( 3 ) SU(3) flavor 𝟑 ¯ F \mathbf{\bar{3}}_{F} representation. Moreover, the Ξ c ​ ( 3123 ) + \Xi_{c}(3123)^{+} possesses a partner state characterized by J P = 1 / 2 − J^{P}=1/2^{-} , denoted as Ξ c ​ ( 1 / 2 − , 2 ​ P ) \Xi_{c}(1/2^{-},2P) . Our analysis predicts its mass and width to be m Ξ c ​ ( 1 / 2 − , 2 ​ P ) − m Ξ c ​ ( 3123 ) + = − 18 ± 7 m_{\Xi_{c}(1/2^{-},2P)}-m_{\Xi_{c}(3123)^{+}}=-18\pm{7} MeV and Γ Ξ c ​ ( 2 ​ P , 1 / 2 − ) = 31 − 27 + 170 \Gamma_{\Xi_{c}(2P,1/2^{-})}=31^{+170}_{-~27} MeV, with m Ξ c ​ ( 3123 ) + = 3122.9 ± 1.3 m_{\Xi_{c}(3123)^{+}}=3122.9\pm{1.3} MeV. We propose to search for it in the Ξ c ​ ( 1 / 2 − , 2 ​ P ) → Σ c ​ K \Xi_{c}(1/2^{-},2P)\to\Sigma_{c}K decay channel.

II
Mass analyses from QCD sum rules

Figure 1:

A charmed baryon consists of a heavy charm quark and two light up/down/strange quarks. Its internal structure encompasses various properties, such as the color, flavor, spin, and orbital degrees of freedom. Notably, the orbital excitation of a
P
-wave charmed baryon can occur between the charmed quark and the light quarks, and it can also occur within the two light quarks. The former is known as the
\lambda
-mode excitation, characterized by
l_{\lambda}=1
and
l_{\rho}=0
, while the latter is termed as the
\rho
-mode excitation with
l_{\lambda}=0
and
l_{\rho}=1
, as depicted in Fig.
1
. When investigating the singly charmed baryon, it is crucial to consider the properties of the two light up/down/strange quarks:

•

Their color structure is antisymmetric (
\mathbf{\bar{3}}_{C}
).

•

Their flavor structure is either symmetric (
\mathbf{6}_{F}
) or antisymmetric (
\mathbf{\bar{3}}_{F}
).

•

Their spin structure is either symmetric (
s_{l}=1
) or antisymmetric (
s_{l}=0
).

•

Their orbital structure is either symmetric (
\lambda
-mode) or antisymmetric (
\rho
-mode).

Applying the Pauli principle to the two light quarks, we can categorize the
P
-wave charmed baryons into eight multiplets, denoted as
[F({\rm flavor}),j_{l},s_{l},\rho/\lambda]
. In this notation
j_{l}=l_{\lambda}\otimes l_{\rho}\otimes s_{l}
represents the total angular momentum of the light components. Each multiplet encompasses one or two baryons with the total angular momenta given by
j=j_{l}\otimes s_{c}=|j_{l}\pm 1/2|
. Within the framework of QCD sum rules, we can construct the corresponding interpolating currents
J^{\alpha_{1}\cdots\alpha_{j-1/2}}_{j,P,F,j_{l},s_{l},\rho/\lambda}
, which couple to the charmed baryons belonging to the
[F,j_{l},s_{l},\rho/\lambda]
multiplet through:

\displaystyle\langle 0|J^{\alpha_{1}\cdots\alpha_{j-{1\over 2}}}_{j,P,F,j_{l},s_{l},\rho/\lambda}|j,P,F,j_{l},s_{l},\rho/\lambda\rangle=f_{F,j_{l},s_{l},\rho/\lambda}u^{\alpha_{1}\cdots\alpha_{j-{1\over 2}}}\,,

with
f_{F,j_{l},s_{l},\rho/\lambda}
the decay constant.

As shown in Fig.
1
, there are four multiplets belonging to the
SU(3)
flavor
\mathbf{\bar{3}}_{F}
representation. In this work we assume that the
\Lambda_{c}(2910)
,
\Lambda_{c}(2940)
, and
\Xi_{c}(3123)
are the
2P
-wave charmed baryons of
J^{P}=1/2^{-}
and
J^{P}=3/2^{-}
belonging to the
[\mathbf{\bar{3}}_{F},1,1,\rho]
doublet. Accordingly, we use the following currents to study them

:

\displaystyle J_{1/2,-,\mathbf{\bar{3}_{F}},1,1,\rho}

(4)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle i\epsilon_{abc}\big([\mathcal{D}_{t}^{\mu}q^{aT}]\mathcal{C}\gamma_{t}^{\nu}q^{b}-q^{aT}\mathcal{C}\gamma_{t}^{\nu}[\mathcal{D}_{t}^{\mu}q^{b}]\big)\sigma^{\mu\nu}h_{v}^{c}\,,

\displaystyle J_{3/2,-,\mathbf{\bar{3}_{F}},1,1,\rho}^{\alpha}

(5)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle i\epsilon_{abc}\big([\mathcal{D}_{t}^{\mu}q^{aT}]\mathcal{C}\gamma_{t}^{\nu}q^{b}-q^{aT}\mathcal{C}\gamma_{t}^{\nu}[\mathcal{D}_{t}^{\mu}q^{b}]\big)

\displaystyle\times

\displaystyle\big(g_{t}^{\alpha\mu}\gamma_{t}^{\nu}\gamma_{5}-g_{t}^{\alpha\nu}\gamma_{t}^{\mu}\gamma_{5}-{1\over 3}\gamma_{t}^{\alpha}\gamma_{t}^{\mu}\gamma_{t}^{\nu}\gamma_{5}+{1\over 3}\gamma_{t}^{\alpha}\gamma_{t}^{\nu}\gamma_{t}^{\mu}\gamma_{5}\big)h_{v}^{c}\,.

\displaystyle~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~=f_{\Xi_{c}(1/2^{-},1P/2P)}u(x)\,,

\displaystyle\langle 0|J_{3/2,-,\mathbf{\bar{3}_{F}},1,1,\rho}(x)|\Lambda_{c}(3/2^{-},1P/2P)\rangle

\displaystyle~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~=f_{\Lambda_{c}(3/2^{-},1P/2P)}u^{\alpha}(x)\,,

\displaystyle~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~=f_{\Xi_{c}(3/2^{-},1P/2P)}u^{\alpha}(x)\,,

,

,

and

\displaystyle\Pi_{j,P,F,j_{l},s_{l},\rho/\lambda}^{\alpha_{1}\cdots\alpha-{j-1/2},\beta_{1},\cdots\beta_{j-1/2}}(\omega)

(10)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle i\int d^{4}xe^{ikx}\langle 0|T[J_{j,P,F,j_{l},s_{l},\rho/\lambda}^{\alpha_{1}\cdots\alpha-{j-1/2}}(x)\bar{J}_{j,P,F,j_{l},s_{l},\rho/\lambda}^{\beta_{1},\cdots\beta_{j-1/2}}(0)]|0\rangle

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\mathbb{S}[g_{t}^{\alpha_{1}\beta_{1}}\cdots g_{t}^{\alpha_{j-1/2}\beta_{j-1/2}}]{1+v\!\!\!/\penalty\over 2}\Pi_{j,P,F,j_{l},s_{l},\rho/\lambda}(\omega)\,,

where

\mathbb{S}

\alpha_{1}\cdots\alpha_{j-1/2}

We employ the four distinct currents for QCD sum rule analyses:

, and
J_{3/2,-,\mathbf{\bar{3}_{F}},1,0,\lambda}

and

10

\displaystyle\Pi_{1/2,-,\mathbf{\bar{3}_{F}},1,1,\rho}(\omega,\omega^{\prime})

(11)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle{f_{\Lambda_{c}({1\over 2}^{-},1P)}^{2}\over\bar{\Lambda}_{\Lambda_{c}({1\over 2}^{-},1P)}-\omega}+{f_{\Lambda_{c}({1\over 2}^{-},2P)}^{2}\over\bar{\Lambda}_{\Lambda_{c}({1\over 2}^{-},2P)}-\omega^{\prime}}+{\rm~higher~~states}\,.

\displaystyle\Pi_{{1/2},-,\mathbf{\bar{3}_{F}},1,1,\rho}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle f_{\Lambda_{c}({1\over 2}^{-},1P)}^{2}e^{-\bar{\Lambda}_{\Lambda_{c}({1\over 2}^{-},1P)}/T}

(12)

\displaystyle+

\displaystyle f_{\Lambda_{c}({1\over 2}^{-},2P)}^{2}e^{-\bar{\Lambda}_{\Lambda_{c}({1\over 2}^{-},2P)}/T}\,.

4
) into Eq. (
10

\displaystyle\Pi_{{1/2},-,\mathbf{\bar{3}_{F}},1,1,\rho}(\omega_{c}^{\prime},T)

(13)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\omega_{c}^{\prime}}e^{-\omega/T}({3\omega^{7}\over 35\pi^{4}}-{\langle g_{s}^{2}GG\rangle\omega^{3}\over 48\pi^{4}})d\omega

3

;

. For the
\Lambda_{c}(2910)
and

\Lambda_{c}(2P,{1\over 2}^{-})
is about
20
MeV smaller than the measurement of
\Lambda_{c}(2910)^{+}

final state would result in a greater width. Similarly, the

can also decay into
D^{0}p
through a

\Lambda_{c}(2940)
within the margin of error. The
\Xi_{c}(3123)^{+}
could be interpreted as the
\Lambda_{c}(2940)^{+}

\Xi_{c}({1\over 2}^{-},2P)\to\Sigma_{c}K

[\mathbf{\bar{3}}_{F},1,1,\rho]
doublet, we also investigate
1P
and
2P
-wave charmed baryons belonging to the
[\mathbf{\bar{3}}_{F},1,0,\lambda]

and
2P
-wave charmed baryons belonging to the

S

\Lambda_{c}(2595)
,
\Lambda_{c}(2625)
,
\Lambda_{c}(2910)
,
\Lambda_{c}(2940)
,
\Xi_{c}(2790)

\Xi_{c}(3123)

\rho

[\mathbf{\bar{3}}_{F},1,1,\rho]
is anticipated to be higher in mass than the
\lambda
-mode multiplet
[\mathbf{\bar{3}}_{F},1,0,\lambda]

\Lambda_{c}(2595)
,
\Lambda_{c}(2635)
,
\Xi_{c}(2790)

\Xi_{c}(2815)

[\mathbf{\bar{3}}_{F},1,1,\rho]

\rho

\Omega_{c}

\lambda

\Omega_{c}

2S
