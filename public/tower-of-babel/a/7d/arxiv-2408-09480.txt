A formula for Fourier coefficients of certain eta-quotients and their expansions as Eisenstein series
Xiao-Jie Zhu

Abstract
. We give a list of 113 113 holomorphic eta-quotients of integral weight ( 66 66 of which are primitive) and provide a uniform closed formula for their Fourier coefficients c ⁡ ( l ) c(l) where l ≡ 1 mod m l\equiv 1\bmod{m} with some fixed m | 24 m\mid 24 . The proof involves Wohlfahrt’s extension of Hecke operators and a dimension formula for spaces of modular forms of general multiplier system. We further provide the expansions of these eta-quotients as linear combinations of standard Eisenstein series.
