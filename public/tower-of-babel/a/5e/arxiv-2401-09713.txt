New type of solutions for the critical Lane-Emden system
Wenjing Chen, Xiaomeng Huang

Abstract
In this paper, we consider the critical Lane-Emden system { − Δ u = K 1 ( y ) v p , y ∈ ℝ N , − Δ v = K 2 ( y ) u q , y ∈ ℝ N , u , v > 0 , \displaystyle\begin{cases}-\Delta u=K_{1}(y)v^{p},\quad y\in\mathbb{R}^{N},&\\ -\Delta v=K_{2}(y)u^{q},\quad y\in\mathbb{R}^{N},&\\ u,v>0,\end{cases} where N ≥ 5 N\geq 5 , p , q ∈ ( 1 , ∞ ) p,q\in(1,\infty) with 1 p + 1 + 1 q + 1 = N − 2 N \frac{1}{p+1}+\frac{1}{q+1}=\frac{N-2}{N} , K 1 ​ ( y ) K_{1}(y) and K 2 ​ ( y ) K_{2}(y) are positive radial potentials. Under suitable conditions on K 1 ​ ( y ) K_{1}(y) and K 2 ​ ( y ) K_{2}(y) , we construct a new family of solutions to this system, which are centred at points lying on the top and the bottom circles of a cylinder. Keywords: critical Lane-Emden systems; Lyapunov-Schmidt reduction; infinitely many solutions. 2020 Mathematics Subject Classification: 35B33, 35B40, 35J47.

Introduction and statement of main result

In this work, we consider the following critical Lane-Emden system

\displaystyle\begin{cases}-\Delta u=K_{1}(y)v^{p},\quad y\in\mathbb{R}^{N},&\\
-\Delta v=K_{2}(y)u^{q},\quad y\in\mathbb{R}^{N},&\\
u,v>0,\end{cases}

(1.1)

where
N\geq 5
,
K_{1}(y)
and
K_{2}(y)
are positive radial potentials,
p,q\in(1,\infty)
and

is a pair of positive numbers lying on the critical hyperbola

\frac{1}{p+1}+\frac{1}{q+1}=\frac{N-2}{N}.

(1.2)

In this paper, without loss of generality, we may assume that
p\leq\frac{N+2}{N-2}\leq q
.

When
u=v
and
K_{1}(y)=K_{2}(y)=K(y)
, system (
1.1

\displaystyle-\Delta u=K(y)u^{\frac{N+2}{N-2}},\ u>0,\ y\in\mathbb{R}^{N},\ u\in D^{1,2}(\mathbb{R}^{N}),

(1.3)

which can be transformed by the prescribed scalar curvature problem on
\mathbb{S}^{N}
through the stereo-graphic projection. There are many works revelent to (
1.3
), see
[
4
,
10
,
22
,
24
,
31
]
and reference therein.
Wei and Yan
[
30
]
showed that if
K(y)
is radially symmetric and
K(|y|)
has a local maximum at
r_{0}>0
, then (
1.3
) has infinitely many non-radial solutions with large number of bubbles lying near the sphere
|y|=r_{0}
and the distance between different bubbles can be made arbitrary small.
Subsequently, Guo et al.
[
17
]
proved a non-degeneracy result for the positive multi-bubbling solutions constructed in
[
30
]
and used this non-degeneracy result to glue
n
-bubbles, whose centers lie in the circle
|y|=r_{0}
in the
(y_{3},y_{4})
-plane to the
k
-bubbling solution described in
[
30
]
.
Under more general assumptions on
K(y)
, i.e.,
K(y)=K(|y^{\prime}|,y^{\prime\prime})
, where
y=(|y^{\prime}|,y^{\prime\prime})\in\mathbb{R}^{2}\times\mathbb{R}^{N-2}
, by combining a finite reduction argument and local Pohozaev type of identities, Peng, Wang and Wei
[
25
]
proved problem (

Recently, Duan, Musso and Wei
[
11
]
constructed a family of solutions to problem (
1.3
) and the solutions are centred at points lying on the top and the bottom circles of a cylinder.

Consider the standard Lane-Emden system

\displaystyle\begin{cases}-\Delta u=v^{p},\quad\text{ in }\Omega,&\\
-\Delta v=u^{q},\quad\text{ in }\Omega,&\\
u=v=0,\quad\text{ on }\partial\Omega,&\end{cases}

For

Later, Alvino et al.

]
, Wang
[

Moreover, Frank, Kim and Pistoia
[
12

deduced that all least energy solutions to (
1.4
) satisfying (
1.2
) are non-degenerate. For more result about the positive or sign-changing solutions to (
1.4

\mathbb{R}^{N}
, we can refer the

[

6

If

Kim and Moon

examined asymptotic behavior of solutions of (

in the subcritical region is close to the critical hyperbola.
Based the non-degeneracy result obtained in

Subsequently, Guo, Hu and Peng

Recently, Jin and Kim

Guo, Liu and Peng
[

considered the supercritical problem for the Lane-Emden system (

In contrast to the Dirichlet problem (

[

obtained the existence of least energy nodal solutions in the subcritical regime. For critical case, Pistoia, Schiera and Tavares

[

studied the Lane-Emden system with Neumann conditions, and investigated the existence of multiple concentrated solutions in the unit ball for

q\geq\frac{N+2}{N-2}+\frac{2(N-2-m)}{m(N-1)(N-2)}-\frac{\varepsilon_{0}}{N-2}
. Consequently, Using the fact that

\displaystyle m\in\begin{cases}[2,N-2),\ &\text{ if }N=5,6,\\
(\frac{(N-2)^{2}}{2N-3},N-2),\ &\text{ if }N\geq 7,\end{cases}

we get

\displaystyle\frac{\partial}{\partial h}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}U_{\overline{x}_{1},\Lambda}^{q}U_{\overline{x}_{j},\Lambda}=

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}U_{0,1}^{q}(z)\frac{b_{N,p}(N-2)}{\Lambda^{N-2}|\overline{x}_{1}-\overline{x}_{j}|^{N-2}}\frac{h}{1-h^{2}}+O\left(\frac{1}{k^{\frac{m(N-2)}{N-2-m}+\frac{N-3}{N-1}+\theta}}\right)

\displaystyle+O\left(k\left(\frac{k}{r}\right)^{N-\varepsilon_{0}}\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}U_{0,1}^{q}(z)\frac{b_{N,p}(N-2)}{\Lambda^{N-2}|\overline{x}_{1}-\overline{x}_{j}|^{N-2}}\frac{h}{1-h^{2}}+O\left(\frac{1}{k^{\frac{m(N-2)}{N-2-m}+\frac{N-3}{N-1}+\theta}}\right).

In the same way,

\displaystyle\frac{\partial}{\partial h}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}U_{\overline{x}_{1},\Lambda}^{q}U_{\underline{x}_{j},\Lambda}=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}U_{0,1}^{q}(z)\frac{b_{N,p}(N-2)}{\Lambda^{N-2}|\overline{x}_{1}-\underline{x}_{j}|^{N-2}}\frac{h}{1-h^{2}}+O\left(\frac{1}{k^{\frac{m(N-2)}{N-2-m}+\frac{N-3}{N-1}+\theta}}\right).

Hence, it follows from
[
11
, Lemma A.1]
that

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}}\frac{\partial}{\partial h}\Bigg\{U_{\overline{x}_{1},\Lambda}^{q}\left(\sum_{j=2}^{k}U_{\overline{x}_{j},\Lambda}+\sum_{j=1}^{k}U_{\underline{x}_{j},\Lambda}\right)\Bigg\}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\frac{k}{\Lambda^{N-2}}\left[\frac{(N-2)B_{4}k^{N-2}h}{r^{N-2}(\sqrt{1-h^{2}})^{N}}-\frac{(N-3)B_{5}k}{r^{N-2}h^{N-2}\sqrt{1-h^{2}}}\right]+O\left(\frac{1}{k^{\frac{m(N-2)}{N-2-m}+\frac{N-3}{N-1}+\theta}}\right).

Then by (
4.16
) and above equation, we conclude the proof.
∎
