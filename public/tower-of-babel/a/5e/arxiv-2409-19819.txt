Anticyclotomic Euler system over biquadratic fields
Kim Tuan Do

Abstract
. We construct a new Euler system (anticyclotomic, in the sense of Jetchev–Nekovář–Skinner) for the Galois representation V f , χ V_{f,\chi} attached to a newform f f of weight k ≥ 2 k\geq 2 twisted by an anticyclotomic Hecke character χ \chi defined over an imaginary biquadratic field K 0 K_{0} . We then show some arithmetic applications of the constructed Euler system, including results on the Bloch–Kato conjecture and a divisibility towards the Iwasawa–Greenberg main conjecture for V f , χ V_{f,\chi} .

In the same vein as
