A Rademacher-type exact formula for partitions without sequences
Walter Bridges, Kathrin Bringmann

Abstract
. In this paper we prove an exact formula for the number of partitions without sequences. By work of Andrews, the corresponding generating function is a product of a modular form and a mock theta function, giving an overall weight of 0. The proof requires evaluating and bounding Kloosterman sums and the Circle Method.

11B57, 11F03, 11F20, 11F30, 11F37, 11P82

1.
Introduction and statement of results

A
partition
of a non-negative integer
n
is a decomposition into the sum of non-increasing non-negative integers. Denote the number of partitions of
n
by
p(n)
. The corresponding generating function is

(1.1)

P(q):=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}p(n)q^{n}=\prod_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{1-q^{n}}=\frac{1}{(q;q)_{\infty}},

where, for
a\in\mathbb{C}
and
n\in\mathbb{N}_{0}\cup\{\infty\}
, we define
(a)_{n}=(a;q)_{n}:=\prod_{j=0}^{n-1}(1-aq^{j})
.

An important question in the theory of partitions is to determine exact formulas or asymptotics for functions such as
p(n)
. Note that the generating function in (
1.1
) is (essentially) a modular form. A Tauberian Theorem
[
11
]
shows that the following asymptotic holds

(1.2)

p(n)\sim\frac{1}{4n\sqrt{3}}e^{\pi\sqrt{\frac{2n}{3}}}\qquad(n\to\infty).

Building on Hardy and Ramanujan’s earlier developments, Rademacher and Zuckerman later proved exact asymptotic series expansions for functions like
p(n)
using the Circle Method
[
15
]
. To state this, define the
Kloostermann sums

A_{k}(n):=\sum_{h\ \,(\mathrm{mod}\,k)^{*}}\omega_{h,k}e^{-\frac{2\pi inh}{k}}.

The

Another important example in the study of coefficients of hypergeometric series and automorphic forms is Ramanujan’s third order mock theta function

(1.4)

f(q)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\alpha(n)q^{n}:=1+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{q^{n^{2}}}{(-q;q)_{n}^{2}}.

The mock theta functions were introduced by Ramanujan in his last letter to Hardy
[

is “the holomorphic part of a harmonic Maass form”. This in particular implies that

obtained by Dragonette

and Andrews

does not influence the shape of the exact formula.

The situation drastically changes if one multiplies mock theta functions with modular forms. These functions arise in many settings, for example in the study of probability, mathematical physics, and partition theory. One particular example gives partitions that do not contain any consecutive integers as parts. Such partitions were first explored by MacMahon
[

with the third order mock theta function

\chi(q):=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{(-q;q)_{n}q^{n^{2}}}{\left(-q^{3};q^{3}\right)_{n}}.

The combinatorics of the generating function was further explored in

\frac{1}{n}e^{\frac{2\pi}{3}\sqrt{n}}\ll p_{2}(n)\ll\frac{1}{\sqrt{n}}e^{\frac{2\pi}{3}\sqrt{n}}.

2.3.
Mordell integrals

Throughout, we let
0\leq h<k\leq N
with
\gcd(h,k)=1
, and
z=k(N^{-2}-i\Phi)
with
-\vartheta_{h,k}^{\prime}\leq\Phi\leq\vartheta_{h,k}^{\prime\prime}
. Here

\vartheta_{h,k}^{\prime}:=\frac{1}{k(k_{1}+k)},\qquad\vartheta_{h,k}^{\prime\prime}:=\frac{1}{k(k_{2}+k)},

where
\frac{h_{1}}{k_{1}}<\frac{h}{k}<\frac{h_{2}}{k_{2}}
are adjacent Farey fractions in the Farey sequence of order
N\in\mathbb{N}
. Below, we let
N\to\infty

The following Mordell-type integral occurs in the modular transformation laws of the mock theta function

I_{k,\nu}(z):=\int_{\mathbb{R}}\frac{e^{-\frac{3\pi zx^{2}}{k}}}{\cosh\left(\frac{\pi i}{k}\left(\nu-\frac{1}{6}\right)-\frac{\pi zx}{k}\right)}dx.

In Lemma 3.1 of
[
6
]
these integrals were approximated. To state this result, let
\mathcal{J}_{b,k,\nu}(z):=ze^{\frac{\pi b}{kz}}I_{k,\nu}(z)
, and define the principal part truncation of

as

\mathcal{J}_{b,k,\nu}^{*}(z):=\sqrt{\frac{b}{3}}\int_{-1}^{1}\frac{e^{\frac{\pi b}{kz}\left(1-x^{2}\right)}}{\cosh\left(\frac{\pi i}{k}\left(\nu-\frac{1}{6}\right)-\frac{\pi}{k}\sqrt{\frac{b}{3}}x\right)}dx.

Lemma 2.1
.

If
b\in\mathbb{R}
and
\nu\in\mathbb{Z}
with
0<\nu\leq k
, then we have the following, as
z\to 0
:

(1)

If
b\leq 0
, then we have

|\mathcal{J}_{b,k,\nu}(z)|\ll\frac{1}{\left|\frac{\pi}{2}-\frac{\pi}{k}\left(\nu-\frac{1}{6}\right)\right|}.

(2)

If

\mathcal{J}_{b,k,\nu}(z)=\mathcal{J}_{b,k,\nu}^{*}(z)+\mathcal{E}_{b,k,\nu}(z)

Lemma 3.2
.

We have

\frac{\omega_{h,k}\omega_{h,\frac{k}{2}}\omega_{h,\frac{k}{3}}}{\omega_{h,\frac{k}{6}}}=-e^{\pi i\frac{5k+18}{36}h}.

Proof.

We use (
2.1
). Since
2\mid k
, we have that
h
is odd. A direct calculation gives that

\frac{\omega_{h,k}\omega_{h,\frac{k}{2}}\omega_{h,\frac{k}{3}}}{\omega_{h,\frac{k}{6}}}=-e^{2\pi iA},

A:=-\frac{5h^{2}h^{\prime}k}{72}+\frac{5k+18}{72}h+\frac{5h^{\prime}k}{72}=\frac{5\frac{k}{6}\left(1-h^{2}\right)h^{\prime}}{12}+\frac{5k+18}{72}h.

Now
\gcd(h,6)=1
. Thus
\frac{1-h^{2}}{12}\in\mathbb{Z}
. This gives the claim.
∎

Lemma 3.3

\displaystyle K_{k}^{[1]}(n,m),\ \mathbb{K}_{k,\ell}^{[1]}(n,m),\ K_{k}^{[2]}(\nu;n,m),\ \mathbb{K}_{k,\ell}^{[2]}(\nu;n,m)\ll_{\varepsilon}n^{\frac{1}{3}}k^{\frac{2}{3}+\varepsilon}.
