High Permittivity Dielectric Field-Plated Vertical (001) β \beta -Ga 2 O 3 Schottky Barrier Diode with Surface Breakdown Electric Field of 5.45 MV/cm and BFOM of > > 1 GW/cm 2
Saurav Roy, Arkka Bhattacharyya, Praneeth Ranga, Heather Splawn, Jacob Leach, and Sriram Krishnamoorthy

Abstract
This paper presents vertical (001) oriented β \beta -Ga 2 O 3 field plated (FP) schottky barrier diode (SBD) with a novel extreme permittivity dielectric field oxide. A thin drift layer of 1.7 μ ​ m \mu m was used to enable a punch-through (PT) field profile and very low differential specific on- resistance (R on-sp ) of 0.32 m Ω \Omega -cm 2 . The extreme permittivity field plate oxide facilitated the lateral spread of the electric field profile beyond the field plate edge and enabled a breakdown voltage ( V b ​ r V_{br} ) of 687 V. The edge termination efficiency increases from 13.5 % \% for non-field plated structure to 63 % \% for high permittivity field plate structure. The surface breakdown electric field was extracted to be of 5.45 MV/cm at the center of the anode region using TCAD simulations. The high permittivity field plated SBD demonstrated a record high Baliga’s figure of merit (BFOM) of 1.47 GW/cm 2 showing the potential of Ga 2 O 3 power devices for multi-kilovolt class applications.
