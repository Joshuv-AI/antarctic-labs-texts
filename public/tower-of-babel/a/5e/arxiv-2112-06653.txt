Ramanujan’s type of product of theta functions and its applications
D. J. Prabhakaran, N. Jayakumar, K.Ranjithkumar

Abstract
. In this paper, we initiate a generous amount of new-found general theorems for explicit evaluations of product of the theta functions b m , n b_{m,n} using Kronecker’s limit formula and other various novel explicit evaluations that were introduced thereupon. Also, we have obtained a few novel values of Ramanujan’s class invariant g n g_{n} using new-found values of b m , n b_{m,n} . Eventually, we have shown that b m , n b_{m,n} is the unit in certain algebraic number fields.
