Abstract
In the paper, we define the q q -Fibonacci bicomplex quaternions and the q q -Lucas bicomplex quaternions, respectively. Then, we give some algebraic properties of q q -Fibonacci bicomplex quaternions and the q q -Lucas bicomplex quaternions.

Theorem 2.1

(Binet’s Formula)
. Let
{\mathbb{BF}_{n}(\alpha;q)}
and
{\mathbb{BL}_{n}(\alpha;q)}
be the
q
-Fibonacci bicomplex quaternion and the
q
-Lucas bicomplex quaternion. For
n\geq 1
, Binet’s formula for these quaternions respectively, is as follows:

\mathbb{BF}_{n}(\alpha;q)=\frac{\alpha^{n}\,\widehat{\gamma}-(\alpha\,q)^{n}\,\widehat{\delta}}{\alpha-\alpha\,q},

(25)

and

\mathbb{BL}_{n}(\alpha;q)={\alpha^{n}\,\widehat{\gamma}+(\alpha\,q)^{n}\,\widehat{\delta}}

(26)

where

\begin{array}[]{l}\widehat{\gamma}=1+{\alpha}\,i+{\alpha}^{2}\,j+{\alpha}^{3}\,i\,j,\,\,\,\,\,\alpha=\frac{1+\sqrt{5}}{2}\end{array}

and

\begin{array}[]{l}\widehat{\delta}=1+(\alpha\,q)\,i+(\alpha\,q)^{2}\,j+(\alpha\,q)^{3}\,i\,j,\,\,\,\,\,\alpha\,q=\frac{-1}{\alpha}.\end{array}

Proof 1

(
25
): Using (
8
) and (
12
), we find that

\begin{array}[]{rl}\mathbb{BF}_{n}(\alpha;q)=&\alpha^{n-1}[{n}]_{q}+\alpha^{n}[{n+1}]_{q}\,i+\alpha^{n+1}[{n+2}]_{q}\,j+\alpha^{n+2}[{n+3}]_{q}\,i\,j\\
\\
=&\alpha^{n}\,\frac{1-q^{n}}{\alpha-\alpha\,q}+\alpha^{n+1}\,\frac{1-q^{n+1}}{\alpha-\alpha\,q}\,i+\alpha^{n+2}\,\frac{1-q^{n+2}}{\alpha-\alpha\,q}\,j+\alpha^{n+3}\,\frac{1-q^{n+3}}{\alpha-\alpha\,q}\,i\,j\\
\\
=&\frac{\alpha^{n}\,[\,1+\alpha\,i+\alpha^{2}\,j+\alpha^{3}\,i\,j\,]-(\alpha\,q)^{n}\,[\,1+(\alpha\,q)\,i+(\alpha\,q)^{2}\,j+(\alpha\,q)^{3}\,i\,j\,]}{\alpha-(\alpha\,q)}\\
\\
=&\frac{\alpha^{n}\,\widehat{\gamma}-(\alpha\,q)^{n}\,\widehat{\delta}}{\alpha-\alpha\,q}\end{array}

In a similar way, equality (
26
) can be derived as follows

\begin{array}[]{rl}\mathbb{BL}_{n}(\alpha;q)=&\alpha^{n}\frac{{[{2\,n}]}_{q}}{{[{n}]}_{q}}+\alpha^{n+1}\,\frac{{[{2\,n+2}]}_{q}}{{[{n+1}]}_{q}}\,i+\alpha^{n+2}\,\frac{{[{2\,n+4}]}_{q}}{{[{n+2}]}_{q}}\,j+\alpha^{n+3}\frac{{[{2\,n+6}]}_{q}}{{[{n+3}]}_{q}}\,i\,j\\
=&\alpha^{2n}\,(\frac{1-q^{2n}}{\alpha^{n}-(\alpha\,q)^{n}})+\alpha^{2n+2}\,(\frac{1-q^{2n+2}}{\alpha^{n+1}-(\alpha\,q)^{n+1}})\,i\\
\\
&+\alpha^{2n+4}\,(\frac{1-q^{2n+4}}{\alpha^{n+2}-(\alpha\,q)^{n+2}})\,j+\alpha^{2n+6}\,(\frac{1-q^{2n+6}}{\alpha^{n+3}-(\alpha\,q)^{n+3}})\,i\,j\\
=&\frac{\alpha^{2n}}{\alpha^{n}}\,(1+\alpha\,i+\alpha^{2}\,j+\alpha^{3}\,i\,j\,)\\
\\
&-\frac{(\alpha\,q)^{2n}}{(\alpha\,q)^{n}}\,(1+(\alpha\,q)\,i+(\alpha\,q)^{2}\,j+(\alpha\,q)^{3}\,i\,j\,)\\
\\
=&\alpha^{n}\,\widehat{\gamma}-(\alpha\,q)^{n}\,\widehat{\delta}.\end{array}

where
\widehat{\gamma}=1+{\alpha}\,i+{\alpha}^{2}\,j+{\alpha}^{3}\,i\,j
,
\widehat{\delta}=1+(\alpha\,q)\,i+(\alpha\,q)^{2}\,j+(\alpha\,q)^{3}\,i\,j
and
\widehat{\gamma}\,\widehat{\delta}=\widehat{\delta}\,\widehat{\gamma}
.

Theorem 2.2

(Exponential generating function)

Let
\mathbb{BF}_{n}(\alpha;q)
be the
q
-Fibonacci bicomplex quaternion. For the exponential generating function for these quaternions is as follows:

\displaystyle g_{\mathbb{BF}_{n}(\alpha;q)}\,(\frac{t^{n}}{n!})=

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{n=0}^{\infty}\,{\mathbb{BF}_{n}(\alpha;q)}\,\frac{t^{n}}{n!}=\frac{\widehat{\gamma}\,e^{\alpha\,t}\,-\widehat{\delta}\,e^{(\alpha\,q)\,t}}{\alpha-\alpha\,q}\,

(27)

Proof 2

Using the definition of exponential generating function, we obtain

\begin{array}[]{rl}\sum\limits_{n=0}^{\infty}\,{\mathbb{BF}_{n}(\alpha;q)}\,\frac{t^{n}}{n!}&=\sum\limits_{n=0}^{\infty}\,(\frac{\alpha^{n}\,\widehat{\gamma}-(\alpha\,q)^{n}\,\widehat{\delta}}{\alpha-\alpha\,q})\,\frac{t^{n}}{n!}\\
&=\frac{\widehat{\gamma}}{\alpha-\alpha\,q}\,\sum\limits_{n=0}^{\infty}\,\frac{t^{n}}{n!}-\frac{\widehat{\delta}}{\alpha-\alpha\,q}\,\sum\limits_{n=0}^{\infty}\,\frac{(\alpha\,q\,t)^{n}}{n!}\\
&=\frac{\widehat{\gamma}\,e^{\alpha\,t}\,-\widehat{\delta}\,e^{(\alpha\,q)\,t}}{\alpha-\alpha\,q}.\end{array}

(28)

Thus, the proof is completed.

Proof 3

(

(Cassini Identity)
