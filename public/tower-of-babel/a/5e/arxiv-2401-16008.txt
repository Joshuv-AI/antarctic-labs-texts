LAPTH-007/24 Farewell to Cotton gravity
Gérard Clément, Khireddine Nouicer

Abstract
We reformulate our proof of the under-determination of Cotton gravity in terms of the Codazzi parametrization.

First, let us correct several erroneous statements on our paper

The field equations of Cotton gravity

C_{\mu\nu\rho}=8\pi GM_{\mu\nu\rho},

(1)

where

C_{\mu\nu\rho}\equiv D_{\mu}R_{\nu\rho}-D_{\nu}R_{\mu\rho}-\displaystyle\frac{1}{6}\left(g_{\nu\rho}D_{\mu}R-g_{\mu\rho}D_{\nu}R\right)

M_{\mu\nu\rho}\equiv D_{\mu}T_{\nu\rho}-D_{\nu}T_{\mu\rho}-\displaystyle\frac{1}{3}\left(g_{\nu\rho}D_{\mu}T-g_{\mu\rho}D_{\nu}T\right).

(3)

Any solution of the Einstein equations with cosmological constant solves the equations of Cotton gravity. On the other hand, the space of solutions of Cotton gravity is clearly larger than in the case of general relativity. In brief, our critique of Cotton gravity in
[

g^{\nu\rho}C_{\mu\nu\rho}\equiv 0

1

Now for the Codazzi approach. Define a symmetric 2-tensor
C_{\mu\nu}
of trace
C
by the generalized Einstein equation

R_{\mu\nu}-\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}R\,g_{\mu\nu}\equiv 8\pi G\left[T_{\mu\nu}+C_{\mu\nu}-Cg_{\mu\nu}\right],

(5)

or equivalently

C_{\mu\nu}\equiv R_{\mu\nu}-\displaystyle\frac{1}{6}R\,g_{\mu\nu}-8\pi G\left[T_{\mu\nu}-\displaystyle\frac{1}{3}T\,g_{\mu\nu}\right].

(6)

If the Cotton gravity equations (
1
) are satisfied, the tensor
C_{\mu\nu}
defined by (
6
) fulfils the condition:

E_{\mu\nu\rho}=0,

(7)

where the 3-tensor
E_{\mu\nu\rho}
is defined by

E_{\mu\nu\rho}\equiv D_{\mu}C_{\nu\rho}-D_{\nu}C_{\mu\rho}.

(8)

The condition (
7
) on the tensor defined by (
8

6

8

E_{\mu\nu\rho}\equiv C_{\mu\nu\rho}-8\pi GM_{\mu\nu\rho}.

(9)

Thus the Codazzi condition (
7
) is equivalent to the fundamental equation (
1
) of Cotton gravity. For the sake of clarity we shall refer to the original formulation of Cotton gravity by equations (
2

1

5

7

Recalling that the components of the Cotton tensor
C_{\mu\nu\rho}
are related by the algebraic identity (
4
)
and that the matter tensor
M_{\mu\nu\rho}
must obey the similar condition

g^{\nu\rho}M_{\mu\nu\rho}=0,

(10)

equivalent to the conservation law of the energy-momentum tensor, it follows that the components of the tensor
E_{\mu\nu\rho}
are related by

g^{\nu\rho}E_{\mu\nu\rho}=0,

(11)

independently of the Codazzi condition (
7
). This constraint lowers the number of independent Codazzi equations, which for highly symmetric configurations becomes smaller than the number of independent metric functions, so that these cannot be determined completely.

Consider the example of static spherically symmetric configurations, which may be parameterized by

ds^{2}=-e^{2\nu(r)}\,dt^{2}+e^{2\lambda(r)}\,dr^{2}+r^{2}(dr^{2}+\sin^{2}\theta\,d\varphi^{2}).

(12)

Owing to the spherical symmetry of both the metric and the matter source, the non-vanishing components of the tensor
C_{\mu\nu}
defined by (
6

C_{\varphi\varphi}=\sin^{2}\theta\,C_{\theta\theta}
. Clearly the radial component
C_{rr}
do not contribute to the Codazzi condition (
7

E_{trt}=0
and
E_{\theta r\theta}=0

11

g^{tt}E_{trt}+2\,g^{\theta\theta}E_{\theta r\theta}=0.

(13)

It follows that there is only one Codazzi equation for two unknown metric functions of
r

r
