Dynamics of nonlinear hyperbolic equations of Kirchhoff type

Abstract

Structure of Nehari manifold
\textit{{N}}_{3}

It is well known that the stationary solution for (

which is defined by (

). Due to (

) and (

), we can see that all critical points of

must lie on the Nehari manifold

For

\textit{{N}}_{p}
connects strongly with the behavior of fiber mapping

in the form of

\displaystyle K_{u}(\tau)=J(\tau u)=\frac{a\tau^{4}}{4}\|\nabla u\|_{2}^{4}+\frac{b\tau^{2}}{2}\|\nabla u\|_{2}^{2}-\frac{\lambda\tau^{2}}{2}\|u\|_{2}^{2}-\frac{\tau^{p+1}}{p+1}\|u\|_{p+1}^{p+1},\quad\forall~\tau>0,

for every

and

. Such a map was studied by Brown and Zhang
[

for the case

, Ikehata and Okazawa
[

]
for the case

,
p=3
,
\lambda=0
.

We have

\displaystyle K_{u}^{\prime}(\tau)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle a\tau^{3}\|\nabla u\|_{2}^{4}+b\tau\|\nabla u\|_{2}^{2}-\lambda\tau\|u\|_{2}^{2}-\tau^{p}\|u\|_{p+1}^{p+1},

(3.2)

and

\displaystyle K_{u}^{\prime\prime}(\tau)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 3a\tau^{2}\|\nabla u\|_{2}^{4}+b\|\nabla u\|_{2}^{2}-\lambda\|u\|_{2}^{2}-p\tau^{p-1}\|u\|_{p+1}^{p+1}.

(3.3)

It is easy to infer that

from (

) and (

). So, for

and

, we have

if and only if
\tau u\in\textit{{N}}_{p}
, which means that elements in

correspond to stationary points of the fiber mapping

if and only if

,

(2) Let
