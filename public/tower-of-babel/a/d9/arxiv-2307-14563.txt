Quantum Phase Transition of Organic Spin Liquid Tuned by Mixing Counterions
K. Ueda, S. Fujiyama, R. Kato

Abstract
We found a plateau in the magnitude of the isolated magnetic moments as a function of the anisotropy of the transfer integral ( t ′ / t \displaystyle t^{\prime}/t ) in the gapless quantum spin liquid (QSL) phase of an S = 1 / 2 \displaystyle S=1/2 triangular lattice molecular solid X [Pd(dmit) 2 ] 2 , accomplished by a fine-tuning of t ′ / t \displaystyle t^{\prime}/t through the mixing of cations, X . In contrast, the magnetic susceptibility at the lowest temperature in the QSL phase parametrized by t ′ / t \displaystyle t^{\prime}/t evinces an unconventional suppression approaching the quantum phase transition, implying significant critical fluctuations.
