Revisiting for maximal flavor violating Z e ​ μ ′ Z^{\prime}_{e\mu} and its phenomenology constraints
Jia Liu, Muyuan Song, Haohao Zhang

Abstract
Lepton flavor violation (LFV), observed conclusively in neutrino oscillations, remains a pivotal area of investigation due to its absence in the Standard Model (SM). Beyond the Standard Model (BSM) physics explores charged lepton flavor violation (CLFV), particularly through new particle candidates such as the Z ′ Z^{\prime} . This article focuses on maximal LFV interactions facilitated by the Z ′ Z^{\prime} boson, specifically targeting its off-diagonal interactions with the first and second generations of charged and neutral leptons. In our ultraviolet (UV) model for the origin of the Z ′ Z^{\prime} , inspired by the work of [R.Foot et al., Phys.Rev. D50 (1994) 4571-4580 ], we utilize the discrete Z 2 Z_{2} symmetry to investigate the maximal LFV mediated by the Z ′ Z^{\prime} between the muon ( μ \mu ) and electron ( e e ) arising from the additional scalars. This symmetry prohibits flavor-conserving interactions between Z ′ Z^{\prime} and μ + ​ μ − , e + ​ e − \mu^{+}\mu^{-},\,e^{+}e^{-} . In conjunction with collider, ( g − 2 ) μ , ( g − 2 ) e (g-2)_{\mu},(g-2)_{e} , inverse μ \mu decay, Muonium-to-Antimuonium conversion and LFV decay constraints, we provide forecasts for anticipated limits derived from processes such as ν μ ​ N → ν e ​ μ + ​ e − ​ N \nu_{\mu}N\to\nu_{e}\mu^{+}e^{-}N in neutrino trident experiments like the DUNE search at the first time. These limits highlight the prospective scope and significance of LFV investigations within these experimental frameworks. Within the mass range of 0.01 GeV to 10 GeV, the most stringent limit arises from ℬ ⁡ ( μ → e + X + γ ) \mathcal{B}(\mu\to e+X+\gamma) when M Z ′ < m μ M_{Z^{\prime}}<m_{\mu} , while Δ ​ a e \Delta a_{e} provides effective constraints as M Z ′ M_{Z^{\prime}} approaches 10 GeV. Looking ahead, the proposed Muonium-to-Antimuonium Conversion Experiment (MACE) is expected to impose the most stringent constraints on Muonium-to-Antimuonium oscillation, improving sensitivity by about one order of magnitude against Δ ​ a e \Delta a_{e} .

3.3
Muon decay limits

The occurrence of LFV decays, characterized as
\ell_{i}\to\ell_{j}+X
, imposes constraints on the underlying interactions. In the context of muon decays, such as
\mu\to e\gamma
,
\mu\to 3e
and
\mu\to e+X
(A summarized overview is provided in
[
68
]
), these processes define significant boundaries for new physics. Notably, the
\mu\to e\gamma
decay has been scrutinized extensively at the MEG experiment
[
69
,
70
,
71
]
, while
\mu\to 3e
has been explored by various experiments
[
72
,
73
,
74
]
. In previous searches, flavor-violating interactions associated with muon decay involving massless Goldstone bosons, also known as familons, have been thoroughly investigated. These studies have addressed not only the phenomenological constraints
[
75
,
76
]
but also the cosmological consequences and implications of such interactions
[
77
,
78
]
. Furthermore, the TWIST collaboration has explored the decay process
\mu\to e+X
involving a non-massless
X

[
79
]
. The absence of kinetic mixing, such as
Z-Z^{\prime}
or
A-Z^{\prime}
interactions, precludes the occurrence of
\mu\to 3e
due to the lack of diagonal coupling vertices between
Z^{\prime}
and charged leptons. Consequently, the constraints on LFV decays primarily focus on two-body processes, where the final decay channel involves
\mu\to eZ^{\prime}
and subsequent
Z^{\prime}
decays exclusively to neutrinos with a branching ratio of 100%. The TWIST experiment utilized muon stopped events to establish an upper limit for
\mathcal{B}(\mu\to e+X)
of approximately
\mathcal{O}(10^{-5})
within the mass region of
X
, ranging from 13 MeV to 80 MeV
[
79
]
. In addition, the PSI experiment with a HPGe detector, testing masses ranging from 100 MeV to 150 MeV, obtained an upper limit of
\mathcal{B}(\mu^{+}\to e^{+}+X)<5.7\times 10^{-4}

[
80
]
. Therefore, we will employ the current limit established by the TWIST collaboration to assess the constraint on the
Z^{\prime}
.

The decay width of
\mu\to e+Z^{\prime}
can be expressed as follows under the approximation of neglecting
m_{e}

\displaystyle\Gamma(\mu\to e+Z^{\prime})=\frac{g_{e\mu}^{2}(m_{\mu}^{2}-M_{Z^{\prime}}^{2})^{2}(m_{\mu}^{2}+2M^{2}_{Z^{\prime}})}{16\pi m^{3}_{\mu}M^{2}_{Z^{\prime}}},

(23)

with its branching ratio
\left(\tau_{\mu}=2.2\times 10^{-6}s\sim\Gamma_{\mu}=3\times 10^{-19}\text{GeV}\right)

[
81
]

\displaystyle\mathcal{B}(\mu\to e+Z^{\prime})=\Gamma(\mu\to e+Z^{\prime})/\Gamma_{\mu},

(24)

However, as reported by Ref.
[
68
]
, the Crystal Box collaboration
[
82
]
has showcased the capability to examine the inclusive
\mu\to e+\gamma+X
decay process with
\mu\to e+\gamma
. They have established an upper bound for the branching ratio
\mathcal{B}(\mu\to e+\gamma+X)<1.3\times 10^{-9}

[
82
]
. The three-body decay,

\displaystyle\Gamma(\mu\to e+\gamma+Z^{\prime})\simeq\frac{\alpha_{\text{EM}}g_{e\mu}^{2}m_{\mu}^{5}}{128\pi^{2}E_{\gamma}^{2}M_{Z^{\prime}}^{2}}.

(25)

where

correspond to minimum energy cut to prevent infrared divergence (typical energy is picked for 38 MeV in
[
82
]
). For
M_{Z^{\prime}}
reduces to

m_{\mu}

Z^{\prime}
approaches
m_{\mu}

\sim 1/M^{2}_{Z^{\prime}}
(arising from the longitudinal polarization mode of

[

84
,

\alpha_{\text{EM}}

5
Conclusions

In this work, we investigated a novel
U(1)^{\prime}
gauge symmetry, focusing on the
Z^{\prime}
boson that couples with
e
and
\mu
through a specific charge configuration. We propose a UV model featuring four scalars—three doublets and one singlet—that contribute to the
M_{Z^{\prime}}
after symmetry breaking, without altering the SM
Z
boson mass. The model employs a discrete symmetry
\ell_{1L}\to\ell_{2L},\,e_{1R}\to e_{2R},Z^{\prime}_{\mu}\to-Z^{\prime}_{\mu}
, leading to maximally off-diagonal interactions between
Z^{\prime}
and leptons, manifesting exclusively as lepton flavor-violating (LFV) processes. A notable aspect indicates that the Yukawa coupling between
\mu/e
and the SM Higgs remains identical. Given recent measurements of the Higgs’ leptonic decay branching ratios, we anticipate that future precision measurements will be sensitive to this model.

Next, we analyzed the existing experiments aimed at constraining the LFV coupling
g_{e\mu}
of
Z^{\prime}
. Specifically, the discrepancy between the SM prediction and the measured magnetic moment of the muon,
\Delta a_{\mu}
, cannot be explained by the
Z^{\prime}
, as the parameter space where
Z^{\prime}
could account for this discrepancy has already been ruled out by other experimental measurements. We considered several LFV search limits, including those from Belle-II, LEP, LFV decays, inverse muon decay, Muonium-anti Muonium oscillation, and the newly proposed neutrino trident process (
\nu_{\mu}+\text{N}\to\nu_{e}+\mu^{+}+e^{-}+\text{N}
).

Neutrino trident processes are measured from the DUNE and some other similar experiments to test the LFV interaction. Most searches have focused on muon pair final states. However, the LFV interaction involve the maximal flavor violation can contribute a distinctive signal
\mu^{+}e^{-}
from
\nu_{\mu}
beams without any SM background as the SM does not produce
\mu^{+}e^{-}
in the final state via
\nu_{\mu}
. We employed the neutrino trident production method to investigate the
Z^{\prime}\mu^{+}e^{-}
vertex, utilizing its framework to incorporate the amplitude of a distinct, background-free signal through the DUNE experiment. In the appendix, we listed the kinematic variables for the 2-to-3 scattering process
\nu_{\mu}+\gamma\to\nu_{e}+\mu^{+}+e^{-}
and calculated the final cross-section for an initial
\nu_{\mu}
collides with the nucleus. The resulting constraint on
Z^{\prime}
is stricter compared to those from colliders and LFV decays when
M_{Z^{\prime}}>m_{\mu}
. This limit forms a quadratic curve with the couplings ranging from
\mathcal{O}(10^{-5})
to
\mathcal{O}(10^{-2})
, though it does not surpass the constraint from
\Delta a_{e}
. For
M_{Z^{\prime}}<m_{\mu}
, the primary constraint comes from
\mathcal{B}(\mu\to e+\gamma+Z^{\prime})
due to the enhancement by the factor
1/M^{2}_{Z^{\prime}}
and the suppression both from the three-body phase space factor and QED coupling
\alpha_{\text{EM}}
. The Mu-
\overline{\rm{Mu}}
oscillation searched at PSI exhibits a transition probability that differs notably from
\Delta a_{e}
. The proposed Muonium-to-Antimuonium Conversion Experiment (MACE), involving Mu-
\overline{\rm{Mu}}
oscillations, is expected to surpass the sensitivity achieved in recent measurements of
\Delta a_{e}
, thereby enhancing validation of scenarios involving LFV
Z^{\prime}
and other LFV (
\mu
-
e
) interactions.
