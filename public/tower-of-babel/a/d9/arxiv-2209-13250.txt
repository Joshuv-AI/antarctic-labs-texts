Lagrangian densities of some 3 3 -uniform hypergraphs
Zilong Yan, Yuejian Peng

Abstract
The Lagrangian density of an r r -uniform hypergraph H H is r ! r! multiplying the supremum of the Lagrangians of all H H -free r r -uniform hypergraphs. For an r r -uniform graph H H with t t vertices, it is clear that π λ ​ ( H ) ≥ r ! ​ λ ​ ( K t − 1 r ) \pi_{\lambda}(H)\geq r!\lambda{(K_{t-1}^{r})} . We say that an r r -uniform hypergraph H H with t t vertices is λ \lambda -perfect if π λ ​ ( H ) = r ! ​ λ ​ ( K t − 1 r ) \pi_{\lambda}(H)=r!\lambda{(K_{t-1}^{r})} . A theorem of Motzkin and Straus implies that all 2 2 -uniform graphs are λ \lambda -perfect. It is interesting to understand what kind of hypergraphs are λ \lambda -perfect. The property ‘ λ \lambda -perfect’ is monotone in the sense that an r r -graph obtained by removing an edge from a λ \lambda -perfect r r -graph (keep the same vertex set) is λ \lambda -perfect. It’s interesting to understand the relation between the number of edges in a hypergraph and the ‘ λ \lambda -perfect’ property. We propose that the number of edges in a hypergraph no more than the number of edges in a linear hyperpath would guarantee the ‘ λ \lambda -perfect’ property. We show some partial result to support this conjecture. We also give some partial result to support the conjecture that the disjoint union of two λ \lambda -perfect r r -uniform hypergraph is λ \lambda -perfect. We show that the disjoint union of a λ \lambda -perfect 3 3 -graph and S 2 , t = { 123,124,125,126 , … , 12 ​ ( t + 2 ) } S_{2,t}=\{123,124,125,126,...,12(t+2)\} is perfect. This result implies the earlier result of Heftz and Keevash, Jiang, Peng and Wu, and several other earlier results. Let Π r = { π ( ℱ ) : ℱ is a family of r − uniform graphs . } \Pi_{r}=\{\pi({\mathcal{F}}):{\mathcal{F}}{\rm\ is\ a\ family\ of\ }r-{\rm uniform\ graphs}.\} , where π ⁡ ( ℱ ) \pi({\mathcal{F}}) is the Turán density of ℱ {\mathcal{F}} . Erdős-Stone-Simonovits showed that Π 2 = { 0 , 1 2 , 2 3 , 3 4 , … } \Pi_{2}=\{0,{1\over 2},{2\over 3},{3\over 4},\ldots\} . However, very few are known for Π r \Pi_{r} when r ≥ 3 r\geq 3 . Sidorenko showed that the Lagrangian density of an r r -uniform graph is the same as the Turán density of its extension. In particular, these two densities of H H equal if every pair of vertices of H H is contained in an edge. Sidorenko gave the Lagrangian densities of infinitely many hypergraphs and this led to first know infinitely many numbers in Π r \Pi_{r} for r ≥ 3 r\geq 3 . Determining the Turán density of K 4 3 − K_{4}^{3-} (a well-known conjecture of Frankl and Füredi) is one of the major open problems in hypergraph Turán problems. The result of Sidorenko implies that the Lagrangian density and the Turán density of K 4 3 − K_{4}^{3-} equal. It would be very interesting to obtain the Turán density of K 4 3 − K_{4}^{3-} by determining the Lagrangian density of K 4 3 − K_{4}^{3-} . As a first attempt, we determine the Lagrangian density of K 4 3 − ⊔ e K_{4}^{3-}\sqcup e , where e e is a single edge. We also find an irrational Lagrangian density. The advantage of transferring to the Lagrangian density is that we can assume that G G is ‘dense’ (so covering pairs) when considering the maximum Lagrangian of an H H -free r r -graph G G . This assumption makes the structural analysis ‘nicer’ in some cases, so we hope that this method helps us to better understand the set Π r \Pi_{r} .
