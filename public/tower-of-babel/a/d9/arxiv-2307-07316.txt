The systematic uncertainties on the cross-section measurement come from reconstruction-related and general sources.

In summary, the Born cross sections and polar angle distributions of the process
e^{+}e^{-}\rightarrow\Lambda_{c}^{+}\bar{\Lambda}_{c}^{-}
are studied at twelve c.m. energies from
4.6119
to
4.9509~\mathrm{GeV}
.
Benefiting from the large data samples, which enable ST and DT approaches via the decay
\Lambda_{c}^{+}\to pK^{-}\pi^{+}
, the cross sections and effective form factors of
\Lambda_{c}^{+}
are determined with an unprecedented precision.
From the threshold up to
4.66~\mathrm{GeV}
, our measured cross sections indicate no enhancement around the
Y(4630)
resonance, which is different from Belle
Bellelplm
.
In contrast to the case for the proton and neutron, no oscillatory behavior is discerned in the effective form-factor spectrum of
\Lambda_{c}^{+}
.
However, the energy-dependence of
|G_{E}/G_{M}|
reveals an oscillation feature with a
significantly higher frequency than that of the proton, which
may imply a non-trivial structure of the lightest charmed baryon.
