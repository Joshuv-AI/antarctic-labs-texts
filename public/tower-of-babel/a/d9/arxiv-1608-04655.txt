Probing nuclear effects using single-transverse kinematic imbalance with MINERvA
X.-G. Lu 1 and M. Betancourt 2 for the MINERvA Collaboration

Abstract
Kinematic imbalance of the final-state particles in the plane transverse to the neutrino direction provides a sensitive probe of nuclear effects. In this contribution, we report the MINERvA measurement of the single-transverse kinematic imbalance in neutrino charged-current quasielastic-like events on CH targets. To improve the momentum measurements of the final-state particles, we develop a method to select elastically scattering contained (ESC) protons and a general procedure to correct the transverse momentum scales.

2
Improvement of proton momentum measurement

The proton momentum in the default reconstruction is determined by the
\textrm{d}E/\textrm{d}x
profile along the track inside the tracker
[
4
]
. However, for non-contained or inelastically scattering protons, the profile is sensitive to the non-zero momentum at the track end point and has a distorted momentum mapping. Elastically scattering contained (ESC) protons, whose end point momentum is 0, are selected by
\textrm{d}E/\textrm{d}x
cuts at the last 6 measurement points as they have higher
\textrm{d}E/\textrm{d}x
near the end point compared to the non-ESC ones. The spread of the reconstructed momentum is reduced to 60% of the default value with a reduction of statistics to 40%. An additional cut on the track fit
\chi^{2}

[
4
]
further reduces the spread to 50% and statistics to 30%.

Correction of the proton and muon

Since a non-unity momentum scale leads to an instrumental apparent acceleration or deceleration of the final-state particles, it is crucial to correct the scales of
