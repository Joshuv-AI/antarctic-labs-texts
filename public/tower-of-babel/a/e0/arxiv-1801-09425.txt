Jacob’s ladders and new families of ζ \zeta -kindred real continuous functions.
Jan Moser

Abstract
. In this paper we obtain, by our method of crossbreeding in certain set of ζ \zeta -factorization formulas, the corresponding complete hybrid formulas. These are playing the role of criterion for selection of new families of ζ \zeta -kindred real continuous functions.

&m=1,\dots,M,\ L\to\infty.\end{split}

(c)

Further, we will suppose that we have obtained the following complete hybrid formula

\begin{split}&\mathcal{F}\left\{\prod_{r=1}^{k_{1}}(\cdots),\prod_{r=1}^{k_{2}}(\cdots),\dots,\prod_{r=1}^{k_{M}}(\cdots),F_{1}[f_{1}(\alpha_{0}^{1,k_{1}})],\dots,F_{M}[f_{m}(\alpha_{0}^{M,k_{M}})]\right\}\\

after the finite number of stages of crossbreeding (every memeber of (
