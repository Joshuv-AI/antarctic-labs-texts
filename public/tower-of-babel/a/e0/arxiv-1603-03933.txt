Lepton mass and mixing in a Neutrino Mass Model based on S 4 S_{4} flavor symmetry
V. V. VIEN (1),(2)

Abstract
We study a neutrino mass model based on S 4 S_{4} flavor symmetry which accommodates lepton mass, mixing with non-zero θ 13 \theta_{13} and CP violation phase. The spontaneous symmetry breaking in the model is imposed to obtain the realistic neutrino mass and mixing pattern at the tree- level with renormalizable interactions. Indeed, the neutrinos get small masses from one S ​ U ​ ( 2 ) L SU(2)_{L} doubplet and two S ​ U ​ ( 2 ) L SU(2)_{L} singlets in which one being in 2 ¯ \underline{2} and the two others in 3 ¯ \underline{3} under S 4 S_{4} with both the breakings S 4 → S 3 S_{4}\rightarrow S_{3} and S 4 → Z 3 S_{4}\rightarrow Z_{3} are taken place in charged lepton sector and S 4 → 𝒦 S_{4}\rightarrow\mathcal{K} in neutrino sector. The model also gives a remarkable prediction of Dirac CP violation δ C ​ P = π 2 \delta_{CP}=\frac{\pi}{2} or − π 2 -\frac{\pi}{2} in the both normal and inverted spectrum which is still missing in the neutrino mixing matrix. The relation between lepton mixing angles is also represented. PACS: 14.60.Pq; 12.60.-i; 14.60.St.
