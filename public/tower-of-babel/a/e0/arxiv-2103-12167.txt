Abstract
. The automorphism groups of the Fano–Mukai fourfold of genus 10 10 were studied in our previous paper [ PZ18 ] . In particular, we found in [ PZ18 ] the neutral components of these groups. In the present paper we finish the description of the discrete parts. Up to isomorphism, there are three special Fano–Mukai fourfold of genus 10 10 with the automorphism groups GL 2 ⁡ ( 𝕜 ) ⋊ ℤ / 2 ​ ℤ \operatorname{GL}_{2}(\Bbbk)\rtimes{\mathbb{Z}}/2{\mathbb{Z}} , ( 𝔾 a × 𝔾 m ) ⋊ ℤ / 2 ​ ℤ ({\mathbb{G}}_{\mathrm{a}}\times{\mathbb{G}}_{\mathrm{m}})\rtimes{\mathbb{Z}}/2{\mathbb{Z}} and 𝔾 m 2 ⋊ ℤ / 6 ​ ℤ {\mathbb{G}}_{\mathrm{m}}^{2}\rtimes{\mathbb{Z}}/6{\mathbb{Z}} , respectively. For any other Fano–Mukai fourfold V V of genus 10 10 one has Aut ⁡ ( V ) = 𝔾 m 2 ⋊ ℤ / 2 ​ ℤ \operatorname{{Aut}}(V)={\mathbb{G}}_{\mathrm{m}}^{2}\rtimes{\mathbb{Z}}/2{\mathbb{Z}} .

1.8 Lemma

(
[

(ii)
