Skin values of 208 Pb and 48 Ca determined from reaction cross sections
Tomotsugu Wakasa, Shingo Tagami, Masanobu Yahiro

Abstract
Background The PREX and the CREX group reported skin values, r skin 208 ​ ( PREX2 ) = 0.283 ± 0.071 ​ fm r_{\rm skin}^{208}({\rm PREX2})=0.283\pm 0.071\,{\rm fm} and r skin 48 ​ ( CREX ) = 0.121 ± 0.026 ​ ( exp ) ± 0.024 ​ ( model ) r_{\rm skin}^{48}({\rm CREX})=0.121\pm 0.026\ {\rm(exp)}\pm 0.024\ {\rm(model)} fm, respectively. Using the Love-Franey (LF) t t -matrix folding model with the neutron and proton densities scaled to the neutron radius r n 208 ​ ( PREX2 ) r_{\rm n}^{208}({\rm PREX2}) and the proton radius of the electron scattering, we found that the reaction cross sections σ R \sigma_{R} reproduce the data for p+ 208 Pb scattering at E lab = 534.1,549,806 E_{\rm lab}=534.1,549,806 MeV. Zenihiro el al. deduce neutron radii r n 48 , 40 ​ ( exp ) r_{\rm n}^{48,40}({\rm exp}) from the angular distributions of the cross sections and analyzing powers of proton elastic scattering, whereas we determine matter radius r m 40 ​ ( exp ) = 3.361 ± 0.075 r_{\rm m}^{\rm 40}({\rm exp})=3.361\pm 0.075 fm from measured σ R \sigma_{R} for 4 He+ 40 Ca scattering. Aim Our first aim is to determine r skin 208 r_{\rm skin}^{\rm 208} from measured σ R \sigma_{R} of p+ 208 Pb scattering at E lab = 534.1,549,806 E_{\rm lab}=534.1,549,806 MeV by using the Love-Franey (LF) t t -matrix folding model. Our second aim is to determine r skin 48 r_{\rm skin}^{\rm 48} from the r m 40 ​ ( exp ) r_{\rm m}^{\rm 40}({\rm exp}) and the difference Δ ≡ r m 48 ​ ( exp ) − r m 40 ​ ( exp ) \Delta\equiv r_{\rm m}^{\rm 48}({\rm exp})-r_{\rm m}^{\rm 40}({\rm exp}) that is evaluated from the r n 48 , 40 ​ ( exp ) r_{\rm n}^{48,40}({\rm exp}) and the r p 48 , 40 ​ ( exp ) r_{\rm p}^{48,40}({\rm exp}) calculated with the isotope shift method based on the electron scattering. Method and results For the first aim, we use the Love-Franey t t -matrix model with the densities scaled from the D1S-GHFB+AMP neutron density, where D1S-GHFB+AMP stands for D1S Gogny HFB (GHFB) with the angular momentum projection (AMP). The D1M-GHFB+AMP is also used to estimate a theoretical error. The resulting skin values are r skin 208 = 0.324 ± 0.047 r_{\rm skin}^{\rm 208}=0.324\pm 0.047 fm for D1S and r skin 208 ​ ( exp ) = 0.333 ± 0.047 r_{\rm skin}^{\rm 208}({\rm exp})=0.333\pm 0.047 fm for D1M. The difference Δ = 0.109 \Delta=0.109 fm and r m 40 ​ ( exp ) = 3.361 ± 0.075 r_{\rm m}^{\rm 40}({\rm exp})=3.361\pm 0.075 fm yield r m 48 = 3.470 ± 0.075 r_{\rm m}^{\rm 48}=3.470\pm 0.075 fm, leading to r skin 48 = 0.144 ± 0.075 r_{\rm skin}^{\rm 48}=0.144\pm 0.075 fm. Conclusion We conclude that r skin 208 ​ ( exp ) = 0.324 ± ( 0.047 ) exp ± ( 0.009 ) th ​ fm r_{\rm skin}^{208}({\rm exp})=0.324\pm(0.047)_{\rm exp}\pm(0.009)_{\rm th}~{\rm fm} for p scattering at E lab = 534.1,549,806 E_{\rm lab}=534.1,549,806 MeV. Our skin value r skin 48 = 0.144 ± 0.083 = 0.061 ∼ 0.227 r_{\rm skin}^{\rm 48}=0.144\pm 0.083=0.061\sim 0.227 fm is consistent with r skin 48 ​ ( CREX ) = 0.071 ∼ 0.171 r_{\rm skin}^{48}({\rm CREX})=0.071\sim 0.171 fm.
