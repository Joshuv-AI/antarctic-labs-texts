On the energy density of linearly polarized, plane gravitational wave
I-Ching Yang

Abstract




ABSTRACT

In this article, the energy density of plane gravitational wave is studied by using Einstein and Møller’s
prescription of energy-momentum pseudotensors. The linearly polarized plan gravitational wave solution
of Einstein field equation, which has been defined by Bondi et al., is represented by four kinds of different
coodrinates. The energy distribution of gravitational wave solution in Einstein and Møller’s prescription
are obtained. Particularly the energy component is zero in null coordinates.

In theory of electromagnetism, the electromagnetic waves can transport energy, so it is believed

term, are acceptable. So, in this article, Einstein and Møller’s prescriptions of energy-momentum
pseudotensors are used to study the energy density of plan gravitational waves, which are the simplest
solutions of wave equation.
