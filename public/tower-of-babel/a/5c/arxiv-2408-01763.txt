Certain products of sums of Lambert series
Aung Phone Maw

Abstract
Using elementary means, we prove an identity giving the infinite product form of a sum of Lambert series originally stated by Venkatachaliengar, then rediscovered by Andrews, Lewis, and Liu. Then we derive two identities expressing certain products of sums of Lambert series.



Keywords:
Lambert series, Basic hypergeometric series, Jacobi triple product
.

Mathematics Subject Classification:
11F11
.

1
Preamble

Throughout this exposition, we shall use the following standard notation :

(x;q)_{\infty}=(x)_{\infty}=\prod_{i\geq 1}(1-xq^{i-1}),

(x;q)_{n}=(x)_{n}=\frac{(x;q)_{\infty}}{(xq^{n};q)_{\infty}},\hskip 9.24994ptn\in\mathbb{C},\hskip 9.24994pt(x;q)_{0}=1.

Then, let us recall, the following two important results from the theory of basic hypergeometric series, the Jacobi triple product identity
[
1
, p. 18]
and the next is just the partial fraction decomposition of
\frac{1}{(z)_{\infty}(z^{-1}q)_{\infty}}
(this identity which appears in
[
2
, p. 452]
can be confirmed by studying the behaviour of functions on both sides of the equation at the points
z=q^{n},\hskip 9.24994ptn\in\mathbb{Z}
.) :

(q)_{\infty}(z)_{\infty}(z^{-1}q)_{\infty}=\sum_{n=-\infty}^{+\infty}(-1)^{n}z^{n}q^{\frac{n^{2}-n}{2}},

\frac{{(q)_{\infty}}^{2}}{(z)_{\infty}(z^{-1}q)_{\infty}}=\sum_{n=-\infty}^{+\infty}\frac{(-1)^{n}q^{\frac{n^{2}+n}{2}}}{1-zq^{n}}.

In this exposition, we shall prove three identities on sums of Lambert series. The first being an identity giving the infinite product form of a sum of Lambert series. The equivalent form of this statement was in fact originally stated by Venkatachaliengar
[
3
, p. 37]
, in which Venkatachaliengar stated the fundamental multiplicative identity of the Jordan-Kronecker function. It was then rediscovered by Andrews, Lewis and Liu
[
4
]
. For our purpose, this identity will be stated in the following form.

Proposition 1
.

For
a,b,c
arbitrary, there holds :

\frac{{(q)_{\infty}}^{2}(ab)_{\infty}(a^{-1}b^{-1}q)_{\infty}(ac)_{\infty}(a^{-1}c^{-1}q)_{\infty}(bc)_{\infty}(b^{-1}c^{-1}q)_{\infty}}{(a)_{\infty}(a^{-1}q)_{\infty}(b)_{\infty}(b^{-1}q)_{\infty}(c)_{\infty}(c^{-1}q)_{\infty}(abc)_{\infty}(a^{-1}b^{-1}c^{-1}q)_{\infty}}\\
=1+\sum_{r\geq 0}\frac{aq^{r}}{1-aq^{r}}-\sum_{r\geq 1}\frac{a^{-1}q^{r}}{1-a^{-1}q^{r}}+\sum_{r\geq 0}\frac{bq^{r}}{1-bq^{r}}-\sum_{r\geq 1}\frac{b^{-1}q^{r}}{1-b^{-1}q^{r}}\\
+\sum_{r\geq 0}\frac{cq^{r}}{1-cq^{r}}-\sum_{r\geq 1}\frac{c^{-1}q^{r}}{1-c^{-1}q^{r}}+\sum_{r\geq 1}\frac{a^{-1}b^{-1}c^{-1}q^{r}}{1-a^{-1}b^{-1}c^{-1}q^{r}}-\sum_{r\geq 0}\frac{abcq^{r}}{1-abcq^{r}}.

(1)

The following two identities relate certain sums of Lambert series to products of sums of Lambert series. Our main objective will be in deriving them.

Proposition 2
.

For
b,c
arbitrary, there holds :

\displaystyle\left\{\sum_{r\geq 0}\frac{bq^{r}}{1-bq^{r}}-\sum_{r\geq 1}\frac{b^{-1}q^{r}}{1-b^{-1}q^{r}}-\sum_{r\geq 0}\frac{bcq^{r}}{1-bcq^{r}}+\sum_{r\geq 1}\frac{b^{-1}c^{-1}q^{r}}{1-b^{-1}c^{-1}q^{r}}\right\}\left\{\sum_{r\geq 0}\frac{cq^{r}}{1-cq^{r}}-\sum_{r\geq 1}\frac{c^{-1}q^{r}}{1-c^{-1}q^{r}}\right.

\displaystyle\left.-\sum_{r\geq 0}\frac{bcq^{r}}{1-bcq^{r}}+\sum_{r\geq 1}\frac{b^{-1}c^{-1}q^{r}}{1-b^{-1}c^{-1}q^{r}}\right\}=\frac{bc}{(1-bc)^{2}}+\sum_{r\geq 1}r(b^{r}c^{r}+b^{-r}c^{-r})\frac{q^{r}}{1-q^{r}}

\displaystyle\hskip 199.16928pt+\sum_{r\geq 1}(b^{r}+b^{-r}+c^{r}+c^{-r}-b^{r}c^{r}-b^{-r}c^{-r}-2)\frac{q^{r}}{(1-q^{r})^{2}}.

(1.4)

Proposition 3
.

For
b,c
arbitrary, there holds :

\displaystyle\left\{\frac{1}{2}+\sum_{r\geq 0}\frac{bq^{r}}{1-bq^{r}}-\sum_{r\geq 1}\frac{b^{-1}q^{r}}{1-b^{-1}q^{r}}+\sum_{r\geq 0}\frac{cq^{r}}{1-cq^{r}}-\sum_{r\geq 1}\frac{c^{-1}q^{r}}{1-c^{-1}q^{r}}-\sum_{r\geq 0}\frac{bcq^{r}}{1-bcq^{r}}+\sum_{r\geq 1}\frac{b^{-1}c^{-1}q^{r}}{1-b^{-1}c^{-1}q^{r}}\right\}^{2}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{4}+\sum_{r\geq 0}\left\{\frac{bq^{r}}{(1-bq^{r})^{2}}+\frac{cq^{r}}{(1-cq^{r})^{2}}+\frac{bcq^{r}}{(1-bcq^{r})^{2}}\right\}+\sum_{r\geq 1}\left\{\frac{b^{-1}q^{r}}{(1-b^{-1}q^{r})^{2}}+\frac{c^{-1}q^{r}}{(1-c^{-1}q^{r})^{2}}+\frac{b^{-1}c^{-1}q^{r}}{(1-b^{-1}c^{-1}q^{r})^{2}}\right\}

\displaystyle\hskip 9.24994pt-6\sum_{r\geq 1}\frac{q^{r}}{(1-q^{r})^{2}}.

(1.5)

We shall begin our exposition by studying the following function
f
, which is also known as the Jordan-Kronecker function :

f(a,b;q)=f(a,b)=\sum_{n=-\infty}^{+\infty}\frac{a^{n}}{1-bq^{n}}.

2
Some q-Identities on
f(a,b)

First, let us write down some trivial facts about
f
:

\displaystyle f(a,b)

\displaystyle=f(b,a),

(2.1)

\displaystyle f(a,b)

\displaystyle=-b^{-1}f(qa^{-1},b^{-1}),

(2.2)

\displaystyle f(a,b)

\displaystyle=\frac{1-ab}{(1-a)(1-b)}+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{ba^{n}q^{n}}{1-bq^{n}}-\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{b^{-1}a^{-n}q^{n}}{1-b^{-1}q^{n}},

(2.3)

\displaystyle\lim_{a\to 1}\left\{f(a,b)-\frac{1}{1-a}\right\}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{bq^{n}}{1-bq^{n}}-\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{b^{-1}q^{n}}{1-b^{-1}q^{n}},

(2.4)

\displaystyle a^{k}f(a,bq^{k})

\displaystyle=f(a,b),

(2.5)

\displaystyle\frac{df(a,b)}{da}=f_{a}(a,b)

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=-\infty}^{+\infty}\frac{b^{n}q^{n}}{(1-aq^{n})^{2}}.

(2.6)

We now consider the following expression :

(q)_{\infty}(a)_{\infty}(a^{-1}q)_{\infty}f(a,b),

which we note that, can be resolved in the following manner :

\displaystyle\left\{\sum_{k=-\infty}^{+\infty}(-1)^{k}a^{k}q^{\frac{k^{2}-k}{2}}\right\}\left\{\sum_{n=-\infty}^{+\infty}\frac{a^{n}}{1-bq^{n}}\right\}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k,n=-\infty}^{+\infty}(-1)^{k}a^{-k}q^{\frac{k^{2}+k}{2}}\frac{a^{n+k}}{1-bq^{n+k}}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k,n=-\infty}^{+\infty}(-1)^{k}q^{\frac{k^{2}+k}{2}}\frac{a^{n}}{1-bq^{n+k}}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k,n=-\infty}^{+\infty}(-1)^{n}a^{n}b^{n}q^{\frac{n^{2}-n}{2}}\frac{(-1)^{k}q^{\frac{k^{2}+k}{2}}}{1-bq^{k}}

\displaystyle=\left\{\sum_{n=-\infty}^{+\infty}(-1)^{n}a^{n}b^{n}q^{\frac{n^{2}-n}{2}}\right\}\left\{\sum_{k=-\infty}^{+\infty}\frac{(-1)^{k}q^{\frac{k^{2}+k}{2}}}{1-bq^{k}}\right\}

\displaystyle=\frac{{(q)_{\infty}}^{3}(ab)_{\infty}(a^{-1}b^{-1}q)_{\infty}}{(b)_{\infty}(b^{-1}q)_{\infty}}.

(2.7)

Thus, we now see that
f
has the following product representation :

f(a,b)=\frac{{(q)_{\infty}}^{2}(ab)_{\infty}(a^{-1}b^{-1}q)_{\infty}}{(a)_{\infty}(a^{-1}q)_{\infty}(b)_{\infty}(b^{-1}q)_{\infty}}.

Then, we shall consider another important expression, which is :

f(a,b)f(a,c).

We see that this expression can also be resolved, in the following way :

\displaystyle\left\{\sum_{k=-\infty}^{+\infty}\frac{a^{k}}{1-bq^{k}}\right\}\left\{\sum_{n=-\infty}^{+\infty}\frac{a^{n}}{1-cq^{n}}\right\}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k,n=-\infty}^{+\infty}\frac{a^{k}a^{n-k}}{(1-bq^{k})(1-cq^{n-k})}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k,n=-\infty}^{+\infty}\frac{a^{n}bq^{k}}{(1-bq^{k})(bq^{k}-bcq^{n})}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k,n=-\infty}^{+\infty}\frac{a^{n}bq^{k}}{1-bcq^{n}}\left\{\frac{1}{bq^{k}-bcq^{n}}+\frac{1}{1-bq^{k}}\right\}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=-\infty}^{+\infty}\frac{a^{n}}{1-bcq^{n}}\left\{\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\left(\frac{1}{1-cq^{n-k}}+\frac{bq^{k}}{1-bq^{k}}\right)+\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\left(\frac{1}{1-cq^{n+k}}+\frac{bq^{-k}}{1-bq^{-k}}\right)\right\}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{a^{n}}{1-bcq^{n}}\left\{\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\left(\frac{1}{1-cq^{n-k}}+\frac{bq^{k}}{1-bq^{k}}\right)+\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\left(\frac{cq^{n+k}}{1-cq^{n+k}}+\frac{1}{1-bq^{-k}}\right)\right\}

\displaystyle\hskip 9.24994pt+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{a^{-n}}{1-bcq^{-n}}\left\{\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\left(\frac{1}{1-cq^{-n-k}}+\frac{bq^{k}}{1-bq^{k}}\right)+\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\left(\frac{cq^{-n+k}}{1-cq^{-n+k}}+\frac{1}{1-bq^{-k}}\right)\right\}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{a^{n}}{1-bcq^{n}}\left\{\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\left(\frac{1}{1-cq^{-k}}+\frac{bq^{k}}{1-bq^{k}}\right)+\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\left(\frac{cq^{k}}{1-cq^{k}}+\frac{1}{1-bq^{-k}}\right)+\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}\frac{1}{1-cq^{n-k}}-\sum_{k=1}^{n}\frac{cq^{k}}{1-cq^{k}}\right\}

\displaystyle\hskip 9.24994pt+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{a^{-n}}{1-bcq^{-n}}\left\{\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\left(\frac{1}{1-cq^{-k}}+\frac{bq^{k}}{1-bq^{k}}\right)+\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\left(\frac{cq^{k}}{1-cq^{k}}+\frac{1}{1-bq^{-k}}\right)-\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}\frac{1}{1-cq^{-k}}+\sum_{k=1}^{n}\frac{cq^{-n+k}}{1-cq^{-n+k}}\right\}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{na^{n}}{1-bcq^{n}}+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{(-n)a^{-n}}{1-bcq^{-n}}+\left\{\sum_{n=-\infty}^{+\infty}\frac{a^{n}}{1-bcq^{n}}\right\}\left\{1+\sum_{r=0}^{\infty}\frac{bq^{r}}{1-bq^{r}}-\sum_{r=1}^{\infty}\frac{b^{-1}q^{r}}{1-b^{-1}q^{r}}\right.

\displaystyle\left.\hskip 9.24994pt+\sum_{r=0}^{\infty}\frac{cq^{r}}{1-cq^{r}}-\sum_{r=1}^{\infty}\frac{c^{-1}q^{r}}{1-c^{-1}q^{r}}\right\}.

We note that what we just arrived is in fact the fundamental multiplicative identity by Venkatachaliengar
[
3
, p. 37]
. And since
\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{na^{n}}{1-bcq^{n}}+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{(-n)a^{-n}}{1-bcq^{-n}}=af_{a}(a,bc)
, using the product representation in (
2
), we have :

af_{a}(a,bc)=f(a,bc)\left\{\sum_{r=0}^{\infty}\frac{aq^{r}}{1-aq^{r}}-\sum_{r=1}^{\infty}\frac{a^{-1}q^{r}}{1-a^{-1}q^{r}}+\sum_{r=1}^{\infty}\frac{a^{-1}b^{-1}c^{-1}q^{r}}{1-a^{-1}b^{-1}c^{-1}q^{r}}-\sum_{r=0}^{\infty}\frac{abcq^{r}}{1-abcq^{r}}\right\}.

Thus, we arrive at the following result :

f(a,b)f(a,c)=f(a,bc)\left\{1+\sum_{r\geq 0}\frac{aq^{r}}{1-aq^{r}}-\sum_{r\geq 1}\frac{a^{-1}q^{r}}{1-a^{-1}q^{r}}+\sum_{r\geq 0}\frac{bq^{r}}{1-bq^{r}}-\sum_{r\geq 1}\frac{b^{-1}q^{r}}{1-b^{-1}q^{r}}\right.\\
\left.+\sum_{r\geq 0}\frac{cq^{r}}{1-cq^{r}}-\sum_{r\geq 1}\frac{c^{-1}q^{r}}{1-c^{-1}q^{r}}+\sum_{r\geq 1}\frac{a^{-1}b^{-1}c^{-1}q^{r}}{1-a^{-1}b^{-1}c^{-1}q^{r}}-\sum_{r\geq 0}\frac{abcq^{r}}{1-abcq^{r}}\right\}.

(2)

Finally, using the product representation of

Proposition 1
.

Which can be rewritten as :

\displaystyle f(a,b)f(a,c)-\frac{f(a,b)}{1-a}-\frac{f(a,c)}{1-a}+\frac{1}{(1-a)^{2}}

\displaystyle=f(a,bc)\{1+l(a)+l(b)+l(c)-l(abc)\}-\frac{f(a,b)}{1-a}-\frac{f(a,c)}{1-a}+\frac{1}{(1-a)^{2}}

\displaystyle\hskip 9.24994pt-\frac{f(a,b)}{1-a}-\frac{f(a,c)}{1-a}+\frac{1}{(1-a)^{2}}

\displaystyle=\left\{f(a,bc)-\frac{1}{1-a}\right\}\left\{l(a)+l(b)+l(c)-l(abc)-\frac{a}{1-a}\right\}+\frac{f(a,bc)-\frac{1}{1-a}}{1-a}

\displaystyle\hskip 9.24994pt+\frac{l(a)+l(b)+l(c)-l(abc)-\frac{a}{1-a}}{1-a}-\frac{\left(f(a,b)-\frac{1}{1-a}\right)}{1-a}-\frac{\left(f(a,c)-\frac{1}{1-a}\right)}{1-a}

\displaystyle=\left\{f(a,bc)-\frac{1}{1-a}\right\}\left\{l(a)+l(b)+l(c)-l(abc)-\frac{a}{1-a}\right\}

\displaystyle\hskip 9.24994pt+\frac{f(a,bc)-\frac{1}{1-a}-l(abc)+l(b)-\left(f(a,b)-\frac{1}{1-a}\right)+l(c)-\left(f(a,c)-\frac{1}{1-a}\right)+l(a)-\frac{a}{1-a}}{1-a},
