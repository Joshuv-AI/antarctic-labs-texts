On Iwasawa invariants of modular forms with reducible and non- p p -distinguished residual Galois representations
Sheng-Chi Shih and Jun Wang

Abstract
. In the present paper, we study the p p -adic L L -functions and the (strict) Selmer groups over ℚ ∞ \mathbb{Q}_{\infty} , the cyclotomic ℤ p \mathbb{Z}_{p} -extension of ℚ \mathbb{Q} , of the p p -adic weight one cusp forms f f , obtained via the p p -stabilization of weight one Eisenstein series, under the assumption that a certain Eisenstein component of the p p -ordinary universal cuspidal Hecke algebra is Gorenstein. As an application, we compute the Iwasawa invariants of ordinary modular forms of weight k ≥ 2 k\geq 2 with the same residual Galois representations as the one of f f , which in our setting, is reducible and non- p p -distinguished. Combining this with a result of Kato [ Kat04 , Theorem 17.4.2] , we prove the Iwasawa main conjecture for these forms. Also, we give numerical examples that satisfy the Gorenstein hypothesis. A crucial point for the p p -adic L L -functions of f f is that under the Gorenstein hypothesis, we are able to define them, following Greenberg–Vatsal, as elements in the one-dimensional Iwasawa algebra by using Mazur–Kitagawa two-variable p p -adic L L -functions and then, to compute them explicitly via local explicit reciprocity law. For the (strict) Selmer groups of f f , we compute them via the knowledge of the Galois representations of f f studied in [ BDP22 ] .

By Lemma 2.6 of

We have

3.4.
Residual Selmer groups

We now define the residual Selmer groups following
[
GV00
, §2]
and then discuss its relation to the usual Selmer groups.

Recall that
\mathbb{F}
denotes the residual field of
\mathcal{O}_{g}
. Consider the exact sequence of
\mathbb{F}[\mathrm{G}_{\mathbb{Q}_{p}}]
-modules

0\to A^{\prime}_{g,j}[\pi_{g}]\to A_{g,j}[\pi_{g}]\to A^{\prime\prime}_{g,j}[\pi_{g}]\to 0.

The residual Selmer group is defined as

\begin{split}\Sel(\mathbb{Q}_{\infty},A_{g,j}[\pi_{g}]):=\ker(H^{1}(\mathrm{G}_{\mathbb{Q}_{\infty},\Sigma_{Np}},A_{g,j}[\pi_{g}])\to\prod_{v\in\Sigma_{Np},v\nmid p}H^{1}(\mathrm{I}_{\infty,v},A_{g,j}[\pi_{g}])\times\mathcal{H}_{p}(\mathbb{Q}_{\infty},A_{g,j}[\pi_{g}])),\end{split}

where
\mathcal{H}_{p}(\mathbb{Q}_{\infty},A_{g,j}[\pi_{g}]):=\mathrm{Im}(H^{1}(\mathbb{Q}_{\infty,w},A[\pi_{g}])\to H^{1}(\mathrm{I}_{\infty,w},A^{\prime\prime}_{g,j}[\pi_{g}]))

Lemma 3.7
.

For any
v|N
, the map

H^{1}(\mathrm{I}_{\infty,v},A_{g,j}[\pi_{g}])\to H^{1}(\mathrm{I}_{\infty,v},A_{g,j})

is injective. Also, the map

H^{1}(\mathrm{I}_{\infty,w},A^{\prime\prime}_{g,j}[\pi_{g}])\to H^{1}(\mathrm{I}_{\infty,w},A^{\prime\prime}_{g,j})

is injective, where

is the unique prime of
\mathbb{Q}_{\infty}
above

.

Proof.

The kernel of these two maps are
A_{g,j}^{\mathrm{I}_{\infty,v}}/\pi_{g}
and
(A^{\prime\prime}_{g,j})^{\mathrm{I}_{\infty,w}}/\pi_{g}
, respectively. They are zero since both
A_{g,j}^{\mathrm{I}_{\infty,v}}
and
(A^{\prime\prime}_{g,j})^{\mathrm{I}_{\infty,w}}=A^{\prime\prime}_{g,j}
are divisible.
Notice that
A_{g,j}^{\mathrm{I}_{\infty,v}}
is divisible follows from the proof of
[
EPW06
, Lemma 4.1.2]
as the character
\chi
is primitive of conductor
N
.
∎

Proposition 3.8
.

We have a short exact sequence

(3.7)

0\to H^{0}(\mathbb{Q}_{\infty},A_{g,j})/\pi_{g}\to\Sel(\mathbb{Q}_{\infty},A_{g,j}[\pi_{g}])\to\Sel(\mathbb{Q}_{\infty},A_{g,j})[\pi_{g}]\to 0

and

(3.8)

\dim_{\mathbb{F}}H^{0}(\mathbb{Q}_{\infty},A_{g,j})/\pi_{g}=\begin{cases}1&\mbox{ if }j=0\\
0&\mbox{ if }j\neq 0.\end{cases}

Proof.

The exact sequence (
3.7
) is known
[
BP19
, Lemma 4.3]
. For the sake of convenience, we give a proof here. Since
A_{g,j}
is
\mathcal{O}_{g}
-divisible, multiplication by
\pi_{g}
yields a short exact sequence

0\to A_{g,j}[\pi_{g}]\to A_{g,j}\xrightarrow{\times\pi_{g}}A_{g,j}\to 0.

Hence, we have the following exact sequence of
\mathcal{O}_{g}
-modules

0\to H^{0}(\mathbb{Q}_{\infty},A_{g,j})/\pi_{g}\to H^{1}(\mathbb{Q}_{\infty},A_{g,j}[\pi_{g}])\to H^{1}(\mathbb{Q}_{\infty},A_{g,j})[\pi_{g}]\to 0

and the following isomorphism of

-vector spaces

(3.9)

H^{0}(\mathbb{Q}_{\infty},A_{g,j}[\pi_{g}])\simeq H^{0}(\mathbb{Q}_{\infty},A_{g,j})[\pi_{g}].

One can verify the surjectivity of
\Sel(\mathbb{Q}_{\infty},A_{g,j}[\pi_{g}])\to\Sel(\mathbb{Q}_{\infty},A_{g,j})[\pi_{g}]
by Lemma
3.7
and by diagram chasing.

To see the injectivity of

,
Lemma
3.3
(2) yields the vanishing of

if

. Thus, there is nothing to show in this case. We now assume

. It suffices to show that

is contained in
\Sel(\mathbb{Q}_{\infty},A_{g,0}[\pi_{g}])
, i.e. it satisfies the Selmer condition at all places of
\mathbb{Q}_{\infty}
above

.

Finally, we show (
3.8
) when

has divisible part and torsion part. One can adapt the proof of
[

(2) can yield
