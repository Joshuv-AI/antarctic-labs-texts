Bounds of the Radio Number of Stacked-Book Graph with Odd Paths
Tayo Charles Adefokun 1, Deborah Olayide Ajayi 2

Abstract
. A Stacked-book graph G m , n G_{m,n} is obtained from the Cartesian product of a star graph S m S_{m} and a path P n P_{n} , where m m and s s are the orders of the star graph and the path respectively. Obtaining the radio number of a graph is a rigorous process, which is dependent on diameter of G G and positive difference of non-negative integer labels f ⁡ ( u ) f(u) and f ⁡ ( v ) f(v) assigned to any two u , v u,v in the vertex set V ⁡ ( G ) V(G) of G G . This paper obtains tight upper and lower bounds of the radio number of G m , n G_{m,n} where the path P n P_{n} has an odd order. The case where P n P_{n} has an even order has been investigated.
