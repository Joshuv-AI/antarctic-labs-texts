The effect of B -site alloying on the electronic and opto-electronic properties of RbPbI 3 \text{RbPbI}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{3}}} : A DFT study
Anupriya Nyayban, Subhasis Panda, Avijit Chowdhury

Abstract
Divalent cations mixed lead halide perovskites with enhanced performances, high stabilities, and reduced toxicity are requisite to make persistent progress in perovskite solar cells. However, the mixing strategy is not reported extensively in search of a lead reduced structure. Herein, we report the structural, electronic and optical properties of RbPb 1 – x ​ M x ​ I 3 \text{RbPb}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{1\hskip 0.90417pt--\hskip 0.90417ptx\/}}}\text{M}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{x\/}}}\text{I}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{3}}} (where, M ={ Sn , Ge } and x ={ 0.25 0.25 , 0.50 0.50 , 0.75 0.75 }) by alloying the B -site with Sn and Ge , using the density functional theory. The formation enthalpy is estimated for all RbPb 1 – x ​ M x ​ I 3 \text{RbPb}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{1\hskip 0.90417pt--\hskip 0.90417ptx\/}}}\text{M}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{x\/}}}\text{I}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{3}}} (with x = 0.25 0.25 , 0.50 0.50 , 0.75 0.75 ), which confirms stability for all the structures. The energy bandgap and density of states (DOS) have been thoroughly investigated. The energy bandgap decreases with the increasing Sn / Ge contents, the lowest bandgap of 1.850 1.850 eV is observed at x = 0.50 {}\mathrm{x}=0.50 in the case of RbPb 1 – x ​ Ge x ​ I 3 \text{RbPb}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{1\hskip 0.90417pt--\hskip 0.90417ptx\/}}}\text{Ge}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{x\/}}}\text{I}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{3}}} systems. Further, the effective masses and the binding energy of excitons and spectroscopic limited maximum efficiency (SLME) are also estimated for all the mixed systems. The exciton type is observed to change from Mott-Wannier to Frenkel type with increasing the contents of both Sn and Ge at the B -site. The maximum efficiency of 23 23 % is achieved using an active layer containing an equal admixture of Sn / Ge and Pb . The estimated parameters of both the mixed systems are consistent with the available literature of similar types.

IV
Conclusions

In summary, the structural, electronic and optical properties of both
\text{RbPb}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{1\hskip 0.90417pt--\hskip 0.90417ptx\/}}}\text{Sn}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{x\/}}}\text{I}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{3}}}
and
\text{RbPb}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{1\hskip 0.90417pt--\hskip 0.90417ptx\/}}}\text{Ge}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{x\/}}}\text{I}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{3}}}
have been systematically investigated using the first principle calculations. The decrease in the volume is observed for the increase in both
Sn
and
Ge
content in the mixed structures except for
{}\mathrm{x}=0.50
. The highest stability is observed for
\text{RbPb}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{0.50}}}\text{Sn}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{0.50}}}\text{I}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{3}}}
and
\text{RbPb}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{0.50}}}\text{Ge}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{0.50}}}\text{I}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{3}}}
whereas all the systems under consideration exhibit stability. The bandstructure calculations show the indirect bandgaps for all the structures while the lowest bandgaps calculated with PBE are
1.951
and
1.850
eV for
\text{RbPb}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{0.25}}}\text{Sn}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{0.75}}}\text{I}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{3}}}
and
\text{RbPb}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{0.50}}}\text{Ge}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{0.50}}}\text{I}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{3}}}
, respectively. The decrease in the bandgaps follow an anomalous behavior similar to the observed in
\text{MAGe}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{x\/}}}\text{Pb}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{1\hskip 0.90417pt--\hskip 0.90417ptx\/}}}\text{I}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{3}}}

Mayengbam et al. 2018
. The comparatively highest absorption in the near infrared and visible spectra region is observed for both
\text{RbPb}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{0.25}}}\text{Sn}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{0.75}}}\text{I}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{3}}}
and
\text{RbPb}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{0.50}}}\text{Ge}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{0.50}}}\text{I}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{3}}}
structures. The lower effective masses of charge carriers and the binding energies of excitons are found upto
{}\mathrm{x}=0.50
for both
Pb
-
Sn
and
Pb
-
Ge
mixed systems. The highest SLME of
22.057
% and
23.248
% are observed for
{}\mathrm{x}=0.75
and
{}\mathrm{Ge}=0.50
in case of
Pb
-
Sn
and
Pb
-
Ge
mixed systems. Among all the investigated structures,
\text{RbPb}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{0.5}}}\text{Ge}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{0.5}}}\text{I}{\vphantom{\text{X}}}_{\smash[t]{\text{3}}}
exhibits the lowest bandgap, highest stability, high absorption and low exciton binding energy, thus, is the most suitable one for the photovoltaic application.
