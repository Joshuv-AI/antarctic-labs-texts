Probe of axion-like particles in vector boson scattering at a muon collider Thanks: Electronic address: alexandre.kisselev@ihep.ru
S.C. İnan

Abstract
We have examined the sensitivity to the axion-like particle (ALP) couplings to electroweak gauge bosons in the diphoton production at a future muon collider. The collisions at the μ + ​ μ − \mu^{+}\mu^{-} energies of 3 TeV, 14 TeV, and 100 TeV are addressed. The differential cross sections versus the invariant mass of the final photons and total cross section versus minimal diphoton invariant mass are presented. We have derived the exclusion regions for the ALP-gauge boson coupling. The obtained bounds are much stronger than the current experimental bounds in the ALP mass region 10 GeV – 10 TeV. The partial-wave unitarity constraints on the ALP-gauge boson coupling are estimated. We have shown that the unitarity is not violated in the region of the ALP coupling studied in the present paper.

2
ALP in gauge boson scattering

The interaction of the ALP
a
with SM gauge bosons is described by
the Lagrangian

\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{int}}=\frac{1}{2}\,\partial_{\mu}a\,\partial^{\,\mu}\!a-\frac{1}{2}m_{a}^{2}a^{2}+g^{2}C_{BB}\frac{a}{\Lambda}B_{\mu\nu}\tilde{B}^{\mu\nu}+g^{\prime 2}C_{WW}\frac{a}{\Lambda}W_{\mu\nu}^{c}\tilde{W}^{c,\mu\nu}\;,

(1)

where
B_{\mu\nu}
and
W_{\mu\nu}^{c}
are the field strength of

U(1)_{Y}
and
SU(2)_{L}
, respectively, while
\tilde{B}_{\mu\nu}
and

\tilde{W}_{\mu\nu}^{c}
are dual field strength tensors. As was
already mentioned above, the ALP mass
m_{a}
and coupling
f_{a}
can
be regarded as independent parameters. After electroweak symmetry
breaking, the ALP couples to the photon and
Z
boson as

\displaystyle\mathcal{L}_{a}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2}\,\partial_{\mu}a\,\partial^{\,\mu}\!a-\frac{1}{2}m_{a}^{2}a^{2}+g_{a\gamma\gamma}aF_{\mu\nu}\tilde{F}^{\mu\nu}+g_{a\gamma Z}aF_{\mu\nu}\tilde{Z}^{\mu\nu}

\displaystyle+g_{aZZ}aZ_{\mu\nu}\tilde{Z}^{\mu\nu}\;,

(2)

Here
\tilde{F}_{\mu\nu}=(1/2)\,\varepsilon_{\mu\nu\alpha\beta}F_{\alpha\beta}
and

\tilde{Z}_{\mu\nu}=(1/2)\,\varepsilon_{\mu\nu\alpha\beta}Z_{\alpha\beta}
are the dual
tensors, and

\displaystyle g_{a\gamma\gamma}

\displaystyle=\frac{e^{2}}{\Lambda}[C_{WW}+C_{BB}]\;,

\displaystyle g_{a\gamma Z}

\displaystyle=\frac{2e^{2}}{\Lambda s_{w}c_{w}}[c_{w}^{2}C_{WW}-s_{w}^{2}C_{BB}]\;,

\displaystyle g_{aZZ}

\displaystyle=\frac{e^{2}}{\Lambda s_{w}^{2}c_{w}^{2}}[c_{w}^{4}C_{WW}+s_{w}^{4}C_{BB}]\;,

(3)

where
s_{w}
and
c_{w}
are sine and cosine of the Weinberg angle,

In what follows, we assume that the ALP couples to hypercharge

U(1)_{Y}

SU(2)_{L}

define

(4)

We also assume that the ALP has a nonzero total width

\Gamma_{a}=\frac{\Gamma(a\rightarrow\gamma\gamma)}{\mathrm{Br}(a\rightarrow\gamma\gamma)}\;,

(5)

where

\Gamma(a\rightarrow\gamma\gamma)=\frac{m_{a}^{3}}{4\pi f_{a}^{2}}

(6)

is the ALP decay width into two photons. In general, the ALP can
also couple to fermions as
\partial^{\mu}a\bar{\psi}\gamma_{\mu}\gamma_{5}\psi

decay should be mainly defined by its decay to two photons. In our

3
Unitarity constraints on ALP coupling

Let us study bounds imposed by partial-wave unitarity. The
partial-wave expansion of the helicity amplitude in the
center-of-mass system was derived in
[
104
]
. It looks
like

\displaystyle M_{\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2}\lambda_{3}\lambda_{4}}(s,\theta,\varphi)

\displaystyle=16\pi\sum_{J}(2J+1)\sqrt{(1+\delta_{\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2}})(1+\delta_{\lambda_{3}\lambda_{4}})}

\displaystyle\times\,e^{i(\lambda-\mu)\phi}\,d^{J}_{\lambda\mu}(\theta)\,T^{J}_{\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2}\lambda_{3}\lambda_{4}}(s)\;,

(16)

where
\lambda=\lambda_{1}-\lambda_{2}
,
\mu=\lambda_{3}-\lambda_{4}
,
\theta(\phi)
is the polar (azimuth) scattering angle,
and
d^{J}_{\lambda\mu}(\theta)
is the Wigner (small)
d
-function

[
105
]
. Relevant formulas for the
d
-functions can be found
in
[
106
]
. If we choose the plane
(x-z)
as a
scattering plane, then
\phi=0
in
(
3
). Parity conservation means that

T^{J}_{\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2}\lambda_{3}\lambda_{4}}(s)=(-1)^{\lambda_{1}-\lambda_{2}-\lambda_{3}+\lambda_{4}}\,T^{J}_{-\lambda_{1}-\lambda_{2}-\lambda_{3}-\lambda_{4}}(s)\;.

(17)

Partial-wave unitarity in the limit
s\gg(m_{1}+m_{2})^{2}
requires
that

\left|T^{J}_{\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2}\lambda_{3}\lambda_{4}}(s)\right|\leq 1\;.

(18)

Using orthogonality of the
d
-functions,

\int\limits_{-1}^{1}d^{J}_{\lambda\lambda^{\prime}}(z)\,d^{J^{\prime}}_{\lambda\lambda^{\prime}}(z)\,dz=\frac{2}{2J+1}\,\delta_{JJ^{\prime}}\;,

(19)

we find from (
3
) that the partial-wave
amplitude is defined as

\displaystyle T^{J}_{\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2}\lambda_{3}\lambda_{4}}(s)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{32\pi}\frac{1}{\sqrt{(1+\delta_{\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2}})(1+\delta_{\lambda_{3}\lambda_{4}})}}\int\limits_{-1}^{1}\!\!M_{\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2}\lambda_{3}\lambda_{4}}(s,z)\,d^{J}_{\lambda\mu}(z)\,dz\;.

(20)

Here and in what follows,
z=\cos\theta
. The helicity amplitudes

M_{\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2}\lambda_{3}\lambda_{4}}
are given in Appendix A.

The
d
-functions obey, inter alia, the relation

d^{J}_{\lambda\mu}(-z)=(-1)^{J-\lambda}d^{J}_{\mu-\lambda}(z)
. In
particular, we have (
J\geq 0
)

d^{J}_{00}(z)=P_{J}(z)\;,

(21)

P_{J}(z)
being the Legendre polynomial, and
[
106
]

\displaystyle d^{J}_{2-2}(z)

\displaystyle=(-1)^{J}\left(\frac{1-z}{2}\right)^{\!2}{}_{2}F_{1}\!\left(2-J,J+3;1;\frac{1+z}{2}\right),

(22)

\displaystyle d^{J}_{22}(z)

\displaystyle=\left(\frac{1+z}{2}\right)^{\!2}{}_{2}F_{1}\!\left(2-J,J+3;1;\frac{1-z}{2}\right),

(23)

where
{}_{2}F_{1}(a,b;c;x)
is the hypergeometric function

[
107
]
, and
J\geq 2
.

1.

As a result, we obtain

\displaystyle T^{J}_{++--}(s)

\displaystyle=\frac{s}{32\pi f_{a}^{2}}\left[\frac{2}{1-\varepsilon}\int\limits_{-1}^{1}\!P_{J}(z)\,dz-\left(1+(-1)^{J}\right)\int\limits_{-1}^{1}\!\frac{(1-z)^{2}}{1-z+2\varepsilon}P_{J}(z)\,dz\right]

\displaystyle=\frac{s}{8\pi f_{a}^{2}}\left[\frac{\varepsilon(3-2\varepsilon)}{1-\varepsilon}\,\delta_{J0}-\left(1+(-1)^{J}\right)\varepsilon^{2}Q_{J}(1+2\varepsilon)\right],

It results in the following unitarity bound

4
Conclusions

We have examined the possibility to search for heavy axion-like
particles in the
\mu^{+}\mu^{-}\rightarrow\mu^{+}\gamma\gamma\mu^{-}

scattering at the future muon collider. The studies are presented
for the collision energies of 3 TeV, 14 TeV, and 100 TeV and
integrated luminosities of 1 ab
-1
, 20 ab
-1
, and 1000
ab
-1
, respectively. We have obtained the explicit expressions
for the helicity amplitudes for the
Z\gamma\rightarrow\gamma\gamma
and
ZZ\rightarrow\gamma\gamma
collisions. Using
these amplitudes (as well as known helicity amplitudes for the

\gamma\gamma\rightarrow\gamma\gamma
collision), the differential
cross sections versus invariant mass of the final photons and total
cross section versus minimal diphoton invariant mass are calculated.
As a result, the 95% C.L.exclusion regions for the ALP-gauge boson
coupling coming from the
\mu^{+}\mu^{-}\rightarrow\mu^{+}\gamma\gamma\mu^{-}
scattering at the high energy muon collider are obtained. The
excluded areas extend to wider regions in comparison to the region
obtained previously for the polarized light-by-light scattering at
the 3 TeV CLIC. Our constraints are also much stronger than the
current experimental bounds presented in
Fig.
6
. The partial-wave unitarity bounds on
the ALP-gauge boson coupling are estimated. We have shown that the
unitarity is not violated in the region of the ALP coupling which
has been studied in our paper. We can conclude that the future muon
collider has a great physical potential in searching for axion-like
particle couplings to the SM gauge bosons.
