The partial null conditions and global smooth solutions of the nonlinear wave equations on ℝ d × 𝕋 \mathbb{R}^{d}\times\mathbb{T} with d = 2 , 3 d=2,3
Fei Hou, Fei Tao, Huicheng Yin[12pt] 1. Department of Mathematics, Nanjing University, Nanjing, 210093, China2. School of Mathematical Sciences and Mathematical Institute,Nanjing Normal University, Nanjing, 210023, China

Abstract
In this paper, we investigate the fully nonlinear wave equations on the product space ℝ 3 × 𝕋 \mathbb{R}^{3}\times\mathbb{T} with quadratic nonlinearities and on ℝ 2 × 𝕋 \mathbb{R}^{2}\times\mathbb{T} with cubic nonlinearities, respectively. It is shown that for the small initial data satisfying some space-decay rates at infinity, these nonlinear equations admit global smooth solutions when the corresponding partial null conditions hold and while have almost global smooth solutions when the partial null conditions are violated. Our proof relies on the Fourier mode decomposition of the solutions with respect to the periodic direction, the efficient combinations of time-decay estimates for the solutions to the linear wave equations and the linear Klein-Gordon equations, and the global weighted energy estimates. In addition, an interesting auxiliary energy is introduced. As a byproduct, our results can be applied to the 4D irrotational compressible Euler equations of polytropic gases or Chaplygin gases on ℝ 3 × 𝕋 \mathbb{R}^{3}\times\mathbb{T} , the 3D relativistic membrane equation and the 3D nonlinear membrane equation on ℝ 2 × 𝕋 \mathbb{R}^{2}\times\mathbb{T} . Keywords. Fully nonlinear wave equation, Klein-Gordon equation, partial null condition, null condition, auxiliary energy, weighted energy estimate 2020 Mathematical Subject Classification. 35L05, 35L15, 35L70.

&-\partial_{t}^{2}v+\Delta_{x}v+(1+u)\partial_{y}^{2}v=\displaystyle\sum_{1\leq i,j\leq 2}N_{2}(w_{i},w_{j}),\\
\end{cases}

(1.21)

where
(t,x,y)\in[0,\infty)\times\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3}\times\mathop{\mathbb{T}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits

w_{1},w_{2}=\{u,v\}
, the nonlinearities

and

are linear combinations of the following quadratic null forms

\begin{split}&\partial_{t}\phi\partial_{t}\psi-\nabla_{x}\phi\cdot\nabla_{x}\psi,\quad\partial_{i}\phi\partial_{j}\psi-\partial_{i}\psi\partial_{j}\phi,\quad 1\leq i<j\leq 3,\\
&\partial_{t}\phi\partial_{i}\psi-\partial_{i}\phi\partial_{t}\psi,\quad 1\leq i\leq 3\end{split}

with
\phi,\psi=\{u,v\}
. For the small and regular initial data of (
1.21
) with polynomial spatial decay at infinity,
the global existence of small data solution
(u,v)
is shown.
Compared with the result in
[
12
]
, we establish the global or almost global solutions of 4D fully nonlinear wave equations
on
\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3}\times\mathop{\mathbb{T}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits
when the general partial null condition is fulfilled or not. In addition, we also establish the global or almost global
small data solutions of 3D fully nonlinear wave equations on
\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{2}\times\mathop{\mathbb{T}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits
with cubic nonlinearities. It is pointed out that
the methods in
[
12
]
and our present paper are different.
On the other hand, the interested readers are also referred to the works

\begin{split}E_{N}(t)+\mathcal{X}_{N}(t)&\leq\varepsilon_{1}(1+t)^{\varepsilon_{2}},\\

(2.8)

where
\varepsilon_{1}>0
and
\varepsilon_{2}\in[0,1/10]
will be determined later.

According to the definition of

and (
2.7
)-(

(2.9)

Based on the bootstrap estimates (

)-(

of the solutions

3
The pointwise estimates of the zero mode

3.1
The pointwise estimates of the zero mode on
\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3}\times\mathop{\mathbb{T}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits

In this subsection, we first give two basic lemmas, from which a series of the pointwise estimates on the zero mode
are derived.

Lemma 3.1
.

For any sufficiently smooth function
f(t,x)
, it holds that

\|f(t,x)\|_{L^{\infty}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3})}\lesssim(1+t)^{-3/2}\|f\|_{L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3})}+\sum_{i=1,2}\|\nabla^{i}_{x}f\|_{L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3})}.

(3.1)

Proof.

For any fixed
t\geq 0
, denote
J=[\log_{2}(2+t)]+1>0
.
Let
\dot{\Delta}_{j}
be the homogeneous dyadic blocks of the frequency on
\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3}
. Then it follows from the Bernstein inequality
and direct computation that

\begin{split}\|f\|_{L^{\infty}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3})}&\lesssim\sum_{j\in\mathop{\mathbb{Z}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits}\|\dot{\Delta}_{j}f\|_{L^{\infty}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3})}\lesssim\sum_{j\in\mathop{\mathbb{Z}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits}2^{3j/2}\|\dot{\Delta}_{j}f\|_{L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3})}\\
&\lesssim\sum_{j\leq-J}2^{3j/2}\|\dot{\Delta}_{j}f\|_{L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3})}+\sum_{-J\leq j\leq 0}2^{3j/2}\|\dot{\Delta}_{j}f\|_{L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3})}+\sum_{j\geq 0}2^{3j/2}\|\dot{\Delta}_{j}f\|_{L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3})}\\
&\lesssim 2^{-3J/2}\|f\|_{L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3})}+\sum_{-J\leq j\leq 0}2^{j/2}\|\nabla_{x}f\|_{L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3})}+\sum_{j\geq 0}2^{-j/2}\|\nabla_{x}^{2}f\|_{L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3})}\\
&\lesssim(1+t)^{-3/2}\|f\|_{L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3})}+\|\nabla_{x}f\|_{L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3})}+\|\nabla_{x}^{2}f\|_{L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3})},\end{split}

which yields (
3.1
).
∎

Lemma 3.2
.

For any sufficiently smooth function
f(t,x)
, we have that

\langle{x}\rangle\langle{t\pm|x|}\rangle^{1/2}|f(t,x)|\lesssim\|\Omega^{\leq 2}f\|_{L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3})}+\sum_{|a|+|b|\leq 1}\|\langle{t\pm|x|}\rangle\nabla_{x}\nabla_{x}^{a}\Omega^{b}f\|_{L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3})}.

(3.2)

Proof.

Recalling (3.19) in
[
29
]
, one has that for smooth function
R(r)
,

\begin{split}&\Big(|x|^{4}|R(r)|^{2}\int_{\mathop{\mathbb{S}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{2}}|f(t,|x|\omega)|^{4}d\omega\Big)^{1/4}\\
\lesssim&\Big(\int_{|x^{\prime}|\geq|x|}[|R(r)|^{2}|\partial_{r}f(t,x^{\prime})|^{2}+|R^{\prime}(r)|^{2}|f(t,x^{\prime})|^{2}]dx^{\prime}\Big)^{1/4}\Big(\int_{|x^{\prime}|\geq|x|}|\Omega^{\leq 1}f(t,x^{\prime})|^{2}dx^{\prime}\Big)^{1/4},\end{split}

(3.3)

where
r=|x|>0
.
Choosing
R(r)=\langle{t\pm r}\rangle
in (
3.3
) and applying the Sobolev embedding
W^{1,4}(\mathop{\mathbb{S}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{2})\hookrightarrow L^{\infty}(\mathop{\mathbb{S}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{2})

to obtain

\begin{split}&\quad\;|x|\langle{t\pm|x|}\rangle^{1/2}|f(t,x)|\lesssim\Big(|x|^{4}|\langle{t\pm|x|}\rangle|^{2}\int_{\mathop{\mathbb{S}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{2}}|\Omega^{\leq 1}f(t,|x|\omega)|^{4}d\omega\Big)^{1/4}\\
&\lesssim\|\Omega^{\leq 2}f(t,x^{\prime})\|_{L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3}:|x^{\prime}|\geq|x|)}+\|\langle{t\pm|x^{\prime}|}\rangle\partial_{r}\Omega^{\leq 1}f(t,x^{\prime})\|_{L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3}:|x^{\prime}|\geq|x|)}.\end{split}

(3.4)

When
|x|\geq 1/3
, (
3.4
) implies (
3.2
) directly.

Next, we prove (
3.2
) for
|x|\leq 1/3
.
Define the cutoff function
\chi(s)\in C^{\infty}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits)
which satisfies

0\leq\chi\leq 1,\qquad\chi(s)=\left\{\begin{aligned} &1,\quad s\leq 1/3,\\
&0,\quad s\geq 1/2.\end{aligned}\right.

(3.5)

Applying the Sobolev embedding
H^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3})\hookrightarrow L^{\infty}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3})
to
\displaystyle\chi(|x|)f(t,x)
yields

|\chi(|x|)f(t,x)|\lesssim\|f(t,x)\|_{L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3}:|x|\leq 1)}+\langle{t}\rangle^{-1}\sum_{i=1,2}\|\langle{t\pm|x|}\rangle\nabla_{x}^{i}f(t,x)\|_{L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3}:|x|\leq 1)}.

(3.6)

Utilizing (
3.4
) to the first term on the right hand side of (
3.6
), we achieve

\begin{split}\langle{t}\rangle^{1/2}\|f(t,x)\|_{L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3}:|x|\leq 1)}&\lesssim\Big\||x|^{-1}\Big\|_{L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3}:|x|\leq 1)}\||x|\langle{t\pm|x|}\rangle^{1/2}f(t,x)\|_{L^{\infty}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3}:0<|x|\leq 1)}\\
&\lesssim\|\Omega^{\leq 2}f\|_{L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3})}+\sum_{|a|+|b|\leq 1}\|\langle{t\pm|x|}\rangle\nabla_{x}\nabla_{x}^{a}\Omega^{b}f\|_{L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3})}.\end{split}

This, together with (
3.6
), leads to (
3.2
) for
|x|\leq 1/3
.
∎

Lemma 3.3
.

For any multi-index
a
with
|a|\leq N-2
, it holds that

\langle{x}\rangle\langle{t-|x|}\rangle^{1/2}|P_{=0}\partial Z^{a}u(t,x,y)|\lesssim E_{|a|+2}(t)+\mathcal{X}_{|a|+2}(t).

(3.7)

In addition, one has that in the region

,

\langle{x}\rangle\langle{t+|x|}\rangle^{1/2}|P_{=0}\bar{\partial}Z^{a}u(t,x,y)|\lesssim E_{|a|+2}(t)+\mathcal{X}_{|a|+2}(t);\

(3.8)

and in the region
|x|\leq\langle{t}\rangle/3
,

\langle{t}\rangle|P_{=0}\partial Z^{a}u(t,x,y)|\lesssim E_{|a|+2}(t)+\mathcal{X}_{|a|+2}(t).

(3.9)

Proof.

For any smooth function
g(t,x,y)
, we have

By the definition of

with

, choosing
f=P_{=0}\partial Z^{a}u
and

in (
3.2
), respectively, and subsequently taking the

norms on the both sides of the resulted inequalities,
then (
3.7
) and (
3.8
) are obtained, respectively.
In addition, applying (
3.1
) to

).
∎

Lemma 3.6
.

For any sufficiently smooth function

\langle{x}\rangle^{1/2}\langle{t\pm|x|}\rangle^{1/2}|f(t,x)|\lesssim\|\Omega^{\leq 1}f\|_{L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{2})}+\sum_{|a|+|b|\leq 1}\|\langle{t\pm|x|}\rangle\nabla_{x}\nabla_{x}^{a}\Omega^{b}f\|_{L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{2})}.

\begin{split}&|x|\langle{t\pm|x|}\rangle|f(t,|x|\frac{x}{|x|})|^{2}\lesssim|x|\langle{t\pm|x|}\rangle\int_{\mathop{\mathbb{S}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{1}}|\Omega^{\leq 1}f(t,|x|\omega)|^{2}d\omega\\
=&-\int_{\mathop{\mathbb{S}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{1}}d\omega\int^{\infty}_{|x|}|x|\partial_{\rho}[\langle{t\pm\rho}\rangle|\Omega^{\leq 1}f(t,\rho\omega)|^{2}]d\rho\\
\lesssim&\int_{\mathop{\mathbb{S}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{1}}d\omega\int^{\infty}_{|x|}[\langle{t\pm\rho}\rangle|\Omega^{\leq 1}f(t,\rho\omega)||\partial_{\rho}\Omega^{\leq 1}f(t,\rho\omega)|+|\Omega^{\leq 1}f(t,\rho\omega)|^{2}]\rho d\rho\\
\lesssim&\int_{\mathop{\mathbb{S}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{1}}d\omega\int^{\infty}_{|x|}[\langle{t\pm\rho}\rangle^{2}|\partial_{\rho}\Omega^{\leq 1}f(t,\rho\omega)|^{2}+|\Omega^{\leq 1}f(t,\rho\omega)|^{2}]\rho d\rho\\
\lesssim&~\|\Omega^{\leq 1}f(t,x^{\prime})\|^{2}_{L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{2}:|x^{\prime}|\geq|x|)}+\|\langle{t\pm|x^{\prime}|}\rangle\nabla_{x}\Omega^{\leq 1}f(t,x^{\prime})\|^{2}_{L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{2}:|x^{\prime}|\geq|x|)}.\end{split}

(3.13)

For
|x|\geq 1/3
, (
3.13
) yields (
3.12
).

Next, we prove (
3.12
) for
|x|\leq 1/3
.
As in the proof of Lemma
3.2
, applying the Sobolev embedding
H^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{2})\hookrightarrow L^{\infty}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{2})
to

derives

|\chi(|x|)f(t,x)|\lesssim\|f(t,x)\|_{L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{2}:|x|\leq 1)}+\langle{t}\rangle^{-1}\sum_{i=1,2}\|\langle{t\pm|x|}\rangle\nabla_{x}^{i}f(t,x)\|_{L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{2}:|x|\leq 1)},

(3.14)

where

).
By using (
3.13
) to the first term on the right hand side of (

), we arrive at

\begin{split}\langle{t}\rangle^{1/2}\|f(t,x)\|_{L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{2}:|x|\leq 1)}&\lesssim\||x|^{-1/2}\|_{L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{2}:|x|\leq 1)}\||x|^{1/2}\langle{t\pm|x|}\rangle^{1/2}f(t,x)\|_{L^{\infty}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{2}:|x|\leq 1)}\\
&\lesssim\|\Omega^{\leq 1}f\|_{L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{2})}+\|\langle{t\pm|x|}\rangle\nabla_{x}\Omega^{\leq 1}f\|_{L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{2})}.\end{split}

This, together with (

) for

.

4.1
The pointwise estimates of the non-zero modes on

Lemma 4.1
.

Let
v
be a solution to
\Box_{t,x}v+v=\mathcal{F}(t,x)
. Then one has

\begin{split}\langle{t+|x|}\rangle^{1.4}|v(t,x)|&\lesssim\sum_{|a|\leq 5}\sup_{\tau\in[0,t]}\langle{\tau}\rangle^{-1/10}\|\langle{\tau+|\cdot|}\rangle\Gamma^{a}\mathcal{F}(\tau,\cdot)\|_{L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3})}\\
&\quad+\sum_{|a|\leq 6}\|\langle{\cdot}\rangle^{2}\Gamma^{a}v(0,\cdot)\|_{L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3})}\end{split}

(4.1)

and

\begin{split}\langle{t+|x|}\rangle^{3/2}|v(t,x)|&\lesssim\sum_{|a|\leq 5}\sup_{\tau\in[0,t]}\langle{\tau}\rangle^{1/10}\|\langle{\tau+|\cdot|}\rangle\Gamma^{a}\mathcal{F}(\tau,\cdot)\|_{L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3})}\\
&\quad+\sum_{|a|\leq 6}\|\langle{\cdot}\rangle^{2}\Gamma^{a}v(0,\cdot)\|_{L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3})}.\end{split}

(4.2)

Proof.

It follows from Theorem 1 in
[
9
]
that

\begin{split}\langle{t+|x|}\rangle^{3/2}|v(t,x)|&\lesssim\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\sum_{|a|\leq 5}\sup_{\tau\in[0,t]}\varphi_{j}(\tau)\|\langle{\tau+|\cdot|}\rangle\Gamma^{a}\mathcal{F}(\tau,\cdot)\|_{L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3})}\\
&\quad+\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\sum_{|a|\leq 6}\|\langle{x^{\prime}}\rangle^{3/2}\varphi_{j}(|x^{\prime}|)\Gamma^{a}v(0,x^{\prime})\|_{L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3})},\end{split}

(4.3)

where
\{\varphi_{j}\}_{j=0}^{\infty}
is a Littlewood-Paley partition of unit satisfying

\begin{split}\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\varphi_{j}(\tau)=1,\tau\geq 0,\quad\varphi_{j}\in C_{0}^{\infty}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits),\varphi_{j}\geq 0,j\geq 0,\\
\operatorname{supp}\varphi_{j}=[2^{j-1},2^{j+1}],j\geq 1,\quad\operatorname{supp}\varphi_{0}\cap\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits_{+}=[0,2].\end{split}

(4.4)

Let

. Then
\varphi_{j}(\tau)\equiv 0
holds for any
\tau\in[0,t]
and

.
Thereby, we arrive at

\begin{split}&\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\sum_{|a|\leq 5}\sup_{\tau\in[0,t]}\varphi_{j}(\tau)\|\langle{\tau+|\cdot|}\rangle\Gamma^{a}\mathcal{F}\|_{L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3})}\\
&\lesssim\Big(\sum_{j=0}^{j_{0}}\sup_{\tau\in[0,t]}\varphi_{j}(\tau)\langle{\tau}\rangle^{1/10}\Big)\sum_{|a|\leq 5}\sup_{\tau\in[0,t]}\langle{\tau}\rangle^{-1/10}\|\langle{\tau+|\cdot|}\rangle\Gamma^{a}\mathcal{F}\|_{L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3})}\\
&\lesssim\Big(\sum_{j=0}^{j_{0}}2^{j/10}\Big)\sum_{|a|\leq 5}\sup_{\tau\in[0,t]}\langle{\tau}\rangle^{-1/10}\|\langle{\tau+|\cdot|}\rangle\Gamma^{a}\mathcal{F}\|_{L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3})}\\
&\lesssim\langle{t}\rangle^{1/10}\sum_{|a|\leq 5}\sup_{\tau\in[0,t]}\langle{\tau}\rangle^{-1/10}\|\langle{\tau+|\cdot|}\rangle\Gamma^{a}\mathcal{F}\|_{L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3})}.\end{split}

(4.5)

On the other hand, it is easy to get that

\begin{split}\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\sum_{|a|\leq 6}\|\langle{x^{\prime}}\rangle^{3/2}\varphi_{j}(|x^{\prime}|)\Gamma^{a}v(0,x^{\prime})\|_{L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3})}\lesssim\sum_{|a|\leq 6}\|\langle{\cdot}\rangle^{2}\Gamma^{a}v(0,\cdot)\|_{L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3})}.\end{split}

(4.6)

Substituting (
4.5
) and (
4.6
) into (
4.3
) yields (
4.1
).
The proof of (
4.2
) is analogous.
∎

Lemma 4.2
.

Let

be the solution to (
1.1
).
Under the bootstrap assumptions (
2.7
), for any

\begin{split}\langle{s+|z|}\rangle^{1.4}|(\overline{Z^{a}u})_{n}(s,z)|&\lesssim|n|^{-2}\sum_{|a^{\prime}|\leq 5}\sum_{\tilde{\Gamma}\in\{\partial_{\tau,z^{\prime}},L,\Omega\}}\sup_{\tau\in[0,s]}\langle{\tau}\rangle^{-0.1}\|\langle{\tau+|z^{\prime}|}\rangle\tilde{\Gamma}^{a^{\prime}}(\overline{Q^{a}})_{n}(\tau,z^{\prime})\|_{L_{z^{\prime}}^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3})}\\

, and the vectors

Then returning to the

\begin{split}\langle{t+|x|}\rangle^{1.4}|(Z^{a}u)_{n}(t,x)|&\lesssim|n|^{1/2}\sum_{|a^{\prime}|\leq 5}\sup_{\tau\in[0,t]}\langle{\tau}\rangle^{-0.1}\|\langle{\tau+|x|}\rangle\Gamma^{a^{\prime}}(Q^{a})_{n}(\tau,x)\|_{L^{2}_{x}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3})}\\
&\quad+|n|^{3/2}\sum_{|a^{\prime}|\leq 6}\|\langle{x}\rangle^{2}\Gamma^{a^{\prime}}(Z^{a}u)_{n}(0,x)\|_{L^{2}_{x}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{3})}.\end{split}

(4.10)

In addition, the definition of
Q^{a}
in (

Lemma 5.1
.

For smooth function

\begin{split}\|\langle{t-|x|}\rangle\partial_{t,x}^{2}f\|_{L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{d})}&\lesssim\sum_{|a|\leq 1}\|\partial_{t,x}\Gamma^{a}f\|_{L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{d})}+\|\langle{t+|x|}\rangle\Box_{t,x}f\|_{L^{2}(\mathop{\mathbb{R}\kern 0.0pt}\nolimits^{d})},\\

(5.2)

Similarly, we can achieve that for

\begin{split}(t^{2}-|x|^{2})\partial_{i}\partial_{t}f&=t(t\partial_{i}+x_{i}\partial_{t})\partial_{t}f-tx_{i}\partial_{t}^{2}f-x_{j}(x_{j}\partial_{t}+t\partial_{j})\partial_{i}f\\

&\lesssim\sum_{b+c+d\leq a}\|P_{=0}\bar{\partial}\partial^{\leq 1}Z^{b}u\|_{L^{\infty}(|x|\geq\langle{s}\rangle/2)}\|(P_{=0}\partial\partial^{\leq 1}Z^{c}u)(P_{=0}\partial\partial^{\leq 1}Z^{d}u)\|_{L^{2}_{x,y}}\\
&\lesssim\sum_{|b|\leq N-9}\langle{s}\rangle^{-1}(E_{|b|+3}(s)+\mathcal{X}_{|b|+3}(s))\cdot\varepsilon_{1}(\varepsilon+\varepsilon_{1})\langle{s}\rangle^{-1/2+\varepsilon_{2}}\\
&\lesssim\varepsilon_{1}^{2}(\varepsilon+\varepsilon_{1})\langle{s}\rangle^{-3/2+2\varepsilon_{2}}.\end{split}

(5.45)

By inserting (

)-(
5.45
) into (

) and then substituting the resulting

) into (

), for any

&\lesssim E_{N-9}^{2}(0)+\varepsilon_{1}(\varepsilon+\varepsilon_{1})^{2}\sup_{s\in[0,t]}E_{N-9}(s).\end{split}

Hence,
