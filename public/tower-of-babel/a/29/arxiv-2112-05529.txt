Error Analysis of Domain Decomposition method for 4D Variational Data Assimilation (4DVAR DA): Consistence, Convergence and Stability.
Rosalba Cacciapuoti, Luisa D’Amore

Abstract




Abstract: We prove consistence, convergence and stability of the Domain Decomposition in space and time method for 4DVAR Data Assimilation problem (DD - 4D VAR). We give the condition number of the DD - 4D VAR method and experimentally validate the analysis on the Shallow Water Equations.
