Nonlocal Boundary Value Problem for Generalized Hilfer Implicit Fractional Differential Equations


Abstract
In this paper, we derive the equivalent fractional integral equation to the nonlinear implicit fractional differential equations involving φ \varphi -Hilfer fractional derivative subject to nonlocal fractional integral boundary conditions. The existence of a solution, Ulam-Hyers, and Ulam-Hyers-Rassias stability has been acquired by means equivalent fractional integral equation. Our investigations depend on the fixed point theorem due to Krasnoselskii and the Gronwall inequality involving φ \varphi -Riemann–Liouville fractional integral. An example is provided to show the utilization of primary outcomes.

Theorem 3.1

Let
1<\mu<2,~0\leq\nu\leq 1
and

be a continuous function. Then the nonlocal BVP for
\varphi
-Hilfer FDEs

{}^{H}\mathcal{D}^{\mu,\,\nu\,;\,\varphi}_{a+}y(t)

\displaystyle=h(t),~~~t\in J^{\prime}=(a,\,b],~

(3.1)

\displaystyle y(a)

\displaystyle=0,

(3.2)

\displaystyle y(b)

\displaystyle=\sum_{i=1}^{m}\lambda_{i}\,\mathcal{I}_{a+}^{\delta_{i}\,;\,\varphi}y(\tau_{i})

\lambda_{i}\in\mbox{\Bbb R}(i=1,2,\cdots,m)
are the constants such that

\Lambda=\frac{(\varphi(b)-\varphi(a))^{\xi-1}}{\Gamma(\xi)}-\sum_{i=1}^{m}\frac{\lambda_{i}}{\Gamma(\xi+\delta_{i})}(\varphi(\tau_{i})-\varphi(a))^{\xi+\delta_{i}-1}\neq 0.

But
\left(1-\nu\right)\left(2-\mu\right)=2-\xi

\displaystyle y\left(t\right)

\displaystyle\qquad+\frac{\left(\varphi\left(t\right)-\varphi\left(a\right)\right)^{\xi-2}}{\Gamma\left(\xi-1\right)}\mathcal{I}_{a+}^{2-\xi\,;\varphi}y\left(t\right)|_{t=a}+\mathcal{I}_{a+}^{\mu\,;\varphi}h(t)

\displaystyle=\frac{\left(\varphi\left(t\right)-\varphi\left(a\right)\right)^{\xi-1}}{\Gamma\left(\xi\right)}\,^{H}\mathcal{D}^{\xi-1,\,\nu\,;\,\varphi}_{a+}y\left(t\right)|_{t=a}

\displaystyle\qquad+\frac{\left(\varphi\left(t\right)-\varphi\left(a\right)\right)^{\xi-2}}{\Gamma\left(\xi-1\right)}\mathcal{I}_{a+}^{2-\xi\,;\varphi}y\left(t\right)|_{t=a}+\mathcal{I}_{a+}^{\mu\,;\varphi}h(t).

Let

\displaystyle=\underset{t\in J}{\max}\left|\left(\varphi\left(t\right)-\varphi\left(a\right)\right)^{2-\xi}\left(Py_{1}(t)-Py_{2}(t)\right)\right|

\displaystyle\leq\left\|y_{1}-y_{2}\right\|_{C_{2-\xi;\,\varphi}\left(J,\,\mbox{\Bbb R}\right)}\frac{K\Gamma(\xi-1)}{1-L}\frac{(\varphi(t)-\varphi(a))^{\mu+\xi-1}}{\Lambda\Gamma(\xi)}\Omega

\displaystyle\leq\sigma\left\|y_{1}-y_{2}\right\|_{C_{2-\xi;\,\varphi}\left(J,\,\mbox{\Bbb R}\right)}.

Since
\sigma<1
,
P
is contraction on
B_{r}.

Step 3:
The operator
Q
is compact and continuous.

We have already proved that,
Q
is self mapping on
C_{2-\xi;\,\varphi}\left(J,\,\mbox{\Bbb R}\right).
Further, from Step 1 we have

\left\|Qy\right\|_{C_{2-\xi;\,\varphi}\left(J,\,\mbox{\Bbb R}\right)}\leq\frac{(\varphi(b)-\varphi(a))^{\mu}\Gamma(\xi-1)}{(1-L)\Gamma(\mu+\xi-1)}\left\{Kr+M\right\},\,y\in B_{r}.

Therefore,
Q(B_{r})=\left\{Qy:y\in B_{r}\right\}
is uniformly bounded. Next, we prove that
Q(B_{r})
is equicontinuous. Let any
y\in B_{r}
and
t_{1},\,t_{2}\in J
with
t_{1}>t_{2}
. Then

\displaystyle\left|Qy(t_{1})-Qy(t_{2})\right|

\displaystyle=\left|\frac{1}{\Gamma(\mu)}\int_{a}^{t_{1}}\varphi^{\prime}(s)(\varphi(t_{1})-\varphi(s))^{\mu-1}g_{y}(s)ds-\frac{1}{\Gamma(\mu)}\int_{a}^{t_{2}}\varphi^{\prime}(s)(\varphi(t_{2})-\varphi(s))^{\mu-1}g_{y}(s)ds\right|

\displaystyle\leq\left|\frac{1}{\Gamma(\mu)}\int_{a}^{t_{1}}\varphi^{\prime}(s)(\varphi(t_{1})-\varphi(s))^{\mu-1}(\varphi(s)-\varphi(a))^{\xi-2}\left|(\varphi(s)-\varphi(a))^{2-\xi}f(s,y(s),g_{y}(s))\right|ds\right.

\displaystyle\left.~-\frac{1}{\Gamma(\mu)}\int_{a}^{t_{2}}\varphi^{\prime}(s)(\varphi(t_{2})-\varphi(s))^{\mu-1}(\varphi(s)-\varphi(a))^{\xi-2}\left|(\varphi(s)-\varphi(a))^{2-\xi}f(s,y(s),g_{y}(s))\right|ds\right|.

Since
g_{y}\left(\cdot\right)=f(\cdot,y(\cdot),g_{y}(\cdot))\in C_{2-\xi;\,\varphi}(J,\mbox{\Bbb R})
for any
y\in C_{2-\xi;\,\varphi}(J,\mbox{\Bbb R})
,
(\varphi(\cdot)-\varphi(a))^{2-\xi}f(\cdot,y(\cdot),g_{y}(\cdot))
is continuous on
J=[a,\,b]
. Therefore, there exists
\mathfrak{K}\in\mbox{\Bbb R}
such that

\left|(\varphi(t)-\varphi(a))^{2-\xi}f(t,y(t),g_{y}(t))\right|\leq\mathfrak{K},~\text{for all}~t\in J.

Thus,

\displaystyle\left|Qy(t_{1})-Qy(t_{2})\right|

\displaystyle\leq\left|\frac{\mathfrak{K}}{\Gamma(\mu)}\int_{a}^{t_{1}}\varphi^{\prime}(s)(\varphi(t_{1})-\varphi(s))^{\mu-1}(\varphi(s)-\varphi(a))^{\xi-2}ds\right.

\displaystyle\left.~-\frac{\mathfrak{K}}{\Gamma(\mu)}\int_{a}^{t_{2}}\varphi^{\prime}(s)(\varphi(t_{2})-\varphi(s))^{\mu-1}(\varphi(s)-\varphi(a))^{\xi-2}ds\right|

\displaystyle\leq\left|\mathfrak{K}\frac{\Gamma(\xi-1)}{\Gamma(\mu+\xi-1)}(\varphi(t_{1})-\varphi(a))^{\mu+\xi-2}-\mathfrak{K}\frac{\Gamma(\xi-1)}{\Gamma(\mu+\xi-1)}(\varphi(t_{2})-\varphi(a))^{\mu+\xi-2}\right|.

Hence,

\left|Qy(t_{1})-Qy(t_{2})\right|\leq\mathfrak{K}\frac{\Gamma(\xi-1)}{\Gamma(\mu+\xi-1)}\left|(\varphi(t_{1})-\varphi(a))^{\mu+\xi-2}-(\varphi(t_{2})-\varphi(a))^{\mu+\xi-2}\right|.

Using continuity of
\varphi
, we observe that
\left|Qy(t_{1})-Qy(t_{2})\right|\to 0
as
\left|t_{1}-t_{2}\right|\to 0
. This proves
Q(B_{r})
is equicontinuous. Thus, by Arzel
\grave{a}
-Ascoli Theorem,
Q(B_{r})
is relatively compact. We proved that, operator
Q
is compact.

Since
P
and
Q
satisfy all the conditions of Krasnoselskii fixed point theorem (Theorem
2.5
), the operator equation defined in the equation (
4.3
), has at least one fixed point in the space
C_{2-\xi;\,\varphi}\left(J,\,\mbox{\Bbb R}\right)
which is the solution of the nonlocal BVP for implicit FDEs (
1.1
)-(
1.3
).

\Box

Theorem 5.2
(Ulam-Hyers stability)

Assume that the hypothesis
(H1)
hold. Then, the equation (

) is Ulam-Hyers stable provided the condition (

) hold.

Proof:

Let any
\epsilon>0
. Let
z\in C_{2-\xi;\,\varphi}(J,\mbox{\Bbb R})
be any solution of the inequality

\left|{}^{H}\mathcal{D}^{\mu,\,\nu\,;\,\varphi}_{a+}z(t)-f(t,z(t),^{H}\mathcal{D}^{\mu,\,\nu\,;\,\varphi}_{a+}z(t))\right|\leq\epsilon,\quad t\in J.

(5.4)

Then, there exists
w\in C(J,\,\mbox{\Bbb R})
such that

^{H}\mathcal{D}^{\mu,\,\nu\,;\,\varphi}_{a+}z(t)=f(t,z(t),^{H}\mathcal{D}^{\mu,\,\nu\,;\,\varphi}_{a+}z(t))+w(t),

(5.5)

and
\left|w(t)\right|\leq\epsilon,\,t\in J.
In the view of the Theorem
3.2
.

z(t)=(\varphi(t)-\varphi(a))^{\xi-1}\tilde{A}_{z}+\mathcal{I}_{a+}^{\mu\,;\,\varphi}g_{z}(t)+\mathcal{I}_{a+}^{\mu\,;\,\varphi}w(t)

(5.6)

is the solution of the equation (
5.5
), where
g_{z}(\cdot)\in C_{2-\xi;\,\varphi}(J,\mbox{\Bbb R})

g_{z}(t)=f\left(t,(\varphi(t)-\varphi(a))^{\xi-1}\tilde{A}_{z}+\mathcal{I}_{a+}^{\mu\,;\,\varphi}g_{z}(t)+\mathcal{I}_{a+}^{\mu\,;\,\varphi}w(t),g_{z}(t)\right),\quad t\in J,

(5.7)

Let

be solution of the problem

\begin{cases}{}^{H}\mathcal{D}^{\mu,\,\nu\,;\,\varphi}_{a+}y(t)=f(t,y(t),^{H}\mathcal{D}^{\mu,\,\nu\,;\,\varphi}_{a+}y(t)),\\
y(a)=z(a),\qquad y(b)=z(b),\end{cases}

and
z(b)=\sum_{i=1}^{m}\lambda_{i}\,\mathcal{I}_{a+}^{\delta_{i}\,;\,\varphi}z(\tau_{i})
.
By Theorem
3.2
, the equivalent fractional integral equation to (
5.8

y(t)=(\varphi(t)-\varphi(a))^{\xi-1}\tilde{A}_{y}+\mathcal{I}_{a+}^{\mu\,;\,\varphi}g_{y}(t),

(5.9)

where,

satisfies functional equation

g_{y}(t)=f\left(t,(\varphi(t)-\varphi(a))^{\xi-1}\tilde{A}_{y}+\mathcal{I}_{a+}^{\mu\,;\,\varphi}g_{y}(t),g_{y}(t)\right),\quad t\in J,

and

\tilde{A}_{y}=\frac{1}{\Lambda\Gamma(\xi)}\left[\sum_{i=1}^{m}\lambda_{i}\,\mathcal{I}_{a+}^{\mu+\delta_{i}\,;\,\varphi}g_{y}(\tau_{i})-\mathcal{I}_{a+}^{\mu\,;\,\varphi}g_{y}(b)\right].

Now,

\displaystyle\left|\tilde{A}_{y}-\tilde{A}_{z}\right|

\displaystyle=\left|\left\{\frac{1}{\Lambda\Gamma(\xi)}\left[\sum_{i=1}^{m}\frac{\lambda_{i}}{\Gamma(\mu+\delta_{i})}\int_{a}^{\tau_{i}}\varphi^{\prime}(s)(\varphi(\tau_{i})-\varphi(s))^{\mu+\delta_{i}-1}g_{y}(s)ds\right.\right.\right.

\displaystyle\left.\left.\left.\quad-\frac{1}{\Gamma(\mu)}\int_{a}^{b}\varphi^{\prime}(s)(\varphi(b)-\varphi(s))^{\mu-1}g_{y}(s)ds\right]\right.\right\}

\displaystyle-\left.\left\{\frac{1}{\Lambda\Gamma(\xi)}\left[\sum_{i=1}^{m}\frac{\lambda_{i}}{\Gamma(\mu+\delta_{i})}\int_{a}^{\tau_{i}}\varphi^{\prime}(s)(\varphi(\tau_{i})-\varphi(s))^{\mu+\delta_{i}-1}g_{z}(s)ds\right.\right.\right.

\displaystyle\left.\left.\left.\quad-\frac{1}{\Gamma(\mu)}\int_{a}^{b}\varphi^{\prime}(s)(\varphi(b)-\varphi(s))^{\mu-1}g_{z}(s)ds\right]\right\}\right|

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{\Lambda\Gamma(\xi)}\left\{\sum_{i=1}^{m}\frac{\lambda_{i}}{\Gamma(\mu+\delta_{i})}\int_{a}^{\tau_{i}}\varphi^{\prime}(s)(\varphi(\tau_{i})-\varphi(s))^{\mu+\delta_{i}-1}\left|g_{y}(s)-g_{z}(s)\right|ds\right.

\displaystyle\left.\quad-\frac{1}{\Gamma(\mu)}\int_{a}^{b}\varphi^{\prime}(s)(\varphi(b)-\varphi(s))^{\mu-1}\left|g_{y}(s)-g_{z}(s)\right|ds\right\}.

(5.10)

By hypothesis (
H1
), we obtain

\displaystyle\left|g_{y}(t)-g_{z}(t)\right|

\displaystyle=\left|f(t,y(t),g_{y}(t))-f(t,z(t),g_{z}(t))\right|

\displaystyle\leq K\left|y(t)-z(t)\right|+L\left|g_{y}(t)-g_{z}(t)\right|.

This gives,

\left|g_{y}(t)-g_{z}(t)\right|\leq\frac{K}{1-L}\left|y(t)-z(t)\right|.

(5.11)

Using equation (
5.11
) in equation (
5

\displaystyle\left|\tilde{A}_{y}-\tilde{A}_{z}\right|

\displaystyle\leq\frac{K}{1-L}\frac{1}{\Lambda\Gamma(\xi)}\left\{\sum_{i=1}^{m}\frac{\lambda_{i}}{\Gamma(\mu+\delta_{i})}\int_{a}^{\tau_{i}}\varphi^{\prime}(s)(\varphi(\tau_{i})-\varphi(s))^{\mu+\delta_{i}-1}\left|y(s)-z(s)\right|ds\right.

\displaystyle\left.\quad-\frac{1}{\Gamma(\mu)}\int_{a}^{b}\varphi^{\prime}(s)(\varphi(b)-\varphi(s))^{\mu-1}\left|y(s)-z(s)\right|ds\right\}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{K}{1-L}\frac{1}{\Lambda\Gamma(\xi)}\left\{\sum_{i=1}^{m}\lambda_{i}\,\mathcal{I}_{a+}^{\mu+\delta_{i}\,;\,\varphi}\left|y(\tau_{i})-z(\tau_{i})\right|+\mathcal{I}_{a+}^{\mu\,;\,\varphi}\left|y(b)-z(b)\right|\right\}.

(5.12)

Since

. Therefore, from inequality (
5

5.7

\displaystyle=\left|z(t)-\left[(\varphi(t)-\varphi(a))^{\xi-1}\tilde{A}_{y}+\frac{1}{\Gamma(\mu)}\int_{a}^{t}\varphi^{\prime}(s)(\varphi(t)-\varphi(s))^{\mu-1}g_{y}(s)ds\right]\right|

\displaystyle~~+\left|\frac{1}{\Gamma(\mu)}\int_{a}^{t}\varphi^{\prime}(s)(\varphi(t)-\varphi(s))^{\mu-1}g_{z}(s)ds-\frac{1}{\Gamma(\mu)}\int_{a}^{t}\varphi^{\prime}(s)(\varphi(t)-\varphi(s))^{\mu-1}g_{y}(s)ds\right|

and

\displaystyle\left\|z-y\right\|_{C_{2-\xi;\,\varphi}\left(J,\,\mbox{\Bbb R}\right)}

(5.13)

where

Theorem 5.4
(Ulam-Hyers-Rassias stability)

Assume that the hypothesis
(H1)
hold. Let there exists non decreasing function such
\chi\in C(J,\,\mbox{\Bbb R}_{+})
and
K^{*}>0
such that

(5.14)

Then, the implicit FDE (
1.1
) is Ulam-Hyers-Rassias stable provided the condition (
4.1
) hold.

Proof:

Let any
\epsilon>0
and let
z\in C_{2-\xi;\,\varphi}(J,\mbox{\Bbb R})
be any solution of the inequality

\left|{}^{H}\mathcal{D}^{\mu,\,\nu\,;\,\varphi}_{a+}z(t)-f(t,z(t),^{H}\mathcal{D}^{\mu,\,\nu\,;\,\varphi}_{a+}z(t))\right|\leq\epsilon\,\chi(t),\,\,t\in J.

(5.15)

Then, proceeding as in the proof of Theorem
5.2

\left|z(t)-(\varphi(t)-\varphi(a))^{\xi-1}\tilde{A}_{z}-\mathcal{I}_{a+}^{\mu\,;\,\varphi}g_{z}(t)\right|\leq\mathcal{I}_{a+}^{\mu\,;\,\varphi}\left|w(t)\right|\leq\epsilon\,\,\mathcal{I}_{a+}^{\mu\,;\,\varphi}\chi(t)\leq\epsilon\,K^{*}\chi(t),\,\,t\in J.

where

. This proves, equation (
1.1
) is Ulam-Hyers-Rassias stable.
