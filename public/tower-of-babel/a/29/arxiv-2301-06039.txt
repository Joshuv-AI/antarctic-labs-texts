Nonperiodic tilings related to Stern’s diatomic series and based on tiles decorated with elements of 𝔽 p \mathbb{F}_{p}
Rémy Sigrist

Abstract
This paper presents the construction and various properties of substitution tilings related to Stern’s diatomic series and based on tiles decorated with elements of 𝔽 p \mathbb{F}_{p} for some odd prime number p p . These substitution tilings are additive in a sense that will be clarified later and lead to new nonperiodic tilings in one and two dimensions.

Introduction

Substitution tilings form a fascinating area of mathematics
at the confluence of several lines of research.
For an introduction to the theory of substitution tilings,
see Priebe
[
5
]
. Also, the Tilings Encyclopedia

In this paper, we will present

The usage of finite fields

,

.

,

6.2.4

Theorem 6.30
.

\forall y\in\mathbb{F}_{p}^{*}
,

Proof.

Remark 6.31
.

As
y
is invertible,
the periodicity of

is equivalent
to the periodicity of the “fusc” function reduced modulo
p
.

Suppose that
V_{y}

Lemma 6.32
.

The least period of
V_{y}
would be odd.

If
V_{y}
is
2m
-periodic with
m>0
, then:

fusc(n)=fusc(2n)=fusc(2n+2m)=fusc(2(n+m))=fusc(n+m)

(99)

So we can assume that the period is odd.

As
V_{y}
is primitive and self-similar,

\forall k\in\mathbb{N}
,

V_{y}
contains
\tau^{k}(\square(\frac{1}{2},\frac{1}{2}))
.

\forall k\geq 0
:

•

the two central tiles of
\tau^{k+1}(\square(\frac{1}{2},\frac{1}{2}))

are
\square(k+\frac{1}{2},1)
and
\square(1,k+\frac{1}{2})
,

•

in particular, when
k=p-\frac{1}{2}
,
we have the central tiles
\square\ 01
and
\square\ 10
(in that order).

\forall k^{\prime}>0
:

•

\tau^{k^{\prime}-1}(\square\ 01\ \square\ 10)
contains
2^{k^{\prime}}
tiles,

•

the leftmost being
t=\square\ 01

and the rightmost being
t^{\prime}=\square\ 10
,

•

the tiles
t
and
t^{\prime}
are at distance
2^{k^{\prime}}-1
,
and
t\neq t^{\prime}
.

Suppose that
V_{y}
is
m
-periodic (with
m
odd):

•

m
divides

,

•

so the leftmost and rightmost tiles
in
\tau^{ord_{2}(m)-1}(\square\ 01\ \square\ 10)

•

such supertiles appear infinitely many times in
V_{y}
,

•

hence

∎
