The bright side of simple heuristics for the TSP
Alan Frieze, Wesley Pegden

Abstract
The greedy and nearest-neighbor TSP heuristics can both have log ⁡ n \log n approximation factors from optimal in worst case, even just for n n points in Euclidean space. In this note, we show that this approximation factor is only realized when the optimal tour is unusually short. In particular, for points from any fixed d d -Ahlfor’s regular metric space (which includes any d d -manifold like the d d -cube [ 0 , 1 ] d [0,1]^{d} in the case d d is an integer but also fractals of dimension d d when d d is real-valued), our results imply that the greedy and nearest-neighbor heuristics have additive errors from optimal on the order of the optimal tour length through random points in the same space, for d > 1 d>1 .

We will prove our results not just for full-dimensional Euclidean space but for any sufficiently regular metric space with dimension
