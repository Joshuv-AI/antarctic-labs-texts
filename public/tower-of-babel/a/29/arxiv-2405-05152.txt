On equivalence of the Mellin-Barnes and the Givental integral representations of the Whittaker functions
A.A. Gerasimov, D.R. Lebedev, S.V. Oblezin

Abstract
. We construct an integral transformation intertwining the Gelfand-Tsetlin and the (modified) Gauss-Givental realizations of principle series representations of 𝔤 ​ 𝔩 3 ​ ( ℝ ) \mathfrak{gl}_{3}(\mathbb{R}) . This provides a direct identification of the corresponding integral representations for the 𝔤 ​ 𝔩 3 ​ ( ℝ ) \mathfrak{gl}_{3}(\mathbb{R}) -Whittaker function. The construction essentially uses integral identities due to Barnes and Gustafson thus providing a basis for their representation theory interpretation. The result of this paper might be useful for constructing the explicit analytic realization of the mirror symmetry map in the case of the flag manifold G ​ L 3 ​ ( ℂ ) / B GL_{3}(\mathbb{C})/B .



1
Introduction

Analytical objects like special functions as well as various
integral identities have been a continuing source of inspiration for
finding their hidden structures, explaining their (sometimes
mysterious) properties. Along this route, going from analysis to
algebra, we usually encounter new structures and understand better
the known ones. One of conceptual frameworks proved to be very
successful in uncovering hidden structures of special functions and
associated integral identities is provided by representation theory
of Lie groups and Lie algebras (see for example
[
V
]
), as well
as by their higher dimensional analogs given by mapping spaces into
the Lie groups. Often the analytical objects are arranged in series
resembling the series of classical Lie groups, which strongly
suggests representation theory background. Following this approach,
we are looking for interpretation of special functions as matrix
elements of representations of algebraic structures, so that
associated integral identities reflect basic structures of their
representation theory. In some cases this line of reasoning leads to
constructions of various generalizations/deformations of Lie
groups/algebras. General context for these considerations is
non-commutative geometry modeled via non-commutative harmonic
analysis on Lie groups.

First example of a class of (rather mysterious) integral identities
was found by Barnes, now known as the First Barnes Lemma
[
Ba
]
:

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\frac{1}{2\pi}\!\int\limits_{\mathbb{R}}\!d{\gamma}\,\prod\limits_{i=1}^{2}\Gamma(a_{i}-\imath{\gamma})\prod\limits_{j=1}^{2}\Gamma(b_{j}+\imath{\gamma})\,=\,\frac{\prod\limits_{i,j=1}^{2}\Gamma(a_{j}+b_{i})}{\Gamma\Big(\sum\limits_{i=1}^{2}a_{i}+b_{i}\Big)}\,,\quad{\rm Re}(a_{j},b_{i})>0\,.\end{array}

A bit later, identities of similar type were reported by Ramanujan
in his last paper published before moving to England
[
Ra
]
. This
line of development culminated in several series of identities found
by Gustafson
[
Gu
]
, in particular, (formula (9.4), for
n=1
)

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\frac{1}{2\pi}\!\int\limits_{\mathbb{R}}\!d\gamma\,\frac{\prod\limits_{i=1}^{4}\Gamma(a_{i}+\imath\gamma)\,\Gamma(a_{i}-\imath\gamma)}{\Gamma(2\imath\gamma)\,\Gamma(-2\imath\gamma)}\,=\,\frac{2\prod\limits_{i<j}\Gamma(a_{i}+a_{j})}{\Gamma(a_{1}+a_{2}+a_{3}+a_{4})}\,,\quad{\rm Re}(a_{i})>0\,.\end{array}

The Gustafson identities arise in series, which makes a relation
with representation theory of classical Lie groups quite plausible.
However to our best knowledge up to now, no a representation theory
interpretation of these identities is found, yet.

In this paper, we report on first steps towards such an
interpretation that has unexpectedly arisen in a problem of
identifying certain matrix elements of principle series
representations of the general linear Lie group in various
realizations. The considered realizations of representations of Lie
algebras by operators in some particular spaces are not arbitrary
but have deep meaning in terms of the Gelfand-Tsetlin construction
of bases in representations
[
GT
]
, positivity structures, as
well as torifications of flag manifolds. Hence one should expect
that thus arising identities reveal deep constructions in
representation theory. Precisely, the identities of this class arise
in the setting of
this paper as follows. We consider the
\mathfrak{gl}_{\ell+1}(\mathbb{R})
-Whittaker
function given by common eigenfunction of the

\mathfrak{gl}_{\ell+1}(\mathbb{R})
-Toda chain Hamiltonians, well-known
finite-dimensional (quantum) integrable system (see e.g.
[
STS
]

and references therein). This entails its identification as a
special matrix element of principle series representation

(\pi_{\mu},\,\mathcal{V}_{\mu})
of the universal enveloping algebra

\mathcal{U}(\mathfrak{gl}_{\ell+1})
of the Lie algebra

\mathfrak{gl}_{\ell+1}(\mathbb{R})
. The representation space
\mathcal{V}_{\mu}

allows various realizations as function spaces thus leading to
various explicit realizations of

\mathcal{U}(\mathfrak{gl}_{\ell+1})
-representations via
differential/difference operators. Two such functional realizations
having special properties were considered in
[
GKL
]
,
[
GKLO
]
.
One realization relies on the recursive (over the rank) construction
of maximal commuting subalgebras in
\mathcal{U}(\mathfrak{gl}_{\ell+1})

and generalizes the Gelfand-Tsetlin construction of bases in
finite-dimensional representations of
\mathfrak{gl}_{\ell+1}(\mathbb{C})
. This
realization has deep connections with the separation of variables
method for quantum integrable systems
[
Skl
]
and the associated
Hecke-Baxter operators
[
GLO08
]
. The other one is the
Gauss-Givental functional realization, which clarifies the works of
Givental on mirror symmetry for flag manifolds and his
integral representations of Whittaker functions
[
Giv
]
.
Along with applications to mirror
symmetry issues, this construction has deep relations with
the positivity structures in flag manifolds. Both considered
functional realizations of principle series representations lead to
corresponding integral representations for the

\mathfrak{gl}_{\ell+1}(\mathbb{R})
-Whittaker functions. In this paper, we
are interested in constructing the
operators intertwining these realizations thus allowing a direct
transformation of one integral representation into another. Our
main result is the construction of such operators for the first
non-trivial case of
\ell=2
(the quite trivial case of
\ell=1

is also considered). It is employing the intertwining operator to
identify the two realizations, we use the Barnes identity
(
1
) and (a degenerate form of) the Gustafson identity
(
1
) in essential way. We also construct a direct
integral transformation between the considered integral
representations of the
\mathfrak{gl}_{3}(\mathbb{R})
-Whittaker function
without reference to its matrix element structure. Here again the
identity (
1
) shows up inevitably. Although we consider
only the first non-trivial case of
\mathfrak{gl}_{3}(\mathbb{R})
, there are
clear hints of a possibility of a recursive (over the rank
\ell
)
construction of intertwining operators for

\mathfrak{gl}_{\ell+1}(\mathbb{R})
. It would be interesting to single out
thus arising integral identities.

There is a different perspective on the problem of explicit
identification of the two types of integral representations for the

\mathfrak{gl}_{\ell+1}(\mathbb{R})
-Whittaker functions (which was our primal
goal to start these investigations). Actually, such an identification
provides an important ingredient of explicit analytic mirror
map construction for flag manifolds. This line of research was started by
Givental
[
Giv
]
who constructed the complete set of linear
differential equations satisfied by the generating function in
certain topological quantum field theory associated with flag
manifolds for
GL_{\ell+1}(\mathbb{C})
. Namely, he identified these linear
differential operators with generators of the ring of quantum
Hamiltonians of the
\mathfrak{gl}_{\ell+1}(\mathbb{R})
-Toda chain and
provided an integral representation for their common
eigenfunctions. In
[
GKLO
]
, the Givental integral formula of
the
\mathfrak{gl}_{\ell+1}(\mathbb{R})
-Whittaker functions was rederived using
representation theory interpretation of the Toda chain via special
choice of realization of the underlying

\mathcal{U}(\mathfrak{gl}_{\ell+1})
-representation. Another type of integral
representation for the
\mathfrak{gl}_{\ell+1}(\mathbb{R})
-Whittaker function is
based on the Gelfand-Tsetlin realization, and it also has a proper
meaning in the topological quantum field theory framework. The
existence of these two forms of integral representations reflects
the dual way to interpret Whittaker functions via mirror symmetry of
the underlying topological quantum field theories associated with
flag manifolds
[
GLO11
]
.

In the case of toric varieties, the mirror symmetry map may be
constructed quite explicitly leading to two different types of
expressions for (deformed) generating function in the relevant
two-dimensional quantum field theories. Since in general flag
manifolds are not toric, one should first construct a toric
degeneration of the flag manifold and then apply mirror symmetry map
to the resulting toric manifold. The approach of
Strominger-Yau-Zaslow to mirror symmetry
[
SYZ
]
provides a
conceptual explanation for such considerations. The relevant
analytic structures are as follows. We start with the Givental
integral representation which relies on a torification of the flag
manifold
GL_{\ell+1}(\mathbb{C})/B
. Then the standard Fourier transform
applied to representation space gives rise to the mirror dual
integral representation given by the GKZ type integral (see

[
GG
]
and references therein). For the representation theoretic
account of the GKZ integrals and the relations with the Givental
integral formulas for the
\mathfrak{gl}_{\ell+1}(\mathbb{R})
-Whittaker functions,
see
[
GLO23
]
. Now, additional transformation (constructed
in this paper for the case of
\mathfrak{gl}_{3}(\mathbb{R})
) identifies
thus obtained integral representation with the Gelfand-Tsetlin one.
This establishes a connection of the torified flag manifolds with
representation theory constructions after Gelfand-Tsetlin. As a
consequence, we thus provide an important link between mirror
symmetry and representation theory of Yangians
[
GKL
]
.

Finally, let us point out that mirror symmetry in the context of
flag manifolds might be also considered as a manifestation of the
Langlands correspondence for Archimedean fields
[
GLO11
]
. It is
important to stress that here we are dealing with the analytical
form of the Langlands correspondence which not only identifies the
sets of parameters of the underlying representations but essentially
works at the level of analytic objects, like
L
-functions and
the Whittaker functions.

The plan of the paper is as follows. In Section 2 we start with
recalling briefly realizations of the

\mathfrak{gl}_{\ell+1}(\mathbb{R})
-Whittaker functions as special matrix
elements in principle series representations of

\mathcal{U}(\mathfrak{gl}_{\ell+1})
. We describe explicitly three
functional realizations of these representations: the
Gelfand-Tsetlin, the Gauss-Givental and the modified Gauss-Givental
realizations. These realizations lead to corresponding integral
expressions for the
\mathfrak{gl}_{\ell+1}(\mathbb{R})
-Whittaker
functions. In the following two sections we construct integral
operators that intertwine different realizations of representations
of
\mathcal{U}(\mathfrak{gl}_{\ell+1})
, for
\ell=1,2
. In particular
application of the intertwining operators to Whittaker vectors
provides an identification of integral expressions of the matrix
elements in the considered realizations. This is done in Section 3
for
\mathfrak{gl}_{2}(\mathbb{R})
and in Section 4 for

\mathfrak{gl}_{3}(\mathbb{R})
(Theorem
4.1
). The latter case
involves applications of the identity (
1
) and a
degenerate version of (
1
). Finally, in Section 5 a
direct identification of the two integral representations for the

\mathfrak{gl}_{2}(\mathbb{R})
- and
\mathfrak{gl}_{3}(\mathbb{R})
- Whittaker functions considered in
Section 2 via integral transform is given. The key step is done via
invoking the Gustafson identity (
1
) from
[
Gu
]
.
Details on the modified Gauss-Givental realization obtained via the
Fourier transform of the original Gauss-Givental realization are
given in Appendix A, while the main part of calculations for the

\mathfrak{gl}_{3}(\mathbb{R})
-intertwining operator is collected in
Appendix B.

2
Matrix element realizations of the
\mathfrak{gl}_{\ell+1}(\mathbb{R})
-Whittaker function

In this section we recall matrix element representation for the

\mathfrak{gl}_{\ell+1}(\mathbb{R})
-Whittaker function. This representation is
instrumental for constructing various integral representations of
the
\mathfrak{gl}_{\ell+1}(\mathbb{R})
-Whittaker function.

Recall from
[
Ko
]
the interpretation of Whittaker functions as
particular matrix elements of infinite-dimensional representations
of universal enveloping algebras of reductive Lie algebras.
For a real reductive Lie algebra
{\mathfrak{g}}
with a Cartan subalgebra

{\mathfrak{h}}\subset{\mathfrak{g}}
, let
\mathcal{U}({\mathfrak{g}})
be the universal enveloping algebra
of
{\mathfrak{g}}
. Let
\Phi({\mathfrak{g}})
be the root system of
{\mathfrak{h}}\subset{\mathfrak{g}}
,

\Phi_{+}({\mathfrak{g}})\subset\Phi({\mathfrak{g}})
be the subset of positive roots, let

\rho=\frac{1}{2}\sum\limits_{{\alpha}\in\Phi_{+}({\mathfrak{g}})}{\alpha}
, and let

\Pi({\mathfrak{g}})\subset\Phi({\mathfrak{g}})
be the set of simple roots. For

{\gamma}\in\mathbb{C}^{\operatorname{rank}({\mathfrak{g}})}
, given a principal series representation

(\pi_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho},\mathcal{V}_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho})
of
{\mathfrak{g}}
, we assume
that the
{\mathfrak{h}}
-action integrates to the action of the torus

A=\exp(\mathfrak{h})
, so that

(\pi_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho},\mathcal{V}_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho})
affords a structure of

({\mathfrak{g}},A)
-module. Let

(\pi_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho},\,\mathcal{V}_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho})
and

(\pi^{\vee}_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho},\,\mathcal{V}^{\vee}_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho})
be a
pair of dual
\mathcal{U}({\mathfrak{g}})
-modules with a sesquilinear pairing (anti

\mathbb{C}
-linear in the first argument and
\mathbb{C}
-linear in the second
argument), for
\phi_{1}\in\mathcal{V}_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho}^{\vee}
and

\phi_{2}\in\mathcal{V}_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho}
:

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\langle\,,\,\rangle\,:\quad\mathcal{V}_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho}^{\vee}\times\mathcal{V}_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho}\,\longrightarrow\,\mathbb{C}\,\,,\\
\langle\pi^{\vee}_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho}(X)\phi_{1},\,\phi_{2}\rangle\,=\,-\langle\phi_{1},\,\pi_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho}(X)\phi_{2}\rangle\,,\qquad\forall X\in{\mathfrak{g}}\,.\end{array}

The dual
\mathcal{U}({\mathfrak{g}})
-module allows the following
identification:

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\mathcal{V}^{\vee}_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho}\,\simeq\,\mathcal{V}_{-\imath\bar{{\gamma}}-\rho}\,.\end{array}

In the case
{\gamma}=\lambda\in\mathbb{R}^{\operatorname{rank}({\mathfrak{g}})}
, (
2
) defines a
non-degenerate
{\mathfrak{g}}
-invariant Hermitian pairing on

\mathcal{V}_{-\imath\lambda-\rho}
, thus providing a structure of unitary
representation on it.

Now, let
{\mathfrak{n}}_{\pm}\subset{\mathfrak{g}}
be the pair of opposite nilpotent
subalgebras corresponding to the choice of positive roots

\Phi_{+}({\mathfrak{g}})
. Introduce the pair of Whittaker vectors

w_{L}\in\mathcal{V}^{\vee}_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho}
and
w_{R}\in\mathcal{V}_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho}

to be non-degenerate characters of the nilpotent subalgebras

{\mathfrak{n}}_{-}\subset{\mathfrak{g}}
and
{\mathfrak{n}}_{+}\subset{\mathfrak{g}}
, respectively. Explicitly,
in terms of the Chevalley-Weyl generators

e_{\alpha_{j}},\,f_{\alpha_{j}},\,\alpha_{j}\in\Pi({\mathfrak{g}})
of

{\mathfrak{n}}_{\pm}\subset{\mathfrak{g}}
, the Whittaker vectors
w_{L}
,
w_{R}
are
defined as solutions of the following equations:

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\pi^{\vee}_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho}(f_{\alpha_{j}})\cdot w_{L}\,=\,-w_{L}\,,\qquad\pi_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho}(e_{\alpha_{j}})\cdot w_{R}\,=\,-w_{R}\,,\qquad\alpha_{j}\in\Pi({\mathfrak{g}})\,.\end{array}

Let us introduce the following matrix element in

(\pi_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho},\mathcal{V}_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho})
defined as a function on

A=\exp({\mathfrak{h}})
:

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\Phi_{{\gamma}}(e^{x})\,=\,\bigl\langle w_{L},\,\pi_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho}\bigl(e^{-\sum\limits_{i=1}^{\operatorname{rank}({\mathfrak{g}})}\varpi_{i}(x)h_{i}}\bigr)w_{R}\bigr\rangle\,,\qquad{\gamma}\in\mathbb{C}^{\operatorname{rank}({\mathfrak{g}})},\quad e^{x}\in A\,,\end{array}

where
\varpi_{i}\in{\mathfrak{h}}^{*}
are the fundamental weights of
{\mathfrak{g}}
. The
matrix element (
2
) gives rise to the
{\mathfrak{g}}
-Whittaker
function,

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\Psi_{{\gamma}}(e^{x})\,=\,e^{-\rho(x)}\,\Phi_{{\gamma}}(e^{x})\,=\,e^{-\rho(x)}\,\bigl\langle w_{L},\,\pi_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho}\bigl(e^{-\sum\limits_{i=1}^{\operatorname{rank}({\mathfrak{g}})}\varpi_{i}(x)h_{i}}\bigr)w_{R}\bigr\rangle\,,\end{array}

which is a solution to the quantum
{\mathfrak{g}}
-Toda chain
[
Ko
]
. The
action of the quadratic quantum
{\mathfrak{g}}
-Toda Hamiltonian is defined
via

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\mathcal{H}_{2}\cdot\Psi_{{\gamma}}(e^{x})\,=\,e^{-\rho(x)}\,\bigl\langle w_{L},\,\pi_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho}\bigl(e^{-\sum\limits_{i=1}^{\operatorname{rank}({\mathfrak{g}})}\varpi_{i}(x)h_{i}}\,c_{2}\,\bigr)w_{R}\bigr\rangle\,,\end{array}

where
c_{2}\in\mathcal{Z}\mathcal{U}(\mathfrak{gl}_{\ell+1})
is a degree two element of the
center of the enveloping algebra. For a special choice of
c_{2}
, we
have

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\mathcal{H}_{2}\,=\,-\frac{1}{2}\sum_{i=1}^{\operatorname{rank}({\mathfrak{g}})}\frac{\partial^{2}}{\partial x_{i}^{2}}\,+\,\sum_{\alpha_{j}\in\Pi({\mathfrak{g}})}e^{\alpha_{j}(x)}\,.\end{array}

In the rest of this Section, we restrict ourselves to the case

{\mathfrak{g}}=\mathfrak{gl}_{\ell+1}(\mathbb{R})
. We consider two different realizations of
the principal series
\mathcal{U}(\mathfrak{gl}_{\ell+1})
-representation

(\pi_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho},\,\mathcal{V}_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho})
introduced in

[
GKL
]
and
[
GKLO
]
that we refer to as the Gelfand-Tsetlin
and the Gauss-Givental realizations, respectively. In addition, we
provide another version of the Gauss-Givental realization obtained
via the Fourier transform, that we refer to as the modified
Gauss-Givental realization. Note that in the Gelfand-Tsetlin and in
the modified Gauss-Givental realizations, the

\mathfrak{gl}_{\ell+1}(\mathbb{R})
-generators are represented by the first-order
difference operators; meanwhile in the Gauss-Givental realization,
the
\mathfrak{gl}_{\ell+1}(\mathbb{R})
-generators are represented by the
first-order differential operators. In each case, we introduce an
explicit form of the pairing (
2
) and the corresponding
integral formulas the matrix element (
2
). Thus, in the
Gelfand-Tsetlin realization, the Mellin-Barnes integral
representation arises. On the other hand, the Gauss-Givental
realization leads to the Givental integral representation

[
Giv
]
,
[
JK
]
. The modified Gauss-Givental realization leads
to yet another integral representation of the

\mathfrak{gl}_{\ell+1}(\mathbb{R})
-Whittaker function.

2.1
The Gelfand-Tsetlin realization

According to
[
KL
]
, the
\mathfrak{gl}_{\ell+1}(\mathbb{R})
-Whittaker function
allows the following integral representation of the Mellin-Barnes
type:

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\Psi_{{\gamma}_{1},\ldots,{\gamma}_{\ell+1}}(e^{x_{1}},\ldots,e^{x_{\ell+1}})\,=\,\frac{1}{(2\pi)^{\frac{\ell(\ell+1)}{2}}}\!\int\limits_{\mathcal{C}}\prod_{k=1}^{\ell}\prod_{i=1}^{k}d{\gamma}_{k,i}\,e^{\imath\sum\limits_{k=1}^{\ell+1}\bigl(\sum\limits_{i}{\gamma}_{k,i}-\sum\limits_{j}{\gamma}_{k-1,j}\bigr)x_{k}}\\
\times\prod_{n=1}^{\ell}\frac{\prod\limits_{i=1}^{n+1}\prod\limits_{j=1}^{n}\Gamma\bigl(\imath{\gamma}_{n+1,i}-\imath{\gamma}_{n,j}\bigr)}{\prod\limits_{a<b}\bigl|\Gamma(\imath{\gamma}_{n,a}-\imath{\gamma}_{n,b})\bigr|^{2}}\,,\end{array}

where
{\gamma}_{\ell+1,i}={\gamma}_{i}\,,1\leq i\leq\ell+1
and the terms

{\gamma}_{k,j},\,j>k
are omitted when occur. The integration domain

\mathcal{C}\subset\mathbb{C}^{\frac{\ell(\ell+1)}{2}}
in (
2.1
) satisfies
the following:

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}{\rm Im}({\gamma}_{k,j})\,>\,\max_{1\leq i\leq k+1}\bigl\{{\rm Im}({\gamma}_{k+1,\,i})\bigr\}\,,\qquad 1\leq j\leq k\leq\ell\,.\end{array}

Upon choosing a special solution to (
2.1
), for

\tau_{k,j}\in\mathbb{R},\,1\leq j\leq k\leq\ell
,

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}{\gamma}_{k,j}\,=\,\tau_{k,j}\,+\,\imath\frac{\ell+1-k}{2}\,,\end{array}

we obtain the following form of integral
representation (see (5.4) in
[
GKL
]
)

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\Psi_{{\gamma}_{1},\ldots,{\gamma}_{\ell+1}}(e^{x_{1}},\ldots,e^{x_{\ell+1}})\,=\,\frac{e^{-\rho(x)}}{(2\pi)^{\frac{\ell(\ell+1)}{2}}}\!\int\limits_{\mathbb{R}^{\frac{\ell(\ell+1)}{2}}}\prod_{k=1}^{\ell}\prod_{i=1}^{k}d\tau_{k,i}\\
\times\,e^{\imath\sum\limits_{k=1}^{\ell+1}\bigl(\sum\limits_{i}\tau_{k,i}-\sum\limits_{j}\tau_{k-1,j}\bigr)x_{k}}\prod_{n=1}^{\ell}\frac{\prod\limits_{i=1}^{n+1}\prod\limits_{j=1}^{n}\Gamma\bigl(\imath(\tau_{n+1,i}-\tau_{n,j})+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)}{\prod\limits_{a<b}\bigl|\Gamma(\imath\tau_{n,a}-\imath\tau_{n,b})\bigr|^{2}}\,,\end{array}

where the terms
\tau_{k,j},\,j>k
are omitted when occur and we
make the identification
\tau_{\ell+1,i}:={\gamma}_{i}\in\mathbb{C},\,1\leq i\leq\ell+1
. Recall that for
{\mathfrak{g}}={\mathfrak{g}}_{\ell+1}(\mathbb{R})
, the half-sum
of positive roots
\rho=(\rho_{1},\ldots,\rho_{\ell+1})
is given by

\rho_{i}=\frac{\ell}{2}+1-i
.

By
[
GKL
]
the integral expression (
2.1
) is obtained as
a matrix element in a particular realization a principal series

\mathfrak{gl}_{\ell+1}(\mathbb{R})
-representation

(\pi_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho},\mathcal{V}_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho})
labeled by

{\gamma}=({\gamma}_{1},\ldots,{\gamma}_{\ell+1})\in\mathbb{C}^{\ell+1}
. This realization
(called the Gelfand-Tsetlin realization in
[
GKL
]
) is defined as follows. The
images
E_{ij}=\pi_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho}(e_{ij})

of the elementary matrix generators
e_{ij}
,
1\leq i,j\leq\ell+1

of
\mathfrak{gl}_{\ell+1}(\mathbb{R})
acting in the space of functions in
variables
\tau_{k,i}\in\mathbb{R},\,1\leq i\leq k\leq\ell
are given by

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}E_{i,i}\,=\,-\imath\Big(\sum_{k=1}^{i}\tau_{i,k}\,-\,\sum_{j=1}^{i-1}\tau_{i-1,j}\Big)\,,\qquad 1\leq i\leq\ell+1\,,\\
E_{i,\,i+1}\,=\,\imath\sum_{k=1}^{i}\frac{\prod\limits_{j=1}^{i+1}\bigl(\tau_{i,k}-\tau_{i+1,j}-\frac{\imath}{2}\bigr)}{\prod\limits_{l\neq j}(\tau_{i,k}-\tau_{i,l})}\,e^{-\imath\partial_{\tau_{i,k}}}\,,\qquad 1\leq i\leq\ell\,,\\
E_{i+1,\,i}\,=\,-\imath\sum_{k=1}^{i}\frac{\prod\limits_{j=1}^{i-1}\bigl(\tau_{i,k}-\tau_{i-1,j}+\frac{\imath}{2}\bigr)}{\prod\limits_{l\neq j}(\tau_{i,k}-\tau_{i,l})}\,e^{\imath\partial_{\tau_{i,k}}}\,,\qquad 1\leq i\leq\ell\,,\end{array}

where the terms
\tau_{k,j},\,j>k
are omitted when occur and

\tau_{\ell+1,k}:={\gamma}_{k}\in\mathbb{C},1\leq k\leq\ell+1
. The proper
subspace in the space of functions in

should be defined as the space of functions obtained by
actions of polynomials in generators (
2.1
) on the Whittaker
vectors defined below. The corresponding functional models for
representations
\mathcal{V}_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho},\,\mathcal{V}^{\vee}_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho}

of

allows the duality pairing

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\langle\psi_{1},\,\psi_{2}\rangle_{GT}\,:=\!\int\limits_{\mathbb{R}^{\frac{\ell(\ell+1)}{2}}}\!\prod_{k,i}d\tau_{k,i}\,\,\mu(\tau)\,\,\overline{\psi_{1}(\tau)}\,\psi_{2}(\tau)\,,\end{array}

where

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\mu(\tau)\,=\,(2\pi)^{-\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\ell}\frac{n(n+1)}{2}}\prod_{n=2}^{\ell}\prod_{a<b}(\tau_{n,a}-\tau_{n,b})\bigl(e^{2\pi\tau_{n,a}}-e^{2\pi\tau_{n,b}}\bigr)\\
=\,\frac{1}{(2\pi)^{\frac{\ell(\ell+1)}{2}}}\prod_{n=2}^{\ell}\prod_{a<b}\frac{e^{\pi(\tau_{n,a}+\tau_{n,b})}}{\bigl|\Gamma(\imath\tau_{n,a}-\imath\tau_{n,b})\bigr|^{2}}\,.\end{array}

The difference operators (

) satisfy, for vectors

\phi_{1}\in\mathcal{V}^{\vee}_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho}
and
\phi_{2}\in\mathcal{V}_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho}

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\langle E_{ij}^{\vee}\phi_{1},\,\phi_{2}\rangle_{GT}\,=\,-\langle\phi_{1},\,E_{ij}\phi_{2}\rangle_{GT}\,,\qquad 1\leq i,j\leq\ell+1\,.\end{array}

The defining relations (
2
) on the Whittaker vectors

\psi_{R}\in\mathcal{V}_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho}
and

\psi_{L}\in\mathcal{V}^{\vee}_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho}
allow the following
solutions:

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\psi_{L}(\tau)\,=\,\prod_{n=1}^{\ell}\prod_{j=1}^{n}e^{-\frac{\pi}{2}\tau_{n,j}}\,,\end{array}

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\psi_{R}(\tau)\,=\,e^{-\pi\sum\limits_{k=1}^{\ell}(n-1)\sum\limits_{i=1}^{k}\tau_{k,i}}\prod_{n=1}^{\ell}\prod_{j=1}^{n}e^{\frac{\pi}{2}\tau_{n,j}}\prod_{i=1}^{n+1}\Gamma\Big(\imath(\tau_{n+1,i}-\tau_{n,j})+\frac{1}{2}\Big)\,.\end{array}

Combining all ingredients in (

representation (

) of the

\mathfrak{gl}_{\ell+1}(\mathbb{R})
-Whittaker function:

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}e^{-\rho(x)}\langle\psi_{L},\,e^{-\sum\limits_{i=1}^{\ell+1}x_{i}E_{ii}}\psi_{R}\rangle_{GT}\,=\,\frac{e^{-\rho(x)}}{(2\pi)^{\frac{\ell(\ell+1)}{2}}}\!\int\limits_{\mathbb{R}^{\frac{\ell(\ell+1)}{2}}}\prod_{k=1}^{\ell}\prod_{i=1}^{k}d\tau_{k,i}\\
\times\,e^{\imath\sum\limits_{k=1}^{\ell+1}\bigl(\sum\limits_{i}\tau_{k,i}-\sum\limits_{j}\tau_{k-1,j}\bigr)x_{k}}\prod_{n=1}^{\ell}\frac{\prod\limits_{i=1}^{n+1}\prod\limits_{j=1}^{n}\Gamma\bigl(\imath(\tau_{n+1,i}-\tau_{n,j})+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)}{\prod\limits_{a<b}\bigl|\Gamma(\imath\tau_{n,a}-\imath\tau_{n,b})\bigr|^{2}}\,,\end{array}

with
\tau_{\ell+1,i}:={\gamma}_{i}\,,1\leq i\leq\ell+1
.

Let us notice that since the
\mathfrak{gl}_{\ell+1}
-generators act
via difference operators (
2.1
) in the space of functions in
variables
\{\tau_{k,i}\}
, there is a large commutant of this
action containing periodic functions in
\{\tau_{k,i}\}
. This
reflects the possibility to pick different solutions to the
equations (
2
) on Whittaker vectors via multiplying the
expressions in (
2.1
),(
2.1
) by periodic
functions:

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\psi_{L}\longmapsto{\sigma}_{L}\psi_{L}\,,\qquad\psi_{R}\longmapsto{\sigma}_{R}\psi_{R}\,,\\
{\sigma}_{L}(\tau\pm\imath)={\sigma}_{L}(\tau)\,,\quad{\sigma}_{R}(\tau\pm\imath)={\sigma}_{R}(\tau)\,.\end{array}

Specialization of the proper subspaces

\mathcal{V}_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho},\,\mathcal{V}^{\vee}_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho}
in the
functional space corresponds to a choice of a model of the principle
series representation, so that in (
2.1
), (
2.1
) a
particular choice is made. However, in the following it will be
convenient not to fix this choice beforehand and impose only the
condition on
{\sigma}_{L},\,{\sigma}_{R}
to satisfy the relation

\overline{{\sigma}_{L}}\times{\sigma}_{R}=1
. This guarantees that the corresponding
matrix elements coincide with the integral expression for the matrix
element (
2.1

2.2
The Gauss-Givental realization

In this subsection we recall from
[
GKLO
]
a construction of
another integral representation of the

\mathfrak{gl}_{\ell+1}(\mathbb{R})
-Whittaker function via representation theory.
This integral representation first introduced by Givental

[

[

is given by

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\Psi_{{\gamma}_{1},\ldots,{\gamma}_{\ell+1}}(e^{x_{1}},\ldots,e^{x_{\ell+1}})\,=\,\int\limits_{\Gamma}\prod_{k=1}^{\ell}\prod_{i=1}^{k}dT_{k,i}\,e^{\mathcal{F}_{{\gamma}}(T)},\end{array}

where

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\mathcal{F}_{{\gamma}}(T)\,=\sum_{k=1}^{\ell+1}\imath{\gamma}_{k}\Big(\sum_{i=1}^{k}T_{k,i}-\sum_{i=1}^{k-1}T_{k-1,i}\Big)\\
-\,\sum_{k=1}^{\ell}\sum_{i=1}^{k}\Big(e^{T_{k+1,i}-T_{k,i}}+e^{T_{k,i}-T_{k+1,i+1}}\Big)\,.\end{array}

The integration domain
\Gamma
in (
2.2
), is a middle
dimensional submanifold in
\mathbb{C}^{\frac{\ell(\ell+1)}{2}}

such that the integral converges. In the following we
make a particular choice
\Gamma=\mathbb{R}^{\frac{\ell(\ell+1)}{2}}
.

By
[
GKLO
]

\mathfrak{gl}_{\ell+1}(\mathbb{R})
-representation

(\pi_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho},\mathcal{V}_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho})
labeled by

, the Gauss-Givental
realization of

is
defined by the first-order differential operators acting in the
space of Schwartz functions in variables
T_{k,i},\,1\leq i\leq k\leq\ell
. Precisely, the following expressions provide a
realization of the principal series

\mathfrak{gl}_{\ell+1}(\mathbb{R})
-representation

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\mathcal{E}_{i,i}\,=\,-\imath{\gamma}_{i}\,-\,\sum_{k=i}^{\ell}\frac{\partial}{\partial T_{k,i}}\,+\,\sum_{k=1}^{i-1}\frac{\partial}{\partial T_{\ell+1+k-i,k}}\,,\qquad 1\leq i\leq\ell+1\,,\\
\mathcal{E}_{i,\,i+1}\,=\,\sum_{n=1}^{i}\left(\,\sum_{k=n}^{i}e^{T_{\ell+1+k-i,k}-T_{\ell+k-i,k}}\right)\!\!\Big(\frac{\partial}{\partial T_{\ell+n-i,n}}-\frac{\delta_{n,1}}{2}-\frac{\partial}{\partial T_{\ell+n-i,n-1}}\Big)\,,\\
\mathcal{E}_{i+1,\,i}\,=\,\sum_{k=i}^{\ell}e^{T_{k,i}-T_{k+1,i+1}}\Big\{\imath({\gamma}_{i+1}-{\gamma}_{i})-1+\frac{\delta_{i,\ell}}{2}\\
+\,\sum_{s=i}^{k}\Big(\frac{\partial}{\partial T_{s,i+1}}+\frac{\delta_{s,1}}{2}-\frac{\partial}{\partial T_{s,i}}\Big)\Big\}\,,\end{array}

where the operators
\mathcal{E}_{i,i+1}
and
\mathcal{E}_{i+1,i}
are defined for

1\leq i\leq\ell
. In (
2.2
), we impose
T_{\ell+1,k}=0
,
also for
k<j
the terms
T_{k,j}
and
\partial/\partial T_{k,j}
are omitted
(when occur). Let us stress that the expressions (
2.2
)
differ from the ones in
[
GKLO
]
via conjugation by

\prod\limits_{k,i}e^{T_{k,i}/2}
. The pairing (
2
) in the
Gauss-Givental representation is given by the standard

L^{2}
-pairing:

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\langle\phi_{1},\,\phi_{2}\rangle\,=\int\limits_{\mathbb{R}^{\frac{\ell(\ell+1)}{2}}}\!\prod_{k,i}dT_{k,i}\,\overline{\phi_{1}(T)}\,\phi_{2}(T)\,.\end{array}

The defining relations (
2
) on the Whittaker vectors

\phi_{R}\in\mathcal{V}_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho}
and

\phi_{L}\in\mathcal{V}^{\vee}_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho}

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\phi_{L}(T)\,=\,\prod_{k=1}^{\ell}e^{[\imath(\bar{{\gamma}}_{k+1}-\bar{{\gamma}}_{k})-\frac{1}{2}]\sum\limits_{i=1}^{k}T_{k,i}\,-\sum\limits_{i=1}^{k}e^{T_{k+1,i}-T_{k,i}}}\,,\end{array}

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\phi_{R}(T)\,=\,\prod_{k=1}^{\ell}\prod_{i=1}^{k}e^{\frac{T_{k,i}}{2}\,-\,e^{T_{k,i}-T_{k+1,i+1}}}\,.\end{array}

Now the integral (
2.2
) coincides with the matrix element
representation in the Gauss-Givental realization:

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\Psi_{{\gamma}_{1},\ldots,{\gamma}_{\ell+1}}(e^{x_{1}},\ldots,e^{x_{\ell+1}})\,=\,e^{-\rho(x)}\langle\phi_{L},\,e^{-\sum\limits_{i=1}^{\ell+1}x_{i}\mathcal{E}_{ii}}\phi_{R}\rangle\,.\end{array}

In the following we consider
yet one more realization of the principal series

\mathcal{U}(\mathfrak{gl}_{\ell+1})
-representation.
This realization is straightforwardly obtained from the

Lemma 3.1

Let
\psi_{L},\,\psi_{R}
be the Whittaker vectors given by
(
3.1

\varkappa\in\mathbb{R}
subjected

\varkappa<\frac{1}{2}-\varepsilon
, the following holds:

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\langle\psi_{L},e^{-\sum x_{i}{{}^{\varkappa}\!\widetilde{E}}_{ii}}\psi_{R}\rangle_{\varkappa}\,=\,\langle\psi_{L},e^{-\sum x_{i}E_{ii}}\psi_{R}\rangle_{GT}\,.\end{array}

Proof
. We need to show that for
\varkappa<\frac{1}{2}-\varepsilon
the following
holds:

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\int\limits_{\mathbb{R}-\imath\varkappa}\!\!d\tau\,\bar{\psi_{1}}(\tau)\,\psi_{2}(\tau)\,-\,\int\limits_{\mathbb{R}}\!d\tau\,\bar{\psi_{1}}(\tau)\,\psi_{2}(\tau)\,=\!\int\limits_{\mathcal{C}_{\varkappa}}\!d\tau\,\bar{\psi_{1}}(\tau)\,\psi_{2}(\tau)=\,0\,,\end{array}

where
\mathcal{C}_{\varkappa}=\mathbb{R}\sqcup(\mathbb{R}-\imath\varkappa)
and

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\psi_{1}(\tau)\,=\,\psi_{L}(\tau)=e^{-\frac{\pi\tau}{2}}\,,\\
\psi_{2}(\tau)\,=\,e^{-\sum x_{i}E_{ii}(\tau)}\psi_{R}(\tau)\,=e^{\frac{\pi\tau}{2}}e^{\imath\tau(x_{1}-x_{2})+\imath({\gamma}_{1}+{\gamma}_{2})x_{2}}\,\prod_{i=1}^{2}\Gamma\Big(\imath({\gamma}_{i}-\tau)+\frac{1}{2}\Big)\,,\end{array}

with
{\gamma}=\lambda+2\imath\varepsilon\rho
, so that the integrand function reads

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}f(\tau)\,=\,\overline{\psi}_{1}(\tau)\,\psi_{2}(\tau)\,=\,e^{\imath\tau x_{1}+\imath({\gamma}_{1}+{\gamma}_{2}-\tau)x_{2}}\prod_{i=1}^{2}\Gamma\Big(\imath({\gamma}_{i}-\tau)+\frac{1}{2}\Big)\,.\end{array}

Consider the contour

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\mathcal{C}_{\varkappa,R}\,=\,[-R;R]\cup[R;R-\imath\varkappa]\cup[-R-\imath\varkappa;R-\imath\varkappa]\cup[-R;-R-\imath\varkappa]\,,\end{array}

then we claim that

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\lim_{R\to+\infty}\int\limits_{-R-\imath\varkappa}^{-R}\!d\tau\,f(\tau)\,=\lim_{R\to+\infty}\int\limits_{R-\imath\varkappa}^{R}\!d\tau\,f(\tau)\,=\,0\,.\end{array}

Indeed, we apply the following version of the Stirling asymptotic
formula (see
[
BE
]
, 1.18, formula (6)),

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\bigl|\Gamma(s+\imath t)\bigr|\,\sim\,\sqrt{2\pi}\,e^{-\frac{\pi}{2}\,|t|}\,|t|^{s-\frac{1}{2}}\,,\quad|t|\to\infty\,,\end{array}

which implies

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}|f(\tau)|\,=\,\prod_{i=1}^{2}\Big|\Gamma\Big(\imath({\gamma}_{i}-\tau)+\frac{1}{2}\Big)\Big|\,\sim\,2\pi\,e^{-\frac{\pi}{2}|{\gamma}_{1}-\tau|-\frac{\pi}{2}|{\gamma}_{2}-\tau|}\,,\quad|\tau|\to\infty\,.\end{array}

Hence the integrals over vertical parts give zero contributions,
therefore

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\lim_{R\to+\infty}\int\limits_{-R}^{R}\!d\tau\,f(\tau)\,=\lim_{R\to+\infty}\int\limits_{-R-\imath\varkappa}^{R-\imath\varkappa}\!d\tau\,f(\tau)\,.\end{array}

Next,
f(\tau)
has the poles, all below the real line

\mathbb{R}\subset\mathbb{C}
, since
{\gamma}=\lambda+2\imath\varepsilon\rho
with

0<\varepsilon<\frac{1}{2}
:

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\tau\,=\,{\gamma}_{i}-\imath\Big(n+\frac{1}{2}\Big)\,=\,\lambda_{i}\,+\,\imath\Big(2\varepsilon\rho_{i}-n-\frac{1}{2}\Big)\,,\quad n\geq 0\,,\quad i=1,2\,.\end{array}

None of the poles are sitting inside the strip bounded by

\mathcal{C}_{\varkappa}
if and only if
\varkappa<\frac{1}{2}-\varepsilon
, hence we may
safely deform the integration contour to establish the equivalence
of the integral expressions in (
3.1
).
\Box

Thus, for
\varkappa<\frac{1}{2}-\varepsilon
by Lemma
3.1
, we
arrive at the following matrix element representation for the

\mathfrak{gl}_{2}(\mathbb{R})
-Whittaker function:

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\Psi_{\lambda_{1}+\imath\varepsilon,\lambda_{2}-\imath\varepsilon}(e^{x_{1}},e^{x_{2}})\,=\,e^{-\rho(x)}\langle\psi_{L},\,e^{-x_{1}{{}^{\varkappa}\!\widetilde{E}}_{11}-x_{2}{{}^{\varkappa}\!\widetilde{E}}_{22}}\psi_{R}\rangle_{\varkappa}\\
=\,\frac{e^{(\varkappa-\frac{1}{2})(x_{1}-x_{2})}}{2\pi}\!\int\limits_{\mathbb{R}}\!d\tau\,e^{\imath\tau x_{1}+\imath(\lambda_{1}+\lambda_{2}-\tau)x_{2}}\\
\times\,\Gamma\Big(\imath(\lambda_{1}-\tau)-\varepsilon-\varkappa+\frac{1}{2}\Big)\,\Gamma\Big(\imath(\lambda_{2}-\tau)+\varepsilon-\varkappa+\frac{1}{2}\Big)\,.\end{array}

This reproduces a more general integral expression for the
matrix element (
3
) in representation theory framework.

3.2
The Gauss-Givental realization

Now we compare the integral representation (
3.1
) for

\mathfrak{gl}_{2}(\mathbb{R})
-Whittaker function considered above with the
corresponding modified Givental integral representation
(
2.2
). By (
2.1
) for
\ell=1
, the modified
Gauss-Givental realization of the principle series

\mathfrak{gl}_{2}(\mathbb{R})
-representation

(\pi_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho},\mathcal{V}_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho})
is given by

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\hat{\mathcal{E}}_{11}=-\imath({\gamma}_{1}+s)\,,\qquad\hat{\mathcal{E}}_{22}=-\imath({\gamma}_{2}-s)\,,\\
\hat{\mathcal{E}}_{12}=\Big(\imath s+\frac{1}{2}\Big)\,e^{-\imath\partial_{s}}\,,\qquad\hat{\mathcal{E}}_{21}=\Big(\imath({\gamma}_{2}-{\gamma}_{1}-s)+\frac{1}{2}\Big)\,e^{\imath\partial_{s}}\,.\end{array}

By (
2
), the dual representation satisfies

\mathcal{V}^{\vee}_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho}\simeq\mathcal{V}_{-\imath\bar{{\gamma}}-\rho}
, so
that

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\hat{\mathcal{E}}^{\vee}_{11}=-\imath(\bar{{\gamma}}_{1}+s)\,,\qquad\hat{\mathcal{E}}^{\vee}_{22}=-\imath(\bar{{\gamma}}_{2}-s)\,,\\
\hat{\mathcal{E}}^{\vee}_{12}=\Big(\imath s+\frac{1}{2}\Big)\,e^{-\imath\partial_{s}}\,,\qquad\hat{\mathcal{E}}^{\vee}_{21}=\Big(\imath(\bar{{\gamma}}_{2}-\bar{{\gamma}}_{1}-s)+\frac{1}{2}\Big)\,e^{\imath\partial_{s}}\,.\end{array}

Then for
{\gamma}=\lambda+2\imath\varepsilon\rho
, the corresponding Whittaker
vectors are given by (
2.2
),(
2.2
) for
\ell=1
:

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\hat{\phi}_{L}(s)=\,\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}\,\Gamma\Big(\imath(\lambda_{1}-\lambda_{2}+s)+2\varepsilon+\frac{1}{2}\Big)\,,\quad\hat{\phi}_{R}(s)\,=\,\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}\,\Gamma\Big(\frac{1}{2}-\imath s\Big)\,,\end{array}

and the resulting integral representation of the

\mathfrak{gl}_{2}(\mathbb{R})
-Whittaker function is as follows

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\Psi_{\lambda_{1}+\imath\varepsilon,\,\lambda_{2}-\imath\varepsilon}(e^{x_{1}},e^{x_{2}})\,=\,e^{-\rho(x)}\langle\hat{\phi}_{L},\,e^{-x_{1}\hat{\mathcal{E}}_{11}-x_{2}\hat{\mathcal{E}}_{22}}\hat{\phi}_{R}\rangle\\

\times\,\Gamma\Big(-\imath(\lambda_{1}-\lambda_{2}+s)+2\varepsilon+\frac{1}{2}\Big)\,\Gamma\Big(-\imath s+\frac{1}{2}\Big)\,.\end{array}

The integrals (
3.2
) and (

) can be

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}s\,:=\,\tau-\lambda_{1}\,\in\,\mathbb{R}\,,\quad\text{for}\quad\varkappa=-\varepsilon\,.\end{array}

Although the transformation (

) is quite trivial, we would
like to represent it by an ope-rator intertwining the two
realizations of the principal series representation

(\pi_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho},\mathcal{V}_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho})
.

3.3
Intertwining operators

The form of the intertwining operator may be easily inferred by
comparing Whittaker vectors in the two realizations of the

\mathfrak{gl}_{2}(\mathbb{R})
-representation

\mathcal{V}_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho},\,{\gamma}=\lambda+2\imath\varepsilon\rho,\,\lambda\in\mathbb{R}^{2}
. The
standard delta-function is defined by

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\int\limits_{\mathbb{R}}\!ds\,\,\delta(s-\tau)\,\phi(s)\,=\,\phi(\tau)\,,\qquad\phi\in\mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R})\,.\end{array}

Introduce the delta-function of complex argument defined by (see
e.g.
[
GS
]
)

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\int\limits_{\mathbb{R}}\!ds\,\,\delta(s-\tau+\imath\varepsilon)\,\phi(s)\,=\,e^{-\imath\varepsilon\partial_{\tau}}\!\int\limits_{\mathbb{R}}\!ds\,\,\delta(s-\tau)\,\phi(s)\,=\,\phi(\tau-\imath\varepsilon)\,.\end{array}

The corresponding space of test functions consists of Schwartz
functions allowing analytic continuation into the strip bounded by

\mathbb{R}
and
\mathbb{R}-\imath\varepsilon
. Now, introduce the operators
\mathcal{B}_{R}
and

\mathcal{B}_{L}
, acting in
\mathcal{V}_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho}
and

\mathcal{V}_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho}^{\vee}\simeq\mathcal{V}_{-\imath\bar{{\gamma}}-\rho}
,
respectively, defined by their integral kernels:

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}B_{R}(\tau;s)\,=\,e^{\frac{\pi\tau}{2}}\,\Gamma\Big(\imath({\gamma}_{2}-{\gamma}_{1}-s)+\frac{1}{2}\Big)\,\delta({\gamma}_{1}+s-\tau)\\
=\,e^{\frac{\pi\tau}{2}}\,\Gamma\Big(\imath(\lambda_{2}-\lambda_{1}-s)+2\varepsilon+\frac{1}{2}\Big)\,\delta(\lambda_{1}+s-\tau+\imath\varepsilon)\,,\end{array}

and

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}B_{L}(\tau;s)\,=\,\frac{e^{-\frac{\pi\tau}{2}}}{\Gamma\bigl(\imath(\bar{{\gamma}}_{1}-\bar{{\gamma}}_{2}+s)+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)}\,\delta(\bar{{\gamma}}_{1}+s-\tau)\\
=\,\frac{e^{-\frac{\pi\tau}{2}}}{\Gamma\bigl(\imath(\lambda_{1}-\lambda_{2}+s)+2\varepsilon+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)}\,\delta(\lambda_{1}+s-\tau-\imath\varepsilon)\,.\end{array}

Let
\mathcal{B}_{L}^{{\dagger}}
be the adjoint operator w.r.t. the standard

L^{2}
-pairing, then its kernel reads

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}B_{L}^{{\dagger}}(s;\tau)\,=\,\overline{B_{L}(\tau;s)}\,=\,\frac{e^{-\frac{\pi\tau}{2}}\,}{\Gamma\bigl(\imath({\gamma}_{2}-{\gamma}_{1}-s)+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)}\,\delta({\gamma}_{1}+s-\tau)\\
=\,\frac{e^{-\pi\tau}}{\Gamma\bigl(\imath({\gamma}_{2}-\tau)+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)^{2}}\,\times\,B_{R}(\tau;s)\,,\end{array}

and we have
\mathcal{B}_{L}^{{\dagger}}=\mathcal{B}_{R}^{-1}
.

Lemma 3.2

The operators
\mathcal{B}_{R},\,\mathcal{B}_{L}
and
\mathcal{B}^{{\dagger}}_{L}
possess the
following intertwining properties:

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}E_{ij}\circ\mathcal{B}_{R}\,=\,\mathcal{B}_{R}\circ\hat{\mathcal{E}}_{ij}\,,\qquad E^{\vee}_{ij}\circ\mathcal{B}_{L}\,=\,\mathcal{B}_{L}\circ\hat{\mathcal{E}}^{\vee}_{ij}\,,\\
\hat{\mathcal{E}}_{ij}\circ\mathcal{B}^{{\dagger}}_{L}\,=\,\mathcal{B}_{L}^{{\dagger}}\circ E_{ij}\,,\qquad 1\leq i,j\leq 2\,.\end{array}

Proof
. The intertwining relations for the Cartan generators
E_{ii}

and
\hat{\mathcal{E}}_{ii}
follow from (
3.2
) due to the presence
of the delta-factors
\delta({\gamma}_{1}+s-\tau)
and

\delta(\bar{{\gamma}}_{1}+s-\tau)
in (
3.3
) and (
3.3
),
respectively. Relations for
E_{ij},\hat{\mathcal{E}}_{ij},\,i\neq j
and

E^{\vee}_{ij},\hat{\mathcal{E}}^{\vee}_{ij},\,i\neq j
can be verified by
direct computations.
\Box

Lemma 3.3

The operators
\mathcal{B}_{R},\,\mathcal{B}_{L}
and
\mathcal{B}^{{\dagger}}_{L}
satisfy the
following:

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\mathcal{B}_{R}\cdot\hat{\phi}_{R}\,=\,\psi_{R}\,,\qquad\mathcal{B}_{L}\cdot\hat{\phi}_{L}\,=\,\psi_{L}\,,\\
(\mathcal{B}^{{\dagger}}_{L}\circ\mathcal{B}_{R})\cdot\hat{\phi}_{R}\,=\,\mathcal{B}^{{\dagger}}_{L}\cdot\psi_{R}\,=\,\hat{\phi}_{R}\,.\end{array}

Proof
. All the three identities can be easily verified in
straightforward way.
\Box

Now we identify the realizations (
3.1
) and
(
3.2
) of the
\mathfrak{gl}_{2}(\mathbb{R})
-matrix element as follows:

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\langle\psi_{L},\,e^{-\sum x_{i}E_{ii}}\psi_{R}\rangle_{GT}\\
=\,\langle\mathcal{B}_{L}\hat{\phi}_{L},\,e^{-\sum x_{i}E_{ii}}\mathcal{B}_{R}\hat{\phi}_{R}\rangle_{GT}\,=\,\langle\hat{\phi}_{L},\,\mathcal{B}^{{\dagger}}_{L}\,e^{-\sum x_{i}E_{ii}}\mathcal{B}_{R}\hat{\phi}_{R}\rangle\\
=\,\langle\hat{\phi}_{L},\,e^{-\sum x_{i}\hat{\mathcal{E}}_{ii}}\mathcal{B}^{{\dagger}}_{L}\circ\mathcal{B}_{R}\hat{\phi}_{R}\rangle\,=\,\langle\hat{\phi}_{L},\,e^{-\sum x_{i}\hat{\mathcal{E}}_{ii}}\hat{\phi}_{R}\rangle\,.\end{array}

where we apply the third identity in Lemma
3.2
providing

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\mathcal{B}^{{\dagger}}_{L}\,e^{-\sum x_{i}E_{ii}}(\mathcal{B}^{{\dagger}}_{L})^{-1}\,=\,e^{-\sum x_{i}\hat{\mathcal{E}}_{ii}}\,.\end{array}

It is instructive to consider the special case of
{\gamma}=\lambda\in\mathbb{R}^{2}

in (
3
), that is setting
\varepsilon=0
. Upon this choice, the

\mathfrak{gl}_{2}(\mathbb{R})
-representation
\mathcal{V}_{-\imath\lambda-\rho}
has unitary
structure and both sets of
\mathfrak{gl}_{2}(\mathbb{R})
-generators (
3.1
)
and (
3.2
) are Hermitian w.r.t. the appropriate pairings.
The transformation between the two realizations of
matrix elements constructed above
does not however take into account the unitary structure of the
underlying representation
\mathcal{V}_{-\imath\lambda-\rho}
. To rectify this,
let us introduce the following operator:

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\mathcal{N}\,:=\,e^{\imath{\mathop{\rm Arg}}\bigl\{\Gamma\bigl(\imath(\lambda_{2}-\tau)+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)\bigr\}}\,e^{-\lambda_{1}\partial_{\tau}}\,=\,{\sigma}_{\lambda_{2}}(\tau)\times\mathcal{B}_{R}\,=\,\frac{1}{{\sigma}_{\lambda_{2}}(\tau)}\times\mathcal{B}_{L}\,,\end{array}

where
{\sigma}_{\lambda_{2}}(\tau)
is the
\imath
-periodic function in
\tau

given by

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}{\sigma}_{\lambda_{2}}(\tau)\,=\,\frac{e^{-\frac{\pi\tau}{2}}}{\Big|\Gamma\bigl(\imath(\lambda_{2}-\tau)+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)\Big|}\,=\,\frac{e^{-\frac{\pi\lambda_{2}}{2}}}{\sqrt{2\pi}}\,\sqrt{1+e^{2\pi(\lambda_{2}-\tau)}}\,.\end{array}

Proposition 3.1

The operator
\mathcal{N}
is the unitary operator intertwining the two
realizations of the
\mathfrak{gl}_{2}(\mathbb{R})
-representation

\mathcal{V}_{-\imath\lambda-\rho}
given by (
3.1
) and
(
3.2
):

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}E_{ij}\circ\mathcal{N}\,=\,\mathcal{N}\circ\hat{\mathcal{E}}_{ij}\,,\qquad 1\leq i,j\leq 2\,.\end{array}

The
\mathcal{N}
-action on the Whittaker vectors (
3.2
) reads

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\mathcal{N}\cdot\hat{\phi}_{R}\,=\,{\sigma}_{\lambda_{2}}\,\psi_{R}\,,\qquad\mathcal{N}\cdot\hat{\phi}_{L}\,=\,\frac{1}{{\sigma}_{\lambda_{2}}}\,\psi_{L}\,\,.\end{array}

Proof
. By (
3.3
), the operator
\mathcal{N}
has the following
integral kernel:

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}N(\tau;s)\,=\,\frac{\Gamma\bigl(\imath(\lambda_{2}-\tau)+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)}{\Big|\Gamma\bigl(\imath(\lambda_{2}-\tau)+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)\Big|}\,\delta(\lambda_{1}+s-\tau)\\
=\,e^{\imath{\mathop{\rm Arg}}\bigl(\Gamma\bigl(\imath(\lambda_{2}-\tau)+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)\bigr)}\,\delta(\lambda_{1}+s-\tau)\,.\end{array}

The intertwining relations (
3.1
) follow from
(
3.2
) in straightforward way. The relations
(
3.1
) follow from (
3.3
) and the identity
(
3.3
).
\Box

The unitary operator
\mathcal{N}
provides a unitary transformation
identifying the two realizations of
\mathfrak{gl}_{2}(\mathbb{R})
-matrix elements as
follows:

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\langle\psi_{L},\,e^{-\sum x_{i}E_{ii}}\psi_{R}\rangle_{GT}\,=\,\langle{\sigma}_{\lambda_{2}}^{-1}\psi_{L},\,e^{-\sum x_{i}E_{ii}}\,{\sigma}_{\lambda_{2}}\,\psi_{R}\rangle_{GT}\\
=\,\langle\mathcal{N}\hat{\phi}_{L},\,e^{-\sum x_{i}E_{ii}}\mathcal{N}\hat{\phi}_{R}\rangle\,=\,\langle\hat{\phi}_{L},\,\mathcal{N}^{{\dagger}}\,e^{-\sum x_{i}E_{ii}}\mathcal{N}\hat{\phi}_{R}\rangle\\
=\,\langle\hat{\phi}_{L},\,e^{-\sum x_{i}\hat{\mathcal{E}}_{ii}}\mathcal{N}^{{\dagger}}\circ\mathcal{N}\hat{\phi}_{R}\rangle\,=\,\langle\hat{\phi}_{L},\,e^{-\sum x_{i}\hat{\mathcal{E}}_{ii}}\hat{\phi}_{R}\rangle\\
=\,\langle\phi_{L},\,e^{-\sum x_{i}\mathcal{E}_{ii}}\phi_{R}\rangle\,.\end{array}

4
Identification of matrix element realizations for
\mathfrak{gl}_{3}(\mathbb{R})

In this section we give a representation-theoretic identification of
the two integral formulas for the
\mathfrak{gl}_{3}(\mathbb{R})
-Whittaker function
provided by the two realizations of principal series

\mathcal{U}(\mathfrak{gl}_{3})
-representation
\mathcal{V}_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho}
, the

For
{\gamma}=({\gamma}_{1},{\gamma}_{2},{\gamma}_{3})\in\mathbb{C}^{3}
, consider the principal series

\mathcal{U}(\mathfrak{gl}_{3})
-representation

(\pi_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho},\,\mathcal{V}_{-\imath{\gamma}-\rho})

\times\,e^{\imath({\gamma}_{21}+{\gamma}_{22})(x_{2}-x_{3})+\imath{\gamma}_{11}(x_{1}-x_{2})}\prod_{j=1}^{2}\Gamma(\imath{\gamma}_{2,j}-\imath{\gamma}_{11})\,\prod\limits_{i=1}^{3}\Gamma(\imath{\gamma}_{i}-\imath{\gamma}_{2,j})\,,\end{array}

where the integration domain
\mathcal{C}
is subjected to the following
conditions

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}{\rm Im}({\gamma}_{11})\,>\,\max\bigl\{{\rm Im}({\gamma}_{21}),\,{\rm Im}({\gamma}_{22})\bigr\}\,,\\
{\rm Im}({\gamma}_{2j})\,>\,\max\bigl\{{\rm Im}({\gamma}_{1}),\,{\rm Im}({\gamma}_{2}),\,{\rm Im}({\gamma}_{3})\bigr\}\,,\quad j=1,2\,.\end{array}

We specify the representation parameters

{\gamma}=({\gamma}_{1},{\gamma}_{2},{\gamma}_{3})\in\mathbb{C}^{3}
in terms of

\lambda=(\lambda_{1},\lambda_{2},\lambda_{3})\in\mathbb{R}^{3}
as follows:

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}{\gamma}\,:=\,\lambda+\imath\varepsilon{\alpha}_{1}\,,\quad{\alpha}_{1}=(1,-1,0),\end{array}

for some fixed
\varepsilon
such that
0<\varepsilon<\frac{1}{2}
.
Then (
4
) takes the following form:

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\Psi_{\lambda_{1}+\imath\varepsilon,\,\lambda_{2}-\imath\varepsilon,\lambda_{3}}(e^{x_{1}},e^{x_{2}},e^{x_{3}})\,=\,\frac{e^{\imath(\lambda_{1}+\lambda_{2}+\lambda_{3})x_{3}}}{(2\pi)^{3}}\!\int\limits_{\mathcal{C}}\!\frac{d{\gamma}_{11}d{\gamma}_{21}d{\gamma}_{22}}{\Gamma(\imath{\gamma}_{21}-\imath{\gamma}_{22})\,\Gamma(\imath{\gamma}_{22}-\imath{\gamma}_{21})}\\
\times\,e^{\imath({\gamma}_{21}(x_{2}-x_{3})+{\gamma}_{22})+\imath{\gamma}_{11}(x_{1}-x_{2})}\,\prod_{j=1}^{2}\Gamma(\imath{\gamma}_{21}-\imath{\gamma}_{11})\\
\times\prod\limits_{j=1}^{2}\Gamma\bigl(\imath\lambda_{1}-\imath{\gamma}_{2,j}-\varepsilon\bigr)\,\Gamma\bigl(\imath\lambda_{2}-\imath{\gamma}_{2,j}+\varepsilon\bigr)\,\Gamma\bigl(\imath\lambda_{3}-\imath{\gamma}_{2,j}\bigr)\,,\end{array}

where the integration domain
\mathcal{C}
is defined by

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}{\rm Im}({\gamma}_{11})>\max\bigl\{{\rm Im}({\gamma}_{21}),\,{\rm Im}({\gamma}_{22})\bigr\},\qquad{\rm Im}({\gamma}_{2j})>\varepsilon,\quad j=1,2\,.\end{array}

By (
2.1
), we specify the integration domain
\mathcal{C}
to be
parameterized by
\tau_{k,i}\in\mathbb{R}
as follows

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}{\gamma}_{11}=\tau_{11}\,+\,\imath,\quad{\gamma}_{2,j}=\tau_{2,j}+\frac{\imath}{2}\,,\quad j=1,2\,,\end{array}

so that after substitution, (
4
) becomes

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\Psi_{\lambda_{1}+\imath\varepsilon,\,\lambda_{2}-\imath\varepsilon,\lambda_{3}}(e^{x_{1}},e^{x_{2}},e^{x_{3}})\,=\,\frac{e^{\imath(\lambda_{1}+\lambda_{2}+\lambda_{3})x_{3}-x_{1}+x_{3}}}{(2\pi)^{3}}\!\int\limits_{\mathbb{R}^{3}}\!d\tau_{11}d\tau_{21}d\tau_{22}\\
\times\,\frac{e^{\imath(\tau_{21}+\tau_{22})(x_{2}-x_{3})+\imath\tau_{11}(x_{1}-x_{2})}}{\Gamma(-\imath\tau_{21}+\imath\tau_{22})\,\Gamma(\imath\tau_{21}-\imath\tau_{22})}\,\prod_{j=1}^{2}\Gamma\Big(\imath(\tau_{21}-\tau_{11})+\frac{1}{2}\Big)\\
\times\prod\limits_{j=1}^{2}\Gamma\Big(\imath(\lambda_{1}-\tau_{2,j})-\varepsilon+\frac{1}{2}\Big)\,\Gamma\Big(\imath\lambda_{2}-\tau_{2,j})+\varepsilon+\frac{1}{2}\Big)\,\Gamma\Bigl(\imath\lambda_{3}-\tau_{2,j})+\frac{1}{2}\Big)\,.\end{array}

The Gelfand-Tsetlin realization

) for

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\widetilde{E}_{11}=-\imath\tau_{11},\qquad\widetilde{E}_{22}=-\imath(\tau_{21}+\tau_{22})+\imath\tau_{11},\\

\times\prod_{j=1}^{2}\frac{\Gamma\bigl(-\imath(\tau_{11}-\tau_{2,j}-s_{21})\bigr)}{\Gamma\big(\imath(\tau_{2,j}-\tau_{11})+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)\,\Gamma\bigl(\imath({\gamma}_{3}-\tau_{2,j})+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)}\\
\times\,\delta({\gamma}_{1}+s_{11}+s_{21}-\tau_{11})\,\delta\bigl({\gamma}_{1}+{\gamma}_{2}+s_{11}+s_{22}-\tau_{21}-\tau_{22}\bigr)\,.\end{array}

The intertwining relations satisfied by
C_{L}
to be checked are
given by the following Lemma.

Lemma 7.2

The intertwining relations (
7.2
) are equivalent to the
following identity:

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\hat{\mathcal{E}}_{ij}\cdot C_{L}(s;\tau)\,=\,E^{\prime}_{ij}\cdot C_{L}(s;\tau)\,,\qquad 1\leq i,j\leq 3\,,\end{array}

where the operators
E^{\prime}_{ij}
are given by

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}E^{\prime}_{11}=-\imath\tau_{11},\qquad E^{\prime}_{22}=-\imath(\tau_{21}+\tau_{22})+\imath\tau_{11},\\
E^{\prime}_{33}=-\imath({\gamma}_{1}+{\gamma}_{2}+{\gamma}_{3})+\imath(\tau_{21}+\tau_{22}),\\
E^{\prime}_{12}=-\imath\Big(\imath(\tau_{11}-\tau_{21})-\frac{1}{2}\Big)\Big(\imath(\tau_{11}-\tau_{22})-\frac{1}{2}\Big)\,e^{\imath\partial_{\tau_{11}}},\\
E^{\prime}_{23}=-\imath\Big\{\frac{\prod\limits_{i=1}^{3}\bigl(\imath\tau_{21}-\imath{\gamma}_{i}-\frac{1}{2}\bigr)}{\imath\tau_{21}-\imath\tau_{22}-1}\,e^{\imath\partial_{\tau_{21}}}\,+\,\frac{\prod\limits_{j=1}^{3}\bigl(\imath\tau_{22}-\imath{\gamma}_{i}-\frac{1}{2}\bigr)}{\imath\tau_{22}-\imath\tau_{21}-1}\,e^{\imath\partial_{\tau_{22}}}\Big\}\,,\\
E^{\prime}_{21}=-\imath e^{-\imath\partial_{\tau_{11}}}\,,\\
E^{\prime}_{32}=\imath\Big\{\frac{\imath\tau_{11}-\imath\tau_{21}-\frac{1}{2}}{\imath\tau_{21}-\imath\tau_{22}+1}\,e^{-\imath\partial_{\tau_{21}}}\,+\,\frac{\imath\tau_{11}-\imath\tau_{22}-\frac{1}{2}}{\imath\tau_{22}-\imath\tau_{21}+1}\,e^{-\imath\partial_{\tau_{22}}}\Big\}\,.\end{array}

Proof
. Similar to the proof of Lemma (
7.1
).
\Box

Similarly to (
7.1
), the operators (
7.2
)
satisfy the “opposite” commutation relations:

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}[E^{\prime}_{ij}\,,E^{\prime}_{k,l}]\,=\,-[E_{ij},\,E_{kl}]^{\prime}\,,\qquad 1\leq i,j,k,l\leq 3\,.\end{array}

Verification of the intertwining relations
(
7.2
) for the operator
\mathcal{C}_{L}
proceeds as follows.

For the Cartan generators
E_{ii}
and
\mathcal{E}_{ii}
, the
relations (
7.2
) hold due to the presence of the
delta-factor in
C_{L}(s;\,\tau)
,

\delta({\gamma}_{1}+s_{11}+s_{21}-\tau_{11})\,\delta\bigl({\gamma}_{1}+{\gamma}_{2}+s_{11}+s_{22}-\tau_{21}-\tau_{22}\bigr)\,.

The relation
\hat{\mathcal{E}}_{12}\cdot C_{L}(s;\tau)=E^{\prime}_{12}\cdot C_{L}(s;\,\tau)
follows since

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\hat{\mathcal{E}}_{12}\cdot C_{L}(s;\tau)\,=\,\Big(\imath s_{21}+\frac{1}{2}\Big)\,C_{L}(s_{11},s_{21}-\imath,s_{22};\,\tau)\\
=\,-\frac{e^{-\frac{\pi\tau_{11}}{2}}}{\Gamma\bigl(\imath\tau_{21}-\imath\tau_{22}\bigr)\,\Gamma\bigl(-\imath\tau_{21}+\imath\tau_{22}\bigr)}\,\frac{\Gamma\bigl(-\imath s_{21}+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)}{\Gamma\bigl(-\imath({\gamma}_{1}-{\gamma}_{2}+s_{11})+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)}\\
\times\,\sqrt{2\pi}\prod_{j=1}^{2}\frac{-\imath(\tau_{11}-\tau_{2,j}-s_{21})\,\Gamma\bigl(-\imath(\tau_{11}-\tau_{2,j}-s_{21})\bigr)}{\Gamma\big(\imath(\tau_{2,j}-\tau_{11})+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)\,\Gamma\bigl(\imath({\gamma}_{3}-\tau_{2,j})+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)}\\
\times\,\delta({\gamma}_{1}+s_{11}+s_{21}-\tau_{11}-\imath)\,\delta\bigl({\gamma}_{1}+{\gamma}_{2}+s_{11}+s_{22}-\tau_{21}-\tau_{22}\bigr)\end{array}

equals to

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}E^{\prime}_{12}\cdot C_{L}(s;\,\tau)\,=\,-\imath\prod_{j=1}^{2}\Big(\imath(\tau_{11}-\tau_{2,j})-\frac{1}{2}\Big)\,C_{L}(s;\,\tau_{11}+\imath,\tau_{21},\tau_{22})\\
=\,-\frac{e^{-\frac{\pi\tau_{11}}{2}}}{\Gamma\bigl(\imath\tau_{21}-\imath\tau_{22}\bigr)\,\Gamma\bigl(-\imath\tau_{21}+\imath\tau_{22}\bigr)}\,\frac{\Gamma\bigl(-\imath s_{21}+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)}{\Gamma\bigl(-\imath({\gamma}_{1}-{\gamma}_{2}+s_{11})+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)}\\
\times\,\sqrt{2\pi}\prod_{j=1}^{2}\frac{-\imath(\tau_{11}-\tau_{2,j}-s_{21})\,\Gamma\bigl(-\imath(\tau_{11}-\tau_{2,j}-s_{21})\bigr)}{\Gamma\big(\imath(\tau_{2,j}-\tau_{11})+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)\,\Gamma\bigl(\imath({\gamma}_{3}-\tau_{2,j})+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)}\\
\times\,\delta({\gamma}_{1}+s_{11}+s_{21}-\tau_{11}-\imath)\,\delta\bigl({\gamma}_{1}+{\gamma}_{2}+s_{11}+s_{22}-\tau_{21}-\tau_{22}\bigr)\,.\end{array}

For the relation
\hat{\mathcal{E}}_{21}\cdot C_{L}(s;\tau)=E^{\prime}_{21}\cdot C_{L}(s;\,\tau)
, we have

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\hat{\mathcal{E}}_{21}\cdot C_{L}(s;\tau)\,=\,\Big(\imath({\gamma}_{2}-{\gamma}_{1}-s_{11})+\frac{1}{2}\Big)\,C(s_{11}+\imath,s_{21},s_{22}-\imath;\,\tau)\\
+\,\Big(\imath({\gamma}_{2}-{\gamma}_{1}-s_{11}-s_{21}+s_{22})+\frac{1}{2}\Big)\,C(s_{11},s_{21}+\imath,s_{22};\,\tau)\\
=\,\sqrt{2\pi}\,\Big\{-\Big(\imath({\gamma}_{2}-{\gamma}_{1}-s_{11}-s_{21}+s_{22})-\frac{1}{2}\Big)\Big(\imath s_{21}+\frac{1}{2}\Big)\\
+\prod\limits_{j=1}^{2}\bigl(\imath(\tau_{11}-\tau_{2,j}-s_{21})-1\bigr)\Big\}\frac{e^{-\frac{\pi\tau_{11}}{2}}}{\Gamma\bigl(\imath\tau_{21}-\imath\tau_{22}\bigr)\,\Gamma\bigl(-\imath\tau_{21}+\imath\tau_{22}\bigr)}\\
\times\frac{\Gamma\bigl(-\imath s_{21}+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)}{\Gamma\bigl(-\imath({\gamma}_{1}-{\gamma}_{2}+s_{11})+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)}\prod_{j=1}^{2}\frac{\Gamma\bigl(-\imath(\tau_{11}-\tau_{2,j}-s_{21})\bigr)}{\Gamma\big(\imath(\tau_{2,j}-\tau_{11})+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)\,\Gamma\bigl(\imath({\gamma}_{3}-\tau_{2,j})+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)}\\
\times\,\delta({\gamma}_{1}+s_{11}+s_{21}-\tau_{11}+\imath)\,\delta\bigl({\gamma}_{1}+{\gamma}_{2}+s_{11}+s_{22}-\tau_{21}-\tau_{22}\bigr)\end{array}

and

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}E^{\prime}_{21}\cdot C_{L}(s;\,\tau)\,=\,-\imath\,C_{L}(s;\,\tau_{11}-\imath,\tau_{21},\tau_{22})\\
=\,\frac{\sqrt{2\pi}\,e^{-\frac{\pi\tau_{11}}{2}}}{\Gamma\bigl(\imath\tau_{21}-\imath\tau_{22}\bigr)\,\Gamma\bigl(-\imath\tau_{21}+\imath\tau_{22}\bigr)}\,\frac{\Gamma\bigl(-\imath s_{21}+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)}{\Gamma\bigl(-\imath({\gamma}_{1}-{\gamma}_{2}+s_{11})+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)}\\
\times\prod_{j=1}^{2}\frac{\imath(\tau_{2,j}-\tau_{11})+\frac{1}{2}}{\imath(\tau_{11}-\tau_{2,j}-s_{21})-1}\frac{\Gamma\bigl(-\imath(\tau_{11}-\tau_{2,j}-s_{21})\bigr)}{\Gamma\big(\imath(\tau_{2,j}-\tau_{11})+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)\,\Gamma\bigl(\imath({\gamma}_{3}-\tau_{2,j})+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)}\\
\times\,\delta({\gamma}_{1}+s_{11}+s_{21}-\tau_{11}+\imath)\,\delta\bigl({\gamma}_{1}+{\gamma}_{2}+s_{11}+s_{22}-\tau_{21}-\tau_{22}\bigr)\,,\end{array}

so that the equality follows by (
7.1
).

The relation
\hat{\mathcal{E}}_{32}\cdot C_{L}(s;\tau)=E^{\prime}_{32}\cdot C_{L}(s;\,\tau)
follows due to

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\hat{\mathcal{E}}_{32}\cdot C_{L}(s;\tau)\,=\,\Big(\imath({\gamma}_{3}-{\gamma}_{2}-s_{22})+\frac{1}{2}\Big)\,C_{L}(s_{11},s_{21},s_{22}+\imath;\,\tau)\\
=\,\frac{e^{-\frac{\pi\tau_{11}}{2}}\,\bigl(\imath({\gamma}_{3}-{\gamma}_{2}-s_{22})+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)}{\Gamma\bigl(\imath\tau_{21}-\imath\tau_{22}\bigr)\,\Gamma\bigl(-\imath\tau_{21}+\imath\tau_{22}\bigr)}\,\frac{\Gamma\bigl(-\imath s_{21}+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)}{\Gamma\bigl(-\imath({\gamma}_{1}-{\gamma}_{2}+s_{11})+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)}\\
\times\,\sqrt{2\pi}\prod_{j=1}^{2}\frac{\Gamma\bigl(-\imath(\tau_{11}-\tau_{2,j}-s_{21})\bigr)}{\Gamma\big(\imath(\tau_{2,j}-\tau_{11})+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)\,\Gamma\bigl(\imath({\gamma}_{3}-\tau_{2,j})+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)}\\
\times\,\delta({\gamma}_{1}+s_{11}+s_{21}-\tau_{11})\,\delta\bigl({\gamma}_{1}+{\gamma}_{2}+s_{11}+s_{22}-\tau_{21}-\tau_{22}+\imath\bigr)\,,\end{array}

so that

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}E^{\prime}_{32}\cdot C_{L}(s;\,\tau)\,=\,\imath\Big\{\frac{\imath(\tau_{11}-\tau_{21})-\frac{1}{2}}{\imath(\tau_{21}-\tau_{22})+1}\,C_{L}(s;\,\tau_{11},\tau_{21}-\imath,\tau_{22})\\
-\,\frac{\imath(\tau_{11}-\tau_{22})-\frac{1}{2}}{\imath(\tau_{22}-\tau_{21})+1}\,C_{L}(s;\,\tau_{11},\tau_{21},\tau_{22}+\imath)\Big\}\\
=\,\frac{1}{\imath(\tau_{21}-\tau_{22})}\Big\{-\imath(\tau_{11}-s_{21}-\tau_{21})\,\Big(\imath({\gamma}_{3}-\tau_{21})+\frac{1}{2}\Big)\\
+\,\imath(\tau_{11}-s_{21}-\tau_{22})\,\Big(\imath({\gamma}_{3}-\tau_{21})+\frac{1}{2}\Big)\Big\}\\
\times\,\frac{e^{-\frac{\pi\tau_{11}}{2}}}{\Gamma\bigl(\imath\tau_{21}-\imath\tau_{22}\bigr)\,\Gamma\bigl(-\imath\tau_{21}+\imath\tau_{22}\bigr)}\,\frac{\Gamma\bigl(-\imath s_{21}+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)}{\Gamma\bigl(-\imath({\gamma}_{1}-{\gamma}_{2}+s_{11})+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)}\\
\times\,\sqrt{2\pi}\prod_{j=1}^{2}\frac{\Gamma\bigl(-\imath(\tau_{11}-\tau_{2,j}-s_{21})\bigr)}{\Gamma\big(\imath(\tau_{2,j}-\tau_{11})+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)\,\Gamma\bigl(\imath({\gamma}_{3}-\tau_{2,j})+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)}\\
\times\,\delta({\gamma}_{1}+s_{11}+s_{21}-\tau_{11})\,\delta\bigl({\gamma}_{1}+{\gamma}_{2}+s_{11}+s_{22}-\tau_{21}-\tau_{22}+\imath\bigr)\\
=\,\Big(\imath({\gamma}_{3}-{\gamma}_{2}-s_{22})+\frac{1}{2}\Big)\,C_{L}(s_{11},s_{21},s_{22}+\imath;\,\tau)\,=\,\hat{\mathcal{E}}_{32}\cdot C_{L}(s;\tau)\,,\end{array}

similarly to (
7.1
).

As for the relation
\hat{\mathcal{E}}_{23}\cdot C_{L}(s;\tau)=E^{\prime}_{23}\cdot C_{L}(s;\,\tau)
, on the one hand, we have

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}\hat{\mathcal{E}}_{23}\cdot C_{L}(s;\tau)\,=\,\Big(\imath s_{11}+\frac{1}{2}\Big)\,C_{L}(s_{11}-\imath,s_{21}+\imath,s_{22};\,\tau)\\
+\,\Big(\imath(s_{11}-s_{21}+s_{22})+\frac{1}{2}\Big)\,C_{L}(s_{11},s_{21},s_{22}-\imath;\,\tau)\\
=\,\Big\{-\frac{\bigl(\imath s_{11}-\frac{1}{2}\bigr)\,\bigl(\imath s_{21}+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)\,\bigl(\imath({\gamma}_{2}-{\gamma}_{1}-s_{11})+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)}{\bigl(\imath(\tau_{11}-\tau_{21}-s_{21})-1\bigr)\,\bigl(\imath(\tau_{11}-\tau_{22}-s_{21})-1\bigr)}\\
+\,\imath(s_{11}-s_{21}+s_{22})-\frac{1}{2}\Big\}\,\frac{e^{-\frac{\pi\tau_{11}}{2}}}{\Gamma\bigl(\imath\tau_{21}-\imath\tau_{22}\bigr)\,\Gamma\bigl(-\imath\tau_{21}+\imath\tau_{22}\bigr)}\\
\times\frac{\Gamma\bigl(-\imath s_{21}+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)}{\Gamma\bigl(-\imath({\gamma}_{1}-{\gamma}_{2}+s_{11})+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)}\\
\times\,\sqrt{2\pi}\prod_{j=1}^{2}\frac{\Gamma\bigl(-\imath(\tau_{11}-\tau_{2,j}-s_{21})\bigr)}{\Gamma\big(\imath(\tau_{2,j}-\tau_{11})+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)\,\Gamma\bigl(\imath({\gamma}_{3}-\tau_{2,j})+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)}\\
\times\,\delta({\gamma}_{1}+s_{11}+s_{21}-\tau_{11})\,\delta\bigl({\gamma}_{1}+{\gamma}_{2}+s_{11}+s_{22}-\tau_{21}-\tau_{22}-\imath\bigr)\,,\end{array}

and on the other hand,

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{cc}E^{\prime}_{23}\cdot C_{L}(s;\,\tau)\,=\,-\imath\Big\{\frac{\prod\limits_{i=1}^{3}\bigl(\imath\tau_{21}-\imath{\gamma}_{i}-\frac{1}{2}\bigr)}{\imath\tau_{21}-\imath\tau_{22}-1}\,C_{L}(s;\,\tau_{11},\tau_{21}+\imath,\tau_{22})\\
+\,\frac{\prod\limits_{j=1}^{3}\bigl(\imath\tau_{22}-\imath{\gamma}_{i}-\frac{1}{2}\bigr)}{\imath\tau_{22}-\imath\tau_{21}-1}\,C_{L}(s;\,\tau_{11},\tau_{21},\tau_{22}+\imath)\Big\}\\
=\,\frac{1}{\imath(\tau_{21}-\tau_{22})}\Big\{\frac{\imath(\tau_{21}-\tau_{11})+\frac{1}{2}}{\imath(\tau_{11}-\tau_{21}-s_{21})-1}\prod_{i=1}^{2}\Big(\imath(\tau_{21}-{\gamma}_{i})+\frac{1}{2}\Big)\\
-\,\frac{\imath(\tau_{22}-\tau_{11})+\frac{1}{2}}{\imath(\tau_{11}-\tau_{22}-s_{21})-1}\prod_{i=1}^{2}\Big(\imath(\tau_{22}-{\gamma}_{i})+\frac{1}{2}\Big)\Big\}\\
\times\,\frac{e^{-\frac{\pi\tau_{11}}{2}}}{\Gamma\bigl(\imath\tau_{21}-\imath\tau_{22}\bigr)\,\Gamma\bigl(-\imath\tau_{21}+\imath\tau_{22}\bigr)}\,\frac{\Gamma\bigl(-\imath s_{21}+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)}{\Gamma\bigl(-\imath({\gamma}_{1}-{\gamma}_{2}+s_{11})+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)}\\
\times\,\sqrt{2\pi}\prod_{j=1}^{2}\frac{\Gamma\bigl(-\imath(\tau_{11}-\tau_{2,j}-s_{21})\bigr)}{\Gamma\big(\imath(\tau_{2,j}-\tau_{11})+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)\,\Gamma\bigl(\imath({\gamma}_{3}-\tau_{2,j})+\frac{1}{2}\bigr)}\\
\times\,\delta({\gamma}_{1}+s_{11}+s_{21}-\tau_{11})\,\delta\bigl({\gamma}_{1}+{\gamma}_{2}+s_{11}+s_{22}-\tau_{21}-\tau_{22}-\imath\bigr)\,.\end{array}

Then applying the substitution (
7.1
) we deduce the required
equality via (
7.1
).
