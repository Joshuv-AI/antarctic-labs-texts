Tunneling radiation of fermions from the non-stationary Kerr black hole
Qun-Chao Ding 1,2,, Zhong-Wen Feng 1,2,, Shu-Zheng Yang 1,2

Abstract
In this paper, the tunneling radiation of fermions with spin 1 / 2 1/2 and 3 / 2 3/2 from the non-stationary Kerr black hole are investigated. First, according to the Dirac equation for spin 1 / 2 1/2 fermions and Rarita-Schwinger equation for spin 3 / 2 3/2 fermions, we construct the corresponding gamma matrixes and the derive Hamilton-Jacobi equation for spin 1 / 2 1/2 and 3 / 2 3/2 fermions. Then, the tunneling behavior of fermions on the event horizon is studied. Finally, we obtain the thermodynamic properties of the non-stationary Kerr black hole. The result shows that the tunneling radiation rate, surface gravity and temperature are all related to r ˙ H {\dot{r}_{H}} and r H ′ {r^{\prime}_{H}} .
