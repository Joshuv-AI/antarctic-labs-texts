Abstract

We deduce bilateral interrelations between Fourier coefficients for lacunar trigonometric series and
modulus of their continuity. We obtain also as an application some conditions for continuity and discontinuity for
Gaussian periodic stationary random centered processes.

Key words and phrases.
Lacunar and superlacunar trigonometric series and functions, necessary condition and
sufficient one, modulus of continuity, Fourier coefficients, ordinary and complex functions, Gaussian random variable
and stationary random processes, convergence, estimation.

1
Introduction. Lacunar and superlacunar trigonometric series.

Let
\ \vec{n}=\{n(k)\},\ k=\pm 1,\pm 2,\pm 3,\ \ldots\
be a set of integer numbers, positive as well
as negative, such that
\ n(1)\geq 0,\ n(-1)\leq 0,\

n(k+1)\geq n(k)+1,\ k\geq 1;\hskip 8.53581ptn(k-1)\leq n(k)-1,\ k<0.

(1)

Let also
\ \vec{c}=\{c(k)\},\ k\in\vec{n}\
be a numerical absolute summable numerical sequence

\sum_{k\in\vec{n}}|c(k)|<\infty.

(2)

Define the following
\ 2\pi\
periodical continuous function by means of absolute convergent Fourier series

f(t)=f[\vec{n},\vec{c}](t)\stackrel{{\scriptstyle def}}{{=}}\sum_{k\in\vec{n}}c(k)\ \exp(i\ t\ n(k)).\ t\in[-\pi,\pi].

(3)

Of course,

c(k)=(2\pi)^{-1}\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}\ \exp(-i\ t\ n(k))\ f(t)\ dt.

Recall that the series of the form (
3
) are named
lacunar,
iff

\underline{\lim}_{k\to\infty}\left[\ \frac{n(k+1)}{n(k)}\ \right]>1.

(4)

and

\underline{\lim}_{k\to\infty}\left[\ \frac{|n(-k-1)|}{|n(-k)|}\ \right]>1.

(5)

Definition 1.1.
The series of the form (
3
) are named
superlacunar,
iff

\underline{\lim}_{k\to\infty}\left[\ \frac{n(k+1)}{n(k)}\ \right]=\infty

(6)

and in addition

\underline{\lim}_{k\to\infty}\left[\ \frac{|n(-k-1)|}{|n(-k)|}\ \right]=\infty,

(7)

see e.g.
[
3
]
, chapter 5;
[
17
]
, chapter 3.

Our claim in this report is to establish the interrelations between modulus of continuity of the
superlacunar function and behavior of its Fourier coefficients.

We consider as an application the continuity properties of Gaussian periodic stationary random processes.

As an example: the famous Weierstrass function.

2
Bilateral inequalities. Examples.

Recall that the modulus of continuity
\ \omega[f](\delta),\ \delta\in[0,2\pi]\
for the continuous
\ 2\pi\

periodical numerical valued function
\ f=f(t)\
is defined as follows

\omega[f](\delta)\stackrel{{\scriptstyle def}}{{=}}\sup_{t}\ \sup_{h:|h|\leq\delta}|\ f(t+h)-f(t)\ |.

(8)

It is well - known
[
17
]
, chapters 2,3 that for the function in particular of the form (
3
)

|c(k)|\leq(4\pi)^{-1}\ \omega[f](\pi/n(|k|)),\ |k|\geq 1.

(9)

Conversely, let the function
\ f=f(t),\ t\in R\
has the form (
3
), such that (we recall)

\ \vec{c}\in l_{1}.\
Let also
\ t,s\in T,\ |t-s|<h,\ h\in[0,2\pi].\
We have

|f(t)-f(s)|\leq h\ ||\vec{c}||l_{1}\ \sum_{k:|k|\leq N}|k|\ |n(|k|)|+

2\ \sum_{k:|k|>N}|c(k)|\ =h\Sigma_{1}[c,n](N)+\Sigma_{2}[c](N),

where

||\vec{c}||l_{1}=\sum_{k}|c(k)|<\infty,\ \Sigma_{1}[c,n](N)=||\vec{c}||l_{1}\cdot\sum_{k:|k|\leq N}|k|\ |n(|k|)|,

\Sigma_{2}[c](N)=2\ \sum_{k:|k|>N}|c(k)|;\ N\geq 2.

To summarize common with (
9
):

Proposition 2.1.
Denote

\Sigma[c,\vec{n}](\delta)\stackrel{{\scriptstyle def}}{{=}}\inf_{N=2,3,4,\ldots}\left[\ \delta\cdot\Sigma_{1}[c,n](N)+\Sigma_{2}[c](N)\ \right].

We get under formulated above notations and conditions

\omega[f](\delta)\leq\Sigma[c,\vec{n}](\delta),\ \delta\in[0,2\pi).

(10)

Remark 2.1.
Obviously,

\lim_{\delta\to 0+}\Sigma[c,\vec{n}](\delta)=0.

Let us consider some examples.

Example 2.1.
Let us put

c(k)=k^{-2\Delta},\ k\geq 1;\ c(k)=0,\ k\leq 0;\ n(k)\stackrel{{\scriptstyle def}}{{=}}2^{k},\ \Delta=\rm const>1/2,

the lacunar case. On the other words, we consider the following complex valued continuous function

g(t)=g_{\Delta}(t):=\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}k^{-2\Delta}\ \exp\left(\ 2^{k}\ i\ t\ \right).

(11)

It follows from (
9
)

\omega[g](\delta)\geq C_{1}\ |\ln\delta|^{-2\Delta},\ 0<\delta<1/e,\ 0<C_{1}<\infty,

a lower bound. The correspondent upper bound may be found on the basis of proposition 2.1:

\omega[g](\delta)\leq C_{2}\ |\ln\delta|^{1-2\Delta},\ 0<\delta<1/e,\ 0<C_{2}<\infty.

Example 2.2.
Let us introduce now

c(k)=k^{-2\Delta},\ k\geq 1;\ c(k)=0,\ k\leq 0;\ n(k)\stackrel{{\scriptstyle def}}{{=}}2^{2^{k}},\ \Delta=\rm const>1/2,

the superlacunar case. We consider the following complex valued continuous function

s(t)=s[\Delta](t):=\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}k^{-2\Delta}\ \exp\left(\ i\cdot 2^{2^{k}}\cdot t\ \right).

(12)

It follows from (
9
) as before

\omega[s](\delta)\geq C_{3}\ \left[\ \ln|\ln\delta|\ \right]^{-2\Delta},\ 0<\delta<e^{-e},\ 0<C_{3}<\infty,

a lower bound. The correspondent upper bound may be found alike on the basis of proposition 2.1:

\omega[s](\delta)\leq C_{4}\ \left[\ \ln|\ln\delta|\ \right]^{1-2\Delta},\ 0<\delta<e^{-e},\ 0<C_{4}<\infty.

We will show in this report that if the condition (

We consider only the case of stationary and periodical centered Gaussian r.p.

To be more concrete, introduce the following Gaussian distributed stationary periodical process of the form (super - lacunar series)

\nu(t)=\nu_{\Delta}(t)\stackrel{{\scriptstyle def}}{{=}}\sum_{|k|\geq 1}|k|^{-\Delta}\ \kappa_{k}\ \exp\left(\ i\cdot\rm sign(k)\cdot 2^{2^{|}k|}\ \right),

4
Concluding remarks.

An open question: let
\ \omega=\omega(\delta),\ \delta\in[0,1/e]\
be arbitrary non - trivial modulus of continuity.
There exists or not a stationary Gaussian random process
\ \zeta=\zeta(t),\ t\in R\
with continuous sample path
for which its covariation function
\ r=r[\zeta](t)\
obeys a property

\omega[r](\delta)\asymp\omega(\delta),\ \delta\in[0,1/e].
