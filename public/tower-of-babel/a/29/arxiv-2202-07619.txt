The Case of Itinerant Magnetism in CaMn 2 Al 10 : Self-Consistent Renormalisation (SCR) Theory Study.
Bharathiganesh Devanarayanan, Akariti Sharma, Pratik D.Patel, Navinder Singh

Abstract
We have applied the powerful self-consistent renormalization theory of spin fluctuations for the system CaMn 2 Al 10 discovered in 2015 2015 and was conjectured to be an itinerant magnet. We have calculated the inverse static i.e. , (paramagnetic) susceptibility and have compared it with the experimental data (Phys. Rev. B 92, 020413, 2015). The agreement is very good. We have calculated spin fluctuations at various temperatures and have also estimated the strength of the electronic correlation i.e. , ( I = 0.3136 I=0.3136 eV) in the Hubbard Hamiltonian. Based on our quantitative explanation of the inverse static i.e. , (paramagnetic) susceptibility data within the framework of SCR theory, we can decisively conclude CaMn 2 Al 10 exhibits the phenomena of itinerant magnetism. Further, our DFT and DFT+U calculations corroborate the strong Mn-Al hybridization which is the key behind the itinerant magnetism in this system. Our estimated correlations strength will provide a foundation for further studies of itinerant magnetism in this system.

The inverses magnetic susceptibility in the system CaMn
2
Al
10
obey the Curie-Weiss law as determined from the experiment. We theoretically examined the problem and shows that SCR theory can explain the observed Curie-Weiss law. With this we (1) verify that CaMn

Our estimated value of electronic correlations can be potentially used to address the optical conductivity and many other probes.
