A contact metric manifold

]

Theorem 3.1
.

A
(2n+1)
-dimensional
N(\kappa)
-contact metric manifold
M
, satisfies

\widetilde{C}(\xi,X)\cdot\widetilde{C}=0

if and only if either the manifold is
N(1-\frac{1}{n})
-contact metric manifold or it is locally isometric to the hyperbolic space
H^{2n+1}(-\kappa)
.

Proof.
In view of (1.17) and (2.6), equation (1.5) reduces

(3.1)

\widetilde{C}(\xi,Y)Z=\left\{\kappa-\frac{r}{2n(2n+1)}\right\}\left[g(Y,Z)\xi-\eta(Z)Y\right],

(3.2)

\widetilde{C}(X,Y)\xi=\left\{\kappa-\frac{r}{2n(2n+1)}\right\}\left[\eta(Y)X-\eta(X)Y\right].

The condition
\widetilde{C}(\xi,X)\cdot\widetilde{C}=0
implies that

(3.3)

\widetilde{C}(\xi,U)\widetilde{C}(X,Y)\xi-\widetilde{C}(\widetilde{C}(\xi,U)X,Y)\xi-\widetilde{C}(X,\widetilde{C}(\xi,U)Y)\xi=0.

Using (3.1) and (3.2) in (3.3), we have

(3.4)

\left\{\kappa-\frac{r}{2n(2n+1)}\right\}\left[\widetilde{C}(X,Y)U-\left(\kappa-\frac{r}{2n(2n+1)}\right)(g(Y,U)X-g(X,U)Y)\right]=0.

So either
r=2n\kappa(2n+1)
, or

(3.5)

\widetilde{C}(X,Y)U=\left(\kappa-\frac{r}{2n(2n+1)}\right)(g(Y,U)X-g(X,U)Y)=0.

If
r=2n\kappa(2n+1)
, comparing the value of
r=2n(2n-2+\kappa)
, we get
\kappa=(1-\frac{1}{n})
. Hence, the manifold is
N(1-\frac{1}{n})
-contact metric manifold. That is, it is locally isometric to Example 2.1.

Also from (1.5) and (3.5), we obtain

(3.6)

R(X,Y)U=\kappa\left\{g(X,U)Y-g(Y,U)X\right\}.

This implies that
M
is of constant curvature
(-\kappa)
. Consequently it is locally isometric to the hyperbolic space
H^{2n+1}(-\kappa)
. Conversely, if the manifold is
H^{2n+1}(-\kappa)
, then (3.6) holds, which yields
r=2n\kappa(2n+1)
. Then from (3.1) it follows that
\widetilde{C}(\xi,X)=0
. Again if
k=(1-\frac{1}{n})
, then in view of (2.14) we have
r=2(n-1)(2n+1)
and hence it follows from (3.1) that
\widetilde{C}(\xi,X)=0
. Consequently,
\widetilde{C}(\xi,X)\cdot R=0
since
\widetilde{C}(\xi,X)
acts as a derivation. Hence
\widetilde{C}(\xi,X)\cdot\widetilde{C}=0
. This proves the theorem.

Theorem 3.2

Making use of (2.10), we have

(3.9)

\left\{\kappa-\frac{r}{2n(2n+1)}\right\}\left[S(X,Y)-2nkg(X,Y)\right]=0.

From (3.9), we obtain that either

In particular, if we consider a
3

N(\kappa)
-contact metric manifold, then

\kappa=0

Using (1.2), (1.6), (2.6) and (2.10) in (3.13), we have

Example.4.1
