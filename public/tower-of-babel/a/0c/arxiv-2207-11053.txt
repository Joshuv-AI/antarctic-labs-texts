Evidence of A C ​ P ​ ( D 0 → π + ​ π − ) A_{CP}(D^{0}\to\pi^{+}\pi^{-}) implies observable C ​ P CP asymmetry in the D 0 → π 0 ​ π 0 D^{0}\to\pi^{0}\pi^{0} decay
Di Wang

Abstract
Inspired by the recent measurement of C ​ P CP asymmetry in the individual mode on LHCb, we study C ​ P CP asymmetry of the D → π ​ π D\to\pi\pi system in the isospin and topological analysis. The ratio between penguin and tree amplitudes P / ( T + C ) P/(T+C) in the D → π ​ π D\to\pi\pi system is found to be greater than two in most values of the relative strong phase. And D 0 → π 0 ​ π 0 D^{0}\to\pi^{0}\pi^{0} is a potential mode to reveal the C ​ P CP asymmetry of the order of 10 − 3 10^{-3} , which would be observed by Belle II in the future. The large C ​ P CP asymmetry in the D → π ​ π D\to\pi\pi system might be understood in the t t -channel final-state interaction.

II
Isospin and topological analysis

In the
D\to\pi\pi
system,
(D^{0},D^{+})
form an isospin doublet,
(\pi^{+},\pi^{0},\pi^{-})
form an isospin triplet.
Isospin decompositions of the
D^{0}\to\pi^{+}\pi^{-}
,
D^{0}\to\pi^{0}\pi^{0}
and
D^{+}\to\pi^{+}\pi^{0}
modes are

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}(D^{0}\to\pi^{+}\pi^{-})

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2\sqrt{3}}\mathcal{A}_{3/2}+\frac{1}{\sqrt{6}}\mathcal{A}_{1/2},

(5)

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}(D^{0}\to\pi^{0}\pi^{0})

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\sqrt{6}}\mathcal{A}_{3/2}-\frac{1}{2\sqrt{3}}\mathcal{A}_{1/2},

(6)

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}(D^{+}\to\pi^{+}\pi^{0})

\displaystyle=\frac{\sqrt{6}}{4}\mathcal{A}_{3/2},

(7)

in which
\mathcal{A}_{3/2}
and
\mathcal{A}_{1/2}
are the amplitudes with
\Delta I=3/2
and
\Delta I=1/2
, respectively.
Topological decompositions of the
D\to\pi\pi
modes can be expressed as

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}(D^{0}\to\pi^{+}\pi^{-})

\displaystyle=\lambda_{d}\,(T+E+\mathcal{P}_{\rm b})-\lambda_{b}\,\mathcal{P},

(8)

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}(D^{0}\to\pi^{0}\pi^{0})

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\lambda_{d}\,(C-E-\mathcal{P}_{\rm b})+\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\lambda_{b}\,\mathcal{P},

(9)

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}(D^{+}\to\pi^{+}\pi^{0})

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\lambda_{d}\,(T+C),

(10)

where
\lambda_{d}=V^{*}_{cd}\,V_{ud}
and
\lambda_{b}=V^{*}_{cb}\,V_{ub}
.
The contributions from the penguin operators
O_{3-6}

8
)
\sim
(

,

and

and

\mathcal{P}_{d}

\mathcal{P}_{s}

.

\pi

,

)

\displaystyle=\frac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}\lambda_{d}(T+C),

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}_{1/2}

\displaystyle=\sqrt{6}\lambda_{d}(E+\mathcal{P}_{\rm b}+\frac{2}{3}T-\frac{1}{3}C)-\sqrt{6}\lambda_{b}\mathcal{P}.

Estimation in the final-state interaction

In the
D\to\pi\pi
system, the penguin contribution is
\lambda_{d}\mathcal{P}_{d}+\lambda_{s}\mathcal{P}_{s}

Considering all the tree amplitudes are proportional to
\lambda_{d}

In this section, we discuss how large the penguin amplitude

For the two-body heavy meson weak decay, the FSI effect can be modeled as exchange of one particle between two particles generated from the short-distance tree emitted process.
There are

-channel contributions in the final state interaction, which are depicted in Fig.

In the

-1.06

.
If the
