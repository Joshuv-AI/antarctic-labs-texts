Abstract
We consider a general two Higgs doublet model which can simultaneously solve discrepancies in neutral B meson decay ( b → s ​ ℓ ​ ℓ ¯ b\to s\ell\overline{\ell} distribution) and charged B meson decay ( b → c ​ τ ​ ν ¯ b\to c\tau\overline{\nu} ) with a charged Higgs. The model contains two additional neutral scalars at the same mass scale and predicts distinctive signals at the LHC. Based on the recent same-sign top search by the ATLAS collaboration, we found the constraint on the scalar mass spectrum. To probe the remaining mass window, we propose a novel c ​ g → t ​ τ ​ τ ¯ cg\to t\tau\overline{\tau} process at the LHC. ——————————————————————————————————————————– Keywords: Two Higgs Doublet Model, b → s ​ ℓ ​ ℓ ¯ b\to s\ell\overline{\ell} , b → c ​ τ ​ ν b\to c\tau\nu , Top-Associated Scalar Production

V
Summary and discussion

Recently the charged Higgs solution to B anomalies became more interesting than ever.
The charged Higgs need to interact with left-handed bottom quark and thus can be a part of an additional doublet.
Hence a two Higgs doublet model is a minimal model and there are also two additional neutral scalars.
The Yukawa interaction of those scalars are related by SU(2)
L
rotation and the simultaneous explanation predicts distinctive signal at the LHC.
The theoretical proposals to probe the solution via charged Higgs mediated processes was made last year, however, the crucial process has not been tested experimentally yet.
Although, in the meantime, the ATLAS experiment reported the game changing constraint on the neutral scalars.
In this letter we reinterpret the ATLAS constraint and obtained the condition for the mass spectrum of the additional neutral scalars:

{\mathcal{O}}(1)\,{\rm GeV}
mass degeneracy among
H
and
A
or
m_{t}\leq m_{\phi}\leq 200\,{\rm GeV}
where
\phi
denotes
H
and
A
.
We also pointed out that the signal cross section of
gc\to t\phi\to t\tau\overline{\tau}
could be as large as 10 fb
\sim
