Measurement of the charge asymmetry in dileptonic decays of top quark pairs in p ​ p pp collisions at s = 7 \sqrt{s}=7 TeV using the ATLAS detector
C Deterre 1 and L Mijović 2,3 on behalf of the ATLAS Collaboration

Abstract
A measurement of the top–antitop ( t ​ t ¯ t\bar{t} ) charge asymmetry is presented using 2011 LHC data collected by the ATLAS detector corresponding to an integrated luminosity of 4.6 fb -1 at a centre-of-mass energy of 7 TeV. The analysis is performed in the dilepton channel, and two different observables are studied: A C ℓ ​ ℓ A^{\ell\ell}_{\mathrm{C}} , based on the identified charged leptons, and A C t ​ t ¯ A^{t\bar{t}}_{\mathrm{C}} , based on the reconstructed t ​ t ¯ t\bar{t} final state. The asymmetries, measured to be A C ℓ ​ ℓ A^{\ell\ell}_{\mathrm{C}} = 0.024 ± 0.015 ​ (stat.) ± 0.009 ​ (syst.) 0.024\pm 0.015\mbox{$\;$(stat.)}\pm 0.009\mbox{$\;$(syst.)} and A C t ​ t ¯ A^{t\bar{t}}_{\mathrm{C}} = 0.021 ± 0.025 ​ (stat.) ± 0.017 ​ (syst.) 0.021\pm 0.025\mbox{$\;$(stat.)}\pm 0.017\mbox{$\;$(syst.)} , are in agreement with the Standard Model predictions.

The measurement of

Assumptions on

\eta

Both

5
Conclusion

The measured inclusive
A^{\ell\ell}_{\mathrm{C}}
and
A^{t\bar{t}}_{\mathrm{C}}
are found to be in good agreement with state-of-the-art SM predictions. A larger data set will enable more precise as well as more differential measurements of the asymmetry in the dileptonic decay channel, thus enhancing the requirements of the SM tests. Future
A^{\ell\ell}_{\mathrm{C}}
and
A^{t\bar{t}}_{\mathrm{C}}
measurements with a larger data set could also further constrain the allowed couplings of the BSM models such as the colour-octet models.
