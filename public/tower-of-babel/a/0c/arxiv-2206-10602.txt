Quantum states defined by using the finite frame quantization
Nicolae Cotfas

Abstract
Finite frame quantization is a discrete version of the coherent state quantization. In the case of a quantum system with finite-dimensional Hilbert space, the finite frame quantization allows us to associate a linear operator to each function defined on the discrete phase space of the system. We investigate the properties of the density operators which can be defined by using this method.

Introduction

The quantum particle moving along a straight line is described by using the Hilbert space
L^{2}(\mathbb{R})
.
For the corresponding classical system,
\mathbb{R}
is the configuration space and
\mathbb{R}^{2}\!=\!\mathbb{R}\!\times\!\mathbb{R}
the phase space. The position operator

\hat{q}\psi(q)\!=\!q\,\psi(q)

(1)

and the momentum operator

\hat{p}\!=\!-{\rm i}\hbar\frac{d}{dq}

(2)

satisfy the commutation relation

[\hat{q},\hat{p}]\!=\!{\rm i}\hbar

(3)

and the relation

\hat{p}\!=\!\hat{F}^{\dagger}\hat{q}\hat{F}

(4)

where

\hat{F}[\psi](p)\!=\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi\hbar}}$}\int\limits_{-\infty}^{\infty}{\rm e}^{-\frac{\rm i}{\hbar}pq}\psi(q)\,dq\!=\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{\sqrt{h}}$}\int\limits_{-\infty}^{\infty}{\rm e}^{-\frac{2\pi\rm i}{h}pq}\psi(q)\,dq

(5)

is the Fourier transform.

In the odd-dimensional case,
d\!=\!2s\!+\!1
, a discrete version can be obtained by using

\mathcal{R}\!=\!\{-s,-s\!+\!1,...,s\!-\!1,s\}

(6)

as a configuration space, the Hilbert space (several representations are presented)

\mathcal{H}\!\equiv\!\mathbb{C}^{d}\!\equiv\!\ell^{2}(\mathcal{R})\!=\!\{\,\psi\!:\!\mathcal{R}\rightarrow\mathbb{C}\,\}\!\equiv\!\left\{\psi\!:\!\mathbb{Z}\rightarrow\mathbb{C}\left|\mbox{\small$\begin{array}[]{r}\psi(n\!+\!d)\!=\!\psi(n),\\[-2.84526pt]
\mbox{for all }n\!\in\!\mathbb{Z}\end{array}$}\!\!\!\right.\right\}

(7)

with

\langle\psi,\varphi\rangle\!=\!\sum\limits_{n=-s}^{s}\overline{\psi(n)}\varphi(n)

(8)

and the
discrete Fourier
transform
\hat{\mathfrak{F}}\!:\!\mathcal{H}\!\rightarrow\!\mathcal{H}
,

\hat{\mathfrak{F}}[\psi](k)\!=\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{\sqrt{d}}$}\sum\limits_{n=-s}^{s}{\rm e}^{-\frac{2\pi{\rm i}}{d}kn}\psi(n).

(9)

The standard basis
\{\delta_{-s},\ \delta_{-s+1},...,\delta_{s-1},\delta_{s}\}
, where

\delta_{m}(n)\!=\!\left\{\begin{array}[]{lll}1&\mbox{if}&n\!=\!m\ \ \mbox{modulo }d\\[-2.84526pt]
0&\mbox{if}&n\!\neq\!m\ \ \mbox{modulo }d\end{array}\right.

(10)

is an orthonormal basis. By using Dirac’s notation
|m\rangle
instead of
\delta_{m}
, we have

\langle m|k\rangle\!=\!\delta_{mk},\qquad\sum\limits_{m=-s}^{s}|m\rangle\langle m|\!=\!\mathbb{I},

(11)

where
\mathbb{I}\!:\!\mathcal{H}\!\rightarrow\!\mathcal{H},\ \,\mathbb{I}\psi\!=\!\psi,
is the identity operator.

In the discrete case, the
position
operator
\hat{\mathfrak{q}}:\mathcal{H}\!\rightarrow\!\mathcal{H}:\psi\!\mapsto\!\hat{\mathfrak{q}}\psi
is

\hat{\mathfrak{q}}\psi(n)\!=\!n\,\psi(n).

(12)

For the
momentum
operator
\hat{\mathfrak{p}}:\mathcal{H}\!\rightarrow\!\mathcal{H}
, the definition

\hat{\mathfrak{p}}\!=\!\hat{\mathfrak{F}}^{\dagger}\hat{\mathfrak{q}}\hat{\mathfrak{F}}

(13)

is more adequate than the use of a finite-difference operator instead of
\frac{d}{dq}
.

In the discrete case, the set

\mathcal{R}^{2}\!=\!\mathcal{R}\!\times\!\mathcal{R}\!=\!\{\,(n,k)\ |\ n,k\!\in\!\{-s,-s\!+\!1,...,s\!-\!1,s\}\,\}

(14)

plays the role of phase space.

The Gaussian function of continuous variable (
\kappa\!>\!0
is a parameter)

g_{\kappa}\!:\!\mathbb{R}\!\rightarrow\!\mathbb{R},\qquad g_{\kappa}(q)\!=\!{\rm e}^{-\frac{\kappa}{2\hbar}q^{2}}\!=\!{\rm e}^{-\frac{\kappa\pi}{h}q^{2}}

(15)

satisfies the relation

\hat{F}[g_{\kappa}]\!=\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{\sqrt{\kappa}}$}g_{\frac{1}{\kappa}}.

(16)

The corresponding
Gaussian function
of discrete variable, defined as
[
11
,
15
]

\mathfrak{g}_{\kappa}\!:\!\mathcal{R}\!\rightarrow\!\mathbb{R},\qquad\mathfrak{g}_{\kappa}(n)\!=\!\sum\limits_{\alpha=-\infty}^{\infty}{\rm e}^{-\frac{\kappa\pi}{d}(n+\alpha d)^{2}}

(17)

satisfies the similar relation

\hat{\mathfrak{F}}[\mathfrak{g}_{\kappa}]\!=\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{\sqrt{\kappa}}$}\mathfrak{g}_{\frac{1}{\kappa}}.

(18)

In this article, we restrict us to the odd-dimensional case, but most of the definitions and results can be extended in order to include the even-dimensional case
d\!=\!2s
also.

2
Coherent state quantization

In the continuous case, the quantum state

|0,0\rangle\!=\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{\sqrt{\langle g_{1},g_{1}\rangle}}$}|g_{1}\rangle

(19)

represents the
vacuum state
. The
coherent states

[
13
]

|q,p\rangle\!=\!\hat{D}(q,p)|0,0\rangle,

(20)

defined by using the
displacement operators

[
13
,
16
]

\hat{D}(q,p)\!=\!{\rm e}^{-\frac{\rm i}{2\hbar}pq}\,{\rm e}^{\frac{\rm i}{\hbar}p\hat{q}}\,{\rm e}^{-\frac{\rm i}{\hbar}q\hat{p}}\!=\!{\rm e}^{-\frac{\pi\rm i}{h}pq}\,{\rm e}^{\frac{2\pi{\rm i}}{h}p\hat{q}}\,{\rm e}^{-\frac{2\pi{\rm i}}{h}q\hat{p}}

(21)

satisfy the resolution of the identity

\mathbb{I}\!=\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{2\pi\hbar}$}\int\limits_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}|q,p\rangle\langle q,p|\,dqdp.

(22)

By using the coherent state quantization, we associate the linear operator
[
9
]

\hat{A}_{f}\!=\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{2\pi\hbar}$}\int\limits_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}f(q,p)\,|q,p\rangle\langle q,p|\,dqdp

(23)

to each function

f\!:\!\mathbb{R}\!\times\mathbb{R}\!\rightarrow\!\mathbb{C},

(24)

defined on the phase space
\mathbb{R}^{2}
, and such that the integral is convergent.

For example, in the case
f(q,p)\!=\!q
, we get
[
9
]

\hat{A}_{f}\!=\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{2\pi\hbar}$}\int\limits_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}q\,|q,p\rangle\langle q,p|\,dqdp\!=\!\hat{q},

(25)

in the case
f(q,p)\!=\!p
, we get
[
9
]

\hat{A}_{f}\!=\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{2\pi\hbar}$}\int\limits_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}p\,|q,p\rangle\langle q,p|\,dqdp\!=\!\hat{p},

(26)

and, in the case
f(q,p)\!=\!\frac{p^{2}+q^{2}}{2}
, we get
[
9
]

\hat{A}_{f}\!=\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{2\pi\hbar}$}\int\limits_{\mathbb{R}^{2}}\frac{p^{2}\!+\!q^{2}}{2}\,|q,p\rangle\langle q,p|\,dqdp\!=\!-\frac{\hbar^{2}}{2}\frac{{\rm d}^{2}}{{\rm d}q^{2}}\!+\!\frac{1}{2}q^{2}\!+\!\frac{1}{2}.

(27)

In the last case, the operator

\hat{A}_{f}\!-\!\frac{1}{2}\!=\!-\frac{\hbar^{2}}{2}\frac{{\rm d}^{2}}{{\rm d}q^{2}}\!+\!\frac{1}{2}q^{2}

(28)

is the Hamiltonian of the quantum harmonic oscillator.

\hat{\mathfrak{D}}(n,k)\!:\!\mathcal{H}\!\rightarrow\!\mathcal{H},

\hat{\mathfrak{D}}(n,k)\!=\!{\rm e}^{-\frac{\pi{\rm i}}{d}nk}\,{\rm e}^{\frac{2\pi{\rm i}}{d}k\hat{\mathfrak{q}}}\,{\rm e}^{-\frac{2\pi{\rm i}}{d}n\hat{\mathfrak{p}}}

(30)

Theorem 1.

The discrete coherent states
[
8
,
17
]

\qquad\qquad\qquad|n;\!k\rangle\!=\!\hat{\mathfrak{D}}(n,k)|0;\!0\rangle,

(31)

satisfy the resolution of the identity

\qquad\qquad\qquad\mathbb{I}\!=\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{d}$}\sum\limits_{n,k=-s}^{s}|n;\!k\rangle\langle n;\!k|.

(32)

Proof.
Since

\begin{array}[]{rl}{\rm e}^{-\frac{2\pi{\rm i}}{d}n\hat{\mathfrak{p}}}\mathfrak{g}_{1}(m)&\!\!\!=\!{\rm e}^{-\frac{2\pi{\rm i}}{d}n\hat{\mathfrak{F}}^{\dagger}\hat{\mathfrak{q}}\hat{\mathfrak{F}}}\mathfrak{g}_{1}(m)\!=\!\hat{\mathfrak{F}}^{\dagger}{\rm e}^{-\frac{2\pi{\rm i}}{d}n\hat{\mathfrak{q}}}\hat{\mathfrak{F}}\mathfrak{g}_{1}(m)\\
&\!\!\!=\!\frac{1}{\sqrt{d}}\sum\limits_{a=-s}^{s}{\rm e}^{\frac{2\pi{\rm i}}{d}ma}{\rm e}^{-\frac{2\pi{\rm i}}{d}n\hat{\mathfrak{q}}}\hat{\mathfrak{F}}\mathfrak{g}_{1}(a)\\
&\!\!\!=\!\frac{1}{\sqrt{d}}\sum\limits_{a=-s}^{s}{\rm e}^{\frac{2\pi{\rm i}}{d}ma}{\rm e}^{-\frac{2\pi{\rm i}}{d}na}\hat{\mathfrak{F}}\mathfrak{g}_{1}(a)\\
&\!\!\!=\!\frac{1}{\sqrt{d}}\sum\limits_{a=-s}^{s}{\rm e}^{\frac{2\pi{\rm i}}{d}ma}{\rm e}^{-\frac{2\pi{\rm i}}{d}na}\frac{1}{\sqrt{d}}\sum\limits_{b=-s}^{s}{\rm e}^{-\frac{2\pi{\rm i}}{d}ab}\mathfrak{g}_{1}(b)\\
&\!\!\!=\!\sum\limits_{b=-s}^{s}\frac{1}{d}\sum\limits_{a=-s}^{s}{\rm e}^{\frac{2\pi{\rm i}}{d}a(m-n-b)}\mathfrak{g}_{1}(b)\\
&\!\!\!=\!\sum\limits_{b=-s}^{s}\delta_{b}(m\!-\!n)\mathfrak{g}_{1}(b)\!=\!\mathfrak{g}_{1}(m\!-\!n)\\
\end{array}

and

\begin{array}[]{rl}\langle m|n;\!k\rangle&\!\!\!=\!\langle m|\hat{\mathfrak{D}}(n,k)|0;\!0\rangle\\
&\!\!\!=\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{\sqrt{\langle\mathfrak{g}_{1},\mathfrak{g}_{1}\rangle}}$}{\rm e}^{-\frac{\pi{\rm i}}{d}nk}\,{\rm e}^{\frac{2\pi{\rm i}}{d}k\hat{\mathfrak{q}}}\,{\rm e}^{-\frac{2\pi{\rm i}}{d}n\hat{\mathfrak{p}}}\mathfrak{g}_{1}(m)\\
&\!\!\!=\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{\sqrt{\langle\mathfrak{g}_{1},\mathfrak{g}_{1}\rangle}}$}{\rm e}^{-\frac{\pi{\rm i}}{d}nk}\,{\rm e}^{\frac{2\pi{\rm i}}{d}km}\,{\rm e}^{-\frac{2\pi{\rm i}}{d}n\hat{\mathfrak{p}}}\mathfrak{g}_{1}(m)\\
&\!\!\!=\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{\sqrt{\langle\mathfrak{g}_{1},\mathfrak{g}_{1}\rangle}}$}{\rm e}^{-\frac{\pi{\rm i}}{d}nk}\,{\rm e}^{\frac{2\pi{\rm i}}{d}km}\,\mathfrak{g}_{1}(m\!-\!n)\\
\end{array}

we get

\begin{array}[]{rl}\left\langle m\left|\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{d}$}\sum\limits_{n,k=-s}^{s}|n;\!k\rangle\langle n;\!k|\right|\ell\right\rangle&\!\!\!=\!\frac{1}{d}\frac{1}{\langle\mathfrak{g}_{1},\mathfrak{g}_{1}\rangle}\sum\limits_{n,k=-s}^{s}{\rm e}^{\frac{2\pi{\rm i}}{d}km}\,\mathfrak{g}_{1}(m\!-\!n){\rm e}^{-\frac{2\pi{\rm i}}{d}k\ell}\,\mathfrak{g}_{1}(\ell\!-\!n)\\
&\!\!\!=\!\frac{1}{\langle\mathfrak{g}_{1},\mathfrak{g}_{1}\rangle}\sum\limits_{n=-s}^{s}\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{d}$}\sum\limits_{k=-s}^{s}{\rm e}^{\frac{2\pi{\rm i}}{d}k(m-\ell)}\,\mathfrak{g}_{1}(m\!-\!n)\,\mathfrak{g}_{1}(\ell\!-\!n)\\
&\!\!\!=\!\frac{1}{\langle\mathfrak{g}_{1},\mathfrak{g}_{1}\rangle}\sum\limits_{n=-s}^{s}\delta_{m\ell}\,\mathfrak{g}_{1}(m\!-\!n)\,\mathfrak{g}_{1}(\ell\!-\!n)\!=\!\delta_{m\ell}.\qquad\Box\\
\end{array}

\hat{\Lambda}_{f}\!=\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{d}$}\sum\limits_{n,k=-s}^{s}f(n,k)\,|n;\!k\rangle\langle n;\!k|

Theorem 2.

Let
f,g\!:\!\mathcal{R}\!\times\!\mathcal{R}\!\rightarrow\mathbb{C}
and
\alpha,\beta\!\in\!\mathbb{C}
. We have:

a)\quad f(n,k)\!=\!1\quad\Rightarrow\quad\hat{\Lambda}_{f}\!=\!\mathbb{I}.

(35)

b)\quad\hat{\Lambda}_{\alpha f+\beta g}\!=\!\alpha\hat{\Lambda}_{f}\!+\!\beta\hat{\Lambda}_{g}.

(36)

c)\quad\begin{array}[]{c}f(n,k)\!\in\!\mathbb{R}\\[-2.84526pt]
\mbox{\it for any }n,k\end{array}\quad\Rightarrow\quad\hat{\Lambda}_{f}^{\dagger}\!=\!\hat{\Lambda}_{f}.

(37)

d)\quad\begin{array}[]{c}f(n,k)\!\geq\!0\\[-2.84526pt]
\mbox{\it for any }n,k\end{array}\quad\Rightarrow\quad\hat{\Lambda}_{f}\!\geq\!0.

(38)

e)\quad{\rm tr}\,\hat{\Lambda}_{f}\!=\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{d}$}\sum\limits_{n,k=-s}^{s}f(n,k).

(39)

Proof.

a) Direct consequence of (
32
).

b) Direct consequence of the definition (
33
).

\begin{array}[]{rl}\!\!\!c)\quad\hat{\Lambda}_{f}^{\dagger}\!=&\!\!\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{d}$}\sum\limits_{n,k=-s}^{s}\overline{f(n,k)}\,(|n;\!k\rangle\langle n;\!k|)^{\dagger}\\
=&\!\!\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{d}$}\sum\limits_{n,k=-s}^{s}f(n,k)\,|n;\!k\rangle\langle n;\!k|\!=\!\hat{\Lambda}_{f}.\end{array}

d) For any
\psi\!\in\!\mathcal{H}
, we have

\quad\ \ \begin{array}[]{rl}\langle\psi,\hat{\Lambda}_{f}\psi\rangle\!=&\!\!\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{d}$}\sum\limits_{n,k=-s}^{s}f(n,k)\,\langle\psi|n;\!k\rangle\langle n;\!k|\psi\rangle\\
=&\!\!\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{d}$}\sum\limits_{n,k=-s}^{s}f(n,k)\,|\langle n;\!k|\psi\rangle|^{2}\!\geq\!0.\\
\end{array}

\begin{array}[]{rl}\!\!\!e)\quad{\rm tr}\,\hat{\Lambda}_{f}\!=&\!\!\!\sum\limits_{m=-s}^{s}\langle m|\hat{\Lambda}_{f}|m\rangle\\
=&\!\!\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{d}$}\sum\limits_{m=-s}^{s}\sum\limits_{n,k=-s}^{s}f(n,k)\,\langle m|n;\!k\rangle\langle n;\!k|m\rangle\\
=&\!\!\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{d}$}\sum\limits_{n,k=-s}^{s}f(n,k)\sum\limits_{m=-s}^{s}\langle n;\!k|m\rangle\langle m|n;\!k\rangle\\
=&\!\!\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{d}$}\sum\limits_{n,k=-s}^{s}f(n,k)\langle n;\!k|\mathbb{I}|n;\!k\rangle\\
=&\!\!\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{d}$}\sum\limits_{n,k=-s}^{s}f(n,k).\qquad\Box\end{array}

4
Density operators obtained through finite frame quantization

The finite frame quantization allows us to define a remarkable class of quantum states.

Theorem 3.

If the function
f\!:\!\mathcal{R}\!\times\!\mathcal{R}\!\rightarrow[0,d]
is such that

\qquad\sum\limits_{n,k=-s}^{s}f(n,k)\!=\!d,

(43)

then the corresponding linear operator
\hat{\varrho}_{f}\!:\!\mathcal{H}\!\rightarrow\!\mathcal{H}
,

\qquad\hat{\varrho}_{f}\!=\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{d}$}\sum\limits_{n,k=-s}^{s}f(n,k)\,|n;\!k\rangle\langle n;\!k|

(44)

is a density operator
.

Proof.
Direct consequence of theorem 2.
\qquad\Box

For example, the state corresponding to
f(n,k)\!=\!\frac{1}{d}
is the mixed state
\hat{\varrho}_{f}\!=\!\frac{1}{d}\mathbb{I}
,

and the state corresponding to

f(n,k)\!=\!\left\{\begin{array}[]{lll}d&\mbox{for}&(n,k)\!=\!(m,\ell)\\
0&\mbox{for}&(n,k)\!\neq\!(m,\ell)\end{array}\right.

(45)

is the pure state
\hat{\varrho}_{f}\!=\!|m;\!\ell\rangle\langle m;\!\ell|
, that is, the discrete coherent state
|m;\!\ell\rangle
.

Theorem 4.

The set
\mathcal{S}_{\rm fr}
of all the density operators of the form (
44
) is a convex set.

Proof.

If
\lambda\!\in\![0,1]
and
\hat{\varrho}_{f},\hat{\varrho}_{g}\!\in\!\mathcal{S}_{\rm fr}
, then

(1\!-\!\lambda)\hat{\varrho}_{f}\!+\!\lambda\hat{\varrho}_{g}\!=\!\hat{\varrho}_{h},\quad\mbox{where}\quad h(n,k)\!=\!(1\!-\!\lambda)f(n,k)\!+\!\lambda g(n,k).\qquad\Box

Theorem 5.

\mathcal{S}_{\rm fr}
is the convex hull of the set of pure states
\{\,|n;\!k\rangle\langle n;\!k|\ |\ n,k\!\in\!\mathcal{R}\,\}
.

Proof.
The purity of a state
\hat{\varrho}_{f}
is

{\rm tr}\,\hat{\varrho}_{f}^{2}\!=\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{d^{2}}$}\sum\limits_{n,k=-s}^{s}\sum\limits_{m,\ell=-s}^{s}f(n,k)\,f(m,\ell)\,|\langle n;\!k|m;\!\ell\rangle|^{2}.

(46)

Since

|\langle n;\!k|m;\!\ell\rangle|^{2}\!\leq\!\langle n;\!k|n;\!k\rangle\,\langle m;\!\ell|m;\!\ell\rangle\!=\!1,

(47)

\hat{\varrho}_{f}
is a pure state if and only if
f
is a function of the form (
45
), that is,
\hat{\varrho}_{f}
is one of the discrete coherent states
|m;\!\ell\rangle\langle m;\!\ell|.\qquad\Box

Theorem 6.

If the function
f\!:\!\mathcal{R}\!\times\!\mathcal{R}\!\rightarrow[0,d]
is such that

\qquad\sum\limits_{n,k=-s}^{s}f(n,k)\!=\!d,

(48)

then the mean value

\qquad\left\langle\hat{A}\right\rangle_{\!\!\hat{\varrho}_{f}}\!=\!{\rm tr}(A\,\hat{\varrho}_{f})

(49)

of an observable
\hat{A}\!:\!\mathcal{H}\!\rightarrow\!\mathcal{H}
in the state
\hat{\varrho}_{f}
is

\qquad\left\langle\hat{A}\right\rangle_{\!\!\hat{\varrho}_{f}}\!=\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{d}$}\sum\limits_{n,k=-s}^{s}f(n,k)\,\langle n;\!k|\hat{A}|n;\!k\rangle.

(50)

Proof.
We have

\qquad\begin{array}[]{rl}\!\!\!\left\langle\hat{A}\right\rangle_{\!\!\hat{\varrho}_{f}}\!=&\!\!\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{d}$}\sum\limits_{n,k=-s}^{s}f(n,k)\ {\rm tr}(\hat{A}\,|n;\!k\rangle\langle n;\!k|)\\
=&\!\!\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{d}$}\sum\limits_{n,k=-s}^{s}f(n,k)\sum\limits_{m=-s}^{s}\langle m|\hat{A}|n;\!k\rangle\langle n;\!k|m\rangle\\
=&\!\!\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{d}$}\sum\limits_{n,k=-s}^{s}f(n,k)\sum\limits_{m=-s}^{s}\langle n;\!k|m\rangle\langle m|\hat{A}|n;\!k\rangle\\
=&\!\!\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{d}$}\sum\limits_{n,k=-s}^{s}f(n,k)\,\langle n;\!k|\hat{A}|n;\!k\rangle.\qquad\Box\end{array}

Theorem 7.

If the function
f\!:\!\mathcal{R}\!\times\!\mathcal{R}\!\rightarrow[0,d]
is such that

\qquad\qquad\sum\limits_{n,k=-s}^{s}f(n,k)\!=\!d,

(51)

then, under the Fourier transform,
\hat{\varrho}_{f}
maps as

\qquad\qquad\hat{\varrho}_{f}\ \mapsto\ \hat{\mathfrak{F}}\hat{\varrho}_{f}\hat{\mathfrak{F}}^{\dagger}\!=\!\hat{\varrho}_{g},

(52)

where

g(n,k)\!=\!f(-k,n)
.

Proof.
Since

\begin{array}[]{rl}\langle m|\hat{\mathfrak{F}}|n;\!k\rangle\!=&\!\!\!\langle m|\hat{\mathfrak{F}}\hat{\mathfrak{D}}(n,k)|0;\!0\rangle\!=\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{\sqrt{\langle\mathfrak{g}_{1},\mathfrak{g}_{1}\rangle}}$}\,\hat{\mathfrak{F}}\hat{\mathfrak{D}}(n,k)\mathfrak{g}_{1}(m)\\
=&\!\!\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{\sqrt{\langle\mathfrak{g}_{1},\mathfrak{g}_{1}\rangle}}$}\,\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{\sqrt{d}}$}\sum\limits_{n=-s}^{s}{\rm e}^{-\frac{2\pi{\rm i}}{d}m\ell}\hat{\mathfrak{D}}(n,k)\mathfrak{g}_{1}(\ell)\\
=&\!\!\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{\sqrt{\langle\mathfrak{g}_{1},\mathfrak{g}_{1}\rangle}}$}\,\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{\sqrt{d}}$}\sum\limits_{n=-s}^{s}{\rm e}^{-\frac{2\pi{\rm i}}{d}m\ell}{\rm e}^{-\frac{\pi{\rm i}}{d}nk}\,{\rm e}^{\frac{2\pi{\rm i}}{d}k\ell}\,\mathfrak{g}_{1}(\ell\!-\!n)\\
=&\!\!\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{\sqrt{\langle\mathfrak{g}_{1},\mathfrak{g}_{1}\rangle}}$}\,{\rm e}^{-\frac{\pi{\rm i}}{d}nk}\,\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{\sqrt{d}}$}\sum\limits_{n=-s}^{s}{\rm e}^{-\frac{2\pi{\rm i}}{d}\ell(m-k)}\,\mathfrak{g}_{1}(\ell\!-\!n)\\
=&\!\!\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{\sqrt{\langle\mathfrak{g}_{1},\mathfrak{g}_{1}\rangle}}$}\,{\rm e}^{-\frac{\pi{\rm i}}{d}nk}\,\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{\sqrt{d}}$}\sum\limits_{n=-s}^{s}{\rm e}^{-\frac{2\pi{\rm i}}{d}(\ell+n)(m-k)}\,\mathfrak{g}_{1}(\ell)\\
=&\!\!\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{\sqrt{\langle\mathfrak{g}_{1},\mathfrak{g}_{1}\rangle}}$}\,{\rm e}^{-\frac{\pi{\rm i}}{d}nk}\,{\rm e}^{-\frac{2\pi{\rm i}}{d}n(m-k)}\,\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{\sqrt{d}}$}\sum\limits_{n=-s}^{s}{\rm e}^{-\frac{2\pi{\rm i}}{d}\ell(m-k)}\,\mathfrak{g}_{1}(\ell)\\
=&\!\!\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{\sqrt{\langle\mathfrak{g}_{1},\mathfrak{g}_{1}\rangle}}$}\,{\rm e}^{\frac{\pi{\rm i}}{d}nk}\,{\rm e}^{-\frac{2\pi{\rm i}}{d}nm}\,\hat{\mathfrak{F}}[\mathfrak{g}_{1}](m\!-\!k)\\

\end{array}

we have

=&\!\!\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{d}$}\sum\limits_{n,k=-s}^{s}f(-k,n)\,|n;\!k\rangle\langle n;\!k|.\qquad\Box\end{array}

5
Composite quantum systems

Let
s_{A},s_{B}\!\in\!\{1,2,3,...\}
,
d_{A}\!=\!2s_{A}\!+\!1
,
d_{B}\!=\!2s_{B}\!+\!1
,
d\!=\!d_{A}d_{B}
,

\mathcal{R}_{A}\!=\!\{-s_{A},-s_{A}\!+\!1,...,s_{A}\!-\!1,s_{A}\}
,
\mathcal{H}_{A}\!=\!\mathbb{C}^{d_{A}}\equiv\{\,\psi\!:\!\mathcal{R}_{A}\!\rightarrow\!\mathbb{C}\,\}
,

\mathcal{R}_{B}\!=\!\{-s_{B},-s_{B}\!+\!1,...,s_{B}\!-\!1,s_{B}\}
,
\mathcal{H}_{B}\!=\!\mathbb{C}^{d_{B}}\equiv\{\,\varphi\!:\!\mathcal{R}_{B}\!\rightarrow\!\mathbb{C}\,\}
,

\mathcal{R}\!=\!\mathcal{R}_{A}\!\times\!\mathcal{R}_{B},

\mathcal{H}\!=\!\mathcal{H}_{A}\!\otimes\!\mathcal{H}_{B}\equiv\{\,\Psi\!:\!\mathcal{R}_{A}\!\times\!\mathcal{R}_{B}\!\rightarrow\!\mathbb{C}\,\}
.

The tensor product of two tight frames is a tight frame. Particularly,

\left\{\,|n,\!m;\!k,\!\ell\rangle\!\equiv\!|n,\!m;\!k,\!\ell\rangle_{AB}\!=\!|n;\!k\rangle_{\!{}_{A}}\!\otimes\!|m;\!\ell\rangle_{\!{}_{B}}\ \left|\ \ n,k\!\in\!\mathcal{R}_{A},\ \ m,\ell\!\in\!\mathcal{R}_{B}\,\right.\right\}

is a tight frame in
\mathcal{H}\!=\!\mathcal{H}_{A}\!\otimes\!\mathcal{H}_{B}
, namely

\begin{array}[]{rl}\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{d}$}\!\!\!\sum\limits_{n,k=-s_{A}}^{s_{A}}\sum\limits_{m,\ell=-s_{B}}^{s_{B}}|n,\!m;\!k,\!\ell\rangle\langle n,\!m;\!k,\!\ell|\!=&\!\!\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{d}$}\!\!\!\sum\limits_{n,k=-s_{A}}^{s_{A}}\sum\limits_{m,\ell=-s_{B}}^{s_{B}}|n;\!k\rangle_{\!{}_{A}}\!\otimes\!|m;\!\ell\rangle_{\!{}_{B}}\ {}_{{}_{A}}\!\langle n;\!k|\otimes\!{}_{{}_{B}}\!\langle m;\!\ell|\\
=&\!\!\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{d_{A}d_{B}}$}\!\!\!\sum\limits_{n,k=-s_{A}}^{s_{A}}\sum\limits_{m,\ell=-s_{B}}^{s_{B}}|n;\!k\rangle_{\!{}_{A}}\!\langle n;\!k|\!\otimes\!|m;\!\ell\rangle_{\!{}_{B}}\!\langle m;\!\ell|\\
=&\!\!\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{d_{A}}$}\!\!\!\sum\limits_{n,k=-s_{A}}^{s_{A}}|n;\!k\rangle_{\!{}_{A}}\!\langle n;\!k|\!\otimes\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{d_{B}}$}\!\!\!\sum\limits_{m,\ell=-s_{B}}^{s_{B}}|m;\!\ell\rangle_{\!{}_{B}}\!\langle m;\!\ell|\\
=&\!\!\!\mathbb{I}_{\mathcal{H}_{A}}\!\otimes\mathbb{I}_{\mathcal{H}_{B}}\!=\!\mathbb{I}_{\mathcal{H}}.\end{array}

By using the finite frame quantization, we associate the linear operator

\hat{\varrho}_{f}\!=\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{d}$}\!\!\!\sum\limits_{n,k=-s_{A}}^{s_{A}}\sum\limits_{m,\ell=-s_{B}}^{s_{B}}f(n,m;k,\ell)\,|n,\!m;\!k,\!\ell\rangle\langle n,\!m;\!k,\!\ell|

(63)

to each function

f\!:\!(\mathcal{R}_{A}\!\times\!\mathcal{R}_{B})\!\times\!(\mathcal{R}_{A}\!\times\!\mathcal{R}_{B})\!\rightarrow\![0,d],

(64)

defined on the discrete phase space
\mathcal{R}^{2}
, and satisfying the relation

\sum\limits_{n,k=-s_{A}}^{s_{A}}\sum\limits_{m,\ell=-s_{B}}^{s_{B}}f(n,m;k,\ell)\!=\!d.

(65)

If
f\!:\!\mathcal{R}_{A}\!\times\!\mathcal{R}_{A}\!\rightarrow[0,d_{A}]
and
g\!:\!\mathcal{R}_{B}\!\times\!\mathcal{R}_{B}\!\rightarrow[0,d_{B}]
are such that

\qquad\qquad\sum\limits_{n,k=-s_{A}}^{s_{A}}f(n,k)\!=\!d_{A},\qquad\sum\limits_{m,\ell=-s_{B}}^{s_{B}}g(m,\ell)\!=\!d_{B}

(66)

then

\quad\begin{array}[]{rl}\quad\hat{\varrho}_{f}\!\otimes\!\hat{\varrho}_{g}\!=&\!\!\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{d_{A}}$}\sum\limits_{n,k=-s_{A}}^{s_{A}}f(n,k)\,|n;\!k\rangle_{\!{}_{A}}\!\langle n;\!k|\otimes\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{d_{B}}$}\sum\limits_{m,\ell=-s_{B}}^{s_{B}}g(m,\ell)\,|m;\!\ell\rangle_{\!{}_{B}}\!\langle m;\!\ell|\\
=&\!\!\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{d}$}\sum\limits_{n,k=-s_{A}}^{s_{A}}\sum\limits_{m,\ell=-s_{B}}^{s_{B}}f(n,k)\,g(m,\ell)\,|n;\!k\rangle_{\!{}_{A}}\!\langle n;\!k|\otimes|m;\!\ell\rangle_{\!{}_{B}}\!\langle m;\!\ell|\\
=&\!\!\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{d}$}\sum\limits_{n,k=-s_{A}}^{s_{A}}\sum\limits_{m,\ell=-s_{B}}^{s_{B}}f(n,k)\,g(m,\ell)\,|n;\!k\rangle_{\!{}_{A}}\!\otimes\!|m;\!\ell\rangle_{\!{}_{B}}\,{}_{{}_{A}}\!\langle n;\!k|\!\otimes\!{}_{{}_{B}}\!\langle m;\!\ell|\\
=&\!\!\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{d}$}\sum\limits_{n,k=-s_{A}}^{s_{A}}\sum\limits_{m,\ell=-s_{B}}^{s_{B}}f(n,k)\,g(m,\ell)\,|n,\!m;\!k,\!\ell\rangle\langle n,\!m;\!k,\!\ell|\\
=&\!\!\!\hat{\varrho}_{h},\end{array}

where

Similar to the proof of a)

6
Quantum channels obtained through finite frame quantization

We continue to use the notations from the previous section and choose
an auxiliary system
\mathcal{H}_{A^{\prime}}
such that
{\rm dim}\,\mathcal{H}_{A^{\prime}}\!=\!{\rm dim}\,\mathcal{H}_{A}\!=\!2s_{A}\!+\!1
, and consequently
\mathcal{H}_{A^{\prime}}\!=\!\{\,\psi\!:\!\mathcal{R}_{A}\!\rightarrow\!\mathbb{C}\,\}
.
The pure quantum state

\qquad\qquad|\Phi\rangle\!=\!|\Phi\rangle_{\!{}_{A^{\prime}\!A}}\!=\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{\sqrt{d_{A}}}$}\sum\limits_{i=-s_{A}}^{s_{A}}|i\rangle_{\!{}_{A^{\prime}}}\!\otimes\!|i\rangle_{\!{}_{A}}\!=\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{\sqrt{d_{A}}}$}\sum\limits_{i=-s_{A}}^{s_{A}}|ii\rangle

(73)

is the most entangled state in
\mathcal{H}_{A^{\prime}}\!\otimes\!\mathcal{H}_{A}
. In view of the channel-state duality (also called Choi-Jamiolkowski isomorphism), a quantum channel
\mathcal{E}\!:\!\mathcal{L}(\mathcal{H}_{A})\!\rightarrow\!\mathcal{L}(\mathcal{H}_{B})
satisfying the relation

(\mathbb{I}\!\otimes\!\mathcal{E})(|\Phi\rangle\langle\Phi|)\!=\!\hat{\varrho}

corresponds to each state
\hat{\varrho}\!:\!\mathcal{H}_{A^{\prime}}\!\otimes\!\mathcal{H}_{B}\!\rightarrow\!\mathcal{H}_{A^{\prime}}\!\otimes\!\mathcal{H}_{B}
, up to a normalization.
Particularly, a quantum channel

\mathcal{E}_{f}\!:\!\mathcal{L}(\mathcal{H}_{A})\!\rightarrow\!\mathcal{L}(\mathcal{H}_{B})

corresponds to each state
\hat{\varrho}_{f}\!:\!\mathcal{H}_{A^{\prime}}\!\otimes\!\mathcal{H}_{B}\!\rightarrow\!\mathcal{H}_{A^{\prime}}\!\otimes\!\mathcal{H}_{B}
with
f\!:\!(\mathcal{R}_{A}\!\times\!\mathcal{R}_{B})\!\times\!(\mathcal{R}_{A}\!\times\!\mathcal{R}_{B})\!\rightarrow\![0,d]
satisfying

\qquad\quad\sum\limits_{n,k=-s_{A}}^{s_{A}}\sum\limits_{m,\ell=-s_{B}}^{s_{B}}f(n,m;k,\ell)\!=\!d.

(74)

In the usual way, we prove that
\mathcal{E}_{f}
admits the representation
[
14
]

\mathcal{E}_{f}(\hat{\varrho})\!=\!\sum\limits_{n,k=-s_{A}}^{s_{A}}\sum\limits_{m,\ell=-s_{B}}^{s_{B}}K_{n,m;k,\ell}\ \hat{\varrho}\,K_{n,m;k,\ell}^{\dagger}

(75)

involving the Kraus operators
\hat{K}_{n,m;k,\ell}\!:\!\mathcal{H}_{A}\!\rightarrow\!\mathcal{H}_{B}
,

\hat{K}_{n,m;k,\ell}|i\rangle_{\!{}_{A}}\!=\!\sqrt{\mbox{\small$\frac{f(n,m;k,\ell)}{d}$}}\ {}_{{}_{A^{\prime}}}\!\langle i|n,\!m;\!k,\!\ell\rangle.

(76)

From the definition of
\hat{K}_{n,m;k,\ell}
written in the form

\hat{K}_{n,m;k,\ell}|i\rangle_{\!{}_{A}}\!=\!\sqrt{\mbox{\small$\frac{f(n,m;k,\ell)}{d}$}}\sum\limits_{j=-s_{B}}^{s_{B}}|j\rangle_{\!{}_{B}}\langle ij|n,\!m;\!k,\!\ell\rangle

(77)

we get the relation

{}_{{}_{B}}\!\langle j|\hat{K}_{n,m;k,\ell}|i\rangle_{\!{}_{A}}\!=\!\sqrt{\mbox{\small$\frac{f(n,m;k,\ell)}{d}$}}\ \langle ij|n,\!m;\!k,\!\ell\rangle,

(78)

whence

{}_{{}_{A}}\!\langle i|\hat{K}_{n,m;k,\ell}^{\dagger}|j\rangle_{\!{}_{B}}\!=\!\sqrt{\mbox{\small$\frac{f(n,m;k,\ell)}{d}$}}\ \langle n,\!m;\!k,\!\ell|ij\rangle

(79)

and consequently

{}_{{}_{A}}\!\langle i|\hat{K}_{n,m;k,\ell}^{\dagger}\!=\!\sqrt{\mbox{\small$\frac{f(n,m;k,\ell)}{d}$}}\ \langle n,\!m;\!k,\!\ell|i\rangle_{\!\!{}_{A^{\prime}}}.

(80)

We have

\begin{array}[]{rl}(\mathbb{I}\!\otimes\!\mathcal{E}_{f})(|\Phi\rangle\langle\Phi|)\!=&\!\!\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{d_{A}}$}\sum\limits_{i,j=-s_{A}}^{s_{A}}(\mathbb{I}\!\otimes\!\mathcal{E}_{f})|ii\rangle\langle jj|\\
=&\!\!\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{d_{A}}$}\sum\limits_{i,j=-s_{A}}^{s_{A}}(\mathbb{I}\!\otimes\!\mathcal{E}_{f})|i\rangle_{\!{}_{A^{\prime}}}\!\langle j|\!\otimes\!|i\rangle_{\!{}_{A}}\!\langle j|\\
=&\!\!\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{d_{A}}$}\sum\limits_{i,j=-s_{A}}^{s_{A}}|i\rangle_{\!{}_{A^{\prime}}}\!\langle j|\!\otimes\!\mathcal{E}_{f}(|i\rangle_{\!{}_{A}}\!\langle j|)\\
=&\!\!\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{d_{A}}$}\sum\limits_{i,j=-s_{A}}^{s_{A}}\sum\limits_{n,k=-s_{A}}^{s_{A}}\sum\limits_{m,\ell=-s_{B}}^{s_{B}}|i\rangle_{\!{}_{A^{\prime}}}\!\langle j|\!\otimes\!\hat{K}_{n,m;k,\ell}|i\rangle_{\!{}_{A}}\!\langle j|\,\hat{K}_{n,m;k,\ell}^{\dagger}\\
=&\!\!\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{d_{A}\,d}$}\!\sum\limits_{i,j=-s_{A}}^{s_{A}}\sum\limits_{n,k=-s_{A}}^{s_{A}}\sum\limits_{m,\ell=-s_{B}}^{s_{B}}\!f(n,m;k,\ell)\,|i\rangle_{\!{}_{A^{\prime}}}\!\langle j|\!\otimes\!{}_{{}_{A^{\prime}}}\!\langle i|n,\!m;\!k,\!\ell\rangle\langle n,\!m;\!k,\!\ell|j\rangle_{\!\!{}_{A^{\prime}}}\\
=&\!\!\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{d_{A}}$}\sum\limits_{i,j=-s_{A}}^{s_{A}}|i\rangle_{\!{}_{A^{\prime}}}\!\langle j|\!\otimes\!{}_{{}_{A^{\prime}}}\!\langle i|\varrho_{f}|j\rangle_{\!{}_{A^{\prime}}}\!=\!\mbox{\small$\frac{1}{d_{A}}$}\,\varrho_{f}\\
\end{array}

because

\begin{array}[]{rl}\langle nk|\sum\limits_{i,j=-s_{A}}^{s_{A}}|i\rangle_{\!{}_{A^{\prime}}}\!\langle j|\!\otimes\!{}_{{}_{A^{\prime}}}\!\langle i|\varrho_{f}|j\rangle_{\!{}_{A^{\prime}}}|m\ell\rangle\!=&\!\!\!\sum\limits_{i,j=-s_{A}}^{s_{A}}\langle n|i\rangle\langle j|m\rangle\langle ik|\varrho_{f}|j\ell\rangle\\
=&\!\!\!\sum\limits_{i,j=-s_{A}}^{s_{A}}\delta_{ni}\,\delta_{jm}\,\langle ik|\varrho_{f}|j\ell\rangle\\
=&\!\!\!\langle nk|\varrho_{f}|m\ell\rangle.\end{array}

So, up to a normalization, we have
(\mathbb{I}\!\otimes\!\mathcal{E}_{f})(|\Phi\rangle\langle\Phi|)\!=\!\varrho_{f}
. In addition,

\quad\begin{array}[]{l}\sum\limits_{n,k=-s_{A}}^{s_{A}}\sum\limits_{m,\ell=-s_{B}}^{s_{B}}K_{n,m;k,\ell}^{\dagger}K_{n,m;k,\ell}|i\rangle_{\!{}_{A}}\\
\quad\qquad=\!\sum\limits_{n,k=-s_{A}}^{s_{A}}\sum\limits_{m,\ell=-s_{B}}^{s_{B}}\sqrt{\mbox{\small$\frac{f(n,m;k,\ell)}{d}$}}\sum\limits_{b=-s_{B}}^{s_{B}}K_{n,m;k,\ell}^{\dagger}|b\rangle_{\!{}_{B}}\langle ib|n,\!m;\!k,\!\ell\rangle\\
\quad\qquad=\!\sum\limits_{n,k=-s_{A}}^{s_{A}}\sum\limits_{m,\ell=-s_{B}}^{s_{B}}\sqrt{\mbox{\small$\frac{f(n,m;k,\ell)}{d}$}}\sum\limits_{b=-s_{B}}^{s_{B}}\sum\limits_{a=-s_{A}}^{s_{A}}|a\rangle_{\!{}_{A}}\!\langle a|K_{n,m;k,\ell}^{\dagger}|b\rangle_{\!{}_{B}}\langle ib|n,\!m;\!k,\!\ell\rangle\\
\quad\qquad=\!\sum\limits_{n,k=-s_{A}}^{s_{A}}\sum\limits_{m,\ell=-s_{B}}^{s_{B}}\mbox{\small$\frac{f(n,m;k,\ell)}{d}$}\sum\limits_{b=-s_{B}}^{s_{B}}\sum\limits_{a=-s_{A}}^{s_{A}}|a\rangle_{\!{}_{A}}\,\langle n,\!m;\!k,\!\ell|ab\rangle\langle ib|n,\!m;\!k,\!\ell\rangle\\
\quad\qquad=\!\sum\limits_{n,k=-s_{A}}^{s_{A}}\sum\limits_{m,\ell=-s_{B}}^{s_{B}}\mbox{\small$\frac{f(n,m;k,\ell)}{d}$}\sum\limits_{b=-s_{B}}^{s_{B}}\sum\limits_{a=-s_{A}}^{s_{A}}|a\rangle_{\!{}_{A}}\,\langle ib|n,\!m;\!k,\!\ell\rangle\langle n,\!m;\!k,\!\ell|ab\rangle\\
\quad\qquad=\!\sum\limits_{b=-s_{B}}^{s_{B}}\sum\limits_{a=-s_{A}}^{s_{A}}|a\rangle_{\!{}_{A}}\,\langle ib|\hat{\varrho}_{f}|ab\rangle\\
\quad\qquad=\!\sum\limits_{b=-s_{B}}^{s_{B}}\sum\limits_{a=-s_{A}}^{s_{A}}|a\rangle_{\!{}_{A}}\,\langle ib|(\mathbb{I}\!\otimes\!\mathcal{E})(|\Phi\rangle\langle\Phi|)|ab\rangle\\
\quad\qquad=\!\sum\limits_{b=-s_{B}}^{s_{B}}\sum\limits_{a=-s_{A}}^{s_{A}}|a\rangle_{\!{}_{A}}\,\langle ib|\sum\limits_{j,\ell=-s_{A}}^{s_{A}}|j\rangle_{\!{}_{A^{\prime}}}\!\langle\ell|\!\otimes\!\mathcal{E}_{f}(|j\rangle_{\!{}_{A}}\!\langle\ell|)|ab\rangle\\
\quad\qquad=\!\sum\limits_{b=-s_{B}}^{s_{B}}\sum\limits_{a=-s_{A}}^{s_{A}}|a\rangle_{\!{}_{A}}\,\sum\limits_{j,\ell=-s_{A}}^{s_{A}}\langle i|j\rangle\!\langle\ell|a\rangle\langle b|\mathcal{E}_{f}(|j\rangle_{\!{}_{A}}\!\langle\ell|)|b\rangle\\
\quad\qquad=\!\sum\limits_{a=-s_{A}}^{s_{A}}|a\rangle_{\!{}_{A}}\,{\rm tr}(\mathcal{E}_{f}(|i\rangle_{\!{}_{A}}\!\langle a|))\!=\!\sum\limits_{a=-s_{A}}^{s_{A}}|a\rangle_{\!{}_{A}}\,{\rm tr}(|i\rangle_{\!{}_{A}}\!\langle a|)\!=\!\sum\limits_{a=-s_{A}}^{s_{A}}|a\rangle_{\!{}_{A}}\,\delta_{ai}\!=\!|i\rangle_{\!{}_{A}},\end{array}

for any
i\!\in\!\mathcal{R}_{A}
, and consequently

\sum\limits_{n,k=-s_{A}}^{s_{A}}\sum\limits_{m,\ell=-s_{B}}^{s_{B}}K_{n,m;k,\ell}^{\dagger}K_{n,m;k,\ell}\!=\!\mathbb{I}_{\!\mathcal{H}_{A}}.
