Semilinear wave equations of derivative type with characteristic weights in one space dimension
Shunsuke Kitamura

Abstract
In this paper, we investigate the lifespan estimates of classical solutions of the initial value problems for semilinear wave equations of derivative type with characteristic weights in one space dimension. Such equations provide us basic principles on extending the general theory for nonlinear wave equations to the non-autonomous case. In our results, two characteristic weights interact with each others and produce a different range of parameters on the global-in-time existence from the nonlinear terms of unknown function itself.

&\displaystyle\quad\leq\frac{C}{1+t-x}\left\{\begin{array}[]{lll}\log(1+t+x)&\mbox{for}\ p+b\geq 0,\\
(1+t+x)^{-p-b}&\mbox{for}\ p+b<0.\\
\end{array}\right.\end{array}

From the above, we have

I_{-}(x,t)\leq\frac{C}{1+t-x}\left\{\begin{array}[]{lll}\log^{p}(t+x+R)&\mbox{for}\ p+b\geq 0,\\
(1+t+x)^{-p-b}&\mbox{for}\ p+b<0.\\
\end{array}\right.

When

, we have

\begin{array}[]{ll}I_{-}(x,t)&\displaystyle\leq\int_{(t-x-R)/2}^{(t-x+R)/2}\frac{(t-x+R)^{-ap}ds}{(1+t-x)^{1+a}(1+|2s-t+x|)^{1+b}}ds\\
&\displaystyle\quad+\int_{(t-x+R)/2}^{t-x}\frac{(1+2s-t+x)^{-p(1+a)}ds}{(1+t-x)^{1+a}(1+|2s-t+x|)^{1+b}}ds\\
&\displaystyle\quad+\int_{t-x}^{t}\frac{(1+t-x)^{-p(1+a)}ds}{(1+|2s-t+x|)^{1+a}(1+t-x)^{1+b}}ds\\
&\displaystyle\leq\frac{C(t-x+R)^{-ap}}{(1+t-x)^{1+a}}\\
&\displaystyle\quad+\frac{C}{(1+t-x)^{1+a}}\left\{\begin{array}[]{lll}1&\mbox{for}\ p(1+a)+b>0\\
\log(1+t-x)&\mbox{for}\ p(1+a)+b=0\\
(1+t-x)^{-p(1+a)-b}&\mbox{for}\ p(1+a)+b<0\\
\end{array}\right.\\
&\displaystyle\quad+\frac{C(1+t+x)^{-a}}{(1+t-x)^{p(1+a)+1+b}}\\
\end{array}

When
a<-1
, we have

\begin{array}[]{ll}I_{-}(x,t)&\displaystyle\leq\int_{(t-x-R)/2}^{(t-x+R)/2}\frac{(t-x+R)^{-ap}ds}{(1+t-x)^{1+a}(1+|2s-t+x|)^{1+b}}ds\\
&\displaystyle\quad+\int_{(t-x+R)/2}^{t-x}\frac{(1+t-x)^{-p(1+a)}ds}{(1+t-x)^{1+a}(1+|2s-t+x|)^{1+b}}ds\\
&\displaystyle\quad+\int_{t-x}^{t}\frac{(1+2s-t+x)^{-p(1+a)}ds}{(1+|2s-t+x|)^{1+a}(1+t-x)^{1+b}}ds\\
&\displaystyle\leq\frac{C(t-x+R)^{-ap}}{(1+t-x)^{1+a}}\\
&\displaystyle\quad+\frac{C(1+t-x)^{-p(1+a)}}{(1+t-x)^{1+a}}\left\{\begin{array}[]{lll}1&\mbox{for}\ b>0\\
\log(1+t-x)&\mbox{for}\ b=0\\
(1+t-x)^{-b}&\mbox{for}\ b<0\\
\end{array}\right.\\
&\displaystyle\quad+\frac{C}{(1+t-x)^{1+b}}(1+t+x)^{-(p+1)(1+a)+1}\\
\end{array}

Therefore we obtain

I_{-}\leq CE_{a,b}(T)\quad\mbox{in}\ {\bf R}\times[0,T]\cap\{t-x\geq R\}.

If
(-R\leq)t-x\leq R
,
|t-s-x|\leq s+R
yields that

\begin{array}[]{ll}I_{-}(x,t)&\displaystyle\leq\int_{0}^{(t-x)_{+}}\frac{w^{-p}(x-t+s,s)ds}{(1+|t-x|)^{1+a}(1+|2s-t+x|)^{1+b}}ds\\
&\displaystyle\quad+\int_{(t-x)_{+}}^{t}\frac{w^{-p}(x-t+s,s)ds}{(1+|2s-t+x|)^{1+a}(1+|t-x|)^{1+b}}ds.\end{array}

Since
t-x
is equivalent to
R
, the first term is evaluated with a constant. Therefore, the classification can be based on the weight of the second term.
When
a<0
, we have

I_{-}(x,t)\leq C+C(1+x)^{-a}\leq C(T+2R)^{-a}.

When
a=0
, we have

When

, we have

Therefore we obtain

I_{-}\leq CE_{a,b}(T)\quad\mbox{in}\ {\bf R}\times[0,T]\cap\{-R\leq t-x\leq 0\}.

Summing up all the estimates for
I_{+}
and
I_{-}
, we have

|L_{a}^{\prime}(|U|^{p})|\leq C\|U\|^{p}E_{a}(T)\quad\mbox{in}\ {\bf R}\times[0,T].

This completes the proof of Lemma
3.1
.
