Estimates for the Kolmogorov widths of weighted Sobolev classes with conditions on the 0 0 ‐th and the greatest derivatives This research was carried out with the financial support of the Russian Science Foundation (grant no. 22-21-00204).
A.A. Vasil’eva

Abstract
In this article, we obtain the order estimates for the Kolmogorov widths of sets with conditions on the norm in the weighted Sobolev space W p 1 r W^{r}_{p_{1}} and in the weighted space L p 0 L_{p_{0}} .

First we recall the definition of the Kolmogorov widths. Let
X
be a normed space,
C\subset X
, and let
n\in\mathbb{Z}_{+}
. The Kolmogorov widths of the set
C
in
X
are defined by

d_{n}(C,\,X)=\inf_{L\in{\cal L}_{n}(X)}\sup_{x\in C}\inf_{y\in L}\|x‐y\|,

where
{\cal L}_{n}(X)
is the family of linear subspaces in
X
of dimension at most
n
. For details, see
[
18
]
and
[
25
]
.

In
[
30
]
, the problem on estimating the widths
d_{n}(M,\,L_{q,v}(\Omega))
was studied, where

\displaystyle M=\left\{f:\Omega\rightarrow\mathbb{R},\;\;\left\|\frac{\nabla^{r}f}{g}\right\|_{L_{p_{1}}(\Omega)}\leqslant 1,\;\;\|wf\|_{L_{p_{0}}(\Omega)}\leqslant 1\right\},

(1)

L_{q,v}(\Omega)=\{f:\Omega\rightarrow\mathbb{R},\;\|f\|_{L_{q,v}(\Omega)}:=\|vf\|_{L_{q}(\Omega)}<\infty\}.

In particular, there was considered the following example:
\Omega
is a John domain, the weights have the form

\displaystyle g(x)={\rm dist}^{‐\beta}(x,\,\Gamma),\;\;w(x)={\rm dist}^{‐\sigma}(x,\,\Gamma),\;\;v(x)={\rm dist}^{‐\lambda}(x,\,\Gamma),

(2)

where
\Gamma\subset\partial\Omega
is an
h
‐set,

\displaystyle h(t)=t^{\theta},\quad 0\leqslant\theta<d.

(3)

The definition of a John domain and of an
h
‐set will be given in §3. Here we notice that John domains have no zero angles, and that the Sobolev embedding condition for such domains is the same as for a cube
[
20
,
21
]
. The examples of John domains are domains with Lipschitz boundary and Koch’s snowflake. The examples of
h
‐sets are
k

h(t)=t^{k}
), Koch’s curve, some Cantor‐type sets.

In
[
30
]
order estimates for

were obtained, where the weight functions are given by (

\displaystyle\begin{array}[]{c}r+\frac{d}{q}‐\frac{d}{p_{1}}>0,\;\;r+\frac{d}{p_{0}}‐\frac{d}{p_{1}}>0,\\
\beta+\sigma‐r‐\frac{d}{p_{0}}+\frac{d}{p_{1}}>0,\;\;\beta+\sigma‐r‐\frac{d‐\theta}{p_{0}}+\frac{d‐\theta}{p_{1}}>0.\end{array}

The case when some of these inequalities does not hold was not studied. Here we consider this case, but instead of
M
we take the set

\displaystyle\widehat{M}=\left\{f:\Omega\rightarrow\mathbb{R},\;\;\left\|\frac{\nabla^{r}f}{g}\right\|^{p_{1}}_{L_{p_{1}}(\Omega)}+\left\|\frac{f}{g_{0}}\right\|^{p_{1}}_{L_{p_{1}}(\Omega)}\leqslant 1,\;\;\|wf\|_{L_{p_{0}}(\Omega)}\leqslant 1\right\},

(6)

\displaystyle g_{0}(x)={\rm dist}^{r‐\beta}(x,\,\Gamma)

In this paper, we obtain the order estimates for
