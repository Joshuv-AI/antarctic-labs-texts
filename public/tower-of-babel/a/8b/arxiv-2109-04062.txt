\displaystyle\frac{p(\mathbf{t}_{Z})^{\alpha}}{p(\alpha\,\mathbf{t}_{Z})}.

Therefore

{\rm Tr}\,Z^{\alpha}=\frac{\left[p(\mathbf{t}_{Z})\right]^{\alpha}}{p(\alpha\,\mathbf{t}_{Z})}\,\left({\rm Tr}\,Z\right)^{\alpha}.

(3.17)
