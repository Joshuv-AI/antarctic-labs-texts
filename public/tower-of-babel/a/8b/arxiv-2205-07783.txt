Strong Equivalence of Logic Programs with Counting
Vladimir Lifschitz

Abstract
In answer set programming, two groups of rules are considered strongly equivalent if they have the same meaning in any context. In some cases, strong equivalence of programs in the input language of the grounder gringo can be established by deriving rules of each program from rules of the other. The possibility of such proofs has been demonstrated for a subset of that language that includes comparisons, arithmetic operations, and simple choice rules, but not aggregates. This method is extended here to a class of programs in which some uses of the #count aggregate are allowed. This paper is under consideration for acceptance in TPLP.

Some of the properties of the translation

discussed below refer to
the deductive system of infinitary propositional logic of here-and-there

The derivable objects of

are

—expressions
of the form

, where

is an infinitary propositional
formula, and

\Gamma

formulas (“

To simplify notation, we write
\Gamma
as a list.
We identify a sequent of the form
\Rightarrow F
with the formula
F
.

The axiom schemas of
\emph{HT\/}^{\infty}
are

F\Rightarrow F,

F\lor(F\to G)\lor\neg G

and

\bigwedge_{\alpha\in A}\ \ \bigvee_{F\in\mathcal{H}_{\alpha}}F\to\bigvee_{(F_{\alpha})_{\alpha\in A}}\ \ \bigwedge_{\alpha\in A}F_{\alpha},

(24)

where

is a non-empty family of sets
of formulas; the disjunction in the
consequent of (

(F_{\alpha})_{\alpha\in A}
of the Cartesian product of the family

(\mathcal{H}_{\alpha})_{\alpha\in A}
.
The inference rules of

are the introduction
and elimination rules for the propositional connectives
shown in the table above and the weakening rule

(W)\;\frac{\textstyle\Gamma\Rightarrow F}{\textstyle\Gamma,\Delta\Rightarrow F}.

\!(\land I)\;\frac{\textstyle\Gamma\Rightarrow H\quad\hbox{for all }H\in\mathcal{H}}{\textstyle\Gamma\Rightarrow\mathcal{H}^{\land}}

\quad(\land E)\;\frac{\textstyle\Gamma\Rightarrow\mathcal{H}^{\land}}{\textstyle\Gamma\Rightarrow H}\quad(H\in\mathcal{H})

\!(\lor I)\;\frac{\textstyle\Gamma\Rightarrow H}{\textstyle\Gamma\Rightarrow\mathcal{H}^{\lor}}\quad(H\in\mathcal{H})

(\lor E)\;\frac{\textstyle\Gamma\Rightarrow\mathcal{H}^{\lor}\qquad\Delta,H\Rightarrow F\quad\hbox{for all }H\in\mathcal{H}}{\textstyle\Gamma,\Delta\Rightarrow F}

\!(\to\!\!I)\;\frac{\textstyle\Gamma,F\Rightarrow G}{\textstyle\Gamma\Rightarrow F\to G}

\quad(\to\!\!E)\;\frac{\textstyle\Gamma\Rightarrow F\quad\Delta\Rightarrow F\to G}{\textstyle\Gamma,\Delta\Rightarrow G}

Table 1:

logic. By

and

we denote the conjunction and disjunction of all

is considered shorthand for

, and

The set of
