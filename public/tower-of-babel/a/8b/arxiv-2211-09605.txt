SMEFT as a slice of HEFT’s parameter space
\firstname Alexandre \lastname Salas-Bernárdez, \firstname Juan J. \lastname Sanz-Cillero, \firstname Felipe J. \lastname Llanes-Estrada, \firstname Raquel \lastname Gómez-Ambrosio

Abstract
The Standard Model Effective Field Theory (SMEFT) is the parametrization chosen to interpret many modern measurements. We have recently discussed, building on the work of other groups, that its overall framework can be experimentally tested, beyond simply constraining its parameters. This is because the Higgs Effective Field Theory (HEFT) is somewhat more general, as it does not assume that the Higgs boson h h needs to be embedded in a complex doublet H H on which the Standard Model (SM) and SMEFT are built. As a result, the HEFT parameter spaces for the various relevant channels contains hypersurfaces over which one may use SMEFT to describe data. If experimental measurements of HEFT’s parameters in any of those various channels yield a point outside of any of the hypersurfaces, SMEFT is falsified; meanwhile, its framework remains appropriate (in particular, as long as the SM remains compatible with data). A common necessity of the various possible tests is that processes involving different number of Higgs bosons (maintaining the number and nature of other particles unchanged) need to be contrasted.



1
Introduction: two EFTs for the electroweak sector

Beyond the Standard Model (BSM) searches in experiments at the LHC and other machines have come to be used to constrain
the well-known SMEFT expansion,

\mathcal{L}_{\rm SMEFT}=\mathcal{L}_{\rm SM}+\sum_{n=5}^{\infty}\sum_{i}\frac{c_{i}^{(n)}}{\Lambda^{n-4}}\mathcal{O}_{i}^{(n)}(H)\ .

(1)

There, the various operators are organized by canonical (field and derivative) dimension as exposed by the powers of
\Lambda

coefficients bound by experiment
Grinstein:2007iv
;
Grzadkowski:2010es
;
Li:2020gnx
;
Ellis:2020unq
. The particle content of the electroweak symmetry breaking sector is packaged in a Higgs doublet field, just as in the SM,

\varphi_{0}+i\varphi_{3}\end{pmatrix}=U(\boldsymbol{\omega})\begin{pmatrix}0\\
(v+h_{\rm SMEFT})/\sqrt{2}\end{pmatrix}\,.

(2)

There, the Cartesian coordinates
\varphi_{a}
can be rearranged into the polar decomposition in terms of the
\omega_{i}
Goldstone bosons (which set the orientation of
H
through the unitary matrix
U(\boldsymbol{\omega})
) and the radial coordinate
h_{\rm SMEFT}
(with
|H|=(v+h_{\rm SMEFT})/\sqrt{2}
). If, alternatively,
no assumption about the Higgs-boson
h
being part of a doublet
H
is made, the appropriate effective theory is rather HEFT, directly written in terms of
h
(see
Dobado:2019fxe
for a recent review)

\displaystyle{\cal L}_{\rm HEFT}=\frac{1}{2}\partial_{\mu}h_{\rm HEFT}\partial^{\mu}h_{\rm HEFT}-V(h_{\rm HEFT})+\frac{1}{2}\!\mathcal{F}(h_{\rm HEFT})\partial_{\mu}\omega^{i}\partial^{\mu}\omega^{j}\!\left(\!\delta_{ij}\!+\!\frac{\omega^{i}\!\omega^{j}}{v^{2}\!-\!\boldsymbol{\omega}^{2}}\!\right)\ .

(3)

The
h_{\rm HEFT}
Higgs (or for simplicity,
h
) is exposed in multiplicative functions rescaling the traditional operators of the old electroweak chiral Lagrangian; for an alternative form see
Graf:2022rco
.

It was believed that one could indistinctly convert them into one another, as between Cartesian and polar coordinates,
by wrapping or unwrapping the
H
field to expose
h
and the Goldstone bosons

But several works led by the San Diego group

;
Alonso:2016btr
;
Alonso:2021rac
;

have clarified that while HEFT can always be cast as a SMEFT, the converse is not always true, there are some requirements on the “flare” function of the Higgs field

{\mathcal{F}}(h_{\rm HEFT})=1+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}{a_{n}}\Big(\frac{h_{\rm HEFT}}{v}\Big)^{n}\,,

that we will shortly expose; saliently,
\mathcal{F}
needs to have a zero

and satisfy certain analyticity properties.

In our recent contributions
Gomez-Ambrosio:2022qsi
;
Gomez-Ambrosio:2022why

2
Regularity conditions for the applicability of SMEFT

Alonso, Jenkins and Manohar
Alonso:2015fsp
formulated HEFT in geometrical terms in the space of Higgs-fields and detailed the regularity conditions that the HEFT Lagrangian needs to satisfy to support a SMEFT, employing an elegant covariant formalism.
We will proceed in a more pedestrian way, converting SMEFT into HEFT and viceversa with

\displaystyle|\partial H|^{2}+\frac{1}{2}B(|H|^{2})(\partial(|H|^{2}))^{2}\longleftrightarrow\frac{v^{2}}{4}\mathcal{F}(h_{\rm HEFT})\,{\rm Tr}\{\partial_{\mu}U^{\dagger}\partial^{\mu}U\}+\frac{1}{2}(\partial h_{\rm HEFT})^{2}\,,

(4)

The change from SMEFT to HEFT is straightforward and always possible, with the canonical, nonlinear change of variables given in differential form as

dh_{\rm HEFT}\,=\,\sqrt{1+(v+h_{\rm SMEFT})^{2}B(h_{\rm SMEFT})}\,\,dh_{\rm SMEFT}\,,

(5)

where the flare-function is provided by
\mathcal{F}(h_{\rm HEFT})\,=\,\left(1+h_{\rm SMEFT}/v\right)^{2}
.

However, the reciprocal conversion

h_{\rm HEFT}\,=\,\mathcal{F}^{-1}\left((1+h_{\rm SMEFT}/v)^{2}\right)
from HEFT to SMEFT is not always possible,
because of the need to reconstruct squared operators of the Higgs doublet field
H
necessary for SMEFT, such as

\displaystyle|H|^{2}

\displaystyle=\frac{(v+h_{\rm SMEFT})^{2}}{2}\,,

\displaystyle(\partial|H|^{2})^{2}

\displaystyle=(v+h_{\rm SMEFT})^{2}\,(\partial h_{\rm SMEFT})^{2}=\,2|H|^{2}\,(\partial h_{\rm SMEFT})^{2}\,.

The extra
|H|^{2}
on the right hand side of the second equation
appears then in a denominator

\displaystyle\mathcal{L}_{\rm HEFT}=\underbrace{|\partial H|^{2}}_{=\mathcal{L}_{\rm SM}}\quad+\quad\underbrace{\frac{1}{2}\bigg[\frac{8|H|^{2}}{v^{2}}\bigg((\mathcal{F}^{-1})^{\prime}\left(2|H|^{2}/v^{2}\right)\bigg)^{2}\,\,\,-\,\,\,1\bigg]\,\frac{(\partial|H|^{2})^{2}}{2|H|^{2}}}_{=\Delta\mathcal{L}_{\rm BSM}}\,.

(6)

As SMEFT requires a Taylor expansion as in Eq. (
1

The existence of that zero

\mathcal{F}
, the Higgs potential

Our findings, in agreement with
Cohen:2020xca

1.

The

function in the HEFT Lagrangian density of Eq. (
3
)
\mathcal{F}(h^{\ast})=0

2.

There, its second derivative must satisfy

All odd derivatives vanish,

The restrictions over

Conclusion:

Testing the embedding of SMEFT into HEFT at colliders

The SM is compatible, to date, with the measurements of the electroweak sector. It is a particular case of SMEFT and as a matter of course, all correlations discussed in this article among the coefficients of HEFT (many trivially) are satisfied.

Nevertheless, if the high-luminosity LHC of a future accelerator such as a new
e^{-}e^{+}
machine finds cracks in the SM, testing
Gonzalez-Lopez:2020lpd
