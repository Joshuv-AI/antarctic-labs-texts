Multivariate Priors and the Linearity of Optimal Bayesian Estimators under Gaussian Noise
Leighton P. Barnes 1 , Alex Dytso 2 , Jingbo Liu 3 , and H. Vincent Poor 4

Abstract
Consider the task of estimating a random vector X X from noisy observations Y = X + Z Y=X+Z , where Z Z is a standard normal vector, under the L p L^{p} fidelity criterion. This work establishes that, for 1 ≤ p ≤ 2 1\leq p\leq 2 , the optimal Bayesian estimator is linear and positive definite if and only if the prior distribution on X X is a (non-degenerate) multivariate Gaussian. Furthermore, for p > 2 p>2 , it is demonstrated that there are infinitely many priors that can induce such an estimator.

III
Main Result

The main result of this paper is the following theorem.

Theorem 7
.

Fix some
0\prec\mathsf{A}\prec\mathsf{I}
and assume that
1\leq p=k\leq 2
. Then,
f_{p,k}(y)=\mathsf{A}y,\forall y\in\mathbb{R}^{n}
if and only if

\mathsf{A}
has an eigenvalue greater than or equal to one, then

The proof is given next in a series of steps.

III-A

\mu
is a Tempered Distribution
