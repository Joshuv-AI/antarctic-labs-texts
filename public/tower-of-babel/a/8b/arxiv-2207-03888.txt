Rotatory power reversal induced by magnetic-current in bi-isotropic media
Pedro D. S. Silva a, Manoel M. Ferreira Jr. a

Abstract
Bi-isotropic media constitute a proper scenario for scrutinizing optical effects stemming from magnetoelectric parameters. Chiral magnetic current is a macroscopic effect arising from the chiral magnetic effect that enriches the phenomenology of a classical dielectric medium. This work examines optical aspects of bi-isotropic media in the presence of magnetic conductivity. The full isotropic scenario manifests circular birefringence described by a dispersive rotatory power that changes sign at a given frequency. For a bi-isotropic medium with antisymmetric magnetic conductivity, an intricate dispersive rotatory power is attained, supplied with sign reversal as well. This scenario also indicates a handedness reversion of the medium, an unusual property in dielectrics, which may work as a signature of bi-isotropic media supporting chiral magnetic current.

II
Dispersion relation for
bi-isotropic medium endowed with
\sigma^{B}

We take as starting point the Maxwell equations for a homogeneous and linear medium, here written in accordance with a plane wave ansatz,

\displaystyle\mathbf{k}\cdot\mathbf{D}

\displaystyle=0\,,\quad\mathbf{k}\times\mathbf{H}+\omega\mathbf{D}=-{\mathrm{i}\mathbf{J},}

(3a)

\displaystyle\mathbf{k}\cdot\mathbf{B}

\displaystyle=0\,,\quad\omega\mathbf{B}=\mathbf{k}\times\mathbf{E}\,.

(3b)

We now suppose the validity of isotropic-anisotropic constitutive relations, in which the anisotropy is constrained into the magnetoelectric parameters, that is, the electric permittivity and magnetic permeability are written as

\zeta_{ka}={\mu}^{-1}\delta_{ka},\text{ \ \ }\epsilon_{ij}=\epsilon\delta_{ij},

(4)

so that the constitutive relations (
1
) take the form,

\displaystyle D^{i}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\epsilon\delta_{ij}E^{j}+\alpha_{ij}B^{j},

(5a)

\displaystyle H^{i}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\beta_{ij}E^{j}+\mu^{-1}\delta_{ij}B^{j}.

(5b)

For obtaining wave equations that describe the propagation of electromagnetic waves in such a dielectric medium, we replace the constitutive relation (
5a
) in the second relation of
Eq. (
3a
)
and use Faraday’s law,
\omega\mathbf{B}=\mathbf{k\times E}
, writing

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\mu\omega}\left[\mathbf{k}\times\left(\mathbf{k}\times\mathbf{E}\right)\right]^{i}

\displaystyle+\omega\epsilon\delta_{ij}E^{j}+\beta_{ka}\epsilon_{ijk}k^{j}E^{a}+

\displaystyle+\alpha_{ij}\epsilon_{jmn}k^{m}E^{n}=-\mathrm{i}\sigma^{B}_{ij}B^{j},

(6)

where we have supposed the constitutive relation for the magnetic current density,

J^{i}=\sigma^{B}_{ij}B^{j}.

(7)

with a general magnetic-conductivity tensor,
\sigma^{B}_{ij}
. Using again the Faraday’s law,
Eq. (
6
)
is rewritten as

\left[\mathbf{k}\times\left(\mathbf{k}\times\mathbf{E}\right)\right]^{i}+\omega^{2}\mu\bar{\epsilon}_{ij}E^{j}=0,

(8)

where

\bar{\epsilon}_{in}(\omega)=\epsilon\delta_{in}+\frac{1}{\omega}\left(\beta_{kn}\epsilon_{imk}+\alpha_{ij}\epsilon_{jmn}+\mathrm{i}\frac{\sigma^{B}_{ij}}{\omega}\epsilon_{jmn}\right)k^{m},

(9)

defines the frequency-dependent extended permittivity tensor, which carries
the electric and magnetic response of the medium.
Eq. (
8

can be cast in the form,

M_{ij}E^{j}=0,

(10)

where the tensor
M_{ij}
is given in terms of the refractive index,

\displaystyle M_{ij}

\displaystyle=n^{2}{\delta}_{ij}-n_{i}n_{j}-c^{2}\mu\bar{{\epsilon}}_{ij}.

We have used

IV
Final Remarks

In this work, we have examined the classical electromagnetic propagation in bi-isotropic media supporting magnetic current associated with the CME, describing optical effects of the medium. For the full isotropic case of Sec.
III.1
, defined by bi-isotropic constitutive relations and isotropic magnetic conductivity, we have found circularly polarized propagating modes, yielding circular birefringence expressed in terms of the linear dispersive RP (
29
). It experiences sign reversal at the frequency
\omega=\omega^{\prime}
, with
\omega^{\prime}
given by
Eq. (
30
)
. Such an effect only occurs when
\Sigma\neq 0
and
\alpha^{\prime\prime}<0

In the scenario of Sec.
III.2
, the bi-isotropic dielectric is endowed with an asymmetric magnetic current represented by the antisymmetric conductivity tensor parametrized in terms of the constant 3-vector,
{\bf{b}}
, as pointed out in
Eq. (
32
)
. The propagating modes are composed of a transversal sector, described by circular polarizations, and a longitudinal component. For the
{\bf{b}}
-longitudinal special case, the electric fields recover left- and right-handed circularly polarized vectors. The birefringence is examined in terms of the RP considering two frequency intervals: i)
0<\omega<\omega_{0}
and ii)
\omega>\omega_{0}
, with
\omega_{0}
of
Eq. (
40

\alpha^{\prime\prime}<0

Gueroult
and graphene systems

32

When the rotatory power,

\alpha^{\prime\prime}<0

III.1
