Using the Swing Lemma and Czédli diagrams for congruences of planar semimodular lattices
George Grätzer

Abstract
. A planar semimodular lattice K K is slim if 𝖬 3 \mathsf{M}_{3} is not a sublattice of K K . In a recent paper, G. Czédli found four new properties of congruence lattices of slim, planar, semimodular lattices, including the No Child Property : Let P P be the ordered set of join-irreducible congruences of K K . Let x , y , z ∈ P x,y,z\in P and let z z be a maximal element of P P . If x ≠ y x\neq y , x , y ≺ z x,y\prec z in P P , then there is no element u u of P P such that u ≺ x , y u\prec x,y in P P . We are applying my Swing Lemma, 2015, and a type of standardized diagrams of Czédli’s, to verify Czédli’s four properties.

Swing Lemma

An
SPS lattice

As in my paper

[

Let

(1)

\mathbin{\varrho}_{i}

\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\textup{dn}}}{{\thicksim}}

)

In addition, the sequence (

(2)

2.2.
Czédli diagrams

In the diagram of a planar lattice

normal edge
(
line

If it is the first, we call it a

(

otherwise, a
normal-down edge
(
line

Any edge of slope strictly between
45\degree

135\degree

We use the symbols

,
, and
,
for normal, normal-up, normal-down, and steep, respectively.

Definition 4

(G. Czédli
[
1
]
)
.

A diagram of an SPS lattice

is a

Czédli-diagram
if the middle edge of any covering
\mathsf{N}_{7}
is steep
and all other edges are normal.

Theorem 5

(G. Czédli
[
1
]
)
.

Every slim, planar, semimodular lattice

See the illustrations in this paper for examples of Czédli diagrams.
G. Czédli

In this paper,

Let

If there is no element of

A four-element cell is a

Opposite edges of a

Planar semimodular lattices are

The following statement illustrates the use of Czédli diagrams.

Lemma 6

Let

be a slim rectangular lattice

-edge of

is up-perspective either to an edge in the upper-left boundary of

-edge.

Proof.

If

then there is a
4

If the upper-left edge is
or it is in the upper-left boundary, then we are done.
Otherwise, we proceed the same way until we reach the upper-left boundary.
