Generalized Einstein-Maxwell theory: Seeley-DeWitt coefficients and logarithmic corrections to the entropy of extremal and non-extremal black holes
Sudip Karan, and Binata Panda

Abstract
We present a consolidated manual of Euclidean gravity approaches for finding the logarithmic corrections to the entropy of the full Kerr-Newman family of black holes in both extremal and non-extremal limits. Seeley-DeWitt coefficients for the quadratic fluctuations of a concern gravity theory appear to be the key ingredients in this manual. Following the manual, we calculate the first three Seeley-DeWitt coefficients and logarithmic corrections to the entropy of extremal and non-extremal black holes in a generalized Einstein-Maxwell theory minimally-coupled to additional massless scalar, vector, spin-1/2 Dirac and spin-3/2 Rarita-Schwinger fields. We finally employ the Seeley-DeWitt data to reproduce the logarithmic entropy corrections for extremal black holes in all 𝒩 ≥ 2 \mathcal{N}\geq 2 Einstein-Maxwell supergravity via an alternative local supersymmetrization method.
