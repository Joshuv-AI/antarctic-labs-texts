Comparative Analysis of Finite Field-dependent BRST Transformations Thanks: Talk presented at SQS’15, 03 August – 08 August, 2015, at JINR, Dubna, Russia
Pavel Yu. Moshin, Alexander A. Reshetnyak

Abstract
We present a review of our recent study [ 1 , 2 , 3 , 4 , 5 , 6 ] , in which the concept of finite field-dependent BRST and BRST-antiBRST transformations for gauge theories was introduced, and their properties investigated. An algorithm of exact calculation for the Jacobian of a respective change of variables in the path integral is presented. Applications to the Yang–Mills theory and Standard Model, in view of infra-red (Gribov) peculiarities, are discussed.
