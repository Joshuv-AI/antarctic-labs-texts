Axion model with the SU ⁡ ( 6 ) {\rm SU}(6) unification
Ning Chen, Yutong Liu, Zhaolong Teng

Abstract
We propose an SU ⁡ ( 6 ) {\rm SU}(6) unified theory that solves the strong CP problem with a high-quality axion. This is achieved by an automatic global Peccei-Quinn U ​ ( 1 ) PQ \rm U(1)_{\rm PQ} symmetry and the gauged discrete ℤ 4 ​ ℛ \mathbb{Z}_{4\mathcal{R}} symmetry. With the axion mass predictions of m a ∼ 𝒪 ⁡ ( 10 − 3 ) − 𝒪 ⁡ ( 0.1 ) ​ eV m_{a}\sim\mathcal{O}(10^{-3})-\mathcal{O}(0.1)\,{\rm eV} , as well as a universal axion-photon coupling in the unification models, the QCD axion can be probed in the upcoming experiments of IAXO. An intermediate gauge symmetry breaking scale characterized by the axion decay constant is obtained by the Peccei-Quinn quality argument, which is likely to achieve a successful leptogenesis through the type-I seesaw mechanism. The Higgs sector at the electroweak scale is a type-II two-Higgs-doublet model. The gauge coupling unification is possible with the supersymmetric extension.

We have put forth a SUSY

Historically, the emergent global symmetry was first mentioned in the study of strongly coupled theories.

A high-quality axion with its decay constant is found to be constrained from the PQ-quality requirement to the dimension-six PQ-breaking operator, and is most likely to be directly probed in the future IAXO searches.
