Figure

shows the MicroBooNE data exclusion contour compared with the recently updated MiniBooNE sterile neutrino oscillation result
Aguilar-Arevalo et al. 2022

3+1
oscillation framework. It is worth noting that a pure
\nu_{e}
excess, the assumption of the
3+1
sterile neutrino oscillation explanation to the MiniBooNE anomaly, is disfavored by the recent MicroBooNE LEE results as mentioned in the Letter. A
2\text{--}3\sigma

\nu_{e}
low-energy excess search
Abratenko et al. 2022
and the sterile neutrino oscillation search as shown in this figure.
