One-loop three-point Feynman integrals with Appell F 1 F_{1} hypergeometric functions
Khiem Hong Phan and Dzung Tri Tran

Abstract
New analytic formulas for one-loop three-point Feynman integrals in general space-time dimension ( d d ) are presented in this paper. The calculations are performed at general configurations for internal masses and external momenta. The analytic results are expressed in terms of hypergeometric series F 1 2 {}_{2}F_{1} , F 2 3 {}_{3}F_{2} for special cases and Appell F 1 F_{1} for general cases. Furthermore, we cross-check our analytic results with other references which have carried out the integrals in several special cases.
