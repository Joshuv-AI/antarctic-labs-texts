Conflict graphs of maximally planar subgraphs of Petersen family graphs
Joel Foisy, Justin Raimondi

Abstract
The purpose of this paper is to show that all maximally planar subgraphs of graphs in the Petersen Family have associated conflict graphs unbalanced. All but three strong conflict graphs arising from Petersen Family Graphs are unbalanced, and the three that are balanced all come from K 4 , 4 − e K_{4,4}-e .

Proposition 6
.

Up to symmetry, there are precisely two maximal planar subgraphs of the classic Petersen graph. Each has an unbalanced strong conflict graph.

Proof.

Recall that there is a well-known one-to-one correspondence between the vertices of the Petersen graph and two element subsets of
\{1,2,3,4,5\}

S_{5}

(4,5)

, and via the permutation
(1,2)

PG-\{(\{1,2\},\{4,5\}),(\{3,5\},\{2,4\})\}
.

We can also see that

PG-
