On the nonlinear Schrödinger–Poisson system with positron–electron interaction

Abstract

Introduction

In this paper, we study the Schrödinger–Poisson type systems:

\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}-\Delta u+\lambda u+\left(\mu_{11}\phi_{u}-\mu_{1,2}\phi_{v}\right)u=\displaystyle\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{0}^{2\pi}\left|u+e^{i\theta}v\right|^{p-1}\left(u+e^{i\theta}v\right)d\theta&\text{ in }\mathbb{R}^{3},\\

Lemma 2.2

Suppose that

Proof.

\displaystyle\mu_{11}s^{2}+\mu_{22}\left(1-s\right)^{2}-2\mu_{12}s\left(1-s\right)
