Let

Let

The generating function for

\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}b_{t}(n)q^{n}=\frac{f_{t}}{f_{1}},

Gordan and Ono

then for any positive integer

the arithmetic density of positive integers

Note that a bipartition

of

(\alpha,\beta)

For any positive integers

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}B_{u,v}(n)q^{n}=\frac{f_{u}f_{v}}{f_{1}^{2}}.

(1.1)

We recall some basic facts and definition on modular forms. For more details, one can see
[
10
]
,
[

. We start with some matrix groups. We define

\displaystyle\Gamma:=\mathrm{SL_{2}}(\mathbb{Z})=

\displaystyle\left\{\begin{bmatrix}a&&b\\
c&&d\end{bmatrix}:a,b,c,d\in\mathbb{Z},ad-bc=1\right\},

\displaystyle\Gamma_{\infty}:=

\displaystyle\left\{\begin{bmatrix}1&n\\
0&1\end{bmatrix}:n\in\mathbb{Z}\right\}.

For a positive integer

\displaystyle\Gamma_{0}(N):=

\displaystyle\left\{\begin{bmatrix}a&&b\\
c&&d\end{bmatrix}\in\mathrm{SL_{2}}(\mathbb{Z}):c\equiv 0\pmod{N}\right\},

\displaystyle\Gamma_{1}(N):=

\displaystyle\left\{\begin{bmatrix}a&&b\\
c&&d\end{bmatrix}\in\Gamma_{0}(N):a\equiv d\equiv 1\pmod{N}\right\}

and

\displaystyle\Gamma(N):=\left\{\begin{bmatrix}a&&b\\
c&&d\end{bmatrix}\in\mathrm{SL_{2}}(\mathbb{Z}):a\equiv d\equiv 1\pmod{N},b\equiv c\equiv 0\pmod{N}\right\}.

A subgroup of
\Gamma=\mathrm{SL_{2}}(\mathbb{Z})
is called a congruence subgroup if it contains
\Gamma(N)
for some

and the smallest
N
with this property is called its level. Note that
\Gamma_{0}(N)
and
\Gamma_{1}(N)
are congruence subgroup of level

whereas
\mathrm{SL_{2}}(\mathbb{Z})
and
\Gamma_{\infty}
are congruence subgroups of level
1.
The index of
\Gamma_{0}(N)

\displaystyle[\Gamma:\Gamma_{0}(N)]=N\prod\limits_{p|N}\left(1+\frac{1}{p}\right)

where
p

.

denote the upper half of the complex plane

. The group

\displaystyle\mathrm{GL_{2}^{+}}(\mathbb{R}):=\left\{\begin{bmatrix}a&&b\\

acts on

\begin{bmatrix}a&&b\\

We identify
