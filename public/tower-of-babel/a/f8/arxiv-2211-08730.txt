Coherent Interlayer Coupling in Quasi-Two-Dimensional Dirac Fermions in α \alpha -(BEDT-TTF) 2 I 3
Naoya Tajima, Takao Morinari, Yoshitaka Kawasugi, Ryuhei Oka, Toshio Naito, Reizo Kato

Abstract
Theoretical and experimental studies have supported that the electronic structure of α \alpha -(BEDT-TTF) 2 I 3 under pressure is described by two-dimensional Dirac fermions. When the interlayer tunneling is coherent, the electronic structure of the system becomes three-dimensional, and we expect the peak structure to appear in the interlayer resistivity under magnetic fields. We theoretically and experimentally show that the peak appears in the interlayer resistivity at low temperatures and high magnetic fields. From the experiment, we estimate that the magnitude of the interlayer tunneling is t 1 ∼ 1 t_{1}\sim 1 meV. Our result opens the door to investigating the three-dimensional electronic structure of α \alpha -(BEDT-TTF) 2 I 3 .

The band calculation suggested

The first band and the second band touch each other

Since there is one hole per two molecules,

Tajima et al. 2009

Furthermore, the phase of the Dirac fermions is confirmed by
