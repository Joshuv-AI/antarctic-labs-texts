|M|=n-1

There exists at least
(m-2)t+2
vertices of

, say

not belongs to
M

It can be check that
|Y|=|V(K)|-2(n-1)

|Y|=jt-2(n-1)=(m-2)t+(j+2-m)t-2(n-1)=(m-2)t+(j+2-m)\lceil\frac{2n}{j+2-m}\rceil-2(n-1)

m
parts of

|Y|=(m-2)t+2
