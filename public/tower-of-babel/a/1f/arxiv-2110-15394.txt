Towards a Combinatorial Model for q \mathchar 29041 -weight Multiplicities of Simple Lie Algebras (Extended Abstract)
Cédric Lecouvey, Cristian Lenart, Adam Schultze

Abstract
. Kostka-Foulkes polynomials are Lusztig’s q \mathchar 29041 -analogues of weight multiplicities for irreducible representations of semisimple Lie algebras. It has long been known that these polynomials have non-negative coefficients. A statistic on semistandard Young tableaux with partition content, called charge , was used to give a combinatorial formula exhibiting this fact in type A \mathchar 28993 . Defining a charge statistic beyond type A \mathchar 28993 has been a long-standing problem. Here, we take a completely new approach based on the definition of Kostka-Foulkes polynomials as an alternating sum over Kostant partitions, which can be thought of as formal sums of positive roots. The hope is that the simplicity of this new crystal-like model will naturally extend to other classical types.

This positive expansion in terms of Kostant partitions gives way to a statistic which is simply read by counting the number of parts of the Kostant partitions. The hope is that the simplicity of this new crystal-like model will naturally extend to other classical types.
