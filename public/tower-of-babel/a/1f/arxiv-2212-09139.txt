Spin- 2 2 operators in AdS 2 /CFT 1


Abstract
We study spin-2 fluctuations around an infinite family of warped backgrounds of the form AdS 2 × S 2 × T 4 × S 1 × ℐ ρ \text{AdS}_{2}\times\text{S}^{2}\times\text{T}^{4}\times\text{S}^{1}\times\mathcal{I}_{\rho} in the type IIB theory, which possesses 𝒩 = 4 \mathcal{N}=4 supersymmetry. We find that there exists a special set of solutions which is independent of the background data; the minimal universal class. This particular class of solutions corresponds to operators with scaling dimension Δ = ℓ + 1 \Delta=\ell+1 , with ℓ \ell being the quantum number of the angular-momentum on the S 2 \text{S}^{2} . Using the mode solutions of these spin-2 states, we compute the central charge of the dual field theories. We comment on the relation of our results to the seed AdS 3 × S 2 × T 4 × ℐ ρ \text{AdS}_{3}\times\text{S}^{2}\times\text{T}^{4}\times\mathcal{I}_{\rho} backgrounds.

Our starting point is the action for the type IIB theory, which we only need schematically. In the Einstein frame it reads
