T. Schneider

Abstract
The superfluid density ρ ∥ s f ( T ) = λ ∥ − 2 ( T ) \rho_{\|sf}\left(T\right)=\lambda_{\|}^{-2}\left(T\right) of overdoped La 2-x Sr x CuO 4 thin films of high quality have been measured with T c T_{c} (defined by the onset of the Meissner effect ) from 5.1 5.1 to 41.6 41.6 K by Božović et al. Given this T c T_{c} the superfluid density ρ ∥ s f ( T ) \rho_{\|sf}\left(T\right) shows no clear evidence of critical fluctuations and no indication of vortex unbinding as T → T c T\rightarrow T_{c} . Nevertheless, ρ ∥ s f ( T ) \rho_{\|sf}\left(T\right) displays the behavior expected for a quantum phase transition (QPT) in the ( 3 + 1 ) \left(3+1\right) D-xy dimensional universality class, with ρ ∥ s f ( T ) ∝ T c − 2 \rho_{\|sf}\left(T\right)\propto T_{c}^{-2} as T → 0 T\rightarrow 0 . Noting that for any finite system the continuous transition will be rounded we perform a finite size scaling analysis. Having established the precondition for the occurrence of a QPT mapping on the ( 3 + 1 ) \left(3+1\right) D-xy model, we explore the consistency with the hallmarks of this transition. \xi_{\perp}^{t}\left(t\right)=\xi_{\perp 0}^{t}t^{-2/3}=\left(f_{\|}^{2}\left(0\right)/\Lambda T_{c}\right)t^{-2/3}
which leads to an inevitable
enhancement of the finite size effect controlled by the ratio
L_{c}/\xi_{\perp}^{t}\left(T\right)=\left(L_{c}/\xi_{\perp 0}^{t}\right)t^{2/3}

(Eq. (
7
)) because
L_{c}/\xi_{\perp}^{t}\left(T\right)=\left(L_{c}/\xi_{\perp 0}^{t}\right)t^{2/3}\propto L_{c}T_{c}t^{2/3}
as

T_{c}\rightarrow 0
. In contrast, the zero temperature counterpart

L_{c}/\xi_{\perp}^{t}\left(0\right)
remains large in spite of

\xi_{\perp}^{t}\left(0\right)\propto 1/T_{c}
for any reasonable
L_{c}

because
t^{2/3}
does not enter.

Another manifestation of the quantum fluctuations comes from their effect on
the condensate and Cooper-pair density. In the Ginzburg-landau version of
the D-xy-model, where amplitude
\left|\Psi\left(R\right)\right|
and phase
\varphi\left(R\right)
are taken into account, these
densities can be expressed in terms of the complex order parameter
\Psi\left(R\right)\exp(i\varphi\left(R\right)
. The superfluid density
\rho_{\|s}\left(0\right)=\lambda_{\|}^{-2}\left(0\right)
, the
helicity modulus
\Upsilon_{\|}
, the condensate density
\left\langle\left|\Psi\right|\right\rangle^{2}
and the Cooper-pair
counterpart
\left\langle\left|\Psi\right|^{2}\right\rangle

are found to scale as
Nguyen and Sudbø 1999

\displaystyle\rho_{\|s}\left(t\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\lambda_{\|0}^{-2}t^{\nu}=(16\pi^{3}/\Phi_{0}^{2})\Upsilon_{\|0}t^{\nu}\neq\left(\hbar^{2}/2m_{\|}\right)\left|\left\langle\Psi\right\rangle\right|^{2},

\displaystyle\text{ }\left\langle\left|\Psi\right|\right\rangle^{2}

\displaystyle\propto

\displaystyle t^{2\beta},\text{ }\left\langle\left|\Psi\right|^{2}\right\rangle>\left\langle\left|\Psi\right|\right\rangle^{2},\text{ }\left\langle\left|\delta\Psi\right|^{2}\right\rangle>0.

(23)

Accordingly, the superfluid and condensate densities vanish as
T\rightarrow T_{c}
while the Cooper pair density remains finite at and above
T_{c}
.
Indeed, at
T_{c}
it scales as
\left\langle\left|\delta\Psi\right|^{2}\right\rangle_{T=T_{c}}\propto T_{c}
. The difference between
the superfluid and condensate density stems from the fact
that in the 3D-xy model
\nu=2\beta-\eta

Fisher 1969
;
Josephson 1967
with

\eta\simeq 0.0381

Hasenbusch and Török 1999
. The appearance of 3D-xy critical
behavior stems from the fact that in extreme type II superconductors the
amplitude fluctuations are dominated by phase fluctuations over a sizeable
temperature regime
Nguyen and Sudbø 1999
. Note that in the QPT considered here, where

T_{c}\propto 1/\lambda_{\parallel}\left(0\right){}\propto 1/\lambda_{\parallel 0}
(Eq. (
21
)), the extreme type II limit (
\lambda_{\parallel}\rightarrow\infty
) is attained as
T_{c}\rightarrow 0
.

The mapping of the QPT on the
\left(3+1\right)
D-xy
model implies mean-field critical behavior. Therefore, taking the quantum
fluctuations in the 3D-xy model into account, the superfluid density
\rho_{\|s}\left(0\right)=\lambda_{\|}^{-2}\left(0\right)
, the
helicity modulus
\Upsilon_{\|}\left(0\right)
, the condensate
density
\left\langle\left|\Psi\right|\right\rangle_{T=0}^{2}

and the Cooper-pair counterpart
\left\langle\left|\Psi\right|^{2}\right\rangle_{T=0}
scale as

\displaystyle\rho_{\|s}\left(0\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\lambda_{\|}^{-2}\left(0\right)=(16\pi^{3}/\Phi_{0}^{2})\Upsilon_{\|}\left(0\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left(\hbar^{2}/2m_{\|}\right)\left|\left\langle\Psi\right\rangle\right|_{T=0}^{2},

\displaystyle\left\langle\left|\Psi\right|\right\rangle_{T=0}^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left\langle\left|\Psi\right|^{2}\right\rangle_{T=0}\propto\delta\propto T_{c}^{2\text{ }},

(24)

where we used Eq. (
17
).
\delta
denotes the tuning parameter of
the QPT. Remarkably, not only the superfluid density is suppressed by
quantum fluctuations as
T_{c}\rightarrow 0
but also the condensation and
Cooper pair density.

IV
Summary and Conclusions

In summary, our analysis of the penetration depth data from Božović

et al.
Božović et al. 2016
for thin overdoped La
2-x
Sr
x
CuO
4

\lambda_{\parallel f}^{-2}\left(T\right)

size effect. It stems from the fact that in an infinite and homogeneous
3D-xy system the transverse correlation length diverges as
\xi_{\perp}^{t}\left(T\right)=\xi_{\perp 0}^{t}t^{-\nu}

t=\left(1-T/T_{c}\right)

\nu\simeq 2/3

\xi_{\perp f}^{t}\left(T_{c}\right)
the divergence is cut off. In homogenous films

L_{cf}
and in
the inhomogeneous ones by
L_{ci}<L_{cf}

domains. Nevertheless, in the films analyzed here, the
3

regime is reached. The finite size scaling analysis of the rounded
transition provided estimates for the
T_{c}

\lambda_{\parallel 0}\left(T_{c}\right)

the onset of the Meissner effect

and
\lambda_{\parallel 0}
uncovered the

0.3

estimate
\lambda_{\parallel}^{-2}\left(0\right)

1

T_{c}
,
