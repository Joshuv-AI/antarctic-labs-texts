Different versions of soft-photon theorems exemplified at leading and next-to-leading terms for pion-pion and pion-proton scattering
Piotr Lebiedowicz, Otto Nachtmann, Antoni Szczurek

Abstract
We investigate the photon emission in pion-pion and pion-proton scattering in the soft-photon limit where the photon energy ω → 0 \omega\to 0 . The expansions of the π − ​ π 0 → π − ​ π 0 ​ γ \pi^{-}\pi^{0}\to\pi^{-}\pi^{0}\gamma and the π ± ​ p → π ± ​ p ​ γ \pi^{\pm}p\to\pi^{\pm}p\gamma amplitudes, satisfying the energy-momentum relations, to the orders ω − 1 \omega^{-1} and ω 0 \omega^{0} are derived. We show that these terms can be expressed completely in terms of the on-shell amplitudes for π − ​ π 0 → π − ​ π 0 \pi^{-}\pi^{0}\to\pi^{-}\pi^{0} and π ± ​ p → π ± ​ p \pi^{\pm}p\to\pi^{\pm}p , respectively, and their partial derivatives with respect to s s and t t . The structure term which is non singular for ω → 0 \omega\to 0 is determined to the order ω 0 \omega^{0} from the gauge-invariance constraint using the generalized Ward identities for pions and the proton. For the reaction π − ​ π 0 → π − ​ π 0 ​ γ \pi^{-}\pi^{0}\to\pi^{-}\pi^{0}\gamma we discuss in detail the soft-photon theorems in the versions of both F.E. Low and S. Weinberg. We show that these two versions are different and must not be confounded. Weinberg’s version gives the pole term of a Laurent expansion in ω \omega of the amplitude for π − ​ π 0 → π − ​ π 0 ​ γ \pi^{-}\pi^{0}\to\pi^{-}\pi^{0}\gamma around the phase-space point of zero radiation. Low’s version gives an approximate expression for the above amplitude at a fixed phase-space point, corresponding to non-zero radiation. Clearly, the leading and next-to-leading terms in theses two approaches must be, and are indeed, different. We show their relation. We also discuss the expansions of differential cross sections for π − ​ π 0 → π − ​ π 0 ​ γ \pi^{-}\pi^{0}\to\pi^{-}\pi^{0}\gamma with respect to ω \omega for ω → 0 \omega\to 0 .

VIII
Conclusions

In this article we have first discussed
\pi^{-}\pi^{0}
elastic scattering
and soft-photon radiation in this reaction.
We have shown that the soft-photon theorems of Low
Low 1958

and Weinberg
Weinberg 1964
;
Weinberg 1965
have a different meaning.
In
Low 1958
an approximate expression for the radiative amplitude
at a
given
phase-space point

(
p_{1}^{\prime}
,
p_{2}^{\prime}
,
k
)
is presented.
In
Weinberg 1965
the pole term of the radiative amplitude
in a Laurent expansion in the photon energy
\omega
around
the phase-space point
(
p_{1}
,
p_{2}
,
k=0
)
is given,
where
p_{1}
,
p_{2}
are the momenta of the non-radiative reaction.
We have recalled and discussed our calculation of
Lebiedowicz et al. 2022a

where we presented the
\omega^{0}
term in the above Laurent expansion.
We have discussed in detail that the next-to-leading terms
presented in
Low 1958
and
Lebiedowicz et al. 2022a
,
respectively, must be different,
since their meaning is different.
We have then constructed the Laurent expansion of Low’s formula (
5.3
)
around the phase-space point
(
p_{1}
,
p_{2}
,
k=0
)

and found complete agreement with our results of
Lebiedowicz et al. 2022a
.
We emphasized that Low’s formula (
5.3
) for
the radiative amplitude
{\cal M}_{\lambda}

is
only
valid for the
one physical value

of
k=p_{a}+p_{b}-p_{1}^{\prime}-p_{2}^{\prime}
.
In contrast, in (
4.25
) we give a Laurent expansion of
{\cal M}_{\lambda}

around the phase-space point (
p_{1}
,
p_{2}
,
k=0
) where,
of course, we can vary the expansion parameters
(k,\bm{l_{1\perp}})

which are defined at the beginning of Sec.
IV
.

In
Lebiedowicz et al. 2022a
we wrongly interpreted Low’s formula
as an expansion of
{\cal M}_{\lambda}

around (
p_{1}
,
p_{2}
,
k=0
).
With this premiss we came to the conclusion that Low’s formula
violates energy-momentum conservation.
We have now discussed in detail
that the above premiss does not hold.
Thus, also our above conclusion does not hold.
Therefore, the part of Sec. III of
Lebiedowicz et al. 2022a
after Eq. (3.29) is obsolete
since it was based on a wrong premiss.
This was already explained in
Nachtmann

and will be subject of a forthcoming erratum
to
Lebiedowicz et al. 2022a
.
We were confused by the fact that in the literature frequently
Weinberg’s formula (
4.26
) is referred to as Low’s formula.
But, as we hope to have demonstrated in the present paper,
these two formulas have a
different
meaning.
This was the subject of our Secs.
IV
and
V
.

In Sec.
VI
we discussed the expansions of different cross sections
of
\pi^{-}(p_{a})+\pi^{0}(p_{b})\to\pi^{-}(p_{1}^{\prime})+\pi^{0}(p_{2}^{\prime})+\gamma(k)
for
\omega\to 0
.
We found that for calculating these expansions for

\omega\text{d}\sigma/(\text{d}\omega\,\text{d}\Omega_{\hat{k}}\,\text{d}\Omega_{\hat{p}_{1}^{\prime}})
and

\omega\text{d}\sigma/(\text{d}\omega\,\text{d}\Omega_{\hat{k}}\,\text{d}\Omega_{\hat{p}_{2}^{\prime}})

it was necessary to have at our disposal the expansion of
the radiative amplitude in
all
directions

(k,\bm{\hat{p}_{1}^{\prime}})
of the phase space around
the non-radiative point
(k=0,\bm{\hat{p}_{1}})
.
We calculated then the expansion in
\omega
for
\omega\to 0

of the cross section
\omega\text{d}\sigma/\text{d}\omega

from the above two differential cross sections.
As it must be, we found the same result using these two ways.

Finally we have discussed in Sec.
VII
the soft-photon expansion
of the amplitudes for the reactions
\pi^{\pm}p\to\pi^{\pm}p\gamma
.
We have presented a strict theorem of QCD.
Given the amplitudes for
\pi^{\pm}p\to\pi^{\pm}p
scattering
the amplitudes for soft-photon production,

\pi^{\pm}p\to\pi^{\pm}p\gamma
,
have been calculated exactly
to the orders
\omega^{-1}
and
\omega^{0}
.
These two orders of the expansion of the

\pi^{\pm}p\to\pi^{\pm}p\gamma
amplitudes
are completely determined by the
\pi^{\pm}p\to\pi^{\pm}p
on-shell amplitudes.
For real photon emission the result is given in (
7.15
)–(
VII
).
The results for soft virtual photon emission and
consequences for cross sections are given
in
Lebiedowicz et al. 2023a
.

The derivation of our results, especially for the
\pi^{\pm}p\to\pi^{\pm}p\gamma

reaction, involved lengthy calculations.
Thus, we found it convenient to check our general results in a model
which satisfies the QFT constraints for these reactions
as listed at the end of Sec.
I
.
Such a model is the tensor-Pomeron model of
Ewerz et al. 2014

but improved for reactions involving photons as shown
in Sec. IV A of
Lebiedowicz et al. 2022a

for
\pi^{-}\pi^{0}\to\pi^{-}\pi^{0}\gamma

and in
Lebiedowicz et al. 2022b
;
Lebiedowicz et al. 2023b

for
pp\to pp\gamma
.
Applying this model as done in
Lebiedowicz et al. 2022a
to

\pi^{-}\pi^{0}\to\pi^{-}\pi^{0}\gamma
and expanding
the resulting expressions [Eqs. (4.19), (4.22)–(4.24) from
Lebiedowicz et al. 2022a
]
in
\omega
for
\omega\to 0
we get indeed
the result expected from
(3.27), (3.28), and (A1),
of
Lebiedowicz et al. 2022a

which, for real photon emission,
we reproduce in
(
4.23
)–(
4.25
) of the present paper.
We also checked that this improved tensor-Pomeron model gives
amplitudes for
\pi^{\pm}p\to\pi^{\pm}p\gamma

where the expansion in
\omega
agrees with the general results
(
VII
)–(
VII
) in our present paper.
The details for this will be discussed elsewhere.

To summarize: we hope to have clarified the meaning of the soft-photon
expansions in the versions of F.E. Low
Low 1958

and S. Weinberg
Weinberg 1964
;
Weinberg 1965
.
These expansions must not be confounded,
as they have a
different
meaning.
We have shown how these two expansions are related.
We have discussed the Laurent expansions in the photon energy
\omega

for
\omega\to 0
for the reactions

\pi^{-}\pi^{0}\to\pi^{-}\pi^{0}\gamma
and

\pi^{\pm}p\to\pi^{\pm}p\gamma
to the orders

\omega^{-1}
(the pole term of
Weinberg 1965
) and
\omega^{0}
.
Our results are strict consequences of QCD.
We hope that they will be helpful also for experimentalists
who are embarked to check soft-photon theorems.
For
\pi^{\pm}p\to\pi^{\pm}p\gamma
this could be done perhaps at COMPASS
and
for
\pi^{\pm}p\to\pi^{\pm}p\gamma^{*}(\to e^{+}e^{-})

in HADES at GSI
Rathod 2022
.
For ALICE 3
ALI 2022

we would need the corresponding theoretical calculations
for
pp\to pp\gamma
.
Our methods are suited to calculate
in a rigorous way the expansion of the amplitude
to the orders
\omega^{-1}
and
\omega^{0}
.
These calculations certainly will not be easy.

After our paper was finished there appeared on the arXiv
the paper
Fadin and Khoze 2024

where our work of
Lebiedowicz et al. 2022a
was criticized.
Some of this criticism is acceptable, but we have already
discussed our misinterpretation
of the soft-photon theorem of
Low 1958
in
Ref.
Nachtmann
.
In our present paper we again discuss extensively this
misinterpretation. But, in this connection,
a main topic of our paper is to show that the soft-photon theorems
of
Weinberg 1964
;
Weinberg 1965

(to which there is no reference in
Fadin and Khoze 2024
)
and of
Low 1958
are different
and should not be confounded.
Of course, they are related, as we have discussed in Sec.
V
.
