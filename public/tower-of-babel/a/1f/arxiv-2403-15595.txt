Connectivity degrees of complements of closed sets in continua Journal: a journal
Mauricio Chacón-Tirado, César Piceno

Abstract
In the literature, various types of points and meager sets whose complements are connected have been studied, such as colocally connected points, non-weak cut points/sets, non-block points/sets, shore points/sets, etc. We extend that study, in the following way: considering a continuum X X and a natural number n n , we investigate sets A ∈ 2 X A\in 2^{X} meeting the criterion that X − A X-A has at most n n components, and we introduce degrees of connectivity of the complement of A A . When n = 1 n=1 and A A is meager or singleton, these new definitions are equivalent to the known definitions of non-cut points/sets.

If the space is not connected, we are also interested in knowing if it is composed of a finite number of components and how “strongly disconnected” is the space. 4

The results presented herein highlight how different types of mappings, such as onto mappings, open mappings, and monotone mappings, preserve some properties of non-
n
-cut sets.

We start with a lemma.

Lemma 4.1
.

Let
f:X\to Y
be an onto mapping between continua. If
y\in Y
, and
D\in 2^{X}
are such that
f^{-1}(y)\subset int_{X}(D)
, then
y\in int_{Y}(f(D))
.

Proof.

Let
y\in Y
,
D\in 2^{X}
and assume
f^{-1}(y)\subset int_{X}(D)
.
Notice that
y\in Y-f(X-int_{X}(D))\subset f(D)
and
Y-f(X-int_{X}(D))
is open in
Y
. Thus
y\in int_{Y}(f(D))
.
∎

Proposition 4.2
.

Let
f:X\to Y
be an onto mapping between continua, let

and let
n\in\mathbb{N}
. The following statements hold:

1.

If

2.

if
f^{-1}(A)\in NWC_{n}(X)
, then
A\in NWC_{n}(Y)
;

3.

if
f^{-1}(A)\in NB_{n}(X)
, then
A\in NB_{n}(Y)
;

4.

if
f^{-1}(A)\in NB_{n}^{*}(X)
, then
A\in NB_{n}^{*}(Y)

5.

if
f^{-1}(A)\in S_{n}(X)
, then
A\in S_{n}(Y)

6.

f^{-1}(A)\in NSC_{n}(X)

Observe that the statements are true if

1.

Assume that
f^{-1}(A)\in CC_{n}(X)

is a
n
-
Q1

contained in

\bigcup_{i=1}^{m}D_{x_{i}}\subset X-f^{-1}(A)

4.1

f(\bigcup_{i=1}^{m}D_{x_{i}})\subset Y-A
is a subcontinuum of

such that
f(B)\cap f(\bigcup_{i=1}^{n}D_{a_{i}})\neq\emptyset
and has

in its interior, so

witnesses that

is a
n
-
Q1

.

2.

Assume that
f^{-1}(A)\in NWC_{n}(X)

witnessing that

is a
n
-
Q2

and

is
n
-
Q2

,

f(D)\subset Y-A
is a continuum,

Y-A
is a
n
-
Q2

.

3.

Assume that
f^{-1}(A)\in NB_{n}(X)
. Let
y\in Y-A
and

is a
n
-
