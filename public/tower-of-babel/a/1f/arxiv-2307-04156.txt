Abstract
. In this article, we characterize the image of the Brylinski-Radon transform in characteristic p > 0 p>0 via Beilinson’s theory of singular supports. We also provide an alternate proof of Brylinski’s results over ℂ {\mathbb{C}} , which also works for sheaves with finite coefficients. Along the way, we also obtain a microlocal criterion for the descent of perverse sheaves which could be of independent interest.
