Abstract
In this paper, we prove an operator version of the Jensen’s inequality and its converse for h h -convex functions. We provide a refinement of the Jensen type inequality for h h -convex functions. Moreover, we prove the Hermite-Hadamard’s type inequality and a multiple operator version of the Jensen’s inequality for h h -convex functions. In particular, a result for convex, P P -class, s s -convex, Godunova-Levin, and s s -Godunova-Levin functions can be deduced.
