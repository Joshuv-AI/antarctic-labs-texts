Disentangling the high- and low-cutoff scales via the trilinear Higgs couplings in the type I two-Higgs-doublet model
Sin Kyu Kang, Jinheung Kim, Soojin Lee, Jeonghyeon Song

Abstract
The type I two-Higgs-doublet model in the inverted Higgs scenario can retain the theoretical stability all the way up to the Planck scale. The Planck-scale cutoff, Λ cut Planck \Lambda_{\rm cut}^{\rm Planck} , directly impacts the mass spectra such that all the extra Higgs boson masses should be light below about 160 GeV. However, the observation of the light masses of new Higgs bosons does not indicate the high-cutoff scale because a low-cutoff scale can also accommodate the light masses. Over the viable parameter points that satisfy the theoretical requirements and the experimental constraints, we show that the trilinear Higgs couplings for low Λ cut \Lambda_{\rm cut} are entirely different from those for the Planck-scale cutoff. The most sensitive coupling to the cutoff scale is from the h h - h h - h h vertex, where h h is the lighter CP -even Higgs boson at a mass below 125 GeV. The gluon fusion processes of g ​ g → h ​ h gg\to hh and g ​ g → A ​ A gg\to AA are insensitive to the cutoff scale, yielding a small variation of the production cross sections, 𝒪 ⁡ ( 1 ) ​ fb \mathcal{O}(1)\,{\rm fb} , according to Λ cut \Lambda_{\rm cut} . The smoking-gun signature is from the triple Higgs production of q ​ q ¯ ′ → W ∗ → H ± ​ h ​ h q\bar{q}^{\prime}\to W^{*}\to H^{\pm}hh , which solely depends on the h h - h h - h h vertex. The cross section for Λ cut = 1 ​ TeV \Lambda_{\rm cut}=1\,{\rm TeV} is about 10 3 10^{3} times larger than that for the Planck-scale cutoff. Since the decay modes of H ± → W ∗ ​ h / W ∗ ​ A H^{\pm}\to W^{*}h/W^{*}A and h / A → b ​ b h/A\to bb are dominant, the process yields the 6 ​ b + ℓ ​ ν 6b+\ell\nu final state, which enjoys an almost background-free environment. Consequently, the precision measurement of p ​ p → H ± ​ h ​ h pp\to H^{\pm}hh can probe the cutoff scale of the model.

VI
Conclusion

Beyond the studies on how high the cutoff scale of a new physics model can go up,
we have pursued an efficient observable to distinguish the high- and low-cutoff scales.
The type I in the 2HDM has been considered for the inverted scenario where
the observed Higgs boson at a mass of
125{\;{\rm GeV}}
is the heavier
CP
-even Higgs boson
H
.
We have first obtained the still-available parameter points
that satisfy the theoretical requirements, the experimental constraints, and the cutoff scale above
1{\;{\rm TeV}}
.
The viable parameter space at the electroweak scale is already limited
such that
M_{A},M_{H^{\pm}}\lesssim 430{\;{\rm GeV}}
and
m_{h}\gtrsim 62.5{\;{\rm GeV}}
.
Through the calculation of the cutoff scale
\Lambda_{\rm cut}
of each viable parameter point by using the RGE,
we have shown that the inverted type I can retain the stability all the way up to the Planck scale.

The condition of
\Lambda_{\rm cut}>10^{18}{\;{\rm GeV}}
requires the light masses of the extra Higgs bosons
like
M_{A},M_{H^{\pm}}\lesssim 160{\;{\rm GeV}}
.
However, the light masses alone cannot guarantee the high-cutoff scale
because the parameter points with light masses accommodate
\Lambda_{\rm cut}
from
1{\;{\rm TeV}}
to
10^{19}{\;{\rm GeV}}
.
Targeting at the phenomenologically challenging case of
t_{\beta}>10
,
we have investigated the trilinear Higgs couplings versus
\Lambda_{\rm cut}
.
Although the values of all the trilinear couplings for
\Lambda_{\rm cut}=1{\;{\rm TeV}}

are different from those for
\Lambda_{\rm cut}=10^{18}{\;{\rm GeV}}
,

\hat{\lambda}_{hhh}
shows a large variation about
\Lambda_{\rm cut}
,

\hat{\lambda}_{hhh}\in[-0.09,\,1.1]
.
Multi-Higgs boson productions at the LHC have been studied
to probe the cutoff scale.
The gluon fusion productions of the di-Higgs,
gg\to hh/AA
,
are not efficient to measure
\hat{\lambda}_{hhh}
because the dominant contribution from
H
dilutes the
h
contribution.
The most remarkable result is in the tri-Higgs process of
pp\to H^{\pm}hh
mediated by the
W
boson.
The signal cross section shows huge variation according to
\Lambda_{\rm cut}
,
like
\sigma_{\Lambda_{\rm cut}=1{\;{\rm TeV}}}/\sigma_{\Lambda_{\rm cut}=10^{18}{\;{\rm GeV}}}\sim 10^{3}
.
The precision measurement of
pp\to H^{\pm}hh

can indeed distinguish the high- and low-cutoff scales of the model.
Considering the dominant decay modes of
H^{\pm}\to W^{\pm*}A/h
,
h\to bb
, and
A\to bb
,
an efficient final state of
pp\to H^{\pm}hh
is
6b+\ell\nu
.
Although it enjoys almost background-free environment at the LHC,
the small cross section (
\lesssim 1{\;{\rm ab}}
) for
\Lambda_{\rm cut}=10^{18}{\;{\rm GeV}}

motivates future high-energy colliders, especially the muon collider with
\sqrt{s}=3-30{\;{\rm TeV}}
.
