Feedback theory approach to positivity and stability of evolution equations
Abed Boulouz, Hamid Bounit and Said Hadd

Abstract
. In this paper, we study the positivity and (uniform) exponential stability of a large class of perturbed semigroups. Our approach is essentially based on the feedback theory of infinite-dimensional linear systems. The obtained results are applied to the stability of hyperbolic systems including those with a delay at the boundary conditions.

\mathcal{U}

(\mathfrak{A},\mathfrak{B},\mathfrak{C})
is given by

\displaystyle\check{H}(\lambda):=\mathfrak{M}\mathfrak{D}_{\lambda}=\mathfrak{C}_{\Lambda}\mathfrak{D}_{\lambda}=\begin{pmatrix}0&\bar{H}(\lambda)\\
\tilde{H}(\lambda)&0\end{pmatrix},\quad\lambda\in\mathbb{C},

where
\bar{H}(\lambda):=Ne_{\lambda}
is a transfer function associated to
(\mathcal{Q}_{l},\beta,N_{0})
and
\tilde{H}(\lambda):=E_{\lambda}
is a transfer function associated to
(\mathcal{Q}_{r},\mathcal{B},I)
. Observe that

\displaystyle\lim_{Re\lambda\to+\infty}\|\tilde{H}(\lambda)\|=0.

On the other hand, for an arbitrary
0<\epsilon<1
and by a simple computation we have

\displaystyle\|\bar{H}(\lambda)\|\leq e^{-\epsilon Re\lambda}|\mu|([-1,0])+|\mu|([-\epsilon,0]).

Since
|\mu|([-\epsilon,0])\longrightarrow 0
as
\epsilon\to 0
then

\displaystyle\lim_{Re\lambda\to+\infty}\|\bar{H}(\lambda)\|=0.

We conclude that

\displaystyle\lim_{Re\lambda\to+\infty}\|\check{H}(\lambda)\|=0.

Thus, there exist
\gamma>0
such that

\displaystyle\sup_{Re\lambda\geq\gamma}\|\check{H}(\lambda)\|<\frac{1}{2},

which implies that
\|(I-\check{H}(\lambda))^{-1}\|\leq 2
for all
Re\lambda\geq\gamma
. Hence
I
is an admissible feedback operator. Thus, the desired result is obtained.
∎

Theorem 5.4
.

The semigroup

generated by

.

Proof.

We first mention that the assumptions of Theorem
3.2
are satisfied and hence the semigroup
(\mathfrak{T}^{cl}(t))_{t\geq 0}
is positive if there exists
\lambda_{0}>0
such that

\displaystyle r(\check{H}(\lambda_{0}))<1,

where
\check{H}(\lambda_{0}):=\mathfrak{M}\mathfrak{D}_{\lambda_{0}}
. On the other hand, let
\lambda\in\mathbb{C}
, we have

\displaystyle I-\check{H}(\lambda)=\begin{pmatrix}I&-\bar{H}(\lambda)\\
-\tilde{H}(\lambda)&I\end{pmatrix},

where
\bar{H}(\lambda):=Ne_{\lambda}
and
\tilde{H}(\lambda):=E_{\lambda}

\displaystyle\lim_{Re\lambda\to+\infty}\|\tilde{H}(\lambda)\|=\lim_{Re\lambda\to+\infty}\|\bar{H}(\lambda)\|=0,

s(\mathfrak{A})<0

3.4

.
∎
