Local Plücker formulas for orthogonal groups
D. O. Degtyarev

Abstract
. Local Plücker formulas relate the vector of metrics on a holomorphic curve in the projective space, induced by the Fubini-Study metrics on projetive spaces via Plücker embeddings of associated curves, to the corresponding vector of curvatures. Givental noted that the vector of curvatures is expressed in terms of the vector of metrics via Cartan matrix of type A n A_{n} and extended this observation from type A n A_{n} to all nonexceptional types. In this paper we show how local Plücker formulas for special orthogonal groups, i.e. for Cartan matrices of type B n B_{n} and D n D_{n} , can be obtained by reduction to the classical A n A_{n} case.



1.
Introduction

1.1.
Classical case

Consider a holomorphic curve
S
and a nondegenerate map
f=f_{1}\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}S\to\mathbb{P}^{n}
. Define a local lift of

in a neighborhood

Fubini-Study metric (

induces Hermitian metric on the

-th associated curve and the corresponding curvature forms via the Plücker embedding of

(1,1)
- forms on the associated curve. We will denote by

(1,1)
-form of the metric on

the curvature form corresponding to the metric

\boldsymbol{\vartheta}\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=(\vartheta_{k})
in terms of the vector of metrics
\boldsymbol{\varphi}\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=(\varphi_{k})
using the Cartan matrix

of the

series in the following form

(1)

\boldsymbol{\vartheta}=C\boldsymbol{\phi}.

Local Plücker formulas according to Givental

One can also construct another vector of associated

-forms of the metrics induced by pullback of the Fubini-Study metrics and the corresponding curvatures via the following morphisms of the associated curves to projective spaces. We will follow the treatment from

(2)

Conjecture 1.1

If the holomorphic curve
f\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}S\to F

\boldsymbol{\theta}

1.3.
Connection with the classical case

Consider the case when
G=SL(n+1,\mathbb{C})
. The condition that the curve
f\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}S\to F
is an integral curve of the distribution

means that the preimage under

of the point

is the

In this case the exterior power

However Plücker embeddings of associated curves on Grassmannians do not always coincide with embeddings into the projectivization of fundamental representations. In particular this can be seen in the example of the special orthogonal groups.

Positselski proved the conjecture

in
[
4
]
using the general technique for line bundles over flag varieties. At the same time in
[
1
]
,
in addition to the general formulation of the conjecture, Givental proposed the proof of the local Plücker formulas for symplectic group

based on the reduction of the problem to the classical formulas for the group

.

However, the question of the geometric interpretation in the case of a special orthogonal group has remained unanswered. In this paper we prove the

for orthogonal groups, i.e. for Cartan matrices of the series

and
D_{n}

2.

yields the desiring equality for the one of the last two rows of Plücker relations for
SO(2n,\mathbb{C})
:

(29)

\theta_{-}=(i_{+}\circ f)^{*}(\widehat{\vartheta}_{n-1})=-(i_{+}\circ f)^{*}(\widehat{\phi}_{n-2})+(i_{+}\circ f)^{*}(2\widehat{\phi}_{n-1})-(i_{+}\circ f)^{*}(\widehat{\phi}_{n})=

=-\varphi_{n-2}+2\varphi_{+}+2\varphi_{-}-2\varphi_{+}=-\varphi_{n-2}+2\varphi_{-}.

Then the same reasoning as before shows that the remaining row of Plücker formulas for

is given by:

(30)

\theta_{+}=(i\circ f)^{*}_{-}(\widehat{\vartheta}_{n-1})=-(i\circ f)^{*}_{-}(\widehat{\phi}_{n-2})+(i\circ f)^{*}_{-}(2\widehat{\phi}_{n-1})-(i\circ f)^{*}_{-}(\widehat{\phi}_{n})=

=-\omega_{n-2}+2\omega_{+}+2\omega_{-}-2\omega_{-}=-\omega_{n-2}+2\omega_{+}.

With matrix notation we get the full collection of Plücker formulas on the curve
S

(31)

\boldsymbol{\theta}=A\boldsymbol{\varphi},

where

∎

3.
Local Plücker formulas for the group

Consider the case
G=SO(2n+1,\mathbb{C})
. Let
V
be a complex vector space of dimension
2n+1
with a nondegenerate symmetric bilinear form
Q
which is preserved by
G

V_{i}\mathrel{\mathop{\mathchar 58\relax}}=\wedge^{i}V
of the standard representation
V
of
\mathfrak{so}(2n+1,\mathbb{C})
is the irreducible representation with highest weight
L_{1}+\ldots+L_{i}
for

n

\mathscr{L}_{i}
on
G/B
corresponding to the fundamental weight
\omega_{i}
.

We immediately get the first
(n-2)
-th rows of Plücker formulas for
