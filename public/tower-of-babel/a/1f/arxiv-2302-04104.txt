Abstract




Let
\mathscr{A}
be an associative
\mathbb{C}
-algebra with identity 1, and let
\mathscr{M}
be some
\mathbb{C}
-linear space,
\dim\mathscr{M}\geqslant 1
.

Definition
.

A cyclic Frobenius algebra (
\rm{CF}
-algebra)
\mathscr{A}
is an algebra
\mathscr{A}

with a
\mathbb{C}
-bilinear skew-symmetric form

\Phi(\cdot,\cdot)\colon\mathscr{A}\otimes_{\mathbb{C}}\mathscr{A}\to\mathscr{M}
such that

\Phi(A,BC)+\Phi(B,CA)+\Phi(C,AB)=0
where
A,B,C\in\mathscr{A}
.

Examples: 1)
\mathscr{M}=\mathscr{A}
and
\Phi(A,B)=AB-BA=[A,B]
;  2)
\mathscr{A}
is a commutative algebra
with Poisson bracket
\{\cdot,\cdot\}
,
\mathscr{M}=\mathscr{A}
and
\Phi(A,B)=\{A,B\}
;
3)
\rm{CF}
-algebras, where
\mathscr{M}
is a field, obtained from the constructions of algebras from
[
1
]
,
[
5
]
.

Let us introduce the
\mathbb{C}
-linear subspace
\rm{Span}\subset\mathscr{A}
, spanned by all commutators
[A,B]\in\mathscr{A}
,
and the projection
\pi\colon\mathscr{A}\to\mathscr{A}/\rm{Span}
.
Set
A\approx B
if
A-B\in\rm{Span}
.

Lemma 1
.

Let
\varphi(\cdot,\cdot)\colon\mathscr{A}\otimes\mathscr{A}\to\mathscr{A}
be a
\mathbb{C}
-bilinear skew-symmetric form
such that
\varphi(A,BC)\approx\varphi(A,B)C+B\varphi(A,C)
.
Then
\mathscr{A}
is a
\rm{CF}
-algebra with
\mathscr{M}=\mathscr{A}/\rm{Span}
and
\Phi=\pi\varphi
.

Let
\mathscr{K}=\{B\in\mathscr{A}\,:\,\Phi(A,B)=0,\text{ for all}\,A\in\mathscr{A}\}
.
From Definition
Definition
it follows that
\mathscr{K}
is the subring in
\mathscr{A}
,
1\in\mathscr{K}
, and

\Phi(A,bC)=\Phi(Ab,C)
for all
A,C\in\mathscr{A}
and
b\in\mathscr{K}
.

Let
{\mathfrak{A}}=\mathbb{C}\langle u_{0},u_{1},\ldots\rangle=\oplus{\mathfrak{A}}_{k},\,k\geqslant 0
, be a free associative graded algebra
with derivation
D
,
|u_{k}|=k+2
,
D(u_{k})=u_{k+1},\,k=0,1,\ldots
.
We introduce the algebra
{\mathfrak{A}}^{D}=\{A=\sum_{i\leqslant m}a_{i}D^{i},\,a_{m}\neq 0,\,m\in\mathbb{Z},\,a_{i}\in{\mathfrak{A}}_{|a_{m}|+m-i}\}
,
where
[D,u_{k}]=u_{k+1},\;[D^{-1},u_{k}]=\sum_{i\geqslant 1}(-1)^{i}\\
u_{k+i}D^{-i-1}
.
Let
A_{+}+A_{-}=A\in{\mathfrak{A}}^{D}
, where
A_{+}=\sum a_{i}D^{i},\,0\leqslant i\leqslant m
,
for
m\geqslant 0
, and
A_{+}=0
, for
m<0
. We have
{\rm res}[D,A]=D({\rm res}A)
, where
{\rm res}A=a_{-1}
.

We introduce a homogeneous
\mathbb{C}
-bilinear skew-symmetric form
\sigma(\cdot,\cdot)\colon{\mathfrak{A}}^{D}\otimes{\mathfrak{A}}^{D}\to{\mathfrak{A}}
,
|\sigma(A,B)|=|A|+|B|
, by

\sigma(aD^{n},bD^{m})\!=\!\begin{cases}\frac{1}{2}\binom{n}{n+m+1}\!\sum\limits_{s=0}^{n+m}\!(-1)^{s}\big(a^{(s)}b^{(n+m-s)}\!+b^{(n+m-s)}a^{(s)}\big),\text{for }n+m\geqslant 0,\,nm<0,\\[1.0pt]
0,\qquad\mbox{otherwise}.\end{cases}

Lemma 2
.

For any
A,B\in{\mathfrak{A}}^{D}
, we have
D\big(\sigma(A,B)\big)={\rm res}\,[A,B]-\text{$\Delta$}(A,B)
, where

\Delta(aD^{n},bD^{m})=\begin{cases}\frac{1}{2}\binom{n}{n+m+1}\Big([a,b^{(n+m+1)}]+(-1)^{n+m}[b,a^{(n+m+1)}]\Big),\text{ for }n+m\geqslant-1,\\[1.0pt]
0,\qquad\mbox{otherwise}.\end{cases}

Lemma 3
.

Under the canonical projection
\pi\colon{\mathfrak{A}}\to{\mathfrak{A}}/\rm{Span}=\mathscr{M}=\oplus\mathscr{M}_{k},\;k\geqslant 0
, the operator
D

gives monomorphisms
\overline{D}\colon\mathscr{M}_{k}\to\mathscr{M}_{k+1},\;k>0
.

Theorem 1

The algebra

\rm{CF}
-algebra with the form

On the set of generators

{\mathfrak{A}}
