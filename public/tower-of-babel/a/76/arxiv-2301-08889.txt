A generalized Kervaire Problem in stable homotopy groups of spheres
Petr M. Akhmetiev

Abstract
We will give an elementary self-contained description of the Mahowald element in the stable homotopy group of spheres Π 2 l \Pi_{2^{l}} , l ≥ 3 l\geq 3 . Using this construction, we prove that a generalized Kervaire Problem, formulated by the author in [ A ] is solved positively.
