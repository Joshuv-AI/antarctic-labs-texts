Introduction

The classical hypergeometric function of one-variable
is defined to be the power series

{}_{s}F_{s-1}\left({a_{1},\ldots,a_{s}\atop b_{1},\ldots,b_{s-1}};t\right)=\sum\limits_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{(a_{1})_{k}\cdots(a_{s})_{k}}{(b_{1})_{k}\cdots(b_{s-1})_{k}}\frac{t^{k}}{k!}

where
(\alpha)_{k}
denotes the Pochhammer symbol (cf.
[
4
]
).
Let
(a_{1},...,a_{s})\in\mathbb{Z}_{p}^{s}
be a
s
-tuple of
p
-adic integers, and consider
the series

F_{a_{1},...,a_{s}}(t)={}_{s}F_{s-1}\left({a_{1},\ldots,a_{s}\atop 1,\ldots,1};t\right)=\sum\limits_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{(a_{1})_{k}}{k!}\cdots\frac{(a_{s})_{k}}{k!}t^{k}.

This is a formal power series with

\mathbb{Z}_{p}
-coefficients.
In the paper
[
2
]
, B. Dwork introduced his
p
-adic hypergeometric functions
\mathscr{F}^{\rm Dw}_{a_{1},...,a_{s}}(t)
(Definition
4.3
) and showed that
\mathscr{F}^{\rm Dw}_{a_{1},...,a_{s}}(t)
satisfy congruence relation (
[
2
, p.41, Theorem 3]
)

\mathscr{F}^{\rm Dw}_{a_{1},...,a_{s}}(t)\equiv\frac{[F_{a_{1},\cdots,a_{s}}(t)]_{<p^{n}}}{[F_{a_{1}^{\prime},\cdots,a_{s}^{\prime}}(t^{p})]_{<p^{n}}}\mod{p^{n}\mathbb{Z}_{p}[[t]]}

where for a power series
f(t)=\sum{c_{n}}t^{n}
, we write
[f(t)]_{<m}:=\sum_{n<m}c_{n}t^{n}

the truncated
polynomial.

In
[
1
]
and
[
5
]
, the
p
-adic hypergeoemtric functions of logarithmic type
\mathscr{F}^{(\sigma)}_{a_{1},\cdots,a_{s}}(t)
(Definition
2.1
)
and
\widehat{\mathscr{F}}_{a,...,a}^{\;(\sigma)}(t)
(Definition
2.2
) are introduced. It is shown that these
p
-adic hypergeometric functions satisfy congruence relations similar to Dwork’s (Theorem
2.4
and Theorem
2.5
).
In
[
1
]
, Asakura studied these functions from the viewpoint of
p
-adic regulators.
In particular, he found that some values of
\mathscr{F}_{a_{1},...,a_{s}}^{(\sigma)}(t)

are expected to be the special values of
p
-adic
L
-function of elliptic curves over
\mathbb{Q}
(
[
1
, §5]
).

Furthermore, two conjectures between these hypergeometric functions are proposed in
[
5
]
. It is called the transformation formula of Dwork’s
p
-adic hypergeometric functions (Conjecture
4.4
)

\mathscr{F}^{{\rm Dw}}_{a,\cdots,a}(t)=((-1)^{s}t)^{l}\mathscr{F}^{{\rm Dw}}_{a,\cdots,a}(t^{-1})

and the transformation formula of
\mathscr{F}_{a,...,a}^{(\sigma)}(t)
and
\widehat{\mathscr{F}}_{a,...,a}^{\;(\sigma)}(t)
(Conjecture
4.1
)

\mathscr{F}_{a,\cdots,a}^{\;(\sigma)}(t)=-\widehat{\mathscr{F}}_{a,\cdots,a}^{\;(\widehat{\sigma})}(t^{-1}).

In
[
5
]
, some special cases of the transformation formula have been proved (the case
s=2
,
a\in\frac{1}{N}{\mathbb{Z}}
,
0<a<1
and
p>N
,
[
5
, Theorem 4.14]
and
[
5
, Theorem 4.20]
). It is proved by geometry method.

In this paper, we find actually these two transformation formulas are closely related. In fact, one of the one of transformation formulas implies another.

The main result (Theorem
4.9
) of this paper is that we prove a slight revision of Conjecture
4.4
(Conjecture
4.7
) implies Conjecture
4.1
. Using this theorem, we can show as a corollary that Conjecture
4.1
is true when
s=1
.

This paper is organized as follows. In §2, we recall the definition and some properties of hypergeometric functions
\mathscr{F}_{a,...,a}^{\;(\sigma)}(t)
and
\widehat{\mathscr{F}}_{a_{1},...,a_{s}}^{\;(\sigma)}(t)
. In §3, we prove some relation between coefficients of these hypergeometric functions. In §4, we first recall the conjectures between these hypergeometric functions and then move on to the transformation formula of Dwork’s
p
-adic hypergeometric functions. Finally, we give the proof of our main result using the relation in §3.

a\in\mathbb{Z}_{p},
then we have

\mathscr{F}^{{\rm Dw}}_{a}(t)=(-t)^{l}\mathscr{F}^{{\rm Dw}}_{a}(t^{-1}),

where
l
is the unique integer in
\{0,1,\cdots,p-1\}
such that
a+l\equiv 0\mod p.

For
p=2
, we have

\mathscr{F}^{{\rm Dw}}_{a}(t)=\pm(-t)^{l}\mathscr{F}^{{\rm Dw}}_{a}(t^{-1}).

In particular, we have a counterexample for Conjecture
4.4
.

Proof.

By
[
2
, p.37, Theorem 2]
, we have

\mathscr{F}^{\rm Dw}_{a}(t)\equiv\frac{F_{a}(t)_{<p^{n}}}{F_{a^{\prime}}(t^{p})_{<p^{n}}}=\frac{(1-t)^{-a}_{<p^{n}}}{(1-t^{p})^{-a^{\prime}}_{<p^{n}}}\equiv\frac{(1-t)^{-N}_{<p^{n}}}{(1-t^{p})^{-N^{\prime}}_{<p^{n}}}\mod p^{n}

for some
N\in\mathbb{Z}_{>0}
and
a\equiv N\mod p.

Then we obtain

\displaystyle\mathscr{F}^{\rm Dw}_{a}(t^{-1})

\displaystyle\equiv\left.\frac{(1-t)^{-N}_{<p^{n}}}{(1-t^{p})^{-N^{\prime}}_{<p^{n}}}\right|_{t^{-1}}\mod p^{n}

\displaystyle\equiv\left.\frac{(1-t)^{-N}}{(1-t^{p})^{-N^{\prime}}}\right|_{t^{-1}}

\displaystyle=\frac{(-t)^{-pN^{\prime}}}{(-t)^{-pN^{\prime}}}\cdot\frac{(1-t^{-1})^{-N}}{(1-t^{-p})^{-N^{\prime}}}

\displaystyle=\frac{(-t)^{-l}(1-t)^{-N}}{(1-t^{p})^{-N^{\prime}}}

when
p
is odd. Therefore the result follows.

For
p=2,
we have

\displaystyle\mathscr{F}^{\rm Dw}_{a}(t^{-1})

\displaystyle\equiv=\pm\frac{(-t)^{-l}(1-t)^{-N}}{(1-t^{2})^{-N^{\prime}}}\mod 2^{n}.

For example, if we take
a=1,
then

\mathscr{F}^{\rm Dw}_{a}(t^{-1})=1+t

but

(-t)\mathscr{F}^{\rm Dw}_{a}(t^{-1})=-(1+t).

∎

So we propose another conjecture about the transformation formula of Dwork
p
-adic hypergeometric functions to include Theorem
4.6
as a special case. And we will find that our new conjecture is enough for us to derive our main Theorem
4.9
.

Conjecture 4.7

(
Transformation formula on Dwork’s
p
-adic Hypergeometric Functions
)
.

Suppose
a_{1}=\cdots=a_{s}=a.
Let
l
is the unique integer in
\{0,1,\cdots,p-1\}
such that
a+l\equiv 0\mod p.
Then for odd prime
p,
we have

\mathscr{F}^{{\rm Dw}}_{a}(t)=((-1)^{s}t)^{l}\mathscr{F}^{{\rm Dw}}_{a}(t^{-1}).

For
p=2,
we have

\mathscr{F}^{{\rm Dw}}_{a}(t)=\pm((-1)^{s}t)^{l}\mathscr{F}^{{\rm Dw}}_{a}(t^{-1}).

4.3
The proof of Conjecture
4.7
implies Conjecture
4.1

First, we prove the following lemma.

Lemma 4.8
.

Let
a\in\mathbb{Z}_{p},

m,k,d\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}
with
0\leq m\leq p^{n}-1
and
0\leq d\leq n.
If Conjecture
4.7
is true, then we have

\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}i\equiv k\mod{p^{n-d}}\\
0\leq i\leq m\\
i+j=m\end{subarray}}A_{i}A_{p^{n}-j-1}-\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}p^{n}-j^{\prime}-1\equiv-k-a\mod p^{n-d}\\
0\leq j^{\prime}\leq m\\
i^{\prime}+j^{\prime}=m\end{subarray}}A_{i^{\prime}}A_{p^{n}-j^{\prime}-1}\equiv 0\mod p^{d+1}.

(4.1)

Proof.

The proof of this lemma is similar to
[
1
, Lemma 3.12]
.

Put

F^{(r)}(t):=\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}A_{i}^{(r)}t^{i}

and

F_{k}^{(r)}(t):=\sum_{i\equiv k\mod p^{n-d}}A_{i}^{(r)}t^{i}=p^{-n+d}\sum_{u=0}^{p^{n-d}-1}\zeta^{-ku}F(\zeta^{u}t)

where
\zeta
is a primitive
p^{n-d}
root of unity. For simplicity, we write
F(t)
and
F_{k}(t)
for
F^{(0)}(t)
and
F_{k}^{(0)}(t),
respectively.

Since we suppose Conjecture
4.7
is true, we have

\displaystyle\frac{F(t)}{F^{(n-d)}(t^{p^{n-d}})}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{F(t)}{F^{(1)}(t^{p})}\frac{F^{(1)}(t^{p})}{F^{(2)}(t^{p^{2}})}\cdots\frac{F^{(n-d-1)}(t^{p^{n-d-1}})}{F^{(n-d)}(t^{p^{n-d}})}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\pm((-1)^{s}t)^{l_{0}}\left.\frac{F(t)}{F^{(1)}(t^{p})}\right|_{t^{-1}}\cdot(\pm)((-1)^{s}t^{p})^{l_{1}}\left.\frac{F^{(1)}(t^{p})}{F^{(2)}(t^{p^{2}})}\right|_{t^{-1}}

\displaystyle\cdots(\pm)((-1)^{s}t^{p^{n-d-1}})^{l_{n-d-1}}\left.\frac{F^{(n-d-1)}(t^{p^{n-d-1}})}{F^{(n-d)}(t^{p^{n-d}})}\right|_{t^{-1}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(\pm 1)^{n-d}(-1)^{s(l_{0}+l_{1}+\cdots+l_{n-d-1})}t^{l_{0}+l_{1}p+\cdots+l_{n-d-1}p^{n-l-1}}\left.\frac{F(t)}{F^{(1)}(t^{p})}\right|_{t^{-1}}\cdots\left.\frac{F^{(n-d-1)}(t^{p^{n-d-1}})}{F^{(n-d)}(t^{p^{n-d}})}\right|_{t^{-1}}

where
l_{0},l_{1},\cdots,l_{n-d-1}
are the numbers in
\{0,1,\cdots,p-1\}
such that

a+l_{0}\equiv a^{\prime}+l_{1}\equiv\cdots\equiv a^{(n-d-1)}+l_{n-d-1}\equiv 0\mod p.

From the Dwork congruence
[
2
, p.37, Theorem 2]
, we have

\frac{F^{(i)}(t)}{F^{(i+1)}(t^{p})}\equiv\frac{F^{(i)}(t)_{<p^{m}}}{F^{(i+1)}(t^{p})_{<p^{m}}}\mod p^{n}

when
m\geq n\geq 1.
Therefore one has

\frac{F^{(i)}(t^{p})}{F^{(i+1)}(t^{p^{2}})}\equiv\frac{F^{(i)}(t^{p})_{<p^{n+1}}}{F^{(i+1)}(t^{p^{2}})_{<p^{n+1}}}\mod p^{n},\frac{F^{(i)}(t^{p^{2}})}{F^{(i+1)}(t^{p^{3}})}\equiv\frac{F^{(i)}(t^{p^{2}})_{<p^{n+2}}}{F^{(i+1)}(t^{p^{3}})_{<p^{n+2}}}\mod p^{n},\cdots

After modulo
p^{d+1}
and writing
s^{\prime}=s(l_{0}+l_{1}+\cdots+l_{n-d-1})
and
l^{\prime}=l_{0}+l_{1}p+\cdots+l_{n-d-1}p^{n-d-1},
we have

\displaystyle\frac{F(t)}{F^{(n-d)}(t^{p^{n-d}})}

\displaystyle\equiv(\pm 1)^{n-d}(-1)^{s^{\prime}}t^{l^{\prime}}\left.\frac{F(t)_{<p^{n}}}{F^{(1)}(t^{p})_{<p^{n}}}\right|_{t^{-1}}\cdots\left.\frac{F^{(n-d-1)}(t^{p^{n-d-1}})_{<p^{n}}}{F^{(n-d)}(t^{p^{n-d}})_{<p^{n}}}\right|_{t^{-1}}\mod p^{d+1}\mathbb{Z}_{p}[[t]]

\displaystyle=(\pm 1)^{n-d}(-1)^{s^{\prime}}t^{l^{\prime}}\left.\frac{F(t)_{<p^{n}}}{F^{(n-d)}(t^{p^{n-d}})_{<p^{n}}}\right|_{t^{-1}}.

Therefore

\frac{F(t)}{F^{(n-d)}(t^{p^{n-d}})}=(\pm 1)^{n-d}(-1)^{s^{\prime}}t^{l^{\prime}}\left.\frac{F(t)_{<p^{n}}}{F^{(n-d)}(t^{p^{n-d}})_{<p^{n}}}\right|_{t^{-1}}+p^{d+1}\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}a_{i}t^{i}

(4.2)

where
a_{i}\in\mathbb{Z}_{p}.

If we substitute
\zeta^{u}t
for
t,
we have

\displaystyle\frac{F(\zeta^{u}t)}{F^{(n-d)}(t^{p^{n-d}})}

\displaystyle=(\pm 1)^{n-d}(-1)^{s^{\prime}}(\zeta^{u}t)^{l^{\prime}}\left.\frac{F(t)_{<p^{n}}}{F^{(n-d)}(t^{p^{n-d}})_{<p^{n}}}\right|_{\zeta^{-u}t^{-1}}+p^{d+1}\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}a_{i}\zeta^{ui}t^{i}

(4.3)

\displaystyle=(\pm 1)^{n-d}(-1)^{s^{\prime}}(\zeta^{u}t)^{l^{\prime}}\left.\frac{F(\zeta^{-u}t)_{<p^{n}}}{F^{(n-d)}(t^{p^{n-d}})_{<p^{n}}}\right|_{t^{-1}}+p^{d+1}\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}a_{i}\zeta^{ui}t^{i}.

Dividing equation (
4.3
) by equation (
4.2
) and expanding the quotient, one has

F(\zeta^{u}t)\left.F(t)_{<p^{n}}\right|_{t^{-1}}\cdot t^{p^{n}-1}-F(t)F(\left.\zeta^{-u}t)_{<p^{n}}\right|_{t^{-1}}\cdot{t^{p^{n}-1}}\cdot\zeta^{ul^{\prime}}=p^{d+1}\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}b_{i}(\zeta^{u})t^{i}

where
b_{i}(x)\in\mathbb{Z}_{p}[x]
are polynomials which does not depend on
u.

Taking
\sum\limits_{u=0}^{p^{n-d}-1}\zeta^{-uk}(-)
on both side and noting that
l^{\prime}\in\{0,1,\cdots,p^{n-d}-1\}
is the number such that
a+l^{\prime}\equiv 0\mod p^{n-d}
, one has

p^{n-d}\bigg(F_{k}(t)\left.F(t)_{<p^{n}}\right|_{t^{-1}}\cdot t^{p^{n}-1}-F(t)F_{-k-a}(\left.t)_{<p^{n}}\right|_{t^{-1}}\cdot{t^{p^{n}-1}}\bigg)=p^{n-d}\cdot p^{d+1}g(x).

for some power series
g(x).

Therefore

\frac{F_{k}(t)}{F(t)}\equiv\left.\frac{F_{-k-a}(t)_{<p^{n}}}{F(t)_{<p^{n}}}\right|_{t^{-1}}\mod p^{d+1}.

On the other hand, using a similar proof, one has

\frac{F_{k}(t)}{F(t)}\equiv\frac{F_{k}(t)_{<p^{n}}}{F(t)_{<p^{n}}}\mod p^{d+1},

(cf. the last paragraph in the proof of
[
1
, Lemma 3.12]
) so we conclude

\frac{F_{k}(t)_{<p^{n}}}{F(t)_{<p^{n}}}\equiv\left.\frac{F_{-k-a}(t)_{<p^{n}}}{F(t)_{<p^{n}}}\right|_{t^{-1}}\mod p^{d+1}.

(4.4)

Using (
4.4
), one has

F_{k}(t)\left.F(t)_{<p^{n}}\right|_{t^{-1}}\cdot t^{p^{n}-1}=F(t)F_{-k-a}(\left.t)_{<p^{n}}\right|_{t^{-1}}\cdot{t^{p^{n}-1}}\mod p^{d+1}.

(4.5)

Then this lemma follows from comparing the coefficients of
t^{m}
of (
4.5
).
∎

Theorem 4.9
.

Conjecture
4.7
implies Conjecture
4.1
.

Proof.

By congruence relations of
\mathscr{F}^{(\sigma)}_{a}(t)
and
\widehat{\mathscr{F}}^{\;(\widehat{\sigma})}_{a}(t)
(Theorem
2.4
and Theorem
2.5
), Conjecture
4.1
is equivalent to the statement

\frac{G^{(\sigma)}_{a}(t)_{<p^{n}}}{F_{a}(t)_{<p^{n}}}\equiv-\left.\frac{\widehat{G}^{\;(\widehat{\sigma})}_{a}(t)_{<p^{n}}}{F_{a}(t)_{<p^{n}}}\right|_{t^{-1}}\mod p^{n}W[[t]]

for all
n\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}.
So it suffices to show that

\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}i+j=m\\
0\leq i,j\leq p^{n}-1\end{subarray}}B_{i}A_{p^{n}-j-1}+\widehat{B}_{p^{n}-j-1}A_{i}\equiv 0\mod p^{n}.

for any
m
with
0\leq m\leq 2(p^{n}-1).

First, we suppose
0\leq m\leq p^{n}-1.
Using the relation
\beta_{k}=B_{k}/A_{k}
and
\widehat{\beta}_{k}=\widehat{B}_{k}/A_{k}
when
k\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0},
we write

\displaystyle\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}i+j=m\\
0\leq i,j\leq m\end{subarray}}B_{i}A_{p^{n}-j-1}+\widehat{B}_{p^{n}-j-1}A_{i}

(4.6)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}i+j=m\\
0\leq i,j\leq m\end{subarray}}\beta_{i}A_{i}A_{p^{n}-j-1}+\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}i^{\prime}+j^{\prime}=m\\
0\leq i^{\prime},j^{\prime}\leq m\end{subarray}}\widehat{\beta}_{p^{n}-j^{\prime}-1}A_{i^{\prime}}A_{p^{n}-j^{\prime}-1}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{p^{n}-1}\bigg[\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}i\equiv k\mod p^{n}\\
0\leq i\leq m\end{subarray}}\beta_{i}A_{i}A_{p^{n}-j-1}+\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}p^{n}-j^{\prime}-1\equiv-k-a\mod p^{n}\\
0\leq j^{\prime}\leq m\end{subarray}}\widehat{\beta}_{p^{n}-j^{\prime}-1}A_{i^{\prime}}A_{p^{n}-j^{\prime}-1}\bigg]

\displaystyle\equiv

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{p^{n}-1}\beta_{k}\bigg[\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}i\equiv k\mod p^{n}\\
0\leq i\leq m\\
i+j=m\end{subarray}}A_{i}A_{p^{n}-j-1}-\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}p^{n}-j^{\prime}-1\equiv-k-a\mod p^{n}\\
0\leq j^{\prime}\leq m\\
i^{\prime}+j^{\prime}=m\end{subarray}}A_{i^{\prime}}A_{p^{n}-j^{\prime}-1}\bigg]\mod{p^{n}}.

By Lemma
4.8
, we have

\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}i\equiv k\mod p^{n}\\
0\leq i\leq m\\
i+j=m\end{subarray}}A_{i}A_{p^{n}-j-1}-\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}p^{n}-j^{\prime}-1\equiv-k-a\mod p^{n}\\
0\leq j^{\prime}\leq m\\
i^{\prime}+j^{\prime}=m\end{subarray}}A_{i^{\prime}}A_{p^{n}-j^{\prime}-1}\equiv 0\mod p.

So we can rewrite the equation (
4.6
) as

\sum_{k^{\prime}=0}^{p^{n-1}-1}\sum_{k\equiv k^{\prime}\mod p^{n-1}}\beta_{k}\bigg[\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}i\equiv k\mod p^{n}\\
0\leq i\leq m\\
i+j=m\end{subarray}}A_{i}A_{p^{n}-j-1}-\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}p^{n}-j^{\prime}-1\equiv-k-a\mod p^{n}\\
0\leq j^{\prime}\leq m\\
i^{\prime}+j^{\prime}=m\end{subarray}}A_{i^{\prime}}A_{p^{n}-j^{\prime}-1}\bigg]

and this equals

\displaystyle\sum_{k^{\prime}=0}^{p^{n-1}-1}\beta_{k^{\prime}}\sum_{k\equiv k^{\prime}\mod p^{n-1}}\bigg[\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}i\equiv k\mod p^{n}\\
0\leq i\leq m\\
i+j=m\end{subarray}}A_{i}A_{p^{n}-j-1}-\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}p^{n}-j^{\prime}-1\equiv-k-a\mod p^{n}\\
0\leq j^{\prime}\leq m\\
i^{\prime}+j^{\prime}=m\end{subarray}}A_{i^{\prime}}A_{p^{n}-j^{\prime}-1}\bigg]

\displaystyle\equiv\sum_{k^{\prime}=0}^{p^{n-1}-1}\beta_{k^{\prime}}\bigg[\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}i\equiv k\mod p^{n-1}\\
0\leq i\leq m\\
i+j=m\end{subarray}}A_{i}A_{p^{n}-j-1}-\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}p^{n}-j^{\prime}-1\equiv-k-a\mod p^{n-1}\\
0\leq j^{\prime}\leq m\\
i^{\prime}+j^{\prime}=m\end{subarray}}A_{i^{\prime}}A_{p^{n}-j^{\prime}-1}\bigg]\mod p^{n}.

Again by Lemma
4.8
and using the same argument, the equation equals

\sum_{k^{\prime\prime}=0}^{p^{n-2}-1}\beta_{k^{\prime\prime}}\bigg[\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}i\equiv k\mod p^{n-2}\\
0\leq i\leq m\\
i+j=m\end{subarray}}A_{i}A_{p^{n}-j-1}-\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}p^{n}-j^{\prime}-1\equiv-k-a\mod p^{n-2}\\

Continuing this process, we finally get the equation equals

\sum_{k=0}^{p-1}\beta_{k}\bigg[\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}i\equiv k\mod p\\
0\leq i\leq m\\
i+j=m\end{subarray}}A_{i}A_{p^{n}-j-1}-\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}p^{n}-j^{\prime}-1\equiv-k-a\mod p\\

\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}i+j=m\\

On the other hand, if

and interchange the role of

and

, we find this is just the case above. Therefore the result follows.
