Comment on “Gravitational Pair Production and Black Hole Evaporation”
Antonio Ferreiro, José Navarro-Salas, Silvia Pla

Abstract




In the Letter
paper
, the authors claim to explore a new avenue to address black hole radiation within the general framework of quantum field theory interacting with a prescribed external background. This is a well-established field and one of its main predictions is the spontaneous creation of particles. The first fundamental example is the creation of pairs by a constant electric field

W

\langle 0_{+}|0_{-}\rangle=e^{iW}

Gravitational pair creation was first derived in a cosmological scenario

Birrell-Davies

Within the framework of quantum field theory in curved spacetime

;

Only the introduction of new ingredients such as backreaction effects or quantum gravity may lead to deviations.

However, the approach of
