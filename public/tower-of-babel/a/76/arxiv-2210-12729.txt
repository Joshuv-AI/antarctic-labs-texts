Abstract
. A field K K in a ring language ℒ \mathcal{L} is finitely undecidable if Cons ​ ( T ) \mbox{Cons}(T) is undecidable for every nonempty finite T ⊆ Th ​ ( K , ℒ ) T\subseteq\mbox{Th}(K;\mathcal{L}) . Introduction

The author was motivated to consider this topic by the following question:
