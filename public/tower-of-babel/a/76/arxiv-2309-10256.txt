Let

Let

Hochster and Eagon proved that in non-modular case (that is, the case that

It has been known that the condition that the finite group

The condition is replaced by the condition that

Lemma 3.1

(cf.

.

In the following cases, we have that the Knop character

-modules.

(4)

(5)

(6)

Proof.

By Lemma

For the case that (2), (3), or (4) is assumed,

We prove (4).

is a closed subgroup scheme, and is a constant finite group.
Note that

As

As a
