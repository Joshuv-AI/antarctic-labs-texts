Abstract
. We give a complete answer to the rationality problem (up to stable k k -equivalence) for norm one tori R K / k ( 1 ) ​ ( 𝔾 m ) R^{(1)}_{K/k}(\mathbbm{G}_{m}) of K / k K/k whose Galois closures L / k L/k are dihedral extensions with the aid of Endo and Miyata [ EM75 , Theorem 1.5, Theorem 2.3] and Endo [ End11 , Theorem 2.1] . By using a similar technique, we give refinements of the proof of stably rational cases of Endo and Miyata’s theorems as an appendix of the paper.

The main theorem of this paper
gives a complete answer to the rationality problem
(up to stable
k
-equivalence)
for norm one tori

of

whose Galois closures

are dihedral extensions
with the aid of Endo and Miyata

, Theorem 2.3, Theorem 1.5]

Theorem 1.1
.

Let
K/k
be a finite non-Galois separable field extension
and
L/k
be the Galois closure of
K/k
.
Let
D_{n}=\langle x,y\mid x^{n}=y^{2}=1,y^{-1}xy=x^{-1}\rangle

be the dihedral group of order
2n

(n\geq 4:
even
)

and
Z(D_{n})=\langle x^{\frac{n}{2}}\rangle
be the center of
D_{n}
.
Assume that
G={\rm Gal}(L/k)\simeq D_{n}

and
H={\rm Gal}(L/K)\leq G
.
Then
|H|=2
with
H\neq Z(D_{n})
and we have:

(i)
if
n\equiv 0\ ({\rm mod}\ 4)
, then

R^{(1)}_{K/k}(\mathbbm{G}_{m})
is not retract
k
-rational;

(ii)
if
n\equiv 2\ ({\rm mod}\ 4)
, then

R^{(1)}_{K/k}(\mathbbm{G}_{m})
is stably
k
-rational.
More precisely,

R^{(1)}_{K/k}(\mathbbm{G}_{m})\times R_{k_{1}/k}(\mathbbm{G}_{m,k_{1}})\times R_{k_{2}/k}(\mathbbm{G}_{m,k_{2}})\times R_{k_{3}/k}(\mathbbm{G}_{m,k_{3}})

of dimension
(n-1)+\frac{n}{2}+\frac{n}{2}+2=2n+1
and

R_{k_{4}/k}(\mathbbm{G}_{m,k_{4}})\times R_{k_{5}/k}(\mathbbm{G}_{m,k_{5}})\times\mathbbm{G}_{m,k}

of dimension
n+n+1=2n+1

are birationally
k
-equivalent where

R_{k_{i}/k}(\mathbbm{G}_{m,k_{i}})
are
k
-rational with

k_{1}=L^{\langle x^{\frac{n}{2}},x^{2}y\rangle}
,

k_{2}=L^{\langle x^{\frac{n}{2}},xy\rangle}
,

k_{3}=L^{\langle x\rangle}
,

k_{4}=L^{\langle x^{\frac{n}{2}}\rangle}
,

k_{5}=L^{\langle xy\rangle}
,

\langle x^{\frac{n}{2}},x^{2}y\rangle\simeq\langle x^{\frac{n}{2}},xy\rangle\simeq C_{2}\times C_{2}
,

\langle x\rangle\simeq C_{n}
,

\langle x^{\frac{n}{2}}\rangle\simeq\langle xy\rangle\simeq C_{2}

[k_{1}:k]=[k_{2}:k]=\frac{n}{2}

[k_{3}:k]=2

[k_{4}:k]=[k_{5}:k]=n

over

Two fields

K(y_{1},\ldots,y_{m})\simeq K^{\prime}(z_{1},\ldots,z_{n})
over

y_{1},\ldots,y_{m}
over

over

When

which is the polynomial ring over

]
