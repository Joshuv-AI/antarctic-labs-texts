Thermal transport and mixed valence in ZrTe 3 doped with Hf and Se
Yu Liu, 1,§,† Zhixiang Hu, 1,2 Xiao Tong, 3 Denis Leshchev, 4 Xiangde Zhu, 1,∗ Hechang Lei, 1,∥ Eli Stavitski 4 , Klaus Attenkofer, 4,¶ and C. Petrovic 1,2,‡

Abstract
Two-dimensional transition metal trichalcogenides (TMTC’s) feature covalently bonded metal-chalcogen layers separated by the van der Waals (vdW) gap. Similar to transition metal dichalcogenides (TMDCs), TMTCs often host charge density waves (CDWs) and superconductivity but unlike TMDCs atomic chains in the crystal structure give rise to quasi one-dimensional (quasi 1D) conduction. ZrTe 3 features CDW below T CDW T_{\textrm{CDW}} = 63 K and filamentary superconductivity below 2 K that can be enhanced by pressure or chemical substitution. Here we report the presence of mixed valent Zr 2+ and Zr 4+ atoms in ZrTe 3 crystals that is reduced by doping in ZrTe 3-x Se x and Zr 1-y Hf y Te 3 . Superconductivity is enhanced via disorder in Te2-Te3 atomic chains that are associated with CDW formation. Hf substitution on Zr atomic site enhances T CDW T_{\textrm{CDW}} due to unperturbed Te2-Te3 chain periodicity and enhanced electron-phonon coupling. Weak electronic correlations in ZrTe 3-x Se x are likely governed by the lattice contraction effects.
