Probing Unification With Precision Higgs Physics
Sibo Zheng 1,2,

Abstract
We propose a novel approach of probing grand unification through precise measurements on the Higgs Yukawa couplings at the LHC. This idea is well motivated by the appearance of effective operators not suppressed by the mass scale of unification M U M_{\mathrm{U}} in realistic models of unification with the minimal structure of Yukawa sector. Such operators modify the Higgs Yukawa couplings in correlated patterns at scale M U M_{\mathrm{U}} that hold up to higher-order corrections. The coherences reveal a feature that, the deviation of tau Yukawa coupling relative to its standard model value at the weak scale is the largest one among the third-generation Yukawa couplings. This feature, if verified by the future LHC, can serve as a hint of unification.

V
Conclusions

Unification is an important theoretical idea of new physics beyond SM.
Yet, there are limited ways in testing it except proton decay experiment in the last a few decades.
Unfortunately, the advance along this line is delayed due to the experimental status.
In this work, we have proposed a novel approach of probing unification through precision measurements on the Higgs Yukawa couplings especially of the third generation.

This proposal is supported by three observations.
The first observation is the appearance of unsuppressed effective operators through integrating out the heavy Higgs freedom

45
or
126
in conventional
\mathrm{SU}(5)
or
\mathrm{SO}(10)
models with the minimal Yukawa sector, respectively.
The second observation is that the corrections to
y_{\alpha}
at the scale
M_{\text{U}}
are in three specific patterns,
as a result of either
\mathrm{SU}(5)
or
\mathrm{SO}(10)
embedding.
Lastly, the deviations to
y_{\alpha}
at the scale
m_{Z}
can be verified by the future LHC limits (see Fig.
2
),
although there are subject to certain uncertainties in the RG trajectory between the scales
m_{Z}
and
M_{\text{U}}
.

Our analysis shows that as a result of coherences,
a large deviation in
y_{\tau}
but small in
y_{t}
and
y_{b}

can serve as a hint of conventional realistic models of unification with the minimal Yukawa sector.
