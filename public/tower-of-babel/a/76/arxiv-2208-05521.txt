Equivariant Chern Weil Forms and The Families Index Theorem
Richard Wedeen

Abstract
. We apply the equivariance → \rightarrow families principle to a geometric family of Clifford module bundles with an action of a compact Lie group G G to prove an equivariant version of Bismut’s families index theorem on the differential Borel quotient of the geometric family.



In certain incarnations of the families index theorem, one considers a geometric family of Clifford module bundles over compact even dimensional Riemannian manifolds parametrized by a base
S
, i.e. an iterated bundle

(1)

\mathscr{E}\rightarrow X\rightarrow S

where
X\rightarrow S
is a fiber bundle with compact even dimensional fibers, a horizontal distribution, and a metric on the vertical tangent bundle
T(X/S)
, and
\mathscr{E}\rightarrow X
is a
\mathbb{C}\ell(T(X/S))
-module bundle with compatible connection.

This is the setting considered in
[
Bis86
]
, where Bismut adapted Quillen’s ideas in
[
Qui85
]
to construct a one-parameter family of superconnections

(2)

\mathbb{B}^{t}:\Omega_{S}(\mathscr{H})\rightarrow\Omega_{S}(\mathscr{H})

on the infinite dimensional vector bundle
\mathscr{H}
of fiberwise sections of
\mathscr{E}\rightarrow X
over
S
and proved that

(3)

\lim_{t\rightarrow 0}\ch(\mathbb{B}^{t})=\int_{X/S}\hat{A}(\Omega^{X/S})\ch_{\mathbb{\mathscr{E}}/\mathbb{S}}(F^{\mathscr{E}/\mathbb{S}})

where
\hat{A}(\Omega^{X/S})
is the
\hat{A}
-genus of the vertical curvature and
\ch_{\mathbb{\mathscr{E}}/\mathbb{S}}(F^{\mathscr{E}/\mathbb{S}})
is the relative Chern character of the twisting curvature of
\mathscr{E}
.

In this paper we consider the case when (
1
) has an action of a Lie group
G
preserving the geometric data. A similar situation was considered in
[
Fre16
]
which studied the case of a
G
-action on a geometric family
X\rightarrow S
which induced a
G
-action on the determinant line bundle

(4)

\mathcal{L}\rightarrow S.

There, the
G
-action was used to construct an iterated map of differential Borel quotients

(5)

(\mathcal{L}_{G})_{\nabla}\rightarrow(S_{G})_{\nabla}\rightarrow B_{\nabla}G

over the simplicial sheaf
B_{\nabla}G
of principal
G
-bundles with connection introduced and studied in
[
FH13
]
. The
G
-equivariant connection on
\mathcal{L}\rightarrow S
from
[
BF86
]
was used to construct a connection on
(\mathcal{L}_{G})_{\nabla}\rightarrow(S_{G})_{\nabla}
from which the moment map and curvature were computed. The curvature was identified with the
G
-equivariant curvature on
\mathcal{L}
using the identification of
\Omega_{(X_{G})_{\nabla}}
with the Weil-model for
X

[
FH13
]
.

We adapt these ideas and consider a geometric family of differential Borel quotients

(6)

(\mathscr{E}_{G})_{\nabla}\rightarrow(X_{G})_{\nabla}\rightarrow(S_{G})_{\nabla}

fibered over
B_{\nabla}G
. From this we construct a family of superconnections
\mathbb{B}^{t}_{G}
on a bundle
(\mathscr{H}_{G})_{\nabla}\rightarrow(S_{G})_{\nabla}
whose curvature is an equivariant form in the Weil-model for
S
. We also construct equivariant curvatures
\Omega_{G}^{X/S}
and
F_{G}^{\mathscr{E}/\mathbb{S}}
on
(X_{G})_{\nabla}
. We then prove our main theorem

(7)

\lim_{t\rightarrow 0}\ch(\mathbb{B}^{t}_{G})=\int_{X/S}\hat{A}(\Omega^{X/S}_{G})\ch_{\mathscr{E}/\mathbb{S}}(F^{\mathscr{E}/\mathbb{S}}_{G}).

An outline of the paper is as follows. In §
1
we set up the necessary differential geometry on manifolds and generalized manifolds, i.e. simplicial sheaves on the site of manifolds. In particular we construct the connection and curvature of the differential Borel quotient of a vector bundle with
G
-action and
G
-equivariant connection. In §
2
we review the families index theorem as proved by Bismut following
[
BGV03
]
. Finally in §
3
we construct the superconnection
\mathbb{B}^{t}_{G}
and prove the main result.
