Introduction and main results

Let

denote the symmetric group on
n

This paper delves into

]
of commuting

Let

Let

.
We

n

This

Let

where
P_{n}^{g_{\ell}}(x)
is a polynomial of degree
n

.

We also refer to Abdesselam et al. (
[
ABDV23
]

[
Wh13
]
.

In the
instance where

[

]

2.
Preliminaries

Let
n,\ell\geq 1
. Let
g_{\ell}(n)
denote the number of subgroups of
\mathbb{Z}^{\ell}
of index
n
. Then

g_{\ell}(n)=\sum_{d\mid n}d\,g_{\ell-1}(d),

where
g_{0}(n)=1
for
n=1
and
0
otherwise. The function
g_{\ell}
is
multiplicative and satisfies the following for
p
prime and
m\in\mathbb{N}
:

g_{\ell}(p^{m})=\frac{\left(p^{\ell}-1\right)\cdots\left(p^{\ell+m-1}-1\right)}{\left(p-1\right)\cdots\left(p^{m}-1\right)}.

Therefore,
g_{1}(n)=g_{\ell}(1)=1
,
g_{\ell}(2)=2^{\ell}-1
, and
g_{\ell}(4)=\frac{1}{3}\left(2^{\ell}-1\right)\,\left(2^{\ell+1}-1\right)
.
Let
\ell\geq 2
. Then

n^{\ell-1}\leq g_{\ell}(n)\leq n^{\ell}\leq\sigma(n)\,n^{\ell-1},

where
\sigma\left(n\right):=\sum_{d\mid n}d
.

Moreover, the
identity (Theorem
1.1
):

\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}N_{\ell}(n)\,t^{n}=\exp\left(\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}g_{\ell}(n)\,\frac{t^{n}}{n}\right)

implies that we have the following two properties:

(2.1)

\displaystyle N_{\ell}(n)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{k\leq n}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}m_{1},\ldots,m_{k}\geq 1\\
m_{1}+\ldots+m_{k}=n\end{subarray}}\frac{1}{k!}\,\frac{g_{\ell}\left(m_{1}\right)\cdots g_{\ell}\left(m_{k}\right)}{m_{1}\cdots m_{k}},

(2.2)

\displaystyle N_{\ell}(n)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{n}\,\sum_{k=1}^{n}\,g_{\ell}(k)\,N_{\ell}(n-k),\text{ where }N_{\ell}(0):=1.

A straightforward calculation leads to:

Lemma 2.1
.

Let
\ell,n\geq 1
, then

\Delta_{1}(n)=0
. Let
\ell>1
, then

\Delta_{\ell}(1)<0
and

\Delta_{\ell}(2)>0
. Further, let
\ell>1
, then
\Delta_{\ell}(3)<0

for
2\leq\ell\leq 13
and
\Delta_{\ell}(3)>0
for
\ell\geq 14
.

3.
Proof of Theorem
1.2

Let
n,\ell\geq 2
. It is well-known
[
BM65
,
MM65
,
SS20
,
HN22
]
that the
maximum
M_{1}\left(n\right)

over all products of partitions of
n
is given by

M_{1}\left(n\right):=\max_{k\leq n}\max_{\begin{subarray}{c}m_{1},\ldots,m_{k}\geq 1\\
m_{1}+\ldots+m_{k}=n\end{subarray}}m_{1}\cdots m_{k}=\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}3^{n/3},&n\equiv 0\pmod{3},\\
4\cdot 3^{\left(n-4\right)/3},&n\equiv 1\pmod{3},\\
2\cdot 3^{\left(n-2\right)/3},&n\equiv 2\pmod{3}.\end{array}\right.

This maximum is obtained for the following values of
k

dependent
on
n\pmod{3}
:

k=\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}n/3,&n\equiv 0\pmod{3},\\
\left(n-1\right)/3\text{ or }\left(n+2\right)/3,&n\equiv 1\pmod{3},\\
\left(n+1\right)/3,&n\equiv 2\pmod{3}.\end{array}\right.

Moreover, for each of these values of
k
,
the number of tuples reaching the maximum
is given by:

\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}1,&n\equiv 0\pmod{3},\\
\frac{n-1}{3}\text{ or }\binom{\left(n+2\right)/3}{2},&n\equiv 1\pmod{3},\\
\frac{n+1}{3},&n\equiv 2\pmod{3}.\end{array}\right.

Since
n^{\ell-1}\leq g_{\ell}(n)\leq g_{2}(n)\,\,n^{\ell-1}
, we obtain:

(3.1)

A_{\ell}\left(n\right)\leq N_{\ell}(n)\leq N_{2}\left(n\right)\,\left(M_{1}\left(n\right)\right)^{\ell-1}.

The values of
A_{\ell}\left(n\right)
are given by

A_{\ell}\left(n\right)=\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\frac{3^{\left(\ell-2\right)n/3}}{\left(n/3\right)!},&n\equiv 0\pmod{3},\\
\frac{3\left(4\cdot 3^{\left(n-4\right)/3}\right)^{\ell-2}}{2\left(\left(n-4\right)/3\right)!},&n\equiv 1\pmod{3},\\
\frac{\left(2\cdot 3^{\left(n-2\right)/3}\right)^{\ell-2}}{\left(\left(n-2\right)/3\right)!},&n\equiv 2\pmod{3}.\end{array}\right.

We recall that
N_{2}(n)=p(n)
.

3.1.
The case
n\equiv 0\pmod{3}

We obtain
via (
3.1
):

\displaystyle\frac{N_{\ell}(n)^{2}}{N_{\ell}(n-1)N_{\ell}(n+1)}

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\frac{3^{-2n/3}}{\left((n/3)!\right)^{2}\,p(n-1)\,p(n+1)}\,\,\left(\frac{9}{8}\right)^{\ell-1}

\displaystyle>

\displaystyle\frac{3^{-2n/3}}{\left(\left(n/3\right)!\right)^{2}p\left(n-1\right)p\left(n+1\right)}\left(1+\frac{\ell-1}{8}\right).

Therefore,
\Delta_{\ell}(n)>0
for

.

3.2.
The case
n\equiv 1\pmod{3}

We obtain
via (
3.1
):

\displaystyle\frac{N_{\ell}(n)^{2}}{N_{\ell}(n-1)N_{\ell}(n+1)}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\left(\left(\frac{n-1}{3}\right)!\,\,\,p\left(n\right)\right)^{2}\,2\cdot 3^{2\left(n-1\right)/3}\,\left(\frac{8}{9}\right)^{\ell-1}

\displaystyle<

\displaystyle\left(\left(\frac{n-1}{3}\right)!p\left(n\right)\right)^{2}2\cdot 3^{2\left(n-1\right)/3}\left(1+\frac{\ell-1}{8}\right)^{-1}.

Therefore,
\Delta_{\ell}(n)<0
for

\ell\geq 1+8\left(\left(\left(\frac{n-1}{3}\right)!p\left(n\right)\right)^{2}2\cdot 3^{2\left(n-1\right)/3}-1\right)
.

3.3.
The case
n\equiv 2\pmod{3}

This particular case is more delicate
compared to all other cases.

A
direct calculation
reveals the following:

Lemma 3.1
.

For fixed
n>1
the leading term of
N_{\ell}\left(n\right)
in terms
of
\ell
is obtained by

B_{\ell}\left(n\right)=C_{1}\left(n\right)\left(M_{1}\left(n\right)\right)^{\ell-1}
,
defined by

C_{1}\left(n\right)=\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\frac{1}{2^{n/3}\left(\frac{n}{3}\right)!},&n\equiv 0\pmod{3},\\
\frac{7}{6}\frac{1}{2^{\left(n-4\right)/3}\left(\frac{n-4}{3}\right)!},&n\equiv 1\pmod{3},\\
\frac{1}{2^{\left(n-2\right)/3}\left(\frac{n-2}{3}\right)!},&n\equiv 2\pmod{3}.\end{array}\right.

The bases of the second order growth terms are obtained by

M_{2}\left(n\right)
defined as

M_{2}\left(n\right)=\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}2,&n=3,\\
2^{3}\cdot 3^{\left(n-6\right)/3},&n>3,n\equiv 0\pmod{3},\\
3,&n=4,\\
10,&n=7,\\
2^{5}\cdot 3^{\left(n-10\right)/3},&n>7,n\equiv 1\pmod{3},\\
5,&n=5,\\
2^{4}\cdot 3^{\left(n-8\right)/3},&n>5,n\equiv 2\pmod{3}.\\
\end{array}\right.

Then we obtain

B_{\ell}\left(n\right)\leq N_{\ell}\left(n\right)\leq B_{\ell}\left(n\right)+p\left(n\right)M_{2}\left(n\right).

Proof.

We obtain for
n\equiv 0\pmod{3}
that the leading term
of

N_{\ell}\left(n\right)
in terms of
\ell
is

\frac{1}{2^{n/3}\left(\frac{n}{3}\right)!}\cdot 3^{\left(\ell-1\right)n/3}
.
For
n\equiv 1\pmod{3}
,
n\geq 4
, the
maximal growth term of

N_{\ell}\left(n\right)
with respect to
\ell

is

2^{-\frac{n-4}{3}}\frac{7}{6\left(\left(n-4\right)/3\right)!}\cdot\left(4\cdot 3^{\frac{n-4}{3}}\right)^{\ell-1}
.
For
n\equiv 2\pmod{3}
,
we obtain

N_{\ell}\left(n\right)=R+\frac{1}{\left(\left(n-2\right)/3\right)!}\cdot 2^{-\frac{n-2}{3}}\cdot\left(2\cdot 3^{\frac{n-2}{3}}\right)^{\ell-1}
,
where
R
collects the terms of strictly slower growth. Similarly
for
M_{2}\left(n\right)
.
∎

Continuing
with the proof for
n\equiv 2\pmod{3}
,
considering for

n\equiv 2\pmod{3}
,
n\geq 5
, the terms of largest growth in
\ell

are obtained as follows:

\displaystyle\frac{\left(N_{\ell}\left(n\right)\right)^{2}}{N_{\ell}\left(n-1\right)N_{\ell}\left(n+1\right)}

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\frac{\frac{\left(C_{1}\left(n\right)\right)^{2}}{C_{1}\left(n-1\right)C_{1}\left(n+1\right)}\left(\frac{\left(M_{1}\left(n\right)\right)^{2}}{M_{1}\left(n-1\right)M_{1}\left(n+1\right)}\right)^{\ell-1}}{\left(1+\frac{p\left(n-1\right)}{C_{1}\left(n-1\right)}\left(\frac{M_{2}\left(n-1\right)}{M_{1}\left(n-1\right)}\right)^{\ell-1}\right)\left(1+\frac{p\left(n+1\right)}{C_{1}\left(n+1\right)}\left(\frac{M_{2}\left(n+1\right)}{M_{1}\left(n+1\right)}\right)^{\ell-1}\right)}

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\frac{6}{7}\frac{n+1}{n-2}\frac{1}{\left(1+\frac{1}{71}\right)^{2}}>\frac{6}{7}\frac{n+1}{n-2}\frac{35}{36}=\frac{5}{6}\frac{n+1}{n-2}\geq 1

if
n\leq 17
and

\frac{p\left(n+s\right)}{C_{1}\left(n+s\right)}\left(\frac{M_{2}\left(n+s\right)}{M_{1}\left(n+s\right)}\right)^{\ell-1}\leq\frac{1}{71}

for
s=\pm 1
. This holds true if

\ell\geq 1+\max_{s=\pm 1}\log_{M_{1}\left(n+s\right)/M_{2}\left(n+s\right)}\left(71\frac{p\left(n+s\right)}{C_{1}\left(n+s\right)}\right)
.

Note that

\frac{M_{1}\left(n-1\right)}{M_{2}\left(n-1\right)}=\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\frac{4}{3}\geq\frac{9}{8},&n=5,\\
\frac{12}{10}=\frac{6}{5}\geq\frac{9}{8},&n=8,\\
\frac{4\cdot 3^{\left(n-5\right)/3}}{2^{5}3^{\left(n-11\right)/3}}=\frac{9}{8},&n>8,n\equiv 2\pmod{3}\end{array}\right.

and

\frac{M_{1}\left(n+1\right)}{M_{2}\left(n+1\right)}=\frac{3^{\left(n+1\right)/3}}{2^{3}\cdot 3^{\left(n-5\right)/3}}=\frac{9}{8}.

Therefore,

\displaystyle\max_{s=\pm 1}\log_{M_{1}\left(n+s\right)/M_{2}\left(n+s\right)}\left(71\frac{p\left(n+s\right)}{C_{1}\left(n+s\right)}\right)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\max_{s=\pm 1}\log_{9/8}\left(71\frac{p\left(n+s\right)}{C_{1}\left(n+s\right)}\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\log_{9/8}\left(71\cdot 2^{\left(n+1\right)/3}\left(\frac{n+1}{3}\right)!p\left(n+1\right)\right)

since
C_{1}\left(n+1\right)<C_{1}\left(n-1\right)

for
n\equiv 2\pmod{3}
and
n>2
.

In the opposite direction, we obtain

\displaystyle\left(N_{\ell}\left(n\right)\right)^{2}-N_{\ell}\left(n-1\right)N_{\ell}\left(n+1\right)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{\left(C_{1}\left(n\right)\right)^{2}}{C_{1}\left(n-1\right)C_{1}\left(n+1\right)}\left(\frac{\left(M_{1}\left(n\right)\right)^{2}}{M_{1}\left(n-1\right)M_{1}\left(n+1\right)}\right)^{\ell-1}\left(1+\frac{p\left(n\right)}{C_{1}\left(n\right)}\left(\frac{M_{2}\left(n\right)}{M_{1}\left(n\right)}\right)^{\ell-1}\right)^{2}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{6}{7}\frac{n+1}{n-2}\left(1+\frac{1}{97}\right)^{2}<\frac{6}{7}\frac{n+1}{n-2}\frac{49}{48}=\frac{7}{8}\frac{n+1}{n-2}\leq 1

if
n\geq 23
and

\frac{p\left(n\right)}{C_{1}\left(n\right)}\left(\frac{M_{1}\left(n\right)}{M_{2}\left(n\right)}\right)^{\ell-1}\leq\frac{1}{97}
.
This holds true if

\ell\geq 1+\log_{M_{1}\left(n\right)/M_{2}\left(n\right)}\left(97\frac{p\left(n\right)}{C_{1}\left(n\right)}\right)=\log_{9/8}\left(97\cdot 2^{\left(n-2\right)/3}\cdot\left(\frac{n-2}{3}\right)!p\left(n\right)\right)

since

\frac{M_{1}\left(n\right)}{M_{2}\left(n\right)}=\frac{2\cdot 3^{\left(n-2\right)/3}}{16\cdot 3^{\left(n-8\right)/3}}=\frac{9}{8}
.
In order to complete the analysis, let us consider the case
n=20
. Including
the
third largest products as well,
we
derive the following through another straightforward calculation:

Lemma 3.2
.

In terms of the first three growth terms, we have

N_{\ell}\left(20\right)\leq C_{1}\left(20\right)\left(M_{1}\left(20\right)\right)^{\ell-1}+C_{2}\left(20\right)\left(M_{2}\left(20\right)\right)^{\ell-1}+p\left(20\right)\left(M_{3}\left(20\right)\right)^{\ell-1}

with

C_{2}\left(20\right)=\frac{43}{4!4!3!2^{3}},\qquad M_{3}\left(20\right)=5\cdot 3^{5}.

With the first two growth terms, we
establish for
n\in\left\{19,21\right\}

N_{\ell}\left(n\right)\geq C_{1}\left(n\right)\left(M_{1}\left(n\right)\right)^{\ell-1}+C_{2}\left(n\right)\left(M_{2}\left(n\right)\right)^{\ell-1}

with

C_{2}\left(19\right)=\frac{41}{6!2^{3}},\qquad C_{2}\left(21\right)=\frac{1}{3!4!2^{5}}.

Proof.

For
n=21\equiv 0\pmod{3}
,
the leading terms of
N_{\ell}\left(n\right)
regarding
\ell

are

\frac{1}{2^{7}\cdot 7!}\cdot 3^{7\ell-7}+\frac{1}{6\cdot 2^{5}\cdot 4!}\left(8\cdot 3^{5}\right)^{\ell-1}
.
For
n=19\equiv 1\pmod{3}
,
we obtain the leading first two terms of

N_{\ell}\left(n\right)
regarding
\ell
as

\frac{41}{6!}\cdot 2^{5\ell-8}\cdot 3^{3\ell-3}+\frac{7}{2^{5}\cdot 6!}\cdot\left(4\cdot 3^{5}\right)^{\ell-1}
.
For
n=20\equiv 2\pmod{3}
we obtain the first two leading terms of

N_{\ell}\left(n\right)
regarding
\ell
as

2^{\ell-7}\cdot 3^{6\ell-6}\cdot\frac{1}{6!}+2^{4\ell-8}\cdot 3^{4\ell-4}\cdot\frac{43}{4!4!3}
.

For
M_{3}\left(20\right)
the calculation is similar.
∎

Therefore, regarding the proof for
n=20
, we establish

\displaystyle\left(N_{\ell}\left(20\right)\right)^{2}-N_{\ell}\left(19\right)N_{\ell}\left(21\right)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\left(C_{1}\left(20\right)\left(M_{1}\left(20\right)\right)^{\ell-1}+C_{2}\left(20\right)\left(M_{2}\left(20\right)\right)^{\ell-1}+p\left(20\right)\left(M_{3}\left(20\right)\right)^{\ell-1}\right)^{2}

\displaystyle{}-\left(C_{1}\left(19\right)\left(M_{1}\left(19\right)\right)^{\ell-1}+C_{2}\left(19\right)\left(M_{2}\left(19\right)\right)^{\ell-1}\right)

\displaystyle\phantom{{}-}\cdot\left(C_{1}\left(21\right)\left(M_{1}\left(21\right)\right)^{\ell-1}+C_{2}\left(21\right)\left(M_{2}\left(21\right)\right)^{\ell-1}\right)

\displaystyle<

\displaystyle-2^{5\ell-15}\cdot 3^{10\ell-10}\cdot\frac{211}{7!3!6!}+\left(C_{2}\left(20\right)\left(M_{2}\left(20\right)\right)^{\ell-1}+p\left(20\right)\left(M_{3}\left(20\right)\right)^{\ell-1}\right)^{2}

\displaystyle{}+C_{1}\left(20\right)p\left(20\right)\left(M_{1}\left(20\right)M_{3}\left(20\right)\right)^{\ell-1}

\displaystyle 1+\log_{16/15}\left(\frac{3!6!7!2^{10}}{211}\left(\left(\frac{43}{4!4!3!2^{3}}+p\left(20\right)\right)^{2}+\frac{p\left(20\right)}{6!2^{5}}\right)\right)

.
