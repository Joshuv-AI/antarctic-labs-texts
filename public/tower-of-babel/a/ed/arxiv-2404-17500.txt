A note on the rational non-integrability of the N N -center problem for almost all degrees of the singularities
Eddaly Guerra-Velasco 1,2 , Boris Percino-Figueroa 2 , Russell-Aarón Quiñones-Estrella 2

Abstract
. In this article, we show that the N N -center problem with rational weak and moderate forces is not rationally integrable for all but a finite number of values α ∈ ( 0 , 2 ) ∩ ℚ \alpha\in(0,2)\cap\mathbb{Q} , where α \alpha is the order of the singularities. We identify the remaining cases and provide the necessary conditions for their integrability.

2.
Rational weak and moderate forces

In this manuscript, we notice that using the ideas,
mutatis mutandis
, of Shibayama, this result can be generalized to a broader family of
N
-center problems to obtain a criterion on the integrability in terms of the degree of the singularities. To be more specific, consider the Hamiltonian system with
n

(2.1)

H_{\alpha}(q,p)=\frac{1}{2}\left\|p\right\|^{2}-\sum_{i=1}^{N}\frac{m_{i}}{\|q-c_{i}\|^{\alpha}},

with centers
c_{1},\dots,c_{N}\in\mathbb{R}^{n}
and
\alpha
a rational number in
(0,2)
called degree of the singularities.

In order to state the generalization, pick some
l\in\mathbb{N},l\leq N
and
e=(e_{1},\dots,e_{n})\in\mathbb{C}^{n}
such that

(e_{1}-c_{k}^{1})^{2}+\cdots+(e_{n}-c_{k}^{n})^{2}=\begin{cases}=0,&k\leq l,\\
\neq 0,&k>l.\end{cases}.

Given a vector
V=(v_{1},\dots,v_{n})\in\mathbb{C}^{n}
define the constant

(2.2)

C:=C_{V}=\sum_{i=1}^{l}\frac{m_{i}}{\alpha[v_{i}(e-c_{i}]^{\frac{\alpha+2}{2}}}(e-c_{i}).

Consider the system

(2.3)

\begin{split}\frac{dq}{dt}&=p\\
\frac{dp}{dt}&=\sum_{i}\frac{m_{i}}{\frac{\alpha}{2}[2q\cdot(e-c_{i})]^{\frac{\alpha+2}{2}}}(e-c_{i}),\end{split}

g(t)

(2.4)

\frac{d^{2}g(t)}{dt^{2}}=\frac{C_{V}}{(g(t))^{\frac{\alpha+2}{2}}},

2.3

(2.5)

A_{ij}=\sum_{k}\frac{(\alpha+2)m_{k}(e_{i}-c_{i}^{k})(e_{j}-c_{j}^{k})}{[2q(t)\cdot(e-c_{k})]^{\frac{\alpha+2}{2}}}.

Finally, consider the eigenvalues
a_{k},k=1,\dots,n
