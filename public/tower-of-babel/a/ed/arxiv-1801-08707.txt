On p/q-recognisable sets
Victor Marsault

Abstract
. Let p q \frac{p}{q} be a rational number. Numeration in base p q \frac{p}{q} is defined by a function that evaluates each finite word over A p = { 0 , 1 , … , p − 1 } A_{p}=\{0,\,1,\,\ldots,\allowbreak{p-1}\} to a rational number. We let N p q N_{\raisebox{0.60275pt}[0.0pt][0.0pt]{\hskip-0.3014pt$\scriptstyle\frac{p}{q}$}} denote the image of this evaluation function. In particular, N p q N_{\raisebox{0.60275pt}[0.0pt][0.0pt]{\hskip-0.3014pt$\scriptstyle\frac{p}{q}$}} contains all nonnegative integers and the literature on base p q \frac{p}{q} usually focuses on the set of words that are evaluated to nonnegative integers; it is a rather chaotic language which is not context-free. On the contrary, we study here the subsets of ( N p q ) d (\smash{N_{\raisebox{0.60275pt}[0.0pt][0.0pt]{\hskip-0.3014pt$\scriptstyle\frac{p}{q}$}}})^{d} that are p q \frac{p}{q} -recognisable, i.e. realised by finite automata over ( A p ) d \left(A_{p}\right)^{d} . First, we give a characterisation of these sets as those definable in a first-order logic, similar to the one given by the Büchi-Bruyère Theorem for integer base numeration systems. Second, we show that the natural order relation and the modulo- q q operator are not p q \frac{p}{q} -recognisable.

Introduction

Let
p

p>q>1

\frac{p}{q}

is an irreducible fraction greater than

The base-
\frac{p}{q}

[

]

The study of such

[
Akiy08
]
).
Like other numerations systems, base
\frac{p}{q}
gives a way to

represent
numbers by words, and to

Here, we consider only finite words over the canonical
alphabet

One paradox of base
\frac{p}{q}

represented by complicated languages and that simple languages are evaluated to
complicated number sets.
For instance,
\mathbb{N}
is represented by a rather chaotic language,
L_{\frac{p}{q}}
,
that does not fit well in the usual hierarchy of formal languages.
On the other hand, the evaluations of all words over
A_{p}

numbers,

Literature on base
\frac{p}{q}
mostly focuses on

.
It is not a context-free language
[
AkiyEtAl08
]

and even defeats any kind of iteration lemma

]

However, there is a very simple periodic procedure to generate

[
MarsSaka17b
]

Some effort have also been made to study

On the contrary, there has been little work on

Here, we start exploring this area by studying the subsets of

realised by finite automata reading synchronously on
d

We call such sets
\frac{p}{q}
-recognisable

In the original article introducing base

[
AkiyEtAl08
]

-recognisable (in fact, normalisation of representations from any

This is a strong property which, in the case of integer base

A subset of

is the function that maps
n

Similar statements are also known for other numeration systems

A subset of
