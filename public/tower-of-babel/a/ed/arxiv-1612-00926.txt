Complex Hadamard matrices attached to even orthogonal schemes of class 4 4
Takuya Ikuta, Akihiro Munemasa

Abstract
A complex Hadamard matrix is a square matrix W W with complex entries of absolute value 1 1 satisfying W ​ W ∗ = n ​ I WW^{\ast}=nI , where ∗ \ast stands for the Hermitian transpose and I I is the identity matrix of order n n . In this paper, we give constructions of complex Hadamard matrices in the Bose–Mesner algebra of a certain 4-class symmetric association scheme. Moreover, we determine the Nomura algebras to show that the resulting matrices are not decomposable into nontrivial generalized tensor products.
