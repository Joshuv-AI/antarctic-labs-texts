Girth Alternative for HNN Extensions
Azer Akhmedov, Pratyush Mishra

Abstract
. We prove the Girth Alternative for a sub-class of HNN extensions as well as for a sub-class of amalgamated free products of finitely generated groups, and indicate counterexamples to show that beyond our class, the alternative fails in general.We also prove the Girth Alternative for HNN extensions of non-elementary word hyperbolic groups.

For the group

(P)
We say a finitely generated group has property

if for all proper subgroups

Britton’s Lemma states that in an HNN extension

Letting

.
