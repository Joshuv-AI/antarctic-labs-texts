Abstract
We consider asymptotic limits of q-oscillator (or Heisenberg) realizations of Verma modules over the quantum superalgebra U q ​ ( g ​ l ​ ( M | N ) ) U_{q}(gl(M|N)) , and obtain q-oscillator realizations of the contracted algebras proposed in [ 1 ] . Based on this result, we obtain explicit q-oscillator representations of a Borel subalgebra of the quantum affine superalgebra U q ​ ( g ​ l ^ ​ ( M | N ) ) U_{q}(\hat{gl}(M|N)) for Baxter Q-operators.
