exponential approximation of functions in Lebesgue spaces with Muckenhoupt weight
Ramazan Akgün

Abstract




Abstract
Using a transference result, several inequalities of
approximation by entire functions of exponential type in
\mathcal{C}(\mathbf{R})
, the class of bounded uniformly continuous functions defined on

\mathbf{R}:=\left(-\infty,+\infty\right)
, are extended to the Lebesgue
spaces
L^{p}\left(\mathbf{\varrho}dx\right)

1\leq p<\infty
with
Muckenhoupt weight
\mathbf{\varrho}
(
1\leq p<\infty
). This gives us a
different proof of Jackson type direct theorems and Bernstein-Timan type
inverse estimates in
L^{p}\left(\mathbf{\varrho}dx\right)
. Results also
cover the case
p=1
.

Lebesgue spaces, Muckenhoupt weight, One sided Steklov
operator, Entire functions of exponential type, Best approximation, Direct
theorem, Inverse theorem, Modulus of smoothness, Marchaud type inequality,

-functional.

2.
Preliminary Notations and Transference Result

A function
\mathbf{\varrho}:\mathbf{R}\mathbb{\rightarrow}\left[0,\infty\right]
will be called weight if
\mathbf{\varrho}
is measurable and
positive a.e. on
\mathbf{R}.
Define
\left\langle\mathbf{\varrho}\right\rangle_{\mathbf{A}}:=\int_{\mathbf{A}}\mathbf{\varrho}(t)dt
for
\mathbf{A}\subset\mathbf{R}
. A weight
\mathbf{\varrho}
belongs to the
Muckenhoupt class
A_{\mathbf{p}}
,
1\leq p<\infty
, if

(2.1)

\left|J\right|^{-1}\left\langle\mathbf{\varrho}\right\rangle_{J}\leq\left[\mathbf{\varrho}\right]_{1}(\mathop{\mathrm{e}ssinf}\nolimits_{x\in J\text{ }}\mathbf{\varrho}\left(x\right))\text{, a.e. on }\mathbf{R}\text{,\quad}\left(p=1\right)\text{,}

(2.2)

\left[\mathbf{\varrho}\right]_{p}:=\sup\limits_{J\subset\mathbf{R}}\left|J\right|^{-p}\left\langle\mathbf{\varrho}\right\rangle_{J}\left\langle\mathbf{\varrho}^{1/(1-p)}\right\rangle_{J}^{p-1}<\infty\text{,\quad}\left(1<p<\infty\right)

with some finite constants independent of
J
.

For a weight
\mathbf{\varrho}
on
\mathbf{R}
, we denote by
L^{p}\left(\mathbf{\varrho}dx\right)
,
1\leq p\leq\infty
the class of real valued
measurable functions, defined on
\mathbf{R},
such that

\left\|f\right\|_{p,\mathbf{\varrho}}\text{:=}\left(\int\nolimits_{\mathbf{R}}\left|f\left(x\right)\right|^{p}\mathbf{\varrho}\left(x\right)dx\right)^{1/p}\infty\text{,\quad(}1<p<\infty\text{) and}

\left\|f\right\|_{\infty,\mathbf{\varrho}}\text{:=}ess.\sup\nolimits_{x\in\mathbf{R}}\left|f\left(x\right)\right|\text{,\quad(}p=\infty\text{).}

Let
C(\mathbf{R})
(respectively
\mathcal{C}(\mathbf{R})
) be the class
of continuous (bounded uniformly continuous ) functions defined on
\mathbf{R}
. We denote by
C_{c}
(respectively
S_{c}
) the collection of real
valued continuous (respectively simple) functions
f
on
\mathbf{R}
such
that support
sptf
of
f
is a compact set in
\mathbf{R}.

For
1<p<\infty
, we

set
(1/p)+(1/p^{\prime})=1
.

2.1
, given below, follows from
[

, (2.7) p.933 and
(2.10) p.934]
.

.

Theorem 2.6
.

We suppose that
1\leq p<\infty
and
\mathbf{\varrho}\in A_{p}
. Then, family of Steklov Mean Operators
\{\mathbf{S}_{1,\tau}\}_{\tau\in\mathbf{R}}
is uniformly bounded (in

) in

, namely,

\left\|\mathbf{S}_{1,\tau}f\right\|_{p,\mathbf{\varrho}}\leq 3\cdot 3^{\frac{2}{p}}\left[\mathbf{\varrho}\right]_{p}^{1/p}\left\|f\right\|_{p,\mathbf{\varrho}}\text{ \ \ for }\tau\in\mathbf{R}\text{.}

Proof of Theorem
2.6
.

Let
Q
be
1
-finite family of open bounded subsets
P_{i}
of
\mathbb{R}

having Lebesgue measure
1
such that
\left(\cup_{i}P_{i}\right)\cup A=\mathbf{R}
for some null-set
A
. Since
\tau\in\mathbf{R}
there exists
m\in\mathbb{Z}
such that
m\leq\tau<(m+2)
. Let
P+m
be translation of
the set
P
by
m
. We set
\left(P_{i}+m\right)^{\pm}:=\left(P_{i-1}\cup P_{i}\cup P_{i+1}\right)+m
. Then

\left\|\mathbf{S}_{1,\tau}f\right\|_{p,\mathbf{\varrho}}^{p}=\sum\limits_{P_{i}\in Q}\int\limits_{P_{i}}\left|\lambda\int\limits_{x+\tau-1/2}^{x+\tau+1/2}f(t)dt\right|^{p}\mathbf{\varrho}(x)dx

\leq\sum\limits_{P_{i}\in Q}\int\limits_{P_{i}}\left[\int\limits_{x+\tau-1/\lambda}^{x+\tau+1/2}\mathbf{\varrho}^{\frac{1}{p}}(t)\left|f(t)\right|\frac{1}{\mathbf{\varrho}^{\frac{1}{p}}(t)}dt\right]^{p}\mathbf{\varrho}(x)dx

\leq\sum\limits_{P_{i}\in Q}\int\limits_{P_{i}}\left(\left(\int\limits_{x+\tau-1/2}^{x+\tau+1/2}\mathbf{\varrho}(t)\left|f(t)\right|^{p}dt\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}\left(\int\limits_{x+\tau-1/2}^{x+\tau+1/2}\mathbf{\varrho}^{\frac{-p^{\prime}}{p}}(t)dt\right)^{\frac{1}{p^{\prime}}}\right)^{p}\mathbf{\varrho}(x)dx

=\sum\limits_{P_{i}\in Q}\int\limits_{P_{i}}\int\limits_{x+\tau-1/2}^{x+\tau+1/2}\mathbf{\varrho}(t)\left|f(t)\right|^{p}dt\left(\int\limits_{x+\tau-1/2}^{x+\tau+1/2}\mathbf{\varrho}^{\frac{-p^{\prime}}{p}}(t)dt\right)^{\frac{p}{p^{\prime}}}\mathbf{\varrho}(x)dx

=\sum\limits_{P_{i}\in Q}\int\limits_{P_{i}}\int\limits_{x+\tau-1/2}^{x+\tau+1/2}\mathbf{\varrho}(t)\left|f(t)\right|^{p}dt\left(\int\limits_{x+\tau-1/2}^{x+\tau+1/2}\mathbf{\varrho}^{-\frac{1}{p-1}}(t)dt\right)^{p-1}\mathbf{\varrho}(x)dx

=\sum\limits_{P_{i}\in Q}\int\limits_{P_{i}}\left(\int\limits_{x+\tau-1/2}^{x+\tau+1/2}\mathbf{\varrho}^{-\frac{1}{p-1}}(t)dt\right)^{p-1}\left(\int\limits_{x+\tau-1/2}^{x+\tau+1/2}\mathbf{\varrho}(t)\left|f(t)\right|^{p}dt\right)\mathbf{\varrho}(x)dx

\leq 3^{p}\sum\limits_{P_{i}\in Q}\left(\frac{1}{\left|P_{i}^{\pm}\right|}\int\limits_{P_{i}^{\pm}}\mathbf{\varrho}(x)dx\right)\left(\frac{1}{\left|\left(P_{i}+m\right)^{\pm}\right|}\int\limits_{\left(P_{i}+m\right)^{\pm}}\mathbf{\varrho}^{-\frac{1}{p-1}}(t)dt\right)^{p-1}\times

\times\int\limits_{\left(P_{i}+m\right)^{\pm}}\mathbf{\varrho}(t)\left|f(t)\right|^{p}dt

\leq 3^{p}\left[\mathbf{\varrho}\right]_{p}\sum\limits_{P_{i}\in Q}\left\{\int\limits_{P_{i-1}+m}+\int\limits_{P_{i}+m}+\int\limits_{P_{i+1}+m}\right\}\mathbf{\varrho}(t)\left|f(t)\right|^{p}dt

\leq 3^{p+1}\left[\mathbf{\varrho}\right]_{p}\int\limits_{\mathbf{R}}\mathbf{\varrho}(t)\left|f(t)\right|^{p}\left\{\sum\limits_{P_{i}\in Q}\chi_{P_{i-1}+m}\left(t\right)+\sum\limits_{P_{i}\in Q}\chi_{P_{i}+m}\left(t\right)+\sum\limits_{P_{i}\in Q}\chi_{P_{i+1}+m}\left(t\right)\right\}dt

=3^{p+2}\left[\mathbf{\varrho}\right]_{p}\int\limits_{\mathbf{R}}\mathbf{\varrho}(t)\left|f(t)\right|^{p}dt=3^{p+2}\left[\mathbf{\varrho}\right]_{p}\left\|f\right\|_{p,\mathbf{\varrho}}^{p}\text{ and}

\left\|\mathbf{S}_{1,\tau}f\right\|_{p,\mathbf{\varrho}}\leq 3\cdot 3^{\frac{2}{p}}\left[\mathbf{\varrho}\right]_{p}^{1/p}\left\|f\right\|_{p,\mathbf{\varrho}}.

For
p=1
, we find

\left\|\mathbf{S}_{1,\tau}f\right\|_{1,\mathbf{\varrho}}=\sum\limits_{P_{i}\in Q}\int\limits_{P_{i}}\left|\int\limits_{x+\tau-1/2}^{x+\tau+1/2}f(t)dt\right|\mathbf{\varrho}(x)dx

\leq\sum\limits_{P_{i}\in Q}\int\limits_{P_{i}}\int\limits_{x+\tau-1/2}^{x+\tau+1/2}\mathbf{\varrho}(t)\left|f(t)\right|\frac{1}{\mathbf{\varrho}(t)}dt\mathbf{\varrho}(x)dx

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle 3\sum\limits_{P_{i}\in Q}\frac{1}{\left|\left(P_{i}+m\right)^{\pm}\right|}\int\limits_{\left(P_{i}+m\right)^{\pm}}\mathbf{\varrho}(x)dx\left(\underset{t\in\left(P_{i}+m\right)^{\pm}}{\mathop{\mathrm{e}sssup}}\frac{1}{\mathbf{\varrho}(t)}\right)\int\limits_{\left(P_{i}+m\right)^{\pm}}\mathbf{\varrho}(t)\left|f(t)\right|dt

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle 3\left[\gamma\right]_{1}\sum\limits_{P_{i}\in Q}\left\{\int\limits_{P_{i-1}+m}+\int\limits_{P_{i}+m}+\int\limits_{P_{i+1}+m}\right\}\mathbf{\varrho}(t)\left|f(t)\right|dt

\leq 3\left[\gamma\right]_{1}\int\limits_{\mathbf{R}}\mathbf{\varrho}(t)\left|f(t)\right|\left\{\sum\limits_{P_{i}\in Q}\chi_{P_{i-1}+m}\left(t\right)+\sum\limits_{P_{i}\in Q}\chi_{P_{i}+m}\left(t\right)+\sum\limits_{P_{i}\in Q}\chi_{P_{i+1}+m}\left(t\right)\right\}dt

\leq 9\left[\gamma\right]_{1}\left\|f\right\|_{1,\mathbf{\varrho}}.

\left|F_{H}\left(u_{1}\right)-F_{H}\left(u_{2}\right)\right|=\left|\int\nolimits_{\boldsymbol{R}}\left(S_{1,u_{1}}H\left(x\right)-S_{1,u_{2}}H\left(x\right)\right)\left|G\left(x\right)\right|\mathbf{\varrho}\left(x\right)dx\right|

\leq\frac{\varepsilon}{2\left(1+\left\langle\mathbf{\varrho}\right\rangle_{\text{supp}\left(G\right)}\right)}\int\nolimits_{\boldsymbol{R}}\left|G\left(x\right)\right|\mathbf{\varrho}\left(x\right)dx

\leq\frac{\varepsilon}{\left(1+\left\langle\mathbf{\varrho}\right\rangle_{\text{supp}\left(G\right)}\right)}\left\langle\mathbf{\varrho}\right\rangle_{\text{supp}\left(G\right)}\left\|G\right\|_{p^{\prime},\mathbf{\varrho}}<\varepsilon.

Now, the conclusion follows for the class
C_{c}
. For the general case
f\in L_{p\left(\cdot\right)}
there exists an
H\in C_{c}\left(\boldsymbol{R}\right)
so that

\left\|f-H\right\|_{p\left(\cdot\right)}<\xi/(12\cdot 3^{\frac{2}{p}}\left[\mathbf{\varrho}\right]_{p}^{1/p})

for any
\xi>0.
Then, for this
\xi
,

\left|F_{f}\left(u_{1}\right)-F_{f}\left(u_{2}\right)\right|\leq\left|\int\nolimits_{\boldsymbol{R}}\mathsf{S}_{1,u_{1}}(f-H)\left(x\right)\left|G\left(x\right)\right|\mathbf{\varrho}\left(x\right)dx\right|+

+\left|\int\nolimits_{\boldsymbol{R}}\left(\mathsf{S}_{1,u_{1}}H\left(x\right)-\mathsf{S}_{1,u_{2}}H\left(x\right)\right)\left|G\left(x\right)\right|\mathbf{\varrho}\left(x\right)dx\right|

+\left|\int\nolimits_{\boldsymbol{R}}\mathsf{S}_{1,u_{2}}(H-f)\left(x\right)\left|G\left(x\right)\right|\mathbf{\varrho}\left(x\right)dx\right|

\leq\left\|\mathsf{S}_{1,u_{1}}\left(f-H\right)\right\|_{p,\mathbf{\varrho}}+\left|\int\nolimits_{\boldsymbol{R}}\mathsf{S}_{1}\left(H\left(x+u_{1}\right)-H\left(x+u_{2}\right)\right)\left|G\left(x\right)\right|\mathbf{\varrho}\left(x\right)dx\right|

+\left\|\mathsf{S}_{1,u_{2}}\left(f-H\right)\right\|_{p,\mathbf{\varrho}}

\leq 6\cdot 3^{\frac{2}{p}}\left[\mathbf{\varrho}\right]_{p}^{1/p}\left\|f-H\right\|_{p,\mathbf{\varrho}}+\xi/2\leq\xi/2+\xi/2=\xi

As a result
F_{f}
is bounded, uniformly continuous function defined on
\boldsymbol{R}.

(b) Let
0\leq f,g\in L^{p}\left(\mathbf{\varrho}dx\right)
. If
\left\|f\right\|_{p,\varrho}=\left\|g\right\|_{p,\varrho}=0
, then, results (
2.6
) is obvious. So we assume that
\left\|f\right\|_{p,\varrho},\left\|g\right\|_{p,\varrho}\in\left(0,+\infty\right)

\displaystyle\left\|F_{f}\right\|_{\mathcal{C}\left(\mathbf{R}\right)}

\displaystyle\leq c_{1}\left\|F_{g}\right\|_{\mathcal{C}\left(\mathbf{R}\right)}=c_{1}\left\|\int\nolimits_{\mathbf{R}}\mathsf{S}_{1,u}(g)\left(x\right)\left|G\left(x\right)\right|\mathbf{\varrho}\left(x\right)dx\right\|_{\mathcal{C}\left(\mathbf{R}\right)}

\displaystyle=c_{1}\sup_{u\in\mathbf{R}}\int\nolimits_{\mathbf{R}}\mathsf{S}_{1,u}(g)\left(x\right)\left|G\left(x\right)\right|\mathbf{\varrho}\left(x\right)dx

\displaystyle=c_{1}\sup_{u\in\mathbf{R}}\left\|\mathsf{S}_{1,u}(g)\right\|_{p,\mathbf{\varrho}}\left\|G\right\|_{p^{\prime},\mathbf{\varrho}}\leq 3c_{1}3^{\frac{2}{p}}\left[\mathbf{\varrho}\right]_{p}^{1/p}\left\|g\right\|_{p,\varrho}.

On the other hand, for any

one can find

\displaystyle\left\|F_{f}\right\|_{\mathcal{C}\left(\mathbf{R}\right)}

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\left|F_{f}\left(0\right)\right|\geq\int\nolimits_{\mathbf{R}}\mathsf{S}_{1,0}f\left(x\right)\left|G\left(x\right)\right|\mathbf{\varrho}\left(x\right)dx

In the general case

Then (

f,g\in L_{p}\left(\mathbf{R},\mathbf{\varrho}\right)
and
\delta>0,
the following properties are hold:

(1)

\Omega_{r}\left(f,\delta\right)_{p,\mathbf{\varrho}}
is
non-negative; non-decreasing function of
\delta
;

(2)

\Omega_{r}(f,\delta)_{p,\mathbf{\varrho}}
is sub-additive of
f
;

(3)

(3.7)

\lim\limits_{\delta\rightarrow 0}K_{r}\left(f,\delta,p,\mathbf{\varrho}\right)=0.

As a result

(3.8)

\lim\limits_{\delta\rightarrow 0}\Omega_{r}(f,\delta)_{p,\mathbf{\varrho}}=0.

Proof of Theorem
3.10
.

Properties (1) and (2) are clear from definition. Since

\lim\limits_{\delta\rightarrow 0}K_{r}\left(f,\delta,p,\mathbf{\varrho}\right)=0,

we have, from Theorem
3.9
, that (
2.1
) holds.

3.3.
Jackson type inequality

Theorem 3.11
.

Let
p\in[1,\infty)
,
\mathbf{\varrho\in}A_{\mathbf{p}}
,
r\in\mathbb{N}
,
\sigma>0
and
f\in L^{p}\left(\mathbf{\varrho}dx\right)
. Then,

(3.9)

A_{\sigma}\left(f\right)_{p,\mathbf{\varrho}}\leq 25\pi 8^{r-1}\mathbb{C}_{2}\mathbb{C}_{1}\left\|\left(I-T_{1/\sigma}\right)^{r}f\right\|_{p,\mathbf{\varrho}}.

Proof of Theorem
3.11
.

First we obtain

(3.10)

A_{2\sigma}\left(f\right)_{p,\mathbf{\varrho}}\leq 25\pi 8^{r-1}\mathbb{C}_{2}\mathbb{C}_{1}\left\|\left(I-T_{1/\left(2\sigma\right)}\right)^{r}f\right\|_{p,\mathbf{\varrho}}

and (
3.9
) follows from (
3.10
). Let us take
g_{\sigma}\in\mathcal{G}_{\sigma}\left(\infty\right)
with
\left\|F_{f}-g_{\sigma}\right\|_{\mathcal{C}(\mathbf{R})}=A_{\sigma}\left(F_{f}\right)_{\mathcal{C}(\mathbf{R})}
. Using

V_{\sigma}F_{f}=F_{V_{\sigma}f}

and
V_{\sigma}g_{\sigma}=g_{\sigma}
we get

A_{2\sigma}\left(f\right)_{p,\mathbf{\varrho}}\leq\left\|f-V_{\sigma}f\right\|_{p,\mathbf{\varrho}}\leq\left\|F_{f-V_{\sigma}f}\right\|_{\mathcal{C}(\mathbf{R})}=\left\|F_{f}-V_{\sigma}F_{f}\right\|_{\mathcal{C}(\mathbf{R})}

\leq\left\|F_{f}-g_{\sigma}+g_{\sigma}-V_{\sigma}F_{f}\right\|_{\mathcal{C}(\mathbf{R})}=\left\|F_{f}-g_{\sigma}+V_{\sigma}g_{\sigma}-V_{\sigma}F_{f}\right\|_{\mathcal{C}(\mathbf{R})}

\leq A_{\sigma}\left(F_{f}\right)_{\mathcal{C}(\mathbf{R})}+\frac{3}{2}A_{\sigma}\left(F_{f}\right)_{\mathcal{C}(\mathbf{R})}=\frac{5}{2}A_{\sigma}\left(F_{f}\right)_{\mathcal{C}(\mathbf{R})}.

For any
g\in W_{\infty}^{r}

A_{\sigma}\left(u\right)_{\mathcal{C}(\mathbf{R})}\leq A_{\sigma}\left(u-g\right)_{\mathcal{C}(\mathbf{R})}+A_{\sigma}\left(g\right)_{\mathcal{C}(\mathbf{R})}

\leq\left\|u-g\right\|_{\mathcal{C}(\mathbf{R})}+\frac{5\pi}{4}\frac{4^{r}}{\sigma^{r}}\left\|\frac{d^{r}}{dx^{r}}g\right\|_{\mathcal{C}(\mathbf{R})}

\leq\frac{5\pi 4^{r}}{4}K_{r}\left(u,\sigma^{-1},C\right)\leq\frac{5\pi 8^{r}}{4}K_{r}\left(u,\frac{1}{2\sigma},\mathcal{C}(\mathbf{R})\right)_{\mathcal{C}(\mathbf{R})}

\leq\frac{5\pi 8^{r}}{4}\mathbb{C}_{2}\left\|\left(I-T_{\left(2\sigma\right)^{-1}}\right)^{r}u\right\|_{\mathcal{C}(\mathbf{R})}.

where
\mathbb{C}_{2}:=\mathbb{C}_{2}\left(1\right)=1
and
\mathbb{C}_{2}\left(r\right)=2^{r}\left(r^{r}+(34)^{r}\right)
for
r>1
(see
Theorem 2.5 of
[
8
]
). Therefore

A_{2\sigma}\left(f\right)_{p,\mathbf{\varrho}}\leq\frac{5}{2}A_{\sigma}\left(F_{f}\right)_{\mathcal{C}(\mathbf{R})}\leq 25\pi 8^{r-1}\mathbb{C}_{2}\left\|\left(I-T_{\frac{1}{2\sigma}}\right)^{r}F_{f}\right\|_{\mathcal{C}(\mathbf{R})}

=25\pi 8^{r-1}\mathbb{C}_{2}\left\|F_{\left(I-T_{1/\left(2\sigma\right)}\right)^{r}f}\right\|_{\mathcal{C}(\mathbf{R})}\leq 25\pi 8^{r-1}\mathbb{C}_{2}\mathbb{C}_{1}\left\|\left(I-T_{1/\left(2\sigma\right)}\right)^{r}f\right\|_{p,\mathbf{\varrho}}.

3.4.
Inverse theorem

Theorem 3.12
.

Let
p\in[1,\infty)
,
\mathbf{\varrho\in}A_{\mathbf{p}}
,
r\in\mathbb{N}
,
\delta\in\left(0,\infty\right)
and
f\in L^{p}\left(\mathbf{\varrho}dx\right)
. Then,

\Omega_{r}\left(f,\delta\right)_{p,\mathbf{\varrho}}\leq\mathbb{C}_{3}\delta^{r}\left(A_{0}\left(f\right)_{p,\mathbf{\varrho}}+\int\nolimits_{1/2}^{1/\delta}u^{r-1}A_{u/2}\left(f\right)_{p,\mathbf{\varrho}}du\right)

holds with
\mathbb{C}_{3}
:=
\mathbb{C}_{1}\left(1+3\mathbb{C}_{1}\right)2^{r+1}\left(1+2^{2r-1}\right)
.

Proof of Theorem
3.12
.

\Omega_{r}\left(f,\delta\right)_{p,\mathbf{\varrho}}=\left\|\left(I-T_{\delta}\right)^{r}f\right\|_{p,\mathbf{\varrho}}\leq\left\|F_{\left(I-T_{\delta}\right)^{r}f}\right\|_{\mathcal{C}(\mathbf{R})}=\left\|\left(I-T_{\delta}\right)^{r}F_{f}\right\|_{\mathcal{C}(\mathbf{R})}

\leq 2^{r}\left(1+2^{2r-1}\right)\delta^{r}\left(A_{0}\left(\Xi_{f}\right)_{\mathcal{C}(\mathbf{R})}+\int_{1/2}^{1/\delta}u^{r-1}A_{u}\left(F_{f}\right)_{\mathcal{C}(\mathbf{R})}du\right)

\leq\mathbb{C}_{1}\left(1+3\mathbb{C}_{1}\right)2^{r}\left(1+2^{2r-1}\right)\delta^{r}\left(A_{0}\left(f\right)_{p,\mathbf{\varrho}}+\int_{1/2}^{1/\delta}u^{r-1}A_{u/2}\left(f\right)_{p,\mathbf{\varrho}}du\right)

because

A_{2\sigma}\left(F_{f}\right)_{\mathcal{C}(\mathbf{R})}\leq\left\|F_{f}-V_{\sigma}F_{f}\right\|_{\mathcal{C}(\mathbf{R})}=\left\|F_{f-V_{\sigma}f}\right\|_{\mathcal{C}(\mathbf{R})}

\leq\mathbb{C}_{1}\left\|f-V_{\sigma}f\right\|_{p,\mathbf{\varrho}}=\mathbb{C}_{1}\left\|f-g_{\sigma}+g_{\sigma}-V_{\sigma}f\right\|_{p,\mathbf{\varrho}}

\leq\mathbb{C}_{1}\left(\left\|f-g_{\sigma}\right\|_{p,\mathbf{\varrho}}+\left\|V_{\sigma}g_{\sigma}-V_{\sigma}f\right\|_{p,\mathbf{\varrho}}\right)

\leq\mathbb{C}_{1}\left(\left\|f-g_{\sigma}\right\|_{p,\mathbf{\varrho}}+3\mathbb{C}_{1}\left\|g_{\sigma}-f\right\|_{p,\mathbf{\varrho}}\right)=\mathbb{C}_{1}\left(1+3\mathbb{C}_{1}\right)A_{\sigma}\left(f\right)_{{}_{p,\mathbf{\varrho}}}.

3.5.
Marchaud inequality

Theorem 3.13
.

Let
r,k\in\mathbb{N}
,
1\leq p<\infty
,
\mathbf{\varrho\in}A_{\mathbf{p}}
,
f\in L^{p}\left(\mathbf{\varrho}dx\right)
and
t\in\left(0,1/2\right)
. Then,

\Omega_{r}\left(f,t\right)_{p,\mathbf{\varrho}}\leq\mathbb{C}_{4}t^{r}\int\nolimits_{t}^{1}\frac{\Omega_{r\text{+}k}\left(f,u\right)_{p,\mathbf{\varrho}}}{u^{r+1}}du

holds with
\mathbb{C}_{4}
:=
20\pi\mathbb{C}_{1}\left(1+2^{2r-1}\right)2_{\;}^{2r+3k}\mathbb{C}_{2}\left(r+k\right)
where
\mathbb{C}_{2}\left(1\right):=36
, and
\mathbb{C}_{2}\left(r\right):=2^{r}\left(r^{r}+(34)^{r}\right)
for
r>1
.

Proof of Theorem
3.13
.

Let
\sigma>0
and
g_{\sigma}
be an exponential type entire function of
degree
\leq\sigma
, belonging to
L^{p}\left(\mathbf{\varrho}dx\right)
, as best approximation of
f\in L^{p}\left(\mathbf{\varrho}dx\right)
.
Then

\Omega_{r}\left(f,t\right)_{p,\mathbf{\varrho}}=\left\|\left(I-T_{t}\right)^{r}f\right\|_{p,\mathbf{\varrho}}\leq\left\|F_{\left(I-T_{t}\right)^{r}f}\right\|_{\mathcal{C}(\mathbf{R})}=\left\|\left(I-T_{t}\right)^{r}F_{f}\right\|_{\mathcal{C}(\mathbf{R})}

\leq(\mathbb{C}_{4}\mathbb{C}_{1})t^{r}\int_{t}^{1}\frac{\left\|\left(I-T_{t}\right)^{r+k}F_{f}\right\|_{\mathcal{C}(\mathbf{R})}}{u^{r+1}}du

=(\mathbb{C}_{4}\mathbb{C}_{1})t^{r}\int_{t}^{1}\frac{\left\|F_{\left(I-T_{t}\right)^{r+k}f}\right\|_{\mathcal{C}(\mathbf{R})}}{u^{r+1}}du

\leq\mathbb{C}_{4}t^{r}\int_{t}^{1}\frac{\left\|\left(I\text{-}T_{t}\right)^{r+k}f\right\|_{p,\mathbf{\varrho}}}{u^{r+1}}du

=\mathbb{C}_{4}t^{r}\int_{t}^{1}\frac{\Omega_{r+k}\left(f,t\right)_{p,\mathbf{\varrho}}}{u^{r+1}}du.
