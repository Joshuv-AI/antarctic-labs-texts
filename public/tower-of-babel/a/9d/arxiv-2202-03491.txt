Scalar Bound States of D ∗ ​ D ¯ ∗ D^{\ast}\bar{D}^{\ast} and B ∗ ​ B ¯ ∗ B^{\ast}\bar{B}^{\ast} in the Bethe-Salpeter Formalism
Rui-Cheng LI

Abstract
We study the scalar bound states of D ∗ ​ D ¯ ∗ D^{\ast}\bar{D}^{\ast} and B ∗ ​ B ¯ ∗ B^{\ast}\bar{B}^{\ast} in the Bethe-Salpeter formalism, with the effective interaction kernel extracted from the chiral perturbative theory and the heavy quark effective theory in the ladder approximation and the covariant instantaneous approximation. The results show that, in the scalar case ( J = 0 J=0 ), there can only exist I = 0 I=0 bound states for parameters in proper range, while there cannot exist the I = 1 I=1 bound states in the whole reasonable parameter range, due to more constraints arising from our definition of the Bethe-Salpeter wavefunction. PACS numbers 11.10.St, 12.39.Fe, 12.40.Yx, 13.20.Jf, 13.30.Eg Key words hadronic molecular states, Bethe-Salpeter equation, chiral perturbative theory, heavy quark effective theory

2.1
The isospin multiplets constituted by

For the single-charmed mesons
\{D^{\ast}\}
and their antiparticles
\{\widetilde{D}^{\ast}\}
,
if we take the notations for the particles

D^{\ast}_{1}=D^{\ast+},\widetilde{D}^{\ast}_{1}=D^{\ast-},\,D^{\ast}_{2}=D^{\ast 0},\widetilde{D}^{\ast}_{2}=\bar{D}^{\ast 0},

(1)

and

\displaystyle D^{\ast\mu}_{i}(x)

\displaystyle\int{d^{3}p\over(2\pi)^{3}}{1\over\sqrt{2E_{\bf p}}}\sum^{3}_{r=1}\left[a_{i}({\bf p},r)\varepsilon_{i}^{\mu}({\bf p},r)e^{-ip\cdot x}+a_{i}^{{\dagger}}({\bf p},r)\varepsilon_{i}^{\mu\,*}({\bf p},r)e^{ip\cdot x}\right],

\displaystyle\widetilde{D}^{\ast\mu}_{i}(x)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int{d^{3}p\over(2\pi)^{3}}{1\over\sqrt{2E_{\bf p}}}\sum^{3}_{r=1}\left[b_{i}({\bf p},r)\xi_{i}^{\mu}({\bf p},r)e^{-ip\cdot x}+b_{i}^{{\dagger}}({\bf p},r)\xi_{i}^{\mu\,*}({\bf p},r)e^{ip\cdot x}\right],\,i=1,2,

there are two isospin doublets
(we choose the convention
on the isospin multiplets the same as that in Ref.
[
12
]
):

\displaystyle\widetilde{D}^{\ast}

\displaystyle\equiv

\displaystyle(\widetilde{D}^{\ast}_{1},\widetilde{D}^{\ast}_{2})^{T},

(3)

\displaystyle D^{\ast}

\displaystyle\equiv

\displaystyle(D^{\ast}_{1},-D^{\ast}_{2})^{T}.

(4)

with the superscript
T

Note, once the doublet in (
3

4
) would automatically arise, which is independent on the details of
\{D^{\ast}\}
and
\{\widetilde{D}^{\ast}\}

Consequently, the isospin quantum numbers of
D^{\ast}\widetilde{D}^{\ast}

0

\displaystyle|P\rangle_{0}={1\over\sqrt{2}}\left|D^{\ast+}D^{\ast-}+D^{\ast 0}\bar{D}^{\ast 0}\right\rangle\,,

(5)

\displaystyle|P\rangle_{1,0}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle{1\over\sqrt{2}}\left|D^{\ast+}D^{\ast-}-D^{\ast 0}\bar{D}^{\ast 0}\right\rangle,

\displaystyle|P\rangle_{1,+1}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left|D^{\ast+}\bar{D}^{\ast 0}\right\rangle,

\displaystyle|P\rangle_{1,-1}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\left|D^{\ast 0}D^{\ast-}\right\rangle.

(6)

In the following of this paper,
it could be out of doubt that, the notation
D^{\ast}_{i}

\widetilde{D}^{\ast}_{i}

D^{\ast\mu}_{i}(x)
and
\widetilde{D}^{\ast\mu}_{i}(x)

[D^{\ast\mu}_{i}(x)]^{\dagger}=D^{\ast\mu}_{i}(x)

Now, we can define a class of hadronic matrix elements, i.e., the so-called Bethe-Salpeter (BS) wave functions,
as

\displaystyle\chi^{I=0,I_{3}=0}_{P}(x_{1},x_{2})

\displaystyle\equiv

\displaystyle\langle\Omega|{\rm T}\,\{{1\over\sqrt{2}}[D^{\ast}_{1}(x_{1})\widetilde{D}^{\ast}_{1}(x_{2})+D^{\ast}_{2}(x_{1})\widetilde{D}^{\ast}_{2}(x_{2})]\}|P\rangle_{0,0},

\displaystyle\chi^{I=1,I_{3}=0}_{P}(x_{1},x_{2})

\displaystyle\equiv

\displaystyle\langle\Omega|{\rm T}\,\{{1\over\sqrt{2}}[D^{\ast}_{1}(x_{1})\widetilde{D}^{\ast}_{1}(x_{2})-D^{\ast}_{2}(x_{1})\widetilde{D}^{\ast}_{2}(x_{2})]\}|P\rangle_{1,0},

\displaystyle\chi^{I=1,I_{3}=+1}_{P}(x_{1},x_{2})

\displaystyle\equiv

\displaystyle\langle\Omega|{\rm T}\,\{D^{\ast}_{1}(x_{1})\widetilde{D}^{\ast}_{2}(x_{2})\}|P\rangle_{1,+1},

\displaystyle\chi^{I=1,I_{3}=-1}_{P}(x_{1},x_{2})

\displaystyle\equiv

\displaystyle\langle\Omega|{\rm T}\,\{D^{\ast}_{2}(x_{1})\widetilde{D}^{\ast}_{1}(x_{2})\}|P\rangle_{1,-1},

(7)

where
\langle\Omega|
is the vacuum in the interaction picture. By recalling the fields
D^{\ast\mu}_{i}(x)

\widetilde{D}^{\ast\mu}_{i}(x)

\displaystyle\langle\Omega|{\rm T}\,\{D^{\ast}_{i}(x_{1})\widetilde{D}^{\ast}_{j}(x_{2})\}|D^{\ast}_{i}\widetilde{D}^{\ast}_{j}\rangle_{I,I_{3}}

(8)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\langle\Omega|{\rm T}\,\{D^{\ast}_{i}(x_{1})\widetilde{D}^{\ast{\dagger}}_{j}(x_{2})\}|D^{\ast}_{i}\widetilde{D}^{\ast}_{j}\rangle_{I,I_{3}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\langle\Omega|{\rm T}\,\{D^{\ast{\dagger}}_{i}(x_{1})\widetilde{D}^{\ast}_{j}(x_{2})\}|D^{\ast}_{i}\widetilde{D}^{\ast}_{j}\rangle_{I,I_{3}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\langle\Omega|{\rm T}\,\{D^{\ast{\dagger}}_{i}(x_{1})\widetilde{D}^{\ast{\dagger}}_{j}(x_{2})\}|D^{\ast}_{i}\widetilde{D}^{\ast}_{j}\rangle_{I,I_{3}}.

\displaystyle\langle\Omega|{\rm T}\,\{D^{\ast({\dagger})}_{i}(x_{1})D^{\ast({\dagger})}_{j}(x_{2})\}|D^{\ast}_{i}\widetilde{D}^{\ast}_{j}\rangle_{I,I_{3}}

\displaystyle\langle\Omega|{\rm T}\,\{\widetilde{D}^{\ast({\dagger})}_{i}(x_{1})\widetilde{D}^{\ast({\dagger})}_{j}(x_{2})\}|D^{\ast}_{i}\widetilde{D}^{\ast}_{j}\rangle_{I,I_{3}}=0,

\displaystyle\chi^{I=1,I_{3}=+1}_{P}(x_{1},x_{2})=\chi^{I=1,I_{3}=0}_{P}(x_{1},x_{2}),\quad\mbox{etc.}.

In this work,

8
Summary

We study the scalar bound states of
D^{\ast}\bar{D}^{\ast}
and
B^{\ast}\bar{B}^{\ast}
in the
Bethe-Salpeter (BS) formalism,
with the effective interaction kernel
extracted
from the chiral perturbative theory (ChPT) and the heavy quark effective theory (HQET)
in the ladder approximation and the covariant instantaneous approximation.
The results show that, in the scalar case (
J=0
), there can only exist
I=0
bound states for parameters in proper range,
while there cannot exist the
