Lemma 2

(5.13)

\begin{split}&\lim_{\tau\to\infty}\frac{1}{\tau}\left\{\int_{\frac{x}{1-c}\tau}^{\frac{x}{1-c}\tau+1}\ln\Gamma(t){\rm d}t-\int_{\varphi_{1}(\frac{x}{1-c}\tau)}^{\varphi_{1}(\frac{x}{1-c}\tau)+1}\ln\Gamma(t){\rm d}t\right\}=x,\ x>0.\end{split}

Next, by making use of the substitution

x\to\frac{x^{n}+y^{n}}{z^{n}},\ x,y,z,n\in\mathbb{N},\ n\geq 3

Lemma 3
.

It is true that

(5.15)

\begin{split}&\lim_{\tau\to\infty}\frac{1}{\tau}\left\{\int_{\frac{x^{n}+y^{n}}{z^{n}}\frac{\tau}{1-c}}^{\frac{x^{n}+y^{n}}{z^{n}}\frac{\tau}{1-c}+1}\ln\Gamma(t){\rm d}t-\int_{\varphi_{1}(\frac{x^{n}+y^{n}}{z^{n}}\frac{\tau}{1-c})}^{\varphi_{1}(\frac{x^{n}+y^{n}}{z^{n}}\frac{\tau}{1-c})+1}\ln\Gamma(t){\rm d}t\right\}=\\
&\frac{x^{n}+y^{n}}{z^{n}}\end{split}

for every fixed Fermat’s rational

\frac{x^{n}+y^{n}}{z^{n}}.

Finally, the following result holds true.

Theorem 2
.

The condition

(5.16)

\begin{split}&\lim_{\tau\to\infty}\frac{1}{\tau}\left\{\int_{\frac{x^{n}+y^{n}}{z^{n}}\frac{\tau}{1-c}}^{\frac{x^{n}+y^{n}}{z^{n}}\frac{\tau}{1-c}+1}\ln\Gamma(t){\rm d}t-\int_{\varphi_{1}(\frac{x^{n}+y^{n}}{z^{n}}\frac{\tau}{1-c})}^{\varphi_{1}(\frac{x^{n}+y^{n}}{z^{n}}\frac{\tau}{1-c})+1}\ln\Gamma(t){\rm d}t\right\}\not=1\end{split}

on the class of all Fermat’s rationals represent the thirteenth equivalent to the Fermat-Wiles theorem.
