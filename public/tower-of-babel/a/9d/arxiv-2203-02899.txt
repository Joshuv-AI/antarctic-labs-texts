Two dynamical generated a 0 a_{0} resonances by interactions between vector mesons
Zheng-Li Wang, Bing-Song Zou, [2mm] CAS Key Laboratory of Theoretical Physics, Institute of Theoretical Physics,Chinese Academy of Sciences, Beijing 100190,China

Abstract
We study dynamically generated a 0 a_{0} resonances by interactions between vector mesons including their coupling to channels of pseudoscalar mesons within coupled-channel approach. Both vector and pseudoscalar mesons are considered as t t -channel exchanged mesons for calculating interactions between vector mesons. Analogous to a 0 ​ ( 980 ) a_{0}(980) as a K ​ K ¯ − π ​ η K\bar{K}-\pi\eta dynamically generated state, there is an a 0 ​ ( 1710 ) a_{0}(1710) as a coupled channel dynamically generated state near K ∗ ​ K ¯ ∗ K^{*}\bar{K}^{*} threshold. The channels involved are ρ ​ ϕ , K ∗ ​ K ¯ ∗ , ρ ​ ω , K ​ K ¯ , π ​ η \rho\phi,K^{*}\bar{K}^{*},\rho\omega,K\bar{K},\pi\eta . This a 0 a_{0} mainly decays to ρ ​ ω \rho\omega , π ​ η \pi\eta and K ​ K ¯ K\bar{K} . This pole is much tied to the coupled channel effect. If we turn off either ρ ​ ϕ \rho\phi or K ∗ ​ K ¯ ∗ K^{*}\bar{K}^{*} , the pole disappears. In addition, it is found that a 0 ​ ( 1450 ) a_{0}(1450) may also be dynamically generated by ρ ​ ω \rho\omega interactions due to π \pi and η \eta exchange.

The basic equation to obtain the unitarized
T
-matrix is

T^{(JI)}(s)=\left[1-V^{(JI)}(s)\cdot G(s)\right]^{-1}\cdot V^{(JI)}(s).

(15)

Here

denotes the partial-wave amplitudes and

with

and

the masses of the particles in the
i
-th channel. The pole

\text{Det}\equiv\text{det}\left[1-V^{(JI)}(s)\cdot G(s)\right].

(17)

The above loop function is logarithmically divergent and can be calculated with a

f_{\Lambda}(q)

g_{i}(s)=i\int\frac{\mathrm{d}^{4}q}{(2\pi)^{4}}\frac{f^{2}_{\Lambda}(q)}{(q^{2}-m^{2}_{i1}+i\epsilon)((P-q)^{2}-m^{2}_{i2}+i\epsilon)}.

(18)

after the

g_{i}(s)=\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{|\mathbf{q}|^{2}\mathrm{d}|\mathbf{q}|}{(2\pi)^{2}}\frac{\omega_{i1}+\omega_{i2}}{\omega_{i1}\omega_{i2}(s-(\omega_{i1}+\omega_{i2})^{2}+\mathrm{i}\epsilon)}f_{\Lambda}^{2}(|\mathbf{q}|).

(19)

where
\mathbf{q}
is the three-momentum and

\omega_{i1,2}=\sqrt{\mathbf{q}^{2}+m^{2}_{i1,2}}
. In order to proceed we need

and smooth cutoff, typically:

f_{\Lambda}(\mathbf{q})=\left\{\begin{array}[]{l}\Theta(\Lambda^{2}-\mathbf{q}^{2})\\
\exp\left[-\frac{\mathbf{q}^{2}}{\Lambda^{2}}\right]\\
\end{array}\right.

(20)

[
15
]
, the same sharp cutoff is used in this paper when channels with pseudoscalar mesons are included in addition. To explore the
position of the poles we need to take into account

q_{i}^{\text{on}>}=\left\{\begin{array}[]{cl}-q_{i}^{\text{on}}&\text{if }\text{Im}q_{i}^{\text{on}}<0\\

\end{array}\right.

(22)

And the analytic continuation to the second Riemann sheet is given by

g^{(2)}_{i}(s)=g_{i}(s)+\frac{i}{4\pi}\frac{q_{i}^{\text{on}>}}{\sqrt{s}}.

(23)
