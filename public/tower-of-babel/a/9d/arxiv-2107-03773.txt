Photoproduction of possible pentaquark states Λ b 0 ​ ( 5912 ) \Lambda^{0}_{b}(5912) and Λ b 0 ​ ( 5920 ) \Lambda^{0}_{b}(5920) in the γ ​ p → Λ b 0 ​ ( ∗ ) ​ B + \gamma{}p\to{}\Lambda_{b}^{0(*)}B^{+} reactions
Yin Huang 1, Hong Qiang Zhu 2

Abstract
In this work, we report on a theoretical study of possible pentaquark states Λ b 0 ​ ( 5912 ) \Lambda^{0}_{b}(5912) and Λ b 0 ​ ( 5920 ) \Lambda^{0}_{b}(5920) in the γ ​ p → Λ b 0 ​ ( ∗ ) ​ B + \gamma{}p\to{}\Lambda_{b}^{0(*)}B^{+} reactions within an effective Lagrangian approach. In addition to the contributions from the s s -channel nucleon pole and t t -channel B ¯ ∗ ⁣ − \bar{B}^{{*}-} exchange, the contact term contribution are also included. Our theoretical approach is based on the chiral unitary theory where the Λ b 0 ​ ( 5912 ) \Lambda^{0}_{b}(5912) and Λ b 0 ​ ( 5920 ) \Lambda^{0}_{b}(5920) resonances are dynamically generated. Within the coupling constants of the Λ b 0 ​ ( 5912 ) \Lambda^{0}_{b}(5912) and Λ b 0 ​ ( 5920 ) \Lambda^{0}_{b}(5920) to B ¯ ​ p \bar{B}p and B ¯ ∗ ​ p \bar{B}^{*}p channels obtained from chiral unitary theory, the total and differential cross sections of the γ ​ p → Λ b 0 ​ ( ∗ ) ​ B + \gamma{}p\to{}\Lambda_{b}^{0(*)}B^{+} are evaluated. Our calculation indicates that the cross section for γ ​ p → Λ b 0 ​ ( 5912 ) ​ B + \gamma{}p\to{}\Lambda_{b}^{0}(5912)B^{+} and γ ​ p → Λ b 0 ​ ( 5920 ) ​ B + \gamma{}p\to{}\Lambda_{b}^{0}(5920)B^{+} reactions are of the order of 0.0164 nb and 0.00527 nb,respectively. If measured in future experiments, such as Electron-Ion Collider in China (EicC) or US(US-EIC), the predicted total cross sections and specific features of the angular distributions can be used to test the (molecular) nature of the Λ b 0 ​ ( 5912 ) \Lambda^{0}_{b}(5912) and Λ b 0 ​ ( 5920 ) \Lambda^{0}_{b}(5920) that they may be pentaquark states.

Thanks to the experimental progress in the sector of heavy baryons in the past decade. Many heavy baryons that can not be ascribed into
3-quark(qqq) configurations have been reported

\Xi_{b}^{-}\to{}J/\psi{}\Lambda{}K^{-}

These findings intrigue an active discussion on the structure of these states. A classical way to describe these states is treating

Investigating the pentaquark states has been a long history. Even before the quark model was proposed by Gell-Mann and Zweig,

Guo:2017jvc

Although people interpret some states as the pentaquark states, other possible explanations such as the three quark state
(as long as quantum numbers allow, it might well be the case) can not be fully excluded. The possible reason is that the quark pair
creation model still suffers from relatively large uncertainties

We take the state

Being considered as a traditional bottom baryon with

Aaij:2019vzc
;

However, in addition to finding the four hidden-charm pentaquark states in LHCb experiment

;
Aaij:2020gdg
, there is no
significant discovery have been made in searching for pentaquark spectrum like
P_{c}
or

In fact, the hidden-charm pentaquarks

In order to computing the diagrams shown in Fig.
1

effective Lagrangian densities for the relevant interaction
vertices. As mentioned in the chiral unitary approach of Refs.
Romanets:2013cqa
;
Lu:2014ina
;
Liang:2014eba
;

,
the
\Lambda^{0}_{b}(5912)

\Lambda^{0}_{b}(5920)

s
-wave
meson-baryon molecules that
\Lambda^{0}_{b}(5912)
include big
N\bar{B}
and
N\bar{B}^{*}
components and
\Lambda^{0}_{b}(5920)

possess big

\Lambda^{0}_{b}N\bar{B}
,
\Lambda^{0}_{b}N\bar{B}^{*}
, and
\Lambda^{*0}_{b}N\bar{B}^{*}

couplings, we can write down the Lagrangian densities as

\displaystyle{\cal{L}}_{\Lambda^{0}_{b}N\bar{B}}

\displaystyle=g_{\Lambda^{0}_{b}N\bar{B}}\bar{\Lambda}_{b}^{0}N\bar{B},

(1)

\displaystyle{\cal{L}}_{\Lambda^{0}_{b}N\bar{B}^{*}}

\displaystyle=g_{\Lambda^{0}_{b}N\bar{B}^{*}}\bar{\Lambda}_{b}^{0}\gamma^{\mu}N\bar{B}^{*}_{\mu},

(2)

\displaystyle{\cal{L}}_{\Lambda^{0*}_{b}N\bar{B}^{*}}

\displaystyle=g_{\Lambda^{*0}_{b}N\bar{B}^{*}}\bar{\Lambda}_{b}^{*0\mu}N\bar{B}^{*}_{\mu},

(3)

The coupling constants in the above Lagrangians were determined in Ref.
GarciaRecio:2012db
in a
hadronic molecular picture with
g_{\Lambda^{0}_{b}N\bar{B}}=4.6
,
g_{\Lambda^{0}_{b}N\bar{B}^{*}}=3.0
, and
g_{\Lambda^{*0}_{b}N\bar{B}^{*}}=5.7
.

To compute the amplitudes of the diagrams shown in Fig.
1
,the effective Lagrangian densities related
to the photon fields are required, which are
Xiao:2017uve

\displaystyle{\cal{L}}_{\gamma{}pp}

\displaystyle=-e\bar{p}(\gamma^{\mu}-\frac{\kappa_{p}}{2m_{p}}\sigma^{\mu\nu}\partial_{\nu})A^{\mu}p,

(4)

\displaystyle{\cal{L}}_{\gamma{}B^{*}\bar{B}}

\displaystyle=\frac{g_{\gamma{}B^{*+}B^{+}}}{4}e\epsilon^{\mu\nu\alpha\beta}F_{\mu\nu}B^{*+}_{\alpha\beta}B^{-}

\displaystyle+\frac{g_{\gamma{}B^{*0}B^{0}}}{4}e\epsilon^{\mu\nu\alpha\beta}F_{\mu\nu}B^{*0}_{\alpha\beta}\bar{B}^{0}+H.c.,

(5)

\displaystyle{\cal{L}}_{\gamma{}B^{-}B^{+}}

\displaystyle=ieA_{\mu}(B^{-}\partial^{\mu}B^{+}-B^{+}\partial^{\mu}B^{-}),

(6)

where the strength tensor is defined as
\sigma^{\mu\nu}=\frac{i}{2}(\gamma^{\mu}\gamma^{\nu}-\gamma^{\nu}\gamma^{\mu})
,
F_{\mu\nu}=\partial_{\mu}A_{\nu}-\partial_{\nu}A_{\mu}
and
B^{*}_{\alpha\beta}=\partial_{\alpha}B^{*}_{\beta}-\partial_{\beta}B^{*}_{\alpha}
.
The anomalous magnetic momentum
\kappa_{p}=1.79
and the
\alpha=e^{2}/4\pi=1/137
is the electromagnetic fine structure constant.
The coupling constant
g_{\gamma{}B^{*+}B^{+}}
and
g_{\gamma{}B^{*0}B^{0}}
are determined from the partial decay
width of
B^{*+}\to{}B^{+}\gamma
and
B^{*0}\to{}B^{0}\gamma
, which is obtained from Eq. (
5
),

\displaystyle\Gamma(B^{*+}\to{}B^{+}\gamma)

\displaystyle=\frac{\alpha{}g^{2}_{\gamma{}B^{*+}B^{+}}}{24}m_{B^{*+}}(m^{2}_{B^{*+}}-m^{2}_{B^{+}}),

(7)

\displaystyle\Gamma(B^{*0}\to{}B^{0}\gamma)

\displaystyle=\frac{\alpha{}g^{2}_{\gamma{}B^{*0}B^{0}}}{24}m_{B^{*0}}(m^{2}_{B^{*0}}-m^{2}_{B^{0}}),

(8)

where the
m_{B^{*}}
and
m_{B}
are the mass of
B^{*}
and
B
,respectively. However, the width of the
B^{*}

meson is not well determined experimentally. In the present work, we use the theoretical
predicted partial widths in Ref.
Choi:2007se
and the coupling
constants are determined to be
g_{\gamma{}B^{*+}B^{+}}=1.308
GeV
-1
and
g_{\gamma{}B^{*0}B^{0}}=-0.745
GeV
-1
.

In evaluating the scattering amplitudes of the
\gamma{}p\to{}\Lambda_{b}^{0(*)}B^{+}
reactions, we need to include the
form factors because hadrons are not pointlike particles. For the
t
-channel
B
and
B^{*}
mesons exchange, we
would like to apply a widely used pole form factor, which is

\displaystyle{\cal{F}}_{i}=\frac{\Lambda_{i}^{2}-m_{i}^{2}}{\Lambda_{i}^{2}-q_{i}^{2}}~~~~~~~i=B,B^{*},

(9)

where
\Lambda_{i}=m_{i}+\alpha{}\Lambda_{QCD}
and the QCD energy scale
\Lambda_{QCD}=220
MeV.
The parameter
\alpha
reflects the nonperturbative property of QCD at the low-energy scale,
which will be taken as a parameter and discussed later. For the
s
-channel nucleon pole process,
we adopt a form factor

\displaystyle{\cal{F}}_{N}(q^{2},m^{2}_{N})=\frac{\Lambda_{N}^{4}}{\Lambda_{N}^{4}+(q^{2}-m^{2}_{N})^{2}}

(10)

with
\Lambda_{N}=0.9
GeV, which can be well used to reproduce experimental
data of some reactions

;
Oh:2004wp
.

With the above effective Lagrangian densities, the scattering amplitudes for the

\gamma{}p\to{}\Lambda_{b}^{0}(5912)B^{+}
and
\gamma{}p\to{}\Lambda_{b}^{0}(5920)B^{+}

amplitudes for the
\gamma{}p\to{}\Lambda_{b}^{0}(5912)B^{+}

\displaystyle{\cal{M}}^{B}_{t}

\displaystyle=-ieg_{\Lambda_{b}^{0}N\bar{B}}\bar{u}_{\Lambda_{b}^{0}}(p_{4},s_{4})u_{p}(p_{2},s_{2})\frac{1}{q_{t}^{2}-m^{2}_{\bar{B}}}(p_{3}^{\mu}-q_{t}^{\mu})

\displaystyle\times\epsilon_{\mu}(p_{1},s_{1}){\cal{F}}_{B},

(11)

\displaystyle{\cal{M}}^{B^{*}}_{t}

\displaystyle=i\frac{eg_{\Lambda_{b}^{0}N\bar{B}^{*}}g_{\gamma{}B^{*+}B^{+}}}{4}\epsilon^{\rho\eta\alpha\beta}(p_{1\rho}g_{\eta\lambda}-p_{1\eta}g_{\rho\lambda})

\displaystyle\times(q_{t\alpha}g_{\beta\sigma}-q_{t\beta}g_{\alpha\sigma})\epsilon^{\lambda}(p_{1},s_{1})\frac{-g^{\mu\sigma}+q_{t}^{\mu}q_{t}^{\sigma}/m^{2}_{\bar{B}^{*}}}{q_{t}^{2}-m^{2}_{\bar{B}^{*}}}

\displaystyle\times{}\bar{u}_{\Lambda_{b}^{0}}(p_{4},s_{4})\gamma_{\mu}u_{p}(p_{2},s_{2}){\cal{F}}_{B^{*}},

(12)

\displaystyle{\cal{M}}^{N}_{s}

\displaystyle=-ieg_{\Lambda_{b}^{0}N\bar{B}}\bar{u}_{\Lambda_{b}^{0}}(p_{4},s_{4})\frac{q\!\!\!/_{s}+m_{p}}{q^{2}_{s}-m_{p}^{2}}[\gamma^{\mu}+\frac{\kappa_{p}}{4m_{p}}(\gamma^{\mu}p\!\!\!/_{1}-p\!\!\!/_{1}\gamma^{\mu})]

\displaystyle\times{}u_{p}(p_{2},s_{2})\epsilon_{\mu}(p_{1},s_{1}){\cal{F}}_{N}.

(13)

Then the amplitudes for the

\displaystyle{\cal{M}}^{B^{*}}_{t2}

\displaystyle=i\frac{eg_{\Lambda_{b}^{*0}N\bar{B}^{*}}g_{\gamma{}B^{*+}B^{+}}}{4}\epsilon^{\rho\lambda\alpha\beta}(p_{1\rho}g_{\lambda\eta}-p_{1\lambda}g_{\rho\eta})

\displaystyle\times(q_{t\alpha}g_{\beta\nu}-q_{t\beta}g_{\alpha\nu})\epsilon^{\eta}(p_{1},s_{1})\frac{-g^{\mu\nu}+q_{t}^{\mu}q_{t}^{\nu}/m^{2}_{\bar{B}^{*}}}{q_{t}^{2}-m^{2}_{\bar{B}^{*}}}

1

\displaystyle=ieg_{\Lambda_{b}^{0}N\bar{B}}\bar{u}_{\Lambda_{b}^{0}}(p_{4},s_{4})[{\cal{A}}(\gamma^{\mu}-1)+{\cal{B}}]

IV
SUMMARY

Stimulated by the newly observed pentaquark spectrum like
P_{c}
or
P_{cs}
structure, pentaquark spectroscopy contain
bottom quark is believed to be emerging.
In 2012, the
\Lambda^{0}_{b}(5912)
and
\Lambda^{0}_{b}(5920)
were first observed by the the LHCb Collaboration
as a narrow peak in the
\Lambda_{b}^{0}\pi^{+}\pi^{-}
invariant mass spectrum
Aaij:2012da
. Many works treat them as
pentaquark molecular states
Romanets:2013cqa
;
Lu:2014ina
;
Liang:2014eba
;
GarciaRecio:2012db
with a
\bar{B}^{*}N

component for
\Lambda^{0}_{b}(5920)
and
\bar{B}^{(*)}N
components for
\Lambda^{0}_{b}(5912)
. And they can not
be detected like
P_{c}
or
P_{cs}
through a heavy hadron decay to
\bar{B}^{(*)}N
plus a light meson, since their masses
are just below the
\bar{B}^{(*)}N
threshold.

In this paper, we made a detailed exploration of the nonresonant contribution to the
\gamma{}p\to{}\Lambda_{b}^{0}(5912)B^{+}

and
\gamma{}p\to{}\Lambda_{b}^{0}(5920)B^{+}
, with the aim to find a reasonable estimation of the
\Lambda_{b}^{0}(5912)
and

\Lambda_{b}^{0}(5920)
production rates at relatively high energies, where no data are available up to now. The production
process is described by the
t
-channel
\bar{B}^{(*)-}
exchange,
s
-channel nucleon pole, and contact term. The coupling constants
of the
\Lambda^{0}_{b}(5912)
to
\bar{B}^{(*)}N
and
\Lambda^{0}_{b}(5920)
to
\bar{B}^{*}N
are obtained from chiral unitary
theory
Romanets:2013cqa
;
Lu:2014ina
;
Liang:2014eba
;
GarciaRecio:2012db
, where
\Lambda^{0}_{b}(5912)
and
\Lambda^{0}_{b}(5920)

are dynamically generated.

Our calculation indicates that the cross section for
\gamma{}p\to{}\Lambda_{b}^{0}(5912)B^{+}
and
\gamma{}p\to{}\Lambda_{b}^{0}(5920)B^{+}

reactions can reach 0.0164 nb and 0.00527 nb, respectively, are too small to be observed due to out of reach of the current luminosity design

(2-4)\times{}10^{33}cm^{-2}s^{-1}
of EicC
Anderle:2021wcy
. If the luminosity of EicC increases at least one order of magnitude,
these states would be hopefully detected in photoproduction reaction. For the proposed Electron-Ion Collider in US (US-EIC) with the
luminosity of
10^{34}cm^{-2}s^{-1}
or higher, it would be possible to observe these states
Accardi:2012qut
. Moreover, the
differential cross sections computed also can be used to test the molecular picture of the
\Lambda^{0}_{b}(5912)
and
\Lambda^{0}_{b}(5920)
.
