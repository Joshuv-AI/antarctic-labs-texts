Eigenvalues of Symmetric Non-normalized Discrete Trigonometric Transforms
Ali Bagheri Bardi, Miloš Daković, Taher Yazdanpanah, Ljubiša Stanković

Abstract
A comprehensive approach to the spectrum characterization (derivation of eigenvalues and the corresponding multiplicities) for non-normalized, symmetric discrete trigonometric transforms (DTT) is presented in the paper. Eight types of the DTT are analyzed. New explicit analytic expressions for the eigenvalues, together with their multiplicities, for the cases of three DTT (DCT (1) , DCT (5) , and DST (8) ), are the main contribution of this paper. Moreover, the presented theory is supplemented by new, original derivations for the closed-form expressions of the square and the trace of analyzed DTT matrices.

III
Square of transformation matrix

In order to compute entries
t_{kl}
of the square of a DTT matrix, we need to apply some well-known trigonometric identities
[
17
, p.37]
.

Lagrange’s trigonometric identity states that for
\theta\neq 2k\pi
, where
k
is an integer, holds

\sum_{m=0}^{n}\cos m\theta=\frac{1}{2}+\frac{\sin(n+\frac{1}{2})\theta}{2\sin\frac{\theta}{2}}.

Using this identity for
\theta=(a\pi)/n
, where
a
is an integer that is not divisible with
2n
, and for
\theta=(2b\pi)/(2n+1)
, where
b
is an integer not divisible with
2n+1
, we get the following two identities

\displaystyle\sum_{m=0}^{n}\cos\frac{ma\pi}{n}=\frac{1+(-1)^{a}}{2}=\begin{cases}0&\text{for odd }a\\
1&\text{for even }a\end{cases}

(1)

\displaystyle\sum_{m=0}^{n}\cos\frac{2mb\pi}{2n+1}=\frac{1}{2}.

(2)

For an integer
a
not divisible by
2n
, the following identity holds:

\sum_{m=0}^{n-1}\cos\frac{(2m+1)a\pi}{2n}=0.

(3)

For an integer
a
not divisible by
2n+1
,

\sum_{m=0}^{n-1}\cos\frac{(2m+1)a\pi}{2n+1}=\frac{(-1)^{a+1}}{2}

(4)

holds, while for an integer
a
not divisible by
2n+2
, we have

\sum_{m=1}^{n}\cos\frac{ma\pi}{n+1}=-\frac{(-1)^{a}+1}{2}=\begin{cases}0&\text{for odd }a\\
-1&\text{for even }a.\end{cases}

(5)

Furthermore, for an integer
a
not divisible by
2n+1
, we have the following:

\sum_{m=0}^{n-1}\cos\frac{2(m+1)a\pi}{2n+1}=-\frac{1}{2}.

(6)

DCT type 1 case

Elements of the squared transformation matrix are

\displaystyle t_{kl}

\displaystyle=\sum_{m=0}^{n-1}\cos\frac{km\pi}{n-1}\cos\frac{ml\pi}{n-1}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2}\sum_{m=0}^{n-1}\cos\frac{m(k+l)\pi}{n-1}+\frac{1}{2}\sum_{m=0}^{n-1}\cos\frac{m(k-l)\pi}{n-1}.

For

t_{kk}=\begin{cases}\frac{n+1}{2}&\text{for }k=1,2,\ldots,n-2\\
n&\text{for }k=0\text{ or }k=n-1.\end{cases}

IV
Trace of transformation matrix

\sum_{k=0}^{m-1}\cos\frac{2k^{2}\pi}{m}=\frac{\sqrt{m}}{2}\left(1+\cos\frac{m\pi}{2}+\sin\frac{m\pi}{2}\right)

(7)

\sum_{k=0}^{m-1}\sin\frac{2k^{2}\pi}{m}=\frac{\sqrt{m}}{2}\left(1+\cos\frac{m\pi}{2}-\sin\frac{m\pi}{2}\right).

(8)

For the DCT
(1)
, DCT
(4)
, DST
(1)
, and DST
(4)

n

\trace{\mathbf{C}_{(1)}}=\trace{\mathbf{C}_{(4)}}=\trace{\mathbf{S}_{(1)}}=\trace{\mathbf{S}_{(4)}}=0,\ \text{for even }n.

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{2n+1}\sin\frac{k^{2}\pi}{n+1}=\sum_{k=0}^{2n}\sin\frac{(k+1)^{2}\pi}{n+1}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}\sin\frac{(k+1)^{2}\pi}{n+1}+\sum_{k=0}^{n}\sin\frac{(n+1+k)^{2}\pi}{n+1}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}\sin\frac{(k+1)^{2}\pi}{n+1}+\sum_{k=0}^{n}\sin\frac{k^{2}\pi}{n+1}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}\sin\frac{(k+1)^{2}\pi}{n+1}+\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}\sin\frac{(k+1)^{2}\pi}{n+1}

\displaystyle=2\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}\sin\frac{(k+1)^{2}\pi}{n+1}.

The trace of this transformation matrix is then

\trace{\mathbf{S}_{(1)}}=\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}\sin\frac{(k+1)^{2}\pi}{n+1}=\begin{cases}0&\text{for even }n\\
\sqrt{\frac{n+1}{2}}&\text{for odd }n.\end{cases}

(14)

IV-F

DST type 4, odd
n

Let start from (
8

m=8n
, to get

\displaystyle 2\sqrt{2n}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{8n-1}\sin\frac{k^{2}\pi}{4n}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{4n-1}\sin\frac{k^{2}\pi}{4n}+\sum_{k=0}^{4n-1}\sin\frac{(4n+k)^{2}\pi}{4n}

\displaystyle=2\sum_{k=0}^{4n-1}\sin\frac{k^{2}\pi}{4n}

\displaystyle=2\sum_{k=0}^{2n-1}\sin\frac{2k^{2}\pi}{2n}+2\sum_{k=0}^{2n-1}\sin\frac{(2k+1)^{2}\pi}{4n}.

Using (

\displaystyle 2\sqrt{2n}

\displaystyle=2\sum_{k=0}^{2n-1}\sin\frac{(2k+1)^{2}\pi}{4n}

\displaystyle=2\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}\sin\frac{(2k+1)^{2}\pi}{4n}+2\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}\sin\frac{(2n+2k+1)^{2}\pi}{4n}

\displaystyle=4\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}\sin\frac{(2k+1)^{2}\pi}{4n}.

VI
Conclusion

An analytic proof for eigenvalues, and corresponding multiplicities is provided for eight symmetric non-normalized DTT. The trace and the square of transformation matrix is derived in all analyzed cases. Our further research will include derivation of eigenvector basis for the analyzed DTT. The proposed approach, based on the decomposition of the eigenspace into orthogonal subspaces invariant under considered DTT, provides eigenvalues and some eigenvectors, for the case when the eigenvalue multiplicity is 1. Since the DTT
are fundamental mathematical tools for signal processing and related applications, we believe that the presented theory is particularly relevant to this field, since it sheds a new light on the understanding of commonly used transforms.
