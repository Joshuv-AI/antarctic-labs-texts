Σ \Sigma -dual-Rickart Modules
Shiv Kumar, Ashok Ji Gupta

Abstract
In this paper, we introduce and study the concept of Σ \Sigma -dual-Rickart modules which is the dual case of Σ \Sigma -Rickart modules. An A A -module X X is said to be a Σ \Sigma -dual-Rickart if every direct sum of copies of X X is a dual-Rickart module. We show when a Σ \Sigma -Rickart module is a Σ \Sigma -dual-Rickart module and vice-versa. Also, we characterize semisimple Artinian rings, regular rings, semi-hereditary rings, cohereditary modules and F ​ P FP -injective modules in terms of Σ \Sigma -dual-Rickart modules. Further, we study the endomorphism ring of a Σ \Sigma -dual-Rickart module.

Proposition 5.1
.

If

\Sigma
-d-Rickart

Proof.

Let

-d-Rickart

is a d-Rickart module for every

Proposition 5.2
.

If
X
is a right
\Sigma
-d-Rickart module and

{{}_{S}X}
is a FP-injective
S

Let

\Sigma
-d-Rickart

is FP-injective

Proposition 5.3
.

If

\Sigma
-d-Rickart module with endomorphism ring

Since

is a

-d-Rickart
