Remark 5.1.
The study of the cohomology of Lie groups began with
coefficients over a field
\mathbb{F}

where the isomorphism follows from the Künneth formula. It
furnishes the cohomology
H^{\ast}(G;\mathbb{F})
with an additional
co-produce
\mu^{\ast}
, making the pair
\left\{H^{\ast}(G;\mathbb{F}),\mu^{\ast}\right\}
an
Hopf algebra
over
\mathbb{F}
. In term of

\mu^{\ast}
one can specify the set of so called ”
primitive
elements
”

of

H^{\ast}(G;\mathbb{F})

P(G;\mathbb{F})=\{a\in H^{\ast}(G;\mathbb{F})\mid\mu^{\ast}(a)=a\otimes 1

\oplus 1\otimes a\}
,

which is clearly a linear subspace of
H^{\ast}(G;\mathbb{F})
. For
the case
\mathbb{F}
is the field
\mathbb{R}
of reals Hopf
[
34
]
proved that

Theorem 5.1.
If
\left\{z_{1},\cdots,z_{k}\right\}
is a homogeneous basis of
P(G;\mathbb{R})
, then

(5.2)

H^{\ast}(G;\mathbb{R})=\Lambda_{\mathbb{R}}(z_{1},\cdots,z_{k})
,
\deg z_{i}\equiv 1\operatorname{mod}2
.
\square

Remark 5.2.
A compact Lie group

We mention at this point that the method developed in this paper applies
