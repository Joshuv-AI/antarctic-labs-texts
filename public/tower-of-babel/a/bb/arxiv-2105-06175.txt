The Minimal Supersymmetric Universal Seesaw Mechanism (MSUSM).


Abstract
We build a supersymmetric model with S ​ U ​ ( 2 ) L ⊗ S ​ U ​ ( 2 ) R ⊗ U ​ ( 1 ) ( B − L ) SU(2)_{L}\otimes SU(2)_{R}\otimes U(1)_{(B-L)} electroweak gauge symmetry, where S ​ U ​ ( 2 ) L SU(2)_{L} is the left-handed currents while S ​ U ​ ( 2 ) R SU(2)_{R} is the right-handed currents and B B and L L are the usual baryonic and leptonic numbers. We can generate an universal seesaw mechanism to get masses for all the usual fermions in this model, it means quarks and leptons, and also explain the mixing experimental data. We will also to study the masses of the Gauge Bosons and also the masses of all usual scalars of this model.
