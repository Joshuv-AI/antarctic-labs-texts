Spaces of real polynomials with constrained real divisors

In this section, for our readers’ convenience, we state a number of results from
[
KSW1
]
and
[
KSW2
]
about the topology of spaces of real monic univariate polynomials with constrained real divisors. These results will be crucial for the applications to follow.

Let
\mathcal{P}_{d}
denote the space of real monic univariate polynomials of degree
d
. Given a polynomial
P(u)=u^{d}+a_{d-1}u^{d-1}+\cdots+a_{0}
with real coefficients, we consider its
real divisor

D_{\mathbb{R}}(P)
.

Let
\omega_{i}
denotes the multiplicity of the
i
-th real root of
P
, the roots being ordered by their magnitude. The ordered
\ell
-tuple

\omega=(\omega_{1},\ldots,\omega_{\ell})
is called
the
real root multiplicity pattern
of
P(x)
, or the
multiplicity pattern
for short.

Such sequences
\omega
form a
universal poset

(\mathbf{\Omega},\succ)
. The partial order “
\succ
” in
\mathbf{\Omega}
is defined in terms of two types of elementary operations:
merges

\{\mathsf{M}_{i}\}_{i}
and
inserts

\{\mathsf{I}_{i}\}_{i}
.

The operation
\mathsf{M}_{i}
merges a pair of adjacent entries
\omega_{i},\omega_{i+1}
of
\omega=(\omega_{1},\dots,\omega_{i},\omega_{i+1},\dots,\omega_{q})
into a single component
\tilde{\omega}_{i}=\omega_{i}+\omega_{i+1}
, thus forming a new shorter sequence
\mathsf{M}_{i}(\omega)=(\omega_{1},\dots,\tilde{\omega}_{i},\dots,\omega_{q})
. The operation
\mathsf{I}_{i}
either insert
2
in-between
\omega_{i}
and
\omega_{i+1}
, thus forming a new longer sequence
\mathsf{I}_{i}(\omega)=(\dots,\omega_{i},2,\omega_{i+1},\dots)
, or, in the case of
\mathsf{I}_{0}
, appends
2
before the sequence
\omega
, or, in the case
\mathsf{I}_{q}
, appends
2
after the sequence
\omega
.

So the
merge
operation

\mathsf{M}_{j}:\mathbf{\Omega}\to\mathbf{\Omega}

sends
\omega=(\omega_{1},\ldots,\omega_{\ell})
to the composition

\mathsf{M}_{j}(\omega)=(M_{j}(\omega)_{1},\ldots,M_{j}(\omega)_{\ell-1}),

where, for any
j\geq\ell
, one has
\mathsf{M}_{j}(\omega)=\omega
, and for
1\leq j<\ell
, one has

\displaystyle\left\{\begin{array}[]{lll}\mathsf{M}_{j}(\omega)_{i}&=&\omega_{i}\;\textrm{ if }\,i<j,\\
\mathsf{M}_{j}(\omega)_{j}&=&\omega_{j}+\omega_{j+1},\\
\mathsf{M}_{j}(\omega)_{i}&=&\omega_{i+1}\;\textrm{ if }\,i+1<j\leq\ell-1.\end{array}\right.

Similarly, we introduce the
insert
operation

\mathsf{I}_{j}:\mathbf{\Omega}\to\mathbf{\Omega}
that
sends
\omega=(\omega_{1},\ldots,\omega_{\ell})
to the composition
\mathsf{I}_{j}(\omega)=(I_{j}(\omega)_{1},\ldots,I_{j}(\omega)_{\ell+1}),
where for any
j>\ell+1
, one has
\mathsf{I}_{j}(\omega)=\omega
, and for
1\leq j\leq\ell+1
, one has

\displaystyle\left\{\begin{array}[]{lll}\mathsf{I}_{j}(\omega)_{i}&=&\omega_{i}\;\textrm{ if }\,i<j,\\
\mathsf{I}_{j}(\omega)_{j}&=&2,\\
\mathsf{I}_{j}(\omega)_{i}&=&\omega_{i-1}\;\textrm{ if }\,j\leq i\leq\ell+1.\end{array}\right.
