General Helicity Formalism for Two-hadron Production in e + ​ e − e^{+}e^{-} Collisions and the Λ \Lambda Polarizing Fragmentation Function
Umberto D’Alesio 1,2,∗, Francesco Murgia 2 and Marco Zaccheddu 1,2

Abstract
We present the complete structure of the azimuthal dependences and polarization observables for two-hadron production in e + ​ e − e^{+}e^{-} annihilation processes within a transverse momentum dependent (TMD) approach adopting the helicity formalism. The leading-twist TMD fragmentation functions (TMD-FFs) for spin-1/2 hadrons are fully accounted for. The role of the polarizing FF, together with its extraction from Belle data for the transverse polarization of Λ \Lambda ’s, are discussed as well. Finally, predictions for SIDIS processes at EIC are presented.

Introduction

Hadron production in
e^{+}e^{-}
annihilation processes represents the cleanest way to access the parton-to-hadron fragmentation mechanism.
The leading order (LO) expressions for all leading-twist (LT) azimuthal dependences and polarization observables are derived, within a TMD helicity formalism, for
e^{+}e^{-}\to h_{1}h_{2}\,X
processes, together with the TMD-FFs for spin-1/2 hadrons
[
1
]
.
We then present a fit of Belle data
[
2
]
for the transverse
\Lambda
polarization, leading to the extraction of the polarizing FF (pFF)
[
3
]
: the distribution of a transversely polarized spin-1/2 hadron in the fragmentation of an unpolarized quark. Finally, we show some estimates for SIDIS processes.

2
Formalism

We consider the process
e^{+}e^{-}\to h_{1}h_{2}\,X
, where
h_{1,2}
are spin-1/2 hadrons produced almost back-to-back. In the
hadron-frame
configuration, the
\hat{z}_{L}
-axis is in the opposite direction w.r.t. the hadron
h_{2}
and the
\widehat{x_{L}z_{L}}
lepton plane is determined by the lepton and the
h_{2}
directions (with the
e^{+}e^{-}
axis at angle
\theta
). The production plane is fixed by
\hat{z}_{L}
and the direction of the hadron
h_{1}
, with transverse momentum
\bm{P}_{1T}
, at an angle
\phi_{1}
w.r.t. the lepton plane.
The master formula at LO is given by

\displaystyle\rho^{h_{1},S_{1}}_{\lambda_{h_{1}},\lambda^{{}^{\prime}}_{h_{1}}}\rho^{h_{2},S_{2}}_{\lambda_{h_{2}},\lambda^{{}^{\prime}}_{h_{2}}}\frac{d\sigma^{e^{+}e^{-}\rightarrow h_{1}h_{2}X}}{d\!\cos\!\,\theta\,dz_{1}\,d^{2}\!\bm{p}_{\perp 1}dz_{2}\,d^{2}\!\bm{p}_{\perp 2}}

\displaystyle=\sum_{q}\sum_{\{\lambda\}}\frac{1}{32\pi s}\frac{1}{4}\hat{M}_{\lambda_{q}\lambda_{\bar{q}},\lambda_{+}\lambda_{-}}\hat{M}^{\ast}_{\lambda^{{}^{\prime}}_{q}\lambda^{{}^{\prime}}_{\bar{q}},\lambda_{+}\lambda_{-}}\hat{D}^{\lambda_{h_{1}},\lambda^{{}^{\prime}}_{h_{1}}}_{\lambda_{q},\lambda^{{}^{\prime}}_{q}}(z_{1},\bm{p}_{\perp 1})\hat{D}^{\lambda_{h_{2}},\lambda^{{}^{\prime}}_{h_{2}}}_{\lambda_{\bar{q}},\lambda^{{}^{\prime}}_{\bar{q}}}(z_{2},\bm{p}_{\perp 2})\,,

(1)

where
z_{i}
are the hadron light-cone momentum fractions and
\bm{p}_{\perp i}
are the hadron transverse momenta w.r.t. the parent parton directions of motion;
\rho^{h,S_{h}}_{\lambda_{h},\lambda^{{}^{\prime}}_{h}}
is the helicity density matrix of the hadron
h
with spin
S_{h}
;
\hat{M}_{\lambda_{q}\lambda_{\bar{q}},\lambda_{+}\lambda_{-}}
’s are the helicity scattering amplitudes for the process
e^{+}(\lambda_{+})+e^{-}(\lambda_{-})\rightarrow q(\lambda_{q})+\bar{q}(\lambda_{\bar{q}})
;
\hat{D}^{\lambda_{h},\lambda^{{}^{\prime}}_{h}}_{\lambda_{q},\lambda^{{}^{\prime}}_{q}}(z,\bm{p}_{\perp})
is the product of the helicity fragmentation amplitudes for the
q\rightarrow h+X
process, defined as

\displaystyle\hat{D}^{\lambda_{h},\lambda^{{}^{\prime}}_{h}}_{\lambda_{c},\lambda^{{}^{\prime}}_{c}}(z,\bm{p}_{\perp})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\mathrel{\mathop{\kern 0.0pt\hbox to0.0pt{\hbox{$\sum$}\hss}}\hbox{\kern-1.25pt$\displaystyle\int$}}_{{X},\lambda_{X}}\hat{\mathcal{D}}_{\lambda_{h},\lambda_{X};\lambda_{c}}(z,\bm{p}_{\perp})\hat{\mathcal{D}}^{\ast}_{\lambda^{{}^{\prime}}_{h},\lambda_{X};\lambda^{{}^{\prime}}_{c}}(z,\bm{p}_{\perp})\,,

(2)

where
\mathrel{\mathop{\kern 0.0pt\hbox to0.0pt{\hbox{$\sum$}\hss}}\hbox{\kern-1.25pt$\displaystyle\int$}}_{{X},\lambda_{X}}
stands for a spin sum and phase space integration over all undetected particles. All other details can be found in Ref.
[
1
]
.

The expression in Eq. (
1
) has then to be integrated over the unobserved variables as

\displaystyle\frac{d\sigma^{e^{+}e^{-}\to h_{1}(S_{1})h_{2}(S_{2})\,X}}{d\!\cos\theta\,dz_{1}\,dz_{2}\,d^{2}\!\bm{P}_{1T}}=\int\!d^{2}\!\bm{p}_{\perp 1}\,d^{2}\!\bm{p}_{\perp 2}\,\delta^{(2)}(\bm{p}_{\perp 1}-\bm{P}_{1T}+\bm{p}_{\perp 2}\,z_{p_{1}}/z_{p_{2}})\,\frac{d\sigma^{e^{+}e^{-}\to h_{1}(S_{1})h_{2}(S_{2})\,X}}{d\!\cos\!\,\theta\,dz_{1}\,d^{2}\!\bm{p}_{\perp 1}dz_{2}\,d^{2}\!\bm{p}_{\perp 2}}\,,

(3)

where
z_{p_{i}}=\frac{2|\bm{P}_{h_{i}}|}{\sqrt{s}}
are the momentum fractions (
z_{h_{i}}=\frac{2E_{h_{i}}}{\sqrt{s}}
are the usual energy fractions).

2.1
Quark TMD-FFs for spin-1/2 hadrons

We start defining the TMD-FF for a polarized quark, with spin
s_{q}
, fragmenting into an unpolarized hadron:
\hat{D}_{h/q,s_{q}}(z,\bm{p}_{\perp})
. Then, we combine the parton and hadron helicity density matrices and the generalized FFs, Eq. (
2
), as
[
1
]

\rho^{h,S_{h}}_{\lambda_{h},\lambda^{{}^{\prime}}_{h}}\hat{D}_{h/q,s_{q}}(z,\bm{p}_{\perp})=\sum_{\lambda_{q},\lambda^{{}^{\prime}}_{q}}\rho^{q}_{\lambda_{q},\lambda^{{}^{\prime}}_{q}}\hat{D}^{\lambda_{h},\lambda^{{}^{\prime}}_{h}}_{\lambda_{q},\lambda^{{}^{\prime}}_{q}}(z,\bm{p}_{\perp})\,.

(4)

Exploiting the left-hand side in Eq. (
4
), in terms of the polarization components, we can define

\displaystyle(P^{h}_{J}\,\hat{D}_{h/q,s_{T}})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\hat{D}_{S_{J}/s_{T}}^{h/q}-\hat{D}_{-S_{J}/s_{T}}^{h/q}\equiv\Delta\hat{D}_{S_{J}/s_{T}}^{h/q}(z,\bm{p}_{\perp})

(5)

\displaystyle(P^{h}_{J}\,\hat{D}_{h/q,s_{z}})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\hat{D}_{S_{J}/s_{z}}^{h/q}-\hat{D}_{-S_{J}/s_{z}}^{h/q}\;\equiv\Delta\hat{D}_{S_{J}/s_{z}}^{h/q}(z,\bm{p}_{\perp})

(6)

\displaystyle\hat{D}_{h/q,s_{T}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle D_{h/q}(z,p_{\perp})+\frac{1}{2}\,\Delta\hat{D}_{h/s_{T}}(z,\bm{p}_{\perp})\,,

(7)

where
J=X,Y,Z
(
P^{h}_{J}
) are the hadron helicity axes (polarizations), and
s_{T}
(
s_{z}
) stands for the quark transverse (longitudinal) spin component in its helicity frame. These correspond to 8 TMD-FFs for a spin-1/2 hadron, with a clear partonic interpretation.

Taking into account the parity properties of
\hat{D}^{\lambda_{h},\lambda^{{}^{\prime}}_{h}}_{\lambda_{q},\lambda^{{}^{\prime}}_{q}}
, only 8 real independent quantities survive, directly related to the above 8 TMD-FFs
[
1
]
. These results can be recast as:

\displaystyle\hat{D}_{h/q}(z,\bm{p}_{\perp})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle D_{h/q}=(D^{++}_{++}+D^{++}_{--})

(8)

\displaystyle\Delta\hat{D}_{h/q,s_{T}}(z,\bm{p}_{\perp})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\Delta^{N}\!D_{h/q^{\uparrow}}\sin{(\phi_{s_{q}}-\phi_{h})}=4{\rm Im}D^{++}_{+-}\sin{(\phi_{s_{q}}-\phi_{h})}\quad[{\rm Collins\ FF}]

(9)

\displaystyle\Delta\hat{D}^{h/q}_{S_{Z}/s_{L}}(z,\bm{p}_{\perp})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\Delta D^{h/q}_{S_{Z}/s_{L}}=(D^{++}_{++}-D^{++}_{--})

(10)

\displaystyle\Delta\hat{D}^{h/q}_{S_{Z}/s_{T}}(z,\bm{p}_{\perp})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\Delta D^{h/q}_{S_{Z}/s_{T}}\cos{(\phi_{s_{q}}-\phi_{h})}=2{\rm Re}D^{++}_{+-}\cos{(\phi_{s_{q}}-\phi_{h})}

(11)

\displaystyle\Delta\hat{D}^{h/q}_{S_{X}/s_{L}}(z,\bm{p}_{\perp})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\Delta D^{h/q}_{S_{X}/s_{L}}=2{\rm Re}D^{+-}_{++}

(12)

\displaystyle\Delta\hat{D}^{h/q}_{S_{X}/s_{T}}(z,\bm{p}_{\perp})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\Delta D^{h/q}_{S_{X}/s_{T}}\cos{(\phi_{s_{q}}-\phi_{h})}=(D^{+-}_{+-}+D^{+-}_{-+})\cos{(\phi_{s_{q}}-\phi_{h})}

(13)

\displaystyle\Delta\hat{D}^{h}_{S_{Y}/q}(z,\bm{p}_{\perp})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\Delta D^{h}_{S_{Y}/q}=\Delta^{N}\!D_{h^{\uparrow}/q}=-2{\rm Im}D^{+-}_{++}\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad[{\rm Polarizing\ FF}]

(14)

\displaystyle\Delta^{-}\hat{D}^{h/q}_{S_{Y}/s_{T}}(z,\bm{p}_{\perp})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\Delta^{-}D^{h/q}_{S_{Y}/s_{T}}\sin{(\phi_{s_{q}}-\phi_{h})}=(D^{+-}_{+-}-D^{+-}_{-+})\sin{(\phi_{s_{q}}-\phi_{h})}\,.

(15)

2.2
Convolutions

By fixing the hadron spins and summing over the helicity indices in Eq. (
1
), one obtains all azimuthal dependences in terms of pairs of TMD-FFs. A tensorial analysis allows to factor out the measurable azimuthal dependences and to express all quantities in terms of convolutions (see Ref.
[
1
]
):

\displaystyle\mathcal{C}[w\Delta D^{h_{1}}\Delta D^{h_{2}}]

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{q}e^{2}_{q}\!\int\!d^{2}\!\bm{p}_{\bot 1}d^{2}\!\bm{p}_{\bot 2}\,\delta^{(2)}\big(\bm{p}_{\bot 1}-\bm{P}_{1T}+\bm{p}_{\bot 2}z_{p_{1}}/z_{p_{2}}\big)

(16)

\displaystyle\times\,w(\bm{p}_{\bot 2},\bm{P}_{1T})\,\Delta D_{h_{1}/q}(z_{1},p_{\bot 1})\,\Delta D_{h_{2}/\bar{q}}(z_{2},p_{\bot 2})\,.

Here we report only few examples (see also Refs.
[
4
,
5
]
):

-
Unpolarized cross section

\frac{d\sigma^{e^{+}e^{-}\rightarrow h_{1}h_{2}X}}{d\!\cos\theta\,dz_{1}dz_{2}d^{2}\!\bm{P}_{1T}}=\frac{3\pi\alpha^{2}}{2s}\bigg\{\big(1+\cos^{2}\theta\big)F_{UU}+\sin^{2}\theta\cos(2\phi_{1})F^{\cos(2\phi_{1})}_{UU}\bigg\}\,,

(17)

with

\displaystyle F_{UU}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{q}e^{2}_{q}\int d^{2}\bm{p}_{\bot 2}\,D_{h_{1}/q}(z_{1},p_{\bot 1})D_{h_{2}/\bar{q}}(z_{2},p_{\bot 2})=\mathcal{C}[D_{h_{1}/q}D_{h_{2}/\bar{q}}]

(18)

\displaystyle F^{\cos(2\phi_{1})}_{UU}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\mathcal{C}\bigg[\frac{1}{4}\bigg\{\frac{P_{1T}}{p_{\perp 1}}\,\hat{\bm{p}}_{\bot 2}\cdot\hat{\bm{P}}_{1T}-\frac{z_{p_{1}}}{z_{p_{2}}}\frac{p_{\perp 2}}{p_{\perp 1}}\,\bigg[2\big(\hat{\bm{p}}_{\bot 2}\cdot\hat{\bm{P}}_{1T}\big)^{2}-1\bigg]\bigg\}\Delta^{N}\!D_{h_{1}/q^{\uparrow}}\Delta^{N}\!D_{h_{2}/\bar{q}^{\uparrow}}\bigg]\,,

(19)

where the latter expression gives access to the Collins FF.

-
Single-transverse polarized cross section

\displaystyle P^{h_{1}}_{T}\,\frac{d\sigma^{e^{+}e^{-}\rightarrow h_{1}h_{2}X}}{d\!\cos\theta\,dz_{1}dz_{2}d^{2}\!\bm{P}_{1T}}=\frac{3\pi\alpha^{2}}{2s}\bigg\{\Big(1+\cos^{2}\theta\Big)\sin(\phi_{1}-\phi_{S_{1}}^{L})\,F^{\sin(\phi_{1}-\phi_{S_{1}}^{L})}_{TU}

\displaystyle\quad+\,\sin^{2}\theta\,\bigg(\sin(\phi_{1}+\phi_{S_{1}}^{L})\,F^{\sin(\phi_{1}+\phi_{S_{1}}^{L})}_{TU}+\sin(3\phi_{1}-\phi_{S_{1}}^{L})\,F^{\sin(3\phi_{1}-\phi_{S_{1}}^{L})}_{TU}\bigg)\bigg\}\,,

(20)

where
\phi_{S_{1}}^{L}
is the azimuthal angle of the spin of the hadron
h_{1}
and

\displaystyle F^{\sin(\phi_{1}-\phi_{S_{1}}^{L})}_{TU}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\mathcal{C}\bigg[\bigg(\frac{z_{p_{1}}}{z_{p_{2}}}\frac{p_{\bot 2}}{p_{\bot 1}}\,\hat{\bm{p}}_{\bot 2}\cdot\hat{\bm{P}}_{1T}-\frac{P_{1T}}{p_{\perp 1}}\bigg)\,\Delta^{N}\!D_{h_{1}^{\uparrow}/q}D_{h_{2}/\bar{q}}\bigg]

(21)

\displaystyle 2F^{\sin(\phi_{1}+\phi_{S_{1}}^{L})}_{TU}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\mathcal{C}\bigg[\big(\hat{\bm{p}}_{\bot 2}\cdot\hat{\bm{P}}_{1T}\big)\frac{1}{2}\big(\Delta D^{h_{1}/q}_{S_{X}/s_{T}}+\Delta^{-}\!D^{h_{1}/q}_{S_{Y}/s_{T}}\big)\,\Delta^{N}\!D_{h_{2}/\bar{q}^{\uparrow}}\bigg]

(22)

\displaystyle 2F^{\sin(3\phi_{1}-\phi_{S_{1}}^{L})}_{TU}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\mathcal{C}\Bigg[\bigg\{\frac{z_{p_{1}}^{2}}{z_{p_{2}}^{2}}\frac{p_{\perp 2}^{2}}{p_{\perp 1}^{2}}\bigg[4\big(\hat{\bm{p}}_{\bot 2}\cdot\hat{\bm{P}}_{1T}\big)^{3}-3\,\big(\hat{\bm{p}}_{\bot 2}\cdot\hat{\bm{P}}_{1T}\big)\bigg]+\frac{P_{1T}^{2}}{p_{\perp 1}^{2}}\,\big(\hat{\bm{p}}_{\bot 2}\cdot\hat{\bm{P}}_{1T}\big)

(23)

\displaystyle\;-2\,\frac{z_{p_{1}}}{z_{p_{2}}}\frac{p_{\perp 2}P_{1T}}{p_{\perp 1}^{2}}\bigg[2\big(\hat{\bm{p}}_{\bot 2}\cdot\hat{\bm{P}}_{1T}\big)^{2}-1\bigg]\bigg\}\frac{1}{2}\bigg(\Delta D^{h_{1}/q}_{S_{X}/s_{T}}-\Delta^{-}\!D^{h_{1}/q}_{S_{Y}/s_{T}}\bigg)\,\Delta^{N}\!D_{h_{2}/\bar{q}^{\uparrow}}\,.

The polarization orthogonal to the hadron plane, that is along

\hat{\bm{n}}\equiv(\cos\phi_{n},\sin\phi_{n},0)=\frac{-\bm{P}_{2}\times\bm{P}_{1}}{|\bm{P}_{2}\times\bm{P}_{1}|}=-\sin\phi_{1}\hat{\bm{x}}_{L}+\cos\phi_{1}\hat{\bm{y}}_{L}\,,

(24)

can be obtained by identifying
\phi_{S_{1}}^{L}\equiv\phi_{n}=\phi_{1}+\frac{\pi}{2}
in Eq. (
20
). By integrating over
\bm{P}_{1T}
we get

P^{h_{1}}_{n}(z_{1},z_{2})=-\frac{F^{\sin(\phi_{1}-\phi_{S_{1}}^{L})}_{TU}}{F_{UU}}\,.

(25)

3
Phenomenology

By adopting a Gaussian Ansatz for the TMD-FFs in Eqs. (
18
), (
21
), Eq. (
25
) becomes

\displaystyle P^{h_{1}}_{n}=\sqrt{\frac{e\pi}{2}}\frac{1}{M_{\rm pol}}\frac{\langle p_{\perp}^{2}\rangle_{\rm pol}^{2}}{\langle p_{\perp 1}^{2}\rangle}\,\frac{z_{2}}{\Big\{\Big[z_{1}\Big(1-\frac{m_{h_{1}}^{2}}{z_{1}^{2}s}\Big)\Big]^{2}\langle p_{\perp 2}^{2}\rangle+z_{2}^{2}\langle p_{\perp}^{2}\rangle_{\rm pol}\Big\}^{1/2}}\,\frac{\sum_{q}e^{2}_{q}\,\Delta D_{h_{1}^{\uparrow}/q}(z_{1})D_{h_{2}/\bar{q}}(z_{2})}{\sum_{q}e^{2}_{q}\,D_{h_{1}/q}(z_{1})D_{h_{2}/\bar{q}}(z_{2})}\,,

(26)

where we have used the following parametrizations:

\displaystyle D_{h/q}(z,p_{\perp})=D_{h/q}(z)\,\frac{e^{-p_{\perp}^{2}/\langle p_{\perp}^{2}\rangle}}{\pi\langle p_{\perp}^{2}\rangle}\quad\quad\quad\Delta^{N}\!D_{h^{\uparrow}\!/q}(z,p_{\perp})=\Delta D_{h^{\uparrow}\!/q}(z)\frac{\sqrt{2e}\,p_{\perp}}{M_{\rm pol}}\frac{e^{-p_{\perp}^{2}/\langle p_{\perp}^{2}\rangle_{\rm pol}}}{\pi\langle p_{\perp}^{2}\rangle}\,,

(27)

with
\langle p_{\perp}^{2}\rangle_{\rm pol}=\frac{M_{\rm pol}^{2}}{M_{\rm pol}^{2}+\langle p_{\perp}^{2}\rangle}\,\langle p_{\perp}^{2}\rangle
.
Another important quantity is the first
p_{\perp}
-moment of the pFF:

\Delta D_{h^{\uparrow}\!/q}^{(1)}(z)=\int\!d^{2}\bm{p}_{\perp}\frac{p_{\perp}}{2zm_{h}}\Delta^{N}\!D_{h^{\uparrow}\!/q}(z,p_{\perp})=\sqrt{\frac{e}{2}}\frac{1}{zm_{h}}\frac{1}{M_{\rm pol}}\frac{\langle p^{2}_{\perp}\rangle^{2}_{\rm pol}}{\langle p^{2}_{\perp}\rangle}\Delta D_{h^{\uparrow}\!/q}(z)\,.

(28)

We also consider the inclusive
\Lambda
production case (within a jet), adopting a simplified approach in terms of TMD-FFs. The transverse polarization (w.r.t. the jet-
\Lambda
plane) is given as

\displaystyle{\cal P}_{T}(z,p_{\perp})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{\sum_{q}e^{2}_{q}\,\Delta^{N}\!D_{\Lambda^{\uparrow}/q}(z,p_{\perp})}{\sum_{q}e^{2}_{q}\,D_{\Lambda/q}(z,p_{\perp})}\,.

(29)

3.1
Results

We first perform a fit of the associated production data alone (see also Ref.
[
6
]
), including in a second phase also the inclusive data set
[
3
]
. The
z
-dependent part of the pFF is parameterized as follows (
q=u,d,s,\rm{sea}
)

\Delta D_{\Lambda^{\uparrow}\!/q}(z)=N_{q}z^{a_{q}}(1-z)^{b_{q}}\frac{(a_{q}+b_{q})^{(a_{q}+b_{q})}}{a_{q}^{a_{q}}b_{q}^{b_{q}}}D_{\Lambda/q}(z)\,,

(30)

where
|N_{q}|\leq 1
. The best parameter choice turns out to be:

N_{u},\;N_{d},\;N_{s},\;N_{\rm sea},\;a_{s},\;b_{u},\;b_{\rm sea}\,,

with all other
a
,
b
parameters set to zero. Together with
\langle p^{2}_{\perp}\rangle_{\rm pol}
(Eq. (
27
)) we have 8 free parameters.

We now use the so extracted pFFs to give predictions for the same observable in SIDIS. In such a case the polarization is measured transversely w.r.t. the plane containing the target and the
\Lambda

P_{T}(x_{\rm B},z_{h})=\frac{\sqrt{2e\pi}}{2m_{p}}\frac{\langle p_{\perp}^{2}\rangle^{2}_{\rm pol}}{\langle p_{\perp}^{2}\rangle}\frac{1}{\sqrt{\langle p_{\perp}^{2}\rangle_{\rm pol}+z_{h}^{2}\langle k_{\perp}^{2}\rangle}}\frac{\sum_{q}e_{q}^{2}f_{q/p}(x_{\rm B})\Delta D_{\Lambda^{\uparrow}/q}(z_{h})}{\sum_{q}e_{q}^{2}f_{q/p}(x_{\rm B})D_{\Lambda/q}(z_{h})}\,.

(31)

4
Conclusions

The complete azimuthal structure in terms of TMD-FFs for the process
e^{+}e^{-}\to h_{1}h_{2}\,X
has been derived within the helicity formalism in full detail and the first extraction of the polarizing FF from Belle data has been presented. Estimates for the transverse
\Lambda
polarization in SIDIS are given, emphasising their role in the study of the (universality) properties of the pFF.
