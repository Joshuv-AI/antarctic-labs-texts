First Order Linear Proportional Difference Equation with Integration Factor the ( s , t ) (s,t) -Pantograph Function
Ronald Orozco López

Abstract
In this paper we find solutions to first-order linear proportional difference equations via the ( s , t ) (s,t) -integration factor method. The ( s , t ) (s,t) -integration factor involves the ( s , t ) (s,t) -Pantograph function, which is a generalization of the partial Theta function. Other equations are solved including the ( s , t ) (s,t) -analogue of the Bernoulli equation.

4.3
Operator method

We use an operator method to deal with a linear proportional difference equation. Two basic operator are
(s,t)
-derivative operator
\mathbf{D}_{s,t}
and the scale
T_{u}
defined by

T_{u}y(x)=y(ux).

Two basic properties of these two operators are

\displaystyle\mathbf{D}_{s,t}T_{u}

\displaystyle=uT_{u}\mathbf{D}_{s,t},

\displaystyle T_{u}T_{v}

\displaystyle=T_{uv}.

Theorem 6
.

For the first-order equation

\mathbf{D}_{s,t}y(x)=a\beta y(x)+\gamma T_{u}y(x)+\delta\mathrm{E}_{s,t}(a,b;\alpha x,u)

(61)

the general solution is given by

y(x)=c\mathrm{E}_{s,t}(a\beta,\gamma;x,u)+\frac{u\delta}{b\alpha-u\gamma}\mathrm{E}_{s,t}(a,b;\alpha x/u,u)

(62)

Proof.

Formally, we have

y(x)=\delta(\mathbf{D}_{s,t}-a\beta 1-\gamma T_{u})^{-1}\mathrm{E}_{s,t}(a,b;\alpha x,u).

To compute the right side of the above equation, we use the following formula

(\mathbf{D}_{s,t}-a\beta 1-\gamma T_{u})\mathrm{E}_{s,t}(a,b;\beta x,u)=(b\beta-\gamma)\mathrm{E}_{s,t}(a,b;u\beta x,u).

So, taking
\beta=\frac{\alpha}{u}
gives

(\mathbf{D}_{s,t}-a\frac{\alpha}{u}1-\gamma T_{u})^{-1}\mathrm{E}_{s,t}(a,b;\alpha x,u)=\frac{1}{\frac{b\alpha}{u}-\gamma}\mathrm{E}_{s,t}(a,b;\alpha x/u,u).

Therefore, a special solution is given by

y^{\ast}(x)=\frac{u\delta}{b\alpha-u\gamma}\mathrm{E}_{s,t}(a,b;\alpha x/u,u)

and the general solution is

y(x)=c\mathrm{E}_{s,t}(a\beta,\gamma;x,u)+\frac{u\delta}{b\alpha-u\gamma}\mathrm{E}_{s,t}(a,b;\alpha x/u,u).

∎
