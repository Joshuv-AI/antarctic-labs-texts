Spectral zeta function and ground state of quantum Rabi model
Fumio Hiroshima, Tomoyuki Shirai

Abstract
The spectral zeta function ζ g ​ ( s , g 2 + τ ) = ∑ n = 0 ∞ 1 ( E g ​ ( n ) + g 2 + τ ) s \zeta_{g}(s;g^{2}+\tau)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{(E_{g}(n)+g^{2}+\tau)^{s}} of the quantum Rabi Hamiltonian is considered, where τ > 0 \tau>0 and E n ​ ( g ) E_{n}(g) denotes n n th eigenvalue of the quantum Rabi Hamiltonian H H . Let ζ ⁡ ( s , τ ) = ∑ n = 0 ∞ 1 ( n + τ ) s \zeta(s;\tau)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{(n+\tau)^{s}} be the Hurwitz zeta function. It is shown that lim | g | → ∞ ζ g ​ ( s , g 2 + τ ) = 2 ​ ζ ​ ( s , τ ) \lim_{|g|\to\infty}\zeta_{g}(s;g^{2}+\tau)=2\zeta(s;\tau) . Moreover the path measure Π ∞ {\Pi_{\infty}} associated with the ground state of H H is constructed on a discontinuous path space, and several applications are shown.

Spectral zeta function of quantum Rabi model

The quantum Rabi model describes a two-level atom coupled to a single mode photon by the dipole interaction term.
The single photon is represented by the
1
D harmonic oscillator.
This model is initially introduced by I.I.Rabi at 1937
[
33
]
in a semiclassical region and then
the quantized version is introduced by E.T. Jaynes and F.W. Cumming
[
24
]
.
Suppose that
the eigenvalues of the two-level atom is
\{-\triangle,\triangle\}
.
Here
\triangle>0
is a constant.
Let
\sigma_{x},\sigma_{y}
and
\sigma_{z}
be the
2\times 2
Pauli matrices:

\displaystyle\sigma_{x}=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\
1&0\end{pmatrix},\quad\sigma_{y}=\begin{pmatrix}0&-i\\
i&0\end{pmatrix},\quad\sigma_{z}=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\
0&-1\end{pmatrix}.

Then
the Hamiltonian of the two-level atom is represented
by

\triangle\sigma_{z}
.
On the other hand let

a
and
a^{\dagger}
be the annihilation operator
and the creation operator in
{L^{2}({R})}
.
They are given by

\displaystyle a=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\left(\frac{{\rm d}}{{\rm d}x}+x\right),\quad a^{\dagger}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\left(-\frac{{\rm d}}{{\rm d}x}+x\right).

They satisfy the canonical commutation relation

[a,a^{\dagger}]={\mathchoice{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.5mul}{\rm 1\mskip-5.0mul}}
, and
a^{\ast}=a^{\dagger}
, where
a^{\ast}
denotes the adjoint of
a
.
The harmonic oscillator is given by
a^{\dagger}a
, i.e.,

a^{\dagger}a=-\frac{1}{2}\frac{{\rm d}^{2}}{{\rm d}x^{2}}+\frac{1}{2}x^{2}-\frac{1}{2}.

The harmonic oscillator
a^{\dagger}a
is self-adjoint on
D(\frac{{\rm d}^{2}}{{\rm d}x^{2}})\cap D(x^{2})
and the spectrum of
a^{\dagger}a
is

{\rm spec}(a^{\dagger}a)={N}\cup\{0\}

The quantum Rabi Hamiltonian is defined

\displaystyle K=\triangle\sigma_{z}\otimes{\mathchoice{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.5mul}{\rm 1\mskip-5.0mul}}+{\mathchoice{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.5mul}{\rm 1\mskip-5.0mul}}\otimes a^{\dagger}a+g\sigma_{x}\otimes(a+a^{\dagger}).

1&0\end{pmatrix}\otimes x

It can be seen that

[K,\sigma_{z}\otimes(-{\mathchoice{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.5mul}{\rm 1\mskip-5.0mul}})^{a^{\dagger}a}]=0.

The parity symmetry sometimes referred to
{Z}_{2}
-symmetry.
Putting

{\rm spec}(\triangle\sigma_{z}\otimes{\mathchoice{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.5mul}{\rm 1\mskip-5.0mul}}+{\mathchoice{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.5mul}{\rm 1\mskip-5.0mul}}\otimes a^{\dagger}a)=\{n\pm\triangle\}_{n=0}^{\infty}

\Lambda\subset[-T,T]
with
\#\Lambda<\infty

and
A_{j}\in\mathcal{B}({R})
.
Since the extension is unique,
M_{T,t}\circ\pi_{t}^{-1}=\bar{M}_{T,t}
.
Similarly
there exists a
unique probability measure

on

such that

.
Then

on

,
which implies the lemma.
∎We shall show that

on

Lemma A.4

We have

\rho_{T}=\Pi_{T}
on
\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\circ}}{{\mathcal{B}}}_{T}
.

Proof.

Let
t\leq T
. It is enough to show that

\Pi_{T}(A)=\rho_{T}(A)
for
A\in\pi_{t}^{-1}(\mathcal{B}_{t})
.
Let
\Lambda=\{t_{0},t_{1},...,t_{n}\}\subset[-t,t]\subset[-T,T]
and
A_{0}\times\cdots\times A_{n}\in\mathcal{B}({R}^{\Lambda})
.
We have

\displaystyle\Pi_{T}\circ\pi_{t}^{-1}(C_{t}^{\Lambda}(A_{0}\times\cdots\times A_{n}))=\frac{1}{Z_{T}}e^{2T\triangle}\frac{1}{2}\sum_{\alpha\in{Z}_{2}}{E}_{\rm Q}^{\alpha}\left[\left(\prod_{j=0}^{n}{\mathchoice{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.5mul}{\rm 1\mskip-5.0mul}}_{A_{j}}(\hat{T}_{t_{j}})\right)e^{\frac{g^{2}}{2}\int_{-T}^{T}{\rm d}t\int_{-T}^{T}{\rm d}sW_{\triangle}(t,s)}\right],

(1.6)

\displaystyle\rho_{T}\circ\pi_{t}^{-1}(C_{t}^{\Lambda}(A_{0}\times\cdots\times A_{n}))

\displaystyle=e^{2Et}e^{2\triangle t}\frac{1}{\|{\mathchoice{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.5mul}{\rm 1\mskip-5.0mul}}_{T}\|^{2}}\sum_{\alpha\in{Z}_{2}}\int_{{R}}{E}_{\rm Q}^{\alpha}{E}_{\bar{\rm P}}^{x}\left[\left(\prod_{j=0}^{n}{\mathchoice{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.5mul}{\rm 1\mskip-5.0mul}}_{A_{j}}(\hat{T}_{t_{j}})\right){{\mathchoice{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.5mul}{\rm 1\mskip-5.0mul}}_{T-t}(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{-t})}{{\mathchoice{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.5mul}{\rm 1\mskip-5.0mul}}_{T-t}(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{t})}e^{-g\int_{-t}^{t}W(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{s}){\rm d}s}\right]{\rm d}\mu(x).

(1.7)

By (
4.15
) we see that

(
1.6
)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\|{\mathchoice{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.5mul}{\rm 1\mskip-5.0mul}}_{T}\|^{2}}({\mathchoice{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.5mul}{\rm 1\mskip-5.0mul}},e^{-(t_{0}+T){L}}{\mathchoice{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.5mul}{\rm 1\mskip-5.0mul}}_{A_{0}}e^{-(t_{1}-t_{0}){L}}{\mathchoice{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.5mul}{\rm 1\mskip-5.0mul}}_{A_{1}}\cdots{\mathchoice{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.5mul}{\rm 1\mskip-5.0mul}}_{A_{n}}e^{-(T-t_{n}){L}}{\mathchoice{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.5mul}{\rm 1\mskip-5.0mul}})

\displaystyle=\frac{e^{2Et}}{\|{\mathchoice{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.5mul}{\rm 1\mskip-5.0mul}}_{T}\|^{2}}({\mathchoice{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.5mul}{\rm 1\mskip-5.0mul}}_{T-t},e^{-(t_{0}+t){L}}{\mathchoice{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.5mul}{\rm 1\mskip-5.0mul}}_{A_{0}}e^{-(t_{1}-t_{0}){L}}{\mathchoice{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.5mul}{\rm 1\mskip-5.0mul}}_{A_{1}}\cdots{\mathchoice{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.5mul}{\rm 1\mskip-5.0mul}}_{A_{n}}e^{-(t-t_{n}){L}}{\mathchoice{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.5mul}{\rm 1\mskip-5.0mul}}_{T-t})=\eqref{finiterho}.

\displaystyle\Pi_{T}\circ\pi_{t}^{-1}(C_{t}^{\Lambda}(A_{0}\times\cdots\times A_{n}))=\rho_{T}\circ\pi_{t}^{-1}(C_{t}^{\Lambda}(A_{0}\times\cdots\times A_{n})).

(1.8)

1.8
) satisfy the Kolmogorov consistency condition, there exists a unique probability measure
\mu
on
(\mathcal{D}_{T},\mathcal{B}_{t})
such that

\mu(\pi_{\Lambda}^{-1}(A_{0}\times\cdots\times A_{n}))=\Pi_{T}\circ\pi_{t}^{-1}(C_{t}^{\Lambda}(A_{0}\times\cdots\times A_{n}))=\rho_{T}\circ\pi_{t}^{-1}(C_{t}^{\Lambda}(A_{0}\times\cdots\times A_{n})).

\Pi_{T}\circ\pi_{t}^{-1}
and

are

on

Proof of Proposition
4.7

By
s\text{-}\lim_{T\to\infty}{\mathchoice{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.5mul}{\rm 1\mskip-5.0mul}}_{T}=\Phi_{\rm g}

we obtain that

s\text{-}\lim_{T\to\infty}{\mathchoice{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.5mul}{\rm 1\mskip-5.0mul}}_{T-t}=\Phi_{\rm g}
and

\lim_{T\to\infty}\|{\mathchoice{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.5mul}{\rm 1\mskip-5.0mul}}_{T}\|=~1
.
Then
for each
\alpha\in{Z}_{2}
,

({\mathchoice{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.5mul}{\rm 1\mskip-5.0mul}}_{T-t}/\|{\mathchoice{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.5mul}{\rm 1\mskip-5.0mul}}_{T}\|)(\cdot,\sigma)\to\varphi_{\rm g}(\cdot,\sigma)

as
T\to\infty
in
L^{2}({R},{\rm d}\mu)
.
Let

\Phi_{\rm g}^{T}=\frac{{\mathchoice{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.5mul}{\rm 1\mskip-5.0mul}}_{T-t}}{\|{\mathchoice{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.5mul}{\rm 1\mskip-5.0mul}}_{T}\|}
.
Note that
\Phi_{\rm g},\Phi_{\rm g}^{T}\in L^{\infty}({Z}_{2}\times{R})
.
Let
A\in\pi_{t}^{-1}(\mathcal{B}_{t})
.
Then
\Pi_{T}(A)=\rho_{T}(A)
by Lemma
A.4

and
\nu(A)={\Pi_{\infty}}(A)
by Lemma
A.1
.
We have

\displaystyle\Pi_{T}(A)-{\Pi_{\infty}}(A)=\rho_{T}(A)-\nu(A)

\displaystyle=e^{2Et}e^{2\triangle t}\sum_{\alpha\in{Z}_{2}}{E}_{\rm Q}^{\alpha}\left[{\mathchoice{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.0mul}{\rm 1\mskip-4.5mul}{\rm 1\mskip-5.0mul}}_{A}\int_{{R}}{E}_{\bar{\rm P}}^{x}\left[\left(\Phi_{\rm g}(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{-t})\Phi_{\rm g}(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{t})-\Phi_{\rm g}^{T}(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{-t})\Phi_{\rm g}^{T}(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{t})\right)e^{-g\int_{-t}^{t}W(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{s}){\rm d}s}\right]{\rm d}\mu(x)\right].

Then

\displaystyle\int_{{R}}{E}_{\bar{\rm P}}^{x}\left[\left|\Phi_{\rm g}(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{-t})\Phi_{\rm g}(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{t})-\Phi_{\rm g}^{T}(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{-t})\Phi_{\rm g}^{T}(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{t})\right|e^{-g\int_{-t}^{t}W(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{s}){\rm d}s}\right]{\rm d}\mu(x)

\displaystyle\leq\int_{{R}}{E}_{\bar{\rm P}}^{x}\left[\left|\Phi_{\rm g}(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{-t})-\Phi_{\rm g}^{T}(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{-t})|\Phi_{\rm g}(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{t})\right|e^{-g\int_{-t}^{t}W(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{s}){\rm d}s}\right]{\rm d}\mu(x)

\displaystyle+\int_{{R}}{E}_{\bar{\rm P}}^{x}\left[|\Phi_{\rm g}^{T}(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{-t})|\left|\Phi_{\rm g}(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{t})-\Phi_{\rm g}^{T}(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{t})\right|e^{-g\int_{-t}^{t}W(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{s}){\rm d}s}\right]{\rm d}\mu(x).

We estimate

\int_{{R}}{E}_{\bar{\rm P}}^{x}\left[\left|\Phi_{\rm g}(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{-t})-\Phi_{\rm g}^{T}(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{-t})|\Phi_{\rm g}(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{t})\right|e^{-g\int_{-t}^{t}W(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{s}){\rm d}s}\right]{\rm d}\mu(x)
.
By the shift invariance we have

\displaystyle\int_{{R}}{E}_{\bar{\rm P}}^{x}\left[\left|(\Phi_{\rm g}(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{-t})-\Phi_{\rm g}^{T}(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{-t}))\Phi_{\rm g}(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{t})\right|e^{-g\int_{-t}^{t}W(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{s}){\rm d}s}\right]{\rm d}\mu(x)

\displaystyle=\int_{{R}}|\Phi_{\rm g}(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{0})-\Phi_{\rm g}^{T}(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{0})|{E}_{\bar{\rm P}}^{x}\left[\left|\Phi_{\rm g}(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{2t})\right|e^{-g\int_{0}^{2t}W(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{s}){\rm d}s}\right]{\rm d}\mu(x).

By the Schwarz inequality we also have

\displaystyle\leq\int_{{R}}|\Phi_{\rm g}(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{0})-\Phi_{\rm g}^{T}(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{0})|\left({E}_{\bar{\rm P}}^{x}\left[\left|\Phi_{\rm g}(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{2t})\right|^{2}\right]\right)^{1/2}\left({E}_{\bar{\rm P}}^{x}\left[e^{-2g\int_{0}^{2t}W(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{s}){\rm d}s}\right]\right)^{1/2}

\displaystyle\leq\left(\int_{{R}}|\Phi_{\rm g}(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{0})-\Phi_{\rm g}^{T}(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{0})|^{2}{\rm d}\mu(x)\right)^{1/2}\!\!\!\!\left(\int_{{R}}{E}_{\bar{\rm P}}^{x}\left[\left|\Phi_{\rm g}(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{2t})\right|^{2}\right]{\rm d}\mu(x)\right)^{1/2}\!\!\!\!\left({E}_{\bar{\rm P}}^{x}\left[e^{-2g\int_{0}^{2t}W(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{s}){\rm d}s}\right]\right)^{1/2}.

Since by Lemma
4.2
,

\displaystyle{E}_{\bar{\rm P}}^{x}\left[e^{-2g\int_{0}^{2t}W(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{s}){\rm d}s}\right]\leq e^{|g|(1-e^{-2t})|x|}e^{{g^{2}}\int_{0}^{(1-e^{-4t})/2}|2t-y|^{2}{\rm d}y},

we have

\displaystyle\int_{{R}}{E}_{\bar{\rm P}}^{x}\left[\left|(\Phi_{\rm g}(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{-t})-\Phi_{\rm g}^{T}(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{-t}))\Phi_{\rm g}(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{t})\right|e^{-g\int_{-t}^{t}W(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{s}){\rm d}s}\right]{\rm d}\mu(x)

\displaystyle\leq C\left(\int_{{R}}|\Phi_{\rm g}(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{0})-\Phi_{\rm g}^{T}(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{0})|^{2}{\rm d}\mu(x)\right)^{1/2}\left(\int_{{R}}{E}_{\bar{\rm P}}^{x}\left[\left|\Phi_{\rm g}(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{2t})\right|^{2}\right]e^{|g|(1-e^{-2t})|x|}{\rm d}\mu(x)\right)^{1/2}

\displaystyle\leq C^{\prime}\left(\int_{{R}}|\Phi_{\rm g}(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{0})-\Phi_{\rm g}^{T}(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{0})|^{2}{\rm d}\mu(x)\right)^{1/2}\left(\int_{{R}}e^{|g|(1-e^{-2t})|x|}{\rm d}\mu(x)\right)^{1/2}.

Here we used that
\Phi_{\rm g}\in L^{\infty}({Z}_{2}\times{R})
.
Since
\int_{{R}}|\Phi_{\rm g}(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{0})-\Phi_{\rm g}^{T}(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{0})|^{2}{\rm d}\mu(x)\to 0
as
T\to\infty
,

\int_{{R}}{E}_{\bar{\rm P}}^{x}\left[\left|\Phi_{\rm g}(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{-t})-\Phi_{\rm g}^{T}(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{-t})|\Phi_{\rm g}(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{t})\right|e^{-g\int_{-t}^{t}W(\hat{q}^{\triangle}_{s}){\rm d}s}\right]{\rm d}\mu(x)\to 0

. Similarly we can also show that
