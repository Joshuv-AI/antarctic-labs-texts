Resolving puzzle in Ξ c 0 → Ξ − ​ e + ​ ν e \Xi_{c}^{0}\to\Xi^{-}e^{+}\nu_{e} with Ξ c − Ξ c ′ \Xi_{c}-\Xi_{c}^{\prime} mixing spectrum within the quark model
Chao-Qiang Geng, Xiang-Nan Jin and Chia-Wei Liu

Abstract
We study the ratio of R = 2 ​ Γ ​ ( Ξ c 0 → Ξ − ​ e + ​ ν e ) / 3 ​ Γ ​ ( Λ c + → Λ ​ e + ​ ν e ) R=2\Gamma(\Xi_{c}^{0}\to\Xi^{-}e^{+}\nu_{e})/3\Gamma(\Lambda_{c}^{+}\to\Lambda e^{+}\nu_{e}) , which is found to be R = 1 ​ ( 0.8 ) R=1~(0.8) from the exact (broken) S ​ U ​ ( 3 ) SU(3) flavor symmetry, in sharp contrast to the average value of R a ​ v = 0.59 ± 0.10 R_{av}=0.59\pm 0.10 from the ALICE collaboration and lattice QCD results. We propose to use the mixing of Ξ c − Ξ c ′ \Xi_{c}-\Xi_{c}^{\prime} to resolve the puzzle. From the mass spectra, we find that the mixing angle is | θ c | = 0.137 ​ ( 5 ) ​ π |\theta_{c}|=0.137(5)\pi , which suppresses Ξ c → Ξ ​ e + ​ ν e \Xi_{c}\to\Xi e^{+}\nu_{e} about 20 % 20\% model-independently, resulting in R ≈ 0.6 R\approx 0.6 with the S ​ U ​ ( 3 ) SU(3) flavor breaking effect. We explicitly demonstrate that R = 0.70 ± 0.09 R=0.70\pm 0.09 from the bag model, which is also consistent with R a ​ v R_{av} . To test the mixing mechanism, we recommend the experiments to measure the decays of Ξ c → Ξ ′ ​ ( 1530 ) ​ e + ​ ν e \Xi_{c}\to\Xi^{\prime}(1530)e^{+}\nu_{e} , whose branching fractions are determined to be ( 4.4 ∼ 8.7 ) × 10 − 3 (4.4\sim 8.7)\times 10^{-3} and ( 1.3 ∼ 2.6 ) % (1.3\sim 2.6)\% for Ξ c 0 \Xi_{c}^{0} and Ξ c + \Xi_{c}^{+} , respectively, but vanish without the mixing. In addition, nonvanishing values of ℬ ⁡ ( Ξ c + → Ξ ′ 0 ​ ( 1530 ) ​ π + ) {\cal B}(\Xi_{c}^{+}\to\Xi^{\prime 0}(1530)\pi^{+}) and ℬ ⁡ ( Ξ c + → Σ ′ ⁣ + ​ ( 1385 ) ​ K ¯ 0 ) {\cal B}(\Xi_{c}^{+}\to\Sigma^{\prime+}(1385)\overline{K}^{0}) will also be evidences of the mixing based on the Körner-Pati-Woo theorem, which are calculated as ( 3.8 ∼ 7.5 ) × 10 − 3 (3.8\sim 7.5)\times 10^{-3} and ( 6.6 ∼ 13 ) × 10 − 4 (6.6\sim 13)\times 10^{-4} , respectively. We emphasize that θ c \theta_{c} is sizable and should be given serious considerations in future studies on the heavy baryon systems.

which do not exist without the mixing. In this work, we take

V
conclusion

We have proposed the existence of the
\Xi_{c}-\Xi_{c}^{\prime}
mixing to resolve the tension between the experimental measurements and theoretical expectations in
\Xi_{c}^{0}\to\Xi_{c}^{-}e^{+}\nu_{e}
.
We have analyzed the
\Xi_{Q}-\Xi_{Q}^{\prime}
mixings from the mass spectra and obtained
|\theta_{c}|=0.137(5)\pi
and
|\theta_{b}|=0.049(13)\pi
.
With
\theta_{c}=-0.137\pi
,
we have found that
{\cal B}(\Xi^{0}_{c}\to\Xi^{-}e^{+}\nu_{e})=(3.02\pm 0.58)\%
and
{\cal B}(\Xi^{+}_{c}\to\Xi^{0}e^{+}\nu_{e})=(9.00\pm 1.74)\%
, which are consistent with the results by the ALICE collaboration
ALICE:2021bli
and LQCD
Lattice
. We have shown that
R(\theta_{c}=-0.137\pi)=0.70\pm 0.09
and
R(\theta_{c}=-0.2\pi)=0.57\pm 0.08
, which are consistent with
R_{av}^{(\prime)}=0.59\pm 0.10~(0.46\pm 0.07)
from the experiments
ALICE:2021bli
;
Belle:2021crz
and LQCD
Lattice
, and successfully resolve the puzzle in
\Xi_{c}^{0}\to\Xi^{-}e^{+}\nu_{e}
.

We recommend the future experiments to measure
\Xi_{c}\to\Xi^{\prime}e^{+}\nu_{e}
, of which the branching fractions for
\Xi_{c}^{0}
and
\Xi_{c}^{+}
have been evaluated to be
(4.4\pm 0.5)\times 10^{-3}
and
(1.3\pm 0.2)\%
with
|\theta_{c}|=0.137\pi
, and
(8.7\pm 1.0)\times 10^{-3}
and
(2.6\pm 0.4)\%
with
|\theta_{c}|=0.2\pi
, respectively.
A nonvanishing branching fraction of these channels in experiments will be a smoking gun of the
\Xi_{c}-\Xi_{c}^{\prime}
mixing. In addition, the nonleptonic decays of
\Xi_{c}^{+}\to\Xi^{\prime}\pi^{+}
and
\Xi_{c}^{+}\to\Sigma^{\prime+}\overline{K}^{0}
are forbidden without the mixing also.
We have shown that
{\cal B}(\Xi_{c}^{+}\to\Xi^{\prime 0}\pi^{+})=(3.8\pm 0.5)\times 10^{-3}
and
{\cal B}(\Xi_{c}^{+}\to\Sigma^{\prime+}\overline{K}^{0})=(6.6\pm 1.0)\times 10^{-4}
with
|\theta_{c}|=0.137\pi
, and

{\cal B}(\Xi_{c}^{+}\to\Xi^{\prime 0}\pi^{+})=(7.5\pm 1.0)\times 10^{-3}
and
{\cal B}(\Xi_{c}^{+}\to\Sigma^{\prime+}\overline{K}^{0})=(1.3\pm 0.2)\times 10^{-3}
with
|\theta_{c}|=0.2\pi
.
These decay channels are accessible at Belle, Belle II, and LHCb. It is worth to note that the Belle collaboration has already observed signals of
\Xi_{c}^{+}\to(\Xi^{-}\pi^{+}\pi^{+},\Lambda K_{S}^{0}\pi^{+})

Belle:2016lhy
, which can come from the cascade decays of
\Xi_{c}^{+}\to(\Xi^{\prime 0}\pi^{+},\Sigma^{\prime+}\overline{K}^{0})
, respectively.

We emphasize that the mixing is sizable and shall be considered seriously in the studies of the charmed baryons. In particular, most of the
SU(3)_{F}
relations in the literature are broken
Xic0
, and revisitations are clearly required.
Finally, we remark that although the mixing of
\Xi_{b}-\Xi_{b}^{\prime}
is found to be small, its effects should be also explored in the future studies.
