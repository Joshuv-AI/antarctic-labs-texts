On the number of terms of some families of the ternary cyclotomic polynomials Φ 3 ​ p 2 ​ p 3 \Phi_{3p_{2}p_{3}}
Ala’a Al-Kateeb, Afnan Dagher

Abstract
We study the number of non-zero terms in two specific families of ternary cyclotomic polynomial, we find formulas for the number of terms by writing the cyclotomic polynomial as a sum of smaller sub-polynomials and study the properties of these polynomial.



Key Words
: Number of terms, Cyclotomic polynomials.

The investigation on maximum gap (maximum difference between the consecutive exponents) became a problem on its own because it could be viewed as a first step

Theorem 1
.

Let
p_{1}<p_{2}<p_{3}

{\mathrm{hw}}(\Phi_{p_{1}p_{2}p_{3}})=\begin{cases}N(p_{3}-1)+1,&r_{3}=1\\
N(p_{3}+1)-1,&r_{3}=p_{2}-1\\

Preliminaries and Notations

3.1
Partition of Cyclotomic polynomials

In
[
21
,
20
]
, a partition of cyclotomic polynomials was introduced, also the following properties were given. This partition can be used to simplify studying several properties of cyclotomic polynomial

Notation 1

(Partition)
.

Let

\displaystyle\Phi_{mp}\left(x\right)

\displaystyle=\sum_{i\geq 0}f_{m,p,i}\left(x\right)\ x^{ip}

\displaystyle\text{where }\deg f_{m,p,i}\left(x\right)<p

\displaystyle f_{m,p,i}\left(x\right)

\displaystyle=\sum_{j\geq 0}f_{m,p,i,j}\left(x\right)x^{jm}

\displaystyle\text{where }\deg f_{m,p,i,j}\left(x\right)<m

Notation 2

(Operation)
.

For a polynomial
f
of degree less than
m
, let

1.

\mathcal{T}_{s}f={\mathrm{rem}}(f,x^{s})

“Truncate”

2.

\mathcal{F}f=x^{m-1}f\left(x\newline
^{-1}\right)

“Flip”

3.

\mathcal{R}_{s}f={\mathrm{rem}}(x^{m-\operatorname*{rem}\left(s,m\right)}f,x^{m}-1)
“Rotate”

4.

\mathcal{E}_{s}f=f(x^{\operatorname*{rem}\left(s,m\right)})

“Expand”

Throughout this paper, for an integer
m
and a prime
p
, we denote

r:=\mathrm{rem}\left(p,m\right),\;\;q:=\mathrm{quo}(p,m)

where
\mathrm{quo,}
of course, stand for quotient.
The formula of the sub-polynomials
f_{m,p,i,j}
is given by the following theorem.

Theorem 4

(Block)
.

For
0\leq i\leq\varphi(m)-1
and
0\leq j\leq q
,

f_{m,p,i,j}=\begin{cases}-\mathcal{R}_{ir}(\Psi_{m}\cdot\mathcal{E}_{r}\mathcal{T}_{i+1}\Phi_{m})&0\leq j\leq q-1\\
~~\mathcal{T}_{r}f_{m,p,i,0}&j=q\end{cases}

where
\Psi_{m}\left(x\right)=\frac{x^{m}-1}{\Phi_{m}\left(x\right)}
,
the
m
-th inverse cyclotomic polynomial.

For more information and properties of
\Psi_{m}(x)
see
[
22
]
.

Notation 3
.

We will also use the following notations

\Phi_{m}=\sum_{s\geq 0}a_{s}x^{s}\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \Psi_{m}=\sum_{t\geq 0}b_{t}x^{t}\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ f_{m,p,i,0}=\sum_{k=0}^{m-1}c_{k}x^{k}

Lemma 1

(Explicit expression for blocks)
.

Let
p>m
. Let

r=\operatorname*{rem}\left(p,m\right)
. Then the blocks
f_{m,p,i,j}
can
be written explicitly as in

1.

f_{m,p,i,j}=\left\{\begin{array}[c]{ll}-\sum\limits_{s=0}^{i}a_{s}\mathcal{R}_{\left(i-s\right)r}\Psi_{m}&\text{if }j<q\\
\mathcal{T}_{r}f_{m,p,i,0}&\text{if }j=q\end{array}\right.

2.

c_{k}=-\sum\limits_{s=0}^{i}a_{s}b_{\operatorname*{rem}\left(k+\left(i-s\right)r,m\right)}

Proof.

1.

Note

\Phi_{mp}=\frac{\Phi_{m}\left(x^{p}\right)}{\Phi_{m}\left(x\right)}=-\Phi_{m}\left(x^{p}\right)\ \Psi_{m}\ \frac{1}{1-x^{m}}=-\ \Phi_{m}\left(x^{p}\right)\ \Psi_{m}\ \sum_{u\geq 0}x^{um}=\sum_{s\geq 0}x^{sp}\ \left(-\ a_{s}\Psi_{m}\ \sum_{u\geq 0}x^{um}\right)

Thus
\Phi_{mp}
is the sum of weighted-shifted
\Psi_{m}

)(2.5,0)2

The claim is immediate from the

2.

Note

f_{m,p,i,0}=-\sum\limits_{s=0}^{i}a_{s}\mathcal{R}_{\left(i-s\right)r}\sum_{k\geq 0}b_{k}x^{k}=-\sum\limits_{s=0}^{i}a_{s}\sum_{k\geq 0}b_{\operatorname*{rem}\left(k+\left(i-s\right)r,m\right)}x^{k}=\sum_{k\geq 0}\left(-\sum\limits_{s=0}^{i}a_{s}b_{\operatorname*{rem}\left(k+\left(i-s\right)r,m\right)}\right)x^{k}

Theorem 5

(Intra-Structure)
.

Within a cyclotomic polynomial, we have

1.

(Repetition)
f_{m,p,i,0}=\cdots=f_{m,p,i,q-1}

2.

(Truncation)
f_{m,p,i,q}=\mathcal{T}_{r}f_{m,p,i,0}

3.

(Symmetry)
f_{m,p,i^{\prime},0}=\mathcal{R}_{\varphi(m)-1-r}\mathcal{F}f_{m,p,i,0}
if
i^{\prime}+i=\varphi(m)-1

Theorem 6

(Inter-Structure)
.

Among cyclotomic polynomials, we have

1.

(Invariance)
f_{m,\tilde{p},i,0}=~~f_{m,p,i,0}

if
\tilde{p}-p\equiv 0\mod m

2.

(Semi-Invariance)
f_{m,\tilde{p},i,0}=-\mathcal{R}_{\varphi(m)-1}\mathcal{F}f_{m,p,i,0}\;\;
if
\tilde{p}+p\equiv 0\mod m

As an application of the properties above we have the following theorem.

Theorem 7

(Hamming weight)
.

Let
{\mathrm{hw}}(f)
stands for the number of non-zero terms in the polynomial
f
. Then we have

1.

{[Linear]}

\ \ \mathrm{hw}(\Phi_{mp})=A\cdot p+B

2.

{[Parallel]}

\ \ \mathrm{hw}(\Phi_{m\tilde{p}})=A\cdot\tilde{p}-B

where
p+\tilde{p}\equiv 0\mod m
.

]

\displaystyle=2q_{3}\left(\sum_{u=0}^{\frac{q_{2}-1}{2}}(24u+22)\right)~~+4q_{2}+3

by Lemma

and

12

\displaystyle+2q_{3}\left(\sum_{u=\frac{q_{2}+1}{2}}^{q_{2}-1}(12u+6q_{2}+17)\right)+2q_{3}(2p_{2}+1)

\displaystyle=2q_{3}(\frac{21}{2}q_{2}^{2}+\frac{21}{2}q_{2}+1)+2q_{3}(2p_{2}+1)+4q_{2}+3

\displaystyle=21q_{2}q_{3}(q_{2}+1)+2q_{3}(2p_{2}+2)+4q_{2}+3
