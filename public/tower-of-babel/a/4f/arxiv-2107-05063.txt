Equidistribution of torsion points in abelian varieties
Jiyao Tang

Abstract
Let A A be an abelian variety over a field K K with a complete non-Archimedean norm. We prove that the torsion points of A A are equidistributed over A an A^{\mathrm{an}} with respect to the canonical measure.
