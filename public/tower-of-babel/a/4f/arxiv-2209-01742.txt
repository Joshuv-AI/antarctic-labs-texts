On products of beta and gamma elements in the homotpy of the first Smith-Toda spectrum
Katsumi Shimomura, Mao-no-suke Shimomura

Abstract
. In this paper, we determine the first cohomology of the monochromatic comodule M 2 1 M^{1}_{2} at an odd prime, and apply the results to show non-trivialities of some products of beta and gamma elements in the homotopy groups of the Smith-Toda spectrum V ⁡ ( 1 ) V(1) . The cohomology for a prime greater than three was determined by the first author [ 10 ] . Here, we verify them and determine the cohomology at the prime 3 by elementary calculation. The cohomology will be a stepping stone for computing the cohomology of the monochromatic comodule M 0 3 M^{3}_{0} , which we hope to determine for a long time.
