On characteristic ideal of Selmer group associated to Artin representations
Dipramit Majumdar & Subhasis Panda

Abstract
. Selmer group for an Artin representation over totally real fields was studied by Greenberg [ Gr1 , Gr4 ] and Vatsal [ Va ] . In this paper we study the Selmer groups for an Artin representation over a totally complex field. We establish an algebraic function of the characteristic ideal of the Selmer group associated to Artin representation over the cyclotomic ℤ p {\mathbb{Z}}_{p} - extension of the rational numbers under certain mild hypotheses and construct several examples to illustrate our result. We also prove that in this situation μ \mu -invariant of the dual Selmer group is independent of the choice of the lattice.



Key words:
Selmer Group, Iwasawa theory of Artin representation.

1.
Introduction

The study of Iwasawa theory of Artin representation of
\Delta:=\mathrm{Gal}(K/{\mathbb{Q}})
, where
K
is a totally real Galois extension of
{\mathbb{Q}}
, was pioneered by the work of Greenberg
[
Gr1
,
Gr4
]
. On the other hand, if
K
is totally complex, the study of Selmer groups associated to Artin representation becomes more subtle. In a recent work, Greenberg-Vatsal
[
GV2
]
, studied Iwasawa theory of Artin representation of
\mathrm{Gal}(K/{\mathbb{Q}})
, where
K
is a totally complex Galois extension of
{\mathbb{Q}}
, under certain
Hypothesis A
, which we explain as follows.

We fix an odd prime
p
. Let
K
be a finite Galois extension of
{\mathbb{Q}}
such that
p\nmid[K:{\mathbb{Q}}]
. If
\mu_{p}\not\subset K
, we can take
K^{\prime}=K(\mu_{p})
, then

is Galois over
{\mathbb{Q}}
and
p\nmid[K^{\prime}:{\mathbb{Q}}]
, here
\mu_{p}
denote the set of
p
-th roots of unity. As a consequence, from now on, we assume that
\mu_{p}\subset K
. We remark that the theory becomes quite complicated if
p\mid[K:{\mathbb{Q}}]
. The authors hope to study that situation in the future.

We fix an embedding
\iota_{\infty}
of a fixed algebraic closure
{\overline{\mathbb{Q}}}
of
{\mathbb{Q}}
inside
{\mathbb{C}}
and for each prime
l
an embedding
\iota_{l}
of
{\overline{\mathbb{Q}}}
into a fixed algebraic closure
{\overline{\mathbb{Q}}}_{l}
of
{\mathbb{Q}}_{l}
.

By an Artin representation of
\Delta
, we denote a finite dimensional irreducible representation
\rho:\Delta=\mathrm{Gal}(K/{\mathbb{Q}})\to\mathrm{Gl}_{\mathcal{F}}(V)
, here
{\mathcal{F}}
is a finite extension of
{\mathbb{Q}}_{p}
and
V
is a
{\mathcal{F}}
-vector space of dimension
d(\rho)
. Let
{\mathcal{O}}
denote the ring of integer of
{\mathcal{F}}
and
\pi
denote an uniformizer of
{\mathcal{O}}
.

For an Archimedian place
\nu
of
K
, let
K_{\nu}
denote the completion of
K
at
\nu
, which can be identified with
{\mathbb{R}}
or
{\mathbb{C}}
. By
\Delta_{\nu}
, we denote the Galois group
\mathrm{Gal}(K_{\nu}/{\mathbb{R}})
, which we identify with a subgroup of
\Delta
. Let
d_{\nu}^{+}(\rho)
denote the multiplicity of the trivial representation of
\Delta_{\nu}
in
\rho|_{\Delta_{\nu}}
.
Since
K
is Galois over
{\mathbb{Q}}
,

is either totally real or totally complex. If
K
is totally real field, then

, as
\mathrm{Gal}(K_{\nu}/{\mathbb{R}})=\{1\}

If

is totally complex and
\nu

are two different places of

and
\Delta_{\nu^{\prime}}

\rho|_{\Delta_{\nu}}
and

is same and

is independent of the choice of
\nu
. Hence simply denote
d_{\nu}^{+}(\rho)

.
Further, we define
d^{-}(\rho):=d(\rho)-d^{+}(\rho)

Greenberg and Vatsal
[
GV2
]
, studied the situation when

. Note that

is totally real implies that

, which was studied in
[
Gr1
,
Gr4
]

\rho

is a non-trivial representation.

Observe that the embedding
\iota_{p}
induces a prime in

above

\wp
in
K
above

. Assume that

has a

of dimension

such that

and
V_{\wp}^{-}:=V/V_{\wp}^{+}

To summarize, we have assumed the following hypothesis on the triple
(\rho,V,V_{\wp}^{+}):

HYP 1:

{\mathbb{Q}}(\mu_{p})\subset K

HYP 2:

The representation

is irreducible of

a finite extension of

3.

Lemma 4.3
.

Assume that

. Let
\varphi:\Gamma\to{\mathcal{O}}^{*}
be any continuous group homomorphism. For any

, let

be a finite place in

. Then under the duality between

and

First, we consider the case where
v_{n}\nmid p.
Recall that

.

(3)

5.

T
, provided that the Artin representation satisfies a mild hypothesis. For elliptic curves, Mazur
[

\mu

[

PR1
]

[

[

Let

-(\mu(H^{1}_{\text{unr}}(G_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}},A_{\wp}^{-}))-\mu(H^{1}_{\text{unr}}(G_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}},{(A_{1})}_{\wp}^{-}))).&\end{split}

(22)

First, we will compute the value of
\mu(H^{1}(G_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}},A))-\mu(H^{1}(G_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}},A_{1}))
. Following the same method as before, one can show that
H^{1}(G_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}},\Phi)
and
H^{2}(G_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}},\Phi)
are cofinitely generated as
\widetilde{\Lambda}_{{\mathcal{O}}}
-modules and the value of
\mu(H^{1}(G_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}},A))-\mu(H^{1}(G_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}},A_{1}))
is same as
\text{corank}_{\widetilde{\Lambda}_{{\mathcal{O}}}}H^{1}(G_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}},\Phi)-\text{corank}_{\widetilde{\Lambda}_{{\mathcal{O}}}}H^{2}(G_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}},\Phi)
.

By inflation-restriction exact sequence, we obtain

0\to H^{1}(\Gamma_{n,{\mathfrak{p}}},\Phi^{G_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}}})\to H^{1}(G_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{n}},\Phi)\to H^{1}(G_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}},\Phi)^{\Gamma_{n,{\mathfrak{p}}}}\to H^{2}(\Gamma_{n,{\mathfrak{p}}},\Phi^{G_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}}}),

where
\Gamma_{n,{\mathfrak{p}}}=G_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{n}}/G_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}}
. As
\Phi^{G_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}}}=0
, we obtain
\text{dim}_{k}(H^{1}(G_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}},\Phi)^{\Gamma_{n,{\mathfrak{p}}}})=\text{dim}_{k}(H^{1}(G_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{n}},\Phi))
.
The
k
-dimension of
H^{2}(G_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{n}},\Phi)
stabilize as
n
varies. Local Euler-Poincare formula yields

\text{dim}_{k}(H^{1}(G_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}},\Phi)^{\Gamma_{n,{\mathfrak{p}}}})=\text{dim}_{k}(H^{1}(G_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{n}},\Phi))=d(\Phi)[{\mathbb{Q}}_{n,{\mathfrak{p}}_{n}}:{\mathbb{Q}}_{p}]+O(1).

Using equation (
18
), we get that
\text{corank}_{\widetilde{\Lambda}_{{\mathcal{O}}}}(H^{1}(G_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}},\Phi))=d(\Phi)
.

From Hochschild-Serre spectral sequence, we get obtain the restriction map
H^{2}(G_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{n}},\Phi)\to H^{2}(G_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}},\Phi)^{\Gamma_{n,{\mathfrak{p}}}}
is surjective. As the order of the group
H^{2}(G_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{n}},\Phi)
stabilizes as
n
varies by using equation (
18
), we obtain
\text{corank}_{\widetilde{\Lambda}_{{\mathcal{O}}}}(H^{2}(G_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}},\Phi))=0
.
Hence,
\mu(H^{1}(G_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}},A))-\mu(H^{1}(G_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}},A_{1}))=d(\Phi)
.

Similarly one can show that
\mu(H^{1}(G_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}},A_{\wp}^{+}))-\mu(H^{1}(G_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}},(A_{1})_{\wp}^{+}))=d^{+}(\Phi)
. As a consequence, equation (
22
) becomes

\mu(H^{1}_{\text{unr}}(G_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}},A_{\wp}^{-}))-\mu(H^{1}_{\text{unr}}(G_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}},{(A_{1})}_{\wp}^{-}))=0.

(23)

Recall that
H^{1}_{\text{unr}}(G_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}},A_{\wp}^{-})=\mathrm{Ker}(H^{1}(G_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}},A_{\wp}^{-})\to H^{1}(I_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}},A_{\wp}^{-}))
. Since
G_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}}/I_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}}
is topologically cyclic, we obtain

0\to H^{1}(G_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}}/I_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}},(A_{\wp}^{-})^{I_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}}})\to H^{1}(G_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}},A_{\wp}^{-})\to H^{1}(I_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}},A_{\wp}^{-})\to 0

and moreover
\text{corank}(H^{1}(G_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}}/I_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}},(A_{\wp}^{-})^{I_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}}}))=\text{corank}(H^{0}(G_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}}/I_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}},(A_{\wp}^{-})^{I_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}}})
.
By our assumption
H^{0}(\Delta_{\wp},V)=0
and Lemma
3.1
, we have
H^{0}(G_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}},V_{\wp}^{-})=0
. Since the order of
\Delta
is co-prime to
p
, therefore
H^{0}(G_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}},V_{\wp}^{-})=0
implies that
H^{0}(G_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}}/I_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}},(A_{\wp}^{-})^{I_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}}})=(A_{\wp}^{-})^{G_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}}}=0
. It follows that
\text{corank}(H^{1}(G_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}}/I_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}},(A_{\wp}^{-})^{I_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}}}))=0
and therefore
\mu(H^{1}_{\text{unr}}(G_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}},A_{\wp}^{-}))=0
.
A similar argument shows that
\mu(H^{1}_{\text{unr}}(G_{{\mathfrak{p}}_{\infty}},{(A_{1})}_{\wp}^{-}))=0
.

∎

In this section, we construct an algebraic functional equation relating characteristic ideals of the dual Selmer groups (provided it is torsion) associated to Artin representations

and

Proposition 6.1

(
Generalised Cassels-Tate pairing of Flach).

Let
(\rho,V,V_{\wp}^{+})
be a triple which satisfies
HYP 1 & 2
. Assume that

. Let
\varphi:\Gamma\to{\mathcal{O}}^{*}
be a continuous group homomorphism. Then for all

, we have a perfect pairing

<,>:\frac{Sel^{g}_{{\mathbb{Q}}_{n}}(A,{A}_{\wp}^{+},\varphi^{-1})}{Sel^{g}_{{\mathbb{Q}}_{n}}(A,{A}_{\wp}^{+},\varphi^{-1})_{\mathrm{div}}}\times\frac{Sel^{g}_{{\mathbb{Q}}_{n}}(A^{*},{(A^{*})}_{\wp}^{+},\varphi)}{Sel^{g}_{{\mathbb{Q}}_{n}}(A^{*},{(A^{*})}_{\wp}^{+},\varphi)_{\mathrm{div}}}\longrightarrow{\mathbb{Q}}_{p}/{\mathbb{Z}}_{p}.

In particular, if the Selmer groups
Sel^{g}_{{\mathbb{Q}}_{n}}(A,{A}_{\wp}^{+},\varphi^{-1})
and
Sel^{g}_{{\mathbb{Q}}_{n}}(A^{*},{(A^{*})}_{\wp}^{+},\varphi)
are finite, then
Sel^{g}_{{\mathbb{Q}}_{n}}(A^{*},{(A^{*})}_{\wp}^{+},\varphi)
is the Pontryagin dual of
Sel^{g}_{{\mathbb{Q}}_{n}}(A,{A}_{\wp}^{+},\varphi^{-1})
under the above paring.

Proof.

From Lemma
4.3
, we obtained that
H^{1}_{g}(G_{v_{n}},V(\varphi^{-1})^{*})
is the orthogonal complement of
H^{1}_{g}(G_{v_{n}},V(\varphi^{-1}))
under the local Tate pairing

H^{1}(G_{v_{n}},V(\varphi^{-1}))\times H^{1}(G_{v_{n}},V(\varphi^{-1})^{*})\longrightarrow{\mathbb{Q}}_{p}/{\mathbb{Z}}_{p},

for each place

of
{\mathbb{Q}}_{n}
such that

. Then from the generalized Cassels-Tate pairing of Flach
[

, Theorem 1]
, we obtain a perfect pairing

<,>:\frac{Sel^{g}_{{\mathbb{Q}}_{n}}(A,{A}_{\wp}^{+},\varphi^{-1})}{Sel^{g}_{{\mathbb{Q}}_{n}}(A,{A}_{\wp}^{+},\varphi^{-1})_{\mathrm{div}}}\times\frac{Sel^{g}_{{\mathbb{Q}}_{n}}(A^{*},{(A^{*})}_{\wp}^{+},\varphi)}{Sel^{g}_{{\mathbb{Q}}_{n}}(A^{*},{(A^{*})}_{\wp}^{+},\varphi)_{\mathrm{div}}}\longrightarrow{\mathbb{Q}}_{p}/{\mathbb{Z}}_{p}.

(24)

If
Sel^{g}_{{\mathbb{Q}}_{n}}(A,{A}_{\wp}^{+},\varphi^{-1})
and
Sel^{g}_{{\mathbb{Q}}_{n}}(A^{*},{(A^{*})}_{\wp}^{+},\varphi)
are finite, then
Sel^{g}_{{\mathbb{Q}}_{n}}(A,{A}_{\wp}^{+},\varphi^{-1})_{\mathrm{div}}
and
Sel^{g}_{{\mathbb{Q}}_{n}}(A^{*},{(A^{*})}_{\wp}^{+},\varphi)_{\mathrm{div}}
are zero. Hence the pairing in equation (

and
