Expansion formulas for multiple basic hypergeometric series over root systems
Gaurav Bhatnagar, Surbhi Rai

Abstract
. We extend expansion formulas of Liu given in 2013 to the context of multiple series over root systems. Liu and others have shown the usefulness of these formulas in Special Functions and number-theoretic contexts. We extend Wang and Ma’s generalizations of Liu’s work which they obtained using q q -Lagrange inversion. We use the A n A_{n} and C n C_{n} Bailey transformation and other summation theorems due to Gustafson, Milne, Milne and Lilly, and others, from the theory of A n A_{n} , C n C_{n} and D n D_{n} basic hypergeometric series.

It is our intent here to provide several infinite families of extensions of Liu’s key formulas to multiple basic hypergeometric series over root systems.

But first we give an exposition of Liu’s key formulas, and their generalization by Jin Wang and Xinrong Ma

{\left({A};q\right)_{0}}:=1

Observe that for

In what follows, we drop the ‘base’

We refer the reader to

Liu’s main expansion formula

\frac{{\left({\alpha q,\alpha ab/q}\right)_{\infty}}}{{\left({\alpha a,\alpha b}\right)_{\infty}}}f(\alpha a)=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{(1-\alpha q^{2k}){\left({\alpha,q/a}\right)_{k}}}{(1-\alpha){\left({q,\alpha a}\right)_{k}}}\Big(\frac{a}{q}\Big)^{k}\sum_{j=0}^{k}\frac{{\left({q^{-k},\alpha q^{k}}\right)_{j}}}{{\left({q,\alpha b}\right)_{j}}}q^{j}f(\alpha q^{j+1}).

\frac{{\left({\alpha q,\alpha ab/q}\right)_{\infty}}}{{\left({\alpha a,\alpha b}\right)_{\infty}}}\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}{\left({q/a}\right)_{j}}(\alpha a)^{j}A_{j}\\
=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{(1-\alpha q^{2k}){\left({\alpha,q/a,q/b}\right)_{k}}}{(1-\alpha){\left({q,\alpha a,\alpha b}\right)_{k}}}\Big(-\frac{\alpha ab}{q}\Big)^{k}q^{\binom{k}{2}}\sum_{j=0}^{k}\frac{{\left({q^{-k},\alpha q^{k}}\right)_{j}}}{{\left({q/b}\right)_{j}}}\Big(\frac{q^{2}}{b}\Big)^{j}A_{j}.

(1.2)

In addition to these two, a third key formula of Liu is mentioned below.

Our work is in the context of multiple basic hypergeometric series over root systems. This type of series has been developed systematically (though sporadically) over the last many years; see Schlosser
[
26

The goal of this paper is to lift Liu’s expansion formulas to basic hypergeometric series over root systems.

In fact, we have exceeded our goal: we have extended Wang and Ma’s

Further, their results are generalizations of Liu’s expansion formulas.
