A sharp convergence theorem for the mean curvature flow in spheres I
Dong Pu

Abstract
. In this paper, we prove a sharp convergence theorem for the mean curvature flow of arbitrary codimension in spheres which improves Baker’s convergence theorem. In particular, we obtain a new differentiable sphere theorem for submanifolds in spheres.

1.
Introduction

Let
M
be an
n
-dimensional compact submanifold isometrically
immersed in a Riemannian manifold
N^{n+p}
. Denoted by
F_{0}
the
isometric immersion. We consider the one-parameter family

F_{t}=F(\cdot,t)
of immersions
F_{t}:M\rightarrow N^{n+p}
with
corresponding images
M_{t}=F_{t}(M)
which satisfies

(1.1)

\left\{\begin{array}[]{l}\frac{\partial}{\partial t}F(x,t)=H(x,t),\\
F(x,0)=F_{0}(x),\end{array}\right.

where
H(x,t)
is the mean curvature vector of
M_{t}
. We call

F_{t}:M\rightarrow N^{n+p}
the mean curvature flow with initial
value
F_{0}
. In 1980’s, Huisken proved the convergence theorems for
the mean curvature flow of hypersurfaces under certain conditions in
a series of papers.
For the initial hypersurfaces satisfying the convexity condition, Huisken
[
4
,
5
]
proved that the
solution of the mean curvature flow converges to a point as the time
approaches the finite maximal time. Motivated by the rigidity theorem for hypersurfaces with constant mean curvature in spheres due to Okumura
[
14
]
, Huisken
[
6
]

proved the convergence theorem for the mean curvature flow of hypersurfaces under the curvature pinching condition for

|H|^{2}
and the squared norm of the second fundamental form
|A|^{2}
in the
sphere
\mathbb{S}^{n+1}
.

Theorem A
.

Let
F_{0}:M^{n}\rightarrow\mathbb{S}^{n+1}(\frac{1}{\sqrt{\bar{K}}})
be a smooth compact
hypersurface in a sphere with constant curvature
\bar{K}
.
Assume
M
satisfies

(1.2)

|A|^{2}<\begin{cases}\frac{3}{4}|H|^{2}+\frac{4}{3}\bar{K},\ &n=2,\\
\frac{1}{n-1}|H|^{2}+2\bar{K},\ &n\geq 3.\end{cases}

Then
the mean curvature flow with the initial value
F_{0}

either converges to a round point in finite time, or
converges to a total geodesic sphere of
\mathbb{S}^{n+1}(\frac{1}{\sqrt{\bar{K}}})
as

t\rightarrow\infty
.

For the mean curvature flow of arbitrary codimension in
the Euclidean space, Andrews and Baker
[
1
]
investigated the
convergence problem. In
[
2
]
, Baker studied the mean curvature flow of arbitrary codimension in spheres and obtained the following result.

Theorem B
.

Let
F_{0}:M^{n}\rightarrow\mathbb{S}^{n+p}(\frac{1}{\sqrt{\bar{K}}})
be a smooth compact
submanifold in a sphere with constant curvature
\bar{K}
.
Assume
M
satisfies

(1.3)

|A|^{2}\leq\begin{cases}\frac{4}{3n}|H|^{2}+\frac{2(n-1)}{3}\bar{K},\ &n=2,3,\\
\frac{1}{n-1}|H|^{2}+2\bar{K},\ &n\geq 4.\end{cases}

Then
the mean curvature flow with the initial value
F_{0}

either converges to a round point in finite time, or
converges to a total geodesic sphere of
\mathbb{S}^{n+p}(\frac{1}{\sqrt{\bar{K}}})
as

t\rightarrow\infty
.

For
n\geq 4
, the compact submanifold
M^{n}=\mathbb{S}^{1}(r)\times\mathbb{S}^{n-1}(s)\subset\mathbb{S}^{n+1}(1)\subset\mathbb{S}^{n+p}(1)
with
r^{2}+s^{2}=1
implies that the pinching conditions of Theorem
A
and Theorem
B
are sharp. In fact, the sharp pinching condition means the linear relationship of
|A|^{2}
,
|H|^{2}
and
\bar{K}
is sharp. If the relationship is not linear, the pinching condition can be improved. Afterwards, Lei-Xu
[
10
]
also obtained a sharp convergence theorem for mean curvature flow of high codimension in spheres. Set

(1.4)

\alpha(x)=n\bar{K}+\frac{n}{2(n-1)}x-\frac{n-2}{2(n-1)}\sqrt{x^{2}+4(n-1)\bar{K}x},

they proved the following theorem.

Theorem C
.

Let
F_{0}:M^{n}\rightarrow\mathbb{S}^{n+p}(\frac{1}{\sqrt{\bar{K}}})
be an n-dimensional
(n\geq 6)
smooth compact
submanifold in a sphere with constant curvature
\bar{K}
.
Assume
M
satisfies

(1.5)

|A|^{2}<\gamma(n,|H|,\bar{K}).

Then
the mean curvature flow with the initial value
F_{0}

either converges to a round point in finite time, or
converges to a total geodesic sphere of
\mathbb{S}^{n+p}(\frac{1}{\sqrt{\bar{K}}})
as

t\rightarrow\infty
.

Here
\gamma(n,|H|,\bar{K})
is an explicit positive scalar defined by

\gamma(n,|H|,\bar{K})=\min\{\alpha(|H|^{2}),\beta(|H|^{2})\},

where

\displaystyle\beta(x)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\alpha(x_{0})+\alpha^{\prime}(x_{0})(x-x_{0})+\frac{1}{2}\alpha^{\prime\prime}(x_{0})(x-x_{0})^{2},

\displaystyle x_{0}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{2n+2}{n-4}\sqrt{n-1}(\sqrt{n-1}-\frac{n-4}{2n+2})^{2}\bar{K}.

The scalar
\gamma(n,|H|,\bar{K})
in Theorem
C
satisfies the following: (i)
\gamma(n,|H|,\bar{K})>\frac{1}{n-1}|H|^{2}+2\bar{K}
; (ii)
\gamma(n,|H|,\bar{K})>\frac{7}{6}\sqrt{n-1}\bar{K}
; (iii)
\gamma(n,|H|,\bar{K})=\alpha(|H|^{2})
when
|H|^{2}\geq x_{0}
.

Lawson-Simons
[
8
]
proved the topological sphere theorem in the unite sphere under the pinching condition
|A|^{2}<2\sqrt{n-1}
. Applying the convergence results of Hamilton and
Brendle for Ricci flow and the Lawson-Simons formula for the
nonexistence of stable currents, Xu and Zhao
[
18
]
proved
a differentiable sphere theorem in spheres.

Theorem D
.

Let
M
be an
n
-dimensional (
n\geq 4
)
oriented complete submanifold in the unit sphere
\mathbb{S}^{n+q}
.
Then

(i)
if
n=4,5,6
and
\sup_{M}(|A|^{2}-2\sqrt{n-1})<0
, then
M
is diffeomorphic to
\mathbb{S}^{n}
;

(ii)
if
n\geq 7
and
|A|^{2}<2\sqrt{2}
, then
M
is
diffeomorphic to
\mathbb{S}^{n}
.

Motivated by the rigidity and topological sphere theorems for submanifolds in spheres, Lei-Xu proposed the conjecture that
\alpha(|H|^{2})
is the optimal pinching condition for mean curvature flow in spheres.

Conjecture 1
.

Let
M_{0}
be an n-dimensional complete submanifold in the sphere

\mathbb{S}^{n+p}(\frac{1}{\sqrt{\bar{K}}})
. Suppose that

\sup_{M_{0}}\big(|h|^{2}-\alpha(|H|^{2})\big)<0
. Then the mean
curvature flow with initial value
M_{0}
converges to a round point in
finite time, or converges to a totally geodesic sphere as
t\rightarrow\infty
. In particular, if
|A|^{2}<2\sqrt{n-1}\bar{K}
,
M_{0}
is diffeomorphic to
\mathbb{S}^{n}
.

For mean curvature flow of submanifolds in hyperbolic spaces, Liu-Xu-Ye-Zhao
[
12
]
proved the convergence theorem.
Recently, Lei-Xu
[
9
]
proved a convergence theorem of arbitrary codimension in hyperbolic spaces that the initial submanifold
M^{n}
of
dimension
n(\geq 6)
under the optimal pinching condition
|A|^{2}<\alpha(|H|^{2}).

Note that initial submanifolds in the almost all convergence
results implies the positive sectional curvatures. However, Lei-Xu’s convergence theorems imply
that the Ricci curvatures of the initial submanifolds are positive, but don’t imply
the positivity of the sectional curvatures. Therefore, their
convergence theorems also imply the differentiable sphere theorems
for submanifolds with positive Ricci curvatures. For other results and applications for the mean curvature flow, we refer the readers to
[
3
,
5
,
7
,
11
,
13
,
17
]
.

Motivated by above theorems, we investigate the submanifold
M^{n}

(n\geq 3,p=1
or
n\geq 4)
pinched by a sharp curvature pinching condition for the mean curvature flow of arbitrary codimension in the sphere
{S}^{n+p}(\frac{1}{\sqrt{\bar{K}}})
.
Putting

(1.6)

b(x)=(1-\delta)\big(\frac{x}{n-1}+2\bar{K}\big)+\delta\alpha(x),\ x\geq 0,

where
\delta=\frac{\sqrt{12n+9}-7}{2(n-2)},\ n=4,5,6.

The pinching condition
b(|H|^{2})
is obvious sharp and satisfies
b(|H|^{2})>\frac{|H|^{2}}{n-1}+2\bar{K}.
Then we prove the following sharp convergence theorem.

Theorem 1.1
.

Let
F_{0}:M\rightarrow\mathbb{S}^{n+p}(\frac{1}{\sqrt{\bar{K}}})
be an n-dimensional
(n\geq 4)
smooth compact submanifold immersed in the sphere. If
M
satisfies

(1.7)

|A|^{2}\leq\begin{cases}\ b(|H|^{2}),\ &n=4,5,6,\\
\\
\sqrt{\big(\frac{|H|^{2}}{n-1}+2\bar{K}\big)^{2}+(2n-4)\bar{K}^{2}},\ &n\geq 7.\end{cases}

then the mean curvature flow with the initial value
F_{0}
converges to a round point in finite time, or
converges to a total geodesic sphere of
\mathbb{S}^{n+p}(\frac{1}{\sqrt{\bar{K}}})
as

t\rightarrow\infty
.

Remark 1.2
.

(i) Consider the compact submanifold
M^{n}=\mathbb{S}^{1}(r)\times\mathbb{S}^{n-1}(s)\subset\mathbb{S}^{n+1}(1)\subset\mathbb{S}^{n+p}(1)
with
r^{2}+s^{2}=1
. We have
|A|^{4}-\big(\frac{|H|^{2}}{n-1}+2\big)^{2}-(2n-4)=\frac{(n-1)^{2}-1}{(n-1)^{2}}\cdot\frac{s^{4}}{r^{4}}
, which implies that the pinching condition is sharp.

(ii) Since
\sqrt{\big(\frac{|H|^{2}}{n-1}+2\bar{K}\big)^{2}+(2n-4)\bar{K}^{2}}>\frac{|H|^{2}}{n-1}+2\bar{K}
, this convergence theorem improves Theorem
A
and Theorem
B
. In fact, whatever the form of the pinching condition is, the relationship of
|A|^{2}
and
|H|^{2}
, i.e.,
\frac{1}{n-1}
is optimal. So this pinching condition largely improved the proportion of
\bar{K}
.

(iii) This convergence theorem implies that the Ricci curvatures of the initial submanifold is positive, but doesn’t imply
the positivity of the sectional curvature.

(iv) For
n\geq 3,p=1
, the mean curvature flow of hypersurfaces in the sphere convergence under the sharp condition

|A|^{2}\leq\sqrt{\Big(\frac{|H|^{2}}{n-1}+2\bar{K}\Big)^{2}+(2n-4)\bar{K}^{2}}.

In particularity, the convergence theorem implies a sharp differentiable sphere theorem in the sphere. Noting that
\big(\frac{|H|^{2}}{n-1}+2\big)^{2}+2n-4\geq 2n
, we obtain a new
differentiable sphere theorem which improves Theorem
D
.

Corollary 1.3
.

Let
M
be an
n
-dimensional (
n\geq 7
) smooth
compact submanifold in the unit sphere
\mathbb{S}^{n+q}
.
If
|A|^{2}\leq\sqrt{2n}
, then
M
is
diffeomorphic to
\mathbb{S}^{n}
.

\nabla_{u}v=(\bar{\nabla}_{u}v)^{\top}

and
|\nabla\mathring{A}|^{2}=|\nabla A|^{2}-\frac{|\nabla H|^{2}}{n}

Denote by
\nabla^{2}_{i,j}T=\nabla_{i}(\nabla_{j}T)-\nabla_{\nabla_{i}e_{j}}T
the second order covariant derivative of tensors.
Then the Laplacian of a tensor is defined by
\Delta T=\sum_{i}\nabla^{2}_{i,i}T
.

\displaystyle-\frac{2x\bar{K}}{n-1}+2n(n-4)\bar{K}^{2}.

∎

3.
Preservation of curvature pinching

In this section, we prove the curvature pinching condition preserves along the mean curvature flow in spheres. First we consider the submanifold
M^{n}(n\geq 7)
of arbitrary codimension in the sphere
{S}^{n+p}(\frac{1}{\sqrt{\bar{K}}})
. For the mean curvature flow of hypersurfaces
M^{n}(n\geq 3)
in spheres, the proof is similar. First, we prove the following inequality.

Lemma 3.1
.

For
x\geq 0
, the following inequality holds.

\displaystyle a(\mathring{a}-x\mathring{a}^{\prime})-n\bar{K}(\mathring{a}+x\mathring{a}^{\prime})+P_{2}\left(2\mathring{a}-\frac{x}{n}+x\mathring{a}^{\prime}\right)-\frac{3}{2}P^{2}_{2}<0.

Proof.

Without loss of generality, we assume that
\bar{K}=1
. Its discriminant satisfies

(3.1)

\displaystyle\Delta=

\displaystyle\left(2\mathring{a}-\frac{x}{n}+x\mathring{a}^{\prime}\right)-\sqrt{6\big(n(\mathring{a}+x\mathring{a}^{\prime})-a(\mathring{a}-x\mathring{a}^{\prime})\big)}.

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{3x^{2}+10(n-1)x+4n(n-1)^{2}}{(n-1)\sqrt{x^{2}+4(n-1)x+2n(n-1)^{2}}}-\frac{4x}{n}

\displaystyle-\sqrt{\frac{12n}{n-1}\bigg(\frac{x^{2}+3(n-1)x+n(n-1)^{2}}{\sqrt{x^{2}+4(n-1)x+2n(n-1)^{2}}}-x-(n-1)\bigg)}.

From Lemma
2.3
(iv),(v), we need to prove the following inequality holds.

(3.2)

\displaystyle\Big(x+\sqrt{2+\sqrt{2n}}(n-1)\Big)\Big(2\sqrt{2n}-\frac{(n-4)x}{n(n-1)}-\frac{6(\sqrt{2n}-2)x}{3x+2\sqrt{2n}(n-1)}\Big)

\displaystyle<

\displaystyle 2\sqrt{3n(n-2)}(n-1).

(3.3)

\displaystyle\Big(x+\sqrt{2+\sqrt{2n}}(n-1)\Big)\Big(2\sqrt{2n}-\frac{(n-4)x}{n(n-1)}-\frac{6(\sqrt{2n}-2)x}{3x+2\sqrt{2n}(n-1)}\Big)

\displaystyle<

\displaystyle\Big(x+\sqrt{2+\sqrt{2n}}(n-1)\Big)\Big(2\sqrt{2n}-\frac{(n-4)x}{n(n-1)}\Big)

\displaystyle-2(\sqrt{2n}-2)x\cdot\frac{3\sqrt{2+\sqrt{2n}}}{2\sqrt{2n}}

\displaystyle<

\displaystyle 2\sqrt{3n(n-2)}(n-1).

The last inequality above is equivalent to

(3.4)

\displaystyle\Leftrightarrow

\displaystyle-\frac{n-4}{n(n-1)}x^{2}+\left(2\sqrt{2n}-\Big(4-\frac{4}{n}-3\sqrt{\frac{2}{n}}\Big)\sqrt{2+\sqrt{2n}}\right)x

\displaystyle-2\sqrt{n}(n-1)\left(\sqrt{3(n-2)}-\sqrt{4+2\sqrt{2n}}\right)<0.

Now we prove its discriminant is negative, i.e.,

(3.5)

\displaystyle 1-\Big(2-\frac{2}{n}-\frac{3}{\sqrt{2n}}\Big)\sqrt{\frac{2+\sqrt{2n}}{2n}}<\sqrt{\frac{n-4}{n\sqrt{n}}\Big(\sqrt{3(n-2)}-\sqrt{4+2\sqrt{2n}}\Big)}.

(i)
n\geq 66

\displaystyle 1+\frac{4}{n-4}+\sqrt{\frac{4+2\sqrt{2n}}{n}}<\sqrt{3\Big(1-\frac{2}{n}\Big)}

\displaystyle\Leftrightarrow 1

\displaystyle<\frac{n-4}{n\sqrt{n}}\Big(\sqrt{3(n-2)}-\sqrt{4+2\sqrt{2n}}\Big).

(ii)
13\leq n\leq 65

Since

\displaystyle 1-\Big(2-\frac{2}{n}-\frac{3}{\sqrt{2n}}\Big)\sqrt{\frac{2+\sqrt{2n}}{2n}}

\displaystyle<1-\Big(2-\frac{2}{13}-\frac{3}{\sqrt{26}}\Big)\sqrt{\frac{2+\sqrt{130}}{130}}

\displaystyle<\sqrt{0.3555},

\displaystyle 0.3555\Big(1+\frac{4}{n-4}\Big)+\sqrt{\frac{4+2\sqrt{2n}}{n}}<\sqrt{3\Big(1-\frac{2}{n}\Big)}

\displaystyle\Leftrightarrow 0.3555<\frac{n-4}{n\sqrt{n}}\Big(\sqrt{3(n-2)}-\sqrt{4+2\sqrt{2n}}\Big).

(iii)
9\leq n\leq 12

We can check that

1-\Big(2-\frac{2}{n}-\frac{3}{\sqrt{2n}}\Big)\sqrt{\frac{2+\sqrt{2n}}{2n}}<\sqrt{0.1814},

then we have

\displaystyle 0.1814<\frac{n-4}{n\sqrt{n}}\Big(\sqrt{3(n-2)}-\sqrt{4+2\sqrt{2n}}\Big).

When
n=8
, (
3.2
) is

\displaystyle 8-\frac{x}{14}-\frac{12x}{3x+56}-\frac{168}{x+7\sqrt{6}}<0

\displaystyle\Leftrightarrow\frac{3}{14}x^{3}-\Big(8-\frac{3\sqrt{6}}{2}\Big)x^{2}-56(\sqrt{6}-1)x+56^{2}(3-\sqrt{6})>0.

We calculate the minimum is

\displaystyle\min\{\frac{3}{14}x^{3}-\Big(8-\frac{3\sqrt{6}}{2}\Big)x^{2}-56(\sqrt{6}-1)x+56^{2}(3-\sqrt{6})\}

\displaystyle=86.697>0,\ x=19.827.

When
n=7

3.1
) is

\frac{x^{2}+20x+336}{2\sqrt{x^{2}+24x+504}}-\frac{4x}{7}-\sqrt{14\Big(\frac{x^{2}+18x+252}{\sqrt{x^{2}+24x+504}}-x-6\Big)}<0.

We calculate the maximum by numerical evaluation.

\displaystyle\max\Big\{\frac{x^{2}+20x+336}{2\sqrt{x^{2}+24x+504}}-\frac{4x}{7}-\sqrt{14\Big(\frac{x^{2}+18x+252}{\sqrt{x^{2}+24x+504}}-x-6\Big)}\Big\}

\displaystyle=-0.264<0,\ x=20.399.

∎

We denote
\mathring{a}^{\prime}(|H|^{2})
and
\mathring{a}^{\prime\prime}(|H|^{2})
by
\mathring{a}^{\prime}
and

\mathring{a}^{\prime\prime}
, respectively. Then the evolution
equation of
\mathring{a}
satisfies

(3.6)

\frac{\partial}{\partial t}\mathring{a}=\Delta\mathring{a}+2\mathring{a}^{\prime}\cdot(-|\nabla H|^{2}+R_{2}+n\bar{K}|H|^{2})-\mathring{a}^{\prime\prime}\cdot|\nabla|H|^{2}|^{2}.

Since
M
is
compact, there exists a small positive number
0<\epsilon\ll\frac{1}{n^{2}},

such that
M

|\mathring{A}|^{2}<\mathring{a}-\epsilon\omega,\ \ \ \ \omega=\frac{|H|^{2}}{n-1}+2n\bar{K}>a.

Then we prove that the pinching condition above is

Proposition 3.2
.

Let
F_{0}:M\rightarrow\mathbb{S}^{n+p}(\frac{1}{\sqrt{\bar{K}}})
be a compact submanifold immersed in the sphere. Suppose there exists a small positive number
\epsilon(\ll\frac{1}{n^{2}})
such that
|A|^{2}\leq a(|H|^{2})-\epsilon\omega,

then this condition holds
along the mean curvature flow for all time
t\in[0,T)
where
T\leq\infty
.

Proof.

Let
U=|\mathring{A}|^{2}-\mathring{a}+\epsilon\omega
.
By Lemmas
2.1
,
2.2
and (
3.6
), we have

\displaystyle\Big(\frac{\partial}{\partial t}-\Delta\Big)U

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-2|\nabla\mathring{A}|^{2}+2(\mathring{a}^{\prime}-\frac{\epsilon}{n-1})|\nabla H|^{2}+\mathring{a}^{\prime\prime}|\nabla|H|^{2}|^{2}

\displaystyle+2R_{1}-\frac{2}{n}R_{2}-2n\bar{K}|\mathring{A}|^{2}-2(\mathring{a}^{\prime}-\frac{\epsilon}{n-1})\cdot(R_{2}+n\bar{K}|H|^{2})

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle 2\Big(-\frac{2(n-1)}{n(n+2)}+\mathring{a}^{\prime}-\frac{\epsilon}{n-1}+2|H|^{2}\mathring{a}^{\prime\prime}\Big)|\nabla H|^{2}

\displaystyle+2|\mathring{A}|^{2}\Big(|\mathring{A}|^{2}+\frac{1}{n}|H|^{2}-n\bar{K}\Big)+2P_{2}\Big(2|\mathring{A}|^{2}-\frac{1}{n}|H|^{2}-\frac{3}{2}P_{2}\Big)

\displaystyle-2(\mathring{a}^{\prime}-\frac{\epsilon}{n-1})\cdot|H|^{2}\Big(|\mathring{A}|^{2}+\frac{1}{n}|H|^{2}+n\bar{K}-P_{2}\Big).

From Lemma
2.3
(ii), the coefficient of
|\nabla H|^{2}
is
negative.

Replacing
|\mathring{A}|^{2}
by
U+\mathring{a}-\epsilon\omega
,
the above formula becomes

\displaystyle\left(\frac{\partial}{\partial t}-\Delta\right)U

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle 2U\left(2\mathring{a}+\frac{1}{n}|H|^{2}-n\bar{K}-\mathring{a}^{\prime}|H|^{2}+2P_{2}+\epsilon\big(\frac{|H|^{2}}{n-1}-2\omega\big)\right)

\displaystyle+2U^{2}+2\bigg(a(\mathring{a}-|H|^{2}\mathring{a}^{\prime})-n\bar{K}(\mathring{a}+|H|^{2}\mathring{a}^{\prime})\bigg)

\displaystyle+2P_{2}\left(2\mathring{a}-\frac{1}{n}|H|^{2}+|H|^{2}\mathring{a}^{\prime}-\epsilon\big(\frac{|H|^{2}}{n-1}+2\omega\big)-\frac{3}{2}P_{2}\right)

\displaystyle+2\epsilon\left(\frac{|H|^{2}}{n-1}\cdot\left(a+n\bar{K}\right)-\omega\left(a+\mathring{a}-n\bar{K}-\mathring{a}^{\prime}|H|^{2}\right)\right)

\displaystyle+2\epsilon^{2}\omega\big(\omega-\frac{|H|^{2}}{n-1}\big).

From Lemma
2.3
(vi) and Lemma
3.1
, we have

\displaystyle a(\mathring{a}-|H|^{2}\mathring{a}^{\prime})-n\bar{K}(\mathring{a}+|H|^{2}\mathring{a}^{\prime})

\displaystyle+P_{2}\left(2\mathring{a}-\frac{1}{n}|H|^{2}+|H|^{2}\mathring{a}^{\prime}-\epsilon\big(\frac{|H|^{2}}{n-1}+2\omega\big)-\frac{3}{2}P_{2}\right)

\displaystyle+\epsilon\left(\frac{|H|^{2}}{n-1}\cdot\left(a+n\bar{K}\right)-\omega\left(a+\mathring{a}-n\bar{K}-\mathring{a}^{\prime}|H|^{2}\right)+2n\bar{K}\epsilon\omega\right)

\displaystyle<

\displaystyle a(\mathring{a}-|H|^{2}\mathring{a}^{\prime})-n\bar{K}(\mathring{a}+|H|^{2}\mathring{a}^{\prime})

\displaystyle+P_{2}\left(2\mathring{a}-\frac{1}{n}|H|^{2}+|H|^{2}\mathring{a}^{\prime}-\frac{3}{2}P_{2}\right)

\displaystyle+2\epsilon\bar{K}\left(n\bar{K}(n-4+2n\epsilon)-\frac{|H|^{2}}{n-1}(1-n\epsilon)\right)

\displaystyle<

\displaystyle 0.

Then the assertion follows from the maximum
principle.
∎

4.
Convergence theorem in the sphere

In this section, we prove the convergence theorem for the mean curvature flow of submanifolds
M^{n}(n\geq 7)
in
\mathbb{S}^{n+p}(\frac{1}{\sqrt{\bar{K}}})
. First, we derive an estimate for the traceless second fundamental
form, which guarantees that
M
becomes
spherical along the mean curvature flow.

Proposition 4.1
.

There exist constants
C<\infty
and
\sigma>0
both depending only on the initial surface such that for all time
t\in[0,T)
where
T\leq\infty
, we have the estimate

(4.1)

\displaystyle|\mathring{A}|^{2}\leq C(|H|^{2}+\bar{K})^{1-\sigma}e^{-2\sigma t}.

To obtain Theorem
4.1
, we need to find the upper bound of

f_{\sigma}:=\frac{|\mathring{A}|^{2}}{\mathring{a}^{1-\sigma}}

with the help of the Stampacchia iteration as in
[
2
]
. Here, we get the evolution equation of
f_{\sigma}
with two parameters.

Lemma 4.2
.

For every
\sigma\in(0,1)
, we have the evolution equation

(4.2)

\displaystyle\frac{\partial}{\partial t}f_{\sigma}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\Delta f_{\sigma}+\frac{2|\nabla f_{\sigma}||\nabla H|}{|\mathring{A}|}-\frac{2\epsilon f_{\sigma}|\nabla H|^{2}}{n|\mathring{A}|^{2}}

\displaystyle+6\sigma|A|^{2}f_{\sigma}-2(n\sigma+\epsilon)\bar{K}f_{\sigma}.

Proof.

From Lemma
2.1
, we have

(4.3)

\displaystyle\frac{\partial}{\partial t}f_{\sigma}

\displaystyle=f_{\sigma}\Big(\frac{\frac{\partial}{\partial t}|\mathring{A}|^{2}}{|\mathring{A}|^{2}}-(1-\sigma)\frac{\frac{\partial}{\partial t}\mathring{a}}{\mathring{a}}\Big).

By direct computation, we have

(4.4)

\displaystyle\Delta f_{\sigma}

\displaystyle=f_{\sigma}\Big(\frac{\Delta|\mathring{A}|^{2}}{|\mathring{A}|^{2}}-(1-\sigma)\frac{\Delta\mathring{a}}{\mathring{a}}\Big)

\displaystyle\quad-\frac{2(1-\sigma)}{\mathring{a}}\big\langle\nabla\mathring{a},\nabla f_{\sigma}\big\rangle+\frac{\sigma(1-\sigma)}{\mathring{a}^{2}}f_{\sigma}|{\nabla\mathring{a}}|^{2}.

Combing (
4.3
) and (
4.4
) and from Lemma
2.1
-
2.3
, we have

\displaystyle\Big(\frac{\partial}{\partial t}-\Delta\Big)f_{\sigma}

\displaystyle=\frac{2(1-\sigma)}{\mathring{a}}\big\langle\nabla\mathring{a},\nabla f_{\sigma}\big\rangle-\frac{\sigma(1-\sigma)}{\mathring{a}^{2}}f_{\sigma}|{\nabla\mathring{a}}|^{2}

\displaystyle\quad+f_{\sigma}\Big(\frac{1-\sigma}{\mathring{a}}(2\mathring{a}^{\prime}|\nabla H|^{2}+\mathring{a}^{\prime\prime}|\nabla|H|^{2}|^{2})-\frac{2|\nabla\mathring{A}|^{2}}{|\mathring{A}|^{2}}\Big)

\displaystyle\quad+2f_{\sigma}\Bigg(\frac{R_{1}-\frac{1}{n}R_{2}-n\bar{K}|\mathring{A}|^{2}}{|\mathring{A}|^{2}}-\frac{(1-\sigma)\mathring{a}^{\prime}}{\mathring{a}}(R_{2}+n\bar{K}|H|^{2})\Bigg)

\displaystyle\leq\frac{4\mathring{a}^{\prime}|H|}{\mathring{a}}|\nabla H||\nabla f_{\sigma}|+2f_{\sigma}\frac{2(n-1)}{n(n+2)}\cdot\frac{|\mathring{A}|^{2}-\mathring{a}}{|\mathring{A}|^{2}\mathring{a}}|\nabla H|^{2}

\displaystyle\quad+2\sigma f_{\sigma}\Bigg(\frac{R_{1}-\frac{1}{n}R_{2}}{|\mathring{A}|^{2}}-n\bar{K}\Bigg)

\displaystyle\quad+2(1-\sigma)f_{\sigma}\Bigg(\frac{R_{1}-\frac{1}{n}R_{2}}{|\mathring{A}|^{2}}-n\bar{K}-\frac{\mathring{a}^{\prime}}{\mathring{a}}(R_{2}+n\bar{K}|H|^{2})\Bigg)

\displaystyle\leq\frac{2|\nabla f_{\sigma}||\nabla H|}{|\mathring{A}|}-\frac{2\epsilon f_{\sigma}|\nabla H|^{2}}{n|\mathring{A}|^{2}}+2\sigma f_{\sigma}\Big(3|A|^{2}-n\bar{K}\Big)

\displaystyle\quad+2(1-\sigma)f_{\sigma}\Bigg(\frac{R_{1}-\frac{1}{n}R_{2}}{|\mathring{A}|^{2}}-n\bar{K}-\frac{\mathring{a}^{\prime}}{\mathring{a}}(R_{2}+n\bar{K}|H|^{2})\Bigg).

The last term in the bracket satisfies

(4.5)

\displaystyle\frac{R_{1}-\frac{1}{n}R_{2}}{|\mathring{A}|^{2}}-n\bar{K}-\frac{\mathring{a}^{\prime}}{\mathring{a}}(R_{2}+n\bar{K}|H|^{2})

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\quad|A|^{2}+2P_{2}-n\bar{K}-\frac{1}{|\mathring{A}|^{2}}\Big(\frac{3}{2}P^{2}_{2}+\frac{|H|^{2}}{n}P_{2}\Big)-\frac{\mathring{a}^{\prime}|H|^{2}}{\mathring{a}}(|A|^{2}-P_{2}+n\bar{K})

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\quad\frac{|A|^{2}}{\mathring{a}}(\mathring{a}-\mathring{a}^{\prime}|H|^{2})-\frac{n\bar{K}}{\mathring{a}}(\mathring{a}+\mathring{a}^{\prime}|H|^{2})+\frac{P_{2}}{\mathring{a}}\Big(2\mathring{a}+\mathring{a}^{\prime}|H|^{2}-\frac{|H|^{2}}{n}-\frac{3}{2}P_{2}\Big)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\quad\frac{1}{\mathring{a}}\Bigg(a(\mathring{a}-\mathring{a}^{\prime}|H|^{2})-n\bar{K}(\mathring{a}+\mathring{a}^{\prime}|H|^{2})+P_{2}\Big(2\mathring{a}+\mathring{a}^{\prime}|H|^{2}-\frac{|H|^{2}}{n}-\frac{3}{2}P_{2}\Big)\Bigg)

\displaystyle-\frac{\epsilon\omega}{\mathring{a}}(\mathring{a}-\mathring{a}^{\prime}|H|^{2}).

The term in the big bracket of the last inequality is non-positive under our pinching assumption.
We complete the lemma with the following inequality.

-\frac{\epsilon\omega}{\mathring{a}}(\mathring{a}-\mathring{a}^{\prime}|H|^{2})\leq-\frac{2\epsilon\omega\bar{K}}{\mathring{a}}\leq-2\epsilon\bar{K}.

∎

Since the term
\sigma|A|^{2}f_{\sigma}
in the evolution equation is positive, we cannot use the ordinary maximum principle. As in
[
2
,
6
]
, we need the negative gradient terms to proceed the iteration. Applying the Simons identity
[
16
]
, we get

(4.6)

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\Delta|\mathring{A}|^{2}

\displaystyle=\langle\mathring{A},\nabla^{2}H\rangle+|\nabla\mathring{A}|^{2}+n\bar{K}|\mathring{A}|^{2}-R_{1}+R_{3}.

Now we have the following estimate.

Lemma 4.3
.

Let
F:M\rightarrow\mathbb{S}^{n+p}(\frac{1}{\sqrt{\bar{K}}})
be a compact submanifold immersed in the sphere with
constant curvature
\bar{K}
. If
F
satisfies pinching condition (
3.7
), then

(4.7)

n\bar{K}|\mathring{A}|^{2}-R_{1}+R_{3}\geq\frac{n}{2}|\mathring{A}|^{2}(\epsilon|A|^{2}-\sqrt{2n}\bar{K}).

Proof.

From Lemma
2.2
, we have

\displaystyle n\bar{K}|\mathring{A}|^{2}-R_{1}+R_{3}

\displaystyle\geq\frac{n}{2}|\mathring{A}|^{2}\Big(\frac{|H|^{2}}{n(n-1)}+2\bar{K}\Big)-\frac{n}{2}|\mathring{A}|^{4}

\displaystyle=\frac{n}{2}|\mathring{A}|^{2}\Big(\frac{|H|^{2}}{n-1}+2\bar{K}-|A|^{2}\Big)

\displaystyle\geq\frac{n}{2}|\mathring{A}|^{2}\Big(\epsilon\omega-(\sqrt{2n}-2)\bar{K}\Big).

∎

Then we can get the required Poincaré inequality.

Lemma 4.4
.

For every
p\geq 4
and
\eta>0
we have the estimate

(4.8)

\displaystyle\int_{M_{t}}|A|^{2}f_{\sigma}d\mu_{t}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\int_{M_{t}}\left(\frac{2(p-1)}{n\epsilon\eta}f_{\sigma}^{p-2}|\nabla f_{\sigma}|^{2}+\frac{2(p-1)\eta+5}{n\epsilon}\cdot\frac{f_{\sigma}^{p}|\nabla H|^{2}}{|\mathring{A}|^{2}}\right)d\mu_{t}

\displaystyle+\frac{\sqrt{2n}}{\epsilon}\int_{M_{t}}\bar{K}f^{p}_{\sigma}d\mu_{t}.

Proof.

From Lemma
4.3
, we have

(4.9)

\displaystyle\Delta f_{\sigma}\geq

\displaystyle\frac{\Delta|\mathring{A}|^{2}}{\mathring{a}^{1-\sigma}}-(1-\sigma)\frac{f_{\sigma}\Delta\mathring{a}}{\mathring{a}}-\frac{2(1-\sigma)\mathring{a}^{\prime}}{\mathring{a}}\big\langle\nabla|H|^{2},\nabla f_{\sigma}\big\rangle

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\mathring{a}^{1-\sigma}}\Big(2\langle\mathring{A},\nabla^{2}H\rangle+n|\mathring{A}|^{2}(\epsilon|A|^{2}-\sqrt{2n}\bar{K})\Big)

\displaystyle-\frac{2|\nabla f_{\sigma}||\nabla H|}{|\mathring{A}|}-(1-\sigma)\frac{f_{\sigma}\Delta\mathring{a}}{\mathring{a}}.

(4.10)

\displaystyle nf_{\sigma}(\epsilon|A|^{2}-\sqrt{2n}\bar{K})\leq

\displaystyle\Delta f_{\sigma}-\frac{2\langle\mathring{A},\nabla^{2}H\rangle}{\mathring{a}^{1-\sigma}}+(1-\sigma)\frac{f_{\sigma}\Delta\mathring{a}}{\mathring{a}}

\displaystyle+\frac{2|\nabla f_{\sigma}||\nabla H|}{|\mathring{A}|}.

We multiply the equation (
4.10
) by
f_{\sigma}^{p-1}
and integrate on both sides, then

(4.11)

\displaystyle\int_{M_{t}}nf^{p}_{\sigma}(\epsilon|A|^{2}-\sqrt{2n}\bar{K})d\mu_{t}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle-\int_{M_{t}}\frac{2f_{\sigma}^{p-1}\langle\mathring{A},\nabla^{2}H\rangle}{\mathring{a}^{1-\sigma}}d\mu_{t}+\int_{M_{t}}(1-\sigma)\frac{f^{p}_{\sigma}\Delta\mathring{a}}{\mathring{a}}d\mu_{t}

\displaystyle+\int_{M_{t}}\frac{2f_{\sigma}^{p-1}|\nabla f_{\sigma}||\nabla H|}{|\mathring{A}|}d\mu_{t}.

The Codazzi equation implies
\nabla_{i}\mathring{A}^{\alpha}_{ij}=\frac{n-1}{n}\nabla_{j}H^{\alpha}
. From this formula and Lemma
2.3
(i), we get

(4.12)

\displaystyle\ \ \ -\int_{M_{t}}\frac{f_{\sigma}^{p-1}\langle\mathring{A},\nabla^{2}H\rangle}{\mathring{a}^{1-\sigma}}d\mu_{t}

\displaystyle=\int_{M_{t}}\nabla_{i}\left(\frac{f_{\sigma}^{p-1}\mathring{A}_{ij}^{\alpha}}{\mathring{a}^{1-\sigma}}\right)\nabla_{j}H^{\alpha}d\mu_{t}

\displaystyle=\int_{M_{t}}\left(\frac{(p-1)f_{\sigma}^{p-2}\mathring{A}^{\alpha}_{ij}}{\mathring{a}^{1-\sigma}}\nabla_{i}f_{\sigma}-\frac{(1-\sigma)f_{\sigma}^{p-1}\mathring{A}^{\alpha}_{ij}}{\mathring{a}^{2-\sigma}}\nabla_{i}\mathring{a}+\frac{f_{\sigma}^{p-1}}{\mathring{a}^{1-\sigma}}\nabla_{i}\mathring{A}^{\alpha}_{ij}\right)\nabla_{j}H^{\alpha}d\mu_{t}

\displaystyle\leq\int_{M_{t}}\left((p-1)\frac{f_{\sigma}^{p-1}|\nabla f_{\sigma}||\nabla H|}{|\mathring{A}|}+\frac{2f_{\sigma}^{p}|\nabla H|^{2}}{|\mathring{A}|^{2}}\right)d\mu_{t}.

and

(4.13)

\displaystyle\int_{M_{t}}\frac{f_{\sigma}^{p}}{\mathring{a}}\Delta\mathring{a}d\mu_{t}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\int_{M_{t}}\left\langle\nabla\left(\frac{f_{\sigma}^{p}}{\mathring{a}}\right),\nabla\mathring{a}\right\rangle d\mu_{t}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int_{M_{t}}\left(-\frac{pf_{\sigma}^{p-1}}{\mathring{a}}\left\langle\nabla f_{\sigma},\nabla\mathring{a}\right\rangle+\frac{f_{\sigma}^{p}}{\mathring{a}^{2}}\left|\nabla\mathring{a}\right|^{2}\right)d\mu_{t}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\int_{M_{t}}\left(\frac{pf_{\sigma}^{p-1}|\nabla f_{\sigma}||\nabla H|}{|\mathring{A}|}+\frac{f_{\sigma}^{p}|\nabla H|^{2}}{|\mathring{A}|^{2}}\right)d\mu_{t}.

Putting (
4.11
)-(
4.13
) together, we obtain

(4.14)

\displaystyle\int_{M_{t}}nf^{p}_{\sigma}(\epsilon|A|^{2}-\sqrt{2n}\bar{K})d\mu_{t}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\int_{M_{t}}\left(\frac{3pf_{\sigma}^{p-1}|\nabla f_{\sigma}||\nabla H|}{|\mathring{A}|}+\frac{5f_{\sigma}^{p}|\nabla H|^{2}}{|\mathring{A}|^{2}}\right)d\mu_{t}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\int_{M_{t}}\left(\frac{2(p-1)}{\eta}f_{\sigma}^{p-2}|\nabla f_{\sigma}|^{2}+\Big(2(p-1)\eta+5\Big)\frac{f_{\sigma}^{p}|\nabla H|^{2}}{|\mathring{A}|^{2}}\right)d\mu_{t}.

Now we show that the

-norm of

is bounded for sufficiently large

Lemma 4.5
.

For any
p\geq\frac{n^{3}}{32\epsilon}+1
and

\sigma\leq\frac{n\epsilon\sqrt{\epsilon}}{24\sqrt{p-1}}
,
there exist a constant
C
depending only on the initial surface such that for all

t\in[0,T)
where

, we have

(4.15)

\displaystyle\bigg(\int_{M_{t}}f_{\sigma}^{p}d\mu_{t}\bigg)^{\frac{1}{p}}\leq Ce^{-2\sigma\bar{K}t}.

Proof.

For
t\geq t_{0}
, form Lemma
4.2
, we have

(4.16)

\displaystyle\frac{\partial}{\partial t}\int_{M_{t}}f_{\sigma}^{p}d\mu_{t}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\int_{M_{t}}pf_{\sigma}^{p-1}\frac{\partial}{\partial t}f_{\sigma}d\mu_{t}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle-p(p-1)\int_{M_{t}}f_{\sigma}^{p-2}|\nabla f_{\sigma}|^{2}d\mu_{t}

\displaystyle 2p\int_{M_{t}}\frac{f^{p-1}_{\sigma}|\nabla f_{\sigma}||\nabla H|}{|\mathring{A}|}d\mu_{t}-2p\epsilon\int_{M_{t}}\frac{f_{\sigma}^{p}|\nabla H|^{2}}{|\mathring{A}|^{2}}d\mu_{t}

\displaystyle+6\sigma p\int_{M_{t}}|A|^{2}f_{\sigma}^{p}d\mu_{t}-2p(n\sigma+\epsilon)\bar{K}\int_{M_{t}}f_{\sigma}^{p}d\mu_{t}.

In view of the pinching condition we can estimate

\displaystyle 2p\int_{M_{t}}\frac{f^{p-1}_{\sigma}|\nabla f_{\sigma}||\nabla H|}{|\mathring{A}|}d\mu_{t}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{p}{\mu}\int_{M_{t}}f_{\sigma}^{p-2}|\nabla f_{\sigma}|^{2}d\mu_{t}+p\mu\int_{M_{t}}\frac{f_{\sigma}^{p}|\nabla H|^{2}}{|\mathring{A}|^{2}}d\mu_{t}.

\displaystyle-\frac{p(p-1)}{2}\int_{M_{t}}f_{\sigma}^{p-2}|\nabla f_{\sigma}|^{2}d\mu_{t}

\displaystyle-p\epsilon\int_{M_{t}}\frac{f_{\sigma}^{p-1}}{|H|^{2(1-\sigma)}}|\nabla A|^{2}d\mu_{t}

\displaystyle+6\sigma p\int_{M_{t}}|A|^{2}f_{\sigma}^{p}d\mu_{t}-2p(n\sigma+\epsilon)\bar{K}\int_{M_{t}}f_{\sigma}^{p}d\mu_{t}.

This together with Lemma

(4.19)

\displaystyle\frac{\partial}{\partial t}\int_{M_{t}}f_{\sigma}^{p}d\mu_{t}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle-p(p-1)\bigg(\frac{1}{2}-\frac{12\sigma}{n\eta\epsilon}\bigg)\int_{M_{t}}f_{\sigma}^{p-2}|\nabla f_{\sigma}|^{2}d\mu_{t}

\displaystyle-p\bigg(\epsilon-\frac{6\sigma\big(2(p-1)\eta+5\big)}{n\epsilon}\bigg)\int_{M_{t}}\frac{f_{\sigma}^{p-1}}{(\alpha|H|+\beta\bar{K})^{1-\sigma}}|\nabla A|^{2}d\mu_{t}

\displaystyle-2\Big(\epsilon-\frac{3\sigma\sqrt{2n}}{\epsilon}\Big)p\bar{K}\int_{M_{t}}f_{\sigma}^{p}d\mu_{t}-2n\sigma p\bar{K}\int_{M_{t}}f_{\sigma}^{p}d\mu_{t}.

Now we pick

,

5.

Putting

Theorem 5.1
.

F_{0}:M\rightarrow\mathbb{S}^{n+p}(\frac{1}{\sqrt{\bar{K}}})

(n\geq 4)

Lemma 5.2
.

For
x\geq 0
,
We have the following properties.

(i)

\frac{4x(\mathring{b}^{\prime})^{2}}{\mathring{b}}<1,

(ii)

2x\mathring{b}^{\prime\prime}+\mathring{b}^{\prime}<\frac{2(n-1)}{n(n+2)},\ \ for\ \delta\leq\frac{2(2n-5)}{n^{2}-4},

(iii)

\frac{n-2}{\sqrt{n(n-1)}}\sqrt{x\mathring{b}}+\mathring{b}<\frac{x}{n}+n\bar{K},

(iv)

\mathring{b}\cdot\left(b-n\bar{K}\right)-x\mathring{b}^{\prime}\cdot\left(b+n\bar{K}\right)<-2(1-\delta)(n-2)\bar{K}^{2}
,

(v)

\frac{x}{n-1}\left(b+n\bar{K}\right)-(\frac{x}{n-1}+2n\bar{K})\left(\mathring{b}+b-n\bar{K}-x\mathring{b}^{\prime}\right)\\
<-\frac{2\bar{K}x}{n-1}+2n(n-4)\bar{K}^{2}
,

(vi)

2\mathring{b}-\frac{x}{n}+x\mathring{b}^{\prime}<2\big(\delta(n-2)+2\big)\bar{K}
.

Proof.

By direct computations, we get

\mathring{b}=\delta n\bar{K}+2(1-\delta)\bar{K}+\frac{\delta n^{2}-2\delta n+2}{2(n-1)n}x-\frac{\delta(n-2)}{2(n-1)}\sqrt{x^{2}+4(n-1)\bar{K}x},

\mathring{b}^{\prime}=\frac{\delta n^{2}-2\delta n+2}{2(n-1)n}-\frac{\delta(n-2)}{2(n-1)}\frac{x+2(n-1)\bar{K}}{\sqrt{x^{2}+4(n-1)\bar{K}x}}\leq\frac{n}{n(n-1)},

\mathring{b}^{\prime\prime}=\frac{2\delta(n-1)(n-2)\bar{K}^{2}}{\big(x^{2}+4(n-1)\bar{K}x\big)^{3/2}}.

(i)

\frac{4x(\mathring{b}^{\prime})^{2}}{\mathring{b}}<\frac{x}{n(n-1)\mathring{b}}<1.

(ii)

\displaystyle 2x\mathring{b}^{\prime\prime}+\mathring{b}^{\prime}=

\displaystyle\frac{\delta n^{2}-2\delta n+2}{2(n-1)n}-\frac{\delta(n-2)\big(x^{3}+3(n-1)\bar{K}x^{2}\big)}{2(n-1)\big(x^{2}+4(n-1)\bar{K}x\big)^{3/2}}

\displaystyle<

\displaystyle\frac{\delta n^{2}-2\delta n+2}{2(n-1)n}\leq\frac{2(n-1)}{n(n+2)},\ \ \ \ \rm{where}\ \delta\leq\frac{2(2n-5)}{n^{2}-4}.

(iii)

\displaystyle\frac{n-2}{\sqrt{n(n-1)}}\sqrt{x\mathring{b}}+\mathring{b}<\frac{n-2}{\sqrt{n(n-1)}}\sqrt{x\mathring{\alpha}}+\mathring{\alpha}=\frac{x}{n}+n\bar{K}.

(iv)

\displaystyle a(\mathring{b}-x\mathring{b}^{\prime})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle a\left(\delta n\bar{K}+2(1-\delta)\bar{K}-\frac{\delta(n-2)\bar{K}x}{\sqrt{x^{2}+4(n-1)\bar{K}x}}\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\big(\delta n\bar{K}+2(1-\delta)\bar{K}\big)^{2}-\big(\delta n\bar{K}+2(1-\delta)\bar{K}\big)\cdot\frac{\delta(n-2)\big(x^{2}+3(n-1)\bar{K}x\big)}{(n-1)\sqrt{x^{2}+4(n-1)\bar{K}x}}

\displaystyle+\frac{\delta^{2}(n-2)^{2}+\big(\delta n+2(1-\delta)\big)^{2}}{2(n-1)}\bar{K}x.

\displaystyle a(\mathring{b}-x\mathring{b}^{\prime})-n\bar{K}(\mathring{b}+x\mathring{b}^{\prime})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(\delta-1)(n-2)\left(\delta n\bar{K}+2(1-\delta)\bar{K}-\frac{\delta(n-2)\big(x^{2}+3(n-1)\bar{K}x\big)}{(n-1)\sqrt{x^{2}+4(n-1)\bar{K}x}}\right)\bar{K}

\displaystyle(\delta-1)(n-2)\bar{K}\left(\delta n\bar{K}+2(1-\delta)\bar{K}+\frac{\delta(n-2)}{n-1}\bigg(x-\frac{x^{2}+3(n-1)\bar{K}x}{\sqrt{x^{2}+4(n-1)\bar{K}x}}\bigg)\right)
