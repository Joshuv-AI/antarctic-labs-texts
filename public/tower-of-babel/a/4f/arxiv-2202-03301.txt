Abstract
Locally repairable codes (LRCs) with ( r , δ ) (r,\delta) locality were introduced by Prakash et al. into distributed storage systems (DSSs) due to their benefit of locally repairing at least δ − 1 \delta-1 erasures via other r r survival nodes among the same local group. An LRC achieving the ( r , δ ) (r,\delta) Singleton-type bound is called an optimal ( r , δ ) (r,\delta) LRC. Constructions of optimal ( r , δ ) (r,\delta) LRCs with longer code length and determining the maximal code length have been an important research direction in coding theory in recent years. In this paper, we conduct further research on the improvement of maximum code length of optimal ( r , δ ) (r,\delta) LRCs. For 2 ​ δ + 1 ≤ d ≤ 2 ​ δ + 2 2\delta+1\leq d\leq 2\delta+2 , our upper bounds largely improve the ones by Cai et al. , which are tight in some special cases. Moreover, we generalize the results of Chen et al. and obtain a complete characterization of optimal ( r = 2 , δ ) (r=2,\delta) -LRCs in the sense of geometrical existence in the finite projective plane P ​ G ​ ( 2 , q ) PG(2,q) . Within this geometrical characterization, we construct a class of optimal ( r , δ ) (r,\delta) LRCs based on the sunflower structure. Both the construction and upper bounds are better than previous ones.

Lemma 3

\delta\end{array}\right)\right\rfloor

\mathcal{V}_{1},\mathcal{V}_{2},\ldots,\mathcal{V}_{\ell}
of

,

t_{i}\triangleq\left|\bigcup_{j\in[\ell],j\neq i}\left({\mathcal{V}_{i}\brack 1}_{q}\bigcap{\mathcal{V}_{j}\brack 1}_{q}\right)\right|\leq q-\delta.

Proof:

The proof is divided into two directions as follows.

•

Necessity:
Let
C
be an optimal
(n,k,d=2\delta+2;r=2,\delta)
-LRC with a parity-check matrix
H
. By Lemma
4
and Claim 1, we have

\textnormal{Span}\{\textbf{u}_{i}\}
,
\textnormal{Span}\{\textbf{v}_{i}\}
,
\textnormal{Span}\{a_{m}\textbf{u}_{i}+b_{m}\textbf{v}_{i}\},m\in[\delta-1]
, are
(\delta+1)
distinct 1-subspaces in
{\mathcal{V}_{i}\brack 1}_{q}
, which do not belong to
{\mathcal{V}_{j}\brack 1}_{q}
for all

. Thus

.

•

Sufficiency:
Suppose there exist
\ell
distinct 2-subspaces
\mathcal{V}_{1},\mathcal{V}_{2},\ldots,\mathcal{V}_{\ell}
of
\mathbb{F}_{q}^{3}
satisfying the intersection condition in the theorem. Since

, there exist
(\delta+1)
distinct 1-subspaces of
\mathcal{V}_{i}
, without loss of generality, we let these
(\delta+1)
distinct 1-subspaces to be
\textnormal{Span}\{\textbf{u}_{i}\}
,
\textnormal{Span}\{\textbf{v}_{i}\}
,
\textnormal{Span}\{a_{m}\textbf{u}_{i}+b_{m}\textbf{v}_{i}\},m\in[\delta-1]
,
(a_{m},b_{m})\in\mathbb{F}_{q}^{*}\times\mathbb{F}_{q}^{*}
. Similar to (

a_{m}p_{i,m}+b_{m}q_{i,m}=0.

(17)

Next, we need to prove that the matrix

formed by these

(\delta-1)

(\delta-1)

(a,b)\in\mathbb{F}_{q}^{*}\times\mathbb{F}_{q}^{*}

,

, which implies

and
\textnormal{Span}\{a_{2}\textbf{u}_{i}+b_{2}\textbf{v}_{i}\}

[\delta+1,\delta-1,3]

(r=2,\delta)

2\delta+1

\delta

\textnormal{Span}\{\textbf{u}_{1}\}
,

, or

must belong to

{\mathcal{V}_{2}\brack 1}_{q}

Lemma 5

Suppose

Proof:

Let
A
be the
\ell\times(q^{2}+q+1)
-incidence matrix of the
\ell

(\delta+1)

\ell(\delta+1)
columns whose Hamming weight is 1. Equivalently, we assume that

A=\left(\begin{array}[]{c|c}A^{\prime}&\underbrace{\begin{array}[]{cccc}\underbrace{1~1~\cdots~1}_{\delta+1}&0~0~\cdots~0&\ldots&0~0~\cdots~0\\
0~0~\cdots~0&\underbrace{1~1~\cdots~1}_{\delta+1}&\ldots&0~0~\cdots~0\\

\end{array}}_{\ell(\delta+1)~\text{columns}}\end{array}\right),

(18)

where

\ell\times(q^{2}+q+1-\ell(\delta+1))

IV
Conclusion

In this paper, we provide a framework for further research on the maximum code length and constructions of optimal
(r,\delta)
LRCs with small localities. Unlike known upper bounds that are asymptotically achieved, our improved upper bounds largely close the gap between the bounds and known construction when
2\delta+1\leq d\leq 3\delta
. It’s worth noting that our bounds can be exactly achieved in some special cases. Furthermore, we establish a generalized characterization of optimal
(r=2,\delta)
-LRCs when
d=2\delta+2
. With this geometrical characterization, we obtain a sunflower based
(r,\delta)
LRCs with longest code length
n=(\delta+1)(q+1)
until now.
