On m m -bi-interior ideals of an ordered semigroup
Susmita Mallick

Abstract
In this article, we concurrently explore the class of m m -bi-ideals as well as the class of m m -interior ideals in an ordered semigroup. We call these class of ideals as m m -bi-interior ideals. Here, m m -simple, m m -regular ordered semigroups and their various subclasses get characterized in diverse manners as per their m m -bi-interior ideals.

-bi-interior ideal,

-bi-interior-simple,

-regular.
