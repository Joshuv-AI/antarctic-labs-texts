1.
Introduction

A complete Riemannian manifold
(M^{n},g)
is called a
gradient Ricci soliton
if there exists
a smooth function
f
on
M^{n}
such that the Ricci tensor
Rc

of the metric
g
satisfies the equation

Rc+\nabla^{2}f=\lambda g

for some
\lambda\in{\mathbb{R}}
, where
\nabla^{2}f
denotes the Hessian of
f
. The Ricci soliton is said to be expanding, or steady, or shrinking if
\lambda<0
, or
\lambda=0
, or
\lambda>0
.
The function
f
is called a
potential function
of the gradient Ricci soliton.
Clearly, when
f
is a constant the gradient Ricci soliton
(M^{n},g,f)
is simply an Einstein manifold.

Gradient Ricci solitons are a natural extension of Einstein metrics. They are also self-similar solutions to Hamilton’s Ricci flow and play an important role in the study of formation of singularities
[
39
]
. In particular, gradient expanding Ricci solitons may arise as Type III singularity models in the Ricci flow (under suitable positive curvature assumptions)
[
8
,
21
]
, and over which the matrix Li-Yau-Hamilton inequality (also known as the matrix differential Harnack inequality) becomes equality
[
38
,
7
]
. Indeed, Schulze-Simon
[
51
]
obtained gradient expanding solitons out of the asymptotic cones at infinity of solutions to the Ricci flow on complete noncompact Riemannian manifolds with bounded and non-negative curvature operator and positive asymptotic volume ratio. See also more recent work
[
32
,
27
]
.

There have been a lot of advances in investigating the geometry of gradient shrinking and steady Ricci solitons in the past two decades, while the research activities on expanding solitons have also picked up in recent years. In this paper, we are mainly concerned with
4
-dimensional complete noncompact gradient expanding solitons with nonnegative Ricci curvature.
By scaling, we shall assume throughout the paper that
\lambda=-\frac{1}{2}
, so the gradient expanding Ricci soliton equation is of the form

Rc+\nabla^{2}f=-\frac{1}{2}g

(1.1)

Like steady solitons, compact expanding solitons are necessarily Einstein. Hence the study of expanding solitons has been focused on complete noncompact ones.
The simplest example of a complete (noncompact) gradient expanding soliton is the
Gaussian expander
on
\mathbb{R}^{n}
, with the standard flat metric and potential function
f(x)=-|x|^{2}/4
. In addition, Bryant
[
4
]
and the first author
[
8
]
have constructed non-flat rotationally symmetric expanding gradient Ricci and Kähler-Ricci solitons on
\mathbb{R}^{n}
and
\mathbb{C}^{n}
, respectively. In particular, they constructed one-parameter families of expanding solitons with positive sectional curvature that are asymptotic to a cone at infinity. Moreover, the constructions in
[
6
,
8
]
have been extended by Feldman-Ilmanen-Knopf
[
34
]
to a construction of gradient expanding Kähler-Ricci solitons on the complex line bundles
O(-k)
(
k>n
) over the complex projective space
{\mathbb{C}}P^{n}
(
n\geq 1
), and further generalized by Dancer-Wang
[
30
]
.
For additional examples and other constructions, see, e.g.,
[
47
,
43
,
36
,
2
,
29
,
35
,
5
,
1
,
52
]
and the references therein. Note especially that the examples constructed in
[
43
]
and
[
2
]
are non-gradient Sol and Nil expanding solitons, respectively.

In recent years, researchers in the field have obtained several rigidity or uniqueness results concerning gradient expanding solitons. For example, Pigola-Rimoldi-Setti
[
49
]
proved that under certain integrability assumptions of the soliton vector field complete gradient expanding solitons must be trivial. Catino-Mastrolia-Monticelli
[
16
]
showed that any complete noncompact gradient expanding solitons with nonnegative sectional curvature and
L^{1}
-integrable scalar curvature is necessarily isometric to a quotient of the Gaussian expanding soliton.
In addition, it was shown in
[
9
]
that a Bach-flat gradient expanding soliton with positive Ricci curvature must be rotationally symmetric; see also related recent results
[
41
,
13
,
44
]
for expanding solitons with harmonic Weyl curvature or vanishing
D
-tensor.
In another direction, Chodosh
[
22
]
showed that any gradient expanding Ricci soliton with positive sectional curvature which is asymptotically conical must be rotationally symmetric, hence one of the Bryant expanding solitons on
\mathbb{R}^{n}
. A similar result for gradient expanding Kähler-Ricci solitons was proved by Chodosh-Fong
[
23
]
, namely expanding Kähler-Ricci solitons which have positive holomorphic bisectional curvature and are
C^{2}
-asymptotic to a conical Kähler manifold at infinity must be the
U(n)
-invariant expanding solitons constructed in
[
8
]
.
We also mention that Chen-Deruelle
[
20
]

have studied the asymptotic geometry of expanding solitons with finite asymptotic curvature ratio.
However, for more general gradient expanding solitons we still know very little about their geometry, except in dimension
n=2
for which there is a complete classification
[
3
,
50
]
.

It is well-known that, in studying complete noncompact Riemannian manifolds, it is very crucial to have information on volume growth rate and asymptotic curvature behavior at infinity.
Concerning the volume growth rate, Hamilton
[
40
]
(see also Proposition 9.46 in
[
25
]
) showed that any
n
-dimensional complete noncompact gradient expanding soliton
(M^{n},g,f)
with nonnegative Ricci curvature
Rc\geq 0
must have positive
asymptotic volume ratio
(equivalently, the maximal volume growth in view of Bishop-Gromov). Namely, for any base point
x_{0}\in M^{n}
,

\lim_{r\to\infty}\frac{V(x_{0},r)}{r^{n}}>0,

(1.2)

where
V(x_{0},r)
denotes the volume of the geodesic ball
B(x_{0},r)
of radius
r>0
centered at
x_{0}
. Subsequently, Carrillo-Ni
[
15
]
extended the above result of Hamilton

to complete noncompact gradient expanding solitons with nonnegative scalar curvature
R\geq 0
(see Proposition 5.1 in
[
15
]
).

As for curvature estimates, very recently P.-Y. Chan
[
18
]
proved that if
(M^{4},g,f)
is a complete noncompact gradient expanding Ricci soliton with bounded scalar curvature
|R|\leq R_{0}
and proper potential function
f
(i.e.,
\lim_{x\to\infty}f(x)=-\infty
), then the curvature tensor
Rm
is bounded. Note that
Rc\geq 0
implies
0\leq R\leq R_{0}
, for some
R_{0}>0
, and
f
proper (see Lemma 2.2 and Lemma 2.3). Hence, it follows that
4
-dimensional complete gradient expanding solitons with nonnegative Ricci curvature
Rc\geq 0
must have bounded Riemann curvature tensor
|Rm|\leq C
.

We point out that recent progress on curvature estimates for 4-dimensional gradient Ricci solitons has been led by the important work of Munteanu-Wang
[
46
]
, in which they proved that any complete gradient shrinking soliton with bounded scalar curvature must have bounded Riemann curvature tensor. More significantly, they showed that the Riemann curvature tensor is controlled by the scalar curvature by
|Rm|\leq CR
so that if the scalar curvature
R
decays at infinity so does the curvature tensor
Rm
; see also
[
12
]
for an extension, and
[
10
,
17
]
for similar estimates in the steady soliton case.
Their curvature estimate, together with the uniqueness result of Kotschwar-Wang
[
42
]
, has played a crucial role in the recent advance of classifying 4-dimensional complete gradient Ricci solitons, as well as in the classification of complex 2-dimensional complete gradient Kähler-Ricci solitons with scalar curvature going to zero at infinity by Conlon-Deruelle-Sun
[
28
]
.

We also remark that one of the important facts used in the work of Munteanu and Wang
[
46
]
is a very useful result of Chow-Lu-Yang
[
26
]
on the scalar curvature lower bound
R\geq C/f
, for some constant
C>0
, for any
n
-dimensional non-flat complete noncompact gradient shrinking soliton. Note that by the optimal asymptotic growth estimate of Cao-Zhou
[
14
]
on the potential function
f
, this is equivalent to the scalar curvature
R
has at most quadratic decay at infinity.

Motivated by the work of Munteanu-Wang
[
46
]
, as well as Cao-Cui
[
10
]
and Chan

[
17
,
18
]
, it is natural to ask if one could also control the Riemann curvature tensor
Rm
of a
4
-dimensional complete gradient expanding soliton with nonnegative Ricci curvature by its scalar curvature
R
. Despite some key differences with the shrinking case, notably the lack of scalar curvature quadratic (or polynomial) decay lower bound for expanding Ricci solitons, this turns out to be possible.

Theorem 1.1
.

Let
(M^{4},g,f)
be a
4
-dimensional complete noncompact
gradient expanding Ricci soliton with nonnegative Ricci curvature
Rc\geq 0
. Then, there exists a constant
C>0
such that, for any
0\leq a<1
, the following estimates hold:

|Rm|\leq\frac{C}{1-a}R^{a}\quad{\mbox{and}}\quad|\nabla Rm|\leq\frac{C}{(1-a)^{2}}R^{a}\quad on\ M^{4}.

(1.3)

Moreover, if in addition the scalar curvature
R
has at most polynomial decay then

{|Rm|}\leq CR\quad on\ M^{4}.

(1.4)

Remark 1.1
.

As mentioned above, P.-Y. Chan
[
18
]
already proved the curvature estimate
|Rm|\leq C
as a special case.

Remark 1.2
.

In Theorem 1.1, if we only assume
Rc\geq 0
outside some compact set
K\subset M
, then estimates (1.3) and (1.4) are valid on
M\setminus K
(see Theorem 5.1).

By using Theorem 1.1, we can obtain the following useful result about asymptotic curvature behavior for
4
-dimensional gradient expanding Ricci solitons with
Rc\geq 0
.

Theorem 1.2
.

Let
(M^{4},g,f)
be a
4
-dimensional complete noncompact gradient expanding Ricci soliton with nonnegative Ricci curvature
Rc\geq 0
. Assume it has finite asymptotic scalar curvature ratio

\limsup_{r\to\infty}Rr^{2}<\infty,

(1.5)

where
r=r(x)
is the distance function to a fixed base point in
M
.
Then
(M^{4},g,f)
has finite asymptotic curvature ratio

A:=\limsup_{r\to\infty}|Rm|r^{2}<\infty.

(1.6)

Remark 1.3
.

In
[
11
]
, joint with J. Xie, we investigate curvature estimates for higher dimensional complete expanding Ricci solitons with nonnegative Ricci curvature and finite asymptotic scalar curvature ratio.

Chen and Deruelle
[
20
]
have shown the existence of a
C^{1,\alpha}
(
0<\alpha<1
) asymptotic cone structure at infinity for
n
-dimensional complete noncompact gradient expanding solitons with finite asymptotic curvature ratio
A<\infty
.
Note that
A
is scaling invariant and independent of the choice of any base point.
By combining Theorem 1.2 with the result of Chen and Deruelle (Theorem 1.2,
[
20
]
), we get

Corollary 1.3.

Let
(M^{4},g,f)
be a
4
-dimensional complete noncompact non-flat
gradient expanding Ricci soliton with nonnegative Ricci curvature
Rc\geq 0
and finite asymptotic scalar curvature ratio. Then
(M^{4},g,f)
has a
C^{1,\alpha}
asymptotic cone structure at infinity, for any
\alpha\in(0,1)
.

Finally, we can also prove certain curvature estimates for
4
-dimensional complete gradient expanding solitons with bounded, positive scalar curvature and proper potential functions.

Theorem 1.4.

Let
(M^{4},g,f)
be a
4
-dimensional complete noncompact gradient expanding Ricci soliton with bounded and positive scalar curvature
0<R\leq R_{0}
. Assume
f
is proper so that

\lim_{x\to\infty}f(x)=-\infty
.
Then, for any
\alpha\in(0,\frac{1}{2})
, we have

|Rm|\leq C_{\alpha}R^{\alpha}\quad{\mbox{and}}\quad|\nabla Rm|\leq C_{\alpha}R^{\alpha}\quad on\ M^{4}

for some positive constant
C_{\alpha}>0
, with
C_{\alpha}\to\infty
as
\alpha\to 1/2
.

The paper is organized as follows. In Section 2, we review some basic facts and collect several useful lemmas about expanding solitons that will be used later in the paper. In Section 3, we present the proofs of the main curvature estimates as stated in Theorem 1.1 and Theorem 1.2. The proof of Theorem 1.4 is carried out in Section 4.
Finally, in Section 5, we briefly describe the extensions of Theorem 1.1 and Theorem 1.2 to
4
-dimensional complete gradient expanding solitons with nonnegative Ricci curvature outside a compact set and raise some questions.

2.
Preliminaries

In this section, we shall fix notations, recall some basic facts, and collect several known results about gradient expanding Ricci solitons.

Throughout the paper, we denote by

Rm=\{R_{ijkl}\},\quad Rc=\{R_{ik}\},\quad R

the Riemann curvature tensor, the Ricci tensor, and the scalar curvature of the metric
g=g_{ij}dx^{i}dx^{j}
in local coordinates
(x^{1},\cdots,x^{n})
, respectively.

Lemma 2.1
.

(Hamilton
[
39
]
)
Let
(M^{n},g,f)

be a complete gradient expanding Ricci soliton satisfying Eq. (1.1).
Then

R+\Delta f=-\frac{n}{2},

(2.1)

\nabla_{i}R=2R_{ij}\nabla_{j}f,

(2.2)

R+|\nabla f|^{2}=-f+C_{0}

(2.3)

for some constant
C_{0}
.

Furthermore, replacing
f
by
f-C_{0}
, we can normalize the potential function
f
in (2.3) so that

R+|\nabla f|^{2}=-f.

(2.4)

In the rest of the paper, we shall always assume this normalization (2.4).

Next, we have the following well-known fact about the asymptotic behavior of the potential function of a complete non-compact gradient expanding soliton with nonnegative Ricci curvature (see, e.g., Lemma 5.5 in
[
9
]
or Lemma 2.2 in
[
20
]
).

Lemma 2.2
.

Let
(M^{n},g,f)
be a complete noncompact gradient expanding Ricci soliton satisfying (1.1) and with nonnegative Ricci curvature
Rc\geq 0
. Then, there exist some constant
c_{1}>0
such that, outside some compact subset of
M^{n}
, the potential function
f
satisfies the estimates

\frac{1}{4}(r(x)-c_{1})^{2}\leq-f(x)\leq\frac{1}{4}(r(x)+2\sqrt{-f(x_{0})})^{2},

(2.5)

where
r(x)
is the distance function from some fixed base point in
M^{n}
. In particular,
-f
is a strictly convex exhaustion function achieving its minimum at a unique interior point
x_{0}
, which we shall take as the base point, and the underlying manifold
M^{n}
is diffeomorphic to
{\mathbb{R}}^{n}
.

Remark 2.1
.

In (2.5), only the lower bound on
-f
requires the assumption of nonnegative Ricci curvature. Also, the level sets of
-f
are diffeomorphic to
{\mathbb{S}}^{n-1}
.

Another useful fact is the boundedness of the scalar curvature of a gradient expanding soliton with nonnegative Ricci curvature. We recently noticed that this result was already observed in Ma-Chen
[
45
]
(see also
[
31
,
33
]
), and its proof essentially follows Hamilton’s argument in proving Theorem 20.1 in
[
39
]
.

Lemma 2.3
.

Let
(M^{n},g,f)
be a complete noncompact gradient expanding Ricci soliton with nonnegative Ricci curvature
Rc\geq 0
. Then its scalar curvature
R
is bounded from above, i.e.,

R\leq R_{0}\quad\mbox{for some positive constant}\ R_{0}.

Moreover,
R>0
everywhere unless
(M^{n},g,f)
is the Gaussian expanding soliton.

Remark 2.2
.

In
[
45
]
, the authors asserted that either
R>0
or
(M^{n},g)
is Ricci flat. However, a complete noncompact Ricci-flat gradient expanding Ricci soliton must be the Gaussian expanding soliton on
{\mathbb{R}}^{n}
(see, e.g., Proposition 3.1 in
[
48
]
).

Furthermore, by combining (2.1) and (2.4), we obtain

\Delta f-|\nabla f|^{2}=f-\frac{n}{2}.

Thus, setting

F=-f+\frac{n}{2},

(2.6)

we have

|\nabla F|^{2}=F-R-\frac{n}{2}\qquad\mbox{and}

(2.7)

\Delta_{f}F=F\qquad({\mbox{i.e.}},\ \Delta_{f}f=f-\frac{n}{2}),

(2.8)

where
\Delta_{f}=:\Delta-\nabla f\cdot\nabla
is the weighted Laplace operator.

Note that, under the assumption of Lemma 2.2, the function
F(x)
defined in (2.6) is proportional to the square of the distance function
r^{2}(x)
from above and below at large distances. In the rest of the paper, we denote by

\displaystyle D(t)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\{x\in M:F(x)\leq t\},\quad{\mbox{and}}

\displaystyle\Sigma(t)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\{x\in M:F(x)=t\}.

We now collect several well-known differential identities on the curvatures
R,Rc
and
Rm
that we shall use later.

Lemma 2.4
.

Let
(M^{n},g,f)

be a gradient expanding Ricci soliton satisfying Eq. (1.1). Then, we have

\displaystyle\Delta_{f}R

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-R-2|Rc|^{2},

\displaystyle\Delta_{f}R_{ik}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-R_{ik}-2R_{ijkl}R_{jl},

\displaystyle\Delta_{f}{Rm}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-Rm+Rm\ast Rm,

\displaystyle\nabla_{l}R_{ijkl}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\nabla_{j}R_{ik}-\nabla_{i}R_{jk}=R_{ijkl}\nabla_{l}f,

where, on the right hand side of the third equation,
Rm\ast Rm
denotes the sum of a finite number of terms involving quadratics in
Rm
.

Based on Lemma 2.4, one can easily derive the following differential inequalities (see also
[
46
,
10
]
for the shrinking and steady ones, respectively):

Lemma 2.5
.

Let
(M^{n},g,f)
be a complete gradient expanding soliton satisfying Eq. (1.1). Then

\displaystyle\Delta_{f}|Rc|^{2}

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle 2|\nabla Rc|^{2}-2|Rc|^{2}-4|Rm||Rc|^{2},

\displaystyle\Delta_{f}|Rm|^{2}

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle 2|\nabla Rm|^{2}-2|Rm|^{2}-c|Rm|^{3},

\displaystyle\Delta_{f}|Rm|

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle-|Rm|-c|Rm|^{2}.

Here
c>0
is some universal constant depending only on the dimension
n
.

Remark 2.3
.

To derive the third inequality, one needs to use the Kato inequality

|\nabla|Rm||\leq|\nabla Rm|
.

Finally, we have the following differential inequalities on the covariant derivative
\nabla Rm
of the curvature tensor (see
[
46
]
for the shrinking case).

Lemma 2.6
.

Let
(M^{n},g,f)
be a complete gradient expanding Ricci soliton satisfying Eq. (1.1). Then

\displaystyle\Delta_{f}|\nabla Rm|^{2}

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle 2|\nabla^{2}Rm|^{2}-3|\nabla Rm|^{2}-c|Rm||\nabla Rm|^{2}\quad\mbox{and}

\displaystyle\Delta_{f}|\nabla Rm|

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle-\frac{3}{2}|\nabla Rm|-c|Rm||\nabla Rm|,

where
c>0
is some universal constant depending only on the dimension
n
.

Proof.

First of all, by commuting covariant differentiations and Lemma 2.4,

\displaystyle\Delta_{f}(\nabla_{q}Rm)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\nabla_{p}\nabla_{p}(\nabla_{q}Rm)-\nabla_{p}(\nabla_{q}Rm)\cdot\nabla_{p}f

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\nabla_{p}\big(\nabla_{q}\nabla_{p}Rm+Rm\ast Rm\big)-\big(\nabla_{q}\nabla_{p}Rm+R_{pq\bullet\bullet}Rm\big)\cdot\nabla_{p}f

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\nabla_{q}(\Delta Rm)-\nabla_{q}(\nabla_{p}Rm\nabla_{p}f)+(\nabla_{p}Rm)(\nabla_{q}\nabla_{p}f)+Rm\ast\nabla_{q}Rm

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\nabla_{q}(\Delta_{f}Rm)-\frac{1}{2}\nabla_{q}Rm+Rm\ast\nabla_{q}Rm

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\frac{3}{2}\nabla_{q}Rm+Rm\ast\nabla_{q}Rm,

where we have used the 4th identity in Lemma 2.4 in the third equality and the expanding soliton equation (1.1) in the 4th equality. Thus,

\displaystyle\Delta_{f}|\nabla Rm|^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 2|\nabla^{2}Rm|^{2}+2(\nabla Rm)\Delta_{f}(\nabla Rm)

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle 2|\nabla^{2}Rm|^{2}-3|\nabla Rm|^{2}-c|Rm||\nabla Rm|^{2}.

This proves the first inequality. The second inequality follows easily from the first one and Kato’s inequality.
∎

3.
The proof of Main Results

First of all, we need the following key fact, valid for all
4
-dimensional gradient Ricci solitons, due to Munteanu and Wang
[
46
]
(see also Lemma 1 in
[
17
]
).

Proposition 3.1
.

(Munteanu-Wang
[
46
]
)
Let
(M^{4},g,f)
be a
4
-dimensional complete noncompact gradient Ricci soliton.
Then, there exists some universal constant
A_{0}>0
such that, at any point where
\nabla f\neq 0
,

|Rm|\leq A_{0}\left(|Rc|+\frac{|\nabla Rc|}{|\nabla f|}\right).

As a consequence of Proposition 3.1, Lemma 2.2 and Lemma 2.3, one gets the following

Proposition 3.2
.

Let
(M^{4},g,f)
be a
4
-dimensional complete noncompact gradient expanding Ricci soliton with nonnegative Ricci curvature.
Then, for any constant
\Lambda>0
, there exists some constant
r_{0}>0
(depending on
\Lambda
) such that

|\nabla Rc|^{2}\geq\frac{\Lambda}{2A_{0}^{2}}|Rm|^{2}-\Lambda|Rc|^{2}\quad\mbox{outside}\ D(r_{0}).

Proof.

From Lemma 2.2, we know that
|\nabla f|(x)\neq 0
for
x\neq x_{0}
. Thus, it follows from Proposition 3.1 that

|Rm|^{2}\leq 2A_{0}^{2}\left(|Rc|^{2}+\frac{|\nabla Rc|^{2}}{|\nabla f|^{2}}\right)\qquad\mbox{on}\ M\setminus\{x_{0}\}.

Hence, for
x\neq x_{0}
,

\frac{|\nabla Rc|^{2}}{|\nabla f|^{2}}\geq\frac{1}{2A_{0}^{2}}|Rm|^{2}-|Rc|^{2}.

(3.1)

On the other hand, by (2.6), (2.7), Lemma 2.2 and Lemma 2.3,

|\nabla f|^{2}(x)=|\nabla F|^{2}(x)=F(x)-R(x)-\frac{n}{2}\rightarrow\infty,\ \mbox{as}\ r(x)\to\infty.

Therefore, for any
\Lambda>0
, there exists constant
r_{0}>0
such that
|\nabla f|^{2}\geq\Lambda
outside the compact set
D(r_{0})=\{F(x)\leq r_{0}\}
, hence

|\nabla Rc|^{2}\geq\Lambda\frac{|\nabla Rc|^{2}}{|\nabla f|^{2}}\geq\frac{\Lambda}{2A_{0}^{2}}|Rm|^{2}-\Lambda|Rc|^{2}.

∎

Now we recall the following result due to P. Y. Chan
[
18
]
.

Proposition 3.3
.

Let
(M^{4},g,f)
be a
4
-dimensional complete noncompact gradient expanding Ricci soliton with nonnegative Ricci curvature
Rc\geq 0
.
Then, there exists some constant
C>0
, depending on the constant
A_{0}
(in Proposition 3.1), the scalar curvature upper bound
R_{0}
(in Lemma 2.3) and the geometry of
D(r_{0})
, such that

\sup_{x\in M}|Rm|\leq C.

Remark 3.1
.

As we pointed out in the introduction, Proposition 3.3 is a special case of a more general result due to P.-Y. Chan
[
18
]
. For the reader’s convenience, we shall provide a direct proof here.

Proof.

The proof follows essentially from the same arguments as in
[
46
]
(see also the proof of Theorem 3.1 in
[
10
]
).
First of all, by the assumption of
Rc\geq 0
and Lemma 2.3, it follows that

0\leq|Rc|\leq R\leq R_{0}.

(3.2)

Thus, by (3.2) and Proposition 3.2 (taking
\Lambda=4A_{0}^{2}
), we get

|\nabla Rc|^{2}\geq 2|Rm|^{2}-4A_{0}^{2}R_{0}^{2}

(3.3)

outside some compact set
D(r_{0})
.

On the other hand, by using the inequalities in Lemma 2.5, (3.2) and (3.3),

\displaystyle\Delta_{f}(|Rm|+\lambda|Rc|^{2})

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle 2\lambda|\nabla Rc|^{2}-c|Rm|^{2}-\lambda C_{1}|Rm|-\lambda C_{1}

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle(4\lambda-c)|Rm|^{2}-\lambda C_{1}|Rm|-\lambda C_{2}

outside
D(r_{0})
, where
C_{1}=C_{1}(R_{0})>0
depends on the scalar curvature upper bound
R_{0}
and
C_{2}=C_{2}(A_{0},R_{0})
depends on the constants
A_{0}
and
R_{0}
.
By picking

4\lambda=c+2,

it follows that, outside
D(r_{0})
,

\displaystyle\Delta_{f}(|Rm|+\lambda|Rc|^{2})

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle 2|Rm|^{2}-\lambda C_{1}|Rm|-\lambda C_{2}

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle(|Rm|+\lambda|Rc|^{2})^{2}-C^{\prime}_{1}(|Rm|+\lambda|Rc|^{2})-C^{\prime}_{2}.

Let
u=|Rm|+\lambda|Rc|^{2}
, then on
M\setminus D(r_{0})

\Delta_{f}u\geq u^{2}-C^{\prime}_{1}u-C^{\prime}_{2}.

(3.4)

Next, let
\varphi(t)
be a smooth function on
\mathbb{R}^{+}
defined by

{\varphi(t)=\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}1,\ \ \quad\rho\leq t\leq 2\rho,\\[11.38109pt]
0,\ \ \quad 0\leq t\leq\rho/2\ \ \mbox{or}\ \ t\geq 3\rho\end{array}\right.}

such that

t^{2}\left(|\varphi^{\prime}(t)|^{2}+|\varphi^{\prime\prime}(t)|\right)\leq c

(3.5)

for some universal constant
c
and
\rho\geq 2r_{0}
(arbitrarily large).

Now we take
\varphi=\varphi(F(x))
as a cut-off function, with support in
D(3\rho)
. Then, on
D(3\rho)\setminus D(\rho/2)
,

|\nabla\varphi|=|\varphi^{\prime}\nabla F|\leq\frac{c}{\rho}|\nabla F|\qquad\mbox{and}

(3.6)

|\Delta_{f}\varphi|=|\varphi^{\prime}\Delta_{f}F+\varphi^{\prime\prime}|\nabla F|^{2}|\leq\frac{c}{\rho}F+\frac{c}{\rho^{2}}|\nabla F|^{2}\leq 2c.

(3.7)

Set
G=\varphi^{2}u
. Then direct computations, (3.4), (3.6) and (3.7) yield that

\displaystyle\varphi^{2}\Delta_{f}G

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\varphi^{4}\Delta_{f}u+G\Delta_{f}(\varphi^{2})+2\varphi^{2}\nabla(G\varphi^{-2})\cdot\nabla\varphi^{2}

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\varphi^{4}\left(u^{2}-C^{\prime}_{1}u-C^{\prime}_{2}\right)+G\left(2\varphi\Delta_{f}\varphi-6|\nabla\varphi|^{2}\right)+2\nabla G\cdot\nabla\varphi^{2}

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle G^{2}-C^{\prime}_{1}G-C^{\prime}_{2}+2\nabla G\cdot\nabla\varphi^{2}.

Now it follows from the standard maximum principle argument that
G\leq C_{3}
on
D(3\rho)
for some constant
C_{3}>0
depending on
A_{0}
and
R_{0}
, but independent of
\rho
. Hence
u=|Rm|+\lambda|Rc|^{2}\leq C_{3}
on
D(2\rho)\setminus D(\rho)
. Since
\rho\geq 2r_{0}
is arbitrarily large, we see that

\sup_{x\in M}|Rm|\leq\sup_{x\in M}\left(|Rm|+\lambda|Rc|^{2}\right)\leq C,

for some constant
C>0
depending on
A_{0}
,
R_{0}
and the geometry of
D(r_{0})
.
This completes the proof of Proposition 3.3.
∎

Proposition 3.4
.

Let
(M^{4},g,f)
be a
4
-dimensional complete noncompact gradient expanding Ricci soliton with nonnegative Ricci curvature
Ric\geq 0
and positive scalar curvature
R>0
.
Then, for any
0<a<1
, we have

\displaystyle(i)\ \Delta_{f-2a\ln R}\large(R^{-a}{|Rm|}\large)

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle-CR^{-a}|Rm|+a(1-a)R^{-a}|Rm||\nabla\ln R|^{2},\ \mbox{and}

\displaystyle(ii)\ \Delta_{f-2a\ln R}\large(R^{-2a}{|Rc|^{2}}\large)

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle 2(1-a)R^{-2a}{|\nabla Rc|^{2}}-CR^{-2a}{|Rc|^{2}}.

Here,
C>0
is a constant depending on
A_{0}
,
R_{0}
and the geometry of
D(r_{0})
.

Proof.

By direct computation and Lemma 2.4, we have

\Delta_{f}(R^{-a})=aR^{-a}[1+2R^{-1}|Rc|^{2}+(a+1)R^{-2}|\nabla R|^{2}].

(3.8)

Next, by Lemma 2.5, Proposition 3.3, identity (3.8) and direct computations,

\displaystyle\Delta_{f}\left(\frac{|Rm|}{R^{a}}\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle R^{-a}\Delta_{f}|Rm|+|Rm|\Delta_{f}(R^{-a})+2\nabla(R^{-a}|Rm|R^{a})\cdot\nabla(R^{-a})

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle-C\frac{|Rm|}{R^{a}}+a\frac{|Rm|}{R^{a}}\left(1+2\frac{|Rc|^{2}}{R}+(a+1)|\nabla\ln R|^{2}\right)

\displaystyle-2a\nabla(\frac{|Rm|}{R^{a}})\cdot\nabla\ln R-2a^{2}\frac{|Rm|}{R^{a}}|\nabla\ln R|^{2}

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle-C\frac{|Rm|}{R^{a}}+a(1-a)\frac{|Rm|}{R^{a}}|\nabla\ln R|^{2}-2a\nabla\left(\frac{|Rm|}{R^{a}}\right)\cdot\nabla\ln R,

where
C
depends on
A_{0}
,
R_{0}
and the geometry of
D(r_{0})
. This proves the first inequality.

Similarly, by Lemma 2.5, Proposition 3.3 and (3.8), we have

\displaystyle\Delta_{f}\left(\frac{|Rc|^{2}}{R^{2a}}\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle R^{-2a}\Delta_{f}(|Rc|^{2})+|Rc|^{2}\Delta_{f}(R^{-2a})+2\nabla|Rc|^{2}\cdot\nabla(R^{-2a})

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle 2\frac{|\nabla Rc|^{2}}{R^{2a}}-C\frac{|Rc|^{2}}{R^{2a}}+2a\frac{|Rc|^{2}}{R^{2a}}\left(1+2\frac{|Rc|^{2}}{R}+(2a+1)|\nabla\ln R|^{2}\right)

\displaystyle-2a\nabla\left(\frac{|Rc|^{2}}{R^{2a}}\right)\cdot\nabla\ln R-4a^{2}\frac{|Rc|^{2}}{R^{2a}}|\nabla\ln R|^{2}-4a\frac{|Rc|}{R^{a}}\frac{|\nabla Rc|}{R^{a}}|\nabla\ln R|

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle 2\frac{|\nabla Rc|^{2}}{R^{2a}}-C\frac{|Rc|^{2}}{R^{2a}}+2a\frac{|Rc|^{2}}{R^{2a}}|\nabla\ln R|^{2}-4a\frac{|Rc|}{R^{a}}\frac{|\nabla Rc|}{R^{a}}|\nabla\ln R|

\displaystyle-2a\nabla\left(\frac{|Rc|^{2}}{R^{2a}}\right)\cdot\nabla\ln R.

On the other hand,

2a\frac{|\nabla Rc|^{2}}{R^{2a}}+2a\frac{|Rc|^{2}}{R^{2a}}|\nabla\ln R|^{2}\geq 4a\frac{|Rc|}{R^{a}}\frac{|\nabla Rc|}{R^{a}}|\nabla\ln R|.

Therefore,

\Delta_{f}\left(\frac{|Rc|^{2}}{R^{2a}}\right)\geq 2(1-a)\frac{|\nabla Rc|^{2}}{R^{2a}}-C\frac{|Rc|^{2}}{R^{2a}}-2a\nabla\left(\frac{|Rc|^{2}}{R^{2a}}\right)\cdot\nabla\ln R,

which is the desired second inequality.
∎

Now we are ready to prove the main estimate on the curvature tensor
Rm
.

Theorem 3.1
.

Let
(M^{4},g,f)
be a
4
-dimensional complete noncompact gradient expanding Ricci soliton with nonnegative Ricci curvature
Rc\geq 0
.
Then, there exists some constant
C>0
, depending on
A_{0}
,
R_{0}
and the geometry of
D(r_{0})
, such that for any
0<a<1
,

|Rm|\leq\frac{C}{1-a}R^{a}\quad\mbox{on}\ M^{4}.

Proof.

By Lemma 2.3, it suffices to consider the case when the scalar curvature
R
is positive.
For any
a\in(0,1)
, let

v=\frac{|Rm|}{R^{a}}+\frac{|Rc|^{2}}{R^{2a}}.

Then, by Proposition 3.4 and Proposition 3.2 (with
\Lambda=4A_{0}^{2}
),

\displaystyle\Delta_{f-2a\ln R}(v)

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle 2(1-a)\frac{|\nabla Rc|^{2}}{R^{2a}}-C_{1}\frac{|Rm|}{R^{a}}-C_{2}\frac{|Rc|^{2}}{R^{2a}}

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle 4(1-a)\frac{|Rm|^{2}}{R^{2a}}-8(1-a)A_{0}^{2}\frac{|Rc|^{2}}{R^{2a}}-C_{1}\frac{|Rm|}{R^{a}}-C_{2}\frac{|Rc|^{2}}{R^{2a}}

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle 2(1-a)v^{2}-C_{1}v-C_{2}\qquad\mbox{on}\ M\setminus D(r_{0}),

where
C_{1}>0
and
C_{2}>0
depend on
A_{0}
and
R_{0}
.

Set
G=\varphi^{2}v
. Then, by taking the same cut-off function
\varphi(t)
as in the proof of Proposition 3.3, we get

\displaystyle\varphi^{2}\Delta_{f-2a\ln R}G

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\varphi^{4}\Delta_{f-2a\ln R}(v)+G\Delta_{f-2a\ln R}(\varphi^{2})+2\varphi^{2}\nabla(G\varphi^{-2})\cdot\nabla\varphi^{2}

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle 2(1-a)G^{2}-C_{1}G-C_{2}+G\Delta_{f-2a\ln R}(\varphi^{2})

\displaystyle+2\nabla G\cdot\nabla\varphi^{2}-8G|\nabla\varphi|^{2}.

Note that, by (3.6),

|\nabla\varphi|^{2}\leq\frac{c^{2}}{\rho^{2}}|\nabla F|^{2}\leq c.

Moreover,

\Delta_{f-2a\ln R}(\varphi)=\Delta_{f}(\varphi)+2a\nabla\varphi\cdot\nabla\ln R

(3.9)

and

|\nabla\ln R|\leq 2\frac{|Rc|}{R}|\nabla F|\leq 2|\nabla F|.

(3.10)

Hence

|\nabla\varphi\cdot\nabla\ln R|\leq\frac{2c}{\rho}|\nabla F|^{2}\leq 2c,

(3.11)

By (3.7), (3.9) and (3.11), we obtain

\Delta_{f-2a\ln R}(\varphi)\geq-6c

(3.12)

for some universal constant
c>0
.

Therefore,

\varphi^{2}\Delta_{f-2a\ln R}G\geq 2(1-a)G^{2}-C_{1}G-C_{2}+2\nabla G\cdot\nabla\varphi^{2}.

Now, by the same maximum principle argument as in the proof of Proposition 3.3, it follows that
v\leq\frac{C}{1-a}
on
M^{n}
for some constant
C>0
depending on
A_{0}
,
R_{0}
and the geometry of
D(r_{0})
. Therefore,

\frac{|Rm|}{R^{a}}\leq\frac{|Rm|}{R^{a}}+\frac{|Rc|^{2}}{R^{2a}}\leq\frac{C}{1-a}\quad\mbox{on}\ M^{4}.

∎

We can also prove a similar estimate for the covariant derivative
\nabla Rm
of the curvature tensor.

Theorem 3.2
.

Let
(M^{4},g,f)
be a
4
-dimensional complete noncompact gradient expanding Ricci soliton with nonnegative Ricci curvature
Rc\geq 0
.
Then, there exists some constant
C>0
, depending on
A_{0}
,
R_{0}
and the geometry of
D(r_{0})
, such that for any
0\leq a<1
,

|\nabla Rm|\leq\frac{C}{(1-a)^{2}}R^{a}\quad\mbox{on}\ M^{4}.

Proof.

Without loss of generality, we can again assume the scalar curvature
R>0
.

First of all, by Lemma 2.6 and Proposition 3.3, we have

\Delta_{f}|\nabla Rm|\geq-\frac{3}{2}|\nabla Rm|-c|Rm||\nabla Rm|\geq-C|\nabla Rm|.

Then, by (3.8) and direct computations,

\displaystyle\Delta_{f}\frac{|\nabla Rm|}{R^{a}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle R^{-a}\Delta_{f}|\nabla Rm|+|\nabla Rm|\Delta_{f}(R^{-a})+2\nabla(R^{-a}|\nabla Rm|R^{a})\cdot\nabla(R^{-a})

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle-CR^{-a}|\nabla Rm|+aR^{-a}|\nabla Rm|[1+2R^{-1}|Rc|^{2}+(a+1)|\nabla\ln R|^{2}]

\displaystyle-2a\nabla(R^{-a}{|\nabla Rm|})\cdot\nabla\ln R-2a^{2}R^{-a}{|\nabla Rm|}\cdot|\nabla\ln R|^{2}

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle-C\frac{|\nabla Rm|}{R^{a}}+a(1-a)\frac{|\nabla Rm|}{R^{a}}|\nabla\ln R|^{2}-2a\nabla\left(\frac{|\nabla Rm|}{R^{a}}\right)\cdot\nabla\ln R.

Also, by Lemma 2.5 and Proposition 3.3, we have

\Delta_{f}|Rm|^{2}\geq 2|\nabla Rm|^{2}-2|Rm|^{2}-c|Rm|^{3}\geq 2|\nabla Rm|^{2}-C|Rm|^{2}.

Then, by (3.8) and direct computations, we obtain

\displaystyle\Delta_{f}\frac{|Rm|^{2}}{R^{2a}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle R^{-2a}\Delta_{f}(|Rm|^{2})+|Rm|^{2}\Delta_{f}(R^{-2a})+2\nabla|Rm|^{2}\cdot\nabla(R^{-2a})

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle 2\frac{|\nabla Rm|^{2}}{R^{2a}}-C\frac{|Rm|^{2}}{R^{2a}}+2a\frac{|Rm|^{2}}{R^{2a}}\left(1+2\frac{|Rc|^{2}}{R}+(2a+1)\frac{|\nabla R|^{2}}{R^{2}}\right)

\displaystyle-2a\nabla\left(\frac{|Rm|^{2}}{R^{2a}}\right)\cdot\frac{\nabla R}{R}-4a^{2}\frac{|Rm|^{2}}{R^{2a}}\frac{|\nabla R|^{2}}{R^{2}}-4a\frac{|Rm|}{R^{a}}\frac{|\nabla Rm|}{R^{a}}\frac{|\nabla R|}{R}

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle 2\frac{|\nabla Rm|^{2}}{R^{2a}}-C\frac{|Rm|^{2}}{R^{2a}}-2a\nabla\left(\frac{|Rm|^{2}}{R^{2a}}\right)\cdot\frac{\nabla R}{R}

\displaystyle+2a\frac{|Rm|^{2}}{R^{2a}}\frac{|\nabla R|^{2}}{R^{2}}-4a\frac{|Rm|}{R^{a}}\frac{|\nabla Rm|}{R^{a}}\frac{|\nabla R|}{R}

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle 2(1-a)\frac{|\nabla Rm|^{2}}{R^{2a}}-C\frac{|Rm|^{2}}{R^{2a}}-2a\nabla\left(\frac{|Rm|^{2}}{R^{2a}}\right)\cdot\frac{\nabla R}{R}.

Thus, by setting

w=\frac{|\nabla Rm|}{R^{a}}+\frac{|Rm|^{2}}{R^{2a}},

we have

\displaystyle\Delta_{f-2a\ln R}(w)

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle 2(1-a)\frac{|\nabla Rm|^{2}}{R^{2a}}-C\frac{|\nabla Rm|}{R^{a}}-C\frac{|Rm|^{2}}{R^{2a}}

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle(1-a)\left(\frac{|\nabla Rm|}{R^{a}}+\frac{|Rm|^{2}}{R^{2a}}\right)^{2}-2(1-a)\frac{|Rm|^{4}}{R^{4a}}

\displaystyle-C\left(\frac{|\nabla Rm|}{R^{a}}+\frac{|Rm|^{2}}{R^{2a}}\right)

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle(1-a)w^{2}-C_{1}w-C_{2}(1-a)^{-3},

where in the last inequality we have used Theorem 3.1.

Again, by setting
G=\varphi^{2}w
, taking the same cut-off function
\varphi(t)
as before, and applying the same maximum principle argument as in the proof of Theorem 3.1, we get the estimate
w\leq{C}/{(1-a)^{2}}
on
M^{n}
for some constant
C>0
depending on
A_{0}
,
R_{0}
and the geometry of
D(r_{0})
. Therefore,

\frac{|\nabla Rm|}{R^{a}}\leq\frac{\nabla|Rm|}{R^{a}}+\frac{|Rm|^{2}}{R^{2a}}\leq\frac{C}{(1-a)^{2}}\quad\mbox{on}\ M^{4}.

This completes the proof of Theorem 3.2.

∎

To finish the proof of Theorem 1.1, it remains to show the following simple fact.

Proposition 3.5
.

Let
(M^{4},g,f)
be a
4
-dimensional complete noncompact gradient expanding Ricci soliton with nonnegative Ricci curvature
Rc\geq 0
. Suppose in addition the scalar curvature
R
has at most polynomial decay, then
there exists some constant
C>0
such that

{|Rm|}\leq CR.

Proof.

Suppose the scalar curvature
R
has at most polynomial decay, say
R\geq C/{r(x)^{d}}
for some positive integer
d\geq 1
outside some compact set. Then, by (2.4) and Lemmas 2.2-2.3, we get

R\geq\frac{C}{r(x)^{d}}\geq\frac{C}{|\nabla f|^{d}}.

(3.13)

Now it follows from Proposition 3.1, (3.13) and Theorem 3.2 (with
a=1-1/d
) that

\displaystyle|Rm|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle A_{0}\left(|Rc|+\frac{|\nabla Rc|}{|\nabla f|}\right)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle A_{0}(R+CdR^{1-1/d}R^{1/d})\leq C_{1}R.

∎

Finally, we prove
Theorem 1.2
stated in the introduction.

Proof.

Recall that, by Proposition 3.1, we have

|Rm|\leq A_{0}\ \left(|Rc|+\frac{|\nabla Rc|}{|\nabla f|}\right).

(3.14)

On the other hand, it follows from
Rc\geq 0
and the assumption of the finite asymptotic scalar curvature ratio (1.5) that

|Rc|\leq R\leq\frac{C_{1}}{r^{2}},

(3.15)

for some constant
C_{1}>0
.
Moreover, by picking
a=1/2
in Theorem 1.1, we get

|\nabla Rc|\leq c|\nabla Rm|\leq CR^{1/2}\leq\frac{C_{2}}{r},

(3.16)

while

|\nabla f|^{2}=-f-R=O(r^{2})

(3.17)

by (2.4), Lemma 2.2, and Lemma 2.3.

Plugging (3.15)-(3.17) into (3.14) leads to

|Rm|\leq\frac{C}{r^{2}}.

This completes the proof of Theorem 1.2.
∎

4.
Curvature estimates for 4D expanders with
R>0
and proper
f

In this section, we consider
4
-dimensional complete noncompact gradient expanding Ricci solitons with bounded, positive scalar curvature and proper potential function, and prove Theorem 1.4 stated in the introduction.

Let
(M^{4},g,f)
be a
4
-dimensional
complete noncompact gradient expanding Ricci soliton with bounded, positive scalar curvature
0<R\leq R_{0}
and proper potential function
f
so that
\lim_{r(x)\to\infty}f=-\infty
. Then it follows from the work of P.-Y. Chan
[
18
]
(see also Remark 1.1) that

\sup_{x\in M}|Rm|\leq C_{0}

(4.1)

for some constant
C_{0}>0
.

On the other hand, by making use of Chan’s curvature bound (4.1), one can obtain the following estimate for the Ricci tensor.

Proposition 4.1
.

Let
(M^{4},g,f)
be a
4
-dimensional complete noncompact gradient expanding Ricci soliton with bounded and positive scalar curvature
0<R\leq R_{0}
. Assume
f
is proper so that
\lim_{r(x)\to\infty}f(x)=-\infty
. Then, there exists a positive constant
C>0
such that

|Rc|^{2}\leq CR\quad on\ M^{4}.

Proof.

We first proceed as in the proof of Proposition 3.4 (ii) to derive another differential inequality for
\Delta_{f}(R^{-1}|Rc|^{2})
.
For any
0<a\leq 1
, by Lemma 2.5, (3.8), curvature bound (4.1) and direct computations, we have

\displaystyle\Delta_{f}\left(\frac{|Rc|^{2}}{R^{a}}\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle R^{-a}\Delta_{f}(|Rc|^{2})+|Rc|^{2}\Delta_{f}(R^{-a})+2\nabla|Rc|^{2}\cdot\nabla(R^{-a})

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle 2\frac{|\nabla Rc|^{2}}{R^{a}}-(4C_{0}+2)\frac{|Rc|^{2}}{R^{a}}+a\frac{|Rc|^{2}}{R^{a}}\left(2\frac{|Rc|^{2}}{R}+(a+1)|\nabla\ln R|^{2}\right)

\displaystyle-4a\frac{|Rc||\nabla Rc|}{R^{a}}|\nabla\ln R\large|.

Since

4a\frac{|Rc||\nabla Rc|}{R^{a}}|\nabla\ln R|\leq\frac{4a}{a+1}\frac{|\nabla Rc|^{2}}{R^{a}}+a(a+1)\frac{|Rc|^{2}}{R^{a}}|\nabla\ln R|^{2},

it follows that

\Delta_{f}\left(\frac{|Rc|^{2}}{R^{a}}\right)\geq\frac{2(1-a)}{1+a}\frac{|\nabla Rc|^{2}}{R^{a}}-(4C_{0}+2)\frac{|Rc|^{2}}{R^{a}}+2a\frac{|Rc|^{4}}{R^{1+a}}.

(4.2)

Hence, by taking
a=1
and setting
u=\frac{|Rc|^{2}}{R}
, we get

\Delta_{f}(u)\geq 2u^{2}-(4C_{0}+1)u.

Now, by using the same cut-off function and the same maximum principle argument as in the proof of Proposition 3.3, we obtain the estimate

|Rc|^{2}\leq CR\quad on\ M,

for some positive constant
C>0
.
∎

Moreover, by considering a quantity of the form
\frac{|Rm|^{2p}}{R^{b}}+\frac{|Rc|^{2}}{R^{a}}
suggested to us by P.-Y. Chan, we can actually derive estimates on
Rm
and
\nabla Rm
in terms of the scalar curvature
R
. Now we restate Theorem 1.4 here.

Theorem 4.1
.

Let
(M^{4},g,f)
be a
4
-dimensional complete noncompact gradient expanding Ricci soliton with bounded and positive scalar curvature
0<R\leq R_{0}
. Assume
f
is proper so that
\lim_{r(x)\to\infty}f(x)=-\infty
. Then, for any
\alpha\in(0,1/2)
,

|Rm|\leq C_{\alpha}R^{\alpha}\quad{\mbox{and}}\quad|\nabla Rm|\leq C_{\alpha}R^{\alpha}\quad on\ M^{4}

for some positive constant
C_{\alpha}>0
, with
C_{\alpha}\to\infty
as
\alpha\to 1/2
.

Proof.

First of all, for any
0<a<1
, from (4.2) we have

\Delta_{f}\frac{|Rc|^{2}}{R^{a}}\geq\frac{2(1-a)}{1+a}\frac{|\nabla Rc|^{2}}{R^{a}}-(4C_{0}+2)\frac{|Rc|^{2}}{R^{a}}.

(4.3)

On the other hand, by using Lemma 2.5, (3.8), curvature bound (4.1) and direct computations, for any
p>0
and
b>0
we get (whenever
|Rm|\neq 0
in case
p<1
)

\displaystyle\Delta_{f}\frac{|Rm|^{2p}}{R^{b}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle R^{-b}\Delta_{f}(|Rm|^{2p})+|Rm|^{2p}\Delta_{f}(R^{-b})+2\nabla(|Rm|^{2p})\cdot\nabla(R^{-b})

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle 2p(2p-1)\frac{|Rm|^{2p-2}}{R^{b}}|\nabla|Rm||^{2}-Cp\frac{|Rm|^{2p}}{R^{b}}

\displaystyle+b(b+1)\frac{|Rm|^{2p}}{R^{b}}|\nabla\ln R|^{2}-4pb\frac{|Rm|^{2p-1}}{R^{b}}|\nabla|Rm||\ \!|\nabla\ln R|

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle[2p(2p-1)-(b+1)^{-1}{4p^{2}}b]\frac{|Rm|^{2p-2}}{R^{b}}|\nabla|Rm||^{2}-Cp\frac{|Rm|^{2p}}{R^{b}}.

Now, for any
b\in(0,1)
, if we choose
2p=1+b\in(1,2)
then

2p(2p-1)-\frac{4p^{2}b}{b+1}=0,

hence

\Delta_{f}\frac{|Rm|^{1+b}}{R^{b}}\geq-C\frac{|Rm|^{1+b}}{R^{b}}.

(4.4)

By combining (4.3) and (4.4), and applying Proposition 3.2 (with
\Lambda=2A_{0}^{2}
) and Proposition 4.1, we obtain

\displaystyle\Delta_{f}\left(\frac{|Rm|^{1+b}}{R^{b}}+\frac{|Rc|^{2}}{R^{a}}\right)

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle(1-a)\frac{|\nabla Rc|^{2}}{R^{a}}-C_{1}\left(\frac{|Rm|^{1+b}}{R^{b}}+\frac{|Rc|^{2}}{R^{a}}\right)

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle(1-a)\frac{|Rm|^{2}}{R^{a}}-C_{1}\left(\frac{|Rm|^{1+b}}{R^{b}}+\frac{|Rc|^{2}}{R^{a}}\right)-C_{2}.

Finally, for any
\alpha\in(0,1/2)
, we choose
a=2\alpha\in(0,1),\ b=\alpha/(1-\alpha)<a
and set

v=\frac{|Rm|^{1+b}}{R^{b}}+\frac{|Rc|^{2}}{R^{a}}.

Without loss of generality, by Proposition 4.1, we may assume
v\leq 2R^{-b}|Rm|^{1+b}
. Then, we have

\Delta_{f}(v)\geq\frac{1-2\alpha}{4}v^{2(1-\alpha)}-C_{1}v-C_{2}.

(4.5)

Since
1<2(1-\alpha)
, by using the same cut-off function as in Proposition 3.3 and applying the standard maximum principle argument, we conclude from (4.5) that
v\leq C_{\alpha}
for some positive constant
C_{\alpha}>0
, with
C_{\alpha}\to\infty
as
\alpha\to 1/2
.

Therefore,
|Rm|^{1+b}\leq C_{\alpha}{R^{b}}
, or equivalently

|Rm|\leq C_{\alpha}R^{\frac{b}{1+b}}=C_{\alpha}R^{\alpha}\quad on\ M^{4}.

Similarly, by using Lemma 2.6, (3.8), curvature bound (4.1) and direct computations, we get (whenever
|Rm|\neq 0
)

\displaystyle\Delta_{f}\frac{|\nabla Rm|^{2p}}{R^{b}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle R^{-b}\Delta_{f}|\nabla Rm|^{2p}+|\nabla Rm|^{2p}\Delta_{f}(R^{-b})+2\nabla(|\nabla Rm|^{2p})\cdot\nabla(R^{-b})

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle[2p(2p-1)-(b+1)^{-1}{4p^{2}}b]\frac{|\nabla Rm|^{2p-2}}{R^{b}}|\nabla|\nabla Rm||^{2}-Cp\frac{|\nabla Rm|^{2p}}{R^{b}}

and, for any
0<a<1
,

\Delta_{f}\frac{|Rm|^{2}}{R^{a}}\geq\frac{2(1-a)}{1+a}\frac{|\nabla Rm|^{2}}{R^{a}}-C\frac{|Rm|^{2}}{R^{a}}.

Combining the above two inequalities and choosing
2p=1+b
, we obtain

\Delta_{f}\left(\frac{|\nabla Rm|^{1+b}}{R^{b}}+\frac{|Rm|^{2}}{R^{a}}\right)\geq(1-a)\frac{|\nabla Rm|^{2}}{R^{a}}-C_{3}\left(\frac{|\nabla Rm|^{1+b}}{R^{b}}+\frac{|Rm|^{2}}{R^{a}}\right).

Now, for any
\alpha\in(0,1/2)
, we choose
a=2\alpha\in(0,1),\ b=\alpha/(1-\alpha)<a
so that

\displaystyle\Delta_{f}\left(\frac{|\nabla Rm|^{1+b}}{R^{b}}+\frac{|Rm|^{2}}{R^{a}}\right)

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle(1-2\alpha)\left(\frac{|\nabla Rm|^{1+b}}{R^{b}}\right)^{2(1-\alpha)}

\displaystyle-C_{3}\left(\frac{|\nabla Rm|^{1+b}}{R^{b}}+\frac{|Rm|^{2}}{R^{a}}\right).

Since
1<2(1-\alpha)
and
|Rm|^{2}\leq C_{a}R^{a}
, by using the same cut-off function as in Proposition 3.3 and applying the standard maximum principle argument, we conclude that

|\nabla Rm|\leq C_{\alpha}R^{\alpha}\quad on\ M^{4}

for some positive constant
C_{\alpha}>0
with
C_{\alpha}\to\infty
as
\alpha\to 1/2
.
∎

5.
Further Remarks

5.1.
Extension of Theorem 1.1 and Theorem 1.2

In the more general case when the Ricci curvature of
(M^{4},g,f)
is only nonnegative outside some compact set, the same argument as in the proof of Theorem 1.1 goes through without any changes. Indeed, under the assumption of
Rc\geq 0
outside a compact set
K\subset M
, it is not hard to check that the asymptotic quadratic growth estimate (2.5), outside
K
, for the potential function and the scalar curvature upper bound
R\leq R_{0}
(for some
R_{0}>0
) remain true. Moreover, the gradient estimate (3.10) for the scalar curvature clearly holds on
M\setminus K
as well. Thus, we have

Theorem 5.1
.

Let
(M^{4},g,f)
be a
4
-dimensional complete noncompact
gradient expanding Ricci soliton with positive scalar curvature
R>0
. Suppose the Ricci curvature of
(M^{4},g,f)
is nonnegative outside some compact set
K\subset M
. Then, for any
0\leq a<1
, there exists a constant
C>0
(independent of
a
) such that

|Rm|\leq\frac{C}{1-a}R^{a}\quad{\mbox{and}}\quad|\nabla Rm|\leq\frac{C}{(1-a)^{2}}R^{a}\quad on\ M^{4}.

(5.1)

Moreover, suppose in addition the scalar curvature
R
has at most polynomial decay at infinity, then

{|Rm|}\leq CR\quad on\ M^{4}.

(5.2)

By Theorem 5.1, (3.17) and Proposition 3.1, we also obtain the following result on asymptotic curvature behavior.

Theorem 5.2
.

Let
(M^{4},g,f)
be a
4
-dimensional complete noncompact
gradient expanding Ricci soliton with positive scalar curvature
R>0
. Suppose the Ricci curvature of
(M^{4},g,f)
is nonnegative outside some compact set
K\subset M
and the asymptotic scalar curvature ratio is finite

\limsup_{r\to\infty}Rr^{2}<\infty,

where
r=r(x)
is the distance function to a fixed base point in
M
.
Then
(M^{4},g,f)
has finite asymptotic curvature ratio

\limsup_{r\to\infty}|Rm|r^{2}<\infty.

5.2.
Open questions

As mentioned in the introduction, the scalar curvature lower bound
R\geq C/f
proved by Chow-Lu-Yang
[
26
]
for complete gradient shrinking solitons played a crucial role in the work of Munteanu-Wang
[
46
]
. On the other hand, if there is any polynomial lower bound for
R
, then we can achieve the sharp curvature estimate
|Rm|\leq CR
in the expanding case by Theorem 1.1.

Question 1
. Suppose
(M^{n},g,f)
is a complete noncompact non-flat gradient expanding soliton with nonnegative Ricci curvature
Rc\geq 0
. Does its scalar curvature
R
have at most polynomial decay, i.e.,
R\geq C/F^{d}
for some
d\geq 2
, at infinity?

Remark 5.1
.

Recently, P.-Y. Chan
[
18
]
has proved an exponential decay scalar curvature lower bound for expanding solitons with positive scalar curvature
R>0
and proper
f
: Suppose
(M^{n},g,f)
,
n\geq 2
, is any
n
-dimensional complete noncompact gradient expanding Ricci soliton with positive scalar curvature
R>0
and proper potential function
F=-f+n/2
. Then

R\geq CF^{1-\frac{n}{2}}e^{-F}\quad on\ M^{n}

for some constant
C>0
.

Remark 5.2
.

Based on the constructions of Deruelle
[
32
,
33
]
, very recently Chan and Zhu
[
19
]
have exhibited an example of
3
-dimensional complete asymptotically conical expanding soliton with nonnegative curvature operator
Rm\geq 0
such that

\liminf_{r\to\infty}\ F|Rm|=0\quad\mbox{and}\ \limsup_{r\to\infty}\ F|Rm|<\infty.

Next, gradient estimates for scalar curvature
R
is quite essential whenever we involve the operator
\Delta_{f-2\ln R}
in the maximum principle argument.
In the shrinking case, Munteanu-Wang
[
46
]
initially obtained the gradient estimate

|\nabla\ln R|^{2}\leq C\ln(f+2),

and used it to derive the sharp
Rc
estimate
|Rc|\leq CR
and the estimate
|\nabla Rm|\leq CR
. Similarly, for Ricci expanders with
Rc\geq 0
, the gradient estimate in (3.10),

|\nabla\ln R|^{2}\leq 4|\nabla F|^{2}\leq 4F,

is used to obtain the almost sharp estimate on
Rm
and the estimate on

|\nabla Rm|
in Theorem 1.1.

Question 2
. Suppose
(M^{4},g,f)
is a complete noncompact gradient expanding Ricci soliton with
0<R\leq R_{0}
and proper
f
. Does the gradient estimate

|\nabla\ln R|\leq C|\nabla F|

(5.3)

(or more precisely,
R^{-1}{Rc(\nabla F,\nabla F)}\leq C
)
hold for some constant
C>0
?

Remark 5.3
.

If (5.3) holds, then one would be able to improve Theorem 1.4 (Theorem 4.1) to
|Rm|^{2}\leq CR
and
|\nabla Rm|^{2}\leq CR
.

Moreover, in the shrinking case, the estimate
|\nabla Rm|\leq CR
of Munteanu and Wang
[
46
]
implies the sharper gradient estimate

|\nabla\ln R|\leq C\quad\text{on}\ M.

(5.4)

In the expanding case, from
|\nabla Rm|\leq CR^{a}
for any
0<a<1
, we also have

|\nabla\ln R|\leq C_{a}R^{a-1}\quad\text{on}\ M.

(5.5)

Question 3
. Does gradient estimate (5.4) for the scalar curvature hold for 4-dimensional gradient expanding Ricci solitons with nonnegative Ricci curvature?

Remark 5.4
.

If the answer to Question 3 is affirmative, then one would be able to improve the conclusions in Theorem 1.1 to
|Rm|\leq CR
and
|\nabla Rm|\leq CR
without any extra assumption.
