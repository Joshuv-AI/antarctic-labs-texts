Abstract
. Let ( V , ω ) (V,\omega) be a compact Kähler manifold such that V V admits a cover by Zariski-open Stein sets with the property that ω \omega has a strictly plurisubharmonic exhaustive potential on each element of the cover. If X ⊂ V X\subset V is an analytic subvariety, we prove that any ω | X \omega|_{X} -plurisubharmonic function on X X extends to a ω \omega -plurisubharmonic function on V V . 1.
Introduction

Let
(V,\omega)
be a compact Kähler manifold of dimension
n
.
We recall that a function
\psi:V\to[-\infty,+\infty)
is called quasiplurisubharmonic (qpsh) if it is locally the sum of a plurisubharmonic (psh) function and a smooth one.
If
\psi
is qpsh then

\psi\in L^{1}(V,\omega^{n})

and

\psi
is called
\omega
-plurisubharmonic (
\omega
-psh) if
\omega+dd^{c}\psi\geq 0
in the sense of currents on
V
. Here
d=\partial+\overline{\partial}
,
d^{c}=\frac{1}{2\pi i}\,(\partial-\overline{\partial})
. We denote by
PSH(V,\omega)
the class of
\omega
-psh functions on
V
and refer the reader to
[
GZ05
,
GZ17
]
for their basic properties.

Let now
X\subset V
be a (closed) analytic subvariety. An upper semicontinuous function
\varphi:X\to[-\infty,+\infty)
is called
\omega|_{X}
-psh if
\varphi\not\equiv-\infty
on
X
and if
there exist an open cover
\{U_{\alpha}\}_{\alpha\in A}
of
X
and psh functions
\varphi_{\alpha},\rho_{\alpha}
defined on
U_{\alpha}
, where
\rho_{\alpha}
is smooth and
dd^{c}\rho_{\alpha}=\omega
, such that
\rho_{\alpha}+\varphi=\varphi_{\alpha}
holds on
X\cap U_{\alpha}
, for every
\alpha\in A
.
Note that we allow
\varphi
to be identically
-\infty
on some (but not all) irreducible components of
X
.

The function
\varphi
is called
strictly

\omega|_{X}
-psh if
\varphi
is not identically
-\infty
on any irreducible component of
X
and if it is
(1-\varepsilon)\omega|_{X}
-psh for some small
\varepsilon>0
. The current
\omega|_{X}+dd^{c}\varphi
is then called a Kähler current on
X
.
We denote by
PSH(X,\omega|_{X})
, resp.
PSH^{+}(X,\omega|_{X})
, the class of
\omega|_{X}
-psh, resp. strictly
\omega|_{X}
-psh functions on
X
. For a detailed discussion of psh functions on complex spaces, we refer the reader to
[
FN80
]
and
[
D85
, section 1]
.

By restriction,
\omega
-psh functions on
V
yield
\omega|_{X}
-psh functions on
X
. An interesting problem is whether every
\omega|_{X}
-psh function
\varphi
on
X
arises in this way:

Problem
.

Does one have

PSH(V,\omega)|_{X}=PSH\left(X,\omega|_{X}\right)\,?

In
[
CGZ13
, Theorem B]
, this was shown to be the case when
\omega
is a Hodge form, i.e.
\omega
is a representative of the Chern class
c_{1}(L)
of a positive holomorphic line bundle
L
over
V
(this requires the manifold
V
to be projective). The proof relies on an extension result with growth control of psh functions on analytic subvarieties of Stein manifolds
[
CGZ13
, Theorem A]
. This in turn used methods of Coltoiu based on Runge domains
[
Co91
, Proposition 2]
, and of Sadullaev
[
Sa82
]
(see also
[
BL03
, Theorem 3.2]
).

Besides being a natural one, this problem has found important applications in Kähler geometry,
allowing one to prove the continuity of Kähler-Einstein potentials
(see e.g.
[
EGZ09
,
EGZ17
,
Dar17
,
GGZ20
]
).

A related and slightly simpler question is:

Problem+
.

Does one have

PSH^{+}(V,\omega)|_{X}=PSH^{+}\left(X,\omega|_{X}\right)\,?

When
X
is a complex submanifold of
V
, it is not difficult to show that any smooth strictly
\omega|_{X}
-psh function on
X
extends to a smooth strictly
\omega
-psh function on
V
(see e.g.
[
CGZ13
, Proposition 2.1]
and
[
Sch98
]
).

In
[
CT14
]

it was shown that the answer to Problem+ is again positive for functions
with analytic singularities.
The proof
uses resolution of singularities and a gluing argument similar to that of Richberg
[
R68
]
.

A further result in this direction was recently obtained in
[
WZ20
,
NWZ21
]
.
Assuming that there exists a holomorphic retraction
U\to X
on a neighborhood
U\subset V
of
X
,
it is proved in
[
NWZ21
]
that any strictly
\omega|_{X}
-psh function on
X
extends to a strictly
\omega
-psh function on
V
.
These results
all assume that
X
is smooth.

When
X
is singular, the equality

PSH^{+}(V,\omega)|_{X}=PSH^{+}\left(X,\omega|_{X}\right)

holds if the
class
\{\omega\}
belongs to the real Neron-Severi space
NS_{\mathbb{R}}(V)
.
This follows from
[
CGZ13
, Theorem B]
through a simple density argument. Recall that

NS_{\mathbb{R}}(V)=NS(V)\otimes\mathbb{R}
, where

NS(V)=H^{1,1}(V,\mathbb{R})\cap H^{2}(V,\mathbb{Z})/torsion.

Thus Hodge classes are rational points of
NS_{\mathbb{R}}(V)
.

The main goal of this note is to show the following stronger result:

Theorem 1.1
.

Let
(V,\omega)
be a compact Kähler manifold and let
X
be an analytic subvariety of
V
.
If
\{\omega\}\in NS_{\mathbb{R}}(V)
then

PSH(V,\omega)|_{X}=PSH\left(X,\omega|_{X}\right).

Note that a compact Kähler manifold
(V,\omega)
such that
\{\omega\}\in NS_{\mathbb{R}}(V)
must be projective (see Lemma
3.1
).

We actually show that the methods developed in
[
CGZ13
]
can be adapted to prove that this extension property
holds for a more general class of compact Kähler manifolds
(V,\omega)
than the one of polarized projective manifolds
considered in
[
CGZ13
, Theorem B]
:

Definition 1.2
.

We say that
(V,\omega)
admits an
adapted Zariski-open Stein cover
if

•

V
can be covered by finitely many open Stein sets
V_{j}
,
1\leq j\leq N
, such that
H_{j}:=V\setminus V_{j}
is an analytic subvariety of
V
, and

•

there exist smooth exhaustion functions
\rho_{j}\geq 0
on each
V_{j}
such that
\omega=dd^{c}\rho_{j}
.

In this case we also say that
(V,\omega)
verifies condition (ZOS).

Note that the sets
V_{j}
are connected, as we assume
V
is connected and since
H_{j}
are analytic subvarieties. This notion has the following basic properties:

Proposition 1.3
.

Let
(V,\omega)
be a compact Kähler manifold. We have the following:

(i) Condition (ZOS) only depends on the cohomology class of
\omega
.

(ii) The set of Kähler classes
\{\omega\}
such that
(V,\omega)
satisfies condition (ZOS) is a convex cone.

(iii) If
\{\omega\}
is a Hodge class then
(V,\omega)
satisfies condition (ZOS).

It follows from the above observations that condition (ZOS) is satisfied for
any Kähler class in the real Neron-Severi space
NS_{\mathbb{R}}(V)
(see Lemma
3.1
).
We show moreover that these conditions are stable under restriction and products, as well as by pull-back by holomorphic coverings.

The main technical result of the note is the following extension theorem in this setting,
which is a generalization of
[
CGZ13
, Theorem B and Theorem 2.2]
:

Theorem 1.4
.

Let
(V,\omega)
be a compact Kähler manifold of dimension
n
which verifies condition (ZOS) and let
X
be an analytic subvariety of
V
. If
\varphi\in PSH(X,\omega|_{X})
then, given any constant
a>0
, there exists
\psi\in PSH(V,\omega)
such that
\psi|_{X}=\varphi
and
\max_{V}\psi<\max_{X}\varphi+a
.

Note that in Theorem
1.4
we do not assume that
V
is projective. Since qpsh functions on a compact Kähler manifold can be regularized (see
[
D92
,
BK07
]
), one has the following immediate corollary:

Corollary 1.5
.

Let
(V,\omega)
be a compact Kähler manifold which verifies condition (ZOS) and let
X
be an analytic subvariety of
V
.
If
\varphi\in PSH(X,\omega|_{X})
then there exists a sequence of smooth functions

\varphi_{j}\in PSH(X,\omega|_{X})\cap{\mathcal{C}}^{\infty}(X)
which decrease pointwise to
\varphi
.

This smoothing property plays a key role in establishing global continuity of singular Kähler-Einstein potentials
(see
[
EGZ09
, Theorem 2.1]
).
Corollary
1.5
thus provides an alternative proof of
[
GGZ20
, Theorem 3.9]
.
Note that the regularizing techniques of Demailly
[
D92
]
and Błocki-Kolodziej
[
BK07
]
break
down when
X
is singular.

Theorem
1.4
is proved in Section
2
. Theorem
1.1
follows from Theorem
1.4
and Proposition
1.3
(see Section
3.1

(v)
and Lemma
3.1
). In Section
3
we prove Proposition
1.3
and we collect some examples of compact Kähler manifolds which admit an adapted Zariski-open Stein cover. Following
[
Mat13
]
, we show in Proposition
3.5
that the reference cohomological class has to be Kähler for the extension property to hold.

Let

of dimension

\omega
-psh function on

on

on

(2.1)

\theta:=\max\{\theta_{j}:\,1\leq j\leq N\}.

It follows that
\theta\leq 0
is a continuous
\omega
-psh function on

(2.2)

The following lemma gives special subextensions of
\omega|_{X}
-psh functions on

Lemma 2.1
.

Let
\varepsilon\geq 0
and

(1+\varepsilon)\omega
-psh function on

such that

for all

and
\varphi

\omega|_{X}
-psh function on

-psh function

\frac{1}{c}\,\psi(z)\leq\frac{1}{1+\varepsilon}\,u(z),\;\forall z\in V,

\psi(z)=\varphi(z)+(c-1)\theta(z)+(c-1)\min_{\zeta\in V}u(\zeta),\;\forall z\in X.

Proof.

Let
X_{j}=X\cap V_{j}
. We may assume that

for

, and
X_{j}=\emptyset
for

Let

\varphi+\rho_{j}+M<u+\rho_{j}+M\leq\frac{1}{1+\varepsilon}\,u+\rho_{j}+M\,\text{ on }X_{j}.

Fix

The function

\psi_{j}:=\widetilde{\psi}_{j}-c\rho_{j}-cM

is

and, since

\psi_{j}<\frac{c}{1+\varepsilon}\,u+(c^{\prime}-c)\rho_{j}+(c^{\prime}-c)\big(\frac{1}{1+\varepsilon}\,u+M\big)\leq\frac{c}{1+\varepsilon}\,u\leq 0.

Since

-psh function

-psh on

\psi\leq\frac{c}{1+\varepsilon}\,u\,\text{ on }V,\,\;\psi=\varphi+(c-1)\max_{1\leq j\leq N^{\prime}}\theta_{j}-(c-1)M\,\text{ on }X.

If

\theta=\max_{1\leq j\leq N}\theta_{j}=\max_{1\leq j\leq N^{\prime}}\theta_{j},\,\;\psi=\varphi+(c-1)\theta-(c-1)M,

3.
Zariski open Stein covers

We study in this section the existence of adapted Zariski-open Stein covers on various types of Kähler manifolds. Since the extension property requires the existence of closed subvarieties of positive dimension, it is natural to explore in particular the case of projective varieties (many Kähler manifolds -e.g. generic tori or very generic K3 surfaces- do not admit
any closed subvariety of positive dimension).

3.1.
Basic properties

We establish here the proof of Proposition
1.3
, and some of its consequences.

(i)
Let
(V,\omega)
be a compact Kähler manifold that satisfies condition (ZOS),
and let
\omega^{\prime}
be another Kähler form cohomologous to
\omega
.
By the
{\partial\overline{\partial}}
-Lemma,
\omega^{\prime}=\omega+dd^{c}\eta
for a smooth function
\eta:V\to\mathbb{R}
. Let
\{V_{j}\}_{j}
be a Zariski-open Stein cover of
V
such that
\omega=dd^{c}\rho_{j}
on
V_{j}
, where
\rho_{j}
is a smooth strictly psh exhaustion function on
V_{j}
. Then
\omega^{\prime}=dd^{c}(\rho_{j}+\eta)
and
\rho_{j}+\eta
is a smooth strictly psh exhaustion function on
V_{j}
, since
\eta
is globally bounded on
V
.
Thus
(V,\omega^{\prime})
also satisfies condition (ZOS).

(ii)
Let
\omega_{1},\omega_{2}
be Kähler forms on
V
such that
(V,\omega_{1}),(V,\omega_{2})
verify condition (ZOS). If
\omega=a_{1}\omega_{1}+a_{2}\omega_{2}
, where
a_{1},a_{2}>0
, then
(V,\omega)
verifies condition (ZOS). Indeed, assume that
V=\bigcup_{j=1}^{N_{1}}V_{j}=\bigcup_{k=1}^{N_{2}}W_{k}
, where
V_{j},W_{k}
are open Stein sets such that
H_{j}=V\setminus V_{j}
,
L_{k}=V\setminus W_{k}
are analytic subvarieties of
V
. Moreover,
\omega_{1}=dd^{c}\rho_{j}
on
V_{j}
,
\omega_{2}=dd^{c}\eta_{k}
on
W_{k}
, where
\rho_{j},\eta_{k}
are smooth strictly psh exhaustion functions on
V_{j}
, respectively on
W_{k}
. Then
V\setminus(V_{j}\cap W_{k})=H_{j}\cup L_{k}
is a proper analytic subvariety of
V
. Moreover, the function
\tau_{jk}:=a_{1}\rho_{j}+a_{2}\eta_{k}
is a smooth strictly psh exhaustion function on
V_{j}\cap W_{k}
, hence
V_{j}\cap W_{k}
is Stein. It follows that
\{V_{j}\cap W_{k}\}_{j,k}
is an adapted Zariski-open Stein cover for
(V,\omega)
.

(iii)
If
(V,\omega)
is a compact Kähler manifold which verifies condition (ZOS) and if
W
is a (closed) complex submanifold of
V
then it is easy to see that
(W,\omega|_{W})
verifies condition (ZOS), by intersecting the open sets
V_{j}
with
W
.

(iv)
If
V
is a projective manifold and
\omega
is a Hodge form on
V
then
(V,\omega)

verifies condition (ZOS). Indeed, replacing
\omega
by
k\omega
, we can assume that
V
is an algebraic submanifold of the complex projective space
{\mathbb{P}}^{N}
, and
\omega=\omega_{FS}|_{V}
is the Fubini-Study Kähler form.
Using the canonical covering by affine charts
{\mathbb{C}}^{N}\hookrightarrow{\mathbb{P}}^{N}
,
and the Fubini-Study potentials
\rho(z)=\log\sqrt{1+\|z\|^{2}}
,
z\in{\mathbb{C}}^{N}
,
we have that
({\mathbb{P}}^{N},\omega_{FS})
verifies condition (ZOS), hence so does
(V,\omega)
by
the previous restriction property.

(v)
If
V
is a projective manifold and
\{\omega\}=\sum_{\ell=1}^{m}a_{\ell}\{\omega_{\ell}\}
, where
a_{\ell}>0
and

\{\omega_{\ell}\}
are Hodge classes on
V
, then
(V,\omega)
verifies condition (ZOS), as follows from the previous observations. It is easy to see that any Kähler class
\{\omega\}
in the real Neron-Severi space
NS_{\mathbb{R}}(V)
can be expressed as a convex combination of Hodge classes. For the convenience of the reader we include a proof in Lemma
3.1
below. Hence
(V,\omega)
satisfies condition (ZOS) if the Kähler class
\{\omega\}\in NS_{\mathbb{R}}(V)
.

Lemma 3.1
.

Let
(V,\omega)
be a compact Kähler manifold such that
\{\omega\}\in NS_{\mathbb{R}}(V)
. Then
V
is projective and
\{\omega\}=\sum_{\ell=1}^{m}a_{\ell}\vartheta_{\ell}\,
, where
a_{\ell}>0
and
\vartheta_{\ell}
are Hodge classes on
V
.

Proof.

Let
\mathcal{K}_{V}\subset H^{1,1}(V,\mathbb{R})
denote the set of Kähler classes on
V
. Then
\mathcal{K}_{V}
is a convex cone and it is open. Let
c_{1}(L_{j})
,
L_{j}\in\Pic(V)
,
1\leq j\leq k
, be a basis of
NS_{\mathbb{R}}(V)
, so

\{\omega\}=\sum_{j=1}^{k}c_{j}c_{1}(L_{j})\,,\text{ where $c_{j}\in\mathbb{R}$.}

Since
\mathcal{K}_{V}
is open it follows that there exists
\varepsilon>0
such that the class

\{\omega^{\prime}\}=\sum_{j=1}^{k}r_{j}c_{1}(L_{j})\in\mathcal{K}_{V}\,,\text{ if $r_{j}\in\mathbb{Q}$, $|r_{j}-c_{j}|<\varepsilon$.}

Note that
\{\omega^{\prime}\}=N^{-1}\vartheta
, where
N\in\mathbb{N}
and
\vartheta
is a Hodge class on
V
, hence
V
is projective. Since
\mathcal{K}_{V}\cap NS_{\mathbb{R}}(V)\neq\emptyset
is open in
NS_{\mathbb{R}}(V)
, we have that
NS_{\mathbb{R}}(V)
has a basis of Kähler classes, and hence a basis of Hodge classes, by the preceding discussion. So we can write

\{\omega\}=\sum_{j=1}^{k}c_{j}\vartheta_{j}\,,\text{ where $\vartheta_{j}$ are Hodge classes and $c_{j}\in\mathbb{R}$.}

Choosing
r_{j}\in\mathbb{Q}
,
r_{j}<c_{j}
sufficiently close to
c_{j}
, we infer by above that

\{\omega\}=\sum_{j=1}^{k}r_{j}\vartheta_{j}+\sum_{j=1}^{k}a_{j}\vartheta_{j}=N^{-1}\vartheta+\sum_{j=1}^{k}a_{j}\vartheta_{j}\,,

where
a_{j}=c_{j}-r_{j}>0
,
N\in\mathbb{N}
and
\vartheta
is a Hodge class on
V
.
∎

(vi)
Let
(V^{1},\omega_{1}),(V^{2},\omega_{2})
be compact Kähler manifolds which verify condition (ZOS). Then
(V^{1}\times V^{2},\omega)
verifies condition (ZOS), where
\omega=\pi_{1}^{\star}\omega_{1}+\pi_{2}^{\star}\omega_{2}
and

\pi_{j}:V^{1}\times V^{2}\to V^{j}

are the canonical projections. Indeed, if
V^{1}=\bigcup_{k}V^{1}_{k}
and
V^{2}=\bigcup_{\ell}V^{2}_{\ell}
are adapted Zariski-open Stein covers for
(V^{1},\omega_{1})
and
(V^{2},\omega_{2})
, then it is easy to see that
\{V^{1}_{k}\times V^{2}_{\ell}\}_{k,\ell}
is an adapted Zariski-open Stein cover for
(V^{1}\times V^{2},\omega)
.

(vii)
Let
V,W
be compact Kähler manifolds and
f:W\to V
be a (unbranched) holomorphic covering map. Assume that
\omega
is a Kähler form on
V
such that
(V,\omega)
verifies condition (ZOS). Then
(W,f^{\star}\omega)
verifies condition (ZOS). Indeed, since
W
is compact
f
is a finite map. If
U\subset V
is an open Stein set such that
H=V\setminus U
is an analytic subvariety of
V
and if
\rho
is a smooth strictly psh exhaustion function on
U
, then
\rho\circ f
is a smooth strictly psh exhaustion function on
f^{-1}(U)
. Moreover
f^{-1}(H)
is a proper analytic subvariety of
W
.

In the case of a
branched
holomorphic cover
f:W\to V
, and with the above notation, it still holds that
f^{-1}(U)
is a Zariski-open Stein set (see e.g.
[
For17
, p. 49]
). However in this case the cohomology class
\{f^{\star}\omega\}
is merely semi-positive.
As noted by Matsumura
[
Mat13
]
, strict positivity is required to expect the extension property to hold true
(see Proposition
3.5
).

3.2.
The real Neron-Severi space

A Fano manifold is a projective
n
-dimensional manifold
V
whose anticanonical bundle

-K_{V}
is ample. It follows from the Kodaira-Nakano vanishing theorem that

H^{0,q}(V,\mathbb{C})=0
for
q>0
, so Hodge symmetry and Hodge decomposition theorem ensure that
H^{2}(V,\mathbb{R})=H^{1,1}(V,\mathbb{R})
. Hence

NS_{\mathbb{R}}(V)=H^{1,1}(V,\mathbb{R}),

and any Kähler class belongs to the real Neron-Severi space. Note also that in this case the map
c_{1}:H^{1}(V,\mathscr{O}^{\star}_{V})\cong\Pic(V)\to H^{2}(V,\mathbb{Z})
is an isomorphism. Thus condition (ZOS) is satisfied and the extension property holds for
any
Kähler class in this case.

Since
h^{0,2}(V)=\dim H^{0,2}(V,\mathbb{C})
is a birational invariant (see e.g.
[
H77
]
), the extension property holds in particular on any
rational
manifold
V
(i.e. birationally equivalent to
\mathbb{P}^{n}
), and hence on any toric manifold.

The Picard number
\rho(V)
(rank of the Neron-Severi group) is always bounded from above
by the Hodge number
h^{1,1}(V)
.
Besides Fano manifolds, there are many manifolds
with maximal Picard number
\rho(V)=h^{1,1}(V)
, especially when
\dim_{\mathbb{C}}V\geq 3
.
We refer the interested reader to
[
Be14
]
for a list of examples and a classification
in complex dimension
2
. The extension property holds true for any Kähler class on such manifolds.

At the other extreme the Picard number is one on a generic abelian variety, as well as on any generic projective
K3
surface, so most Kähler classes do not belong
to the real Neron-Severi space and we do not know if the extension property holds for these.

3.3.
Projective toric manifolds

Let
V
be a compact toric manifold of dimension
n
. Then
V
is a compactification of the complex torus
(\mathbb{C}^{\star})^{n}
such that the canonical action by multiplication of
(\mathbb{C}^{\star})^{n}
on itself extends to a holomorphic action of
(\mathbb{C}^{\star})^{n}
on
V
. As noted in Section
3.2
the extension property is valid for any Kähler class on
V
, since
V
is rational.

A toric Kähler form on
V
is a Kähler form
\omega
which is
(S^{1})^{n}
-invariant. We give here an alternative proof that
(V,\omega)
satisfies condition (ZOS). For this, we will need the following lemma:

Lemma 3.2
.

Let
f:[0,+\infty)^{n}\to\mathbb{R}
be such that
\rho(z_{1},\ldots,z_{n}):=f(|z_{1}|,\ldots,|z_{n}|)
is a smooth strictly psh function on
{\mathbb{C}}^{n}
. Then
\rho
is an exhaustion function for
{\mathbb{C}}^{n}
.

Proof.

It follows from the maximum principle applied to
\rho
on polydiscs that for each
1\leq j\leq n
and for fixed
x_{k}\geq 0
,
k\neq j
, the function
x_{j}\to f(x_{1},\ldots,x_{j},\ldots,x_{n})
is increasing. For
x_{j}>0
and
x_{k}\geq 0
,
k\neq j
, we obtain using Green’s formula that

(3.1)

\begin{split}\frac{\partial}{\partial x_{j}}\,f(x_{1},\ldots,x_{n})&=\frac{1}{2\pi}\,\frac{\partial}{\partial x_{j}}\,\int_{0}^{2\pi}\rho(x_{1},\ldots,x_{j}e^{it},\ldots,x_{n})\,dt\\
&=\frac{2}{\pi x_{j}}\int_{\{|z_{j}|\leq x_{j}\}}\frac{\partial^{2}\rho}{\partial z_{j}\partial\overline{z}_{j}}\,(x_{1},\ldots,z_{j},\ldots,x_{n})\,d\lambda(z_{j}),\end{split}

where
\lambda
is the Lebesgue measure on
\mathbb{C}
. Let

m:=\min\Big\{\frac{\partial^{2}\rho}{\partial z_{j}\partial\overline{z}_{j}}\,(0,\ldots,z_{j},\ldots,0):\,|z_{j}|\leq 1,\,1\leq j\leq n\Big\}.

We have
m>0
since
\rho
is strictly psh. Equation (
3.1
) implies that

\frac{\partial}{\partial x_{j}}\,f(0,\ldots,x_{j},\ldots,0)\geq\frac{2m}{x_{j}}

holds for
x_{j}\geq 1
. Since
f
is increasing in each variable we infer that

f(x_{1},\ldots,x_{j},\ldots,x_{n})\geq f(0,\ldots,x_{j},\ldots,0)\geq 2m\log x_{j}+f(0),

for every
1\leq j\leq n
,
x_{j}\geq 1
, and
x_{k}\geq 0
for
k\neq j
. Hence

\rho(z)\geq 2m\log^{+}\max\{|z_{1}|,\ldots,|z_{n}|\}+\rho(0),\;\forall\,z\in{\mathbb{C}}^{n}.

∎

Proposition 3.3
.

Let
V
be a projective toric manifold of dimension
n
equipped with a toric Kähler form
\omega
. Then
(V,\omega)
verifies condition (ZOS).

Proof.

Let
T:=(\mathbb{C}^{\star})^{n}
be the complex torus in
V
, and let
p_{1},\ldots,p_{N}
be the toric points of
V
. By
[
ALZ16
, Proposition 4.4]
we have that
V=\bigcup_{j=1}^{N}V_{j}
, where the sets
V_{j}\ni p_{j}
are open,
T\subset V_{j}
,
V\setminus V_{j}
are analytic subvarieties of
V
, and the following properties hold: For
1\leq j\leq N
, there exists a biholomorphic map
\Phi_{j}:V_{j}\to\mathbb{C}^{n}
such that
\Phi_{j}(p_{j})=0
,
\Phi_{j}(T)=(\mathbb{C}^{\star})^{n}\subset{\mathbb{C}}^{n}
,
V_{j}
is invariant under the action of
T
and the action of
T
on
V_{j}
coincides via
\Phi_{j}
with the canonical action by multiplication of
(\mathbb{C}^{\star})^{n}
on
{\mathbb{C}}^{n}
.

Set
\omega_{j}=(\Phi_{j}^{-1})^{\star}(\omega|_{V_{j}})
. Then
\omega_{j}
is a Kähler form on
{\mathbb{C}}^{n}
, invariant under the canonical action of
(S^{1})^{n}
on
{\mathbb{C}}^{n}
. We have
\omega_{j}=dd^{c}v_{j}
for some smooth strictly psh function
v_{j}
on
{\mathbb{C}}^{n}
. It follows that

w_{j}(z_{1},\ldots,z_{n}):=\frac{1}{(2\pi)^{n}}\,\int_{[0.2\pi]^{n}}v_{j}(e^{i\theta_{1}}z_{1},\ldots,e^{i\theta_{n}}z_{n})\,d\theta_{1}...d\theta_{n}

is a smooth, polyradial, strictly psh function such that
dd^{c}w_{j}=\omega_{j}
. Lemma
3.2
shows that
w_{j}
is an exhaustion function for
{\mathbb{C}}^{n}
. Thus
\rho_{j}:=w_{j}\circ\Phi_{j}
is a smooth strictly psh exhaustion function on
V_{j}
with
dd^{c}\rho_{j}=\omega
.
∎

For a brief description of the construction of
n
-dimensional projective toric manifolds
V
, and of very ample toric line bundles on
V
, from fans of cones in
{\mathbb{R}}^{n}
we refer to
[
ALZ16
, Section 4]
(see also
[
Fu93
]
).

By Lemma
3.2
(and its proof) it follows that every smooth strictly psh function
\rho
on
\mathbb{C}^{n}
which is polyradial is an exhaustion, and in fact it grows at least like
c\log\|z\|
for some constant
c>0
. We conclude this section by noting that the hypothesis of Lemma
3.2
that
\rho
is polyradial is necessary. Namely, we give below an example of a smooth strictly psh function on
\mathbb{C}^{n}
which is not an exhaustion, and an example of a smooth strictly psh exhaustion function on
\mathbb{C}^{n}
which does not grow at least logarithmically (i.e. it is not bounded below by
c\log\|z\|
, for any constant
c>0
).

Example 1
.

Let
w_{j}=(j,0,\ldots,0)\in\mathbb{C}^{n}
,
j\geq 1
, and
S=\{w_{1},w_{2},\ldots\}
. The function

\displaystyle u(z)=\sum_{j=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{2^{j+1}}\log(3\|z-w_{j}\|)

is psh on
\mathbb{C}^{n}
and
\mathscr{C}^{\infty}
-smooth on
\mathbb{C}^{n}\setminus S
. Moreover
u(z)\leq\frac{1}{2}\log^{+}\|z\|+C
on
\mathbb{C}^{n}
for some constant
C
, and
u(z)\geq 0
on
D:=\mathbb{C}^{n}\setminus\big(\bigcup_{j\geq 1}B_{j})
, where
B_{j}
is the open ball in
\mathbb{C}^{n}
centered at
w_{j}
and of radius
\frac{1}{3}
. Let
\{a_{j}\}_{j\geq 1}
be a sequence such that
a_{j}<0
. By a regularized maximum construction on each
B_{j}
we obtain a
\mathscr{C}^{\infty}
-smooth psh function
v
on
\mathbb{C}^{n}
which has the following properties:

(i)
v=u
on
D
and
v(z)\leq\frac{1}{2}\log^{+}\|z\|+C
on
\mathbb{C}^{n}
for some constant
C
.

(ii)
v\geq a_{j}
on
B_{j}
and
v(w_{j})=a_{j}
.

Let
\rho(z)=\frac{1}{4}\log(1+\|z\|^{2})+v(z)
. Then
\rho
is a smooth strictly psh function
\mathbb{C}^{n}
and by (i),

\rho(z)=\frac{1}{4}\log(1+\|z\|^{2})+u(z)\geq\frac{1}{4}\log(1+\|z\|^{2})\text{ on $D$.}

Moreover
\rho
is in the Lelong class, i.e.
\rho(z)\leq\log^{+}\|z\|+C
on
\mathbb{C}^{n}
for some constant
C
. Choosing
a_{j}=-\frac{1}{4}\log(1+j^{2})
we have
\rho(w_{j})=0
so
\rho
is not an exhaustion. If we take
a_{j}=\log\log(j+1)-\frac{1}{4}\log(1+j^{2})
we obtain a smooth strictly psh exhaustion function
\rho
such that
\rho(w_{j})=\log\log(j+1)
.

3.4.
Positivity vs. extension property

We finally study a necessary condition for a cohomology class
\alpha
to have the extension property.
Recall that a class
\alpha\in H^{1,1}_{psef}(V,\mathbb{R})
is pseudo-effective if it is the deRham cohomology class of a positive closed current
T
of bidegree
(1,1)
. Fixing
\theta\in\alpha
a smooth closed
(1,1)
-form, it follows from the
\partial\overline{\partial}
-lemma that
T=\theta+i\partial\overline{\partial}\varphi
, for some function
\varphi\in PSH(V,\theta)
. Recall that a class
\alpha\in H^{1,1}(V,\mathbb{R})
is numerically effective (nef) if it lies in the closure of the Kähler cone of
V
.

Definition 3.4
.

We say that a class
\alpha=\{\theta\}\in H^{1,1}_{psef}(V,\mathbb{R})
has the
extension property
if
PSH(V,\theta)|_{X}=PSH\left(X,\theta|_{X}\right)
holds for any irreducible analytic subset
X\subset V
. We say that
\alpha
has the
bounded extension property
if, for any irreducible analytic subset
X\subset V
, any
\theta|_{X}
-psh function on
X
that is bounded below near some point
p\in X
has a
\theta
-psh extension to
V
that is bounded below near
p
in
V
.

Inspired by Matsumura
[
Mat13
]
we prove the following result:

Proposition 3.5
.

Let
V
be a projective manifold of dimension
n\geq 2
and let
\alpha\in H^{1,1}_{psef}(V,\mathbb{R})
,
\alpha\neq 0
, be a pseudo-effective class.

(i) If
\alpha
has the extension property then
\alpha
is nef.

(ii) If
\alpha
has the bounded extension property then
\alpha
is a Kähler class.

This result is due to Matsumura
[
Mat13
, Theorem 1.2]
when
\alpha=c_{1}(L)
is the first Chern class of some holomorphic line bundle

L
on
V
. His strategy of proof can be adapted replacing the algebraic Nakai-Moishezon criterion by the transcendental version due to Demailly-Paun
[
DP04
]
. A similar observation has been made by Meng-Wang in
[
MW22
]
, who moreover noticed that a stronger extension property is needed for
\alpha
to be a Kähler class. We give here a direct proof of Proposition
3.5
, without using the results of
[
DP04
]
.

Proof of Proposition
3.5
.

We fix a Kähler form
\omega_{V}
on
V
. Let
\theta\in\alpha
be a smooth closed
(1,1)
-form and let
\varphi_{0}\in PSH(V,\theta)
.

We observe first that the restriction of
\alpha
to any irreducible curve
{\mathcal{C}}\subset V
is Kähler, provided that
{\mathcal{C}}=D_{1}\cap\cdots\cap D_{n-1}
is a complete intersection of ample divisors (this is in analogy to
[
Mat13
, Lemma 2.1]
). Indeed, let
h_{j}
be a Hermitian metric on the line bundle
\mathscr{O}_{V}(D_{j})
such that
\omega_{j}:=c_{1}(\mathscr{O}_{V}(D_{j}),h_{j})
is a Kähler form. By the Lelong-Poincaré formula we have
[D_{j}]=\omega_{j}+dd^{c}\log|S_{j}|_{h_{j}}
, where
[D_{j}]
is the current of integration on
D_{j}
and
S_{j}
is the canonical section of
\mathscr{O}_{V}(D_{j})
. Fix
\varepsilon>0
such that
\omega_{j}\geq\varepsilon\omega_{V}
for
1\leq j\leq n-1
. Since
\mathcal{C}
is a complete intersection it follows from
[
D93
]
that
[C]=[D_{1}]\wedge\ldots\wedge[D_{n-1}]
. Therefore

\int_{\mathcal{C}}\theta=\int_{V}\theta\wedge[\mathcal{C}]=\int_{V}(\theta+dd^{c}\varphi_{0})\wedge\omega_{1}\wedge\ldots\wedge\omega_{n-1}\geq\varepsilon^{n-1}\int_{V}(\theta+dd^{c}\varphi_{0})\wedge\omega_{V}^{n-1}>0,

as
\alpha\neq 0
. Hence
\alpha|_{\mathcal{C}}
is Kähler.

Next, a Bertini type theorem
[
Zha09
, Theorem 1.3]
ensures that for any
p,q\in V
there exists a smooth complete intersection curve
{\mathcal{C}}
as above with
p,q\in{\mathcal{C}}
(see
[
Mat13
, Theorem 2.2]
).

(i)
Following Demailly we consider

\Psi_{\theta}(x):=\left(\sup\{\varphi(x):\,\varphi\in PSH(V,\theta)\text{ with }\varphi\leq 0\}\right)^{*}.

This is a
\theta
-psh function with minimal singularities on
V
. Fix
x\in V
and
{\mathcal{C}}
a smooth curve as above with
x\in{\mathcal{C}}
. Since
\alpha|_{\mathcal{C}}
is Kähler, one can find
\phi\in PSH({\mathcal{C}},\theta|_{\mathcal{C}})
that is bounded below on
{\mathcal{C}}
. It follows from the extension property that there exists
\varphi\in PSH(V,\theta)
such that
\varphi|_{\mathcal{C}}=\phi
. Shifting
\varphi,\phi
by a constant, we can assume that
\varphi\leq 0
, hence
\Psi_{\theta}(x)\geq\varphi(x)=\phi(x)>-\infty
. Thus
\Psi_{\theta}
has zero Lelong number at each
x\in V
, so by Demailly’s regularization theorem
[
D92
]
one can find smooth functions
v_{\varepsilon}\in PSH(V,\theta+\varepsilon\omega_{V})
decreasing to
\Psi_{\theta}
. Therefore
\alpha
is a nef class, limit of the Kähler classes
\alpha+2\varepsilon\{\omega_{V}\}
.

(ii)
Using the bounded extension property, we start by constructing, for every point
p\in V
, a function
\psi_{p}\in PSH(V,\theta)\cap L^{\infty}(V)
such that

\theta+dd^{c}\psi_{p}\geq\omega_{V}\text{ in a neighborhood $W_{p}$ of $p$.}

Let
B(p,r)
be a small coordinate ball centered at
p=0
. Let
A>0
such that
Add^{c}\|z\|^{2}+\theta\geq\omega_{V}
in
B(p,r)
. For
q\in V\setminus B(p,r)
we let
{\mathcal{C}}
be a smooth curve as above passing through
p
and
q
. As
\alpha|_{\mathcal{C}}
is Kähler, we can find a
\theta
-psh function
u
on
{\mathcal{C}}
such that
u(p)=-\infty
and
u
is bounded below near
q
(see e.g.
[
CG09
]
). By the bounded extension property,
u
is the restriction of a
\theta
-psh function
U_{q}
on
V
that is bounded below on a neighborhood
G_{q}
of
q
. Since
V\setminus B(p,r)
is compact, we have
V\setminus B(p,r)\subset\bigcup_{j=1}^{N}G_{q_{j}}
, for some
q_{j}\in V\setminus B(p,r)
,
1\leq j\leq N
. Let
\phi=\max_{1\leq j\leq N}U_{q_{j}}+C
. Then
\phi\in PSH(V,\theta)
,
\phi>0
on
V\setminus B(p,r)
if
C
is sufficiently large, and
\phi(p)=-\infty
. We now use a gluing construction: the function

\psi_{p}=\begin{cases}\phi&\text{ in }V\setminus B(p,r),\\
\max\left\{A(\|z\|^{2}-r^{2}),\phi\right\}&\text{ in }B(p,r),\end{cases}

is bounded and
\theta
-psh on
V
, and it coincides with
A(\|z\|^{2}-r^{2})
in some ball
B(p,\delta)\subset B(p,r)
. Hence
\theta+dd^{c}\psi_{p}\geq\omega_{V}
on
W_{p}=B(p,\delta)
.

We now pick a finite cover
V=\bigcup_{j=1}^{\ell}W_{p_{j}}
, and we set

\psi=\frac{1}{\ell}\,\sum_{j=1}^{\ell}\psi_{p_{j}}.

Then
\psi\in PSH(V,\theta)\cap L^{\infty}(V)
and
\theta+dd^{c}\psi_{p}\geq\frac{1}{\ell}\,\omega_{V}
on
V
. By Demailly’s regularization theorem
[
D92
]
there exists a smooth
\theta
-psh function
\varphi\geq\psi
such that
\theta+dd^{c}\varphi\geq\frac{1}{2\ell}\,\omega_{V}
, hence
\alpha
is a Kähler class.
∎
