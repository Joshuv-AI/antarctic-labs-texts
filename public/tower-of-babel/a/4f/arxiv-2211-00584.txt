Jens Ahrens

Abstract
The equatorial microphone array presented in (Ahrens et al., 2021) computes a spherical harmonic (SH) representation of a sound field based on pressure sensors along the equator of a rigid spherical baffle. The original formulation uses complex-valued SH basis functions. This is inconvenient if the SH representation of the captured sound field is intended to be stored in time domain by means of real-valued audio signals as it is common in the spatial audio format of ambisonics. The present document summarizes the modifications that need to be applied to the mathematical formulation from (Ahrens et al., 2021) to produce an ambisonic representation of the captured sound field that is compatible with the established ambisonic software tools like SPARTA and the IEM Plugin Suite. An example MATLAB script that implements this formulation is provided.

•

The definition of the spherical harmonics (SHs) needs to be a specific real one instead of a complex one

•

The definition of the radial terms need to include the factor

We define the orthonormal, real-valued circular harmonics (CH)
C_{m}(\alpha)

C_{m}(\alpha)=\begin{cases}\sqrt{2}\sin{(|m|\alpha)},\ \forall m<0\\
\ \ \ \ \ \ \ 1\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \,,\ \forall m=0\\
\sqrt{2}\cos{(m\alpha)}\ ,\ \forall m>0\end{cases}\ .

(1)

Consequently, we define the orthonormal SHs

Y_{n,m}(\beta,\alpha)=N_{n,m}(\beta)\,C_{m}(\alpha)\ ,

(2)

N_{n,m}(\beta)=(-1)^{m}\,\sqrt{\frac{2n{+}1}{4\pi}\frac{(n{-}|m|)!}{(n{+}|m|)!}}\,P_{n}^{|{m}|}(\cos\beta)\ .

(3)

\beta
and

Y_{n,m}(\beta,\alpha)

The sound pressure
S^{\text{\;\!int}}(\beta,\alpha,r,\omega)
in an interior domain i.e., the sound pressure inside a spherical domain centered at the coordinate origin that is free of sound sources and free of reflecting boundaries, can be represented in SHs in different ways
(
Gumerov and Duraiswami 2005
)
. To be consistent with the ambisonics formulation, we represent it as
(
Ahrens 2022a
, Sec. 5.5)

\displaystyle S^{\text{\;\!int}}(\beta,\alpha,r,\omega)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\sum_{m=-n}^{n}\!\!4\pi\mathrm{i}^{n}\,\breve{S}_{n,m}(\omega)\,\,j_{n}\!\left(\omega\frac{r}{c}\right)\,Y_{n,m}(\beta,\alpha)

(4)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\sum_{m=-n}^{n}\!\!4\pi\mathrm{i}^{n}\,\breve{S}_{n,m}(\omega)\,\,j_{n}\!\left(\omega\frac{r}{c}\right)\,N_{n,m}(\beta)\,C_{m}(\alpha)\ .

(5)

\breve{S}_{n,m}(\omega)
is the frequency-domain representation of the ambisonic signals
\breve{s}_{n,m}(t)
that we would like to extract from the microphone signals.

The sound pressure
S(\beta,\alpha,R,\omega)
on the equator of a sphere of radius
R
is consequently given by

S(\pi/2,\alpha,R,\omega)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\sum_{m=-n}^{n}\!\!\breve{S}_{n,m}(\omega)\,\,b_{n}\!\left(\omega\frac{r}{c},R\right)\,N_{n,m}(\pi/2)\,C_{m}(\alpha)

(6)

with radial term

b_{n}\!\left(\omega\frac{R}{c},R\right)=-4\pi\mathrm{i}^{n}\,\frac{\mathrm{i}}{\left(\omega\frac{R}{c}\right)^{2}}\frac{1}{h_{n}^{\prime\;\!(2)}\!\left(\omega\frac{R}{c}\right)}\ .

(7)

h_{n}^{\prime\;\!(2)}\!\left(\cdot\right)
is the derivative of the spherical Hankel function
h_{n}^{(2)}\!\left(\cdot\right)
with respect to the argument.
