Electronic structures and stability investigation of the new class of large band gap topological insulators M M Tl 4 Te 3 ( M M = Cd, Hg)
Ying Li, Gang Xu

Abstract
By means of ternary chemical potential phase diagram and phonon spectrum calculations, we propose that M M Tl 4 Te 3 ( M M = Cd, Hg), the derivatives of Tl 5 Te 3 , are thermodynamically and dynamically stable in the body centered tetragonal crystal structure with I I 4/ m ​ c ​ m mcm symmetry. Our electronic structures calculations confirm that a robust s s - p p band inversion occurs near the Fermi level in M M Tl 4 Te 3 , and a topological band gap about 0.13 eV in CdTl 4 Te 3 is induced by the spin-orbit coupling. These results suggest that M M Tl 4 Te 3 are a new class of large band gap 3D strong topological insulators that are stable and synthesizable in experiment, which could be used to design efficient spin torque equipment and spin device.
