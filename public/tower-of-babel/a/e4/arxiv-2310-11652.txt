Abstract
In the framework of quasi-regular strongly local Dirichlet form ( ℰ , D ⁡ ( ℰ ) ) (\mathscr{E},D(\mathscr{E})) on L 2 ​ ( X , 𝔪 ) L^{2}(X;\mathfrak{m}) admitting minimal ℰ \mathscr{E} -dominant measure μ \mu , we construct a natural p p -energy functional ( ℰ p , D ⁡ ( ℰ p ) ) (\mathscr{E}^{\,p},D(\mathscr{E}^{\,p})) on L p ​ ( X , 𝔪 ) L^{p}(X;\mathfrak{m}) and ( 1 , p ) (1,p) -Sobolev space ( H 1 , p ( X ) , ∥ ⋅ ∥ H 1 , p ) (H^{1,p}(X),\|\cdot\|_{H^{1,p}}) for p ∈ ] 1 , + ∞ [ p\in]1,+\infty[ . It is easy to see that for

We can define

\displaystyle\Gamma_{\mu}(u,v):=\Gamma_{\mu}\left(\frac{u+v}{2}\right)-\Gamma_{\mu}\left(\frac{u-v}{2}\right)\in L^{\frac{p}{2}}(X;\mu),

and Cauchy-Schwarz inequality (3) above also remains valid for

For

and

, we define

\displaystyle\mathscr{E}^{\,p}(u,v):

\displaystyle=\int_{X}\Gamma_{\mu}(u)^{\frac{p-2}{2}}\Gamma_{\mu}(u,v)\mathrm{d}\mu,

(1.1)

\displaystyle\mathscr{E}^{\,p}_{\alpha}(u,v):

\displaystyle=\mathscr{E}^{\,p}(u,v)+\alpha\int_{X}|u|^{p-2}uv\,\mathrm{d}\mathfrak{m}.

(1.2)

Clearly we see

\displaystyle|\mathscr{E}^{\,p}(u,v)|

(1.3)

Indeed, the first inequality in (
1.3

\displaystyle\left|\alpha\int_{X}|u|^{p-2}uv\,\mathrm{d}\mathfrak{m}\right|\leq\left(\alpha\int_{X}|u|^{p}\mathrm{d}\mathfrak{m}\right)^{1-\frac{1}{p}}\left(\alpha\int_{X}|v|^{p}\mathrm{d}\mathfrak{m}\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}

(1.4)

We call

Theorem 1.8

(Clarkson’s inequality)
.

Suppose
p\in]1,+\infty[
.
The energy functional
\mathscr{E}^{\,p}_{1}
satisfies the following inequalities of Clarkson type: for
\alpha\geq 0
and
f,g\in D(\mathscr{E}^{\,p})=H^{1,p}(X)

\displaystyle\mathscr{E}^{\,p\!}_{\alpha}\left(\frac{f+g}{2}\right)+\mathscr{E}^{\,p\!}_{\alpha}\left(\frac{f-g}{2}\right)

\displaystyle\leq\frac{\mathscr{E}^{\,p}_{\alpha}(f)+\mathscr{E}^{\,p}_{\alpha}(g)}{2},\quad p\in[2,+\infty[,

(1.5)

\displaystyle\mathscr{E}^{\,p\!}_{\alpha}\left(\frac{f+g}{2}\right)^{\frac{1}{p-1}}+\mathscr{E}^{\,p\!}_{\alpha}\left(\frac{f-g}{2}\right)^{\frac{1}{p-1}}

\displaystyle\leq\left(\frac{\mathscr{E}^{\,p}_{\alpha}(f)+\mathscr{E}^{\,p}_{\alpha}(g)}{2}\right)^{\frac{1}{p-1}},\quad p\in]1,2].

(1.6)

In particular,
(H^{1,p}(X),\|\cdot\|_{H^{1,p}})
is a uniformly convex Banach space and consequently it is reflexive.

Thanks to the reflexivity of
(H^{1,p}(X),\|\cdot\|_{H^{1,p}})
, we can establish that the
(generalized) normal contraction operates on
(H^{1,p}(X),\|\cdot\|_{H^{1,p}})
.
For this, we define such notion below.

For a given function
T:\mathbb{R}^{N}\to\mathbb{R}
,
T
is said to be a
(generalized) normal contraction
if

\displaystyle T(0)=0\quad\text{ and }\quad|T(x)-T(y)|\leq\sum_{k=1}^{N}|x_{k}-y_{k}|

for any
x=(x_{1},\cdots,x_{N})
,
y=(y_{1},\cdots,y_{N})\in\mathbb{R}^{N}
.
When
N=1
, we call such
T:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}
a
normal contraction
.
Note that
\phi(t):=t^{\sharp}:=0\lor t\land 1
,
\phi(t):=|t|
,
\phi(t):=t^{+}:=t\lor 0
and
\phi(t):=t^{-}:=(-t)\lor 0
are typical examples of normal contractions. More generally, the real-valued function

\phi_{{\varepsilon}}
on
\mathbb{R}
depending on
{\varepsilon}>0
such that
\phi_{{\varepsilon}}(t)=t
for
t\in[0,1]
,
-{\varepsilon}\leq\phi_{{\varepsilon}}(t)\leq 1+{\varepsilon}
for all
t\in\mathbb{R}
, and
0\leq\phi_{{\varepsilon}}(t)-\phi_{{\varepsilon}}(s)\leq t-s
whenever

, is also a normal contraction.

\displaystyle|f(\gamma_{1})-f(\gamma_{0})|\leq\int_{0}^{1}{\rm lip}(f)(\gamma_{t})|\dot{\gamma}_{t}|\mathrm{d}t

(\mathscr{E},D(\mathscr{E}))
defined by

\displaystyle D(\mathscr{E}):=W^{1,2}(X),\quad\mathscr{E}(f,g):=\frac{1}{2}\int_{X}\langle Df,Dg\rangle\mathrm{d}\mathfrak{m}

is a strongly local Dirichlet form on
L^{2}(X;\mathfrak{m})
admitting carré du champ.

Denote by
(P_{t})_{t\geq 0}
the semigroup on
L^{2}(X;\mathfrak{m})
associated with
(\mathscr{E},D(\mathscr{E}))
. We call
(P_{t})_{t\geq 0}

heat flow
. Under (
3.4
), let
(\Delta,D(\Delta))
be the

L^{2}
-generator associated with
(\mathscr{E},D(\mathscr{E}))
defined by

\displaystyle\left\{\begin{array}[]{rl}D(\Delta)&:=\{u\in D(\mathscr{E})\mid\text{there exists }w\in L^{2}(X;\mathfrak{m})\text{ such that }\\
&\hskip 85.35826pt\mathscr{E}(u,v)=-\int_{X}wv\,\mathrm{d}\mathfrak{m}\quad\text{ for any }\quad v\in D(\mathscr{E})\},\\
\Delta u&:=w\quad\text{ for }w\in L^{2}(X;\mathfrak{m})\quad\text{specified as above,}\end{array}\right.

Definition 3.6

(
{\sf RCD}(K,\infty)
-spaces).

A metric measure space
(X,{\sf d},\mathfrak{m})
is said to be an
RCD
(K,\infty)
-space
if
it satisfies
the following conditions:

(1)

(X,{\sf d},\mathfrak{m})
is infinitesimally Hilbertian.

(2)

There exist
x_{0}\in X
and constants
c,C>0
such that

\mathfrak{m}(B_{r}(x_{0}))\leq C\mathrm{e}^{cr^{2}}
.

(3)

If
f\in W^{1,2}(X)
satisfies

|Df|_{2}\leq 1

\mathfrak{m}
-a.e., then
f
has a
1
-Lipschitz representative.

(4)

For any
f\in D(\Delta)

with
\Delta f\in W^{1,2}(X)

and
g\in D(\Delta)\cap L^{\infty}(X;\mathfrak{m})

with
g\geq 0
and
\Delta g\in L^{\infty}(X;\mathfrak{m})
,

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\int_{X}|Df|_{2}^{2}\Delta g\,\mathrm{d}\mathfrak{m}-\int_{X}\langle Df,D\Delta f\rangle g\,\mathrm{d}\mathfrak{m}\geq K\int_{X}|Df|_{2}^{2}g\,\mathrm{d}\mathfrak{m}

Let
{\rm Test}(X)
be the family of
test functions
defined by

\displaystyle{\rm Test}(X):=\{u\in D(\Delta)\cap L^{\infty}(X;\mathfrak{m})\mid\Gamma(u)^{\frac{1}{2}}=|Du|_{2}\in L^{\infty}(X;\mathfrak{m})\text{ and }\Delta u\in D(\mathscr{E})\}.

It is shown in
[
38
, Lemma 3.2]
that
{\rm Test}(X)
forms an algebra under
{\sf RCD}(K,\infty)
-condition for
(X,{\sf d},\mathfrak{m})
.

Theorem 3.7
.

Let
K\in\mathbb{R}
and
p\in]1,+\infty[
. Suppose that
(X,{\sf d},\mathfrak{m})
is an
RCD
(K,\infty)
-space. Then

(\mathscr{D}_{1,p},\|\cdot\|_{H^{1,p}})
is closable on
L^{p}(X;\mathfrak{m})
and
its closure
(H^{1,p}(X),\|\cdot\|_{H^{1,p}})
on
L^{p}(X;\mathfrak{m})
coincides with
(W^{1,p}(X),\|\cdot\|_{W^{1,p}})
.

Proof.

It is easy to see
(H^{1,2}(X),\|\cdot\|_{H^{1,2}})=(W^{1,2}(X),\|\cdot\|_{W^{1,2}})
.
Suppose that
RCD
(K,\infty)
-condition holds for
(X,{\sf d},\mathfrak{m})
. Then, by
[
22
, Theorem 3.4]
,
for any given
p,q\in]1,+\infty[
,
u\in W^{1,p}(X)
such that
u,|Du|_{p}\in L^{q}(X;\mathfrak{m})
implies

u\in W^{1,q}(X)
and
|Du|_{p}=|Du|_{q}

\mathfrak{m}
-a.e. From this, we can conclude that

\mathscr{D}_{1,p}\subset W^{1,p}(X)
with
\Gamma(u)^{\frac{1}{2}}=|Du|_{p}

\mathfrak{m}
-a.e., hence

\|u\|_{H^{1,p}}=\|u\|_{W^{1,p}}
for any
u\in\mathscr{D}_{1,p}
.
Since (
W^{1,p}(X),\|\cdot\|_{W^{1,p}})
is a Banach space, the closability of
(\mathscr{D}_{1,p},\|\cdot\|_{H^{1,p}})
on
L^{p}(X;\mathfrak{m})
is clear.
Thus, we have the inclusion

H^{1,p}(X)\subset W^{1,p}(X)
with
\|u\|_{H^{1,p}}=\|u\|_{W^{1,p}}
for
u\in H^{1,p}(X)
.

We claim that
{\rm Test}(X)\cap W^{1,p}(X)
is
W^{1,p}
-dense in
W^{1,p}(X)
.
For this, we show that
any
u\in W^{1,p}(X)
can be
\|\cdot\|_{W^{1,p}}
-approximated by a sequence of
W^{1,p}(X)\cap L^{\infty}(X;\mathfrak{m})\cap L^{2}(X;\mathfrak{m})
.
By using the distance function
{\sf d}:X^{2}\to[0,+\infty[
, we can construct functions
\rho_{n}\in{\rm Lip}(X)
such that

0\leq\rho_{n}\leq 1
on
X
,
\rho_{n}=1
on
B_{n}(o)
,
\rho_{n}=0
on
X\setminus B_{n+1}(o)
and
{\rm lip}(\rho_{n})=|D\rho_{n}|_{p}=|D\rho_{n}|_{2}\leq 1

\mathfrak{m}
-a.e. on
X
. Then
u_{n}:=(-n)\lor(u\rho_{n})\land n\in W^{1,p}(X)\cap L^{\infty}(X;\mathfrak{m})\cap L^{2}(X;\mathfrak{m})
forms an
\|\cdot\|_{W^{1,p}}
-approximation to
u
. For any
u\in W^{1,p}(X)\cap L^{\infty}(X;\mathfrak{m})\cap L^{2}(X;\mathfrak{m})
, we see
P_{t}u\in{\rm Test}(X)\cap W^{1,p}(X)
in view of

|DP_{t}u|_{p}^{p}\leq e^{-pKt}P_{t}|Du|_{p}^{p}
by
[
22
, Proposition 3.1]
and

|DP_{t}u|_{2}^{2}\leq\frac{e^{K^{-}t}}{2t}\|u\|_{L^{\infty}(X;\mathfrak{m})}
by
[
15
, Proposition 6.8]
.
From this,
\{P_{t}u\}
is a
\|\cdot\|_{W^{1,p}}
-bounded sequence in
{\rm Test}(X)\cap W^{1,p}(X)
.
Thanks to the uniform convexity of
(W^{1,p}(X),\|\cdot\|_{W^{1,p}})
and Banach-Saks type theorem by Kakutani
[
32
]
, any
u\in W^{1,p}(X)\cap L^{\infty}(X;\mathfrak{m})\cap L^{2}(X;\mathfrak{m})
can be
\|\cdot\|_{W^{1,p}}
-approximated by a sequence in
{\rm Test}(X)\cap W^{1,p}(X)
. Thus the claim is confirmed.

Applying
[
22
, Theorem 3.4]
again, we see the inclusion

{\rm Test}(X)\cap W^{1,p}(X)\subset\mathscr{D}_{1,p}\subset H^{1,p}(X)
. Therefore we obtain the
inclusion
W^{1,p}(X)\subset H^{1,p}(X)
with
\|u\|_{H^{1,p}}=\|u\|_{W^{1,p}}
for
u\in W^{1,p}(X)
.
∎

Example 3.8

(Configuration space over metric measure spaces)
.

Let
(M,g)
be a complete smooth Riemannian manifold without boundary.
The configuration space
\Upsilon
over
M
is the space of all locally finite point measures, that is,

\displaystyle\Upsilon:=\{\gamma\in\mathcal{M}(M)\mid\gamma(K)\subset\mathbb{N}\cup\{0\}\quad\text{ for all compact sets}\quad K\subset M\}.

In the seminal paper Albeverio-Kondrachev-Röckner
[
1
]
, they identified a natural geometry on
\Upsilon
by lifting the geometry of
M
to
\Upsilon
. In particular, there exists a natural gradient
\nabla^{\Upsilon}
, divergence
{\rm div}^{\Upsilon}
and
Laplace operator
\Delta^{\Upsilon}
on
\Upsilon
. It is shown in
[
1
]
that the Poisson point measure
\pi
on

\Upsilon
is the unique (up to intensity) measure on
\Upsilon
under which the gradient and divergence become
dual operator in
L^{2}(\Upsilon;\pi)
. Hence, the Poisson measure
\pi
is the natural volume measure on
\Upsilon

and
\Upsilon
can be seen as an infinite dimensional Riemannian manifold. The canonical Dirichlet form

\displaystyle\mathscr{E}(F)=\int_{\Upsilon}|\nabla^{\Upsilon}F|_{\gamma}^{2}\pi(\mathrm{d}\gamma)

constructed in
[
1
, Theorem 6.1]
is quasi-regular and strongly local
and it induces the heat semigroup
T_{t}^{\Upsilon}
and a Brownian motion
{\bf X}^{\Upsilon}
on
\Upsilon
which can be identified with the independent infinite particle process.
By Proposition
1.1
, one can construct the
(1,p)
-Sobolev space
(H^{1,p}(\Upsilon),\|\cdot\|_{H^{1,p}})
for
p\in[2,+\infty[
based on the quasi regular strongly local
Dirichlet form
(\mathscr{E}^{\Upsilon},D(\mathscr{E}^{\Upsilon}))
on
L^{2}(\Upsilon;\mu)
.

More generally, in Dello Schiavo-Suzuki
[
14
]
, configuration space
\Upsilon
over proper complete and separable metric space
(X,{\sf d})
is considered. The configuration space
\Upsilon
is endowed with the
vague topology

\tau_{V}
, induced by duality with continuous compactly supported functions on
X
, and with a reference Borel probability measure
\mu
satisfying
[
14
, Assumption 2.17]
, commonly understood as the law of a proper point process on
X
. In
[
14
]
,
they constructed the strongly local Dirichlet form
\mathscr{E}^{\Upsilon}
defined to be the
L^{2}(\Upsilon;\mu)
-closure of a certain pre-Dirichlet form on a class of certain cylindrical functions and prove its quasi-regularity for a wide class of measures
\mu
and base spaces (see
[
14
, Proposition 3.9 and Theorem 3.45]
).

By Proposition
1.1
, one can construct the
(1,p)
-Sobolev space
(H^{1,p}(\Upsilon),\|\cdot\|_{H^{1,p}})
for
p\in[2,+\infty[
based on the quasi regular strongly local
Dirichlet form
(\mathscr{E}^{\Upsilon},D(\mathscr{E}^{\Upsilon}))
on
L^{2}(\Upsilon;\mu)
.
The contents of Theorem
1.13
are not known for the
(1,p)
-Sobolev spaces
based on
(\mathscr{E}^{\Upsilon},D(\mathscr{E}^{\Upsilon}))
.
