Abstract
A (semiclassical) holographic computation of the deconfinement temperature at intermediate coupling from (a top-down) ℳ {\mathscr{M}} -theory dual of thermal QCD-like theories, has been missing in the literature. In the process of filling this gap, we demonstrate a novel UV-IR connection, (conjecture and provide evidence for) a non-renormalization beyond one loop of 𝓜 − 𝐜 \boldsymbol{\mathscr{M}}-{\bf c} hiral perturbation theory [ 1 ] -compatible deconfinement T emperature, and show equivalence with an E ntanglement (as well as Wald) entropy [ 2 ] computation, up to terms Q uartic in curvature ( R R ). We demonstrate a F lavor- M emory (FM) effect in the ℳ {\mathscr{M}} -theory uplifts of the gravity duals, wherein the no-braner ℳ {\mathscr{M}} -theory uplift retains the "memory" of the flavor D ​ 7 D7 -branes of the parent type IIB dual in the sense that a specific combination of the aforementioned quartic corrections to the metric components precisely along the compact part (given by S 3 S^{3} as an S 1 S^{1} -fibration over the vanishing two-cycle S 2 S^{2} ) of the non-compact four-cycle "wrapped" by the flavor D ​ 7 D7 -branes, is what determines, e.g., the Einstein-Hilbert action at O ( R 4 ) (R^{4}) . The aforementioned linear combination of 𝒪 ⁡ ( R 4 ) {\cal O}(R^{4}) corrections to the ℳ {\mathscr{M}} -theory uplift [ 3 , 4 ] metric, upon matching the holographic result from ℳ ​ χ {\mathscr{M}}\chi PT [ 1 ] with the phenomenological value of the coupling constant of one of the S ​ U ​ ( 3 ) SU(3) NLO χ \chi PT Lagrangian of [ 5 ] , is required to have a definite sign. Interestingly, in the decompactification (or “ M KK → 0 M_{\rm KK}\rightarrow 0 ") limit of the spatial circle in [ 1 ] to recover a QCD-like theory in four dimensions after integrating out the compact directions, we not only derive this, but in fact obtain the values of the relevant 𝒪 ⁡ ( R 4 ) {\cal O}(R^{4}) metric corrections. Further, equivalence with Wald entropy for the black hole in the high-temperature ℳ {\mathscr{M}} -theory dual at 𝒪 ⁡ ( R 4 ) {\cal O}(R^{4}) imposes a linear constraint on a similar linear combination of the abovementioned metric corrections. Remarkably, when evaluating the deconfinement temperature from an entanglement entropy computation in the thermal gravity dual, due to a delicate cancellation between the contributions arising from the metric corrections at 𝒪 ⁡ ( R 4 ) {\cal O}(R^{4}) in the ℳ {\mathscr{M}} theory uplift along the S 1 S^{1} -fiber and an S 2 S^{2} (which too involves a similar S 1 S^{1} -fibration) resulting in a non-zero contribution only along the vanishing S 2 S^{2} surviving, one sees that there are consequently no corrections to T c T_{c} at quartic order in the curvature supporting the conjecture made on the basis of a semiclassical computation.
