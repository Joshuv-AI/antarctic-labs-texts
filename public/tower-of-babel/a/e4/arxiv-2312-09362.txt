Abstract
. Let K / F K/F be a finite extension of number fields and S S be a finite set of primes of F F , including all the archimedean ones. In this paper, using some results of González-Avilés [ 6 ] , we generalize the notions of the relative Pólya group Po ⁡ ( K / F ) \Po(K/F) [ 4 , 12 ] and the Ostrowski quotient Ost ⁡ ( K / F ) \Ost(K/F) [ 13 ] to their S S -versions. Using this approach, we obtain generalizations of some well-known results on the S S -capitulation map, including an S S -version of Hilbert’s theorem 94.

1.
On Relative Pólya groups and Ostrowski Quotients in finite extensions of number fields

A number field
K
, with ring of integers
\mathcal{O}_{K}
, is called a
Pólya field
if for every prime number
p
and every integer
f\geq 1
, the
Ostrowski ideal

(1.1)

\Pi_{p^{f}}(K)=:\prod_{\begin{subarray}{c}\mathfrak{p}\in\mathbb{P}_{K}\\
N_{K/\mathbb{Q}}(\mathfrak{p})=p^{f}\end{subarray}}\mathfrak{p}

is principal (by convention, if
K
has no ideal with norm
p^{f}
, we set
\Pi_{p^{f}}(K)=\mathcal{O}_{K}
)
[
16
]
. As an obstruction measure for
K
to be a Pólya field, the notion of
Pólya group
was introduced later in
[
3
,
\lx@sectionsign
II.4]
.

Definition 1.1
.

The Pólya group of a number field
K
, denoted by
\Po(K)
, is the subgroup of the ideal class group
\Cl(K)
generated by the classes of all the Ostrowski ideals
\Pi_{p^{f}}(K)
.

Hence the Pólyaness of
K
is equivalent to the triviality of its Pólya group. In particular, Pólya fields encompass all class number one number fields. However, there are many Pólya fields with non-trivial ideal class group; for instance, every cyclotomic field is a Pólya field
[
16
, Proposition 2.6]
. The reader is referred to
[
4
,
11
,
12
,
16
]
for some results on Pólya fields and Pólya groups.

The notion of Pólya group has been recently generalized to the
relative Pólya group
in the following sense
[
4
,
12
]
.

Definition 1.2
.

Let
K/F
be a finite extension of number fields. The
relative Pólya group
of
K
over
F
, denoted by
\Po(K/F)
, is the subgroup of
\Cl(K)
generated by the classes of the
relative Ostrowski ideals

(1.2)

\displaystyle\Pi_{\mathfrak{p}^{f}}(K/F):=\prod_{\begin{subarray}{c}\mathfrak{P}\in\mathbb{P}_{K}\\
N_{K/F}(\mathfrak{P})=\mathfrak{p}^{f}\end{subarray}}\mathfrak{P},

where
\mathfrak{p}\in\mathbb{P}_{F}
,
f
is a positive integer. By convention, if
K
has no ideal with relative norm
\mathfrak{P}^{f}
(over
F
), then
\Pi_{\mathfrak{P}^{f}}(K/F)=\mathcal{O}_{K}
.

Remark 1.3
.

Note that
\Po(K/\mathbb{Q})=\Po(K)
and
\Po(K/K)=\Cl(K)
. Also,
one can show that for
F\subseteq M\subseteq K
, a tower of finite extensions of number fields, if
K/M
is Galois then
\Po(K/F)\subseteq\Po(K/M)

[
12
, Lemma 2.10]
. In this case, for the tower

F=M_{0}\subseteq M_{1}=M\subseteq\dots\subseteq M_{n-1}\subseteq M_{n}=K,

we get the
filtration

\Po(K/F)\subseteq\Po(K/M)\subseteq\dots\subseteq\Po(K/M_{n-1})\subseteq\Po(K/K)=\Cl(K)

between
\Po(K/F)
and
\Cl(K)
, or equivalently a filtration on
\frac{\Cl(K)}{\Po(K/F)}
. For instance, for a prime ideal
\mathfrak{p}
of
F
, let
\mathfrak{P}
be a prime ideal of
K
above
\mathfrak{p}
. Denote by
Z(\mathfrak{P}/\mathfrak{p})
and
T(\mathfrak{P}/\mathfrak{p})
the
decomposition group
and the
inertia group
of
K/F
at
\mathfrak{P}
, respectively
[
5
, Chapter 1]
. By the Galois correspondence, let
K_{Z}
be the fixed field of
Z(\mathfrak{P}/\mathfrak{p})
, and
K_{T}
be the fixed field of
T(\mathfrak{P}/\mathfrak{p})
. Then for the tower
F\subseteq K_{Z}\subseteq K_{T}\subseteq K
, we get the above filtration.

In the case that
K/F
is a finite Galois extension, the relative Pólya group
\Po(K/F)
coincides with the group of strongly ambiguous ideal classes of
K/F

[
12
, Section 2]
. Moreover, using some cohomological results of Brumer and Rosen
[
2
,
\lx@sectionsign
2]
and Zantema
[
16
]
one may find the following exact sequence (“BRZ” stands for these authors).

Theorem 1.4
.

[
12
, Theorem 2.2]

Let
K/F
be a finite Galois extension of number fields with Galois group
G
. Then the following sequence is exact:

(BRZ)

0\rightarrow\Ker({\epsilon}_{K/F})\xrightarrow{\theta_{K/F}}H^{1}(G,U_{K})\rightarrow\bigoplus_{\mathfrak{p}\in\mathbb{P}_{F}}\frac{\mathbb{Z}}{e_{\mathfrak{p}(K/F)}\mathbb{Z}}\rightarrow\frac{\Po(K/F)}{{\epsilon}_{K/F}(\Cl(F))}\rightarrow 0,

where
\epsilon_{K/F}:\Cl(F)\rightarrow\Cl(K)
denotes the capitulation map, and
e_{\mathfrak{p}(K/F)}
denotes the ramification index of the prime
\mathfrak{p}\in\mathbb{P}_{F}
in
K/F
.

Some interesting consequences of Theorem
1.4
can be found in
[
4
,
12
]
.

1.1.
On the Ostrowski Quotient
\Ost(K/F)

It worth noting that, for
K/F
a finite Galois extension of number fields, by (
BRZ
) if
\epsilon_{K/F}\left(\Cl(F)\right)=0
, then there exists a surjective map from
\bigoplus_{\mathfrak{p}\in\mathbb{P}_{F}}\mathbb{Z}/e_{\mathfrak{p}(K/F)}\mathbb{Z}
onto the relative Pólya group
\Po(K/F)
. Roughly speaking, in this case,
\Po(K/F)
is
controlled
by ramification. However, this assertion may not hold, in general. For instance, for a regular prime number
p>19
and an integer
n>1
, let
F=\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_{p})
and
K=\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_{p^{n}})
, where
\zeta_{p}
(resp.
\zeta_{p^{n}}
) denote the
p
-th (resp.
p^{n}
-th) primitive root of unity. Then
\Po(K/F)\neq 0
, while the only map from
\bigoplus_{\mathfrak{p}\in\mathbb{P}_{F}}\mathbb{Z}/e_{\mathfrak{p}(K/F)}\mathbb{Z}
to
\Po(K/F)
is the zero map, see
[
13
, Example 3.1]
for the details. Motivating by this example, the authors and A. Rajaei
[
13
, Section 3]
introduced the notion of Ostrowski quotient.

Definition 1.5
.

Let
K/F
be a finite extension of number fields. The
Ostrowski quotient
of
K
over
F
, denoted by
\Ost(K/F)
, is defined as

(1.3)

\Ost(K/F):=\dfrac{\Po(K/F)}{\Po(K/F)\cap\epsilon_{K/F}(Cl(F))}.

In particular,
\Ost(K/\mathbb{Q})=\Po(K/\mathbb{Q})=\Po(K)
and
\Ost(K/K)=0
. The extension
K/F
is called “
Ostrowski
” (or
K
is called
F
-Ostrowski) if
\Ost(K/F)=0
.

Remark 1.6
.

Note that
K/F
is a finite Galois extension of number fields, then
\epsilon_{K/F}\left(\Cl(F)\right)\subseteq\Po(K/F)
and (
BRZ
) can be re-written as

0\rightarrow\Ker({\epsilon}_{K/F})\xrightarrow{\theta_{K/F}}H^{1}(\Gal(K/F),U_{K})\rightarrow\bigoplus_{\mathfrak{p}\in\mathbb{P}_{F}}\frac{\mathbb{Z}}{e_{\mathfrak{p}(K/F)}\mathbb{Z}}\rightarrow\Ost(K/F)\rightarrow 0.

The reader is referred to
[
13
, Section 3]
for some results on Ostrowski quotients.

2.
The
S
-versions of relative Pólya group and Ostrowski Quotient

Throughout this section, we fix the following notations:

•

K/F
: a finite extension of number fields;

•

S_{\infty}
: the set of all the archimedean primes of
F
;

•

S
: a finite set of primes of
F
containing
S_{\infty}
(We also denote by
S
the set of the primes of
K
which lie above the primes in
S
);

•

U_{F,S}
(resp.
U_{K,S}
) : the group of
S
-units of
F
(resp.
K
);

•

I(F)_{S}
(resp.
I(K)_{S}
) : the group of fractional ideals of
F
(resp.
K
) with support outside
S
;

•

P(F)_{S}
(resp.
P(K)_{S}
) : the group of principal fractional ideals of
F
(resp.
K
) with support outside
S
;

•

\Cl(F)_{S}
(resp.
\Cl(K)_{S}
) : the
S
-ideal class group of
F
(resp.
K
).

In the case that
K/F
is Galois, González-Avilés
[
6
]
found some interesting results on the kernel and cokernel of the
S
-capitulation map

(2.1)

\epsilon_{K/F,S}:\Cl(F)_{S}\rightarrow\left(\Cl(K)_{S}\right)^{G}

where
G=\Gal(K/F)
. A part of his cohomological tools has been restated as follows.

Proposition 2.1
.

[
6
, Section 2]

Let
K/F
be a finite Galois extension of number fields with Galois group
G
. With the notations of this section, the following sequences are exact:

(2.2)

\displaystyle 0\rightarrow\left(P(K)_{S}\right)^{G}\rightarrow\left(I(K)_{S}\right)^{G}\rightarrow\left(\Cl(K)_{S}\right)_{\trans}^{G}\rightarrow 0,

(2.3)

\displaystyle 0\rightarrow\left(\Cl(K)_{S}\right)_{\trans}^{G}\rightarrow\left(\Cl(K)_{S}\right)^{G}\rightarrow H^{1}(G,P(K)_{S})\rightarrow 0,

(2.4)

\displaystyle 0\rightarrow H^{1}(G,P(K)_{S})\rightarrow H^{2}(G,U_{K,S})\rightarrow H^{2}(G,K^{\times}).

Remark 2.2
.

Using the exact sequences (
2.3
) and (
2.4
), we obtain the map

(2.5)

\trans_{K/F,S}:\left(\Cl(K)_{S}\right)^{G}\rightarrow H^{2}(G,U_{K,S})

which is called the
transgression map
. Also its kernel, i.e.,
\left(\Cl(K)_{S}\right)_{\trans}^{G}
, is called the group of
transgressive ambiguous classes

[
6
, Section 2]
.

As mentioned before, the relative Pólya group
\Po(K/F)
coincides with the group of strongly ambiguous ideal classes in
K/F
, i.e.,
\Po(K/F)=I(K)^{G}/P(K)^{G}

[
12
,
\lx@sectionsign
2]
. Hence using the exact sequence (
2.2
) for
S=S_{\infty}
we obtain

(2.6)

\Po(K/F)=(\Cl(K)_{S_{\infty}})^{G}_{trans}.

This leads us to define the notion of
S
-relative Pólya group
in the following sense.

Definition 2.3
.

Let
K/F
be a finite extension of number fields, and
S
be a finite set of the primes of
F
, containing all the archimedean ones. The
S
-relative Pólya group of
K
over
F
, denoted by
\Po(K/F)_{S}
, is the subgroup of the ideal class group of
K
generated by all the classes of the relative Ostrowski ideals
\Pi_{\mathfrak{p}^{f}}(K/F)
(
1.2
) with support outside
S
:

\Po(K/F)_{S}:=\left<\left[\Pi_{\mathfrak{p}^{f}}(K/F)\right]\,:\,\mathfrak{p}\in\mathbb{P}_{F}\backslash S,\,f\in\mathbb{N}\right>.

Remark 2.4
.

Let
S_{1}\subseteq S_{2}
be two finite sets of the primes of
F
, containing
S_{\infty}
. Then by the above definition, it immediately follows that

\Po(K/F)_{S_{2}}\subseteq\Po(K/F)_{S_{1}}.

In particular,
\Po(K/F)_{S_{\infty}}=\Po(K/F)
is the maximal element in the family

\{\Po(K/F)_{S}\,:\,\text{$S$ a finite set of the primes of $F$ with $S_{\infty}\subseteq S$}\}

of the subgroups of
\Cl(K)
.

Theorem 2.5
.

Let
K/F
be a finite extension of number fields with Galois group
G
. Let
S
be a finite set of the primes of
F
, containing all the archimedean ones. Denote also by
S
the set of the primes of
K
which lie above the primes in
S
. Then

\{\Pi_{\mathfrak{p}^{f}}(K/F)\,:\,\mathfrak{p}\in\mathbb{P}_{F}\backslash S,\,f\in\mathbb{N}\}

is a set of free generators for
\left(I(K)_{S}\right)^{G}
.

Proof.

For a prime
\mathfrak{p}\in\mathbb{P}_{F}
, consider its decomposition in
K/F
as

\mathfrak{p}\mathcal{O}_{K}=\left(\mathfrak{P}_{1}\dots\mathfrak{P}_{g}\right)^{e_{\mathfrak{p}(K/F)}}=\left(\Pi_{\mathfrak{p}^{f_{\mathfrak{p}(K/F)}}}(K/F)\right)^{e_{\mathfrak{p}(K/F)}},

where
e_{\mathfrak{p}(K/F)}
and
f_{\mathfrak{p}(K/F)}
denote the ramification index and the residue class degree of
\mathfrak{p}
in
K/F
, respectively. Since the Galois group
G
permutes the primes
\mathfrak{P}_{i}
’s transitively, we have

\left(\Pi_{\mathfrak{p}^{f_{\mathfrak{p}(K/F)}}}(K/F)\right)^{\sigma}=\Pi_{\mathfrak{p}^{f_{\mathfrak{p}(K/F)}}}(K/F),\quad\forall\sigma\in G.

Hence if
\mathfrak{p}\not\in S
, then
\Pi_{\mathfrak{p}^{f_{\mathfrak{p}(K/F)}}}(K/F)\in\left(I(K)_{S}\right)^{G}
. Now let
\mathfrak{a}\in\left(I(K)_{S}\right)^{G}
. For each prime
\mathfrak{P}
of
K
dividing
\mathfrak{a}
, let

\mathfrak{a}=\mathfrak{P}^{\nu_{\mathfrak{P}}(\mathfrak{a})}.\mathfrak{m},\quad\mathfrak{P}\nmid\mathfrak{m}.

For every
\sigma\in G
, we have

\mathfrak{a}=\mathfrak{a}^{\sigma}=\left(\sigma(\mathfrak{P})\right)^{\nu_{\mathfrak{P}}(\mathfrak{a})}.\sigma(\mathfrak{m}),

which implies that the prime ideal
\sigma(\mathfrak{P})
is also dividing
\mathfrak{a}
with the same exponent
\nu_{\mathfrak{P}}(\mathfrak{a})
. Therefore

\mathfrak{a}=\prod_{\begin{subarray}{c}\mathfrak{P}\in\mathbb{P}_{K},\,\mathfrak{P}\mid\mathfrak{a}\\
\mathfrak{P}\cap F=\mathfrak{p}\end{subarray}}\left(\Pi_{\mathfrak{p}^{f_{\mathfrak{p}(K/F)}}}(K/F)\right)^{\nu_{\mathfrak{P}}(\mathfrak{a})},

and the assertion is proved, since
\mathfrak{a}\in\left(I(K)_{S}\right)^{G}
.
∎

Corollary 2.6
.

Let
K/F
be a finite extension of number fields with Galois group
G
. Let
S
be a finite set of the primes of
F
, containing all the archimedean ones. Then

(2.7)

\Po(K/F)_{S}=\left(\Cl(K)_{S}\right)^{G}_{\trans}.

Proof.

By Theorem
2.5
we have
\Po(K/F)_{S}=\left(I(K)_{S}\right)^{G}/\left(P(K)_{S}\right)^{G}
. Now the assertion immediately follows from the exact sequence (
2.2
).
∎

2.1.
S
-Ostrowski quotient

Recall that, as a modification of the notion of the relative Pólya group, the notion of Ostrowski quotient
\Ost(K/F)
has been recently defined in
[
13
]
, see Definition
1.5
.
Similar to the
S
-relative Pólya group, one can naturally define the
S
-relative version of the Ostrowski quotient.

Definition 2.7
.

Let
K/F
be a finite extension of number fields, and
S
be a finite set of the primes of
F
, containing all the archimedean ones. The
S
-relative Ostrowski quotient

\Ost(K/F)_{S}
is defined as

(2.8)

\Ost(K/F)_{S}:=\dfrac{\Po(K/F)_{S}}{\Po(K/F)_{S}\cap\epsilon_{K/F,S}(Cl(F)_{S})}.

In particular,
\Ost(K/F)_{S_{\infty}}=\Ost(K/F)
. The extension
K/F
is called
S
-Ostrowski
(or
K
is called
(F,S)
-Ostrowski
) if
\Ost(K/F)_{S}
is trivial.

Remark 2.8
.

Let
K/F
be a finite Galois extension with Galois group
G
. Then

\epsilon_{K/F,S}\left(Cl(F)_{S}\right)\subseteq\left(\Cl(K)_{S}\right)^{G}_{trans}=\Po(K/F)_{S},

where

\epsilon_{K/F,S}:\Cl(F)_{S}\rightarrow\left(\Cl(K)_{S}\right)^{G}

denotes the
S
-capitulation map. Hence by considering the map

(2.9)

\epsilon_{K/F,S}^{\prime}:\Cl(F)_{S}\rightarrow\Po(K/F)_{S},

induced by
\epsilon_{K/F,S}
, we have

(2.10)

\Ost(K/F)_{S}=\Coker\left(\epsilon^{\prime}_{K/F,S}\right).

2.2.
The
S
-BRZ exact sequence

One can restate some results of Avilés
[
6
]
in terms of the
S
-relative Pólya group and the
S
-relative Ostrowski quotient.

Proposition 2.9
.

[
6
, Proposition 2.2]

For
K/F
a finite Galois extension with Galois group
G
, the following sequence is exact

0\rightarrow\Ost(K/F)_{S}\rightarrow\Coker(\epsilon_{K/F,S})\rightarrow H^{1}(G,P(K)_{S})\rightarrow 0.

In particular, for
S=S_{\infty}
we obtain the exact sequence

0\rightarrow\Ost(K/F)\rightarrow\frac{\Cl(K)^{G}}{\epsilon_{K/F}\left(\Cl(F)\right)}\rightarrow H^{1}(G,P(K))\rightarrow 0,

where
P(K)
denotes the group of principal fractional ideals of
K
.

Lemma 2.10
.

[
6
, Lemma 2.2]

Let
K/F
be a finite Galois extension of number fields with Galois group
G
. Let
\mathfrak{p}
be a non-archimedean prime of
F
which is ramified in
K
. Then there exists a canonical isomorphism

\displaystyle\mathbb{Z}/e_{\mathfrak{p}(K/F)}\mathbb{Z}\simeq H^{1}(G_{\mathfrak{P}},U_{K_{\mathfrak{P}}}),

where
e_{\mathfrak{p}(K/F)}
denotes the ramification index of
\mathfrak{p}
in
K
,
\mathfrak{P}
is a prime of
K
above
\mathfrak{p}
,
K_{\mathfrak{P}}
(resp.
F_{\mathfrak{p}}
) is the localizations of
K
(resp.
F
) at
\mathfrak{P}
(resp. at
\mathfrak{p}
),
G_{\mathfrak{P}}
is the Galois group
\Gal(K_{\mathfrak{P}}/F_{\mathfrak{p}})
and
U_{K_{\mathfrak{P}}}
is the group of units in
K_{\mathfrak{P}}
.

Theorem 2.11
.

[
6
, Theorem 2.4]

For
K/F
a finite Galois extension of number fields with Galois group
G
, there exists an exact sequence

(2.11)

\textstyle{0\rightarrow\Ker({\epsilon}_{K/F,S})\rightarrow H^{1}(G,U_{K,S})\xrightarrow{\lambda}\bigoplus_{\mathfrak{p}\in R_{K/F}\backslash S}H^{1}(G_{\mathfrak{P}},U_{K_{\mathfrak{P}}})\rightarrow\Ost(K/F)_{S}\rightarrow 0,}

where
\epsilon_{K/F,S}:\Cl(F)_{S}\rightarrow\left(\Cl(K)_{S}\right)^{G}
denotes the
S
-capitulation map,
R_{K/F}
denotes the set of all non-archimedean primes of
F
ramified in
K
,
\mathfrak{P}
is a prime of
K
above
\mathfrak{p}
, and
G_{\mathfrak{P}}=\Gal(K_{\mathfrak{P}}/F_{\mathfrak{p}})
for
K_{\mathfrak{P}}
(resp.
F_{\mathfrak{p}}
) the localization of
K
(resp.
F
) at
\mathfrak{P}
(resp. at
\mathfrak{p}
).

Remark 2.12
.

By Lemma
2.10
the exact sequence (
2.11
) can be re-written as

(S-BRZ)

\textstyle{0\rightarrow\Ker({\epsilon}_{K/F,S})\rightarrow H^{1}(G,U_{K,S})\xrightarrow{\theta_{K/F,S}}\bigoplus_{\mathfrak{p}\in R_{K/F}\backslash S}\frac{\mathbb{Z}}{e_{\mathfrak{p}(K/F)}\mathbb{Z}}\rightarrow\Ost(K/F)_{S}\rightarrow 0,}

which is a vast generalization of (
BRZ
). Indeed, the above exact sequence can be thought of as the “
S
-relative version of (
BRZ
)
” which we call “
S
-BRZ
”.

2.3.
Some applications of
S
-BRZ

In
[
13
,
\lx@sectionsign
2]
, the authors and A. Rajaei used the exact sequence (
BRZ
) to give short and simple proofs for some well-known results in the literature. Using (
S-BRZ
), one can obtain these results in a more general setting, namely in their
S
-relative versions.

Theorem 2.13
.

(generalizing Iwasawa-Khare-Prasad result
[
8
,
9
]
)
Let
K/F
be a finite Galois extension of number fields and
S
be a finite set of primes of
F
with
S_{\infty}\subseteq S
.
If
K/F
is unramified outside
S
, then

\Ker(\epsilon_{K/F,S})\simeq H^{1}(\Gal(K/F),U_{K,S}),\quad\text{and}\qquad\Ost(K/F)_{S}=0.

Proof.

Immediately follows from (
S-BRZ
).
∎

Corollary 2.14
.

Let
K/F
be a finite cyclic extension of number fields and
S
be a finite set of primes of
F
with
S_{\infty}\subseteq S
. If
K/F
is unramified outside
S
, then
H^{2}(G,U_{K,S})\simeq\Coker(\epsilon_{K/F,S})
.

Proof.

Using the exact sequence (
2.3
) and Corollary
2.6
we get the following exact sequence

(2.12)

0\rightarrow\Po(K/F)_{S}\rightarrow\left(\Cl(K)_{S}\right)^{G}\rightarrow H^{1}(G,P(K)_{S})\rightarrow 0.

Since
K/F
is cyclic and unramified outside
S
, one can easily obtain the “
S
-version” of Kisilevsky’s result in
[
10
, Lemma 1]
:

(2.13)

H^{1}(G,P(K)_{S})\simeq H^{2}(G,U_{K,S}).

By Theorem
2.13
one has
\Ost(K/F)_{S}=0
, i.e.,
\Po(K/F)_{S}=\epsilon_{K/F,S}\left(\Cl(F)_{S}\right)
. Using the relations (
2.12
) and (
2.13
), the proof is completed.
∎

Theorem 2.15
.

(generalizing a result of Iwasawa
[
8
]
)
Let
K/F
be finite Galois extension of number fields. For a modulus
\mathfrak{m}
of
F
, let
F^{\mathfrak{m}}
be the ray class field of
F
of modulus
\mathfrak{m}
, and
S
be the support of
\mathfrak{m}

Theorem 2.16
.

(generalizing a result of Tannaka
[
14
, Theorem 8]
)
For a number field
F
and a modulus
\mathfrak{m}
of
F
, let
F^{\mathfrak{m}}
be the ray class field of
F
of modulus
\mathfrak{m}
, and
S
be the support of
\mathfrak{m}
along with the set of all archimedean places of
F
.
Then there exists a surjective map

\Gal(F^{\mathfrak{m}}/F)\twoheadrightarrow H^{1}(\Gal(F^{\mathfrak{m}}/F),U_{F^{\mathfrak{m}},S}).

Proof.

By Theorem
2.15
we have

\Cl(F)_{S}\simeq H^{1}(\Gal(F^{\mathfrak{m}}/F),U_{F^{\mathfrak{m}},S}).

On the one hand, by the
Artin Reciprocity
one has

\Gal(F^{\mathfrak{m}}/F)\simeq\Cl(F)_{\mathfrak{m}},

where
\Cl(F)_{\mathfrak{m}}
denotes the
ray class group
of
F
for
\mathfrak{m}
, i.e. the group
I(F)_{\mathfrak{m}}/P(F)_{\mathfrak{m}}
for
I(F)_{\mathfrak{m}}
the subgroup of
I(F)
that do not involve the primes dividing
\mathfrak{m}
, and
P(F)_{\mathfrak{m}}\subseteq I(F)_{\mathfrak{m}}
the group of principal ideals generated by some
totally positive
elements
a\in\mathcal{O}_{F}
with
a\equiv 1(\mathrm{mod}\,\mathfrak{m})
, see
[
5
, Chapter 3]
.

On the other hand, there exists a surjective map
\Cl(F)_{\mathfrak{m}}\twoheadrightarrow\Cl(F)_{S}
and the proof is completed.
∎

Theorem 2.17
.

(a generalization of Hilbert’s theorem 94
[
7
]
)
Let
K/F
be a finite cyclic extension of number fields with Galois group
G
, and
S
be a finite set of primes of
F
with
S_{\infty}\subseteq S
. If
K/F
is unramified outside
S
, then

\left(\prod_{v\in S}\left|G_{w}\right|\right).\left|\Ker(\epsilon_{K/F,S})\right|=\left(U_{F,S}:N_{K/F}(U_{K,S})\right).[K:F],

where
G_{w}
denotes the decomposition group of
G
at
w
for a prime
w
of
K
above
v\in S
.

Proof.

On the one hand, since
K/F
is unramified outside
S
, the exact sequence (
S-BRZ
) implies that

\left|H^{1}(G,U_{K,S})\right|=\left|\Ker(\epsilon_{K/F,S})\right|.

On the other hand, since
K/F
is cyclic, one can use the
Herbrand quotient

Q(G,U_{K,S})=\frac{\left|\widehat{H^{0}}(G,U_{K,S})\right|}{\left|H^{1}(G,U_{K,S})\right|},

where

\widehat{H^{0}}(G,U_{K,S})=\frac{U_{K,S}^{G}}{N_{K/F}(U_{K,S})}=\frac{U_{F,S}}{N_{K/F}(U_{K,S})},

and

Q(G,U_{K,S})=\frac{\prod_{v\in S}\left|G_{w}\right|}{[K:F]},

see
[
5
, Proof of Proposition 5.10]
. The above equalities give the desired result.
∎
