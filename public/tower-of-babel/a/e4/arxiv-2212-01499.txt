Neumann Boundary Problem of Second Order Parabolic Quasi-Linear System with Variable Coefficient on a Vector Bundle
Zonglin Jia

Abstract
. Classical results of second order parabolic quasi-linear equations always require that the nonlinear terms are controlled by a power of the unknown functions and their first derivatives. We improve the previous results. More precisely, in the present article the upper bound of nonlinear term can be extended to a power series.

\displaystyle\left\{\begin{array}[]{rl}&\partial_{t}u(t,x)=\Delta u(t,x)+F(u(t,x),\partial u(t,x)),\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,(t,x)\in\mathbb{R}^{+}\times\Omega\\

I_{10}\leq\sum\limits_{j=1}^{k}j(1+||\omega||_{W^{k+1,0}_{p}})^{2j}\leq k^{2}(1+||\omega||_{W^{k+1,0}_{p}})^{2k}.

Similarly,
\sum\limits_{\tilde{i}_{j}=1}^{\tilde{s}_{j}}(\tilde{p}_{\tilde{i}_{j}}\tilde{q}_{\tilde{i}_{j}}+\tilde{r}_{\tilde{i}_{j}})=j
and
\tilde{p}_{\tilde{i}_{j}}\geq 1
implies
\tilde{s}_{j}\leq j+1
and
\sum\limits_{\tilde{i}_{j}=1}^{\tilde{s}_{j}}\tilde{q}_{\tilde{i}_{j}}\leq j
. Hence,

I_{11}\leq\sum\limits_{j=0}^{k}(j+1)(1+||\omega||_{W^{k+1,0}_{p}})^{2j+1}\leq(k+1)^{2}(1+||\omega||_{W^{k+1,0}_{p}})^{2k+1}.

In summary, we obtain

||\not{\Delta}\omega||_{W^{k,0}_{p}}\lesssim||\mathcal{L}_{A}\omega||_{W^{k,0}_{p}}+(1+||\omega||_{W^{k+1,0}_{p}})^{2k+1}.

From Theorem 3.2 of
[
5
]
it follows that

||\omega||_{W^{k+2,0}_{p}}\lesssim||\mathcal{L}_{A}\omega||_{W^{k,0}_{p}}+||\not{D}_{\vec{n}}\omega||_{W^{k+1,0}_{p}}+(1+||\omega||_{W^{k+1,0}_{p}})^{2k+1}.

∎

Lemma 4.3
.

Suppose
\omega
is smooth and satisfies the following equation

\displaystyle\left\{\begin{array}[]{rl}&\not{D}_{t}\omega+\mathcal{L}_{A}\omega=f\\
&\omega(0,\cdot)=\omega_{0},\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\not{D}_{\vec{n}}\omega=\theta,\end{array}\right.

then, for
p\in[2,\infty)
, we have

\displaystyle||\mathcal{L}_{A}\omega||^{p}_{L^{p}}\leq e^{Ct}||\mathcal{L}_{A}\omega_{0}||_{L^{p}}^{p}+\int_{0}^{t}ds\,e^{C(t-s)}\left(||\mathcal{L}_{A}f||_{L^{p}}^{p}+||\not{D}_{\vec{n}}f-\not{D}_{t}\theta||_{L^{p}(\partial M)}^{p}\right).

Proof.

Tediou computation gives

\displaystyle\frac{d}{dt}||\mathcal{L}_{A}\omega||^{p}_{L^{p}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle p\int|\mathcal{L}_{A}\omega|^{p-2}\langle\mathcal{L}_{A}\omega,\mathcal{L}_{A}f\rangle-p\int_{\partial M}|\mathcal{L}_{A}\omega|^{p-2}\langle\mathcal{L}_{A}\omega,A(\not{D}_{\vec{n}}f-\not{D}_{t}\theta)\rangle

\displaystyle-p\int|\mathcal{L}_{A}\omega|^{p-2}g^{-1}\sharp\langle\not{D}_{x}\mathcal{L}_{A}\omega,A(\not{D}_{x}\mathcal{L}_{A}\omega)\rangle

\displaystyle-p(p-2)\int|\mathcal{L}_{A}\omega|^{p-4}g^{-1}\sharp\langle\mathcal{L}_{A}\omega,A(\not{D}_{x}\mathcal{L}_{A}\omega)\rangle\otimes\langle\mathcal{L}_{A}\omega,\not{D}_{x}\mathcal{L}_{A}\omega\rangle

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\int|\mathcal{L}_{A}\omega|^{p-1}|\mathcal{L}_{A}f|+\int_{\partial M}|\mathcal{L}_{A}\omega|^{p-1}|\not{D}_{\vec{n}}f-\not{D}_{t}\theta|-\int|\mathcal{L}_{A}\omega|^{p-2}|\not{D}_{x}\mathcal{L}_{A}\omega|^{2}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle||\mathcal{L}_{A}\omega||_{L^{p}}^{p}+||\mathcal{L}_{A}f||_{L^{p}}^{p}+||\mathcal{L}_{A}\omega||_{L^{p}(\partial M)}^{p}+||\not{D}_{\vec{n}}f-\not{D}_{t}\theta||_{L^{p}(\partial M)}^{p}-\int|\mathcal{L}_{A}\omega|^{p-2}|\not{D}_{x}\mathcal{L}_{A}\omega|^{2}.

Boundary Trace Theorem tells us

\displaystyle||\mathcal{L}_{A}\omega||_{L^{p}(\partial M)}^{p}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle||\,|\mathcal{L}_{A}\omega|^{p}||_{L^{1}(\partial M)}\lesssim||\,|\mathcal{L}_{A}\omega|^{p}||_{W^{1,0}_{1}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle||\mathcal{L}_{A}\omega||_{L^{p}}^{p}+||\not{D}_{x}(|\mathcal{L}_{A}\omega|^{p})||_{L^{1}}\leq||\mathcal{L}_{A}\omega||_{L^{p}}^{p}+p||\,|\mathcal{L}_{A}\omega|^{p-1}|\not{D}_{x}\mathcal{L}_{A}\omega|\,||_{L^{1}}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle C(\epsilon)||\mathcal{L}_{A}\omega||_{L^{p}}^{p}+\epsilon||\,|\mathcal{L}_{A}\omega|^{p-2}|\not{D}_{x}\mathcal{L}_{A}\omega|^{2}||_{L^{1}},

where we have used Kato’s Inequality. In conclusion, one are able to obtain

\displaystyle\frac{d}{dt}||\mathcal{L}_{A}\omega||^{p}_{L^{p}}\lesssim||\mathcal{L}_{A}\omega||_{L^{p}}^{p}+||\mathcal{L}_{A}f||_{L^{p}}^{p}+||\not{D}_{\vec{n}}f-\not{D}_{t}\theta||_{L^{p}(\partial M)}^{p},

which implies the desired result.
∎

Lemma 4.4
.

Suppose
\omega
is smooth and satisfies the following equation

\displaystyle\left\{\begin{array}[]{rl}&\not{D}_{t}\omega+\mathcal{L}_{A}\omega=f\\
&\omega(0,\cdot)=\omega_{0},\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\not{D}_{\vec{n}}\omega=\theta,\end{array}\right.

then, for
p\in[2,\infty)
, we have

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}^{k}(t)\leq Ce^{Ct}\left[\sum\limits_{r+q=0}^{k-1}||\not{D}_{t}^{r}\mathcal{L}_{A}^{q}f(0,\cdot)||_{L^{p}}^{p}+\sum\limits_{i=0}^{k}||\mathcal{L}_{A}^{i}\omega_{0}||_{L^{p}}^{p}\right]+C\int_{0}^{t}e^{C(t-s)}\left[||f(s,\cdot)||^{p}_{W_{p}^{0,k}}+\sum\limits_{i=0}^{k}||\not{D}_{t}^{i}\theta(s,\cdot)||_{L^{p}(\partial M)}^{p}\right]ds,

where
\mathcal{A}^{k}(t):=\sum\limits_{i=0}^{k}\int|\not{D}_{t}^{i}\omega(t,\cdot)|^{p}/p
.

Proof.

Taking
i
-th derivatives with respect to
t
on both sides of
(\ref{cb})
leads to

\displaystyle\left\{\begin{array}[]{rl}&\not{D}_{t}^{i+1}\omega+\mathcal{L}_{A}\not{D}_{t}^{i}\omega=\not{D}_{t}^{i}f\\
&\omega(0,\cdot)=\omega_{0},\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\not{D}_{t}^{j}\omega(0,\cdot)=\not{D}_{t}^{j-1}f-\mathcal{L}_{A}\not{D}_{t}^{j-1}\omega(0,\cdot),\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\not{D}_{\vec{n}}\not{D}_{t}^{i}\omega=\not{D}_{t}^{i}\theta\end{array}\right.

for
1\leq j\leq i
. Tedious computation yields

\displaystyle\frac{d}{dt}\mathcal{A}^{k}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{i=0}^{k}\int|\not{D}_{t}^{i}\omega|^{p-2}\langle\not{D}_{t}^{i}\omega,\not{D}_{t}^{i}f\rangle-\sum\limits_{i=0}^{k}\int_{\partial M}|\not{D}_{t}^{i}\omega|^{p-2}\langle\not{D}_{t}^{i}\omega,A(\not{D}_{t}^{i}\theta)\rangle

\displaystyle-\sum\limits_{i=0}^{k}\int|\not{D}_{t}^{i}\omega|^{p-2}\langle\not{D}_{x}\not{D}_{t}^{i}\omega,A(\not{D}_{x}\not{D}_{t}^{i}\omega)\rangle\sharp g^{-1}

\displaystyle-(p-2)\sum\limits_{i=0}^{k}\int|\not{D}_{t}^{i}\omega|^{p-4}\langle\not{D}_{t}^{i}\omega,A(\not{D}_{x}\not{D}_{t}^{i}\omega)\rangle\otimes\langle\not{D}_{t}^{i}\omega,\not{D}_{x}\not{D}_{t}^{i}\omega\rangle\sharp g^{-1}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}^{k}+||f||^{p}_{W_{p}^{0,k}}+\sum\limits_{i=0}^{k}\int_{\partial M}|\not{D}_{t}^{i}\omega|^{p}+\sum\limits_{i=0}^{k}\int_{\partial M}|\not{D}_{t}^{i}\theta|^{p}-\sum\limits_{i=0}^{k}\int|\not{D}_{t}^{i}\omega|^{p-2}|\not{D}_{x}\not{D}_{t}^{i}\omega|^{2}.

Boundary Trace Theorem tells us

\displaystyle||\not{D}_{t}^{i}\omega||_{L^{p}(\partial M)}^{p}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle||\,|\not{D}_{t}^{i}\omega|^{p}||_{L^{1}(\partial M)}\lesssim||\,|\not{D}_{t}^{i}\omega|^{p}||_{W^{1,0}_{1}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle||\not{D}_{t}^{i}\omega||_{L^{p}}^{p}+||\not{D}_{x}(|\not{D}_{t}^{i}\omega|^{p})||_{L^{1}}\leq||\not{D}_{t}^{i}\omega||_{L^{p}}^{p}+p||\,|\not{D}_{t}^{i}\omega|^{p-1}|\not{D}_{x}\not{D}_{t}^{i}\omega|\,||_{L^{1}}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle C(\epsilon)||\not{D}_{t}^{i}\omega||_{L^{p}}^{p}+\epsilon||\,|\not{D}_{t}^{i}\omega|^{p-2}|\not{D}_{x}\not{D}_{t}^{i}\omega|^{2}||_{L^{1}},

which implies

\displaystyle\frac{d}{dt}\mathcal{A}^{k}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}^{k}+||f||^{p}_{W_{p}^{0,k}}+\sum\limits_{i=0}^{k}||\not{D}_{t}^{i}\theta||_{L^{p}(\partial M)}^{p}.

Gronwall’s Inequality leads to

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}^{k}(t)\leq e^{Ct}\mathcal{A}^{k}(0)+C\int_{0}^{t}e^{C(t-s)}\left[||f(s,\cdot)||^{p}_{W_{p}^{0,k}}+\sum\limits_{i=0}^{k}||\not{D}_{t}^{i}\theta(s,\cdot)||_{L^{p}(\partial M)}^{p}\right]ds.

In the next, we shall estimate
\mathcal{A}^{k}(0)
. Induction argument gives

\not{D}_{t}^{i}\omega(0,\cdot)=\sum\limits_{r+q=0}^{i-1}\not{D}_{t}^{r}\circ(-\mathcal{L}_{A})^{q}f(0,\cdot)+(-\mathcal{L}_{A})^{i}\omega_{0},

which means

\mathcal{A}^{k}(0)\lesssim\sum\limits_{r+q=0}^{k-1}||\not{D}_{t}^{r}\mathcal{L}_{A}^{q}f(0,\cdot)||_{L^{p}}^{p}+\sum\limits_{i=0}^{k}||\mathcal{L}_{A}^{i}\omega_{0}||_{L^{p}}^{p}.

∎
