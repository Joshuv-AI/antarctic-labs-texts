Decoupled algorithms for non-linearly coupled reaction-diffusion competition model with harvesting and Stocking
Muhammad Mohebujjaman, Clarisa Buenrostro, Md. Kamrujjaman, Taufiquar Khan

Abstract
We propose, analyze and test two novel fully discrete decoupled linearized algorithms for a nonlinearly coupled reaction-diffusion N N -species competition model with harvesting or stocking effort. The time-stepping algorithms are first and second order accurate in time and optimally accurate in space. Stability and optimal convergence theorems of the decoupled schemes are proven rigorously. We verify the predicted convergence rates of our analysis and efficacy of the algorithms using numerical experiments and synthetic data for analytical test problems. We also study the effect of harvesting or stocking and diffusion parameters on the evolution of species population density numerically, and observe the co-existence scenario subject to optimal harvesting or stocking.



Key words.

Harvesting or stocking, splitting method, finite element method, stability analysis, convergence analysis

1.1
Significance of the work

In this paper, we propose, analyze, and test two fully discrete and decoupled linearized stable time-stepping algorithms of a non-linearly coupled system of reaction-diffusion equations that describes an
N
-species competition model in a heterogeneous environment with harvesting or stocking. We provide rigorous analysis of the existence and uniqueness of the solutions of the algorithms together with the priori error estimates by proving their stability and convergence theorems. We prove that the both algorithms are optimally accurate in time and space. The numerical tests are presented showing their convergence rates on some known analytical test problems varying number of species. The solution at each time-step can be computed simultaneously for each species in the competition, which can reduce a huge computational cost when compared to coupled non-linear algorithms. A series of numerical experiments are given that show the effect of exponentially varying carrying capacity, non-stationary intrinsic growth rates, varying diffusion parameters, and harvesting or stocking on the population density of the species in the competition.

To the best of our knowledge, the proposed efficient fully-discrete algorithms of the
N
-species reaction-diffusion competition model in (
1
) with harvesting or stocking have not been investigated to date. The proposed algorithms are expected to enable new tools for large-scale computing in population dynamics.

41
). Using Hölder’s, and Poincaré inequalities, we have

\displaystyle(1-\mu_{i})\left(r_{i}(t^{n+1})\phi_{i,h}^{n+1},\phi_{i,h}^{n+1}\right)

\displaystyle\leq|1-\mu_{i}|\|r_{i}(t^{n+1})\|_{\infty}\|\phi_{i,h}^{n+1}\|^{2}\leq C|1-\mu_{i}|\|r_{i}\|_{\infty,\infty}\|\nabla\phi_{i,h}^{n+1}\|^{2}.

Next, using triangle, Hölder’s, and Poincaré inequalities together with the Assumption
3.1
, we have

\displaystyle-\sum\limits_{j=1}^{N}\left(\frac{r_{i}(t^{n+1})}{K(t^{n+1})}\phi_{i,h}^{n+1}(2u_{j,h}^{n}-u_{j,h}^{n-1}),\phi_{i,h}^{n+1}\right)

\displaystyle\leq\sum\limits_{j=1}^{N}\Big\|\frac{r_{i}(t^{n+1})}{K(t^{n+1})}\Big\|_{\infty}\|2u_{j,h}^{n}-u_{j,h}^{n-1}\|_{\infty}\|\phi_{i,h}^{n+1}\|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{C\|r_{i}\|_{\infty,\infty}}{K_{\min}}\|\nabla\phi_{i,h}^{n+1}\|^{2}.

With the assumption
\alpha_{i}>0
, use Hölder’s inequality, Sobolev embedding theorem, Poincaré and Young’s inequalities, and regularity assumption, we obtain

\displaystyle-\sum\limits_{j=1}^{N}\bigg(\frac{r_{i}(t^{n+1})}{K(t^{n+1})}u_{i}(t^{n+1})(2\phi_{j,h}^{n}-\phi_{j,h}^{n-1}),

\displaystyle\phi_{i,h}^{n+1}\bigg)\leq\sum\limits_{j=1}^{N}\frac{\|r_{i}\|_{\infty,\infty}}{K_{\min}}\|u_{i}(t^{n+1})\|_{L^{6}}\|\phi_{i,h}^{n+1}\|_{L^{3}}\|2\phi_{j,h}^{n}-\phi_{j,h}^{n-1}\|

\displaystyle\leq\sum\limits_{j=1}^{N}\frac{\|r_{i}\|_{\infty,\infty}}{K_{\min}}\|u_{i}(t^{n+1})\|_{H^{1}}\|\phi_{i,h}^{n+1}\|^{\frac{1}{2}}\|\nabla\phi_{i,h}^{n+1}\|^{\frac{1}{2}}\|2\phi_{j,h}^{n}-\phi_{j,h}^{n-1}\|

\displaystyle\leq\sum\limits_{j=1}^{N}\left(\frac{\alpha_{i}}{14N}\|\nabla\phi_{i,h}^{n+1}\|^{2}+\frac{C\|r_{i}\|_{\infty,\infty}^{2}}{\alpha_{i}K_{\min}^{2}}\|2\phi_{j,h}^{n}-\phi_{j,h}^{n-1}\|^{2}\right)

\displaystyle\leq\frac{\alpha_{i}}{14}\|\nabla\phi_{i,h}^{n+1}\|^{2}+\frac{C\|r_{i}\|_{\infty,\infty}^{2}}{\alpha_{i}K_{\min}^{2}}\sum\limits_{j=1}^{N}\|2\phi_{j,h}^{n}-\phi_{j,h}^{n-1}\|^{2}.

Use Cauchy-Schwarz, and Young’s inequalities, to obtain

\displaystyle d_{i}\left(\nabla\eta_{i}^{n+1},\nabla\phi_{i,h}^{n+1}\right)\leq d_{i}\|\nabla\eta_{i}^{n+1}\|\|\nabla\phi_{i,h}^{n+1}\|\leq\frac{\alpha_{i}}{14}\|\nabla\phi_{i,h}^{n+1}\|^{2}+\frac{7d_{i}^{2}}{2\alpha_{i}}\|\nabla\eta_{i}^{n+1}\|^{2}.

Using Hölder’s, Sobolev embedding theorem, Poincaré, and Young’s inequalities, we have

\displaystyle(1-\mu_{i})\left(r_{i}(t^{n+1})\eta_{i}^{n+1},\phi_{i,h}^{n+1}\right)

\displaystyle\leq|1-\mu_{i}|\|r_{i}(t^{n+1})\|_{\infty}\|\eta_{i}^{n+1}\|\|\nabla\phi_{i,h}^{n+1}\|

\displaystyle\leq\frac{\alpha_{i}}{14}\|\nabla\phi_{i,h}^{n+1}\|^{2}+\frac{7(1-\mu_{i})^{2}\|r_{i}\|^{2}_{\infty,\infty}}{2\alpha_{i}}\|\eta_{i}^{n+1}\|^{2}.

Using Hölder’s inequality, and triangle inequality, Assumption
3.1
, Poincaré, and Young’s inequalities, we have

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{j=1}^{N}\left(\frac{r_{i}(t^{n+1})}{K(t^{n+1})}\eta_{i}^{n+1}\big(2u_{j,h}^{n}-u_{j,h}^{n-1}\big),\phi_{i,h}^{n+1}\right)

\displaystyle\leq\sum\limits_{j=1}^{N}\frac{\|r_{i}\|_{\infty,\infty}}{K_{\min}}\|\eta_{i}^{n+1}\|\|2u_{j,h}^{n}-u_{j,h}^{n-1}\|_{\infty}\|\phi_{i,h}^{n+1}\|

\displaystyle\leq\sum\limits_{j=1}^{N}\frac{\|r_{i}\|_{\infty,\infty}}{K_{\min}}\|\eta_{i}^{n+1}\|\left(2\|u_{j,h}^{n}\|_{\infty}+\|u_{j,h}^{n-1}\|_{\infty}\right)\|\nabla\phi_{i,h}^{n+1}\|

\displaystyle\leq\frac{C\|r_{i}\|_{\infty,\infty}}{K_{\min}}\|\eta_{i}^{n+1}\|\|\nabla\phi_{i,h}^{n+1}\|

\displaystyle\leq\frac{\alpha_{i}}{14}\|\nabla\phi_{i,h}^{n+1}\|^{2}+\frac{C\|r_{i}\|^{2}_{\infty,\infty}}{\alpha_{i}K_{\min}^{2}}\|\eta_{i}^{n+1}\|^{2}.

Using Hölder’s inequality, Sobolev embedding theorem,, Poincaré inequality, regularity assumption, and Young’s inequality, we have

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{j=1}^{N}\left(\frac{r_{i}(t^{n+1})}{K(t^{n+1})}u_{i}(t^{n+1})(2\eta_{j}^{n}-\eta_{j}^{n-1}),\phi_{i,h}^{n+1}\right)

\displaystyle\leq\sum\limits_{j=1}^{N}\frac{\|r_{i}\|_{\infty,\infty}}{K_{\min}}\|u_{i}(t^{n+1})\|_{L^{6}}\|2\eta_{j}^{n}-\eta_{j}^{n-1}\|\|\phi_{i,h}^{n+1}\|_{L^{3}}

\displaystyle\leq\sum\limits_{j=1}^{N}\frac{\|r_{i}\|_{\infty,\infty}}{K_{\min}}\|u_{i}(t^{n+1})\|_{H^{1}}\|2\eta_{j}^{n}-\eta_{j}^{n-1}\|\|\phi_{i,h}^{n+1}\|^{1/2}\|\nabla\phi_{i,h}^{n+1}\|^{1/2}

\displaystyle\leq\sum\limits_{j=1}^{N}\frac{C\|r_{i}\|_{\infty,\infty}}{K_{\min}}\|u_{i}\|_{L^{\infty}\big(0,T;H^{1}(\Omega)^{d}\big)}\|2\eta_{j}^{n}-\eta_{j}^{n-1}\|\|\nabla\phi_{i,h}^{n+1}\|

\displaystyle\leq\frac{\alpha_{i}}{14}\|\nabla\phi_{i,h}^{n+1}\|^{2}+\frac{C\|r_{i}\|_{\infty,\infty}^{2}}{K_{\min}^{2}}\sum\limits_{j=1}^{N}\|2\eta_{j}^{n}-\eta_{j}^{n-1}\|^{2}.

Using Taylor’s series, Cauchy-Schwarz and Young’s inequalities the last term is evaluated
as

\displaystyle\Big|-G\left(t,u_{i},\phi_{i,h}^{n+1}\right)\Big|

\displaystyle\leq\Delta t^{2}\frac{C\|r_{i}\|_{\infty,\infty}}{K_{\min}}\|u_{i}\|_{L^{\infty}\big(0,T;H^{1}(\Omega)^{d}\big)}\sum\limits_{j=1}^{N}\|u_{j,tt}\|_{L^{\infty}\big(0,T;L^{2}(\Omega)^{d}\big)}\|\nabla\phi_{i,h}^{n+1}\|

\displaystyle+C\Delta t^{2}\|u_{i,ttt}\|_{L^{\infty}\big(0,T;L^{2}(\Omega)^{d}\big)}\|\nabla\phi_{i,h}^{n+1}\|

\displaystyle\leq\frac{\alpha_{i}}{7}\|\nabla\phi_{i,h}^{n+1}\|^{2}+\frac{C\Delta t^{4}\|r_{i}\|_{\infty,\infty}^{2}}{\alpha_{i}K_{\min}^{2}}.

Using these estimates in (
41
) and reducing yields

\displaystyle\frac{1}{4\Delta t}\Big(\|\phi_{i,h}^{n+1}\|^{2}-\|\phi_{i,h}^{n}\|^{2}+\|2\phi_{i,h}^{n+1}-\phi_{i,h}^{n}\|^{2}-\|2\phi_{i,h}^{n}-\phi_{i,h}^{n-1}\|^{2}+\|\phi_{i,h}^{n+1}-2\phi_{i,h}^{n}+\phi_{i,h}^{n-1}\|^{2}\Big)

\displaystyle+\frac{\alpha_{i}}{2}\|\nabla\phi_{i,h}^{n+1}\|^{2}\leq\frac{C\|r_{i}\|_{\infty,\infty}^{2}}{\alpha_{i}K_{\min}^{2}}\sum\limits_{j=1}^{N}\|2\phi_{j,h}^{n}-\phi_{j,h}^{n-1}\|^{2}+\frac{7d_{i}^{2}}{2\alpha_{i}}\|\nabla\eta_{i}^{n+1}\|^{2}+\frac{7(1-\mu_{i})^{2}\|r_{i}\|^{2}_{\infty,\infty}}{2\alpha_{i}}\|\eta_{i}^{n+1}\|^{2}

\displaystyle+\frac{C\|r_{i}\|^{2}_{\infty,\infty}}{\alpha_{i}K_{\min}^{2}}\|\eta_{i}^{n+1}\|^{2}+\frac{C\|r_{i}\|_{\infty,\infty}^{2}}{K_{\min}^{2}}\sum\limits_{j=1}^{N}\|2\eta_{j}^{n}-\eta_{j}^{n-1}\|^{2}+\frac{C\Delta t^{4}\|r_{i}\|_{\infty,\infty}^{2}}{\alpha_{i}K_{\min}^{2}}

\displaystyle\leq C\sum\limits_{j=1}^{N}\|2\phi_{j,h}^{n}-\phi_{j,h}^{n-1}\|^{2}+Ch^{2k}+Ch^{2k+2}+C\Delta t^{4}.

(42)

Dropping non-negative term from left-hand-side, multiplying both sides by
4\Delta t
, using
\|\phi_{i,h}^{0}\|=\|\phi_{i,h}^{1}\|=0
, and summing over the time-steps
n=1,2,\cdots,M-1
, we have

\displaystyle\|\phi_{i,h}^{M}\|^{2}+\|2\phi_{i,h}^{M}-\phi_{i,h}^{M-1}\|^{2}+2\alpha_{i}\Delta t\sum\limits_{n=2}^{M}\|\nabla\phi_{i,h}^{n}\|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq C\Delta t\sum\limits_{n=1}^{M-1}\sum\limits_{j=1}^{N}\|2\phi_{j,h}^{n}-\phi_{j,h}^{n-1}\|^{2}+C(h^{2k}+\Delta t^{4}).

(43)

Sum over
i=1,2,\cdots,N
, drop non-negative terms from left-hand-side, and reducing, gives

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{i=1}^{N}\|\phi_{i,h}^{M}\|^{2}+2\alpha_{i}\Delta t\sum\limits_{n=2}^{M}\sum\limits_{i=1}^{N}\|\nabla\phi_{i,h}^{n}\|^{2}\leq C\Delta t\sum\limits_{n=2}^{M-1}\sum\limits_{i=1}^{N}\|\phi_{i,h}^{n}\|^{2}+C(h^{2k}+\Delta t^{4}).

Applying the discrete Grönwall Lemma
1
, we have

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{i=1}^{N}\|\phi_{i,h}^{M}\|^{2}+2\alpha_{i}\Delta t\sum\limits_{n=2}^{M}\sum\limits_{i=1}^{N}\|\nabla\phi_{i,h}^{n}\|^{2}\leq C(h^{2k}+\Delta t^{4}),

(44)

for
i=1,2,\cdots,N
, which gives

\displaystyle\|\phi_{i,h}^{M}\|^{2}+2\alpha_{i}\Delta t\sum\limits_{n=2}^{M}\|\nabla\phi_{i,h}^{n}\|^{2}\leq C(h^{2k}+\Delta t^{4}).

(45)

Use of triangle and Young’s inequalities, and regularity assumption completes the proof.
∎

Now we proof the assumption
\|u_{i,h}^{n}\|_{\infty}\leq C
that was used in stability Theorems
2
-
3
and in convergence Theorems
4
-
5
by principle of mathematical induction. The strategy of this proof is adopted from the idea of Wong in the analysis of three-species competition model
[
28
]
.

Lemma 6
.

\|u_{i,h}^{n}\|_{\infty}\leq C
,
\forall n\in\mathbb{N}
.

Proof.

Basic step:
u_{i,h}^{0}=I_{h}(u_{i}(0,\boldsymbol{x})),

where
I_{h}
is an appropriate interpolation operator. If
u_{i}(0,\boldsymbol{x})
is sufficiently regular for
\boldsymbol{x}\in\Omega
, we have
\|u_{i,h}^{0}\|_{\infty}\leq C
, for some constant
C>0
.

Inductive step: Assume for some
L\in\mathbb{N}
,
\|u_{i,h}^{L}\|_{\infty}\leq C
holds true. Then we have

\displaystyle\|u_{i,h}^{L+1}\|_{\infty}

\displaystyle\leq Ch^{-\frac{3}{2}}\|u_{i,h}^{L+1}\|\hskip 5.69054pt\text{(Agmon’s inequality \cite[cite]{[\@@bibref{}{Robinson2016Three-Dimensional}{}{}]} and discrete inverse inequality)}

\displaystyle=Ch^{-\frac{3}{2}}\|u_{i,h}^{L+1}-u_{i}(t^{L+1})+u_{i}(t^{L+1})\|

\displaystyle\leq Ch^{-\frac{3}{2}}\left(\|u_{i,h}^{L+1}-u_{i}(t^{L+1})\|+\|u_{i}(t^{L+1})\|\right)\hskip 5.69054pt\text{(Triangle inequality)}

\displaystyle\leq Ch^{-\frac{3}{2}}\left(\|\phi_{i,h}^{L+1}\|+\|\eta_{i}^{L+1}\|+\|u_{i}(t^{L+1})\|\right)\hskip 5.69054pt\text{(Triangle inequality)}.

Using inductive hypothesis in (
34
), and regularity assumption, the above bound can be written as

\displaystyle\|u_{i,h}^{L+1}\|_{\infty}\leq C\big(h^{k-\frac{3}{2}}+h^{-\frac{3}{2}}\Delta t+h^{k-\frac{1}{2}}+h^{-\frac{3}{2}}\big).

(46)

Therefore,
\|u_{i,h}^{L+1}\|_{\infty}\leq C
holds also true. Hence, by the principle of mathematical induction,
\|u_{i,h}^{n}\|_{\infty}\leq C
holds true
\forall n\in\mathbb{N}\cup\{0\}
.
∎

5
Conclusion and Future Research

Time evolutionary reaction-diffusion equation is the basis of harvesting and/or stocking model in population dynamics. In this paper, we propose a time-dependent system of non-linear coupled partial differential equations representing the dynamics of an
N
-species competition model with harvesting and/or stocking effect. We also propose, analyze and test two fully discrete decoupled stable algorithms for numerical computation. We have proven the first scheme is first-order accurate and the second scheme is second-order accurate in time and both are optimally accurate in space. Numerical tests verify the predicted convergence rates with some analytical test problems for both two- and three-species competition models. The linearized decoupled algorithms are efficient as at each time-step the solution for each species can be computed simultaneously, which can save a huge computational time in large-scale computing with complex problems.

Numerical experiments exhibit (a) if the diffusion rate increases the population density decreases and faster the process of extinction, (b) if the harvesting parameter of a species increases, its density decreases, and other species get benefit in the competition, and (c) if the stocking effect of a species intensify, the population density increases and tends to win over the other species in the competition, that is, the survival period of the species increases. For a particular competition model, a set of values of the harvesting or stocking parameters can be found for which co-existence of all species will be ensured. The harvesting and/or stocking parameter can be a useful controlling tool in the population dynamics with limited natural resources.

As a future research, we will propose, analyze and test an efficient decoupled algorithm for this
N
-species competition model following the works in
[
22
,
23
]
so that at each time-step each species solver shares a common system matrix which will further save computer memory and assembling time of system matrix.
