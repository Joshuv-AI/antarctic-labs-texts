Three-body resonant states of Λ 9 {}^{9}_{\Lambda} Be with α + α + Λ \alpha+\alpha+\Lambda cluster model
Qian Wu, Yasuro Funaki, Emiko Hiyama, Hongshi Zong

Abstract
We study the structure of Λ 9 {}^{9}_{\Lambda} Be in the framework of three body α + α + Λ \alpha+\alpha+\Lambda cluster model using YNG-NF interaction with the Gaussian expansion method. Employing the complex scaling method, we obtain the energies of bound states as well as energies and decay widths of the resonant states. By analyzing our wave functions of bound states and resonant states, we confirm three analogue states of Λ 9 {}^{9}_{\Lambda} Be pointed out by Band o ¯ {\rm\bar{o}} and Motoba et al. motoba1983 ; motoba1985 ; bando1983 , 8 Be analogue states, Λ 9 {}^{9}_{\Lambda} Be genuine states and 9 Be analogue states. The new states of Λ 9 {}^{9}_{\Lambda} Be are also obtained at a high energy region with broader decay widths.

As explained in Introduction, the classification of the spectra shown in Fig.

However, since this state is obtained by the CSM as having a very broad width, it is no more trivial to physically interpret any physical quantities calculated by using the resonant wave function. Thus we first construct an approximate
1_{1}^{-}

1^{-}_{1}

V=V_{0}e^{-\mu(r_{1}^{2}+r_{2}^{2}+r_{3}^{2})},

(9)

\displaystyle\hskip 30.00005pt\propto\sum_{l=0,2}C_{l}^{(5,0)}\mathscr{A}|\,(4l,11)_{J=1}\phi_{\alpha}\phi_{\alpha}\rangle,

(10)

\displaystyle|\,(5,0)_{1}\rangle\equiv\sum_{l=0,2}C_{l}^{(5,0)}|\,(4l,11)_{J=1}\rangle,

Summary

We have calculated energy spectra of
{}^{9}_{\Lambda}
Be within the framework of

\alpha+\alpha+\Lambda
three-body model.
In this work, we employed the
\alpha-\alpha

so as to reproduce the observed
\alpha

\Lambda N

\alpha

\Lambda N

{}^{5}_{\Lambda}
He and
{}^{9}_{\Lambda}

{}^{9}_{\Lambda}
