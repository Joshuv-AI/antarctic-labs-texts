The replacement property of PSL ( 2 , p ) (2,p) and PSL ( 2 , p 2 ) (2,p^{2})
Hy P.G Lam

Abstract
In 2014, Benjamin Nachman [ 1 ] showed that when p ≡ p\equiv 1 mod 8, the 2-dimensional projective linear group over the field of p p elements fails the replacement property if the maximal length m m of an irredundant generating sequence for the group is 3. In addition, if m = 4 m=4 , the group satisfies the property for any prime p p . In this paper, we will extend such classification for PSL ( 2 , q ) (2,q) where q q is p p and p 2 p^{2} with conditions of modulo 8 and 10 on p p .

Definition
: A collection of subgroups
\{H_{i}\leq G\}_{i\in I}
with index set

if for every

\bigcap_{i\in I}H_{i}
is properly contained in
\bigcap_{i\in I-\{j\}}H_{i}

This definition arises naturally from an analogous example from linear algebra: consider an

with basis
\mathcal{B}
=
\{v_{i}:1\leq i\leq n\}

W_{i}
=
span(v_{j}:j\neq i)
are in general positions.

To relate the definition with the study of finite generating sets of group, suppose we are given an irredundant sequence
s=(g_{1},...,g_{k})
of a group
G

be the subgroup generated by the generators

where

H_{i}=\langle g_{j}:i\neq j\rangle
. At this point, it readily follows that the subgroups
H_{i}

, whereas intersection of any
k-1
members of the collection must contain the subgroup generated by exactly one generator. Yet, the same argument can be made to obtain a collection of maximal subgroups in general positions. Since each
H_{i}
is a proper subgroup of

M_{i}
strictly containing
H_{i}
, therefore, containing generators

for

[
4

For the interest of this section, we note several important relations between maximal subgroups in general position and RP-satisfaction.

Proposition 3.1
(D. Collins and R.K.
Dennis) : Let

a group

satisfies the replacement

Proof.

Suppose

∎

Proposition 3.4
