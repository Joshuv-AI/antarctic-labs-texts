Minimal submanifolds in certain types of kaehler product manifold
Xingda Liu Bang Xiao

Abstract
Let M M be a real l l -dimensional minimal submanifold with flat normal connection in a kaehler product manifold M ¯ m × M ¯ n \overline{M}^{m}\times\overline{M}^{n} where M ¯ m \overline{M}^{m} and M ¯ n \overline{M}^{n} are complex m m -dimensional and complex n n -dimensional kaehler manifolds with constant holomorphic sectional curvature c 1 c_{1} and c 2 c_{2} respectively. We give a formula for the Laplacian of the second fundamental form of M M . Specially we discuss the F-anti invariant case. We also give some applications of this formula.



Key words:
Kaehler product manifold,
minimal submanifolds.
