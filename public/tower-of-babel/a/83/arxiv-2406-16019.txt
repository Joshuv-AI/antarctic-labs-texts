On spherical fibrations and Poincare complexes
Wen Shen

Abstract
. In this paper, we prove that certain spherical fibrations over certain CW-complexes are stably fibre homotopy equivalent to TOP \mathrm{TOP} -spherical fibrations (see Definition 1.1 ). Applying this result, we get a sufficient condition for whether a Poincar e ´ \mathrm{\acute{e}} complex is of the homotopy type of a topological manifold. Moreover, we present the classification for some highly connected manifolds by the homotopy types of highly connected Poincar e ´ \mathrm{\acute{e}} complexes.
