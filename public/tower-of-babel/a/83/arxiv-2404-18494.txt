Abstract
. By Perelman’s ℒ \mathcal{L} -geodesic theory, we study the blow-down solutions on a noncompact κ \kappa -noncollapsed steady gradient Ricci soliton ( M n , g ) (M^{n},g) ( n ≥ 4 ) (n\geq 4) with nonnegative curvature operator and positive Ricci curvature away from a compact set of M M . We prove that any compact split ancient solution of codimension one from the blow-down of ( M , g ) (M,g) is of type I. The result is a generalization of our previous work from n = 4 n=4 to any dimension.

Preliminaries

A complete Riemannian metric
g
on
M
is called a gradient Ricci soliton if there exists a smooth function

(which is called a potential function) on

(1.1)

R_{ij}(g)+\rho g_{ij}=\nabla_{i}\nabla_{j}f,

where
\rho

\rho>,=,<0

[
14

In the case of steady Ricci solitons, we can rewrite (
1.1

(1.2)

\displaystyle 2\operatorname{Ric}(g)=L_{X}g,

where

is the Lie operator along the gradient vector field (VF)

generalized by

Let
\{\phi^{*}_{t}\}_{t\in(-\infty,\infty)}
be the one-parameter subgroup of transformations generated by
-X
. Then

g(t)=\phi^{*}_{t}(g)
(

is a solution of Ricci flow. Namely,

satisfies

(1.3)

\displaystyle\frac{\partial g}{\partial t}=-2{\rm Ric}(g),~g(0)=g.

For simplicity, we call

the soliton Ricci flow of
(M,g)
.

By (
1.2
), we have

(1.4)

\displaystyle\langle\nabla R,\nabla f\rangle=-2\operatorname{Ric}(\nabla f,\nabla f),

where

is the scalar curvature of

It follows

R+|\nabla f|^{2}={\rm Const.}

Since any (non Ricci-flat) steady gradient Ricci soliton has positive scalar curvature (
[

,

(1.5)

\displaystyle R+|\nabla f|^{2}=1.

Lemma 1.2
.

Let
(M,g)
be the
\kappa
-noncollapsed steady gradient Ricci soliton with
{\rm Rm}\geq 0
on
M\setminus K
. Then there exists a uniform constant
C>0
, such that

(1.6)

\displaystyle\frac{|\Delta R(p,t)|}{R^{2}(p,t)}\leq C,~\forall(p,t)\in M\times(-\infty,0].

Proof.

On the contrary, if (
1.6
) is not true,
there is a sequence of
p_{i}
and
t_{i}
such that

(1.7)

\displaystyle\frac{|\Delta R(p_{i},t_{i})|}{R^{2}(p_{i},t_{i})}\to\infty.

Then we consider rescaled flows
(M,g_{i}(t);p_{i})
, where

\displaystyle g_{i}(t)=R(p_{i},t_{i})g(R^{-1}(p_{i},t_{i})t+t_{i}).

By the isometry
(M,g(t_{i});p_{i})\cong(M,g,\phi_{t_{i}}(p_{i}))
, it is easy to see

(1.8)

\displaystyle(M,g_{i}(t);p_{i})\cong(M,g_{\phi_{t_{i}}(p_{i})}(t);\phi_{t_{i}}(p_{i})),

where

\displaystyle g_{\phi_{t_{i}}(p_{i})}(t)=R_{g}(\phi_{t_{i}}(p_{i}))g(R_{g}^{-1}(\phi_{t_{i}}(p_{i}))t).

We may assume that
\phi_{t_{i}}(p_{i})\to\infty
, otherwise,

\displaystyle\frac{|\Delta R(p_{i},t_{i})|}{R^{2}(p_{i},t_{i})}=\frac{|\Delta_{g}R_{g}(\phi_{t_{i}}(p_{i}))|_{g}}{R_{g}^{2}(\phi_{t_{i}}(p_{i}))}\leq C_{0},

which contradicts to (
1.7
).

By Proposition
1.1
, the scaled Ricci flows
(M,g_{\phi_{t_{i}}(p_{i})}(t);\phi_{t_{i}}(p_{i}))
converge in the Cheeger-Gromov sense. Thus

\displaystyle\left|\operatorname{Rm}\left(q,g_{\phi_{t_{i}}(p_{i})}(t)\right)\right|\leq C_{1},\forall(q,t)\in B\left(\phi_{t_{i}}(p_{i}),1;g_{\phi_{t_{i}}(p_{i})}(-1)\right)\times[-1,0].

By the Shi’s estimate
[
21
]
, we get

\displaystyle\left|\Delta R\left(q,g_{\phi_{t_{i}}(p_{i})}(t)\right)\right|\leq C_{2},\forall(q,t)\in B\left(\phi_{t_{i}}(p_{i}),\frac{1}{2};g_{\phi_{t_{i}}(p_{i})}(-1)\right)\times[-\frac{1}{2},0].

In particular,

\displaystyle\left|\Delta R\left(\phi_{t_{i}}(p_{i}),g_{\phi_{t_{i}}(p_{i})}(0)\right)\right|=\frac{|\Delta_{g}R_{g}(\phi_{t_{i}}(p_{i}))|_{g}}{R_{g}^{2}(\phi_{t_{i}}(p_{i}))}=\frac{|\Delta R(p_{i},t_{i})|}{R^{2}(p_{i},t_{i})}\leq C_{2},

which is a contradiction with (
1.7
). The lemma is proved.
