On the other hand,

E_{2}^{0,1}(S^{0})=\mathrm{Ker}[E_{1}^{0,1}(S^{0})\to E_{1}^{1,1}(S^{0})]
.
Finally

E_{\infty}^{2,0}(S^{0})=E_{3}^{2,0}(S^{0})=\mathrm{Coker}[E_{2}^{0,1}(S^{0})\to E_{2}^{2,0}(S^{0})].

Since
\mathrm{Codim}_{S}F\geq 3
, we have
E_{1}^{i,1}(S^{0})=E_{1}^{i,1}(S)
and

E_{1}^{i,0}(S^{0})=E_{1}^{i,0}(S)
for all
i
. Hence
E_{2}^{0,1}(S^{0})=E_{2}^{0,1}(S)
and
E_{2}^{2,0}(S^{0})=E_{2}^{2,0}(S)
.
This implies that
E_{\infty}^{2,0}(S^{0})=E_{\infty}^{2,0}(S)
.

These show that

F^{1}(\mathbf{H}^{2}(S,\Omega^{\cdot}_{S/\mathbf{C}^{1}}))=F^{1}(\mathbf{H}^{2}(S^{0},\Omega^{\cdot}_{S^{0}/\mathbf{C}^{1}})).

We next show that
H^{2}(S,p_{2}^{-1}\mathcal{O}_{\mathbf{C}^{1}})=0
.
In order to do, we apply the Leray spectral sequence

E_{2}^{p,q}:=H^{p}(S,R^{q}(p_{2})_{*}p_{2}^{-1}\mathcal{O}_{\mathbf{C}^{1}})\Rightarrow H^{2}(S,p_{2}^{-1}\mathcal{O}_{\mathbf{C}^{1}}).

Since
S=(\mathfrak{t}^{n})^{*}\times\mathbf{C}^{1}
, we have an isomorphism

R^{p}(p_{2})_{*}p_{2}^{-1}\mathcal{O}_{\mathbf{C}^{1}}\cong R^{p}(p_{2})_{*}\mathbf{C}\otimes_{\mathbf{C}}\mathcal{O}_{\mathbf{C}^{1}}.

Note that
R^{p}(p_{2})_{*}\mathbf{C}=0
for
p>0
and
(p_{2})_{*}\mathbf{C}=\mathbf{C}
. Since

H^{2}(\mathbf{C}^{1},\mathcal{O}_{\mathbf{C}^{1}})=0
, we see that
H^{2}(S,p_{2}^{-1}\mathcal{O}_{\mathbf{C}^{1}})=0
.

In particular, we have
F^{1}(\mathbf{H}^{2}(S,\Omega^{\cdot}_{S/\mathbf{C}^{1}}))=0
; hence,

\mathrm{Ker}[H^{2}(S^{0},p_{2}^{-1}\mathcal{O}_{\mathbf{C}^{1}})\to H^{2}(S^{0},\mathcal{O}_{S^{0}})]=0.

By the first exact sequence in the proof, we see that

H^{1}(S^{0},{\mathcal{A}ut}^{\mathcal{Z}^{0}/\mathbf{C}^{1}})=0.

\square
