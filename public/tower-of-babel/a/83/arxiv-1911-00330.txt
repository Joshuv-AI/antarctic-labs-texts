Thermodynamics for the k-essence Emergent Reissner-Nordstrom-de Sitter Spacetime
Goutam Manna, Bivash Majumder, Ashoke Das

Abstract
The k- essence emergent Reissner-Nordstrom-de Sitter spacetime has exactly mapped on to the Robinson-Trautman (RT) type spacetime with cosmological constant Λ \Lambda for certain configuration of k- essence scalar field. Theoretically, we evaluated that the thermodynamical quantities for the RT type emergent black hole is different from the usual one in the presence of kinetic energy of the k- essence scalar field i.e., the dark energy density. We restrict ourselves into the fact that the dark energy density (K) is to be unity, then the effective temperature and pressure both are negative for the RT type emergent black hole which implies that the system is thermodynamically unstable when the charge Q ≠ 0 Q\neq 0 and the emergent spacetime is only dark energy dominated and it does not radiate when Q = 0 Q=0 . The thermodynamically unstable situation is physically plausible only when we consider spin degrees of freedom of a system. We have made this analysis in the context of dark energy in an emergent gravity scenario having k- essence scalar fields ϕ \phi with a Dirac-Born-Infeld type lagrangian. The scalar field also satisfies the emergent equation of motion at r → ∞ r\rightarrow\infty .
