Further study on the production of P P -wave doubly heavy baryons from Z Z -boson decays
Hai-Jiang Tian ∗, Xuan Luo, Hai-Bing Fu

Abstract
In this paper, we carried out a systematic investigation for the excited doubly heavy baryons production in Z Z -boson decays within the NRQCD factorization approach. Our investigation accounts for all the P P -wave intermediate diquark states, i.e. ⟨ c c ⟩ [ 1 P 1 ] 3 ¯ \langle cc\rangle[^{1}P_{1}]_{\bar{3}} , ⟨ c c ⟩ [ 3 P J ] 6 \langle cc\rangle[^{3}P_{J}]_{6} , ⟨ b c ⟩ [ 1 P 1 ] 3 ¯ / 6 \langle bc\rangle[^{1}P_{1}]_{\bar{3}/6} , ⟨ b c ⟩ [ 3 P J ] 3 ¯ / 6 \langle bc\rangle[^{3}P_{J}]_{\bar{3}/6} , ⟨ b b ⟩ [ 1 P 1 ] 3 ¯ \langle bb\rangle[^{1}P_{1}]_{\bar{3}} , and ⟨ b b ⟩ [ 3 P J ] 6 \langle bb\rangle[^{3}P_{J}]_{6} with J = ( 0 , 1 , 2 ) J=(0,1,2) . The results show that contributions from all diquark states in P P -wave were 7 % 7\% , 8 % 8\% , and 3 % 3\% in comparing with S S -wave for the production of Ξ c ​ c \Xi_{cc} , Ξ b ​ c \Xi_{bc} and Ξ b ​ b \Xi_{bb} via Z Z -boson decay, respectively. Based on these results, we predicted about 0.539 × 10 3 ​ ( 10 6 ) 0.539\times 10^{3}(10^{6}) events for Ξ c ​ c \Xi_{cc} , 1.827 × 10 3 ​ ( 10 6 ) 1.827\times 10^{3}(10^{6}) events for Ξ b ​ c \Xi_{bc} , and 0.036 × 10 3 ​ ( 10 6 ) 0.036\times 10^{3}(10^{6}) events for Ξ b ​ b \Xi_{bb} can be produced annually at the LHC (CEPC). Additionally, we plot the differential decay widths of Ξ c ​ c \Xi_{cc} , Ξ b ​ c \Xi_{bc} and Ξ b ​ b \Xi_{bb} as a function of the invariant mass s 23 s_{23} and energy function z z distributions, and analyze the theoretical uncertainties in decay width arising from the mass parameters of heavy quark.

According to the widely accepted hadron production mechanism for doubly heavy baryons

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{{l_{z}}}{\varepsilon_{\alpha}^{l}\varepsilon_{\alpha^{\prime}}^{l*}=\Pi{{}_{\alpha\alpha^{\prime}}}}.

(15)

In which the symbol

represents the excited states
[^{1}P_{1}]
. In the case of excited states
[^{3}P_{J}]
, the sum over polarized tensors are represented by

\displaystyle\varepsilon_{\alpha\beta}^{0}\varepsilon_{\alpha^{\prime}\beta^{\prime}}^{0*}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{3}\Pi{{}_{\alpha\alpha^{\prime}}},

(16)

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{{J_{z}}}{\varepsilon_{\alpha\beta}^{1}\varepsilon_{\alpha^{\prime}\beta^{\prime}}^{1*}}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2}(\Pi{{}_{\alpha\alpha^{\prime}}}\Pi{{}_{\beta\beta^{\prime}}}-\Pi{{}_{\alpha\beta^{\prime}}}\Pi{{}_{\beta\alpha^{\prime}}}),

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{{J_{z}}}{\varepsilon_{\alpha\beta}^{2}\varepsilon_{\alpha^{\prime}\beta^{\prime}}^{2*}}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2}(\Pi{{}_{\alpha\alpha^{\prime}}}\Pi{{}_{\beta\beta^{\prime}}}+\Pi{{}_{\alpha\beta^{\prime}}}\Pi{{}_{\alpha^{\prime}\beta}})

\displaystyle-\frac{1}{3}\Pi{{}_{\alpha\beta^{\prime}}}\Pi{{}_{\beta\alpha^{\prime}}},

with the definition

\displaystyle\Pi{{}_{\alpha\beta}=-{g_{\alpha\beta}}-\frac{{{p_{1\alpha}}{p_{1\beta}}}}{{M_{QQ^{\prime}}^{2}}}}.

According to Fig.

, it is easy to write the expression for color factor
C_{ij,k}
,

\displaystyle C_{ij,k}=N\times\sum\limits_{a,m,n}(T^{a})_{im}(T^{a})_{jn}\times G_{mnk},

(18)

with letters
k
and

are color indices of diquark and gluon, respectively, and
N=\sqrt{1/2}
refers to the normalization factor. In addition, those marks
i,j,m
, and
n=(1,2,3)
are also the color indices, which correspond to two outgoing antiquarks and two constituent active quarks in the diquark state, respectively. For the
\bar{3}(6)
state, the value of
G_{mnk}
is related to the function
\varepsilon_{mjk}(f_{mjk})
, by given as

\displaystyle\varepsilon_{mjk}\varepsilon_{m^{\prime}j^{\prime}k}=\delta_{mm^{\prime}}\delta_{jj^{\prime}}-\delta_{mj^{\prime}}\delta_{jm^{\prime}},

\displaystyle f_{mjk}f_{m^{\prime}j^{\prime}k}=\delta_{mm^{\prime}}\delta_{jj^{\prime}}+\delta_{mj^{\prime}}\delta_{jm^{\prime}}.

(19)

II.3
Hadronization from diquark
(QQ^{\prime})[n]
to
\Xi_{QQ^{\prime}}

The process of diquark states
\langle QQ^{\prime}\rangle[n]
hadronization into the final states
\Xi_{QQ^{\prime}}
is regarded as a non-perturbative, and its impact is reflected by the overall factors
\left\langle\mathcal{O}^{H}(n)\right\rangle
. The factors
\left\langle\mathcal{O}^{H}(n)\right\rangle
, also known as the transition probabilities, can be represented by
h_{\bar{3}}
and
h_{6}
in color
\bar{3}
and color
6
, respectively. Detailed researches and discussions about
h_{\bar{3}}
(
h_{6}
) can be found in Refs.
Zheng:2015ixa
;
Ma:2003zk
;
Jiang:2012jt
;
Niu:2018ycb
;
Zhang:2022jst
.

In accordance with the velocity scaling rule of the NRQCD factorization approach, one can assume that the transition probabilities
h_{\bar{3}}
and
h_{6}
hold an equal horizon
Chang:2006xp
;
Chang:2006eu
;
Ma:2003zk
. Therefore, the wave function evaluated at the origin can be interconnected with them
Chang:2006eu
.

\displaystyle h_{6}\sim h_{\bar{3}}=\langle\mathcal{O}^{H}(n)\rangle=|\Psi(0)|^{2},

(20)

for
S
-wave, and for
P
-wave

\displaystyle h_{6}\sim h_{\bar{3}}=\langle\mathcal{O}^{H}(n)\rangle=|\Psi^{\prime}(0)|^{2}.

(21)

The wave function at the origin can be naturally connected to the radial wave function at the origin:

\displaystyle|\Psi(0)|^{2}=\dfrac{1}{4\pi}|R(0)|^{2},

\displaystyle|\Psi^{\prime}(0)|^{2}=\dfrac{3}{4\pi}|R^{\prime}(0)|^{2}.

(22)

Based on the NRQCD factorization approach,
\Xi_{QQ^{\prime}}
is a bound state of two heavy quarks with other light dynamical freedom of QCD and can be can be described by a series of Fock states:

\displaystyle\left|{{\Xi_{QQ^{\prime}}}}\right\rangle

\displaystyle={c_{1}}(v)\left|{(QQ^{\prime})q}\right\rangle+{c_{2}}(v)\left|{(QQ^{\prime})qg}\right\rangle

(23)

\displaystyle+{c_{3}}(v)\left|{(QQ^{\prime})qgg}\right\rangle+\cdots

Due to the light quark may produce gluons easily, the constituents
c_{i}(v)
with
i=(1,2,3...)
in Eq. (
23
) have equivalent significance with each other,
e.g.

c_{1}(v)\sim c_{2}(v)\sim c_{3}(v)
. In the
\langle QQ^{\prime}\rangle[^{3}S_{1}]_{\bar{3}}
state, one of heavy quarks can produce a gluon without altering its spin, and the gluon can divide into a light quark pair
q\bar{q}
.
Then the heavy diquark state
\langle QQ^{\prime}\rangle
can form a final baryon state by capturing a light quark
q
.
In
\langle QQ^{\prime}\rangle[^{1}S_{0}]_{6}
state, the heavy quark spin must be altered by the emitted gluon when a baryon is formed by
\left|{(QQ^{\prime})q}\right\rangle
. The reason lies in a suppression from
h_{6}
.
In the case of the heavy quark’s spin remaining unchanged, one of the heavy quarks produces a gluon, and this gluon can separate into a quark-antiquark
q\bar{q}
pair. Additionally, the light quark
q
has the ability to produce a gluon, which can be used to construct the component with
qg
.
Based on the above analysis, the
h_{6}
and
h_{\bar{3}}
hold the same order in
v_{c}
. We assume
h_{6}
and
h_{\bar{3}}
hold the same order in
v_{c}
for convenience.

Summary

In this paper, we carry out an investigation of the excited doubly heavy baryons
\Xi_{QQ^{\prime}}
production through processes
Z\to\Xi_{QQ^{\prime}}+\bar{Q}+\bar{Q}^{\prime}
based on the NRQCD factorization approach. By considering all the contributions from these intermediate
P
-wave diquark states with spin and color quantum number,
i.e
.,
\langle cc\rangle[^{1}P_{1}]_{\bar{3}}
,
\langle cc\rangle[^{3}P_{0}]_{6}
,
\langle cc\rangle[^{3}P_{1}]_{\bar{3}(6)}
, and
\langle cc\rangle[^{3}P_{2}]_{\bar{3}(6)}

\langle bc\rangle[^{1}P_{1}]_{\bar{3}(b)}
,
\langle bc\rangle[^{3}P_{0}]_{\bar{3}(6)}
,
\langle bc\rangle[^{3}P_{1}]_{\bar{3}(6)}
, and
\langle bc\rangle[^{3}P_{2}]_{\bar{3}(6)}

\langle bb\rangle[^{1}P_{1}]_{\bar{3}}
,
\langle bb\rangle[^{3}P_{0}]_{6}
,
\langle bb\rangle[^{3}P_{1}]_{6}
, and
\langle bb\rangle[^{3}P_{2}]_{6}
, the decay widths for
P
-wave states
\Xi_{cc}
,
\Xi_{bc}
and
