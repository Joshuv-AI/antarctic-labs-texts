Iyama–Solberg correspondence for exact dg categories
Xiaofa Chen

Abstract
. We generalize the notions of d d -cluster tilting pair and d d -Auslander exact dg category to d d -precluster tilting triple and d d -minimal Auslander–Gorenstein exact dg category. We give a bijection between equivalence classes of d d -precluster tilting triples and equivalence classes of d d -minimal Auslander–Gorenstein exact dg categories. Our bijection generalizes Iyama–Solberg correspondence for module categories.

It is not straightforward to carry out this strategy because in certain constructions, the induced morphisms are not canonical.
