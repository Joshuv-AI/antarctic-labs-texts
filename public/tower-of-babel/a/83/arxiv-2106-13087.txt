Low-dimensional antiferromagnetic fluctuations in the heavy-fermion paramagnetic ladder UTe 2
W. Knafo, G. Knebel, P. Steffens, K. Kaneko, A. Rosuel, J.-P. Brison, J. Flouquet, D. Aoki, G. Lapertot, S. Raymond

Abstract
Inelastic-neutron-scattering measurements were performed on a single crystal of the heavy-fermion paramagnet UTe 2 above its superconducting temperature. We confirm the presence of antiferromagnetic fluctuations with the incommensurate wavevector 𝐤 1 = ( 0 , 0.57 , 0 ) \mathbf{k}_{1}=(0,0.57,0) . A quasielastic signal is found, whose momentum-transfer dependence is compatible with fluctuations of magnetic moments μ | 𝐚 \mu\parallel\mathbf{a} , with a sine-wave modulation of wavevector 𝐤 1 \mathbf{k}_{1} and in-phase moments on the nearest U atoms. Low dimensionality of the magnetic fluctuations, consequence of the ladder structure, is indicated by weak correlations along the direction 𝐜 \mathbf{c} . These fluctuations saturate below the temperature T 1 ∗ ≃ 15 T_{1}^{*}\simeq 15 K, in possible relation with anomalies observed in thermodynamic, electrical-transport and nuclear-magnetic-resonance measurements. The absence or weakness of ferromagnetic fluctuations, in our data collected at temperatures down to 2.1 K and energy transfers from 0.6 to 7.5 meV, is emphasized. These results constitute constraints for models of magnetically-mediated superconductivity in UTe 2 .

V
Fit to the energy scans by a quasielastic Lorentzian dynamical susceptibility

The complex magnetic susceptibility, expressed as:

\displaystyle\chi(\mathbf{k},E)=\chi^{\prime}(\mathbf{k},E)+i\chi^{\prime\prime}(\mathbf{k},E),

(S4)

where
\chi^{\prime}(\mathbf{k},E)
is its real part and
\chi^{\prime\prime}(\mathbf{k},E)
its imaginary part, is generally used to described magnetic fluctuations in the reciprocal space.

Knowing that the neutron intensity
I(\mathbf{Q},E)
is related to the imaginary part of the dynamical susceptibility
\chi^{\prime\prime}(\mathbf{Q},E)
by:

\displaystyle I(\mathbf{Q},E)-I_{BG}(E)\propto\frac{F_{m}(\mathbf{Q})^{2}}{1-\exp(-E/k_{B}T)}\chi^{\prime\prime}(\mathbf{Q},E),

(S5)

where
I_{BG}(E)
is the background, data shown in Fig. 4(a) of the paper were fitted assuming a quasielastic Lorentzian variation of
\chi^{\prime\prime}(\mathbf{Q}_{1},E)
:

\displaystyle\chi^{\prime\prime}(\mathbf{Q}_{1},E)=\chi^{\prime}(\mathbf{k}_{1})\frac{E/\Gamma(\mathbf{k}_{1})}{1+(E/\Gamma(\mathbf{k}_{1}))^{2}},

(S6)

where
\Gamma(\mathbf{k}_{1})
and
\chi^{\prime}(\mathbf{k}_{1})
are the relaxation rate and the real part of the static susceptibility, respectively, at the wavevector
\mathbf{k}_{1}
measured at the momentum transfer
\mathbf{Q}_{1}
.
We note that a quasielastic Lorentzian variation implies:

\displaystyle\chi(\mathbf{k},E)=\frac{\chi^{\prime}(\mathbf{k})}{1-iE/\Gamma(\mathbf{k})},

(S7)

and that the real part of static susceptibility is given by:

\displaystyle\chi^{\prime}(\mathbf{k})=\chi^{\prime}(\mathbf{k},E=0)=\frac{1}{\pi}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{\chi^{\prime\prime}(\mathbf{k})}{E}dE.

(S8)

For a
\mathbf{k}
-dependent quasielastic-Lorentzian magnetic susceptibility, the NMR spin-lattice relaxation rate
1/T_{1}T
is related to
\chi^{\prime}(\mathbf{k})
and
\Gamma(\mathbf{k})
by:

\displaystyle\frac{1}{T_{1}T}\propto\sum_{\mathbf{k}}|A(\mathbf{k})|^{2}\frac{\chi^{\prime}(\mathbf{k})}{\Gamma(\mathbf{k})},

(S9)

where
A(\mathbf{k})
is a
\mathbf{k}
-dependent hyperfine coupling. A strong
\mathbf{k}
dependence of the weighting factor
|A(\mathbf{k})|^{2}
can enhance the contribution at given wavevectors.
