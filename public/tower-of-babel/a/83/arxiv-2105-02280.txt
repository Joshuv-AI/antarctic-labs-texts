Groupoid semidirect product Fell bundles II — Principal actions and stabilization
Lucas Hall, S. Kaliszewski, John Quigg, Dana P. Williams

Abstract
. Given a free and proper action of a groupoid on a Fell bundle (over another groupoid), we give an equivalence between the semidirect-product and the generalized-fixed-point Fell bundles, generalizing an earlier result where the action was by a group. As an application, we show that the Stabilization Theorem for Fell bundles over groupoids is essentially another form of crossed-product duality.

5.
Semidirect-product actions

Give an action of
G
on
H
by isomorphisms, we want to consider how
to build actions of their semidirect product
S(H,G)
on a space
T

from actions of
H
and
G
on
T
. For motivation, consider the
case where
H
and
G
are groups acting on a space
T
and
S(H,G)

is the semidirect product of groups. The following notation may be
helpful. Let
\alpha:G\to\operatorname{Aut}H
be a homomorphism and let
L(x)
and

\pi(h)
be the homeomorphisms of
T
induced by
x\in G
and
h\in H

respectively. It is basic algebra to verify that we get an action of

S(H,G)
restricting to the given actions on
H
and
G
exactly when
the pair
(\pi,L)
is
covariant
in that the diagram

(5.1)

{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces T}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces T}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces T}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces T}
\scriptstyle{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces L(x)}
\scriptstyle{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\pi(h)}
\scriptstyle{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\pi(\alpha_{x}(h))}
\scriptstyle{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces L(x)}

commutes for all

and

. Alternatively,

(5.2)

\displaystyle L(x)\circ\pi(h)=\pi\bigl(\alpha_{x}(h)\bigr)\circ L(x),

which is a formula that is familiar to that
for covariant representations of dynamical
systems. (This is the motivation for our terminology below.)

Now we return to the case where
G
and
H
are groupoids. We let

\rho_{G}:T\to G^{(0)}
and
\rho_{H}:T\to H^{(0)}
be the moment maps for
the actions on
T
, and

\rho_{G}^{H}:H\to G^{(0)}
the moment map for the action of
G
on
H
by
isomorphisms. As above, we use notations

and
\pi(h)
for the
partially defined maps on
T
induced by

and
h\in H
, and

\alpha_{x}
for the isomorphism of

onto

. Then
we want these actions to satisfy a similar covariance condition

) even though both sides are not everywhere defined. Thus,
written in the usual notation for groupoid actions, we want

\displaystyle x\cdot(h\cdot t)=(x\cdot h)\cdot(x\cdot t)

to hold when both sides are defined. More precisely, let

(5.4)

\displaystyle L=\{\,(x,h,t):\text{$s_{H}(h)=\rho_{H}(t)$
and $s_{G}(x)=\rho_{G}(h\cdot t)$}\,\}

be the subset of

where the left-hand side of

\displaystyle R=\{\,(x,h,t):\text{$s_{G}(x)=\rho_{G}^{H}(h)$,
$s_{G}(x)=\rho_{G}(t)$,

be the subset where the right-hand side is defined.
Then we require that

,

) holds on this common set.

While this might seem overly difficult to establish,
the following observation greatly simplifies
checking the validity of these requirements.

Lemma 5.1

Suppose that the

- and

{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces T}

\scriptstyle{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\rho_{H}}
\scriptstyle{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\rho_{G}^{H}}

\displaystyle\rho_{H}(x\cdot t)=x\cdot\rho_{H}(t)\quad\text{if

Suppose that

On the other hand,

\displaystyle\rho_{H}(x\cdot t)=s_{H}(x\cdot h)=x\cdot s_{H}(h)=x\cdot\rho_{H}(t).

For the other direction, now assume

is

Assume

\displaystyle=\rho_{G}^{H}(r_{H}(h))=\rho_{G}^{H}(h).

(5.12)

\displaystyle=\rho_{G}^{H}\bigl(\rho_{H}(t)\bigr)=\rho_{G}(t).

Theorem 7.1

The

Start with a Fell bundle

and the moment map

for the first action
is a principal
G
-bundle for the second action.
Thus Theorem
7.1
applies,
and Theorem

below shows how
we recover the stabilization theorem

4
]

(
[
