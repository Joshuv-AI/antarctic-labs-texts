Abstract
. A vertex triple ( u , v , w ) (u,v,w) of a graph is called a 2 2 -geodesic if v v is adjacent to both u u and w w and u u is not adjacent to w w . A graph is said to be 2 2 -geodesic transitive if its automorphism group is transitive on the set of 2 2 -geodesics. In this paper, a complete classification of 2 2 -geodesic transitive graphs of order p n p^{n} is given for each prime p p and n ≤ 3 n\leq 3 . It turns out that all such graphs consist of three small graphs: the complete bipartite graph 𝖪 4 , 4 \mathsf{K}_{4,4} of order 8 8 , the Schläfli graph of order 27 27 and its complement, and fourteen infinite families: the cycles 𝐂 p , 𝐂 p 2 \mathbf{C}_{p},\mathbf{C}_{p^{2}} and 𝐂 p 3 \mathbf{C}_{p^{3}} , the complete graphs 𝖪 p , 𝖪 p 2 \mathsf{K}_{p},\mathsf{K}_{p^{2}} and 𝖪 p 3 \mathsf{K}_{p^{3}} , the complete multipartite graphs 𝖪 p ⁡ [ p ] \mathsf{K}_{p[p]} , 𝖪 p ⁡ [ p 2 ] \mathsf{K}_{p[p^{2}]} and 𝖪 p 2 ​ [ p ] \mathsf{K}_{p^{2}[p]} , the Hamming graph H ⁡ ( 2 , p ) H(2,p) and its complement, the Hamming graph H ⁡ ( 3 , p ) H(3,p) , and two infinite families of normal Cayley graphs on extraspecial group of order p 3 p^{3} and exponent p p .

Lemma 4.1

Let

Proof.
The necessity is well known. To prove the sufficiency, let

\mathrm{soc}(A)=L

be a minimal normal subgroup of

for a positive integer

and a simple group
T
. Set

LZ\unlhd A
and
A/LZ\cong\mathrm{PGL}(2,p)

\mathrm{soc}(\mathrm{PGL}(2,p))\cong\mathrm{PSL}(2,p)
. By
[
21
, P. 181, (6.10)]
,
\mathrm{GL}(2,p)^{\prime}=\mathrm{SL}(2,p)\leq\mathrm{GL}(2,p)
.

Suppose that
T
is a nonabelian simple group. Then
L\cap N=1
and
LZ\cap N=1
. It follows
N\cong N/(LZ\cap N)\cong NLZ/LZ\unlhd A/LZ\cong\mathrm{PSL}(2,p)
, and then
\mathrm{PSL}(2,p)\cong N\cong N/(N\cap L)\cong NL/L\unlhd A/L\cong\mathrm{GL}(2,p)
, which is impossible.

Thus,
T
is abelain, and
N=T^{n}
with
T\cong\mathbb{Z}_{q}
for a prime
q
. Suppose that
q\not=p
. Since
L
is
p
-group,
L\cap\mathrm{GL}(2,p)N=1
, and
\mathrm{GL}(2,p)N\cong\mathrm{GL}(2,p)N/(L\cap\mathrm{GL}(2,p)N)\cong N\mathrm{GL}(2,p)L/L=A/L\cong\mathrm{GL}(2,p)

N\mathrm{GL}(2,p)=\mathrm{GL}(2,p)

N\leq\mathrm{GL}(2,p)
and hence
N\leq Z
. On the other hand, since
N\unlhd A
and
L\unlhd A
,
N\cap L=1
implies that
N
commutes with
L
pointwise, contradicting the hypothesis. Thus,

and
N=\mathbb{Z}_{p}^{n}
.

If

, then

is non-trivial
p

. Thus,

and

.

To prove

is a minimal normal subgroup of

. Suppose that

such that

and

. Since
\mathrm{SL}(2,p)\leq\mathrm{GL}(2,p)
, we consider the conjugate action of
\mathrm{SL}(2,p)
on

.
By
[
39
, P. 393, (6.3)]
,
\mathrm{SL}(2,p)
has a unique involution, say
\beta
, and
\mathrm{SL}(2,p)
has an element of order
4
, say
\gamma
. Then
\gamma^{2}=\beta
. Clearly,
\beta\in Z
. By assumption,
\sigma\not=\sigma^{\beta}\in\langle\sigma\rangle
, and since
\beta

. Then the orbit set of

Lemma 4.2

3

\ell\geq 4
, and again by Eq. (
3

\mathrm{\Gamma}_{N}\cong\mathsf{K}_{p^{2}}
or
\overline{H(2,p)}

\mathrm{\Gamma}

p^{2}-1

Suppose
\mathrm{\Gamma}_{N}\cong\overline{H(2,p)}

3.3
,
\mathrm{Aut}(\mathrm{\Gamma}_{N})\cong\mathrm{Aut}(H(2,p))\cong(\mathrm{S}_{p}\times\mathrm{S}_{p})\rtimes\mathbb{Z}_{2}

and

|\mathrm{GL}(2,p):A_{1}|\leq p-1

Since

fixes
N
setwise,
A_{1}
is a subgroup of
\mathrm{Aut}(M)_{N}
, the block stabilizer of
N
in
\mathrm{Aut}(M)
. We may assume that
M=\mathbb{Z}_{p}^{3}=\langle a\rangle\times\langle b\rangle\times\langle c\rangle
and
N=\langle c\rangle

5.1
(3),
\mathrm{Aut}(M)_{N}

\mathrm{SL}(2,p)
, of which one has orbit set

Thus, each orbit of
A_{1}
on

is union of some orbits of
\mathrm{SL}(2,p)

Since
A_{1}
is transitive on
S

S

with length

, and hence

Case 2:

\mathrm{\Gamma}

. Thus,
\langle x,y\rangle=M

Suppose that

If

\langle y\rangle^{*}

, and by Eq. (

Let
