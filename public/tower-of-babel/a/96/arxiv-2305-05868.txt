Hadwiger’s Conjecture for some graphs with independence number two Journal: Discrete Mathematics
Qiang Zhou

Abstract
Let h ⁡ ( G ) h(G) denote the largest t t such that G G contains K t K_{t} as a minor, χ ⁡ ( G ) \chi(G) the chromatic number of G G respectively. In 1943, Hadwiger conjectured that h ⁡ ( G ) ≥ χ ⁡ ( G ) h(G)\geq\chi(G) for any graph G G . In this paper, we will prove Hadwiger’s Conjecture holds for H-free graphs with independence number two, where H H is any one of some given graphs.
