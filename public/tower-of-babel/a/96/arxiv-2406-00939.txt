Bounds on f f -Divergences between Distributions within Generalized Quasi- ε \varepsilon -Neighborhood
Xinchun Yu, Shuangqing Wei, Shao-Lun Huang, Xiao-Ping Zhang

Abstract
A general reverse Pinsker’s inequality is derived to give an upper bound on f f -divergences in terms of total variational distance when two distributions are close measured under our proposed generalized local information geometry framework. In addition, relationships between two f-divergences equipped with functions that are third order differentiable are established in terms of the lower and upper bounds of their ratio, when the underlying distributions are within a generalized quasi- ε \varepsilon -neighborhood.

\begin{split}+&\int_{h(\bm{x})<0}\left[f^{\prime}_{-}(1)(\frac{dP_{1}}{dP_{0}}-1)+R^{-}_{f}(\frac{dP_{1}}{dP_{0}},1)\right]dP_{0}\\

+&\left.\int_{h(\bm{x})<0}\left(f^{\prime}_{-}(1)h(\bm{x})+\frac{\varepsilon}{2}df_{-}^{\prime}(\theta_{2})h^{2}(\bm{x})\right)dP_{0}\right]\end{split}

There are
n

(112)

With given

in our framework.

(118)

VI
Conclusion

In this work, we proved new bounds on
f
-divergences under the framework of a local geometric setting. Our findings on the first order derivative permit us to divide these
f
-divergences into two types, which further leads to the reverse Pinsker’s inequality when two distributions are close. Our technique on bounding one divergence by another relies on both the domain of the ratio of the pdfs, specific
f
-divergences, as well as their second and third order derivatives.
