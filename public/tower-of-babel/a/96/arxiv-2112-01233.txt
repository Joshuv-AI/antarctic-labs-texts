Introduction

The asymptotic behavior of semigroups and their orbits has been a subject of an intense study for the last few decades, see e.g.
BoTo10
,
BaBoTo16
,
Hu93
.
In
SkSh82
,
LuPh88
,
ArBa88
the authors obtained necessary and sufficient conditions for strong stability of bounded semigroups. In particular, for the case of

\sigma(A)
, the spectrum of the generator, is contained in the open left-half plane
\{z\in\mathbb{C}:\Re(z)<0\}
the semigroup is strongly stable. Due to the Banach-Steinhaus Theorem, if the growth bound
\omega_{0}(T)=0
this stability cannot be uniform. However, due to the works

,
BaDu08
of Batty and Batty and Duyckaerts, we have the following theorem

Theorem 1

Let
T=\{T(t)\}_{t\geq 0}
be a bounded
C_{0}
-semigroup acting on a Banach space
X
and let

be its generator. Then
\|T(t)A^{-1}\|\to 0

if and only if
\sigma(A)\cap(i\mathbb{R})=\emptyset

The above means that for a bounded semigroup
T
for which

\sigma(A)\subset\{z\in\mathbb{C}:\Re(z)<0\},

(1)

the operator-valued function
T(t):\mathbb{R}^{+}\ni t\to\mathcal{L}(D(A),X)

With this being the case, we call the semigroup, after Batty and Duyckaerts, who defined it for the case of bounded semigroups

Moreover, the semi-uniform stability may occur even for unbounded semigroups (see

For the case of unbounded semigroups it was shown in

. On the other hand for an unbounded semigroup

\lim_{t\to+\infty}\frac{\|T(t)R_{\mu}\|}{\|T(t)\|}=0,\quad\textnormal{ for }\mu\not\in\sigma(A).

For bounded semigroups with
\omega_{0}(T)=0
, (

) clearly reduces to the semi-uniform stability. A question arises here about the necessary and sufficient conditions for the property (
2
) to hold for general semigroups. The condition

(\omega_{0}(T)+i\mathbb{R})\cap\sigma(A)=\emptyset

is not necessary for (

) to occur, as it is shown in Example

,

T_{n}(t)A_{n}^{-1}=\frac{in}{1-n^{4}}e^{tni}\begin{bmatrix}(n^{2}-1)e^{i\frac{t}{n}}&\textnormal{ }(n^{2}-1)n\sin\frac{t}{n}+ine^{-i\frac{t}{n}}\\
0&(n^{2}+1)e^{-i\frac{t}{n}}\end{bmatrix},

hence

\|T(t)A^{-1}\|=\sup_{n\geq 2}\|T_{n}(t)A_{n}^{-1}\|\sim 1,\quad t\geq 0.

Finally, it follows that

\frac{\left\|T(t)A^{-1}\right\|}{\|T(t)\|}\sim\frac{1}{t}\to 0,\quad t\to\infty.

Example 7

Consider
\widetilde{T}:\widetilde{H}_{1}\to\widetilde{H}_{1},
then

\|\widetilde{x}\|_{1}=\Big(\sum^{\infty}_{n=2}\Big|c_{n+1}-c_{n}\Big|^{2}+|c_{2}|^{2}\Big)^{\frac{1}{2}},\quad\widetilde{x}\in\widetilde{H}_{1}.

We will prove that, for this case

\frac{\|\widetilde{T}(t)\widetilde{R}_{\mu}\|}{\|\widetilde{T}(t)\|}\ \sim\frac{1}{\log(t)}.

(25)

In further considerations, we will use the following inequality

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{|c_{n}|^{2}}{n^{2}}\leq 4\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}|c_{n+1}-c_{n}|^{2},\quad\{c_{n}\}_{n=1}^{\infty}\subset\mathbb{C},

which is a special case of Hardy’s inequality:

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\Big(\frac{1}{n}\sum^{n}_{k=1}a_{k}\Big)^{p}\leq\Big(\frac{p}{p-1}\Big)^{p}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_{n}^{p},\quad a_{n}\geq 0,

for p = 2. To prove (
25
) we will estimate
\|\widetilde{T}(t)\widetilde{A}^{-1}\widetilde{x}\|^{2}_{1}
. It is given by

\displaystyle\|\widetilde{T}(t)\widetilde{A}^{-1}\widetilde{x}\|_{1}^{2}=\sum^{\infty}_{n=2}\Big|c_{n+1}\frac{e^{it\log(n+1)}}{i\log(n+1)}-c_{n}\frac{e^{it\log(n)}}{i\log(n)}\Big|^{2}+|c_{2}|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq 2\sum^{\infty}_{n=2}\Big|c_{n+1}\frac{e^{it\log(n+1)}}{i\log(n+1)}-c_{n+1}\frac{e^{it\log(n)}}{i\log(n)}\Big|^{2}+2\sum^{\infty}_{n=2}\Big|(c_{n+1}-c_{n})\frac{e^{it\log(n)}}{i\log(n)}\Big|^{2}+|c_{2}|^{2}.

The second and third elements of the right-hand side of the inequality are clearly bounded by
B\Big(\frac{t}{\log(t)}\Big)^{2}\|\widetilde{x}\|_{1}^{2}
and
C\Big(\frac{t}{\log(t)}\Big)^{2}\|\widetilde{x}\|_{1}^{2},

B,C>0
, for
t>e
. We only need to look at the first sum then.

\displaystyle\sum^{\infty}_{n=2}\Big|c_{n+1}\frac{e^{it\log(n+1)}}{i\log(n+1)}-c_{n+1}\frac{e^{it\log(n)}}{i\log(n)}\Big|^{2}

\displaystyle=\sum^{\infty}_{n=2}\Big|\frac{c_{n+1}}{n}\frac{n(e^{it\log(n)}\log(n+1)-e^{it\log(n)}\log(n))}{\log(n+1)\log(n)}

\displaystyle+\frac{c_{n+1}}{n}\frac{n(e^{it\log(n)}\log(n)-e^{it\log(n+1)}\log(n))}{\log(n+1)\log(n)}\Big|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq 2\sum^{\infty}_{n=2}\Big|\frac{c_{n+1}}{n}\frac{n(e^{it\log(n)}\log(n+1)-e^{it\log(n)}\log(n))}{\log(n+1)\log(n)}\Big|^{2}

\displaystyle+\Big|\frac{c_{n+1}}{n}\frac{n(e^{it\log(n)}\log(n)-e^{it\log(n+1)}\log(n))}{\log(n+1)\log(n)}\Big|^{2}

\displaystyle=2\sum^{\infty}_{n=2}\Big|\frac{c_{n+1}}{n}\frac{n\log(1+\frac{1}{n})}{\log(n+1)\log(n)}\Big|^{2}+2\sum^{\infty}_{n=2}\Big|\frac{c_{n+1}}{n}\frac{n(1-e^{it\log(1+\frac{1}{n})})}{\log(n+1)}\Big|^{2}.

The first of the above sums, due to Hardy’s inequality (see (
26
)), is bounded by

, and thus by

for
t>e
. We estimate the remaining sum by splitting it into two

\displaystyle\sum^{\infty}_{n=2}\Big|\frac{c_{n+1}}{n}\frac{n(1-e^{it\log(1+\frac{1}{n})})}{\log(n+1)}\Big|^{2}

\displaystyle=\sum_{2\leq n<t}\Big|\frac{c_{n+1}}{n}\frac{n(1-e^{it\log(1+\frac{1}{n})})}{\log(n+1)}\Big|^{2}+\sum_{n\geq t}\Big|\frac{c_{n+1}}{n}\frac{n(1-e^{it\log(1+\frac{1}{n})})}{\log(n+1)}\Big|^{2}

Where we have used the boundedness of
