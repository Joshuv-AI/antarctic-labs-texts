Jacobi algebra presentations for fundamental group algebras
Vivek Mistry

Abstract
. We prove a special case of a conjecture of Davison which pertains to superpotential descriptions of fundamental group algebras k ​ [ π 1 ​ ( X ) ] k[\pi_{1}(X)] . We consider the case in which the manifold X X is the mapping torus M g , φ M_{g,\varphi} of a genus g g Riemann surface Σ g \Sigma_{g} and a finite order automorphism φ \varphi , and the superpotential structure is given by the Jacobi algebra of a quiver with potential.



1.
Introduction

Given a
d
-dimensional manifold
X
we can define its fundamental group algebra over a field
k
as the group ring
k[\pi_{1}(X)].
In
[
14
]
it is stated that if
X
is compact, orientable and has a contractible universal cover then
k[\pi_{1}(X)]
is a Calabi-Yau algebra of dimension
d
(see [
[
14
]
Corollary 6.1.4] and [
[
9
]
Proposition 5.2.6]). Many Calabi-Yau algebras turn out to be what are known as superpotential algebras and so it was conjectured in
[
14
]
that in the dimension 3 case these fundamental group algebras which were Calabi-Yau were also superpotential algebras.

However in
[
9
]
Davison showed that this was not the case in general (for
d\geq 2
), and that in order to have a superpotential structure the algebra
k[\pi_{1}(X)]
(and hence the manifold
X
) had to have certain specific properties. In particular a superpotential structure implies a stronger notion of an
exact
Calabi-Yau structure which Davison showed required non-trivial central units in
k[\pi_{1}(X)]
. Therefore Davison proposed an updated conjecture [
[
9
]
Conjecture 7.1.1] regarding the possibility of a superpotential structure on
k[\pi_{1}(X)]
when
X
is a circle bundle, precisely because in such a manifold
X
non-trivial central units in
k[\pi_{1}(X)]
are easily found. In this paper we focus on the 3-dimensional case of this conjecture ie. when
X
is a Seifert fibre bundle.

The ultimate goal of such a superpotential description of
k[\pi_{1}(X)]
is in regards to calculating the Donaldson-Thomas (DT) invariants of
X
. Given a superpotential structure the DT invariants can then be though of in terms of vanishing cycles and Milnor fibres of a globally defined function on a smooth space which gives us a much stronger possibility of calculating them concretely. We remark on how this may be accomplished towards the end of the paper.

2.1.
Calabi-Yau algebras and Ginzburg differential graded algebras

The Calabi-Yau structure is essential for defining DT invariants and suggests a superpotential description so we give an overview here following
[

[

. The subsequent definition for Calabi-Yau algebras is due to Ginzburg.

Definition 2.2
.

An object

in the derived category

A
-modules.

Definition 2.3
.

An algebra

-module.

Definition 2.4
.

A
Calabi-Yau structure of dimension

on a homologically finite algebra

is an isomorphism in
D(A\textnormal{-{Bimod}})

f:A\xrightarrow{\,\,\sim\,\,}A^{\vee}[d]\quad\textnormal{such that}\quad f=f^{\vee}[d].

If
A
is homologically finite we have an isomorphism

\textnormal{HH}_{d}(A)\cong\textnormal{Ext}^{-d}_{A^{e}}(A^{\vee},A)=\textnormal{Hom}_{A^{e}}(A^{\vee}[d],A)

where
\textnormal{HH}_{d}(A)
is the
d
th Hochschild homology of

So an isomorphism
A\cong A^{\vee}[d]
corresponds to an element in
\textnormal{HH}_{d}(A)
(we call such elements
non-degenerate
), and the self-duality
f=f^{\vee}[d]
corresponds to the element being fixed by the induced map on homology of the flip isomorphism
\beta:A\otimes_{A^{e}}A\xrightarrow{\sim}A\otimes_{A^{e}}A
that swaps the copies of
A
in the tensor product. However it turns out that the map on Hochschild homology induced by

is just the identity (see [
[
22
]
Proposition C.1]) and so any non-degenerate element in
\textnormal{HH}_{d}(A)
or indeed any isomorphism

gives rise to a

-dimensional Calabi-Yau structure on
A
.

For an algebra
A
recall we have the following long exact sequence in cyclic and Hochschild homology

\cdots\rightarrow\textnormal{HC}_{n+1}(A)\rightarrow\textnormal{HC}_{n-1}(A)\xrightarrow{\partial}\textnormal{HH}_{n}(A)\rightarrow\textnormal{HC}_{n}(A)\rightarrow\cdots

Definition 2.5
.

An
exact Calabi-Yau structure of dimension

is a non-degenerate element

that is in the image of the boundary map

.
