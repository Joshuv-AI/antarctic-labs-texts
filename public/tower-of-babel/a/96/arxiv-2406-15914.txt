Three weak solutions for a ( p , q ) \displaystyle(p,q) -Schrödinger-Kirchhoff type equation
Ahmed AHMED *, Taghi AHMEDATT, Ahmed ABERQI, ahmedmath2001@gmail.com *, taghi-med@hotmail.fr, ahmed.aberqi@usmba.ac.ma

Abstract
In this manuscript, we investigate a ( p , q ) \displaystyle(p,q) -Schrödinger-Kirchhoff equation involving a continuous positive potential that meets the del Pino-Felmer type conditions. Using Recceri’s classical variational approach, we prove the existence of three weak solutions.



Key words:
Double phase problems, Recceri’s vartianonel principle, Nonlocal Schrödinger-Kirchhoff type equation.

AMS:
35Q55,35J60, 35D30, 35J20.
