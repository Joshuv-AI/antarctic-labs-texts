Superconducting Properties and μ \mu SR Study of the Noncentrosymmetric Superconductor Nb 0.5 Os 0.5
D. Singh, J. A. T. Barker, A. Thamizhavel, A. D. Hillier, D. McK. Paul, R. P. Singh

Abstract
The properties of the noncentrosymmetric superconductor ( α \alpha - Mn structure) Nb 0.5 Os 0.5 is investigated using resistivity, magnetization, specific heat, and muon spin relaxation and rotation ( μ \mu SR) measurements. These measurements suggest that Nb 0.5 Os 0.5 is a weakly coupled ( λ e − p ​ h \lambda_{e-ph} ∼ \sim 0.53) type-II superconductor ( κ G ​ L \kappa_{GL} ≈ \approx 61) having a bulk superconducting transition temperature T c T_{c} = 3.07 K. The specific heat data in the superconductive regime fits well with the single-gap BCS model indicating nodeless s-wave superconductivity in Nb 0.5 Os 0.5 . The μ \mu SR measurements also confirm s -wave superconductivity with the preserved time-reversal symmetry.
