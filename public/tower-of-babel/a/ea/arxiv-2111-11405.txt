Molecular interpretation of the LHCb pentaquarks from an analysis of J / ψ ​ p J/\psi p spectrum
\firstname Meng-Lin \lastname Du \fnsep

Abstract
A coupled-channel approach including the Λ c ​ D ¯ ( ∗ ) \Lambda_{c}\bar{D}^{(*)} and η c ​ p \eta_{c}p channels in addition to the Σ c ( ∗ ) ​ D ¯ ( ∗ ) \Sigma_{c}^{(*)}\bar{D}^{(*)} and J / ψ ​ p J/\psi p channels, as required by unitarity and heavy quark spin symmetry (HQSS) obtain cutoff independent results, the one-pion exchange potential in the multichannel systems is to be supplemented with next-leading order counter terms responsible for the S S -wave-to- D D -wave transitions. We show that the experimental data for the J / ψ ​ p J/\psi p mass distributions are fully in line with the Σ c ​ D ¯ \Sigma_{c}\bar{D} and Σ c ​ D ¯ ∗ \Sigma_{c}\bar{D}^{\ast} hadronic molecular interpretation of the P c ​ ( 4312 ) P_{c}(4312) and P c ​ ( 4440 ) / P c ​ ( 4457 ) P_{c}(4440)/P_{c}(4457) , respectively. A narrow Σ c ∗ ​ D ¯ \Sigma_{c}^{\ast}\bar{D} molecule around 4.38 GeV is required by the HQSS with the evidence for its existence seen in the J / ψ ​ p J/\psi p spectrum. Moreover, we predict the line shapes for the elastic and inelastic channels.



1
Introduction

The observation of the
P_{c}(4380)
and
P_{c}(4450)

LHCb:2015yax
, as the first well established signals of pentaquarks, in 2015 builds a landmark in searching for the so-called exotic states allowed by quantum chromodynamics (QCD), i.e., the color neutral objects composed rather than a pair of quark and antiquark (
q\bar{q}
) or three quarks (
qqq
).
These exotic structures are observed in the
J/\psi p
channel in
\Lambda_{b}^{0}\to J/\psi K^{-}p
decays, which implies that they contain at least five quarks,
c\bar{c}uud
. The
P_{c}(4450)
is confirmed by an updated analysis based on an combined data set collected in Run 1 and Run 2 by LHCb Collaboration in 2019
LHCb:2019kea
, and it is observed to consist of two narrow overlapping structures,
P_{c}(4440)
and
P_{c}(4457)
. Nevertheless, since the one-dimensional fits to the
m_{J/\psi p}
distributions alone cannot distinguish broad
P_{c}
structures from background varying slowly with
m_{J/\psi p}
, a verification of the broad
P_{c}(4380)
observed in Ref.
LHCb:2015yax
awaits an full amplitude analysis of the new data. At the same time, a new narrow pentaquark state
P_{c}(4312)
is discovered in the same channel
LHCb:2019kea
. The discovery of these pentaquark states immediately spurred many theoretical works and various interpretations on the nature of these pentaquark states were proposed, including hadronic molecules
Chen:2019bip
;
Chen:2019asm
;
Liu:2019tjn
;
Guo:2019kdc
;
Xiao:2019aya
;
Meng:2019ilv
;
Du:2019pij
;
Du:2021fmf
, compact pentaquark states
Cheng:2019obk
;
Ali:2019npk
;
Zhu:2019iwm
, hadrocharmonia
Eides:2019tgv
, and cups effects
Kuang:2020bnk
;
Nakamura:2021qvy
. An amplitude analysis focusing on the
P_{c}(4312)
peak suggests the virtual state origin of the
P_{c}(4312)
in Ref.
Fernandez-Ramirez:2019koa
. Among these explanations, the molecular picture is of particular interest since all of the three narrow pentaquark states can be simultaneously interpreted as
\Sigma_{c}\bar{D}^{(*)}
bound states with help of heavy quark spin symmetry (HQSS). The charmonium pentaquark with strangeness was predicted in Refs.
Wu:2010jy
;
Chen:2016ryt
;
Xiao:2019gjd
;
Wang:2019nvm
and its signal was first observed by LHCb Collaboration in 2020
LHCb:2020jpq
, i.e.,
P_{cs}(4459)
. Its observation has promoted numerous studies of this resonance
Peng:2020hql
;
Chen:2020uif
;
Lu:2021irg
;
Du:2021bgb
. These studies typically favor a molecular picture with a dominant component of
\Xi_{c}\bar{D}^{*}
.

The proximity of the
\Sigma_{c}\bar{D}^{(*)}
thresholds to these narrow
P_{c}
suggests an important role played by the corresponding two-hadron states in the dynamics of the
P_{c}
states. In the common molecular picture, the
P_{c}(4312)
is regarded as an
S
-wave
\Sigma_{c}\bar{D}
bound state, while the
P_{c}(4440)
and
P_{c}(4457)
are bound states of
\Sigma_{c}\bar{D}^{*}
with different spin structures. In order to describe the measured
J/\psi p
mass distributions, the inelastic channels need to be considered to account for the width of
P_{c}
’s. In addition to the
J/\psi p
channel, there are more inelastic channels, most prominently
\Lambda_{c}\bar{D}^{(*)}
and
\eta_{c}p
. The latter is connected to the
J/\psi p
channel via HQSS. When the
\Lambda_{c}\bar{D}^{(*)}
channel is included explicitly, the three-body cut
\Lambda\bar{D}^{\ast}\pi
effects need to be taken into account as the width of
\Sigma_{c}^{(*)}
is almost saturated by the decay
\Sigma_{c}^{(*)}\to\Lambda_{c}\pi
. In this talk, we will include all the elastic channels
\Sigma_{c}^{(*)}\bar{D}^{(*)}
and inelastic channels,
J/\psi p
,
\eta_{c}p
and
\Lambda_{c}\bar{D}^{(*)}
, explicitly, and investigate the role played by the
\Lambda_{c}\bar{D}^{(*)}
and the nonperturbative dynamical pions as the proximity of the
P_{c}
states to the
\Lambda_{c}\bar{D}^{(*)}\pi
thresholds. For the case of only short-distance contact potentials, two solutions were found describing the data almost equally well
Du:2019pij
. The most prominent contribution from the one-pion exchange (OPE) originates from the
D
-waves, especially the
S
-wave-to-
D
-wave transitions
Baru:2016iwj
;
Baru:2017gwo
;
Wang:2018jlv
. Renormalization of the OPE in the energy region of interest requires an inclusion of higher order contact potentials
Baru:2017gwo
;
Wang:2018jlv
. When the OPE potentials are included together with the necessary next-leading order (NLO)
S
-
D
counter terms, only one of the two solutions present in purely contact potentials survives under the requirement of the renormalizability (regulator-independent), which suggests that the quantum numbers of the
P_{c}(4440)
and
P_{c}(4457)
should be
J^{P}=\frac{3}{2}^{-}
and
\frac{1}{2}^{-}
, respectively. We also show that there is evidence for the existence of a narrow
\Sigma_{c}^{*}\bar{D}
bound state, required by the HQSS, in the
m_{J/\psi p}
mass distribution around 4.38 GeV. The width of this
P_{c}(4380)
is of the same order of the three narrow
P_{c}
states as a consequence of HQSS and thus different from the broad one reported by LHCb in 2015
LHCb:2015yax
.

2
Framework

2.1
Interactions

The effective interactions contain both short-range contact potentials and long-range OPE. The leading-order (LO) effective Lagrangian for the
\Sigma_{c}^{(*)}\bar{D}^{(*)}
and
\Lambda_{c}\bar{D}^{(*)}
interactions respecting HQSS reads

\displaystyle\mathcal{L}_{\text{LO}}

\displaystyle\!\!\!\!=

\displaystyle\!\!\!\!-C_{a}S^{i{\dagger}}_{ab}S_{ba}^{i}\langle\bar{H}_{c}^{\dagger}\bar{H}_{c}\rangle-C_{b}i\epsilon_{jik}S_{ab}^{j{\dagger}}S_{ba}^{k}\langle\bar{H}_{c}^{\dagger}\sigma^{i}\bar{H}_{c}\rangle

(1)

\displaystyle\!\!\!\!+C_{c}\Big(S_{ab}^{i{\dagger}}T_{ca}\langle\bar{H}_{c}^{\dagger}\sigma^{i}\bar{H}_{b}\rangle-T^{\dagger}_{ca}S_{ab}^{i}\langle\bar{H}^{\dagger}_{b}\sigma^{i}\bar{H}_{c}\rangle\Big)-C_{d}T^{\dagger}_{ab}T_{ba}\langle\bar{H}_{c}^{\dagger}\bar{H}_{c}\rangle,

where
\langle\ldots\rangle
represents the trace in the spinor space and
\sigma
denotes the Pauli matrices. The subindices
a
,
b
,
c
are the flavor indices, and the superindices
i
,
j
,
k
denote the polarization. The heavy quark spin doublets for the ground states
(\Sigma_{c},\Sigma_{c}^{*})
,
(\bar{D},\bar{D}^{*})
, and
\Lambda_{c}^{+}
are grouped in
S^{i}
,
\bar{H}
, and
T
, respectively,
Manohar:2000dt

\displaystyle S^{i}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\sigma^{i}\Sigma_{c}+\Sigma^{*i}_{c},\qquad\bar{H}=-\bar{D}+\sigma\cdot\bar{D}^{*},

(2)

with

\displaystyle S_{c}^{(*)}=\begin{pmatrix}\Sigma_{c}^{(*)++}&\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\Sigma_{c}^{(*)+}\\
\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\Sigma_{c}^{(*)+}&\Sigma_{c}^{(*)0}\\
\end{pmatrix},\quad\quad\bar{D}^{(*)}=\begin{pmatrix}\bar{D}^{(*)0}\\
D^{(*)-}\\
\end{pmatrix},\quad T=\begin{pmatrix}0&\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\Lambda_{c}^{+}\\
-\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\Lambda_{c}^{+}&0\\
\end{pmatrix}.

(3)

The transitions between the
\Sigma_{c}^{(*)}\bar{D}^{(*)}
and
J/\psi p
(
\eta_{c}p
) can be described by
Sakai:2019qph
:

\displaystyle\mathcal{L}_{\text{ine}}=\frac{g_{S}}{\sqrt{3}}N^{\dagger}\sigma^{i}\bar{H}J^{\dagger}S^{i}-\sqrt{3}g_{D}N^{\dagger}\sigma^{i}\bar{H}(\partial^{i}\partial^{j}-\frac{1}{3}\delta^{ij}\partial^{2})J^{\dagger}S^{j},

(4)

where
N
denotes the nucleon doublet, and
J=-\eta_{c}+\boldsymbol{\sigma}\cdot\boldsymbol{\psi}
collects the ground state charmonium fields. The LO contact potentials can be derived from a more intuitive way by decomposing the spin structures into the heavy and light degrees of freedom, see e.g. in Refs.
Du:2019pij
;
Du:2021fmf
.

The LO OPE potential can be obtained by the effective Lagrangian for the axial coupling of pions to charmed mesons and baryons
Yan:1992gz
,

\displaystyle\mathcal{L}=\frac{g_{1}}{4}\langle\boldsymbol{\sigma}\cdot\boldsymbol{u}_{ab}\bar{H}_{b}\bar{H}_{a}^{\dagger}\rangle+ig_{2}\epsilon_{ijk}S^{i{\dagger}}_{ab}u^{j}_{bc}S^{k}_{ca}-\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}g_{3}\big(S_{ab}^{i{\dagger}}u_{bc}^{i}T_{ca}+T^{\dagger}_{ab}u_{bc}^{i}S^{i}_{ca}\big),

(5)

where
\boldsymbol{u}=-\nabla\Phi/F_{\pi}
with
\Phi=\boldsymbol{\tau}\cdot\boldsymbol{\pi}
. Here
F_{\pi}=92.1
MeV is the pion decay constant and
g_{1}=0.57
is determined from the width of
D^{*+}\to D^{0}\pi^{+}
,
g_{2}=0.42
and
g_{3}=0.71
are taken from a lattice QCD calculation
Detmold:2012ge
.

2.2
Lippmann-Schwinger equations

In order to describe the
\Lambda_{b}^{0}\to J/\psi pK^{-}
decay, we need the bare weak production vertices as well. In this work, we only consider the
S
-wave
\Sigma_{c}^{(*)}\bar{D}^{(*)}
source for the
\Lambda_{b}^{0}\to\Sigma_{c}^{(*)}\bar{D}^{(*)}K^{-}
process since the
\Sigma_{c}^{(*)}\bar{D}^{(*)}
thresholds are close to the energy of interest. We introduce seven parameters for the all seven
S
-wave
\Sigma_{c}^{(*)}\bar{D}^{(*)}
sources
Du:2019pij
;
Du:2021fmf
.

With the above ingredients, one can obtain the production amplitude satisfying unitarity by solving the Lippmann-Schwinger equations (LSEs), with a diagrammatic representation shown in Fig.
1
,

\displaystyle U^{J}_{\alpha}(E,p)

\displaystyle\!\!\!\!=

\displaystyle\!\!\!\!P^{J}_{\alpha}{-}\!\sum_{\beta}\!\!\int\!\!\frac{d^{3}\boldsymbol{q}}{(2\pi)^{3}}V^{J}_{\alpha\beta}(E,p,q)G_{\beta}(E,q)U^{J}_{\beta}(E,q),

\displaystyle U^{(\prime)J}_{i}(E,k)

\displaystyle\!\!\!\!=

\displaystyle\!\!\!\!{-}\!\sum_{\beta}\!\!\int\!\!\frac{d^{3}\boldsymbol{q}}{(2\pi)^{3}}{V}^{(\prime)J}_{i\beta}(k)G_{\beta}(E,q)U^{J}_{\beta}(E,q),

(6)

with
U_{\alpha}^{J}
and
P_{\alpha}
denote the production amplitude and the source for the
\alpha^{\text{th}}
elastic channel (
\Lambda_{c}\bar{D}^{(*)}
channels as well when they are included) and
U_{i}^{(\prime)J}
for the
i^{\text{th}}
inelastic channel
J/\psi p
(
\eta_{c}p
). The direct
J/\psi p
and
\eta_{c}p
interactions are neglected since they are Okubo-Zweig-Iizuka suppressed and were found very weak in a recent lattice QCD calculation
Skerbis:2018lew
and a coupled-channel model in Ref.
Du:2020bqj
. In addition, we assume that the effect from the interactions of the
\Lambda_{c}\bar{D}^{(*)}
with
J/\psi p
and
\eta_{c}p
can be neglected.

Figure 1:
Diagram for the
\Lambda_{b}^{0}\to J/\psi pK^{-}
through the
\Lambda_{b}^{0}\to\Sigma_{c}^{(*)}\bar{D}^{(*)}K^{-}
.

To take into account the widths of
\Sigma_{c}^{(*)}
which are comparable to those of the
P_{c}
states, the two-body propagator is taken as the following form

\displaystyle G_{\beta}(E,\boldsymbol{q})=\frac{m_{\Sigma_{c}^{(*)}}m_{D^{(*)}}}{E_{\Sigma_{c}^{(*)}}(\boldsymbol{q})E_{D^{(*)}}(\boldsymbol{q})}\frac{1}{E_{\Sigma_{c}^{(*)}}(\boldsymbol{q})+E_{D^{(*)}}(\boldsymbol{q})-E-{\tilde{\Sigma}_{R}(s,m_{\Sigma_{c}^{(*)}})}/{(2E_{\Sigma_{c}^{(*)}}(\boldsymbol{q})})},

(7)

with
s=\big(E-E_{D^{(*)}}(\boldsymbol{q})\big)^{2}-\boldsymbol{q}^{2}
for the off-shell
\Sigma_{c}^{(*)}
and
\tilde{\Sigma}_{R}(s,m_{\Sigma_{c}^{(*)}})=ig^{2}{\lambda^{3/2}(s,m_{\Lambda_{c}}^{2},m_{\pi}^{2})}/{(8s^{5/2})}
the renormalized self-energy loop diagram. The effect of the
\bar{D}^{(*)}
width is neglected as it is only several tens of keV. To render the integrals in the LSEs in Eq. (
2.2
) well defined we introduce a regulator, with a hard cutoff
\Lambda
larger than all typical three-momenta scales to the relevant degrees of freedom.

3
Fit results

To fit the
J/\psi p
invariant mass distribution, a smooth incoherent background is exploited to model the contributions from misidentified non-
\Lambda_{b}^{0}
events, crossed-channel effects and possibly additional broad
P_{c}^{+}
structures
Du:2021fmf
. First we perform fits employing only the contact potentials, referred to as Scheme I. In this circumstance, it is found that the data can be well described without including the
\Lambda_{c}\bar{D}^{(*)}
channels and the inclusion of these channels does not improve the fit quality. As in Ref.
Du:2019pij
, two solutions, denoted as
A
and
B
, are found describing the data almost equally well. Both solutions give seven poles for the
\Sigma_{c}^{(*)}\bar{D}^{(*)}
scattering amplitudes, i.e., seven
P_{c}
states. In both solutions, the
P_{c}(4312)
corresponds to a
\Sigma_{c}\bar{D}
bound state with
J^{P}=\frac{1}{2}^{-}
. The
P_{c}(4440)
and
P_{c}(4457)
are bounds states of
\Sigma_{c}\bar{D}^{*}
, with quantum numbers
\frac{1}{2}^{-}
and
\frac{3}{2}^{-}
, respectively, in solution
A
and however interchanged in solution
B
. Three
P_{c}
states dominated by the
\Sigma_{c}^{*}\bar{D}^{*}
are found in two solutions with
m_{\frac{1}{2}^{-}}<m_{\frac{3}{2}^{-}}<m_{\frac{5}{2}^{-}}
for solution
A
and the opposite mass pattern for
B
. Those states are invisible in the
m_{J/\psi p}
mass distributions and were suggested to search for in a prompt production at the center-of-mass energy 7 TeV in the
pp
collision
Ling:2021sld
. In particular, in both solutions there is a narrow pole around 4.38 GeV corresponding to a
\Sigma_{c}^{*}\bar{D}
bound state with
J^{P}=\frac{3}{2}^{-}
. It is a consequence of the HQSS and different to the broad resonance reported by LHCb in 2015
LHCb:2015yax
.

Du:2021fmf

-

A

B

B

\Lambda>1

A

J/\psi p

P_{c}(4440)

A
and only focus on the results of solution
B
