Proposition 2.4
.

(g|W)\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix}1&0\\

d^{2}&ad-bc-bd\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)\in SL(2,\mathbb{F}_{q}).
Set

Then

\frac{ac}{e},\frac{a^{2}}{e},\frac{c^{2}}{e},\frac{b^{2}}{e},\frac{d^{2}}{e},\frac{bd}{e}\in\mathbb{F}_{q}.

If
b=0
