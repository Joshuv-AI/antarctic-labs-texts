P c P_{c} Resonances in the Compact Pentaquark Picture
W. Ruangyoo, K. Phumphan, C. C. Chen, A. Limphirat, Y. Yan

Abstract
P c P_{c} resonances are studied in the approach of quark model and group theory. It is found that there are totally 17 possible pentaquark states with the quark contents q 3 ​ Q ​ Q ¯ q^{3}Q\bar{Q} ( q q are u u and d d quarks; Q Q is c c quark) in the compact pentaquark picture, where the hidden heavy pentaquark states may take the color singlet-singlet ( [ 111 ] q ​ q ​ q ⊗ [ 111 ] c ​ c ¯ [111]_{{qqq}}\otimes[111]_{{c\bar{c}}} ) and color octet-octet ( [ 21 ] q ​ q ​ q ⊗ [ 21 ] c ​ c ¯ [21]_{{qqq}}\otimes[21]_{{c\bar{c}}} ) configurations. The partial decay widths of hidden heavy pentaquark states are calculated for all possible decay channels. The results show that the p ​ J / ψ pJ/\psi is the dominant decay channel for both the spin 3 / 2 3/2 and 1 / 2 1/2 pentaquark states, and indicate that the P c ​ ( 4440 ) P_{c}(4440) may not be a compact pentaquark state while P c ​ ( 4312 ) P_{c}(4312) and P c ​ ( 4457 ) P_{c}(4457) could be the spin- 1 2 \frac{1}{2} and spin- 3 2 \frac{3}{2} pentaquark states, respectively.

In 2015, the two pentaquark-like states,
P_{c}(4380)

LHCb1

P_{c}(4312)
,
P_{c}(4440)
and
P_{c}(4457)

LHCb3

9.8\pm 2.7^{+3.7}_{-4.5}
MeV. The

P_{c}(4440)

20.6\pm 4.9^{+8.7}_{-10.1}
MeV and
6.4\pm 2.0^{+5.7}_{-1.9}

P_{c}

J^{P}=\frac{1}{2}^{-}
,

, and

are only suggested for the
P_{c}(4312)
,
P_{c}(4440)
and
P_{c}(4457)
states, respectively. Moreover, the LHCb has proposed
a structure of the bound state of a baryon and a meson for the
P_{c}
states stemming from that the masses of
P_{c}(4312)
and

II
P_{c}
wave functions in compact pentaquark picture

The construction of the
P_{c}
pentaquark states follows the rules that the color wave function of
P_{c}
must be singlet, and the total
P_{c}
wave functions should be antisymmetric under any permutation of the three light quarks. The color wave function of the
P_{c}
states is required to be a
{[222]_{1}}
singlet, and the total wave function for the ground
P_{c}
pentaquark states is

\Psi(q^{3}c\bar{c})=\psi^{O}_{sym}\psi^{C}_{[222]}\psi^{SF},

(1)

where
\psi^{O}
,
\psi^{C}
and
\psi^{SF}
are the spatial, color and spin-flavor wave functions, respectively. The spatial wave function,
\psi^{O}
, is fully symmetric for the ground state.

The
P_{c}
wave function in this work can be considered in two parts,
q^{3}
and
c\bar{c}
. For
q^{3}
part, the permutation symmetry of the
q^{3}
part is characterized by the
S_{3}
young tabloids [3], [21], [111]. The color part of
q^{3}
can be both the [111] and [21] configurations while the color part of
c\bar{c}
are also [111] and [21], so the color singlet wave functions of
P_{c}
states are either in a color singlet-singlet or a color octet-octet configuration, taking the form,

\psi^{C}_{[222]}=\left\{\begin{array}[]{l}\psi^{C}_{[111]}(q^{3})\otimes\psi^{C}_{[111]}(c\bar{c})\\
\psi^{C}_{[21]}(q^{3})\otimes\psi^{C}_{[21]}(c\bar{c})\end{array}.\right.

(2)

In the color octet-octet configuration, the color wave function is composed of eight possible octet states to be in the color singlet, taking the form,

\psi^{C}_{[222]}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{8}}\sum^{8}_{i=1}\psi^{C}_{[21]_{i},q^{3}}\otimes\psi^{C}_{[21]_{i},c\bar{c}}.

(3)

The
q^{3}
part is required to be fully antisymmetric. For the color singlet-singlet configuration, the general wave function of the
q^{3}
part is

\Psi_{[A]}^{singlet}(q^{3})=\psi_{[111]}^{C}\psi^{O}_{Sym}\psi_{[3]}^{SF}.

(4)

The spin-flavor part of the
q^{3}
cluster is in the [21] configuration for the color octet-octet configuration, and thus the
q^{3}
wave function is in the form,

\Psi_{[A]}^{octet}(q^{3})=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\psi^{O}_{Sym}(\psi^{C}_{[21]_{\lambda}}\psi^{SF}_{[21]_{\rho}}-\psi^{C}_{[21]_{\rho}}\psi^{SF}_{[21]_{\lambda}}).

(5)

The
\lambda
and
\rho
color wave functions are listed in Appendix
A
. The possible spin-flavor configurations of the
q^{3}
cluster, listed in Table

Yan:2011

Sorakrai2012

Kai2019PRC
;
Kai2020PRD
.

IV
DISCUSSION

We have calculated the partial decay widths for all the 17 compact pentaquark states since the quantum numbers of the three LHCb
P_{c}
resonances have not been determined yet. The results in Table
4
for the nine
I=1/2
pentaquark states show that the
pJ/\psi
channel is open for only two states with the configuration
\psi_{[111]_{C}[21]_{F}[21]_{S}[\chi_{1}]}
and spin
\frac{3}{2}
and
\frac{1}{2}
. If there is no mixing among the
I=\frac{1}{2}
and
J=\frac{3}{2}
states as well as among the
I=\frac{1}{2}
and
J=\frac{1}{2}
states, there are only two compact charmonium-like pentaquark states which decay through the
pJ/\psi
channel. Therefore, one may accommodate only two of the three observed
P_{c}
in the compact pentaquark picture. Our results show that the
pJ/\psi
partial decay widths of
P_{c}(4312)
,
P_{c}(4440)
and
P_{c}(4457)
in the configuration
\psi_{[111]_{C}[21]_{F}[21]_{S}[\chi_{1}]}
are almost the same due to the mass closeness, that is

\displaystyle\frac{P_{c}(4312)\rightarrow pJ/\psi}{P_{c}(4457)\rightarrow pJ/\psi}

\displaystyle=

P_{c}(4440)

The branching ratios of the dominant channels are the key to reveal the nature of these charmonium-like pentaquark states. In addition to the
pJ/\psi

p\eta_{c}
channel should be targeted to search for
P_{c}
