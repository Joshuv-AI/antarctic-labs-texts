Fermions in the 6D Standing Wave Braneworld with Real Scalar Field
Pavle Midodashvili

Abstract
In the article, we investigate localization problem for spinor fields within the 6D standing wave braneworld with the bulk real scalar field, introduced earlier in [ 30 ] , and explicitly show that there is no normalizable fermion field zero mode trapped on the brane.
