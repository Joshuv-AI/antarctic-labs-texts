A stability result for almost perfect matchings
Mingyang Guo, Hongliang Lu

Abstract
Let n , k , s n,k,s be three integers and β \beta be a sufficiently small positive number such that k ≥ 3 k\geq 3 , 0 < 1 / n ≪ β ≪ 1 / k 0<1/n\ll\beta\ll 1/k and k ​ s + k ≤ n ≤ ( 1 + β ) ​ k ​ s + k − 2 ks+k\leq n\leq(1+\beta)ks+k-2 . A k k -graph is called non-trivial if it has no isolated vertex. In this paper, we determine the maximum number of edges in a non-trivial k k -graph with n n vertices and matching number at most s s . This result confirms a conjecture proposed by Frankl (On non-trivial families without a perfect matching, European J. Combin. , 84 (2020), 103044) for the case when s s is sufficiently large.

Let

Corollary 1.4

For any integers

be two integers such that

Proof.
By Theorem
1.3
, there exists a positive integer
s_{0}^{\prime}
such that
|\mathcal{F}|\leq\max_{i\in\{0,1\}}\{|\mathcal{E}_{i}(n,k,s)|\}

|\mathcal{E}_{0}(n,k,s)|=\binom{ks+k-2}{k}+\binom{ks+k-2}{k-1}-\binom{ks-1}{k-1}+\ell+1

For

such that for

\begin{split}|\mathcal{E}_{0}(n,k,s)|-|\mathcal{E}_{1}(n,k,s)|&=\binom{ks+k-2}{k-1}-\binom{ks-1}{k-1}+\ell+1-\left(\ell+2\right)\binom{ks+k-3}{k-2}\\
&>(k-1)\binom{ks-1}{k-2}-\left(\ell+2\right)\binom{ks+k-3}{k-2}\\
&=\binom{ks-1}{k-2}-\left((\ell+2)\binom{ks+k-3}{k-2}-(k-2)\binom{ks-1}{k-2}\right)\\
&\geq\binom{ks-1}{k-2}-(k-2)\left(\binom{ks+k-3}{k-2}-\binom{ks-1}{k-2}\right)\\
&>\binom{ks-1}{k-2}-(k-2)^{2}\binom{ks+k-3}{k-3}\\
&>0.\end{split}

For

\begin{split}&|\mathcal{E}_{1}(n,k,s)|-|\mathcal{E}_{0}(n,k,s)|\\
=&(\ell+2)\binom{ks+k-3}{k-2}-\left(\binom{ks+k-2}{k-1}-\binom{ks-1}{k-1}+\ell+1\right)\\

\geq&0\end{split}

holds for all

and

&|\mathcal{E}_{1}(n,k,s)|,\ \text{if}\ k\leq\ell+3.\end{aligned}\right.

for

2
shifting

Let
\mathcal{F}
be a
k
-graph on vertex set
[n]
. For vertices
1\leq x<y\leq n
, we define the
(x,y)
-shift

S_{x,y}
by
S_{x,y}(\mathcal{F})=\{S_{x,y}(F):F\in\mathcal{F}\}
, where

S_{x,y}(F)=\begin{cases}F\setminus\{y\}\cup\{x\},&\text{if $y\in F$, $x\notin F$ and $F\setminus\{y\}\cup\{x\}\notin\mathcal{F}$;}\\
F,&\text{otherwise.}\end{cases}

The following well-known result can be found in
[
6
]
.

Lemma 2.1
.

The
(x,y)
-shift satisfies the following properties.

(
i
)

|\mathcal{F}|=|S_{x,y}(\mathcal{F})|
and
|F|=|S_{x,y}(F)|
for any
F\in\mathcal{F}
,

(
ii
)

\nu(S_{x,y}(\mathcal{F}))\leq\nu(\mathcal{F})
.

For a
k
-graph
\mathcal{F}
, define
N_{\mathcal{F}}(x)=\{F\setminus\{x\}:x\in F\in\mathcal{F}\}
,
N_{\mathcal{F}}(x,y)=\{F\setminus\{x,y\}:x,y\in F\in\mathcal{F}\}
and
N_{\mathcal{F}}(x,\overline{y})=\{F\setminus\{x\}:x\in F\in\mathcal{F},y\notin F\}
.

Proposition 2.2

(Frankl
[
13
]
)
.

Suppose that
\mathcal{F}\subseteq\binom{[n]}{k}
, then the following hold.

(
i
)

If
|N_{\mathcal{F}}(x)|\geq|N_{\mathcal{F}}(y)|
and
\widetilde{\mathcal{F}}:=S_{x,y}(\mathcal{F})\neq\mathcal{F}
, then

\sum_{i\in[n]}|N_{\widetilde{\mathcal{F}}}(i)|^{2}>\sum_{i\in[n]}|N_{\mathcal{F}}(i)|^{2}.

(2)

(
ii
)

If
\mathcal{F}
is non-trivial and
\widetilde{\mathcal{F}}=S_{x,y}(\mathcal{F})
has isolated vertex, then
|N_{\mathcal{F}}(x,y)|=\emptyset
and
|N_{\mathcal{F}}(x,\overline{y})|+|N_{\mathcal{F}}(y,\overline{x})|\leq\binom{n-2}{k-1}
.

Let us say that the
(x,y)
-shift
S_{x,y}
is
meaningful
if (
2
) holds. Noting the obvious upper bound

\sum_{x\in[n]}|N_{\mathcal{F}}(x)|^{2}\leq n|\mathcal{F}|^{2},

one concludes that there can be only a
limited
number of successive meaningful shifts. For a subset
Y\subset[n]
, we say that
\mathcal{F}\subseteq\binom{[n]}{k}
is
shifted
on
Y
if
S_{i,j}(\mathcal{F})=\mathcal{F}
holds for all
1\leq i<j\leq n
,
\{i,j\}\subset Y
. It is not difficult to see that if
\mathcal{F}
is shifted on
Y
, then for any subsets
\{u_{1},\ldots,u_{k}\},\{v_{1},\ldots,v_{k}\}\subset Y
such that
u_{i}\leq v_{i}
for
i\in[k]
,
\{v_{1},\ldots,v_{k}\}\in\mathcal{F}
implies
\{u_{1},\ldots,u_{k}\}\in\mathcal{F}
. Let
\omega(\mathcal{F})
be the number of vertices in the largest complete subgraph of
\mathcal{F}
.

Lemma 2.3
.

For any integer
k\geq 3
and positive real
\varepsilon<1/k
, there exists
s_{0}(k,\varepsilon)
such that the following holds. Let
0<\eta<\frac{\varepsilon^{k}}{3^{k+2}k!}
and
n,s
be positive integers such that
s>s_{0}
and
(1-\eta)ks\leq n\leq(1+\eta)ks+k-2
. Suppose that
\mathcal{F}\subseteq\binom{[n]}{k}
and
|\mathcal{F}|>\binom{ks+k-2}{k}-3\eta(ks)^{k}
. If
\mathcal{F}
is shifted on
[n]
, then
\omega(\mathcal{F})\geq(1-\varepsilon)ks
.

Proof.
Suppose that
\omega(\mathcal{F})<(1-\varepsilon)ks
. Let
S=[n]\setminus[(1-\varepsilon)ks]
, then
\mathcal{F}[S]=\emptyset
since
\mathcal{F}
is shifted on
[n]
. Thus
|\mathcal{F}|<\binom{n}{k}-\binom{|S|}{k}
. Note that
|S|\geq n-(1-\varepsilon)ks>(\varepsilon-\eta)ks\geq\varepsilon ks/2
. Combining with
|\mathcal{F}|>\binom{ks+k-2}{k}-3\eta(ks)^{k}
, we have
\binom{n}{k}-\binom{|S|}{k}>\binom{ks+k-2}{k}-3\eta(ks)^{k}
. That is

\binom{n}{k}-\binom{ks+k-2}{k}+3\eta(ks)^{k}>\binom{|S|}{k}.

(3)

Since

\begin{split}\binom{n}{k}-\binom{ks+k-2}{k}\leq&\binom{(1+\eta)ks+k-2}{k}-\binom{ks+k-2}{k}\\
\leq&\eta ks\binom{(1+\eta)ks+k-2}{k-1}\\
\leq&\eta ks\binom{(1+2\eta)ks}{k-1}\\
\leq&\frac{(1+2\eta)^{k-1}\eta(ks)^{k}}{(k-1)!}\\
\leq&\frac{2^{k-1}\eta(ks)^{k}}{(k-1)!}\end{split}

and

\binom{|S|}{k}\geq\frac{(\varepsilon ks/2-k)^{k}}{k!}\geq\frac{\varepsilon^{k}(ks)^{k}}{3^{k}k!},

inequality (
3
) implies

\frac{\varepsilon^{k}}{3^{k}k!}\leq\frac{2^{k-1}\eta}{(k-1)!}+3\eta\leq 5\eta,

a contradiction.
∎

\mathcal{F}
and rename the elements such that
|N_{\mathcal{F}}(1)|\geq\cdots\geq|N_{\mathcal{F}}(n)|
.
In view of Proposition
2.2
this process is going to terminate after a finite number of steps. The only thing that we have to show is that
\widetilde{\mathcal{F}}=S_{x,y}(\mathcal{F})
is non-trivial for
1\leq x<y\leq n-3\beta ks
.

If
N_{\widetilde{\mathcal{F}}}(y)=\emptyset
, then
|N_{\mathcal{F}}(x,y)|=\emptyset
and
|N_{\mathcal{F}}(x,\overline{y})|+|N_{\mathcal{F}}(y,\overline{x})|<\binom{n-2}{k-1}
by Proposition
2.2
. It follows that

|N_{\mathcal{F}}(x)|+|N_{\mathcal{F}}(y)|=2|N_{\mathcal{F}}(x,y)|+|N_{\mathcal{F}}(x,\overline{y})|+|N_{\mathcal{F}}(y,\overline{x})|<\binom{n-2}{k-1}.

Thus

N_{\mathcal{F}}(v)\leq N_{\mathcal{F}}(y)<\frac{1}{2}\binom{n-2}{k-1}

(4)

for each
v\in\{y+1,\ldots,n\}
.

Suppose that
y\leq n-3\beta ks
and
N_{\widetilde{\mathcal{F}}}(y)=\emptyset
, we are going to get a contradiction. To this end we introduce the notation

\alpha_{j}=\binom{n-1}{k-1}-|N_{\mathcal{F}}(j)|.

Note that
\alpha_{x}
counts the number of missing sets in
N_{\mathcal{F}}(x)
, namely the sets
G\in\binom{[n]}{k}
with
x\in G\notin\mathcal{F}
. Let
W=\{n-3\beta ks+1,\ldots,n\}
. By inequality (
4
), for each
v\in W

\alpha_{v}>\binom{n-1}{k-1}-\frac{1}{2}\binom{n-2}{k-1}>\frac{1}{2}\binom{n-1}{k-1}.

(5)

Let
\alpha
denote the total number of missing sets for
\mathcal{F}
. On the one hand,

\alpha=\binom{n}{k}-|\mathcal{F}|<\beta ks\binom{n-1}{k-1}

(6)

since
|\mathcal{F}|\geq\binom{ks+k-2}{k}
. On the other hand,
by inclusion-exclusion principle and inequality (
5
), we have

\begin{split}\alpha>&\sum_{i\in W}\alpha_{i}-\binom{|W|}{2}\binom{n-2}{k-2}\\
\geq&\frac{|W|}{2}\binom{n-1}{k-1}-\binom{|W|}{2}\binom{n-2}{k-2}\\
=&\left(\frac{|W|}{2}\frac{n-1}{k-1}-\binom{|W|}{2}\right)\binom{n-2}{k-2}\\
>&\frac{|W|}{2}\left(\frac{n-1}{k-1}-|W|\right)\binom{n-2}{k-2}\\
=&\frac{3\beta ks}{2}\left(\frac{n-1}{k-1}-3\beta ks\right)\binom{n-2}{k-2}\\
\geq&\frac{3\beta ks}{2}\cdot\frac{2(n-1)}{3(k-1)}\cdot\binom{n-2}{k-2}\\
=&\beta ks\binom{n-1}{k-1},\end{split}

(7)

a contradiction.

∎
