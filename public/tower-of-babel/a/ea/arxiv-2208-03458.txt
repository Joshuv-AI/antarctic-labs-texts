Constraints on primordial black holes with CMB spectral distortions
Yupeng Yang

Abstract
In the mixed dark matter scenarios consisting of primordial black holes (PBHs) and weakly interacting massive particles (WIMPs), WIMPs can be accreted onto PBHs to form ultracompact minihalos (UCMHs) with a density spike in the early universe. Compared with the classical dark matter halo, UCMHs are formed earlier and have a higher density of center. Since the annihilation rate is proportional to the squared number density of WIMPs, it is expected that WIMPs annihilation within UCMHs is enhanced and has influences on the early universe. Between the time of recombination and matter-radiation equality, the energy released from WIMPs annihilation within UCMHs is injected into the Universe resulting in CMB y y -type distortion. We investigate these effects and derive the upper limits on the abundance of PBHs taking advantage of the observational results of Far Infrared Absolute Spectrophotometer (FIRAS). We find that for the WIMPs mass range 1 ≤ m χ ≤ 1000 ​ GeV 1\leq m_{\chi}\leq 1000~\rm GeV , the upper limits on the abundance of PBHs are 5 × 10 − 3 ≤ Ω PBH ≤ 5 × 10 − 2 5\times 10^{-3}\leq\Omega_{\rm PBH}\leq 5\times 10^{-2} .

II
The basic properties of UCMHs and upper limits on the abundance of PBHs

The formation mechanism of UCMHs has been studied in previous works, see, e.g., Refs.
Ricotti and Gould 2009
;
Eroshenko 2016
;
Adamek et al. 2019
;
Boucenna et al. 2018
. There are two possible mechanisms: (i) direct collapse of large density perturbation (
10^{-4}\lesssim\delta\rho/\rho\lesssim 0.3
) in the early universe, which is not large enough to form PBHs; (ii) accretion of DM particles onto PBHs. We will not discuss the formation mechanism of UCMHs and adopt the second scenario for our purpose.

In the radiation dominated early universe, a large density perturbation,
\delta\rho/\rho\gtrsim 0.3
, can directly collapse to form PBHs
Carr et al. 2010
;
Carr 2005
. Then DM particles can be accreted onto PBHs resulting in the formation of UCMHs. The mass of a UCMH changes slowly during the radiation dominated period and increases significantly after the redshift of matter-radiation equality
z_{\rm eq}

Ricotti and Gould 2009
. The specific form of the changes of mass with redshift is
Ricotti and Gould 2009
;
Scott and Sivertsson 2009

\displaystyle M_{\rm UCMH}=\left(\frac{1+z_{\rm eq}}{1+z}\right)M_{\rm PBH}

(1)

The density profile of DM in a UCMH is in the form of
Scott and Sivertsson 2009
;
Zhang 2011

\displaystyle\rho_{\rm DM}(r)=\frac{3M_{\rm UCMH}(z)}{16\pi R_{\rm UCMH}(z)^{3/4}r^{9/4}},

(2)

where
R_{\rm UCMH}(z)
is the radius of UCMH
Ricotti and Gould 2009
,

\displaystyle R_{\rm UCMH}(z)=0.019~{\rm pc}\left(\frac{1000}{1+z}\right)\left(\frac{M_{\rm UCMH}(z)}{M_{\odot}}\right)^{1/3}

(3)

For the density profile shown in Eq. (
2
),
\rho_{\rm DM}(r)\to\infty
for
r\to 0
. Taking into account the annihilation of DM particles, there is a maximum core density,
\rho_{\rm max}
, at the center of a UCMH
Scott and Sivertsson 2009
;
Yang et al. 2011
,

\displaystyle\rho_{\rm max}=\frac{m_{\chi}}{\left<\sigma v\right>(t-t_{i})},

(4)

where
t_{i}
is the formation time of UCMH and here we set
t_{i}=t_{\rm eq}
.
m_{\chi}
and
\left<\sigma v\right>
are the mass and thermally averaged annihilation cross section of DM particles, respectively. The corresponding core radius,
r_{\rm core}
, is defined as
\rho_{\rm max}=\rho_{\rm DM}(r_{\rm core})
. In short, the density profile of the DM particle within a UCMH used here is as follows:

\displaystyle\rho(r)=\left\{\begin{array}[]{rcl}\rho_{\rm max}&&{r\leq r_{\rm core}}\\
\rho_{\rm DM}(r)&&{r\geq r_{\rm core}}\\
\end{array}\right.

The annihilation rate of the DM particle within a UCMH can be written as

\displaystyle\Gamma_{\rm anni}

\displaystyle=\int_{0}^{R_{\rm UCMH}}2\pi n^{2}_{\rm DM}(r)\left<\sigma v\right>r^{2}dr

(8)

\displaystyle=2\pi\frac{\left<\sigma v\right>}{m_{\chi}^{2}}\int_{0}^{R_{\rm UCMH}}\rho_{\rm DM}^{2}(r)r^{2}dr

\displaystyle=2\pi\frac{\left<\sigma v\right>}{m_{\chi}^{2}}\left[\int_{0}^{r_{\rm core}}\rho_{\rm max}^{2}+\int_{r_{\rm core}}^{R_{\rm UCMH}}\rho_{\rm DM}^{2}\right]r^{2}dr

In the redshift range
1100\lesssim z\lesssim 5\times 10^{4}
, the heated electrons produced by Compton scattering or pair production on ions transfer their energy by inverse Compton scattering to the CMB photons, producing a distorted spectrum with phase-space distribution
McDonald et al. 2001
;
Chluba and Sunyaev 2012
;
Hu and Silk 1993
,

\displaystyle f(x,y)\simeq f(x,0)+y\frac{xe^{x}}{(e^{x}-1)^{2}}\left[\frac{x}{{\rm tanh}(x/2)}-4\right],

(9)

where
f(x,0)=1/(e^{x}-1)
is the Planck distribution with
x=E/T
.
y
is the parameter used to measure the strength of CMB
y
-type distortion. The distribution function
f(x,y)
is governed by the Kompaneets equation
Kompaneets 1957
, and after some algebraic calculations one can find the relation between the parameter
y
and the energy injected into the Universe
McDonald et al. 2001
;
Chluba and Sunyaev 2012
,

\displaystyle y=\frac{1}{4}\frac{\delta\rho_{\gamma}}{\rho_{\gamma}},

(10)

where
\delta\rho_{\gamma}
is the injected energy and
\rho_{\gamma}
is the energy density of the CMB photons.

The energy injection rate of DM annihilation per unit volume can be written as

\displaystyle E_{\rm inj}=n_{\rm PBH}m_{\chi}\Gamma_{\rm anni}=\Omega_{\rm PBH}\rho_{c,0}\frac{m_{\chi}}{M_{\rm PBH}}\Gamma_{\rm anni},

(11)

where
n_{\rm PBH}=\rho_{\rm PBH}/M_{\rm PBH}
is the number density of PBH,

\rho_{c,0}
is the critical density of the Universe at
z=0
and
\Omega_{\rm PBH}=\rho_{\rm PBH}/\rho_{c,0}
. In this paper, we have adopted a monochromatic PBH mass function for our calculations.

Using the above equations, the parameter
y
caused by DM annihilation within UCMHs can be rewritten as follows:

\displaystyle y_{\rm DM}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{4}\frac{\delta\rho_{\gamma}}{\rho_{\gamma}}=\frac{1}{4}\int^{t_{2}}_{t_{1}}\frac{{\dot{\rho}_{\rm anni}}}{\rho_{\gamma}}dt=\frac{1}{4}\int^{t_{2}}_{t_{1}}\frac{E_{\rm inj}(z)}{\rho_{\gamma 0}(1+z)^{4}}dt

(12)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{4}\frac{1}{\rho_{\gamma 0}}\int^{z_{\rm eq}}_{z_{\rm rec}}\frac{E_{\rm inj}(z)}{H(z)(1+z)^{5}}dz,

where
H(z)=H_{0}\sqrt{\Omega_{\rm m}(1+z)^{3}+\Omega_{\Lambda}+{\Omega_{\gamma}}(1+z)^{4}}
,

\rho_{\gamma 0}
is the energy density of CMB at
z=0
. Here we have used the relation
dt=1/H(z)(1+z)~dz

III
conclusions

We have investigated the abundance of PBHs in the mixed dark matter scenarios consisting of PBHs and WIMPs. In this scenario, UCMHs can be formed around PBHs with a density profile
\rho(r)\sim r^{-9/4}
. The annihilation rate of particle DM (WIMPs) within UCMHs is enhanced and has influence on the evolution of the Universe. Previous works have focused these influences on the epoch after recombination. Since UCMHs can be formed in the early universe, they can effect CMB
y
-type spectral distortion in the redshift range
z_{\rm rec}\leq z\leq z_{\rm eq}
. We have investigated the influence of DM annihilation within UCMHs on the CMB
y
-type distortion. By requiring that the parameter
y
caused by DM annihilation within UCMHs does not exceed the current limits
|y|<1.5\times 10^{-5}
, we derived the upper limits on the abundance of PBHs,
5\times 10^{-3}\leq\Omega_{\rm PBH}\leq 5\times 10^{-2}
for the WIMPs mass range
1\leq m_{\chi}\leq 1000~\rm GeV
. Compared with other existing limits, although our limits are not the strongest, they are new results obtained in a different way.
