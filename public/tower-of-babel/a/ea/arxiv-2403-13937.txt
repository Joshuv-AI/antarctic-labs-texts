k k -non-crossing trees and edge statistics modulo k k
Helmut Prodinger

Abstract
. Instead of k k -Dyck paths we consider the equivalent concept of k k -non-crossing trees. This is our preferred approach relative to down-step statistics modulo k k (first studied by Heuberger, Selkirk, and Wagner by different methods). One symmetry argument about subtrees is needed and the rest goes along the lines of a paper by Flajolet and Noy.

We enumerate non-crossing trees with

As one can see, the distribution isn’t fair, as the root has all thes right edges as successors. We will use variables:

n
for the number of nodes and
\ell
and
r
for the two types of edges. We will use the butterfly decomposition due to Flajolet and Noy
[
3
]
.

T=\frac{z}{1-B},\quad B=\frac{T^{2}}{z};

T
stands for tree and
B
, which is only an auxiliary quantity, for butterfly. However, because of the anomaly of the root, we temporarily make
B
the center of
interest:

B=\frac{T^{2}}{z}=\frac{z}{(1-B)^{2}}.

Using the substitution
z=v(1-v)^{2}
, this can be solved, and the relevant solution is just
B=v
, and further
\frac{F}{z}=\frac{1}{1-v}
. This can be extended with our extra variables

\ell
and
r
:

B=\frac{z}{(1-\ell B)(1-rB)},

then
z=v(1-\ell v)(1-rv)
and
B=v
and
\frac{F}{z}=\frac{1}{1-rB}=\frac{1}{1-rv}
. Now we read off coefficients:

\displaystyle[z^{n}]F

\displaystyle=[z^{n-1}]\frac{F}{z}=[z^{n-1}]\frac{1}{1-rv}=\frac{1}{n-1}[z^{n-2}]\frac{d}{dz}\frac{1}{1-rv}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{n-1}[z^{n-2}]\frac{dv}{dz}\frac{d}{dv}\frac{1}{1-rv}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{n-1}[z^{n-2}]\frac{1}{1-2\ell v-2rv+3\ell rv^{2}}\frac{r}{(1-rv)^{2}}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{n-1}\frac{1}{2\pi i}\oint\frac{dz}{z^{n-1}}\frac{1}{1-2\ell v-2rv+3\ell rv^{2}}\frac{r}{(1-rv)^{2}}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{n-1}\frac{1}{2\pi i}\oint\frac{dv}{v^{n-1}(1-\ell v)^{n-1}(1-rv)^{n-1}}\frac{r}{(1-rv)^{2}}

\displaystyle=\frac{r}{n-1}[v^{n-2}]\frac{1}{(1-\ell v)^{n-1}(1-rv)^{n+1}}.

As we can see, it is unnecessary to explicitly compute
\frac{dv}{dz}
as it cancels out anyway. This will be very beneficial in the following sections. Furthermore,

\displaystyle[z^{n}\ell^{j}r^{n-1-j}]F

\displaystyle=[v^{n-2}\ell^{j}r^{n-2-j}]\frac{1}{n-1}\frac{1}{(1-\ell v)^{n-1}(1-rv)^{n+1}}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{n-1}\binom{n-2-j}{j}\binom{2n-2-j}{n-2-j}.

This is the number of non-crossing trees with
n
nodes,
j
left edges and
n-1-j
right edges.

The butterfly equation is

B=\frac{T^{k}}{z}=\frac{z}{(1-r_{1}B)\dots(1-r_{k}B)}

with variables
r_{1},\dots,r_{k}
to count the down-steps ending (or beginning) on a level
\equiv i(\bmod k)
. Eventually
T=\frac{z}{1-r_{k}B}
. For the solution,
the substitution
z=v(1-r_{1}v)\dots(1-r_{k}v)
works, and
B=v
, and thus
\frac{T}{z}=\frac{1}{1-r_{k}v}
.

Reading off coefficients is similar to the previous case
k=2
:

\displaystyle[z^{n}]F

\displaystyle=[z^{n-1}]\frac{F}{z}=\frac{1}{n-1}[z^{n-2}]\frac{d}{dz}\frac{F}{z}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{n-1}[z^{n-2}]\frac{dv}{dz}\frac{d}{dv}\frac{F}{z}=\frac{1}{n-1}[z^{n-2}]\frac{dv}{dz}\frac{r_{k}}{(1-r_{k}v)^{2}}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{n-1}\frac{1}{2\pi i}\oint\frac{dz}{z^{n-1}}\frac{dv}{dz}\frac{r_{k}}{(1-r_{k}v)^{2}}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{n-1}\frac{1}{2\pi i}\oint\frac{dv}{v^{n-1}(1-r_{1}v)^{n-1}\dots(1-r_{k}v)^{n-1}}\frac{r_{k}}{(1-r_{k}v)^{2}}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{n-1}[v^{n-2}]\frac{r_{k}}{(1-r_{1}v)^{n-1}\dots(1-r_{k-1}v)^{n-1}(1-r_{k}v)^{n+1}}.

Furthermore (with
a_{1}+\dots+a_{k}=n-1
)

\displaystyle[z^{n}r_{1}^{a_{1}}\dots r_{k}^{a_{k}}]F

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{n-1}[v^{n-2}r_{1}^{a_{1}}\dots r_{k}^{a_{k}}]\frac{r_{k}}{(1-r_{1}v)^{n-1}\dots(1-r_{k-1}v)^{n-1}(1-r_{k}v)^{n+1}}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{n-1}[v^{n-2}r_{1}^{a_{1}}\dots r_{k}^{a_{k}-1}]\frac{1}{(1-r_{1}v)^{n-1}\dots(1-r_{k-1}v)^{n-1}(1-r_{k}v)^{n+1}}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{n-1}\binom{n-2+a_{1}}{a_{1}}\dots\binom{n-2+a_{k-1}}{a_{k-1}}\binom{n-1+a_{k}}{a_{k}-1}.

\displaystyle=[z^{n-t-1}]\frac{F}{z^{t+1}}=[z^{n-t-1}]\prod_{j=k-t}^{k}\frac{1}{1-r_{j}v}=\frac{1}{n-t-1}[z^{n-t-2}]\frac{d}{dz}\prod_{j=k-t}^{k}\frac{1}{1-r_{j}v}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{n-t-1}[z^{n-t-2}]\frac{dv}{dz}\prod_{j=k-t}^{k}\frac{1}{1-r_{j}v}\cdot\sum_{i=k-t}^{k}\frac{r_{i}}{1-r_{i}v}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{n-t-1}\frac{1}{2\pi i}\oint\frac{dz}{z^{n-t-1}}\frac{dv}{dz}\prod_{j=k-t}^{k}\frac{1}{1-r_{j}v}\cdot\sum_{i=k-t}^{k}\frac{r_{i}}{1-r_{i}v}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{n-t-1}\frac{1}{2\pi i}\oint\frac{dv}{v^{n-t-1}(1-r_{1}v)^{n-t-1}\dots(1-r_{k}v)^{n-t-1}}\prod_{j=k-t}^{k}\frac{1}{1-r_{j}v}\cdot\sum_{i=k-t}^{k}\frac{r_{i}}{1-r_{i}v}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{n-t-1}\frac{1}{2\pi i}\oint\frac{dv}{v^{n-t-1}\prod_{h=1}^{k-t-1}(1-r_{h}v)^{n-t-1}\prod_{\ell=k-t}^{k}(1-r_{\ell}v)^{n-t}}\sum_{i=k-t}^{k}\frac{r_{i}}{1-r_{i}v}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{n-t-1}[v^{n-t-2}]\frac{1}{\prod_{h=1}^{k-t-1}(1-r_{h}v)^{n-t-1}\prod_{\ell=k-t}^{k}(1-r_{\ell}v)^{n-t}}\sum_{i=k-t}^{k}\frac{r_{i}}{1-r_{i}v}.
