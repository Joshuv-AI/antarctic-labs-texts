Data Fusion for Radio Frequency SLAM with Robust Sampling
Erik Leitinger 1 3 , Bryan Teague 2 , Wenyu Zhang 4 , Mingchao Liang 4 , and Florian Meyer 4

Abstract
Precise indoor localization remains a challenging problem for a variety of essential applications. A promising approach to address this problem is to exchange radio signals between mobile agents and static pa that bounce off flat surfaces in the indoor environment. slam ( slam ) methods can be used to jointly estimates the time-varying location of agents as well as the static locations of the flat surfaces. Recent work on slam methods has shown that each surface can be efficiently represented by a single mva . The measurement model related to this mva -based slam method is highly nonlinear. Thus, Bayesian estimation relies on sampling-based techniques. The original mva -based slam method employs conventional “bootstrap” sampling. In challenging scenarios it was observed that the original method might converge to incorrect mva positions corresponding to local maxima. In this paper, we introduce mva -based slam with an improved sampling technique that succeeds in the aforementioned challenging scenarios. Our simulation results demonstrate significant performance advantages.



I
Introduction

Maintaining accurate and timely situational awareness in indoor environments is challenging in a variety of applications. This is particularly true when no prior geometric information about the environment is available. In such scenarios, it is desirable to generate a map and localize mobile “agents” within that map.

slam
is a promising methodology for precise localization in the aforementioned scenarios. Here, radio signals that bounce off flat surfaces are used to estimate the locations of mobile agents and the surfaces themselves.

slam

agent –

[
1

2
,
3
,
4

. By detecting and localizing the
va

V
Robust Sampling for
mva
-based

To address these limitations, we introduce an alternative strategy for obtaining a proposal distribution. In what follows, we will again discuss the proposal distribution for the belief

The main idea is to construct a weighted mixture that consists of

VII
Conclusions and Future Work

In this paper, we introduced
mva
-based
slam
with an improved sampling technique that is suitable for challenging scenarios where only range measurements are available, and only one or two
pa
are deployed. Our numerical evaluation demonstrated significant performance advantages of the proposed method compared to the recently introduced conventional
mva
-based
slam
. Promising directions for future research are an extension of
mva
-based
slam
to (i) angle measurements provided by antenna arrays and (ii) higher-order reflections from flat

surfaces.
