Abstract
. In this paper, for p > 1 p>1 and s > 1 s>1 , we give a complete description of the boundedness and compactness of a Cesàro-like operator from the Besov space B p B_{p} into a Banach space X X between the mean Lipschitz space Λ 1 / s s \Lambda^{s}_{1/s} and the Bloch space.

1.
Introduction

Let
\mu
be a finite positive Borel measure on
[0,1)
. For a nonnegative integer
n
, denote by
\mu_{n}
the moment of order
n
of
\mu
. More precisely,

\mu_{n}=\int_{[0,1)}t^{n}\mathrm{d}\mu(t).

Let
f(z)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}a_{n}z^{n}
belong to
H(\mathbb{D})
, the space of functions analytic in the open unit disk
\mathbb{D}
of the complex plane
\mathbb{C}
.
A Cesàro-like operator
\mathcal{C}_{\mu}
is given by

\mathcal{C}_{\mu}(f)(z)=\sum^{\infty}_{n=0}\left(\mu_{n}\sum^{n}_{k=0}a_{k}\right)z^{n},\quad z\in\mathbb{D}.

When
\mathrm{d}\mu(t)=\mathrm{d}t
, then
\mathcal{C}_{\mu}
is the classical Cesàro operator
\mathcal{C}
(see
[
16
]
).
In
[
12
,
17
]
, the authors initiated the study of the operator
\mathcal{C}_{\mu}
acting on spaces of analytic functions. Very recently, the operator
\mathcal{C}_{\mu}
has attracted a lot of attention (cf.
[
2
,
3
,
10
,
11
,
20
]
).

For
p>1
, let
B_{p}
be the Besov space consisting of those functions
f
in
H(\mathbb{D})
such that

\|f\|_{B_{p}}=|f(0)|+\left(\int_{\mathbb{D}}|f^{\prime}(z)|^{p}(1-|z|^{2})^{p-2}\mathrm{d}A(z)\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}<\infty,

where
\mathrm{d}A
is the area measure on
\mathbb{C}
normalized so that
A(\mathbb{D})=1
.
It is well known that
B_{2}
is the Dirichlet space
\mathcal{D}
. Denote by
\mathcal{B}
the Bloch space of functions
f\in H(\mathbb{D})
satisfying

\|f\|_{\mathcal{B}}=|f(0)|+\sup_{z\in\mathbb{D}}(1-|z|^{2})|f^{\prime}(z)|<\infty.

If
1<p_{1}<p_{2}<\infty
, then
B_{p_{1}}\subsetneqq B_{p_{2}}\subsetneqq\mathcal{B}
. See
[
24
, Chapter 5]
for the theory of these spaces.

For
1\leq p<\infty
and
0<\alpha\leq 1
, recall that the mean Lipschitz space
\Lambda^{p}_{\alpha}
is the class of functions
f
in
H(\mathbb{D})
having a non-tangential limit at almost everywhere point of the unit circle

\mathbb{T}
such that
\omega_{p}(t,f)=O(t^{\alpha})
as
t\to 0
, where
\omega_{p}(\cdot,f)
is the integral modulus of continuity of order
p
of the function
f(e^{i\theta})
. From
[
7
, Chapter 5]
,
\Lambda^{p}_{\alpha}
is always
contained in the Hardy space
H^{p}
and consists of functions
f
in
H(\mathbb{D})
such that

\|f\|_{\Lambda^{p}_{\alpha}}=|f(0)|+\sup_{0<r<1}(1-r)^{1-\alpha}M_{p}(r,f^{\prime})<\infty,

where

M_{p}(r,f^{\prime})=\left(\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{0}^{2\pi}|f^{\prime}(re^{i\theta})|^{p}\mathrm{d}\theta\right)^{1/p}.

If
1<p_{1}<p_{2}<\infty
, then
\Lambda^{p_{1}}_{1/{p_{1}}}\subsetneqq\Lambda^{p_{2}}_{1/{p_{2}}}
.
For
p>1
, it is known (cf.
[
5
, Theorem 2.5]
) that
\Lambda^{p}_{1/p}
is a proper subset of the Bloch space
\mathcal{B}
.

Carleosn type measures are key tools in the study of function spaces and related operator theory. Let
I
be an arc of
\mathbb{T}
with arclength
|I|
normalized so that
|\mathbb{T}|=1
. Denote by
S(I)
the Carleson box, namely,

S(I)=\{r\zeta\in\mathbb{D}:1-|I|<r<1,\ \zeta\in I\}.

By
[
22
]
, for
0\leq\alpha<\infty
and
0<s<\infty
, a finite positive Borel measure
\nu
on
\mathbb{D}
is said to be an
\alpha
-logarithmic
s
-Carleson measure if

\sup_{I\subseteq\mathbb{T}}\frac{\nu(S(I))(\log\frac{e}{|I|})^{\alpha}}{|I|^{s}}<\infty,

and a finite positive Borel measure
\nu
on
\mathbb{D}
is called a vanishing
\alpha
-logarithmic
s
-Carleson measure if

\lim_{|I|\to 0}\frac{\nu(S(I))(\log\frac{e}{|I|})^{\alpha}}{|I|^{s}}=0.

For
\alpha=0
,
\alpha
-logarithmic
s
-Carleson measures and vanishing
\alpha
-logarithmic
s
-Carleson measures are said to be
s
-Carleson measures and vanishing
s
-Carleson measures (cf.
[
8
]
), respectively.

A finite positive Borel measure
\nu
on [0, 1) can be viewed as a measure on
\mathbb{D}
by identifying it
with the measure
\tilde{\nu}
given by

\tilde{\nu}(E)=\nu(E\cap[0,1)),

for each Borel subset
E
of
\mathbb{D}
. Consequently,
\nu
is an
\alpha
-logarithmic
s
-Carleson measure on
[0,1)
if

\sup_{t\in[0,1)}\frac{\nu([t,1))(\log\frac{e}{1-t})^{\alpha}}{(1-t)^{s}}<\infty,

and
\nu
is a vanishing
\alpha
-logarithmic
s
-Carleson measure on
[0,1)
if

\lim_{t\to 1^{-}}\frac{\nu([t,1))(\log\frac{e}{1-t})^{\alpha}}{(1-t)^{s}}=0.

In
[
10
]
, the authors gave a series of interesting results on operators induced by radial measures acting on some spaces of analytic functions.
From
[
10
, p. 14]
, the Cesàro operator
\mathcal{C}
is not bounded from the Dirichlet space
\mathcal{D}
to the Bloch space
\mathcal{B}
. Then it is quite natural to describe the measures
\mu
such that
\mathcal{C}_{\mu}
is bounded from
\mathcal{D}
to
\mathcal{B}
. The following conclusion is Theorem 7 in
[
10
]
.

Theorem A
.

Let
X
be a Banach space of analytic functions in
\mathbb{D}
with
\Lambda^{2}_{1/2}\subseteq X\subseteq\mathcal{B}
and let
\mu
be a finite positive Borel measure on

[0,1)
.

(i)

If
\mu
is a
\frac{1}{2}
-logarithmic
1
-Carleson measure, then
\mathcal{C}_{\mu}
is a bounded operator from
\mathcal{D}
into
X
.

(ii)

If
\mathcal{C}_{\mu}
is a bounded operator from
\mathcal{D}
into
X
and
0<\beta<\frac{1}{2}
, then
\mu
is a
\beta
-logarithmic
1
-Carleson measure.

In this paper, motivated by Theorem
A
, for
p>1
and
s>1
we characterize completely
the boundedness and compactness of the operator
\mathcal{C}_{\mu}
from the Besov space
B_{p}
into a Banach space
Y
between the mean Lipschitz space
\Lambda^{s}_{1/s}
and the Bloch space
\mathcal{B}
.
Consequently, taking
p=s=2
in our result, we finish the gap between condition (i) and condition (ii) in Theorem
A
.

Throughout this paper, the symbol
A\thickapprox B
means that
A\lesssim B\lesssim A
. We say that
A\lesssim B
if there exists a positive
constant
C
such that
A\leq CB
.

2.
Logarithmic Carleson measures on
[0,1)

In this section, we give some characterizations of logarithmic Carleson measures
\nu
on
[0,1)
, which will be useful in next sections. We will see that the interval

[0,1)
is essential in these characterizations; that is, when
\nu
is
a finite positive Borel measure on
\mathbb{D}
, it is possible that these characterizations do not hold.

Proposition 2.1
.

Suppose
0\leq\alpha<\infty
,
0<s<\infty
, and
\nu
is a finite positive Borel measure on
[0,1)
.

(i)

\nu
is a
\alpha
-logarithmic
s
-Carleson measure if and only if

\nu_{n}=O\left(\frac{1}{(n+1)^{s}[\log(n+1)]^{\alpha}}\right).

(ii)

\nu
is a vanishing
\alpha
-logarithmic
s
-Carleson measure if and only if

\nu_{n}=o\left(\frac{1}{(n+1)^{s}[\log(n+1)]^{\alpha}}\right).

Proof.

(i) For
0\leq\alpha<\infty
, it is well known (cf.
[
25
, p.192]
) that

\frac{1}{1-x}\Big(\log\frac{e}{1-x}\Big)^{-\alpha}\thickapprox\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}[\log(k+e)]^{-\alpha}x^{k}

(2.1)

for all
x\in[0,1)
,
and

\sum^{n}_{k=0}[\log(k+e)]^{-\alpha}\thickapprox n[\log(n+e)]^{-\alpha}

(2.2)

for all
n\geq 1
. Suppose
\nu
is an
\alpha
-logarithmic
s
-Carleson measure. By a useful formula of the distribution function (see
[
13
, p. 20]
), (
2.1
), and (
2.2
), we deduce

\displaystyle\nu_{n}=

\displaystyle n\int^{1}_{0}\nu([x,1))x^{n-1}\mathrm{d}x

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\ n\int^{1}_{0}x^{n-1}(1-x)^{s}\Big(\log\frac{e}{1-x}\Big)^{-\alpha}\mathrm{d}x

\displaystyle\thickapprox

\displaystyle\ n\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}[\log(k+e)]^{-\alpha}\int^{1}_{0}x^{n+k-1}(1-x)^{s+1}\mathrm{d}x

\displaystyle\thickapprox

\displaystyle\ n\Big(\sum_{k=0}^{n}+\sum_{k=n+1}^{\infty}\Big)[\log(k+e)]^{-\alpha}(n+k)^{-s-2}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\ n^{-s-1}\sum^{n}_{k=0}[\log(k+e)]^{-\alpha}+n[\log(n+1+e)]^{-\alpha}\sum_{k=n+1}^{\infty}(n+k)^{-s-2}

\displaystyle\thickapprox

\displaystyle\ n^{-s}[\log(n+e)]^{-\alpha}+n[\log(n+1+e)]^{-\alpha}\int_{n+1}^{\infty}(n+x)^{-s-2}\mathrm{d}x

\displaystyle\thickapprox

\displaystyle\frac{1}{n^{s}[\log(n+1)]^{\alpha}}

for all
n>1
. Conversely, let

\nu_{n}=O\left(\frac{1}{(n+1)^{s}[\log(n+1)]^{\alpha}}\right).

For
t\in[0,1)
, there is a positive integer
n
such that

1-\frac{1}{n}\leq t<1-\frac{1}{n+1}.

Then

\displaystyle\nu([t,1))\leq

\displaystyle\nu([1-\frac{1}{n},1))

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\int_{[1-\frac{1}{n},1)}t^{n}\mathrm{d}\nu(t)

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\frac{1}{(n+1)^{s}[\log(n+1)]^{\alpha}}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle(1-t)^{s}\left(\log\frac{e}{1-t}\right)^{-\alpha}.

(ii) The proof is similar to (i) with minor modifications. We omit it.
∎

Remark 2.
If
\nu
is a finite positive Borel measure on
\mathbb{D}
and we also write

\nu_{n}=\int_{\mathbb{D}}w^{n}d\nu(w),\ \ n=0,1,2,\ldots,

Lemma B

Proposition 2.2
.

0<t<\infty

\nu

\displaystyle\sup_{a\in\mathbb{D}}\int_{[0,1)}\frac{(1-|a|)^{t}(\log\frac{e}{1-|a|})^{\alpha}}{(1-x)^{r}|1-ax|^{s+t-r}}\mathrm{d}\nu(x)<\infty;

\displaystyle\sup_{a\in\mathbb{D}}\Big|\int_{[0,1)}\frac{(1-|a|)^{t}(\log\frac{e}{1-|a|})^{\alpha}}{(1-x)^{r}(1-ax)^{s+t-r}}\mathrm{d}\nu(x)\Big|<\infty.

(i)\Rightarrow(ii)

[

\nu

\alpha

\displaystyle\sup_{|a|\leq 1/2}\Big(\log\frac{e}{1-|a|}\Big)^{\alpha}\int_{[0,1)}\frac{(1-|a|)^{t}}{(1-x)^{r}(1-|a|x)^{s+t-r}}\mathrm{d}\nu(x)\lesssim 1.

Now consider

with
|a|>1/2
and set

\displaystyle S_{n}(a)=\{x\in[0,1):1-2^{n}(1-|a|)\leq x<1\}

for every positive integer

.
Let

be the minimal integer satisfying

Then

.
If

(2.3)

If

(2.4)

\displaystyle\Big(\log\frac{e}{1-|a|}\Big)^{\alpha}\int_{[0,1)}\frac{(1-|a|)^{t}}{(1-x)^{r}(1-|a|x)^{s+t-r}}\mathrm{d}\nu(x)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\Big(\log\frac{e}{1-|a|}\Big)^{\alpha}\int_{S_{1}(a)}\frac{(1-|a|)^{t}}{(1-x)^{r}(1-|a|x)^{s+t-r}}\mathrm{d}\nu(x)

\displaystyle+\Big(\log\frac{e}{1-|a|}\Big)^{\alpha}\sum^{n_{a}}_{n=2}\int_{S_{n}(a)\backslash S_{n-1}(a)}\frac{(1-|a|)^{t}}{(1-x)^{r}(1-|a|x)^{s+t-r}}\mathrm{d}\nu(x)

\displaystyle=:

\displaystyle J_{1}(a)+J_{2}(a).

If

and

.

\displaystyle\thickapprox

\displaystyle\frac{(\log\frac{e}{1-|a|})^{\alpha}}{(1-|a|)^{s-r}}r\int_{0}^{\infty}\lambda^{r-1}\nu(\{x\in[1-2(1-|a|),1):1-\frac{1}{\lambda}<x\})\mathrm{d}\lambda

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle 1.

),

3.

into some spaces of analytic functions

In this section, for

and

given in Section 2, we characterize the boundedness and the compactness of
\mathcal{C}_{\mu}
operators from
B_{p}
into a Banach space
X
between the mean Lipschitz space
\Lambda^{s}_{1/s}
and the Bloch space.

We recall a representation of the operator

below, which is Proposition 1 in
[

Lemma C
.

Suppose
f\in H(\mathbb{D})
and
\mu
is a finite positive Borel measure on
[0,1)
. Then

\mathcal{C}_{\mu}(f)(z)=\int_{[0,1)}\frac{f(tz)}{1-tz}d\mu(t),\ \ z\in\mathbb{D}.

We give the first main result in this section as follows.

Theorem 3.1
.

Suppose
1<p<\infty
,
1<s<\infty
,
X
is a Banach space of analytic functions in
\mathbb{D}
with
\Lambda^{s}_{1/s}\subseteq X\subseteq\mathcal{B}

\mu
is a finite positive Borel measure on
[0,1)

q
be the real number satisfying
\frac{1}{p}+\frac{1}{q}=1
. Then the

(i)

\mathcal{C}_{\mu}
is a bounded operator from
B_{p}
into
X
;

(ii)

\mu
is a
\frac{1}{q}
-logarithmic 1-Carleson measure.

Proof.

Suppose
\mu
is a
\frac{1}{q}
-logarithmic 1-Carleson measure. Note that
s>1
. Then Proposition
2.2

and

Let

\displaystyle\sup_{0<r<1}(1-r)^{1-\frac{1}{s}}\Big(\int_{0}^{2\pi}\Big|\int_{[0,1)}\frac{tf^{\prime}(tre^{i\theta})}{1-tre^{i\theta}}\mathrm{d}\mu(t)\Big|^{s}\mathrm{d}\theta\Big)^{\frac{1}{s}}

\displaystyle\sup_{0<r<1}(1-r)^{1-\frac{1}{s}}\int_{[0,1)}\Big(\int_{0}^{2\pi}\Big|\frac{tf^{\prime}(tre^{i\theta})}{1-tre^{i\theta}}\Big|^{s}\mathrm{d}\theta\Big)^{\frac{1}{s}}\mathrm{d}\mu(t)

\displaystyle+\sup_{0<r<1}(1-r)^{1-\frac{1}{s}}\int_{[0,1)}\Big(\int_{0}^{2\pi}\Big|\frac{tf(tre^{i\theta})}{(1-tre^{i\theta})^{2}}\Big|^{s}\mathrm{d}\theta\Big)^{\frac{1}{s}}\mathrm{d}\mu(t).

) and

\displaystyle\|f\|_{B_{p}}\sup_{0<r<1}(1-r)^{1-\frac{1}{s}}\int_{[0,1)}\Big(\log\frac{2}{1-tr}\Big)^{\frac{1}{q}}\mathrm{d}\mu(t)\Big(\int_{0}^{2\pi}\frac{1}{|1-tre^{i\theta}|^{2s}}\mathrm{d}\theta\Big)^{\frac{1}{s}}
