1.
Introduction

In this paper, we study the Cauchy problem of the incompressible Hall-MHD system

(1.1)

\begin{cases}\vskip 5.69054ptu_{t}-\Delta u+(u\cdot\nabla)u-(\nabla\times b)\times b+\nabla p=0&\quad{\mdmathbb R}^{3}\times(0,\infty),\\
\vskip 5.69054ptb_{t}-\Delta b+\nabla\times((\nabla\times b)\times b)-\nabla\times(u\times b)=0&\quad{\mdmathbb R}^{3}\times(0,\infty),\\
\vskip 5.69054pt\divg u=\divg b=0,\\
u|_{t=0}=u_{0},\,\,b|_{t=0}=b_{0},\end{cases}

where
u
and
b
are the velocity and the magnetic field, and
p
is the pressure.
The initial velocity field
u_{0}
and the initial magnetic field
b_{0}
satisfy
\divg u_{0}=\divg b_{0}=0
.
The Hall term
\nabla\times((\nabla\times b)\times b)
makes the Hall-MHD system significantly different in the mathematical theory from the MHD system.
It also prevents straightforward adaptations from arguments used in the mathematical analysis of MHD and related models.

We study the global existence of the solution of (
1.1
) in
C_{b}([0,T];\dot{B}^{\alpha}_{p,q}({\mdmathbb R}^{3}))
.

\displaystyle\lesssim\|\tilde{f}\|_{\dot{B}^{\alpha,\frac{\alpha}{2}}_{p,q}({\mdmathbb R}^{3}\times(-\infty,t))}\lesssim\|f\|_{\dot{B}^{\alpha,\frac{\alpha}{2}}_{p,q}({\mdmathbb R}^{3}\times(0,t))}\rightarrow 0\quad\mbox{as}\quad t\rightarrow 0.

Let
F(x,t)=\Gamma_{t}*f_{0}(x)
for
f_{0}(x)=f(x,0)
. Since
f_{0}\in\dot{B}^{\alpha-\frac{2}{p}}_{p,q}({\mdmathbb R}^{3})
, we have

F\in\dot{B}^{\alpha,\frac{\alpha}{2}}_{p,q}
with
\|F\|_{\dot{B}^{\alpha,\frac{\alpha}{2}}_{p,q}}\lesssim\|f_{0}\|_{\dot{B}^{\alpha-\frac{2}{p}}_{p,q}({\mdmathbb R}^{3})}
and
\|F(t)-f_{0}\|_{\dot{B}^{\alpha-\frac{2}{p}}_{p,q}({\mdmathbb R}^{3})}\rightarrow 0
as
t\rightarrow 0
. From the above argument, we have
\|F(t)-f(t)\|_{\dot{B}^{\alpha-\frac{2}{p}}_{p,q}({\mdmathbb R}^{3})}\rightarrow 0
as
t\rightarrow 0
.
Thus,

\displaystyle\|f(t)-f_{0}\|_{\dot{B}^{\alpha-\frac{2}{p}}_{p,q}({\mdmathbb R}^{3})}\leq\|f(t)-F(t)\|_{\dot{B}^{\alpha-\frac{2}{p}}_{p,q}({\mdmathbb R}^{3})}+\|F(t)-f_{0}\|_{\dot{B}^{\alpha-\frac{2}{p}}_{p,q}({\mdmathbb R}^{3})}\rightarrow 0\quad\mbox{as}\quad t\rightarrow 0.

Therefore,
\dot{B}^{\alpha,\frac{\alpha}{2}}_{p,q}\subset C([0,\infty);\dot{B}^{\alpha-\frac{2}{p}}_{p,q}({\mdmathbb R}^{3}))
.
This proves Lemma
2.9
for
\frac{2}{p}<\alpha<2
.

Let
2k+\frac{2}{p}<\alpha<2k+2
for
k\in\mdmathbb{N}
.
Then we have
D_{x}^{2k}f\in\dot{B}^{\alpha-2k,\frac{\alpha}{2}-k}_{p,q}
and
D_{x}^{2k}f_{0}\in\dot{B}^{\alpha-2k-\frac{2}{p}}_{p,q}({\mdmathbb R}^{3})
.
By the same argument, we have

\displaystyle\|f(t)-f_{0}\|_{\dot{B}_{p,q}^{\alpha-\frac{2}{p}}({\mdmathbb R}^{3})}\leq\|D_{x}^{2k}(f(t)-f_{0})\|_{\dot{B}_{p,q}^{\alpha-2k-\frac{2}{p}}({\mdmathbb R}^{3})}\rightarrow 0\quad\mbox{as}\quad t\rightarrow 0.

This proves Lemma
2.9
for
2k+\frac{2}{p}<\alpha<2k+2
for
k\in\mdmathbb{N}
.
For the case
2k\leq\alpha\leq 2k+\frac{2}{p},\,\,k\in{\mdmathbb N}
, we use the property of real interpolation. We complete the proof of Lemma
2.9
.

Appendix C

Let
\Phi=\phi_{-1}+\phi_{0}+\phi_{1}

\Phi_{j}(x,t)=2^{5}\Phi(2^{j}x,2^{2j}t)

\phi_{j}=\phi_{j}*\Phi_{j}

\displaystyle\phi_{j}*(D_{t}^{k}D_{x}^{\beta}f)=\rho_{j}*\phi_{j}*f,

where
\widehat{\rho_{j}}(\xi,\tau)=\widehat{\Phi}(2^{-j}\xi,2^{-2j}\tau)(2\pi i\tau)^{k}(-2\pi\xi)^{\beta}
.
The
L^{p}(\mdmathbb{R}^{4})
-multiplier norm
M_{j}
of
\widehat{\rho}_{j}(\xi,\tau)
equals the
L^{p}(\mdmathbb{R}^{4})
-multiplier norm of

\widehat{\rho_{j}^{{}^{\prime}}}(\xi,\tau):=2^{(|\beta|+2k)j}\widehat{\Phi}(\xi,\tau)(2\pi i\tau)^{k}(-2\pi\xi)^{\beta}
for
1\leq p\leq\infty
(see
Lemma 6.1.5 in
[
4
]

[
4
]
, the
L^{p}(\mdmathbb{R}^{4})
-multiplier norm of

\widehat{\rho_{j}^{{}^{\prime}}}
is bounded by
2^{(|\beta|+2k)j}
.
Thus,

\displaystyle\|D^{k}_{t}D_{x}^{\beta}f\|_{\dot{B}^{s-|\beta|-2k,\frac{s-|\beta|-2k}{2}}_{p,q}({\mdmathbb R}^{4})}

\displaystyle=\big(\sum_{j\in{\mdmathbb Z}}2^{(s-|\beta|-2k)qj}\|\phi_{j}*(D_{t}^{k}D_{x}^{\beta}f)\|^{q}_{L^{p}({\mdmathbb R}^{4})}\big)^{\frac{1}{q}}

\displaystyle\lesssim\big(\sum_{j\in{\mdmathbb Z}}2^{2sqj}\|\phi_{j}*f\|^{q}_{L^{p}({\mdmathbb R}^{4})}\big)^{\frac{1}{q}}

\displaystyle=\|f\|_{\dot{B}^{s,\frac{s}{2}}_{p,q}({\mdmathbb R}^{4})}.

This proves (
2.8
).

Note that
\frac{|\xi|^{2|\beta|}(2\pi i\tau)^{k}}{(|\xi|^{2}+2\pi i\tau)^{|\beta|+k}}
is
L^{p}({\mdmathbb R}^{4})
-multiplier for
1<p<\infty
(see Theorem 4.6´ in
[
13
]
).
Since

\displaystyle(|\xi|^{2}+2\pi i\tau)^{s-|\beta|-k}(-2\pi\xi)^{2\beta}(2\pi i\tau)^{k}\widehat{f}(\xi,\tau)=\frac{|\xi|^{2\beta}(2\pi i\tau)^{k}}{(|\xi|^{2}+2\pi i\tau)^{|\beta|+k}}(|\xi|^{2}+2\pi i\tau)^{s}\widehat{f}(\xi,\tau),

we have

\|D^{k}_{t}D_{x}^{\beta}f\|_{\dot{W}^{s-|\beta|-2k,\frac{s-\beta-2k}{2}}_{p}({\mdmathbb R}^{4})}\lesssim\|f\|_{\dot{W}^{s,\frac{s}{2}}_{p}({\mdmathbb R}^{4})}.

This proves (
2.9

Appendix D
Proof of Proposition
2.12

Let
f\in\dot{W}^{s,\frac{s}{2}}_{p}({\mdmathbb R}^{4})
for
s\in{\mdmathbb R}
.
Let

\Gamma*f(x,t)=\begin{cases}\vskip 5.69054pt\int_{-\infty}^{t}\int_{{\mdmathbb R}^{3}}\Gamma(x-y,t-s)f(y,s)dyds&\quad t\geq 0,\\
<\Gamma(x-\cdot,t-\cdot),f>&\quad t<0.\end{cases}

Here,
<,>
is duality pairing between
\dot{W}^{s,\frac{s}{2}}_{p}({\mdmathbb R}^{4})
and
\dot{W}^{-s,-\frac{s}{2}}_{p^{\prime}}({\mdmathbb R}^{4})=(\dot{W}^{s,\frac{s}{2}}_{p}({\mdmathbb R}^{4}))^{\prime}
(the dual space of
\dot{W}^{s,\frac{s}{2}}_{p}({\mdmathbb R}^{4})
), where
\frac{1}{p}+\frac{1}{p^{\prime}}=1
.

Note that
{\mathcal{F}}_{x,t}(h_{s}*\Gamma*f)(\xi,\tau)=(|\xi|^{2}+2\pi i\tau)^{\frac{s}{2}-1}{\mathcal{F}}_{x,t}(f)(\xi,\tau)
,
s\in{\mdmathbb R}
, where
h_{s}
is defined in Section
2
.
From the definition of
\dot{W}^{s,\frac{s}{2}}_{p}({\mdmathbb R}^{4})
, we have for
s\in{\mdmathbb R}
,

\|\Gamma*f\|_{\dot{W}^{s,\frac{s}{2}}_{p}({\mdmathbb R}^{4})}=\|f\|_{\dot{W}^{s-2,\frac{s}{2}-1}_{p}({\mdmathbb R}^{4})}.

Since
D_{x}\Gamma*f=\Gamma*D_{x}f
, we have

(4.1)

\displaystyle\|D_{x}\Gamma*f\|_{\dot{W}^{s,\frac{s}{2}}_{p}({\mdmathbb R}^{4})}

\displaystyle=\|D_{x}f\|_{\dot{W}^{s-2,\frac{s}{2}-1}_{p}({\mdmathbb R}^{4})}\lesssim\|f\|_{\dot{W}^{s-1,\frac{s}{2}-\frac{1}{2}}_{p}({\mdmathbb R}^{4})}.

Let
\dot{W}^{-1,-\frac{1}{2}}_{p0}
be a dual space of
\dot{W}^{1,\frac{1}{2}}_{p^{\prime}}
. Let
f\in\dot{W}^{-1,-\frac{1}{2}}_{p0}
and
\tilde{f}\in\dot{W}^{-1,-\frac{1}{2}}_{p}
be a zero extension of
f
, that is,
<\tilde{f},\phi>_{{\mdmathbb R}^{4}}=<f,\phi|_{{\mdmathbb R}^{3}\times(0,\infty)}>_{{\mdmathbb R}^{3}\times(0,\infty)}
, so that

\|\tilde{f}\|_{\dot{W}^{-1,-\frac{1}{2}}_{p}({\mdmathbb R}^{4})}\leq c\|f\|_{\dot{W}^{-1,-\frac{1}{2}}_{p0}}
. Note that
\Gamma*\tilde{f}(x,t)=\Gamma*f(x,t)
for
(x,t)\in{\mdmathbb R}^{4}
.
From (
4.1
) and (2) of Proposition
2.1
, we have

(4.2)

\begin{split}\|D_{x}\int_{0}^{t}\Gamma(t-s)f(x,s)ds\|_{L^{p}}=\|D_{x}\Gamma*\tilde{f}\|_{L^{p}({\mdmathbb R}^{4})}\lesssim\|\tilde{f}\|_{\dot{W}^{-1,-\frac{1}{2}}_{p}({\mdmathbb R}^{4})}\lesssim\|f\|_{\dot{W}^{-1,-\frac{1}{2}}_{p0}}.\end{split}

Similarly, we obtain

(4.3)

\displaystyle\|D_{x}\int_{0}^{t}\Gamma(t-s)f(x,s)ds\|_{\dot{W}^{1,\frac{1}{2}}_{p}}\lesssim\|f\|_{L^{p}}.

Using (
4.2
), (
4.3
), and the properties of real interpolation of dual spaces, we have for
0<s<1
,

\|D_{x}\int_{0}^{t}\Gamma(t-s)f(x,s)ds\|_{\dot{B}^{s,\frac{s}{2}}_{p,q}}\lesssim\|f\|_{\dot{B}^{s-1,\frac{s}{2}-\frac{1}{2}}_{p,q0}},

where
\dot{B}^{s-1,\frac{s}{2}-\frac{1}{2}}_{p,q0}
is the dual space of
\dot{B}^{-s+1,-\frac{s}{2}+\frac{1}{2}}_{p^{\prime},q^{\prime}}
.

Let
-1-\frac{5}{p}=-\frac{5}{r}
.
Since
\|f\|_{\dot{W}^{-1,-\frac{1}{2}}_{p0}}\leq c\|f\|_{L^{r}}
, from (
4.2
), we have

(4.4)

\displaystyle\|D_{x}\int_{0}^{t}\Gamma_{\alpha}(t-s)f(x,s)ds\|_{L^{p}}\lesssim\|f\|_{L^{r}}.

Let
\frac{1}{p^{*}}=\frac{1-s}{r}+\frac{s}{p}
and
-\frac{5}{p^{*}}=s_{1}-\frac{5}{p_{1}}
. Using the real interpolation in (
4.3
) and (
4.4
), and by Lemma
2.7
, we have for
0<s<1
,

\|D_{x}\int_{0}^{t}\Gamma(t-s)f(x,s)ds\|_{\dot{B}^{s,\frac{s}{2}}_{p,q}}\lesssim\|f\|_{L^{p^{*},q}}\lesssim\|f\|_{\dot{B}^{s_{1},\frac{s_{1}}{2}}_{p_{1},q}}.

This proves (1) of Proposition
2.12
.

Let
|\beta|=2k
with
k\in\mdmathbb{N}
.
Since
D_{x}^{\beta}D_{x}\int_{0}^{t}\Gamma(t-s)f(x,s)ds=D_{x}\int_{0}^{t}\Gamma(t-s)D_{x}^{\beta}f(x,s)ds
, from (
4.3
), we have

(4.5)

\displaystyle\begin{split}&\|D_{x}\int_{0}^{t}\Gamma(t-s)f(x,s)ds\|_{L^{p}(0,\infty;\dot{W}^{k}_{p}({\mdmathbb R}^{3}))}\\
&\leq\|D_{x}\int_{0}^{t}\Gamma(t-s)D_{x}^{\beta-1}f(x,s)ds\|_{L^{p}(0,\infty;\dot{W}^{1}({\mdmathbb R}^{3})}\\
&\lesssim\|D_{x}^{\beta-1}f\|_{L^{p}}\lesssim\|f\|_{L^{p}(0,\infty;\dot{W}^{2k-1}_{p}({\mdmathbb R}^{3}))}\lesssim\|f\|_{\dot{W}^{2k-1,k-\frac{1}{2}}_{p}}.\end{split}

Since

\displaystyle D_{t}^{k}D_{x}\int_{0}^{t}\Gamma(t-s)f(x,s)ds=(-1)^{k}\Delta^{k}D_{x}\int_{0}^{t}\Gamma(t-s)f(x,s)ds+\sum_{l=0}^{k-1}(-1)^{l}D_{t}^{l}\Delta^{k-1-l}D_{x}f,

we have, from (
4.5
) and Lemma
2.10

(4.6)

\begin{split}&\|D_{x}\int_{0}^{t}\Gamma(t-s)f(x,s)ds\|_{L^{p}({\mdmathbb R}^{3};\dot{W}^{k}_{p}(0,\infty))}\\
&\lesssim\|D_{x}\int_{0}^{t}\Gamma(t-s)f(x,s)ds\|_{L^{p}(0,\infty;\dot{W}^{2k}_{p}({\mdmathbb R}^{3}))}+\|\sum_{l=0}^{k-1}D_{t}^{l}\Delta^{k-1-l}D_{x}f\|_{L^{p}}\\
&\lesssim\|f\|_{\dot{W}^{2k-1,k-\frac{1}{2}}_{p}}.\end{split}

From (
4.5
), (
4.6
) and (3) of Proposition
2.5
, we obtain that for
k\in\mdmathbb{N}
,

(4.7)

\|D_{x}\int_{0}^{t}\Gamma(t-s)f(x,s)ds\|_{\dot{W}^{2k,k}_{p}}\lesssim\|f\|_{\dot{W}^{2k-1,k-\frac{1}{2}}_{p}}.

Using the real interpolation in (
4.3

2.5
), we have for

,

\|D_{x}\int_{0}^{t}\Gamma(t-s)f(x,s)ds\|_{\dot{B}^{s,\frac{s}{2}}_{p,q}}\lesssim\|f\|_{\dot{B}^{s-1,\frac{s}{2}-\frac{1}{2}}_{p,q}}.

This proves (2) of Proposition
2.12
.
