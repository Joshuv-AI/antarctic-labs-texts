Effects of 𝚲 \bm{\Lambda} hyperons on the deformations of even-even nuclei
C. F. Chen (陈超锋), Q. B. Chen (陈启博), Xian-Rong Zhou (周先荣), Yi-Yuan Cheng (程奕源), Ji-Wei Cui (崔继伟), H.-J. Schulze

Abstract
The deformations of multi- Λ \Lambda hypernuclei corresponding to even-even core nuclei ranging from 8 Be to 40 Ca with 2, 4, 6, and 8 hyperons are studied in the framework of the deformed Skyrme-Hartree-Fock approach. It is found that the deformations are reduced when adding 2 or 8 Λ \Lambda hyperons, but enhanced when adding 4 or 6 Λ \Lambda hyperons. These differences are attributed to the fact that Λ \Lambda hyperons are filled gradually into the three deformed p p orbits, of which the [110]1/2 - orbit is prolately deformed and the degenerate [101]1/2 - and [101]3/2 - orbits are oblately deformed.

II
Formalism

In the framework of the SHF approach,
the energy of a hypernucleus is given by an energy-density functional,

E=\int d^{3}\bm{r}\;\varepsilon(\bm{r})\ ,\quad\varepsilon=\varepsilon_{NN}+\varepsilon_{\Lambda N}+\varepsilon_{\Lambda\Lambda}\>,

(1)

where
\varepsilon_{NN}
,
\varepsilon_{\Lambda N}
, and
\varepsilon_{\Lambda\Lambda}

account for the nucleon-nucleon interaction,
the hyperon-nucleon interaction,
and the hyperon-hyperon interaction, respectively.
The energy-density functional
depends on the one-body densities
\rho_{q}
,
kinetic densities
\tau_{q}
,
and spin-orbit currents
\bm{J}_{q}
,

\Big[\rho_{q},\;\tau_{q},\;\bm{J}_{q}\Big]=\sum_{i=1}^{N_{q}}{n_{q}^{i}}\Big[|\phi_{q}^{i}|^{2},\;|\nabla\phi_{q}^{i}|^{2},\;{\phi_{q}^{i}}^{*}(\nabla\phi_{q}^{i}\times\bm{\sigma})/i\Big]\>,

(2)

where
\phi_{q}^{i}
(
i=1,N_{q}
) are the self-consistently calculated
single-particle (s.p.) wave functions
of the
N_{q}
occupied states for the species
q=n,p,\Lambda

in a hypernucleus.
They satisfy the Schrödinger equation,
obtained by the minimization of the total energy functional (
1
)
according to the variational principle, as

\bigg[\nabla\cdot{1\over 2m_{q}^{*}(\bm{r})}\nabla-V_{q}(\bm{r})+i\bm{W}_{q}(\bm{r})\cdot\left(\nabla\times\bm{\sigma}\right)\bigg]\phi_{q}^{i}(\bm{r})=e_{q}^{i}\,\phi_{q}^{i}(\bm{r})

(3)

in which
\bm{W}_{q}(\bm{r})
is the spin-orbit interaction part for the
nucleons as given in Refs.
Vautherin and Brink 1972
;
Bender et al. 1999
.
The central mean fields
V_{q}(\bm{r})
,
corrected by the effective-mass terms
following the procedure described in

Cugnon et al. 2000
;
Schulze and Rijken 2013
;
Margueron et al. 2017

are

\displaystyle V_{N}=

\displaystyle V_{N}^{\text{SHF}}+\frac{\partial\varepsilon_{N\Lambda}}{\partial\rho_{N}}

\displaystyle+\frac{\partial}{\partial\rho_{N}}\left(\frac{m_{\Lambda}}{m_{\Lambda}^{*}(\rho_{N})}\right)\left(\frac{\tau_{\Lambda}}{2m_{\Lambda}}-\frac{3}{5}\frac{\rho_{\Lambda}(3\pi^{2}\rho_{\Lambda})^{2/3}}{2m_{\Lambda}}\right)\>,

(4)

\displaystyle V_{\Lambda}=

\displaystyle\frac{\partial(\varepsilon_{N\Lambda}+\varepsilon_{\Lambda\Lambda})}{\partial\rho_{\Lambda}}-\left(\frac{m_{\Lambda}}{m_{\Lambda}^{*}(\rho_{N})}-1\right)\frac{(3\pi^{2}\rho_{\Lambda})^{2/3}}{2m_{\Lambda}}\>.

IV
Summary

In summary, we study
within the deformed SHF formalism
the impurity effects of
\Lambda
hyperons on the
deformation of even-even nuclei ranging from
8
Be to
40
Ca,
employing an effective
\Lambda N
interaction that reproduces well
the experimental binding energies of single-
\Lambda
hypernuclei.

The effects of
\Lambda
hyperons on the deformation of the core nuclei
are studied in detail.
Those deformations are generally reduced by adding 2 or 8
\Lambda
hyperons,
while they are enhanced by adding 4 or 6.
These behaviors are interpreted in a microscopic manner
by analyzing the
\Lambda
s.p. orbits
and the density distributions for the occupied ones.
It is demonstrated that the order of filling the hyperons into the
p
orbits
is determined by the shape of the core nucleus.
When the core nucleus is oblate,
the hyperons are filled first into the degenerate oblate
[101]1/2
-
and [101]3/2
-
orbits,
so that the deformation of the core nucleus increases to the oblate side,
and can reach a maximum when 6 hyperons are added.
When the core nucleus is prolate,
the hyperons are filled first into the prolate [110]1/2
-
orbit,
which leads to an increase of the prolate deformation of the core nucleus,
and the deformation reaches a maximum when 4 hyperons are added.
When the core nucleus is spherical,
hyperons have no preferences for the shape of the
p
orbits and result in
a soft potential energy surface.

Future experimental data of multi-
\Lambda
hypernuclei are necessary
to examine these effects and refine the assumptions and ingredients
of the model calculations presented here.
