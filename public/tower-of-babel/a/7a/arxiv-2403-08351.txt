Solution on strong partition of 2 2 -balanced regular multipartite tournaments
Jiangdong Ai, Fankang He, Yihang Liu

Abstract
We call a partition of a c c -partite tournament into tournaments of order c c is strong if each tournament is strongly connected. The strong partition number denoted as S ​ T ​ ( r ) ST(r) , represents the minimum integer c ′ c^{\prime} such that every regular r r -balanced c c -partite tournament has a strong partition with c ≥ c ′ c\geq c^{\prime} . Figueroa, Montellano-Ballesteros and Olsen showed the existence of S ​ T ​ ( r ) ST(r) for all r ≥ 2 r\geq 2 and proved that 5 ≤ S ​ T ​ ( 2 ) ≤ 7 5\leq ST(2)\leq 7 . In this note, we establish that S ​ T ​ ( 2 ) = 6 ST(2)=6 and we also show the unique 2 2 -balanced 5 5 -partite tournament which has no strong partition.
