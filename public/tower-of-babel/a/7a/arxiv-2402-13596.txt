Constraining anisotropic universe under f ⁡ ( R , T ) f(R,T) theory of gravity
Lokesh Kumar Sharma, Suresh Parekh, Saibal Ray, Anil Kumar Yadav

Abstract
Abstract: We try to find the possibility of a Bianchi V universe in the modified gravitational field theory of f ⁡ ( R , T ) f(R,T) . We have considered a Lagrangian model in connection between the trace of the energy-momentum tensor T T and the Ricci scalar R R . In order to solve the field equations a power law for the scaling factor was also considered. To make a comparison of the model parameters with the observational data we put constraint on the model under the datasets of the Hubble parameter, Baryon Acoustic Oscillations, Pantheon, joint datasets of Hubble parameter + Pantheon and collective datasets of the Hubble parameter + Baryon Acoustic Oscillations + Pantheon. The outcomes for the Hubble parameter in the present epoch are reasonably acceptable, especially our estimation of this H 0 H_{0} is remarkably consistent with various recent Planck Collaboration studies that utilize the Λ \Lambda -CDM model. Keywords: Cosmological Parameters, Observational constraints, Om Diagnostics, Cosmography, MCMC Model

IV.1
Energy Conditions

The energy conditions, analogously establish cosmic laws that elucidate the distribution of the matter and energy throughout the cosmos which can be derived from Einstein’s gravitational equations. Thus we obtain certain conditions, viz. Weak Energy Condition (WEC), Null Energy Condition (NEC), Strong Energy Condition (SEC) and Dominant Energy Condition (DEC) which reveal the distribution of matter and energy in the cosmic space.

1.

WEC:
\rho\geq 0

According to the WEC, energy can nowhere be negative or zero. This requirement keeps the laws of the cosmos impartial and constant. Using the parameters discovered using the Bayesian Analysis, Fig.
14

NEC:
\rho-p\geq 0

The NEC deals with light. It asserts that energy is a necessary component of all space transit for light and cannot exist in a vacuum. This constraint prevents odd occurrences from occurring and maintains the physics of the cosmos. Figure

SEC:
\rho+3p\geq 0

DEC:

The DEC guarantees that the energy is not just non-negative but also that its distribution cannot be too volatile. It is comparable to asserting that the energy cannot go out of control or travel faster than light. Based on the Bayesian Analysis DEC is shown in Fig.
