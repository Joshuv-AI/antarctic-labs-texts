Weak and Strong Convergence of Generalized Proximal Point Algorithms with Relaxed Parameters
Hui Ouyang

Abstract
In this work, we propose and study a framework of generalized proximal point algorithms associated with a maximally monotone operator. We indicate sufficient conditions on the regularization and relaxation parameters of generalized proximal point algorithms for the equivalence of the boundedness of the sequence of iterations generated by this algorithm and the non-emptiness of the zero set of the maximally monotone operator, and for the weak and strong convergence of the algorithm. Our results cover or improve many results on generalized proximal point algorithms in our references. Improvements of our results are illustrated by comparing our results with related known ones.

Proposition 2.3
.

Let
(t_{k})_{k\in\mathbb{N}}

(\alpha_{k})_{k\in\mathbb{N}}

, and let
(\beta_{k})_{k\in\mathbb{N}}

The following statements hold.

(i)

(iii)

Suppose that

\alpha_{k}\in\left[0,1\right]

\beta_{k}\in\left[0,1\right]

\alpha_{k}+\beta_{k}\leq 1

, and that

.

(iv)

Suppose that
(\forall k\in\mathbb{N})

\alpha_{k}\in\left[0,1\right[

\beta_{k}\in\mathbb{R}_{+}

\sup_{k\in\mathbb{N}}\frac{\beta_{k}}{1-\alpha_{k}}<\infty
and
\sum_{k\in\mathbb{N}}(1-\alpha_{k})=\infty
,

\limsup_{k\to\infty}\omega_{k}\leq 0
, and that
\sum_{k\in\mathbb{N}}\left|\gamma_{k}\right|<\infty

Based on

2.2

\displaystyle(\forall m\in\mathbb{N})(\forall k\in\mathbb{N})\quad t_{m+k}\leq\prod^{m+k-1}_{j=m}\alpha_{j}t_{m}+\sum^{m+k-1}_{i=m}\prod^{m+k-1}_{j=i+1}\alpha_{j}\beta_{i}\omega_{i}+\sum^{m+k-1}_{i=m}\prod^{m+k-1}_{j=i+1}\alpha_{j}\gamma_{i}.

(2.3)

Item

(i)
: Because

Proposition 2.13
.

Let
(y_{k})_{k\in\mathbb{N}}
be a sequence in
\mathcal{H}
. Set
\Omega
as the set of all weak sequential cluster points of
(y_{k})_{k\in\mathbb{N}}
. Suppose that for every
z\in\Omega
, the limit
\lim_{k\to\infty}\left\lVert y_{k}-z\right\rVert
exists.
Then there is at most one element in
\Omega
, that is, there cannot be more than one weak sequential cluster point of
(y_{k})_{k\in\mathbb{N}}
.

Proof.

Suppose to the contrary that there exist
z_{1}
and
z_{2}
in
\Omega
with
z_{1}\neq z_{2}
. Then based on the assumption, there exist
q_{1}
and
q_{2}
in
\mathbb{R}_{+}
such that
(\forall i\in\{1,2\})

\lim_{k\to\infty}\left\lVert y_{k}-z_{i}\right\rVert=q_{i}
.
Note that for every
k\in\mathbb{N}
,

\displaystyle\left\lVert y_{k}-z_{2}\right\rVert^{2}=\left\lVert y_{k}-z_{1}\right\rVert^{2}+2\left\langle y_{k}-z_{1},z_{1}-z_{2}\right\rangle+\left\lVert z_{1}-z_{2}\right\rVert^{2}\text{and}

\displaystyle\left\lVert y_{k}-z_{1}\right\rVert^{2}=\left\lVert y_{k}-z_{2}\right\rVert^{2}+2\left\langle y_{k}-z_{2},z_{2}-z_{1}\right\rangle+\left\lVert z_{2}-z_{1}\right\rVert^{2},

which imply, respectively, that

\displaystyle 2\left\langle y_{k}-z_{1},z_{1}-z_{2}\right\rangle=\left\lVert y_{k}-z_{2}\right\rVert^{2}-\left\lVert y_{k}-z_{1}\right\rVert^{2}-\left\lVert z_{1}-z_{2}\right\rVert^{2}\to q^{2}_{2}-q^{2}_{1}-\left\lVert z_{1}-z_{2}\right\rVert^{2},\text{ and}

(2.11a)

\displaystyle 2\left\langle y_{k}-z_{2},z_{2}-z_{1}\right\rangle=\left\lVert y_{k}-z_{1}\right\rVert^{2}-\left\lVert y_{k}-z_{2}\right\rVert^{2}-\left\lVert z_{2}-z_{1}\right\rVert^{2}\to q^{2}_{1}-q^{2}_{2}-\left\lVert z_{1}-z_{2}\right\rVert^{2}.

(2.11b)

On the other hand,
\{z_{1},z_{2}\}\subseteq\Omega
forces that once
\lim_{k\to\infty}\left\langle y_{k}-z_{1},z_{1}-z_{2}\right\rangle
and
\lim_{k\to\infty}\left\langle y_{k}-z_{2},z_{2}-z_{1}\right\rangle
exist, these two limits must be
0
. Combine this with
Equation

2.11
and
z_{1}\neq z_{2}
to deduce that

\displaystyle q^{2}_{2}-q^{2}_{1}=\left\lVert z_{1}-z_{2}\right\rVert^{2}>0\quad\text{and}\quad q^{2}_{1}-q^{2}_{2}=\left\lVert z_{1}-z_{2}\right\rVert^{2}>0,

which is absurd. Therefore, the desired result holds.
∎

Asymptotic regularity and convergence

Given a sequence
(y_{k})_{k\in\mathbb{N}}
in
\mathcal{H}
and a sequence
(c_{k})_{k\in\mathbb{N}}
in
\mathbb{R}_{++}
, we say the
asymptotic regularity holds for
(y_{k})_{k\in\mathbb{N}}
and
(c_{k})_{k\in\mathbb{N}}
, if
y_{k}-\operatorname{J}_{c_{k}A}y_{k}\to 0
.
We shall see that the asymptotic regularity plays an important role in the proof of the convergence of generalized proximal point algorithms.

Proposition 2.17
.

Suppose that
\operatorname{zer}A\neq\varnothing
. Let
p\in\operatorname{zer}A
and let
(y_{k})_{k\in\mathbb{N}}
be a sequence in
\mathcal{H}
. Suppose that
\lim_{k\to\infty}\left\lVert y_{k}-p\right\rVert
exists in
\mathbb{R}_{+}
and that
y_{k+t}-\operatorname{J}_{c_{k}A}y_{k}\to 0
for some
t\in\mathbb{N}
. Then
y_{k}-\operatorname{J}_{c_{k}A}y_{k}\to 0
and
y_{k}-y_{k+t}\to 0
.

Proof.

Taking
2.9
Item

(iii)
\&
Item

(iv)
into account and employing
Definition

2.8
Item

(i)
, we observe that

\displaystyle(\forall k\in\mathbb{N})\quad\left\lVert\operatorname{J}_{c_{k}A}y_{k}-p\right\rVert^{2}+\left\lVert y_{k}-\operatorname{J}_{c_{k}A}y_{k}\right\rVert^{2}\leq\left\lVert y_{k}-p\right\rVert^{2},

which yields that for every
k\in\mathbb{N}
,

\displaystyle\left\lVert y_{k}-\operatorname{J}_{c_{k}A}y_{k}\right\rVert^{2}-\left\lVert y_{k}-p\right\rVert^{2}+\left\lVert y_{k+t}-p\right\rVert^{2}

\displaystyle\leq-\left\lVert\operatorname{J}_{c_{k}A}y_{k}-p\right\rVert^{2}+\left\lVert y_{k+t}-p\right\rVert^{2}

\displaystyle=\left\langle y_{k+t}-p-\left(\operatorname{J}_{c_{k}A}y_{k}-p\right),y_{k+t}-p+\left(\operatorname{J}_{c_{k}A}y_{k}-p\right)\right\rangle

\displaystyle\leq\left\lVert y_{k+t}-\operatorname{J}_{c_{k}A}y_{k}\right\rVert\left(\left\lVert y_{k+t}-p\right\rVert+\left\lVert y_{k}-p\right\rVert\right),

where in the last inequality we use the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality, the nonexpansiveness of
\operatorname{J}_{c_{k}A}
, and
p\in\operatorname{zer}A=\operatorname{Fix}\operatorname{J}_{c_{k}A}
. Hence,

\displaystyle(\forall k\in\mathbb{N})\quad\left\lVert y_{k}-\operatorname{J}_{c_{k}A}y_{k}\right\rVert^{2}\leq\left\lVert y_{k}-p\right\rVert^{2}-\left\lVert y_{k+t}-p\right\rVert^{2}+\left\lVert y_{k+t}-\operatorname{J}_{c_{k}A}y_{k}\right\rVert\left(\left\lVert y_{k+t}-p\right\rVert+\left\lVert y_{k}-p\right\rVert\right),

which ensures
y_{k}-\operatorname{J}_{c_{k}A}y_{k}\to 0
, since the existence of
\lim_{k\to\infty}\left\lVert y_{k}-p\right\rVert
yields
\left\lVert y_{k}-p\right\rVert^{2}-\left\lVert y_{k+t}-p\right\rVert^{2}\to 0
and the boundedness of
\left(\left\lVert y_{k+t}-p\right\rVert+\left\lVert y_{k}-p\right\rVert\right)_{k\in\mathbb{N}}
.

Moreover, in consideration of
(\forall k\in\mathbb{N})

\left\lVert y_{k}-y_{k+t}\right\rVert\leq\left\lVert y_{k}-\operatorname{J}_{c_{k}A}y_{k}\right\rVert+\left\lVert\operatorname{J}_{c_{k}A}y_{k}-y_{k+t}\right\rVert
, we reach the last required convergence by using
y_{k}-\operatorname{J}_{c_{k}A}y_{k}\to 0
and
y_{k+t}-\operatorname{J}_{c_{k}A}y_{k}\to 0
.
∎

3
Generalized proximal point algorithms

Recall that

A:\mathcal{H}\to 2^{\mathcal{H}}\text{ is a maximally monotone operator}.

In the rest of this work,

and

are arbitrary but fixed, and the generalized proximal point algorithm is generated by conforming the following recursion:

\displaystyle(\forall k\in\mathbb{N})\quad x_{k+1}=\alpha_{k}u+\beta_{k}x_{k}+\gamma_{k}\operatorname{J}_{c_{k}A}(x_{k})+\delta_{k}e_{k},

(\forall k\in\mathbb{N})
