Dynamical mass generation of spin-2 fields in de Sitter space for an O ⁡ ( N ) O(N) symmetric model at large N N
Nahomi Kan, Kiyoshi Shiraishi

Abstract
We consider the strong-coupling phase in a model of O ⁡ ( N ) O(N) spin-2 field theory in de Sitter spacetime and the effective mass of spin-2 fields therein. In the strong-coupling phase, the Higuchi bound limits the mass parameter in the theory. The analysis using the large N N approximation finds the critical value of the mass parameter with numerical calculation.

Conclusion and outlook

In this paper, in the

vacuum is studied by the large

mass parameter, which exceeds the Higuchi bound, are numerically estimated.
If we take a large enough

Furthermore, for sufficiently large coupling constant, it is possible that the

The bound has been obtained in our toy

The stability of the vacuum is the most important topic that should be investigated

In this paper, we have also presented expressions in general

A.2
Negative coupling constant
\lambda

The equation of motion can be read as, for
\lambda<0

\frac{m^{2}}{\Lambda}=\frac{\sigma}{\Lambda}+\frac{|\lambda|}{\sqrt{\Lambda}}\frac{1}{\sqrt{\Lambda}}\frac{dL}{d\sigma}\,,

(33)

where
\frac{|\lambda|}{\sqrt{\Lambda}}
is dimensionless.
