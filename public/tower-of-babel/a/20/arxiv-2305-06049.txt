Extremal function for a sharp Moser-Trudinger type inequality on the upper half space Keywords: Moser-Trudinger inequalities, Optimal constant, Extremal functions, Euler Lagrange equation.
Yubo Ni

Abstract
We establish a sharp Moser-Trudinger type inequality on the upper half space for two dimensions. Then we investigate the existence of the extremal functions for this sharp Moser-Trudinger type inequality under dynamic changes in the unit ball. Additionally, we derive a logarithmic inequality named weighted Euclidean Onofri inequality and another Trudinger type inequality. 2010 Mathematics Subject Classification: 35A15, 35A23.

After using L’Hopital’s rule,

\displaystyle\quad\ \frac{-\frac{c_{1}(1+\alpha)}{2}\ln x\cdot x^{-1}+\frac{c_{1}}{4}\left(1-(1-\frac{2\ln x}{x})^{1+\alpha}\right)}{x^{-1}}

\displaystyle=\frac{c_{1}(1+\alpha)}{2}(1-\ln x)\left(1-{\left(1-\frac{2\ln x}{x}\right)}^{\alpha}\right).

Since
