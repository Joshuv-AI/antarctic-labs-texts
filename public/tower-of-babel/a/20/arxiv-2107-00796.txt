Nonlinear estimates

In this section, we establish the nonlinear estimates. The main tool in establishing the nonlinear estimates is the fractional Hardy inequality given in Corollary
2.9
.

We divide the study in two cases:
s\in\mathbb{N}
and
s\notin\mathbb{N}
.

When
s\in\mathbb{N}
, we obtain the following nonlinear estimates.

Lemma 3.11
.

Let
1<p,\;r<\infty
,
s\in\mathbb{N}
and
\frac{b}{s}<\sigma
. Assume that
f\in C^{\left\lceil s\right\rceil}
satisfies following condition:

\left|f^{\left(k\right)}(u)\right|\lesssim\left|u\right|^{\sigma+1-k},\penalty\ 0\leq k\leq\left\lceil s\right\rceil,\penalty\ \left\lceil s\right\rceil\leq\sigma+1.

(3.1)

Suppose that

\frac{1}{p}=\sigma\left(\frac{1}{r}-\frac{s}{n}\right)+\frac{1}{r}+\frac{b}{n},\penalty\ \frac{1}{r}-\frac{s}{n}>0.

(3.2)

Then we have

\left\||x|^{-b}f(u)\right\|_{\dot{H}_{p}^{s}}\lesssim\left\|u\right\|_{\dot{H}_{r}^{s}}^{\sigma+1}.

(3.3)

Proof.

By Lemma
2.5
, we have

\left\||x|^{-b}f(u)\right\|_{\dot{H}_{p}^{s}}=\sum_{\left|\alpha\right|=s}\left\|D^{\alpha}\left(|x|^{-b}f(u)\right)\right\|_{p}=\sum_{\left|\alpha^{\prime}\right|+\left|\alpha^{\prime\prime}\right|=s}\left\|D^{\alpha^{\prime}}\left(|x|^{-b}\right)D^{\alpha^{\prime\prime}}f(u)\right\|_{p}.

So it suffices to show that

\left\|D^{\alpha^{\prime}}\left(|x|^{-b}\right)D^{\alpha^{\prime\prime}}f(u)\right\|_{p}\lesssim\left\|u\right\|_{\dot{H}_{r}^{s}}^{\sigma+1},

(3.4)

for any
\alpha^{\prime},\;\alpha^{\prime\prime}
satisfying
\left|\alpha^{\prime}\right|+\left|\alpha^{\prime\prime}\right|=s
. We divide the study in two cases:
\left|\alpha^{\prime\prime}\right|=0
and
\left|\alpha^{\prime\prime}\right|\neq 0
.

Case 1.
We consider the case
\left|\alpha^{\prime\prime}\right|=0
, i.e.
\left|\alpha^{\prime}\right|=s
. In view of (
3.2
), we have
\frac{1}{p}=\frac{\sigma+1}{\rho_{1}}
, where

\frac{1}{\rho_{1}}=\frac{1}{r}-\frac{1}{n}\left(s-\frac{b+s}{\sigma+1}\right).

(3.5)

We can see that
s-\frac{b+s}{\sigma+1}>0
if, and only if,
\sigma>\frac{b}{s}
. Thus using (
3.5
) and Lemma
2.7
, we have the embedding
\dot{H}_{r}^{s}\subset\dot{H}_{\rho_{1}}^{\frac{b+s}{\sigma+1}}
. Since
\frac{n}{r}>s>\frac{b+s}{\sigma+1}
, we also have
{\frac{b+s}{\sigma+1}}<\frac{n}{\rho_{1}}
. Hence it follows from Corollary
2.9
that

\displaystyle\begin{split}\left\|D^{\alpha^{\prime}}\left(|x|^{-b}\right)f(u)\right\|_{p}&\lesssim\left\||x|^{-b-s}\left|u\right|^{\sigma+1}\right\|_{p}=\left\||x|^{-\frac{b+s}{\sigma+1}}u\right\|_{\rho_{1}}^{\sigma+1}\\
&\lesssim\left\|u\right\|_{\dot{H}_{\rho_{1}}^{\frac{b+s}{\sigma+1}}}^{\sigma+1}\lesssim\left\|u\right\|_{\dot{H}_{r}^{s}}^{\sigma+1}.\end{split}

Case 2.
We consider the case
\alpha^{\prime}\neq\alpha
, i.e. we have to show that

\left\|D^{\alpha^{\prime}}\left(|x|^{-b}\right)D^{\alpha^{\prime\prime}}\left(f(u)\right)\right\|_{p}\lesssim\left\|u\right\|_{\dot{H}_{r}^{s}}^{\sigma+1},

where
\left|\alpha^{\prime}\right|+\left|\alpha^{\prime\prime}\right|=s
and
\left|\alpha^{\prime\prime}\right|\geq 1
. Without loss of generality and for simplicity, we assume that
f
is a function of a real variable. It follows from the Leibniz rule of derivatives that

D^{\alpha^{\prime\prime}}(f(u))=\sum_{q=1}^{\left|\alpha^{\prime\prime}\right|}\sum_{\Lambda_{\alpha^{\prime\prime}}^{q}}C_{\alpha^{\prime\prime},\;q}f^{\left(q\right)}(u)\prod_{i=1}^{q}D^{\alpha_{i}}u,

(3.6)

where
\Lambda_{\alpha^{\prime\prime}}^{q}=\left(\alpha_{1}+\cdots+\alpha_{q}=\alpha^{\prime\prime},\;\left|\alpha_{i}\right|\geq 1\right)
. Thus we have

\displaystyle\begin{split}\left\|D^{\alpha^{\prime}}\left(|x|^{-b}\right)D^{\alpha^{\prime\prime}}\left(f(u)\right)\right\|_{p}&\lesssim\left\||x|^{-b-\left|\alpha^{\prime}\right|}\sum_{q=1}^{\left|\alpha^{\prime\prime}\right|}\left|u\right|^{\sigma+1-q}\sum_{\Lambda_{\alpha^{\prime\prime}}^{q}}\prod_{i=1}^{q}D^{\alpha_{i}}u\right\|_{p}\\
&\lesssim\sum_{q=1}^{\left|\alpha^{\prime\prime}\right|}\sum_{\Lambda_{\alpha^{\prime\prime}}^{q}}\left\||x|^{-b-\left|\alpha^{\prime}\right|}\left|u\right|^{\sigma+1-q}\prod_{i=1}^{q}D^{\alpha_{i}}u\right\|_{p}.\end{split}

So it suffices to show that

I\equiv\left\||x|^{-b-\left|\alpha^{\prime}\right|}\left|u\right|^{\sigma+1-q}\prod_{i=1}^{q}D^{\alpha_{i}}u\right\|_{p}\lesssim\left\|u\right\|_{\dot{H}_{r}^{s}}^{\sigma+1},

where
\alpha_{1}+\cdots+\alpha_{q}=\alpha^{\prime\prime}
,
\left|\alpha_{i}\right|\geq 1
,
\left|\alpha^{\prime}\right|+\left|\alpha^{\prime\prime}\right|=s
and
\left|\alpha^{\prime\prime}\right|\geq q\geq 1
.

Case 2.1.
First, we consider the case
\left|\alpha^{\prime\prime}\right|=s
and
q=1
, i.e. we have to show that

I=\left\||x|^{-b}\left|u\right|^{\sigma}D^{\alpha^{\prime\prime}}u\right\|_{p}\lesssim\left\|u\right\|_{\dot{H}_{r}^{s}}^{\sigma+1},

where
\left|\alpha^{\prime\prime}\right|=s

\sigma\left(\frac{1}{r}-\frac{1}{n}\left(s-\frac{b}{\sigma}\right)\right)+\frac{1}{r}=\frac{1}{p}.

Case 2.2.
Next, we consider the case
s>\left|\alpha^{\prime\prime}\right|\geq 1
or
q\geq 2
. In this case, we can see that
s-\left|\alpha_{i}\right|\geq 1
, for
1\leq i\leq q
. Since
\sigma>\frac{b}{s}
, we have

b-sq+s<s\left(\sigma+1-q\right).

(3.7)

Putting

b+\left|\alpha^{\prime}\right|=c+\sum_{i=1}^{q}\left(s-\left|\alpha_{i}\right|\right).

(3.8)

We also divide the study in two cases:
c\geq 0

\cdot
If

\sigma+1-q>0

Using (

\frac{1}{\rho_{3}}:=\frac{1}{r}-\frac{1}{n}\left(s-\frac{c}{\sigma+1-q}\right)
,

\frac{n}{r}>s>\frac{c}{\sigma+1-q}

2.9

\displaystyle\begin{split}I&=\left\||x|^{-b-\left|\alpha^{\prime}\right|}\left|u\right|^{\sigma+1-q}\prod_{i=1}^{q}D^{\alpha_{i}}u\right\|_{p}\\
&\lesssim\left\||x|^{-c}\left|u\right|^{\sigma+1-q}\prod_{i=1}^{q}|x|^{-\left(s-\left|\alpha_{i}\right|\right)}D^{\alpha_{i}}u\right\|_{p}\\
&\lesssim\left\|\left(|x|^{-\frac{c}{\sigma+1-q}}\left|u\right|\right)^{\sigma+1-q}\prod_{i=1}^{q}|x|^{-\left(s-\left|\alpha_{i}\right|\right)}D^{\alpha_{i}}u\right\|_{p}\\
&\lesssim\left\||x|^{-\frac{c}{\sigma+1-q}}u\right\|_{\rho_{3}}^{\sigma+1-q}\prod_{i=1}^{q}\left\||x|^{-\left(s-\left|\alpha_{i}\right|\right)}D^{\alpha_{i}}u\right\|_{r}\\
&\lesssim\left\|u\right\|_{\dot{H}_{\rho_{3}}^{\frac{c}{\sigma+1-q}}}^{\sigma+1-q}\prod_{i=1}^{q}\left\|D^{\alpha_{i}}u\right\|_{\dot{H}_{r}^{s-\left|\alpha_{i}\right|}}\lesssim\left\|u\right\|_{\dot{H}_{r}^{s}}^{\sigma+1},\end{split}

where the last inequality follows from
\dot{H}_{r}^{s}\subset\dot{H}_{\rho_{3}}^{\frac{c}{\sigma+1-q}}
.

\cdot
If
c<0
, then we have

c=b+\left|\alpha^{\prime}\right|-\sum_{i=1}^{q}\left(s-\left|\alpha_{i}\right|\right)<0.

Thus we can take a minimal integer

such that

b+\left|\alpha^{\prime}\right|-\sum_{i=1}^{j}\left(s-\left|\alpha_{i}\right|\right)\leq 0,

indeed, we have

\sum_{i=1}^{j-1}\left(s-\left|\alpha_{i}\right|\right)<b+\left|\alpha^{\prime}\right|\leq\sum_{i=1}^{j}\left(s-\left|\alpha_{i}\right|\right),

where we assume that
\sum_{i=1}^{0}a_{i}=0
. Thus there exists

such that

b+\left|\alpha^{\prime}\right|=\sum_{i=1}^{j-1}\left(s-\left|\alpha_{i}\right|\right)+\left(s_{0}-\left|\alpha_{j}\right|\right)

(3.9)

and
s\geq s_{0}>\left|\alpha_{j}\right|
. Hence we have

I=\left\|\left|u\right|^{\sigma+1-q}|x|^{-\left(s_{0}-\left|\alpha_{j}\right|\right)}D^{\alpha_{j}}u\prod_{i=1}^{j-1}|x|^{-\left(s-\left|\alpha_{i}\right|\right)}D^{\alpha_{i}}u\prod_{k=j+1}^{q}D^{\alpha_{k}}u\right\|_{p},

(3.10)

where we assume that
\prod_{i=1}^{0}a_{i}=1

) and (

\frac{1}{p}=\left(\sigma+1-q\right)\left(\frac{1}{r}-\frac{s}{n}\right)+\frac{j-1}{r}+\sum_{i=j+1}^{q}\left(\frac{1}{r}-\frac{s-\left|\alpha_{i}\right|}{n}\right)+\frac{1}{r}-\frac{s-s_{0}}{n}.

\frac{1}{a_{0}}:=\frac{1}{r}-\frac{s}{n},\penalty\ \frac{1}{a_{i}}:=\frac{1}{r}-\frac{s-\left|\alpha_{i}\right|}{n},\penalty\ \frac{1}{a}:=\frac{1}{r}-\frac{s-s_{0}}{n},

and using (
3.10
), (
3.11
), Hölder inequality and Corollary
2.9
, we have

\displaystyle\begin{split}I&\lesssim\left\|u\right\|_{a_{0}}^{\sigma+1-q}\left\||x|^{-\left(s_{0}-\left|\alpha_{j}\right|\right)}D^{\alpha_{j}}u\right\|_{a}\prod_{i=1}^{j-1}\left\||x|^{-\left(s-\left|\alpha_{i}\right|\right)}D^{\alpha_{i}}u\right\|_{r}\prod_{i=j+1}^{q}\left\|D^{\alpha_{i}}u\right\|_{a_{i}}\\
&\lesssim\left\|u\right\|_{\dot{H}_{r}^{s}}^{\sigma+1-q}\left\|D^{\alpha_{j}}u\right\|_{\dot{H}_{a}^{s_{0}-\left|\alpha_{j}\right|}}\prod_{i=1}^{j-1}\left\|D^{\alpha_{i}}u\right\|_{\dot{H}_{r}^{s-\left|\alpha_{i}\right|}}\prod_{i=j+1}^{q}\left\|u\right\|_{\dot{H}_{a_{i}}^{\left|\alpha_{i}\right|}}\\
&\lesssim\left\|u\right\|_{\dot{H}_{r}^{s}}^{\sigma}\left\|u\right\|_{\dot{H}_{a}^{s_{0}}}\lesssim\left\|u\right\|_{\dot{H}_{r}^{s}}^{\sigma+1},\end{split}

where we use the embeddings:
\dot{H}_{r}^{s}\subset L^{a_{0}}
,
\dot{H}_{r}^{s}\subset\dot{H}_{a}^{s_{0}}
and
\dot{H}_{r}^{s}\subset\dot{H}_{a_{i}}^{\left|\alpha_{i}\right|}
.
∎

If

Lemma 3.12

Let

\left|\alpha^{\prime\prime}\right|=0

So it suffices to show that

&\penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \penalty\ \lesssim\left\||x|^{-b-\left[s\right]-1}\left|u\right|^{\sigma}u\right\|_{p_{1}}+\left\||x|^{-b-\left[s\right]}\left|u\right|^{\sigma}\partial_{x_{i}}u\right\|_{p_{1}}\equiv II_{1}+II_{2}\end{split}

Case 2.

\left|\alpha^{\prime\prime}\right|\geq 1

(3.14)
