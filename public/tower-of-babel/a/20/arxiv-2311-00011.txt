Characterization of entire solutions of systems of quadratic trinomial difference and partial differential difference equations in ℂ n \mathbb{C}^{n}
Goutam Haldar

Abstract
. In this paper we establish some results about the existence and precise forms of finite order entire solutions of some systems of quadratic trinomial functional equations one of which in ℂ n \mathbb{C}^{n} , n ∈ ℕ n\in\mathbb{N} and other two in ℂ 2 \mathbb{C}^{2} . Our results are the generalizations and improvements of the previous theorems given by Xu-Cao [ 42 , 43 ] and Xu-Jiang [ 45 ] . Moreover, we exhibit some examples in support of our claims.

Proof of Theorem

Let

(4.1)

Then first equation of (

\displaystyle(1+w)u^{2}+(1-w)v^{2}=e^{g_{1}(z)},

(4.2)

Let

\displaystyle\gamma_{1}(z)=p_{1}(z)+\frac{1}{2}g_{1}(z),\;\;\gamma_{2}(z)=-p_{1}(z)+\frac{1}{2}g_{1}(z).

(4.3)

Then from (

) and (
4.3

\displaystyle u=\frac{1}{2\sqrt{1+w}}\left[e^{\gamma_{1}}+e^{\gamma_{2}}\right],\;\;v=\frac{1}{2i\sqrt{1-w}}\left[e^{\gamma_{1}}-e^{\gamma_{2}}\right].

Therefore, in view of (

) and (

\displaystyle f(z)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\left[A_{1}e^{\gamma_{1}}+A_{2}e^{\gamma_{2}}\right],\;\;g(z+c)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\left[A_{2}e^{\gamma_{1}}+A_{1}e^{\gamma_{2}}\right].

(4.5)

Similarly, in view of the second equation of (

), there exists a polynomial

such that

(4.6)

where
