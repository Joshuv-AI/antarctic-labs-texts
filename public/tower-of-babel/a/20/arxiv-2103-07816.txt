Painlevé V for a Jacobi unitary ensemble with random singularities
Mengkun Zhu, Chuanzhong Li, Yang Chen

Abstract
In this paper, we focus on the relationship between the fifth Painlevé equation and a Jacobi weight perturbed with random singularities, w ⁡ ( z ) = ( 1 − z 2 ) α ​ e − t z 2 − k 2 , z , k ∈ [ − 1 , 1 ] , α , t > 0 . w(z)=\left(1-z^{2}\right)^{\alpha}{\rm e}^{-\frac{t}{z^{2}-k^{2}}},~~~z,k\in[-1,1],~\alpha,t>0. Introduction

The study of random matrix theory (RMT) and orthogonal polynomials (OPs) for varying weights is often related to Painlevé equations. For example, the properties of Hankel determinants which are always playing the most fundamental role in RMT, as well as the asymptotic behavior of the recurrence coefficients with respect to OPs, can be found by the analysis of the associated particular Painlevé equations. Below we include a few related research works.
