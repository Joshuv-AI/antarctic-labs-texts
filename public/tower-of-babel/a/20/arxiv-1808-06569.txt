Splitter Theorems for Graph Immersions
Matt DeVos, Mahdieh Malekian

Abstract
We establish splitter theorems for graph immersions for two families of graphs, k k -edge-connected graphs, with k k even, and 3-edge-connected, internally 4-edge-connected graphs. As a corollary, we prove that every 3 3 -edge-connected, internally 4 4 -edge-connected graph on at least seven vertices that immerses K 5 K_{5} also has K 3 , 3 K_{3,3} as an immersion.
