1.
Introduction and main results

In this paper we define a class of continuous nonlinearities
f
so that the problem

\displaystyle\begin{gathered}\Delta u+f(u)=0,\quad x\in\mathbb{R}^{N},N>2,\\
\lim\limits_{|x|\to\infty}u(x)=0,\end{gathered}

has multiple positive solutions. To this end we consider the radial version of (
1

\displaystyle\begin{gathered}u^{\prime\prime}+\frac{N-1}{r}u^{\prime}+f(u)=0,\quad u(r)>0\mbox{ for all }r>0,\quad N>2,\\
u^{\prime}(0)=0,\quad\lim\limits_{r\to\infty}u(r)=0,\end{gathered}

where all throughout this article
u^{\prime}
denotes differentiation of

with respect to
r

Any nonconstant solution to (
1

u(x)>0
for all
x\in\mathbb{R}^{N}
are referred to as a first bound state solution, or a ground state solution.

The uniqueness problem for positive solutions to problem (
1

[

]

The multiplicity problem has been studied for the following non-autonomous problem
