Torsor structure of level-raising operators
A. Zuevsky

Abstract
. We consider families of reductive complexes related by level-raising operators and originating from an associative algebra. In the main theorem it is shown that the multiple cohomology of that complexes is given by the factor space of products of reduction operators. In particular, we compute explicit torsor structure of the genus g g multiple cohomology of the families of horizontal complexes with spaces of of canonical converging reductive differential forms for a C 2 C_{2} -cofinite quasiconformal strong-conformal field theory-type vertex operator algebra associated to a complex curve. That provides an equivalence of multiple cohomology to factor spaces of products of sums of reduction functions with actions of the group of local coordinates automorphisms. AMS Classification: 53C12, 57R20, 17B69
