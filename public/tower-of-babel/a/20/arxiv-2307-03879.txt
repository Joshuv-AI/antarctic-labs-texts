A direct approach to sharp Li-Yau estimates on closed manifolds with negative Ricci lower bound
XingYu Song, Ling Wu and Meng Zhu

Abstract
. Recently, Qi S.Zhang [ 30 ] has derived a sharp Li-Yau estimate for positive solutions of the heat equation on closed Riemannian manifolds with the Ricci curvature bounded below by a negative constant. The proof is based on an integral iteration argument which utilizes Hamilton’s gradient estimate, heat kernel Gaussian bounds and parabolic Harnack inequality. In this paper, we show that the sharp Li-Yau estimate can actually be obtained directly following the classical maximum principle argument, which simplifies the proof in [ 30 ] . In addition, we apply the same idea to the heat and conjugate heat equations under the Ricci flow and prove some Li-Yau type estimates with optimal coefficients.



1.
Introduction

The renowned Li-Yau estimate
[
20
]
states that on an
n
-dimensional complete Riemannian manifold
(M^{n},g)
with the Ricci curvature
\Ric\geq-K
,
K\geq 0
, any positive solution
u
of the heat equation

\left(\Delta-\frac{\partial}{\partial t}\right)u=0

(1.1)

satisfies

\frac{|\nabla u|^{2}}{u^{2}}-\alpha\frac{\partial_{t}u}{u}\leq\frac{n\alpha^{2}}{2t}+\frac{n\alpha^{2}K}{2(\alpha-1)},\ \forall\alpha>1,\ t>0.

(1.2)

Although (
1.2
) was discovered earlier by Aronson-Bénilan on Euclidean spaces
[
1
]
, Li and Yau were the first to prove it on general differentiable manifolds and demonstrated numerous crucial applications in the research of differential geometry and geometric analysis. The Li-Yau estimate is closely related to parabolic Harnack inequality, Sobolev and Poincaré inequalities, volume doubling property, heat kernel estimates, estimates of the Green’s functions, eigenvalue estimates, etc. Even more powerfully, when the Ricci curvature is nonnegative, i.e.,
K=0
, one can let
\alpha
in (
1.2
) tend to
1
and obtain the sharp Li-Yau estimate

\frac{|\nabla u|^{2}}{u^{2}}-\frac{\partial_{t}u}{u}\leq\frac{n}{2t},

(1.3)

which in term gives an optimal heat kernel estimate, as well as the laplacian and Bishop-Gromov volume comparison theorems.

Due to its significancy, many efforts have been made to improve or generalize Li-Yau estimates. Assuming
\Ric
bounded below by a negative constant, the coefficient
\alpha
in (
1.2
) was improved in e.g.
[
27
,
14
,
19
,
24
,
3
]
. The Li-Yau estimate was also generalized to various linear and semilinear parabolic equations, as well as matrix versions (see e.g.
[
14
,
7
,
18
,
8
]
). Li-Yau type estimates under time-dependent metrics, for example the Ricci flow and Kähler-Ricci flow, were studied in the past two decades, and are very useful tools in characterizing the singularities of the Ricci flow (see e.g.
[
15
,
16
,
6
,
2
,
17
,
23
,
10
,
9
,
22
]
). Furthermore, the Ricci lower bound assumption for Li-Yau estimate has recently been relaxed to integral Ricci conditions and Bakry-Émery Ricci curvature condition, or even the
RCD^{*}(K,N)
spaces (see e.g.
[
32
,
33
,
4
,
12
,
25
,
29
,
21
]
).

Estimate (
1.3
) is sharp in the sense that the coefficient of
\frac{\partial_{t}u}{u}
is equal to
1
, and the equality is achieved by the heat kernel
(4\pi t)^{-n/2}exp\{-\frac{|x-y|^{2}}{4t}\}
on
\mathbb{R}^{n}
. However, estimate (
1.2
) is not sharp for
K>0
. For example, Hamilton
[
14
]
showed that

\frac{|\nabla u|^{2}}{u^{2}}-e^{2Kt}\frac{\partial_{t}u}{u}\leq e^{4Kt}\frac{n}{2t},

(1.4)

where the constant
\alpha>1
is improved to an exponential function approaching
1
as
t
tending to
0
.
Indeed, an open question asks if one can take
\alpha=1
even for
K>0
.

Very recently, Qi S. Zhang
[
30
]
has invented a method which answers the above question affirmatively for closed manifolds. More precisely, he showed that

Theorem 1.1
.

(
[
30
]
)
Let
(M^{n},g)
be an
n
-dimensional closed Riemannian manifold with
\Ric\geq-K
for some constant
K\geq 0
, and
u
a positive solution of the heat equation (
1.1
) on
M\times[0,+\infty)
. Then there exist constants
c_{1}
and
c_{2}
depending only on
n
such that

t\left(\frac{|\nabla u|^{2}}{u^{2}}-\frac{\partial_{t}u}{u}\right)\leq\frac{n}{2}+\sqrt{2nK(1+Kt)(1+t)}diam_{M}+\sqrt{K(1+Kt)(c_{1}+c_{2}K)t}

(1.5)

for all
(x,t)\in M\times[0,+\infty)
, where
diam_{M}
is the diameter of
M
.

The proof in
[
30
]
is mainly based on an integral iteration argument with dedicated estimates, in which Hamilton’s gradient estimate for the heat equation
[
14
]
, Gaussian bounds of the heat kernel and differential Harnack inequality are used.

In this paper, we observe that Theorem
1.1
can actually be obtained by directly following the classical maximum principle arguments, and the proof can be simplified. Our proof is inspired by the usage of Hamilton’s gradient estimate and the heat kernel bounds in
[
30
]
.

In Theorem
1.1
, the Laplace operator is associated with a fixed metric. We can also consider the heat equation and conjugate heat equation under the Ricci flow, in which the Laplace operator evolves as the metric changes in time. For the heat equation, we show that

Theorem 1.2
.

Let
M^{n}
be an n-dimensional closed Riemannian manifold, and
g(x,t)
,
(x,t)\in M\times[0,T]
, a solution to the Ricci flow
\frac{\partial}{\partial t}g(x,t)=-2\Ric(x,t)
on
M

K_{1},K_{2}\geq 0
,
-K_{1}g(x,t)\leq\Ric(x,t)\leq K_{2}g(x,t)

\forall(x,t)\in M\times[0,T]
. Let
u
be a smooth positive solution of the heat equation
\left(\Delta_{t}-\frac{\partial}{\partial t}\right)u=0

\Delta_{t}
is the Laplace operator of the metric
g(x,t)
. Then there exist constants
c_{5}
and
c_{6}
depending only on

such that

t\left(\frac{|\nabla u|^{2}}{u^{2}}-\frac{\partial_{t}u}{u}\right)\leq\frac{n}{2}+nK_{3}t+\sqrt{c_{5}K_{1}[e^{c_{6}K_{3}t}(t+d^{2}_{0}(M))]}

for all

(M^{n},g(x,t))

Remark 1.3
.

When

, Theorem

[

]
.

In Theorem
1.2
, the Ricci curvature is assumed to be uniformly bounded up to time
T
before the singular time. The main reason is that the heat kernel Gaussian bounds may not hold uniformly till the singular time. This assumption can be relaxed to a so-called ”Type-I” Ricci curvature bound, if we assume in addition that Perelman’s
\nu
entropy is bounded from below.

Recall that Perelman’s
\mathcal{W}
functional is defined as

\mathcal{W}(g,f,\tau)=(4\pi\tau)^{-\frac{n}{2}}\int_{M}(\tau(R+|\nabla f|^{2})+f-n)e^{-f}dg,

where
f\in C^{\infty}(M)
and
\tau>0
. Then the
\mu
and
\nu
entropy functionals are

\mu[g,\tau]:=\inf\left\{\mathcal{W}(g,f,\tau);\ f\in C^{\infty}(M)\ and\ \int_{M}(4\pi\tau)^{-\frac{n}{2}}e^{-f}dg=1\right\},

and

\nu[g,\tau]:=\inf\limits_{s\in[0,\tau]}\mu[g,s].

Theorem 1.4
.

Let
M^{n}
be an n-dimensional closed Riemannian manifold and
g(x,t),(x,t)\in M\times[0,2)
, a solution to the Ricci flow
\frac{\partial}{\partial t}g(x,t)=-2\Ric(x,t)
on
M
. Assume that for some constant
K\geq 0
,
-\frac{K}{2-t}g(x,t)\leq\Ric(x,t)\leq\frac{K}{2-t}g(x,t)
and
\nu[g_{0},4]\geq-nK
,
\forall(x,t)\in M\times[0,2)
. Let
u
be a smooth positive solution of the heat equation
\left(\Delta_{t}-\frac{\partial}{\partial t}\right)u(x,t)=0
on
M\times[0,2)
. Then there exists a constant
c_{9}
depending on
n,K
such that

t\left(\frac{|\nabla u|^{2}}{u^{2}}-\frac{\partial_{t}u}{u}\right)\leq\frac{n}{2}+\frac{nKt}{2-t}+\sqrt{\frac{Kc_{9}}{2-t}\left(t+d_{0}^{2}(M)t+d_{0}^{2}(M)\right)}

for all
(x,t)\in M\times[0,2)
, where
d_{0}(M)
is the diameter of
(M,g(x,0))
.

Finally, we consider the Li-Yau estimate for positive solutions of the conjugate heat equation under the Ricci flow.

.

Let
M^{n}
be an n-dimensional closed Riemannian manifold and

2.
The heat equation with fixed metric

In this section, we prove the sharp Li-Yau estimate (
1.5
) .

Proof of Theorem
1.1
.
Let
u=u(x,t)
be a positive solution of the heat equation on
M\times[0,+\infty)
. Denote

Q=\frac{|\nabla u|^{2}}{u^{2}}-\frac{\partial_{t}u}{u}=|\nabla\ln u|^{2}-\partial_{t}\ln u=-\Delta\ln u.

(2.1)

From the Bochner formula and the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality, it follows that

\displaystyle(\Delta-\partial_{t})Q+2\left<\nabla\ln u,\nabla Q\right>

\displaystyle=2\left[|\Hess\ln u|^{2}+\Ric(\nabla\ln u,\nabla\ln u)\right]

(2.2)

\displaystyle\geq 2\left[\frac{Q^{2}}{n}-K|\nabla\ln u|^{2}\right].

Hence,

(\Delta-\partial_{t})(tQ)+2\left<\nabla\ln u,\nabla(tQ)\right>\geq 2t\left(\frac{1}{n}Q^{2}-K|\nabla\ln u|^{2}\right)-Q.

(2.3)

For any
T>0
, the maximum of
tQ
on
M\times[0,T]
is achieved at some
(x_{0},t_{0})
. We may assume that
tQ
is positive at
(x_{0},t_{0})
, for otherwise the theorem is trivial. Thus,
t_{0}>0
, and at
(x_{0},t_{0})
we have

\nabla(tQ)=0,\ \frac{\partial}{\partial t}(tQ)\geq 0,\ and\ \Delta(tQ)\leq 0.

Evaluating (
2.3
) at
(x_{0},t_{0})
, we obtain

0\geq\frac{2t_{0}}{n}Q^{2}(x_{0},t_{0})-Q(x_{0},t_{0})-2Kt_{0}|\nabla\ln u|^{2}(x_{0},t_{0}).

Multiplying both sides above by
t_{0}
gives

0\geq\frac{2}{n}(t_{0}Q(x_{0},t_{0}))^{2}-t_{0}Q(x_{0},t_{0})-2Kt_{0}^{2}|\nabla\ln u|^{2}(x_{0},t_{0}),

which infers

t_{0}Q(x_{0},t_{0})\leq\frac{n}{2}+\sqrt{nK}t_{0}|\nabla\ln u|(x_{0},t_{0}).

(2.4)

Next we will bound
t_{0}|\nabla\ln u|(x_{0},t_{0})
. From
[
30
]
, the heat kernel
H(x,t,y)
,
x,y\in M
,
t>0
satisfies the estimates

t|\nabla_{x}\ln H(x,t,y)|^{2}\leq 2(1+Kt)\left(2C_{1}Kt+4C_{2}^{2}K+\frac{diam_{M}^{2}}{t}+2\ln C_{3}\right),\ \forall\ 0<t\leq 8,

(2.5)

and

t|\nabla_{x}\ln H(x,t,y)|^{2}\leq 2(1+Kt)(n\ln 2+2C_{4}K+diam_{M}^{2}),\ \forall\ t\geq 8,

(2.6)

where the positive constants
C_{i}
,
1\leq i\leq 4
, depend only on
n
. Actually, (
2.5
) comes from Hamilton estimate
[
14
]
and Gaussian bounds of the heat kernel
[
20
]
, and (
2.6
) relies further on the parabolic Harnack inequality.

Combining (
2.5
) and (
2.6
), we deduce that

t^{2}|\nabla_{x}\ln H(x,t,y)|^{2}\leq 2(1+KT)(C_{5}T+C_{6}KT+diam_{M}^{2}+diam_{M}^{2}T),\ \forall\ 0<t\leq T.

(2.7)

Since
T
is arbitrary, we conclude that

t^{2}|\nabla_{x}\ln H(x,t,y)|^{2}\leq 2(1+Kt)(C_{5}t+C_{6}Kt+diam_{M}^{2}+diam_{M}^{2}t),\ \forall\ t>0\ and\ x,y\in M.

(2.8)

A short argument from
[
28
]
implies that the bound (
2.8
) also holds if one replaces the heat kernel by any positive solution
u(x,t)
of the heat equation. For completeness, we present the proof below. By Duhamel’s principle, we have
u(x,t)=\int_{M}H(x,t,y)u(y,0)dy
. Then

\displaystyle|\nabla_{x}u(x,t)|^{2}

\displaystyle=\int_{M}\int_{M}\left<\nabla_{x}H(x,t,y),\nabla_{x}H(x,t,z)\right>u(y,0)u(z,0)dydz

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{2}\int_{M}\int_{M}\left(\frac{|\nabla_{x}H(x,t,y)|^{2}}{H(x,t,y)}H(x,t,z)+\frac{|\nabla_{x}H(x,t,z)|^{2}}{H(x,t,z)}H(x,t,y)\right)u(y,0)u(z,0)dydz.

Using (
2.8
) in the integrand, one has

t^{2}|\nabla\ln u(x,t)|^{2}\leq 2(1+Kt)(C_{5}t+C_{6}Kt+diam_{M}^{2}+diam_{M}^{2}t),\ \forall\ t\geq 0\ and\ x\in M.

(2.9)

Plugging (
2.9
) into (
2.4
), we obtain

TQ(x,T)\leq t_{0}Q(x_{0},t_{0})\leq\frac{n}{2}+\sqrt{K(1+KT)(C_{7}T+C_{8}KT)}+\sqrt{2nK(1+KT)(1+T)}diam_{M}.

(2.10)

By the arbitrariness of
T
again, Theorem
1.1
follows from (
2.10
). ∎

One may replace (
2.4
) by the Li-Yau type inequalities of Yau

Remark 2.2

If

Remark 2.3
.

Under the same condition in Theorem

, for any

, using Theorem

and (

), one gets that there exist constants
c_{i}

(i=1,2,3,4)
depending only on

such that

\displaystyle t\left(\alpha\frac{|\nabla u|^{2}}{u^{2}}-\frac{u_{t}}{u}\right)

\displaystyle=t\left(\frac{|\nabla u|^{2}}{u^{2}}-\frac{u_{t}}{u}\right)+(\alpha-1)t\frac{|\nabla u|^{2}}{u^{2}}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{n}{2}+\sqrt{2nK(1+Kt)(1+t)}diam_{M}+\sqrt{K(1+Kt)(c_{1}+c_{2}K)t}

\displaystyle+2(\alpha-1)\frac{1+Kt}{t}\left(c_{3}t+c_{4}Kt+diam_{M}^{2}+diam_{M}^{2}t\right)

for all

.

The heat equation under the Ricci flow with uniform Ricci bound

In this section, we consider Li-Yau estimates in the Ricci flow case and prove Theorem

. First, we need the following Hamilton type estimate by Qi S.Zhang

]
and X. Cao-R. Hamilton

]

Lemma 3.1
.

(
[
31
]
,
[
9
]
)
Suppose that
M^{n}
is an
n
-dimensional closed manifold, and
g(x,t)
,

is a solution to the Ricci flow
\frac{\partial}{\partial t}g(x,t)=-2\Ric(x,t)
on
M
. Let

be a positive solution of the heat equation
\left(\Delta_{t}-\frac{\partial}{\partial t}\right)u(x,t)=0

. Then it holds that

\frac{|\nabla u|}{u}\leq\sqrt{\frac{1}{t}\ln\frac{\A}{u}}

for all

with
\A=\sup\limits_{x\in M}u(x,0)

In
[
34
]

Lemma 3.2
.

(
[
34
]
)
Let
(M^{n},g(x,t))_{t\in[0,T)}
be a complete solution to the Ricci flow
\frac{\partial}{\partial t}g(x,t)=-2\Ric(x,t)
,
(x,t)\in M\times[0,T)
for

. Suppose that
\Ric(x,t)\geq-K_{1}g(x,t)
on

for some nonnegative constant
K_{1}
and
\Lambda=\int_{0}^{T}\sup\limits_{x\in M}|\Ric|(x,t)dt<\infty
. Let
H(x,t;y,l)
be the heat kernel of the heat equation
\left(\Delta_{t}-\frac{\partial}{\partial t}\right)u(x,t)=0
with
0\leq l<t<T
. Then we have the following upper and lower bounds:

H(x,t;y,l)\leq\tilde{c}_{1}(n)e^{\tilde{c}_{2}(n)e^{{\tilde{c}_{3}(n)\Lambda+{\tilde{c}_{4}(n)K_{1}T}}}}\min\left\{\frac{e^{-\frac{d_{t}^{2}(x,y)}{8e^{4K_{1}T}(t-l)}}}{\vol_{l}(B_{l}(x,\sqrt{\frac{t-l}{8}}))},\ \frac{e^{-\frac{d_{t}^{2}(x,y)}{8e^{4K_{1}T}(t-l)}}}{\vol_{t}(B_{t}(y,\sqrt{\frac{t-l}{8}}))}\right\}

and

H(x,t;y,l)\geq\frac{\tilde{c}_{5}(n)e^{-\tilde{c}_{6}(n)e^{\tilde{c}_{7}(n)\Lambda+\tilde{c}_{8}(n)K_{1}T}}e^{-\frac{4d_{t}^{2}(x,y)}{t-l}}}{\vol_{t}(B_{t}(y,\sqrt{\frac{t-l}{8}}))},

where
B_{t}(x,\sqrt{\frac{t-l}{8}})
is the geodesic ball of radius
\sqrt{\frac{t-l}{8}}
in

at time
t
and,
\vol_{t}(.)
denotes the volume of a region at time

.

Proof of Theorem
1.2
.
Denote by
f=\ln u
and
F=t(|\nabla f|^{2}-f_{t})=-t\Delta f
. By direct computation, we obtain

\Delta F=t(2|\Hess f|^{2}+2\left<\nabla f,\nabla\Delta f\right>+2\Ric(\nabla f,\nabla f)-(\Delta f)_{t}+2\left<\Ric,\Hess f\right>),

where we have used the identity

\Delta(f_{t})=(\Delta f)_{t}-2\left<\Ric,\Hess f\right>.

On the other hand,

F_{t}=(|\nabla f|^{2}-f_{t})+t(2\left<\nabla f,\nabla f_{t}\right>+2\Ric(\nabla f,\nabla f)-f_{tt}).

Since
(\Delta-\partial_{t})f=-|\nabla f|^{2}
, we have

\displaystyle 2t

\displaystyle\left<\nabla f,\nabla\Delta f\right>-t(\Delta f)_{t}-2t\left<\nabla f,\nabla f_{t}\right>+tf_{tt}

\displaystyle=2t\left<\nabla f,\nabla(\Delta f-f_{t})\right>+t(f_{t}-\Delta f)_{t}

\displaystyle=-2t\left<\nabla f,\nabla|\nabla f|^{2}\right>+t(|\nabla f|^{2})_{t}

\displaystyle=-2\left<\nabla f,\nabla(F+tf_{t})\right>+2t\left<\nabla f,\nabla f_{t}\right>+2t\Ric(\nabla f,\nabla f)

\displaystyle=-2\left<\nabla f,\nabla F\right>+2t\Ric(\nabla f,\nabla f).

Therefore,

(\Delta-\partial_{t})F=-2\left<\nabla f,\nabla F\right>+2t(|\Hess f|^{2}+\left<\Ric,\Hess f\right>)+2t\Ric(\nabla f,\nabla f)-(|\nabla f|^{2}-f_{t}).

(3.1)

For any
0<a<1
, by the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality and the bound of the Ricci curvature, one has

\displaystyle|\Hess f|^{2}+\left<\Ric,\Hess f\right>

\displaystyle=\sum_{i,j=1}^{n}(f^{2}_{ij}+R_{ij}f_{ij})

(3.2)

\displaystyle\geq\sum_{i,j=1}^{n}\left(f_{ij}^{2}-(1-a)f^{2}_{ij}-\frac{1}{4(1-a)}R_{ij}^{2}\right)

\displaystyle\geq\sum_{i,j=1}^{n}af_{ij}^{2}-\frac{n}{4(1-a)}K_{3}^{2}

\displaystyle\geq\frac{a}{n}(\Delta f)^{2}-\frac{n}{4(1-a)}K_{3}^{2}

\displaystyle=\frac{a}{n}(f_{t}-|\nabla f|^{2})^{2}-\frac{n}{4(1-a)}K_{3}^{2}.

Plugging (
3.2
) into (
3.1
), we see that

(\Delta-\partial_{t})F+2\left<\nabla f,\nabla F\right>\geq\frac{2a}{n}\frac{F^{2}}{t}-\frac{F}{t}-\frac{nt}{2(1-a)}K_{3}^{2}-2K_{1}t|\nabla f|^{2}

(3.3)

for any
0<a<1
.

For any
0<\tau\leq T
, let
(x_{0},t_{0})\in M\times[0,\tau]
be a maximum point of
F
. Without loss of generality, we may assume
F(x_{0},t_{0})>0
. Then
t_{0}>0
, and by the maximum principle, we have

\Delta F(x_{0},t_{0})\leq 0,\ \frac{\partial}{\partial t}F(x_{0},t_{0})\geq 0,\ and\ \nabla F(x_{0},t_{0})=0.

(3.4)

It follows from (
3.3
) and (
3.4
) that at
(x_{0},t_{0})
,

\frac{2a}{n}F^{2}(x_{0},t_{0})-F(x_{0},t_{0})-\frac{nt_{0}^{2}}{2(1-a)}K_{3}^{2}-2K_{1}t_{0}^{2}|\nabla f|^{2}(x_{0},t_{0})\leq 0,

which yields

F(x_{0},t_{0})\leq\frac{n}{4a}\left(1+\sqrt{1+\frac{4at_{0}^{2}}{1-a}K_{3}^{2}}\right)+\sqrt{\frac{n}{a}K_{1}}t_{0}|\nabla f|(x_{0},t_{0}).

By choosing
a=\frac{1+K_{3}t_{0}}{1+2K_{3}t_{0}}\in[\frac{1}{2},1)
, one has

F(x_{0},t_{0})\leq nK_{3}t_{0}+\frac{n}{2}+\sqrt{2nK_{1}}t_{0}|\nabla f|(x_{0},t_{0}).

(3.5)

Again, to estimate
t|\nabla f|
, we first find a bound for
t|\nabla\ln H(x,t;y,0)|
with
x,y\in M
and
t\in(0,\tau]
, where
H(x,t;y,0)
is the heat kernel of the heat equation
\left(\Delta_{t}-\frac{\partial}{\partial t}\right)H(x,t;y,0)=0
.

For a time
0<t_{1}\leq\frac{\tau}{2}
, we denote by
h(x,t)=H(x,t+t_{1};y,0)
with
x,y\in M
and
t\in[0,t_{1}]
.

Since
h(x,t)
is a positive solution of the heat equation, by Lemma
3.1
, we derive

t|\nabla\ln H(x,t+t_{1};y,0)|^{2}\leq\ln\frac{A}{H(x,t+t_{1};y,0)},\ (x,t)\in M\times(0,t_{1}],

(3.6)

where
A=\sup\limits_{x\in M}H(x,t_{1};y,0)
.

According to Lemma
3.2
, since
\Lambda\leq 2\sqrt{n}K_{3}t_{1}
, the following upper and lower bounds for
h(x,t)
hold

\frac{\tilde{c}_{5}(n)e^{-\tilde{c}_{6}(n)e^{\tilde{c}_{10}(n)K_{3}t_{1}}}e^{-\frac{4d^{2}_{t+t_{1}}(M)}{t_{1}}}}{\vol_{t+t_{1}}(B_{t+t_{1}}(y,\sqrt{\frac{t_{1}}{4}}))}\leq h(x,t)\leq\frac{\tilde{c}_{1}(n)e^{\tilde{c}_{2}(n)e^{\tilde{c}_{9}(n)K_{3}t_{1}}}}{\vol_{t+t_{1}}(B_{t+t_{1}}(y,\sqrt{\frac{t+t_{1}}{8}}))},\ t\in[0,t_{1}].

The upper bound gives

A=\sup\limits_{x\in M}H(x,t_{1};y,0)\leq\frac{\tilde{c}_{1}(n)e^{\tilde{c}_{2}(n)e^{\tilde{c}_{9}(n)K_{3}t_{1}}}}{\vol_{t_{1}}(B_{t_{1}}(y,\sqrt{\frac{t_{1}}{8}}))},

which, combining with the lower bound of
h(x,t)
, yields that

\frac{A}{H(x,t+t_{1};y,0)}\leq\tilde{c}_{11}(n)e^{\tilde{c}_{12}(n)e^{\tilde{c}_{13}(n)K_{3}t_{1}}}e^{\frac{4d^{2}_{t+t_{1}}(M)}{t_{1}}}\frac{\vol_{t+t_{1}}(B_{t+t_{1}}(y,\sqrt{\frac{t_{1}}{4}}))}{\vol_{t_{1}}(B_{t_{1}}(y,\sqrt{\frac{t_{1}}{8}}))}.

(3.7)

The uniform boundedness of the Ricci curvature implies that
g(x,t)
is uniformly equivalent to the initial metric
g(0)
(see Corollary 6.11 in
[
11
]
), that is ,

e^{-2K_{2}t}g(0)\leq g(x,t)\leq e^{2K_{1}t}g(0).

(3.8)

Then, according to the Bishop-Gromov volume comparison theorem, we have

\displaystyle\frac{\vol_{t+t_{1}}(B_{t+t_{1}}(y,\sqrt{\frac{t_{1}}{4}}))}{\vol_{t_{1}}(B_{t_{1}}(y,\sqrt{\frac{t_{1}}{8}}))}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{e^{3nt_{1}K_{3}}\vol_{t_{1}}(B_{t_{1}}(y,\sqrt{\frac{t_{1}}{4}}))}{\vol_{t_{1}}(B_{t_{1}}(y,\sqrt{\frac{t_{1}}{8}}))}

\displaystyle\leq e^{3nt_{1}K_{3}}(\sqrt{2})^{n}e^{\sqrt{(n-1)K_{3}t_{1}}}

\displaystyle\leq e^{\tilde{c}_{14}(n)(K_{3}t_{1}+1)}.

Plugging this into (
3.7
), we see that (
3.6
) becomes

t|\nabla\ln H(x,t+t_{1};y,0)|^{2}\leq\tilde{c}_{15}(n)\left(e^{\tilde{c}_{16}(n)K_{3}t_{1}}+\frac{d^{2}_{t+t_{1}}(M)}{t_{1}}\right),\ \forall t\in(0,t_{1}].

Taking
t=t_{1}
and using the arbitrariness of
t_{1}
, one concludes that

t^{2}|\nabla\ln H(x,t;y,0)|^{2}\leq\tilde{c}_{17}(n)\left(e^{\tilde{c}_{19}(n)K_{3}t}(t+d^{2}_{0}(M))\right),\forall\ 0<t\leq\tau.

(3.9)

Since the solution
u
to the heat equation can also be written as

u(x,t)=\int_{M}H(x,t;y,0)u(y,0)d\mu_{0}(y),

where
d\mu_{0}
is the volume element at
t=0
, the same bound as in (
3.9
) actually holds for any positive solution of the heat equation, that is,

t^{2}|\nabla\ln u(x,t)|^{2}\leq\tilde{c}_{17}(n)\left(e^{\tilde{c}_{19}(n)K_{3}t}(t+d^{2}_{0}(M))\right),\forall\ 0\leq t\leq\tau.

(3.10)

Combining (
3.10
) with (
3.5
), we obtain

F(x,\tau)\leq F(x_{0},t_{0})\leq nK_{3}\tau+\frac{n}{2}+\sqrt{\tilde{c}_{20}(n)K_{1}[e^{\tilde{c}_{19}(n)K_{3}\tau}(\tau+d^{2}_{0}(M))]}.

(3.11)

Since
\tau\in(0,T]
can be chosen arbitrarily, it finishes the proof of Theorem
1.2
. ∎

Remark 3.3
.

Under the assumptions and notations as in Theorem
1.2
, similar to Remark
2.3
, we have for any
\alpha>1
, there exist constants
c_{i}

(i=5,6,7,8)
depending only on
n
such that

\displaystyle t\left(\alpha\frac{|\nabla u|^{2}}{u^{2}}-\frac{\partial_{t}u}{u}\right)

\displaystyle\leq\frac{n}{2}+nK_{3}t+\sqrt{c_{5}K_{1}[e^{c_{6}K_{3}t}(t+d^{2}_{0}(M))]}

\displaystyle+(\alpha-1)\frac{1}{t}c_{7}e^{c_{8}K_{3}t}(t+d_{0}^{2}(M))

for all
(x,t)\in M\times(0,T]
.

4.
The heat equation under the Ricci flow with blowing-up Ricci curvature

To prove Theorem
1.4
, we will need the following Gaussian upper and lower bounds for the heat kernel near the singular time.

Lemma 4.1
.

(
[
13
]
)
Let
(M^{n},g(x,t))_{t\in[-2,0)}
be a closed solution of Ricci flow satisfying
\nu[g_{-2},4]\geq-K
and
|R(x,t)|\leq K|t|^{-1}
or some constant
K\geq 0
. Let
H(x,t;y,l)
be the heat kernel of the equation
\left(\Delta_{t}-\frac{\partial}{\partial t}\right)H(x,t;y,l)=0
with
-2\leq l<t<0
and
x,y\in M
. Then, for any
x,y\in M
and
-\frac{1}{2}\leq l<t<0
, there exists a constant
C^{*}=C^{*}(K,n)
such that

\frac{1}{C^{*}(t-l)^{\frac{n}{2}}}e^{-\frac{C^{*}d^{2}_{l}(x,y)}{t-l}}\leq H(x,t;y,l)\leq\frac{C^{*}}{(t-l)^{\frac{n}{2}}}e^{-\frac{d^{2}_{l}(x,y)}{C^{*}(t-l)}}.

Proof of Theorem
1.4
.

Denote
f=\ln u
and
F=t(|\nabla f|^{2}-f_{t})
.

For any
0<T<2
, let
(x_{0},t_{0})\in M\times(0,T]
be a maximum point of
F
and
F(x_{0},t_{0})>0
. Similar to (
3.5
), we get

F(x_{0},t_{0})\leq nt_{0}\frac{K}{2-t_{0}}+\frac{n}{2}+\sqrt{\frac{2nK}{2-t_{0}}}t_{0}|\nabla f|(x_{0},t_{0})\ \ for\ \ 0<t_{0}\leq T.

(4.1)

Since the Ricci curvature bound implies
|R(x,t)|\leq\frac{nK}{2-t}
, combining the condition
\nu[g_{0},4]\geq-nK
, by applying Lemma
4.1
, there exists a constant
\bar{C_{1}}(n,K)
such that for
\frac{3}{2}<t<2
and any
x,y\in M
,

\frac{1}{\bar{C}_{1}(n,K)\left(t-\frac{3}{2}\right)^{\frac{n}{2}}}e^{-\frac{\bar{C_{1}}(n,K)d^{2}_{\frac{3}{2}}(x,y)}{t-\frac{3}{2}}}\leq H(x,t;y,\frac{3}{2})\leq\frac{\bar{C}_{1}(n,K)}{\left(t-\frac{3}{2}\right)^{\frac{n}{2}}}e^{-\frac{d^{2}_{\frac{3}{2}}(x,y)}{\bar{C}_{1}(n,K)\left(t-\frac{3}{2}\right)}}.

(4.2)

For a time
\frac{3}{2}<t_{1}<\frac{8}{5}
, we denote
h(x,t)=H(x,t+t_{1};y,\frac{3}{2})
,
t\in\left[0,\frac{1}{4}t_{1}\right]
, then
t+t_{1}\in\left[t_{1},\frac{5}{4}t_{1}\right]\subset\left(\frac{3}{2},2\right)
. Hence, (
4.2
) implies

\bar{C}_{2}(n,K)e^{-\frac{\bar{C}_{1}(n,K)d^{2}_{\frac{3}{2}}(M)}{t_{1}-\frac{3}{2}}}\leq H(x,t+t_{1};y,\frac{3}{2})\leq\frac{\bar{C}_{1}(n,K)}{\left(t+t_{1}-\frac{3}{2}\right)^{\frac{n}{2}}}\ \ for\ \ t\in\left[0,\frac{1}{4}t_{1}\right].

The upper bound gives

A=\sup\limits_{x\in M}H(x,t_{1};y,\frac{3}{2})\leq\frac{\bar{C}_{1}(n,K)}{(t_{1}-\frac{3}{2})^{\frac{n}{2}}}.

By lemma
3.1
,

t|\nabla\ln H(x,t+t_{1};y,\frac{3}{2})|^{2}\leq\bar{C}_{3}(n,K)+\ln(t_{1}-\frac{3}{2})^{-\frac{n}{2}}+\frac{\bar{C}_{1}(n,K)d^{2}_{\frac{3}{2}}(M)}{t_{1}-\frac{3}{2}}.

Since

-\frac{K}{2-t}g(x,t)\leq\Ric(x,t)\leq\frac{K}{2-t}g(x,t),

then

\left(\frac{2}{2-t}\right)^{-2K}g(0)\leq g(x,t)\leq\left(\frac{2}{2-t}\right)^{2K}g(0),

therefore

t|\nabla\ln H(x,t+t_{1};y,\frac{3}{2})|^{2}\leq\bar{C}_{3}(n,K)+\ln(t_{1}-\frac{3}{2})^{-\frac{n}{2}}+\frac{\bar{C}_{4}(n,K)d^{2}_{0}(M)}{t_{1}-\frac{3}{2}}.

We take
t=\frac{1}{4}t_{1}
and use
t_{1}\in\left(\frac{3}{2},\frac{8}{5}\right)
to conclude that

\frac{1}{5}t|\nabla\ln H(x,t;y,\frac{3}{2})|^{2}\leq\bar{C}_{3}(n,K)+\ln\left(\frac{4}{5}t-\frac{3}{2}\right)^{-\frac{n}{2}}+\frac{\bar{C}_{4}(n,K)d^{2}_{0}(M)}{\frac{4}{5}t-\frac{3}{2}},\ \ \forall t\in\left(\frac{15}{8},2\right),

in particular,

t|\nabla\ln H(x,t;y,\frac{3}{2})|^{2}\leq\bar{C}_{5}(n,K)(1+d^{2}_{0}(M)),\ \ \forall t\in\left(\frac{17}{9},2\right).

Since
u(x,t)=\int_{M}H(x,t;y,\frac{3}{2})u(y,\frac{3}{2})d\mu_{\frac{3}{2}}(y)
,
t\geq\frac{3}{2}
, the above upper bound actually implies

t|\nabla\ln u(x,t)|^{2}\leq\bar{C}_{5}(n,K)(1+d^{2}_{0}(M)),\ \ \forall t\in\left(\frac{17}{9},2\right).

(4.3)

For
t\in\left[0,\frac{17}{9}\right]
, since the Ricci curvature is uniformly bounded, from (
3.10
), we derive

t^{2}|\nabla\ln u(x,t)|^{2}\leq\bar{C}_{6}(n)\left(e^{\bar{C}_{7}(n)Kt}(t+d_{0}^{2}(M))\right),\ \ \forall t\in\left[0,\frac{17}{9}\right].

(4.4)

Combining (
4.3
) and (
4.4
), we conclude that

t^{2}|\nabla\ln u(x,t)|^{2}\leq\bar{C}_{8}(n,K)(t+d^{2}_{0}(M)t+d^{2}_{0}(M)).\ \ \forall t\in[0,2).

Plugging above into (
4.1
) gives

F(x_{0},t_{0})\leq\frac{n}{2}+\frac{nKt_{0}}{2-t_{0}}+\sqrt{\frac{K\bar{C}_{9}(n,K)}{2-t_{0}}(t_{0}+d_{0}^{2}(M)t_{0}+d_{0}^{2}(M))},

which implies

F(x,T)\leq F(x_{0},t_{0})\leq\frac{n}{2}+\frac{nKT}{2-T}+\sqrt{\frac{K\bar{C}_{9}(n,K)}{2-T}(T+d_{0}^{2}(M)T+d_{0}^{2}(M))}.

The arbitrariness of
T
gives the theorem.

∎

5.
The Conjugate heat equation under the Ricci flow

In this section, we deal with the conjugate heat equation under the Ricci flow and prove Theorem
1.5
.

First, we recall the following Hamilton type estimate for the positive solution to the conjugate heat equation under the Ricci flow.

Lemma 5.1
.

(
[
11
]
)
Let
(M^{n},g(t))_{t\in[0,T]}
be a solution to the Ricci flow
\frac{\partial}{\partial t}g(x,t)=-2\Ric(x,t)
. For a fixed point
o\in M
, assume that for some constants
K_{1},\ K_{2}\geq 0
,
\Ric(x,t)\geq-K_{1}g(x,t)
and
|\nabla R|(x,t)\leq K_{2}^{\frac{3}{2}}
in
B_{\tau}(o,2r)\times[0,T]
,
\tau=T-t
. Let
u
be a solution to the conjugate heat equation
\left(\Delta_{t}+\frac{\partial}{\partial t}-R\right)u=0
in
B_{\tau}(o,2r)\times[0,T]
satisfying
0<u\leq A
. Then there exist nonnegative constants
\tilde{C_{1}}(n)
and
\tilde{C_{2}}(n)
such that

\displaystyle\sup\limits_{x\in B_{\tau}(o,r)}\frac{|\nabla u|^{2}}{u^{2}}(x,\tau)

\displaystyle\leq\left(1+\ln\frac{A}{u}\right)^{2}\left(\frac{1}{\tau}+\tilde{C_{2}}(n)K_{1}+2K_{2}+\frac{1}{r^{2}}(\tilde{C_{1}}(n)\sqrt{K_{1}}r\coth(\sqrt{K_{1}}r)+\tilde{C_{2}}(n))\right).

Remark 5.2
.

If the conditions
\Ric(x,t)\geq-K_{1}g(x,t)
,
|\nabla R|(x,t)\leq K_{2}^{\frac{3}{2}}
and
0<u\leq A
are satisfied on
M\times[0,T]
, then by letting
R\rightarrow\infty
, we have that

\frac{|\nabla u|^{2}}{u^{2}}(x,\tau)\leq\left(1+\ln\frac{A}{u}\right)^{2}\left(\frac{1}{\tau}+\tilde{C_{3}}(n)(K_{1}+K_{2})\right)

holds on
M\times(0,T]
.

In
[
17
]
, Kuang-Zhang established a Li-Yau type estimate for positive solutions of the conjugate heat equation under the Ricci flow.

Lemma 5.3
.

(
[
17
]
)
Let
M^{n}
be an n-dimensional closed Riemannian manifold and
g(x,t),t\in[0,T)
a solution to the Ricci flow
\frac{\partial}{\partial t}g(x,t)=-2\Ric(x,t)
on
M
. Assume that
R(x,t)\geq 0
,
(x,t)\in M\times[0,T)
. Let
u
be a positive solution to the conjugate heat equation
\left(\Delta_{t}+\frac{\partial}{\partial t}-R\right)u=0
on
M\times[0,T)
. Let
u=(4\pi\tau)^{-\frac{n}{2}}e^{-f}
with
\tau=T-t
. Then

2\Delta f-|\nabla f|^{2}+R\leq\frac{2n}{\tau},

or equivalently,

\frac{|\nabla u|^{2}}{u^{2}}+2\frac{u_{t}}{u}-R\leq\frac{2n}{T-t}

holds on
M\times[0,T)
.

Proof of Theorem
1.5
.
Since the heat kernel
G(y,l;x,t)
of the conjugate heat equation satisfies
G(y,l;x,t)=H(x,t;y,l)
, where
H(x,t;y,l)
is the heat kernel of the heat equation and
x,y\in M
and
0\leq l<t\leq T
, by Lemma
3.2
, we have

\frac{\tilde{C_{7}}(n)e^{-\tilde{C_{8}}(n)e^{\tilde{C_{9}}(n)K_{1}T}}e^{-\frac{4d_{T}^{2}(M)}{T-l}}}{\vol_{T}(B_{T}(y,\sqrt{\frac{T-l}{8}}))}\leq G(y,l;x,T)\leq\frac{\tilde{C_{4}}(n)e^{\tilde{C_{5}}(n)e^{\tilde{C_{6}}(n)K_{1}T}}}{\vol_{l}(B_{l}(x,\sqrt{\frac{T-l}{8}}))}.

(5.1)

For a time
0<s\leq\frac{T}{2}
, we denote
h(y,l)=G(y,l;x,T)
,
x,y\in M
and
l\in[0,s]
.

Since
h(y,l)
is a positive solution of the conjugate heat equation
\left(\Delta_{l,y}+\frac{\partial}{\partial l}-R\right)h(y,l)=0
, by Remark
5.2
, we derive

|\nabla_{y}\ln G(y,l;x,T)|^{2}\leq\left(1+\ln\frac{A}{G(y,l;x,T)}\right)^{2}\left(\frac{1}{l}+\tilde{C}_{10}(n)(K_{1}+K_{2})\right),\ (y,l)\in M\times(0,s],

(5.2)

where
A=\sup\limits_{(y,l)\in M\times[0,s]}G(y,l;x,T)
.

The upper bound in (
5.1
) gives

A\leq\frac{\tilde{C_{4}}(n)e^{\tilde{C}_{11}(n)e^{\tilde{C}_{12}(n)K_{1}T}}}{\vol_{0}(B_{0}(x,\sqrt{\frac{T}{16}}))}.

Hence, combining with the lower bound in (
5.1
), we have

\displaystyle\frac{A}{G(y,l;x,T)}

\displaystyle\leq\tilde{C}_{13}(n)e^{\tilde{C}_{14}(n)e^{\tilde{C}_{15}(n)K_{1}T}}e^{\frac{8d^{2}_{T}(M)}{T}}\frac{\vol_{0}(B_{0}(y,\sqrt{\frac{T}{8}}))}{\vol_{0}(B_{0}(x,\sqrt{\frac{T}{16}}))}

(5.3)

\displaystyle\leq\tilde{C}_{13}(n)e^{\tilde{C}_{14}(n)e^{\tilde{C}_{15}(n)K_{1}T}}e^{\frac{8d^{2}_{T}(M)}{T}}\frac{\vol_{0}(B_{0}(x,d_{0}(M)+\sqrt{\frac{T}{8}}))}{\vol_{0}(B_{0}(x,\sqrt{\frac{T}{16}}))}.

According to the Bishop-Gromov volume comparison theorem, we compute

\frac{\vol_{0}(B_{0}(x,d_{0}(M)+\sqrt{\frac{T}{8}}))}{\vol_{0}(B_{0}(x,\sqrt{\frac{T}{16}}))}\leq\left(\frac{d_{0}(M)+\sqrt{\frac{T}{8}}}{\sqrt{\frac{T}{16}}}\right)^{n}e^{\tilde{C}_{16}(n)\sqrt{K_{1}}(d_{0}(M)+\sqrt{T})}.

(5.4)

Combining (
5.3
) and (
5.4
), one has

\frac{A}{G(y,l;x,T)}\leq\tilde{C}_{13}(n)e^{\tilde{C}_{14}(n)e^{\tilde{C}_{15}(n)K_{1}T}}e^{\left[\frac{8d^{2}_{T}(M)}{T}+\tilde{C}_{17}(n)(\frac{d_{0}(M)}{\sqrt{T}}+1+d_{0}(M)\sqrt{K_{1}}+\sqrt{K_{1}T})\right]}.

Plugging this into (
5.2
) results in

|\nabla_{y}\ln G(y,l;x,T)|^{2}\leq\tilde{C}_{20}(n)e^{\tilde{C}_{21}(n)K_{1}T}\left(1+\frac{d^{2}_{0}(M)}{T}\right)^{2}\left(\frac{1}{l}+K_{1}+K_{2}\right),\ (y,l)\in M\times(0,s].

We take
l=s
and use the arbitrariness of
0<s\leq\frac{T}{2}
to conclude

|\nabla_{y}\ln G(y,l;x,T)|^{2}\leq\tilde{C}_{20}(n)e^{\tilde{C}_{21}(n)K_{1}T}\left(1+\frac{d^{2}_{0}(M)}{T}\right)^{2}\left(\frac{1}{l}+K_{1}+K_{2}\right),\ (y,l)\in M\times(0,\frac{T}{2}].

Since a positive solution
u(x,t)
to the conjugate heat equation can be written as
u(x,t)=\int_{M}G(x,t;y,T)u(y,T)d\mu_{T}(y)
with
t\in[0,T)
, the above estimate holds for any
u(x,t)
, i.e.,

|\nabla\ln u(x,t)|^{2}\leq\tilde{C}_{20}(n)e^{\tilde{C}_{21}(n)K_{1}T}\left(1+\frac{d^{2}_{0}(M)}{T}\right)^{2}\left(\frac{1}{t}+K_{1}+K_{2}\right),\ (x,t)\in M\times(0,\frac{T}{2}].

Combining Lemma
5.3
, we achieve that

\displaystyle\frac{|\nabla u|^{2}}{u^{2}}+\frac{u_{t}}{u}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{|\nabla u|^{2}}{u^{2}}+2\frac{u_{t}}{u}-R\right)+\frac{1}{2}|\nabla\ln u|^{2}+\frac{1}{2}R

\displaystyle\leq\frac{n}{T-t}+\tilde{C}_{22}(n)e^{\tilde{C}_{21}(n)K_{1}T}\left(1+\frac{d^{2}_{0}(M)}{T}\right)^{2}\left(\frac{1}{t}+K_{1}+K_{2}\right),

where
(x,t)\in M\times(0,\frac{T}{2}].
This completes the proof of Theorem
1.5
. ∎
