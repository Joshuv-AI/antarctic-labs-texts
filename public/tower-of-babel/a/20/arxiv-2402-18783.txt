J = 1 / 2 J=1/2 Pseudospins and d d - p p Hybridization in the Kitaev Spin Liquid Candidates Ru X 3 X_{3} ( X X = Cl, Br, I)
H. Gretarsson, H. Fujihara, F. Sato, H. Gotou, Y. Imai, K. Ohgushi, B. Keimer, H. Suzuki

Abstract
The recent synthesis of ruthenium trihalides Ru X 3 X_{3} ( X X = Cl, Br, I) has enlarged the set of material candidates for Kitaev spin liquid. The realization of Kitaev model necessitates the formation of J = 1 / 2 J=1/2 pseudospins in the octahedral crystal field. We use Ru L 3 L_{3} -edge resonant inelastic x-ray scattering to investigate the evolution of multiplet structures in Ru X 3 X_{3} . We identified quasi-elastic magnetic correlations and spin-orbit transitions to the J = 3 / 2 J=3/2 states without discernible trigonal splitting, thereby validating the J = 1 / 2 J=1/2 description of magnetism in the Ru X 3 X_{3} family. Lineshape broadening in RuI 3 provides evidence for its bulk metallicity in the vicinity of a bandwidth-controlled metal-insulator transition. Our results highlight the pivotal role of halogen p p orbitals in controlling the electronic properties of Ru X 3 X_{3} .
