Integrated Sensing and Communication in the Finite Blocklength Regime
Homa Nikbakht 1 , Michèle Wigger 2 , Shlomo Shamai (Shitz) 3 , and H. Vincent Poor 1

Abstract
A point-to-point integrated sensing and communication (ISAC) system is considered where a transmitter conveys a message to a receiver over a discrete memoryless channel (DMC) and simultaneously estimates the state of the channel through the backscattered signals of the emitted waveform. We derive achievability and converse bounds on the rate-distortion-error tradeoff in the finite blocklength regime, and also characterize the second-order rate-distortion-error region for the proposed setup. Numerical analysis shows that our proposed joint ISAC scheme significantly outperforms traditional time-sharing based schemes where the available resources are split between the sensing and communication tasks.

The finite-blocklength behavior of ISAC has already been studied in
[

In our setup, the state is described by a memoryless sequence impacting the various channel uses and the goal of the estimation is to reconstruct this sequence with minimum distortion.

Main Results

Given two random variables

\displaystyle i(X;Y):=\log\frac{P_{Y|X}(y|x)}{P_{Y}(y)},

\displaystyle\mathsf{V}

\displaystyle:=

\displaystyle\text{Var}[i(X;Y)]

(15)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{x,y}P_{X}(x)P_{Y|X}(y|x)\log^{2}\frac{P_{Y|X}(y|x)}{P_{Y}(y)}-I(X;Y)^{2},

\displaystyle\mathsf{T}

\displaystyle:=

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}[|i(X;Y)-\mathbb{I}(X;Y)|^{3}]

(16)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{x,y}P_{X}(x)P_{Y|X}(y|x)\left|\log\frac{P_{Y|X}(y|x)}{P_{Y}(y)}-I(X;Y)\right|^{3}.

V-A

Many practical systems employ a basic resource-sharing approach where a fraction of the resources (here

VI-E

Error Analysis

To analyze
{\mathbb{P}}[\hat{M}\neq M]
, we use the threshold-based metric bound in
[
22
]
. For any
\gamma\in\mathbb{R}
, we have

\displaystyle{\mathbb{P}}[\hat{M}\neq M]

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle{\mathbb{P}}[i(X^{n};Y^{n})\leq\gamma]+\mathsf{M}\cdot{\mathbb{P}}[i(\bar{X}^{n};Y^{n})\geq\gamma],

(45)

where
\bar{X}^{n}\sim P_{X^{n}}
and is independent of
X^{n}
and
Y^{n}
.
We will set

\displaystyle\gamma

\displaystyle:=

\displaystyle\log\mathsf{M}+\mathsf{K}\log n,

(46)

for some
\mathsf{K}>0
, and
employ the Berry-Esseen theorem and Bayes’ formula to evaluate the two terms on the right-hand side of (

By the strengthening of the Berry-Esseen theorem in
[
24
]
, and because
\mathbb{E}[i(X^{n};Y^{n})]=nI(X;Y)
, we have with the definition in (
46
)

\displaystyle{\mathbb{P}}\left[i(X^{n};Y^{n})\leq\gamma\right]

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\mathbb{Q}\left(\frac{-\log\mathsf{M}+nI(X;Y)-\mathsf{K}\log(n)}{\sqrt{n\mathsf{V}}}\right)

(47)

\displaystyle+\frac{0.7975\mathsf{T}}{\sqrt{n\mathsf{V}^{3}}}.

To bound
{\mathbb{P}}[i(\bar{X}^{n};Y^{n})\geq\gamma]
, we first use Bayes’ formula to write

\displaystyle P_{{X}^{n}}(\overline{x}^{n})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{P_{{Y}^{n}}({y^{n}})P_{{X}^{n}|{Y}^{n}}(\overline{x}^{n}|{y}^{n})}{P_{{Y}^{n}|{X}^{n}}({y}^{n}|\overline{x}^{n})}

(48)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle P_{\bar{X}^{n}|{Y}^{n}}(\overline{x}^{n}|{y}^{n})2^{-i(\bar{x}^{n};y^{n})}.

(49)

For any
{y}^{n}\in\mathbb{R}^{n}
, we then have

\displaystyle\sum_{\bar{x}^{n}\in\mathcal{X}}\mathbbm{1}\left\{i(\bar{x}^{n};y^{n})>\gamma\right\}P_{{X}^{n}}(\overline{x}^{n})

\displaystyle=\sum_{\bar{x}^{n}\in\mathcal{X}}2^{-i(\bar{x}^{n};y^{n})}\mathbbm{1}\left\{\frac{P_{Y^{n}|X^{n}}(y^{n}|\overline{x}^{n})}{P_{Y^{n}}(y^{n})}>2^{\gamma}\right\}

\displaystyle\hskip 85.35826pt\cdot P_{{X}^{n}|{Y}^{n}}(\overline{x}^{n}|{y}^{n})

(50)

\displaystyle\leq\sum_{\bar{x}^{n}\in\mathcal{X}}2^{-i(\bar{x}^{n};y^{n})}\frac{P_{Y^{n}|X^{n}}(y^{n}|\overline{x}^{n})}{P_{Y^{n}}(y^{n})}2^{-\gamma}P_{{X^{n}}|{Y^{n}}}(\overline{x}^{n}|{y}^{n})

\displaystyle=\sum_{\bar{x}^{n}\in\mathcal{X}}P_{{X}^{n}|{Y}^{n}}(\overline{x}^{n}|{y}^{n})2^{-\gamma}

\displaystyle=2^{-\gamma}.

(51)

As a consequence,

\displaystyle{\mathbb{P}}[i(\bar{X}^{n};Y^{n})\geq\gamma]\leq 2^{-\gamma}

(52)

and

\displaystyle\mathsf{M}{\mathbb{P}}[i(\bar{X}^{n};Y^{n})\geq\gamma]\leq 2^{-\gamma+\log\mathsf{M}}=n^{-\mathsf{K}}.

(53)

Combining (
45
), (
47
), and (

), we obtain

\displaystyle{\mathbb{P}}[\hat{M}\neq M]\leq\mathbb{Q}\left(\frac{-\log\mathsf{M}+nI(X;Y)-\mathsf{K}\log(n)}{\sqrt{n\mathsf{V}}}\right)+\beta_{u},

where
\beta_{u}
is defined in (
19
).

Thus, the probability of error stays below

whenever

\displaystyle\epsilon-\beta_{u}\geq\mathbb{Q}\left(\frac{-\log\mathsf{M}+nI(X;Y)-\mathsf{K}\log(n)}{\sqrt{n\mathsf{V}}}\right),

(55)

or equivalently when

\displaystyle\log\mathsf{M}\leq nI(X;Y)-\sqrt{n\mathsf{V}}\mathbb{Q}^{-1}(\epsilon-\beta_{u})-\mathsf{K}\log(n),

(56)

establishing the bound in (

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\mathbb{E}[d(S_{i},\hat{S}_{i})]

(57)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{x\in\mathcal{X}}\sum_{s\in\mathcal{S}}\sum_{z\in\mathcal{Z}}d(s,\hat{s}^{*}(x,z))P_{X}(x)P_{S}(s)P_{Z|XS}(z|x,s).

By our choice of
P_{X}
, our scheme thus satisfies the requirement on the distortion.

VII
Conclusions

We have studied the rate-distortion-error tradeoff of a point-to-point ISAC system where a transmitter conveys a message to a receiver over a discrete memoryless state-dependent channel and simultaneously estimates the state of the channel. We have derived achievability and converse bounds on the rate-distortion-error tradeoff in the finite blocklength regime. We also have characterized the second-order rate-distortion-error region of the proposed setup. Our numerical analysis shows that our joint design scheme significantly outperforms the resource-sharing baseline schemes where the available resources are split between the sensing and communication tasks. In our model the receiver has no state-information. The generality of our model allows however to obtain results for perfect or partial state-information as special cases from our Theorems
1
and
2
, simply by including the state-information as part of the receiver’s output. An interesting line of future work is to study the ISAC problem with general state and channel distribution in the finite blocklength regime
[
25
]
.
