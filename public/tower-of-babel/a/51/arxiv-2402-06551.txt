Existence of arbitrary large numbers of non- ℝ {\mathbb{R}} -covered Anosov flows on hyperbolic 3 3 -manifolds
François Béguin and Bin Yu

Abstract
. The purpose of this paper is to prove that, for every n ∈ ℕ n\in{\mathbb{N}} , there exists a closed hyperbolic 3 3 -manifold M M which carries at least n n non- ℝ {\mathbb{R}} -covered Anosov flows, that are pairwise orbitally inequivalent. Due to a recent result by Fenley, such Anosov flows are quasi-geodesic. Hence, we get the existence of hyperbolic 3 3 -manifolds carrying many pairwise orbitally inequivalent quasi-geodesic Anosov flows. One of the main ingredients of our proof is a description of the clusters of lozenges that appear in the orbit spaces of the Anosov flows that we construct. The number of lozenges involved in each cluster, as well as the orientation-type of this cluster, provide powerful dynamical invariants allowing to prove that the flows are orbitally inequivalent. We believe that these dynamical invariants could be helpful in a much wider context.

\displaystyle\|DX_{-t}(v)\|

Understanding the qualitative dynamics (that is the dynamics up to orbital equivalence) of

1.1.
Main result

The Anosov flows constructed by Bowden and Mann are

-covered case and non-

]

Theorem 1.1

For every

For every

1.2.
Outline of the proof of Theorem

The proof of Theorem

We will study some particular sets of lozenges in

Recall that the Anosov flow

Another important ingredient is the possibility to distinguish

are pairwise orbitally inequivalent.

2.
Construction of a hyperbolic plug

A
hyperbolic plug
is a pair

where
U
is a compact orientable
3

C^{1}

\Lambda:=\bigcap_{t\in{\mathbb{R}}}X_{t}(U)
is a saddle hyperbolic set for the flow
X_{t}
. The purpose of the present section is to build a hyperbolic plug with some specific topological and dynamical features. Before proceeding to the construction, we will recall some basic definitions and properties concerning hyperbolic plugs. A much more detailed discussion, including proofs of the properties stated below, can be founded in
[
BBY

2.1.
General facts about hyperbolic plugs

Let

be such a hyperbolic plug, and
\Lambda:=\bigcap_{t\in{\mathbb{R}}}X_{t}(U)
be its maximal invariant set. For sake of simplicity, we assume that
\Lambda
is a transitive set, not reduced to a periodic orbit. A periodic orbit

is called a

if one stable separatrix of

(that is one connected component of

) is disjoint form

. Roughly speaking, this means that

is not accumulated by

-boundary periodic orbits. Moreover, our hypothesis implies that the lamination

has no isolated leaf; it follows that every

-boundary periodic orbit

is a cylinder, not a Möbius band), only one of which is a free separatrix. Of course,

Since the vector field

\partial U=\partial^{in}U\sqcup\partial^{out}U
