Pressure tunable magnetic skyrmion phase in Co 8 Zn 8 Mn 4 single crystals Preprint: APS/123-QED
Zhun Li, Xinrun Mi, Xinming Wang, Jian Lyu, Na Su, Aifeng Wang, Yisheng Chai, Bao Yuan, Wanju Luo, Hui Cheng, Jianxiang Gao, Hongliang Wang, Lijie Hao, Mingquan He, Junying Shen, Young Sun, Xin Tong

Abstract
In a magnetic skyrmion phase, magnetic moments form vortex-like topological textures which are of both fundamental and industrial interests. In β \beta -Mn-type Co-Zn-Mn alloys, chrial magnetic skyrmions emerge above room temperature, providing a unique system for studying the skrymion physics and exploring spintronics applications. However, the magnetic skyrmion phase is typically confined in a narrow and limited temperature ( T T ) and magnetic field ( H H ) range. Here, we demonstrate that hydrostatic pressure can expand the skyrmion phase in the T − H T-H phase diagram of single-crystalline Co 8 Zn 8 Mn 4 . At ambient pressure, signatures of skyrmions are seen within T ∼ 302 − 308 T\sim 302-308 K and H ∼ 50 − 100 H\sim 50-100 Oe. Applying a moderate pressure of 6 kbar extends this range to T ∼ 300 − 310 T\sim 300-310 K and H ∼ 50 − 150 H\sim 50-150 Oe. However, further escalation of pressure to 10 kbar results in a slight contraction of the skyrmion phase. These findings underscore the sensitivity of the skyrmion phase in Co 8 Zn 8 Mn 4 to external pressures, and hint at the potential of strain engineering, particularly in β \beta -Mn-type Co-Zn-Mn thin films, as a promising avenue to customize the skyrmion phase. Keywords : magnetic skyrmions, DM interaction, CoZnMn, pressure PACS : 12.39.Dc, 64.70.K-



I
Introduction

Magnetic skyrmions, distinguished by their unique vortex-like spin configurations carrying integer topological charges, have become a forefront in the study of condensed matter physics and material science
Bogdanov and Yablonskii 1989
;
Mühlbauer et al. 2009
;
Yu et al. 2010
. The nontrivial spin textures of skyrmions are fascinating both from a fundamental physics perspective and for their potential technological applications. Fundamentally, magnetic skyrmions can arise through diverse mechanisms, such as the interplay between ferromagnetic exchange, Dzyaloshinskii-Moriya (DM), Ruderman-Kittel-Kasuya-Yosida (RKKY), and magnetic dipolar interactions
Mühlbauer et al. 2009
;
Yu et al. 2010
;
Yu et al. 2014
;
Wang et al. 2016
;
Kurumaji et al. 2019
;
Hirschberger et al. 2019
. From a technological standpoint, skyrmions stand out for their emergent electromagnetic properties and the ability to be manipulated with low current densities, making them highly efficient for advanced spintronics applications
Jonietz et al. 2010
;
Yu et al. 2012
;
Iwasaki et al. 2013
.

In bulk materials, magnetic skyrmions are commonly observed in chiral magnets, such as metallic B20 compounds (MnSi, Fe

. However, the

;
Bocarsly et al. 2019
;

;

;

;
Nii et al. 2015

OSeO

;

;
Ritz et al. 2013
;
Chacon et al. 2015
;
Nii et al. 2015
;
Seki et al. 2017
;
Levatić et al. 2016

Ritz et al. 2013

4

IV
Conclusions

In summary, we have studied the evolution of the skyrmion phase in Co
8
Zn
8
Mn
4
under pressure. It is found that the size of skyrmion pocket on
T-H
phase diagram is considerably enlarged under a moderate pressure of about 10 kbar. The enhanced stability of the skyrmion phase is likely caused by pressure-induced magnetic anisotropy and/or easy-plane anisotropy. It would be interesting to further manipulate the skyrmion phase using strain engineering on Co
x
Zn
y
Mn
z
thin films.
