A Construction of Arbitrarily Large Type-II Z Z Complementary Code Set
Rajen Kumar, Prashant Kumar Srivastava, Sudhan Majhi

Abstract
For a type-I ( K , M , Z , N ) (K,M,Z,N) -ZCCS, it follows K ≤ M ​ ⌊ N Z ⌋ K\leq M\left\lfloor\frac{N}{Z}\right\rfloor . In this paper, we propose a construction of type-II ( p k + n , p k , p n + r − p r + 1 , p n + r ) (p^{k+n},p^{k},p^{n+r}-p^{r}+1,p^{n+r}) - Z Z complementary code set (ZCCS) using an extended Boolean function, its properties of Hamiltonian paths and the concept of isolated vertices, where p ≥ 2 p\geq 2 . However, the proposed type-II ZCCS provides K = M ⁡ ( N − Z + 1 ) K=M(N-Z+1) codes, where as for type-I ( K , M , N , Z ) (K,M,N,Z) -ZCCS, it is K ≤ M ​ ⌊ N Z ⌋ K\leq M\left\lfloor\frac{N}{Z}\right\rfloor . Therefore, the proposed type-II ZCCS provides a larger number of codes compared to type-I ZCCS. Further, as a special case of the proposed construction, ( p k , p k , p n ) (p^{k},p^{k},p^{n}) -CCC can be generated, for any integral value of p ≥ 2 p\geq 2 and k ≤ n k\leq n .

Introduction

A collection of codes is described as a complete complementary code (CCC) if the aperiodic auto-correlation function (AACF) value of each code is zero for all non-zero time shifts and the aperiodic cross-correlation function (ACCF) value between any two codes is zero for all time shifts
Suehiro and Hatori 1988
. CCC has shown a significant role in asynchronous multi-carrier code division multiple access (MC-CDMA) for interference-free communication
Chen, H.H. 2007
. Rathinakumar and Chaturvedi proposed
(2^{k+1},2^{k+1},2^{k+m})
-CCC using a generalized Boolean function (GBF) and by associating it with a graph, where a Hamiltonian path of
m
vertices is obtained after deleting the
k
vertices from the graph
Rathinakumar and Chaturvedi 2008
. Recently, construction of CCCs with new and flexible parameters has been proposed in
Sarkar et al. 2021
based on a multivariable function. To utilise more users in the MC-CDMA system, the zero correlation zone (ZCZ) has been introduced and formed
Z
-complementary code set (ZCCS) in
Fan et al. 2007
;
Feng et al. 2008
. ZCCSs are capable of supporting interference-free MC-CDMA in a quasi-synchronous environment without the need for power control
Chen, H.H. 2007
. Depending on whether the ZCZ width exists close to zero time shift or end time shift, the code set is referred to as a type-I ZCCS or type-II ZCCS, respectively. A
(K,M,Z,N)
-ZCCS is a collection of
K
matrices, where each matrix has
M
constituent sequences of length
N
with ZCZ width
Z
. The number of constituent sequences is referred to as flock size. For a type-I
(K,M,Z,N)
-ZCCS,
K\leq M\left\lfloor\frac{N}{Z}\right\rfloor

Fan et al. 2007
and there are several such type-I ZCCS constructions provided in
Tian et al. 2021
;
Das et al. 2019
;
Sarkar and Majhi 2021
;
Sarkar et al. 2021
;
Ghosh et al. 2022
;
Wu et al. 2021
. Recently, type-II ZCCS has been proposed to increase the number of codes and ZCZ width
Kumar et al. 2024
. As type-II ZCCS has a wider ZCZ width and more codes compared to type-I ZCCS, it is suitable for the Quasi-synchronous MC-CDMA system with delays
Kumar et al. 2024
.

In this paper, we propose a direct construction of type-II
(p^{r+k},p^{k},p^{n+r}-p^{r}+1,p^{n+r})
-ZCCS, where
p\geq 2
,
0<k<n
and
r\geq 0
, i.e., we have
p^{r}
times more codes compared to the flock size by compromising the
p^{r}-1
time shift of ZCZ width. Therefore, in the proposed construction, a large number of codes is generated compared to type-I ZCCS with the same flock size. An extended Boolean function (EBF) is used to construct a type-II ZCCS. The construction is associated with a graph of a collection of
k
Hamiltonian paths and
r
isolated vertices. The proposed method also generates
(p^{k},p^{k},p^{n})
-CCC for
k\leq n
,
p\geq 2
. The works present in
Davis and Jedwab 1999
;
Rathinakumar and Chaturvedi 2008
;
Shen et al. 2023
are special
cases
of the proposed construction.

The remainder of this manuscript has been structured as follows. Section
2
comprehensively describes the notations, important definitions, and relations between a graph and an EBF. In Section
3
, we propose a construction of type-II ZCCS and provide an example. In Section
4
, a comparison is made between the proposed construction and existing work. Section
5
comprises the concluding remarks of the paper.

Definition 1
.

Let
\mathbf{a}=(a_{0},a_{1},\ldots,a_{N-1})

\mathcal{C}_{\mathbf{a},\mathbf{b}}(\tau)=\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}\sum_{i=0}^{N-1-\tau}a_{i+\tau}^{1}b_{i}^{*},&0\leq\tau<N,\\
\sum_{i=0}^{N+\tau-1}a_{i}^{1}b_{i-\tau}^{*},&-N<\tau<0,\\
0,&\text{ otherwise, }\end{array}\right.

(1)

where

. When
\mathbf{a}=\mathbf{b}

\mathcal{C}_{\mathbf{a},\mathbf{b}}(\tau)

\mathbf{a}
and is denoted as
\mathcal{C}_{\mathbf{a}}(\tau)
.

Let
C_{i}
be a code as

C_{i}=\left[\begin{array}[]{c}\mathbf{a}_{0}^{i}\\
\mathbf{a}_{1}^{i}\\
\vdots\\
\mathbf{a}_{M-1}^{i}\end{array}\right]_{M\times N},

(2)

where,
\mathbf{a}^{i}_{\nu}
is
(\nu+1)
th row of the matrix
C_{i}
with
N
elements.

Definition 2
.

Let
C_{s}
and
C_{t}
be any two codes defined in (
2
), then ACCF between
C_{s}
and
C_{t}
is defined by

\mathcal{C}\left({C}_{s},{C}_{t}\right)(\tau)=\sum_{\nu=0}^{M-1}\mathcal{C}\left(\mathbf{a}_{\nu}^{s},\mathbf{a}_{\nu}^{t}\right)(\tau).

(3)

When
{C}_{s}={C}_{t}
,
\mathcal{C}(C_{s},C_{t})(\tau)

{C}_{s}
and is denoted as
\mathcal{C}(C_{s})(\tau)

Definition 3
.

Let
\mathbf{C}=\left\{{C}_{0},{C}_{1},\ldots,{C}_{K-1}\right\}

K

\mathcal{C}\left(C_{k_{1}},C_{k_{2}}\right)(\tau)=\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}NM,&\tau=0,k_{1}=k_{2},\\

(4)

Definition 4

Let
\mathbf{C}=\left\{{C}_{0},{C}_{1},\ldots,{C}_{K-1}\right\}

K

2

\mathbf{C}

Relation with Graph and EBF

Let
Q:\mathbb{Z}_{p}^{n}\to\mathbb{Z}_{q}

(7)

where

(
p
divides
q
),

is a quadratic form in
n

\pi

\{0,1,\ldots,n-1\}
. This

Lemma 1
.

Let
p,k\in\mathbb{N}
, such that
p\geq 2

p\nmid k

\zeta_{p}=\exp{\frac{2\pi\sqrt{-1}}{p}}

\sum_{i=0}^{p-1}\zeta_{p}^{k\cdot i}=0.

(12)

Proof.

Suppose

, we have

. To complete the proof, we consider two cases.

Case 1.

k_{1}
is relatively prime with
p
. Then

\sum_{i=0}^{p-1}\zeta_{p}^{k\cdot i}=\sum_{i=0}^{p-1}\zeta_{p}^{k_{1}\cdot i}=\sum_{i=0}^{p-1}\zeta_{p}^{i}=0,~~\text{sum of $p$th root of unity.}

(13)

Case 2.

k_{1}
is not relatively prime with
p
then there exist a

such that
p=p_{1}d
,
k_{1}=k_{2}d

k_{2}
is relatively prime with
p_{1}
. Now,

\sum_{i=0}^{p-1}\zeta_{p}^{k\cdot i}=\sum_{i=0}^{p-1}\zeta_{p}^{k_{1}\cdot i}=\sum_{i=0}^{p-1}\zeta_{p}^{k_{2}d\cdot i}=\sum_{i=0}^{p-1}\zeta_{p_{1}}^{k_{2}\cdot i}=d\sum_{i=0}^{p_{1}-1}\zeta_{p_{1}}^{k_{2}\cdot i}=0,~~\text{from {Case $1$}.}

(14)

By concluding the above two cases, the proof is complete.
