Abstract
We provide the MS ¯ \overline{\mathrm{MS}} factor of the renormalization constant of the singlet axial-current operator in dimensional regularization at 𝒪 ⁡ ( α s 5 ) \mathcal{O}(\alpha_{s}^{5}) in perturbative Quantum Chromodynamics (QCD). The result is obtained from a formula derived using the Adler-Bell-Jackiw equation in terms of renormalized operators. The required input consists of the complete four-loop results for operator renormalization constants obtained by explicit diagrammatic computation in a previous publication.
