Is d=4 Maximal Supergravity Special?
Renata Kallosh

Abstract
We study candidate counterterms (CT)’s in maximal supergravities in diverse integer dimensions d ≥ 4 d\geq 4 . We find that UV divergences in these theories occur at the number of loops L L below certain critical value L c ​ r L_{cr} . At L ≥ L c ​ r L\geq L_{cr} the CT’s have nonlinear local supersymmetry and local ℋ \mathcal{H} symmetry, but the ones below L c ​ r L_{cr} break these symmetries. Therefore deforming the theories by adding the CT’s to the action to cancel UV divergences would be inconsistent with BRST. Such divergences with L < L c ​ r L<L_{cr} were found in maximal supergravities for all integer d > 4 d>4 , but not for 𝒩 = 5 , 6 , 8 \mathcal{N}=5,6,8 in d = 4 d=4 so far, which renders the case d = 4 d=4 special.

Introduction

We will study here loop computations available in maximal supergravities in diverse dimensions. The physical scalars in these theories are coordinates of the coset space
{\mathcal{G}\over\mathcal{H}}

\mathcal{H}

The main question we would like to address here is a possibility of a consistent deformation of these theories: can we cancel UV divergences by adding to the theory UV divergent counterterms? For example, one can add the
R^{3}

In Sec.
