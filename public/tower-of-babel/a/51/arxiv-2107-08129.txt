Proof of the Jacobi Property for the guiding-center Vlasov-Maxwell Bracket
Alain J. Brizard

Abstract
The proof of the Jacobi property of the guiding-center Vlasov-Maxwell bracket underlying the Hamiltonian structure of the guiding-center Vlasov-Maxwell equations is presented.



I
Introduction

The variational formulations of the guiding-center Vlasov-Maxwell equations were presented by Brizard and Tronci
Brizard_Tronci_2016
, whose work also included a derivation of exact conservation laws for energy-momentum and angular momentum through the Noether method. In a companion paper
Brizard_2021
, the guiding-center Vlasov-Maxwell equations are expressed in Hamiltonian form

\displaystyle\frac{\partial F_{\rm gc}}{\partial t}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\;\nabla\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\left(F_{\rm gc}\;\frac{d{\bf X}}{dt}\right)\;-\;\frac{\partial}{\partial p_{\|}}\left(F_{\rm gc}\;\frac{dp_{\|}}{dt}\right)\;\equiv\;-\;\frac{\partial}{\partial Z^{\alpha}}\left(F_{\rm gc}\;\frac{dZ^{\alpha}}{dt}\right),

(1)

\displaystyle\frac{\partial\bf E}{\partial t}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 4\pi c\,\nabla\,\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}\,\frac{\delta{\mathcal{H}}_{\rm gc}}{\delta\bf B}-4\pi q\int_{P}F_{\rm gc}\;\frac{d{\bf X}}{dt}\;-\;\nabla\,\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}\,\left(4\pi q\int_{P}F_{\rm gc}\;\mathbb{P}_{\|}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\frac{d{\bf X}}{dt}\right),

(2)

\displaystyle\frac{\partial\bf B}{\partial t}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\,4\pi c\;\nabla\,\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}\,\frac{\delta{\mathcal{H}}_{\rm gc}}{\delta\bf E},

(3)

where

\frac{dZ^{\alpha}}{dt}\;=\;\left\{Z^{\alpha},\;\frac{\delta{\mathcal{H}}_{\rm gc}}{\delta F_{\rm gc}}\right\}_{\rm gc}+4\pi q\left(\frac{\delta{\mathcal{H}}_{\rm gc}}{\delta\bf E}+\mathbb{P}_{\|}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\nabla\,\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}\,\frac{\delta{\mathcal{H}}_{\rm gc}}{\delta\bf E}\right)\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\{{\bf X},\;Z^{\alpha}\}_{\rm gc}.

(4)

Here,
q
denotes the charge of a guiding-center particle, with guiding-center position
{\bf X}
and guiding-center parallel (kinetic) momentum
p_{\|}
, and summation over charge species is implied wherever an integral of the guiding-center
phase-space density
F_{\rm gc}
appears (with
\int_{P}
denoting an integral over
p_{\|}
and the magnetic moment
\mu
). In addition, we introduced the symmetric dyadic tensor

\mathbb{P}_{\|}\;\equiv\;\frac{cp_{\|}}{qB}\;\left({\bf I}\;-\;\widehat{{\sf b}}\,\widehat{{\sf b}}\right),

(5)

and the guiding-center Poisson bracket is

\displaystyle\{f,\;g\}_{\rm gc}

\displaystyle\equiv

\displaystyle\frac{{\bf B}^{*}}{B_{\|}^{*}}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\left(\nabla f\;\frac{\partial g}{\partial p_{\|}}\;-\;\frac{\partial f}{\partial p_{\|}}\;\nabla g\right)\;-\;\frac{c\widehat{{\sf b}}}{qB_{\|}^{*}}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\nabla f\,\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}\,\nabla g

(6)

\displaystyle\equiv

\displaystyle\frac{1}{B_{\|}^{*}}\nabla\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\left(B_{\|}^{*}\,f\frac{}{}\{{\bf X},\;g\}_{\rm gc}\right)\;+\;\frac{1}{B_{\|}^{*}}\;\frac{\partial}{\partial p_{\|}}\left(B_{\|}^{*}\,f\frac{}{}\{p_{\|},\;g\}_{\rm gc}\right),

which can also be expressed in divergence form, where we have omitted the ignorable gyromotion pair
(\mu,\theta)
, and

\left.\begin{array}[]{rcl}{\bf B}^{*}&\equiv&{\bf B}\;+\;(p_{\|}c/q)\,\nabla\,\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}\,\widehat{{\sf b}}\\
B_{\|}^{*}&\equiv&\widehat{{\sf b}}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,{\bf B}^{*}\;=\;B\;+\;(p_{\|}c/q)\,\widehat{{\sf b}}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\nabla\,\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}\,\widehat{{\sf b}}\end{array}\right\}.

(7)

We note that the guiding-center Poisson bracket (
6
) satisfies the Jacobi property

\left\{\{f,g\}_{\rm gc},\frac{}{}h\right\}_{\rm gc}+\left\{\{g,h\}_{\rm gc},\frac{}{}f\right\}_{\rm gc}+\left\{\{h,f\}_{\rm gc},\frac{}{}g\right\}_{\rm gc}=0,

(8)

which holds for arbitrary functions
(f,g,h)
, subject to the condition

\nabla\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,{\bf B}^{*}\;=\;\nabla\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,{\bf B}\;=\;0,

(9)

which is satisfied by the definition (
7
).

Lastly, the guiding-center Hamiltonian functional in Eqs. (
1
)-(
3
) is

{\mathcal{H}}_{\rm gc}\;\equiv\;\int_{Z}F_{\rm gc}\;K_{\rm gc}\;+\;\int_{X}\frac{1}{8\pi}\left(|{\bf E}|^{2}+|{\bf B}|^{2}\right),

(10)

so that the Hamiltonian functional derivatives in the guiding-center Vlasov-Maxwell equations (
1
)-(
3
) are

\left(\begin{array}[]{c}\delta{\mathcal{H}}_{\rm gc}/\delta F_{\rm gc}\\
\delta{\mathcal{H}}_{\rm gc}/\delta{\bf E}\\
\delta{\mathcal{H}}_{\rm gc}/\delta{\bf B}\end{array}\right)\;=\;\left(\begin{array}[]{c}K_{\rm gc}\\
{\bf E}/4\pi\\
{\bf B}/4\pi+\int_{P}F_{\rm gc}\;\mu\,\widehat{{\sf b}}\end{array}\right),

(11)

where the guiding-center kinetic energy
K_{\rm gc}=p_{\|}^{2}/2m+\mu\,B
yields the functional derivative
\delta K_{\rm gc}/\delta{\bf B}=\mu\,\widehat{{\sf b}}
.

II
Guiding-center Vlasov-Maxwell bracket

The guiding-center Vlasov-Maxwell bracket is initially constructed from the guiding-center Vlasov-Maxwell equations (
1
)-(
3
) and the Hamiltonian functional (
10
) as the functional identity

\frac{\partial\mathcal{F}}{\partial t}\;=\;\left[{\mathcal{F}},\frac{}{}{\mathcal{H}}_{\rm gc}\right]_{\rm gc}\;=\;\int_{Z}\frac{\partial F_{\rm gc}}{\partial t}\;\frac{\delta\mathcal{F}}{\delta F_{\rm gc}}+\int_{X}\left(\frac{\partial\bf E}{\partial t}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\frac{\delta\mathcal{F}}{\delta\bf E}+\frac{\partial\bf B}{\partial t}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\frac{\delta\mathcal{F}}{\delta\bf B}\right)\;\equiv\;\left\langle\frac{\delta\mathcal{F}}{\delta\Psi^{a}}\left|\frac{}{}\right.\frac{\partial\Psi^{a}}{\partial t}\right\rangle,

(12)

where the guiding-center Vlasov-Maxwell bracket for two arbitrary functionals
({\mathcal{F}},{\mathcal{G}})
of the fields
\mbox{\boldmath$\Psi$}=(F_{\rm gc},{\bf E},{\bf B})
is defined in terms of the Poisson structure:

\left[{\mathcal{F}},\frac{}{}{\mathcal{G}}\right]_{\rm gc}\;\equiv\;\left\langle\frac{\delta\mathcal{F}}{\delta\Psi^{a}}\;\left|\frac{}{}\right.{\sf J}_{\rm gc}^{ab}(\mbox{\boldmath$\Psi$})\,\frac{\delta\mathcal{G}}{\delta\Psi^{b}}\right\rangle.

(13)

Here, the antisymmetric Poisson operator
{\sf J}_{\rm gc}^{ab}(\mbox{\boldmath$\Psi$})
guarantees the antisymmetry property:
[{\mathcal{F}},{\mathcal{G}}]_{\rm gc}=-\,[{\mathcal{G}},{\mathcal{F}}]_{\rm gc}
; and the bilinearity of Eq. (
13
) guarantees the Leibnitz property:
[{\mathcal{F}},{\mathcal{G}}\,{\mathcal{K}}]_{\rm gc}=[{\mathcal{F}},{\mathcal{G}}]_{\rm gc}\,{\mathcal{K}}+{\mathcal{G}}\,[{\mathcal{F}},{\mathcal{K}}]_{\rm gc}
. The Jacobi property:

{\mathcal{J}ac}[{\mathcal{F}},{\mathcal{G}},{\mathcal{K}}]\;\equiv\;\left[[{\mathcal{F}},{\mathcal{G}}]_{\rm gc},\frac{}{}{\mathcal{K}}\right]_{\rm gc}+\left[[{\mathcal{G}},{\mathcal{K}}]_{\rm gc},\frac{}{}{\mathcal{F}}\right]_{\rm gc}+\left[[{\mathcal{K}},{\mathcal{F}}]_{\rm gc},\frac{}{}{\mathcal{G}}\right]_{\rm gc}=0,

(14)

which holds for arbitrary functionals
({\mathcal{F}},{\mathcal{G}},{\mathcal{K}})
, involves constraints on the Poisson operator
{\sf J}_{\rm gc}^{ab}(\mbox{\boldmath$\Psi$})
. The purpose of the present notes is to provide an explicit proof that the guiding-center Vlasov-Maxwell bracket defined in Eq. (
12
) satisfies the Jacobi property (
14
) exactly.

From Eq. (
12
), we can now extract the guiding-center Vlasov-Maxwell bracket expressed in terms of two arbitrary guiding-center functionals
({\mathcal{F}},{\mathcal{G}})
as

\displaystyle\left[{\mathcal{F}},\frac{}{}{\mathcal{G}}\right]_{\rm gc}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int_{Z}F_{\rm gc}\left(\left\{\frac{\delta{\mathcal{F}}}{\delta F_{\rm gc}},\;\frac{\delta\mathcal{G}}{\delta F_{\rm gc}}\right\}_{\rm gc}\;+\;4\pi q\frac{\delta^{\star}{\mathcal{F}}}{\delta{\bf E}}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\left\{{\bf X},\;{\bf X}\right\}_{\rm gc}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,4\pi q\frac{\delta^{\star}{\mathcal{G}}}{\delta{\bf E}}\right)

(15)

\displaystyle+\;4\pi q\int_{Z}F_{\rm gc}\left(\frac{\delta^{\star}{\mathcal{G}}}{\delta{\bf E}}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\left\{{\bf X},\;\frac{\delta{\mathcal{F}}}{\delta F_{\rm gc}}\right\}_{\rm gc}-\frac{\delta^{\star}{\mathcal{F}}}{\delta{\bf E}}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\left\{{\bf X},\;\frac{\delta\mathcal{G}}{\delta F_{\rm gc}}\right\}_{\rm gc}\right)

\displaystyle+\;4\pi c\int_{X}\left(\frac{\delta\mathcal{F}}{\delta\bf E}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\nabla\,\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}\,\frac{\delta\mathcal{G}}{\delta\bf B}-\frac{\delta\mathcal{G}}{\delta\bf E}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\nabla\,\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}\,\frac{\delta\mathcal{F}}{\delta\bf B}\right),

where we introduced the definition

\frac{\delta^{\star}{\mathcal{F}}}{\delta{\bf E}}\;\equiv\;\frac{\delta\mathcal{F}}{\delta\bf E}\;+\;\mathbb{P}_{\|}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\nabla\,\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}\,\frac{\delta\mathcal{F}}{\delta\bf E},

(16)

and

\displaystyle\left\{\frac{\delta{\mathcal{F}}}{\delta F_{\rm gc}},\;\frac{\delta\mathcal{G}}{\delta F_{\rm gc}}\right\}_{\rm gc}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{{\bf B}^{*}}{B_{\|}^{*}}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\left(\nabla f\;\frac{\partial g}{\partial p_{\|}}\;-\;\frac{\partial f}{\partial p_{\|}}\;\nabla g\right)\;-\;\frac{c\widehat{{\sf b}}}{qB_{\|}^{*}}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\left(\nabla f\,\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}\,\nabla g\right),

(17)

\displaystyle\frac{\delta^{\star}{\mathcal{G}}}{\delta{\bf E}}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\left\{{\bf X},\;\frac{\delta{\mathcal{F}}}{\delta F_{\rm gc}}\right\}_{\rm gc}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle{\bf G}^{\star}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\left(\frac{{\bf B}^{*}}{B_{\|}^{*}}\;\frac{\partial f}{\partial p_{\|}}\;+\;\frac{c\widehat{{\sf b}}}{qB_{\|}^{*}}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}\,\nabla f\right),

(18)

\displaystyle\frac{\delta^{\star}{\mathcal{F}}}{\delta{\bf E}}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\left\{{\bf X},\;{\bf X}\right\}_{\rm gc}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\frac{\delta^{\star}{\mathcal{G}}}{\delta{\bf E}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\,\frac{c\widehat{{\sf b}}}{qB_{\|}^{*}}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,{\bf F}^{\star}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}\,{\bf G}^{\star},

(19)

with the notation
(f,g,k)\equiv(\delta{\mathcal{F}}/\delta F_{\rm gc},\delta{\mathcal{G}}/\delta F_{\rm gc},\delta{\mathcal{K}}/\delta F_{\rm gc})
and
({\bf F}^{\star},{\bf G}^{\star},{\bf K}^{\star})\equiv(\delta^{\star}{\mathcal{F}}/\delta{\bf E},\delta^{\star}{\mathcal{G}}/\delta{\bf E},\delta^{\star}{\mathcal{K}}/\delta{\bf E})
. It is clear that the bracket (
15
) is antisymmetric and, since it is bilinear in functional derivatives, it satisfies the Leibnitz property.

We now need to verify that the guiding-center bracket (
15
) satisfies the Jacobi property (
14
). According to the Bracket theorem
Morrison_2013
, the proof of the Jacobi property involves only the explicit dependence of the Poisson operator
{\sf J}_{\rm gc}^{ab}(F_{\rm gc},{\bf B})
, where we note that the dependence on the magnetic field
{\bf B}
enters through the guiding-center Poisson bracket (
6
), while the electric field
{\bf E}
is explicitly absent. Hence, we can therefore write the double-bracket involving three arbitrary guiding-center functionals
({\mathcal{F}},{\mathcal{G}},{\mathcal{K}})
:

\displaystyle\left[[{\mathcal{F}},{\mathcal{G}}]_{\rm gc},\frac{}{}{\mathcal{K}}\right]_{\rm gc}^{\rm P}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int_{Z}F_{\rm gc}\left\{\frac{\delta^{P}[{\mathcal{F}},{\mathcal{G}}]_{\rm gc}}{\delta F_{\rm gc}},\frac{\delta\mathcal{K}}{\delta F_{\rm gc}}\right\}_{\rm gc}+\,4\pi q\int_{Z}F_{\rm gc}\;\frac{\delta^{\star}{\mathcal{K}}}{\delta{\bf E}}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\left\{{\bf X},\frac{\delta^{P}[{\mathcal{F}},{\mathcal{G}}]_{\rm gc}}{\delta F_{\rm gc}}\right\}_{\rm gc}

(20)

\displaystyle-\;4\pi c\int_{X}\frac{\delta^{P}[{\mathcal{F}},{\mathcal{G}}]_{\rm gc}}{\delta{\bf B}}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\nabla\,\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}\,\frac{\delta\mathcal{K}}{\delta\bf E},

where the Poisson functional derivative
\delta^{P}[{\mathcal{F}},{\mathcal{G}}]/\delta F_{\rm gc}
only involves variations of the Poisson operator
\partial{\sf J}_{\rm gc}^{ab}(F_{\rm gc},{\bf B})/\partial F_{\rm gc}
in the Vlasov and Interaction sub-brackets, while
\delta^{P}[{\mathcal{F}},{\mathcal{G}}]_{\rm gc}/\delta{\bf B}
in the Maxwell sub-bracket involves the explicit dependence of
{\sf J}_{\rm gc}^{ab}(F_{\rm gc},{\bf B})
on the magnetic field
{\bf B}
appearing through
(\widehat{{\sf b}},{\bf B}^{*},B_{\|}^{*})
in the guiding-center Poisson bracket (
6
) and the dyadic tensor (
5
), where

\left.\begin{array}[]{rcl}\delta{\bf B}^{*}&=&\delta{\bf B}+\nabla\,\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}\,(\mathbb{P}_{\|}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\delta{\bf B})\\
(c/q)\,\delta\widehat{{\sf b}}&=&\delta{\bf B}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\partial\mathbb{P}_{\|}/\partial p_{\|}\end{array}\right\},

(21)

with
\mathbb{P}_{\|}
defined in Eq. (
5
).

II.1
Vlasov and Interaction sub-brackets

From the guiding-center Vlasov-Maxwell bracket (
15
), we find the Poisson functional derivative

\displaystyle\frac{\delta^{P}[{\mathcal{F}},{\mathcal{G}}]_{\rm gc}}{\delta F_{\rm gc}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left\{f,\;g\right\}_{\rm gc}\;-\;4\pi q\left({\bf F}^{\star}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\left\{{\bf X},\;g\right\}_{\rm gc}-{\bf G}^{\star}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\left\{{\bf X},\;f\right\}_{\rm gc}\right)\;+\;(4\pi q)^{2}\left({\bf F}^{\star}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\left\{{\bf X},\;{\bf X}\right\}_{\rm gc}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,{\bf G}^{\star}\right),

(22)

where the functional derivatives
(f,g;{\bf F}^{\star},{\bf G}^{\star})
are left intact according to the Bracket Theorem. Hence, the Vlasov sub-bracket in Eq. (
20
) includes the terms

\displaystyle\left\{\frac{\delta^{P}[{\mathcal{F}},{\mathcal{G}}]_{\rm gc}}{\delta F_{\rm gc}},\frac{\delta\mathcal{K}}{\delta F_{\rm gc}}\right\}_{\rm gc}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left\{\left\{f,\;g\right\}_{\rm gc},k\right\}_{\rm gc}\;-\;4\pi q\left\{\left({\bf F}^{\star}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\left\{{\bf X},\;g\right\}_{\rm gc}-{\bf G}^{\star}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\left\{{\bf X},\;f\right\}_{\rm gc}\right),k\right\}_{\rm gc}

(23)

\displaystyle+\;(4\pi q)^{2}\left\{\left({\bf F}^{\star}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\left\{{\bf X},\;{\bf X}\right\}_{\rm gc}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,{\bf G}^{\star}\right),k\right\}_{\rm gc},

while the Interaction sub-bracket in Eq. (
20
) includes the terms

\displaystyle 4\pi q\,\frac{\delta^{\star}{\mathcal{K}}}{\delta{\bf E}}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\left\{{\bf X},\;\frac{\delta^{P}[{\mathcal{F}},{\mathcal{G}}]_{\rm gc}}{\delta F_{\rm gc}}\right\}_{\rm gc}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 4\pi q\,{\bf K}^{\star}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\left\{{\bf X},\;\left\{f,\;g\right\}_{\rm gc}\right\}_{\rm gc}\;-\;(4\pi q)^{2}{\bf K}^{\star}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\left\{{\bf X},\;\left({\bf F}^{\star}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\left\{{\bf X},\;g\right\}_{\rm gc}-{\bf G}^{\star}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\left\{{\bf X},\;f\right\}_{\rm gc}\right)\right\}_{\rm gc}

(24)

\displaystyle+\;(4\pi q)^{3}{\bf K}^{\star}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\left\{{\bf X},\;\left({\bf F}^{\star}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\left\{{\bf X},\;{\bf X}\right\}_{\rm gc}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,{\bf G}^{\star}\right)\right\}_{\rm gc}.

We note, here, that the ordering
(4\pi q)^{n}
, with
0\leq n\leq 3
, will be useful in verifying the Jacobi property of the guiding-center Vlasov-Maxwell bracket (
15
), i.e., the Jacobi property must hold separately for each power
n
.

At the zeroth order in
4\pi q
in Eqs. (
23
)-(
24
), the Vlasov-Maxwell contributions to the guiding-center Jacobi property (
14
) are

\int_{Z}F_{\rm gc}\left(\left\{\{f,\;g\}_{\rm gc},\frac{}{}k\right\}_{\rm gc}+\left\{\{g,\;k\}_{\rm gc},\frac{}{}f\right\}_{\rm gc}+\left\{\{k,\;f\}_{\rm gc},\frac{}{}g\right\}_{\rm gc}\right)\;\equiv\;\int_{Z}F_{\rm gc}\,{\sf Jac}_{VM}^{(0)}[f,g,k].

(25)

Next, at the first order in
4\pi q
in Eqs. (
23
)-(
24
), the Vlasov-Interaction (VI) contributions to the guiding-center Jacobi property (
14
) are

4\pi q\int_{Z}F_{\rm gc}\left({\sf Jac}_{VI}^{(1)}[f,g;{\bf K}^{\star}]\;+\;{\sf Jac}_{VI}^{(1)}[g,k;{\bf F}^{\star}]\;+\;{\sf Jac}_{VI}^{(1)}[k,f;{\bf G}^{\star}]\right),

(26)

where

\displaystyle{\sf Jac}_{VI}^{(1)}[f,g;{\bf K}^{\star}]

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle{\bf K}^{\star}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\left\{{\bf X},\frac{}{}\{f,g\}_{\rm gc}\right\}_{\rm gc}\;+\;\left\{{\bf K}^{\star}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\{{\bf X},g\}_{\rm gc},\frac{}{}f\right\}_{\rm gc}\;-\;\left\{{\bf K}^{\star}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\{{\bf X},f\}_{\rm gc},\frac{}{}g\right\}_{\rm gc}

(27)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle{\sf Jac}_{VM}^{(1)}[f,g;{\bf K}^{*}]\;+\;\left\{{\bf K}^{\star},\;f\right\}_{\rm gc}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\{{\bf X},\;g\}_{\rm gc}-\left\{{\bf K}^{\star},\;g\right\}_{\rm gc}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\{{\bf X},\;f\}_{\rm gc},

which is obtained after using the Leibnitz formula:

\left\{{\bf K}^{\star}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\{{\bf X},g\}_{\rm gc},\frac{}{}f\right\}_{\rm gc}\;=\;\{{\bf K}^{\star},f\}_{\rm gc}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\{{\bf X},g\}_{\rm gc}\;+\;{\bf K}^{\star}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\left\{\{{\bf X},g\}_{\rm gc},\frac{}{}f\right\}_{\rm gc},

and the first-order Vlasov-Maxwell (VM) contribution is defined as

{\sf Jac}_{VM}^{(1)}[f,g;{\bf K}^{*}]\;\equiv\;K_{i}^{\star}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\left(\left\{X^{i},\frac{}{}\{f,g\}_{\rm gc}\right\}_{\rm gc}+\left\{f,\frac{}{}\{g,\;X^{i}\}_{\rm gc}\right\}_{\rm gc}+\left\{g,\frac{}{}\{X^{i},f\}_{\rm gc}\right\}_{\rm gc}\right).

(28)

At the second order in
4\pi q
in Eqs. (
23
)-(
24
), the Vlasov-Interaction contributions to the guiding-center Jacobi property (
14
) are

(4\pi q)^{2}\int_{Z}F_{\rm gc}\left({\sf Jac}_{VI}^{(2)}[f;{\bf G}^{\star},{\bf K}^{\star}]\;+\;{\sf Jac}_{VI}^{(2)}[g;{\bf K}^{\star},{\bf F}^{\star}]\;+\;{\sf Jac}_{VI}^{(2)}[k;{\bf F}^{\star},{\bf G}^{\star}]\right),

(29)

where

\displaystyle{\sf Jac}_{VI}^{(2)}[f;{\bf G}^{\star},{\bf K}^{\star}]

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle{\bf K}^{\star}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\left\{{\bf X},\frac{}{}{\bf G}^{\star}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\{{\bf X},\;f\}_{\rm gc}\right\}_{\rm gc}-{\bf G}^{\star}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\left\{{\bf X},\frac{}{}{\bf K}^{\star}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\{{\bf X},\;f\}_{\rm gc}\right\}_{\rm gc}+\left\{{\bf G}^{\star}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\{{\bf X},{\bf X}\}_{\rm gc}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,{\bf K}^{\star},\frac{}{}f\right\}_{\rm gc}

(30)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle{\sf Jac}_{VM}^{(2)}[f;{\bf G}^{\star},{\bf K}^{\star}]\;+\;\left\{{\bf G}^{\star},\;f\right\}_{\rm gc}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\{{\bf X},{\bf X}\}_{\rm gc}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,{\bf K}^{\star}\;+\;{\bf G}^{\star}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\{{\bf X},{\bf X}\}_{\rm gc}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\left\{{\bf K}^{\star},\;f\right\}_{\rm gc}

\displaystyle+\;\left({\bf K}^{\star}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\left\{{\bf X},{\bf G}^{\star}\right\}_{\rm gc}-{\bf G}^{\star}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\left\{{\bf X},{\bf K}^{\star}\right\}_{\rm gc}\right)\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\{{\bf X},\;f\}_{\rm gc},

with the second-order Vlasov-Maxwell contribution defined as

{\sf Jac}_{VM}^{(2)}[f;{\bf G}^{\star},{\bf K}^{\star}]\;\equiv\;G_{i}^{*}K_{j}^{*}\left(\left\{X^{j},\frac{}{}\{X^{i},\;f\}_{\rm gc}\right\}_{\rm gc}+\left\{X^{i},\frac{}{}\{f,\;X^{j}\}_{\rm gc}\right\}_{\rm gc}+\left\{f,\frac{}{}\{X^{j},\;X^{i}\}_{\rm gc}\right\}_{\rm gc}\right).

(31)

Lastly, at the third order in
4\pi q
in Eqs. (
23
)-(
24
), the Vlasov-Interaction contributions to the guiding-center Jacobi property (
14
) are

(4\pi q)^{3}\int_{Z}F_{\rm gc}\;{\sf Jac}_{VI}^{(3)}[{\bf F}^{\star},{\bf G}^{\star},{\bf K}^{\star}],

(32)

where

\displaystyle{\sf Jac}_{VI}^{(3)}[{\bf F}^{\star},{\bf G}^{\star},{\bf K}^{\star}]

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle{\bf F}^{\star}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\left\{{\bf X},\frac{}{}{\bf G}^{\star}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\{{\bf X},{\bf X}\}_{\rm gc}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,{\bf K}^{\star}\right\}_{\rm gc}\;+\;{\bf G}^{\star}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\left\{{\bf X},\frac{}{}{\bf K}^{\star}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\{{\bf X},{\bf X}\}_{\rm gc}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,{\bf F}^{\star}\right\}_{\rm gc}

(33)

\displaystyle+\;{\bf K}^{\star}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\left\{{\bf X},\frac{}{}{\bf F}^{\star}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\{{\bf X},{\bf X}\}_{\rm gc}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,{\bf G}^{\star}\right\}_{\rm gc}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle{\sf Jac}_{VM}^{(3)}[{\bf F}^{\star},{\bf G}^{\star},{\bf K}^{\star}]\;+\;\left({\bf F}^{\star}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\left\{{\bf X},{\bf G}^{\star}\right\}_{\rm gc}-{\bf G}^{\star}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\left\{{\bf X},{\bf F}^{\star}\right\}_{\rm gc}\right)\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\{{\bf X},{\bf X}\}_{\rm gc}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,{\bf K}^{\star}

\displaystyle+\;\left({\bf G}^{\star}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\left\{{\bf X},{\bf K}^{\star}\right\}_{\rm gc}-{\bf K}^{\star}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\left\{{\bf X},{\bf G}^{\star}\right\}_{\rm gc}\right)\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\{{\bf X},{\bf X}\}_{\rm gc}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,{\bf F}^{\star}

\displaystyle+\;\left({\bf K}^{\star}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\left\{{\bf X},{\bf F}^{\star}\right\}_{\rm gc}-{\bf F}^{\star}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\left\{{\bf X},{\bf K}^{\star}\right\}_{\rm gc}\right)\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\{{\bf X},{\bf X}\}_{\rm gc}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,{\bf G}^{\star},

with the third-order Vlasov-Maxwell contribution defined as

{\sf Jac}_{VM}^{(3)}[{\bf F}^{\star},{\bf G}^{\star},{\bf K}^{\star}]\;\equiv\;F_{i}^{*}G_{i}^{*}K_{\ell}^{*}\left(\left\{X^{i},\frac{}{}\{X^{j},\;X^{\ell}\}_{\rm gc}\right\}_{\rm gc}+\left\{X^{j},\frac{}{}\{X^{\ell},\;X^{i}\}_{\rm gc}\right\}_{\rm gc}+\left\{X^{\ell},\frac{}{}\{X^{i},\;X^{j}\}_{\rm gc}\right\}_{\rm gc}\right).

(34)

II.2
Jacobian term

The next set of terms in Eq. (
20
) come from contributions due to the magnetic variations of the guiding-center Jacobian, where
\delta B_{\|}^{*}
is given by Eq. (
70
). The Jacobian contributions to the Jacobi property (
14
) are given by Eq. (
72
):

4\pi q\int_{Z}F_{\rm gc}\left({\sf Jac}_{J}^{(1)}[f,g;{\bf K}^{\star}]+\leftturn\right)+(4\pi q)^{2}\int_{Z}F_{\rm gc}\left({\sf Jac}_{J}^{(2)}[f;{\bf G}^{\star},{\bf K}^{\star}]+\leftturn\right)+(4\pi q)^{3}\int_{Z}F_{\rm gc}\;{\sf Jac}_{J}^{(3)}[{\bf F}^{\star},{\bf G}^{\star},{\bf K}^{\star}],

(35)

where
\leftturn
denotes cyclic permutations of the functionals
({\mathcal{F}},{\mathcal{G}},{\mathcal{K}})
and the Jacobian contributions are

\displaystyle{\sf Jac}_{J}^{(1)}[f,g;{\bf K}^{\star}]

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left\{X^{i},K_{i}^{\star}\right\}_{\rm gc}\;\{f,g\}_{\rm gc},

(36)

{\bf F}^{\star}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}\,\frac{c\widehat{{\sf b}}}{qB_{\|}^{*}}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\nabla{\bf K}^{\star}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,{\bf G}^{\star}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}\,\frac{c\widehat{{\sf b}}}{qB_{\|}^{*}}-{\bf G}^{\star}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}\,\frac{c\widehat{{\sf b}}}{qB_{\|}^{*}}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\nabla{\bf K}^{\star}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,{\bf F}^{\star}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}\,\frac{c\widehat{{\sf b}}}{qB_{\|}^{*}}\;=\;\left(\frac{c\widehat{{\sf b}}}{qB_{\|}^{*}}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\nabla\,\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}\,{\bf K}^{\star}\right){\bf F}^{\star}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\{{\bf X},{\bf X}\}_{\rm gc}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,{\bf G}^{\star}.

Hence, by combining Eqs. (
33
), (
38
), and (
60
), we finally obtain

{\sf Jac}_{VI}^{(3)}[{\bf F}^{\star},{\bf G}^{\star},{\bf K}^{\star}]+{\sf Jac}_{J}^{(3)}[{\bf F}^{\star},{\bf G}^{\star},{\bf K}^{\star}]+{\sf Jac}_{M}^{(3)}[{\bf F}^{\star},{\bf G}^{\star},{\bf K}^{\star}]\;=\;{\sf Jac}_{VM}^{(3)}[{\bf F}^{\star},{\bf G}^{\star},{\bf K}^{\star}],

(61)

where the third-order Vlasov-Maxwell term
{\sf Jac}_{VM}^{(3)}[f;{\bf G}^{\star},{\bf K}^{\star}]
is defined in Eq. (
34
).

III
Proof of the Jacobi property

By combining the Vlasov-Maxwell contributions (
25
), (
28
), (
31
), and (
34
), the Jacobi property (
14
) of the guiding-center Vlasov-Maxwell bracket (
15
) can now be expressed as a series in powers of
(4\pi q)
up to third order:

\displaystyle{\mathcal{J}ac}[{\mathcal{F}},{\mathcal{G}},{\mathcal{K}}]

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int_{Z}F_{\rm gc}\;{\sf Jac}_{VM}^{(0)}[f,g,k]\;+\;4\pi q\int_{Z}F_{\rm gc}\left({\sf Jac}_{VM}^{(1)}[f,g;{\bf K}^{\star}]+\leftturn\right)

(62)

\displaystyle+\;(4\pi q)^{2}\int_{Z}F_{\rm gc}\left({\sf Jac}_{VM}^{(2)}[f;{\bf G}^{\star},{\bf K}^{\star}]+\leftturn\right)+(4\pi q)^{3}\int_{Z}F_{\rm gc}\;{\sf Jac}_{VM}^{(3)}[{\bf F}^{\star},{\bf G}^{\star},{\bf K}^{\star}]

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int_{Z}F_{\rm gc}\left(\left\{\{f,\;g\}_{\rm gc},\frac{}{}k\right\}_{\rm gc}+\left\{\{g,\;k\}_{\rm gc},\frac{}{}f\right\}_{\rm gc}+\left\{\{k,\;f\}_{\rm gc},\frac{}{}g\right\}_{\rm gc}\right)

\displaystyle+4\pi q\int_{Z}F_{\rm gc}\left[F_{i}^{\star}\left(\left\{\{X^{i},\;g\}_{\rm gc},\frac{}{}k\right\}_{\rm gc}+\left\{\{g,\;k\}_{\rm gc},\frac{}{}X^{i}\right\}_{\rm gc}+\left\{\{k,\;X^{i}\}_{\rm gc},\frac{}{}g\right\}_{\rm gc}\right)\;+\;\leftturn\right]

\displaystyle+(4\pi q)^{2}\int_{Z}F_{\rm gc}\left[F_{i}^{\star}\,G_{j}^{\star}\left(\left\{\{X^{i},\;X^{j}\}_{\rm gc},\frac{}{}k\right\}_{\rm gc}+\left\{\{X^{j},\;k\}_{\rm gc},\frac{}{}X^{i}\right\}_{\rm gc}+\left\{\{k,\;X^{i}\}_{\rm gc},\frac{}{}X^{j}\right\}_{\rm gc}\right)\;+\;\leftturn\right]

\displaystyle+(4\pi q)^{3}\int_{Z}F_{\rm gc}\left[F_{i}^{\star}\,G_{j}^{\star}\,K_{\ell}^{\star}\left(\left\{\{X^{i},X^{j}\}_{\rm gc},\frac{}{}X^{\ell}\right\}_{\rm gc}+\left\{\{X^{j},X^{\ell}\}_{\rm gc},\frac{}{}X^{i}\right\}_{\rm gc}+\left\{\{X^{\ell},X^{i}\}_{\rm gc},\frac{}{}X^{j}\right\}_{\rm gc}\right)\right],

where all additional terms have cancelled out exactly as shown in Sec.
II
. In Eq. (
62
), it is clear that the Jacobi property of the guiding-center Vlasov-Maxwell bracket (
15
) is inherited from the Jacobi property (
8
) of the guiding-center Poisson bracket (
6
), since each term in Eq. (
62
) vanishes identically because of this latter property. Hence, the Jacobi property for the guiding-center Vlasov-Maxwell bracket (
15
) holds under the condition (
9
), which holds according to Eq. (
7
).

IV
Summary

The proof of the Jacobi property of a functional bracket that forms the basis for the Hamiltonian structure of reduced Vlasov-Maxwell equations is often a challenging task (see, for example, Ref.
Brizard_2016
). While we can also appeal to rigorous theoretical grounds for the validity of the Jacobi property of the guiding-center Vlasov-Maxwell bracket (
15
), an explicit proof of Eq. (
14
) was presented in these notes. As expected, the Jacobi property is inherited from the Jacobi property (
8
) of the guiding-center Poisson bracket (
6
).

Appendix A
Poisson-bracket Identities

In this Appendix, we derive several identities associated with the guiding-center Poisson bracket (
6
). First, we have the integral identity

\int_{Z}B_{\|}^{*}\,\{f,\;g\}_{\rm gc}\;=\;\int_{Z}\frac{\partial}{\partial Z^{\alpha}}\left(B_{\|}^{*}\,f\;\left\{Z^{\alpha},\;g\right\}_{\rm gc}\right)\;=\;0,

(63)

which holds for any functions
(f,g)
. This identity yields the formula
\int_{Z}B_{\|}^{*}\{f,g\}_{\rm gc}\,k=\int_{Z}B_{\|}^{*}\,f\,\{g,k\}_{\rm gc}
after integration by parts is performed.

Next, for any arbitrary vector-valued function
{\bf R}
, we find the divergence identity

\left\{X^{i},\frac{}{}R_{i}\right\}_{\rm gc}\;=\;\frac{c\widehat{{\sf b}}}{qB_{\|}^{*}}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\nabla\,\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}\,{\bf R}\;+\;\frac{{\bf B}^{*}}{B_{\|}^{*}}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\frac{\partial\bf R}{\partial p_{\|}},

(64)

where summation over repeated indices on the left side is implied. In addition, for an arbitrary function
f
, we find

\{{\bf R},\,f\}_{\rm gc}\;=\;\{{\bf X},\,f\}_{\rm gc}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\nabla{\bf R}\;-\;\left(\frac{{\bf B}^{*}}{B_{\|}^{*}}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\nabla f\right)\,\frac{\partial\bf R}{\partial p_{\|}},

(65)

where
\{{\bf X},f\}_{\rm gc}=({\bf B}^{*}/B_{\|}^{*})\,\partial f/\partial p_{\|}+(c\widehat{{\sf b}}/qB_{\|}^{*})\,\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}\,\nabla f
.

Lastly, for two arbitrary functions
(f,g)
, we find the Poisson-bracket identity

\left(\frac{c}{q}\right)\,\nabla f\,\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}\,\nabla g\;=\;\left\{{\bf X},\,g\right\}_{\rm gc}\;{\bf B}^{*}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\nabla f\;-\;\left\{{\bf X},\,f\right\}_{\rm gc}\;{\bf B}^{*}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\nabla g\;-\;{\bf B}^{*}\;\{f,\,g\}_{\rm gc},

(66)

which is derived from the identity

\widehat{{\sf b}}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}\,\left[{\bf B}^{*}\frac{}{}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}\,\left(\nabla f\,\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}\,\frac{}{}\nabla g\right)\right]\;=\;\left\{\begin{array}[]{l}\widehat{{\sf b}}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,(\nabla f\,\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}\,\nabla g)\,{\bf B}^{*}\;-\;B_{\|}^{*}\;\nabla f\,\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}\,\nabla g\\
\\
\widehat{{\sf b}}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}\,\nabla f\;({\bf B}^{*}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\nabla g)\;-\;\widehat{{\sf b}}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}\,\nabla g\;({\bf B}^{*}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\nabla f)\end{array}\right.

Next, we use the identity

\displaystyle\{{\bf X},f\}_{\rm gc}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}\,\{{\bf X},g\}_{\rm gc}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left(\frac{{\bf B}^{*}}{B_{\|}^{*}}\,\frac{\partial f}{\partial p_{\|}}+\frac{c\widehat{{\sf b}}}{qB_{\|}^{*}}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}\,\nabla f\right)\,\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}\,\left(\frac{{\bf B}^{*}}{B_{\|}^{*}}\,\frac{\partial g}{\partial p_{\|}}+\frac{c\widehat{{\sf b}}}{qB_{\|}^{*}}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}\,\nabla g\right)

(67)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{{\bf B}^{*}}{B_{\|}^{*}}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}\,\left[\frac{\partial f}{\partial p_{\|}}\left(\frac{c\widehat{{\sf b}}}{qB_{\|}^{*}}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}\,\nabla g\right)-\frac{\partial g}{\partial p_{\|}}\left(\frac{c\widehat{{\sf b}}}{qB_{\|}^{*}}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}\,\nabla f\right)\right]\;+\;\left(\frac{c\widehat{{\sf b}}}{qB_{\|}^{*}}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}\,\nabla f\right)\,\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}\,\left(\frac{c\widehat{{\sf b}}}{qB_{\|}^{*}}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}\,\nabla g\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{c}{qB_{\|}^{*}}\left[\left(\nabla f\,\frac{\partial g}{\partial p_{\|}}-\frac{\partial f}{\partial p_{\|}}\,\nabla g\right)\;-\;\widehat{{\sf b}}\;\{f,\;g\}_{\rm gc}\right],

to obtain

\displaystyle\nabla\,\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}\,{\bf R}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\left(\{{\bf X},f\}_{\rm gc}\frac{}{}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}\,\{{\bf X},g\}_{\rm gc}\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{c}{qB_{\|}^{*}}\left[\nabla\,\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}\,{\bf R}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\left(\nabla f\,\frac{\partial g}{\partial p_{\|}}-\frac{\partial f}{\partial p_{\|}}\,\nabla g\right)\;-\;\widehat{{\sf b}}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\nabla\,\mbox{\boldmath$\times$}\,{\bf R}\;\{f,\;g\}_{\rm gc}\right]

(68)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\{{\bf R},f\}_{\rm gc}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\{{\bf X},g\}_{\rm gc}\;-\;\{{\bf R},g\}_{\rm gc}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\{{\bf X},f\}_{\rm gc}

\displaystyle+\;\frac{\partial\bf R}{\partial p_{\|}}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\left[\{{\bf X},\,g\}_{\rm gc}\left(\frac{{\bf B}^{*}}{B_{\|}^{*}}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\nabla f\right)\;-\;\{{\bf X},\,f\}_{\rm gc}\left(\frac{{\bf B}^{*}}{B_{\|}^{*}}\,\mbox{\boldmath$\cdot$}\,\nabla g\right)\right].
