Generalizing Cographs to 2 2 -cographs
James Oxley, Jagdeep Singh

Abstract
. A graph in which every connected induced subgraph has a disconnected complement is called a cograph. Such graphs are precisely the graphs that do not have the 4-vertex path as an induced subgraph. We define a 2 2 -cograph to be a graph in which the complement of every 2 2 -connected induced subgraph is not 2 2 -connected. We show that, like cographs, 2 2 -cographs can be recursively defined. But, unlike cographs, 2 2 -cographs are closed under induced minors. We characterize the class of non- 2 2 -cographs for which every proper induced minor is a 2 2 -cograph. We further find the finitely many members of this class whose complements are also induced-minor-minimal non- 2 2 -cographs.

Lemma 2.5

. Since

, we can write

and

The following recursive-generation result for cographs is due to Royle

Lemma 2.6

Let

For graphs

Lemma 3.1

Let

Proof.

-cograph for all edges

of

, the complement of

be a red 2-cut

. Since

is empty, applying Proposition

to

gives that

is contained in

. Let

be a vertex in

and

be the two green neighbours of

has exactly two components. The second component must have

Let

∎

meets only four green edges. Assume that
