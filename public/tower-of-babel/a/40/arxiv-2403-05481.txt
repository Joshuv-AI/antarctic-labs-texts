The Zilber–Pink conjecture for products of curves with highly degenerate reduction

Abstract

0.2.
Katz–Rabinoff–Zureick-Brown’s Manin–Mumford bound

In

1.3.
Conclusion of proof

The conclusion of the proof of Proposition

2.
The Katz–Rabinoff–Zureick-Brown strategy

Clearly the above strategy does not tell us about

\overline{\mathbb{F}}_{p}
-points of the special fibre. Secondly, at each

We now explain how the Katz–Rabinoff–Zureick-Brown strategy can be used to get around these issues, in the case of highly degenerate reduction. This allows us to deduce Theorem
1
from Ax–Schanuel in the same manner as Proposition
1
. We first recall some things from
[

]

[

]

\Gamma
denote the dual graph of
\mathcal{X}_{\overline{\mathbb{F}}_{p}}
. For

,

denote the corresponding irreducible components. Let

denote the genus of

(1)

(2)

Let

Completion of proof

Via an isomorphism
