Conditions for semi-boundedness and discreteness of the spectrum to Schrödinger operator and some nonlinear PDEs
Leonid Zelenko

Abstract
For Schrödinger operator H = − Δ + V ( 𝐱 ) ⋅ H=-\Delta+V({\mathbf{x}})\cdot , acting in the space L 2 ​ ( 𝐑 d ) ​ ( d ≥ 3 ) L_{2}({\mathbf{R}}^{d})\,(d\geq 3) , necessary and sufficient conditions for semi-boundedness and discreteness of its spectrum.are obtained without assumption that the potential V ⁡ ( 𝐱 ) V({\mathbf{x}}) is bounded below. By reduction of the problem to investigation of existence of regular solutions for Riccati PDE necessary conditions for discreteness of the spectrum of operator H H are obtained under assumption that it is bounded below. These results are similar to ones obtained by author in [ 26 ] for the one-dimensional case. Furthermore, sufficient conditions for the semi-boundedness and discreteness of the spectrum of H H are obtained in terms of a non-increasing rearrangement, mathematical expectation and standard deviation from the latter for positive part V + ​ ( 𝐱 ) V_{+}({\mathbf{x}}) of the potential V ⁡ ( 𝐱 ) V({\mathbf{x}}) on compact domains that go to infinity, under certain restrictions for its negative part V − ​ ( 𝐱 ) V_{-}({\mathbf{x}}) . This type of optimization refers to a divergence constrained transportation problem.

6.1.

Consider the quantity:
