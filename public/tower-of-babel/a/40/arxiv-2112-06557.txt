MIN-turns and MAX-turns in k k -Dyck paths: a pure generating function approach
Helmut Prodinger

Abstract
. k k -Dyck paths differ from ordinary Dyck paths by using an up-step of length k k . We analyze at which level the path is after the s s -th up-step and before the ( s + 1 ) (s+1) st up-step. In honour of Rainer Kemp who studied a related concept 40 years ago the terms max -terms and min -terms are used. Results are obtained by an appropriate use of trivariate generating functions; practically no combinatorial arguments are used.

Introduction

The objects of our interest in this paper are
k
-Dyck paths, having up-steps
(1,k)
and down-steps
(1,-1)
, and never go below the
x
-axis.
At the end, they reach the
x
-axis, but we also need versions that end at a prescribed level different from 0.
Much material about such paths can be found in
[
9
]
,
[
8
]
and in many standard textbooks on combinatorics.

We consider
max
-turns, where each up-step ends and
min
-turns, where each up-step starts.

The
k
-Dyck paths can only exist for a length of the form
(k+1)N
, which is clear for combinatorial reasons or otherwise.
We want to know the average level of the
s
-th
max
-turn resp.
min
-turn, amoung all
k
-Dyck of the same length.
In order to do this, we sum the level of the
s
-th
max
-turn resp.
min
-turn, over all
k
-Dyck paths of length
(k+1)N
.
The generating functions we obtain, enumerate the (partial)
k
-Dyck paths that end at a prescribed level
j
as well; this is of interest in
[
2
]
and
later in
[
8
]
, in the section on Hoppy’s walks.

To get the average, one only needs to divide by the total number of such
k
-Dyck paths.
Our main achievement is to get the
fully explicit
functions

\displaystyle\textsc{max}(z,w)=\sum_{N\geq 0,\,s\geq 1}z^{(k+1)N}w^{s}[

cumulative level of the
s
-th
max
-turn

\displaystyle\text{in all $k$-Dyck paths of length $(k+1)N$}]

and

\displaystyle\textsc{min}(z,w)=\sum_{N\geq 0,\,s\geq 1}z^{(k+1)N}w^{s}[

cumulative level of the
s
-th
min
-turn

\displaystyle\text{in all $k$-Dyck paths of length $(k+1)N$}].

As a bonus we get
\textsc{osc}(z,w):=\textsc{max}(z,w)-\textsc{min}(z,w)
; this cumulates the lengths of the wavy line between
the
s
-th
max
-turn and
s
-th
min
-turn, see Figure
1
. The function
\textsc{osc}(z,w)
is somewhat simpler than

\textsc{min}(z,w)
and
\textsc{max}(z,w)
, because of cancellations. In this way, we recover some of the results
from
[
1
]
without resorting to any bijective combinatorics. Note that such a wavy line might have length zero as well, if two
up-steps follow each other immediately. Rainer Kemp in
[
5
]
has considered
max
- and
min
-turns for Dyck paths,
although his definitions were slightly different (peaks and valleys).

The key to the success of our method is the simple but perhaps unusual identity

\sum_{i\geq 0}\big([z^{i}]f(z)\big)\cdot y^{i}=f(y).

2.
Some basic observations

As we will see soon, the equation
u=z+zwu^{k+1}
plays a major role when enumerating
k
-Dyck paths. The equation is of the form

u=z\Phi(u)
, with
\Phi(u)=1+wu^{k+1}
, so it is amenable to the Lagrange inversion
[
4
]
, and the coefficients of the inverse function

u=u(z)
can be computed:

\displaystyle[z^{(k+1)N+1}]u

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{(k+1)N+1}[u^{(k+1)N}](1+wu^{k+1})^{(k+1)N+1}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{(k+1)N+1}w^{N}\binom{(k+1)N+1}{N}.

One solution of the equation, which is analytic at the origin and has combinatorial significance, is called
\overline{u}
. We will use the formula
(again by the Lagrange inversion formula)

\overline{u}=z\sum_{N\geq 0}w^{N}z^{(k+1)N}\frac{1}{kN+1}\binom{(k+1)N}{N}.

(1)

We also need the specialization where
w=1
, which we call
\widehat{u}
, which satisfies the equation
u=z+zu^{k+1}
:

\widehat{u}=z\sum_{\lambda\geq 0}\frac{1}{(k+1)\lambda+1}\binom{1+(k+1)\lambda}{\lambda}z^{(k+1)\lambda}

(2)

and (again by the Lagrange inversion formula)

\widehat{u}^{-k}=z^{-k}-z\sum_{\lambda\geq 0}\frac{k}{\lambda+1}\binom{(k+1)\lambda}{\lambda}z^{(k+1)\lambda}.

(3)

3.
min
-turns

After these classical observations about the implicit function, we move to the treatment of
min
-turns.
Our method of choice is
adding a new slice
.
The following substitution is essential for adding a new slice (which is an up-step, followed by a maximal sequence of down-steps):

u^{j}\longrightarrow zw\sum_{0\leq i\leq j+k}z^{i}u^{j+k-i}=\frac{zwu^{k+1}}{u-z}u^{j}-\frac{wz^{k+2}}{u-z}z^{j}.

The substitution tells us what can become of a partial
k
-ary path landing at level
j
when adding (attaching) another slice landing on level
j+k-i
.
The technique of adding-a-new slice is described in
[
3
]
.

Now let
F_{m}(u)=F_{m}(u;z)
be the generating function according to
m
slices;
z
refers to the lengths and
u
to the level of the
m
-th
min
-turn. The substitution leads to

F_{m+1}(u)=\frac{zwu^{k+1}}{u-z}F_{m}(u)-\frac{wz^{k+2}}{u-z}F_{m}(z),\quad F_{0}(u)=1.

Let
F(u)=\sum_{m\geq 0}F_{m}(u)
, so that we do not care about the number
m
anymore, since the variable
w
takes care of it; then

F(u)=F(u;z,w)=1+\frac{zwu^{k+1}}{u-z}F(u)-\frac{wz^{k+2}}{u-z}F(z),

or

F(u)=\frac{u-z-wz^{k+2}F(z)}{u-z-zwu^{k+1}}.

u=\overline{u}
is a factor of the denominator, but for
z
and
u
small, we have
u\sim z
, so this factor must cancel in the numerator as well. This is what
one learns from the kernel method
[
6
]
.
We find

F(z)=\frac{\overline{u}-z}{wz^{k+2}}

and further

F(u)=F(u;z,w)=\frac{u-\overline{u}}{u-z-zwu^{k+1}}.

That ends the computation of the “left” part of the
k
-Dyck path. For the right one, we start at level
h
with an up-step and end eventually at the zero level.
We don’t use the variable
w
here. The kernel method could also be used, but there is a simpler way.
Reading the path from right to left, there is a decomposition when the path leaves a level and never comes back to it.
Recall that
\overline{u}/z
is the generating function of
k
-Dyck paths.
With this, we find for the enumeration of the “right” part

\Big(\frac{\overline{u}}{z}\Big)^{h}z^{h}\Big(\frac{\overline{u}}{z}-1\Big)=\frac{(\overline{u}-z)\overline{u}^{h}}{z};

the minus 1 term happens since the reversed path must end with a down-step. The formula works only for
h\geq 1
, but the
instance
h=0
is not needed (although easy).
The fact that this generating function is essentially a
power
is part of our successful approach.
We now have

\displaystyle\textsc{min}(z,w)

\displaystyle=\sum_{h\geq 1}h[u^{h}]F(u)\cdot\frac{(\overline{u}-z)\overline{u}^{h}}{z}

\displaystyle=\sum_{h\geq 1}[u^{h-1}]\frac{d}{du}F(u)\cdot\frac{(\overline{u}-z)\overline{u}^{h}}{z}

\displaystyle=\frac{(\overline{u}-z)\overline{u}}{z}\cdot\frac{-zu+uzw{u}^{k+1}k+\overline{u}u-\overline{u}zw{u}^{k+1}k-\overline{u}zw{u}^{k+1}}{u(u-z-zwu^{k+1})^{2}}\bigg|_{u=\widehat{u}}

\displaystyle=\frac{(\overline{u}-z)\overline{u}}{z}\cdot\frac{-z\widehat{u}+uzw{\widehat{u}}^{k+1}k+\overline{u}\widehat{u}-\overline{u}zw{\widehat{u}}^{k+1}k-\overline{u}zw{\widehat{u}}^{k+1}}{\widehat{u}(\widehat{u}-z-zw\widehat{u}^{k+1})^{2}}.

A simplification that only uses
\widehat{u}^{k+1}=\frac{\widehat{u}-z}{z}
eventually leads to

\displaystyle\textsc{min}(z,w)=\frac{kw\widehat{u}}{z(1-w)^{2}}+\frac{(\overline{u}-z)}{z^{2}(1-w)^{2}\widehat{u}^{k}}-\frac{(k+1)\overline{u}w}{z(1-w)^{2}}

which can be checked easily, even by hand.

Theorem 1
.

The generating funtion
\textsc{min}(z,w)
where the coefficient of
z^{(k+1)N}w^{s}
refers to the cumulative levels of the
s
-th
min
-turn, is given by

\displaystyle\textsc{min}(z,w)=\frac{kw\widehat{u}}{z(1-w)^{2}}+\frac{(\overline{u}-z)}{z^{2}(1-w)^{2}\widehat{u}^{k}}-\frac{(k+1)\overline{u}w}{z(1-w)^{2}}.

The next step is to expand this function:

\displaystyle[w^{s}]\textsc{min}(z,w)

\displaystyle=[w^{s}]\frac{kw\widehat{u}}{z(1-w)^{2}}-[w^{s}]\frac{(k+1)\overline{u}w}{z(1-w)^{2}}+[w^{s}]\frac{(\overline{u}-z)}{z^{2}(1-w)^{2}\widehat{u}^{k}}

\displaystyle=\frac{sk\widehat{u}}{z}-(k+1)\sum_{i=0}^{s-1}(s-i)[w^{i}]\frac{\overline{u}}{z}

\displaystyle\quad+\sum_{i=0}^{s}(s+1-i)[w^{i}]\frac{z\sum_{N\geq 0}w^{N}z^{(k+1)N}\frac{1}{kN+1}\binom{(k+1)N}{N}-z}{z^{2}\widehat{u}^{k}}

\displaystyle=\frac{sk\widehat{u}}{z}-(k+1)\sum_{i=0}^{s-1}(s-i)[w^{i}]\frac{\overline{u}}{z}

\displaystyle\quad+\sum_{i=0}^{s}(s+1-i)[w^{i}]\frac{\sum_{N\geq 1}w^{N}z^{(k+1)N}\frac{1}{kN+1}\binom{(k+1)N}{N}}{z\widehat{u}^{k}}

\displaystyle=\frac{sk\widehat{u}}{z}-(k+1)\sum_{i=0}^{s-1}(s-i)z^{(k+1)i}\frac{1}{ki+1}\binom{(k+1)i}{i}

\displaystyle\quad+\sum_{i=1}^{s}(s+1-i)\frac{1}{z\widehat{u}^{k}}z^{(k+1)i}\frac{1}{ki+1}\binom{(k+1)i}{i}

\displaystyle=\frac{sk\widehat{u}}{z}-(k+1)\sum_{i=0}^{s-1}(s-i)z^{(k+1)i}\frac{1}{ki+1}\binom{(k+1)i}{i}

\displaystyle\quad+\sum_{i=1}^{s}(s+1-i)z^{(k+1)(i-1)}\frac{1}{ki+1}\binom{(k+1)i}{i}

\displaystyle\quad-\sum_{i=1}^{s}(s+1-i)z^{(k+1)i}\frac{1}{ki+1}\binom{(k+1)i}{i}\sum_{\lambda\geq 0}\frac{k}{\lambda+1}\binom{(k+1)\lambda}{\lambda}z^{(k+1)\lambda}.

And now we read off the coefficient of
z^{(k+1)N}
; we assume that
N\geq s
, otherwise a path would not have an
s
-th
min
-turn:

\displaystyle[w^{s}z^{(k+1)N}]\textsc{min}(z,w)

\displaystyle=sk\frac{1}{kN+1}\binom{(k+1)N}{N}

\displaystyle-\sum_{i=1}^{s}(s+1-i)\frac{1}{ki+1}\binom{(k+1)i}{i}\frac{k}{(N-i)+1}\binom{(k+1)(N-i)}{(N-i)}.

Theorem 2
.

The sum of levels of the
s
-th
min
-turns in all the
k
-Dyck paths of length
(k+1)N
is given by

\displaystyle\frac{sk}{kN+1}\binom{(k+1)N}{N}-\sum_{i=1}^{s}(s+1-i)\frac{1}{ki+1}\binom{(k+1)i}{i}\frac{k}{(N-i)+1}\binom{(k+1)(N-i)}{(N-i)}.

This formula is fully explicit and covers all instances of
N\geq s
.

4.
max
-turns

It is easy to go from a
min
-turn to the next
max
-turn, just by doing
one
up-step. On the level of generating functions, this means

G(u;z,w)=F(u;z,w)wzu^{k}.

The right part of a part (everything from a given
max
-turn until the end) is even easier than before, since level
h
must be reached without further restriction. The result for the “right” side of the path is then

\Big(\frac{\widehat{u}}{z}\Big)^{h+1}z^{h}=\widehat{u}^{h-1}\frac{\widehat{u}^{2}}{z}.

After these preliminaries, we can engage into the big computation:

\displaystyle\textsc{max}(z,w)

\displaystyle=\sum_{h\geq 1}h[u^{h}]G(u)\cdot\widehat{u}^{h-1}\frac{\widehat{u}^{2}}{z}=\frac{\widehat{u}^{2}}{z}\sum_{h\geq 1}[u^{h-1}]\frac{d}{du}G(u)\cdot\widehat{u}^{h-1}

\displaystyle=\frac{wk\widehat{u}}{z(1-w)^{2}}-\frac{wk\overline{u}}{z(1-w)^{2}}+\frac{w(\overline{u}-z)}{z^{2}\widehat{u}^{k}(1-w)^{2}}-\frac{w^{2}\overline{u}}{z(1-w)^{2}}.

The same type of simplifications as before has been applied.

And now we go to the coefficients of this, in a similar style as it was done for the
min
-turns:

\displaystyle[w^{s}]\textsc{max}(z,w)

\displaystyle=s\frac{k\widehat{u}}{z}-\sum_{i=0}^{s-1}(s-i)[w^{i}]\frac{k\overline{u}}{z}

\displaystyle\quad+\frac{1}{z\widehat{u}^{k}}\sum_{i=0}^{s-1}(s-i)[w^{i}]\frac{(\overline{u}-z)}{z}-\sum_{i=0}^{s-2}(s-1-i)[w^{i}]\frac{\overline{u}}{z}

\displaystyle=sk\sum_{N\geq 0}z^{(k+1)N}\frac{1}{kN+1}\binom{(k+1)N}{N}

\displaystyle\quad-\sum_{i=0}^{s-1}(s-i)z^{(k+1)i}\frac{1}{ki+1}\binom{(k+1)i}{i}

\displaystyle\quad+\sum_{i=1}^{s-1}(s-i)z^{(k+1)(i-1)}\frac{1}{ki+1}\binom{(k+1)i}{i}

\displaystyle\quad-\sum_{\lambda\geq 0}\frac{k}{\lambda+1}\binom{(k+1)\lambda}{\lambda}z^{(k+1)\lambda}\sum_{i=1}^{s-1}(s-i)z^{(k+1)i}\frac{1}{ki+1}\binom{(k+1)i}{i}

\displaystyle\quad-\sum_{i=0}^{s-2}(s-1-i)z^{(k+1)i}\frac{1}{ki+1}\binom{(k+1)i}{i}.

And, again for
N\geq s
, we read off the coefficient of
z^{(k+1)N}
:

\displaystyle[w^{s}z^{(k+1)N}]\textsc{max}(z,w)

\displaystyle=sk\frac{1}{kN+1}\binom{(k+1)N}{N}

\displaystyle-\sum_{i=1}^{s-1}(s-i)\frac{1}{ki+1}\binom{(k+1)i}{i}\frac{k}{N-i+1}\binom{(k+1)(N-i)}{N-i}.

Theorem 3
.

The generating funtion
\textsc{min}(z,w)
where the coefficient of
z^{(k+1)N}w^{s}
refers to the cumulative levels of the
s
-th
max
-turn, is given by

\displaystyle\textsc{max}(z,w)

\displaystyle=\frac{wk\widehat{u}}{z(1-w)^{2}}-\frac{wk\overline{u}}{z(1-w)^{2}}+\frac{w(\overline{u}-z)}{z^{2}\widehat{u}^{k}(1-w)^{2}}-\frac{w^{2}\overline{u}}{z(1-w)^{2}}.

The coefficient
[w^{s}z^{(k+1)N}]\textsc{max}(z,w)
is for
N\geq s
given by

\displaystyle\frac{sk}{kN+1}\binom{(k+1)N}{N}-\sum_{i=1}^{s-1}(s-i)\frac{1}{ki+1}\binom{(k+1)i}{i}\frac{k}{N-i+1}\binom{(k+1)(N-i)}{N-i}.

5.
The oscillation

The cumulative function of the
s
-th oscillation (= total length of the
s
-th wavy line, cf. Figure
1
in all paths of length
(k+1)N
) is

\textsc{osc}(z,w):=\textsc{max}(z,w)-\textsc{min}(z,w).

The simplication is easy:

\displaystyle\textsc{osc}(z,w)

\displaystyle=\frac{wk\widehat{u}}{z(1-w)^{2}}-\frac{wk\overline{u}}{z(1-w)^{2}}+\frac{w(\overline{u}-z)}{z^{2}\widehat{u}^{k}(1-w)^{2}}-\frac{w^{2}\overline{u}}{z(1-w)^{2}}

\displaystyle-\bigg[\frac{kw\widehat{u}}{z(1-w)^{2}}+\frac{(\overline{u}-z)}{z^{2}(1-w)^{2}\widehat{u}^{k}}-\frac{(k+1)\overline{u}w}{z(1-w)^{2}}\bigg]

\displaystyle=\frac{w(\overline{u}-z)}{z^{2}\widehat{u}^{k}(1-w)^{2}}-\frac{(\overline{u}-z)}{z^{2}\widehat{u}^{k}(1-w)^{2}}-\frac{w^{2}\overline{u}}{z(1-w)^{2}}+\frac{w\overline{u}}{z(1-w)^{2}}

\displaystyle=\frac{w\overline{u}}{z(1-w)}-\frac{(\overline{u}-z)}{z^{2}\widehat{u}^{k}(1-w)}.

Further, by reading off coefficients (
s\geq 1
),

\displaystyle[w^{s}]

\displaystyle\textsc{osc}(z,w)=\sum_{i=1}^{s}[w^{i}]\frac{\overline{u}}{z}-\sum_{i=0}^{s}[w^{i}]\frac{(\overline{u}-z)}{z^{2}\widehat{u}^{k}}

\displaystyle=\sum_{i=1}^{s}z^{(k+1)i}\frac{1}{kN+1}\binom{(k+1)i}{i}-\frac{1}{z\widehat{u}^{k}}\sum_{i=0}^{s}[w^{i}]\frac{(\overline{u}-z)}{z}

\displaystyle=\sum_{i=1}^{s}z^{(k+1)i}\frac{1}{kN+1}\binom{(k+1)i}{i}-\frac{1}{z\widehat{u}^{k}}\sum_{i=1}^{s}[w^{i}]\sum_{N\geq 1}w^{N}z^{(k+1)N}\frac{1}{kN+1}\binom{(k+1)N}{N}

\displaystyle=\sum_{i=1}^{s}z^{(k+1)i}\frac{1}{kN+1}\binom{(k+1)i}{i}-\frac{1}{z\widehat{u}^{k}}\sum_{i=1}^{s}z^{(k+1)i}\frac{1}{ki+1}\binom{(k+1)i}{i}.

The next step is to read off
[w^{s}z^{(k+1)N}]\textsc{osc}(z,w)
(for
N\geq s
):

\displaystyle[w^{s}

\displaystyle z^{(k+1)N}]\textsc{osc}(z,w)=[z^{(k+1)N}]\sum_{i=1}^{s}z^{(k+1)i}\frac{1}{kN+1}\binom{(k+1)i}{i}

\displaystyle\quad-[z^{(k+1)N}]\frac{1}{z\widehat{u}^{k}}\sum_{i=1}^{s}[w^{i}]z^{(k+1)i}\frac{1}{ki+1}\binom{(k+1)i}{i}

\displaystyle=-[z^{(k+1)N}]\bigg[z^{-k-1}-\sum_{\lambda\geq 0}\frac{k}{\lambda+1}\binom{(k+1)\lambda}{\lambda}z^{(k+1)\lambda}\bigg]\sum_{i=1}^{s}z^{(k+1)i}\frac{1}{ki+1}\binom{(k+1)i}{i}

\displaystyle=[z^{(k+1)N}]\sum_{i=1}^{s}z^{(k+1)i}\frac{1}{ki+1}\binom{(k+1)i}{i}\sum_{\lambda\geq 0}\frac{k}{\lambda+1}\binom{(k+1)\lambda}{\lambda}z^{(k+1)\lambda}

\displaystyle=\sum_{i=1}^{s}z^{(k+1)i}\frac{1}{ki+1}\binom{(k+1)i}{i}\frac{k}{N-i+1}\binom{(k+1)(N-i)}{N-i}.

We summarize the results.

Theorem 4
.

The cumulative (summed over all
k
-Dyck paths of length
(k+1)N
) difference of the
s
-th
max
turn and
the
(s+1)
-st
min
turn (nicknamed the
s
-th wavy line) is given by the explicit formula (for
N\geq s\geq 1
)

\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{s}\frac{1}{ki+1}\binom{(k+1)i}{i}\frac{k}{N-i+1}\binom{(k+1)(N-i)}{N-i}.

(4)

In this paper his result that has been derived by manipulating power series only, was obtained in
[
1
]
by other (mostly bijective) methods.
