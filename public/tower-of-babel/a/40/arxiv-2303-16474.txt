Dimpi and Hemant Kumar Singh

Abstract
. In this paper, we have determined the orbit spaces of free involutions on a finitistic space having mod 2 2 cohomology of the product of three spheres 𝕊 n × 𝕊 m × 𝕊 l , 1 ≤ n ≤ m ≤ l \mathbb{S}^{n}\times\mathbb{S}^{m}\times\mathbb{S}^{l},1\leq n\leq m\leq l . This paper generalizes the results proved by Dotzel et al. [ 6 ] for free involutions on the product of two sphere 𝕊 n × 𝕊 m , 1 ≤ n ≤ m . \mathbb{S}^{n}\times\mathbb{S}^{m},1\leq n\leq m. As an application, we have also derived the Borsuk-Ulam type results.

Let

The study of orbit space

[

[
9

proved that the orbit space of a free involution on
n

\mathbb{R}P^{n}.
Further, the orbit spaces of finite group actions on
n
-sphere
\mathbb{S}^{n}

[
14
,
17
,
18
,
19
]

[

[
22

determined the orbit spaces of free involutions on
\mathbb{S}^{1}\times\mathbb{S}^{2}.
Ritter
[
20
]
extended the Tao’s results to cyclic groups of order
2n.
In 1972, Ozeki et al.
[
16
]
determined the orbit space of a
free circle action on a manifold whose cohomology ring is isomorphic to the product of spheres
\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}\times\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}
with integer coefficients. The orbit spaces
of free actions of
G=\mathbb{Z}_{p},p
a prime, or
G=\mathbb{S}^{d},d=1
or
3,
on a finitistic space
X
whose mod
2

\mathbb{S}^{n}\times\mathbb{S}^{m}
have been studied in
[
6
]
and
[

]
. However, the results for compact manifolds other than spheres are less known. Myers

determined the orbit spaces of free involutions on three dimensional lens spaces. Recently, Dey et al.

discussed the orbit spaces of free involutions on real Milnor manifolds, Dold manifolds,

Continuing this thread of research, this paper is concerned with the study of the orbit spaces of free involutions on a finitistic space having mod

For example: (1) The complex Stiefel manifold

2.
Preliminaries

In this section, we review some basic definitions and results that are used in this paper.
A paracompact Hausdroff space is called finitistic if every open covering has a finite dimensional open refinement. This is the most suitable topological space for the study of the relationship between the cohomology structure of the total space and that of the orbit space of a transformation group. It includes all compact Hausdroff spaces and paracompact spaces with finite covering dimension. Let
G
be a finite cyclic group acting on a finitistic space
X.
The associated Borel fibration is
X\stackrel{{\scriptstyle i}}{{\hookrightarrow}}X_{G}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\pi}}{{\rightarrow}}B_{G},
where
X_{G}=(E_{G}\times G)/G
(Borel space) obtained by diagonal action of
G
on space
X\times E_{G}
and
B_{G}
(classifying space) is the orbit space of free action of
G
on contractible space
E_{G}.
We recall some results of Leray-Serre spectral sequence associated with Borel fibration
X\stackrel{{\scriptstyle i}}{{\hookrightarrow}}X_{G}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\pi}}{{\rightarrow}}B_{G}.
For proofs, we refer
[
3
,
10
]
.

Proposition 2.1
.

(
[
10
]
)
Suppose that the system of local coefficients on
B_{G}
is simple. Then, the homomorphisms
i^{*}:H^{*}(X_{G})\rightarrow H^{*}(X)
and
\pi^{*}:H^{*}(B_{G})\rightarrow H^{*}(X_{G})
are the edge homomorphisms,

H^{k}(B_{G})=E_{2}^{k,0}\rightarrow E_{3}^{k,0}\rightarrow\cdots E_{k}^{k,0}\rightarrow E_{k+1}^{k,0}=E_{\infty}^{k,0}\subset H^{k}(X_{G}),
and
H^{i}(X_{G})\rightarrow E_{\infty}^{0,i}\hookrightarrow E_{l+1}^{0,i}\hookrightarrow E_{l}^{0,i}\hookrightarrow\cdots\hookrightarrow E_{2}^{0,i}\hookrightarrow E_{2}^{0,i}\hookrightarrow H^{i}(X),
respectively.

Proposition 2.2
.

(
[
10
]
)
Let
G=\mathbb{Z}_{2}
act on a finitistic space
X
and
\pi_{1}(B_{G})
acts trivially on

Then, the system of local coefficients on

E^{k,i}_{2}=H^{k}(B_{G})\otimes H^{i}(X),~k,i\geq 0.

Proposition 2.3

(

3.
The Cohomology Algebra of The Orbit Space of Free involutions on
\mathbb{S}^{n}\times\mathbb{S}^{m}\times\mathbb{S}^{l}

X\sim_{2}\mathbb{S}^{n}\times\mathbb{S}^{m}\times\mathbb{S}^{l},
where
n\leq m\leq l.
We first prove the following lemma.

Lemma 3.1
.

Let
G=\mathbb{Z}_{2}
act freely on a finitistic space
X\sim_{2}\mathbb{S}^{n}\times\mathbb{S}^{m}\times\mathbb{S}^{l},
where

Then,
\pi_{1}(B_{G})
acts trivially on

Proof.

For

\pi_{1}(B_{G})

Next, suppose

If
\pi_{1}(B_{G})

g^{*}(c)=ab+c,
where
g

\pi_{1}(B_{G}).
So, we get
cg^{*}(c)=abc\neq 0,
which contradicts Proposition
\ref{prop 5}.
Hence, our claim.
∎

Note that

X\sim_{2}\mathbb{S}^{n}\times\mathbb{S}^{n}\times\mathbb{S}^{l},n\leq l.
First, we determine the cohomology algebra of free involutions on

\pi_{1}(B_{G})
acts nontrivially on
H^{*}(X).

Theorem 3.2
