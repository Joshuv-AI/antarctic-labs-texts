Abstract
We have studied anomalous four-photon couplings in the μ + ​ μ − → μ + ​ γ ​ γ ​ μ − \mu^{+}\mu^{-}\rightarrow\mu^{+}\gamma\gamma\mu^{-} scattering at a future muon collider. The collision energies of 3 TeV, 14 TeV, and 100 TeV are addressed. Both differential and total cross sections versus invariant mass of the outgoing photons are calculated. The best 95% C.L. exclusion bounds on anomalous couplings are obtained to be g 1 = 2.23 × 10 − 8 g_{1}=2.23\times 10^{-8} TeV -4 and g 2 = 4.22 × 10 − 8 g_{2}=4.22\times 10^{-8} TeV -4 . They correspond to the muon collision energy of 100 TeV. The partial-wave unitary constraints on g 1 g_{1} and g 2 g_{2} are examined. We have demonstrated that the unitarity is not violated in a region of the anomalous couplings obtained in the present paper.

Unitarity limits on anomalous couplings

To examine bounds imposed by partial-wave unitarity, we will use the
partial-wave expansion of the helicity amplitude in the
center-of-mass system
[
112
]
,

\displaystyle M_{\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2}\lambda_{3}\lambda_{4}}(s,\theta,\varphi)

\displaystyle=16\pi\sum_{J}(2J+1)\sqrt{(1+\delta_{\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2}})(1+\delta_{\lambda_{3}\lambda_{4}})}

\displaystyle\times\,e^{i(\lambda-\mu)\phi}\,d^{J}_{\lambda\mu}(\theta)\,T^{J}_{\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2}\lambda_{3}\lambda_{4}}(s)\;,

(15)

where
\lambda=\lambda_{1}-\lambda_{2}
,
\mu=\lambda_{3}-\lambda_{4}
,
\theta(\phi)
is the polar (azimuth) scattering angle,
and
d^{J}_{\lambda\mu}(\theta)
is the Wigner (small)
d
-function

[
113
]
. It obeys the relation
d^{J}_{\lambda\mu}(-z)=(-1)^{J-\lambda}d^{J}_{\mu-\lambda}(z)
, where
z=\cos\theta
. If we
choose the plane
(x-z)
as a scattering plane, then
\phi=0
in
(
3
). In what follows, we will need only

d^{J}_{00}(z)=P_{J}(z)\;,

(16)

where
P_{J}(z)
is the Legendre polynomial, and
[
2
]

d^{J}_{2\pm 2}(z)=(\pm 1)^{J}\!\left(\frac{1\pm z}{2}\right)^{\!2}\!{}_{2}F_{1}\!\left(2-J,J+3;1;\frac{1\mp z}{2}\right),

(17)

where
{}_{2}F_{1}(a,b;c;x)
is the hypergeometric function

[
114
]
.

Partial-wave unitarity requires that

\left|T^{J}_{\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2}\lambda_{3}\lambda_{4}}(s)\right|\leq 1\;.

(18)

Using orthogonality of the
d
-functions,

\int\limits_{-1}^{1}d^{J}_{\lambda\lambda^{\prime}}(z)\,d^{J^{\prime}}_{\lambda\lambda^{\prime}}(z)\,dz=\frac{2}{2J+1}\,\delta_{JJ^{\prime}}\;,

(19)

we find from (
3
) that the partial-wave
amplitude is given by

\displaystyle T^{J}_{\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2}\lambda_{3}\lambda_{4}}(s)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{32\pi}\frac{1}{\sqrt{(1+\delta_{\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2}})(1+\delta_{\lambda_{3}\lambda_{4}})}}\int\limits_{-1}^{1}\!\!M_{\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2}\lambda_{3}\lambda_{4}}(s,z)\,d^{J}_{\lambda\mu}(z)\,dz\;.

(20)

The anomalous helicity amplitudes

M_{\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2}\lambda_{3}\lambda_{4}}
are collected in
Appendix.

1.
Helicity amplitude
M_{++++}
. Then
\lambda_{1}=\lambda_{2}=\lambda_{3}=\lambda_{4}=1
, and
\lambda=\mu=0
.
Using explicit expression for
M_{++++}

(
Appendix. Anomalous helicity amplitudes
), we find that the partial-wave

T^{J}_{++++}(s)=\frac{(4g_{1}+3g_{2})s^{2}}{128\pi}\!\int\limits_{-1}^{1}\!P_{J}(z)\,dz=\frac{(4g_{1}+3g_{2})s^{2}}{64\pi}\delta_{J0}\;.

(21)

We find from (

), (

|4g_{1}+3g_{2}|\leq 64\pi s^{-2}\;.

(22)

2.
Helicity amplitude
M_{++--}
. In such a case,

\lambda_{1}=\lambda_{2}=-\lambda_{3}=-\lambda_{4}=1
, and

\displaystyle=\frac{(4g_{1}+g_{2})s^{2}}{256\pi}\int\limits_{-1}^{1}\!P_{J}(z)(3+z^{2})\,dz

\displaystyle=\frac{(4g_{1}+g_{2})s^{2}}{256\pi}\!\left[\frac{20}{3}\delta_{J0}+\frac{4}{15}\delta_{J2}\right].

Thus, non-trivial amplitudes are

and

(24)

M_{+-+-}

Using equation (

4
Conclusions

In the present paper we have studied the anomalous quartic couplings
of the
\gamma\gamma\gamma\gamma
vertex in the unpolarized

\mu^{+}\mu^{-}\rightarrow\mu^{+}\gamma\gamma\mu^{-}
scattering at the
future muon collider. We require the scattering angles of the
outgoing muons to be in the region
10^{\circ}\div 170^{\circ}

[
84
]
. To derive the bounds on the four-photon couplings,
we have assumed that anomalous couplings of the vertices

Z\gamma\gamma\gamma
and
ZZ\gamma\gamma
are zero. The collision
energies of 3 TeV, 14 TeV, and 100 TeV are examined.

Using analytical expressions for the helicity amplitudes for the
light-by-light scattering, we have calculated both differential and
total cross sections. To reduce the SM background, we have imposed
the cuts:
m_{\gamma\gamma}>0.5
TeV, 3 TeV, and 20 TeV for

\sqrt{s}=3
TeV, 14 TeV, and 100 TeV, respectively. Additionally
we demand the invariant mass of the outgoing photons to satisfy the
inequality
m_{\gamma\gamma}<0.9\sqrt{s}
. Finally, we use the cut
on the rapidity of the final photons,
|\eta|<2.5
. The 95% C.L.
exclusion regions for the anomalous four-photon couplings
g_{1}
and

g_{2}
are calculated. For
\sqrt{s}=3
TeV the best bounds
appeared to be
g_{1}=6.9\times 10^{-3}
TeV
-4
(for
g_{2}=0
)
and
g_{2}=1.41\times 10^{-2}
TeV
-4
(for
g_{1}=0
).
Correspondingly, for
\sqrt{s}=14
TeV we have obtained the limits

g_{1}=2.2\times 10^{-5}
TeV
-4
,
g_{2}=4.6\times 10^{-5}

TeV
-4
. Finally, for
\sqrt{s}=100
TeV the most stringent
bounds on anomalous couplings are equal to
g_{1}=2.23\times 10^{-8}
TeV
-4
and
g_{2}=4.22\times 10^{-8}
TeV
-4
.
Thus, new scales of the order of 14 TeV and 82 TeV can be probed at
the future muon collider with the collision energy of 14 TeV and 100
TeV, respectively.

The partial-wave unitary constraints on the anomalous couplings are
examined in detail. We have demonstrated that the unitarity is not
violated in the region of these values of couplings
g_{1}
,
g_{2}
.

The AQGCs can originate from one-loop contribution with a new
charged particle inside the loop. Then one expect that

\Lambda

and our constraint

1/f

g_{i}\sim 1/(f^{2}\Lambda^{2})

This new particle can be, for instance, a KK graviton or radion in
the Randall-Sundrum (RS) model with one extra dimension and warped

116

-1

the RS model), we obtain from
g_{2}=4.2\times 10^{-8}
TeV

\Lambda\simeq 500
TeV. Thus, the use of EFT and our cuts are
justified even for the 100 TeV muon collider.
