Categorified Path Calculus
Simon Burton

Abstract
Path calculus, or graphical linear algebra, is a string diagram calculus for the category of matrices over a base ring. It is the usual string diagram calculus for a symmetric monoidal category, where the monoidal product is the direct sum of matrices. We categorify this story to develop a surface diagram calculus for the bicategory of matrices over a base bimonoidal category. This yields a surface diagram calculus for any bimonoidal category by restricting to diagrams for 1 × 1 1\times 1 matrices. We show how additional structure on the base category, such as biproducts, duals and the dagger, adds structure to the resulting calculus. Applied to categorical quantum mechanics this yields a new graphical proof of the teleportation protocol.

.
These are all unitary and so the
associator and left and right unitor of the bicategory
\mathrm{Mat}(C)

will have unitary components.
∎

Definition 3.9
.

A
dagger symmetric monoidal bicategory

(\mathcal{B},\boxplus,0,\sigma^{\boxplus},\nu^{\boxplus},{\dagger})

is a dagger bicategory
(\mathcal{B},\alpha,\lambda,\rho,{\dagger})

and a symmetric monoidal bicategory

(\mathcal{B},\boxplus,0,\sigma^{\boxplus},\nu^{\boxplus})

such that

(f\boxplus g)^{\dagger}=f^{\dagger}\boxplus g^{\dagger}
, for 2-cells
f,g\in\mathcal{B}_{2}

and the components of the syllepsis
\nu^{\boxplus}
are unitary.

Theorem 3.5
.

Given a dagger bimonoidal category
\mathcal{C}
,

(\mathrm{Mat}(\mathcal{C}),\boxplus,0,\sigma^{\boxplus},\nu^{\boxplus},{\dagger})

is a dagger symmetric monoidal bicategory.

Proof.

The dagger
{\dagger}_{m,n}
acts componentwise on
\mathrm{Mat}_{m,n}(\mathcal{C})

and this commutes with
\boxplus
.
The components of
\nu^{\boxplus}
are compositions of unitary morphisms
of
\mathcal{C}
.
∎
