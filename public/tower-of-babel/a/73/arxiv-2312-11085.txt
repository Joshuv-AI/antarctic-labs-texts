Half-sided Translations and Islands
Krishna Jalan, Roji Pius

Abstract
Recently Liu and Leutheusser argued that the operators in the interior of a black hole can be reconstructed by the half-sided evolution of the non-local operators in the boundary CFT which are dual to the bulk operators outside the black hole horizon. However, for an AdS 2 black hole in equilibrium with a finite temperature bath, the continuous exchange of Hawking quanta, emitted by the black hole, and the bath quanta give rise to an information paradox. The resolution of this paradox requires transferring the ownership of the black hole interior from the boundary CFT to the bath at late time. As a result, the reconstruction method proposed by Liu and Leutheusser must cease to reconstruct the black hole interior after the Page time using the boundary CFT. In this paper, we demonstrate this by showing that the relevant half-sided translations are trivial at late time.

Introduction

One of the major goal of quantum gravity is the description of the physics near the singularity of a blackhole. The AdS/CFT duality provides a conformal field theory (CFT) description of quantum gravity in asymptotically Anti-de Sitter (AdS) spacetimes
Maldacena:1997re
. In principle, we can use the dual boundary CFT to describe the physics near the singularity of an AdS black hole. However, we must reconstruct the bulk degrees of freedom inside the horizon of a black hole in terms of the boundary degrees of freedom
Banks:1998dd
;
Hamilton:2006az
;
Hamilton:2006fh
;
Hamilton:2005ju
;
Hamilton:2007wj
;
Balasubramanian:1999ri
;
Bena:1999jv
in order to study the physics inside a black hole using AdS/CFT. There are two interesting proposals for the reconstruction of bulk fields inside a black hole horizon. The proposal of Papadodimas and Raju requires introducing a “mirror operator ” for each of the operators in the dual boundary CFT. The field inside the black hole horizon is then expressed as a sum of linearly independent operators and its mirror operators with appropriate weight factors
Papadodimas:2012aq
;
Papadodimas:2013jku
. On the other hand, the method proposed by Liu and Leutheusser reconstructs bulk operators inside the horizon by the half-sided evolution of the operators in the boundary CFT dual to bulk operators that are outside the horizon
Leutheusser:2021qhd
;
Leutheusser:2021frk
.

Both proposals are claimed to be capable of reconstructing the bulk fields inside the horizon of one-sided black holes and eternal black holes. For an eternal AdS
2
black hole, the black hole interior belongs to the entanglement wedge of the left and right boundaries. Therefore, the reconstruction of fields behind the horizon of an eternal AdS
2
black hole requires both the right and the left dual CFTs denoted as CFT
R
, CFT
L
respectively
Maldacena:2001kr
. The interior reconstruction using the mirror operators is consistent with this demand, because the construction of the mirror operators require the information of the entangled state of the dual left and right CFTs that corresponds to the eternal black hole. Since the modular operators acts both on the left and right CFTs, the reconstruction method using the half-sided translation also depends on the left and right CFTs.

The interior of an eternal black hole does not always belong to the entanglement wedge of the left and right dual CFTs. Such a situation is present in an eternal AdS
2
black hole in equilibrium with a finite temperature bath. The continuous exchange of Hawking quanta emitted by black hole and the bath quanta builds an ever-growing entanglement between the black hole and the bath and give rise to a linear growth of von Neumann entropy for the black hole with respect to the boundary time
1
1

1

However, the classical geometry of the eternal black hole is unaffected by this exchange due to the vanishing net flow of stress tensor across the cut out boundary of the AdS
2
region.
. Eventually, the entropy of the black hole will grow beyond the thermodynamic entropy of the black hole. The resulting information paradox can be resolved by transferring the ownership of the interior of the black hole from the combined system of CFT
L
and CFT
R
after the black hole Page time to the bath
Almheiri:2019yqk
. Hence, the black hole interior can not be reconstructed using the left and the right CFTs after the Page time. The goal of this paper is to derive this fact using the bulk reconstruction proposal of Liu and Leutheusser.

In this paper, we show that at late time, the half-sided translation defined using the algebra of observables restricted to the gravitational region outside the right horizon of the black hole is trivial. The bulk operators in the right/left side of the black hole can be expressed as non-local operators in CFT
R
/CFT
L
. Moreover, a bulk half-sided modular translation is dual to an appropriate boundary half-sided modular translation. Therefore, our result can be interpreted as the inability of the Liu-Leutheusser proposal to reconstruct the interior bulk operators using boundary CFTs, the CFT
R
and the CFT
L
after the Page time. This is in perfect agreement with the general principles of the entanglement wedge reconstruction
Dong:2016eik
and the discussion in
Engelhardt:2023xer

2
The half-sided translation and the in-falling

The main ingredient of the Liu-Leutheusser proposal for reconstructing the bulk operators in the black hole interior is the half-sided translation. In this section, we shall briefly discuss the definition of a half-sided translation, and describe how a primary field in an arbitrary CFT defined on (1+1)-Minkowski spacetime can be translated along a null direction using the half-sided translation.

2.1
The half-sided translation

Half-sided translation is an element of a continuous one-parametric unitary group defined with respect to a Type III
1
von Neumann algebra
\mathcal{M}
, having a cyclic and separating vector
|\Omega\rangle
, and a subalgebra
\mathcal{N}
having the same cyclic and separating vector. Since
|\Omega\rangle
is a seprating vector of
\mathcal{M}
, it is not annihilated by any of the operators in
\mathcal{M}
. The cyclic property of
|\Omega\rangle
enables us to construct a dense subspace of the Hilbert space associated with
\mathcal{M}
by acting the elements of
\mathcal{M}
on
|\Omega\rangle
. An element
U(s)
of this unitary group is called a half-sided translation. It has the following properties:

\displaystyle i.

\displaystyle\quad U(s)|\Omega\rangle=|\Omega\rangle\qquad\forall s\in\mathbb{R},

\displaystyle ii.

\displaystyle\quad U(s)=e^{-\text{i}Gs},~where~G~is~a~Hermitian~positive~operator,

\displaystyle iii.

\displaystyle\quad U^{\dagger}(s)\mathcal{M}U(s)\subset\mathcal{M},\quad\forall s\leq 0,

\displaystyle iv.

\displaystyle\quad\mathcal{N}=U(-1)^{\dagger}\mathcal{M}U(-1).

(1)

This structure exists only if
\mathcal{M}
is a type III
1
von Neumann algebra
borchers1998half
. The half-sided translation
U(s)
has the following expression
Borchers:2000pv
in terms of the modular operators
\Delta_{\mathcal{M}}
and
\Delta_{\mathcal{N}}
associated with the algebras
\mathcal{M}
and
\mathcal{N}
respectively:

U(s)=\Delta_{\mathcal{M}}^{-\text{i}t}\Delta_{\mathcal{N}}^{\text{i}t},\quad\quad s=e^{-2\pi t}-1\quad\forall~t\leq 0.

(2)

Modular operator
\Delta_{\mathcal{M}}
has the property that it preserves the operator algebra
\mathcal{M}
, i.e. its action on any operator in
\mathcal{M}
produces another operator in
\mathcal{M}
. Moreover, it keeps the cyclic and the separating vector invariant. Its acts both on
\mathcal{M}
and it’s commutant
\mathcal{M}^{\prime}
, the set of all observables that commute with all the elements in
\mathcal{M}
. In general,
\Delta_{\mathcal{M}}
can not be factorised into two operators
\bar{\Delta}_{\mathcal{M}}
and
\hat{\Delta}_{\mathcal{M}}
which acts only on
\mathcal{M}
and
\mathcal{M}^{\prime}
respectively.

Modular operator
\Delta_{\mathcal{M}}
can be expressed as the exponential of the modular Hamiltonian
\mathcal{K}_{\mathcal{M}}
of the algebra
\mathcal{M}
,
\Delta_{\mathcal{M}}=e^{-\mathcal{K}_{\mathcal{M}}}
. Since
\mathcal{N}
is a subalgebra of
\mathcal{M}
, their modular Hamiltonians satisfy the following algebra
Borchers:2000pv

\left[\mathcal{K}_{\mathcal{M}},\mathcal{K}_{\mathcal{N}}\right]=-2\pi\text{i}\left(\mathcal{K}_{\mathcal{M}}-\mathcal{K}_{\mathcal{N}}\right).

(3)

Using (
2
), (
3
) and the Baker-Campbell-Hausdorff formula we can then express
U(s)
as follows

U(s)=e^{-\text{i}sG},\quad\quad s=e^{-2\pi t}-1,

(4)

where the generator of the half-sided translation is given by

G=\frac{1}{2\pi}(\mathcal{K}_{\mathcal{M}}-\mathcal{K}_{\mathcal{N}}).

4
The half-sided translations and the islands

The half-sided translation translates a field outside the horizon to the inside. As a result, the degrees of freedom inside the horizon can be reconstructed using the degrees of freedom in the gravitational region outside the horizon with the help of half-sided translation. However, according to the entanglement wedge reconstruction principle it is not possible to reconstruct the degrees of freedom inside an AdS
2
eternal black hole in equilibrium with a finite temperature non-gravitating bath using the degrees of freedom in the left and right gravitational regions outside the horizon, after the Page time. This is a consequence of the appearance of a nontrivial island that includes the black hole interior for the bath after the Page time
Almheiri:2019yqk
. The island rule
Almheiri:2019hni
then demands the failure of the half-sided translation to take a field in the gravitational regions outside the horizon to the interior, after the Page time. In this section, we show that after the Page time, the action of the half-sided translation on a field in the gravitational regions outside the black hole horizon is trivial, and thus cease to translate a field in the gravitational region outside the event horizon to the black hole interior.

4.2
The appearance of an island

The classical geometry of an eternal black hole in equilibrium with a thermal bath is the same as that of an isolated eternal black hole. However, quantum mechanically the two systems are significantly different. This is due to the continuous exchange of the Hawking radiation and the radiation from the bath. The exchange leads to an ever-growing entanglement between the black hole and the bath. This can be seen by computing the entanglement entropy of the bath. Assume that the CFT living in the glued spacetime has central charge
c
. Then the entropy of the bath can be calculated using the replica method
Calabrese:2004eu
, and is given by

\displaystyle S_{\text{bath}}(u)=S_{\text{BH}}(u)

\displaystyle=\frac{c}{3}~\text{ln}\left(\frac{\beta}{\pi\epsilon}\text{cosh}\left(\frac{2\pi u}{\beta}\right)\right)\xrightarrow{u>>\beta}\frac{2\pi c}{3\beta}u.

(32)

The entanglement entropy of the bath/black hole increases linearly at late time. Such an unbounded growth of entanglement entropy makes it bigger than the thermal entropy of the black hole at sufficiently large times. The paradoxical growth of the von Neumann entropy can be resolved by including the non-perturbative gravitational correction (replica wormhole) to the entanglement entropy after the Page time, the time at which the von Neumann entropy of the black hole is equal to its Bekenstein-Hawking entropy
Almheiri:2019yqk
. This additional contribution to the replica path integral introduces a non-trivial entanglement wedge island, containing the black hole interior, for the bath. Consequently, after the Page time the degrees of freedom inside the black hole can not be reconstructed using the degrees of freedom outside the horizon restricted to the gravitational region, i.e. inside the left and the right cut-out AdS
2
boundaries.

5
Discussion

In this paper, we showed that after Page time, the half-sided modular translation operator defined using the algebra of observables restricted to the gravitational region of black hole becomes trivial. The bulk half-sided modular translations can also be interpreted as appropriate boundary half-sided modular translations. Therefore, our result can be interpreted as the inability of the Liu-Leutheusser proposal to reconstruct the interior bulk operators using the boundary CFTs, the CFT
R
and the CFT
L
after Page time. This implies, after Page time, the black hole interior does not belong to the entanglement wedge of the dual boundary CFTs
Almheiri:2019yqk
. Thus the result upholds the general principles of the entanglement wedge reconstruction
Dong:2016eik
and agrees with the discussion in
Engelhardt:2023xer
.

Let us briefly mention some of the directions that deserve immediate attention. It is known that non-trivial half-sided translation exists only if the von Neumann operator algebra is type III
1

Wiesbrock:1992mg
. Hence, we expect that the algebra of observables restricted to the gravitational region outside the horizon of the black hole in equilibrium with a bath have type III
1
algebra before Page time. However, our result suggests that after Page time the algebra of observables must cease to be a type III
1
algebra. This can be checked by finding the spectrum of the logarithm of the modular operator of the operator algebra. If the spectrum does not cover the full real axis, then the operator algebra of our interest is not of type III
1

connes1973classification
;
connes1974caracterisation
. Another interesting direction is to check that after Page time whether half-sided translation allows us to express a field inside the black hole interior in terms of degrees of freedom of bath. We hope to report on this soon.
