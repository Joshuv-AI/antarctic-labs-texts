Recipes to compute the algebraic K K -theory of Hecke algebras of reductive p p -adic groups
Bartels, A, Lück, W

Abstract
. We compute the algebraic K K -theory of the Hecke algebra of a reductive p p -adic group G G using the fact that the Farrell-Jones Conjecture is known in this context. The main tool will be the properties of the associated Bruhat-Tits building and an equivariant Atiyah-Hirzebruch spectral sequence. In particular the projective class group can be written as the colimit of the projective class groups of the compact open subgroups of G G .

Introduction

We begin with stating the main theorem of this paper, explanation will follow:

Theorem 1.1

(Main Theorem)
.

Let

p
-adic group. Let

induces

A

p
-adic group

-adic group.

A ring is called

-modules of length at most

is a unit in

We denote by

We denote by

G

-

-complex, whose isotropy groups are all compact open
