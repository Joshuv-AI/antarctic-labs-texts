Cycles of Time in Classical Cosmology
A. E. Pavlov

Abstract
We present exact solutions to the Friedmann equation in standard Λ ​ CDM {\rm\Lambda CDM} cosmology in Weierstrass and Jacobi functions. The right hand side of the Friedmann equation describing various contributions of matter sources is considered in generic form. It is proved that the problem of integration of the Friedmann equation for simple EoS of medium is reduced to solving Abel integrals for algebraic functions.
