On the discrete analogues of Appell function F 2 F_{2}
Ravi Dwivedi, Vivek Sahai

Abstract
In this paper, we introduce two distinct discrete forms of Appell function F 2 F_{2} . We determine their convergence domains, integral representations as well as difference-differential equations that are satisfied by these discrete analogues of F 2 F_{2} . We have also obtained the difference-differential formulas, finite and infinite summation formulas, and a complete list of difference-differential recursion relations obeyed by these discrete functions. AMS Subject Classification: 33C65. Keywords: Appell functions, discrete hypergeometric functions.

2.1
Difference equations

If
\Theta_{t}
is considered as the discrete analogue of differential operator
t\frac{d}{dt}
, defined by
\Theta_{t}:=t\,\rho_{t}\,\Delta_{t}
, where
\Delta_{t}f(t)=f(t+1)-f(t)
and
\rho_{t}\,f(t)=f(t-1)

\displaystyle\Theta_{t}\,((-1)^{nk}\,(-t)_{nk})

\displaystyle=n\,k\,(-1)^{nk}\,(-t)_{nk}.

(2.15)

With this assumption, we get

\displaystyle\Theta_{t_{1}}\left(\frac{1}{k_{1}}\Theta_{t_{1}}+c_{1}-1\right)\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}

\displaystyle=\sum_{m,n\geq 0}\frac{(a)_{m+n}\,(b_{1})_{m}\,(b_{2})_{n}\,(-1)^{mk_{1}}\,(-t_{1})_{mk_{1}}\,(-1)^{nk_{2}}(-t_{2})_{nk_{2}}}{(c_{1})_{m}\,(c_{2})_{n}\,m!\,n!}\ x^{m}\,y^{n}\,mk_{1}\,(c_{1}+m-1)

\displaystyle=k_{1}\,\sum_{m,n\geq 0}\frac{(a)_{m+n+1}\,(b_{1})_{m+1}\,(b_{2})_{n}\,(-1)^{(m+1)k_{1}}\,(-t_{1})_{(m+1)k_{1}}\,(-1)^{nk_{2}}\,(-t_{2})_{nk_{2}}}{(c_{1})_{m}\,(c_{2})_{n}\,m!\,n!}\ x^{m+1}\,y^{n}

\displaystyle=k_{1}\,\sum_{m,n\geq 0}(a+m+n)\,(b_{1}+m)\,(-1)^{k_{1}}\,(-t_{1})_{k_{1}}\,x\,\rho_{t_{1}}^{k_{1}}

\displaystyle\quad\times\frac{(a)_{m+n}\,(b_{1})_{m}\,(b_{2})_{n}\,(-1)^{mk_{1}}\,(-t_{1})_{mk_{1}}\,(-1)^{nk_{2}}\,(-t_{2})_{nk_{2}}}{(c_{1})_{m}\,(c_{2})_{n}\,m!\,n!}\ x^{m}\,y^{n}

\displaystyle=k_{1}\,(-1)^{k_{1}}\,(-t_{1})_{k_{1}}\,x\,\rho_{t_{1}}^{k_{1}}\,\left(\frac{1}{k_{1}}\Theta_{t_{1}}+\frac{1}{k_{2}}\Theta_{t_{2}}+a\right)\left(\frac{1}{k_{1}}\Theta_{t_{1}}+b_{1}\right)\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}.

(2.16)

Thus, we arrive at

\displaystyle\left[\Theta_{t_{1}}\left(\frac{1}{k_{1}}\Theta_{t_{1}}+c_{1}-1\right)-k_{1}\,(-1)^{k_{1}}(-t_{1})_{k_{1}}\,x\,\rho_{t_{1}}^{k_{1}}\left(\frac{1}{k_{1}}\Theta_{t_{1}}+\frac{1}{k_{2}}\Theta_{t_{2}}+a\right)\left(\frac{1}{k_{1}}\Theta_{t_{1}}+b_{1}\right)\right]\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}=0.

(2.17)

Similarly

\displaystyle\left[\Theta_{t_{2}}\left(\frac{1}{k_{2}}\Theta_{t_{2}}+c_{2}-1\right)-k_{2}\,(-1)^{k_{2}}(-t_{2})_{k_{2}}\,y\,\rho_{t_{2}}^{k_{2}}\left(\frac{1}{k_{1}}\Theta_{t_{1}}+\frac{1}{k_{2}}\Theta_{t_{2}}+a\right)\left(\frac{1}{k_{2}}\Theta_{t_{2}}+b_{2}\right)\right]\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}=0.

(2.18)

Equations (
2.17
) and (
2.18
) are difference equations satisfied by the discrete Appell function
\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}
.

(3.1)

\displaystyle(\Delta_{t_{2}})^{r}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a,b_{1},b_{2};c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},1,x,y)

\displaystyle=\frac{(a)_{r}\,(b_{2})_{r}\,y^{r}}{(c_{2})_{r}}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a+r,b_{1},b_{2}+r;c_{1},c_{2}+r;t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},1,x,y);

(3.2)

\displaystyle(\theta)^{r}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a,b_{1},b_{2};c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y)

\displaystyle=\frac{(-1)^{rk_{1}}\,(a)_{r}\,(b_{1})_{r}\,(-t_{1})_{rk_{1}}\,x^{r}}{(c_{1})_{r}}

\displaystyle\quad\times\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a+r,b_{1}+r,b_{2};c_{1}+r,c_{2};t_{1}-rk_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y);

(3.3)

\displaystyle(\phi)^{r}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a,b_{1},b_{2};c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y)

\displaystyle=\frac{(-1)^{rk_{2}}\,(a)_{r}\,(b_{2})_{r}\,(-t_{2})_{rk_{2}}\,y^{r}}{(c_{2})_{r}}

\displaystyle\quad\times\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a+r,b_{1},b_{2}+r;c_{1},c_{2}+r;t_{1},t_{2}-rk_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y);

(3.4)

\displaystyle\left(\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\right)^{r}\left[x^{b_{1}+r-1}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a,b_{1},b_{2};c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y)\right]

\displaystyle=x^{b_{1}-1}\,(b_{1})_{r}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a,b_{1}+r,b_{2};c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y);

(3.5)

\displaystyle\left(\frac{\partial}{\partial y}\right)^{r}[y^{b_{2}+r-1}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a,b_{1},b_{2};c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y)]

\displaystyle=y^{b_{2}-1}\,(b_{2})_{r}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a,b_{1},b_{2}+r;c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y);

(3.6)

\displaystyle\left(\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\right)^{r}[x^{a+r-1}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a,b_{1},b_{2};c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,xy)]

\displaystyle=x^{a-1}\,(a)_{r}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a+r,b_{1},b_{2};c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,xy);

(3.7)

\displaystyle\left(\frac{\partial}{\partial y}\right)^{r}[y^{a+r-1}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a,b_{1},b_{2};c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},xy,y)]

\displaystyle=y^{a-1}\,(a)_{r}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a+r,b_{1},b_{2};c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},xy,y);

(3.8)

\displaystyle\left(\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\right)^{r}[x^{c_{1}-1}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a,b_{1},b_{2};c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y)]

\displaystyle=(-1)^{r}\,(1-c_{1})_{r}\,x^{c_{1}-r-1}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a,b_{1},b_{2};c_{1}-r,c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y);

(3.9)

\displaystyle\left(\frac{\partial}{\partial y}\right)^{r}[y^{c_{2}-1}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a,b_{1},b_{2};c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y)]

\displaystyle=(-1)^{r}\,y^{c_{2}-r-1}\,(1-c_{2})_{r}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a,b_{1},b_{2};c_{1},c_{2}-r;t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y).

(3.10)

Proof.

The proofs of these differential formulae are very elementary and hence we give only the proof of (
3.1
). The action of difference operator
\Delta_{t_{1}}
on the discrete function
\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}
yields

\displaystyle(\Delta_{t_{1}})\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a,b_{1},b_{2};c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},1,k_{2},x,y)

\displaystyle=\sum_{m,n\geq 0}\frac{(a)_{m+n}\,(b_{1})_{m}\,(b_{2})_{n}\,(-1)^{m-1}\,m\,(-t_{1})_{m-1}\,(-1)^{nk_{2}}(-t_{2})_{nk_{2}}}{(c_{1})_{m}\,(c_{2})_{n}\,m!\,n!}\ x^{m}\,y^{n}

\displaystyle=\sum_{m,n\geq 0}\frac{(a)_{m+n+1}\,(b_{1})_{m+1}\,(b_{2})_{n}\,(-1)^{m}\,(-t_{1})_{m}\,(-1)^{nk_{2}}(-t_{2})_{nk_{2}}}{(c_{1})_{m+1}\,(c_{2})_{n}\,m!\,n!}\ x^{m+1}\,y^{n}

\displaystyle=\frac{a\,b_{1}\,x}{c}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a+1,b_{1}+1,b_{2};c_{1}+1,c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},1,k_{2},x,y).

(3.11)

The result is true for
r=1
. We can assume that this is also true for
r=p
and using this it can be proved for
r=p+1
. Thus, we get the required result. Similarly, we can show that the formula (
3.2
)-(
3.4
) hold true. To prove (
3.5
), we start with

\displaystyle\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\left[x^{b_{1}}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a,b_{1},b_{2};c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k,x,y)\right]

\displaystyle=\sum_{m,n\geq 0}\frac{(a)_{m+n}\,(b_{1})_{m}\,(b_{2})_{n}\,(-1)^{(m+n)k}\,(-t_{1})_{mk}\,(-t_{2})_{nk}}{(c_{1})_{m}\,(c_{2})_{n}\,m!\,n!}\,\frac{\partial}{\partial x}x^{b_{1}+m}\,y^{n}

\displaystyle=\sum_{m,n\geq 0}\frac{(a)_{m+n}\,(b_{1})_{m}\,(b_{2})_{n}\,(-1)^{(m+n)k}\,(-t_{1})_{mk}\,(-t_{2})_{nk}}{(c_{1})_{m}\,(c_{2})_{n}\,m!\,n!}\,(b_{1}+m)\,x^{b_{1}+m-1}\,y^{n}

\displaystyle=x^{b_{1}-1}\,b_{1}\sum_{m,n\geq 0}\frac{(a)_{m+n}\,(b_{1}+1)_{m}\,(b_{2})_{n}\,(-1)^{(m+n)k}\,(-t_{1})_{mk}\,(-t_{2})_{nk}}{(c_{1})_{m}\,(c_{2})_{n}\,m!\,n!}\,x^{m}\,y^{n}

\displaystyle=x^{b_{1}-1}\,b_{1}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a,b_{1}+1,b_{2};c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k,x,y).

(3.12)

So (
3.5
) is true for
r=1
. Consider the result to be true for
r=p
leads the validity of results for
r=p+1
. Hence, we can conclude that (
3.5
) is true for each natural number
r
. Similarly, the results (
3.6
)-(
3.10
) can be proved.
∎

4
Finite and infinite summation formulas

In this section, we establish some finite and infinite sums in terms of discrete Appell function
\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}
in the following theorem.

Theorem 4.1
.

Following summation formulas hold:

\displaystyle\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a,b_{1}+r,b_{2};c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y)

\displaystyle=\sum_{s=0}^{r}{r\choose s}\frac{(a)_{s}\,(-1)^{sk_{1}}\,(-t_{1})_{sk_{1}}}{(c_{1})_{s}}\,x^{s}

\displaystyle\quad\times\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a+s,b_{1}+s,b_{2};c_{1}+s,c_{2};t_{1}-sk_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y);

(4.1)

\displaystyle\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a,b_{1},b_{2}+r;c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y)

\displaystyle=\sum_{s=0}^{r}{r\choose s}\frac{(a)_{s}\,(-1)^{sk_{2}}\,(-t_{2})_{sk_{2}}}{(c_{2})_{s}}\,y^{s}

\displaystyle\quad\times\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{1}(a+s,b_{1},b_{2}+s;c_{1},c_{2}+s;t_{1},t_{2}-sk_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y);

(4.2)

\displaystyle\sum_{r=0}^{\infty}\frac{(a)_{r}}{r!}\,z^{r}\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a+r,b_{1},b_{2};c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y)

\displaystyle=(1-z)^{-a}\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}\left(a,b_{1},b_{2};c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},\frac{x}{1-z},\frac{y}{1-z}\right);

(4.3)

\displaystyle\sum_{r=0}^{\infty}\frac{(b_{1})_{r}}{r!}\,z^{r}\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a,b_{1}+r,b_{2};c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y)

\displaystyle=(1-z)^{-b_{1}}\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}\left(a,b_{1},b_{2};c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},\frac{x}{1-z},y\right);

(4.4)

\displaystyle\sum_{r=0}^{\infty}\frac{(b_{2})_{r}}{r!}\,z^{r}\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a,b_{1},b_{2}+r;c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y)

\displaystyle=(1-z)^{-b_{2}}\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}\left(a,b_{1},b_{2};c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,\frac{y}{1-z}\right).

(4.5)

Proof.

To prove (
4.1
), we start by applying the Leibnitz rule and get

\displaystyle\left(\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\right)^{r}\left[x^{b_{1}+r-1}\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a,b_{1},b_{2};c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y)\right]

\displaystyle=\sum_{s=0}^{r}{r\choose s}\left[\left(\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\right)^{r-s}\,x^{b_{1}+r-1}\right]\,\left[\left(\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\right)^{s}\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a,b_{1},b_{2};c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y)\right]

\displaystyle=\sum_{s=0}^{r}{r\choose s}(-1)^{r-s}\,(1-b_{1}+s)_{r-s}\,x^{b_{1}+s-1}\,\frac{(a)_{s}\,(b_{1})_{s}\,(-1)^{sk_{1}}\,(-t_{1})_{sk_{1}}}{(c_{1})_{s}}

\displaystyle\quad\times\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a+s,b_{1}+s,b_{2};c_{1}+s,c_{2};t_{1}-sk_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y)

\displaystyle=\sum_{s=0}^{r}{r\choose s}\,(-1)^{r-s}\frac{(-1)^{s}\,(1-b_{1}-r)_{r}}{(b_{1})_{s}}\,x^{b_{1}+s-1}\,\frac{(a)_{s}\,(b_{1})_{s}\,(-1)^{sk_{1}}\,(-t_{1})_{sk_{1}}}{(c_{1})_{s}}

\displaystyle\quad\times\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a+s,b_{1}+s,b_{2};c_{1}+s,c_{2};t_{1}-sk_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y)

\displaystyle=(b_{1})_{r}\,\sum_{s=0}^{r}{r\choose s}\,x^{b_{1}+s-1}\,\frac{(a)_{s}\,(-1)^{sk_{1}}\,(-t_{1})_{sk_{1}}}{(c_{1})_{s}}

\displaystyle\quad\times\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a+s,b_{1}+s,b_{2};c_{1}+s,c_{2};t_{1}-sk_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y).

(4.6)

Taking into account the equation (
3.5
), we have

\displaystyle x^{b_{1}-1}\,(b_{1})_{r}\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a,b_{1}+r,b_{2};c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y)

\displaystyle=(b_{1})_{r}\,\sum_{s=0}^{r}{r\choose s}\,x^{b_{1}+s-1}\,\frac{(a)_{s}\,(-1)^{sk_{1}}\,(-t_{1})_{sk_{1}}}{(c_{1})_{s}}

\displaystyle\quad\times\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a+s,b_{1}+s,b_{2};c_{1}+s,c_{2};t_{1}-sk_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y).

(4.7)

This implies

\displaystyle\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a,b_{1}+r,b_{2};c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y)

\displaystyle=\sum_{s=0}^{r}{r\choose s}\frac{(a)_{s}\,(-1)^{sk_{1}}\,(-t_{1})_{sk_{1}}}{(c_{1})_{s}}\,x^{s}

\displaystyle\quad\times\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a+s,b_{1}+s,b_{2};c_{1}+s,c_{2};t_{1}-sk_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y).

(4.8)

This completes the proof. A similar procedure can be used to get equation (
4.2
). To prove (
4.3
), we start with the right hand side of the equation and thus get

\displaystyle(1-z)^{-a}\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}\left(a,b_{1},b_{2};c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},\frac{x}{1-z},\frac{y}{1-z}\right)

\displaystyle=\sum_{m,n\geq 0}(1-z)^{-(a+m+n)}\,\frac{(a)_{m+n}\,(b_{1})_{m}\,(b_{2})_{n}\,(-1)^{mk_{1}}\,(-t_{1})_{mk_{1}}\,(-1)^{nk_{2}}\,(-t_{2})_{nk_{2}}}{(c_{1})_{m}\,(c_{2})_{n}\,m!\,n!}\ x^{m}\,y^{n}.

(4.9)

Using the binomial theorem
(1-z)^{-(a+m+n)}=\sum_{r=0}^{\infty}\frac{(a+m+n)_{r}}{r!}\,z^{r}
and identity
(a)_{m+n+r}=(a)_{m+n}\,(a+m+n)_{r}=(a)_{r}\,(a+r)_{m+n}
, we have

\displaystyle(1-z)^{-a}\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}\left(a,b_{1},b_{2};c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},\frac{x}{1-z},\frac{y}{1-z}\right)

\displaystyle=\sum_{r=0}^{\infty}\frac{(a)_{r}}{r!}\,z^{r}\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a+r,b_{1},b_{2};c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k,x,y).

(4.10)

It completes the proof of (
4.3
). A similar procedure can be used to prove remaining infinite summation formulas.
∎

5
Recursion formulae

We have the following

Theorem 5.1
.

The following recursion formulas hold for the discrete Appell function
\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}
:

\displaystyle\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a+s,b_{1},b_{2};c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k_{1},k_{2},x,y)

\displaystyle=\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a,b_{1},b_{2};c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k,x,y)

\displaystyle\quad+\frac{(-1)^{k}\,(-t_{1})_{k}\,b_{1}\,x}{c_{1}}\sum_{r=1}^{s}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a+r,b_{1}+1,b_{2};c_{1}+1,c_{2};t_{1}-k,t_{2},k,x,y)

\displaystyle\quad+\frac{(-1)^{k}\,(-t_{2})_{k}\,b_{2}\,y}{c_{2}}\sum_{r=1}^{s}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a+r,b_{1},b_{2}+1;c_{1},c_{2}+1;t_{1},t_{2}-k,k,x,y);

(5.1)

\displaystyle\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a-s,b_{1},b_{2};c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k,x,y)

\displaystyle=\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a,b_{1},b_{2};c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k,x,y)

\displaystyle\quad-\frac{(-1)^{k}\,(-t_{1})_{k}\,b_{1}\,x}{c_{1}}\sum_{r=0}^{s-1}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a-r,b_{1}+1,b_{2};c_{1}+1,c_{2};t_{1}-k,t_{2},k,x,y)

\displaystyle\quad-\frac{(-1)^{k}\,(-t_{2})_{k}\,b_{2}\,y}{c_{2}}\sum_{r=0}^{s-1}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a-r,b_{1},b_{2}+1;c_{1},c_{2}+1;t_{1},t_{2}-k,k,x,y);

(5.2)

\displaystyle\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a,b_{1}+s,b_{2};c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k,x,y)

\displaystyle=\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a,b_{1},b_{2};c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k,x,y)

\displaystyle\quad+\frac{(-1)^{k}\,(-t_{1})_{k}\,a\,x}{c_{1}}\sum_{r=1}^{s}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a+1,b_{1}+r,b_{2};c_{1}+1,c_{2};t_{1}-k,t_{2},k,x,y);

(5.3)

\displaystyle\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a,b_{1}-s,b_{2};c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k,x,y)

\displaystyle=\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a,b_{1},b_{2};c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k,x,y)

\displaystyle\quad-\frac{(-1)^{k}\,(-t_{1})_{k}\,a\,x}{c_{1}}\sum_{r=0}^{s-1}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a+1,b_{1}-r,b_{2};c_{1}+1,c_{2};t_{1}-k,t_{2},k,x,y);

(5.4)

\displaystyle\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a,b_{1},b_{2};c_{1}-s,c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k,x,y)

\displaystyle=\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a,b_{1},b_{2};c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k,x,y)

\displaystyle\quad+(-1)^{k}\,(-t_{1})_{k}\,a\,b_{1}\,x\sum_{r=1}^{s}\frac{\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a+1,b_{1}+1,b_{2};c_{1}+2-r,c_{2};t_{1}-k,t_{2},k,x,y)}{(c_{1}-r)\,(c_{1}-r+1)}

\displaystyle\quad+(-1)^{k}\,(-t_{2})_{k}\,a\,b_{2}\,y\sum_{r=1}^{s}\frac{\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a+1,b_{1},b_{2}+1;c_{1}+2-r,c_{2};t_{1},t_{2}-k,k,x,y)}{(c_{1}-r)\,(c_{1}-r+1)}.

(5.5)

Proof.

To prove the formula (
5.1
), we begin with

\displaystyle\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a,b_{1},b_{2};c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k,x,y)

\displaystyle\quad+\frac{(-1)^{k}\,(-t_{1})_{k}\,b_{1}\,x}{c_{1}}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a+1,b_{1}+1,b_{2};c_{1}+1,c_{2};t_{1}-k,t_{2},k,x,y)

\displaystyle\quad+\frac{(-1)^{k}\,(-t_{2})_{k}\,b_{2}\,y}{c_{2}}\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a+1,b_{1},b_{2}+1;c_{1},c_{2}+1;t_{1},t_{2}-k,k,x,y)

\displaystyle=\sum_{m,n\geq 0}\,\frac{(a)_{m+n}\,(b_{1})_{m}\,(b_{2})_{n}\,(-1)^{(m+n)k}\,(-t_{1})_{mk}\,(-t_{2})_{nk}}{(c_{1})_{m}\,(c_{2})_{n}\,m!\,n!}\ x^{m}\,y^{n}+\frac{(-1)^{k}\,(-t_{1})_{k}\,b_{1}\,x}{c_{1}}

\displaystyle\quad\times\sum_{m,n\geq 0}\,\frac{(a+1)_{m+n}\,(b_{1}+1)_{m}\,(b_{2})_{n}\,(-1)^{(m+n)k}\,(-t_{1}+k)_{mk}\,(-t_{2})_{nk}}{(c_{1}+1)_{m}\,(c_{2})_{n}\,m!\,n!}\ x^{m}\,y^{n}+\frac{(-1)^{k}\,(-t_{2})_{k}\,b_{2}\,y}{c_{2}}

\displaystyle\quad\times\sum_{m,n\geq 0}\,\frac{(a+1)_{m+n}\,(b_{1})_{m}\,(b_{2}+1)_{n}\,(-1)^{(m+n)k}\,(-t_{1})_{mk}\,(-t_{2}+k)_{nk}}{(c_{1})_{m}\,(c_{2}+1)_{n}\,m!\,n!}\ x^{m}\,y^{n}

\displaystyle=\sum_{m,n\geq 0}\,\frac{(a)_{m+n}\,(b_{1})_{m}\,(b_{2})_{n}\,(-1)^{(m+n)k}\,(-t_{1})_{mk}\,(-t_{2})_{nk}}{(c_{1})_{m}\,(c_{2})_{n}\,m!\,n!}\ x^{m}\,y^{n}

\displaystyle\quad+\sum_{m,n\geq 0}\frac{m}{a}\,\frac{(a)_{m+n}\,(b_{1})_{m}\,(b_{2})_{n}\,(-1)^{(m+n)k}\,(-t_{1})_{mk}\,(-t_{2})_{nk}}{(c_{1})_{m}\,(c_{2})_{n}\,m!\,n!}\ x^{m}\,y^{n}

\displaystyle\quad+\sum_{m,n\geq 0}\frac{n}{a}\,\frac{(a)_{m+n}\,(b_{1})_{m}\,(b_{2})_{n}\,(-1)^{(m+n)k}\,(-t_{1})_{mk}\,(-t_{2})_{nk}}{(c_{1})_{m}\,(c_{2})_{n}\,m!\,n!}\ x^{m}\,y^{n}

\displaystyle=\sum_{m,n\geq 0}\frac{a+m+n}{a}\,\frac{(a)_{m+n}\,(b_{1})_{m}\,(b_{2})_{n}\,(-1)^{(m+n)k}\,(-t_{1})_{mk}\,(-t_{2})_{nk}}{(c_{1})_{m}\,(c_{2})_{n}\,m!\,n!}\ x^{m}\,y^{n}

\displaystyle=\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a+1,b_{1},b_{2};c_{1},c_{2};t_{1},t_{2},k,x,y).

(5.6)

This shows the result is true for
s=1
. Assuming the result to be true for
s=p
leads to the validity of results for
s=p+1
.
Hence the formula is true for each natural number. The other formulae can be proved in the same manner.
∎

Besides, we have several other difference and differential recursion formulae. We start our findings with the differential recursion formulae first. To obtain, we list simple differential relations as

\displaystyle a\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a+1)=(a+\theta+\phi)\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2};

(5.7)

\displaystyle(a+\theta+\phi-1)\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a-1)=(a-1)\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2};

(5.8)

\displaystyle b_{1}\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(b_{1}+1)=(b_{1}+\theta)\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2};

(5.9)

\displaystyle(b_{1}+\theta-1)\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(b_{1}-1)=(b_{1}-1)\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2};

(5.10)

\displaystyle b_{2}\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(b_{2}+1)=(b_{2}+\phi)\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2};

(5.11)

\displaystyle(b_{2}+\phi-1)\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(b_{2}-1)=(b_{2}-1)\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2};

(5.12)

\displaystyle(c_{1}-1)\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(c_{1}-1)=(c_{1}+\theta-1)\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2};

(5.13)

\displaystyle(c_{1}+\theta)\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(c_{1}+1)=c_{1}\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2};

(5.14)

\displaystyle(c_{2}-1)\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(c_{2}-1)=(c_{2}+\phi-1)\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2};

(5.15)

\displaystyle(c_{2}+\phi)\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(c_{2}+1)=c_{2}\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}.

(5.16)

On combining any two of the above relations, we get first order or second order differential recursion relations. We produce here a list of such relations.

\displaystyle a\,(a-1)\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a+1)-(a+\theta+\phi)\,(a+\theta+\phi-1)\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a-1)=0;

(5.17)

\displaystyle a\,(b_{1}-1)\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a+1)-(a+\theta+\phi)\,(b_{1}+\theta-1)\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(b_{1}-1)=0;

(5.18)

\displaystyle a\,(b_{2}-1)\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a+1)-(a+\theta+\phi)\,(b_{2}+\phi-1)\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(b_{2}-1)=0;

(5.19)

\displaystyle a\,c_{1}\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a+1)-(a+\theta+\phi)\,(c_{1}+\theta)\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(c_{1}+1)=0;

(5.20)

\displaystyle a\,c_{2}\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a+1)-(a+\theta+\phi)\,(c_{2}+\phi)\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(c_{2}+1)=0;

(5.21)

\displaystyle a\,(b_{1}+\theta)\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(a+1)-b_{1}\,(a+\theta+\phi)\,\mathcal{F}^{(1)}_{2}(b_{1}+1)=0;

(5.22)
