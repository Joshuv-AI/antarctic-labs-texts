Brauer group of moduli stack of stable parabolic PGL ​ ( r ) \textnormal{PGL}(r) -bundles over a curve
Indranil Biswas, Sujoy Chakraborty, Arijit Dey

Abstract
. Let k k be an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero. We prove that the Brauer group of the moduli stack of stable parabolic PGL ​ ( r , k ) \textnormal{PGL}(r,k) -bundles on a curve X X , with full flag quasi-parabolic structures at an arbitrary parabolic divisor, coincides with the Brauer group of the smooth locus of the corresponding coarse moduli space of parabolic PGL ​ ( r , k ) \textnormal{PGL}(r,k) -bundles. We also compute the Brauer group of the smooth locus of this coarse moduli for more general quasi-parabolic types and weights satisfying certain conditions.

Let

Definition 2.1
.

A

•

a string of positive integers
(m^{i}_{1},\,m^{i}_{2},\,\cdots,\,m^{i}_{l_{i}})

, and

•

an increasing sequence of real numbers

.

If

, we say that it is a

parabolic data.

A
parabolic structure

over

, with parabolic data of rank

as above, consists of the following:
for each

, a weighted filtration

\displaystyle E_{p_{i}}\,=\,E^{i}_{1}\,\supsetneq\,E^{i}_{2}\,\supsetneq\,

\displaystyle\,\cdots\,\supsetneq\,E^{i}_{l_{i}}\,\supsetneq\,E^{i}_{l_{i}+1}\,=\,0

\displaystyle 0\,\leq\,\alpha^{i}_{1}\,<\,\alpha^{i}_{2}\,<\,

\displaystyle\,\cdots\,<\,\alpha^{i}_{l_{i}}\,<\,\alpha^{i}_{l_{i}+1}\,=\,1

.

The above collection

, and the above integer

Let

, where

, while

Definition 2.2
.

Let

be a parabolic bundle on

of rank

. The

The parabolic bundle

\textnormal{par}\mu(F_{*})\,\leq\,\textnormal{par}\mu(E_{*})\ \ \,(\text{respectively, }\ \textnormal{par}\mu(F_{*})\,<\,\textnormal{par}\mu(E_{*})),

denotes the parabolic bundle defined by

Definition 2.3

A system of weights

\displaystyle<\,\alpha^{q}_{2}\,<\,\cdots\cdots<\,\alpha^{q}_{n}\,<\,\alpha^{q}_{n+1}\,=\,1.

and its complement will still have codimension 2. Of course, the same is true for
V_{\gamma}/\Gamma\subset PN^{sm}_{\gamma,i}
as well.
From this we conclude that

\Br(PN^{sm}_{\alpha,i})\simeq\Br(PN^{sm}_{\gamma,i})\,.

Note that
\Br(PN^{sm}_{\alpha,i})
is already known by Proposition
5.1
.
