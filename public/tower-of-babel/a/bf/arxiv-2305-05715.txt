Second Dome of Superconductivity in YBa 2 Cu 3 O 7 at High Pressure
Johannes Nokelainen, Matthew E. Matzelle, Christopher Lane, Nabil Atlam, Ruiqi Zhang, Robert S. Markiewicz, Bernardo Barbiellini, Jianwei Sun, Arun Bansil

Abstract
Evidence is growing that a second dome of high- T c T_{\mathrm{c}} superconductivity can be accessed in the cuprates by increasing the doping beyond the first dome. Here we use ab initio methods without invoking any free parameters, such as the Hubbard U U , to reveal that pressure could turn YBa 2 Cu 3 O 7 into an ideal candidate for second-dome-superconductivity, displaying the predicted signature of strongly hybridized d x 2 − y 2 d_{x^{2}-y^{2}} and d z 2 d_{z^{2}} orbitals. Notably, pressure is found to induce a phase transition replacing the antiferromagnetic phases with an orbitally-degenerate d d – d d phase. Our study suggests that the origin of the second dome is correlated with the oxygen-hole fraction in the CuO 2 planes and the collapse of the pseudogap phase.
