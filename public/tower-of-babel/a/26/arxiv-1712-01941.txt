1. Introduction

Quaternion algebras and of symbol algebras have applications in various branches of mathematics, but also in computer science, physics, signal theory.

In this chapter we introduce special numbers, special quaternions, special symbol elements, and we present some of their properties and their applications in combinatorics, number theory and associative algebra theory.

3. Some properties of special quaternions

Let
l
be a nonzero natural number. We consider the sequence
\left(a_{n}\right)_{n\geq 0}

a_{n}=l\cdot a_{n-1}+a_{n-2},\;n\geq 2,a_{0}=0,a_{1}=1

and let the sequence
\left(b_{n}\right)_{n\geq 0}

b_{n}=l\cdot b_{n-1}+b_{n-2},\;n\geq 2,b_{0}=2,b_{1}=l.

Let
\alpha=\frac{l+\sqrt{l^{2}+4}}{2}
and
\beta=\frac{l-\sqrt{l^{2}+4}}{2}.
It results immediately the following relations:

Binet’s formula for the sequence

\left(a_{n}\right)_{n\geq 0}.

a_{n}=\frac{\alpha^{n}-\beta^{n}}{\alpha-\beta}=\frac{\alpha^{n}-\beta^{n}}{\sqrt{l^{2}+4}},\ \ \left(\forall\right)n\in\mathbb{N}.

Binet’s formula for the sequence

\left(b_{n}\right)_{n\geq 0}.

b_{n}=\alpha^{n}+\beta^{n},\ \ \left(\forall\right)n\in\mathbb{N}.

In the following, we show that the product of two elements belonging to the sequences
\left(a_{n}\right)_{n\geq 0},

\left(b_{n}\right)_{n\geq 0}
are transformed into sums of elements belonging to the same sequences. Also, we find another properties of these sequences.

Proposition 3.1.

Let

\left(a_{n}\right)_{n\geq 0},

\left(b_{n}\right)_{n\geq 0}

be the sequences previously defined.
Then, the following equalities are true
:

i)

b_{n}b_{n+m}=b_{2n+m}+\left(-1\right)^{n}b_{m},\ \left(\forall\right)n,m\in\mathbb{N};

ii)

a_{n}b_{n+m}=a_{2n+m}+\left(-1\right)^{n+1}a_{m},\ \left(\forall\right)n,m\in\mathbb{N};

iii)

a_{n+m}b_{n}=a_{2n+m}+\left(-1\right)^{n}a_{m},\ \left(\forall\right)n,m\in\mathbb{N};

iv)

a_{n}a_{n+m}=\frac{1}{l^{2}+4}\left[b_{2n+m}+\left(-1\right)^{n+1}b_{m}\right],\ \left(\forall\right)n,m\in\mathbb{N};

v)

b_{n}+b_{n+2}=\left(l^{2}+4\right)\cdot a_{n+1},\ \left(\forall\right)n\in\mathbb{N};

vi)

a^{2}_{n}+a^{2}_{n+1}=a_{2n+1},\ \left(\forall\right)n\in\mathbb{N};

vii)

b^{2}_{n}+b^{2}_{n+1}=\left(l^{2}+4\right)\cdot a_{2n+1},\ \left(\forall\right)n\in\mathbb{N};

Proof.
Let
n,m
be two positive integers. Applying Binet’s formulae, we have:

i)

b_{n}b_{n+m}=\left(\alpha^{n}+\beta^{n}\right)\cdot\left(\alpha^{n+m}+\beta^{n+m}\right)=

=\alpha^{2n+m}+\beta^{2n+m}+\alpha^{n}\beta^{n}\left(\alpha^{m}+\beta^{m}\right)=b_{2n+m}+\left(-1\right)^{n}b_{m}.

ii)

a_{n}b_{n+m}=\frac{\alpha^{n}-\beta^{n}}{\alpha-\beta}\left(\alpha^{n+m}+\beta^{n+m}\right)=

=\frac{\alpha^{2n+m}-\beta^{2n+m}}{\alpha-\beta}-\frac{\alpha^{n}\beta^{n}\left(\alpha^{m}-\beta^{m}\right)}{\alpha-\beta}=a_{2n+m}+\left(-1\right)^{n+1}a_{m}.

iii)

a_{n+m}b_{n}=\frac{\alpha^{n+m}-\beta^{n+m}}{\alpha-\beta}\left(\alpha^{n}+\beta^{n}\right)=

=\frac{\alpha^{2n+m}-\beta^{2n+m}}{\alpha-\beta}+\frac{\alpha^{n}\beta^{n}\left(\alpha^{m}-\beta^{m}\right)}{\alpha-\beta}=a_{2n+m}+\left(-1\right)^{n}a_{m}.

iv)

a_{n}a_{n+m}=\frac{\alpha^{n}-\beta^{n}}{\alpha-\beta}\cdot\frac{\alpha^{n+m}-\beta^{n+m}}{\alpha-\beta}=

=\frac{1}{\left(\alpha-\beta\right)^{2}}\cdot\left[\alpha^{2n+m}+\beta^{2n+m}-\alpha^{n}\beta^{n}\left(\alpha^{m}+\beta^{m}\right)\right]=

=\frac{1}{l^{2}+4}\left[b_{2n+m}+\left(-1\right)^{n+1}b_{m}\right].

v)

b_{n}+b_{n+2}=\alpha^{n}+\beta^{n}+\alpha^{n+2}+\beta^{n+2}=

=\alpha^{n+1}\cdot\left(\alpha+\frac{1}{\alpha}\right)+\beta^{n+1}\cdot\left(\beta+\frac{1}{\beta}\right)=

=\alpha^{n+1}\cdot\sqrt{l^{2}+4}-\beta^{n+1}\cdot\sqrt{l^{2}+4}=\left(l^{2}+4\right)\cdot a_{n+1}.

vi) Applying iv) for
m=0,
we have:

a^{2}_{n}+a^{2}_{n+1}=\frac{1}{l^{2}+4}\left[b_{2n}+\left(-1\right)^{n+1}b_{0}+b_{2n+2}+\left(-1\right)^{n+2}b_{0}\right]=

=\frac{1}{l^{2}+4}\left[b_{2n}+b_{2n+2}\right].

Applying v) we obtain:

a^{2}_{n}+a^{2}_{n+1}=a_{2n+1}.

vii) Applying v) we have:

b^{2}_{n}+b^{2}_{n+1}=\left(\alpha^{n}+\beta^{n}\right)^{2}+\left(\alpha^{n+1}+\beta^{n+1}\right)^{2}=

=\alpha^{2n}+\beta^{2n}+2\left(-1\right)^{n}+\alpha^{2n+2}+\beta^{2n+2}+2\left(-1\right)^{n+1}=

=b_{2n}+b_{2n+2}=\left(l^{2}+4\right)\cdot a_{2n+1}.
