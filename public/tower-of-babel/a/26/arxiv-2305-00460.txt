A Family of Bipartite Separability Criteria Based on Bloch Representation of Density Matrices
Xue-Na Zhu 1, Jing Wang 2, Gui Bao 1, Ming Li 2, Shu-Qian Shen 2, Shao-Ming Fei 3,4

Abstract
Abstract We study the separability of bipartite quantum systems in arbitrary dimensions based on the Bloch representation of density matrices. We present two separability criteria for quantum states in terms of the matrices T α ​ β ​ ( ρ ) T_{\alpha\beta}(\rho) and W a ​ b , α ​ β ​ ( ρ ) W_{ab,\alpha\beta}(\rho) constructed from the correlation tensors in the Bloch representation. These separability criteria can be simplified and detect more entanglement than the previous separability criteria. Detailed examples are given to illustrate the advantages of results.

Introduction

Quantum entanglement
F
;
K
;

;

;

information processing and quantum computation

The quantification of quantum entanglement has drawn much attention in the last decade. A prior question in the study of quantum entanglement is to determine whether a given quantum state is entangled or not. Denote
H_{M}
and
H_{N}
the vector spaces with dimensions
M
and
N
, respectively. A bipartite
M\otimes N
state
\rho\in H_{M}\otimes H_{N}
is said to be separable if it can be written as a convex sum of tensor products of the states of subsystems,

\rho=\sum_{i}p_{i}\rho^{i}_{M}\otimes\rho^{i}_{N},

(1)

where
p_{i}\geq 0
and
\sum_{i}p_{i}=1
. Otherwise
\rho
is said to be entangled.

As a consequence, much efforts have been devoted to the so-called separability problem. The most well-known one is the positive partial transpose (PPT) criterion
ph1
;
ph2
, which is both necessary and sufficient for low-dimensional systems
2\otimes 2
and
2\otimes 3
. For high-dimensional states, the PPT criterion is only a necessary one. A variety of separability criteria have been proposed so far, such realignment criteria
r1
;
r2
, covariance matrix criterion (CMC)
151
and so on
1
;
2
;
3
;
S
. In particular, much subsequent works
VJ
;
shen
;
Liming
;
2020
have been devoted to finding necessary conditions for separability based on Bloch representation of density matrices.

In terms of the Bloch representation any quantum state
\rho\in H_{M}\otimes H_{N}
can be written as,

\rho=\frac{1}{MN}\big(I_{M}\otimes I_{N}+\sum_{k=1}^{M^{2}-1}r_{k}\lambda^{M}_{k}\otimes I_{N}+\sum_{l=1}^{N^{2}-1}s_{l}I_{M}\otimes\lambda^{N}_{l}+\sum_{k=1}^{M^{2}-1}\sum_{l=1}^{N^{2}-1}t_{kl}\lambda^{M}_{k}\otimes\lambda^{N}_{l}\big),

(2)

where
I_{i}

(i=M,N)
denote the
i\times i
identity matrix,

\lambda^{M}_{i}
,
i=1,2,...,M^{2}-1
, are the generators of
SU(M)
given by
\{\omega_{l},u_{jk},v_{jk}\}
with

\omega_{l}=\sqrt{\frac{2}{(l+1)(l+2)}}\left(\sum_{i=0}^{l}|i\rangle\langle i|-(l+1)|l+1\rangle\langle l+1|\right)
,
u_{jk}=|j\rangle\langle k|+|k\rangle\langle j|
,
v_{jk}=-i(|j\rangle\langle k|-|k\rangle\langle j|)
,
0\leq l\leq M-2
and
0\leq j<k\leq M-1
,
r_{i}=\frac{M}{2}Tr(\rho\lambda^{M}_{i}\otimes I_{N})
,

s_{i}=\frac{N}{2}Tr(\rho I_{N}\otimes\lambda^{N}_{i})

and

t_{ij}=\frac{MN}{4}Tr(\rho\lambda^{M}_{i}\otimes\lambda^{N}_{j}).

Denote

r=(r_{1},...,r_{M^{2}-1})^{t}
and
s=(s_{1},...,s_{N^{2}-1})^{t}
, where
t
stands for transpose.
Let
T(\rho)
be the matrix with entries
t_{kl}
.
If the bipartite state
\rho\in H_{M}\otimes H_{N}
with Bloch representation
(
2
) is separable, it has been shown that
VJ

||T(\rho)||_{KF}\leq\sqrt{\frac{MN(M-1)(N-1)}{4}},

(3)

where the Ky Fan matrix norm is defined as the sum of the singular value of the matrix,
||A||_{KF}=Tr\sqrt{A^{\dagger}A}.

In
Liming
the authors presented a stronger separability criteria,

||T^{{}^{\prime}}(\rho)||_{KF}\leq\frac{\sqrt{(M^{2}-M+2)(N^{2}-N+2)}}{2MN}

(4)

for separable states, where
T^{{}^{\prime}}(\rho)=\begin{pmatrix}1&s^{t}\\
r&T(\rho)\end{pmatrix}.

In
shen
, the authors constructed the following matrix,

S^{m}_{ab}(\rho)=\begin{pmatrix}abE_{m\times m}&aw^{t}_{m}(s)\\
bw_{m}(r)&T(\rho)\end{pmatrix},

where
a
and
b
are nonnegative real numbers,
E_{mm}
is the
m\times m
matrix with all entries being
1
,
m
is a given natural number,

w_{m}(x)
denotes
m
columns of the column vector
x
, i.e.,
w_{m}(x)=(x...x).

The Theorem 1 of
shen
showed that if the state
\rho\in H_{M}\otimes H_{N}
is separable, then
\rho
satisfies

||S^{m}_{ab}(\rho)||_{KF}\leq\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{(2ma^{2}+M^{2}-M)(2mb^{2}+N^{2}-N)},

(5)

which is even stronger than the previous criteria.

II
Separability Conditions from the Bloch Representation based on
T_{\alpha\beta}(\rho)

Denote
\alpha=(a_{1},...,a_{n})^{t}
and
\beta=(b_{1},...,b_{m})^{t}
, where

a_{i}

(i=1,...,n)
and
b_{j}

(j=1,...,m)
are given real numbers,
m
and
n
are positive integers. We define the following matrix,

T_{\alpha\beta}(\rho)=\begin{pmatrix}\alpha\beta^{t}&\alpha s^{t}\\
r\beta^{t}&T(\rho)\end{pmatrix}.

(6)

Using
T_{\alpha\beta}(\rho)
, we have the following separability criterion for bipartite states.

Theorem 1

If the state
\rho\in H_{M}\otimes H_{N}
is separable, then

||T_{\alpha\beta}(\rho)||_{KF}\leq\sqrt{||\alpha||^{2}_{2}+\frac{M(M-1)}{2}}\sqrt{||\beta||^{2}_{2}+\frac{N(N-1)}{2}},

(7)

where
||\cdot||_{2}
is the Euclidean norm on
R^{N^{2}-1}
.

[Proof]
A bipartite quantum state with Bloch representation (
2
)
is separable if and only if there exist vectors
\mu_{i}\in R^{M^{2}-1}
and

\nu_{i}\in R^{N^{2}-1}
with
||\mu_{i}||_{2}=\sqrt{\frac{M(M-1)}{2}}
and

||\nu_{i}||_{2}=\sqrt{\frac{N(N-1)}{2}}
, and
0<p_{i}\leq 1
with
\sum_{i}p_{i}=1
such that

T(\rho)=\sum_{i}p_{i}\mu_{i}\nu^{t}_{i},r=\sum_{i}p_{i}\mu_{i},s=\sum_{i}p_{i}\nu_{i}.

The matrix
T_{\alpha\beta}(\rho)
can then be written as,

\displaystyle T_{\alpha\beta}(\rho)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\begin{pmatrix}\alpha\beta^{t}&\alpha s^{t}\\
r\beta^{t}&T(\rho)\end{pmatrix}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{i}p_{i}\begin{pmatrix}\alpha\beta^{t}&\alpha\nu^{t}_{i}\\
\mu_{i}\beta^{t}&\mu_{i}\nu^{t}_{i}\end{pmatrix}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{i}p_{i}\begin{pmatrix}\alpha\\
\mu_{i}\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}\beta^{t},\nu^{t}_{i}\end{pmatrix}.

Therefore,

\displaystyle||T_{\alpha\beta}(\rho)||_{KF}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\sum_{i}p_{i}\left|\left|\begin{pmatrix}\alpha\\
\mu_{i}\end{pmatrix}\right|\right|_{2}\cdot\left|\left|\begin{pmatrix}\beta^{t},\nu^{t}_{i}\end{pmatrix}\right|\right|_{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sqrt{||\alpha||^{2}_{2}+\frac{M(M-1)}{2}}\sqrt{||\beta||^{2}_{2}+\frac{N(N-1)}{2}}.

\Box

It can be seen that if we chose
a_{i}=a
and
b_{j}=b
for
i,j=1,...,n

and
m=n
, Theorem
1
reduces to the separability criterion (
5
) given in
shen
.

Define

R(\beta)=\begin{pmatrix}p\beta\beta^{t}&\beta c^{t}\\
c\beta^{t}&\textrm{W}\end{pmatrix},

where
p
is a nonzero real number,
\beta
(
c
) is a nonzero
n
(
m
)-dimensional real vector,
W
is an
m\times m
Hermitian matrix.
We denote
\lambda_{i}(R(\beta))

(i=1,...,m+n)
the singular values of
R(\beta)
with
\lambda_{i}(R(\beta))\leq\lambda_{j}(R(\beta))

(i\leq j)
.

Lemma 1

For
\beta_{1}\not=\beta_{2}
but
||\beta_{1}||_{2}=||\beta_{2}||_{2}
, we have

\lambda_{i}(R(\beta_{1}))=\lambda_{i}(R(\beta_{2}))

(i=1,...,m+n).

[Proof]

With respect to any nonzero real vector
\beta=(b_{1},b_{2},...,b_{n})^{t}
, there exists a unitary matrix

U
such that
\textrm{U}\beta=(0,0,...,0,||\beta||_{2})^{t}
. Then we have

\begin{pmatrix}U&0\\
0&I\end{pmatrix}R\begin{pmatrix}U^{\dagger}&0\\
0&I\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}0&0&0\\
0&p||\beta||^{2}_{2}&||\beta||_{2}c^{t}\\
0&||\beta||_{2}c&\textrm{W}\\
\end{pmatrix}.

Denote

D(\beta)=\begin{pmatrix}p||\beta||^{2}_{2}&||\beta||_{2}c^{t}\\
||\beta||_{2}c&\textrm{W}\\
\end{pmatrix}.

Since the singular values of an Hermitian matrix do not change under the unitary transformations, we have
\lambda_{i}(R(\beta))=\lambda_{i}\left(\begin{pmatrix}0&0\\
0&D(\beta)\\
\end{pmatrix}\right),

(i=1,...,m+n).

Because of
D(\beta_{1})=D(\beta_{2})
, we complete the proof.
\Box

Since the Ky Fan matrix norm

||T_{\alpha\beta}(\rho)||_{KF}=Tr\sqrt{T_{\alpha\beta}(\rho)^{\dagger}T_{\alpha\beta}(\rho)}
,

r\in\emph{R}^{M^{2}-1}
,
s\in\emph{R}^{N^{2}-1}
and
T(\rho)\in\emph{R}^{(M^{2}-1)(N^{2}-1)}
, we have

T_{\alpha\beta}(\rho)^{\dagger}T_{\alpha\beta}(\rho)=\begin{pmatrix}(||\alpha||^{2}_{2}+||r||^{2}_{2})\beta\beta^{t}&\beta(||\alpha||_{2}^{2}s^{t}+r^{t}T)\\
(||\alpha||_{2}^{2}s+T^{t}r)\beta^{t}&||\alpha||_{2}^{2}ss^{t}+T^{t}T\end{pmatrix}.

By using Lemma 1 we have the following corollary.

Corollary 1

For any quantum state
\rho
,

||T_{\alpha\beta}(\rho)||_{KF}=||T_{||\alpha||_{2}||\beta||_{2}}(\rho)||_{KF}.

From the Corollary

||T_{ab}(\rho)||_{KF}\leq\sqrt{\frac{NM(N-1)(M-1)}{4}}+|ab|,

W{ab,\alpha\beta}(\rho)

Next we define

W_{ab,\alpha\beta}(\rho)=\begin{pmatrix}ab&a\alpha^{t}\otimes s^{t}\\

Theorem 3

If the state
\rho\in H_{M}\otimes H_{N}
is separable, then

||W_{ab,\alpha\beta}(\rho)||_{KF}\leq\sqrt{a^{2}+||\beta||^{2}_{2}\frac{M(M-1)}{2}}\sqrt{b^{2}+||\alpha||^{2}_{2}\frac{N(N-1)}{2}},

where
||\cdot||_{2}
is the Euclidean norm on
R^{N^{2}-1}
.

[Proof]
A bipartite quantum state with Bloch representation (
2
)
is separable if and only if there exist vectors
\mu_{i}\in R^{M^{2}-1}
and

\nu_{i}\in R^{N^{2}-1}
with
||\mu_{i}||_{2}=\sqrt{\frac{M(M-1)}{2}}
and

||\nu_{i}||_{2}=\sqrt{\frac{N(N-1)}{2}}
,
0<p_{i}\leq 1
with
\sum_{i}p_{i}=1
such that

T(\rho)=\sum_{i}p_{i}\mu_{i}\nu^{t}_{i}
,

and

.
Therefore, for separable states
\rho
the matrix
W_{ab,\alpha\beta}(\rho)
reduces to

\displaystyle W_{ab,\alpha\beta}(\rho)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\begin{pmatrix}ab&a\alpha^{t}\otimes s^{t}\\
b\beta\otimes r&\beta\alpha^{t}\otimes T(\rho)\end{pmatrix}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{i}p_{i}\begin{pmatrix}ab&a\alpha^{t}\otimes\nu_{i}^{t}\\
b\beta\otimes\mu_{i}&\beta\alpha^{t}\otimes\mu_{i}\nu_{i}^{t}\end{pmatrix}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{i}p_{i}\begin{pmatrix}a\\
\beta\otimes\mu_{i}\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}b&\alpha^{t}\otimes\nu^{t}_{i}\end{pmatrix}.

Hence one gets

\displaystyle||W_{ab,\alpha\beta}(\rho)||_{KF}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\sum_{i}p_{i}\left|\left|\begin{pmatrix}a\\
\beta\otimes\mu_{i}\end{pmatrix}\right|\right|_{2}\cdot\left|\left|\begin{pmatrix}b&\alpha^{t}\otimes\nu^{t}_{i}\end{pmatrix}\right|\right|_{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sqrt{a^{2}+||\beta||^{2}_{2}\frac{M(M-1)}{2}}\sqrt{b^{2}+||\alpha||^{2}_{2}\frac{N(N-1)}{2}},

which proves the theorem.
\Box

Conclusions and Remarks

In summary, based on the Bloch representation of a bipartite quantum state
