Coupled Stochastic Allen-Cahn equations: Existence and Uniqueness
Tran Hoa Phu

Abstract
In this paper, we concern a system of stochastic PDE’s. Our system consists of two components. Each component evolves according to the sotchastic Allen-Cahn equation with a symmetric double well potential and with addtional small space-time white noise. The two components interact with each other by an attractive linear force. We aim to give a global existence and uniqueness result in a space of continuous functions on ℝ × ℝ + \mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{R}^{+} . We apply methods proposed by Doering [ 1 ] .

Lemma 3.2
.

The operator
H
with heat kernel
H_{t}(x,y)
is a bounded map from
L^{p}(d\mu)\rightarrow L^{p}(\mu)
for any

Proof.

Let
g\in L^{p}(\mu)
and set
f:=Hg
. We prove that
f
is also in
L^{p}(\mu)
and
\left\lVert H\right\rVert<\infty
. We have

\displaystyle f(x,t)

\displaystyle=\int_{0}^{\infty}\int_{\mathbb{R}}\chi_{[0,\infty]}(t-s)H_{t-s}(x,y)\chi_{[0,\infty]}(s)g(y,s)dyds

(3.3)

Multiplying both sides
e^{-\alpha|x|/p-\alpha^{2}t/2p}
and note that
e^{-\alpha|x|}\leq e^{\alpha|x-y|}e^{-|y|}
, we derive

\displaystyle e^{-\alpha|x|/p-\alpha^{2}p.t/2}|f(x,t)|

(3.4)

\displaystyle\leq\int_{0}^{\infty}\int_{\mathbb{R}}\bigg(\chi_{[0,\infty]}(t-s)H_{t-s}(x,y)e^{\alpha|x-y|/p-\alpha^{2}(t-s)/2p}

\displaystyle\times\chi_{[0,\infty]}(s)g(y,s)e^{-\alpha|y|/p-\alpha^{2}.s/2p}\bigg)dyds

H_{t}
and it is suggested to apply Young’s inequality

\left\lVert f\right\rVert_{L^{p}(\mu)}\leq\left\lVert g\right\rVert_{L^{p}(\mu)}\int_{0}^{\infty}\int_{\mathbb{R}}H_{t}(x,y)e^{\alpha|x-y|/p-\alpha^{2}t/2p}dydt

(3.5)

Moreover,

\displaystyle\left\lVert H\right\rVert_{L^{p}(\mu)\rightarrow L^{p}(\mu)}

\displaystyle\leq\int_{0}^{\infty}\int_{\mathbb{R}}H_{t}(x,y)e^{\alpha|x-y|/p-\alpha^{2}t/2p}dydt

(3.6)

\displaystyle\leq\int_{0}^{\infty}e^{-\alpha^{2}t/2p}\int_{\mathbb{R}}\dfrac{e^{-(x-y)^{2}/2t+\alpha|x-y|/p}}{\sqrt{2\pi t}}dydt

\displaystyle\leq\int_{0}^{\infty}e^{-\alpha^{2}t/2p(1-1/p)}dt\int_{\mathbb{R}}e^{-(z-\dfrac{\sqrt{t}\alpha}{\sqrt{2}p})^{2}}dz<\infty\Leftrightarrow p>1.

∎

Lemma 3.3

\displaystyle e^{-\alpha|x|-\alpha^{2}.t/2}|f(x,t)|

\overline{A}_{1}\geq\int_{\mathbb{R^{+}}}\int_{\mathbb{R}}e^{-(2n+2)\beta t}(m_{1}^{\prime}-m_{1})^{2n+4}d\mu\geq\left\lVert e^{-\beta t}(m_{1}^{\prime}-m_{1})\right\rVert_{L^{2n+4}(\mu)}^{2n+4}

(3.45)

and

\overline{A}_{2}=\int_{\mathbb{R^{+}}}\int_{\mathbb{R}}e^{-(2n+2)\beta t}X_{1}^{2n+1}(X_{1}-X_{2})d\mu

(3.46)

and from
\overline{A}_{1}+\overline{A}_{2}\leq 0
, we derive

\left\lVert e^{-\beta t}(m_{1}^{\prime}-m_{1})\right\rVert_{L^{2n+4}(\mu)}^{2n+4}+\int_{\mathbb{R^{+}}}\int_{\mathbb{R}}e^{-(2n+2)\beta t}X_{1}^{2n+1}(X_{1}-X_{2})d\mu\leq 0

(3.47)

Analogously,

\left\lVert e^{-\beta t}(m_{2}^{\prime}-m_{2})\right\rVert_{L^{2n+4}(\mu)}^{2n+4}+\int_{\mathbb{R^{+}}}\int_{\mathbb{R}}e^{-(2n+2)\beta t}X_{2}^{2n+1}(X_{2}-X_{1})d\mu\leq 0

(3.48)
