Generalized Heisenberg-Virasoro algebra and matrix models from quantum algebra
Fridolin Melong † and Raimar Wulkenhaar ‡

Abstract
. In this paper, we construct the Heisenberg-Virasoro algebra in the framework of the ℛ ⁡ ( p , q ) \mathcal{R}(p,q) -deformed quantum algebras. Moreover, the ℛ ⁡ ( p , q ) \mathcal{R}(p,q) -Heisenberg-Witt n n -algebras is also investigated. Furthermore, we generalize the notion of the elliptic hermitian matrix models. We use the constraints to evaluate the ℛ ⁡ ( p , q ) \mathcal{R}(p,q) -differential operatos of the Virasoro algebra and generalize it to higher order differential operators. Particular cases corresponding to quantum algebras existing in literature are deduced.

We introduce a family of deformations of the commutator:

[A,B]_{a,b}=aAB-bBA,

Proposition 1

The

\displaystyle\,\big[L_{m_{1}},L_{m_{2}}\big]_{x,y}\phi(z)

\displaystyle,\big[L_{m_{1}},I_{m_{2}}\big]_{u,v}\phi(z)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-[m_{2}]_{\mathcal{R}(p,q)}\,I_{m_{1}+m_{2}}\phi(z),

\displaystyle\,\big[I_{m_{1}},I_{m_{2}}\big]_{\mathcal{R}(p,q)}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 0,

where

\displaystyle\left\{\begin{array}[]{l}x=q^{m_{1}-m_{2}}\,p^{m_{1}}\,\Theta_{mn}(p,q)\mbox{,}\quad y=p^{m_{1}}\,\Theta_{mn}(p,q),\\

\mathcal{R}(p,q)

\displaystyle{\mathcal{D}}_{\mathcal{R}(p,q)}\big(f(z)g(z)\big)

\displaystyle xL_{m_{1}}L_{m_{2}}\phi(z)

By analogy,

\displaystyle yL_{m_{2}}L_{m_{1}}\phi(z)

\displaystyle\left\{\begin{array}[]{l}x=q^{m_{1}-m_{2}}\,p^{m_{1}}\,\Theta_{mn}(p,q),\\

and

4.2.
Heisenberg Virasoro constraints and a toy model

Here, we use the generalized Heisenberg Virasoro constraints to study a toy model. Particular cases are derived. They play an important role in the study of matrix models. We consider the generating function with infinitely many parameters
given by
[
23
]
:

Z^{toy}(t)=\int\,\,x^{\gamma}\,\exp\left(\displaystyle\sum_{s=0}^{\infty}{t_{s}\over s!}x^{s}\right)\,dx,

which encodes many different integrals.
We consider the following expansion:

\displaystyle\exp\left(\displaystyle\sum_{s=0}^{\infty}{t_{s}\over s!}x^{s}\right)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}B_{n}(t_{1},\cdots,t_{n}){x^{n}\over n!},

(35)

where

are the complete Bell polynomials.

The following property holds for the
{\mathcal{R}}(p,q)
-deformed derivative

\displaystyle\int_{{\mathbb{R}}}{\mathcal{D}}_{{\mathcal{R}}(p^{a},q^{a})}f(x)d\,x={K(p^{a},q^{a})\over\epsilon^{a}_{1}-\epsilon^{a}_{2}}\Big(\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}{f(\epsilon^{a}_{1}\,x)\over x}dx-\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}{f(\epsilon^{a}_{2}\,x)\over x}dx\Big)=0,

where

K(p^{a},q^{a})={p^{a}-q^{a}\over p^{P^{a}}-q^{Q^{a}}}{\mathcal{R}}\big(p^{P^{a}},q^{Q^{a}}\big).

For
f(x)=x^{m+\gamma+1}\,\exp\left(\displaystyle\sum_{s=0}^{\infty}{t_{s}\over s!}x^{s}\right),
we have:

\displaystyle\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}{\mathcal{D}}_{{\mathcal{R}}(p^{a},q^{a})}\left(x^{m+\gamma+1}\,\exp\left(\sum_{s=0}^{\infty}{t_{s}\over s!}x^{s}\right)\right)d\,x=0.

Thus,

\displaystyle{\mathcal{D}}_{{\mathcal{R}}(p^{a},q^{a})}\bigg(x^{m+\gamma+1}\,

\displaystyle\exp\left(\displaystyle\sum_{s=0}^{\infty}{t_{s}\over s!}x^{s}\right)\bigg)=\frac{[z\partial_{z}]_{{\mathcal{R}}(p^{a},q^{a})}\,x^{m+\gamma}}{\epsilon^{a\,m}_{1}}\exp\left(\displaystyle\sum_{s=0}^{\infty}{t_{s}\over s!}x^{s}\right)

\displaystyle+{K(p^{a},q^{a})\epsilon^{\alpha(m+1+\gamma)}_{2}\over(\epsilon^{a}_{1}-\epsilon^{a}_{2})x^{-k-m}}\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}{B_{k}(t^{a}_{1},\cdots,t^{a}_{k})\over k!}x^{\gamma}\exp\left(\displaystyle\sum_{s=0}^{\infty}{t_{s}\over s!}x^{s}\right),

where
t^{a}_{k}=(\epsilon^{a\,k}_{1}-\epsilon^{a\,k}_{2})t_{k}.
Then, from the constraints on the partition function,

{\mathbb{T}}^{\mathcal{R}(p^{a},q^{a})}_{m}\,Z^{(toy)}(t)=0,\quad m\geq 0

and

{\mathbb{I}}^{\mathcal{R}(p^{a},q^{a})}_{m}\,Z^{(toy)}(t)=0,\quad m\geq 0,

we have:

\displaystyle{\mathbb{T}}^{\mathcal{R}(p^{a},q^{a})}_{m}

\displaystyle=[z\partial_{z}]_{{\mathcal{R}}(p^{a},q^{a})}\,m!\,\epsilon^{-a\,m}_{1}\,{\partial\over\partial t_{m}}

\displaystyle+K(p^{a},q^{a})\frac{\epsilon^{a(m+1+\gamma)}_{2}}{\epsilon^{a}_{1}-\epsilon^{a}_{2}}\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{(k+m)!}{k!}B_{k}(t^{a}_{1},\cdots,t^{a}_{k})\frac{\partial}{\partial t_{k+m}}.

(36)

Similarly, we obtain:

\displaystyle{\mathbb{I}}^{\mathcal{R}(p^{a},q^{a})}_{m}=\tau^{a(m+1+\gamma)}\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}{(k+m)!\over k!}B_{k}(t^{a}_{1},\cdots,t^{a}_{k})\frac{\partial}{\partial t_{k+m}}.

(37)

Proof.
