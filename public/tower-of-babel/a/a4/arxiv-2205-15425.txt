Lemma 2
.

Let

-regular balanced subgraphs.

We prove the equivalence by proving both implications.

(
\Leftarrow)
In the

(
\Rightarrow)
We consider the two cases separately:

(1)

-regular graph, so

be an arbitrary

-edge-coloring of
(G
,

(2)

Complete bipartite graphs

A graph is bipartite if and only if its vertices can be divided into two sets

V_{2}
