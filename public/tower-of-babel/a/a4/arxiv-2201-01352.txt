Abstract

Heim, Neuhauser and Tröger recently established some inequalities for MacMahon’s plane partition function PL ⁡ ( n ) \mathrm{PL}(n) that generalize known results for Euler’s partition function p ⁡ ( n ) p(n) . Proof.

Thanks to the elementary fact that

Since
