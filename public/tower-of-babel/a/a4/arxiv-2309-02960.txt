Digital k k -continuity, digital k k -isomorphism, local k k -isomorphism, radius 2 2 -local k k -isomorphism, and digital k k -homotopy Thanks: AMS Classification: 68U05 Keywords: digital topology, digital image, digital k k -connectivity, k k -adjacency, digital continuity, digital k k -homotopy
Sang-Eon Han

Abstract
. The present paper refers to the notions of digital continuity, digital k k -isomorphism, local k k -isomorphism, radius 2 2 -local k k -isomorphism, and digital k k -homotopy motivated by the Khalimsky’s version. We discuss something incorrectly mentioned in Boxer’s papers and suggest some accurate information.
