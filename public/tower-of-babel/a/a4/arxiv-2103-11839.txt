Explicit evaluation of some integrals involving polylogarithm functions
Rusen Li

Abstract
In this paper, we give explicit evaluation for some integrals involving polylogarithm functions of types ∫ 0 x t m ​ L ​ i p ​ ( t ) ​ 𝑑 t \int_{0}^{x}t^{m}Li_{p}(t)\mathrm{d}t and ∫ 0 x log m ⁡ ( t ) ​ L ​ i p ​ ( t ) ​ 𝑑 t \int_{0}^{x}\log^{m}(t)Li_{p}(t)\mathrm{d}t . Some more integrals involving the logarithm function will also be derived. Keywords: polylogarithm function, recurrence relation, zeta function



1
Introduction and preliminaries

Let
\mathbb{Z}
,
\mathbb{N}
,
\mathbb{N}_{0}
and
\mathbb{C}
denote the set of integers, positive integers, nonnegative integers and complex numbers, respectively. The well-known polylogarithm function is defined as

Li_{p}(x):=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{x^{n}}{n^{p}}\quad(\lvert x\lvert\leq 1,\quad p\in\mathbb{N}_{0})\,.

Note that when
p=1
,
-Li_{1}(x)
is the logarithm function
\log(1-x)
. Furthermore,
Li_{n}(1)=\zeta(n)
, where
\zeta(s)
denotes the Riemann zeta function which is defined as
\zeta(s):=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}n^{-s}
. The famous generalized harmonic numbers of order
m
is defined by the partial sum of the Riemann Zeta function
\zeta(m)
as:

H_{n}^{(m)}:=\sum_{j=1}^{n}\frac{1}{j^{m}}\quad(n,m\in\mathbb{N})\,.

Before going further, we introduce some notations. Let
p\in\mathbb{N}
and
m\in\mathbb{N}_{0}
, define

\displaystyle J_{0}(m,p):=\int_{0}^{1}x^{m}Li_{p}(x)\mathrm{d}x\,.

Let
p,m\in\mathbb{N}_{0}
and
0\leq x\leq 1
, define

\displaystyle J_{0}(m,p,x):=\int_{0}^{x}t^{m}Li_{p}(t)\mathrm{d}t\,,\quad J_{1}(m,p,x):=\int_{0}^{x}\log^{m}(t)Li_{p}(t)\mathrm{d}t\,.

Let
p,q\in\mathbb{N}
and
m\in\mathbb{Z}
with
m\geq-2
, define

\displaystyle J(m,p,q):=\int_{0}^{1}x^{m}Li_{p}(x)Li_{q}(x)\mathrm{d}x\,.

Let
p,q\in\mathbb{N}_{0}
with
p+q\geq 1
,
r\in\mathbb{N}
, define

\displaystyle K(r,p,q):=\int_{0}^{1}\frac{\log^{r}(x)Li_{p}(x)Li_{q}(x)}{x}\mathrm{d}x\,.

Freitas
[
1
]
showed that integrals
J_{0}(m,p)
,
J(m,p,q)
and
K(r,p,q)
satisfy the following recurrence relations:

\displaystyle J_{0}(m,q)=\frac{\zeta(q)}{m+1}-\frac{1}{m+1}J_{0}(m,q-1)\quad(q\geq 2,m\geq 0)\,,

\displaystyle J(m,p,q)=\frac{\zeta(p)\zeta(q)}{m+1}-\frac{1}{m+1}\bigg(J(m,p-1,q)+J(m,p,q-1)\bigg)

\displaystyle(p,q\geq 2,m\in\mathbb{N}_{0}\cup\{-2\})\,,

\displaystyle K(r,p,q)=-\frac{1}{r+1}\bigg(K(r+1,p-1,q)+K(r+1,p,q-1)\bigg)\quad(p,q,r\in\mathbb{N})\,.

From this Freitas proved that integrals
K(r,p,q)
with
p+q+r
even and
J(m,p,q)
could be reduced to zeta values. Note that the proof was constructive, Freitas didn’t give explicit evaluations for these integrals. On the contrary, Freitas
[
1
]
gave explicit evaluations for
J(-1,p,q)
and
K(r,0,q)
with
r+q
even.

An anonymous reviewer told the author that Sofo
[
2
,
3
,
4
]
and Xu
[
5
,
6
,
7
]
had made many progresses in the area of integrals involving polylogarithm functions, which the author was initially unaware of. It is known to all that polylogarithmic functions are intrinsically connected with sums of harmonic numbers. For instance, Sofo
[
2
]
developed closed form representations for infinite series containing generalized harmonic numbers of type

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^{n+1}H_{n}^{(3)}}{n^{p}\binom{n+k}{k}}\quad(p=0,1)\,.

The author
[
8
]
gave closed form representations for generalized hyperharmonic number sums with reciprocal binomial coefficients, which greatly extend Sofo’s result. Sofo
[
2
]
also obtained explicit evaluations for some integrals involving polylogarithm functions. Motivated by the work of Freitas
[
1
]
, Sofo
[
3
]
investigated the representations of integrals of polylogarithms with negative argument of the type

\int_{0}^{1}x^{m}Li_{p}(-x)Li_{q}(-x)\mathrm{d}x\,

for
m\geq-2
, and for integers
p
and
q
. For
m=-2,-1,0
, Sofo also gave explicit
representations of the integral in terms of Euler sums and for
m\geq 0
, Sofo obtained a
recurrence relation for the integral. As a more general consideration, Sofo
[
4
]
considered integrals of polylogarithms with alternating argument of the type

\int_{0}^{1}x^{m}Li_{p}(x)Li_{q}(-x)\mathrm{d}x\,

for integers
p
and
q
. Similarly, for
m=-2,-1,0
, Sofo gave explicit representations of the integral in terms of Euler sums. Some more integrals involving polylogarithms were obtained.
Xu
[
5
]
showed that quadratic Euler sums of the form

\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}H_{n}^{(m)}}{n^{p}}\quad(m+p\leq 8)\,,

and some integrals of polylogarithm functions of the form

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{1}\frac{Li_{r}(x)Li_{p}(x)Li_{q}(x)}{x}\mathrm{d}x\quad(r+p+q\leq 8)\,

can be written in terms of Riemann zeta values. It is interesting that integrals of polylogarithm functions can be related to multiple zeta (star) values. By using integrals of polylogarithm functions, Xu
[

[
6
]
were also re-discovered by the author in different forms. Furthermore, by using the iterated integral representation of multiple polylogarithm functions, Xu
[
7
]
proved some conjectures proposed by J. M. Borwein, D. M. Bradley and D. J. Broadhurst
[
9
]
. Xu also obtained numerous formulas for alternating multiple zeta values.

In this paper, we mainly give explicit expressions for integrals of types

will also be derived.

2
Integrals involving logarithm function

De Doelder
[
10
]
used the integral
\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\log^{2}(1-t)}{t}\mathrm{d}t
to evaluate infinite series of type
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}}{n^{2}}x^{n}
. As a natural consideration, The author
[
11
]
gave explicit evaluations for infinite series involving generalized (alternating) harmonic numbers of types

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}}{n^{3}}x^{n}
,

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}}{n^{3}}(-x)^{n}
,

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}^{(2)}}{n}x^{n}
,

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}^{(2)}}{n}(-x)^{n}
,

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}^{(2)}}{n^{2}}x^{n}
,

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}^{(2)}}{n^{2}}(-x)^{n}
,

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{\overline{H}_{n}}{n}x^{n}
,

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{\overline{H}_{n}^{(2)}}{n}x^{n}
,

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{\overline{H}_{n}^{(2)}}{n}(-x)^{n}
in terms of polylogarithm functions. However, it seems difficult to give explicit expressions for infinite series of types

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}}{n^{4}}x^{n}
and

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}^{(2)}}{n^{3}}x^{n}
, since the integrals
\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\log^{2}(t)\log^{2}(1-t)}{t}\mathrm{d}t
and
\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\log^{2}(t)Li_{2}(t)}{1-t}\mathrm{d}t
are not known to be related to the polylogarithm functions, even with the help of a mathematical package. It is interesting to evaluate similar type integrals, e.g.,
\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\log^{m}(1-t)}{t^{n}}\mathrm{d}t
. Before going further, We introduce some notations.

Definition 1
.

For
m,n\in\mathbb{N}
with
m\geq n
and
0\leq x\leq 1
, define the quantities
A(m,n,x)
,
B(m,n,x)
and
C(m,n,x)
as

\displaystyle A(m,n,x):=\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\log^{m}(1-t)}{t^{n}}\mathrm{d}t\,,\quad B(m,n,x):=\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\log^{m}(1+t)}{t^{n}}\mathrm{d}t\,,

\displaystyle C(m,n,x):=\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\log^{m}(t)}{(1-t)^{n}}\mathrm{d}t\,.

Lemma 1
.

Let
m\in\mathbb{N}
and
0\leq x\leq 1
, then we have

\displaystyle\quad A(m,1,x)

\displaystyle=\log(x)\log^{m}(1-x)+\sum_{k=0}^{m-2}(-1)^{k}(m-k)_{k+1}\log^{m-k-1}(1-x)Li_{k+2}(1-x)

\displaystyle\quad+(-1)^{m-1}m!Li_{m+1}(1-x)+(-1)^{m}m!\zeta(m+1)\,.

In particular, we have
A(m,1,1)=(-1)^{m}m!\zeta(m+1)
.

Proof.

From the definition of
A(m,1,x)
, by using integration by parts, we can write

\displaystyle\quad A(m,1,x)

\displaystyle=\log(x)\log^{m}(1-x)-m\int_{1}^{1-x}\frac{\log(1-t)\log^{m-1}(t)}{t}\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle=\log(x)\log^{m}(1-x)+m\log^{m-1}(1-x)Li_{2}(1-x)

\displaystyle\quad-m(m-1)\int_{1}^{1-x}\frac{Li_{2}(t)\log^{m-2}(t)}{t}\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle=\log(x)\log^{m}(1-x)+\sum_{k=0}^{m-2}(-1)^{k}(m-k)_{k+1}\log^{m-k-1}(1-x)Li_{k+2}(1-x)

\displaystyle\quad+(-1)^{m-1}m!Li_{m+1}(1-x)+(-1)^{m}m!Li_{m+1}(1)\,.

∎

Lemma 2

(
[
6
]
)
.

Let
m\in\mathbb{N}
and
x\geq 0
, then we have

\displaystyle B(m,1,x)

\displaystyle=\log(x)\log^{m}(1+x)-\frac{m}{m+1}\log^{m+1}(1+x)+m!\zeta(m+1)

\displaystyle\quad-\sum_{i=1}^{m}\binom{m}{i}i!\log^{m-i}(1+x)Li_{i+1}(\frac{1}{1+x})\,.

In particular, we have

B(m,1,1)=-\frac{m}{m+1}\log^{m+1}(2)+m!\zeta(m+1)-\sum_{i=1}^{m}\binom{m}{i}i!\log^{m-i}(2)Li_{i+1}(\frac{1}{2})\,.

Proof.

From the definition of
B(m,1,x)
, by using integration by parts, we can write

\displaystyle\quad B(m,1,x)

\displaystyle=\log(x)\log^{m}(1+x)-m\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\log(t)\log^{m-1}(1+t)}{1+t}\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle=\log(x)\log^{m}(1+x)-m\int_{0}^{x}\log^{m-1}(1+t)\bigg(\frac{\mathrm{d}Li_{2}(\frac{1}{1+t})}{\mathrm{d}t}+\frac{\log(1+t)}{1+t}\bigg)\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle=\log(x)\log^{m}(1+x)-\frac{m}{m+1}\log^{m+1}(1+x)-m\log^{m-1}(1+x)Li_{2}(\frac{1}{1+x})

\displaystyle\quad+m(m-1)\int_{0}^{x}\frac{Li_{2}(\frac{1}{1+t})\log^{m-2}(1+t)}{1+t}\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle=\log(x)\log^{m}(1+x)-\frac{m}{m+1}\log^{m+1}(1+x)+m!Li_{m+1}(1)

\displaystyle\quad-\sum_{i=1}^{m}\binom{m}{i}i!\log^{m-i}(1+x)Li_{i+1}(\frac{1}{1+x})\,.

∎

Proposition 1
.

Let
m\in\mathbb{N}
and
0\leq x\leq 1
, then we have

\displaystyle\quad C(m,1,x)

\displaystyle=-\log(1-x)\log^{m}(x)+m\sum_{i=2}^{m+1}(-1)^{i-1}\binom{m-1}{i-2}(i-2)!\log^{m+1-i}(x)Li_{i}(x)\,.

In particular, we have
C(m,1,1)=(-1)^{m}m!\zeta(m+1)
.

Proof.

From the definition of
C(m,1,x)
, by using integration by parts, we can write

\displaystyle\quad C(m,1,x)

\displaystyle=-\log(1-x)\log^{m}(x)+m\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\log(1-t)\log^{m-1}(t)}{t}\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle=-\log(1-x)\log^{m}(x)-m\log^{m-1}(x)Li_{2}(x)+m(m-1)\int_{0}^{x}\frac{Li_{2}(t)\log^{m-2}(t)}{t}\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle=-\log(1-x)\log^{m}(x)+m\sum_{i=2}^{m+1}(-1)^{i-1}\binom{m-1}{i-2}(i-2)!\log^{m+1-i}(x)Li_{i}(x)\,.

∎

Now we develop explicit expressions for
A(m,n,x)
,
B(m,n,x)
and
C(m,n,x)
.

Theorem 1
.

Let
m,n\in\mathbb{N}
with
m\geq n\geq 2
and
0<x\leq 1
, then we have

\displaystyle\quad A(m,n,x)

\displaystyle=\sum_{y=0}^{n-2}\binom{m}{y}y!(-1)^{y+1}\sum_{i_{0}=n}^{n}\frac{1}{i_{0}-1}\sum_{i_{1}=2}^{i_{0}-1}\frac{1}{i_{1}-1}\cdots\sum_{i_{y}=2}^{i_{y-1}-1}\frac{1}{i_{y}-1}\cdot\frac{\log^{m-y}(1-x)}{x^{i_{y}-1}}

\displaystyle\quad+\sum_{y=0}^{n-2}\binom{m}{y}y!(-1)^{y}\sum_{i_{0}=n}^{n}\frac{1}{i_{0}-1}\sum_{i_{1}=2}^{i_{0}-1}\frac{1}{i_{1}-1}\cdots\sum_{i_{y}=2}^{i_{y-1}-1}\frac{1}{i_{y}-1}\log^{m-y}(1-x)

\displaystyle\quad+\sum_{y=0}^{n-2}\binom{m}{y+1}(y+1)!(-1)^{y+1}\sum_{i_{0}=n}^{n}\frac{1}{i_{0}-1}\sum_{i_{1}=2}^{i_{0}-1}\frac{1}{i_{1}-1}\cdots\sum_{i_{y}=2}^{i_{y-1}-1}\frac{1}{i_{y}-1}

\displaystyle\qquad\times A(m-y-1,1,x)\,,

and

\displaystyle\quad C(m,n,x)

\displaystyle=\frac{(-1)^{m}m!}{n-1}\sum_{y=0}^{n-2}\zeta(m-y)\sum_{i_{1}=2}^{i_{0}-1}\frac{1}{i_{1}-1}\cdots\sum_{i_{y}=2}^{i_{y-1}-1}\frac{1}{i_{y}-1}+\sum_{y=0}^{n-2}\binom{m}{y}y!(-1)^{y}

\displaystyle\quad\times\sum_{i_{0}=n}^{n}\frac{1}{i_{0}-1}\sum_{i_{1}=2}^{i_{0}-1}\frac{1}{i_{1}-1}\cdots\sum_{i_{y}=2}^{i_{y-1}-1}\frac{1}{i_{y}-1}\bigg(\frac{\log^{m-y}(x)}{(1-x)^{i_{y}-1}}-\log^{m-y}(x)\bigg)

\displaystyle\quad+\sum_{y=0}^{n-2}\binom{m}{y+1}(y+1)!(-1)^{y}\sum_{i_{0}=n}^{n}\frac{1}{i_{0}-1}\sum_{i_{1}=2}^{i_{0}-1}\frac{1}{i_{1}-1}\cdots\sum_{i_{y}=2}^{i_{y-1}-1}\frac{1}{i_{y}-1}

\displaystyle\qquad\times A(m-y-1,1,1-x)\,,

where
A(m-y-1,1,x)
and
A(m-y-1,1,1-x)
are given in Lemma
1
. In particular, we have

\displaystyle A(m,n,1)=C(m,n,1)=\frac{(-1)^{m}m!}{n-1}\sum_{y=0}^{n-2}\zeta(m-y)\sum_{i_{1}=2}^{i_{0}-1}\frac{1}{i_{1}-1}\cdots\sum_{i_{y}=2}^{i_{y-1}-1}\frac{1}{i_{y}-1}\,.

Proof.

From the definition of
A(m,n,x)
, when
n\geq 2
, by using integration by parts, we can write

\displaystyle\quad A(m,n,x)

\displaystyle=-\frac{1}{n-1}\cdot\frac{\log^{m}(1-x)}{x^{n-1}}-\frac{m}{n-1}\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\log^{m-1}(1-t)}{t^{n-1}(1-t)}\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle=-\frac{1}{n-1}\cdot\frac{\log^{m}(1-x)}{x^{n-1}}-\frac{m}{n-1}\sum_{i=1}^{n-1}\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\log^{m-1}(1-t)}{t^{i}}\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle\quad-\frac{m}{n-1}\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\log^{m-1}(1-t)}{1-t}\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle=-\frac{1}{n-1}\cdot\frac{\log^{m}(1-x)}{x^{n-1}}+\frac{1}{n-1}\log^{m}(1-x)-\frac{m}{n-1}\sum_{i=1}^{n-1}A(m-1,i,x)\,,

successive application of the above relation
n-2
times, we can obtain that

\displaystyle\quad A(m,n,x)

\displaystyle=-\frac{1}{n-1}\cdot\frac{\log^{m}(1-x)}{x^{n-1}}+\frac{1}{n-1}\log^{m}(1-x)-\frac{m}{n-1}A(m-1,1,x)

\displaystyle\quad+\frac{m}{n-1}\sum_{i_{1}=2}^{n-1}\frac{1}{i_{1}-1}\cdot\frac{\log^{m-1}(1-x)}{x^{i_{1}-1}}-\frac{m}{n-1}\sum_{i_{1}=2}^{n-1}\frac{1}{i_{1}-1}\log^{m-1}(1-x)

\displaystyle\quad+\frac{m}{n-1}\sum_{i_{1}=2}^{n-1}\frac{m}{i_{1}-1}A(m-2,1,x)+\frac{m}{n-1}\sum_{i_{1}=2}^{n-1}\frac{m}{i_{1}-1}\sum_{i_{2}=2}^{i_{1}-1}A(m-2,i_{2},x)

\displaystyle=\sum_{y=0}^{n-2}\binom{m}{y}y!(-1)^{y+1}\sum_{i_{0}=n}^{n}\frac{1}{i_{0}-1}\sum_{i_{1}=2}^{i_{0}-1}\frac{1}{i_{1}-1}\cdots\sum_{i_{y}=2}^{i_{y-1}-1}\frac{1}{i_{y}-1}\cdot\frac{\log^{m-y}(1-x)}{x^{i_{y}-1}}

\displaystyle\quad+\sum_{y=0}^{n-2}\binom{m}{y}y!(-1)^{y}\sum_{i_{0}=n}^{n}\frac{1}{i_{0}-1}\sum_{i_{1}=2}^{i_{0}-1}\frac{1}{i_{1}-1}\cdots\sum_{i_{y}=2}^{i_{y-1}-1}\frac{1}{i_{y}-1}\log^{m-y}(1-x)

\displaystyle\quad+\sum_{y=0}^{n-2}\binom{m}{y+1}(y+1)!(-1)^{y+1}\sum_{i_{0}=n}^{n}\frac{1}{i_{0}-1}\sum_{i_{1}=2}^{i_{0}-1}\frac{1}{i_{1}-1}\cdots\sum_{i_{y}=2}^{i_{y-1}-1}\frac{1}{i_{y}-1}

\displaystyle\qquad\times A(m-y-1,1,x)\,.

Note that
C(m,n,x)=A(m,n,1)-A(m,n,1-x)
, thus we get the desired result.
∎

Theorem 2
.

Let
m,n\in\mathbb{N}
with
m\geq n\geq 2
and
x>0
, then we have

\displaystyle\quad B(m,n,x)

\displaystyle=\sum_{y=0}^{n-2}\binom{m}{y}y!\sum_{i_{0}=n}^{n}\frac{1}{i_{0}-1}\sum_{i_{1}=2}^{i_{0}-1}\frac{1}{i_{1}-1}\cdots\sum_{i_{y}=2}^{i_{y-1}-1}\frac{1}{i_{y}-1}\cdot\frac{(-1)^{n+i_{y}+y+1}\log^{m-y}(1+x)}{x^{i_{y}-1}}

\displaystyle\quad+\sum_{y=0}^{n-2}\binom{m}{y}y!\sum_{i_{0}=n}^{n}\frac{1}{i_{0}-1}\sum_{i_{1}=2}^{i_{0}-1}\frac{1}{i_{1}-1}\cdots\sum_{i_{y}=2}^{i_{y-1}-1}\frac{1}{i_{y}-1}\cdot(-1)^{n+y+1}\log^{m-y}(1+x)

\displaystyle\quad+\sum_{y=0}^{n-2}\binom{m}{y+1}(y+1)!\sum_{i_{0}=n}^{n}\frac{1}{i_{0}-1}\sum_{i_{1}=2}^{i_{0}-1}\frac{1}{i_{1}-1}\cdots\sum_{i_{y}=2}^{i_{y-1}-1}\frac{1}{i_{y}-1}

\displaystyle\qquad\times(-1)^{n+y}B(m-y-1,1,x)\,,

where
B(m-y-1,1,x)
are given in Lemma
2
. In particular, we have

\displaystyle B(m,n,1)

\displaystyle=\sum_{y=0}^{n-2}\binom{m}{y}y!\sum_{i_{0}=n}^{n}\frac{1}{i_{0}-1}\sum_{i_{1}=2}^{i_{0}-1}\frac{1}{i_{1}-1}\cdots\sum_{i_{y}=2}^{i_{y-1}-1}\frac{1}{i_{y}-1}

\displaystyle\qquad\times\log^{m-y}(2)(-1)^{n+y+1}((-1)^{i_{y}}+1)

\displaystyle\quad+\sum_{y=0}^{n-2}\binom{m}{y+1}(y+1)!\sum_{i_{0}=n}^{n}\frac{1}{i_{0}-1}\sum_{i_{1}=2}^{i_{0}-1}\frac{1}{i_{1}-1}\cdots\sum_{i_{y}=2}^{i_{y-1}-1}\frac{1}{i_{y}-1}

\displaystyle\qquad\times(-1)^{n+y}\bigg\{-\frac{m-y-1}{m-y}\log^{m-y}(2)+(m-y-1)!\zeta(m-y)

\displaystyle\qquad-\sum_{i=1}^{m-y-1}\binom{m-y-1}{i}i!\log^{m-y-1-i}(2)Li_{i+1}(\frac{1}{2})\bigg\}\,.

Proof.

From the definition of
B(m,n,x)
, when
n\geq 2
, by using integration by parts, we can write

\displaystyle B(m,n,x)

\displaystyle=-\frac{1}{n-1}\cdot\frac{\log^{m}(1+x)}{x^{n-1}}+\frac{m}{n-1}\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\log^{m-1}(1+t)}{t^{n-1}(1+t)}\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle=-\frac{1}{n-1}\cdot\frac{\log^{m}(1+x)}{x^{n-1}}+\frac{m}{n-1}\sum_{i=1}^{n-1}(-1)^{n-1-i}\int_{0}^{x}\frac{\log^{m-1}(1+t)}{t^{i}}\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle\quad+\frac{m}{n-1}\int_{0}^{x}\frac{(-1)^{n-1}\log^{m-1}(1+t)}{1+t}\mathrm{d}t

\displaystyle=-\frac{1}{n-1}\cdot\frac{\log^{m}(1+x)}{x^{n-1}}+\frac{(-1)^{n-1}}{n-1}\log^{m}(1+x)

\displaystyle\quad+\frac{m}{n-1}\sum_{i=1}^{n-1}(-1)^{n-1-i}B(m-1,i,x)\,,

similar with Theorem
1
, successive application of the above relation
n-2
times, we get the desired result.
∎

3
Integrals involving polylogarithms

In this section, we develop explicit expressions for integrals of types
J_{0}(m,p,x)
,
J_{1}(m,p,x)
,
J(m,p,q)
and
K(r,p,q)
. Before going further, we introduce some notations and lemmata. Following Flajolet-Salvy’s paper
[
12
]
, we write the classical linear Euler sums as

S_{p,q}^{+,+}:=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}^{(p)}}{{n}^{q}}\,.

Lemma 3

(
[
6
,
8
]
)
.

Let
n,m\in\mathbb{N}_{0}
and
x\geq 0
, defining

\displaystyle L(n,m,x):=\int_{0}^{x}y^{n}\log^{m}(y)\mathrm{d}y\,,

then we have

\displaystyle L(n,m,x)=\frac{x^{n+1}}{n+1}\sum_{j=0}^{m}\frac{(m+1-j)_{j}}{(n+1)^{j}}(-1)^{j}\log^{m-j}(x)\,,

where
(t)_{n}=t(t+1)\cdots(t+n-1)

Lemma 7

(Abel’s lemma on summation by parts
[

,

]
)
.

Let
\{f_{k}\}
and
\{g_{k}\}
be two sequences, and define the forward difference and backward difference, respectively, as

\Delta\tau_{k}=\tau_{k+1}-\tau_{k}\quad\hbox{and}\quad\nabla\tau_{k}=\tau_{k}-\tau_{k-1}\,,

then, there holds the relation:

\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}f_{k}\nabla g_{k}=\lim_{n\to\infty}f_{n}g_{n}-f_{1}g_{0}-\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}g_{k}\Delta f_{k}\,.

We now provide a criterion concerning the exchange of summation and integral for improper integrals.

Lemma 8
.

Given a series of functions
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}u_{n}(x),a\leq x\leq b
with
u_{n}(x)\geq 0
,
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}u_{n}(b)=\infty
and
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}u_{n}(x)
converges for
a\leq x<b
. Suppose
u_{n}(x)
is integrable (Riemann integrable or integrable as an improper integral) on
[a,b]
, the improper integral
\int_{a}^{b}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}u_{n}(x)\mathrm{d}x
converges, and
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}u_{n}(x)
internally closed uniform converges on
[a,b)
, i.e. for any
a\leq c<d<b
,
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}u_{n}(x)
converges uniformly on
[c,d]
, then we can exchange summation and integral, i.e.

\int_{a}^{b}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}u_{n}(x)\mathrm{d}x=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\int_{a}^{b}u_{n}(x)\mathrm{d}x\,.

Proof.

Since the improper integral
\int_{a}^{b}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}u_{n}(x)\mathrm{d}x
converges, then for any
\epsilon>0
, there exists
\delta>0
, s.t.
\lvert\int_{b-\delta}^{b}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}u_{n}(x)\mathrm{d}x\rvert\leq\frac{\epsilon}{3}
. It is not hard to see that we can choose
\delta>0
such that
b-\delta-a>0
. Note that,
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}u_{n}(x)
converges uniformly on
[a,b-\delta]
, then for fixed
\epsilon,\delta>0
, there exists
N>0
, for any
M\geq N
, we have

\left\lvert\sum_{n=M+1}^{\infty}u_{n}(x)\right\rvert\leq\frac{\epsilon}{3(b-\delta-a)}\quad x\in[a,b-\delta]\,.

Thus we can obtain that

\displaystyle\quad\left\lvert\sum_{n=1}^{M}\int_{a}^{b}u_{n}(x)\mathrm{d}x-\int_{a}^{b}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}u_{n}(x)\mathrm{d}x\right\rvert

\displaystyle\leq\left\lvert\int_{a}^{b-\delta}\sum_{n=1}^{M}u_{n}(x)-\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}u_{n}(x)\mathrm{d}x\right\rvert+\left\lvert\int_{b-\delta}^{b}\sum_{n=1}^{M}u_{n}(x)\mathrm{d}x\right\rvert+\left\lvert\int_{b-\delta}^{b}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}u_{n}(x)\mathrm{d}x\right\rvert

\displaystyle\leq(b-\delta-a)\frac{\epsilon}{3(b-\delta-a)}+\frac{\epsilon}{3}+\frac{\epsilon}{3}

\displaystyle=\epsilon\,,

which completes the proof.
∎

Lemma 9
.

Let
m\in\mathbb{N}_{0}
and

\displaystyle\quad J(m,p,1)

\displaystyle=\sum_{j=2}^{p}(-1)^{p-j}\zeta(j)\bigg(\sum_{i=2}^{p+1-j}\frac{-1}{(m+1)^{p+2-j-i}}\zeta(i)+\sum_{i=1}^{p+1-j}\frac{1}{(m+1)^{p+2-j-i}}H_{m+1}^{(i)}\bigg)

\displaystyle\quad+(-1)^{p-1}\bigg\{\sum_{i=2}^{p}\frac{-1}{(m+1)^{p-i+1}}\bigg((1+\frac{i}{2})\zeta(i+1)-\frac{1}{2}\sum_{k=1}^{i-2}\zeta(k+1)\zeta(i-k)

From the definition of

\displaystyle J(m,p,1)

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{n}\int_{0}^{1}x^{m+n}Li_{p}(x)\mathrm{d}x

\displaystyle\quad\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{n(m+n+1)^{p+1-j}}

\displaystyle\quad\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{m+n+1}}{n(m+n+1)^{p}}

\displaystyle=\sum_{i=2}^{p}\frac{-1}{(m+1)^{p-i+1}}\bigg(\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}}{n^{i}}-\sum_{n=1}^{m+1}\frac{H_{n}}{n^{i}}\bigg)+\frac{1}{(m+1)^{p-1}}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{m+n+1}}{n(n+m+1)}\,.

Note that

\displaystyle S_{1,i}^{+,+}=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{H_{n}}{n^{i}}=(1+\frac{i}{2})\zeta(i+1)-\frac{1}{2}\sum_{k=1}^{i-2}\zeta(k+1)\zeta(i-k)\,.\quad\cite[cite]{[\@@bibref{}{Flajolet}{}{}]}

\displaystyle\quad-\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{(m+1)H_{n+m+1}}{n(n+m+1)}

\displaystyle=-H_{m+1}^{2}-\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\sum_{b=0}^{m}\frac{1}{n+b}\cdot\frac{1}{n+m+1}

\displaystyle=-H_{m+1}^{2}-\sum_{b=0}^{m}\frac{1}{m+1-b}\bigg(H_{m+1}-H_{b}\bigg)\,.

Theorem 6

Let

\displaystyle\quad K(m,p,q)

\displaystyle=\sum_{x=1}^{q}K(m+p+x-1,0,q-x+1)\frac{(-1)^{p+x-1}}{(m+1)_{p+x-1}}\sum_{i_{1}=1}^{p}\cdots\sum_{i_{x-1}=1}^{p-i_{1}-\cdots-i_{x-2}+x-2}1

\displaystyle\quad+\sum_{i_{1}=1}^{p}\frac{(-1)^{i_{1}}}{(m+1)_{i_{1}}}\cdots\sum_{i_{q}=1}^{p-i_{1}-\cdots-i_{q-1}+q-1}\frac{(-1)^{i_{q}}}{(m+i_{1}+\cdots+i_{q-1}+1)_{i_{q}}}

\displaystyle\quad\times K(m+i_{1}+\cdots+i_{q},p-i_{1}-\cdots-i_{q}+q,0)\,.

K(m,p,q)
can be reduced to zeta values and generalized harmonic numbers.

Proof.

It is known that

]

K(m,p,q)=-\frac{1}{m+1}\bigg(K(m+1,p-1,q)+K(m+1,p,q-1)\bigg)\,,

successive application of the above relation

\displaystyle K(m,p,q)=\sum_{i_{1}=1}^{p}\frac{(-1)^{i_{1}}}{(m+1)_{i_{1}}}K(m+i_{1},p-i_{1}+1,q-1)+\frac{(-1)^{p}}{(m+1)^{p}}K(m+p,0,q)\,.

\displaystyle\quad K(m,p,q)

\displaystyle=\sum_{x=1}^{q}K(m+p+x-1,0,q-x+1)\frac{(-1)^{p+x-1}}{(m+1)_{p+x-1}}\sum_{i_{1}=1}^{p}\cdots\sum_{i_{x-1}=1}^{p-i_{1}-\cdots-i_{x-2}+x-2}1

\displaystyle\quad+\sum_{i_{1}=1}^{p}\frac{(-1)^{i_{1}}}{(m+1)_{i_{1}}}\cdots\sum_{i_{q}=1}^{p-i_{1}-\cdots-i_{q-1}+q-1}\frac{(-1)^{i_{q}}}{(m+i_{1}+\cdots+i_{q-1}+1)_{i_{q}}}

\displaystyle\quad\times K(m+i_{1}+\cdots+i_{q},p-i_{1}-\cdots-i_{q}+q,0)\,.

\displaystyle K(m,0,q)=m!(-1)^{m}\bigg(S_{q,m+1}^{+,+}-\zeta(m+q+1)\bigg)\,,\quad\cite[cite]{[\@@bibref{}{Freitas}{}{}]}
