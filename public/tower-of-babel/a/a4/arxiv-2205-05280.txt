Orthogonal Polynomials of Askey-Wilson Type
Mourad E.H. Ismail, Ruiming Zhang, Keru Zhou

Abstract
We study two families of orthogonal polynomials. The first is a finite family related to the Askey–Wilson polynomials but the orthogonality is on ℝ \mathbb{R} . A limiting case of this family is an infinite system of orthogonal polynomials whose moment problem is indeterminate. We provide several orthogonality measures for the infinite family and derive their Plancherel-Rotach asymptotics.

\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{q^{n^{2}\alpha+n}}{t_{1}^{n}}V_{n}\left(x_{1}(n);\,t_{1}q^{-n\alpha},\,t_{2},\,t_{3}|q\right)=\frac{\left(-q^{3}z/t_{1}t_{2}t_{3};q\right)_{\infty}}{\left(-q^{2}/t_{2}t_{3};q\right)_{\infty}^{2}}.

Theorem 6.2
.

Let

(6.15)

\alpha>\beta>0,\ \gamma,\delta>0,\ \gamma+\delta=\alpha-\beta=1

and

(6.16)

z,\,t_{1},\,t_{2},\,t_{3}\in\mathbb{C},\quad z\cdot t_{1}\cdot t_{2}\cdot t_{3}\neq 0.

Then there exists a positive number

(6.17)

0<\eta<\min\left\{1,\,(\alpha+\beta),\,(\beta+\delta),\,(\beta+\gamma)\right\}

such that

(6.18)

V_{n}\left(x_{2}(n);\,t_{1}q^{-n\beta},\,t_{2}q^{-n\gamma},\,t_{3}q^{-n\delta}\big|q\right)=\frac{t_{1}^{n}}{q^{n^{2}\beta+n}}\left(A_{q}\left(\frac{q^{2}z}{t_{1}t_{2}t_{3}}\right)+\mathcal{O}\left(q^{\eta n}\right)\right)

as
n\to\infty
.

Proof.

Let

t_{1}\to t_{1}q^{-n\beta},\ t_{2}\to t_{2}q^{-n\gamma},\ t_{3}\to t_{3}q^{-n\delta},\ x_{2}(n)=\frac{q^{-n\alpha}z-q^{n\alpha}/z}{2}.

Then,

\displaystyle V_{n}\left(x_{2}(n);\,t_{1}q^{-n\beta},\,t_{2}q^{-n\gamma},\,t_{3}q^{-n\delta}\big|q\right)\frac{q^{n^{2}\beta+n}\left(-q^{2+n}/t_{2}t_{3};q\right)_{n}}{t_{1}^{n}\left(-q^{2+n(\beta+\delta)}/t_{1}t_{3};q\right)_{n}}

\displaystyle=_{3}\phi_{2}\begin{pmatrix}\begin{array}[]{c}q^{-n},-q^{1+n(\alpha+\beta)}/t_{1}z,\,q^{1-n(\gamma+\delta)}z/t_{1}\\
-q^{2+n(\beta+\delta)}/t_{1}t_{3},-q^{2+n(\beta+\gamma)}/t_{1}t_{2}\end{array}&\bigg|q,\,-\frac{q^{2+2n}}{t_{2}t_{3}}\end{pmatrix}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\frac{\left(-q^{2}/t_{2}t_{3}\right)^{k}}{\left(q;q\right)_{k}}\frac{\left(q^{-n},\,q^{1-n}z/t_{1};q\right)_{k}q^{2kn}\left(-q^{1+n(\alpha+\beta)}/t_{1}z;q\right)_{k}}{\left(-q^{2+n(\beta+\delta)}/t_{1}t_{3},-q^{2+n(\beta+\gamma)}/t_{1}t_{2};q\right)_{k}}

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\frac{q^{k^{2}}}{\left(q;q\right)_{k}}\frac{\left(q;q\right)_{n}}{\left(q;q\right)_{n-k}}\frac{\left(t_{1}/z;q\right)_{n}}{\left(t_{1}/z;q\right)_{n-k}}\frac{\left(-q^{2}z/t_{1}t_{2}t_{3}\right)^{k}\left(-q^{1+n(\alpha+\beta)}/t_{1}z;q\right)_{k}}{\left(-q^{2+n(\beta+\delta)}/t_{1}t_{3},-q^{2+n(\beta+\gamma)}/t_{1}t_{2};q\right)_{k}}

\displaystyle=S_{1}(n)+S_{2}(n),

where

S_{1}(n)=\sum_{k=0}^{\left\lfloor\sqrt{n}\right\rfloor}\frac{q^{k^{2}}}{\left(q;q\right)_{k}}\frac{\left(q;q\right)_{n}}{\left(q;q\right)_{n-k}}\frac{\left(t_{1}/z;q\right)_{n}}{\left(t_{1}/z;q\right)_{n-k}}\frac{\left(-q^{2}z/t_{1}t_{2}t_{3}\right)^{k}\left(-q^{1+n(\alpha+\beta)}/t_{1}z;q\right)_{k}}{\left(-q^{2+n(\beta+\delta)}/t_{1}t_{3},-q^{2+n(\beta+\gamma)}/t_{1}t_{2};q\right)_{k}}

and

S_{2}(n)=\sum_{k=\left\lfloor\sqrt{n}\right\rfloor+1}^{n}\frac{q^{k^{2}}}{\left(q;q\right)_{k}}\frac{\left(q;q\right)_{n}}{\left(q;q\right)_{n-k}}\frac{\left(t_{1}/z;q\right)_{n}}{\left(t_{1}/z;q\right)_{n-k}}\frac{\left(-q^{2}z/t_{1}t_{2}t_{3}\right)^{k}\left(-q^{1+n(\alpha+\beta)}/t_{1}z;q\right)_{k}}{\left(-q^{2+n(\beta+\delta)}/t_{1}t_{3},-q^{2+n(\beta+\gamma)}/t_{1}t_{2};q\right)_{k}}.

Since

\frac{\left(q;q\right)_{n}}{\left(q;q\right)_{n-k}}\frac{\left(t_{1}/z;q\right)_{n}}{\left(t_{1}/z;q\right)_{n-k}}=1+\mathcal{O}\left(q^{n-\sqrt{n}}\right),\quad n\to\infty

and

\displaystyle\frac{\left(-q^{1+n(\alpha+\beta)}/t_{1}z;q\right)_{k}}{\left(-q^{2+n(\beta+\delta)}/t_{1}t_{3},-q^{2+n(\beta+\gamma)}/t_{1}t_{2}\right)_{k}}=\frac{\left(-q^{1+n(\alpha+\beta)}/t_{1}z;q\right)_{\infty}}{\left(-q^{1+n(\alpha+\beta)+k}/t_{1}z;q\right)_{\infty}}

\displaystyle\times\frac{\left(-q^{2+n(\beta+\delta)+k}/t_{1}t_{3};q\right)_{\infty}}{\left(-q^{2+n(\beta+\delta)}/t_{1}t_{3};q\right)_{\infty}}\frac{\left(-q^{2+n(\beta+\gamma)+k}/t_{1}t_{2};q\right)_{\infty}}{\left(-q^{2+n(\beta+\gamma)}/t_{1}t_{2};q\right)_{\infty}}

\displaystyle=1+\mathcal{O}\left(q^{n-\sqrt{n}}\right),\quad n\to\infty,

then,

(6.19)

\displaystyle S_{1}(n)=\sum_{k=0}^{\left\lfloor\sqrt{n}\right\rfloor}\frac{q^{k^{2}}}{\left(q;q\right)_{k}}\left(\frac{-q^{2}z}{t_{1}t_{2}t_{3}}\right)^{k}+\mathcal{O}\left(q^{\eta n}\right)

\displaystyle=A_{q}\left(\frac{q^{2}z}{t_{1}t_{2}t_{3}}\right)-\sum_{k=\left\lfloor\sqrt{n}\right\rfloor+1}^{n}\frac{q^{k^{2}}}{\left(q;q\right)_{k}}\left(\frac{-q^{2}z}{t_{1}t_{2}t_{3}}\right)^{k}+\mathcal{O}\left(q^{\eta n}\right)

where

(6.20)

and Theorem

6.19

6.20
)

∎

Theorem 6.3
.

, where

(6.25)
