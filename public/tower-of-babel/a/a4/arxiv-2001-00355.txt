Contributions of K 0 ∗ ​ ( 1430 ) K^{*}_{0}(1430) and K 0 ∗ ​ ( 1950 ) K^{*}_{0}(1950) in the three-body decays B → K ​ π ​ h B\to K\pi h
Wen-Fei Wang 1,2, Jian Chai 1,2, Ai-Jun Ma 3

Abstract
We study the contributions of the resonant states K 0 ∗ ​ ( 1430 ) K_{0}^{*}(1430) and K 0 ∗ ​ ( 1950 ) K_{0}^{*}(1950) in the three-body decays B → K ​ π ​ h B\to K\pi h (with h = π , K h=\pi,K ) in the perturbative QCD approach. The crucial nonperturbative input F K ​ π ​ ( s ) F_{K\pi}(s) in the distribution amplitudes of the S S -wave K ​ π K\pi system is derived from the matrix element of vacuum to K ​ π K\pi pair. The C ​ P CP averaged branching fraction of the quasi-two-body decay process B → K 0 ∗ ​ ( 1950 ) ​ h → K ​ π ​ h B\to K^{*}_{0}(1950)h\to K\pi h is about one order smaller than that of the corresponding decay B → K 0 ∗ ​ ( 1430 ) ​ h → K ​ π ​ h B\to K^{*}_{0}(1430)h\to K\pi h . In view of the important contribution from the S S -wave K ​ π K\pi system for the B → K ​ π ​ h B\to K\pi h decays, it is not appropriate to neglect the K 0 ∗ ​ ( 1950 ) K_{0}^{*}(1950) in the theoretical or experimental studies for the relevant three-body B B meson decays. The predictions in this work for the relevant decays are consistent with the existing experimental data.

IV
Conclusion

In this work, we studied the contributions from the resonant state
K_{0}^{*}(1430)
and, for the first time,
from the resonance
K_{0}^{*}(1950)
in the three-body decays
B\to K\pi h
in the PQCD approach.
The crucial nonperturbative input factor
F_{K\pi}(s)
in the distribution amplitudes of the
S
-wave

K\pi
system was derived from the matrix element of the vacuum to
K\pi
final state and was related to the scalar time-like form
factor
F_{0}^{K\pi}(s)
by the relation
F_{K\pi}(s)={B_{0}}/{m_{K_{0}^{*}}}F_{0}^{K\pi}(s)
. This relation also means that the LASS
parametrization for the
(K\pi)^{*}_{0}
system which frequently appeared in the experimental works cannot be adopted directly for the

K\pi
