On the Twin-Width of Smooth Manifolds
Édouard Bonnet, Kristóf Huszár

Abstract
Building on Whitney’s classical method of triangulating smooth manifolds, we show that every compact d d -dimensional smooth manifold admits a triangulation with dual graph of twin-width at most d O ⁡ ( d ) d^{O(d)} . In particular, it follows that every compact 3 3 -manifold has a triangulation with dual graph of bounded twin-width. This is in sharp contrast to the case of treewidth, where for any natural number n n there exists a closed 3-manifold such that every triangulation thereof has dual graph with treewidth at least n n . To establish this result, we bound the twin-width of the incidence graph of the d d -skeleton of the second barycentric subdivision of the 2 ​ d 2d -dimensional hypercubic honeycomb. We also show that every compact, piecewise-linear (hence smooth) d d -dimensional manifold has triangulations where the dual graph has an arbitrarily large twin-width.

A

Abstract simplicial complexes.

Given a finite ground set

.
Any element of

is called a

of
\mathscr{X}

the

The

, is then the maximum dimension of a face of
\mathscr{X}
.
If
\dim\mathscr{X}=d
, we also say that
\mathscr{X}
is a
simplicial
d
-complex
.
For
0\leqslant i\leqslant\dim\mathscr{X}
, we let
\mathscr{X}(i)=\{\sigma\in\mathscr{X}:\dim\sigma=i\}
denote the set of
i

i

i
-simplices) of
\mathscr{X}
.
The
i
-skeleton

\mathscr{X}_{i}=\bigcup_{j=0}^{i}\mathscr{X}(j)
of
\mathscr{X}
is the union of all faces of
\mathscr{X}
up to dimension
i
. Note that any simple graph
G=(V,E)
can be regarded as a
1
-dimensional simplicial complex
\mathscr{X}_{G}
with
\mathscr{X}_{G}(0)=\{\{v\}:v\in V\}
and
\mathscr{X}_{G}(1)=E
. For

the

are respectively called the

,

Geometric realization of simplicial complexes.

Every abstract simplicial complex
\mathscr{X}
may be realized geometrically as follows. To each abstract

we associate a

, see
