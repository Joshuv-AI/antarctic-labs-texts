Global choice is not conservative over local choice for Zermelo set theory
Elliot Glazer

Abstract
We prove that Global Choice is not conservative over ZC and that ZF − - Union does not prove existence of x ∪ y x\cup y for all x x and y y . Each proof is by constructing a pathological model inside a symmetric extension of L . L.

Proposition 5.2
.

For infinite
A,
define

H^{\prime}(A)=\{x:\forall y\in TC(\{x\})(|y|\leq|A|)\}.

Then
H^{\prime}(A)\subset V_{\aleph^{*}(A)^{+}}.
In particular,
H^{\prime}(A)
is a transitive set.

Proof.

Let
\kappa=\aleph^{*}(A).
It suffices to find a surjection from
\kappa
onto
\text{rk}``(TC(\{x\})
for
x\in H^{\prime}(A).

We will recursively define surjections
f_{x,n}:\kappa\rightarrow\text{rk}``(\bigcup^{n}\{x\})
for all
x\in H^{\prime}(A).
Suppose we have defined all such functions up through
n.
Define a surjection
g_{x,n+1}:\kappa\times x\rightarrow\text{rk}``(\bigcup^{n+1}\{x\})
by
(\alpha,y)\mapsto f_{y,n}(\alpha).
For each
\alpha,\text{ot}(g_{x,n+1}``(\{\alpha\}\times x))<\kappa,
yielding a canonical surjection
f_{x,n+1}:\kappa\simeq\kappa\times\kappa\rightarrow\text{rk}``(\bigcup^{n+1}\{x\}).
This completes the recursion, and now we define a surjection
f_{x}:\kappa\simeq\kappa\times\omega\rightarrow\text{rk}``(TC(\{x\}))
by
(\alpha,n)\mapsto f_{x,n}(\alpha).

∎

To prove

Let

M=\{x:\forall y\in TC(\{x\})\exists n<\omega(|y|\leq|\mathcal{P}^{n}(A)|\vee|y|\leq|\mathcal{P}^{n}(\kappa)|)\}.

Applying

We get Extensionality and Foundation from
