A new approach to construct minimal linear codes over 𝔽 3 \mathbb{F}_{3}
Wajid M. Shaikh, Rupali S. Jain, B. Surendranath Reddy, Bhagyashri S. Patil, Sahar M. A. Maqbol

Abstract
In this article, we present two new approaches to construct minimal linear codes of dimension n + 1 n+1 over 𝔽 3 \mathbb{F}_{3} using characteristic and ternary functions. We also obtain the weight distributions of these constructed minimal linear codes. We further show that a specific class of these codes violates Ashikhmin-Barg condition.

In this section, we provide some definitions and results that are necessary prior knowledge for understanding this article. We denote a finite field with
p

Definition 2.1
.

[
10
]

The
support

is the set of the coordinates at which
{c}

\text{Supp}({\bf c})=\{i\ |\ c_{i}\neq 0,\ 1\leq i\leq n\}

Note that,
\text{wt}({\bf c})=|\text{Supp}({\bf c})|

\text{wt}({\bf x})

Definition 2.2
.

For

, then we say
{\bf w}_{2}

{\bf w}_{1}
and denoted by
{\bf w}_{2}\preceq{\bf w}_{1}
.

Definition 2.3
.

A codeword
{\bf w}

{\bf w}
covers only the codeword of the form
\alpha{\bf w}
, where
\alpha\in\mathbb{F}_{p}^{*}
. A code

Definition 2.4

A ternary function

is a function from

to
\mathbb{F}_{3}
. The Walsh transform for a ternary function is given by

\widehat{f}({\bf w})=\sum_{{\bf x}\in\mathbb{F}_{3}^{n}}\zeta_{3}^{f({\bf x})-{\bf w}\cdot{\bf x}}

\zeta_{3}
is a cube root of unity,
{\bf w}\in\mathbb{F}_{3}^{n}
and

is the standard inner product of
{\bf w}
and
{\bf x}
.

Throughout this paper, we assume that

Let

f_{ij}({\bf x})=\begin{cases}1,&\text{ if }{\bf x}\in W_{i}\setminus\{0\}\\
2,&\text{ if }{\bf x}\in W_{j}\setminus\{0\}\\
0,&\text{Otherwise}\end{cases}

where,

and

. Note that

For
1\leq s\leq\frac{3^{t}+1}{2}
and
\mathcal{A}=\{i_{1},i_{2},\ldots,i_{s},i_{s+1},\ldots,i_{2s}\}

f({\bf x})=\sum_{k=1}^{s}f_{i_{k}i_{s+k}}({\bf x})

(3.1)

Using the function

\mathscr{C}_{f}

\mathbb{F}_{3}

Theorem 3.1
.

Let
\mathbb{F}_{p}^{n}
be an
n
-dimensional vector space over a finite field
\mathbb{F}_{p}
and
W
be a
m
-dimensional subspace of
\mathbb{F}_{p}^{n}

\mathcal{S}_{k}=\{{\bf x}\in W\ |\ {\bf y}\cdot{\bf x}=k\}

Proof.

We prove this theorem by mathematical induction on
m
.

If
m=1
, then we have
W=\text{span}(\{{\bf w}\})
. Since
{\bf y}\notin W^{\perp}
,
we have
{\bf y}\cdot{\bf w}\neq 0
, which implies
{\bf y}\cdot{\bf w}=k
, for some

. From this we get
{\bf x}\cdot\alpha{\bf w}=\alpha k
, for all
\alpha\in\mathbb{F}_{p}

.

Assume that result is true for
m-1
and we prove the result for
m
.

Let
W
be a
m
-dimensional subspace of
\mathbb{F}_{p}^{n}
. Then
W=\text{span}(\{{\bf w_{1}},{\bf w_{2}},\ldots,{\bf w_{m}}\})
. Since
{\bf y}\notin W^{\perp}
, we have
{\bf y}\cdot{\bf w}_{i}\neq 0
, for some
1\leq i\leq m
. Without loss of generality, consider the case
{\bf y}\cdot{\bf w}_{i}\neq 0
, for some
1\leq i\leq m-1
and
{\bf y}\cdot{\bf w}_{m}=k
, for some
k\in\mathbb{F}_{p}
. Now
W^{\prime}=\text{span}(\{{\bf w}_{1},{\bf w}_{2},\ldots,{\bf w}_{m-1}\})
is
(m-1)
-dimensional subspace of
\mathbb{F}_{p}^{n}
. Hence, the result is true for
W^{\prime}
, from this we have,
\mathcal{S}^{\prime}_{0},\mathcal{S}^{\prime}_{1},\ldots,\mathcal{S}^{\prime}_{p-1}
be a collection of subsets of
W^{\prime}
, with
|\mathcal{S}^{\prime}_{i}|=p^{m-2}
, for all
0\leq i\leq p-1
such that
{\bf y}\cdot{\bf x}=i
, for all
{\bf x}\in\mathcal{S}^{\prime}_{i}
and
0\leq i\leq p-1
. Note that
\mathcal{S}^{\prime}_{0},\mathcal{S}^{\prime}_{1},\ldots,\mathcal{S}^{\prime}_{p-1}
is also subsets of
W
. Now, consider the sets
{\bf w}_{m}+\mathcal{S}^{\prime}_{0},{\bf w}_{m}+\mathcal{S}^{\prime}_{1},\ldots,{\bf w}_{m}+\mathcal{S}^{\prime}_{p-1}
be a collection of subsets of
W
, with
|{\bf w}_{m}+\mathcal{S}^{\prime}_{i}|=p^{m-2}
and
{\bf y}\cdot({\bf w}_{m}+{\bf x})=k+i
, for all
0\leq i\leq p-1
and for all
{\bf x}\in\mathcal{S}^{\prime}_{i}
. Also, observe that
\mathcal{S}^{\prime}_{i}\neq{\bf w}_{m}+\mathcal{S}^{\prime}_{j}

. Similarly,
2{\bf w}_{m}+\mathcal{S}^{\prime}_{0},2{\bf w}_{m}+\mathcal{S}^{\prime}_{1},\ldots,2{\bf w}_{m}+\mathcal{S}^{\prime}_{p-1}
be a collection of subsets of

and for all

such that
{\bf y}\cdot{\bf x}=i

{\bf x}\in\mathcal{S}_{i}
. Hence, the result.

5
Conclusion

In this paper, we constructed two classes of ternary minimal linear codes of dimension
n+1
. One class is constructed using characteristic function over partial spread and another class is constructed using ternary function on partial spread. We also obtained weight distribution for these minimal linear codes. We further prove that a family of these constructed minimal linear codes violates Ashikhmin-Barg condition. Using the functions defined in this paper, constructing minimal linear codes over
\mathbb{F}_{3}
of dimension greater than
n+1
can be a further scope of the study.
