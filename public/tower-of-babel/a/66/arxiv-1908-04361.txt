Asymptotic and exterior Dirichlet problems for the minimal surface equation in the Heisenberg group with a balanced metric
Fidelis Bittencourt, Edson S. Figueiredo, Pedro Fusieger, Jaime Ripoll

Abstract
It is proved that the Heisenberg group Nil 3 \operatorname*{Nil}\nolimits_{3} with a balanced metric, the sum of the left and right invariant metrics, splits as a Riemannian product 𝕋 × ℤ \mathbb{T\times Z} , where 𝕋 \mathbb{T} is a totally geodesic surface and ℤ \mathbb{Z} the center of Nil 3 . \operatorname*{Nil}\nolimits_{3}. It is then proved the existence of complete properly embedded minimal surfaces in Nil 3 \operatorname*{Nil}\nolimits_{3} by solving the asymptotic Dirichlet problem for the minimal surface equation on 𝕋 \mathbb{T} . It is also proved the existence of complete properly embedded minimal surfaces foliating an open set of Nil 3 \operatorname*{Nil}\nolimits_{3} having as boundary a given curve Γ \Gamma in 𝕋 , \mathbb{T}, satisfying the exterior circle condition, by solving the exterior Dirichlet problem for the minimal surface equation in the unbounded connected component of 𝕋 \ Γ \mathbb{T}\backslash\Gamma .

\displaystyle\leq\sup_{\partial\Omega_{m}\backslash\partial\Omega}\left\|\nabla w_{s}\right\|_{\partial\Omega}=f^{\prime}(m)

\displaystyle=\frac{ce^{\sqrt{2}\alpha\arctan\left(\frac{m+\alpha}{2^{3/2}}\right)}}{\left(m+\alpha\right)^{2}+8}

where

Since

for any

]
).

Consider now a sequence
\left\{z_{j}\right\}\subset T_{m}
converging to

as

\overline{\Omega}_{m}
on the

C^{2}
norm to a solution

\mathcal{M}=0
in
\Omega_{m}
. From PDE regularity

]
)
.

w_{m}

\mathcal{M}=0

\Omega_{m}
that

,
w_{m}|_{\Gamma_{m}}=t_{m}

It follows that

. Moreover, it

Since the bound

We prove that given if

on

\Lambda.
Suppose that
u_{s_{i}}=\lim_{m}w_{m}^{i},

. Given

for all

. We also have

Assume that
\limsup_{r(p)\rightarrow\infty}\left(u_{s_{2}}(p)-u_{s_{1}}(p)\right)=0.
We claim that for any
\varepsilon>0
,

Indeed, if

\left\{p\in\Lambda\text{

}u_{s_{2}}(p)-\varepsilon\geq u_{s_{1}}(p)\right\}
