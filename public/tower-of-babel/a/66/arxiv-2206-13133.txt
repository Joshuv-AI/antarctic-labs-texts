Gluonic nature of the newly observed state X ⁡ ( 2600 ) X(2600)
Sheng-Qi Zhang 1 , Bing-Dong Wan 1,2 , Liang Tang 3 and Cong-Feng Qiao 1

Abstract
Motivated by the newly observed resonance X ⁡ ( 2600 ) X(2600) by BESIII Collaboration, we examine the trigluon glueball interpretation for it in the framework of QCD sum rules. We evaluate the mass spectra of the trigluon glueballs with quantum numbers 0 − ⁣ + 0^{-+} and 2 − ⁣ + 2^{-+} up to dimension 8 condensate in the operator product expansion. Our numerical results indicate that the mass of the 2 − ⁣ + 2^{-+} trigluon glueball is about 2.66 ± 0.06 2.66\pm 0.06 GeV, which is consistent with the mass of the X(2600) within the uncertainties, while 0 − ⁣ + 0^{-+} has a mass of 2.01 ± 0.14 2.01\pm 0.14 GeV. The possible decay channels of the 2 − ⁣ + 2^{-+} state are analyzed, which are crucial in decoding X ⁡ ( 2600 ) X(2600) ’s internal structure and are hopefully measurable in BESIII, BEllEII, PANDA, and LHCb experiments.

In order to evaluate the mass spectra of the glueballs, one has to construct the appropriate currents that possesses the foremost information about the concerned hadron, such as quantum numbers and the constituent quarks or gluons. \displaystyle\frac{1}{\pi}\text{Im}\,\Pi_{J^{PC}}^{k,\,\text{phe}}

\displaystyle(\lambda^{k}_{J^{PC}})^{2}\,\delta\left(s-(M_{J^{PC}}^{k})^{2}\right)+\rho_{J^{PC}}^{k,\,\text{cont}}(s)\theta(s-s_{0})\;.

\displaystyle\Pi_{J^{PC}}^{k}(q^{2})

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\pi}\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{\text{Im}\,\Pi_{J^{PC}}^{k,\,\text{QCD}}(s)}{s-q^{2}}\,ds+\cdots

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{(\lambda^{k}_{J^{PC}})^{2}}{(M^{k}_{J^{PC}})^{2}-q^{2}}+\int_{s_{0}}^{\infty}\frac{\rho^{k,\,\text{cont}}_{J^{PC}}(s)\,\theta(s-s_{0})}{s-q^{2}}\,ds\;.

(17)

To limit the contributions of higher order condensates on the QCD side and the contributions of high excited and continuum states in the phenomenological side, an effective way is to impose Borel transformation simultaneously on both sides. The form of Borel transformation is as follows,

{\cal B}\!\left[g(q^{2})\right]\equiv g(\tau)=\lim\limits_{\tiny\begin{matrix}-q^{2},n\rightarrow\infty\\
\frac{-q^{2}}{n}=\frac{1}{\tau}\end{matrix}}\frac{(q^{2})^{n}}{(n-1)!}\left(-\frac{\partial}{\partial q^{2}}\right)^{n}\!g(q^{2})\;.

(18)

Here,

is the function of the independent variable

and
\tau

Performing the Borel transformation to Eq. (
17

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\pi}\int_{0}^{\infty}\!\>\text{Im}\,\Pi_{J^{PC}}^{k,\,\text{QCD}}(s)~e^{-s\tau}\,ds

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(\lambda^{k}_{J^{PC}})^{2}~e^{-(M^{k}_{J^{PC}})^{2}~\tau}+\int_{s_{0}}^{\infty}\!\>\rho^{k,\,\text{cont}}_{J^{PC}}(s)~e^{-s\tau}\,ds~.

(19)

16

Adopting quark-hadron duality

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\pi}\int_{s_{0}}^{\infty}\!\>\text{Im}\,\Pi_{J^{PC}}^{k,\,\text{QCD}}(s)~e^{-s\tau}\,ds

\displaystyle\simeq

\displaystyle\int_{s_{0}}^{\infty}\!\>\rho^{k,\,\text{cont}}_{J^{PC}}(s)~e^{-s\tau}\,ds~,

\displaystyle(\lambda^{k}_{J^{PC}})^{2}~e^{-(M^{k}_{J^{PC}})^{2}~\tau}=\frac{1}{\pi}\int_{0}^{s_{0}}\!\>\text{Im}\,\Pi_{J^{PC}}^{k,\,\text{QCD}}(s)~e^{-s\tau}\,ds\;,

V
Conclusions

In this work, we investigate the trigluon glueballs with
J^{PC}=0^{-+}
and
2^{-+}
which are possible quantum numbers possessed by
X(2600)
in the framework of QCD sum rules. The numerical results indicate that there are two possible configurations of
0^{-+}
trigluon glueball states with mass
2.01\pm 0.14
GeV, and three patterns of
2^{-+}
trigluon glueball states with mass
2.66\pm 0.06
GeV.

We find there are three degenerate
2^{-+}
trigluon glueballs related to currents (
5
), (
6
), and (
8
), in agreement with the experimental observation of
X(2600)
in mass within the scope of errors. To disentangle these states, one needs to find some yet unknown extra conditions, physical observables, to experimentally measure. In case it is realized, the other two disentangled
2^{-+}
trigluon glueballs may be treated as partners of
X(2600)
.

The possible trigluon glueball decay modes are analyzed and confronted to the corresponding baryonium state
\Xi-\bar{\Xi}
, by which the internal structure of
X(2600)
may be decoded with the further experimental verifications, like in BESIII, BELLEII, PANDA, and LHCb experiments.
